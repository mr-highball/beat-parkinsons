Community and context: Late Ming Chinese materia medica compilation. The main-uses passage attributes the wording to Shennong Shijing (神農食經). Its independent date and witness are unverified.
Exposure and reported symptom: Tea leaves prepared as an oral drink. The historical 茶 / 茗 terminology does not establish a modern green tea variety, beverage strength, brew method or daily intake.
Reported direction: The attributed phrase 令人少睡有力悅志 describes sleeping less, feeling stronger and improved spirits. The same scan includes cautions about amounts and drinking conditions, attributed to historical writers.
Authenticity: The original page images show the entry heading and the attributed passage. The publication year follows the accessioned copy's catalog.
Efficacy evidence: The source gives a recorded customary belief without a diagnosed Parkinson cohort, comparator, fatigue scale, or measured insomnia result. Wakefulness, vigor and clinical fatigue are distinct outcomes.
