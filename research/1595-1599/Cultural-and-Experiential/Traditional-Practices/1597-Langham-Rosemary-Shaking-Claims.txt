Title: Langham's rosemary recommendations for trembling and shaking
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Original historical practitioner text
Source: William Langham, Garden of Health, p.563, entries 81 and 85; https://wellcomecollection.org/works/aq8kfpxg
Publication date: 1597
Date basis: Wellcome corrects printed 1579 to 1597. Copy 3's inspected Nn2 leaf is not among its listed 1633 replacements.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17
Location inspected: Copy 3, canvas 581; https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b30328317_0003_0581.jp2/full/2000,/0/default.jpg

Entry 81 recommends rosemary in a drink or applied for trembling among other complaints. Entry 85 separately claims benefit against shaking and palsy without specifying route. Neighboring recipes concern other conditions and must not supply its missing preparation. No individual outcome is reported.

Reported direction: Intended benefit, not an observed clinical effect.
Authenticity: Official public-domain page visually inspected; relevant signature and corrected edition date checked against institutional metadata.
Efficacy evidence: No comparator, measurement, Parkinson diagnosis or rest-tremor subtype. Exact species and dried-leaf exposure remain unverified.
Open trail: The separate 1633 edition cited by a later literary witness remains unread; do not count it as a second patient observation.
