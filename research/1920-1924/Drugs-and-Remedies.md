# Drugs and remedies, 1920-1924

### 1924 — Critchley describes symptom-specific effects in clinical practice

Macdonald Critchley discussed hyoscine, belladonna, and other agents in a paper on parkinsonism. He described little improvement in tremor or rigidity from a phosphorus preparation in one case, despite reduced discomfort, and warned that strychnine could increase tremor. He discussed adding belladonna for profuse salivation. These are historical clinical impressions without formal comparative outcome data.
