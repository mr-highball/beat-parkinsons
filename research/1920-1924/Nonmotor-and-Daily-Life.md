# Nonmotor symptoms and daily life, 1920-1924

### 1923 — Dundas-Grant and Worster-Drought report subjective nasal obstruction

J. Dundas-Grant and C. C. Worster-Drought described perceived nasal stenosis in a patient diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease. The complaint is a historical example of a patient-reported symptom beyond limb movement. The single case does not establish a characteristic symptom or link with loss of smell.

[Dundas-Grant and Worster-Drought, Proceedings of the Royal Society of Medicine, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2103704/)
