Title: Chronic diarrhoea in persons repatriated from the Indies
Author: G. J. W. Koolemans Beynen
Source: https://archive.nsgh.nl/acta-1-1926
Publication date: 1922
Date basis: The accessible 1926 Acta Leidensia reprint cites the earlier publication in Tijdschrift voor Vergelijkende Geneeskunde, volume VIII, 1922. Original journal issue not independently recovered.
Text coverage: Distilled from the society archive reprint, especially printed pages 244–247.

The clinician recounts the use of fresh strawberries for sprue and credits a predecessor with introducing a fruit-only regimen. This is a historical description of clinical practice. When milk disagreed with a patient, a fruit-based regimen was among the alternatives. The report discusses the difficulty of isolating underlying causes and describes patients receiving other diets and interventions.

The detailed tetany case used a milk regimen, cod-liver oil and calcium treatment. The report does not attribute the observed improvement in tetany to strawberries. No fall counts, Parkinson cohort, or controlled berry comparison are reported.
