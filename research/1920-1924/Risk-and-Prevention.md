# Risk and prevention, 1920-1924

### 1921 — Legrand questions an emotional origin using wartime observations

Bernard Legrand examined whether wartime stress and shock supported a causal role for emotion in paralysis agitans. He challenged simple attribution of tremor to emotional events and discussed confusion with other tremor syndromes. The thesis is a historical critique with incomplete ascertainment and no modern exposure estimates.
