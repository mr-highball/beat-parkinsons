# Mechanisms and models, 1920-1924

### 1923 — Lewy publishes a clinical and pathology monograph

Friedrich H. Lewy’s monograph assembled clinical, physiological, and pathological research on paralysis agitans and muscle tone. The catalog provides the original bibliographic record. Modern retrospective terminology and methods should be checked against the original text when individual observations are extracted.

[Lewy, Die Lehre vom Tonus und der Bewegung, catalog entry](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA0910800X)

### 1924 — Pallidal iron deposits are examined in a parkinsonian autopsy

Jean Lhermitte and colleagues found unusual iron-containing deposits in the globus pallidus of a parkinsonian case, with a distribution they distinguished from changes reported in some other cases. They explicitly lacked evidence that the deposits explained the symptoms. The tissue findings do not measure dietary iron exposure.

[Lhermitte and colleagues, Journal of Neurology and Psychopathology, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1068263/)
