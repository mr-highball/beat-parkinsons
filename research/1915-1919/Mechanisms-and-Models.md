# Mechanisms and models, 1915-1919

### 1919 — Tretiakoff identifies substantia nigra pathology

In a Paris medical thesis, Konstantin Tretiakoff described neuronal loss and inclusion bodies in the substantia nigra in paralysis agitans, drawing on postmortem comparisons that included secondary parkinsonian cases. He used Lewy’s name for the inclusions. The GHU catalog identifies the original thesis. Later work was needed to assess how consistently the finding applied.

[Tretiakoff, Contribution à l’étude de l’anatomie pathologique du Locus Niger de Soemmering, Paris thesis catalog](https://bibliotheques.ghu-paris.fr/index.php?id=114707&lvl=notice_display)
