Text coverage: Distilled text from physical PDF pages 439–552. Verified the psychiatry title page, the contents page listing the combined meeting, its separately numbered proceedings, the start of the first Arabic-numbered paper, and the section's last page.

The preliminary pages introduce the Psychiatry section and a joint Psychiatry and Neurology meeting. The joint meeting covers shell shock. The psychiatry papers concern how rumours spread and the use of a serological reaction in patients with mental illness. Bernard Hart uses perceiving an orange as an example of how perception relies on prior knowledge (physical page 494). A potato field serves as an analogy for the role of an observer's environment (physical page 502). Date instances concern time. None of these examples describes assigned food intake.

There is no strawberry, raspberry, grape or olive oil exposure in the matching section. A separate section earlier in the bound volume discusses an appendiceal concretion containing mixed seeds. Its scope and anatomical findings are distinct from this proceedings record and give no food-specific Parkinson falls outcome.
