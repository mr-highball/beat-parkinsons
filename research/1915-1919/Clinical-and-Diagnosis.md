# Clinical findings and diagnosis, 1915-1919

### 1918 — Von Economo publishes an encephalitis lethargica monograph

Constantin von Economo’s monograph documented epidemic encephalitis with neurological and sleep or wakefulness findings. This is a precursor source for the later clinical literature on postencephalitic parkinsonism. The catalog dates this monograph to 1918. His initial description is separately dated to 1917.

[Von Economo, Die Encephalitis lethargica, Wellcome collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/fduqv2c9)

### 1919 — Brasher and Caldwell publish British encephalitis lethargica cases

C. W. Brasher and J. R. Caldwell reported two cases of encephalitis lethargica. Their report expands the geographic record of an illness subsequently linked with secondary parkinsonism. These are reports of the antecedent illness and offer no estimate of the subsequent risk of idiopathic Parkinson’s disease.

[Brasher and Caldwell, British Medical Journal, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2341353/)
