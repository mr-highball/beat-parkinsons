# Movement and Rehabilitation for 1980–1984

### 1981 — Physiotherapy and occupational therapy crossover trial

Gibberd and colleagues tested a four-week outpatient program combining physiotherapy and occupational therapy against inactive treatment. Twenty-four people enrolled and 16 completed both periods. Timed tasks and observer assessments showed no significant change with the program studied. The short treatment period and attrition limit interpretation. [Original publication](https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/282/6271/1196.1.full.pdf)
