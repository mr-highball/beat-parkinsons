# Nonmotor and Daily Life for 1980–1984

### 1981 — Depression and cognition in clinic patients

Mayeux and colleagues assessed 55 consecutive patients without dementia and 31 spouses with depression and cognitive measures. Depression was frequent, and cognitive scores tracked depression severity as well as motor severity. The clinical sample and cross-sectional measurements leave the direction and cause of these relationships uncertain. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7195481/)

### 1983 — Odor detection and discrimination

Ward and colleagues found lower odor detection and discrimination scores in people with Parkinson’s disease than in matched controls. Measured impairment showed no relation to symptom duration or treatment in this sample. The study identified a nonmotor feature using objective sensory tests. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6683381/)
