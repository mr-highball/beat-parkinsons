Title: Food allergy: the celery-mugwort-spice syndrome. Association with mango allergy?
Authors: B Wüthrich and T Hofer
Date: 1984-06-22
Identifiers: PMID 6734454; DOI 10.1055/s-2008-1069310
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6734454/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the authors' English abstract. Case-level details require the full German report.

Of 31 people diagnosed with celery allergy in 1978–1982, 27 were female and four male. Follow-up in 1983 tested spices and vegetables using skin and RAST methods. The authors classified clinically relevant caraway hypersensitivity in 26% of this selected group and highlighted differences between clinical allergy and skin sensitization. The abstract does not identify ground or toasted caraway in individual reactions or report voice or speech measurements.
