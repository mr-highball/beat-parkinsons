Title: Changes in superoxide dismutase, catalase, glutathione peroxidase, and glutathione reductase activities and thiobarbituric acid-reactive products levels in early stages of development in dystrophic chickens
PMID: 6705887
DOI: 10.1016/0014-4886(84)90006-2
Publication: Experimental Neurology, April 1984
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6705887/
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original author abstract

Mizuno studied antioxidant enzyme activities and reactive products in chicken muscle over early development. The dystrophic animals had elevated activities compared with controls at the measured stages in the author's abstract. These are avian muscle measures. The comparison is related to the later neurology poster 17.07.04, with sample overlap unverified. There is no cooked chicken or Parkinson fatigue outcome.
