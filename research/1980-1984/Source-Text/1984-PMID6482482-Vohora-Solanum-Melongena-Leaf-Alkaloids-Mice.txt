Title: Effect of alkaloids of Solanum melongena on the central nervous system.
PMID: 6482482
DOI: 10.1016/0378-8741(84)90078-3
First published: 1984
Authors: Vohora SB, Kumar I, Khan MS
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6482482/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Mouse pharmacology screen injecting a crude alkaloid fraction extracted from inedible LEAVES of Solanum melongena intraperitoneally (LD50 > 1 g/kg i.p.), reporting non-narcotic analgesia and CNS depression without anticonvulsant action. Inedible leaf alkaloids administered intraperitoneally are unusable as evidence for baked, grilled, or roasted eggplant fruit.
