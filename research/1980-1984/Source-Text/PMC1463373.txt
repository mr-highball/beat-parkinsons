Title: The effects of acute or chronic ingestion of propranolol or metoprolol on the physiological responses to prolonged, submaximal exercise in hypertensive men.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1463373/
PMID: 6712861
PMCID: PMC1463373
DOI: 10.1111/j.1365-2125.1984.tb02342.x
Publication date: 1984-03
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC printed-page scans 273–281.

The drug comparison assessed work capacity, perceived leg fatigue and exertion in hypertensive men receiving beta blockers. Printed page 274 specifies a coffee restriction during testing. The results concern drugs and do not attribute fatigue to brewed coffee.
