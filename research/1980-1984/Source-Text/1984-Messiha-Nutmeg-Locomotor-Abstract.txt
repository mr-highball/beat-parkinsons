Injected nutmeg extract and mouse locomotion
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6543064/

1984, Veterinary and Human Toxicology 26 supplement 2, pages 17–20. Messiha and Zaki report moderately lower spontaneous locomotor activity in mice after repeated intraperitoneal nutmeg extract. In separate activity comparisons, pretreatment amplified ethanol-associated locomotor depression and reduced amphetamine-associated locomotor stimulation. These are locomotion observations from the author abstract. They do not establish a separate rest-tremor outcome, Parkinson model or ground-nutmeg meal. The complete original methods, figures and case boundaries remain inaccessible through the checked public routes.
