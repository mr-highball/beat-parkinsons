The authors describe their own human drug–ethanol research and a masking placebo using unsweetened orange juice with peppermint water. Page 28S says they evaluated the formulation in tasting and psychomotor contexts. Table 1 records whether 12 participants believed they had received alcohol in a blinded experiment involving ethanol and analgesics; it is not an orange-versus-no-orange comparison and does not report a citrus-specific treatment effect. The research population is young healthy volunteers, not Parkinson patients. Sway is one test in the wider test battery, but this page does not provide an orange-attributable balance or fall outcome. No inference about benefit or harm of citrus is warranted.
