Title: Beware of bay leaf

Two meals were followed by symptoms linked to intact leaves lodged in a pharyngeal pouch or hypopharynx. Each case was managed by removal. Preparation and mechanical mechanism differ from ground bay leaf, and the report supplies no Parkinson-specific severity comparison. This is the previously assessed Johns publication and has one study family.
