Title: British Medical Journal 1984 volume 289 index and separate clinical references
PMCID: PMC1440947
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1440947/
Published: 1984-12
Archive scope: Factual note from the original article, original page scans or identified original abstract member.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The official 33 scanned pages are the original July–December volume index (printed pp. 3–35). Twenty-nine pages produced native OCR text. Four dense pages without native text were checked directly against the images. The page 25 Parkinson entry concerns drug treatment. Page 12 separately lists drug-induced dyskinesia and dystonia. CHARD on page 8, BARLEY on page 5, and SALMON on page 29 occur in author entries. These are distinct from food exposures. The collection supplies no assigned ginger, rosemary, citrus, date, chard, salmon or barley preparation with the requested symptom comparisons.
