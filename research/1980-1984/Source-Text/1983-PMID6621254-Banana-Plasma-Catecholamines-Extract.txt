Title: The effect of ascorbic acid on plasma sulfate conjugated catecholamines after eating bananas
PMID: 6621254
DOI: 10.1016/0024-3205(83)90855-X
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6621254/
First published: 1983-10-10, Life Sciences 33(15).
Archive scope: Author abstract. Full original methods, arm denominators and numerical results remain unresolved.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

After banana consumption, the authors report increased conjugated plasma dopamine and norepinephrine in healthy men. The overall free plasma dopamine change was inconclusive, and free norepinephrine and epinephrine showed no significant change. A vitamin C exposure modified the conjugated measures in different directions. The study identifies peripheral chemistry, with no brain-tissue dopamine or apathy measurement.
