Title: Psychopharmacology in medical practice—the benefits and the risks
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1272608/
PMID: 7269559
PMCID: PMC1272608
Publication date: 1981-03
Citation: Sack RL, Shore JH. Western Journal of Medicine 134(3), 223–233.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 223–233.

This clinical review addresses psychiatric illness in general medical practice, diagnostic overlap and risks of psychotropic drugs. Pain and apathy appear in clinical discussions rather than an assigned Parkinson exposure comparison. Its discussion of dietary drug interactions does not establish consumption of dried dates or observation of a response to them.
