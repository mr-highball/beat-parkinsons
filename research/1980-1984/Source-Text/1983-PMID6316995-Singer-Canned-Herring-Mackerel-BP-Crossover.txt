Title: Lipid and blood pressure-lowering effect of mackerel diet in man
PMID: 6316995
DOI: 10.1016/0021-9150(83)90151-x
First published: 1983
Authors: Singer P, Jaeger W, Wirth M, Voigt S, Naumann E, Zimontkowski S, Hajdu I, Goedicke W
Journal: Atherosclerosis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6316995/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Isocaloric crossover feeding trial in 15 healthy volunteers consuming canned mackerel vs canned herring (2 cans/day in tomato pulp for 2 weeks each). Whereas higher-EPA mackerel lowered blood pressure, the canned herring comparator arm produced NO significant blood-pressure reduction (replicated in subsequent Singer 1985-1990 hypertensive/hyperlipidemic crossover trials, e.g., PMID:2189040). Argues against orthostatic BP-lowering risk from canned herring.
