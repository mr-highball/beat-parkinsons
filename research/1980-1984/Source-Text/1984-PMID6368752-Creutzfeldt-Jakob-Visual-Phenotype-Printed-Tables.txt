Title: A retrospective study of Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease in England and Wales 1970-79. I: Clinical features.
PMID: 6368752
PMCID: PMC1027681
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.47.2.134
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1027681/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 1984-02-01
Coverage: all seven original printed page images, pp. 134–140.

The 1984 retrospective series identifies 121 confirmed and 31 probable Creutzfeldt–Jakob cases, with possible diagnoses handled separately. In printed tables 4 and 5, visual hallucinations are recorded at presentation in 1% and during the subacute course in 17%. These are proportions of patients with the sign in a different disease and time window. The series supplies no dried fig exposure or Parkinson episode-rate estimate.
