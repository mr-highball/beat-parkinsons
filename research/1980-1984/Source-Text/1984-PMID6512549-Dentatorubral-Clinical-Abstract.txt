Title: Dentato-rubro-pallido-luysian atrophy: a clinico-pathological study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6512549/
PMID: 6512549
PMCID: PMC1028136
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.47.12.1288
Publication date: 1984-12
Coverage: Current factual extract from the author abstract. The historical article body is open.

The authors describe four clinicopathological cases of combined dentato-rubral and pallido-luysian degeneration and consider sixteen previously reported cases. The comparison concerns classification and differentiation of multisystem degeneration. The accessible abstract gives no food comparison or separate evidence about dried figs, dates, measured pain intensity or visual hallucination frequency in idiopathic Parkinson disease.
