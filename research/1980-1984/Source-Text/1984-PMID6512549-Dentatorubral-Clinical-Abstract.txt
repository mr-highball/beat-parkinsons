Title: Dentato-rubro-pallido-luysian atrophy: a clinico-pathological study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1028136/
PMID: 6512549
PMCID: PMC1028136
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.47.12.1288
Publication date: 1984-12
Citation: Iizuka R, Hirayama K, Maehara K. Journal of Neurology, Neurosurgery & Psychiatry 47(12), 1288–1298.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 1288–1298.

The authors described four autopsied cases and compared them with 16 earlier cases. On printed page 1294, the report recounts visual hallucinations during episodic delirium in a previously reported pseudo-Huntington phenotype. Those observations are retrospective narrative from another neurological condition. The article does not measure hallucination frequency or study figs or dates.
