Maria do Socorro Cordeiro Ferreira. Estudo farmacológico do Cymbopogon citratus (D.C.) Stapf. Universidade Federal do Ceará, Fortaleza, 1984.
Relevant printed pages: Methods 52–53 and Table 6 on p. 103. The cover and institutional repository identify the thesis and year.

Two groups of six male Hooded rats were compared. One group received an injected barbiturate called tionembutal. The other received intraperitoneal lemongrass essential oil in a cottonseed-oil dilution before barbiturate. The comparison does not state a matching vehicle pretreatment. Table 6 reports mean sleep duration of 3.05 hours in control versus 8.38 hours after pretreatment, with printed errors 0.31 and 2.49. The table gives p < 0.001 for duration. Induction time averaged 3.40 versus 3.60 minutes. The Methods print 50 mg/kg for the barbiturate while the Table 6 caption prints 25 mg/kg, leaving exact administration uncertain.

Drug-induced sleep duration differs from spontaneous sleep stages or dream behavior. No brewed leaf intake or Parkinson comparison was included.
