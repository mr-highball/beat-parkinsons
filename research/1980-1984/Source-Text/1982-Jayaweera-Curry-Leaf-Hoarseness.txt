D. M. A. Jayaweera. Medicinal Plants (Indigenous and Exotic) Used in Ceylon. Part V, Rutaceae–Zygophyllaceae. National Science Council of Sri Lanka, Colombo, 1982. Printed page 29, Rutaceae entry 14.
The Murraya koenigii species entry identifies curry-leaf and records boiled leaves as a traditional preparation for hoarseness and hiccups. It describes separate roasted-powder digestive uses and use of the leaf to flavor foods. The account supplies no individual doses, clinical measurements, Parkinson diagnosis or quantified voice response.
