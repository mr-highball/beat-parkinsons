Title: Immediate skin test reactions to spices
Authors: A Niinimäki and M Hannuksela
Date: 1981-10
Identifiers: PMID 7337198; DOI 10.1111/j.1398-9995.1981.tb01860.x
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7337198/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the primary abstract.

The investigators scratch-tested common spices in 1,120 atopic and 380 non-atopic patients. Paprika and curry frequently elicited positive skin reactions. Fourteen of 35 skin-positive participants reacted locally to spice applied to the oral mucosa, while 20 unselected participants had negative challenges with encapsulated spices. The abstract does not break down these results by paprika form or report speech intelligibility.
