# Lentil and kidney bean evidence

## British case reports after inadequately prepared red kidney beans

Noah and colleagues published seven reported incidents in 1980 involving 43 people who became ill after eating red kidney beans. The preparation history included soaking and insufficient heating. Reported outcomes were acute nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain and diarrhea. This is a general-population foodborne illness series. It contains no monitored Parkinson drooling or hallucination outcome. The later surveillance analysis spans these same outbreak years and should be treated as potentially overlapping.
