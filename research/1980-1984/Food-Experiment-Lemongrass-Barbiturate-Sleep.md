# Lemongrass oil and drug-induced sleep

## Injected lemongrass oil and barbiturate sleep in rats

The original dissertation reports longer barbiturate-induced sleep after injected lemongrass essential oil. Methods and Table 6 disagree on the barbiturate dose. The experiment measured induction and duration of a drug-induced state and supplies no REM behavior or brewed-tea outcome.
