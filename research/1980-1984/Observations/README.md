# Observations for 1980–1984

## Major observations

- The [human MPTP case series](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md) connected an accidental exposure with rapid-onset parkinsonism. It gave researchers a concrete example of a toxin-associated syndrome while leaving idiopathic disease causation open.
- Longer [levodopa follow-up](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) recorded continued motor benefit in a subset alongside wearing off, dyskinesia, and psychiatric adverse effects. The treated group’s survival remained hard to interpret because treatment tolerance and selection could influence who stayed in follow-up.
- The [levodopa meal study](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) separated two possible contributors to off periods. Meals affected oral drug absorption, and selected amino acids impaired clinical response even when intravenous delivery kept plasma levels stable.

## Minor observations

- The original food table places hallucinations in rows distinct from the dried fig group. The collapsed search display joins phrases across those rows. Participant and item-specific effects remain unverified. [Source record](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Personal-Reports/1982-Douglass-Raw-Food-Symptom-Table.txt)

- The UK case series identified acute gastrointestinal illness after inadequately prepared red kidney beans. The preceding bucket contains incidents later reviewed retrospectively. Clinical Parkinson symptom effects remain unmeasured in these reports. [Lentil and kidney bean evidence](../Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md)
- [Postural tasks](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md), [depression, and smell testing](../Nonmotor-and-Daily-Life.md) broadened the measured outcomes beyond rigidity and tremor.
- A [four-week therapy program](../Movement-and-Rehabilitation.md) yielded a null result on its measured tasks in a small crossover study with attrition.

## Comparison with [1975–1979](../../1975-1979/Observations/README.md)

- The earlier small dietary-protein challenge showed worse later-day motor scores with higher protein in patients with fluctuations. The 1984 levodopa study added absorption and transport measurements that helped explain the earlier clinical observation.
- Earlier bromocriptine comparisons documented symptomatic response and mixed control of fluctuations. The 1981 longer follow-up added tolerability and durability constraints. Earlier gait and speech studies established practical disability measures. This bucket added a short controlled rehabilitation study and further objective assessment of posture and nonmotor symptoms.

- A 1983 report tied acute gastrointestinal illness to unusually bitter zucchini and detected cucurbitacin in a bitter fruit sample. This precedes the larger non-edible squash surveillance series. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md#1983-bitter-zucchini-poisoning-and-fruit-chemistry)

- The 1982 crossover measured standing balance and a timed speech task under encapsulated safflower oil in Friedreich ataxia. Static balance was largely unchanged and the speech result was descriptive. This establishes an endpoint-specific early comparator record for subsequent oil studies. [Crossover clinical results](../Food-Experiment-Safflower-Oil-Endpoints.md#melancon-friedreich-ataxia-crossover-clinical-results)

## Collection gaps

- Look for contemporaneous feeding and swallowing studies, longer controlled rehabilitation studies, and international community cohorts. The nutrition records address medication response and supplementation. The archive has yet to verify a food-incidence study in this period.

- The publisher-deposited abstract preserves a stimulated muscle comparison after an oral peanut-oil vehicle. The contrast tests a toxicant, with no oil-free arm, oil grade or fatigue severity outcome. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Peanut-Oil-PubMed.md)

- The selected celery-allergy cohort included caraway skin testing. Sensitization records leave species-specific oral reactions and clinical speech outcomes unresolved. [Caraway follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Caraway-Replay.md)

- Paprika appeared in diagnostic skin or mucosal testing. The group results leave oral preparation and intelligibility effects open. [Paprika outcome follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Paprika-Gap-Replay.md)

- The 1982 Ceylon account names boiled leaves in a hoarseness indication and supplies no measured clinical response. [Boiled curry leaves and traditional hoarseness use](../Food-Experiment-Curry-Leaf-Voice-History.md)

- The printed clinical tables distinguish visual hallucinations at presentation and through the course of subacute Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease. [Full printed clinical summary](../Source-Text/1984-PMID6368752-Creutzfeldt-Jakob-Visual-Phenotype-Printed-Tables.txt).

### Peruvian Ficus accounts and plant-associated experiences

The original interviews distinguish ojé from the genus-level identity of renaco and record disagreement about visions, dreams and dizziness. The older clinical period and these ethnographic accounts use different outcome definitions. The later [East-Central Peru field study](../../2010-2014/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2011-PMID21295130-Oje-Plant-Teachers.txt) identifies several plants called ojé and contributes a separate attributed account. [Original Iquitos account](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/1984-PMID6492831-Luna-Ficus-Plant-Teachers.txt).

The [1980 muscle-twitch study](../Source-Text/1980-PMID7431032-Raw-Fig-Rest-Tremor-Original-Access.txt) applies a timed perturbation to analyze tremor. The [1979 rhythmic-force report](../../1975-1979/Source-Text/1979-PMID762588-Raw-Fig-Rest-Tremor-Original-Access.txt) measures the interaction between an external force and resting tremor. The original methods distinguish a perturbation response from any dietary exposure.
