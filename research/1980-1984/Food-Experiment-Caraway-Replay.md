# Caraway allergy in a celery-allergic case series

## Caraway hypersensitivity in a selected allergy cohort

Wüthrich and Hofer followed 31 people diagnosed with celery allergy in 1978–1982. The 1984 report describes follow-up skin and RAST testing and classifies caraway hypersensitivity as clinically relevant in 26% of that selected cohort. Skin positivity was more common than clinically relevant reactions for several spices. The accessible primary abstract gives no case-specific preparation, standardized voice outcome or Parkinson cohort.
