# Squash, mushroom, eggplant, and zucchini evidence

## 1983 Bitter zucchini poisoning and fruit chemistry

Fischer, Ferguson, and Metcalf documented Australian gastrointestinal poisonings linked to unusually bitter zucchini. In one five-person incident, two people collapsed and three had severe cramps and diarrhea. Laboratory analysis of a very bitter fruit sample detected cucurbitacin E-glycoside. The report describes a distinct acute toxic exposure and provides no Parkinson's cohort, REM sleep, or cognitive assessment.
