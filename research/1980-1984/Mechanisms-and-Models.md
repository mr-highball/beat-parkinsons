# Mechanisms and Models for 1980–1984

### 1983 — Human toxin-associated parkinsonism

Langston and colleagues reported four people who developed abrupt parkinsonism after an accidental exposure to a contaminated illicit drug. The authors linked the syndrome to MPTP and selective injury in the substantia nigra. This clinical series established a lead for toxin-associated parkinsonism. Its relevance to the cause of idiopathic Parkinson’s disease remained uncertain. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6823561/)
