# Boiled curry leaves in a 1982 Ceylon medicinal plant account

## Boiled leaves and traditional hoarseness use

In Part V of *Medicinal Plants (Indigenous and Exotic) Used in Ceylon*, Jayaweera's species entry names *Murraya koenigii* and records boiled leaves for hoarseness and hiccups on printed page 29. The publication was issued in Colombo by the National Science Council of Sri Lanka in 1982. The entry records traditional use without describing participants, voice measurements, duration, route details, or clinical response. It separately describes culinary use as seasoning.
