# Clinical and Diagnosis for 1980–1984

### 1982 — Postural movements and reflexes

Lakke assessed posture and movement in 41 people with Parkinson’s disease. Dynamic tasks such as getting up and turning responded differently to levodopa than static postural reflexes and classic motor signs. The clinical comparison raised the possibility of additional pathways involved in balance and movement. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6301734/)

### 1984 — Rochester incidence and mortality

Rajput and colleagues updated Rochester, Minnesota, case ascertainment for 1967–1979. Idiopathic Parkinson’s disease represented 86% of diagnosed parkinsonism, followed by drug-induced parkinsonism at 7%. Mortality was higher than in matched controls. The authors attributed the favorable survival in the treated subgroup to likely selection of healthier patients. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6333204/)
