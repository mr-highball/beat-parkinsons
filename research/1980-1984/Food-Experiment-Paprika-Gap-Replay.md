# Early paprika skin and oral exposure comparisons

## Paprika and mixed-spice skin and mucosal testing

Niinimäki and Hannuksela report scratch testing of 1,120 atopic and 380 non-atopic patients. Paprika and curry were among the most frequent skin positives. Five patients reported clinical symptoms after ingesting spices. A separate oral mucosal application provoked local reactions in 14 of 35 skin-positive people, whereas capsule challenges were negative in 20 unselected participants. The accessible abstract does not identify the oral-tested product or outcome by paprika preparation or name a speech symptom.
