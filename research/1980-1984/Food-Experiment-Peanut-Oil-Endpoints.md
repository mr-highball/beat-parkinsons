# Peanut oil oral challenge and voice outcomes

## Peanut and olive oil in a blinded allergy challenge

Taylor and colleagues reported that ten people with a history of systemic reactions to peanuts completed blinded crossover challenges with commercial peanut oil and olive oil. There were no allergic reactions to either oil at the tested capsule doses. The comparator controlled for presentation and taste. The report measures acute allergy and gives no acoustic, speech-intelligibility or Parkinson outcome. The protocol names commercially available peanut oil. Later comparison with the 1997 crude-oil challenge helps establish why the grade matters.
