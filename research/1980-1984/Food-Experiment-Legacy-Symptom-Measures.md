# Recovered original reports of Parkinson symptoms and clinical context

## Motor and salivary signs in a primate report

July 1983. Burns et al., PNAS 80(14), 4546–4550. The rhesus model reported motor signs, freezing and drooling. The Results on pp. 4547–4548 describe postural tremor and reduced movement. Table 2 on p. 4550 compares motor and other clinical signs. The five-page report provides no fall-frequency result. The model observations supply symptom context for the drooling, falls and rest-tremor screens, with no human culinary exposure.
