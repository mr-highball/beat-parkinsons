# Recovered Parkinson Clinical Context

## Kinesigenic foot dystonia as a presenting Parkinson feature

Lees and colleagues describe three people with Parkinson disease and foot dystonia during activities such as running or walking. One reported painful foot curling and later showed cogwheel rigidity. Another described painful twisting during jogging. The letter records symptom presentations and no food exposure or sage or date-fruit comparison. Its original body shares the printed page with two other letters.
