# History and Context for 1980–1984

### 1984 — France Parkinson and early local committees

France Parkinson’s institutional history records that Yves Agid founded the organization at the Salpêtrière in 1984. The association counted 916 members and launched its first six local committees that year. This retrospective record documents the growth of organized community support in France.
