# Clinical findings and diagnosis, 1925-1929

### 1925 — Bramwell examines the syndrome associated with epidemic encephalitis

Edwin Bramwell compared the clinical features of parkinsonism after epidemic encephalitis with those historically grouped under paralysis agitans. The lecture provides contemporary diagnostic context and clinical examples. Its diagnoses and sampling predate standardized criteria.

[Bramwell, Edinburgh Medical Journal, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5316813/)

### 1927 — Young compares 50 idiopathic and postencephalitic cases

A. W. Young clinically compared 50 patients with parkinsonian syndromes. Several ocular and tongue-movement findings occurred more often in the postencephalitic group, while the study found little difference in several general motor tests. Arm swing was reduced or absent in 48 cases. Classification relied on historical clinical criteria.

[Young, Journal of Neurology and Psychopathology, original paper](https://doi.org/10.1136/jnnp.s1-8.29.9)
