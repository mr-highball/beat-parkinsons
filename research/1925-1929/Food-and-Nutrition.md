# Food and nutrition, 1925-1929

### 1926 — Fructose challenge gives little evidence of liver dysfunction

E. Weston Hurst tested levulose (fructose) tolerance in 18 people with paralysis agitans as a probe of liver function. One had an abnormal rise in blood glucose after the test. Hurst also noted some hyperglycemia and judged that coincident vascular disease could explain it. The test was diagnostic and did not measure dietary effects on motor symptoms or disease risk.

[Hurst, Journal of Neurology and Psychopathology, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1068378/)
