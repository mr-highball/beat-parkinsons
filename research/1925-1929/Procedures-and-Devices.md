# Procedures and devices, 1925-1929

### 1927 — Ultraviolet exposure tried in postencephalitic parkinsonism

R. Fawcitt described ultraviolet treatment in a series of patients with encephalitis lethargica and parkinsonism. The paper describes improvements in gait, perceived well-being, or salivation in some patients, with persistent stiffness or tremor in others. The series used no controlled comparator and cannot establish an effect or safe use.

[Fawcitt, British Medical Journal, original report](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.1.3452.422)

### 1929 — Fever induced by baths in one reported case

P. S. Pouppirt reported induced fever baths for a patient with Parkinson’s syndrome. Discussion of the case describes an immediate increase in walking distance after treatment and uncertainty about heat exposure risks. It was an uncontrolled, potentially dangerous procedure, with no basis for inferring durable benefit.

[Pouppirt, California and Western Medicine, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1657160/)
