# Mechanisms and models, 1925-1929

### 1926 — McAlpine reports substantia nigra injury after epidemic encephalitis

D. McAlpine examined postmortem material from patients with the parkinsonian syndrome after epidemic encephalitis. He described substantia nigra cell loss and free pigment. In the nine cases discussed in his series, tremor was absent clinically. This pathology applies to a secondary group.

[McAlpine, Proceedings of the Royal Society of Medicine, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1948338/)
