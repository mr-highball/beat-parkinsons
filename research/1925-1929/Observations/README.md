# Observations for 1925-1929

## Major observations

- Young compared idiopathic and postencephalitic patients directly. There was considerable overlap in motor signs, alongside some differences in ocular and tongue findings. McAlpine reported nigral injury in postencephalitic cases, including a series without tremor. [Clinical sources](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md) · [Mechanisms](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md)

## Minor observations

- The remedy literature records symptomatic relief, toxicity, relapse, and several outcome measures that move independently. Hurst’s fructose challenge largely failed to support the proposed liver-function abnormality. [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) · [Food and nutrition](../Food-and-Nutrition.md)

## Comparison with 1920–1924

- Earlier papers established cases with secondary parkinsonism after encephalitis. Young compared their examination findings with idiopathic cases, and McAlpine added postmortem observations. Historical treatment trials began reporting more explicit limits, including relapse after nicotine. [Prior observations](../../1920-1924/Observations/README.md)

## Collection gaps

- Compare follow-up in the hyoscine, ultraviolet, and stramonium literature and look for patient and household accounts beyond institutions. No verified diet trial addressed symptoms or progression.
