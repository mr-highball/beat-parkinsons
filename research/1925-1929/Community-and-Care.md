# Community and care, 1925-1929

### 1929 — Ranchi Indian Mental Hospital records postencephalitic cases

J. E. Dhunjibhoy published cases from the Ranchi Indian Mental Hospital and private practice, with clinical photographs. He described facial immobility, altered gait, salivation, and other lasting symptoms in the parkinsonian cases. One history records years of care at home before behavioral changes preceded hospital admission. This selected institutional record illustrates caregiver burden without estimating its frequency.
