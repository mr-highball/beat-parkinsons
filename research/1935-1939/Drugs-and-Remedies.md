# Drugs and remedies

### 1938 — Bulgarian and English belladonna compared

Denis Hill published a comparison of Bulgarian and English belladonna for postencephalitic parkinsonism in The Lancet. The publisher record establishes the preparations and population. Verify outcomes in the article before attributing an advantage to either preparation. Belladonna root preparations were medicinal alkaloid exposures. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(00)41784-8)

### 1939 — Clinical monograph on belladonna root

A contemporaneous JAMA book notice described a monograph on belladonna-root treatment for postencephalitic parkinsonian syndromes, with separate chapters addressing paralysis agitans and postencephalitic disease. The notice documents circulation of the remedy and supplies an archival lead for its original case records. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1939.02800300076032)
