# Almond oil and historical clinical practice

## 1936 society proceedings and a cross-section search match

On printed pages 990–991, the discussion of laryngeal tuberculosis describes hoarseness, weakness of the voice, aphonia and voice rest. The almond-oil reference on page 993 occurs in a separate report on injections for hemorrhoids. Scans of all six printed pages were recovered and OCR-checked. The volume shares both search terms because it combines these discussions. The oil was an injection vehicle; vocal loudness after almond-oil ingestion was not evaluated.
