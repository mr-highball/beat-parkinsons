# Procedures and devices

### 1939 — Cortical excision in post-traumatic tremor

Bucy and Case reported that removal of a precentral cortical area abolished both resting and action tremor in a man with severe prior cerebral trauma. They invoked parallels with parkinsonian tremor and monkey experiments. The single case concerns post-traumatic tremor, and symptom abolition is an incomplete measure of functional benefit after destructive surgery. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneurpsyc.1939.02270160077007)
