Title: Over een merkwaardig geval van Tropische Spruw
Author: E. P. Snijders
Source: https://archive.nsgh.nl/gtni-1936-26
Publication date: 1936-06-30
Date basis: Journal issue 26. The title footnote records receipt on March 14, 1936.
Text coverage: Distilled from Clinical Lesson 69, especially the longitudinal descriptions and original figures. Physical PDF page 2 has the title. The two plotted time courses are on physical pages 5 and 11. Pages 14–17 show printed pages 1614–1617.

Snijders follows one adult with severe post-tropical sprue through repeated treatment phases. The author described a brief stabilization with strawberries in July 1924, a more substantial but temporary improvement in June–July 1925, and the largest sustained improvement in May–July 1926. During the 1926 period, reported weight rose from 49.8 kg on May 14 to 54.3 kg on June 3 and 61.5 kg on July 1. Stool frequency and consistency also improved. The diet included milk, applesauce and greens, and expanded to include other foods. Calcium treatment, fluctuating symptoms, seasonal timing and other regimens were part of the same single course. Improvement continued after strawberries ended. There is no matched control or isolated causal estimate.

The author separately reported marked intestinal irritation in some other strawberry recipients, with no denominator. In some cases clinicians used juice or stopped strawberries altogether. The detailed case also includes a sternal fracture while the patient was being transferred for care, in the setting of marked bone demineralization. It is documented as a transfer injury and supplies no measured fall outcome. The report studied sprue and did not identify a Parkinson cohort.
