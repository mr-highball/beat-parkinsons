Historical nasal spray discussion and voice attribution
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/003591573803101218
Coverage: factual extract from original proceedings, printed page 1410.

In the October 1938 Royal Society of Medicine proceedings, E. A. Peters asked about paraffin or an almond-oil spray base for dry noses and described mixed impressions from practice. A separate discussion participant, Douglas Guthrie, asked about nasal sinuses and voice. The discussion provides no patient count, treatment standardization, oral almond exposure or vocal loudness data. Full original meeting text shows that the almond oil and voice terms referred to separate discussion points.
