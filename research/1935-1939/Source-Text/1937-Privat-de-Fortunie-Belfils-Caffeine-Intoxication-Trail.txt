Title: Privat de Fortunié and Belfils on clinical presentations of cerebral caffeinism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1088099/?page=4
Bibliographic confirmation: https://www.persee.fr/doc/psy_0003-5033_1937_num_38_1_5550
Publication date: 1937-12
Date basis: Annales médico-psychologiques, year 95, part 2, issue 5, pp. 725–741, as indexed by the 1938 English digest and L’Année psychologique.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Evidence depth: Visually verified original English digest, printed p. 263. The 1937 French case material remains unverified.

The digest of a report on excessive coffee use mentions perceived fatigue lessening during intoxication, alongside insomnia and rapid tremor chiefly affecting the hands. It says tremor was observed in 60 percent of the cases without supplying the case denominator. The digest describes tremor sometimes persisting after coffee cessation, alongside a broader statement about improvement after withdrawal. It gives neither a defined rest-tremor phenotype nor a Parkinson diagnosis, preparation verification or quantified fatigue assessment.

The independent French bibliographic digest confirms the original authors, volume and page span. It belongs to the same case-report lineage. It cannot serve as an independent comparison or verify the precise fatigue finding.
