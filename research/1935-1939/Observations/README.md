# Observations for 1935–1939

## Major observations

The anatomical work subdivided the substantia nigra and the primate experiments linked specific cortical regions with cerebellar tremor. The latter supplied a rationale for an operation in a person with post-traumatic tremor. [Models](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md) · [Procedures](../Procedures-and-Devices.md)

## Minor observations

Belladonna reports narrowed the comparison from different alkaloids to extracts from different geographic sources. The monograph notice shows that clinicians continued distinguishing postencephalitic syndromes from paralysis agitans. [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md)

## Comparison with 1930–1934

[Previous observations](../../1930-1934/Observations/README.md)

The 1931 clinicopathological study linked patient histories to histology. Hassler’s anatomical map allowed more precise localization of nigral changes. The 1933 adjacent tract operations and the 1939 cortical case show how different movement disorders influenced surgical hypotheses. The individual trauma case leaves efficacy for Parkinson’s disease unresolved.

The 1938 sage tolerability account gives a urinary observation after a fresh-plant preparation. It supplies neither patient counts nor the endpoint definition needed to compare it with later Parkinson evidence.
