# Strawberry phases in the historical sprue case

The [1930 Slot report](../../1930-1934/Source-Text/1930-Slot-Fruit-Regimen-and-Sprue-Context.txt) connects the earlier strawberry practice with fruit and fruit juices more generally. Its detailed case only identifies fruit as a group. The [1936 Snijders report](../Source-Text/1936-Snijders-Strawberry-Periods-and-Tropical-Sprue.txt) distinguishes three strawberry phases over several years in one patient and gives dates, weights and bowel changes.

Repeated observations within one patient can show timing. Other diets, calcium treatment, longer exposure, seasonal changes and the relapsing illness limit attribution. The author also reports irritation and stopping strawberries in some patients. A fracture during transfer does not provide a fall endpoint.
