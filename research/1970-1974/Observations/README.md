# Observations for 1970–1974

## Major observations

Adding carbidopa reduced levodopa-related nausea and vomiting in a selected trial population. Bromocriptine showed symptomatic benefit in a small double-blind add-on study. [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md)

## Minor observations

Autonomic, mental and swallowing symptoms appeared as separate questions. A dietary protein study opened a new interaction inquiry, while family and smoking studies raised etiologic questions that their designs could only partly address. [Daily life](../Nonmotor-and-Daily-Life.md) · [Food and nutrition](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) · [Risk](../Risk-and-Prevention.md)

## Comparison with 1965–1969

[Previous observations](../../1965-1969/Observations/README.md)

The 1969 pyridoxine reversal finding was qualified by human metabolic measurements showing that carbidopa minimized the interaction. The earlier focus on establishing symptomatic benefit expanded to making chronic treatment tolerable and distinguishing severe motor fluctuations from mean motor response.

The 1968 controlled nutmeg publication is indexed without an abstract in PubMed. A 1970 clinical letter describes acute intoxication with attention lapses and prior LSD use. The original controlled methods and attention outcomes remain open. [1968 trial record](../../1965-1969/Food-Experiment-Nutmeg-Paprika.md#historical-controlled-nutmeg-experiment) · [1970 case](../Food-Experiment-Nutmeg-Paprika.md#attention-changes-in-a-nutmeg-intoxication-case)
