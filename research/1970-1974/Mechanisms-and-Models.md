# Mechanisms and models

### 1973 — Brain dopamine, pathology and clinical findings compared

Bernheimer and colleagues compared clinical findings, morphology and postmortem brain neurochemistry in Parkinson and Huntington syndromes. The work related marked striatal dopamine loss to parkinsonian pathology and signs. The tissue comparisons map changes seen at death and cannot alone show the sequence of disease initiation. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4272516/)
