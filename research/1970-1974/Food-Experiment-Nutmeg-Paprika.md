# Nutmeg and paprika evidence

## Attention changes in a nutmeg intoxication case

A March 1970 BMJ letter describes perceptual changes, poor concentration and lapses of attention after acute nutmeg intoxication in a 19-year-old. The report also records prior LSD use. The observations were descriptive and made during acute poisoning. [Primary case letter, p. 754](https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/1/5698/754.3.full.pdf)
