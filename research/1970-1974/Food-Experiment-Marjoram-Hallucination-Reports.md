# Marjoram Hallucination Reports

## Historical marjoram report without a documented case

Richard Evans Schultes described occasional reports of people using large amounts of sage or marjoram in attempts to induce hallucinations. The 1970 report gives no named source for this statement, authenticated species, confirmed ingestion, observed result, visual modality, individual history or incidence. Its following description of hallucinations concerns a different plant. This is historical documentation of alleged use, with no clinical comparison or verified marjoram-related event.
