# Food and nutrition

### 1972 — Banana and levodopa case letter

Garfinkel described a levodopa-treated patient who vomited dark material after swallowing a banana with the tablet. The author separately mixed powdered levodopa with banana, grape, and plum and observed darkening. These observations identify a food–drug compatibility lead. The letter gives no drug concentrations, comparative motor outcomes, or dyskinesia rating. [Primary letter, January 29, 1972, p. 312](https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/1/5795/311.3.full.pdf) · [2005 rat pharmacokinetics](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#banana-juice-and-levodopa-exposure-in-rats)

### 1973 — Early dietary protein and levodopa study

An original study in the Journal of the American Dietetic Association examined how dietary protein related to levodopa treatment. The indexed article and publisher preview establish the dietary intervention question. The full report is needed to extract design details, direction of effects and subgroup results. ### 1973 — Caffeine study in drug-induced parkinsonism

Arushanian, Stoliarov and Tolpyshev reported a Russian-language clinical and experimental study of caffeine. The title specifies drug-induced parkinsonism, and the subject index lists schizophrenia and cats. The citation gives volume 73, issue 5, pages 759–761. A Russian regional library lists this issue among its print holdings. No public full report or outcome estimate was located in the indexed journal and repository searches. [Primary citation, PMID 4718620](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4718620/) · [Tver Regional Library holdings](https://podpiska.tverlib.ru/zhurnal_nevrologii_i_psikhiatrii_im._s._s._korsakova)

### 1974 — Carbidopa modifies the pyridoxine–levodopa interaction

H. Mars studied 15 patients on long-term levodopa. Administered pyridoxine lowered plasma levodopa and increased synthesis of a dopamine metabolite. Carbidopa increased circulating levodopa and minimized the pyridoxine effect. The measured outcomes were metabolic, and the exposure was administered vitamin B6. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneur.1974.00490360020005)
