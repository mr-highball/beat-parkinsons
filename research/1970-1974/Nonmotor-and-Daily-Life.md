# Nonmotor symptoms and daily life

### 1971 — Autonomic function measured in parkinsonian syndromes

A clinical study evaluated autonomic responses in people with a parkinsonian syndrome, including standardized cardiovascular responses. The work made autonomic changes a measurable outcome. Diagnostic mix and treatment exposure matter when comparing with idiopathic Parkinson’s disease cohorts. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.4.5779.80)

### 1973 — Mental symptoms and parkinsonism

A British Medical Journal report addressed mental symptoms, including cognitive impairment, in a parkinsonian population. The article documents contemporary attention to cognitive outcomes and offers a primary-source lead for extracting methods and prevalence with the study’s own definitions. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4700322/)

### 1974 — One-site hypothesis for dysphagia

E. D. Palmer’s clinical correspondence attributed most parkinsonian dysphagia to dysfunction involving the cricopharyngeus. This proposed anatomical explanation was challenged by observations in the following period. The short correspondence did not establish how frequently different swallowing abnormalities occurred. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1974.03230480065037)
