# Drugs and remedies

### 1974 — Carbidopa trial in people with levodopa-related nausea and vomiting

Markham, Diamond and Treciokas studied 20 levodopa responders with longstanding nausea and vomiting. In a double-blind trial followed by combination treatment for all participants, adding carbidopa reduced nausea and vomiting, and most participants had some additional symptomatic improvement. The cohort was selected for levodopa response and gastrointestinal side effects. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneur.1974.00490380076010)

### 1974 — Bromocriptine as add-on therapy

Calne and colleagues reported symptomatic benefit with the dopamine receptor agonist bromocriptine in a double-blind study of 20 people with idiopathic parkinsonism who were already receiving conventional treatment including levodopa. This was a small add-on trial and later studies directly compared bromocriptine with levodopa. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.4.5942.442)

### 1974 — Severe on–off fluctuations during levodopa treatment

A clinical report examined five people with severe on–off fluctuations during levodopa therapy and assessed additional drug combinations. The case series illustrates that fluctuations were already a distinct outcome in chronic therapy, with variability and severe episodes warranting focused study. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.24.5.431)
