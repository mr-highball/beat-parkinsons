Title: Combined use of clofibrate and cholestyramine or DEAE sephadex in hypercholesterolaemia

PMCID: PMC1800097

Publication date: 1971-07-03
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: NLM original journal scans, printed pages 25-27. The next article on page 27 was excluded. Factual extract.

Study 1 placed three groups of five patients with hypercholesterolaemia on a common modified diet, including corn oil for cooking, before placebo or one of two resin regimens. Two participants in the cholestyramine group withdrew because of constipation or stomach acidity. The report also describes occasional constipation among other participants. It does not isolate the oil, identify its refinement, or give a Parkinson constipation comparison. Study 2 used a separate medication and resin comparison without the shared corn-oil diet assignment.
