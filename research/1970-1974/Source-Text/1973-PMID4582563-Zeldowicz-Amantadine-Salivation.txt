Title: Long-term therapy of Parkinson's disease with amantadine, alone and combined with levodopa
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1946937/
PMID: 4582563
PMCID: PMC1946937
Citation: Zeldowicz LR, Huberman J. Canadian Medical Association Journal. 1973 Oct 6. 109:588–593.
Coverage: Complete six-page PMC scan with OCR. Part I, assessment methods and Tables II–III, p. 589. Distilled factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

The investigators scored salivation separately on an ordinal neurological rating, alongside tremor, rigidity and other signs. A selected group of 19 patients who had already responded to amantadine continued treatment for a mean of 21 months. The report describes control of salivation in that selected group, which limits inference for all treated patients. Its combined-treatment and withdrawal comparisons also involve medicinal exposures. Feeding is scored as an activity of daily living. The paper does not describe a test of raw figs.
