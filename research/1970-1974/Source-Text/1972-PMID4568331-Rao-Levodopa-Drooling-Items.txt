Six patients with parkinsonism took levodopa with or without L-alpha methyldopahydrazine in a double-blind crossover. Table 1 defines a separate 0–4 ordinal drooling item. With versus without the add-on, paired patient mean scores were 0 versus 0, 2 versus 2, 0 versus 0, 0.5 versus 0.25, 1.75 versus 2, and 2 versus 2. One patient scored lower and one higher on the add-on, with four unchanged. The paper supplies no separate statistical estimate for that item. Nausea and blood pressure are distinct outcomes. The study compares medication regimens without a fig exposure.
