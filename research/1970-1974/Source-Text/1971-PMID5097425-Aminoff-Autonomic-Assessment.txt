Title: Assessment of Autonomic Function in Patients with a Parkinsonian Syndrome
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1799334/
PMID: 5097425
PMCID: PMC1799334
DOI: 10.1136/bmj.4.5779.80
Publication date: 1971-10-09
Date basis: Printed BMJ issue date.
Coverage: Paraphrased original page scans 80–84, including Tables I–VII. Page 84 contains the end of the references and the start of a separate article.

Eleven people with parkinsonism and suspected autonomic dysfunction underwent a physiological assessment. Three had the additional features of the syndrome the authors called Shy–Drager. The study compared vasomotor, sudomotor, pilomotor, parasympathetic and bladder measures. Its introduction quotes the historical description of drooling. Table I records binary ptyalism in one of the eleven cases, in the group with additional autonomic features. No graded drooling, salivary flow or dietary comparison was reported. Sweating and gastric acid measurements were separate outcomes.
