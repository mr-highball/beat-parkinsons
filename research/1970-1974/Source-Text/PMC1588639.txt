Text coverage: Publisher PDF, original pages 633-637, with NLM scans available. Factual extract.

The randomized study compared maintenance fluphenazine decanoate and placebo injections among 81 chronic schizophrenia outpatients. The article reports no significant difference in treatment needed for depression. Among nonrelapsing patients, two of 12 on placebo and one of 33 on active drug received an antidepressant. This is treatment use rather than depression severity. A separate reference mentions sesame oil in a fluphenazine enanthate preparation. The assigned study comparison does not test cold-pressed sesame oil intake or Parkinson depression.
