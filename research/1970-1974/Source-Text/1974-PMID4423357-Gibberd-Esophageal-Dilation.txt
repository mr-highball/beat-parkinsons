Title: Oesophageal dilatation in Parkinson's disease

Coverage: Complete three-page PMC scan with OCR. Introduction, Patients and Methods, Results and Figure 1, pp. 938–939. Distilled factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

In a series of 37 patients described as having parkinsonism, six had gross esophageal dilation on barium studies. The mean measurable diameter was 3.11 cm in the patient group versus 2.24 cm in controls (reported p < .01). Some patient films could not supply an adequate diameter. The introduction discusses several possible contributors to drooling, including swallowing and lip control. Esophageal diameter was the measured comparison. The report gives no raw-fig exposure or separate measured estimate of drooling severity.
