Title: Treatment of chronic hepatic encephalopathy with levodopa
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1412974/
PMID: 4430473
PMCID: PMC1412974
DOI: 10.1136/gut.15.7.555
Publication date: 1974-07
Citation: Lunzer M, James IM, Weinman J, Sherlock S. Gut 15(7), 555–561.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 555–561.

Six patients had chronic hepatic encephalopathy and had previously responded poorly to conventional measures, including dietary protein restriction. Investigators maintained prior therapy and introduced levodopa. Gastrointestinal adverse effects took three participants out before the double-blind levodopa–lactose phase. Clinicians described resting or intention tremor at baseline and recorded improvement in some participants alongside changes in coordination and speed-based tasks. The intervention was levodopa and the participants had hepatic encephalopathy. The term Plum is a cited surname. There was no fig or plum intervention and no dyskinesia outcome attributed to a food.
