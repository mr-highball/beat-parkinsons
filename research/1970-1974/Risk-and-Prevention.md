# Risk and prevention

### 1971 — Smoking history and Parkinson’s disease association

An American Journal of Epidemiology study investigated the inverse association between smoking history and Parkinson’s disease and discussed possible explanations. This historical observational result can generate hypotheses. It cannot establish protection or justify tobacco use. [Source](https://academic.oup.com/aje/article-abstract/94/1/16/88343)

### 1973 — Family data and a multifactorial interpretation

A Rochester analysis used records and family data on patients with parkinsonism to investigate familial aggregation. The authors interpreted their observations as compatible with multifactorial etiology. The historical grouping and family ascertainment limit attribution to specific genes or exposures. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4716218/)
