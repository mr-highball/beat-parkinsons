# Macadamia oil and gastric emptying

## Intragastric macadamia oil comparator in healthy men

Little and colleagues first published the journal report online July 3, 2007. Nine healthy men received an intragastric macadamia oil emulsion, an approximately isocaloric free oleic acid emulsion and a milk-protein control on separate visits. Gastric emptying of the free fatty acid preparation was slower than that of the macadamia oil preparation. The oil served as a triglyceride comparator. Scintigraphy, gut hormones, appetite and subsequent food intake were studied, with no clinical constipation or Parkinson endpoint. The author's thesis contains the journal experiment and the associated abstract is treated as the same study.
