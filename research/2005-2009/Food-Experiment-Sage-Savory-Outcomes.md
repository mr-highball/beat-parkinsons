# Sage and summer savory symptom studies

## Common sage registration in mild Alzheimer disease

NCT00110552 was first posted May 11, 2005. The completed Oregon Health and Science University study lists oral sage capsules, cognitive function at eight weeks, and secondary stress and electrophysiology outcomes. The description calls the intervention an extract. The register lists 111 participants and no posted results. A cited 2003 dementia study predates this registration and cannot be its results. The listed outcomes give no explicit apathy scale. [Official study record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00110552)

## Sage and hot flash interference registration

NCT00541788 was first posted October 10, 2007. The completed phase 1 study enrolled ten men receiving androgen deprivation for prostate cancer. It registers a single group, side-effect diaries and quality of life measured by the hot flash-related daily interference scale. The registry has no results module. The investigator article gives the scale contents and aggregate findings. [Official study record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00541788) · [2011 primary results](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Sage-Savory-Outcomes.md#sage-extract-and-hot-flash-interference-in-prostate-cancer)
