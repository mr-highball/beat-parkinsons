# Facial expression, falls, voice and related food outcomes

## A published balance experiment indexed under basil

Freeman et al. used lavender oil, black pepper oil and distilled-water sham in 17 older adults. The original body explicitly excluded Parkinson disease. Basil appears in the paper's keyword list but no basil exposure is described. Sway measured with other inhaled odors gives no estimate for consuming fresh basil.
