# Ground sumac and symptom outcomes

## 2009 Ground sumac aroma and flavor ratings

Bahar and Altug characterized volatile components and sensory flavor ratings for ground *Rhus coriaria* and two extracts. Assessors identified shared aroma and flavor characteristics across the preparations. The outcome describes perception of sumac as a stimulus. It does not measure an eater's olfactory function before and after consumption or include participants with Parkinson disease. [Primary article, first published March 26](https://doi.org/10.1080/10942910701799249)
