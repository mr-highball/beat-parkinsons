# Fennel preparations and fatigue outcomes

## Fennel extract and fatigue in a separate dysmenorrhea trial

Torkzahrani et al. report 46 students assigned fennel extract and 44 assigned identical placebo in a double-blind comparison over two menstrual cycles. Systemic symptoms declined in both groups and the abstract gives fatigue p=.058 between groups. The abstract gives no arm-level fatigue values or direction. The Persian article requires subscription at this route. The 2003 crossover used oil with different participants and methods.
