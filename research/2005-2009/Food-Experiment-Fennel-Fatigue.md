# Fennel preparations and fatigue outcomes

## Fennel extract and fatigue in a separate dysmenorrhea trial

Torkzahrani et al. report 46 students assigned fennel extract and 44 assigned identical placebo in a double-blind comparison over two menstrual cycles. Systemic symptoms declined in both groups and the abstract gives fatigue p=.058 between groups. The abstract gives no arm-level fatigue values or direction. The Persian article requires subscription at this route. The 2003 crossover used oil with different participants and methods. They do not provide the original fatigue table or establish a between-arm fatigue direction. The 2017 IAPCON member adds separately documented mixed-extract cancer-pain context.
