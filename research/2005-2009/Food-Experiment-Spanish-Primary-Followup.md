# Spanish-language dietary research follow-up

## Broad bean germination and L-DOPA content

Carmen Goyoaga Jorba’s Universidad Complutense dissertation was defended November 10, 2005 and circulated in the university’s thesis collection in the 2005–2009 period. It measured compounds in Alameda and Brocal broad beans during germination. L-DOPA accumulated in the embryonic axis as the seedlings grew. The whole-seedling total of the favism-associated compounds vicine and convicine changed little despite redistribution between tissues. A separate feeding experiment compared germinated and ungerminated beans in growing rats and measured nutritional utilization. The 2008 journal report from the same research group identified the highest measured embryonic-axis L-DOPA concentration in the Brocal variety six days after imbibition. The reports characterize plant composition and rat nutrition. They give no Parkinson symptom outcomes or evidence that boiled and germinated beans deliver the same exposure. ## 2009 Havana proposal for Parkinson nutrition care

Marcos Plasencia and Padrón Sánchez proposed an interdisciplinary Parkinson nutrition protocol as Annex 2 to a November 9, 2009 course at the Instituto de Neurología y Neurocirugía in Havana. The supplement presents nutritional assessment, individualized food texture changes, and practical sessions on chewing, swallowing, and meal preparation. The annex gives recommendations and no implementation results for a patient cohort. It cites the Japanese dietary survey that measured potatoes and fluid consumption without examining the effect of a particular potato preparation.
