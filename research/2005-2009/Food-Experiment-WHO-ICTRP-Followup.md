# Botanical and nutritional registrations and outcomes, 2005–2009

## Yokukansan registrations and early Parkinson report

UMIN000001511 was first disclosed November 20, 2008. It enrolled people with probable Lewy body dementia, with Parkinson’s disease dementia eligible, for four weeks of open-label yokukansan and Neuropsychiatric Inventory assessment. A distinct Phase IV record, jRCT1080220598, gives August 27, 2008 as its first public date and plans assessment at 4, 8 and 12 weeks in people with Parkinson’s and neuropsychiatric symptoms, with a target of 76. The 2011 and 2012 publications explicitly identify the UMIN registration. The primary jRCT record leaves its results and publication fields blank. [UMIN registration](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000001811) · [jRCT registration](https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/latest-detail/jRCT1080220598) · [Later reports](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-WHO-ICTRP-Followup.md#yokukansan-in-lewy-body-dementia)

Kawanabe et al. first published a 14-person report on December 3, 2009. Seven participants had Parkinson’s and seven had Parkinson’s disease dementia. The authors reported improvement after four weeks of yokukansan, especially in hallucination incidence and duration. The sample, follow-up and reporting do not identify it as the Phase IV registry cohort. The intervention was a multicomponent formula. [Primary report, PMID 19948198](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19948198/)

## Vitamin D3 supplementation registration

UMIN000001841 was first disclosed April 2, 2009. It planned a double-blind trial of vitamin D3 against placebo for one year, with neurological and quality-of-life outcomes in people with Parkinson’s. The registry links the 2013 Suzuki et al. report. [Primary registration](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000002217) · [2013 results](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-WHO-ICTRP-Followup.md#vitamin-d3-and-parkinson-disability-scales)
