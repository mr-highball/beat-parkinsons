# Rosemary Voice Registry

## Aromatic essential oil mixture registered for acute hoarseness

First posted February 8, 2008. Placebo-controlled spray for viral laryngitis or tracheitis specifies cough or hoarseness after 20 minutes and a three-day symptoms-and-signs composite. The linked primary publication explicitly identifies rosemary essential oil among five constituents. The registration gives no isolated culinary rosemary or acoustic loudness outcome.
