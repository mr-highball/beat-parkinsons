# Capsaicin and tongue motor performance under experimental pain

## Topical tongue capsaicin and tongue-protrusion performance

Boudreau and colleagues used a randomized two-session crossover in nine healthy adults. Capsaicin cream applied to the tongue was compared with vehicle before training on a tongue-protrusion task. Overall performance was lower in the capsaicin session, and the observed training-associated enhancement of tongue corticomotor responses occurred in the vehicle session. These measures quantify an experimental tongue movement, with no speech sample, intelligibility rating or smoked paprika ingestion.
