# Seafood Exposure Speech

## Original Minamata survey and later cohort reuse

First published online July 20, 2007 and in an issue in 2008. The original survey studied 197 residents historically exposed through contaminated fish and shellfish. The accessible original discusses sensory and ataxia findings. Its full neurological tables, exact dysarthria counts and food species or preparation remain unverified. The 2023 reanalysis explicitly reuses these 197 exposed participants and selects 130 controls from the earlier control-area population, changing some eligibility decisions. Later dysarthria percentages should be cited to the later analysis. [2023 primary reanalysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10748001/).
