# Shrimp and clinical case context

## 2005 Steamed shrimp before probable autoimmune hepatitis

In the 2005 Society of General Internal Medicine abstract collection (PMID 15842728, PMCID PMC1490295), Bohning, Taqui and Shishodia report a 25-year-old woman who ate steamed shrimp two weeks before onset of nausea, vomiting and jaundice. The abstract reports subsequent probable autoimmune hepatitis and transplantation, and describes the shrimp history as a red herring. It does not report hallucinations or an attribution to eating shrimp. [Original conference PDF](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC1490295.1/PMC1490295.1.pdf), case tracking 134821.
