# Pomegranate rinse and salivary biochemistry

## Mouth rinse in healthy young adults

Robert A. DiSilvestro, Daniel J. DiSilvestro, David J. DiSilvestro. Pomegranate extract mouth rinsing effects on saliva measures relevant to gingivitis risk. Phytother Res. 2009. DOI `10.1002/ptr.2759`, PMID 19170139. The 32-person comparison used a pomegranate extract rinse or cornstarch control three times daily for four weeks. Reported changes included lower total protein and activities of aspartate aminotransferase and alpha-glucosidase, and higher ceruloplasmin activity. The radical-scavenging comparison depended on the statistical method. These are salivary biochemical measures. The source does not give a direction for clinically rated drooling or for eating raw arils in people with Parkinson disease.
