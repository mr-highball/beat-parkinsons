# Chervil and lovage leaf outcomes

## 2009 Lovage root in a cholinesterase screen

Gholamhoseinian and colleagues screened methanol extracts against acetylcholinesterase in vitro. Table 1 identifies *Levisticum officinale* **roots**. Table 2 reports an IC50 of 0.5 mg/mL, and kinetic analysis describes noncompetitive inhibition. The paper studied a root extract against isolated enzyme without measuring attention or another clinical symptom. [Local primary text, Tables 1–2](Source-Text/PMC3093628.txt)
