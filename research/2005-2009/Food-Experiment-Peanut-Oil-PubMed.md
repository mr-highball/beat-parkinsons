# Peanut oil comparator in isolated muscle fatigue resistance

## Genioglossus fatigue resistance in an injected hormone study

Liu and colleagues randomized 24 aged male rats to testosterone, oestrogen or sterile peanut oil control injections over four weeks. Under controlled nerve stimulation, testosterone lowered genioglossus fatigue resistance against the oil comparator. The oestrogen comparison was inconclusive. This measure is endurance of an experimentally stimulated tongue muscle. It does not estimate experienced fatigue severity, a dietary oil effect or response in Parkinson disease. Peanut oil was injected and its processing grade was unspecified. The report appeared online in December 2007 before the 2008 issue. [Primary abstract and first release record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18070614/)
