# Veterinary Herbal Source Context

## Veterinary herbal chapter and ingredient outcome boundaries

The veterinary chapter describes traditional preparations and cites laboratory and clinical studies from different species and indications. Examples include aqueous parsley seed extract in rat colon perfusion, mixed ginger and ginkgo extracts in rat behavior studies, and topical sunflower oil as a comparator in a dementia agitation trial. The tarragon diabetes citation tested seven individual plant diets and a separate mixture. The chapter also uses black cumin for Nigella sativa and describes clove oil as an anesthetic for fish. These entries do not report one shared dietary intervention or Parkinson clinical outcome.
