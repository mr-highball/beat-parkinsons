# Isolated piperine and rodent memory

## Oral isolated piperine in an AF64A rat model

Published online December 23, 2009, with a March 2010 journal issue. PMID
20034530. DOI 10.1016/j.fct.2009.12.009. The original Methods and Results
describe male Wistar rats in groups of eight. Rats received isolated oral
piperine at 5, 10, or 20 mg/kg for two weeks before and one week after
bilateral intracerebroventricular AF64A. The study included a vehicle group and a
donepezil comparison.

Figures 1 and 2 and Results 3.1 describe shorter Morris water maze escape
latency and more time in the target area during the 24-hour retention test.
The authors report p<0.001 for the piperine groups against the AF64A vehicle
group. The paper presents means graphically. The outcomes concern spatial
learning and memory after an experimental cholinergic injury, with no measure
of sustained attention or test of cracked culinary black pepper. The study
used a rat model of Alzheimer-like impairment.
