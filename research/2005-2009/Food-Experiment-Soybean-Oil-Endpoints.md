# Soybean oil comparator and clinical endpoints

## Registered DHA trial in Alzheimer disease

NCT00440050 first posted on 26 February 2007. It assigned 402 people with Alzheimer disease to algal DHA or placebo capsules for 18 months. The original registry gives the placebo capsule schedule. The 2010 paper describes the placebo capsules as containing corn or soy oil and gives no formulation-specific counts.
