# Rosemary aromatherapy in dementia

## Dementia mixed-aroma psychosis composite

Jimbo and colleagues reported a sequential control, aromatherapy and washout study involving 28 people with dementia in the December 2009 issue. Morning aroma used rosemary and lemon oils. Evening aroma used lavender and orange oils. The GBSS-J-D domain is labelled differently in methods and results. Table 4 gives means of 8.71 before aromatherapy and 7.18 afterward, with P = .499 across four measurement times. It does not give a pairwise estimate of visual hallucination frequency, assess dietary fresh rosemary or identify a Parkinson subgroup.
