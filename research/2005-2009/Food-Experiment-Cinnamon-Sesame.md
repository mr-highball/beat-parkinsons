# Cinnamon and sesame oil evidence

## Cinnamon-containing Kampo formula and overactive bladder

Ogushi and Takahashi reported a six-week single-group study in 30 men with overactive bladder in December 2007. Overactive Bladder Symptom Score decreased from 7.5 to 4.9, and the authors reported a reduction in urgency and voiding frequency. Flow and post-void residual comparisons were inconclusive. Three patients had mild adverse effects including urinary frequency, diarrhea or nausea. The intervention was Gosha-jinki-gan, a multi-ingredient formula whose manufacturer lists cinnamon bark. The design gives no isolated cinnamon effect. ## Early Chinese odor test modifications

First online December 7, 2007, ahead of its 2008 issue. Shu and Yuan describe adapting five odors to test smell identification in Asia. The accessible original abstract does not name individual replacement scents or their results. The later Dong Methods attribute sesame and soy sauce substitutions to this paper. That is a methodological citation, and the original item table and concentrations are still unverified.
