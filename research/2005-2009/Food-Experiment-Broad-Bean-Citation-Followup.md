# 2009 Raguthu correspondence on fava beans

The National Library of Medicine indexes Raguthu and colleagues’ letter as a case report (PMID 19678834) and gives August 12, 2009 as the electronic publication date. The Wiley page displays September 14, 2009 as first publication and labels the issue October 2009. Its publicly visible references include earlier work on glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency and favism, Guggenheim’s chemical isolation, the Apaydin broad bean series, and Ladha’s withdrawal case. The letter has no public abstract, and the case body was unavailable through the publisher, library indexes and author-title searches. The patient, exposure, clinical event, and any overlap with the Ladha case remain unverified.
