# Nonmotor and Daily Life

### 2007 — International nonmotor symptom screening

A study using the 30-item NMSQuest in 545 patients documented a wide range of concerns patients can report directly, including sleep and autonomic symptoms. The questionnaire screens for issues and follow-up evaluation establishes their causes. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17546669/)

### 2007–2008 — Aspiration and airway sensation in a referred parkinsonism cohort

Diesener presented a 50-person endoscopic swallowing series at Zihlschlacht in February 2007. The June 2008 Rehabilitation Schweiz abstract and poster identify 29 participants with idiopathic Parkinson’s and 21 with atypical parkinsonism. Across the referred cohort, 17 had aspiration with a cough response and nine aspirated without a sufficient cough response. Laryngeal and tracheal hyposensitivity was more frequent in those with aspiration, with the highest proportion among the nine with insufficient cough. These pooled observations describe patients referred for assessment and provide clinical context for the later capsaicin stimulation research. The cohort report gives no dietary intervention estimate. [2007 investigator slides](https://www.yumpu.com/de/document/view/8019950/dysphagie-bei-parkinson-syndrom-dysphagie-netzwerk-sudwest) · [2008 investigator poster reproduced in the 2024 slides, pp. 10–11](https://maik-online.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/2024_MAIK_Kongress_Vortrag_Diesener.pdf#page=10) · [Later capsaicin study](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#randomized-capsaicin-aerosol-and-airway-clearance)

### 2008 — Timed swallowing revealed problems that self-report missed

Miller and colleagues measured water swallowing in 137 people with Parkinson’s during an off-medication assessment. Measured swallowing speed was often below published norms and correlated poorly with self-reported difficulty. The test assessed drinking performance, and the authors called for direct assessment of aspiration. The journal issue appeared in 2009. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19028764/)

### 2008 — Small randomized trial of antidepressants for Parkinson’s depression

Menza and colleagues randomized 52 people with Parkinson’s and depression to nortriptyline, controlled-release paroxetine, or placebo. At eight weeks, nortriptyline improved clinician-rated depression more than placebo. The trial did not detect an advantage for paroxetine. The small, short trial leaves uncertainty about comparative efficacy, particularly across formulations and patient groups. The journal issue appeared in 2009. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19092112/)

### 2009 — Longitudinal risk after isolated REM sleep behavior disorder

A specialist cohort of 93 people with isolated REM sleep behavior disorder tracked subsequent parkinsonism or dementia. The probability increased over years. Estimates encompass several neurodegenerative outcomes and reflect a referred cohort. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19109537/)
