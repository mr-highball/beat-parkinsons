# Oats and carrot evidence

## 2007 Contaminated carrot and millet dish in a food poisoning outbreak

Fretz and colleagues investigated an Austrian canteen outbreak after a carrot-and-millet dish. Seven of eight people who ate the dish reported illness. One reported auditory hallucinations about 12 hours after eating. Investigators traced the toxic exposure to Jimson weed seeds in the millet supply. The dish contained carrot, but the evidence identifies a contaminant and does not establish a raw-carrot effect on visual hallucinations in Parkinson's. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijid.2007.03.003) · [Primary text copy](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/6319294_Food_poisoning_due_to_Jimson_weed_mimicking_Bacillus_cereus_food_intoxication_in_Austria_2006)

## 2008 Raw carrot aroma and sensory ratings

Kreutzmann and colleagues measured volatiles and trained-panel ratings across raw carrot genotypes. The panel evaluated the shredded carrot samples for aroma and flavor attributes. Those ratings measure the food stimulus during testing and do not assess changes in Parkinson-related smell impairment. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2621.2007.01662.x)

## Rolled oats as a food comparator in chronic insomnia

Hudson and colleagues reported a 2005 three-arm randomized trial in chronic primary insomnia with 57 assigned and 49 completing. The control food provided 50 g of rolled oats for one week, with 17 assigned and 15 completing. A separate active tryptophan preparation also contained oats. Table I gives 359 to 376 diary minutes of sleep and 74.8% to 76.9% sleep efficiency in the oat arm from baseline to treatment week. Wake time moved from 122 to 114 minutes. The oat-arm changes were inconclusive, and the week-by-condition interactions were inconclusive across the reported sleep variables. Participants had no Parkinson diagnosis and there was no randomized no-oat control. [Original report and Table I](https://www.academia.edu/87973134/Protein_source_tryptophan_versus_pharmaceutical_grade_tryptophan_as_an_efficacious_treatment_for_chronic_insomnia) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/10284150500069561) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2005-Hudson-Rolled-Oats-Insomnia-Extract.txt)
