# Corn Oil Depression

## Early coronary disease depression trial registration

The registered study concerns major depression in coronary heart disease, with sertraline provided in both randomized groups. The current registry lists 122 enrollees, week-10 Beck Depression Inventory change and omega-3 versus placebo capsules. The original 2009 publication identifies the placebo as corn oil; the registry first posted its results in 2012. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00116857) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/NCT00116857-Corn-Oil-Sertraline.txt)

## Corn oil placebo in the earlier coronary disease sertraline trial

Carney and colleagues randomized 122 coronary disease patients with major depression after a sertraline run-in. Sixty received 2 g/day corn oil and 62 received EPA plus DHA, both with sertraline 50 mg/day for 10 weeks. The final article reports unadjusted week-10 BDI-II means 14.8 versus 16.1 and baseline-adjusted corn minus omega group difference −1.26 (95% CI −4.48 to 1.97, p=.44). The adjusted Hamilton difference was +0.14 (−2.15 to 2.44, p=.90). The accessible author manuscript instead gives unadjusted BDI-II means 15.0 and 15.9. Use the final publication for that comparison. The study has no oil-free control, verified refining grade or Parkinson cohort. [Author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3477788/) [Original source](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/184742) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/PMID19843899-Corn-Oil-Sertraline.txt)

## Adolescent depression imaging substudy registration

The registration describes a ten-week adolescent major depression neuroimaging study of omega-3 compared with matched corn-oil capsules. Its participant-flow fields specify that participants for the imaging work came from a separate protocol, GCO 12-1321, and list 16 enrollees, seven corn and nine omega-3. The later clinical publication reports 51 randomized and names the same registry. The sources leave the relationship between the 16 registered participants and the full clinical analysis unresolved. Results first posted May 24, 2017. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00962598) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/NCT00962598-Corn-Oil-Imaging.txt)
