# Chicken soup and measured fatigue

## Reconstituted chicken soup and ordinary fatigue ratings

Midoh and Noguchi's study was first available on J-STAGE on February 1, 2009. Thirty healthy adults took each of two soup preparations in a randomized crossover. The active soup used freeze-dried extract from heated whole chickens reconstituted in hot water. Table 3 gives fatigue T-score changes of −0.5 ± 0.9 with chicken soup and +0.1 ± 0.8 with placebo, as means and standard errors (p = .61). The tension–anxiety subscale showed a separate favorable comparison. This was oral soup without an imposed fatigue task. No meat was assigned and Parkinson fatigue was outside the study.
