# Kefir Fatigue

## Kefir and colorectal chemotherapy quality of life pilot

Can and colleagues randomized 40 patients receiving colorectal cancer treatment, 20 to kefir and 20 to control. The authors describe no clear overall between-arm quality-of-life benefit, less sleep disturbance and more gastrointestinal complaints in kefir recipients. The available primary materials did not expose fatigue-specific rows, product flavor or arm-level fatigue estimates. Full article pages beyond the publisher preview remain open.
