# Dietary trial follow-up from WHO ClinicalTrials.gov records, 2005–2009

## Fudan herbal mixture trial registration

NCT00656253 first posted on April 11, 2008 and later reported 158 participants and completion in 2011. It compared a multicomponent medicinal mixture listing roucongrong and heshouwu with placebo. The public record includes levodopa-equivalent dose and UPDRS among the outcomes, and does not give the full ingredient composition. The archived record had no posted results. An identifier search and sponsor, mixture, and enrollment search did not locate a linked primary outcome. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00656253) [Local registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT00656253.txt)

## VIUSID and ALZER trial registration

NCT01016470 first posted on November 19, 2009. It lists combined VIUSID and ALZER supplements versus matching placebo in 100 people with early Parkinson disease. The registration names motor UPDRS as the primary outcome. The 2012 exact-NCT report instead presents Parkinson Fatigue Scale as primary and identifies the two multicomponent formulations. The archived registry has no results table. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01016470) [Local registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT01016470.txt) [2012 outcome](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-WHO-CTG-Followup.md#viusid-and-alzer-fatigue-outcome)
