# Risk and Prevention

### 2006 — Pesticide exposure and incident disease

A prospective analysis of US adults linked self-reported pesticide exposure to higher subsequent Parkinson’s incidence, with adjusted relative risk 1.7. The study did not identify a single responsible chemical. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16802290/)

### 2007 — Occupational and medical exposures in Geoparkinson

A multicountry case-control study associated higher pesticide exposure and repeated loss of consciousness after head injury with higher odds of Parkinson’s disease or parkinsonism. The mixed diagnostic population and retrospective exposure histories constrain causal inference. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17332139/)
