# Summer savory preparation and endpoint search

## Carvacrol and thymol odor thresholds in sunflower oil

Bitar and colleagues used a three-alternative forced-choice procedure to measure detection thresholds in sunflower oil. The primary 2008 paper assigns 30.97 mg/kg to carvacrol and 124 mg/kg to thymol. A third isolated test compound, p-cymene 2,3-diol, had a threshold of 794.33 mg/kg. The tested materials are individual thyme-related compounds that overlap with the summer savory phytochemical route. These values quantify detection of the stimuli in the tested matrix and supply no therapeutic change in olfactory impairment. ## Summer savory vernacular in a Portuguese interview survey

Neves and colleagues first published online May 3, 2009. The survey table pairs voucher 2723, *Satureja hortensis*, with the vernacular name segurelha and flowering tops. Reported uses include tonsillitis and bronchitis. The adjacent laryngitis entry belongs to *Salvia officinalis*. This records local plant identity and reported practice, with no speech or voice outcome or established treatment response.
