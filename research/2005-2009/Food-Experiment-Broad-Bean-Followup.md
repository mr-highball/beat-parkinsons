# Broad bean discontinuation and neuroleptic malignant-like syndrome

Ladha and colleagues reported neuroleptic malignant-like syndrome in a person with Parkinson’s after abrupt discontinuation of fava bean ingestion. The authors identified withdrawal of naturally occurring levodopa as a possible explanation. A single report establishes a clinical signal under its circumstances and supplies no incidence estimate. The publisher first released the report on February 17, 2005. [Primary case report, PMID 15719433](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15719433/) · [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.20380)

The case concerns cessation of *Vicia faba*. The frozen food experiment evaluates ingestion of different bean species. [Earlier feeding studies](../1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md) · [Later dyskinesia case](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Broad-Bean-Followup.md)
