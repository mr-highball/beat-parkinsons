# Mechanisms and Models

### 2008 — Lewy pathology in long-surviving fetal grafts

Kordower and colleagues described Lewy body-like inclusions in implanted embryonic nigral cells at autopsy. This small human pathology series raised the possibility that host processes affect grafts. The observation cannot isolate the route or cause of the pathology. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18391962/)

### 2009 — Common genetic susceptibility across populations

Two genome-wide association studies found common susceptibility variants in European and Japanese samples. Signals included SNCA, with differences across populations at other loci. The associations estimate susceptibility and support mapping mechanisms in diverse cohorts. [Primary source](https://www.nature.com/articles/ng.487) [Related primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/ng.485)

### 2009 — GBA variants across centers

An international case-control study found that glucocerebrosidase gene variants occurred more often in Parkinson’s cases than in controls. Sequencing strategy and population ancestry affected detection. The association supported investigation of lysosomal mechanisms and genetic subgroups. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19846850/)

### 2009 — Urate and clinical progression

Analyses of PRECEPT in 2008 and DATATOP in 2009 linked higher baseline urate to slower progression on some clinical or imaging measures. Urate itself was observed, while the original trials randomized other agents. These analyses generated an intervention hypothesis. [2008 PRECEPT analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18413464/) [2009 DATATOP analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2795011/)
