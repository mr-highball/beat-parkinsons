# Direct pharyngeal olive oil stimulation and voluntary swallows

## Direct pharyngeal olive oil stimulation and voluntary swallows

The original abstract reports longer voluntary swallowing intervals during olive oil pharyngeal stimulation than with water stimulation. Grade, an ordinary eating exposure, and a clinical dysphagia estimate remain unverified.
