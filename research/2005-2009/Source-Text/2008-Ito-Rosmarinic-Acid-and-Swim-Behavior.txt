Isolated rosmarinic acid and mouse swim behavior
https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bpb/31/7/31_7_1376/_pdf

July 1, 2008 per J-STAGE. Ito and colleagues introduce Perilla as a source of rosmarinic acid and test an isolated commercial material administered by injection in mice. The full Methods and Results report reduced forced-swim immobility in several comparisons and no statistically significant alteration in open-field activity. The tested material was neither fresh rosemary nor oral leaf; the behavioral assays do not measure clinical Parkinson depression.
