Original study scope
The investigators used intrathecal recombinant TGF-beta1 after rat sciatic nerve ligation and reported attenuation of mechanical and thermal hypersensitivity in Figures 1–2. The study's therapeutic contrast uses a recombinant cytokine, with no culinary sage or date-palm intervention.
