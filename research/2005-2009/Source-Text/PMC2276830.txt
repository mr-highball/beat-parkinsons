Title: A comparison of glycemic control, sleep, fatigue, and depression in type 2 diabetes with and without restless legs syndrome.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2276830/
PMID: 18350963
PMCID: PMC2276830
Publication date: 2008-02-15
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC complete article HTML.

The 39-person study compared adults with type 2 diabetes with or without restless legs syndrome. Table 1 records daily cups of coffee. Table 2 gives Fatigue Severity Scale results grouped by restless legs status. The authors do not estimate a coffee–fatigue association or identify brewing method.
