Title: R-(+)- and S-(−)-carvone: influence of chirality on locomotion activity in mice
Authors: Gerhard Buchbauer, Walter Jäger, Alexandra Gruber and H Dietrich
Date: 2005-03-31 first online
Identifier: DOI 10.1002/ffj.1600
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/ffj.1600
Coverage: Original factual extract of the publisher abstract. The title's stereochemistry is retained as published.

The report studied movement in a light-barrier cage and compared mouse responses to inhaled carvone after different drug pretreatments and at different times. The narrative identifies (+)-carvone as the caraway-associated optical form and describes effects on movement dependent on the stimulus, with reduced hyperactivity in an amphetamine comparison. Group means, formulation details and the source of the reversed stereochemical labels in the article title were not resolved in the accessible abstract.
