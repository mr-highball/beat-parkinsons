Title: Oral Fat Exposure Pattern and Lipid Loading Effects on the Serum Triacylglycerol Concentration of Humans
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2843922/
PMCID: PMC2843922
PMID: 20352072
DOI: 10.1007/s12078-009-9062-4
Publication date: 2009 (journal issue 2009-12-01; PMC author manuscript released 2010-06-01)
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled original abstract and its explicit administration methods.

Fifteen healthy adults took part in a six-condition crossover involving 10 g or 30 g of safflower oil in swallowed capsules. The separately administered cream-cheese stimuli were chewed and spat out. Serum triacylglycerol increased in the early and late windows after the 30 g loads. Sequencing the stimuli and oil load did not distinguish the responses. The paper measured serum lipids and did not compare swallowing safety or dysphagia severity. This is a different protocol from the earlier 50 g load report.
