Title: Extrapyramidal parkinsonism complicating acute organophosphate insecticide poisoning.
PMID: 16243228
DOI: 10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2005.05.016
First published: 2005
Authors: Shahar E, Bentur Y, Bar-Joseph G, Cahana A, Hershman E
Journal: Pediatric neurology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16243228/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Pediatric case report of acute extrapyramidal parkinsonism (resting tremor, mask face, cogwheel rigidity, slow gait, responsive to amantadine within 1 week) developing 5 days after accidental ingestion of a raw eggplant heavily contaminated with the organophosphate insecticide dimethoate (Rogor). Illustrates acute agrochemical/organophosphate poisoning rather than an intrinsic effect of baked, grilled, or roasted eggplant.
