Title: Flavonoids, flavonoid-rich foods, and cardiovascular risk: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 18614722
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/88.1.38
First published: 2008
Authors: Hooper L, Kroon PA, Rimm EB, Cohn JS, Harvey I, Le Cornu KA, et al.
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18614722/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 133 flavonoid RCTs: acute black tea intake (4 trials) significantly increased systolic BP (+5.69 mmHg; 95% CI 1.52 to 9.86) and diastolic BP (+2.56 mmHg; 95% CI 1.03 to 4.10). Limits: context-only meta-analysis of acute resting BP in non-PD adults without orthostatic symptom outcomes.
