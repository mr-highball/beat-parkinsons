Title: Supplementation of fish oil and olive oil in patients with rheumatoid arthritis.
PMID: 15723739
DOI: 10.1016/j.nut.2004.03.023
First published: 2005
Authors: Berbert AA, Kondo CR, Almendra CL, Matsuo T, Dichi I
Journal: Nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15723739/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
24-week 3-arm parallel RCT in 43 patients with rheumatoid arthritis: G1 soy oil placebo, G2 3 g/d fish oil omega-3, G3 3 g/d fish oil + 9.6 mL olive oil (grade unspecified). Joint pain intensity, morning stiffness, fatigue onset, and Ritchie articular index improved in BOTH G2 and G3 vs G1; direct G3 vs G2 comparison differed only on patient global assessment at week 12, NOT on joint pain intensity. Cannot isolate oral EVOO analgesia from omega-3 fish oil.
