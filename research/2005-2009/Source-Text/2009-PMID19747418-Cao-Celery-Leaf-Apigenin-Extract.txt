Title: The relationship between fasting plasma concentrations of selected flavonoids and their ordinary dietary intake
Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/S000711450999170X
PMID: 19747418
DOI: 10.1017/S000711450999170X
First public article date: 2009-09-14
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated primary Methods and Table 3

Cao and colleagues studied diet and plasma flavonoids in
92 students. A subset of 20 participated in the celery-leaf
intervention. Table 3 gives a mean apigenin concentration
of 190.41 nmol/L (SD 98.54) at seven hours. The reported
average time of the individual peak is 7.70 hours (SD 0.82).
Plasma preparation included hydrolysis. The measurements
describe recovered apigenin and do not quantify unbound
apigenin or a brain concentration.

Zhao 2008 Table 1 (p447) and Cao Table 3 (p253) have identical
entries for all 35 displayed values at seven time points. Both
reports give the same 7.70-hour mean peak time (SD 0.82), area
under the curve of 89.02 (SD 40.57) minute-micromol/L, and
detection limit of 2.32 nmol/L. Zhao describes 10 people and Cao
describes 20. The results are a single numerical lineage with
unresolved participant provenance. Neither report tests raw stalk
or a motor outcome.
