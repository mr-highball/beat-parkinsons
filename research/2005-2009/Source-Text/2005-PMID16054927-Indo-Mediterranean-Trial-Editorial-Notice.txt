The Lancet placed an expression of concern on the 2002 Indo-Mediterranean
Diet Heart Study, PMID 12433513, DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(02)11472-3.
The related 2002 paper concerns a whole dietary pattern and coronary
outcomes. It cannot isolate mustard seed, soybean oil, cumin, cardamom
or turmeric against a frozen Parkinson symptom. The Thai-Mediterranean
viewpoint (PMID 37334427) cites it when describing regional diet examples.

The verified publisher action is an expression of concern. The concern
is retained as a property of the cited 2002 trial, not a separate
efficacy observation or independent source family.
