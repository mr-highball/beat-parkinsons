Title: Treatment of oral thrush in HIV/AIDS patients with lemon juice and lemon grass (Cymbopogon citratus) and gentian violet
Publication: Wright and colleagues, online before the March 2009 issue, Phytomedicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19109001/
Coverage: factual extract of the accessible primary source

The trial assigned oral-thrush treatment with an infusion made of Cymbopogon citratus cultivated and dried at the hospice, lemon juice, or 0.5% gentian violet solution. Its outcome was oral candidiasis rather than voice loudness or articulation. The primary abstract names 90 randomized and 83 completed. The authors reported no clear between-arm difference in the intention-to-treat analysis. The completer comparison favored lemongrass infusion over gentian violet (p < .05). It provides a real oral exposure route without evaluating minced stalk, low voice volume or Parkinson disease.
