Title: Low cost, high risk: accidental nutmeg intoxication.

First published: 2005
Authors: Demetriades AK, Wallman PD, McGuiness A, Gavalas MC

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Primary Abstract:
Nutmeg poisoning is rare but probably underreported and should be considered in recreational substance users with acute psychotic symptoms as well as central nervous system neuromodulatory signs that may mimic in part an anticholinergic hyperstimulation.

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Emergency Medicine Journal clinical report (PMC1726685) documenting acute psychotic/hallucinatory symptoms and CNS anticholinergic-like hyperstimulation from nutmeg intoxication; underscores underreporting and diagnostic mimicry.
