The word cinnamon in an olfactory brain imaging experiment
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S105381190600231X
PMID: 16651007
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2006.03.037
Coverage: original article abstract and inspected body; verified exact citation in Dugoua review

González and colleagues reported functional brain imaging in 23 participants reading odor-associated words, including cinnamon. The written-word stimuli elicited activity in olfactory regions. Neither cinnamon scent nor cinnamon ingestion was administered. The study gives no Parkinson cohort or clinical olfactory treatment comparison. Dugoua and colleagues explicitly cite this article in the olfactory subsection, Table 3 and bibliography of their later cinnamon review.
