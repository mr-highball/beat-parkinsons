The investigators analyzed baseline serum phospholipids and subsequent self-reported prescriptions used as a proxy for recurrence of depressive episodes. They report 222 complete matched case-control pairs and a separate restriction to 371 people without baseline depression or history. The primary analyses found no consistent association for EPA, DPA or DHA. The exploratory tobacco interaction results did not confirm the earlier directional pattern for food intake. The paper explicitly cites its prior analysis in the same SU.VI.MAX cohort. Individual overlap across the dietary and serum samples is not quantified here.

Claim 0874. This is contextual contrary evidence for a constituent hypothesis using a baseline biomarker. It has no separate trout exposure, cooking comparison or Parkinson symptom scale. Keep both source records in one cohort family.
