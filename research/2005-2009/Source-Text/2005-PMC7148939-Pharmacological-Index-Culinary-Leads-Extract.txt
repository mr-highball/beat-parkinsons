The Melissa officinalis entry points to volume 22, page 526, under an anticonvulsant context. The cited page falls within a 2000 review, DOI 10.1016/S1572-5995(00)80034-4. Its original page, preparations, primary references and effects remain unverified.

Food and constituent references are retained as citation leads at title/index depth. Complete access to this index does not establish access to the referenced studies. Brine shrimp are Artemia, not culinary shrimp; Asparagus racemosus is not automatically A. officinalis. No Parkinson symptom or clinical efficacy follows from an index label.
