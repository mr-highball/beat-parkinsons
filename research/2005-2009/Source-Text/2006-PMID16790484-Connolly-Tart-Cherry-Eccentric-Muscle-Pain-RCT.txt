Title: Efficacy of a tart cherry juice blend in preventing the symptoms of muscle damage.
PMID: 16790484
PMCID: PMC2579450
DOI: 10.1136/bjsm.2005.025429
First published: 2006
Authors: Connolly DA, McHugh MP, Padilla-Zakour OI, Carlson L, Sayers SP
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2579450/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled crossover trial (Connolly et al., 2006, Br J Sports Med, PMC2579450) in 14 male college students consuming 12 fl oz tart cherry juice blend vs placebo twice daily for 8 days around maximal eccentric elbow flexion contractions (opposite arm crossed over after 2-week washout). Time-by-treatment interactions favored tart cherry juice for isometric strength loss (4% vs 22% average over 4 days, p<0.0001) and subjective pain (p=0.017), whereas muscle tenderness (p=0.81) and relaxed elbow angle (p=0.85) did not differ. Limits: tiny young athletic sample (n=14), acute eccentric injury pain, liquid juice blend rather than dried fruit.
