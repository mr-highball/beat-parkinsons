Stover and colleagues reported 24 months of follow-up from the six-person, open-label unilateral pilot. They elicited adverse events from patient reports through open questioning. Three participants without a previous hallucination history reported mild visual hallucinations during the first six months. The adverse-event section attributes resolution to reducing an antiparkinsonian medication in one person and reports spontaneous resolution in the other two.

The 2008 article summarizes the same cohort and groups resolution of visual hallucinations and dyskinesia after medication reduction. Preserve the primary case split and the later wording. These are participant occurrence and course reports following a procedure, with no controlled visual-event rate or dried-fig contrast.
