Title: Daniel et al. oral isolated eugenol and separate chemical and thermal nociception outcomes

Original source scope
Orally administered isolated eugenol reduced chemically evoked abdominal writhing in mice. The hot-plate latency comparison was inconclusive, with a separate positive control. Inflammation assays in rats answer a different question. Preserve this distinct oral eugenol family and distinguish the compound from each ordinary spice preparation and clinical Parkinson pain.
