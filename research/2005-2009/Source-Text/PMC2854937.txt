Title: A Case of Biopsy-proven Early-onset Alzheimer's Disease with Hemiparkinsonism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2854937/
PMID: 20396477
PMCID: PMC2854937
DOI: 10.3988/jcn.2005.1.1.97
Publication date: 2005-04-30
Date basis: NLM electronic date and Journal of Clinical Neurology issue
Source depth: Complete official PMC main article
Format: Original-source factual extract

The case describes asymmetric parkinsonism with early-onset dementia. Biopsy findings were interpreted as Alzheimer pathology. Dopamine transporter imaging and later metabolism scans concern diagnostic observations. The course reports motor change and continuing cognitive decline. The visual cortex is named in the imaging account, without a separately measured visual hallucination result or any fig comparison.
