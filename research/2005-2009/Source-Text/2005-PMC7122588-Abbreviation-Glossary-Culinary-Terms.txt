Archive scope: Factual note from the original article, original page scans or identified original abstract member.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The complete 169-page German and English glossary spans printed pp. 305–473. It expands G’ale as ginger ale, GTE as green tea extract, and OJ as orange juice. BRAT-type entries list banana alongside other soft foods. Cod liver oil appears under an abbreviation. Chicken pox is a disease term. The definition list supplies no patient assignments, meal interventions or reported symptom changes.
