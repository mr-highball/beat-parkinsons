NCT00663871, Evaluating the Effects of Omega-3 Fatty Acids on Heart Disease and Behavior. Official record https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00663871. First posted 2008-04-22. Native JSON retrieved 2026-11-15 UTC. Coverage: selected factual design, formulations and endpoints.

This parallel randomized study enrolled 272 participants and compared marine oil with 2 g/day soybean oil. Prespecified behavior endpoints include negative affect and impulsivity. A 2014 substudy report describes mint flavor and vitamin E along with the oil comparator. In the final behavioral report the participants were generally healthy adults with low dietary marine-fat intake.
