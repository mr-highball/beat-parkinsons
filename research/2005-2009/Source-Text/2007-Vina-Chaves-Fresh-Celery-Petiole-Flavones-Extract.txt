Title: Respiratory activity and phenolic compounds in pre-cut celery
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S030881460600029X
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodchem.2005.12.060
Issue date: 2007, Food Chemistry 100(4)
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated original investigator repository manuscript and publisher article

The investigators studied fresh celery petioles and reported
both apigenin and luteolin. The reported initial quantities
are 4.61 and 0.88 micrograms per gram of fresh tissue,
respectively. Concentrations changed after cutting and
storage. These amounts are expressed per fresh tissue mass.

The two compounds were also measured in a separately
prepared whole-plant extract studied in mice in 2018. Their
presence does not identify which compounds caused the model
findings or establish comparable amounts under ingestion.
This source measures plant chemistry, not motor symptoms.
