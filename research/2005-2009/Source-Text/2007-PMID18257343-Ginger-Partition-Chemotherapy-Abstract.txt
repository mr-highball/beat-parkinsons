Title: Multi-central clinical evaluation of ginger-partitioned moxibustion for treatment of leukopenia induced by chemotherapy
PMID: 18257343
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18257343/
Date basis: October 2007 journal issue, Zhongguo Zhen Jiu 27(10), pp. 715–720.
Coverage: Factual extract of the primary indexed author abstract. Full original tables remain open.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The authors describe a multicentre randomized comparison of topical ginger-partitioned moxibustion versus an oral Chinese patent medicine for chemotherapy-associated leukopenia. Of 221 participants in the reported analysis, 113 received the topical treatment and 108 received the oral comparator. The abstract gives two leukopenia-related response percentages per arm and describes clinical symptom improvement collectively. It gives no separate constipation counts, scores or change.

The 2011 review (PMCID PMC3136359, Table 1) places constipation within a group of gastrointestinal toxicities including nausea, vomiting and diarrhea. The review's grouped p value cannot establish an item effect. The topical procedure, comparator and clinical population differ from an oral ginger and Parkinson constipation comparison.
