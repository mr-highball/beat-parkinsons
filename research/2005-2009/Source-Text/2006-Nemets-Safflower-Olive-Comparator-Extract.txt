Verified oil identity in the Nemets childhood depression trial
Source: https://www.giulemanidaibambini.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/24Trattamento-con-Omega-3-della-depressione-infantile-1.pdf
PMID: 16741212
DOI: 10.1176/ajp.2006.163.6.1098
Coverage: Original published Methods on page 1098 reviewed for comparator identity

Nemets and colleagues report two comparator capsule formulations, identified as 500 mg olive oil and 1,000 mg safflower oil. The 2017 Trebatická paper (PMC5497377) names sunflower oil in one description of this earlier study and safflower oil in another. The original Methods establish safflower or olive oil as the comparator identities. The Nemets trial cannot be counted as a sunflower-oil comparison.
