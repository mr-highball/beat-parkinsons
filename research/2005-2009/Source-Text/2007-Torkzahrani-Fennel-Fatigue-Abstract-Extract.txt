Topic: Fennel extract and fatigue in a separate dysmenorrhea trial
Source: https://www.magiran.com/paper/434449/clinical-effects-of-foeniculum-vulgare-extract-on-primary-dysmenorrhea?lang=en
Coverage: Original journal abstract, factual extract
Journal of Reproduction & Infertility 2007;8(1):45–51. The extract was identified in capsules in the primary abstract and compared with identical placebo. Fatigue was assessed in the systemic-symptom scale. The between-group p-value was .058. Exact fatigue arm values, baseline scores and direction await the original article body. The disease abbreviation PD in this article refers to primary dysmenorrhea.
