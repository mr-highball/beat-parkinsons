Title: Metabolic effects of caffeine and exercise in older adults
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1365-2265.2006.02579.x
PMID: 16886964
DOI: 10.1111/j.1365-2265.2006.02579.x
Publication date: 2006-07-06
Date basis: Publisher first online publication.
Review depth: Author abstract and publisher reference list. The full body remained unavailable on the inspected route.

Thirty healthy older adults completed randomized blinded crossover visits after 48 hours of caffeine avoidance. Isolated caffeine or placebo preceded cycling. The abstract reports increased epinephrine, free fatty acids, lactate and HOMA-IR across measurements. The biochemical results address the acute exercise response and do not estimate a fatigue symptom outcome.

The recruitment description, sample size, abstinence, caffeine dose, cycling protocol and authors overlap with PMID 16081625, cited as reference 24. Treat the 2006 report as a possible companion rather than an additional independent endurance or fatigue result. The abstract does not confirm participant identity. For round 1 claims 0121 and 0948, the missing exposures are ordinary brewed coffee or green tea, the missing population is diagnosed Parkinson disease and no daily fatigue contrast was measured.
