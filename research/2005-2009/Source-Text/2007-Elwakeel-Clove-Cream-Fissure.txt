Clove oil cream and chronic anal fissure healing
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1463-1318.2006.01185.x
PMID: 17573751
DOI: 10.1111/j.1463-1318.2006.01185.x
Coverage: Publisher first publication date and author abstract, full body pending

Elwakeel and colleagues first published June 15, 2007. The single-blind randomized trial compared six weeks of topical 1% clove-oil cream (30 participants) with conventional care including stool softeners and 5% lignocaine cream (25 participants). The primary abstract reports fissure healing at three months in 60% versus 12% (p < .001) and reduced anorectal pressures in the oil group. It does not report a pain score or an arm-level pain difference. This comparison addresses a topical oil and local fissure healing outside Parkinson disease.
