The original review separately discusses the gastric emptying test meal, symptoms and treatments. Chicken is among example solid meals for measuring emptying. The review does not estimate fatigue after poached chicken. The ginger paragraph concerns antiemetic use and gastric electrical rhythms, citing Gupta's rat extract gastric-emptying study and Gonlachanvit's healthy-volunteer electrical study. It does not supply a clinical ginger constipation, anxiety, dyskinesia or pain comparison.

Constipation and tardive dyskinesia also appear as effects of other interventions in the antiemetic sections. Read the two cited ginger studies at their own outcome and preparation depths.
