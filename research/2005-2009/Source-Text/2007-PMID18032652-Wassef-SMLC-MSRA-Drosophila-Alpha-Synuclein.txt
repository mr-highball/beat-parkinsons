Title: Methionine sulfoxide reductase A and a dietary supplement S-methyl-L-cysteine prevent Parkinson's-like symptoms.
PMID: 18032652
PMCID: PMC6673285
DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0322-07.2007
First published: 2007
Authors: Wassef R, Haenold R, Hansel A, Brot N, Heinemann SH, Hoshi T
Journal: The Journal of neuroscience : the official journal of the Society for Neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18032652/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Preclinical Drosophila alpha-synuclein overexpression model (PMC6673285) showing dietary supplementation with purified S-methyl-L-cysteine (SMLC, an amino acid derivative found in garlic, cabbage, and turnips) rescued locomotor and circadian defects via methionine sulfoxide reductase A (MSRA). Purified compound in fruit flies; unusable as clinical evidence for cooked, raw, or fermented green cabbage in human PD.
