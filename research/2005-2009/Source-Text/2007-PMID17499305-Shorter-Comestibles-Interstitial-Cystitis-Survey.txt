Title: Effect of comestibles on symptoms of interstitial cystitis.
PMID: 17499305
DOI: 10.1016/j.juro.2007.03.020
First published: 2007
Authors: Shorter B, Lesser M, Moldwin RM, Kushner L
Journal: The Journal of urology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17499305/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Cross-sectional survey of 104 patients with interstitial cystitis/bladder pain syndrome rating 175 comestibles (-2 to +2). 90.2% reported food-triggered exacerbations, with citrus fruits and juices among the most bothersome items (mean score <-1.0). Retrospective non-PD IC/BPS survey, balanced by Ha 2025 SR and Maserejian 2013 cohort.
