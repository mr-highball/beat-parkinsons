The authors separate the normal role of salivation in softening chewed food from lip closure and containment of saliva. Reduced lip closing pressure can contribute to drooling, while weak oral control can lead to premature bolus leakage. The paper provides swallowing and oral-motor context without assigning fig or estimating its effect on drooling.
