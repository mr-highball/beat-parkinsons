# Canola comparator and unusable grip measurements in arthritis

Source https://vuir.vu.edu.au/869/1/Power_et.al_2004.pdf
Coverage original thesis, Methods and Appendix 2

The Victoria University thesis is dated 2004 and its institutional metadata records 2008 as the online deposit. The study randomized 120 people and analyzed 101 completers. Its oral, topical and combined emu oil comparisons used Crisco canola on matching routes. The planned grip-strength instrument proved faulty and the thesis discarded that measurement. The document supplies a fatty-acid specification and no verified refining or separate manual dexterity outcome.
