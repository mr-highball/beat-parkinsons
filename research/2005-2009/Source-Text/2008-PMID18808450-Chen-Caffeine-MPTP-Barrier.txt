Title: Isolated caffeine and MPTP-induced striatal barrier dysfunction
First published: 2008-09-20
PMID: 18808450
PMCID: PMC3692355
DOI: 10.1111/j.1471-4159.2008.05697.x
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3692355/
Archive scope: Original methods, abstract, tissue comparisons and figure captions. The conference precursor is page S59 of doi:10.1007/s11302-008-9116-0.

FVB mice received caffeine or saline before repeated neurotoxin exposure. The full experiment reports protection of tyrosine-hydroxylase-positive striatal tissue and lower dye or albumin barrier leakage with caffeine pretreatment, with the leakage result specific to the striatum among the reported regions. Occludin, ZO-1 and glial activation were measured tissue outcomes. The conference abstract has the same model, team and main contrast. It should not be treated as independent replication. The injection of isolated caffeine, preclinical prevention design and tissue endpoints leave the oral coffee and clinical symptom claims unanswered.
