Title: Hallucinations and sleep disorders in PD: six-year prospective longitudinal study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15642908/
PMID: 15642908
DOI: 10.1212/01.WNL.0000148479.10865.FE
Publication date: 2005-01-11
Date basis: Journal issue date
Source depth: Primary author abstract; body remains open
Evidence type: Longitudinal Parkinson hallucination and sleep cohort
Format: Factual digest of the inspected source

The authors followed 89 deliberately stratified Parkinson participants at five assessments. At six years 49 were interviewed and 40 were deceased or too ill to participate. Nightmares and vivid dreams were associated with concurrent hallucinations but did not predict subsequent hallucinations in initially unaffected participants in the abstract (OR 0.94, p=.51). The cohort does not test dried figs. The exact visual modality ascertainment and full outcome tables remain open.
