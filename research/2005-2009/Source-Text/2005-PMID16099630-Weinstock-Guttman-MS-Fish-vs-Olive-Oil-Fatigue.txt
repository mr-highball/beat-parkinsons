Title: Low fat dietary intervention with omega-3 fatty acid supplementation in multiple sclerosis patients.
PMID: 16099630
DOI: 10.1016/j.plefa.2005.05.024
First published: 2005
Authors: Weinstock-Guttman B, Baier M, Park Y, Feichter J, Lee-Kwen P, Gallagher E
Journal: Prostaglandins, Leukotrienes, and Essential Fatty Acids
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16099630/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
1-year DB-RCT in 31 RRMS patients randomized to low-fat diet (15% fat) + fish oil vs AHA Step I diet (30% fat) + unspecified olive oil capsules. At 6 months, PCS/SF-36 and MHI favored fish oil (p = 0.050), while reduced fatigue was reported on the AHA Step I + olive oil arm (p = 0.035). Multi-component dietary difference (15% vs 30% fat), unspecified olive oil grade, active comparator, non-PD MS cohort.
