Member T1:PO.44 (SA42, printed p. 65) reports a blinded trial in 60 postmenopausal women with hyperlipidemia. The three groups received yogurt, yogurt with 16 g soy lecithin, or yogurt with 10 g sunflower oil matched to the lecithin's linoleic acid. Strawberry syrup was included for blinding. The authors report no significant between-group lipid differences. Reported within-group improvements refer to soy lecithin. The poster gives no sunflower pressing grade, executive task, mood or anxiety outcome, or Parkinson cohort.
