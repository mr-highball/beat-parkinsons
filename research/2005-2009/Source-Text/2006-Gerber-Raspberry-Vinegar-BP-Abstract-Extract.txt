Raspberry vinegar and unreported blood pressure outcomes
Deposited conference author abstract and Crossref year
Gerber, Hutchins and Johnston’s March 2006 abstract compares red raspberry vinegar and cranberry juice before meals over eight weeks. Four of eight assigned vinegar and five of eight assigned cranberry juice completed the intervention. Both groups had blood pressure scheduled at baseline and weeks four and eight. The abstract prints no pressure values or orthostatic assessment. A secondary summary gives different dosing and duration. The full original poster and pressure estimates remain open.
