Title: Further characterization of a furanocoumarin-free grapefruit juice on drug disposition: studies with cyclosporine
Source: https://ajcn.nutrition.org/article/s0002-9165%2823%2923555-8/fulltext
PMID: 18400708
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/87.4.863
Publication date: 2008-04
Date basis: American Journal of Clinical Nutrition issue citation.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Review depth: abstract
Format: Original controlled trial scope digest from the public report and journal abstract.

Eighteen healthy adults participated in a randomized crossover comparing normal grapefruit juice, grapefruit juice depleted of furanocoumarins, and orange juice with cyclosporine. The original abstract reports higher dose-corrected systemic exposure with ordinary grapefruit juice than with orange juice. The same comparison against orange juice was inconclusive for furanocoumarin-depleted grapefruit juice.

The trial compares drug exposure in healthy adults and a distinct drug from the felodipine trial. It supplies no whole-orange, Parkinson, fall-frequency or constipation outcome.
