Title: Comparison of 2 interventions for liquid aspiration on pneumonia incidence: a randomized trial.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2364726/
PMID: 18378947
PMCID: PMC2364726
DOI: 10.7326/0003-4819-148-7-200804010-00007
Publication date: 2008-04-01
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC complete article HTML.

The randomized aspiration trial compared chin-down posture and thickened liquids in adults with dementia or Parkinson disease. The introduction lists coffee among examples of thin liquids. The trial assigns swallowing strategy and measures pneumonia, without beverage-specific administration or a fatigue comparison.
