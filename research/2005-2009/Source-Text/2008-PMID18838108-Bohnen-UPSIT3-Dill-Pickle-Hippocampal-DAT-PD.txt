Title: Selective hyposmia in Parkinson disease: association with hippocampal dopamine activity.
PMID: 18838108
PMCID: PMC2634293
DOI: 10.1016/j.neulet.2008.09.070
First published: 2008
Authors: Bohnen NI, Gedela S, Herath P, Constantine GM, Moore RY
Journal: Neurosci Lett
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18838108/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Clinical PET/olfactory study (Bohnen et al., 2008, Neurosci Lett, PMC2634293; building on Bohnen 2007 PMID:17508142) in 29 Parkinson's disease patients showing selective hyposmia to three UPSIT odors (banana, licorice, dill pickle; UPSIT-3) correlated strongly with hippocampal DAT PET binding (Rs=0.65, p=0.0001). Diagnostic smell-identification stimulus only — NOT dietary consumption of ground dill seed.
