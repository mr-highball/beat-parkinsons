Title: Перспективные антидепрессанты растительного происхождения
Author: Dina Viktorovna Kadatskaya (Д. В. Кадацкая).
Affiliation printed in the paper: Samara State Medical University.
Publication: Разработка, исследование и маркетинг новой фармацевтической продукции. Issue 60. Pyatigorsk, 2005, pp. 348–350.
Source: https://pmedpharm.ru/upload/iblock/a0e/p75gt2p85p5twouqjhyhaglyrawpvnfu.pdf
Text coverage: Original factual extraction from the university-hosted original primary paper. The parent proceedings remain at the provider.

The test preparation is полыни эстрагон настойка, an A. dracunculus tincture. The paper gives 50 mg/kg mixed with purified distilled water and oral administration once daily. In reported behavior experiments the run-in was a week for the open field, two weeks for the elevated plus maze and forced swim, and three days for a separate physical endurance protocol. The first three assays were performed in outbred albino rats of both sexes, observed for five minutes. Tarragon group sample size and raw time metrics were unavailable. The concentration of its tincture was not given.

The open-field results describe mean grooming as 2.7 times the control level. On the first day, exploration and upright rearing were 1.7 and 2.3 times the control level, respectively, with p ≤ .05 reported for the latter comparisons. The paper prints the elevated plus maze tables for other plants. In the reported forced-swim experiment, a statistically detectable activity increase was described for St. John's wort, a less clear difference for peony rhizome, and no activity for the remaining preparations, including tarragon. No tarragon-specific immobility values or comparison p value were printed. In the mouse loaded-swimming protocol, results for the remaining preparations including tarragon did not show endurance activity.

The paper attributes its tested doses to prior dose selection from пентобарбиталового и хлоралгидратного сна (pentobarbital- and chloral-hydrate-induced sleep). The accessible dissertation Contents §3.1 says тиопенталового и хлоралгидратного сна (thiopental- and chloral-hydrate-induced sleep). Sleep durations, group sizes, route of the specific preliminary sleep assay and control results remain unavailable from these portions. Dose and route for the behavior report cannot fill the sleep methods gap. Neither source supplied an ordinary sleep outcome.
