Title: Artichoke leaf extract (Cynara scolymus) reduces plasma cholesterol in otherwise healthy hypercholesterolemic adults: a randomized, double blind placebo controlled trial.
PMID: 18424099
DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2008.03.001
First published: 2008
Authors: Bundy, Walker, Middleton, Wallis, Simpson
Journal: Phytomedicine : international journal of phytotherapy and phytopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18424099/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-week double-blind placebo-controlled parallel RCT in 75 healthy hypercholesterolemic adults randomized to 1280 mg/d standardized Cynara scolymus leaf extract vs placebo. Plasma total cholesterol decreased 4.2% with ALE vs +1.9% with placebo (p = 0.025), while LDL, HDL, and triglycerides did not differ. General well-being improved in both ALE (11%) and placebo (9%) with no significant between-group difference. Limits: leaf extract, general well-being scale rather than depression/anxiety/fatigue symptom score.
