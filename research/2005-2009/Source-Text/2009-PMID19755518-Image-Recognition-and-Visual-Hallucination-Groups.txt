Title: Impaired visual processing preceding image recognition in Parkinson's disease patients with visual hallucinations
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19755518/
PMID: 19755518
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awp223
Publication date: 2009-09-15
Date basis: Verified electronic publication
Source depth: Primary abstract; full article remains open
Evidence type: Primary visual task fMRI comparison
Format: Factual digest of the original abstract

The reported groups were nine nondemented Parkinson patients with visual hallucinations, 14 without them and 13 controls. Before recognition of gradually revealed images, patients with hallucinations showed reduced activation in lateral occipital and extrastriate temporal areas. The report did not find the hypothesized increase in frontal activation. Hallucination status defined a group, and the main response was task-related imaging rather than the frequency of spontaneous visual events.
