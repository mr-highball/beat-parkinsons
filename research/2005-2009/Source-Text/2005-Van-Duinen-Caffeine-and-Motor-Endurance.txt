van Duinen H, Lorist MM, Zijdewind I. The effect of caffeine on cognitive task performance and motor fatigue. Psychopharmacology. First published February 19, 2005.
PMID 15723227. DOI 10.1007/s00213-005-2191-9.
Author manuscript: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8010700_The_effect_of_caffeine_on_cognitive_task_performance_and_motor_fatigue

Twenty-three healthy crossover completers took decaffeinated coffee with experimental caffeine or decaf alone in a double-blind, semi-randomized design with balanced allowance for a usual morning coffee. Reaction times improved with caffeine. Sustained finger endurance (F[1,21]=1.20) and absolute force (F[1,21]=.15) were inconclusive. The experiment measured motor fatigability, with no Parkinson cohort or symptom severity questionnaire. Both study drinks contained the decaffeinated matrix.
