Title: Anosmia is very common in the Lewy body variant of Alzheimer's disease
Source: https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/76/10/1342.full.pdf
PMID: 16170073
PMCID: PMC1739380
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.2003.032003
Publication year: 2005
Text coverage: Paraphrased findings checked against complete publisher PDF.

The clinicopathologic comparison includes 106 autopsy-confirmed dementia cases, 89 with Alzheimer pathology alone and 17 with Lewy body variant. It excludes Parkinson motor onset more than 12 months before dementia. Visual hallucination history predicted Lewy body pathology in the fitted model (OR 7.3, 95% CI 1.71–31.08). The model predicts neuropathology from a clinical feature and provides no fig exposure or exposure-linked episode rate.

The autopsy series used butanol odor thresholds. Loss of smell occurred in 65% of Lewy body variant cases and 23% of Alzheimer-only cases. Adding it as a diagnostic feature identified five additional Lewy body variant cases and produced four additional false positives. The publisher PDF supplied the actual Parkinson exclusion and interval for the visual feature predictor.
