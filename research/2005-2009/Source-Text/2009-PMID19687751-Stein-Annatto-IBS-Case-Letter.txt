Title: Annatto and IBS.
PMID: 19687751
DOI: 10.1097/MCG.0b013e3181ae4e1b
First published: 2009
Authors: Stein
Journal: Journal of clinical gastroenterology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19687751/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Clinical case letter (with companion editorial by Floch, PMID 19727005) describing 1 adult patient (author's spouse) with multi-year IBS symptoms (chronic diarrhea, abdominal pain, bloating) that completely resolved upon eliminating annatto-colored foods (non-dairy creamers, ice cream) and recurred upon rechallenge. Limits: single unblinded clinical case letter involving processed annatto food dye rather than ground culinary seed, no quantitative pain scale or PD diagnosis.
