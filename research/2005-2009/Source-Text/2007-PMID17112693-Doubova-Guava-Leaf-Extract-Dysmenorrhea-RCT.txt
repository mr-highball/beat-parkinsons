Title: Effect of a Psidii guajavae folium extract in the treatment of primary dysmenorrhea: a randomized clinical trial
PMID: 17112693
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2006.09.033
First published: 2007
Authors: Doubova SV, Morales HR, Hernández SF, Martínez-García Mdel C, Ortiz MG, Soto MA
Journal: Journal of Ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17112693/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Four-month 4-arm double-blind RCT in 197 women (mean age 19 y) with primary dysmenorrhea assigned standardized Psidii guajavae folium (guava LEAF) extract 3 mg/d or 6 mg/d flavonols, ibuprofen 1200 mg/d, or placebo. Authors reported reduced menstrual VAS pain intensity with 6 mg/d in adjusted regression (p<0.001; 3 mg/d inconsistent). CRITICAL PLANT-PART & INDEPENDENT COCHRANE CAVEAT: (1) Strictly guava LEAF extract, NOT raw guava fruit. (2) Independent Cochrane 2016 meta-analysis (Pattanittum et al., PMID:27000311, PMC7387104) found raw VAS pain difference vs placebo was NULL (MD +0.59 points, 95% CI -0.13 to 1.31; n=151) and significantly WORSE than NSAIDs (MD +1.19 points, 95% CI 0.42 to 1.96; n=155).
