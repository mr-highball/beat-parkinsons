Title: Fructan and free fructose content of common Australian vegetables and fruit (leek bulb vs green tops FODMAP profile).
PMID: 17625872
DOI: 10.1021/jf070623x
First published: 2007
Authors: Muir JG, Shepherd SJ, Rosella O, Rose R, Barrett JS, Gibson PR
Journal: Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17625872/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Foundational Monash University analytical survey of vegetable fructans demonstrating high fructan concentration in white leek bulbs/pseudostem versus low fructan content in green leek leaf tips, informing low-FODMAP GI tolerance guidance. Paired with Steenhagen 2006 (PMID:16963353, flatulence/stool output in IPAA patients), Takamatsu 2024 (PMID:38345321, rare leek-dependent exercise-induced anaphylaxis), and botanical exclusions (Xie 2023 PMID:37685158 tested Allium fistulosum Welsh onion misnamed 'Leek'; Smith 2015 PMC4663569 tested chicory inulin rather than leek).
