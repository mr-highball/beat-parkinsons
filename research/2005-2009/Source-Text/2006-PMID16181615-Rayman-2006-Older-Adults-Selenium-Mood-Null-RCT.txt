Title: Impact of selenium on mood and quality of life: a randomized, controlled trial.
PMID: 16181615
DOI: 10.1016/j.biopsych.2005.06.019
First published: 2006
Authors: Rayman M, Thompson A, Warren-Perry M, Galassini R, Catterick J, Hall E, et al.
Journal: Biological psychiatry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16181615/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

6-month double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in n=501 UK older adults (60-74 y) allocated to 100, 200, or 300 ug/d selenium yeast vs placebo: completely null effect on POMS-BI mood, depression, anxiety, and quality of life (F=0.25, p=0.86). Major competing null older-adult RCT contradicting small selenium mood trials.
