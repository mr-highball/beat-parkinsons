Title: The dry plant extract of common bean seed (Phaseoli vulgari pericarpium) does not have an affect on postprandial glycemia in healthy human subject

Cerović and colleagues report a randomized placebo-controlled crossover in 18 healthy participants. Although the English title uses common bean seed, the abstract and intervention describe a dry Phaseoli vulgari pericarpium extract in a capsule. The results report no significant treatment difference in incremental glucose or glucose area under the curve during an oral glucose challenge. The paper gives sex differences in glucose observations across both conditions.

The authors also state that the two conditions did not differ in spontaneously reported short-term symptoms, including insomnia and anxiety during test and washout. These were tolerability observations, separate from a graded symptom efficacy outcome. The population, formulation and endpoints are distinct from cooked green beans and Parkinson fatigue, movement or falling.
