Title: Effect of Repeated Intake of Imidazole Dipeptides-containing Drink on Healthy People with Feeling of Fatigue from Daily Activities
Source: https://japanpm.com/pdf/research01.pdf
Bibliography: https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/en/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=200902213358207559
First published: March 2009, Japan Pharmacology and Therapeutics 37(3):255–263.
Archive scope: Original Japanese report, Methods, Tables 1–5, Results and discussion. Table 3 gives fatigue scores as means and standard deviations.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

The study by Shimizu, Fukuda and Yamamoto randomized 212 healthy adults reporting everyday fatigue to a placebo drink or 200 mg or 400 mg/day of imidazole dipeptides for eight weeks. The source identifies the dipeptides as chicken breast-derived carnosine and anserine. The 60 mL preparations differed in protein and modestly in energy. Five people did not complete participation. The analyzed group counts were 67, 70 and 70 for placebo, 200 mg and 400 mg, respectively. The paper calls this the first recruitment analysis.

The primary endpoint was weekly self-rated fatigue on a 100 mm visual analogue scale. The 400 mg arm favored the active drink at weeks 2–8 using the reported per-time-point comparisons. The 200 mg arm favored the drink at weeks 3, 4 and 6, without a reported significant difference at week 8. At baseline the placebo and 400 mg groups averaged 57.9 ± 16.6 and 54.8 ± 14.8 mm. At week 8 these were 44.0 ± 16.6 and 35.6 ± 16.8 mm. The raw between-arm week-8 difference is −8.4 mm. Subtracting each baseline gives a descriptive difference in change of −5.3 mm. The latter calculation has no reported covariance, confidence interval or adjusted model. The authors compared groups using ANOVA and Dunnett tests. The reporting does not establish control of multiplicity across weekly tests.

The Chalder fatigue score favored 400 mg at week 2 only. A smaller subgroup attempted two objective exercise performance tests, with no detected between-arm effect on those tests. These differences separate subjective daily fatigue from physical performance. The concentrated drink tested in healthy adults provides constituent and outcome context, without a culinary meat arm or Parkinson comparison.
