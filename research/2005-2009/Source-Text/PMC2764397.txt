Title: Pathologic findings in retinal pigment epithelial cell implantation for Parkinson disease

Format: Original-source factual extract

The authors report an autopsy from one active-treatment participant enrolled in the STEPS bilateral randomized trial. He reported no motor benefit and died six months after implantation. The study examined one cerebral hemisphere and reports low survival of implanted cells in the sampled tissue, with inflammation and reactive astrocytosis near the implant tracks.

The case belongs to the randomized trial rather than the earlier unilateral pilot. It gives no separately coded visual hallucination result, and the experiment has no dried-fig exposure.
