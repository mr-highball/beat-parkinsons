2007 — Aqueous coriander seed extract infused into rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17961943/
Coverage: Factual extract of Methods and Table 2.

Anesthetized rats received an intravenous aqueous coriander seed infusion. Diuretic indices against saline at two doses were 2.43 and 3.96. The furosemide comparator was 7.38. Measured outcomes include urine flow and electrolytes. The report discusses arterial pressure without a standing result.
