Title: [Frozen berries as a risk factor for outbreaks of norovirus gastroenteritis. Results of an outbreak investigation in the summer of 2005 in Hamburg].
PMID: 17238056
DOI: 10.1007/s00103-007-0142-9
First published: 2007
Authors: Fell G, Boyens M, Baumgarte S
Journal: Bundesgesundheitsblatt, Gesundheitsforschung, Gesundheitsschutz
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17238056/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Epidemiologic food-safety outbreak investigation linking contaminated commercial frozen blackberries to acute viral norovirus gastroenteritis. Infectious contamination hazard rather than an intrinsic effect of frozen blackberries on chronic constipation.
