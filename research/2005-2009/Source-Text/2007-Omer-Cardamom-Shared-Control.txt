Cardamom seed shared by the wormwood and control groups
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.phymed.2007.01.001
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: factual extract

Omer and colleagues compared added wormwood in 40 adults with Crohn disease. The original indexed methods describe 40 mg cardamom seeds in each capsule of both arms, alongside common botanicals. The 21-item Hamilton Depression Scale could estimate the incremental wormwood comparison. The protocol did not provide a cardamom-free group or verify grinding of the seeds. The original numerical tables remain unavailable through the checked publisher route.
