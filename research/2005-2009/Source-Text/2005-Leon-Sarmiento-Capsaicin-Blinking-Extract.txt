2005 — Topical capsaicin and a blepharospasm case

Original source: https://www.arquivosdeneuropsiquiatria.org/wp-content/uploads/articles_xml/1678-4227-anp-S0004-282X2005000500004/1678-4227-anp-S0004-282X2005000500004.pdf
Identifier: PMID 16258648 · 10.1590/S0004-282X2005000500004

Leon-Sarmiento and colleagues used transdermal capsaicin in five healthy volunteers and one woman with blepharospasm. The mean healthy blink rate was 17 ± 3 before and 15 ± 5 after, with no change judged significant. The uncontrolled case's reported blepharospasm severity rating fell from 4 at baseline to 1 at six months (source Table). The patient had focal eyelid dystonia, and the healthy assay measured spontaneous blinking. Neither cohort had a Parkinson facial expression assessment or ingested ground cayenne.
