Title: Grape extract, mitochondrial measures and locomotor dysfunction in a Drosophila Parkinson model

Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract and publisher article summary. NLM metadata carries no linked retraction or correction for this record as checked.

The tested product, Regrapex-R, contains whole grape and Japanese knotweed extracts. The author abstract reports better climbing in male flies in a Parkinson model and longer mean survival in female flies. Rat liver mitochondria and human-derived cells supply separate biochemical results. Neither the mix nor its animal climbing endpoint yields a raw-grape effect on muscle rigidity in people with Parkinson disease.
Evidence boundary: A mixed extract in a model with a locomotion proxy. Full sample-level results and the isolated contributions of each plant are unresolved.
