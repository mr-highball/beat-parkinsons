Source depth: NLM metadata and original publisher section previews; complete original letter remains open

PubMed dates electronic publication to 2009-06-12 and the issue to January 2010, pages 71–72. PubMed provides no abstract. The journal citation is listed in later Groningen apomorphine reports and in the NCT02702076 registration. Its original patient count, comparison depth and overlap with later patients remain unverified at primary body depth. The clinical trial was registered years later, so the letter cannot report its randomized comparison. Its title is a discovery lead rather than an independent visual episode-frequency estimate.

The publisher section preview identifies an open-label study of continuous subcutaneous infusion in fluctuating Parkinson patients with pre-existing visual hallucinations. The available discussion text reports improvements in visual hallucination severity and caregiver distress. The preview does not verify visit timing, numerical results, a control group, changes in co-treatment or later cohort overlap. The full original remains open.
