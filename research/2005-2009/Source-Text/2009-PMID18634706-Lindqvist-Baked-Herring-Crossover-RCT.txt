Title: Herring (Clupea harengus) intake influences lipoproteins but not inflammatory and oxidation markers in overweight men
PMID: 18634706
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114508003073
First published: 2009
Authors: Lindqvist HM, Langkilde AM, Undeland I, Sandberg AS
Journal: British Journal of Nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18634706/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Randomized crossover feeding trial in 35 overweight men consuming 150 g/day oven-baked Atlantic herring fillets 5 days/week vs matched lean pork/chicken fillets for 6 weeks each (12-week washout). Baked herring significantly increased HDL (1.04 vs 0.99 mmol/L) with NO significant effect on blood pressure, CRP, IL-6, IL-18, ICAM-1, bleeding time, or oxidized LDL (confirming earlier 4-week n=13 crossover PMID:17268414). No neurological, sleep, mood, or PD symptoms assessed.
