Title: Effects of an olive oil-enriched diet on glucagon-like peptide 1 release and intestinal content, plasma insulin concentration, glucose tolerance and pancreatic insulin content in an animal model of type 2 diabetes
Publication date: 2006-02
PMID: 16523410
DOI: 10.1055/s-2006-925126
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16523410/
Text coverage: Distilled original author abstract.

Adult rats with neonatal streptozotocin exposure were compared on standard and olive-oil-enriched diets. The oil diet increased the immediate plasma GLP-1 response in meal-trained rats. After 50 days, it lowered intestinal GLP-1 content and weight gain relative to standard diet. Glucose tolerance and insulin response did not improve in the oral glucose test. This differs from the earlier normal-rat study for chronic outcomes. The cohort is a separate model from PMID 15888922 and does not test human or Parkinson bowel symptoms.
