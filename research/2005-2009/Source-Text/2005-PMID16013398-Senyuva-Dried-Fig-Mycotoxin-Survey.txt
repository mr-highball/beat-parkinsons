Title: Survey for co-occurrence of ochratoxin A and aflatoxin B1 in dried figs in Turkey
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16013398/
Author article rendition consulted: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/7732067_Survey_for_Co-occurrence_of_Ochratoxin_A_and_Aflatoxin_B_1_in_Dried_Figs_in_Turkey_by_Using_a_Single_Laboratory-Validated_Alkaline_Extraction_Method_for_Ochratoxin_A
PMID: 16013398
DOI: 10.4315/0362-028X-68.7.1512
Publication date: 2005-07
Date basis: Original Journal of Food Protection 68(7), pages 1512–1515.
Text coverage: Author abstract, original methods and results, and study tables in the article rendition.

Senyuva and colleagues directly tested Turkish dried figs for ochratoxin A and aflatoxin B1. The 2003 export series contained 58 samples, with seven positive for aflatoxin B1 alone, two for ochratoxin A alone and two for both under the reported thresholds. The 2004 export series contained 41 samples, with 16, four and two respectively. Among 20 retail samples, one was positive for ochratoxin A and none for aflatoxin B1. These were separate sampling sets. The article describes prior screening of export samples and different detection limits, both of which matter when comparing prevalence.

This records contaminant occurrence in tested food. Serve-level ingestion, neurological symptoms and Parkinson outcomes were outside the sampling design. The Turkish findings do not supply concentrations for Morocco’s Skoura or Taounate preparations.
