Title: Inter- and intraindividual pharmacokinetic variations in the treatment of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16447756/
Original: https://mhlw-grants.niph.go.jp/system/files/2005/057011/200500270A/200500270A0006.pdf
PMID: 16447756
Publication: November 2005
Depth: Review text, Japanese sections on cabergoline and selegiline and accompanying English author abstract.

The Japanese discussion of grapefruit juice and cabergoline refers to the Nagai conference report. The English abstract includes grapefruit juice among possible modifiers of selegiline bioavailability. The Japanese selegiline discussion instead describes a hormone interaction, with no quantified independent grapefruit and selegiline experiment. Neither section reports a measured rest-tremor result after grapefruit. Separate dietary protein and levodopa observations address medication response under other exposures.
