# Paced tapping rate and BOLD during the Slade crossover

Source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6871241/
Coverage original Methods and Results

Slade and colleagues first published February 11, 2008, ahead of the March 2009 print issue. Eight of ten healthy adults were analyzed in the crossover of a liquid meal with or without 50 mL canola oil. In the simple paced pressure-bulb tapping task, average rate was 2.09 ± 0.27 Hz SEM across trials with no significant variation. The paper reports no significant meal effect on task-activated BOLD at three hours. Task timing, image analysis and participant sample differ from the 2003 experiment. The processing grade, hand dexterity accuracy and Parkinson effects remain unresolved.
