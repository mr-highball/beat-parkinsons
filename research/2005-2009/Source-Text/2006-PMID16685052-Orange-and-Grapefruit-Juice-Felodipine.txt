Title: A furanocoumarin-free grapefruit juice establishes furanocoumarins as the mediators of the grapefruit juice–felodipine interaction
Source: https://ajcn.nutrition.org/article/S0002-9165%2823%2913885-8/fulltext
PMID: 16685052
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/83.5.1097
Publication date: 2006-05
Date basis: American Journal of Clinical Nutrition issue citation.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Review depth: abstract
Format: Original controlled trial scope digest from the public report and journal abstract.

Eighteen healthy adults took part in a crossover comparing normal grapefruit juice, grapefruit juice depleted of furanocoumarins, and orange juice with felodipine. The comparison preserved the principal flavonoids while changing the concentration of the furanocoumarin fraction. Felodipine exposure was higher with normal grapefruit juice than with either orange juice or the depleted juice. This supports a role for the furanocoumarin fraction in this particular drug comparison.

The study used a beverage comparator and pharmacokinetic endpoints. It did not test falls, constipation, whole oranges or Parkinson disease. These results concern felodipine and do not establish the mechanism of a separate amitriptyline case.

A journal erratum is indexed separately at https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/84.1.264. The correction body was not confirmed in this pass.
