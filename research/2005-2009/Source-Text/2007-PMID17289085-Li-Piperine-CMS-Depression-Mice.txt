Title: Antidepressant like effects of piperine in chronic mild stress treated mice and its possible mechanisms.
PMID: 17289085
DOI: 10.1016/j.lfs.2006.12.027
First published: 2007
Authors: Li S, Wang C, Wang M, Li W, Matsumoto K, Tang Y
Journal: Life sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17289085/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse chronic mild stress (CMS) experiment showing 14-day repeated piperine (2.5, 5, 10 mg/kg) reversed CMS-induced deficits in sucrose consumption, plasma corticosterone, and open-field activity while upregulating hippocampal BDNF and progenitor proliferation. Corroborated by Li 2007 (PMID:17701559), where chronic 10-20 mg/kg piperine reduced FST/TST immobility without locomotor confound, while high-dose 80 mg/kg returned to control level (inverted-U dose-response).
