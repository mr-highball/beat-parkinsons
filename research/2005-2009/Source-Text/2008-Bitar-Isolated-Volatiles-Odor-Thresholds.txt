Carvacrol and thymol odor thresholds in sunflower oil
Primary source: https://aocs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1007/s11746-008-1244-8
DOI: 10.1007/s11746-008-1244-8
Coverage: factual extract from primary publisher abstract.

Bitar and colleagues used a three-alternative forced-choice procedure to measure detection thresholds in sunflower oil. The primary 2008 paper assigns 30.97 mg/kg to carvacrol and 124 mg/kg to thymol. A third isolated test compound, p-cymene 2,3-diol, had a threshold of 794.33 mg/kg. The tested materials are individual thyme-related compounds that overlap with the summer savory phytochemical route. These values quantify detection of the stimuli in the tested matrix and supply no therapeutic change in olfactory impairment.
