Gold kiwi ingestion and acute hypotension
PMID 19150037. Original letter clinical presentation and testing.
Henríquez Santana and colleagues describe a 35-year-old woman with itching, hives, dyspnea and uvular edema within minutes of eating gold kiwifruit. Hypotension was measured, but the paper supplies no pressure value or postural comparison. Skin tests supported sensitization and no oral challenge was reported. The distinct cultivar and acute allergic episode provide context for kiwi safety rather than a chronic symptom effect.
