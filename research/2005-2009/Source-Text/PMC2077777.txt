Title: Alpha4beta2 nicotinic receptor status in Alzheimer's disease using 123I-5IA-85380 single-photon-emission computed tomography
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2077777/
PMID: 17135460
PMCID: PMC2077777
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.2006.108209
Publication date: 2006-11-29
Date basis: NLM electronic date. The journal issue appeared in April 2007.
Source depth: Complete official PMC main text
Format: Original-source factual extract

Sixteen people with probable Alzheimer disease and 16 controls underwent receptor and perfusion imaging. Several regions showed lower receptor tracer binding in the Alzheimer group. References to visual hallucinations relate to prior Lewy body dementia research in the discussion. This original comparison does not measure a visual event rate in Parkinson disease or fig exposure.
