Berry SEE, Tydeman EA, Lewis HB, Phalora R, Rosborough J, Picout DR, Ellis PR. Manipulation of lipid bioaccessibility of almond seeds influences postprandial lipemia in healthy human subjects. Am J Clin Nutr. 2008 Oct;88(4):922–929. DOI 10.1093/ajcn/88.4.922. PMID 18842777.

Local factual summary from the original methods and results. Twenty healthy men completed a randomized crossover of three muffin-and-custard meals. Meals used almond oil with defatted almond flour, whole almond particles, or a sunflower blend. The oil and flour came from the same almond batch by commercial hexane extraction. The oil was filtered with minimal refining, without bleaching or deodorizing. The muffins were baked and frozen. Whole almond particles produced an incremental postmeal triacylglycerol area 74% lower than the almond-oil meal and 58% lower than the sunflower comparison (P < 0.001). The vascular augmentation differences were not significant. This was a separate cohort from the 2004 satiety study. It specifies solvent extraction and measures no Parkinson voice or facial-expression endpoint.
