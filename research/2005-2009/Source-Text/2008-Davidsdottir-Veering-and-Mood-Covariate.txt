Depression score as a covariate in a Parkinson veering study
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2577802/

October 28, 2008 first online. Thirty-one Parkinson participants were enrolled and 23 completed walking assessment. The two Parkinson laterality groups scored higher than controls on BDI-II, with an overall group comparison F(2,46)=8.44, p=.001. The study examines visual navigation and veering. Cervical dystonia is referenced in the discussion. The source does not test a depression treatment, dystonia outcome or lemon balm.
