Title: A randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of a herbal medicinal product containing Tropaeoli majoris herba (Nasturtium) and Armoraciae rusticanae radix (Horseradish) for the prophylactic treatment of patients with chronically recurrent lower urinary tract infections
PMID: 17723159
DOI: 10.1185/030079907X233089
First published: 2007
Authors: Albrecht U, Goos KH, Schneider B
Journal: Current Medical Research and Opinion
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17723159/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Ninety-day double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (174 enrolled, 45 screening failures) of the fixed 2-herb combination Angocin Anti-Infekt N (200 mg Tropaeolum majus nasturtium + 80 mg Armoracia rusticana horseradish root) BID for recurrent bacterial UTI prophylaxis. Mean per-protocol recurrent UTIs were 0.43 vs 0.77 placebo (p=0.035), with similar AEs (36 vs 37). Fixed 2-herb combination (attribution_fit=remote, exposure_fit=remote) testing infectious UTI prophylaxis rather than non-infectious PD urinary urgency.
