Title: Incidence of ochratoxin A in rice and dried fruits from Rabat and Salé area, Morocco
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17364931/
Publisher record: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/02652030600967230
Original article rendition consulted: https://www.academia.edu/5523130/Incidence_of_ochratoxin_A_in_rice_and_dried_fruits_from_Rabat_and_Sale
PMID: 17364931
DOI: 10.1080/02652030600967230
Publication date: 2007-02-13
Date basis: Publisher online date. March 2007 print issue of Food Additives & Contaminants 24(3), pages 285–291.
Text coverage: Author abstract, original sampling methods, Figure 2 and results around page 289.

Zinedine and colleagues bought 20 samples of each of five dried fruit or nut products and 20 rice samples at Rabat and Salé retail shops in July–August 2005. Ochratoxin A was reported in 65% of the 20 dried fig samples. The reported positive fig concentrations ranged from 0.03 to 1.42 ng/g. The result describes one food sampling campaign and does not connect it to a person’s diet or symptoms.

The 2004 doctoral survey used five fig samples from an earlier campaign. The 2008 Juan article used the same five-food counts but acquired its products from January through October 2006 and assayed aflatoxins. These three series are distinct for attribution. Their different methods and sample frames prevent treating the findings as a longitudinal follow-up of particular batches or consumers.
