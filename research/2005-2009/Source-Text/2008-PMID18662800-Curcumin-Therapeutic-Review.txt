Original source scope
The original review cites isolated curcumin reducing thermal hyperalgesia in a diabetic mouse model. It separately describes a rat oral-antigen experiment with different dietary fats, including safflower and coconut oils, assessing intestinal mast-cell degranulation.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The first comparison concerns a constituent and a model of diabetic pain, distant from ground turmeric and Parkinson pain. The oil experiment concerns immune outcomes. Exact cold-pressed safflower or virgin coconut preparation and anxiety or fatigue outcomes were not reported.
