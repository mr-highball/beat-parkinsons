Title: Pharmacological evidence for the potential of Daucus carota in the management of cognitive dysfunctions.
PMID: 16755009
DOI: 10.1248/bpb.29.1154
First published: 2006
Authors: Vasudevan M, Parle M
Journal: Biol Pharm Bull
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16755009/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse study of ethanolic Daucus carota seed extract (100-400 mg/kg oral x 7 d) improving elevated plus maze and passive avoidance memory and inhibiting brain AChE by 19-22%. Plant-part caveat: carrot seed extract rather than edible carrot root, and directly contradicted in direction by Jafarinejad Bajestani 2022.
