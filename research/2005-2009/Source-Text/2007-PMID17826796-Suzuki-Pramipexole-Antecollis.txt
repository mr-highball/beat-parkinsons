Title: Pramipexole-induced antecollis in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022510X07005321
PMID: 17826796
DOI: 10.1016/j.jns.2007.08.008
Publication date: 2007-09-10
Date basis: First online date in the article and NLM record. The issue appeared in January 2008.
Coverage: Author abstract, case and discussion in a public copy of the original pages 195–197.
Original body copy: https://www.academia.edu/97996622/Pramipexole_induced_antecollis_in_Parkinsons_disease

The authors described a single person with Parkinson disease who developed pronounced forward neck flexion after pramipexole. Neck position, restricted visual field and gait improved after stopping it while the background levodopa combination remained stable. The authors interpreted the sequence as medication-associated dystonic posture. There was no scored drooling or food exposure. The same case appears in later case compilations.
