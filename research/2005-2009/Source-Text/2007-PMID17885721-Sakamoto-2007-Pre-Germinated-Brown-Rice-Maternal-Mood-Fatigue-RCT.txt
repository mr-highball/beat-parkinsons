Title: Pre-germinated brown rice could enhance maternal mental health and immunity during lactation.
PMID: 17885721
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-007-0678-3
First published: 2007
Authors: Sakamoto S, Hayashi T, Hayashi K, Murai F, Hori M, Kimoto K
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17885721/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Two-week randomized controlled trial in n=41 breastfeeding mothers consuming cooked pre-germinated brown rice vs polished white rice as staple diet. Pre-germinated brown rice significantly reduced Profile of Mood States (POMS) depression, anger-hostility, fatigue, and total mood disturbance (TMD) scores, increased stress resistance (salivary amylase), and raised breast-milk s-IgA. Limits: pre-germinated rather than standard ungerminated brown rice, postpartum lactating women, short 2-week duration.
