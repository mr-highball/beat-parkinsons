Title: Topical olive oil combination and swallowing due to dry mouth
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1365-2842.2006.01718.x
Format: Factual extract from the cited original.

Ship and colleagues, first published February 22, 2007. Of 40 adults with medication-related dry mouth, 39 completed a two-period crossover. A multi-product topical olive oil, betaine and xylitol regimen was compared with usual care. Figure 2 item 2 assessed swallowing difficulty due to dryness. It is not one of three items with reported between-regimen differences. Aggregate dryness improved (p = .011). Oil grade and Parkinson subgroup were not specified.
