Title: The oral bioavailability of nitrate from nitrate-rich vegetables in humans.
PMID: 18723086
DOI: 10.1016/j.toxlet.2008.07.019
First published: 2008
Authors: van Velzen AG, Sips AJ, Schothorst RC, Lambers AC, Meulenbelt J
Journal: Toxicology letters
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18723086/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Human pharmacokinetic crossover study showing oral nitrate from 300 g cooked beetroot has 106+-15% bioavailability relative to intravenous/aqueous reference, confirming cooked whole beetroot reliably delivers systemic dietary nitrate.
