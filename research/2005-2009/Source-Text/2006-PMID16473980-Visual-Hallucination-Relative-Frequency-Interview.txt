Title: Characteristics of visual hallucinations in Parkinson disease dementia and dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16473980/
PMID: 16473980
DOI: 10.1097/01.jgp.0000192480.89813.80
Publication date: 2006-02
Date basis: NLM journal issue month
Source depth: Primary abstract; full article remains open
Evidence type: Cross-sectional clinical interviews
Format: Factual digest of the original abstract

The study assessed 35 Parkinson dementia and 21 DLB participants already experiencing recurring visual hallucinations. Patients described episode frequency, typical duration and perceived content through a structured interview. Caregivers completed a separate neuropsychiatric inventory. Most patients described complex experiences daily, usually lasting minutes. This recruited sample addresses episode characteristics, with no estimate across all Parkinson patients or comparison by fig intake.
