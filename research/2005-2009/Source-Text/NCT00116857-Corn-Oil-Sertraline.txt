Early coronary disease depression trial registration
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00116857
Registration: NCT00116857
Coverage: Official initial registration, first posted July 1, 2005 (estimated)

The registered study concerns major depression in coronary heart disease, with sertraline provided in both randomized groups. The current registry lists 122 enrollees, week-10 Beck Depression Inventory change and omega-3 versus placebo capsules. The original 2009 publication identifies the placebo as corn oil; the registry first posted its results in 2012.
