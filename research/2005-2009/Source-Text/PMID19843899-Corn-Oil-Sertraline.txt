Corn oil placebo in the earlier coronary disease sertraline trial
Source: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/184742
PMID: 19843899
PMCID: PMC3477788
DOI: 10.1001/jama.2009.1487
Registration: NCT00116857
Coverage: Published final October 21, 2009, Tables 2–3; author manuscript checked separately

Carney and colleagues randomized 122 coronary disease patients with major depression after a sertraline run-in. Sixty received 2 g/day corn oil and 62 received EPA plus DHA, both with sertraline 50 mg/day for 10 weeks. The final article reports unadjusted week-10 BDI-II means 14.8 versus 16.1 and baseline-adjusted corn minus omega group difference −1.26 (95% CI −4.48 to 1.97, p=.44). The adjusted Hamilton difference was +0.14 (−2.15 to 2.44, p=.90). The accessible author manuscript instead gives unadjusted BDI-II means 15.0 and 15.9. Use the final publication for that comparison. The study has no oil-free control, verified refining grade or Parkinson cohort. [Author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3477788/)
