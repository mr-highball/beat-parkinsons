Title: The effects of intra-oral pain on motor cortex neuroplasticity associated with short-term novel tongue-protrusion training in humans
Authors: Shellie Boudreau and colleagues
Date: 2007-09-17 first online
Identifiers: PMID 17870237; DOI 10.1016/j.pain.2007.07.019
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17870237/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the primary abstract.

Nine healthy volunteers learned a novel tongue-protrusion task in separate randomized topical capsaicin and vehicle sessions. Performance averaged higher in the vehicle session. The training-associated enhancement of tongue motor evoked potentials and decrease in motor threshold were reported for the vehicle condition. The tongue task did not contain speech or test oral culinary paprika in Parkinson disease.
