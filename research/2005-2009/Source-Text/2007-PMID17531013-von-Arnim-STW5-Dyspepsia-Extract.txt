Title: STW 5, a phytopharmacon for patients with functional dyspepsia: results of a multicenter, placebo-controlled double-blind study

The trial randomized 315 adults with functional dyspepsia; 308 had intention-to-treat efficacy data. Treatment was 20 drops of STW5 three times daily for eight weeks versus a matched alcoholic placebo. Table 1 specifies nine extracts, including 10 mL lemon balm leaf extract per 100 mL tincture.

The investigator-rated ten-item gastrointestinal score improved 6.9 (SD 4.8) versus 5.9 (SD 4.3) points, reported p=.0407. Treatment-by-center interaction was significant. The diary comparison and symptom-free proportions were inconclusive. Patient-rated tolerability favored placebo, reported p=.0048 for the overall ratings, while adverse-event reporting was similar. The paper describes a small clinical benefit and reports manufacturer funding.

The gastrointestinal composite and dyspepsia quality-of-life measure do not provide a separate depression result in the inspected original. This is a mixed tincture in a non-Parkinson population; no isolated lemon balm, brewed-tea or individual pain effect is established.
