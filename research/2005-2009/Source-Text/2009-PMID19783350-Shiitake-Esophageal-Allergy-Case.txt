Title: Food allergy to Shiitake (Lentinus edodes) manifested as oesophageal symptoms in a patient with probable eosinophilic oesophagitis
Source: https://www.elsevier.es/en-revista-allergologia-et-immunopathologia-105-pdf-download-S0301054609000342

DOI: 10.1016/j.aller.2009.05.002
Citation: Goikoetxea and colleagues, Allergologia et Immunopathologia 37(6), pp. 333–334.
Publication date: 2009
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Complete two-page publisher letter.
Original SHA256: 4b247c0853970bec2c69367dfee1e57266ca2684453b5dc599dbffcfb17293f

An adult reported transient food-stopping and choking after eating shiitake, followed by esophageal discomfort. Other mushrooms were tolerated by history. Fresh shiitake used in skin testing does not establish the preparation ingested. Endoscopic abnormalities and up to nine esophageal eosinophils were documented. The authors describe probable eosinophilic esophagitis and explicitly note that the histology count did not establish the diagnosis. Test results and clinical history support a suspected food-specific reaction. There was no controlled ingestion challenge or Parkinson comparison.

This is a separate clinical trajectory from a mechanically confirmed esophageal obstruction. The available report does not identify a roasted preparation or a comparative Parkinson outcome.
