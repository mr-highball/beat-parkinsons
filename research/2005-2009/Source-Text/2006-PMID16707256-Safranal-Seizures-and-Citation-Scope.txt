Source type: Experimental study of induced seizures in rats.

The original experiment evaluates induced seizure incidence and mortality with isolated safranal administered by two nonculinary routes. One route showed anticonvulsant activity and the central administration comparison did not show a clear effect. The original gives seizure responses and survival. Depression appears only in background citation context.

The 2019 saffron bioavailability review, PMID 31382514, cites this source as reference 110 in a sentence that assigns both antidepressant and anticonvulsant activity to safranal. This accession reports the induced-seizure experiment. Its Introduction directly cites the distinct 2004 forced-swim experiment for antidepressant findings, DOI 10.17660/ActaHortic.2004.650.54. Its Discussion cites Zhang 1994, PMID 8205119, for historical use and the separate extract study. The two citation trails have different sources and outcomes. Neither comparison directly tests the frozen ground-saffron Parkinson claim.
