The full collection was screened for fig fruit, common and botanical aliases, Parkinson disease and visual hallucination outcomes. The fig tokens located in the page text were illustration references. No dried-fig comparison appeared. The recovered text contains 909 tracking-ID markers.

In member 173207 on page 209, a seizure evaluation uncovered biochemical evidence of an insulin-secreting tumor. The clinical history includes episodic visual hallucinations. Low glucose recorded during admission was not accompanied by those symptoms and the abstract does not report a measured link to a specific food or Parkinson disease.

Member 173018 on page 251 describes visual and auditory hallucinations during an acute admission in a person with high reported intake of grapefruit and grapefruit juice and elevated measured amitriptyline concentrations. The authors proposed a food–drug interaction. Concurrent medications, head injury and the uncontrolled course limit interpretation. The member does not report a Parkinson diagnosis or a dried-fig comparison. This is separate from the banana and mocha case 173526 in the same issue.
