Title: Visual scan paths in first-episode schizophrenia and cannabis-induced psychosis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1911188/
PMID: 17653295
PMCID: PMC1911188
DOI: 10.1139/jpn.0727
Publication date: 2007-07
Date basis: July issue, Journal of Psychiatry and Neuroscience 32(4), 267–274. DOI confirmed in Crossref deposit.
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML main text
Format: Original-source factual extract

The study records eye movements while 11 people with first-episode schizophrenia, six with cannabis-induced psychosis and 22 controls viewed images. The outcomes are counts and distributions of gaze fixations and saccades. The discussion mentions clinical visual hallucinations in other reports. The paper does not count those events in a Parkinson cohort or study figs. The figures show visual scan models and other gaze results.
