Title: Grapefruit juice and cabergoline pharmacokinetics in Parkinson disease
First published: 2005-02-05
DOI: 10.1016/j.clpt.2004.12.213
Source: https://ascpt.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1016/j.clpt.2004.12.213
Text type: original conference abstract evidence summary

Nagai and colleagues report that cabergoline plasma concentration was approximately 1.7 times higher when five patients with Parkinson disease took the medicine with grapefruit juice. The abstract does not give uncertainty, a measured rest-tremor or stride change, or a raw-fruit exposure. Its suggestion of a clinical effect is unmeasured. The experiment evaluates a juice–medicine interaction.

A separate November 2005 Nagai and Nomoto article (DOI 10.3999/jscpt.36.6_273) gives a chart of plasma cabergoline with seven days of juice coadministration. Participant overlap with the conference report remains unverified. Its panels on levodopa, food protein and domperidone describe separate comparisons.
