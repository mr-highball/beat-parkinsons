Primary factual extract: Nagai M, Nakatsuka A, Yabe H, Moritoyo T, Nomoto M. Clinical Pharmacology & Therapeutics 77(2):P84, February 5, 2005. DOI 10.1016/j.clpt.2004.12.213.
Five patients with Parkinson disease receiving cabergoline had plasma concentration measured by liquid chromatography tandem mass spectrometry before and after grapefruit juice coadministration. The authors reported approximately 1.7 times the plasma concentration with juice and stated that no adverse events were observed during the study. They supplied no numerical walking or stride outcome, no raw grapefruit comparison and no dose or precision estimate in the abstract.
Companion chart: Nagai M and Nomoto M. Japanese Journal of Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics, 36(6):273–276, November 2005, DOI 10.3999/jscpt.36.6_273. Figure 4 on printed page 275 shows higher plasma cabergoline with seven days of juice coadministration than with cabergoline alone. Cohort overlap with P84 is unverified. The figures using levodopa, domperidone and dietary protein concern separate exposure comparisons.
