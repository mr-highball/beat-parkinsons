Title: Effects of an olive oil-enriched diet on plasma GLP-1 concentration and intestinal content, plasma insulin concentration, and glucose tolerance in normal rats
Publication date: 2005-03
PMID: 15888922
DOI: 10.1385/ENDO:26:2:107
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15888922/
Text coverage: Distilled author abstract and comparison with the 2004 EASD congress abstract 671.

Meal-trained normal rats receiving an olive-oil-enriched diet had an early plasma GLP-1 increment 1.39 ± 0.23 ng/mL greater than the control group (p < 0.001). The groups had no significant intestinal GLP-1 content separation at day 50. The oil-fed group had higher weight gain, and the abstract describes larger insulin responses on days 19 and 36 and better glucose tolerance at day 36. EASD 671 reports the same early GLP-1 estimate, investigator roster, day-36 outcome and day-50 assessment. Treat these records as one study family. The abstract does not document the oil grade, intestinal motility, sleep or affect.
