Title: Fixed herbal drug combination with and without butterbur (Ze 185) for the treatment of patients with somatoform disorders
PMID: 19274698
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.2771
Source: https://doi.org/10.1002/ptr.2771
First published online: 2009-03-09
Text coverage: Original Methods, Tables 2, 3 and 5, and Discussion. Public first-author-uploaded copy verified against journal pages 1303–1308.

The randomized, double-blind two-week study assigned 182 adults with somatoform disorders to four herbal extracts, three extracts, or placebo (63, 58 and 61). Both active tablets contained lemon balm leaf ethanol extract, valerian and passionflower. The four-extract tablet added butterbur.

Table 3 gives Beck Depression Inventory means in the per-protocol group. Four extracts went from 12.6 to 5.6 (n=59), three extracts from 15.1 to 10.5 (n=49), and placebo from 11.9 to 11.3 (n=54). The printed p-values are .0001, .019 and .012 for four versus placebo, three versus placebo, and four versus three. Methods describe stratified nonparametric end-of-study comparisons. Table intervals describe individual group means. Table 5 reports at least 50% improvement in 33/59, 17/49 and 7/54.

The article reports 167 in the intention-to-treat analysis and 162 per protocol. Its description of 15 additional exclusions cannot be reconciled with the five-person difference in Table 2. Depression outcomes from the combination cannot isolate lemon balm. The active-arm comparison varies butterbur. No brew or Parkinson cohort was studied.
