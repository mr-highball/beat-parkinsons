Lemongrass in a patented multicomponent sleep beverage
Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2009015454A1/en
Coverage: Original public patent abstract, description and claims

The international application first published February 5, 2009. It describes a concentrated drink with brewed Cymbopogon citratus leaves, passion fruit, chamomile and lemon balm. The inventor proposes it for sleep and insomnia. The document supplies a formulation and manufacturing description, with no enrolled sample, insomnia scale, dream observations or controlled outcome comparison. The later Brazilian publication BRPI0703312A2 describes the same family.
