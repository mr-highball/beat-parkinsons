Title: Influence of the chirality of (R)-(-)- and (S)-(+)-carvone in the central nervous system: a comparative study.
PMID: 17299731
DOI: 10.1002/chir.20379
First published: 2007
Authors: de Sousa DP, de Farias Nóbrega FF, de Almeida RN
Journal: Chirality
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17299731/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse neuropharmacology study (de Sousa, de Farias Nobrega & de Almeida, 2007, Chirality) comparing (S)-(+)-carvone (major constituent of dill seed and caraway essential oils) and (R)-(-)-carvone (spearmint). (S)-(+)-Carvone (LD50 484.2 mg/kg) decreased ambulation and touch response, increased sedation/ptosis, produced antinociception, potentiated pentobarbital sleeping time (200 mg/kg), and increased PTZ/picrotoxin convulsion latency. Limits: isolated monoterpene at high parenteral/pharmacological doses in non-PD mice; cannot directly sign culinary toasted/ground dill seed claims.
