Title: Herbs containing L- Dopa: An update.
PMID: 22557260
PMCID: PMC3330839
First published: 2007
Authors: Ramya KB, Thaakur S
Journal: Ancient science of life
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22557260/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Secondary narrative review (Ramya & Thaakur, 2007, Anc Sci Life, PMC3330839) compiling older literature tables of L-DOPA-containing plants, citing a 2003 table listing 0.45% L-DOPA in Vigna unguiculata seeds while explicitly stating that animal and clinical anti-Parkinson efficacy remained unestablished. Subsequent 2025 LC-QToF profiling (PMID:40002056) did not detect L-DOPA in Vigna unguiculata seeds.
