Baseline hallucination and apathy scores in Parkinson dementia
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2117797/

July 4, 2006 online and January 2007 issue. Aarsland and colleagues use baseline Neuropsychiatric Inventory data from 537 Parkinson dementia patients recruited to the parent rivastigmine trial. Hallucinations were recorded in 236 and apathy in 291. The mean apathy score was 2.95 with SD 3.43. These descriptive data concern symptom prevalence and scores in one parent cohort. There are no episode counts or fig or date-fruit comparisons.
