Original source scope
The study describes purified eugenol and structural analogs in enzyme comparisons. A selected subset was also examined in mouse forced-swim tests. Irie and Keung were coauthors. This extends the compound-level investigation and cites the earlier behavioral work. The current scope does not establish independent replication of all earlier eugenol outcomes. The botanical motivation concerns Acorus, with no grated nutmeg or Parkinson depression test. Round 1 claim 0042.
