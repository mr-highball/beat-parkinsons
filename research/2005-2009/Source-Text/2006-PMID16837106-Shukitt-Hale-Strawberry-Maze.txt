Title: Beneficial effects of fruit extracts on neuronal function and behavior in a rodent model of accelerated aging
PMID: 16837106
DOI: 10.1016/j.neurobiolaging.2006.05.031
Publication: First online July 11, 2006; print August 2007
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16837106/
Investigators' technical abstract: https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=190020
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Original author abstract, USDA technical summary and available publisher methods excerpt

Shukitt-Hale and colleagues compared 2% strawberry or blueberry extract diets with control feed before an irradiation exposure in rats. The visible author reports describe Morris water maze and striatal dopamine release one month after irradiation. Strawberry-fed rats better retained learned platform location compared with controls. The authors describe a separate learning contrast for blueberry. These outcomes concern cognition and tissue signaling rather than recurrence of spontaneous falls.

The complete methods and figures remain open. Preparation, endpoint and possible cohort overlap with the same-dose earlier irradiation research must be tracked before treating the report as an independent result.
