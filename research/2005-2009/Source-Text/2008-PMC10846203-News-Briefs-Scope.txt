Complete 10-page publisher-rendered PDF corroborated by official NCBI search text. p107 reports a grape juice and wine polyphenol item. p109 summarizes a dementia-risk cohort about dietary oils, and p112 is a separate Parkinson pathology piece. The brief includes flaxseed in its description of omega-3 oils. The underlying Three-City publication specifies rapeseed, walnut and soybean oil for that analytic category. Read the medical-news report and original cohort as one evidence family.
