Title: Artichoke leave extract for chronic hepatitis C - a pilot study.
PMID: 19427774
DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2009.03.007
First published: 2009
Authors: Huber, Müller, Naumann, Schenk, Lüdtke
Journal: Phytomedicine : international journal of phytotherapy and phytopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19427774/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-week uncontrolled single-arm pilot trial in 17 patients with chronic hepatitis C given 3200 mg/d standardized artichoke leaf extract (ALE). Primary outcome ALT normalization occurred in 0/17 patients; aminotransferases and viral load were unchanged. Secondary subjective fatigue and joint problems significantly improved at week 4, but showed no significant difference from baseline at week 12. Tolerability was good to excellent. Limits: uncontrolled n=17 pilot, transient week-4 effect null by week 12, leaf extract.
