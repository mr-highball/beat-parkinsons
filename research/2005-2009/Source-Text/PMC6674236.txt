The frontal cortex comparison examined mitochondrial preparations from 10 Parkinson and 12 control samples. The biochemical results concern complex I oxidative damage, assembly and function. The case-history table records visual hallucinations in one Parkinson donor with a history of seizures. The paper does not systematically ascertain visual symptoms, estimate episode frequency or compare any fig preparation.
