Oral isolated carvacrol in mouse anxiety and sleep assays
2009-11-10
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1472-8206.2009.00788.x

Melo and colleagues report increased elevated plus-maze open-arm behavior after isolated oral carvacrol in male mice. The abstract reports no evident change in rotarod, open-field crossing, or drug-induced sleep duration and latency. Flumazenil reversed the maze result. The journal body remains unavailable. Thyme and oregano are named as plant sources of carvacrol. Neither was fed as a culinary herb. The author's related 2010 thesis supplies figures from closely matching experiments.
