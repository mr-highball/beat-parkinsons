The article reviews canine feeding studies by its authors. Table 2 identifies carrot granules and tomato pomace among five produce components. Each ingredient contributed 1 percent of the enriched diet in place of corn, alongside supplements. Aged dogs showed some favorable landmark and discrimination task results. There is no isolated roasted-carrot, cooked-tomato or Parkinson clinical estimate.
