Guénette SA, Ross A, Marier JF, Beaudry F, Vachon P. Pharmacokinetics of eugenol and its effects on thermal hypersensitivity in rats. Eur J Pharmacol. 2007;562(1-2):60–67. First online February 1, 2007 per original first page. PMID 17321520; DOI 10.1016/j.ejphar.2007.01.044. Coverage: original Methods, Results and Figures 3–4, opened via https://www.academia.edu/6158642/Pharmacokinetics_of_eugenol_and_its_effects_on_thermal_hypersensitivity_in_rats

Six rats per behavior group were assigned to daily oral gavage with isolated eugenol 40 mg/kg or saline after sciatic nerve cuff implantation, for five days. Authors balanced pretreatment thermal responsiveness. Eugenol solution included excipients and the reported comparison was saline. The pharmacokinetic arm is a separate experiment within this report.

A thermal withdrawal effect was significant only on day five under the stated p < .01 correction, at 11.4 ± 1.23 versus 6.1 ± 0.53 seconds (mean ± SEM). Pressure response to von Frey filaments showed no significant group difference. The cuffed model did not develop verified allodynia, limiting interpretation of the mechanical test. These are evoked responses to isolated eugenol, not ground clove ingestion or a clinical pain outcome.
