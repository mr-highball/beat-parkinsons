BMJ investigative report documenting decade-long editorial and statistical investigations into suspected data fabrication across Ram B. Singh's Moradabad dietary intervention cardiology trials from the early 1990s through 2002, published alongside statistical fabrication analysis (Al-Marzouki et al. BMJ 2005, PMID:16052019, PMC1181267) and the Lancet Expression of Concern (Horton 2005, PMID:16054927).
