Title: Special low-protein foods ameliorate postprandial off in patients with advanced Parkinson's disease
Publication date: 2006-10
PMID: 16773618
DOI: 10.1002/mds.21003
Citation: Barichella M and colleagues. Movement Disorders 21:1682–1687.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16773618/
Coverage: Author abstract. The full tables and individual symptom record remain open.

The randomized, single-blind crossover pilot compared two months on each of a balanced diet and a diet using special low-protein products earlier in the day. Eighteen treated Parkinson participants had motor fluctuations. The authors report postprandial OFF time of 49 ± 73 versus 79 ± 72 minutes and total OFF time of 164 ± 148 versus 271 ± 174 minutes for the product and balanced diets, with p < .0001 for both comparisons. Nine participants did not report subjective benefit and the authors report that subgroup's total OFF time also favored the diet containing the products. OFF time does not isolate resting-tremor severity, and the abstract gives no baked turkey contrast.
