Title: Validation of a new scale for the evaluation of sialorrhea in patients with Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17089393/
PMID: 17089393
DOI: 10.1002/mds.21152
Publication date: 2006-11-06
Date basis: First online date stated on the original article. The journal issue appeared in January 2007.
Coverage: Paraphrased original author abstract and verified printed date line.

The authors evaluated the seven-item SCS-PD using 39 people with Parkinson disease for internal consistency. The abstract reports a second phase involving saliva-volume measurements in 49 people with Parkinson disease and 27 controls. Internal consistency was .78. Scale scores and volume had a modest correlation (r=.41, p=.004). People reporting drooling had higher scale scores without a distinct saliva-volume difference in the reported comparison. The scale evaluates symptom-related discomfort and functional burden. It supplies no measured response to fig.
