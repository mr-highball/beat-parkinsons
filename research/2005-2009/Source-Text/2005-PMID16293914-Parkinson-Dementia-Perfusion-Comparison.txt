The comparison included 30 Parkinson participants with dementia, 22 without dementia, and 10 controls. The study compared regional SPECT perfusion and assessed cognitive status. The Parkinson dementia group had lower occipital perfusion in the reported comparison. A cognitive score correlated with occipital perfusion. Dementia status, cognitive performance and perfusion are different endpoints from occurrence or frequency of hallucinations. The paper does not measure a dried-fig exposure.
