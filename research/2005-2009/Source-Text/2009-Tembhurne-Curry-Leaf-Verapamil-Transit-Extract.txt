Two mouse transit reports with shared control and extract data
Source: https://www.apsronline.com/Documents/October%202009/01022009015.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

Tembhurne and Sakarkar extracted shade-dried curry leaf powder with ethanol or petroleum ether and measured small-intestinal charcoal progression in mice. Table 1 gives 60.74 ± 2.46% in vehicle and 76.43 ± 2.73% and 74.92 ± 3.72% in the higher-dose ethanol and petroleum-ether arms. The vehicle and four extract-only means and SDs recur exactly in the authors' 2010 atropine paper. A row comparison preserves one shared basic data set plus the different pharmacological challenge arms. The 2009 Discussion reverses the extract order against the table. The outcome measures intestinal movement after a charcoal meal.
