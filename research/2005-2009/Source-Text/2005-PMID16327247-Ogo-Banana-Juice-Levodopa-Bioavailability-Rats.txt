Title: Banana juice reduces bioavailability of levodopa preparation.
PMID: 16327247
DOI: 10.1248/yakushi.125.1009
First published: 2005
Authors: Ogo Y, Sunagane N, Ohta T, Uruno T
Journal: Yakugaku zasshi : Journal of the Pharmaceutical Society of Japan
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16327247/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Rat pharmacokinetic study examining co-administration of EC-Doparl levodopa tablets with fresh banana juice (fresh banana mixed with water) vs commercial 10% banana beverage. Fresh banana juice significantly halved levodopa Cmax (17.4+/-2.5 vs 8.6+/-3.1 ug/mL, alpha=0.05) and AUC (1882.8+/-49.2 vs 933.5+/-286.6 ug*min/mL, alpha=0.05), whereas commercial 10% banana beverage caused no significant PK change. Limits: rodent pharmacokinetic surrogate without clinical PD motor or daily OFF-time measurements.
