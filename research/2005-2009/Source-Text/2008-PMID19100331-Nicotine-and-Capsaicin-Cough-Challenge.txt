Title: Nicotine, cough provocation and anxiety measurement
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19100331/
PMID: 19100331
PMCID: PMC3121142
DOI: 10.1016/j.pupt.2008.11.013
Publication date: 2008-12-07
Date basis: PubMed electronic publication; journal issue followed in 2009.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: Author abstract and accessible methods excerpt.

Nicotine withdrawal and administration were the anxiety-related contrasts. Inhaled capsaicin was used to provoke cough and measure the urge to cough. The protocol did not compare ground-cayenne intake, and result attribution concerns nicotine or smoking state rather than the cough-provoking material.
