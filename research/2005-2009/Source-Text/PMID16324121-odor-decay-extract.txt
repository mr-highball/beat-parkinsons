Title: Short-term survival of newborn neurons in the adult olfactory bulb after exposure to a complex odor environment
Authors: Christelle Rochefort and Pierre-Marie Lledo
First published: 25 November 2005
Journal: European Journal of Neuroscience 22(11), 2863–2870
DOI: 10.1111/j.1460-9568.2005.04486.x
PMID: 16324121
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1460-9568.2005.04486.x
Coverage: Original factual extract of the accessible primary abstract. The exact methods and full quantitative results remain under retrieval.

The authors counted survival of BrdU-labeled adult mouse olfactory bulb cells after a period of olfactory enrichment. More labeled cells were present immediately after release from enriched housing, with cell counts back at the control level a month later. The authors also report the boost in short-term odor-memory performance lasted less than a month. The published abstract supplies no tarragon-only result or exact odor list. A 2002 same-laboratory experiment with tarragon in its odor set is independently documented. The 2005 protocol and any reused groups are unverified.
