The primary review document has separate herbal sections, including Melissa officinalis and Salvia officinalis, and references the authors' prior trials. For Melissa, reference 48 concerns acute mood and cognition in healthy volunteers. Reference 49 is the 42-person Alzheimer dementia extract trial, with cognition scales and agitation from adverse-event reporting. Treating its earlier publication as fresh evidence would duplicate the cohort. Subsequent replication work used topical oil rather than oral leaf extract. The review summary here preserves the endpoint and population of the studies it cites.
