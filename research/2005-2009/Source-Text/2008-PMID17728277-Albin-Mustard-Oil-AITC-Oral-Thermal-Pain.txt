Title: Modulation of oral heat and cold pain by irritant chemicals
PMID: 17728277
DOI: 10.1093/chemse/bjm056
First published: 2008
Authors: Albin KC, Carstens MI, Carstens E
Journal: Chemical Senses
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17728277/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Human half-tongue 2-AFC and pain-rating study testing lingual mustard oil (AITC), capsaicin, cinnamaldehyde, and menthol against evoked thermal pain. Mustard oil acutely enhanced lingual heat pain (49 C) and weakly enhanced lingual cold pain (9.5 C). Acute local lingual thermal hyperalgesia in healthy volunteers using concentrated mustard oil, not culinary grated horseradish or systemic PD pain.
