The June–July 2008 Copenhagen proceedings include a Chen-led caffeine and striatal barrier study on supplement page S59. Male FVB mice received intraperitoneal caffeine or saline before MPTP exposure. Barrier leakage and tight junction endpoints were region-specific. The 2008 full report (PMID 18808450) is a companion from the same team with the same main design. Other members report caffeine for memory tasks, receptor physiology and exposure during pregnancy. These proceedings compare isolated compounds and animal or laboratory outcomes. They supply no brewed coffee or lemon-balm depression comparison in Parkinson disease.
