Title: Tiredness after acute caffeine with usual use or prior abstention
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2941158/
PMID: 19777214
PMCID: PMC2941158
DOI: 10.1007/s00213-009-1668-3
Publication date: 2009-09-24
Date basis: First electronic publication.
Review depth: Complete identified PMC structured original, measures, mood analysis and Figure 1.

Seventeen regular caffeine consumers completed four within-person days, receiving caffeine or placebo after either 30-hour abstention or their normal dietary caffeine use. The authors report state, drug and time effects for tiredness. The condition-specific response in mood and tiredness favored acute caffeine after abstention. The same response was not established under usual use. Some selective attention outcomes favored caffeine in both states, which is a different endpoint.

The experimental agent was isolated caffeine. No controlled same-beverage contrast, whole green tea exposure or Parkinson diagnosis was present. A transient tiredness rating in the abstained state provides contextual evidence for interpreting prior beverage tests.
