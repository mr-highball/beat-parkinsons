Text coverage: Curated German dermatology reference entries

The book's entries list strawberries among foods discussed in the
context of allergy, intolerance and possible pseudoallergic skin
reactions. Entries about strawberry tongue and strawberry naevus
use a descriptive comparison for a clinical sign. These phrases
do not identify fruit exposure.

The food-related entries give definitions and practice context.
They do not provide a strawberry-specific intake comparison, a
reaction frequency estimate or a Parkinson fall outcome. The
1,025-page original PDF supplies the relevant surrounding entries.
