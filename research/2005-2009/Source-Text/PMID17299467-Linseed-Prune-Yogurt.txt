Linseed in a mixed yogurt constipation trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17299467/
PMID: 17299467
DOI: 10.1038/sj.ejcn.1602670
Coverage: Original NLM author abstract, first online February 14, 2007

Sairanen and colleagues randomized a crossover in 43 older adults with self-reported constipation. The test yogurt supplied 6 g/day linseed, 12 g/day prunes and 12 g/day galacto-oligosaccharides. Each test or control period lasted three weeks. Mean bowel frequency was 8.0 versus 7.1 per week (p=.011) and ease-of-defecation scores also favored the blend (p=.010). The author abstract calls the ingredient linseed and provides no oil-only group. The later OTC review labels this ingredient linseed oil. Preserve the original exposure wording and the combined formulation.
