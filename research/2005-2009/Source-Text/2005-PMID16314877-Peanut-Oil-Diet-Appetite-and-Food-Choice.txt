Title: Effects of peanut oil consumption on appetite and food choice
PMID: 16314877
DOI: 10.1038/sj.ijo.0803180
Publication date: 2005-11-29
Citation: Iyer and colleagues. International Journal of Obesity 30(4):704–710, April 2006 issue.
Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/0803180
Text coverage: Publisher abstract, with the energy and physical activity report checked separately.

The controlled study assigned 129 healthy adults in three countries to peanut oil, olive oil, safflower oil or a no-intervention control for eight weeks. The investigators report no clear treatment-by-time effects in appetitive ratings. The peanut oil group's estimated dietary compensation was 46%. The 2007 paper by Akuamoah-Boateng and colleagues describes the same participant count, sites and four arms and directly cites the appetite report as a previous result from the intervention. Keep the activity and appetite reports together when counting families. The exposures have no stated cold-pressing grade in this abstract, and tremor was not an outcome.
