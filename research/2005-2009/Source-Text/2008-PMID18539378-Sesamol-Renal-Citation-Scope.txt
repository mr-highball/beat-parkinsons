Original source scope and evidence boundary
Reference 181 in the 2013 Huntington botanical review (PMID 24347915) concerns acute renal injury in mice. The authors report improved renal measures and reductions in oxidative injury after sesamol. The experiment assessed kidney function and biochemical outcomes. It does not establish the adjacent hippocampal-memory claim or a direction for Parkinson anxiety, depression, fatigue or dystonia. Complete renal tables were not verified and are unnecessary for the anatomical boundary established in the author abstract.
