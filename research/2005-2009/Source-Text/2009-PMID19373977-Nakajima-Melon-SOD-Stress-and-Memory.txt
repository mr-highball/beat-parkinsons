Title: Oral supplementation with melon superoxide dismutase extract promotes antioxidant defences in the brain and prevents stress-induced impairment of spatial memory
PMID: 19373977
DOI: 10.1016/j.bbr.2008.12.038
Publication date: 2009-01-23
Citation: Nakajima and colleagues. Behavioural Brain Research. June 8, 2009 issue.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19373977/
Author copy: https://researchmap.jp/toshiomikami/published_papers/11344856/attachment_file.pdf
Coverage: Original paper, Methods 2.1 and Results.

The authors compared GliSODin, vitamin E, a restraint-stressed group and a group without restraint. After five weeks the melon-enzyme preparation favored spatial learning in a water maze relative to stressed controls. The authors also report differences in hippocampal lipid-peroxidation and cell-proliferation markers. The intervention was a formulated extract in mice and the reported outcomes concern restraint stress and cognition.
