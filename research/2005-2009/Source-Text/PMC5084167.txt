The review and follow-up account includes the six-person unilateral pilot previously described by Bakay and Stover. Page 255 reports new visual hallucinations in three of six and peak-dose dyskinesia in four of six. Its summary groups their resolution after medication reduction. The 2005 primary adverse-event narrative specifies a medication reduction in one of the three visual cases and spontaneous resolution in the other two. These are repeated records from the same pilot.

The 2008 article also describes the design of a subsequent bilateral sham-controlled trial and reports motor outcomes by follow-up interval for the earlier pilot. Figure 3 charts the pilot's motor score. It is not a visual-hallucination rate or a food exposure. The publications do not assess dried figs.
