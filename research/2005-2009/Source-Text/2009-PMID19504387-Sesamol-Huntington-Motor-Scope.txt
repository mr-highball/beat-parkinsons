Original source scope and evidence boundary
Reference 180 in the 2013 Huntington botanical review (PMID 24347915) tests isolated sesamol in a rat toxin-injury model. The investigators report better locomotor activity and coordination, with accompanying biochemical outcomes. The accessible text provides no dystonic posture scale, no freezing of gait measure, and no isolated anxiety, depression or fatigue score. It uses isolated sesamol instead of dietary sesame oil. The 2010 memory report (PMID 20102363) has the same author team and a related model. Cohort overlap has not been established. Both reports contribute one provisional source family until their methods can be compared. The original publisher route did not expose its full results or figures.
