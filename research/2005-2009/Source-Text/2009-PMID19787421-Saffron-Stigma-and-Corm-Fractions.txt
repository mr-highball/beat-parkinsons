Title: Distinct saffron stigma and corm fractions in behavioral experiments
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11418-009-0360-6
Source abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19787421/
PMID: 19787421
DOI: 10.1007/s11418-009-0360-6
Publication date: 2009-09-29
Date basis: First online journal publication, ahead of the 2010 issue.
Review basis: Original author abstract, matched against publisher preview. Full methods and tables were subscription restricted.
Source type: Controlled preclinical comparison.

Wang and colleagues report less immobility in forced-swim and tail-suspension assays after two extracted corm fractions, without clear concurrent change in open-field ambulation. They also report a distinct behavioral signal for an aqueous stigma extract, which was characterized for crocin components. Corm and stigma results come from distinct preparations in one paper. The abstract does not provide enough numerical outcome data to compare the magnitudes, uncertainty or sample-size details across assays.

For Round 1 claim 0063, this concerns animal behavior after fraction or extract administration. It does not isolate clinical depression after consumption of ground saffron or assign an outcome in Parkinson disease. Keep the plant-part distinction when consulting this report.
