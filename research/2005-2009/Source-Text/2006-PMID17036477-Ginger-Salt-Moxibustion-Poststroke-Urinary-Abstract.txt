Title: Randomized controlled study on ginger-salt-partitioned moxibustion on urination disorders poststroke
PMID: 17036477
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17036477/
Date basis: September 2006 issue, Zhongguo Zhen Jiu 26(9), pp. 621–624.
Coverage: Factual extract of the original indexed author abstract. Original Chinese tables remain open.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The authors randomized 82 poststroke patients with neurogenic bladder symptoms to routine acupuncture with or without a topical ginger and salt moxibustion procedure. Thirty-nine intervention and 36 control participants completed the three-week study. The abstract reports differences in selected daytime and nighttime urinary outcomes, with no stool or constipation assessment.
