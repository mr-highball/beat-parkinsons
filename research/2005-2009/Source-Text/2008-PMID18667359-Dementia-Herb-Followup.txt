Kalaria and colleagues reviewed dementia prevalence, clinical practice and management in developing countries. The treatment section mentions sage (Salvia officinalis and Salvia lavandulaefolia) and lemon balm (Melissa officinalis) as research leads for behavior or cognition. The discussion calls for larger studies. The herbal statement is review-level context, alongside other medicines, and adds no new cohort, culinary preparation, Parkinson diagnosis or depressive symptom endpoint. The local sage and lemon-balm 2003 primary records give the original Alzheimer trial comparisons.
