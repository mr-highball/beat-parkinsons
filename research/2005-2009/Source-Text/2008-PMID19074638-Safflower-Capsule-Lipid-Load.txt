Title: Brief oral stimulation, but especially oral fat exposure, elevates serum triglycerides in humans
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2643923/
PMCID: PMC2643923
PMID: 19074638
DOI: 10.1152/ajpgi.90591.2008
First public article date: 2008-12-12
Journal issue: 2009
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled original indexed abstract.

Thirty-four healthy adults swallowed capsules with a 50 g safflower oil load plus water. The protocol used capsules to bypass oral exposure to the load and then varied separate sensory stimuli. Early triglyceride response frequencies were 88.2% with full-fat, 70.5% with nonfat, and 50% with no oral stimulation. The response is metabolic. Oral exposure, ease of taking capsules and diagnostic swallowing testing are distinct. There was no monitored Parkinson swallowing result.
