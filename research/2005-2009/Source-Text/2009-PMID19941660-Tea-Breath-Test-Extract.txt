Title: Blood profile holds clues to role of infection in a premonitory state for idiopathic parkinsonism and of gastrointestinal infection in established disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2795757/
PMID: 19941660
PMCID: PMC2795757
DOI: 10.1186/1757-4749-1-20
Publication: 2009-11-27
Depth: Factual extract from the original Methods and Results.

Charlett and colleagues list black tea, coffee or water as breakfast options in a breath-testing protocol. They do not give randomized beverage assignment or beverage-specific effects. The clinical Results describe separate resting and postural tremor findings in the examined control group, but report them by immune measures and not by beverage. The study gives no change in Parkinson rest-tremor severity after brewed black tea.
