The trial assigned 54 people with mild to moderate Alzheimer disease to encapsulated standardized dried stigma extract or donepezil. The primary outcomes were cognition and global dementia staging. The preparation section identifies an ethanol-derived extract from dried and milled saffron stigmas.

Table 2 on printed page 641 reports fatigue in one of 27 assigned extract and four of 27 assigned donepezil, with reported Fisher p=.35. These are adverse-event counts. The active-comparator design and small counts leave an absolute effect on fatigue severity unresolved. This supplies context for Round 1 claim 0485 without substituting Alzheimer disease and an extract for Parkinson disease and dried culinary saffron.
