Title: Effects of Applephenon and ascorbic acid on physical fatigue.
PMID: 17483009
DOI: 10.1016/j.nut.2007.03.002
First published: 2007
Authors: Ataka S, Tanaka M, Nozaki S, Mizuma H, Mizuno K, Tahara T et al.
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17483009/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled three-way crossover RCT in n=18 healthy adult volunteers randomized to oral Applephenon (apple polyphenol extract, 1200 mg/day), ascorbic acid (1000 mg/day), or placebo for 8 days per period. During a 4-hour bicycle ergometer workload protocol, Applephenon significantly attenuated the physical-fatigue decline in 10-second maximum non-workload cycling velocity between the 30-min and 210-min trials compared with placebo (p<0.05), whereas ascorbic acid had no effect. Limits: healthy non-PD volunteers, physical ergometer fatigue rather than PD fatigue scales, and isolated apple polyphenol extract rather than raw whole apple.
