A chicken-wing tip is used as a spectral-imaging specimen. This is a genuine animal-derived material in an imaging-method experiment, not a dietary chicken intervention. Neither poached preparation nor fatigue or Parkinson symptom effects are measured. Respiratory depression elsewhere in the collection is not depressive symptom severity.

The specimen lead remains related with its route and endpoint limitations. Separate abstracts cannot supply an ingredient–symptom comparison, and a later archive deposit does not change the original publication period.
