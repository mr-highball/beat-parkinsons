Liston RE. The Short-Term Effects of Milk Consumption on Selected Perceptual and Objective Measures of Vocal Fold Function and Appearance. University of Kansas catalog identifies December 2005 dissertation, lists PhD-ME 2006. Institutional abstract https://music.ku.edu/memt-dissertation-and-thesis-catalog . N=30 drank milk, cola, orange juice and water; self-reported mouth/throat feel, sustained /a/ voice measures jitter, shimmer and noise-to-harmonics and stroboscopic mucus ratings were used. Abstract reports increased mucus ratings after each beverage and no significant acoustic measure differences. It does not provide a listener intelligibility endpoint, identifiable Parkinson group, milk species/processing, detailed comparison estimates or full methods. Full dissertation not located.
