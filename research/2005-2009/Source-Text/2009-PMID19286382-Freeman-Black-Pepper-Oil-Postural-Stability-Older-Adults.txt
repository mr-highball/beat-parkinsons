Title: Olfactory stimuli and enhanced postural stability in older adults.
PMID: 19286382
DOI: 10.1016/j.gaitpost.2009.02.005
First published: 2009
Authors: Freeman S, Ebihara S, Ebihara T, Niu K, Kohzuki M, Arai H
Journal: Gait & posture
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19286382/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled within-subject force-plate experiment in n=17 older adults (mean age 78+/-6 y, neurological deficits including Parkinson disease explicitly excluded) comparing brief olfactory stimulation with black pepper essential oil, lavender oil, and distilled-water sham during quiet standing (eyes open and eyes closed). With eyes closed, black pepper oil significantly decreased mediolateral (ML) RMS velocity (p<0.05), anteroposterior (AP) RMS velocity (p<0.01), and total center-of-pressure (CoP) trajectory length (p<0.001) vs baseline (sham NS); eyes-open effects were minimal/NS and RMS displacements were mostly non-significant. Limits: inhaled essential oil (not ingested cracked black pepper), acute laboratory sway in non-PD older adults.
