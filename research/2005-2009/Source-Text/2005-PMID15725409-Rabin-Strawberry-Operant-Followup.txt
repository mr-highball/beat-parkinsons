Title: Effects of age and diet on the heavy particle-induced disruption of operant responding produced by a ground-based model for exposure to cosmic rays
PMID: 15725409
DOI: 10.1016/j.brainres.2004.12.041
Publication: Brain Research, volume 1036, pages 122–129, March 2, 2005
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15725409/
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original author abstract. Full methods and figures remain open.

In this separate experiment the rats ate 2% blueberry or strawberry extract for two months before 1.5 Gy iron-ion irradiation. At six months the abstract reports no group differences on the ascending fixed-ratio operant task. At twelve months, the irradiated strawberry group performed better than the irradiated blueberry and control-diet groups on the task. The paper's abstract does not provide arm-level intervals. This is a different radiation dose and timing from PMID 23598605, with no clinical falls endpoint.
