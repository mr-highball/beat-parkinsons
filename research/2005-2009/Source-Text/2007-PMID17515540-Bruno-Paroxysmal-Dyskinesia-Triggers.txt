Title: Genotype-phenotype correlation of paroxysmal nonkinesigenic dyskinesia
PMID: 17515540
DOI: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000262029.91552.e0
Publication: Neurology, May 22, 2007
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17515540/
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original author abstract

Bruno and colleagues compare familial episodic movement presentations with and without variants in the gene then called MR-1. The author summary describes caffeine and alcohol as common precipitants in the mutation-associated phenotype. Other families have different triggers, with some reports of prolonged exercise preceding attacks. These are clinical ascertainment and genotype-phenotype observations. They supply neither a brewed coffee treatment comparison nor a Parkinson symptom result.
