BRIEF SUMMARY

This study assesses dopamine transporter density using single photon emission computed
tomography (SPECT) brain imaging with an investigational radiopharmaceutical,
[123I]ß-CIT, in research participants with Parkinson's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

The brain imaging is conducted at the Institute for Neurodegenerative Disorders in New
Haven, Connecticut. The imaging procedure occurs over a two day period.

After a screening visit, including review of the potential subject's neurological history
and a thorough neurologic exam, subjects are injected with [123I]ß CIT, an
investigational radioactive material that localizes in the brain.

Twenty-four hours later study participants return to the Institute for Neurodegenerative
Disorders where an investigational scanning procedure is used to obtain SPECT (single
photon emission computed tomography) images of the brain.
