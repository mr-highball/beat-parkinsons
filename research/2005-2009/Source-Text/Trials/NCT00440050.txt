Title: DHA (Docosahexaenoic Acid), an Omega 3 Fatty Acid, in Slowing the Progression of Alzheimer's Disease
ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT00440050
First public posting: 2007-02-26 (estimated)
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00440050
API record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00440050
Coverage: Factual extract of registration, intervention and population fields.

Randomized parallel intervention in mild to moderate Alzheimer disease, actual enrollment 402. The placebo schedule was two placebo capsules twice daily for 18 months. Current registry intervention descriptions give no oil identity. The trial paper (PMID 21045096) specifies corn or soy oil in the placebo capsules and gives no separate arm or outcome for either oil.
