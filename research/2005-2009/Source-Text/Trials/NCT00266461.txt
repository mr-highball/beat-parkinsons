Title: Daikenchuto tolerability after large bowel resection
NCT ID: NCT00266461
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00266461
First posted: 2005-12-16
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00266461
Archive scope: Factual summary of the registered design, outcomes and results status.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The official record describes a completed open randomized comparison of two TU-100 doses and water. Actual enrollment was 24. The prespecified primary endpoint is treatment-emergent adverse events during postoperative ileus follow-up. The registry has no posted results. Registry protocol TU100P2T1 and the 24-person cohort distinguish this comparison from the 60-person healthy transit study and the 45-person constipation study. The publication link remains open.
