BRIEF SUMMARY

The primary purpose of this study is to evaluate clinical and serologic outcomes after
the discontinuation of liatermin therapy as assessed by development of new clinical
conditions beyond those expected in patients with advancing Parkinson's disease. In
addition, the development or resolution of anti-r-metHuGDNF binding and neutralizing
antibodies will be monitored.
