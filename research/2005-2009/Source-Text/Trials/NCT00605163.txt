BRIEF SUMMARY

The purpose of this study is to longitudinally follow consenting clinical trial
participants who participated in PRECEPT (A Randomized, Double Blind, Placebo Controlled,
Dose Finding Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of CEP 1347 in Patients With Early
Parkinson's Disease). The study will assess the clinical and imaging outcomes relevant to
the natural history of Parkinson's disease (PD), as well as determine early biomarkers of
the disease.
