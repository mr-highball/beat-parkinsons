OFFICIAL TITLE

Randomized, Multicenter, Prospective, Parallel-Group Study of the Effect of Deep Brain Stimulation (Subthalamic Nucleus) on Quality of Life and Motor Complications in Patients With Advanced Parkinson's Disease Compared With Best Medical Treatment

LEAD SPONSOR

German Parkinson Study Group (GPS)

Overall status: COMPLETED
Start date
  Date: 2001-01
Completion date
  Date: 2005-01

BRIEF SUMMARY

Assessment of the therapeutic effect of deep brain stimulation of the subthalamic nucleus
compared with drug treatment alone in patients with advanced Parkinson's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Patients are randomized either to medical treatment or subthalamic stimulation. The
observation period was 6 months. The primary outcome criterium: PDQ-39 and UPDRS III.
