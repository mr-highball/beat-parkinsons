BRIEF SUMMARY

The purpose of this study is to develop a task to measure executive thinking skills that
can be performed in a laboratory setting, but has clear parallels to the thinking demands
of real life situations, and can be administered to patients such as those with
Parkinson's who have difficulty moving.
