NCT00848172 — Double-Blind, Placebo Controlled Pilot Study of Octanoic Acid in Essential Tremor
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00848172
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Coverage: Selected official registration fields, summarized as plain text.

First public registration: 2009-02-20.
Status: Completed. First public results: 2012-12-27.
Population: Adults with alcohol-responsive essential tremor.
Interventions: Octanoic acid in one period and an identical-appearing placebo in the other.
Primary measure: Normalized accelerometric dominant-hand tremor power 80 minutes after administration, in the weighted condition.
Formulation detail: The associated 2013 article identifies soybean oil in the active formulation. The API entry does not identify soybean oil as an independent intervention.
