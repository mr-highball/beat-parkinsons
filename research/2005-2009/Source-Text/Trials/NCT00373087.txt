BRIEF SUMMARY

Entacapone is an antiparkinsonian drug which block L-dopa metabolism, inhibiting the
C-O-methyltransferase (COMT) enzyme. There is an individual variability of the COMT
activity determined by a genetic polymorphism. The aim of this study is to investigate
whether the genetic variability influences entacapone efficacy in Parkinson's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

COMT protein is dependent of a single autosomal locus with two co-dominant alleles with a
high activity (allele H) and a low activity (allele L) form of the enzyme. L and H allele
frequency in the Caucasian population is around 50%. This is a monocentric randomized
blinded cross-over study comparing acute challenge of L-dopa + placebo versus L-dopa +
200 mg entacapone, in Parkinson's disease patients with HH and LL genotypes.
