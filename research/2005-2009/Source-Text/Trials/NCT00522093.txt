Title: Planned single-arm TJ-100 study for chronic constipation in Japan
NCT ID: NCT00522093
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00522093
First posted: 2007-08-29
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00522093
Archive scope: Factual summary of the registered design, outcomes and results status.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The completed registry lists a single open, nonrandomized group at Showa Inan General Hospital and estimated enrollment of 60. It specifies six weeks of one dose, with a four-week baseline and two-week follow-up. Planned outcomes are bowel frequency, symptoms, bowel gas, rescue medication and adverse effects. The record has no posted results. The published Horiuchi cohort at that hospital reported two active dose groups and 22 participants. The overlap in regimen timing, population and site is a lead to follow, while the allocation and enrollment differences leave linkage uncertain.
