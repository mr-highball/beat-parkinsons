Title: Eicosapentaenoic acid and mood in middle-aged women
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN69617477
Record: ISRCTN69617477
DOI: 10.1186/ISRCTN69617477
Registration date: 2007-02-13
Coverage: Factual summary of official registry fields.

Submitted December 20, 2006; registered February 13, 2007; record last edited January 6, 2009. First enrollment is listed as March 1, 2005, before registration. The completed double-blind trial concerned EPA and mood in middle-aged women. The registry links published results. The author's subsequent clinical report names this registration and identifies the comparator as sunflower oil with a small amount of fish oil for masking. See PMID 19116322 for clinical results and preparation details.
