BRIEF SUMMARY

To determine the long-term consequences (8 years) of initiating patients with Parkinson's
disease on either pramipexole or levodopa. We hypothesize that patients initiating
therapy with pramipexole compared with levodopa will demonstrate less self-reported
disability as measured by the Modified Schwab and England (S/E) scale 8 years after
randomization.
