BRIEF SUMMARY

The objective of this open-label extension is to assess the safety and tolerability of
long-term treatment of the rotigotine patch in subjects with early-stage idiopathic
Parkinson's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This is the open-label extension to the randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled
SP512 trial that assessed the efficacy and safety and tolerability of the Rotigotine
patch in subjects with early-stage idiopathic Parkinson's Disease.
