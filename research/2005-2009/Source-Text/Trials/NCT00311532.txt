Observational Study to Determine the Incidence of New-Onset Valvulopathy in Patients Treated With Pergolide as Second-Line Therapy for Parkinson's Disease

BRIEF SUMMARY

The primary objective of this study is to estimate the incidence of new-onset
valvulopathy, determined by baseline and follow-up echocardiograms, in patients with
Parkinson's Disease who are receiving pergolide as second-line therapy.

The secondary objective of the study is to estimate the prevalence of valvulopathy, as
determined by baseline echocardiograms, among all patients who have Parkinson's Disease
and are being considered for pergolide as second-line therapy.
