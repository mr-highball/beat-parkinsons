BRIEF SUMMARY

This study is to follow-up with workers from a local company that had exposure to TCE
that may have symptoms of Parkinson's disease. We hope to learn more about TCE exposure
and Parkinson's disease. Eligible participants will be those who participated in previous
research study entitled "Survey of Selected Exposures and Neurologic Symptoms in an
Industrial Population"
