BRIEF SUMMARY

The objective of this trial was to assess subject preference for PARCOPA,
carbidopa/levodopa Orally Disintegrating Tablets (ODT), compared with conventional
carbidopa/levodopa tablets, in subjects with stable Parkinson's disease.
