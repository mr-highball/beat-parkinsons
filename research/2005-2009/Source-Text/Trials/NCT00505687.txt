BRIEF SUMMARY

The objective of this open-label extension is to assess the safety and tolerability of
long-term treatment of rotigotine in subjects with idiopathic PD.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This is the open-label extension to the open-label trials SP824 (NCT00242008), SP825
(NCT00243971), and SP826 (NCT00243945) that assessed the efficacy and safety and
tolerability of rotigotine in subjects with idiopathic Parkinson's Disease.
