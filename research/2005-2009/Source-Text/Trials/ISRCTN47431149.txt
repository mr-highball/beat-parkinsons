Title: Treating Depression with Omega-3
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN47431149
Record: ISRCTN47431149
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting or registration: 2005-10-31

Completed. Eight-week randomized, double-blind comparison in adults with major depression. Participants received fish-oil capsules or a matching comparator described as sunflower oil. The later article gives the comparator's fish-oil fraction. The registry identifies depressive symptom change and tolerability as objectives. It links the results paper and lists no intention to share individual participant data.
