BRIEF SUMMARY

The purpose of this study is to evaluate the safety and efficacy of the long-term
treatment of entacapone in patients with Parkinson's disease with end-of-dose
wearing-off.

This study is not recruiting patients in the United States.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION
