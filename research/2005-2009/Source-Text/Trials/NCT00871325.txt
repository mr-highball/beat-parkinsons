Title: Daikenchuto, gastrointestinal and colonic transit in healthy volunteers
NCT ID: NCT00871325
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00871325
First posted: 2009-03-30
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00871325
Archive scope: Factual summary of the registered design, outcomes and results status.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

This completed randomized parallel study enrolled 60 people and used two daily amounts of TU-100 granules and placebo over five days. The protocol specifies gastric emptying, 24-hour colonic geometric center and ascending colon emptying. It identifies masking of participants, caregivers, investigators and assessors. The official registration has no posted results. Allocation, enrollment, intervention duration and endpoints fit the investigator reports PMID 20378829 and W1413. The other 45-person trial of rectal sensation and functional constipation is NCT01139216.
