BRIEF SUMMARY

The purpose of this pilot program is to develop and evaluate a new treatment program for
depression in Parkinson's disease (PD). The treatment uses Cognitive Behavior
Therapy(CBT)to teach patients how to become more aware of their thoughts and feelings and
to change thinking patterns and behaviors that may be related to depressive symptoms.
This is the first time that this treatment has been used in a group setting for
depression in PD.

Target population is patients in the Movement Disorders Clinic at Oregon Health & Science
University who have Parkinson's disease and mild to moderate depression.
