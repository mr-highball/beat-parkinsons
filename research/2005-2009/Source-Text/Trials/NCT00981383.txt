NCT00981383. Official ClinicalTrials.gov API https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00981383, retrieved 2026-11-16. First submitted 2009-09-10, first posted 2009-09-22 (date type estimated), last updated 2017-04-28; status completed, actual enrollment 92. Parallel randomized masked intervention among 45–80-year-olds with stable coronary artery disease; Parkinson disease and other neurological conditions excluded. The active intervention supplied 1.9 g omega-3 fatty acids in three capsules daily; placebo consisted of three 1-g capsules containing 50:50 soybean/corn oil, with oil processing unspecified. Primary Hamilton Depression Rating Scale and secondary Beck Depression Inventory-II were to be assessed at baseline and weeks 4, 8 and 12. Current API has no posted results section; PMID27529771 is listed as a results publication and PMID26379437 and PMID29730646 as derived analyses.
