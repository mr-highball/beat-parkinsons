Registered olive-oil comparator in primary insomnia
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00484497
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: official record with publication linkage

First posted June 11, 2007. The registry identifies a controlled oral supplement trial in insomnia, listed as completed with no posted result module at the November 15, 2026 replay. Cornu and colleagues published the active supplement versus olive-oil placebo arms in 2010. The article supplies the sleep assessments and leaves the olive-oil grade unspecified.
