NCT00102596 — Clinical Trial Characterizing the Bioavailability of 1-Octanol in Adults With Ethanol-responsive Essential Tremor
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00102596
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Coverage: Selected official registration fields, summarized as plain text.

First public registration: 2005-01-31.
Status: Completed. First public results: 2012-02-01.
Sponsor: National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke.
Population: Adults with alcohol-responsive essential tremor.
Formulations: Oral 1-octanol adsorbed to microcrystalline cellulose and silica, compared with oral 1-octanol embedded in a soybean oil soft gel.
Primary measure: Normalized mean tremor amplitude over 360 minutes in the fixed-dose formulation comparison.
The official entry does not specify soybean oil refining grade. Both formulations contain the study drug.
