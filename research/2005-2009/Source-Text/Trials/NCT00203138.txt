BRIEF SUMMARY

Patients who completed the study TVP-1012/232 are eligible to enter the extension study
to continue their rasagiline therapy for their Parkinson's disease (PD). During this
study the patient's safety, tolerability of rasagiline, and effectiveness of this therapy
will be monitored.
