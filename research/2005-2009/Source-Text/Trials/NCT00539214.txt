Non-motor Symptoms (Depressive Symptoms) of Parkinson's Disease and Their Course Under Pramipexole Treatment

BRIEF SUMMARY

-  To compare the effect of pramipexole on depressive symptoms in early and advanced PD
     patients

  -  To evaluate the course of depressive symptoms measured with UPDRS Part I (mentation,
     behavior and mood) and with HADS-D under pramipexole treatment

  -  To investigate whether improvement of depressive symptoms is linked to improvement
     in motor function (UPDRS Part III)

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Improvement of depressive symptoms (UPDRS item 3, HADS-D) compared to baseline in early and advanced Parkinsons's disease
    Time frame: 9-16 weeks
  -
    Measure: Change in sum scores of UPDRS Part III (motor examination) from baseline and UPDRS Part I (mentation, behavior and mood) from baseline
    Time frame: 9-16 weeks
  -
    Measure: Change in HADS subscores (anxiety and depression) from baseline
    Time frame: 9-16 weeks
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Baseline pattern of patient rated symptom list (HADS)
    Time frame: 9-16 weeks
  -
    Measure: Correlation of the change in UPDRS Part I and UPDRS Part III at the final visit
    Time frame: 9-16 weeks
  -
    Measure: Frequency of adverse events
    Time frame: 9-16 weeks

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion criteria:

    1. Idiopathic Parkinsons disease with or without fluctuations

    2. Indication for treatment with Sifrol®

    3. Male or female patients of age

    4. Presence of at least mild depressive symptoms (as judged by the treating physician)

    5. Ability to reliably complete a self-rating scale (HADS)

  Exclusion criteria:

    1. Any contraindications according to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SPC):
       hypersensitivity to pramipexole or to any of the excipients.

    2. Ongoing treatment with Sifrol®.
