The registry lists a cannabidiol preparation in olive oil and an olive-oil placebo. It supplies no oil grade, oil-free arm, insomnia outcome or Parkinson comparison. The linked 2017 investigator abstract reports Crohn disease activity, with no clear between-arm difference. The protocol and published abstract differ on the reported drug dose.
