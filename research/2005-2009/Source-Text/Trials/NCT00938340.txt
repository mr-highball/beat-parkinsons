Title: Acute effects of whole walnuts and walnut fractions on cardiovascular risk factors
Registry ID: NCT00938340
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00938340
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00938340
First posted: 2009-07-13, date typed estimated in current API.
Coverage: Factual current official registration extraction; later posted results are separately bucketed in 2015–2019.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

The randomized unmasked four-condition crossover compares whole walnuts, oil, skins and defatted nutmeat. Registered actual enrollment is 20; 15 completed. The record lists study start August 2007, primary completion February 2009 and completion May 2009, preceding first public posting.

The 2013 paper PMID23616506 names this trial, supplies treatment amounts and explicitly describes n-hexane Soxhlet extraction of the oil. The 2011 cell paper PMC3180353 also identifies the same trial family. It is not another independent human oil trial.

Results were first submitted 2013-11-14 and first posted 2018-10-29; first quality-control submission is separately dated 2018-02-19. Current update is 2023-08-21. Results and registration dates are distinct. No Parkinson symptom or pain outcome is registered; the comparator conditions do not include a no-walnut arm.
