BRIEF SUMMARY

Patients with Parkinson's disease who completed the prior double-blind study 6002-009 are
eligible to enter into this long-term study. The purpose of this study is to evaluate the
safety and efficacy of KW-6002 administered long-term in patients with advanced
Parkinson's disease treated with levodopa.
