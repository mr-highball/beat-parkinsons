Title: MEMO study: Mental health in Elderly Maintained with Omega-3
First public registration date: 2005-09-12
ISRCTN: ISRCTN46249783
DOI: 10.1186/ISRCTN46249783
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN46249783
Official registration PDF: https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN46249783
Text coverage: Distilled official four-page trial registration.

The original registration identifies a blinded randomized trial of omega-3 and placebo in older adults with outcomes for cognitive function and mental well-being. The primary cognition paper and investigator thesis identify the placebo oil as high-oleic sunflower and report clinical task outcomes for the same study family. This entry preserves registration provenance and contributes no separate result.
