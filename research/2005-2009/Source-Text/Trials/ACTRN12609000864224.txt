Title: Assessing the anti-stress effects of lemon balm-containing foods in healthy participants
Registry ID: ACTRN12609000864224
Source: https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=320632
Registration date: 2009-10-06. The official date field labels registration retrospective.
Coverage: Original ANZCTR registration, treatment arms, outcomes, design and recruitment notes, checked 2026-11-18 UTC.

The record plans randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover comparisons in healthy young adults. It lists a beverage study, a dairy study and a fruit-bar study, with 20 participants planned per study. The record allows possible overlap among volunteers recruited for different comparisons. The beverage and dairy sections list different amounts and formulations of lemon balm and sweetener. For beverages the active amounts listed are 1.0 and 1.8 g. The primary stress-reactivity outcome uses mood questionnaires and cortisol around a computerized stressor at one and three hours. Cognitive performance is secondary.

The 2014 linked report cites this registration and gives a pilot plus two crossover comparisons of extract beverages and yoghurt. The main paper's active amounts were 0.3 and 0.6 g of concentrated extract, and it gives no fruit-bar results. The register and paper provide different arm descriptions. Equivalence across the product specifications remains unresolved. The source registers stress-related outcomes in healthy adults.
