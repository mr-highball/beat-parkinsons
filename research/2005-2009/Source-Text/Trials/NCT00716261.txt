BRIEF SUMMARY

Our hypothesis is that intensive training with positive biofeedback may enhance postural
control and stability in Parkinson's disease patients and in elderly fallers.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

The aim of the study is to treat Parkinson's patients and elderly fallers. The
intervention is intensive training with Biofeedback to try to correct stooped posture,
and to improve stability in standing and in walking.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Idiopathic PD for >2 yrs

    -  Between 50-90 Yrs

    -  On anti-Parkinsonian medication

    -  Hoehn & Yahr 2-3 in "ON"

    -  Free of serious co-morbidities

    -  Able to walk but reports difficulties in gait

    -  Can use assistive device

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Significant orthopedic disturbances or pain

    -  Major depression (DSM IV)

    -  Dementia (MMSE<24)

    -  Clinically significant hearing problems

    -  Unstable medical condition (2 wks from meds change)

    -  Deep Brain Stimulation
