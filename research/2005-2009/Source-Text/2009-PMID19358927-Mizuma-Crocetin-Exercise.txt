Title: Daily oral administration of crocetin attenuates physical fatigue in human subjects
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19358927/
PMID: 19358927
DOI: 10.1016/j.nutres.2009.02.003
Publication: 2009-03 issue
Coverage: Original indexed investigator abstract. Full data tables pending.

Mizuma and colleagues randomized 14 healthy adults, seven men and seven women, in a double-blind three-way crossover of oral crocetin, ascorbic acid and placebo. Participants completed brief maximal-velocity trials while performing prolonged cycling. The change in maximal velocity between an early and late test favored crocetin over placebo in the male subgroup (p<.05). The abstract reports the effect as specific to men and gives no numerical effect size or confidence interval. Ascorbic acid did not change this outcome. The measured endpoint was cycling performance. Whole dried saffron, diagnosed Parkinson disease and patient-reported fatigue severity were outside the study.
