Original source scope and evidence boundary
Twenty-four undergraduates completed a balanced placebo-controlled crossover, and 23 supplied the analyzed caffeine research visual analog ratings. The four conditions were matched drinks with placebo, isolated caffeine, isolated L-theanine or both. The peach-flavored shared base had tea powder removed. Compared with placebo, isolated caffeine and the combination favored lower mental fatigue, t(22)=2.83, p<.01, d=.6 and t(22)=2.49, p<.05, d=.7, respectively. Tiredness was a different rating with a reported significant reduction for the combination. Habitual caffeine use was not logged and overnight abstinence was verified. This does not measure steeped tea, brewed coffee or diagnosed Parkinson fatigue.
