Tremor and apathy scope in a bilateral pallidal lesion case
https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/76/4/482.full.pdf

April 2005 issue. The case developed parkinsonism after bilateral pallidal injury and was assessed with several motor tasks. The original Discussion reports no distal limb tremor and no clinical evidence of abulia. Apathy in longstanding Parkinson disease appears as prior context. The mapped fig and date-fruit exposures were absent.
