Title: Effect of insulin and its combination with resveratrol or curcumin in attenuation of diabetic neuropathic pain: participation of nitric oxide and TNF-alpha
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17199240/
PMID: 17199240
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.2070
Publication date: 2007-03
Date basis: Phytotherapy Research issue. Earlier publisher date was not verified.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract and exact bibliographic identity. Publisher full article remains open.

In a diabetic mouse model, the investigators used tail-immersion and hot-plate measures. The abstract reports that insulin alone and insulin combined with isolated curcumin or with resveratrol affected thermal hyperalgesia. It also reports inflammatory marker differences with combinations. Arm sizes, per-arm pain estimates, and the numerical incremental curcumin-versus-insulin comparison require original results access.

This is investigational constituent and combination context for claim 0812. The model, bundled treatment and endpoints do not resolve a separate ground turmeric effect on Parkinson pain. The 2008 and 2009 review citations are the same underlying study and add no independent observation.
