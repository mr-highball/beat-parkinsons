Zou and colleagues tested a combined oregano and cranberry extract in Mexican fruit flies. Survival effects depended on diet composition and sex. The blend increased some lifespan outcomes under the higher-carbohydrate feeding condition. Under the higher-protein condition the corresponding survival difference was inconclusive. Egg-laying measurements did not show an evident fecundity penalty under the tested comparisons.

The response belongs to the combined extract, with no isolated dry oregano or dried cranberry estimate. Fly survival and reproduction do not provide a measured anxiety or depression comparison. The later phytochemical review cites these experiments without adding a new study.
