Title: Phenolic acids and flavonoids of fig fruit (Ficus carica L.) in the northern Mediterranean region
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodchem.2007.05.061
Publication date: 2008-01-01
Citation: Veberic R, Colaric M, Stampar F. Food Chemistry 106:153–157.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0308814607005201
Coverage: Factual extract from the primary publication and its Table 1.

The investigators measured seven phenolics in three cultivars at harvests in July and September, including chlorogenic acid, epicatechin and rutin. For example, reported rutin in Miljska figa was 27.3 and 28.7 mg/100 g fresh weight at the two reported September samplings, while other cultivars had lower values. This establishes an ingredient chemistry and cultivar context. It does not identify a measured active mediator in the 2020 rat extract experiment, nor test tremor, dosing response or Parkinson disease.
