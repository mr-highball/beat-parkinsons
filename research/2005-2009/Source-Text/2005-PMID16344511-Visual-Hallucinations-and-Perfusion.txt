Title: Regional cerebral blood flow in Parkinson disease with nonpsychotic visual hallucinations
Source: https://www.neurology.org/doi/10.1212/01.wnl.0000187116.13370.e0
PMID: 16344511
DOI: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000187116.13370.e0
Publication date: 2005-12-12
Date basis: Neurology first online date; next-day issue
Source depth: Primary author abstract at publisher and NLM; body remains open
Evidence type: Parkinson visual-status SPECT comparison
Format: Factual digest of the inspected source

The investigators compared 24 Parkinson participants who reported visual hallucinations with retained insight and 41 without prior visual hallucinations. The right fusiform signal was lower at a corrected cluster threshold. Higher right superior and middle temporal signals were described at an uncorrected threshold. These are group perfusion comparisons, without a dried-fig intervention or repeated visual episode counts.
