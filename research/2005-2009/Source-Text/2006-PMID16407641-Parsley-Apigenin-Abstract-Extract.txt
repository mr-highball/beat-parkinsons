Title: Bioavailability of apigenin from apiin-rich parsley in humans
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16407641/
PMID: 16407641
DOI: 10.1159/000090736
First public article date: 2006-01-10
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated primary author abstract

The study describes 11 healthy people after ingestion of
blanched parsley. In the author abstract, mean maximum
plasma apigenin is 127 ± 81 nmol/L at 7.2 ± 1.3 hours.
The endpoint varies between participants and is below
the detection limit by 28 hours. It measures human
pharmacokinetics for parsley. There is no raw celery
stalk, Parkinson population or separate tremor endpoint.
