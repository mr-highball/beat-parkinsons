Melissa officinalis points to volume 22:526 and volume 2:404; the latter is labeled callus culture. A bare Melissa entry points to volume 29:581, whose exact entity remains unverified. Index labels do not establish preparations, participants or measured symptom effects.

Volume 22:526 falls within a 2000 anticonvulsant review. Volume 2 is a 1988 structure-elucidation volume, but the exact chapter identity at page 404 remains unresolved. Volume 29:581 falls within Polya's 2003 protease-inhibitor review; its original page was inaccessible.

The index contains genuine foods, botanical species and constituents, with taxonomic distinctions retained. Clinical evidence is not inferred from its complete availability. These are title/index-level leads, not full-body review of referenced studies.

The complete 868-page PDF includes a cover followed by the printed index. PDF page 344 lists gingerol, analogues, and digestive-organ motility at underlying volume 24 pp. 900–902. PDF page 867 gives Zingiber officinale and related species separately. The Cinnamomum cassia and cinnamon bark entries on PDF page 176 differ from camphor species. PDF page 123 attributes an indexed constituent to canola oil. PDF page 608 identifies Penaeus vannamei separately from the unrelated brine-shrimp bioassays on pages 108 and 124. Other examined entries concern Salvia officinalis oil, tea components, and fruit constituents. The exact preparations, models and measured endpoints must be taken from the cited original papers.
