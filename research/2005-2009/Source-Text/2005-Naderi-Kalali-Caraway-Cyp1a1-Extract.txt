Title: Suppressive effects of caraway (Carum carvi) extracts on 2,3,7,8-tetrachloro-dibenzo-p-dioxin-dependent gene expression of cytochrome P450 1A1 in the rat H4IIE cells
Authors: B Naderi-Kalali and colleagues
Date: 2005
Identifiers: PMID 15713544; DOI 10.1016/j.tiv.2004.11.003
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0887233305000020
Coverage: Original factual extract of the primary abstract.

In dioxin-treated rat H4IIE hepatoma cultures, seed extracts made with three organic solvents reduced ethoxyresorufin dealkylation activity and suppressed induced CYP1A1 at the RNA level. The cells were the exposure target. The study did not evaluate the effects of consuming caraway on systemic drug concentrations, CYP1A2, or Parkinson treatment.
