Bonarska-Kujawa and colleagues compared extracts of apple, strawberry
fruit and chokeberry in red-cell and model-membrane experiments.
They describe mostly discocytes and discoechinocytes in the strawberry
condition. Among the three extracts, strawberry showed the smallest
change in the reported hydrophilic membrane-fluidity measurement.

The fuller 2010 publication by the same authors measures several
membrane properties in the same three-fruit comparison. The ranking
depends on the readout and model, and the abstract and paper are
treated as a likely shared study lineage. The preparations are
directly applied extracts in laboratory assays. The reports give no
oral fruit or fall-frequency result.
