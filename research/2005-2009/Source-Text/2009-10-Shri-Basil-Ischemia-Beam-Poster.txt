Sweet basil leaf extracts and beam walking in a 2009 poster
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1038/jcbfm.2009.156
DOI: 10.1038/jcbfm.2009.156
Coverage: Original publisher poster abstract 554, printed pages S442–S443

Shri, Bora and Arora reported oral pretreatment with methanol or ethyl acetate extracts of Ocimum basilicum leaves in Swiss mice. They used a 10-minute bilateral carotid occlusion and 24-hour reperfusion model. The reported inclined-beam impairment was attenuated with tested extracts. The poster supplies no arm counts or numerical beam outcomes, and has no stride-length endpoint or fresh culinary intake. A later report with the same authors lists a different occlusion duration. Animal overlap is unresolved.
