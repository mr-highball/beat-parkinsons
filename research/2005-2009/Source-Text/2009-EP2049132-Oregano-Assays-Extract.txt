Oregano extract in early enzyme and mouse assays
Primary source: https://patents.google.com/patent/EP2049132A1/en
Coverage: factual extract of primary patent test methods and results.

The European application published April 22, 2009. Example 6 describes dose-dependent inhibition of monoamine oxidase A and B by an oregano extract in an enzyme assay. Example 7 uses an injected preparation in a mouse observation grid. The grid lists salivation and catalepsy, while the reported changes concern sedation, touch reactivity, decreased muscle tone and fear. It labels the tested extract inconsistently between Methods and Results. The patent does not give a separately quantified salivation result, clinical dystonia or daily OFF hours after culinary oregano. The enzyme result is mechanistic context pending direct human evidence.
