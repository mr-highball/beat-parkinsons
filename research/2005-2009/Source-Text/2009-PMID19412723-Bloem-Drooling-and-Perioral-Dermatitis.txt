Title: Debilitating consequences of drooling
PMID: 19412723
PMCID: PMC2721959
DOI: 10.1007/s00415-009-5144-0
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2721959/
First published: 2009-05-03 online
Coverage: Original case letter and figure caption.

Bloem and colleagues describe one person with longstanding Parkinson disease whose nighttime drooling progressed to severe daytime drooling, with dysphagia, dysarthria and perioral dermatitis. The team reports that treatment of the salivary glands suppressed secretion and that the skin findings improved. They separately discuss reduced automatic swallowing as a possible cause of saliva accumulation. The publication supplies neither a controlled comparison nor a graded dietary response. The figure depicts the skin complication and the case supplies no raw fig exposure.
