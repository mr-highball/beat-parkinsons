Title: Obstetric case chapter: ginger discussion, separate case care and separate constipation history
PMCID: PMC7124088
DOI: 10.1007/978-1-84628-582-0_5
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7124088/

Archive scope: Factual note from the original article, original page scans or identified original abstract member.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The full 75-page case chapter covers printed pp. 97–171. Ginger appears in the discussion of nausea or hyperemesis during pregnancy, alongside pyridoxine and other options. Those discussion passages are separate from the described patient's treatments. The chapter's constipation history concerns a different case in the thrombocytopenia discussion, before pregnancy. It does not pair ginger dosing with that bowel history, pain, anxiety or skeletal muscle rigidity.
