Title: Rubiscolin-6, a delta opioid peptide derived from spinach Rubisco, has anxiolytic effect via activating sigma1 and dopamine D1 receptors
PMID: 17766012
DOI: 10.1016/j.peptides.2007.07.024
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17766012/
First published: 2007-07-27 online, Peptides 28(10):1998–2003, October 2007.
Archive scope: Factual extract of the original author abstract, with the full paper unresolved in this review.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

Hirata and colleagues reported a response to isolated rubiscolin-6, a spinach Rubisco-derived peptide, in the mouse elevated plus-maze. The reported oral and injected amounts differed, at 100 and 10 mg/kg, respectively. Pharmacologic blocking experiments in the abstract point to delta-opioid, sigma-1 and dopamine D1 involvement. The abstract gives no arm-level effect size or intervals. These data describe an isolated-peptide behavioral response in ordinary mice, without a Parkinson model, whole spinach, a culinary parsley preparation or a dyskinesia outcome.

The 2023 biophysics S1.97 report models rubiscolin structure and proposes other leaves as possible dietary sources. It is a separate computational report and does not replicate the behavioral result. The published peer-reviewed article body and its arm-level uncertainty remain open.
