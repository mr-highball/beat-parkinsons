Title: Fatigue measurements in stable Filipino patients with idiopathic Parkinson disease

PMCID: PMC4027701

Date basis: First electronic publication indexed by NLM.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full structured original.

The original hospital cohort compared 28 stable, independent participants with Parkinson disease and 28 age-matched controls. Fatigue was measured with the Fatigue Severity Inventory and Multicomponent Fatigue Index. Table 1 reports mean fatigue inventory scores of 139.50 versus 86.88 and mean overall Multicomponent Fatigue Index scores of 59.65 versus 38.62, with Parkinson participants listed first.

The comparison establishes fatigue in an eligible clinical sample. The measured contrast is Parkinson status. There is no assessment of coffee consumption or a coffee-specific fatigue outcome. The later community survey cites this cohort as fatigue background, alongside its own two clinical cases.
