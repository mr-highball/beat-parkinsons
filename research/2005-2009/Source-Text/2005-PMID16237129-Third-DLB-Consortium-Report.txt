Title: Diagnosis and management of dementia with Lewy bodies: third report of the DLB Consortium
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16237129/
PMID: 16237129
DOI: 10.1212/01.WNL.0000187889.17253.B1
Publication date: 2005-10-19
Date basis: NLM electronic date. Neurology 65(12), 1863–1872, December issue.
Source depth: Primary consensus report abstract
Format: Original-source factual extract

The published report revises clinical and pathological classification for dementia with Lewy bodies, including how to weigh dopaminergic imaging, sleep behavior and drug sensitivity. Its abstract discusses difficulties using symptom onset timing to separate dementia with Lewy bodies from Parkinson disease dementia. It mentions prior visual-symptom observations on cholinesterase inhibitors as management evidence. This is consensus synthesis and is not another independent patient series or a dried-fig test.
