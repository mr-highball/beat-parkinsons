Title: Wholegrain foods made from a novel high-amylose barley variety (Himalaya 292) improve indices of bowel health in human subjects.
PMID: 17919346
DOI: 10.1017/S000711450783902X
First published: 2008
Authors: Bird AR, Vuaran MS, King RA, Noakes M, Keogh J, Morell MK
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17919346/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

4-week 3-arm randomized crossover trial in n=17 adults (age 31-66 y) comparing wholegrain high-amylose hull-less barley (Himalaya 292; 45 g/d total fiber) vs wholegrain wheat (32 g/d) vs refined cereal foods (21 g/d). Compared with refined cereal, Himalaya 292 barley increased fecal weight by 33%, lowered fecal pH (7.24 to 6.98), raised fecal butyrate concentration (+42%) and excretion (+91%), raised total SCFA excretion (+57%), and reduced fecal p-cresol (-33%), with superior pH and butyrate vs wholemeal wheat. Limits: healthy non-PD adults; specialized high-amylose barley cultivar foods.
