Title: Omega-3 augmentation of sertraline in treatment of depression in patients with coronary heart disease: a randomized controlled trial
Publication date: 2009-10-21
PMID: 19843899
PMCID: PMC3477788
DOI: 10.1001/jama.2009.1487
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3477788/
Text coverage: Original article methods and ten-week clinical results.

Carney and colleagues enrolled 122 adults with major depression and coronary heart disease. Both randomized arms took sertraline. The active capsule added EPA plus DHA and the comparison capsule contained corn oil. Baseline-adjusted comparisons did not distinguish BDI-II (p=.44) or Hamilton depression scores (p=.90) at ten weeks. The oil provenance and refining grade are unspecified, and treatment response cannot isolate an oil effect. NCT00116857 tracks the same trial, without a Parkinson depression outcome.
