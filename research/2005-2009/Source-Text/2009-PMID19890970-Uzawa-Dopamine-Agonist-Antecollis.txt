Title: Dopamine agonist-induced antecollis in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mds.22779
PMID: 19890970
DOI: 10.1002/mds.22779
Publication date: 2009-12-15
Date basis: NLM issue date. Wiley lists December 18 as first publication online and NLM indexed the abstract earlier.
Coverage: Original author abstract and publisher bibliography. Full case tables remain open.

The report contributes three new Parkinson cases to a 16-case literature grouping. The authors describe forward neck flexion after increases in dopamine agonist treatment and improvement after withdrawal. Their bibliography includes the earlier Suzuki case. These case changes concern posture. The author abstract supplies no distinct saliva outcome or culinary exposure.
