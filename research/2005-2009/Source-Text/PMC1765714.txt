The review compares common tissue findings in dementias and discusses why clinical history and the sequence of manifestations matter to pathological classification. Its Lewy body section discusses dementia with Lewy bodies and Parkinson disease dementia as overlapping clinical–pathological presentations. Visual hallucinations are invoked as clinical context. It does not report a cohort-level visual episode result or an evaluation of dried figs.
