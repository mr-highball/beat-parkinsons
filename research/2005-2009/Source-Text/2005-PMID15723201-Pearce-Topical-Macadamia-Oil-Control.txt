Title: Reduction of nickel-induced contact hypersensitivity reactions by topical tea tree oil in humans
PMID: 15723201
DOI: 10.1007/s00011-004-1317-6
Publication year: 2005
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15723201/
Text coverage: Original author abstract and verification of the named macadamia comparator.

Pearce, Finlay-Jones and Hart compared applications to skin after nickel exposure. The comparisons included tea-tree oil and lotion, vehicle lotion and undiluted macadamia oil. The macadamia arm did not significantly alter flare or erythema in the author abstract. The tested material went on skin and its pressing grade is unspecified. No dietary intervention, Parkinson population or tremor measure is described.
