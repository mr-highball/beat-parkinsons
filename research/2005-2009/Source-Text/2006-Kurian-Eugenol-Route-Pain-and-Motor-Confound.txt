Original source scope
Injected eugenol reduced chemically evoked abdominal constrictions and formalin paw-licking. The study reported mild thermal effects and decreased rotarod endurance at the highest tested condition. Compound delivery and motor suppression limit interpretation of behavioral readouts. The study is independent of Daniel et al.'s later oral experiment. It does not estimate ground clove, cinnamon, ginger or turmeric effects in Parkinson disease. PMID 34394824 reference 128.
