Title: Ethyl-eicosapentaenoic acid for the treatment of psychological distress and depressive symptoms in middle-aged women: a double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized clinical trial
Publication date: 2008-12-30 (online first; 2009 print issue)
PMID: 19116322
DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.2008.26749
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19116322/
Text coverage: Distilled investigator report and published primary result summary.

The trial randomized 120 women with psychological distress to eight weeks of EPA-enriched or sunflower-oil capsules. Distress and depressive scale changes did not differ clearly for the whole study group, while some results favored EPA in the subgroup without a major depressive episode. The Cochrane review extracts the 29-person major depressive episode subset, not an independent trial. The Clinical registration ISRCTN69617477 and the investigators' thesis describe the same study family. In this article the abbreviation PD is psychological distress. The cohort supplies no Parkinson outcome or oil-free comparison.
