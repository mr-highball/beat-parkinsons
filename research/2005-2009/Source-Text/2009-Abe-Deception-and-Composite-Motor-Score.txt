Composite motor scores in a Parkinson cognition study
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2677797/

March 31, 2009 online. Abe and colleagues tested deceptive responses in 32 Parkinson patients and 20 healthy controls and related behavior to resting-state brain imaging. The Methods and Table 1 report a total UPDRS part III score of 18.1 with SD 7.2. Rest tremor and bradykinesia appear as clinical background. There are no item-level rest tremor or bradykinesia outcomes or fig or salmon exposure.
