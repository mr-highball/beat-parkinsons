Title: Helicobacter pylori eradication and l-dopa absorption in patients with PD and motor fluctuations
PMID: 16801644
DOI: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000221672.01272.ba
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16801644/
Published: 2006-06-27, Neurology 66(12), pp. 1824–1829.
Archive scope: Original author abstract. Complete primary allocation and effect tables remain open. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

In 34 infected participants with Parkinson motor fluctuations, the abstract describes 17 receiving eradication and 17 in its antioxidant comparison. The abstract separately mentions placebo treatment without specifying its full analysis structure. Levodopa absorption, motor response, ON duration and gastric inflammation were measured over short and longer follow-up. The authors report observations favoring eradication in this comparison. Effect estimates, masking details and the exact placebo structure await the original tables and Methods. These are medication and infection outcomes without a measured culinary exposure.
