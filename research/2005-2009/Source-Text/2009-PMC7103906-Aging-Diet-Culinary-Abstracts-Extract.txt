PB6-147, PDF page 28, surveys 2,922 cardiology-institute patients' dietary habits. Carrot, kale and orange are actual dietary items, without assigned preparation-specific neurological outcomes.

PB7-296, page 227, describes coffee use among 86 older women in a fracture/lipid analysis. PB7-427, page 253, describes 70 independent older adults: combined daily meat/fish/poultry intake is 36% with impaired appetite versus 79% without, p=.002. It does not isolate poultry or establish a chicken-related fatigue effect.

PC7-609, page 288, is a narrative turmeric/curcumin literature summary, not a newly conducted treatment experiment. Separate members' ingredients and symptoms must not be combined into an imagined comparison or counted as independent new clinical trials.
