Early Chinese odor test modifications

Primary source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00405-007-0551-2
Coverage: First-online publisher record and abstract
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First online December 7, 2007, ahead of its 2008 issue. Shu and Yuan describe adapting five odors to test smell identification in Asia. The accessible original abstract does not name individual replacement scents or their results. The later Dong Methods attribute sesame and soy sauce substitutions to this paper. That is a methodological citation, and the original item table and concentrations are still unverified.
