The aromatherapy discussion names lavender and Melissa essential oils and describes routes such as inhalation, bathing and topical application. This is discussion of behavioral management in dementia. The separate depression section addresses diagnosis and treatment with reference to dementia broadly. The layout places these topics side by side on page v58. The adjacent depression heading does not turn the aromatherapy discussion into a depression outcome. This review supplies no new primary trial, brewed leaf comparison or measured Parkinson depression outcome. The 2002 and 2011 topical agitation trials in this catalog are independent primary references.
