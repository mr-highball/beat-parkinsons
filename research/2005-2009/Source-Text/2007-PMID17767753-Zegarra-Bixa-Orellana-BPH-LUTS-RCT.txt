Title: Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled study of Bixa orellana in patients with lower urinary tract symptoms associated to benign prostatic hyperplasia.
PMID: 17767753
DOI: 10.1590/s1677-55382007000400006
First published: 2007
Authors: Zegarra, Vaisberg, Loza, Aguirre, Campos
Journal: International braz j urol : official journal of the Brazilian Society of Urology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17767753/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-month prospective double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in Peru enrolling 136 men (68/arm, screened from 1,478) with BPH and moderate lower urinary tract symptoms (LUTS) assigned to Bixa orellana leaf powder capsules (250 mg three times daily = 750 mg/d) or matched placebo. Completely null across all urinary endpoints: final mean delta symptom score BO -0.79 +/- 1.87 vs placebo -1.07 +/- 1.49 (p = 0.33); Qmax delta +0.44 +/- 1.07 vs +0.47 +/- 1.32 (p = 0.88); post-void residual urine delta +4.24 +/- 11.69 vs +9.01 +/- 18.66 (p = 0.07). Adverse effects were similar between arms. Limits: leaf powder (not ground annatto seed), BPH LUTS in men without PD, completely null.
