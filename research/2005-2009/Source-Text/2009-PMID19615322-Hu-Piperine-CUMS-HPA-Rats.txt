Title: [Antidepressant effects of piperine and its neuroprotective mechanism in rats].
PMID: 19615322
DOI: 10.3736/jcim20090712
First published: 2009
Authors: Hu Y, Liao HB, Liu P, Guo DH, Wang YY
Journal: Zhong xi yi jie he xue bao = Journal of Chinese integrative medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19615322/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled CUMS rat experiment (n=60 rats: 50 stressed across untreated, desipramine, and low/mid/high piperine arms + 10 normal controls) over 21 days. Piperine 10 and 20 mg/kg significantly improved sucrose preference and 5-20 mg/kg reduced elevated serum ACTH and CRH. Limits: isolated piperine in rodent model.
