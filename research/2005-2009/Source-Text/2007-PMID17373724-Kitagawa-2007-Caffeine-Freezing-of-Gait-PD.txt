Title: Effects of caffeine on the freezing of gait in Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 17373724
DOI: 10.1002/mds.21208
First published: 2007
Authors: Kitagawa M, Houzen H, Tashiro K
Journal: Movement disorders : official journal of the Movement Disorder Society
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17373724/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Open clinical study evaluating oral caffeine 100 mg/day for freezing of gait (FOG) in people with Parkinson disease. Caffeine improved the "total akinesia" FOG subtype but had no effect on "trembling in place"; tolerance developed within a few months and was restored after a 2-week caffeine withdrawal. Limits: isolated caffeine rather than brewed black tea; uncontrolled small clinical report with exact sample size and quantitative FOG scores absent from indexed abstract.
