Title: Carotene-rich plant foods ingested with minimal dietary fat enhance the total-body vitamin A pool size in Filipino schoolchildren as assessed by stable-isotope-dilution methodology.
PMID: 17413103
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/85.4.1041
First published: 2007
Authors: Ribaya-Mercado JD, Maramag CC, Tengco LW, Dolnikowski GG, Blumberg JB, Solon FS
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17413103/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Nine-week feeding trial (Ribaya-Mercado et al., 2007, Am J Clin Nutr) in 116 Filipino schoolchildren (aged 9-12 y; groups n=39, 39, 38) fed standardized meals containing pechay (bok choy) pooled with carrots, squash, and kangkong alongside 7, 15, or 29 g/day dietary fat. Multi-vegetable meals doubled total-body vitamin A pool size and lowered low liver vitamin A prevalence from 35% to 7%. Pediatric micronutrient trial bundling bok choy with three other vegetables; no isolated bok choy or neurological symptom outcomes.
