Original factual extract from public primary abstract and figure

The intrastriatal 6-OHDA rat study gives 1.5% black tea extract orally, in two intervention schedules beginning before or after lesioning. The paper reports significant recovery in amphetamine-evoked circling and spontaneous locomotor activity versus lesioned controls after each schedule. The authors describe stronger overall recovery when treatment started before the lesion. Primary Figure 1 bars give approximately 190 lesion-only, 143 in the post-lesion extract group and 102 in the pre-lesion extract group, in rotations per 30 minutes. The sham and extract-only estimates are approximately 17 and 15. Plot indicators are ***a for lesion-only, *b for post-lesion and ***b for pre-lesion. Exact statistical thresholds are unresolved without the legend. No numerical spontaneous locomotion estimates were recovered. Neither clinical walking speed nor rest tremor is reported.

Secondary preparation trail
Table 14 of the chapter Medicinal Plants Extraction as Neuroprotective Agents attributes to Chaturvedi 2006 an aqueous extract freshly brewed each day at 85 °C for five minutes, cooled to room temperature and filtered. Table 6.3 of Tea Polyphenols in Parkinson's Disease labels the 1.5% extract ad libitum. Those specifics require primary Methods confirmation.
