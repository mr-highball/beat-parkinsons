Hallucination discontinuation and motor scale in a donepezil trial
https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/76/7/934.full.pdf

June 16, 2005 online according to the original PDF. Ravina and colleagues randomized 22 patients with Parkinson disease dementia to a donepezil and placebo crossover. One participant stopped during placebo after worsening visual hallucinations. Reported total and motor UPDRS results do not isolate rest-tremor severity. The original has no fig exposure or food comparison.
