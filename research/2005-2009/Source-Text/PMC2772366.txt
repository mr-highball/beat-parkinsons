The review distinguishes the conviction of an infestation and related skin sensations from possible additional visual illusions or hallucinations. It discusses primary delusional infestation and varied secondary presentations. Descriptions of seeing pathogens in this setting can coexist with tactile experiences and a fixed delusion. Those syndromes require separate outcome coding.

The discussion cites Morris 1991 (PMID 1840787) for Parkinson and Huntington associations and Kumbier and Kornhuber 2002 (PMID 12040988) for a multiple system atrophy case. These are citations within a review. They cannot be counted again as new Parkinson observations here.
