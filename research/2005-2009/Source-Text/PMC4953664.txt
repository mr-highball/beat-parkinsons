Tatsch and Ell review diagnostic PET and SPECT for parkinsonian syndromes, dementias and brain tumors. The Lewy body dementia section names visual hallucinations and gait or parkinsonian symptoms as clinical features and describes imaging patterns, including occipital differences. Images and visual scan interpretation are diagnostic context. The review does not report a new symptom-frequency outcome or dried-fig exposure.
