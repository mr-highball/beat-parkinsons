Title: Fast and slow metabolizers of Hoasca
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16149329/

Date basis: NLM and journal issue month
Source depth: Primary abstract and article body rendering
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The analysis reuses samples for 14 participants from the original 15-person pharmacokinetic study. Plasma alkaloid trajectories were grouped by faster or slower apparent harmine metabolism. CYP2D6 activity was proposed as an explanation. These time-course observations should not be counted as an independent clinical cohort or substituted for a genotype intervention. They concern a mixed ceremonial exposure and healthy participants. No Parkinson visual episode endpoint was measured.
