Title: Effects of caffeine on levodopa pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16966563/
PMID: 16966563
DOI: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000233916.57415.9d
Citation: Deleu D, Jacob P, Chand P, Sarre S, Colwell A. Neurology. 2006 Sep 12. 67(5):897–899.
Coverage: Author abstract from PubMed. Local factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

In a double-blind randomized crossover in 12 people with idiopathic Parkinson disease, pretreatment with isolated caffeine was associated with earlier walking and tapping responses to levodopa and a greater reported walking response. The abstract describes a shorter measure related to peak plasma levodopa without clearly specifying time to peak versus another kinetic measure. The original tables are needed to resolve the phrasing and quantify gait performance.

The comparison tested an acute medication context and isolated caffeine. It did not assign prepared black tea or separate resting tremor. Time to walking response and its magnitude should be kept separate from habitual walking slowness until the original measurements are retrieved.
