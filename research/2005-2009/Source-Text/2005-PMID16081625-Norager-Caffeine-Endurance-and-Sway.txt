Original source scope and evidence boundary
Thirty older adults underwent a randomized capsule caffeine versus placebo crossover after 48 hours of abstinence. The authors report 25% longer cycling endurance (95% CI 13% to 38%) and 11% lower effort at five minutes (95% CI 5% to 17% lower). Postural stability worsened with eyes open by 25% (95% CI 2% to 53%), and the original Results describe worse stability with eyes closed. Walking speed did not clearly change. Exertion during cycling is different from spontaneous fatigue severity. The comparison isolates caffeine, not brewed coffee. The later eight-hour-abstinence report is separately tracked, including the uncertainty about participant overlap.
