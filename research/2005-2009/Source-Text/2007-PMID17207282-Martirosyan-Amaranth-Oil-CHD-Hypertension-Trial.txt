Title: Amaranth oil application for coronary heart disease and hypertension.
PMID: 17207282
PMCID: PMC1779269
DOI: 10.1186/1476-511X-6-1
First published: 2007
Authors: Martirosyan DM, Miroshnichenko LA, Kulakova SN, Pogojeva AV, Zoloedov VI
Journal: Lipids in health and disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17207282/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-week clinical trial (Martirosyan et al., 2007, Lipids Health Dis, PMC1779269) in 125 obese Russian patients with coronary heart disease and hypertension (85 in pressed Amaranthus hybridus seed oil arms at 3, 6, 12, or 18 mL/day [100-600 mg squalene] replacing sunflower oil vs 40 in a hyposodium antiatherogenic diet [HAD] comparison group; all participants received low-salt diet, exercise, and hydrotherapy). Resting systolic blood pressure fell similarly in HAD controls (-18%, 146 to 121 mmHg) and amaranth oil groups (-18% to -21%, 144 to 114 mmHg) without between-group statistical comparison or orthostatic blood pressure testing. Narrative text mentioned subjective decreases in headaches, weakness, and fatigability without standardized symptom scales, numerical counts, or controlled comparison. Limits: bundled multimodal inpatient regimen, pressed oil from A. hybridus rather than cooked grain, and unquantified symptom narrative.
