Title: Quality variation in commercial saffron and laboratory receptor binding
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18496783/
PMID: 18496783
DOI: 10.1055/s-2008-1074535
Publication date: 2008-05-21
Date basis: NLM electronic publication date.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Investigator abstract and university publication record.
University publication record: https://www.uni-muenster.de/Chemie.pb/en/forschung/NEM-Botanicals/Publikationsliste_NEM-Botanicals.html

The investigators report variable composition across market saffron samples, particularly in volatile components, and propose a research specification based on chemical assays. They also report different in vitro binding behavior for the extract, crocetin and other constituents.

The report supports keeping preparation and quality details when comparing saffron studies. The assays do not judge a consumer product's symptom effect and do not establish a tested retail grade, ground-saffron efficacy or a Parkinson depression outcome.
