Title: Food composition
Patent publication: JP2008048673A
First public publication: 2008-03-06
Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/JP2008048673A/ja
Format: Factual extraction from the original Japanese application, human Study 2 and Table 3.
The application describes a one-week evaluation among 10 women troubled by constipation. The tested green-shiso tablet also included young barley leaf, piperine and resistant dextrin. The comparison contained young barley leaf without those additions. The source describes powdered material and gives no isolated fresh-leaf contrast.
Table 3 records mean subjective bowel-improvement scores of 2.8 for both Example 4 and Comparative Example 3 on a three-point scale. The narrative claims that all outcomes improved relative to the comparator. The printed bowel scores contradict that claim. Arm assignment, randomization, variance and Parkinson status are unspecified. This is applicant-reported patent evidence.
