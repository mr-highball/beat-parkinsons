Title: Correlation of visual hallucinations with occipital rCBF changes by donepezil in DLB
Source: https://mhlw-grants.niph.go.jp/system/files/2008/083011/200821015B/200821015B0023.pdf
PMID: 16567718
DOI: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000203114.03976.b0
Publication date: 2006-03-28
Date basis: NLM journal issue date
Source depth: Original journal pages 935–937 in government-hosted compilation
Evidence type: Primary uncontrolled pre-post clinical and SPECT comparison
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Twenty outpatients with probable DLB and a history of visual hallucinations received donepezil over 12 weeks. Eighteen had reported a visual hallucination in the month before scanning. Table 1 reports a mean NPI hallucination score of 6.4 at baseline and 1.4 after treatment (paired p = 0.0002). Reported hallucinations were visual. Both occipital peak comparisons crossed the uncorrected imaging threshold, with corrected p values of 0.144 and 0.220. The study has no parallel placebo comparator. The rating aggregates symptoms across follow-up rather than supplying a stand-alone episode count. Exposure was a drug in DLB.
