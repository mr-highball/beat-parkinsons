Title: Aflatoxins levels in dried fruits and nuts from Rabat-Salé area, Morocco

Text coverage: Author abstract, publisher excerpts and original article rendition, including sampling methods and the commodity results in Table 2.

The team bought 20 samples each of dried figs, dried raisins, peanuts, walnuts and pistachios in Rabat-Salé from January to October 2006. Six of the 20 dried fig samples had detectable total aflatoxins and one contained aflatoxin B1. Three of the 20 exceeded the historical total-aflatoxin benchmark used in the paper. Table 2 gives a maximum total aflatoxin value of 32.9 µg/kg and 0.28 µg/kg in the sole aflatoxin B1 positive. The result describes market products. The paper gives no clinical outcome, ingested amount or cultivar for the sampled figs.
