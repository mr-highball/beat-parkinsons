Title: Aflatoxins levels in dried fruits and nuts from Rabat-Salé area, Morocco
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foodcont.2007.08.010
Publisher material consulted: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0956713507001715
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodcont.2007.08.010
Publication date: 2008-09
Date basis: Original Food Control 19(9), pages 849–853, and deposited Crossref publication fields. The DOI string includes 2007.
Text coverage: Author abstract and publisher sampling, method-performance and conclusion excerpts. Complete results tables remain open.

The team bought 20 samples each of dried figs, dried raisins, peanuts, walnuts and pistachios in Rabat-Salé during 2006. Six of the 20 dried fig samples had detectable total aflatoxins and one contained aflatoxin B1. Three of the 20 exceeded the historical total-aflatoxin benchmark used in the paper. These are direct measurements from the sampled market products. The abstract does not identify a clinical outcome, ingested amount, cultivar, or Taounate or Skoura product origin.
