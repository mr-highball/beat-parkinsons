Kaempferol was an isolated commercial reagent identified as a saffron petal constituent. The investigators found fewer sampled immobility behaviors in normal mice and rats in forced swim compared with saline at selected exposure levels. Figures 1–2 show distinct rodent comparisons and fluoxetine as a positive control. The test is a preclinical behavioral proxy. It neither assigns ground saffron stigma nor measures a depressive symptom scale in people with Parkinson disease. Reference 21 in PMID 33542784. Round 1 0063.
