Of 39 eligible people with advanced Parkinson disease, 23 received bilateral subthalamic stimulation and 16 chose continued drug adjustment. Table 2 gives median drooling scores of 0 at baseline and 12 months and 1 at 24 months in both groups. The two time-by-treatment tests were reported as nonsignificant. All 39 supplied 12-month follow-up and 21 supplied 24-month follow-up. Drooling and gait use different subitems, and cutting food appears as an activity measure. The compared exposures were surgery and clinical medication management, without a culinary fig intervention.
