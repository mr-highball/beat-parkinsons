Primary original, pp. 174–176 and Table 4: 28 elderly people with dementia (17 Alzheimer disease, 3 vascular dementia, 8 other/mixed). Sequential 28-day control, 28-day aroma and 28-day washout. Morning diffuser oil 0.08 mL rosemary plus 0.04 mL lemon; evening lavender and orange. Four assessments. GBSS-J-D was described in results as other psychotic manifestations (methods also calls it other moral condition); Table 4 reported mean ± SD: before control 7.24 ± 7.44; immediately before aroma 8.71 ± 5.97; after aroma 7.18 ± 4.64; after washout 8.47 ± 6.74; p=0.499 for the across-time repeated-measures analysis, not a pairwise effect estimate. The paper does not isolate a visual hallucination frequency result or identify a Parkinson subgroup; this is mixed aroma, not ingested fresh rosemary, and cannot establish a 0617 direction.
