Title: Hallucinations, dreaming and frequent dozing in Parkinson’s disease: impact of right-hemisphere neural networks

The 14 left-onset and 17 right-onset Parkinson participants and 17 chronic-condition controls completed the Parkinson Disease Sleep Scale. Nocturnal hallucinations differed in the unadjusted comparison (overall p=.005). With mood adjustment, the left-onset group differed from controls (p=.007) and the right-onset group (p=.02). The measure represents reported nighttime frequency, without separate visual event counts or an assigned food exposure. The 2010 disconnection review reused this sample.
