Title: Dementia with Lewy bodies: a comparison of clinical diagnosis, FP-CIT single photon emission computed tomography imaging and autopsy

Walker and colleagues obtained complete autopsy findings for 20 prospectively followed dementia cases following earlier FP-CIT imaging. Eight had dementia with Lewy bodies at autopsy and 12 had other findings. Table 2 codes visual hallucinations as once only (2/8 and 1/12) or persistent (4/8 and 5/12). The authors found no significant visual-hallucination difference between autopsy groups in this sample.

The separate accuracy comparison found 88% sensitivity and 100% specificity for the scan against autopsy in this sample, versus 75% and 42% for initial clinical diagnosis. These are diagnostic measures. Persistent symptoms describe a clinical category without a daily episode count, and the study did not measure dried-fig intake.
