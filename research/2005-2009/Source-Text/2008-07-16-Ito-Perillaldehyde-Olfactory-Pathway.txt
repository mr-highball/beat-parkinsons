Inhaled perillaldehyde tested after induced smell loss in mice
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3136537/
PMID: 18955354
PMCID: PMC3136537
DOI: 10.1093/ecam/nen045
Coverage: Original paper, advance access July 2008, 2011 volume; methods and author abstract

Ito and colleagues tested inhaled purified l-perillaldehyde, a constituent of Perilla frutescens, in mice exposed to stress. The compound reduced forced-swim immobility at two tested exposure levels and the authors report unchanged locomotor activity. Experimentally induced anosmia prevented the immobility effect. The design tests whether intact smell pathways are needed for the behavioral response. It provides no estimate of restored smell and does not test eating dried shiso or Parkinson olfactory impairment.
