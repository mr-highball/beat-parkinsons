Title: Blood harmane concentrations and dietary protein consumption in essential tremor
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4993192/?report=reader
PMCID: PMC4993192
PMID: 16087903
DOI: 10.1212/01.wnl.0000172352.88359.2d
Publication: Neurology, August 2005
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated full original author manuscript review

This essential-tremor study compared 106 cases and 161 controls.
Reported animal protein intake averaged 50.2 and 49.4 g/day
respectively (p=.74). Blood harmane was higher in cases. Correlation
between animal protein and the blood measurement was close to zero
in cases and positive in controls.

The food questionnaire estimates aggregate intake. Essential tremor
is the study diagnosis. The study does not isolate baked turkey or
estimate Parkinson rest-tremor severity. Exact participant overlap
with other reports from the same research program is unverified.
