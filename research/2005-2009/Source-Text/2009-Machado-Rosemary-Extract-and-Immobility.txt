Coverage: publisher abstract and matching original body in a public third-party rendering.

The preparation was a Brazilian hydroalcoholic extract of dried rosemary stems and leaves. Acute oral extract reduced male Swiss mouse immobility in tail suspension (22.9% and 28% at two active doses) and forced swimming (49.5% in the reported active group). Repeated oral treatment was also associated with lower tail-suspension immobility. The original reports no clear locomotor change in open-field testing. These procedures do not measure human depression or the effect of eating fresh leaves.
