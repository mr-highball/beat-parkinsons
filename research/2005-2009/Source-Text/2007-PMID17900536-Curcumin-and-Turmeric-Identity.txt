Title: Curcumin as “Curecumin”: from kitchen to clinic

Publication date: 2007-08-19
Date basis: PubMed electronic publication date. The journal issue appeared in February 2008.
Collection: Source text
Review depth: primary_source, abstract and publisher excerpt
Checked: 2026-11-25

The authors identify curcumin as a compound from turmeric
(Curcuma longa). They discuss evidence for curcumin as a
constituent as well as the spice's history. The article provides no
basis for attributing its curcumin outcomes to Crocus sativus.

A subsequent diabetes and Alzheimer polyphenol review,
PMID 30081787, cites this article but describes saffron as a source
of curcumin in its section 4.6. The cited text names turmeric.
This citation does not establish a saffron exposure for depression
or fatigue in Round 1.
