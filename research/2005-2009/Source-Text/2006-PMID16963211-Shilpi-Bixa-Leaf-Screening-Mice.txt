Title: Preliminary pharmacological screening of Bixa orellana L. leaves.
PMID: 16963211
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2006.05.008
First published: 2006
Authors: Shilpi, Taufiq-Ur-Rahman, Uddin, Alam, Sadhu
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16963211/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse pharmacological screening of Bixa orellana leaf methanol extract (125, 250, 500 mg/kg). Reduced pentobarbitone sleep-onset latency (500 mg/kg), dose-dependently prolonged pentobarbitone sleeping time (250, 500 mg/kg), decreased open-field and hole-cross locomotor activity (all doses), dose-dependently reduced acetic-acid writhing, decreased castor-oil diarrheal stools, and delayed charcoal-meal GI transit (500 mg/kg). Limits: leaf methanol extract (different plant part), barbiturate-potentiation and acute screening proxies.
