Cumin extract and rifampicin exposure in rats
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17128432/

First published online November 24, 2006, before the February 2007 issue. Sachin and colleagues report increased plasma rifampicin after aqueous Cuminum cyminum seed extract in rats. For the isolated cumin flavonoid glycoside, they report a 35% higher maximum concentration and a 53% higher area under the concentration curve. The percentages describe the isolated compound with rifampicin. The accessible report gives no levodopa or Parkinson motor fluctuations. This extract or isolate differs from ground cumin in a meal.
