Title: Jerusalem artichoke and chicory inulin in bakery products affect faecal microbiota of healthy volunteers.
PMID: 17445348
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114507730751
First published: 2007
Authors: Kleessen, Schwarz, Boehm, Fuhrmann, Richter
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17445348/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-week double-blind RCT in 45 healthy adults testing snack bars with Jerusalem artichoke (Helianthus tuberosus) inulin vs chicory inulin vs placebo (companion Jerusalem artichoke trials: Ramnani 2010 PMID 20187995, Visuthranukul 2024 PMID 39033197). Included solely to document botanical species exclusion: Jerusalem artichoke (Helianthus tuberosus) is a distinct tuber-bearing Asteraceae genus/species and does not transfer to culinary globe artichoke (Cynara scolymus).
