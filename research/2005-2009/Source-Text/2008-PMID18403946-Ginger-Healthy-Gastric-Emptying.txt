Title: Effects of ginger on gastric emptying and motility in healthy humans
PMID: 18403946
DOI: 10.1097/MEG.0b013e3282f4b224
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18403946/
Institutional record: https://digital.library.adelaide.edu.au/items/0cc0e2c6-c5d1-4f3a-bb65-4fa1a6dea3d6
Published: 2008-05, European Journal of Gastroenterology & Hepatology 20(5), 436–440.
Archive scope: Factual summary of original author abstract corroborated by the institutional record.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Wu and colleagues studied 24 healthy adults twice in a blinded randomized comparison of three ginger capsules (total 1.2 g) or placebo before a low-nutrient liquid meal. Mean ultrasound half-emptying time was 13.1 ± 1.1 minutes with ginger and 26.7 ± 3.1 with placebo (mean ± SE, p<.01). The authors report more frequent antral contractions and no significant difference on the gastrointestinal sensation measures. The 2023 Aregawi paper gives 12 healthy volunteers for its citation 46. The original author abstract gives 24. Hu's later dyspepsia experiment reports 11 different participants.

The preparation and symptom item results need body verification. Faster emptying of a test meal in healthy adults is separate from colonic transit, constipation severity, pain relief or a clinical Parkinson effect.
