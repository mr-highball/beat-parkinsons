Mouth rinse in healthy young adults

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19170139/
Coverage: Original author abstract, factual extract.

Thirty-two young adults examined four weeks of rinsing three times daily with an aqueous pomegranate extract or a cornstarch control. Salivary total protein and activities of two enzymes decreased, and ceruloplasmin activity increased. The scavenging result depended on analysis. The reported outcomes address oral-health biochemistry without a raw aril or drooling comparison.
