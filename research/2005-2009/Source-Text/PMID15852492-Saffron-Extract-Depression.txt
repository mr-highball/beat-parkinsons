Title: Crocus sativus L. in the Treatment of Mild to Moderate Depression: A Double-blind, Randomized and Placebo-controlled Trial
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ptr.1647
PMID: 15852492
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.1647
Publication date: 2005-02
Date basis: February journal issue. Online record dated April 25, 2005.
Coverage: Original full report, Methods pp. 149–150 and Hamilton result

Akhondzadeh and colleagues randomized 40 adults with major depression to saffron capsules or placebo for six weeks. The publisher records first online publication on April 25, 2005, for its February 2005 issue. The investigators milled dried stigma and extracted it with 80% ethanol, then evaporated the solvent. Each capsule supplied 15 mg dried extract, taken twice daily for 30 mg extract daily. The Hamilton depression comparison favored the extract (F = 18.89, p < .001). This establishes an extract comparison in major depression. Transfer to whole ground saffron or Parkinson disease remains unresolved. [Primary report PDF](https://www.cbd-vital.de/media/solwin/productattachment/attachment/file/a/k/akondzadeh_et_al_2005.pdf)

The published Methods and Results disagree about completion. The Results describe 35 completers of 40 randomized, with one dropout under stigma extract and four under placebo. Analysis used last observation carried forward. The reported HAM-D change was −12.20 (SD 4.67) versus −5.10 (SD 4.71), p<.0001 for the comparison of changes. Recruitment was January 2002 to March 2004. This period precedes the 2006 petal report at the same center.
