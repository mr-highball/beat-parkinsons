Title: Differential progression of brain atrophy in Parkinson's disease with and without visual hallucinations
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19965847/
PMID: 19965847
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.2009.179655
Publication date: 2009-12-03
Date basis: Verified electronic publication
Source depth: Primary abstract; full article remains open
Evidence type: Longitudinal MRI and cognitive comparison
Format: Factual digest of the original abstract

Twelve initially nondemented Parkinson patients with visual hallucinations, 14 without them and 12 controls were assessed again at roughly 30 months. Three quarters of the visual hallucination group were diagnosed with dementia, compared with none in the other Parkinson group. The first group also had more widespread gray matter loss. These results describe disease progression within baseline visual symptom groups rather than an effect of changing dried fig intake.
