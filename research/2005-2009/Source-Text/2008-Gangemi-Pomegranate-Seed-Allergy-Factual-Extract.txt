Publication: S. Gangemi and colleagues, Pomegranate-dependent exercise-induced anaphylaxis. J Investig Allergol Clin Immunol 2008;18(6):491–492. PMID 19123450.
Primary scan: https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=ea3f98becc692c4700ef34a3ce8fbff79a717a40&repid=rep1&type=pdf
Coverage: Factual summary of original presentation and testing.

A patient reported pomegranate seed intake before dance and the subsequent development of widespread urticaria and lip edema. Fresh seed skin testing was positive. The reported allergy history included peach exposure. Investigators did not perform a combined repeat exposure and exercise challenge. The case provides allergy evidence with a possible exercise cofactor, without a visual symptom series or Parkinson diagnosis.
