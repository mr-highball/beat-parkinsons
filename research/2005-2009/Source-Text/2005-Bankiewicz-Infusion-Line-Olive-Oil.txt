Olive oil in a Parkinson model delivery apparatus
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2766604/
PMID: 16337943
PMCID: PMC2766604
DOI: 10.1016/j.expneurol.2005.10.022
Coverage: author manuscript Methods and results; sections on surgery and clinical rating

First published online December 9, 2005. The study used five parkinsonian monkeys in a preclinical gene-transfer comparison. The clinical rating covered posture, balance and arm bradykinesia. The Methods place olive oil in a nonsterile line in the delivery apparatus. That reference establishes apparatus use only. The measured behavior does not identify a dietary olive-oil or oil-grade effect. The later issue date is 2006.
