Title: Linoleic acid increases lectin-like oxidized LDL receptor-1 (LOX-1) expression in human aortic endothelial cells
Publication date: 2005-05
PMID: 15855339
DOI: 10.2337/diabetes.54.5.1506
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15855339/
Text coverage: Distilled original author abstract and comparison with 2004 EASD congress abstract 1275.

Cultured endothelial cells exposed to linoleic acid showed increased LOX-1 expression. The article abstract reports attenuation with several inhibitors or antioxidants. The same authors' conference abstract identifies curcumin among the individual pretreatments. Retain that compound-specific detail at conference depth until the full article tables are verified. The abstract and article belong to one study family. This is a cultured-cell exposure rather than a clinical turmeric test.
