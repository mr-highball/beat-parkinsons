Title: Health nutraceutical containing selfheal, Job's tears and adzuki bean
Patent publication: KR20070090485A
First public publication: 2007-09-06
Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/KR20070090485A/en
Format: Factual extraction from the original Korean application, composition examples and human Experimental Example 2, Table 5.
The patent evaluates four oral products in four healthy women per group over one week. The inactive comparison was a brown-rice product. Example 4 contained the base herbal mixture without perilla. Example 5 added perilla leaf and several other extracts to that mixture. Example 6 included those components with additional herbs.
Reported bowel-condition ratings on a five-point scale were 4.25, 4.50, 4.75 and 3.25 for Examples 4, 5, 6 and the comparator, respectively. There is no separate change from baseline, dispersion or randomized allocation detail. The comparison varies several components and supplies no fresh-shiso or Parkinson constipation result. Rat measurements in a separate experiment do not form part of this human comparison.
