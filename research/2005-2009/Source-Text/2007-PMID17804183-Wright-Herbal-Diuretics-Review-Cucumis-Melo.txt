Title: Herbal medicines as diuretics: a review of the scientific evidence.
PMID: 17804183
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2007.07.023
First published: 2007
Authors: Wright CI, Van-Buren L, Kroner CI, Koning MM
Journal: J Ethnopharmacol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17804183/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Ethnopharmacological review of herbal diuretics identifying Cucumis melo among species with traditional and limited preclinical diuretic claims (urine volume and sodium excretion), concluding clinical evidence remains scarce and insufficient to establish efficacy.
