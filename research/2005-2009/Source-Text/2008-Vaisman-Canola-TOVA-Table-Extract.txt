# Canola comparator and TOVA results in children

Source https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/87.5.1170
Coverage original Table 5 and author abstract

Vaisman and colleagues compared fish-oil and phospholipid omega-3 formulations against a canola-oil spread in children with inattention. The canola arm’s standardized total Test of Variables of Attention change was −0.45 ± 2.51 in Table 5 (within-arm p = .418), versus −0.42 ± 2.51 in the article abstract. The table gives response-time and variability changes without clear within-arm differences. These measurements compare different fats and leave an isolated refined-oil effect and Parkinson applicability unresolved. The contemporaneous patent follows the same study family.
