Title: Analgesic and sedative activities of lactucin and some lactucin-like guaianolides in mice.
PMID: 16621374
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2006.03.003
First published: 2006
Authors: Wesolowska A, Nikiforuk A, Michalska K, Kisiel W, Chojnacka-Wojcik E
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16621374/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract Facts: Mouse pharmacology study evaluating purified lactucin, lactucopicrin, and 11beta,13-dihydrolactucin (characteristic guaianolides of Lactuca virosa and Cichorium intybus, also present in trace quantities in Cichorium endivia) at 15 and 30 mg/kg: reduced hot-plate and tail-flick pain responses comparable to ibuprofen (30-60 mg/kg), and lactucin/lactucopicrin reduced spontaneous locomotor activity (sedation proxy). Crucial Matrix Boundary: Tested purified high-dose compounds (15-30 mg/kg) isolated from other Asteraceae species; as Guo et al. 2026 (PMID:41985394, Food Chemistry) emphasize, natural concentrations in whole leafy Cichorium plants are far too low for culinary eating to achieve pharmacological dosing, and no human or PD trial exists.
