Title: Curcumin inhibits glucose production in isolated mice hepatocytes
Publication date: 2008-01-24 (online first)
PMID: 18221818
DOI: 10.1016/j.diabres.2007.12.004
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18221818/
Original article proof: https://mhlw-grants.niph.go.jp/system/files/2007/072031/200712043A/200712043A0006.pdf
Text coverage: Distilled abstract, methods, results and selected figures from the seven-page original article proof in the public research-report appendix, printed appendix pages 106–112.

The laboratory comparison treated isolated mouse hepatocytes with extracted curcumin. At 25 micromolar, pyruvate-derived glucose production fell by about 40–45% over studied time points, and glycogenolysis was lower by about 20% after 120 minutes. Figure 7 reports about 30% lower activity of both glucose-6-phosphatase and phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase (PEPCK), with no significant effect on fructose-1,6-bisphosphatase. Figure 8 reports increased AMPK phosphorylation.

The 2004 EASD abstract 799 from the same research group reports no effect on PEPCK in its described work. The materials and exposure conditions cannot be fully reconciled between reports. Keep the discrepancy and treat them as overlapping research, with neither a whole-spice intervention nor a Parkinson endpoint.
