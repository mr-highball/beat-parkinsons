The authors compared two concentrations of Concord grape juice with a calorie-matched control in aging Fischer 344 rats. The lower concentration group showed changes in an ex vivo dopamine measure and spatial learning. The higher concentration group improved on motor performance tasks. The accessible abstract supplies no estimate for muscle rigidity and no human Parkinson population.

The AGE abstract 128 and the journal report match on the leading investigators, rat strain, ages, concentrations, comparator and arm-specific results. Treat the pair as one likely study family. The journal abstract does not give uncertainty estimates for the individual motor outcomes.
