The authors distinguish saffron from isolated crocetin and crocin. Their review discusses outcomes and pharmacology across several models. It supplies no isolated ground culinary saffron comparison for examiner-rated Parkinson rigidity. The introductory historical passage dates the Bencao Gangmu to 1596. The later review PMID 33817811 gives 1956 while citing this paper. The traditional record and inspected historical entry are cataloged separately.
