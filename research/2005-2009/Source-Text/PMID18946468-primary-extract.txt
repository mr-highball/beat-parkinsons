Title: Novelty Determines the Effects of Olfactory Enrichment on Memory and Neurogenesis Through Noradrenergic Mechanisms
Source: https://doi.org/10.1038/npp.2008.191
Author-uploaded primary full text: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/23411643_Novelty_Determines_the_Effects_of_Olfactory_Enrichment_on_Memory_and_Neurogenesis_Through_Noradrenergic_Mechanisms
PMID: 18946468
Publication: Online 22 October 2008. Neuropsychopharmacology 34, 786–795, issue 2009.
Text coverage: Original factual extraction from Methods and Figures 1–4. The complete publisher article is copyrighted.

Adult male C57BL/6J mice were assigned to standard housing, rotating single-odor enrichment, stable mixed-odor enrichment, and rotating enrichment with labetalol. Tarragon was one of 20 odors. The instrument was a suspended tea ball. Rotating groups received one odor daily and reused the sequence after 20 days. The stable mixture contained all 20 odors. The protocol lasted 47 days.

Olfactory testing used a 60-minute delay and a two-hole limonene versus carvone task. After the authors excluded animals with exploration time or visits outside two group SDs, sample sizes were 21 standard, 20 rotating, 15 stable, and 16 rotating with labetalol. Within the rotating group, novel versus familiar investigation time p = .03 and visits p = .004 by the author's one-sided paired tests. Against 50% chance, novel preference indices gave p = .004 for time and p = .002 for visits. The other conditions showed no clear preference. Separate between-group comparisons in total novel or familiar exploration time were p = .09 and p = .07, respectively. The behavioral comparisons do not estimate a tarragon effect.

Granule-layer BrdU-positive cell density was about 40% higher in the rotating condition, with the reported omnibus F(3,18) = 11.205, p = .0001 and pairwise Bonferroni p < .005. Immediate subventricular-zone BrdU counts were similar. The Article Fig. 4A legend timing and the Methods schedule differ. Figure 1 and Methods describe 47 total days and BrdU given on day 20, making the survival interval 27 days. The number of surviving cells cannot be treated as initial proliferation or clinical ability.
