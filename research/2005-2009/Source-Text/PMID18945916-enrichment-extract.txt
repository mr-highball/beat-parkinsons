Title: Turning Astrocytes from the Rostral Migratory Stream into Neurons: A Role for the Olfactory Sensory Organ
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6671355/
PMID: 18945916
PMCID: PMC6671355
DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3713-08.2008
Publication: 22 October 2008. Journal of Neuroscience 28, 11089–11102.
Text coverage: Original factual extraction from Methods, Table 2, and Figure 8. Copyright Society for Neuroscience.

Adult mice were randomized to odor pots presenting three distinct daily fragrances for three weeks or empty pots. Tarragon is one of many natural and chemical odors listed in Table 2. The study records tissue changes after a mixed enrichment schedule. It does not measure tarragon discrimination, threshold, memory or recognition. Six mice per group contributed to Figure 8. BrdU-labeled candidate stem-cell density increased across subventricular zone and both rostral migratory stream regions (overall treatment F(1,30) = 55.37, p < .0001). Neurogenesis measurements and the electrophysiology experiments do not give the combined odors' clinical or behavioral efficacy.
