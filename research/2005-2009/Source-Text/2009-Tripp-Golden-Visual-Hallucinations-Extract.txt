Title: Transient Complex Visual Hallucinations With Venlafaxine Treatment: A Case Report
Source: https://www.psychiatrist.com/pcc/transient-complex-visual-hallucinations-venlafaxine/
PMID: 19617939
PMCID: PMC2707121
DOI: 10.4088/pcc.08l00647
First published: 2009-04-16
Coverage: Factual extract of the original published letter.

The case describes complex visual hallucinations about one week after venlafaxine was started for depression. The hallucinations resolved while the dose continued. The authors discuss recent alcohol withdrawal and mild biochemical abnormalities as alternative explanations. They report no celery exposure or Parkinson diagnosis. This is a separate patient from the 2016 celery-root case and provides context for a possible medication explanation.
