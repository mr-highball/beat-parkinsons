Two tree workers developed skin inflammation and blistering after handling damaged fig-tree branches with sun exposure. The report concerns a topical plant contact reaction. It does not describe either fruit ingestion or a drooling symptom comparison. It does not add a clinical Parkinson signal to the separate oral-fig allergy cases.
