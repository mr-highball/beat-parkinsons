Primary prospective colonoscopy lubrication comparison, 2005, DOI 10.1055/s-2005-861051.
PMID: 15824944
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15824944/
Coverage: factual extract of original abstract.
The investigators compared standard colonoscopy lubrication with the additional instillation of corn oil through the colonoscope. They report lower procedure pain with the additional oil. The use is local and procedural, with grade unspecified and no oral or Parkinson comparison.
