Original source scope
Fourteen older adults with dysphagia underwent acute testing with several menthol concentrations and temperature-related comparisons. The investigators administered the test solutions directly to the pharynx in a clinical reflex assessment. The highest concentration reduced mean latency from 13.8 seconds with room-temperature distilled water to 9.4 seconds. Table 1 of the later synthesis describes menthol as oral, but this procedure did not examine ingestion of a menthol food. This is the crossover paper cited as reference 22 in PMID 34337829. The same review's reference 15 is the distinct black pepper oil parallel trial. Menthol does not replicate a black pepper outcome and cannot supply a cracked culinary pepper contrast.
