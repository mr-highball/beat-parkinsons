Arm pain and rest tremor in FMR1 premutation carriers
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685192/

June 20, 2008 online, with a 2009 issue. In a four-case series, Hall and colleagues describe arm pain in case 1. The report describes resting tremor and qualitative dopaminergic responses. Case 1 has a composite motor UPDRS score of 11, and case 3 has no quantitative motor scale. The source provides neither a separate pain or rest-tremor treatment estimate nor a dried date or raw fig comparison.
