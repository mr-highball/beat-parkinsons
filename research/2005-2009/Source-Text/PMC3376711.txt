Title: Dopaminergic drugs modulate learning rates and perseveration in Parkinson's patients in a dynamic foraging task.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3376711/
PMID: 19955362
PMCID: PMC3376711
DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3524-09.2009
Publication date: 2009-12-02
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC complete article HTML.

The Parkinson experiment compares behavior in a dynamic choice task across dopaminergic medication conditions. Its outcomes are learning and perseveration. It neither administers sage nor measures a separate dystonia outcome.
