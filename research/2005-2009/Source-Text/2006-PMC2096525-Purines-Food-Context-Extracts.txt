The 2006 invited lectures and meeting abstracts include isolated caffeine across human and animal pharmacology. Members studied acute EEG and sleep, sensory gating, stress physiology, pain combinations, and adenosine-mediated mechanisms. Results and doses are member-specific. The restraint/DNFB mouse experiment used acetone and olive oil to deliver a skin sensitizer. A separate mouse sensitization abstract used ovalbumin as an antigen to measure a response. References to chicken receptor proteins, tomato lectin, acridine orange and investigator names denote laboratory models or lexical collisions. The selected members do not establish an effect of brewing or eating the ingredients on a Parkinson symptom.
