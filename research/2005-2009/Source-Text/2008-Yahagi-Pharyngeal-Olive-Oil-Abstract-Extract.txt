Title: Direct pharyngeal olive oil stimulation and voluntary swallows
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18930115/
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Yahagi and colleagues, Neuroscience Letters, first posted October 10, 2008. The indexed abstract describes slow infusion of liquids by tube to tongue and pharyngeal regions, with voluntary swallowing intervals recorded by submental electromyography.
The abstract reports shorter intervals under pharyngeal water stimulation and prolonged intervals with oil and salt. The oil was identified as olive oil with no grade. The accessible abstract gives no numerical estimates or clinical swallowing severity. This local stimulus procedure differs from eating a meal.
