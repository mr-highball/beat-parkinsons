Original study scope
PubMed indexes the paper as Evaluation of a new device to prevent falls in persons with Parkinson’s disease. The same PMID, PMCID and DOI identify the author manuscript titled An evaluation of self-administration of auditory cueing to improve gait in people with Parkinson’s disease. Twenty-one participants took their regular medication. On the instrumented walkway, stride length improved after a week of home pacing in the uncued follow-up comparison (paired p=.001). A therapist guarded against falls; the gait comparisons did not estimate a fall incidence rate. The exposure is an auditory pacer.

Continuing evidence gaps
The author manuscript gives the intervention and gait results. Neither the PubMed title nor stride results establish a sage effect.
