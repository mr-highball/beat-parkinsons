Anxiety scores in a dementia memory notebook pilot
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/reader/10.1177/1533317508320794

June 30, 2008 first online, with an October–November issue. Five participants with very mild dementia and four spouse coaches attended memory notebook sessions. Tables 6 and 7 give DASS-21 anxiety means of 2.00 to 3.20 for dementia participants and 1.00 to 0.75 for spouses, with no significance marker for these anxiety comparisons. The study has no Parkinson-specific group, salmon exposure, or fall-frequency outcome.
