Title: Efficacy of soy isoflavones for depressive symptoms of the climacteric syndrome.
PMID: 19339127
DOI: 10.1016/j.maturitas.2009.02.008
First published: 2009
Authors: de Sousa-Muñoz RL, Filizola RG
Journal: Maturitas
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19339127/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind, placebo-controlled randomized trial in 84 climacteric outpatient women in Brazil receiving 120 mg/day soy isoflavone extract (SIE) vs placebo for 16 weeks, evaluated on the Center for Epidemiologic Studies Depression (CES-D) scale at baseline, week 8, and week 16. Results: both groups declined from baseline (SIE 12.5 -> 8.2; placebo 13.0 -> 9.4), with NULL statistically significant between-group difference at 16 weeks. Authors concluded soy isoflavones did not significantly reduce affective depressive symptoms beyond placebo. Directly counters positive depression claims from high-RoB/open-label isoflavone studies.
