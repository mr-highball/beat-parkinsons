Original source scope and evidence boundary
Twelve healthy volunteers participated in a randomized placebo-controlled crossover. The trial beverage included decaffeinated extract plus added L-theanine and theogallin. The author abstract reports changes in delta and theta EEG, and shorter late P300 latency interpreted as attention. It provides no fatigue questionnaire, Parkinson cohort or steeped leaf group. The European Medicines Agency lists the report under an uncontrolled heading, whereas the original explicitly describes randomization, crossover and placebo. The preparation and outcome boundaries remain regardless of that classification difference.
