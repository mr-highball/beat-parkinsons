Title: Pomegranate juice and extracts provide similar levels of plasma and urinary ellagitannin metabolites in human subjects
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3196216/
PMID: 18598186
PMCID: PMC3196216
DOI: 10.1089/jmf.2007.650
Publication date: 2008-06
Date basis: Original journal issue.
Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: full_text

Sixteen healthy volunteers consumed pomegranate juice, pomegranate liquid extract and pomegranate powder extract in sequence with a week between exposures. The original reports no statistically clear difference in plasma ellagic acid exposure over six hours or in urinary urolithin A glucuronide across the three products. Peak plasma concentration occurred later after powder than after the two liquid products. This study reports short-term metabolite exposure. It provides no cross-fruit beverage antioxidant ranking, tested grape exposure or Parkinson symptom comparison.

Citation resolution: This is the Seeram 2008 human pharmacokinetic paper listed in the bibliography of PMC13340997. The primary beverage antioxidant comparison is PMID 18220345, DOI 10.1021/jf073035s.
