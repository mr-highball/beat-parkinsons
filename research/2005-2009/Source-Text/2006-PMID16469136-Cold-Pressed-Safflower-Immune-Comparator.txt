Title: Cold-pressed flaxseed oil reverses age-associated depression in a primary cell-mediated adaptive immune response in the mouse
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16469136/
PMID: 16469136
DOI: 10.1079/bjn20051647
First public article date: 2006-02
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled indexed original abstract. Full diet and test tables remain an access lead.

Old C57BL/6J mice received complete diets differing in cold-pressed oil source for eight weeks. The groups used safflower or flaxseed oil and were compared with young adult and older zero-time controls. At the immune challenge, the safflower group had a cell-mediated response at 20% of the young adult level (p=.0002). The flaxseed group exceeded the safflower group and the older zero-time controls. This is an immune response to sheep red blood cell antigen. It does not show that the safflower diet itself worsened an age-related swallowing symptom. The exact cold-pressed grade is documented, while disease and outcome fit are remote.
