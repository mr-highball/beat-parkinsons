NCT00235716, TEAM-AD vitamin E and memantine trial. Official record https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00235716. First public posting 2005-10-10. Native official JSON retrieved 2026-11-15 UTC. Coverage: original factual registry extract.

The four arms combine active and matching placebo vitamin E and memantine in patients with Alzheimer disease. The Neuropsychiatric Inventory measures frequency and severity for 12 domains and uses their total as the trial endpoint. The original paper at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4109898/ identifies soybean oil in the vitamin E placebo capsule; its source was Arista Industries. The official full results were first posted January 29, 2014.
