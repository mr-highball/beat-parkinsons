The retrospective brain bank chart review included 782 pathologically diagnosed cases, including 474 with Parkinson disease. Medical records supplied clinical feature histories. Falls and fractures and their timing were the study outcomes. In the Parkinson column of Table 2, hallucinations were recorded in 53.3% of fallers and 37.5% of nonfallers in the univariable comparison, p=0.004. The methods describe checking visual hallucinations, while the table labels the row generically.

The association concerns documented symptom presence and fall status. It does not compare within-person visual episode frequency or any dried-fig exposure.
