Title: Efficacy and tolerability of a spray with Salvia officinalis in the treatment of acute pharyngitis
Source: https://daignet.de/media/filer_public/0f/6a/0f6a5d05-8fad-4163-a528-8e86ae454d80/hubbert_kopie.pdf
Identifier: PMID 16504956
First published: January 31, 2006
Coverage: Factual extract from primary Methods and Results.

The adaptive randomized study enrolled 122 people in its dose-selection phase and 164 in its comparison phase. The latter compared topical 15% common-sage ethanolic leaf extract with a matched local spray containing 0.3% sage to aid blinding. The two-hour VAS pain area under the curve favored the higher concentration at p = .0021 in the comparison phase. Approximate two-hour pain reductions were 44% and 34%. Differences in achieving 50% or complete relief were inconclusive. The study did not test ordinary fresh-leaf ingestion or Parkinson pain.
