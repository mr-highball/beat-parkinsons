Source depth: Complete official PMC main text with symptom denominators in Table 5
Format: Original-source factual extract

The record-based clinical comparison assessed Alzheimer disease with and without amygdala Lewy bodies. Unrecorded items were not coded as absent. Table 5 reports 10 of 24 and 9 of 30 for documented hallucination history, respectively, without a significant contrast. Those are item-specific denominators from larger groups of 62 and 52. The table does not disaggregate visual events or report repeated episode counts. The disease groups and pathology measures differ from the dried-fig Parkinson claim.
