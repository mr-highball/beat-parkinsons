Title: Cannabidiol for the treatment of psychosis in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18801821/
PMID: 18801821
DOI: 10.1177/0269881108096519
First public article date: 2008-09-18
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated original Methods and Table 1

The open-label study follows six Parkinson outpatients with
psychosis during a four-week course of cannabidiol in corn
oil alongside their usual treatment. The Table 1 aggregate
motor UPDRS medians are 44.5 at baseline and 36 at endpoint,
with a nonsignificant change. The report gives a different
result for the UPDRS total and no rest-tremor item.

The Methods mention a matching corn-oil placebo, but describe
all six participants receiving cannabidiol and give no placebo
phase or separate placebo results. The carrier grade is
unreported. These measurements cannot isolate an oil effect.
