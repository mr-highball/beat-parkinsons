Title: Cortical alpha-synuclein load is associated with amyloid-beta plaque burden in a subset of Parkinson's disease patients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18185940/
PMID: 18185940
DOI: 10.1007/s00401-007-0336-0
Publication date: 2008-01-08
Date basis: Publisher and NLM electronic date
Source depth: Primary author abstract; body remains open
Evidence type: Postmortem plaque and Lewy body association
Format: Factual digest of the inspected source

The authors compared 40 Parkinson autopsies with 20 age-matched controls. The cortical association was stronger in the subgroup with moderate or greater amyloid plaque burden. The [2011 Compta report] identifies 23 of its 56 Parkinson cases as partly assessed previously in this study. The abstract has no visual-episode recurrence or assigned dried-fig exposure.
