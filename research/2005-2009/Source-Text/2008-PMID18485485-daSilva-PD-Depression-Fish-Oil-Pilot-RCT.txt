Title: Depression in Parkinson's disease: a double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled pilot study of omega-3 fatty-acid supplementation
PMID: 18485485
DOI: 10.1016/j.jad.2008.03.008
First published: 2008
Authors: da Silva TM, Munhoz RP, Alvarez C, Naliwaiko K, Kiss A, Andreatini R, Ferraz AC
Journal: Journal of Affective Disorders
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18485485/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Double-blind placebo-controlled pilot RCT in 31 PD patients with DSM-IV major depression (29 completed) given isolated fish-oil omega-3 capsules vs mineral oil for 12 weeks. Fish-oil group showed lower MADRS and CGI-Depression scores (42% >=50% MADRS reduction, 22% remission) with NO between-group difference on BDI. Tested isolated fish-oil capsules rather than canned or cooked herring (remote exposure fit) and requires replication given conflicting adult omega-3/fish depression evidence (VITAL-DEP, Ness 2003, Appleton 2021 Cochrane).
