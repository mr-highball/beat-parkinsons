The study compares diagnostic findings in human prion disease and control samples. The brain-biopsy section describes visual hallucinations during a rapidly progressive illness evaluated for Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease. The vignette and main diagnostic results do not provide a Parkinson cohort, a separated visual episode rate or a fig exposure. The figures are illustrations of study findings.
