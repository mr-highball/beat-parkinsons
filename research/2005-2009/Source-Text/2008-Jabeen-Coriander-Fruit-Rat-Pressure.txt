2008 — Intravenous dried coriander fruit extract lowered arterial pressure in anesthetized rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19146935/
Coverage: Factual extract of Methods, Results and Figure 5.

Ground dried coriander fruit was extracted with aqueous methanol. Intravenous treatment of anesthetized rats produced a reported maximum arterial pressure reduction of 40.84 ± 6.34% (n = 5). That experiment gives no oral leaf result. The first online date was December 25, 2008.
