Title: Aqueous saffron extract, crocin, safranal and drug-induced sleep in mice
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ptr.2597
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19142981/
PMID: 19142981
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.2597
Publication date: 2009-01-13
Date basis: Wiley first online publication, before the June issue.
Review basis: Original author abstract verified against the publisher and NLM. The complete comparative tables and group denominators were unavailable.
Source type: Controlled animal pharmacology experiment.

Hosseinzadeh and Noraei compared injected aqueous saffron stigma extract, purified crocin and safranal in mice. The sleep outcome was duration of pentobarbital-induced hypnosis. The accessible original reports a longer total induced sleep at only the highest tested extract level and a dose-ordered increase with safranal. Crocin did not show an apparent hypnotic effect in this assay. The aqueous extract also reduced locomotor activity and affected rotarod motor coordination in separate comparisons.

This result distinguishes three preparations and an induced-sleep endpoint. There was no oral ground-spice comparison, spontaneous insomnia measure or Parkinson population. The 2018 cardiovascular review cites this original as a hypnotic lead. For Round 1 claim 0218 the experiment supplies pharmacology context and preserves the crocin comparison at its observed depth.
