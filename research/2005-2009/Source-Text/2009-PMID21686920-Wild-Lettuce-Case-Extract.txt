Title: Wild lettuce (Lactuca virosa) toxicity
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3031874/
PMCID: PMC3031874
PMID: 21686920
DOI: 10.1136/bcr.06.2008.0134
First public article date: 2009-04-28
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated full original case-report review

Besharat and colleagues describe eight patients with an acute
illness after ingestion of a plant identified as wild lettuce.
One had ataxia, visual blurring and later loss of consciousness.
Others had combinations of agitation, anxiety, hallucinations,
gastrointestinal symptoms, pupillary dilation or urinary
retention. All eight recovered. The case descriptions give no
separate tremor finding or Parkinson cohort. This was wild
Lactuca virosa, a species distinct from cultivated romaine.
