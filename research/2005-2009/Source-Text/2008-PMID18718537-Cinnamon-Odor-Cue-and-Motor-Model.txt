Original source scope
In the social transmission of food preferences assay, mice received cinnamon- or cocoa-flavored chow. Flavor and jar position were counterbalanced, and preference was evaluated by the proportion of cued flavor consumed after social exposure. Other assessments examined mood or motor outcomes by lesion status.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Cinnamon is an actual sensory cue and chow ingredient, with no cinnamon-specific contrast for anxiety or rigidity. Figure references do not represent raw fig exposure.
