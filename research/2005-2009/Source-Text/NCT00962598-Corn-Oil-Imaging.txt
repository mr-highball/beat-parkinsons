Adolescent depression imaging substudy registration
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00962598
Registration: NCT00962598
Coverage: Official registration, first posted August 20, 2009 (estimated); current flow checked

The registration describes a ten-week adolescent major depression neuroimaging study of omega-3 compared with matched corn-oil capsules. Its participant-flow fields specify that participants for the imaging work came from a separate protocol, GCO 12-1321, and list 16 enrollees, seven corn and nine omega-3. The later clinical publication reports 51 randomized and names the same registry. The sources leave the relationship between the 16 registered participants and the full clinical analysis unresolved. Results first posted May 24, 2017.
