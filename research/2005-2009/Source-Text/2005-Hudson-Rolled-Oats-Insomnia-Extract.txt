Factual text from Methods and Table I, original copy https://www.academia.edu/87973134/Protein_source_tryptophan_versus_pharmaceutical_grade_tryptophan_as_an_efficacious_treatment_for_chronic_insomnia
Fifty-seven adults with chronic primary insomnia were randomized, with 49 completing. The control Food3 used 50 g rolled oats nightly for one week, 17 assigned and 15 completing. Active foods used gourd seed or pharmaceutical tryptophan, with oats also in an active food.
Rolled-oat arm baseline, treatment and post-treatment means: total sleep 359, 376 and 400 min; sleep efficiency 74.8%, 76.9% and 81.0%; total wake 122, 114 and 93.7 min. These are diary outcomes. Treatment-week improvements were inconclusive. Week-by-condition interactions were inconclusive across sleep variables. The later no-food week was a follow-up period, not a randomized no-oat parallel arm. The study gives no clinical Parkinson result.
