Title: The use of medicinal herbs by diabetic Jordanian patients
Authors: S A Otoom, S A Al-Safi, Z K Kerem, A Alkofahi
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17182483/
Date: 2006, Journal of Herbal Pharmacotherapy 6(2):31–41
Coverage: factual extract of the primary abstract

The survey interviewed 310 patients with diabetes. Ninety-six reported using herbs, of whom 10.4% mentioned Coriandrum sativum. An aggregate 36.5% reported adverse symptoms, including dizziness, among a range of complaints. The abstract does not link dizziness to coriander users or give preparation, standing challenge or timing.
