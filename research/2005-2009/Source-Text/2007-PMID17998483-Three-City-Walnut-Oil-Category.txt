The cohort analyzed 8,085 community-dwelling older adults who were dementia-free at baseline. The omega-3 rich cooking or dressing oil category grouped colza (rapeseed), walnut and soybean oils.
The adjusted all-cause dementia estimate for regular use of oils in this category was HR 0.46 (95% CI 0.19–1.11). The category does not isolate walnut oil, capture a cold-pressed preparation, or include a Parkinson rigidity or bradykinesia endpoint.
The separately archived 2008 News Briefs report mentions flaxseed oil in this grouping. The original analytical definition lists colza, walnut and soybean oils and supplies no flaxseed oil estimate. The news report and the cohort share one underlying study.
