Title: Influence of cabbage processing methods and prebiotic manipulation of colonic microflora on glucosinolate breakdown in man.
PMID: 17403273
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114507709091
First published: 2007
Authors: Fuller Z, Louis P, Mihajlovski A, Rungapamestry V, Ratcliffe B, Duncan AJ
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17403273/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Human randomized crossover feeding study (n=6 volunteers) quantifying urinary isothiocyanate breakdown products after consuming raw vs cooked green/white cabbage with or without inulin prebiotic. Confirms that cooking cabbage denatures endogenous plant myrosinase and dramatically lowers isothiocyanate bioavailability relative to raw macerated cabbage. Directly constrains cooked green cabbage bioactive transfer.
