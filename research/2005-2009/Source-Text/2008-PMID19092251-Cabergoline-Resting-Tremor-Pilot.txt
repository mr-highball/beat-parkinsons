Title: Effect of cabergoline on parkinsonian tremor assessed by long-term actigraphy
PMID: 19092251
DOI: 10.1159/000186505
Publication date: 2008-12-18
Date basis: Electronic publication. The printed issue appeared in 2009.
Citation: Binder S, Deuschl G and Volkmann J. European Neurology 61:149–153.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19092251/
Publisher: https://karger.com/ene/article-abstract/61/3/149/124244/Effect-of-Cabergoline-on-Parkinsonian-Tremor
Coverage: Author abstract and publisher issue record. Publisher full-text and PDF routes expose the abstract preview. Exact effect size, comparator and dosing context need the full paper.

The clinical pilot studied cabergoline in ten people with tremor-dominant Parkinson disease. Its clinical measure includes UPDRS Part III item 20 for resting tremor. The abstract reports lower clinical tremor scores, shorter tremor duration and lower amplitude on the corresponding actigraphy and diary outcomes. It provides no numeric item effect, interval, or fruit exposure. The finding applies directly to the study's drug and clinical measure, with the food-to-symptom transfer untested.
