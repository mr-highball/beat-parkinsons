Title: Impact of estragole and other odorants on the flavour of anise and tarragon
Source: https://doi.org/10.1002/ffj.1765
DOI: 10.1002/ffj.1765
Publication: Online 8 December 2006. Flavour and Fragrance Journal 22, 105–113, issue 2007.
Text coverage: Original factual extraction of the publisher's abstract.

Primary aroma chemistry work on tarragon leaf samples used aroma extract dilution analysis, and divided chemical concentrations by thresholds reported for starch or cellulose. The published tarragon odor activity values were beta-ionone 5862, eugenol 2653, and estragole 460. Estragole was abundant at 2900 mg/kg. These values characterize a material and a measurement matrix. Human health, symptom change and tarragon ingestion effects were unmeasured. Preparation details beyond the leaf samples and matrices in the publisher abstract remain unresolved.
