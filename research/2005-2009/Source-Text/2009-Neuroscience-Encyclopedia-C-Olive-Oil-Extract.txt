Both XML copies identify the same C section of the reference work.
Each includes 615 top-level entries. Screening member bodies for
olive and compound aliases and rest tremor phrases surfaced two
separate entries requiring closer reading.

Entry 131, Central Nervous System Disease – Natural Neuroprotective
Agents as Therapeutics (Sec466), mentions olive-oil flavonoids in a
general discussion of candidate anti-inflammatory compounds. It
does not test oral extra-virgin olive oil or evaluate rest tremor.

Entry 203, Chaperones: Protein Trafficking (Sec735), names resting
tremor as a Parkinson clinical feature in a discussion of protein
processing. It has no olive-oil administration or comparative
rest-tremor finding. The two terms occur in distinct entries.
