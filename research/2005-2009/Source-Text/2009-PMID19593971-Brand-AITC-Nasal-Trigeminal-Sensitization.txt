Title: Peripheral and central levels in nasal trigeminal sensitization and desensitization
PMID: 19593971
First published: 2009
Authors: Brand G, Jacquot L
Journal: Rhinology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19593971/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Human monorhinal psychophysical and skin-conductance study of volatile allyl isothiocyanate (AITC) nasal stimulation during normal breathing, showing central trigeminal sensitization and peripheral trigeminal desensitization. Demonstrates intranasal AITC acts on cranial nerve V trigeminal chemesthesis (pungency/irritation) rather than restoring cranial nerve I olfactory acuity.
