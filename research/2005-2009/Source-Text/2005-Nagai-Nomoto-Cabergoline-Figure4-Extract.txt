Coverage: Original Japanese text and visual review of Figure 4, printed page 275.

Figure 4 depicts cabergoline concentration after seven days of grapefruit juice coadministration and cabergoline alone. The two series span hours 0–4. The plot reads approximately 670 versus 280 pg/mL at hour 0, and 750 versus 370 pg/mL at hour 4. These are visual approximations. The display lacks a denominator, variability bars and a measure of symptom severity. The relation of the plotted patients to the five-person conference report is unverified; the two reports are grouped conservatively in one family. Figure 4 identifies a grapefruit-juice comparison with cabergoline. The adjacent selegiline text describes possible metabolism interactions generally without an independent grapefruit comparison.
