Title: HPLC quantification of major active components from 11 different saffron (Crocus sativus L.) sources

The chemical comparison measured ten constituents in 11 certified
Crocus sativus samples from different sources. The reported range
in carotenoid composition concerned the particular samples tested.
The paper also notes that preparation and measurement methods can
influence the estimates reported across studies.

The analysis supports treating saffron material and its chemical
profile as separate attributes from the label on a package. There
was no human intake, disease outcome, or comparison of Parkinson
symptoms. The article is cited as reference 41 of PMID 30479218.
