Title: Separate fatigue ratings after controlled caffeine or placebo maintenance
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2738587/
PMID: 19241060
PMCID: PMC2738587
DOI: 10.1007/s00213-009-1489-4
Publication date: 2009-02-25
Date basis: First electronic publication.
Review depth: Complete identified PMC structured original, crossover design, Results and Figure 3 locators.

Sixteen regular caffeine users underwent blinded caffeine or placebo maintenance and short matched or crossed challenges. The POMS fatigue item differed by condition (F(3,45)=4.64, p=.007). Acute placebo after caffeine maintenance had the highest fatigue rating and exceeded caffeine challenge after caffeine or placebo maintenance and the two chronic conditions pooled in the item analyses. The fatigue subscale had a condition effect (p=.039). Its only significant individual post-hoc contrast was between acute abstinence after caffeine and acute caffeine after placebo maintenance. The strict chronic placebo comparison must be distinguished from chronic conditions combined.

This study supports a withdrawal-linked fatigue contrast for isolated caffeine. It does not assign whole brewed beverages or measure fatigue in Parkinson disease. EEG and blood velocity outcomes do not provide additional independent fatigue findings.
