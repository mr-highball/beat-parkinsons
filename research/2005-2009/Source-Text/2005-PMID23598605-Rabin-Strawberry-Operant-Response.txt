Title: Effect of diet on the disruption of operant responding at different ages following exposure to 56Fe particles
PMID: 23598605
DOI: 10.1007/s11357-005-4000-0
Publication: AGE, volume 27, pages 69–73, 2005
Source: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11357-005-4000-0.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original article, methods, figures and limitations

Rabin and colleagues compared rat diets containing 2% strawberry or blueberry extract with a control feed in a heavy-ion irradiation experiment. The outcome in Figures 1–3 is operant bar presses across reinforcement ratios. For the 2.0 Gy exposure, early testing at five and eight months favored the strawberry regimen in the reported patterns. Figure 1 compares irradiated strawberry-fed rats with several small groups (seven in the irradiated strawberry group and six in the irradiated control group). The plotted significance labels in that figure use the nonirradiated control comparison.

The study reports no significant treatment or treatment-by-reinforcement-schedule comparison at 13 and 18 months. Only two irradiated rats remained in the control-diet group at 13 months and one at 18 months. The paper cites a separate 1.5 Gy experiment. It measured a reward-driven task rather than frequency of spontaneous falls, and it did not study people with Parkinson disease.
