Title: Effects of pre-germinated brown rice on depression-like behavior in mice.
PMID: 17258802
DOI: 10.1016/j.pbb.2006.12.008
First published: 2007
Authors: Mamiya T, Kise M, Morikawa K, Aoto H, Ukai M, Noda Y
Journal: Pharmacology, biochemistry, and behavior
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17258802/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Thirty-day mouse feeding study showing pre-germinated brown rice (PGBR) pellets reduced forced swim test immobility and learned-helplessness escape failures vs control diet, accompanied by increased frontal cortex serotonin (5-HT) and lower 5-HIAA/5-HT ratio. Preclinical non-PD depression model.
