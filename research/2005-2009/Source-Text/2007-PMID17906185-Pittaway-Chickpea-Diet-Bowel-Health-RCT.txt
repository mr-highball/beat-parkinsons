Title: Effects of a controlled diet supplemented with chickpeas on serum lipids, glucose tolerance, satiety and bowel function.
PMID: 17906185
DOI: 10.1080/07315724.2007.10719620
First published: 2007
Authors: Pittaway JK, Ahuja KD, Robertson IK, Ball MJ
Journal: J Am Coll Nutr
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17906185/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized crossover controlled dietary trial (Pittaway et al., 2007, J Am Coll Nutr, n=27 free-living healthy adults) comparing 5 weeks of a chickpea-supplemented diet (canned drained chickpeas plus 30% chickpea-flour bread and shortbread biscuits) versus a fiber-matched wheat diet. Alongside modest total and LDL cholesterol reductions, perceived general bowel health improved significantly during the chickpea phase compared with the wheat phase. Limits: non-Parkinson healthy adults, mixed exposure combining canned drained chickpeas with chickpea-flour baked foods, subjective perceived bowel-health measure rather than Rome/Bristol constipation severity.
