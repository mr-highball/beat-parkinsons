Title: Effects of bilateral deep brain stimulation of the subthalamic nucleus on olfactory function in Parkinson's disease patients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2790799/
PMID: 18477840
PMCID: PMC2790799
DOI: 10.1159/000131662
Publication date: 2008-05-13
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: NLM original article HTML, full methods and outcome tables. Factual extract.

The Parkinson study measured odor detection and identification under medication and stimulation conditions. A banana-like amyl acetate stimulus was used among other test odours. This stimulus did not involve eating raw bananas. Anxiety appears among possible nonmotor features in the background and is not a banana-treatment endpoint.
