Title: Pharmacokinetic interaction of some antitubercular drugs with caraway: implications in the enhancement of drug bioavailability
Authors: B S Sachin and colleagues
Date: 2009-04
Identifiers: PMID 19734267; DOI 10.1177/0960327108097431
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19734267/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the primary abstract.

In Wistar rats, the standardized butanolic caraway seed fraction CC-1a increased Cmax and AUC of rifampicin, isoniazid and pyrazinamide. The authors report that a comparison using a 40% lower drug regimen with the fraction reached comparable concentration endpoints to the full-dose regimen in that model. They separately tested intestinal permeation. No levodopa or companion Parkinson drug was administered, and no neurological symptom was the outcome.
