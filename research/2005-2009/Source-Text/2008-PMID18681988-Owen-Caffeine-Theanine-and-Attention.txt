Original source scope and evidence boundary
Twenty-seven healthy volunteers received caffeine, caffeine plus L-theanine and placebo in repeated visits. The author abstract reports improved subjective alertness after caffeine at one assessment, and favored attention switching and distraction outcomes after the combination. It does not give a separate fatigue-severity estimate. This study uses different ingredient amounts from the 2010 Giesbrecht report. Some authors overlap across the industry research program. Recruitment overlap with other reports is unverified and this source supplies no independent beverage-fatigue effect.
