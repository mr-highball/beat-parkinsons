Title: Piperine, the potential functional food for mood and cognitive disorders.
PMID: 18639606
DOI: 10.1016/j.fct.2008.06.014
First published: 2008
Authors: Wattanathorn J, Chonpathompikunlert P, Muchimapura S, Priprem A, Tankamnerdthai O
Journal: Food and chemical toxicology : an international journal published for the British Industrial Biological Research Association
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18639606/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled rat experiment administering oral piperine (5, 10, 20 mg/kg/d x 4 weeks) to male Wistar rats with behavioral testing after single dose and weeks 1-4. All tested doses demonstrated antidepressant-like activity and spatial cognitive enhancement across treatment durations. Limits: isolated piperine in healthy rats.
