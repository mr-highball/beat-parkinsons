Sunflower oil blend in a midlife distress trial
Source: https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/obj/thesescanada/vol1/QQLA/TC-QQLA-25538.pdf
PMID: 19116322
DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.2008.26749
Coverage: Author thesis Chapter 6 Methods, Results and Table 2 reviewed; linked paper first posted December 30, 2008

Lucas and colleagues enrolled 120 women aged 40–55 with psychological distress. The 61-person comparator arm took 500 mg sunflower-oil capsules three times daily for eight weeks. The author methods specify 0.2% fish oil for masking and leave refining grade unspecified. The adjusted EPA-minus-comparator changes were −0.08 for HSCL-D-20 (95% CI −0.27 to 0.12) and −1.5 for HAM-D-21 (95% CI −3.7 to 0.7). Both groups improved on symptom scales. The trial used an active oil blend and did not recruit for Parkinson disease. Registry ISRCTN69617477 identifies this study family. The later ISRCTN47431149 trial used a different cohort and formulation.
