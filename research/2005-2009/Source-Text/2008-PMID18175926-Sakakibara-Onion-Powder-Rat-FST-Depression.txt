Title: Antidepressant-like effect of onion (Allium cepa L.) powder in a rat behavioral model of depression.
PMID: 18175926
DOI: 10.1271/bbb.70454
First published: 2008
Authors: Sakakibara H, Yoshino S, Kawai Y, Terao J
Journal: Bioscience, Biotechnology, and Biochemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18175926/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Controlled rat forced swimming test (FST): daily oral onion powder at 50 mg/kg/day for 14 days significantly reduced FST immobility without altering locomotor function, independent of plasma corticosterone, while suppressing hypothalamic dopamine turnover. Important dose caveat: a higher onion dosage produced NO significant antidepressant effect in the FST. Non-PD rodent behavioral despair assay.
