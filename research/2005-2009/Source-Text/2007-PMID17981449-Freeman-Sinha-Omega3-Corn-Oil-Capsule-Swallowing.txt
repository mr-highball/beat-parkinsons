Title: Tolerability of omega-3 fatty acid supplements in perinatal women.
PMID: 17981449
DOI: 10.1016/j.plefa.2007.09.004
First published: 2007
Authors: Freeman MP, Sinha P
Journal: Prostaglandins Leukot Essent Fatty Acids
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17981449/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Tolerability report from a perinatal depression RCT (Freeman & Sinha, 2007, J Clin Psychiatry) comparing omega-3 fish-oil capsules versus corn oil plus 1% fish oil placebo capsules. Difficulty swallowing large gelatin capsules was reported comparably across arms (6 vs 7 adverse events), reflecting physical pill size rather than any pharmacological effect of culinary corn oil on neurogenic dysphagia.
