Title: Cochrane review of postoperative antiemetics: ginger arms and endpoint distinctions
PMID: 16856030
PMCID: PMC6463839
DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD004125.pub2
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6463839/
Published: 2006-07-19
Archive scope: Factual note from the original article, original page scans or identified original abstract member.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The complete original review includes oral ginger in trials such as Arfeen 1995, Bone 1990 and Phillips 1993. For ginger versus placebo the review reports postoperative nausea RR 0.87 (95% CI 0.62–1.23) and rescue antiemetic RR 0.40 (0.18–0.88). These are separate endpoint sets. The summary table and results prose give different numbers for vomiting (1.04 versus 1.00) and the composite (1.02 versus 0.79). Their intervals all include 1. No dose-response relationship for ginger was established.

This is a review of postoperative nausea and vomiting, with drug safety measured in separate comparisons. The included ginger trials provide no grated-ginger estimate for chronic Parkinson constipation, pain or anxiety. Later network reviews use different study arm eligibility.
