Title: Oral curcumin and thermal hyperalgesia in diabetic mice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16584726/
PMID: 16584726
DOI: 10.1016/j.ejphar.2006.03.006
Publication date: 2006-03-13
Date basis: First online journal publication.
Review basis: Original author abstract.

Sharma and colleagues, online March 13, 2006. PMID 16584726. DOI 10.1016/j.ejphar.2006.03.006.
The primary abstract reports orally administered isolated curcumin for four weeks in streptozotocin diabetic mice and attenuation of thermal hyperalgesia on tail-immersion and hot-plate assays. Verified numerical group contrasts and full methods remain unavailable. No ground turmeric preparation or clinical Parkinson pain endpoint.
