Neves et al. original ethnopharmacology report
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jep.2009.04.041

Neves and colleagues first published online May 3, 2009. The survey table pairs voucher 2723, Satureja hortensis, with the vernacular name segurelha and flowering tops. Reported uses include tonsillitis and bronchitis. The adjacent laryngitis entry belongs to Salvia officinalis. This records local plant identity and reported practice, with no speech or voice outcome or established treatment response.
