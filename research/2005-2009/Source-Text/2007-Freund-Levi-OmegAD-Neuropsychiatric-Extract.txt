The OmegAD neuropsychiatric report follows the same randomized cohort as PMID17030655. A total of 204 were randomized and 174 completed 12 months. For six months the comparator received four corn-oil capsules daily, each containing 1 g oil and 4 mg vitamin E. Both groups received omega-3 during the second six months. Refining grade is unspecified, with no untreated comparator and no Parkinson cohort.

Table 3 gives full-sample MADRS means for corn oil of 1.9 (95% CI 1.5 to 2.4) at baseline and 1.6 (1.2 to 2.1) at six months. The active arm means were 1.8 (1.3 to 2.3) and 1.5 (1.0 to 1.9). The between-group change comparison is p=.49. A subgroup comparison favors omega-3 in APOE epsilon-4 noncarriers, n=27 versus 18, p=.005. This differs from the overall comparison.

Table 4 uses participants with a positive score at each assessment for each Neuropsychiatric Inventory item. Corn-oil apathy means were 5.1 and 5.8 among 51 and 52 item-positive participants; the between-group six-month test gives p=.13. Corn-oil sleep/nighttime behavior means were 4.9 and 3.7 among 33 and 19 item-positive participants; p=.69 for the between-group comparison. Changing denominators mean the descriptive sequences are not individual changes. The sleep item is wider than clinical insomnia.
