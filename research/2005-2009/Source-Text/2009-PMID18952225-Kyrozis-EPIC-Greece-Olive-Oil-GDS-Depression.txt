Title: Dietary lipids and geriatric depression scale score among elders: the EPIC-Greece cohort.
PMID: 18952225
DOI: 10.1016/j.jpsychires.2008.09.003
First published: 2009
Authors: Kyrozis A, Psaltopoulou T, Stathopoulos P, Trichopoulos D, Vassilopoulos D, Trichopoulou A
Journal: Journal of Psychiatric Research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18952225/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Prospective epidemiological cohort within EPIC-Greece (Attika region) enrolling 610 healthy older adults (aged >=60 years) with baseline FFQ and Geriatric Depression Scale (15-point GDS) assessed 6 to 13 years later. In multivariable linear and quantile regression adjusted for confounders, higher dietary MUFA and its main source, olive oil, were inversely associated with GDS score—specifically predicting a lower chance of scoring at the 90th percentile (highest depressive symptom burden)—whereas seed oils/PUFA showed positive associations. Limitations: observational design, baseline-only diet, unspecified olive oil processing grade, non-PD elders.
