Title: Fish oil supplementation of control and (n-3) fatty acid-deficient male rats enhances reference and working memory performance and increases brain regional docosahexaenoic acid levels
Publication date: 2008-06
PMID: 18492851
DOI: 10.1093/jn/138.6.1165
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18492851/
Text coverage: Primary indexed abstract.

Four male-rat groups received a nonpurified or sunflower-oil-based omega-3-deficient diet with or without added fish oil. Reference and working memory in the Morris water maze were poorer under the deficient dietary pattern and partially recovered with added fish oil. Fish oil also improved these outcomes in the nonpurified-diet groups. The comparison changes overall fatty acid sufficiency and the whole diet across basal conditions. It does not isolate sunflower preparation, grade or effects in Parkinson disease. Full group-level estimates await the original tables.
