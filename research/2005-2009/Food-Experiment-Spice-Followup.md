# Spice evidence follow-up, 2005–2009

## Piperine conference abstract in 6-OHDA rats

Shrivastava, Vaibhav, and Islam reported isolated oral piperine in 6-OHDA-lesioned rats at the January 2009 Winter Conference on Brain Research. Poster abstract P76 describes a narrow-beam improvement and antioxidant measures. A 2012 journal article shares these authors, model, isolated compound, and several endpoints. The reports do not establish whether they used the same animals. Both concern rat motor proxies and leave daily off time after ground black pepper unresolved.
