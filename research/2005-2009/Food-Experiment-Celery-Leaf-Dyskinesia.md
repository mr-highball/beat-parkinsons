# Raw celery leaf and stalk composition

## Raw celery leaves and stalks contain different amounts of phthalides

Kurobayashi and colleagues used gas chromatography to compare raw and closed-system boiled celery leaves and stalks. Table 2 reports 3-n-butylphthalide concentrations of 0.87 and 1.05 mg/kg in raw and boiled leaves, respectively, and 0.29 and 0.36 mg/kg in raw and boiled stalks. The measurements refer to the whole sampled plant material. This establishes that culinary celery leaves can contain the compound used in pharmaceutical Parkinson studies. The experiment measured composition and aroma, with no consumption intervention, levodopa exposure, or dyskinesia outcome.
