# Community and care

### 2005 — Caregiver burden tracked disability, falls and psychiatric symptoms

Schrag and colleagues surveyed 123 Parkinson’s caregivers by mail. Caregiver burden was associated with patient disability, falls, depression and other psychiatric symptoms. The cross-sectional design measured how these problems clustered with burden and leaves the direction of those relationships unresolved. The journal issue appeared in 2006. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16271496/)

### 2009 — Racial differences in recorded Parkinson’s diagnoses

Dahodwala and colleagues used Pennsylvania Medicaid claims to compare new diagnoses across racial and ethnic groups. Black enrollees were less likely than White enrollees to receive a Parkinson’s diagnosis after adjustment for demographics, Medicaid eligibility and visit frequency. A claims-based diagnosis rate can reflect access and ascertainment as well as the underlying occurrence of disease. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2858583/)
