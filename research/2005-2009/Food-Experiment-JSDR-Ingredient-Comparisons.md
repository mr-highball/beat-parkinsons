# Ingredient and texture comparisons in early Japanese swallowing proceedings

## 2005 udon and white rice conference report

Iida and colleagues presented a videofluorographic comparison of udon and white rice on September 2, 2005. The item at page 386 gives the named foods and method. Its abstract requires MedicalOnline member access. Iida's university bibliography confirms the date. The 2010 full paper has the same authors and food comparison. Whether the conference report included the same 22 volunteers is unverified. [Conference record](https://mol.medicalonline.jp/journal/abstract?GoodsID=cb5dvsph/2005/000903/209&name=0386-0386j) · [Asahi University bibliography](https://gyoseki.asahi-u.ac.jp/ashuhp/KgApp?resId=S000121) · [2010 primary paper results](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-JSDR-Ingredient-Comparisons.md#2010-full-paper-on-udon-and-white-rice)

## Water and milk in Kawai's swallowing studies

The 2007 meeting title specifies water, milk and levator veli palatini muscle activity. The government research report cites the presentation and records related experiments. The August 31, 2009 journal paper by Kawai and colleagues tested 10 healthy adults. Each used the same personalized swallow volume for the two liquids. With water first in every participant, mean normalized muscle activity was 87.2% with water and 77.6% with commercial Meiji milk (p < .01). Seven of ten participants had a lower individual mean with milk at p < .05. The measurements concern muscle recruitment during a test swallow. Clinical swallowing severity, airway outcomes and a Parkinson subgroup were outside the study. The 2007 abstract remains gated and cohort overlap with the 2009 article is unverified. [2007 presentation](https://mol.medicalonline.jp/journal/abstract?GoodsID=cb5dvsph/2007/001103/200&name=0296-0297j) · [Grant report](https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/file/KAKENHI-PROJECT-17592021/17592021seika.pdf) · [2009 primary report](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jsdr/13/2/13_128/_article/-char/ja/) · [Full methods and Table 1](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jsdr/13/2/13_128/_pdf/-char/en)

## Caspian Sea yogurt and endoscopic assessment in 2008

Watanabe and colleagues' 2008 presentation II-4-27 names Caspian Sea yogurt and a swallowing endoscopy assessment. The institution list includes a hospital dysphagia service and the manufacturer Fujicco. The abstract link leads to member login. The exact title and author searches did not yield a public original that establishes the participants, comparison, swallowing scores, adverse events or Parkinson status. This remains a title-level lead. ## Ichihara's expanded meal symptom survey and bolus figures

Ichihara's September 20, 2005 article revisits a videofluoroscopy comparison of eight people with Parkinson’s, eight with progressive supranuclear palsy and ten older controls. Figure 1 shows pharyngeal transfer timing for simulated jelly, paste and liquid. Between-diagnosis differences are marked for paste and liquid. Figures 7 and 8 name Calpis, thickener and iopamidol for separate PSP illustrations. The paper gives no participant-level link from that recipe to the Parkinson cohort in Figure 1. [2005 article and date](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/iryo1946/59/9/59_9_491/_article/-char/ja/) · [Figure 1](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/iryo1946/59/9/59_9_491/_pdf#page=2) · [Original 2000 study](../2000-2004/Food-Experiment-JSDR-Legacy-Early.md#takamatsu-videofluoroscopy-and-the-2001-and-2003-proceedings)

Table 1 reports mealtime coughing or choking in 24% of 435 Parkinson patients and 49% of 82 PSP patients across 31 facilities. The stage V percentages were 54% and 86%, respectively, without stage-specific denominators. The 31-site survey expanded a 22-site survey, so their participants overlap. Food identity and preparation were unreported. [Table 1 and survey description](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/iryo1946/59/9/59_9_491/_pdf#page=3) · [Earlier 2003 report holding](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/AA11902977)
