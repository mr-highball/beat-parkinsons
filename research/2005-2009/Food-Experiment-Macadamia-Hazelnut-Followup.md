# Hazelnut flakes in an orthostatic challenge

## Shared hazelnut flakes in orthostatic crossover drinks

Gao and colleagues studied ten healthy men in a randomized double-masked crossover published in February 2008. Both the astragalus and placebo drinks contained hazelnut flakes with sugar and water. Graded orthostatic stress produced presyncope at 18 ± 7 versus 17 ± 6 minutes for astragalus and placebo, with no significant treatment difference. The shared flakes provide no hazelnut-free comparison. The experiment did not use cold-pressed oil or enroll people with Parkinson disease.
