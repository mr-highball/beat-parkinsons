# Procedures and Devices

### 2005 — Duodenal levodopa infusion compared with oral medication

A small randomized crossover study found better functional on time and quality of life during intestinal infusion in people with advanced disease. The short periods address symptom control and limited assessment of tube complications over time. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15668416/)

### 2006 — Subthalamic deep brain stimulation versus medication

In a randomized trial, stimulation plus medical therapy improved quality of life and off-medication motor scores more than medical therapy alone at six months. Serious adverse events were more frequent with stimulation. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16943402/)

### 2009 — Bilateral deep brain stimulation in advanced disease

A US multicenter randomized trial found more daily on time without troublesome dyskinesia with stimulation than with best medical therapy at six months. Surgery increased serious adverse events and the sample included adults aged 70 and older. [Primary source](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/183161)
