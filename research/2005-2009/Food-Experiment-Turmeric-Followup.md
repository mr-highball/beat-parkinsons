# Turmeric preparation and symptom outcomes

## Falls or dizziness in an Alzheimer curcumin trial

Baum and colleagues reported that 34 participants began the six-month trial of placebo, 1 g/day curcumin, or 4 g/day curcumin. Participants could choose capsules or powder mixed with food, and all received standardized ginkgo leaf extract. The primary report describes one adverse event in each of the three arms under the combined heading falls or dizziness. No event was separated by symptom or formulation, and the study did not measure recurrent falls or include a Parkinson cohort. The paper also reports inconclusive differences in MMSE change.
