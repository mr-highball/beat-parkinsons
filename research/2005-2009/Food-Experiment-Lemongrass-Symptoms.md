# Lemongrass preparation and symptom evidence

## Oral thrush trial used a lemongrass infusion

The trial assigned oral-thrush treatment with an infusion made of Cymbopogon citratus cultivated and dried at the hospice, lemon juice, or 0.5% gentian violet solution. Its outcome was oral candidiasis rather than voice loudness or articulation. The primary abstract names 90 randomized and 83 completed. The authors reported no clear between-arm difference in the intention-to-treat analysis. The completer comparison favored lemongrass infusion over gentian violet (p < .05). It provides a real oral exposure route without evaluating minced stalk, low voice volume or Parkinson disease. [Wright primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19109001/) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2008-Wright-Lemongrass-Infusion-Oral-Thrush-Extract.txt)

## Lemongrass essential oil and earlier mouse rotarod duration

The 2007 study used oral lemongrass essential oil in mice. Table 1 gives median accumulated time on a rotating rod at 30 minutes of 160 seconds for control, 180 seconds at 0.5 g/kg and 160 seconds at 1 g/kg, with five mice per group and p > .05. The authors describe a similar pattern at later assessments without displaying the values. This metric differs from the later first-fall latency and fall-count outcomes. The experiment supplies no evidence about minced herb ingestion or clinical Parkinson balance.
