# Registration of a hospital nutrition intervention

## Registered nutrition support and follow-up

ISRCTN29617677 was registered January 27, 2006, with Dutch secondary identifier NTR476. The record describes nutrition support for hospitalized older adults with malnutrition. The separately published 2012 falls analysis specifies fortified oatmeal in the hospital diet, along with several other nutrition and care components.
