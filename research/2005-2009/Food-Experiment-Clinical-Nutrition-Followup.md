# Enteral nutrition and Parkinson symptoms

## Tube feeding and rigidity during levodopa treatment

Cooper, Brock and McDaniel published this case online February 26, 2008. A 77-year-old man with Parkinson’s was treated in hospital after a right basal ganglia hemorrhage with intraventricular extension. He developed severe rigidity despite continuing his preadmission Parkinson’s medicines through gastric access. The initial tube-feeding regimen combined continuous Nutren 1.5 with Prosource protein, totaling 132 g of protein daily or 1.4 g/kg/day. The team switched to bolus Nutren 1.5 with fiber, withdrew Prosource and reduced protein to 90 g/day or 0.9 g/kg/day. Levodopa was also rescheduled between feeds. Rigidity improved visibly within one day, before a later increase in levodopa dosage. Formula, protein amount and medication timing changed together. The authors describe a probable drug–nutrition interaction in one critically ill patient. The case gives no effect of consuming a single culinary ingredient. [Publisher record, PMID 18272698](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1345/aph.1K450) · [Original report, case description on pp. 439–440](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Cara-Mcdaniel/publication/5578815_Interaction_Between_Levodopa_and_Enteral_Nutrition/links/0c96053a02c928ce1d000000/Interaction-Between-Levodopa-and-Enteral-Nutrition.pdf)

A surviving copy of Nestlé Nutrition’s 2006 U.S. guide lists corn oil, canola oil, cow’s milk caseinate, soy lecithin and fractionated coconut and palm kernel triglycerides in the unflavored ingredient panels for both product variants. The fiber formula also lists pea fiber, oligofructose and inulin. The case does not give its product codes, flavor or batch composition. The guide documents that both contemporary formulations could contain corn oil. It gives neither refining method nor a corn-oil dose for this patient. No ingredient removal can be assigned to the improvement, and the current U.S. regular formula’s manufacturer label has a different ingredient list. [2006 manufacturer guide, pp. 34–37](https://doczz.net/doc/962011/nutritional-information) · [Current U.S. manufacturer label](https://www.nestlenutritionstore.com/nutrenr-1-5.html)
