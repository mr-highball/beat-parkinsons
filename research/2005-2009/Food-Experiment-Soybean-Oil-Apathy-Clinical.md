# Soybean-oil comparison registrations

## TEAM-AD and the Neuropsychiatric Inventory

NCT00235716 was first posted October 10, 2005. It assigned patients with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer disease to vitamin E, memantine, both active therapies, or both matching placebos. The planned behavioral measure was the 12-domain Neuropsychiatric Inventory total. The published formulation identifies soybean oil in the matching vitamin E capsules, and the primary report names Arista Industries as the source. The article gives no refining process or batch identification. ## Registered soybean-oil comparison for negative affect

NCT00663871 was first posted April 22, 2008 and assigned 272 adults with low omega-3 intake to fish oil or 2 g/day of soybean-oil capsules. Its primary behavioral outcomes include negative affect and impulsivity. The 2014 supplement substudy clarifies that each comparator capsule also contained vitamin E and mint flavor. The registry is a mood and cardiovascular intervention in generally healthy adults.
