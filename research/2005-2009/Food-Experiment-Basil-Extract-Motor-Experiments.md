# Basil Extract Motor Experiments

## Sweet basil leaf extracts and beam walking in a 2009 poster

Shri, Bora and Arora reported oral pretreatment with methanol or ethyl acetate extracts of Ocimum basilicum leaves in Swiss mice. They used a 10-minute bilateral carotid occlusion and 24-hour reperfusion model. The reported inclined-beam impairment was attenuated with tested extracts. The poster supplies no arm counts or numerical beam outcomes, and has no stride-length endpoint or fresh culinary intake. A later report with the same authors lists a different occlusion duration. Animal overlap is unresolved. [Original source](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1038/jcbfm.2009.156) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2009-10-Shri-Basil-Ischemia-Beam-Poster.txt)
