# Anise seed symptom and safety evidence, 2005–2009

## Nocturnal tongue angioedema after aniseed liqueur

The case describes recurrent tongue swelling after aniseed liqueur, with swallowing and breathing difficulty in two episodes. Seed extract bound IgE, whereas liqueur extracts did not show a specific band in the reported immunoblot. The report supplies an oral-allergy differential for an acute articulation complaint. It contains no measured speech-intelligibility estimate or ground-anise food comparison.
