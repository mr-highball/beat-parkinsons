# Tarragon odor methods follow-up

## Tarragon leaf odor potency and estragole

Zeller and Rychlik evaluated aroma extracts and estimated the contributions of chemical constituents to tarragon leaf odor. They divided each compound’s leaf content by its odor threshold in starch or cellulose. β-Ionone had an odor activity value of 5,862, eugenol 2,653, and estragole 460, though estragole was abundant at 2,900 mg/kg. The reported threshold-based values rank aroma compounds in the analytical matrices. Effects of ingesting herbs on sensory function were outside this food-chemistry design.
