# European dietary and botanical trial records, 2005–2009

## Omega-3 capsules and Parkinson mood symptoms

EudraCT 2006-003717-41 was entered July 12, 2007. The Gemelli Hospital protocol planned to enroll 40 people with Parkinson’s and stable medication and compare omega-3 capsules with placebo for mood and motor outcomes. The index lists the trial complete without results. Pomponi’s 2014 report used DHA and EPA versus corn oil at the same hospital and measured depression and motor scores, with 24 randomized. The accessible records lack a common registration identifier, so cohort identity remains unconfirmed. [Primary protocol](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/trial/2006-003717-41/IT) · [2014 outcome report assessment](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#corn-oil-as-the-comparison-in-a-parkinsons-depression-trial)

## High-fat breakfast and ropinirole pharmacokinetics

NCT00460148 was first posted April 13, 2007 and identifies the same study as EudraCT 2006-006209-94 and sponsor ID ROP109087. A 2008 FDA review includes fed versus fasted steady-state data for prolonged-release ropinirole in people with Parkinson’s. The high-fat breakfast increased mean total exposure and peak concentration, with geometric mean ratios of 1.20 and 1.44, respectively. The study measured a meal effect on drug exposure. The separate symptom effects of the foods remain unmeasured. [First public registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00460148) · [2008 FDA clinical pharmacology review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2008/022008s000_clinpharmr_p1.pdf) · [2016 sponsor results attachment](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-European-Registry-Followup.md#ropinirole-meal-study-sponsor-summary)
