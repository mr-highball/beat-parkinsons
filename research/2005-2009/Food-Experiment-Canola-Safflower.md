# Canola and safflower oil evidence

## Nutrient validation of the Tehran food questionnaire

Mirmiran and colleagues’ questionnaire validation was first published online October 7, 2009. It compared two 168-item questionnaires with repeated dietary recalls and nutrient biomarkers. The authors reported nutrient-level validation results. Later Parkinson cohort papers cite a validation publication and describe a shorter 147-item instrument, whose original oil questions remain unverified.
