# Tarragon endpoint follow-up

## Tarragon tincture in Russian neurotropic pharmacology

Dina V. Kadatskaya's 2005 Samara State Medical University dissertation lists assays of thiopental and chloral hydrate sleep duration in section 3.1. Its author synopsis classifies tarragon tincture as sedative, antidepressant and nootropic. The chapter tables are still needed for tarragon-specific sleep duration, effect estimates and complete methods. The synopsis gives no comparison of ordinary sleep or baseline insomnia.
