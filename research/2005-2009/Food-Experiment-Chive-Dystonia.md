# Chives, related Allium species and dystonia

## 2005 — Red onion bulb saponins and isolated intestinal spasm

Corea and colleagues isolated furostanol saponins from a polar extract of red Tropea onion (*Allium cepa*) bulbs. The smooth muscle preparation was isolated guinea-pig ileum. The authors reported reduced spasms in this preparation. Those intestinal contractions are distinct from the involuntary patterned skeletal muscle contractions of dystonia. The publicly available primary abstract identifies the species, bulb, preparation and tissue. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15713001/)

## 2005 — Blepharospasm and medication states in parkinsonism

Yoon and colleagues identified 35 patients with blepharospasm with or without apraxia of eyelid opening among 1,113 people with parkinsonism, including 913 with idiopathic Parkinson’s disease. The ophthalmic outcome was a focal cranial dystonia. The investigators separated reflex blepharospasm and eyelid-opening apraxia clinically and used electromyography for selected cases. Blepharospasm occurred as an OFF manifestation in two people with idiopathic Parkinson’s and as a peak-dose occurrence in one. This study supplies an outcome definition and levodopa-state context, with no food intervention. [Original article](https://www.thejcn.com/DOIx.php?id=10.3988%2Fjcn.2005.1.2.159)
