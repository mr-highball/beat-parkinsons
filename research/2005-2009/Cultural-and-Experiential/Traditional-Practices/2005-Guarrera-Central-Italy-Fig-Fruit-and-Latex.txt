Guarrera reports research in 175 localities across Marche, Abruzzo and Latium. Table 1 lists common fig in 40 localities. It counts respiratory use of the fruit in 28 and wart remedies involving latex in 16. These are locality counts, with practices capable of overlapping.

Table 2 lists a decoction of dried fig fruit with other ingredients such as apples, mallow and prunes for cough and respiratory complaints. It separately records external application of latex for warts and corns. The mild-laxative passage close to the fig table row refers to Malva sylvestris leaves. Table 5 records a distinct practice involving fig buds for livestock digestive complaints.

This is evidence that the author recorded these customary uses. It contains no Parkinson visual diagnoses, episode diaries, or controlled effects of dried fig fruit independently of the other ingredients.
