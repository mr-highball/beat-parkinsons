Title: Reported Ayurvedic use of turmeric for skin and muscle pain
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2781139/
Related study identifier: PMID 19966973
Publication date: 2008-01
Date basis: January 2008 issue of the review that records the practice. The review describes a much older tradition without dating an individual observation.
Source kind: Traditional practice, reported in a secondary scientific overview
Checked: 2026-11-24

The authors describe turmeric in Ayurvedic practice as having been used for pain and inflammation of skin and muscle. They also describe drying and grinding turmeric rhizomes for culinary use. Their account does not establish that the traditional pain applications used the same preparation, route or dose.

No firsthand case, clinical comparison, Parkinson diagnosis, or measured symptom change is given. The note records customary use as context for the ground turmeric pain claim 0812. The same review's laboratory and pharmacokinetic claims belong to its separate clinical catalog summary.
