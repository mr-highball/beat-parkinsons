Hirakawa et al. (Tokai J Exp Clin Med, 2009; PMID:21318994) document the traditional Japanese Kampo formula Dai-kenchu-to (DKT)—combining Zanthoxylum fruit (sansho), dried ginger rhizome, ginseng root, and malt sugar—used clinically in Japan for postoperative ileus and chronic constipation. Across two severe functional intestinal pseudo-obstruction cases, DKT improved gastrointestinal motility in one patient (MMIHS) but was ineffective in the second (CIIP). This record illustrates both the long-standing East Asian GI framing of Zanthoxylum-containing multi-herb Kampo medicine and two decisive evidence limits: non-response cases occur and multi-herb bundling precludes attributing clinical effects to toasted or ground Sichuan pepper alone.
