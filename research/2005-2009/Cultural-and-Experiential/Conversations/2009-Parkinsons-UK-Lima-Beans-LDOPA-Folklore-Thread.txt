Title: Parkinson UK forum discussion mentioning lima beans in L-DOPA bean folklore (refuted by LC-QToF analysis)
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/mucuna-pruriens/5276
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2009-04-26
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson UK public Discourse forum (2009-04-26).

In an April 2009 Parkinson UK Community Forum thread discussing Mucuna pruriens as a natural levodopa source, a participant commented that all flat beans, including lima beans, produce L-DOPA to some extent while clarifying that only fava beans (Vicia faba) and Mucuna pruriens produce enough to be clinically active. No participant reported firsthand symptom trials or motor benefit from eating lima beans. All usernames and personal details are omitted. Cross-linking this community folklore to modern analytical chemistry clarifies an important botanical boundary: Avula et al. (2025, Foods, PMID:40002056, PMC11854323) profiled 22 legume species including three Phaseolus lunatus accessions by LC-QToF mass spectrometry and found L-DOPA was UNDETECTED (-) in lima beans (present only in Vicia faba, Mucuna pruriens, and Mimosa pudica).
