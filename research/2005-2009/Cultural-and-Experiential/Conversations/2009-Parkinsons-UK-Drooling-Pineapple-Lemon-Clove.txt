Title: Parkinson's UK Forum discussion on drooling coping tips involving pineapple, lemon and clove
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/drooling/3857
Publication date: 2009-02-05
Date basis: Opening post date shown on the public forum (2009-02-05); selected replies reviewed through 2009-02-16.
Reviewed: 2026-11-21 UTC

A caregiver of a spouse with long-standing Parkinson disease (reportedly 28 years) asked for practical coping tips for persistent drooling and wet clothing. Several community participants and caregivers shared suggestions rather than controlled trials.

One caregiver reported that a Parkinson's nurse had recommended pineapple chunks (or crushed pineapple/puree) for thick saliva pooling at the back of the throat when cough reflex is reduced; the opening caregiver replied that they planned to try pineapple chunks, puree, or pineapple juice without posting a later outcome. Another participant passed along an indirect suggestion to squirt lemon juice into the mouth (explicitly noting it as an anecdotal canine travel-sickness trick rather than tested human Parkinson use). A third participant passed along a second-hand tip about chewing or holding a whole clove in the mouth for several hours to reduce drooling while noting they did not personally use it.

Other replies discussed non-food strategies (a tissue-lined spitting bowl, mopping with tissues, waterproof-backed cloth bibs, checking for salivary gland infection, and following an investigational pharmaceutical trial). No participant reported a verified dose, standardized drooling score, or confirmed symptom improvement after pineapple, lemon, or clove; the thread preserves community suggestions and nurse-attributed practical advice rather than clinical evidence.
