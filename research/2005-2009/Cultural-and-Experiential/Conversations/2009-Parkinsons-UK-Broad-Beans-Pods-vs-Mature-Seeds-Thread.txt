Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion contrasting raw broad beans, mature cooked seeds, and cooked whole young pods
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/broad-beans/5078
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2009-08-18
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2009-08-18; follow-up firsthand reports dated 2012-07-05 and 2016-09-05).

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened in August 2009 with follow-up replies in July 2012 and September 2016, participants shared contrasting experiences with broad (fava) beans as a dietary source of levodopa. A caregiver initially reported giving 3 to 4 raw broad beans daily to a frail older relative with Parkinson's disease, who subsequently experienced stomach ache; another responder shared published dietitian cautions regarding unpredictable levodopa dosing. In a July 2012 firsthand report, a participant taking Stalevo 125 mg wrote that eating home-grown mature broad beans for a week caused no noticed change in symptoms until one evening when they cooked whole small green pods together with the beans, after which their dose took 6.25 hours to wear off instead of their usual 2 to 3 hours—noting this single occasion required replication. In September 2016, another participant remarked that lifelong dietary broad-bean intake had no apparent effect, while a third mentioned steaming pods to blend and freeze. All personal identifiers are omitted. These unverified, uncontrolled observations align qualitatively with analytical studies showing much higher L-DOPA in green pods than mature seeds but provide no standardized dosing or safety evidence.
