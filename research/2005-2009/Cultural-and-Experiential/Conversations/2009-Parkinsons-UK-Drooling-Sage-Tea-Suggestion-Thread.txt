Title: Parkinson's UK forum thread on PD drooling including community recommendation of sage tea to reduce saliva
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/drooling/3857
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2009-02-05
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2009-02-05; sage tea reply posted 2023-12-12).

In a long-running Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on severe Parkinson's drooling (opened February 2009; reply December 2023), caregivers and people with Parkinson's shared practical remedies including pineapple juice, cloves, and hyoscine patches. A December 2023 reply recommended sage tea alongside ginger tea, lemon, and dark grape juice as astringent liquids believed to lessen saliva production. All personal identifiers are omitted. Together with a separate 2014 Parkinson's UK thread mentioning hibiscus and sage teas for excess saliva, this reflects recurring folk interest in sage for PD sialorrhea without controlled human verification.
