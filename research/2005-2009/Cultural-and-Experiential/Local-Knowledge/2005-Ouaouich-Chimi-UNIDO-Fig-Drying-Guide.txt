The guide treats harvest maturity, drying, and storage as separate determinants of the final product. It describes traditional sun drying and the distinct criteria for a pilot drying process. The authors advise using a separate sanitation manual. The guide’s sensory evaluation concerns the characteristics of fruit samples and supplies no patient outcome. It does not establish that the Bouadel process was the same as the chriha method later described in Bouhouda.

Open trail: Trace the separately named dryer and hygiene manuals only where they clarify the actual preparation or measured food quality. Keep the place and process distinct from any reported symptom use.
