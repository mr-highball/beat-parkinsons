The report describes 50 community interviews, including eight postmenopausal women. Its dietary description names black beans with tortillas and rice. Its sleep-remedy section separately identifies preparations such as bitter-orange leaves for night sweats and insomnia. The text does not attribute that practice or an individual sleep response to black beans and does not specify canned beans.

Claim 0594 | Related evidence: Black beans are documented as customary food. Sleep remedies are separately attributed to other plants. There is no bean-related sleep measurement, canned preparation or Parkinson diagnosis.
