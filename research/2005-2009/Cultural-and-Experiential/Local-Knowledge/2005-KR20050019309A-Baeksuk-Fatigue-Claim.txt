Title: The Korean baeksuk patent and its reported fatigue tasting comparison
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Patent describing a preparation of an established dish and a tasting evaluation
Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/KR20050019309A/ko
Publication date: 2005-03-03
Date basis: South Korean publication of the application KR20050019309A. Filing was in 2003. The later granted text is a companion within the same patent family.
Community/context: A South Korean patent describes baeksuk, poultry cooked in water, and a proposed mixed preparation with plant ingredients. Traditional names and source attribution follow the original Korean text.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19
Reported direction: Early no response and later subjective benefit in the tasting protocol. The strongest wording belongs to the modified duck dish, with a milder favorable description for conventional chicken.
Authenticity: Original-language public patent inspected through a patent document mirror. The document verifies what the applicant reported, and the participant observations lack independent verification.
Efficacy evidence: Low-weight ordinal tasting report with an undescribed assignment and incomplete comparators. No measured effect on fatigue severity in Parkinson disease.
Access and follow-up: Compare the later patent text within the same family if it clarifies allocation or endpoints. The modified chicken palatability table supplies no fatigue measure.

In Experiment 1, the applicant describes a 20-person trained tasting panel and checks self-reported stamina or tiredness. Table 1 gives ordinal descriptions with no early response in any group. After repeated tastings, the modified duck dish received the strongest wording, while conventional chicken baeksuk received a milder favorable rating and the conventional duck comparator a still weaker favorable description. The proposed chicken dish was assessed separately in Experiment 2 for taste, aroma, juiciness and texture. Those scores do not measure tiredness.

Exposure and limits: The broader named dish can involve chicken or duck and, in the modified recipe, multiple plant ingredients. The early and late observations are reported by the patent applicant. The text does not establish blinded assignment, baseline severity, ratings for individual participants, participant overlap across tasting panels, or an untreated comparator. Parkinson diagnosis, medication use and validated clinical fatigue outcomes are unspecified. The description supplies a preparation and perception lead with high uncertainty about efficacy.

Related research: The 2009 Shimizu beverage comparison and 2021 Barbaresi cycling broth experiment have different preparations, controls and outcomes. The registered chicken meat crossover UMIN000033042 has no posted results. The patent neither supplies those outcomes nor counts as an independent replication of them.
