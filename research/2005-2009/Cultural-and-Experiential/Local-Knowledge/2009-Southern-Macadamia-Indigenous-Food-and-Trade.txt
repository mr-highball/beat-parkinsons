The conservation plan reports discussions with the Kabi Kabi and Yuggera groups and the Southeast Queensland Traditional Owner Land and Sea Management Alliance. The plan attributes to members of the Alliance a description of macadamia nuts as a source of food, trade goods and cultural value. It also notes nut-shell finds near Brisbane. The consultation describes the nuts without specifying pressed oil use. It records no treatment, symptom observation or claimed tremor outcome. The report and descriptions that cite this consultation supply one source lineage.
