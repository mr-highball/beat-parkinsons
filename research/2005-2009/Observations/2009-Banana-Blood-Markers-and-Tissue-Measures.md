# Banana intake markers and measurements in different tissues

The [Lee author manuscript](../Source-Text/2009-PMID19951298-Banana-Plasma-and-Rat-Tissue-Salsolinol.txt) reports higher circulating salsolinol after banana in healthy volunteers. A separate rat experiment finds increased blood levels without clear changes in the measured brain regions. This is evidence of an exposure marker in blood, with a different measured result in the rat tissue analysis. There is no human brain measurement or clinical Parkinson anxiety outcome.

The [later review](../../2025-2029/Source-Text/2025-PMID41169281-Salsolinol-Review-and-Dietary-Banana-Citations.txt) cites the two branches of the study. The original distinguishes the species and sampling sites, which matters when evaluating evidence about clinical mood or motor changes after banana intake.
