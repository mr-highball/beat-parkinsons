# A spinach-derived peptide in a mouse anxiety assay

The 2007 original abstract reports that isolated rubiscolin-6 affects elevated-plus-maze behavior in mice. Oral and injected doses differ by a factor of ten. This identifies a preparation, route and behavioral readout. The later 2023 proceedings supply peptide modelling and a hypothesis about other leaf sources. They give no measured serving, and the 2007 animal abstract gives no Parkinson cohort or dyskinesia result.
