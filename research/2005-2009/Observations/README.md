## Major observations

- Randomized trials showed that deep brain stimulation improved motor function and useful on time in selected patients with advanced disease. Serious adverse events were more frequent after stimulation. [Procedures and devices](../Procedures-and-Devices.md)
- Rasagiline improved daily off time in levodopa-treated participants. The two ADAGIO doses gave different answers on delayed-start criteria, leaving the effect on progression uncertain. [Drugs and remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md)
- Nonmotor screening and the revised MDS-UPDRS captured symptoms and daily function that a motor examination alone could miss. [Clinical and diagnosis](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md) · [Nonmotor and daily life](../Nonmotor-and-Daily-Life.md)

## Minor observations

The previous bucket's grass-pea report involved a different plant and neurologic endpoint. [Lentil and kidney bean evidence](../Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md)
