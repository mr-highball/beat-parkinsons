# Olive oil in both registered Crohn comparison arms

The [first posted registry](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT01037322.txt) describes olive oil as the medication vehicle and the placebo. The [2017 abstract](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28349233-Cannabidiol-Olive-Oil-and-Crohn-Activity.txt) reports no clear Crohn disease activity difference. The reports differ on the drug dose, and the [later review](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2023-PMID37312194-Purified-Cannabidiol-and-Olive-Oil-Carrier.txt) summarizes the same trial. The sources give no olive oil grade or clinical insomnia outcome.
