# Clinical fatigue measurements and the coffee exposure gap

The [2009 clinical comparison](../Source-Text/2009-PMID24868347-Filipino-Parkinson-Fatigue-Measurement.txt) found higher fatigue inventory scores in stable Parkinson participants than age-matched controls. Coffee was outside its exposure measures. The [later community study](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-PMID36352842-Parkinson-Community-Coffee-and-Fatigability.txt) notes coffee use in one of two confirmed cases, with easy fatigability in both. The different designs establish symptom context without estimating a beverage effect.
