# Dietary oil and curcumin reports following the 2004 diabetes meeting

The normal-rat publication reproduces the earlier 1.39 ng/mL difference in the meal-triggered GLP-1 increment. The new streptozotocin cohort likewise had an early GLP-1 response, but its 50-day weight change went in the opposite direction relative to the control diet and its oral glucose test showed no improvement. Bowel motility and constipation were outside these studies. The 2004 abstract reports that curcumin lowered hepatic glucose production without altering PEPCK activity. The 2008 proof reports significantly lower PEPCK activity in a specified assay. The sources overlap in authors and broad model, with incomplete comparability of the underlying experimental conditions. The constituent results give no direction for culinary turmeric and a Parkinson symptom.
