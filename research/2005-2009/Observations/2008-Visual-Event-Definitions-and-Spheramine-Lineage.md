# Visual event definitions and the Spheramine publications

The [2004 six-month pilot report](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID14766394-Spheramine-Six-Month-Pilot.txt), [2005 primary follow-up](../Source-Text/2005-PMID16344341-Spheramine-Pilot-Visual-Events.txt), [2008 review](../Source-Text/PMC5084167.txt) and [pilot registry](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT00761436.txt) describe a six-person single-group study. The 2004 record gives no separate hallucination count. The 2005 and 2008 publications describe three new visual cases. The primary report specifies one resolution after medication reduction and two spontaneous resolutions. The 2008 summary groups resolution after a medication reduction. Counting the two publications as separate clinical evidence would duplicate the participants.

The [STEPS registration](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT00206687.txt) identifies the later sham-controlled comparison. Its [2011 outcome abstract](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID21565557-Spheramine-STEPS-Randomized-Results.txt) reports no significant comparative motor difference. It leaves visual adverse-event item counts open. The [2009 pathology report](../Source-Text/PMC2764397.txt) is one participant from STEPS and provides a tissue outcome. It is not another trial or result for the earlier pilot.

The [2008 dementia autopsy cohort](../Source-Text/PMC2745052.txt) distinguishes overall visual symptom occurrence from onset within three years. The [2002 temporal-pathology study](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2002-PMID11844739-Formed-Visual-Hallucinations-and-Temporal-Pathology.txt) evaluates the presence and timing of formed hallucinations in a different autopsy cohort. The subsequent [2009 GBA series](../Source-Text/PMC2702833.txt) excludes selected medication-associated events. None of these comparisons estimates within-person visual episode frequency following dried fig intake.
