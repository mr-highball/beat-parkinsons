# Lucania reports of fig and linden use

The original fieldwork tables distinguish dried common fig as a household remedy from linden flowers reported for body shaking. The shaking entry supplies no diagnostic or measured endpoint. The previous period includes [historical accounts of dietary treatment and motor symptoms](../../2000-2004/Observations/README.md) for wider context.
