# Mixed tincture and distinct symptom measures

The STW5 trial reports a small improvement in investigator-rated dyspepsia, with inconclusive diary and symptom-free comparisons and patient-rated tolerability favoring placebo. It supplies no isolated depression score or lemon balm component effect. Later gastric stress models need to retain this difference between a clinical gastrointestinal composite and a psychiatric outcome.
