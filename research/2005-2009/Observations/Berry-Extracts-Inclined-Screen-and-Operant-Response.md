# Berry extract, test latency and fall frequency

The 2005 four-berry study used an inclined screen to time how long aged rats could remain on the apparatus. Blueberry and cranberry improved that comparison. The strawberry references point to separate cohorts. The 2002 proceedings abstract could be an earlier report of the same four-berry work. Keep the possible linkage without counting it as independent replication. [Four-berry article](../Source-Text/2005-PMID23598603-Shukitt-Hale-Four-Berry-Rat-Diets.txt) · [Preliminary meeting report](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2002-Galli-Four-Berry-Diets-SfN.txt)

Strawberry extract has separately reported effects on operant responding in irradiated rats. They depend on age at testing and radiation conditions. The early favorable pattern under 2.0 Gy is absent in the later, depleted comparisons. The 1.5 Gy paper reports a favorable twelve-month contrast. A distinct paper assesses conditioned taste avoidance after a drug challenge. These tasks and preparations provide indirect research context for the ingredient. Recurrent falls, ordinary raw fruit intake and a Parkinson population would each require an additional evidence link.
