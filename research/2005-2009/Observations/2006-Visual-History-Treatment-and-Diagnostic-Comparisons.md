# Visual history, treatment and diagnostic comparisons

The [brain bank chart study](../Source-Text/PMC2077491.txt) treats hallucination history as a possible correlate of falling. The [mitochondrial study](../Source-Text/PMC6674236.txt) mentions a visual history in one donor and tests tissue function. The [amygdala pathology study](../Source-Text/PMC5706655.txt) uses features explicitly recorded in charts and gives item-specific denominators for generic hallucinations. The [2007 autopsy comparison](../Source-Text/PMC2117602.txt) separately codes once-only and persistent visual history. None measures recurrence after a food exposure.

The [2006 neuropharmacology review](../Source-Text/PMC5471533.txt) cites a [1999 sleep and dream comparison](../../1995-1999/Source-Text/1999-PMID9918353-Hallucinations-Dreams-and-Sleep.txt) and distinct open-label studies of [rivastigmine](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2001-PMID11748755-Rivastigmine-Parkinson-Psychosis.txt), [tacrine or donepezil](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2001-PMID11768630-Cholinesterase-Cognition-and-Motor.txt), and [quetiapine](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15090935-Quetiapine-Parkinson-Psychosis.txt). Their abstract outcomes distinguish hallucinations or illusions as a category, broader psychosis change, and cognition. The review discusses both favorable and inconclusive or unfavorable treatment findings across different studies.

The [clinical imaging review](../Source-Text/PMC4953664.txt) describes diagnostic patterns. The [Alzheimer receptor imaging study](../Source-Text/PMC2077777.txt) measures tracer uptake and mentions visual symptoms in prior work. The [SGIM collection](../Source-Text/PMC1484878.txt) supplies separate reports of biochemical illness, diagnostic revision to supranuclear palsy and visual impairment. These findings define scope and possible confounders for a symptom review.
