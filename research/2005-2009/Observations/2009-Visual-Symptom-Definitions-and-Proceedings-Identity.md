# Visual symptom definitions and proceedings identity

The [GBA carrier series](../Source-Text/PMC2702833.txt) records whether a patient had experienced visual hallucinations after motor onset and how long after onset they began. It excludes medication-associated events that resolved after medication changes and febrile or delirium events. The separate [2006 interview study](../Source-Text/2006-PMID16473980-Visual-Hallucination-Relative-Frequency-Interview.txt) asks about the relative frequency of reported experiences. Comparing these descriptions requires retaining the different denominators and time measures.

The [infestation review](../Source-Text/PMC2772366.txt) treats a fixed infestation belief, tactile sensations and visual experiences as distinct features. Its reference for [multiple system atrophy](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2002-PMID12040988-Infestation-in-MSA.txt) describes a different diagnostic population. The later [five Parkinson cases](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC6353320.txt) and [ropinirole case](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7899248.txt) explicitly describe visual and tactile experiences. The [piribedil case](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC10917070.txt) reports infestation belief and tactile symptoms without a separately assessed visual event. All are potential context for interpreting symptom terms, with no dried-fig comparison.

The [2012 RECOVER extension abstract](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-RECOVER-Open-Label-Extension.txt) and [posted results](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2010-RECOVER-Registry-Visual-Adverse-Events.txt) describe the same open-label group. The paper's 13% figure is consistent with 11 affected participants among 84 in the registry. The extension uses a general hallucination entry. The parent trial's separately coded serious visual event cannot disaggregate the extension.

The [2009 conference abstracts](../Source-Text/PMC2651108.txt) and their separately published author index occupy adjacent page ranges under different DOIs. The apparent DOI mismatch comes from the PubMed field. The conference body remains pending.
