# Visual history, symptom measurement and proceedings

The [2007 autopsy and scan comparison](../Source-Text/PMC2117602.txt) separates one-time visual events from persistent symptoms and from diagnostic accuracy. The [1999 autopsy case](../../1995-1999/Source-Text/1999-PMID10466669-FP-CIT-Lewy-Body-Autopsy-Case.txt) and the [2004 clinical imaging comparison](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15210531-FP-CIT-Clinical-Diagnosis-Comparison.txt) give different diagnostic reference standards. The [1997 clinical imaging study](../../1995-1999/Source-Text/1997-PMID9143472-Lewy-Body-Perfusion-and-Transporter-Imaging.txt) and [2004 image analysis](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15528096-FP-CIT-Statistical-Mapping-Comparison.txt) further illustrate why a visual scan rating is distinct from a visual symptom. The [2008 autopsy cohort](../Source-Text/PMC2745052.txt) also distinguishes onset from overall symptom occurrence.

The [SGIM collection](../Source-Text/PMC2219573.txt) keeps its insulinoma and suspected grapefruit–medication cases separate. The [banana and mocha case](../Source-Text/2007-PMC2219573-SGIM-Banana-Hypoglycemia-Extract.txt) is another member. The [West European psychiatry proceedings](../Source-Text/PMC7103174.txt) separate the Lewy body clinical case and the Parkinson stimulation series. The [gaze study](../Source-Text/PMC1911188.txt) measures fixations and saccades.

The [Oxford schizophrenia abstracts](../Source-Text/PMC2632307.txt) have a confirmed issue identity and an open whole-body gap. None of the recovered 2007 records compare dried-fig intake with visual episode frequency in Parkinson disease.
