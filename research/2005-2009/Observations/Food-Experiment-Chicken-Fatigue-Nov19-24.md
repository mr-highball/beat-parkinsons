# Chicken-derived drinks and fatigue measurements

The 2009 daily-fatigue study found a favorable high-dose VAS contrast across several weeks in healthy adults. The Chalder result appeared at a single time point and objective performance comparisons were inconclusive. The earlier period's 1996 mental-load study found a favorable result after arithmetic and an inconclusive comparison after a memory task. The later trial tested everyday fatigue rather than testing tolerance of another assigned workload.

The 2006 extract sprint trial also separates biochemical results from physical output. A 2005 mixture patent preserves an early no-response and a later favorable ordinal tasting report for conventional chicken baeksuk. Its later preferred duck comparison and separate chicken palatability experiment do not supply a controlled patient fatigue result.
