# Drugs and Remedies

### 2005 — Rasagiline as an add-on for motor fluctuations

PRESTO randomized 472 levodopa-treated participants with daily off time. The two rasagiline groups recorded about 0.49 and 0.94 fewer hours of daily off time than placebo over 26 weeks. The outcome measured symptomatic function. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15710852/)

### 2005 — Compulsive gambling during dopamine agonist treatment

Dodd and colleagues documented gambling emerging after dopamine agonist use, with reversal in some patients after stopping the implicated treatment. The selected case series identified a safety signal and left its incidence uncertain. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16009751/)

### 2007 — Pergolide and heart-valve safety

The FDA announced the voluntary US market withdrawal of pergolide products after reports and evidence of heart-valve damage. The regulatory history distinguishes the 2007 market action from subsequent formal withdrawal of approvals. [FDA regulatory history](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2014-15371.pdf)

### 2009 — Delayed-start rasagiline in early disease

ADAGIO randomized 1,176 people with untreated Parkinson’s. The lower-dose early-start group met the three prespecified endpoints, while the higher-dose group did not. The discordant results left disease modification unresolved. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19776408/)
