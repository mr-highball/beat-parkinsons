# Curcumin and evoked pain in a diabetes model

## Oral curcumin and thermal hyperalgesia in diabetic mice

Sharma and colleagues first published March 13, 2006. Their primary abstract reports oral isolated curcumin for four weeks in a streptozotocin mouse model, with attenuation of thermal hyperalgesia on tail-immersion and hot-plate testing. Full methods and group estimates remain access-limited. The preparation, disease model and evoked pain measures differ from clinical pain after ground turmeric in Parkinson disease.
