# Clinical and experimental outcome boundaries

## Tremor and apathy scope in a bilateral pallidal lesion case

April 2005 issue. The case developed parkinsonism after bilateral pallidal injury and was assessed with several motor tasks. The original Discussion reports no distal limb tremor and no clinical evidence of abulia. Apathy in longstanding Parkinson disease appears as prior context. The mapped fig and date-fruit exposures were absent. [Original source](https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/76/4/482.full.pdf) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2005-Kuoppamaki-Pallidal-Case-Symptoms.txt)

## Zebrafish development and the Basil fellowship reference

June 3, 2005 online. Willer and colleagues investigate developmental genetics in zebrafish eggs and embryos. Basil O’Connor appears in the acknowledgments as a fellowship name. The egg is a biological specimen. Neither appears as a culinary exposure, and human fall frequency is outside the design. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1449754/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2005-Willer-Zebrafish-Egg-and-Basil-Context.txt)

## Hallucination discontinuation and motor scale in a donepezil trial

June 16, 2005 online according to the original PDF. Ravina and colleagues randomized 22 patients with Parkinson disease dementia to a donepezil and placebo crossover. One participant stopped during placebo after worsening visual hallucinations. Reported total and motor UPDRS results do not isolate rest-tremor severity. The original has no fig exposure or food comparison. [Original source](https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/76/7/934.full.pdf) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2005-Ravina-Donepezil-Hallucination-and-Motor-Context.txt)

## Baseline hallucination and apathy scores in Parkinson dementia

July 4, 2006 online and January 2007 issue. Aarsland and colleagues use baseline Neuropsychiatric Inventory data from 537 Parkinson dementia patients recruited to the parent rivastigmine trial. Hallucinations were recorded in 236 and apathy in 291. The mean apathy score was 2.95 with SD 3.43. These descriptive data concern symptom prevalence and scores in one parent cohort. There are no episode counts or fig or date-fruit comparisons. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2117797/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2006-Aarsland-Baseline-Hallucinations-and-Apathy.txt)

## Composite motor scores in a Parkinson cognition study

March 31, 2009 online. Abe and colleagues tested deceptive responses in 32 Parkinson patients and 20 healthy controls and related behavior to resting-state brain imaging. The Methods and Table 1 report a total UPDRS part III score of 18.1 with SD 7.2. Rest tremor and bradykinesia appear as clinical background. There are no item-level rest tremor or bradykinesia outcomes or fig or salmon exposure. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2677797/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2009-Abe-Deception-and-Composite-Motor-Score.txt)

## Isolated rosmarinic acid and mouse swim behavior

July 1, 2008 per J-STAGE. Ito and colleagues introduce Perilla as a source of rosmarinic acid and test an isolated commercial material administered by injection in mice. The full Methods and Results report reduced forced-swim immobility in several comparisons and no statistically significant alteration in open-field activity. The tested material was neither fresh rosemary nor oral leaf; the behavioral assays do not measure clinical Parkinson depression. [Original source](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bpb/31/7/31_7_1376/_pdf) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2008-Ito-Rosmarinic-Acid-and-Swim-Behavior.txt)

## Arm pain and rest tremor in FMR1 premutation carriers

June 20, 2008 online, with a 2009 issue. In a four-case series, Hall and colleagues describe arm pain in case 1. The report describes resting tremor and qualitative dopaminergic responses. Case 1 has a composite motor UPDRS score of 11, and case 3 has no quantitative motor scale. The source provides neither a separate pain or rest-tremor treatment estimate nor a dried date or raw fig comparison. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685192/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2008-Hall-FMR1-Case-Pain-and-Rest-Tremor.txt)

## Anxiety scores in a dementia memory notebook pilot

June 30, 2008 first online, with an October–November issue. Five participants with very mild dementia and four spouse coaches attended memory notebook sessions. Tables 6 and 7 give DASS-21 anxiety means of 2.00 to 3.20 for dementia participants and 1.00 to 0.75 for spouses, with no significance marker for these anxiety comparisons. The study has no Parkinson-specific group, salmon exposure, or fall-frequency outcome. [Original source](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/reader/10.1177/1533317508320794) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2008-Schmitter-Edgecombe-Anxiety-and-Notebook-Pilot.txt)

## Depression score as a covariate in a Parkinson veering study

October 28, 2008 first online. Thirty-one Parkinson participants were enrolled and 23 completed walking assessment. The two Parkinson laterality groups scored higher than controls on BDI-II, with an overall group comparison F(2,46)=8.44, p=.001. The study examines visual navigation and veering. Cervical dystonia is referenced in the discussion. The source does not test a depression treatment, dystonia outcome or lemon balm. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2577802/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2008-Davidsdottir-Veering-and-Mood-Covariate.txt)
