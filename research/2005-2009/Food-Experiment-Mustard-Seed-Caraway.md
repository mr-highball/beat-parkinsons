# Mustard seed and caraway research

## Mustard constituent and rat bladder voiding

Streng and colleagues’ study first appeared online in 2007. In conscious rats, bladder instillation of allyl isothiocyanate shortened the voiding interval and reduced voided volume. Table 1 reports a mean interval of 4.2 minutes at baseline and 2.1 minutes after the lower instilled concentration (p = .004). More frequent voiding in this model points away from the frozen reduction hypothesis. The isolated compound was applied directly to the bladder, and the experiment did not assess perceived urgency, Parkinson’s, or food intake. ## Carvone enantiomers and drug-induced sleep

De Sousa and colleagues found that injected S-(+)-carvone, a major constituent of caraway oil, reduced ambulation and prolonged pentobarbital-induced sleep in mice. The R-(−) enantiomer associated with spearmint had a different dose pattern. Drug-induced sleep duration and general sedation leave dream enactment and fatigue after culinary caraway unresolved.
