# Food experiment evidence traced through Spanish-language records

## 2006 Chilean food-odor recognition test

Miranda, Slachevsky and Pérez compared 40 adults with idiopathic Parkinson’s disease, 40 healthy adults and 10 people with other parkinsonian diagnoses in an August 2006 letter. The Sniffin’ Sticks odor-identification test included orange, lemon, pineapple, clove, mint, fish, anise, banana, cinnamon and coffee, along with leather and rose. Participants identified the scents from pens. All 40 participants with idiopathic Parkinson’s recognized fewer than 10 of 12 odors, while 38 of 40 healthy controls recognized all 12. The letter reports scores across the entire test. It gives no results for individual odors or for eating the corresponding foods. ## 2006–2007 meal studies and the food-level comparison

The 2006 single-blind crossover by Barichella and colleagues, first published June 13, reported shorter postprandial OFF time and shorter total OFF time during a diet with formulated low-protein products. The report compared two-month dietary regimens in 18 levodopa-treated people with Parkinson’s and motor fluctuations. [Primary 2006 report and online date](https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/mds.21003) · [Existing summary of both studies](Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#low-protein-replacement-foods-and-levodopa-off-time)

In the 2007 follow-up, all six participants completed both seven-day periods and supplied 84 diary-days, with 42 per diet. Table III shows an example menu with formulated low-protein biscuits and pasta replacing ordinary biscuits and pasta. Tea or coffee, jam, vegetables, oil, fruit and the scheduled dinner proteins appear in both example menus. The full regimen yielded less daily OFF time (3.5 vs 5 hours, p = .01) and more dyskinetic ON time (6 vs 4.5 hours, p = .04). Measured energy expenditure, the primary outcome, also rose (1903 vs 1731 kcal/day, p = .02). Reported sleep duration was similar. The dietary comparison changed several foods and overall protein intake. It supplies no separate symptom estimates for the menu ingredients or any version of the roasted potato, raw pear or raw apricot in statements `0020`, `0667` and `0723`.
