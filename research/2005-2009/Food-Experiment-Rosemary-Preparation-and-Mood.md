# Extracted rosemary and murine behavior

## Rosemary stem-and-leaf extract in mouse behavioral assays

Machado and colleagues tested a hydroalcoholic dried-stem and leaf extract in Swiss mice. The March 2009 online paper reports reduced immobility after acute oral extract and after a 14-day regimen. In the reported acute comparisons, the tail-suspension reductions were 22.9% and 28% in two active groups, and the forced-swim reduction was 49.5% in one group. The open-field locomotor comparison was inconclusive. This Brazilian study uses an extract and a different laboratory model from the later Tsukuba studies.
