# Tarragon and oregano

## Tarragon in a diabetic rat pain model

Roghani and colleagues’ 2006 article reports 40 male Wistar rats in five groups, including diabetic rats receiving oral tarragon for one month. Formalin pain scores fell in both early and late phases relative to diabetic controls. Sodium salicylate lowered scores in the late phase. The 2004 conference report had a matching design and author overlap, so these results are counted together for claim 0285. They are from a diabetic pain model, with no human Parkinson or fresh-leaf comparison. The English summary identifies the oral route without establishing equivalence to culinary leaves. [2006 primary article and English summary](https://brieflands.com/journals/jid/articles/155163.pdf) · [2004 report](../2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#tarragon-and-formalin-responses-in-diabetic-rats)

## Oregano leaf infusion and mouse coordination and sedation

Santisteban Archila tested an oral aqueous infusion of dried *Origanum vulgare* leaves in healthy male albino mice. The higher infusion dose reduced rotarod retention to roughly 0.49–0.81 min versus the 3 min control ceiling and delayed chimney climbing to about 24 s versus 7.5 s. The reported significance wording and tables differ. The higher dose prolonged pentobarbital-induced hypnosis from 141.8 to 195 min. The lower dose gave 145.6 min. The positive-control group received haloperidol separately. These observations address coordination and drug-induced sedation in mice and are indirect for shortened stride (0490) and daytime sleepiness (0842). Neither stride length nor spontaneous clinical daytime sleepiness was measured. [March 2006 thesis, methods and results](https://biblioteca-farmacia.usac.edu.gt/tesis/QF954.pdf)

## Oregano extract and L-DOPA in sprouting velvet bean

Randhir, Kwon and Shetty reported a 33% increase in day-one L-DOPA in *Mucuna pruriens* sprouts from oregano extract-primed seeds compared with controls. The paper measured the sprouts’ chemistry. It provides no clinical OFF-time observation for fresh oregano (0854). The earlier seed experiment used fava. [May 2009 primary abstract, PMID 19457656](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19457656/) · [2002 fava experiment](../2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#oregano-extract-and-l-dopa-in-sprouting-fava)
