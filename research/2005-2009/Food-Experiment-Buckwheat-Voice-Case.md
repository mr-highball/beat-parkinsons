## Speech loss during a buckwheat allergy episode

A 2006 clinical case describes a 36-year-old man who became unable to speak during acute throat-closing symptoms shortly after eating buckwheat. In a separate exposure, crackers containing buckwheat preceded another anaphylactic episode. IgE sensitization was assessed. The first report’s public abstract gives no cooking method or controlled speech measurement. Acute airway symptoms after an allergenic exposure do not determine parkinsonian speech intelligibility after cooked groats. [Original author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16948356/)
