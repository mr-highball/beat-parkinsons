# Nutrition and bowel-care leads in the 2007 NHO meeting

The National Hospital Organization lists the 61st meeting in Nagoya on November 16–17, 2007. The National Diet Library catalogs the printed abstracts. Two indexed records warrant checking in the issue. [Official meeting record](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/research/cnt1-0_000250.html) · [Exact print issue](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I000008338786-i24351875)

## Agar-gelled feeding and bowel movements

A Japanese Consumer Affairs Agency filing cites a 2007 presentation by Yasuko Okuda on agar-gelled tube feeds for bowel movements in bedbound patients with Parkinson-related disorders. The filing lists the citation in Form V-8, excluded-literature row 138, and gives the disease population as the reason for exclusion from that review. The cited title identifies the feeding route and general outcome. The original abstract, Parkinson subgroup, cohort size and results remain unverified. [Filing citation, Form V-8 row 138](https://www.fld.caa.go.jp/caaks/cssc06/youshiki5?yousiki5216File=C120%255CC120_youshiki5.pdf) · [Consumer Affairs Agency search for filing C120](https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/food_labeling/foods_with_function_claims/search/)

## Nutrition management in neurological dysphagia

The Japanese Science and Technology Agency indexes Ogasawara and colleagues’ report on nutrition management in people with progressive neurological conditions and dysphagia at page 623. Texture-modified foods appear in its subject indexing. The public record gives no separate Parkinson diagnosis, ingredient or clinical result.
