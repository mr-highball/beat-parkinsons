# 2005-2009

Earlier visual symptom studies report [recalled episode characteristics](Source-Text/2006-PMID16473980-Visual-Hallucination-Relative-Frequency-Interview.txt), [task-related MRI activation](Source-Text/2009-PMID19755518-Image-Recognition-and-Visual-Hallucination-Groups.txt) and [longitudinal atrophy](Source-Text/2009-PMID19965847-Clinical-Visual-Hallucination-Groups-and-Atrophy.txt) in distinct clinical contrasts.

The [2006 DLB perfusion study](Source-Text/2006-PMID16567718-Donepezil-and-Visual-Hallucination-Perfusion.txt) measures caregiver-rated visual symptoms before and after donepezil. [Comparison with the later imaging and randomized studies](../2010-2014/Observations/2012-Lewy-Body-Hallucination-Measures.md).

The [2005 Hoasca pharmacokinetic reanalysis](Source-Text/2005-PMID16149329-Hoasca-Metabolizer-Reanalysis.txt) reuses 14 of the original 15 volunteer profiles.

The [grapefruit juice and nilotinib crossover](Source-Text/2009-PMID19948946-Grapefruit-Juice-and-Nilotinib.txt) measures serum drug exposure in healthy participants. [Later Parkinson protocol context](../2015-2019/Observations/2016-Visual-Outcome-Definitions-and-Nilotinib-Lineage.md).

The [complete ripe dried-fig extract study](Source-Text/2008-PMID18586078-Fig-Fruit-Extract-and-Lab-Endpoints-Abstract.txt) verifies the botanical voucher, isolated gut assay and separate platelet result. These are laboratory measurements of an extract.

The [central Italian field study](Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2005-Guarrera-Central-Italy-Fig-Fruit-and-Latex.txt) records a mixed dried-fig decoction for respiratory complaints and separate latex practices. The [ripe dried-fig extract assays](Source-Text/2008-PMID18586078-Fig-Fruit-Extract-and-Lab-Endpoints-Abstract.txt) measure isolated gut tissue and platelets.

Two original reports specify the comparison behind their visual findings. The [autopsy study](Source-Text/2005-PMID16170073-Olfaction-and-Lewy-Body-Phenotype.txt) treats hallucination history as a predictor of Lewy body pathology, and the [vision rehabilitation study](Source-Text/2006-PMID17035271-Contrast-Sensitivity-and-Formed-Visual-Hallucinations.txt) reports formed hallucinations alongside measured contrast sensitivity.

The [original Usp14 mouse study](Source-Text/2006-PMID17079671-Usp14-Ataxia-Rescue-and-Award-Name.txt) identifies the basil string in a research award and separates genetic motor rescue from dietary exposure.

[Coffee history and fatigue measured separately](Source-Text/PMC2276830.txt) · [PMC2364726](Source-Text/PMC2364726.txt) · [PMC3376711](Source-Text/PMC3376711.txt).

[gastrointestinal tumor case series with saffron staining](Source-Text/PMC3207561.txt) · [Parkinson olfactory testing with a banana-like odor](Source-Text/PMC2790799.txt).

The [dried common-sage crossover](Source-Text/2005-PMID16205785-Dried-Sage-Mood-Stressor.txt) records different alertness comparisons before and during a task.

An [injected rosmarinic acid rat study](Source-Text/2005-PMID16026713-Rosmarinic-Acid-Behavior.txt) separates the authors' interpretation of motivation from a clinical apathy outcome. [Comparison with later work](../2025-2029/Observations/Clinical-Outcome-Specificity-and-Food-Claims.md).

The [normal-rat olive-oil study](Source-Text/2005-PMID15888922-Olive-Oil-GLP1-Normal-Rats.txt) and [streptozotocin model](Source-Text/2006-PMID16523410-Olive-Oil-GLP1-STZ-Rats.txt) report different chronic outcomes. The [mouse hepatocyte curcumin experiment](Source-Text/2008-PMID18221818-Curcumin-Hepatocyte-Glucose.txt) and [human endothelial-cell assay](Source-Text/2005-PMID15855339-Linoleate-Curcumin-LOX1-Cells.txt) document separate compound tests. [Comparison with the 2004 proceedings](Observations/EASD-Oil-and-Curcumin-Report-Comparison.md).

A [topical nickel challenge](Source-Text/2005-PMID15723201-Pearce-Topical-Macadamia-Oil-Control.txt) used macadamia oil as a comparison. The [Southern Macadamia plan](Cultural-and-Experiential/Local-Knowledge/2009-Southern-Macadamia-Indigenous-Food-and-Trade.txt) records separate consultation on kernel food and trade uses.

A single healthy-adult oil-assignment trial reported [appetite and food choice](Source-Text/2005-PMID16314877-Peanut-Oil-Diet-Appetite-and-Food-Choice.txt) and later [energy and general activity](Source-Text/2007-Akuamoah-Boateng-Peanut-Oil-Energy-and-Activity.txt).

The [grapefruit juice concentration figure](Source-Text/2005-Nagai-Nomoto-Cabergoline-Figure4-Extract.txt), [clarithromycin interaction study](Source-Text/2006-PMID16410677-Cabergoline-Clarithromycin-Outcomes.txt) and [cabergoline resting-tremor pilot abstract](Source-Text/2008-PMID19092251-Cabergoline-Resting-Tremor-Pilot.txt) tested different exposures and outcomes. [Evidence comparison](../2020-2024/Observations/Grapefruit-Preparation-and-Tremor.md).

A [2006 randomized crossover](Source-Text/2006-PMID16773618-Low-Protein-Foods-and-Off-Time-Abstract.txt) reports postprandial OFF time on two diets.

A Japanese [ordinary-fatigue drinking study](Source-Text/2009-Shimizu-Imidazole-Dipeptide-Drink-Fatigue-Extract.txt), [extract sprint abstract](Source-Text/2006-PMID16531904-CBEX-Buffering-Extract.txt) and [baeksuk tasting report](Cultural-and-Experiential/Local-Knowledge/2005-KR20050019309A-Baeksuk-Fatigue-Claim.txt) give different measures of fatigue, performance and perceived recovery.
