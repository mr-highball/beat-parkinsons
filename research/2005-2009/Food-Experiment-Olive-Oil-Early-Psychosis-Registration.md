## Olive oil comparator in an early schizophrenia protocol

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT00585390 on January 3, 2008. The protocol describes olive oil as placebo against DHA or EPA in early schizophrenia. Positive symptoms are the primary outcome domain. The registry describes neither refined grade nor a separate occurrence count for visual hallucinations. The completed registration shows no posted results. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00585390)
