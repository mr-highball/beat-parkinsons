# Black tea, walking and tremor

## Tea intake and transition to Hoehn and Yahr stage three

Kandinov, Giladi and Korczyn studied the historical charts and interview histories of 278 consecutively recruited Parkinson patients in Tel Aviv. Their 2007 paper compared time from motor symptom onset to Hoehn and Yahr stage 3 and did not find a clear association with tea drinking. Its abstract and publisher excerpt do not establish black tea separately, walking speed or tremor severity. The same authors explicitly used the population for a separate article first published in 2008. More than three cups of tea per day was associated with a motor onset 7.7 years later (p<.01) in that analysis.
