# Original symptom reports and source context

## Journal notice linking the two thalamotomy reports

April 20, 2007. The Korean Journal of Radiology notice identifies the 2002 Korean Journal of Radiology article and the 2003 European Journal of Radiology article by Baik as largely identical. The corresponding author agreed they describe the same study. Both articles trace to one thalamotomy cohort, so separately indexing the publications adds no independent clinical sample.
