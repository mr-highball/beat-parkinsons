# Peanut oil in a rodent fine-motor task

## Skilled reaching with a corticosterone mixture containing peanut oil

Smith and colleagues used peanut oil mixed with corticosterone, water and crushed food in one arm of a 6-OHDA rat experiment. After lesioning, both restraint stress and the corticosterone mixture reduced skilled reaching recovery relative to lesion-only controls. Figure 2 shows that restraint stress without the mixture had the most severe effect. The experiment had no matched oil-only arm. Reaching accuracy is a fine-motor measure. Writing size, refined grade, separate dystonia and separate rigidity scores were unavailable.
