# Cumin drug interaction context

## Cumin extract and rifampicin exposure in rats

The original author abstract reports an interaction of aqueous cumin seed extract with rifampicin and quantified exposure changes for an isolated flavonoid glycoside. The first public date is the 2006 online release. These measurements are from a different drug in rats.
