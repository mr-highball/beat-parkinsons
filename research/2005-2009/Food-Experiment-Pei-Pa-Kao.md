# Pei Pa Kao voice-fatigue dissertation

## Vocal fatigue after a tonic without verified shiso

Fung Kar-yan Cecilia’s May 6, 2005 University of Hong Kong dissertation compared Pei Pa Kao, glucose solution and no fluid in 28 young women after a loud-reading task. Perceptual voice assessments, vocal range and videostroboscopy found no clear difference across the three groups. The dissertation’s introduction incorrectly connects pinellia to perilla leaf. The manufacturer lists processed pinellia rhizome. This is a documented false match for shiso and low voice volume in Parkinson’s.
