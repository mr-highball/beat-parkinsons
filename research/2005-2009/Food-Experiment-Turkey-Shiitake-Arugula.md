# Turkey, shiitake, and arugula evidence

## 2006 Acute tryptophan depletion and attention-related tasks in Parkinson disease

Scholtissen and colleagues used a randomized, double-blind crossover in 15 people with early Parkinson's and 15 matched healthy volunteers. Acute tryptophan depletion impaired delayed verbal recall and recognition and improved some short-interval reaction and finger precueing results. Parkinson motor UPDRS did not change. The intervention depleted an amino acid rather than administering a meat meal, and the result does not estimate roasted turkey's effect on attention. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16756999/)
