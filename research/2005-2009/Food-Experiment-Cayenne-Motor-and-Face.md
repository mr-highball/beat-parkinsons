# Cayenne pepper motor and facial outcome follow-up

## Topical capsaicin and a blepharospasm case

Leon-Sarmiento and colleagues used transdermal capsaicin in five healthy volunteers and one woman with blepharospasm. The mean healthy blink rate was 17 ± 3 before and 15 ± 5 after, with no change judged significant. The uncontrolled case's reported blepharospasm severity rating fell from 4 at baseline to 1 at six months (source Table). The patient had focal eyelid dystonia, and the healthy assay measured spontaneous blinking. Neither cohort had a Parkinson facial expression assessment or ingested ground cayenne.

## TRPV1 antagonist and levodopa dyskinesia in rats

First published online August 22, 2007. In levodopa-treated rats with 6-OHDA lesions, WIN reduced certain abnormal involuntary movements. The FAAH inhibitor URB597 alone did not show a clear reduction. URB597 plus the TRPV1 antagonist capsazepine reduced all movement categories at 100 minutes, while capsazepine alone was inconclusive. No capsaicin or cayenne food was administered. The authors distinguish this dyskinesia model from vertical hyperactivity after capsaicin in reserpine-treated rats.
