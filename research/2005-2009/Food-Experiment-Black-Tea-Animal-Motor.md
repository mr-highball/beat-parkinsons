# Black tea preparations and animal motor outcomes

## Oral black tea extract in a lesioned rat model

Chaturvedi and colleagues, February 9, 2006. Rats in the pre-lesion and post-lesion intervention groups received 1.5% black tea extract orally. The authors reported significant improvement in amphetamine-evoked circling and spontaneous locomotor activity in both groups compared with the lesioned group. The overall motor and biochemical recovery was stronger with pre-lesion administration. The primary Fig. 1 shows about 190 rotations per 30 minutes with lesion alone, 143 with post-lesion extract and 102 with pre-lesion extract, compared with 17 sham and 15 extract alone. These are readings from the plot. The graph marks lesion alone ***a, post-lesion *b and pre-lesion ***b. The caption is needed to map the symbols to exact contrasts and thresholds. The spontaneous locomotor values and detailed tea Methods remain unavailable. The recovered abstract and figures give no straight-line walking-speed or rest-tremor result.
