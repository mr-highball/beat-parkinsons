# Movement and Rehabilitation

### 2007 — Home gait cueing in the RESCUE trial

A randomized crossover trial found small short-term gains in gait-related mobility after training with external rhythm cues at home. Effects diminished after training stopped, raising questions about maintenance. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17229744/)

### 2008 — Tai chi pilot for balance and mobility

Hackney and Earhart randomized 33 people to tai chi or a control group. Participants who completed the tai chi program improved on some balance and mobility measures. The small sample and attrition limit certainty about fall prevention. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2552999/)
