# Strawberry and fisetin follow-up, 2005–2009

## No detectable fisetin protection in 6-OHDA rats

Zbarsky and colleagues reported in October 2005 that isolated fisetin pretreatment did not protect substantia nigra tyrosine hydroxylase-positive cell counts or striatal dopamine in rats with unilateral medial forebrain bundle 6-hydroxydopamine lesions. Curcumin and naringenin showed protection on those outcomes in the same experiment. The study addresses a constituent in a toxin model. It gives no raw strawberry exposure or handwriting measure for claim `0349`. [Primary report, PMID 16298737](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16298737/)

## Oral fisetin and striatal dopamine in a Salk patent example

Pamela Maher’s patent application WO2008011538A2 was published January 24, 2008. Example 10 and Figure 9 describe an MPTP mouse experiment with four mice in each of the two isolated fisetin arms, with and without MPTP. Oral fisetin at 25 mg/kg began before toxin exposure. The disclosure describes lower striatal dopamine with MPTP alone and values at least as high as untreated controls with MPTP plus fisetin. The report gives a biochemical endpoint and no raw strawberry or handwriting result. The 2017 paper from the same investigator describes a related design with different group sizes and treatment arms. Cohort overlap cannot be determined from the two reports.
