# Ground bay leaf clinical exposure

## Ground bay leaf capsules in a type 2 diabetes trial

Khan, Zaman and Anderson first published their trial abstract on April 1, 2007. The full report was published online December 27, 2008 for the January 2009 issue and identifies the abstract as an earlier report of the study. Investigators randomly assigned 40 people with type 2 diabetes to 1, 2 or 3 g/day of finely ground *Laurus nobilis* or a corn flour placebo in capsules for 30 days, followed by 10 days without capsules. The study excluded people with a history of neurologic disease. [Original 2007 abstract](https://doi.org/10.1096/fasebj.21.6.LB46-d) · [Original full report, Methods and Acknowledgments](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jcbn/44/1/44_08-188/_pdf) · [Local CC BY text](Source-Text/PMC2613499.txt#L68)

The trial measured fasting glucose and serum lipids. The authors state that participants consumed all capsules and reported no significant side effects, without a structured swallowing or drooling assessment or symptom-specific safety counts. The reported preparation matches finely ground leaf, and capsule delivery limits direct exposure of the mouth and throat to the powder. [Original 2009 report, Methods and Results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2613499/)
