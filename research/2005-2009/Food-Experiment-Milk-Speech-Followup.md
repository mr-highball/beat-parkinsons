# Milk and voice follow-up, 2005–2009

## Beverage exposure and sustained-vowel measures in a dissertation

The University of Kansas catalog describes Robin E. Liston’s December 2005 dissertation. Thirty volunteers drank milk, cola, orange juice and water with pre- and post-drink self-reports, sustained-vowel recordings and videostroboscopic observations. The institutional abstract reports increased mucus ratings after each beverage, and no significant differences on the acoustic jitter, shimmer and noise-to-harmonics measures. These are voice-quality measures, not a listener speech-intelligibility test. The abstract does not establish milk species or pasteurization, and the dissertation full methods, timing, blinding and item estimates were not recovered.
