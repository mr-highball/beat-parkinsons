# Food and Nutrition

### 2009 — Food-frequency instrument used in the later Milan Parkinson cohort

Festi and colleagues describe the Osservatorio Nutrizionale Grana Padano tool, launched in 2005 for Italian primary care. The later Cassani study used its 66-item food-frequency questionnaire for the preceding 12 months and a separate 24-hour recall to check portions. The public tool descriptions do not establish that baked potato and extra-virgin olive oil were separate interview fields. The 2017 results list potatoes, fried potatoes and olive oil without those further specifications. [Instrument paper and university record](https://iris.univr.it/handle/11562/473385) · [Program documentation](https://www.educazionenutrizionale.granapadano.it/downloads/razionale-scientifico.pdf) · [Milan cohort methods and results](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-MDS-Diet-Followup.md#food-intake-by-swallowing-status-in-the-italian-dietary-cohort)

### 2005 — Milk intake and incident Parkinson’s in the Honolulu cohort

Over roughly 30 years of follow-up in 7,504 men, the highest milk intake group had higher Parkinson’s incidence than nonconsumers. Calcium intake showed no apparent association. This observational study measured incidence before diagnosis and left possible confounding unresolved. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15781824/)

### 2007 — Dairy intake and incidence in a prospective US cohort

The highest dairy intake quintile had higher Parkinson’s incidence than the lowest quintile in a prospective study of 130,864 adults. Patterns by sex and intake level varied, and the study could not establish causation or effects after diagnosis. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17272289/)

### 2007 — Prudent and Mediterranean-style patterns and incidence

Two prospective cohorts totaling more than 130,000 people yielded 508 incident cases. Higher diet-quality scores were associated with lower incidence, with uncertainty around some estimates. The Western pattern showed no clear association. Food-frequency measurements and confounding limit interpretation. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17991663/)

### 2008 — Fish oil for depression in a pilot trial

A three-month randomized pilot compared fish oil with mineral oil in participants with Parkinson’s and depression. Some clinician-rated depression measures favored fish oil. The small sample and differences among outcome measures limit the inference. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18485485/)

### 2008 — Dayak onion bulb chemistry

Han et al. isolated compounds from bulbs identified as *Eleutherine americana*, an Iridaceae plant called Dayak onion. The article, published online June 16, 2008, reports eleutherin, isoeleutherin, and 1,5-dihydroxy-3-methylanthraquinone. In stimulated mouse macrophages, eleutherin and isoeleutherin reduced nitric oxide production, with IC₅₀ values of 11.4 and 7.7 μM. The substituted methylanthraquinone was inactive in that assay. This chemistry and cell assay concern isolated compounds and provide no clinical symptom outcome for *Eleutherine* or *Allium cepa*. [Original article, DOI 10.1248/cpb.56.1314](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/cpb/56/9/56_9_1314/_pdf) · [2026 computational study](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-MDS-Botanical-Followup.md#dayak-onion-docking-and-bulb-chemistry).


### 2009 — Isolated piperine and induced rat pain

Oral isolated piperine in corn oil was compared with vehicle in rat paw and arthritis tests; the higher tested dose reduced evoked pain-related responses. This is a constituent and animal-model result, not a clinical comparison of cracked black pepper in Parkinson disease.
