# Canola oil and motor task comparators

## Paced tapping rate and BOLD during the Slade crossover

Slade and colleagues first published February 11, 2008, ahead of the March 2009 print issue. Eight of ten healthy adults were analyzed in the crossover of a liquid meal with or without 50 mL canola oil. In the simple paced pressure-bulb tapping task, average rate was 2.09 ± 0.27 Hz SEM across trials with no significant variation. The paper reports no significant meal effect on task-activated BOLD at three hours. Task timing, image analysis and participant sample differ from the 2003 experiment. The processing grade, hand dexterity accuracy and Parkinson effects remain unresolved. ## Canola comparator and unusable grip measurements in arthritis

The Victoria University thesis is dated 2004 and its institutional metadata records 2008 as the online deposit. The study randomized 120 people and analyzed 101 completers. Its oral, topical and combined emu oil comparisons used Crisco canola on matching routes. The planned grip-strength instrument proved faulty and the thesis discarded that measurement. The document supplies a fatty-acid specification and no verified refining or separate manual dexterity outcome.
