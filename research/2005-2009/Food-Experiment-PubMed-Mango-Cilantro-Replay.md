# Cilantro and adverse-symptom survey follow-up

## Jordanian diabetes survey reports herb use and aggregate dizziness

Otoom and colleagues interviewed 310 patients with diabetes in Jordan during 2003–2004. Of 96 respondents who reported using herbs, 10.4% mentioned coriander. The paper’s abstract reports dizziness among complaints by herb users as a group. It gives no coriander-specific adverse-event breakdown, exposure preparation, timing, blood pressure measurement or Parkinson subgroup. The abstract and an academic author-affiliation publication list were accessible. The journal body was outside available access on the search date.
