# Grapeseed and flaxseed oil evidence

## Grapeseed oil in two constipation-related patent filings

A 2008 UK/WIPO patent lists grapeseed oil alongside senna, olive oil, honey and several dried fruits in a laxative paste. It describes the intended outcome of relieving constipation but gives no controlled outcome or clinical dataset. The 2009 Korean filing gives an uncontrolled account of 27 adult respondents after fermented garlic and ginger capsules made with grapeseed oil. It reports improvement in a combined diarrhea-and-constipation symptom category. The filing gives no constipation-specific change, and its oil processing is unspecified. It also describes substituting other oils. [2008 UK patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/GB2446373A/en) · [2009 Korean patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/KR20090081707A/en)
