# Flaxseed oil evidence

## Flaxseed oil versus fish oil in a human fatty acid comparison

Barceló-Coblijn and colleagues published a 12-week randomized comparison in September 2008. Sixty-two firefighters were assigned to doses of flaxseed oil or fish oil, or to sunflower oil. Fish oil rapidly increased erythrocyte DHA, while the two higher flaxseed oil doses increased erythrocyte alpha-linolenic acid, EPA and docosapentaenoic acid. The trial reported no clear group differences in inflammatory markers or lipid profile. This comparison documents distinct biomarker responses without a Parkinson population or symptom measures. [Original report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18779299/)

## Tremor and rigidity descriptions in a combined regimen patent

The January 4, 2007 U.S. filing names cold-pressed organic flaxseed oil as a possible component of a sunflower or safflower oil blend. Example 1 Case 4 lists small handwriting and insomnia at baseline, then reports reduced tremor and rigidity following an oral and intravenous regimen. Handwriting size and insomnia were not reported after treatment. Case 5 describes less arm stiffness, faster gait and clearer thinking after a combined regimen. These are uncontrolled narratives with several coexposures and no verified oil dose or formulation for each case. Example 4 repeats a vignette already described elsewhere in the filing and gives no independent outcome series.
