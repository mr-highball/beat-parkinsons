# Grapefruit, cabergoline and stride outcomes

## February 2005 clinical pharmacokinetic abstract

Nagai and colleagues measured plasma cabergoline in five people with Parkinson disease receiving cabergoline, before and after coadministration of grapefruit juice. The abstract reports approximately 1.7 times the concentration with juice and no observed adverse events during this small study. The result is a blood concentration observation. The abstract gives no stride length, raw fruit intake, precision estimate, or measured motor effect. [Original publication P84](https://ascpt.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1016/j.clpt.2004.12.213) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2005-Nagai-Grapefruit-Cabergoline-Factual-Extract.txt)

## November 2005 chart

Nagai and Nomoto show serial cabergoline concentrations with and without seven days of grapefruit juice coadministration in Figure 4 of their 2005 Japanese article. The figure gives no sample count or stride outcome. They do not establish independent replication of the February abstract. Their chart for levodopa and domperidone is a separate comparison. [Japanese journal Figure 4](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jscpt1970/36/6/36_6_273/_pdf) · [Government-hosted journal copy](https://mhlw-grants.niph.go.jp/system/files/2005/057011/200500270A/200500270A0006.pdf)

## March 2005 movement disorders presentation

A university bibliography gives the page S123 citation for Nagai and colleagues’ conference presentation on grapefruit juice and dopamine agonists. The complete printed presentation on S123 remains open. An earlier conference from the same group tested changes in cabergoline concentration, with participant overlap unverified.
