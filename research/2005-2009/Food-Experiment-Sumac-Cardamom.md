# Food experiment evidence for sumac and cardamom

## 2007 Green cardamom extract, intestinal contraction and sedation

Gilani and colleagues tested a crude extract of *Elettaria cardamomum*. The extract stimulated isolated guinea pig ileum at 3–10 mg/mL and relaxed spontaneous and high-potassium contractions in isolated rabbit jejunum. The aqueous and organic fractions separated the two effects. Intravenous administration lowered arterial pressure in anesthetized rats. The extract prolonged pentobarbital-induced sleeping time in mice. These endpoints inform 0015, 0453 and 0945. The report gives tissue contractions, pressure under anesthesia and drug-induced sleep duration. It contains no measure of bowel movements, postural lightheadedness or daytime sleepiness in people with Parkinson’s. Published online October 22, 2007 and in print in 2008. [Primary report, PMID 18037596](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18037596/) · [Full primary article](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=b0030d5259f67d116b62a026f7ac4dc43c513a11&repid=rep1&type=pdf)

## 2009 Green cardamom powder and resting blood pressure

Verma and colleagues administered 3 g/day of small cardamom fruit powder to 20 people with newly diagnosed stage 1 hypertension for 12 weeks. Table 1 follows the same 20 participants over four visits. Mean systolic pressure decreased from 154.2 to 134.8 mmHg. All participants reported well-being without side effects in the report. The results supply an uncontrolled blood-pressure lead for 0453. The protocol did not measure blood pressure after standing or elicit posture-linked lightheadedness. [Original paper, Table 1](https://nopr.niscpr.res.in/bitstream/123456789/7254/1/IJBB%2046%286%29%20503-506.pdf) · [PubMed, PMID 20361714](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20361714/)

## Cardamom seed shared by the wormwood and control groups

Omer and colleagues compared added wormwood in 40 adults with Crohn disease. The original indexed methods describe 40 mg cardamom seeds in each capsule of both arms, alongside common botanicals. The 21-item Hamilton Depression Scale could estimate the incremental wormwood comparison. The protocol did not provide a cardamom-free group or verify grinding of the seeds. The original numerical tables remain unavailable through the checked publisher route.
