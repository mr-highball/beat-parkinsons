# Finfish hallucination case reports

## Hallucinations after baked sea bream

De Haro and Pommier reported two cases following consumption of Sarpa salpa. One adult described visual hallucinations and auditory symptoms after baked sea bream. Another described auditory hallucinations after consuming the same fish species. The authors did not identify the toxin. The food was finfish, and no shrimp exposure or Parkinson-specific outcome was established.
