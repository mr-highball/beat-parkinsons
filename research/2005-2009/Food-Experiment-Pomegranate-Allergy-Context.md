# Pomegranate seeds and exercise-associated allergic symptoms

## Reported seed ingestion and exercise-related allergy

Gangemi and colleagues described a patient who reported eating pomegranate seeds before dancing and developed generalized urticaria and lip edema. Skin testing with fresh seeds was positive. The investigators did not repeat the combined ingestion and exercise challenge. The case concerns an allergic reaction with a possible exercise cofactor. It supplies no visual-hallucination or Parkinson outcome.
