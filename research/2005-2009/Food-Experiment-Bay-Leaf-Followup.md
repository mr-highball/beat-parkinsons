# Bay Leaf Followup

## Ground bay leaf capsules in a metabolic trial

Khan, Zaman and Anderson’s article first appeared December 27, 2008. Forty adults with type 2 diabetes were divided into three capsule groups of finely ground Laurus nobilis leaf or a corn-flour comparator for 30 days. The original methods and tables describe glucose and circulating lipids. The authors report no significant side effects overall. The report gives no separate stool frequency, constipation score or Parkinson comparison.
