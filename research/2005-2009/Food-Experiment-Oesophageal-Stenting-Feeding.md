## Earlier oesophageal stenting and postoperative feeding

Sabău and colleagues' 2009 primary report describes treatment of 142 patients with oesophageal stenosis from 1996 through 2008. Sixty malignant cases received a prosthesis. The authors describe restarting liquids before progression to semi-solids and solids. No olive oil regimen is identified in the primary report. This dates an earlier local technique and outcome before the 2018 and 2023 oil-containing descriptions.
