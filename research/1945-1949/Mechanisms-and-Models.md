# Mechanisms and models

### 1949 — Deep brain electrical recordings in parkinsonism

Meyers and colleagues described electrostriograms from people with parkinsonism undergoing neurosurgical assessment. They could not find a consistent relationship between recording type and the clinical pattern or degree of disability. The negative result is an early test of an electrical signal as a possible marker in a surgical population. [Source](https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/12/2/111.full.pdf)

### 1949 — Subthalamic lesions and hyperkinesia in rhesus monkeys

Whittier and Mettler investigated motor effects of experimental subthalamic lesions in rhesus monkeys, with particular attention to the subthalamic nucleus. Hyperkinesia supported the importance of this motor circuit and offered a model of a different movement syndrome. The study supplied anatomical evidence for later questions about basal ganglia circuitry. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18136718/)
