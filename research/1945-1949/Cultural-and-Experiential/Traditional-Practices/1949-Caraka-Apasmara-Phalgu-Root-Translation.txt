The older translation renders the relevant item as “root of common fig.” It is one part of the compound preparation mahāpañcagavya ghṛta. The Sanskrit text specifies phalgu root, and the Indian root monograph assigns the drug to Ficus hispida. That monograph identifies its standardized material. The vernacular word can have other botanical meanings and this edition alone does not identify a historical specimen.

Open trail: Compare a dated print witness and classical commentary for the compound name and botanical identification. Keep the root preparation distinct from culinary dried fig fruit and the described apasmāra episodes distinct from Parkinson symptoms.
