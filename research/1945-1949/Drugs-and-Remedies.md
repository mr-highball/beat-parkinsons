# Drugs and remedies

### 1948 — Diphenhydramine for paralysis agitans

Joseph Budnitz published a preliminary series of eight cases treated with diphenhydramine, following an initial trial in 1946. The brief publisher excerpt documents use and rationale. A 1951 review reports perceived improvement and notes concurrent atropine in half the series, which complicates attribution. The series was small and preliminary. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM194806172382504) · [1951 review](https://academic.oup.com/pmj/article-pdf/27/310/390/50390484/postgradmedj-27-390.pdf)

### 1949 — Trihexyphenidyl in 44 cases

Schwab and Leigh published a clinical evaluation of synthetic trihexyphenidyl (Artane) in 44 people with Parkinson’s disease. They framed the study as a comparison with prior treatments such as diphenhydramine and parpanit. This period record documents adoption and early clinical evaluation. The journal preview gives limited outcome detail. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM194909292411303)

### 1949 — Trihexyphenidyl in 117 cases

Doshay and Constable described their preliminary clinical experience in 117 cases and recorded drug reactions as well as therapeutic effects. The authors mentioned favorable impressions from further cases outside the reported series. An uncontrolled clinical series can document tolerability and reported relief while leaving the comparative effect uncertain. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1949.02900520003002)

### 1949 — Diparcol and separate measures of daily function

R. S. Duff recorded favorable and adverse findings in eight cases treated with diparcol. The report describes improved rigidity and bed mobility in one patient whose tremor was unchanged, with concurrent belladonna and adverse symptoms complicating interpretation. It also outlines a way to record changes in chronic movement disability. [Source](https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/1/4605/613.full.pdf)
