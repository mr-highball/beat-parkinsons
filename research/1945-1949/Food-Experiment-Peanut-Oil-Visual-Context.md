# A historical visual report during topical drug exposure

## Apparent visual experience with a topical pharmaceutical preparation

Summers reported a delirious child who appeared to see things following topical exposure to a camphor medicine. The physician diagnosed camphor poisoning. The author separately said arachis oil was the contemporary formulation's vehicle. The patient-specific product and grade were unverified. A 2006 guideline interprets the described experience as a visual hallucination and cites the same patient. This is a single case in the context of a topical drug, with no oral food or Parkinson comparison.
