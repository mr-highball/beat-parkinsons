# Procedures and devices

### 1947 — Human stereotactic apparatus

Spiegel, Wycis, Marks and Lee described equipment for targeting deep structures in the human brain. This engineering advance provided a way to investigate focal surgical lesions for movement disorders. The instrument paper is evidence for the apparatus and its development. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17777432/)

### 1948 — Surgical investigation of the tremor–rigidity syndrome

A Medical Journal of Australia paper documented clinical investigations of surgery in patients with tremor and rigidity. It cites Meyers’s earlier operations on the basal ganglia and is an independent archival lead for comparing surgical targets and case outcomes. The bibliographic record alone cannot establish symptom benefit or harms. [Source](https://doi.org/10.5694/j.1326-5377.1948.tb28054.x)
