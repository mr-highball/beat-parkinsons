Text coverage: All ten original page scans visually checked. Pages 522, 523 and 528 provide formulation, cohort scope and Parkinson commentary.

Schlesinger describes a historical injected d-tubocurarine preparation in wax and peanut oil. The 200-patient total on page 522 covers multiple conditions, including muscle spasm, spasticity, dystonia and Parkinsonian rigidity. The author reports that painful or immobilizing rigidity sometimes eased and that response varied. Page 528 describes tremor as outside the observed indication. It gives no number of Parkinson patients, separate tremor scale or comparison capable of attributing an effect to the oil.

Bird and Meyers cite this report and evaluate the drug formulation in a later clinical series. Neither paper evaluates oral cold-pressed oil.
