Title: 1-5-Vinyl-2-thiooxazolidone, an antithyroid compound from yellow turnip and from Brassica seeds.
PMID: 15390398
First published: 1949
Authors: ASTWOOD EB, GREER MA, ETTLINGER MG
Journal: The Journal of biological chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15390398/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Landmark endocrinology isolation paper (J Biol Chem 1949; companion Science 1949 PMID:17792403, DOI:10.1126/science.109.2843.631) by Astwood, Greer, and Ettlinger isolating L-5-vinyl-2-thiooxazolidone (goitrin) as the active antithyroid factor from yellow turnip / rutabaga (Brassica napobrassica) and Brassica seeds. Establishes the historical phytochemical basis for goitrogenic concerns regarding raw rutabaga; later contextualized by Galanty et al. 2024 (PMID:38612798) showing thermal cooking deactivates myrosinase and prevents clinical hypothyroidism in iodine-replete diets. Supplies no PD symptom data.
