Title: Olive oil mentioned in an esophageal ulcer treatment regimen
Source: https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pmc&id=5283489
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

A. S. Johnstone, April 1946, Edinburgh Medical Journal 53(4):160–172. Printed from a lecture delivered in June 1945.
In the section on peptic ulceration of the esophagus, the author recounts Dick and Hurst's recommendation of olive oil with atropine as one part of a combined regimen. The lecture discusses treatment in structural or inflammatory esophageal disease. It gives no olive oil grade, comparison isolating oil, or Parkinson swallowing outcome.
The underlying Dick and Hurst reports remain a distinct historical citation trail.
