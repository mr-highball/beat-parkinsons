Page 331 indexes a ginger infusion report for rheumatic complaints in volume 37, p. 342. The bowel obstruction and constipation references under Intestines on p. 335 are separate. Page 343 indexes olive oil in an obstetric context. It also indexes a review of James Parkinson's shaking palsy essay in volume 38, p. 71. The index supplies no link between the ginger or olive-oil exposures and Parkinson's cases. The ginger item and the index references to it are one published account family.
