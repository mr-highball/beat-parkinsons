This set indexes earlier essays and cases. The ginger entries on pp. 207, 210 and 211 point to separate gout or rheumatism reports in volume 12 p. 66 and volume 19 p. 527. The constipation reference on p. 192 concerns a separate mercurial-friction case. Page 178 points to the earlier garden-parsley account about fits. The oats entry on p. 159 concerns crop yield. Page 205 lists a cayenne amaurosis case. On p. 210 cucumber and grapes are jointly reported in a gout entry, and p. 211 describes a multicomponent saffron infusion for gout. Orange County on p. 170 is a place.
