# Food experiment evidence for romaine lettuce, celery stalk, cabbage and Brussels sprouts

## 1990 Celery mastication and parotid saliva

Mackie and Pangborn measured one parotid gland in ten healthy participants chewing celery, bread or parafilm and receiving other oral stimuli. Chewing food produced more alpha-amylase secretion than chewing nonfood stimuli, and bread produced more than celery. Chewing celery elicited more parotid flow than parafilm. The abstract does not specify celery plant part. The endpoints were salivary output and composition, with no swallowing safety, clinical drooling or Parkinson outcome. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2359773/)
