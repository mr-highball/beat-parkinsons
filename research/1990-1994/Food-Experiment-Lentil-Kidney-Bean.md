# Lentil and kidney bean evidence

## Surveillance of red kidney bean poisoning

Rodhouse and colleagues reviewed 50 suspected UK incidents reported between July 1976 and February 1989. Nine were confirmed through food hemagglutinin detection and compatible nausea, vomiting and diarrhea beginning one to seven hours after eating. Another 23 had compatible symptoms, timing and preparation histories. This surveillance concerns inadequately processed or raw red kidney beans. Drooling and hallucination were outside the case definition and reported outcomes. Early UK incidents may overlap the 1980 Noah case series.
