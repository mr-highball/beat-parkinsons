# Almond oil in an edited historical casebook

## Separate oil, facial and voice entries

Ward and Yell’s 1993 edited casebook preserves Brownrigg’s eighteenth-century clinical entries. Separate patients and different routes occur on the same pages: a topical almond-oil facial application, an oral multi-ingredient preparation, and patients with difficulty speaking or hoarse throat. They do not establish an oral cold-pressed oil effect on Parkinson voice or facial expression.
