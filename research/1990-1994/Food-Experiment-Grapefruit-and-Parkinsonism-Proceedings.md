# Grapefruit juice pharmacokinetics and stride studies in the 1994 proceedings

## Grapefruit juice and cyclosporine in renal transplant recipients

Van Rooij and colleagues reported an open crossover in 12 kidney transplant recipients. The comparison used grapefruit juice or water with scheduled oral cyclosporine. Peak cyclosporine concentration was higher with juice, and the metabolite-to-cyclosporine AUC ratio was lower. The original presentation reports no statistically significant difference in overall cyclosporine AUC. It concerns pharmacokinetics in transplant recipients. [Original page 497P](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/1854/1364906/beebc2e75da6/brjclinpharm00021-0099.png)

## Stride measurements in siblings of people with Parkinsonism

O’Neill and colleagues compared gait and standing sway in siblings of people with idiopathic Parkinsonism and matched controls. The abstract reports group differences in measures including stride length. It did not study grapefruit intake. The original abstract begins on 505P and continues onto 506P. [Original page 505P](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/1854/1364906/8c6add6b953c/brjclinpharm00021-0107.png) · [Original page 506P](https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/1854/1364906/2db1ca974ec7/brjclinpharm00021-0108.png)

Both presentations appear in the May 1994 issue of the British Journal of Clinical Pharmacology. The later Japanese grapefruit juice study measured a different drug in people with Parkinson disease. [Original 1994 collection](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1364906/) · [2005 grapefruit and cabergoline](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Grapefruit-Stride-and-Drug-Exposure.md)

## Grapefruit juice and acute drug-induced tremor signs in rats

Sharif and Ali describe a separate 1994 drug-metabolism experiment. In the author abstract, the onset of a cluster of drug-induced signs that included tremor was about two to three minutes with saline and seven minutes with prior grapefruit juice. The authors describe reduced severity for the cluster without numerical rest-tremor or tremor-only estimates.
