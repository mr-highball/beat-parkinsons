# Safflower oil and neurologic outcomes in 1990–1994

## Developmental safflower oil and exploratory behavior

Enslen, Milon and Malnoë used semipurified maternal and offspring diets with 5.5% safflower oil or 6% soybean oil. They also compared standard chow and changing from safflower to soybean at weaning. Spontaneous locomotion was similar across diet groups. Horizontal movements and rearings in a novel environment were lower after developmental safflower exposure, including rats switched at weaning. The feed design tested omega-3 availability across development, and processing grade is unspecified. This study separates spontaneous movement from novelty exploration and shows why the latter cannot be read as human apathy or Parkinsonian bradykinesia.
