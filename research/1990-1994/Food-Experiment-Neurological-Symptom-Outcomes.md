# Parkinson and related neurological symptom outcomes

## Subcutaneous apomorphine and reported off-period symptoms

February 1990. In 57 levodopa-treated patients receiving subcutaneous apomorphine, the clinical narrative reports improvement of off-period limb or pelvic pain in three patients and off-period hallucinations in one. The report does not identify the sensory modality of the hallucinations or provide an ingredient comparison. The observations concern a medication in a clinical series.
