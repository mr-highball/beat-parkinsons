# Swallowing sources from Crossref citations, 1990–1994

## Nutritional depletion and swallowing after adrenal transplant

Waxman and colleagues' October 1990 abstract describes seven people with Parkinson disease and three with progressive supranuclear palsy who underwent adrenal medullary transplantation. Several Parkinson participants had mild to moderate nutritional depletion before surgery. Six of the ten participants underwent videofluoroscopy, and the report describes dysphagia with or without aspiration. The abstract identifies underlying disease, medication timing and postoperative complications as possible contributors. It gives no bolus composition, food-linked case or Parkinson-specific aspiration count. The full report remains unavailable through the DOI, Wiley and Sage PDF routes checked on November 12, 2026. [Primary abstract, PMID 2122203](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2122203/)
