# Mechanisms and Models for 1990–1994

### 1990 — Complex I activity in postmortem substantia nigra

Schapira and colleagues compared postmortem substantia nigra samples from nine Parkinson’s cases and nine matched controls. Complex I activity was reduced despite similar mitochondrial mass and total protein. Postmortem measures cannot determine whether the activity difference preceded neuron loss. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2154550/)

### 1994 — Glutathione measurements across basal ganglia disorders

Sian and colleagues measured glutathione across brain regions in Parkinson’s disease and several other neurodegenerative disorders. Reduced glutathione in the Parkinson’s substantia nigra supported an oxidative stress hypothesis. The comparison offered clues about disease specificity while leaving temporal direction open. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8080242/)
