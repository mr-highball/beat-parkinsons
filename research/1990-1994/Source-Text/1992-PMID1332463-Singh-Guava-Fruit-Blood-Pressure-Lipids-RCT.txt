Title: Effects of guava intake on serum total and high-density lipoprotein cholesterol levels and on systemic blood pressure
PMID: 1332463
DOI: 10.1016/0002-9149(92)90763-o
First published: 1992
Authors: Singh RB, Rastogi SS, Singh R, Ghosh S, Niaz MA
Journal: The American Journal of Cardiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1332463/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Twelve-week single-blind RCT in 120 patients with essential hypertension (61 guava fruit before meals vs 59 usual diet) reporting net reductions of 9.0/8.0 mmHg in BP, 9.9% total cholesterol, and 7.7% triglycerides with +8.0% HDL-C. CRITICAL INTEGRITY & RELIABILITY CAVEAT: Led by Ram B. Singh (Moradabad, India), whose 1990s-2002 dietary cardiology trials became the subject of the landmark BMJ 2005 statistical fabrication investigation (White 2005 PMID:16052022; Al-Marzouki et al. 2005 PMID:16052019) and Lancet 2005 Expression of Concern (Horton 2005 PMID:16054927), specifically scrutinizing his 1992-1993 cardiovascular nutrition papers. Additionally measured supine/seated BP in essential hypertension without orthostatic standing challenge or lightheadedness.
