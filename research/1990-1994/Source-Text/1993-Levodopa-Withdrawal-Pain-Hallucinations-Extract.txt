Pain and hallucinations during acute levodopa withdrawal
July 1, 1993 · PMID 8331352 · PMC1015058 · DOI 10.1136/jnnp.56.7.771
https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/56/7/771.full.pdf
Factual extract from original printed Methods and Results pp. 771–775.

The JNNP report assessed nine patients with motor fluctuations during acute levodopa withdrawal. On p. 772 the authors describe one participant who withdrew after 23 hours with severe off-period confusion and visual hallucinations. Another patient reported increasing leg pain and discomfort after 96 hours despite little change in the recorded motor score. These are individual events during medication withdrawal. The study yields no food-effect estimate or hallucination-frequency outcome. The surname Sage in a citation is outside the exposure assessment.
