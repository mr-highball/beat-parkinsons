Title: Evaluation of a maize-cowpea-palm oil diet for the dietary management of Nigerian children with acute, watery diarrhea.
PMID: 7981559
DOI: 10.1111/j.1651-2227.1994.tb13153.x
First published: 1994
Authors: Grange AO, Santosham M, Ayodele AK, Lesi FE, Stallings RY, Brown KH
Journal: Acta paediatrica (Oslo, Norway : 1992)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7981559/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Hospital randomized trial (Grange et al., 1994, Acta Paediatr) in 69 Nigerian boys (6-24 months) with acute watery diarrhea comparing a bundled maize-cowpea-palm oil diet vs lactose-free soy formula. Median duration of liquid stool was shorter on maize-cowpea-palm oil (42 h vs 140 h, p<0.001) with similar treatment failure (5.7% vs 8.8%, p=0.67), though baseline stool output and bicarbonate differed across groups. Limits: acute infectious pediatric diarrhea and bundled 3-food porridge; irrelevant to adult Parkinson's constipation.
