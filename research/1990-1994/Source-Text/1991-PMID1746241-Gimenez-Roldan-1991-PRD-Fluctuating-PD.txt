Title: Predicting beneficial response to a protein-redistribution diet in fluctuating Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 1746241
First published: 1991
Authors: Giménez-Roldán S, Mateo D
Journal: Acta neurologica Belgica
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1746241/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Clinical study of protein-redistribution diet (PRD) in fluctuating PD patients: beneficial motor-fluctuation response concentrated in levodopa-responsive fluctuators with reduced daily OFF hours on evening-shifted protein. Limits: pooled dietary protein, small sample, open-label outpatient monitoring.
