Ferrous sulfate and clinical response to levodopa
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2291872/
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

In nine patients with Parkinson disease, taking ferrous sulfate together with carbidopa–levodopa lowered levodopa AUC by 30% and carbidopa AUC by more than 75%. Change in levodopa exposure correlated with change in therapeutic response (r = .83, p < .01). The average clinical response comparison was inconclusive (p = .055). This experiment used an iron tablet and supplied no daily OFF-time diary or curry leaf test.
