Title: Anaphylaxis to annatto dye: a case report.
PMID: 1994783
First published: 1991
Authors: Nish, Whisman, Goetz, Ramirez
Journal: Annals of allergy
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1994783/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Clinical case report of 1 patient developing urticaria, angioedema, and severe hypotension within 20 minutes of eating annatto-dyed cereal with milk; skin testing and IgE immunoblotting confirmed sensitization to ~50 kD residual annatto seed protein. Companion double-blind oral challenge (Sadowska 2021, PMID 34320336) and older urticaria provocation series (Mikkelsen 1978, PMID 150265) confirm rare hypersensitivity reactions to annatto dye. Limits: acute IgE anaphylactic shock / cutaneous allergy, not PD orthostatic hypotension.
