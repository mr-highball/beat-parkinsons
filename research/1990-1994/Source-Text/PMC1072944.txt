Title: Dystonia-parkinsonism syndrome resulting from a bullet injury in the midbrain
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1072944/
PMID: 8201353
PMCID: PMC1072944
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.57.5.658
Publication date: 1994-05
Citation: Rondot P, Bathien N, de Recondo J, Gueguen B, Fredy D, de Recondo A, Samson Y. Journal of Neurology, Neurosurgery & Psychiatry 57(5), 658.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 658.

The case describes secondary parkinsonism after a focal midbrain injury, with intermittent resting tremor as well as postural and intention components. The authors used examination, an imaging comparison across sides and treatment observations. They describe tremor improvement on a dopamine-agonist regimen. The report has no fig exposure or dried-date comparison, and the pain mention is sensory testing.
