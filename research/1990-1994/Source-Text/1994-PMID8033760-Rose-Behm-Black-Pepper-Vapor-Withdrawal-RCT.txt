Title: Inhalation of vapor from black pepper extract reduces smoking withdrawal symptoms.
PMID: 8033760
DOI: 10.1016/0376-8716(94)90160-0
First published: 1994
Authors: Rose JE, Behm FM
Journal: Drug and alcohol dependence
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8033760/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-arm randomized controlled trial in n=48 overnight-deprived cigarette smokers completing a 3-h laboratory session puffing a device delivering vapor from black pepper essential oil vs mint/menthol cartridge vs unflavored empty cartridge. Black pepper essential oil vapor significantly reduced cigarette craving relative to both controls and alleviated negative affect and somatic symptoms of anxiety relative to unflavored placebo. Limits: inhaled essential oil vapor (not oral ground black pepper), acute 3-h nicotine withdrawal session in non-PD smokers.
