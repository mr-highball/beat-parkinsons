Title: Levodopa induced ON-OFF motor fluctuations in Parkinson's disease related to rhythmical masticatory jaw movements.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC489044/
PMID: 1316430
PMCID: PMC489044
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.55.4.304
Publication year: 1992
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC
Coverage: Original article verified. This local record is a distilled factual summary.
Round 1 claims: 0502

Karlsson and colleagues recorded 12 patients with Parkinson disease chewing peanuts before and after levodopa. The same food task was used in both medication states. Opening and closing durations did not clearly change. The occlusal phase shortened, and jaw velocity and vertical displacement increased in the ON state.

The food served as a standardized test task. The study has no no-peanut or other food comparator, swallowing-safety assessment, dried-date exposure, or separately graded pain response. All original pages 304–307 were inspected.
