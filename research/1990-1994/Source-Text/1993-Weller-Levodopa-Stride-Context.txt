Instrumented stride in a levodopa formulation comparison
April 1993 · PMID 8485018 · PMC1381548 · DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2125.1993.tb04154.x
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1381548/
Factual extract from visually verified original scans pp. 379–385.

Weller et al., Br J Clin Pharmacol 35(4), 379–385. Nine patients with idiopathic parkinsonism received two controlled-release levodopa formulations in random order with placebo balancing. Printed p. 382 reports adjusted grand mean stride of 1,009 (SE 14) mm with Madopar CR and 1,058 (SE 14) mm with Sinemet CR (p = .04). Pages 380 and 384 discuss earlier work on fallers and possible mechanisms involving rigidity, respectively. The trial quantified gait and leaves fall frequency, rigidity severity and a sage effect unevaluated.
