Title: The effect of raw onions on acid reflux and reflux symptoms
Authors: M L Allen and colleagues
Primary indexed original abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2327378/
Identifiers: PMID 2327378
Date: April 1990, Am J Gastroenterol 85(4):377–380
Coverage: factual extract of the original abstract

The study compared 16 people with heartburn and 16 people without. Participants ate a plain hamburger and drank water with or without one slice of raw onion on counterbalanced days. Investigators monitored esophageal pH for two hours. Compared with the no-onion condition, the heartburn group showed more reflux events, acid exposure, heartburn and belching. The other group did not show an increase. The study did not include cooked onion or a laryngopharyngeal or speech assessment. Complete article text and subsequent short letters were not available through the reviewed route.
