Title: Gastric acid secretion and absorption of levodopa in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8200147/
PMID: 8200147
Publication: Clinical Neurology 34(3):264–266, March 1994
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated original English author abstract of Japanese report

The study evaluated gastric acid secretion in 38 participants with a
refractory response to levodopa. Twenty-two had low secretion. Ten
of those participants received lemon juice alongside levodopa for
two weeks. The authors report increased plasma levodopa at two
measurement times and less marked improvement in tremor than in
rigidity, akinesia or gait.

The abstract supplies no numeric tremor result, rest/action phenotype,
or clearly described concurrent comparison. The full Japanese report
remains unverified. The tested exposure is lemon juice given with
levodopa. It does not evaluate raw grapefruit or cabergoline.
