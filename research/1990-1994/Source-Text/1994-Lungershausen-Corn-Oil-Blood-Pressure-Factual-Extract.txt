Topic: Corn oil comparator and seated blood pressure
Source: https://downloads.regulations.gov/FDA-2005-S-0518-0054/attachment_83.pdf
Coverage: Original printed pages 1041–1045, factual extract
The comparator was four 1-g corn-oil capsules per day in the crossover. Mean within-subject fish/corn differences in seated blood pressure were 3.1 ± 1.0 mmHg systolic and 1.8 ± 0.6 mmHg diastolic, with p<.01 as reported. A randomized no-oil phase and orthostatic symptoms were outside the design.
