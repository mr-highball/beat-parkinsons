Title: Clinical and demographic data in 75 patients with near-fatal choking episodes.
PMID: 1424833
DOI: 10.1007/BF02493471
First published: 1992
Authors: Ekberg O, Feinberg M
Journal: Dysphagia
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1424833/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Observational case series of n=75 patients surviving near-fatal choking episodes (30 with neurologic disease including stroke, Parkinson disease, or dementia). 60 choked on solid boluses and 4 choked on mashed banana. Descriptive choking case series without dietary comparator or incidence denominator.
