Van Hilten and colleagues used wrist activity monitoring in 65 people with idiopathic Parkinson disease and 68 healthy controls. The methods on p. 875 describe exploratory grouping by resting tremor and other motor or sleep variables. These did not change the pattern presented. The investigators found no consistent diurnal fatigue pattern, and the most severely affected groups showed flattened daily motor activity.

Rest tremor was a classification variable, and the measured outcomes were general activity and movement indices. The study provides no assigned raw-fig exposure or separate fig effect on rest tremor. All original pages 874–877 were inspected.
