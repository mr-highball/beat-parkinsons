Acute and repeated beta-myrcene differ in a pentobarbital assay
1993-05 (issue date)
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8257941/

Freitas and colleagues compared isolated oral beta-myrcene exposure before a pentobarbital challenge in rats. The author abstract reports 131 versus 64 minutes of drug-induced sleep after one 1 g/kg dose and 21 versus 35 minutes when the barbiturate was given 24 hours after the last of 14 daily doses. Exposure timing differs, and the authors interpret this in the context of barbiturate metabolism. The original group sizes and full tables remain open. The outcome cannot identify an effect on spontaneous insomnia after eating lemongrass, mango or basil.
