Rigidity and tremor in two levodopa-responsive parkinsonism cases
Publication: November 1991, Journal of Neurology, Neurosurgery and Psychiatry 54(11), 972–975
Authors: S. E. Daniel and A. J. Lees
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.54.11.972
PMID: 1800669
PMCID: PMC1014618
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1014618/?page=4
Coverage: Four original journal page scans and NLM title, authors and article identifiers

Two nondemented people with levodopa-responsive parkinsonism were described clinically and at autopsy. One case had bilateral cogwheel rigidity. The other was initially reported to have a leg tremor and later had mild rigidity without observed tremor at the described examination. The report does not specify a resting tremor scale or measured falls. Autopsy showed cortical and striatal plaques, some tangles, and substantia nigra neuronal loss without Lewy bodies. These two descriptions contain no assigned dietary exposure. PMID 1619437 and PMC1014933 refer to separate same-title correspondence, not the 1991 case report.
