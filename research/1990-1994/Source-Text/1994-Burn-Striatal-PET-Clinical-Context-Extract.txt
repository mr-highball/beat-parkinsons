The diagnostic PET study compares 28 people with Parkinson disease, 25 with multiple system atrophy, 10 with progressive supranuclear palsy and 27 controls. The analysis uses striatal uptake to classify diagnostic categories. The Methods mention a light breakfast without specifying ingredients. Reference 15 lists Sage as an author. Axial rigidity is a descriptive finding in the progressive supranuclear palsy group, while apathy appears in background discussion. The article compares diagnostic categories and gives no food-related symptom estimate.
