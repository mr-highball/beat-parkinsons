Title: Can guava fruit intake decrease blood pressure and blood lipids?
PMID: 8383769
First published: 1993
Authors: Singh RB, Rastogi SS, Singh NK, Ghosh S, Gupta S, Niaz MA
Journal: Journal of Human Hypertension
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8383769/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Four-week single-blind RCT in 145 patients with essential hypertension (72 assigned 0.5-1.0 kg/d guava fruit vs 73 usual diet) reporting net BP drop of 7.5/8.5 mmHg and lipid reductions. CRITICAL INTEGRITY & RELIABILITY CAVEAT: Companion Moradabad dietary trial by Ram B. Singh from the exact 1992-1993 publication series subject to serious suspected data fabrication concerns documented in BMJ 2005 (White PMID:16052022; Al-Marzouki PMID:16052019) and Lancet 2005 (PMID:16054927). Also lacks orthostatic standing BP or lightheadedness outcomes.
