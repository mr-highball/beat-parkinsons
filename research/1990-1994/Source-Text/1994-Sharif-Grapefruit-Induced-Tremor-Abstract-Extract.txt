Title: Effect of grapefruit juice on drug metabolism in rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7813990/
PMID: 7813990
DOI: 10.1016/0278-6915(94)90134-1
Publication: December 1994
Depth: Factual summary of the author abstract.

Sharif and Ali report signs after a pharmacological challenge in rats. The signs, including tremor, began within about two to three minutes with saline and at about seven minutes after grapefruit juice or a separate cimetidine comparison. The authors describe reduced severity for the cluster of signs. The abstract does not give a separate severity estimate or uncertainty for tremor. The experiment used juice and induced signs, with no raw-fruit arm or human Parkinson rest-tremor observation.
