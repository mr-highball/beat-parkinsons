Pinnock CB, Arney WK. Appetite 1993;20:61–70, PMID 8452378, DOI 10.1006/appe.1993.1006. Primary abstract: randomized double-blind 300 mL flavored UHT cow milk or matched UHT soy beverage. Coating, urge to swallow and thick-feeling saliva increased in both arms; no speaker intelligibility or Parkinson outcome. The 169-person interview and 185-person matching pretest counts are not necessarily trial arm sample sizes. Publisher full report requires access. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8452378/.
