The article studies movement abnormalities in a mouse model. Its introduction separately recounts a previously published human case of acute illness with visual hallucinations and parkinsonian features, attributed to Richter and colleagues. The human observation is a citation in the background. It adds no independently observed Parkinson cases, food exposure or visual episode rate to the animal paper.
