Title: Clinicians and the coronial system: ability of clinicians to recognise reportable deaths
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1677018/
PMID: 8490498
PMCID: PMC1677018
DOI: 10.1136/bmj.306.6884.1038
Publication date: 1993-04-24
Citation: Start RD, Delargy-Aziz Y, Dorries CP, Silcocks PB, Cotton DWK. BMJ 306(6884), 1038–1041.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 1038–1041.

The study tested recognition of reportable deaths using 16 fictitious questionnaire scenarios. Falls and bodily symptoms occur in example scenarios. The counts describe clinician decisions and do not measure participant falls or pain. There was no basil or dried-date exposure.
