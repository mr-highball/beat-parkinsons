Title: Withdrawal effects of metoclopramide
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1002885/
First published: 1991-06
PMID: 1877215
PMCID: PMC1002885
Text coverage: original page scans 726–728. The preceding multiple-sclerosis report ends above this case and the urinary sphincter report begins after its references.

The authors describe secondary parkinsonian rigidity and tremor-like symptoms in one person during long-term metoclopramide treatment and after withdrawal. She recorded intervals of rigidity or slowness alternating with restlessness and involuntary movements. The authors describe a slight tremor and the absence of two classic Parkinson disease signs in their case. They report partial recovery with multiple simultaneous treatments and time. The term “cold turkey” on page 727 refers to drug withdrawal. The case contains no culinary turkey exposure or comparison.
