Title: Subjective tiredness after different caffeine abstinence periods
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22282364/
PMID: 22282364
DOI: 10.1177/026988119100500206
Publication date: 1991
Date basis: NLM issue year.
Review depth: Original author abstract. Full tiredness contrasts and timing tables remain open.

Two groups of volunteers had avoided caffeine for 24 hours or seven days, respectively nine and twelve people. They received anhydrous caffeine or placebo and underwent repeat testing at one, three and five hours. The abstract identifies visual analogue tiredness as a sensitive indicator. It gives an alertness effect only after the shorter restriction, and does not give the treatment estimate or uncertainty for tiredness itself.

The study addresses the difference between a short withdrawal window and longer abstinence. Tiredness, alertness and clinical fatigue are separate measurements. The abstract supplies no effect of a prepared beverage or Parkinson outcome.
