Title: Soybean-oil meal and gastric emptying in healthy and duodenal-ulcer participants
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7939393/
PMID: 7939393
DOI: 10.3109/00365529409092476
Publication date: 1994-07
Date basis: First journal issue publication. Later publisher online archive date is 2009-07-08.
Coverage: Original author abstract and journal citation. Full meal methods and outcome table remain unavailable.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

Konturek and colleagues compared a standard meal with and without added soybean oil in 10 healthy participants and 10 people with duodenal ulcers. The author abstract lists unmodified meal gastric-emptying half-times of 44 and 34 minutes and reports that added oil lengthened emptying by about 50 percent. The abstract leaves oil processing and exact full meal details unresolved.

The separately published Nagashima study PMID26810548 tested ground soybeans in an acute levodopa crossover in Parkinson disease. Its outcomes and exposure belong to a different study. The 1994 oil experiment gives no levodopa response or daily OFF-time comparison.
