Title: Evidence for the role of spinal cord systems in Parkinson's disease-associated pain
PMID: 2328508
DOI: 10.1097/00002826-199004000-00006
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2328508/
Publication: 1990-04
Coverage: Original author abstract. The complete case report and pain or dystonia measurements remain unavailable.

The authors describe one Parkinson disease case with levodopa-linked leg pain in beginning-of-dose and peak-dose periods. Pain persisted after two types of regional block and resolved during spinal anesthesia in the case as reported. The authors proposed a spinal dopaminergic contribution. This was an anesthesia and medication-timing observation rather than a comparison of sage intake. Sage is an author surname. The indexed dystonia terms do not establish a distinct dystonia severity measurement.
