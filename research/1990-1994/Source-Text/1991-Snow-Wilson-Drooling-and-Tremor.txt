Drooling and tremor phenotypes in Wilson disease cases
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/21255336_The_nigrostriatal_dopaminergic_pathway_in_Wilson%27s_disease_studied_with_positron_emission_tomography

January 1991 issue. The full original journal rendering on pages 12–17 describes five Wilson disease patients. Cases 2 and 4 have drooling in their clinical histories. Patient 5 has resting tremor of the right hand on examination. Patients 3 and 4 have intention tremor at the stated examinations. The quantitative comparison is PET tracer uptake. No rating-based drooling or rest-tremor contrast or raw fig exposure is reported.
