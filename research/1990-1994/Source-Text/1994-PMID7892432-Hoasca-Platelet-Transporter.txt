Title: Platelet serotonin uptake sites increased in drinkers of ayahuasca
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7892432/
Primary PDF rendering: https://bibliography.maps.org/resources/download/12198
PMID: 7892432
DOI: 10.1007/BF02245347
Publication date: 1994-11
Date basis: Original journal issue
Source depth: Full three-page primary paper at pages 385–387
Format: Factual digest of the original material

In a cross-sectional comparison of 13 long-term ceremonial beverage users and 10 controls, the investigators measured citalopram binding to platelet serotonin transporter sites. Maximum binding was higher in the long-term user group, with no clear difference in binding affinity. The comparison measures a peripheral marker. It does not establish a brain serotonin concentration or a causal clinical effect.

This paper is linked to the broader Hoasca research program. The 1996 psychological and 1999 pharmacokinetic reports use different sample counts and measured outcomes. Exact participant overlap with the 13-person laboratory comparison cannot be assumed from shared investigators and community, and independence also cannot be assumed. No Parkinson symptom or dried fig test was performed.
