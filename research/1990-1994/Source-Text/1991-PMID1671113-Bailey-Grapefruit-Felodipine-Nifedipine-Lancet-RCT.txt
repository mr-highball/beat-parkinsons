Title: Interaction of citrus juices with felodipine and nifedipine.
PMID: 1671113
DOI: 10.1016/0140-6736(91)90872-m
First published: 1991
Authors: Bailey DG, Spence JD, Munoz C, Arnold JM
Journal: Lancet (London, England)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1671113/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Landmark randomized crossover trial in 6 men with borderline hypertension (felodipine 5 mg with water, grapefruit juice, or orange juice) and 6 healthy men (nifedipine 10 mg with water or grapefruit juice). Grapefruit juice increased mean felodipine bioavailability to 284% (range 164-469%) of water, lowered diastolic blood pressure, elevated heart rate, and increased vasodilatation-related side effects (lightheadedness/flushing/headache); orange juice had no effect. Nifedipine bioavailability increased to 134% (108-169%). Directly bridged to unprocessed raw grapefruit segments by Bailey 2000 (PMID:11103749) and fresh pulp by Ohtani 2002 (PMID:12040751).
