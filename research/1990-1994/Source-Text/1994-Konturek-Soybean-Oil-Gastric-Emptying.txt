Soybean oil in a meal and gastric emptying
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7939393/
Original abstract paraphrase

Published July 1994. In ten healthy young adults and ten people with duodenal ulcers, the original abstract reports a 500 mL standard meal with or without added 15% soybean oil. Gastric half-emptying times were 44 and 34 minutes for the populations before oil. Adding oil prolonged emptying by about 50%. Separate tests included placebo or loxiglumide. Arm-specific emptying times, sequence, refining and Parkinson medication were not reported in the accessible abstract. The study measures gastric physiology, without a clinical OFF outcome.
