Original source scope and evidence boundary
Twenty-four healthy young men were randomized to blinded caffeine or placebo. Caffeine increased daytime sleep latency and auditory vigilance. The authors note attenuation of the sleep latency effect over repeated administrations. Their abstract describes physiological alertness, divided attention and auditory vigilance. It supplies no standalone patient-reported fatigue scale. The 2004 chapter cites the trial as evidence about tiredness and arousal. The original endpoint should stay distinct from subjective fatigue and Parkinson symptom severity.
