Title: Improvement of parkinsonian features correlate with high plasma levodopa values after broad bean (Vicia faba) consumption
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC489215/
Publisher PDF: https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/55/8/725.full.pdf
PMID: 1527547
PMCID: PMC489215
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.55.8.725
Publication date: 1992-08
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Publisher PDF, original printed pages 725-727. Factual extract.

Six people with Parkinson disease and five healthy volunteers received cooked broad beans. The Parkinson group also received a medication comparison on a later visit. Overall Webster motor scores and separately scored dyskinesia were reported. In the bean condition, the motor total fell from 140 at baseline to 95 after 90 minutes. The three Parkinson participants with baseline medication-associated dyskinesia had severe dyskinesia after the beans. Table 3 shows the same cumulative peak score of 12 for the three affected participants after beans and after the medication comparison, with different time courses. Plasma levodopa rose after the beans. There was no oat exposure or no-food comparator.
