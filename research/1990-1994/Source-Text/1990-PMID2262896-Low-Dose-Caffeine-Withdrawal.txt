Title: Blinded substitution after low-dose caffeine maintenance
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2262896/
PMID: 2262896
Publication date: 1990-12
Date basis: NLM issue metadata.
Review depth: Original author abstract. Full outcome tables remain open.

Seven volunteers received caffeine maintenance and blinded placebo substitutions. Four showed an orderly syndrome over the 12-day substitution phase, with reported peaks on the first or second day. Each showed an effect in a later sequence of repeated one-day substitutions. Which symptoms occurred varied. Fatigue was in the reported symptom range, with no separate fatigue denominator or estimate in the abstract.

The study concerns withdrawal after a standardized capsule exposure. Its result cannot identify an effect of brewed coffee or green tea on Parkinson fatigue. Participant overlap with other early papers from the same research group remains unverified.
