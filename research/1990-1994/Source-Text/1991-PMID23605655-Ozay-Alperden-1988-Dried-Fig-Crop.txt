Title: Aflatoxin and ochratoxin contamination of dried figs from the 1988 crop
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23605655/
Publisher record: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF03192171
PMID: 23605655
DOI: 10.1007/BF03192171
Publication date: 1991-09
Date basis: September 1991 issue of Mycotoxin Research 7(2), pages 85–91. The sampled crop dates to 1988.
Text coverage: Primary author abstract and publisher bibliographic record. Full methods and stage-specific tables remain open.

Özay and Alperden report 103 fig samples collected across orchards and stages of processing. The abstract reports aflatoxins in 29% and ochratoxin A in 3% of the samples overall. These figures combine processing stages. The sample design also measured moisture and water activity, with the abstract describing conditions associated with firm-ripe and shrivelled fruit. The abstract does not give a finished retail product denominator or a clinical endpoint.
