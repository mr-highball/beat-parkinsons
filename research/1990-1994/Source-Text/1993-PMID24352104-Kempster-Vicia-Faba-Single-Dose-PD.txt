Title: Motor effects of broad beans (Vicia faba) in Parkinson's disease: single dose studies.
PMID: 24352104
First published: 1993
Authors: Kempster PA, Bogetic Z, Secombei JW, Martin HD, Balazs ND, Wahlqvist ML
Journal: Asia Pacific journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24352104/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract:
Broad beans (Vicia faba) are a natural source of L-dopa. To investigate a possible role for this substance in the treatment of Parkinsonian motor oscillations, we carried out single dose studies of Vicia faba pod mixture plus carbidopa in six patients. Motor responses of equivalent magnitude to those of conventional L-dopa medication occurred in five cases with mean onset of 39 min and mean duration of 104 min. Vicia faba meals produced comparable L-dopa blood levels to fasting standard tablet doses and recovery studies yielded 0.25% L-dopa per weight of bean pod mixture. Vicia faba contains sufficient L-dopa to be pharmacologically active in patients with Parkinson's disease and can potentially be incorporated into dietary strategies to manage Parkinsonian motor oscillations.

Verified Round 4 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Single-dose open-label clinical feeding study in 6 PD patients with moderate-to-severe motor fluctuations (Asia Pac J Clin Nutr 1993;2:85-89). Patients fasted overnight and consumed 100-200 g microwaved, frozen, blended broad-bean pods (separated from seeds; assayed 0.25% L-DOPA by weight) plus 0-50 mg carbidopa (5/6 received carbidopa), compared on another morning with their usual levodopa/DCI tablet. Five of six had an unequivocal motor response: mean modified Webster improvement 15 points (pods) vs 16 points (tablet); mean onset 39 min, mean duration 104 min (one serial case lasted 285 min vs 75 min tablet). Hand tapping, timed rise-and-walk, tremor, and dyskinesia were recorded; dyskinesia pattern and severity resembled conventional levodopa. Limitations: pod-only mixture rather than mature boiled beans, carbidopa coadministration, n=6, open-label single dose, no placebo control.
