Title: Single photon emission computed tomographic imaging demonstrates loss of striatal dopamine transporters in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8265656/
PMID: 8265656
PMCID: PMC48106
DOI: 10.1073/pnas.90.24.11965
Publication date: 1993-12-15
Coverage: Factual extract from the author abstract. The historical article body is open.

In a comparison with healthy controls, Parkinson participants showed reduced striatal radiotracer uptake and earlier peak uptake. The authors' kinetic model estimated approximately 65% lower transporter target availability, with more involvement in the putamen and on the side opposite initial symptoms. The estimate concerns transporter imaging. The abstract gives no exposure comparison for raw figs or grilled salmon and no corresponding separately assessed symptom effect.
