Title: Single photon emission computed tomographic imaging demonstrates loss of striatal dopamine transporters in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC48106/
PMID: 8265656
PMCID: PMC48106
DOI: 10.1073/pnas.90.24.11965
Publication date: 1993-12-15
Citation: Innis RB et al. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences USA 90(24), 11965–11969.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 11965–11969.

The original imaging comparison included five participants with idiopathic Parkinson disease and five matched healthy participants. Rest tremor and bradykinesia are clinical ascertainment features. The reported differences concern radioligand uptake and inferred transporter target sites, with putamen and caudate distinguished. Salmon appears as a surname in a cited imaging study. The paper does not report a symptom response to salmon or fig consumption.
