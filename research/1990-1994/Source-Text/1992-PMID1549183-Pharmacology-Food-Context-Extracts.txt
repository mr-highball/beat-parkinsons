The archived 129-page German pharmacology and toxicology meeting PDF contains food-derived compounds, oil carriers and organ preparations. PDF page 17, abstract 65, studies oral eugenol and deuterated eugenol metabolism and identifies clove oil as a source of the compound. On PDF page 23, Vahlensieck and colleagues studied phenolic acids including rosmarinic and caffeic acid in an isolated perfused rat liver. They mention Melissa officinalis as a botanical source. Neither isolated compound can be equated with brewed leaf tea. Corn oil appears in rat lipid or toxicant comparisons, peanut oil is a comparator vehicle in an arsenic model, and sesame oil is a subcutaneous vehicle in rat neurochemistry work. An olive-oil passage uses it alongside other lipids. These meeting extracts supply route and toxicology context rather than human Parkinson symptom results.
