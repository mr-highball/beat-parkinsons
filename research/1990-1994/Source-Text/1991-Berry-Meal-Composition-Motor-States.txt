1991 — Meal composition, dystonia on a motor continuum and reported dyskinesia
Source: https://web.mit.edu/dick/www/pdf/816.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of the original author archive

Nine men on carbidopa and levodopa received three equal-calorie breakfasts in randomized order. A composite scale included dystonic and hyperkinetic states. After the higher-carbohydrate breakfast, three participants had dyskinesia or restlessness. One had dyskinesia after the balanced meal. The original results do not identify chives or separately quantify dystonia.
