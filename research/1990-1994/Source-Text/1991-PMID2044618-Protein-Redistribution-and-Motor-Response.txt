Title: Protein redistribution diet and antiparkinsonian response to levodopa
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2044618/
PMID: 2044618
DOI: 10.1159/000116649
Citation: Bracco F, Malesani R, Saladini M, Battistin L. European Neurology. 1991. 31(2):68–71.
Coverage: Author abstract from PubMed. Local factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

Sixteen people with Parkinson disease and fluctuating daily response to levodopa followed a regimen with protein concentrated at the evening meal and afterward. The authors identified a subgroup of five with a clear reduction in daytime motor performance fluctuations and described a general tendency toward a steadier response.

The available abstract gives no randomized or blinded comparison, minutes of OFF time, separate individual-symptom contrasts or named ingredient assignment. The five-year bucket follows the publication year of the original report.
