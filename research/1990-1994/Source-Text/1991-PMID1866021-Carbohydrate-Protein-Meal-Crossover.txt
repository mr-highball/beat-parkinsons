Title: A balanced carbohydrate: protein diet in the management of Parkinson's disease
Publication date: 1991-08
PMID: 1866021
DOI: 10.1212/wnl.41.8.1295
Citation: Berry EM, Growdon JH, Wurtman JJ, Caballero B, Wurtman RJ. Neurology 41:1295–1297.
Source: https://web.mit.edu/dick/www/pdf/816.pdf
Coverage: Author-hosted reprint, Methods, clinical assessment, motor-performance paragraph and Tables 1–2.

Nine men with Parkinson disease received three nearly isocaloric breakfasts in random order on separate days with their usual medication. Meals contained 80, 6 or 20 g of protein and differed in carbohydrate and fat. All ate with carbidopa/levodopa, with four also taking an earlier dose. After the high-protein breakfast five reported worsening of Parkinson symptoms. Three of those five also had dyskinesia or restlessness after the high-carbohydrate breakfast. The balanced breakfast showed continued improvement over two hours on the Purdue Pegboard, while performance after the other breakfasts peaked earlier. Diet-by-time interaction p=.028. The qualitative symptom scale includes trembling in a low-motor-state category. Separate resting-tremor values or the precise culinary ingredients were not provided.
