Review depth: Indexed original abstract and linked cohort methods

The investigators report diet survey results from 38,121 Iowa women and a semiquantitative questionnaire tested previously among Boston-area participants. The published abstract compares nutrient intakes and does not supply the Iowa study's item-by-item fruit list. The later Iowa cohort methods identify the baseline as a 127-item adaptation from the Nurses' Health Study. The publicly archived 1986 Nurses' Health form names raisins or grapes and prunes, without a separate date-fruit fruit-row in the displayed list. That form cannot verify exactly which items the Iowa adaptation used.
