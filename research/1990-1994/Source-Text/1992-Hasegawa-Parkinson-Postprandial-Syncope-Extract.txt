Title: Parkinson's disease with syncope as a chief complaint induced by prominent postprandial hypotension
Authors: Y Hasegawa and S Okamoto
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1300258/
Date: September 1992, Rinsho Shinkeigaku 32(9):1017–1021
Coverage: factual extract of the primary English abstract indexed for the Japanese article

A 77-year-old man had frequent syncope after meals and an 18-year history of anti-Parkinson treatment. Blood pressure was measured every 20 minutes for one day; the clinicians observed post-meal pressure reductions accompanying episodes. Separate intake of 50 g of glucose in solution induced prolonged hypotension while resting supine. The English abstract does not describe individual meal ingredients except for the glucose challenge, a comparison of cilantro, or a rating of standing-induced lightheadedness. The Japanese full body was unavailable through the searched public routes.
