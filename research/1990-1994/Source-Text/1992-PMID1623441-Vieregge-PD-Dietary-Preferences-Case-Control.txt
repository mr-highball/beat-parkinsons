Title: Life-style and dietary factors early and late in Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 1623441
First published: 1992
Authors: Vieregge, von Maravic, Friedrich
Journal: The Canadian journal of neurological sciences. Le journal canadien des sciences neurologiques
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1623441/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Structured interview case-control study of 71 idiopathic Parkinson's disease patients and 103 controls examining early- and late-life lifestyle and diet. Actual current food preference was greater for almonds and plums in PD patients vs controls, while premorbid vitamin E reduction was not observed in overt PD. Limits: cross-sectional preference survey; no symptom severity contrast or preparation detail.
