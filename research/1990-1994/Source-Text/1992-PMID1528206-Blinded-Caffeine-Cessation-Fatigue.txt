Title: Abnormally high fatigue ratings during blinded caffeine cessation
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1528206/
PMID: 1528206
DOI: 10.1056/NEJM199210153271601
Publication date: 1992-10-15
Date basis: Original issue date.
Review depth: Original author abstract and identifiable publisher methods and results excerpts. Full tables were not recovered.

Sixty-two adults with low or moderate dietary intake completed usual-intake assessments and two blinded two-day periods with caffeine-free diets. Capsules replaced each participant's usual caffeine in one period, while placebo was given in the other. The abstract reports abnormally high Profile of Mood States fatigue ratings in 8% during placebo and 0% during usual intake or replacement. It reports p<.05 for the placebo contrasts. Other withdrawal outcomes include headache and lower vigor.

The reported endpoint is the proportion with abnormally high fatigue. It is not an average change in fatigue severity. The short abrupt-cessation and capsule-replacement design supplies a conditional withdrawal comparison in non-Parkinson volunteers, with the named beverage, preparation and disease-specific endpoint untested.
