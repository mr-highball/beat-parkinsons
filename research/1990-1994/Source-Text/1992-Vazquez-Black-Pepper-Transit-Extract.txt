Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1578101/
PMID 1578101 · DOI 10.1080/07315724.1992.12098248
Checked: 2026-11-15
Coverage: Factual extract.

In a pilot in 16 healthy volunteers, black pepper capsule comparison yielded 90 ± 51 minutes at baseline and 122 ± 88 after powdered black pepper (p=.09) on an orocecal lactulose breath test. Red and black pepper were separate conditions. Source reports no clinical constipation endpoint.
