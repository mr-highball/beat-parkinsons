Title: Effects of increasing dietary palmitoleic acid compared with palmitic and oleic acids on plasma lipids of hypercholesterolemic men
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8006520/
PMID: 8006520
DOI: 10.1016/S0022-2275(20)41179-4
First public article date: 1994-04
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated original author abstract

Nestel, Clifton and Noakes compared three three-week diets in
random order in 34 hypercholesterolemic men. Macadamia oil
was the palmitoleic source. Total and LDL cholesterol under
palmitoleic and palmitic diets were similar and higher than
under the oleic diet. The abstract does not give an oil-free
comparison, processing method, individual adverse events,
Parkinson cohort or separate resting-tremor outcome.

The official full-text and PDF routes were inaccessible in
