Corn oil comparator in intermittent claudication
Primary source: https://research.rug.nl/nl/publications/fish-oil-supplementation-in-patients-with-stable-claudication/
PMID: 2240382
DOI: 10.1016/S0002-9610(05)81012-8
Coverage: Factual extract of original abstract and metadata.

Gans et al. randomized 32 people with stable claudication to fish-oil or oral corn-oil capsules for four months. The original abstract reports no clear change in walking distances. It provides no stride length, oil-free comparison, refining grade or Parkinson cohort. The institution lists its article body under embargo.
