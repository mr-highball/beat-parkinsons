The hospital series includes 49 admitted esophageal foreign-body cases from 1980 to September 1992. The authors record one adult shiitake case. Swallowing difficulty began during dinner and persisted. Imaging initially suggested an esophageal tumor. Direct endoscopy identified a shiitake impaction in the lower esophagus. The preparation and size of the portion are unreported. A Parkinson diagnosis and a comparative rate among people who ate shiitake are unavailable.

This is an observed acute mechanical event. It does not supply a comparison of oropharyngeal Parkinson swallowing severity after roasted shiitake.
