Title: Depression followed by dementia and disordered movement. Clinicopathologic correlation
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1022389/
PMID: 8191757
PMCID: PMC1022389
Publication date: 1994-03
Citation: Harris CP, Townsend JJ, Adair JC. Western Journal of Medicine 160(3), 243–247.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 243–247.

A clinicopathological conference described progressive cognitive decline, depression, anxiety attacks, and evolving tremor and motor slowing. The clinical discussion distinguishes drug-related motor features from features that persisted, with an ultimate diffuse Lewy body disease interpretation. Anxiety was described in the case history, and there was no standardized anxiety or bradykinesia outcome linked to food. The report does not examine poached or grilled salmon.
