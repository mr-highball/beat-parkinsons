# Parkinson meal-related syncope

## Recurrent syncope and a supine glucose challenge

In September 1992, Hasegawa and Okamoto described a 77-year-old man with idiopathic Parkinson disease and syncope after meals that began at age 75. Ambulatory monitoring with readings every 20 minutes over one day linked the recurrent episodes with an after-meal pressure fall. A separate 50 g oral glucose solution produced a marked, prolonged pressure drop during supine rest. The abstract does not identify foods behind the spontaneous episodes, provide a standing lightheadedness score or attribute the response to any seasoning. The original Japanese article body was unavailable through the sources checked.
