# Facial expression, falls, voice and related food outcomes

## Mustard and Tabasco at an evening meal

Edwards et al. studied six healthy men with and without Tabasco sauce and mustard in the evening meal. They reported less slow-wave and stage 2 sleep and more wake time in the spicy condition. The original formulation does not assign black pepper. This is neighboring dietary evidence for spice timing and sleep, with no ingredient-specific comparison or Parkinson cohort.
