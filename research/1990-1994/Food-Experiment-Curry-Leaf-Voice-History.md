# The 1994 curry-leaf dysphonia citation

## Book identity and original page access

The IMPPAT dysphonia row for *Murraya koenigii* cites ISBN 81-204-0828-4. The Indian Institute of Spices Research and Wellcome catalog both identify it as *Ayurvedic Drugs and Their Plant Sources* by V. V. Sivarajan and Indira Balachandran, published in New Delhi in 1994. The exact curry-leaf text and its page image remain unverified. Two papers on *Murraya koenigii* cite p. 199 for background, so it is a retrieval lead to check against the original index and adjacent pages. [IISR printed holding and ISBN](https://www.spices.res.in/opac/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=2724) · [Wellcome printed holding and ISBN](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/c3fu4zdq) · [Dash and colleagues' page citation](https://www.aryavaidyanjournal.org/uploads/articlepdf/JP_711683016929.pdf)

The later Kerala compilation cites this book in the paragraph on Kaidaryah and voice. That source does not independently establish the original wording. Jayaweera's distinct 1982 Sri Lanka account describes boiled curry leaves.
