# Food experiment choking evidence

## Mashed potato and pharyngeal pressure in healthy adults

Shaker and colleagues studied 14 younger and 12 older healthy volunteers. Mashed potato increased hypopharyngeal peristaltic pressure amplitude and duration compared with water (p < .05). The study measured swallowing mechanics and gives no Parkinson outcome. The same research team later used mashed potato during testing in a Parkinson cohort. [Primary 1993 abstract, PMID 8460698](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8460698/) · [1998 Parkinson study](../1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#mashed-potato-in-a-wisconsin-parkinson-manometry-study)

## Liquid and solid food videofluoroscopy in London

Stroudley and Walsh’s October 1991 report describes videofluoroscopy in 24 people with Parkinson’s and dysphagia. The abstract reports abnormal oral, pharyngeal, and esophageal swallowing in 92%, 54%, and 8% of the sample, respectively, and tracheal aspiration in 46%. Abnormalities were more marked for liquids than for semisolids or solids. The publisher provides an abstract with broad texture classes. The full methods and tables are needed to identify the solid foods and establish any food-specific findings. [Primary abstract, PMID 1954529](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1954529/) · [Publisher abstract and PDF availability](https://academic.oup.com/bjr/article-abstract/64/766/890/7454769)
