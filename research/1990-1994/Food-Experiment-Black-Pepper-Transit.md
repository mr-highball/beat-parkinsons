# Black pepper powder and orocecal transit

## Orocecal transit after black pepper capsules

Vazquez-Olivencia and colleagues reported a pilot in 16 healthy volunteers. Mean lactulose breath-test transit time in the black pepper comparison rose from 90 ± 51 to 122 ± 88 minutes (p = .09). The study used powdered pepper in capsules and an upper-gut transit proxy. The separate red pepper comparison had a clear difference, which does not establish a black pepper effect. No measure of colonic transit, stool frequency, patient-rated constipation or Parkinson disease was reported.
