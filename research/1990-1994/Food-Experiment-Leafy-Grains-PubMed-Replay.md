# Leafy grain search replay evidence, 1990–1994

## Mandrake mixed with spinach and chard leaves in a poisoning series

Two families yielded 15 cases of acute anticholinergic poisoning. The report counted hyperactivity or hallucination together in 14. Investigators found mandrake mixed among spinach and chard leaves and identified atropine and hyoscyamine. This pooled endpoint and a confirmed toxic coexposure explain why the report gives no estimate of visual hallucination frequency after the named cooked foods in Parkinson’s.
