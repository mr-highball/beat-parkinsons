# Nonmotor and Daily Life for 1990–1994

### 1990 — Cognitive performance and age at onset

Dubois and colleagues compared younger- and older-onset Parkinson’s groups with age-matched controls. Older-onset patients showed broader cognitive differences, consistent with interacting effects of aging and disease. The comparison used groups defined by onset age and did not follow conversion to dementia. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2296380/)

### 1991 — Objective measurements of colonic transit

Jost and Schimrigk measured colonic transit in 20 patients and 20 age- and sex-matched controls. Transit was prolonged in 16 patients and two controls. The small study supported a physiological correlate of constipation without determining when it first developed. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1795497/)

### 1992 — Population-based Parkinson’s dementia study

Mayeux and colleagues combined a community registry and neurological review in a New York neighborhood. Parkinson’s disease with dementia was more frequent at older ages, and later motor onset distinguished the dementia group. The study measured prevalence in one population. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1580811/)
