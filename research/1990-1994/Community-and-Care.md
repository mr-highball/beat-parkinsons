# Community and Care for 1990–1994

### 1991 — Living with Parkinson’s disease in participants’ own words

Marr interviewed six people in their homes. Participants described the effort required to maintain independence and ordinary activities, shifts in self-concept, and the importance of social and health support. The qualitative study captures experiences rather than their prevalence. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1835998/)

### 1992 — Patient and caregiver priorities for coordinated care

A multidisciplinary panel discussed a video account from people with Parkinson’s disease and caregivers. Priorities included diagnosis counseling, access to rehabilitation and specialist advice, community support, driving, and sexual health. This report documents patient-informed priorities without testing a service model. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1564503/)
