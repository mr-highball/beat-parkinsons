# Food and Nutrition for 1990–1994

### 1990 — Follow-up survey of vitamin E-rich foods and early-life exposures

Golbe and colleagues surveyed 106 people with Parkinson’s disease and their spouses. Some comparisons reproduced inverse associations with recalled intake of vitamin E-rich foods, including peanuts or peanut butter and salad dressing. Cases also recalled more rural experience and pesticide application. Recall, choice of spouse controls, and multiple comparisons limited interpretation. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2296261/)

### 1990 — Ferrous sulfate and carbidopa–levodopa in patients

Campbell and colleagues measured drug exposure in people with Parkinson’s disease when carbidopa–levodopa was taken with ferrous sulfate. Levodopa and carbidopa exposure fell. The study observed an association between reduced exposure and reduced efficacy across individuals, with uncertainty about the average clinical effect and repeated use. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2291872/)

### 1993 — DATATOP follow-up on supplemental vitamin E and selegiline

In the 800-participant randomized trial, supplemental alpha-tocopherol failed to delay disability prompting levodopa. Selegiline delayed the endpoint, with clinical differences evident early and deterioration on withdrawal. The findings supported a symptomatic contribution and left neuroprotection unresolved. The vitamin E result applies to the trial supplement and endpoint. [Original publication](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJM199301213280305)

### 1993 — Liquid meal challenge and head-up tilt

Thomaides and colleagues tested the response to a balanced liquid meal. Supine blood pressure fell after the meal in idiopathic Parkinson’s disease. The meal did not elicit a fall on head-up tilt in the Parkinson group, while the multiple system atrophy and pure autonomic failure groups showed lower pressures on post-meal tilt. In a separate study, the investigators of a 75-g glucose challenge observed postprandial blood pressure declines in patients without reported postprandial symptoms. Neither report assesses brown rice or orthostatic lightheadedness severity. [Liquid meal study, May 1993](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8492945/) · [Glucose challenge, November 1993](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8124870/)

### 1994 — Early-life food recall in Sapporo

Watanabe interviewed 95 cases and 190 matched controls across 218 items. Lower recalled fruit intake in adolescence was among the variables retained in a stepwise model. Other unadjusted food signals included lower meat and dairy intake earlier in life. Retrospective recall and the large number of variables make these findings exploratory. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8111093/)
