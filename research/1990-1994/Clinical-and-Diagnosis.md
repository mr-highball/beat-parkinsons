# Clinical and Diagnosis for 1990–1994

### 1990 — Fluorodopa PET across three parkinsonian syndromes

Brooks and colleagues compared PET patterns in 16 people with levodopa-responsive Parkinson’s disease, 18 with multiple system atrophy, and 10 with progressive supranuclear palsy. The Parkinson’s group showed disproportionate involvement of the posterior putamen. Group patterns from this small selected sample leave individual diagnostic accuracy open. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2132742/)

### 1992 — Autopsy comparison with clinical Parkinson’s diagnoses

Hughes and colleagues examined 100 brains from people diagnosed clinically with idiopathic Parkinson’s disease. Seventy-six had the expected pathology, and retrospective application of recommended diagnostic criteria raised agreement to 82%. Alternative diagnoses included progressive supranuclear palsy, multiple system atrophy, and vascular disease. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1564476/)
