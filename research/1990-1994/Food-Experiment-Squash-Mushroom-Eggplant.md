# Squash, mushroom, eggplant, and zucchini evidence

## 1994 Butternut squash contact dermatitis

The case described hand dermatitis after a 30-year-old woman peeled and cut a butternut squash fruit. The exposure was skin contact during food preparation. The accessible primary record provides no ingestion comparison or voice outcome. [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0536.1994.tb00588.x)
