## Occasional resting tremor in a diffuse Lewy body case

February 1991. The patient in Fearnley and colleagues’ original neuropathological case had occasional left-predominant rest tremor on page 159 and later a history of hallucinations. This clinical description provides no item-level severity change or edible fig comparison. The report gives no food-related pain observation. ## Drooling and tremor phenotypes in Wilson disease cases

January 1991 issue. The full original journal rendering on pages 12–17 describes five Wilson disease patients. Cases 2 and 4 have drooling in their clinical histories. Patient 5 has resting tremor of the right hand on examination. Patients 3 and 4 have intention tremor at the stated examinations. The quantitative comparison is PET tracer uptake. No rating-based drooling or rest-tremor contrast or raw fig exposure is reported.
