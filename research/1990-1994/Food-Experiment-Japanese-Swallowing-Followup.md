# Japanese primary swallowing evidence for the food thought experiment

## Swallowing symptoms fluoroscopy and manometry in Chiba

Kurihara and colleagues published a Parkinson’s series in February 1993. About 30% of 97 participants recalled swallowing difficulty. More than 40% reported difficulty among those with disease duration beyond ten years or disease severity above Hoehn–Yahr stage III. The authors examined nine people with imaging and manometry. Six had radiologic abnormalities, including two with aspiration. The abstract leaves the exact test foods and aspiration outcomes by food open. The original issue is held by Japanese medical libraries listed in CiNii.
