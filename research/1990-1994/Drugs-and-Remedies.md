# Drugs and Remedies for 1990–1994

### 1991 — Repeated apomorphine and motor response duration

Hughes and colleagues studied 15 people with levodopa-related fluctuations after sequential apomorphine challenges. The motor response did not shorten significantly with the repeated exposures tested. The short experiment weighed against rapid changes in receptor sensitivity as an explanation for the fluctuations. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2056323/)

### 1992 — Later controlled-release levodopa in fluctuating disease

Hutton and colleagues reported a double-blind comparison involving 202 people with moderate or advanced disease and fluctuations. This controlled-release formulation had better efficacy on the reported measures and similar tolerability to the standard preparation. The trial addressed patients with established fluctuations. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1549202/)
