# Corn oil and standing symptom evidence

## Corn oil comparator and seated blood pressure

Lungershausen et al. compared six-week fish-oil and corn-oil phases in a blinded randomized crossover with 42 completers receiving treated-hypertension care. The mean fish-oil versus corn-oil seated pressure difference favored fish oil by 3.1 mmHg systolic and 1.8 mmHg diastolic. Seated pressure on corn oil did not show a clear change versus run-in. The paper supplies no standing symptom comparison, corn-oil refining specification or Parkinson cohort.
