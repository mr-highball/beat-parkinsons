# Thyme and clove evidence, 1990–1994

## Acute drowsiness after concentrated clove oil

Hartnoll, Moore and Douek reported in September 1993 that a two-year-old was mildly drowsy on arrival after accidentally ingesting concentrated clove oil and then developed seizures, coma and liver injury. This single acute poisoning case involved neither ordinary ground clove nor measured daytime sleepiness in Parkinson’s. Brown and colleagues described a two-year-old with similar poisoning in 1992. The published abstracts and case descriptions are compatible with the same event, and the two reports are treated as one possible case family.
