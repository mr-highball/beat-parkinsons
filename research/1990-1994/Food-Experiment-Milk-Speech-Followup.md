# Milk and speech follow-up, 1990–1994

## Blinded milk and soy sensory comparison

Pinnock and Arney’s 1993 randomized double-blind trial compared 300 mL of flavored ultra-high-temperature cow’s milk with a matched soy beverage. Self-reported mouth and throat coating, frequent swallowing and thicker-feeling saliva increased after both drinks. The published abstract does not report a measured speech intelligibility outcome or Parkinson sample. UHT processing is described; ordinary pasteurized milk should not be assumed to be the same product. The full article was not accessible in this pass.
