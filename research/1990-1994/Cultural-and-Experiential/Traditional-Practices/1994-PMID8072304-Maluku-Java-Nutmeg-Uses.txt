Vendors of herbal remedies, healers and midwives provided information about Myristica fragrans in Maluku and Central and East Java. The original abstract lists insomnia, diarrhea and mouth sores as uses and mentions a mild sedative action. The abstract supplies no preparation, route, dose, count or severity data for insomnia. It supplies no Parkinson diagnosis or before-and-after sleep comparison. The publisher preview and PubMed establish the dated report. Review of the full pages 117–124 remains open. The report of mild sedation does not resolve the exposures or time courses in later clinical poisoning accounts.
