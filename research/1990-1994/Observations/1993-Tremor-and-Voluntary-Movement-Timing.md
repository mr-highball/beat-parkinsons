# Tremor timing in a voluntary movement study

The 1993 printed study relates voluntary contraction onset to the tremor cycle in participants with Parkinson disease. This extends the preceding period's [instrumented finger-tremor study](../../1985-1989/Observations/1989-Printed-Tremor-Measurement.md), which distinguished clinically obvious rest tremor from transient tremor after movement. The studies answer different measurement questions about tremor around a motor task. The 1993 report supplies no estimate of rest tremor following raw fig consumption.
