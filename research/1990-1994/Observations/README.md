## Major observations

[Macadamia-oil blends and tremor leads](Macadamia-Oil-Blends-and-Tremor.md) compares the recovered 1994 original with subsequent preparation, animal and laboratory comparisons.

- The [DATATOP follow-up](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) found no benefit from supplemental vitamin E for delaying disability requiring levodopa. Selegiline delayed the endpoint, with evidence that symptomatic benefit contributed.
- [Autopsy correlation](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md) identified alternative pathologies among people diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease during life. Applying stricter criteria retrospectively improved agreement in this series. Case definition became important for interpreting exposure studies and treatment samples.
- [Surgical reports](../Procedures-and-Devices.md) described sustained thalamic tremor control, broader motor responses and serious visual complications after lesion surgery, and encouraging but uncontrolled fetal graft findings.

## Minor observations

- The randomized diet comparison confirms macadamia oil as a dietary lipid source. Its author abstract gives lipid results while processing and adverse effects remain open. [Original abstract extract](../Source-Text/1994-PMID8006520-Macadamia-Oil-Lipid-Abstract-Extract.txt)
- The later UK series added food hemagglutinin confirmation to earlier outbreak descriptions. Its years overlap the case report in the 1980–1984 bucket. The study measured acute digestive illness. [Lentil and kidney bean evidence](../Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md)
- [Controlled rehabilitation](../Movement-and-Rehabilitation.md) produced short-term motor and daily activity gains. The gains receded during follow-up without regular exercise.
- [Interviews and patient accounts](../Community-and-Care.md) documented support, independence, driving, and counseling as care priorities.
- [Colonic transit](../Nonmotor-and-Daily-Life.md) gave clinicians an objective measure of constipation. [Food histories](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) generated dietary hypotheses with substantial recall and multiple-comparison limitations.

The 1989 one-year follow-up records early-morning dystonia and time in different motor states during carbidopa–levodopa treatment. The 1994 retrospective series distinguishes dystonic and choreic dyskinesias across medication states in a severe-dyskinesia cohort. The shared author surname Sage explains the search match for both papers. Read the designs separately when comparing their motor findings. ## Comparison with [1985–1989](../../1985-1989/Observations/README.md)

- The earlier interim selegiline report was followed by a more complete factorial trial report. Vitamin E lacked benefit on the measured outcome, and symptom effects complicated the selegiline progression claim.
- The earlier ferrous sulfate study measured levodopa exposure in healthy participants. The follow-up measured lower drug exposure in people with Parkinson’s disease taking carbidopa–levodopa, with uncertainty about long-term clinical effects. The follow-up to the early-life food survey remained retrospective.
- The earlier short reports on stimulation and grafts gained longer follow-up and imaging, while controlled graft evidence was still pending. The postmortem complex I signal was quantified in a matched comparison.
- Agricultural studies moved from regional comparisons toward individual occupational histories, and some associations survived adjustment. Their exposure measures still left agent-specific causality uncertain.

- Concentrated clove oil poisoning produced acute drowsiness and severe toxicity. The similar 1992 report may describe the same child. Earlier periods provide no matched culinary daytime sleepiness comparison in this trail. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Thyme-Clove.md)

- A healthy-adult chewing experiment distinguished saliva secretion after celery from a clinically assessed swallowing outcome. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Romaine-Celery-Stalk-Cabbage.md#1990-celery-mastication-and-parotid-saliva)

- A case identifies butternut squash during peeling as a skin exposure. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md#1994-butternut-squash-contact-dermatitis)

- The 1988 sibling comparison reported lower early-adult plum preference among Parkinson cases. The 1992 clinic comparison found higher current plum preference. The studies cover different life periods and neither measured dyskinesia. [Earlier survey](../../1985-1989/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#early-adult-plum-preference-and-parkinson-case-status) · [Current preference study](../Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#current-plum-preference-and-fish-intake-in-parkinson)

- The developmental rat study found reduced exploration in a novel setting alongside similar spontaneous movement. Earlier Friedreich results also had task-specific differences. Preserve the distinction between spontaneous activity, exploratory behavior and clinical symptom ratings. [1982 trial](../../1980-1984/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Oil-Endpoints.md#melancon-friedreich-ataxia-crossover-clinical-results) · [1991 experiments](../Food-Experiment-Safflower-Oil-Endpoints.md#developmental-safflower-oil-and-exploratory-behavior)
