# A macadamia-rich diet and later tremor leads

The original 1994 randomized dietary paper gives more detail than the author abstract. Volunteers consumed spreads and milk drinks prepared from differing oil formulations. The margarine was partially hydrogenated. Two people reported transient gastrointestinal discomfort during the macadamia-rich period. The fasting lipid measurements and this tolerability observation address different outcomes, and the pressing grade and resting tremor effect remain unresolved. [1994 original and page locators](../Source-Text/1994-PMID8006520-Nestel-Macadamia-Oil-Primary-Extract.txt)

Later work separates whole-kernel acute reactions in dogs, an oil-blend transit study in healthy adults, and tissue changes in a rat chemical-injury experiment. The room-temperature pressing comparison establishes the preparation in a laboratory sample and measures oil properties. These observations retain different evidential roles in the frozen resting-tremor claim.
