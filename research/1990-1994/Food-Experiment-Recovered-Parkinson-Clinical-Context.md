## Striatal PET differential diagnosis and clinical context

The diagnostic PET study compares 28 people with Parkinson disease, 25 with multiple system atrophy, 10 with progressive supranuclear palsy and 27 controls. The analysis uses striatal uptake to classify diagnostic categories. The Methods mention a light breakfast without specifying ingredients. Reference 15 lists Sage as an author. Axial rigidity is a descriptive finding in the progressive supranuclear palsy group, while apathy appears in background discussion. The article compares diagnostic categories and gives no food-related symptom estimate. ## Motor findings in two parkinsonism autopsies

Daniel and Lees describe rigidity in two nondemented people with levodopa-responsive parkinsonism. The second history reports initial leg tremor and a later examination without tremor. The case narratives contain no resting tremor severity or fall-frequency measure. The brain pathology had plaques, tangles and nigral cell loss without Lewy bodies. A same-title later letter has distinct NLM identifiers.
