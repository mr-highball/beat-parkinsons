# Chives, related Allium species and dystonia

## 1991 — Meal composition, dystonia on a motor continuum and reported dyskinesia

Berry and colleagues randomized the order of three equal-calorie breakfasts in nine men receiving carbidopa/levodopa. One meal contained a higher proportion of protein, one a higher proportion of carbohydrate and one a carbohydrate-to-protein ratio of about 5:1. Participants rated a composite motor-state continuum that included dystonic and hyperkinetic states, and an assessor unaware of meal assignment checked for involuntary movements. Three participants experienced dyskinesia or restlessness following the higher-carbohydrate meal and one developed dyskinesia after the balanced meal. Results do not give dystonia-specific counts, changes in severity or a chive ingredient.
