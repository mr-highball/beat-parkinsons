# Procedures and Devices for 1995–1999

### 1997 — U.S. thalamic stimulation approval for tremor

The FDA approved the original Activa Tremor Control System for unilateral thalamic stimulation to suppress disabling upper-extremity tremor in specified Parkinsonian and essential tremor populations. This regulatory record concerns the tremor indication in 1997. [Original source](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P960009)

### 1998 — Subthalamic stimulation for advanced disease

Limousin and colleagues assessed 24 people with bilateral subthalamic stimulation. One-year motor and daily-activity scores improved in the off-medication state among patients followed, and dopaminergic medication use fell. One person had severe neurological injury after an intracerebral hematoma. The study was an uncontrolled surgical series. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9770557/)

### 1999 — Randomized immediate versus delayed pallidotomy

De Bie and colleagues randomized 37 people with advanced disease to immediate or delayed unilateral pallidotomy. Off-state motor scores and on-state dyskinesia favored earlier surgery, and two treated people had major adverse effects. The trial compared treatment timing in a selected surgical population. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10568567/)
