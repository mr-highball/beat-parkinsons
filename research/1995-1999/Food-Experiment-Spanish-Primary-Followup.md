# Spanish-language dietary research follow-up

## 1999 authors’ follow-up on the 1997 Spanish diet survey

Jiménez-Jiménez, Ayuso-Peralta, Molina and Cabrera-Valdivia revisited their [1997 survey of 119 spouse pairs](Food-Experiment-Multilingual-Followup.md#1997-dietary-recall-in-119-spanish-spouse-pairs) in the October 16–31, 1999 issue of *Revista de Neurología*. Page 742 describes peas as the only significant premarital difference among the 91 foods, with lower consumption in people later diagnosed with Parkinson’s. The January 1997 abstract describes the result as a trend. The authors give no exact p value or effect size in the 1999 account. They also explain that the period after marriage ended at clinical onset for younger-onset cases or age 40 otherwise. Three of 11 foods rich in selenium were consumed more often by cases, including two also rich in copper. [Authors’ 1999 account, p. 742](https://storage.imrpress.com/journal/RN/29/8/10.33588/rn.2908.99159/pdf/34cc12da72025682a44e11d51c3a43db.pdf#page=2) · [1999 journal abstract and issue date](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10560110/)

The 1997 publication compares past food intake by diagnosis and provides no measure of symptom change after eating a food. ScienceDirect exposes an abstract and references. The journal full-text routes returned HTTP 403, the author listing offers a copy by request, and OpenAlex lists no open version. The original 1997 tables remain unverified.
