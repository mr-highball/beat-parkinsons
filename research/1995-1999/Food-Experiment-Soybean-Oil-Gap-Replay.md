# Soybean lipid emulsion and clinical dystonia

## Dystonia in a Leigh syndrome intravenous treatment case

Kumagai and colleagues describe a woman with adult Leigh syndrome, partial pyruvate dehydrogenase complex deficiency, episodic dystonia and acute central respiratory failure. The authors report rapid clinical and biochemical improvement after an intravenous ketogenic emulsion. The title and indexing identify soybean oil. The accessible original abstract gives no distinct quantified dystonia response or oil grade. The case concerns emergency intravenous emulsion in a different neurologic disease.
