# Food experiment evidence traced through Spanish-language records

## 1997 dietary recall in 119 Spanish spouse pairs

Ayuso-Peralta and colleagues interviewed 119 people with Parkinson’s and their 119 spouses separately about 91 foods. The premarital survey suggested lower pea consumption among future cases. In the survey covering marriage to age 40, participants with Parkinson’s tended to report more rice, oily fish, liver pâté and eggs and less tea than their spouses. The investigators accepted only concordant answers from spouses for the latter period. These are retrospective dietary comparisons by later diagnosis. The journal abstract gives no food-specific effect estimates, preparation details or outcomes for people eating these foods after diagnosis. The full journal tables remain unavailable in the publisher and indexing routes checked. [Primary journal abstract, January 1997, PMID 18591056](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18591056/)

Lucía Ayuso Peralta’s University of Alcalá thesis was defended in January 1998. The thesis abstract gives the same 119 cases, sex counts, ages, spouse comparison, 91-food survey and directions as the 1997 article. The matching characteristics suggest one cohort. The thesis describes the pâté as foie gras, while the journal calls it liver pâté. The source tables are needed to resolve the exact ingredient.
