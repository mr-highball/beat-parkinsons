# Almond oil preparation and endpoint search

## Canon edition records an almond oil and raisin remedy for rough voice

The 1999 Beirut edition of Ibn Sīnā’s Canon, annotated by Muḥammad Amīn al-Ḍinnāwī, preserves a Book 3 chapter on rough or harsh voice. In volume 2, page 326 of the print-paginated transcription, the chapter recommends taking emollient foods and raisins, especially raisins soaked in almond oil. The Arabic wording establishes intended oral intake of the mixture. It specifies no extraction method or sweet/bitter variety and reports no cohort or recorded vocal loudness. The date refers to the modern edition of a much older text, and the recommendation concerns altered voice quality. ## Meal timing and short term levodopa motor response

Contin and colleagues studied controlled-release levodopa/carbidopa before or after a standardized meal in eight patients with Parkinson disease already using levodopa. The published abstract reports later onset and shorter duration of the observed motor response when medication was given after the meal. The magnitude and overall extent of the motor effect showed no clear change. The accessible primary abstract does not identify any separate almond oil exposure. The short test-session motor profile does not provide a diary-based daily OFF-time estimate.
