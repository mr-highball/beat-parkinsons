# Basil and parsley evidence

## Fresh and heated parsley leaves in a dietary crossover

Nielsen and colleagues published a randomized crossover in June 1999. Fourteen adults ate controlled diets with and without curly parsley leaves. Half of the parsley in the intervention diet was consumed fresh and half was microwaved. The study measured flavone excretion and erythrocyte antioxidant enzymes. It did not report Parkinson’s status or a separate estimate for either preparation on constipation, fatigue, medication response or movement.
