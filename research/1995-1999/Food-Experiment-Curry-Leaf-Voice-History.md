# Curry leaf and voice in a Kerala plant compilation

## Use attributed to a curry-leaf preparation

Joy, Thomas, Mathew and Skaria's *Medicinal Plants* was issued by Kerala Agricultural University in 1998. The curry-leaf entry identifies *Murraya koenigii* and the name Kaidaryah. Its paragraph attributes voice-related use to a preparation made from the plant and cites Sivarajan and Balachandran's 1994 book. The paragraph leaves the precise plant part for this indication and the route, dose, patient population and measured change open. The 1994 page is still required to check the original attribution.
