# Early Japanese dysphagia proceedings and primary reports, 1995–1999

## 1995 videofluoroscopy with a single barium liquid

Nagaya and colleagues' December 1995 Japanese Association of Rehabilitation Medicine proceedings describe 16 people with Parkinson's disease and swallowing complaints and 15 healthy participants. They imaged seated swallows of 5 mL of 140% barium. Nine of the 16 Parkinson participants showed aspiration and 12 showed vallecular residue. Oral transit was significantly longer than in the older control group. The abstract specifies only barium for the Parkinson cohort. The jelly-versus-liquid aspiration counts appearing on the next page belong to the neighboring abstract on patients after tongue or floor-of-mouth cancer surgery. The team's 1998 journal abstract lists the same number of Parkinson participants and total controls and describes overlapping videofluoroscopy findings. It is a likely companion and provides no independent ingredient comparison. [Original 1995 proceedings, abstract 8, pp. 861–862](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjrm1964/32/12/32_12_849/_pdf/-char/ja#page=13) · [1998 author abstract, PMID 9513304](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9513304/)

## 1996 water swallowing before and after exercise

Nagaya, Kachi and Yamada studied eight people with Parkinson's disease and eight healthy adults using surface electromyography during a visually cued 5 mL water swallow. Mean onset of suprahyoid contraction was 603.6 ms in the Parkinson group before training and 415.7 ms after exercise (p < .05). The healthy comparison had a 240.7 ms mean onset before training and no statistically significant change after exercise. The experiment evaluates training and does not vary the drink. The same team's 2000 paper enlarged the groups to 10 Parkinson participants and 12 healthy controls, with a matching experiment and a reported patient improvement in initiation time. Individual overlap is unknown. [Original 1996 proceedings, abstract 25, p. 876](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjrm1964/33/11/33_11_818/_pdf/-char/ja#page=59) · [2000 journal record, PMID 10782935](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10782935/)

## 1999 Toneyama nutrition and swallowing presentation records

MedicalOnline lists two 1999 appearances of the same title and author team led by Sonoko Nozaki at Toneyama National Hospital. One is in the conference supplement on p. 85 and the other in volume 3(2), pp. 79–80. The presentation concerns dysphagia, nutrition and complications in Parkinson's disease. Both links lead to a member login before displaying the abstracts. The same hospital's October 1999 journal study uses a five-day food history and reports aggregate intake and meal-related choking. It shares five authors with the proceedings entries and is a plausible companion, with no verified participant link. The public abstract gives no named food or preparation linked to a choking event.
