# Flaxseed oil and pain, 1995–1999

## Flax oil preparation versus linoleic acid in rheumatoid arthritis

Nordström and colleagues studied 22 adults with rheumatoid arthritis under a three-month double-blind comparison of a flaxseed-derived alpha-linolenic acid preparation and a linoleic acid control. The original abstract lists a visual analogue scale and pain tenderness among the outcomes and reports no statistical change in clinical or subjective parameters overall. Original arm-level pain values and processing grade remain unavailable. The clinical setting and active comparator restrict transfer to Parkinson pain.
