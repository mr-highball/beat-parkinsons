# Seafood and Parkinson’s symptom evidence

## Shell chitosan and stool measures

Terada and colleagues studied eight healthy men across a baseline period, two dietary chitosan doses, and a return to the baseline diet. Table 1 reports mean fecal weights of 95.5, 111.2, 116.3, and 97.1 g/day across those phases. The weight and water-content differences were inconclusive. The preparation was purified crustacean-shell chitosan, and the study had no parallel control or participants with Parkinson’s. [1995 primary report](https://doi.org/10.3109/08910609509141377)
