# Safflower oil and neurologic outcomes in 1995–1999

## Kingham Parkinson meal replacement patent

This patent family first published in August 1995. Examples 10 and 11 contain 4% and 5.5% safflower oil by weight, respectively. Example 12 describes a meal-replacement case series involving 18 people with Parkinson’s who received examples 2, 4 and 5. Those preparations contain canola and sunflower oil. The clinical reports of motor fluctuations, response timing and perceived function in Example 12 therefore supply no safflower exposure–outcome observation. Examples 10 and 11 set out recipes without patient outcomes. The safflower grade is unspecified. The patent is a useful formulation trail for 0187 and a search-routing source for Parkinson diet reports.
