# Kiwifruit, blackberry and raspberry studies

## Blackberry anaphylaxis and conflicting botanical names

Armentia and colleagues reported in December 1999 that a 45-year-old developed systemic anaphylaxis after eating berries. The report calls the fruit both black mulberry (Morus nigra) and blackberry (Rubus fruticosus), assigns it to the genus Rubus in the text and gives positive skin testing to Morus nigra. The original fruit identity and preparation are uncertain. No oral challenge or measure of vocal loudness was reported.
