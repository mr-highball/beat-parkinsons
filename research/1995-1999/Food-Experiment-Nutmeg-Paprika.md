# Nutmeg and paprika evidence

## Nutmeg in a multi-ingredient tonic study

Rani and colleagues reported changes in weakness, sleep, concentration and mood after a multi-ingredient tonic in an open study of 251 participants. The formula included nutmeg, ashwagandha, mucuna, licorice and other ingredients. The report supplies no ingredient-specific or Parkinson outcome estimate. [Primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3331163/)
