# Posture, pain, urinary urgency and related outcomes

## Fresh and heated parsley in a controlled feeding study

Nielsen et al. used two randomized one-week feeding periods in 14 healthy adults. The parsley condition added 10 g fresh and 10 g microwave-heated curly parsley each day. Apigenin excretion and antioxidant measures were analyzed. The intervention tests a real culinary exposure but provides no fatigue score, fresh-only contrast or Parkinson group.
