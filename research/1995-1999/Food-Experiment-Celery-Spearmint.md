# Food experiment evidence for celery leaf and spearmint

## 1995 Spearmint-flavored gum and saliva production

Dawes and Macpherson tested 12 adults with Wrigley’s Spearmint gum and unflavored gum base in a randomized chewing-frequency experiment. The study measures secretion rate during chewing. That outcome differs from the amount of saliva escaping the lips and the rate of swallowing. The commercial flavor is an oral sensory lead for 0952, with no dried leaf, Parkinson diagnosis or drooling outcome. [Primary report, PMID 7503931](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7503931/)
