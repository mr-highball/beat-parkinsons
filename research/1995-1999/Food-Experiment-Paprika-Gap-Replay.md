# Oral capsaicin toxicology and paprika allergy

## Isolated oral capsaicin and tremor during acute rodent toxicity

Saito and Yamamoto report walking unsteadiness and tremor among acute toxic signs in mice given isolated oral capsaicin, with tremor occurring among more severe signs in some animals. The report did not score a resting tremor phenotype or stride distances. The dosing preparation and toxicology setting differ from a culinary ground-paprika intervention. ## Paprika ingestion and separate occupational airway symptoms

Vega de la Osada and colleagues describe anaphylaxis after paprika intake and separately describe occupational rhinoconjunctivitis in a spice seller. The abstract reports paprika-positive conjunctival provocation and a use test with fresh Solanaceae. Smoking status, the case symptom chronology at the level of voice and speech, and any standardized speech result remain unresolved in the accessible abstract.
