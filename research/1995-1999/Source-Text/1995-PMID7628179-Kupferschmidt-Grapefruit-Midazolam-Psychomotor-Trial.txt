Title: Interaction between grapefruit juice and midazolam in humans.
PMID: 7628179
DOI: 10.1016/0009-9236(95)90068-3
First published: 1995
Authors: Kupferschmidt HH, Ha HR, Ziegler WH, Meier PJ, Krähenbühl S
Journal: Clinical pharmacology and therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7628179/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Open crossover study in 8 healthy men receiving oral (15 mg) or IV (5 mg) midazolam after grapefruit juice vs water. IV midazolam was unaffected, whereas oral midazolam bioavailability increased from 24% to 35% (p<0.01; Cmax +56%, AUC +52%, tmax +79%), significantly increasing pharmacodynamic AUC for reaction time and DSST (prolonged sedation/psychomotor impairment, while peak maximal effect was unchanged).
