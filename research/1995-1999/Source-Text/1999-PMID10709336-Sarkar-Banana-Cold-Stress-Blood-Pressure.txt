Title: Effect of banana on cold stress test & peak expiratory flow rate in healthy volunteers.
PMID: 10709336
First published: 1999
Authors: Sarkar C, Bairy KL, Rao NM, Udupa EG
Journal: The Indian journal of medical research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10709336/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Human controlled physiological trial evaluating banana consumption on cold-pressor stress blood pressure and plasma ACE activity in healthy volunteers. Banana significantly attenuated cold-stress systolic BP (p<0.005), diastolic BP (p<0.025), mean arterial pressure (p<0.005), and plasma ACE activity without changing heart rate. Measures pressor response in healthy adults, not orthostatic hypotension or lightheadedness in PD.
