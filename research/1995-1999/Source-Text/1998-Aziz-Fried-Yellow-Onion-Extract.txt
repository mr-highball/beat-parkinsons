Title: Absorption and excretion of conjugated flavonols by human volunteers after the consumption of onions
Authors: A A Aziz, C A Edwards, M E Lean, A Crozier
Primary original abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9802557/
Identifiers: PMID 9802557, DOI 10.1080/10715769800300291
Date: September 1998
Coverage: factual extract of the original abstract

Five healthy volunteers followed a low-flavonoid diet for three days and consumed 300 g of lightly fried yellow onions after an overnight fast on day four. The study measured flavonol glycosides in blood and urine through 24 hours. It reported different plasma and urinary recoveries for the conjugates of isorhamnetin and quercetin. The experiment verified oral intake of a named bulb preparation and did not assess speech, voice or Parkinson outcomes. The full journal article was unavailable in this review.
