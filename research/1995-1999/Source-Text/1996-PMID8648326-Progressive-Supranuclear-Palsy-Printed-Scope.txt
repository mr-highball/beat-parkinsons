Original study scope
The six printed pages describe the clinical course of 24 cases of progressive supranuclear palsy confirmed at necropsy. Table 2 gives distinct motor and falls measures, including 14 with falls in the first year. Sage appears in a cited author list. The printed header labels volume 61, while PubMed and the DOI give volume 60. Title, authors, archive identity and pages 615–620 agree.

Continuing evidence gaps
The retrospective diagnosis and symptom outcomes do not provide a tested sage or dried-date diet. No direction transfers to the five frozen Parkinson food comparisons.
