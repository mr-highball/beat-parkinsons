Dose dependent ginger and ginkgo mixture behavior in rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8808131/
PMID: 8808131
DOI: 10.1016/0091-3057(95)02001-2
Coverage: Original author abstract

Hasenohrl and colleagues tested a combined standardized ginger and ginkgo extract, administered intragastrically to rats. The lowest tested dose increased time and excursions on the open arms of an elevated plus maze. At the highest dose the animals made fewer excursions and scanned less. At 1 and 10 mg/kg the authors reported no significant maze effect. The comparison concerns combined extracts and a rodent behavior measure, with no isolated grated ginger or clinical anxiety result.
