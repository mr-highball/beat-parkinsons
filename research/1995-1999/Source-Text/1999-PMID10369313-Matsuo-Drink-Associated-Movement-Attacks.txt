Title: Familial Paroxysmal Dystonic Choreoathetosis: Clinical Findings in a Large Japanese Family and Genetic Linkage to 2q
PMID: 10369313
DOI: 10.1001/archneur.56.6.721
Publication: Archives of Neurology, June 1999
Source: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/775082
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original pedigree, clinical findings and discussion

The investigators describe at least 17 affected members across six generations and examined 26 family members. In the clinical report, coffee and green or black tea are described as triggers of attacks. Roasted green tea was reported without that trigger effect in the family. This is a family phenotype observation without a randomized beverage exposure, measured beverage caffeine or Parkinson rest-tremor or fatigue endpoint. The affected population has a familial episodic movement disorder rather than Parkinson disease.
