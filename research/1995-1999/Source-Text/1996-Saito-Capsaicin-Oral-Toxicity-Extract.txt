Title: Acute oral toxicity of capsaicin in mice and rats
Authors: Akemi Saito and Masaharu Yamamoto
Date: 1996-08
Identifiers: PMID 8887888; DOI 10.2131/jts.21.3_195
Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jts1976/21/3/21_3_195/_article/-char/en
Coverage: Original factual extract of the primary abstract, with no regimen reproduced.

Isolated oral capsaicin produced acute toxicity across two rodent species. The authors describe unsteady gait among major observations in mice and tremor among severe signs in a subset of animals. Other acute symptoms included respiratory signs and convulsions. The findings do not isolate tremor at rest or provide standardized gait or tremor severity. This was neither a Parkinson model nor a culinary paprika intervention.
