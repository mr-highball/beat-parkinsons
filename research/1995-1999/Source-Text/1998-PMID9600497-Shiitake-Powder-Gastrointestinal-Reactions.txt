Title: Eosinophilia and gastrointestinal symptoms after ingestion of shiitake mushrooms
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9600497/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S009167499870168X
PMID: 9600497
DOI: 10.1016/S0091-6749(98)70168-X
Publication date: 1998
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Original author abstract and indexed publisher sections. Whole article unavailable on review.

Ten healthy adults took shiitake powder in a repeated open-label study. Two reported gastrointestinal symptoms coincident with eosinophilia. No verified swallowing outcome, Parkinson population or roasting grade appears in the inspected material. A prior withdrawal count in a distinct study does not supply an additional swallowing case or confirm overlap.
