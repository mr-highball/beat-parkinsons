Canon edition records an almond oil and raisin remedy for rough voice
Primary source: https://shamela.ws/book/10706
Coverage: factual extract from Arabic primary book 3, modern-edition volume 2 page 326.

The 1999 Beirut edition of Ibn Sīnā’s Canon, annotated by Muḥammad Amīn al-Ḍinnāwī, preserves a Book 3 chapter on rough or harsh voice. In volume 2, page 326 of the print-paginated transcription, the chapter recommends taking emollient foods and raisins, especially raisins soaked in almond oil. The Arabic wording establishes intended oral intake of the mixture. It specifies no extraction method or sweet/bitter variety and reports no cohort or recorded vocal loudness. The date refers to the modern edition of a much older text, and the recommendation concerns altered voice quality.
