Suspected oil identity after cosmetic injection

Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/internalmedicine1992/34/5/34_5_380/_pdf
Coverage: Original full case, factual extract.

After a cosmetic injection, an adult developed acute respiratory injury and fat embolism. The case description includes apathetic altered consciousness. Fatty acid profiling suggested a resemblance to rice bran oil. Processing, oil identity, and standardized apathy were not established. No oral exposure or Parkinson assessment occurred.
