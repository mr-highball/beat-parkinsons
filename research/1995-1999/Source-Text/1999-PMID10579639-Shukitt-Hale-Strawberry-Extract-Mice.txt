Title: Dietary antioxidants and psychomotor performance in aged mice
PMID: 10579639
DOI: 10.1016/S0531-5565(99)00039-X
Authors: Shukitt-Hale et al.
Publication date: 1999-09
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10579639/
Coverage: original author abstract via PubMed. Full report tables were not verified.

Male C57BL/6NIA mice began one of six dietary regimens at 18 months, including a strawberry extract regimen and control. The motor battery included rod walk, wire hang, plank walk and inclined screen. Old mice performed worse than young controls on some tasks before treatment, with no reported further falloff from 18 to 24 months. Over six months the abstract describes no reversal from the strawberry extract regimen. The reported exception concerned glutathione on the inclined screen. The abstract provides neither strawberry-specific numerical contrasts nor uncertainty intervals.

This is a separate experiment from Joseph et al. 1999 in rats. The abstract establishes an aged-animal motor test with a related preparation. It does not estimate human therapeutic effects or recurrent fall frequency.
