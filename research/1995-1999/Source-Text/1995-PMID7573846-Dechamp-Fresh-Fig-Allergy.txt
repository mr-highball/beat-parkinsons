Title: First report of anaphylactic reaction after fig (Ficus carica) ingestion
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7573846/
PMID: 7573846
DOI: 10.1111/j.1398-9995.1995.tb01188.x
Publication date: 1995-06
Coverage: Paraphrased original author abstract, with the publisher issue listing and pages 514–516 verified.

The authors describe an acute anaphylactic reaction shortly after fresh fig ingestion. Tests supported sensitization to common fig and suggested cross-reactivity with weeping fig. The primary abstract gives no separate account of drooling, saliva flow or a Parkinson diagnosis. The exact oral findings and symptom sequence in the original case body remain to be checked.
