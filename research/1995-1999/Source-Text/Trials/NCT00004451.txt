BRIEF SUMMARY

This study will determine whether glucose facilitates memory in healthy elderly people
and those with Parkinson's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Subjects fast for 8 hours before receiving a test to determine their blood glucose
levels. Following this test, subjects are randomized to receive either a glucose or
saccharin sweetened drink. A series of memory tests and an additional 3 blood glucose
level tests are administered approximately 15 minutes later. A minimum of 1 week
afterward, the subjects return and receive the alternate sweetener followed by additional
memory tests.
