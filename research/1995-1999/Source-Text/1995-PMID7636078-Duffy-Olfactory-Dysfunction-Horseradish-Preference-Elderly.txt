Title: Olfactory dysfunction and related nutritional risk in free-living, elderly women
PMID: 7636078
DOI: 10.1016/S0002-8223(95)00244-8
First published: 1995
Authors: Duffy VB, Backstrand JR, Ferris AM
Journal: Journal of the American Dietetic Association
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7636078/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Cross-sectional observational study of 80 free-living elderly women (mean age 76 y; 37/80 with measured olfactory dysfunction). Lower olfactory perception was associated with lower preference for sour/bitter foods and pungent foods such as horseradish. Observational food-preference correlate in non-PD elderly women; does not test horseradish as an olfactory treatment.
