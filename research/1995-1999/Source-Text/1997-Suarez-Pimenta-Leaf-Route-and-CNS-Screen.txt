Injected Pimenta leaf extract and acute CNS depression
Primary source: https://tropicalstudies.org/rbt/attachments/volumes/vol44-3A/06_Suarez_Pimenta_dioica.pdf
PMID: 9404514
Coverage: factual extract from the primary Spanish Methods and Results. The text below records source facts and scope.

Suárez Urhan, Ulate Montero and Cicció reported a concentrated Pimenta dioica leaf extract in the 1996–1997 Revista de Biología Tropical joint issue, indexed March 1997. Their acute intraperitoneal screening recorded dose-related reductions in activity, loss of righting, altered alarm response and hypothermia, with peritoneal irritation as a possible contributor. Their separate 14-day oral aqueous-fraction comparison tracked systolic pressure, heart rate and weight without a reported change in those measures. The quantified behavioral signs belong to the injected cohort. The paper supplies no natural REM measures, clinical attention or hallucination ratings, speech or handwriting data.
