Title: Skin prick tests and in vitro immunoassays with native spices and spice extracts
Authors: A Niinimäki, M Hannuksela and S Mäkinen-Kiljunen
Date: 1995-09
Identifiers: PMID 7552932
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7552932/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the primary abstract.

Forty-nine patients received skin prick tests with the native commercial powdered spices coriander, caraway, paprika, cayenne, mustard and white pepper, with repeat tests at intervals ranging from two months to 2.9 years. Across the panel, the repeat native spice tests reproduced 67–100% of skin results. For most spices, extracts elicited fewer positive skin results than native powder. The report abstract does not provide caraway-specific phonation, intelligibility, or a culinary ingestion comparison.
