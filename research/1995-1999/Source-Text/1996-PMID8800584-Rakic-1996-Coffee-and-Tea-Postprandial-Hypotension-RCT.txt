Title: Effect of coffee and tea drinking on postprandial hypotension in older men and women.
PMID: 8800584
DOI: 10.1111/j.1440-1681.1996.tb02779.x
First published: 1996
Authors: Rakic V, Beilin LJ, Burke V
Journal: Clinical and experimental pharmacology & physiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8800584/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled intervention in n=171 healthy non-smoking adults aged >50 y (normotensive, untreated hypertensive, and treated hypertensive) testing coffee vs abstinence, with brewed tea as a third arm in treated hypertensives. Among treated hypertensives drinking tea, postprandial fall was attenuated by 3.8 +/- 1.2 mmHg in supine SBP (p=0.029) and 5.2 +/- 2.1 mmHg in standing SBP. Limits: non-PD older adults; blood pressure proxy only without orthostatic lightheadedness symptom scores.
