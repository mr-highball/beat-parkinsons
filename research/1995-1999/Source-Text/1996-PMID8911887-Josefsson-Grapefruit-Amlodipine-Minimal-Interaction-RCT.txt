Title: Effect of grapefruit juice on the pharmacokinetics of amlodipine in healthy volunteers.
PMID: 8911887
DOI: 10.1007/s002280050183
First published: 1996
Authors: Josefsson M, Zackrisson AL, Ahlner J
Journal: European journal of clinical pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8911887/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized crossover trial in 12 healthy men taking amlodipine 5 mg with 250 mL grapefruit juice vs water. Amlodipine Cmax (115%) and AUC (116%) rose minimally with NO significant difference in BP or HR between treatments—demonstrating substrate-specificity of grapefruit hemodynamic interactions.
