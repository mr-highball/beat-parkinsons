1999 | Kumagai et al. | Intravenous soybean-oil emulsion during a Leigh syndrome crisis

Source: original indexed abstract, PMID 10457398, DOI 10.1046/j.1468-1331.1999.650613.x, September 1999.

The 38-year-old patient had adult Leigh syndrome associated with partial pyruvate dehydrogenase complex deficiency. Episodic manifestations included dystonia, diplopia, vision loss, respiratory failure and impaired consciousness. The authors describe rapid clinical and biochemical improvement after intravenous ketogenic emulsion. Soybean oil is identified in the title and indexing. The accessible abstract gives no separate dystonia time course or severity measure, no oil processing or lot, and no oral comparison. Full original text remains pending.
