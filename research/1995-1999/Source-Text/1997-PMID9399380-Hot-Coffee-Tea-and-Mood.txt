Original source scope and evidence boundary
Sixteen healthy participants abstained from caffeine for 14 hours before a crossover comparing hot tea, coffee and water, with matched caffeine conditions and milk changes for tea and coffee. The abstract describes improved mood and reduced anxiety after caffeine and milk addition. The investigators report similar general responses to coffee and tea and attribute much of the pattern to water temperature and caffeine. The available original gives no separate clinical fatigue-severity estimate. Confirm from the full Methods whether coffee was instant or brewed and how tea was chosen. The identified general mood outcomes carry no transferable direction for brewed coffee and Parkinson fatigue.
