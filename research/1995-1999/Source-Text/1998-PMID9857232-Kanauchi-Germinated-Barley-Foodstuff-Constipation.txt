Title: Germinated barley foodstuff increases fecal volume and butyrate production at relatively low doses and relieves constipation in humans.
PMID: 9857232
DOI: 10.3892/ijmm.2.4.445
First published: 1998
Authors: Kanauchi O, Mitsuyama K, Saiki T, Nakamura T, Hitomi Y, Bamba T
Journal: International journal of molecular medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9857232/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Human feeding study of germinated barley foodstuff (GBF, hemicellulose-rich aleurone/scutellum/germ fraction). In n=9 healthy volunteers (9 g/d x 10 d, then 18 g/d x 10 d vs 3-d control), GBF significantly increased fecal weight and fecal butyrate at both doses. In a second study of n=16 mildly chronic constipated volunteers given 9 g/d GBF for 14 days, GBF significantly improved defecation frequency and volume without severe adverse effects. Corroborated by Kanauchi 1998 (PMID:9852628, n=10 healthy volunteers at 30 g/d x 28 d increasing fecal weight, water content, and butyrate). Limits: germinated barley fiber fraction rather than whole boiled grain; small uncontrolled constipation cohort, non-PD.
