Caregiver-rated apathy in Parkinson dementia and Alzheimer disease
October 1, 1996 · PMID 8890777 · PMC486579 · DOI 10.1136/jnnp.61.4.381

Factual extract from original printed Methods and Tables 1–2 pp. 381–387.

Starkstein et al., JNNP 61(4), 381–387. Investigators compared 33 patients with Parkinson dementia and 33 with probable Alzheimer disease. The Methods on p. 383 specify the 14-item caregiver apathy scale (0–42). Table 2, p. 384 reports mean 17.8 (SD 7.9) in Parkinson dementia and 15.5 (11.1) in probable Alzheimer disease. The difference was not identified as statistically significant. An Alzheimer-group eligibility criterion on p. 382 specifies zero UPDRS items for bradykinesia, rigidity and tremor. This does not estimate those separate outcomes in the Parkinson cohort. Table 1 reports a total motor score. The study supplies apathy measurement context without a date-fruit comparison.
