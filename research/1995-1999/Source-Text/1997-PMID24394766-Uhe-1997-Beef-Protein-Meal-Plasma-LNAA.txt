Title: Amino acid levels following beef protein and amino acid supplement in male subjects.
PMID: 24394766
First published: 1997
Authors: Uhe AM, O'Dea K, Collier GR
Journal: Asia Pacific journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24394766/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled crossover amino acid kinetic study in n=6 lean male subjects comparing a 50 g cooked beef protein meal against a 4 g commercial amino acid supplement: the cooked beef meal produced sustained elevations in plasma large neutral amino acids (tyrosine, valine, leucine, isoleucine, phenylalanine, methionine) and sustained trp/LNAA and tyr/LNAA shifts longer than the 15-30 min supplement spike. Establishes the direct constituent bridge from cooked beef to plasma LNAAs that compete with levodopa.
