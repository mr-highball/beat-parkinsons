Díez-Gómez ML, Quirce S, Aragoneses E, Cuevas M. Asthma caused by Ficus benjamina latex: Evidence of cross-reactivity with fig fruit and papain. Ann Allergy Asthma Immunol. 1998;80:24–30.
DOI: 10.1016/S1081-1206(10)62934-1
PMID: 9475562
Primary sources: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1081120610629341 and https://www.academia.edu/110803502/Asthma_Caused_by_Ficus_benjamina_Latex_Evidence_of_Cross_Reactivity_with_Fig_Fruit_and_Papain
Coverage: publisher identity and author abstract, case history from publicly indexed body pp. 24–25 with interleaved column order.

One patient exposed to ornamental weeping fig reported oral itching, tongue and oropharyngeal angioedema and dizziness after eating fresh edible fig, then similar symptoms with kiwi and dried fig. The clinical and laboratory work examined allergic sensitization and potential cross-reactivity. The history gives no measured hallucination outcome or Parkinson diagnosis. Dizziness is described in the allergic episode.
