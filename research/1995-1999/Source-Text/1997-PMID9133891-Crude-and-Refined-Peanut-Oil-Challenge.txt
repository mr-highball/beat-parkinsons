Title: Randomised, double blind, crossover challenge study of allergenicity of peanut oils in subjects allergic to peanuts
PMID: 9133891
PMCID: PMC2126478
DOI: 10.1136/bmj.314.7087.1084
Publication date: 1997-04-12
Citation: Hourihane, Bedwani, Dean and Warner. BMJ 314(7087):1084–1088.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9133891/
Original paper copy: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/14084239_Randomised_Double_Blind_Crossover_Challenge_Study_of_Allergenicity_of_Peanut_Oils_in_Subjects_Allergic_to_Peanuts
Text coverage: Primary abstract and original article Methods, Table 2 and funding statements.

Sixty participants with positive evidence of peanut allergy completed the blinded oil challenge. The authors reported reactions in 6/60 after crude oil and 0/60 after refined oil. Table 2 lists oral or throat itching, wheeze and lip swelling among the six crude-oil reactions. Four of the six were subjective without observable or measurable signs in the authors' discussion. These were acute allergy outcomes and no separate resting-tremor value is reported. A processing trade association supplied the oils and funded the work. The paper describes crude and refined grades. Further documentation would be needed to equate the tested crude grade to a particular cold-pressed product.
