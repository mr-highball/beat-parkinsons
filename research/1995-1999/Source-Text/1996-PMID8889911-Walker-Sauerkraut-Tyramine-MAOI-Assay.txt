Title: Tyramine content of previously restricted foods in monoamine oxidase inhibitor diets.
PMID: 8889911
DOI: 10.1097/00004714-199610000-00007
First published: 1996
Authors: Walker SE, Shulman KI, Tailor SA, Gardner D
Journal: Journal of clinical psychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8889911/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

HPLC food tyramine assay of 51 foods restricted in MAOI diets, measuring ~7.75 mg tyramine per 250 g serving of sauerkraut (exceeding the classic 6 mg tyramine threshold for non-selective MAOI pressor reactions). Provides pharmacological safety context for fermented green cabbage in PD patients taking MAO-B inhibitors (selegiline/rasagiline at higher doses or non-selective MAOIs); does not measure PD motor or mood symptom severity.
