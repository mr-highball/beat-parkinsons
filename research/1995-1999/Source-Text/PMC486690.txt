Title: Rhythmic auditory-motor facilitation of gait patterns in patients with Parkinson's disease

Text coverage: Original printed article PDF, pages 22-26, matched to PubMed title, author list and journal issue. Factual extract.

Thirty-one participants with Parkinson disease and ten healthy older participants walked with and without rhythmic auditory cues. The study reports changes in walking speed, cadence and stride length under faster cues. Postural instability is clinical context rather than an isolated intervention outcome in these experiments. Brown and Rice are authors' surnames. Cooked brown rice was not consumed as part of the tested exposure.
