UPDRS item comparisons across Parkinson disease and dementia groups

Authors: Kanna K. Gnanalingham, E. Jane Byrne, Andrew Thornton, Mike A. Sambrook and Paul Bannister

Coverage: Publisher PDF, original page scans and Table 2

The cross-sectional study included 15 patients with Parkinson disease, 16 with Lewy body dementia, 25 with Alzheimer disease and 22 controls, with some assessments available for fewer participants. For the Parkinson group, Table 2 reports median rigidity item 22 of 3 (range 1–4), hand and foot bradykinesia items 23–26 of 7 (1–16), body bradykinesia item 31 of 2 (0–5) and falls item 13 of 1 (0–2.5). Item 13 is a clinical rating at assessment. Visual hallucination frequency was not measured. These comparisons between diagnostic groups involved no assigned food intake.
