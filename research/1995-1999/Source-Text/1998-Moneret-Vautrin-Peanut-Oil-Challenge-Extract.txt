French oral peanut oil challenges and individual reactions
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1046/j.1365-2222.1998.00370.x
Coverage: Factual extract of primary Methods and Table 2

Fourteen of 62 oral peanut-oil challenges among people with peanut allergy were positive. The comparator was paraffin oil, with negative placebo challenges reported. The paper gives individual allergic signs and describes refined oil separately for a skin-test preparation. The grade of oral oil and quantitative voice outcomes were not established.
