Meal timing and controlled-release levodopa
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9696954/
Original abstract paraphrase

Published June 1998. In a randomized crossover of eight people with Parkinson disease, a standard meal 30 minutes before controlled-release levodopa/carbidopa delayed absorption compared with a meal two hours after dosing. The six-hour area under the plasma concentration curve was about 24% smaller in the fed condition. Two participants peaked at five hours, limiting the window. The abstract does not identify soybean oil and does not supply an all-day OFF estimate.
