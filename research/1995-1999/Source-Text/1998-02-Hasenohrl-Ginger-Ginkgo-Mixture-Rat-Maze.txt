Ginger and ginkgo mixture ratios and open arm behavior
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9477004/
PMID: 9477004
DOI: 10.1016/S0091-3057(97)00406-1
Coverage: Original author abstract and cited earlier comparison

Hasenohrl and colleagues compared standardized combined ginger and ginkgo extracts at three ratios in rats. At 1 mg/kg the standard 2.5 to 1 preparation increased time, scanning and excursions on the open arms. The 1 to 1 and 1 to 2.5 preparation did not clearly affect behavior over the tested range. The 1996 report had an inconclusive result at the same 1 mg/kg dose. Neither comparison supplies a patient anxiety measure or an isolated fresh grated ginger effect. Exact numerical arm estimates remain outside the accessible abstracts.
