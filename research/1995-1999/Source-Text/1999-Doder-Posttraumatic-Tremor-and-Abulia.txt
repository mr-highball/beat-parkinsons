Rest tremor and abulia after closed head injury
March 1, 1999 · PMID 10084539 · PMC1736270 · DOI 10.1136/jnnp.66.3.380
https://www.academia.edu/164672773/Parkinsons_syndrome_after_closed_head_injury_a_single_case_report
Factual extract from original JNNP pp. 380–385.

Doder and colleagues describe one patient with parkinsonism after closed-head trauma. The printed examination records a right-hand resting tremor alongside separately described postural tremor. Neuropsychological assessment describes abulia. An individual post-traumatic examination and laboratory reaction-time measures provide symptom context. The paper contains no edible fig or date intervention or parallel symptom comparison.
