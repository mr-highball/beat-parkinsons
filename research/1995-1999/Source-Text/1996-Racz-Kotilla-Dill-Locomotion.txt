Dill stem leaf and fruit extracts in mouse locomotor assays
Source: https://horizon.documentation.ird.fr/exl-doc/pleins_textes/pleins_textes_6/colloques2/010005561.pdf
Coverage: Original proceedings chapter pp 303–305, methods and Tables 1–7

The chapter by Elisabeth Rácz-Kotilla and Gabriel Rácz appeared in the 1996 ORSTOM/SFE proceedings of the March 1993 Heidelberg meeting. It describes earlier work cited as Note Botanice 19:57 (1988) and Revista Medicala 35:191 (1989). The journal's online issue assigns volume 19 to 1989. The exact earlier locomotor article remains unresolved. Male albino mice received injected aqueous stem, leaf or fruit extracts, isolated carvone or fruit essential oil. Relative spontaneous movement declined with higher leaf and fruit extract amounts, whereas low fruit-oil groups had values above control. Separate tests examined induced hyperactivity and nociceptive responses. The study did not test natural sleep, clinical insomnia, hand dexterity, or oral fresh or dried dill leaves.
