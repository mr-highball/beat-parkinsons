Title: Cytochrome c oxidase deficiency associated with the first stop-codon point mutation in human mtDNA

Citation: Hanna MG et al. American Journal of Human Genetics 63(1), 29–36. First published electronically June 12, 1998.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Original publisher PDF, printed pages 29–36.

The case documents exertional fatigue, myalgia and recurrent encephalopathy in an adult with isolated mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase deficiency. The analyses concern muscle and other specimens and distinguish a metabolic myopathy from a dietary intervention. Chicken appears in a mitochondrial-genome reference, not as consumed food. Visual hallucination frequency was not an assessed outcome and the study did not assign figs or chicken.
