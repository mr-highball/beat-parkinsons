Title: Anti-nociceptive and anti-inflammatory effects of some Jordanian medicinal plant extracts.
PMID: 9582001
DOI: 10.1016/s0378-8741(97)00137-2
First published: 1998
Authors: Atta AH, Alkofahi A
Journal: J Ethnopharmacol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9582001/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical screening study of ethanolic extracts of 11 Jordanian medicinal plants in mice/rats. Apium graveolens extract showed dose-dependent antinociceptive effects in acetic-acid writhing and hot-plate tests in mice, while failing to rank among the plants active in ear-edema/granuloma inflammation models. Non-PD acute rodent assay; plant part not restricted to culinary stalk.
