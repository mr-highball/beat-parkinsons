Diego MA et al. Aromatherapy positively affects mood, EEG patterns of alertness and math computations. Int J Neurosci. 1998;96:217–224.
DOI: 10.3109/00207459808986469
PMID: 10069621
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10069621/
Coverage: original author abstract only. Full publisher body returned HTTP 403 through the checked route.

Forty adults experienced rosemary or lavender aroma. The abstract reports lower POMS depressed mood in the lavender group. The rosemary group had lower reported state anxiety and evidence of increased alertness by the authors' EEG interpretation. A rosemary-specific depression estimate or between-aroma change comparison is not disclosed in the abstract. This is inhaled aroma in adults, without an oral fresh leaf Parkinson comparison.
