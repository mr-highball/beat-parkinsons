Title: The effects of grapefruit juice on sertraline metabolism: an in vitro and in vivo study.
PMID: 10890261
DOI: 10.1016/S0149-2918(00)86737-5
First published: 1999
Authors: Lee AJ, Chan WK, Harralson AF, Buffum J, Bui BC
Journal: Clinical therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10890261/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Small in vitro and in vivo study (n=5 depressed patients, mean age 68.6 y) showing 1 week of grapefruit juice raised serum sertraline trough levels from 13.7 to 20.2 ug/L (p=0.047). Pharmacokinetic change only; no clinical depression ratings reported.
