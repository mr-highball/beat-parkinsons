Galton and Hodges review diagnostic presentations of dementia. Table 2 adapts prior diagnostic guidelines to distinguish typical formed visual hallucinations and other clinical features of dementia with Lewy bodies. Hallucinations in other sensory modalities appear separately. The review suggests a possible cholinergic contribution. It does not contribute a new cohort, an episode-rate measure or a dried-fig exposure.
