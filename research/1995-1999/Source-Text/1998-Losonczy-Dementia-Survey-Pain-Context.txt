Factual extract from complete CDC-hosted original scan pp. 273–280.

Losonczy et al., Public Health Reports 113(3), 273–280. The sample consists of 1,227 next-of-kin interviews, used in this report to analyze dementia and function. Printed p. 274 describes great pain at death among the aims of the parent survey. This report gives no separate pain-severity comparison or fall-frequency result. The publication is in the 1998 issue. The paper helps distinguish survey aims from reported outcomes for mapped pain and fall claims.
