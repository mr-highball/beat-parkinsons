Title: Neurotropic components from star anise (Illicium verum Hook. fil.).
PMID: 8904818
DOI: 10.1248/cpb.44.1908
First published: 1996
Authors: Nakamura T, Okuyama E, Yamazaki M
Journal: Chemical & pharmaceutical bulletin
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8904818/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Phytochemical and mouse pharmacology study (Nakamura, Okuyama & Yamazaki, 1996, Chem Pharm Bull) isolating three neurotropic sesquiterpenoids — veranisatins A, B, and C — from authentic Chinese star anise (Illicium verum Hook. f.). At 3 mg/kg orally in mice, veranisatins caused convulsions and lethal toxicity; at lower oral doses (0.1 mg/kg p.o.), veranisatin A caused hypothermia, reduced methamphetamine-enhanced locomotion, and produced analgesia in acetic acid writhing and tail-pressure tests. Establishes intrinsic dose-dependent neurotoxic ceiling even in authentic I. verum, while isolated purified veranisatin A cannot sign culinary ground star anise efficacy.
