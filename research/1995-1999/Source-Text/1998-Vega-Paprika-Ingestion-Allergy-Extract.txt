Title: Sensitization to paprika: anaphylaxis after intake and rhinoconjunctivitis after contact through airways
Authors: F Vega de la Osada and colleagues
Date: 1998-09-12
Identifier: PMID 9789242
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9789242/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the accessible primary abstract.

The patient had anaphylaxis after eating paprika and occupational exposure to spices with nose and eye symptoms. The workup used prick tests, assays and provocation with Solanaceae. Paprika was positive in conjunctival testing and in a reported use test. The abstract does not specify smoking or any assessment of speech intelligibility or vocal acoustics.
