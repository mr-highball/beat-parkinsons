Dietary peanut oil and two different mouse rotarod tests
Source: https://www.bourre.fr/pdf/publications_scientifiques/210.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of the primary article, Tables 1 and 3

Mice received a purified peanut-oil diet or a diet replacing a portion of peanut oil with rapeseed. Table 3 gives low-rotarod times of 669.2 and 74.6 seconds, respectively (p < .0002). Elevated-rotarod, grasping and swimming measures did not differ significantly. The study did not use a Parkinson model or measure handwriting.
