Twenty-four habitual coffee drinkers were evaluated at midday on a usual caffeinated beverage day and on a brief caffeine-abstinent day, in counterbalanced order. The abstract reports lower vigor and greater questionnaire fatigue on the abstinent day. Computerized psychomotor performance did not differ according to the author abstract.

Round 1 claim 0121. The comparison concerns short-term abstinence among habitual users. It does not assign a specific brewed coffee or measure Parkinson fatigue.
