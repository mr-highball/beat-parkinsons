Title: Fatigue during brief blinded caffeine deprivation in habitual coffee drinkers
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9402612/
PMID: 9402612
DOI: 10.1016/s0031-9384(97)00384-3
Publication date: 1997-12-31
Date basis: Original issue date in NLM metadata.
Review depth: Original author abstract. Exact item size and full tables remain open.

Thirty-one regular coffee drinkers were tested at midday four hours after blinded caffeine or placebo. Deprivation was associated with higher fatigue and lower vigor, with other symptoms including sleepiness and yawning. The abstract has no separate numeric fatigue estimate. The active contrast is a fixed caffeine dose and does not identify the effect of a specific brewed coffee.

The earlier September Lane report enrolled 24 regular coffee drinkers and used normal ad libitum caffeinated beverages versus abstinence. Both report selected midday fatigue outcomes. Treat them as a related investigation series with unresolved participant overlap, and avoid counting the capsule study as independent corroboration for a brewed-beverage effect until recruitment and study dates can be verified.
