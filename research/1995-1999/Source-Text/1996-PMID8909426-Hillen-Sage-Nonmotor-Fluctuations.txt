Title: Nonmotor fluctuations in patients with Parkinson's disease
PMID: 8909426
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.47.5.1180
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8909426/
Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.47.5.1180
Publication: 1996-11, Neurology 47(5), pp. 1180–1183
Coverage: Original author abstract and institutional publication record. Full article remains open.

Hillen and Sage describe 130 consecutive Parkinson disease patients with motor fluctuations. Twenty-two reported end-of-dose nonmotor symptoms (17%). The abstract includes proximal limb and trigeminal-neuralgia-like pain among the reported phenotypes. Twelve underwent treatment modification, with improvement in nonmotor symptoms in nine. This is a combined nonmotor count without a separate pain outcome proportion or pain severity estimate.

Sage identifies the coauthor. The abstract describes medication-linked observational context and gives no fresh sage exposure. The complete article would be needed to verify symptom-specific counts and treatment details.
