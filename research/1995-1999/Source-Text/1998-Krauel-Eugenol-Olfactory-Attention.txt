Attention instructions during linalool and eugenol odor stimulation
Primary source: https://academic.oup.com/chemse/article-pdf/23/4/423/978093/23-4-423.pdf
PMID: 9759529
DOI: 10.1093/chemse/23.4.423
Coverage: factual extract from the primary paper and Experiment 1. The text below records source facts and scope.

Krauel and colleagues published Experiment 1 in August 1998. Six men received linalool and eugenol through an olfactometer in an odor oddball experiment. Attention was manipulated with instructions to address the rare odor or to attend a distractor task. Attending produced shorter early chemosensory component latencies and larger late positivities. Those are effects of attention allocation on odor-evoked signals. Odor-specific clinical attention worsening, oral ground allspice and a Parkinson population were outside the experiment. The paper reports a separate body-odor experiment that did not administer eugenol.
