Title: Effects of ginger on gastroduodenal motility
PMID: 10442508
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10442508/
Published: 1999-07, International Journal of Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics 37, 341–346.
Archive scope: Factual summary of the original author abstract. The full printed article and numerical tables remain open.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Stationary manometry in 12 healthy volunteers examined an oral ginger rhizome extract given as two 100 mg units. The authors report greater fasting phase III antral activity and a larger post-meal corpus motor response. Other recorded regions had trends in the same direction. The abstract supplies no quantitative effect estimates, clinical pain or constipation severity comparison, or Parkinson population. The EMA assessment describes a blinded placebo crossover. Keep these design details secondary pending access to the original print methods. This is the 12-person extract study cited separately from the 24-person Wu ultrasound study in the four-week 2023 dyspepsia paper.
