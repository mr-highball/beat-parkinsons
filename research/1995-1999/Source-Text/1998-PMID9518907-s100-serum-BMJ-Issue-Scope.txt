Otto and colleagues originally measured serum S100 in patients referred for suspected Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease and compared findings with other diagnoses and controls. Table 5 lists one Parkinson case among the differential diagnoses and reports a biomarker value. Back pain appears as a separate diagnostic entry in that table. Methods and results do not compare dried date fruit consumption or measure its effect on pain severity.
