Coverage: factual summary of original interview methods and results, especially p. 435.

Of 129 patients with idiopathic Parkinson disease, 32 reported hallucinosis or illusions: four illusions only, 21 hallucinations only and seven both. Visual phenomena were identified in 30 of the 32 and can include any visual component. The paper analyzes lifetime reports and clinical correlates, rather than episodes per patient over a defined follow-up window. It supplies no edible fig comparison.
