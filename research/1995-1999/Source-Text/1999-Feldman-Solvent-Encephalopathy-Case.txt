Pain sensation and affect in a solvent encephalopathy case
PMID 10210698
DOI 10.1289/ehp.99107417
https://open.bu.edu/bitstream/2144/2822/1/envhper00510-0127.pdf

April 8, 1999 online, May 1999 issue. In a 57-year-old painter with chronic solvent exposure, Feldman, Ratner and Ptak record normal pain sensation during physical examination on page 418. Testing assessed anxiety and depression. The discussion on page 420 describes apathy as a general feature of chronic toluene encephalopathy, without a patient-level apathy outcome. The complete identity-matched institutional PDF and official full text establish the clinical scope. The study did not evaluate dried date or a Parkinson’s treatment.
