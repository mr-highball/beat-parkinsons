Title: Falling asleep at the wheel: motor vehicle mishaps in persons taking pramipexole and ropinirole
Source: https://www.neurology.org/doi/10.1212/WNL.52.9.1908
PMID: 10371546
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.52.9.1908
Publication date: 1999-06
Date basis: Publisher and NLM agree on June 1999 and differ on the day of the issue.
Source depth: Primary publisher and NLM abstract
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The published case series describes nine Parkinson patients taking dopamine agonists who fell asleep while driving. Eight were taking pramipexole and one was taking ropinirole. The authors report that the sleep attacks ceased after the drugs were withdrawn. The 2014 secondary tabulation (PMID 30363881) assigns a visual hallucination to one of the source cases and separates it from an auditory report. The original abstract does not specify modalities, concurrent drug effects or episode frequency. Original case-table access stays open.
