Title: Postprandial hypotension and parkinsonian state in Parkinson's disease
Publication date: 1997-11
PMID: 9399210
DOI: 10.1002/mds.870120608
Citation: Movement Disorders 12(6), 1997.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9399210/
Coverage: Author abstract retrieved through PubMed EFetch. The original item-level results remain unverified.

Twenty participants with idiopathic Parkinson disease and sixteen age-matched controls consumed the same standardized balanced liquid meal. In the Parkinson group the authors observed a post-meal blood-pressure drop while supine and a worsening in a combined motor score that included tremor, rigidity, slowness, posture and gait. They also described postural intolerance. The reported results do not separate resting tremor or identify an ordinary food exposure. This study helps distinguish an autonomic and motor response after a meal from a turkey-specific mechanism.
