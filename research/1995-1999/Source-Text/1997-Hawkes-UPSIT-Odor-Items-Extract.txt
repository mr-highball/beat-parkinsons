Cinnamon and other odor cues in the 1997 UPSIT comparison

PMID: 9153598
PMCID: PMC486843
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.62.5.436
Coverage: Indexed original publisher PDF and Figure 4

Hawkes, Shephard and Daniel published this Parkinson and control comparison in May 1997. Figure 4 labels cinnamon, clove, onion and grape among the diagnostic UPSIT stimuli. The text reports that 71 of 96 Parkinson participants scored below age-matched identification norms. A matched subset of 70 Parkinson participants and 70 controls contributed to individual odor comparisons. The odors were presented for identification. Eating these ingredients and post-exposure rigidity were outside the comparison. This is the same study as the pizza and wintergreen entry above.
