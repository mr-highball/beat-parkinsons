Title: The interaction effect of grapefruit juice is maximal after the first glass.
PMID: 9591935
DOI: 10.1007/s002280050424
First published: 1998
Authors: Lundahl JU, Regårdh CG, Edgar B, Johnsson G
Journal: European journal of clinical pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9591935/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized crossover trial in 12 healthy men taking felodipine ER 10 mg daily for 14 days with grapefruit juice vs water. Grapefruit juice increased felodipine AUC (+73% day 1, +57% day 14) and Cmax (+138% day 1, +114% day 14), produced significant additional heart rate increase (days 1 and 14), additional diastolic BP reduction (day 14), and MORE frequent vascular-related adverse events during the grapefruit period.
