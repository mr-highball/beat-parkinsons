Composition of dried leaf and lemon balm infusion
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0031-6865(97)00026-5
Coverage: factual extract of primary abstract
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Carnat and colleagues measured both dried lemon balm leaves and the infusion. The abstract gives 1.07 g/L of total polyphenols and 10 mg/L of essential oil in the infusion, corresponding to reported recoveries of 93% and 31%, respectively. The extraction fractions concern this sample and method. They supply chemical overlap with the starting leaf and no clinical depression comparison.
