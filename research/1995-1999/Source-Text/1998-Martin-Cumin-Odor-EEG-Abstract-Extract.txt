Title: Synthetic cumin odor and human EEG
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167876098000257
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

G. Neil Martin, November 1998. Twenty-one participants encountered synthetic odors including a cumin stimulus and a no-odor control in experiment one. EEG theta on chocolate was lower than with cumin and almond. The abstract supplies no separate cumin-versus-no-odor attention task result. The authors interpret EEG theta as relevant to cognitive load. There was no culinary consumption or Parkinson cohort.
