The cohort included 139 people with diagnosed Parkinson disease from a population-based study. Caregivers answered Neuropsychiatric Inventory questions that scored occurrence, frequency and severity over the previous month. The Results reports 23 people with apathy, including six with a zero score for depression. Apathy and anxiety grouped together in the factor analysis, while depression also correlated with several psychiatric domains. The analysis addresses clinical symptom structure and supplies no dried-date intake measure.
