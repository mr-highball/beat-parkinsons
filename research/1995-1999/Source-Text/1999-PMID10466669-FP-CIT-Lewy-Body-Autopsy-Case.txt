Title: In-vivo demonstration of dopaminergic degeneration in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10466669/
PMID: 10466669
DOI: 10.1016/S0140-6736(99)01178-2
Publication date: 1999-08-21
Date basis: Lancet issue date in PubMed
Source depth: Primary author abstract
Format: Original-source factual extract

The authors report low dopaminergic transporter tracer uptake in one patient with Lewy body dementia later confirmed at autopsy. The abstract gives neither a visual outcome nor an exact match to a case in the later 20-autopsy study. The two reports share investigators. Case-level independence remains unverified and the study gives no dried-fig comparison.
