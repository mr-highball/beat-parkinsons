Title: Usefulness of pallidotomy in advanced Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9048711/
PMID: 9048711
PMCID: PMC486722
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.62.2.125
Publication date: 1997-02
Coverage: Factual extract from the author abstract and verified official archive identity. The historical article body is open.

The study evaluated pallidotomy in 22 people with advanced levodopa-sensitive Parkinson disease undergoing concurrent medical management. The authors used timed movement, video and optoelectronic measures before and after surgery. This provides early surgical and motor measurement context. The abstract does not present an isolated dietary comparison or evidence assigning raw figs for rest tremor or dried dates for pain severity.
