Title: Usefulness of pallidotomy in advanced Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC486722/
PMID: 9048711
PMCID: PMC486722
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.62.2.125
Publication date: 1997-02
Citation: Johansson F, Malm J, Nordh E, Hariz M. Journal of Neurology, Neurosurgery & Psychiatry 62(2), 125–132.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 125–132.

Twenty-two participants with advanced levodopa-sensitive Parkinson disease were evaluated around pallidotomy under concurrent medical management. Printed page 126 separates resting tremor, dyskinesia, and other video-rated items. On page 130 and in Table 2, maximum axial tremor values fell significantly at four months, while median values showed an inconclusive tendency. This is a surgical comparison and did not assign figs or report a dried-date pain effect.
