Amar and Wilcock contrast vascular dementia with other dementias, including dementia with Lewy bodies and Parkinson disease dementia. The diagnostic box uses early prominent visual hallucinations as one contextual feature. The review reports no new symptom-frequency or dried-fig comparison.
