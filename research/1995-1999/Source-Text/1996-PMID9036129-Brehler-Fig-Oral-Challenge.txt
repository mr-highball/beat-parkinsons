Title: Ficus benjamina allergy
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9036129/
PMID: 9036129
DOI: 10.1007/s001050050509
Publication date: 1996-10
Coverage: Paraphrased original English-language author abstract. The original journal title is Ficus benjamina Allergie.

The authors describe respiratory and eye symptoms attributed to an ornamental weeping fig, Ficus benjamina. Laboratory testing suggested reactivity to fruit allergens, but the patient tolerated figs during an oral challenge. The abstract leaves the fruit preparation and individual oral-observation fields open and supplies no graded drooling outcome. This case keeps laboratory sensitization and clinical food reactions distinct.
