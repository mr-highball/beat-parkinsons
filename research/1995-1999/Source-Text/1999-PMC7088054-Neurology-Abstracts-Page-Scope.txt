P489 (I/102) reports clinical histories for five patients with dementia with Lewy bodies seen prospectively by a surveillance unit. The related 2001 publication at PMID 11413259 reports larger diagnostic groups. Shared investigators and setting imply possible cohort overlap. P495 (I/103) compares 54 Parkinson patients' cognitive, affect and motor trajectories over a mean 2.7 years. Early-onset hallucination history and matched controls had similar cognitive change. Late-onset history was associated with greater cognitive and affect decline. This abstract references the 1997 Graham interview study. It does not establish individual overlap or enumerate visual episodes. P764 (I/154) reports 17 admissions for mixed acute psychiatric problems during combined medication changes, with both broadly rated improvement and one adverse evening hallucination report. The modality and recurrence rate are unspecified. None of these comparisons measured dried-fig intake.
