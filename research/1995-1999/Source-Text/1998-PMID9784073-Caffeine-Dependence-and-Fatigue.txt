Title: Caffeine reinforcement and fatigue after blinded withdrawal
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9784073/
PMID: 9784073
DOI: 10.1007/s002130050704
Publication date: 1998-10
Date basis: Original issue month.
Review depth: Original author abstract. Full item statistics and cohort provenance remain open.

Participants completed counterbalanced caffeine and placebo maintenance periods of 9–12 days, then took caffeine or placebo in each condition. The authors report more withdrawal symptoms including fatigue after placebo following caffeine maintenance than in the other study conditions. The principal reinforcing outcome examined choices about receiving the drug or money.

Without the original fatigue table, the reported symptom sign is contextual. The comparisons used isolated caffeine and prolonged protocol-controlled maintenance. They do not isolate brewed coffee, brewed green tea or Parkinson fatigue. Cohort overlap with other reports from the same study group was not excluded.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
