Title: Effect of chronic oral domperidone therapy on gastrointestinal symptoms and gastric emptying in patients with Parkinson's disease
PMID: 9399220
DOI: 10.1002/mds.870120618
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9399220/
Original PDF copy: https://pillbuys.com/research/Domperidone/12.pdf
Published: 1997-11, Movement Disorders 12, 952–957.
Archive scope: Factual summary from the six-page original, Figures 1–2 and Methods.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Eleven Parkinson patients with GI symptoms received domperidone in an uncontrolled longitudinal series. The solid-meal test measured two-hour gastric retention at 60.2 ± 6.4% at baseline and 37.0 ± 2.2% on treatment, reported as mean ± SEM, p < .05. Several upper-GI symptom scores improved. Dysphagia and constipation did not show a clear change on the four-category symptom scale. Patients began at different disease severities and received clinically adjusted doses. The gastric measure and bowel result are separable. This cohort predates the 2018 active-comparator MRI and 2020 PASS-GI studies and provides no ginger or dried-date comparison.
