Unstimulated flow in Parkinson disease

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10442250/
Coverage: Original author abstract, factual extract.

Clinical Neuropharmacology 22(4):213–215. The study compared unstimulated salivary flow in 83 Parkinson patients and 65 matched controls. Eight patients were untreated and 75 received combined dopaminergic therapy. Flow was reduced in the Parkinson cohort. The authors discuss swallowing as a possible explanation for excess-saliva complaints. No oral culinary intervention was assigned.
