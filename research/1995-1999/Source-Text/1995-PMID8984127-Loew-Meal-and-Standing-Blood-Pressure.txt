Title: Postprandial hypotension and orthostatic blood pressure responses in elderly Parkinson's disease patients
PMID: 8984127
DOI: 10.1097/00004872-199511000-00011
Publication: Journal of Hypertension, November 1995, volume 13, pp. 1291–1297
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8984127/
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original author abstract

Loew and colleagues compare ten older patients with Parkinson disease with ten age-matched controls. The reported supine systolic blood-pressure change after eating was a drop of 27 mm Hg in the Parkinson group and 8 mm Hg in controls. Standing and tilt tests were done before and after lunch on the following day. Prior lunch did not amplify the orthostatic decrease in this experiment. The postprandial and orthostatic responses were only moderately correlated.

Meal composition and individual food effects are unavailable in the located author abstract. This comparison reports measured pressure rather than a count of falls.
