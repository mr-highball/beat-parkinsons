Title: Brain perfusion scintigraphy with 99mTc-HMPAO or 99mTc-ECD and 123I-beta-CIT single-photon emission tomography in dementia of the Alzheimer-type and diffuse Lewy body disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9143472/
PMID: 9143472
DOI: 10.1007/BF01728771
Publication date: 1997-03
Date basis: European Journal of Nuclear Medicine March issue
Source depth: Primary author abstract
Format: Original-source factual extract

The authors studied seven people with probable diffuse Lewy body disease and six with probable Alzheimer-type dementia. They report different brain perfusion patterns and lower striatal transporter tracer binding in the probable Lewy body group. They mention visual and auditory hallucinations as possible features in background. The abstract does not give symptom episode counts by imaging group or evaluate dried figs.
