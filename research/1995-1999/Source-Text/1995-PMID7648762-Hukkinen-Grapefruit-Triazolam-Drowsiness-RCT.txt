Title: Plasma concentrations of triazolam are increased by concomitant ingestion of grapefruit juice.
PMID: 7648762
DOI: 10.1016/0009-9236(95)90188-4
First published: 1995
Authors: Hukkinen SK, Varhe A, Olkkola KT, Neuvonen PJ
Journal: Clinical pharmacology and therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7648762/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized crossover trial in 10 healthy subjects taking oral triazolam 0.25 mg with 250 mL grapefruit juice vs water. Grapefruit juice increased triazolam AUC 1.5-fold (p<0.001) and Cmax 1.3-fold (p<0.05), delayed tmax from 1.6 to 2.5 h (p<0.05), and SIGNIFICANTLY ENHANCED DROWSINESS (p<0.05). Direct human RCT evidence that grapefruit worsens sedative-induced drowsiness via CYP3A4 inhibition.
