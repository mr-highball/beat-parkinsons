Title: Pharmacokinetics of Hoasca alkaloids in healthy humans
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10404423/

PMID: 10404423

Publication date: 1999-06
Date basis: NLM issue date
Source depth: Published abstract and publisher-indexed context
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The investigators measured four alkaloids in a multi-plant beverage and in blood from 15 healthy volunteers after use in a ceremonial setting. Pharmacokinetic measures were compared with timed psychoactive observations. The report describes earlier laboratory assay and participant-characterization papers from the broader investigation. Those publications require cohort linkage before they can be used as independent observations.

This study establishes measured combined exposure in a documented human setting. The participants were healthy, the endpoint was pharmacokinetic and the exposure was a ceremonial beverage. It does not test the 2001 single-vine extract in Parkinson disease, evaluate dried figs, or estimate how often people with Parkinson disease experience visual hallucinations.
