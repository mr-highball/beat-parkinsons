Title: An alternative medicine treatment for Parkinson's disease: results of a multicenter clinical trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9395621/
Publisher: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1089/acm.1995.1.249
PMID: 9395621
DOI: 10.1089/acm.1995.1.249
Publication date: 1995
Date basis: NLM issue in 1995
Source depth: Primary published abstract
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The open 12-week study evaluated the Mucuna-derived HP-200 powder in 60 people with Parkinson disease. Twenty-six had used levodopa/carbidopa before enrollment and 34 were levodopa-naive. The authors report lower motor and stage scores relative to baseline and predominantly gastrointestinal adverse effects. No concurrent comparator is identified in the abstract, and visual hallucinations or a separate visual event count are not provided.

The branded powder differs from the 2014 commercial Zandopa used in fruit flies and from subsequent randomized Mucuna formulations. Direct composition comparability and the detailed original tables remain open.
