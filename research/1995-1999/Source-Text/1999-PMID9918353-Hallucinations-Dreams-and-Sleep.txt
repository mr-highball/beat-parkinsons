Title: Hallucinations, sleep fragmentation, and altered dream phenomena in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9918353/
PMID: 9918353
Publication date: 1999-01
Date basis: Movement Disorders 14(1), 117–121, January issue
Source depth: Primary author abstract
Format: Original-source factual extract

Pappert and colleagues examined overlapping symptom categories among Parkinson outpatients. They report 26% with hallucinations or illusions, 48% with altered dream phenomena and 62% with sleep fragmentation. Altered dreams were associated with hallucinations or illusions, while the sleep-fragmentation and hallucination/illusion categories were not associated in the model. The abstract combines hallucinations with illusions and gives no dried-fig comparison or separate visual episode rate.
