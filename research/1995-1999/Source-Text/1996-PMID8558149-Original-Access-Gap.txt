Robertson and Hammerstad tested eight patients with Parkinson disease and 11 controls. Tests included voluntary jaw movements, clenching and rhythmic gum chewing. Before versus after usual medication in the patient group, some jaw measures improved and some deteriorated. The exam and discussion distinguish jaw movement deficits from tenderness and pain on palpation.

The comparison concerns medication state and jaw kinematics. It provides no dried-date exposure or clinical pain response to dates. All original pages 41–50 were inspected.
