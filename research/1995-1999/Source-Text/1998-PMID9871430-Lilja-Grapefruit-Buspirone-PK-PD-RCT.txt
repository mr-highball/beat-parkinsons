Title: Grapefruit juice substantially increases plasma concentrations of buspirone.
PMID: 9871430
DOI: 10.1016/S0009-9236(98)90056-X
First published: 1998
Authors: Lilja JJ, Kivistö KT, Backman JT, Lamberg TS, Neuvonen PJ
Journal: Clinical pharmacology and therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9871430/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized 2-phase crossover trial in 10 healthy volunteers taking 10 mg oral buspirone with double-strength grapefruit juice vs water. Buspirone Cmax increased 4.3-fold (p<0.01) and AUC 9.2-fold (range 3.0-20.4-fold, p<0.01), with significant increase in subjective overall drug effect (p<0.01) though individual psychomotor subtests were mixed.
