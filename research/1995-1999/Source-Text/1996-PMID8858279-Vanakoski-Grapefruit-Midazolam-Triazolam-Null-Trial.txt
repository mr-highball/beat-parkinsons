Title: Grapefruit juice does not enhance the effects of midazolam and triazolam in man.
PMID: 8858279
DOI: 10.1007/s002280050148
First published: 1996
Authors: Vanakoski J, Mattila MJ, Seppälä T
Journal: European journal of clinical pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8858279/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Three-part study in healthy young students evaluating single-dose oral midazolam 10 mg and triazolam 0.25 mg with grapefruit juice vs water assessed at 30-90 min. Concluded single-dose grapefruit juice did NOT enhance subjective drowsiness or plasma concentrations meaningfully (though DSS at 90 min was slightly more impaired with GraMid vs Mid, p<0.05). Key competing null trial tempering single-dose CYP3A4 sedation claims.
