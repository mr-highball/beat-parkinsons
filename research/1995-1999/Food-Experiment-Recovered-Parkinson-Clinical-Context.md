## UPDRS items in Parkinson and Lewy body dementia

Gnanalingham and colleagues compared four diagnostic groups. Table 2 gives individual items for rigidity, bradykinesia and falls. The Parkinson group's median falls rating was 1, with a reported range of 0–2.5. This was an ordinal at-assessment item in a cross-sectional cohort. The study did not count visual hallucination episodes or assign a food exposure. ## Visual hallucinosis reports and medication intoxication

Graham and colleagues interviewed 129 people with idiopathic Parkinson’s disease. Thirty-two described hallucinosis or illusions and 30 of those 32 reported a visual component. The analysis includes four with illusions alone, 21 with hallucinations alone and seven with both. The figures concern interviewed symptom occurrence and clinical factors. They do not provide a longitudinal episode rate. A separate 1999 case letter discusses an individual with parkinsonism and visual hallucinations in the setting of acute medication intoxication. The letter’s author-uploaded file includes adjoining publications. The exact contribution is bounded by its title on page 122 and conclusion on page 123.
