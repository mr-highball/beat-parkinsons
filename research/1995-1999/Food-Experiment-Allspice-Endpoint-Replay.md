# Allspice preparation and endpoint search

## Attention instructions during linalool and eugenol odor stimulation

Krauel and colleagues published Experiment 1 in August 1998. Six men received linalool and eugenol through an olfactometer in an odor oddball experiment. Attention was manipulated with instructions to address the rare odor or to attend a distractor task. Attending produced shorter early chemosensory component latencies and larger late positivities. Those are effects of attention allocation on odor-evoked signals. Odor-specific clinical attention worsening, oral ground allspice and a Parkinson population were outside the experiment. The paper reports a separate body-odor experiment that did not administer eugenol. ## Injected Pimenta leaf extract and acute CNS depression

Suárez Urhan, Ulate Montero and Cicció reported a concentrated Pimenta dioica leaf extract in the 1996–1997 Revista de Biología Tropical joint issue, indexed March 1997. Their acute intraperitoneal screening recorded dose-related reductions in activity, loss of righting, altered alarm response and hypothermia, with peritoneal irritation as a possible contributor. Their separate 14-day oral aqueous-fraction comparison tracked systolic pressure, heart rate and weight without a reported change in those measures. The quantified behavioral signs belong to the injected cohort. The paper supplies no natural REM measures, clinical attention or hallucination ratings, speech or handwriting data. [Primary Spanish Methods and Results](https://tropicalstudies.org/rbt/attachments/volumes/vol44-3A/06_Suarez_Pimenta_dioica.pdf) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/1997-Suarez-Pimenta-Leaf-Route-and-CNS-Screen.txt)

## University of Costa Rica thesis and route specific outcome tables

Adriana Suárez Urhan completed this University of Costa Rica biomedical sciences thesis in 1995. The institutional repository holds the original scan and provider-generated text. The oral protocol in the Spanish manuscript (pp. 17–18) randomized 24 rats by normotensive or hypertensive group, then assigned four per strain to each of two extract levels or distilled water. Oral delivery used an intragastric cannula. Weight, systolic blood pressure and heart rate were measured at three times during 14 days. The Hippocratic behavioral screen used a separate injected-extract protocol. The thesis and the later 1997 article have the same lead author, strains, extract fractions, oral group structure and schedule. Their general-effect data may be from the same experiments. The university catalog dates the thesis to 1995 and the Costa Rican national bibliography lists it in the 1996 volume. The institutional record was accessioned digitally later.
