# Nonmotor and Daily Life for 1995–1999

### 1995 — Incident dementia in community-dwelling Parkinson’s disease

Marder and colleagues followed 140 nondemented people with Parkinson’s disease and 572 nondemented controls. Incident dementia was more common in the Parkinson’s group. Greater motor impairment and higher depression scores predicted dementia in this cohort. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7619026/)

### 1996 — Parkinsonian disorders after idiopathic REM sleep behavior disorder

Schenck and colleagues followed 29 older men initially diagnosed with idiopathic REM sleep behavior disorder. Eleven later developed a parkinsonian disorder. The selected male sleep-clinic series included more than one diagnosis and did not estimate risk for the general population. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8614500/)
