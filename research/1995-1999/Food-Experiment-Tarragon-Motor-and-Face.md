# Tarragon motor and facial outcome follow-up

## Isolated estragole and evoked skeletal muscle responses

Albuquerque and colleagues compared Croton zehntneri oil and separate anethole or estragole exposures in isolated skeletal muscle preparations. The experiments describe changes in transmission and contractile behavior. Estragole is an isolated constituent found in some tarragon varieties. The study neither used tarragon as its source oil nor measured patterned dystonia, speech or gait in a living animal.
