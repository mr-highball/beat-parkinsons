# Clinical and Diagnosis for 1995–1999

### 1995 — A patient-reported outcome measure for Parkinson’s disease

Peto and colleagues developed the 39-item Parkinson’s Disease Questionnaire (PDQ-39) and assessed it with postal surveys. The authors reported satisfactory reliability and construct validity. The questionnaire added patient-reported functioning and well-being to motor and clinician-rated outcomes. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7613534/)

### 1996 — First international consensus criteria for dementia with Lewy bodies

McKeith and colleagues proposed clinical and pathological criteria for dementia with Lewy bodies. The workshop explicitly identified the relation between that diagnosis and later dementia in Parkinson’s disease as unresolved. This is a consensus document for an overlapping syndrome. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8909416/)

### 1997 — Community screening for Parkinson’s disease in Europe

De Rijk and colleagues combined in-person surveys of 14,636 people aged 65 and older from five European populations. Parkinson’s prevalence increased with age, and screening identified previously unrecognized cases. Prevalence estimates depended on diagnostic criteria, age distribution, and case ascertainment. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9010393/)
