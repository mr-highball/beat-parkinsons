# Portuguese-language dietary studies, 1995–1999

## Dietary iron in parkinsonism conference record

The University of São Paulo lists Paula, Farhud and Marucci’s 1999 report on dietary iron in parkinsonism in the *Ciências biológicas e agropecuária* proceedings of its scientific initiation symposium. The repository page identifies the proceedings and authors. The abstract body, population, methods and results remain unrecovered. [University record](https://repositorio.usp.br/item/001177146) · [OpenAlex W3168630409](https://openalex.org/W3168630409)

The USP repository’s RIS export has no abstract or page field, and the linked Dedalus route did not yield a readable copy. The 2007 *Nutrire* minerals review cites the later Farhud and Marucci diet study. It does not cite the 1999 Paula abstract or supply its iron results. The diet study is documented in the [2001 thesis follow-up](../2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Portuguese-Primary-Followup.md#farhud-dietary-assessment-at-usp).
