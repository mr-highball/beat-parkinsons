# Recovered original reports of Parkinson symptoms and clinical context

## Caregiver-rated apathy in Parkinson dementia and Alzheimer disease

October 1, 1996. Starkstein et al., JNNP 61(4), 381–387. Investigators compared 33 patients with Parkinson dementia and 33 with probable Alzheimer disease. The Methods on p. 383 specify the 14-item caregiver apathy scale (0–42). Table 2, p. 384 reports mean 17.8 (SD 7.9) in Parkinson dementia and 15.5 (11.1) in probable Alzheimer disease. The difference was not identified as statistically significant. An Alzheimer-group eligibility criterion on p. 382 specifies zero UPDRS items for bradykinesia, rigidity and tremor. This does not estimate those separate outcomes in the Parkinson cohort. Table 1 reports a total motor score. The study supplies apathy measurement context without a date-fruit comparison. ## Dementia outcomes in a follow-back survey

May–June 1998. Losonczy et al., Public Health Reports 113(3), 273–280. The sample consists of 1,227 next-of-kin interviews, used in this report to analyze dementia and function. Printed p. 274 describes great pain at death among the aims of the parent survey. This report gives no separate pain-severity comparison or fall-frequency result. The publication is in the 1998 issue. The paper helps distinguish survey aims from reported outcomes for mapped pain and fall claims.
