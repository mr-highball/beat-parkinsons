# Food experiment evidence for sumac and cardamom

## 1996 Cardamom seed oil and experimental pain

Al-Zuhair and colleagues tested extracted oil from commercial *Elettaria cardamomum* seed. The oil reduced p-benzoquinone-induced writhing in mice. The authors estimated 50% protection at 233 μL/kg. They also reported inhibition of carrageenan-induced paw edema in rats and relaxation of isolated rabbit intestine. These outcomes give an opposite-direction pain lead for 0363 using oil and acute chemically evoked animal pain. The study did not assess Parkinson-related pain or ground cardamom. [Primary report, PMID 8981560](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8981560/)
