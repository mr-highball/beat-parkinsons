# Bay leaf symptom evidence

## Gastric emptying in an ethanol-loaded rat experiment

Yoshikawa and colleagues’ September 1999 study of *Laurus nobilis* measured blood ethanol after ethanol loading in rats. Leaf methanol extract inhibited the rise in blood ethanol, and experiments with bay-leaf sesquiterpenes linked the effect in part to slower gastric emptying. The study measured neither levodopa exposure nor medicine OFF duration. It supplies a route-specific gastrointestinal lead for 0036. [Primary report, PMID 10509909](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10509909/)
