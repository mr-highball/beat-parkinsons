# Meal context and perceived motor response

The [1991 breakfast comparison](../../1990-1994/Source-Text/1991-PMID1866021-Carbohydrate-Protein-Meal-Crossover.txt) separately reports general motor experience and pegboard performance after different nutrient mixtures. In the 1995–1999 bucket, a [standardized liquid meal study](../Source-Text/1997-PMID9399210-Meal-Hypotension-and-Parkinsonisms.txt) links post-meal blood-pressure decline with worsening on a motor composite. The [1998 mailing-list account](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/1998-PARKINSN-Turkey-and-Perceived-Medication-Effect.txt) gives a named food, repeated snacking and perceived medication changes. The clinical comparison and conversation leave a separate resting-tremor outcome unresolved. These records support tracking autonomic, medication and symptom effects as distinct observations.
