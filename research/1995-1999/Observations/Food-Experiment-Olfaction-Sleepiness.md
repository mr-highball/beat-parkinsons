# Diagnostic odors and culinary exposures

The 1997 mixed-odor UPSIT documents which scents were presented and compared in people with Parkinson’s. The existing pizza and wintergreen result and the newly checked cinnamon, clove, onion and grape labels come from the same study. Its setup tests identification and gives no time course after food consumption. [Source](../Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#cinnamon-and-other-odor-cues-in-the-1997-upsit-comparison)
