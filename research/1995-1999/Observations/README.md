# Observations for 1995–1999

The [1995–1998 visual outcome review](1995-1998-Visual-Symptom-and-Access-Scope.md) links the historical differential diagnoses, clinical event definitions and remaining original full-text gaps.

The [1999 poster and archive notes](1999-Hallucination-Poster-and-Edition-Scope.md) describe longitudinal cognition, episode definitions and original journal review identities.

## Major observations

- [Family genetics and human tissue](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md) converged on alpha-synuclein. The filament results refined the earlier hypothesis about what forms Lewy-body filaments. Parkin mutations identified a separate inherited juvenile syndrome.
- [Motor treatment options](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) expanded to newer dopamine agonists and COMT inhibitors. Tolcapone’s postmarketing liver injury reports added a serious harm that the preapproval trial had limited power to detect.
- [Surgical evidence](../Procedures-and-Devices.md) included subthalamic stimulation with effects on several motor signs and a randomized delayed-surgery comparison for pallidotomy. Both lines documented serious adverse events.

## Minor observations

- The lemon-balm infusion measurement distinguishes extracted polyphenols from essential oil. Source chemistry alone provides no depression estimate. [Leaf and infusion comparison](../Food-Experiment-Rosemary-Lemon-Balm.md#composition-of-dried-leaf-and-lemon-balm-infusion)

- Laboratory work added a canned comparator, while the clinical allergy report documented reactions after a mixed bean meal and reactivity to a canned extract. These outcomes extend the previous bucket's gastrointestinal case history to distinct exposures and mechanisms. [Lentil and kidney bean evidence](../Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md)
- [Rhythmic cues, intensive speech treatment, and axial exercise](../Movement-and-Rehabilitation.md) changed specific functional measurements. Trial size, treatment duration, and follow-up differed.
- [Studies of caregivers and service access](../Community-and-Care.md) measured effects extending beyond the clinic visit. [The PDQ-39](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md) captured patient-reported health domains.
- [REM sleep behavior disorder follow-up](../Nonmotor-and-Daily-Life.md) linked dream enactment with later parkinsonian disorders in a selected clinic sample, opening a longitudinal research question.
- The [vitamin E, coffee, and food results](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) depended on exposure source and study design. Cross-sectional dietary vitamin E was inversely associated with prevalent Parkinson’s disease in Rotterdam, while prospectively recorded total vitamin E was null in Honolulu.
- The Toneyama survey recorded post-meal choking in 22% of participants with Parkinson’s and 6% of family controls. The five-day food history was summarized as nutrients in the author abstract, leaving food-level responses open. [Questionnaire and food-record study](../Food-Experiment-Japanese-Swallowing-Followup.md#post-meal-choking-and-food-records-at-toneyama)

## Comparison with [1990–1994](../../1990-1994/Observations/README.md)

- The 1993 liquid-meal study found a fall in supine pressure after eating and no fall elicited by head-up tilt in the Parkinson group. The 1995 comparison also found a supine decrease and reported a smaller orthostatic drop after lunch. A separate 1997 liquid-meal study found postural intolerance with presyncopal symptoms. [1993 challenge](../../1990-1994/Food-and-Nutrition.md#1993--liquid-meal-challenge-and-head-up-tilt) · [1995 comparison](../Food-and-Nutrition.md#1995--lunch-and-orthostatic-blood-pressure-in-older-adults) · [1997 motor and presyncope report](../Food-and-Nutrition.md#1997--post-meal-presyncope-and-aggregate-motor-scores)
- Earlier thalamic stimulation targeted tremor. Subthalamic stimulation reports assessed bradykinesia, rigidity, gait, and dyskinesia as well. Pallidotomy moved from uncontrolled follow-up into a randomized comparison.
- DATATOP’s earlier vitamin E trial addressed supplemental treatment after diagnosis. The later food studies addressed diet and occurrence of disease. Their mixed observations leave any incidence effect uncertain. The 1995 UK selegiline combination mortality signal was absent in the 1998 DATATOP analysis, with important differences in treatment and trial design.
- The 1992 community dementia investigation measured prevalence. The later prospective study followed people without dementia and observed incident cases. A consensus for dementia with Lewy bodies defined a related clinical and pathological research population.
- A controlled-release study in already fluctuating patients reported better control in 1992. The five-year study in patients starting levodopa found no significant reduction in subsequent complications from starting with a controlled-release formulation.
- The 1997 Spanish spa communication discussed shifting protein intake to the evening to manage fluctuations. It gives no clinical data, while the preceding period’s broad-bean feeding study measured levodopa and observed acute motor effects. The spa source concerns dietary protein timing and the earlier feeding study concerns a food that contains levodopa. [Spa communication](../Food-and-Nutrition.md#1997--diet-during-a-parkinsons-spa-regimen) · [1992 feeding study](../../1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#cooked-broad-beans-and-acute-motor-response)
- Kurihara’s 1993 series reported more perceived swallowing difficulty with longer duration or more advanced Parkinson’s. Nozaki’s 1999 questionnaire found no clear association with Hoehn–Yahr stage. The earlier report used perceived difficulty and imaged a subset. The later report counted any of six symptoms following food intake. [Chiba series](../../1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Japanese-Swallowing-Followup.md#swallowing-symptoms-fluoroscopy-and-manometry-in-chiba) · [Toneyama survey](../Food-Experiment-Japanese-Swallowing-Followup.md#post-meal-choking-and-food-records-at-toneyama)

- The 1993 healthy-volunteer comparison reported higher hypopharyngeal pressure and longer contractions with mashed potato than with water. In 1998 the same Wisconsin research team administered mashed potato to a Parkinson cohort, but the published pressure table reports only water. The two reports leave the pressure response to mashed potato in Parkinson disease unresolved. [1993 healthy comparison](../../1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#mashed-potato-and-pharyngeal-pressure-in-healthy-adults) · [1998 Parkinson report](../Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#mashed-potato-in-a-wisconsin-parkinson-manometry-study)

- The 1995 formula trial measured stool outcomes after a mixed-oil and mixed-fiber diet. The earlier safflower capsule study recorded bloating as an adverse event. The formula trial added direct bowel measures while leaving individual oil effects unresolved. [1995 formula](../Food-Experiment-Canola-Safflower.md#canola-and-high-oleic-safflower-oil-in-defined-formula-diets) · [1986 capsule trial](../../1985-1989/Food-Experiment-Canola-Safflower.md)

- The gum experiment introduced a distinction between saliva production and saliva escaping the mouth that later Parkinson swallowing work can test. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Celery-Spearmint.md#1995-spearmint-flavored-gum-and-saliva-production)

- A chronic-fatigue randomized trial identified sunflower oil as a comparator in a study extraction. Its physical-symptom result supplies an early indirect fatigue comparison. The published outcome does not isolate a sunflower effect, and extraction grade was not verified. [Dated research note](../Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md)

- The Parkinson meal study separated supine postprandial pressure from orthostatic changes, which moved differently. The aging rat study reported no separate spinach advantage on stationary rod balance. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Leafy-Grains.md)

- The shell-chitosan feeding study recorded fecal weight and moisture in eight healthy men. Weight increased numerically at both doses, with inconclusive estimates and no parallel control. This is an extracted material exposure. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Trout-Sardine-Shrimp.md#shell-chitosan-and-stool-measures)

- Turkey and chicken allergy cases documented a separate pathway through cross-reactive IgE. The acute cases do not identify an orthostatic Parkinson outcome. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Turkey-Shiitake-Arugula.md#1998-turkey-and-chicken-meat-allergy-cases)

- The April 1999 NIH report identifies flax oil as the dietary ALA source and gives an odor discrimination effect with similar general motor activity. The matching second-generation findings in the 1999 paper and 2001 report count as one study family. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md)

- A meal-replacement patent names safflower oil and reports Parkinson cases. The detailed formula numbers show that the patients received different oils, so the human outcome cannot be assigned to safflower. [Patent exposure check](../Food-Experiment-Safflower-Oil-Endpoints.md#kingham-parkinson-meal-replacement-patent)

## Food experiment follow-up, November 14, 2026

- The earlier archive reports concerned mechanically lodged whole leaves. Controlled ethanol experiments introduced extract-specific gastric-emptying results without a levodopa measure. [gastric emptying in an ethanol loaded rat experiment](../Food-Experiment-Bay-Leaf-Followup.md#gastric-emptying-in-an-ethanol-loaded-rat-experiment)

- The tarragon panel study used the oil as a coded scent and recorded familiarity and odor labels. Those measures provide separate context for later recognition and discrimination work. [Tarragon panel study](../Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Odor-Methods.md)

- Compared with the preceding raw onion and workplace studies, the lightly fried yellow onion study standardized a real culinary exposure but measured only plasma and urinary compounds. A separate 1999 dysphonia study used multi-food skin provocations with onion-specific results still unresolved. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Onion-Speech.md)

- A Japanese family reported garlic benefits for hereditary paroxysmal attacks. Compared with the earlier Parkinson meal cases, both the diagnosis and reported exposure differ. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md)

- Dietary mouse performance depended on the rotarod task. Human allergy cohorts added a distinct oral oil challenge and pooled reports of hoarseness. The oil grade needed for the frozen claims remains unresolved. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Peanut-Oil-PubMed.md)

- The powdered caraway was a prick-test preparation. This verifies physical form for diagnostic exposure and leaves the culinary oral comparison open. [Caraway follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Caraway-Replay.md)

- Odor attention instructions altered chemosensory responses when linalool and eugenol were stimuli. This study supplies a human electrophysiology baseline and no direct test of attention severity after ground allspice. [Allspice outcome follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Allspice-Endpoint-Replay.md)
- The recovered 1995 thesis and 1997 paper use matching rat strata and leaf-extract protocols. Acute reductions in activity and loss of righting belong to the injected Hippocratic screen. The separate oral arm measured vitals and weight, so the direction of oral effects on behavioral symptoms remains open. [Allspice outcome follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Allspice-Endpoint-Replay.md)

- Acute tremor after isolated capsaicin in a toxicology experiment and paprika-allergy symptoms are different clinical questions from usual dietary exposure. [Paprika outcome follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Paprika-Gap-Replay.md)

- Isolated estragole changed experimentally evoked muscle responses. Such tissue assays leave clinical dystonia after edible tarragon unresolved. [Tarragon outcome follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Motor-and-Face.md)
