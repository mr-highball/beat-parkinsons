# Printed clinical records and diagnostic tremor

Earlier NLM XML contained metadata and lacked bodies for these historical sources. Official PMC scan carousels now supply 66 original journal pages from 11 records, and the original publisher PDF supplies the mitochondrial case. The complete records show which symptoms were examined and which food terms came from a figure reference, date field, or surname.

The [1993 imaging study](../../1990-1994/Source-Text/1993-PMID8265656-Striatal-Transporter-Abstract.txt) uses motor symptoms as diagnostic criteria and analyzes transporter imaging. The [1994 secondary-parkinsonism letter](../../1990-1994/Source-Text/PMC1072944.txt) separates resting tremor from action and postural components in one case. The [1997 pallidotomy study](../Source-Text/1997-PMID9048711-Pallidotomy-Abstract.txt) separately assesses resting tremor and distinguishes maximum from median ratings. The contrast is surgery under concurrent clinical management. These distinctions keep an imaging, diagnostic or surgical result from being read as a trial of fig consumption.

The [1988 secondary-parkinsonism report](../../1985-1989/Source-Text/PMC1032927.txt) documents observed drooling and swallowing difficulty in the same case. Its clinical drug-response claim concerns bradykinesia and eyelid opening. The [1984 pathology paper](../../1980-1984/Source-Text/1984-PMID6512549-Dentatorubral-Clinical-Abstract.txt) reports narrative visual hallucinations from an earlier case with a different disorder. Keep outcome presence, quantitative measurement, and diagnosis separate.
