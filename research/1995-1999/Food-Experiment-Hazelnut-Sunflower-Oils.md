# Hazelnut and sunflower oil evidence

## Sunflower oil comparator in a chronic fatigue trial

Warren and colleagues published in February 1999. Fifty adults with chronic fatigue syndrome were randomized to an evening primrose and fish oil preparation or a sunflower-oil capsule comparator over three months. The journal reports no significant treatment difference in physical symptoms or depression. The original public abstract does not name the comparator oil, while a university evidence report identifies sunflower oil and notes vitamin E and trace riboflavin in both arms. The trial supplies no Parkinson cohort, standalone oil-versus-no-oil comparison or known refining grade.
