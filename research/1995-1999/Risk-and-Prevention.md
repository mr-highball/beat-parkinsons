# Risk and Prevention for 1995–1999

### 1999 — Twin concordance and age at onset

Tanner and colleagues examined monozygotic and dizygotic male twin pairs. Concordance was similar overall, while the small subgroup with onset at or before 50 showed higher concordance in identical twins. The design had limited power for specific genetic effects and drew from a male veteran registry. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9929087/)
