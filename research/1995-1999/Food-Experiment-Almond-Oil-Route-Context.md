# Meal timing and almond oil context

## Controlled-release levodopa crossover with a standard meal

The July 1998 publisher abstract describes an intrasubject crossover with eight Parkinson participants already taking levodopa. The study compared giving the same controlled-release tablet 30 minutes after the meal with giving it two hours before the meal. It measured tapping, walking speed and plasma levels every 20 minutes over six hours. With post-meal dosing, motor response began later and lasted less time. The largest peak concentration did not clearly differ. The six-hour area under the curve averaged 24% lower in the fed condition, with two later peaks and incomplete observation of absorption identified by the authors as contributing factors. This acute response profile does not give daily OFF hours.

The full meal ingredient list and original correction on volume 54, issue 7, page 577, were not recovered. The [publisher abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s002280050464) contains methods and pharmacokinetic details missing from the [current PubMed abstract and erratum record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9696954/). Neither accessible record specifies almond oil. The [local earlier extract](Source-Text/1998-Contin-Levodopa-Meal-Motor-Response.txt) documents the retained source chain.
