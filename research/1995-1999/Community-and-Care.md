# Community and Care for 1995–1999

### 1996 — Health and social effects on spouses who provide care

O’Reilly and colleagues compared spouses caring for people with Parkinson’s disease with controls. Providing more care was associated with fewer social activities, and higher care intensity with worse psychological and physical health measures. Observed associations can reflect other differences between groups. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8917868/)

### 1997 — Access to services in a Northampton community sample

Peto and colleagues surveyed people with Parkinson’s disease about health and access to services. A substantial minority reported access problems. Respondents dissatisfied with access tended to report worse health, with the direction of influence unresolved. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9134352/)
