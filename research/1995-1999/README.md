The [1995 fresh-fig allergy case](Source-Text/1995-PMID7573846-Dechamp-Fresh-Fig-Allergy.txt) describes an acute reaction after ingestion. The [1996 weeping-fig sensitization case](Source-Text/1996-PMID9036129-Brehler-Fig-Oral-Challenge.txt) reports a tolerated fruit challenge despite laboratory reactivity.

The [oyster mushroom cultivation comparison](Source-Text/1995-PMID7626144-Jwanny-Date-and-Mango-Waste-Mushroom.txt) and [date-waste fibre rat diet](Source-Text/1996-Jwanny-Date-Waste-Fibre-Rat-Feeding.txt) distinguish growth substrate, processed dietary material, and measured endpoints.

The [1995–1998 visual outcome review](Observations/1995-1998-Visual-Symptom-and-Access-Scope.md) links the historical differential diagnoses, clinical event definitions and remaining original full-text gaps.

The [1999 poster and archive notes](Observations/1999-Hallucination-Poster-and-Edition-Scope.md) describe longitudinal cognition, episode definitions and original journal review identities.

The [1999 Parkinson sleep and hallucination study](Source-Text/1999-PMID9918353-Hallucinations-Dreams-and-Sleep.txt) distinguishes sleep fragmentation and altered dreams from the combined hallucination and illusion category.

An [early clinical perfusion comparison](Source-Text/1997-PMID9143472-Lewy-Body-Perfusion-and-Transporter-Imaging.txt) and [autopsy-confirmed imaging case](Source-Text/1999-PMID10466669-FP-CIT-Lewy-Body-Autopsy-Case.txt) give antecedents to the [later visual history and pathology comparison](../2005-2009/Observations/2007-Visual-History-Symptom-Measurement-and-Proceedings.md).

The [1995 open HP-200 study](Source-Text/1995-PMID9395621-HP200-Mucuna-Open-Study.txt), [1996 ceremonial cohort](Source-Text/1996-PMID8596116-Hoasca-Ceremonial-Cohort.txt) and [dopamine agonist sleep-attack series](Source-Text/1999-PMID10371546-Dopamine-Agonist-Sleep-Attacks.txt) document distinct populations, preparations and outcomes.

The [1999 ceremonial account](Cultural-and-Experiential/Ceremonial-Accounts/1999-UDV-Hoasca-Research-Setting.txt) and [primary pharmacokinetic record](Source-Text/1999-PMID10404423-Hoasca-Ceremonial-Pharmacokinetics.txt) identify the healthy-volunteer setting and measured exposure.

A [Parkinson urinary salsolinol comparison](Source-Text/1996-PMID9617786-Urinary-Salsolinol-and-Parkinson-Visual-History-Abstract.txt) reports differences by visual hallucination status and cites the earlier dried banana feeding study.

The [1996 anatomical tracing paper](Source-Text/1996-PMID8710931-Basal-Ganglia-and-Temporal-Projection-Scans.txt) maps pathways from basal ganglia to temporal cortex. Its clinical interpretation refers to separately reported human observations.

Original printed context: [Usefulness of pallidotomy in advanced Parkinson’s disease](Source-Text/1997-PMID9048711-Pallidotomy-Abstract.txt) · [Cytochrome c oxidase deficiency associated with the first stop-codon point mutation in human mtDNA](Source-Text/1998-PMID9634511-Mitochondrial-Case-Abstract.txt). [Comparative observations](Observations/Printed-Clinical-Records-and-Diagnostic-Tremor.md).

[auditory gait cueing in Parkinson disease](Source-Text/PMC486690.txt).

The [blinded crude and refined peanut-oil challenge](Source-Text/1997-PMID9133891-Crude-and-Refined-Peanut-Oil-Challenge.txt) reports allergy outcomes. [Dietary lipid deficiency and rod performance](Source-Text/1995-PMID7475943-Frances-Peanut-Oil-Diet-and-Rotarod.txt) and [fish oil versus a peanut-rapeseed blend](Source-Text/1998-PMID9868201-Chalon-Peanut-Rapeseed-Comparator-and-Activity.txt) give distinct animal comparisons.
