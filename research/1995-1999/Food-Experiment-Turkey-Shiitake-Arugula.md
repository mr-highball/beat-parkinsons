# Turkey, shiitake, and arugula evidence

## 1998 Turkey and chicken meat allergy cases

Cahen and colleagues evaluated two people with histories of reactions after ingesting chicken and turkey. Skin testing and specific IgE were positive to both meats, and inhibition experiments indicated cross-reactivity. Both could eat egg products. The accessible record does not give a baked turkey protocol or assess Parkinson-related orthostatic lightheadedness, attention, speech, tremor or smell. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9756209/)
