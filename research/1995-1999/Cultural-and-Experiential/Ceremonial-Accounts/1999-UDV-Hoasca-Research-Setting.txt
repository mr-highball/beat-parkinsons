Date basis: NLM journal issue date. This is the publication date of the inspected account. The age and origin of the practices are separate historical questions.

Context: The primary paper documents the use of hoasca in a ceremonial setting and notes support from members of União do Vegetal in Brazil. The investigators studied a group of 15 healthy volunteers. Names or personal attributes at participant level are not reproduced here.

Reported direction: A documented practice accompanied by psychoactive observations. The source makes no claim about improving clinical Parkinson symptoms.

Authenticity: The original published abstract verifies a ceremonial context. The publisher summary corroborates community involvement. This record does not infer that local practices are uniform across groups or periods.

Efficacy evidence: The paper measures constituents and timed pharmacokinetics. Its healthy participants and outcomes do not test Parkinson symptoms or clinical efficacy. The single-vine extract tested in Parkinson patients in 2001 is a distinct preparation.
