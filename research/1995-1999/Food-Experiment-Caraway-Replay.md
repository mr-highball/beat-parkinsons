# Powdered caraway in clinical allergy testing

## Commercial powdered caraway used for skin testing

Niinimäki and colleagues used native commercial powdered spices including caraway for repeated skin prick testing in 49 patients. They compared native spices with extracts and serum-specific IgE. The powdered preparation provides a direct form match for grinding, while exposure was cutaneous. The accessible primary abstract gives aggregate clinical-symptom and testing information. It does not attribute an individual voice or speech outcome to an ingested caraway preparation.
