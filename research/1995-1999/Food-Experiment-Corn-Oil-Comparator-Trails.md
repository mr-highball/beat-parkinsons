# Corn oil comparisons for olfaction

## Corn oil injection comparator in a rat olfactory-loss experiment

Published April 10, 1999. Dhong and colleagues measured odor discrimination in 22 ovariectomized rats before and after a model olfactory injury. Daily injected estradiol in corn oil (12 rats) was compared with injected corn oil alone (10 rats). The author abstract reports faster recovery and better discrimination with estradiol. Corn oil was common to both arms. No oil-free contrast, refining information or Parkinson comparison is described. Arm-level effect estimates await the full paper.
