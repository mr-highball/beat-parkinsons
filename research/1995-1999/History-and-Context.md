# History and Context for 1995–1999

### 1997 — First World Parkinson’s Day and patient charter

The European Parkinson’s Disease Association and World Health Organization sponsored the first World Parkinson’s Day in April 1997. The organizer records the launch of a charter for people with Parkinson’s disease at the event, followed by a WHO working group. [Parkinson’s Europe institutional history](https://parkinsonseurope.org/campaigns/world-parkinsons-day/world-parkinsons-day-a-brief-history/)
