# Onion exposure and speech intelligibility

## Lightly fried yellow bulb and plasma flavonols

Aziz and colleagues gave five healthy volunteers 300 g of lightly fried yellow onion after three days on a low-flavonoid diet. The study reported timed plasma measurements of two conjugated flavonols and urinary excretion after ingestion. It verifies oral consumption of onion prepared by frying, with no speech test or Parkinson sample.
