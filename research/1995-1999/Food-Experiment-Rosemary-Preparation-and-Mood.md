# Rosemary aroma and reported mood

## Rosemary aroma compared with lavender

Diego and colleagues studied 40 adults before and after a brief lavender or rosemary aroma exposure. The original author abstract assigns the reported reduction in Profile of Mood States depressed mood to lavender. Its rosemary findings concern state anxiety, relaxation, alertness and calculation speed. The publisher body and separate rosemary depression values remain unverified. This source reports inhaled aromatherapy and cannot supply a clinical outcome after eating fresh rosemary.
