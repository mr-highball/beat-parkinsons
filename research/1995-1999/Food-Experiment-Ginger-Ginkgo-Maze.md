# Ginger Ginkgo Maze

## Dose dependent ginger and ginkgo mixture behavior in rats

Hasenohrl and colleagues tested a combined standardized ginger and ginkgo extract, administered intragastrically to rats. The lowest tested dose increased time and excursions on the open arms of an elevated plus maze. At the highest dose the animals made fewer excursions and scanned less. At 1 and 10 mg/kg the authors reported no significant maze effect. The comparison concerns combined extracts and a rodent behavior measure, with no isolated grated ginger or clinical anxiety result. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8808131/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/1996-02-Hasenohrl-Ginger-Ginkgo-Open-Arms.txt)

## Ginger and ginkgo mixture ratios and open arm behavior

Hasenohrl and colleagues compared standardized combined ginger and ginkgo extracts at three ratios in rats. At 1 mg/kg the standard 2.5 to 1 preparation increased time, scanning and excursions on the open arms. The 1 to 1 and 1 to 2.5 preparation did not clearly affect behavior over the tested range. The 1996 report had an inconclusive result at the same 1 mg/kg dose. Neither comparison supplies a patient anxiety measure or an isolated fresh grated ginger effect. Exact numerical arm estimates remain outside the accessible abstracts.
