# Clinical symptom measures and case boundaries

## Rest tremor and abulia after closed head injury

March 1, 1999. Doder and colleagues describe one patient with parkinsonism after closed-head trauma. The printed examination records a right-hand resting tremor alongside separately described postural tremor. Neuropsychological assessment describes abulia. An individual post-traumatic examination and laboratory reaction-time measures provide symptom context. The paper contains no edible fig or date intervention or parallel symptom comparison.
