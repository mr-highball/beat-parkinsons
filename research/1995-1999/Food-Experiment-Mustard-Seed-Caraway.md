# Mustard seed and caraway research

## Mustard sauce and allergic swallowing symptoms

Jorro and colleagues reported three systemic reactions after eating mustard sauce. Skin tests and serum IgE to mustard were positive for all three. The primary abstract does not list the individual symptoms. A later case table attributes acute dysphagia with dysphonia and respiratory symptoms to two of the cases. The original case pages remain to be recovered. The sauce preparation and allergy setting leave the effect of ground mustard on Parkinson’s dysphagia open. [Primary case-series abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7551207/) · [Case table and original citation](https://doi.org/10.1155/2012/503241)
