# Observations for 1975–1979

## Major observations

The 1975 protein study documented worse afternoon levodopa response with higher protein intake in a small comparison. A 1979 drug study showed that early-morning dystonia, wearing-off, on–off episodes and freezing responded differently to adjunct therapies. [Food and nutrition](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) · [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md)

## Minor observations

Photographic gait measurements and expert speech ratings characterized function quantitatively. A drug trial recorded an incidental report of relief of constipation after more fruit and roughage. A community survey in Finland and a separate age analysis documented continuing occurrence of disease. ## Comparison with 1970–1974

[Previous observations](../../1970-1974/Observations/README.md)

The 1974 bromocriptine add-on signal was followed by a direct comparison with levodopa. Average efficacy was statistically indistinguishable in a small sample, with tolerability problems and differences between individuals. Logemann and colleagues challenged Palmer’s 1974 one-site swallowing explanation by describing problems across multiple stages. The age-distribution findings also challenged the [1961 disappearance forecast](../../1960-1964/Risk-and-Prevention.md).

## Collection gaps

Look for follow-up to the small protein study and independent nutrition evidence, including formulation, meal timing, nutritional status and effect on fluctuations. Expand speech, exercise, cognition and caregiver coverage. The period’s diet records chiefly address treatment interactions and daily symptoms.

- Clinical observations separate peak-dose and biphasic levodopa-related involuntary movements. Individual meal observations include an adverse high-protein comparison and a reported favorable carbohydrate response. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md)

## Bowel studies and symptom comparison

Carrot feeding studies document a stool bulk response in healthy people. The early studies introduce a physiologic outcome and leave symptom severity unmeasured. [Original source notes](../Food-Experiment-Bowel-and-Motor-Outcomes.md)

Ground nutmeg and bowel frequency are documented within the 1975 treatment switch. The 1976 letter remains an access gap for the reported constipation cases. The later extract experiments used stool output or an intestinal marker, which address distinct mechanisms and preparations.
