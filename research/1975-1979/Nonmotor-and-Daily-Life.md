# Nonmotor symptoms and daily life

### 1975 — Swallowing abnormalities extended beyond one site

Logemann, Blonsky and Boshes reported fluoroscopic observations from more than 100 patients and a group of age-matched controls, challenging Palmer’s 1974 attribution of parkinsonian dysphagia to a single muscle dysfunction. Their report measured oral, pharyngeal and esophageal swallowing and described disturbances beyond one site. The editorial format and referral sample limit estimates of frequency. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1975.03240130051031)

### 1978 — Speech and voice patterns in 200 patients

Logemann and colleagues had expert listeners evaluate recorded speech from 200 Parkinson’s patients. Laryngeal problems were common, often accompanied by specific articulation problems. This descriptive sample documents communication difficulties and their co-occurrence. It did not test a speech therapy intervention. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1044/jshd.4301.47)
