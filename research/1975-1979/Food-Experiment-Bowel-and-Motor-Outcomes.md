# Bowel outcomes and selected motor or nonmotor follow-up

## Concentrated carrot fiber and stool bulk

Published January 1978. Nineteen healthy volunteers participated across separate dietary fiber exposures. The carrot fiber condition increased stool weight by 59% relative to the basal diet. The abstract does not give a carrot-arm sample size or report a constipation severity measure. Fiber concentration and food preparation differ from steamed whole carrot. [Original PubMed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/74533/) · [Local text](Source-Text/1978-Cummings-Carrot-Fiber.txt)

## Raw carrot and measured stool weight

Published September 1979. Five healthy volunteers ate 200 g raw carrot daily for three weeks. Mean wet stool weight rose from 142 ± 37 to 177 ± 33 g/day (p < .05), then returned to 145 ± 15 after washout. Whole-gut transit was 3.0 days at both baseline and supplementation. There was no randomized control, clinical constipation severity measure or steamed exposure.
