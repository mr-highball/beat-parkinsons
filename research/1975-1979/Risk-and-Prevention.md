# Risk and prevention

### 1976 — Age at onset changed less than a disappearing cohort would predict

An age-distribution analysis reported that the most common age of onset had shifted by less than a decade across roughly thirty years. This finding challenged the idea that nearly all cases were a single cohort aging after the encephalitis epidemic. Referral and diagnostic patterns remain relevant to interpreting the age distributions. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/765387/)

### 1976 — Community prevalence in southwestern Finland

Marttila and Rinne’s epidemiologic survey identified 484 cases on its 1971 prevalence date and personally examined 444. It provides community-based estimates beyond North American clinic cohorts and evidence of an ongoing disease burden. Population structure, diagnostic criteria and ascertainment matter in comparisons. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1266565/)
