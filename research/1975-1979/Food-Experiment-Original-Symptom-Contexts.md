# Original symptom reports and source context

## Apathy and pain in anticonvulsant toxicity cases

December 1976. Chadwick, Reynolds and Marsden describe eight patients with anticonvulsant-associated movement disorders. In Case 2 on page 1213, increasing apathy accompanied high phenobarbitone and phenytoin exposure. Case 5 on page 1214 records slowing and apathy during phenytoin intoxication. Case 3 reports that trigeminal neuralgia pain had been partly controlled by carbamazepine before it worsened. The reports use clinical histories without a quantitative apathy or pain instrument. The body, tables and cases were read from the indexed original journal PDF. The population and exposures do not provide a dried-date comparison in Parkinson’s disease.
