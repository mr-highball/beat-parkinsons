# Movement and rehabilitation

### 1978 — Photographic measurement of walking

Murray, Sepic, Gardner and Downs recorded multiple body segments during self-paced and fast walking in 44 men with parkinsonism. The measurements characterized gait abnormalities across tasks. A men-only descriptive study documents impairments and measurement techniques without evaluating a gait treatment. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/742658/)
