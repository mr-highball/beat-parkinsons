The archive supplied 33 page scans for the indexed record. Printed page 25, center column under NUTMEG, directs the reader to correspondence in volume I on page 96. The linked original letter is Venables and colleagues (PMID 942686), in the January 10 issue of the British Medical Journal. It describes reduced stool frequency after medicinal-dose ground nutmeg in secretory diarrhea, with adverse symptoms that improved as the dose was reduced. The index itself is a cross-reference. The independently indexed Dietz and Stuart letter (PMID 942762) appeared in the New England Journal of Medicine and remains a separate full-text access gap.
