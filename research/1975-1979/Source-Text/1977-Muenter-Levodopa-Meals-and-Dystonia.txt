1977 — Meal observations in two levodopa dystonia patterns
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0025619625098039
Coverage: Factual extract of public publisher text and clinical comparison

With the same levodopa dose in one person, water yielded a plasma peak of 5.9 micrograms per mL after 18 minutes. A protein-rich meal yielded 2.9 after more than two hours and prolonged the biphasic episode. The authors report that one person experienced less intense dystonia after high-carbohydrate meals. The paper uses dystonia broadly for levodopa-associated involuntary movements and does not identify chives. The published text describes Figure 3, whose image remained inaccessible.
