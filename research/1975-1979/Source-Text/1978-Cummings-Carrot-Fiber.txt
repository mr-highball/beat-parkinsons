Concentrated carrot fiber and stool bulk
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/74533/
original abstract paraphrase

Published January 1978. Nineteen healthy volunteers participated across separate dietary fiber exposures. The carrot fiber condition increased stool weight by 59% relative to the basal diet. The abstract does not give a carrot-arm sample size or report a constipation severity measure. Fiber concentration and food preparation differ from steamed whole carrot.
