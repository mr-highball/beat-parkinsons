Title: Iatrogenic 'torsade de pointes' ventricular tachycardia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2425796/
PMID: 523378
PMCID: PMC2425796
DOI: 10.1136/pgmj.55.649.832
Publication date: 1979-11
Citation: Kounis NG. Postgraduate Medical Journal 55(649), 832–835.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 832–835.

Three patients developed a characteristic ventricular arrhythmia while exposed to medicines associated with prolonged repolarization. The chest-pain history in the second case concerned an acute cardiac presentation. Millet appears as an author name in the bibliography. There was no date or millet intervention and no Parkinson pain assessment.
