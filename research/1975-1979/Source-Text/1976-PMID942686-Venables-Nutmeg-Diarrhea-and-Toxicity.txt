The original letter in the right-hand column of printed page 96 describes medicinal-dose ground Myristica fragrans for severe diarrhea associated with medullary carcinoma of the thyroid. The clinicians report an appreciable reduction in daily bowel movements. They give no counts or constipation assessment. Dry eyes and mouth, blurred vision, dizziness, tingling and depersonalization developed within three days and receded with dose reduction. The report follows the earlier Barrowman therapeutic letter. It describes the clinicians' own patient, with no evidence linking that patient to the prior report. This is an adverse-exposure and secretory-diarrhea observation. A symptom bridge from diarrhea treatment to constipation in Parkinson disease remains unestablished. The letter is distinct from the February Dietz and Stuart letter (PMID 942762).
