Title: Nomifensine in parkinsonism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC492568/
PMID: 796415
PMCID: PMC492568
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.39.12.1219
Citation: Teychenne PF, Park DM, Findley LJ, Rose FC, Calne DB. Journal of Neurology, Neurosurgery & Psychiatry. 1976 Dec. 39:1219–1221.
Coverage: Complete three-page PMC scan with OCR. Results and Figure 1, p. 1220. Distilled factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

The study substituted placebo for nomifensine in a blinded evaluation. Nineteen of 20 patients in that evaluation completed three placebo assessments. The authors describe some improvement in drooling during active treatment and report a statistical comparison for the total clinical deficit, which pools drooling with other signs and activities. A distinct drooling effect estimate is not given. The feeding measure reflects disability and the experiment assigned nomifensine, without a fig comparison.
