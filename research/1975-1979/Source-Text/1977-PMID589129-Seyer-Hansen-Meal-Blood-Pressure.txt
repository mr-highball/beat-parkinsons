Title: Postprandial hypotension
PMID: 589129
PMCID: PMC1632456
DOI: 10.1136/bmj.2.6097.1262
Publication: British Medical Journal, November 12, 1977, page 1262
Source: https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/2/6097/1262.1.full.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original case report and figure

Seyer-Hansen describes one hospitalized adult with parkinsonism who repeatedly reported dizziness and visual changes during meals. The ward observations describe a marked fall in blood pressure during those events. A separate oral glucose test showed a systolic decrease from 200 to 105 mm Hg within ten minutes, without the same reported symptoms. The figure and text distinguish the glucose test from ordinary meals. It is one case, with no randomized meal or individual culinary ingredient comparison.
