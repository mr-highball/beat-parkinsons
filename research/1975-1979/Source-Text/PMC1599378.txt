Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 1768–1773. The next article beginning on 1773 was excluded.

The first conference case involved abdominal pain, constipation, falls, and a subsequent clinicopathological discussion of thiamine deficiency and Wernicke encephalopathy. The second concerned cognitive decline and Alzheimer-type pathology. Apathy is discussed in the diagnostic differential of the first case. Neither patient received a studied dried-date exposure or provided a Parkinson apathy or pain comparison.
