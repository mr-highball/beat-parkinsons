The Dietz and Stuart letter is indexed February 26, 1976, at page 503. The original has no PubMed abstract and no full body was verified. A secondary Martindale summary attributes constipation in two people after unusually large amounts of freshly ground nutmeg to this letter. An April 1976 NEJM response corroborates self-administration at high intake but does not independently verify the bowel outcome. Dose frequency differs between the secondary account and response. The constipation account stays pending original verification. [Shafran response](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM197604082941526)
