Title: Studies with bromocriptine: III. Concomitant administration of caffeine to patients with idiopathic parkinsonism
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/181692/
PMID: 181692
DOI: 10.1212/wnl.26.8.741
Citation: Kartzinel R, Shoulson I, Calne DB. Neurology. 1976 Aug. 26(8):741–743.
Coverage: Author abstract from PubMed. Local factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

A double-blind study examined adding isolated caffeine to bromocriptine in six patients described as having idiopathic parkinsonism. The authors did not observe enhanced antiparkinsonian action in the combination. The abstract supplies no independently reported resting-tremor, walking-speed or dyskinesia comparison, no beverage exposure and no effect estimate or confidence interval.

The report shares an investigator and a historical research period with the 1975 levodopa or piribedil study. Medication contexts differ and overlap of individual participants remains unknown.
