The original 22-page review (pp. 75–96) examines environmental toxicants and other exposures using anatomical divisions of the nervous system. One dietary example concerns mercury-contaminated tuna in an animal study. The review provides no assigned ginger or dried date exposure. Its movement and nervous system material gives no ingredient-specific constipation, anxiety, pain or dyskinesia estimate for the frozen preparations.
