# Watermelon ingestion and oral reactions, 1985–1989

## Oral symptoms after watermelon ingestion

The November 1988 report by Enberg and colleagues examined 29 watermelon-sensitive patients, six of whom reported oropharyngeal symptoms after eating it. Serum immunoblotting used a 14-person subset. The investigators measured IgE responses and separated proteins from watermelon extract. The report concerns an allergy-selected population and does not report a Parkinson cohort, change in drooling, or speech intelligibility testing. [Primary article record, PMID 3192863](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3192863/)
