# Peanut oil vehicle and nociception

## Hot-plate responses with peanut oil and saline vehicles

Paul G. Green’s November 1985 dissertation compared the vehicle used for injected diisopropyl fluorophosphate in a mouse hot-plate experiment. The text reports no change in hot-plate response time when the drug was prepared in peanut oil compared with saline. Table 2.4 gives six observations per mean. This was an injected drug preparation and a thermal withdrawal assay. The study supplies no oral refined-oil comparison or Parkinson pain outcome. The discussion attributes prior sedation with the oil to a 1982 Pinsky paper. The thesis bibliography names that paper, but its original full methods and vehicle comparison still need independent verification.
