# Clinical and Diagnosis for 1985–1989

### 1985 — Population survey across six Chinese cities

Li and colleagues screened 63,195 people in six cities in 1983. Twenty-eight prevalent Parkinson’s cases were identified, yielding an age-adjusted estimate of 57 per 100,000. This urban survey and its small number of cases limit comparisons with national estimates. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4015461/)

### 1988 — Age at onset and untreated progression

Goetz and colleagues paired 31 people with rapid progression and 31 with slower progression by starting disease stage and symptom duration. Older onset was the only factor associated with faster progression among the factors examined. The retrospective matching and selected endpoints limit inference about individual trajectories. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3194061/)
