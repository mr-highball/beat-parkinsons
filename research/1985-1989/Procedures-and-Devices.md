# Procedures and Devices for 1985–1989

### 1987 — Early thalamic stimulation for tremor

Benabid and colleagues described thalamic stimulation to manage tremor on the opposite side from a prior or concurrent thalamotomy. Tremor decreased, and the report explored reversible stimulation in people for whom bilateral lesioning raised concerns. This early series focused on tremor. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3329873/)

### 1987 — Adrenal tissue autograft in two patients

Madrazo and colleagues described two people with severe Parkinson’s disease who received adrenal medullary tissue grafts and reported short-term improvement. The uncontrolled case report could not distinguish an effect of the graft from other changes around surgery. [Original publication](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJM198704023161402)

### 1989 — Early fetal dopamine cell grafts

Lindvall and colleagues followed two people with advanced disease for six months after a unilateral fetal dopamine neuron graft. The findings were compatible with modest motor improvement and small surviving grafts. The team treated the result as preliminary and called for continued scientific evaluation. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2786405/)
