# Movement and Rehabilitation for 1985–1989

### 1986 — Exercise and upper-body movement training

Palmer and colleagues compared a Parkinson exercise program with upper-body karate training over 12 weeks in two matched patient groups. Both groups improved on several gait, strength, and motor coordination measures. Rigidity was unchanged and one whole-body coordination task worsened. With two active programs, the contribution of practice and attention remained uncertain. [Original publication via PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3767624/)
