# Food experiment clinical screening

## Reported coffee-associated tremor and a caffeine challenge

Koller and colleagues reported that 6% of participants with Parkinson’s disease thought coffee worsened their tremor. In formal testing, a single oral caffeine dose did not increase parkinsonian tremor in the following three hours. The survey and challenge examined different exposures and contexts. Neither measured the fatigue severity outcome in frozen claim `0121`. [Primary abstract, PMID 3796831](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3796831/)
