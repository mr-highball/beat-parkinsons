Title: Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase, 6-phosphogluconate dehydrogenase and glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase activities in early stages of development in dystrophic chickens
PMID: 3989580
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3989580/
Publication year: 1985
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original author abstract

The report distinguishes several pectoral-muscle enzyme markers in dystrophic and control chickens during development. The author reports lower GAPDH activity at two weeks, four weeks and four months. The report refers to the author's earlier antioxidant-enzyme work. The closely matching age range and material appear in the 1985 Hamburg poster. This supports biochemical context, with cohort linkage and full-body comparisons still open.
