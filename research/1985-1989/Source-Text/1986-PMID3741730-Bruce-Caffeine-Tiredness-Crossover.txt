Original source scope and evidence boundary
Nine healthy participants took isolated anhydrous caffeine and placebo in a double-blind crossover. They had abstained from caffeinated beverages for 24 hours before each occasion. The authors report lower subjective tiredness and headache ratings following caffeine. The abstract does not give arm-scale estimates or matched estimates by habitual use. The design leaves withdrawal relief as an alternative explanation. This is an acute isolated constituent comparison outside Parkinson disease. It does not identify a separate effect of brewed coffee or brewed green tea. Followed from the 2004 chapter on arousal and its mental-fatigue discussion.
