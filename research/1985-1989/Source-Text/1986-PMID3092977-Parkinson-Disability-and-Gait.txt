Mutch and colleagues identified 267 people with idiopathic Parkinson disease in a community descriptive study. The motor sign assessment and Hoehn and Yahr grading included 214 patients after exclusions for other disabling conditions or extreme illness. The text reports severe gait abnormality in 84 of these patients (39.3%), grade 4 disability in 78 (36.4%), and grade 5 disability in 23 (10.7%). The study inventoried medication, access to therapy, review and disability.

The clinical gait and disability assessments are separate from counting recurrent falls. This article has no assigned food, produce exposure or fall rate. The journal index at page 24 cites this report and several other Parkinson publications as distinct entries.
