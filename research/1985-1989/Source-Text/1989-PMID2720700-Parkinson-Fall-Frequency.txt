Title: Falls and Parkinson's disease

Koller and colleagues questioned 100 people with Parkinson disease and five with progressive supranuclear palsy about falling, circumstances and consequences. They rated Parkinson signs using the unified rating scale. Thirty-eight percent of the Parkinson group reported falls, and 13% reported more than one per week. Falls correlated with several motor signs. Among the measured signs, severity of postural instability was the only one reported to correlate with fall frequency. The authors found no fall association with tremor in their study.

This is a clinical description of fall occurrence and frequency. The accessible abstract does not specify a nutrition exposure or present a strawberry comparison. Verification of the questionnaire's reference period and adjusted estimates requires the full paper. The abstract does not establish that modifying a correlated symptom would change fall frequency.
