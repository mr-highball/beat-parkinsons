Title: Influence of fluctuations of plasma large neutral amino acids with normal diets on the clinical response to levodopa.
PMID: 2738591
PMCID: PMC1032296
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.52.4.481
First published: 1989
Authors: Nutt JG, Woodward WR, Carter JH, Trotman TL
Journal: Journal of neurology, neurosurgery, and psychiatry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2738591/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Inpatient pharmacokinetic/clinical evaluation of n=11 fluctuating PD patients on a regular hospital diet: hourly plasma levodopa varied 2-3x more than plasma LNAAs, clinical status did not uniformly deteriorate as daytime LNAAs rose, and adjusting for plasma LNAAs substantially improved levodopa-response correlation in only 1/11 patients. Demonstrates inter-individual variability and tempers universal worsening claims.
