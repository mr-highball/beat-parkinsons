Title: A double-blind crossover comparison of Sinemet CR4 and standard Sinemet 25/100 in patients with Parkinson's disease and fluctuating motor performance.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1032507/
PMID: 2649640
PMCID: PMC1032507
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.52.2.207
Publication date: 1989-02
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC printed-page scans 207–212.

This crossover tests controlled-release and standard levodopa/carbidopa in Parkinson disease. Four participants had end-dose dystonia at baseline on printed page 207. The serial scales and diary comparisons concern fluctuations and dyskinesia. There is no fresh-sage contrast.
