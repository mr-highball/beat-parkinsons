1986 — Boiled dried coriander seed control in a human diuresis study
Source: https://ijpp.com/IJPP%20archives/1986_30_1/91-97.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of Methods, Results and Figure 1.

Male medical students aged 18–25 drank 200 mL of a comparator liquid. Urine was collected every 15 minutes for two hours. Results report no significant diuresis after boiled dried coriander seed extract at 100 g/L. Figure 1 labels the comparator at 50 g/L. Results separately describe very mild diuresis at 800 g/L, peaking at 1.5 mL/min at 45 minutes. No standing blood pressure was measured.
