Title: CSF sulfoconjugated catecholamines in man: their relationship with plasma catecholamines
PMID: 3839521
DOI: 10.1007/BF01260418
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3839521/
First published: March 1985, Journal of Neural Transmission.
Archive scope: Original author abstract. The full text and specific post-banana comparison denominator remain open.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

The authors report simultaneous cerebrospinal-fluid and plasma measurements in 38 patients with various neurologic disorders. They report an approximately threefold increase in plasma dopamine sulfate following banana ingestion, with no accompanying increase in CSF dopamine sulfate. The abstract does not specify how many of the 38 patients took part in the banana comparison. This is an observed dissociation between a peripheral marker and one measured CSF analyte. It cannot establish effects on all central monoamines or clinical Parkinson apathy.
