Title: Pathogenesis of progressive muscular dystrophy: studies on free radical metabolism in an animal model
PMID: 3364152
DOI: 10.1111/j.1600-0404.1988.tb05880.x
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3364152/
Publication: Acta Neurologica Scandinavica, February 1988
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original author abstract

The report measures radical-metabolism enzymes in superficial pectoral muscle of dystrophic and control chickens. Several activities were elevated from the first week. The scope overlaps the earlier experimental program and 1985 Hamburg report. Cohort identities are unavailable, so the overlap remains unresolved. The study doesn't assign chicken as a diet.
