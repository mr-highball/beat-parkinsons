Title: 'On-off' phenomenon in Parkinson's disease: relationship between dopa and other large neutral amino acids in plasma.
PMID: 3135513
DOI: 10.1212/wnl.38.8.1245
First published: 1988
Authors: Eriksson T, Granérus AK, Linde A, Carlsson A
Journal: Neurology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3135513/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled crossover in fluctuating PD comparing low-protein vs high-protein diets: low-protein diet consistently increased total daily ON time alongside marked reductions in plasma large neutral amino acids (LNAAs) competing with levodopa for blood-brain barrier transport. Limits: pooled dietary protein rather than isolated cooked beef; small historical study.
