## 1989 cardamom-containing mixture and depression withdrawal

The international patent [WO1989012454A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO1989012454A1/en) published 28 December 1989. The example uses ingredients powdered separately before blending. Capsules have 30 mg cardamom fruit among multiple plant ingredients in a 500 mg combination. The patent describes 50 patients with depression, three weeks of open mixture exposure followed by two randomized double-blind weeks of either continued mixture or placebo. It says worsening during the treatment period or lack of improvement early led to withdrawal, and claims differences on composite depression scales. Allocation sizes, individual cardamom intake and a cardamom-specific effect are not established. The intervention was a multiherb medicine and the reported endpoint is depression, not Parkinson apathy. EP0396638A1 is the European publication of the same family, not a separate experiment. Patent-described results are not independently verified.
