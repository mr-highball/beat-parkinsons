Title: Pilot study of the efficacy of spent grain dietary fiber in the treatment of constipation.
PMID: 3007392
First published: 1986
Authors: Odes HS, Madar Z, Trop M, Namir S, Gross J, Cohen T
Journal: Israel journal of medical sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3007392/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Uncontrolled 4-week pilot study in n=19 ambulatory adults with chronic laxative-dependent constipation given 20-25 g/d brewer's spent barley grain fiber baked into biscuits/scones. Bowel frequency improved in 15/19 (79%), laxative dependence in 14/19 (74%), and stool consistency in 8/19 (42%), regressing on withdrawal in 11/13 followed. Limits: uncontrolled pilot, brewer's spent grain byproduct rather than boiled whole barley.
