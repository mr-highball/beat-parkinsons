Title: Caffeine and tremor
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3796831/
PMID: 3796831
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.37.1.169
Publication date: 1987-01-01
Publisher abstract: https://www.neurology.org/doi/10.1212/WNL.37.1.169
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated author abstract and provider version comparison

In the coffee perception survey, 6% of respondents with Parkinson
disease attributed more tremor to coffee. A separate acute oral
caffeine challenge reported no increase in measured physiologic,
essential or parkinsonian tremor after one dose. The severity
measure, clinical rest posture, medication state, and group sizes
are not established in the available abstract.

PubMed's indexed author abstract lists checks at one, two and three
hours. The current publisher HTML repeats three and drops two.
The official full-body and PDF routes did not supply the original
article during this check. The discrepancy remains open at original
body depth. This evidence concerns coffee perception and isolated
caffeine, with no brewed-black-tea intervention.
