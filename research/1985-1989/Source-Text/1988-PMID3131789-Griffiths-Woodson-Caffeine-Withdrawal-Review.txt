The authors synthesized 37 older clinical reports and experiments and identified fatigue and headache as common symptoms after caffeine cessation. Their Table 2 lists the 1986 coffee-to-decaf withdrawal experiment and distinguishes seven participants from other experimental arms. Near Figure 1 the review reports increased Profile of Mood States Fatigue and reduced Vigor during withdrawal. The figure is attributed to the earlier Griffiths study, PMID 3772801. The review includes inconsistent reports from other studies and calls out sources with uncertain symptom ascertainment. It provides no pooled effect for brewed coffee on Parkinson fatigue.

The vascular review PMID 21188209 cites this publication. Treat the 1986 experiment, its reprinted figure, and the later vascular discussion as one primary result with a secondary citation trail.
