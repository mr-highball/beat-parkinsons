Daytime protein contrasts and observed motor fluctuations
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2590325/

March–April 1987. Pincus and Barry reported 15 people with Parkinson disease with fluctuations or a limited medication response. The monitored inpatient comparison used equal-calorie diets with different daytime protein amounts, held medication timing and dose fixed, and measured disability, dyskinesia, plasma levodopa and amino acids. Seven of eight fluctuators showed immediate clinical improvement with the low-protein regimen in the clinical report. The comparison did not isolate a potato preparation or measure hours of daily OFF by ingredient. Exact patient overlap with the same authors' 1988 report remains unresolved.
