Title: Horseradish horrors: sushi syncope
PMID: 3336137
DOI: 10.1001/jama.1988.03720020020024
First published: 1988
Authors: Spitzer DE
Journal: JAMA
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3336137/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

JAMA clinical case report describing acute reflex vasovagal syncope ('sushi syncope') provoked by pungent horseradish ingestion. Uncontrolled non-PD case report documenting acute transient autonomic presyncope/syncope risk with bolus pungent horseradish exposure.
