Title: Oral mucosal reactions to cinnamon-flavored chewing gum
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3164031/
PMID: 3164031
DOI: 10.14219/jada.archive.1988.0003
Publication date: 1988
Coverage: Original author abstract and publisher bibliographic record.

In a selected series of 10 patients, burning was the main complaint in most and the exposure was usually attributed to cinnamon-flavored chewing gum. The reported oral lesions generally resolved within one or two days after withdrawal.
The accessible author record does not establish ground spice or controlled exposure and leaves original case-level details open. The series gives no denominator of all consumers.
