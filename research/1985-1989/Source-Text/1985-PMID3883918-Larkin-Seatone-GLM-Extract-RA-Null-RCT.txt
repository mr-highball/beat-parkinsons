Title: Seatone in rheumatoid arthritis: a six-month placebo-controlled study.
PMID: 3883918
PMCID: PMC1001605
DOI: 10.1136/ard.44.3.199
First published: 1985
Authors: Larkin JG, Capell HA, Sturrock RD
Journal: Annals of the rheumatic diseases
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3883918/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Primary Abstract:
Thirty-five patients with rheumatoid arthritis were randomly allocated to either Seatone (green-lipped mussel extract) or placebo in order to assess the former's claimed effectiveness in rheumatoid disease. After six months there was no significant improvement in any laboratory or clinical measurement of disease activity in the Seatone group. The patients on active drug fared no better than those on placebo. These results suggest that Seatone is not effective in rheumatoid arthritis.

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Randomized double-blind 6-mo placebo-controlled trial (Ann Rheum Dis 1985; PMC1001605) in n=35 RA patients receiving freeze-dried Seatone (Perna canaliculus) extract vs placebo. Completely NULL across all clinical pain, stiffness, articular index, and laboratory endpoints.
