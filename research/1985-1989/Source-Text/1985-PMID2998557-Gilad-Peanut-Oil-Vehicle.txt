Text coverage: Original authors' abstract and reproduced article methods

Gilad and colleagues administered corticosterone in an intraperitoneal peanut-oil vehicle. The Methods state that vehicle-injected rats were controls. The outcomes concern choline uptake and acetylcholine release following stress or hormone exposure. The oil is a carrier or control and no oral peanut-oil contrast was reported.

The 1985 Hamburg member 05.09.04 uses the same type of oil carrier and reports a different hormone dose. The two records have overlapping investigators and tissue endpoints. Shared cohorts or experiments remain unverified.
