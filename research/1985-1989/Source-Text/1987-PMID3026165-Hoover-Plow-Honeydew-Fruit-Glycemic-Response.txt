Title: The glycemic response to meals with six different fruits in insulin-dependent diabetics using a home blood-glucose monitoring system
PMID: 3026165
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/45.1.92
First published: 1987
Authors: Hoover-Plow J, Savesky J, Dailey G
Journal: The American Journal of Clinical Nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3026165/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Crossover meal trial in 10 adults with insulin-dependent diabetes comparing postprandial blood glucose responses after adding 10-g available-carbohydrate portions of apple, banana, grapes, honeydew melon, orange, or strawberries to a standard meal. Meals with honeydew, grapes, orange, or strawberries produced slightly higher blood glucose than apple, banana, or no fruit. Direct raw honeydew melon human trial, but blood-glucose endpoint only; zero Parkinson's motor or non-motor symptoms measured.
