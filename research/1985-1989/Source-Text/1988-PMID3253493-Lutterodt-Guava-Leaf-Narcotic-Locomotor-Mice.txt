Title: Effects on mice locomotor activity of a narcotic-like principle from Psidium guajava leaves
PMID: 3253493
DOI: 10.1016/0378-8741(88)90155-9
First published: 1988
Authors: Lutterodt GD, Maleque A
Journal: Journal of Ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3253493/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Preclinical pharmacology study of a non-polar methanolic fraction from dried Psidium guajava LEAVES (3.3-13.2 mg/kg i.p. and oral) in normal mice, producing narcotic-like locomotor suppression, catalepsy, analgesia, and shallow breathing. Strictly non-polar dried LEAF fraction in non-PD mice, NOT raw culinary guava fruit.
