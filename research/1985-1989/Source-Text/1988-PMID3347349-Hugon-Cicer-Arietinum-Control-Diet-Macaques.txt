Title: Studies on the etiology and pathogenesis of motor neuron diseases. II. Clinical and electrophysiologic features of pyramidal dysfunction in macaques fed Lathyrus sativus and IDPN.

First published: 1988
Authors: Hugon J, Ludolph A, Roy DN, Schaumburg HH, Spencer PS
Journal: Neurology

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Historical primate neurolathyrism study (Hugon et al., 1988, Neurology) using Cicer arietinum (chickpea) as the non-neurotoxic legume control diet against Lathyrus sativus (grass pea) in macaques. Confirms dietary chickpeas lacked motor-neuron toxicity in primates without testing Parkinson motor symptom relief.
