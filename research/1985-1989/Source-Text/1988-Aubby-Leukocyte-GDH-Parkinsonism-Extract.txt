Leukocyte enzyme activity and diagnosis-group vignettes

DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.51.7.893
PMID: 3204397

Coverage: Identity-matched original published body and clinical or assay results.

Aubby and colleagues measured leukocyte glutamate dehydrogenase in 158 people with ataxia or movement disorders and 71 controls. The clinical descriptions include a four-person dystonia-parkinsonism group and vignettes involving cervical and limb dystonia, rigidity and rest tremor. The primary comparison concerns enzyme activity across diagnostic groups. No food exposure was assigned and no food-linked severity contrast was reported.
