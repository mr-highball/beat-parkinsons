Title: Practical application of a low-protein diet for Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 3386817
DOI: 10.1212/wnl.38.7.1026
First published: 1988
Authors: Riley D, Lang AE
Journal: Neurology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3386817/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Outpatient series of n=38 PD patients placed on a protein-restricted diet alongside usual dopaminergic therapy: 60% of patients with levodopa motor fluctuations improved (primarily increased ON-to-OFF hour ratio within 1 week); non-responders to levodopa did not benefit; well tolerated with manageable dyskinesia via levodopa dose reduction. Limits: uncontrolled series, pooled dietary protein.
