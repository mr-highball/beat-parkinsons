Title: Bilateral ballism induced by ibuprofen in a schizophrenic patient.
PMID: 3658874
PMCID: PMC2428391
DOI: 10.1136/pgmj.63.741.593
Publication year: 1987
First public date basis: NLM and original journal record.
Source: https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/538d/2428391/7862c8b43726/postmedj00199-0069.png
Coverage: Complete article-body context reviewed 2026-11-22.

The original p. 593 describes orofacial dyskinesia and bilateral ballistic movements after ibuprofen prescribed for osteoarthritic pain in a patient with prior neuroleptic-related dyskinesia. The ballistic movements diminished after ibuprofen withdrawal. The case gives no dried-date exposure or Parkinson pain comparison.
