Title: Postprandial and orthostatic hypotension in Parkinson's disease
PMID: 3822130
DOI: 10.1212/wnl.37.3.386
Publication: Neurology, March 1987, volume 37, pages 386–393
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3822130/
Publisher record: https://www.neurology.org/doi/10.1212/WNL.37.3.386
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original author abstract

Micieli and colleagues compare 13 untreated Parkinson patients with 11 age-matched healthy controls. The reported outcome is measured blood pressure. Seven of the 13 Parkinson participants had a supine systolic decline after eating that exceeded 20% of the premeal value. The authors also report orthostatic hypotension under a tilt-test comparison. Neither outcome counts falls or assigns a specific food.

The 1985 conference member 05.20.07 reports similar measurement domains and has overlapping investigators. The conference report does not give matching enrollment figures. Cohort overlap is plausible but unverified. The full article tables and meal details remain open.
