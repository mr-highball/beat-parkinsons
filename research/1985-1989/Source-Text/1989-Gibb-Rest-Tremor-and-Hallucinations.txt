Coverage: factual summary from the indexed original pages, especially pp. 186–187.

Two of five cases with parkinsonism had rest tremor. Case 2 had later occasional visual hallucinations, while case 3 had visual and auditory hallucinations before later rest tremor and bradykinesia. These case histories do not assess an episode rate or a standardized rest-tremor severity response. No food intervention is described.
