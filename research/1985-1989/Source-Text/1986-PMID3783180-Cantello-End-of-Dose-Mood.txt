Title: Mood changes associated with end-of-dose deterioration in Parkinson's disease: a controlled study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1029054/
PMID: 3783180
PMCID: PMC1029054
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.49.10.1182
Citation: Cantello R, Gilli M, Riccio A, Bergamasco B. Journal of Neurology, Neurosurgery & Psychiatry. 1986 Oct. 49:1182–1190.
Coverage: Complete nine-page PMC scan with OCR. Population and comparison definitions, pp. 1182–1184. Distilled factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

Eighteen patients with Parkinson disease who experienced end-of-dose deterioration and 12 people with active rheumatoid arthritis were compared in more and less mobile periods. Morning stiffness and temporary pain exacerbation characterized the rheumatoid arthritis comparison group. Temporary immobility coincided with worse mood and mental activation, with larger fluctuations in the Parkinson group. The report concerns medication-state and mobility-related mood. It did not assign date fruit or publish a date-fruit pain contrast.
