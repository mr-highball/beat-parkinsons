The pulmonary hypertension study tests drug responses using hemodynamic and exertion outcomes. Printed page 697 says caffeinated drinks were prohibited. Coffee is cited in a pharmacokinetic reference. The study does not compare brewed coffee or measure Parkinson fatigue.
