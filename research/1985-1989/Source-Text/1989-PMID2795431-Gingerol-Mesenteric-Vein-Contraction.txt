Title: Chemical structural requirement in gingerol derivatives for potentiation of prostaglandin F2 alpha-induced contraction in isolated mesenteric veins of mice
PMID: 2795431
DOI: 10.1248/bpb1978.12.220
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2795431/
Original PDF: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bpb1978/12/4/12_4_220/_pdf
Date basis: April 1989 journal issue, pp. 220–227.
Coverage: Factual summary of the original author abstract. The primary eight-page scan is located.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

In isolated mouse mesenteric veins, 8-gingerol potentiated prostaglandin-induced contraction more than 6-gingerol in the reported comparisons. The abstract describes inhibition with 6-shogaol. The same compounds inhibited noradrenaline-induced contraction in separate comparisons. Ginger derivative identity, the stimulus and preparation time affected the results.

The measured tissue is a blood vessel. The authors assessed vascular tension and chemical structure, without a live intestinal transport, stool or clinical Parkinson outcome.
