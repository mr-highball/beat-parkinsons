Title: Chemical structural requirement in gingerol derivatives for potentiation of prostaglandin F2 alpha-induced contraction in isolated mesenteric veins of mice
PMID: 2795431
DOI: 10.1248/bpb1978.12.220
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2795431/
Original PDF: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bpb1978/12/4/12_4_220/_pdf
Date basis: April 1989 journal issue, pp. 220–227.
Coverage: Factual extract of the original eight-page paper. Tables I and II were checked visually against the scan on p. 223.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Table I gives control-normalized contractions after applying isolated compounds to mouse mesenteric veins. At the respective tested concentrations, 6-gingerol and 8-gingerol increased prostaglandin-induced contraction to 128% and 115% of control, while 6-shogaol yielded 65%. Noradrenaline responses for those compounds were 62%, 45% and 41% of control, respectively. The concentrations differed, so these rows give no equal-concentration potency ranking. Table II reports that 6-gingerdione inhibited prostaglandin-induced contraction immediately after preparation, showed little effect after two hours, and potentiated it at five hours.

The measured tissue is a blood vessel. The authors assessed vascular tension and chemical structure, without a live intestinal transport, stool or clinical Parkinson outcome.
