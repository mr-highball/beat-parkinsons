Title: Dietary influences on the antiparkinsonian response to levodopa
Publication date: 1987-10
PMID: 3632369
DOI: 10.1001/archneur.1987.00520220009006
Citation: Juncos JL, Fabbrini G, Mouradian MM, Serrati C, Chase TN. Archives of Neurology 44:1003–1005.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3632369/
Coverage: Author abstract. Original tables and per-item reporting remain open.

Six people with idiopathic Parkinson disease and motor fluctuations were observed during dietary contrasts. A high-protein oral formula raised plasma large neutral amino acids and shortened the response to levodopa/carbidopa. During oral or intravenous levodopa, diets meeting the reference protein allowance did not significantly affect drug or amino acid measurements or the general Parkinson score. Neither an isolated turkey meal nor separately reported resting-tremor severity can be established from the abstract.
