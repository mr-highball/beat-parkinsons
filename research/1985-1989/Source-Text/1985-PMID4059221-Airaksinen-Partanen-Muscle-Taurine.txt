Title: Muscle taurine in chicken and diseased human muscle
PMID: 4059221
Publication: Progress in Clinical and Biological Research, volume 179, pp. 407–412, 1985
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4059221/
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: NLM bibliographic identity and investigators' quoted comparison in a separate original conference abstract

The author group is Airaksinen and Partanen. Their 1985 Hamburg abstract 11.30.11 explicitly cites this chapter for a difference in taurine content between white and red chicken muscle fibres. The reported 25-fold comparison is taken from that citation in the original poster. The chapter's full tables and tissue sampling are unavailable in the located PubMed record. The chicken result concerns the material's composition and does not test fatigue after a meal.
