Title: Subacute combined degeneration of the cord, dementia and parkinsonism due to an inborn error of folate metabolism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1028954/
PMID: 3755752
PMCID: PMC1028954
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.49.8.920
Publication date: 1986-08
Coverage: Paraphrased from the complete official printed-page scans, pp. 920–927. The relevant passages are on pp. 921–922.

The clinical case describes secondary parkinsonism in a child with a severe inherited disorder of folate metabolism. Drooling and tremor were already prominent during the presenting illness. An acute crisis with worsening movement symptoms and drooling appeared over eight days during a combination of folic acid, methionine, vitamin B12 and carnitine. The additional symptoms resolved over the following week after withdrawal. The sequence is clinical case evidence for a mixed treatment exposure in this particular metabolic disorder. It gives no isolated folate comparison, fig intake, or graded drooling measure. Figure references describe pathology and biochemical pathways.
