DOI: 10.3109/00207458608985678
Original abstract and source comparison. Five people in an uncontrolled cannabidiol series. Improvement in dystonia varied. At higher drug doses, two people with coexisting parkinsonian features had more hypokinesia and resting tremor. Unofficial Methods transcription https://web.acsalaska.net/~warmgun/es017.html identifies sesame oil as vehicle; tables are absent and publisher pages did not yield the original full text. Companion PMID3749389 names oral cannabidiol without specifying oil. No separate sesame or processing contrast.
