Title: Amount and distribution of dietary protein affects clinical response to levodopa in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2648188/
PMID: 2648188
DOI: 10.1212/wnl.39.4.552
Citation: Carter JH, Nutt JG, Woodward WR, Hatcher LF, Trotman TL. Neurology. 1989 Apr. 39(4):552–556.
Coverage: Author abstract from PubMed. Local factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

The authors compared three regimens in five people with Parkinson disease and motor fluctuations. Mean time ON was 51% with 1.6 g protein per kg per day, 67% with 0.8 g/kg/day distributed evenly and 77% with the same lower amount concentrated in the evening. Hourly mean levodopa concentrations did not differ across regimens. Mean large neutral amino acid concentrations were 732, 640 and 542 nmol/mL in the same order.

The abstract supports two regimen contrasts, including equal total protein with different timing. It does not give an ingredient comparison, a separate resting-tremor or dyskinesia result, or uncertainty intervals for the time ON comparison. Nutt et al. 1984 shares authors and a mechanistic question. Subject-level overlap remains unresolved.
