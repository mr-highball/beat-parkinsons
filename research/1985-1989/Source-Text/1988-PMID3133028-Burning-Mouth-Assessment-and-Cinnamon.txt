Title: Prospective study of aetiological factors in burning mouth syndrome
Source: https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/296/6631/1243.full.pdf
PMID: 3133028
DOI: 10.1136/bmj.296.6631.1243
Publication date: 1988-04-30
Coverage: Original full BMJ article, pp. 1243–1246.

In a referred burning-mouth sample of 150, three patients with intermittent oral burning received patch tests for food-related materials. One reacted to a cinnamon test material. The other reactions involved different additives.
The paper reports symptom resolution after dietary advice for two of the three selected patients. It does not map that outcome to the cinnamon-positive patient, identify the culinary preparation, or report a cinnamon rechallenge.
