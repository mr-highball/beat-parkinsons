Title: Plasma levels of amino acids correlate with motor fluctuations in parkinsonism
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3632370/
PMID: 3632370
DOI: 10.1001/archneur.1987.00520220012007
Citation: Pincus JH, Barry KM. Archives of Neurology. 1987 Oct. 44(10):1006–1009.
Coverage: Author abstract from PubMed. Local factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

Seven people with Parkinson disease and severe motor fluctuations were studied on different dietary protein regimens. Each participant's levodopa dose was held fixed and no other antiparkinsonian drugs were used. During regular and high-protein regimens, higher plasma large neutral amino acid concentrations coincided with parkinsonism despite high plasma levodopa. During the low-protein days, the authors reported that all seven were consistently dyskinetic even though mean levodopa concentrations were somewhat lower than during the high-protein diet.

This is regimen-level clinical context and a separate adverse motor endpoint. There was no isolated culinary exposure or individual-symptom effect size in the abstract. The same authors reported other small series in the same period. Potential participant overlap remains open.
