Title: Influence of dietary protein on motor fluctuations in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3827678/
PMID: 3827678
DOI: 10.1001/archneur.1987.00520150026014
Citation: Pincus JH, Barry K. Archives of Neurology. 1987 Mar. 44(3):270–272.
Coverage: Author abstract from PubMed. Local factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

Eleven people with Parkinson disease and motor fluctuations showed better levodopa response and fewer fluctuations during the very-low-protein daytime regimen. Chorea became more prominent and the study team changed medications in eight participants to address it. The authors describe marked bradykinesia during much of the day on the high-protein regimen.

The author abstract gives no isolated culinary exposure or item-level effect size. There is potential participant overlap with the authors' 1987 Yale Journal of Biology and Medicine and Archives of Neurology reports and 1988 Neurology report. Keep the reports together as a possible shared family pending participant lineage evidence.
