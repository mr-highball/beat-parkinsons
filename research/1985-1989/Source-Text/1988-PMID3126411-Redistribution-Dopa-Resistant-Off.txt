Title: Protein redistribution diet restores motor function in patients with dopa-resistant off periods
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3126411/
PMID: 3126411
DOI: 10.1212/wnl.38.3.481
Citation: Pincus JH, Barry K. Neurology. 1988 Mar. 38(3):481–483.
Coverage: Author abstract from PubMed. Local factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

Sixteen people with Parkinson disease and medication-resistant OFF periods without dyskinesia at entry followed a regimen concentrating protein in the evening. The authors reported restored levodopa response in 88%. Ten people continued the regimen for a mean of seven months. The clinical and plasma details of immobility alongside higher large neutral amino acid levels came from two participants.

The abstract does not give a separate food exposure, a randomized comparator or hours of daily OFF by ingredient. Cohort overlap with the authors' related 1987 series is unresolved.
