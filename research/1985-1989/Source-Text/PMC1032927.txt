Title: Dystonic-Parkinsonian syndrome after cyanide poisoning: clinical and MRI findings
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1032927/
PMID: 3225591
PMCID: PMC1032927
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.51.10.1345
Publication date: 1988-10
Citation: Carella F, Grassi MP, Savoiardo M, Contri P, Rapuzzi B, Mangoni A. Journal of Neurology, Neurosurgery & Psychiatry 51(10), 1345–1348.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete original. Official NLM page scans, printed pages 1345–1348.

This single case documents delayed parkinsonism with oromandibular dystonia, marked drooling, dysphagia, and difficulty voluntarily opening the eyes after poisoning. The authors describe drooling at the bedside, without a quantified saliva outcome. They report a separate medication response for bradykinesia and eyelid opening. The case provides secondary parkinsonism context and does not test a culinary fig or date exposure.
