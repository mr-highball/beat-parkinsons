Title: The interaction between alcohol and marijuana: A dose dependent study of the effects on human moods and performance skills
Authors: G. B. Chesher and colleagues
Citation: Australian Federal Office of Road Safety, consultant report CR 40, 1986.
Publication date: 1986
Date basis: Official PDF cover January 1986; the agency publication record separately displays 1 July 1986. Earliest online public availability is not established.
Source: https://www.infrastructure.gov.au/sites/default/files/migrated/roads/safety/publications/1986/pdf/Alc_Drug.pdf
Official catalog: https://www.infrastructure.gov.au/department/media/publications/cr-40-interaction-between-alcohol-and-marijuana-1986
Coverage: Original government report printed page 34, and official publication metadata; inspected 2026-11-27.

The report describes human psychomotor and mood testing with alcohol and marijuana dose conditions. Page 34 specifies orange juice and peppermint in alcohol and comparison drinks to mask sensory cues; a small amount of alcohol was placed on the comparison beverage immediately before serving. Orange juice and peppermint were components of mixed vehicles, rather than randomized citrus exposures. Their independent contribution to performance, balance, or falls cannot be isolated. The report does not provide a Parkinson-specific orange comparison. It corroborates the type of methodological practice described in Wetherell 1996 PMID9182033, but the 1996 review does not identify the report as its source and no direct citation linkage has been verified.
