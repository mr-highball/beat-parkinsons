Title: Strawberry Receptacle Growth and Endogenous IAA Content as Affected by Growth Regulator Application and Achene Removal
DOI: 10.21273/JASHS.110.6.816
Publication: Journal of the American Society for Horticultural Science, November 1985, volume 110, pages 816–820
Source: https://journals.ashs.org/downloadpdf/view/journals/jashs/110/6/article-p812.pdf?amp%3BinlineView=true&pdfJsInlineViewToken=1013849676
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Authors' abstract and opening text on printed page 816

Archbold and Dennis experimentally changed hormone treatment or removed achenes from developing strawberry fruit. The authors report reduced subsequent receptacle enlargement when achenes were removed early and sustained enlargement following treatment with naphthaleneacetic acid. They measured fruit growth and indoleacetic acid in plant tissues. This is a reference in the 1994 auxin-regulated gene review and supplies plant physiology context for the strawberry term. Pages 817–820 lie beyond the available page.
