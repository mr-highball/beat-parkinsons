Title: Effect of food intake on exercise fatigue in trained and untrained subjects.
PMID: 4065113
DOI: 10.1007/BF00426148
First published: 1985
Authors: Arieli, Epstein, Brill, Winer, Shapiro
Journal: European journal of applied physiology and occupational physiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4065113/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled physiological field study in 30 untrained and 24 trained subjects completing a 34-km backpack march (25 kg; 8.5 h untrained, 6.33 h trained) divided into three dietary groups: free access to almonds, sugar cubes, or control. In untrained subjects, ingestion of almonds delayed the subjective sensation of exhaustion, whereas 50% of controls and sugar-consuming subjects complained of exhaustion. Limits: 1985 march protocol, small arm sizes, almond preparation unspecified, abstract reports no statistical test or variance.
