Factual extract from original Methods, Tables 1 and 4, Figure 1 and the author abstract.

Methods describe eight healthy participants aged 21–49 and assays of serotonin in foods, urinary 5-HIAA and platelet serotonin. The analyses cover several foods and different elicitation conditions. Table 1 reports 5.8 ± 0.9 µg/g serotonin by food weight for kiwi fruit (mean ± SEM). Table 4 includes one kiwi together with walnut and banana. The oral comparison in Figure 1 uses four bananas with seven participants. The authors report increased urinary metabolite excretion after foods and no change in platelet serotonin. The reported kiwi-containing meal cannot isolate a kiwi effect. Cognitive attention and Parkinson symptoms were outside the study.
