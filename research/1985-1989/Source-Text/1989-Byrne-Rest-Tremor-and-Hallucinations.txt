Coverage: factual summary, official original scans of all nine pages screened using OCR with visual checks of pp. 713 and 716.

All six cases presenting with a typical parkinsonian syndrome had rest tremor. Cases 13 and 14 describe visual hallucinations in the clinical history. The five-cases statement on p. 716 combines auditory or visual hallucinations and paranoid ideation, without a separate count of visual hallucinations. The original identifies case 12 as previously reported in 1987. Individual overlap with the separate Gibb series is unverified. The paper gives no food exposure, standardized tremor severity estimate or visual episode rate.
