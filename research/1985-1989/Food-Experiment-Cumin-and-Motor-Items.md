# Cumin, edible oils and specific motor outcomes

## Soybean oil emulsion and gastric emptying after vagotomy

The original report compares a soybean-oil emulsion with water given before an oral glucose meal in nine patients who had undergone vagotomy. Initial gastric emptying slowed with the emulsion. Responses varied by postoperative syndrome. This is an indirect gastric timing comparison, with no Parkinson cohort, levodopa measurements or diary of daily OFF periods.
