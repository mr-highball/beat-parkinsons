# Tarragon Diabetes Models

## Tarragon diet and feeding and drinking in diabetic mice

Swanston-Flatt and colleagues tested separate diets containing seven individual plants and an eighth herbal mixture in streptozotocin-diabetic mice. The tarragon-containing diet reduced hyperphagia and polydipsia and slowed body-weight loss. The distinct herbal mixture did not alter the feeding and drinking measures. Plasma glucose and insulin did not show clear treatment changes. The study reports no pain endpoint and does not specify fresh culinary leaf consumption.
