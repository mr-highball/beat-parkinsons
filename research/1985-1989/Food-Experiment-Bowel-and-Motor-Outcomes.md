# Bowel outcomes and selected motor or nonmotor follow-up

## Corn oil bolus and reported bowel changes

Published October 1988. Thirty healthy volunteers were randomized to basal diet or 90 g corn oil daily divided between meals or as one evening bolus for five days. Three bolus participants reported cramping and looser stool. The paper describes little overall bowel-habit change. It gives no standardized constipation severity estimate, full exposed event denominator or verified refining grade. The bolus is a large dose.
