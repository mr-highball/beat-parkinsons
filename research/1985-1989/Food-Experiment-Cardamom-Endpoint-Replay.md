# Cardamom endpoint evidence

## Cardamom-containing multiherb mixture and depression

International patent WO1989012454A1, published 28 December 1989, describes a finely powdered multiherb mixture in a 500 mg capsule with 30 mg cardamom fruit. The formulation is a mixture and the inventors do not report an authenticated culinary cardamom product. It reports 50 patients with depression, a three-week open mixture period, then a randomized double-blind continuation or placebo withdrawal for two weeks. Participants whose condition worsened or did not improve early were withdrawn. Composite depression scales are described, but no separate apathy measurement, cardamom-only comparison, or Parkinson population. The subsequent EP0396638A1 is in the same family. Patent-described outcomes are unverified independently.
