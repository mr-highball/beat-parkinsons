# Canola and safflower oil evidence

## Safflower oil capsules and lying and standing blood pressure

Heagerty and colleagues’ 1986 randomized crossover included 22 normotensive adults. After the safflower-oil period, mean lying systolic pressure was 125 mmHg versus 129 during placebo (p < .01). Mean standing systolic pressure was 116 versus 117 mmHg. The report did not give a symptomatic orthostasis endpoint or a paired estimate of standing change. Some participants reported bloating during the oil period. The publication gives no Parkinson cohort or cold-pressed grade. A 1989 report from overlapping investigators also compared safflower-oil and paraffin capsules in normotensive adults, including first-degree relatives of hypertensive patients. Cohort overlap remains unverified. [1986 primary paper, Table IV](https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/293/6542/295.full.pdf) · [1989 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2724276/)
