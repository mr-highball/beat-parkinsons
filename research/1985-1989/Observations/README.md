# Observations for 1985–1989

The [printed tremor study](1989-Printed-Tremor-Measurement.md) distinguishes resting tremor from instrumented tremor after movement.

## Major observations

- [Protein redistribution](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) extended meal challenges into repeated clinical observations. Better daytime mobility and more dyskinesia could occur together, and continued adherence varied.
- [Selegiline trials](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) delayed the clinical milestone of starting levodopa. The milestone was responsive to symptoms, leaving the effect on neuron loss open.
- [Thalamic stimulation and grafting](../Procedures-and-Devices.md) opened two separate lines of investigation. Stimulation targeted tremor, and transplantation sought to restore dopamine function. Early case series could establish feasibility and generate follow-up questions.

## Minor observations

- [Regional exposure patterns](../Risk-and-Prevention.md) differed. Quebec prevalence tracked intensive agriculture at a geographic level, while the Saskatchewan team found no link to the water metals or agricultural chemicals it studied.
- [Olfactory testing](../Nonmotor-and-Daily-Life.md) showed that people could have measurable impairment without realizing it. Clinical reports also gave separate attention to pain, dementia, cognition, and an adjacent REM sleep disorder.
- [Incidental Lewy bodies and complex I deficiency](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md) gave investigators tissue findings to compare with symptoms and environmental hypotheses.
- A [sibling diet study and ferrous sulfate experiment](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) addressed different endpoints. The first compared early-adult food recall, and the second measured acute levodopa exposure. [Nigral iron in autopsy tissue](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md) was another distinct measure.

## Comparison with [1980–1984](../../1980-1984/Observations/README.md)

- The 1984 meal study measured levodopa response after changing meal composition. The 1987 caffeine study measured tremor directly over several hours and separately asked about experience with coffee. Some participants reported worse tremor after coffee, and the acute caffeine comparison found no measured increase. The exposures and outcomes address different questions. [Earlier meal challenge](../../1980-1984/Food-and-Nutrition.md#1984--meals-amino-acids-and-levodopa-response) · [1987 tremor study](../Food-and-Nutrition.md#1987--reported-coffee-and-acute-oral-caffeine-in-tremor)

- The nine-person meal and levodopa experiment provided a mechanistic lead. The later case series followed redistribution over time, and the 1989 blinded crossover found better motor performance during the lower-protein week. Some participants reached higher levodopa peaks during the higher-protein week while performing worse. [Food and nutrition](../Food-and-Nutrition.md#1989--blinded-dietary-protein-crossover-and-motor-performance)
- A French thesis catalog adds a separate 1989 lead on diet and levodopa motor fluctuations after the 1984 meal challenge. Its original results are still needed to determine the foods tested and whether the findings replicate the earlier comparisons. [1984 meal experiment](../../1980-1984/Food-and-Nutrition.md#1984--meals-amino-acids-and-levodopa-response) · [Bellonnet thesis and holdings](../Food-and-Nutrition.md#1989--clermont-ferrand-thesis-on-diet-and-levodopa-motor-fluctuations)
- Following the acute MPTP cases, investigators pursued rural exposure histories, brain tissue biochemistry, and selegiline trials. The new studies broadened the hypothesis space and left the cause of typical Parkinson’s disease unresolved.
- The earlier four-week combined therapy study found no measured improvement. The later two-program exercise study reported changes on some motor tests, with different programs, comparators, and endpoints limiting direct comparison.

- The 1980 red kidney bean report documented vomiting and diarrhea after insufficient cooking. The 1988 watermelon series documented oral symptoms in people selected for watermelon sensitivity. The case series point to distinct mechanisms and provide no Parkinson swallowing or saliva measurements. [1980 bean cases](../../1980-1984/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md#british-case-reports-after-inadequately-prepared-red-kidney-beans) · [1988 watermelon series](../Food-Experiment-Cherry-Cranberry-Watermelon.md#oral-symptoms-after-watermelon-ingestion)

- The 1986 safflower capsule comparison measured lying and standing blood pressure. It supplies a postural measurement for comparison with later studies, which often report resting pressure alone. The study did not assess Parkinson lightheadedness. [Dated trial](../Food-Experiment-Canola-Safflower.md#safflower-oil-capsules-and-lying-and-standing-blood-pressure)

- An oral protein load worsened tapping and walking during intraduodenal levodopa in three cases. The participant given a smaller load showed no motor decline. This study measured a medication-related food pathway rather than an isolated turkey effect. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Turkey-Shiitake-Arugula.md#1989-oral-protein-load-during-intraduodenal-levodopa)

The dyspepsia trial was an early controlled comparison involving oral turmeric rhizome. The EMA safety summary describes sleepiness in each of the trial arms without separate counts. [sleepiness reports in a turmeric rhizome trial](../Food-Experiment-Turmeric-Followup.md#sleepiness-reports-in-a-turmeric-rhizome-trial)

- The 1985 mouse hot-plate study compared injected drug vehicles. The prior period used oral challenges and measured acute allergy. Neither design isolates a food oil effect on pain. [1981 allergy challenge](../../1980-1984/Food-Experiment-Peanut-Oil-Endpoints.md) · [1985 dissertation](../Food-Experiment-Peanut-Oil-Endpoints.md)

## Collection gaps

- Look for the original 1987 UPDRS chapter, international trials of continuous levodopa delivery, patient and caregiver reports, and prospectively recorded dietary studies. Seek controlled data on the durability and risks of grafts.

- A human dried-seed decoction produced no significant diuresis in the Results comparison. The separate anesthetized-rat blood pressure report remains available as an indexed citation. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Cilantro-Orthostatic.md)

- Human dried-leaf infusion was examined in a controlled sleep questionnaire and in separate small safety sets. Dream recall, urinary volume complaints and acute somnolence describe different outcomes from dream enactment, sudden urinary urgency or validated daytime sleepiness severity. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Lemongrass-Symptoms.md)

## Ingredient and endpoint context, November 15, 2026

- A 1989 patent lists powdered cardamom in a multiherb depression capsule. The patent describes a depression comparison between continued mixture and switching to placebo after three open weeks. Individual herb effects and a Parkinson apathy scale were unreported. Later isolated cineole experiments change both exposure and outcome. [Primary patent context](../Food-Experiment-Cardamom-Endpoint-Replay.md)

## Bowel studies and symptom comparison

The corn-oil bolus study records cramps and loose stools in a subset of healthy volunteers. Its symptom reporting extends beyond the earlier stool-mass measurements, but nonsystematic event capture and dose leave a clinical comparison unresolved. [Original source notes](../Food-Experiment-Bowel-and-Motor-Outcomes.md)

## Bowel and motor outcome measurements

The postvagotomy gastric-emptying experiment adds a timing comparison of a soybean-oil emulsion before a meal. It does not identify an effect on a Parkinson medication or daily OFF time. [Dated original sources](../Food-Experiment-Cumin-and-Motor-Items.md)

An early series measured dystonia under cannabidiol. A transcription identifies the sesame carrier, while the earlier companion report does not. These records give a drug-exposure interpretation and illustrate why oil in a vehicle cannot be treated as an oil trial. [Original report and provenance](../Food-Experiment-Sesame-Oil-Dystonia.md)

## Culinary preparation and symptom follow-up, November 16, 2026

The 1986 lemongrass report recorded a morning sleepiness item after both coded tea and placebo. Its two active conditions were numerically higher. The separate acute component documented daytime symptoms. Neither estimates measured clinical Parkinson severity. [Original source context](../Food-Experiment-Lemongrass-Symptoms.md#morning-sleepiness-and-acute-somnolence-in-the-1986-paper)

## Separate tarragon and mixed-herb experimental diets

The primary diabetes paper separates the tarragon diet from the herbal mixture and limits its observations to feeding, drinking and metabolic outcomes. [Original study](../Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Diabetes-Models.md)

The original report and adapted figure share the same people and differ from later Parkinson fatigue trials by exposure and population. The exposures answer drug-effect questions and supply no Parkinson symptom comparison for the foods themselves.
