# Round 1 observations on subject indexes and abstract collections

The [1988 BMJ index](../Source-Text/1988-PMC1833619-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) distinguishes culinary subjects from similar surnames and clinical phrases. Its culinary entries point to separate articles and supply no outcomes. The 1949 occupational medicine abstracts put some of the same food search hits in a different context, including oil as a laboratory vehicle and foods in safety reports.

The prior five-year bucket includes [an AJNR symposium whole-issue record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6410704/). On November 26 the original body was still unavailable for member-by-member confirmation. Review depth therefore differs between the 1983 issue and the completely imaged 1988 index. The exact source closure records retain the individual access limits.
