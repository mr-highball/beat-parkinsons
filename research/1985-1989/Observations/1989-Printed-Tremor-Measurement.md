# Printed tremor measurements and food exposure

The 1989 instrumented finger-tremor study distinguishes established resting tremor from brief tremor recorded after voluntary movement. In people without obvious tremor during examination, the signal after movement made a diagnostic group difference observable. Four participants had supplementary EMG recordings. The plotted frequency spectra and the clinical symptom at rest are distinct observations. [Printed original scope](../Source-Text/1989-PMID2926426-Finger-Tremor-Rest-and-After-Movement.txt)

The [preceding period](../../1980-1984/README.md) gives historical context for Parkinson research. This diagnostic paper establishes the condition and measurement setting for the recorded traces. It contains no raw fig exposure or estimate of an effect from eating figs.
