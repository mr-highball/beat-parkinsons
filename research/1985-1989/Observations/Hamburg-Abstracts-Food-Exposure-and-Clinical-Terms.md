# Beverage reports and chicken tissue at the Hamburg neurology congress

The 1985 program contains a stroke case-control abstract that lists tea and coffee, without reporting a beverage-specific estimate. A distinct familial movement abstract mentions coffee in its differential discussion. A later Japanese pedigree directly reports drinks as attack triggers within a familial episodic diagnosis. The studies preserve context for the apparent exposure links without conflating fatigue as an outcome with exercise, stress or fatigue as a reported attack precipitant. The chicken contexts concern muscle specimens and experimental avian disease. The traceable 1984, 1985 and 1988 enzyme papers have overlapping source teams and tissue comparisons. Treat the chronology as a research program whose sampling independence still needs verification.
