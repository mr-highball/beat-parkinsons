# Broad bean citation trail in the 1988 Jerusalem abstracts

Kempster and colleagues cite Spengos and Vassilopoulos’s *Improvement of Parkinson’s disease after Vicia faba consumption*, p. 46 of the book of abstracts for the Ninth International Symposium on Parkinson’s Disease, held June 5–9, 1988 in Jerusalem. Rabey and Apaydin also cite the presentation. A copy of the meeting program is identifiable, and the 1990 proceedings have a distinct indexed record. The original page 46, participant counts, preparation, and outcome measures remain unverified.
