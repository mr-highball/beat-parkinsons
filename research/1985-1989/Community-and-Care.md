# Community and Care for 1985–1989

### 1989 — First specialist Parkinson’s nurse post supported by Parkinson’s UK

Parkinson’s UK traces its specialist nursing role to a 1989 post funded by the charity. This retrospective institutional account documents an early service model later evaluated in controlled research.
