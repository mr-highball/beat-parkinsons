# Kiwi serotonin measurements and urinary metabolites

## Kiwi composition and mixed-meal serotonin metabolites

Feldman and Lee’s October 1985 study assayed food serotonin and monitored urinary 5-HIAA after specified foods in healthy participants. Table 1 reports 5.8 ± 0.9 µg/g of serotonin for kiwi fruit. Table 4 includes one kiwi with walnut and banana. The group’s metabolite measurements do not identify a kiwi-only effect, and the study has no attention test or Parkinson group.
