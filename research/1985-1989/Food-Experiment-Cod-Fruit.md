# Food experiment evidence for cod, pineapple, plum, and cantaloupe

## Early adult plum preference and Parkinson case status

Golbe, Farrell and Davis interviewed 81 people with Parkinson and their same-sex married siblings without Parkinson in 1988. The questionnaire asked about eating habits between marriage and age 40. The reported odds ratio for plum preference and Parkinson case status was 0.24. The retrospective measure concerns food preference before diagnosis, with no poaching details, dose or dyskinesia outcome. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3196195/)
