# History and Context for 1985–1989

### 1985 — Founding of the Movement Disorder Society

The society’s Archives Committee records its 1985 founding on the initiative of Stanley Fahn and C. David Marsden. Its journal, *Movement Disorders*, began publication in 1986, providing a specialist venue for Parkinson’s disease and related disorders.
