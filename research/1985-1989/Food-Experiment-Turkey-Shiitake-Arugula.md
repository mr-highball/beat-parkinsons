# Turkey, shiitake, and arugula evidence

## 1989 Oral protein load during intraduodenal levodopa

Frankel and colleagues studied four people with Parkinson's and levodopa-related motor fluctuations. Three had worse tapping and walking after the revised protocol's 60 g oral protein load while plasma levodopa was relatively stable. The first participant showed no motor decline with the earlier lower-load protocol. In Figures 2 and 3, general tremor reappears after the protein drink in two participants. Figure 1 uses a different protocol and shows no comparable post-challenge interval. The table has no numeric tremor estimate, and the rating method does not separate the rest subtype. The challenge was not baked turkey.
