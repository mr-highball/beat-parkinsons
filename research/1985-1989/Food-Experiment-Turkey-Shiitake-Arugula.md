# Turkey, shiitake, and arugula evidence

## 1989 Oral protein load during intraduodenal levodopa

Frankel and colleagues studied four people with Parkinson's and levodopa-related motor fluctuations. Three had worse tapping and walking after the revised protocol's 60 g oral protein load while plasma levodopa was relatively stable. The first participant showed no motor decline with the earlier lower-load protocol. Investigators recorded clinical tremor as well as tapping, walking and dyskinesia, but their before-and-after table gives no separate tremor estimate or preparation-specific turkey exposure.
