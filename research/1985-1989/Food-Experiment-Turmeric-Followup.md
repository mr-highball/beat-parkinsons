# Turmeric preparation and symptom outcomes

## Sleepiness reports in a turmeric rhizome trial

Thamlikitkul and colleagues’ 1989 Thai trial compared oral *Curcuma domestica* rhizome capsules, an active herbal comparison, and placebo for one week in dyspepsia. The European Medicines Agency review of the trial records tiredness and sleepiness among mild effects reported in all three arms. The indexed primary abstract does not enumerate those events and the agency report gives no arm-level sleepiness count. The trial concerns reported adverse effects after an encapsulated rhizome in a different disease.
