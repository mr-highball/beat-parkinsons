# Observations for 1910-1914

## Major observations

- Lewy reported intracellular inclusions in postmortem tissue. Wilson and Casamajor documented adjacent movement syndromes with systemic or occupational leads. [Mechanisms](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md) · [Clinical sources](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md) · [Occupational exposure](../Risk-and-Prevention.md)

## Minor observations

- The synthesis of racemic DOPA and isolation of L-DOPA from broad beans are separate chemistry findings. Neither report measured a clinical food effect. [Mechanisms](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md) · [Food and nutrition](../Food-and-Nutrition.md)

## Comparison with 1905–1909

- Thompson had reported no distinctive parathyroid morphology. Gauthier’s functional hypothesis persisted and was not directly tested in either source. Lewy’s microscopy supplied a different class of tissue observation. [Prior observations](../../1905-1909/Observations/README.md)
