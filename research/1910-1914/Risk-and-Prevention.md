# Risk and prevention, 1910-1914

### 1913 — Casamajor investigates a movement syndrome in mineral workers

Louis Casamajor published a report of neurological illness in workers exposed to mineral dust and raised manganese as a candidate. The publication is relevant to occupational causes of secondary parkinsonism. The attribution was provisional and the report supplies no estimate of idiopathic Parkinson’s risk.

[Casamajor, JAMA, original article](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/article-abstract/214461)
