# Clinical findings and diagnosis, 1910-1914

### 1912 — Wilson defines a distinct lenticular degeneration syndrome

S. A. Kinnier Wilson characterized a progressive disorder with tremor, rigidity, and liver disease and described pathology in the lenticular nucleus. The original series provides a differential diagnosis and a model for connecting a movement phenotype with a systemic disorder.

[Wilson, Brain, original monograph](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/34.4.295)

### 1913 — Collier records a childhood tremor resembling shaking palsy

James Collier reported a child with unilateral rhythmic tremor, asymmetry, and a hemiplegic gait. He described the tremor as resembling paralysis agitans. The comparison documents a pediatric mimic of a motor sign.

[Collier, Proceedings of the Royal Society of Medicine, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2006337/)
