# Food and nutrition, 1910-1914

### 1913 — Guggenheim isolates L-DOPA from broad-bean material

Markus Guggenheim isolated naturally occurring L-DOPA from *Vicia faba* in a chemistry study. This establishes a food-source constituent relevant to later Parkinson’s pharmacology. Effects of eating broad beans, including benefit or harm in Parkinson’s disease, were outside the study.

[Guggenheim, Hoppe-Seyler’s Zeitschrift für physiologische Chemie](https://doi.org/10.1515/bchm2.1913.88.4.276)
