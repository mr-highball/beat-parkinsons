The account distinguishes green and Bohea teas, compares the size and texture of leaves after infusion, and notes the use of fragrant blossoms in exported teas. A speaker reports that Chinese and Japanese consumers drink tea after aging it and without sugar or milk. The stated reason for aging is a perceived change in narcotic properties. This records ideas about preparation and effects in that publication, with no symptom outcome that can be graded.
