# Drugs and remedies

### 1956 — Benztropine in 302 patients with different parkinsonian syndromes

Doshay’s clinical series reported relief of rigidity, tremor, insomnia and some gait problems with benztropine. The paper also documented severe muscle weakness at higher doses. The uncontrolled series mixed clinical subgroups and cannot isolate efficacy or establish a safety profile comparable with modern trials. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1956.02970280011004)

### 1956 — Chlorpromazine adverse reactions observed in a treated cohort

Hall, Jackson and Swain observed neurological adverse reactions in 36 of 90 people exposed to chlorpromazine over about two months. This treatment cohort corroborates the earlier clinical warning about drug-associated movement syndromes. The total covers the range of reported neurotoxic reactions. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1956.02970030032008)

### 1959 — Chlorphenoxamine in a clinical series

Doshay and Constable tested chlorphenoxamine in 25 patients initially and then treated 136 patients who had responded poorly to other drugs. The authors reported better movement and endurance in some patients with akinesia and weakness. Tremor seldom improved and sometimes worsened. The abstract gives no validated fatigue scale or controlled comparison. A subsequent blinded comparison examined the motor findings. [Primary report abstract, May 2, 1959](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/article-abstract/325874) · [1961 controlled comparison](../1960-1964/Drugs-and-Remedies.md#1961--controlled-chlorphenoxamine-trial)
