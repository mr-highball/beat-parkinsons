Title: March 1955 California Medicine administrative context and the strawberry hemangioma program item
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1532759/
PMCID: PMC1532759
Publication date: 1955-03
Date basis: California Medicine volume 82 issue 3 masthead and NLM administrative record, first folio 151.
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Full bound-volume scan: https://archive.org/details/californiamedici8216cali
Text coverage: Distilled administrative context from the University of Maryland, Baltimore scan. The bound issue was checked with layout text and page images.

Physical PDF page 271 confirms the March 1955 issue, volume 82 number 3, and printed folio 151. The same bound volume places a pre-convention program with a presentation about the natural history of untreated strawberry hemangiomas on physical page 362. In that context strawberry refers to infant vascular lesions. The program entry supplies neither an assigned fruit preparation nor any comparison of recurrent falls.

The print-volume scan provides a wider view of the journal contents and meeting program than the bibliographic administrative stub. Its physical page numbers do not match printed folios.
