Gotoh, Japanese Journal of Veterinary Science 18(5):171–177, DOI 10.1292/jvms1939.18.171. The 1956 issue's listed October 30 date precedes the printed December 25, 1956 receipt. The original pp.171–172 applied the precursor crude bark fraction in water or saline to fish and earthworms. Earthworm activity initially increased and then declined to paralysis. Fish had respiratory disturbance and convulsions. This is a related toxicology report with no oral culinary or patient rigidity comparison. It documents preparation lineage for the 1960 mouse report.
