Bark identity in the volume 17 paper

Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jvms1939/17/6/17_6_205/_article/-char/ja
Coverage: Original publisher scan, printed pp.205–206 and 213–216, factual extract.

Jiro Gotoh, Japanese Journal of Veterinary Science 17(6):205–216, DOI 10.1292/jvms1939.17.205. J-STAGE assigns this volume to 1955. Its listed December 30 publication precedes the January 17, 1956 receipt date printed on the first page. Both dates fall within this period. The original p.205 identifies dried bark of Japanese Xanthoxylum piperitum collected in Iwate and Fukushima as the source of the material. The author's comparisons also mention fruit and leaves. The studies evaluated animal toxicity, including convulsions and respiratory arrest in frogs, and distinguished the named toxic bark fraction from separate crystals. The isolated fraction is distinct from ground culinary fruit. No Parkinson rigidity outcome was measured.
