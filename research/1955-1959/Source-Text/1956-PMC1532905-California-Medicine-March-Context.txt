Physical PDF page 241 confirms volume 84 number 3, March 1956, and printed folio 137. The pre-convention program has separate foliation and starts with a contents page at physical page 335. Its dermatology schedule on physical page 348 lists a presentation on the natural history of untreated strawberry hemangiomas. Strawberry denotes a lesion in this member. There is no assigned berry or post-exposure fall outcome in the program entry.

The NLM stub's initial printed page 2 must be distinguished from the main volume's running clinical pagination. Bibliographic scope is the administrative package and nearby bound print material.
