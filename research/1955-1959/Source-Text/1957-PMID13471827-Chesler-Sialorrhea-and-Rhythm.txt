In a single person with episodic excessive salivation, the authors observed a fluctuating atrioventricular conduction pattern during the salivation episodes. The figures depict electrocardiographic intervals. The report does not give a Parkinson diagnosis, test fig intake or quantify a dietary effect on drooling.
