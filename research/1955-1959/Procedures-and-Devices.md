# Procedures and devices

### 1955 — Chemopallidectomy for parkinsonism

Cooper and Poloukhine described a historical procedure intended to produce a localized lesion in the globus pallidus in older patients with parkinsonism. It illustrates the shift from indirect vascular injury to direct targeting. The article is early clinical experience and cannot determine comparative safety or effectiveness. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1532-5415.1955.tb00936.x)

### 1959 — Chemical lesions of pallidal and thalamic targets

Cooper documented surgical experience targeting the pallidum and thalamus in parkinsonism and dystonia, with symptom changes recorded on the side opposite surgery. The intervention was destructive and the report grouped distinct movement disorders. Its role here is to trace evolving anatomy, symptom targets, and limitations. [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1869056/)
