# Community and care

### 1957 — Parkinson’s Disease Foundation founded

The successor organization’s institutional history dates the founding of the Parkinson’s Disease Foundation by William Black to 1957 and describes the organization’s relationship with research at Columbia. This is an advocacy and research infrastructure milestone based on an institutional retrospective. [Source](https://www.parkinson.org/about-us/vision-mission/history)

### 1959 — Margaret Bourke-White’s first-person account

The LIFE magazine archive documents Bourke-White’s account of living and working with Parkinson’s disease, including diagnostic delays, exercise, surgery, and continuing to use her camera. It adds the perspective of a person with Parkinson’s and describes functional outcomes in daily life. It is an individual account. [Source](https://www.life.com/lifestyle/parkinsons-disease-life-magazine/)
