# Strawberry as a clinical term

The 1955 exhibits and the 1956 pre-convention program use “strawberry” in the vascular-lesion name. This term can retrieve a clinical result whose intervention never involved fruit. The 1956 issue also places ingredient listings, caffeine administration, and a separate Parkinson pharmacology report in adjacent sections. Their treatments and outcomes belong to different members.
