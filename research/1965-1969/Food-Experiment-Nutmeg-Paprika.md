# Nutmeg and paprika evidence

## Historical controlled nutmeg experiment

Beattie’s 1968 paper describes a controlled investigation of nutmeg intoxication. The indexed record provides the bibliographic details and no abstract. The original results and methods remain an accession lead for acute neurobehavioral effects. ## Paprika and salivary response study

Blumberger and Glatzel's 1965 report occupies pages 222–237. Its first page describes an earlier comparison with strong salivary stimulation after paprika and substantial variability within and between participants. The introduction motivates new paprika and chilli work. Pages 223–237 remain gated. Neither the smoking of any botanical preparation nor a numerical salivation or drooling result for this report was established by the first page.
