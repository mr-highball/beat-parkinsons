Title: Effect of onions on blood fibrinolytic activity
Authors: I Sudhakaran Menon, R Y Kendal, H A Dewar, D J Newell
Primary original PDF: https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/3/5614/351.full.pdf
Continuation: https://www.bmj.com/content/3/5614/352.full.pdf
Identifiers: PMID 5662989, PMCID PMC1991202, DOI 10.1136/bmj.3.5614.351
Date: 1968-08-10
Coverage: factual extract of Methods and Results from the primary paper

The series included 22 patients convalescing from bleeding peptic ulcers. Fourteen had a fat-rich breakfast with or without 60 g fried onion on two days, with order counterbalanced. Eight underwent the corresponding test with boiled onion. The investigators measured postprandial blood fibrinolytic activity and related blood measures. The paper reports an increase in fibrinolytic activity with both cooked onion preparations compared with an expected decrease with the control meal. There was no dedicated neurological or speech endpoint. The primary pages were accessible through indexed publisher PDFs, while the native BMJ request returned HTTP 403.
