Repeated saliva collection during mustard rice meals

Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/247995765_Wirkungen_von_Riech-_und_Schmeckstoffen_der_Nahrung_auf_die_Speichelsekretion_Untersuchungen_uber_Wirkungsdauer_und_Sekretmenge
Coverage: Original author abstract, factual extract.

Five healthy participants supplied whole saliva measurements while fasting and during test meals. Unspiced rice produced about threefold fasting secretion and mustard rice about fourfold. All five had higher secretion with the mustard meal. Mustard form, dose and statistical uncertainty require the original full text. Session duration was 14 minutes. Drooling and Parkinson disease were outside the design.
