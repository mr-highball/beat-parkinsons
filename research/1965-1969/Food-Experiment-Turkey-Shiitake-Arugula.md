# Turkey, shiitake, and arugula evidence

## 1969 Aroma compounds in fat from roasted turkey

Pippen, Mecchi, and Nonaka identified aroma compounds in fat obtained from roasted turkey and compared sources of aroma in cooked poultry fat. The analysis characterizes the food stimulus and provides no measurement of an eater's smell function after ingestion. [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2621.1969.tb12799.x)
