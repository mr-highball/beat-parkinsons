# Clinical features and diagnosis

### 1965 — Clinical differences after encephalitis

A contemporary report distinguished the younger age at which postencephalitic parkinsonism most often appeared from paralysis agitans. This continued clinical efforts to separate etiologies with overlapping motor signs. The original diagnostic labels and ascertainment influence interpretation. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneur.1965.00460270003001)

### 1967 — Onset, progression, mortality and clinical stages

Hoehn and Yahr characterized progression and mortality in a clinic population and introduced a five-stage clinical description ranging from unilateral involvement to severe disability. The staging system makes motor disability easier to compare across studies. Clinic ascertainment and treatment before modern regimens limit direct extrapolation to current outcomes. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6067254/)

### 1968 — Corticodentatonigral degeneration as a separate clinicopathological entity

Rebeiz, Kolodny and Richardson reported characteristic cortical and subcortical pathology in three cases in work later associated with corticobasal degeneration. Its relevance is the differential diagnosis of atypical parkinsonism. Case-based pathology establishes the finding in the examined patients. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5634369/)
