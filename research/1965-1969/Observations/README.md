# Observations for 1965–1969

## Major observations

Repeated oral DOPA and L-DOPA studies established symptomatic benefit under chronic regimens. Hoehn and Yahr’s staging and outcomes gave later studies a common clinical reference. [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) · [Diagnosis](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md)

## Minor observations

A vitamin B6 interaction was reported for administered pyridoxine during L-DOPA treatment. Experimental neuronal injury and studies of chronic manganese poisoning raised distinct questions about how parkinsonian motor features can arise. [Food and nutrition](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) · [Models](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md) · [Risk](../Risk-and-Prevention.md)

## Comparison with 1960–1964

[Previous observations](../../1960-1964/Observations/README.md)

The earlier discordant acute or small-scale DOPA reports were followed by longer oral regimens and blinded evidence. That strengthened confidence in symptomatic benefit and brought tolerability and treatment interactions into clearer view. Natural history and differential diagnosis also became more explicit.

- Roasted turkey aroma was chemically characterized as a food property. Later olfactory hypothesis reviews need a separate consumer smell endpoint. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Turkey-Shiitake-Arugula.md#1969-aroma-compounds-in-fat-from-roasted-turkey)

## Collection gaps

Collect full trial details, records of rehabilitation, and contemporaneous examples of long-term drug response. The vitamin report does not provide evidence about consumption of particular foods.

- Breakfast challenges establish that fried or boiled onion can be isolated in a controlled human meal design. Follow-up separated the cooking water and raw tissue. The outcomes were blood fibrinolysis throughout this interval. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Onion-Speech.md)

## Culinary exposure and endpoint follow-up, November 16, 2026

The mustard meal series followed the preceding period's same-author spice series and quantified an acute mixed-meal secretion effect in healthy participants. The two reports may share participants, and neither available abstract gives a Parkinson drooling measure. [Source record](../Food-Experiment-Mustard-Meal-Saliva.md)
