# Primary magnesium-depletion study access

The 1969 Medicine and Annals articles have separate accessions. Both concern experimental depletion in people and may report overlapping observations from one investigator's program. The earlier two surgical case histories track gastrointestinal losses and the route of replacement, and the earlier bibliography identifies further clinical tetany discussions. No separate visual hallucination effect is established from the historical abstracts or indexes. The original experimental bodies are still needed for symptom and cohort comparisons.
