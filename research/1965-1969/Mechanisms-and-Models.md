# Mechanisms and models

### 1968 — 6-hydroxydopamine-induced degeneration

Urban Ungerstedt reported experimentally induced degeneration of central monoamine neurons. This became an important basis for animal models that study the effects of dopamine pathway injury. A chemically induced lesion captures selected features of human parkinsonism and leaves the origins and course of idiopathic disease unresolved. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5718510/)
