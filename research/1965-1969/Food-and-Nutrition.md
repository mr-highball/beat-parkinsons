# Food and nutrition

### 1967 — Caffeine in a chlorphenoxamine comparison

Strang compared chlorphenoxamine combined with caffeine with methixene in patients with Parkinson’s disease. PubMed indexes a controlled clinical trial in the July–August 1967 issue and supplies no abstract. The full article and outcome tables were unavailable in the publisher and alternate public routes checked. The indexed treatment was a drug combination, and the accessible record supplies no brewed coffee or fatigue result. A 1961 trial tested chlorphenoxamine alone. [Primary citation, PMID 5340692](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5340692/) · [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1552-4604.1967.tb00050.x) · [Earlier controlled comparison](../1960-1964/Drugs-and-Remedies.md#1961--controlled-chlorphenoxamine-trial)

### 1969 — Vitamin B6 administration reduced L-DOPA effect

Duvoisin and colleagues reported reversal of L-DOPA effects after administered pyridoxine (vitamin B6) in parkinsonism. This is an adverse interaction with levodopa in the studied treatment setting. The exposure was administered pyridoxine, and the report did not measure ordinary dietary intake. Later studies examined whether carbidopa modified the interaction. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5374494/)
