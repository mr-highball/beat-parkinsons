Harrison used the term rheumatic gout for a painful abdominal episode. He reported initially worse pain and later relief after powdered ginger taken with sago and brandy. In his account, the later bedtime opium follows the described relief. He reported urinary discomfort the next morning. The combined intake and natural course leave the contribution of each substance unresolved.

Reported direction: Initially worse pain followed by reported relief in one episode.
Authenticity: The letter begins on printed page 527 and continues on page 528, ahead of an unrelated next contribution.
Efficacy evidence: First-person historical experience with multiple simultaneous exposures. Constipation severity and Parkinson outcomes were not observed.
Open trail: Verify independent observations of both pain phases after a specified ginger preparation.
