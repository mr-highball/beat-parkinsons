# Banana, eggs, fatigue and related symptom evidence

## Bananas after breast surgery and anxiety reporting

First published March 14, 2025. The report describes random allocation of 154 women after breast surgery to one extra banana daily or no banana. The published endpoint means were 27.64 ± 4.27 and 32.16 ± 4.33 on the Self-Rating Anxiety Scale, with 77 per arm. Table 2 reports t = −2.08. The stated means, SDs and sample sizes imply t ≈ −6.52 for the stated independent-sample comparison. The paper also reports 58 scores ≥50, hard to reconcile with the table given the reported score spread. Scoring or reporting may differ across sections. The direction was recorded as a sensitivity lead, with no reliance on its effect size or significance. Raw preparation and relevance to Parkinson disease remain unclear. ## Dried basil powder and postpartum adverse events

Table 5 and Section 3.6 report one nausea event with dried basil and one gastrointestinal complaint with placebo. The trial planned depression and sleep outcomes. It supplies no constipation frequency, severity or prospective ascertainment from which to infer bowel benefit. This is a focused reread of an already archived original.
