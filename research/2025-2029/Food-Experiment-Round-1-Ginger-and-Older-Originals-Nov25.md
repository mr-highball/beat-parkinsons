# Round 1 ginger, saffron and older original-access review

The November 25 review inspected 12 original article bodies covering 26 claim–source pairs. Thirteen pairs supplied related context and 13 had verified scope mismatches. The published queue contains 31,764 pairs across 21,412 sources. [Pair dispositions and queue proof](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/ginger-lipids-and-bmj-originals-nov25/README.md)

## Ginger and traditional preparations

- The Eucommia review's black and green teas were made from Eucommia leaves. Table 3 separately records ginger juice as a processing material for the bark. A Chinese historical article describes this preparation for customary back and knee indications. The articles give no measured Parkinson pain response attributable to culinary ginger. [Eucommia review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC10834829-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Traditional practice](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2024-Ginger-Processed-Eucommia-Back-and-Knee-Pain.txt) · [Cross-period observation](../2020-2024/Observations/2024-Eucommia-Ginger-Preparation-and-Pain.md)
- The Scutellaria review documents ginger as an excipient for processed root. The primary inhalation study tested essential oil in a mouse forced-swim assay, where the abstract provides no ginger-specific percentage of change. The Phlai experiment used a different ginger species and measured hippocampal outcomes. The retro-curcuminoid article studies synthetic analogs. These records keep the actual route, species and endpoint visible. [Root processing](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC11388511-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Stem cell review](../2010-2014/Source-Text/PMC2885092-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Original inhalation abstract](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID16114490-Ginger-Inhalation-Mouse-Activity.txt) · [Phlai comparison](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7273477-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Synthetic analogs](../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC6155822-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)
- Four original bodies contain lexical matches unrelated to the frozen ginger or dried date exposures. The remaining research statement concerns recruitment and retention, where dates describe time. ## Saffron and caffeine citations

- The original C57BL/6J mouse study corrects the rat description at reference 68 of the crocin review. It studied isolated crocin-I and depressive-like behavior after corticosterone exposure. The review also cites an already indexed saffron extract comparison outside Parkinson disease. The original has no ground-saffron Parkinson comparison or insomnia endpoint. [Original abstract and excerpt](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMID31614158-Crocin-I-Depression-Model.txt) · [Crocin review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC11022409-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Saffron extract comparison](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID15707766-Saffron-Stigma-Fluoxetine.txt)
- The caffeine review reports black tea incidence and caffeine capsule motor results in separate studies. The incidence cohort gives no treatment outcome and the capsule trial does not assign brewed black tea. This review adds related context to the published bradykinesia scorecard. [Caffeine review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC11593559-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Incidence cohort](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2007-PMID18156141-Singapore-Black-Tea-Incidence.txt) · [Cafe-PD](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28954882-Postuma-Cafe-PD-Adverse-Outcomes.txt)
