# Capsaicin and anxiety, 2025–2029

## Capsaicin and recorded anxiety in a runner crossover

The 2026 report randomized trained runners to four acute crossover conditions, of which one used isolated capsaicin without caffeine and one used placebo. Twenty completed. Supplement Tables S12–S13 report anxiety in 5% in both arms immediately after the run and 15% versus 10% at 24 hours. The model pooling times gives capsaicin versus placebo OR 1.43 (95% CI 0.26–7.74, p=.671). Caffeine and combination arms had larger estimates. These were imprecise anxiety-event results under exercise, without a population with Parkinson disease or smoked paprika.
