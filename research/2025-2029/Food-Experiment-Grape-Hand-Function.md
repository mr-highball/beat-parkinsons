# Grape preparations and hand function

## Registered grape powder and hand strength outcomes

First posted October 6, 2025. The single-group protocol plans 35 older women and two 36 g grape-powder packets mixed with water per day during two two-week intervals. Secondary outcomes include grip and pinch strength. The available record gives a recruiting status last verified October 2025 and no posted results. It provides no isolated raw-grape Parkinson or dexterity contrast. [Official grape-powder registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07208916) · [Local text](Source-Text/2025-NCT07208916-Grape-Powder-Hand-Strength-Extract.txt)

## Enovita subset report and the missing tapping outcome

First published April 30, 2026. Papa and colleagues identify NCT06309914 and report 16 Enovita grape-seed extract and 16 placebo participants from the parent four-arm trial. The paper's two outcome tables concern working-memory continuous performance and mood. It provides no numerical registered bilateral finger-tapping result. The subset is part of the registry study family and does not independently estimate raw-grape Parkinson hand dexterity.
