The Cochrane multiple-sclerosis review led back to the 24-person Svendsen crossover. The median pain comparison favored the drug period (4.0 versus 5.0, p=.02). Both periods used sesame oil. The 2005 secondary report and later review reuse the same trial. The frozen 0144 question needs an oil-specific oral contrast and a matched pain population. Separating the existing topical and carrier studies into their own trial families makes that boundary visible.
