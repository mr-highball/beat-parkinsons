# Almond preparations in sleep, attention, saliva and movement studies

## Whole almond chewing and stimulated salivary flow

Fitri and colleagues published December 9, 2025. Whole almond chewing and wax-gum chewing increased stimulated salivary flow in participants with and without caries. Table 3 gives larger mean increases for wax gum than almonds in both strata. The post-chewing flow measurements used whole nuts and did not score spontaneous drooling, study oil ingestion or enroll a Parkinson cohort. [Local primary text, Table 3](Source-Text/2025-Fitri-Almond-Salivary-Flow.txt) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.22159/ijap.2025.v17s4.03)

## Acetonic almond skin extract and movement in a zebrafish model

Carneiro and colleagues first published March 12, 2026. Zebrafish larvae exposed to an acetonic almond skin extract in a 6-OHDA model showed different responses across endpoints. The lower extract exposure yielded a nonsignificant trend on distance traveled. The higher co-exposure increased immobility relative to the lower co-exposure, acetone and extract-only controls, despite preservation of a dopaminergic fluorescence signal. A change in swim immobility or turning angle does not measure Parkinson dystonia, rigidity, falls or daily off time. The extract was an almond skin preparation administered in water. [Local primary text](Source-Text/PMC13027280.txt) · [Primary paper, Sections 2.2–2.3 and Figures 2–3](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms27062590)

## Powdered almond and attention in Alzheimer disease

Mohajeri and colleagues reported a three-month 60-participant comparison in the March–April 2026 issue. Participants continued baseline prescriptions. The intervention added powdered sweet almonds and rock candy. The MoCA attention subscore changed from 5.26 to 5.20 with the almond mixture and 4.86 to 4.83 in controls, with no clear difference in change (p=.776). Some memory and sleep-quality measures favored the intervention. The intervention used almond powder, and Parkinson disease and dream enactment were outside the study. [Primary paper, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13092157/)

## Sweet almond oil massage and delirium after myocardial infarction

Kharatha and colleagues first published June 23, 2026. In a 198-participant randomized cardiac care trial, unscented sweet almond oil was the medium for head and face massage. Other arms received lavender-oil massage or no massage. The NEECHAM total improved in both massage arms and the time-by-group test was significant (p<.001). Sleep questionnaire scores also differed over time (time-by-group p=.019). This reports delirium scores and subjective sleep, with no attention subscore, hallucination frequency or REM dream enactment outcome. The no-massage arm does not isolate oil from massage, and the oil was used externally. The separate 2020 postoperative trial did not find a significant group difference on the daily NEECHAM comparisons. [Primary PDF, Tables 2–3](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0965229926000816/pdfft?download=true&isDTMRedir=true) · [Earlier cardiac surgery trial](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Almond-Oil-Followup.md#sweet-almond-oil-massage-after-open-heart-surgery)

## Whole almonds and polysomnography in adults with poor sleep

Desai and colleagues first published July 27, 2026. Adults with poor sleep received raw whole almonds or isocaloric savory snacks for 20 weeks. Adjusted endline polysomnography showed REM latency of 158.17 versus 158.20 minutes (p=.998) and REM duration of 37.30 versus 36.43 minutes (p=.776), even though the total subjective PSQI score favored almonds (p=.013). This trial recorded sleep stages and did not report dream enactment or REM sleep without atonia as outcomes. Participants received whole almonds, and the study was outside Parkinson disease. The linked original lists CTRI/2023/02/049727. [Local primary text, Table 3](Source-Text/PMC13454318.txt#L276) · [Primary paper](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnut.2026.1892442) · [Registry](https://ctri.nic.in/Clinicaltrials/showallp.php?mid1=80395&EncHid=&userName=Almond)

## Within-arm sleep changes in the Desai supplement

Supplementary Table 2 reports unadjusted REM duration falling from 45.87 to 38.57 minutes in the whole-almond arm (paired p=.003) and from 40.43 to 35.15 minutes in the control arm (paired p=.045). The adjusted between-arm comparison in the main paper remains inconclusive. The supplement gives group-level tables for sleep and assay characteristics. It supplies no dream enactment, REM without atonia or participant-level observations.
