# Carrot and sleepiness in a plant-rich nutrition comparison

## Plant rich diet and Epworth scores in Parkinson disease

Tağraf and colleagues compared a tailored plant-rich, carbohydrate-restricted and time-restricted program plus usual care against usual care over six months in 40 people with Parkinson disease. Mean Epworth Sleepiness Scale scores were 6.65 to 6.50 with usual care and 6.55 to 6.70 with the program. Table 2 reports a post-intervention p=1.000. The public registry lists the motor outcome and does not give a separate Epworth endpoint. The trial does not specify assignment or measured intake of raw carrot.
