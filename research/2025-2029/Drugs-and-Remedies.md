# Drugs and Remedies

### 2025 — Phase 3 exenatide misses motor progression endpoint

The multicenter randomized trial found no significant difference in off-medication motor-score change at 96 weeks. This larger trial directly revisited the earlier exenatide signal. Lixisenatide was studied separately and is a different drug. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39919773/)

### 2025 — Ambroxol in Parkinson’s disease dementia

A 52-week double-blind phase 2 trial in 55 patients found no between-group differences on its primary outcomes. The study collected tolerability and target-engagement data and did not establish a cognitive benefit. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40587145/)

### 2026 — PADOVA prasinezumab trial

In 586 people with early Parkinson’s on stable symptomatic monotherapy, the phase 2b trial missed its primary endpoint of time to confirmed motor progression. The hazard ratio was 0.84 with a 95% confidence interval of 0.69–1.01. Exploratory findings require independent confirmation. [Primary source](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0140673626008652)

### 2026 — LRRK2 inhibitor BIIB122 misses endpoints in LUMA

The sponsor reported that the phase 2b trial missed its primary and secondary endpoints in early-stage Parkinson’s. This SEC-filed sponsor announcement is a top-line disclosure. It supplies less methodological and subgroup detail than a full results paper. [Primary source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1714899/000171489926000070/ex9912026pressrelease52126.htm)

### 2026 — Tavapadon trials and US approval

Phase 3 placebo-controlled trials measured improvements in motor and daily function scores in early disease and more daily on time without troublesome dyskinesia as an add-on in people with fluctuations. The FDA approved tavapadon for adults with Parkinson’s disease on September 25. The trials and approval address symptomatic treatment. [Early-stage trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13005179/) · [Add-on trial](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2026.0577) · [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220415Orig1s000lbl.pdf)
