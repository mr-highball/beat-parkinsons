# Lemongrass and lemon in a postpartum comparison

## Mixed lemongrass and lemon drink and self-reported sleep duration

The postpartum comparison reports more participants sleeping over eight hours in the group offered a combined drink. The paper labels the same two counts as groups and as before-and-after observations. The results do not establish a valid change contrast, a lemongrass-only effect or dream enactment.
