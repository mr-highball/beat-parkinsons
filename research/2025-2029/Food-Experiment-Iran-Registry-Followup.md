# Iranian dietary trial registrations, 2025–2029

## Standardized ginger powder in Parkinson disease

IRCT20251028067794N1 was registered April 22, 2026. Maziar Ghasemi and Reza Amani’s double-blind comparison at Isfahan University of Medical Sciences targets 48 people with Parkinson’s. The intervention is two 500 mg standardized ginger powder tablets daily for 12 weeks, compared with corn-starch placebo. The primary outcome is total MDS-UPDRS. Secondary outcomes include PDQ-39 and oxidative stress markers. Individual dyskinesia and stride-length outcomes are unspecified. On November 12, 2026, the registry showed recruitment complete, with actual recruitment dates blank and no posted outcome results. English and Persian title, registration and ethics identifier, investigator, and institutional searches located no public outcome report. The preparation and population make this a follow-up lead for the ground-ginger claims `0271` and `0566`. [English registration](https://irct.ir/trial/87235) · [Persian registration](https://fa.irct.ir/trial/87235) · [Publication trail](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/iran-ginger-and-saffron-publication-trails.md)

The registry gives planned recruitment dates of April 21 and July 22, 2026. Both language versions retained April 22 as their latest update on the November 12 recheck. The first secondary outcome in Persian names serum superoxide dismutase in its description and time point, but its measurement field specifies an enzyme immunoassay for glial cell line-derived neurotrophic factor. The corresponding English fields refer to superoxide dismutase throughout. The registration therefore leaves the implemented assay unresolved. [English secondary outcomes](https://irct.ir/trial/87235#trial-secondary_outcome) · [Persian secondary outcomes](https://fa.irct.ir/trial/87235#trial-secondary_outcome)

Both registry versions exclude severe anxiety at enrollment. Neither names an anxiety-specific outcome for `0937`. The data-sharing fields describe release of a protocol, statistical analysis plan and de-identified participant data beginning six months after primary results publication. No documents or dataset were linked in the November 12 recheck. [English registry, eligibility and sharing plan](https://irct.ir/trial/87235#trial-sharing_plan) · [Ginger outcome and publication recheck](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/ginger-gait-dyskinesia-anxiety-recheck.md)

## Quercetin and bromelain combination

IRCT20201129049534N13 was registered May 30, 2026. The triple-blind trial compares a proprietary combination capsule with placebo. Prespecified endpoints include clinical stage, sleep quality, and DASS-21. The registry showed recruitment in progress and no results. The study assigns the two constituents together. [Primary registration](https://irct.ir/trial/89515)
