## Turmeric, postural immobility and clinical rigidity

Cui's rodent study measured latency to release an imposed posture in bar and grid tests, with shorter latency after oral isolated curcumin pretreatment in the rotenone model. A three-week congress report gives a favorable latency direction for a turmeric extract and an isolated constituent, with dose-specific cohort and protocol questions still open. A separate full publication by the same research group reports no detected rearing effect of oral turmeric extract compared with rotenone alone. Its positive abstract wording includes findings beyond the rearing comparison. Similar investigators and doses leave lineage uncertain. [Cui primary figure](https://www.spandidos-publications.com/10.3892/mmr.2015.4657?text=fulltext) · [Local congress record](Source-Text/2025-MDS-Darbinyan-Turmeric-Catalepsy-Abstract.txt) · [Original cylinder comparison](Source-Text/2025-PMID40694716-Manukyan-Turmeric-Rotenone-Cylinder.txt)

The nine-week injected-curcumin paper provides a separate reported catalepsy figure. The 2009 cell study observed enhanced toxicity after adding isolated curcumin during paraquat exposure, and the 2014 fly study observed favorable climbing in young flies without comparable rescue at a later life stage. These outcomes describe different models. Their directions remain visible in the catalog.
