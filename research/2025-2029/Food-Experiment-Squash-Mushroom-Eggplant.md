# Squash, mushroom, eggplant, and zucchini evidence

## 2025 Isolated ergothioneine cognition and sleep trial

Zajac and colleagues randomized 147 adults with subjective memory concerns to 10 mg, 25 mg, or placebo daily for 16 weeks. The 25 mg group showed a short-lived within-group improvement in composite memory. The paper describes null or limited effects for other cognitive measures and improved subjective sleep initiation at the higher dose. This trial used a compound, with no button mushroom, Parkinson cohort, or dream-enactment endpoint. [Primary full report](https://doi.org/10.3390/nutraceuticals5030015)

## 2025 Whole UV-treated cremini and cognitive performance

Glover and colleagues compared six weeks of fresh ultraviolet-exposed cremini Agaricus bisporus with breadcrumbs in 41 middle-aged and older adults who completed the trial. The intervention permitted raw, quickly sautéed, microwaved, or boiled mushrooms. Table 6 reports inconclusive time-by-group effects across the five RBANS domains. RBANS attention mean change was 4.00 ± 2.61 SEM with mushrooms and 2.29 ± 2.41 with control (interaction p = .705). Both groups improved in immediate memory. This separates a whole Agaricus cultivar in habitual diets from the earlier mixed-species feeding comparison, with no roasted button assignment or Parkinson population. ## 2026 Agaricus bisporus protein extract in a Parkinson mouse model

Zhgenti and colleagues orally administered a tyrosinase-enriched Agaricus bisporus protein fraction to MPTP-treated mice for 20 days. The accessible primary abstract reports fewer beam-walking errors (p < .001) and changes in dopaminergic and inflammatory measures. The intervention was an isolated fraction, and the measured motor outcome provides no assessment of olfaction or executive function. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42133882/)

## 2026 Dried oyster mushroom and task switching

Cha and colleagues randomized 80 healthy adults aged 60–80 to dried oyster mushroom or placebo for 12 weeks. Task-switch accuracy, Corsi block, and N-back outcomes had no reported intervention-related change. Task-switch reaction time worsened in the placebo group, with a reported time-by-group interaction of p = .029. This isolates Pleurotus ostreatus, a species also used in the 2024 combined trial. The study supplies no roasted Agaricus bisporus or Parkinson outcome.
