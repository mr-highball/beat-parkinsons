# Round 1 priority-three original-source access, November 22, 2026

Twelve claim–source pairs remain queued across nine original publications. The records below summarize what can be read now and identify the outstanding source material. Eligibility and clinical direction remain separate judgments. [Exact pair map](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/printed-pages-and-clinical-context-nov22/retained-priority-three.tsv)

- **Saffron and depression, insomnia, rigidity or fatigue.** The Kaur and Gupta review has a verified abstract and Crossref reference deposit. Its preview and reference list point to distinct exposures, including isolated crocin, saffron-containing fly feed, and cognitive or motor outcomes. The complete review tables remain inaccessible. The Bej and Abdian reviews likewise provide partial text and references, with their full study tables still open. These three reviews do not supply independent symptom estimates from new trial participants. [Kaur and Gupta](Source-Text/2025-Kaur-Gupta-Parkinson-Herbal-Review-References.txt) · [Bej](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-PMID38446350-Bej-Saffron-Review-Citations.txt) · [Abdian](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-PMID39577115-Abdian-Saffron-Review-Citations.txt)
- **Saffron and fatigue, 0485.** The first paper in the natural-products series addresses Parkinson disease. The second, which is the queued record, addresses Alzheimer drug discovery. The second paper's complete original remains open. [Archive scope](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID20874701-Natural-Products-Alzheimer-Review.txt)
- **Rosemary and depression, 0128.** The rosmarinic acid review gives ingredient-constituent leads. The complete review remains needed to verify individual studies, populations and endpoints. [Archive scope](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32725282-Rosmarinic-Review-Citations.txt)
- **Ginger and dyskinesia or anxiety.** The dopamine-agonist review has a ginger nausea lead and its original wording remains open. The permeability article examines isolated compounds in an artificial assay, with the article body still restricted. The recognized experimental measurements concern drug adverse effects or transport and do not measure culinary ginger effects on Parkinson dyskinesia or anxiety. [Dopamine-agonist review](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2010-PMID20359261-Wood-Ginger-Nausea-Review.txt) · [Permeability article](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMID31476432-Simon-Ginger-Permeability-Abstract.txt)
- **Cranberry and depression, 0722.** The public abstracts discuss cranberry for recurrent urinary infection, acupuncture for depression and tai chi for Parkinson disease in separate passages. The original Hebrew review body remains restricted. [Archive scope](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-PMID25796668-Gamus-Cranberry-and-Depression-Contexts.txt)
- **Brewed green tea and fatigue, 0948.** The Jiang fly study tests isolated EGCG and records locomotor outcomes. Traditional tea use for fatigue appears as background. Crossref confirms the authors' SSRN record, with both bodies and outcome figures still open at inspection. The current human scorecard retains one uncontrolled brewed-tea Parkinson cohort and contrary indirect evidence. A controlled or otherwise attributable human brewed-tea fatigue estimate remains open.
