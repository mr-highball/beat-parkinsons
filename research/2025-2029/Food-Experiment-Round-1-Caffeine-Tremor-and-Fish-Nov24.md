## Coffee withdrawal and fatigue

The 1986 original abstract describes nine heavy coffee drinkers across residential experiments. The later synthesis by the same investigator reproduces the withdrawal result for seven of them. Profile of Mood States fatigue increased after substitution with decaf, alongside lower vigor. This establishes a withdrawal context for acute fatigue comparisons made after abstention. Both conditions used coffee. The original methods remain access-limited, and this population and comparator differ from culinary brewed coffee versus control for Parkinson fatigue. Claim 0121 retains its minimal easing lean across 15 source families. ## Fatty fish and depression

The SU.VI.MAX dietary analysis included trout in its broader fatty-fish category. The recurrent prescription association was lower among nonsmokers and higher among smokers, with an imprecise smoker estimate. Prescriptions served as an endpoint proxy. A companion serum analysis from the same parent cohort found no consistent association for baseline long-chain omega-3 measures. These are different exposures and analyses from one cohort. ## Kampavata and the cowhage citation

A 2011 clinical tremor review points to a 1991 journal discussion of Ayurvedic source texts. The discussion compares the terminology for shaking, stiffness and depression with Parkinson features and describes combined remedies, including medicated edible fats and separate botanical preparations. Its favorable cowhage report comes from a distinct 1978 article. Original pages for that clinical citation remain open. Historical term and diagnosis fit is uncertain and the original cohort is outside the frozen ingredient vocabulary.
