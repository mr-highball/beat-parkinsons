## Evidence still needed

- **Whole dried date and a separate Parkinson apathy outcome.** A measured, attributable food comparison would record the amount and preparation of dried date fruit, a clinical Parkinson cohort, a comparator, medication state and a separate clinician-, informant- or participant-rated apathy measure. The 1999 Aarsland original documents caregiver-rated apathy separately from depression. The ICICLE-PD original provides longitudinal neuropsychiatric context. These sources supply measurement context without a date fruit comparison. [1999 original](../1995-1999/Source-Text/1999-PMID10486397-Original-Access-Gap.txt) · [ICICLE-PD original](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32024222-Dried-Date-Apathy-Scope.txt)
- **The healthy whole-date crossover and Parkinson fiber bar.** The exact NCT02288611 journal paper describes subjective mood diaries but supplies no report of an apathy result. The completed older-adult NCT05358210 study uses dried dates as the comparator and specifies cognition rather than apathy. The three checked registries carried no posted results. The Parkinson fiber-bar records use a mixture containing date paste and list participant-reported MDS-UPDRS Part IB, which omits the clinician-rated apathy item. Seek a separately disclosed apathy measure and a food contrast that isolates the dates. [Whole dried Ajwa trial](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-Eid-Whole-Ajwa-Mood-Diary.txt) · [Fiber-bar ingredients and outcomes](Food-Experiment-Date-Pain-Followup.md#date-paste-in-a-parkinson-fiber-bar-and-its-pilot)
- **Clinical symptom and trial lineage.** The June–August 2024 Ajwa adjunct study reported an improvement in the total PANSS negative symptom subscale. It did not report item N4 for passive or apathetic social withdrawal separately. The 44-person quality-of-life comparison reports an earlier study period, May 2023 through March 2024. A further 44-person cognition thesis has matching arm sizes and exposure to the quality-of-life report. Its cohort and result tables remain unverified at full thesis depth. [PANSS original](Source-Text/2025-PMID41070421-Syamsuddin-Ajwa-PANSS.txt) · [Quality-of-life source and thesis](Source-Text/2025-PMID40170205-Rismayanti-Ajwa-Quality-of-Life.txt) · [Cognition thesis](Source-Text/2025-Djoeddawi-Ajwa-Cognition-GDNF-Thesis.txt)
- **Alertness, vigor and animal behavior.** Two acute date-food comparisons report alertness changes in healthy adults. An uncontrolled whole-Ajwa stress study has a small, imprecise decline in the vigor chart. Dried-fruit extracts reduced exploration while prolonging sleep in mice. A separate stress model shows greater sucrose preference after pulp extract. The measures distinguish acute alertness, vigor, sedation, movement and reward preference. Their relationship to a date-attributable change in Parkinson apathy remains open. [Date drink trial](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-Al-Tuwairki-Date-Drink-and-Mood.txt) · [Whole Ajwa stress study](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-Abdullah-Ajwa-and-Stress.txt) · [Freeze-dried unripe date flesh](Source-Text/PMC13440079.txt#L353) · [Exploration and sleep](../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC5040737.txt) · [Sucrose preference](Source-Text/2025-Asdaq-Ajwa-Pulp-and-Sucrose-Preference.txt)
- **Ayurvedic terminology in source retrieval.** The original menopausal case lists dates among foods to avoid under a heading printed as “Apathy to be avoided.” Its neighboring Pathya heading and dietary list indicate the Apathya dietary meaning. No clinical Parkinson apathy observation is attached to the advice. [Dated cultural note](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2024-Patel-Makim-Apathya-Diet-Terminology.txt)
