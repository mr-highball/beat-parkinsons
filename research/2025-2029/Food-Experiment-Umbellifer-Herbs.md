# November 2026 searches for dill weed, chervil, lovage leaf and cilantro

## Plant identity and preparation

- Dill weed refers to culinary foliage of *Anethum graveolens*. Dill pickle odor tests exposure to a prepared smell and dill seed trials use a different part of the plant. [Kew plant record](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/837530-1) · [Local Parkinson olfaction reports](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#dill-pickle-odor-as-an-assessment-stimulus).
- Culinary garden chervil is *Anthriscus cerefolium* (synonym *Scandix cerefolium*). Searches for wild chervil (*Anthriscus sylvestris*) retrieve material for a different species. [Kew plant record](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/837913-1).
- Culinary lovage is *Levisticum officinale*. Some relevant-looking Parkinson records use Sichuan lovage rhizome, also called chuanxiong or 川芎, in formulas with additional herbs. The culinary claim specifies leaves. The cystitis trial tested root combined with centaury and rosemary. [Kew culinary plant record](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/844187-1) · [Original Parkinson animal formula study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4281412/) · [Local cystitis trial annotation](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Umbellifer-Herbs.md#lovage-root-mixture-in-acute-cystitis).
- Cilantro refers to leaves of *Coriandrum sativum*. English-language articles may use coriander for the whole plant, leaves, or fruits often called seeds. The German Parkinson menu supplies no plant part, the Canadian challenge specifies fresh leaf, and the mixed-meal case reports fresh coriander in skin testing. [Kew plant record](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/840760-1) · [Parkinson menu and fresh leaf challenge](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Umbellifer-Herbs.md) · [Mixed-meal case](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Umbellifer-Herbs.md#coriander-with-exercise-associated-swallowing-symptoms).
