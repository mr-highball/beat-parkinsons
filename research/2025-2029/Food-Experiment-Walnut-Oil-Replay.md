# Whole-walnut sleep trial and its supplement

Zerón-Rugerio et al. published August 12, 2025 an open-label crossover in 76 young adults, assigning 40 g whole walnuts daily at dinner versus no nuts for eight weeks each, with a two-week washout. NCT04799821 is its registration. The outcome instruments included wrist-based sleep continuity, global sleep quality and Epworth Sleepiness Scale; the supplementary tables list sleep and circadian measures, not dream enactment. The report and its supplement do not identify a dream-enactment frequency outcome. Whole nuts, a non-Parkinson population and sleep continuity measures cannot establish effects of consumed cold-pressed walnut oil on dream enactment in Parkinson's.
