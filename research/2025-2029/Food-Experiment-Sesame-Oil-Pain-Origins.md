# Sesame oil and measured pain

## 2025 oral sesame oil and episiotomy pain

Mehrabi Bardar and colleagues first published on 8 December 2025. Eighty-eight primiparous women were studied with oral sesame oil or placebo capsules every six hours postpartum. The formulation section states 1.1 g of sesame oil per capsule. Another Methods paragraph uses 1.1 mg. The original gives no roasting or pressing method. Pain intensity was lower with sesame after doses three and four (2.69 vs 3.46, p=.038, and 2.16 vs 2.86, p=.029), with no clear differences after the first two doses and no significant group-by-time interaction (p=.148). The report identifies IRCT20210124050128N2. The trial does not estimate the effects of toasted sesame oil or Parkinson pain. The publisher's open full text identifies CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
