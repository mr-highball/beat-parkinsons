# Dried basil and daily OFF time

## The Gayakwad basil extract experiment and its displayed groups

Published 31 March 2026, International Journal of Ayurvedic Medicine 17(1):123–129, [publisher PDF](https://www.ijam.co.in/index.php/ijam/article/download/6636/1615/13600), DOI 10.47552/ijam.v17i1.6636. Already described in [the earlier catalog note](Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#sweet-basil-extract-in-a-rotenone-model); these are additional methods and data for the same publication.

Authors identify *Ocimum basilicum* aerial material, dry 400 g, macerate in 5 L 70% ethanol, concentrate and freeze-dry. Methods describe extract and essential oil mixed with saline and 0.5% Tween 80 and administered intraperitoneally; the relative amount and role of oil are unclear. Rotenone 1.5 mg/kg/day was injected for 28 days. The design section states 30 male Wistar rats in five groups of six: saline, rotenone, rotenone plus oral levodopa 100 mg/kg and carbidopa 25 mg/kg, and rotenone plus extract 200 or 400 mg/kg. Extract starts day 15 for 14 days. Elsewhere the paper refers to male and female rats. The abstract describes combination groups and claims enhanced levodopa/carbidopa effects, but Tables 1–2 provide only these five groups and no extract-plus-levodopa combination arm; the claimed combination cannot be verified from the displayed group data.

Table 1 day 28 rotarod retention (mean ± SEM, seconds): rotenone 57.34 ± 1.892; 200 mg/kg 124.32 ± 1.352; 400 mg/kg 144.59 ± 2.548; standard 148.76 ± 2.216. Table 2 day 28 stair latency: rotenone 35.80 ± 0.39; 200 mg/kg 34.30 ± 0.395; 400 mg/kg 29.32 ± 0.410; standard 23.82 ± 0.755. Table legends label low-dose comparisons *p<0.05, but narrative calls them non-significant while also writing p<0.05. Abstract mentions a pole test, while the accessible group tables supply rotarod and staircase outcomes. Do not infer a quantified synergy or certainty from the contradictory report.

For claim 0266, injected concentrated extract and a toxin-induced rat motor model do not match dietary dried leaves, human PD, or daily OFF duration; no direction of the frozen premise is established. The lack of a combination arm also precludes using the abstract as evidence about human levodopa wearing-off.
