# Dried date and Parkinson pain original-source scope, November 22, 2026

Round 1 claim 0502 asks whether eating dried date reduces pain severity in Parkinson disease. Its scorecard remains open at 50. The best whole dried-date crossover compared abdominal pain outside Parkinson disease and reported very similar diary means. Date seed powder was associated with lower exercise soreness at 24 hours in an active-adult trial. A later biomarker publication uses the same cohort. Date-syrup labor trials, extracts, and the seed powder differ from ordinary dried date fruit. The primary Parkinson bar papers or registries do not isolate a date-fruit and pain estimate.
