# Ginger and olfactory outcomes, 2025–2029

## Dried ginger within a six-herb smell training program

Zhang and colleagues reported a prospective nonrandomized comparison of six inhaled herbs, including dried ginger, versus nasal mometasone. The 73 completers had mean identification changes of 11.31 (SD 6.30) versus 6.22 (SD 4.99), p<.001. Attrition differed substantially. Subjective and quality-of-life outcomes did not survive correction, and the mixed etiologies were not reported as a Parkinson-specific subgroup. The study tested a combined odor regimen without an ingredient-level or oral effect.
