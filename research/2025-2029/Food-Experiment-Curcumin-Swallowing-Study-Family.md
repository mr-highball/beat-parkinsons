# Nanocurcumin and swallowing study reports

## Nanocurcumin and instrumental swallowing after chemoradiotherapy

Arshida and colleagues report one head and neck chemoradiotherapy trial. The narrative screens 88, allocates 70 (35 per arm) and analyzes 64 completers (32 per arm). The comparison uses nanocurcumin and placebo. The article prints CTRI submission reference REF/2023/02/063210. This report establishes the shared cohort and precedes the published swallowing table. [May 28, 2025 acute-toxicity report](https://doi.org/10.4103/jrcr.jrcr_11_25) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2025-Arshida-May-Nanocurcumin-Toxicities-Factual-Extract.txt)

Table 5 reports mean baseline Penetration-Aspiration Scale (PAS) 2.91 in each arm. Post-treatment means were 2.91 with nanocurcumin and 3.72 with placebo, difference −0.81 (95% CI −1.39 to −0.22, p=.007). The paper prints the same submission reference and allocation as the May report. The group means indicate less worsening during treatment in the nanocurcumin arm. Participants were receiving chemoradiotherapy, with no grated turmeric or Parkinson comparison. [November 28, 2025 original Table 5](https://doi.org/10.4103/AMJM.AMJM_34_25) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2025-Arshida-November-Nanocurcumin-Swallowing-Factual-Extract.txt)

The conference abstract reports median PAS change 0 (IQR −1 to 1) with nanocurcumin and +1 (IQR 0 to 1) with placebo, between-arm p=.006. Authors, intervention, two sets of 32, baseline PAS, treatment response and within-arm p-values link it to the 2025 study family. The abstract calls 64 randomized, while the two journal reports distinguish 70 allocated and 64 analyzed. Use one study for direction.
