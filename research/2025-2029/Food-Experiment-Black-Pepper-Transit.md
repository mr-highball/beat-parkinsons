# A mixed extract gastrointestinal symptom pilot

## Piperine blend and reported constipation in an uncontrolled pilot

Suman and colleagues report ten adults with gastrointestinal complaints given a tablet combining piperine, curcumin and virgin coconut oil plus counseling. The original publication date is February 1, 2026, despite a 2025 running header. Table 1 groups constipation and bowel function. It shows six initial improvements in the older subgroup even though three older participants were reported at enrollment. There was no control arm, product-specific comparison, validated standalone constipation scale or Parkinson cohort.
