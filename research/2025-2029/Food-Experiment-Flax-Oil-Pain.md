# Flaxseed oil and pain, 2025–2029

## Flaxseed oil with vitamin D and the SF-36 pain domain

In older adults with type 2 diabetes, the combined treatment gave a median bodily-pain domain score of 32.5 at baseline and 25 after treatment, versus 78.75 and 55 with matched placebos. The scale describes higher values as better. The initial imbalance is large, and Table 7 has ambiguous statistical labels and gives no clear adjusted between-arm change. The abstract describes improved pain. These limitations and concurrent vitamin D leave both the direction and the separate oil effect unresolved.
