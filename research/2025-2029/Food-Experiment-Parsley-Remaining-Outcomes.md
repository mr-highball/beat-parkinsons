# Parsley preparation and Parkinson symptom evidence

## Isolated apigenin in Parkinson model motor and molecular tests

The investigators selected apigenin through compound screening and studied molecular and cell responses, alongside motor testing in a mouse Parkinson model. The motor tests named in the abstract are rotarod, pole, open field, footprints and gait. No dried parsley matrix, levodopa-induced abnormal involuntary movement scale or human dyskinesia outcome is reported in the accessible source. [Phytomedicine primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40215824/).

## Parsley-containing recipe in a hospital texture programme

A hospital programme served texture-modified diets to older adults with dysphagia. Appendix A lists an identical 1.9% combined garlic-and-parsley ingredient in both versions of the fideuà recipe. The article reports aggregate meal acceptance and food viscosity. Meal services increased from 9,766 to 14,806 across the programme years. There is no identified dried versus fresh parsley, individual consumption of that dish, ingredient contrast or change in dysphagia severity. The published formulation matches the earlier laboratory puree article. [Nuñez-Lara full article and Appendix A](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12943711/).
