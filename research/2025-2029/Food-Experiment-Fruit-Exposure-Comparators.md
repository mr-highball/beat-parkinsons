# Cherry and watermelon preparation comparisons

## Watermelon and cherry cues in Parkinson-plus smell testing

Sarkar and Tandon compared smell identification in multiple system atrophy, progressive supranuclear palsy and healthy controls. Watermelon and cherry appear as diagnostic odor labels. The participants were tested on odor recognition, and no food intake effect was estimated.
