# Herb and spice searches for ingredients with zero initial records

Reviewed on 2026-11-12. This follow-up started with all 13 herbs and spices with no records in the experiment’s first ingredient export. It searched exposures in either direction before mapping the findings to frozen symptoms. It also checked local full text for dietary packages and translated Kampo formulation names.

Each search combined `TITLE_ABS:parkinson* OR TITLE_ABS:"shaking palsy" OR TITLE_ABS:"paralysis agitans"` with the ingredient terms below. ## Claim review

The result titled “Selective hyposmia and nigrostriatal dopaminergic denervation in Parkinson’s disease” and its follow-up use a dill pickle odor, a test stimulus relevant to [0600](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0600.txt). The cilantro leaf and fennel studies found in the expanded searches measured animal or laboratory outcomes. The lovage search returned Sichuan lovage rhizome in a rat mixture and a patent. Sichuan lovage rhizome represents a different plant material from culinary *Levisticum officinale* leaf. The expanded shiso search also found animal and laboratory work using perilla leaf constituents or seed oil. [Dill assessment](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#dill-pickle-odor-as-an-assessment-stimulus) · [Cilantro animal study](Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#oral-coriander-leaf-preparation-in-mouse-models) · [Fennel laboratory and animal studies](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#fennel-oil-in-laboratory-and-animal-models) · [Lovage name collision, PMID 25558224](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25558224/)

The accessible sources in this recheck yielded no human study matching a frozen preparation and symptom for the 13 ingredients. This includes allspice, tarragon, and summer savory, whose searches were dominated by botanical listings, constituent work, and laboratory assays. Unindexed local publications and records without accessible methods remain outside the verified coverage.
