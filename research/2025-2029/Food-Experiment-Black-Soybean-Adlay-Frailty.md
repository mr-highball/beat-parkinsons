# Black Soybean Adlay Frailty

## Registered fermented black soybean and adlay frailty comparison

The National Taiwan University Hospital record has a brief title referring to algae, but its structured intervention arms specify fermented and nonfermented black soybean plus dehulled adlay for 12 weeks. Registered outcomes include frailty, five-meter walking speed, timed up and go, chair stands and grip strength. The latest record marks 141 enrolled and the trial completed, with no posted results on November 16, 2026. A 2025 conference report describes a matching preparation and setting with 116 randomized, but does not name the accession, so study linkage and participant counts remain open. ## Fermented black soybean and adlay in an older-adult frailty abstract

Wen and colleagues report 116 older adults randomized to a 12-week supplement containing fermented black soybean and adlay or a matching comparison. Both groups improved on functional tasks including a five-meter walk, grip and timed up and go. Only the active group is described as showing clear within-group frailty and nutrition score changes. The abstract provides no arm-level effect estimates for walking or direct between-group frailty contrast. Black soybean is a different species from common black bean. The result concerns a combination and an older-adult cohort, with no Parkinson postural outcome. The similar registry NCT06882941 reports a different enrollment count.
