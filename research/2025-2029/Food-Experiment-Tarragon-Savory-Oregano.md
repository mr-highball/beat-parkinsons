# Tarragon and knee osteoarthritis

## Topical tarragon oil extract and osteoarthritis pain

Abedpour and colleagues randomized 90 adults with knee osteoarthritis to a topical 3% tarragon oil extract in sweet almond oil, topical sweet almond oil alone, or routine care alone. Immediately after the three-week treatment period, mean WOMAC pain scores were 10.13, 13.73 and 13.43, respectively (p = .001 overall). At one and three weeks after the intervention, between-group p values were .376 and .858. The scent prevented participant blinding. The 2021 registration and article disagree on enrollment chronology, randomization, treatment schedule and participant blinding. The article identifies an underlying Birjand University master’s thesis whose separate public record remains unresolved. The topical intervention and osteoarthritis pain provide indirect human evidence for 0285. The trial measured no effect of eating fresh tarragon or Parkinson’s pain. ## Topical tarragon gel for dental injection pain

IRCT20241115063712N1 registered retrospectively on April 14, 2025. The Mazandaran trial targets 100 adults across five groups receiving an oral mucosal gel before an inferior alveolar nerve block. The arms distinguish tarragon extract alone, jujube extract alone, the combined extracts, lidocaine and a vehicle control. Its primary outcome is visual analogue pain at three minutes after injection. The registry reports completed recruitment. Searches by registration, ethics code, title and investigator yielded no verified result. The planned endpoint and topical intervention differ from fresh tarragon ingestion and Parkinson pain in 0285. ## Oregano essential oil and rodent coordination in a toxicology thesis

Oliveira studied oral Origanum vulgare essential oil in healthy animals. The UFPB record identifies an April 2011 thesis and 2025 repository availability. The 28-day rat comparison gives no clear rotating-rod retention change across oil groups. The original Graph 6 prints three animals. The open-field section describes more crossing and rearing in highest-concentration males. The study measured no stride length, Parkinson model or fresh-leaf exposure.
