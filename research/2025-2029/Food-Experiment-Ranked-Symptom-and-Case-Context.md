# Clinical symptom measures and case boundaries

## Grid writing and upper limb freezing in a Parkinson case

October 8, 2026. Gál and colleagues describe handwriting freezing in one person with diagnosed Parkinson disease. Grid paper immediately assisted writing and a home program combined writing and drawing practice. The report gives clinical observations of legibility and freezing, without a structured pre/post score. Rigidity is described at diagnosis and foot dystonia has a separate treatment history. Neither serves as a separate outcome of the writing intervention. The case adds upper-limb freezing and symptom context without a sage comparison.
