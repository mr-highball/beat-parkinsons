# Parkinson dry mouth and swallowing severity survey

## Parkinson dry mouth and swallowing severity survey

Swallowing complaints and measured questionnaire dryness scores were associated in an independent Parkinson survey. The study did not assign oil and cannot estimate a response to oral lubrication.
