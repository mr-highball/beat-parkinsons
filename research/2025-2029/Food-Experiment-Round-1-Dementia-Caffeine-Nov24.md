## Exposure and outcome findings

The [pallidal stimulation series](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2002-PMID11796777-Pallidal-Stimulation-Cervical-Dystonia-Access.txt) measures pain in complex cervical dystonia after stimulation. It lacks a dried-date intervention. The [paternal exposure cohort](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15471733-Agent-Orange-and-Birth-Outcomes.txt) uses Orange in the name of a herbicide and studies birth outcomes. The [historical Parkinson therapy review](../1950-1954/Source-Text/1950-PMID14772655-Historical-Parkinson-Therapy.txt) describes treatment and fatigue without an isolated culinary sage or dried-date comparison. A separate JAMA summary was excluded as a DOI alias for the original Canadian oxyphenbutazone paper.

The [asymmetric postural tremor series](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID15608009-Postural-Tremor-Diagnostic-Followup.txt) follows selected patients and considers the evolution of their diagnosis. Fig references and postural tremor are distinct from dietary fig and a rest-tremor intervention comparison. The [finger oscillation study](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2001-PMID11254769-Levodopa-Piper-Oscillations-Access.txt) still has abstract-only access. Its resisted-contraction measure also differs from rest tremor.

## Coffee fatigue and withdrawal

The [caffeine pharmacology review](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15095008-Caffeine-Stimulation-and-Exercise-Fatigue.txt) discusses isolated caffeine, intermediate signaling and exercise fatigue. The [1997 counterbalanced study](../1995-1999/Source-Text/1997-PMID9264092-Lane-Caffeine-Abstinence-and-Fatigue.txt) offers a separate withdrawal comparison. Its author abstract reports more subjective fatigue after brief abstinence from caffeinated beverages among habitual coffee drinkers. The [2025 randomized comparison](Source-Text/2025-PMID39897922-Lesar-Coffee-and-Arousal.txt) used the same decaffeinated coffee base in both arms, with caffeine powder added to one. It measured performance and physiological outcomes. A fatigue scale was absent.

The [existing conversation](Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2025-Reddit-Coffee-Tea-Energy-and-Tremor.txt) includes perceived energy from coffee and a separate reply about using unspecified caffeine for fatigue with a diminishing effect. Its records remain one conversation family with unresolved comparator, preparation and diagnosis. The updated 12-family scorecard retains the coffee claim's minimal easing lean. A controlled clinical comparison of brewed coffee on Parkinson fatigue remains open.
