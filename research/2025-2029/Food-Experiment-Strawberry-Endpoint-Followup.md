# Strawberry attention trial, 2025

## Freeze-dried strawberry crossover and flanker attention

Delaney et al. published online March 18, 2025, a randomized crossover in 35 cognitively healthy older adults comparing 26 g/day freeze-dried strawberry powder with control for eight weeks per phase. For the Flanker Inhibitory Control and Attention Test, the time-by-phase interaction was p = .824: mean scores were 7.6 to 7.7 in control and 7.7 to 7.8 in the strawberry phase. Processing speed improved within the strawberry phase, but its time-by-phase interaction was p = .125, so the within-phase result is not a demonstrated differential effect. This is not a trial of fresh raw strawberries or Parkinson-related attention impairment. The 2023 Tsang conference abstract (DOI 10.1016/j.cdnut.2023.100183) and registry NCT04786665 concern the same trial family and are not independent replications.
