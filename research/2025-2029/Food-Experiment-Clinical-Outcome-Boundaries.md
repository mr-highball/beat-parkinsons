# Clinical and experimental outcome boundaries

## Rosemary and daylily mixture in two mouse behavior assays

August 20, 2026. Forty mice were assigned to four groups. A commercial combination of rosemary-leaf and daylily-flower extracts was compared with vehicle in challenged mice. Total immobility in forced swimming was lower with the mixture (p=.0188). The latency endpoint and both tail suspension endpoints had no statistically significant group differences. The report has no rosemary-only arm, fresh herb exposure or clinical Parkinson depression measurement.
