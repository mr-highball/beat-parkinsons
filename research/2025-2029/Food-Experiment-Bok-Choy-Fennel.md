# Bok choy and fennel seed evidence

## Crushed fennel seed infusion and reported fatigue and focus

Kwiatkowska and colleagues reported a 2021 study in a nominal 2024 issue, after the manuscript was accepted on February 20, 2025. Ten women with menopausal symptoms consumed a strained infusion of crushed fennel seeds for two weeks. Ten unsupplemented participants served as laboratory controls, but only infusion recipients completed the symptom questionnaire. Question 6 combined perceived memory, concentration and ease of focusing and rose from 0 to 12 points (p = .014). Question 15 rose from 0 to 8 points and grouped unspecified other effects. The narrative mentions fatigue and reduced nervousness or calmness without reporting a separate fatigue direction or count. No attention performance or Parkinson outcome was measured. ## Fennel oil and immobility in a zebrafish novel tank test

Brinza and colleagues published July 4, 2025. Zebrafish received fennel essential oil by aquatic exposure before a scopolamine challenge. The authors report no clear oil-associated change in freezing duration, swimming velocity or distance in the novel tank test. They did report some changes in exploration and memory tasks. Freezing in this experiment was immobility during swimming, and the disease challenge was a model of cognitive impairment. The experiment measured neither clinical freezing of gait nor an effect of eating toasted seeds. ## Fennel seed essential oil massage and reported sleep quality

Félix and colleagues published May 13, 2026. Forty adults were randomized to massage with fennel seed essential oil diluted in sweet almond oil or massage with sweet almond oil alone. The mean Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index showed no clear between-group difference. The authors describe the questionnaire’s daytime dysfunction component but report global scores and overall classifications, without publishing the daytime component separately. The oil was used during massage, and the study did not examine oral toasted seed or Parkinson’s.
