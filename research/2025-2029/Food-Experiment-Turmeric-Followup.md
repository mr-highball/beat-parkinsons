# Turmeric preparation and symptom outcomes

## Turmeric extract and eye-tracking attention

The July 2026 trial randomized 40 adults with subtle cognitive weaknesses and no diagnosed dementia to capsules containing a mixture of hot-water and supercritical carbon dioxide turmeric extracts or placebo for 24 weeks. The primary Cognitrax composite memory outcome and its computerized attention measures were inconclusive. Table 5 reports an exploratory between-group difference at 24 weeks on eye-tracking attention (93.9 ± 4.3 vs 82.4 ± 18.6, p < .05). Baseline eye-tracking attention values were 85.5 and 76.2, and the authors tested 35 cognitive outcomes. The product includes turmeronols and bisacurone. It is a formulated extract study outside Parkinson’s. [Primary article, Tables 3–5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13467768/) · [Local CC BY article text](Source-Text/PMC13467768.txt) · [2024 registration](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Turmeric-Followup.md#plant-extract-cognition-trial-registration)

## Turmeric extract capsules and hemodialysis olfaction

Nasution and colleagues randomized 48 participants undergoing hemodialysis to two nominal curcumin capsule doses or placebo. After 12 weeks, Table 3 reported a favorable composite Sniffin’ Sticks change with both doses relative to placebo (p = .010 and p = .002). The component discrimination test improved, while the between-group changes in threshold and identification were inconclusive. The capsule standardization text is unclear. The measured olfactory results apply to this renal population and extract. [Primary article, Tables 2–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13474629/)

## Curcumin and time to fall from a rotarod

The 2026 6-OHDA mouse study found longer latency to fall from a rotating rod after intravenous curcumin, with p = .0018, .0004, .0013 and .0033 against lesioned controls at the four weekly measurements. Curcumin alone had inconclusive open-field locomotor changes. The comparison concerns coordination during a forced test with isolated curcumin. It provides no count of real-world falls or evidence about ground turmeric. [Primary article, Figure 3B](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12926497/) · [Local CC BY article text](Source-Text/PMC12926497.txt)

The 2012 and 2019 dietary curcumin studies report opposing rotating-rod task effects in distinct Huntington mouse models. The 2026 Parkinson animal synthesis finds a pooled longer time with high heterogeneity. The corresponding Parkinson clinical trail still lacks an attributable fall-frequency result.
