# Tarragon motor and facial outcome follow-up

## Nonfood arousal cues in a freezing of gait case series

This paper first published December 22, 2025. One participant with Parkinson disease described transient mobility improvement following a startling auditory cue during difficulty rising or OFF-state freezing. The other participant's diagnosis had changed to parkinsonian multiple system atrophy, and his case concerned a separate nonfood stimulus. Both observations were uncontrolled. The authors tested neither an ingested herb nor an odor cue, so the report records a clinical comparator for the outcome rather than a tarragon effect.
