# Chinese Clinical Trial Registry follow-up, 2025–2029

## Congrong Shujing granules and depression

Fan and colleagues randomized 70 people with Parkinson’s and depression and a specified traditional-medicine diagnosis. The full report describes masked granules and matched placebo, both alongside conventional treatment. Four participants per group withdrew. Table 2 gives 12-week mean scores of 2.28 versus 4.81 for UPDRS Part I, 13.58 versus 18.23 for Part III, and 10.42 versus 15.12 for HAMD-24. All three post-treatment comparisons favored the granules at reported p < 0.05. The adverse-event comparison was statistically inconclusive. This December 28, 2025 report concerns a five-component medicinal formula at a different hospital, with ethics approval `2020-KY-007`. No exact ChiCTR registration link was established. ## Ketogenic diet and pre-post symptom scores

Luo and colleagues reported the ChiCTR2100045394 study on January 23, 2026. Twenty-seven people with Parkinson’s began a 12-week ketogenic diet and 16 completed it. Eleven withdrew, mostly because of difficulty adapting to the regimen. Twenty-seven healthy relatives maintained their usual diet and supplied microbiota samples. The 16 completers’ motor examination scores, constipation scores, daytime sleepiness, and anxiety scores improved in paired comparisons. Table 2 reports p < 0.001 for MDS-UPDRS Part III and HAMA anxiety and p = 0.002 for PAC-SYM constipation and Epworth sleepiness. The Parkinson’s sleep-scale and hyposmia changes were inconclusive (p = 0.076 and 0.182). The clinical comparison is within completers, and Table 2 reports p-values without effect estimates. The whole-diet intervention gives no estimate for an individual food.
