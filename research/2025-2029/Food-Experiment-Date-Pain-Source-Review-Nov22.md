# Round 1 dried-date and Parkinson pain evidence gaps

November 22, 2026. Claim 0502 asks whether eating dried dates reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson disease. The catalog now contains 42 newly verified originals in their respective publication periods. Forty-one exclude a measured date and pain comparison. The Daily Habit Scale is related context. It scores fast-food habits and painkiller consumption separately, and it reports neither dried-date intake nor a pain severity result. Several other original studies measured Parkinson pain, including on the King's Parkinson's Disease Pain Scale.

## Evidence that is still missing

- **Exact clinical comparison.** The open question requires an independently attributable culinary whole dried-date exposure and a separately reported pain severity outcome in a diagnosed Parkinson cohort. Record the cultivar or drying details when reported, the control diet or comparison, medication state, pain domain, timing and between-arm estimate or paired uncertainty. Clinical trials or cohort tables that isolate both variables are the next useful routes.
- **Parkinson fiber-bar composition and pain endpoint.** The existing Parkinson fiber-bar records provide mixed-ingredient exposure. Check the registration, intervention composition, pain-related outcome tables and any later results for an identifiable date-paste contrast. [Parkinson date-paste and fiber-bar evidence](Food-Experiment-Date-Pain-Followup.md#date-paste-in-a-parkinson-fiber-bar-and-its-pilot)
- **Whole culinary fruit outside Parkinson disease.** The small healthy-adult crossover reported very similar abdominal pain diary values. The pediatric randomized preprint reports comparable improvement counts across the three arms, with registry and manuscript design descriptions differing. A paired estimate or complete trial report would clarify those outcome-specific comparisons. [Healthy crossover](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-Eid-Whole-Ajwa-Mood-Diary.txt) · [Pediatric preprint summary](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2023-2511698-Metwally-Ajwa-Abdominal-Pain.txt)
- **Date syrup and acute labor pain.** Two trials report lower pain at some time points. Holding saffron constant in a third study left the comparison during labor inconclusive, with a postpartum difference. Sweetness, calorie exposure, outcome timing, masking and obstetric population limit transfer to the frozen claim. Keep comparisons within their original cohort. [2018 syrup](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-69207-Taavoni-Date-Syrup-and-Labor-Pain.txt) · [2018 adjunct sweetener](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-61289-Mohammadierad-Saffron-Date-Sugar-and-Pain.txt) · [2022 syrup](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-20328-Sohrabi-Date-Syrup-and-Labor-Pain.txt)
- **Migraine distillate and related pain context.** The migraine registry still needs verified results and clarification of its preparation and outcome fields. The Parkinson VHIP protocol gives aggregate Mediterranean diet measures. Its registry, later report and diet or pain instruments can clarify those fields if results become available. [Migraine registry](Source-Text/Trials/IRCT20260702070048N1.txt) · [VHIP and prior clinical review](Food-Experiment-Date-Pain-and-Priority-Three-Originals-Nov22.md)
