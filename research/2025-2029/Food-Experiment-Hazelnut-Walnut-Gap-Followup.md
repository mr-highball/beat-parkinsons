# Hazelnut and walnut oil outcome follow-up

## Whole walnuts and Epworth daytime sleepiness

Zerón-Rugerio and colleagues first published August 12, 2025. In open-label randomized crossover NCT04799821, 76 young adults ate 40 g whole walnuts at dinner daily or no nuts for eight weeks per period, with two weeks of washout. The paper reports a mixed-model Epworth Sleepiness Scale change of −1.37 points (95% CI −2.21 to −0.52, p = .002) after eight weeks of walnut intake relative to baseline. The control period showed no clear change from baseline in the authors’ paired analysis. The trial used whole walnuts, measured melatonin and tryptophan in the nuts, and enrolled healthy young volunteers. The same registration appears in the earlier 2024 proceedings, which describe 80 adults. The full report explains the four exclusions from its final analysis and adds effect size and confidence intervals. It provides related evidence for the symptom in 0714 and does not establish the isolated cold-pressed walnut oil or Parkinson effect.
