## Whole walnuts and total mood in a young adult crossover

Zerón-Rugerio and colleagues’ 2026 original author abstract reports a randomized open-label crossover with 31 healthy young adults. The eight-week whole-walnut phase compared with the nut-free period showed a total mood disturbance difference of −4.85 (p = .030). The public abstract reports no tension-anxiety subscale comparison. Urinary metabolites change alongside some reported mood outcomes and do not establish an oil-specific or centrally mediated anxiety effect.
