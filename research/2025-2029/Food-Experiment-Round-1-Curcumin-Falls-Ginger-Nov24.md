## Opposite signs in curcumin movement tasks

Dietary isolated curcumin shortened time on a rotating rod in both genotypes in Hickey 2012. The adult wild-type comparison reproduced the adverse task direction. A different Huntington model and curcumin added to a shared bioperine and vehicle background gave better performance in Elifani 2019. Task, model, and preparation matter, and these findings do not estimate fall frequency in Parkinson disease. [Hickey original](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMID22475209-Hickey-Dietary-Curcumin-and-Opposing-Motor-Outcomes.txt) · [Elifani original](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMID31630202-Elifani-Curcumin-and-Rotarod.txt)

The 2026 synthesis of Parkinson animal models reported longer rotarod latency across nine studies, including several dosing comparisons. Pooled standardized mean difference was 2.49 (95% CI 1.65–3.33) with I² of 79%. The review uses preclinical motor tests and does not estimate spontaneous falls or add an independent cohort. [Preclinical synthesis, Section 3.4.1.2](Source-Text/PMC13006684.txt)

## Clinical falls outcome and safety reporting

The Parkinson registry reports an amended, discontinued arm with nine people receiving four supplements, including curcumin. The posted results give aggregate adverse-event totals and no fall-specific outcome. In the clinical evidence trail, the Ghodsi randomized pilot and Donadio observational study used specialized curcumin formulations. Their reported clinical outcomes do not isolate falls. Donadio reports no serious adverse events in its supplemented group. Ghodsi item-level and adverse-event tables still need original access. The ground-turmeric and fall-frequency scorecard stays Unknown. ## Botanical relationship and clinical advice

The botanical review mentions ginger as a relative of turmeric. Grated ginger and anxiety do not form a comparison in that source. A separate public conversational report remains part of the ginger evidence trail. In a Parkinson medication article, a banana portion is one suggested snack for nausea. The author's anxiety and dyskinesia discussions concern medication and time course. Keep the banana recommendation as related context, with its two relevant outcomes both unmeasured.
