# Raw fig and rest-tremor follow-through

This review checked 18 complete originals against 21 frozen claim–source pairs, including 18 for raw fig and rest tremor. Every fig occurrence in those pairs referred to an illustration. The other pairs used orange as a figure color, a salmon term as a citation surname, or a date term in clinical chronology. The scope decisions do not determine the direction of the hypotheses. [Exact scopes, source links and replay](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/fig-rest-tremor-new-originals-nov25/README.md)

The clinical comparison of 130 and 185 Hz deep brain stimulation measured rest tremor with and without provocation, as well as postural and kinetic tremor. It tested stimulation settings. The one-year trial measured arm action tremor and related endpoints. The distinct motor outcomes clarify the search scope without contributing a fig result. [Frequency trial scope](Source-Text/PMC13728422-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [One-year trial scope](Source-Text/PMC13651327-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)

The Bangladesh original, printed page 838, attributes paralysis use to sacred fig fruit. The later common-fig review cites that record as a common fig use. The original laboratory results table has no Ficus-labelled row and the use entry has no measured effect. The 2024 Sudhnoti field study gives a distinct preparation, an edible or customary laxative decoction of common fig fruit. It identifies four Ficus taxa separately and records no rest-tremor assessment. [Bangladesh original](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2000-PMID10830501-Khanom-Ficus-Species-and-Paralysis-Scope.txt) · [Later citation check](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID25017517-Ficus-Review-Paralysis-Attribution.txt) · [Sudhnoti field record](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2024-PMID39333361-Sudhnoti-Fig-Edible-and-Laxative-Use.txt)

The accessible report describes catalepsy, locomotion and rotarod performance for concentrated dried-fruit extract. The recovered material does not give a separate resting-tremor outcome.
