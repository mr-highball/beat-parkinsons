# Rosemary and lemon balm evidence

## Inhaled lemon balm oil and reported dry mouth during dialysis

Pasyar and colleagues published March 13, 2025. Sixty-eight hemodialysis patients were randomized to inhaled lemon balm essential oil or refined sweet almond oil. The paper reports a lower post-treatment prevalence of dry mouth with lemon balm and an inconclusive difference in the total Dialysis Symptom Index (p = .16). The dry-mouth item was a self-report of a separate symptom, and the trial measured neither salivary flow nor drooling. [Original report and tables](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12906-025-04840-y)

## Rosemary infusion and blood pressure

The March 2025 Tunisian pilot enrolled 18 healthy adults and 35 adults with grade 1 hypertension. Both groups drank an infusion prepared from powdered rosemary leaves for 45 days. Blood pressure declined in the hypertensive group in the reported pre–post comparison. With both groups exposed and no placebo group, the change is observational. The study did not test a Parkinson population or use an orthostatic symptom endpoint. [Original article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.phyplu.2025.100783)

## Rosemary extract and resting blood pressure in nursing staff

Pretto and colleagues published March 16, 2026. In 36 completers from a 2022 emergency nursing cohort, mean systolic pressure was 117.03 mm Hg at baseline and 113.28 at follow-up (p = .048). Mean diastolic pressure was 78.31 and 74.36 (p = .016). The dry-extract study was uncontrolled and measured resting pressure. The article identifies the 2023 doctoral thesis as the same dataset. The thesis is filed in the prior period. ## Rosemary and lemon balm oils and mouse rotarod performance

Sá Filho and colleagues published September 30, 2026. They tested hydrodistilled oils from separately dried rosemary and lemon balm leaves in Swiss mice. Figure 4 marks shorter first-fall latency and more falls at the highest level for both oils. At the intermediate level it marks more falls for lemon balm only (p < .05). The Results prose describes an increased latency and the Discussion groups both oils among those without intermediate-level motor impairment. The figure and prose therefore disagree on latency direction and on the interpretation of the intermediate lemon balm result. The study used general coordination in mice without Parkinson modeling and provides no clinical fall, postural instability or freezing estimate. [Publisher Figure 4](https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/27/19/8786#fig4) · [Local CC BY article text](Source-Text/PMC13664909.txt)

## Phospholipid lemon balm dose comparison and overlapping pilot

Rondanelli and colleagues used open-label chronological allocation of 32 adults to two extract doses for three weeks. DASS-21 depression changed by −6.1% at 200 mg and −26.4% at 400 mg. The high-dose within-group comparison had p < .0001. There was no placebo. The authors describe this as an extension of their 2024 pilot with shared ethics identifier 1267/21092023. The 12-person salivary cortisol subgroup also overlaps. Original report: https://foodandnutritionresearch.net/index.php/fnr/article/download/13729/20830/.
