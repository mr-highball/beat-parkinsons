## Cinnamon and ginger anxiety-related behavior

In streptozotocin-diabetic rats, combined water extracts of cinnamon and ginger increased elevated-plus-maze open-arm time from 11.99 to 17.80 seconds compared with untreated diabetic rats, p=0.004, with six rats per arm. Each separate extract comparison had an inconclusive increase. Closed-arm entries also changed in the combined arm and may reflect activity. The exposure was gastric-gavage extract, the model was diabetes and the outcome was rodent behavior. The result cannot be attributed to either frozen culinary ingredient alone or transferred to clinical anxiety severity. [Dated original comparison](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID34568337-Cinnamon-Ginger-and-Diabetic-Rat-Maze.txt)

The cinnamon metabolite sodium benzoate increased dopamine or aspects of open-field movement in mouse experiments. These data supply no passive rigidity or dyskinesia measure. A 2024 erratum replaces two images in Figure 5C and belongs to the same experimental family.
