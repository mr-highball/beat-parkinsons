# Oats and carrot evidence

## 2025 Oats and probiotics in a rotenone rat diet

Al-Okbi and colleagues combined a diet containing 10% oat with a probiotic mixture in a rat Parkinson model. They compared it with untreated model controls and a dark chocolate arm. The report found several microbiota, inflammatory, gene-expression and histological changes, with no standalone oat arm or clinical symptom test. [Existing local primary text, Table 2 and Methods](Source-Text/PMC12227552.txt#L184)

## 2025 Raw and cooked carrot items in a Parkinson case-control analysis

Gigante and colleagues' questionnaire separately recorded raw carrot, cooked carrot and salads containing carrot. The raw and cooked items loaded 0.53 and 0.36 on the salad factor. The case-control comparison for the full salad factor was inconclusive (p = .64). Factor loadings describe the dietary pattern and do not measure a symptom effect. The paper supplies no raw-carrot estimate on hallucinations, dreaming or olfaction, and no specified steamed carrot or roasted carrot estimate. It was first published October 28, 2025. [Primary report, Tables 2–4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13347557/)
