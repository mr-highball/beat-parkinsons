# Oregano, salivary measures and Parkinson outcome context

## Oral oregano oil jelly and measured salivary pH

The 2025 study measured salivary pH before and after seven days of an oral jelly that participants dissolved without chewing. The active jelly contained Origanum vulgare essential oil from Sigma-Aldrich. In 91 adults, mean pH increased from 6.94 to 7.07. It fell from 7.03 to 6.78 in the menstruating subgroup, while it rose from 6.93 to 7.10 among the other participants. The commercial comparison jelly appears in the tests of laboratory antimicrobial activity. The authors describe the human comparison as an observational pre/post series. They collected saliva by passive drooling and state that resting flow and buffering capacity were outside the measurements. This is a related saliva chemistry result with no scale of clinical drooling or fresh-leaf Parkinson comparison. ## Isolated carvacrol and marjoram tea

Injected carvacrol altered experimentally elicited postures in two different rodent models. Lins reports lower reserpine-associated catalepsy and vacuous chewing with persistent impairment in open-field activity. Albaqami reports shorter catalepsy after haloperidol, with group means of 51.50 and 73.08 seconds versus 114.72 in the haloperidol-only group. That experiment replaced nine animals after deaths. These selected compound assays provide no dried oregano or clinical dystonia result. A separate Parkinson pilot of filtered dried marjoram tea reported a within-tea-arm motor examination p value of .069 and gives no daily OFF duration in the accessible material. Origanum majorana is a different species and the exposure is a strained infusion. ## Registered dried oregano and basil leaves in a coronary study

The registry first posted August 14, 2025. Participants in the intervention arm are to eat dried oregano and dried basil leaves, 4 to 5 grams of each per day for two months, in addition to usual medical treatment. The control receives standard treatment. The planned outcomes include circulating inflammatory markers and lipids in people with unstable angina. In the checked snapshot the study was recruiting and had no results posted. The preparation is verified as an assigned mixed-leaf intervention, with no individual intake or Parkinson outcome available.
