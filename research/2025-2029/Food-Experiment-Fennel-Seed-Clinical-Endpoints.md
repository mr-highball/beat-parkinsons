# Fennel seed clinical endpoint follow-up

## Full report on region specific gastric effects of fennel seed tea

Annahazi and colleagues published the full guinea pig stomach organ-bath study on November 10. Fennel seed tea relaxed corpus and fundus muscle strips and increased spontaneous antral contraction amplitude, without detected changes in antral contraction frequency or basal tone. The peak response pattern is consistent with the earlier conference report from the group. The preparations tested regional muscle response. Whole-stomach emptying, levodopa concentrations and clinical OFF time were not measured.
