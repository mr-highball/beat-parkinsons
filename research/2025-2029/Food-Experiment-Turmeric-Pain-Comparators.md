# Turmeric powder and hand osteoarthritis pain

## Placebo-controlled turmeric powder pilot for hand pain

Tuntiyatorn and colleagues first published October 6, 2025. Their double-blind pilot assigned 27 adults with hand osteoarthritis to whole turmeric powder capsules or placebo. The primary one-month pain comparison was inconclusive. At three months, resting pain scores went from 4.48 to 2.30 with powder and 3.44 to 2.62 with placebo. Activity pain changed from 4.92 to 2.50 and 4.18 to 3.51 respectively. The groups started at different values. Table 3 labels a favorable three-month estimate as a within-group versus baseline result. Some Results and Discussion wording describes it as a placebo contrast. A separate between-group Results paragraph reports inconclusive comparisons at each visit. These distinctions limit the directional inference. The trial reports registration TCTR20210929003. The verified original trial registry posting date remains open. The original preparation is whole turmeric powder capsules measuring 3 g daily and 172.5 mg total curcuminoids in the report. Three-month Table 3 p-values of .032 at rest and .017 during activity apply to within-group tests. Table 3 reports differences of 1.37 and 1.74, while subtracting the raw Table 2 means yields 2.18 and 2.42. An arm-specific adjusted change and group-by-time contrast were unavailable in the checked body. The official registry URL opened its application shell without trial details.
