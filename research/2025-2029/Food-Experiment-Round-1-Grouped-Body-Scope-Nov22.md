## Sources affecting rice bran oil claims

The 2026 antipsychotic movement review cites the older Samad rice bran oil reports. The 2015 original confirms 16 rats and reduced chewing stereotypies and coordination impairment in its haloperidol comparison. It provides no directly measured rigidity or dystonia and no identified commercial refining grade. A prior 0426 family row categorized the study as an observation in people. The corrected row identifies a controlled animal experiment and uncertain independence across companion reports. Claim 0476 now includes the 2015 original as context and retains its previously graded minimal easing lean from the separately identified catalepsy comparison. Claim 0426 remains Unknown. The complete abstract is readable on PDF page 340, and the report does not measure falls or fatigue in Parkinson disease. The older EANM collection independently includes caffeine in a myocardial imaging setting.
