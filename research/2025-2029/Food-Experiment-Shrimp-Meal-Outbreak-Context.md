# Grilled shrimp in a documented mixed meal

## Grilled shrimp in a mixed outbreak meal

Li and colleagues published February 7, 2025. Table 2 lists 10 grilled-shrimp consumers across 11 diners, with five illness cases among consumers. All six case descriptions report fatigue and dizziness, with additional symptoms in some diners. The report does not record visual hallucinations or a prespecified hallucination measure. The investigation implicated contaminated vegetable and tofu soup. Table 2 prints a shrimp risk ratio of 0.500, confidence interval 0.269–0.929 and p=1.000. The entries are internally inconsistent, and the small mixed meal offers no reliable isolated shrimp contrast. ## Shrimp within mixed diets in a psychiatric case

Laurent and colleagues first published February 7, 2025. Case 1 describes auditory and visual hallucinations before a ketogenic intervention for schizoaffective disorder. Shrimp is listed in both the usual diet and the later ketogenic plan alongside other foods. At six weeks the patient reported that hallucinations overall had resolved. The narrative separately mentions auditory cessation and supplies no visual frequency series, shrimp preparation, quantity or isolated exposure comparison. Medication, diet and clinical support changed during the case.
