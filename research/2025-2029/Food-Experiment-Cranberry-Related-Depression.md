# Cranberry-containing mixture and measured mood

## Final high-altitude depression comparison

Beckner and colleagues first published the full report on September 10, 2025. Thirteen healthy adults contributed to the three-condition analysis. The intervention contained cranberry extract, blueberry, cocoa, green tea and several fibers. POMS depression was higher under both high-altitude conditions than at low altitude, with no detected difference between the high-altitude mixture and placebo. HADS depression showed no condition, time or interaction effect. The comparison tests the complete mixture in healthy adults.
