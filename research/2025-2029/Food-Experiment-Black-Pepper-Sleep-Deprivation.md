# Pepper extract in sleep-deprived mice

## Black pepper extract and spatial learning after sleep interruption

The 2025 original reports longer target-quadrant time in a water maze after oral black pepper extract in sleep-deprived mice, with no clear crossing-count difference. It uses a concentrated extract and spatial task. The article gives no sustained-attention comparison, cracked culinary pepper or clinical Parkinson outcome.
