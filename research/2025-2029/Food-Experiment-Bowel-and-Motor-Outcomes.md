# Bowel outcomes and selected motor or nonmotor follow-up

## Corn oil and constipation in the STRENGTH subgroup analysis

First published May 27, 2025. Constipation was reported in 14 of 657 Asian and 36 of 5,878 non-Asian adults receiving high cardiovascular risk corn-oil comparator capsules, versus 24 of 697 and 32 of 5,835 in the respective omega-3 arms. Subgroup p values were .14 and .65. Corn-oil dose was 4 g daily, with grade unverified. This post-hoc secondary analysis shares the original STRENGTH trial participants, has no oil-free comparator and gives no Parkinson constipation severity estimate. ## Cranberry juice and motor multitasking registration

First posted March 6, 2026. Recruiting healthy adults for cranberry juice or matched placebo over 70 days. Motor accuracy appears in the summary as part of multitasking, but no standalone graded bradykinesia or specific clinical motor outcome appears in the current outcome list. No results. Cranberry juice and cooked whole cranberry are different exposures. [Official ClinicalTrials.gov record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07453537) · [Local text](Source-Text/2026-NCT07453537-Cranberry-Motor-Scope.txt)

## Rosemary extract trial and constipation surveillance

First posted April 21, 2026. Active, no longer recruiting. Oral methanolic rosemary extract is compared with placebo in type 2 diabetes. Protocol Annex 4 asks a combined adverse-event question about diarrhea or constipation. This neither assigns ground culinary rosemary nor uses a dedicated constipation severity measure. No results are posted. The protocol and current registry should be reconciled for duration and dosing at results review.
