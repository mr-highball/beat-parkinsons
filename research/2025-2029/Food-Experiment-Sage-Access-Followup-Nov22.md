## What the originals measured

The Parkinson dementia study reports separate Apathy Evaluation Scale and depression ratings. Of 40 people with Parkinson disease dementia, 39 met the study’s apathy criterion and 27 met the depression criterion. The dementia pugilistica case records caregiver-rated apathy separately from depression and appetite. Those records provide symptom context with no measured dried sage. [Parkinson apathy and depression](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28871794-Sage-Original-Access-Gap.txt) · [Dementia pugilistica case and symptom table](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMID22017610-Sage-Original-Access-Gap.txt)

The published apomorphine follow-up included joint rigidity as part of its treatment response assessment. The Parkinson metabolic syndrome cohort analyzed aggregate motor UPDRS in the parent creatine study. Its one Sage match names a data organization in a disclosure. [Apomorphine original](../1995-1999/Source-Text/1998-PMID9810943-Sage-Original-Access-Gap.txt) · [Metabolic syndrome and motor UPDRS](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28972194-Sage-Original-Access-Gap.txt)
