# Sichuan pepper follow-up

## Two Nepalese pepper extract gaming trial registrations

NCT07047105 first posted on July 2, 2025 and records completion with 57 healthy competitive gamers enrolled. The crossover compared 150 mg and 300 mg of *Zanthoxylum armatum* extract, 75 mg caffeine, and placebo. It registered attention speed and subjective fatigue. NCT07752329 first posted on August 7, 2026 and planned a 70-person crossover comparison of daily 275 mg extract and placebo. It registers subjective fatigue, attention outcomes and PROMIS Sleep Disturbance. At the November 13, 2026 check, the latter trial was not yet recruiting, and neither registry displayed results. They provide evidence of planned measurements for the related symptoms in 0117, 0250 and 0836. The participants and exposure remain distinct from the frozen claims. ## Nepalese pepper essential oil in a Parkinson cell model

Krishna and colleagues’ 2026 report exposed MPP+-challenged SH-SY5Y cells to *Zanthoxylum armatum* essential oil. Cell viability and neural-marker outcomes favored the oil over toxin exposure, and SPTLC1 expression was lower. The study measured cultured cells. It supplies disease-related evidence without behavioral measures of freezing, rigidity or dyskinesia, or a whole-pepper dietary exposure. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41852096/)

## Dry stir-frying changes red Sichuan pepper chemistry

An October 16, 2026 laboratory study used botanically authenticated dried mature pericarp of *Zanthoxylum bungeanum*. In a plain stir-frying series starting with a wok preheated to 120 °C, measured hydroxy-α-sanshool and hydroxy-β-sanshool reached maxima at five minutes, then declined with longer heating. Total polyphenols declined through the sampled interval. This directly distinguishes raw and heated pepper composition. No person ate the samples and no clinical endpoint was recorded. It provides exposure context for the toasted claims. [Primary local full text, Sections 2.1–2.2 and 3.5](Source-Text/PMC13697283.txt) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fochx.2026.104644)
