# Pinto bean sleep and nutmeg motor source context

## Bean frequency and sleep measured against fecal bacteria

March 9, 2026. Tume and colleagues recruited 111 older people in Lima and analyzed 69 with quantifiable fecal Akkermansia. Table 3 compares bacterial levels in 21 participants with good and 48 with poor Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index classifications (p=.59). Table 4 separately compares bacterial levels across canary-bean intake groups. Supplementary Table S1 gives marginal intake counts for canary and bayo beans, without a pinto item or bean-by-sleep cross-tab. The questionnaire captured frequency, with neither preparation nor quantity resolved. None of these comparisons estimates an insomnia association with eating boiled pinto beans. ## Authors clarify sampling and food questionnaire scope

June 25, 2026 online. The same authors explain that bacterial levels below detection or quantification were not included in the continuous analysis. They confirm the questionnaire did not quantify portions or record preparation and the population came from one aging center. The response supplies no new data or bean-by-sleep analysis. The cover identifies the 2027 issue and DOI ending 31.1.2848, while the landing page and some page headers retain the ahead-of-print DOI ending 29.4.2848. Both identify this response, separate from the original cohort paper.
