# Lentil and kidney bean evidence

## Lentils among options in a prebiotic Parkinson dietary pilot

Bedarf and colleagues enrolled ten people with Parkinson's and ten household controls in a four-week open-label diet and lactulose intervention. The food options included lentils, potatoes, green beans, onions, oat bran, lettuce, olive oil, bananas and strawberries, in addition to a specified daily apple component. The published adherence measure used apples and overall fiber. The Parkinson group had a small, statistically inconclusive decline in composite MDS-UPDRS III (p > .05), and the between-group stool-frequency difference persisted. The report does not specify individual lentil intake or how lentils were prepared. The motor analysis does not report separate rigidity, gait or facial expression item changes. ## Lentil dish in the proposed Brain Bites Parkinson meal box

The ANZCTR June 12, 2025 provisional entry names chicken and lentil tagine as an example dish and lists lentils among optional ingredients. This single-group program planned to enroll 20 people with Parkinson's for two weeks and record feasibility, cooking and eating behaviors and quality of life. The protocol excludes diagnosed dysphagia. The public entry still displayed June 12, 2025 as its last update, ethics submitted and not yet approved, and recruitment not yet started, with blank actual enrollment and data collection dates. The sponsor lists a 2025–26 grant under the same title. No trial results, recipe specifying whether lentils were canned or dry, or frozen symptom outcome were found as of November 13, 2026. ## Grouped lentil intake in a MIND diet insomnia trial

In the 2025 open-label randomized trial, 44 women with type 2 diabetes and insomnia were assigned the MIND low-calorie diet or a low-calorie comparator. The study enrolled with an insomnia screening criterion and reported Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index as the sleep outcome. The adjusted between-arm coefficient was −2.25 (reported p = .006). Lentils were pooled with beans and other legumes, and no lentil-only contrast or preparation was reported.
