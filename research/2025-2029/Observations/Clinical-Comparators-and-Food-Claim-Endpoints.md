# Clinical comparators and food-claim endpoints

The 2015–2019 neuropathy report records King's Parkinson's Pain Scale and pain thresholds as distinct measures. The earlier ODYSSEI registrations prespecify time to dyskinesia. The 2025 journal article adds the randomized outcome and the adverse-reaction table. That table reports affected participants under medication comparisons. Counts of visual hallucination adverse events do not measure changes in episode frequency. The limb-pain count has no severity scale. [Neuropathy study](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC6383044.txt) · [Earlier ODYSSEI registration](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000024536.txt) · [2025 trial report](../Source-Text/PMC12623527.txt)

The 2020–2024 group-boxing article uses mobility and balance outcomes with substantial loss to follow-up. The subsequent 2025 diary study captures drooling and pain alongside observed motor state, while the participation study captures apathy in adjusted analyses of daily functioning. These naturalistic and intervention measures improve endpoint selection for food claims. The studies did not compare the preparations in the queued hypotheses. [Boxing full-body extract](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID39262882-Group-Boxing-Abstract.txt) · [Parkinson home diary](../Source-Text/PMC12275004.txt) · [Participation analysis](../Source-Text/PMC12958334.txt)

Occurrence in text can arise from figure labels, background discussion, clinical differential diagnosis or data unrelated to an exposure. The orange text in the multiple system atrophy case is a plot annotation. The unusual visual symptoms in the colitis case occurred with cerebellar dysfunction. Read the studied population, clinical comparison and endpoint before entering ingredient evidence.
