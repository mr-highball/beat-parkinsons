# Corrections and route-specific pain evidence

The 2025 cinnamon correction replaces duplicated sodium-benzoate histology panels, not the original cinnamon motor panel. This qualifies interpretation of the preceding bucket's small animal report without silently deleting its motor outcomes. The correction is a separate dated source, not a new efficacy study. [Correction](../Source-Text/2025-PMID40072646-Cinnamon-Correction-Extract.txt)

The 2026 green-walnut work reports favorable induced-pain measures after local paw injection of an immature whole-fruit extract; edema is inconclusive. Compared with earlier oral oil or mixed-diet evidence, the fruit stage, solvent, route and model differ. It cannot establish oral cold-pressed walnut-oil benefit for Parkinson pain. [Primary extraction](../Source-Text/2026-PMID41596967-Marinaccio-Green-Walnut-Pain-Extract.txt)
