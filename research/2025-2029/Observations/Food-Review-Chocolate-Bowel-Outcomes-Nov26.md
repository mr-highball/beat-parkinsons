# Dark chocolate and different bowel outcomes

In the 2015–2019 bucket, a healthy-adult crossover found firmer stools and a possible slowing of transit under a high daily amount of dark chocolate. In the 2025–2029 bucket, a 14-day comparison in women prone to constipation found more bowel movements with a lower daily amount. The two comparisons measured different outcomes and enrolled different populations. The recent results paper links to a registry made public after the stated study dates. Neither study directly addresses Parkinson constipation severity.
