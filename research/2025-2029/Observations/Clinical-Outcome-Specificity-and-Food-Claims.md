# Clinical outcome specificity and food claims

Reviewed 2026-11-19. See the [exact source review](../../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/clinical-pair-clusters-nov19-65/README.md).

## Apathy and voluntary effort

The [2026 Parkinson effort-choice study](../Source-Text/PMC13442672.txt) measures apathy separately with the Apathy Evaluation Scale. Its experimental endpoint is a physical effort choice. The [OPTI-ON clinical cohort](../Source-Text/PMC13092661.txt) reports follow-up changes in a clinician apathy item after medication initiation. Neither provides the response to dried dates or sage.

The [earlier mouse study](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-PMID36526017-Rosmarinic-Acid-Parkinson-Model.txt) reports less model-associated hyperlocomotion after a sage constituent. An [older rat study](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID16026713-Rosmarinic-Acid-Behavior.txt) describes increased exploration after an injected constituent as motivation. The difference in animals, exposure route and outcomes prevents a clinical direction inference. The [dried-sage Parkinson pilot](../../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#sage-cognitive-trial-results-attachment) provides the closer exposure and population, with no named apathy outcome in the available report.

## Composite outcomes and single symptoms

In the [2018 irsenontrine registration and 2022 results](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT03467152.txt), the posted neuropsychiatric and motor outcomes are composite scores. The [2026 subset analysis](../Source-Text/PMC13244894.txt) brings biomarker detail from the same clinical trial. Separate visual hallucination frequency and rest tremor outcomes remain unreported in these records.

For pain, the [earlier SAFINONMOTOR series](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC8402181.txt) uses a dedicated measure without an untreated comparison. A [separate registered controlled study](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT03841604.txt) reports a week-16 pain-intensity interval spanning zero. The 2026 narrative synthesis follows several different outcomes and study families. These records refine how pain is assessed in Parkinson disease and leave the dried-date hypothesis open.

## Falls and injury

The [2026 registry study](../Source-Text/PMC13691003.txt) describes outcomes after hip fracture. The [cognition model](../Source-Text/PMC13347602.txt) uses self-reported history of falling as a clinical feature. Injury, fall history and prospectively counted recurrent falls answer different questions. The exact diet-claim review preserves those boundaries.
