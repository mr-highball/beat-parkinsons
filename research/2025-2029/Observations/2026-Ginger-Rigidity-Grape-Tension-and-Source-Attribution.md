# Ginger rigidity, grape tension and source attribution

Earlier [ginger rigidity research](../../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Ginger-Olfaction-and-Rigidity.md) separates culinary exposure from extract studies and shoulder hardness outcomes. The [new 6-shogaol original](../Source-Text/2026-PMID42375089-6Shogaol-Levodopa-Gut-Motility.txt) separates mouse bowel transit from a motor proxy and provides no rigidity measure. The [Jambi secondary review](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2026-Jambi-Ginger-and-Turmeric-Cultural-Review.txt) concerns customary blood-pressure use. The [2012 field excerpt](../../2010-2014/Observations/2012-Jambi-Constipation-Practice-and-Plant-Names.md) concerns constipation in a preparation with unresolved species. Each record addresses a different question and requires different attribution.

The [2026 grape seed pilot](../Source-Text/2026-Papa-Enovita-Grape-Seed-Tension-Outcome.txt) reports a favorable descriptive tension difference and an inconclusive group-by-time test. That differs in preparation and mood measurement from the earlier [grape fruit extract trial](../../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#grape-fruit-extract-and-anxiety-ratings-in-healthy-older-adults). The two studies cannot establish how raw grapes affect anxiety in Parkinson disease.

The Ginkgo companion and the grape seed report share their first registered study, NCT06309914. A result from one arm or one report should never be counted as an outcome measured in another arm. [Clinical audit and original records](../Food-Experiment-Round-1-Jambi-Ginger-and-Grape-Nov24.md)
