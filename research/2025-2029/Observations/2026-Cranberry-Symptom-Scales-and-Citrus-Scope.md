# Cranberry symptom scales and citrus source scope

Earlier cranberry trials in the 2020–2024 period measured beverage or powder exposures, including computerized attention and other cognition tests. The 2026 CRANMOOD juice trial also found no clear attention-domain contrast. Two selected short-term memory outcomes favored juice. Attention and memory remain separate when applying the result to cooked cranberry and Parkinson attention. [Earlier cranberry studies](../../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cherry-Cranberry-Watermelon.md) · [CRANMOOD methods and outcomes](../Source-Text/2026-CRANMOOD-Depression-Model-and-Enrollment-Extract.txt)

CRANMOOD measured depression on two separate instruments. At week four, cranberry juice participants had higher POMS depression-dejection scores. Later contrasts and HADS depression were inconclusive. The population was healthy final-year students and the frozen claim concerns eating dried cranberry in Parkinson disease. The scorecard captures the time-specific adverse observation and the limits of carrying it to that claim. [Depression comparisons](../Food-Experiment-Cranberry-Depression-Followup.md)

The animal paper adds a concrete citrus exposure but leaves the raw-fruit and measured fall-frequency gaps open.
