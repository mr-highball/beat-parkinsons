# Preserving food, symptom and article identities

The 2005–2009 meeting cases link a mixed banana meal to hypoglycemia and grapefruit intake to a medication-associated admission. Neither gives an isolated food estimate for the frozen clinical symptom. The two controlled citrus studies clarify which beverage and drug were tested, while the 2020–2024 pediatric abstract provides a separate orange-juice and gastric-emptying comparison.

The source cross-check exposed a second identity issue. A congress may be a real, useful resource while its PMC link is attached to an unrelated journal article elsewhere. Keeping the proceedings and the indexed articles under their independently verified DOIs preserves the new food context and the remaining article access gaps.
