# Strawberry preparation and fall-endpoint boundaries

The 2025 randomized whole-fruit data add measured postural sway for a six-fruit intervention with strawberry. The time-by-phase balance comparison was inconclusive (p=.457) and this study has no falls counts. The SAGE survey linked combined produce intake with retrospectively reported fall injuries, without estimating individual fruit effects. These should be read with the 2018 pooled fruit null result and its different design. The 2026 garden-strawberry leaf paper extends the related cell poster with a mouse motor comparison. Its longer latency to fall from a rotating rod measures task performance, and it has no spontaneous falls data. The same search surfaced 2025 horticultural results for melatonin applied to strawberry plants. Neither preparation is the raw fruit used in the frozen hypothesis.
