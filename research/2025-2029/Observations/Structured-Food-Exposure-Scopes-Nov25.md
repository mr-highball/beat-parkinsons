# Dietary grouping, shared vehicles and original-source review

The archived 2021 Geisinger cohort measured physical fatigability at the participant level and scored diet with the HEI-2015. Its Greens and Beans result concerns dark-green vegetables and mature legumes. The USDA coding guide places string beans with Other Vegetables. A cohort with a relevant outcome can leave the ingredient-specific claim open when the published exposure combines foods. The earlier Nurses’ Health Study questionnaire names string beans explicitly. The published group contrast still does not identify an effect for that item. The 2021 autism trial and 2022 companion sleep paper provide another attribution boundary. Olive oil was shared across treatment and placebo, much like the shared flavoring in the preceding archive review. The sleep paper gives an outcome comparison for the assigned cannabinoid formulations. A study with olive oil as a common vehicle cannot supply a separate effect for the oil.
