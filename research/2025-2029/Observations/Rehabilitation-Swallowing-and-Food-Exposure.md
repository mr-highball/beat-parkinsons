# Rehabilitation and dietary guidance in outcome records

The [2024 upper-limb rehabilitation registration](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/DRKS00034193.txt) and its [later randomized report](../Source-Text/2026-PMID41508050-Virtual-Reality-Upper-Limb-Training.txt) both identify hand and arm function as the primary comparisons. The report observes similar arm-function changes across the active arms. This protocol-to-report linkage preserves the exposure and endpoint even though food-like terms appear in the surrounding text.

The [2026 nurse-led drooling study](../Food-Experiment-Rehab-and-Orange-Context.md#orange-juice-in-nurse-led-dietary-guidance) presents an explicit food recommendation inside a larger randomized intervention. Unlike the [2017 orange-juice comparison](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28802068-Citrus-Juice-and-Ionized-Calcium.txt), it does not quantify an exposure period for juice or measure a juice effect. Neither study compares raw-orange intake with fall frequency in Parkinson disease. The [serial swallowing data](../Source-Text/2026-PMID41744403-Longitudinal-Swallow-Function.txt) discuss solid and liquid textures directly, with no individual sage food exposure.
