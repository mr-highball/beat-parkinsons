# Ingredient attribution in mango and tremor research

The [2020–2024 observations](../../2020-2024/Observations/2022-Mangiferin-Model-Comparisons.md) distinguish compound model behavior from clinical food effects. The new [clinical audit](../Food-Experiment-Round-1-Mangiferin-and-Clinical-Nov24.md) tracks the corrected Cao result and the fresh-mango registry status. The Alzheimer's review includes a multi-herb treatment called GRAPE. That acronym does not identify fruit intake. Green coffee extract and shared margarine oils also appear in a food-chemistry study with storage and sensory measurements instead of clinical fatigue.

Wearable tremor detection and repeated rest-tremor ratings provide relevant measurement approaches. The wearable validation reported sensitivity 0.61 and specificity 0.97. In a separate repeated rating study, the rest-tremor component had an ICC of 0.69 (95% CI 0.42–0.85). Those results describe test performance, leaving the raw-fig clinical claim open.
