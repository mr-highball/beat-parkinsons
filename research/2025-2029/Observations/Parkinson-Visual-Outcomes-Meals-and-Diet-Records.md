# Visual outcomes, meals and dietary records

The latest originals continue the method differences identified in the [2020–2024 source comparison](../../2020-2024/Observations/Visual-Hallucination-Outcome-and-Dietary-Exposure.md). In a selected group of people with minor perceptual phenomena at baseline, 24 of 34 developed psychosis during prospective follow-up. The outcome combines hallucinations and delusions and measures first onset. A small virtual-reality study instead records visual hallucinations and misperceptions during controlled scenes. The two studies supply different measures and neither stratifies an outcome by food intake. [Prospective cohort](../Source-Text/2025-PMID40331365-Minor-Phenomena-and-Psychosis-Followup.txt) · [Virtual-reality pilot](../Source-Text/2026-PMID41933015-Virtual-Reality-Visual-Perception-Pilot.txt).

The ambulatory blood pressure series includes meal times in its timeline, but its events are cognitive fluctuations reported by caregivers of four people with Parkinson disease dementia. An earlier case captures changes in alertness with altered blood pressure. A separate cross-sectional analysis reports higher fluctuation measures in a group defined by hallucinations or psychosis. Their varying outcomes and time windows prevent a causal chain from a meal to a visual episode. The pure autonomic failure case likewise describes visual misidentification and a glucose-associated pressure drop as separate observations. [Meal and pressure timeline](../Source-Text/2026-PMID41837340-Ambulatory-Pressure-Cognition-and-Meal-Timing.txt) · [2018 case](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID29456869-Blood-Pressure-and-Cognitive-Fluctuations.txt) · [Group analysis](../Source-Text/2026-PMID41835016-Blood-Pressure-and-Parkinson-Psychosis.txt) · [Autonomic case](../Source-Text/2026-PMID42419929-Autonomic-Failure-and-Visual-Phenomena.txt).

DREAMER plans clinical observation for isolated REM sleep without atonia and considers visual hallucinations in evolving phenotypes. Its earlier registration identifies enrollment as the primary registered measure. The later dried-fruit genetic analysis studies Parkinson diagnosis and inherits a generic food category.
