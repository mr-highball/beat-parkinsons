# Visual symptom outcomes and cohort reuse

The earlier clinical source comparisons distinguish first visual symptom onset, symptoms recorded in the prior week and episode frequency or duration measured by interview. The patient-specific network study uses those clinical group histories to compare resting scans. It divides scans into segments for an imaging identification analysis. Its results describe concurrent cognitive correlates. The 18 patients with minor hallucinations and 14 without are also described in the 2018 analysis and the 2025 preprint. Count the clinical cohort once. [2018 abstract](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID30536829-Minor-Hallucination-Network-Abstract.txt) · [2025 preprint](../Source-Text/2025-BioRxiv-Parkinson-Minor-Hallucination-Fingerprints.txt) · [2026 article](../Source-Text/PMC13652722.txt).

The MRI study of cortical atrophy describes 178 participants at intake and a final group of 83 people with dementia with Lewy bodies and 86 controls in both publication versions. The measurements compare structural brain maps with molecular and connectivity maps. The reports share the same cohort and do not follow hallucination episodes after a dietary exposure. [Preprint](../Source-Text/2026-MedRxiv-Lewy-Body-Cortical-Atrophy.txt) · [Article](../Source-Text/PMC13251274.txt).

The 2022 Parkinson biomarker study compared 34 participants with minor hallucinations and 65 without. Fasting plasma homocysteine was higher in the minor-hallucination group in the univariate comparison. It also measured folate and vitamin B12 and considered medication and disease-duration strata. The 2026 cell study measured protein changes after a homocysteine exposure. Neither provides an ingredient-specific intake measure or a food-timed clinical visual outcome. These are distinct human and laboratory observations, and the findings do not establish a dietary cause. [Clinical comparison](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC8920637.txt#L119) · [Cell study](../Source-Text/PMC13193807.txt).

The World Parkinson Congress collection presents whole-diet plans, education and psychosis reports as individual abstracts. The Plants for Parkinson poster, the later registered protocol and the SENSS protocol keep their own planned intervention and comparator. The MediPD pilot reports mainly diet scores and self-reported changes over three weeks. Post-surgical psychosis and a pimavanserin trial subgroup use different samples and outcomes. [Congress proceedings](../Source-Text/PMC13360760.txt) · [Diet protocol comparisons](../Food-Experiment-WHO-ICTRP-Followup.md#plants-for-parkinson).
