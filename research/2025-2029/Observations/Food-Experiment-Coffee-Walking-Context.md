# Coffee observations and outcomes

The earlier crossover provided secondary fatigue ratings while both conditions used brewed espresso. The later focus groups record perceptions of better walking after coffee without an assigned preparation or matched comparator. These studies address different outcomes and leave the effect of consuming brewed coffee on Parkinson fatigue unresolved.
