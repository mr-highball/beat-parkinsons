# Green bean preparations and frailty outcomes

In the 2020–2024 period, the Nurses' Health Study report found lower incidence of the fatigue component of frailty in its higher total-vegetable group. String beans were in the dietary instrument. Its ingredient-level supplemental results are still unverified. The separate NHANES study used a wider adult population, grouped green beans with other vegetables, and measured a composite frailty index. Adjustment weakened that broad group association. [Nurses' Health Study fatigue component](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID33022693-Green-Bean-Questionnaire-and-Aggregated-Frailty-Fatigue.txt) · [NHANES Model 3](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-PMID39651324-Other-Vegetables-and-NHANES-Frailty.txt)

The later Parkinson pilot recorded food choices and adherence during a multi-food and lactulose intervention. It supplies disease-specific preparation context, with no separate cooked green bean consumption or fatigue result. The previously cataloged clinic garden describes produce distribution. Both observations help identify where food exposure was named and what researchers actually measured. The traditional product uses the pod without seed as an infusion. The historic urinary indication concerns increasing the volume of urine. The clinical hypothesis concerns eating cooked green beans and fatigue.
