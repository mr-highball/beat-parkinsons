# Structured reporting adds context, not replication

The [Pacific Talanoa study](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Local-Knowledge/2026-Pacific-PINK1-Talanoa-Narratives.txt) adds caregiver and patient accounts of the role of faith, family and traditional healers. It broadens the preceding bucket's [Tanzania and Kenya observations](../../2020-2024/Observations/Cultural-and-Experiential-Tremor.md) to a small PINK1-associated Parkinson population. The articles support contextual comparisons about care and coping. No isolate or separate rest-tremor response warrants combining them into an efficacy estimate.

The [Ghana source audit](../Ghana-Mucuna-Tremor-and-Reporting-Audit.md) reuses an existing study. The recovered supplement improves source completeness; it does not resolve the prior bucket's individual accounts.

Compared with the preceding bucket's individual posts, the [survey context](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Personal-Reports/2026-Mucuna-Patient-Survey-Context.txt) adds structured patient reporting. It remains observational. The existing accession and this cultural context describe one study family; they do not form independent replications or establish treatment causality.
