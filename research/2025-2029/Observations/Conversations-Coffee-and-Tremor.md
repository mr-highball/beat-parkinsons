# Public conversations on caffeine, tea and tremor

The two Reddit threads expand the prior forum records to include green tea, unspecified tea, decaf blends and levodopa context. Reports distinguish alertness and mood from tremor, including worse tremor and no perceived change. One thread describes shaking after exposure in a participant without usual tremor. Repeat participants and a duplicated reply limit the count of distinct accounts. The threads raise questions about preparation, medication timing and type of tremor. The black-tea assessment retains its existing evidence basis.
