# Cinnamon and flaxseed preparation boundaries

The [2020 cinnamon extract trial](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-Imani-Cinnamon-Extract-Constipation-and-Abdominal-Pain.txt) explicitly counts constipation and reports p=.10 in the comparative table. The [2025 tea pilot](../Source-Text/2025-PMC12689000-Marjoram-Cinnamon-Tea-Bowel-Reports.txt) combines two herbs and describes bowel symptoms qualitatively. The [Koyna Valley record](../../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Local-Knowledge/2024-BSI-Koyna-Cinnamon-Root-and-Bark-Traditions.txt) pairs root paste with constipation and powdered bark with joint pain. These sources preserve three different preparations and levels of outcome information.

Clinical comparisons using [baked flaxseed](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMC5944250-Baked-Flaxseed-and-Constipation.txt) and [flaxseed flour](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32229443-Flaxseed-Flour-and-Lactulose-Constipation.txt) report improved bowel outcomes in distinct cohorts. The earlier [three-oil dialysis trial](../../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Constipation-Origins.md#registered-three-oil-dialysis-constipation-trial) used active oil comparators. The [flaxseed oil plus carnitine trial](../Source-Text/2025-Flax-Oil-Carnitine-Migraine-Results-Factual-Extract.txt) reports anxiety improvement under combined treatment and supplies no sleep results. The seed and combination results cannot assign an outcome to cold-pressed flaxseed oil alone.
