# Fatigue outcomes and shared flavor across periods

The three Alzheimer trials from 2009 through 2014 measured cognition or functioning. Their fatigue entries are adverse-event counts under standardized stigma extract, with placebo and two different active comparators. The 2021 perimenopause trial separately measured energy and fatigue, with an inconclusive extract-versus-placebo trajectory. These provide earlier contexts for the directly measured Parkinson fatigue direction in the Isfahan powder trial. Each preparation, symptom metric and comparator retains its own evidence family.

In a 2020 Parkinson constipation trial, orange flavoring was present in both intervention and placebo. A 2021 laboratory study compared a cocoa-fiber and purified D-limonene blend with fructooligosaccharides. These sources connect orange to an intervention vehicle or a constituent. Neither isolates raw orange as the causal exposure. An unrelated olfactory study also uses orange as a smell stimulus.
