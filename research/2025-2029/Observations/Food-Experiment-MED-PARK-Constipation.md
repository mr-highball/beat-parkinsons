# Planned bowel outcomes in olive oil dietary programs

The earlier MEDI-PD trial reports an inconclusive eight-week constipation comparison. The later MED-PARK protocol also assigns an entire dietary pattern and discusses raw olive oil. Its registry specifies constipation-related measures over six months and has no posted results. Neither randomized program assigns an isolated oil grade.
