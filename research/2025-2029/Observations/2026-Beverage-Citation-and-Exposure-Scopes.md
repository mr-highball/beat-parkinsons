# Fruit beverage and constituent evidence across periods

The 2005–2009 period has distinct primary sources for beverage antioxidant assays and human metabolism of pomegranate products. The 2025–2029 review connects these topics under one author and year. Matching the analytical methods to the original publication restores the correct citation and separates beverage chemistry from a clinical symptom outcome. [2008 beverage study](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2008-PMID18220345-Seeram-Beverage-Antioxidant-Assays.txt) · [Human absorption study](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2008-PMID18598186-Pomegranate-Metabolite-Exposure.txt) · [2026 review scope](../Source-Text/PMC13340997-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)

The 2015–2019 resveratrol trial gives a borderline POMS anxiety result in postmenopausal women. The 2025–2029 trial studied a different population and compound dose and reports anger/hostility in its main article. The 2026 supplement is needed to inspect the numeric anxiety result. Neither exposure establishes how much of the isolated compound is delivered by raw grapes. [2017 mood data](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28054939-Resveratrol-Postmenopause-Anxiety.txt) · [2026 mood data](../Source-Text/2026-PMID42459802-Resveratrol-POMS-Outcome-Scope.txt)

The 2020–2024 annual sage study and later atrophy review provide an example of a shared constituent across species. Cultured cell diameter and atrophy markers are poor substitutes for clinical rigidity. The observation applies to future checks that use material from a different member of the same genus.
