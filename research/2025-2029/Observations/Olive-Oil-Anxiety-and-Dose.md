# Olive oil processing and anxiety follow-through

The [2025 Memecik study](../Food-Experiment-Olive-Oil-Anxiety.md) resolves the pressing temperature and polyphenol contrast for two oils used in a rodent test. The open-field center result remains inconclusive, as in an [older female rat experiment](../../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Olive-Oil-Anxiety.md#aged-rats-and-open-field-behavior). More open-arm time in a separate elevated plus maze under the higher-polyphenol preparation is compatible with the [earlier Wistar result](../../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Olive-Oil-Anxiety.md). Different ages and protocols limit comparison across the studies. The [human quantity survey](../Food-Experiment-Facial-Falls-and-Voice.md#olive-oil-intake-and-hads-anxiety-in-a-convenience-survey) measures HADS anxiety but omits pressing grade and has no Parkinson-specific result.
