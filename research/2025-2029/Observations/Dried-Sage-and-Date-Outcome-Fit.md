# Dried sage and date outcome fit

The [2005 controlled dried-leaf crossover](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID16205785-Dried-Sage-Mood-Stressor.txt) shows that one formulation produced opposite short-term alertness comparisons depending on whether the participant was doing a stress task. Both estimates belong to one group of healthy volunteers. The same paper's anxiety and computer task outcomes give additional context for testing conditions. Its original tables provide no clinical apathy or voluntary-motivation measure.

An [earlier mixed-sage registration](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT03695003.txt) and the [2021 outcome paper](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID33466627.txt) closely match in composition, actual enrollment, age and task timing. The journal report found no clear mood effect by either named measure. Cite them together while the exact identifier link remains uncertain. The [dried-sage Parkinson pilot](../../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#sage-cognitive-trial-results-attachment) used a more relevant population and preparation, but its available report lists memory and salivation outcomes without a named apathy result.

The [2022 blueberry comparator registration](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/NCT05358210.txt) illustrates the importance of reading comparator arms. It assigns dried dates despite a title mentioning only blueberries. Planned cognition, lower-extremity function and falls outcomes do not isolate motivation, and results are unavailable in the registry. The [2025 date trial about hedonic hunger](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT06826235.txt) lists a food-specific reward scale outside Parkinson disease, with no posted results.

The [Ajwa crossover from 2014](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/NCT02288611.txt) registered gut outcomes. The [2015 paper](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-Eid-Whole-Ajwa-Mood-Diary.txt) reports collection of mood diaries and still provides no comparative diary result. Follow-up remains open for publication of comparative diary outcomes.
