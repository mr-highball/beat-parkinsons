# Comparator ingredients and anticipated side effects

The [1971 hypercholesterolaemia report](../../1970-1974/Source-Text/PMC1800097.txt) used corn oil for cooking in the common diet given to all three study-one groups. Its original scans attribute two withdrawals to gastrointestinal effects in the cholestyramine group. The common oil exposure prevents a separate causal estimate for corn oil. The [almond oil study](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/PMC3700459.txt) uses the oil as a control and carrier in an inhalation experiment. The specific route and physiologic stress measures are distinct from an orally administered oil and depression severity.

The [2020 ACID protocol](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7492209.txt) lists possible constipation and stool color changes as potential sources of unmasking. The [2022 primary results](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-PMID36372613-ACID-Cognition-and-Adverse-Events.txt) report one mild constipation event among 106 active participants and two among 100 placebo participants. The product is a purified bilberry and black currant preparation. The [whole-blueberry powder comparison](../../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Blueberry-Followup.md#blueberry-powder-and-constipation-in-functional-gastrointestinal-disorders) directly measured constipation severity in an adult functional gastrointestinal population. Its blueberry-placebo estimate favors powder slightly, with uncertainty spanning both directions. The outcome and exposure boundaries matter when interpreting the raw-blueberry Parkinson hypothesis.

The [1992 broad-bean report](../../1990-1994/Source-Text/PMC489215.txt) separates improvement on an overall Parkinson motor scale from severe dyskinesia in three previously susceptible participants. The direction varies by outcome despite one tested food. The [2025 clove odor protocol](../Source-Text/PMC12352155.txt) plans a Hamilton Anxiety Scale endpoint for smell training in an older cognitively at-risk population. It tests combinations of odors, with no isolated oral clove condition or results.
