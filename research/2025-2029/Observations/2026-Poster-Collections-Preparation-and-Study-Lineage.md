# Poster collections, preparation and study lineage

The 2015 IBS trial enrolled 75 participants and reported bowel and fatigue measures for the group-level diet comparison. Its 2018 poster and final 2020 paper analyze diaries from 66 participants in the same trial. The orange listing describes a food-group example, and coffee consumption persisted in one arm alongside many dietary adjustments. Retaining the parent protocol and analysis dates makes one trial family visible across periods. [Parent trial](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-PMID26255043-Bohn-IBS-Diets-Stool-and-Fatigue.txt) · [Later adherence analysis](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32086111-Bohn-IBS-Adherence-Orange-and-Coffee-Scope.txt)

The 2024 kefir-related posters report distinct laboratory endpoints. The 2025 peptide abstract repeats the four peptide identities and the cell model used in P509, so possible data reuse matters when interpreting the publication count. Animal retinal outcomes, cellular viability and clinical fall frequency are different measurements. [ACNP member scopes](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-ACNP-Poster-Abstracts-P305-P608-Food-Scopes.txt) · [Kefir peptide companion](../Source-Text/2025-PMC12741607-Kefir-Peptide-Cell-Companion.txt)

The earlier European traditional urinary account describes a seedless-pod infusion. The 2006 crossover measured acute glucose outcomes with a capsule, and the 2021 report assessed a laboratory pod extract. These preparations and measurement domains are documented in separate records.
