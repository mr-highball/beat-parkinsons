# Fig parts and endpoints across periods

The 2020 reports cover [concentrated fruit extract](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-Gafoor-Fig-Fruit-Extract.txt) and a [mixed meal in diagnosed Parkinson disease](../../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Fig-Clinical-and-Model-Traces.md#figs-among-foods-in-the-hegelmaier-parkinson-intervention). The 2025 [bark phytosome experiment](../Source-Text/2025-Singh-Verma-Fig-Bark-Phytosomes.txt) adds a different formulation and biochemical measurements. Its discussion of fruit extract cannot expand the reported experimental arms. None of these comparisons supplies a separate resting-tremor outcome after raw fig. The 2014 [mouse behavioral study](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-Bhanushali-Fig-Aerial-Extract.txt) helps distinguish general suppression of activity from a symptom-specific measure.
