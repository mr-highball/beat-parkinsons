# Coffee during wearing off and the distinction between alertness and fatigue

The [2025 public thread](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2025-Reddit-Coffee-and-Wearing-Off-Tiredness.txt) documents tiredness and sleepiness during medication wearing off. The [2026 public thread](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2026-Reddit-Coffee-and-Variable-Energy-Reports.txt) adds variable perceived effects with several beverage preparations. The [2019 account](../../2015-2019/Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2019-Parkinsons-UK-Coffee-and-Persistent-Fatigue.txt) includes no relief after morning coffee. Preserve the differences among feeling alert, being able to move, and changes in measured fatigue when comparing the [2009 clinical cohort](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID24868347-Filipino-Parkinson-Fatigue-Measurement.txt) and the public reports.
