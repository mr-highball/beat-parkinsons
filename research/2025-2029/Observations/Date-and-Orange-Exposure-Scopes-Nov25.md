# Date and orange exposures across publication periods

The 2004 date fruit and seed extracts suppressed paw swelling in a rat arthritis model. The 2017 Moroccan cultivar study reported distinct paw and ear edema measures. A later Saudi cultivar trial abstract adds a composite arthritis score and leaves full Methods access open. These studies show why fruit preparation and the measured outcome need separate entries. The older evidence describes edema and inflammatory markers. The later migraine registry asks about pain severity after a distillate but still reports no results.

A 2021 ethnography records a question about the color or flavor of a fiber laxative during a constipation consultation. The clinician did not confirm the product form, and the patient did not recall a response. In a 2022 animal experiment, orange juice is explicitly part of a fermented milk mixture. A separate 2024 review repeats an earlier Parkinson trial with orange flavoring in both arms. The original comparisons support keeping composition, flavoring and fruit exposure separate.
