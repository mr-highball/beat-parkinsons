# Sweet basil exposure, intestinal transit and group differences

[Previous period observations](../../2020-2024/Observations/README.md)

The preceding period’s Manobo field survey documented a decoction or soup use for several indications, including constipation. This period’s tissue study used solvent extracts made from identified dried sweet basil leaves and measured reduced ileal contraction. The earlier whole-animal water-extract study measured faster dye progress. A laboratory contraction tracing and a whole-animal transit measure describe different functions, and testing a different basil species can yield the opposite transit result. The separate Parkinson transit cohort shows why these are weak proxies for symptoms people experience.
