# Neurological examinations, voice outcomes and exposure attribution

The Finley feeding study examined Parkinson-related clinical signs in healthy participants receiving two different dietary fat sources. The exams found no neurological impairment. Comparing Figure 1 and Table 1 restores the original fat-group labels and identifies the separate enrollment and tracer-analysis denominators. The 2005 proceedings used cinnamon as an odor stimulus, the 2010 allergy case described dysphonia with multiple triggers, and the 2025 series reported contact stomatitis from selected flavored products. The prospective Kampo pilot includes clinician-rated speech items within a combined oral score. It leaves separate pronunciation outcomes and attribution to cassia open. The 2024 West Bank survey records practitioner knowledge about voice disorders and identifies anise, ginger and spearmint among its leading recommendations. It supplies cultural context without measured speech intelligibility outcomes in a Parkinson population.
