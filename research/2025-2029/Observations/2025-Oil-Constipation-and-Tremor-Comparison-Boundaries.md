# Constipation and tremor outcomes across study designs

The [2024 MEDI-PD trial](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-MEDI-PD-Rusch-Participant-Flow-and-Supplement-Extract.txt) found an inconclusive between-arm constipation score contrast. The [Fernandez waitlist trial](../Source-Text/2026-Fernandez-Mediterranean-Counseling-Preprint.txt) reported improved bowel habits in both groups and an inconclusive three-month comparison between immediate and delayed counseling. Each trial evaluated a dietary pattern. Olive oil intake and processing grade were not assigned independently.

The [2025 Joukar comparison](../Source-Text/2025-PMID41383802-Joukar-EVOO-vs-Refined-Constipation.txt) compared two grades of olive oil in adults with functional constipation. In cardiac bypass patients, [Bahrami](../Source-Text/2025-PMID40642904-Bahrami-EVOO-and-Postoperative-Constipation.txt) compared extra-virgin olive oil with usual care and reports a favorable bowel outcome at abstract depth. Acute postoperative causes, medication use and stool scoring may differ from chronic constipation in Parkinson disease. The [NHANES analysis](../Source-Text/2025-PMID40822527-Mediterranean-Score-and-Constipation-in-NHANES.txt) gives a cross-sectional whole-diet association, with no oil-specific estimate. The three comparisons complement different questions about formulation, study setting and dietary pattern.

In the [long-term stimulation cohort](../Source-Text/2025-PMID41038844-Rest-and-Action-Tremor-After-STN-Stimulation.txt), resting tremor measured over follow-up had an inconclusive change in the aggregate population, while action tremor mildly worsened at years ten and fifteen. Attendance declined from 301 at baseline and year one to 57 at year fifteen. The source supports keeping the two tremor phenotypes distinct when interpreting food comparisons.
