# Rosemary and depression comparisons

The 2026 poster combines five oral and inhaled comparisons and reports a pooled depression interval that spans zero with substantial heterogeneity. It shows why two favorable dried-capsule reports warrant separate inspection. The student trial's endpoint contrast in the poster is imprecise, and the major-depression trial's large week-eight difference includes an already lower baseline score in the rosemary arm. The 2025 crossover used a depression screen at entry and reported anxiety after treatment. The earlier clinical capsule evidence and the newer synthesis leave Parkinson depression after fresh culinary rosemary unmeasured.
