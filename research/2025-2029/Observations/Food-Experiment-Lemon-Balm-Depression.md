# Lemon balm formulations and repeated study reports

The Hester journal paper and earlier Major abstract likely describe the same capsule experiment, with differences in masking, formulation wording and placebo outcome. Mazzola explicitly identifies the parent high-dose group for its cortisol analysis. The cited registry describes a different plan. Count each clinical family once and retain its outcome and preparation limits. The inhaled Melissa trial in dialysis separately measures a sadness item. Its post-treatment prevalence and severity comparisons are inconclusive. This result adds detail to the earlier anxiety and dry-mouth report from the same 68-person study. [Original item extract](../Source-Text/2025-PMID40082838-Pasyar-Lemon-Balm-Dialysis-Extract.txt)

The ECT study follows cognition in participants under treatment for depression. It links to the earlier cognitive-outcome registration, with no separate depression severity result. [ECT original extract](../Source-Text/2025-Nikfarjam-Melissa-ECT-Cognition-Extract.txt)

Fatali’s full 2025 report prints the exact crossover registration. Its 20 participants and one-week washout differ from the registered target of 40 and two-week washout. The original verifies the anxiety score SD and clarifies the discrepancy between the abstract versions. The methods name the Beck Depression Inventory but describe anxiety scoring. No separate depression result was identifiable. [Original tea and anxiety report](../Source-Text/2025-Fatali-Rose-Tea-Anxiety-Full-Text-Extract.txt) · [Earlier registration](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/IRCT20220821055767N1.txt)

The 2026 Lemcog study uses an acute standardized hydroalcoholic extract and reports no significant overall calmness effect. Its supplement shows mental fatigue increased in both arms, numerically more with extract, with an inconclusive interaction. Task-specific results coexist with baseline imbalance and exploratory analyses. A separate computational study selects plant-associated compounds and predicts molecular targets. Neither supplies a brewed-leaf Parkinson depression estimate.
