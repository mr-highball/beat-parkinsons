# Coffee exposure in stroke and internal medicine proceedings

The European Stroke Organisation member and its later journal article report the same 598-person SECRETO exposure analysis. The registry represents the broader observational project. Recent coffee timing and young-adult cryptogenic stroke onset are distinct from symptom response to ordinary brewed coffee in Parkinson disease. [2025 proceedings](../Source-Text/2025-PMID40726475-ESOC-SECRETO-and-Aphasia-Listings.txt) · [2026 outcome paper](../Source-Text/2026-PMID42274784-SECRETO-Coffee-and-Tea.txt) · [Original registration](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/NCT01934725.txt)

The internal medicine proceedings report clinical contexts involving a combined analgesic and workout supplements. Those mixtures contain caffeine or discuss it as a stimulant. The case outcomes and coincident exposures do not isolate a dietary coffee effect. [2021 case members](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID34297318-SGIM-Caffeine-Cases.txt)
