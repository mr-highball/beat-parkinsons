# Historical routes and human food fiber outcomes

The Folger manuscript's anise sleep entry records scent. An independently inspected Boyle opening places an anise scent remedy and an external nutmeg-and-rosemary sleep remedy on the same folio. Its facing page describes oral nutmeg alongside other spices for a different symptom. The observations support preserving preparation, route and intended symptom at entry level. Food-derived carrot and orange fibers were studied separately from their whole-food forms. The 2020 carrot laboratory data describe fermentation, the 2022 orange pilots describe microbial and plasma proteins, and the 2025 carrot intervention includes a measured constipation subscale. The later trial's published statistically clear gastrointestinal outcome is diarrhoea. This prevents a laboratory or neighboring symptom result from being assigned to the whole-food constipation claim.
