# Olive-oil anxiety evidence across cohorts

The 2014 Australian cohort reported a higher odds estimate for self-reported diagnosed anxiety at higher estimated oleic acid intake. It did not compare olive-oil preparations. A preliminary 2016 active-oil trial reported changes in composite mental health in fibromyalgia, with no dedicated anxiety result. The 2024 scoping review groups those outcome domains, so its broad summary cannot be transferred directly to Parkinson anxiety.

The 2025 AU-ARROW baseline report and its 2024 protocol belong to one study. Olive oil is part of a broader dietary and lifestyle intervention. The baseline report supplies no between-arm change. The 2026 olive-leaf pilot adds a controlled null result for a different plant part and population. Taken together, the records qualify the transfer of the Round 1 anxiety claim and leave the cold-pressed Parkinson comparison open.
