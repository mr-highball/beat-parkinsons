# Grape powder, anxiety outcomes and a later retraction

The [2020–2024 grape follow-up](../../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md) distinguishes grape concentrate, a whole-grape preparation, and the animal open-field and elevated-plus-maze measures. The [2025 retraction](../Source-Text/2025-PMID40058952-Grape-Powder-Study-Retraction.txt) withdraws a different 2013 rat experiment cited by the 2014 phytochemical review. The earlier citation no longer adds efficacy evidence regardless of the direction the paper originally reported.

The [2017 fruit-extract trial](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID29163162-Calapai-Grape-Extract-Anxiety.txt) measured Hamilton anxiety in healthy older adults. Its registry first posted after the recorded primary completion date. A [2024 trial](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-PMID39335927-Amone-Grape-Extract-Cognition.txt) used the same branded extract and measured cognition. Shared products across independent studies can help trace preparations, while the study-specific endpoints determine which comparisons can inform a claim.

The [ongoing college grape-powder trial](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT07214428.txt) plans separate psychological outcomes. The November 25 check found no posted results.
