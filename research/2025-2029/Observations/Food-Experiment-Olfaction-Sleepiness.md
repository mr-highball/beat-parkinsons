# Daytime dysfunction and sleepiness on rising

The MIND trial’s inconclusive daytime dysfunction contrast and the black soybean trial’s favorable sleepiness-on-rising finding use different populations, interventions and scales. The earlier three-arm patent reports no between-arm difference for sleep factors. These comparisons leave boiled black bean consumption and daytime sleepiness in Parkinson’s unresolved.
