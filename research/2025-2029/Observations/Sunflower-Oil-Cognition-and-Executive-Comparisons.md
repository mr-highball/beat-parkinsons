# Sunflower oil cognition and executive comparisons

The 2025 mouse study isolates sunflower seed oil against water in a scopolamine model. Both doses improved novel-object discrimination. The pressing grade was unspecified and the investigators did not measure executive function separately. The related L-DOPA paper used sunflower oil as an injected carrier and estimated drug-related gene expression. [Recognition-memory study](../Source-Text/2025-PMID39973828-Sunflower-Recognition-Memory.txt) · [Carrier study](../Source-Text/2025-PMID40293707-Parkinson-Model-Gene-Expression-Vehicle.txt).

The previous period included worse eight-week radial-maze performance on both very high-fat formulations, improved recall in an uncontrolled student study and a faster switching result on refined sunflower oil versus coconut oil in a feasibility trial. These comparisons changed the task, population, control, processing or overall diet. They cannot be combined into a single directional estimate. A larger earlier randomized study had an inconclusive executive comparison against two doses of fish oil.
