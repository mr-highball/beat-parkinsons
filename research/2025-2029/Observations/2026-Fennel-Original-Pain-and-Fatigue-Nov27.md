# Original evidence and outcome scope

The 2026 original dysmenorrhea trial measures pain and duration. The 2019 fennel-drops comparison measured whether fatigue occurred during menstruation. The new original therefore adds a different endpoint and a distinct extract formulation, with no fatigue estimate to synthesize across the periods.
