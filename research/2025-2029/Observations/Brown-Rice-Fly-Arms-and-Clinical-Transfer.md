# Brown rice fly outcomes and clinical transfer

A related 2025 author report links brown-rice experimental feed to better short-duration climbing in iron-exposed flies. The Methods and figure label different climbing distances. The previous 2020–2024 bucket contains a separate publication from the same program that reports cooked-ground feed, and an abstract describing a ten-day iron and five-day intervention schedule. Related publications can clarify assay and preparation differences without establishing cohort independence.

These experiments measure fly locomotion. No human Parkinson postural-instability estimate was recovered. The archived reports preserve the favorable behavioral comparison and the measurement limits for a future source audit.
