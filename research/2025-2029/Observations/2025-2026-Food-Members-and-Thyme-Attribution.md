# Ingredient identity and attribution in conference and clinical sources

The 2025 UEG poster volume contains a blueberry crossover and retains the distinction between a stool-scale protocol and a reported constipation estimate. The 2026 IUNS collection uses the label African thyme for a different genus in an ingredient inventory. These full-volume results preserve the member-level exposures while keeping names separate from the culinary ingredients under review.

The recovered rhinosinusitis original adds a measured olfactory comparison for a multicomponent treatment that includes thyme. Its design and aggregate benefit leave the effect of a single ingredient undetermined. This extends the older olfactory-training records, which also use several stimulus compounds in a single intervention.
