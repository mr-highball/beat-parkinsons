# Asparagus stem extract, roasted shoots and fatigue

The 2016 randomized crossover recorded less POMS fatigue after a mental challenge during the extract phase. Inspecting both sets of bars in Figure 5B suggests roughly two additional T points of reduction with extract relative to placebo. The figure supplies a descriptive comparison with no paired confidence interval, and the formal comparison shown is within the extract period. [Original trial and Figure 5B](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27465727-Asparagus-Stem-Extract-POMS-Fatigue.txt)

The preceding 2020–2024 period contains a shift-worker study with a fatigue score whose reported sign conflicts with the written scale anchors, and a registration focused on sleep. These outcomes cannot corroborate a direct Parkinson fatigue effect. The 2026 synthesis repeats those source families and overstates the original fatigue comparison to placebo. [Earlier trials and registration](../../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md) · [2026 synthesis review](../Source-Text/2026-PMID42311153-Asparagus-Stem-Human-Trial-Review.txt)

The roasting study concerns chemistry of discarded lower stems. Its result supports keeping preparation transfer tentative. A separate ethnopharmacology review concerns a different genus, Polygonatum. The directional score rests on the original same-species stem-extract crossover with substantial transfer limits.
