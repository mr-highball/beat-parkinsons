# Exposure and endpoint differences in the recovered proceedings

The 2015–2019 proceedings include oil chemistry, test meals and isolated constituents. The original biochemistry poster measured blood markers after fresh-orange consumption. The exposure and duration are concrete, with a possible carryover typo in the baseline label. Falls and constipation were outside its endpoint list.

The 2020–2024 proceedings add measured espresso hemodynamics and independent comparisons of fatty-acid composition. The espresso crossover measured circulation rather than fatigue. The triglyceride experiment replaced corn oil in both arms. The independent monoarthritis study used oxidized oil in one multi-component diet and found no clear behavioral pain difference. These papers narrow the attribution question while leaving the three frozen clinical outcomes open or at their existing scorecard tiers.
