# Cinnamon intake in integrative group visits

The 2026 dietary abstract reports more cinnamon intake following a broad group-visit program for adults with chronic pain and depression. The likely parent report from 2019 tested the full program against primary care and found inconclusive pain and depression differences. The shared sample and program support the link, while the 2026 abstract omits the registration. The dietary result gives no ground-cinnamon amount or independent symptom effect.
