# Fig formulations and tremor ascertainment

The [registered Iranian trial](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/IRCT20220406054433N1.txt) and its [2025 results](../Source-Text/2025-Mohammadi-Kenari-Fig-Almond-Drooling.txt) provide a rare assigned oral common-fig formulation with an explicitly named tremor event. Three events occurred in the fig-and-almond syrup arm, with none in the almond-and-raisin or placebo arms. Each arm had 40 children with ADHD taking methylphenidate. Formulations differed, and the safety table lacks tremor subtype and severity. The observed comparison stays available as a possible formulation or co-treatment effect. Drooling was reported in both active arms, while the result provides no independent fig comparison for saliva severity.

The [2022 flower-bud study](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-Chhoud-Fig-and-Date-Flower-Buds-in-Parkinson-Plasma.txt) exposed isolated Parkinson plasma to extracts and measured enzyme activity. The [2025 rat study](../Source-Text/PMC12455911.txt) used fresh fig puree and assessed liver measures and Y-maze behavior in an intermittent-hypoxia model. The [2024 gallstone trial](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-PMID39799455-Vinegar-Soaked-Fig-Gallstone-Trial.txt) compared vinegar-soaked fig with an active treatment and gives aggregate safety counts. These sources cover different preparations and outcomes. They supply no symptom-specific estimate for raw fig in Parkinson disease.

Earlier [fig observations](../../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Fig-Clinical-and-Model-Traces.md) cover the shared fig-containing diet in a small Parkinson intervention and the inaccessible full methods for the oral fruit-extract animal study. The new oral formulation comparison merits a search for a specified tremor phenotype and a documented link to the actual culinary exposure.
