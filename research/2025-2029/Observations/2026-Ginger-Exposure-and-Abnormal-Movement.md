# Linking a culinary preparation to an animal outcome

The current period's Korean disclosure identifies an explicit movement outcome after levodopa and repeats the prior WO experiment. Rat arthritis withdrawal tests and fly climbing give different evidence. A shared ingredient source can connect comparisons without equating an animal outcome with a human symptom measure. The 2010 retail analysis verifies the presence and approximate concentration of 6-shogaol in ground dried ginger. It supplies a chemical bridge, while the Korean preparation is an ethanol extract and does not establish equivalent human exposure.
