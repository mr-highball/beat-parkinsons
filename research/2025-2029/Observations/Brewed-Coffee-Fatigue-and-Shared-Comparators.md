# Brewed coffee, fatigue, and shared comparators

The [paper-filtered coffee crossover](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC6213082.txt) reports less acute mental fatigue with regular coffee than flavored water after caffeine abstention in healthy adults. The independent [capsule trial in Parkinson disease](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/PMC3414662.txt) reports small favorable point estimates on the Fatigue Severity Scale and intervals spanning both directions. Caffeine is present in the tested drip coffee. The bridge rests on acute fatigue measurement and a directly tested coffee constituent, with substantially different populations, time horizons and formulations.

The [four-person espresso crossover](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC4782159.txt) reports individual differences in both directions from regular versus decaffeinated espresso. Both interventions were brewed coffee. The [Turkish coffee crossover](../Source-Text/PMC11901579.txt) reports an inconclusive fatigue comparison, and its later raw means favor water. The newer [espresso breakfast report](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID33640376-Shared-Espresso-Breakfast-Tiredness.txt) reports less tiredness after a meal with espresso in both conditions. That time contrast supplies no separate coffee effect.

Earlier clinical work also shows why source scope matters. The [restless legs comparison](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/PMC2276830.txt) records cups of coffee and a separate fatigue scale, with fatigue grouped by restless legs status. The [oil crossover](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC8417778.txt) shares coffee and cream across conditions and reports biomarkers by oil assignment. The [longer Café-PD registration](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/NCT01738178.txt) names fatigue among planned items. Its body and registry still do not yield an item-specific effect. These studies clarify which measurements can support a directional comparison and which provide context.
