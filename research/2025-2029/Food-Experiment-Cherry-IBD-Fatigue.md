# Cherry concentrate and fatigue in inflammatory bowel disease

## Fatigue severity and impact in ulcerative colitis

Sinclair and colleagues first published February 17, 2025. Thirty-five adults with mild to moderate ulcerative colitis completed the Montmorency concentrate or placebo trial, with 18 and 17 participants by arm. Table 3 reports a difference of −2.05 on fatigue severity (95% CI −4.87 to 0.77, p=.148) and −8.16 on fatigue impact (95% CI −20.65 to 4.34, p=.193). Both secondary outcomes were inconclusive. The article reports one disease flare in each arm. The concentrated drink, disease population and IBD outcome scale differ from dried whole cherry and Parkinson fatigue. ## Registered Crohn disease fatigue scale

NCT06901986 first posted March 30, 2025. The separate trial registers tart cherry supplementation and includes inflammatory bowel disease fatigue among its planned measures. The official record showed not yet recruiting and no posted results on November 15, 2026. The prospective outcome and diagnosis differ from the completed ulcerative colitis cohort and the frozen Parkinson question.
