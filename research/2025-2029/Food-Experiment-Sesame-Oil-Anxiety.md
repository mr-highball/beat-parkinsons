# Follow-up publication from the sesame and sunflower oil trial

## Same-cohort oil use and metabolic outcomes

The 2025 BMC Nutrition article uses trial IRCT20140208016529N6 and the same 60-person sesame-versus-sunflower design as the 2022 publications. Its Methods state that participants used the assigned oils unheated on salads or after cooking. The paper reports metabolic and oxidative outcomes. It adds preparation and trial identity context, without independent anxiety data.
