# Isolated thymol and motor performance

## Isolated thymol and motor performance in aging mice

The 2025 report tested isolated thymol in corn oil versus corn oil in male mice with accelerated aging. The treadmill comparison involved 11 control and 13 treated survivors. The reported average treated running distance roughly doubled from baseline while the control average stayed near baseline. Grip strength declined 36% in controls and stayed near baseline in the treated group. Rotarod performance also favored thymol. The authors used one-tailed tests for several outcomes and excluded animals that died.

These are endurance, strength and coordination tests after isolated-compound dosing in an aging model. No fresh oregano, step length or clinical Parkinson outcome was measured. The earlier conference report describes overlapping models and liver/autophagy work.
