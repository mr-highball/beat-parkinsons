# ISRCTN dietary registrations and outcomes, 2025–2029

## Hydrogen-rich water thesis results

Kimberly Dargan’s University of Kent thesis, deposited September 17, 2025, reports the ISRCTN17961308 crossover in chapter 7. Twelve participants completed four weeks per period. The thesis reports increased interleukin-6 with an outlier included and a transient increase in total antioxidant capacity. It found no improvement in functional movement or motor and nonmotor symptoms. The hydrogen-rich water formulation is outside the frozen vocabulary. [Primary thesis](https://kar.kent.ac.uk/111286/) · [2023 registration](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#hydrogen-rich-water-crossover)

## Ghana Mucuna trial final results

Cilia and colleagues published the final report for PACTR201611001882367 online November 21, 2025. This open-label Ghana trial randomized 35 participants, with 32 in the efficacy analysis, to powder from roasted *Mucuna pruriens* seeds or levodopa plus benserazide for 12 months. Motor examination scores improved in both groups. The adjusted difference in PDQ-39 change was 0.5 points (95% CI −9.6 to 10.7). The authors report noninferiority, and the confidence interval extends beyond the +2-point margin specified in their methods. Nausea and diarrhea each prompted one powder-arm discontinuation. The study concerns a levodopa-containing seed powder from a species distinct from broad bean. [Primary publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13347555/) · [2018 interim report](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#ghana-mucuna-trial-interim-report)

## Gastric mapping after a standardized meal

ISRCTN87623941 was registered January 13, 2025. Ten people with Parkinson’s took a standardized muesli bar and milky drink before body-surface gastric mapping. Two 2026 meeting abstracts from the matching Auckland cohort reported feasibility and abnormal postprandial gastric profiles in six participants, including four with a low gastric rhythm index. Every participant received the meal as a gastric test stimulus, and the reported comparison used matched controls with the same test. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN87623941) · [Meeting abstracts](https://doi.org/10.1111/jgh.70203)

## MIND-style diet within a spa rehabilitation program

ISRCTN11301892 was registered May 12, 2026 after completion of a 21-day program in 32 people with mild-to-moderate Parkinson’s. The Slovak program combined rehabilitation, sensory stimulation, medicinal mineral water and a diet based on MIND principles. Registered outcomes include PDQ-39, modified Parkinson Activity Scale and activities of daily living. The diet and its individual foods were delivered as part of the package. No posted results or matching public report were verified. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN11301892)

## Lactiplantibacillus plantarum OL3246 capsule trial

ISRCTN22987788 was registered August 14, 2026 after the study’s recorded 2023 completion. It lists 36 people with Parkinson’s in a blinded placebo-controlled comparison of OL3246 capsules over three months and a PDQ-39 endpoint. The 2026 healthy-aging publication used the same strain, with 30 recruited healthy older adults and Parkinson’s disease as an exclusion. The Parkinson’s trial’s outcomes remain unverified. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN22987788) · [Healthy-aging study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-026-00338-0)
