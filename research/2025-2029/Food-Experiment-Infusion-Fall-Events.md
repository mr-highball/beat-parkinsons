# Fall events reported during Parkinson infusion treatment

## Fall reports during foslevodopa and foscarbidopa infusion

April 15, 2026. Henriksen et al., Advances in Therapy 43(6), 2736–2751. This post hoc analysis describes 244 participants on a 52-week open-label subcutaneous foslevodopa and foscarbidopa infusion study. Table 2 reports at least one fall as a treatment-emergent adverse event for 3/24 people with prior deep brain stimulation and 37/220 without prior stimulation. These are participant-level safety counts by stimulation history, without an event-rate analysis or a food intervention. The parent study is NCT03781167. The report supplies fall-reporting context for ground sage claim 0839.
