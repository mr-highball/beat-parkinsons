## Caffeine exposure and fatigue outcomes

The stroke study compared balance before and after instant coffee in 30 people with stroke and 15 healthy controls. Both groups received the drink. Green tea was named as a source of caffeine and a pretest restriction. The rest period intended to limit fatigue was procedural. The study does not measure a separate fatigue outcome. The dyskinesia article cites coffee and fatigue as separate triggers and evaluates synaptic function. [Instant coffee study](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID25566476-Instant-Coffee-Stroke-Posture-and-Fatigue-Scope.txt) · [Dyskinesia article](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-PMID25730884-Paroxysmal-Dyskinesia-Coffee-Trigger-and-Synaptic-Scope.txt)

The 2005 caffeine crossover reported less mental fatigue in habitual and low-use participants after overnight abstinence, with differences across their dose-specific comparisons. A four-condition 2007 trial compared isolated caffeine and L-theanine, alone and together, using a shared peach-flavored base with tea powder removed. Its separate mental-fatigue and tiredness ratings favored some active conditions. The 2015 trial's condition means suggest smaller fatigue increases after the blend, with an inconclusive treatment term (p=.08) and no clear interaction by habitual use. The 2003 article offers a partly opposing withdrawal interpretation and does not give a separately reported fatigue contrast in the accessible abstract.

The older-adult exercise trials gave different result profiles. After 48 hours of abstinence, the 2005 comparison favored endurance and lower exercise effort and worsened sway. The later eight-hour report gave a cycling-endurance contrast of −10.6% (95% CI −22.7% to 3.3%) and increased sway. NCT01048515 calls the secondary Borg cycling-effort measure fatigue. The publications compare each caffeine condition with placebo. The difference between their abstinence periods was not randomized across studies. The papers do not establish participant independence or a diagnosis-specific fatigue effect.

## Ingredient and motor scope

The resveratrol comparison uses an isolated compound and reports open-field and coordination outcomes in a Parkinson mouse model, with no separate passive muscle rigidity measure. The grape seed-extract rigidity study still carries the outcome-specific lean for claim 0881. The European vitamin D report gives 3.2 micrograms per 100 grams for raw chicken egg and reports group-level dietary sources. It supplies no poached-egg fall rate. The sodium benzoate study measures cerebellar morphology. Its cinnamon and anxiety background does not supply a measured ground-cinnamon anxiety effect.
