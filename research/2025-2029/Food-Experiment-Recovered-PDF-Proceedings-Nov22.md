# Recovered PDF proceedings and the Italian neurology poster gap

The November 22 Round 1 member review resolves 35 of the 47 claim–source pairs previously awaiting review of two recovered PDFs. The combined page inventory covers 832 labeled pages. The three labels absent from text in the CIRSE covers and the label absent from the Italian supplement's opening page were verified from the continuous PDF page labels. The pair decisions preserve member scope. [Audit ledger](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/recovered-pdf-proceedings-nov22/README.md)

- **CIRSE 2019, PMC7103159.** The original review includes the late presentations on S535–S547 after the first author index. Presentation 1101.2 names canola oil, olive oil and lentils as components of the broader Mediterranean diet discussed for vascular prevention. It gives no component-specific Parkinson outcomes. The 24 original pair decisions include eight related contexts and 16 contextual exclusions. [Original member scope](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMC7103159-CIRSE-Collection-Review-Gap.txt)
- **Keys 1995, PMID 7754982.** The original abstract supplies historical dietary pattern context. The publisher identifies pages 1321S–1323S and offers the full body through restricted access. The three pages remain an open citation follow-up. [Original abstract and access](../1995-1999/Source-Text/1995-PMID7754982-Keys-Mediterranean-Diet-Abstract-Scope.txt)
- **Italian Neurology 2020, PMC7683589.** Eleven pairs were resolved at original text depth. The original page S284 reports results for a mixed medical cannabis series. The olive oil serves as a formulation ingredient in one form. Figures 1–3 mentioned in the abstract are missing from its PDF page. The official program identifies pain poster 212 and the congress documents a separate virtual poster area. The twelve still-open olive-oil pairs have their individual claim, preparation context and symptom preserved in the source note. A verified figure or poster copy is needed to assess diagnosis and formulation subgroups. [Source text and twelve exact gaps](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMC7683589-Italian-Neurology-Collection-Review-Gap.txt)
