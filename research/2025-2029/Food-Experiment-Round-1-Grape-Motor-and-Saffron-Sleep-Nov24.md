## Saffron sleep results and adverse events

The cardiovascular review describes both hypnotic activity and possible insomnia. The hypnotic lead traces to a 2009 controlled mouse report. After injection, safranal and the highest aqueous stigma extract level prolonged pentobarbital-induced sleep. The isolated crocin arm had no apparent hypnotic signal in that assay. The clinical safety review traces the insomnia row to a 40-person depression trial that compared petal extract with fluoxetine. It records three events in each arm, with reported p=1.00. The row belongs to the same underlying trial already in the scorecard. The amounts, route, preparation, symptom instrument and population prevent direct application to the ground-saffron claim.
