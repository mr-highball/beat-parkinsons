# Pumpkin seed oil endpoint follow-up, 2025–2029

## Cold pressed pumpkin oil in the colchicine behavioral study

The original indexed article names the open field and elevated plus maze as Table 2 behavior outcomes under cold-pressed oil and colchicine. These are anxiety-related test outcomes. Full behavior tables and exact challenge route still require verification. [Publisher indexed Methods and Table 2 context](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0040816625000722) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2025-Hegab-Cold-Pressed-Pumpkin-Oil-Behavioral-Tests.txt)

## Cucurbita extract and rotarod in a demyelination model

This primary abstract reports rotarod performance using a Cucurbita extract in rats with cuprizone demyelination. The source confirms a motor coordination assay without clinical dexterity, Parkinson disease, or cold-pressed oil. [Primary indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40254703/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2025-Cucurbita-Extract-Cuprizone-Rotarod.txt)

## Topical pumpkin seed oil and oral tissue in aspartame exposed rats

The aspartame rat model added topical pumpkin seed oil and reported tongue histology. Discussion of another salivary-gland study does not establish a salivary outcome in this experiment. [Primary paper, Methods and histology](https://journals.ekb.eg/article_462603_46cb7325d832a1b0eebea26f68e66147.pdf) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2025-Abdelrahman-Topical-Pumpkin-Oil-Aspartame-Tongue.txt)

## Oral pumpkin seed oil and tongue tissue with paclitaxel

The study uses daily oral oil in a rat paclitaxel model, and reports downstream tongue tissue outcomes. It provides no saliva flow, drooling, oil processing or Parkinson result in the accessible primary abstract. [Primary indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41518703/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2026-Kassab-Pumpkin-Oil-Paclitaxel-Tongue.txt)

## Mung bean in pumpkin oil and wire hanging in rats

The 2025 rat study compared mung bean extracts prepared in heated pumpkin oil or soybean oil, a toxin group, water control and rivastigmine. No oil-only arm was assigned. The results report longer wire suspension versus the toxin group, under an Alzheimer-like model. The original supplement labels group values for Figure 9. The experiment does not provide a separate oil effect or a manual dexterity, handwriting or Parkinson result.
