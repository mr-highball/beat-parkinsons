# Peanut Oil Carriers

## Peanut oil comparator in registered bergamot aromatherapy

ReBEC first posted this study on June 19, 2026. The registry reports that data were collected in 2024 and that 120 nursing students were randomized, 60 to bergamot in peanut oil and 60 to peanut oil alone. Participants applied drops to their hands and inhaled the scent. Grade and culinary consumption were unreported. The peanut-only group mean HADS anxiety score went from 11.18 to 8.63 and depression from 6.92 to 5.54. These are within-arm summaries and the registry gives no oil-free comparison. Participant flow gives 88 completers, whereas another adherence summary gives 82 adherent and 32 nonadherent. Analysis denominators remain unresolved. The exact study title and registry search did not identify a separately verified results publication.
