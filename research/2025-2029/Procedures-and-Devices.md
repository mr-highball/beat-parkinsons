# Procedures and Devices

### 2025 — Embryonic stem-cell-derived dopaminergic graft phase 1

In a 12-participant open-label safety study, PET signal consistent with graft survival increased, and some exploratory motor scores improved. There was no randomized control group and participants received immunosuppression. [Primary source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08845-y)

### 2025 — Induced pluripotent stem-cell-derived dopaminergic graft phase 1/2

The Kyoto team’s small uncontrolled study found imaging consistent with graft survival and exploratory motor improvements in some participants, with no graft overgrowth reported during follow-up. Larger controlled trials and longer surveillance are needed. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40240591/)

### 2025 — FDA approval of an adaptive DBS programming option

The FDA approved a specific optional adaptive programming feature for compatible Parkinson’s DBS hardware in February. The regulatory action describes that device and its indications. [Primary source](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpma/pma.cfm?id=P960009S478)

### 2025 — Sham-controlled bemdaneprocel trial registration

The exPDite-2 registration describes a randomized phase 3 comparison of the dopaminergic cell product and sham surgery. The registry records the design and planned outcomes. The earlier phase 1 report was open-label. [Trial registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06944522)
