# Peanut oil in an oral mouse sleep formulation

## Diphenhydramine carrier and sleep stage duration

Copeland and colleagues’ October 2026 report compares a diphenhydramine formulation with its vehicle in 5XFAD Alzheimer model and wild-type mice. Section 2.4 names peanut oil in the oral dough formulation. Diphenhydramine increased time in REM during the dark phase (p = .0365), while the analysis reported sleep states and bout lengths. There is no separate isolated oil comparison or dream enactment analysis. Baseline recordings preceded daily treatment, and normal development during the ensuing month prevents interpreting a simple baseline–vehicle difference as an oil effect. The preprint and article give one study family. [Primary results and dosing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13715419/) · [Early version](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42619761/)
