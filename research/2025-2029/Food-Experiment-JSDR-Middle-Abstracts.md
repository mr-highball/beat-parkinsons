# Final publication of the ground red chili and mochi study

## 2025 Taniguchi manometry results

Wiley first published Taniguchi and colleagues’ article online on January 24, 2025. PubMed confirms that date and lists the June issue, volume 52, pp. 825–832. The sample and chewing results match the 2022 conference abstract and 2024 funder report. The 12 healthy adults ate five plain mochi portions before five portions containing ground red chili. The 2025 results list the chili condition first, whereas the 2024 table lists the plain condition first. All three reports describe the same fixed serving order. This design leaves a possible order effect. The funder report identifies the ground red chili as House Foods *ichimi togarashi*. The cultivar and capsaicin content were unverified. [Wiley publication record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/joor.13935) · [PubMed, PMID 39861968](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39861968/) · [Earlier reports and preparation](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-JSDR-Middle-Abstracts.md#2022-ground-red-chili-in-mochi-and-manometry-in-healthy-adults)

The final article also reports lower upper esophageal sphincter basal pressure with chili (93.6 vs. 114.5 mm Hg, P < .001). The results measure swallowing physiology in healthy adults. Claim `0724` specifies ground cayenne and swallowing difficulty in Parkinson’s, which remain untested in this cohort. [2025 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39861968/)
