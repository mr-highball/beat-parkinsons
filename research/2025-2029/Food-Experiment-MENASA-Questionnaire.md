# MENASA Parkinson database questionnaire

Khalil and colleagues published the protocol on June 2, 2025 (PMID 40671919, PMC12264538, DOI 10.3389/ijph.2025.1608016). It describes an observational Parkinson database across 20 centers in nine countries. The primary article describes planned environmental and motor or nonmotor comparisons. It does not name black tea as a planned predictor of tremor. [Primary protocol](https://www.ssph-journal.org/journals/international-journal-of-public-health/articles/10.3389/ijph.2025.1608016/full) · [Earlier conference record](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-MDS-Diet-Followup.md#earlier-menasa-parkinson-database-development-abstract)

The official supplementary questionnaire distinguishes black tea, black tea with milk and other beverages. Item 32 asks frequency and years of use, with no assigned preparation or measured dose. Item 35 asks whether rest tremor ever occurred since diagnosis. Item 39 includes apathy and pain or muscle cramps among symptoms at onset. The symptom fields describe history or presence. The report supplies no tea-by-tremor analysis, symptom severity estimate or date-fruit variable. [Original questionnaire supplement](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC12264538.1/DataSheet1.docx) · [Local primary protocol text](Source-Text/PMC12264538.txt#L281)

The November 17, 2026 follow-up checked both consortium spellings, CDG-PD and CGD-PD, and the exact protocol identifiers. It found earlier development material and a commentary. Three scoped ClinicalTrials.gov expressions found no matching registration, and the checked routes did not authenticate a later tea–tremor outcome report. The questionnaire provides a specific exposure and symptom trail for a future evidence check. [Database search route](https://clinicaltrials.gov/search?term=MENASA%20Parkinson)
