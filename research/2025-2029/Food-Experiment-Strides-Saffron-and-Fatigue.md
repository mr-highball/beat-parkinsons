# Stride, saffron and fatigue follow-up

## Saffron extract and working memory in childhood ADHD

First published October 19, 2026. Six weeks of oral aqueous dry-stigma extract plus methylphenidate versus methylphenidate alone were compared in children. The 22 and 21 completers had inconclusive group differences on backward Corsi working-memory span (p = .628) and score (p = .906) changes. CPT reaction-time change p = .437 between groups. Abstract and Methods allocation totals differ. The preparation, population, treatment context and endpoints differ from the adult Parkinson global cognitive finding. ## Saffron stigma powder and Parkinson cognitive measures

First published December 24, 2025. In the same Isfahan cohort of 92 randomized adults, adjusted AMT total difference was +0.78 (95% CI 0.16 to 1.40, p = .01) and PDQ-39 cognitive quality-of-life domain difference was −8.07 (95% CI −14.39 to −1.75, p = .01). Intervention used 100 mg daily of milled Sargol stigma powder in a tablet. These are a brief global cognitive screen and a separate patient-rated domain. No executive-function subscore is available. This note verifies outcome details for the same trial already assessed. [Original Tables 4 and 5](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2026/fo/d5fo01924a) · [Local text](Source-Text/2025-Hajhashemy-Saffron-AMT-and-PDQ.txt)

## Marjoram-cinnamon tea and unquantified sleep observations

First published online October 22, 2025. Thirty participants per arm were randomized to multiherb tea or placebo and 21 and 15 completed, respectively. The paper gives a qualitative statement about sleep in people receiving tea. It supplies no insomnia scale or placebo sleep contrast. This is combined tea, outside Parkinson disease. [Original PCOS randomized study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12689000/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2025-Majeed-Marjoram-Cinnamon-Sleep.txt)

## MiniMeal protocol and pending single-leg balance results

Published September 17, 2026. Data collection finished February 27, 2026, with 114 enrolled and 106 completing all six visits. Analysis is ongoing. The protocol lists eyes-open or closed single-leg balance as secondary and compares bioprocessed oat bran/chickpea products with reference products and no intervention. The registry status is older than the study collection report. Neither diagnosis nor preparation matches rolled oats in Parkinson disease. [Original 2026 MiniMeal protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13631491/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2026-Tingo-MiniMeal-Balance-Status.txt)

## Injected crocetinate and rat catalepsy

A 2025 journal report evaluated trans-sodium crocetinate by intraperitoneal injection in a bisphenol-A model. Its Figure 1 reports less catalepsy at 40 mg/kg versus BPA alone (p < .001). This is a single animal stiffness proxy after a water-soluble derivative and injection. It supplies no clinical rigidity after ground saffron.
