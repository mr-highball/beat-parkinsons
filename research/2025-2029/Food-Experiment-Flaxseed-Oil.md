# Flaxseed oil evidence

## Flaxseed powder and insomnia severity in migraine

Jafarpour and colleagues published online December 30, 2025. They randomized 68 people with migraine to flaxseed powder or roasted wheat powder for eight weeks. The intention-to-treat Insomnia Severity Index changed by −4.6 versus −1.6 (p = .029). The paper also gives an improvement in headache severity and no clear changes in headache frequency or duration. The intervention was powdered seed in a migraine cohort. It did not test cold-pressed oil or Parkinson insomnia. The article identifies IRCT20230616058497N1 and reports that data are available on reasonable request. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41478596/) · [Publisher methods and data statement](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022316625008181)

## Mixed omega-3 supplements and cognitive trajectories in ADNI

The April 17, 2026 ADNI analysis matched 273 supplement users to 546 non-users. Pooled self-reported use included fish, flaxseed, and krill oils. Users declined faster on three broad cognitive or functional scales. The MMSE annual group-by-time estimate was −0.266 points (95% CI −0.360 to −0.173). The study supplies no flax-specific or Parkinson executive-domain estimate. ## Registered flaxseed oil comparison with an executive composite

First posted February 6, 2026. NCT07392723 compares flaxseed oil with corn oil for six months in older adults with mild cognitive impairment and an APOE4 allele. Executive function is a registered secondary composite that includes Trail Making, digit span and category fluency. The registry showed recruiting without results and gave no pressing method.
