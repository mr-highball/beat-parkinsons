# Soluble mango leaf extract and POMS depression

## Initial trial with two extract doses

The 2025 crossover studied single doses of 100 or 150 mg of standardized soluble mango leaf extract and placebo in 119 healthy university students. For the POMS Depression subscale, the group main effect was inconclusive (p = .401), with a group-by-time interaction (p < .001). The Results state that pointwise post hoc comparisons between groups at baseline, 30 minutes, three hours and five hours were all inconclusive. This study measured transient mood states and excluded diagnosed major depression. [Local primary report, Section 2.5 and Figure 5](Source-Text/PMC12030201-Zynamite-Initial-POMS.txt#L133) · [Journal publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12030201/)

## Replication with exploratory mood comparisons

In 2026, the authors analyzed 88 healthy university students given 100 mg of soluble mango leaf extract or matched placebo. The POMS Depression subscale had a treatment main effect (p < .001) and inconclusive treatment-by-time interaction (p = .341). Adjusted comparisons favored extract at three hours (−1.39 points, 95% CI −2.47 to −0.32, p = .011) and five hours (−1.44 points, 95% CI −2.51 to −0.36, p = .009). POMS comparisons were secondary and exploratory without multiplicity correction. The studies used a leaf extract at a university in healthy people, without dried fruit consumption or a Parkinson cohort. [Local primary report, Section 2.5 and exploratory analysis](Source-Text/PMC13414551-Zynamite-Replication-POMS.txt#L121) · [Journal publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13414551/)
