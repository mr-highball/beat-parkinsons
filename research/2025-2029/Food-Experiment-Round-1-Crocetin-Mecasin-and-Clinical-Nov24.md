## How the mixture pain evidence changed over time

The [cell study](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID33884029-Mecasin-Turmeric-Component-and-Cellular-Inflammation.txt) includes Curcuma longa as a separate cell condition and Mecasin as a nine-herb mixture. It measures inflammatory markers. A [2018 mouse experiment](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-Ji-Mecasin-Mouse-Neuropathic-Pain.txt) reports less mechanically and thermally evoked pain-related behavior after the formulation. Its relationship to the [cited 2015 thesis](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-Kim-Mecasin-Neuropathic-Pain-Thesis-Access-Gap.txt) remains unresolved.

Two independent randomized clinical trials compared the mixture with placebo alongside riluzole in ALS. The [phase IIa protocol](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMC5899359-Mecasin-ALS-Phase-IIa-Protocol.txt) and [original journal and meeting abstracts](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2023-PMID37257710-Mecasin-ALS-Phase-IIa-Pain.txt) belong to KCT0001984. The meeting report gives p=.916 for secondary pain VAS. The [phase IIb publication](../2025-2029/Source-Text/2026-DOI15229076-Mecasin-ALS-Phase-IIb-Pain.txt) reports five regional pain scores for KCT0003792, all numerically higher with Mecasin and all with 90% confidence intervals crossing zero. The shoulder difference was +1.05 (90% CI −0.25 to 2.35). The [Korean dissertation abstract](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-RISS-T16964903-Mecasin-ALS-Phase-IIb-Abstract.txt) has matching phase, clinical setting and follow-up, but reports different participant totals. Its independence from IIb is unresolved and it adds no pain estimate. The [cross-period observation](Observations/2026-Mecasin-Pain-and-Mixed-Exposure-Boundaries.md) separates those clinical outcomes from the animal and formulation evidence. The frozen culinary turmeric exposure remains untested by either trial.

## Constituents, culturally reported uses and diagnostic tests

The [crocetin review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID33817811-Saffron-Crocetin-Formulations-and-Rigidity-Word-Sense.txt) uses rigidity to describe molecular structure. The [cited Xi and Qian review](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-DOI1934578X0600100112-Xi-Qian-Crocetin-Crocin-and-Saffron.txt) dates the Bencao Gangmu to 1596. Its downstream citation prints 1956. The [cultural record](../2005-2009/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2006-Xi-Qian-Saffron-Compendium-and-Tibetan-Incense.txt) links the historical 番紅花 entry and distinguishes its uses for distress from the modern review's separate Tibetan incense attribution.

The [Iranian mixed-extract original](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID34235220-Bunium-Black-Pepper-Memory-and-Culinary-Cumin-Citation.txt) uses Bunium persicum and black pepper. Its [traditional calming attribution](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2021-Bunium-Black-Pepper-Calming-Use-Persian-Medicine.txt) supplies provenance for the mixture. The [2011 culinary Cuminum cyminum original](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID21639683-Culinary-Cumin-Extract-Stress-and-Memory.txt) concerns urinary stress biomarkers and a memory task, with no separate anxiety measurement. The [clinical FXTAS review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID33990099-FXTAS-Clinical-Review-and-Antioxidant-Diet-Accounts.txt) provides an [indirect report of patient experience](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Personal-Reports/2021-FXTAS-Patient-Reports-Antioxidant-Diet-Turmeric.txt) across diet options with no ingredient-specific symptom effect.

The [olfactory study](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID33853062-Cinnamon-Coffee-and-Clove-Odor-Testing-in-Acute-Illness.txt) includes cinnamon, coffee and clove in a diagnostic test, with a panel-level result and no dietary intervention. The [Parkinson prismatic lenses report](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID34150363-Prismatic-Lenses-and-Parkinson-Back-Pain-Pilot.txt) describes pain improvement in a small uncontrolled series after a visual intervention. Its date expression is not evidence of fruit exposure.
