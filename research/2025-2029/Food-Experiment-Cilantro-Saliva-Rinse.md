# Coriander oil rinse and salivary microbiome

## Seed essential oil rinse in periodontal disease

[Kazarina et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13633974/) published October 3, 2026 (PMID 42828686; DOI 10.1007/s00784-026-07198-8). In a randomized three-arm study in adults with periodontitis, participants rinsed for 30 seconds twice daily for 14 days with 20 mL of water, chlorhexidine 0.12%, or a 4 mg/mL preparation of steam-distilled *Coriandrum sativum* **seed** essential oil. Paired salivary microbiome data were analyzed from nine, ten and eight participants, respectively. The seed-oil versus water species-level microbiome change was inconclusive (q=0.413); no salivary flow rate or Parkinson drooling severity was measured. Oral rinsing with seed oil is not consumption of dried cilantro leaves. The ISRCTN79156900 registry was retrospective. [Factual extract](Source-Text/2026-Kazarina-Coriander-Seed-Rinse-Extract.txt).
