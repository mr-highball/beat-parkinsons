## Saffron, crocin and active drug controls

The fibromyalgia trial tested an ethanolic stigma extract against duloxetine. Its eight-week Hamilton depression change difference was +0.16 points (95% CI −2.39 to +2.71) and the Global Fatigue Index difference was +0.68 (−6.52 to +7.87). The registry lists fatigue among its primary outcomes, and the manuscript's primary-outcome description names a different set. The burning-mouth trial compared isolated crocin with citalopram and reported similar week-11 scores with conflicting citalopram baseline values across tables. Both trials leave an untreated contrast and transfer to culinary saffron in Parkinson disease unresolved. [Fibromyalgia original and outcome tables](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID30456199-Shakiba-Saffron-Duloxetine-Fibromyalgia.txt) · [Fibromyalgia registration](../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/IRCT201604261556N91.txt) · [Burning-mouth original and table discrepancy](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMID31763210-Pakfetrat-Crocin-Citalopram-Burning-Mouth.txt)

The new and previously published families were submitted together through the Oracle rubric. Claims 0063 and 0485 each have eight source families, with three new related assessments across the two claims. Both frozen wording directions predict worsening. The provisional easing signals for Parkinson depression and fatigue remain anchored to the earlier placebo-controlled comparison with ground Sargol stigma in a study tablet. Ordinary meal quantities and independent matched replication are open. The two active-comparator papers provide no separately measured insomnia severity or fall events.
