# Registered cognitive outcomes for a black pepper extract blend

## Registered pepper extract blend and numeric working memory

The registry plans numeric working-memory and cognitive-failure outcomes in adults with mild cognitive impairment and insulin resistance. The active product combines two botanical extracts. The two capsule counts in the registry disagree, and no results were posted in the retrieved record. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07220694) · [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT07220694.txt)
