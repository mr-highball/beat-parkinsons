# Pomegranate product labels and hallucination coding in FDA reports

## FDA pomegranate product report audit

The October 28, 2026 openFDA drug snapshot returned three report keys for pomegranate product terms and a hallucination term. All three list pomegranate as a concomitant product and use a generic hallucination term. Two put pomegranate among identical sets of 32 reported product names and have identical sets of reaction terms. These records are possible duplicate patients or submissions. The other record lists an oral pomegranate product among 20 co-reported products, with preparation unverified. The separate visual-specific drug and food queries returned provider no-match responses. Such results describe the selected terms and dataset snapshot. The report structure supplies no verified frequency, fruit form, Parkinson diagnosis or ingredient-specific attribution.
