# Vitamin D3 motor and mood outcome reporting in Parkinson disease

## Vitamin D3 trial composite motor results and missing item estimates

Li and colleagues published July 11, 2025 from the registered cohort first posted as a December 2024 preprint. Of 51 people with Parkinson disease in the observational component, 30 with vitamin D deficiency were randomized, 15 per group. Table 3 reports UPDRS-III changes of −4.27 (SD 3.77) with isolated vitamin D3 and +0.40 (SD 1.84) with vegetable-oil placebo (p = .001). The publisher supplement gives the placebo UPDRS-III as 34.00 at baseline and 34.40 at follow-up, consistent with the reported change. Table 2 prints a minus sign on the baseline value. The Berg Balance comparison is inconclusive (p = .102). There is no separate bradykinesia change estimate. Anxiety and depression ratings appear in baseline correlations, with no reported arm-level treatment estimates for the registry-listed scales. No fish exposure was identified in either arm.
