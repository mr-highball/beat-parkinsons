## Comparing a symptom with an experimental proxy

The curcumin pain-model experiment measured nociceptive behavior and separately checked locomotion and motor coordination. The injected curcumin arm had less later-phase formalin licking and less visceral writhing in separate experiments. The earlier formalin phase was inconclusive. The rotarod control in healthy mice, sometimes described as time to fall, gave t=.2988, df=8 and author-reported p>.05. This measures experimental motor impairment, while the frozen falls claim concerns events in people with Parkinson disease. Ground turmeric and repeated clinical fall frequency remain open. The pain scorecard now represents four separate rodent families rather than one grouped family. The perinatal cardamom study found altered development and differing results across maze tasks in offspring. The isolated crocin aging experiment instead used the water maze for spatial learning. Its references point to separate trials that measured immobility in forced-swim or tail-suspension assays. The 2014 purified-crocin comparison found reduced immobility without consistent dose ordering. The 2009 report distinguishes corm fractions and aqueous stigma extract. Their role is contextual for the frozen saffron claim, whose direction is based on a direct human comparison. ## Actual exposures in constipation and fatigue reports

The modified Buzhong-Yiqi-Tang review covers 25 trials with 2,089 participants and lists dried mature citrus peel under Chenpi in the mixture. The named preparations can include Citrus reticulata and C. sinensis. The study set evaluated the entire formula or modified variants. The review identified no double-blind placebo-controlled trial and describes low methodological quality. This expands the evidence for combination preparations and leaves a raw-orange component estimate open. A separate synucleinopathy review lists extra-virgin olive oil among customary constipation advice and flags the lack of trials at publication. Its controlled food result concerns a probiotic fermented milk preparation. [Citrus mixture review](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID29552087-Citrus-Peel-in-Mixed-Herbal-Constipation-Trials.txt) · [Olive oil advice](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID29508455-Synucleinopathy-Autonomic-Care-and-Olive-Oil-Advice.txt)

The British Museum's 2022 account attributes greater wakefulness during Yemeni Sufi dhikr to brewed coffee. The passage treats the origin event as reported tradition. The 2018 coffee and cacao review describes a related account. The qualifying evidence describes wakefulness during devotion without a direct fatigue-severity contrast. A separate qualitative osteoarthritis interview context mentions coffee alongside medication and exercise. The caffeine review distinguishes withdrawal fatigue from the direction during continued consumption. No new fatigue efficacy weight was assigned.
