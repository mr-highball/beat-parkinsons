# Terpene and lovage primary sources, 2025-2029

## Inhaled isolated beta-myrcene in a mouse insomnia model

Xu and colleagues report that inhaled isolated beta-myrcene shortened latency and prolonged duration in a barbiturate-induced sleep assay in a mouse insomnia model. The study also administered the compound by injection as a pharmacodynamic control. The full methods, numerical effect and route-control contrasts are unresolved. The use of an isolated inhaled compound limits relevance to minced lemongrass or raw fruit and the outcome does not test Parkinson insomnia.
