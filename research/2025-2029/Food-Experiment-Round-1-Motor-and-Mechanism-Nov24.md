## Whole dates, syrup and reported practice

A 2014 Spanish synthesis documents *Phoenix dactylifera* fruit as a traditional analgesic and in postpartum care. The passage leaves the fruit's drying state and patient outcomes unresolved. References to date-palm spathes and Canary Island palm sap concern other materials. The prior chronological period includes a whole dried-date mouse experiment with an inconclusive between-diet result. [Spanish ethnobotanical record](../2010-2014/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2014-Rivera-Date-Fruit-and-Pain-Spain.txt) · [Earlier mouse comparison](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#date-fig-and-olive-diets-in-a-mouse-pain-threshold-study)

A late-pregnancy whole-date cohort reported labor progression without a standalone pain measure. The Tabuk fresh Rotana Rutab trial reported pooled pain ratings from 76 of 89 participants and supplied no comparison by arm. The 2024 review traced its two independent favorable labor-pain comparisons to date syrup. Fathi and Taavoni report overlapping patients. The review could not combine the available pain measures statistically. [Whole date fruit](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID21280989-Whole-Date-Fruit-and-Labor-Outcomes.txt) · [Fresh fruit and pooled pain](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMC6695006-Fresh-Date-Fruit-and-Pooled-Labor-Pain.txt) · [Syrup and cohort review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-PMC10759543-Date-Syrup-Labor-Pain-Lineage.txt)

The strongest currently indexed comparisons for dried fruit itself are the similar abdominal-pain results in the small Ajwa crossover, the inconclusive pediatric between-group pain measure, and the inconclusive mouse comparison. The date-seed trial concerns seed powder and post-exercise soreness. Public accounts describe both relief and discomfort. The combined patterns leave the frozen Parkinson pain claim open. [Whole Ajwa crossover](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-Eid-Whole-Ajwa-Mood-Diary.txt) · [Date pain follow-up](Food-Experiment-Date-Pain-Followup.md) · [Claim 0502](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0502.txt)

## Coffee, sweet potato and salmon

The chlorogenic acid review links separate plant materials and clinical preparations. Its sweet potato citation includes roots, stems and leaves, with no boiled-root fatigue comparison. Its coffee citation resolves to one four-condition soluble-coffee crossover in 39 healthy older adults. Caffeinated soluble coffee favored less acute mental fatigue, and the enriched-versus-regular decaf contrast was inconclusive. A different coffeeberry extract increased acute task-related mental fatigue at one dose and timepoint. The primary 120-minute comparison, higher dose and separate fatigue scale were inconclusive. [Sweet potato chemistry](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2008-DOI200705053-Sweet-Potato-Root-and-Leaf-Phenolics.txt) · [Soluble coffee trial](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID21773723-Soluble-Coffee-and-Mental-Fatigue.txt) · [Coffeeberry trial](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2023-PMID37299382-Coffeeberry-and-Mental-Fatigue.txt)

The receptor review traces melanin-concentrating hormone to isolated chum salmon pituitaries and spans animal receptor and behavior models. The original discovery concerns pigment activity. Both sources supply mechanistic context for the claim packet. The existing favorable anxiety signal comes from a separate non-Parkinson controlled food comparison that pooled grilled and boiled Atlantic salmon. [Peptide isolation](../1980-1984/Source-Text/1983-PMID6621686-Chum-Salmon-Pituitary-Peptide.txt) · [Receptor review](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID27152164-Melanin-Concentrating-Hormone-Receptors.txt) · [State and trait anxiety trial](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Salmon-Anxiety.md#atlantic-salmon-and-state-versus-trait-anxiety-in-forensic-inpatients)
