## Cultural leads

- The Gönen and Kırklareli abstracts confirm the origin of two traditional-use sources cited by the 2016 review. Kırklareli identifies lemon balm among frequently reported plants, while the checked abstract leaves that plant's individual indication unresolved. [Gönen record](../2000-2004/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2001-PMID11395255-Gonen-Ethnobotany-Abstract.txt) · [Kırklareli record](../2005-2009/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2006-PMID17257791-Kirklareli-Lemon-Balm-Survey-Abstract.txt)
- The Edremit table lists lemon balm flowering-branch infusion for insomnia and stomach ache. The Elazığ table describes sweet basil for stomach ache and a lemon balm preparation as a sedative. These are documented uses, with no measured Parkinson symptom results. The two surveys preserve different preparations, and distinguish sweet basil, spearmint and other mints as separate taxa.
