# Round 1 pain, mobility and source lineage

The November 24 review resolved 41 claim–source checks across 30 complete publications. Thirty-two scopes exclude an unrelated exposure or result. Nine retain an ingredient, constituent, product or test material with a separate clinical scope. Seven additional source records resolve original citation routes. The exact packet gives the local source for every check. [Exact review and source references](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/priority-five-pain-and-mobility-nov24/README.md)

## Labor pain and attribution

The 2019 Persian date-syrup original reports 64 participants and an inconclusive 30-minute pain contrast. At 60 minutes, the mean VAS values were 7.59 for date syrup and 9.12 for water control (p=.001). The 80-person English date report gives corresponding means of 7.77 and 9.05 (p=.001). The originals share IRCT201405182172N16. The English date and honey reports also reproduce five of six control means and standard deviations exactly. One control row differs and actual participant-level overlap remains unresolved. Keep these publications together as one study family. [Persian date-syrup study](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-DOI3111618-Fathi-Date-Syrup-and-Labor-Pain.txt) · [English date-syrup study](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-69207-Taavoni-Date-Syrup-and-Labor-Pain.txt) · [Honey and control data](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMID30858948-Honey-Syrup-and-Shared-Labor-Trial.txt)

At Gonabad, the 175-person food-access comparison reports mean late labor pain of 4.8 versus 6.2 at 6–7 cm dilation and 7.0 versus 9.0 in transition, both p=.0001. Access was to dates, orange juice and water as a group. The fasting control and pooled exposures leave the separate contribution of dates unresolved. A report with overlapping authors and setting evaluated labor progression and analgesic use across a different set of allowed foods. Participant linkage remains open. [Original pain tables](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-ZJRMS93776-Food-Choices-and-Labor-Pain.txt) · [Related labor-progression comparison](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMID23304677-Rahmani-Carbohydrate-Choices-and-Labor.txt)

The Alicante repository copy supplies a report that people in Matola used a preparation of dates, figs and honey for throat pain. This supplements the customary analgesic account and retains uncertain route and ingredient attribution. The standard dried-date Parkinson pain comparison remains open. [Traditional-practice record](../2010-2014/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2014-Rivera-Date-Fruit-and-Pain-Spain.txt) · [Dried-date pain scorecard](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/priority-five-pain-and-mobility-nov24/0502-scorecard-check.json)

## Separating ingredients from test materials and outcomes

The carotenoid capsule papers use corn oil in both active and placebo capsules and measure monocyte markers. A separate capsule citation points to hydroalcoholic saffron stigma extract compared with fluoxetine. The small sample and inconclusive between-arm comparison do not establish equivalence. The direct Parkinson ground-stigma trial remains the basis for the saffron direction. [Corn-oil carrier](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2000-PMID10944479-Corn-Oil-Carrier-and-Monocytes.txt) · [Saffron extract trial](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID15707766-Saffron-Stigma-Fluoxetine.txt) · [Saffron scorecard](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/priority-five-pain-and-mobility-nov24/0063-scorecard-check.json)

Egg yolk served as a shared matrix for the plant-extract assay. A fly climbing comparison administered whole-grape and Japanese knotweed extracts together. The isolated EGCG trial tested energy use in multiple sclerosis. These findings belong to their measured endpoints. The claim verdicts still reflect the prior strongest symptom-specific evidence. [Egg-yolk assay](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27409335-Sida-Extracts-and-Egg-Yolk-Lipid-Peroxidation.txt) · [Grape and knotweed study](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID19929256-Whole-Grape-Knotweed-and-Climbing.txt) · [EGCG exercise study](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-PMID25733633-EGCG-and-MS-Exercise-Metabolism.txt)

Olive oil appears in the red-wine paper as part of a Mediterranean dietary-pattern description. Its cited Parkinson dietary studies estimate adherence or disease risk and carry no measured oil-specific bradykinesia or postural-instability result. In the kinase review, caffeic and chlorogenic acids, curcumin, rosmarinic acid and EGCG provide biochemical context with separate constituent and endpoint scopes.
