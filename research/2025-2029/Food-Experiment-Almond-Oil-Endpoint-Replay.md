# Almond oil preparation and endpoint search

## Later randomized blepharospasm protocol uses coconut oil comparator

The protocol published December 1, 2025 cites the two earlier frankincense cases and identifies ChiCTR2400091987. Both groups are to receive botulinum toxin, with topical frankincense essential oil compared with coconut oil. It plans duration of symptomatic improvement and predefined focal-dystonia outcomes. The almond-oil possibility in the 2023 case report is absent from the prospective design. The protocol gives no result for any almond oil. [Primary trial protocol, intervention and planned outcomes](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12703971/) · [Licensed local full text](Source-Text/PMC12703971.txt)

## Randomized topical almond oil trial measured skin appearance

Khan and colleagues published the NCT06571721 comparison on November 9, 2026. The full Methods and Results report 90 randomized, 30 per arm, and 79 completers. The completer counts were 28 for topical almond oil, 26 for almond oil with 0.5% vitamin E and 25 for 0.025% tretinoin in castor oil. The abstract conflates completers and assignment. At week 16, wrinkle severity decreased within each arm, with no reported between-arm difference. The almond-only arm had a 4% melanin index increase reported at week 16. Imaging measures facial surface appearance. There was no oral oil intervention, movement endpoint or Parkinson assessment.
