# European dietary and botanical registrations and outcomes, 2025–2029

## Resistant starch and high-fiber vegetarian meals

DRKS00037268 was registered June 24, 2025. The paper by Petrov and colleagues appeared online December 11, 2025 and names the registry ID. In the two-week open-label randomized phase, 26 participants received a conventional diet, 25 received the same diet with the SymbioIntest supplement, and 23 received high-fiber vegetarian meals. The formulation contains type 3 resistant starch and other ingredients including glucomannan and isomaltulose. Ten people from the supplement arm entered an extension. The paper reports lower MDS-UPDRS, nonmotor symptom and PDQ-39 scores during that extension, which followed only the supplement arm. The comparison supports further study of the formulation and gives no estimate for a single culinary ingredient. The registry results field remains empty and its supplement dose lists milligrams where the paper reports grams. [Primary registration](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00037268/details) · [Full primary report](https://orbilu.uni.lu/handle/10993/67302) · [Publication date and abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41389850/)

The publisher’s supplementary workbook gives adjusted probabilities for the extension models. The before-and-after model reports a coefficient of −7.65 for total motor MDS-UPDRS Part III (adjusted p = .000021) and −20.79 for total NMSS (adjusted p = .0000000037). Only Part III has a clear linear time trend after adjustment (adjusted p = .0011). The workbook labels Table S17 as a supplement-versus-control comparison, while the Methods and Limitations describe longitudinal assessments in the supplement arm only. These coefficients cannot establish a concurrent controlled effect. The workbook reports scale totals and provides no estimates for individual foods or individual symptoms. The manufacturer’s contemporaneous label confirms 5 g of resistant starch per sachet and does not identify its botanical source. ## Short-chain fatty acid and prebiotic outcomes

Hegelmaier and colleagues reported that motor examination scores decreased by at least five MDS-UPDRS Part III points in all three supplement arms over six months. Olfaction improved in the 2′-fucosyllactose and combination arms. The later constipation analysis reported the strongest constipation-score improvement in the combination arm. The two reports identify DRKS00027061 and the same randomized 72-person cohort. Every arm received an active supplement, which limits attribution of within-group change. ## Mediterranean diet advice in a multidomain lifestyle program

DRKS00037933 was registered September 17, 2025. Prevention-in-PD is a single-arm feasibility study in early-to-moderate Parkinson’s and isolated REM sleep behavior disorder. It combines Mediterranean diet advice, exercise, sleep advice and cognitive training. The primary endpoint is adherence, with exploratory motor and nonmotor measures. The study was recruiting in the available record. [Primary registration](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00037933/details)
