# Chocolate Anxiety

## Dark chocolate and measured anxiety after thyroid biopsy

Yaşar randomized 99 patients scheduled for thyroid fine-needle biopsy. Fifty-two received about 20 g commercial 90% dark chocolate and 47 received no snack. The STAI mean changed from 39.79 to 34.31 with chocolate and from 38.60 to 38.89 with control. The article reports a group-by-time result p = .01 and a postbiopsy Cohen d of .48. The bar included vanilla flavoring. Patients and the radiologist knew group assignment, and the paper leaves the assessor masking unclear. This result concerns acute procedural anxiety outside Parkinson disease. ## Registered dark chocolate trial for perceived stress and quality of life

On August 14, 2026 the registry listed a prospective University of Crete study of 70% dark chocolate versus no cocoa product in 100 adults undergoing routine laboratory tests. Follow-up is planned for 16 weeks and includes cortisol, perceived stress and quality of life. It excludes major anxiety and depressive disorders and does not specify a Parkinson cohort or an anxiety-severity outcome. It was listed as not yet recruiting and did not have published results in the checked record.
