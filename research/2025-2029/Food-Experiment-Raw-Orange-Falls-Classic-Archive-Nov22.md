The complete bioRxiv source concerns SOD1 in postmortem Parkinson brain tissue and a murine motor model. Its recorded latency to fall is a laboratory measure. The complete medRxiv source studies adaptive stimulation and compares observations such as freezing during walking and turning. Orange appears in figures of both articles. Their outcomes do not establish an effect of eating raw orange on fall frequency in people with Parkinson disease.
