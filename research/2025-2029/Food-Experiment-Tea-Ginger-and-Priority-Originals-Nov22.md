# Brewed green tea, grated ginger and Round 1 original-source gaps

## Brewed green tea and fatigue

Claim 0948 retains a tentative worsening direction at anchor 51. The closest comparison is the 14-person three-month Parkinson observation. Fatigue Severity Scale means rose from 28.4 to 32.1. The corresponding dissertation reports medians of 27 and 30.5 for the same cohort. The study had a single group, and the reviewed material gives no paired variance or exact fatigue p value. [Original and dissertation lineage](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#green-tea-and-fatigue-in-parkinsons-disease)

The 61-person Parkinson trial found that isolated caffeine versus placebo had a six-week estimate of −2.85 Fatigue Severity Scale points (95% credible interval −7.73 to 2.06). The trial assigned capsules and studied participants who had daytime somnolence. Shimbo's 380-person community survey found weak inverse correlations between habitual brewed-tea intake and perceived fatigue, without an adjusted fatigue comparison or Parkinson stratum. A caregiver also reported lower fatigue during a brewed-tea and fruit mixture, more walking and simultaneous dietary changes. Eleven independent or uncertain source families preserve these distinctions. ## Grated ginger and anxiety

Claim 0937 retains Unknown at anchor 50. The registered Parkinson trial compares two strengths of a ginger-root extract and currently has no posted results or separate anxious-mood endpoint. The original Simon abstract describes an artificial membrane assay for seven isolated constituents. The complete paper and assay tables remain restricted. In a separate mouse experiment, Nieoczym and colleagues measured isolated 6-gingerol in brain and serum after injection. This provides a tissue exposure comparison without an anxiety endpoint. [Registry replay](Source-Text/Trials/NCT07882108.txt) · [Simon original access](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMID31476432-Simon-Ginger-Permeability-Abstract.txt) · [Nieoczym original results](Source-Text/2026-PMID42110551-Nieoczym-6-Gingerol-Brain-Exposure.txt)

A public contributor recalled feeling calmer after fresh ginger described as either a honey-containing tea or chewed ginger. The preparation, timing, concurrent treatments and diagnosis are unresolved. The claim's 34 evidence families retain this report at conversational depth alongside inconclusive clinical extract and powder comparisons. The controlled Parkinson anxiety outcome and an attributable fresh grated-ginger exposure remain open.
