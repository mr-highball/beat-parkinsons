# Round 1 motor and fatigue source review, November 24, 2026

This pass checked the original article bodies for 36 publications in this period, resolving 53 Round 1 source checks. The [experiment review](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/priority-five-motor-and-fatigue-nov24/README.md) preserves pair-level roles and independent validation.

## Findings and limits

The caffeine review describes sources, alertness and variability. The cannabinoid review tabulates caffeine metabolism as a drug-interaction assay. Neither reports a brewed beverage comparison on Parkinson fatigue. The curcumin hypoxia review is secondary evidence from a different disease setting. The original nanoparticle experiment measured movement, exploratory behavior and microbiome changes in a mouse Parkinson model. A culinary turmeric pain comparison remains open. Its reference to orange describes color. The stroke exergame study uses EGG as a group abbreviation and did not test egg consumption.

The [Kagoshima study](Source-Text/2026-PMID42816813-Sakakima-Green-Tea-Nonmotor-Symptoms.txt) studied nighttime sleep and constipation among other outcomes. Its selected MDS-UPDRS Part I items leave the specific fatigue and daytime sleepiness questions unanswered. The [polyphenol trial registry](../2005-2009/Source-Text/Trials/NCT00461942.txt) has no posted results and concerns an extract. The [Jiang fly study](Source-Text/2026-Jiang-EGCG-PINK1-Glycolysis-Abstract-Extract.txt) remains author-abstract only in the catalog. The November 24 exact search confirmed the abstract record. The publisher route returned HTTP 403 and the public repository search located no readable full copy. Full methods and figures remain open. A controlled comparison of brewed green tea on Parkinson fatigue also remains open.

The [2019 wearing-off account](../2015-2019/Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2019-Reddit-Brewed-Green-Tea-and-Medication-Wearing-Off.txt) and [2022 beverage-switch account](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2022-Reddit-Green-Tea-Coffee-and-Muscle-Symptoms.txt) are two public observations from one participant. They document a mixed individual experience with tea, coffee, muscle symptoms and medication timing. Neither measures fatigue.
