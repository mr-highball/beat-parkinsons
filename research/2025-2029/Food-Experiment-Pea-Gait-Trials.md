# Pea Gait Trials

## Pea beverage with resistance training and planned walking speed

First posted 2025-04-27. The protocol plans 150 adults aged 50 and older, assigned chocolate milk, pea beverage or almond beverage with year-long resistance training three times weekly. Participants are to drink 375 mL twice after each session. The secondary measures include 10-meter walking speed and timed up-and-go. The registry lists recruiting and no posted results as captured 2026-11-16. The study assigns beverages and exercise, without a cooked-pea comparison in Parkinson.
