# Curry leaf preparation and measured outcomes

## Ground curry leaf chutney tablets with added ferrous sulfate

A food engineering paper prepared three powder-based chutney tablet formulations including a curry-leaf product. The study added ferrous sulfate. Across formulations the authors report 2.04–3.96 g iron per kg and a cell ferritin-based in vitro availability comparison. Release time, tablet properties and cell assays were measured. No trial combined a curry-leaf tablet with levodopa, and the added pharmaceutical iron makes this a different exposure from ordinary ground leaves. ## Traditional voice indication and exact clinical outcomes

The IMPPAT catalog indexes a traditional dysphonia indication for *Murraya koenigii*, with the plant part unspecified. Its exact citation ISBN 81-204-0828-4 identifies the 1994 Sivarajan and Balachandran book. The 1998 Kerala compilation attributes a voice-related use to a preparation made from the plant. Jayaweera's separate 1982 Ceylon flora specifies boiled leaves for hoarseness. The original 1994 page, clinical voice-volume outcome and Parkinson use remain open.
