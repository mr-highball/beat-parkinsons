# Round 1 sage originals and remaining research gaps

The November 22, 2026 review pinned 68 inherited Round 1 source pairs for five sage claims. It found 48 original article bodies and retained 20 access gaps across 19 source identities. The [experiment ledger](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/sage-originals-nov22/archive-gaps.tsv) links dated text for each held pair. The paired source records distinguish a missing original, a bibliographic identity mismatch and an original assessed at body depth. The larger Round 1 original-source queue contains 40,372 pairs across 25,621 source keys.

The papers read at original depth concern other exposures and outcomes. Sage commonly appears as a publisher, researcher, data organization, company or software name. [ELLDOPA Parkinson fatigue analysis](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2008-PMID18695158-Sage-Scope.txt) measured worsening of Fatigue Severity Scale scores under levodopa and placebo. [EASE LID Parkinson dyskinesia trial](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28604926-Sage-Scope.txt) recorded falls as safety events under an amantadine comparison. Neither supplied a culinary sage comparison.

The [Menosan original and fatigue reporting gap](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-02-10-Dimpfel-Menosan-Sage-Extract-Sleep.txt) supplies a secondary fatigue signal from an ethanolic fresh-tip extract outside Parkinson disease. These outcomes belong to different studies. An unpublished secondary schedule and arm-level reporting remain open. The original review left all five claim grades unchanged.
