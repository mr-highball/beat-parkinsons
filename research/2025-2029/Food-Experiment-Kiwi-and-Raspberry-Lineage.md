# Kiwifruit and raspberry study lineages and outcomes

## KINGS protocol clarifies cultivar and prespecified outcomes

Journal publication January 13, 2026, following a 2025 preprint. The study used two whole peeled green Hayward kiwifruit daily versus maltodextrin in adults with functional constipation or constipation-predominant irritable bowel syndrome. The protocol reports 63 randomized and 57 included in its intention-to-treat sample description. It identifies the outcomes and does not report an attention result. The Ng thesis and registry describe the same trial family. ## State and trait anxiety in the mixed berry and cocoa trial

October 7, 2026 online. Gómez Nieto and colleagues analyze 59 healthy older adults assigned to dried berry mixture, cocoa, or both for 12 weeks. Table 1 gives STAI state means of 16.05 to 13.91 with berry powder, 12.30 to 15.35 with cocoa and 14.60 to 12.78 with the mixture plus cocoa. The within-group p-values were .185, .241 and .502. The authors report a Dunn berries-versus-cocoa change contrast of z=−1.88, p=.031. A conventional two-sided normal comparison for this z is about .060, and the displayed result does not clarify sidedness or adjustment. The berry powder was 16.7% raspberry in the related methods paper. The anxiety analysis did not include an untreated arm, frozen whole fruit or Parkinson participants.
