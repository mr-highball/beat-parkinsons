# Cucumber extract and black cumin oil protocols

## Cucumber extract and registered mood outcomes

UMIN000059721 was initially registered November 10, 2025 and publicly disclosed October 13, 2026. The completed double-blind four-week protocol intended 30 healthy adults on cucumber extract or placebo. POMS-2 was listed along with pain outcomes, with Chalder fatigue secondary. The registry reports unpublished results and gives no apathy scale or raw cucumber exposure. [Local factual text](Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000059721.txt) · [Official UMIN protocol, public disclosure and outcomes](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000068302)

## Black cumin seed oil and registered sleep quality

NCT06950424 first posted April 30, 2025. The registry lists a standardized black cumin oil intervention and the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index within a panel of questionnaires. The protocol concerns mood and stress and reports no posted results. It leaves ground seed and Parkinson insomnia open. [Local factual text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT06950424.txt) · [Official ClinicalTrials.gov protocol, intervention and outcomes](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06950424)
