# Facial expression, falls, voice and related food outcomes

## Medium-chain triglyceride case in GLUT1 deficiency

The original May 2025 LIMPE–DISMOV poster P93 describes one patient with a genetic glucose transport disorder, on a ketogenic diet, whose exercise-induced dystonic episode resolved after oral MCT oil. Instrumented walking speed rose within minutes; joint kinematics were described as normal in both assessment conditions. The case gives no virgin-grade coconut oil comparison and the movement disorder differs from Parkinson levodopa dyskinesia. The October journal report is subscription-only beyond its figures and metadata. ## Olive oil intake and HADS anxiety in a convenience survey

Religa et al. analyzed 115 adults without prior mental-illness diagnosis. Participants reporting at least four tablespoons of olive oil daily had median HADS anxiety 6 versus 8. The adjusted association was −1.86 points (95% CI −3.54 to −0.18, nominal p=.031). The unadjusted comparison had multiplicity-adjusted q=.084. Olive oil as the main cooking fat gave an inconclusive comparison (q=.949). The survey records no pressing grade, lacks a Parkinson-specific sample and establishes no temporal or causal direction. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13713344/) · [Local text](Source-Text/PMC13713344.txt)

## Isolated hydroxytyrosol and anxiety-like behavior in stressed rats

Tantipongpiradet et al. report favorable elevated-plus-maze behavior after isolated biotechnologically produced hydroxytyrosol in an oral jelly. Their stress model used no whole olive oil and no Parkinson cohort. The work concerns a compound found in multiple plants and gives no measured effect of cold-pressed olive oil.
