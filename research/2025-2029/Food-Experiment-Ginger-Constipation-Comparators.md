# Ginger constipation comparators, 2025-2029

## Ginger and Zataria IBS trial and registration

Rostamkhani and colleagues reported a three-arm randomized IBS trial in the December 2025 issue, DOI 10.1016/j.aimed.2025.100584. The abstract describes 66 adults, 22 each receiving ginger, Zataria multiflora or placebo for four weeks. The reported post-treatment difference in total gastrointestinal symptom score for ginger versus placebo was −12.77, p < .001. The accessible material gives no constipation-domain comparison or preparation equivalent to grated culinary ginger. [Original publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2212958825001430)

The paper cites IRCT20240609062057N3. WHO ICTRP dates first registration after the listed enrollment start, which differs from the paper’s description as prospective. The registry target field lists 66, matching the paper, while its narrative lists 96. Its sponsor, enrollment period and design distinguish it from the 2018 two-arm protocol. The accessible records leave individual participant overlap unverified. ## Registered adult ginger and fennel comparison for functional constipation

WHO ICTRP identifies CTRI/2026/05/110493, sponsored by Ingex Botanicals, with target enrollment 90 and a randomized, participant- and investigator-masked comparison. The title names ginger, fennel extract and their combination in adults with functional constipation. The public intervention field is blank, leaving dose and formula unverified. Secondary outcomes include pain, severity and life impact at baseline, day 45 and day 90. No public results appear in the WHO snapshot, refreshed June 1, 2026. Its Not Yet Recruiting status conflicts with the secondary mirror. The current recruitment status and local CTRI ID route could not be independently confirmed. There is no Parkinson-specific cohort or inactive arm identified in the title.
