# Swallowing outcomes and medication adjustment in Parkinson’s

## Colored water during endoscopy before and after levodopa

Okubo and colleagues described an 80-year-old with dysphagia, suspected drug-induced parkinsonism and subsequently diagnosed coexisting Parkinson’s. Simplifying her medicines was followed by no clear initial improvement. Levodopa started on day 23. Repeat endoscopy reported improvement in the swallow-reflex and post-swallow clearance ratings. The exam used colored water as the test stimulus. It measures the course during medication management and gives no food-specific intervention. [Original 2025 report](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/7/2/7_83/_pdf/-char/en)

## Nutritional status and mood in the 2026 program

The Japan Science and Technology Agency indexes a 2026 poster by Tomoya Tsuruta and Sanae Honma about nutritional status and mood in hospitalized Parkinson’s patients. The organizer lists the O-18 session in its public schedule. The meeting viewer requires attendee access for the abstract body. The public title gives no group sizes, mood instrument, dietary items or outcomes. A related earlier report covers nutritional status in an outpatient cohort.
