# Tarragon endpoint follow-up

## Tarragon fragrance enjoyment during anosmia recovery

Bhargava and colleagues described one woman with lifelong anosmia, partial recovery and parosmia, who reported newly enjoying tarragon aroma and taste after intranasal vitamin A and olfactory training together with earlier steroid treatment. The report describes tarragon enjoyment as an outcome and does not assign it as an intervention. Later measured psychophysical scores were consistent with residual hyposmia. This source does not estimate tarragon's effect on smell impairment.
