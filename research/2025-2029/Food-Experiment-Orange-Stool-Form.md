# Orange Stool Form

## Orange frequency and Bristol stool categories in a Lima adult dissertation

Lorena Aracelli Arias Velásquez's dissertation is dated September 1, 2024. The [university repository](https://repositorio.upn.edu.pe/backend/api/core/items/4360d6c4-254b-4533-88ff-8498d0e99bfe) recorded public availability on March 5, 2025, under [handle 11537/40436](https://hdl.handle.net/11537/40436). The cross-sectional sample contained 100 adults aged 20–59. Table 4 compares orange consumption frequency with Bristol stool form categories (Pearson χ²=56.548, 35 df, p=.012). It gives no orange-by-stool category counts or directional estimate. The appendix screenshots do not provide paired rows for this comparison. The frequency questionnaire gives no preparation. Parkinson disease and clinical constipation severity were outside the study design. ## Orange frequency and stool form in a Lima student survey

The publisher dates this article to April 2, 2025. Gómez Rutti and colleagues report a cross-sectional survey of 1,144 health-science students aged 18–42. Participants with diagnosed constipation or diarrhea were excluded. Table 3 gives orange frequency marginals and a stool form association (p=.001), without the paired cells needed to infer an orange-specific direction. Table 5 reports stool form by pooled fruit portions. The first author advised the separate 100-adult thesis. The reported methods, sample sizes and author lists differ, while participant overlap remains unknown. Clinical Parkinson constipation severity and orange preparation were outside this study.
