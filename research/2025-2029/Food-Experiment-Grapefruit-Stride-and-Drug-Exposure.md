# Grapefruit-derived vesicles and stride in tendon injury

## Topical vesicles in a mouse Achilles tendon model

The July 28, 2025 primary report describes grapefruit-derived extracellular vesicles delivered locally by hyaluronic-acid microneedles over an injured Achilles tendon in mice. Figure 7 compares the treated group, blank microneedles, untreated tendon injury and healthy mice. The authors describe stride length after treatment as near the healthy range in the CatWalk analysis. The measured change concerns tendon injury and a local delivery system. The study provides no dietary raw-grapefruit or Parkinson gait outcome. [Original article and Figure 7](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/bioengineering-and-biotechnology/articles/10.3389/fbioe.2025.1615650/full)
