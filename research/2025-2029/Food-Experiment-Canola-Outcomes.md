# Canola and rapeseed oil outcome follow-up

## Secondary muscle imaging from the MCT SMILE gait trial

Mutoh et al. published this analysis in May 2025, using thigh and trunk muscle imaging from the 63 MCT SMILE participants also described in the primary 2022 gait report. The comparator was the matching canola oil formula. The authors reported MCT-associated changes in psoas and vastus medialis FDG uptake and CT density, with no apparent changes in the canola control. This follow-on used the same UMIN000033447 participants and provides proxy imaging outcomes. Count the papers as one study and use the 2022 gait table for walking balance. ## Canola and coconut meal crossover and vascular outcomes

2025-06-19. Kienēs and colleagues randomized the order of four mixed meals, each containing canola or coconut oil at one of two fat amounts. Twenty-nine participants with cardiometabolic risk completed all visits. The paper calls the coconut oil virgin cold-pressed and names Brölio canola without specifying a pressing grade. Resting blood pressure appears as a screening measure. Post-meal tests report carotid-femoral pulse wave velocity and augmentation index. No standing challenge or orthostatic lightheadedness measure was reported. [Primary report, sections 2.1–2.4](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/mnfr.70147)

## Anxiety results in the microalgae capsule trial

Maltz and colleagues published the NCT06425094 trial on March 10, 2025. Figure 6A gives baseline mean GAD-7 scores of 5.3 and 4.7, with no significant between-group or time effect. The trial report describes the microalgae and maltodextrin interventions. The official registry arm description supplies the hydrogenated rapeseed excipient detail. Neither source assigns an oil-only comparison. A low-baseline-anxiety sample and processing differ from the frozen claim. ## Six-month CHILEMED report and unreported anxiety comparison

The original 2026 CHILEMED preliminary report includes 156 participants at six months and reports diet adherence and metabolic outcomes. Its reported comparisons do not supply the planned anxiety scale results. The Mediterranean groups were pooled for the comparison and canola was one optional component. Retain an open route to later psychological outcomes and preserve the original planned measure separately.
