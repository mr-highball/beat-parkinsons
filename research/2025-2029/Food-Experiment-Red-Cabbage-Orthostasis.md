# Red cabbage and orthostatic symptoms

## 2025 Happy Kraut registration and 2026 protocol

The prospective allergy trial compares fermented red cabbage with unfermented red cabbage. General adverse-event monitoring and unspecified vital parameters may supply additional safety detail when results appear. The planned outcomes give no specific standing test or lightheadedness score. No trial results were posted at the last review. [Official registry](Source-Text/Trials/DRKS00036475.txt) · [Primary protocol](Source-Text/2026-Ngoumou-Red-Cabbage-Allergy-Protocol.txt)

## 2025 Separate Brassica smoothie registration

The four-period crossover lists red cabbage among the background examples. Its arm descriptions identify Brassica smoothies by diversity and isothiocyanates without individual vegetables. Gut and related outcomes are planned. [Official registry](Source-Text/Trials/DRKS00037133.txt)

## 2026 Mixed fermented vegetables preprint

One group consumed red cabbage with beetroot and caraway in a fermented mixture. The report contains no orthostatic test and no postural symptom or adverse-event counts. Between-group DASS-21 results were inconclusive. [Primary preprint extract](Source-Text/2026-Bester-Fermented-Foods-Preprint-Extract.txt)

## 2026 Planned microgreen pressure trial

USDA project 1034009 describes a new, planned eight-week trial of 15 g or 30 g of red-cabbage microgreens versus no intervention in 105 adults with above-normal blood pressure. The project start is listed as January 2026. Its plan includes blood pressure and vascular function. It supplies no observed endpoint or enrollment result. This is a distinct design from the completed red cabbage versus beet microgreens crossover. [Official USDA project](https://portal.nifa.usda.gov/enterprise-search/projects/1034009) · [Completed study](Food-Experiment-Romaine-Celery-Stalk-Cabbage.md#2025-red-cabbage-microgreens-and-blood-pressure)
