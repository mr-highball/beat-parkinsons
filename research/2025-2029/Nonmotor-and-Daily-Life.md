# Nonmotor symptoms and daily life

## Drooling, dysphagia, and autonomic symptoms

Okusa and colleagues published a single-center survey of 208 Parkinson's outpatients on June 18, 2026. The study classified 116 as having chronic drooling using the salivation item of MDS-UPDRS Part II. Mean Swallowing Disturbances Questionnaire scores were 7.1 among participants with drooling and 2.7 among the others. Swallowing scores and autonomic symptom scores remained associated with drooling after adjustment for age, sex, disease duration, Hoehn and Yahr stage, and levodopa equivalent daily dose. The autonomic score excluded its drooling item in that model. The cross-sectional study helps characterize symptoms that can co-occur. It did not test an individual food.
