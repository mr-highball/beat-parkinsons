# Allspice and cumin evidence

## Cumin seed infusion and blood pressure in metabolic syndrome

Aslam and colleagues compared a carbohydrate-controlled diet with the same diet and an infusion made from coarsely ground *Cuminum cyminum* seed. Mean systolic blood pressure was 146.34 to 142.00 mm Hg in the comparison group and 144.18 to 136.84 mm Hg in the infusion group, with a reported time-by-group p < .001. The blood pressure comparison concerns metabolic syndrome and a seed infusion. The report did not record standing changes or orthostatic lightheadedness. ## Anaphylaxis after inhaling cumin powder

Pessina and colleagues' 2026 clinical case concerned a 17-year-old with asthma and other allergies who developed anaphylaxis after inhaling cumin during cooking. Prick-by-prick testing with freshly ground dried cumin seed produced a 9 mm wheal. The patient did not undergo a cumin oral challenge. This documents an inhalational reaction and does not estimate swallowing difficulty after toasted cumin in Parkinson's disease.
