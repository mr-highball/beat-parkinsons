# Shiso and perilla follow-up

## Acute leaf extract crossover and bowel movements

Muralidharan and colleagues first posted the results of NCT05603416 on September 3, 2025 and published the matching article on August 3, 2026. In the crossover, 30 healthy adults completed the extract and placebo visits. Over 90 minutes after a large meal, overall gastrointestinal discomfort iAUC was 3073 ± 1255 versus 3742 ± 1611 mm·min (p = .0061). The paper reports no significant difference in bowel movements across visits and does not present those data. The study uses an acute leaf extract and gastrointestinal discomfort outcome outside Parkinson’s. It does not provide an estimate of fresh shiso and constipation severity. [Preprint](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-6886069/v1) · [Published results](https://www.agro-food-industry.com/articles/acute-postprandial-gastrointestinal-symptom-relief-with-a-standardized-perilla-frutescens-leaf-extract/) · [2022 registration](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Shiso-Followup.md#acute-leaf-extract-crossover-registration)

## Culinary shiso and odor pleasantness

The September 7, 2026 report for jRCT1060240004 describes 41 healthy women smelling steam from rice and onion, with or without finely ground shiso leaves. The leaf-free condition always came first. Adding shiso increased median odor pleasantness from 4.1 to 6.1 (p = .029). It did not yield a significant improvement in the measured affect scores. The study assessed odor pleasantness before eating and did not measure clinically impaired smell or its change after consuming dried leaves. [Primary article and Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13550764/) · [Local full text](Source-Text/PMC13550764.txt) · [2024 registration](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Shiso-Followup.md#japanese-registration-for-shiso-as-a-food-odor)

## Perilla and peppermint extract sleep trial

CRIS first posted KCT0012330 on July 24, 2026. It lists 38 planned adults aged 19–64 with poor sleep quality, selected using a Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index score over five. Severe insomnia is excluded. The randomized, double-blind crossover compares a perilla and peppermint extract tablet with control for four weeks per treatment. The primary outcomes are a sleep-health questionnaire and sleep habits assessed by actigraphy, diary and polysomnography. The registry does not name dream enactment as an outcome and provides no numerical results or Parkinson subgroup. [Official CRIS record, sections 9–11](https://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/search/detailSearch.do/34178)

## Korean formula and insomnia and anxiety outcomes

In the 2025 Society for Acupuncture Research congress abstract 113, Kim and colleagues report 83 of 84 participants with major depressive disorder completing a randomized waitlist comparison. The abstract describes improvement in secondary anxiety and sleep outcomes but does not provide separate numerical estimates for those outcomes. The tested Banhahubak-tang formula has multiple ingredients, including perilla, and the report does not estimate the effect of fresh or dried culinary leaves. [Primary congress abstract 113](https://doi.org/10.1177/27683605251386132) · [2022 protocol](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Shiso-Followup.md#korean-perilla-containing-formula-trial-protocol)

## Leaf flavonoids and exercise exhaustion

Liu and Liang’s September 20, 2026 paper reports longer time to exhaustion and higher peak oxygen uptake after a purified perilla leaf flavonoid preparation in 16-year-old male track athletes. The accessible abstract describes extraction, purification and exercise measures but does not give numerical effect sizes or enough trial detail to judge the comparison fully. Exercise endurance in adolescent athletes and Parkinson fatigue severity are different outcomes. [Primary report, pp. 169–174](https://www.ifoodmm.cn/journal/vol42/iss8/21/)

## Purified perillaldehyde and movement in Parkinson models

A 2025 paper reports shorter pole and balance-beam times and better rotarod performance after purified perillaldehyde in 6-OHDA and MPTP mouse experiments. In the 6-OHDA experiment, the lower level did not significantly shorten beam crossing or pole descent. Gao and colleagues’ separate study, first published on August 26, 2026, reports improvement in broad motor and gastrointestinal outcomes after perillaldehyde in rotenone mice. Its publicly accessible abstract does not provide task-level estimates. These are exposures to a purified constituent. The measurements do not establish clinical bradykinesia, rigidity or constipation responses after fresh culinary leaves. [2025 primary report, Figures 6 and 7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11984871/) · [Existing local full text](Source-Text/PMC11984871.txt) · [2026 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42648207/)

## Claim search coverage and unresolved endpoints

Targeted searches joined shiso, perilla leaf, *Perilla frutescens*, *P. frutescens* var. *crispa*, perillaldehyde, 紫蘇, シソ, 紫苏叶, 苏叶, 자소엽, 들깨 and 차즈기 to the frozen symptom names and clinical synonyms. Follow-up used primary journal pages, PubMed and Europe PMC identifiers, the Chinese Food and Machinery publisher, the Korean CRIS exact-accession query, ClinicalTrials.gov and jRCT. Formula searches added 半夏厚朴湯, Banhahubak-tang and Banxia Houpo Tang. [Earlier formula swallowing study](../2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#a-perilla-and-ginger-formula-and-the-swallowing-reflex) · [Korean prescribing study](Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#perilla-and-ginger-formula-use-in-korean-insurance-records)

The closest leads for constipation, bradykinesia, orthostatic lightheadedness, insomnia, fatigue, anxiety, urinary urgency and olfactory impairment have different preparations, populations or outcome measures. Rodent REM duration and the general sleep trial leave dream enactment unresolved. The swallowing and globus reports assess pharyngeal function or throat sensation without a voice-volume measure. The 2005 Hong Kong voice-fatigue dissertation tested Pei Pa Kao and describes pinellia as perilla leaf. The manufacturer lists *Rhizoma Pinelliae Praeparatum* as an ingredient. It gives no verified shiso exposure for that study. The targeted search identified no human result matching fresh or dried shiso and any of the eleven frozen Parkinson symptom comparisons.
