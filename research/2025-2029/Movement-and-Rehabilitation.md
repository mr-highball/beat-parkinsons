# Movement and Rehabilitation

### 2026 — Dual-task telerehabilitation

An eight-week randomized study compared remote cognitive-motor and motor-motor training with control. Both training groups improved on several short-term mobility and balance measures, with no between-group difference in quality of life. Effects on actual falls and longer-term function remain open. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42468927/)
