# Saffron and fatigue in comparator populations

## Jollab beverage and fatigue in multiple sclerosis

Gholami et al. reported on March 17, 2025 a four-week trial of Jollab or placebo in multiple sclerosis. The investigators allocated 70 people and analyzed 66. The beverage macerated saffron in a mixture of rose water, brown sugar, and distilled water. Table 2 reports mean Fatigue Severity Scale scores of 44.36 before and 34.66 after Jollab, and 45.03 before and 39.12 after placebo. The 4.46-point endpoint difference favored Jollab (95% CI 0.26 to 8.64, p = .03). The interval applies to the endpoint comparison. The mixed exposure and diagnosis limit relevance to `0485`. The publisher PDF is CC BY-NC 4.0. [Primary article, Figure 1 and Table 2](https://publish.kne-publishing.com/index.php/tim/article/download/18231/16947)

## Standardized saffron extract and fatigue with subclinical low mood

Amadieu et al.’s October 3, 2025 double-blind randomized Saffromfood trial analyzed 51 adults with subclinical symptoms. Participants took 30 mg/day of standardized Activ’Inside saffron extract or placebo for six weeks. In Table 2, Multidimensional Fatigue Inventory total scores changed from 60.1 to 50.0 with saffron and from 58.5 to 47.5 with placebo (time-by-treatment p = .66). The prespecified combined depression, anxiety, and fatigue score was also inconclusive (p = .95), as was Beck Depression Inventory alone (p = .74). Table 2 reports no interval for the fatigue contrast. This randomized symptom comparison concerns a standardized extract and participants without Parkinson’s. ## Dry saffron extract and fatigue in COPD

Dastan et al. published a double-blind randomized trial on October 29, 2025. The two-month comparison included 71 people with COPD and chronic fatigue. The intervention was isolated dry saffron extract in a capsule, standardized to 2% safranal, and the comparator was a matching placebo. In Table 3, adjusted Manchester COPD Fatigue Scale means changed from 37.19 to 29.93 (endpoint 95% CI 26.37 to 33.50) with extract and from 32.79 to 31.40 (endpoint 95% CI 27.68 to 35.12) with placebo. The paper reports p < .001 for the mixed repeated-measures analysis and gives no interval for the between-group difference in changes. The baseline means differ and the symptom scale is COPD-specific. [Primary report, Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12749208/) · [Local CC BY source text](Source-Text/PMC12749208.txt)

## Dry saffron extract and fatigue in pulmonary sarcoidosis

Moghimi Dehkordi et al.’s June 8, 2026 single-blind trial randomized 76 adults with pulmonary sarcoidosis and poor sleep quality. Seventy-two completed the trial. The intervention was a standardized dry saffron extract capsule, DuraLife, plus sleep-hygiene counseling. The control group received counseling alone. Table 3 reports 4.58 points lower Fatigue Assessment Scale scores (95% CI −5.86 to −3.29, p < .001) with extract at day 60 after baseline adjustment. Sleep quality was the prespecified primary outcome and the fatigue comparison was exploratory. A different diagnosis, isolated extract, and absence of a matched placebo limit relevance to `0485`.
