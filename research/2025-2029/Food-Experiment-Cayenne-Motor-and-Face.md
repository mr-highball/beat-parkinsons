# Cayenne pepper and movement outcomes

## Acute oral spice and postural sway in healthy adults

2025-10-17. Aylward, Korf, Colleti and Haworth reported seven healthy adults aged 18–25 who completed balance testing at baseline and after two oral spice conditions. The abstract gives mean BTrackS sway of 19.57 ± 4.47 cm at baseline, 17.29 ± 4.57 after the lower-heat condition and 16.71 ± 4.72 after the higher-heat condition. It reports omnibus p > .05 and nominal pairwise p = .043 and .035. Limits-of-stability comparisons were inconclusive. The program supplies no pepper identity, ground material verification, sham, counterbalancing or Parkinson population. Practice or order could explain the reported changes.
