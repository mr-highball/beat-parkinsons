# Sixth group registered comparators

## High-oleic safflower comparator and anxiety screening

The protocol compares a synbiotic with a placebo product containing high-oleic safflower oil and includes GAD-7 in its secondary outcomes. The completed record has no posted results. The formulation, indication, comparator and outcome collection leave Parkinson anxiety and oil attribution unresolved. ## Zanthoxylum-containing perioperative formula and bowel recovery

First posted July 23, 2025. Protocol NCT07081802 lists an oral Da Jian Zhong Tang decoction containing Zanthoxylum, ginger, ginseng and maltose as part of a perioperative bundle, plus transcutaneous electrical stimulation, prehabilitation and enhanced recovery. The protocol lists time to first defecation and flatus. The current record has no posted results. It does not isolate toasted fruit or measure chronic Parkinson constipation. [Local factual text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT07081802.txt) · [Official protocol arms and first defecation](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07081802)

## Safflower oil in a placebo capsule with planned stool measures

Posted February 19, 2025. The active intervention is a synbiotic. High-oleic safflower oil appears as one placebo capsule constituent with excipients. The protocol plans daily bowel counts, a Bristol stool diary and gastrointestinal symptom scores. The September 2026 record lists completed status and no results posted. A treatment contrast would compare the synbiotic with a multicomponent comparator, without isolating safflower oil or Parkinson constipation. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06834984) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2025-NCT06834984-Safflower-Placebo-Stool-Measures.txt)
