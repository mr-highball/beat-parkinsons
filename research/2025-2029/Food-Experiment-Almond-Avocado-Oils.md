# Almond and avocado oil studies, 2025–2029

## Almond oil vehicle in a rat model of parkinsonism

De Campos’s UEL dissertation is dated June 24, 2024 and the repository records first public availability August 28, 2026. It describes oral almond oil given as the vehicle both alone and with estradiol in ovariectomized rats with either a 6-OHDA lesion or sham surgery. The October 2, 2026 journal article matches the lead investigator, regimen and outcomes. Basal pressure, heart rate and baroreflex measures did not clearly differ among groups. All four groups were exposed to the almond oil carrier. No postural lightheadedness, oil-free comparison or cold-pressed product is reported. [Repository metadata](https://repositorio.uel.br/items/c9054c06-e7b2-49d7-94ab-79a1f6901866/full) · [Dissertation, hormonal treatment and Figures 6–7](https://repositorio.uel.br/bitstreams/fd4efa6f-ef92-403b-be6f-25a679f24fbe/download#page=30) · [Primary article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42826823/)

## Avocado oil and anxiety-like behavior after maternal exposure

De Melo and colleagues’ February 2025 report describes shorter open-arm time for dams and offspring in both the oil and pulp groups (reported p < .001). Adult offspring in the oil group moved less, while offspring in the pulp group moved more. Lower locomotion can complicate open-field interpretation. The abstract does not establish that the oil was refined. The 2019 article and thesis from the same lead investigator report developmental and memory endpoints. Public methods are insufficient to verify whether their animals overlap with the anxiety report. The animal studies give no clinical anxiety or walking-speed result for Parkinson’s. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40024360/) · [Earlier research](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Almond-Avocado-Oils.md#maternal-avocado-oil-and-offspring-memory)

## Avocado oil diet and rotational behavior

González-Guevara and colleagues’ July 2025 article reports fewer ipsilateral turns after an avocado oil diet in rats with quinolinic-acid lesions. The 2022 poster from the same group gives an approximate 89% reduction. Both describe the same model, diet duration and principal motor readout. The journal identifies this as a Huntington’s disease model. The outcome measures rotations after unilateral injury, with no separate walking-speed comparison or verified cold-pressed specification in the accessible abstract. [Primary journal report](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11064-025-04496-7) · [Earlier report](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Almond-Avocado-Oils.md#avocado-oil-and-rat-turning-in-a-2022-conference-report)

## Extra-virgin avocado oil in a second memory model

The April 2025 SONA proceedings describe a separate one-week aluminum-plus-D-galactose model in ovariectomized rats from the same investigators as the earlier 70-day model. The abstract lists novel-object, Y-maze and water-maze tests, but its results report hippocampal biomarkers without numerical behavioral outcomes. The same-titled journal supplement DOI is indexed. Public copies checked leave task-level results open. [SONA proceedings, printed pp. 257–258](https://sona2025.uca.ma/VERSION%20FINALE%20DU%20PROCEEDINGS%20SONA_final.pdf) · [Journal supplement DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroscience.2025.05.271)
