# Clinical comparators and food identification

Fourteen original reports document reviewed comparisons behind the food experiment's indexed hits. The Parkinson and atypical-parkinsonism reports identify motor and nonmotor measurements that can guide later source screening. The other clinical records distinguish symptoms observed in dementia, Lyme disease or residential care from the frozen Parkinson outcomes.

## Food mentions and endpoint scope

- In the Lyme case series, Mango identifies a flavor of a formulated powder in a combined regimen. The case record gives no verified whole fresh or dried fruit administration or lot-specific formulation. [Lyme report](Source-Text/PMC12109650.txt).
- The levodopa-response study uses shrimp alkaline phosphatase in genotyping. It discusses constipation as a potential influence on pharmacokinetics. The reagent did not constitute a patient dietary exposure. [Pharmacogenetic comparison](Source-Text/PMC12264220.txt).
- The safinamide analysis gives broader domains that combine mood with apathy and put pain alongside other miscellaneous items. The separate amyloid cohort uses the Starkstein scale to measure apathy directly. [Safinamide domains](Source-Text/PMC12293413.txt) · [Apathy cohort](Source-Text/PMC13347539.txt).
- The REPOSE article reports exploratory clinical outcomes for the previously filed 2023 registration. Shared rehabilitation and a sit-to-stand measure give context for fall risk. Neither specifies nutritional exposure or a food-assigned fall-frequency contrast. [REPOSE original](Source-Text/PMC13735452.txt) · [Earlier registry record](../2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/NCT06141317.txt).
