Text coverage: Original PMC XML body and embedded tables with separate Supplementary Table 2. Factual extract.

An effort-choice comparison analyzed 21 participants with Parkinson disease and 20 healthy controls. Apathy Evaluation Scale scores were higher in the Parkinson group (p=0.006). The measured outcome was a force-effort choice. Apathy was measured as a participant characteristic. Supplementary Table 2 reports an effort ratio by apathy interaction for choice behavior in the off-medication model (p=0.003). Its separate model lists the effort ratio by rigidity term at p=0.054. The task measures choices under different effort conditions. The task and medication-state comparison did not evaluate sage or a dietary effect on symptom severity.
