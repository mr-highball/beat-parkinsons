All within-pair comparisons of continuous and FFQ variables in this manuscript use the paired Student’s t-test, applied
uniformly at T0 and T1. To demonstrate that this methodological choice does not drive any conclusion, every such
comparison was repeated with the non-parametric Wilcoxon signed-rank test. As shown in Supplementary Table S2, the
significance status is identical under the two tests for all comparisons except two cells, both at the boundary and neither

2
affecting a reported result: added sugars at T0 (t-test p=0.050 vs Wilcoxon p=0.047, i.e. borderline either way) and weight
at T0 in the MZ subgroup (t-test 0.069 vs Wilcoxon 0.032 — a comparison that is not a study finding, since nutritional status
did not differ within pairs). This confirms the robustness of the analysis to the parametric/non-parametric choice and supports
the use of a single uniform test throughout.
