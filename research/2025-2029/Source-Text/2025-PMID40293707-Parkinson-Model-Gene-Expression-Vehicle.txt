Title: L-DOPA Induces Spatially Discrete Changes in Gene Expression in the Forebrain of Mice with a Progressive Loss of Dopaminergic Neurons
Publication date: 2025-04-28 (online first)
PMID: 40293707
PMCID: PMC12367957
DOI: 10.1007/s12035-025-04957-8

Text coverage: Distilled primary full text, methods and results boundaries.

The male progressive dopaminergic-loss model was induced with tamoxifen delivered in sunflower oil before L-DOPA or vehicle treatment. The observed comparisons concern drug-related patterns of forebrain gene expression and genotype differences. The oil's grade is unspecified and this carrier is neither an assigned oral dietary exposure nor an independently estimated treatment. The model family relates to the earlier behavioral protocol, but this report has a different schedule and tissue endpoints. It supplies no separate executive, mood, anxiety or rigidity response to sunflower oil.
