Prospective Gaucher disease cohort with repeated clinical, cognitive and sensory evaluation after a median of 37 months. The Non-Motor Symptoms Scale includes a mood/apathy domain and miscellaneous symptoms such as pain. The source presents domain and selected item results, with complete values referred to Supplementary Table S1. Neither outcome reflects a dietary contrast. Gaucher disease and the individual clinical findings should be kept distinct from a diagnosed Parkinson food cohort.
