Title: The Effects of Geranylgeraniol Supplementation on Testosterone in Middle-Aged Healthy Adults: An 18-Week, Single-Group Crossover Placebo-Controlled Trial.
PMID: 42222203
PMCID: PMC13218815
First published: 2026
Authors: Jurgelewicz, D'Adamo, Malaya
Journal: Integrative medicine (Encinitas, Calif.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42222203/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

18-week single-group sequential placebo-controlled crossover trial (8 wks placebo, 2 wks washout, 8 wks 300 mg/d annatto-associated geranylgeraniol) in 34 completed middle-aged healthy adults (40-65 y). No significant changes in total/free testosterone or SHBG in the overall sample or females; subgroup of men with baseline testosterone <600 ng/dL (n=8) showed increased total testosterone (+28.44 ng/dL, P = 0.015), and serum CoQ10 remained stable during GG vs declining on placebo. Abstract reports no symptom-specific fatigue, mood, or sleep separation. Limits: nonrandomized sequential order, small subgroup, no PD symptom endpoints.
