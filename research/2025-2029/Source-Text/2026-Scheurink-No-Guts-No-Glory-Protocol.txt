Title: Efficacy of an anti-inflammatory dietary pattern on global functioning, gut microbiome, and health in patients with psychiatric disorders and neurodegenerative diseases
Source: https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.02.26354674v1.full

Publication date: 2026-06-03
Registration: NL-OMON52339, cross-referenced by NL-OMON20827 and NL9851

Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated investigator protocol preprint and supplement review

The study allocated participants 1:3 to two treatment sequences. Each
sequence provides a 12-week BrAIN diet period and a 12-week habitual diet
period separated by 24 weeks. The preprint reports 107 enrolled across
four groups, including 49 with Parkinson disease. The final visit was
2025-09-05. Analysis was ongoing at first posting, with a Parkinson
results manuscript planned. The protocol gives no efficacy estimates.

The diet emphasizes olive oil alongside several other food groups and
uses food boxes and individual dietary support. Supplementary Table S3
lists apples with peel and olive oil. Table S4 separately uses apple
syrup and olive oil for preparation in an example menu. The text
does not specify oil grade, an assigned amount of apple or oil, or
participant-level intake. The Parkinson motor examination is
MDS-UPDRS Part III. The protocol supplies no separate outcome for rest
tremor or ingredient-level contrast.
