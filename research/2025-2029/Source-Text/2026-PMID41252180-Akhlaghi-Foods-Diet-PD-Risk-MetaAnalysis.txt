Study design and findings
- Systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis of 29 prospective cohort studies totaling 1,307,337 participants.
- Higher risk of incident PD: total dairy (OR 1.26, 95% CI 1.12-1.41), low-fat dairy (OR 1.30, 95% CI 1.06-1.58), and milk (OR 1.23, 95% CI 1.07-1.43; +13% per 1 cup/day; +5-7% per 100 g/day dairy; +4% per 10 g/day cheese), stronger in men.
- Lower risk of incident PD: legumes/nuts (OR 0.71, 95% CI 0.62-0.81) and healthy dietary patterns (OR 0.63, 95% CI 0.54-0.74), while unhealthy dietary patterns carried higher risk (OR 1.40, 95% CI 1.07-1.83).

Relevance and limits for Parkinson's food experiments
- Contemporary 2026 prospective dietary-pattern synthesis providing epidemiological context across milk/dairy, legumes, and nuts.
- Limits: Context-only review/meta-analysis of observational PD incidence rather than symptom-severity trials in diagnosed Parkinson's disease.
