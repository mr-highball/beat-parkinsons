Title: Water-dispersible turmeric extract in Thanawala et al. knee osteoarthritis trial
PMID: 39882178
PMCID: PMC11776925
DOI: 10.2147/JPR.S501505
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11776925/
Registry reported in the article: CTRI/2023/07/055411
First publication: 2025.
Coverage: Original PMC article body, study flow and reported outcomes.

The double-blind, placebo-controlled trial randomized 140 adults with knee osteoarthritis. The analysis included 139, 70 assigned to extract and 69 assigned to placebo. The treatment was a proprietary water-dispersible extract containing 60% curcuminoids. At day 90, mean reduction in the primary 100-mm pain VAS was 14.41 mm versus 6.02 mm (reported p<.0001). The raw between-arm difference in mean change is 8.39 mm favoring the extract. The difference and formulation are distinct from a culinary powder and there was no Parkinson cohort. Several authors disclosed commercial or patent interests related to the intervention. The independently published Cochrane review uses multiple formulations. Whether this trial entered that synthesis remains unverified.

The review team's institute supplement includes a Thanawala 2025 row with 70 and 69 participants and the same trial label in the pain risk-of-bias workbook. The supplement contains 11 studies, compared with four in the final published pain estimate. The row establishes supplementary coverage and leaves final membership open.
