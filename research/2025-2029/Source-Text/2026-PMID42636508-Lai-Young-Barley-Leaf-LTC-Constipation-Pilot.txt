Title: Non-pharmacological dietary support for functional constipation in long-term care: A quasi-experimental pilot study of young barley leaf.
PMID: 42636508
DOI: 10.1016/j.gerinurse.2026.104337
First published: 2026
Authors: Lai YJ, Yang CM
Journal: Geriatric nursing (New York, N.Y.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42636508/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

One-group pretest-posttest pilot study in n=28 long-term care residents with Rome IV functional constipation given young barley leaf powder (3 g twice daily for 2 weeks). Defecation frequency increased during week 2 (p<0.05) and declined post-discontinuation; bowel sound frequency increased (p<0.001); bisacodyl use did not change significantly. Limits: uncontrolled pilot using young barley leaf (different plant part from cooked barley grain).
