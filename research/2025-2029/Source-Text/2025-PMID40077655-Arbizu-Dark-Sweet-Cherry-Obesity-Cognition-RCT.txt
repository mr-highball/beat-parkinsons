Title: Assessing the Role of Dark Sweet Cherry (Prunus avium L.) Consumption on Cognitive Function, Neuropeptides, and Circadian Rhythm in Obesity: Results from a Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 40077655
PMCID: PMC11901987
DOI: 10.3390/nu17050784
First published: 2025
Authors: Arbizu S, Mertens-Talcott SU, Talcott S, Riviere A, Riechman SE, Noratto GD
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11901987/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (Arbizu et al., 2025, Nutrients, PMC11901987) in 40 obese adults (BMI 30-40 kg/m2) consuming 200 mL dark sweet cherry (Prunus avium) drink with 3 g cherry powder (n=19) or isocaloric placebo (n=21) twice daily for 30 days. Dark sweet cherry improved working memory and concentration on Digit Span Forward (p=0.006) and Digit Span Backward (p=0.01), whereas Trail Making Test (TMT), Digit Symbol Substitution Test (DSST), and NeuroTracker 3D visual cognitive performance showed NO significant between-group differences. Limits: single-blind design, obese non-PD cohort, mixed juice+powder drink, selective subtest improvement alongside null TMT/DSST.
