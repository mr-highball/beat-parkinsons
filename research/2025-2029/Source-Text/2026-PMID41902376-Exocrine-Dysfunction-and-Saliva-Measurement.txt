Title: Exocrine Gland Dysfunction in Parkinson's Disease: Pathophysiology, Clinical Manifestations, and Therapeutic Perspectives—A Narrative Review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13338979/
PMID: 41902376
PMCID: PMC13338979
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70610
Publication date: 2026-03-27
Coverage: Paraphrased original JATS narrative review and salivary-gland table.

The authors distinguish subjective dry mouth, measured low salivary production and saliva loss. The article discusses impaired clearance, drug effects and autonomic dysfunction, and cites the 2021 assessment-tool review PMID 34583888, which selected ROMP–Saliva under its stated criteria. The review's prevalence ranges summarize prior reports. They are not new participant estimates. The article gives no primary fig intervention or comparison.
