Title: Brown rice attenuates iron-induced Parkinson's disease phenotypes in male wild-type drosophila: insights into antioxidant and iron metabolism modulation.
PMID: 39588846
DOI: 10.1139/apnm-2024-0110
First published: 2025
Authors: Yankuzo HM, Sulaiman I, Muhammad SA, Raji AA, Uthman YA, Imam MU
Journal: Applied physiology, nutrition, and metabolism = Physiologie appliquee, nutrition et metabolisme
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39588846/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Fruit-fly iron-induced parkinsonism study (10 days Fe followed by 5 days brown rice, white rice, or L-dopa; overlapping author team with Uthman 2025 PMID:39235924). Brown rice significantly improved fly motor/climbing and cognitive functions and lowered head MDA/Fe vs Fe and white rice groups. Invertebrate model; overlapping lineage with Uthman 2025.
