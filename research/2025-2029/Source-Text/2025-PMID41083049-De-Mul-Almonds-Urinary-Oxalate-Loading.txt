Title: Snacks and urinary oxalate: Which wins, almonds or chocolate?
PMID: 41083049
DOI: 10.1016/j.fjurol.2025.103003
First published: 2025
Authors: De Mul, Acquaviva-Bourdain, Abid, Desenclos, Wehrlen
Journal: The French journal of urology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41083049/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Acute oxalate loading study in 7 healthy volunteers comparing baseline 24-h urine oxalate with 100 g dark chocolate and 150 g almonds. Almonds significantly increased 24-h urinary oxalate (931 [680-990] vs 690 [430-870] umol/24 h, P = 0.01) while chocolate did not (P = 0.9). Relevant for nephrolithiasis safety context; no urinary urgency frequency outcome.
