Title: Outcomes of isolated hip fractures in people with Parkinson's disease: An observational cohort study using propensity score methods

Observational registry study of 5,553 admissions with an isolated hip fracture, including 208 identified with Parkinson disease or related coding. Outcomes include discharge, hospital stay and mortality following fracture. Most fractures followed low falls, but the study design has no denominator for person-level fall frequency in the underlying Parkinson population. Rigidity severity and dried-sage intake were not outcomes or exposures.
