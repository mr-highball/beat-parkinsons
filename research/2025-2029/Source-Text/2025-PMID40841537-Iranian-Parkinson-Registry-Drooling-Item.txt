Title: A report of the Iranian Parkinson’s disease registry
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12370882/
PMID: 40841537
PMCID: PMC12370882
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-01108-7
Publication date: 2025
Coverage: Paraphrased original article registry methods and regression results.

Over six years the report records 4,048 interviews. The multivariable analysis associates drooling with male gender, with a reported odds ratio of 1.95 (95% CI 1.66–2.30). It reflects a symptom comparison in this registry sample, with no planned fig exposure or food-response measure. Geographic context alone gives no link to the separately published Persian traditional dietary advice.
