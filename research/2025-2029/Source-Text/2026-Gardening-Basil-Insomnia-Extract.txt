The Egyptian quasi-experimental nursing intervention included 62 male patients with depression. Ten group sessions combined exercise, plant care, meditation and play. Sessions 3 and 4 involved planting and tending basil and jasmine. There was no assigned oral basil exposure.
The study compared scores on a six-item insomnia dimension and the Beck Hopelessness Scale. The broader sleep instrument includes narcolepsy, but the investigators did not use that dimension. No dedicated daytime sleepiness or apathy estimate was reported.
