Methanolic anise seed extract and experimental nociception
Original author abstract with outstanding full methods
Jawed and colleagues first published November 30, 2025. The original abstract reports methanolic Pimpinella anisum seed extract in experimental hot-plate, tail and writhing nociception paradigms. The abstract gives evidence of changed nociceptive behavior and describes inflammation and fever tests. Full numerical effects, administration context and statistical details of individual experiments remain open. The extract in these models supplies no direct patient estimate of pain after eating ground anise.
