Topic: Nanocurcumin and instrumental swallowing after chemoradiotherapy
Source: https://doi.org/10.4103/jrcr.jrcr_11_25
Coverage: May 28, 2025 acute-toxicity report, factual extract
Arshida and colleagues report one head and neck chemoradiotherapy trial. The narrative screens 88, allocates 70 (35 per arm) and analyzes 64 completers (32 per arm). The comparison uses nanocurcumin and placebo. The article prints CTRI submission reference REF/2023/02/063210. This report establishes the shared cohort and precedes the published swallowing table.
