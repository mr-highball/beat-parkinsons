Soybean oil vitamin D emulsion gel

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0268005X25010124
Coverage: Original abstract and publisher excerpts, factual extract.

Han and colleagues, Food Hydrocolloids 172:112052, March 2026 issue, DOI 10.1016/j.foodhyd.2025.112052. First online date remains to verify within this period. The publisher abstract and snippets describe a pea-protein-stabilized soybean oil and vitamin D3 emulsion with calcium alginate. The authors report consistency within the IDDSI 4–5 range for a selected formulation using food texture tests. These results describe product mechanics and formulation, with no observed swallowing outcome in people or separate effect of refined soybean oil. IDDSI classification describes texture and does not establish individual swallowing safety.
