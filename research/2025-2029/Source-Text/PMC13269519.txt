Title: Biomarkers stewardship in parkinsonism: integrating alpha-synuclein seed amplification assays and neurofilament light chain into diagnostic pathways and patient communication
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13269519/
PMID: 42295486
PMCID: PMC13269519
DOI: 10.1007/s00415-026-13933-8
Publication date: 2026-06-15 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The biomarker stewardship discussion describes how synuclein and neurofilament tests could inform diagnosis, classification and patient communication. These clinical uses do not supply food exposures or measured visual outcomes.
