Title: Quantitative electroencephalography in the diagnosis of dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12271817/
PMID: 40687078
PMCID: PMC12271817
DOI: 10.1016/j.cnp.2025.06.008
Publication date: 2025-07-02 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The review describes resting quantitative EEG signatures used to help distinguish prodromal or manifest dementia with Lewy bodies. It reviews diagnostic spectral patterns, not a culinary exposure or visual episode response over time.
