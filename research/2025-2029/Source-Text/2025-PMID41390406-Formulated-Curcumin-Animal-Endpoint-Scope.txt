Original study scope
This experimental publication compares a formulated curcumin intervention in Parkinson-related laboratory and animal research. Example abbreviation Fig denotes figures, without a fig-food exposure. The model reports motor outcomes alongside other measures.

Continuing evidence gaps
The administered formulation and animal outcomes do not establish culinary turmeric effects on clinical bradykinesia, rigidity or fall events. Figure references do not inform the raw fig tremor claim.
