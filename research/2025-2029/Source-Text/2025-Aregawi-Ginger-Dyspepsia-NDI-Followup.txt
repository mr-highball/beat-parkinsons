Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Aregawi and Zoltan report 124 screened, 62 enrolled and 51 completers after eight weeks of open-label ginger capsules. The 11 men, 40 women and mean age 49.74 years (SD 16.38) match the 2023 four-week report. The later thesis repeats the quality-of-life results. Table 2 gives a mean short-form NDI total of 19.07 before and 9.10 after. The reported mean change is −9.97 with SD 16.20 and p=.039. The tension domain is distinct and the reported comparison has p=.670. The authors describe using paired t or Wilcoxon tests as appropriate. The short-form NDI impact score is not an isolated pain or anxiety diagnosis or a placebo-controlled observation.

The registry lists EQ-5D quality of life at four weeks. This article uses short-form NDI at eight weeks instead. Separate the actual measurement from the planned instrument and group related reports as one study family unless independently recruited samples are established.
