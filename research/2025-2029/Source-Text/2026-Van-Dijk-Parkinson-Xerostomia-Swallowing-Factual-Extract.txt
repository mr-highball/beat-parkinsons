Title: Parkinson dry mouth and swallowing severity survey
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ger.70049
Format: Factual extract from the original article.

Van Dijk and colleagues, Gerodontology, first published January 16, 2026. Online survey of 111 people with Parkinson disease. Xerostomia occurred in 64.9%, drooling in 27.0% and both in 18.0%. Scale-based swallowing difficulties were associated with xerostomia severity in multiple linear regression, B 0.41 (95% CI 0.04 to 0.78). The survey assesses symptoms together and administers no oil or food intervention. It supplies a population bridge for perceived dryness and dysphagia, with no causal estimate or instrumental swallowing measure.
