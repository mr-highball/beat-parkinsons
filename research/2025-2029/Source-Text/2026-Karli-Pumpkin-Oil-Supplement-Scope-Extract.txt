Pumpkin oil fatty-acid supplement and clinical table access
Source: https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art:10.1007%2Fs11845-026-04706-3/MediaObjects/11845_2026_4706_MOESM1_ESM.pdf
PMID: 42955537
DOI: 10.1007/s11845-026-04706-3
Registration: NCT07123844
Coverage: Official four-page supplement inspected in full; primary clinical tables remain restricted

The October 2026 supplemental material for the Karli trial contains pumpkin product sample preparation and fatty-acid GC-MS laboratory reports. It contains no blood-pressure table, orthostatic symptom assessment or oil processing grade. The registered blood-pressure procedure is seated after five minutes of rest. The accessible primary abstract records that blood pressure was measured. It does not provide arm-specific values. The main clinical table and comparison with the registered participant count remain open.
