Dongyan Guan and colleagues. The Extract of Piper nigrum Improves the Cognitive Impairment and Mood in Sleep-Deprived Mice Through the JAK1/STAT3 Signalling Pathway. International Journal of Molecular Sciences 26(5):1842. First published February 21, 2025. DOI 10.3390/ijms26051842, PMID 40076470, PMCID PMC11899911.

The intervention was a concentrated ethanolic extract of milled Piper nigrum fruits. The assay identified piperine as about 64.67% of the extract. Sleep-interrupted mice received the preparation by oral gavage. Figure 4 reports eight to ten animals per group for behavioral plots. Results describe more target-quadrant time in the Morris water maze at some times, including the medium group on days 3 and 4. Crossing counts did not show a clear favorable contrast. The Methods group count and reported agomelatine comparisons require care when interpreting study flow.

The observed outcomes include spatial learning, locomotion and immobility tests. They do not give a sustained-attention result, an isolated culinary cracked-pepper intervention or a Parkinson cohort.
