Title: Network based statistics associates increased connectivity to REM sleep disorder and hallucinations in early DLB
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13586286/
PMID: 42259833
PMCID: PMC13586286
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-026-01412-w
Publication date: 2026-06-08 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Resting high-density EEG compares 33 early dementia with Lewy bodies participants with 21 controls. Visual hallucination and REM sleep behavior status are examined separately in relation to alpha-band connectivity. There is no monitored fig exposure or change in the number of visual episodes.
