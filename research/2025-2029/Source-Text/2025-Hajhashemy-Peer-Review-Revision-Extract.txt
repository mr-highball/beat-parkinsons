Protocol peer review and the timing of the fatigue outcome

Coverage: factual extract of page 3, reviewer timestamps and outcome-fit check.

The protocol's public peer-review file contains dated reviewer comments on October 12, 2024 and May 15, 2025. The comments do not name the Parkinson Fatigue Scale. The undated author response on PDF page 3 lists fatigue, blood pressure and appetite among secondary outcomes. This shows the investigator team discussed fatigue during manuscript revision and leaves its date of inclusion unresolved. The original IRCT registration listed a broad MDS-UPDRS measure, which includes a fatigue item in Part I. Its list did not specify the separate Parkinson Fatigue Scale comparison used in the reported fatigue result.
