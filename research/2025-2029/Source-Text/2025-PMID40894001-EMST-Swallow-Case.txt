Title: Feasibility of expiratory muscle strength training to address oropharyngeal dysphagia in a patient living with mixed dementia: A case report
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40894001/
PMID: 40894001
PMCID: PMC12394874
DOI: 10.1177/25424823251361032
First public: 2025-08-28
Coverage: Factual extract from original methods and Tables 1–2. Identifying patient detail omitted.

The clinical case follows expiratory muscle strength training and videofluoroscopic swallowing evaluation in mixed dementia. Test boluses included barium in several consistencies and a cookie–barium solid trial. The maximum Penetration–Aspiration Scale score remained 8 before and after training, while the most frequent score changed from 8 to 3. These are uncontrolled observations of swallowing in one patient. The study did not use sage or assign a food intervention for falls or fatigue.
