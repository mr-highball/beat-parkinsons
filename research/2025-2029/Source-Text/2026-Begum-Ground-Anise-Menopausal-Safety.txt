Ground anise capsules and composite menopausal symptoms in a clinical trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42152642/
PMID: 42152642
DOI: 10.2174/0113816128433736260209142133
Coverage: factual extract from primary trial abstract

Arfa Begum and colleagues. Current Pharmaceutical Design. PubMed gives May 12, 2026 as first online date. DOI 10.2174/0113816128433736260209142133.
Sixty menopausal women were assigned to 4 g/day of anise or matched placebo for eight weeks. Weekly safety interviews and hepatic and kidney tests were described. The abstract reports that there were no adverse effects. DASS-21 and overall Modified Kupperman Index scores were reported, with no estimate for insomnia, daytime sleepiness, fatigue or a daytime adverse-effect severity item. Companion primary paper PMID 42855851 documents mechanically ground fruit in capsules. Its own text explicitly references the clinical study among 60 randomized menopausal participants. Treat the reports as one trial dataset. The source refers to menopausal, rather than Parkinson, symptoms.
