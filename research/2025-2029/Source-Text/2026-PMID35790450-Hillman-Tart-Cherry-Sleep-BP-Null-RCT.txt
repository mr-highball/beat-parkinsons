Title: Montmorency tart cherry supplementation does not impact sleep, body composition, cellular health, or blood pressure in healthy adults.
PMID: 35790450
DOI: 10.1177/02601060221111230
First published: 2026
Authors: Hillman AR, Trickett O, Brodsky C, Chrismas B
Journal: Nutrition and health
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35790450/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized placebo-controlled trial (Hillman et al., 2026, Nutr Health; online earlier DOI 10.1177/02601060221111230) analyzing 44 healthy adults assigned to 30 days of Montmorency tart cherry juice (2x240 mL/day), powdered Montmorency tart cherry capsules (2 capsules/day), or matched placebos. Daily sleep questionnaires over 30 days showed NO statistically significant between-group differences in sleep time or sleep quality (both groups increased over time), and resting blood pressure and cellular health were likewise unaffected. Important null comparator across both juice and powdered dried tart cherry forms in healthy non-PD adults.
