Title: Holistic Care Clinic for People with Parkinson's Disease: Outcome from a Newly Developed Service
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12839184/
PMID: 41594764
PMCID: PMC12839184
DOI: 10.3390/brainsci16010043
Publication date: 2025-12-29
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC article XML and included tables; supplementary files are separate.

Thirty Parkinson patients with bothersome nonmotor symptoms attended 12 multidisciplinary clinics. The service assessed motor and nonmotor symptoms and offered individualized care, including general dietary and hydration guidance, medication review, and interventions for anxiety. Results were reported for the service and its neuropsychiatric focus. It did not compare date intake or isolate a food-associated pain or apathy response.
