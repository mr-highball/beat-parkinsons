Protocol for longitudinal clinical and digital measures in Parkinson disease, multiple sclerosis and Alzheimer disease across six sites. Its proposed cohort includes healthy controls. A Parkinson depression instrument is discussed in relation to several symptoms including apathy. A bodily-pain domain belongs to a multiple sclerosis quality-of-life measure. The protocol gives planned measurements, without results or a dried-date comparison.
