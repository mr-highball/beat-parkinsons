Title: Functional benefits of ultra-high hydrostatic pressurized brown rice on cognition, apathy, and bone health in older adults: A 12-month randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 40901038
PMCID: PMC12400291
DOI: 10.3892/etm.2025.12942
Registry ID: UMIN000053587
First published: 2025
Authors: Matsuzaki K, Yano S, Kuroda Y, Nakahata H, Matsuda T, Kinoshita H
Journal: Experimental and therapeutic medicine
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12400291/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-month randomized controlled trial (UMIN000053587, retrospectively registered) in n=44 community-dwelling older adults (65-85 y) assigned to daily ultra-high hydrostatic pressurized brown rice (UBR) vs polished white rice. At 12 months, UBR significantly lowered Starkstein Apathy Scale scores vs white rice (p=0.008), increased CADi total score and shortened CADi execution time (p<0.05), and yielded higher adjusted MMSE (p=0.046; change score NS), with no adverse events; Zung depression did not significantly differ. Limits: pressurized cooked brown rice, retrospective registration, non-PD older adults.
