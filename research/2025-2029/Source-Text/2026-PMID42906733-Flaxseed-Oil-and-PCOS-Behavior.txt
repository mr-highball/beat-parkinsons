Title: Flaxseed and fish oils in the letrozole polycystic ovary syndrome mouse model
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42906733/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0014299926009325
PMID: 42906733
DOI: 10.1016/j.ejphar.2026.179450
Publication date: 2026-10-21
Source depth: Original author abstract and publication metadata.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27

Thirty female mice were assigned across five groups including a model control and groups given metformin, flaxseed oil or fish oil. Open-field and elevated-plus-maze tests appear among several behavioral endpoints. The author abstract summarizes moderate behavioral findings for flaxseed oil and metformin. It does not identify an anxiety-specific group estimate, uncertainty or the oil pressing method. The original numeric comparison and methods table remain inaccessible. A similar separately published conference report on ovarian endpoints does not verify the identity of the animals or supply the anxiety table.

The oil and anxiety-like behavior are related to the research question. The disease model, exposure grade and unclear outcome-specific results prevent attribution to cold-pressed oil or generalization to Parkinson anxiety.
