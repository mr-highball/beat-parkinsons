Title: Hypotensive Episodes Precede Cognitive Fluctuations in Parkinson's Disease Dementia.
PMID: 41837340
PMCID: PMC13339593
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70590
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13339593/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2026-03-16
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch original article JATS, with complete body.

The series links caregiver-recorded cognitive fluctuations in four people with Parkinson disease dementia to immediately preceding low blood pressure on ambulatory monitoring. Meal timing appears in the plots and the authors propose more detailed food documentation in future studies. Visual hallucinations are recorded in case histories, rather than as the events associated with meals. No fig is identified and the study gives no comparable visual episode frequency.
