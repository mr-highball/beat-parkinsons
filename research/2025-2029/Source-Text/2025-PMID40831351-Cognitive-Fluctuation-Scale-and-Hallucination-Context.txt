Title: Cognitive Fluctuation Scale in Non-demented Patients with Parkinson's Disease.
PMID: 40831351
PMCID: PMC12625139
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70295
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12625139/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2025-08-20
Coverage: Official NIH PMC complete article page, with methods and results.

The article assesses the Dementia Cognitive Fluctuation Scale in people with Parkinson disease without dementia. The investigators distinguish fluctuations of attention or arousal and discuss visual hallucinations as part of the clinical background. Scale performance and correlates are separate from the effect of eating dried figs on visual hallucinations.
