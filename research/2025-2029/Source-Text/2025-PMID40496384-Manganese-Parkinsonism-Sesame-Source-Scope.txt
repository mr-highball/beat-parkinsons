Original study scope
The review describes an animal experiment that coadministered sesame oil during manganese exposure. The cited primary report is PMID 38548024. Outcomes include movement in an open field, rotarod performance and nonclinical measures.

Continuing evidence gaps
The primary comparison does not verify refined or cold-pressed culinary oil, an isolated idiopathic Parkinson rigidity endpoint, clinical dystonia or pain. Preserve the distinction between experimental rod performance and clinical symptoms.
