Original source scope and evidence boundary
The article gives 60 randomized and 53 analyzed in a 12-week comparison of sesame and sunflower oils with calorie restriction. Three sesame and four sunflower participants dropped out. Fasting glucose, insulin and other metabolic biomarkers are the reported endpoints. No separate anxiety, depression or fatigue measure is presented. The same IRCT20140208016529N6 registration, award 399632, participant flow and oil comparison link this paper to the 2022 and 2023 reports. The 2022 reports provide the refined-oil specification. Use the 2025 publication to establish lineage and participant-flow context. It provides no additional independent vote on claims 0039, 0262 or 0404.
