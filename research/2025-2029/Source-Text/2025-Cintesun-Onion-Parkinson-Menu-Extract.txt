Title: Inflammatory Potential of Medical Nutrition Therapy Plans in Some Neurologic Diseases: A Descriptive Cross-Sectional Research

Coverage: factual extract of full licensed article, Methods and Tables 1 and 4

Investigators adapted source menus for five neurological diseases to 2,000 kcal and modeled nutrients and dietary inflammatory index. Table 4 assigns onion weights of 38 g for Alzheimer disease, 59 g for Parkinson disease, 67 g for multiple sclerosis, 18 g for epilepsy and 50 g for migraine. The weights are inputs in modeled menus. Table 1 enumerates meals but does not identify a specific onion cooking method, portion served or participant intake. The paper reports no data collection from patients, speech outcomes or effect of consuming onion.
