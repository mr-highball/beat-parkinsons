Coverage: primary report exposure and outcome scope. Local factual extract.

This horticultural experiment evaluated foliar melatonin under aluminum exposure in garden strawberry plants. The reported comparisons involve plant growth, photosynthetic measures, nitrogen and carbohydrate metabolism, oxidative stress and fruit yield or quality. The intervention was applied to plants and included assessment of the fruit. The report contains no feeding comparison, human Parkinson group or fall counts.
