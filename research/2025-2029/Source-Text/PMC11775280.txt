Title: Clinically probable RBD is an early predictor of malignant non-motor Parkinson's disease phenotypes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11775280/
PMID: 39875387
PMCID: PMC11775280
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-00874-8
Publication date: 2025-01-29 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The reported early probable REM sleep behavior disorder group had a higher prevalence and greater later severity of hallucinations on clinical assessment. The report compares phenotype and severity over disease-time intervals. It does not count visual-only episodes after a food exposure.

The source analyzes severity across intervals since Parkinson diagnosis. The reported between-group difference is clearer at the four- to six-year interval than the six- to eight-year interval. The published six- to eight-year corrected P value is 0.060.
