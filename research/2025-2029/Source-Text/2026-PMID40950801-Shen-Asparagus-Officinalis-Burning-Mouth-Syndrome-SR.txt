Title: Effects of Asparagus officinalis for burning mouth syndrome: A systematic review of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 40950801
PMCID: PMC12423344
DOI: 10.1016/j.imr.2025.101223
First published: 2026
Authors: Shen C, Wang XF, Wu XT, Liu XX, Robinson N, Liu JP
Journal: Integrative medicine research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40950801/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

PRISMA systematic review and meta-analysis (Shen et al., 2026, Integr Med Res, PMC12423344, PROSPERO CRD420250651920) of 6 Chinese RCTs (n=336 adults) evaluating fresh Asparagus officinalis capsules or oral liquid alone or combined with conventional medicines in burning mouth syndrome (BMS). Meta-analysis reported improved pain effective rate vs vitamin B complex alone (RR 6.00, 95% CI 1.61-22.34), improved 10-point VAS with oral liquid + mecobalamin (MD -1.40, 95% CI -2.19 to -0.61), improved unstimulated salivary flow, sleep duration score, and anxiety/depression scores. All outcomes were GRADE-rated LOW certainty due to lack of blinding/placebo controls, high risk of bias, small sample sizes, and Chinese-database primary trials whose original full texts were inaccessible for independent audit; review-level context only, non-PD chronic oral pain.
