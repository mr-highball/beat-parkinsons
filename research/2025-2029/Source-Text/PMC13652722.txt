Title: Patient-specific functional networks track early cognitive alterations and minor hallucinations in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13652722/
PMID: 42859526
PMCID: PMC13652722
DOI: 10.1038/s44220-026-00657-x
Publication date: 2026-06-08 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The journal article uses 18 Parkinson participants with minor visual phenomena, 14 without hallucinations and 31 controls. Clinician assessments distinguish presence and passage phenomena, illusions or pareidolias, with overlap across categories. The paper describes cross-sectional functional connectivity and concurrent cognitive scores. The 2025 preprint describes the same Parkinson cohort.

Version lineage: https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.17.649310. The Methods cite a previous report for the clinical and Parkinson imaging data. The split of one scan into two halves measures identifiability within the scan. It does not provide a future visual episode outcome.
