Title: ETAS reduces wake time after sleep onset in healthy adults who are dissatisfied with their sleep: A randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind crossover study.
PMID: 42571331
PMCID: PMC13452218
DOI: 10.1016/j.sleepx.2026.100196
Registry ID: UMIN000055685
First published: 2026
Authors: Misu M, Goto K, Takanari J
Journal: Sleep medicine: X
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42571331/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized crossover trial (Misu, Goto & Takanari, 2026, Sleep Med X, PMC13452218, UMIN000055685) in 38 healthy Japanese adults (18 men, 20 women; BMI 21.7 +/- 2.1) with subjective sleep dissatisfaction taking 300 mg/day ETAS50 (enzyme-treated Asparagus officinalis stem extract standardized to >=50 ug/g diketopiperazines) vs dextrin placebo for 14 days each with a 14-day washout. Primary outcome Stage N3 proportion (%TST) was NULL (Model 2 p=0.928/0.904). However, ETAS50 significantly improved secondary insomnia measures: Athens Insomnia Scale-Japanese (AIS-J) total score (Model 1 -0.80, p=0.014; Model 2 -0.74, 95% CI -1.39 to -0.08, p=0.037), AIS-J daytime functioning capacity (-0.16, 95% CI -0.30 to -0.02, p=0.030), objective single-channel EEG wake after sleep onset (WASO: Model 1 -8.83 min, 95% CI -15.93 to -1.73, p=0.020; Model 2 -8.70 min, p=0.029; high baseline WASO +1SD subgroup -16.71 min, 95% CI -27.2 to -6.2, p=0.003), and Stage N1 duration (-1.71 min, p=0.049). AIS-J daytime sleepiness (-0.03, p=0.777), OSA-MA sleepiness on rising (-0.91, p=0.201), OSA-MA initiation/maintenance (-0.68, p=0.343), and OSA-MA frequent dreaming (+0.17, p=0.886) were all NULL. Zero adverse events. Limits: Amino Up Co. authored/funded; standardized enzyme-treated stem extract rather than roasted/steamed/grilled culinary spears; healthy non-PD adults; primary N3 endpoint null.
