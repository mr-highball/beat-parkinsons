Original study scope
The review lists olive and coconut oil among foods associated with lower overall Parkinson burden, citing primary study PMID 29081890. That study analyzed baseline food-frequency data cross-sectionally against aggregate patient-reported severity. It did not estimate within-person change over follow-up. Date words in the review refer to chronology.

Continuing evidence gaps
The primary study does not grade coconut or olive oils, or isolate outcomes such as dyskinesia, constipation, postural instability or anxiety by oil type. The cited composite associations remain an observational lead and cannot establish a symptom-specific effect or longitudinal progression.
