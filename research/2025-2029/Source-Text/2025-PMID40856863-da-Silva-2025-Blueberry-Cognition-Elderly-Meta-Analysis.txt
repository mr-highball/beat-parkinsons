Title: Blueberries for brainpower: A systematic review and meta-analysis with Bayesian post hoc analysis of RCTS exploring cognitive function in the elderly with prior cognitive decline.
PMID: 40856863
DOI: 10.1007/s10522-025-10308-w
First published: 2025
Authors: da Silva ABN, de Oliveira GM, Gallo Ruelas M, Gadelha MSM, de Farias Santos ACF, Zamora FV
Journal: Biogerontology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40856863/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and random-effects meta-analysis of 9 chronic blueberry RCTs (n=513 older adults with prior cognitive decline/MCI/SCD): blueberry significantly improved episodic memory (SMD 0.34, 95% CI 0.11 to 0.57, p<0.05) and language memory in MCI (SMD 0.30, 0.01 to 0.60, p<0.05), but showed no statistically significant pooled improvement in processing speed (SMD -0.33, 95% CI -0.85 to 0.19, p>0.05), working memory (SMD 0.09), recognition memory (SMD 0.14), or visuospatial learning (SMD 0.32).
