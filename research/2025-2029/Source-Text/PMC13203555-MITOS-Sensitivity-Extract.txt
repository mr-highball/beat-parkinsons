Table S3 uses the first period only, with 18 participants in the low-lycopene arm and 24 in the paste arm. The adjusted difference in the Modified Wisconsin Card Sorting Test Executive Function Index was +2.5 (95% CI −2.8 to +7.8, p=.359). The differences in categories completed, perseverative errors and total errors also had intervals spanning zero.

Table S4 reports adjusted executive-index estimates of +0.5 (95% CI −2.0 to +3.0, p=.695) after adding the one-period participants (n=45), +1.7 (−0.8 to +4.2, p=.183) after excluding high triglycerides (n=40), +0.8 (−1.9 to +3.6, p=.554) after excluding high BMI (n=41), and +1.9 (−0.8 to +4.5, p=.162) after applying both exclusions (n=39). Other executive-test contrasts in these models also crossed zero.

Table S5 exclusions based on plasma lycopene responses produced executive-index contrasts of +1.8 (95% CI −1.5 to +5.2, p=.285; n=35), +1.4 (−2.4 to +5.3, p=.463; n=33), and +3.11 (−1.4 to +7.6, p=.176; n=28) for the combined restriction. These are sensitivity analyses of one open-label crossover in healthy midlife adults, with the same pooled paste preparations as the primary report.
