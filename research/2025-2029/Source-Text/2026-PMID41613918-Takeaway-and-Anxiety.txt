The cross-sectional analysis included 15,440 college students. The exposure questionnaire identified eight kinds of takeout, including Malatang, a mixed spicy hot pot or skewer preparation. Tables 4 and 5 report adjusted anxiety odds of 1.23 (95% CI 1.09–1.40) for the broader takeaway-only group and 1.67 (1.43–1.95) for the milk-tea-and-takeaway group.

These estimates are for broader groups, and the type-specific analysis still concerns whole meals. The study does not identify a ground cayenne exposure or a Parkinson symptom outcome. The observed diet-level estimates are catalog context for claim 0096.
