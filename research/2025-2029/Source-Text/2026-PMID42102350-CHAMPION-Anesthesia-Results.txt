Title: CHAMPION randomized anesthesia comparison and published outcomes
PMID: 42102350
DOI: 10.1097/ALN.0000000000006133
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42102350/
First publication: 2026-05-08 online, October issue.
Study family: NCT05550714.
Coverage: Original structured abstract and protocol identity. The results article body is still open.

The report compares desflurane general anesthesia and dexmedetomidine conscious sedation for bilateral subthalamic deep brain stimulation in Parkinson disease. It reports high-quality microelectrode recording in 89.4% versus 90.3% and a difference of -0.96 percentage points (95% CI -9.62 to 7.70). The abstract reports 188 randomized but gives 94 and 93 as the displayed group counts. It does not account for the one-participant discrepancy at this access depth. The six-month UPDRS difference was -2.50 points (95% CI -7.20 to 2.20). The structured abstract provides no separate pain-severity comparison and no date-fruit exposure.

The original registry currently displays no posted structured results even though the journal report exists. The 2023 published protocol and 2026 outcome report refer to one trial.
