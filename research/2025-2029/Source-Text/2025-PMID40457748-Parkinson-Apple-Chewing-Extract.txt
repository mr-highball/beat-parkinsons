Title: Maximum Muscle Output and Electromyographic Activity of Masticatory Muscles in Persons With Parkinson's Disease. A Case–Control Study
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/joor.14044
PMID: 40457748
PMCID: PMC12426465
DOI: 10.1111/joor.14044
First public article date: 2025-06-02
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated original Methods, Tables 2–3 and cohort statement

The 20 participants with Parkinson's and 20 matched controls
performed chewing sequences with 10 g peeled apple samples.
The Parkinson oral motor assessment took place in the ON
medication state. Table 2 gives chewing masseter activity
74 versus 82 microvolts at the median (p=.74). Relative
contraction compared with maximal voluntary biting was
82% versus 49% (p=.02). The separate masseter workload
column gives 26% versus 16% (p=.01).

The paper explicitly identifies the same group studied in
the 2023 Copenhagen report (PMID 37308874), which analyzed
other oral-function outcomes. Registry NCT05356845 links
this cohort. Each participant performed the apple task.
The comparison is between participants with Parkinson's
and controls. UPDRS characterizes clinical severity, and
there is no separate rest-tremor change attributed to apple.
