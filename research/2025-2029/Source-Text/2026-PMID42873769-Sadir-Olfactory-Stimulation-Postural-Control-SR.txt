Title: The effects of olfactory stimulation on postural control in adults: A systematic review.
PMID: 42873769
PMCID: PMC13667811
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0358888
First published: 2026
Authors: Sadir Y, Khosravi M, Khazanin H, Fakoor Rashid H, Alavi E, Ceylan T
Journal: PloS one
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42873769/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

PRISMA 2020 systematic review (PMC13667811, PROSPERO CRD420261326528) synthesizing 10 studies (n=365 adults) on olfactory stimulation and postural control, including Freeman 2009 (black pepper oil in older adults) and Gim 2015 (black pepper oil in chronic stroke). Concludes olfactory cues including black pepper oil may acutely reduce sway in some static tasks, but rates overall GRADE certainty as very low. Secondary review; context only.
