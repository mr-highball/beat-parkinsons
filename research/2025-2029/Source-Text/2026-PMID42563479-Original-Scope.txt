Round 1 claims: 0839

A randomized pilot compares remotely delivered meditation and breathwork with a waitlist in Parkinson disease. The paper provides an anxiety outcome table and discusses feasibility and baseline imbalance.

The intervention is behavioral. It does not provide ground sage exposure or an isolatable sage effect on fall frequency.
