Original source scope
The prospective multi-cohort study analyzes dietary patterns and separate food components. UK Biobank Supplementary Table S23 (PDF page 102) reports olive-oil intake at the 90th versus 10th percentile and incident anxiety HR 0.905 (p=.00234, FDR q=.020475). The separate incident Parkinson estimate is HR 1.01 (p=.878, FDR q=.92925).

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Body text and Figure 5 give 169,916 participants, while the abstract gives 166,916. The exposure has no verified oil grade and the endpoint is incident diagnosis after recruitment free of major brain disorders. The study does not assess cold-pressed olive oil and anxiety symptom severity in Parkinson disease.
