Title: Sunflower oil emulsion gels and bench texture classification
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0963996925011123
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

June 2025. Polysaccharide emulsion gels with sunflower oil were prepared for incorporation into surimi. The original abstract reports level 4 texture criteria in certain 1–8% oil gels and levels 4 to 5 in surimi recipes with 1–4% oil in emulsion gels. These are food physical classifications. The abstract reports no patient dysphagia severity or effect specific to refined oil.
