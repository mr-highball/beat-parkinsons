Title: Progression of Dysarthria, Drooling, and Swallowing Disorders in Parkinson's Disease: A 1-Year Prospective Cohort Study
Source: https://pubs.asha.org/doi/10.1044/2026_AJSLP-25-00087
PMID: 42340753
DOI: 10.1044/2026_AJSLP-25-00087
Publication date: 2026-06-24
Date basis: First indexed online date. The publisher issue page shows September 8, 2026.
Coverage: Paraphrased original structured abstract. The publisher lists supplemental material 10.23641/asha.32764596.

Seventy-three people with Parkinson disease underwent a one-year comparison. The authors assessed drooling with MDS-UPDRS item 2.2, and used separate clinician-rated instruments for speech and swallowing. Speech worsened significantly. The reported change for drooling and swallowing was inconclusive. No raw-fig exposure was measured. Exact participant overlap with the 2025 Nascimento reports and the individual item statistics require full methods and tables.
