Factual extract from the author-posted preprint body, Sections 2.4, 3.1, 3.3, 4.3 and 5 and the behavioral tables.

The pilot used adult wild-type fish, separate oral thymoquinone and edible-bird’s-nest arms, untreated controls and separate vehicle controls. The protocol grouped swimming measurements across days 1, 7 and 14. Section 3.3 reports 7.1 cm/s swimming velocity and 6,224 cm distance for the intermediate thymoquinone group on day 14. The methods do not describe a Parkinsonian lesion or a combined treatment arm. The conclusion proposes future MPTP evaluation.

Section 3.1 reports 100% survival in control and low-dose groups. The intermediate thymoquinone group had 50% survival and the intermediate bird’s-nest group 66.7%. The highest groups had acute toxicity and substantial mortality and did not enter behavioral comparisons. The abstract calls lower and intermediate exposures well tolerated. The body records the survival losses and discusses compound toxicity, individual variation and oral-administration stress as possible explanations. The source does not give the data needed to adjust the swimming comparison for differential survival.

Freezing bouts and durations varied over time, with no consistent anxiogenic signal described. The exposure and outcome boundaries leave ground Nigella seed and human Parkinson bradykinesia (0503) and toasted seed and clinical freezing of gait (0449) unresolved.
