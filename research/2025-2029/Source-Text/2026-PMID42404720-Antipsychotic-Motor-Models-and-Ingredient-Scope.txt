Original study scope
The review cites Samad and colleagues for rice bran oil in haloperidol motor models. It also describes isolated crocin, lycopene and curcumin in separate models. The rice bran studies largely use chewing stereotypies and coordination, distinct from examiner-rated rigidity or dystonic posture. Other food candidates appear in general discussion or separate study contexts.

Continuing evidence gaps
Reconcile possible overlap among Samad reports and verify oil refining. Matching human Parkinson and symptom comparisons remain open.
