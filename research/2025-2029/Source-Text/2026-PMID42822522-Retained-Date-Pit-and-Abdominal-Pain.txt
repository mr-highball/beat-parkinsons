Title: Endoscopic removal of a date pit masquerading as an appendicolith in a young patient with recurrent right lower quadrant pain
PMID: 42822522
PMCID: PMC13652693
DOI: 10.1055/a-2954-0379
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13652693/
Publication: 2026-10-01 electronic publication
Coverage: Original NCBI JATS full case and follow-up.

Clinicians reported recurrent right lower abdominal pain and found a retained object in the appendiceal lumen, identified grossly as a date pit. Pain resolved after removal and was absent at the three-month follow-up. This is an individual mechanical foreign-body observation. It does not establish an effect of eating dried date flesh, a population risk or a Parkinson symptom response.
