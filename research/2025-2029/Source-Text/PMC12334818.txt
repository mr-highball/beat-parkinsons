Levodopa-carbidopa intestinal gel in multiple system atrophy
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12334818/
PMID: 40786524
PMCID: PMC12334818
DOI: 10.1177/17562864251360048
First publication: 2025-08-08 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC original Methods, three clinical narratives and case table

The authors selected three patients who were ultimately diagnosed with multiple system atrophy with predominant parkinsonism after receiving intestinal levodopa-carbidopa gel. Dystonia is described in the case histories. Changes in motor fluctuations were assessed largely through records and clinical impressions. The series did not compare fresh sage for dystonia or ground sage for rigidity.
