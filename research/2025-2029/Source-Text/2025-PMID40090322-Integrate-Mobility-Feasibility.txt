Title: Feasibility of a Multidomain Intervention for Safe Mobility in People With Parkinson's Disease and Recurrent Falls
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40090322/
PMID: 40090322
PMCID: PMC12061618
DOI: 10.14802/jmd.24237
First public: 2025-03-14
Coverage: Factual methods and results extract from the original body.

Allen and colleagues enrolled 29 people with Parkinson disease and recurrent falls. Twenty-six provided post-test data following a six-month program of home fall-hazard reduction, exercise and safer mobility training. Twenty-one met or exceeded a mobility goal, and median Short Physical Performance Battery change was one point. The published feasibility analysis used within-person pre/post comparisons. The registry plans a broader randomized evaluation. The publication describes home mobility interventions and gives no assigned date palm food exposure.
