Title: Crocin I in experimental Parkinson models with in vivo validation
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13191495/
PMID: 42179644
PMCID: PMC13191495
DOI: 10.1021/acsomega.5c10445
Publication: 2026-05-05
Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article, composition analysis and motor results.

The investigators measured crocin markers in separately prepared saffron stigma samples. The 32-mouse motor experiment assigned purified crocin I and model comparators. Ground stigma was used for the composition work. The animals received the isolated constituent. Pole descent latency and open-field distance and speed improved under crocin I in the experimental model. These voluntary or task-based motor outcomes do not isolate muscle rigidity. Molecular-docking discussion of conformational rigidity refers to ligand complexes. No humans or ground-saffron comparison are present in the motor work.
