Title: A Biomarker-Based Classification of Corticobasal Syndrome
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12882055/
PMID: 41048081
PMCID: PMC12882055
DOI: 10.1002/mds.70070
Publication date: 2025-10-06 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

A prospective corticobasal syndrome cohort is classified by biomarker patterns and related clinical observations. The study identifies possible underlying pathologies and contains no dried fig intervention or clinical Parkinson visual episode outcome.
