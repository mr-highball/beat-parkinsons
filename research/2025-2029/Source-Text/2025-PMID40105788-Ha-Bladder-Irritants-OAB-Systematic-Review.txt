Title: Potential Bladder Irritants and Overactive Bladder Symptoms: A Systematic Review.
PMID: 40105788
DOI: 10.1097/SPV.0000000000001567
First published: 2025
Authors: Ha B, Seo Y, Weaver AM, Khan FZA, Handa VL
Journal: Urogynecology (Philadelphia, Pa.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40105788/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

PROSPERO systematic review (CRD42023466251) of 51 studies assessing 6 NIDDK bladder irritants (including 27 studies on caffeinated/carbonated/citrus beverages and 2 on high-acid foods) on OAB, urinary urgency (15 studies), frequency, and incontinence. Associations were mixed and inconsistent; concluded NO consistent evidence links citrus or other candidate irritants to overactive bladder symptoms.
