Original study scope
The review discusses coffee or caffeine and Parkinson incidence, grape-seed extracts and resveratrol, isolated tea catechins, curcumin, citrus hesperidin, and watermelon as a lycopene source. Orange also occurs as pigment terminology. The reviewed models and effects differ by compound and disease. The coffee associations address diagnosis risk and cannot be read as fatigue change in people with Parkinson disease.

Continuing evidence gaps
Separate actual cultivar, whole food and extract exposure from compound identity. The review contains no attributable outcome for any of the 12 exact culinary Parkinson comparisons.
