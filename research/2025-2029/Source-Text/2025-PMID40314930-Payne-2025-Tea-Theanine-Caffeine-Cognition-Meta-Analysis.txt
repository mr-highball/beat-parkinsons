Title: Effects of Tea (Camellia sinensis) or its Bioactive Compounds l-Theanine or l-Theanine plus Caffeine on Cognition, Sleep, and Mood in Healthy Participants: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.
PMID: 40314930
PMCID: PMC12422004
DOI: 10.1093/nutrit/nuaf054
First published: 2025
Authors: Payne ER, Aceves-Martins M, Dubost J, Greyling A, de Roos B
Journal: Nutrition reviews
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12422004/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review of 50 RCTs (15 eligible for meta-analysis) evaluating Camellia sinensis tea, L-theanine, and caffeine on short-term human cognition and mood. Pooled L-theanine + caffeine interventions improved attention-switching task (AST) accuracy in hour 1 (SMD 0.40, p=0.004) and hour 2 (SMD 0.33, p=0.008) and self-reported alertness; underlying brewed black tea trials included De Bruin 2011 (n=26 and n=32) and Kahathuduwa 2017 (n=20). Limits: review/context only; mixes isolated compounds with brewed tea in non-PD adults.
