Title: Exogenous myrosinase from mustard seed increases bioavailability of sulforaphane from a glucoraphanin-rich broccoli seed extract in a randomized clinical study.
PMID: 41692762
PMCID: PMC12996352
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-39389-4
First published: 2026
Authors: Mastaloudis, Holcomb, Fahey
Journal: Scientific reports
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12996352/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized double-blind crossover trial in n=16 adults (9F/7M) comparing glucoraphanin (GR) in broccoli seed extract alone vs with mustard seed myrosinase (both with ascorbic acid). GR+myrosinase doubled sulforaphane bioavailability (39.8 +/- 3.1% vs 18.6 +/- 3.1% for GR alone) and accelerated 0-8 h conversion (25.4% vs 8.0%); four gut bacterial GR-converting genes correlated with conversion (p<0.0155). Quantifies heat-inactivated/gut-dependent vs myrosinase-active sulforaphane yield.
