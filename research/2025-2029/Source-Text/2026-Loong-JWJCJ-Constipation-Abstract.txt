Title: Gastrointestinal symptoms in Parkinson's disease treated in a controlled trial using traditional Chinese medicine (Jia-Wei-Ji-Chuan-Jian decoction) with network pharmacology analysis of active agents and mechanism of action
PMID: 42084228
DOI: 10.5414/CP204864
Publication: July 2026 issue. The first online date is not verified.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42084228/
Format: Factual extraction from the original author abstract. The full original was not accessible in this follow-through.
The report allocated 72 people with Parkinson constipation to two groups of 36 for five weeks. Both continued Parkinson medication. The comparison was Jia-Wei-Ji-Chuan-Jian decoction versus Huang-Xing Run-Chang tablets. The authors report constipation-score efficacy percentages of 88.57% versus 52.94% (p<.001), without defining the response or giving analysis denominators. Wording about score direction also needs the original scale and tables.
The abstract gives no itemized formula, registration identity or separable Perilla component. It cannot establish whether the tested formulation included shiso or provide a shiso-specific result. This is a distinct formulation from the 2017 Jiawei-Liujunzi Tang controlled trial.
