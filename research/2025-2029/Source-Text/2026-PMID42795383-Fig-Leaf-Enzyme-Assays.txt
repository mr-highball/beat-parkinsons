Coverage: Primary report, methods and enzyme assay outcomes, with docking results read separately.

The authors compared extracts of common fig leaves collected in two seasons. Experimental endpoints were extract chemistry, chemical antioxidant tests and inhibition of enzymes including acetylcholinesterase, butyrylcholinesterase and tyrosinase. They also modeled ligand binding and predicted pharmacokinetic and toxicity properties. Results identify activity in selected leaf preparations. These assays and predictions give no observed direction for any Parkinson symptom.
