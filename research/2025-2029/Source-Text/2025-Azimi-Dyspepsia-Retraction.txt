Title: Retraction Statement for the Persian medicine formula functional dyspepsia trial
Source: https://doi.org/10.1159/000544803
PMID: 40188809
DOI: 10.1159/000544803
First published: 2025-04-04
Coverage: Publication status and study linkage from the publisher notice.

The notice retracts the 2023 report PMID 36716715, DOI 10.1159/000529386. That report evaluated a celery-containing mixture in functional dyspepsia. The retracted results are excluded from the experiment's evidence synthesis. The bibliographic record remains available for provenance and search reconciliation.
