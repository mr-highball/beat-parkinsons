Title: Genetic risk and plasma biomarkers of dementia with Lewy bodies in a Chinese population
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12081849/
PMID: 40374660
PMCID: PMC12081849
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-00988-z
Publication date: 2025-05-15 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

A genetic and plasma biomarker study includes 151 participants with dementia with Lewy bodies and 2,010 controls. Genetic and blood markers are the comparative exposures. The paper supplies no individual fig intake or clinical episode change.
