Title: Regional gene expression and brain atrophy in dementia with Lewy bodies: an imaging transcriptomics study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13087014/
PMID: 41991538
PMCID: PMC13087014
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-026-01355-2
Publication date: 2026-04-16 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The imaging transcriptomics comparison uses data from 164 people with dementia with Lewy bodies and regional expression profiles. It models spatial relationships to brain atrophy. No meals or clinical visual episode rates are used as exposures or outcomes.
