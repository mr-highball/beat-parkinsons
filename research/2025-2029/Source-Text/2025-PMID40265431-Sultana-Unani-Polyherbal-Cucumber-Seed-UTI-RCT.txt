Title: A Randomized Comparative Study of Luteolin-Based Herbal Unani Formulations versus Nitrofurantoin in Women with Uncomplicated Urinary Tract Infections.
PMID: 40265431
DOI: 10.2174/0113816128389673250415101004
First published: 2025
Authors: Sultana A, Rahman K, Alam SS
Journal: Curr Pharm Des
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40265431/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized comparative clinical trial (Sultana et al., 2025) comparing a 5-herb Unani luteolin-based powder containing Cucumis sativus seeds (combined with Viola odorata syrup) against nitrofurantoin in patients with bacterial urinary tract infection. Critical limits: multi-herb polyformulation using cucumber seeds (not pickled cucumber flesh) for infectious UTI rather than Parkinson neurogenic urinary urgency.
