Music Attuned Technology Care for complex behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12046627/
PMID: 40317914
PMCID: PMC12046627
DOI: 10.1002/alz.70124
First publication: 2025-05 (earliest verified issue month)
Coverage: PMC original Methods, feasibility outcomes and scoring notes

This small pre/post dementia-ward feasibility study evaluates staff-supported music care. Fourteen participants began the intervention and twelve completed it. Pain is recorded as a baseline comorbidity. The NPI-Q frontal composite includes apathy/indifference alongside three other items, so a frontal total does not quantify apathy alone. No dried date fruit or date-associated Parkinson symptom comparison is reported.
