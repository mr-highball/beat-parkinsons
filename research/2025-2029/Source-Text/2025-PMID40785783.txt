Title: Personalized granola and Athens Insomnia Scale scores
First published or posted: 2025-07-25
PMID: 40785783
PMCID: PMC12332753
DOI: 10.3389/fmicb.2025.1607918
Source URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12332753/
Text type: original-source evidence summary

In a separate eight-week uncontrolled intervention, Sasaki and colleagues analyzed 93 of 99 assigned adults who consumed granola with individualized combinations of three toppings, plus milk. There was no significant overall Athens Insomnia Scale change. An exploratory microbiota subgroup of 19 had lower scores at eight weeks than baseline. The paper describes granola generally as containing oats and rye. The precise experimental recipe and oat form or dose remain unverified because the original supplementary recipe table was inaccessible. The study supplies no controlled oat or Parkinson estimate.
