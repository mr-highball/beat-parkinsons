Sun and colleagues studied 519 community-dwelling older adults. The original abstract associates weekly egg consumption with lower odds of osteosarcopenia (OR 0.713, 95% CI 0.564 to 0.900). Gait comparisons were reported by musculoskeletal diagnosis. The publisher supplement confirms dietary-frequency collection and gives no egg-by-gait estimate.
First online December 11, 2025. Twenty-seven participants had osteosarcopenia. The publisher supplement is an author response to review and describes 613 initial recruits in 2021. The gated full body may contain additional tables. The available material does not report gait by egg intake or specify the cooking method.
