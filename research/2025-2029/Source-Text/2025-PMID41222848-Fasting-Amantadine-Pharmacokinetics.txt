Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch original article JATS, with complete body.

Healthy participants received tested and reference formulations of amantadine under fasting conditions. The article discusses prior visual hallucination adverse events in clinical use, and its own comparisons concern drug exposure and safety. Fasting is a controlled condition and the paper has no dried fig or Parkinson hallucination comparison.
