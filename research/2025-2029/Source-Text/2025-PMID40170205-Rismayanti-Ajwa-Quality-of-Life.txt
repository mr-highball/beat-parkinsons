The two-group randomized report analyzes 44 people with schizophrenia. The comparison is added whole Ajwa dates alongside risperidone versus risperidone alone. The indexed publisher Methods place the study from May 2023 to March 2024 at Dadi Makassar Regional Mental Hospital. The author's abstract reports week-eight total WHOQOL-BREF scores of 69.3 versus 66.8 (p=.015). The groups had baseline differences in education and the physical quality-of-life domain. The other reported endpoint, serum TNF-α, is a biomarker. The published comparison supplies no Parkinson apathy measure, and separate apathy ratings are absent from the described protocol.

Related thesis: https://repository.unhas.ac.id/id/eprint/48781/
