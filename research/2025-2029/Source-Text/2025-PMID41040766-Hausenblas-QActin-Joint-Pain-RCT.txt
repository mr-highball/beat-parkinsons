Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (Hausenblas et al., 2025, PMC12486685, NCT06246383, n=80 US adults with mild-to-moderate joint pain: 42 Q-Actin 20 mg/day vs 38 rice-protein placebo for 60 days; partially funded by Gateway Health Alliances). Primary WOMAC pain/function showed a non-significant Condition effect (p=0.06) and non-significant Group x Time interaction (p=0.13); Lequesne Functional Index interaction was null (p=0.13; placebo actually lower at Day 60, p=0.04); Pain Disability Index was null (p=0.56); BPI pain severity Condition p=0.05 / Interaction p=0.07; only BPI pain interference reached marginal Interaction significance (p=0.05) amid substantial baseline imbalance (baseline WOMAC 30.52 Q-Actin vs 19.11 placebo). Crucial independent competing/mixed trial tempering Nash et al. 2018/2023.
