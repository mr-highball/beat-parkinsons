The publisher identifies this volume as the proceedings of the
2024 American Society for Neurochemistry meeting, published in 2025.
The official JATS download includes 186 numbered abstract or body
members. The corresponding archive PDF has 224 physical pages.
The short archive text presents the contents list.

In P01.025, a 20-animal cohort of aged female mice used peanut oil
as an operant reward in an experiment on B-cell depletion after
prefrontal stroke. These mice averaged 26 trials per autoshaping
session. The separate 13-animal aged female cohort used diluted
strawberry milkshake. The conference abstract describes it as
ongoing and supplies no results for that cohort. Its young-mouse
reference (PMID 36564387) used milkshake to measure cue responses.

The same investigators' 2024 Society for Neuroscience poster
PSTR397.23/D3 reports a pretreatment comparison of the two distinct
reward products. The surviving project and cohort details suggest
some overlap, so the conference reports are linked as one likely
lineage. Their different models, products and endpoints do not
establish a raw fruit or a cold-pressed peanut oil effect on
Parkinson symptoms.
