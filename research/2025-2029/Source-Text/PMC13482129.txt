Title: GABAergic dysfunction in Parkinson's disease: insights from in vivo proton magnetic resonance spectroscopy

The systematic review examines GABA-related magnetic resonance spectroscopy across 22 Parkinson studies. Regional neurochemical differences are distinct from the effect of any ingested food on clinical visual episodes.
