Ghannadi and colleagues, original indexed report

Original source https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41604557/

First online October 30, 2025 according to the NCBI article date. The study randomized 70 children to sulforaphane or placebo for eight weeks alongside methylphenidate. Thirty-two and 31 completed the two groups. The primary abstract reports favorable parent- and teacher-rated inattention results, with Cohen d of 1.446 and 1.055. The public abstract does not establish the botanical source, preparation, raw broccoli exposure or a Parkinson comparison. Original full methods and trial accession remain open.
