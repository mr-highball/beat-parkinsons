Title: DBS-STN&SNr combined stimulation versus DBS-STN monotarget stimulation for Parkinson's disease freezing: A comparative efficacy study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40760816/
PMID: 40760816
PMCID: PMC13347519
DOI: 10.1177/1877718X251359241
First public: 2025-08-04
Coverage: Factual extract from the original methods and results.

The multicenter crossover assigned participants with Parkinson disease and freezing of gait to conditions using subthalamic stimulation alone or combined subthalamic and substantia nigra stimulation. The reported freezing and quality-of-life scores favored combined stimulation. The treatment comparison is between stimulation settings. Freezing of gait differs from observed fall frequency, and the study did not assign sage or identify a culinary rigidity effect.
