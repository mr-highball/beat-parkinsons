Title: Short-Term Beetroot Juice Supplementation Enhances Strength, Reduces Fatigue, and Promotes Recovery in Physically Active Individuals: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Crossover Trial.
PMID: 40431460
PMCID: PMC12113839
DOI: 10.3390/nu17101720
First published: 2025
Authors: Salem A, Ammar A, Kerkeni M, Boujelbane MA, Uyar AM, Köbel LM, et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40431460/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=12 adults: beetroot juice significantly attenuated delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS) pain after eccentric upper-limb exercise vs placebo (p<0.05). Limits: small non-PD sample.
