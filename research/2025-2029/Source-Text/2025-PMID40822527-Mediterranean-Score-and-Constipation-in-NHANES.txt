Original source scope
This cross-sectional NHANES analysis includes 11,612 adults. Higher aMED scores correlate with lower odds of hard stool-classified constipation, including OR 0.82 (95% CI 0.75–0.89) per point in the fully adjusted model.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The fat component is the dietary monounsaturated-to-saturated fat ratio, which can reflect multiple foods. The analysis does not assign or distinguish extra-virgin oil, or estimate symptom response in Parkinson disease.
