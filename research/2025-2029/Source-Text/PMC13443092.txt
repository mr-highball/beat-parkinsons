Title: Agreement between relatives of Parkinson's patients and clinical observer in home diary assessments

Home diary measurement study in 28 Parkinson participant-relative pairs. Off-dose dystonia was recorded at baseline in 17. The diary comparison measured agreement about motor states, including Off and On with or without dyskinesia. Relatives and clinician raters showed fair temporal agreement (Cohen kappa 0.250), while participant and clinician rater agreement was slight (0.120). The study does not compare dystonia severity over a dietary intervention, measure fatigue separately, or give participants sage.
