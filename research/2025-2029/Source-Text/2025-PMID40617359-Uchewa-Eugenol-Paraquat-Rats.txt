Title: Evaluating the impact of eugenol on paraquat-altered motor, anxiety, memory, and cognitive abilities in Wistar rats.
PMID: 40617359
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2025.120223
First published: 2025
Authors: Uchewa OO, Ibegbu AO, Alobu EJ, Agbo JO, Uduak IA, Okposhi FT, Osu FO, Nwaji AR, Okoronkwo SO
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40617359/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical paraquat rat study (40 male Wistar rats, 5 groups of n=8: control, paraquat 10 mg/kg, paraquat + eugenol 200, 400, or 600 mg/kg for 14 days) assessing open-field line crossings, T-maze, novel object recognition, brain acetylcholine, oxidative stress, alpha-synuclein, and tau. Authors frame eugenol specifically as 90-98% of clove (Syzygium aromaticum), not Pimenta dioica ground allspice. Reported increased open-field line crossings (p < 0.05) and improved T-maze/NOR/fear outcomes versus paraquat-untreated rats; cohort design (40 male Wistar rats, identical doses and AEFUNAI animal house) overlaps with Uchewa 2025 cerebellar gene-expression paper (PMC11734554), leaving independence uncertain.
