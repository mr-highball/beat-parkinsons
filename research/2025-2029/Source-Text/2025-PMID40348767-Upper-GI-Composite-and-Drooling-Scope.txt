Title: The profile of gastrointestinal dysfunction in prodromal to late-stage Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12065915/
PMID: 40348767
PMCID: PMC12065915
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-00900-9
Publication date: 2025
Coverage: Paraphrased primary article Methods, Results and longitudinal comparisons.

The study enrolled 404 people with Parkinson disease, 37 with isolated REM sleep behavior disorder and 105 controls. Its upper gastrointestinal domain groups sialorrhea with dysphagia, heartburn and nausea. Model results on disease stage concern that domain score. Over repeated follow-up the upper gastrointestinal score did not show a significant time effect in the published model. An ordinal symptom item, a domain score and whole-gut transit are separate outcomes. The published comparisons supply no raw-fig exposure or separately estimated change in drooling from diet.
