Reviewed: 2026-11-26

The systematic review selected 58 reports from 4,132 database records. It searched through July 31, 2026. It surveyed heterogeneous studies of spicy eating, mood and related features, including potential bidirectional relationships. It recaps the 2022 Zhang cohort and repeats the abstract denominator of 1,771. This is a secondary record for the same study and gives no independent ground-cayenne and Parkinson anxiety comparison.
