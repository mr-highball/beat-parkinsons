Title: Almond for mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease: A randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 42011351
PMCID: PMC13092157
DOI: 10.22038/ajp.2025.26297
First published: 2026
Authors: Mohajeri, Hashem-Dabaghian, Raeesi, Mehroozade
Journal: Avicenna journal of phytomedicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42011351/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-month open-label parallel RCT in 60 adults with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease (30/arm completed, mean age ~71.6 y) randomized to 10 g/d powdered sweet almond plus 1 teaspoon powdered rock candy alongside usual prescriptions vs usual prescriptions alone. Table 2 showed between-group improvement favoring almond+candy for MoCA total (23.86 -> 24.46 vs 22.3 -> 22.3, p = 0.001), MoCA memory (3.23 -> 3.73 vs 3.03 -> 3.16, p = 0.005), MMSE orientation (p = 0.024), FAST staging (p = 0.022), and PSQI sleep quality percent change (10.89% vs 1.66%, p < 0.001). MoCA attention (5.26 -> 5.20 vs 4.86 -> 4.83, p = 0.776) and MoCA visuospatial/visual scale (2.56 -> 2.56 vs 2.60 -> 2.56, p = 0.334) did not differ. Limits: open-label no placebo, baseline MMSE/MoCA imbalance, co-administered with rock candy, non-PD Alzheimer's cohort.
