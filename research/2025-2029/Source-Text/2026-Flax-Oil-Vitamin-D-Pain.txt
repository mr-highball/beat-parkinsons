Factual Table 7 summary. Older adults with type 2 diabetes received flaxseed oil plus vitamin D or placebo. Median SF-36 bodily-pain domain scores changed from 32.50 to 25.00 and from 78.75 to 55.00, respectively. Higher scores indicate better health. Large baseline differences, ambiguous analysis labels and combined treatment undermine attribution.
