Title: The Mild Behavioral Impairment Checklist for Parkinson's Disease: An Ancillary Instrument in Cognitive Assessment
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12839493/
PMID: 40755134
PMCID: PMC12839493
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70243
Publication date: 2025-08-04 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The Canadian study assesses the Mild Behavioral Impairment Checklist in Parkinson and compares model structures and agreement with other clinical ratings. Visual and auditory items are separable. The authors report instability in the psychosis domain, and the design does not test any food.
