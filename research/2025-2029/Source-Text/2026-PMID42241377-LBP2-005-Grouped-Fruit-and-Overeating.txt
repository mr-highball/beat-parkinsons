The cross-sectional poster describes 120 women recruited from
nutrition counseling centers. In Table 1, 4 of 39 in the food
addiction group and 2 of 81 in the other group listed the
combined strawberry, cherry or grape category among foods
associated with overeating. The table prints test statistic 1.921
and p=.087 for that row. The pairing of these numbers is not
explained in the poster.

The row does not separate the three fruits or their preparation.
The comparison concerns self-report about overeating, with no
randomized intake, fall-frequency measurement or Parkinson stratum.
