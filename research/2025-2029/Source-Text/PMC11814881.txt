Title: CURRENT STATUS AND PROBLEMS OF NEUROIMAGING-GUIDED DIAGNOSIS USING NEAR-INFRARED SPECTROSCOPY IN JAPAN
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11814881/
PMCID: PMC11814881
DOI: 10.1093/ijnp/pyae059.316
Publication date: 2025-02-12 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML abstract

The complete meeting abstract reports retrospective near-infrared prefrontal measurements during verbal fluency in patients with bipolar, depressive or schizophrenia-spectrum disorders. Oxygenated hemoglobin traces are the measured outcome. The abstract contains no Parkinson cohort or fig exposure.
