Schönenberger KA et al. J Int Soc Sports Nutr. October 3, 2025. PMID 41040018. DOI 10.1080/15502783.2025.2568048. NCT04946981.

The randomized crossover analyzed 44 moderately active adults receiving a proprietary turmeric extract capsule or placebo around downhill running. The extract suspension included quillaja extract, sunflower oil and acacia gum. The primary 72-hour soreness area under the curve after squatting was inconclusive. A post hoc contrast of improvement from maximum soreness to 72 hours favored the formulation by 10.7 percentage points in men and 7.9 overall. Reported models omitted the covariate measured after the first product dose. The trial was funded by Givaudan France Naturals. It does not estimate ground turmeric effects on Parkinson pain. Conference abstract OAP36_02 and the registry describe the same cohort.
