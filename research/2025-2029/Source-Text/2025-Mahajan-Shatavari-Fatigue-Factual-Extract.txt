Title: Efficacy and Safety of Shatavari (Asparagus racemosus) Root Extract for Perimenopause: Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12593836/
Identifiers: PMID 41209045, DOI 10.2147/IJWH.S544267
First published: November 3, 2025
Coverage: Factual extract from original Methods and Table 3.

Eighty perimenopausal women were randomized to 300 mg/day of standardized Asparagus racemosus root extract or starch. Seventy-three completed for the per-protocol analysis (37 and 36). Profile of Mood States fatigue changes were −0.49 and −0.97 at week four (p = .579), and −2.49 and −0.72 at week eight (p = .019). This is a secondary outcome in a study of a particular root extract. It provides no result for roasted Asparagus officinalis shoots or a Parkinson cohort. The paper cites registration CTRI/2024/09/074239.
