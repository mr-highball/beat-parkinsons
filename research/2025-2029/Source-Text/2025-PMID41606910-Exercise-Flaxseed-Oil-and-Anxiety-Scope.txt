Title: Interaction between exercise and flaxseed oil intake on hippocampal function of obese mice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41606910/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0963996925014206
PMID: 41606910
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2025.117082
Publication date: 2025-08-05
Source depth: Original author abstract and publication metadata.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27

The authors studied exercise and flaxseed oil in a diet-induced mouse obesity model. Their abstract describes a small improvement in anxiety-related measures for the exercise-plus-oil condition without improvement in hippocampal function. The abstract does not provide quantitative estimates for the oil-only arm, a test-specific contrast or the pressing grade. The original tables were not recovered.

This contextual finding concerns a bundled intervention and an animal behavioral proxy. It provides no attributable oil-only response in human Parkinson anxiety.
