Title: Serotonergic dysfunction in patients with impulse control disorders in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12129733/
PMID: 40042882
PMCID: PMC12129733
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awaf087
Publication date: 2025-03-05
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC article XML and included tables; supplementary files are separate.

PET case-control study of 30 Parkinson participants with or without an impulse-control disorder and 15 healthy controls. The study assessed serotonin transporter and 5-HT2A receptor binding. The clinical evaluation included MDS-UPDRS and the Starkstein Apathy Scale. Three participants with an impulse-control disorder reached the study's apathy threshold. The analysis did not demonstrate an association of the reported transporter findings with mild or moderate apathy, anxiety or depression.

Appendices are listed separately. The primary paper is an imaging and behavior comparison. Figure abbreviations do not identify edible figs. No fresh fig or date exposure was compared, and no figure or receptor signal can establish an effect on rest tremor.
