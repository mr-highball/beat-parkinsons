Title: Clinical efficacy and patient-reported outcomes of coconut oil as adjunctive periodontitis treatment: triple-blinded pilot randomized trial.
PMID: 42057009
PMCID: PMC13188242
DOI: 10.1186/s12903-026-08411-6
Registry ID: NCT06049589
First published: 2026
Authors: Pardiñas López S, Khouly I, Aellos F, Alccayhuaman KA, Díaz Prado SM
Journal: BMC oral health
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13188242/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Triple-blind randomized pilot clinical trial (Pardinas Lopez et al., 2026, BMC Oral Health, PMC13188242; NCT06049589) in 30 adults with stage II-III periodontitis assigned to coconut oil mouth rinse, 0.12% chlorhexidine, or placebo rinse for 1 month before periodontal therapy. Coconut oil mouth rinse improved plaque, bleeding, and probing metrics and significantly reduced mouth-dryness perception (p<0.001) and OHIP-14 quality of life (p=0.024) without chlorhexidine burning/staining, though placebo was preferred for taste. Limits: non-ingested oral rinse relieving dry mouth (opposite of Parkinson drooling/sialorrhea) in non-PD periodontitis.
