Rosemary extract trial and constipation surveillance
https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07541729
selected public registry and protocol fields

First posted April 21, 2026. Active, no longer recruiting. Oral methanolic rosemary extract is compared with placebo in type 2 diabetes. Protocol Annex 4 asks a combined adverse-event question about diarrhea or constipation. This neither assigns ground culinary rosemary nor uses a dedicated constipation severity measure. No results are posted. The protocol and current registry should be reconciled for duration and dosing at results review.
