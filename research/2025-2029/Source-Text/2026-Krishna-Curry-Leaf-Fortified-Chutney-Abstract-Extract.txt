Ground curry leaf chutney tablets with added ferrous sulfate
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42860708/
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

A food engineering paper prepared three powder-based chutney tablet formulations including a curry-leaf product. The study added ferrous sulfate. Across formulations the authors report 2.04–3.96 g iron per kg and a cell ferritin-based in vitro availability comparison. Release time, tablet properties and cell assays were measured. No trial combined a curry-leaf tablet with levodopa, and the added pharmaceutical iron makes this a different exposure from ordinary ground leaves.
