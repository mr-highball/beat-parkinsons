Title: The role of quantitative susceptibility mapping in dementia with Lewy Bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13186079/
PMID: 42105648
PMCID: PMC13186079
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2026.104000
Publication date: 2026-05-01 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Quantitative susceptibility MRI of 39 people with dementia with Lewy bodies and 30 controls compares tissue signal as an indirect marker of regional iron. Phenotype classification and image-derived measures do not establish dietary iron or fig exposure or visual event frequency.
