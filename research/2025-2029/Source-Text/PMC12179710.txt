Title: Narcolepsy and psychiatric comorbidity: a review of the literature
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12179710/
PMID: 40546276
PMCID: PMC12179710
DOI: 10.1016/j.ijchp.2025.100591
Publication date: 2025-06-09 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The literature review distinguishes sleep-transition hallucinations and psychiatric comorbidities in narcolepsy cohorts. It describes a separate sleep disorder and does not measure Parkinson visual episode frequency after fig consumption.
