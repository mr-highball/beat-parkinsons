Figure 2 reports better short-distance climbing after the brown-rice feed than with iron alone. The reported threshold is p < .05 and the caption gives three replicates of 20 flies. Methods describe 6 cm in six seconds, while the figure axis describes 8 cm. The report uses 1% brown rice in experimental feed and does not establish a clinical postural-instability outcome.

The original describes five groups and a ten-day iron exposure followed by five days of the assigned diets. The assay measures negative geotaxis. It differs from a human balance or pull test.
The authors, setting, exposure schedule, arms, and endpoints overlap closely with PMID 39588846. The related report can clarify the program, but no participant-level linkage or proof of independent replication is available. Treat this article as an associated report within the existing family.
