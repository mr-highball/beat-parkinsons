Figure S2 and Table S4 show cumulative MDS-UPDRS Part II results from a PPMI Non-Online analysis. Table S4 reports β −0.41, SE .18, p .026 for black tea. The table caption calls the population LRRK2-PD, while the row header labels iPD and gives 11 exposed and 25 unexposed. The article narrative reports LRRK2-PD n=39. Explanation of the header and denominator difference is unavailable. Table S3C reports β −0.53, SE .23, p .028 after adjusting the Fox Insight LRRK2 analysis for sex. The models contain a black-tea main effect and give no tea-by-assessment-time term or symptom-specific coefficients.
