Analytical LC-QToF mass spectrometry study (Avula et al., 2025, Foods, PMC11854323) profiling free proteinogenic and non-proteinogenic amino acids across 22 legume species (Table 4). L-DOPA was detected in only 3 of 22 species (Vicia faba [High], Mucuna pruriens [High], Mimosa pudica [Low]) and was UNDETECTED (-) in both Vigna unguiculata (black-eyed pea/cowpea) and Vigna angularis (adzuki bean), whereas GABA was detected across all 22 species. Refutes older secondary assumptions that black-eyed peas serve as a dietary L-DOPA source comparable to fava bean or Mucuna pruriens.
