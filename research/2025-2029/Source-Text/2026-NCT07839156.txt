Title: Oat-milk products, cow dairy and sleep in toddlers
First published or posted: 2026-09-24
Registration: NCT07839156
Source URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07839156
Text type: original-source evidence summary

First posted September 24, 2026. This recruiting study compares oat-milk products with cow dairy over twelve months in toddlers. Planned sleep measures use parent questionnaires, actigraphy and diaries. A product description is inconsistent with its assigned group label, leaving some formulation uncertain. No results were posted at the November 17, 2026 check. Its population and beverage preparation differ from adults with Parkinson's eating rolled oats.
