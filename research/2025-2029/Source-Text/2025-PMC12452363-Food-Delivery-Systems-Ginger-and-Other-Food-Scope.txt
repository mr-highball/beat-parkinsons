The review describes ginger-derived delivery material in experimental intestinal inflammation and distinct active payloads. The orange-to-yellow transition concerns a food freshness indicator. Shrimp is the food whose freshness is measured there. Neither color nor food-storage measures establish a human dietary comparison. The functional constipation introduction does not identify a ginger-only treatment effect.
