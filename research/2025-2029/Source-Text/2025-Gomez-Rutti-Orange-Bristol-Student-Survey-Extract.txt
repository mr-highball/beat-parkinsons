Orange frequency and stool form in a Lima student survey
Source: https://revistaold.nutricion.org/index.php/ncdh/article/download/872/601
DOI: 10.12873/451gomez-rutti
Coverage: Original article Methods and Tables 3 and 5

The publisher dates this article to April 2, 2025. Gómez Rutti and colleagues report a cross-sectional survey of 1,144 health-science students aged 18–42. Participants with diagnosed constipation or diarrhea were excluded. Table 3 gives orange frequency marginals and a stool form association (p=.001), without the paired cells needed to infer an orange-specific direction. Table 5 reports stool form by pooled fruit portions. The first author advised the separate 100-adult thesis. The reported methods, sample sizes and author lists differ, while participant overlap remains unknown. Clinical Parkinson constipation severity and orange preparation were outside this study.
