Title: Whole cranberry fruit powder supplement reduces the incidence of culture-confirmed urinary tract infections in females with a history of recurrent urinary tract infection: A 6-month multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 39863114
PMCID: PMC12002188
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2025.01.022
Registry ID: NCT03042273
First published: 2025
Authors: Stonehouse W, Benassi-Evans B, Bednarz J, Vincent AD
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12002188/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Multicenter 6-month double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial (Stonehouse et al., 2025, Am J Clin Nutr, PMC12002188; NCT03042273) enrolling 150 healthy adult women (ages 18-65) with recurrent UTI history randomized to 500 mg/day whole cranberry fruit powder capsule (Pacran) vs placebo. Whole cranberry powder reduced culture-confirmed UTI risk by 52% (adjusted RR 0.48, 95% CI 0.26-0.87, p=0.01), specifically reduced incidence of UTI with urinary frequency and urgency symptomatology (RR 0.29, 95% CI 0.13-0.63, p<0.01), delayed first UTI (HR 0.36, p=0.01), and reduced total UTIs per participant (IRR 0.41, p=0.01). Symptomatic suspected UTIs without culture and culture-confirmed dysuria did NOT differ significantly between arms; no safety concerns. Limits: infection-related urgency in healthy women with rUTI rather than non-infectious Parkinson neurogenic detrusor overactivity.
