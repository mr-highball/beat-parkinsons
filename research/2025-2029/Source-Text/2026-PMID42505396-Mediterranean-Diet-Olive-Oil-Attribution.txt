Original study scope
This review discusses Mediterranean dietary patterns, isolated olive constituents and related neurodegenerative studies. Olive oil is often one component of a combined diet and laboratory studies assess different interventions.

Continuing evidence gaps
The named diet and mechanistic observations do not isolate separately prepared olive oil effects on Parkinson bradykinesia, constipation or postural instability.
