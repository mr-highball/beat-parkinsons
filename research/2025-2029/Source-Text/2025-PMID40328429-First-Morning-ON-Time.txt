Title: Efficacy of Levodopa/Benserazide Dispersible Tablets on Delayed ON to the First Morning Dose in Patients With Parkinson Disease With Motor Fluctuations

Forty Parkinson participants with delayed first-morning ON were randomized in an open-label two-period crossover of dispersible versus regular levodopa/benserazide tablets. Nine dropped out. The reported mean changes from baseline in time to ON after the first-morning dose were −34.72 minutes with the dispersible tablets and −23.81 minutes with the regular tablets. The model comparing treatment while accounting for possible period effects reports an 11.72-minute advantage in time to ON with p=.014.
A change in latency after the first morning tablet differs from total waking-day OFF duration. The trial assigns two medication formulations and supplies no refined soybean oil exposure, isolated food effect or daily OFF comparison for oil.
