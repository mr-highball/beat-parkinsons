Publication: Li et al., Scientific Reports, July 11, 2025. DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-10821-5. PMID 40646117, PMC12254499.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12254499/?report=classic
Coverage: Original paper and publisher supplement, factual extract.

In a randomized comparison of 30 participants with Parkinson disease and low vitamin D, isolated D3 was compared with unspecified vegetable oil. Table 3 gives mean UPDRS-III changes of −4.27 (SD 3.77) and +0.40 (SD 1.84), respectively, p = .001. The original supplement corroborates the placebo group's 34.00 baseline and 34.40 follow-up, while Table 2 gives a stray minus sign before the baseline value. The comparison provides no separate bradykinesia estimate. The article and supplement do not report between-arm treatment effects for the registry-listed anxiety and depression scales. The reported mood measures concern baseline observational analyses. The intervention was a nutrient supplement without a source food.
