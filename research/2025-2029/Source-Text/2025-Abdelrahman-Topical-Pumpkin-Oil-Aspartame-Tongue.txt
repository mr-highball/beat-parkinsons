Topical pumpkin seed oil and oral tissue in aspartame exposed rats

DOI: 10.21608/erurj.2025.371772.1246
Coverage: factual extract from primary Methods and histology

Egyptian Russian University Research Journal 4(4), 2025. DOI 10.21608/erurj.2025.371772.1246. Accepted October 28, 2025.
Thirty male rats were randomized to control, oral aspartame, or oral aspartame plus topical pumpkin seed oil for four weeks. The source describes structural tongue outcomes under histology. It reports less damage in the co-treatment arm. A mention of salivary-gland changes in the discussion cites a separate aspartame study rather than measuring saliva in the pumpkin oil experiment. No serum, saliva or clinical data are inferred from the tissue appearance. The route was topical, and no roasting or cold pressing was verified.
