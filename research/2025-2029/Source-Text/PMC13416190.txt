Title: Peripheral and central inflammation associated with progressive cognitive decline in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13416190/
PMID: 42529064
PMCID: PMC13416190
DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcag274
Publication date: 2026-07-14 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The NIMROD study relates inflammatory markers and PET measures to cognitive trajectories in dementia with Lewy bodies. Baseline inflammation and cognitive decline are measured without attributing them to dried figs, and the study does not count visual episodes after intake.
