Chemotherapy kefir thesis and physical symptom burden
Source: https://acikerisim.sbu.edu.tr/yayinaea/yrd_20-05-2025__05-25_891992.pdf
Registration: NCT06517420
Coverage: Indexed original institutional thesis, pp 32–33, tables 4.3–4.4, appendix p 103

The thesis bears a 2024 date. The earliest located institutional public file is dated in its path May 2025, with no earlier verified public release. Öztürk Çetin reports 84 chemotherapy patients divided evenly between usual care and kefir. The product uses pasteurized cow milk, lactase, B. lactis and kefir culture, with flavor unspecified. The intervention had more in-person visits and telephone reminders. The physical symptom domain includes one tiredness item, with no separate item outcome. Table 4.4 gives baseline means of 0.47 control and 0.50 kefir. At cycle four the means were 1.16 ± 0.48 and 0.53 ± 0.25, p=.002, with higher burden worse. Those values are for physical symptoms together. Table 4.3 prints an intervention total symptom mean of 1.16, whereas Table 4.4 gives 0.63 ± 0.34. The directly downloaded file returned HTTP 500 during verification, while the indexed PDF pages were accessible for the checks above.
