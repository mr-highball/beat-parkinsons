Radiomic profiling and delirium after deep brain stimulation in Parkinson disease

In this radiomics pilot, 7 of 34 Parkinson participants developed postoperative delirium after subthalamic deep brain stimulation. The comparison concerns imaging features and delirium status. Resting tremor is part of the description of Parkinson disease, and apathy appears in the discussion of potential psychiatric complications. Fig labels identify illustrations. The study provides no raw fig or dried date intake comparison.
