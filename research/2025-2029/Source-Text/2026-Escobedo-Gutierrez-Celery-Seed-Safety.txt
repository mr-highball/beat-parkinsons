Title: Effect of Celery Seed (Apium graveolens L.) Administration on the Components of Metabolic Syndrome, Insulin Sensitivity, and Insulin Secretion: A Clinical Trial
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8247/19/1/110
PMID: 41599709
PMCID: PMC12845499
DOI: 10.3390/ph19010110
First published: 2026-01-07
Coverage: Factual extract of original Methods and section 2.3.

The 28-person randomized trial assigned 14 participants per arm to a standardized celery seed extract or calcined magnesia placebo for 12 weeks. The seed preparation was labeled 85% butylphthalide. Its registration is NCT06061926. The population had metabolic syndrome.
Participants kept adverse-event diaries and were interviewed during follow-up. The active group reported diarrhea and abdominal distension, each 7.1%. The placebo group reported headache in 21.4%, and nausea, diarrhea, abdominal distension and constipation each in 7.1%. With 14 people per arm these percentages represent one or three participants by event category. The article reports mild events and no clear incidence difference. A hallucination-specific assessment and an isolated leaf intervention were outside the report.
