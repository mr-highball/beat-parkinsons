Original source scope
The major depression network meta-analysis reports a saffron placebo SMD of minus 0.87 (95% CI minus 1.37 to minus 0.37) in the unrestricted depressive-symptom network. The network had high heterogeneity and global inconsistency. The paper also reports a separate anxiety comparison based on one trial.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The network pools prior trials in major depressive disorder across formulations and does not supply a new independent Parkinson ground-stigma cohort. The anxiety outcome is separate from depression and insomnia. No dried date fruit pain comparison is reported.

Sensitivity analysis
The unrestricted depression network estimate for saffron favors lower symptom severity. Saffron was not among the confirmed results in the reported restricted depression analyses. A separate low-risk-of-bias analysis favored saffron for anxiety in major depression. Its single-study comparison cannot substitute for the depression or insomnia endpoint.
