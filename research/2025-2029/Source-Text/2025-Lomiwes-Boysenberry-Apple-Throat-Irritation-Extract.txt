Title: The dose-dependent effect of acute ozone exposure on lung function and the efficacy of a Boysenberry apple powder blend (BerriQi) in reducing throat irritation and symptom recovery time in healthy adults
Authors: Dominic Lomiwes and colleagues
Author institutional record of primary paper: https://mro.massey.ac.nz/items/e9f401fe-c9e8-4921-a0a1-cc5f7aac5302
Publisher record: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2773049225000157
Identifiers: DOI 10.1016/j.heha.2025.100132
Date: September 2025 issue
Coverage: factual extract of the original abstract, prepared 2026-11-14

The investigators conducted an initial spirometry assessment and a separate, randomized double-blind crossover comparison in 20 healthy adults. In the crossover the treatments were boysenberry plus apple powder and placebo for five days before a chamber visit. Recorded symptoms included throat irritation, soreness of coughing, shortness of breath and pain on deep inspiration, sampled up to 48 hours. The abstract reports decreased throat irritation with the blend at p=0.04. It gives no acoustic speech recording or individual loudness endpoint, verified administered dose or comparator ingredients. The age and disease context differ from Parkinson-associated hypophonia. The institutional record exposes the abstract and the publisher blocked body retrieval through the available routes.
