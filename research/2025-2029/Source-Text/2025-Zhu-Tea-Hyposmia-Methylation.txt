Title: Characterization of methylation profile in biofluid cell-free DNA and identification of differentially methylated genes for phenotypic representations in Parkinson's disease
Authors: Aiqin Zhu, Fenglin Wang, Jie Meng and colleagues
Publication: Clinical Neurology and Neurosurgery 256 (2025), 109014; online 10 June 2025
PMID: 40527220
DOI: 10.1016/j.clineuro.2025.109014
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40527220/
Coverage: Indexed author abstract only. Full Methods and tables not verified.

The study recruited 20 Parkinson patients and nine healthy participants, and analyzed cell-free DNA methylation in cerebrospinal fluid and/or blood. The abstract lists tea consumption and hyposmia among distinct phenotypes correlated with CSF methylation signatures; it also names both among candidate blood diagnostic phenotypes. It supplies no direct tea-versus-hyposmia estimate, tea type or preparation, smell-assessment method or symptom-specific change. The small study was not a tea intervention. Whether additional tea-versus-olfaction data exist in its inaccessible full tables is unresolved.

This study differs in described setting and design from the 2024 Zhang GBA1 intrafamilial multicenter survey, PMID 39581168. The abstracts do not establish or exclude participant overlap.
