Title: Empowering Patients: The Role of Group Health Coaching in Improving Self-Efficacy and Well-Being in Parkinson's Disease

PMCID: PMC12971501

Text coverage: Original PMC XML article body and embedded tables inspected. Factual extract. Separately distributed supplements are outside this extract.

A mixed-methods Parkinson group-coaching program used self-efficacy and positive affect or well-being scales. Seventeen participants responded before the intervention and twelve afterwards. Participants described some changes in nutrition and exercise. Fatigue and apathy appear as background barriers to activity, with no separate measured fatigue or apathy result and no sage intervention.
