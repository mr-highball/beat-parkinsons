Pearled barley in a low glycemic home dinner

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11848139/
Coverage: Original Table 3 and home-meal results, factual extract.

Farrow and colleagues, Topics in Spinal Cord Injury Rehabilitation, February 14, 2025. DOI 10.46292/sci24-00044. Eleven adults with chronic spinal cord injury were randomized in a crossover comparison. Table 3 lists pearled barley as a component of the low-glycemic home dinner alongside black and pinto beans and other ingredients, without its preparation method. In the home comparison, maximal post-meal systolic decline showed no clear diet effect (p = .994), with nine participants and incomplete measurements across meals. The laboratory breakfast contained no barley and showed a separate 14 ± 12 versus 24 ± 25 mm Hg maximum decline (p = .056). This does not estimate a barley effect or test orthostatic symptoms in Parkinson disease.
