Cucurbita extract and rotarod in a demyelination model
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40254703/
PMID: 40254703
DOI: 10.1007/s12035-025-04934-1
Coverage: factual extract from primary abstract

Neuroprotective Effects of Cucurbita pepo Extract in Cuprizone-Induced Multiple Sclerosis Animal Model. Molecular Neurobiology, first online April 20, 2025.
Rats were assigned to normal saline, cuprizone, or two extract arms under cuprizone for five weeks. The abstract reports better rotarod motor coordination and separate forced-swim and elevated-plus-maze outcomes for treated animals. It calls the exposure a Cucurbita pepo extract without identifying it as a seed oil or verifying cold pressing. Exact plant part and separate motor means require full Methods and Figures. Whole-body motor coordination in a model of demyelination differs from hand dexterity or micrographia in Parkinson’s.
