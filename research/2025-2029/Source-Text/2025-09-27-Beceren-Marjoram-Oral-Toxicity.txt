Acute oral marjoram extract and behavioral observation in rats
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/1422-0067/26/19/9461
PMID: 41096727
PMCID: PMC12524804
DOI: 10.3390/ijms26199461
Coverage: Full original article, species voucher, methods and acute toxicity results

Beceren and colleagues studied authenticated Origanum majorana aerial material extracted in methanol after drying. One rat died during the 14-day main observation after the higher tested oral exposure. There were no reported deaths at the lower main-study exposure. The authors reported no overt behavioral abnormalities in surviving treated rats. Observation of overt toxicity cannot measure self-reported hallucinations. The experiment supplies no human exposure estimate or result for fresh leaves in Parkinson disease.
