The dissertation confirms 124 assessed, 62 enrolled, 11 discontinued and 51 analyzed at eight weeks. Figure 2 records 39 screening exclusions and 23 people declining participation before enrollment. Table 4 gives 11 men, 40 women and mean age 49.74 years. It cites the 2023 four-week paper (DOI 10.7759/cureus.46061) as preliminary. These descriptors match the 2023 paper and the 2025 quality-of-life publication. Figure 3 displays approximately 66.6% reporting epigastric pain initially and 31.4% at eight weeks. There is no placebo group and the figure gives frequency rather than intensity. No matched participant-level course is available.

Table 5 repeats the 2025 quality-of-life paper's baseline 19.07, week-eight 9.10, mean change −9.97 and p=.039. Its Tables 12–13 also repeat the 47-person adverse-effect counts and tolerance results from PMID 40474983. The thesis registers the study under NCT06313814 and describes eight-week short-form NDI and InBody 720. The registry describes four-week endpoints with NDI, InBody 270 and EQ-5D. These are measured and planned fields from the same program, with different timepoints and instruments. Link the results without treating each publication as an independent trial. None evaluates grated ginger in people with Parkinson disease.
