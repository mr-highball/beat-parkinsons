Study design and findings
- Prospective UK Biobank cohort analysis (N = 123,655; 796 incident hospital-recorded PD cases) evaluating a composite flavonoid diet score (FDS).
- Higher FDS was associated with lower incident PD (Q4 vs Q1 HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.61-0.93; per-SD HR 0.93, 95% CI 0.86-1.00).
- In decomposition analyses, no single food source or flavonoid subclass survived multiple-testing correction, and the association became borderline after coffee adjustment and disappeared in 6-10-year lag analyses.

Relevance and limits for Parkinson's food experiments
- Important 2026 observational null decomposition showing composite flavonoid-diet PD associations attenuate after coffee adjustment and longer lags without isolating individual culinary foods.
- Limits: Observational disease-incidence cohort rather than symptom-severity intervention in diagnosed Parkinson's disease.
