Title: Fermented Cabbage Brine as a Functional Dietary Modulator of Neuroinflammation, Oxidative Stress, and Anxiety-Like Behavior in Lipopolysaccharide-Challenged Mice.
PMID: 41992068
DOI: 10.1002/biof.70100
First published: 2026
Authors: Andjela V, Milica P, Valentina S, Sladjan P, Slavica BM, Aleksandra M
Journal: BioFactors (Oxford, England)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41992068/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Preclinical mouse study pretreating mice orally for 4 weeks with spontaneously fermented cabbage brine (sauerkraut brine, 1.8x10^6 CFU/mL viable LAB) or heat-treated brine vs saline before acute or repeated LPS injection (0.5 mg/kg). Live brine reduced prefrontal TNF-alpha/IL-1beta/IL-6 mRNA and preserved GSH/TAC, whereas heat-treated brine lost antioxidant/anti-inflammatory protection (increasing protein carbonylation). Behaviorally, both brines prevented LPS-induced reduction in open-field center time (anxiety-like proxy), while live brine unexpectedly prolonged LPS-induced locomotor reduction by 1 day longer than saline. Preclinical LPS brine model only; does not test cooked green cabbage (assigned for anxiety claim 0525) or PD.
