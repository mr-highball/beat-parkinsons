Neonatal odor responses in the same prenatal cohort
Source: https://research.aston.ac.uk/files/200676845/Flavor_learning_and_memory_in_utero_as_assessed_through_the_changing_pattern_of_olfactory_responses.pdf
Coverage: Factual summary of the primary report.

First online January 30, 2025. Follow-up from the earlier fetal study tested 32 infants at two to four weeks, 14 prenatally exposed to kale and 18 to carrot. Food powder on moistened swabs and water control were used as odor stimuli. Outcomes are hedonic facial behaviors. The first fetal measurements and the neonatal follow-up belong to the same study family, with no independent replication. The Durham thesis record was dated 2023, but the first public version of its current PDF could not be verified. No roasting, target impairment or adult Parkinson outcome was evaluated.
