Title: Factors influencing activity performance and participation in usual life situations among people with Parkinson's disease: A mixed methods study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12958334/
PMID: 41267477
PMCID: PMC12958334
DOI: 10.1080/09638288.2025.2589718
Publication date: 2025-11-20
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC article XML and included tables; supplementary files are separate.

This analysis used 137 participants from the WHIP-PD walking and strengthening program (NCT03517371), with a purposive interview subset of 13. Adjusted models accounted for 70% of the variance in activities of daily living performance and 54% of participation variance. Apathy contributed to both adjusted models. The models and participant interviews address the effect of symptom burden on participation. They provide neither a date exposure nor an estimate of change in pain or apathy from eating dates.
