Title: A Comparative Study of the Effect of Rose Essential Oil and Lemon Balm Tea on Stress Reduction Among Gynecology Residents
Source: https://www.magiran.com/volume/242733
English author abstract: https://www.magiran.com/paper/2948782/a-comparative-study-of-the-effect-of-rose-essential-oil-and-lemon-balm-tea-on-stress-reduction-among-gynecology-residents?lang=en
Reference: Fatali, Moridi and Farjami. Journal of Islamic and Iranian Traditional Medicine, 16(2), pp.87–94. September 1, 2025.
Text coverage: Persian and English journal abstracts.

The abstract describes a crossover with 20 residents and compares lemon balm tea with rose-oil inhalation. It reports anxiety. The Persian abstract states a tea result of 16.85 with SD 3.46; the English abstract states the same mean with SD 2.46. Neither supplies a separate numerical depression result.
