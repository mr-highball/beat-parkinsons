Title: A Comparative Study of the Effect of Rose Essential Oil and Lemon Balm Tea on Stress Reduction Among Gynecology Residents
Source: https://www.magiran.com/volume/242733
English author abstract: https://www.magiran.com/paper/2948782/a-comparative-study-of-the-effect-of-rose-essential-oil-and-lemon-balm-tea-on-stress-reduction-among-gynecology-residents?lang=en
Reference: Fatali, Moridi and Farjami. Journal of Islamic and Iranian Traditional Medicine, 16(2), pp.87–94. September 1, 2025.
Text coverage: Earlier Persian and English abstract review. The later full original is distilled in 2025-Fatali-Rose-Tea-Anxiety-Full-Text-Extract.txt.

The abstracts describe a crossover with 20 residents and compare lemon balm tea with rose-oil inhalation. They report anxiety. The Magiran Persian abstract states a tea result of 16.85 with SD 3.46. The English abstract states the same mean with SD 2.46. Neither supplies a separate numerical depression result. The full original later verified 2.46 as the reported SD.

The full paper later confirmed the registry linkage to IRCT20220821055767N1. The investigators and comparisons also overlap with a 2024 conference abstract whose linkage remains provisional. Reported durations, washout, target and terminology vary across records.
