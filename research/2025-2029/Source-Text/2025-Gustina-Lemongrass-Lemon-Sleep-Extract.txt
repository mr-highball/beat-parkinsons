Irwanti Gustina, Moudy E. U. Djami and Mella Yuria RA. Benefit of Lemongrass and lemon on Sleep Quality and Elimination Pattern of Postpartum Mothers. International Journal of Multidisciplinary Research and Analysis 8(1), 247–251, January 2025. DOI 10.47191/ijmra/v8-i01-30.

The original Methods describe purposive sampling, 30 postpartum participants per group and three weeks of a lemongrass-and-lemon drink in the case group. Controls received no herbal beverage. The article does not specify botanical dose, a standardized brewing method or matched placebo.

Results section 1 reports sleep above eight hours in 22 of 30 case participants versus one of 30 controls. The sleep change table repeats one and 22 under before and after labels with coded means 1.03 and 1.73 and p = 0.0001. Since the displayed counts are also the case/control counts, a paired change cannot be independently verified. The units are coded sleep categories. The outcome does not establish clinical insomnia severity or dream enactment. The group, mixture and method prevent attribution to brewed lemongrass in Parkinson disease.
