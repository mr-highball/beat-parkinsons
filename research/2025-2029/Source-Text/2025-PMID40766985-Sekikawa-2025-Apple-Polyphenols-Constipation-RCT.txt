Title: Apple Polyphenols and Defecation in Overweight Adults with Constipation - A Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 40766985
DOI: 10.1080/19390211.2025.2539878
First published: 2025
Authors: Sekikawa T, Li Y, Takara T
Journal: Journal of dietary supplements
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40766985/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled parallel RCT (UMIN000050393) in 24 randomized / 23 analyzed (12 active, 11 placebo) overweight (BMI 25-30 kg/m2) Japanese adults with constipation. Four tablets/day providing 600 mg/day unripe apple-derived polyphenols vs placebo for 12 weeks significantly increased primary weekly defecation frequency (7.0 +/- 2.1 vs 4.9 +/- 1.4 times; between-group difference +2.2 times, 95% CI 0.8-3.6, p=0.004). Fecal Akkermansia muciniphila and body composition did not differ. Limits: non-PD overweight adults; isolated unripe apple polyphenol tablets rather than baked whole apple.
