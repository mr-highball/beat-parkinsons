Title: New Onset Medically Refractory Seizures in an Adult With Parkinson's Disease and Vitamin B6 Deficiency Case Report.

Round 1 claims: 0370

A case describes medically refractory seizures in an adult receiving Parkinson medication with measured vitamin B6 deficiency. The authors report resolution of seizures after pyridoxine; other clinical treatments were also involved.

The reported intervention is correction of a nutrient deficiency. SAGE is the journal publisher. No ground sage exposure or sage-attributed rigidity measure was assessed.
