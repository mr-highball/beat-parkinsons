Intranasal sesame oil and measured nasal outcomes

Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40716461/
Coverage: Original Methods and result tables via author rendering
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

Published online July 26, 2025. Healthy young adults were randomized to sesame oil, medicated nasal oil or no nasal intervention (102 allocated and 99 completed). Nasal barrier biomarkers, respiratory symptoms, stress and sleep were assessed. Olfaction appears in background and discussion, with no verified measured threshold, discrimination or identification result. The protocol identifies CTRI/2022/06/043096. Nasal application does not test eating cold-pressed oil.
