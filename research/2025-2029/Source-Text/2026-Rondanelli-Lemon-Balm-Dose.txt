Phospholipid lemon balm dose comparison and overlapping pilot
Source: https://doi.org/10.29219/fnr.v70.13729
Coverage: factual extract of primary Methods and depression results
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Rondanelli and colleagues used open-label chronological allocation of 32 adults to two extract doses for three weeks. DASS-21 depression changed by −6.1% at 200 mg and −26.4% at 400 mg. The high-dose within-group comparison had p < .0001. There was no placebo. The authors describe this as an extension of their 2024 pilot with shared ethics identifier 1267/21092023. The 12-person salivary cortisol subgroup also overlaps. Original report: https://foodandnutritionresearch.net/index.php/fnr/article/download/13729/20830/.
