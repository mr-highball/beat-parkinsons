Round 1 claims: 0502

The clinical nursing study evaluates a structured touch, communication and relief program framed around spiritual care. It adds the program to standard care and measures quality of life and nursing-related outcomes.

Dietary advice is part of standard care rather than a date-fruit assignment. The trial provides no independently attributed effect of dried dates on Parkinson pain.
