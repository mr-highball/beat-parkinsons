Black soybean seed coat extract and sleepiness on rising
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/fsn3.70156
PMID: 40501501
PMCID: PMC12153013
DOI: 10.1002/fsn3.70156
Registration: UMIN000051261
Coverage: Original full journal report Methods, Figure 2, Results and declarations

First published June 11, 2025. Akagi and colleagues randomized 64 healthy Japanese adults, 32 per arm, to black soybean seed coat extract capsules or placebo for 12 weeks. OSA-MA factor I was measured immediately upon waking. In the full analysis set, scores favored extract at weeks eight and twelve, with higher scores denoting a more favorable waking state. The trial concerns Glycine max extract and a waking-time measure. Ordinary boiled Phaseolus vulgaris beans, clinical daytime sleepiness, and Parkinson disease are outside its scope. Fujicco employees were among the authors. The paper identifies the earlier task-day crossover as a separate study.
