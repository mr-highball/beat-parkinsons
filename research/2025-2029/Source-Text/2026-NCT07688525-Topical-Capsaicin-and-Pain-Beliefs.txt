Title: Registration of topical capsaicin pain provocation and pain-related beliefs
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07688525
Registration: NCT07688525
Publication date: 2026-07-07
Date basis: First posted date in the official registry.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: Official registration without results.

The registry describes a planned single-group experimental pain study using topical capsaicin. Anxiety-related beliefs relate to the pain and cortical excitability protocol. The current posted status is not yet recruiting and no results report is available. The intervention differs from eating ground cayenne, and no Parkinson symptom comparison is registered.
