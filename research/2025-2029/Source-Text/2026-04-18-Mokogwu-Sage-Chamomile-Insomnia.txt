Dried common sage tea and primary insomnia
Source: https://currentsci.com/images/articlesFile/1778339879.pdf
DOI: 10.58985/jpam.2026.v04i01.42
Coverage: Original publisher PDF, complete Methods, Table 2 and Discussion

The publisher PDF reports 30 adults, 15 per group, given tea made with 2 g dried common sage or 2 g chamomile for 14 days. Reported ISI means were 13.36 to 6.04 for sage and 16.14 to 6.36 for chamomile, with follow-up between-group p = .78. The raw mean changes were −7.32 and −9.78 points, respectively, and the study did not report an inactive control contrast. PSQI follow-up means were 4.86 versus 4.58 (p = .46). Neither ISI baseline mean satisfies the reported eligibility range 18–21. The report also uses inconsistent follow-up timing language. No trial registration identifier was present in the PDF. These findings describe tea in primary insomnia outside Parkinson disease.
