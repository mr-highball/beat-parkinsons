Title: Limosilactobacillus reuteri fermented brown rice alleviates anxiety improves cognition and modulates gut microbiota in stressed mice.
PMID: 39799113
PMCID: PMC11724862
DOI: 10.1038/s41538-025-00369-z
First published: 2025
Authors: Tyagi A, Choi YY, Shan L, Vinothkanna A, Lee ES, Chelliah R
Journal: NPJ science of food
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11724862/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Four-week mouse chronic mild stress (CMS) study showing Limosilactobacillus reuteri-fermented brown rice reduced corticosterone, restored brain GABA and serotonin, modulated gut microbiota/SCFAs, and improved anxiety-like and cognitive behaviors. Limits: fermented brown rice in non-PD stressed mice.
