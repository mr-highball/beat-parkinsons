Title: Evaluation of Cranberry as a Novel Therapeutic Strategy for Intracerebroventricular (ICV) Quinolinic Acid-induced Cognitive Impairment in Rats.
PMID: 40442918
DOI: 10.2174/0113862073375293250520050009
First published: 2026
Authors: Tao L, Kumari D, Badam SK, Kaur H, Dalwal V, Kumari P, Kainth R
Journal: Combinatorial chemistry & high throughput screening
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40442918/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rat experiment (Tao et al., 2026, Comb Chem High Throughput Screen; online May 2025) in 30 Wistar rats exposed to intracerebroventricular quinolinic acid (non-Parkinson excitotoxic model) treated with oral cranberry 0.5 or 2 g/kg/day for 21 days. Abstract reports improved Morris water maze, novel object recognition, rotarod, and footprint coordination alongside restored GSH and mitochondrial complexes I/II/IV, without task-specific numeric effect estimates or culinary preparation details. Non-PD excitotoxic animal model.
