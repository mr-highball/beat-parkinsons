Registered ginger extract trial in sciatica
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06817018
Registration: NCT06817018
Coverage: Official registry first posted February 10, 2025, current snapshot

The trial schedules an eight-week ginger-root extract comparison with placebo in 80 adults with chronic sciatica. Registered primary measures include Brief Pain Inventory and the Short-Form McGill Pain Questionnaire. The official record shows actual start July 1, 2025 and recruiting status last verified December 2025. No results were posted at retrieval. The 2026 protocol explicitly links the same accession.
