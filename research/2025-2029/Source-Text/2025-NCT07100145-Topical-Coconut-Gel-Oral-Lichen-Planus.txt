Title: Efficacy of Coconut Gel Compared to Corticosteroid Gel in the Management of Oral Lichen Planus
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07100145
Trial registration: NCT07100145
Collection: Source text
Publication date: 2025-08-03
Date basis: First public ClinicalTrials.gov posting.
Review depth: registry record and current structured trial status
Checked: 2026-11-25

The registry describes topical coconut gel compared with topical
corticosteroid in oral lichen planus. The main outcome is an oral
clinical score. The current registry gives completed status and 36
actual participants, with no posted results.

The oral-delivery review (PMID 42923958) reports an earlier status
and planned enrollment of 30. The current registry distinguishes
registration progress from results. Neither preparation or outcome
matches ingested coconut oil and Parkinson falls.
