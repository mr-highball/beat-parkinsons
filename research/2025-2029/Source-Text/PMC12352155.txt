Title: Assessing modified olfactory training to prevent cognitive decline in MCI high-risk individuals: a multicentre randomised controlled trial protocol in Beijing tertiary hospitals

PMID: 40812823
PMCID: PMC12352155

Publication date: 2025-08-13
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Verified NLM original PMC XML body and official Trial NCT06821828 outcome fields. Factual extract.

The protocol plans three arms in older people with olfactory dysfunction and subjective cognitive decline. One smell-training regimen includes clove among several named odors, and the methods identify eugenol as the clove odorant. The Hamilton Anxiety Scale is among registered secondary measures. The record and article supply planned outcomes only. There is neither an isolated clove contrast nor the studied exposure of consuming ground clove. The publication says recruitment began in March 2025, while the checked registry snapshot still lists not yet recruiting.
