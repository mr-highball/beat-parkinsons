Title: Randomized carrot rhamnogalacturonan-I trial and its constipation outcome
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12472267/
PMID: 41011488
PMCID: PMC12472267
DOI: 10.3390/microorganisms13092156
Registration: NCT06081972
Publication date: 2025-09-16
Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: full_text

Fifty-four healthy adults were randomized to carrot-derived RG-I capsules or maltodextrin for four weeks. The primary aim was to measure bifidobacterial responses. The Gastrointestinal Symptom Rating Scale included a constipation subscale. The paper reports diarrhoea as the only gut symptom scale with a statistically clear change. Eight of 26 participants receiving the extract and seven of 28 receiving placebo had lower diarrhoea scores. The article does not give a numerical constipation effect estimate and confidence interval. A diarrhoea result does not resolve constipation severity.

The extracted fiber and the healthy population differ from a steamed carrot Parkinson trial. The paper discloses provision of the product and author relationships with the supplier. Round 1 scope: 0623.
