Registry title: Saffron Extract Supplementation and Sleep Quality in Middle-Aged Adults

The registry lists the study as recruiting, with 80 estimated participants and no posted results. The six-week intervention is a standardized oral saffron extract with declared crocin and safranal specifications. The comparator is a matching placebo capsule. Objective sleep efficiency is primary. The planned secondary outcomes include the Insomnia Severity Index, Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index and separate daytime sleepiness. The registry supplies no efficacy result or Parkinson subgroup.
