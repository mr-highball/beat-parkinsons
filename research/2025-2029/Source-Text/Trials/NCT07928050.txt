BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a non-randomized controlled trial with two parallel groups, with the general
objective of developing and applying an assessment and intervention protocol based on a
serious game for the rehabilitation of hand function in individuals with Parkinson's
disease (PD). The experimental group trained with the RehaBEElitation serious game,
controlled by hand and wrist movements through a wearable sensor glove; the control group
trained the same five movements with a home exercise booklet. Both groups trained twice a
week for six weeks (12 sessions of 30 to 45 minutes) and kept their usual physiotherapy.
Participants were allocated in sequence: those enrolled first received the serious game
and those enrolled later received the booklet. Manual dexterity, motor signs, quality of
life, functioning and upper-limb muscle activation (surface electromyography) were
assessed before and after the intervention. This trial was registered retrospectively.

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

Arm groups
  -
    Label: RehaBEElitation serious game
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description
      Group of subjects with Parkinson's disease trained with the RehaBEElitation serious game,
      twice a week for six weeks (12 sessions of 30 to 45 minutes), in addition to usual
      physiotherapy.
    Intervention names
      - Device: RehaBEElitation serious game
  -
    Label: Home exercise booklet
    Type: ACTIVE_COMPARATOR
    Description
      Group of subjects with Parkinson's disease trained with a home exercise booklet with the
      same five movements, twice a week for six weeks (12 sessions of 30 to 45 minutes), with
      remote follow-up, in addition to usual physiotherapy.
    Intervention names
      - Behavioral: Home exercise booklet
Interventions
  -
    Type: DEVICE
    Name: RehaBEElitation serious game
    Description
      3D serious game controlled by hand and wrist movements (open/close the hand, pinch grip,
      wrist flexion-extension and radial-ulnar deviation, forearm pronation-supination) through
      a wearable glove with inertial and force sensors and haptic feedback. Twice a week for
      six weeks (12 sessions of 30 to 45 minutes).
    Arm group labels
      - RehaBEElitation serious game
  -
    Type: BEHAVIORAL
    Name: Home exercise booklet
    Description
      Printed booklet with the same five hand and wrist movements, performed in two sets of 2
      minutes with 1 minute of rest, twice a week for six weeks (12 sessions of 30 to 45
      minutes), with remote follow-up.
    Arm group labels
      - Home exercise booklet

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Diagnosis of Parkinson's disease confirmed by a neurologist or geriatrician

    -  Age between 40 and 100 years

    -  Mild to moderate disease (Hoehn & Yahr stages I to III)

    -  Mini-Mental State Examination score above 25

    -  No severe visual (Snellen chart) or hearing impairment

    -  No other neurological or musculoskeletal disease

    -  Functional and body-structure requirements for the training, assessed with the ICF
       checklist

    -  Read and agreed to the informed consent form

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Uncontrolled hypertension, unstable angina or recent myocardial infarction

    -  History of seizures

    -  Participation in another clinical trial involving rehabilitation or an experimental
       drug

    -  Missing more than three sessions or withdrawing from the intervention
