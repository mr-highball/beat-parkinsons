Title: Determination of Glycemic Index and Glycemic Load of Selected Melon Varieties in Healthy Adults
Registry: NCT07017491
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07017491
First posted: 2025-06-12
Record status: Not yet recruiting as displayed by the registry on 2026-11-19 UTC
Coverage: Official protocol record and results availability.

A single-group crossover protocol plans 16 healthy adults aged 18–50. Its interventions separately name orange candy melon, rock melon identified as Cucumis melo var. cantalupensis, and honeydew. Test portions match available carbohydrate and use a glucose reference. The primary outcome is glycemic index using two-hour glucose response. Glycemic load is secondary. The registry gives no posted results and its outcome list concerns glycemia.
