Text coverage: Curated official registry version and posted results

The primary registry design lists a target enrollment of 100. Its
updated results summarize 107 enrolled across diagnoses and mark
the trial completed. There were 49 people with Parkinson disease and
47 completed. See pages 9–12 of the registry PDF for the results.

The registry narrative reports no significant dietary intervention
effect on the Parkinson group's overall motor symptom severity
measured by MDS-UPDRS Part III. It also reports no significant
change in the primary OQ-45 outcome, clinician-rated functioning,
or quality of life. The Parkinson summary describes lower fatigue
and nonmotor symptom burden and a trend in gastrointestinal symptoms.
The registry provides no separate rest-tremor item values or numerical
effect estimates for these reported comparisons.

The intervention was a mixed BrAIN diet. The preprint supplement
lists apples with peel and olive oil among food choices, without
an assigned amount for either ingredient or an oil grade. The
trial compares the dietary pattern with habitual intake. The posted
motor result does not estimate a separate apple or olive-oil effect.
