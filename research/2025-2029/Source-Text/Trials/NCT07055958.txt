BRIEF SUMMARY

The goal of this clinical trial is to learn that metformin has effect on motor aspects of
daily living and oxidative stress in levodopa treated Parkinson disease patients.
Researcher will compare drug metformin to a placebo. Participants will take metformin or
placebo for 8 weeks.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This clinical trial is an effort to explore whether administration of metformin has
effect on motor aspects of daily living and oxidative stress in levodopa treated
Parkinson's disease patients. After completion of necessary formalities, each participant
will be assessed by translated and validated Bangla version of the Unified Parkinson's
Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS) Part questionnaire and malondialdehyde (MDA) and
glutathione (GSH) will be measured at baseline and after 8 weeks.
