Title: NeuroDance mixed-style dance intervention for people with Parkinson disease
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00038241
Registry ID: DRKS00038241
First public: 2026-03-10
Coverage: Selected original German Clinical Trials Register fields.

The retrospective registration describes an 18-week mixed-style dance program and a passive comparison group. Functional mobility includes components of gait, balance, strength and motor control. The current registry lists recruitment ongoing, planned sample 520 and an update dated September 15, 2026. The protocol publication is PMID 42824393. The planned exposure is dance participation, with no assigned dietary date fruit.
