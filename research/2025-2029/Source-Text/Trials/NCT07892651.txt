BRIEF SUMMARY

The primary objective of this multicenter observational registry is to characterize the
clinical, epidemiological, and biological profiles of individuals of Middle Eastern,
North African, and Levantine ancestry, including individuals with chronic medical
conditions and healthy volunteers. The registry will collect standardized clinical and
participant-reported data, along with biological samples, to support the investigation of
disease patterns, health-related characteristics, and population-specific biological and
immunological profiles in populations historically underrepresented in biomedical
research. The resulting data and samples may support future research into
disease-associated biomarkers and underlying biological mechanisms.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This is a multicenter, multi-purpose, observational registry designed to characterize the
clinical, epidemiological, and biological profiles of individuals of Middle Eastern,
North African, and Levantine ancestry. The registry will include individuals with chronic
medical conditions as well as healthy volunteers.

The registry will collect standardized clinical information, medical history, relevant
participant-reported information, and biological samples to support a broad range of
current and future research investigating health-related characteristics, disease
patterns, and biological variation in populations historically underrepresented in
biomedical research.

Participants with chronic medical conditions may include individuals with, but are not
limited to, the following conditions:

  -  Cardiovascular diseases

  -  Metabolic conditions, including hyperlipidemia and type 2 diabetes mellitus

  -  Autoimmune diseases

  -  Chronic gastrointestinal diseases

  -  Dermatologic diseases, including atopic dermatitis and psoriasis

  -  Respiratory diseases, including asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease

  -  Renal and urological disorders

  -  Cognitive and neurological disorders

  -  Other chronic medical conditions meeting the study's eligibility criteria Healthy
     volunteers may also participate.

Study procedures will include informed consent, eligibility assessment, collection of
medical history and relevant clinical information, review of medical records where
applicable, and collection of biological samples, as specified in the study protocol.
Samples may include serum, plasma, peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs), and urine.
The types of samples collected may vary among participants and participating sites.

This is an observational registry with no randomization. No investigational interventions
or investigator-assigned treatments will be administered as part of the study.
Participants' routine clinical care and treatment decisions will not be directed or
modified by the registry.

The data and samples collected through the registry may support multiple future research
projects addressing different scientific hypotheses across clinical, biological, and
immunological domains. Research activities may include the investigation of
disease-associated biomarkers and molecular or cellular characteristics, subject to
participant consent.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Distribution of Enrolled Participants by Clinical Condition
    Description
      Number and percentage of enrolled participants with each eligible chronic medical
      condition, including disease subtype where applicable. Healthy volunteers will be
      summarized separately from participants with chronic medical conditions.
    Time frame: At the enrollment/sample collection visit
Other outcomes
  -
    Measure: Demographic and Anthropometric Characteristics of the Enrolled Population
    Description
      Demographic and anthropometric characteristics of enrolled participants, including age
      (years), sex, race/ethnicity, self-reported Middle Eastern and North African
      (MENA)/Levant ancestry, smoking status, body weight (kilograms), and height
      (centimeters). Categorical variables will be summarized using counts and percentages.
      Age, weight, and height will be summarized using appropriate descriptive statistics.
    Time frame: At the enrollment/sample collection visit
  -
    Measure: Geographic Distribution of the Enrolled Population
    Description
      Geographic distribution of enrolled participants by the country or region recorded at
      enrollment. Findings will be summarized using counts and percentages, as appropriate.
    Time frame: At the enrollment/sample collection visit

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Individuals of Middle Eastern, North African, or Levantine ancestry, as determined
       by participant self-report.

    -  Individuals aged 18 years or older with chronic medical conditions; a confirmed
       diagnosis or self-reported history of one or more chronic medical conditions,
       supported by medical record review where available.

    -  Individuals below 18 years of age with a relevant chronic condition, where pediatric
       enrollment is subject to applicable ethical approvals and the required parental or
       legally authorized representative permission and participant assent, as applicable.

    -  Healthy volunteers: Individuals aged 18 years or older without known chronic medical
       conditions relevant to the registry, based on participant self-report and
       investigator assessment.

    -  Willing and able to provide written informed consent before initiation of any study
       procedures or, for pediatric participants, participation will be subject to
       applicable written parental/legal guardian permission and assent procedures, as
       appropriate.

    -  Eligible and willing to undergo the applicable study procedures, including
       collection of relevant clinical information and biological samples.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Inability to obtain the required informed consent, parental or legally authorized
       representative permission, or participant assent, as applicable.

    -  Acute illness, including fever (≥38°C), active infection, or a clinically unstable
       condition at the time of enrollment that, in the opinion of the investigator, may
       compromise participant safety or interfere with study procedures. Enrollment may be
       deferred until the condition resolves or the participant is clinically stable.
