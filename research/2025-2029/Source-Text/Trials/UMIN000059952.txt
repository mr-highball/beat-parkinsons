Title: Study of measurement on body temperature for functional foods
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000068520
Format: Selected official registry fields summarized for this catalog.
Registry: UMIN000059952, receipt R000068520.
Institution: Okayama Prefectural University.
Public disclosure: 2025-12-03. Last modified on the English record: 2025-12-18.
Design: Single-blind, nonrandomized crossover in healthy adults. Five arms compare bread alone and bread with powder or liquid formulations of macadamia nut or soybean oil.
Planned outcomes: Core temperature, visual analogue scores for sleepiness, fatigue and hunger, and psychomotor vigilance for up to 120 minutes after the test meal. Previous-night sleep timing and Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index are secondary information.
Registry results: No posted outcome data.
