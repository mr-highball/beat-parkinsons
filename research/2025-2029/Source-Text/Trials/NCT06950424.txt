Topic: Black cumin seed oil and registered sleep quality
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06950424
Coverage: Official ClinicalTrials.gov protocol, intervention and outcomes, factual extract
Official API inventory reviewed November 15, 2026. Status active, not recruiting, with no posted results in the accessed record version. The supplement contains black cumin seed oil standardized for thymoquinone. The protocol includes subjective measures from baseline through eight weeks, including PSQI, alongside cortisol and other measures.
