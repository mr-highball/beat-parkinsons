Title: The Construction and Application Study of Salivary Intervention Plan Based on Symptom Management Theory for Parkinson's Disease Patients
Source: https://www.chictr.org.cn/showproj.html?proj=254869
Registry ID: ChiCTR2500096034
Registration date: 2025-01-16
Coverage: Selected original bilingual public registry fields, inspected through its indexed official snapshot. Direct page fetch returned HTTP 405.

The prospective record identifies people with diagnosed Parkinson disease and drooling. It lists swallowing function and salivation severity as primary and self-management and quality of life as secondary. The comparison places a personalized salivation management program alongside standardized medication and compares it with standard medication plus general rehabilitation, psychological care and health education. The listed procedures include swallowing practice and oral-motor exercises. Randomization uses a number table and the record specifies participant masking. The recorded planned dates run from January 23 to May 31, 2025. The accessible registry excerpt gives no orange-juice assignment or falls outcome.

The 2026 randomized report PMID 41939767 cites this ID and reports recruitment and trial work in July–September 2025, with SCS-PD drooling score as the primary outcome. The published dietary guidance lists orange juice within the assigned whole program. The article supplies no separately assigned juice comparison. The separately published journey map PMID 41868492 also cites this ID and reports qualitative interviews. Those interviews provide care-pathway context. They are not an additional randomized trial.
