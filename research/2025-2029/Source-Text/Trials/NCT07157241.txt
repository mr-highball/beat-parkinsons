Title: Corn oil sleepiness comparator and retracted results publication
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07157241
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

NCT07157241. The official record lists 400 mg corn oil as the comparator for an omega-3 intervention, with Epworth Sleepiness Scale among listed outcomes for adults with psychological distress. Corn oil grade is unspecified and no isolate-versus-oil-free comparison is assigned. The registry lacks posted results. The identifier-matched article PMID 41461240, DOI 10.1016/j.jad.2025.121055, was retracted by notice PMID 42469048, DOI 10.1016/j.jad.2026.122090. No outcomes from the retracted report are used.
