Related journal article: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42030620/

Randomized 78 participants, with 45 allocated to intervention and 33 to control. The intention-to-treat analysis found no clear between-group difference in physical performance or muscle mass through 24 weeks. The between-group percentage change in knee extension strength had p=.016. Catechin-enriched green tea was associated with changes in urinary titin N-terminal fragment. The registry lists five reports of unpalatable taste and four reports of transient gastrointestinal discomfort as possibly intervention-related. No serious events were considered intervention-related. Handwriting and separately rated fatigue were not included in the registry result fields.
