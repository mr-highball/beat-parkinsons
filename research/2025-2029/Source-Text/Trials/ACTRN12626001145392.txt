Title: Lemon balm tea and a sensory wellbeing program in healthcare professionals
Registry ID: ACTRN12626001145392
Source: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=ACTRN12626001145392
URL: https://anzctr.org.au/ACTRN12626001145392.aspx
Registration date: 2026-09-15.
Text coverage: WHO registration bridge and Australian government registry mirror. Direct ANZCTR reader returned HTTP 403.

The randomized pilot compares lemon balm tea with rooibos for 28 days in health professionals. Both arms receive the same program of breathing, reflection and journaling. The structured target is 24 and the narrative description gives 24–26. The sources reviewed show not yet recruiting and do not report actual enrollment or outcomes.

Feasibility is primary. Registered secondary outcomes include GAD-7 anxiety, perceived stress, flourishing and reflective functioning. This protocol does not specify a separate depression outcome or a Parkinson group. The comparison varies the tea within a shared program.
