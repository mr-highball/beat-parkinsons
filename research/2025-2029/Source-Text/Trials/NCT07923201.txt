BRIEF SUMMARY

The goal of this clinical trial is to learn whether the Zeen Mobility Device can improve
mobility and function during outpatient rehabilitation for adults with mobility
impairments. The study will also evaluate the safety of the device and how easy it is for
participants and therapists to use during routine therapy.

The main questions it aims to answer are:

  -  Does using the Zeen Mobility Device improve walking, standing, transfers, and daily
     function compared with a participant's usual mobility device?

  -  Can the Zeen Mobility Device be safely incorporated into outpatient rehabilitation?

  -  How satisfied are participants and therapists with using the device during therapy?

Researchers will compare participants' performance using the Zeen Mobility Device with
their performance using their usual mobility device, such as a walker or wheelchair.
Participants will serve as their own comparison group.

Participants will:

  -  Complete a baseline assessment before starting the study, a midpoint assessment
     after 4 treatment sessions, and a final assessment after 8 treatment sessions.

  -  Attend 8 outpatient physical therapy sessions that include use of the Zeen Mobility
     Device.

  -  Use the Zeen Mobility Device for at least 15 minutes during each study visit for
     balance, mobility, walking, transfer, or standing activities selected by their
     therapist.

  -  Complete mobility and functional tests using both the Zeen Mobility Device and their
     usual mobility device at each assessment visit.

  -  Provide feedback about their experience using the device while researchers monitor
     safety and any device-related problems.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Timed Up and Go
    Description
      The Timed Up and Go Test is a clinical tool used to evaluate functional mobility,
      balance, walking ability, and fall risk in older adults and individuals with neurological
      or musculoskeletal conditions. Scoring is measured in seconds to complete the task.
      Higher scores indicate worse mobility, balance, and walking ability.
    Time frame: Baseline, 2 weeks, and 4 weeks
  -
    Measure: 10 Meter Walk Test
    Description
      The 10 Meter Walk Test is an assessment of a person's walking speed as they walk 10
      meters, measured in seconds. A higher score indicates worsening balance and walking
      capacity, and is associated with poorer home and community access and increased fall risk
    Time frame: Baseline, 2 weeks, and 4 weeks
  -
    Measure: 6 Minute Walk Test
    Description
      The 6 Minute Walk Test is an assessment of a person's walking endurance as they walk
      during a 6-minute period, measured in total distance walked in meters. A higher score is
      associated with better walking endurance and capacity, and is associated with better home
      and community access and decreased fall risk
    Time frame: Baseline, 2 weeks, and 4 weeks
  -
    Measure: Barthel Index
    Description
      The Barthel Index is an ordinal clinical scale that measures a patient's level of
      functional independence in 10 basic activities of daily living and mobility. The scale
      calculates 10 items into a point total, from 0 to 100, with a higher score indicating a
      greater level of independence and decreased need for nursing and caregiver support.
    Time frame: Baseline, 2 weeks, and 4 weeks
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Patient Satisfaction and Exertion Survey
    Description
      Participants will report satisfaction with the device on a 5-point Likert Scale, or a
      10-point scale, including:

        -  Comfort using the device

        -  Choosing to use the device during rehabilitation

        -  Comfort, ease of use

        -  Effort required for sit-to-stand transitions

        -  Difficulty with 90-degree turns

        -  Difficulty with larger turns

        -  Exertion during ambulation

        -  Hand strain during operation

        -  Overall fatigue during walking

      Total scores range from 9 to 50, with a higher score indicating more difficulty with the
      device, and decreased satisfaction.
    Time frame: Baseline, 2 weeks, and 4 weeks

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Be 18 years of age or older

    -  Be enrolled in outpatient PT and/or OT

    -  Demonstrate mobility limitations appropriate for Zeen use

    -  Meet the following functional thresholds:

         -  6MWT: 800-1800 feet

         -  10MWT: 0.4-1.3 m/s

         -  Grip strength ≥1 pound in at least one hand

         -  Knee extension strength ≥ MMT grade 3/5

    -  Be capable of providing informed consent

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Severe cognitive impairment

    -  Medical instability that would preclude participation

    -  Inability to safely participate in mobility assessments
