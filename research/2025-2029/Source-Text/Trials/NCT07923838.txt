BRIEF SUMMARY

The goal of this clinical trial is to learn if intranasal umbilical cord mesenchymal
stromal cell-derived exosomes (UCMSC-Exo) are safe and can help treat early Parkinson's
disease in adults aged 50-75. It will also learn about the safety and tolerability of
UCMSC-Exo. This is a single-center, open-label, dose-escalation study using a 3+3 design;
there is no placebo and no blinding.

The main questions it aims to answer are:

  1. What local and systemic adverse events and serious adverse events occur with
     intranasal UCMSC-Exo?

  2. Does intranasal UCMSC-Exo improve motor and non-motor symptoms, biomarkers, and
     imaging measures in Parkinson's disease?

  3. Researchers will compare low-, medium-, and high-dose UCMSC-Exo groups sequentially
     to assess safety and preliminary efficacy.

Participants will:

  1. Receive UCMSC-Exo by nasal spray twice weekly (Monday/Thursday) for 12 weeks (24
     administrations) at their assigned dose.

  2. Continue their usual dopaminergic medication during the study.

  3. Visit the clinic for safety assessments at weeks 4, 12, 16, and 48, and for efficacy
     assessments at weeks 12, 16, 24, 36, and 48.

  4. Undergo blood sampling, symptom questionnaires, and PET-MR imaging at scheduled time
     points.

  5. Be followed for a total of 48 weeks, including the 12-week treatment period and 36
     weeks after treatment.

Interventions
  -
    Type: BIOLOGICAL
    Name: Umbilical cord mesenchymal stromal cell-derived exosomes (UCMSC-Exo)
    Description
      UCMSC-Exo is an open-label product of exosomes from umbilical cord mesenchymal stromal
      cells (UCMSCs) in saline. It is a transparent liquid given by intranasal spray, not
      intravenously, and contains no living cells. Each dose is 0.4 mL total (200 µL/nostril; 2
      sprays/nostril at ~100 µL/spray), twice weekly (Mon/Thu) for 12 weeks (24 doses), with
      usual dopaminergic therapy continued. A 3+3 design tests low (5×10^8 particles/mL),
      medium (1×10^9 particles/mL), and high (2×10^9 particles/mL) concentrations; volume is
      identical, concentration differs. Escalation proceeds only with no DLT; 1 DLT expands a
      cohort to 6; >1 DLT stops escalation. Store at -80°C; use within 2 hours after thawing.
      Key distinctions: acellular UCMSC-derived exosomes, intranasal route, fixed twice-weekly
      12-week regimen, sequential dose escalation, no placebo.
    Arm group labels
      - High-dose intranasal UCMSC-Exo
      - Low-dose intranasal UCMSC-Exo
      - Medium-dose intranasal UCMSC-Exo

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Incidence, severity, and relatedness of local and systemic adverse events and serious adverse events after intranasal UCMSC-Exo in early Parkinson's disease
    Description
      Primary safety outcome: incidence, severity, and relationship to study intervention of
      local and systemic adverse events (AEs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) after
      intranasal UCMSC-Exo. Local AEs include pain, itching, sneezing, bleeding, allergy, and
      hyposmia. Systemic AEs include fever, systemic allergic reactions (rash, redness),
      abnormal inflammatory markers (WBC, CRP), hepatic impairment (ALT/AST >2× ULN), renal
      impairment (serum creatinine > ULN), anemia (Hb <120 g/L in men; <110 g/L in women), and
      abnormal tumor markers. SAEs include severe allergic reactions (edema, asthma). Safety is
      assessed by physical examination, vital signs, laboratory tests (CBC/CRP, liver and renal
      function, electrolytes, fasting glucose, IgE, EKG), and AE/SAE questionnaires at Weeks 4,
      12, 16, and 48; tumor markers, cardiac markers, DIC, and lipids are assessed at Week 48.
    Time frame: From first dose through Week 48; safety assessments at Weeks 4, 12, 16, and 48

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    1. Confirmed sporadic idiopathic Parkinson's disease according to the UK Brain Bank
       clinical diagnostic criteria.

    2. On dopaminergic therapy for ≥60 days.

    3. Hyposmia, defined as SS-16 score <9.

    4. Hoehn and Yahr stage ≤2.5.

    5. Age 50-75 years.

    6. MMSE score ≥25.

    7. Good general health with no clinically significant abnormalities on laboratory
       tests.

    8. No contraindications to MRI.

    9. No autoimmune immunosuppressive disease and no current use of any immunosuppressive
       drug.

   10. Able to independently and voluntarily provide dated informed consent.

   11. Willing to comply with study procedures and able to adhere to the full study and
       intervention period.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    1. Severe allergic constitution.

    2. Previous treatment with allogeneic mesenchymal stromal cells or their exosomes.

    3. Any of the following laboratory abnormalities: neutrophil count <1.0×10^9/L;
       platelet count <100×10^9/L; serum albumin <30 g/L; serum creatinine > upper limit of
       normal; ALT/AST >2× upper limit of normal.

    4. Severe or poorly controlled comorbidities, including but not limited to
       cardiovascular, cerebrovascular, hepatic, renal, endocrine, hematologic,
       dermatologic, and other systemic diseases.

    5. Severe depression.

    6. Other neurological diseases, including but not limited to stroke, traumatic brain
       injury, parkinsonism, or cognitive impairment.

  8、Severe psychiatric symptoms or hallucinations.

  9、History of malignancy or markedly abnormal tumor markers.

  10、Severe systemic infection within the past 3 months.

  11、MRI contraindications, including but not limited to cardiac pacemaker, claustrophobia,
  or implanted metal devices.

  12、Positive infectious disease screening for HIV, HBV, HCV, or Treponema pallidum
  (syphilis).

  13、Drug abuse or addiction within the past 10 years.

  14、Participation in another clinical trial within the past 6 months.

  15、Current pregnancy or breastfeeding, or pregnancy/breastfeeding within the past 6
  months.

  16、Any condition that the investigator considers unsuitable for enrollment.
