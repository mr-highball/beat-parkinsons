Topic: High-oleic safflower comparator and anxiety screening
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06834984
Coverage: Official protocol and outcome fields, factual extract
The protocol compares a synbiotic with a placebo product containing high-oleic safflower oil and includes GAD-7 in its secondary outcomes. The completed record has no posted results. The formulation, indication, comparator and outcome collection leave Parkinson anxiety and oil attribution unresolved.
