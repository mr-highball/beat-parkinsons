Title: Thyme and cinnamon as smell training stimuli
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07190911
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

NCT07190911. First posted September 24, 2025. The official snapshot lists completion. The intervention uses multiple spices including thyme and cinnamon as olfactory training after SARS-CoV-2. Taste and smell are study outcomes. It does not assign thyme ingestion or measure a Parkinson symptom.

The intervention contains dill among 11 odors. Actual enrollment 40, actual completion March 9, 2022, first public posting September 24, 2025. Taste and Smell Survey is primary and Sniffin' Sticks secondary. No structured results, isolated dill arm or information about a fresh culinary preparation is available in the official snapshot.
