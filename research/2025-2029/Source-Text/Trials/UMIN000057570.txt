Title: Study of effect on body temperature and performance of admission for functional foods
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000065779
Format: Selected official registry fields summarized for this catalog.
Registry: UMIN000057570, receipt R000065779.
Institution: Okayama Prefectural University.
Public disclosure: 2025-04-09.
Design: Single-blind, nonrandomized crossover in healthy adults. Bread alone is compared with bread plus either macadamia or soybean oil at three dose levels, 2, 4 and 8 g.
Planned outcomes: Core temperature, blood glucose and visual analogue scores for sleepiness, fatigue and hunger through 120 minutes. Previous-night sleep timing and Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index are secondary information.
Registry results: No posted outcomes. Recruitment listed as preinitiation.
