The Japanese title specifies a black tea drink (紅茶飲料). The English title says tea without naming the type. The randomized parallel design plans four weeks of test tea versus control tea in healthy adults, with investigators and outcome assessors blinded. The fatigue and recovery outcomes are described collectively. The registration does not identify the competing drinks' detailed compositions or define individual fatigue measures.

The posted status remains preinitiation and the results fields are blank. This is a black-tea fatigue protocol in healthy adults. It helps distinguish an English search hit for tea from the frozen brewed-green-tea question in diagnosed Parkinson disease. Recheck the registry or publication results by UMIN identifier.
