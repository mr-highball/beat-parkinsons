Title: DHA supplementation and exercise in multiple sclerosis
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07346703
NCT ID: NCT07346703
First posted: 2026-01-16
Last update posted: 2026-01-16
Coverage: Factual extract of official registration.

Four randomized groups are planned, comparing 2 g/day DHA and 2 g/day sunflower oil, with and without exercise, over three months. The estimated enrollment is 100. Right-leg isokinetic strength is primary. The registered depressive symptom item in MSQoL-54 is described as yes/no. The registry does not supply a graded depression severity measure or a Parkinson group.
The latest status is enrolling by invitation. Its narrative gives a January 2024 to January 2026 period, while the structured fields give January 2026 start and estimated July 2026 completion. There are no posted results. Oil refining grade is unspecified.
