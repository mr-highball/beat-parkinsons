Title: Lute-gen, macular pigment and cognitive performance in teenagers
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/ACTRN12625000047493.aspx
Record: ACTRN12625000047493
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting or registration: 2025-01-20

Completed, 82 participants aged 13–18. A matching placebo contained sunflower oil and the active capsules added lutein and zeaxanthin. The registry lists objective and self-reported measures of attention, visual memory, processing speed, reasoning and sleep. It linked the journal results in August 2026 and says participant-level data may be considered subject to sponsor and investigator approval. Oil processing is unspecified.
