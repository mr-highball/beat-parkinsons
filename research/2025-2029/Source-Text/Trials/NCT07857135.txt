BRIEF SUMMARY

This study is a multi-center, open-label, dose-escalation, dose-expansion, and
cohort-expansion first-in-human Phase 1/2 study to evaluate the safety, tolerability, PK
characteristics, and preliminary anti-tumor efficacy of SYS6088 in patients with
advanced/metastatic solid tumors.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    1. Age ≥ 18 years

    2. Malignant solid tumors confirmed by histology or cytology

    3. Patients who have failed standard anti-tumor treatment (disease progression or
       intolerance), have no available standard treatment or refuse standard treatment.
       (Phase 1)

    4. At least one measurable lesion that meets RECIST 1.1 criteria at baseline (the
       measurable lesion area has not received radiotherapy previously, or there is
       evidence that the lesion has clearly progressed after the end of radiotherapy)
       exists

    5. ECOG PS score ranges 0-1

    6. Expected survival period ≥ 3 months

    7. Adequate organ function.

    8. Participants must give informed consent to this study before the trial and
       voluntarily sign a written informed consent form.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    1. Subjects with a history of severe allergies; Known hypersensitivity or intolerance
       to any component of the antibody drug and its excipients;

    2. Subjects with central nervous system metastasis or meningeal metastasis. Patients
       who have undergone targeted radiotherapy or surgical treatment and have no clinical
       symptoms and signs without drug symptomatic treatment can be enrolled;

    3. Patients with tumor invasion of important arteries leading to high risk of bleeding,
       obvious risk of perforation, or fistula formation; presence of diseases with
       impaired wound healing ability;

    4. Uncontrolled pleural effusion, pericardial effusion, or ascites that requires
       repeated drainage;

    5. Adverse reactions of previous anti-tumor treatment (including radiotherapy) have not
       recovered to CTCAE 6.0 ≤ Grade 1;

    6. Occurrence of grade ≥3 immune-related adverse events (irAEs), or treatment
       discontinuation due to immune-related adverse events previously.

    7. Diagnosed with a second primary malignant tumor within 5 years before the first
       administration of the study drug; Except for effectively treated basal cell
       carcinoma of skin, squamous cell carcinoma of skin, and/or effectively resected
       cervix cancer and/or breast cancer in situ;

    8. Received chemotherapy, biological therapy, targeted therapy and other anti-tumor
       treatments within 21 days before the first administration of the study drug;

    9. Received live or live-attenuated vaccines within 28 days prior to the first
       administration of study drug or planned to receive during the study;

   10. Use of immunosuppressive drugs within 14 days before the first administration of
       study drug;

   11. Use drugs with anticoagulant effects within 14 days before the first administration
       of study drug.

   12. Received palliative treatment for local lesions (including local radiotherapy or
       other local treatments) within 14 days before the first administration of study
       drug;

   13. Have undergone major surgery (excluding puncture biopsy) or severe traumatic injury
       28 days before the first administration of study drug;

   14. Subjects with a history of severe cardiovascular disease；

   15. History or presence of interstitial pneumonia/lung disease;

   16. History or presence of inflammatory bowel disease

   17. History of autoimmune diseases;

   18. History of immunodeficiency, including positive HIV test, or other acquired or
       congenital immunodeficiency diseases, or organ transplantation;

   19. Infectious diseases requiring systemic anti-infective treatment;

   20. Active hepatitis B; Hepatitis C infection; Active syphilis infection, active
       tuberculosis;

   21. Lactating or pregnant women; women of childbearing potential have a positive blood
       pregnancy test within 7 days prior to trial enrollment; [A participant is of
       childbearing potential (judged by the investigator) when he/she is biologically
       capable of fathering a child and is sexually active. Female participants who are not
       of childbearing potential (i.e., meet at least 1 of the following criteria): have
       had a hysterectomy or bilateral oophorectomy, or have medically confirmed ovarian
       failure, or are medically confirmed postmenopausal (cessation of menses for at least
       12 consecutive months without a pathological or physiological cause).]

   22. Any male and female participants of childbearing potential who refuse to use
       effective contraceptive methods throughout the trial and within half a year after
       the last administration;

   23. Other conditions that the investigator believes will affect the safety or compliance
       of the study drug treatment, including but not limited to: mental illness, any
       severe or uncontrolled disease, etc
