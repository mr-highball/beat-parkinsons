Title: Registered vegetable oil food and sleep in young adults
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000068464
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

UMIN000059869, receipt R000068464. Meiji University. Registered November 24, 2025, first publicly disclosed December 22, 2025.
The open randomized crossover compares daily test food after dinner with no test food in healthy adults in Japan aged 18 to 26. Primary outcomes are sleep quality and duration. Target and reported enrollment both read 15.
The public arm text leaves the vegetable oil identity, quantity and processing grade unspecified. No secondary outcome is named. Results are listed as unpublished, the results fields are empty, and recruitment is listed as by invitation on the record checked November 16, 2026 UTC.
