BRIEF SUMMARY

Parkinson's disease (PD) is an age-related movement disorder with a complex
pathophysiology. Among the risk factors for PD, variants in beta-glucocerebrosidase
(encoded by the gene GBA1) represent one of the most significant heritable risk factors
for lifetime PD risk. Biallelic variants in GBA1 cause Gaucher disease (GD), a
multisystem disorder, and individuals with late/adult-onset GD1 have a ~1-in-9 lifetime
risk for PD. This study aims to develop a cross-sectional cohort of clinical and
laboratory phenotyped adult GD1 patients and controls for investigation of
clinical/laboratory risk factors for current and future Parkinson risk. Neurological
evaluations will be video and audio recorded to allow the investigators to quantify
neurological function using automated tools. Data and specimens generated in this study
will be incorporated into a de-identified biobank that will be used for further research
into GD and PD.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Movement Disorder Society Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS): Parts I, II, and IV
    Description
      The MDS-UPDRS is a standardized clinical assessment used by neurologists to study PD.
      Three parts (I/II/IV) are used to evaluate individuals' symptoms of Parkinson's Disease,
      including non-motor and motor experiences of daily living and common PD comorbidities.
      Each part contains several items (each rated by severity on a 0 to 4 scale) Part I
      (subscale from 0-52), Part II (subscale from 0-52), and Part IV (subscale from 0-24) are
      summed for a total score from 0-128. A higher score indicates higher severity of
      PD-related symptoms.
    Time frame: Day 1
  -
    Measure: REM Behavioral Disorder Screening Questionnaire (RBDSQ)
    Description
      RBDSQ is a simple 10-item yes/no response screening questionnaire to screen for REM sleep
      behavior disorders, a known risk factor for PD. The range for this scale is 0 to 13
      points. Each item contributes 1 point except a single item that contributes 4 points for
      up to four specific manifestations of REM Behavior Disorders. A score of 5 or higher
      indicates a positive screening threshold for REM Behavioral Disorder (RBD).
    Time frame: Day 1
  -
    Measure: Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA)
    Description
      MoCA is a 10-minute cognitive screening tool used to detect mild-cognitive impairment
      (MCI) by assessing short-term memory, visuospatial abilities, executive functions,
      attention and concentration, working memory, language, and orientation to time and place.
      The scale ranges from 0 to 30 points. Severe impairment scores between 0-9 and normal
      cognitive performance scored at 26 and above. The higher the score, the less cognitive
      impairment.
    Time frame: Day 1
  -
    Measure: Scales for Outcomes in Parkinson's Disease - Autonomic Dysfunction (SCOPA-AUT)
    Description
      SCOPA-AUT is a 25-item questionnaire used to assess autonomic symptoms (gastrointestinal,
      urinary, cardiovascular, thermoregulatory, pupillomotor, sexual) in patients with
      Parkinson's disease. This scale includes subscales for gastrointestinal (0-7), urinary
      (0-6), cardiovascular (0-3), thermoregulatory (0-4), pupillomotor (0-1), and sexual (2
      items for men and 2 items for women) symptoms. Total scale ranges from 0-23 where a
      higher score in any or total symptoms indicates higher severity.
    Time frame: Day 1
  -
    Measure: University of Pennsylvania Smell Identification Test (UPSIT)
    Description
      UPSIT is a reliable 40-item test of olfactory function using microencapsulated odorants
      which are released by scratching scent strips across four small booklets. The individual
      then picks the answer from a list of four multiple-choice options. The final score is the
      number of correct answers between 0 and 40. A lower score indicates olfactory
      dysfunction, and a higher score indicates a more normal sense of smell.
    Time frame: Day 1
  -
    Measure: Movement Disorder Society-Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS) Part III
    Description
      This section includes 18 items assessing tone, tremor, gait, movement, and posture at
      various sites of the body which are all rated on a scale of 0 to 4, with 4 indicating
      more severe symptoms. It also includes four yes/no questions regarding current medication
      / treatment for ongoing dyskinesia during the examination as well as a single 0 to 5
      rating on overall functional status, with higher ratings reflecting greater functional
      impairment. The score ranges from 0-133 with higher score indicating more severe
      impairment.
    Time frame: Day 1
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Number of individuals with movement disorder other than Parkinson's Disease
    Description
      The number of individuals that have another movement disorder on this examination not
      covered by the UPDRS III.
    Time frame: Day 1

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Subjects with a documentation of GBA1 phenotype

    -  Subjects older than 40 years old

    -  Either GD1 participants with or without PD

    -  GBA1 heterozygotes with or without PD

    -  Non-carrier controls without neurological disorder and no first-degree family
       relative with PD

  Exclusion Criteria:

  • Subjects with diminished mental capacity and unable to consent for themselves

Study population
  Type 1 Gaucher Disease patients currently receiving their care at the Mount Sinai Health
  System or partnering Gaucher Disease treatment programs will be screened for eligibility.
  GBA1 carrier and non-carrier participants will be recruited from the interested relatives
  of our GD patients. It is expected most of our participants live in the northeast United
  States, speak fluent English, and are of Ashkenazi Jewish ancestry as there is a higher
  prevalence of GD in this ethnic group.
