BRIEF SUMMARY

This multicenter study aims to characterize the language profiles of patients with
various neurodegenerative diseases, including Alzheimer's disease (AD), Primary
Progressive Aphasia (PPA), behavioral variant Frontotemporal Dementia (bvFTD), Dementia
with Lewy Bodies (DLB), and Parkinson's disease (PD), using artificial intelligence (AI)
models. By analyzing speech and language data collected during remote neuropsychological
assessments, the researchers intend to identify objective linguistic biomarkers suitable
for early and differential diagnosis, overcoming the limitations of traditional,
invasive, and costly diagnostic tools. The project will develop and validate an AI-driven
pipeline to provide scalable and individualized linguistic profiles, ultimately
supporting personalized treatment strategies and improving clinical workflow efficiency
in the Italian population.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Neurodegenerative disorders pose significant diagnostic challenges, particularly in
differentiating conditions with overlapping clinical symptoms and neuroanatomy. Although
language impairment is a hallmark symptom across several forms of dementia, existing
diagnostic tools are often costly, time-consuming, and lack cultural sensitivity,
particularly for the Italian language. Artificial Intelligence (AI) offers a promising,
non-invasive approach to extracting objective acoustic, prosodic, and linguistic markers
from speech data.

The primary objective of this study is to characterize the language profiles of patients
diagnosed with AD, PPA, bvFTD, DLB, and PD compared to healthy controls (HC). Specific
objectives include: 1) Identifying distinct linguistic and speech features that act as
surrogate biomarkers for early and differential diagnosis; 2) Using machine learning to
optimize classification algorithms; and 3) Implementing Explainable AI (XAI) to ensure
the interpretability of diagnostic outputs, thereby facilitating clinical trust and
adoption of AI-driven tools in the diagnostic process.

This is a prospective, experimental, multicenter study. A total of 168 participants (24
per group: AD, PPA, bvFTD, DLB, PD, and HC) will be enrolled. Participants will undergo a
1.5-hour remote neuropsychological and logopedic evaluation via a secure, HIPAA-compliant
videoconferencing platform. The assessment includes spontaneous speech tasks (e.g., image
descriptions) and standardized language and cognitive tests. Audio data will be recorded
and processed using an AI pipeline, including automatic speech recognition for
transcription and various Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques-such as BERT,
RoBERTa, and UmBERTo-to extract complex linguistic and semantic features.

Data analysis will involve training machine learning classifiers (e.g., Support Vector
Machines, Random Forests) to distinguish between diagnostic groups, utilizing nested
cross-validation to ensure robustness. XAI techniques, including SHAP (Shapley Additive
Explanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations), will be applied
to enhance the transparency of the model's decisions, allowing clinicians to interpret
the pathological language patterns driving the classification. The study aims to
establish the first structured Italian corpus for the linguistic characterization of
neurodegenerative disorders, providing a foundation for future cross-linguistic analysis.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Participants with neurodegenerative diseases

  Inclusion criteria

    -  Diagnosis of mild cognitive impairment due to Alzheimer's disease (AD), primary
       progressive aphasia (PPA), behavioral variant frontotemporal dementia (bvFTD),
       dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB), or Parkinson's disease (PD).

    -  Age 40-85 years.

    -  Italian native speaker.

    -  Able to provide informed consent.

  Exclusion criteria

    -  Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) score below 15.

    -  Other neurological or psychiatric diagnoses, including cerebrovascular disease
       documented by MRI or CT.

    -  Severe, uncorrected hearing or visual impairment.

    -  Clinical Dementia Rating (CDR) score above 1.

  Healthy controls

  Inclusion criteria

    -  Italian native speaker.

    -  Matched to the patient groups for age, sex, and education level.

  Exclusion criteria

    -  History of systemic, neurological, or psychiatric disease.

    -  Substance abuse.

    -  Family history of neurodegenerative disease.

    -  MMSE score below 27/30.
