The original UMIN record reports that participants were still being accepted by invitation as of the end of May 2026. The study compares 10 mL barium preparations made with tap water and carbonated water in adults with Parkinson disease. The planned last follow-up date is March 31, 2028. No enrollment count, effect estimate or publication link was posted in the registry results fields when checked. The status differs from the related UMIN000051942 registration, which was terminated.
