BRIEF SUMMARY

This retrospective, single-center observational cohort study evaluates
inflammatory-metabolic, cachexia-related, and computed tomography (CT)-derived
body-composition markers in patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (mNSCLC)
treated with immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI)-based therapy.

The study includes 155 consecutive adult patients who initiated ICI-based therapy at
Ankara Etlik City Hospital between December 2022 and March 2025. Baseline assessments
include the C-reactive protein-triglyceride glucose index (CTI), cachexia index (CXI),
CT-derived skeletal muscle index (SMI), skeletal muscle attenuation, sarcopenia status,
body weight, and clinical characteristics.

The principal survival outcomes are progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival
(OS). Associations of baseline CTI and CXI with PFS and OS are evaluated using Cox
proportional hazards regression models. The incremental prognostic performance of CTI and
CXI beyond conventional clinical factors is further explored using discrimination,
calibration, and decision-curve analyses.

Early longitudinal changes in CT-derived body composition and body weight are evaluated
in a landmark cohort of 140 patients with an eligible follow-up CT performed 8-12 weeks
after ICI initiation. Associations of early skeletal muscle and body-weight changes with
subsequent PFS and OS are assessed using landmark survival analyses.

The survival data cutoff is June 11, 2026.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) have improved outcomes in metastatic non-small cell
lung cancer (mNSCLC), but substantial heterogeneity in clinical outcomes remains.
Host-related factors reflecting systemic inflammation, metabolic status, nutritional
reserve, and skeletal muscle composition may provide prognostic information beyond
conventional tumor- and treatment-related characteristics.

This retrospective, single-center observational cohort study includes consecutive adult
patients with histologically confirmed mNSCLC who initiated ICI-based systemic therapy at
Ankara Etlik City Hospital between December 2022 and March 2025. A total of 155 patients
meeting the eligibility criteria constitute the overall cohort. The survival data cutoff
is June 11, 2026.

Baseline CT-derived body composition is assessed at the third lumbar vertebral level
using an eligible abdominal CT examination performed within 30 days before ICI
initiation. Skeletal muscle area (SMA), skeletal muscle index (SMI), and skeletal muscle
attenuation are evaluated using a standardized segmentation protocol. SMI is calculated
as skeletal muscle area divided by height squared (cm²/m²). Sarcopenia is defined using
sex-specific L3 SMI thresholds of <52.4 cm²/m² for men and <38.5 cm²/m² for women.

Two composite baseline indices are evaluated. The C-reactive protein-triglyceride glucose
index (CTI) is calculated as:

TyG = ln[(fasting triglyceride [mg/dL] × fasting glucose [mg/dL]) / 2]

CTI = 0.412 × ln[CRP (mg/L)] + TyG.

The cachexia index (CXI) is calculated as:

CXI = (SMI × serum albumin [g/dL]) / neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio.

CTI and CXI are analyzed as continuous variables and are evaluated in separate
multivariable models because they capture partly overlapping inflammatory, metabolic,
nutritional, and body-composition domains.

The principal survival outcomes are progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival
(OS), calculated from ICI initiation. Associations of baseline CTI and CXI with PFS and
OS are evaluated using multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression models
incorporating prespecified clinically relevant covariates. Model performance is explored
using Harrell's concordance index and time-dependent receiver operating characteristic
analyses for 12-month OS and 6-month PFS. Calibration and decision-curve analyses are
also performed. These analyses describe apparent performance within the study cohort and
are not externally validated.

Early longitudinal changes in body composition are evaluated in a landmark cohort of 140
patients who have both an eligible baseline CT and an early follow-up CT performed 8-12
weeks after ICI initiation and who are evaluable at the landmark assessment. Changes in
SMI, skeletal muscle attenuation, sarcopenia status, and body weight are assessed.
Landmark PFS and OS are calculated from the date of the early follow-up CT to reduce
immortal-time bias.

Radiologic response at the first response assessment is additionally described using
RECIST version 1.1 whenever adequate measurable disease and anatomic imaging are
available. Sensitivity analyses are performed in patients treated with ICI monotherapy,
and exploratory analyses assess relationships among CTI, CXI, sarcopenia, body
composition, and other clinical characteristics.

The study is observational and retrospective; all treatments were administered as part of
routine clinical care, and no study-specific therapeutic intervention was performed.

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

Arm groups
  -
    Label: Metastatic NSCLC Patients Treated With ICI-Based Therapy
    Description
      This cohort includes 155 adult patients with histologically confirmed metastatic
      non-small cell lung cancer who initiated immune checkpoint inhibitor-based systemic
      therapy at Ankara Etlik City Hospital between December 2022 and March 2025 and met the
      predefined eligibility criteria for baseline prognostic analyses. All patients had the
      clinical and laboratory data required for CTI and CXI calculation and an eligible

  -
    Type: DRUG
    Name: Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor-Based Therapy

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Progression-Free Survival (PFS)
    Description
      Time from initiation of immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI)-based therapy to radiologic or
      clinical disease progression or death from any cause, whichever occurred first.
      Progression-free survival (PFS) is evaluated in relation to baseline C-reactive
      protein-triglyceride glucose index (CTI), cachexia index (CXI), and prespecified clinical
      covariates.
    Time frame: From ICI initiation through the survival data cutoff of June 11, 2026.
  -
    Measure: Overall Survival (OS)
    Description
      Time from initiation of immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI)-based therapy to death from any
      cause. Overall survival (OS) is evaluated in relation to baseline C-reactive
      protein-triglyceride glucose index (CTI), cachexia index (CXI), and prespecified clinical
      covariates.
    Time frame: From ICI initiation through the survival data cutoff of June 11, 2026.
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Early Change in Skeletal Muscle Index (SMI)
    Description
      Percentage change in computed tomography (CT)-derived skeletal muscle index (SMI)
      measured at the third lumbar vertebral (L3) level between baseline and the early
      follow-up CT assessment. Skeletal muscle index is calculated as skeletal muscle area
      divided by height squared (cm²/m²). Early SMI change is evaluated both continuously and
      according to skeletal muscle loss of at least 5%.
    Time frame: Baseline to early follow-up assessment, approximately 8-12 weeks after initiation of ICI-based therapy.
  -
    Measure: Early Change in Skeletal Muscle Attenuation
    Description
      Absolute change in mean computed tomography (CT)-derived skeletal muscle attenuation,
      expressed in Hounsfield units (HU), between baseline and the early follow-up CT
      assessment at the third lumbar vertebral (L3) level.
    Time frame: Baseline to early follow-up assessment, approximately 8-12 weeks after initiation of ICI-based therapy.
  -
    Measure: Early Change in Body Weight
    Description
      Percentage change in body weight between initiation of immune checkpoint inhibitor
      (ICI)-based therapy and the early follow-up assessment. Body-weight change is evaluated
      continuously and according to body-weight loss of at least 5%.
    Time frame: Baseline to early follow-up assessment, approximately 8-12 weeks after initiation of ICI-based therapy.
  -
    Measure: Sarcopenia Transition Status
    Description
      Change in computed tomography (CT)-defined sarcopenia status between baseline and the
      early follow-up assessment. Patients are categorized as persistently non-sarcopenic,
      incident sarcopenia, resolved sarcopenia, or persistently sarcopenic according to
      sex-specific third lumbar vertebral (L3) skeletal muscle index (SMI) thresholds.
    Time frame: Baseline to early follow-up assessment, approximately 8-12 weeks after initiation of ICI-based therapy.
  -
    Measure: Objective Response Rate (ORR)
    Description
      Proportion of patients achieving complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) at the
      first response assessment according to Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors
      version 1.1 (RECIST 1.1), whenever measurable disease and adequate anatomic imaging are
      available. Patients with clinical or radiologic progression or death before the first
      scheduled response assessment are considered nonresponders.
    Time frame: At the first response assessment, approximately 8-12 weeks after initiation of ICI-based therapy.
  -
    Measure: Disease Control Rate (DCR)
    Description
      Proportion of patients achieving complete response (CR), partial response (PR), or stable
      disease (SD) at the first response assessment according to Response Evaluation Criteria
      in Solid Tumors version 1.1 (RECIST 1.1), whenever measurable disease and adequate
      anatomic imaging are available. Patients with clinical or radiologic progression or death
      before the first scheduled response assessment are classified as not achieving disease
      control.
    Time frame: At the first response assessment, approximately 8-12 weeks after initiation of ICI-based therapy.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria

    -  Age ≥18 years.

    -  Histologically confirmed metastatic non-small cell lung cancer.

    -  Receipt of at least one dose of ICI-based systemic therapy as part of routine
       clinical care.

    -  Eligible baseline abdominal CT obtained within 30 days before ICI initiation and
       suitable for L3-level body-composition analysis.

    -  Availability of baseline clinical and laboratory data required for CTI and CXI
       calculation.

    -  Availability of body-weight/cachexia-related information and survival follow-up.

  Additional criterion for the landmark longitudinal analyses

  - Eligible early follow-up CT performed approximately 8-12 weeks after ICI initiation and
  availability for landmark assessment.

  Exclusion Criteria

    -  No eligible baseline abdominal CT or inadequate image quality for L3 segmentation.

    -  Missing laboratory data required for CTI or CXI calculation.

    -  Missing relevant body-weight/cachexia data.

    -  Incomplete survival follow-up.

    -  Absence of a usable L3 level or other clinical/imaging circumstances precluding
       reliable body-composition assessment.

Study population
  The study population comprised 155 consecutive adult patients with histologically
  confirmed metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (mNSCLC) who initiated immune checkpoint
  inhibitor (ICI)-based therapy at Ankara Etlik City Hospital between December 2022 and
  March 2025. All patients included in the overall cohort had available baseline clinical
  and laboratory data required for calculation of the C-reactive protein-triglyceride
  glucose index (CTI) and cachexia index (CXI), together with an eligible abdominal
  computed tomography (CT) examination obtained within 30 days before ICI initiation and
  suitable for L3-level body-composition assessment. Longitudinal body-composition analyses

  follow-up CT performed 8-12 weeks after ICI initiation and were alive and evaluable at
