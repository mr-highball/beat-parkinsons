BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a single-center, open-label clinical study designed to evaluate alpha-synuclein
(ASYN) deposition in the brain of participants with Parkinson's disease (PD), as compared
with healthy elderly (HE) participants. Up to 18 participants (12 PD, 6 HE) are planned
to be enrolled. Each participant will receive a single IV injection of [¹⁸F]MK-0947 as
part of Part 1A followed by PET imaging using the Investigational United Imaging
NeuroEXPLORER(NX) camera or the Siemens Biograph mCT camera. If enrolled in Part 1B a
second injections and PET scan may be conducted in participants with PD and HE from Part
1A.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This is a single-center, open-label clinical study to evaluate the brain uptake and
pharmacokinetics of [¹⁸F]MK-0947 in participants with PD, compare to HE participants, and
demonstrate the utility of [18F]MK-0947 to quantify alpha synuclein pathology in
participants with PD.

Part 1A will enroll participants with PD (up to 12) and HE (up to 6) to evaluate
[¹⁸F]MK-0947 in the brain of participants with PD using two different PET scanners:
either standard resolution (~5 mm) Siemens Biograph mCT PET scanner (Std PET), or an
advanced high resolution (~1.5 mm) Investigational United Imaging NeuroEXPLORER® PET
scanner (NX-PET).

Safety assessments will be collected during this time. The characteristics of
[¹⁸F]MK-0947 in PD will be compared to those in HE participants.

Part 1B will acquire a second [¹⁸F]MK-0947 scan from some of the PD participants (up to
12) and HE participants (up to 6) from Part 1A on the scanner they were not scanned on in
Part 1A.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Brain uptake of [¹⁸F]MK-0947
    Description
      To visually and quantitatively assess brain uptake of [¹⁸F]MK-0947 as a PET radioligand
      for alpha-synuclein pathology (e.g. Standard Uptake Value Ratios (SUVR)).
    Time frame: From imaging to follow up, an average of 1 year
  -
    Measure: Safety of [¹⁸F]MK-0947: Incidence of Adverse Events
    Description: Incidence of adverse events (AEs) following administration
    Time frame: From enrollment to follow up, an average of 1 year
  -
    Measure: Standard Uptake Value Ratios (SUVR)
    Description
      To evaluate Standard Uptake Value Ratios (SUVR) in relevant brain regions from
      [18F]MK-0947 PET in PD and healthy participants.
    Time frame: From imaging to follow up, an average of 1 year
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Binding Comparison of [¹⁸F]MK-0947 PD and Healthy Elderly
    Description
      To compare the binding of [¹⁸F]MK-0947 in the brain of participants with PD with
      similarly age-matched HE via evaluation of suitable regional quantification parameter
      (e.g., SUVR) in regions known to be involved in α-synuclein pathology and regions known
      to not be involved in such pathology.
    Time frame: From imaging to follow up, an average of 1 year
  -
    Measure: Binding Comparison - Camera
    Description
      To compare the binding of [¹⁸F]MK-0947 in the brain of participants with PD with that in
      HE using the standard- and NX- PET
    Time frame: From imaging to follow up, an average of 1 year

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  All Participants (PD, HE)

         1. Must voluntarily sign and date each informed consent, approved by the Research
            Ethics Committee (REC) prior to the initiation of screening or study-specific
            procedures.

         2. For women of childbearing potential, agreement to remain abstinent (refrain
            from heterosexual intercourse) or use contraception, and agreement to refrain
            from donating eggs for 90 days following the last PET scan in each study
            period, as defined below:

         3. A woman is considered to be of childbearing potential if she is postmenarchal,
            has not reached a postmenopausal state (12 continuous months of amenorrhea with
            no identified cause other than menopause), and is not permanently infertile due
            to surgery (i.e., removal of ovaries, fallopian tubes, and/or uterus) or
            another cause as determined by the Investigator (e.g., Müllerian agenesis). The
            definition of childbearing potential may be adapted for alignment with local
            guidelines or regulations. Women of childbearing potential must remain
            abstinent or use 2 methods of contraception, one of which is a barrier method
            (i.e., male or female condom), for the study duration and 90 days after the
            last investigational agent injection.

         4. Male participants with partners of childbearing potential must commit to the
            use of 2 methods of contraception, one of which is a barrier method (i.e., male
            condom with or without spermicidal jelly), for the study duration and 90 days
            after the last investigational agent injection.

         5. Male participants must not donate sperm for the duration of the study and 90
            days after the last investigational agent injection.

         6. In the opinion of the Investigator, deemed to be physically able to participate
            in all visits throughout the duration of the trial.

    -  PD and HE Participants

         1. Adult males and/or females between the ages of 40 and 80 years old (inclusive)
            at the time of providing informed consent.

         2. The participant is in good health based on medical history and physical
            examination at screening.

         3. Score of ≥25 out of 30 on the MMSE, or score of ≥25 out of 30 on the MoCA.

    -  Participants with PD

         1. Diagnosed with clinically established Parkinson's disease according to UK-Brain
            bank clinical criteria. At least 2 cardinal motor features (including
            bradykinesia with resting tremor, rigidity, or postural instability) PD
            diagnosis established for at least 3 years, at the Screening Visit.

         2. Hoehn and Yahr score between 2-3.

         3. UPSIT score of ≤26

         4. Currently on stable PD medication in the judgment of the Investigator

         5. Participants are judged to be L-DOPA responsive and able to ambulate
            independently and without significant history of falls within 6 months.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  All Participants (PD, HE)

         1. Unwilling or unable to provide written informed consent and/or follow the
            procedures outlined in the protocol, in the opinion of the investigator.

         2. A general medical condition that in the opinion of the investigator may impair
            participant safety or the scientific integrity of the study, including, but not
            limited to:

              1. Significant hepatic impairment

              2. Significant renal insufficiency

              3. Significant cardiovascular, pulmonary, endocrine, or systemic chronic
                 inflammatory disorder

              4. Clinically significant infection within 30 days at study entry

              5. History of severe allergic reactions or hypersensitivity to PET tracers or
                 related compounds

         3. History of cancer that in the opinion of the investigator may impair
            participant safety or the scientific integrity of the study.

         4. Any disability that would, in the opinion of the investigator, prevent
            completion of study procedures or assessments (including but not limited to
            blindness or significant visual impairment, deafness or significant hearing
            impairment, speech impairment, or sensory or motor dysfunction).

         5. Participant is unable or unwilling to undergo CT, PET procedures or for whom
            MRI (Part 1 only) is contraindicated (e.g. have aneurysm clip, metal fragments,
            internal electrical devices such as a cochlear implant, spinal cord stimulator
            or pacemaker), have claustrophobia that cannot be medically managed or are
            unable to lie still for the imaging procedures.

         6. Prior participation in other radiologic research protocols or clinical care in
            the last year in addition to the radiation exposure expected from participation
            in this clinical study, such that radiation exposure exceeds the effective dose
            of 50 mSv, which would be above the acceptable annual limit established by the
            US Federal Guidelines.

         7. Pregnant or breastfeeding female.

         8. Refusal to use highly effective contraception if of childbearing potential (if
            applicable).

         9. Approximately 30 days prior enrollment in an experimental study evaluating
            pharmacological therapies.

        10. Current or prior participation in a trial of an investigational therapeutic
            product targeting alpha-synuclein, unless documented to have only received
            placebo.

        11. Any finding (including history and medication use) that would in the opinion of
            the Investigator compromise participant safety or the scientific integrity of
            the study.

        12. Unwillingness or inability to follow the procedures outlined in the protocol.

        13. Has received an investigational drug within 30 days or 5 plasma half-lives
            (whichever is longer) before inclusion into this study, unless in the opinion
            of the Investigator, such medication will not constitute a risk for participant
            safety or the scientific integrity of the study.

        14. Participant is mentally or legally incapacitated.

        15. Has any other clinically significant condition or situation that has the
            potential to interfere with study conduct, in the judgment of the investigator

    -  HE participants

         1. Current or active neurological brain disorder.

         2. First degree relative with idiopathic PD (parent, sibling, child).

         3. Major surgery or donation or loss of 400 mL or more of blood within 4 weeks
            prior to dosing, or longer if required by local regulation

    -  Participants with PD

         1. Have evidence of an alternative form of parkinsonism (e.g., drug-induced
            parkinsonism due to neuroleptic use) or other parkinsonian disorders (e.g.,
            MSA, PSP), as determined by UK Brain Bank Clinical criteria or investigator
            judgement.

         2. Participant is a known mutation carrier of LRRK2, PINK1, PARK2, or DJ-1

         3. Have known history of negative SAA test (via skin punch or CSF sample) and/or
            DaTscan without evidence of striatal dopaminergic deficit.

         4. Have a modified Hoehn and Yahr score of 4 or greater

         5. Be living in a nursing home or assisted living facility

         6. Have a history of dementia (due to AD, PD, or other cause)

         7. Have a history of significant disabling CNS disease other than PD

         8. Have had a prior history of brain surgery

         9. Major surgery or donation or loss of 400 mL or more of blood within 4 weeks
            prior to dosing, or longer if required by local regulation
