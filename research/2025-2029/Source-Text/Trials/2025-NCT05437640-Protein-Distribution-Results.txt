Title: Protein distribution and Parkinson disease crossover trial, posted results
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05437640?tab=results

Registry ID: NCT05437640
Publication date: 2025-06-15
Date basis: First results posting in the official registry. Original registration first posted 2022-06-29. This summary uses the registry snapshot last updated on 2026-06-22, which includes later outcome reporting.
Coverage: Official registry results tables, participant flow, and adverse events. Local factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

Six participants started each treatment sequence and all 12 completed the first period. Eleven completed the second period after one participant in the consistent-protein-first sequence withdrew during washout with an adverse event. The flow period lists a one-week washout while both sequence descriptions say two weeks. The washout duration is unresolved.

The motor outcome is an aggregate MDS-UPDRS Part II score. Redistribution-labeled results (n = 11) report a mean of 11.27 at baseline and 9.45 afterward. Consistent-labeled results (n = 12) report 9.58 and 9.42, respectively. Read these as descriptive registry cells. The results table supplies no paired crossover contrast, no period-adjusted treatment effect, and no individual symptom estimates. The group descriptions invoke both sequences.

The two actigraphy sleep efficiency summaries were 72.7% for redistribution and 72.6% for consistent protein, with 11 participants reported for each. These figures concern time asleep while in bed. They do not measure daytime sleepiness. The other-adverse-event table reports one participant with migraines and one with pulmonary edema under the consistent-protein label, against no events under the redistribution label, with 12 at risk per label as reported. The registry text does not establish attribution, whether the events involved different individuals, or which led to withdrawal.

Both regimens concerned the timing of total dietary protein. The trial is a single source family with the 2022 registration entry and does not isolate or grade any food preparation.
