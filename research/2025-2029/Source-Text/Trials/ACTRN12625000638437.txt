The prospective record compares oral saffron extract tablets totaling 28 mg/day with a matched cellulose-based placebo for 12 weeks. The registration plans 80 participants. The completed record reports 86. Eligibility selects women aged 50 to 70 with elevated DASS-21 depression and PROMIS sleep disturbance scores. The exclusions explicitly include Parkinson and Alzheimer disease.

The Depression, Anxiety, and Stress Scale depression subscale is the primary endpoint at week 12. PROMIS Sleep Disturbance and PROMIS Sleep-Related Impairment are two separate secondary outcomes. The latter includes daytime functioning. The trial registers no independent fatigue-severity or insomnia severity scale.

The current record links the final journal article (PMID 42389708, PMCID PMC13318664, DOI 10.3389/fnut.2026.1838513). The results paper uses a registry link ending in p. The official page displays the canonical identifier without that suffix. Registry and article describe the same cohort. The article's Table 2 reports DASS depression and sleep-related impairment differences favoring the extract, and an inconclusive sleep-disturbance comparison.
