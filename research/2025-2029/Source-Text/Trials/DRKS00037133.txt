Title: Randomised, double-blind, controlled, crossover intervention study to investigate the health-promoting effects of a Brassica oleracea-based diet in humans (pilot study)
Registry identifier: DRKS00037133
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00037133
Format: Selected official German Clinical Trials Register fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public posting: 2025-09-03
Last posted update: 2026-03-23
Status: Recruiting in the available record, 50 healthy adults planned. Actual start 2026-01-13. No results posted.
Design: Four crossover Brassica smoothies vary plant diversity or isothiocyanates. Red cabbage appears in background material, and the record does not identify which arm contains it.
Outcomes: Gut microbiome and related urine and breath measures. The registry gives no standing blood pressure, lightheadedness or Parkinson outcome. Distinct from the red-cabbage allergy trial DRKS00036475.
