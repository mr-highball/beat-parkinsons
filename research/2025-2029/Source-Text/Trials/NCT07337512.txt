BRIEF SUMMARY

The narrative describes a randomized, three-group study in 363 postpartum mothers with depression. It compares dates, moringa capsules, and a control over 28 days and assesses depression, salivary cortisol, and breast milk production.

ARMS AND INTERVENTIONS

The API supplies one structured arm labeled 3 and typed ACTIVE_COMPARATOR. The description under that arm gives the following three groups:

Dates (Phoenix dactylifera L.): 120 g daily, divided into three 40 g portions, for 28 days.
Moringa oleifera capsules: 1,000 mg daily, divided into two 500 mg doses, for 28 days.
Control, called placebo in the registry: regular meals three times a day and no added intervention for 28 days.

PRIMARY OUTCOME FIELD

The record lists one primary outcome field covering changes in depression score, cortisol, and breast milk production. Its description specifies that each measure is reported separately at week 0 and week 4 (day 28):

Depression: Beck Depression Inventory (BDI) score.
Salivary cortisol: concentration in μg/dL.
Breast milk production: volume in mL.
