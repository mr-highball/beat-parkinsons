Source: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04175106, Evaluating the Availability of Berry Phytonutrients Post-consumption of Fresh and Processed Blueberry by Healthy Adults
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04175106
First registration posting: November 22, 2019
First public results: February 9, 2026
Coverage: selected registration and adverse-event fields verified November 12, 2026

Four-way randomized crossover in healthy adults. Conditions were single-time consumption of 150 g Elliott blueberries, 150 g Olympia blueberries, a blueberry-rich protein bar, or a macronutrient-matched control beverage. The registered outcomes were serum and urinary polyphenol metabolites.

The adverse-event module lists an observation period from enrollment to study completion (mean 3.9 months, SD 1.7) and a reporting threshold of 0%. All four conditions list 0 of 18 participants with other adverse events, 0 of 18 with serious adverse events, and 0 of 18 deaths. The crossover assessed the same participants under the four conditions. It did not report a constipation severity score or enroll a Parkinson cohort.
