Registered split belt gait learning under topical capsaicin and heat

Official record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07054840
First public posting: 2025-07-08
Selected public registry fields, paraphrased.

This registry names capsaicin cream with a local heat application on the lower leg in older adults. Planned primary endpoints are learning magnitude, retention magnitude and forgetting index. It records a recruiting status with no posted results in the captured record. It does not assign smoked or ground paprika ingestion or measure a Parkinson freezing rate.
