Topic: Zanthoxylum-containing perioperative formula and bowel recovery
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07081802
Coverage: Official protocol arms and first defecation, factual extract
First posted July 23, 2025. Protocol NCT07081802 lists an oral Da Jian Zhong Tang decoction containing Zanthoxylum, ginger, ginseng and maltose as part of a perioperative bundle, plus transcutaneous electrical stimulation, prehabilitation and enhanced recovery. The protocol lists time to first defecation and flatus. The current record has no posted results. It does not isolate toasted fruit or measure chronic Parkinson constipation.
