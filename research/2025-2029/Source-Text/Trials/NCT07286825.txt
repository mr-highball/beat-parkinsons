Title: Aromatherapy on upper limb spasticity in stroke survivors
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07286825
NCT ID: NCT07286825
First posted: 2025-12-16
Coverage: Factual extract of official registry fields.

The completed 30-person pilot compares topical sweet-marjoram essential oil in fractionated coconut oil with the same carrier with massage after stroke. Upper-limb range of motion is primary. The design plans GAD-7 and PHQ-9 assessments at baseline and four weeks. The official record has no posted outcome results. It uses topical Origanum majorana and has no Parkinson cohort.
