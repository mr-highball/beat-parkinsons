Swallowing CT results in Parkinson disease

Source: UMIN Clinical Trials Registry, UMIN000050721, receipt R000057788
Official record: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000057788
Sponsor: Fujita Health University
Registration first disclosed: 2023-03-30
Registry results posted: 2026-11-03
Coverage: original English summary of selected results and participant flow, checked 2026-11-12

Of 27 patients who consented and underwent swallowing CT, 22 qualified for analysis. Three exclusions had progressive supranuclear palsy, and two were imaged upright. The 22 included 13 men and nine women, with a mean age of 73.3 years and a median Hoehn and Yahr stage of III (range II–IV). At least 14 were at risk of malnutrition or malnourished according to MNA-SF screening. The investigators did not collect medication ON/OFF status.

Compared with controls matched on age, sex and height, Parkinson participants had more penetration or aspiration and more multiple swallows with thin liquids, and more pharyngeal residue with both tested consistencies. Upper esophageal sphincter opening was shorter and smaller. The results also describe reduced anterior hyolaryngeal displacement and peak hyoid velocity. One aspiration occurred across 43 swallows and cleared with cough. The investigators reported no serious procedure-related adverse events.

The registry supplies no named culinary ingredient and no linked journal publication. The Fujita team previously presented thin and honey-thick liquid swallowing CT results in 17 Parkinson participants and 19 controls. The institutions and methods align, but the meeting abstract does not give a registration ID. The registry result and meeting abstract may report overlapping participants.
