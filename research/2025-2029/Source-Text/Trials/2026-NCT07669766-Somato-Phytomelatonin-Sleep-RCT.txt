Interventions: 2 mg/night plant-based phytomelatonin capsule (Somato tomato fruit extract) vs 10 mg synthetic melatonin vs placebo for 28 days.
Primary Outcome: Change from baseline in Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) Global Score at Day 28.

Verified Round 7 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
