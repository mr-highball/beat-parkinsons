REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Number of Participants Able to Independently Exit a Stimulation Program Using the Cionic Neural Sleeve
    Description
      Participants' ability to independently exit (terminate) an active stimulation program on
      the Cionic Neural Sleeve will be assessed during study visits. Independence is defined as
      successful completion of the task without verbal cueing or physical assistance from study
      staff. Study staff will document whether the participant is able to exit the program
      independently (yes/no) at each visit.
    Time frame: Day 0, Week 6, Week 12
  -
    Measure: Number of Participants Able to Independently Test Stimulation Using the Cionic Neural Sleeve
    Description
      Participants' ability to independently initiate and test stimulation on the Cionic Neural
      Sleeve will be assessed during study visits. Successful task completion is defined as the
      participant correctly activating stimulation and confirming perception of stimulation
      without assistance. Study staff will document successful independent testing (yes/no).
    Time frame: Day 0, Week 6, Week 12
  -
    Measure: Number of Participants Able to Independently Calibrate the Cionic Neural Sleeve System
    Description
      Participants' ability to independently complete device calibration of the Cionic Neural
      Sleeve system will be assessed during study visits. Calibration is defined as completion
      of the device-guided calibration procedure without verbal or physical assistance. Study
      staff will document successful independent calibration (yes/no).
    Time frame: Day 0, Week 6, Week 12
  -
    Measure: Safety of the Cionic Neural Sleeve
    Description
      To understand the device's safety profile and potential side effects, researchers will
      record any adverse events during their home use.
    Time frame: Day 0, Week 6, Week 12
Other outcomes
  -
    Measure: Qualitative Feedback on Stimulation Timing
    Description
      Qualitative feedback regarding stimulation timing during gait will be collected every 6
      weeks using structured interviews. Data will be used to assess user experience and
      perceived synchronization between stimulation delivery and functional movement.
    Time frame: Day 0, Week 6, Month 3
  -
    Measure: Usability of the Cionic Neural Sleeve
    Description
      Usability of the Cionic Neural Sleeve will be evaluated using the 10-item System
      Usability Scale with total scores ranging from 0 to 100. Scores greater than or equal to
      68 indicate above-average usability.
    Time frame: Week 12
  -
    Measure: Impact of the Cionic Neural Sleeve
    Description
      The Timed Up and Go (TUG) test will be used to assess functional mobility. Participants
      are instructed to stand up from a chair, walk 3 meters, turn around, return to the chair,
      and sit down. The total time to complete the task is recorded in seconds, with shorter
      times indicating better mobility. A minimum clinically important difference (MCID) of
      0.77 seconds will be used to interpret meaningful change in performance over time.
    Time frame: Day 0, Week 6, Week 12

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Self-reported gait impairment

    -  Have access to a smartphone with iOS or Android operating system with Bluetooth
       capabilities and internet connection

    -  Ability to walk with or without an assistive device for up to 1 minute

    -  Leg measurements within sizing range of the Cionic Neural Sleeve

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Daily occurrence of falls

    -  Implanted demand-type cardiac pacemaker or defibrillator

    -  Malignant tumor or existing thrombosis in the leg

    -  Fracture or dislocation in the leg that could be adversely affected by motion from
       stimulation
