Title: Investigating the effect of a nasal drop based on Persian medicine for insomnia in depressed patients
Registry ID: IRCT20250606066088N1
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/84570
First registered: 2026-05-11.
Text coverage: Official IRCT fields and WHO mirror.

The randomized masked trial specifies an intranasal mixture of lavender, lemon balm and poppy in olive oil. Beck depression is a secondary outcome at baseline, day 14 and day 28. The provider target field gives 50 and the allocation narrative mentions 60. The official reader says recruitment complete while the WHO snapshot says recruiting. Actual enrollment and result fields are blank at review.

The preparation combines several constituents and does not isolate the brewed oral exposure. A separate numerical depression result remains open.
