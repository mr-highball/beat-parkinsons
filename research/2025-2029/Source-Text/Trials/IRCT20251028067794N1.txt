Title: Investigating the Effect of Ginger Supplementation on Antioxidant and Oxidant Markers, Clinical Outcome and Quality of Life in Patients with Parkinson's Disease
Registry ID: IRCT20251028067794N1
Source: https://irct.ir/trial/87235
Persian registry: https://fa.irct.ir/trial/87235
Registration date: 2026-04-22.
Archive scope: Factual summary of original English and Persian registration fields.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The randomized double-blind Isfahan trial lists 48 planned participants, with standardized 500 mg ginger powder tablets twice daily for 12 weeks or corn-starch placebo. The primary entry is total MDS-UPDRS. Quality of life and oxidative markers are secondary. The reviewed outcomes do not specify an individual constipation measure. The English record shows recruitment complete and the public records inspected here give no reported outcome estimates. The older bilingual catalog note records a discrepancy in the Persian antioxidant assay description. Neither page supplies an outcome for cooking with grated ginger.

November 25, 2026 registry recheck: The English and Persian pages resolve to the same registration. The English page still reports recruitment complete. The inspected records supply no posted outcome estimates.
