Registered oral mixed oils and adverse smell symptoms
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07518394
Registration: NCT07518394
Coverage: official ClinicalTrials.gov API source, intervention, planned adverse-event and posting fields

The first public posting was April 8, 2026. The official record lists completion on September 30, 2024 and no posted results. The respiratory illness trial compares an oral formulation containing cinnamon essential oil and six other essential oils with placebo. Follow-up asks about adverse symptoms including changes in smell. The protocol does not identify ground bark, an isolated cinnamon dose comparison, a validated olfactory impairment scale or a Parkinson subgroup. Planned symptom collection gives no result or oil-specific attribution. [Official API record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT07518394).
