The registry proposes a randomized comparison in 64 adults with type 2 diabetes. The assigned intervention description specifies three dates added to the breakfast meal in the dietary program. The public brief summary also uses the word persimmon in a question about appetite hormones. The structured assignment and study title refer to dates, without a cultivar or drying specification. Keep the wording discrepancy open.

Proposed three-month outcomes include the Power of Food Scale for hedonic hunger and appetite hormone concentrations. Food-specific reward is distinct from clinical apathy. The latest record still lists recruiting and does not post outcome data. No Parkinson population is registered.
