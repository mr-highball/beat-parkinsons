BRIEF SUMMARY

The goal of this clinical trial is to test Safety and tolerability of single and repeated
dose of IMM02-KORA in subjects with PD-associated dyskinesia.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

The proposed study will assess the safety of IMM02-KORA (nalfurafine hydrochloride) - a
selective kappa opioid receptor agonist - and will comprise two phases. The first phase
(Ia) will aim to establish safety and tolerability by an accelerated dose titration
approach, concomitant with pharmacokinetic (PK) studies. The second phase (Ib) will aim
to establish a safe dosing regimen for repeated administration based on the previously
determined PK parameters.

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

Arm groups
  -
    Label: IMM02-KORA 1a and PK
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description
      Phase Ia will be a single oral dose accelerated titration administered at assumed
      clearance intervals. The doses will escalate in 2.5µg increments (2.5, 5.0, 7.5, 10.0,
      12.5µg) p.o., daily. Titration will continue until at least one participant responds
      positively, which will define the minimum inhibiting dose (MID) of dyskinesia symptoms.
      After a dose limiting toxicity (DLT), the accelerated titration will convert into a 3+3
      design, enrolling 3 additional participants at the same dose. If additional DLTs occur,
      that dose will be considered the maximum tolerated dose (MTD). If MTD is reached and
      fewer than 2 of 6 participants respond positively, a MID will not have been defined and
      the trial will stop.

      The PK study will start one week after the last administered dose while participants are
      monitored for AEs. For the PK study, 3 participants without DLTs will be admitted to the
      hospital for 3 days, at least 7 days after their previous IMM02-KORA dose.
    Intervention names
      - Drug: IMM02-KORA
  -
    Label: IMM02-KORA 1b
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description
      The phase Ib will start after completion of PK study. For this part, additional 10
      patients will be enrolled to take a repeated dose of IMP medication with the appropriate
      size and frequency for 4 weeks total duration, followed by additional 4 weeks of
      observations in the absence of the IMP administration.
    Intervention names
      - Drug: IMM02-KORA

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Incidence and severity of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) and DLT
    Description: Safety and tolerability of single increasing IMM02-KORA doses
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Obtain information on a minimum inhibitory dose (MID) on peak dose dyskinesia and evaluate initial efficacy of repeated administration on peak dose dyskinesia
    Description
      Determined by change from baseline of Abnormal Involuntary Movements Scale (AIMS). AIMS
      is 12-item scale scored by an observer to measure dyskinesia severity. Seven items are
      scored from 0-4 with zero being no dyskinesia observed and 4 being severe dyskinesia.
      Higher scores indicate a worse outcome.
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain information on a minimum inhibitory dose (MID) on peak dose dyskinesia and evaluate initial efficacy of repeated administration on peak dose dyskinesia
    Description: Determined by change from baseline of Hauser motor symptom diary
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain PK parameters necessary to calculate a repeated dose regimen
    Description: Determined by obtaining information on Cmax of IMM02-KORA in serum.
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain PK parameters necessary to calculate a repeated dose regimen
    Description: Determined by obtaining information on Tmax of IMM02-KORA in serum.
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain PK parameters necessary to calculate a repeated dose regimen
    Description: Determined by obtaining information on T1/2 of IMM02-KORA in serum.
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain PK parameters necessary to calculate a repeated dose regimen
    Description
      Determined by obtaining information on area under the plasma concentration versus time
      curve (AUC) of IMM02-KORA in serum.
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain PK parameters necessary to calculate a repeated dose regimen
    Description
      Determined by calculating a dose size and interval of administration that maintain a MID
      serum concentration
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain information on treatment interference with neurotransmitters
    Description
      Determined by change from pre-administration of serum dopamine, norepinephrine,
      epinephrine, and serotonin
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain information on treatment interference with neurotransmitters
    Description
      Determined by change from baseline of pre-administration of testosterone, FSH and LH
      levels
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Evaluate initial efficacy of repeated administration on peak dose dyskinesia
    Description
      Determined by post-treatment change from baseline of Abnormal Involuntary Movements Scale
      (AIMS) during the 1b portion. AIMS is 12-item scale scored by an observer to measure
      dyskinesia severity. Seven items are scored from 0-4 with zero being no dyskinesia
      observed and 4 being severe dyskinesia. Higher scores indicate a worse outcome.
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Evaluate initial efficacy of repeated administration on peak dose dyskinesia
    Description
      Determined by post-treatment change from baseline of Hauser motor symptom diary during
      the 1b portion.
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain additional efficacy information
    Description
      Determined by change from baseline of Dyskinesia as measured by MDS-UPDRS (Unified
      Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale) Part IV. This scale has a minimum of 0 and a maximum of
      24. Higher scores mean a worse outcome.
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Obtain additional efficacy information
    Description: Determined by change from baseline of grip strength measured with hand dynamometer
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks
  -
    Measure: Assess PK parameters during repeated dose administration
    Description
      Determined by assessing bi-weekly the steady state serum concentration of IMM02-KORA
      during repeated administration during the 1b portion
    Time frame: baseline up to 15 weeks

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  The subject presents with a diagnosis of idiopathic Parkinson's disease

    -  PD subjects taking stable doses of IR levodopa for at least 4 weeks before Screening
       Visit

    -  The dose of levodopa and all PD drugs used during the trial anticipated to remain
       unchanged throughout the duration of the trial.

    -  The patient must present symptomatic levodopa induced dyskinesias that is
       predictable (peak-dose dyskinesia) and is not troublesome, as assessed by the
       investigator

    -  The patient did not start taking anti-dyskinetic medication, however, is qualified
       to start anti-dyskinetic medication based on investigator's assessment

    -  The subject shows adequate compliance with the schedule of the trial visits,
       admission in the hospital for the PK study, and the completion of the
       self-evaluation with Hauser diary.

    -  Capable of giving signed informed consent which includes compliance with the
       requirements and restrictions listed in the informed consent form (ICF) and in this
       protocol.

    -  In order to understand the nature of this study it is expected that most
       participants will be able to speak, read, and understand English. However,
       additional translations of the informed consent may be made available after study
       initiation to include additional languages for participants

    -  Study participant must have the capacity and willingness to comply with
       contraception requirements.

    -  Study participant is willing and able to comply with all study requirements,
       according to the judgment of the Investigator.

    -  To be eligible for enrollment in phase Ia and Ib and for each treatment
       administration in phase Ia, vital signs should be in the following ranges: Heart
       rate >50 and <100 bpm, Systolic pressure >100 and <180 mmHg, Diastolic pressure >50
       and <100 mmHg, Blood oxygenation (by pulse-oximetry) >95%. If the vitals are outside
       of the range, they will be reassessed 3 times at 10 minutes resting intervals and
       the investigator will decide the course of action including reassessment of the
       vitals, or excluding enrollment.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Unmanaged/uncontrolled comorbidities, including current clinically significant
       gastro intestinal, renal, hepatic, endocrine, pulmonary or cardiovascular disease,
       acute gastric/duodenal ulcer, hypertension that is not well controlled, asthma,
       chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and unstable Type I diabetes. Subjects
       with a history of gastric/duodenal ulcer who have not had a recent episode of acute
       gastritis and are not currently experiencing gastric pain will be eligible for
       inclusion.

    -  Neoplastic disorder, which is either currently active or has been in remission for
       less than one year.

    -  Patients with severe renal or hepatic impairment.

    -  Concomitant disease likely to interfere with trial medication (e.g. capable of
       altering absorption, metabolism, or elimination of the trial drug).

    -  The subject has any clinically significant illness that, in the Investigator's
       opinion, might interfere with the subject's ability to participate in the trial.

    -  The subject is suffering from any cognitive decline, dementia or other psychiatric
       illness that prevents him/her from giving informed consent or will not allow the
       determination of trial outcomes

    -  Signs and symptoms suggestive of transmissible spongiform encephalopathy, or family
       members who suffer(ed) from such.

    -  Female subject that is pregnant or lactating.

    -  The subject is experiencing exclusively diphasic, off-state, myoclonic, dystonic, or
       akathitic dyskinesias without peak dose dyskinesia.

    -  The subject has received neurosurgical intervention related to PD or slow-release
       implants (e.g. deep brain stimulation, thalamotomy, focused ultrasound therapy,
       continuous levodopa-carbidopa intestinal gel infusion, continuous subcutaneous
       apomorphine infusion, etc) or is scheduled to do so during the trial period.

    -  Patients that started to take anti-dyskinetic medication

    -  Patients taking prohibited medication as provided in Table 7 (Prohibited
       medications).

    -  The subject has legal incapacity or limited legal capacity.

    -  Study participant has been exposed to an investigational drug within 90 days, prior
       to the first dose of IMP, or is currently participating or planning to participate
       in another study of an investigational drug.

    -  Subjects with current diagnosis of substance abuse (DSM-IV) or history of alcohol or
       drug abuse in the past three months.

    -  Smoking or vaping products containing nicotine, tobacco or any other products,
       currently and in the past 1 year.

    -  Patients that manifested DLTs at any dose level during phase Ia will be excluded
       from the PK and repeated dose studies (Ib).
