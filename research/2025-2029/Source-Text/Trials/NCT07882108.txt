Title: Ginger-PD: Ginger as a Modifier of Ghrelin Levels and Gut Function in Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07882108
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT07882108
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting: 2026-10-20
Last posted update: 2026-10-21
Status: Recruiting at verification. Target enrollment 72. No posted results.
Design: Randomized two-dose trial of the same ginger-root extract capsules in Parkinson’s, REM sleep behavior disorder, and comparison groups. Sunflower seed oil is one ingredient in both capsule regimens. Its amount and processing grade are unspecified.
Outcomes: Ghrelin, gut function and tolerability, plus MDS-UPDRS I–IV, MoCA and semantic fluency. No sunflower oil contrast.
