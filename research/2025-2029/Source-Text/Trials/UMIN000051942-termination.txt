Termination of the NCNP carbonated-water contrast study

Source: UMIN Clinical Trials Registry, UMIN000051942, receipt R000057259
Official record: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000057259
Registration first disclosed: 2023-08-18
Registry update: 2025-08-19
Coverage: original English summary of selected recruitment and results fields, checked 2026-11-12

The UMIN record lists the study as terminated. Its progress note says the study had not begun and recruitment difficulty led to discontinuation. Enrollment count and results fields are blank. The planned comparison was videofluoroscopy after two 10 mL barium preparations made with tap water and carbonated water. The termination supplies no observed swallowing outcome. A ministry mirror has shown a conflicting recruitment status, so the termination explanation is attributed to the original UMIN record.
