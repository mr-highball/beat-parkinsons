A Phase 1/2a, Open-Label, Single Ascending Dose Study to Evaluate the Safety, Tolerability, and Preliminary Efficacy of KL002, an AAV9 Gene Therapy, in Subjects With Parkinson's Disease

BRIEF SUMMARY

To assess the safety and tolerability of single doses of KL002 over 78 weeks in subjects
with Parkinson's disease。

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This is an open-label, non-randomized, phase 1/2a single ascending dose study, consisting
of Dose Escalation and Dose Expansion parts, to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and
efficacy of KL002 in patients with Parkinson's disease. Eligible subjects will enter the
dose escalation part of the study, receive a single stereotactic intraputaminal
administration of KL002, and be followed for 18 months in the core study and up to 5
years total in long-term follow-up (LTFU) with safety and efficacy assessments.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    1. Males or females ≥ 40 and ≤ 75 years old

    2. Diagnosis of Parkinson's disease per UK Parkinson's Disease Society Brain Bank
       Clinical Diagnostic Criteria

    3. Duration of Parkinson's disease motor symptoms: 3-12 years

    4. Hoehn and Yahr Stage II-IV (as determined in Practically Defined OFF state)

    5. The OFF period MDS-UPDRS III score > 30 ， and the percent improvement in MDS-UPDRS
       III in the levodopa/carbidopa challenge test is ≥ 30%

    6. Must have been on a stable regimen of Parkinson's disease symptomatic therapy for at
       least 4 weeks prior to KL002 administration, and must remain stable through dosing
       unless otherwise approved by the Medical Monitor

    7. Must agree to postpone any Parkinson's disease-related neurological surgery,
       including deep brain stimulation, until after completing the 24-month follow-up

    8. Patient has the ability to understand the purpose and risks of the study and provide
       written informed consent and authorization to use protected health information in
       accordance with national and local privacy regulations

  Exclusion Criteria:

    1. The diagnosis of a significant central nervous system (CNS) disease other than
       Parkinson's disease that may be a cause for the patient's Parkinson's disease
       symptoms or may confound study objectives

    2. Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA) score of < 24

    3. Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HAMD) score of > 17

    4. Hypersensitivity or contraindications to corticosteroid use

    5. History of previous brain surgery for Parkinson's disease, such as deep brain
       stimulation or focused ultrasound

    6. Clinically significant abnormalities in laboratory test results at screening

    7. Atypical Parkinson's disease or secondary Parkinson's disease caused by factors such
       as chemicals or toxins

    8. Use of ambroxol within 8 weeks of dosing

    9. Any type of prior gene or cell therapy

   10. Contraindications to general anesthesia

   11. Any factors that may cause the subject to be unable to follow the study protocol,
       including medical or social factors, or geographical inconvenience
