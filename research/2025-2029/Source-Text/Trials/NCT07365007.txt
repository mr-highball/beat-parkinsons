Title: LASO-3 dietary counseling feasibility in cancer survivors with chemotherapy-induced neuropathy
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07365007
Registration: NCT07365007
Publication date: 2026-01-23
Date basis: First public registry posting.
Coverage: Official registered fields checked November 26, 2026.

The study compares a dietary-counseling program with general health education in adults with chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy. Flaxseed oil is one optional source of alpha-linolenic acid, alongside other oil, nut and seed options. The primary registered outcomes concern feasibility, with acceptability as a secondary outcome. Recruitment was open and no numerical results were posted. The program does not assign flaxseed oil or a Parkinson pain comparison.
