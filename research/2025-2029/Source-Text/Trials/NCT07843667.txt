BRIEF SUMMARY

In this study, the relationships between pain severity, chest mobility, cough strength,
muscle strength, physical activity levels, and quality of life in Parkinson's disease
patients will be investigated.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

In patients with Parkinson's disease, motor and non-motor symptoms can vary depending on
physical inactivity, the nature of the disease, and its treatment. The relationships
between pain, chest wall immobility, cough strength, muscle strength, physical activity
levels, and quality of life in Parkinson's patients have not yet been fully elucidated in
the literature. Therefore, this study aims to shed light on this subject.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Total quality of life score evaluated by Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire (PDQ-39) questionnaire.
    Description
      The status of quality of life was evaluated using Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire
      (PDQ-39) questionnaire. Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire-39 (PDQ39) is a 39-item quality
      of life questionnaire. Evaluation for each question item ranges from 0 (no problem) to 4
      (constant problem). Low scores in total indicate an improvement in people's quality of
      life.
    Time frame: through study completion, an average of 1 year
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Cough Strength
    Description: Cough Strength was evaluated using a Peak Flow Meter.
    Time frame: through study completion, an average of 1 year
  -
    Measure: Daily average step count
    Description
      Daily average step counts were recorded via a pedometer everyday within 3 days after
      confirming enrolment. All individuals will be asked to carry their pedometers with them
      during the day and record to their diaries.
    Time frame: through study completion, an average of 1 year
  -
    Measure: Hand grip strength
    Description: Hand grip strength was evaluated using a hand dynamometer.
    Time frame: through study completion, an average of 1 year
  -
    Measure: Chest mobility
    Description: Chest mobility was evaluated using a tape measure.
    Time frame: through study completion, an average of 1 year
  -
    Measure: Cognitive status
    Description
      Cognitive state will be evaluated using The mini-mental state examination (MMSE). It is
      an 11 question test. The highest score from the test is 30 points. Scores of 23 and lower
      indicate cognitive dysfunction, and scores of 24 and higher indicate normal cognitive
      level.
    Time frame: through study completion, an average of 1 year

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria for Parkinson's patients;

    -  Being over 18 years old,

    -  Volunteering to participate in the research,

    -  Being diagnosed with Parkinson's disease

    -  Comorbid conditions (such as hypertension, diabetes) are under control

    -  Being able to perceive assessments (Standardized Mini Mental Test score of 24 and
       above)

    -  Having the ability to ambulate and walk independently

    -  Being in stage 1 - 4 according to the Modified Hoehn-Yahr Scale

  Exclusion criteria for Parkinson's patients;

    -  Sudden drug or dose change during the study period

    -  A history of any acute/chronic orthopedic, cardiovascular, pulmonary, psychiatric or
       neuromuscular disease that may prevent measurements from being made.

    -  Having had myocardial infarction and pulmonary embolism in the last 30 days,

    -  Having a history of additional neurological (stroke, head trauma, peripheral nerve
       diseases, brain and spinal cord tumors, etc.) diseases other than Parkinson's
       disease

    -  Having severe vision loss or vestibular dysfunction

    -  Patients with blood pressure values above 140/90 and below 90/50 in blood pressure
       measurements made before the study, with arrhythmia on ECG, with a history of
       shortness of breath and/or palpitation
