Title: Date-palm distillate for migraine symptoms, randomized clinical trial
Registry ID: IRCT20260702070048N1
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/91429
Persian registration: https://fa.irct.ir/trial/91429
First registered: 2026-07-19, prospective.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC
Coverage: Factual extract of the official registry, with study aim, arms and outcome fields inspected. Results are unreported.

The registry describes a masked parallel study targeting 80 adults with migraine. The intervention arm specifies oral Phoenix dactylifera distillate and the comparison arm specifies placebo. Both receive standard migraine care. The planned exposure lasts eight weeks. The record does not identify the part of the tree used to make the distillate or establish that the product corresponds to whole dried date fruit.

The primary outcome is migraine attack severity. The outcome description names a numerical rating scale, days 30 and 60, and an extract labeled Taroona. The measurement and timepoint fields describe a visual analogue scale at baseline, week 4 and week 8. The exact administered product label, primary scale and assessment schedule need confirmation from a protocol or results report. Secondary fields include recorded attack frequency and migraine-related disability.

Round 1 claim 0502 concerns dried dates and pain severity in people with Parkinson disease. This registration provides a possible pain-outcome source from a different population and preparation. No observed effect is available.

Registry follow-up, 2026-11-25. The Persian original continues to list expected recruitment from September 2026 through May 2027 and leaves actual recruitment start and end blank. The inspected record provides no group results or administered product clarification. Keep this as a prospective distillate source and preserve the differences in product, scale and timepoint across registry fields.
