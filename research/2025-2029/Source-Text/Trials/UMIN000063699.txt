Title: Effect of Acute Medium-Chain Triglyceride Ingestion on Cognitive Fatigue in Healthy Young Adults: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Crossover Trial
Registry: UMIN000063699; receipt R000072895
Official source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000072895
Registration: 2026-11-19; first public disclosure 2026-11-26; inspected 2026-11-27.

This randomized, double-blind crossover record targets 30 healthy adults aged 18–39. It compares single administrations of 16.3 mL canola oil and 16.3 mL medium-chain triglyceride oil, each mixed with oatmeal, before cognitive fatigue tasks. Stroop reaction time and accuracy are primary; self-rated mental fatigue on a visual analog scale is secondary. It excludes neurological diseases, specifies no refining grade, and has no oil-free control.

The registry says results are unpublished and leaves outcome and publication fields empty. It lists 30 enrolled and recruitment no longer active, but also an anticipated start of 2026-11-27 and last follow-up of 2029-03-31; actual recruitment and completion chronology therefore remain uncertain. No outcome direction is available, and it is not a Parkinson cohort.
