Title: Oregano and basil leaves in coronary artery disease
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07123181
NCT ID: NCT07123181
First posted: 2025-08-14
Last update posted: 2026-06-16
Coverage: Factual summary of updated official registry fields.

The planned comparison adds dried oregano and dried basil leaves to usual treatment in people with unstable angina. It plans inflammation and lipid outcomes. The record shows 70 planned participants, recruiting status and no posted results. The estimated completion date of October 22, 2026 has passed. Actual completion has not been verified. The combined intervention, population and registered outcomes do not establish an oregano-specific Parkinson anxiety result.
