Title: Effects of Grape Powder on Psychological Distress and Gut Microbiota in College Students

Rutgers registers a four-week randomized comparison of freeze-dried table-grape powder with placebo. College students report psychological outcomes. The primary outcome list includes the 14-item Hamilton Anxiety Scale, 17-item Hamilton Depression Rating Scale, and 10-item Perceived Stress Scale. The snapshot status is ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING and hasResults is false. A Parkinson population is outside the registration scope.
