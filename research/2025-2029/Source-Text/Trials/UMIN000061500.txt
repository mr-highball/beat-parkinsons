Title: Exploratory study of anti-fatigue effects of tea ingestion during exercise under heat stress
Trial ID: UMIN000061500
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000070370
First public disclosure: 2026-05-11
Publication year: 2026
Coverage: Official public registry design, intervention and outcome fields

The registry describes an open, nonrandomized crossover with a target of eight healthy men aged 20 to 39, comparing a test beverage and a placebo during exercise under heat stress. The primary measures concern autonomic activity and salivary stress markers in recovery. Subjective fatigue in recovery is secondary. The public intervention fields provide neither the tea type nor ingredients, preparation or caffeine content. The record has no posted numerical results. This is a candidate trail for an indirect fatigue comparison once the beverage identity and outcomes are available.
