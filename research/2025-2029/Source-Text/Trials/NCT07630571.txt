Official ClinicalTrials.gov protocol (NCT07630571, start 2026-06-05, n=30 healthy volunteers, status COMPLETED, hasResults=False). Uses oral horseradish stimulation as a human acute oral and upper-airway discomfort provocation model (0-10 NRS) alongside chili oil burning pain to test relief by oral HA35 (companion to NCT07947511 AITC oral pain challenge, n=24, not yet recruiting). No posted results; confirms horseradish's acute pungent local oral-discomfort profile rather than chronic PD pain relief.
