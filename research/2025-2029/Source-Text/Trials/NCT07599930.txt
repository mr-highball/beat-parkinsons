Design: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover. Estimated enrollment is 20 perimenopausal women aged 40–55. Participants consume each assigned snack for four weeks.

Intervention: TulaCode Cinnamon Granola Bark. The registered ingredient list includes nutmeg along with amaranth, coconut flakes, chia seeds, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, pecans, Brazil nuts, almonds, dates, banana, extra-virgin olive oil, cocoa mass, cocoa butter, chicory root fiber, cinnamon, cloves, ginger, vanilla pod, vanilla flavoring and salt.

Comparator: Calorie-matched commercially available chocolate cereal snack.

Secondary mood outcome: Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale, assessed at baseline and four weeks.
Secondary sleep outcome: Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, assessed weekly from baseline through four weeks.
