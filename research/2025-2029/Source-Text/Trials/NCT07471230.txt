BRIEF SUMMARY

This clinical trial investigates the safety and therapeutic potential of mesenchymal stem
cell-based therapies in patients with parkinsonism.

The study evaluates neuroinduced mesenchymal stem cells and mesenchymal stem cell-derived
exosomes as potential regenerative treatments targeting neurodegeneration within
dopaminergic pathways.
