Results summary posting: 2025-02-04

The open randomized crossover enrolled 15 and compared brown rice with white rice. The population was female university students aged 18–25. Its primary endpoint was post-meal Stanford Sleepiness Scale. The registry reports less drowsiness after a retort-packaged processed brown-rice meal and no correlation of drowsiness with glycemic index. Arm-level Stanford means, uncertainty, nocturnal outcomes and journal-publication links are absent from the public summary.
