The completed Tunisian randomized comparison reports 59 participants and double blinding. It compares EVACUR with starch placebo over eight weeks. The supplement includes dry nigella seed extract, ginger, several other botanicals and micronutrients. Registered outcomes include the Functional Assessment of Chronic Illness Therapy Fatigue score and disease activity. The registry provides a protocol and no summary results.

The protocol describes 2:1 allocation to EVACUR or matched starch placebo, FACIT-F at baseline and months one and two, and a baseline FACIT-F eligibility threshold below 34. One capsule contains 80 mg of dry Nigella sativa extract among other botanicals and micronutrients. The posting contains methods but no numeric outcomes.
The official registration shows completion and does not have a results module. References labeled as results have dates preceding the trial and concern general fatigue. They cannot establish this study outcome. No isolated Parkinson or ground-seed result is available.
