BRIEF SUMMARY

This multicenter retrospective study aims to develop, validate, and clinically apply an
artificial intelligence (AI)-powered electrocardiography (ECG) algorithm for the
diagnosis and prognostic prediction of cardiovascular diseases, including coronary artery
disease and heart failure. By utilizing encrypted raw 12-lead ECG text data and deep
neural network models, the study evaluates the effectiveness of AI-enhanced ECG in
detecting cardiac abnormalities and predicting clinical outcomes.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC) of AI-ECG Models for Differentiating Tachycardia Mechanisms Confirmed by Electrophysiological Study
    Description
      Diagnostic performance of Transformer-based deep learning models applied to 12-lead ECG
      raw data for differentiating tachycardia mechanisms, including atrioventricular nodal
      reentrant tachycardia (AVNRT) versus atrioventricular reentrant tachycardia (AVRT). The
      reference standard is the diagnosis confirmed by electrophysiological study and catheter
      ablation. Performance is summarized as AUC with 95% confidence interval (range 0-1;
      higher values indicate better discrimination).
    Time frame: Baseline
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Area Under the ROC Curve (AUC) of AI-ECG Models for Localizing Arrhythmia Origin Sites (Accessory Pathway, PVC, and VT) Confirmed by Successful Catheter Ablation
    Description
      Performance of AI-ECG models in classifying the anatomical site of the arrhythmia
      substrate from the pre-ablation 12-lead ECG, including (1) accessory pathway location in
      Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome, (2) the origin of premature ventricular contractions, and
      (3) the origin of ventricular tachycardia. The reference standard is the site identified
      by electrophysiological mapping and confirmed by successful catheter ablation. For each
      classification task, performance is reported as AUC with 95% confidence interval (range
      0-1; higher values indicate better classification).
    Time frame: Baseline

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Age 19 years or older

    -  Diagnosis of one of the following arrhythmias: paroxysmal supraventricular
       tachycardia (PSVT), Wolff-Parkinson-White (WPW) syndrome, premature ventricular
       contractions (PVC), or ventricular tachycardia (VT)

    -  Underwent an electrophysiological study (EPS) and catheter ablation

    -  Available 12-lead electrocardiogram (ECG) records that can be extracted as text
       (XML) files

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Inadequate ECG data quality, as judged by the investigator

    -  Missing medical records required for the study, as judged by the investigator
