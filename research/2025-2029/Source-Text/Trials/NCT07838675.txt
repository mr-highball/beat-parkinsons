BRIEF SUMMARY

The aim of this pilot study is to assess whether an online Mediterranean diet programme
called MediPD is acceptable and practical for people living with Parkinson's disease, and
whether it can help them make and maintain changes towards a Mediterranean-style diet.

The study will include 120 adults living in the United Kingdom with Parkinson's disease
who have access to an internet-enabled device and currently have low adherence to a
Mediterranean-style diet. Participants will be randomly assigned to one of two groups.
One group will receive access to the MediPD online programme, which includes short
educational videos, personalised dietary information, practical resources, goal-setting
tools, and online dietetic support. The other group will continue with their usual care
and will receive access to MediPD after completing the final study assessment.

Researchers will compare the groups to see whether MediPD improves adherence to a
Mediterranean diet after 24 weeks. The study will also examine dietary intake, non-motor
symptoms, quality of life, gastrointestinal symptoms, cognitive function, and how
acceptable and practical the programme is to use. Assessments will take place at the
start of the study, at 12 weeks, and at 24 weeks.

    Description

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in Mediterranean Diet Adherence using a Mediterranean Diet Score at 24 Weeks
    Description
      Adherence to a Mediterranean diet will be assessed using the Mediterranean Diet Score
      (MDS), derived from a validated 14-item questionnaire. Total scores range from 0 to 14,
      with higher scores indicating greater adherence to a Mediterranean diet.
    Time frame: Baseline to 24 weeks
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in Mediterranean Diet Adherence using a Mediterranean Diet Score at 12 Weeks
    Description
      Adherence to a Mediterranean diet will be assessed using the Mediterranean Diet Score
      (MDS), derived from a validated 14-item questionnaire. Total scores range from 0 to 14,
      with higher scores indicating greater adherence to a Mediterranean diet.
    Time frame: Baseline to 12 weeks
  -
    Measure: Change in self-reported dietary intake using a 24-hour Dietary Recall
    Description
      Dietary intake will be assessed using Intake24, an online 24-hour dietary recall tool.
      Outcomes will include mean daily intake of energy (kcal/day), foods and macronutrients
      (g/day and, where appropriate, percentage of energy), and micronutrients (mg or µg/day,
      as appropriate). Higher values indicate greater intake of food/nutrients.
    Time frame: Baseline, 12 weeks and 24 weeks.
  -
    Measure: Change in Parkinson's Disease Non-Motor Symptoms using the Movement Disorder Society Non-Motor Rating Scale (MDS-NMS)
    Description
      Non-motor symptoms will be assessed using the validated 52-item, rater-administered
      Movement Disorder Society Non-Motor Rating Scale (MDS-NMS). Total scores range from 0 to
      832, with higher scores indicating a greater non-motor symptom burden and a worse
      outcome.
    Time frame: Baseline, 12 weeks and 24 weeks.
  -
    Measure: Change in reported Gastrointestinal Symptoms using the Gastrointestinal Symptom Rating Scale (GSRS)
    Description
      Gastrointestinal symptoms will be assessed using the 15-item Gastrointestinal Symptom
      Rating Scale (GSRS). Items are scored from 1 to 7 and summed to provide a total score
      ranging from 15 to 105. Higher total scores indicate greater gastrointestinal symptom
      severity and therefore a worse outcome.
    Time frame: Baseline, 12 weeks and 24 weeks.
  -
    Measure: Change in quality of life using the Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire (PDQ-39)
    Description
      Quality of life will be assessed using the 39-item Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire
      (PDQ-39), which covers eight domains. Each domain score is transformed to a 0-100 scale,
      and the mean of the eight domain scores provides the PDQ-39 Summary Index. Higher Summary
      Index scores indicate poorer health-related quality of life and therefore a worse
      outcome.
    Time frame: Baseline, 12 weeks and 24 weeks.
  -
    Measure: Change in cognitive function using TestMyBrain cognitive test battery
    Description
      Cognitive function will be assessed using the TestMyBrain cognitive test battery,
      comprising Verbal Paired Associates, Visual Paired Associates, Backward Digit Span, Digit
      Symbol Matching, and Trail Making A and B. Each test score will be converted to a z-score
      and oriented so that higher scores consistently indicate better performance. The z-scores
      will be summed to provide an overall cognitive composite score with higher scores
      indicating better cognitive function.
    Time frame: Baseline, 12 weeks and 24 weeks.
Other outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in whole-gut transit time using the Blue Poop Challenge (optional assessment)
    Description
      Consenting participants who opt into this assessment will consume a standardised
      food-grade blue dye preparation and record the date and time of ingestion and the first
      appearance of blue colouring in their stool. Whole-gut transit time will be calculated as
      the elapsed time in hours between ingestion and the first appearance of blue stool.
      Higher values indicate a longer, slower whole-gut transit time.
    Time frame: Baseline, 12 weeks and 24 weeks.
  -
    Measure: Intervention acceptability using a study-specific semi-structured qualitative interview
    Description
      Intervention acceptability will be explored through a study-specific semi-structured
      interview with a purposive sample of approximately 10-15 participants from the MediPD
      intervention group. Interviews will explore satisfaction and acceptability, relevance and
      usefulness, engagement, feasibility, platform usability, perceived impact, participant
      burden, barriers and facilitators, and suggestions for improvement. Interviews will be
      audio-recorded, transcribed verbatim and analysed qualitatively to identify key themes.
      This assessment does not generate a numerical score and therefore has no minimum or
      maximum value.
    Time frame: Week 24

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Self-reported idiopathic Parkinson's disease

    -  Age 18 years or older

    -  Community-dwelling and resident in the United Kingdom

    -  Access to an internet-enabled device and an active email address

    -  Mediterranean Diet Score ≤7/13, indicating low adherence to a Mediterranean diet

    -  Proficient in English

    -  Able to provide informed consent

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Atypical or secondary parkinsonism

    -  Living in an institution

    -  Self-reported body mass index <18.5 kg/m²

    -  High risk of malnutrition, defined as MUST score ≥2

    -  Receiving prescribed nutritional support

    -  Significant cognitive impairment, defined as T-CogS ≤21

    -  Pregnancy

    -  Active inflammatory bowel disease

    -  Dysphagia requiring a pureed diet

    -  Food allergies that would prevent adherence to a Mediterranean-style diet

    -  Following a vegan diet that would prevent adherence to the study's
       Mediterranean-style dietary intervention.

    -  Current participation in another interventional trial

    -  Participation in an investigational medicinal product trial within the previous 90
       days where this may affect study outcomes or participant burden

    -  Previous participation in a My Moves Matter dietary programme.
