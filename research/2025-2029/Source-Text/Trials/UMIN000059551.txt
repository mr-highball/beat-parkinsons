Registered Mucuna and soybean tea sleep comparison
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000068082
Coverage: official registry selected design, food and outcome fields

First public registry date October 30, 2025. The planned crossover compares roasted Mucuna and black soybean tea with black soybean tea in healthy adults. Planned primary assessments include sleep quality and mood. The last inspected registry update lists recruiting, with results unavailable. Mucuna and soybean are separate genera from common bean, and the exposure is a tea rather than boiled pinto beans.
