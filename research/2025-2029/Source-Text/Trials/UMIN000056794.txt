Title: Paprika xanthophyll registration for everyday fatigue
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000064785
Registry: UMIN000056794
Publication date: 2025-01-27
Date basis: The original UMIN public registry date. Its December 2025 update still lists results as unpublished.
Coverage: Original UMIN registration and results status.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

Parallel individual randomization, double blinding and matched placebo. The registry specifies daily oral paprika-xanthophyll softcaps or placebo for eight weeks in a sample of 23 enrolled participants.
The primary measure is an everyday-fatigue visual analogue scale. The completed registration still marks results unpublished and carries no results values. This concerns red-pepper constituents and a registered measure, with no raw bell-pepper effect for Parkinson fatigue.

November 26, 2026 original follow-through.
The current Japanese public registry still describes results as unpublished. Enrollment is 23 compared with a planned 30, with no allocation counts or everyday fatigue visual analogue score values. It describes a paprika xanthophyll supplement. The adverse-event field supplies a separate observation.
