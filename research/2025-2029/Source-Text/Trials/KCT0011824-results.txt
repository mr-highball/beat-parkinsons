Korean registry results on urinary frequency and storage symptoms
Registry accession: KCT0011824
Registry: Korean Clinical Research Information Service
Source: https://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/search/detailSearch.do/33164
Review/registration date: 2026-04-09
Latest posted update: 2026-04-09
Coverage: selected verified public registry fields and source facts.

The official results summary lists 25 participants and reports favorable total IPSS, frequency, quality of life and composite storage symptoms. Storage combines frequency, urgency and nocturia. The summary gives no separate urgency contrast. The journal publication has the individual urgency comparison (p = .257). The registry links result and protocol attachments as well as the summary.
