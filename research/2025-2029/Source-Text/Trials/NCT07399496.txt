BRIEF SUMMARY

This single-site, open-label pilot study will evaluate the feasibility, tolerability, and
preliminary efficacy of accelerated intermittent theta-burst stimulation (iTBS) targeting
the dorsomedial prefrontal cortex (dmPFC) for apathy in individuals with Parkinson's
Disease (PD). Fifteen participants with PD and clinically significant apathy will undergo
imaging (EEG and MRI) and one day of TMS including twelve iTBS sessions across two
visits. Outcomes include adherence, tolerability, changes in apathy (Lille Apathy Rating
Scale), functional engagement, and neural target engagement assessed via resting-state
fMRI and EEG. Follow-up assessments will occur at one week, two weeks, and four weeks
post-treatment. Fifteen participants with PD without clinically significant apathy will
act as a comparison group and will undergo imaging (EEG and MRI), assessments, and
questionnaires at baseline. Caregivers for each participant will complete questionnaires
regarding their co-participant's symptoms as well as answer questions about their
experience as a caregiver.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This study is designed to explore a new treatment option for people with Parkinson's
disease who experience apathy, which means loss of motivation or interest in daily
activities. Apathy is common in Parkinson's disease and can lower quality of life, but
current treatments are limited. Investigators are testing whether a non-invasive brain
stimulation technique called repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) is
tolerable, acceptable and can be used to improve apathy in patients with Parkinson's
disease. TMS delivers brief magnetic pulses to specific areas of the brain that are
linked to motivation and decision-making. In this study, Investigators will use an
"accelerated" version of TMS, which gives several short sessions in a single day,
reducing the number of visits required. The purpose of this research is to see whether
this treatment approach is feasible, tolerable and and potentially effective at treating
apathy in people with Parkinson's disease. If successful, this research study will
hopefully lead to a larger study in the future where the efficacy of the treatments can
be studied.

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

Arm groups
  -
    Label: PD Apathetic group
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description
      Participants with Parkinson's disease and clinically significant apathy receive
      accelerated iTBS rTMS targeting the left dorsomedial prefrontal cortex (dmPFC) using a
      MagVenture MagPro system with a cooled figure-of-eight coil and Brainsight
      neuronavigation (slightly off midline). Resting motor threshold (rMT) is determined on
      the first stimulation visit (PEST) and stimulation is delivered at 120% rMT. Treatment
      occurs on one day, with 12 sessions separated by 10-15 min. Each session delivers 600
      pulses (50 Hz triplets; 2 s on/8 s off; ~190 s), totaling 7,200 pulses overall. Coil
      position/angle and scalp-to-cortex distance are tracked; tolerability/acceptability
      (headache, pain, scalp irritation, facial twitching, fatigue, fear/anxiety) is assessed
      before/after sessions.
    Intervention names
      - Device: Accelerated intermittent theta-burst stimulation (iTBS) rTMS to left dorsomedial prefrontal cortex (dmPFC)
  -
    Label: Non-Apathetic PD Control Group
    Type: NO_INTERVENTION
    Description
      Participants with Parkinson's Disease without clinically significant apathy will not
      undergo TMS but will act as a comparison group at baseline. This group will undergo
      imaging (EEG and MRI), assessments, and answer questionnaires with their caregiver.
Interventions

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: TMS adherence
    Description
      Proportion of planned accelerated iTBS sessions completed. Adherence is calculated as
      number of iTBS sessions completed out of 48 scheduled sessions (6 treatment days × 8
      sessions/day).
    Time frame: Day 1 through Day 14 (6 treatment days over approximately 2 weeks)
  -
    Measure: Frequency and Severity of TMS-Induced Side effects (TMS tolerability and acceptability)
    Description
      Participant-reported tolerability/acceptability assessed with a standardized
      questionnaire capturing frequency and severity of common TMS side effects (e.g.,
      headache, pain, scalp irritation, facial twitching, fatigue, fear/anxiety), collected
      during treatment days (pre/post sessions).
    Time frame: Measured during each treatment day (6 days within a 2-week period)
  -
    Measure: Apathy severity as measured by the Lille Apathy Rating Scale
    Description
      Change in apathy measured by the Lille Apathy Rating Scale (LARS) patient and
      caregiver/informant versions; analyzed as change from baseline to post-treatment and
      follow-up timepoints. Possible scores range from -36 to 36 with higher scores indicating
      greater apathy.
    Time frame: Baseline (Day 0), immediately post-treatment (Day 14), 2 weeks post-treatment, and 4 weeks post-treatment
  -
    Measure: Target engagement (dmPFC network modulation)
    Description
      Change in dmPFC target engagement measured by (1) resting-state fMRI functional
      connectivity of dmPFC to motivation/effort-related nodes and (2) EEG waveform/response
      metrics during motivation/effort tasks, comparing pre- vs post-intervention.
    Time frame: Baseline MRI/EEG assessments (Days 0-1) and post-treatment MRI/EEG assessments (Days 14-15)
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Goal attainment
    Description
      Change in goal-directed behavior measured by Bangor Goal-Setting Interview outcomes,
      including proportion/percent attainment of individualized goals across assessment
      timepoints.
    Time frame: Baseline (Day 0), immediately post-treatment (Day 14), 2 weeks post-treatment, and 4 weeks post-treatment
  -
    Measure: Change in apathy as measured by the Dimensional Apathy Scale
    Description
      Change in apathy-related symptoms measured using the Dimensional Apathy Scale (DAS),
      including patient/informant reports where applicable. Scores range from 0-24 on each of
      the three subscales (executive, emotional, and behavior/ cognitive initiation) with
      higher scores indicating greater apathy.
    Time frame: Baseline (Day 0), immediately post-treatment (Day 14), 2 weeks post-treatment, and 4 weeks post-treatment"
  -
    Measure: Change in apathy-related behavior as measured by the Frontal Systems Behavior Scale
    Description
      Change in apathy-related symptoms measured using the Frontal Systems Behavior Scale
      (FRSBE), including patient/informant reports where applicable. Apathy components include
      14 questions scored 0-4 for a range of scores 0-56 with higher scores indicating greater
      apathy.
    Time frame: Baseline (Day 0), immediately post-treatment (Day 14), 2 weeks post-treatment, and 4 weeks post-treatment"

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  PD Apathy Participant

  Inclusion criteria:

    1. 45-85 years of age

    2. Diagnosis of PD based on UK Brain Bank diagnostic criteria

    3. Symptomatic apathy as confirmed by a total score on the Apathy Evaluation Scale by
       the participant or the caregiver/co-participant (AES) of ≥37

    4. Stable on Parkinsons medications (not expected to change through the course of the
       treatment)

    5. Ability to have a co-participant/caregiver who meets criteria below.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    1. Claustrophobia or inability to lie supine in the scanner for an extended period of
       time

    2. barriers to making contact between the TMS coil and the skin (e.g. braids that
       cannot be removed)

    3. Contraindications to MRI/TMS safety screening: This includes but is not limited to
       implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemakers), metallic objects or fragments, and
       non-removable hair clips or piercings.

    4. Individuals with a diagnosis of bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, and/or active
       substance abuse disorder.

    5. Pregnancy

    6. Individuals with a history of seizure(s).

    7. Presence of cognitive impairment or dementia that would impact participant's
       capacity to give informed consent or ability to participate in study procedures as
       indicated by chart review and/or measured by the MoCA (<21)

    8. Acute suicidality as assessed by the Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS)
       or suicide attempt in the previous year

  Co-participant/Caregiver

  Inclusion Criteria:

    1. 18 years of age or older

    2. Is a reliable informant who has at least weekly contact with the participant and can
       speak to the participant's apathy and everyday functioning.

  Exclusion criteria:

  1) Unable to engage with study procedures in which co-participant input is needed.

  PD Control Group

       study)

  Exclusion Criteria:

    1. Symptomatic apathy as confirmed by a total score on the Apathy Evaluation Scale by
       the participant or the caregiver/co-participant (AES) of ≥37

    2. Claustrophobia or inability to lie supine in the scanner for an extended period of
       time

    3. Contraindications to MRI safety screening: This includes but is not limited to
       implanted medical devices (e.g., pacemakers), metallic objects or fragments, and
       non-removable hair clips or piercings

    4. Neuropsychiatric disorder diagnosis

    5. Uncorrected visual or hearing impairments, as indicated by self-report

    6. Pregnancy

    7. Presence of cognitive impairment or dementia that would impact participant's
       capacity to give informed consent or ability to participate in study procedures as
