Title: Effects of sauerkraut (from red cabbage) on symptoms of allergic rhinitis, inflammatory markers and the gut microbiome in young adults
Registry identifier: DRKS00036475
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00036475
Format: Selected official German Clinical Trials Register fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public posting: 2025-04-09
Last posted update: 2026-01-26
Status: Recruiting in the available record, 158 planned. Actual start 2025-06-01. No final enrollment or results posted.
Design: Parallel comparison of red-cabbage sauerkraut and an unfermented red-cabbage comparator in young adults with allergic rhinoconjunctivitis. Both foods are part of the comparison rather than a no-cabbage control.
Outcomes: Allergy symptom burden, rescue medication, quality of life, gastrointestinal symptoms, mood questionnaires, and safety. No designated orthostatic or Parkinson outcome.
Linked protocol: PMID 41857857 (2026). Distinct from DRKS00037133.
