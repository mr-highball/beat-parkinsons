Registry entry date: May 31, 2026
Public disclosure date: August 15, 2026
Coverage: original English summary of selected registry fields, checked November 12, 2026

The observational protocol targets 80 participants. In patients undergoing clinical esophageal manometry who have no clinically evident upper esophageal sphincter dysfunction, the primary outcome compares pressure and 12-direction ultrasound measurements for a 5 mL swallow of OS-1 oral rehydration solution. A separate Parkinson cohort undergoes ultrasound alone to compare participants with and without mild subjective swallowing discomfort. The registry marks the study preinitiation and the results unpublished. The anticipated last follow-up date is June 30, 2028.
