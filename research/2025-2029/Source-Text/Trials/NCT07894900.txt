BRIEF SUMMARY

This retrospective observational study aims to evaluate the efficacy and toxicity of
advanced radiotherapy (ART) techniques, including intensity-modulated radiotherapy
(IMRT), image-guided radiotherapy (IGRT), and stereotactic radiotherapy (SRT), used at
IRCCS Ospedale San Raffaele over the past 25 years and 5 months. The study includes
treatments delivered using conventional fractionation, moderate hypofractionation, and
ultra-hypofractionation. The primary endpoint is local control, defined as biochemical
control in patients with prostate cancer.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

25-ART-HSR is a retrospective observational study including patients treated between
January 2001 and May 2026. Patients received radiotherapy according to the standard
departmental protocols in place at the time of treatment and were followed for variable
periods after completion of radiotherapy, in some cases for more than 10 years, depending
on the anatomical site treated.

The study will include patients with a minimum follow-up of 3 months. The date of
approval by the Ethics Committee will serve as the cutoff date for the final follow-up
assessment.

Patient assessments were conducted in accordance with standard departmental clinical
practice and included evaluations at the initial consultation, at the end of treatment,
and during follow-up visits. Follow-up visits were scheduled every 3-6 months during the
first 3-5 years and annually thereafter, depending on the specific pathology. Assessments
included imaging examinations, such as CT, bone scintigraphy, MRI, and/or PET/CT, as
appropriate to the treated site and the histology of the primary tumor, as well as
laboratory tests, including tumor markers (e.g., PSA and CA 125) and pituitary hormone
levels, where applicable.

At each visit, the following data were recorded: medical history, physical examination
findings, toxicity assessments according to the Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse
Events (CTCAE), and the occurrence of local or regional (lymph node) recurrence,
skeletal-related events, visceral metastases, subsequent courses of radiotherapy,
additional lines of systemic therapy, or palliative treatments.

Data on biochemical monitoring, including tumor markers (e.g., PSA and CA 125) and
pituitary hormone levels, where applicable, will be collected. Data on local, regional
(lymph node), and distant disease control will also be collected from CT, MRI, and PET
scans performed during follow-up. The follow-up period will range from a minimum of 3
months for recently treated patients to more than 10 years for patients treated in
earlier years. Statistical, textural, and morphological radiomic features will be
extracted from three-dimensional (3D) imaging datasets.

Radiotherapy is a locoregional treatment primarily aimed at achieving local disease
control, a common objective across all subcohorts included in this study. Accordingly,
local control will serve as the primary endpoint for the oncological cohorts and will be
evaluated in comparison with historical controls, considering the potential improvements
associated with advanced radiotherapy techniques that have enabled dose escalation.

For patients with prostate cancer, biochemical relapse-free survival will be the primary
endpoint.

For patients treated for vascular pathologies or functional neurosurgical indications,
the primary objective will be either definitive treatment success, defined as the
obliteration of a vascular malformation, or improvement in neurological symptoms, such as
those associated with trigeminal neuralgia, essential tremor, or Parkinson's disease.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Local Relapse Free Survival
    Description: Local control of the disease (at the treated site)
    Time frame: From the date of radiotherapy end until the date of local progression or date of death from any cause, whichever came first, assessed up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Biochemical Relapse Free Survival
    Description: Biochemical control for prostate cancer
    Time frame: From the date of radiotherapy end until the date of biochemical progression or date of death from any cause, whichever came first, assessed up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Obliteration rate
    Description: Obliteration rate of arteriovenous malformations
    Time frame: Up to 5 years after the treatment
  -
    Measure: Improvement of clinical symptoms
    Description
      BNI (Barrow National Institute) scale for trigeminal neuralgia, a 5-point grading system,
      from Grade I (Excellent outcome) without pain to Grade V (Poor treatment response) with
      trigeminal pain with no improvement despite treatment
    Time frame: Up to 5 years after the treatment
  -
    Measure: Functional improvement after the treatment
    Description
      TETRAS score or UPDRS for movement disorders, which includes two scales, Activities of
      Daily Living Scale with 12 items and a range of points from 0 to 48 and Performance scale
      with 9 items and a range of points from 0 to 64. Lower means less tremor on both
      subscales, and the two are reported separately rather than summed
    Time frame: Up to 5 years after the treatment
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Marginal Relapse Free Survival
    Description: Marginal relapses for brain tumors
    Time frame: Up to 5 years after the treatment
  -
    Measure: Regional Relapse Free Survival
    Description: Relapses in regional lymph-nodes
    Time frame: Up to 5 years after the treatment
  -
    Measure: Distant Metastasis Free Survival
    Description: Distant recurrences
    Time frame: Up to 5 years after the treatment
  -
    Measure: Clinical Relapse Free Survival
    Description: Absence of local, regional and distant relapses during the follow-up
    Time frame: Up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Cancer Specific Survival
    Description: Cancer Survival
    Time frame: From the date of radiotherapy end until the date of death from disease progression, assessed up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Overall Survival
    Description: Survival from all causes
    Time frame: From the date of radiotherapy end until the date of death from any cause, assessed up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Acute toxicity
    Description
      Toxicity developed in the first three months after the treatment, assessed with Common
      Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0 scale, which displayes grades from 1
      to 5, with grade 1 meaning mild toxicity and grade 5 death related to toxicity
    Time frame: Up to three months from the start of radiotherapy
  -
    Measure: Late Toxicity
    Description
      Toxicity developed after three months until the end of follow-up or death, assessed with
      Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0 scale, assessed with Common
      Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) v5.0 scale, which displayes grades from 1
      to 5, with grade 1 meaning mild toxicity and grade 5 death related to toxicity
    Time frame: From three months after the start of radiotherapy until the end of follow-up or death, assessed up to 120 months
Other outcomes
  -
    Measure: Organs Movement
    Description: Modeling of organ movements during treatment.
    Time frame: Perioperative
  -
    Measure: Predictive factors
    Description
      actors predicting survival. Survival trees, univariate/multivariate Cox regression models
      will be estimated to identify potential risk factors associated with survival and
      toxicity outcomes
    Time frame: From radiotherapy end to date of local, regional progression, distant failure, death, toxicity assessed up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Radiomic characteristics
    Description
      Evaluation of radiomic features on CT images in relation to clinical and histological
      parameters and their predictive role for treatment response
    Time frame: Up to 5 years

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Patients with cancer

    -  Patients with benign tumors/conditions

    -  Treated with advanced radiotherapy techniques (IMRT, IGRT, SBRT, including Gamma
       Knife, CyberKnife, Tomotherapy, VMAT machines)

    -  Treated between January 2001 and May 2026

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Patients with cancer

    -  Treated with 2D or 3D conformational radiotherapy,

    -  Treated with palliative radiotherapy, not including an advanced radiotherapy
       technique

    -  Treated before January 2001 or after May 2026
