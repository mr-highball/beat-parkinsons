NCT07284316. First posted December 16, 2025. Last update posted March 25, 2026. Official protocol https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT07284316.

The first public year is 2025. The retrieved record is active, not recruiting, with an estimated enrollment of 100 and no results module. Participants are adults aged 55 to 70 with insulin resistance. Both groups receive a program with exercise, cognitive training and nutrition guidance. The supplement comparison lists turmeric and black pepper alongside the program versus placebo with the same program. No separate pepper or turmeric-only arm is described. The grade and identity of cracked culinary pepper are unverified.

The registry lists timed Trail Making and Digit Symbol, verbal learning and fluency tests. The primary cognitive schedules list baseline, week 24 and week 52. Walking outcomes include gait speed and dual-task stride measures. No observed estimate or Parkinson cohort can be inferred from the protocol.
