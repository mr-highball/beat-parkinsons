BRIEF SUMMARY

Patients with PD and SCS treatment for gait impairment entered a cross-over trial of four
stimulation paradigms; high frequency/low pulse width, low frequency/high pulse width,
Fast-Acting Sub-perception Therapy, and MicroBurst.

CONDITIONS

- Parkinson's Disease (PD)
- Spinal Cord Stimulation (SCS)
