BRIEF SUMMARY

This clinical trial is designed to test the safety and tolerability of the study
intervention, RNDP-001, which will be implanted into the brain of study participants
during a surgical procedure.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Diagnosis of idiopathic Parkinson's disease consistent with the Movement Disorders
       Society Clinical Criteria

    -  Age 45 to 75

    -  Medically stable to undergo a surgical procedure

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Known Parkinson's disease gene mutation or variant

    -  Previous infusion therapy or surgery for Parkinson's disease

    -  History of allergic reaction or intolerance to an immunotherapeutic agent

    -  Contraindication to MRI

    -  Clinically significant medical conditions likely to interfere with the surgical
       procedure or study assessments
