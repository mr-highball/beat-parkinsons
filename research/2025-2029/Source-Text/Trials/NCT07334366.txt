The computerized neurocognitive battery includes auditory continuous performance, digit span and verbal learning. The retrieved record says Recruiting with its verification dated December 2025, and no results were posted. The extract, general cognitive-decline cohort and protocol outcomes do not supply a raw broccoli result in Parkinson disease.
