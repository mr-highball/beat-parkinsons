UMIN000060916 — A Study on the Effects of Consuming Foods Containing Fermented Rice Bran on Bowel Movements
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000069232
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Coverage: Selected official registration fields, summarized as plain text.

First public disclosure: 2026-03-13.
Status: No longer recruiting. Results: Unpublished in the official entry.
Design: Double-blind, randomized, parallel-group comparison of fermented rice bran food and placebo over four weeks.
Population: Adults age 20 to under 65 with mild constipation and relatively hard stool, with chronic constipation and major chronic conditions excluded. Target 100.
Primary outcome: Bowel movement count from diaries.
Secondary outcomes include number of days with a bowel movement, volume, stool consistency, incomplete evacuation, relief, pain, straining, salivary measures, quality of life and safety.
The assigned preparation is fermented bran food. No refined rice bran oil or Parkinson group is specified.
