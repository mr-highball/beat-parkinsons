NCT07220694. First posted October 24, 2025. Last update posted January 28, 2026. Official protocol https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT07220694.

The trial plans 140 adults with mild cognitive impairment and insulin resistance. It is recruiting in the retrieved record and has no posted results. The randomized comparison assigns the botanical blend or matched inert capsules. A separate black pepper arm is absent. The registry does not identify a Parkinson cohort.

The supplement description gives 250 mg standardized Oroxylum indicum bark extract (Sabroxy) plus 5 mg BioPerine black pepper extract per capsule. The intervention records describe one daily capsule. Both arm descriptions specify two capsules each morning. The intended total dose cannot be settled from these two descriptions.

The primary outcome is insulin resistance by HOMA-IR. Cognitive outcomes include immediate word recall, a computerized Numeric Working Memory Test described as assessing attention, processing speed and memory, the Cognitive Failures Questionnaire total, and total MoCA. The timed working-memory task does not provide an isolated culinary pepper contrast or an observed outcome.
