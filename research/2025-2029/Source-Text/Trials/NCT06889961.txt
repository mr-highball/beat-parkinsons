Title: Effect of daily mixed spice consumption on memory function
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06889961
NCT ID: NCT06889961
First posted: 2025-03-21
Last update posted: 2026-07-24
Coverage: Factual extract of the official registration.

The planned six-month study compares a six-spice capsule with placebo. The formula includes oregano, sage, thyme, cinnamon, black pepper and turmeric. Memory is primary. The SF-36 mental-health domain combines dimensions of emotional well-being, including depression and anxiety. Eligibility excludes signs of Parkinson disease. The registry lists 50 planned participants, recruiting status and no posted outcomes. This combined intervention cannot identify an oregano-specific effect.
