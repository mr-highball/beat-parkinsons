Title: Pumpkin-seed-oil and bladder function pilot

Completed, no results. A single-arm trial uses cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil and prespecifies urinary symptom measures, including a symptom index containing pelvic pain and urgency. It does not supply a controlled Parkinson pain estimate.

November 26, 2026: official API still reports completion with no results module. The comparator and measured urinary or composite endpoints do not isolate Parkinson pain.

November 26, 2026 original follow-through.
The official API still lists completed status and no posted results, with the last posted update on September 21, 2026. Its primary symptom index reports no standalone numerical pain comparison. It is a separate registry from the Khalil tadalafil combination study.

Additional registry fields verified November 26. The single-arm study recorded 40 enrolled, actual completion September 1, 2026, and a September 21, 2026 posted update. Pelvic Pain and Urgency/Frequency is a secondary combined endpoint. The registration excludes neurogenic bladder and supplies no results or document attachment.
