Source: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05735587, Blueberries, Inflammation, Motivation, and Physical Activity
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05735587
First registration posting: February 21, 2023
First public results: July 30, 2025
Coverage: selected registration, participant flow and adverse-event fields verified November 12, 2026

Randomized parallel trial with participant, investigator and outcomes-assessor masking. The population was sedentary older adults with depressive symptoms. The intervention was 48 g/day freeze-dried blueberry powder or matched placebo powder for twelve weeks. The primary outcome was change in median daily steps. 15 blueberry and 14 placebo participants started the allocated powder. 12 and 10 completed, respectively.

Adverse events were captured during approximately 14 weeks, with reporting threshold 0%. Both groups reported four participants with other adverse events. There were no serious adverse events or deaths.
Constipation, systematic assessment: 1 event in 1 of 15 blueberry participants and 0 events in 0 of 14 placebo participants.
Increased bowel movement frequency, systematic assessment: 1 event in 1 of 15 blueberry participants and 1 event in 1 of 14 placebo participants.
These are adverse-event counts rather than measured constipation severity. The study did not enroll a Parkinson cohort.
