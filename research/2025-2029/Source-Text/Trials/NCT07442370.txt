BRIEF SUMMARY

Turning is a frequent cause of falls in people with Parkinson's disease, but it is rarely
trained specifically in conventional exercise programs. This randomized controlled trial
examines whether adding functional turning exercises to a conventional exercise program
improves fall risk, gait speed, and balance in patients with early-stage Parkinson's
disease. Fifty patients are randomly assigned to conventional exercise or to conventional
exercise plus functional turning exercises. Both groups exercise three times per week for
12 weeks with similar session duration. Outcomes are assessed before and after the
intervention.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Patients with idiopathic Parkinson's disease (Hoehn and Yahr stage 1-2, aged 50-70 years)
are recruited from the physiotherapy and rehabilitation clinic of a medical center in
Istanbul. Participants are allocated to two groups (25 per group) by drawing lots from an
opaque bag containing 50 slips. The control group receives a conventional program of
stretching, strengthening, balance, and gait exercises. The experimental group receives
the same conventional program at a reduced dose together with 18-20 minutes of functional
turning exercises, so that total session duration is similar in both groups
(approximately 45-50 minutes). All sessions are delivered individually by a
physiotherapist, three times per week for 12 weeks (36 sessions). The Timed Up and Go
test, the 10-Meter Walk Test, and the Berg Balance Scale are administered at baseline and
two days after the end of the intervention, in the "on" medication state; each test is
performed three times and the mean is used. Outcome assessment is not blinded

Interventions
  -
    Type: OTHER
    Name: Conventional Exercise
    Description
      Stretching (pectorals, trunk rotators, hip flexors, hamstrings, quadriceps,
      gastrocnemius-soleus; 2 x 20-30 s per muscle group), strengthening (sit-to-stand,
      mini-squat, standing hip abduction and extension, knee extension, heel raises, trunk
      extensors; 2 x 10-12 repetitions, with dumbbell resistance increased every two weeks),
      balance training (weight shifting, reaching, semi-tandem and tandem stance,
      multidirectional stepping), and gait training (overground walking with emphasis on step
      length and arm swing, start-stop practice, obstacle crossing, directional changes). In
      the experimental arm the same exercises are performed with fewer sets and repetitions and
      shorter balance and gait practice
    Arm group labels
      - Conventional Exercise Group
      - Functional Turning Exercise Group
  -
    Type: OTHER
    Name: Functional Turning Exercises
    Description
      Structured program of about 18-20 minutes per session: (1) seated trunk rotation while
      reaching with a ball to targets on both sides, 3 x 10 repetitions per direction; (2)
      seated resisted trunk rotation with an exercise ball against an elastic band, 2 x 10
      repetitions per direction; (3) standing object transfer from front to a target behind the
      body with trunk rotation, 3 x 10 repetitions; (4) figure-of-eight walking around two
      points about 2 m apart, 3 laps; (5) trunk rotation in single-leg stance, 3 x 30 s; (6)
      trunk rotation with a ball in tandem stance, 3 x 30 s; (7) sit-to-stand, 90-degree turn
      on verbal command, return and sit, 3 x 10 repetitions. Elastic band resistance is
      increased every two weeks (red, green, blue, black).
    Arm group labels
      - Functional Turning Exercise Group

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Timed Up and Go Test
    Description
      This outcome measure will be used to assess functional mobility using a virtual
      reality-based adaptation of the Timed Up and Go test. Test completion time will be
      automatically recorded by the system. Validity and reliability will be examined through
      comparison with the conventional Timed Up and Go test and repeated measurements.
    Time frame: Baseline and after 12 weeks of intervention
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: 10-Meter Walk Test (gait speed)
    Description
      Gait speed in m/s at normal, comfortable pace over the central 10 m of a 14 m walkway.
      Mean of three trials. Higher values indicate better performance.
    Time frame: Baseline and after 12 weeks of intervention
  -
    Measure: Berg Balance Scale (BBS)
    Description
      14-item scale of functional balance; total score 0-56. Higher scores indicate better
      balance. Mean of three administrations, rounded to the nearest whole number.
    Time frame: Baseline and after 12 weeks of intervention

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria

    -  Individuals with a clinical diagnosis of idiopathic Parkinson's disease (Hoehn &
       Yahr stages 1-2)

    -  Individuals aged 50-70 years

    -  Mini Mental State Test score ≥ 24

    -  Individuals whose medical treatment has been stable in the last 6 weeks

    -  Individuals with stable vital signs

    -  Individuals who volunteer to participate in the study and understand the purpose and
       process of the study, and who have signed a written informed consent form

  Exclusion Criteria

    -  Advanced Parkinson's disease (H&Y 3-5)

    -  Severe orthopedic problems

    -  Vestibular system disorders

    -  History of serious falls in the last 6 months
