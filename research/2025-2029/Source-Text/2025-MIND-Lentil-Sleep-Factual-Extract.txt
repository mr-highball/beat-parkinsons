Original MIND dietary pattern trial

Original source https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-91389-y

In the 2025 open-label randomized trial, 44 women with type 2 diabetes and insomnia were assigned the MIND low-calorie diet or a low-calorie comparator. The study enrolled with an insomnia screening criterion and reported Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index as the sleep outcome. The adjusted between-arm coefficient was −2.25 (reported p = .006). Lentils were pooled with beans and other legumes, and no lentil-only contrast or preparation was reported.

Table 4 gives the daytime dysfunction component separately from the global PSQI score. The adjusted 12-week contrast was −0.41 points (95% CI −1.00 to 0.18, p=.163). Both arms improved within group. The randomized comparison covers the full diet. The study does not identify black bean consumption or a Parkinson daytime sleepiness effect.
