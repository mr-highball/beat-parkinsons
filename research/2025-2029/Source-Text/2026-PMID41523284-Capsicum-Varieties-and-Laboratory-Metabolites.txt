Original study scope
The original compares Capsicum frutescens with C. annuum using chemical profiling and laboratory bioactivity assays. Cayenne is discussed among Capsicum varieties. The discussion cites capsaicin and TRPV1 analgesia mechanisms.

Continuing evidence gaps
The plant comparison gives a genuine pepper-related lead, without a pain endpoint after eating the frozen preparation. It does not supply an attributable date or turmeric contrast for Parkinson pain.
