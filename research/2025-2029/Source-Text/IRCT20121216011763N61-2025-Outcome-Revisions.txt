The 2025 registration revisions and manuscript chronology
Primary source: https://irct.ir/trial/75378?revision=350954
Later revision: https://irct.ir/trial/75378?revision=354286
Registry ID: IRCT20121216011763N61

Coverage: official registry outcome changes and journal dates.

IRCT history dates revision 350954 to July 30, 2025. The revision page gives August 9 as its last update. It raises the target from 86 to 92 and adds fatigue measured with the 16-item Parkinson Fatigue Scale, appetite and blood pressure. It specifies Mojtahedi as the saffron supplier, replaces superoxide dismutase with catalase and drops protein carbonyl from the listed biochemical outcomes. The reason field refers to possible dropout and correction of omitted items. Revision 354286 retains the revised outcomes. The history dates it September 6 and its page gives September 7 as the last update.

BMJ Open marks September 2, 2024 as the date it received the protocol, September 25, 2025 as acceptance and October 29, 2025 as first publication. The submitted version was inaccessible, so the date the investigators first included fatigue internally is unresolved. Food & Function received the outcomes manuscript on April 27, 2025 and first published it on December 24. The first archived IRCT revision listing fatigue follows the reported manuscript receipt. Actual recruitment and study completion dates remain unresolved. The record documents public outcome-list changes and leaves the reason for the changes open.
