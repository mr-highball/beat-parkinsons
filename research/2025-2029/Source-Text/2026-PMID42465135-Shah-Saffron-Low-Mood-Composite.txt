Title: UO SAF 02 standardized saffron extract in adults with subclinical low mood
First published: 2026-06-15
PMID: 42465135
PMCID: PMC13373286
DOI: 10.7759/cureus.110935
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13373286/
Archive scope: Primary article methods, tables and disclosures checked. The registration ID is reported in the paper. Its official registry history remains a separate access lead.

The 56-person trial assigned 28 participants per arm to a proprietary extract or placebo for 56 days. The paper specifies a 15 mg formulation with marker minima of 11% crocin and 0.1% safranal. A Beck score screened eligibility and was not a longitudinal outcome. Table 2 reports an unusually large between-arm difference on the overall Profile of Mood States, −42.79 points (95% CI −45.20 to −40.38, reported d=9.47). Table 4 reports a DASS-21 total-score difference of −9.68 (95% CI −11.12 to −8.24). Results and figures report the aggregate DASS-21 score. They do not give a separate depression score. The paper also reports a subjective global sleep-quality outcome.

Table 1 reports baseline DASS-21 total means of 23.79 (SD 1.17) and 22.37 (SD 2.15) with 28 per group, marked p>.05. Using the reported summaries and the stated independent-group comparison yields |t| about 3.07. Rounded values cannot readily account for this finding. Three missing cases used last observation carried forward. The sponsor and several authors have disclosed commercial ties, and the article reports a relevant patent. The paper reports CTRI/2023/11/059603. The tested WHO bridge and public CTRI routes did not return an independently authenticated record. Product formulation, baseline and effect-size checks, and the missing depression-specific endpoint limit inference for the frozen claim.
