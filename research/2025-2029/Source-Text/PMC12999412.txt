Title: Optimizing sialorrhea management in Parkinson's disease: a patient journey mapping approach
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12999412/
PMID: 41868492
PMCID: PMC12999412
DOI: 10.3389/fneur.2026.1768568
First published: 2026-03-05
Trial reference: ChiCTR2500096034
Coverage: Distilled abstract and original qualitative methods.

Ten participants with Parkinson disease and drooling were interviewed in July–September 2025. The report synthesizes experience across pre-consultation, consultation, treatment and long-term care. The authors describe 32 themes involving awareness, communications, treatment experience and accessibility. Participant comments concern care and lived experience. The paper did not assign a food, quantify a fruit-specific symptom effect or provide a controlled arm. It shares a registration and care-program context with PMID 41939767. Participant overlap is unverified.
