Registered ginger granules and menstrual pain
Source: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=CTRI%2F2025%2F05%2F086338
Registration: CTRI/2025/05/086338
Coverage: Official WHO ICTRP copy of May 5, 2025 registration

The registry lists 100 planned participants, enrollment from May 13, 2025 and double-blind parallel arms. Standardized ginger extract granules or matched placebo were assigned twice daily for 90 days, with VAS and WaLIDD pain measures. The linked publication's exact pain table remains subject to its internal reporting discrepancy.
