Title: Pre-exercise chickpea milk consumption supports glycemic stability during endurance exercise in female runners.
PMID: 42860736
PMCID: PMC13655422
DOI: 10.20463/pan.2026.0041
First published: 2026
Authors: Hiruncharoen P, Nokkaew N
Journal: Phys Act Nutr
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13655422/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized, double-blind, counterbalanced crossover trial (Hiruncharoen & Nokkaew, 2026, PMC13655422, TCTR20260515005, n=14 trained eumenorrheic female half-marathon runners tested in the follicular phase) comparing 250 mL pasteurized chickpea milk vs soy milk vs energy-matched maltodextrin placebo 30 min before treadmill running (60 min at 60% HRmax then 80% HRmax to exhaustion). Blood glucose CV was lower after chickpea milk (13.07 +/- 4.33%) and soy milk (12.37 +/- 4.62%) than placebo (16.07 +/- 4.89%, p=0.031), but Rating of Perceived Exertion (p>0.05), time to exhaustion (66.40 +/- 3.58 vs 65.65 +/- 3.17 vs 64.23 +/- 2.46 min, p=0.075), heart rate, lactate, FFA, CK, and RER were all null. Limits: liquid filtered chickpea beverage rather than cooked/canned/roasted whole chickpeas, acute single-dose endurance setting in young athletes.
