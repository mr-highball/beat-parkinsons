# Anxiety results in the microalgae capsule trial

Original source https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/17/6/960
Source coverage original Figure 6A and official registry formulation

Maltz and colleagues published the NCT06425094 trial on March 10, 2025. Figure 6A gives baseline mean GAD-7 scores of 5.3 and 4.7, with no significant between-group or time effect. The trial report describes the microalgae and maltodextrin interventions. The official registry arm description supplies the hydrogenated rapeseed excipient detail. Neither source assigns an oil-only comparison. A low-baseline-anxiety sample and processing differ from the frozen claim.
