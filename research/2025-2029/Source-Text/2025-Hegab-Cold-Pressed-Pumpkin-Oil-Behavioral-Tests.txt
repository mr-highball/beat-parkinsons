Cold pressed pumpkin oil in the colchicine behavioral study
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0040816625000722
PMID: 39965508
DOI: 10.1016/j.tice.2025.102792
Coverage: factual extract from primary abstract and indexed article sections

Dina Y. Hegab and colleagues, Tissue and Cell 94:102792, first online February 2025. Forty male rats formed four groups. The article specifies commercial cold-pressed oil from Imtenan and reports oil and colchicine doses in the abstract. The indexed primary article identifies the open-field test and elevated plus maze in Table 2, which the authors position as anxiety-related outcomes. The authors also describe other neurobehavioral and memory findings in the abstract and discussion, with no separate attention, skilled paw use, or dexterity estimate recoverable from the indexed sections. Full methods, challenge route, and all group behavioral values remain unverified in the accessible preview. It is a colchicine toxicity model rather than a direct Parkinson dyskinesia experiment. Related publications report liver, spleen and reproductive outcomes, which should not be counted as behavioral extensions without verified cohort linkage.
