Title: Where does excessive beta in Parkinson's disease originate? Insights from a case of unilateral cortico-pallidal pathway dysfunction

Text coverage: Original PMC XML article body and embedded tables inspected. Factual extract. Separately distributed supplements are outside this extract.

A Parkinson clinical case used an unexpected postoperative complication to examine changes in pallidal beta activity. The authors observed short-term changes in limb rigidity and rest tremor, with a later total motor score comparable to the preoperative score. A single clinically complex course and a later aggregate score cannot isolate a food effect. Falls were not an outcome and sage was not evaluated.
