Title: Diagnostic and therapeutic challenges in Lyme disease and co-infections
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12109650/
PMID: 40453233
PMCID: PMC12109650
First publication: 2025-06 (earliest verified issue month)
Coverage: PMC public full HTML, clinical narrative and product table

This ten-case series describes neurological and psychiatric symptoms in people investigated for tick-borne disease and co-infections. The treatment list attached to a Lyme neuroborreliosis case includes a mango-labelled UltraInflamX Plus 360 product amid a multicomponent treatment course. The manufacturer identifies Mango as a powder flavor and lists a formulated product. The clinical record does not establish fresh or dried whole mango exposure or verify the powder's lot-specific ingredients. Other members' fatigue or depressive symptoms cannot be paired with this product entry. The series does not supply the frozen Parkinson exposure–outcome comparisons.
