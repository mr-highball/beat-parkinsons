Title: Tiredness findings for a multi-ingredient supplement containing cayenne extract
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06023082
Trial registration: NCT06023082
Publication date: 2025-01-13
Date basis: Official public posting of results. The registration was first posted on 2023-09-05.
Protocol: https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/82/NCT06023082/Prot_SAP_000.pdf
Source depth: Official protocol and reported registry result tables.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27

The single-group study enrolled 120 participants. The trial materials list cayenne fruit extract, psyllium, carnitine, minerals and vitamins in the tested product. The reported tiredness analysis includes 98 people. Mean ratings on the five-point scale, with higher scores designated more favorable, were 1.80 ± 0.72 at baseline and 2.90 ± 1.07 at week 12. The record reports p < .0001 for this within-group comparison.

The study has no concurrent comparison and cannot separate cayenne from the other ingredients, time effects or reporting changes. The result neither isolates raw sweet bell pepper nor addresses Parkinson fatigue.
