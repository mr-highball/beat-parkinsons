Title: Sex and gender differences in Lewy body dementia: a narrative review

PMID: 41262225
PMCID: PMC12624417
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuros.2025.100009

The narrative review synthesizes published differences in diagnosis, symptom profiles, progression and treatment in Lewy body disorders. Reported visual symptom differences are secondary clinical context without ingredient intake or measured food-timed recurrence.
