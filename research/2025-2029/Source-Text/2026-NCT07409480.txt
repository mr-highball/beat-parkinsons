Title: Functional plant beverage with sleep quality as a secondary outcome
First published or posted: 2026-02-13
Registration: NCT07409480
Source URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07409480
Text type: original-source evidence summary

First posted February 13, 2026. The Spain protocol plans 70 older adults and a twelve-week comparison of a functional oat-based beverage development product with a plant-based control beverage. The selected ingredients are vitamin D, zinc, fructooligosaccharides and L-leucine. The arm descriptions do not establish ingredient proportions, whether oat amounts match or whether either arm contains rolled oats. Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index is secondary. The latest public update still listed not yet recruiting and no results were posted at the November 17, 2026 check.
