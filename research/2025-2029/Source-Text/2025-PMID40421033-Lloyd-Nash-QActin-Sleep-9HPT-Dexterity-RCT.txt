Title: Oral supplement in healthy older adults to support physical fitness and mental wellbeing.
PMID: 40421033
PMCID: PMC12104766
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2025.1563999
Registry ID: NCT05878847
First published: 2025
Authors: Lloyd AJ, Nash RJ, Warren-Walker A, Watson A, Martinez Martin MP, Davies C et al.
Journal: Front Nutr
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12104766/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (Lloyd, Nash et al., 2025, Front Nutr, PMC12104766, NCT05878847, n=47 randomized healthy older adults aged 50-78 years) testing 20 mg/day Q-Actin fresh-cucumber extract gummies versus placebo for 12 weeks. Total PSQI sleep score was NULL both within and between groups at all timepoints (p>0.05; only one subcomponent sleep-quality item shifted). For 9-Hole Peg Test (9HPT) hand dexterity, authors reported only within-group p-values (W0 vs W12 p=0.011, W6 vs W12 p=0.007; placebo p>0.05) on n=29 longitudinal completers (38% missingness) with ZERO published 9HPT means, standard deviations, or between-group interaction p-values, alongside Phytoquest co-authorship and fresh-cucumber gummy extract matrix (not pickled whole cucumber).
