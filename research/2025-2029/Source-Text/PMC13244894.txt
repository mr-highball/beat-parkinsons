Post-hoc analysis of paired cerebrospinal fluid samples from fifteen participants in a larger Lewy body dementia trial. The separate amyloid groups included nine positive and six negative participants. The analysis focused on protein changes associated with the investigational medicine. Visual hallucinations and rest tremor are described in the clinical background. The analysis did not report a hallucination-frequency or rest-tremor item result or compare fig exposures. The parent trial's posted aggregate clinical outcomes are part of the same study family.
Trial registration: NCT03467152.
