Title: Classification and Genotype-Phenotype Relationships of GBA1 Variants: MDSGene Systematic Review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12006889/
PMID: 39927608
PMCID: PMC12006889
DOI: 10.1002/mds.30141
Publication date: 2025-02-10 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The MDSGene review collates reported GBA1 variants and genotype–phenotype relationships in Parkinson, parkinsonism and Gaucher disease. Genetic classification and clinical reports provide no food intervention or timed visual episode outcome.
