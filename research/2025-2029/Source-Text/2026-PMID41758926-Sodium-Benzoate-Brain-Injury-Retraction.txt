Review depth: Complete editorial notice.

The journal confirmed overlapping subimages in Figure 2 that represented different experimental conditions. It reports that the materials supplied during the investigation did not resolve its concerns. This notice pertains to the controlled cortical impact study published in 2021, PMID 35008615. It does not pertain to the separately published study of sodium benzoate and dopaminergic outcomes, PMID 34113786.

The authors subsequently republished a corrected version under PMID 42221706. This is one original study lineage. The notice provides no efficacy outcome for ground cinnamon or Parkinson anxiety.
