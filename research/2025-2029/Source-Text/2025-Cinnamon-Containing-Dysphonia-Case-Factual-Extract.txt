Topic: Cinnamon among therapies for postoperative dysphonia
Source: https://www.jchr.org/index.php/JCHR/article/download/10650/5873
Coverage: Original postoperative case report, factual extract
A postoperative vocal nodule case used the multicomponent Vyoshadi Vati preparation, listing Cinnamomum zeylanicum, alongside gargles and other interventions. The report describes patient-reported voice clarity after treatment. It provides no acoustic volume measure, independent ingredient contrast, ground culinary bark specification or Parkinson diagnosis.
