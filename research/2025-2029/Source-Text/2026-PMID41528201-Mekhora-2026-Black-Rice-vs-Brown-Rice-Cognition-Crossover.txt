Title: Impact of anthocyanin-rich black rice consumption on cognitive function, inflammation and microvascular function in older adults: a crossover intervention trial.
PMID: 41528201
DOI: 10.1039/d5fo04351d
Registry ID: NCT06583785
First published: 2026
Authors: Mekhora, Lamport, Spencer
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41528201/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Nine-day single-blind randomized crossover trial (NCT06583785) in n=24 older adults (65 +/- 7 y) consuming 210 g/d anthocyanin-rich black rice vs brown rice control (0 mg anthocyanins). Black rice outperformed brown rice on 8-day RAVLT verbal recall (p=0.02-0.04), digit span backward (p=0.03), and IL-6 (-0.67, p=0.03); acute post-meal recall declined after brown rice; Stroop and BP unchanged. Uses brown rice as the low-anthocyanin comparator against black rice rather than white rice.
