Title: From plate to pillow, and vice versa: diet-sleep dynamics in free-living adults with obesity.
PMID: 41697374
PMCID: PMC12909376
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-026-03894-z
First published: 2026
Authors: Martin-Olmedo JJ, Clavero-Jimeno A, Migueles JH, Camacho-Cardenosa A, Piernas C, Ruiz JR, et al.
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41697374/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional baseline analysis in n=146 adults with obesity examining meal-specific dietary intake and subsequent wrist-accelerometry sleep: greater dinner intake of red meat (alongside energy, fat, cholesterol, protein, alcohol, and french fries) correlated with poorer subsequent sleep quality parameters (p<=0.048). Limits: observational/cross-sectional, pooled red meat in an obesity cohort, conflicts with null crossover RCT O'Connor 2018.
