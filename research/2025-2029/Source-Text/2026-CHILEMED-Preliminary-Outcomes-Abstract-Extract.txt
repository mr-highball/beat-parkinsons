# Six-month CHILEMED report and unreported anxiety comparison

Original source https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41819423/
Source coverage original preliminary report abstract

The original 2026 CHILEMED preliminary report includes 156 participants at six months and reports diet adherence and metabolic outcomes. Its reported comparisons do not supply the planned anxiety scale results. The Mediterranean groups were pooled for the comparison and canola was one optional component. Retain an open route to later psychological outcomes and preserve the original planned measure separately.
