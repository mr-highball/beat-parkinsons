Title: Oral rosemary extract and general safety
Source: https://doi.org/10.3164/jcbn.25-157

Kuwata and colleagues studied an oral rosemary extract in a four-week open-label safety study in 22 healthy adults and a separate placebo-controlled crossover with 40 participants. The article reports no clinically relevant adverse events in the safety trial and reports stress measures. This is an extract in healthy adults, with no fresh culinary leaf intervention or Parkinson visual-hallucination frequency measure; a general safety statement is not a hallucination-specific result.
