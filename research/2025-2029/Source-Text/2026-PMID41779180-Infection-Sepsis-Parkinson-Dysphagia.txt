Archive scope: Factual text from the original 12-page publisher PDF, including main Tables 1–4 and the separate seven-page supplementary material.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Sasegbon and colleagues screened clinical records for adults hospitalized with infection or sepsis from February 2022 to February 2023. Prior dysphagia and selected acute neurologic causes were excluded. The study tracks recorded new dysphagia, including specialist assessments. The authors note that subtle cases may be missed without a referral. There were 189 cases among 4,475 in the infection group and 54 among 865 in the sepsis group. The paper merges multiple records for the same inpatient stay. Infection-category subtotals should be kept separate from whole-group denominators.

In main Table 4 the Parkinson row reports beta 1.356, SE 0.336, adjusted odds ratio 3.879 and p<.001. Supplementary Table 5 reports VIF 1.349 for Parkinson in that model. The association describes new recorded dysphagia in this hospital cohort. It does not measure severity after eating a specific preparation. PP0011 at UEG Week 2025 reports the same cohort and estimate. The team's 2018 source, PMID 30245787, uses an earlier referral-selected population.
