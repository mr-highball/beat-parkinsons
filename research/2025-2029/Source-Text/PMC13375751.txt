Title: Recent Developments in the Drug Treatment of Parkinson's Disease

This review describes pharmacological developments for Parkinson motor and nonmotor symptoms. Its interventions and limitations concern drug treatment and published drug trials, with no original dried fig and visual episode-frequency comparison.
