Title: Inflammatory biomarker candidates in dementia with Lewy bodies: diagnostic classification, clinical associations, and survival
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13734266/
PMID: 42990786
PMCID: PMC13734266
DOI: 10.21203/rs.3.rs-11368506/v1
Publication date: 2026-11-01 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The plasma-protein comparison enrolls 20 participants with dementia with Lewy bodies, 15 with mild cognitive impairment or Alzheimer disease and 15 normal controls. No group protein result survived multiple-testing correction. Diagnostic and survival associations are distinct from measured food consumption or visual episode frequency.
