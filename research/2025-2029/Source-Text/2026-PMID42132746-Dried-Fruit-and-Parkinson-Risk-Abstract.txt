Title: Causal associations of dietary habits and blood metabolites with Parkinson's disease risk
PMID: 42132746
DOI: 10.1039/D5FO02160J
Source: https://pubs.rsc.org/no/content/articlelanding/2026/fo/d5fo02160j/unauth
NLM record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42132746/
Publication year: 2026
Coverage: Primary publisher and NLM abstracts. Main article and supplement not recovered in this review.

Using UK Biobank dietary GWAS and Parkinson outcome summary data, the authors report an odds ratio of 1.70 for a generic dried-fruit genetic proxy in a Mendelian randomization analysis. The public abstract reports diagnosis risk rather than visual hallucinations among people with Parkinson. It does not specify a dried fig exposure or give the full instrument list and per-analysis uncertainty for the dried-fruit result.

The publisher-listed supplement returned an unavailable response on its original route. Confirm the exposure identifier, contrast units, confidence interval and sample overlap in the main tables before treating this as independent of other UK Biobank analyses.
