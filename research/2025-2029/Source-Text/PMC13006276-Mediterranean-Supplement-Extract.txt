The March 9, 2026 report includes a separate supplement with models for 59 Parkinson participants. Supplementary Table 7 gives model-3 odds ratios of 0.88 (95% CI 0.76–0.99, p=.043) for anxiety and 0.88 (0.77–0.98, p=.035) for constipation per point of the composite Mediterranean score. Its footnote lists disease duration, age, gender, activity and income. The main text narrates a partly different adjustment list. Table 3 gives an olive-oil cooking frequency and relates it to the composite score. The supplement lacks an olive-oil-specific symptom model, exact questionnaire wording, processing grade and participant data.
