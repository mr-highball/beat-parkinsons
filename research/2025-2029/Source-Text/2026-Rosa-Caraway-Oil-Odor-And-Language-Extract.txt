Title: Age-Related Olfactory and Cognitive Decline: Potential Effects of Rosmarinus officinalis and Carum carvi Essential Oils
Authors: Antonella Rosa and colleagues
Date: 2026-03-07
Identifiers: PMID 41830032; DOI 10.3390/nu18050862
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/18/5/862
Coverage: Original factual extract of Methods, subsections 3.4 and 3.7, Figure 6 and Tables 1 and 6.

The 402-person sample took cognitive, olfactory and gustatory tests. A subset of 59 rated the odor properties of rosemary and caraway essential oils. The caraway oil contained about 53.0% carvone and 46.4% limonene by chromatographic peak area. The cohort excluded people with neurodegenerative disease. The study reports a smaller group’s odor ratings and qualitative descriptors, while Montreal Cognitive Assessment language scores were analyzed in relation to age and sensory measures. There was no assigned ground-caraway ingestion, pre/post language contrast for the essential oil, or acoustic voice outcome.
