Real world kefir consumption and planned global health fatigue item
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07790666
Registration: NCT07790666
Coverage: Official registry, intervention and outcome fields

First posted August 27, 2026. The real-world study records two weeks of commercial Activia Kefir use in usual or new consumers and before-and-after digestive outcomes. PROMIS Global Health includes a fatigue question along with other domains. Recruitment was ongoing and no results were posted at November 16, 2026. The protocol lists neither a Parkinson cohort nor a controlled fatigue-specific comparison.
