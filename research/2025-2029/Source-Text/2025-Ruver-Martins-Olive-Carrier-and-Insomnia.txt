Shared olive oil in a Parkinson cannabis case series
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11891160/
PMID: 40066073
PMCID: PMC11891160
DOI: 10.3389/fnhum.2024.1466438
Coverage: original Methods, Results, baseline Table 1 and insomnia figure

First public article date February 24, 2025, despite a 2024 collection year. Six people with Parkinson disease were assigned to two doses of the same cannabis extract diluted in olive oil. The study had three people per dose and no oil-only or oil-free comparator. Grade was unreported. Table 1 lists tremor, postural instability and bradykinesia at baseline. The report supplies no separate serial change for those motor items. At day 60 the higher-dose group reported a lower Insomnia Severity Index than its own baseline (p=.03). This comparison does not isolate olive oil and does not supply a measured anxiety endpoint.
