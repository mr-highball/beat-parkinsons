The cross-sectional DUMP study enrolled 1,579 people with Parkinson disease at 16 French sites in 2021. Table 3 includes 96 people who reported nausea during the visit. One person in the group without domperidone was recorded with ginger among alternative nausea treatments. Ginger preparation, amount, timing and response were unreported. The four supplementary tables provide subgroup and medication details without a separate ginger outcome.

Table 1 reports baseline constipation across domperidone exposure groups. It does not cross-tabulate constipation by ginger exposure or follow the participant who used ginger. The clinical source supplies a Parkinson use observation without a measured culinary effect.

The Methods registration, NCT04653584, matches the DUMP-observational title, 1,579 participants, cross-sectional design and dates in the official registry. The front-matter ID NCT02850705 is listed in the official registry as NOMADMUS, a separate neuromyelitis optica cohort. The EUPAS26319 catalogue entry describes the broader DUMP program.
