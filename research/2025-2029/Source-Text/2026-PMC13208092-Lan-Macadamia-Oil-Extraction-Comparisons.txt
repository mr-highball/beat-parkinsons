Title: Aqueous enzymatic extraction of macadamia oil: Effects on physicochemical properties, composition and antioxidant activity
PMID: 42205556
PMCID: PMC13208092
DOI: 10.1016/j.fochx.2026.103974
Publication date: 2026-05-11
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13208092/
Text coverage: Original Methods 2.2–2.4 and composition and physicochemical results from the primary article.

Lan and colleagues studied four oil preparations from macadamia kernels. Their reference pressing preparation used hydraulic pressing of dried raw kernels at room temperature. It yielded 63.14 ± 2.21 g oil per 100 g kernels. Separate preparations used organic solvent, aqueous extraction and enzyme-assisted aqueous extraction. Acid and peroxide values were lower in enzyme-assisted oil than in pressed oil, and iodine value was higher. The body assigns its 8.23% comparison in summed measured fatty acids to pressed oil and its 28.25% comparison to solvent-extracted oil. The abstract assigns 28.25% to pressed oil. This extract preserves the discrepancy and uses the Methods for product identity. The assays concern the oils and their physicochemical properties. The report tests no neurological outcome or person.
