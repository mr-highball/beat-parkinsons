Common olive oil intake and longitudinal attention scores
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40168-025-02306-4
PMID: 41578342
PMCID: PMC12910899
DOI: 10.1186/s40168-025-02306-4
Coverage: Original article Methods and Table 2

Ni and colleagues published January 24, 2026. The prospective PREDIMED-Plus analysis followed 656 older adults with overweight or obesity and metabolic syndrome for two years. The baseline questionnaire recorded refined oil and olive-pomace oil separately, then combined them as common olive oil in the reported exposure. Attention was a standardized composite of cognitive test outcomes. In Table 2 the fully adjusted attention change per 10 g/day of common olive oil was −0.006 z-score units (95% CI −0.047 to 0.035, p=.771), and the top versus bottom tertile estimate was −0.073 (95% CI −0.209 to 0.062). The basic top-tertile estimate was −0.137 (95% CI −0.271 to −0.003) and attenuated with adjustment. Total olive oil had a different fully adjusted continuous association, +0.046 (95% CI 0.008 to 0.084). The study was observational and leaves the refined-only association and Parkinson symptom outcome unresolved.
