Title: Apathy and gender in progressive supranuclear palsy
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13347500/
PMID: 40462618
PMCID: PMC13347500
DOI: 10.1177/1877718X251343094
First publication: 2025-06-04 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC original Methods, results and follow-up table

This retrospective analysis of a prospective multicenter cohort included 348 people with progressive supranuclear palsy. Men scored higher on the Starkstein Apathy Scale at baseline and at 52 weeks. The authors discuss falls as a common early feature of this distinct disease. Neither the baseline nor follow-up comparisons evaluate culinary sage.
