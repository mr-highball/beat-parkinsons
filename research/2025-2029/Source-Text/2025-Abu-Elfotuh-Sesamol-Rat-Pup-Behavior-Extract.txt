Experimental sesamol and vigilance interpretation

Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11481-025-10188-4
Coverage: Original article and Figure 1, factual extract.

The rat-pup experiment compared isolated sesamol, astaxanthin, physical activity and combinations in a monosodium glutamate behavioral model. Figure 1 reports open-field latency, ambulation and grooming, and Y-maze spontaneous alternation. These endpoints do not provide a dedicated sustained-attention outcome. The exposure and population both differ from an edible oil comparison in Parkinson disease.
