Title: NeuroDance: study protocol for a non-randomized multicenter study of a mixed-style dance intervention for people with Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42824393/
PMID: 42824393
PMCID: PMC13627397
DOI: 10.3389/fnhum.2026.1893119
First public: 2026-09-17
Coverage: Factual extract from original methods and outcome plan.

The protocol compares participation in mixed-style dance with a passive control group. Planned functional mobility includes gait, balance, strength and motor measures. The calendar and intervention dates record study scheduling. The article describes a study plan with no date palm food assignment or pain and apathy effect estimate.
