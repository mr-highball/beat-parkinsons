Oral ginger extract and behavior after prenatal valproate exposure
Source: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2026/fo/d5fo04364f
PMID: 41632024
DOI: 10.1039/D5FO04364F
Coverage: Publisher date and author abstract, full-text access pending

Choi and colleagues first published February 3, 2026. The authors administered oral ginger extract for four weeks starting at age six weeks to mice exposed prenatally to valproate. The abstract reports improvement in anxiety-like behavior, social and memory tests. It does not give the anxiety assay estimates, exact effect sizes or extract preparation details.
