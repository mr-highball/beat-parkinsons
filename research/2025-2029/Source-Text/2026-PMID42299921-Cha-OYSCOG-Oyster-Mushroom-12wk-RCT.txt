Title: A randomised controlled study to investigate the cognitive, mood, metabolic and anti-inflammatory effects of chronic oyster mushroom intervention in healthy older adults.
PMID: 42299921
DOI: 10.1039/d5fo04951b
Registry ID: NCT06846827
First published: 2026
Authors: Cha S, Bell L, Eastwood J, Fisher DR, Shukitt-Hale B, Zhang Z, Williams CM
Journal: Food & Function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42299921/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
OYSCOG 12-week randomized placebo-controlled trial (NCT06846827) in 80 healthy older adults aged 60-80 years consuming 4 portions/week of dried whole Pleurotus ostreatus (oyster mushroom, OM) vs placebo (PL), with EEG subset (n=40). Key findings from PubMed abstract and local factual summary (Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md):
1) Anxiety: DASS-21 anxiety improved in OM, increased in PL, and OM outperformed PL at 12 weeks.
2) Negative mood / depressive affect: PL worsened on PANAS-X fear, sadness, and shyness; at 12 weeks OM outperformed PL on PANAS-X sadness and shyness (note: DASS-21 depression subscale improvement was NOT reported).
3) Executive function / cognitive flexibility: ANCOVA revealed slower task-switching reaction times (worsened performance) in PL vs preserved performance in OM (time-by-group p = 0.029), whereas task-switch accuracy, Corsi block, and N-back showed no intervention-related change.
4) Episodic memory: OM improved and outperformed PL on RAVLT delayed word recall and delayed word recognition.
5) Inflammation: ex vivo serum reduced COX-2 and NOX-2 in rodent microglia.
Limitations: healthy non-PD older adults, dried/prepared whole P. ostreatus rather than specifically roasted, full arm means/CIs behind journal paywall, some between-group contrasts driven by placebo worsening.
