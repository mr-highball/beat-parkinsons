Title: Effectiveness of mixed reality-based rehabilitation on motor function in Parkinson's disease. A two-arm randomized trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42539816/
PMID: 42539816
PMCID: PMC13424508
DOI: 10.1177/20552076261473784
First public: 2026-07-29
Coverage: Factual extract from original methods and results.

Thirty people with Parkinson disease were randomized between mixed-reality rehabilitation and traditional physiotherapy. A separate group of 15 healthy older adults served as a control comparison. The Parkinson groups had no clear different change over time on Mini-BESTest (p=.72). This measures balance rather than the number of falls. The assigned interventions were rehabilitation modalities. The report does not give an assigned sage intake or separately estimate a sage rigidity or fall effect.
