Title: Inflammatory markers after supplementation with marine n-3 or plant n-6 PUFAs: A randomized double-blind crossover study
Publication date: 2025-03-08 (online first)
PMID: 40058591
PMCID: PMC11999210
DOI: 10.1016/j.jlr.2025.100770
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11999210/
Text coverage: Distilled original methods and selected pooled and subgroup outcomes.

A secondary analysis of NCT02647333 examined circulating and adipose inflammatory markers and included separate cell experiments. Some markers, including TNF and RANTES, fell after safflower supplementation in the reported analyses. Several others did not change, and some results varied with baseline fatty-acid status or period. These are biomarker and subgroup measurements from the same human trial as the 2021 and 2023 lipid reports. The preparation is supplemental oil, with cold pressing unverified and no assessment of Parkinson swallowing, falls or anxiety.
