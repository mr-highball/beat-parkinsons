The PPMI cohort study identifies memory-predominant and visuospatial-predominant profiles among 75 participants with Parkinson disease dementia. Table 2 reports apathy in 23 of 50 and 14 of 25, respectively, with p = 0.469.

The apathy values describe groups defined by cognitive profiles. The authors do not compare dried-date exposure or a date-induced apathy change.
