General population CHRIS study, 6,690 participants. Cross-sectional food-frequency questionnaire and 16-item Sniffin' Sticks. Hyposmia defined as at most 12 odors correctly identified. Lower odds in tea consumers versus nonconsumers, OR 0.90 (95% CI 0.84 to 0.96). The binary tea category in Table S2 consists of black and herbal teas. Green tea is absent from the specified group. Table S3 gives better identification ordinal OR 1.07 (95% CI 1.02 to 1.12). Table S4 gives the hyposmia estimate. Both are marked significant after false discovery rate correction. There is no Parkinson subgroup comparison; this analysis cannot resolve temporal ordering.
