Title: Consumption of dewaxed brown rice for a six-month period improves cognitive function in older adults
Source: https://doi.org/10.1080/09581596.2025.2598713
DOI: 10.1080/09581596.2025.2598713
Registration: UMIN000039161
Publication date: 2025-12-11
Date basis: Publisher first online date. The issue is dated 2026.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Original published Methods and Results, with the official Japanese trial registration.

Eligible older adults chose either cooked dewaxed brown rice or cooked white rice after tasting the foods. Of 74 initially allocated by preference, 56 remained in the published analysis. The trial was open-label and nonrandomized with cognitive endpoints, including the Frontal Assessment Battery and Mini-Mental State Examination. The official registry specifies cognition, psychological measures and autonomic tracking. None of the inspected outcome sections presents a standalone postural-stability comparison. The trial did not recruit a diagnosed Parkinson cohort.

The product supplier and industry support are disclosed. This is a close culinary exposure with a different outcome and nonrandomized allocation. The study cannot supply a conclusion about a standing-balance effect.

Official registration: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000044572
