Rye bread on a Parkinson food questionnaire
Source: https://doi.org/10.1163/18750796-bja10031
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: author-hosted article Methods and Table 2

Vermeire and colleagues’ first verified release was December 12, 2025. Twenty-six people with Parkinson disease and 26 controls completed a food questionnaire with rye bread among the individually named options. The paper reports blood mycotoxins and exploratory intake associations without assigned rye bread, technique-specific bread consumption or an acoustic voice measure.
