Title: Avocado Oil Prevents Neurological and Behavioral Alterations in a Quinolinic Acid-Induced Model of Huntington's Disease.
PMID: 40705210
DOI: 10.1007/s11064-025-04496-7
First published: 2025
Authors: González-Guevara E, Martínez-Gopar PE, Sánchez-Mendoza A, Angeles-López QD, López-Orozco D, Tristán-López L, El-Hafidi M, Becerril-Cavazos MD, Peña-Segura C, Silva-Islas CA, Pérez-Severiano F
Journal: Neurochemical research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40705210/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical quinolinic-acid (QA) intrastriatal excitotoxicity rat model of Huntington's disease (Wistar rats fed an avocado oil-supplemented diet for 20 days prior to QA lesion). Avocado oil diet did not alter baseline glucose, lipids, or hepatic enzymes, and after QA lesion reduced striatal oxidative damage, Fluoro-Jade B neurodegeneration, and ipsilateral turning while preserving striatal GABA. Excitotoxic Huntington striatal turning model does not directly model nigrostriatal Parkinson's disease bradykinesia, dystonia, or dyskinesia, and pressing/refining grade is unspecified in the abstract.
