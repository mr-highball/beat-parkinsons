The September 2, 2024 submitted protocol names overall Parkinson motor and nonmotor measurements, depression and general sleep quality. Its primary and secondary outcome lists and measurement methods omit the standalone Parkinson Fatigue Scale. Fatigue appears in background as a Parkinson symptom. The broad MDS-UPDRS includes a different fatigue item.

The manuscript marked R1 and dated August 9, 2025 includes fatigue among secondary outcomes and defines the standalone PFS-16 in a dedicated methods section. R2 dated August 27 and R3 dated September 12 retain it. The registry's July 30 revision, with August 9 last update, is the first archived IRCT version that lists the scale. The dates describe separate documents. The journal record provides no date for the original use of the scale in the clinic. Food & Function received the outcome manuscript on April 27, 2025 and published it on December 24, 2025. Planned recruitment dates listed in the amended protocol do not establish actual recruitment, data lock, or how the endpoint was chosen.

The final protocol describes first-grade Sargol stigma powder in a study tablet and a lactose placebo, with a 92-person target. DASS-21 depression, overall Parkinson Disease Sleep Scale and MDS-UPDRS motor examinations have different endpoints. The protocol gives no outcome estimate, no separately analyzed rigidity result and no isolated insomnia-severity result. The registered study and outcomes report are a single trial family. The peer-review versions are evidence of the protocol chronology.
