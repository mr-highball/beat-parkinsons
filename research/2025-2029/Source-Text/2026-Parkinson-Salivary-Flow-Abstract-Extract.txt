Title: Oral health and oral microbiota composition in patients with Parkinson's disease: a case–control study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42769625/
Identifiers: PMID 42769625, PMCID PMC13592001, DOI 10.1080/20002297.2026.2726612
First published: September 19, 2026
Coverage: Factual extract from the primary abstract.

Twenty participants with Parkinson disease and twenty controls underwent an oral examination, whole saliva flow tests and assessment of symptoms. The Parkinson group had lower unstimulated salivary flow and more commonly reported drooling and dry mouth. This design illustrates coexistence of reported drooling with lower unstimulated flow. It assigned no edible oil and measured no avocado-specific exposure.
