Davis JR et al. Caffeine withdrawal reversal effects on mood and cognitive performance measures. J Cogn Enhanc. Published 2026-08-06.
DOI 10.1007/s41465-026-00368-2.
Publisher original: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41465-026-00368-2
Coverage: factual summary of original Methods, Figure 1 and visual-analogue outcomes.

Twenty-five habitual caffeine consumers completed a double-blind randomized crossover with a personalized caffeine dose, a fixed anhydrous caffeine dose, and maltodextrin placebo. Those supplements were in flavored water, with the same snack. Participants were not a Parkinson cohort. VAS fatigue and energy were recorded on arrival before ingesting each assigned supplement at the experimental visits. The VAS comparisons were inconclusive for fatigue (F[2,44]=1.233, p=.301, partial eta squared .053) and energy (p=.214). These arrival ratings assess assigned-order and withdrawal context. The repeated task tests have later measurements, but the study did not repeat the fatigue rating after that visit's dose. It cannot estimate a direct acute effect of brewed coffee.
