Title: Self-care and its assessment in the patient–caregiver dyad in Parkinson's disease: a mixed-method study protocol
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12587928/
PMID: 41193192
PMCID: PMC12587928
DOI: 10.1136/bmjopen-2025-107471
Publication date: 2025-11-04
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC article XML and included tables; supplementary files are separate.

This protocol concerns self-care in Parkinson patient–caregiver dyads. The planned questionnaires assess maintenance, monitoring and management of self-care, confidence and quality of life. Qualitative work concerns how the dyad handles ongoing care. Background paragraphs enumerate Parkinson symptoms, including pain and apathy. The article presents study plans and does not report outcome comparisons or test dried dates.
