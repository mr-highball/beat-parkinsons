Title: Outcomes Following Constipation Treatments Between Parkinson’s Disease and Non-Parkinson’s Disease Patients Evaluated by the Constipation Scoring System — food and outcome scope
PMID: 41737295
PMCID: PMC12927904
DOI: 10.1155/padi/1105437
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12927904/
Published: 2026-02-23.
Archive scope: Original Methods and Table 3, with exposure and baseline limits. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

The retrospective comparison included 67 Parkinson and 50 non-Parkinson outpatients with constipation. Both clinics used mixed care, including laxatives as prescribed and general education on fruits, vegetables, water and exercise. In the dietary measurements, fruit and vegetables supplied an aggregate estimate, with no specific date fruit or ginger exposure. Coffee was reported separately, without a culinary coffee assignment.

Post-treatment mean Constipation Scoring System scores were 6.07 and 5.24, respectively, with p=.172. Table 3 separately reports an abdominal-pain item. The distributions do not establish a between-group abdominal-pain difference, and no pre-treatment Constipation Scoring System scores were available. The article supplies no within-patient change attributable to a food or treatment.
