Title: Ajwa date and menstrual pain review, checked citation scope
Source: https://doi.org/10.37899/journallamedihealtico.v6i5.2721
Publisher original: https://newinera.com/index.php/JournalLaMedihealtico/article/view/5541
DOI: 10.37899/journallamedihealtico.v6i5.2721
Publication date: 2025-12-30
Citation: Zhafirah et al. Journal La Medihealtico 6(5), 1458-1470.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: Original review, eligibility criteria, Table 1 and citation check.

The review claims to summarize empirical Ajwa interventions for menstrual pain and states reviews were excluded. Its results table nevertheless incorporates reviews of estrogen and progesterone and a non-menstrual labor-pain synthesis. The cited Al-Sayyed original abstract supplies a 36-person premenstrual intervention, without a separate comparative pain-severity estimate or verification that the fruit was dried or Ajwa. Treat the review's pain claim as a citation lead from another population. The review supplies no new independent cohort or Parkinson pain result.
