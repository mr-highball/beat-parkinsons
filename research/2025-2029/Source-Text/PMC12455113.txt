Title: Impact of levodopa and deep brain stimulation on visual impairments in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12455113/
PMID: 40995535
PMCID: PMC12455113
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2025.100390
Publication date: 2025-09-06 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The original uses within-person levodopa or stimulation comparisons for reading speed and color contrast acuity. Immediate comparisons were generally inconclusive, with exploratory changes in selected conditions after surgery. The visual tasks do not count spontaneous hallucinations after eating.
