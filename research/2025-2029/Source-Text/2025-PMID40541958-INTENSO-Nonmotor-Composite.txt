Title: Impact of an intensive outpatient rehabilitation on non-motor patients’ reported outcomes in PD: the INTENSO study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12181338/
PMID: 40541958
PMCID: PMC12181338
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-01035-7
Publication date: 2025
Coverage: Paraphrased primary paper Methods and Results and official registration fields.

The paper uses a historical cohort assigned by amount of training received, with 24 people in each rehabilitation group. It reports favorable composite nonmotor changes for the higher-intensity group at the immediate and medium-term assessments. The study cites NCT06695286, a completed observational study with 48 enrolled and no posted summary results on the checked registry. The schedule and composite comparisons do not identify a separate drooling severity effect or an ingredient exposure.
