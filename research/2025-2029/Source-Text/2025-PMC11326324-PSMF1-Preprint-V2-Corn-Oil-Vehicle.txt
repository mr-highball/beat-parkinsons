The preprint and later paper use injected corn oil as the vehicle control in a mouse experiment. Rotarod and open-field measurements concern motor behavior in that model. The human case series describes genetic phenotypes with no assigned oil consumption. No oral refined corn-oil comparison or clinical bradykinesia estimate is attributable to the oil. The 2024 preprint, 2025 revision and 2026 paper are one study lineage. The study supplies comparator and preparation context for Round 1 claim 0296. The separate editions preserve publication history without increasing the number of experiments.
