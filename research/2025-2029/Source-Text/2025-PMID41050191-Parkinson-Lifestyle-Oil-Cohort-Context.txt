Original study scope
The lifestyle review cites the same baseline self-report Parkinson survey for olive and coconut oil and aggregate PRO-PD. It discusses anxiety, sleep, balance and falls chiefly in separate nonfood interventions. It gives no per-symptom coefficient for either grade of oil and no individual sage contrast.

Continuing evidence gaps
Check the original survey and subsequent cohort analyses by preparation and symptom. Reused baseline survey results are a single study family.
