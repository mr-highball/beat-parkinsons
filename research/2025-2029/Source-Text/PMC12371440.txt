Title: Natural History and Progression of Dentatorubral-Pallidoluysian Atrophy (DRPLA): A Retrospective Study of 22 Patients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12371440/
PMID: 40237283
PMCID: PMC12371440
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70088
Publication date: 2025-04-16 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML

A retrospective analysis of 22 people with dentatorubral-pallidoluysian atrophy compares juvenile and adult onset and tracks motor milestones. Differences in psychiatric features belong to a different inherited disorder, with no dried fig exposure.
