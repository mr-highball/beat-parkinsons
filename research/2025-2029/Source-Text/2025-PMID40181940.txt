Title: Multicomponent granola and sleep in stressed adults
First published or posted: 2025-03-20
PMID: 40181940
PMCID: PMC11965129
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2025.1551313
Source URL: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2025.1551313/full
Text type: original-source evidence summary

Sasaki and colleagues followed 27 adults in UMIN000053189. They consumed granola with milk daily for eight weeks, compared with their own baseline. Specified ingredients include inulin, resistant starch, fructooligosaccharide, galactooligosaccharide, cacao and barley. The published description does not give a rolled-oat amount or confirm oats in the test recipe. Athens Insomnia Scale scores were lower than baseline at weeks four and eight. EEG sleep outcomes showed no significant changes. Objective and subjective measures differed, and the design gives no controlled oat-specific or Parkinson estimate.
