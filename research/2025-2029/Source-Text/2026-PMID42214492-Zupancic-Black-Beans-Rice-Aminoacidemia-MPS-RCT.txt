Title: Complementary plant protein pairing does not further increase postexercise myofibrillar protein synthesis after a 20 g protein dose within a high-carbohydrate whole-food matrix in young adults: a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 42214492
PMCID: PMC13494084
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2026.101380
Registry ID: NCT06781723
First published: 2026
Authors: Zupančič Ž, Barnes TM, Deutz MT, Irwin GL, Zimring JE, Chen C, et al.
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42214492/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized crossover trial (Zupancic et al., 2026, Am J Clin Nutr, PMC13494084, NCT06781723) in 11 physically active young adults (10 completers; 10 men, 1 woman) comparing post-exercise myofibrillar protein synthesis (MPS) after a 20 g protein complementary meal of canned black beans and rice (COMP) vs a nutrient-matched crystalline amino acid/carbohydrate/fiber/oil mixture (ISO). MPS did not differ between COMP (0.057 +/- 0.013 %/h) and ISO (0.052 +/- 0.013 %/h, p=0.260), while an exploratory cross-trial comparison showed lower MPS than ground pork (p<0.05). Indirect amino-acid handling context; non-PD healthy young adults with no levodopa or OFF-time outcomes.
