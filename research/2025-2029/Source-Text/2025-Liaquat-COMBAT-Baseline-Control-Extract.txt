Of 170 participants, 37 cognitively healthy controls came from COMBAT at baseline. The other 133 came from CANN with subjective (73) or mild (60) cognitive impairment. The Methods specify a score below 10 on the 15-item Geriatric Depression Scale for classification in the CANN mild cognitive impairment group. The analysis relates habitual fiber, APOE genotype, microbiome measures and cognitive and health outcomes. Supplementary Figures S1–S6 describe associations and biomarker models. Tables S1–S2 report bacterial genera and participant demographic covariates. No randomized cranberry exposure comparison or arm-specific PHQ-9 result is reported.
