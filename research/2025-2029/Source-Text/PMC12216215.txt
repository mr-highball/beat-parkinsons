Title: The relationship between paracingulate sulcus length and visual hallucinations in Parkinson's disease suggests a neurobiological predisposition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12216215/
PMID: 40592933
PMCID: PMC12216215
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-04513-3
Publication date: 2025-07-02 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The comparison uses MRI in 32 Parkinson participants with visual hallucinations and 33 without them. The regression explicitly uses presence versus absence. The anatomic findings address group status without an ingredient exposure or a counted change in frequency.
