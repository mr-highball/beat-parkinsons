The paper builds composite progression scores from longitudinal clinical measurements. Its self-reported scoring example gives weight to MDS-UPDRS item 2.2, saliva and drooling, once the value reaches the stated threshold. That fitted weight is a score contribution. It does not represent a probability, standalone symptomatic trajectory or efficacy estimate for an ingredient. The study contains no tested dietary fig exposure.
