Title: Effectiveness of Cucumis sativus L. Supplementation on Mood, Anxiety, and Sleep Quality: A Randomized Double-Blind Placebo-Controlled Study.
PMID: 40636535
PMCID: PMC12239509
DOI: 10.1002/hsr2.70979
Registry ID: NCT06246383
First published: 2025
Authors: Hausenblas HA, Hooper D, Hooper S
Journal: Health Sci Rep
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12239509/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (Hausenblas et al., 2025, PMC12239509, same NCT06246383 cohort of n=80 US adults: 42 Q-Actin 20 mg/day standardized Cucumis sativus extract vs 38 rice-protein placebo for 60 days). Full-text table verification confirms EVERY Group x Time interaction was statistically NON-SIGNIFICANT: Flinders Fatigue Scale (p=0.60), POMS Total Mood Disturbance (p=0.61), Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (p=0.51), Perceived Stress Scale (p=0.15), STAI Trait Anxiety (p=0.32), HRQoL sad/blue days (p=0.32), and worried/anxious days (p=0.22), despite substantial baseline imbalance favoring placebo. Strong competing null clinical evidence against raw cucumber extract improving anxiety, depression, fatigue, or insomnia.
