Title: Lower Urinary Tract Symptoms Correlation With Motor and Cognitive Function in Patients With Parkinson Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12516142/
PMID: 41077794
PMCID: PMC12516142
DOI: 10.5213/inj.2550094.047
Publication date: 2025-09-30
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC article XML and included tables; supplementary files are separate.

Cross-sectional study of 18 Parkinson clinic patients. The researchers compared validated lower urinary tract questionnaires with UPDRS motor examination, modified Hoehn and Yahr staging and MoCA. Results include relationships with global motor severity and comparisons by medication status. The paper's introductory description of rest tremor gives clinical background. The study reports no separate fig exposure, drooling result or rest-tremor contrast.
