Original study scope
This Cochrane review compares topical skin cleansing and protective products for incontinence-associated dermatitis in adults. Symptoms and tolerability are considered within this indication.

Continuing evidence gaps
The tested route is topical. No oral fresh-sage or date intervention was identified for Parkinson pain.
