The two-centre crossover provided 39 participants with prehypertension three standardized mixed diets for two weeks each. Thirty-six participants completed the trial. Carrot and bell pepper were among the common produce. The comparator was low in total fruit and vegetables. The poster reports total cholesterol −0.19 mmol/L (95% CI −0.32 to −0.05) and LDL −0.15 mmol/L (−0.26 to −0.03) for the common produce diet, with no detected difference in office blood pressure, pulse wave velocity, standardized augmentation index or CRP. These are diet-level measures and do not estimate a carrot-specific effect.

The already cataloged 2025 supplement abstract verifies the same trial family by registration number. The 2024 protocol identifies planned cognition and mood measurements.
