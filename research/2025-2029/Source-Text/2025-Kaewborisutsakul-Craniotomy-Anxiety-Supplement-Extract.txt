Ginger extract and anxiety in a blinded craniotomy comparison
October 3, 2025 · PMID 41112738 · PMC12532379 · DOI 10.3389/fnut.2025.1624176
https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC12532379.1/Table_1.docx
Factual extract from official NLM supplemental Table 1 and original Methods.

The 48-person randomized trial gave 24 participants per group a preoperative carbohydrate drink with or without standardized ginger extract. Supplementary Table 1 reports anxiety VAS mean ± SD for ginger versus control of 2.96 ± 3.62 versus 3.38 ± 3.52 at baseline (p = .604), 2.00 ± 2.60 versus 1.21 ± 1.56 on day one (p = .420), and 1.62 ± 2.53 versus 0.71 ± 1.23 on day two (p = .266). These are per-visit contrasts, without a baseline-adjusted change estimate or equivalence test. Surgical and anesthesia durations were longer in the ginger group. The existing study identifier TCTR20220124002 is retained. Its official registered outcomes remain unverified. The supplement belongs to the existing article and adds no independent cohort.
