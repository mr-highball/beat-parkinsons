Title: Phase 2a/b randomised placebo-controlled dose-escalation trial of triheptanoin for ataxia-telangiectasia: treating mitochondrial dysfunction with anaplerosis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12271818/
PMCID: PMC12271818
PMID: 40616902
DOI: 10.1016/j.ebiom.2025.105840
First public article date: 2025-07-04
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled original publisher PDF, Methods, results and swallowing analysis.

Lynch and colleagues enrolled 31 participants with ataxia-telangiectasia at ages 4 through 37. The placebo oil was a Nutricia medium-chain triglyceride preparation. At maximum triheptanoin dose, the authors estimated a −0.9 difference in swallowing safety score (95% CI −1.6 to −0.3) relative to placebo or periods without treatment. The comparison combines conditions and is a secondary outcome. The oil comparator is expressly identified as MCT. No safflower arm or Parkinson cohort appears in the trial.

This study has a distinct registration (NCT04513002) and cohort from the Huntington and Glut1 comparisons. Its specific swallowing result cannot be combined with either trial's safflower arms.
