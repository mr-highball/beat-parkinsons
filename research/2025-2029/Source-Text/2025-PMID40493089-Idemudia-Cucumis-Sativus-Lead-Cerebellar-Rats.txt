Title: Upregulation of Caspase-3 and TNF-α in a rat model of cerebellar motor disorder: role of Cucumis sativus (cucumber).
PMID: 40493089
DOI: 10.1007/s10735-025-10482-8
First published: 2025
Authors: Idemudia OU, Enogieru AB
Journal: J Mol Histol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40493089/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rodent toxicology study (Idemudia & Enogieru, 2025) evaluating Cucumis sativus extract against lead-acetate-induced cerebellar oxidative stress, TNF-alpha/Caspase-3 upregulation, and motor coordination deficits in rats. Non-Parkinson heavy-metal cerebellar toxicity model using extract rather than raw or pickled dietary cucumber.
