Title: A combined workplace intervention including exercise and nutrition for health promotion in obese office workers, a randomized controlled study
Source: https://doi.org/10.3390/jfmk11040418
DOI: 10.3390/jfmk11040418
Trial registration: NCT07603141
Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07603141
Publication date: 2026-10-13
Source depth: Original full article, methods, standing balance results and linked registry.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27

Thirty-six office workers with obesity were randomized, 18 per group, to six months of exercise plus dietary and hydration education or a control condition. The single-leg stance group-by-time interactions favoring the program were F(1,34) = 30.29 on the right and 30.06 on the left, each p < .001. Brown rice appears as a nonrefined cereal example within a Mediterranean diet score. The program did not randomize brown rice consumption or report a brown rice-specific outcome.

The linked registry was first posted in May 2026 after its recorded study completion. Treat the registry and paper as one trial family. For Round 1 claim 0471, the outcome is a balance measure, while the intervention and population differ from cooked brown rice and Parkinson-related postural instability.
