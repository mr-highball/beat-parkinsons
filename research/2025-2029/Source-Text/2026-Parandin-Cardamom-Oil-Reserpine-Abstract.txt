The abstract describes 42 male mice in six groups of seven and reports favorable behavioral proxies with cardamom oil in a reserpine model. It names forced swimming, tail suspension and elevated-plus-maze outcomes. The abstract leaves the oil's botanical species, administration route, group-level results and uncertainty open. A locomotion change can affect behavior in immobility assays.

Different authorship, animal species and group counts distinguish this bibliographic record from the 2023 Abdel-Rasoul rat report (PMID36781058). Independent original data await verification. The published abstract does not study culinary ground cardamom, people with Parkinson disease or clinical depressive symptoms. Round 1 claim 0777 retains its existing verdict.
