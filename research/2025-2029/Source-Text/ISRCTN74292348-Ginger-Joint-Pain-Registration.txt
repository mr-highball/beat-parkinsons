Joint pain ginger extract registration and enrollment chronology
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/74292348
Registration: ISRCTN74292348
Coverage: Official registry, first posted May 7, 2025

The registration identifies a blinded randomized comparison of standardized ginger extract and placebo for 58 days. Its primary outcomes include pressure-evoked muscle pain, function and inflammatory measures. It lists first enrollment April 6, 2023, final enrollment August 8, 2024 and completion December 16, 2024. Public registration therefore followed enrollment and study completion. The conference and journal accounts identify the same study.
