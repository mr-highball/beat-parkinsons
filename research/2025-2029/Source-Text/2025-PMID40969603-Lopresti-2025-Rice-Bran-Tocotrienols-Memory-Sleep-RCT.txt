Title: An examination into the effects of tocotrienols (TheraPrimE® rice) on cognitive abilities and sleep in healthy adults: a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 40969603
PMCID: PMC12440894
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2025.1621516
Registry ID: ACTRN12624000351516
First published: 2025
Authors: Lopresti AL, Smith SJ, Ding L, Li Y, Zhang P
Journal: Frontiers in nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12440894/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (ACTRN12624000351516) in n=91 adults (40-80 y) with subjective memory complaints taking 100 mg/d rice bran-derived tocotrienols (TheraPrimE rice) vs placebo. Tocotrienols significantly improved general memory (p=0.045), non-verbal memory (p=0.039), and sleep disturbance (p=0.015, 95% CI -4.80 to -0.55), with NO group differences in verbal memory or self-reported executive function, and no serious AEs. Limits: concentrated rice bran tocotrienol extract in non-PD adults.
