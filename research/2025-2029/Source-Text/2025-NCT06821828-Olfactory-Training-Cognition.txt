Registered odor training with a cognitive test
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06821828
Coverage: Factual summary of the official registry.

First posted February 12, 2025. The registered program targets older adults with idiopathic olfactory dysfunction and subjective cognitive decline. The protocol compares modified and conventional olfactory training and a control. The February registry snapshot still says not yet recruiting and shows no posted results. The August journal protocol describes March 2025 recruitment and planned Trail Making assessment. It uses clove odor with other scents and no culinary ingestion, separate clove arm or Parkinson outcome.
