Title: Feeding the mind: preliminary insights into the effects of Anthocyanin-Rich Extract from black carrots on brain activity and gut microbiota in patients with cognitive impairments.
PMID: 41310168
PMCID: PMC12749660
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-29256-z
First published: 2025
Authors: Muduroglu-Kirmizibekmez A, Onder A, Ozdemir MY, Gurerk G, Aydin S, Eryigit OY et al.
Journal: Sci Rep
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41310168/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Uncontrolled single-arm 12-week pre-post study (n=50 institutionalized older adults with neurocognitive impairment) given an anthocyanin-rich extract from black carrots. Reported increases in resting-state EEG alpha/beta/gamma connectivity and nonlinear complexity plus taxonomic gut shifts (higher Alistipes, Streptococcus thermophilus, Flavonifractor; lower Hungatella) without overall diversity change. Critical limitations: no placebo/control arm, no post-intervention clinical cognitive/attention/executive score reported (only baseline MMSE/MoCA characterization), anthocyanin extract rather than culinary orange steamed/raw carrot, and non-PD dementia cohort.
