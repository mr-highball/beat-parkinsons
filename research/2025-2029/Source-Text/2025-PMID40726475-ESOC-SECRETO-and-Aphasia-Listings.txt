ESOC25-2386 reports a case-crossover analysis of 598 younger adults with cryptogenic ischemic stroke under the SECRETO study. Habitual intake and the timing of reported coffee, tea and cola or energy-drink intake were used to estimate exposure associations. Reported Mantel–Haenszel relative risks for coffee were 1.729 (95% CI 1.056–2.831) at one hour and 2.147 (1.401–3.291) at two hours. The outcome was stroke onset. The 2026 Journal of Neurology report describes the same 598-person analysis.

ESOC25-825 presents words chosen for personalized speech and language therapy after stroke. Banana and lentils appear as examples of food nouns. The abstract does not assign ingestion, food odor or tasting. It is a separate member from the coffee analysis.
