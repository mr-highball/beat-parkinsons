Title: Opicapone in Parkinson's patients with motor fluctuations: clinical assessments and patient-reported outcomes from the OPTI-ON study

PMID: 42016531
PMCID: PMC13092661

Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19

Text coverage: Original PMC XML body and embedded tables with separately distributed Supplementary Tables S1, S5 and S6. Factual extract.

Opti-ON observed patients with Parkinson disease and motor fluctuations after a clinician initiated opicapone. Of 239 recruited participants, 232 entered the treated set and 161 had a six-month follow-up. Supplementary Table S5 reports clinician-rated apathy improvement in 12 of 65 (18.5%) at month three and 25 of 117 (21.4%) at month six. Table S6 reports patient-rated NoMoFa pain improvement in 34 of 116 at month one, 30 of 99 at month three and 33 of 100 at month six. The separate patient-rated apathy/loss-of-interest item improved in 26 of 118, 20 of 100 and 16 of 100, respectively. Item denominators are consistent with the table's counts and percentages and differ from the whole treated cohort. NoMoFa includes symptom severity and fluctuation. Its item changes are distinct from a standalone pain-intensity contrast. The uncontrolled follow-up, incomplete follow-up and medication changes constrain attribution. No dried-date intervention was evaluated.
Trial registration: NCT04787965.
