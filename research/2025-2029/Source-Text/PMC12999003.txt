Title: VPS13C-Related Parkinson's Disease: Report of a Novel Variant from India and Systematic Review of the Literature
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12999003/
PMID: 40726379
PMCID: PMC12999003
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70248
Publication date: 2025-07-29 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML

A clinical case with an identified VPS13C variant and a systematic review describe inherited Parkinson and parkinsonism phenotypes. The original comparison is genotype and case presentation, without dried fig intake or food-related visual outcomes.
