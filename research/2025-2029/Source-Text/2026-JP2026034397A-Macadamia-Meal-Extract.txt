Chusei Oil patent publication with Ikeguchi among the inventors, JP2026034397A, February 27, 2026. Original Japanese text, paragraphs 0013 and 0069–0084.

Test Example 1 names Nisshin Macadamia Nut Oil 145 g Fresh Keep Bottle for an oral breakfast comparison with three participants. Outcomes included sleepiness and fatigue ratings, core temperature and glucose. Test Example 2 describes 16 participants, two bread rolls with 8 g of Summit oil, and bread-only and soybean-oil comparisons. It reports a sleepiness rating and specifies a palmitoleic acid range of approximately 21%–25%. The design and sample size match the 16-person Okayama breakfast report closely enough to flag probable overlap. The accessible sources do not identify the participants across reports. The patent examples supply no cold-press temperature, manual dexterity, vigilance test result, or Parkinson measurement. The two human test examples and separate rat experiment remain distinct.
