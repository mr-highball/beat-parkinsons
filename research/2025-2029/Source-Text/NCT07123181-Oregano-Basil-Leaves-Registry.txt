Registered dried oregano and basil leaves in a coronary study
Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07123181
Coverage: factual extract of official registration.

The registry first posted August 14, 2025. Participants in the intervention arm are to eat dried oregano and dried basil leaves, 4 to 5 grams of each per day for two months, in addition to usual medical treatment. The control receives standard treatment. The planned outcomes include circulating inflammatory markers and lipids in people with unstable angina. In the checked snapshot the study was recruiting and had no results posted. The preparation is verified as an assigned mixed-leaf intervention, with no individual intake or Parkinson outcome available.
