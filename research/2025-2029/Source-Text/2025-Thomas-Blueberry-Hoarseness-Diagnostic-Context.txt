Hoarseness initially attributed to blueberries
Source: https://assets.cureus.com/uploads/case_report/pdf/438662/20260101-44238-wny7vv.pdf
Coverage: Factual summary of the primary report.

Published December 2, 2025. Hoarseness and swelling in a young adult were initially attributed to frequent blueberry intake and briefly improved after avoidance. Symptoms progressed despite avoidance. Imaging found an anterior mediastinal mass encasing major vessels and partially compressing the trachea. Histology indicated a round cell neoplasm, with exact subtype unconfirmed because immunohistochemistry was declined. The case gives no measured vocal intensity, controlled rechallenge, verified berry preparation or Parkinson diagnosis. The clinical timeline does not establish blueberry causation.
