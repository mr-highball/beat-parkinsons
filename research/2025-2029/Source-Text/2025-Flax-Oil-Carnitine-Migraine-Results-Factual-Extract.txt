Title: The effects of concurrent alpha-linolenic acid, L-carnitine supplementation on clinical symptoms, mental health, and quality of life in women with migraine

Publication date: 2025-03-13
Trial registration: IRCT20121216011763N57

IRCT20121216011763N57. Published protocol PMID 39380085 and original results PMID 40082970, March 13, 2025. The protocol specified the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index. Protocol original https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11463146/ .
The results report describes Barij flaxseed oil 1,000 mg containing 350 mg alpha-linolenic acid plus 500 mg L-carnitine, against two placebos. It reports migraine, mood, stress and quality-of-life outcomes. No PSQI score or sleep outcome appears in the accessible article. Other protocol outcomes were reserved for separate reporting. Oil processing was not specified.
The two active ingredients prevent attribution to flaxseed oil. Sleep scores remain unverified and the cohort had migraine.

November 26, 2026 outcome review. The report randomized 80 women and 75 completed. In the intention-to-treat analysis with last observation carried forward, Table 2 gives DASS-21 anxiety change of −5.70 points (95% CI −7.26 to −4.14) with the flaxseed oil and carnitine combination, and −0.65 (−2.33 to 1.03) with placebo. The article reports p<.001 for the group contrast. Its design does not separate flaxseed oil from carnitine, and the authors do not verify cold pressing. The protocol lists PSQI, but the inspected results article provides no sleep estimate. The protocol and results belong to one registered cohort.
