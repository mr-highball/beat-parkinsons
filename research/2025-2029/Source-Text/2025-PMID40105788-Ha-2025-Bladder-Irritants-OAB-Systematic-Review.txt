Title: Potential Bladder Irritants and Overactive Bladder Symptoms: A Systematic Review.
PMID: 40105788
DOI: 10.1097/SPV.0000000000001567
First published: 2025
Authors: Ha B, Seo Y, Weaver AM, Khan FZA, Handa VL
Journal: Urogynecology (Philadelphia, Pa.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40105788/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

PROSPERO-registered systematic review (CRD42023466251) of 51 studies (27 on caffeinated/carbonated/citrus drinks; 15 evaluating urinary urgency) examining bladder irritants and OAB symptoms. Concluded that across heterogeneous observational and interventional studies, overall associations were mixed and inconsistent. Context-only secondary synthesis.
