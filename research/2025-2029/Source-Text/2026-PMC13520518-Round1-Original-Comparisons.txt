Round 1 full publication review. The archive retains the focal exposure and outcome boundaries for each queued claim.

The Huperzia formulation study compares olive, soybean and sesame oils in the initial solubility screen. None is the assigned food exposure in the motor experiment. The animal arm uses a Huperzia-based formulation with a different vehicle. Motor and biochemical results relate to that treatment package.

Claim 0001 | cold-pressed olive oil | postural instability severity | related
Olive oil appears in the preliminary huperzine A solubility table. The animal comparison used the selected Huperzia formulation, leaving cold-pressed olive oil and postural instability unmeasured.
Claim 0107 | refined soybean oil | dyskinesia severity | related
Soybean oil is an excipient-screen candidate. It was not assigned as a refined oral exposure in a dyskinesia experiment.
Claim 0262 | cold-pressed sesame oil | depressive symptom severity | related
The sesame oil entry is a formulation solubility measurement, with no independent clinical depression endpoint.
Claim 0377 | cold-pressed sesame oil | muscle rigidity severity | related
Sesame oil is one of the preliminary solubility comparators. The selected formulation has different components and gives no cold-pressed sesame oil rigidity result.
Claim 0629 | cold-pressed olive oil | bradykinesia severity | related
Olive oil was screened for huperzine solubility and was not the assigned oral motor intervention. The reported tests of the Huperzia preparation do not estimate olive oil bradykinesia.
Claim 0856 | refined soybean oil | bradykinesia severity | related
The soybean oil solubility measurement does not isolate a refined oral soybean oil or clinical bradykinesia effect.
