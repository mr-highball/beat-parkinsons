Title: Oatmeal-rice lunch after skipping breakfast
First published or posted: 2025
DOI: 10.11402/ajscs.36.0_58
Source URL: https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1390305411857313664
Text type: original-source evidence summary

Furutani and colleagues reported a four-day crossover in 23 adults at the Japan Society of Cookery Science. Participants skipped breakfast and ate lunches with white rice or oatmeal rice. The abstract identifies a daily OSA-MA sleep questionnaire and reports glycemic results. It gives no numerical sleep effect. The design resembles the later UMIN000048192 lunch narrative, which reports 22 participants. The abstract and registry linkage remain uncertain.
