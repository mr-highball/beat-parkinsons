Title: Evaluation of the Gastroprotective Effects of Hazelnut Oil Against Ethanol-Induced Gastric Mucosal Injury in Rats
Authors: Cagri Akalin and colleagues
First published: 2025-08-31
DOI: 10.56941/odutip.1737767
Source: https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/5035755
Coverage: Original factual extract of preparation, gross gastric outcome and histology in the version of record.

The investigators describe cold pressing Corylus avellana from Türkiye's Black Sea region. They allocated 35 male Wistar rats to five groups. Oil was administered orally during the seven days preceding an acute gastric ethanol challenge. Separate arms received saline, ethanol alone or ranitidine plus ethanol.

Table 1 reports median ulcer area of 19.00 mm² after ethanol alone (IQR 17.36–21.23), 15.00 with the lower oil dose (IQR 13.25–17.25), and 4.00 with the higher dose (IQR 3.60–5.40). The overall test was p<.001. The report also states that both oil arms differed from ethanol on the post hoc gross-lesion comparison (p<.001 for each). Table 2 median histopathology totals were 10, 4 and 2.5, respectively. The measured outcomes were lesion extent and microscopic tissue pathology. No pain intensity, nociceptive response or Parkinson symptom was recorded.
