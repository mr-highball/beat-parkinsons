Archive scope: Original article Methods, Tables 1–2 and planned analyses. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

The clinical database protocol describes 20 institutions in nine countries and a questionnaire available in four translated languages in addition to English. Table 2 distinguishes black tea, black tea with milk, green tea and several coffee types. It records a categorical frequency for coffee or tea intake. The rest tremor item asks whether it has occurred since the Parkinson diagnosis. This is a history variable, separate from a clinically measured rest-tremor severity contrast.

The article describes a planned multilevel analysis of exposures and clinical variables. It does not supply an estimated black-tea comparison for tremor presence or severity. The earlier verified questionnaire specifies item 32 for frequency and duration of beverage use and item 35 for rest-tremor history. Preparation concentration, brew time and the relation to examination timing remain unquantified. The outcome-publication trail remains open.
