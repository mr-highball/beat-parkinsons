Title: Effects of Kefir Consumption on Gastrointestinal Health, Biochemical Parameters, Sleep, and Mental Well-Being in Healthy Young Adults: a Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 41314291
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2025.11.016
First published: 2026
Authors: Bakırhan H, Ozyurek Arpa F, Pehlivan M, Kalkan I
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41314291/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized controlled 6-week trial (NCT06612164) in 65 healthy young adults consuming 250 mL/day lactose-free kefir vs control. Kefir significantly reduced gastrointestinal symptoms (p<0.05) along with serum cholesterol, creatinine, and uric acid, whereas SLEEP QUALITY, MENTAL HEALTH scores, CRP, and immune markers showed NO statistically significant between-group differences (p>0.05).
