Title: Deviations in effective connectivity explain different hallucination subtypes in Parkinson's disease psychosis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13259922/
PMID: 42291779
PMCID: PMC13259922
DOI: 10.1038/s44220-026-00669-7
Publication date: 2026-06-10 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The clinical sample from NCT03661125 comprised 18 people with Parkinson disease without hallucinations and 20 meeting Parkinson psychosis criteria. A dedicated interview characterizes visual phenomena. The experiment compares visual mismatch EEG and model-based connectivity, with no food intervention or food-timed clinical episode rate.

Registration: NCT03661125. The previously indexed registration supplies the original plan and should be linked to this result rather than counted as a separate clinical sample. See ../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT03661125.txt.
