Title: Brain-clinical signatures of basal ganglia-related dysfunctional reorganisation in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12571575/
PMID: 40939293
PMCID: PMC12571575
DOI: 10.1016/j.ebiom.2025.105917
Publication date: 2025-09-11 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The cross-sectional study compares 102 people with Parkinson and 88 matched controls on basal-ganglia functional gradients and clinical measures. Network reorganization and diagnosis status are distinct from an ingested ingredient and an episode-frequency outcome.
