# Spinach and leek pooled in a Parkinson diet questionnaire

Source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12955576/
Coverage original clinical questionnaire table

Li and colleagues recruited 60 people with Parkinson’s and 60 matched controls for a hair-metal case-control study first published February 12, 2026. Its questionnaire combined spinach and leek in a single leafy-green item. Fifty-five Parkinson cases and 57 controls reported intake at least weekly. This is an observational intake category. It does not separate raw spinach from roasted leek or estimate effects on dyskinesia or muscle rigidity.
