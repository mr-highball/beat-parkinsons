Source: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlepdf/2025/fo/d5fo01172h
DOI: 10.1039/d5fo01172h
PMID: 40878144
Format: Original methods and results, paraphrased.
Design: Placebo-controlled double-blind 12-week randomization of 80 adults with functional dyspepsia. GGE03 was steamed, ethanol-extracted and spray-dried ginger.
Outcome: GSRS constipation change was -1.5 ± 4.4 with GGE03 and +1.4 ± 2.9 with placebo. The original comparison reports p<.001. The population and processing differ from the frozen experiment claim.
