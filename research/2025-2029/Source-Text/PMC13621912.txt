Title: Reduced volume of neuromodulatory brainstem nuclei is associated with early hallucination-related vulnerability in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13621912/
PMID: 42813096
PMCID: PMC13621912
DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcag357
Publication date: 2026-09-20 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The study uses PPMI structural scans and item 1.2 during screening or early follow-up as a proxy for hallucination-related status. The item does not separate all visual subtypes and modalities. MRI-derived volume and presence or absence of the broad proxy do not provide food-timed episode frequency.
