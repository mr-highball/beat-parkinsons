Thirteen people with Parkinson disease completed airway-protection interviews and questionnaires. The interview material includes distinct reports of swallowing difficulty, throat clearing, perceived excess saliva and nighttime drooling.

The study did not assign dried dates or estimate their effect on pain. Airway and drooling experience are useful context, with no ingredient-specific response.
