Safinamide and non-motor symptom domains in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12293413/
PMID: 40722259
PMCID: PMC12293413
DOI: 10.3390/brainsci15070666
First publication: 2025-06-20 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC original Methods, visit measures and subgroup tables

This post hoc analysis of the single-arm SAFINONMOTOR study used data from 44 of 50 people with Parkinson disease who completed the six-month visit. It reports lower scores in the mood/apathy and miscellaneous domains across analyzed strata. Pain is one of the miscellaneous items. The domain contrasts cannot isolate apathy or pain, and the exposure was safinamide rather than dried date fruit.
