Title: Radium levels in Brazil nuts: A review of the literature.
PMID: 39489716
PMCID: PMC11815606
DOI: 10.1111/nbu.12717
First published: 2025
Authors: Koeder C, Keller M
Journal: Nutrition bulletin
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39489716/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Review of naturally occurring radioactivity (radium-226 and radium-228) accumulated in Brazil nuts (Bertholletia excelsa), noting routine small consumption (1-2 nuts/day) yields low radiation doses while excessive intake increases cumulative exposure alongside selenium overload.
