Original study scope
This observational analysis compares chronic pain prevalence and types among insured people with and without tic disorders.

Continuing evidence gaps
Administrative diagnoses and mood context do not identify interventions using sage, dates or lemon balm, or a matched Parkinson pain or depression outcome.
