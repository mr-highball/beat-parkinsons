Apigenin enhances Nrf2-induced chaperone-mediated autophagy and mitigates α-synuclein pathology: Implications for Parkinson's disease therapy. Phytomedicine, 2025.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40215824/
Access and coverage: Indexed original author abstract and accessible publisher summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

The investigators selected apigenin through compound screening and studied molecular and cell responses, alongside motor testing in a mouse Parkinson model. The motor tests named in the abstract are rotarod, pole, open field, footprints and gait. No dried parsley matrix, levodopa-induced abnormal involuntary movement scale or human dyskinesia outcome is reported in the accessible source.
