Title: Acute hemodynamic and autonomic modulation following beetroot and grapefruit juice consumption, alone and in combination, in adults with hypertension: A randomized crossover pilot study.
PMID: 41873556
DOI: 10.1177/02601060261434455
First published: 2026
Authors: Sai A, Shetty P, Shetty GB
Journal: Nutrition and health
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41873556/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized crossover pilot in 15 hypertensive adults testing acute beetroot juice, grapefruit juice, and their combination over 4.5 h. Time-dependent drops occurred in SBP (p=0.002), DBP (p=0.006), and HR (p=0.004), with NO significant main effect of condition or condition x time interaction between juices. Tempered/null acute differential hemodynamic study.
