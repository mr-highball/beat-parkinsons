Title: Intracerebroventricular anaerobic dopamine in Parkinson's disease with L-dopa-related complications: a phase 1/2 randomized-controlled trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11922744/
PMID: 39775041
PMCID: PMC11922744
DOI: 10.1038/s41591-024-03428-2
Publication date: 2025-01-07
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Factual extract from the verified PMC XML body and Figure 2.

The DIVE-I study evaluated a device-assisted medication approach in Parkinson disease. Twelve participants entered the phase 1 component. Nine were in the randomized open-label phase 2 crossover, which used blinded scoring of the primary actimetry outcome. The combined dyskinesia and bradykinesia time measure favored the device-assisted medication state, with a median paired difference of -10.4 percentage points (p=0.027). The cited dystonia-related secondary outcomes combine dystonia and dyskinesia, or use broader motor and fluctuation scales. Standalone dystonia outcomes and any participant overlap with a later published clinical case were unverified.

The trial did not evaluate sage or a dietary exposure. Trial registration NCT04332276.
