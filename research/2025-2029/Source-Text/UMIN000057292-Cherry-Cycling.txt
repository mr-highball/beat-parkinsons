Tart cherry and repeated cycling registry
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000065488
Registration: UMIN000057292
Coverage: Official Japan UMIN registration, disclosed March 14, 2025

The registered crossover aims to enroll 15 men and compare a 200 g beverage made with 30 mL tart cherry concentrate to maltodextrin and flavored powder placebo twice daily for seven days and before exercise. The listed outcomes are endurance, anaerobic performance, neuromuscular function, maximal voluntary contraction and force steadiness. Posted results and a patient-reported fatigue scale are absent from the checked record. The title refers to fatigue during exercise, which the registered measures define as performance and neuromuscular outcomes.
