Review depth: Publisher abstract and supporting-information description.
Access: Subscription article. The public supporting-material description lists laboratory viability, molecular-modeling and docking items.

The study used isolated 6-shogaol in a mouse model and a cell system.
The authors report improved mouse behavioral performance, more
tyrosine-hydroxylase-positive neurons and less alpha-synuclein
accumulation. Their cellular interpretation concerns VEGFR2-related
autophagy signaling. The available abstract and supplementary
description do not identify a separate rigidity, dyskinesia or
stride endpoint or evaluate culinary ginger in people. Behavioral
test names and estimates need the complete main article.

Source independence: The Jin and colleagues ACS report is distinct
from the Kim and colleagues March 2025 neurotransmitter article and
the May 2026 STAT3 abstract. Their compounds and general experimental
domain are shared. The cohorts and individual endpoint estimates
should remain separate.
