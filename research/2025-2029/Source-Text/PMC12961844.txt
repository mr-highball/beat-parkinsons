Title: Exploring eye movement abnormalities as objective biomarkers for Parkinson's disease utilizing virtual reality-based eye tracking
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12961844/
PMID: 41530703
PMCID: PMC12961844
DOI: 10.1186/s12883-026-04634-w
Publication date: 2026-01-14 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

A clinical comparison evaluates virtual reality eye tracking for oculomotor dysfunction in Parkinson disease. Gaze and saccade features relate to Parkinson status and severity. Task-evoked eye behavior is a different outcome from spontaneous visual hallucination frequency.
