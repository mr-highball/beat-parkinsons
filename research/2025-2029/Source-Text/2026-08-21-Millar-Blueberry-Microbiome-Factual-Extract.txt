The 2026 original article specifies NCT05817383 as the Berries, Bugs, and the Blues substudy nested in NCT05735587. The paper analyzes 18 stool contributors, ten assigned freeze-dried blueberry and eight placebo, after three months. Mixed models did not show a clear group-by-visit effect on mood measures. The findings on gut microbial enzymes and metabolites do not measure constipation severity or bowel frequency. The parent trial collects adverse events separately.
