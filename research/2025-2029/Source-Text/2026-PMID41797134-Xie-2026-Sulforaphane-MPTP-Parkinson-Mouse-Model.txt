Title: The H2S donor sulforaphane inhibits NLRP3 inflammasome activation by inducing mitochondrial autophagy and mitigating CBS-H2S axis damage in in-vitro and in-vivo models of Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 41797134
DOI: 10.1016/j.bioorg.2026.109708
First published: 2026
Authors: Xie W, Wu K, Zhang L, Feng X, Yang S, Jia S
Journal: Bioorganic chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41797134/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

MPTP-induced PD mouse model and MPP+ BV-2 microglial study showing sulforaphane improved motor performance, increased nigral TH+ dopaminergic neuron survival, normalized striatal DOPAC/DA and 5-HIAA/5-HT ratios, and suppressed mtROS/NLRP3 inflammasome activation via CBS-H2S-driven mitophagy independently of Nrf2. Limits: purified sulforaphane in MPTP mouse model.
