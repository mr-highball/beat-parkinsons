Title: Dietary Ficus carica inhibits cognitive impairment in hypoxia-induced non-alcoholic fatty liver disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12455911/
PMID: 40288909
PMCID: PMC12455911
DOI: 10.24546/0100495569
Publication date: 2025-04-14
Citation: Kobe Journal of Medical Sciences 71, E19–E27.
Coverage: Factual summary from the published PMC XML, including preparation, outcomes and the behavioral results.

The investigators used homogenized fresh common-fig fruit as a puree in a rat intermittent-hypoxia model. They assessed Y-maze behavior and liver, plasma and brain markers. The reported memory comparison does not measure resting tremor, a Parkinson motor syndrome or an effect in people. Y-maze exploratory activity and memory are distinct from a tremor measure. The methods give a narrative total of 24 rats while the five listed group counts sum to 29. The study gives fresh fruit preparation context and has no interpretable Parkinson tremor effect.
