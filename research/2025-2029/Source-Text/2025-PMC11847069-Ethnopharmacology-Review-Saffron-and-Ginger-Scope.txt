Coverage: Factual extract of the complete primary publication and relevant ingredient contexts.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The paper distinguishes Crocus sativus and its constituents from plants with similar common names. A cited processing example uses ginger with a separate toxic seed. The original review gives no ginger-only effect or tested culinary orange comparison. Constipation indications in other plant sections cannot be attributed to ginger or saffron.
