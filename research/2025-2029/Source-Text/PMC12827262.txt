Title: Disrupted visual-to-semantic dynamics promote visual hallucinations in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12827262/
PMID: 41453976
PMCID: PMC12827262
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-01235-1
Publication date: 2025-12-26 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The clinical sample has 93 Parkinson outpatients, 55 without hallucinations and 38 with minor phenomena. The group comparison uses visual-to-semantic assessments. Those task measures and a history of minor phenomena do not provide repeated visual event counts after a meal.
