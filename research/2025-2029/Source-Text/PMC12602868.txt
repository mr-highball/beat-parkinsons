Title: Sentence comprehension in Lewy body diseases: a functional magnetic resonance imaging study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12602868/
PMID: 41229612
PMCID: PMC12602868
DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcaf423
Publication date: 2025-10-30 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The sentence-comprehension fMRI study compares 25 people with mild cognitive impairment in Lewy body disease and 25 healthy controls. Language and processing measurements do not establish episode frequency or dietary exposure.
