Title: The effect of lavender herbal tea on the mental health of individuals with misophonia: A randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 41579454
DOI: 10.1016/j.jpsychires.2026.01.028
Registry ID: NCT06785649
First published: 2026
Authors: Koroglu Gokbel S, Durat G
Journal: Journal of psychiatric research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41579454/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single-blind randomized clinical trial (Koroglu Gokbel & Durat, 2026, J Psychiatr Res, NCT06785649) in 60 adults with misophonia (30 lavender tea, 30 no-intervention control). Experimental group consumed 2 g brewed lavender herbal tea twice daily for 14 days. Mixed ANOVA suggested more pronounced reductions in misophonia, Beck Depression Inventory-II, Anxiety Rating Scale, and Trait Anger scores in the lavender tea group vs control, though abstract omits numeric group means/p-values and authors explicitly caution about lack of participant blinding, self-report measures, and expectancy/placebo bias.
