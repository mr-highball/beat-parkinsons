Original study scope
The original mouse study compared intravenous curcumin preparations after MPTP exposure, using spontaneous activity, open field, water maze, and microbiome measures. Locomotor measures favored nanoparticles versus the model group.

Continuing evidence gaps
Turmeric constituent context only. Oral culinary turmeric and pain were not measured. Orange describes curcumin's color, and the study did not test orange fruit or quantify constipation.
