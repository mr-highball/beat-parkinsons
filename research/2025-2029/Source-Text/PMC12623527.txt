Title: Impact of Adjunctive Istradefylline on Dyskinesia Onset in Patients with Parkinson's Disease Exhibiting Wearing-Off: An Open-Label Randomized Controlled Trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12623527/
PMID: 40991184
PMCID: PMC12623527
DOI: 10.1007/s40120-025-00809-8
Publication date: 2025-09-24
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC article XML and included tables; supplementary files are separate.

ODYSSEI randomized 214 Parkinson participants with wearing-off and no previous dyskinesia to istradefylline plus adjusted medical therapy or adjusted therapy without the drug. The efficacy analysis had 103 and 107 participants. Dyskinesia arose in 39/103 versus 44/107. The median times were 1,100 and 1,082 days, with no clear group difference. Age subgroup directions differed and cannot substitute for the primary comparison.

The adverse-reaction table includes one nonspecific hallucination in the istradefylline arm and two visual hallucinations in the comparator arm. Its limb-pain row has one affected participant in the istradefylline arm and none in the comparator. These are adverse-reaction counts under different medication strategies. They are not visual hallucination episode frequency or pain severity. The drug trial did not test dried figs, dates or ground ginger.
