Title: Aromatherapy Massage Reduces Psychological Distress in Rheumatoid Arthritis
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1524904226001700
PMID: 42191515
DOI: 10.1016/j.pmn.2026.04.024
First published online: 2026-05-26
Coverage: Factual extract of the 2026 primary abstract and companion trial methods.

The authors identify NCT04549857 as the three-arm randomized trial and link it to the earlier pain and sleep report. Of 102 randomized people with rheumatoid arthritis, 95 completed follow-up. The active intervention used a topical almond-oil blend of cedarwood, sweet marjoram and sweet orange with self-massage. The other groups used almond-oil massage or usual care.
The authors report improved HADS anxiety and depression relative to usual care in the blended oil group. The direct aromatherapy versus almond-oil massage comparison does not show a clear difference. Almond-oil massage also favored anxiety compared with usual care. The complete 2026 adjusted effect estimates were unavailable. The multiple-oil topical comparison cannot establish an effect of ingested oregano in Parkinson disease.
