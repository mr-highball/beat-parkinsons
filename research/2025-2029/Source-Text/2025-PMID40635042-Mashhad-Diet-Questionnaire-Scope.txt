This PERSIAN Organizational Cohort Study in Mashhad analysis says trained nutritionists administered a validated 118-item semiquantitative food-frequency questionnaire covering the previous year. The paper calculates a composite Dietary Inflammatory Index and relates it to hematological markers in groups with and without depression. It does not publish the 118 individual items or a fruit-specific comparison. Its supplementary record is listed as a PNG. The item-level questionnaire and its version at baseline remain unverified.

The fitted comparisons detected no significant associations across the index tertiles in the depressed group. The abstract and Results give different group counts, so those denominators should be reconciled before reusing numeric subgroup results. The report gives no Parkinson pain effect or separate date-fruit estimate.

Mapped scope: Round 1 claim 0502, PMID:33138802. This is a later analysis of the same occupational cohort. Verify the exact baseline questionnaire and any date-palm food code before transferring this later analysis to the original protocol.
