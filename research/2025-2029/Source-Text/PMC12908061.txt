Title: Functional brain connectivity in patients with de novo Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12908061/
PMID: 41704899
PMCID: PMC12908061
DOI: 10.1016/j.ynirp.2026.100327
Publication date: 2026-02-09 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Untreated, recently diagnosed Parkinson participants and controls undergo resting fMRI for network connectivity assessment. Follow-up helps confirm diagnosis. Connectivity measures have no linked dried fig consumption or clinical visual episode count.
