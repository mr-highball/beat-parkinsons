Dark chocolate and measured anxiety after thyroid biopsy
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1155/ijcp/5687455
DOI: 10.1155/ijcp/5687455
Coverage: Original publisher full text and STAI Table 2

Yaşar randomized 99 patients scheduled for thyroid fine-needle biopsy. Fifty-two received about 20 g commercial 90% dark chocolate and 47 received no snack. The STAI mean changed from 39.79 to 34.31 with chocolate and from 38.60 to 38.89 with control. The article reports a group-by-time result p = .01 and a postbiopsy Cohen d of .48. The bar included vanilla flavoring. Patients and the radiologist knew group assignment, and the paper leaves the assessor masking unclear. This result concerns acute procedural anxiety outside Parkinson disease.
