Title: Timing of risk factors, prodromal features, and comorbidities of dementia from a large health claims case-control study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11736938/
PMID: 39819557
PMCID: PMC11736938
DOI: 10.1186/s13195-024-01662-x
Publication date: 2025-01-16 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

German outpatient claims identify 1,686,759 adults with incident dementia and 3,373,518 matched controls. Coded presentations over the preceding years measure diagnoses and signs, without specifying eaten figs or post-meal visual episode frequency.
