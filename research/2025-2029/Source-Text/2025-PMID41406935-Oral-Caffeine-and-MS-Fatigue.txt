Registration reported by the investigators: IRCT20241129063892N1.

The investigators report 60 adults with multiple sclerosis and fatigue in a double-blind comparison of isolated oral caffeine and placebo over 12 weeks. The MFI-20 primary fatigue outcome favors caffeine with a reported between-group difference of −8.55 points and p<.009. The accessible results excerpt reports 30 per arm and no participant dropout after randomization. The report names improvements in selected SF-36 domains. Exact arm means, confidence intervals, missing-data conventions and complete adverse-event tables were unavailable in the inspected body fragments.

A published letter and the investigators' reply concern the same trial. Only the identities and opening context could be verified. They supply no independent cohort. The article provides no trials of coffee or clinical Parkinson fatigue.
