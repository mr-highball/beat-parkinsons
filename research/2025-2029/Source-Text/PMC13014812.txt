Title: Autopsy-confirmed Lewy body dementia with additional progressive supranuclear palsy
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13014812/
PMID: 41699544
PMCID: PMC13014812
DOI: 10.1186/s12883-026-04704-z
First publication: 2026-02-17 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC original presentation, pathology and differential discussion

This autopsy case documents visual hallucinations and frequent falls in a person with dementia with Lewy bodies and additional progressive supranuclear palsy pathology. Resting tremor appears in the general description of Lewy body disorders. The case gives neither a dried fig-associated hallucination trajectory nor a raw fig-associated Parkinson rest tremor comparison.
