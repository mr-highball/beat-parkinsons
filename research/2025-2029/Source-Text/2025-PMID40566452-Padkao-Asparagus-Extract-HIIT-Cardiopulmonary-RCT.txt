Title: Effects of High-Intensity Intermittent Training Combined with Asparagus officinalis Extract Supplementation on Cardiovascular and Pulmonary Function Parameters in Obese and Overweight Individuals: A Randomized Control Trial.
PMID: 40566452
PMCID: PMC12194011
DOI: 10.3390/jfmk10020202
First published: 2025
Authors: Padkao T, Prasertsri P
Journal: Journal of functional morphology and kinesiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40566452/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week 4-arm randomized controlled trial (Padkao & Prasertsri, 2025, J Funct Morphol Kinesiol, PMC12194011) assigning 72 overweight/obese adults (n=18/group) to control, HIIT, Asparagus officinalis extract standardized to 20-hydroxyecdysone (1.71 mg/kg/day), or HIIT + extract. No significant between-group differences were observed in heart rate, resting blood pressure, or blood pressure variability; HRV and respiratory improvements occurred only when extract was combined with HIIT. Limits: non-PD overweight/obese adults, ecdysteroid extract rather than culinary asparagus, null BP/BP-variability findings, and no standing orthostatic challenge.
