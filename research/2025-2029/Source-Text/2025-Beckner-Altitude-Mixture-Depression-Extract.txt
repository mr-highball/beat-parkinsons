Depression scores in the final report of the altitude mixture trial
First published: 2025-09-10
DOI: 10.14814/phy2.70541
PMID: 40930836
PMCID: PMC12422811
Registration: NCT04111263
Source: https://physoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.14814/phy2.70541
Coverage: Published Methods 2.2, Results 3.1 and 3.3 and Figure 3d

The crossover exposed healthy adults to low-altitude placebo, high-altitude placebo and high-altitude fiber/polyphenol treatment. Each intervention bar contained cranberry extract alongside blueberry, cocoa, green tea and multiple fibers. Thirty-three volunteered, 26 completed at least one condition and 13 completed all three and entered the self-report analysis. POMS depression was higher in both high-altitude conditions than in low-altitude placebo, with no detected difference between the two high-altitude conditions. HADS depression showed no condition, time or interaction effect. The final comparison bears on the entire mixture. It does not identify a cranberry-only effect or test Parkinson disease.
