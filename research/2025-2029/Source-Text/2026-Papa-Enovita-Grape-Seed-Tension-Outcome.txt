Title: Papa grape seed extract pilot and the mood tension subscale
Source: https://cdn.fortunejournals.com/articles/exploring-the-neurocognitive-potential-of-a-sustainable-grape-seed-extract-results-fro-6740.pdf
DOI: 10.26502/jfsnr.2642-110000189
Publication date: 2026-04-30
Date basis: Publication date printed by the journal.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full publisher PDF, including Methods and visually checked Tables 2–3 on printed page 21.
Related registration: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06309914

This report analyzes the Enovita grape seed extract and placebo arms of a registered four-arm trial, 16 healthy adults in each arm. The other arms used ginkgo plus phosphatidylserine and bilberry, and have separate reports. The June Ginkgo report indexed as PMID 42294065 does not provide the grape-seed result.

In Table 2, four-week Profile of Mood States tension change was −0.06 (SD 0.94) in the grape seed extract arm and +1.12 (SD 0.37) in placebo. The published group-by-time p value was .549. The point estimates have a favorable descriptive direction and the authors label it as such. The comparison is inconclusive by the reported test and has no interval for the interaction. The study reports a different comparison for working-memory errors (p=.050 at four weeks), which is not evidence on tension or anxiety.

This is a standardized grape seed extract in healthy adults rather than raw grapes in Parkinson disease. The registry lists no posted results and the original paper supplies the arm-specific estimates. The paper and registration represent one family.
