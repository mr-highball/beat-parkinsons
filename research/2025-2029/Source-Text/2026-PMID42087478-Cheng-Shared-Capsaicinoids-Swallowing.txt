Title: Cortical stimulation with shared oral capsaicinoids and timed swallowing
Publication date: 2026-05-05
PMCID: PMC13358419
PMID: 42087478
DOI: 10.1111/joor.70214
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13358419/
Verified depth: original article including trial design, outcome results and author limitations
Checked: 2026-11-22

In a randomized crossover, 21 healthy adults received either active or sham cortical stimulation and then the same oral capsaicinoids. The active-versus-sham comparison favored greater volume per swallow (p=.003). Swallow duration and capacity did not show a clear between-condition difference. The design includes neither a capsaicinoid-free arm nor a Parkinson group. It cannot isolate a capsaicinoid effect or identify a clinical response to ground cayenne.
