Review depth: full_text

The review searches reports through August 2024 and tabulates adverse events after saffron preparations. The publication date is later than the search window. Table 1 led to the original three Alzheimer trial tables, which record fatigue as an event count in standardized extract arms against placebo, donepezil or memantine. These different active and inactive comparators remain separate. The table also points to a perimenopausal placebo trial, in which the original measures an energy/fatigue subscale as well as collecting adverse events. The event counts cannot be treated as changes in symptom severity.

The review lists insomnia in several adverse-event tables. Those populations and interventions do not establish insomnia caused by ground saffron in people with Parkinson disease. The current Round 1 depression, fatigue and insomnia assessments retain the existing clinically matched measures and the original inclusion and reporting limitations in their respective source records.
