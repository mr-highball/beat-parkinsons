Title: Prebiotics and SCFA Alleviate Parkinson’s-Related Constipation: Results from a Randomized Controlled Trial
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40120-026-00972-6
PMID: 42315784
PMCID: PMC13396302
DOI: 10.1007/s40120-026-00972-6
Publication date: 2026-06-18
Coverage: Paraphrased complete primary publication, Methods, Results, and Figures 1–3.

This is a secondary outcomes report on the same DRKS00027061 cohort as PMID 41350593. The paper reports 72 randomized, and the displayed groups contain 23, 24 and 24 participants. All three arms received an active regimen involving 2-fucosyllactose, short-chain fatty acids or both. The paper reports favorable within-arm constipation comparisons for the combination and 2-fucosyllactose alone. Its bowel-attempt count is separate from stool-passage frequency. The stated item list includes abdominal pain, but a separate abdominal-pain estimate was not reported alongside the summed constipation scores. The registry's two allocation descriptions and different final count remain unresolved. There was no separate placebo-only group, isolated fig exposure or drooling outcome.
