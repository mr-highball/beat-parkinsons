Title: Two traditional Apulian varieties of Cucumis melo L. exhibit superior sensory and digestive profiles over common cucumber: a population-based and clinical study
PMID: 41195070
PMCID: PMC12583024
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2025.1662952
Publication date: 2025-10-21
Citation: Khalil and colleagues. Frontiers in Nutrition 12, 1662952.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41195070/
Coverage: Author abstract, cohort description and original figure captions.

The 493-person survey records experiences with two local unripe melon landraces. A separate 15-person healthy-adult randomized crossover compared 80 g servings of each with cucumber. The trial reports differences in food reward and similar appetite and satiety measures. The main study addresses sensory and digestive effects of named unripe Apulian landraces. The survey and crossover answer separate questions.
