Olive oil advice in the modified Mediterranean diet protocol
Source: https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/bmjopen/15/10/e101946.full.pdf
PMID: 41047258
PMCID: PMC12506048
DOI: 10.1136/bmjopen-2025-101946
Registration: NCT06705517
Coverage: Original full protocol, dietary intervention and outcome sections

The first-public article date is October 5, 2025. The protocol assigns a six-month modified Mediterranean diet or usual diet. Its cooking advice specifies using olive oil in place of other cooking fats and including raw olive oil in dishes. The intervention text gives no grade, separate assigned oil dose or an oil-only arm. The proposed outcomes include a bowel symptom score within the broader dietary comparison. Trial registration identifies the same MED-PARK study.
