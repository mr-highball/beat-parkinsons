The review compares vesicle materials from blueberry, broccoli, carrot, ginger, orange and turmeric across separate experimental studies. Outcomes and preparations include material characterization, plant-derived fractions and preclinical responses. These do not identify the effects of eating the foods in their assigned preparations or a clinical Parkinson symptom estimate.
