Factual extract: Randomized open-label periodontitis study compared drinking-water control, 0.12% chlorhexidine and steam-distilled coriander SEED essential oil diluted at 4 mg/mL in water. Participants were instructed to shake the bottle, rinse 20 mL for 30 seconds twice daily for 14 days; habitual oral hygiene and diet were not standardized. Paired saliva microbiome samples were analyzed for 9 water, 8 seed-oil and 10 chlorhexidine participants. Species-level beta-diversity change versus water for seed oil had q=0.413. There is no reported salivary flow rate or clinical drooling measure. Oil mouthwash is not a dried cilantro leaf meal or a Parkinson exposure.
