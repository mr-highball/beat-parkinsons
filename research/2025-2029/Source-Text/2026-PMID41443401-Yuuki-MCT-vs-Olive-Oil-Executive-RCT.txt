Title: Both a single dose and a 4-week daily regimen of medium-chain triglycerides boost certain aspects of cognitive function in young adults: A randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 41443401
DOI: 10.1016/j.physbeh.2025.115212
First published: 2026
Authors: Yuuki I, Dora K, Matsumura K, Fujimoto Y, Hashimoto T
Journal: Physiology & Behavior
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41443401/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
RCT in 36 healthy young adults (21 +/- 1 y) comparing 12 g/d MCT vs long-chain triglyceride (olive oil) acutely (75 min) and over 4 weeks. MCT outperformed olive oil on acute inhibitory control (p < 0.05) and 4-week working memory (p = 0.04). No oil-free control, unspecified olive oil grade, young healthy adults.
