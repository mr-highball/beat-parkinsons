Dopaminergic polymorphisms and levodopa response in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12264220/
PMID: 40671874
PMCID: PMC12264220
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2025.100333
First publication: 2025-04-20 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC original Methods, participant data and levodopa challenge results

Ninety people with Parkinson disease entered this pharmacogenetic study. Comparisons use genotype, levodopa exposure and motor response to an acute levodopa challenge. Resting tremor appears in the clinical background and constipation appears in a discussion of possible influences on levodopa absorption. Shrimp alkaline phosphatase was used in a laboratory genotyping procedure, outside the patient's food exposure. No raw fig or boiled shrimp comparison was performed.
