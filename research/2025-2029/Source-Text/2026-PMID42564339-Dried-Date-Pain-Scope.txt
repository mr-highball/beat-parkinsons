The VHIP cohort protocol schedules broad Parkinson symptom scales and a 14-item Mediterranean diet adherence screener. The screener records aggregate fruit servings without a dried-date item. The original reports no food-specific pain result.

The PREDIMED investigators’ tools link the cited Schröder 14-item adherence score. It groups fruit servings. The VHIP protocol separately lists clinician-rated apathy with the Lille Apathy Rating Scale. That planned outcome is distinct from pain.
Research gap. Follow the public VHIP cohort reports for food-specific dietary fields and item-level pain observations. A Mediterranean adherence score alone cannot provide an attributable dried-date comparison. The available protocol gives no efficacy evidence for this pair.
