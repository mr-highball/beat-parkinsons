Title: Safety and efficacy of pimavanserin in patients with Lewy body dementia experiencing dementia-related psychosis in the HARMONY study.
PMID: 41286676
PMCID: PMC12642364
DOI: 10.1186/s12883-025-04496-8
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12642364/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2025-11-24
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch original article JATS, with complete body.

A post hoc HARMONY subgroup analysis evaluates continued pimavanserin versus randomized withdrawal in selected responders with Parkinson disease dementia or dementia with Lewy bodies. Relapse occurred in 1 of 19 continuing drug and 11 of 20 on placebo in the interim efficacy set. The endpoint combines hallucinations and delusions with other relapse criteria, and there is no food exposure. The subgroup is part of NCT03325556.
