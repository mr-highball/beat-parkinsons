Title: Phenotypic variability in early-onset dementia segregating with a novel APP (p.I718M) variant
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12670831/
PMID: 41327373
PMCID: PMC12670831
DOI: 10.1186/s13195-025-01890-9
Publication date: 2025-12-01 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

A family series describes five siblings with a new APP variant and case-level dementia phenotypes. The genotype and clinical trajectories concern inherited Alzheimer disease and contain no dietary fig exposure.
