Clove odor and Trail Making in a Beijing training protocol
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12352155/
Coverage: Factual summary of the primary report.

Published August 13, 2025. The NCT06821828 publication plans allocation of 114 older adults to modified training, conventional training or control. Four odors include clove; conventional training uses eugenol for that odor. Trial methods plan cognition, olfactory testing and imaging, and identify Trail Making for executive function. The article gives procedures without results. A wording error labels the control description as conventional training, while the design and flow separate the two. No ground clove, Parkinson cohort or isolated scent comparison was assigned.
