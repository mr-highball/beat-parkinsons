Title: Red rooibos tea mitigates recognition memory deficits in female rats fed a low vitamin D and calcium diet.
PMID: 42033351
DOI: 10.1139/apnm-2025-0399
First published: 2026
Authors: Bellaflor S, Mohammad A, Madanat S, Goncharow M, Feld R, Kays D, Ianiero M, Beaudette S
Journal: Applied physiology, nutrition, and metabolism = Physiologie appliquee, nutrition et metabolisme
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42033351/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Primary Abstract:
Low vitamin D status is associated with cognitive decline, while polyphenols may support brain health. This study investigated whether 3 months of supplementation with red rooibos (RR) tea, as a source of polyphenols, could mitigate recognition memory deficits in female Sprague-Dawley rats fed a low vitamin D and calcium diet. Results showed that RR tea attenuated memory impairments induced by this diet (p = 0.0034), suggesting its potential as a strategy to support recognition memory under conditions of low vitamin D and calcium status.

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
2026 preclinical rat study (Appl Physiol Nutr Metab 2026) showing 3 months of red rooibos tea mitigated object-recognition memory deficits induced by a low vitamin D and calcium diet in female Sprague-Dawley rats (p=0.0034). Nutrient-deficiency recognition-memory model; does not test PD or executive/attention impairment.
