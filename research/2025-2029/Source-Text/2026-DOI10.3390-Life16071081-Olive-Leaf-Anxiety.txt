Title: Effects of Olive Leaf Extract on Anxiety Symptoms and Metainflammation in Women with Excess Weight: A Randomized Double-Blind Placebo-Controlled Pilot Trial
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2075-1729/16/7/1081
PMID: 42514151
DOI: 10.3390/life16071081
Publication date: 2026-06-28
Accessed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: Distilled primary trial methods, patient flow, Table 1, Figure 3 and controlled inference.

Nineteen women with excess weight completed the three-month olive-leaf-extract pilot (nine extract and ten placebo). Symptoms improved on several within-arm measures in both groups. The authors report no significant effect of leaf extract compared with placebo and identify recruitment shortfall. Leaf extract differs from cold-pressed oil. No participant population with Parkinson disease was studied.

The article reports changes in Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale, Beck Anxiety Inventory and State-Trait Anxiety Inventory. Its within-arm measurements do not isolate an intervention effect. For Round 1 claim 0343 the preparation and clinical population differ.
