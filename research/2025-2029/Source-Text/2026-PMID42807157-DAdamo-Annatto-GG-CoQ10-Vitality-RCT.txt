Title: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial Comparing the Effects of Commercially Available Ubiquinone, Ubiquinol, and Geranylgeraniol Dietary Supplements on Plasma CoQ10 Concentrations.
PMID: 42807157
PMCID: PMC13617315
DOI: 10.1155/jnme/5589963
Registry ID: NCT06640465
First published: 2026
Authors: D'Adamo, Leary, Mote, Ackerman, Holthouse
Journal: Journal of nutrition and metabolism
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42807157/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

8-week double-blind 5-arm parallel RCT (NCT06640465) in 150 healthy adults (mean age 52.1 y) randomized to ubiquinone, ubiquinol, Annatto-GG 300 (annatto-derived geranylgeraniol), ubiquinol + GG, or placebo. GG alone did not raise plasma CoQ10 vs placebo (p >= 0.15). Secondary ANCOVA analyses showed no significant differences between any active arm (including Annatto-GG) and placebo on PROMIS Global Health (physical/mental/pain/fatigue), POMS-Vigor, or SF-36 Vitality (all p > 0.44 in results text; abstract p > 0.16). Adverse events were rare. Limits: isolated annatto diterpene in healthy non-PD adults without baseline fatigue/pain.
