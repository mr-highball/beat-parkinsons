Exposure and design
Twenty people with physician-confirmed Parkinson disease enrolled and 16 completed the 32-week pilot. The publication describes a fixed sequence without randomization. Phase I was the usual diet, Phase II the usual diet plus an organic MCT supplement containing C8 and C10, and Phase III a ketogenic diet plus the same specified MCT formulation. Each phase lasted eight weeks, with four-week washouts between phases. Participants chose their MCT products. The final report does not identify assigned virgin or refined coconut oil or show composition and outcomes by product.

Results
Table 2 reports mean (SD) for baseline, Phase I, Phase II and Phase III, respectively.
Parkinson Fatigue Scale: 45.3 (16.7), 43.2 (17.6), 42.1 (15.5), 44.9 (15.9). The Phase II mean is 1.1 points below Phase I.
Epworth Sleepiness Scale: 7.3 (5.0), 8.0 (5.9), 7.5 (5.0), 7.6 (4.9). The Phase II mean is 0.5 points below Phase I and 0.2 points above baseline.
The report found no significant adjusted or rank-based phase effect for these or the other four Parkinson scales. They all had p greater than 0.05. There is no standalone executive-function estimate. Global cognition and the Parkinson sleep scale address distinct measures from executive function and daytime sleepiness.
Eight of 16 completers volunteered for exit interviews. Some reported better energy or less fatigue, while motor and symptom experiences varied. These interviews and measured scales describe the same cohort.

Prior record and unresolved scope
The investigator, condition, three intervention patterns, duration of each phase and the washouts closely match the preliminary ISRCTN13221188 lineage. The registry describes a planned 36-week randomized sequence, whereas the final report describes 32 weeks and no randomization. The publication does not give the registration ID. Keep these as a likely shared family with the discrepancy recorded. For claims 0229, 0513 and 0598, the pilot gives clinical context without an attributable effect of the frozen oil grade.
