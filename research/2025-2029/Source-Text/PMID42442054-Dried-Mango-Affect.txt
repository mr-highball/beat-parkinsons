Dried mango offered during an acute affect experiment
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442054/
PMID: 42442054
DOI: 10.1016/j.eatbeh.2026.102111
Coverage: Original July 8, 2026, author abstract and author manuscript Methods sections 2.2.1–2.2.2

Gao and colleagues randomly assigned 88 university students to positive, negative or neutral videos and offered six optional snacks, including dried mango. Intake was analyzed as total snack calories. The Positive and Negative Affect Schedule measured short-term emotional states before and after viewing. The manuscript describes no subject-specific mango exposure or independent mango effect and does not measure depressive symptom severity.
