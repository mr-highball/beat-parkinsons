Review depth: Original author abstract. Complete methods and comparator results unavailable.

The experimental Parkinson research uses an engineered magnetoelectric oral delivery system with capsaicin as a targeting component. The abstract discusses potential motor and nonmotor anxiety effects of the system with external stimulation. It gives no tested dietary ground-cayenne comparator or independent anxiety effect attributable to capsaicin.
