Title: Topical tarragon oil extract and pain in knee osteoarthritis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13657636/
PMCID: PMC13657636
PMID: 42866378
DOI: 10.4103/ijnmr.ijnmr_327_25
Publication date: 2026-09-21
Coverage: Original journal Methods, Results and Table 2 plus trial registration.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

The original report identifies IRCT20211203053264N1 and describes 90 participants assigned to topical tarragon extract in sweet almond oil, topical sweet almond oil, or routine care. Immediately after treatment the WOMAC pain means (SD) were 10.13 (1.63), 13.73 (1.45) and 13.43 (1.54), respectively. Table 2 gives p=.001 for the immediate overall comparison and p=.376 and .858 for its week-one and week-three overall comparisons. The article's timing and treatment descriptions differ from some prospective registry fields.

The published trial reports block randomization, alternate-day topical application, and scent interfering with participant blinding. The registry describes a card draw, daily application, participant blinding and different enrollment chronology. The exact underlying thesis and a reconciliation of prespecified analysis remain unavailable. The trial measures short-term pain in knee osteoarthritis under a topical extract. It gives no isolated fresh-tarragon ingestion or Parkinson pain estimate.

Registration: https://en.irct.ir/trial/60390
