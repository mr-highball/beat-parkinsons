Title: A case of monotherapy with intracerebroventricular anaerobic dopamine allows complete control of Parkinson's disease symptoms

Single Parkinson case reporting less dystonia and dyskinesia during changes to dopaminergic treatment. Transient drowsiness was reported during a medication adjustment. The report distinguishes this adverse experience from a dedicated fatigue outcome. Neither fresh nor dried sage was evaluated. The case and the separate DIVE-I feasibility study must retain their distinct provenance.
