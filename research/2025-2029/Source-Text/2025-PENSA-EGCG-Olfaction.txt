Title: A multimodal lifestyle intervention complemented with epigallocatechin gallate to prevent cognitive decline in APOE-ε4 carriers with Subjective Cognitive Decline (PENSA study)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12413734/

First published: 2025-07-15
Text coverage: factual extraction from the original Methods and supplementary Table 6.

PENSA compared isolated oral EGCG capsules plus a multimodal lifestyle intervention with placebo plus the same intervention in older adults with subjective cognitive decline and APOE-ε4. This is NCT03978052. The exploratory olfaction measure was the University of Pennsylvania Smell Identification Test (UPSIT) at baseline and 12 months. Table 6 reports modified intention-to-treat group sizes of 51 and 50. These totals do not establish the number of complete UPSIT records.

Baseline UPSIT mean was 28.5 in each group. Adjusted change was −0.9 (95% CI −2.0 to +0.2) in the EGCG group and −0.9 (−2.1 to +0.3) in the placebo group. The adjusted between-group difference was +0.1 point (95% CI −1.3 to +1.5, p=.853). The interval spans both directions. This exploratory result concerns an isolated supplement and a different population from brewed green tea in Parkinson disease.
