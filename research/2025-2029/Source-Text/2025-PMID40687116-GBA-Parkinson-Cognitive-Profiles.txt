Title: Distinctive cognitive phenotypes in Parkinson's disease patients with GBA mutations and without dementia: a multicentre cross-sectional retrospective study.
PMID: 40687116
PMCID: PMC12274794
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2025.100365
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12274794/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2025-07-04
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch original article JATS, with complete body.

The retrospective multicenter comparison studies cognition in non-demented Parkinson participants with and without GBA variants. Hallucinations were among nonmotor characteristics assessed and appear in interpretation of phenotype differences. The comparisons are defined by genotype and cognition, without dried fig exposure or a food-associated change in visual episode frequency.
