The retrospective FOSAPO article studies people switched from apomorphine to foslevodopa and foscarbidopa. It records new apathy in two participants after the medication switch.

These apathy events provide drug-transition context. The cohort does not assign dried dates or distinguish any diet-attributable apathy effect.
