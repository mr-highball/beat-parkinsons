Title: Efficacy and safety of Nrf2 activators for schizophrenia: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
PMID: 42154264
DOI: 10.1007/s00406-026-02264-4
First published: 2026
Authors: Okazaki M, Sakuma K, Jo H, Nagata Y, Hamanaka S, Nishii Y
Journal: European archives of psychiatry and clinical neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42154264/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 16 Nrf2-activator RCTs (n=953) including 4 sulforaphane schizophrenia trials. Sulforaphane reduced all-cause discontinuation and improved lipid profile, with NO statistically significant pooled differences over placebo in PANSS overall, positive, negative, depressive, or cognitive outcomes. Context-only secondary synthesis.
