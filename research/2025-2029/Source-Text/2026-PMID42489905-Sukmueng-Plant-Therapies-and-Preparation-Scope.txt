Title: Plant-derived therapies in Parkinson’s disease: a systematic review of clinical evidence
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00702-026-03239-4
PMID: 42489905
DOI: 10.1007/s00702-026-03239-4
Publication date: 2026-07-23
Citation: Sukmueng N, Phokaewvarangkul O, Bhidayasiri R. Journal of Neural Transmission.
Review registration reported by authors: PROSPERO CRD420261276805
Text coverage: Factual digest of the complete author abstract and public publisher reference list. Individual study tables remain gated.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The authors report 23 included studies and six plant-derived therapy categories. They describe evidence grades as reflecting study hierarchy. The public abstract mentions motor, sleep and quality-of-life findings under the Apium graveolens category and motor-complication findings under Glycine max. The public bibliography contains the Zhou pharmaceutical DL-3-n-butylphthalide trial and the Nagashima ground soybean crossover with levodopa and carbidopa.

The Zhou original used a synthetic pharmaceutical and reports historical identification of the compound from celery seed. Its tremor and sleep table anomalies and retrospective enrollment reporting remain material limitations. The Nagashima original added ground soybeans to levodopa and carbidopa. An improvement with ground beans alongside medication cannot establish a refined soybean oil effect. The abstract does not expose the exact study-to-grade table, and the bibliography also contains contextual references. These cited original studies must remain separate from one another and should not be counted again through the review.
