Grid writing and upper limb freezing in a Parkinson case

Factual extract from official Europe PMC full article XML, case report and outcome discussion.

Gál and colleagues describe handwriting freezing in one person with diagnosed Parkinson disease. Grid paper immediately assisted writing and a home program combined writing and drawing practice. The report gives clinical observations of legibility and freezing, without a structured pre/post score. Rigidity is described at diagnosis and foot dystonia has a separate treatment history. Neither serves as a separate outcome of the writing intervention. The case adds upper-limb freezing and symptom context without a sage comparison.
