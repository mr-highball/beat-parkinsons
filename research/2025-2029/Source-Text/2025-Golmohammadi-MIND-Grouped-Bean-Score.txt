Grouped bean score in the MIND cardiometabolic companion
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12859884/
PMID: 41466280
PMCID: PMC12859884
DOI: 10.1186/s12937-025-01275-6
Coverage: original companion body, Table 2 and published Supplementary Material 1

First published December 29, 2025, with a January 30, 2026 version of record. The report uses the same 44 participants and registration IRCT20181111041611N8 as the sleep trial. Table 2 gives a grouped bean score changing from 0.23 to 0.70 in the MIND arm and 0.23 to 0.45 in control, with adjusted follow-up p=.003. The baseline difference is printed as zero with a confidence interval spanning zero, alongside p=.007. That discrepancy remains unresolved. The supplement groups beans, lentils, chickpeas and soybeans. Food diaries were collected and participant data are unavailable publicly. The report gives no pinto identity, preparation or ingredient-specific sleep response.
