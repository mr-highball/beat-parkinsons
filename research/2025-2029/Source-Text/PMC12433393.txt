Title: Electrophysiological Signatures of Visual Sensations Elicited by Direct Electrical Stimulation
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12433393/
PMID: 40699543
PMCID: PMC12433393
DOI: 10.1007/s12264-025-01464-7
Publication date: 2025-07-23 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML

The original study records intracranial EEG and elicited visual sensations in 69 people with medically refractory epilepsy selected for reporting an induced percept. The experimental comparison is stimulation-related physiology. It does not measure clinical Parkinson visual episodes or food exposure.

The inclusion criteria require a reported stimulation-elicited visual percept and concurrent recordings. Thirty-two participants had been reported in an earlier cortical distribution analysis, and 37 were recruited subsequently. Cross-study recurrence of those cases does not imply independent replication.
