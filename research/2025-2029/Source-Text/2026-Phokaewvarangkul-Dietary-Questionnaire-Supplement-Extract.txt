Coverage: factual extract of original supplemental survey sections 32, 33 and 40 and matching expert assessment table, prepared 2026-11-14

The bilingual questionnaire asks whether a respondent eats berries (32.1). It asks respondents who eat berries for the total cups consumed in the prior seven days (33.1). The berry questions do not name blackberry or record whether the fruit was fresh, frozen, thawed or processed. The separate frozen and ready-made meal question (40.2) asks for a count over the prior seven days. No question pairs an individual fruit with the frozen-food response. Both fields are also present in the expert item–objective congruence table. The published article reports 20/70 weekly consumers of berry fruit and 16/70 of frozen foods. These totals cannot be intersected to measure intake of frozen blackberry. The questionnaire does not elicit a voice-volume or speech item. The article reports motor severity aggregates and does not give a voice result paired with either food category.
