Wang and colleagues, original Figure 2 and supplementary analysis

Original source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12060789/

First published May 7, 2025. The Nurses’ Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-Up Study used repeated food questionnaires and later questionnaire-defined probable REM sleep behavior disorder. Higher cumulative ultraprocessed-food consumption was associated with increased odds of probable REM sleep behavior disorder. The analysis overlaps the cohorts used in the earlier Molsberry diet-quality paper. Different food class definitions and estimands preclude treating their results as repeated estimates of the same contrast. The reported analyses do not isolate raw carrot or count episodes.
