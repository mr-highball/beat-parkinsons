Title: Fermented foods and inflammation: a crossover intervention trial with fresh and pasteurized sauerkraut.
PMID: 42321414
DOI: 10.1038/s41430-026-01776-5
First published: 2026
Authors: Schropp N, Bauer A, Stanislas V, Michels KB
Journal: European journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42321414/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Companion report from the randomized crossover trial of 87 healthy German adults (same cohort as Schropp 2025 PMC11817299) consuming 100 g/day fresh vs pasteurized sauerkraut for 4 weeks each with 4-week washouts. Results: both fresh and pasteurized sauerkraut yielded a small reduction in resting systolic blood pressure (indicating live bacteria are unnecessary for modest SBP drop), NULL effect on circulating gut-barrier ('leaky gut') integrity markers, and no appreciable overall systemic inflammatory or glycemic benefit in healthy adults. No standing orthostatic blood pressure or PD symptoms measured.
