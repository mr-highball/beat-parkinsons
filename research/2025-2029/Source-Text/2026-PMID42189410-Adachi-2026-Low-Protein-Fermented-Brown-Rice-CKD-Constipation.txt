Title: Effects of processed low-protein brown rice on patients with chronic kidney disease.
PMID: 42189410
DOI: 10.1007/s10157-026-02877-y
First published: 2026
Authors: Adachi K, Yasuda M, Ida M, Oshima Y, Azegami T, Yoshifuji A
Journal: Clinical and experimental nephrology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42189410/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-month single-arm dietary intervention in n=30 enrolled (n=29 analyzed) stage 3-4 CKD outpatients consuming fermented low-protein brown rice (LPBR) as staple food. Significantly improved Constipation Scoring System (CSS) score (p=0.023), increased fecal acetic acid (p=0.024), and tended to raise Bifidobacterium longum (p=0.094). Limits: uncontrolled single-arm study, processed fermented low-protein brown rice in non-PD CKD adults.
