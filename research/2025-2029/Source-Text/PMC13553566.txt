Title: A systematic review of the clinical correlates of delusions in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13553566/
PMID: 42718908
PMCID: PMC13553566

The review includes seven eligible studies. It treats delusions, delusional misidentification and visual hallucinations as related but distinct outcomes. The source includes no dried fig intervention and supplies no fig-attributed recurrent visual episode counts.
