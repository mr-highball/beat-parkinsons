Apathy, amyloid burden and cognition in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13347539/
PMID: 40961243
PMCID: PMC13347539
DOI: 10.1177/1877718X251370969
First publication: 2025-09-17 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC original Methods, amyloid and cognitive results, and models

The observational study enrolled 48 people with Parkinson disease and classified baseline apathy with the Starkstein Apathy Scale. The apathetic group had greater regional amyloid burden and showed worse performance on several cognitive measures. The adjusted apathy-only model did not establish a significant independent prediction of cognitive conversion. No dietary exposure to dried sage or comparison on fall frequency is provided.
