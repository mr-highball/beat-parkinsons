Title: Exploring the mechanism of sunflower seed oil against Alzheimer's disease through experimental and network pharmacology studies
Publication date: 2025-02-19 (public publication; November–December 2024 issue designation)
PMID: 39973828
PMCID: PMC11913338
DOI: 10.4103/ijp.ijp_761_23
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11913338/
Text coverage: Distilled primary full text, including the Figure 1 comparisons and source description.

Investigators compared two oral doses of sunflower seed oil with water in a scopolamine amnesia model in 24 male Swiss albino mice, including a control without scopolamine. Both oil groups had better novel-object discrimination than the scopolamine water group (both p<.001, Figure 1a). Figure 1b does not establish a separate locomotor improvement versus scopolamine. The oil came from Sigma-Aldrich, without an identified pressing grade or measured batch composition. The compound list was assembled using databases, and docking predictions do not establish effects of compounds present in the tested oil. This is an isolated oil comparison for rodent recognition memory against a drug-induced insult. There was no Parkinson population, oil-only study in an unchallenged group, or separate executive task.
