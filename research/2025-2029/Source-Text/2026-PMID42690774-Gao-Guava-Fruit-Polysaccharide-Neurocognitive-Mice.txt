Title: Guava Polysaccharide Interventions Ameliorate Delayed Neurocognitive Recovery by Modulating the Gut Microbiota-Arachidonic Acid Metabolism Axis and Attenuating Abeta/Tau Pathology
PMID: 42690774
DOI: 10.1021/acs.jafc.5c17336
First published: 2026
Authors: Gao Q, Chen Y, Yu P, Zeng H, Nan L, Zhang D
Journal: Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42690774/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Preclinical mouse study evaluating a purified pectin polysaccharide (Mw 94.5 kDa) isolated from guava fruits in a post-anesthesia/surgery delayed neurocognitive recovery (dNCR) model. Reported improved cognitive performance, reduced hippocampal neuroinflammation/pTau/Abeta, and altered gut-microbiota SCFAs abolished by antibiotics. Purified isolated polysaccharide in a non-PD surgical mouse model; cannot establish whole raw guava fruit cognitive effects in PD (and human raw guava RCT Roy Choudhury 2021 was null on cognition).
