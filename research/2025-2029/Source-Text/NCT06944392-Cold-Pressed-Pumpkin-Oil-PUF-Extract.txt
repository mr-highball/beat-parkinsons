# Cold-pressed label for registered pumpkin seed oil bladder pilot

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06944392
Source coverage official registration and matching manufacturer label

NCT06944392 first posted April 25, 2025, following the recorded actual start of April 11. The one-arm Northwestern pilot enrolled 40 adults with overactive bladder, excludes neurogenic bladder, and pairs the LURN-SI with a planned Pelvic Pain and Urgency/Frequency questionnaire. It specifies one NOW Cucurbita pepo seed-oil softgel each day for 12 weeks and identifies the 1,000 mg preparation and 100-count bottle. The corresponding manufacturer product is described as cold pressed. The protocol lists actual completion September 1, 2026, with no results in the November 16 UTC retrieval. The design does not control changes against an oil-free arm and the pelvic symptom composite is broader than Parkinson pain severity.
