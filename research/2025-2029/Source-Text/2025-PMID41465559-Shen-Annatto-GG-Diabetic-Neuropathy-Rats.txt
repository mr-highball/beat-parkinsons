Title: Dietary Geranylgeraniol Mitigates Pain-Associated Behaviors via Improving Mitochondrial Function and Colon Integrity and Suppressing Neuroinflammation in Male Diabetic Neuropathy Rats.
PMID: 41465559
PMCID: PMC12733174
DOI: 10.3390/ijms262412133
First published: 2025
Authors: Shen, Liu, Cao, Neugebauer, Miranda
Journal: International journal of molecular sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41465559/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

8-week controlled study in male Sprague-Dawley rats (30 analyzed: 11 control, 9 HFD+STZ diabetic neuropathy [DNP], 10 DNP + 800 mg/kg dietary geranylgeraniol gifted by American River LLC [annatto-derived GG]). Dietary GG reduced von Frey mechanosensitive pain by 35% vs untreated DNP (p < 0.05) and significantly increased elevated plus maze open-arm entries and open-arm duration (p < 0.05), alongside improved colonic tight-junction claudin-3, mitochondrial markers, and reduced TNF-alpha/GFAP neuroinflammation. Limits: isolated annatto diterpene in diabetic neuropathy rats.
