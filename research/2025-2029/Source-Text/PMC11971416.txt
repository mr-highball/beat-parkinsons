Title: Spatial and functional profiles distinguish target sets of Parkinson's disease and antipsychotic drugs with different clinical effects
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11971416/
PMID: 40185727
PMCID: PMC11971416
DOI: 10.1038/s41398-025-03351-1
Publication date: 2025-04-04 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Transcriptomic maps and network methods compare molecular targets of Parkinson and antipsychotic drugs grouped by clinical effects. The analysis projects drug targets and pathways rather than measuring food exposure or hallucination episodes in a clinical study.
