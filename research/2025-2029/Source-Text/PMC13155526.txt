Scoping review of striatal hand deformity, related diagnoses and available treatment reports in Parkinson disease. Reported hand pain and function outcomes trace to clinical case series and movement-disorder treatments, some of which reuse the same patients. The review is secondary evidence. It supplies neither a dried-date pain comparison nor an isolated raw-fig rest-tremor result.
