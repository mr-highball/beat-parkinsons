Title: Ajwa dates, cognition and GDNF in risperidone-treated schizophrenia
Source: https://repository.unhas.ac.id/id/eprint/51501/
Publication date: 2025-12-15
Date basis: First verified public university deposit date.
Coverage: Public author abstract and repository file availability. The full thesis is restricted to repository staff until March 1, 2027.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

The university record for Djoeddawi's thesis describes 44 participants assigned to risperidone alone or risperidone with whole Ajwa dates. The reported outcomes are Indonesian Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA-Ina) scores and serum glial cell line-derived neurotrophic factor. The abstract reports between-group differences for changes in the cognitive total at weeks four and eight. At week eight the attention and memory domains differed, with no clear differences for the other domains. The abstract supplies no Parkinson apathy scale or separate apathy result. No quantitative domain differences or confidence intervals are given in the public summary.

The 44 participants and arm sizes match the Rismayanti report's design. Full study dates, recruitment records and participant flow are needed to verify whether these reports share a cohort. Count this as an uncertain family and retain the stated access restriction in future searches.
