Publication status: Retracted

The publication reports a randomized comparison of an omega-3 supplement and a corn-oil control in adults with psychological distress. The editor-in-chief retracted it in July 2026 following material errors and discrepancies in its data and analyses. The original estimates are excluded from efficacy assessment. The official registration remains historical protocol context with unposted results.
