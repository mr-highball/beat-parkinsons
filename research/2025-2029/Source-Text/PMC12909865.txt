Title: Early retinal synaptic dysfunction and proteomic remodeling precede neurodegeneration in a Parkinson's disease model
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12909865/
PMID: 41540050
PMCID: PMC12909865

In a preclinical Parkinson model, retinal measurements span synaptic transmission, inflammation, proteomics and structural changes. Visual hallucinations appear in the rationale for studying vision. The study measures retinal physiology and does not include a human visual endpoint or dried fig exposure.
