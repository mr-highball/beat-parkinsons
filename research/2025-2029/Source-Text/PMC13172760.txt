Title: Clinical Outcome Assessments in Parkinson's Disease: A Scoping Review of Current Rating Scales and Future Needs
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13172760/
PMID: 41159296
PMCID: PMC13172760
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70410
Publication date: 2025-10-29 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The Movement Disorder Society scoping review surveys outcome assessment instruments across 19 Parkinson constructs and 504 included studies. It discusses measurement and validation context, with no primary exposure or food-attributed episode-frequency measurement.
