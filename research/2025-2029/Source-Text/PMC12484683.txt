Title: Thalamic nuclei volume changes associated with cognitive and motor manifestations of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12484683/
PMID: 41028722
PMCID: PMC12484683
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-01129-2
Publication date: 2025-09-30 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

In 165 Parkinson participants and 82 controls from PPMI, the study relates MRI thalamic subregion volumes to motor and cognitive performance. No individual dried fig exposure or visual recurrence comparison is reported. PPMI is shared with other imaging studies.
