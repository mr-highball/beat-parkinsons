Peanut butter and mobility tests in older adults at fall risk

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12867520/
Coverage: Original intervention and outcome tables
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

Published in February 2026. One hundred twenty older adults at fall risk were randomized to 43 g/day peanut butter or usual care for six months. The adjusted five-times sit-to-stand estimate was −1.23 s (95% CI −2.09 to −0.37), while gait speed and most other function endpoints showed no clear contrast. SARC-F includes a history-of-falls item. The publication provides no verified prospective fall-frequency comparison. Whole peanut butter, measured physical function and the non-Parkinson cohort leave refined oil and Parkinson falls unresolved. Registration ACTRN12622001291774.
