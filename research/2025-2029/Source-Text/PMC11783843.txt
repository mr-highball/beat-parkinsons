Title: Hallucinations and disturbed behaviour in the critically ill: incidence, patient characteristics, associations, trajectory, and outcomes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11783843/
PMID: 39891214
PMCID: PMC11783843
DOI: 10.1186/s13054-025-05290-1
Publication date: 2025-01-31 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete primary PMC JATS

In 7,525 critically ill adults, clinical note screening identified 625 with hallucinations. Among those 625, 281 had more than one documented episode of any modality. Median time until the first episode resolved was 15 hours. Medication associations use a nested cohort. The design does not measure dried figs, Parkinson-specific episodes or a food-timed effect.

Conference lineage: The ESICM LIVES 2025 supplement, abstract 000132, reports the same participant total, hallucination total and clinical setting. The study's patient counts, episode duration and nested medication analysis are separate outcomes. The published methods rely on clinical notes. An independent symptom-interview protocol was not used.
