Title: Fructanase during shared oatmeal challenge and next-night sleep
First published or posted: 2026-08-11
PMID: 42580594
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnut.2026.101782
Source URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022316626004311/pdf
Text type: original-source evidence summary

Mah and colleagues report the NCT06628869 crossover with 30 healthy adults. Both periods provided 40 g oatmeal and 25 g inulin. The randomized difference was fructanase versus placebo. The secondary single-item sleep-quality difference was 0.0 (95% CI −1.0 to 0.0, P=.91). Good or excellent sleep was reported in 23 of 30 enzyme periods and 25 of 29 placebo periods, with one questionnaire missing. This estimates the enzyme contrast under the shared meal challenge.
