2025 MDS International Congress, Yu et al.
https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/the-role-of-diet-in-the-relationship-between-hyposmia-and-constipation-in-patients-with-parkinsons-disease/

Yu and colleagues reported an observational study of 173 people with Parkinson disease. At baseline 172 completed Sniffin’ Sticks testing. In baseline multiple regression, a higher smell test total was associated with a lower constipation score (coefficient −0.333, SE 0.123, p=.007). The investigators collected food-frequency questionnaires and described diet mediation as a future analysis. The abstract gives no measured food or preparation effect on olfactory function. It cites the Canadian gut-microbiome study as the parent cohort.
