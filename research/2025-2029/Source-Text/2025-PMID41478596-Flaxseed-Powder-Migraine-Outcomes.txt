Title: Randomized flaxseed powder trial in people with migraine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41478596/
PMID: 41478596
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2025.101312
Publication date: 2025
Date basis: First online publication in 2025.
Coverage: Original author abstract. Complete anxiety table unavailable through the publisher routes checked on November 26, 2026.

Sixty-eight adults with migraine were randomized to flaxseed powder or roasted wheat powder for eight weeks. Mean headache severity changes were −5.0 and −1.0, respectively, with reported p<.001. Anxiety was listed among the secondary outcomes. The original abstract did not provide an anxiety contrast. The preparation was whole-seed powder and the endpoint was migraine headache severity. This result does not establish a separate cold-pressed flaxseed oil response or a Parkinson pain effect.
