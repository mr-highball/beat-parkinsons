Title: Comparing self and informant perspectives on neuropsychiatric symptoms in older adults in a memory clinic
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13687618/
PMID: 41395817
PMCID: PMC13687618
DOI: 10.1177/13872877251401620
Publication date: 2025-12-15
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC article XML and included tables; supplementary files are separate.

A memory clinic study compared self and informant Mild Behavioral Impairment Checklist ratings. There were 127 clinical participant–informant dyads, split between subjective cognitive decline and mild cognitive impairment, and 26 cognitively healthy controls. The clinical group definition excluded Parkinson disease among neurological causes that could affect cognition or neuropsychiatric symptoms. The results concern informant reliability and neuropsychiatric symptom measurement in a different population. No date fruit or sage preparation was examined.
