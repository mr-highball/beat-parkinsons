Title: Parkinson nutritional support and curcumin mixture, posted fall-endpoint scope
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02445651?tab=results
Registry API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02445651
Registry: NCT02445651
Publication date: 2025-12-15
Date basis: First public summary results in the registry, separate from initial registration.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Posted participant flow, outcome tables and adverse-event reporting fields.

The registry reports 28 participants in the N-acetylcysteine group, 14 in the control group and nine in the oral nutritional mixture group. The mixture included curcumin, omega-3, baicalin and Ganoderma. The flow table states that the protocol amendment dropped the oral mixture arm. The outcome tables cover a distribution-volume ratio and baseline clinical variables. They provide no fall frequency or fall timing comparison.

The registry reports no participants affected by serious or other adverse events in its three aggregate columns, with no event-specific rows. An aggregate safety total without a separate falls ascertainment description cannot establish the clinical fall count. The mixture and protocol amendment further limit attribution to curcumin.
