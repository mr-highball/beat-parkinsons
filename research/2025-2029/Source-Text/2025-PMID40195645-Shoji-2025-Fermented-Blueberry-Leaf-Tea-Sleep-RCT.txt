Title: Effects of dietary fermented blueberry leaf tea on sleep: a randomized double-blind controlled trial.
PMID: 40195645
DOI: 10.1039/d4fo05341a
First published: 2025
Authors: Shoji K, Kitajima Y, Yuasa T, Hirakawa Y
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40195645/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-week double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in n=50 healthy adults testing 200 mL x 3/d fermented blueberry LEAF tea (1.8 mg hyperoside/serving) vs placebo tea. Reported higher actigraphic sleep efficiency at 3 weeks, lower WASO at 2 weeks, and subjective sleep onset/maintenance improvement. Limits: fermented blueberry LEAF tea (hyperoside-standardized different plant part and preparation) rather than raw or frozen blueberry fruit; non-PD healthy adults.
