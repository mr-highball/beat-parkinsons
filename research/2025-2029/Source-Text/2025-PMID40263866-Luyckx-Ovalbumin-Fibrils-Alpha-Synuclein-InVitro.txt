Title: Impact of amyloid-like ovalbumin fibril consumption on health-related markers: An in vitro approach.
PMID: 40263866
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2025.116288
First published: 2025
Authors: Luyckx T, Grootaert C, Delcour JA, Housmans JAJ, Rousseau F, Schymkowitz J
Journal: Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40263866/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

In vitro cell-culture and simulated-digestion study examining engineered amyloid-like fibrils (ALFs) formed from egg white ovalbumin on intestinal/neuronal cell lines (Caco-2, SH-SY5Y) and alpha-synuclein-overexpressing cells. Results: ovalbumin ALFs caused no direct SH-SY5Y cytotoxicity and demonstrated ZERO heterologous cross-seeding of intracellular phosphorylated alpha-synuclein aggregation (only homologous alpha-synuclein seeded aggregates). Supports lack of prion-like cross-seeding risk; laboratory-engineered fibrils in vitro are not clinical symptom evidence.
