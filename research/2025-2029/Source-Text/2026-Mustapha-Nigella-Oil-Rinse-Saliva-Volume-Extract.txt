2026 | Mustapha et al. | Cold-pressed Nigella oil rinse and saliva volume

Source: original full report, PMID 42592226 and DOI 10.7759/cureus.112590, published July 13, 2026.

Ten healthy adults rinsed with saline followed by pure cold-pressed commercial Nigella sativa oil conditions. The order was fixed. Participants were instructed to spit out the mixture and avoid ingestion. The investigators report the measured saliva volume after expectoration. The published methods do not specify how much residual rinse fluid was recovered or how it was excluded from the volume value. No pre-rinse sample was collected.

At one minute, oil and saline means were 4.89 and 1.43 mL, adjusted p<.0001. At five minutes they were 8.39 and 6.88 mL. At ten minutes they were 9.69 and 11.40 mL. No clear difference was found at five or ten minutes. Salivary pH did not clearly differ by rinse treatment. Each collection combined the 5 mL rinse and saliva. The report gives no volume procedure or retained-rinse adjustment. The recorded volume leaves saliva production and clinical drooling unmeasured.
