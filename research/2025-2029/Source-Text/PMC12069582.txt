Title: Cortical microstructural abnormalities in dementia with Lewy bodies and their associations with Alzheimer's disease copathologies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12069582/
PMID: 40355490
PMCID: PMC12069582
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-00944-x
Publication date: 2025-05-12 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The MRI and biomarker comparison includes 57 participants in a Lewy body dementia spectrum clinical group and examines cortical microstructure and co-pathologies. Imaging abnormalities are separate from dietary fig exposure and clinical visual episode frequency.
