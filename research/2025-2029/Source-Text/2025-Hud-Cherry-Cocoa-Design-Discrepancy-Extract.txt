Cherry cocoa report with unresolved study design
Publication: 2025-11-15
Source: https://jurnal.stikes-ibnusina.ac.id/index.php/an-Najat/article/download/3456/3480/17157
Coverage: Athirah Khanayah Hud and colleagues, An-Najat 3(4), 186–202. Publisher posting November 15, 2025. Original publisher text, Methods pages 190–191 and Table 3.
DOI: 10.59841/an-najat.v3i4.3456

The abstract describes 30 young athletes in a 14-day randomized comparison of a tart cherry and cocoa drink. The Methods instead specify laboratory measurements and a sensory panel of 20, and give no athlete allocation, intervention or placebo description. Table 3 gives perceived exertion means of 8.5 before and 5.2 after without identifying arms. The fruit extract appears in a cocoa and milk mixture. No authenticated correction or exact trial registration was found in the routes checked.
