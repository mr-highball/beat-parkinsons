Original study scope
The review includes a saffron hydroalcoholic extract and endurance-training experiment in rats. It separately discusses an oligonol mixture from lychee and green tea and isolated EGCG in disease or mechanistic models.

Continuing evidence gaps
Mitochondrial outcomes and training synergy do not supply clinical insomnia or fatigue estimates for saffron. The extracts and blends give no brewed-green-tea fatigue effect.
