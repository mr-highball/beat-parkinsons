Title: Curcuma longa or its active ingredients for osteoarthritis, Cochrane synthesis
PMID: 43051256
PMCID: PMC13781522
DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD014683.pub2
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43051256/
Publisher summary: https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD014683_what-are-benefits-and-risks-turmeric-curcuma-longa-supplements-people-osteoarthritis
Published: 2026-11-19.
Coverage: Original structured abstract and Cochrane results summary. The PMC record has a published availability date of 2027-11-19. The original institute aggregate supplement is available separately.

The synthesis included nine screened randomized trials with 1,245 people with knee osteoarthritis. The search ended November 7, 2025. In four placebo-controlled trials reporting pain (349 participants), the pooled change favored oral turmeric formulations by 0.72 points on a 0–10 scale at up to three months (95% CI 0.06 to 1.39 points lower pain). The authors judged this low-certainty evidence and used 1.5 points as the threshold for clinical importance. The result and its interval are below that threshold. Function and quality-of-life intervals cross zero. Adverse-event conclusions have very low certainty.

The interventions were extracts and curcuminoid formulations in knee osteoarthritis, with study durations at most 90 days. They do not give a culinary ground-turmeric exposure or a Parkinson pain effect estimate. The institute supplement contains the Thanawala 2025 row with matching arm sizes in an 11-study publication-bias dataset. The final abstract specifies a four-study placebo-pain estimate.
