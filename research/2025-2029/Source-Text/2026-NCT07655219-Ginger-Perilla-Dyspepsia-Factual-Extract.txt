Ginger and perilla registered for functional dyspepsia
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07655219
Registration: NCT07655219
Coverage: Official registry protocol fields

First posted June 17, 2026. The protocol compares a standardized ginger plus Perilla frutescens formulation, alone or with a proton-pump inhibitor, with full-dose proton-pump inhibitor. The standalone condition field reads functional dysphonia, while the official title, intervention descriptions and registered outcomes concern functional dyspepsia and gastrointestinal symptoms. No voice outcome or posted results appear in the accessible record. The protocol does not specify the Perilla plant part, a dried culinary leaf preparation or an isolated Perilla comparison.
