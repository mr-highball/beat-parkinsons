The report describes mice assigned to sham surgery, asymmetric carotid stenosis with vehicle, or stenosis with oral ethanolic Prunus cerasoides extract or isolated prunetin. Behavioral outcomes included passive avoidance, elevated plus maze and open field measures. The report separately discusses survival, white-matter injury, barrier leakage and Akt-associated changes. An oxygen-glucose-deprivation neuron experiment tested Akt-associated effects and an inhibitor. That cell experiment is separate from the oral mouse comparison.

The authors report improvements in behavioral performance and several tissue outcomes in the induced vascular dementia model. The inspected material supplies no Parkinson cohort, apathy measure or dietary dried cherry comparison. Behavioral measures of learning, exploration and anxiety cannot stand in for apathy. The result cannot be carried from a medicinal extract of this species to the frozen culinary exposure.

Study family: Brain & Brain PET abstract 0634 describes an overlapping author team, oral extract and prunetin groups, the carotid stenosis model and similar behavioral outcomes. Treat the two records as likely related reports and refrain from counting an independent replication unless cohort provenance is resolved. The separate stroke-model proceedings member supplies a different design.
