Title: Daily consumption of polyphenol-rich grape powder improves muscle strength markers in postmenopausal women.
PMID: 40407719
DOI: 10.1039/d5fo01523e
First published: 2025
Authors: Mendez JL, Rodriguez Lanzi C, Quan Z, Chen S, Krga I, Mackenzie GG
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40407719/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Pilot double-blind parallel dietary intervention RCT in 15 healthy postmenopausal women consuming freeze-dried whole table grape powder vs matched control powder daily for 6 weeks. Women consuming grape powder significantly increased hand grip strength and completed the gait speed test significantly faster vs control in age-adjusted models, with non-significant trend for plasma irisin (+14.4% p=0.07 vs -7.8% p=0.08). Limits: very small pilot (n=15), non-PD postmenopausal sample, directly balanced on PD walking slowness by null direct PD RCT Oliveira 2020 (PMID:32798568, n=19 analyzed, 6MWT p=0.11, TUG p=0.28).
