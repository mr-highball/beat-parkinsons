Title: Efficacy and Safety of IncobotulinumtoxinA for Treatment of Sialorrhea: A Multicenter, Phase 3 Study in Japan

This open-label phase 3 study enrolled patients with chronic sialorrhea from different neurological conditions. Fifty-eight received the study treatment in the primary evaluation group and 34 in a broader safety group. The primary group covered Parkinson disease, atypical parkinsonism and stroke. The endpoint was the within-group change in unstimulated salivary flow at four weeks. The adjusted change was −0.08 g/min (95% CI −0.10 to −0.06), evaluated against a prespecified threshold rather than a concurrent control. The study also used patient-reported drooling and swallowing instruments.

The changes concern medical treatment. Salivary flow and patient-rated drooling describe different aspects of the symptom. The paper provides no raw-fig or dried-date intervention. The study record at research/2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/jRCT2051210082.txt is part of the same trial family.
