# Isolated thymoquinone and movement outcomes in a mouse study

Source https://narraj.org/main/article/download/2439/870/18034
Coverage original motor methods and figures

Kusumawati and colleagues compared several preparations in groups of three rotenone-exposed mice. The single-agent oral thymoquinone arm at the highest of the tested single-agent doses improved open-field distance and mean velocity against rotenone control (p < .05 for each). The corresponding beam and hanging-wire comparisons were inconclusive. Active constituent, small groups and exploratory movement measures limit transfer to ground seed in Parkinson bradykinesia.
