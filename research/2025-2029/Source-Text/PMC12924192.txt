Title: Factors associated with epileptiform discharges in patients visiting a memory clinic: A retrospective study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12924192/
PMID: 41727509
PMCID: PMC12924192
DOI: 10.1016/j.ebr.2026.100853
Publication date: 2026-02-04 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The memory-clinic chart review compares patients by epileptiform activity on EEG. The measured outcome is electrical discharge status and its associated clinical characteristics. The design supplies no fig ingestion or Parkinson visual episode frequency.
