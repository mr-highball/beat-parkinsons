The Taiwan insurance cohort compares acupuncture use and hospital admission for accidental injury in Parkinson disease. Its primary matched analysis reports adjusted hazard ratio 0.67 (95% CI 0.42 to 0.85) for injury admission. Falls contribute to the association, and residual confounding remains possible. The intervention is acupuncture, and the study does not compare dried date fruit or a pain-severity endpoint.
