Title: Effects of 24-Week Almond Supplementation on Executive Function and Processing Speed in Middle-Aged Asian Indians with Prediabetes: An Open-Label Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 41850683
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2026.101484
Registry ID: NCT05322304
First published: 2026
Authors: Gulati, Misra, Tiwari, Pandey, Sharma
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41850683/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

24-week open-label parallel RCT (NCT05322304; New Delhi) in 60 middle-aged Asian Indians (40-60 y) with prediabetes randomized to daily almonds providing 20% of daily energy with diet/exercise (n=29) or isocaloric diet/exercise without almonds (n=31). Assessed on CANTAB at baseline and 24 weeks, the almond group showed significant improvements in executive function (One Touch Stockings of Cambridge: beta = -2.5, 95% CI -4.4 to -0.6, P = 0.01) and processing speed (reaction time: beta = 73.8, 95% CI 25.7 to 122.0, P = 0.003; beta = 39.3, 95% CI 9.4 to 69.1, P = 0.011), alongside reductions in weight, BMI, waist circumference, glucose, HbA1c, and TBARS (all P < 0.05). Limits: open-label design, concurrent weight/glycemic improvements, prediabetes non-PD cohort, abstract does not explicitly specify raw vs roasted state.
