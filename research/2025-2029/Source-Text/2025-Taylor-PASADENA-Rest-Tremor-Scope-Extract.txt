Rest tremor measured in the PASADENA digital analysis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12170883/
PMID: 40523921
PMCID: PMC12170883
DOI: 10.1038/s41746-025-01572-8
Registration: NCT03100149
Coverage: Official June 2025 original Methods and exploratory results tables verified

The PASADENA digital analysis includes 316 participants with early Parkinson disease assigned to prasinezumab or placebo in the first year. It measures rest tremor with a seated phone-in-hand sensor feature. The reported week-52 contrasts give p = .50 for the more affected side and p = .32 for the less affected side. The exploratory early-versus-delayed-start comparison at week 104 gives −0.172 (SE .087, p = .05) on the more affected side. The second year uses active drug in both groups. The study tracks digital motor features and does not give fall frequency or fatigue outcomes or assign food.
