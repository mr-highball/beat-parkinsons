The report uses the CIRCUS feeding crossover for validation of a metabolite score. It identifies the registration and 39 participants with prehypertension. The score distinguished two-week mixed fruit-and-vegetable diet periods in the reported analysis (p < 0.00006). In another cohort, the authors examine associations with long-term health outcomes. Those observational associations and the CIRCUS validation are separate analyses.

This abstract gives a result from the trial family without reporting its planned cognitive tasks. Carrots were included within more than one mixed-produce condition in the protocol. No independent roasted-carrot estimate or Parkinson executive-function result is reported here.
