Rosemary mood review and uncertainty in pooled estimates
Public publisher abstract verified 2026-11-16; December 2026 issue; Crossref record created October 28
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S221080332600062X

Aucoin and colleagues report 14 studies and 865 participants. Nine studies used inhalation and five used oral preparations. The abstract reports inconclusive pooled depression, anxiety and stress estimates and high heterogeneity. The planned oral subgroup anxiety comparison favored rosemary, with a standardized mean difference of −0.61 (95% CI −0.99 to −0.23). Anxiety is a separate endpoint. The full study table, depression forest plot and preparation-specific estimates remain open. This secondary synthesis is distinct from the original oral trials.
