Title: The effect of Rosmarinus officinalis on depression, anxiety, and psychological stress in adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S221080332600062X
DOI: 10.1016/j.hermed.2026.101147
Date basis: December 2026 issue. Crossref created the record on October 28.
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled publisher abstract and bibliographic metadata, with an earlier separate poster in the same family.

Aucoin and colleagues report 14 studies and 865 participants. Nine studies used inhalation and five used oral preparations. The abstract reports inconclusive pooled depression, anxiety and stress estimates and high heterogeneity. The planned oral subgroup anxiety comparison favored rosemary, with a standardized mean difference of −0.61 (95% CI −0.99 to −0.23). Anxiety is a separate endpoint. The earlier poster in this review family provides its own five-study depression forest plot. The final journal forest plot, study table and preparation-specific depression estimates remain open. The review summarizes the original oral trials. Count the trial and review evidence separately without treating reuse as independent replication.
