Title: The Persistence of Dopamine Dysregulation Syndrome after Pallidal Deep Brain Stimulation in Parkinson's Disease.
PMID: 40346925
PMCID: PMC12481421
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70114
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12481421/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2025-05-10
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch original article JATS, with complete body.

The retrospective case series follows Parkinson dopamine dysregulation around pallidal stimulation. Five of six patients had preoperative psychosis, most commonly visual hallucinations described in the context of high dopaminergic doses. The recorded clinical and drug course is a separate exposure and provides no dried fig comparison.
