Title: Does Consuming Fresh Ultraviolet Light-Exposed Mushrooms Offset the Seasonal Decline in Serum Total 25OHD in Adults Classified as Overweight and Class I Obese? Results from a Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 42121515
PMCID: PMC13163831
DOI: 10.3390/foods15091572
Registry ID: NCT05559112
First published: 2026
Authors: Comboni LM, Glover ES, Napolitano SC, Fleet JC, Foti D, Olson MR
Journal: Foods (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13163831/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Companion report to Glover 2025 (NCT05559112, n=41 adults eating 168 g/d fresh UV-exposed cremini A. bisporus vs breadcrumbs for 6 wk in winter). Raised 25(OH)D2 but accelerated 25(OH)D3 decline with no net prevention of winter total 25(OH)D decline; only 67% of sampled UV mushrooms contained measurable D2.
