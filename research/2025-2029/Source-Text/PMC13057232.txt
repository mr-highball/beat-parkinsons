Title: Machine learning integration of serial blood biomarkers enhances cognitive decline prediction in early Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13057232/
PMID: 41741476
PMCID: PMC13057232
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-026-01298-8
Publication date: 2026-02-25 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The early Parkinson prediction analysis uses clinical measures and a limited series of repeated blood biomarkers to predict cognitive decline over five years. Cognition and model performance are distinct from a clinical food exposure and visual symptom assessment.
