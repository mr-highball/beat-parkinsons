Title: Comparative Effects of Durian and Banana Consumption on Thermic Effect of Food and Metabolic Responses in Healthy Adults.
PMID: 39531559
DOI: 10.1080/27697061.2024.2426563
First published: 2025
Authors: Ling CY, Yeo MTY, Kang Y, Ng SM, Bi X, Henry CJ
Journal: Journal of the American Nutrition Association
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39531559/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized crossover trial in n=16 healthy young Chinese adults (8 M, 8 F) ingesting isocaloric 367.5 kcal portions of durian vs banana (5-d washout) with 3-h postprandial monitoring. Durian acutely lowered BP and raised triglycerides, whereas banana had no significant acute effect on postprandial BP or triglycerides.
