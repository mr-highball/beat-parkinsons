Title: Strawberry dietary intervention influences diversity and increases abundances of SCFA-producing bacteria in healthy elderly people
Author: Meiners and colleagues
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11792484/
PMID: 39772703
PMCID: PMC11792484
DOI: 10.1128/spectrum.01913-24
Publication date: 2025-01-08
Date basis: Electronic publication. February 2025 issue.
Text coverage: Distilled from the original paper, especially the cohort description, arms, and microbiome results.

This is a microbiome analysis within the ErdBEHR randomized study (DRKS00026998). It analyzes 69 older adults. The five groups received fresh strawberries at different amounts, with freeze-dried strawberries or capers in olive oil added to selected arms. Stool sequencing was used to characterize bacterial diversity and relative abundances. The high fresh-and-freeze-dried group without capers showed a weighted UniFrac diversity difference (p = 0.0035) and increases in some bacteria associated with short-chain fatty acid production. These are microbial outcomes from the same trial family as the 2024 clinical-marker and chair-rise paper. The report includes no Parkinson-specific or falls comparison.
