Wang and colleagues, primary indexed source

Original source https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41174460/

First online August 23, 2025 according to the indexed original. The food engineering study tested ion-dependent formation of protein fibrils from black kidney bean and prepared textured hydrogels. The resulting materials were described in IDDSI texture levels 4–5. The hydrogel involves isolated protein, not a serving of boiled whole beans. The paper's abstract does not report a participant feeding, a swallowing examination or a Parkinson sample. The full original remains a depth gap.
