Original factual extract of primary text and Supplementary Table 1

This Parkinson cohort was nested within NCT05902065, a comparison of conventional versus augmented treadmill rehabilitation. The authors analyzed paired serum samples from 30 participants over eight weeks. They estimated the partial correlations of Raman measures of circulating particles and gait, with age and disease duration as covariates. Table 1 of the supplement labels the two baseline carotenoid-associated signals as AUC 1154 T0 and AUC 1514 T0, given wavenumbers in cm⁻¹.

Baseline maximum step length: signal correlations −.49 and −.49, each p = .01.
Post-rehabilitation maximum step length: −.42 and −.41, each p = .03.
Baseline self-selected step length: −.34 (p = .09) and −.37 (p = .05).
Post-rehabilitation self-selected step length: −.41 (p = .04) and −.43 (p = .03).
Baseline maximum gait speed: −.50 and −.47, each p = .01.
Post-rehabilitation maximum gait speed: −.49 (p = .01) and −.41 (p = .04).

Supplementary Figure 5 compares the spectral regions with lutein and β-carotene standards. The reported signals do not give an individual chemical lycopene concentration. The research report does not give dietary intake, tomato preparation or same-foot stride-length effects. These correlations describe a biomarker in a rehabilitation study.
