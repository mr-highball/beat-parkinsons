Title: Nurse interviews about topical rosemary care
Source: https://doi.org/10.1186/s12906-026-05423-1

A qualitative study interviewed 49 intensive-care nurses about implementing topical herbal care. Its protocol table lists rosemary essential oil as a 10% body oil or bath milk for tiredness and circulation, while the delirium entry concerns a different plant, Oxalis acetosella. The study measured nurse perceptions, not patient clinical effects, and gives no Parkinson fresh-leaf or hallucination-frequency comparison.
