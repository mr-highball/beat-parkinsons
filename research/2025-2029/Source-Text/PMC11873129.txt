Title: Epigenetic insights into neuropsychiatric and cognitive symptoms in Parkinson's disease: A DNA co-methylation network analysis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11873129/
PMID: 40025048
PMCID: PMC11873129
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-00877-5
Publication date: 2025-03-02 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The original labels hallucination presence or absence from antemortem clinical notes and examines methylation networks in postmortem Parkinson brain tissue. It relates biology to recorded symptom occurrence without dried fig exposure or counts of recurrent visual episodes.
