Title: Date palm fruit and premenstrual symptoms over five menstrual cycles
Source: https://www.benthamscience.com/article/145562
DOI: 10.2174/0115734013339565241107050018
Publication date: 2025-01-08
Citation: Al-Sayyed et al. Current Nutrition & Food Science 21(5), 618-627.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: Published author abstract. Full groups and outcome tables are restricted.

The abstract describes a convenience sample of 36 participants, 20 in the date-fruit group and 16 controls. It reports timing changes in mood and bleeding measures and a relationship between abdominal-cramp onset and prolactin. Its conclusion includes improvement of cramps. The abstract gives no between-arm cramp severity estimate or dispersion and leaves the date processing, cultivar and masking details unverified. A hormone correlation and a symptom-onset result do not establish a change in pain severity. Keep the full-text preparation and symptom tables open. This is a premenstrual study with no identified Parkinson comparison.
