Corn oil and constipation in the STRENGTH subgroup analysis
https://www.atherosclerosis-journal.com/action/showPdf?pii=S0021915025011268
selected methods and findings paraphrase

First published May 27, 2025. Constipation was reported in 14 of 657 Asian and 36 of 5,878 non-Asian adults receiving high cardiovascular risk corn-oil comparator capsules, versus 24 of 697 and 32 of 5,835 in the respective omega-3 arms. Subgroup p values were .14 and .65. Corn-oil dose was 4 g daily, with grade unverified. This post-hoc secondary analysis shares the original STRENGTH trial participants, has no oil-free comparator and gives no Parkinson constipation severity estimate.
