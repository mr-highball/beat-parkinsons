Title: Parkinsonism secondary to cobra bite
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13071304/
PMID: 40750440
PMCID: PMC13071304
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70276
First publication: 2025-08-01 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC public full HTML, clinical findings, management and discussion

The clinicians report secondary parkinsonism following a cobra bite with basal ganglia abnormalities. The management and course concern envenomation and dopaminergic treatment. The discussion uses the absence of retro-orbital pain when considering a different explanation for vision changes. No dried date fruit exposure or reproducible pain or apathy comparison follows from this case.
