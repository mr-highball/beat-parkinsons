Title: Combined botanical extract and smell after acute rhinosinusitis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40899893/
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Dobretsov and colleagues. Vestnik Otorinolaringologii, 2025, 90(4):64–71. Original indexed English abstract only.
Seventy adults aged 18 to 65 with acute sinusitis were evaluated. The product combined elderberry, primrose, hops and thyme extracts with vitamin C, zinc and dihydroquercetin. The abstract reports an olfactory index 0.2 higher on the product and no participants with persistent smell loss at three weeks, compared with 8.5% in controls. Allocation, arm counts, significance for these comparisons, and the full measures require the original report.
The acute sinusitis mechanism, combined product and unspecified thyme processing limit use for a fresh thyme hypothesis in Parkinson disease.
