Title: Intraspecific Variability in the Gray Oyster Culinary-Medicinal Mushroom Pleurotus ostreatus (Agaricomycetes): Glucan, Ergothioneine, and Lovastatin Profiles with Biological Implications.
PMID: 42962183
DOI: 10.1615/IntJMedMushrooms.2026066509
First published: 2026
Authors: Kuru B, Atli B, Soylu S
Journal: International Journal of Medicinal Mushrooms
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42962183/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Analytical profiling of 115 wild isolates and 5 commercial strains of Pleurotus ostreatus quantifying beta-glucan (up to 61.53 g/100 g dw), lovastatin (up to 1.51 mg/g dw), and ergothioneine (up to 10.73 mg/g dw), demonstrating high ergothioneine potential alongside substantial strain-to-strain variability.
