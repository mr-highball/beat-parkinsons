Original study scope
The review discusses culinary saffron, traditional uses and non-Parkinson mood comparisons. Its Parkinson passages address constituent models, preclinical movement or cognitive endpoints and disease mechanisms. Nutmeg appears in a separate ingredient context and turmeric in combinations. Orange and shrimp are unrelated to the claimed fruit or constipation effects.

Continuing evidence gaps
Clinical outcomes of feeding standard-ground saffron, separately assessed falls and transfer from non-Parkinson extract trials remain open.
