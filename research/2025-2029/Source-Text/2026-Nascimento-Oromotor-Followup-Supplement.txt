Title: Oromotor disorders in Parkinson's disease (Nascimento et al., 2026)
Source: https://asha.figshare.com/articles/dataset/Oromotor_disorders_in_Parkinson_s_disease_Nascimento_et_al_2026_/32764596
DOI: 10.23641/asha.32764596
Version DOI: 10.23641/asha.32764596.v1
Publication date: 2026-06-24
Date basis: First public ASHA Figshare posting.
Coverage: Both original one-page tables, S1 and S2, verified visually against the repository files. The tables belong to PMID 42340753.

S1 separates the six participants receiving speech-language pathology during follow-up from the 67 others. Mean saliva and drooling scores moved from 2.83 to 3.50 and from 1.15 to 1.25, respectively, with within-group p=.180 and p=.437. The groups differed at baseline. The table does not report a randomized or between-group treatment comparison.

S2 separates five people who underwent deep brain stimulation from the 68 others. Mean drooling scores were 1.80 to 1.60 and 1.25 to 1.43, with within-group p=.564 and p=.185. Speech and clinical swallowing scores are separate rows. The subgroup results belong to the same 73 participants as the main report. There is no food exposure or fig estimate.
