Cited ginger trial in the neurodegenerative constipation review
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42459041/
PMID: 42459041
DOI: 10.1177/01939459261451721
Coverage: Author abstract and publisher-deposited Crossref references; original inclusion table pending

Park and colleagues first published this scoping review online July 16, 2026. The abstract counts 13 reports across multiple sclerosis, Parkinson disease and dementia, with one ginger intervention. It gives no identity or separate outcome for ginger. The [publisher-deposited bibliography](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1177%2F01939459261451721) includes DOI 10.1186/s12906-023-04227-x, an earlier randomized trial of ginger tablets in relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis. A reference entry establishes citation. The full review inclusion table remains needed to determine whether this was the counted ginger report. The separate Parkinson ginger registration NCT07882108 first posted in October 2026 after the review search and has no posted results.
