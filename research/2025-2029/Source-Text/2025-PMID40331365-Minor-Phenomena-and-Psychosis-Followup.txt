Title: Long-Term Outcomes in a Parkinson's Disease Minor Phenomena Cohort.
PMID: 40331365
PMCID: PMC12528953
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70121
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12528953/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2025-05-07
Coverage: Official NIH PMC complete article page, with methods and results.

The prospective cohort followed people with baseline minor phenomena, including illusions, feelings of presence and passage hallucinations. Of 38 recruited, 34 entered the primary analysis and 24 developed psychosis during follow-up. Time to onset on the MDS-UPDRS and enhanced SAPS-PD is a composite incident outcome, with no dried fig intake measurement or food-specific visual episode rate.
