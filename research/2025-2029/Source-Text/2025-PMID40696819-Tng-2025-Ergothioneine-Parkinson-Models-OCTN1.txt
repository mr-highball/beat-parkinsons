Title: Ergothioneine Treatment Ameliorates the Pathological Phenotypes of Parkinson's Disease Models.
PMID: 40696819
PMCID: PMC12284465
DOI: 10.1111/jnc.70168
First published: 2025
Authors: Tng TJW, Leow DMK, Goh G, Wang Z, Liu Y, Tang RMY
Journal: Journal of neurochemistry
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12284465/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Multi-model Parkinson study showing oral L-ergothioneine improved climbing scores and preserved dopaminergic neurons/mitochondria in parkin and LRRK2 mutant Drosophila, rescued 6-OHDA-induced dopaminergic loss and apomorphine rotations in wild-type mice (abolished in OCTN1-knockout mice), and protected human LRRK2-G2019S iPSC dopaminergic neurons from rotenone. Limits: isolated ergothioneine in preclinical PD models (rotations/fly climbing rather than specific human PD motor symptom).
