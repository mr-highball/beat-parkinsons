Abdominal pain in the existing GGE03 dyspepsia trial
Source: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2025/fo/d5fo01172h
PMID: 40878144
DOI: 10.1039/d5fo01172h
Registration: CTRI/2020/04/024448
Coverage: Original first published August 12, 2025, Methods and Table 2

Baek and colleagues randomized 40 participants per arm to steamed ginger extract GGE03 or placebo for functional dyspepsia. GSRS abdominal pain changed by −5.0 (SD 4.4) with extract versus −1.2 (3.2) with placebo (p<.001). SF-36 bodily pain changed +30.8 (36.9) versus +10.2 (27.1), p=.006, where higher scores indicate better function. The article identifies CTRI/2020/04/024448 and April 3, 2020 registration. This is the same trial as the catalog's constipation report. The intervention uses processed extract, with no Parkinson cohort.
