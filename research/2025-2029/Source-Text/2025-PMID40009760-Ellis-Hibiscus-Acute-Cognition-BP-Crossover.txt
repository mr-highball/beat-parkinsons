Title: Acute effects of Hibiscus sabdariffa on blood pressure and cognitive function
PMID: 40009760
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2025.2462944
First published: 2025
Authors: Ellis LR, Aragón-Rojas S, Dye L, Boesch C
Journal: Nutritional Neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40009760/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Pilot randomized, placebo-controlled acute crossover trial in 20 overweight adults (mean age 35 +/- 2.8 years, BMI 30 +/- 1.2 kg/m2) consuming a hibiscus-containing beverage vs inert placebo alongside a high-carbohydrate breakfast over 2 hours. Hibiscus significantly lowered postprandial glucose at 30 and 45 min with NO acute blood-pressure change. Cognitively, hibiscus attenuated decline in delayed word recall (episodic memory) and decreased retroactive interference, while executive function and pattern separation showed only non-significant trends (P=0.063), and attention was not specifically improved. Small pilot, young overweight non-PD adults, acute 2-hour window.
