Title: Remote caffeine reduction and reported caffeine-related problems
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41864111/
PMID: 41864111
PMCID: PMC13220181
DOI: 10.1016/j.drugalcdep.2026.113123
Publication date: 2026-03-12
Date basis: Electronic publication through NLM. Print issue June 2026.
Review depth: Original author abstract and repository release metadata. NLM lists PMC availability for 2027-03-12.

The open-label waitlist comparison evaluated a remote caffeine-reduction manual in 109 healthy adults seeking to reduce use, 53 immediate and 56 delayed. The authors report lower total caffeine consumption after treatment. They also report improvements on named sleep, anxiety, reflux and diarrhea scales. The public abstract does not provide a stand-alone fatigue measure or effect size.

The design, investigators, enrollment and endpoints align with NCT04560595. Preserve the registry and article in a provisional single family while verifying direct registration in the full paper. Neither the available abstract nor the registry reports an isolated oral brewed-coffee or brewed-green-tea Parkinson fatigue outcome.
