Review depth: Publisher Section 3.3 and reference 88, checked against the cited original abstract and its behavior figure.

The review attributes a dyskinesia improvement to a mouse experiment
using isolated 6-shogaol. Its reference 88 identifies PMID 40063144.
The original behavior figure and caption instead document rotarod
retention, pole movement times and immobility tasks. Those measured
endpoints supply no abnormal involuntary movement score or
levodopa-induced dyskinesia comparison.

The review and its cited paper are a single citation trail. The
evidence rule for Round 1 claim 0271 records no transferable effect
on ground ginger and clinical dyskinesia. The reported improvement in
mouse motor performance and immobility remains indexed at its own
measured outcome and preparation.
