Safflower oil in a placebo capsule with planned stool measures
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06834984
Registration: NCT06834984
Coverage: Official API snapshot, comparator formulation and bowel outcome definitions

Posted February 19, 2025. The active intervention is a synbiotic. High-oleic safflower oil appears as one placebo capsule constituent with excipients. The protocol plans daily bowel counts, a Bristol stool diary and gastrointestinal symptom scores. The September 2026 record lists completed status and no results posted. A treatment contrast would compare the synbiotic with a multicomponent comparator, without isolating safflower oil or Parkinson constipation.
