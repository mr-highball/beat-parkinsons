The review's Daikenchuto constipation citation reuses the published 2004 report of six Parkinson participants with constipation. The preparation uses processed ginger, ginseng and Japanese pepper. The original subgroup and the review constitute one evidence family.

The Rikkunshi-to entry cites a 20-person open Parkinson gastric-emptying pilot (PMID 24457529). It describes orange peel and jujube in a multiherb formula. Jujube belongs to Ziziphus and differs from Phoenix dactylifera. The entry gives no raw-orange constipation or dried date pain contrast. STW5 is labeled with cumin in the review's footnote. The recorded formula's caraway component is Carum carvi and has a distinct botanical identity from Cuminum cyminum. The PASS-GI drug and its adverse-event mentions belong to the separate DA-9701 formula without ginger. None provides isolated ground ginger and dyskinesia data.
