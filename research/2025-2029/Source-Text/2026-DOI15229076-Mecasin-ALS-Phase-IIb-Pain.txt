Title: Shin et al. Mecasin phase IIb ALS trial and pain VAS outcomes

Review depth: Complete published trial. Methods, formulation Table 1 and outcomes Table 4.

Fifty adults with ALS were randomized. Forty-three contributed to the efficacy analysis (20 Mecasin, 23 placebo). Both groups received riluzole. At 24 weeks, all five regional pain VAS point estimates were higher for the multicomponent formulation, with all 90% confidence intervals including zero. The shoulder estimate was +1.05 (90% CI −0.25 to 2.35, reported one-sided p=.0905). The low-back estimate was +0.15 (90% CI −1.02 to 1.32, p=.4146). The authors used one-sided efficacy tests. The prespecified total functional score comparison was inconclusive. Exploratory respiratory findings answer a different question from pain.

Table 1 identifies Curcuma longa as a component of the extracted nine-herb product. There is no turmeric-only arm, ground culinary preparation, or Parkinson pain comparison. KCT0003792 is independent of the phase IIa KCT0001984 trial. Similarities to RISS T16964903 and differing participant counts leave the thesis overlap unresolved. Round 1 claim 0812.
