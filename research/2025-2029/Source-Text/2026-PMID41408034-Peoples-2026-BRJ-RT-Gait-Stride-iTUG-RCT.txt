Title: Beyond strength: dietary nitrate augments self-selected functional mobility after resistance training in middle-aged adults.
PMID: 41408034
PMCID: PMC13601425
DOI: 10.1007/s11357-025-02048-z
First published: 2026
Authors: Peoples BM, McIntosh MC, Harrison KD, Smith BR, Guzman KGS, Campos-Vargas SE, et al.
Journal: GeroScience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41408034/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-week double-blind, placebo-controlled parallel RCT (PMC13601425) in n=28 inactive middle-aged/older adults (mean age 56+-7 y; 14 BRJ+RT vs 14 nitrate-depleted PLA+RT, 7M/7F per arm): primary self-selected gait speed significantly improved with BRJ vs PLA (ANCOVA F(1,24)=7.92, p=0.010, partial eta^2=0.25; BRJ 1.30+-0.13 to 1.39+-0.12 m/s, d=-0.98). FDR-corrected secondary outcomes significantly favored BRJ for stride length (F(1,24)=8.10, q<0.001, partial eta^2=0.25), shorter step time (F(1,24)=4.55, q<0.05), and faster instrumented Timed Up and Go (iTUG: F(1,23)=5.41, q<0.05; 9.4+-1.8 to 8.2+-1.8 s). Fast-paced gait and i6MWT were null (q>0.10); resting systolic/diastolic BP did not differ (p=0.25/0.88). Limits: non-PD adults, concentrated BRJ combined with resistance training.
