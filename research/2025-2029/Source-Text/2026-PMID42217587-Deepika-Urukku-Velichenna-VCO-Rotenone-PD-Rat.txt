Title: Neuroprotective effect of Urukku Velichenna (Traditionally processed virgin coconut oil) in Parkinson's disease: Bridging ethnopharmacology and experimental validation using in vitro, in vivo, and in silico models.
PMID: 42217587
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2026.121933
First published: 2026
Authors: Deepika NP, Sharma R, Banerjee S, Verma T, Prajapati PK, Muhasina KM, Parihar VS, Kondengadan SM
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42217587/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Ethnopharmacological and experimental study (Deepika et al., 2026, J Ethnopharmacol) evaluating Urukku Velichenna, a Kerala village traditional hot-processed virgin coconut oil (HPVCO), in rotenone-treated SH-SY5Y cells (50 ug/mL) and male Wistar rats (rotenone 2.5 mg/kg i.p. x 10 days followed by oral HPVCO 1,306.4 mg/kg/day for 21 days). Abstract reports 26 GC-MS bioactive phytoconstituents and neuroprotective improvements in dopamine, ROS/SOD, IL-6/IL-1beta/TNF-alpha/nitrite, histopathology, and unspecified behavioral assays on days 0/7/14/21. Limits: animal/cellular model only, abstract omits specific motor task names, group sample sizes, numeric means, and p-values; tests traditional hot-processed VCO rather than commercial refined oil and supplies no clinical human Parkinson symptom contrast.
