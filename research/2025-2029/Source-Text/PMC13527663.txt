Title: The Influence of Motor and Nonmotor Factors on Ambulatory Activity in People With Parkinson Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13527663/
PMID: 42360793
PMCID: PMC13527663
DOI: 10.1097/NPT.0000000000000573
Publication date: 2026-06-26
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC XML article body and embedded tables; separate supplements are outside this extract.

Baseline analysis of 198 participants enrolled in the CYCLE-II exercise trial (NCT04000360). The researchers measured daily steps over seven days and related levels to motor examination under different medication states and to nonmotor symptoms. The clinicians assessed individual MDS-UPDRS Part I items, including apathy. This observational comparison addresses contributors to real-world activity. It provides no dried-date comparison, food-associated severity estimate or treatment effect of the randomized exercise program at baseline.
