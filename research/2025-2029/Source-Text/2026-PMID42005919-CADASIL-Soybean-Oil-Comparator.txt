The randomized CADASIL trial compares a tocotrienol complex against vitamin-E-stripped soybean oil capsules. The target is clinical worsening in small-vessel disease and the primary analysis does not establish a between-group benefit. The source does not isolate a refined culinary soybean-oil effect versus no oil or report a Parkinson walking comparison.

November 26 primary scope update

The CADASIL randomized trial used vitamin-E-stripped soybean oil as the placebo comparator. Dysphagia appears among reported fatal or withdrawal events without an attributable soybean-oil or Parkinson swallowing outcome.
