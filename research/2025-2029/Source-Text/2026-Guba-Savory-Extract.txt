Original 2026 Guba, Azerbaijan ethnobotanical survey, Table 1. PMCID PMC13666791.
The primary field survey published September 30, 2026 identifies Satureja hortensis in Table 1 (voucher AZE-IJ-113, local name Çöl nanəsi). It records oral decoction for gastrointestinal and urogenital complaints among the regional uses. Freshness, individual intake and measured speech results were unreported.
