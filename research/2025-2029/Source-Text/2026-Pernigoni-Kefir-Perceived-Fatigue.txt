Kefir milk and water after exercise in young athletes
Source: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2026/fo/d5fo05127d/unauth
PMID: 41800708
DOI: 10.1039/D5FO05127D
Coverage: Original journal and PubMed author abstract

First published March 3, 2026. Sixteen young female athletes, mean age 14.2, completed high-intensity cycling followed by kefir, milk or water in a crossover. Perceived fatigue and soreness decreased during recovery with both water and kefir (reported within-condition p≤.024), while milk within-condition p≥.075. The authors judged perceptual recovery at least as favorable with water and attributed modest differences to subjective variability. The accessible abstract does not supply the precise kefir-versus-water fatigue estimate or kefir flavor. Full text route returned the publisher landing page. This is acute exercise recovery, distinct from persistent Parkinson fatigue.
