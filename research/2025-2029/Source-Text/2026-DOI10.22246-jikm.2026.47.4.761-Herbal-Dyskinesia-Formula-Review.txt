Title: Ingredient and outcome scope of six Chinese herbal formula trials for levodopa-induced dyskinesia
Source: https://www.jikm.or.kr/journal/view.php?doi=10.22246/jikm.2026.47.4.761
DOI: 10.22246/jikm.2026.47.4.761
Publication date: 2026-09-30
Date basis: Journal citation metadata and Crossref publication record.
Source depth: Published systematic review body, included-trial comparison table and complete formula composition table.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25

The review identified six randomized trials of multi-ingredient formulas used with conventional treatment for levodopa-induced dyskinesia. They were Chen 2011, Li 2011, Wei 2014, Chen 2015, Zhang 2024 and Deng 2025. The outcome table reports improvements on one or more dyskinesia measures in each trial. Between-study differences include UPDRS Part IV items, abnormal movement scores, the Unified Dyskinesia Rating Scale, durations and medication comparators. The review highlights limitations in sample size and methods.

The formula table was checked ingredient by ingredient. It lists no Zingiber officinale, ginger rhizome, fresh ginger or dried ginger in the six base formulas or in the listed symptom-based additions. Alpinia oxyphylla fruit and Curcuma root in selected additions are different botanicals and preparations. These reports provide human dyskinesia context but do not count as ginger comparisons. The present review records the published synthesis and does not count the trial families as new findings.
