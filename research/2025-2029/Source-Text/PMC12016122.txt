Title: Cerebral microbleeds: prevalence and relationship to clinical features in cognitive impairment with lewy body disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12016122/
PMID: 40269723
PMCID: PMC12016122
DOI: 10.1186/s12883-025-04181-w
Publication date: 2025-04-23 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Among 929 mixed Lewy body and Parkinson cognitive syndrome patients, the crude odds ratio linking microbleeds with visual hallucination presence was 1.264 with a confidence interval crossing one. The fully adjusted OR was 1.597 with 95% CI 1.014–2.517. This is diagnostic-cohort symptom presence, with no dietary exposure or recurrence measure.

Table 3 shows that the crude visual presence comparison and the adjusted model differ. The baseline descriptive comparison also did not establish a statistically clear difference. Clinical adjudication tracks symptom presence. Individual food intake was not included.
