Title: The effect of date seed powder supplementation on anxiety- and depression-like behaviours, sleep quality and tryptophan-kynurenine metabolism in patients with type 2 diabetes: targeting gut-brain axis.
PMID: 41157892
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114525105424
First published: 2025
Authors: Momeniyan Z, Dehghan P, Tutunchi H, Azadi H, Azizi-Soleiman F
Journal: Br J Nutr
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41157892/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Triple-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial (Momeniyan, Dehghan et al., 2025, Br J Nutr, n=43 adults with type 2 diabetes) testing 5 g/day roasted date seed (pit) powder versus placebo for 8 weeks. Date seed powder significantly improved DASS depression, anxiety, and stress scores, sleep quality, quality of life, and lowered cortisol and kynurenine-to-tryptophan ratio. Critical exposure limit: tests date seed/pit powder—a distinct inedible plant part with a different polyphenol/fiber profile—rather than whole dried Phoenix dactylifera fruit flesh.
