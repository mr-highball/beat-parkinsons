Coverage: selected public registry fields in an original summary.

Registration date: 2025-04-14
Registration timing: retrospective
Enrollment date displayed: 2024-12-05
Target population and size: 100 adults aged 20 to 60
Design: Five blinded parallel arms compare topical tarragon extract, jujube extract, the combined extracts, lidocaine and vehicle before inferior alveolar nerve block injection.
Outcomes and status: Primary measure was injection pain on a visual analogue scale three minutes after injection. The registry lists complete recruitment. Ethics reference IR.MAZUMS.REC.1403.278. No result was present in the record reviewed.
