Asdaq et al. Potential neuroprotective and antidepressant effects of Ajwa date extract in an animal model of chronic mild stress. Scientific Reports 15:39641, 2025-11-12.
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-23235-0
PMID: 41224947
PMCID: PMC12612045
Primary source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-23235-0
Coverage: factual digest of full article, particularly Figures 2 and 4.

Male mice under chronic mild stress received Ajwa fruit-pulp extract, seed preparation, a drug comparison or vehicle, with separate doses and a nonstressed control. The stressed vehicle group had substantially lower sucrose preference after stress. Both pulp doses had higher measured sucrose preference than stressed vehicle in the Results (p<.001), while the figure legend prints a p<.01 threshold for the corresponding stars. Movement also changed. The behavioral assays measure preference, movement and immobility in a stress model. The actual dietary exposure and clinical apathy endpoint of the frozen claim were not tested.
