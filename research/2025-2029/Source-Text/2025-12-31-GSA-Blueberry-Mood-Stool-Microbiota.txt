Blueberry powder conference report on stool microbiota and mood
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12760583/
PMCID: PMC12760583
DOI: 10.1093/geroni/igaf122.2436
Registration: NCT05735587
Coverage: Original published conference abstract, parent and nested registrations linked through later paper

The Millar conference report describes a 12-week randomized comparison of freeze-dried blueberry powder and placebo in sedentary older adults with mild depressive symptoms. Twenty-two completed, and ten blueberry and eight placebo participants supplied stool for microbiome analysis. The report describes no clear within-group change in inflammation, motivation, steps or microbial diversity. It reports an exploratory change in selected microbial enzymes and preliminary mood results. Stool analysis did not provide stool frequency or constipation severity. The later paper identifies the microbiome substudy NCT05817383 and parent NCT05735587; these reports should not be counted as independent trials.
