Title: Gastrointestinal Manifestations in Parkinson's Disease Using a Validated Arabic Version of Gastrointestinal Dysfunction Scale: A Multicenter Study

The investigators assessed 162 people with Parkinson disease and 165 matched controls. The Arabic upper gastrointestinal subscore groups sialorrhea with the other upper symptoms and correlates with separate MDS-UPDRS salivation and chewing/swallowing items. This is validation and cross-sectional characterization. The article provides no response to fig ingestion or causal comparison of salivary measures.
