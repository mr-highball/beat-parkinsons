Paprika oleoresin as a colorant in a clinical sleep trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13357646/
PMID: 41698831
PMCID: PMC13357646
DOI: 10.1111/jsr.70284
Registration: NCT05344170
Coverage: Original full Methods and outcomes

Lavender and colleagues first published February 16, 2026. Twenty participants with insomnia completed a randomized three-period crossover of two cannabinol conditions and matched placebo. The liquid preparation lists paprika oleoresin as a colorant and the paper describes placebo as excipient only. Its polysomnography found inconclusive wake after sleep onset after either cannabinol dose versus placebo. Some secondary sleep measures favored the higher cannabinol arm. The Methods print concentrations inconsistent by a factor of ten with the reported dose totals and volumes. The publication gives no separately assigned paprika exposure, paprika amount, ground culinary preparation or Parkinson subgroup.
