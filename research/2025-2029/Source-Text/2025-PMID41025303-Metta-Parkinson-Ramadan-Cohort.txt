Reviewed: 2026-11-20

This four-week observational study followed 75 fasting people with idiopathic Parkinson disease. Fifty chose an adapted medication schedule and 25 chose to stop medication during fasting. Group membership reflected participant choices. It did not arise from random allocation.

Table 2 lists mean NMSQ totals of 13.6 at baseline and 12.2 after Ramadan in the adapted schedule group. The corresponding means in the group stopping medication were 13.3 and 21.8. The table gives a post-Ramadan group comparison of p < 0.001. The scales and wearable measures address overall motor and nonmotor differences during and after the fasting period. The report supplies no individual pain or apathy estimates and no item-level counts for those symptoms.

The methods and tables do not identify date fruit, serving amounts, preparation, or participant-level meal composition. The medication groups and nonmotor totals cannot establish an ingredient effect, and group selection limits causal interpretation. This is a distinct cohort from the 2016 Kuwait work.
