Title: Continuous subcutaneous foslevodopa/foscarbidopa in advanced Parkinson's disease: A clinical workflow from 50 consecutive patients at a single U.S. center
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13487280/
PMID: 42621320
PMCID: PMC13487280
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2026.100492
Publication date: 2026-08-06 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Of 50 consecutive advanced Parkinson patients who initiated subcutaneous treatment, two discontinued after visual hallucinations and both had prior psychosis. This implementation report counts discontinuations, without a controlled dietary exposure or recurrent visual episode frequencies.

Results give a median follow-up of 7.2 months. Drug use, adverse reports and decisions to stop treatment are clinical workflow outcomes. Two patients discontinued because of visual hallucinations, both with previous psychosis.
