Title: Chemometric Assessment of the Relationship Between Extraction Methods and the Fatty Acid Composition of Walnut Oil
PMID: 42982915
DOI: 10.1007/s11130-026-01580-3
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11130-026-01580-3
First published: 2026-11-05, official publisher.
Coverage: Factual extract of original publisher abstract; complete subscription body unavailable.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

The abstract compares cold pressing, solvent extraction and aqueous enzymatic processing. Total unsaturated fatty acids are reported as 643.4 (dispersion 19.9), 652.8 (10.7) and 753.3 (16.1) mg/g, respectively. The dispersion type, exact solvent, group denominator and individual linoleic values are not verified from the abstract.

The authors interpret chemical profiles using cluster and principal-component analyses. Those methods do not establish a measured clinical health or analgesic benefit. Sterol, vitamin E and squalene contents are described as similar, without proving complete oil equivalence. This is chemistry at abstract depth, not a human or Parkinson symptom trial.
