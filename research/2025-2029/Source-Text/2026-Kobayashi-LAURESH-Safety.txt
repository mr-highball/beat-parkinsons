Laurel leaf extract safety comparison in healthy adults
Source: https://www.lifescience.co.jp/yk/yk26/yke2607.html
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: publisher citation and original abstract

Kobayashi and colleagues published a separate blinded four-week safety comparison on July 20, 2026. The available abstract reports 12 active and 10 placebo participants and a primary adverse-event occurrence outcome, with no adverse events reported. Blood and urine outcomes were secondary. This is an extract study and does not supply a separate constipation scale. Original abstract mirror: https://corporate.eurekamag.com/research/110/452/110452079.php. Publication date and authors: https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/en/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=202602217538274457.
