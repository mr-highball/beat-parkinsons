Title: Context-agnostic machine learning for Parkinson's disease motor symptom detection using wearable sensors
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13660283/
PMID: 42469783
PMCID: PMC13660283
DOI: 10.1186/s12911-026-03665-0
Publication date: 2026-07-17 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The research evaluates wearable motion data and classifiers for tremor, bradykinesia and dyskinesia. These motor measures and detection performance are unrelated to food-timed visual effects.
