The review covers caffeine trials in shift workers and simulated shift-work settings. Its focus includes sleepiness, alertness, performance and recovery sleep. Those outcomes must remain distinct from persistent Parkinson fatigue.

Figure S123 assigns means 3.60 and 5.22 to caffeine-plus-nap and placebo-plus-nap in the Centofanti pilot. It shows six observations in each condition and an estimated mean difference of −1.62 (95% CI −3.13 to −0.11), after extraction from the published graph. The review article and Table S9 report N = 12, whereas the supplement's trial characteristics and the original crossover describe six unique people. The fixed-effect display shows per-condition totals and does not distinguish 12 independent participants or expose pairing covariance. Use the original mixed-model comparison and six-person sample for the study family. The trial-characteristics table says placebo without a nap, while the original methods and Figure S123 comparator both indicate a nap opportunity.

This synthesis and the Centofanti report share one set of participants. The result applies to added caffeine within a shared decaf and nap protocol. Its connection to an ordinary brewed beverage or Parkinson fatigue remains indirect.
