Marjoram blend by inhalation and massage in a bruxism protocol
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07665398
Registration: NCT07665398
Coverage: official protocol including oil constituents and prespecified endpoints

First posted 2026-06-24. The proprietary blend contains sweet marjoram, lavender and vetiver and is compared with sesame carrier by twice-daily inhalation and daily extraoral massage. Masseter thickness and elasticity by ultrasound are primary measures. Other measures include pain, headache, tooth sensitivity, fatigue and perceived stress or anxiety. No dream-enactment, polysomnographic REM without atonia or dietary exposure is specified. Status was active, not recruiting; no registry results were posted as captured 2026-11-16.
