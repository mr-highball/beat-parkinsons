Title: Effect of genetic factors on [18F]FDG PET metabolic phenotypes in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13727925/
PMID: 42566020
PMCID: PMC13727925
DOI: 10.1007/s00259-026-08124-6
Publication date: 2026-08-07 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The retrospective genetic imaging analysis compares FDG PET metabolic signatures across genetic risk groups in dementia with Lewy bodies. Cerebral glucose uptake and model classification do not identify an individual food exposure or a visual outcome.
