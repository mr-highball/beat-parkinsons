Clove odor in a multiodor diabetes cognition trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40908892/
Coverage: Factual summary of the original author abstract.

First online September 5, 2025. Chao and colleagues randomized 60 adults with type 2 diabetes and mild cognitive impairment to six-odor training or routine care. Clove was one of six stimuli. The original abstract reports mean MoCA change +1.9 versus +0.4 after 16 weeks (p=.001) and describes executive-domain improvement. The abstract gives no executive-domain effect estimate and the full Wiley results were not accessible in this pass. There was no separated clove arm, culinary ingestion or Parkinson population.
