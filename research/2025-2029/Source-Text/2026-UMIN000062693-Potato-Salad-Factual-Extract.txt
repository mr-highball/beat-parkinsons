Title: Registered potato salad meal and postprandial sleepiness
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000071097

UMIN000062693, disclosed 3 September 2026, describes a single-meal comparison in healthy Japanese males aged 20–59, target 40. One condition is 73.7 g white bread; the other is 56.7 g white bread plus 80 g potato salad containing 6.8 g reduced digestible dextrin. Outcomes include postprandial visual analogue and Stanford Sleepiness Scale scores alongside continuous glucose monitoring. The registration title calls it randomized crossover, while structured fields label it nonrandomized, single-arm and self-controlled. It says results are unpublished and recruitment preinitiation. Actual ingestion, potato boiling, Parkinson disease and results are not established; the altered bread amount and added dextrin further prevent a potato-only interpretation. [Official UMIN registry](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000071097).
