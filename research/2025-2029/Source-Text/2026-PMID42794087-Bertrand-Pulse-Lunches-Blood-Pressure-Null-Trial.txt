Controlled human feeding trial (Bertrand et al., 2026, PMC13606205) evaluating daily pulse-containing lunches (including chickpeas/legumes) in adults, finding acceptable palatability and metabolic markers with no significant blood-pressure lowering versus control. Competing null seated-BP pulse evidence; no orthostatic standing BP or Parkinson data.
