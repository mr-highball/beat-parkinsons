Title: The effects of blueberry consumption on cerebral blood flow and brain glutathione concentrations in older adults: a double-blind randomized, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 42951114
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2026.101613
Registry ID: NCT05024916
First published: 2026
Authors: Choi IY, Payne C, Lee P, Spaeth K, Taylor M, Sullivan DK
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42951114/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-month double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (NCT05024916) in n=60 older adults (65-89 y) consuming 1-cup equivalent freeze-dried whole blueberry powder vs placebo. Blueberry did not alter brain glutathione vs placebo, but significantly increased MRI cerebral blood flow in the anterior cingulate cortex vs placebo (p=0.03; within-group +7%, 95% CI 1.0%-14.7%), with within-group CBF gains in caudate (+7%) and putamen (+6%). Limits: mechanistic neuroimaging perfusion endpoint without cognitive task or PD symptom scores.
