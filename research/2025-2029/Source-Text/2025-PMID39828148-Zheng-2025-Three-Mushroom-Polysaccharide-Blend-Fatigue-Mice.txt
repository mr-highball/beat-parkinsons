Title: Blending polysaccharides from three edible mushrooms represents a promising approach for augmenting their anti-oxidant and anti-fatigue activities.
PMID: 39828148
DOI: 10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2025.140020
First published: 2025
Authors: Zheng P, Dai W, Lin X, Lin S, Zhang Y, Hu J
Journal: International journal of biological macromolecules
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39828148/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Mouse swimming-exhaustion study of a 1:2:2 polysaccharide blend from Pleurotus eryngii, Agrocybe aegerita, and Agaricus bisporus extending swimming time (9.45 to 27.65 min). Multi-species isolated polysaccharide blend; does not isolate A. bisporus.
