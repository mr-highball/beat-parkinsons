Title: Chronological Diagnostic Algorithm Predicting Neuropathology in Parkinsonism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13206486/
PMID: 41830446
PMCID: PMC13206486
DOI: 10.1002/ana.78193
Publication date: 2026-03-14 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The autopsy-linked study uses onset data for clinical features from 949 patients who developed parkinsonism within three years of disease onset. The model predicts later neuropathological classification using clinical history. Occurrence or onset of symptoms provides no culinary exposure or episode-rate comparison.
