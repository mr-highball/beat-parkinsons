Text coverage: Distilled comparison of original body, trial tables and linked registrations.

The TRIHEP3 table row names safflower oil as the comparator for triheptanoin and lists caudate atrophy as the main early Huntington disease outcome. This is the same study reported in the March update and primary Neurology article. There is no oil-arm swallowing result or isolated dystonia estimate in these reports.

Two different rows describe a deutetrabenazine-versus-matching-tablet trial for dysphagia (NCT04301726) and a swallowing skills program for Huntington disease and ALS (ACTRN12617001269325). Neither allocated safflower oil. Their inclusion in the same review does not connect the oil to their swallowing outcomes. SAGE-718 is dalzanemdor in two previously reviewed trials. Sage in the source identifiers and study text refers to a drug developer or publisher. No culinary sage exposure was identified.
