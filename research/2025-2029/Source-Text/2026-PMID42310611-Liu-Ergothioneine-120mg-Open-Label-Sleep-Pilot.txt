Title: Oral L-ergothioneine supplementation in women with suboptimal ovarian reserve: a single-center, open-label, self-controlled pilot study of ovarian reserve markers, endocrine profiles, and reproductive aging-related symptoms.
PMID: 42310611
DOI: 10.1186/s12905-026-04604-x
First published: 2026
Authors: Liu Y, Guo X, Ding L
Journal: BMC Women's Health
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42310611/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Open-label single-arm pilot (ChiCTR2500104484; n = 40 enrolled, 36 completed women aged 35-45 y) giving 120 mg/day isolated oral L-ergothioneine for 3 cycles. Secondary PSQI fell from 6.89 +/- 1.82 to 5.50 +/- 1.40 (p < 0.0001). Uncontrolled open-label design and supra-dietary isolated dose; contextual corroboration only.
