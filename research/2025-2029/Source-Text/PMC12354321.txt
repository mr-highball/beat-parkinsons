Title: Substantia Nigra Iron Deposition in Lewy Body Disease: A Magnetic Resonance Imaging and Neuropathology Study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12354321/
PMID: 40545947
PMCID: PMC12354321
DOI: 10.1002/mds.30265
Publication date: 2025-06-23 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Antemortem MRI and autopsy of 54 participants compare substantia nigra susceptibility in 13 with Lewy pathology and 41 without. Imaging-based tissue iron estimates do not measure dietary iron, figs or a rate of visual episodes.
