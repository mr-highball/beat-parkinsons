Title: Longitudinal change in swallow function in patients with Parkinson's disease (PD)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41744403/
PMID: 41744403
PMCID: PMC13347595
DOI: 10.1177/1877718X261420003
First public: 2026-02-26
Coverage: Factual extract from the original methods, results and tables.

Marshall and colleagues retrospectively followed 19 people with Parkinson disease across 38 videofluoroscopic examinations, a mean of 2.6 years apart. The standardized test used barium liquids, pudding and a coated cracker among its boluses. The authors report worsening overall Dynamic Imaging Grade of Swallowing Toxicity (p=.024) and reduced solid consistency level (p=.041). The observations concern swallowing progression and texture, with no standardized culinary sage exposure or fall and rigidity comparison.
