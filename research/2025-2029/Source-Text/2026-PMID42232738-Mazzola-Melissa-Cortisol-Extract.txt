Text coverage: Original Methods, Tables 2 and 4–5, and Discussion, distilled.

The parent report is Rondanelli et al. PMID 41777239. This report explicitly analyzes 12 consenting people from its 400 mg phospholipid-formulated lemon balm arm. All 12 received the same formulated extract for three weeks. Table 2 gives baseline DASS-21 depression 21.58 (SD 3.00). The paper states that a group of psychometric measures including depression improved from baseline to week three (p≤.007). It gives no separate magnitude of change for depression at that location. Week-three and baseline scores correlated at r=.923, which measures score stability. Exploratory cortisol associations did not survive adjustment for multiple tests.

The subgroup adds measurements to the original cohort. It has no comparator, separate brewed leaf arm, or Parkinson population, and supplies no independent efficacy cohort. The cited registry NCT06942897 lists a different single-group planned design and calendar.
