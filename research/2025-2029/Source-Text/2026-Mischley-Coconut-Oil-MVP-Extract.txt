Longitudinal coconut-oil association in the Modifiable Variables in Parkinsonism cohort
Primary source: https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202610.1107
DOI: 10.20944/preprints202610.1107.v1
First posted: 2026-10-13
Coverage: factual original extract of preprint status, exposure, outcome, analysis and scope.

The unreviewed study analyzed reported use of complementary therapies and composite patient-reported Parkinson symptoms. It identified 2,612 participants at baseline. Self-reported coconut oil was not associated with a statistically significant baseline difference in the individual model and was not retained in the full baseline model. The mixed-effects time interaction was reported in the direction of slower accrual of composite symptoms in baseline users. The figures and Methods concern observational associations and the analysis did not correct for multiple comparisons. The paper names no virgin or refined grade and supplies no coconut oil contrast for self-reported balance, dream enactment or speech intelligibility. Earlier MVP publications are from the same cohort family.
