Title: Conservative iron chelation for VAC14: Two-year clinical-radiological follow-up
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13347475/
PMID: 40221969
PMCID: PMC13347475
DOI: 10.1177/1877718X251331820
First publication: 2025-04-13 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC original article, clinical course and supplementary-video descriptions

This single case concerns VAC14-associated neurodegeneration with brain iron accumulation and atypical parkinsonism-dystonia. The clinicians followed the course during deferiprone treatment. They describe dystonia in the presentation and prior treatment, and include rigidity among the items contributing to a change in the overall motor examination from 51 to 46 over two years. This uncontrolled trajectory does not isolate an effect on dystonia or rigidity. SAGE identifies the publisher's video player. The article provides no fresh or ground culinary sage exposure.
