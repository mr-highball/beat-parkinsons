Ginger granules menstrual pain and inconsistency in baseline table
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13590112/
PMID: 42765050
PMCID: PMC13590112
DOI: 10.7759/cureus.114903
Registration: CTRI/2025/05/086338
Coverage: Original August 21, 2026 body and Table 2; official WHO trial record

The randomized study reports a standardized ginger-extract granule group of 49 and placebo of 47. Table 2 shows baseline VAS means 5.36 (SD 0.56) and 5.30 (0.58) but prints a between-group t=5.15 and p<.001, inconsistent with the displayed summary and the prose. The text describes cycle-three changes without giving the cycle-specific arm VAS values in the table. The official registry verifies design and timing but gives no corrected results. The exact pain contrast remains unresolved.
