Title: Expanding the Phenotypic Spectrum of PUS3 Deficiency: A p.Tyr71Cys Case Demonstrating a Stable, Nonprogressive Leukoencephalopathy Pattern

The clinical and genetic case report describes a PUS3-associated leukoencephalopathy phenotype and longitudinal stability. It supplies a different population, with neither a dried fig comparison nor a measured Parkinson visual endpoint.
