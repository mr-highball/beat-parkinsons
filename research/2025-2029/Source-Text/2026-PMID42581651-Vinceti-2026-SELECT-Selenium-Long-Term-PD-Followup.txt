Title: The selenium and vitamin E cancer prevention trial (SELECT): a secondary, post-intervention analysis on potential excess chronic disease incidence induced by selenium administration.
PMID: 42581651
DOI: 10.1093/aje/kwag189
Registry ID: NCT00006392
First published: 2026
Authors: Vinceti M, Till C, Goodman PJ, Filippini T, Rothman KJ, Thompson IM
Journal: American journal of epidemiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42581651/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Post-intervention secondary follow-up of the randomized SELECT trial (n=34,887 men >=55 y assigned to 200 ug/d L-selenomethionine, vitamin E, both, or placebo over 7-12 y): near-null Parkinson disease incidence with L-selenomethionine while flagging higher ALS/lymphoma risk signals. Crucial long-term human safety/neurodegeneration evidence for the exact chemical form of selenium (selenomethionine) present in Brazil nuts.
