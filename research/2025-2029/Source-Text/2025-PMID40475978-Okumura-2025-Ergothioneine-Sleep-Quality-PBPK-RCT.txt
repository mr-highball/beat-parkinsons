Title: Estimation and Validation of an Effective Ergothioneine Dose for Improved Sleep Quality Using Physiologically Based Pharmacokinetic Model.
PMID: 40475978
PMCID: PMC12138820
DOI: 10.1002/fsn3.70382
First published: 2025
Authors: Okumura H, Araragi Y, Nishioka K, Yamashita R, Suzuki T, Watanabe H
Journal: Food science & nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12138820/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Two-part human PK/clinical study including Study 2: a 16-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in n=99 Japanese adults (40-64 y; n=92 OSA-MA analyzed: 45 EGT 8 mg/d vs 47 placebo) with subjective cognitive/sleep complaints (excluding high mushroom consumers >100 g/d). Plasma EGT rose 2.3-fold (4.07 to 9.38 uM). Compared with placebo, 8 mg/d EGT significantly improved OSA-MA Factor V Sleep Length (+2.7 vs -0.4, p=0.007) and Factor IV Refreshing (+2.5 vs -0.1, p=0.026), with an improving trend in Factor II sleep maintenance (p=0.107), while Factor I sleepiness on rising (p=0.276) and Factor III frequent dreaming (p=0.922) did not differ. Limits: isolated L-ergothioneine capsules in non-PD adults.
