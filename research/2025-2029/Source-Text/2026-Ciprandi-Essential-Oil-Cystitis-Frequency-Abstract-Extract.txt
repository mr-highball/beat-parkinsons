Mixed essential oil capsules and urinary frequency

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42980616/
Coverage: Original author abstract, factual extract.

Ciprandi, Paganotti and colleagues. Minerva Medica, first public November 4, 2026. DOI 10.23736/S0026-4806.26.09937-4. This observational acute cystitis report compares 24 fosfomycin recipients and two groups of 21 and 23 receiving different amounts of an eight-essential-oil blend containing Cymbopogon citratus. Reported percentages at up to four voids per day changed from 25.0 to 70.8, 19.0 to 85.7 and 21.7 to 69.6, respectively. The matched study outcome is void frequency. The available original abstract gives no separately tabulated urgency score, comparison of brewed tea, single ingredient or Parkinson cohort. Original full methods and tables remain to retrieve.
