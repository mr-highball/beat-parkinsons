Avocado and mango report omits registered cognitive outcomes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13055767/
Coverage: Factual original Methods and supplement outcome coverage

Published online February 16, 2026. Preiss and colleagues report vascular and dietary results from the registered trial of avocado plus mango in prediabetes. The Methods say cognitive testing took place after breakfast at the prestudy visit and week 7. The paper excludes cognition and ocular data and says those will be reported separately. Supplement Tables S1–S10 have no cognition results. No separate executive estimates were located through the checked public routes. The assigned exposure is a whole-fruit dietary package.
