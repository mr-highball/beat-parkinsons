Title: Aroma Massage for Pain Relief and Quality of Life in Parkinson’s Disease Patients
Publication: Aoyama and colleagues, 2025, Tokai Journal of Experimental and Clinical Medicine 50(3):85–99
Source: https://www.mj-med-u-tokai.com/pdf/500303.pdf
Coverage: factual extract of the accessible primary source

The same four-case Parkinson series already used in the archive gave inhalation of lemongrass oil, massage with plain jojoba and massage with jojoba plus 3% lemongrass essential oil over three sequential conditions. Table 15's columns for each case are Pre, inhalation, massage and aroma massage. General speech difficulty had fixed scores 2, 2, 2 and 0 across participants. Clear writing difficulty stayed at 0, 0, 1 and 1 and fear of falling in public stayed at 1, 0, 0 and 0. One participant reported less difficulty walking half a mile during massage and aroma massage and two participants improved on the 100-yard item, with no standardized postural challenge or observed falls count. For the combined item on distressing dreams or hallucinations, one participant had a score of 2 during baseline and the first two phases and 0 in the aroma-massage phase. The other three were 0 throughout. The questionnaire does not isolate low voice volume, character size, actual falls, dream enactment or Parkinson postural instability, and the intervention was nonoral. The report labels the oil lemongrass without an unambiguous botanical species assignment in the Methods consulted.
