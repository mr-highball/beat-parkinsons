Clove in a registered odor kit and cognitive follow up
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07221123
Coverage: Factual summary of the official registry.

First posted October 27, 2025. The BOOST trial registers conventional olfactory training with clove odor among its stimuli and lists Trail Making Test B among cognition measures. It targets people with HIV. The June 11, 2026 update lists recruiting and no posted results. Clove odor and the other components are not separated into ingredient-specific arms. The registry does not test ground clove ingestion or Parkinson executive impairment.
