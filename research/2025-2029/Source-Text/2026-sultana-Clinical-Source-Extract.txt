Perimenopausal fatigue after a standardized saffron extract
2026-10-22 (preprint first version)
https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.12.26365297v1.full.pdf

Sultana and colleagues reported a 12-week randomized trial of 30 mg/day standardized Safr'Inside extract or placebo in 104 perimenopausal women. Table 4 gives POMS fatigue changes of −7.6 and −1.7, respectively, with an interaction p=.0003. Depression findings differed between intention-to-treat and per-protocol analyses. Registration timing and item estimates from the per-protocol analysis remain unresolved. This preprint addresses an extract and perimenopausal symptoms, with no Parkinson fatigue estimate.
