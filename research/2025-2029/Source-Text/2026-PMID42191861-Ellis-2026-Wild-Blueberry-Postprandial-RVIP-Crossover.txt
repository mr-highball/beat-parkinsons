Title: A randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial to assess the postprandial dose-dependent effects of wild blueberries on metabolic and cognitive outcomes following a high-carbohydrate breakfast.
PMID: 42191861
PMCID: PMC13212708
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-026-03974-0
First published: 2026
Authors: Ellis LR, O'Connor D, Khan H, Dye L, Boesch C
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13212708/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover trial in n=24 healthy young adults (mean age 28 y) consuming wild blueberry drinks providing 0, 150, 300, or 450 mg anthocyanins with a high-carbohydrate breakfast. Medium/high doses lowered 1-h postprandial glucose/insulin and raised GLP-1/PYY/GIP, but 90-min cognitive assessments (RVIP sustained attention, Visual Verbal Learning Test, Corsi) and blood pressure showed no intervention effects. Limits: acute single-meal test in young healthy adults.
