Title: Cinnamon-containing gum and candy in a retrospective stomatitis series
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40914980/
Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1111/odi.70081
PMID: 40914980
DOI: 10.1111/odi.70081
Publication date: 2025-09-07
Date basis: Online publication, ahead of the February 2026 issue.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Review depth: abstract

The primary retrospective series includes 74 selected contact stomatitis cases associated with cinnamon-containing chewing gum or candy. The buccal mucosa was affected in 67 cases and the lateral tongue in 50. The abstract reports resolution within one week of stopping the implicated products. Resolution after withdrawal was also part of case ascertainment, and the paper does not estimate incidence among product users.

The separate literature review found 40 documented cases. It does not contribute further patients to the 74-case primary series here. The gum and candy exposures and oral lesions give no separately measured effect of ground cinnamon on Parkinson speech intelligibility. Full article tables remain open.
