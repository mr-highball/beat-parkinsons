Title: MIND diet adherence and Parkinson's disease risk, progression, and age at onset: A systematic review
PMID: 42858790
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnesp.2026.105193
First published: 2026
Authors: Pitton Rissardo J, McGarry A, Fornari Caprara AL, Walker R
Journal: Clinical Nutrition ESPEN
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42858790/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Systematic review (PROSPERO CRD420251123365; searched through June 2026) identifying 5 observational studies (n=73,790; including Agarwal 2018 Rush MAP parkinsonism cohort PMID:30498828, HR 0.89). Higher MIND diet adherence was observationally associated with lower incident parkinsonism and slower progression, though one cohort was null, 3/5 studies were rated poor quality, and GRADE certainty was low to very low with zero PD RCTs and no kale-specific isolation.
