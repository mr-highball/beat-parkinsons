Oral pumpkin seed oil and tongue tissue with paclitaxel
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41518703/
PMID: 41518703
DOI: 10.1016/j.tice.2026.103314
Coverage: factual extract from primary abstract

Amira Adly Kassab and colleagues. Tissue and Cell 99:103314. First online January 8, 2026, issue April 2026.
Forty rats were assigned to control, oil, paclitaxel, or paclitaxel plus oil. Comparison oil was administered orally daily and paclitaxel was administered by injection weekly for six weeks. Histology, immunohistochemistry, biochemical outcomes and gene expression were assessed in tongue tissue. The authors report less epithelial and papillary damage with oil coadministration, without a reported saliva measurement or Parkinson cohort. The public abstract does not identify a preparation as cold pressed or toasted. This study uses a different chemical injury from the same investigator’s 2020 orlistat experiment.
