The May 2026 medRxiv version starts with 178 participants from Newcastle and COMPASS-ND and analyzes 169 after quality control, including 83 with dementia with Lewy bodies. Its measured outcomes are imaging-derived cortical measures with molecular and spatial-map associations. It shares the analyzed sample with the journal article and gives no dried fig or food-timed measure.
