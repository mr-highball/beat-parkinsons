Title: Efficacy of 42-month oral administration of glucoraphanin in preventing cognitive decline in individuals at elevated risk of dementia, including those with mild cognitive impairment: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot study.
PMID: 41669080
PMCID: PMC12884063
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2026.1740494
First published: 2026
Authors: Shimizu S, Kasai S, Suzuki C, Kon T, Suganuma H, Suzuki S
Journal: Frontiers in nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12884063/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Forty-two-month double-blind placebo-controlled pilot RCT in n=26 older adults (63-90 y, 19 completed: 10 GLR 30 mg/d vs 9 placebo) at elevated dementia/MCI risk. Linear mixed model showed significant group-by-time interaction favoring glucoraphanin on MCI Screen Memory Performance Index (p=0.012; MCI subgroup p=0.029) and immediate/delayed free recall (p<0.001 and p=0.012); 42-mo endpoint difference was marginal (p=0.079). Limits: small pilot, isolated glucoraphanin, memory rather than PD executive function.
