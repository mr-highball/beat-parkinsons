Title: Valerian, chamomile, and lavender for insomnia and sleep disturbance: a narrative review of mechanisms, clinical efficacy, and safety.

Authors: Alhaj OA, Elsahoryi NA, Alfailakawi O, Trabelsi K, Pandi-Perumal SR, Pakpour A, Jahrami H
Journal: Sleep & breathing = Schlaf & Atmung

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Narrative review (Alhaj et al., 2026, Sleep Breath) evaluating valerian, chamomile, and Lavandula angustifolia for insomnia and sleep disturbance. Concludes limited evidence shows standardized oral lavender oil (Silexan) improves anxiety-associated disturbed sleep primarily mediated by anxiolysis, and explicitly cautions that results are NOT transferable across preparations, doses, routes, or primary insomnia/neurological populations.
