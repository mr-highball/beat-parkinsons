Oral gingerol extract and open-field behavior after spinal nerve ligation
Published: 2025-04-25
DOI: 10.3390/nu17091444
PMID: 40362753
PMCID: PMC12073668
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/17/9/1444
Coverage: Version of record Methods 2.1–2.3, Results 3.2 and Figure 2

Forty female rats were assigned to sham surgery with vehicle, spinal nerve ligation with vehicle, or ligation with one of two oral gingerol-enriched extract doses. At four weeks, the group assigned 600 mg/kg entered the center of the open field more often than the ligation vehicle group (p < .05, Figure 2 n = 5–8 per group). The abstract broadly attributes behavioral improvement to both doses. Results 3.2 explicitly identifies the 600 mg/kg center-entry result. This is a behavioral proxy in an experimental neuropathy model.
