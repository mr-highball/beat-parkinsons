Title: Egg Consumption and Cognitive Function: A Systematic Review and Dose-Response Pooled Analysis Exploring Optimal Daily Intake.
PMID: 41492966
DOI: 10.1111/jhn.70198
First published: 2026
Authors: Liu J, Shen Q, Wang X
Journal: Journal of human nutrition and dietetics : the official journal of the British Dietetic Association
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41492966/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

PROSPERO-registered systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis (CRD42024613229) pooling 21 observational studies (13 longitudinal, 8 cross-sectional; n=99,453 participants, 18,893 cases) on habitual egg consumption and cognitive decline. Reported pooled RR = 0.91 (95% CI 0.83-0.99) near 50-60 g/day (~1 egg/day) with a U-shaped dose-response curve. Purely observational (zero adult RCTs), does not isolate preparation (poached/scrambled/boiled), does not separate PD executive/attention impairment from general aging dementia/cognitive screens, and contrasts with null NHANES, CHNS, Rancho Bernardo Trails B, and O'Connor 2022 RCT results.
