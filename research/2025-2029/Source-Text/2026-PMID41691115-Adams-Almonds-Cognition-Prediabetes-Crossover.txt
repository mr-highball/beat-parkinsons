Title: Long-term effects of almond consumption on cognitive performance and brain vascular function: a randomized crossover trial in overweight/obese adults with prediabetes.
PMID: 41691115
DOI: 10.1038/s41430-026-01705-6
First published: 2026
Authors: Adams, Mensink, Plat, Gravesteijn, Joris
Journal: European journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41691115/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

5-month single-blind randomized crossover trial (2-month washout; Maastricht University) in 43 overweight/obese adults with prediabetes (median age 66 y, IQR 59-69; 34 completed, 33 cognitive analysis, 26 MRI pCASL CBF analysis) comparing 50 g/d whole almonds vs control period. Linear mixed models showed no significant intervention effects on any CANTAB cognitive outcome (executive function, psychomotor speed, memory) or on whole-brain/regional cerebral blood flow. Limits: non-PD prediabetic cohort, null between-period contrast.
