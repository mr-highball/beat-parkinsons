Registered grape powder and hand strength outcomes

Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07208916
Coverage: Official allocation and outcome modules
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First posted October 6, 2025. The single-group protocol plans 35 older women and two 36 g grape-powder packets mixed with water per day during two two-week intervals. Secondary outcomes include grip and pinch strength. The available record gives a recruiting status last verified October 2025 and no posted results. It provides no isolated raw-grape Parkinson or dexterity contrast.
