This narrative review (Zhao, Hong & Cao) synthesizes preclinical epigenetic, mitochondrial, anti-inflammatory, and neuroprotective literature on the citrus flavanone hesperidin across aging, Alzheimer disease, and Parkinson disease models. The sole human clinical study cited for fall-risk reduction is reference 46 — the 36-participant Altemor (hesperidin, diosmin, and proanthocyanidins) elderly crossover pilot trial (Giovannini et al., PMID:40694049, NCT06352099).

The review explicitly cautions that human clinical data remain preliminary, underpowered, and vulnerable to positive-result publication bias, and identifies no randomized trial of whole raw orange or isolated hesperidin on fall frequency in people with Parkinson disease.
