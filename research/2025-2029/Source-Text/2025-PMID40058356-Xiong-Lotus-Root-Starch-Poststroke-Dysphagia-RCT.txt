Title: Preparation and Application of Thickened Foods Using Lotus Root Starch Powder for Poststroke Dysphagic Patients.
PMID: 40058356
DOI: 10.1159/000545168
First published: 2025
Authors: Xiong M, Ng ML
Journal: Folia phoniatrica et logopaedica : official organ of the International Association of Logopedics and Phoniatrics (IALP)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40058356/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized trial in n=76 poststroke dysphagic patients comparing lotus root starch thickener (n=26), xanthan gum (n=26), and control (n=24) across IDDSI consistencies. Does not isolate baked or raw banana as an experimental dysphagia treatment.
