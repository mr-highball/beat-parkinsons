Title: Assessing the longitudinal relationship between whole egg consumption and cognition in Chinese older adults.
PMID: 37461359
DOI: 10.1177/02601060231186648
First published: 2025
Authors: An R, Wang S
Journal: Nutrition and health
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37461359/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Longitudinal analysis of the China Health and Nutrition Survey (CHNS 1997-2006; n=4,737 older Chinese adults aged >=55 y; 46% whole egg consumers averaging 47.4 g/d) using 3-day 24-h recalls and repeated recall/serial-7/backward-counting cognitive tests. Results: after adjusting for socioeconomic and demographic confounders in multivariate mixed-effects models, daily whole egg intake showed NULL longitudinal association with cognitive functioning.
