Round 1 claims: 0305

The essential tremor study compares six adaptive eating devices and a standard spoon. Participants transferred pearl couscous to a cup near the mouth. Investigators compared transfers and device preferences.

Couscous provides the same test material across devices. The experiment does not assess fresh sage, swallowing of the test food, or a Parkinson dystonia response.
