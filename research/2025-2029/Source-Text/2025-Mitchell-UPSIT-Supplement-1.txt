Supplementary Figure 1: Two illustrative examples of the 100 decision trees used by the trained Gradient Boost
model with the Response_SA feature set. Overall model prediction is determined by the sum of the values at the
end of each tree. A positive or negative final value indicates the prediction of PD or non-PD, respectively. Due to
the large number of trees, direct analysis is challenging. Tools such as SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP)
framework are used to summarise and highlight the most impactful response features (see Figure 3).
