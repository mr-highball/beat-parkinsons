Title: Effects of grape seed extract supplementation in people with migraine. Depression and anxiety outcomes from Table 4.

Fifty adults with migraine were randomized to grape seed extract or placebo for eight weeks. Baseline-adjusted changes in DASS depression were −5.64 ± 0.89 for extract and −5.56 ± 0.89 for placebo, p=.95. Anxiety changes were −3.61 ± 0.80 and −2.79 ± 0.80, p=.47. The uncertainties are standard errors. The contrasts do not establish benefit or equivalence. The trial used extract and studied migraine. It supplies no independent Parkinson mood outcome for refined fixed oil.
