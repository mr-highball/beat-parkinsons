Title: The effectiveness of Melissa officinalis L. essential oil inhalation on anxiety and symptom burden of hemodialysis patients
PMID: 40082838
PMCID: PMC11907815
DOI: 10.1186/s12906-025-04840-y
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12906-025-04840-y
First published: 2025-03-13
Text coverage: Original Methods and Tables 3–4.

The randomized trial compared inhaled Melissa essential oil with inhaled refined sweet almond oil in 68 hemodialysis patients. It used anxiety scales and the Dialysis Symptom Index rather than a depression questionnaire.

After one month, Table 3 shows that 22 of 34 in the essential-oil group and 21 of 34 in the comparator group reported feeling sad (p=.80). Table 4 gives post-treatment severity means of 2.02 (SD 1.69) and 2.14 (SD 1.94), respectively (p=.84). Baseline means were 2.64 and 2.05. These p-values accompany post-treatment comparisons. The paper does not give an adjusted change estimate for this item or a separate depression scale. The trial addresses inhaled oil exposure in dialysis outside Parkinson disease. The paper cites IRCT20191021045178N3.
