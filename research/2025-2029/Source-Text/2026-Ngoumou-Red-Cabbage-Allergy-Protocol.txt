Title: Effects of fermented versus unfermented red cabbage on symptoms, immune response, inflammatory markers and the gut microbiome in young adults with allergic rhinoconjunctivitis: a randomised controlled trial protocol
PMID: 41857857
PMCID: PMC13007187
DOI: 10.1136/bmjopen-2025-115290
First public publication: 2026-03-16
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13007187/
Format: Factual extraction from the original trial protocol.
Ngoumou and colleagues plan to randomize 158 adults aged 18–35 with allergic rhinoconjunctivitis to fermented red cabbage or sensory-matched unfermented red cabbage for eight weeks. Planned outcomes include allergy scores, medication, quality of life, GI symptoms, mood questionnaires and laboratory measures. The protocol plans general adverse-event capture and notes vital parameters without specifying a standing procedure or lightheadedness endpoint. It reports enrollment progress but no arm outcomes. Later safety tables or public datasets may permit a symptom-specific check.
Registration: DRKS00036475.
