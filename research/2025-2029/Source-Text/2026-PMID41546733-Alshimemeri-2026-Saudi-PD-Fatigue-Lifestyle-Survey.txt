Title: The role of exercise and lifestyle factors in fatigue among Parkinson's disease patients: a cross-sectional study.
PMID: 41546733
DOI: 10.1007/s10072-025-08597-7
First published: 2026
Authors: Alshimemeri S, Shadid AM, Alsannat IA, Alamri NK, Almuslih RA
Journal: Neurological sciences : official journal of the Italian Neurological Society and of the Italian Society of Clinical Neurophysiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41546733/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional survey of n=131 Saudi adults with Parkinson disease evaluating fatigue with the validated Arabic Fatigue Severity Scale (FSS >=4 in 61.1%). Infrequent exercise and low fluid intake correlated with higher fatigue, whereas caffeine consumption showed no significant association with fatigue. Limits: cross-sectional self-report; pooled caffeine rather than brewed black tea.
