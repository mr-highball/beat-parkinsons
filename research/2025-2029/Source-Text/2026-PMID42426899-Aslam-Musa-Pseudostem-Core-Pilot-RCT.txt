Title: Feasibility and tolerability of Musa sp. pseudo-stem core powder as a dietary supplement with gut microbiota-modulating potential: a pilot randomised controlled trial.
PMID: 42426899
PMCID: PMC13656367
DOI: 10.1186/s40814-026-01877-9
First published: 2026
Authors: Aslam H, Loftalani M, O'Hely M, Berk M, Keast R, Lewin S
Journal: Pilot and feasibility studies
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42426899/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

4-week triple-blind placebo-controlled pilot RCT (PMC13656367, ACTRN12623000454673) in n=30 healthy adults (age 18-65 y) randomized to 10 g/d Musa sp. pseudo-stem core powder vs maltodextrin placebo. Demonstrated feasibility (86% adherence) and GI tolerability with no between-group differences in GI symptoms, stool consistency, psychological symptoms, quality of life, or urinary hippurate at week 4, alongside weighted UniFrac microbiota shifts. Limits: banana pseudo-stem core (not fruit pulp), healthy non-PD adults.
