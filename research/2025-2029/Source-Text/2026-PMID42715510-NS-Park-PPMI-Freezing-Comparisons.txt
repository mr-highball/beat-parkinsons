Title: Levodopa use and characteristics of freezing-of-gait in patients with Parkinson disease in the NS-Park and PPMI cohorts

Coverage: Original structured abstract and institutional reference. The full article and supplement remain open.

The two-cohort analysis uses MDS-UPDRS II item 2.13 to assess gait freezing and adjusts for disease duration and severity. The adjusted relationship between levodopa status and freezing score had OR 1.84 (95% CI 0.94–3.61) in NS-Park and 0.76 (0.38–1.51) in PPMI. Both uncertainty intervals cross 1, and the point-estimate directions differ. These are observational comparisons for medication status and an ordinal freezing measure. The inspected material contains no fresh lemon balm intervention or intake-based freezing episode count.
