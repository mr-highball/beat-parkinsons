COMBAT baseline depression screening in the Connell thesis
Source: https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/99207/1/2025ConnellEPhD.pdf
Registration: NCT03679533
Coverage: Original university thesis deposited May 8, 2025; methods section 2.2.1 and sample lineage verified

Connell's doctoral thesis uses the baseline data of COMBAT and CANN. Fifty cognitively healthy COMBAT participants were used as controls in a comparison with two cognitive-impairment groups. The Methods state that participants completed PHQ-9 and GAD-7 screening and that significant depression or anxiety was an exclusion criterion. This verifies before-treatment questionnaire collection for the described analysis. It gives no post-treatment depression estimate by randomized cranberry group. The original randomized study remains one cohort.
