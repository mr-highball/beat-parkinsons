Title: Alpha-synuclein seeding amplification assays in Lewy body dementia: a brief review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12210522/
PMID: 40598304
PMCID: PMC12210522
DOI: 10.1186/s13024-025-00868-3
Publication date: 2025-07-01 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

A review compares alpha-synuclein seed amplification across specimens and diagnostic stages in Lewy body disease. The clinical focus is assay performance rather than an ingredient-specific visual outcome.
