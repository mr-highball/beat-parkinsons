Title: AquaDualPark Study - Effects of Aquatic and Land-Based Dual-Task Exercise Program on Motor and Cognitive Functions of People With Parkinson's Disease: Protocol for a Randomized Clinical Trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40688414/
PMID: 40688414
PMCID: PMC12276479
DOI: 10.1177/11786329251338730
First public: 2025-07-18
Coverage: Factual extract from original protocol methods.

The protocol describes four active exercise groups combining single or dual tasks with land or aquatic exercise. Its Methods separately identify a usual-activity control group, consistent with the five groups in ReBEC registration RBR-5yjyr7. The active programs last 12 weeks and measure motor and cognitive function. The article is a protocol. It supplies neither a sage exposure nor a completed effect on muscle rigidity or falls.
