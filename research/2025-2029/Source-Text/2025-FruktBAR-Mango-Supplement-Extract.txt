The questionnaire contains three fatigue items with responses on a 1–7 scale. Table S1 lists 250 ml nutrient estimates for four commercial mixed smoothies. Two combinations are mango with passion fruit and pineapple with mango. The other listed blends are pineapple with coconut and blueberries with apple. The table records nutritional composition, with no weight of individual mango ingredients, cultivar or participant-by-flavor exposure. Figures S9 and S11 show fatigue scores by randomized group and in per-protocol analysis, respectively. The results do not compare individual smoothie recipes.

Supplementary Text S1 also lists the ten psychological distress questionnaire items. The study queried difficulty falling asleep or staying asleep, along with anxiety-related experiences such as fearfulness and feeling tense and depressive symptoms such as feeling blue and hopeless. The main paper reports the combined SCL-10 change in Table 2 without item-level sleep, anxiety or depression results. This questionnaire detail documents what was collected without identifying a symptom-specific effect of an individual fruit.
