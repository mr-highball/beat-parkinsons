The preprint follows contralateral tremor spread in the Parkinson's
Progression Markers Initiative cohort. It separates examiner-rated
rest, postural and kinetic tremor. No food assignment, fig intake or
diet-linked tremor result is reported. The peer-reviewed article
(PMID 40956636) uses the same analysis cohort and remains a distinct
publication identity. The dated version 2 PDF and JATS both resolved
from authoritative preprint-provider routes.
