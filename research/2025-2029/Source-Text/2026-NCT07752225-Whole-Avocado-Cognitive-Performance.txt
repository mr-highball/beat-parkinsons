Registered whole avocado and cognitive performance in prediabetes
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07752225
Coverage: Factual official protocol and endpoint summary

First posted August 7, 2026. Maastricht investigators plan a 34-participant crossover in prediabetes, one whole avocado daily for 12 weeks versus no avocado. Cerebral blood flow is primary. Secondary cognitive endpoints include CANTAB tests and flanker performance. The August 27 version showed not yet recruiting with no posted results as checked November 16, 2026.
