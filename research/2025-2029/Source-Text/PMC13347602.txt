Title: Creation of a machine learning-based model for early identification of mild cognitive impairment risk in Parkinson's disease: A PD research center-based population study

Retrospective Parkinson disease records supplied 523 development participants and 139 external-validation participants for a model of mild cognitive impairment. Smell testing was included among clinical features. Falls were captured as history in a questionnaire, without prospective fall counts in the reported outcome. The model predicts cognitive classification. Sage was not evaluated.
