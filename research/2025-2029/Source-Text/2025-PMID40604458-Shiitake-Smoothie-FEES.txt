Title: Instrumental swallowing comparison of chicken–shiitake and other smoothies
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12877-025-06126-x
PMID: 40604458
PMCID: PMC12220473
DOI: 10.1186/s12877-025-06126-x
Publication date: 2025-07-02
Coverage: Original article and Supplementary Tables S1, S2 and S4.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

Sixty-three older adults underwent immediate fiberoptic endoscopic evaluation of swallowing in a blinded randomized crossover of three formulas. Thirty-one reported swallowing difficulty. Among those 31, the chicken–shiitake smoothie and thin Ensure had premature-spillage scores of 4.9 and 4.7, respectively, with higher values indicating better performance and a reported paired p=.023. Their total scores were 13.8 and 13.6. The reported three-formula overall comparison was inconclusive (p=.444).

The symptomatic subgroup had laryngeal penetration in 2 of 31 with chicken–shiitake and 4 of 31 with Ensure. No aspiration was recorded with either in that subgroup. The reported counts do not imply a demonstrated pairwise difference. The food formulas varied simultaneously in ingredients and viscosity. The study does not isolate shiitake, specify a roasted preparation, or provide a result for a diagnosed Parkinson subgroup.

Original methods follow-through, 2026-11-26. The chicken–shiitake formula is reported at 81 cP. The endoscopic comparisons were immediate after a 5 mL bolus. The small penetration counts, absence of observed aspiration and varied formulas leave food-specific and preparation-specific attribution open.
