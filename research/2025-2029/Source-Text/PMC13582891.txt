Title: Synucleinopathy-like clinical features and biologically defined synuclein seeding in PSP-Parkinsonism: an imperfect match
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13582891/
PMID: 42259819
PMCID: PMC13582891
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-026-01387-8
Publication date: 2026-06-08 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The cross-sectional comparison tracks synuclein-related and nonmotor features in Parkinson disease and two progressive supranuclear palsy phenotypes. The comparison of symptom patterns and biomarker status does not measure timing after dried fig or Parkinson visual event rates.
