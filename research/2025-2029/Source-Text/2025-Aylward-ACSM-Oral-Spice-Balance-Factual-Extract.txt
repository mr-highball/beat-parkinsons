Title: Aylward and colleagues, acute oral spice and movement strategy
Original source: https://midwest.acsm.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/MWACSM-Annual-Meeting-long-program.pdf#page=155
Presented: October 17, 2025, Midwest ACSM annual meeting, poster session 5, presentation 11
Coverage: Factual extract from the original program, printed p. 155.

Seven healthy adults completed baseline and two oral spice measurements. The abstract expresses heat conditions in Scoville units and omits product identity, amount, placebo and allocation order. Mean static BTrackS sway was 19.57 ± 4.47 cm at baseline, 17.29 ± 4.57 after lower heat, and 16.71 ± 4.72 after higher heat. The omnibus comparison was inconclusive (p > .05); reported nominal baseline comparisons had p = .043 and .035. The limits-of-stability comparisons were inconclusive. The report does not identify what the ± values represent or describe adjustment for the pairwise tests.
