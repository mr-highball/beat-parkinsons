Title: The correlation between RLS and motor or other non-motor symptoms of PD patients: an observational study
PMID: 40684087
PMCID: PMC12275443
DOI: 10.1186/s12883-025-04307-0
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12275443/
Publication: 2025-07-19
Coverage: Original Methods, Results and Table 2.

The observational group included 392 people with Parkinson disease, with 98 meeting criteria for restless legs syndrome. The restless-legs group had higher swallowing-disturbance scores (mean 6.04 versus 4.15, P=.032). The Sialorrhea Clinical Scale for Parkinson's Disease comparison did not show a clear difference (mean 3.56 versus 3.88, P=.561). The two questionnaire outcomes measure different symptom domains. The comparison is based on the presence of restless legs syndrome and supplies no food exposure or experimental measure of salivary flow.
