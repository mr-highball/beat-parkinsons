Title: Blackberry extract prevents lipopolysaccharide-induced depressive-like behavior in female mice: implications for redox status, inflammation, and brain enzymes.
PMID: 38861649
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2024.2363570
First published: 2025
Authors: Custódio SV, Piccoli RC, Goularte KCM, Simões WS, de Mello JE, de Souza AA et al.
Journal: Nutritional neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38861649/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled experiment in female mice subjected to lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced depressive-like behavior. Oral Rubus sp. blackberry extract prevented LPS-induced depressive-like behavioral deficits, attenuated cerebral cortex reactive species, nitrites, acetylcholinesterase activity, and IL-1beta elevation, and preserved cortical and serum catalase activity. Limits: non-PD mouse LPS depression model and concentrated extract rather than frozen whole blackberries; abstract omits group sample size.
