Title: The relationship between physical activity levels and measures of cognition and disease severity in prodromal Parkinson's disease: A longitudinal study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13595541/

PMCID: PMC13595541
DOI: 10.1186/s12883-026-05077-z

Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC XML article body and embedded tables; separate supplements are outside this extract.

Secondary analysis of the Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative (NCT01141023) evaluated 108 participants classified as prodromal. The authors used four-year activity measures, cognitive tests and MDS-UPDRS Parts I–III. Their reported models found no clear association between the activity variables and the evaluated disease severity or cognitive outcomes. The study did not report a fig exposure effect on rest tremor or a date exposure effect on pain severity. Part totals and a prodromal population limit transfer to the individual frozen symptoms in diagnosed patients.
