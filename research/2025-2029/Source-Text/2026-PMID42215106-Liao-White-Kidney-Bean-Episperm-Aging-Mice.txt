Title: White kidney bean episperm polysaccharides improve cognitive impairment in D-galactose-induced aging mice via modulation of oxidative stress, neuroinflammation, and the gut-brain axis.
PMID: 42215106
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2026.119439
First published: 2026
Authors: Liao S, Hu Y, Li X, Guo Z, Wang Y, Kong C, Wang B, de Vos P
Journal: Food research international
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42215106/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract Facts: Mouse experiment in D-galactose-induced aging model fed isolated white kidney bean episperm (seed-coat) non-digestible polysaccharides (WKBEP): improved spatial learning/memory, preserved hippocampal histology, attenuated neuroinflammation, and increased fecal SCFAs. Isolated seed-coat polysaccharide extract in non-PD aging mice; cannot establish effects of whole cooked/canned kidney beans on human PD attention or executive function.
