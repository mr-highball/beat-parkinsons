Registered baked and fried potato experiment A
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07230951
Coverage: Factual summary of official registry fields.

First posted November 17, 2025. Institutional identifier REB 25-172 [A] compares baked home fries, baked russet potato, baked fries, quick-serve fried fries, baked cauliflower and no side in healthy adults. An acute secondary emotion inventory includes sleepiness. Recruiting without posted results. The registry does not assign boiled potato or measure Parkinson daytime sleepiness.
