Title: Prunus cerasoides and prunetin in two Brain & Brain PET 2025 abstracts
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12134523/
Parent PMCID: PMC12134523
Parent DOI: 10.1177/0271678X251338700
Members: Publisher abstracts on prunetin and ischemic stroke recovery, and abstract 0634 on carotid stenosis.
Publication date: 2025-06-02
Text coverage: Two complete published conference abstracts. Full later experiments and study-family relations remain open.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18

One member investigated isolated prunetin in experimental ischemic stroke and evaluated Prunus cerasoides extract or prunetin in a cell experiment. The abstract reports favorable brain injury and barrier outcomes with prunetin in the animal model. A different member compared oral Prunus cerasoides extract, oral prunetin and controls after carotid stenosis in a mouse model. It reports improvements in passive avoidance and some elevated plus maze measures.

The botanical species, plant preparation, and outcomes differ from consuming dried cherry fruit. The conference abstracts provide no fruit preparation, apathy scale or clinical Parkinson outcome. The two members have different model designs. A later journal report with closely matching terminology is a follow-up lead, and its overlap with the conference data needs verification before counting it separately.
