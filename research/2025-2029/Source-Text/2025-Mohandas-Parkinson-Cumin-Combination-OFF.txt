Text coverage: factual extract of the medication timeline, intervention description, Table 3 and discussion.

The paper lists pre-admission levodopa 100 mg and carbidopa 25 mg. Administration frequency and inpatient and follow-up conventional medication schedules were not specified. The case combined inpatient procedures, counseling and internal preparations over eight days. Among those was one proprietary bowel-clearing tablet orally at bedtime. The abstract identifies Anuloma DS, and the discussion names Jeeraka (Cuminum cyminum) among seven constituents of the bowel treatment. It does not state the amount of cumin, processing, or lot. Concurrent oral proprietary Kapikacchu powder (Zandopa, Mucuna pruriens) was given before food with milk twice daily. It was still advised after discharge. The discharge regimen omits Anuloma DS and includes other therapies.

Table 3 labels its scoring schedule UPDRS-8. The five dated rows have Off Time entries 2 (50%), 2 (50%), 2 (50%), 2 (25%) and 2 (25%) on February 9, 11, 14, 16 and March 17, 2025, respectively. The first assessment followed the February 8 admission. Total scores on those dates were 25, 20, 19, 12, and 10 out of 40. The article supplies no clinical diary or OFF-hours conversion, so both the unchanged ordinal entry and the changed parenthetical percentage are preserved. It reports improvement in bowel movements and other complaints during the regimen without an isolated ingredient comparison. There is no ingredient-specific ground-cumin endpoint or levodopa pharmacokinetic result.
