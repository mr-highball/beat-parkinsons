Title: Toward a biological definition of neuronal and glial synucleinopathies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12466200/
PMID: 39885358
PMCID: PMC12466200
DOI: 10.1038/s41591-024-03469-7
Publication date: 2025-01-30 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The original review discusses incorporating biological markers into definitions of neuronal and glial synuclein disorders. The comparisons concern pathology, biomarker status and disease nomenclature. No dried fig exposure or visual episode frequency is measured.
