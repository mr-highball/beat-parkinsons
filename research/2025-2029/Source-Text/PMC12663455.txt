Title: Retinal alterations resemble brain pathology in a rat model of Parkinson's disease induced by intranigral infusion of α-synuclein oligomers
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12663455/
PMID: 41315232
PMCID: PMC12663455
DOI: 10.1038/s41420-025-02830-0
Publication date: 2025-11-28 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The animal study assesses retinal and brain pathology after an experimental Parkinson-model exposure. Retinal pathology is measured directly, but the model cannot supply clinical participant reports of visual episodes with or without dried figs.
