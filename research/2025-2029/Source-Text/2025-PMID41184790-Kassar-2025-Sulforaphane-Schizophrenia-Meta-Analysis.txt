Title: Efficacy and safety of sulforaphane in schizophrenia: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 41184790
PMCID: PMC12581406
DOI: 10.1186/s12888-025-07515-7
First published: 2025
Authors: Kassar O, M Mansour ME, Farag N, Selim A, Kewiaa Y, Yousef O
Journal: BMC psychiatry
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12581406/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 4 sulforaphane schizophrenia RCTs (n=369). Pooled PANSS total and positive scores were null; negative symptoms showed a modest 12-week reduction (MD -1.06, 95% CI -1.95 to -0.16, p=0.02) NOT maintained at latest follow-up (18-24 wk); general psychopathology improved (MD -1.5, p=0.02); NO cognitive benefits were observed; discontinuation was lower (RR 0.68, p=0.02). Context-only secondary synthesis.
