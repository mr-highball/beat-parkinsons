Title: Brain activity associated with breakthrough food preoccupation in an individual on tirzepatide
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12705427/
PMID: 41249493
PMCID: PMC12705427
DOI: 10.1038/s41591-025-04035-5
First published: 2025-11-17
Coverage: Distilled original methods and results or protocol fields, with article identity checked against PubMed.

The study reports intracranial recordings from an early-feasibility neurostimulation program (NCT03868670). The focal case reports a return of severe food preoccupation during tirzepatide treatment and corresponding electrophysiologic observations. Reference participants and observations establish a comparison of recorded states. Food preoccupation is the clinical phenomenon; it is not a dietary date intervention or a Parkinson pain measure.
