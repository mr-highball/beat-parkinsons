Title: The REPOSE pilot trial of adipose-derived cells in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13735452/
PMID: 42775498
PMCID: PMC13735452
DOI: 10.1177/09636897261492438
First publication: 2026-09-23 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC original randomization, outcome schedule, results and interpretation

Forty Parkinson participants were randomized equally to an intravenous cell product or placebo, with the same neurological rehabilitation in both groups. Safety and feasibility framed the trial. The exploratory motor assessment included aggregate MDS-UPDRS III and functional measures. The authors discuss sit-to-stand performance as relevant to fall risk. These observations do not provide a ground-sage comparison, a sage-specific rigidity item contrast, or a sage-related fall count.
