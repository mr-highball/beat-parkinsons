Riby LM, Kardzhieva D, Fenwick S, Fowler S, Moss M. The impact of a rosemary containing drink on cognition and mood: The role of eye blink dynamics.
First-public author preprint: 2025-11-20, DOI 10.20944/preprints202511.1599.v1, https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202511.1599
Journal original: 2026-01-17, DOI 10.3390/neurosci7010015, PMID 41562840, https://www.mdpi.com/2673-4087/7/1/15
Coverage: factual extract of original Methods, Results and Table 1.

Forty-eight healthy adults were assigned a rosemary drink or still water. The commercial intervention included a fresh-derived steam hydrolat and volatile extract. The measured Bond–Lader dimensions were alertness, calmness and contentedness before and after a visual task. Table 1 contentedness means were 26.67 and 30.97 for rosemary, and 24.22 and 28.34 for water. Reported paired one-tailed p-values are .008 and .031. These arm-specific tests do not estimate a group-by-time contrast. The group mean changes and the descriptive difference of +0.18 were calculated from tabulated means. The trial excluded current depression or anxiety diagnosis, and there was no depression scale. The 2023 and later papers share identical participant summary and related protocol; treat as one report family pending individual-level proof.
