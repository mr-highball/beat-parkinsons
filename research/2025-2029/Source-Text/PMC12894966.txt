Title: MCI-LB brain networks reorganization in relation to specific cognitive domains deficits
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12894966/
PMID: 41565763
PMCID: PMC12894966
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-36953-w
Publication date: 2026-01-21 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Functional connectivity and specific cognitive domains are compared in 38 people with mild cognitive impairment with Lewy bodies and 24 healthy controls. The exposures are diagnostic group and task-independent network properties. The outcomes do not measure visual episodes after eating figs.
