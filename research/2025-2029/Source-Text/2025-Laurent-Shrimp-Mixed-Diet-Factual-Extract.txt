Publication: Laurent et al., Frontiers in Nutrition, first published February 7, 2025. DOI 10.3389/fnut.2025.1506304. PMID 39990610, PMC11844221.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11844221/
Coverage: Original Case Presentation 1, factual extract.

The patient with schizoaffective disorder reported auditory and visual hallucinations at baseline. Shrimp was one of several foods in both the reported baseline diet and the mixed ketogenic intervention. The follow-up mentions cessation of auditory hallucinations and resolution of hallucinations broadly by six weeks. It does not track visual-hallucination frequency or shrimp intake and specifies no cooking method. Multiple dietary, medication and support changes occurred. The report supplies no separate food-specific comparison or Parkinson cohort.
