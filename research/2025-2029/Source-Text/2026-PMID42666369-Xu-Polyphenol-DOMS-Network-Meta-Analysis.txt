Title: Comparative effects of plant-derived anthocyanin- and flavanol-rich supplements on recovery outcomes after exercise-induced muscle damage: a systematic review and network meta-analysis.
PMID: 42666369
PMCID: PMC13522125
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2026.1857527
First published: 2026
Authors: Xu KX, Chang J, Wu YJ, Chen HW, Zheng JW, Liu C
Journal: Frontiers in nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13522125/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Systematic review and frequentist random-effects network meta-analysis (Xu et al., 2026, Front Nutr, PMC13522125; PROSPERO CRD420261340555) pooling 30 RCTs (n=595 healthy adults with acute exercise-induced muscle damage) across 8 anthocyanin/flavanol supplements. Tart cherry and pomegranate were the only supplements significantly reducing 48 h DOMS vs placebo, and tart cherry was the only supplement associated with significant DOMS reductions at both 24 h and 72 h, while strength, CK, and CRP showed no consistent advantage; overall certainty was low. Context-only meta-analysis of acute exercise soreness.
