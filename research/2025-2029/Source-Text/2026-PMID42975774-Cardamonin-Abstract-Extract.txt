Title: Neuroprotective potential of cardamonin in reserpine- and haloperidol-induced mice models of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42975774/
PMID: 42975774
DOI: 10.1007/s10787-026-02425-w
Publication date: 2026-11-03
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated author abstract and publisher preview

The authors tested isolated cardamonin in mouse models of
parkinsonism. The abstract reports improved general motor activity
and lists open-field, pole, catalepsy and object recognition tests.
It does not report a separately measured rest-tremor outcome.
The isolated compound is a separate exposure from culinary ground
cardamom. The publisher's body and detailed outcome tables were not
authenticated on this date.
