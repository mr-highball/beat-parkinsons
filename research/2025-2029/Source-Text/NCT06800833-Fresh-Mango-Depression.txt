Fresh mango depression questionnaire registry
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06800833
Registration: NCT06800833
Coverage: Official registration first posted January 30, 2025, current API checked

The randomized open-label crossover plans 60 generally healthy adults. The intervention compares 100 kcal/day fresh mango with 100 kcal/day low-fat cookies for four weeks in each period. One primary outcome is change in depression questionnaire scores from baseline to week 4. The record does not name the instrument or report results. The current record is recruiting, status last verified January 2026, with actual start January 23, 2025 and estimated completion December 31, 2027. The food preparation and population differ from dried mango in Parkinson disease.
