Giovannini and colleagues, online July 22, 2025, ahead of the January 2026 issue. PMID 40694049, NCT06352099. Primary abstract reports 36 randomized older adults across two Altemor dose sequences and control. Fall-risk and cognitive scores favored intervention versus control, with no clear dose-group difference. No observed fall count, standalone fatigue outcome or Parkinson cohort was reported in the accessible abstract. Full paper outcome tables and administered lot remained unverified. The registered Silver Index estimates risk, rather than counting events.
