Title: Effects of Cyperus esculentus infused diet on the hippocampus in a rotenone-induced Parkinson-like model in male Wistar rats
Publication date: 2026-10-19 (Europe PMC first preprint publication)
Europe PMC preprint ID: PPR1346503
DOI: 10.64898/2026.10.13.758640
Source: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.13.758640
Text coverage: Original-author abstract indexed by Europe PMC. Preprint figures and method tables remain unchecked.

The authors report 32 male rats assigned to chow with sunflower oil, a tiger nut diet, rotenone, or rotenone with tiger nut diet. Their model comparison reports poorer spatial working memory after rotenone and partial mitigation in the tiger nut work, with the abstract emphasizing antioxidant and histological results. Comparison of the sunflower reference group with the toxin group also changes toxin exposure. The abstract does not establish a matched sunflower-oil-only effect, its preparation, administration details, or a human executive outcome.
