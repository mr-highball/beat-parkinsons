Original study scope
The review cites cranberry or red grape juice in a small older-adult study with memory-related outcomes and discusses isolated protocatechuic acid in other models. Saffron appears in a background list of other possible neuroprotective agents, outside the tested comparisons.

Continuing evidence gaps
Different juice or compound exposures do not establish dried cranberry, raw or frozen grape, or culinary saffron on the frozen Parkinson symptoms.
