Title: Seeing Invisible Oligomers: Rethinking α-Synuclein Pathology Through Proximity Ligation Assay
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13387933/
PMID: 42036389
PMCID: PMC13387933
DOI: 10.1002/mds.70335
Publication date: 2026-04-26 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The review compares proximity ligation approaches to characterize alpha-synuclein oligomers. It cites a tissue association with visual hallucination presence in dementia with Lewy bodies. This is a cited neuropathological association without a culinary exposure or clinical recurrence measure.
