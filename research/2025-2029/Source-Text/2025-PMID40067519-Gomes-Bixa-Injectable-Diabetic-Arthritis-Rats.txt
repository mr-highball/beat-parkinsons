Title: The action of injectable nanodispersion of Bixa orellana (Chronic-in®) on arthritis in diabetic rats: pharmacological and histopathological studies.
PMID: 40067519
DOI: 10.1007/s10787-025-01703-3
First published: 2025
Authors: Gomes, de Oliveira Carvalho, Lopes, da Costa Furtado, Gonçalves
Journal: Inflammopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40067519/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled diabetic arthritis rat study (alloxan + Freund's complete adjuvant; n=25, 5/group) testing subcutaneous and intramuscular injectable nanodispersion of Bixa orellana oil (Chronic-in). Reduced paw edema (p < 0.001) and arthritic index, enhanced open-field exploration, improved Rotarod retention, and preserved joint proteoglycans. Limits: parenteral injectable nanodispersion in inflammatory rat arthritis, not oral ground seed or PD.
