Title: Dopamine alters functional gradients in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12319910/
PMID: 40800773
PMCID: PMC12319910
DOI: 10.1162/imag_a_00564
Publication date: 2025-05-06 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

A Parkinson group is compared while ON and OFF dopamine medication using functional gradients derived from brain imaging. The measured responses involve resting network organization under medication, with no dried fig exposure or visual episode-frequency endpoint.
