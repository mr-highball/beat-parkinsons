Roberts et al., Omega-3 Status and Mental Health Symptoms in Parkinson's Disease. Preprint version 1 posted 2026-10-15. https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202610.1439/v1. DOI 10.20944/preprints202610.1439.v1. Original factual extract of Methods, Table 4 and Appendix Table A1.

The 235-person cohort reports separate ratings of loss of interest and motivation/initiative. Base DHA differences per unit of whole-blood percentage were −5.37 for loss of interest and −4.97 for motivation difficulty, nominal p < .001 and p=.001. With cognitive self-report adjustment the estimates were −2.47 (p=.031) and −2.20 (p=.070). The blood omega-6 to omega-3 ratio yielded positive associations and weakened after the same adjustment. The study did not collect an identifiable soybean-oil intake variable. Its data came from the MVP parent study, also named in a prior diet pattern analysis.
