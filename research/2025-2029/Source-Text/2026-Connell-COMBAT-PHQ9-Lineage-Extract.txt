The published analysis uses baseline COMBAT samples as 50 matched healthy controls and explicitly identifies NCT03679533. The Methods confirm that participants completed the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 and that those with clinically significant symptoms were excluded. The paper compares cognitive-status groups using pretreatment observations and provides no estimate of a cranberry-powder effect on depression. It is a related publication using the same original COMBAT cohort as the thesis.
