MIND trial third report on quality of life and biomarkers
Source: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1186/s12902-026-02582-7_reference.pdf
DOI: 10.1186/s12902-026-02582-7
Coverage: full publisher accepted version, Methods, quality-of-life outcomes and data availability

First public accepted version September 26, 2026. It explicitly identifies the same 44 enrolled women, registration IRCT20181111041611N8 and three-month comparison as the earlier sleep and cardiometabolic reports. The modified MIND program had adjusted improvements in SF-36 energy/fatigue and emotional well-being. The reported comparisons concern the assigned diet and biomarkers. The third paper does not add a separate sleep/insomnia result or participant pinto intake. The accepted article is not yet the final version of record on the inspected route.
