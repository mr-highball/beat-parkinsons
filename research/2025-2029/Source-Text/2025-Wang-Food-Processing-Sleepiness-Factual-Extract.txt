Title: Dietary processing and daytime sleepiness in a community cohort
Source: https://edepot.wur.nl/694829

Wang et al. analyzed NHS/HPFS participants without a history of Parkinson disease, with repeated dietary questionnaires and later Epworth Sleepiness Scale screening, defining excessive daytime sleepiness as a score above 9. Potato/corn chips were treated as non-ultraprocessed in the primary analysis because classification evidence was insufficient. The paper reports extreme quintile comparison for ultraprocessed food intake was not statistically significant for excessive daytime sleepiness, though an overall trend was reported. It does not isolate boiled potato or assess treatment in diagnosed Parkinson disease. These are overlapping NHS/HPFS cohorts with the Molsberry dietary-pattern analysis, not independent populations.
