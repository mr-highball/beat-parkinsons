Text coverage: Distilled comparison of original body, trial tables and linked registrations.

The TRIHEP3 row lists safflower oil as the comparator for oral triheptanoin in early Huntington disease, with caudate atrophy as the main outcome. The registry and primary publication identify a six-month blinded comparison. The Huntington motor scale includes dystonia, but the linked publication reports an overall score and supplies no isolated dystonia or swallowing result.

A separate row lists NCT04301726, a deutetrabenazine trial with a matching oral tablet comparison and dysphagia endpoints. The trial table gives a planned enrollment of 48. A third row lists ACTRN12617001269325, a single-group swallowing skill intervention for Huntington disease and ALS with a planned sample of 54. Neither swallowing row assigned safflower oil.

SAGE-718 in DIMENSION and PURVIEW refers to the investigational drug dalzanemdor. Sage refers to its developer or the publication platform, with no identified Salvia intervention. The word date appears in administrative and trial timing context. No date fruit intervention was identified.
