Title: Assessing Acute and Subacute Toxicity and Phytochemical Screening of the Methanolic Extract of Foeniculum vulgare in Wistar Rats
PMID: 40958122
DOI: 10.1002/jat.4907
Publication date: 2025-09-16
Citation: Bougrine and colleagues. Journal of Applied Toxicology 46(3), 877–885, March 2026 issue.
Source: https://analyticalsciencejournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jat.4907
Coverage: Publisher and PubMed author abstract. Full clinical observation sheet, group counts and any item-level adverse observations remain open.

A single oral exposure study in female rats tested a methanolic fennel extract at four doses up to 5,000 mg/kg and observed animals over 14 days. The authors report no deaths. In the separate repeated oral study over 28 days the abstract reports no significant group differences on weight, intake, blood measurements or organ examinations. The abstract does not isolate any tremor result. It does not establish a no-tremor finding or contradict the specific highest-dose essential-oil observation in an earlier different preparation.
