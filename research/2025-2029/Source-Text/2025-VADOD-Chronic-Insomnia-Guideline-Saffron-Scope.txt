Title: VA/DoD clinical practice guideline for the management of chronic insomnia disorder and obstructive sleep apnea
Source: https://www.healthquality.va.gov/HEALTHQUALITY/guidelines/CD/insomnia/I-OSA-CPG_2025-Guideline_final_20250915.pdf
Publication date: 2025-01
Date basis: Version 3.0 publication month in the federal guideline.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Coverage: Recommendation 14 and its supporting saffron discussion, printed pages 66–68.
Format: Clinical guideline scope digest

Recommendation 14 expresses a weak preference against using saffron and several other supplements to treat chronic insomnia disorder. Its saffron evidence discussion cites the Pachikian extract trial, DOI 10.3390/nu13051473, and rates the evidence very low. The work group found the subjective outcomes and actigraphy insufficient to support clinical use, and describes one participant's report of palpitations resolving after discontinuation.

The guideline weighs harms, imprecision, uncertain supplement quality and potential delay of effective treatment. The recommendation applies to chronic insomnia care and provides no independent participant-level estimate or ground-saffron study in Parkinson disease. The reviewed trial is a member of the earlier Munirah synthesis. Treat the recommendation and evidence review as context for the already tracked trials.
