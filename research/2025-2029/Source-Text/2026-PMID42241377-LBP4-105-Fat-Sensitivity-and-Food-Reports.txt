The cross-sectional study reports questionnaires and laboratory
fat-taste sensitivity measurements from 117 young Lebanese adults.
The dietary tables include olive oil, eggs, Chicken Taouk and a
thyme-containing manakish. A single fruit row pools apricot, plum
and peach. Food-frequency and preference measures are reported by
sensitivity groups.

These results concern dietary patterns rather than a Parkinson
intervention. The oil grade, individual grouped fruit estimates
and frozen symptom outcomes are unavailable in the poster.
