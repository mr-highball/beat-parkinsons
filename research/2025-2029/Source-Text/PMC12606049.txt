Title: A Causal Association Between Drug Use and Cognitive Impairment: A Two-Sample Mendelian Randomization Study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12606049/
PMID: 41220200
PMCID: PMC12606049
DOI: 10.1002/brb3.71057
Publication date: 2025-11-11 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The analysis uses genetic instruments to examine medication use and different cognitive diagnoses. Its exposures are medication proxies and its endpoints are cognitive disorders, without any tested fig intake or visual symptom rate.
