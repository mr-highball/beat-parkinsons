Round 1 claims: 0559

The eye-movement study enrolls 128 Parkinson patients and 28 healthy controls and assesses eye-movement measures before and after levodopa in the Parkinson cohort.

The comparison concerns medication responsiveness and eye-movement biomarkers. It does not measure an oral raw-fig effect on rest tremor.
