Watermelon and cherry cues in Parkinson-plus smell testing
Publication: 2025-04-25
Source: https://ruralneuropractice.com/content/150/2025/0/1/pdf/JNRP-263-2024.pdf
Coverage: Zubair Sarkar and Ruchika Tandon, Journal of Neurosciences in Rural Practice 16(2), 217–223. Original first-online date April 25, 2025. Original item results and cohort definitions.
DOI: 10.25259/jnrp_263_2024

The study included 14 participants with multiple system atrophy, 16 with progressive supranuclear palsy and 30 controls. UPSIT item results identify watermelon and cherry odor labels. The watermelon item gave mean correct scores of 0.21, 0.13 and 0.83 in the three groups in the reported item tables. The cohorts and odor task provide diagnostic context without an assigned culinary preparation.
