Title: Macadamia oil alleviates brain injury induced by potassium dichromate via modulation of galectin-3/caspase-3 and Sitruin1
PMID: 40818780
DOI: 10.1016/j.fitote.2025.106821
Publication date: 2025-08-14
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40818780/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0367326X25004472
Text coverage: Original author abstract and publisher preview. The full paper and PDF were inaccessible during review.

Salama and colleagues studied macadamia kernel oil in a rat model of potassium dichromate brain injury. The abstract reports 47.53% oleic and 16.48% palmitoleic acid in the tested oil. It describes inflammatory and oxidative brain marker differences and lower caspase-3 and GFAP staining in cerebellar cortex under the oil intervention. The accessible text supplies no rest-tremor or separate motor behavior result and no standardized Parkinson model. Oil grade, allocation, exposure route and group denominators remain unverified from the accessible material. Tissue and in-vitro antioxidant changes do not establish a signed motor outcome.
