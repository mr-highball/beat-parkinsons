Title: Efficacy and safety of medicine-food homology Shenqi paste in older adults with diabetic sarcopenia: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 41763617
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2026.121444
First published: 2026
Authors: Hua L, Wang Y, Yuan X, Wang R, Li X, Fan Y
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41763617/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (n=90 randomized, n=74 secondary outcomes) in older adults (>=60 y) with diabetic sarcopenia testing Shenqi paste (an 8-ingredient TCM formula containing Astragalus, Codonopsis, Polygonatum, Dioscorea, Poria, Eucommia, Hordeum vulgare, and Ophiopogon). Improved handgrip strength, SMI, chair-stand time (all interact p<0.001), and secondary sleep quality and depression scores (p<0.05). Limits: 8-herb formula cannot isolate barley.
