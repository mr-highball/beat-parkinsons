Title: Green extraction of bioactive polysaccharides from Zanthoxylum bungeanum using deep eutectic solvents
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13694982/
PMID: 42927227
PMCID: PMC13694982
DOI: 10.1016/j.fochx.2026.104612
Publication date: 2026-10-13
Source depth: Original full article and its references.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27

The 2026 study compares chemical extraction from Zanthoxylum bungeanum leaf and pericarp. It measures extraction yields, antioxidant assay results and physical or blood-compatibility properties of formulated hydrogels. Under the reported optimized conditions, extracted yields are 25.88% pericarp and 29.79% leaf, compared with 6.27% and 12.13% in the water extraction comparison. These are laboratory yields, not symptom or efficacy outcomes.

The introduction cites the separate Zhou and Yang 2023 mouse exercise-fatigue study using a leaf polysaccharide. The new paper does not repeat the animal experiment, provide its arm-level outcomes or measure fatigue. Using the reference to document the historical lead does not add an independent fatigue effect. The isolated fractions and laboratory endpoints differ from ground culinary pericarp and Parkinson fatigue.
