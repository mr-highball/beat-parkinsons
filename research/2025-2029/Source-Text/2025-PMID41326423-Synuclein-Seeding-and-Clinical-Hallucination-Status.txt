Title: Quantification of cerebrospinal fluid α-synuclein seeds by endpoint dilution seed amplification assay in Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 41326423
PMCID: PMC12775113
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-01221-7
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12775113/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2025-12-01
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch original article JATS, with complete body.

The study quantifies cerebrospinal fluid alpha-synuclein seed activity through endpoint dilution. It compares assay estimates by baseline symptoms, including visual hallucination status. The clinical comparison is presence rather than frequency or a response to an exposure, and dried fig intake was not measured.
