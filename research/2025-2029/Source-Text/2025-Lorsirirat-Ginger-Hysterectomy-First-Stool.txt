Title: The Efficacy of Oral Ginger Powder in Prevention of Postoperative Ileus after Benign Gynecologic Hysterectomy: A Randomized Controlled Trial
DOI: 10.14456/tjog.2025.27
Source: https://he02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/tjog/article/view/271390
Original PDF: https://he02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/tjog/article/download/271390/186165
Published: 2025-05-28, Thai Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology 33, 266–275.
Archive scope: Factual summary of original Methods, Table 2 and Figures 2–3.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Fifty-six abdominal hysterectomy patients for benign disease were allocated 28 per arm to ginger powder capsules or placebo. The paper gives 500 mg per capsule, two capsules after meals three times daily, starting after postoperative oral intake. First flatus occurred at 29.5 versus 38.9 hours, difference 9.31 hours, 95% CI 4.2–14.3. Mean time to first defecation was 45.8 versus 58.5 hours, difference 12.6 hours, 95% CI 4.5–20.8. The article notes a later start of feeding and treatment in this protocol than in the separate Siriraj enhanced-recovery trial. This is a postoperative time-to-event comparison. Repeated bowel outcomes, chronic constipation severity and a Parkinson population were outside the study scope. Registration identity remains unverified.
