Title: Targeting dopaminergic neuronal death: Luteolin as a therapeutic modulator in Parkinson's disease
Publication year: 2025
PMID: 40255448
PMCID: PMC12003224
DOI: 10.1007/s13205-025-04267-9
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12003224/
Coverage: Factual extract of the original article body. Round 1 food exposure and outcome scope.

The background lists parsley, spinach, onion and tomato as dietary sources of luteolin. The investigators used purchased isolated luteolin in a rat Parkinson model and examined motor behavior and laboratory outcomes. The study did not feed the listed foods or test their prepared forms. Its motor outcomes cannot isolate effects of fresh parsley, steamed spinach, cooked tomato or raw onion on clinical Parkinson symptoms.
