Original source scope
The verified mouse study compares levodopa alone with levodopa plus isolated 6-shogaol. The add-on improved the reported gut motility measure, while pole-test motor improvement was not detectably greater with the addition.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Pole descent and turning are motor proxies and the report gives no separately measured clinical rigidity outcome. Isolated oral constituent and levodopa differ from grated culinary ginger.

This preclinical study by the same Biomolecules & Therapeutics group assigned male C57BL/6 mice to normal control, MPTP/probenecid, levodopa, or oral 6-shogaol plus levodopa arms to evaluate motor performance, gastrointestinal motility, striatal dopamine/levodopa levels, and brain-gut metabolomics.

While levodopa improved motor deficits without restoring bowel transit, oral 6-shogaol coadministration preserved levodopa motor benefits, restored gastrointestinal motility, and increased striatal dopamine and levodopa concentrations. The study tested an isolated ginger compound in a mouse toxin model rather than culinary ground or grated ginger in humans and did not score dyskinesia (abnormal involuntary movements).
