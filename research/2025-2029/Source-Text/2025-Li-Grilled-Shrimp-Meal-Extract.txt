Li and colleagues published February 7, 2025. Table 2 lists 10 grilled-shrimp consumers across 11 diners, with five illness cases among consumers. All six case descriptions report fatigue and dizziness, with additional symptoms in some diners. The report does not record visual hallucinations or a prespecified hallucination measure. The investigation implicated contaminated vegetable and tofu soup. Table 2 prints a shrimp risk ratio of 0.500, confidence interval 0.269–0.929 and p=1.000. The entries are internally inconsistent, and the small mixed meal offers no reliable isolated shrimp contrast.
