Registered flaxseed oil comparison with an executive composite
First posted 2026-02-06. Official source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07392723
Coverage: Factual official registry extract. Checked 2026-11-16.
This pilot plans a masked randomized comparison of flaxseed oil supplying 2.6 g/day of alpha-linolenic acid versus corn oil for six months. It enrolls older adults with mild cognitive impairment and at least one APOE4 allele. Global cognition is primary. The secondary executive composite averages z-scores from Trail Making A and B, forward and backward digit span, and animal and vegetable fluency.
The checked record reports recruiting with no posted results. It does not describe a Parkinson cohort or specify pressing method.
