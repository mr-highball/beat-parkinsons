Original study scope
The article allocates 15 male Wistar rats to control, rotenone and rotenone plus turmeric extract. The author abstract describes oral extract at 1,100 mg/kg. The main text describes the regimen as three weeks of rotenone plus three weeks of Curcuma, and the abstract describes concurrent treatment. Timing is therefore uncertain. The original cylinder analysis and Figure 5 report no detected rearing difference for Curcuma versus rotenone alone (Tukey p>.05), despite a broadly favorable statement about motor function in the abstract. Forelimb asymmetry persisted in both exposed groups. Hippocampal response patterns differed in the electrophysiology comparisons.

Continuing evidence gaps
This original does not report a bar catalepsy result or passive rigidity examination. The similar dose and overlapping experimental group in the 2025 MDS report require cohort reconciliation. The MDS abstract uses four arms and a different stated rotenone dose. Keep those arms distinct from this three-group experiment unless their actual animal enrollment and test schedules can be matched. The Figure 5 comparison provides a contextual null for turmeric extract and rearing, without a grated-turmeric Parkinson estimate.
