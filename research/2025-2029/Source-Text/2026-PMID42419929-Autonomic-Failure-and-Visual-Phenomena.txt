Title: A case of pure autonomic failure with a long-term 45-year clinical course since the initial detection of abnormal blood pressure fluctuation
PMID: 42419929
DOI: 10.5692/clinicalneurol.cn-002226
Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/clinicalneurol/66/8/66_cn-002226/_html/-char/en
First publication date: 2026-07-08 (publisher advance publication).
Coverage: Complete publisher article, clinical course and provocation test.

The article follows longstanding pure autonomic failure. During later follow-up a visual misidentification is described, and Parkinson disease or dementia with Lewy bodies was not confirmed under the authors' clinical assessment. A glucose challenge lowered supine blood pressure, which the authors interpret as postprandial hypotension. The challenge does not report inducing the visual event. No dried fig was identified and no diet-specific frequency estimate was made.
