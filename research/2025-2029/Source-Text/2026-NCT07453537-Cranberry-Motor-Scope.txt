First posted March 6, 2026. Recruiting healthy adults for cranberry juice or matched placebo over 70 days. Motor accuracy appears in the summary as part of multitasking, but no standalone graded bradykinesia or specific clinical motor outcome appears in the current outcome list. No results. Cranberry juice and cooked whole cranberry are different exposures.

Official update posted November 25, 2026. Still recruiting and reports no results. The descriptive summary mentions motor accuracy as multitasking context. The structured outcomes list cognitive task performance, mood, anxiety, fatigue, sleep and physiologic outcomes, with no separate bradykinesia or motor-accuracy endpoint.
