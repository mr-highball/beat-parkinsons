The 250 mg PeptiSleep subgroup compared brown-rice protein hydrolysate with microcrystalline cellulose. It randomized 30 and analyzed 26 per protocol. Oura ring algorithms estimated REM as 21–23% of sleep in both arms and a largest between-arm difference in change of 11.63 minutes across reported windows, with no clear between-arm difference. Secondary analyses were exploratory without correction for multiplicity. No dream enactment or REM electromyography is reported.
