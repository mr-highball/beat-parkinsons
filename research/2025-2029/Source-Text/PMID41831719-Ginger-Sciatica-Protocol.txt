Published sciatica ginger extract pain protocol
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13489092/
PMID: 41831719
PMCID: PMC13489092
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnesp.2026.103117
Registration: NCT06817018
Coverage: Original protocol first published March 12, 2026; linked official registration

The planned blinded comparison assigns supercritical carbon-dioxide ginger-root extract or placebo to 80 adults with chronic sciatica for eight weeks. It schedules pain-associated outcomes at baseline and study end. The official registry names the Brief Pain Inventory and Short-Form McGill Pain Questionnaire. Outcomes remain pending. The preparation and population differ from grated ginger and Parkinson pain.
