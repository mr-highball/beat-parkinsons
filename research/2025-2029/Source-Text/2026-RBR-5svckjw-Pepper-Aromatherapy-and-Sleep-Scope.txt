Title: Essential oil comparison for smoking, stress, anxiety, and sleep in health workers
Source: https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-5svckjw/
Registration: RBR-5svckjw
Publication date: 2026-03-03
Date basis: First registration shown on the Brazilian clinical trials registry.
Source depth: Official ReBEC trial record, intervention and posted results sections.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27

The intervention section describes 29 healthcare workers who smoke receiving either black pepper (Piper nigrum) or Angelica essential oil by skin application with inhalation. It gives 16 and 13 participants and a 60-day duration. The baseline/results sections instead describe 35 randomized, 20 and 15 with six later losses, and a 30-day intervention. There is no placebo comparator. Fields for first result posting or publication give 2024 dates, earlier than the displayed 2026 registration. The record does not reconcile these differences.

Insomnia or sleep quality appears in the title. The posted results describe smoking measures and contain no group-level sleep result. The aromatherapy route, preparation and participant population differ from ground culinary pepper and insomnia in Parkinson disease.
