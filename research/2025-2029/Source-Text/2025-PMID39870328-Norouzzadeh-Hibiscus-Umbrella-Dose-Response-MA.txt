Title: Efficacy and safety of Hibiscus sabdariffa in cardiometabolic health: An overview of reviews and updated dose-response meta-analysis
PMID: 39870328
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2025.103135
First published: 2025
Authors: Norouzzadeh M, Hasan Rashedi M, Azizi N, Teymoori F, Farhadnejad H, Mirmiran P
Journal: Complementary Therapies in Medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39870328/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Umbrella review and updated dose-response meta-analysis of 26 randomized controlled trials (n=1,797 participants) evaluated via Cochrane RoB, AMSTAR-II, GRADE, and ICEMAN. H. sabdariffa dose-dependently lowered SBP and DBP compared with placebo and other teas, showing no significant difference from antihypertensive drugs and moderate credibility for therapeutic BP reductions >10 mmHg especially in adults aged >50 years and trials >4 weeks. Adverse event risks were not elevated overall, though authors emphasized careful monitoring with therapeutic/long-term dosing.
