Title: Adherence, safety, bioavailability, and acceptability of amaranth grain flatbread during pregnancy: evidence from a cluster randomized trial in Sidama region, Ethiopia.
PMID: 41947250
PMCID: PMC13173720
DOI: 10.1186/s40795-026-01315-2
Registry ID: NCT06536153
First published: 2026
Authors: Mussie L, Belayneh M, Guillen-Grima F, Yoseph A
Journal: BMC nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41947250/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cluster randomized controlled trial (Mussie et al., 2026, BMC Nutr, PMC13173720, NCT06536153) in 306 pregnant women in Sidama, Ethiopia (n=293 analyzed) over 6 months comparing 200 g/day flatbread made from 70% amaranth grain and 30% chickpea vs maize flatbread. The bundled amaranth-chickpea flatbread increased hemoglobin (adjusted beta +2.67 g/dL, 95% CI 2.41-2.91) and reduced iron deficiency anemia (ARR 0.47, 95% CI 0.26-0.84). Minor transient GI symptoms did not differ significantly (7.8% vs 5.9%, ARR 1.31, 95% CI 0.57-3.02). Qualitative focus groups reported cultural acceptability similar to injera and perceived higher energy/less tiredness, but no quantitative fatigue or constipation severity scale was administered and amaranth was bundled with 30% chickpea.
