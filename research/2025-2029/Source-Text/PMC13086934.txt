Title: Clinical correlates of a negative cerebrospinal fluid α-synuclein seed amplification assay result in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13086934/
PMID: 41980956
PMCID: PMC13086934
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-026-01346-3
Publication date: 2026-04-14 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Sixty-two of 473 participants with a Parkinson diagnosis at follow-up were negative on a CSF alpha-synuclein assay. The study compares biomarker-defined phenotype and diagnostic change, with no dietary fig intake or episode response.
