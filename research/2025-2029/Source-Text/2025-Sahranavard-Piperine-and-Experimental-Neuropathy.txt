Sahranavard T et al. Piperine relieves neuropathic pain induced by paclitaxel in mice. Acta Neurobiol Exp. 84(4):332–340. Publisher dates first release January 14, 2025. PMID 39812529. DOI 10.55782/ane-2024-2590.
Original: https://ane.pl/index.php/ane/article/view/2590

Forty-eight male mice were divided into six groups. Figures 2–3 report lower thermal hyperalgesia for all isolated piperine doses versus paclitaxel control. The mechanical paw withdrawal threshold was higher at the two higher doses, with an inconclusive low-dose comparison. These are experimental mouse sensory outcomes.
