Sponsor sleep scale comparison in the corn oil control trial
Source: https://live.euronext.com/en/products/equities/company-news/2025-03-26-aelis-farma-announces-final-analysis-landmark-phase-2b
Coverage: Factual summary of original sponsor disclosure.

The sponsor's March 26, 2025 disclosure for NCT05322941 gives end-of-treatment MOS Sleep Scale values of 355.4 ± 5.31 for corn-oil placebo and 364.2 ± 5.2 with AEF0117 1 mg (p=.24). It does not identify the analysis denominator or what the error terms represent. This is a sponsor disclosure from the drug trial with no structured registry results. The data do not identify an effect of corn oil itself.
