Title: Agaricus bisporus polysaccharides alleviate high-fat diet-induced cognitive impairment via microbiota-gut-brain axis modulation.
PMID: 42692712
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2026.120317
First published: 2026
Authors: Fu, Ye, Qiu
Journal: Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42692712/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Mouse high-fat diet study showing Agaricus bisporus polysaccharides (ABP) alleviated anxiety-like behavior and cognitive deficits, increased hippocampal BDNF and synaptic plasticity genes, reduced microglial TNF/IL-1beta, and enriched Akkermansia/Bacteroides and SCFAs. Limits: isolated polysaccharides in HFD mice; outweighed clinically by three null human A. bisporus RCTs.
