Title: The effect of sulforaphane on autism spectrum disorder: systematic review and meta-analysis.
PMID: 40458076
PMCID: PMC12127520
DOI: 10.17179/excli2025-8239
First published: 2025
Authors: Wang R, Ren Z, Li Y
Journal: EXCLI journal
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12127520/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 6 sulforaphane RCTs in ASD. Significant pooled reductions in total ASD symptoms (SMD -0.27) and aberrant behavior (SMD -0.43), whereas effects on anxiety, irritability, sensory sensitivity, and social motivation were not statistically significant. Context-only secondary synthesis.
