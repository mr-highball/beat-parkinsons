Fried or roasted potatoes in a Parkinson case-control questionnaire
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13347557/
PMID: 41148145
PMCID: PMC13347557
DOI: 10.1177/1877718X251388058
Coverage: Original article Table 2 and clinical outcome scope checked; journal first available in 2025

The food-frequency questionnaire asked 680 Parkinson cases and 612 controls about a combined fried or roasted potato category. The paper evaluates dietary patterns and case status. Its clinical descriptions do not give daily OFF duration by that food. The survey combines frying and roasting and does not report an equivalent isolated preparation comparison. It is the same study family used in other protocol notes for pooled fish and individual carrot terms.
