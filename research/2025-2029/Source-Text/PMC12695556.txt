This narrative review discusses saffron, constituents and exercise as distinct and potentially complementary exposures. The Parkinson review covers cell and animal models, and cites a separate patient mood trial. The cited rigidity outcomes concern resistance training or composite motor scores, not a symptom-specific saffron estimate. It discusses fatigue in the context of exercise training, quality of life and proposed mechanisms. Orange in the pharmacology section describes pigment color, and falls and apathy descriptions do not identify orange fruit or dates.
