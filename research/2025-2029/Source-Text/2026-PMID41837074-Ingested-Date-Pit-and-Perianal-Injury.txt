Title: Fournier's gangrene caused by accidental ingestion of a date pit: a case report
PMID: 41837074
PMCID: PMC12981825
DOI: 10.1097/RC9.0000000000000185
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12981825/
Publication: 2026-02-13 electronic publication
Coverage: Original NCBI JATS full case.

The case describes an adult presenting with severe perianal pain and injury. The treating clinicians attributed the subsequent complication to a retained foreign body identified as a date pit. This is a separate mechanically mediated safety report without an exposure comparator or incidence estimate. It does not measure an effect of dried date flesh in Parkinson disease.
