Title: Clinical Characterization of Probable Dementia with Lewy Bodies Based on the Initial Clinical Presentation in a Latin American Low- and Middle-Income Country
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12890269/
PMID: 41525410
PMCID: PMC12890269
DOI: 10.1159/000550415
Publication date: 2026-01-12 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The retrospective cohort stratifies dementia with Lewy bodies by initial cognitive, motor or behavioral symptoms. Cognitive-onset patients progressed to dementia sooner in the observed cohort. Onset phenotype and later clinical domain observations provide no food-timed changes in visual episode count.
