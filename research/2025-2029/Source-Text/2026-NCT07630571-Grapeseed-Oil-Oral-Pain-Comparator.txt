Title: Oral HA35 trial and grapeseed-oil pain comparator
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07630571
Publication date: 2026-06-05
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Original access depth: registry
Registration: NCT07630571

A completed 30-participant crossover registers a grapeseed-oil solution containing an additional component, saline and other comparisons for experimentally induced oral discomfort. Results were unposted at review. Processing grade is unreported and the oil cannot be separated from the additional component. This is a targeted pain-outcome gap rather than evidence for a clinical direction.
