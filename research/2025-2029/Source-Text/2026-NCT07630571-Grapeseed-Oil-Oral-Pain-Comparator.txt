Title: Oral HA35 trial and grapeseed-oil pain comparator
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07630571
Publication date: 2026-06-05
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Original access depth: registry
Registration: NCT07630571

The structured registry reports 30 healthy adults aged 18–65 randomized in parallel to six groups. The official title calls the trial single-arm. The detailed description likewise lists six groups, and the registered material does not establish a crossover. The last posted update is September 10, 2026, and has no results.

The six groups combine one of two induced oral-discomfort challenges with HA35 solution, C60 in grapeseed oil, or saline. The registered pain outcomes use separate 0–10 rating schedules after treatment. The challenge involving horseradish uses baseline, 3 and 30 seconds. The challenge involving chili oil uses baseline, 30, 60 and 120 seconds. The site of exposure and brief time scale differ from the frozen pain claim. The comparator includes C60 and the record gives neither oil pressing grade, an oil-only arm, nor an isolated effect estimate.
