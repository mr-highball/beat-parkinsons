The Korean counterpart calls the intervention an ethanol extract from dried ginger rhizome. The automated translation sometimes renders the Korean drug name as health. The disclosed abnormal involuntary movement results match the prior WO report. Claim form, oral-use possibilities and the outcomes in another publication do not establish the experimental administration route in the actual animals. The earlier WO source and this counterpart are one evidentiary family.

November 25 check of the Korean original. On page 8, Example 1 defines the animal groups. On page 9, Example 2, paragraphs 0053–0060, identifies axial, limb and orolingual AIM subscores and gives the time series and AUC in tables 2 and 3. Figure 3 on page 12 identifies the levodopa/benserazide comparison. The dried ginger extract arm had lower means at each nonzero post-dose observation. The displayed AUC scale is about one-quarter of a simple trapezoidal integration using minutes, across all five active and comparator arms. The relative direction is stable across the common scale. No sample size, animal administration route, masking or exact pairwise interval was recovered.
