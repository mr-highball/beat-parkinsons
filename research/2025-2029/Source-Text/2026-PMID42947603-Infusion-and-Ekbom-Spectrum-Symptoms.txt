Title: Ekbom Spectrum Psychosis Associated with 24-hour Continuous Subcutaneous Foslevodopa/Foscarbidopa Infusion in Parkinson's Disease.
PMID: 42947603
PMCID: PMC13720495
DOI: 10.9758/cpn.26.1449
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13720495/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2026-10-04
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch original article JATS, with complete body.

A case follows visual misperceptions and then predominant somatic infestation sensations during continuous foslevodopa and foscarbidopa infusion. Earlier visual hallucinations appeared with another dopaminergic treatment. The history distinguishes modalities and medication periods and gives no dried fig exposure or food-specific visual episode frequency.
