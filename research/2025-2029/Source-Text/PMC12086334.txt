Title: Cerebrovascular co-pathology and cholinergic white matter pathways along the Lewy body continuum
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12086334/
PMID: 40391186
PMCID: PMC12086334
DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcaf173
Publication date: 2025-05-06 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The study compares MRI measures of cholinergic white-matter hyperintensity with global white matter and other biomarkers along the Lewy body spectrum. Core clinical features are examined in relation to imaging, without a dried fig exposure or episode rate.
