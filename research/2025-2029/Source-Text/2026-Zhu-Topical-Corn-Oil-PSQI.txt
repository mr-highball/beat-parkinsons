Zhu S, Wu C, Pu B, et al. Front Pain Res (Lausanne). 6:1637449. First public Jan 5, 2026 despite the 2025 volume/DOI label. PMID 41561473; PMC12813113; DOI 10.3389/fpain.2025.1637449. Original Methods, Table 1, Table 5 and section 3.4. Reported registration ChiCTR2200056614.

134 randomized; 57 active and 60 comparison completers. The four-week acupoint treatments differed as mixtures. A topical white-mustard substitute in the comparison arm included flour, cocoa powder, corn oil and one-tenth of the active white-mustard powder. No processing grade or isolated corn-oil arm. Global sleep quality (PSQI) mean (SD): active 7.88 (4.65) to 4.35 (3.27); comparison 7.83 (4.64) to 6.25 (4.18). Baseline-adjusted active minus comparison difference -1.95 (95% CI -3.18 to -0.72). The prose gives p=.002 and the table prints p<.002. Global sleep quality in a pain cohort is different from insomnia severity after consuming refined corn oil.
