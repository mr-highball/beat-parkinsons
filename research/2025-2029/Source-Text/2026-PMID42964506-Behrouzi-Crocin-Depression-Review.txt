Title: Crocin dose-response review for depressive and anxiety symptoms
First published: 2026-10-30
PMID: 42964506
DOI: 10.1155/da/8303882
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/da/8303882
Archive scope: Original review methods, Table 1, pooled Results and GRADE limitations checked. Its citations are study leads.

The authors included 14 randomized studies, with depression data in 13. The populations and outcomes vary. The model's pooled depression estimate per 30 mg/day increase in isolated crocin was −3.37 (95% CI −4.80 to −1.94) on the authors' standardized scale, with I²=97.1%. Egger and Begg tests in the Results also suggested asymmetry. The authors rate depression evidence moderate while describing very serious inconsistency. No independent cohort or Parkinson-specific culinary comparison is supplied by this synthesis. The Talaei 2015 abstract is one included add-on cohort. Mood pilot, selected sleep and mixed-comorbidity populations need individual review before judging fit.
