Title: Beans Improve Satiety to an Effect that Is Not Significantly Different from Beef in Older Adults: A Randomized, Crossover Trial.
PMID: 39954740
PMCID: PMC12107252
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2025.02.008
Registry ID: NCT05499819
First published: 2025
Authors: Fluit MJ, Adams BF, Ribau ZJ, Duncan AM
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39954740/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized crossover trial (Fluit et al., 2025, J Nutr, PMC12107252, NCT05499819) in 35 older adults (mean age 72.4 +/- 6.66 y) comparing breakfast tortillas containing 135 g black beans, 135 g red kidney beans, or 80 g beef. Black beans increased fullness and reduced hunger equivalently to beef, with no significant differences in appetite AUCs, ad libitum lunch intake, or 24-h energy intake. Demonstrates tolerability in older adults but assessed no Parkinson's motor, fatigue, constipation, or cognitive outcomes.
