Title: Breakfast oat products and glycemic responses
First published or posted: 2026-05-24
PMID: 42254431
PMCID: PMC13240552
DOI: 10.1002/fsn3.71791
Source URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13240552/
Text type: original-source evidence summary

Sasaki and colleagues report two open crossover pilot studies in healthy Japanese adults under UMIN000048192. Eleven and ten participants were analyzed. Comparisons use breakfast rice, fruit granola, baked oatmeal, cooked oats and skipped breakfast across the experiments. The paper reports glucose responses and gives no sleep results. The connection to the 22-person lunch narrative is unresolved.
