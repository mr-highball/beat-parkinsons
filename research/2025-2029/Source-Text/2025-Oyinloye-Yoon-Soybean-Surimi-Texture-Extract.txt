Soybean oil gradient and fish gel texture

Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2310-2861/11/4/295
Coverage: Original full formulation study, factual extract.

Oyinloye and Yoon. Gels, April 16, 2025. DOI 10.3390/gels11040295. The study combined fish myofibrillar protein with soybean or coconut oil at several concentrations and used rheology, mechanical texture analysis, microscopy and printing as outcomes. One no-oil control had 1396.4 g hardness and the 5% soybean-oil condition had 886.8 g. Softening also compromised printed layer stability. These are matrix-specific food texture measurements. The study did not give a clinical swallowing outcome or a Parkinson study.
