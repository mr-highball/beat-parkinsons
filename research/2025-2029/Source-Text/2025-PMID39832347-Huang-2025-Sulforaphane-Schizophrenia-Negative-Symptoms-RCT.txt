Title: Efficacy and Safety of Sulforaphane Added to Antipsychotics for the Treatment of Negative Symptoms of Schizophrenia: A Randomized Controlled Trial.

DOI: 10.4088/JCP.24m15272
Registry ID: NCT04521868
First published: 2025
Authors: Huang J, Chen A, Jin H, Liu F, Hei G, Teng Z
Journal: The Journal of clinical psychiatry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39832347/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twenty-four-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (NCT04521868) in n=77 analyzed antipsychotic-treated schizophrenia patients (53 in the Avmacol Extra Strength product arm versus 24 placebo). Sulforaphane significantly reduced PANSS negative symptom total score (p=0.01; 24-wk p<=0.001, d=0.8) and PANSS negative factor score (p=0.02), not mediated by depression or cognition. Limits: high-dose broccoli-seed glucoraphanin+myrosinase tablets in schizophrenia negative symptoms rather than PD apathy.

November 27, 2026 original and supplement recheck
The 2020 NCT04521868 record names two different sites in separate fields and has no posted results. Chen analyzed 42 long-stay patients at Jiangyin and reports no clear MCCB composite or subscore difference. The exact attention values remain unavailable. Huang analyzed 77 patients at Xiangya and used the PANSS cognitive factor, with no separate attention measure in the paper or supplement. Participant overlap remains unresolved, so the reports contribute one linked family of uncertain independence. Preparation and population differ from the frozen claim.
