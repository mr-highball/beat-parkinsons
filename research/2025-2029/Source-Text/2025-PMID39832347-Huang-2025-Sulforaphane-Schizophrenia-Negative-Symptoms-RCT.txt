Title: Efficacy and Safety of Sulforaphane Added to Antipsychotics for the Treatment of Negative Symptoms of Schizophrenia: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 39832347
DOI: 10.4088/JCP.24m15272
Registry ID: NCT04521868
First published: 2025
Authors: Huang J, Chen A, Jin H, Liu F, Hei G, Teng Z
Journal: The Journal of clinical psychiatry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39832347/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twenty-four-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (NCT04521868) in n=77 analyzed antipsychotic-treated schizophrenia patients (53 Avmacol Extra Strength sulforaphane 1,700 mg/d vs 24 placebo). Sulforaphane significantly reduced PANSS negative symptom total score (p=0.01; 24-wk p<=0.001, d=0.8) and PANSS negative factor score (p=0.02), not mediated by depression or cognition. Limits: high-dose broccoli-seed glucoraphanin+myrosinase tablets in schizophrenia negative symptoms rather than PD apathy.
