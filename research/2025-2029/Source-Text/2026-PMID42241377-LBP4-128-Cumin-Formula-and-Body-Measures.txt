The original poster reports 200 adults allocated across four
groups comparing a multi-ingredient cumin-containing formula with
capsule placebo and acupuncture with sham. The report gives
p<.05 for several weight and body-size measurements in the active
formula comparison.

The tested formulation includes several components and does not
isolate culinary cumin. Outcomes concern anthropometry. No
separate constipation, rigidity, anxiety or Parkinson comparison
is presented in the poster.
