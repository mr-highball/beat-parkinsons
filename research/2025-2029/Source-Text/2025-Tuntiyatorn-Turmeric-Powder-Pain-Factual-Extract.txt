Coverage: Original Methods and Tables 2–3, factual extract.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12502247/

In the double-blind hand osteoarthritis feasibility study, 13 were assigned whole turmeric powder capsules and 14 placebo. The primary result at one month was inconclusive. At three months the raw means favored the powder arm for both change in rest pain and change in pain during activity. Baseline means differed; these arithmetic differences do not independently estimate uncertainty. The report gives inconsistent wording about whether the later estimates in Table 3 are within-group or versus placebo. The intervention and placebo comparison supplies limited pain evidence in osteoarthritis.
