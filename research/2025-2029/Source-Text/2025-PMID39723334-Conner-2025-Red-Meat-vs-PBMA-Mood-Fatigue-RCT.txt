Title: Effect of Moderate Red Meat Intake Compared With Plant-Based Meat Alternative on Psychological Well-Being: A 10-Wk Cluster Randomized Intervention in Healthy Young Adults.
PMID: 39723334
PMCID: PMC11666937
DOI: 10.1016/j.cdnut.2024.104507
First published: 2025
Authors: Conner TS, Gillies NA, Worthington A, Bermingham EN, Haszard JJ, Knowles SO, et al.
Journal: Current developments in nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39723334/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

10-week randomized controlled trial in n=80 young adults comparing 3 weekly servings of New Zealand pasture-raised beef and lamb vs plant-based meat alternatives (PBMA): no significant between-group differences in DASS-21 depression, anxiety, or stress, nor in MFSI-SF fatigue or mood measures. Limits: young non-PD cohort, beef pooled with lamb.
