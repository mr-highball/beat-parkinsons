Title: Studies on the anticonvulsant potential of 6-gingerol, an active ingredient of ginger rhizome

Coverage: Paraphrased original pharmacokinetic methods and Tables 2–3. The author archive points to the same article.

Nieoczym and colleagues measured isolated 6-gingerol in mouse serum and brain after intraperitoneal administration. The brain-to-serum ratio was 0.185 at 60 minutes and 1.25 at 360 minutes. These measurements extend the mechanistic evidence beyond an artificial barrier and describe relative concentrations under the studied route. The larger ratio at a later time does not by itself show an increase in absolute brain concentration.

The paper assesses seizure models and pharmacokinetics. It does not study consumption of grated ginger, diagnosed Parkinson disease or an anxiety severity endpoint. Its independent experiment is a mechanism and exposure lead. It does not recover the Simon artificial membrane study's missing assay tables.
