Title: Behaviour Change for Parkinson's Disease: A Randomised Controlled Feasibility Study to Promote Physical Activity and Exercise Adherence Among People with Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40901341/
PMID: 40901341
PMCID: PMC12401215
DOI: 10.22540/JFSF-10-128
First public: 2025-09-01
Coverage: Factual extract from original methods and Tables 1–2.

Ahern and colleagues randomized 16 people with Parkinson disease and 14 completed. Both groups received exercise and education, including general dietary education. One group received additional behavior change techniques. Reported home exercise adherence was 70% versus 63%. The intervention did not individually assign date fruit, which is distinct from the education context. The adherence measure is not a food-related pain or apathy outcome.
