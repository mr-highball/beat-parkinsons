Title: Effects of Tart Cherry Juice Supplementation on Recovery from Exercise-Induced Muscle Damage in Athletes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

PRISMA systematic review and meta-analysis (Daab et al., 2026, Sports Med Open, PMC13057130) of 19 RCTs of tart cherry juice in trained athletes. Tart cherry juice improved isometric MVC recovery across timepoints (ES 0.63-4.82, high heterogeneity I2 69-93%) and reduced CRP post-exercise through 48 h (ES -0.46 to -0.73), whereas pooled effects on muscle soreness, CK, IL-6, TNF-alpha, and ROM were NOT statistically significant (very low to moderate certainty). Important competing synthesis highlighting inconsistency in pooled soreness.
