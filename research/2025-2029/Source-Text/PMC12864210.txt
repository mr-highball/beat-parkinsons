Title: Exploring Dynamic Alpha Band Connectivity in Parkinson's Disease: A Novel Approach to Postural Control Assessment Using the BioVRSea Paradigm
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12864210/
PMID: 41627573
PMCID: PMC12864210
DOI: 10.1007/s10548-025-01170-7
Publication date: 2026-02-02 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The BioVRSea study pairs a virtual balance paradigm with EEG in Parkinson disease. Postural control and dynamic connectivity are recorded during the visuomotor task. This visual stimulation differs from clinical visual hallucinations and there is no food comparison.
