Abstract reports two elderly patients and numeric pain 5 to 3. Methods, Results and Table 1 instead describe one 65-year-old woman, pain 5 to 2, and uric acid 8.0 to 4.1 mg/dL. A decoction was made from ten fresh celery leaves and administered for seven days alongside low-purine diet and hydration advice. No control is described; the preparation was boiled and the patient was not reported to have Parkinson disease.
