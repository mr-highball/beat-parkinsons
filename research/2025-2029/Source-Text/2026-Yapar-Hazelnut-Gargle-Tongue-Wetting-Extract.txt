Title: Herbal Gargle for Dry Mouth and Care: In vitro and Ex vivo Evaluations
Authors: Evren Algın Yapar and colleagues
Year: 2026
PMID: 42337897
DOI: 10.2174/0113862073411825251212024407
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42337897/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the publicly accessible primary abstract.

The study measured how much liquid was retained on excised sheep tongues in a model oral chamber and used a texture assay to measure adhesiveness. The named combination consisted of 4% hazelnut oil, 2% cinnamon leaf oil, and a 0.5% carboxymethyl cellulose solution. The abstract describes high retention and reduced adhesiveness for the formula but gives no group estimates or exact oil processing. This is a compound local wetting comparison. It does not measure production of saliva or swallowing, drooling or voice difficulty in patients.
