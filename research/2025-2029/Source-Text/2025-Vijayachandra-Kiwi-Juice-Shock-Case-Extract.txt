Vijayachandra and colleagues reported giddiness, breathlessness and swelling after street-vendor kiwi juice in a 51-year-old man. Initial systolic pressure was 60 mm Hg. Clinicians diagnosed anaphylactic shock followed by acute myocardial infarction. The report gives no orthostatic measurements or defined juice ingredients.
First online February 20, 2025. The acute allergic presentation, unspecified juice composition, and population do not provide an estimate of everyday Parkinson orthostatic lightheadedness.
