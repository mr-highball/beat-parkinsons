Factual extract of primary article in Spanish checked 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

Twenty menstruating volunteers, age 15–30, were informed about the proposed analgesic use and supplied with four 0.85 g confection units per participant. The reported weight is for the full unit, with no measured content of Rubus ulmifolius extract and no specified freezing method. Discussion mentions fruit and leaves, but the final amount of each plant part is not reported. The abstract names niacin while Results names thiamine. Questionnaires asked about cramp severity on a five-point scale and perceived relief. The authors report 75% considered it helpful. The baseline share at level four in prose (41.4%) does not agree with Figure 1's 10 of 20. Figures provide aggregate distributions and the article provides no paired participant data, concurrent control or blinded outcomes. Its population and pain subtype differ from Parkinson pain.
