Title: Efficacy of Cymbopogon citratus Aromatherapy Inhaler on Sleep Quality Among Smokers
Publication: Kumboyono and colleagues, June 8, 2026 early view
Source: https://www.benthamscience.com/article/156163
Coverage: factual extract of the accessible primary source

One hundred smokers aged 18–30 received a 3% Cymbopogon citratus essential-oil inhaler or a sunflower-oil placebo four times daily for 60 days. The abstract gives Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index means changing from 7.84 to 4.42 versus 7.68 to 7.54 (p < .001). Global sleep quality is distinct from witnessed dream enactment and the intervention was inhaled essential oil. The sample lacked a Parkinson cohort. The early-view article did not report a separate dream enactment result. Related nicotine-dependence registration NCT07366294 was screened, but cohort linkage was not assumed.
