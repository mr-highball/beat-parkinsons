Title: Establishing a robust triangulation framework to explore the relationship between hearing loss and Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11698951/
PMID: 39753591
PMCID: PMC11698951
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-024-00861-5
Publication date: 2025-01-03 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

UK Biobank analyses and genetic approaches compare hearing loss with subsequent Parkinson diagnosis risk, including hearing aid use as a separate observational variable. Auditory function and Parkinson incidence are distinct from Parkinson visual episode frequency after a food exposure.
