Title: Helicobacter pylori eradication therapy and clinical outcomes in Parkinson's disease: a double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial
PMID: 42579018
DOI: 10.1007/s10072-026-09307-7
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10072-026-09307-7
Published: 2026-08-11.
Archive scope: Original publisher abstract and WHO registry linkage. Full paper, statistical tables and protocol remain open. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

Thirty adults with Parkinson disease and a positive breath test were randomized to two-week eradication treatment or matched placebo. The primary 12-week ON-medication motor comparison reported a mean difference of −3.9 points, 95% CI −15.5 to 7.6, p=.49. Baseline adjustment remained inconclusive. Estimates for the aggregate rating scale, nonmotor symptoms and quality of life were also imprecise. The small study and wide intervals allow sizeable effects in either direction.

This was a drug regimen tested in infected patients. It does not isolate a food exposure or record a separate ingredient and symptom contrast. The registry contains broader outcome wording and a combined target sample field. Keep baseline prevalence screening and the randomized treatment analysis separate.
