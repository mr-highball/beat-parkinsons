Title: Dietary Fatty Acids Differentially Modulate Hippocampal Glutamate-Related Gene Expression in Mice: Sex-Specific in Vivo and in Vitro Effects
Publication date: 2026-08-09
PMID: 42572423
PMCID: PMC13454707
DOI: 10.1002/mnfr.70580
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13454707/
Text coverage: Distilled original methods, results and selected metabolite and transcription measures.

Male and female mice received standard chow, chow with 40% high-oleic sunflower oil, or chow with 40% palm-kernel oil for eight weeks. The authors report diet- and sex-dependent hippocampal changes. In females, the sunflower formulation increased the glutamate-to-glutamine ratio (table means 1.75 versus 1.55 for chow) without the same change in the glutamate-to-GABA ratio. The article extends earlier biochemical work and does not itself measure executive function or replicate the prior behavioral contrast. The results do not identify the effect of culinary cold-pressed oil in Parkinson disease.
