Title: Effect of Coconut Oil Pulling and Benzydamine Hydrochloride Mouthwash in the Management of Radiation-Induced Oral Mucositis: a Randomized Controlled Clinical Trial.
PMID: 41054470
PMCID: PMC12496380
DOI: 10.1007/s12663-025-02515-2
First published: 2025
Authors: Agarwal N, Shetty P, Shetty S, Dhanashree B, Sandeep KM
Journal: Journal of maxillofacial and oral surgery
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12496380/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled clinical trial (Agarwal et al., 2025, J Maxillofac Oral Surg, PMC12496380) in 127 adults with oral malignancies receiving radiotherapy comparing non-ingested coconut oil pulling (swish-and-spit oral rinse) vs benzydamine hydrochloride mouthwash. Coconut oil pulling reduced Candida counts, mucositis severity (p=2e-7), local oral mucositis pain (p<0.001), and trismus (p=0.006). Limits: non-ingested topical/mucosal rinse for radiation mucositis, NOT ingested dietary virgin coconut oil or systemic Parkinson pain.
