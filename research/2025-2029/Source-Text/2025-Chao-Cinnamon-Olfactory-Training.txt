Source: https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.70103
First online: 2025-09-05. PMID: 40908892.
Original abstract summary. In 60 adults with type 2 diabetes and mild cognitive impairment, 16-week training with six inhaled odors including cinnamon was compared with control. Authors report improved overall olfaction without numerical odor-domain values in the accessible abstract. Design, center, group size and intervention closely match NCT06327633, without a verified explicit paper-to-registry identifier in the accessible routes. The treatment bundles odorants and does not test consumption.
