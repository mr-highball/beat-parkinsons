Title: Speech subtypes are associated with worsened tremor and axial symptoms in Parkinson's disease patients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12320695/
PMID: 40761234
PMCID: PMC12320695
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2025.100373
Publication date: 2025-07-21
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC XML article body and embedded tables; separate supplements are outside this extract.

A cross-sectional specialist-clinic sample included 90 individuals with idiopathic Parkinson disease. Researchers compared acoustic speech subtypes against tremor and axial symptom measures. The tremor score pooled MDS-UPDRS item 2.10 with items 3.15–3.18. The latter range includes rest tremor and its constancy, alongside other tremor measures. The study did not report an isolated food effect on the corresponding rest-tremor item. The methods explicitly say sialorrhea item 2.2 was not collected. The comparison concerns speech subtypes, with no fig exposure.
