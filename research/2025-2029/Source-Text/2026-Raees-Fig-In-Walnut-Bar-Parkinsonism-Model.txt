Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

Raees and colleagues assigned 24 rats to four groups, six each. Table 1 lists common fig (Ficus carica) among five solid ingredients in the ground, baked energy bar, with coconut water as binder. The design compares the complete bar with control, a disease-only group, and a medication group. The publication does not report the source fig's initial raw or dry state or an arm that removes only fig.

The results narrative reports shorter maintained posture in the bar test versus the disease group at days 15 and 30. For the rotating rod, the narrative describes longer fall latency over time in the blend group. Figure 6's caption mentions an almond-bar comparison absent from the four listed groups. The caption label remains unreconciled. The outcomes concern catalepsy and motor coordination in a drug-induced rat model. Rest tremor appears only in the disease background.

Research gap: A separately measured resting tremor response and an isolated oral raw common fig contrast in diagnosed human Parkinson disease remain open. The baked mixture result supplies pathway and preparation context.
