Thirty-five marathon runners received a Vistula tart cherry product or calorie-matched placebo. A 200 mm fatigue scale was measured before and after the race and over 48 hours of recovery. The report gives no clear fatigue treatment or interaction effect. The abstract describes freeze-dried powder, while the Methods and conclusion describe spray-dried extract. The product and exertional fatigue context differ from eating dried cherries in Parkinson disease.
