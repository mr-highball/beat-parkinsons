# Oregano essential oil and rodent coordination in a toxicology thesis

Source https://repositorio.ufpb.br/jspui/bitstream/123456789/36657/1/JanaLuizaToscanoMendesDeOliveira_Tese.pdf
Coverage original thesis, printed pages 49–51 and 59–61

Oliveira studied oral Origanum vulgare essential oil in healthy animals. The UFPB record identifies an April 2011 thesis and 2025 repository availability. The 28-day rat comparison gives no clear rotating-rod retention change across oil groups. The original Graph 6 prints three animals. The open-field section describes more crossing and rearing in highest-concentration males. The study measured no stride length, Parkinson model or fresh-leaf exposure.
