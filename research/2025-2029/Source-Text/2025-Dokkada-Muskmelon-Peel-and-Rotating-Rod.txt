Coverage: Original PDF Methods and Tables 2–4.

Table 3 gives shorter time on the rotating rod for aqueous Cucumis melo peel extract tested alone. It reports 90.33 ± 2.95 and 72.00 ± 3.57 seconds in the two extract groups versus 153.16 ± 5.80 seconds in controls, means ± SEM for six observations per row. The abstract calls the result improved rotarod performance. The full results interpret shorter retention as muscle relaxation. The same paper reports more actophotometer activity in the extract groups. The exposure is aqueous rind extract in rats and the measured outcomes concern coordination and locomotion.
