Title: Lavender oil preparation Silexan is effective in mild-to-moderate major depression: a randomized, placebo- and reference-controlled trial.
PMID: 38558147
PMCID: PMC12500811
DOI: 10.1007/s00406-024-01783-2
First published: 2025
Authors: Kasper S, Volz HP, Möller HJ, Schläfke S, Klement S, Anghelescu IG, Seifritz E
Journal: European archives of psychiatry and clinical neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38558147/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Multicenter double-blind, double-dummy randomized controlled trial (Kasper et al., 2025, Eur Arch Psychiatry Clin Neurosci, PMC12500811, EudraCT 2020-000688-22) enrolling 498 treated/analyzed adults with mild-to-moderate major depressive disorder (MADRS 19-34; silexan 80 mg/d n=170, sertraline 50 mg/d n=171, placebo n=157) for 8 weeks (56 days). Primary MADRS total score reduction beat placebo by 2.17 points (95% CI 0.58 to 3.76, p<0.01) for Silexan and 2.59 points (1.02 to 4.17, p<0.01) for sertraline; Silexan also improved Sheehan Disability Scale by 2.40 points vs placebo (p<0.001). Eructation was the most frequent adverse event. Limits: proprietary oral essential oil capsule rather than brewed/dried culinary lavender; non-PD MDD cohort.
