Title: Clinical correlates of sleep profiles in dementia with Lewy bodies: a latent class approach
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13343939/
PMID: 42129862
PMCID: PMC13343939
DOI: 10.1186/s13195-026-02059-8
Publication date: 2026-05-13 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The 359-person clinical series groups dementia with Lewy bodies participants by sleep profiles and relates them to disease onset characteristics. A psychiatric-onset group and sleep symptom prevalence do not supply a dried fig exposure or a rate of recurrent visual episodes.
