SONA Nigella oil plan and reported stress experiment
Original SONA abstract S1-24, page 231
The 2025 SONA proceedings describe a restraint-stress experiment in a 6-OHDA animal model and list Nigella oil for future evaluation. The reported motor findings concern stress and the model. The proceeding supplies no administered Nigella oil comparison or result. It is retained as a lead for later source and study-result follow-up.
