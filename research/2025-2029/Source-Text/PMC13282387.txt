Title: Characterization of patients with isolated REM sleep behavior disorder and cerebrospinal fluid α-synuclein negativity
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13282387/
PMID: 42248896
PMCID: PMC13282387
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-026-01410-y
Publication date: 2026-06-05 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The polysomnography-confirmed isolated REM sleep behavior disorder cohort includes 191 participants and compares features by CSF assay status. It describes clinical evolution and markers of prodromal disease without an ingredient intake or response in Parkinson visual symptoms.
