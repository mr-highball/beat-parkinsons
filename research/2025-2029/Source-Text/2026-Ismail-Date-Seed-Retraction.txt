Coverage: factual digest of full publisher notice.

The publisher and editor retracted the paper after investigating concerns about values in Tables 1–5 and finding the raw data supplied by the authors unreliable. The original findings and conclusions cannot be used as valid behavioral evidence. One author agreed with the retraction and the others disagreed.
