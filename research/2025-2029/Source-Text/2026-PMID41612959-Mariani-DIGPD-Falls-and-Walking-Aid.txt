The prospective analysis included 415 people with Parkinson disease. The abstract reports estimated five-year incidences of 26.1% for falls and 2.1% for walking-aid need. Ten-year figures were 66.5% and 17%, respectively. Depression is listed as a predictor of falls. Freezing is listed as a predictor of falls and walking-aid need. These are cohort associations and leave the underlying model coefficients and missingness open at current access depth.

The registry identifies an observational cohort and lists motor and nonmotor symptom assessment. No identified material describes a lemon balm intervention or an outcome change after brewed tea. PMC lists 2027-01-30 as the release date, and the separately linked publisher supplement remains unread. Exact ingredient-pair review stays open pending full original access.
