Title: A New Phenotype–Genotype Correlation for FIG4 and Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12911447/
PMID: 40884084
PMCID: PMC12911447
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70319
First public: 2025-08-30
Depth: Factual summary of original case letter. The issue date is February 2026.

The clinical letter reports resting tremor in one person with a Parkinson phenotype and a homozygous FIG4 I41T genetic finding. The authors describe clinical responses during medication treatment. FIG4 identifies the gene, and the paper describes no dietary fig exposure.
