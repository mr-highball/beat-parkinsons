Title: Connectome-based brain fingerprints predict early cognitive decline in Parkinson's patients with minor hallucinations
Source: https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.17.649310
DOI: 10.1101/2025.04.17.649310
Publication date: 2025-04-22 (preprint version 1 posting)
Source depth: complete official bioRxiv PDF

The 2025 bioRxiv original uses 18 Parkinson participants with minor hallucinations and 14 without, plus healthy controls. It relates resting functional connectivity and concurrent cognitive performance; the source reports no longitudinal food-linked visual episode comparison. The 2026 journal article is a later report of this sample.

Original PDF: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.17.649310v1.full.pdf

Journal version: PMID 42859526, PMCID PMC13652722, DOI 10.1038/s44220-026-00657-x. See the June 2026 report for the peer-reviewed framing and 18/14/31 clinical/control group counts.
