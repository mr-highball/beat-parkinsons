Title: Computational screening of natural compounds from traditional Persian medicinal plant for the finding of potential antidepressant
PMID: 42168279
PMCID: PMC13503924
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-53032-2
Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-53032-2
Published: 2026-05-21.
Text coverage: Original article abstract, plant-selection results and interpretation.

The study screened candidate compounds associated with 11 plants, including lemon balm and saffron, using predicted target overlaps and molecular modeling. The reported lead is isolated carnosic acid, based on prediction and simulation. It does not assign a culinary ingredient, use a human or Parkinson cohort, or measure mood or depression severity. This is mechanism discovery context. It supplies no direction on the food hypotheses.
