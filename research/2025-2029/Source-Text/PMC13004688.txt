Title: A quantitative DOPA decarboxylase biomarker for diagnosis in Lewy body disorders
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13004688/
PMID: 41699128
PMCID: PMC13004688
DOI: 10.1038/s41591-026-04212-0
Publication date: 2026-02-16 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The diagnostic study analyzes DOPA decarboxylase in cerebrospinal fluid and other specimen types. Clinical grouping and biomarker performance address differential diagnosis in Lewy body disorders. No food exposure or visual event rate is measured.
