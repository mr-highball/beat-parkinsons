Factual extract from a protocol presentation
Original: Warz SI, Nygard OK, Tzoulis C, and Dierkes J. Dietary Risk Factors for Parkinson Disease, EPO6_127, International Congress of Nutrition 2025, p. 1257.
Published in Journal of Nutritional Science on July 23, 2026. Proceedings DOI: 10.1017/jns.2026.10126.
Planned population: prospective observational cohort of 4,000.
Exposure: food-frequency questionnaire assessments of niacin, tryptophan and foods rich in niacin.
Outcome: first prescription of selected Parkinson medications from a Norwegian prescription register, used as a proxy date for incident diagnosis.
Analysis: prospective covariate-adjusted Cox regression. The presentation supplies the investigators' hypothesis, with no observed effect estimates or completed analysis. It contains no food-specific daytime sleepiness outcome.
