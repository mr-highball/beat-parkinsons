Title: Effects of sulforaphane on negative symptoms and cognitive impairments in chronic schizophrenia patients: A randomized double-blind trial.
PMID: 40133020
DOI: 10.1016/j.jpsychires.2025.03.023
Registry ID: NCT04521868
First published: 2025
Authors: Chen H, Lu J, Zou T, Teng Z, Qin Y, Wu R
Journal: Journal of psychiatric research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40133020/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twenty-four-week double-blind RCT report linked to NCT04521868 analyzing n=42 chronic schizophrenia patients (28 sulforaphane vs 14 placebo). Sulforaphane reduced PANSS 5-factor negative score (p=0.007; time-by-group p=0.023) and original PANSS negative score (p=0.029), but produced NO significant effects on MATRICS Consensus Cognitive Battery composite or subscores. Same trial registration family as Huang 2025 (NCT04521868).
