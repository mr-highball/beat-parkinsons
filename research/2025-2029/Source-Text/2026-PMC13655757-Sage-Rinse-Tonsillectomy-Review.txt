The sage arm is from the 2004 active-comparator trial. The review lists postoperative bleeding in 140 children assigned sage rinse, with a reported 4.3 percent proportion. This is a bleeding outcome. The review supplies neither a second sage trial nor a culinary fresh-sage comparison for pain in Parkinson disease.
