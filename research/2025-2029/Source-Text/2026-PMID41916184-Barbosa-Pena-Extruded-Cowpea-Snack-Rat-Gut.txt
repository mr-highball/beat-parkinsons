Title: Ready-to-eat expanded parboiled brown rice and bean snack consumption promotes good protein quality and improves lipid and glycemic profiles and intestinal health.
PMID: 41916184
DOI: 10.1016/j.nut.2026.113184
First published: 2026
Authors: Barbosa Pena F, Stampini Duarte Martino H, Celi Lopes Toledo R, Duarte Villas Mishima M, Barreto Santos M, Antoniassi R, et al.
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41916184/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Thirty-day preclinical study (Barbosa Pena et al., 2026, Nutrition) in 29 male Wistar rats comparing casein control (n=8), extruded parboiled brown rice + cowpea snack (n=8), extruded cowpea snack (n=8), and protein-free control (n=5). Compared with casein control, both cowpea-containing snacks reduced triglycerides and increased fecal moisture and colonic goblet cell counts, while the extruded cowpea snack lowered total cholesterol and increased colonic longitudinal muscle thickness. Limits: healthy non-constipated rats fed extruded dry snacks rather than cooked whole black-eyed peas; no constipation severity or transit score.
