Title: Dietary nitrate supplementation suppresses sympathetic neural responses to mechanoreflex activation during static handgrip exercise in postmenopausal women.
PMID: 40929353
DOI: 10.1152/japplphysiol.00108.2025
First published: 2025
Authors: Manabe K, Yoo JK, Takeda R, Washio T, Fu Q
Journal: Journal of applied physiology (Bethesda, Md. : 1985)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40929353/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=10 postmenopausal women: 7-day nitrate-rich beetroot juice significantly prolonged time to fatigue compared with nitrate-depleted placebo (delta +79 s vs +9 s; p=0.03). Limits: small non-PD exercise sample.
