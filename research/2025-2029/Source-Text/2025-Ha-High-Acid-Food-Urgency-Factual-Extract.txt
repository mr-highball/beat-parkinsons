Title: Purported Bladder Irritant Intake in Women With Urgency Urinary Incontinence
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39841448/
DOI: 10.1097/SPV.0000000000001652
Publication: 2025, volume 31, pages 352–360
Coverage: Factual extract of primary abstract. Full source and supplemental coding list remain unresolved.

651 respondents with urgency urinary incontinence and 8,890 controls in NHANES data were compared. High-acid food prevalence was 50.3% and 44.3%, respectively. After adjustment, the association was OR 1.29, 95% CI 1.03–1.62. The accessible abstract does not identify an individual pineapple result, food coding, preparation, or a Parkinson cohort.
