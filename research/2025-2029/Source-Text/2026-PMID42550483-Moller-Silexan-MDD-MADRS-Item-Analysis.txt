Title: Effects of lavender oil preparation silexan on different symptoms of major depression - results from a randomized, controlled trial.
PMID: 42550483
PMCID: PMC13533546
DOI: 10.1093/ijnp/pyag041
First published: 2026
Authors: Möller HJ, Anghelescu IG, Volz HP, Seifritz E, Schläfke S, Kasper S
Journal: The international journal of neuropsychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42550483/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Pre-planned exploratory item-level analysis (Moller et al., 2026, Int J Neuropsychopharmacol, PMC13533546) of the LEAD MDD DB-RCT (PMID:38558147; N=498: silexan 170, sertraline 171, placebo 157; 8 weeks). Adjusted week-8 change vs placebo favored Silexan on 5 of 9 MADRS items: apparent sadness (-1.56 vs -1.24, p=0.014), reported sadness (-1.83 vs -1.49, p=0.011), reduced appetite (-0.75 vs -0.54, p=0.048), concentration difficulties (-1.40 vs -1.08, p=0.011), and lassitude (-1.46 vs -1.06, p=0.003). Crucially, MADRS item 4 'reduced sleep' was NULL (-1.39 vs -1.28, p=0.447) and item 8 'inability to feel' was borderline/non-significant (-1.25 vs -1.01, p=0.058). Authors explicitly note exploratory analysis without studywise multiplicity control; same cohort as PMID:38558147.
