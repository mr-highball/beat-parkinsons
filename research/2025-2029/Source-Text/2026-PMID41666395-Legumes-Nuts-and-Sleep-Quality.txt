Title: Legumes and nuts intake in relation to sleep duration and quality, oxidative stress and inflammation in Iranian adults
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41666395/
PMID: 41666395
DOI: 10.1039/d5fo04053a
Publication date: 2026
Coverage: Original author abstract and publisher bibliographic record. Publisher body was restricted.

A cross-sectional sample of 535 Iranian adults reported diet by food-frequency questionnaire and sleep through the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index. Higher combined legumes and nuts consumption was associated with lower odds of short sleep, OR 0.56 (95% CI 0.33–0.94), and poor sleep quality, OR 0.51 (95% CI 0.31–0.85), in the adjusted models.
These group-level observational estimates leave separate lentil preparations, measured insomnia severity, Epworth daytime sleepiness and Parkinson-specific effects unresolved.
