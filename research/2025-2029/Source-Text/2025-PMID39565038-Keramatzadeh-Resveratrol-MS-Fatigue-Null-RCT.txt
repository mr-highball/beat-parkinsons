Title: Effects of resveratrol supplementation on inflammatory markers, fatigue scale, fasting blood sugar and lipid profile in relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis patients: a double-blind, randomized placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 39565038
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2024.2425649
First published: 2025
Authors: Keramatzadeh S, Hosseini SA, Majdinasab N, Cheraghian B, Zilaee M
Journal: Nutritional neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39565038/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind randomized 8-week trial (IRCT20230315057731N1) in 55 patients with relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis receiving 500 mg/day isolated resveratrol vs placebo. TNF-alpha and MDA decreased (p<0.001), but fatigue scale remained completely unchanged (NULL). Isolated synthetic stilbene (exposure_fit remote from raw/frozen grapes).
