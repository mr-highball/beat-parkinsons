Title: Pharmacokinetic and Pharmacodynamic Interactions between Food or Herbal Products and Oral Anticoagulants: Evidence Review, Practical Recommendations, and Knowledge Gaps.
PMID: 39288907
DOI: 10.1055/s-0044-1790258
First published: 2025
Authors: Talasaz AH, McGonagle B, HajiQasemi M, Ghelichkhan ZA, Sadeghipour P, Rashedi S, Cuker A, Lech T
Journal: Seminars in thrombosis and hemostasis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39288907/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Evidence review of food/herbal interactions with oral anticoagulants (Talasaz et al., 2025, Semin Thromb Hemost), concluding that controlled clinical evidence indicates moderate cranberry juice intake (up to 240 mL/day) is generally safe with warfarin, consistent with Zikria et al. 2010 (PMID:20399311) finding no pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic interaction in 7 controlled trials despite sporadic bleeding/INR case reports (Tan & Lee 2021 PMID:32478963). Safety context for older Parkinson patients taking anticoagulants; supplies no PD symptom efficacy sign.
