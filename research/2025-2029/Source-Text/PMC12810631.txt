Title: Individual and institutional factors associated with urinary incontinence among nursing home residents
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12810631/
PMID: 40238962
PMCID: PMC12810631
DOI: 10.1111/jan.16986
First publication: 2025-04-16 (electronic publication date)
Coverage: PMC original Methods, results and resident-association tables

The investigators used a multilevel analysis of urinary incontinence among older nursing home residents. Pain appears among resident-level data. The final model identified associations with cognitive impairment, neurological disease and age. The exposure and outcome variables supply no comparison of dried date fruit and no Parkinson-specific pain or apathy outcome.
