Title: Chemical Constituents from the Leaves of Eruca sativa (Rocket Salad) Exhibiting Dual Cardioprotective and Antiplatelet Activities.
PMID: 40821514
PMCID: PMC12355429
DOI: 10.1021/acsomega.5c05585
First published: 2025
Authors: Zheng R, Park JW, Oh EB, Chang TS, Chin YW
Journal: ACS omega
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40821514/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Phytochemical and cellular laboratory study (Zheng et al., 2025, ACS Omega, PMC12355429) isolating 20 compounds from edible Eruca sativa (arugula/rocket salad) leaves, including a new cyclic thiocarbamate and four new kaempferol glycosides alongside known flavonoids. Confirms kaempferol glycosides and sulfur-containing thiocarbamate/glucosinolate chemistry in culinary arugula leaves; bioassays evaluated only H9c2 cardiomyocyte ROS/apoptosis and washed human platelet aggregation without animal or human pain or Parkinson's symptom endpoints.
