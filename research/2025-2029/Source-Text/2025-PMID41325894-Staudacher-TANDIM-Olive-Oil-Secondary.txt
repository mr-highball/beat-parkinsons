The post hoc analysis uses dietary data from 48 completers of TANDIM. Olive oil intake increased in the Mediterranean group. Across the pooled completers, increased olive oil intake was associated with the probability of a HADS depression response (reported model beta 0.55, 95% CI 0.10 to 1.10, p = .03). The authors found no other food-group association with HADS anxiety, HADS depression or overall IBS severity or response. Increased overall adherence predicted an anxiety response in the pooled analysis. The separate estimated change in anxiety score within the intervention group was inconclusive. The paper does not provide a constipation-specific oil regression.

The exploratory responder analysis had no separately assigned oil arm. The recorded exposure was olive oil intake, with preparation or grade unspecified for the item estimate. Other diet changes and multiple tested outcomes limit attribution. This is the same cohort as the randomized trial and the UEG 2023 abstract PP1101. The depression result belongs to that measured endpoint, and the paper supplies no independent Parkinson anxiety estimate. Round 1 has no olive oil and depression hypothesis.
