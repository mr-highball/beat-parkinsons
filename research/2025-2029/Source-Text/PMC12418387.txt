Title: Mapping the distribution of neurotransmitters to resting-state functional connectivity in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12418387/
PMID: 40933286
PMCID: PMC12418387
DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcaf308
Publication date: 2025-09-09 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The TRANSEURO baseline imaging analysis maps neurotransmitter-related signals and network measures in 30 Parkinson participants. The study compares molecular imaging and resting functional connectivity without a food intervention or visual outcomes after eating.
