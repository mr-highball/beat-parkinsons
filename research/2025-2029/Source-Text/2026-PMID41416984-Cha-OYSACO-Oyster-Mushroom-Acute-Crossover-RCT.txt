Title: A randomised controlled study to investigate the cognitive, mood, metabolic and anti-inflammatory effects of acute oyster mushroom intervention in healthy older adults.
PMID: 41416984
DOI: 10.1039/d5fo03075g
Registry ID: NCT05594329
First published: 2026
Authors: Cha S, Bell L, Fisher DR, Shukitt-Hale B, Zhang Z, Rodriguez-Mateos A, Williams CM
Journal: Food & Function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41416984/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
OYSACO acute 4-arm crossover RCT (NCT05594329) in 33 healthy older adults consuming noodle soup containing 0.5 (OM0.5), 1 (OM1), or 2 (OM2) servings of whole Pleurotus ostreatus vs calorie-matched control soup (OM0), separated by 1-week washouts, with mood, cognitive, and appetite assessments at baseline, 2, 4, and 6 h. Linear mixed modeling (baseline-adjusted) showed a significant decline in Positive Affect and concomitant increase in Mental Fatigue over the 6-h test day after control soup (OM0), whereas mood and mental fatigue did not worsen following OM interventions. Cognitive findings were mixed with no consistent pattern vs OM0. Postprandial inflammatory markers (nitrite, NOX-2, iNOS) were significantly lower after OM, while serum BDNF was unexpectedly dose-dependently LOWER after OM vs OM0. Limitations: acute 6-h test-day design, soup matrix vs roasted, healthy non-PD older adults, paywalled full numeric tables.
