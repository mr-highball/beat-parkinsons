IUNS Abstract OAP17_06. Journal of Nutritional Science 15:e62, July 23, 2026. Collection DOI 10.1017/jns.2026.10126. Printed page 670.

The presentation reports 120 colorectal surgery patients in a randomized perioperative comparison. Both groups received carbohydrate and one also received medium-chain triglycerides. The abstract reports lower secondary fatigue ratings in the combination group without a quantitative contrast. It leaves the botanical source and oil processing unspecified. The 40-rat experiment is a separate population. No measured Parkinson outcome is reported.
