Title: Psychiatric-onset neuronal intranuclear inclusion disease in a psychiatry-based dementia-enriched cohort in Japan
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12405813/
PMID: 40515658
PMCID: PMC12405813
DOI: 10.1111/pcn.13854
Publication date: 2025-06-14 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Three of 958 patients in a psychiatry-based dementia cohort had pathological NOTCH2NLC expansions. Their case histories and diagnostic work-up describe different disorders and do not measure a response to dried fig consumption.
