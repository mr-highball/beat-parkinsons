Title: Journal retraction of a 2013 grape powder rat study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12612586/
PMID: 40058952
PMCID: PMC12612586
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2025.02.023
Publication date: 2025-03-07
Date basis: Electronic publication in PMC metadata.
Review depth: Matching PMC metadata, original article's forward retraction link and the publisher's public retraction text.

The journal editor withdrew the study of grape powder and oxidative-stress-induced anxiety-like behavior, memory impairment and high blood pressure in rats. The notice identifies concerns involving several immunoblot panels in Figures 5 to 7 and the lack of original image files to resolve those concerns. The 2013 paper and any secondary citation of its directional result supply no reliable efficacy sign.
