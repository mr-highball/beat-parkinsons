Title: Extra virgin olive oil and swallowing during tonsil cancer treatment
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2227-9032/13/8/944
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Uher and colleagues, April 19, 2025. One 67-year-old man with HPV-positive tonsil cancer underwent chemoradiotherapy and used oral organic extra-virgin olive oil in a changing nutritional and supportive plan. Swallowing difficulty visual-analog ratings varied over seven treatment weeks, with eventual improvement. The uncontrolled trajectory accompanies changes in disease treatment, nutrition and other care. No Parkinson cohort or comparator was studied.
