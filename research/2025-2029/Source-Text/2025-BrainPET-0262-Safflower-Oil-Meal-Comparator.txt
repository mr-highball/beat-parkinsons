The human crossover component included 44 healthy adults, 29 APOE ε3 homozygotes and 15 ε4 heterozygotes. Mixed meals contained cocoa butter, safflower oil, or no added fat. The human outcomes were post-meal triglycerides, ApoB48 and plasma amyloid-beta fractions. The abstract reports a higher ApoB48 response after saturated fat in ε4 carriers compared with the ε3 group. An isolated safflower-arm contrast and the safflower oil refining or pressing method are unspecified in this abstract.

The same report includes a separate transgenic mouse experiment on cognition, vascular function and amyloid deposition. Mouse findings do not supply the missing human safflower-arm estimate. The human results do not report apathy, anxiety, muscle rigidity, falls, or a Parkinson cohort.
