The narrative review emphasizes differences in cultivar, ripening, drying and food matrix. Its inflammation and well-being discussion cites secondary work and describes arthritis models. It does not identify a clinical Parkinson pain comparison after dried dates.

The primary-source audit separately traces rat paw swelling in an older edible-part extract experiment, two acute edema assays in a Moroccan cultivar study and a four-cultivar arthritis abstract. Paw thickness, edema and composite arthritis severity are distinct from pain severity.
