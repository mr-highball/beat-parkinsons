Nutmeg infusion with sleep hygiene in a student study
Source: https://repository.uinjkt.ac.id/dspace/handle/123456789/93691
Coverage: factual extract of primary repository abstract
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Riyanni’s July 2026 university thesis abstract reports a purposive sample of 30 students with baseline Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index above five. Seven days of ground-seed infusion and 21 days of sleep-hygiene education were combined. The mean score changed from 7.50 to 4.97 (within-group p < .001). The single-arm design cannot isolate the infusion effect or separate it from the education, and did not include Parkinson disease.
