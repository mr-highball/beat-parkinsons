Title: Long-term safety and efficacy of repeated cycles of rimabotulinumtoxinB in the treatment of chronic sialorrhea
PMID: 40498248
PMCID: PMC12255616
DOI: 10.1007/s40120-025-00777-z
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12255616/
Registered study: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02610868
Publication: 2025-06-11
Coverage: Original article, outcomes, participant flow and registry linkage.

The open-label cohort included 187 adults with troublesome sialorrhea from several conditions. The article classifies 65.8% as having Parkinson disease. Mean unstimulated salivary flow fell by 0.34 g/min from baseline to week 4 of the initial session. Clinician and patient symptom measures are distinct from the flow measure. Dry mouth and dental caries were among the reported adverse events.

Only 50 participants entered session three and 24 entered session four, primarily because the study stopped once the planned regulatory safety information was captured. The later results represent smaller subsets. The publication follows the same group as the registry and does not provide a raw fig or culinary comparison.
