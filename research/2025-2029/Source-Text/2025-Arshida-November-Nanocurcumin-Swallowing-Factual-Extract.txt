Topic: Nanocurcumin and instrumental swallowing after chemoradiotherapy
Source: https://doi.org/10.4103/AMJM.AMJM_34_25
Coverage: November 28, 2025 original Table 5, factual extract
Table 5 reports mean baseline Penetration-Aspiration Scale (PAS) 2.91 in each arm. Post-treatment means were 2.91 with nanocurcumin and 3.72 with placebo, difference −0.81 (95% CI −1.39 to −0.22, p=.007). The paper prints the same submission reference and allocation as the May report. The group means indicate less worsening during treatment in the nanocurcumin arm. Participants were receiving chemoradiotherapy, with no grated turmeric or Parkinson comparison.
