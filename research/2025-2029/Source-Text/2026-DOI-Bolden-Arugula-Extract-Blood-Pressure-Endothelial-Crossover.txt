Title: What Is the Dose?: Effects of Arugula Extract on Blood Pressure and Endothelial Function
DOI: 10.1021/acsnutrsci.6c00024
First published: 2026
Authors: Bolden LK, Gallucci AR, Chapman-Lopez TJ, Pickler MC, Russell OC, Lee KM, et al.
Journal: ACS Nutrition Science
Source: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsnutrsci.6c00024
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized single-blind acute crossover trial (Bolden et al., 2026, ACS Nutrition Science) in 8 healthy males comparing 3,000 mg arugula extract, 6,000 mg arugula extract, and placebo over 3 hours post-ingestion. Both doses increased total nitric oxide metabolites/nitrate, lowered resting systolic blood pressure vs placebo (p<0.001), and improved 3-hour flow-mediated dilation without dose-response gain from 6,000 over 3,000 mg. Limits: tiny healthy-male sample (n=8), concentrated extract rather than raw arugula leaves, and seated/resting BP with no standing orthostatic hypotension or PD symptom assessment.
