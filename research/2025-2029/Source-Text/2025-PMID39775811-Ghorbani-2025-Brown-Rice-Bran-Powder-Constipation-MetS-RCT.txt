Title: Supplementing the standard diet with brown rice bran powder might effectively improve the metabolic syndrome characteristics and antioxidant status: an open label randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 39775811
DOI: 10.1039/d4fo03642e
First published: 2025
Authors: Ghorbani Z, Shoaibinobarian N, Zamani E, Salari A, Mahdavi-Roshan M, Porteghali P
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39775811/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Eight-week open-label randomized controlled trial in n=50 metabolic syndrome adults comparing 15 g/d brown rice bran powder (BRBP) plus standard diet vs standard diet alone. ANCOVA adjusted for baseline and medications showed significant reduction in GSRS constipation rate (p=0.018, ES -0.85), alongside improvements in BMI, waist circumference, LDL, glucose, GPx, and SOD. Limits: open-label, brown rice bran powder rather than cooked whole grain, non-PD MetS adults.
