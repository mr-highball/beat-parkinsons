Tağraf et al. A personalized plant-rich, time-restricted nutritional intervention for motor and non-motor symptoms in Parkinson disease. J Parkinsons Dis. Published online 2026 Feb 10.
DOI 10.1177/1877718X261422067. PMID 41666212. PMCID PMC13347589. NCT06892418.
Original: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/1877718X261422067
Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT06892418
Coverage: Factual comparison of Methods, Table 2 and official outcomes.

Forty participants with Parkinson disease were randomized to six months of a personalized plant-rich carbohydrate-restricted and time-restricted nutrition program plus usual care or usual care alone. Table 2 reports Epworth Sleepiness Scale means (SD) from 6.65 (2.58) to 6.50 (2.60) in control and 6.55 (2.65) to 6.70 (2.50) with the program. The displayed post-intervention comparison was p=1.000. The article also gives a paired Wilcoxon W=37.0 (p=.904). The public registry lists the motor symptom outcome and does not list a separate Epworth endpoint. No carrot assignment, raw-carrot intake or ingredient-specific effect was reported.
