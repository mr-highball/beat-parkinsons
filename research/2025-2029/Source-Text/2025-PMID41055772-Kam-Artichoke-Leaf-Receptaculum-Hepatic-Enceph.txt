Title: Protective effect of artichoke (Cynara scolymus L.) leaf and receptaculum extracts against hepatic encephalopathy in bile duct ligated rats.
PMID: 41055772
DOI: 10.1007/s11011-025-01701-y
First published: 2025
Authors: Kam, Bebitoğlu, Şener, Oğuz, Erdoğan
Journal: Metabolic brain disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41055772/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

28-day Wistar rat bile-duct-ligation hepatic encephalopathy model testing oral Cynara scolymus leaf and receptaculum (edible heart) extracts (250 and 500 mg/kg). High-dose receptaculum extract improved Morris water maze target-quadrant performance and both leaf and receptaculum extracts improved novel object recognition while reducing brain/liver 8-OHdG and cytokines. Limits: cholestatic hepatic encephalopathy model measuring spatial/recognition memory rather than PD attention/executive function.
