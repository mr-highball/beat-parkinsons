Title: Damani and colleagues, sleep component in an ancillary analysis of HAT
Source: https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.124.039130
Publication: February 19, 2025
Coverage: Factual original Methods, Table 3 and sensitivity results.

The primary ancillary sample included 969 adults with abdominal obesity. For the sleep component, 464 avocado and 473 habitual-diet participants provided available data. The 26-week change contrast was +3.20 points (95% CI 0.38 to 6.02). The complete-data sensitivity estimate was +2.93 (95% CI −0.10 to 5.98). The component uses categories of self-reported nightly sleep duration from the Pittsburgh questionnaire. It is not a daytime sleepiness score. The exposure was whole avocado. The separate Food & Function microbiome supplement from the same trial reports no clear global sleep-quality contrast.
