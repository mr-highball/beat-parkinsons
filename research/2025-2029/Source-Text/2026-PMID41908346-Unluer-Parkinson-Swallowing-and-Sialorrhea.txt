Fifty-five people with Parkinson disease completed swallowing and sialorrhea questionnaires and cervical position testing during medication ON. Swallowing impairment and sialorrhea scale scores correlated (Spearman rho .618, p < .001). Cervical position errors had no clear association with either outcome. The cross-sectional study measured eating disturbance as part of a sialorrhea scale and cough after food or drink, without testing particular foods or comparing pain after date fruit.
