The original supplement recovered on November 15, 2026 contains a general preparation glossary. It allows fresh or dried material in infusions and describes syrup as heating herbal material with sugar and water. The species-level aerial-part and preparation list belongs to the main article, published June 11, 2026. The glossary supplies no plant-specific freshness, measured outcome or interview-level pairing. This supplement belongs to the existing Papp survey. The earlier 2011 sore-throat interview report is already cataloged and possible participant overlap remains unresolved.
