Title: Resolution of Levodopa/Dopa-Decarboxylase Inhibitor-Associated Microscopic Colitis with Subcutaneous Foslevodopa/Foscarbidopa: A Case Series and Review of the Literature
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13339309/
PMID: 41668674
PMCID: PMC13339309
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70554
Publication date: 2026-02-11 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Two case reports and a literature review track microscopic colitis during oral dopaminergic therapy and resolution following treatment changes. The observations concern bowel symptoms and drug routes. Dietary fig and visual episode frequency are outside the comparison.
