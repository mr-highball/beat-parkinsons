Title: C19orf12 gene variants causing mitochondrial membrane protein-associated neurodegeneration (MPAN)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12229627/
PMID: 39755877
PMCID: PMC12229627
DOI: 10.1038/s41431-024-01778-6
Publication date: 2025-01-04 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML

A series of seven patients in six families describes clinical and imaging features of C19orf12-associated mitochondrial membrane protein neurodegeneration. Inherited parkinsonism and psychiatric features are observed in a different disease context without dietary fig comparisons.
