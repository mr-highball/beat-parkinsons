Title: Efficacy of Hibiscus sabdariffa L. extract on metabolic parameters in participants with abdominal obesity and mild metabolic syndrome in Bangkok, Thailand: A double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial
PMID: 40334927
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2025.103185
First published: 2025
Authors: Chaisungnern S, Rattananupong T, Klinhom S, Sithisarankul P
Journal: Complementary Therapies in Medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40334927/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Adversarial null BP clinical trial: double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial in 108 Thai adults with abdominal obesity and mild metabolic syndrome assigned to 1,000 mg/day aqueous H. sabdariffa calyx extract capsules (45.04 mg total polyphenols/day) or placebo for 12 weeks. Compared with placebo, hibiscus extract produced NO significant change in blood pressure, HOMA-IR, glycemic markers, BMI, waist circumference, or overall lipids (only within-group LDL decreased -7.98 mg/dL, 95% CI -14.80 to -1.15), with no hepatic, renal, or hematologic toxicity.
