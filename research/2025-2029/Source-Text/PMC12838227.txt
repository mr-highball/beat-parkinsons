The full published proceedings contain independent hydrocephalus reports. One diagnostic member notes visual hallucinations and falls in a case with imaging-supported possible normal-pressure hydrocephalus. A separate shunt member tracks neuropsychiatric and functional measures. Neither measures a dried fig exposure, and the clinical cases are distinct.

The report of possible normal-pressure hydrocephalus with falls and visual hallucinations concerns a single member's diagnostic case. The member relating the hydrocephalus triad and neuropsychiatric symptoms to caregiver burden and shunt surgery reports its own 24-patient sample.
