Title: Exploring the relationship between Alzheimer's disease and lower extremity isokinetic muscle strength in community-dwelling older adults: A cross-sectional observational study

PMCID: PMC13688339

Text coverage: Original PMC XML article body and embedded tables inspected. Factual extract. Separately distributed supplements are outside this extract.

A cross-sectional muscle-strength comparison enrolled 80 community-dwelling older adults, half with Alzheimer disease. Models related diagnosis to knee and ankle torque or work measurements. The adjusted finding concerned ankle plantar-flexor strength. The discussion relates strength to potential fall risk, while the study does not count falls or measure symptom fatigue. No sage exposure was assessed.
