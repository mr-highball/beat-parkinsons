Title: The vestibulo-ocular and vestibulospinal reflexes minimally impact the freezing of gait in patients with early-to-moderate Parkinson's disease
PMID: 40256685
PMCID: PMC12008544
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2025.100319
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590112525000234
Publication: 2025-04-06 electronic publication
Coverage: Verified original NCBI JATS body and Tables 1–2.

The retrospective Korean sample had 138 newly diagnosed Parkinson participants, including 23 with freezing and 115 without it. Head-impulse vestibulo-ocular reflex gain did not clearly differ by freezing status in the horizontal (p = .560), anterior (p = .399) or posterior (p = .419) canal comparisons. The gains had no clear association with the new freezing questionnaire score. Neither type of vestibular evoked myogenic potential differed by freezing status. The adjusted motor examination association in Table 2 was OR 1.05 per MDS-UPDRS III point (95% CI 1.01–1.10).

The cohort and head-impulse test differ from the ARO SU212 rotary-chair comparison at 0.32 Hz. The reports supply separate method-specific observations. No culinary exposure was tested.
