Title: Effects of Blueberry Supplementation on Depression and Anxiety Symptoms in a Rural Louisiana Population.
PMID: 41374012
PMCID: PMC12694358
DOI: 10.3390/nu17233720
Registry ID: NCT04398784
First published: 2025
Authors: Venable KE, Kelley DP, Jeansonne A, Beyl R, O'Bryan S, Vatsavayi V, et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12694358/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover pilot trial (NCT04398784; n=45 enrolled; 21 paired GAD-7 and 20 paired HDRS completers) in rural Louisiana adults diagnosed with MDD and/or GAD comparing 12 weeks daily 24 g whole freeze-dried blueberry powder vs placebo (4-week washout). Compared with placebo from baseline to 12 weeks, blueberry significantly reduced GAD-7 anxiety (-3.134 +/- 1.476, p=0.035 ITT; -3.67 pts, p=0.02, d=0.54 completers; 6-wk null p=0.0987) and HDRS depression (-3.686 +/- 1.791, p=0.0431 ITT; -4.64 pts, p=0.02, d=0.57 completers), while self-rated MDI was null (p=0.811) and CRP/cytokines were unchanged. Limits: non-PD adults, freeze-dried powder rather than frozen whole fruit, high crossover attrition.
