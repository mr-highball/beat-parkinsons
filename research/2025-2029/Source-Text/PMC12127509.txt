Title: Disrupted functional brain network associated with presence of hallucinations in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12127509/
PMID: 40458458
PMCID: PMC12127509
DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcaf185
Publication date: 2025-05-16 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

PPMI classified 25 Parkinson participants with a broad hallucination or psychosis item and compared them with 56 Parkinson participants without symptoms and 24 controls. The separate ICICLE-PD sample used a dedicated visual interview and a severity score combining frequency, duration and type. Resting connectivity and subsequent clinical measures were compared without dried fig or other food-timed episode counts.

Methods identify the independent replication sample as ICICLE-PD. Participant and caregiver version screening and a severity composite are distinct from the PPMI hallucinations and psychosis item. PPMI also underlies later brainstem imaging research in the catalog, with possible participant overlap.
