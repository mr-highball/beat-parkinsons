Title: Association of a five-metabolite and early-symptom profile with Parkinson's disease and its clinical progression
PMID: 41565969
PMCID: PMC12894904
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-36756-z
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12894904/
Publication: 2026-01-21
Coverage: Original article, participant selection, Table 2 and classification analysis.

The authors measured plasma metabolites in 20 healthy controls and two groups of 20 people with Parkinson disease, classified by stage. Table 2 records the presence of drooling in 11 intermediate-stage and 18 later-stage participants (P=.0132). The classifier treats drooling as a binary input alongside depression and REM sleep behavior disorder. The authors call for independent validation.

The cross-sectional grouping does not establish within-person progression or a graded symptom response. Plasma metabolites and symptoms were measured separately, without a recorded comparison of raw fig or another assigned food.
