Title: Accurate detection of pathologic α-synuclein in CSF, skin, olfactory mucosa, and urine with a uniform seeding amplification assay
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12102825/
PMID: 40413531
PMCID: PMC12102825
DOI: 10.1186/s40478-025-02034-8
Publication date: 2025-05-24 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The study compares pathological alpha-synuclein detection in cerebrospinal fluid, skin, nasal material and urine across diagnostic groups. Assay sensitivity and specificity measure diagnostic performance, with no diet or hallucination exposure–outcome comparison.
