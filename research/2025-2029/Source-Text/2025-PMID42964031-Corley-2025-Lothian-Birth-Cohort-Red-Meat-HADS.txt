Title: Dietary exposures and risk of anxiety and depression symptoms in the Lothian Birth Cohort 1936: a cohort-level GLAD Project analysis.
PMID: 42964031
PMCID: PMC13717163
DOI: 10.12688/wellcomeopenres.24950.2
First published: 2025
Authors: Corley J, Redmond P, Lane MM, Jacka FN, Ashtree DN, Cox SR
Journal: Wellcome open research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42964031/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional GLAD Project analysis of n=880 older Scottish adults (mean age 70 y, Lothian Birth Cohort 1936): standardized red meat and processed meat intakes showed no statistically significant association with Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS >=8) anxiety or depression symptoms in adjusted or energy-adjusted models.
