Title: Comparison of metabolic profiles, regulatory pathways, and quorum sensing inhibition activity of brown mustard seeds (Brassica juncea L.) post germination and roasting treatments
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12606101/
First published online: 2025-11-11
PMID: 41219224
PMCID: PMC12606101
DOI: 10.1038/s41538-025-00606-5
Text coverage: original article, Methods and comparative glucosinolate results.

This experiment applied untargeted metabolite profiling to raw, sprouted and roasted brown mustard seed. Reported relative signals for glucosinolates fell during roasting, while other compounds varied by chemical class and processing. This is a relative plant chemical comparison rather than a dose estimate for a meal. The study did not score tremor or administer seed to people with Parkinson disease. Its analytical methods and Brassica juncea exposure are distinct from the 2019 roasted Brassica nigra study.
