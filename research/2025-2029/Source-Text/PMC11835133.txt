Title: Progress and prospects of Parkinson's disease with depression research: A global bibliometric analysis based on CiteSpace
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11835133/
PMID: 39960944
PMCID: PMC11835133

Publication date: 2025-02-14 (electronic publication date in original JATS)

The bibliometric study analyzes publications and citation trends on Parkinson disease with depression. It counts literature records and research topics, with no clinical food exposure or visual episode comparison.
