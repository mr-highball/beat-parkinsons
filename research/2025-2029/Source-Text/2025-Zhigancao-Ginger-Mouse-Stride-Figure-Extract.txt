Ginger containing decoction and mouse stride length

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12818266/
Coverage: Original recipe Table 1 and visually verified CatWalk figure
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published December 18, 2025. A nine-component boiled Zhigancao decoction included ginger rhizome in its adult-equivalent formula. CatWalk Figure 3L used six mice per group and showed reduced stride length after MPTP. Moderate and high whole-decoction groups showed longer strides than the MPTP group. The method gives no ginger-only group or exact culinary ground-ginger exposure and the tested model is nonhuman.
