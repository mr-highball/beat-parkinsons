Title: Efficacy of rice bran extract for alleviating depressive symptoms in adults: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 40409466
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2025.05.021
Registry ID: NCT05180136
First published: 2025
Authors: Huh SY, Lee YL, Kim SH, Lee SY
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40409466/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Eight-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (NCT05180136; corrigenda PMID:40701183 & PMID:40912673) in n=100 randomized (n=97 completed: 47 RBS vs 50 placebo) adults (19-75 y) with mild-to-moderate depressive symptoms (K-HDRS 7-24) taking 1 g/d rice bran extract supplement (RBS) vs placebo. RBS significantly lowered K-HDRS vs placebo (p<0.001; adjusted difference -5.73 [95% CI -7.07, -4.39] ITT; -5.95 [-7.33, -4.57] PP) and lowered K-PHQ-9 (p=0.026 PP), with only a non-significant trend on K-BDI-II and K-BAI anxiety and no adverse events. Limits: concentrated rice bran extract supplement in non-PD depressed adults.
