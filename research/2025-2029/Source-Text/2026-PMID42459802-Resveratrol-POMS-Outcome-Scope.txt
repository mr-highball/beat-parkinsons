Wightman and colleagues report 99 participants completing a parallel, double-blind comparison of cellulose placebo and 500 mg per day of synthetic trans-resveratrol for 12 weeks. In the main article, the only significant Profile of Mood States contrast was its anger/hostility subscale at the final visit before dosing. The change was 1.48 (SE 0.35) for resveratrol and 0.60 (SE 0.16) for placebo, p=0.02. Anger/hostility is distinct from the anxiety subscale. The main article selectively tabulates effects and points to a supplement for all outcomes. The supplement DOI resolved to the publisher article during this check. The complete continuous anxiety result could not be extracted and remains open.

Compare PMID 28054939 for the older borderline anxiety subscale result in a different population and at a different supplement dose. Both use isolated constituents. Neither evaluates raw table grapes in a Parkinson population. NCT07214428 tests grape powder in a different population and had no posted results on the November 25 registry check.
