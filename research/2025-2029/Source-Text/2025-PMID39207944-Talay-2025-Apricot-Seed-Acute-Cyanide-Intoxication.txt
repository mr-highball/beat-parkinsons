Title: Acute cyanide intoxication due to apricot seed ingestion.
PMID: 39207944
DOI: 10.5546/aap.2024-10390.eng
First published: 2025
Authors: Talay MN, Güngör E, Orhan Ö
Journal: Archivos argentinos de pediatria
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39207944/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Retrospective pediatric ICU case series (n=14 patients aged 1-18 y, 2017-2022) documenting acute cyanide poisoning after ingesting apricot pits/seeds containing cyanogenic glycosides, presenting with weakness, fatigue, and neurologic toxicity. Confirms strict safety separation between culinary apricot flesh and cyanogenic kernels/seeds.
