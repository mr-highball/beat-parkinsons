Md Belal Bin Heyat, Arshiya Sultana and colleagues. Published October 9, 2026 as part of the Brain and Behavior report on the 60-person NIUM trial.
Table S1 separates the total Modified Kupperman Index from its individual symptom-level fields. Examples of individual score fields include insomnia, hot flashes and nervousness. The supplement documents these variables as numeric symptom-level scores and possible modeling inputs. It provides neither the raw observations nor arm-level insomnia summaries or an effect estimate. The table names insomnia, not daytime sleepiness or dream enactment.
Table S2 defines model labels as membership in the ground-anise or placebo trial arm. Classification accuracy is not a participant insomnia benefit measure. Table S3 describes last-observation-carried-forward imputation and data scaling. The supplement makes insomnia ascertainment discoverable but does not reveal its direction or magnitude. The primary article says the data are available upon request. The clinical comparison was in menopause rather than Parkinson disease.
