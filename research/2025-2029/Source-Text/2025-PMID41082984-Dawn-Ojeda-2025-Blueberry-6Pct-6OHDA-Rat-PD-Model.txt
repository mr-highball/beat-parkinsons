Title: Dietary Supplementation with Six Percent Blueberries (Vaccinium corymbosum L.) Attenuates Neurodegeneration and Motor Deficits in a Parkinson's Disease Model.
PMID: 41082984
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2025.10.015
First published: 2025
Authors: Dawn-Ojeda AA, Marín-Canul JE, Granados-Patrón DA, Cuevas-Carbonell SG, Mut-Martín MC, Pérez-Izquierdo O, et al.
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41082984/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical intrastriatal 6-OHDA rat Parkinson disease model comparing 6% freeze-dried Vaccinium corymbosum blueberry-enriched diet vs standard chow. Blueberry-fed rats had significantly fewer contralateral narrow-beam paw slips, fewer apomorphine-induced rotations, lower cylinder motor asymmetry (all p<0.05), and reduced striatal/nigral TH denervation and Iba1 microglial activation. Limits: preclinical toxin-lesion rodent model; broad motor proxy cannot directly establish clinical bradykinesia, balance, or gait speed efficacy in human PD.
