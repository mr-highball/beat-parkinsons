Original study scope
The abstract describes daily isolated curcumin or rotenone injections in male rats over nine weeks. The published Figure 6 as recorded in the earlier original capture gives approximately 2, 13 and 5 for control, rotenone and rotenone plus curcumin latency. Figure 7 describes a separate rearing outcome. The original findings therefore concern a direction in catalepsy or motor behavior in an injection model. The 2026 congress and Yerevan records with the same nine-week conditions are companion reports.

Continuing evidence gaps
The publisher supplies a preview and figure record. The full report is needed to check dose protocol, analyzable cohort and blinding. Read the injection figure together with the separately reported 2025 MDS condition that used turmeric extract. Independence between the nine-week study and the three-week abstract should be confirmed from enrollment and experiment dates. No separate passive-resistance measure or oral grated-turmeric comparison is available.
