The animal study compares date palm pollen extract with controls in an experimental colitis model. The abstract reports changes in inflammatory, tissue and disease activity measures. It does not identify a separate pain severity comparison. The exposure is pollen extract, with no whole dried date flesh or human Parkinson outcome. The model is distinct from the clinical oral mucositis pilot and supplies no replication of its pain result.
