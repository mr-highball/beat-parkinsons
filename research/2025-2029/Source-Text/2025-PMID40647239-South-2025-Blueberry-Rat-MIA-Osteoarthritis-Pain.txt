Title: Anti-Inflammatory and Joint-Protective Effects of Blueberries in a Monosodium Iodoacetate (MIA)-Induced Rat Model of Osteoarthritis.
PMID: 40647239
PMCID: PMC12251009
DOI: 10.3390/nu17132134
First published: 2025
Authors: South SM, Crabtree K, Averitt DL, Vijayagopal P, Juma S
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12251009/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical rat monosodium iodoacetate (MIA) knee osteoarthritis study (n=40 female rats; 30 MIA randomized to 0%, 5%, or 10% whole blueberry powder diets). Reported limited, non-dose-dependent reductions in mechanical allodynia and joint inflammation that were not consistently statistically significant across endpoints. Limits: non-PD preclinical rodent OA model; inconsistent significance.
