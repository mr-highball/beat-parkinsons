Title: Missense Variants in the A Isoform of FGF13 as a Novel Cause of Paroxysmal Dyskinesia

The report describes paroxysmal dyskinesia in four unrelated children with variants in an FGF13 isoform. The authors observed drooling during one described attack pattern. The body uses orange as an illustration color and provides no orange or fig exposure comparison. The cases do not measure Parkinson drooling severity or longitudinal fall frequency.
