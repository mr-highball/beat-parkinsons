Title: Polyphenol-Rich Snack Consumption during Endurance Exercise Training Improves Nitric Oxide Bioavailability but does not Improve Exercise Performance in Male Cyclists: A Randomised Controlled Trial.
PMID: 40321836
PMCID: PMC12049943
DOI: 10.1016/j.cdnut.2025.106006
First published: 2025
Authors: d'Unienville, Coates, Hill, Nelson, Croft
Journal: Current developments in nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40321836/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

5-week parallel RCT (ACTRN12618000360213) in 96 male cyclists randomized to 125 g/d mixed snack (almonds, dried grapes, and dried cranberries) vs isocaloric oat bar across heavy training and taper. No between-group difference in 5-min cycling time trial (P > 0.81). The mixed snack yielded higher subjective energy during heavy training (d = 0.21, P = 0.02) and less fatigue during taper (d = -0.20, P = 0.05). Limits: multi-ingredient blend combining almonds with grapes and cranberries; cannot isolate almonds.
