Title: Beneficial Effects of Apple Polyphenol Extract and Time-Restricted Feeding on Ulcerative Colitis and Accompanying Depression-like Behaviors Are Correlated with Gut-Brain Axis Modulation.
PMID: 40751698
DOI: 10.1021/acs.jafc.5c05176
First published: 2025
Authors: Qian Q, Luan W, Ma J, Chen S, Xie Y, Wu Z et al.
Journal: Journal of agricultural and food chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40751698/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled 36-day DSS-induced ulcerative colitis experiment in male C57BL/6 mice testing oral apple polyphenol extract (APE, 500 mg/kg/d), time-restricted feeding (TRF), and their combination. APE individually and in combination ameliorated ulcerative colitis and accompanying anxiety- and depression-like behaviors, suppressed glial activation, upregulated BDNF/PSD-95, activated Parkin/PINK1 mitophagy, and increased serum serotonin and norepinephrine. Limits: non-PD colitis mouse model and concentrated extract; abstract omits exact group sizes.
