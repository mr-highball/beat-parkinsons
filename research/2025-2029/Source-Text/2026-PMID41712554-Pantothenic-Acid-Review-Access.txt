Title: Pantothenic Acid and Parkinson Disease: A Systematic Review of Metabolomics Analysis Studies
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41712554/
PMID: 41712554
DOI: 10.1093/nutrit/nuaf232
Publication date: 2026-02-19
Source depth: original author abstract and version-of-record metadata; complete review body remains open

The review includes three dietary assessments and sixteen metabolomics studies. Two of three dietary studies were described as reporting lower odds of Parkinson disease with greater dietary pantothenic acid. Metabolomics studies reported lower levels in twelve, higher in two and disturbed pathway measurements in two. The paper concerns disease status and biochemical measures. It supplies no result for dried fig and visual hallucination frequency. The full study tables and exact component overlap cannot be verified from the open abstract. The publisher version of record and three primary dietary reports remain separate access trails.
