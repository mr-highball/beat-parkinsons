Title: Heterogeneity of cognitive progression and clinical predictors in Parkinson's disease-subjective cognitive decline
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11882643/
PMID: 40042541
PMCID: PMC11882643
DOI: 10.1007/s00415-024-12808-0
Publication date: 2025-03-05 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

A longitudinal analysis follows 198 people with Parkinson and subjective cognitive complaints, selected from 326 without objective cognitive impairment. Its outcome is cognitive progression, with no dated dried fig intake or visual episode estimates after eating.
