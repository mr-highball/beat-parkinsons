Title: Effects of banana blossom supplementation on metabolic parameters in prediabetic adults: a double-blind randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 41309754
PMCID: PMC12660947
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-26505-z
First published: 2025
Authors: Chotiprakornkul N, Phimsen W, Sintuprapa W, Nitisinprasert S, Tunsagool P, Suwannakarn M
Journal: Scientific reports
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41309754/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

8-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (PMC12660947, TCTR20240326003) in n=42 prediabetic Thai adults receiving 3,000 mg/d freeze-dried banana blossom powder vs placebo. Reduced body weight, BMI, and diastolic BP and noted improved bowel movements without glycemic changes. Uses banana flower blossom, not banana fruit pulp.
