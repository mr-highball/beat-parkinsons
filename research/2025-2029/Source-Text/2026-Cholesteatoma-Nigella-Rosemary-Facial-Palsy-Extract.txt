2026 | Congenital petrous cholesteatoma with prior mention of Nigella and rosemary use

Source: original case report and timeline, DOI 10.1097/rc9.0000000000001174, publisher first posted November 2, 2026.

A 25-year-old woman developed progressive unilateral peripheral facial paralysis. The case timeline says use of Nigella sativa and Rosmarinus officinalis did not improve the symptoms. It gives no oil or seed identity, grade, amount, route, or separate herb estimate. At specialist assessment facial nerve dysfunction was House–Brackmann grade V. The report describes improvement to grade II three days after cholesteatoma surgery. The timeline has no assigned comparator or Parkinson facial-expression measure.
