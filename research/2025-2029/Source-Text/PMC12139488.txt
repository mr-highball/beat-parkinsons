Title: Visual hallucinations in neurosurgery: A systematic review and two case insights into Charles Bonnet Syndrome
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12139488/
PMID: 40476156
PMCID: PMC12139488
DOI: 10.1016/j.bas.2025.104257
Publication date: 2025-04-24 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Two institutional cases and 17 patients from 13 papers document visual phenomena in a neurosurgical Charles Bonnet context. Some episodes were restricted to visual field loss. These are different underlying conditions and there is no oral food comparison.
