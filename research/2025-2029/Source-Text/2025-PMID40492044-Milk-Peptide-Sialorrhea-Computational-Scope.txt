Title: Effect of bovine milk-derived peptide on SNAP-25 of the neurotransmitter system in treating the sialorrhoea in chronic neurological diseases
PMID: 40492044
PMCID: PMC12145395
DOI: 10.1007/s10068-025-01872-5
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12145395/
Publication: 2025-03-29
Coverage: Original article and assessment of study design and clinical outcome availability.

The authors drew candidates from a milk-peptide repository and used computational predictions to prioritize one molecule. The disease application in the title describes the research motivation. The paper reports no participants or animals treated, no milk or fig consumption, and no measured saliva output or drooling score. The authors identify in vivo efficacy and safety as remaining research. This is a biochemical lead without a verified clinical direction.
