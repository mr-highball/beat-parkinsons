Medium-chain triglyceride case in GLUT1 deficiency
Primary source: https://www.parkinsonlimpedismov.it/web/image/399154/Abstract%20Congresso%20Roma%202025.pdf
Coverage: Factual original conference case extract; related journal PMID41071454.

The original May 2025 LIMPE–DISMOV poster P93 describes one patient with a genetic glucose transport disorder, on a ketogenic diet, whose exercise-induced dystonic episode resolved after oral MCT oil. Instrumented walking speed rose within minutes; joint kinematics were described as normal in both assessment conditions. The case gives no virgin-grade coconut oil comparison and the movement disorder differs from Parkinson levodopa dyskinesia. The October journal report is subscription-only beyond its figures and metadata.
