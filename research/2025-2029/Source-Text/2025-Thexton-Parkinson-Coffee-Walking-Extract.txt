Title: Reported coffee and walking in Parkinson focus groups
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12413210/
PMID: 40919379
PMCID: PMC12413210
DOI: 10.14200/jrm.2025.0003
Coverage: Original public author manuscript Methods and Results

Thexton and colleagues published the edited article June 3, 2025. It describes five focus groups involving 36 people with Parkinson disease and 30 caregivers. A couple of participants described better walking after coffee. This is a qualitative perception without documented coffee preparation, assigned dose, comparison or measured walking change. The study discusses fatigue as a Parkinson symptom without linking fatigue severity to coffee. The finding preserves a separate walking lead and leaves claim 0121, which concerns fatigue severity after brewed coffee, unresolved.

The discussion summarizes tremor relief after gluten withdrawal. Table 5 describes a participant with celiac disease who changed diet and then started carbidopa–levodopa before reporting better hand function. The record supplies no examination-rated rest tremor or dietary contrast for wheat berries.
