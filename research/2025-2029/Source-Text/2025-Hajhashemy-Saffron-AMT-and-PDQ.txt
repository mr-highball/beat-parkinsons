First published December 24, 2025. In the same Isfahan cohort of 92 randomized adults, adjusted AMT total difference was +0.78 (95% CI 0.16 to 1.40, p = .01) and PDQ-39 cognitive quality-of-life domain difference was −8.07 (95% CI −14.39 to −1.75, p = .01). Intervention used 100 mg daily of milled Sargol stigma powder in a tablet. These are a brief global cognitive screen and a separate patient-rated domain. No executive-function subscore is available. This note verifies outcome details for the same trial already assessed.
