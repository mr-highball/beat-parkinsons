Title: Neuroprotective effects of Agaricus bisporus protein extract in MPTP-induced Parkinson's disease: modulation of oxidative stress, neuroinflammation, and apoptotic pathways in the Substantia Nigra.
PMID: 42133882
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2026.2672543
First published: 2026
Authors: Zhgenti, Bibilashvili, Davitashvili
Journal: Nutritional neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42133882/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twenty-day MPTP Parkinson mouse model testing oral tyrosinase-enriched Agaricus bisporus protein extract (ABE). ABE retained tyrosinase activity through simulated digestion, increased plasma L-DOPA (~55%, p<0.001) and dopamine (~30%, p<0.01), elevated nigral dopamine (p<0.001) and AADC (p<0.01), attenuated motor deficits with reduced beam-walking errors (p<0.001), preserved nigral TH+ neurons (p<0.001), and reduced MDA/NF-kB/IL-6/IL-1beta/TNF-alpha. Critical culinary caveat: cooking/roasting denatures mushroom tyrosinase enzyme.
