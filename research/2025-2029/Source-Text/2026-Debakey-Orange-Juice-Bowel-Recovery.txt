Title: Impact of Coffee Versus Orange Juice on Enhancing Bowel Recovery After Laparoscopic Colorectal Surgery
Source: https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8279639/v1
First posted: January 9, 2026
Coverage: Factual extract from the preprint and linked public registry.

Median time to the first bowel movement was 47.5 hours for coffee versus 73 hours for fresh orange juice, p < .001. The preprint reports 238 people per arm and no untreated group. NCT05167890 is linked by the manuscript but shows estimated enrollment 80 in its sole, older public update. The population was recovering from colorectal surgery. The comparison estimates the relative timing under coffee and juice and gives no primary evidence about untreated constipation, ordinary raw sweet orange or Parkinson disease.
