Title: Immersive virtual reality upper-limb exercises in people with Parkinson's disease: an observer-blinded randomised controlled trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41508050/
PMID: 41508050
PMCID: PMC12781375
DOI: 10.1186/s12984-025-01851-1
First public: 2026-01-08
Coverage: Factual extract from original methods and results.

Fifty-eight participants in inpatient rehabilitation were randomized to immersive virtual reality upper-limb exercises or conventional upper-limb training. The primary tests were the Action Research Arm Test and Jebsen–Taylor Hand Function Test. The arm-function group-by-time interaction was inconclusive. The study's Fig. labels refer to diagrams and result displays. The trial does not test fig or date fruit consumption or measure a fruit-related tremor or pain comparison.
