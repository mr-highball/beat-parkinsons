Title: The effectiveness of a plant-based milk with fermented brown rice on constipation symptoms via gut microbiota modulation: a double-blind randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 42371112
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-026-04029-0
Registry ID: ChiCTR2400088688
First published: 2026
Authors: Tang A, Cao Q, Wang M, Li W, Xu H, Wang Y
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42371112/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-week double-blind randomized controlled trial (ChiCTR2400088688) in n=100 randomized (n=99 completed) adults with functional constipation comparing 500 mL/d fermented brown rice plant-based milk vs isocaloric plant protein milk. Primary complete spontaneous bowel movement (CSBM) rate and BMF increased in both arms with NO significant between-group difference, whereas secondary straining, bloating, and abdominal pain improved significantly more with fermented brown rice milk (all p<0.05) alongside Blautia enrichment. Limits: fermented beverage, primary CSBM null, non-PD population.
