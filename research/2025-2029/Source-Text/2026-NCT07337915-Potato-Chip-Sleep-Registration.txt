Potato chips in a midlife sleep trial
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07337915
Coverage: Factual summary of official registry fields.

First posted January 13, 2026. The planned 72-participant study in midlife women with poor sleep compares potato chips and pistachios for 30 days under matched healthy-habits coaching, with a separate sleep-coaching arm. Primary PSQI and secondary PROMIS Sleep Related Impairment, vigilance testing and Insomnia Severity Index include daytime effects. The current record is recruiting without posted results and contains no boiled-potato or Parkinson arm.
