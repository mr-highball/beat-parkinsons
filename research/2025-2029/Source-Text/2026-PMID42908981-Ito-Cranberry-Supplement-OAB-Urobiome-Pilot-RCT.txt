Title: Efficacy of a standardized cranberry supplement on overactive bladder symptoms and urobiome alterations in young women: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot trial.
PMID: 42908981
PMCID: PMC13683685
DOI: 10.3389/fcimb.2026.1952206
First published: 2026
Authors: Ito A, Akagawa S, Akagawa Y, Tsuji S, Kato S, Yamanouchi S, Kaneko K
Journal: Frontiers in cellular and infection microbiology
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13683685/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled pilot trial (Ito et al., 2026, Front Cell Infect Microbiol, PMC13683685; UMIN000057544) enrolling 26 young women (median age 23; 25 analyzed) with overactive bladder (OABSS >=3) randomized to standardized cranberry supplement (36 mg proanthocyanidins/day) vs placebo for 4 weeks. At 4 weeks, the Overactive Bladder Symptom Score (OABSS) reduction rate was significantly greater with cranberry than placebo (median 66.7% vs 40.0%, p=0.039), accompanied by a within-group decrease in urinary Lactobacillus abundance (-4.39%, p=0.027, q=0.096). Limits: small exploratory pilot (n=25), short 4-week duration, young non-PD women, standardized PAC extract rather than whole culinary dried cranberries.
