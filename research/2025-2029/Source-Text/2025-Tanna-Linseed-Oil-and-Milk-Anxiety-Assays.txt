Title: Anxiolytic potential of linseed oil and Ashwagandharishta in Wistar albino rats
Source: https://doi.org/10.4103/jdras.jdras_28_24
DOI: 10.4103/jdras.jdras_28_24
Publication date: 2025
Date basis: Journal of Drug Research in Ayurvedic Sciences volume 10, issue 2, pp.148–154. Exact first-public day remains unverified.
Coverage: Original author abstract as indexed alongside the publisher bibliographic record. Main tables unavailable.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

The study compares distilled water, milk vehicle, linseed oil administered with milk, a separate botanical formulation, and a combination. Authors report reduced freezing in multiple open-field groups including milk and oil with milk. The abstract does not specify a direct oil-plus-milk versus milk effect estimate. It reports no anxiolytic effect from linseed oil in the elevated plus maze.

The milk background, unknown oil processing, lack of pairwise group estimates and nonspecific open-field behavior limit transfer. The abstract does not demonstrate equivalence, worsening, or an isolated clinical effect. Formal links to reports by the same authors that studied depression or seizures require design-specific verification.
