Registered fermented black soybean and adlay frailty comparison
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06882941
Registration: NCT06882941
Coverage: Official structured trial registry and result status

The National Taiwan University Hospital record has a brief title referring to algae, but its structured intervention arms specify fermented and nonfermented black soybean plus dehulled adlay for 12 weeks. Registered outcomes include frailty, five-meter walking speed, timed up and go, chair stands and grip strength. The latest record marks 141 enrolled and the trial completed, with no posted results on November 16, 2026. A 2025 conference report describes a matching preparation and setting with 116 randomized, but does not name the accession, so study linkage and participant counts remain open.
