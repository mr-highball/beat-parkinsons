Original scope and consistency check
The flow and results describe 14 papers contributing 16 studies, of which 13 are randomized and three are pre/post designs, with 503 participants. The abstract instead says 18 studies. The body accounts for two papers reporting two study units apiece. The review evaluates several stem-extract products, asparagus powder and mixed products across healthy adults, sleep complaints and cognitive impairment. It discloses funding relationships and manufacturer-employed coauthors.

Fatigue citation audit
The review describes the Takanari 2016 POMS fatigue result as a significant contrast against placebo. In the original Figure 5B, the significance marker compares the end and start of the extract period. The review table also lists 50 mg per day, while the original methods describe three 50 mg extract capsules per day. A separate rough reading of both bar pairs gives a small estimate favoring the extract and lacks comparative uncertainty. The review table also describes the Sakai study as a fatigue improvement, but its original scale description places less fatigue at the high end while the reported score decreases. The Deguchi work is open-label, and the cited Kato comparison did not detect a clear fatigue change. The Takahashi crossover found favorable patient ratings of sleepiness on rising and feeling refreshed, outcomes that apply to awakening rather than broad clinical fatigue.

Scope for claim 0837
The review reuses the reported cohorts. Its synthesis carries no independent directional weight. It contributes an index of original studies and brings out endpoint, exposure and interpretation differences. No included comparison provides a roasted-shoot estimate in adults with Parkinson disease.
