The complete meeting publication includes separate cases of recurrent Charles Bonnet phenomena, severe malnutrition after sleeve gastrectomy, medication-associated perceptual symptoms and vision loss in a person with Parkinson. The original members do not connect measured dried fig intake to Parkinson visual episode frequency.

Member control 4273198 describes nightly visual experiences after retinal surgery and substantial vision loss. Control 4266728 reports severe nutritional deficiencies and encephalopathy with visual symptoms after sleeve gastrectomy. Control 4273242 describes audio-visual symptoms around changes in baclofen exposure. Control 4273432 documents loss of vision in one person with Parkinson disease. Control 4273068 concerns interviews on coping and family support. Sample, diagnosis and exposure remain separate across members.
