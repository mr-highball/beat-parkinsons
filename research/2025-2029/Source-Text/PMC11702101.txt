Title: Positron emission tomography tracers for synucleinopathies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11702101/
PMID: 39757220
PMCID: PMC11702101
DOI: 10.1186/s13024-024-00787-9
Publication date: 2025-01-05 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The review compares PET tracer development for alpha-synuclein and imaging by synucleinopathy subtype. Its evidence concerns diagnostic signals and research limitations, without an ingredient exposure or clinical visual episode-frequency endpoint.
