Title: Kefir peptides enhance neuron viability in a in vitro model of β-amyloid aggregation

Inspected extent: Full published conference abstract with exact peptide identifiers and the before- and after-aggregation outcome units.

The authors report cell viability and oxidative stress measures after treatment with four kefir-derived peptides in an Alzheimer-related cellular model. M11, M20 and M25 favored viability in the early comparison, while PW did not give a clear early effect. The later comparison reports higher viability. These are cellular endpoints. The preparation and model do not provide evidence on drinking plain kefir or clinical fall frequency.

The peptide names, cell model and treatment staging match ACNP 2024 poster P509. Treat this as a companion report with possible data reuse. Poster P496 concerns separate kefir fractions and retinal outcomes in flies and cells and does not establish an independent effect on falls.
