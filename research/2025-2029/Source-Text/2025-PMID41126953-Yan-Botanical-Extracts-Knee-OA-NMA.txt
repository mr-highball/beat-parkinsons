Title: Efficacy of botanical extracts for knee osteoarthritis: a network meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 41126953
PMCID: PMC12537895
DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2025.1619589
First published: 2025
Authors: Yan C, Du X, Liu Y, Xia F, Yu H, Zhu Y et al.
Journal: Front Pharmacol
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12537895/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Systematic review and network meta-analysis of botanical extracts for knee osteoarthritis (Yan et al., 2025, PMC12537895) pooling the two Nash et al. Q-Actin Cucumis sativus extract RCTs (2018 and 2023), yielding a pooled pain mean difference of 6.65 (95% CI 3.83 to 9.48) while explicitly warning that the evidence relies on only two modest trials from the same investigator group and requires independent confirmation. Secondary review synthesis; does not test whole pickled cucumbers or Parkinson pain.
