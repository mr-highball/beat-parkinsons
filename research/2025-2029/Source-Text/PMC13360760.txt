The complete published congress supplement contains separate whole-diet protocols, a nutrition education pilot, dietary risk associations and medication or surgery psychosis analyses. No inspected member exposed a Parkinson cohort to dried figs and tracked visual episode frequency. The whole-diet, constipation and psychosis findings belong to distinct sets of participants.

Member boundaries: P26.01 is a dietary questionnaire association, P26.02 and P23.04 describe separate trials, and P39.29 measures participation and diet score in a short nutrition-education pilot. P30.02 describes retrospective psychosis after deep brain stimulation. P31.03 analyzes trial NCT01174004 by baseline sleep status, using a composite psychosis scale. Each member has its own participants and outcomes. The frozen dried fig exposure was absent.

Other members report separate clinical outcomes. P25.07 compares harmonica plus speech work against home speech work in 22 people, and the abstract reports speech results without separately measured saliva control. P25.08 is a single-group 20-person swallowing-exercise pilot. It reports improved spontaneous swallowing frequency through one month, with drooling identified among baseline symptoms and no standalone drooling change in the published abstract. The registered study NCT07356414 is a separate randomized comparison with a target of 80 and had no posted outcomes on the checked snapshot. P27.17 compares 65 Parkinson participants and 25 controls: the reported mean unstimulated flow was 0.32 versus 0.20 mL/min, within the authors' normal ranges, while questionnaire-reported dry mouth did not clearly differ. LBP17.02 surveys the impact of self-image and names drooling among reported symptoms, and LBP42.02 describes symptom frequencies across separate groups in a prodromal interview study. None of these members assigned raw fig or measured an effect from its ingestion.
