The complete published congress supplement contains separate whole-diet protocols, a nutrition education pilot, dietary risk associations and medication or surgery psychosis analyses. No inspected member exposed a Parkinson cohort to dried figs and tracked visual episode frequency. The whole-diet, constipation and psychosis findings belong to distinct sets of participants.

Member boundaries: P26.01 is a dietary questionnaire association, P26.02 and P23.04 describe separate trials, and P39.29 measures participation and diet score in a short nutrition-education pilot. P30.02 describes retrospective psychosis after deep brain stimulation. P31.03 analyzes trial NCT01174004 by baseline sleep status, using a composite psychosis scale. Each member has its own participants and outcomes. The frozen dried fig exposure was absent.
