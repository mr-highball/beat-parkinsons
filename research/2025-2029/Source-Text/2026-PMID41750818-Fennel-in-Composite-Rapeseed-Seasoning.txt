Title: Effects of Frying Temperature and Composite Spices on the Release Characteristics of Rapeseed Seasoning Oil
PMID: 41750818
PMCID: PMC12939693
DOI: 10.3390/foods15040626
Publication date: 2026-02-09
Citation: Mulati and colleagues. Foods 15(4), 626.
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2304-8158/15/4/626
Coverage: Primary study body and processing methods.

Fennel seeds were held at 0.3% relative to the rapeseed oil mass in the composite formulation. The authors changed frying conditions and the proportions of several other ingredients. Outcomes covered volatile profiles, sensory properties and oil characteristics. The experiment verifies heated fennel as part of a food preparation with other spices. It gives no separate fennel contrast, consumption study or tremor outcome.
