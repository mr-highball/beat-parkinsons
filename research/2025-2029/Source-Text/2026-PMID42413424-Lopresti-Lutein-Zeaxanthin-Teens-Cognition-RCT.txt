Title: Supplementation with lutein and zeaxanthin increases macular pigment optical density and cognitive performance in healthy teenagers in a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
PMID: 42413424
DOI: 10.1016/j.nutres.2026.06.005
First published: 2026
Authors: Lopresti AL, Smith SJ
Journal: Nutrition Research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42413424/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (ACTRN12625000047493) in 82 healthy teenagers aged 13-18 receiving isolated lutein (10 mg) + zeaxanthin (2 mg) capsules vs sunflower oil placebo for 6 months. Improved MPOD, objective attention (P=0.026), and processing speed (P=0.034), with NO effect on visual reasoning, nonverbal memory, self-reported attention, or sleep. Isolated carotenoid capsule in teenagers is remote from roasted whole kale in older adults with Parkinson's disease.
