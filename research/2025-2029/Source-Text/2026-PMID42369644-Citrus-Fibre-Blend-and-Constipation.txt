Title: Citrus-containing fibre blend in adults with functional constipation
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13305325/
PMID: 42369644
PMCID: PMC13305325
DOI: 10.1002/fsn3.72048
Publication date: 2026-06-26
Date basis: Publication date in the NLM record.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full original, including participant criteria, formulation, Table 2, Table 3 and fitted contrasts.

The blinded randomized trial assigned 54 adults with functional constipation, 27 per arm. Parkinson and Alzheimer disease were exclusion criteria. The active formulation combined resistant wheat dextrin, pectin, insoluble citrus fibre and oat fibre. Orange flavor was present in the comparison product as well. There was no arm isolating citrus fibre or administering raw orange.

Reported mean bowel movements per week were about 1.2 in the active group and 1.1 in placebo at baseline, increasing to 3.6 and 1.8 at week four. The results section gives a fitted within-active-arm change of 2.43 (SE .23, p<.001) and a separate week-four active-versus-placebo contrast of 1.88 (SE .25, p<.001). The fitted week-four stool-form contrast also favored the blend. The trial estimates an effect for the formulation in its enrolled population.
