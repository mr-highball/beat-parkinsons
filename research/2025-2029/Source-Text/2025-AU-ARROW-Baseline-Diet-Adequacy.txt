Title: Baseline diet quality and adequacy of AU-ARROW participants: preliminary findings

DOI: 10.1002/alz70860_106476
Publication year: 2025
Registry: ACTRN12621001760864

Text coverage: published baseline conference report and university publication metadata.

The conference report concerns diet quality before the intervention in AU-ARROW participants. The reports describe nutrient adequacy and food groups, without giving a randomized anxiety change or an isolated olive-oil effect. The baseline report, protocol and trial registry are one family. The ANZCTR record listed 510 enrolled and final data collection anticipated for August 31, 2027 at the November 26, 2026 check.
