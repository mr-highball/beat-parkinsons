Title: Heterogeneity of dementia with lewy bodies: Insights from clinical presentations, neuropathology, and biomarkers
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12727820/
PMID: 41432804
PMCID: PMC12727820
DOI: 10.1007/s10072-025-08725-3
Publication date: 2025-12-23 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The review synthesizes differences in dementia with Lewy bodies clinical presentation, biomarkers and neuropathology. It distinguishes co-pathologies but contains no new dated food-intake or visual-episode comparison.
