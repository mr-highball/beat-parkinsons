Title: Predictors of shunt responsiveness and outcomes in idiopathic normal pressure hydrocephalus: a retrospective cohort study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13009103/
PMID: 41870646
PMCID: PMC13009103
DOI: 10.1007/s00701-026-06839-x
Publication date: 2026-03-23 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The retrospective clinical series asks which patients assessed for idiopathic normal-pressure hydrocephalus responded to shunting. Its exposure is surgery in another diagnostic group with motor and cognitive outcomes. Fig consumption and the frozen Parkinson symptom endpoint are absent.
