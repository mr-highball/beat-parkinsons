Bilateral deep brain stimulation for severe Alzheimer's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12004396/
PMID: 40243219
PMCID: PMC12004396
DOI: 10.1111/cns.70285
First publication: 2025-04 (earliest verified issue month)
Coverage: PMC original Methods, clinical scales and adverse-event table

The nonrandomized study compares stimulation of the fornix and the nucleus basalis of Meynert in 20 people with severe Alzheimer disease. Apathy is listed as an example of the disease's neuropsychiatric symptoms. The adverse-event table records postoperative pain, and the article compares composite neuropsychiatric scores. There is no dried-date exposure or identifiable date-associated pain or apathy contrast in Parkinson disease.
