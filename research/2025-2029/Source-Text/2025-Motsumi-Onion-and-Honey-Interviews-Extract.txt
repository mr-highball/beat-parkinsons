Title: Coping with COVID-19 using traditional medicine: perspectives from Joe Morolong, Northern Cape
Authors: Masego M Motsumi, Livhuwani D Nemakonde
Original publisher full text: https://hsag.co.za/index.php/hsag/rt/printerFriendly/2773/5469
Date: 2025-01-23
Coverage: factual extract of Methods and the section naming spring onion and common onion

The qualitative study held focus groups with a convenience sample of 100 participants in five villages in the Joe Morolong Local Municipality. Interviewees described two practices involving onion. One was placing cut onion in a room and breathing its odor. Another was drinking onion prepared with boiled water and honey for cough and influenza. The section distinguishes common onion from spring onion by scientific name but does not confirm the species for the particular honey mixture cited. Descriptions represent recalled practice and perceptions, with no measured voice changes, controlled onion effect or Parkinson cohort.
