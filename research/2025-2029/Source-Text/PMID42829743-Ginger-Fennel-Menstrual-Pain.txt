Ginger and fennel menstrual pain and crossover inconsistency
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13633784/
PMID: 42829743
PMCID: PMC13633784
DOI: 10.4103/jehp.jehp_391_25
Registration: CTRI/2020/03/024407
Coverage: Original August 31, 2026 body, Tables 2–3 and official WHO registry

The article reports 384 participants, all-person crossover in its Methods, and three independent groups of 128 in the reported analysis. The registry gives a 378-person planned crossover. The ginger-labeled arm uses a Neotea Thirikadum product identified with Zingiber officinale, and the article mentions hydroalcoholic extracts. Exact product contents and the analysis partition remain unresolved. Table 2 reports post-treatment fennel VAS 4.44 while the text and abstract give 4.61. A precise independently attributable ginger treatment estimate is unavailable.
