Title: Predictors of drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease

PMID: 39812680
DOI: 10.1007/s00415-024-12739-w
Publication date: 2025-01-15
Coverage: Paraphrased complete institutional copy of the original, including Tables 2–4.

The study compared 42 participants reporting daytime drooling with 59 participants with Parkinson disease who did not report daytime loss, using the authors' MDS-UPDRS item 2.2 threshold. Table 2 reports inconclusive group comparisons for unstimulated and stimulated salivary flow (p=.057 and .357). Across the ordinal severity scores, the unstimulated flow correlation was small and positive (rho=.196, p=.034), while the stimulated association had p=.166. A contrast of thresholded groups is a different analysis from a correlation across ranks. The clinical comparisons provide no food intervention.

The same hospital, February–July 2023 collection period, 101 Parkinson participants and approval reference 2284 are reported in the separate 2025 case–control paper, PMID 40257190. Treat the Parkinson estimates as likely overlapping. The 101 matched controls and perceived-impact comparison belong to the later publication. The 2026 one-year follow-up reports 73 participants and separate within-person comparisons. The individual overlap with that follow-up is still being verified.
