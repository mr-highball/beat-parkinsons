A case report describes behavioral-variant frontotemporal dementia with genetic findings. A caregiver reported persistent apathy within a broader behavioral course. The course supplies neither a Parkinson apathy comparison nor one involving dried dates. The clinical report does not isolate pain severity as an outcome.
