Title: Olive oil and dysphagia items in a Thai Parkinson cohort
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/1877718X261445952
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Phokaewvarangkul and colleagues, 2026. Seventy Thai participants with Parkinson disease contributed dietary recall, clinical swallowing status and nutritional assessments. Twelve reported daily olive oil use. The table contrasts BMI groups, with 2/13 underweight versus 10/57 normal or overweight daily users, p = .609. Dysphagia and food consistency are measured separately; no contrast by olive oil grade or consumption group predicts or explains swallowing severity. The data are cross-sectional.
