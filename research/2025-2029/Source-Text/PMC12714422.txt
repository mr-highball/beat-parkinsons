Title: A Unified Continuous Staging Framework for Alzheimer's Disease and Lewy Body Dementia via Hierarchical Anatomical Features
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12714422/
PMID: 41424643
PMCID: PMC12714422
DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-04947-6_2
Publication date: 2025-09-21 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The machine-learning framework uses anatomical imaging features to model continuous diagnostic stages across Alzheimer and Lewy body dementia groups. Its performance measures are classification and staging, with no dietary or clinical visual-event contrast.
