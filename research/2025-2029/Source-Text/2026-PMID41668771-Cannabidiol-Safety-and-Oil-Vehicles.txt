Original study scope
The safety assessment identifies coconut-derived MCT, olive oil, sunflower oil and corn oil as carriers or comparators. A bipolar depression example uses a CBD formulation. Its drug exposure and population differ, and date is temporal. The record does not test customary oils against frozen Parkinson symptoms.

Continuing evidence gaps
Separate oil effects from drug and vehicle measurements. Look for an oil-only randomized comparison, a matched culinary preparation and clinical symptom data.
