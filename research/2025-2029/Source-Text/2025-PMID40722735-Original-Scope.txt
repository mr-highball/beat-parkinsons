The pilot study tests repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation for levodopa-induced dyskinesias in Parkinson disease and reports no clear efficacy for the tested protocol.

The experimental exposure is stimulation, and the main clinical endpoint is dyskinesia. The article gives no dried-date pain comparison.
