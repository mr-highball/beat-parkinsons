Forehead oregano essential oil and depression scores
Source: https://www.ijmfmap.in/view_pdf.php?id=353
DOI: 10.53552/ijmfmap.11.1.2025.225-234
Coverage: June 2025 issue; original Methods, Results and Tables 1 through 4 read in full

The report describes a before-and-after series using Origanum vulgare essential oil applied to the forehead. The paired analysis used 200 observations. The mean PHQ-9 score moved from 22.32 to 5.60 and the reported PROMIS depression score from 26.49 to 7.25. There was no concurrent control arm and the results did not give a separately measured anxiety outcome. The design cannot separate change over time from an effect of the essential oil. It provides no ingested dried-leaf or Parkinson anxiety comparison.
