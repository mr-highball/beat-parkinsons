Millar et al. Feasibility of implementing a dietary intervention in older adults with mild depressive symptoms and low activity: lessons learned. Nutrients. Published November 7, 2026. PMID 43030321. DOI 10.3390/nu18213694.

Linked trial and results: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05735587

The parent RCT randomized 29 non-Parkinson participants and 22 completed. Table 4 records constipation at about six weeks, judged possibly related, in one blueberry-powder participant who did not withdraw. This is the same adverse event as the one of 15 versus none of 14 counts in the official registry. One blueberry participant withdrew for increased bowel movement frequency, a distinct event. The study gives no constipation severity or raw fruit effect. The published supplement contains baseline characteristics. The paper does not give longitudinal depression or motivation estimates; the registry reports motivation changes with no reported inferential comparison.
