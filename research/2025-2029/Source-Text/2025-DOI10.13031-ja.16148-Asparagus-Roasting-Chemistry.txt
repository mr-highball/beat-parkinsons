Title: Effects of Drying and Roasting on Value-Added Utilization of Discarded Part of Asparagus (Asparagus officinalis L.).
Source: https://elibrary.asabe.org/abstract.asp?aid=55213
DOI: 10.13031/ja.16148
Publication date: 2025
Review depth: abstract
Inspected extent: Official Journal of the ASABE summary with its original chemistry findings.

The researchers characterize the hard lower stem usually discarded during preparation. Drying and roasting changed several reported nutrients and volatiles. Roasting lowered measured rutin and reduced some compounds concentrated by drying, while heating formed characteristic volatiles. The report concerns composition rather than a human symptom experiment.

Relevance to roasted asparagus in claim 0837
This preparation study supports treating roasting as an exposure variable. The accessible summary gives no comparison of roasted edible spears against standardized enzyme-treated stem extract, and no fatigue or Parkinson endpoint. The composition results cannot establish how much of any extract-relevant compound a serving of roasted spears delivers.
