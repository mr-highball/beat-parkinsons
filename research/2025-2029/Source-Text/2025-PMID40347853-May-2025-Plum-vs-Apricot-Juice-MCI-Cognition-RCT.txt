Title: Queen garnet plum juice supplementation does not provide additional cognitive benefits over a group-based memory program in older adults with mild cognitive impairment: A randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 40347853
DOI: 10.1016/j.nutres.2025.04.006
First published: 2025
Authors: May N, Fitzgerald Z, Broyd S, Rosario VD, Roodenrys S, Bliokas V et al.
Journal: Nutrition research (New York, N.Y.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40347853/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind 8-week randomized clinical trial in N=42 older adults with mild cognitive impairment (MCI) enrolled in a 6-week memory program, receiving either anthocyanin-rich Queen Garnet plum juice or apricot juice (control comparator). Cognition (RAVLT, CFT, RPA-ProMem), mood, and BP were assessed; apricot juice served as the low-anthocyanin comparator with no cognitive benefit over baseline/program.
