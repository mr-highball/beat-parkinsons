Marjoram oil massage and jaw ultrasound in bruxism
Source: https://bezmialemscience.org/media/issues/2025-013-002-supplement/en/2025-013-002-supplement.pdf
Coverage: Original Bezmialem proceedings abstract OP-11 page S11

Tekçe, Alagöz and colleagues report a randomized 30-person comparison of marjoram oil manually applied as massage versus botulinum toxin. They report no clear between-group masseter thickness or elastography difference at any observed time. The study did not give dream-enactment or bruxism episode counts in the abstract. Its design, author and enrollment closely fit NCT06674733, but the conference record omits a registered identifier.
