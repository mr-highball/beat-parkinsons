Topical extracted clove eugenol and acute toothache
Source: https://kstom.ru/ks/article/download/0113-13/pdf
DOI: 10.37988/1811-153X_2025_1_92
Coverage: Original Methods and Tables 2–3, factual text

Razzaq, Nasir and Al-Salihi first published April 7, 2025. Fifty-one volunteers with acute toothache received 2% extracted eugenol in gel, vehicle gel or 10% benzocaine, with 17 per arm. Participants applied the product to the painful area or cavity and sent modified McGill questionnaire feedback. At five minutes 14/17 eugenol recipients, 0/17 vehicle recipients and 15/17 benzocaine recipients reported relief. At 72 hours 13/17, 0/17 and 12/17, respectively, reported relief lasting that long. The Discussion calls the study randomized, but the Methods do not describe allocation or blinding, and the tables give descriptive counts without comparative uncertainty. The product was topical clove-derived eugenol.
