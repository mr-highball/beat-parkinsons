Title: Effects of rice bran arabinoxylan compound on quality of life of cancer patients during active treatment: a randomised placebo-controlled pilot trial.
PMID: 41771978
PMCID: PMC13057111
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-41554-8
Registry ID: ACTRN12619000562178p
First published: 2026
Authors: Ooi SL, Micalos PS, Zielinski R, Lacey J, Grant S, Kao S
Journal: Scientific reports
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13057111/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twenty-four-week double-blind placebo-controlled pilot RCT (ACTRN12619000562178p) in n=29 solid-tumor cancer patients on systemic therapy (12 RBAC 3 g/d vs 17 placebo). Rice bran arabinoxylan compound improved global QoL (p=0.031), role/social functioning (p<0.001, p=0.037), and cognitive functioning trend (p=0.055), while placebo experienced significantly worse fatigue, pain, dyspnea, and appetite loss (all p<0.05), with no intervention-related AEs. Limits: enzymatically modified rice bran arabinoxylan extract, small n=29 oncology sample.
