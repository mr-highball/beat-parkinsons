Pumpkin seed oil and weekly pressure in hypertensive rats
Source: https://www.medical-health-psychiatry.com/1/article/view/42
DOI: 10.70844/jmhrp.2025.2.1.34
Coverage: Primary Methods p. 2 and Results p. 3 checked in publisher PDF; Figure 2 shows the weekly series

Adeniran and Oladapo published on March 27, 2025. The week-8 Results paragraph gives systolic pressure of 134.20 ± 1.53 mmHg in the hypertensive pumpkin-oil group and 149.40 ± 0.40 in the hypertensive control (p = .0022). The 162.17 ± 2.30 control value in the abstract is the week-5 control in the Results. It is not the concurrent week-8 comparator. The paper describes commercial oral oil and leaves its pressing grade unverified. The Methods describe the normotensive oil group inconsistently and specify different delivery details for the hypertension group. Tail-cuff pressure in animals with induced hypertension supplies vascular context and no standing, lightheadedness or Parkinson outcome.
