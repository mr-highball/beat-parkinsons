Title: Exploring the promising potential of alcohol extract from the aerial part of dill in ameliorating DSS-induced ulcerative colitis in mice
PMID: 39667686
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2024.119237
First published: 2025
Authors: Qiao Y, Liu H, Li X, Wang Y
Journal: Journal of Ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39667686/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Preclinical mouse study and Uyghur ethnopharmacology report on 70% ethanol extract of fresh aerial parts (leaves/stems) of Anethum graveolens L. (200 and 400 mg/kg/day gavage x 7 days) in 2.5% DSS-induced ulcerative colitis C57BL/6 mice. Extract lowered Disease Activity Index, colonic IL-6/IL-1beta/MDA/MPO, protected goblet cells/MUC2 and tight-junction proteins (ZO-1, Occludin, Claudin-1/2), and improved gut-microbiota balance. Notes traditional Uyghur aerial-dill use for food stagnation and intestinal obstruction; however, mouse DSS colitis is remote from PD constipation.
