Tağraf et al., primary randomized trial PMID41666212, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13347589/. Official registration NCT06892418. Original factual extract of Methods, Results and Table 2 and current registration.

Forty people with Parkinson disease received standard care or standard care plus a six-month personalized plant-rich nutritional and time-restricted eating program. Mean SAS went from 13.65 to 9.00 and 13.05 to 13.60, respectively. The longitudinal time-by-group comparison was F(1,38)=99.30, p<.001. Table 2 reports p=.333 for the post-intervention between-group comparison, and separate prose describes a significant endpoint difference. The registry lists only motor change and no posted group results. Its actual 2024 study dates precede the paper's March 2025 ethics date. No soybean oil assignment, intake amount, or refining method is available.
