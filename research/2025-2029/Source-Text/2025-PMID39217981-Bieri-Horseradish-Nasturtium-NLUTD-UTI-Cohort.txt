Title: Effects of a Prophylactic Treatment with Horseradish Root and Nasturtium Herb on Urinary Tract Infections in Individuals with Chronic Neurogenic Lower Urinary Tract Dysfunction: A Retrospective Cohort Study
PMID: 39217981
DOI: 10.1159/000541248
First published: 2025
Authors: Bieri AM, Wöllner J, Pannek J, Krebs J
Journal: Urologia Internationalis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39217981/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Retrospective single-arm pre-post cohort (n=43 adults with chronic neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction, mean age 49 y, median NLUTD duration 17.9 y) taking fixed 2-herb horseradish root + nasturtium herb prophylaxis for >=12 mo. Recurrent symptomatic UTIs (>=3/y) fell from 58.1% to 23.3% (p<0.0001) and antibiotic prescriptions fell (p=0.008). Uncontrolled retrospective 2-herb combo targeting bacterial UTI episodes rather than non-infectious PD urinary urgency.
