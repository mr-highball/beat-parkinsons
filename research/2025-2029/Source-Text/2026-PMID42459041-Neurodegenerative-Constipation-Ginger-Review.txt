Title: Non-Pharmacological Constipation Management in People With Neurodegenerative Disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42459041/
Publisher: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/01939459261451721
PMID: 42459041
DOI: 10.1177/01939459261451721
Publication date: 2026-07-16
Text coverage: Distilled original author abstract and publisher references. Complete review body remains an access lead.

This scoping review identifies one ginger intervention among 13 included neurodegenerative studies. The publisher bibliography lists the original Foshati randomized trial, PMID 37891539, as reference 46. That trial enrolled people with relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis and used ginger capsules. The ginger study is one underlying cohort to which the review points. Parkinson studies in the bibliography include separate bowel interventions. The review's juxtaposition does not make the ginger arm a Parkinson study or identify it as fresh grated ginger.
