Title: Boiled potato passes fork cut but fails fork pressure
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jtxs.70095
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Kovan Ismael-Mohammed and colleagues. Journal of Texture Studies, first published July 2, 2026. Methods and Tables 5, 6 and 11.
Laboratory texture results varied by test. Boiled potato failed the instrumental fork-pressure criterion, and passed fork cut with hardness 270.86 ± 33.53 g against the automatic 378 g threshold. It also failed the instrumental spoon-pressure test. A subset of the 24 healthy older volunteers using the IDDSI system placed boiled potato across levels 5 to 7.
The people rated texture and classifications. The study did not measure an effect on swallowing difficulty severity in Parkinson disease or use baked potato.
