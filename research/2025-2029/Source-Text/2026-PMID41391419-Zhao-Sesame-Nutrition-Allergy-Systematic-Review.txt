Primary Abstract:
Sesame (Sesamum indicum L.), an ancient oilseed crop, is prized for its rich nutritional and bioactive profile, which confers various health benefits. However, despite its widespread culinary and industrial applications, sesame has emerged as a growing allergenic concern, eliciting IgE-mediated reactions in approximately 0.1-0.2 % of the population. A comprehensive understanding of its phytochemistry, health-promoting properties, and allergenic risks is essential to ensure safer utilization and effective allergen management in food products. This review provides a systematic overview of sesame seed, examining their nutritional composition, health-promoting properties, and associated allergenic hazards. Future research should focus on clarifying bioactive mechanisms, dose effects, and synergistic interactions to optimize therapeutic applications. Additionally, innovations in hypoallergenic processing and clinical validation are essential to maximize safety and functionality, facilitating broader use in functional foods and sustainable diets.
