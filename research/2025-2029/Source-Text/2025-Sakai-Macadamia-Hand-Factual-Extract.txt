Title: Aromatherapy treatment in a carpal tunnel syndrome case
Original title: 手根管症候群の被験者に対するアロマセラピートリートメント事例報告
Author: 坂井恭子 (Sakai)
Source: The Journal of Holistic Sciences 19(1), 15–24. Issued April 10, 2025.
Original: https://rahos.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/9c1a55f609bbc6037901e711f467c512.pdf
Issue date: https://rahos.org/magazines
Coverage: English factual extract from the original Japanese report and Figures 1–2. No open reuse license verified.

A participant with carpal tunnel syndrome had seven visits between December 2023 and February 2024. The treatment included manual treatment of the upper body and a topical application of five essential oils carried in macadamia nut oil. Application over the affected forearm and hand used no direct pressure. The examiner checked writing, coin pickup and clothespin use. Improvements varied by visit and task, and the reported longer-term changes were qualitative. Figure 1 and the numbered visit observations give right grip of 23.6 kg at visit one and 24.4 kg at visit seven. The difference is 0.8 kg. The discussion gives a conflicting 0.9 kg figure for the right hand. Left grip went from 24.8 to 25.7 kg. The report is an uncontrolled case with mixed treatments and no documented cold pressing, oral exposure or Parkinson diagnosis.
