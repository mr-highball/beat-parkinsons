Title: Tadalafil monotherapy versus tadalafil combined with pumpkin seed oil for chronic pelvic pain syndrome
Source: https://doi.org/10.21608/aimj.2025.446526
DOI: 10.21608/aimj.2025.446526
Publication date: 2025-04-30
Date basis: Publisher Crossref metadata.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Author abstract reproduced in bibliographic index, independently matched to publisher metadata. Full tables were not recovered.

The randomized study compared tadalafil alone and tadalafil plus pumpkin seed oil in 50 men with chronic pelvic pain syndrome. The authors report clinically significant overall improvement in 12 of 25 in the medication-only group and 11 of 25 in the combination group. They report a relative advantage in the pain domain without an effect size in the accessible abstract. No preparation grade or Parkinson outcome was verified.
