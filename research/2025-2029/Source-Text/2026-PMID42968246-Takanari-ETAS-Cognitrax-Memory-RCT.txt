Title: Intake of ETAS® Improves Cognitive Function in Healthy Middle-Aged and Older Adults: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial.
PMID: 42968246
DOI: 10.3177/jnsv.72.415
Registry ID: UMIN000048006
First published: 2026
Authors: Takanari J, Goto K, Misu M, Shio T, Fukuchi Y, Homma K, et al.
Journal: Journal of nutritional science and vitaminology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42968246/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled parallel trial (Takanari et al., 2026, J Nutr Sci Vitaminol, UMIN000048006) assigning 66 cognitively normal Japanese adults aged >=40 years (MMSE-J >=24; n=60 in efficacy analysis) to 300 mg/day ETAS (Asparagus officinalis stem extract) or placebo for 12 weeks. Primary endpoint Cognitrax composite memory (verbal + visual memory) improved significantly vs placebo at 6 weeks (+12.8 points, p=0.006) and 12 weeks (+9.0 points, p=0.048) with zero ETAS-related adverse events. Limits: Amino Up authored/funded; abstract does not report separate significant between-arm effects on Cognitrax attention or executive-function domains; healthy non-PD adults and concentrated stem extract rather than culinary asparagus.
