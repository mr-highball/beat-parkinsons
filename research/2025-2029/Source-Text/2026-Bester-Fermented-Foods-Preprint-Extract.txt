Title: Real-World Fermented Foods and Their Impact on Gut and Brain Health: A Multi-Arm Intervention Study in Healthy Adults
DOI: 10.20944/preprints202601.0406.v1
First public publication: 2026-01-07
Source: https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202601.0406/v1
Format: Factual extraction from the original version-1 preprint. Peer review is unverified for this version.
Bester and colleagues analyzed 43 healthy adults in a four-arm intervention with dairy kefir, coconut kefir, a fermented mixture of red cabbage, beetroot, caraway and salt, or no intervention. The mixed-vegetable arm varied several foods. Adverse symptoms were to be recorded at interim and final visits, but the paper gives neither an orthostatic test nor symptom-specific counts. Between-group DASS-21 comparisons were inconclusive, including total score p=.451. Within-group mood results do not establish a treatment contrast. No Parkinson group or stand-related outcome was reported. The main text provides no registry link for this cohort.
