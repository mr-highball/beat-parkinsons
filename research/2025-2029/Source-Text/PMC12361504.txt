Title: Association of the number of minor hallucinations and well-structured visual hallucinations in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12361504/
PMID: 40825977
PMCID: PMC12361504
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-025-01106-9
Publication date: 2025-08-18 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The study included 372 people with Parkinson disease. It recorded 114 with one or more minor phenomena and 55 with visual hallucinations, with overlap. These counts describe people and clinical phenotypes, rather than the number of episodes after food consumption.
