Original study scope
The healthy-aging cohort collected dietary frequencies in broad groups and tracked mobility and cognition. Lentils are included in a combined plant-food group. The source does not give standalone olive oil or raw orange intake estimates.

Continuing evidence gaps
Gait and dietary categories cannot be used to attribute walking speed to lentils separately or falls to raw orange. The cohort was studied as older adults, not as a Parkinson treatment comparison.
