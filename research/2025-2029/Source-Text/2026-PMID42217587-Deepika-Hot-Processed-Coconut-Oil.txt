The paper studies hot-processed virgin coconut oil, called Urukku Velichenna, separately from the earlier coconut oil and edible-crab preparation. It describes origin in particular villages in Kerala. Cell and rat experiments report favorable biochemical, tissue and broad behavioral findings. The abstract provides no separately identified endpoints for Parkinson rest tremor, daytime sleepiness or executive impairment, and no human outcomes. The original behavioral assays should be checked by outcome before changing the scope of a frozen claim. The earlier Deepika publications and P90 poster use preparations and research designs whose findings should remain identifiable separately.
