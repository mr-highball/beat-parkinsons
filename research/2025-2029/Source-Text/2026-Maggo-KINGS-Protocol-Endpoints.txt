KINGS protocol clarifies cultivar and prespecified outcomes
https://www.researchprotocols.org/2026/1/e75286/

Journal publication January 13, 2026, following a 2025 preprint. The study used two whole peeled green Hayward kiwifruit daily versus maltodextrin in adults with functional constipation or constipation-predominant irritable bowel syndrome. The protocol reports 63 randomized and 57 included in its intention-to-treat sample description. It identifies the outcomes and does not report an attention result. The Ng thesis and registry describe the same trial family.
