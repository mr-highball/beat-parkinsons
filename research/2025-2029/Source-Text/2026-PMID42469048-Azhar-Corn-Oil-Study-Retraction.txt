Reviewed: 2026-11-27

The editor-in-chief withdrew the report after review of updated data identified major discrepancies and inaccuracies in the data and analyses that materially affected the results and conclusions. The withdrawn trial report first appeared online on December 27, 2025. Its publication identifier is PMID41461240 and its DOI is 10.1016/j.jad.2025.121055.

The trial registration NCT07157241 independently documents an omega-3 comparison using corn oil as the control. It lists the Epworth Sleepiness Scale and supplies no structured results. Retain the registry as protocol context. The withdrawn article contributes zero weight to directions for refined corn oil in depression, daytime sleepiness and insomnia (Round 1 claims 0205, 0308 and 0726).
