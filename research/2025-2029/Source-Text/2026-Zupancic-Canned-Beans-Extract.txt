Factual extract: randomized crossover, eleven healthy physically active adults (10 male, 1 female; mean age 24.4 years); ten completed both trial conditions. The beans-and-rice condition used 143 g commercial canned Bush's black beans, drained and microwaved, and 108 g long-grain jasmine rice cooked 12 minutes, with approximately 250 mL water. Comparator was a matched isolated mixture of crystalline amino acids, maltodextrin, cellulose, pectin and soy oil. Food ingestion followed resistance exercise; biopsies and blood samples assessed outcomes through 5 hours. Postexercise myofibrillar protein synthesis was 0.057 ± 0.013 %/h for the meal versus 0.052 ± 0.013 %/h for the isolated mixture; mean difference 0.005 %/h, 95% CI −0.008 to 0.018, p=0.260. No Parkinson sleep or balance outcome was reported. This is not an isolated black-bean comparison.
