Title: Toxicological profile and neuromodulatory effects of a secoiridoid-rich extra-virgin olive oil extract in a zebrafish model of chemically induced seizures
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42772005/
PMID: 42772005
DOI: 10.1016/j.biopha.2026.119950
First public: 2026-09-22
Coverage: Factual extract from the original abstract.

The investigators tested a laboratory extract enriched in oleocanthal and oleacein against vehicle-matched controls in zebrafish larvae undergoing a chemically induced seizure challenge. Behavioral measures were integrated activity and movement bursts during light–dark recording. The authors report reductions on some within-run contrasts and context-dependent interaction estimates. The solvent influenced baseline behavior, and the study did not use electrophysiology to confirm a seizure effect. The light–dark recording conditions do not represent an anxiety assessment. There was no culinary oil or Parkinson comparison.
