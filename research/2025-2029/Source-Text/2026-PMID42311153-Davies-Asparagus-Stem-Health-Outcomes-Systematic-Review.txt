Title: The effect of asparagus stem consumption on health outcomes in adults: a systematic review.
PMID: 42311153
DOI: 10.1017/S000711452610779X
First published: 2026
Authors: Davies A, Lamport D, Homma K, Takanari J, Hepsomali P
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42311153/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

PRISMA systematic review (Davies et al., 2026, Br J Nutr, PROSPERO CRD42025636834; co-authored with Amino Up investigators) synthesizing 18 human studies (13 RCTs, 3 pre-post studies; 503 total participants) of orally administered Asparagus officinalis stem products through April 2025. Reports modest benefits across small heterogeneous trials for sleep quality/duration, anxiety/depressive symptoms, cortisol, reaction time, and mental fatigue, while noting small sample sizes, dose heterogeneity, and need for larger long-term clinical trials. Identifies NO Parkinson's disease clinical trials and focuses on stem extracts rather than whole steamed, grilled, or roasted asparagus spears.
