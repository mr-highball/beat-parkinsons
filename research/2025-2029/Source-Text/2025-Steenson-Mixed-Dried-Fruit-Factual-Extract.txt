Steenson et al., Gut 74 Suppl 1, O22, 2025, DOI 10.1136/gutjnl-2025-BSG.22. Registered NCT04086134.

The trial included 150 adults with Rome IV functional constipation. It compared dried prunes, raisins and apricots taken together, a juice mixture from the fruits, and an energy-matched cordial. The primary change in stool weight differed across groups (P = .027). The Cambridge IUNS version gives P = .047 across groups with the same rounded arm changes; the abstracts leave the analysis difference unexplained. PAC-SYM assessments include an abdominal pain item; published summaries do not provide a separate pain estimate. The reports do not establish a raw apricot exposure or a Parkinson pain effect.
