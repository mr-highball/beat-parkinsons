Title: Imposter in the brain: aetiological, clinical and neuroimaging characteristics of Capgras syndrome
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13058470/
PMID: 41059767
PMCID: PMC13058470
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awaf378
Publication date: 2025-10-08 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML

The retrospective study extracts Capgras misidentification and other clinical features from Lewy body disease records, with matched imaging subsets. Co-reported visual hallucinations and the delusional misidentification phenotype are distinct observations. There is no fruit exposure or post-intake episode rate.
