Title: A Multimodal Nomogram Predicting the Risk of Cognitive Impairment in Parkinson Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13728480/
PMID: 42972958
PMCID: PMC13728480
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.70884
Publication date: 2026-11-02 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The study derives and internally validates a multivariable nomogram for future cognitive impairment in Parkinson disease. Predictors include clinical and neuropsychiatric measures. The predicted outcome is cognition with no food-timed hallucination comparison.
