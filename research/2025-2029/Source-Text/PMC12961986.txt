Title: Real-world data of opicapone in patients with Parkinson's disease experiencing motor fluctuations: the OPTIMO study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12961986/
PMID: 41798821
PMCID: PMC12961986
DOI: 10.3389/fneur.2026.1738500
Publication date: 2026-02-19
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC XML article body and embedded tables; separate supplements are outside this extract.

Retrospective treatment records at 16 centers included 245 people with Parkinson disease and motor fluctuations receiving opicapone as an adjunct to existing medication. A pooled item listing nonmotor fluctuations of pain, anxiety and distress was recorded in 53/245 participants before treatment and 37/245 at follow-up (p=.010). The cohort did not have a concurrent untreated comparison. The pooled item counts affected participants and does not isolate pain intensity. The report does not separately compare apathy or dried date consumption.
