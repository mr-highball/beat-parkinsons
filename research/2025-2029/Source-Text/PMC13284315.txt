Title: Personalized metabolite biomarker predictions reveal heterogeneous characteristics of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13284315/
PMID: 42014729
PMCID: PMC13284315
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-026-01337-4
Publication date: 2026-04-21 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The study applies genome-scale models to infer patient-specific metabolite secretion patterns and summarize candidate Parkinson biomarkers. Predicted metabolites, including vitamin-related compounds, are distinct from a measured dietary source. It records no dried fig intake or visual episode frequency after food.
