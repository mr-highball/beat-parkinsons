Title: Vibration stimulation to the lower limbs improves freezing of gait and modifies cortical electrical activities in Parkinson's disease.

The investigators compare lower-limb vibration and gait or cortical outcomes in Parkinson disease. Visual hallucinations appear in the discussion of other nonmotor presentations. The assessed outcomes concern gait freezing and electrophysiology and do not include a dietary visual episode comparison.
