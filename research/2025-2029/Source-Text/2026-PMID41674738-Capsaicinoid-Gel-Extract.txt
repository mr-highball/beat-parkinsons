Title: Acute Dermal Toxicity and Analgesic Effect of a Capsaicinoid Gel Against Formalin-Induced Pain in Wistar Rats

Mwandah and colleagues observed acute toxicity signs in Wistar
rats exposed dermally to a concentrated Capsicum annuum
capsaicinoid gel. Table 2 lists tremors in the two higher
exposure groups within the first day, together with autonomic
and local reactions. The clinical signs in its toxicity
observations resolved within 24–48 hours. The table marks
group-level signs without giving per-animal tremor severity,
a resting state or an estimate in a Parkinson model.

The separate formalin test measures pain behavior. Both studies
use dermal gel, which differs in route and composition from
oral culinary ground paprika.
