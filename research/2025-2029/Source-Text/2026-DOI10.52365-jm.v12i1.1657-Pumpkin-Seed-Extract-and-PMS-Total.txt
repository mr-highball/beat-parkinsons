The companion 42-person report uses a total premenstrual symptom score without a separate pain outcome. The authors, setting, participant count, and ethics reference match the 2025 trial, while reported dose schedules differ. Participant overlap cannot be established. The reports contribute one potentially overlapping family.

This report gives the higher and lower extract schedules as twice and once daily, respectively. The 2025 pain report uses different frequency and tablet-count shorthand. Neither report specifies a cold-pressed oil exposure.
