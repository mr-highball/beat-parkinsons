Title: Extracted oat fiber doses and exploratory daily sleep duration
First published or posted: 2026-06-10
PMID: 42358297
PMCID: PMC13290552
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2026.1745303
Source URL: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2026.1745303/full
Text type: original-source evidence summary

Marcobal and colleagues report NCT06739941, an open-label study of three doses of extracted short-chain oat fiber in healthy adults over two weeks. Exploratory daily sleep duration did not vary significantly over time or between groups (P>.05). The study used no inactive control and assessed duration rather than clinical insomnia severity. The June paper and preprint DOI 10.64898/2026.01.21.26343795 report the same trial. Neither the fiber preparation nor the sample matches the frozen Parkinson claim.
