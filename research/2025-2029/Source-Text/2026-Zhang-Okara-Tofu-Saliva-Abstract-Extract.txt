Okara tofu and saliva secretion during chewing

Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41931940/
Coverage: Author abstract, publisher body route gated
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First electronically published March 30, 2026. The original abstract compared soybean residue-enriched tofu containing 15% or 50% okara with comparator tofu during chewing and reported higher salivary secretion with the enriched formulations. Number of participants, detailed contrasts and a baked preparation were unverified in the abstract. The study measured acute oral processing and secretion. It did not measure saliva escaping the mouth or Parkinson drooling.
