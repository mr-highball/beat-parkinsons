The published protocol specifies ginger as one odorant in a multiodor intervention for persistent post-COVID olfactory dysfunction. Registered outcomes concern olfaction, and the recruiting record had no results. The protocol and registry are one study family. It does not compare ingested grated ginger or Parkinson olfactory impairment.
