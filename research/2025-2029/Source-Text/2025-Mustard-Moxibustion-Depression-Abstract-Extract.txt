Title: Mixed topical mustard formula and depression scores
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41741978/
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

Original published Chinese trial abstract, first online December 9, 2025. It reports 105 participants with subthreshold depression randomized to two versions of mustard-seed, curcuma and evodia acupoint patches, or placebo. Both active groups had greater PHQ-9 improvement at three and six weeks and at follow-up, with reported p < .05. Original quantified arm scores, detailed species assignments and allocation were not obtained. Exposure was topical and bundled, without oral ground mustard or Parkinson disease.
