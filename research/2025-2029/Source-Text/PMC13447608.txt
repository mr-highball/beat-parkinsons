A cross-sectional diagnostic study analyzed routine samples from 356 participants across neurologic and psychiatric diagnostic groups. Three participants in a dystonia comparison group had negative assay results. Hyposmia was captured in medical histories and was not confirmed by a diagnostic test in every case. Neither clinical feature is a response to sage.
