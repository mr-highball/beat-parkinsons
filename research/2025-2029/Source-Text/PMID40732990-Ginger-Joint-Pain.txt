Ginger extract and joint pain during exercise testing
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/17/14/2365
PMID: 40732990
PMCID: PMC12297875
DOI: 10.3390/nu17142365
Registration: ISRCTN74292348
Coverage: Original July 18, 2025 article, sections 2 and 4.3.2; linked official registry

Broeckel and colleagues report 30 completers, 15 per arm, on supercritical carbon-dioxide-extracted and fermented ginger or placebo. The multivariate WOMAC pain, stiffness and function group-by-time test was inconclusive (p=.690), and no between-group univariate difference was clear. Selected items and within-arm tests show favorable changes. The registration was retrospective, and the conference abstract is another report of the same group. The population and extracted preparation limit transfer to grated ginger or Parkinson pain.
