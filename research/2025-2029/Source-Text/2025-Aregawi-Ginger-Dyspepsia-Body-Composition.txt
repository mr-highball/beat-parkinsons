Title: Investigating the effect of dietary ginger supplementation on body fat loss in patients with functional dyspepsia: An open-label, clinical trial
DOI: 10.1016/j.fbio.2025.107752
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2212429225019297
Registration: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06313814
Published: 2025-11 issue, Food Bioscience 73, 107752.
Archive scope: Factual summary of the original publisher abstract and available methods and ethics excerpts, with matching dissertation section.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The report identifies ethics reference DE RKEB/IKEB 5622-2020 and registration NCT06313814. It gives 62 enrolled and 51 completers. Body composition is assessed in this open-label trial separately from pain and bowel outcomes. The eight-week dissertation describes small body-fat changes without a clear difference on the principal body composition measurements. The publication, the dissertation and the 51-person quality-of-life report share the site, intervention and enrollment profile. The available methods do not establish an independently recruited cohort or a new pain, constipation or Parkinson result. Original paper tables remain open.
