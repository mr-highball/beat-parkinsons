Title: Selective molecular and network architecture features underlie brain cortical atrophy in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13251274/
PMID: 42265698
PMCID: PMC13251274
DOI: 10.1186/s12929-026-01267-6
Publication date: 2026-06-10 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The journal results analyze 83 people with dementia with Lewy bodies and 86 controls after quality control, as in the May preprint. Cortical shape and thickness, regional transcriptomic maps and connectivity are measured or compared. The design contains no food exposure or within-person visual episode-rate outcome.

Version lineage: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.26.26354105. The Newcastle and COMPASS-ND data sources, 178 eligible participants and quality-controlled 83/86 group totals match the preprint. Treat as one study family.
