Title: S1203 Chia Seeds for the Treatment of Chronic Constipation in Adults: A Randomized Multi-Armed Prospective Trial
DOI: 10.14309/01.ajg.0001132272.68553.35
First published: 2025
Authors: Poltiyelova E, Morano G, Hanesworth I, Rahni S, Vernord K, Weinstein S, Wong J, Park H, Nolan S, Pais S
Journal: American Journal of Gastroenterology 120(10S2):S259
Source: https://doi.org/10.14309/01.ajg.0001132272.68553.35
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

ACG 2025 Annual Scientific Meeting abstract S1203 published in American Journal of Gastroenterology 120(10S2):S259 (October 2025; DOI 10.14309/01.ajg.0001132272.68553.35).
Design: Randomized multi-armed prospective 4-week trial in adults meeting Rome IV criteria for chronic constipation assigned to chia seeds (2 tablespoons/day), psyllium (12 g/day), or polyethylene glycol (PEG 17 g/day).
Sample size: 71 randomized; 29 completed the 4-week trial (chia n=13, psyllium n=10, PEG n=6; high attrition).
Results: Chia seeds significantly increased complete spontaneous bowel movements (CSBMs) per week (P = 0.001), comparable to PEG (P = 0.019), and was reported non-inferior to psyllium and PEG. PEG also improved secondary bloating (P = 0.011), whereas chia significantly improved CSBM frequency without changing specific secondary symptoms in this cohort.
Limitations for Round 3 PD review: Conference abstract only (no full peer-reviewed manuscript yet), high dropout (29/71 completers), non-PD general chronic constipation cohort, and hydration/pre-soaking protocol not standardized to PD slow-transit constipation.
