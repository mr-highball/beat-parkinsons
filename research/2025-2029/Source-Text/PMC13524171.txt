Title: The phenotype of "pure" autonomic failure
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13524171/
PMID: 40053259
PMCID: PMC13524171
DOI: 10.1007/s10286-025-01121-z
Publication date: 2025-03-07 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The extended follow-up of 202 people initially presenting with pure autonomic failure tracks sustained autonomic phenotype and conversion to other diagnoses. Timelines of autonomic and motor or cognitive features provide no culinary exposure comparison.
