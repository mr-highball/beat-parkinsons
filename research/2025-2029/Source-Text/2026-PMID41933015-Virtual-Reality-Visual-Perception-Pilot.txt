Title: A pilot study of assessing visual hallucinations using virtual reality in lewy body disorders.
PMID: 41933015
PMCID: PMC13194656
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-46644-1
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13194656/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2026-04-03
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch original article JATS, with complete body.

A pilot in Lewy body disorders uses immersive scenes, eye tracking and verbal descriptions to distinguish visual hallucinations from misperceptions and ordinary perceptions. Its laboratory event counts are compared with clinical history groups in eleven participants. Elicited events under ambiguous visual conditions do not measure daily episodes after eating dried figs.
