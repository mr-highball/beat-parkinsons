Title: Caraway essential oil used in a human smell test
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/18/5/862
Format: Factual extract from the cited original.

2026. Original Methods, Nutrients 18(5):862. Healthy adults took part in a cross-sectional odor and sensory evaluation. Caraway essential oil was one sniffed odor test stimulus. The study does not administer toasted caraway or compare baseline and post-intervention olfactory impairment in Parkinson disease.
