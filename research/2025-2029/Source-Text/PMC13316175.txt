Title: Longitudinal biomarkers in dementia with Lewy bodies: A systematic review and meta-analysis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13316175/
PMID: 42383163
PMCID: PMC13316175
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2026.100470
Publication date: 2026-06-17 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The 17-study systematic review covers longitudinal dementia with Lewy bodies biomarkers, including imaging markers of disease progression. Follow-up of biomarkers differs from testing a dietary exposure against visual episode frequency.
