Title: Pharmacological content in the television series "House MD": analysis from a German pharmacologist's perspective
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13152976/
PMID: 41652009
PMCID: PMC13152976
DOI: 10.1007/s00210-026-04995-6
Publication date: 2026-02-07 (first electronic publication in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The analysis codes how medicines are depicted in episodes of House MD and compares their portrayal with other fictional works. Its observations concern television content, without a clinical exposure or Parkinson symptom outcome.
