Mani and Gautam, Himalayan raspberry case series for essential hypertension, 2025. DOI 10.61081/htnj/25v11i305.
Five adults reportedly consumed 50–100 g/day of Himalayan raspberries for three to four years. The article discusses Rubus ellipticus and Rubus niveus without assigning individual fruit species or frozen preparation. The abstract summarizes systolic declines as 8–12 mm Hg, although the published case values include 150 to 132 and 146 to 132. Table 2 lists mild asymptomatic low readings among three participants and medication reductions in two. Position, orthostatic symptoms and Parkinson diagnosis were not reported. The uncontrolled time course includes drug adjustments and changes in metabolic measures. The series gives a blood pressure observation in hypertensive patients, with the frozen raspberry lightheadedness hypothesis still open.
