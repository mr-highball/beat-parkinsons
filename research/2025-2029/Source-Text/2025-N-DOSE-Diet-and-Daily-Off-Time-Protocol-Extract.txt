Broad diet answer and daily OFF time in the N-DOSE protocol
Source: https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/66/NCT05589766/Prot_000.pdf
Registration: NCT05589766
Coverage: Original protocol version dated June 5, 2025, uploaded July 2; study registry updated July 8, 2025

The N-DOSE protocol includes MDS-UPDRS IV item 4.3 at printed page 103, where typical daily OFF hours contribute to motor complication scoring. Its lifestyle questionnaire at printed page 129 offers a broad Western diet option with examples including meat and roasted potatoes or vegetables. The fields do not assign or quantify potato intake. The retrieved version gives no estimate relating the diet answer to OFF hours. The registered intervention concerns nicotinamide riboside dose.
