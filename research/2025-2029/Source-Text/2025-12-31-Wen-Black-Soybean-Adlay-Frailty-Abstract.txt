Fermented black soybean and adlay in an older-adult frailty abstract
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12762600/
PMCID: PMC12762600
DOI: 10.1093/geroni/igaf122.4106
Coverage: Original published GSA oral/poster abstract and official registration comparison

Wen and colleagues report 116 older adults randomized to a 12-week supplement containing fermented black soybean and adlay or a matching comparison. Both groups improved on functional tasks including a five-meter walk, grip and timed up and go. Only the active group is described as showing clear within-group frailty and nutrition score changes. The abstract provides no arm-level effect estimates for walking or direct between-group frailty contrast. Black soybean is a different species from common black bean. The result concerns a combination and an older-adult cohort, with no Parkinson postural outcome. The similar registry NCT06882941 reports a different enrollment count.
