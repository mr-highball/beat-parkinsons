Original source scope
This real-world advanced Parkinson cohort of 802 describes motor and nonmotor symptoms during good ON time. The analysis linked additional good ON time to fewer falls (IRR 0.91, 95% CI 0.87–0.95).

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The comparison centers on hours of symptom control. It gives no dietary estimates for sage, date fruit or fig across falls, pain, rigidity, rest tremor, apathy or fatigue.
