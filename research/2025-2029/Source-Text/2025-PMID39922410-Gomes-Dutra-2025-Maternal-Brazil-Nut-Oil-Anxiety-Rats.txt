Title: Maternal consumption of nut oil (Bertholletia excelsa): Evidence of anxiolytic-like behavior and reduction in brain lipid peroxidation on the progeny of rats.
PMID: 39922410
DOI: 10.1016/j.brainres.2025.149501
First published: 2025
Authors: Gomes Dutra LM, Ferreira Alves ME, Oliveira de Lima E Silva TD, Dantas de Araújo JM, Alves Silva MDC, Elias Pereira D, et al.
Journal: Brain research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39922410/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical rat study showing maternal supplementation with extracted Brazil nut oil produced anxiolytic behavioral effects in offspring. Limits: extracted oil and developmental maternal model rather than adult whole-nut PD exposure; contrasts with null whole-nut study Apolinario 2023.
