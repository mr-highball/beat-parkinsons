Title: Protein with mixed-spice capsules and sleep quality
Registration: NCT07806838
First posted: 2026-09-08
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07806838
Text type: official-registry evidence summary

This recruiting pilot plans three groups among adults using GLP-1 medications, including usual care, protein with placebo and protein with six mixed spices. The mix includes sage, with species and preparation unspecified. Muscle mass is primary and Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index is secondary. No results were posted at the November 17, 2026 check. The registered contrast cannot isolate sage.
