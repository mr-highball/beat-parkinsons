Randomized topical almond oil trial measured skin appearance
Primary source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jocd.71279
DOI: 10.1111/jocd.71279
Registry ID: NCT06571721
Coverage: factual extract from the primary publisher report and registry.

Khan and colleagues published the NCT06571721 comparison on November 9, 2026. The full Methods and Results report 90 randomized, 30 per arm, and 79 completers. The completer counts were 28 for topical almond oil, 26 for almond oil with 0.5% vitamin E and 25 for 0.025% tretinoin in castor oil. The abstract conflates completers and assignment. At week 16, wrinkle severity decreased within each arm, with no reported between-arm difference. The almond-only arm had a 4% melanin index increase reported at week 16. Imaging measures facial surface appearance. There was no oral oil intervention, movement endpoint or Parkinson assessment.
