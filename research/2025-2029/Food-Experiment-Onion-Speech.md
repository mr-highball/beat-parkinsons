# Onion exposure and speech intelligibility

## Onion assigned to a modeled Parkinson menu

Ede Çintesun and colleagues published a nutrient model of published therapeutic menus on 11 April 2025. The team adapted five disease-specific menus to 2,000 kcal and calculated their dietary inflammatory index. Table 4 lists 59.0 g of onion for the Parkinson menu. The paper does not explain which portion of that onion belongs to which meal or the culinary preparation. It reports nutrient modeling without patient enrollment, observed consumption or neurological outcomes. ## Boiled onion with honey recalled in community interviews

Motsumi and Nemakonde reported focus groups with 100 participants in five South African villages on 23 January 2025. A small number of participants described drinking an onion-and-honey preparation for cough or influenza, and a different account described room exposure to vapors from chopped onion. The subsection covers common onion and spring onion, and the cited recipe does not identify which species each respondent used. These records document recalled use. They do not measure treatment response, speech intelligibility or Parkinson disease.
