# Dutch dietary trials and Parkinson motor outcomes

## No Guts No Glory BrAIN diet

The two Dutch records date to August 2021. The 2026 investigator protocol describes an open-label crossover study with 107 participants across four diagnoses, including 49 with Parkinson disease. The archived registration gives 100 and the updated record retains 100 as its target size. The preprint reports final follow-up on September 5, 2025. Its food-choice table lists apples with peel and olive oil, while the example menu includes apple syrup. Source materials do not identify olive-oil grade or show what any Parkinson participant consumed. [Archived registration](../2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/NL-OMON20827.txt) · [Preprint extract](Source-Text/2026-Scheurink-No-Guts-No-Glory-Protocol.txt) · [Original protocol and supplement](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.02.26354674v1.full.pdf)

The newer registry entry posted results on August 26, 2026. It records 47 of 49 Parkinson participants completing the trial and reports no significant effect on the Part III motor total, the primary global functioning score or quality of life. The narrative reports reduced fatigue and nonmotor burden without numerical estimates. A separate rest-tremor estimate and ingredient-specific effects remain open. [Official results, pp. 9–12](https://onderzoekmetmensen.nl/en/node/52339/pdf) · [Results extract](Source-Text/Trials/2026-NL-OMON52339-Results.txt)

The protocol's food list places apples with peel among unprocessed or frozen fruit choices. Its example menu calls for olive oil in food preparation. The 42 Dutch dinner recipes at the cited repository revision include 40 whose extracted text names olive oil. None specifies extra-virgin grade. These recipes show possible preparations. Intake and dietary substitutions by individual participants remain unreported. [Protocol supplement, pp. 52–58](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.02.26354674v1.full.pdf) · [Investigator recipe repository](https://github.com/ToonScheurink/No_Guts_No_Glory)

The similarly titled NCT07606014 study tests a probiotic for metabolic effects in people taking antipsychotic medication. Its identifiers and treatment differ from the dietary crossover study. [Probiotic registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07606014)

## Plants for Parkinson

The 2026 Movement Disorder Society abstract describes 118 planned participants per group with early Parkinson disease. The design compares six months of supported whole-food plant-based eating with usual diet, with vitamins in both groups and six months of further follow-up. Primary outcomes are MDS-UPDRS Part III and Wexner constipation score. The conference abstract supplies no outcomes. An earlier presentation slide gives a planned size of 260. Published registration or amendment history would be needed to reconcile the figures. The Radboud recruitment page describes one year of study participation and visits at baseline, six months and one year. It does not specify apple or olive oil assignment or a separate rest-tremor analysis. [Conference abstract](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/the-clinical-and-biological-effects-of-a-whole-foods-and-plant-based-dietary-intervention-in-parkinsons-disease/) · [Presentation, slide 30](https://www.meandermc.nl/wcm/connect/www/908091f6-bdba-448f-8837-c7f4fcddee47/Lezing%2B1%3B%2Bprof%2Bv.%2BLaar%3B%2Bmedicatie%2Bontwikkelingen%2Bin%2BParkinson.pdf?CVID=pDucba0&MOD=AJPERES) · [Radboud study page](https://www.radboudumc.nl/expertisecentra/parkinson-en-bewegingsstoornissen/onderzoek-en-innovatie/research-support/onderzoeken/lopend/plants-for-parkinson) · [Groningen study overview](https://www.rug.nl/aletta/research/aletta-health-grant-2026/winners/ahg5?lang=en)
