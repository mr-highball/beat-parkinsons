# Raw fig and Parkinson rest tremor evidence gaps

November 22, 2026. Round 1 claim 0559 asks whether raw common fig reduces resting tremor severity in people with Parkinson disease. The review screened 202 exact inherited sources at their available depth. Eighty-two checks were resolved using original articles or archived original methods. The other 120 are source-access holds, with 99 authenticated abstracts and 21 authenticated bibliographic records. Each hold has a dated local file that records the verified identity and route for the complete original. ## Evidence that is still missing

- **A human food comparison.** The open question requires raw common fig (Ficus carica), a diagnosed Parkinson population and a separately measured rest-tremor severity comparison. Look for future dietary protocols and supplements that report individual fig intake, medication state, the tremor rating or measured amplitude, and results by arm. The Hegelmaier diet includes fig in both interventions, with no attributable fig contrast. [Parkinson dietary study](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Fig-Clinical-and-Model-Traces.md#figs-among-foods-in-the-hegelmaier-parkinson-intervention)
- **Genuine fig in a motor model.** Fig is a confirmed ingredient in the baked walnut-bar study. The intervention is a mixed food in rats, with motor coordination and catalepsy outcomes. The fig-specific arm, the source fruit preparation and a resting tremor endpoint remain open. The fruit-extract article also needs its numeric tables, and its available methods use different outcomes. [Baked fig-containing bar](Source-Text/2026-Raees-Fig-In-Walnut-Bar-Parkinsonism-Model.txt) · [Concentrated fig extract](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-Gafoor-Fig-Fruit-Extract.txt)
- **The adverse signal in children.** The fig-and-almond preparation produced three reported tremor events versus zero in each comparator. The clinical records leave the tremor phenotype, the influence of the other formulation components and the transfer to raw fig and diagnosed Parkinson disease unresolved. [Fig-and-almond formulation](Source-Text/2025-Mohammadi-Kenari-Fig-Almond-Drooling.txt)
- **Tremor outcome boundaries.** Separate rest, postural, kinetic and load-induced phenomena. Distinguish oscillation frequency from amplitude and patient-rated severity. One deep brain stimulation comparison reports a separate rest-tremor subscore, and a de novo Parkinson case demonstrates re-emergent postural tremor without clinically detected rest tremor. The older pramipexole study has a pooled tremor result at abstract depth. The separate tremor items still need the original. [Conventional and adaptive stimulation](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID34584095-Raw-Fig-Rest-Tremor-Scope.txt) · [Re-emergent postural tremor case](Source-Text/2026-PMID42178480-Raw-Fig-Rest-Tremor-Scope.txt) · [Pramipexole original-access note](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2002-PMID12023411-Raw-Fig-Rest-Tremor-Original-Access.txt)
- **Traditional terminology and species.** The Unani review and the 2010 primary report identify peepal (Ficus religiosa) in the Majoon mixture. The other source, a distinct 2011 series, measured rest and postural frequencies after a mixture that has no fig species. These original tables document two traditions-related clinical observations and resolve a search-result species conflation. The Gokand ethnobotanical survey verifies other customary uses of common fig fruit or latex. Fig-specific clinical outcomes and an attributable traditional common-fig rest-tremor account remain open.
