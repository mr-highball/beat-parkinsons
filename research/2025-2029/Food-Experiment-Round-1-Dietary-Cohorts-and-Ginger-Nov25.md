# Round 1 dietary cohorts and ginger model outcomes

- The Singapore Chinese Health Study reports on dietary fats and dietary antioxidants reuse the same cohort. Both place black tea in their adjustment models. Their endpoints are new Parkinson diagnoses, with no estimate of brewed black tea and bradykinesia after diagnosis. The prospective dietary-pattern study records olive oil as a contribution to a Mediterranean diet score. The flavonoid cohorts examine incidence or mortality, with onion as a questionnaire input and grape-related compounds in their rationale. - Corn oil is a fixed component of the shared diet in the fat-1 mouse study. In another experiment, it is a vehicle for DHA, while the separate dietary contrast changes alpha-ketoglutarate. The dietary-fat sources supply model outcomes without isolating the direction of refined corn oil for human rigidity, depression, bradykinesia or falls. [Fixed-diet study](../2010-2014/Source-Text/PMC3023546-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [DHA vehicle study](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC11073026-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)
- The nutrition review cites paired reports on flax-derived omega-3 with vitamin E and describes their small sample as shared. A drug–food review identifies orange juice as one part of a mixed meal in a bromocriptine pharmacokinetic experiment. These records preserve potentially relevant preparation and interaction leads while leaving the individual constipation, anxiety and fatigue comparisons open. [Nutrition review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7280935-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Drug–food review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC9881082-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)
- The 2013 paper measures turning and descent in a pole test after isolated 6-shogaol in a Parkinson mouse model. The 2023 paper compares a heated ginger extract and isolated 6-shogaol in separate mouse arms. Both studies report faster pole movement in treated animals. Only the heated-extract arm in the 2023 report has the separately described improvement in both open-field distance and rotarod retention. A 2026 abstract reports a separate STAT3 and motor experiment with isolated 6-shogaol. The food preparation and passive muscle rigidity needed for claim 0626 are outside these comparisons. - The human Ginger-PD extract registry still lists recruitment, while the Isfahan powder-tablet study lists recruitment complete. The inspected registry records give no public outcomes. The earlier Bougainville ethnobotanical record describes ginger among treatments for abdominal pain and constipation in another setting. Its route and context remain distinct from the registered trial designs. A book chapter's dated sections cover food processing and separately discuss reserpine for dyskinesia in Huntington disease. Original access resolved 12 checks on these two sources.
