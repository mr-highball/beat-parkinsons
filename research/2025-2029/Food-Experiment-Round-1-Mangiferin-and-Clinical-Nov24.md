# Round 1 mangiferin and clinical original source checks

This audit checked 91 exact scopes across 60 original publications and 31 frozen claims. Seventy-two scopes were excluded and 19 retained as contextual. Two previously known records were used to verify the clinical curcumin and fresh-mango leads. Three additional mangiferin originals are archived by first confirmed publication year.

The [2025 review](Source-Text/2025-PMID40696232-Mango-Constituent-and-Mouse-Behavior-Citation-Trail.txt) describes lower sucrose intake for the Cao mouse experiment. The [Cao author abstract](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28410265-Mangiferin-and-Mouse-Sucrose-Consumption.txt) reports higher sucrose consumption. The [2014 inflammatory model](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID25064341-Mangiferin-and-Mouse-Affect-Tests.txt) and the [2022 postpartum model](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-PMID35721155-Mangiferin-and-Postpartum-Model-Behavior.txt) describe other favorable mouse behavior comparisons. The three studies test isolated mangiferin. Their formulation, population and behavioral endpoints do not estimate dried mango flesh effects on Parkinson depressive symptoms. The [fresh-mango registry](Source-Text/NCT06800833-Fresh-Mango-Depression.txt) currently lists recruitment, an intended depression questionnaire and no posted results.

The eight raw-fig checks include wearable tremor detection, repeated rest-tremor ratings, motor phenotype grouping and tremor in another clinical population. They supply measurement context. The research on 38 dried-date pain scopes and five dried-date apathy scopes gives no assigned dried date fruit outcome. The research packet keeps the underlying scope judgments by publication.
