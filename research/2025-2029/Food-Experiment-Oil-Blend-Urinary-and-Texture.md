# Oil formulations, urinary frequency and texture

## Mixed essential oil capsules and urinary frequency

Ciprandi, Paganotti and colleagues. Minerva Medica, first public November 4, 2026. DOI 10.23736/S0026-4806.26.09937-4. This observational acute cystitis report compares 24 fosfomycin recipients and two groups of 21 and 23 receiving different amounts of an eight-essential-oil blend containing Cymbopogon citratus. Reported percentages at up to four voids per day changed from 25.0 to 70.8, 19.0 to 85.7 and 21.7 to 69.6, respectively. The matched study outcome is void frequency. The available original abstract gives no separately tabulated urgency score, comparison of brewed tea, single ingredient or Parkinson cohort. Original full methods and tables remain to retrieve. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42980616/)

## Soybean oil gradient and fish gel texture

Oyinloye and Yoon. Gels, April 16, 2025. DOI 10.3390/gels11040295. The study combined fish myofibrillar protein with soybean or coconut oil at several concentrations and used rheology, mechanical texture analysis, microscopy and printing as outcomes. One no-oil control had 1396.4 g hardness and the 5% soybean-oil condition had 886.8 g. Softening also compromised printed layer stability. These are matrix-specific food texture measurements. The study did not give a clinical swallowing outcome or a Parkinson study. [Original source](https://www.mdpi.com/2310-2861/11/4/295)
