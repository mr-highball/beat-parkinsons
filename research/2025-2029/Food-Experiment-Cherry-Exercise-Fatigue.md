# Tart cherry preparations and exercise fatigue

## Vistula tart cherry product and marathon fatigue ratings

Thirty-five marathon runners received a Vistula tart cherry product or calorie-matched placebo. A 200 mm fatigue scale was measured before and after the race and over 48 hours of recovery. The report gives no clear fatigue treatment or interaction effect. The abstract describes freeze-dried powder, while the Methods and conclusion describe spray-dried extract. The product and exertional fatigue context differ from eating dried cherries in Parkinson disease. [Primary source](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00394-025-03847-y) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2026-Squires-Vistula-Marathon-Fatigue-Extract.txt)

Table 2 gives fatigue means of 48, 147, 68 and 46 mm for cherry, and 41, 144, 74 and 44 mm for placebo at baseline, immediately after, 24 hours and 48 hours, respectively. The calculation of sample size used a strength measure. The paper describes no intervention side effects. The current registry explicitly names fatigue and was posted after the listed actual study start. ## Powdered tart cherry and sprint fatigue

Hagele and colleagues first published January 29, 2026. Forty of 50 randomized participants completed the powdered tart cherry capsule or rice-flour placebo trial. The article describes a capsule coding problem and final groups of 17 and 23. Calculated sprint fatigue (p=.814) and the group-by-time change on a subjective energy scale (p=.398) were inconclusive. Calculated sprint fatigue and energy are distinct from the frozen clinical fatigue measure. The current registry was posted after the registered study start.
