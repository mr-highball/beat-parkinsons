# Follow-up on enteral nutrition and levodopa case reports

## Parkinsonism-hyperpyrexia with nutrition support

Burslem and colleagues published a case on April 13, 2025 involving a critically ill patient with Parkinson’s who developed parkinsonism-hyperpyrexia syndrome during enteral and parenteral nutrition support. The public abstract gives no formula or isolated exposure and does not distinguish the timing and contribution of the two routes. Qiu and colleagues’ June 15, 2026 case report and review is indexed separately. Its PubMed record provides no abstract. The food or formula, chronology, patient-level outcomes and co-treatments remain unresolved. Both articles cite the older acute-care literature.
