# Turkey, shiitake, and arugula evidence

## 2025 Rocket leaf sensory perception and participant genotype

Bell and colleagues compared sensory ratings for Eruca vesicaria subsp. sativa leaves, plant chemistry and participant genotype. They report that certain bitter-sensitive groups rated several aroma and flavor attributes less strongly. The endpoint was product-specific perception during tasting, with no intervention on general smell impairment or Parkinson cohort. [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.1093/ijfood/vvaf055)

## 2025 Textural softening of shiitake for older adults

Li and colleagues tested commercial enzymes, vacuum impregnation and sterilization to soften whole and sliced shiitake. The treatment reached IDDSI Level 6 criteria in food tests. There was no swallowing assessment in a Parkinson cohort. This appeared online November 10, 2025, before its 2026 issue. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42603941/)

## 2026 Lentinan-containing nanoparticles in a Parkinson model

Wei and colleagues evaluated a lentinan and manganese oxide nanosystem. The accessible abstract reports improvements in model behavior and pathological measures alongside anti-inflammatory effects. It does not give a clinical endpoint for roasted or cooked shiitake, and the reported intervention combines lentinan with an engineered carrier. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42015685/)

## 2026 Shiitake variety and printable dysphagia texture

Li and colleagues compared five shiitake varieties and several solid-to-liquid ratios in printable material. The Qingke212 preparation at a selected ratio met IDDSI Level 5 food texture criteria. The study does not report clinical swallowing changes and did not use roasted shiitake. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42409513/)

## 2026 Arugula extract and vascular measures in healthy men

Bolden and colleagues reported an acute randomized crossover in eight healthy men. Both arugula extract doses reduced systolic pressure versus placebo and increased nitrate-related measures. The higher dose did not show a greater effect. It is a study of an extract and vascular outcomes without a Parkinson's fatigue or fall endpoint. [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsnutrsci.6c00024)
