# Coffee preparations and fatigue follow-up

## Unfiltered Turkish coffee and acute fatigue in healthy adults

Elsahoryi et al.’s February 27, 2025 crossover report describes 52 healthy female university students. The intervention was unfiltered Turkish coffee made with finely ground Arabica beans, compared with water. In Table 4, mean 0–10 fatigue ratings were 3.54 at baseline and 3.92 at 120 minutes with coffee, versus 3.27 and 2.62 with water. The authors report no significant fatigue interaction. The paper describes a trend toward reduced fatigue, while the coffee group’s reported mean rose above baseline at 90 and 120 minutes. The separate sleepiness item declined after coffee. The fatigue comparison was unblinded and supplies no estimate for people with Parkinson’s. [Primary report, Methods, Table 4 and Results](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/17/5/823) · [Local CC BY text](Source-Text/PMC11901579.txt)

## Coffee reported in the expanded Australian Parkinson cohort

Cao et al.’s February 17, 2026 Australian Parkinson’s Genetics Study report includes 10,929 people. Table 4 records 8,235 caffeinated coffee drinkers among 10,886 respondents (75.65%). Among 7,682 reporting quantity, mean intake was 2.02 cups/day (SD 1.29). The report describes coffee use and clinical features but supplies no fatigue score by coffee exposure or brewing information. The prior pilot is part of the same cohort program. The report gives a qualified-researcher data request route with ethics and data-transfer requirements. Its article license is CC BY-NC-ND. [Primary report, Table 4 and data sharing statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13069442/) · [2022 pilot](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Beverage-Fatigue-Followup.md#coffee-and-first-symptom-in-the-australian-parkinson-cohort-pilot)

## Instant coffee after withdrawal in healthy habitual drinkers

Boscaini et al.’s April 21, 2026 report includes 31 healthy habitual coffee drinkers who abstained for two weeks, then received caffeinated (n = 16) or decaffeinated (n = 15) Nescafé Classic instant coffee for three weeks. Each daily allocation was four 1.8 g sachets, with water, milk and sugar amounts chosen by participants. The baseline diary listed preparations such as filtered coffee, cappuccino and instant coffee, while the assigned products were soluble. Supplementary Figure S10a marks higher combined withdrawal drowsiness and fatigue for decaf at intervention days 2 and 14 (p < .05). Figure S10h’s separate fatigue visual analogue scale has no marked between-group or within-group differences. The study uses healthy participants and provides indirect evidence about withdrawal as an alternative explanation for apparent effects in a regular-versus-decaf comparison. The publisher’s license is CC BY-NC-ND. [Primary report, Methods and Results](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71264-8) · [Original supplementary Figure S10](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13100100.1/41467_2026_71264_MOESM1_ESM.pdf#page=11) · [2023 registrations](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Beverage-Fatigue-Followup.md#2023-registrations-for-instant-coffee-reintroduction-and-withdrawal)

## ParkWest fatigue fields in the fall-prediction thesis

Lici and Lyngstad’s University of Stavanger thesis is dated June 1, 2026. The Norwegian National Research Archive records its public deposit on August 7. The two records name the same authors and describe an analysis of falls in 207 incident ParkWest patients, followed for a median of 8.9 years. The authors’ public code defines FSS mean and sum fields in its fatigue group and identifies the input as `ParkVest_ClinicalData_with_Metadata.xlsx`. The repository directs researchers to the owners for the underlying confidential data. Its public materials give no coffee variable or coffee-to-fatigue analysis. [University thesis record](https://nva.sikt.no/registration/019fdbfd2137-c782924b-95fc-4f38-a8da-284119884e17) · [Coauthor thesis record](https://nva.sikt.no/registration/019fdbfdba22-a849a424-3e56-4fd8-bcdf-0b0379456f0e) · [Authors’ field definitions](https://github.com/AlanLici/PARKAI_MASTER_THESIS/blob/main/Programs/Cox/Common/domain_feature_lists.py) · [Data access statement](https://github.com/AlanLici/PARKAI_MASTER_THESIS#data-access)

Macleod et al.’s April 11, 2026 individual-data meta-analysis includes ParkWest and five other European incidence cohorts. It models postural instability, dependency, dementia and death. It provides no coffee or fatigue model. The authors describe the contributing participant data as available on request subject to privacy and ethics restrictions. ## Arrival fatigue ratings during caffeine withdrawal

Davis and colleagues’ August 6, 2026 crossover studied 25 habitual caffeine consumers. The assigned drinks used a caffeine supplement or placebo in flavored water. Arrival VAS fatigue was measured before administration on each visit and gave an inconclusive condition comparison (p=.301). The design offers evidence about fatigue ratings under the withdrawal schedule. It supplies no post-dose fatigue trajectory, brewed-coffee comparison or Parkinson population.
