# Dietary and botanical registry leads, 2025–2029

## Habb-e-Ishrat combination and motor outcomes

CTRI/2026/02/102865 was registered February 3, 2026. The WHO ICTRP snapshot identifies an open-label, randomized, active-controlled study at Government Nizamia Tibbi College and Hospital with a target of 30. It compares tablets containing the multi-ingredient Unani preparation Habb-e-Ishrat with levodopa/carbidopa. The formula includes black pepper, ginger, Ceylon cinnamon, cassia cinnamon and an *Apium graveolens* ingredient whose plant part is unspecified in the export. Registered outcomes are PDQ-39 and total UPDRS Part III at 12 weeks. The record gives no separate symptom estimates or results. The underlying CTRI page timed out, and exact-identifier, preparation and institution searches located no public result report. The combined preparation and outcomes provide an indirect lead for [0146, ground cinnamon and rigidity](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0146.txt) and [0626, grated ginger and rigidity](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0626.txt). [Primary CTRI link](https://ctri.nic.in/Clinicaltrials/pmaindet2.php?trialid=150505) · [WHO ICTRP search portal](https://trialsearch.who.int/)

## Plants for Parkinson

NL-OMON61547 first published June 4, 2025. The Dutch registry describes a randomized study targeting 236 people with early Parkinson’s, with study visits at baseline, six months and twelve months. The six-month intervention adds a dietitian-supported whole-food plant-based diet, with supplements provided to both groups. The co-primary clinical measures are motor MDS-UPDRS Part III and the Wexner Constipation Scoring System. The available primary record lists recruiting. A 2026 conference design report specifies 118 per arm and a six-month follow-up after active dietary guidance ends. The intervention evaluates a dietary pattern. [Primary Dutch registry](https://onderzoekmetmensen.nl/en/trial/61547) · [Participating center](https://www.radboudumc.nl/expertisecentra/parkinson-en-bewegingsstoornissen/onderzoek-en-innovatie/research-support/onderzoeken/lopend/plants-for-parkinson) · [Conference abstracts](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.70479)

Postma and colleagues' World Parkinson Congress abstract P26.02, published May 22, 2026, describes 260 participants across disease stages, a 12-month dietary intervention and a 12-month MDS-UPDRS Part II primary outcome. The October 24, 2026 Dutch registry revision still gives 236 participants, a six-month intervention and motor examination and constipation endpoints at six months. The reports share a title and investigator team but give different protocol details. Both are design reports. Protocol revision history and outcome publication remain open. [World Parkinson Congress abstract P26.02](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13360760/) · [Current Dutch registry](https://onderzoekmetmensen.nl/en/trial/61547)

Boon and colleagues’ October 2026 MDS abstract describes 118 early-stage participants per arm, six months of dietary guidance followed by six months of follow-up, and MDS-UPDRS Part III and Wexner constipation scores as the primary measures. Its results and conclusions fields are blank. The current Dutch XML lists October 19, 2026 as the actual enrollment date, with recruitment ongoing and 236 as the target. It identifies protocol NL-010182 and September 3, 2026 as the ethics approval date. It specifies a six-month between-group comparison of the ON-medication motor examination and constipation scores. The control receives vitamin B12, vitamin D3 and omega-3 supplements. Both groups provide diet records. These are design fields without enrollment totals or results. [October MDS abstract, Boon et al.](https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mds.70479) · [Dutch registry](https://onderzoekmetmensen.nl/en/trial/61547) · [Official XML](https://onderzoekmetmensen.nl/api/v1/trial/61547)

The university’s June 2, 2026 update confirms that the team and Hanze dietetics program developed recipes and a cooking workshop. Hanze’s June 2026 account of the April preparatory workshop identifies bean burgers and seasonal vegetable stew among six plant-based dishes prepared with people with Parkinson’s and their partners. The account gives no ingredient quantities, treatment assignment, symptom measurements or confirmation that participants ate these dishes during the randomized trial. Radboudumc’s participant page requires diagnosis less than three years before enrollment, as does the registry’s design narrative. The English and Dutch structured eligibility lists give no three-year cutoff. The center describes a year on the assigned diet, consistent with a year of follow-up and six months of active coaching in the MDS protocol. No earlier full registry version or public trial recipe booklet was recovered. [Groningen study team](https://www.rug.nl/aletta/research/aletta-health-grant-2026/winners/ahg5?lang=en) · [Hanze workshop report](https://nl.linkedin.com/posts/academie-voor-gezondheidsstudies-hanzehogeschool-groningen_plantsforparkinson-voedingendietetiek-activity-7473356975066738688-abMJ) · [Radboudumc screening and study visits](https://www.radboudumc.nl/expertisecentra/parkinson-en-bewegingsstoornissen/onderzoek-en-innovatie/research-support/onderzoeken/lopend/plants-for-parkinson) · [October protocol abstract](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/the-clinical-and-biological-effects-of-a-whole-foods-and-plant-based-dietary-intervention-in-parkinsons-disease/)

Van Laar’s October 13, 2025 briefing, slide 30, already described the 260-person, one-year plan later reported at the World Parkinson Congress. The Groningen team’s June 2, 2026 grant description gives six months of diet and explains the dietitian and cooking workshop roles. The Radboud participant page describes one year of assigned diet and supplements. Together the dates establish that the longer plan was circulating before the May abstract and the six-month guidance plan was public by June. The 2025 Dutch Parkinson Scientists booklet is password protected, and the current public register supplies no protocol version history. ## Thai-style Mediterranean diet workshops

TCTR20251224006 registered December 24, 2025. It describes an intervention centered on a Thai-style Mediterranean diet cooking workshop and associated guidance in people with Parkinson’s, with planned motor, nonmotor, cognitive, quality-of-life and diary measures. The trial XML supplied no outcome summary at review. The registry labels recruitment ongoing and lists a June 2026 study end, while several endpoints have 12-month time frames. The duration and completion therefore require clarification from a later public update. [Primary Thai registry XML](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/export/xml/TCTR20251224006)

## Effervescent Mucuna extract and off time

TCTR20260811005 registered August 11, 2026 and lists a July 2024 start and May 2025 completion. This placebo-controlled pilot targeted 30 people with wearing-off symptoms, enrolled 12 according to the primary registry XML, and tested a standardized oral *Mucuna pruriens* seed extract added to usual levodopa. Its primary outcome is diary-recorded daily off time. The WHO and Thai records provide no results summary, and exact-ID, formulation and institutional searches located no public outcome report. This is a separate study from the Thai acute crossover and uses a different species from broad bean (*Vicia faba*). [Primary Thai registry XML](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/export/xml/TCTR20260811005) · [Previously published Thai crossover](Food-Experiment-TCTR-Followup.md#thai-mucuna-crossover-trial)

## Vitamin D supplementation and clinical outcomes

TCTR20240708007 was registered in July 2024 and linked by the authors to the July 2026 report. Sixty people with Parkinson’s were randomized equally to vitamin D3 or no added treatment for 12 weeks. Table 4 reports mean between-arm change differences of −1.03 on motor MDS-UPDRS Part III (95% CI −5.39 to 3.33) and +0.43 on Thai MMSE (95% CI −0.54 to 1.41). Serum vitamin D rose with supplementation. The clinical comparisons are inconclusive, and the trial tests a supplement outside the frozen ingredients. [Primary registration](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/show/TCTR20240708007) · [Primary report, Table 4, PMID 42499083](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13406316/)
