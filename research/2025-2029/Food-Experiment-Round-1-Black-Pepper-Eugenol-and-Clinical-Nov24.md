# Black pepper, eugenol and clinical source checks

The [Round 1 source inventory](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/priority-five-pepper-and-clinical-nov24/verified-originals.tsv) records the inspected originals and the [exact dispositions](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/priority-five-pepper-and-clinical-nov24/dispositions.tsv) distinguish 15 related comparisons from 62 exposure or endpoint exclusions.

## Black pepper and the swallowing reflex

The [2021 review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID34337829-Neurogenic-Dysphagia-Review-Pepper-Oil-and-Menthol.txt) pools eight heterogeneous TRP-agent trials. It includes a black pepper oil comparison and a separate menthol crossover. The [full pepper trial](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-Ebihara-Baseline-Olfaction-Extract.txt) allocated 35 older post-stroke residents to each of three parallel arms. After the first exposure, reflex latency averaged 6.4 seconds with inhaled black pepper oil, 13.2 with lavender and 15.2 with odorless water (overall p=.03). Day-30 means were 4.4, 13.6 and 14.4 seconds (p<.001). No cough-reflex difference emerged. The later review reports 34 oil and 33 odorless participants, matching the trial's follow-up counts and excluding the separate lavender arm from the tabulated contrast.

The [menthol trial](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-PMC1885135-Ebihara-Menthol-Swallow-Reflex.txt) delivered test solutions directly to the pharynx. It is the crossover cited in the review's sensitivity analysis, not another pepper trial. The [food chemistry study](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID33732511-Lee-Piperine-in-Pepper-and-Food-Matrices.txt) measured bulk piperine in pepper, curry and noodles. It leaves release during a meal, timing at swallowing-relevant receptors and comparable clinical outcomes unresolved. The [oral piperine comparison](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID24326980-Oral-Piperine-and-Swallowing-Physiology.txt) provides a separate instrumental contrast in standardized nectar. Neither route nor test population establishes the effect of cracked pepper in a meal in Parkinson disease.

## Eugenol, nociception and mood

The [review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID34394824-Eugenol-Food-Source-List-and-Pain-Mood-Citations.txt) lists multiple spice sources for a constituent. The [oral animal comparison](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-DOIS0102695X2009000200006-Daniel-Oral-Eugenol-Pain.txt) reported lower chemical writhing with no clear thermal result. The [injected comparison](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-Kurian-Eugenol-Route-Pain-and-Motor-Confound.txt) reported chemical and formalin findings and motor suppression at the highest condition, a potential confound for behavior. Keep those routes and endpoints distinct from the clinical clove results. Separate [rodent mood](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15157811-Irie-Eugenol-Immobilization-and-Mood.txt) and [compound or analog](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID15936201-Tao-Eugenol-Analogs-and-Forced-Swim.txt) evidence supplies no grated nutmeg or Parkinson depression contrast.

The [autism bioactives review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID34471461-Food-Bioactives-in-Autism-and-Endpoint-Attribution.txt) cites separate [green tea extract](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID21820650-Banji-Green-Tea-Extract-and-Behavioral-Endpoints.txt) and [purified piperine](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID24803211-Pragnya-Piperine-Nociception-Scope.txt) mouse experiments. Its reduced-pain-threshold sentence concerns Bacopa monnieri. Those model outputs supply no separate effects on the matched Parkinson fatigue, constipation or pain claims.
