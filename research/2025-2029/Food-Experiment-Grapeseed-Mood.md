# Grape seed extract and depressive symptom scores

## Grape seed extract depression result in a migraine trial

Eshaghian and colleagues randomized 50 adults with migraine to black grape seed extract or placebo for eight weeks. Table 4 reports adjusted DASS depression changes of −5.64 ± 0.89 versus −5.56 ± 0.89, as means and standard errors (p = .95). The comparison is inconclusive and the preparation is seed extract. The older topical grapeseed studies uses this fixed oil as a massage carrier or part of a mixture. Neither exposure resolves depression after ingesting refined grapeseed oil in Parkinson disease.
