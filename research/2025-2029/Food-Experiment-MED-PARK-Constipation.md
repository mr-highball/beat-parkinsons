# MED PARK Constipation

## Olive oil advice in the modified Mediterranean diet protocol

The first-public article date is October 5, 2025. The protocol assigns a six-month modified Mediterranean diet or usual diet. Its cooking advice specifies using olive oil in place of other cooking fats and including raw olive oil in dishes. The intervention text gives no grade, separate assigned oil dose or an oil-only arm. The proposed outcomes include a bowel symptom score within the broader dietary comparison. Trial registration identifies the same MED-PARK study. [Original source](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/bmjopen/15/10/e101946.full.pdf) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2025-MED-PARK-Olive-Oil-Protocol-Extract.txt)

## Motor examination and rest-tremor coverage

The modified Mediterranean intervention includes olive oil among several assigned dietary changes. The 2024 registration and 2025 protocol include the MDS-UPDRS Part III motor examination. The accessible protocol leaves the medication state at examination unspecified, and the stated outcomes do not include a separate rest-tremor comparison. On November 17, 2026 the public registration still showed recruiting, last updated July 11, 2025, with no posted results. This status predates its estimated completion and leaves actual enrollment progress and results unverified.
