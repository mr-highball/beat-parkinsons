# Bell pepper and symptom outcomes

## Optional bell pepper in the Mexican sleep apnea protocol

2025-08-21. Torres San Miguel and colleagues planned a whole-diet comparison for adults with obstructive sleep apnea. The Mediterranean adaptation lists bell pepper as an optional vegetable and includes an Epworth Sleepiness Scale. The body identifies NCT06782737 and matches its official ethics code and study team. The abstract prints NCT06278571, a different mobile-app study in type 2 diabetes. The official apnea record lists no posted results. It gives no confirmed bell-pepper preparation, trial intake or isolated pepper outcome. ## Public registration for the Mexican sleep apnea diet trial

2025-01-20. ClinicalTrials.gov lists R-2024-3609-055 for the apnea diet study led by Lubia Velázquez López. It showed enrolling by invitation and no results in the recheck. Its timing fields differ from the article’s planned timetable. This is a registration and supplies no observed food intake or sleepiness contrast.
