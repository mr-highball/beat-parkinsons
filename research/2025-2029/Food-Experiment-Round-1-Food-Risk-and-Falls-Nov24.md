## Tomato consumption and rigidity

In the Solanaceae case-control study, the main analysis included 486 cases and 636 controls. For each additional daily intake frequency, the adjusted odds ratios were 0.83 (95% CI 0.56–1.24) for tomato and 0.77 (0.41–1.44) for tomato juice. The outcome was case status. Cooked tomato sauce and salsa data were unavailable, and the study gives no rigidity result. The prior cucurbit reply used the same cohort. The separate MVP analysis included tomato sauce in a composite sofrito item and its uncertainty spans zero on total self-reported symptoms. The 2026 isolated-lycopene report used five animals per group for behavior and found favorable pole timing and open-field movement, with longer rotarod latency reported. The earlier isolated-lycopene abstracts give various water-maze, locomotion, coordination, grip and orofacial dyskinesia outcomes. Their full behavioral methods, arm-level estimates and rigidity specificity remain open. No retrieved comparison isolates clinical rigidity after cooked tomato. The scorecard remains Unknown and retains each animal comparison in its own family. ## Public accounts and outcome boundaries

A gardening forum includes a caregiver report of more joint stiffness during raw tomato season and improvement after stopping, alongside accounts of no comparable arthritis flare. A participant in a Parkinson support thread describes leg stiffness after cheese and better walking after cutting back multiple foods. Tomato and sauce are on that list. Their Parkinson diagnosis was unconfirmed, and no tomato-specific response was reported. The threads preserve differences in exposure, diagnosis and outcome. ## Contaminants and molecular evidence

The Vogt report models contaminant intake for food categories including a crucifer group, a dairy-and-egg group, poultry and cantaloupe. Coffee and oats were not among the modeled exposures. Modeled chemical exposure has no reported fall or fatigue outcome. The Singh study compares isolated curcumin in protein and cell systems, with no measured fall frequency. Both materials remain available as related source context.
