# Strawberry leaf extract in a Parkinson-related cell model

## Concurrent leaf extract and rotenone in cultured cells

Ueda and colleagues added garden-strawberry leaf extract concurrently with rotenone to human SH-SY5Y neuroblastoma cultures. The presentation reports higher viability and lower reactive oxygen species than with rotenone exposure alone, with Nrf2 nuclear translocation and related expression changes. It appeared as EPO8_118 in the Paris 2025 meeting collection published July 23, 2026. The endpoints are cellular and there were no participants, falls measurements or strawberry fruit doses.
