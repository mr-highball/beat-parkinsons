# Ghana Mucuna trial: tremor and reporting audit

Cilia et al., PMID 41269916, [original article](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/1877718X251383721), first online November 21, 2025; February 2026 issue. Reuse the [existing full text](Source-Text/PMC13347555.txt).

The open-label comparison randomized 35 participants; 32 entered efficacy analyses. Roasted seed powder contained 6.3% levodopa; the comparator included benserazide. Global motor improvement does not supply a controlled rest-tremor estimate. An author-captioned illustration reports marked resting tremor followed by improvement at 120 minutes, without an isolated tremor score or individual comparator. No patient video was accessed.

The primary PDQ-39 difference, +0.5 (95% CI −9.6 to +10.7), crosses the stated two-point noninferiority margin. Our interpretation: this interval does not establish the authors' noninferiority conclusion.

Reporting conflicts remain:

- Abstract adverse events: 56%/37.5%; body/Table 3: 12/18 versus 13/17.
- Withdrawal causes: prose nausea/diarrhea; table somnolence/diarrhea. Two withdrawals are called 12.5%, without reconciling 18 randomized versus 16 analyzed.
- Supplementary Table 1 ferritin means are 50.9 versus 76.6 ng/mL, contrary to the prose direction; comparator out-of-range counts are two for each renal test, despite the prose's all-normal statement.

The [official laboratory supplement](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13347555.1/sj-docx-1-pkn-10.1177_1877718X251383721.docx) contains 27 rows. Both named publisher versions retain these safety discrepancies. No cause, correction or new cohort is inferred.
