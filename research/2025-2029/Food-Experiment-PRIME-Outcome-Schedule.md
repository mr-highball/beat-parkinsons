# PRIME Outcome Schedule

## PRIME visit schedule and ingredient exposure

The schedule lists inertial gait, ten-meter walk, GSRS and Bristol stool form at baseline and weeks 24, 36 and 48. Three-day diet records are planned at screening and weeks 24 and 48. Protocol section 3.5.2 defines same-foot stride length, height-normalized stride and sensor walking speed. The registry names the ten-meter walk separately and includes constipation within GSRS. Extra-virgin olive oil is recommended within Mediterranean diet arms of the 80-person planned factorial diet and activity study, with no oil supplied and no separate oil assignment. The schedule provides planned outcomes and no observed measurements. Its control row says Group C while the corrected protocol and registry identify Group D. The official registry had no posted results on November 16, 2026.
