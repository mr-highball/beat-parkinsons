## Theacrine and fatigue

The [2014 conference pilot](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMC4271659-P49-Theacrine-Pilot-and-Fatigue.txt), [2016 journal study](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27164220-Theacrine-Pilot-Fatigue-Full-Report.txt) and [two](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-US20180104248A1-Theacrine-Caffeine-Patent-Fatigue-Lineage.txt) [patent filings](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-US20190374546A1-Theacrine-Caffeine-Continuation-and-Fatigue.txt) share the same acute 15-person fatigue comparison. On the isolated supplement, the reported change was −6.7%, compared with +5.8% on placebo. The open-label component and blinding terminology vary across records. The nominal review count does not replace the primary study enrollment. The [cross-period lineage note](../2015-2019/Observations/2018-Theacrine-Fatigue-Source-Lineage.md) links the separate longer-duration [fatigue-scale trial](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID26766930-Theacrine-and-Eight-Week-Fatigue.txt), the 50-person [conference abstract](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-ISSN-P36-Theacrine-Caffeine-and-Subjective-Feelings.txt) and its likely [full-report counterpart](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-DOI100034-Theacrine-Caffeine-Crossover-Full-Report.txt), and the evidence boundaries. Exposure mismatch leaves the closer brewed-tea Parkinson pilot as the basis of the existing rating. An [injected-constituent experiment](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMID22579816-Isolated-Theacrine-and-Rat-Locomotion.txt) measures locomotor activation in rats.

## Coffee, olive oil and saffron

The [soluble coffee crossover](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID21773723-Soluble-Coffee-and-Mental-Fatigue.txt) reports lower acute fatigue after caffeinated soluble coffee in exploratory tests. The enriched-decaf contrast against regular decaf was inconclusive at p=.074. A general coffee review summarizes the direction more favorably than this original warrants. The [olive-oil stress comparator](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15566625-Olive-Oil-Control-and-Perceived-Stress.txt) had favorable within-arm change, with inconclusive olive-oil between-group comparisons and no Parkinson anxiety scale. The [saffron drinking-water model](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID23938314-Saffron-and-Dopaminergic-Cell-Counts.txt) and [isolated petal kaempferol experiment](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2007-PharmacologyOnline1205-Saffron-Petal-Kaempferol-and-Forced-Swim.txt) measure dopaminergic-cell counts and rodent immobility, respectively. The latter did not administer ground culinary stigma.
