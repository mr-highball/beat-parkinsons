## Cacao and isolated epicatechin

The cacao-mass author abstract reports favorable delayed avoidance in the acute rat comparison, an opposite trend in the two-week comparison and no escape-latency change in either. The longer comparison provides no clear behavioral benefit. Group-level estimates and design details require fuller access. In an independent comparison, healthy mice receiving isolated epicatechin showed greater open-field center exploration with similar total distance. The elevated plus maze analysis compares open and closed arms within groups. These animal measures add context to the anxiety scorecard, which retains its minimal easing lean. The direct Parkinson comparison for plain solid dark chocolate remains open. ## Distinct exposures and outcomes

- The author abstract of the Arora rat comparison reports relief in mechanical pain measures after injected isolated curcumin. The full result tables and uncertainty remain open. Du's original report measures neuronal markers after curcumin and gives no between-group movement result for its rotation screen. Keep the pain proxy, motor eligibility screen and clinical Parkinson outcomes separate. [Arora author abstract](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID21612876-Arora-Curcumin-Pain-and-Mood-Model.txt) · [Du original](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMID22622825-Du-Curcumin-and-Neuronal-Markers.txt)
- The Wardle caffeine comparison found a faster button-press rate and no clear change in effort choices. The outcome is a task measure in healthy adults rather than a fatigue severity scale. A separate review discusses coffee for hypnic headaches. [Caffeine comparison](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMID22750066-Wardle-Caffeine-Effort-and-Tapping.txt) · [Headache review](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID23054583-Coffee-in-Hypnic-Headache-Care.txt)
