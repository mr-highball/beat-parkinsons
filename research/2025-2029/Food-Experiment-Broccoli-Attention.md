# Broccoli-related preparations and attention

## Inattention after a sulforaphane adjunct in children with ADHD

First online October 30, 2025 according to the NCBI article date. The study randomized 70 children to sulforaphane or placebo for eight weeks alongside methylphenidate. Thirty-two and 31 completed the two groups. The primary abstract reports favorable parent- and teacher-rated inattention results, with Cohen d of 1.446 and 1.055. The public abstract does not establish the botanical source, preparation, raw broccoli exposure or a Parkinson comparison. Original full methods and trial accession remain open. ## Registered broccoli sprout extract and auditory sustained attention

First posted January 12, 2026. The Korean protocol compares broccoli sprout extract with cellulose for 12 weeks. The computerized neurocognitive battery includes auditory continuous performance, digit span and verbal learning. The retrieved record says Recruiting with its verification dated December 2025, and no results were posted. The extract, general cognitive-decline cohort and protocol outcomes do not supply a raw broccoli result in Parkinson disease.
