# Registered vegetable oil food and sleep in young adults

## Registered vegetable oil food and sleep in young adults

The two periods compare test food after dinner with no test food. Before applying the trial to flaxseed oil insomnia or canola oil daytime sleepiness, the oil identity and result measures must be established.
