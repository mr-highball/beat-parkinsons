# Round 1 green-bean fatigue, caffeine and orange source audit

The November 24, 2026 review checked 81 source scopes across 60 originals. It resolved 72 contextual exclusions and eight related scopes. One green-bean pair remains open for the original supplement. Eight related assessments, one access-pending assessment and seven scorecard rechecks preserve the evidence limits.

## Green beans and fatigue

The Nurses’ Health Study analysis followed 78,366 women aged 60 and older. Its food-frequency validation identifies string beans. The main paper reports a separately measured fatigue component for total vegetable intake. The adjusted hazard ratio was 0.83 (95% CI 0.78–0.88) for at least five daily servings versus fewer than two. This is a broad vegetable comparison in older women. The main paper does not give a distinct cooked-bean estimate for fatigue or assess Parkinson symptom change. Supplemental Tables 2–3 contain more detail about the fatigue and vegetable-category analyses. Their exact rows are still unavailable from the checked routes. Claim 0561 retains Unknown and the source remains queued. [Original cohort and access gap](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID33022693-Green-Bean-Questionnaire-and-Aggregated-Frailty-Fatigue.txt)

## Caffeine and treadmill endurance

In an animal experiment, injected isolated caffeine improved treadmill performance in mice expressing forebrain adenosine A2A receptors. The improvement was absent in the studied knockout groups. Workload and treadmill performance are separate from fatigue severity after drinking brewed coffee. This mechanism result is recorded as indirect context for claim 0121. [Original experiment](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32770138-Injected-Caffeine-and-Mouse-Treadmill-Endurance.txt)

## Orange flavor and constipation

A randomized probiotic trial used orange-flavored granules and a placebo described as similar in appearance and taste. Stool-frequency differences follow assignment to the probiotic intervention. The formulation description does not establish a raw-orange exposure contrast. The prior orange-pomace comparison remains the basis of the weak easing lean for claim 0878. [Original probiotic trial](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID33244562-Orange-Flavored-Probiotic-Granules-and-Constipation.txt)

## Olive and sunflower oil

A study in older people at risk of dementia measures anxiety with GAD-7 and includes olive oil in its Mediterranean diet screener. It does not estimate a separate olive-oil intake or preparation effect. In a Parkinson-related animal model, injected sunflower-oil emulsion serves as a vehicle. Its comparison does not measure clinical rigidity after consuming cold-pressed oil. [Olive oil screener scope](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID33173523-Mediterranean-Diet-Screener-Olive-Oil-and-Anxiety.txt) · [Sunflower oil vehicle source](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID33062012-Sunflower-Oil-Vehicle-in-Parkinson-Rat-Model.txt)
