# Topical piperine and swallowing symptoms

## Piperine in a burning-mouth case series

Varoni and colleagues described 17 people with burning mouth syndrome, of whom 14 were reassessed after eight weeks. A piperine tablet was dissolved in the mouth and expectorated. Dysphagia was recorded in 4 of 17 at baseline and 3 of 14 at follow-up. The change in denominators prevents a paired inference from those counts. The series gives no Parkinson subgroup or comparator.
