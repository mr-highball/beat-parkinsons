# Ferulic acid behavioral evidence related to chives in 2025–2029

## Oral ferulic acid and anxiety-like behavior

Simran and colleagues first published on 21 July 2026. They exposed Swiss albino mouse pups to maternal separation and tested adult responses after 15 days of oral purified ferulic acid at 40 or 80 mg/kg. The study also included a 20 mg/kg oral fluoxetine comparator. Behavioral assessments included the elevated plus maze, open field, tail suspension, and Morris water maze. The abstract reports that maternal separation induced anxiety-like and depressive-like behaviors and that ferulic acid attenuated behavioral deficits. The public abstract does not give arm-level estimates or a direct effect comparison with fluoxetine for either anxiety-related task. The publisher showed a subscription preview, and PubMed lists public PMC availability from 1 August 2027.

The stress model addresses an affective outcome after isolated compound exposure. It contains no dried chives and no Parkinson's population. Its favorable direction does not establish a direction for the clinical anxiety outcome in claim 0789.
