# Mechanisms and models

### 2026 — Genetic variants across ancestries

The Global Parkinson’s Genetics Program analyzed established Parkinson’s and parkinsonism-associated genes in a multi-ancestry sample of about 100,000 cases and controls. The spectrum of GBA1 and LRRK2 variants differed substantially across ancestry groups. This case-control sample estimates the distribution in its participants and informs whom genetic studies and targeted trials should include. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42456684/)
