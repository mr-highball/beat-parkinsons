# Registered turmeric and black pepper multidomain intervention

## Registered turmeric and pepper blend with timed cognitive tests

Both groups receive a program that includes exercise, cognitive training and nutrition guidance. The supplement comparison tests turmeric with black pepper against placebo, with timed cognitive tests and gait measures planned. The retrieved registry record has no posted results or separate pepper arm.
