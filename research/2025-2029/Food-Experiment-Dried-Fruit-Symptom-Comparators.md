# Dried Fruit Symptom Comparators

## Fresh mango depression questionnaire registry

The randomized open-label crossover plans 60 generally healthy adults. The intervention compares 100 kcal/day fresh mango with 100 kcal/day low-fat cookies for four weeks in each period. One primary outcome is change in depression questionnaire scores from baseline to week 4. The record does not name the instrument or report results. The current record is recruiting, status last verified January 2026, with actual start January 23, 2025 and estimated completion December 31, 2027. The food preparation and population differ from dried mango in Parkinson disease. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06800833) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/NCT06800833-Fresh-Mango-Depression.txt)

## Tart cherry and repeated cycling registry

The registered crossover aims to enroll 15 men and compare a 200 g beverage made with 30 mL tart cherry concentrate to maltodextrin and flavored powder placebo twice daily for seven days and before exercise. The listed outcomes are endurance, anaerobic performance, neuromuscular function, maximal voluntary contraction and force steadiness. Posted results and a patient-reported fatigue scale are absent from the checked record. The title refers to fatigue during exercise, which the registered measures define as performance and neuromuscular outcomes. [Original source](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000065488) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/UMIN000057292-Cherry-Cycling.txt)

## Dried mango offered during an acute affect experiment

Gao and colleagues randomly assigned 88 university students to positive, negative or neutral videos and offered six optional snacks, including dried mango. Intake was analyzed as total snack calories. The Positive and Negative Affect Schedule measured short-term emotional states before and after viewing. The manuscript describes no subject-specific mango exposure or independent mango effect and does not measure depressive symptom severity. [Original author manuscript](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/403152042_The_effect_of_screen_use_while_eating_on_emotions_A_lab-based_experimental_study_on_the_moderating_role_of_emotional_valence_of_videos) [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42442054/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/PMID42442054-Dried-Mango-Affect.txt)
