# Cold-pressed olive oils and exploratory behavior

## Measured cold pressing and separate open-field and maze contrasts

Ilgin and colleagues verified cold pressing for two Memecik olive oils at or below 27 °C and measured 775 versus 77 mg/kg total polyphenols. They assigned 24 adolescent male rats to either oil or water. The higher-polyphenol group spent more time in open arms than water controls, while open-field center time was inconclusive across groups. Oil preparation groups did not clearly differ on the anxiety-like outcomes. The water comparison does not account for energy intake or identify a phenolic dose effect. The rat study has no Parkinson model.
