Interviews with 105 people in Gorontalo took place from June 2021 to March 2022. In Table 2, Pala is identified as Myristica fragrans (voucher GOR-2152). The row lists 20 reports of use for diarrhea and one each in three other indication categories. Fruit and seed, two preparation methods, and oral or topical routes are pooled in the row. The investigators give no method, dose or route specific to the diarrhea reports. The adverse-effect table reports nausea and vomiting for one nutmeg response. The mention of constipation in that table is in the separate guava row. The discussion's mechanistic column cites an animal toxicology paper for its nutmeg assertion. The separate 1975 and 1976 clinical letters describe cancer-related secretory diarrhea.
