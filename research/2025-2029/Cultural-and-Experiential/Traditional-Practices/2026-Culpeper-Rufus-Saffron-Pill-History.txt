The historian describes a pill attributed to Rufus of Ephesus. The translated London formulation lists aloes, myrrh and saffron, with lemon syrup. The historian reports that a similarly named preparation in the 1914 British Pharmacopoeia no longer lists saffron. Preserve the actual ingredient lists when comparing editions. A common remedy name does not establish continuity of exposure or observations about saffron alone.
