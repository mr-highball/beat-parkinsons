The field inventory interviewed 205 people during 2021–2024 and recorded 119 plant species. Table 3 identifies Ficus carica L. as Anjeer and records it under voucher CDKU50. The table reports fruit given for constipation. Its separate root entry concerns a skin application.

The constipation entry gives neither a precise preparation nor an individual outcome. The survey does not establish a Parkinson diagnosis, document rest tremor or report change attributable to the fruit. The count of participants applies to the survey as a whole. It cannot be read as the number who used this fruit or observed benefit.
