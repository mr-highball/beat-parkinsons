The researchers conducted interviews with 57 participants from three communities. The source distinguishes oral decoctions, topical mixtures and postpartum food practices. Zingiber officinale occurs in compound drinks and a topical mixture, cinnamon and nutmeg appear in mixed preparations, and turmeric and coconut oil are part of a topical lotion. A raw-egg mixture uses Piper nigrum as white pepper. Separate preparations refer to black pepper or the same species with a different application.

The basil entry identifies holy basil, Ocimum tenuiflorum. Cymbopogon citratus and a separate species, C. nardus, occur in bathing practices. The authors describe reported aims such as warmth, postpartum recovery and energy. These are culturally attributed uses without measured clinical outcomes or ingredient-specific comparative effects.
