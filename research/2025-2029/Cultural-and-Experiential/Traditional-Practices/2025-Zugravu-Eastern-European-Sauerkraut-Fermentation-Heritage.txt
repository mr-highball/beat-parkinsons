Zugravu & Constantin (2025, Foods, PMC12785569) document spontaneous lacto-fermentation of shredded white/green cabbage (Brassica oleracea var. capitata) in salt brine—sauerkraut—as one of the oldest and most enduring everyday household and artisanal preservation traditions across Poland, Romania, Slovakia, Bulgaria, and broader Central/Eastern Europe. Traditionally consumed throughout winter for vitamin C, dietary fiber, organic acids, and perceived digestive support, artisanal sauerkraut varies substantially in microbial consortia, salt content, and fermentation length. The authors note that despite centuries of culinary use and modern interest in gut-health bioactives, rigorous human clinical trials remain sparse.
