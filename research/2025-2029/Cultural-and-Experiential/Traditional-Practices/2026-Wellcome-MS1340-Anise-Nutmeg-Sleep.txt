Title: Scent-based anise and external nutmeg sleep preparations in Wellcome MS.1340
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Historic household manuscript, inspected original page image
Source: https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b19703181_MS1340_0124.JP2/full/1700,/0/default.jpg
Catalogue: https://wellcomecollection.org/works/wv6vn7hx
Original locator: Wellcome MS.1340, folio 122v, entries 516–517.
Publication date: undated manuscript entries
Capture date: 2026-11-25
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Date basis: First documented capture and inspection of the original image for this entry. The catalog dates the compilation approximately 1675 to 1710. The individual entries have no year on the inspected page.

Entry 516 records an anise and rosewater scent preparation for difficulty falling asleep. Entry 517 records grated nutmeg with green rosemary and other ingredients in an externally applied mixture intended to promote sleep. These are separate records and neither describes eating nutmeg. They record household practices rather than measured responses.

Reported direction: Both entries intend to aid sleep.
Authenticity: The original image confirms the titles, entries and routes. The 2026 historical article cites the anise entry. The neighboring nutmeg entry was read independently from the folio.
Efficacy evidence: No measured sleep response, control, Parkinson diagnosis or attribution to individual ingredients.
Related research: research/2025-2029/Source-Text/PMC13242262-Round1-Original-Scope.txt. Round 1 claim 0774 asks about oral ground nutmeg.
Access and follow-up: The separate ground nutmeg and rosewater passage attributed to Folger V.b.286, p. 53, remains verified through the historical article. Its original page image was not recovered.
