Aslan (2025, J Ethnobiol Ethnomed, PMID:41204229, PMC12595648) documents ethnobotanical practices across central and rural neighborhoods of the Ergani district in Diyarbakir, Turkey. Eruca sativa Mill. (local name: Roka) leaves and tender shoots are traditionally consumed raw in salads and local dishes as a culinary green credited with aiding digestion and stimulating appetite. No informants reported Parkinson's disease symptoms or constipation-severity outcomes, so this record serves strictly as cultural and dietary context.
