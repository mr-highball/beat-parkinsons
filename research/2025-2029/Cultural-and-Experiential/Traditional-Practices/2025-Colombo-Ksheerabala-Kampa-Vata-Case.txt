Title: Ksheerabala Taila as part of a reported Panchakarma regimen for Kampa Vata
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://archive.cmb.ac.lk/server/api/core/bitstreams/7fc5f5ae-5c96-45bb-ab22-5e642064e677/content
Source kind: Traditional-practice clinical case in university symposium proceedings
Publication date: 2025
Date basis: University of Colombo Annual Research Symposium 2025 proceedings. The older Ayurvedic text date is attributed by the abstract.
Source locator: Proceedings of the Annual Research Symposium 2025, p. 445, one-page original abstract.
Community/context: Clinicians in a Sri Lankan Ayurvedic hospital describe a contemporary regimen they associate with the cited Vangasena text. The abstract identifies the diagnosis as Kampa Vata and discusses clinical resemblance to Parkinson disease.
Exposure and reported symptom: Multiple treatments included Ksheerabala Taila in massage, scalp and enema applications. Other oils, formulations and procedures were used. Sesame oil is a base ingredient of the documented formula. Symptoms included hand and leg tremor, rigidity, slow movement and postural instability.
Reported direction: The authors reported improvement with the full regimen, with reported Hoehn and Yahr stage changing from 3 to 2 and tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia and instability composite items described as improved.
Authenticity: The university-hosted published abstract is available. Independent confirmation of diagnostic criteria, full case tables or the text's historical attribution was not available in the abstract.
Efficacy evidence: A single bundled before-and-after case lacks an isolated ingredient, independent control or daily off-time contrast. The abstract does not resolve concurrent medications, the tremor subtype or duration of benefit. It supplies a lead for the clinical context, with no effect assigned to eating sesame oil.
