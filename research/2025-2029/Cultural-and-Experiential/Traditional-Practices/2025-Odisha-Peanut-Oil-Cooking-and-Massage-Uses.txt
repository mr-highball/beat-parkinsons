Title: Peanut seed oil in the Mayurbhanj traditional oil survey
Source kind: Traditional practices, ethnobotanical field study
Publication date: 2025-12-27
Date basis: First public publication date printed by the journal.
DOI: 10.32859/era.32.58.1-21
Citation: Behera and colleagues. Ethnobotany Research and Applications 32:58.
Source: https://ethnobotanyjournal.org/index.php/era/article/download/7650/2251/74514
Text coverage: Original methods and Table 2.

The peanut seed oil entry describes cooking, newborn massage, dry-skin treatment and minor burn care. Seven citations and 17 total use reports relate to the combined oil uses in the table. These counts measure reported use, with no documented tremor response, clinical comparison, pressing grade or Parkinson-specific use. The survey is geographically and contextually distinct from the 2018 Sultanpur plant-use account.
