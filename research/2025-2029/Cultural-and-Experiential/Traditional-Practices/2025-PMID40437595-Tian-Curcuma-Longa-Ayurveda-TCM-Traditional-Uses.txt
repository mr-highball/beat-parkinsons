Tian et al. (Chinese Medicine, 2025; PMID:40437595; PMC12117689) review the traditional applications of dried Curcuma longa L. rhizome across Ayurveda and Traditional Chinese Medicine alongside modern health foods and dietary supplements. Historically, turmeric has been employed across South and East Asia as both an everyday culinary spice and a traditional preparation for digestive disturbances, circulatory sluggishness, respiratory complaints, and skin conditions. The review underscores the distinction between traditional culinary/whole-rhizome preparations and concentrated isolated curcumin or nano-formulated supplements investigated in modern clinical trials. Publication date (2025) is distinct from centuries-old Ayurvedic/TCM textual traditions; documenting historical usage does not verify clinical efficacy in Parkinson's disease.
