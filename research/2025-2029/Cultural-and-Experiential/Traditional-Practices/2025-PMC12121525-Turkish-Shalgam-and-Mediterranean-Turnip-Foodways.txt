Reported direction: Traditional culinary consumption of boiled, pickled, and lactic-acid fermented turnip (Turkish shalgam with black carrot and rock salt; Anatolian and Levantine stewed/pickled roots) valued for winter digestion and palatability, with practical awareness of cruciferous flatulence and traditional thorough cooking mitigating raw goitrogens; no Parkinson efficacy claim.
Authenticity: Peer-reviewed open-access food-science documentation (Sengun et al. 2025, PMC12121525; Safkan et al. 2024, PMID:39653493) cross-referenced with Ye et al. 2026 (PMC13703587) and Galanty et al. 2024 (PMC11012840).
Efficacy evidence: Culinary and traditional fermentation context only; probiotic lactic-acid fermentation and high sodium content of pickled/fermented turnip differ materially from plain boiled or roasted turnip root and do not establish Parkinson symptom effects.

Title and source: Traditional Turkish shalgam (salgam) lactic-acid fermented turnip-and-black-carrot beverage and regional boiled/pickled turnip foodways, documented in Sengun et al., Food Sci Nutr 2025 (PMC12121525), Safkan et al., Fungal Biol 2024 (PMID:39653493), and Ye et al. 2026 (PMC13703587).
Community/context: Across southern Turkiye (Adana, Mersin, Hatay), the Levant, and Iran, turnip roots (Brassica rapa) are consumed in winter soups, stews, roasted dishes, pink salt-brine pickles, and shalgam—a traditional lactic-acid fermented drink combining black carrot, sliced turnip root, bulgur flour, sourdough starter, and rock salt dominated by Pediococcus acidilactici and lactobacilli.
Exposure, tolerability, and limits: Culinary practices emphasize thorough boiling/stewing or fermentation to soften fibrous turnip flesh, mellow sharp glucosinolate pungency, and reduce raw myrosinase-mediated goitrin formation (consistent with Galanty et al. 2024 PMC11012840). High salt content in pickled/fermented shalgam differs sharply from unsalted boiled or roasted turnip (relevant to blood-pressure/orthostatic management in PD), and cruciferous sulfur compounds can cause bloating in sensitive individuals. Supplies zero controlled Parkinson clinical outcomes.
