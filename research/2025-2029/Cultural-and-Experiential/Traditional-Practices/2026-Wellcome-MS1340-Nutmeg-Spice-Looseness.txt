The entry headed for looseness gives an orally consumed wine-based mixture including nutmeg, ginger, cinnamon and galangal, alongside a separate external preparation. It addresses loose stools in a household setting.

Reported direction: Intended relief of loose stools for the mixture.
Authenticity: Verified against the original page and entry number.
Efficacy evidence: No individual patient comparison, exposure to nutmeg alone, Parkinson diagnosis or measured constipation outcome. An intended use for diarrhoea does not establish a constipation effect.
Related research: Round 1 claim 0168, ground nutmeg and constipation.
Access and follow-up: Keep the oral preparation separate from the neighboring external nutmeg sleep recipe on folio 122v.
