The already cataloged survey has 71 respondents: 29 current users, 16 former users and 26 never-users. Reported experiences include tremor and other motor relief, commonly with powder in liquid alongside prescribed medication. Reasons for stopping include gastrointestinal intolerance and no perceived effect.

Reported direction: Mixed perceived benefit and discontinuation/adverse experiences.
Authenticity: Original conference abstract inspected; existing accession reused.
Efficacy evidence: Self-selected observational reports, without a tremor-specific denominator, controlled comparison or verified resting-tremor phenotype. Lower weight than controlled intervention studies.
Study family: This record adds cultural/experiential context to the existing source, not another cohort or independent replication.
Open trail: Symptom-level measurements, standardized product chemistry and medication attribution.
