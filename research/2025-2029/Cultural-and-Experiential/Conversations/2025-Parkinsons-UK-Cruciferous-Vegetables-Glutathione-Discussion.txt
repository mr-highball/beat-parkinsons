Title: Parkinson's UK public forum discussion recommending sulfur-rich cruciferous vegetables to support glutathione in Parkinson's disease
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/glutathione/44808
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2025-04-30
Date basis: Opening post timestamp (2025-04-30; dietary list post #4 dated 2025-05-01; acknowledgement 2025-05-04) on public Parkinson's UK Discourse forum thread.

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened on April 30, 2025, a member requested accessible information about glutathione to share with their neurologist. Alongside a moderator link to the Michael J. Fox Foundation, a contributor on May 1, 2025 posted a summary of dietary glutathione-supporting foods highlighting sulfur-rich vegetables—specifically garlic, onions, and cruciferous vegetables—together with protein sources, selenium-rich foods, and vitamin C fruits, which the opening poster thanked them for on May 4, 2025. All participant names and handles are omitted. The exchange captures contemporary patient interest in cruciferous vegetables for oxidative-stress biology in Parkinson's while supplying no firsthand symptom contrast for red cabbage or radish.
