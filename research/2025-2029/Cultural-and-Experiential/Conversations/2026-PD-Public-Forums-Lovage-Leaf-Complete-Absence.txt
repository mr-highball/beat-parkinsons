Title: Public Parkinson's forum audit: absence of lovage leaf discussions
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2026-11-26
Date basis: Live public forum search capture date (2026-11-26) across Parkinson's UK Community Forum Discourse search endpoint and public HealthUnlocked Cure Parkinson's / Reddit r/Parkinsons web indexes.

A live full-index query of the public Parkinson's UK Community Forum Discourse platform on 2026-11-26 returned zero topics and zero posts mentioning 'lovage'. Parallel public web-index queries across HealthUnlocked Cure Parkinson's and Reddit r/Parkinsons likewise found lovage virtually absent from Parkinson's patient and caregiver dietary discussions (unlike culinary parsley or celery). This explicitly documents the absence of standalone real-world experiential data for fresh or dried Levisticum officinale leaf across major English-language Parkinson's disease forums.
