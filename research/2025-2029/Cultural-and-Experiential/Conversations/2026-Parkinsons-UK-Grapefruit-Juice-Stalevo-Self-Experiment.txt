Title: Public Parkinson forum firsthand report on trying grapefruit juice with Stalevo and medication interaction cautions
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/changing-to-madopar-from-stalevo/8189
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2026-02-25
Date basis: Post date on Parkinson UK public forum thread 8189 (2026-02-25).

In a February 2026 post within a Parkinson UK medication discussion (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/changing-to-madopar-from-stalevo/8189), a person with Parkinson disease described previously trying grapefruit juice alongside Stalevo (levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone) after reading that grapefruit might prolong dose half-life. The participant reported that while results felt slightly positive, any perceived benefit was too small to be worth continuing, and explicitly warned peers that chemical constituents in grapefruit juice interfere with the response of other prescription medications. A separate May 2021 caregiver thread (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/cbd-and-medication-mixes/27831) similarly cited official grapefruit medication warnings when evaluating drug-interaction safety in a polymedicated parent with Parkinson disease.
