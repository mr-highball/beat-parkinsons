Title: Parkinson's UK forum caregiver discussions on peanut butter for weight maintenance, texture blending, and medication timing
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/losing-weight/44044
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2025-03-09
Date basis: Reply timestamp on Parkinson's UK public Community Forum weight-loss thread (2025-03-09; companion swallowing/blending discussion dated 2021-10-16 to 2024-02-19).

In a March 2025 Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on unintended weight loss in Parkinson's disease (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/losing-weight/44044), a caregiver noted the frequent challenge of maintaining weight while balancing carbohydrate, calorie, and protein intake around Parkinson's medication schedules, recommending full-fat milk and peanut butter on buttered toast as accessible high-calorie options alongside PD nurse input. In a companion caregiver thread on blending foods for a parent with Parkinson's struggling to eat solid foods (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/blending-food/29827, October 2021 through February 2024), contributors discussed thinning protein-rich nut butters such as peanut butter into smooth fruit smoothies to improve ease of swallowing rather than consuming dry hard nuts or thick sticky spreads unmodified. All personal names and account handles are omitted. Together these discussions highlight real-world PD culinary tradeoffs: calorie density vs choking/sticking hazards in dysphagia and protein-levodopa spacing.
