Title: Parkinson's UK 2025 forum thread on fading broad-bean benefit with disease progression and dietary protein balance
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/broad-beans/44138
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2025-03-11
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2025-03-11; replies through 2025-03-18).

In a March 2025 Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread, a participant relayed a secondhand account of a person with Parkinson's who had been advised to eat broad beans for natural levodopa and initially appeared to benefit, but found they stopped helping as the condition progressed. In replies, another participant noted that a Parkinson's nurse had mentioned broad beans (and sweet potatoes) alongside a largely plant-based, lower-protein dietary pattern, while another contributor cautioned that restricting dietary protein in older adults with Parkinson's risks losing skeletal muscle mass. All personal identifiers are omitted. The thread highlights ongoing patient awareness of broad beans as a dietary levodopa source alongside practical limits during disease progression and nutritional protein-timing trade-offs.
