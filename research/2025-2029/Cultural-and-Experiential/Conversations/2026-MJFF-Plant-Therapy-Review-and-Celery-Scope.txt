A community member summarized the new clinical review, including its celery, soybean and other plant categories and the findings assigned to them. The celery passage presented reported motor, sleep and quality-of-life improvement from a randomized study and did not specify the administered material. The post discusses research findings. It supplies no firsthand outcome from eating celery and no celery-specific symptom measurement.

The linked review's public references contain a Parkinson trial that administered pharmaceutical DL-3-n-butylphthalide, whose historical isolation from celery seed the original describes. That trial does not test raw celery stalk, fresh leaf or culinary celery. Treat the discussion as a citation and communication trail from the same review, without adding an independent efficacy observation.
