Privacy-safe summary: In a follow-up discussion to earlier anti-seed-oil debates on the Cure Parkinson's HealthUnlocked community, a contributor reviewed a molecular-physiology science communicator's video analyzing seed-oil studies (including cardiovascular and all-cause mortality data). The contributor noted that published studies showed conflicting narratives—some warning against high omega-6 seed oils and others concluding seed oils appear neutral or acceptable alongside adequate omega-3 intake—and specifically highlighted that the reviewed literature did not contain direct clinical studies demonstrating whether seed oils drive neuroinflammation or Parkinson's disease progression. This thread provides an important skeptical, nuanced community counterbalance to blanket anti-seed-oil warnings.

Evidence and limits: Informal community commentary on secondary nutrition videos; provides no controlled clinical data isolating refined corn oil.
