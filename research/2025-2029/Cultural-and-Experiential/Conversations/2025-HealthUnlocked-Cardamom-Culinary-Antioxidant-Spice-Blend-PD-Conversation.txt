In a July 2025 HealthUnlocked Cure Parkinson's discussion centered on Ceylon cinnamon and everyday neuroprotective food habits, community members described incorporating culinary amounts of ground cardamom together with cinnamon, ginger, cloves, and nutmeg into daily oatmeal/porridge or hot drinks for general antioxidant flavor and dietary enjoyment. No participant reported a specific before-and-after improvement or worsening in bradykinesia, tremor, cognition, fatigue, anxiety, or sleep attributable to ground cardamom itself, reflecting typical real-world multi-spice exposure without standalone experiential efficacy.
