Title: Parkinson's UK forum experiential reports on onion odor as a hyposmia benchmark and fresh onion chopping difficulties
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/fortunate-or-not/43653/2
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2025-02-02
Date basis: Public post timestamp on Parkinson's UK Community Forum (2025-02-02; companion posts dated 2013-11-26 and 2025-08-21).

Public threads on the Parkinson's UK Community Forum illustrate two lived-experience intersections between onions and Parkinson's disease unrelated to dietary symptom modification. First, in February 2025 (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/fortunate-or-not/43653/2), a member with PD hyposmia noted that even the normally strong smell of onions was barely detectable, echoing a November 2013 post by an unmedicated PD member noticing an unexpected return of onion aroma and eye-watering while peeling onions (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/breakthrough-research/6775/32). Second, in August 2025 (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/frustrated-rant/46401/3), a contributor described practical motor frustration preparing fresh onions, potatoes, and carrots when frozen pre-chopped alternatives proved unappetizing. All usernames are omitted. These accounts contextualize sensory and kitchen-prep realities without constituting efficacy or harm evidence for consuming onions.
