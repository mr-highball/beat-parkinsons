Title: Parkinson's UK public forum lived-experience report of losing the smell of nutmeg in Parkinson's hyposmia
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/fortunate-or-not/43653
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2025-02-02
Date basis: Exact opening post timestamp (2025-02-02) on the public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread 'Fortunate or Not?'.

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread dated February 2, 2025 discussing partial loss of smell in Parkinson's disease, a contributor described selective hyposmia in which certain pungent outdoor odors remained detectable while the aroma of nutmeg—specifically when baking Welsh cakes—had faded, expressing sadness at missing the scent of freshly cooked spiced cakes. A replying participant noted similarly blunted smells for onions and strawberries. All personal names and handles are omitted. This lived-experience account highlights how culinary nutmeg features in the everyday sensory impact of Parkinson's hyposmia rather than functioning as a tested oral treatment.
