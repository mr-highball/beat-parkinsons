Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion recommending oily fish such as salmon and mackerel during workup of orthostatic hypotension, fatigue, and severe vitamin D deficiency
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/severe-vitamin-d-deficiency/44745
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2025-04-26
Date basis: Opening post and reply timestamps (2025-04-26 through 2025-05-01) in public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread 44745.

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread from April-May 2025 (topic 44745), a member reported a five-day hospital admission following collapse from low blood pressure, during which blood tests revealed severe vitamin D deficiency (level 14) that the member suspected contributed to persistent fatigue. In replies, community participants noted that low winter sunlight in northern latitudes requires dietary or supplemental vitamin D, highlighting oily fish such as salmon and mackerel—alongside liver and egg yolk—as the richest dietary sources, while noting that severe deficiency ultimately required clinician-prescribed high-dose vitamin D3 loading therapy. All personal identifiers are omitted.
