In a public Cure Parkinsons HealthUnlocked thread opened on 2026-11-18 discussing gut-brain microbiome interventions in Parkinson disease, a participant detailed a daily lunchtime smoothie combining 10 g raw potato starch (explicitly added cold to preserve Type 2 resistant starch) with kefir, mixed berries, green apple or pineapple, walnuts, ground flaxseed, chia seeds, Ceylon cinnamon, cacao, 3 g MSM, 3 g creatine, and beetroot powder. Another participant in the thread reported substantial constipation relief after six weeks of separate inulin/FOS prebiotic powder with yogurt. All usernames and personal identifiers are omitted. This thread demonstrates real-world PD patient experimentation with raw cold potato starch (RS2) for gut-microbiome support while underscoring both extreme multi-ingredient confounding and the crucial biochemical gap between uncooked cold RS2 starch powder and hot boiled, baked, or roasted potatoes.
