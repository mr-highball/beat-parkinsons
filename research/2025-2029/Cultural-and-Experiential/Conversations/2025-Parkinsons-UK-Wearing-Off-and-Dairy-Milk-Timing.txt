Title: Parkinson's UK 2025 wearing-off discussion showing mixed personal experiences with dairy milk and levodopa timing
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/wearing-off/45475
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2025-06-17
Date basis: Public thread opening timestamp on the Parkinson's UK Community Forum (2025-06-17; replies spanning 2025-06-17 to 2025-06-19).

In a June 2025 Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussion on shortening levodopa ON-time windows (wearing off from ~5 hours down to ~3 hours with stiffness, slowness, and mental fogginess), participants compared practical medication and dietary strategies. One person taking carbidopa-levodopa four times daily noted leaving roughly an hour around meals (including morning porridge with milk) to limit protein interference, while a second participant remarked that drinking tea with milk shortly after levodopa did not seem to curb their medication effect. Another participant planned to reduce dairy and test non-dairy alternative milks out of concern over dairy and wearing off, and a caregiver reported that watching diet, pill timing, avoiding constipation, shifting protein later in the day, and switching to alternative milks appeared to give their partner more ON time. Together the thread reflects substantial individual heterogeneity in whether modest cow's milk intake noticeably alters daily OFF time.
