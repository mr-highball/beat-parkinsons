Title: Parkinson's UK Forum discussion of melatonin for vivid dreams and insomnia mentioning tart cherries
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/melatonin-yes-or-no/48308
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2026-01-11
Date basis: Public thread opening post dated 2026-01-11; reply suggesting tart cherries dated 2026-07-31 (Post #23).

In a Parkinson's UK public forum thread opened January 11, 2026 (all participant names/handles omitted), a person with Parkinson's asked about taking prescribed melatonin for constant vivid dreams and insomnia while worrying about next-day grogginess. Several participants shared mixed experiences with pharmaceutical melatonin (from major relief of dream-enactment self-injury/shouting to no benefit at 2 mg or variable 'hit or miss' effects), and the original poster expressed skepticism toward herbal remedies.
In Post #23 (July 31, 2026), a forum contributor suggested eating 1/2 cup of tart cherries 1 hour before bed as a natural source of melatonin.
Observation and limits: This is a brief community dietary suggestion rather than an inspectable firsthand before-and-after trial; preparation (fresh, frozen, or dried), cultivar, and individual sleep/dream-enactment outcomes were not reported. Cross-linked with controlled tart cherry sleep trials (PMID:20438325, PMID:28901958, PMID:39683518).
