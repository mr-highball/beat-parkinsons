Title: A public account of a broad dietary change and perceived walking improvement
Source: https://connect.mayoclinic.org/discussion/are-there-are-any-young-onset-parkinsons-patients-in-our-group/
Publication date: 2026
Date basis: The post is dated March 21 and describes an April 2025 experience as nearly a year earlier.
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Graph ingredients: tomato
Graph symptoms: -

Reported experience: A participant reported improved walking after a broad dietary change that reduced several foods. Tomatoes and tomato sauces were among the many foods discussed.
Evidence limits: Parkinson diagnosis was unconfirmed. The post does not measure stride length or test a separate tomato exposure. Tomato preparation, independent effects, and attribution remain unresolved.
