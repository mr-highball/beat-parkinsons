Summary of public Cure Parkinson's HealthUnlocked thread urging avoidance of industrial vegetable/seed oils (posted 2025-09-27; privacy-safe summary with all member handles omitted).

The opening post shared popular commentary warning against refined industrial seed and vegetable oils (a category in which lay commenters routinely include refined canola oil) on grounds of lipid oxidation, linoleic acid processing, and perceived brain-health harm, advocating replacement with olive oil, avocado oil, coconut oil, butter, ghee, or tallow. Replies reflected mixed reactions, with some members agreeing to minimize ultra-processed fried seed oils while others questioned sweeping claims and requested clearer human evidence. This thread documents prominent unfavorable/cautionary patient attitudes toward refined seed oils in online PD communities alongside the absence of controlled human PD worsening data.
