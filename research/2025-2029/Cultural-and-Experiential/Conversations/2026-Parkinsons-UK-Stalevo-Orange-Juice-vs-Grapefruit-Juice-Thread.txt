Title: Parkinson's UK forum exchange contrasting orange juice dissolution and grapefruit juice interactions with Stalevo
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/changing-to-madopar-from-stalevo/8189
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2026-02-25
Date basis: Public reply timestamps (posts #6 and #7, 2026-02-25) on Parkinson's UK Community Forum topic 8189 (opened 2015-03-21).

In February 2026 replies appended to a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on switching between Stalevo and Madopar (originally opened in 2015 by a caregiver describing swallowing difficulties and pill drooling), one member asked whether another contributor dissolved large Stalevo tablets in orange juice because of swallowing difficulty. The responder clarified they had tried citrus/grapefruit juice hoping to boost Stalevo duration, describing their personal results as slightly positive 'but not enough to be worth using,' and explicitly warned that grapefruit juice contains chemicals that interfere with other medications. All personal identifiers and usernames are omitted.
