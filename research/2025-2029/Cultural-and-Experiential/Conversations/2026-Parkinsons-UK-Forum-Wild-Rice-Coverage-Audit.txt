Public forum inspection summary (anonymized):
A direct search of the public Parkinson UK Discourse forum for "wild rice" (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/search?q=%22wild+rice%22, inspected 2026-11-26) returned zero posts across the public archive.

Contextual distinction from general rice discussions:
By contrast, general PD constipation threads (e.g., https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-and-parkinson/3694, 2013-06-11) contain humorous first-person remarks describing refined fried white rice as binding/constipating, while other constipation threads (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-any-cure/14613, 2020-02-04) recommend broader high-fiber swaps (oats, bran, psyllium, fluids) without isolating Zizania wild rice. Because Zizania aquatic wild rice is nutritionally and botanically distinct from Oryza sativa white or brown rice, general rice remarks cannot be extrapolated to wild rice.
