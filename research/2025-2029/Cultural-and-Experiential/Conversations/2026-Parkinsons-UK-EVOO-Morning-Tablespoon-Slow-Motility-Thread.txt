Title: Parkinson's UK forum reports on morning extra-virgin olive oil for slow motility, Mediterranean fish salads, and topical calf massage
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/slow-motility-constiption/50272/5
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2026-10-19
Date basis: Public post timestamp on Parkinson's UK Community Forum (2026-10-19; additional EVOO diet and topical leg-massage threads dated 2021-08-11 and 2018-11-22).

In an October 2026 Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on slow motility and constipation, a contributor described taking one tablespoon of extra-virgin olive oil upon waking as one element of a strict multi-part routine alongside 30 to 60 daily heel drops, eliminating constipating foods, and regular exercise—explicitly noting all steps had to be combined. Earlier public threads on the same forum include a 2021 dietary post using drizzled extra-virgin olive oil over vegetable salads and oily fish (sardines, salmon, mackerel) (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/treating-parkinsons-with-diet/29027/4) and a 2018 post mixing 4 drops of CBD oil into warm olive oil for topical calf massage to relax tight leg muscles (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/cbd-oil/16547/8). All personal identifiers and forum handles are omitted. These uncontrolled community discussions reflect real-world culinary and self-care interest in olive oil but do not isolate olive oil or establish clinical efficacy for Parkinson's symptoms.
