Public forum inspection summary (anonymized):
A direct search of the public Parkinson UK Discourse community (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/search?q=sorghum, inspected 2026-11-26) returned only one matching post across the archive (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/med-work-is/9076, opened 2016-04-13). That post did not describe eating sorghum grain or tracking any Parkinson symptom; it only quoted an encyclopedic note mentioning cyanogenic glycosides (dhurrin) in sorghum roots and pest resistance, which another community member dismissed as unfocused.

Broader community context and limits:
Across broader public PD nutrition discussions regarding gluten-free eating and microbiome diversity, sorghum is occasionally named alongside other naturally gluten-free whole grains as a wheat substitute, but no identifiable firsthand patient or caregiver before-and-after account isolates boiled or cooked sorghum grain for motor, cognitive, mood, sleep, or bowel outcomes. Absence of community threads reflects limited Western culinary uptake rather than evidence of benefit or harm.
