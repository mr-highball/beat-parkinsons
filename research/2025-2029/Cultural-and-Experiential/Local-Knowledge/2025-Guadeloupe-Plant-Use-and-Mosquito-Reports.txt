Date basis: 2025-07-19 original publication. The survey ran from September 2022 through January 2023.
Reported direction: Respondents reported plant use for mosquito control. Culinary or clinical effects were unspecified.
Authenticity: The original survey and printed counts were checked. The denominator discrepancy is recorded below.
Efficacy evidence: Reported use provides no comparison of Parkinson symptom outcomes.
Coverage: Factual summary of methods, sections 3.5–3.6 and the botanical tables.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The survey used a preselected panel of 38 plants and obtained 216 responses. The anti-mosquito section reports 187 regular plant users and 60 respondents using plants for that purpose. The reported 93.3% for Cymbopogon citratus corresponds to 56 of 60, while the printed fraction is 56/187. Preserve that denominator discrepancy. Basil, turmeric, mango and ginger identities appear in the survey or its separate literature tables. The text distinguishes low local selection for ginger from literature about ginger-related preparations.

The citrus entry is key lime, Citrus × aurantiifolia, and chickenpox is disease vocabulary. The design records local recognition and reported use for mosquito control. It does not measure efficacy on Parkinson symptoms or a culinary intervention.
