# Cumin formulations and swallowing-related outcomes

## Salivary flow in a hemodialysis crossover study

The original paper first appeared in 2026. The hemodialysis xerostomia crossover phase compared cumin-and-ginger spray with placebo. Salivary flow after seven or more days was 0.06 ± 0.08 versus 0.02 ± 0.06 g/min (p = .04). Dry-mouth severity favored the mixture at 14 days (p = .024). The outcome named in the abstract is salivary flow and perceived dryness. No clinical swallowing score or toasted cumin intake was assessed. Registry NCT06476223 matches the review-board number and design, with the primary source's complete registration text still an access trail.
