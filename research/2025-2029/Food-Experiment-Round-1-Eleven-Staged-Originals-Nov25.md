The proceedings retain each abstract’s own methods and findings. The stroke-model poster describes peanut oil as a reward. An IANCON poster reports coffee within grouped dietary observations in early-onset dementia. A genetics ePoster studies an apricot-seed constituent in a cell line. Each is a related lead with its separate preparation, subject group and outcome.

The black tea EEG study involved eight healthy volunteers, while a separate primate model used a catechin fraction and reported aggregate motor improvement. The cited studies resolve preparation and outcome differences that are easy to miss in the narrative review. A blackberry study reported different cell responses for commercial and wild extracts. These records retain the different results and document the source boundaries.

The new practitioner record preserves the Chinese medicine dietary framework and links to its clinical discussion. The separate Lumbee manuscript extends the exact botanical and recipe context with its earlier interview extract and culture record still available. Its supplementary tables remain an open source trail.
