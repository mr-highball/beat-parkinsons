# Cranberry Depression Followup

## COMBAT baseline depression screening in the Connell thesis

Connell's doctoral thesis uses the baseline data of COMBAT and CANN. Fifty cognitively healthy COMBAT participants were used as controls in a comparison with two cognitive-impairment groups. The Methods state that participants completed PHQ-9 and GAD-7 screening and that significant depression or anxiety was an exclusion criterion. This verifies before-treatment questionnaire collection for the described analysis. It gives no post-treatment depression estimate by randomized cranberry group. The original randomized study remains one cohort. ## COMBAT baseline controls in the published Connell analysis

The published analysis uses baseline COMBAT samples as 50 matched healthy controls and explicitly identifies NCT03679533. The Methods confirm that participants completed the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 and that those with clinically significant symptoms were excluded. The paper compares cognitive-status groups using pretreatment observations and provides no estimate of a cranberry-powder effect on depression. It is a related publication using the same original COMBAT cohort as the thesis. ## COMBAT baseline controls in the Liaquat analysis

Liaquat and colleagues first published this cross-sectional analysis on July 2, 2025. The 170 participants include 73 with subjective cognitive impairment and 60 with mild cognitive impairment from CANN and 37 healthy older controls from COMBAT at baseline. A Geriatric Depression Scale threshold below 10 belongs to CANN's mild cognitive impairment classification. The 2025 report and its supplement address diet, APOE genotype, microbial associations and cognitive and health measures. They provide no PHQ-9 comparison or depression estimate by cranberry assignment. The related 2026 Connell analysis uses 50 COMBAT controls and explicitly reports screening with PHQ-9 and GAD-7. ## CRANMOOD depression model and week twelve estimate

The publication describes 82 screened and 72 randomized and included in the intention-to-treat sample, with 34 cranberry-drink and 38 placebo assignments. The older official registry records actual enrollment of 64 and has no posted results. The available records leave that count difference unresolved. Table 2 gives HADS depression overall treatment p = .98, treatment-by-visit p = .70, and a separate adjusted week-12 difference of −0.404 points (95% CI −1.88 to 1.08, p = .59). The Profile of Mood States depression-dejection subscale was higher after four weeks of cranberry drink (+3.34 points, 95% CI 0.526 to 6.16, p = .02). The week 8 and week 12 contrasts were inconclusive, as was the overall treatment test. The treatment-by-visit interaction was p = .02. Section 3.2 summarizes the individual mood scores as inconclusive, while Table 2 gives the early visit result. The study measured a drink in healthy young adults and leaves dried cranberry and Parkinson depression unresolved.
