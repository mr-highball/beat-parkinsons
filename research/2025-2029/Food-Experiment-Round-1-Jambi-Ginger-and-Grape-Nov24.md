# Round 1 Jambi cultural sources, ginger and grape trials

This pass verified 98 exact checks across 60 publications and 38 frozen claims. It records 79 scope exclusions and 19 related scopes, plus a complementary original grape seed report. Three publications were already indexed and their existing archives were reused.

The [Papa trial](Source-Text/2026-Papa-Enovita-Grape-Seed-Tension-Outcome.txt) reports four-week POMS tension changes of −0.06 in the grape seed extract arm and +1.12 in placebo among 32 healthy adults. The reported group-by-time p=.549 leaves the tension comparison inconclusive. The [2026 Ginkgo report](Source-Text/2026-PMID42294065-Ginkgo-Cognitive-Trials-and-Omitted-Grape-Seed-Arm.txt) identifies the same parent trial but reports different arms. Its findings cannot be applied to grape seed. Neither report measures raw grape and Parkinson anxiety severity. The official NCT06309914 registry still has no posted result tables.

The [6-shogaol study](Source-Text/2026-PMID42375089-6Shogaol-Levodopa-Gut-Motility.txt) found a separate bowel motility benefit in the levodopa-plus-constituent mouse group. The reported pole-test result did not show added motor improvement and no rigidity-specific clinical outcome was measured. [Ginger-PD](Source-Text/Trials/NCT07882108.txt) is recruiting and has no posted results. The official registry lists biomarker and tolerability endpoints, leaving a separate clinical rigidity estimate open.

The [2012 Jambi field excerpt](../2010-2014/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2012-RISTOJA-Jambi-Constipation-Field-Excerpt.txt) records a constipation preparation using two locally named leaves. Its botanical identities and affiliation of the specific table entry are unresolved. The [2026 secondary review](Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2026-Jambi-Ginger-and-Turmeric-Cultural-Review.txt) lists ginger and turmeric in a different indication, attributed blood-pressure use. It also demonstrates why Jamaica cherry and Indonesian bay leaf require species verification. The records are dated and linked as separate cultural accounts with distinct provenance.

Thirty-six checked scopes concern dried dates and pain. The original archives distinguish treatment, biomarkers, measurement and dietary exposures without imputing a dried-date outcome.
