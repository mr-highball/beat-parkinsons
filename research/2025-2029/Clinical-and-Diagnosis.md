# Clinical and Diagnosis

### 2025 — α-synuclein seeding and hyposmia in PARS

A cross-sectional analysis of older adults with hyposmia examined seed amplification results alongside established prodromal markers. It extends smell-based enrichment research with a biological marker. Selected participants and cross-sectional measurement limit estimates of conversion. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39893720/)

### 2026 — Five-year outcomes within seeding-positive sporadic Parkinson’s

An observational cohort applying biological and functional staging found that people starting at stage 4 reached disability, postural instability, cognitive and autonomic milestones more frequently than participants in earlier stages. The analysis selected seeding-positive participants. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41808287/)
