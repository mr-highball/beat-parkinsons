# Parkinson feeding cases in the 2025 JSMCN supplement

## Commercial tube feed and trace elements during wound care

Gotō and colleagues describe a man in his sixties with young-onset Parkinson’s, gastrostomy, recurrent aspiration pneumonia in his history and a sacral pressure injury. His existing feed was Terumeal PG Soft. A trace-element supplement was added alongside zinc and multidisciplinary wound care. The pressure injury score went from 30 to 13 by transfer. Small amounts of oral intake resumed during swallowing rehabilitation. The report gives no ingredient-level intervention contrast or measured aspiration response. [2025 O-332, PDF p169](https://www.eiyou.or.jp/gakujutsu/28th/pdf/jsmcn_Vol28.Supplement_vo.pdf#page=169)

## Soft food and increased intake in parkinsonian syndrome

Fujita and colleagues describe a man in his seventies with parkinsonian syndrome, spinal disease, fatigue and reduced intake. Endoscopy showed a small amount of pharyngeal residue that cleared with additional swallows or throat clearing. He advanced from a modified texture to soft dishes while receiving rehabilitation, supplements and food brought from home. Intake and daily function improved during care. Food identities and a separate fatigue or swallowing response are unreported. [2025 O-146, PDF p123](https://www.eiyou.or.jp/gakujutsu/28th/pdf/jsmcn_Vol28.Supplement_vo.pdf#page=123)
