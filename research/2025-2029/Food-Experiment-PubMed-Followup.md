## Annonaceae exposure and atypical parkinsonism in French Guiana

Nasri and colleagues compared 67 patients with degenerative parkinsonism in French Guiana with 305 in the French West Indies. Atypical syndromes represented 41.8% and 66.2% of the two groups, respectively. Reported exposure to any of three *Annona* fruits was 79.2% and 93%, respectively. The species were soursop (*A. muricata*), sugar apple (*A. squamosa*), and custard apple (*A. reticulata*). The design compared patient cohorts and did not estimate an individual-level effect of *Annona* intake on risk of an atypical syndrome. The fruits are outside the frozen ingredient set, where apple refers to *Malus domestica*.
