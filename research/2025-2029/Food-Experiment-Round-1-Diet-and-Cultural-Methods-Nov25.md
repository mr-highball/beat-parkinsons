# Round 1 diet, cultural methods and original archive trail

- The printed 1986 aluminium review describes onion roots as specimens for observing cell injury. Orange appears in referenced laboratory colorants, and trout appears as a toxicology organism. The 1990 food-systems original discusses crop production, commodity forecasts and food markets. [1986 aluminium review](../1985-1989/Source-Text/PMC1474689-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [1990 food systems](../1990-1994/Source-Text/PMC1567760-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)
- The 2019 Parkinson inflammation review discusses Mediterranean dietary patterns and distinct cinnamon and curcumin research. The randomized and observational contexts require their own preparation and symptom judgments. The earlier cinnamon experiment tested actual ground Ceylon cinnamon in animals. The Sharma rat experiment used an isolated injected compound and broader motor tests. [2019 diet review](../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC6732577-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [2014 cinnamon report](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID24946862-Cinnamon-Motor-Extract.txt) · [2017 curcumin report](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28409389-Curcumin-Inflammation-and-Motor-Tests.txt)
- A review of dietary compounds distinguishes human green-tea polyphenols, black-tea theaflavin animal work, and oral tea-polyphenol work in nonhuman primates. These sources cannot be counted as separate outcomes of drinking brewed black tea for Parkinson bradykinesia. [Dietary compound review](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7140513-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Primary primate report](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID25498223-Tea-Polyphenols-Primate-Motor-Scope.txt)
- The 2021 Sri Lankan interviews describe skin, hair and oral care with culinary ingredient names in some topical products. Of 210 named candidates, 120 participated. Their accounts preserve route and purpose, without a falls comparison. [Cultural record](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2021-PMID34257686-Sri-Lankan-Cosmetic-Plants.txt)
- Coffee and sleep restriction yielded a behavioral report and a later imaging analysis with the same registry accession. Better attention can coexist with inconclusive individual-day sleepiness contrasts. The 2021 coffee poster likely overlaps the registered imaging cohort. The accessible reports leave its exact participant mapping unresolved. [2018 registration](../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/DRKS00014379.txt) · [2020 behavioral report](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID33373678-Baur-Coffee-Sleepiness-and-Attention.txt) · [2021 poster](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC8851538-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [2024 PET-MRI report](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-PMID38830861-Lin-Coffee-PET-MRI.txt)
- The Sri Lankan interview source and the dated Pikuni-Blackfeet source differ in community, setting and investigational aims. The Pikuni-Blackfeet manuscript describes culinary or traditional context separately from selected extracts. Its supporting tables remain open, as do two imaging supplements. [Pikuni-Blackfeet manuscript context](../2015-2019/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2017-PMID28088492-de-Rus-Jacquet-Pikuni-Blackfeet-PD-Plants.txt) · [Imaging proceeding locators](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Round-1-Imaging-Proceedings-Access-Nov25.md)
