## Exposure and outcome gaps

- **Raw fig and drooling, 0188.** The Wilson disease series counts eight cases with drooling among presenting features. The Parkinson airway interviews describe excess saliva, swallowing difficulty and drooling as separate experiences. A dietary comparison of ordinary oral raw fig with a separate clinical Parkinson drooling measure remains open. [Wilson disease original](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15146013-Original-Scope.txt) · [Parkinson airway interviews](Source-Text/2025-PMID40043228-Original-Scope.txt)
- **Dried fig and visual hallucinations, 0175.** The verified historical abstract concerns belladonna, a different plant. The older Parkinson-related hallucination case has an open body-access gap. A usable exposure comparison would measure visual hallucination frequency after culinary dried common fig in a diagnosed Parkinson cohort. [Autumnal psychosis abstract](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2003-PMID12743348-Autumnal-Psychosis-Original-Abstract.txt) · [Historical case access](../1995-1999/Source-Text/PMC2170387.txt)
- **Raw fig and rest tremor, 0559.** The recent gait study differentiates Parkinson motor subtypes. The fibrosis study evaluates a marker against aggregate motor ratings. The recovered 1993 actigraphy paper used rest tremor in exploratory grouping while tracking daily movement and fatigue. A documented culinary raw-fig exposure and a separate rest-tremor response remain needed.
