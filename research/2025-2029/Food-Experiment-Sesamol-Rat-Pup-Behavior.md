# Isolated sesamol and rat-pup behavioral endpoints

## Experimental sesamol and vigilance interpretation

Abu-Elfotuh and colleagues. J Neuroimmune Pharmacol. 2025;20(1):27. First public March 18, 2025. DOI `10.1007/s11481-025-10188-4`, PMID 40102360. Seven rat-pup groups included a monosodium glutamate model, an isolated sesamol arm, an astaxanthin arm, activity and combined arms. The authors describe better attention, locomotion and cognition after several interventions, including sesamol. Figure 1 measures open-field latency, ambulation and grooming and Y-maze spontaneous alternation. These are mixed behavioral endpoints, without a dedicated sustained-attention assay. No toasted oil or Parkinson group was studied. The constituent result remains adjacent evidence for claim 0528.
