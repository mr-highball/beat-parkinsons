# Round 1 medication and constituent originals

- In the Parkinson rat experiment, sunflower oil was injected into both the sham and disease-model groups. The drug comparison used isolated nardosinone and recorded levodopa-induced dyskinesia. The paper cites a 2020 national pharmacopoeia for traditional Gansong background. The inspected 2010 Hunan standard documents the dried root and rhizome, routes and customary indications in its own edition. Those indications establish customary knowledge without measuring the effects of sunflower oil, isolated nardosinone or Gansong on clinical Parkinson symptoms. [Rat study](Source-Text/PMC13003038-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Hunan monograph](../2010-2014/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2010-Hunan-Gansong-Root-Monograph.txt)
- The 2017 mouse experiment reports better pole, stride and balance-beam performance after isolated beta-caryophyllene in an MPTP model. The sources listed in the introduction include oils from cinnamon leaves and clove leaves or stems. The assigned culinary exposures use ground spice and their symptoms are rigidity, anxiety and pain. The experiment supplies no separate measurements of these outcomes after either spice. [Beta-caryophyllene scope](../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC5620604-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)
- Two 2018 nutrition meeting abstracts report laboratory alpha-synuclein findings for hydroxytyrosol alone or combinations of isolated compounds. The separate grape-powder trial in that collection measures metabolic lipids. The raspberry trial measures circulating metabolites. A 2021 thrombosis congress used resveratrol in a platelet assay and rosemary oil in hand massage. A 2022 prion meeting reported soybean oil as placebo in multiple sclerosis. Each source distinguishes the food name from the actual intervention, disease context and outcome. [Nutrition abstracts](../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC6265820-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Thrombosis proceedings](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC8495465-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Prion proceedings](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC9467582-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)
- In a vitamin B6 and kynurenine comparison, salmon identifies the CSF-only curve in Figure 4. A medication cost model priced orange-flavored ispaghula for constipation. A cannabidiol comment described sesame oil as a placebo for a Huntington disease crossover study. These original matches resolved the literature-screening question while leaving the frozen food hypotheses open to other evidence.
