## Coffee, sleepiness and fatigue

The nighttime driving crossover papers share the 12 young participants. The expanded 2007 paper adds 12 middle-aged participants. Caffeinated instant coffee improved nighttime sleepiness. Fatigue was measured separately and gave an inconclusive comparison, with mean fatigue ratings of 50 with caffeinated instant coffee and 58 with decaf. The two papers supply one family for the scorecard. [2006 original](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-PMID16754920-Philip-Coffee-Fatigue-and-Driving.txt) · [2007 expanded cohort](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2007-PMID18246990-Sagaspe-Coffee-Fatigue-and-Driving.txt) · [2010 citation trail](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2010-PMID20464765-Caffeine-Sleepiness-and-Shift-Work-Errors.txt)

In the six-person postnap study, participants experienced both the fortified-decaf and plain-decaf conditions on separate visits with a scheduled nap in each. The original reports lower acute fatigue after added caffeine. The 2026 AASM review displays six observations per condition and carries forward the same people. The original protocol and supplementary figure clarify the nap schedule. [Centofanti original](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32819191-Centofanti-Fortified-Decaf-and-Postnap-Fatigue.txt) · [AASM full report and supplement](Source-Text/2026-PMID42925443-AASM-Caffeine-Shiftwork-Review.txt)

Commercial canned coffee given to drivers with obstructive sleep apnea supplies a sleepiness comparison. The original Café-PD trial and registry identify its reported outcomes, with the adverse-event table still behind an access screen. Neither comparison supplies a separate outcome for chronic Parkinson fatigue from culinary brewed coffee. The validated 0121 scorecard retains a minimal easing lean across 14 evidence families. ## Grape, cinnamon and turmeric scope

A grape-polyphenol review included grape powder and resveratrol comparisons in different contexts. Its mention of Parkinson rigidity does not correspond to a frozen-grape result. The anxiety candidate tested a different plant, with grape only in background. The cinnamon and turmeric review hits relate to constituent pathways and molecular outcomes. Their measured outcomes do not establish directions for the frozen pain and rigidity claims.
