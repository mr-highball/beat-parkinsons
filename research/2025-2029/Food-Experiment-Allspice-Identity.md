# Ground allspice identity in an Argentine food standard

## Argentine product definition, November 2025

Article 2 of joint resolution 5/2025 identifies pimienta inglesa and pimienta de Jamaica as the fruit sold whole or ground under the name *Pimenta officinalis* Berg. Separate articles assign cayenne pepper to *Capsicum* and pink pepper to *Schinus molle*. This establishes wording and preparation for search terms. The instrument measures no neurological outcome. [Official publication, articles 2–4](https://nuevaweb.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/334375/20251112)

Plants of the World Online identifies *Pimenta officinalis* Lindl. as a synonym for *Pimenta dioica* (L.) Merr. Retain Berg in citations to the Argentine regulation and Lindl. in citations to the Kew record. Regional names also require checking against plant identity in the original source. [Kew synonym record](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:600557-1)
