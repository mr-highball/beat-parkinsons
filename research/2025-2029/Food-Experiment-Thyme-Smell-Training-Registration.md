# Thyme and cinnamon as smell training stimuli

## Thyme and cinnamon as smell training stimuli

The completed protocol identifies thyme among smell training stimuli and does not supply an isolated dietary effect.

[Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07190911) · [Local source text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT07190911.txt)

## Dill in a multiodor post COVID smell protocol

The official record has 40 actual participants and lists completed status without posted results. The two-month odor-training arm includes dill among 11 odors, with no isolated dill arm. The Taste and Smell Survey and Sniffin' Sticks compare the whole training intervention with no training in people after SARS-CoV-2. No cooking, leaf preparation or dill intake is identified. [Official source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07190911) · [Local registration text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT07190911.txt)
