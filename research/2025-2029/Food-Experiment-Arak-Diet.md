# Dietary diversity and food intake in the Arak case-control sample

## Two 2026 analyses of the same dietary cohort

The September 7 and September 9 papers share all 12 authors, ethics approval IR.MUI.RESEARCH.REC.1398.774, recruitment in Arak from August 2020 to April 2021, and counts of 105 newly diagnosed Parkinson cases and 215 controls. Both use a 147-item food-frequency questionnaire. These reports are analyses of one cohort. The 2023 dietary-pattern article gives the same ethics approval and counts and shares investigators. [September 7 accepted article](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1186/s40795-026-01473-3_reference.pdf) · [September 9 accepted article](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1186/s13104-026-08015-x_reference.pdf) · [Earlier article and food codes](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Arak-Diet.md)

Seyedmahalleh and colleagues reported inverse associations with Parkinson diagnosis for dietary diversity and the Lifelines Diet Score. The adjusted odds ratios were 0.287 (95% CI 0.185–0.447) and 0.879 (95% CI 0.803–0.963), respectively. The adjusted protein-source diversity association was inconclusive. The food table gives median potato intake of 38.95 g/day among cases and 17.00 g/day among controls. The Lifelines score includes filtered coffee and broad oil categories. The analyses model diagnosis, with no symptom outcome or controlled food comparison. [Primary results and Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40795-026-01473-3)

Amrollahi and colleagues reported lower odds of diagnosis at higher Comprehensive Diet Quality Index scores. The plant component had an inverse association and the animal component a positive association. The Adjusted Plant-Based Diet Index association was inconclusive. The paper reports mean white-potato intake of 54.70 versus 19.40 g/day in cases and controls, with separate group means for vegetable oils and a pooled tea and coffee variable. The means and medians come from different analyses of the same cohort. Neither paper links baked potato to choking, identifies olive oil grade or estimates within-Parkinson symptoms after a food exposure. [Primary results and Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13104-026-08015-x)
