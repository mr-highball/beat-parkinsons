# Eugenol behavioral results after experimental insults

## Eugenol nanoemulsion after experimentally imposed REM deprivation

Khan and colleagues compared isolated eugenol solution, drug-loaded nanoemulsion, and chitosan-coated nanoemulsion in rats. The formulation used a clove-oil phase and the principal nanoemulsion route was intranasal. Pharmacodynamic tests included forced-swim behaviors and measured locomotion. The authors describe changes in both groups of measures, which limits attribution of forced-swim changes to a distinct mood effect. The sleep-deprivation experiment imposed REM deprivation with a modified multiple-platform method, then assessed startle, prepulse inhibition and rotarod learning. Figure 18 reports better fourth-day rotarod performance in the coated nanoemulsion arm than in sleep-deprived controls (p<.0001), and a change with oral isolated solution (p<.01). Post-deprivation behavior is an indirect sleep-related outcome. The study did not measure restored sleep architecture, clinical insomnia, dream enactment, or ground allspice. One Results sentence references melatonin despite the eugenol protocol and figure. [Primary Sections 2.13–2.14 and Figure 18](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12044476/)

## Oral eugenol and depressive-like behavior after paraquat

Uchewa and colleagues reported 40 male Wistar rats in five groups. Three toxin-exposed groups received oral isolated eugenol for 14 days after paraquat exposure. Their abstract describes attenuation of social-interaction and tail-suspension immobility deficits. It gives no behavioral effect sizes or score distributions. Dopamine, tissue and biochemical readouts were separate from those behavioral measures. The high compound doses and toxin model define an indirect lead for claim 0809.

## Study-family checks for the Uchewa paraquat reports

Two reports give the same animal ethics reference, AE-FUNAI-2023/00321. The January 2025 Laboratory Animal Research paper (PMID 39810258) describes 40 male Wistar rats in five groups of eight. It measured wire grip and beam walking alongside cerebellar biochemistry, histology and gene expression. The Methods place the wire-grip test on experimental day 40 and tissue collection after the 42-day sequence. The 2026 Nigerian Journal of Pure and Applied Sciences paper (DOI 10.48198/NJPAS/25.B40) describes 40 adult Wistar rats in the same group structure, the same three oral eugenol dose levels, and the same toxin and treatment sequence. It reports sociability-chamber and inflammatory cytokine outcomes. The source gives no animal identifiers or explicit reuse statement, and no calendar date for the behavioral measurements. These records should be treated as potentially overlapping while their cohort linkage remains open. The July 2025 Journal of Ethnopharmacology report (PMID 40617359) gives a different approval, AE-FUNAI/FBMS/EAHC/24/004, despite another 40-rat, five-group comparison. It measures motor, open-field, memory and recognition outcomes. The June 2026 hepatic and renal study led by Alobu prints that same approval, but describes 32 rats in four groups and two different eugenol dose levels. The shared approval and distinct designs indicate that the ethics number alone cannot establish animal reuse or independence. The October 24, 2026 Brain Research Bulletin report (PMID 42926000) matches the 40-rat, five-group design and three oral eugenol levels and reports attenuation of sociability and tail-suspension immobility deficits. Its indexed primary abstract contains no ethics identifier, behavioral test dates, numerical behavioral differences, or statement of prior animal reuse. Publisher metadata confirm DOI 10.1016/j.brainresbull.2026.112176 and PII S0361923026004636. The public publisher API returned only core metadata with an open-access flag. Its full view required authorization and Europe PMC did not expose the Methods. The May report's sociability endpoint and the October report's social-interaction endpoint may overlap. One behavioral assessment for claim 0809 preserves the relevant data while replication status is unresolved.
