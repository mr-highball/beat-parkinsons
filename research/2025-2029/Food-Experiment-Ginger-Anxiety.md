# Ginger preparations and measured anxiety outside Parkinson disease

## Ginger extract and anxiety in a blinded craniotomy comparison

Kaewborisutsakul and colleagues randomized 48 adults undergoing craniotomy to a preoperative carbohydrate drink with standardized ginger extract or a matched drink using artificial flavor. Figure 2 reports no clear anxiety visual analogue scale difference at baseline or postoperative days one and two. The design directly tests addition of extract in this perioperative setting. ## Oral ginger extract and behavior after prenatal valproate exposure

Choi and colleagues first published February 3, 2026. The authors administered oral ginger extract for four weeks starting at age six weeks to mice exposed prenatally to valproate. The abstract reports improvement in anxiety-like behavior, social and memory tests. It does not give the anxiety assay estimates, exact effect sizes or extract preparation details. ## Oral gingerol extract and open-field behavior after spinal nerve ligation

After four weeks in a female rat nerve-ligation model, the higher of two gingerol-extract doses increased open-field center entries relative to vehicle (Figure 2, p < .05, n = 5–8 per group). The paper's abstract describes both doses broadly. The Results specifically identify the higher-dose comparison for center entries. This behavioral measure supplies indirect context for anxiety.
