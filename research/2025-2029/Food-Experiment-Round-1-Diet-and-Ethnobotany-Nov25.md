# Round 1 diet citation and ethnobotany trail

- A gut microbiota review attributes motor improvement to vegetable and quinoa fiber in 24 people with Parkinson disease and 69 controls. The cited Baert original reports 24 Parkinson donors and 39 controls. The researchers incubated donated fecal samples with different substrates and measured fermentation products. The study had no assigned dietary intervention or motor outcome. [Review, Table 2 and reference 300](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC10658262-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Baert original](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC8363715.txt)
- The Parkinson ketogenic review cites Choi's Parkinson feasibility trial and Harvey's trial in healthy adults. Harvey used sunflower oil as the long-chain comparator during a ketogenic diet. The estimates concern broader symptoms during diet induction and total mood disturbance. Choi assigned whole diets and provides no isolated sunflower comparison. [Review](Source-Text/PMC12647266-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Harvey original comparison](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID29951312-Harvey-Sunflower-MCT-Keto-Induction.txt) · [Choi original feasibility trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10983636/)
- In the Thai Parkinson nutrition survey, 12 of 70 participants reported daily olive oil and 45 reported constipation. The published table gives separate prevalences by weight category. The study supplies neither a constipation contrast by oil consumption nor a known oil grade. [Thai primary survey](Source-Text/PMC13435194-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)
- The Society Islands interview team documented sweet basil in childhood restlessness accounts and coconut oil in customary external use. The Yoruba inventory combines published sources and author experience, with food and medicinal uses documented at plant-part level. Across these sources and the earlier Sri Lankan interviews, ingredient identity, route, interview design and measured outcomes are separate questions. [Society Islands interviews](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2023-PMID37853377-Society-Islands-Child-Care.txt) · [Yoruba inventory](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Local-Knowledge/2021-PMID35024488-Ajao-Yoruba-Plant-Uses.txt) · [Sri Lankan interviews](../2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2021-PMID34257686-Sri-Lankan-Cosmetic-Plants.txt)
- The 1871 Georgia archive preserves historical reports of cinnamon, anise, barley and olive oil in several distinct contexts. The NLM record exposes 38 scanned pages. The seven mapped checks remain open while the two missing printed labels are unverified. [Dated archival scope](../1870-1874/Source-Text/PMC8893456-Round1-Scanned-Scope.txt)
- The newer diet review repeats a banana-juice finding through a pharmacokinetic review. The primary experiment studied levodopa exposure in rat groups and supplied no dyskinesia estimate. The separate Parkinson letter remains an original-access lead, with other reports describing the same clinical account.
