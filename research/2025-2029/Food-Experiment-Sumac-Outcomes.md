# Ground sumac and symptom outcomes

## 2025 Adult allergy case after a meal containing sumac

Cuthbert, Dua and Clark reported rapid throat itching and breathing difficulty in a 36-year-old after a meal containing sumac. Skin testing was positive to sumac, and the meal was reported to contain neither cashew nor pistachio, for which the patient had a known allergy. The poster presents a probable food allergy after a mixed meal. It reports no visual hallucinations, speech intelligibility assessment, Parkinson disease or separate culinary dose comparison. Its priority claim needs to be read alongside the earlier pediatric report, including exercise during the first episode.
