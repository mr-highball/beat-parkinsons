# Round 1 cinnamon, piperine and falls source audit

The November 24, 2026 review checked 74 exact source scopes in 60 originals. Of the 74 checks, 13 supply related context and 61 are contextual exclusions. Fifteen related assessments were added and the previously secondary piperine citation was refreshed with the full report. The twelve checked scorecards retain their verdicts.

## Black pepper and swallowing

The 2013 Rofes study used isolated piperine in matched nectar-thick boluses for 40 adults with swallowing difficulties. Each participant took the unfortified control before the piperine boluses. The higher concentration lowered mean penetration–aspiration scores from 3.25 to 1.85 (p=.003). The lower concentration changed 2.10 to 1.95 (p=.521). All four participants listed with neurodegenerative disease were in the lower group. The authors did not report a separate Parkinson estimate. The comparison leaves the effect of eating cracked pepper open because its texture, release and dose differ. Claim 0124 stays Unknown. [Original trial record](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID24326980-Oral-Piperine-and-Swallowing-Physiology.txt) · [Cochrane stroke review scope and excluded study entry](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID30376602-Stroke-Swallowing-Review-and-Piperine-Boundaries.txt)

The clinical comparison extends the earlier [aroma patch record](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Black-Pepper.md). The later uncontrolled report in Parkinson with Lewy body dementia recorded two improvements, one unchanged case and two discontinuations after inhaled black pepper oil. The oral edible-film study measured detection and temporary adaptation to isolated piperine. These reports involve distinct routes and outcomes. ## Cinnamon and orange experimental outcomes

The oral ground Ceylon cinnamon group in the 2019 MPTP mouse study is separable from the sodium-benzoate arm. The rotarod interaction p value was .021. The rearing and grooming interaction p values were .164 and .322. Figure 8 offers no separate muscle rigidity measure. Claim 0146 stays Unknown. The same article's figure labels are a lexical exclusion for figs and lower-limb pain in claim 0559. [Inspected cinnamon study and figure](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMID31119595-Cinnamon-GDNF-Motor-Extract.txt)

A 2019 Parkinson model used orange odor during a scent task and measured stool output in a separate comparison by genotype and age. A raw-orange intake effect was outside the scope of the bowel comparisons. [Orange odor and bowel study scope](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMID31139367-Orange-Odor-and-Separate-Constipation-in-Parkinson-Model.txt)

## Soybean oil and falls

The TEAM-AD Alzheimer's trial randomized medication and vitamin E regimens. Its soybean oil capsule served as the vitamin E comparator in two arms. Table 3 reports separate recurring event counts and percentages of people with an event. The authors reported no clear difference in serious falls across the four assigned regimens (p=.89). The trial has no separate preparation grade or comparison of dietary soybean oil in Parkinson. The Cochrane review citing the trial shares the original study family.
