## Peppermint oil trial and secondary Epworth sleepiness

Sinclair and colleagues published April 23, 2026. Forty adults with pre- or stage 1 hypertension were assigned peppermint oil or flavored placebo. In Table 2 placebo changed from 5.05 to 4.40, and peppermint oil from 7.15 to 6.51 on the Epworth scale. The reported baseline-adjusted peppermint-minus-placebo comparison was +0.57 points (95% CI −0.43 to 1.57, p = .257). The trial measures daytime sleepiness, with a different mint species, an extracted oil and a non-Parkinson population.
