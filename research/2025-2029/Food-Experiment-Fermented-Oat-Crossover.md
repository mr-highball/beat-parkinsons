# Oat gurt and porridge crossover

## Oat product formulation and GHQ-12 outcomes

Liikonen and colleagues analyzed 56 adults with mild metabolic deterioration in a 12-week crossover, with three-week periods of fermented gurt and nonfermented porridge. The foods combined whole-grain oat flour, oat bran concentrate and faba bean protein isolate. The July 23, 2026 IUNS presentation EPO6_170 and the later journal article describe one trial registered as NCT06393114. The journal publication history gives July 28 online, while NLM and ScienceDirect give July 29. The September issue has DOI 10.1016/j.clnu.2026.106744 and PMID 42574785. [Current registry](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Fermented-Oat-Crossover.md) · [Original conference collection](https://doi.org/10.1017/jns.2026.10126) · [Journal report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42574785/)

Supplementary Table 11 gives an adjusted GHQ-12 change of −0.97 during gurt (95% CI −1.80 to −0.14, P = .023) and −0.63 during porridge (95% CI −1.47 to 0.21, P = .144). The direct comparison between product periods was inconclusive (P = .569). The earlier conference abstract reported no significant aggregate change during gurt. The reports use different summaries of the same cohort. The questionnaire includes an item on sleep lost through worry. Neither the original article supplement nor the repository questionnaire manifest supplies a separate sleep-item estimate. The study gives a related outcome context for 0174. It supplies no estimate for plain rolled oats, a separate clinical insomnia endpoint, or a Parkinson group.
