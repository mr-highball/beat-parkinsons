# Pineapple and urinary symptom comparisons

## Grouped high-acid foods and urgency incontinence

Ha and colleagues’ 2025 report compared 651 urgency urinary incontinence cases with 8,890 controls using NHANES. High-acid foods were reported by 50.3% and 44.3%, respectively. The adjusted odds ratio was 1.29 (95% CI 1.03–1.62). The public abstract does not list the foods or give a pineapple-specific estimate. Urgency with leakage and urgency episode frequency are distinct outcomes.
