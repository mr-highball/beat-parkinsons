# Peanut oil in a scoped FDA adverse-event search

## Product-name report search and reaction coding

On November 15, 2026 the current openFDA drug-event snapshot returned 15 records for an arachis oil product-name phrase and eight for a peanut oil product-name phrase. None of those 23 returned record entries lists a hallucination term. Three arachis records list confusional state. Records include topical, mixed and nutritional products, plus a product description that mentions excluding peanut oil. Counts are report records, with possible overlap or updates. The searches do not authenticate oral cold-pressed oil exposures or estimate clinical rates. Scope is the two recorded product-name phrases, with both reported result sets examined. The phrase and field extension queried twelve combinations across product name, recorded active substance and harmonized substance name. The successful pages contained 46 appearances, reconciling to 42 report ID/version keys. The additional Arachis hypogaea oil product-name query returned a mixed zinc product record with a generic hallucination code and a separate visual impairment code. It supplies no coded visual hallucination, verified oral oil exposure, pressing grade, Parkinson status or event frequency. Four reported active-substance matches repeated product-query keys. A representative HTTP 404 gave the provider's no-matches response. The remaining 404 bodies were unavailable for independent inspection.
