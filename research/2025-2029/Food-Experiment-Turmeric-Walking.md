# Curcumin and walking, 2025–2029

## Curcumin formulation with or without resistance training

In sedentary older adults, the exercise plus curcumin arms had larger changes in six-minute walking distance than comparator groups receiving the same training. For the two arms without exercise, rounded distances declined by four meters in both groups (599 to 595 m with curcumin and 549 to 545 m with placebo). The original reports an adjusted p=.002 for that pair despite the same rounded change and a 50-meter baseline gap. This does not establish a gain from unaccompanied curcumin. The trial used a formulation in older adults outside Parkinson disease.
