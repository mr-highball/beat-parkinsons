# Oregano Anxiety

## Forehead oregano essential oil and depression scores

The report describes a before-and-after series using Origanum vulgare essential oil applied to the forehead. The paired analysis used 200 observations. The mean PHQ-9 score moved from 22.32 to 5.60 and the reported PROMIS depression score from 26.49 to 7.25. There was no concurrent control arm and the results did not give a separately measured anxiety outcome. The design cannot separate change over time from an effect of the essential oil. It provides no ingested dried-leaf or Parkinson anxiety comparison. [Original source](https://www.ijmfmap.in/view_pdf.php?id=353) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2025-Singh-Oregano-Topical-Depression-Extract.txt)

## Oregano in a five-herb animal extract

Khabaeva and colleagues reported more open-arm visits with the combined extract in rats. Open-arm duration gave an inconclusive comparison and locomotion increased. Oregano was an extract ingredient alongside four other botanicals. The study provides animal behavioral context without an isolated ingredient contrast. [Original Methods and Table 2](Source-Text/2025-Khabaeva-Mixed-Herbs-Anxiety-Assays.txt)

## Six-spice capsule and registered mental health outcome

The March 21, 2025 registry includes oregano in a six-spice capsule. SF-36 mental health is a secondary domain and the eligibility criteria exclude Parkinson disease. No results were posted at the checked update. [Official registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT06889961.txt)

## Marjoram-containing massage blend and HADS anxiety in rheumatoid arthritis

The 2026 psychological results report followed the same randomized trial as the 2023 pain and sleep paper. HADS favored the blended oil massage compared with usual care and gave no clear contrast against almond-oil massage. Almond-oil massage also favored anxiety versus usual care. The species, mixture, topical route and rheumatoid arthritis population differ from the frozen claim. [Original results and companion trial scope](Source-Text/2026-Wu-Chiou-Marjoram-Massage-HADS-Extract.txt)

## Registered topical marjoram trial with GAD-7 after stroke

The December 16, 2025 registration records a 30-person comparison of sweet marjoram in coconut carrier with carrier massage alone. GAD-7 and PHQ-9 were planned alongside upper-limb measures. The study status is completed, and the original registry supplies no outcomes. [Official registration summary](Source-Text/Trials/NCT07286825.txt)
