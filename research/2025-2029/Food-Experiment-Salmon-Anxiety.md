# Salmon and anxiety follow-up

## Lithuanian Parkinson cohort and Mediterranean diet

Guk and colleagues, 2026. Among 59 people with Parkinson's disease and 54 controls, the reported Parkinson within-group odds ratio for anxiety was 0.88 (95% CI .79–.99) per point of overall Mediterranean diet adherence. Fish was a food-frequency component. The symptom model used the overall score and gives no separate fish, species or preparation estimate. [Existing local record, PMID 41878573](Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#lithuanian-food-frequency-and-mediterranean-diet-study)

## UK student survey of diet and GAD-7

The September 1, 2026 report retained 50 students from 96 who provided responses. Its food-frequency instrument included a salmon item listed as 100 g cooked. A grilled salmon portion appeared as an illustrative item in its nutrient calculation. The anxiety regression used estimated total dietary EPA plus DHA, giving a GAD-7 slope of −0.325 points (95% CI −0.725 to 0.074, p = .108) per 100 mg per day in the linear model. It did not analyze GAD-7 against the salmon item itself. [Primary paper and local text, PMID 42739029](Source-Text/PMC13567499.txt)
