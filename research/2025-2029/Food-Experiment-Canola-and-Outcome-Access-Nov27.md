# Canola fatigue and open outcome records

The newly registered canola versus MCT crossover includes subjective mental fatigue in healthy adults. Its exact publisher record has no posted results, its recruitment fields are internally inconsistent, and oil refining is unspecified. [Official registry scope](Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000063699.txt)

The 2015 lung-cancer oil trial reports an inconclusive fatigue comparison in the presence of chemotherapy. Its preliminary poster may report the same cohort and describes a different result without a standalone estimate. This study family uses rapeseed oil with unspecified refining and gives no outcome for culinary refined canola oil in Parkinson disease. [2015 fatigue table and family](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-Andersen-Rapeseed-Fish-Oil-Chemotherapy-Fatigue.txt)

The COPD trial registry (NCT03796455) records 3 g/day of a soy–corn blend and the authentic supplement specifies 2 g/day. The gait and balance tables were not recovered. A baseline pooled analysis from that registry cannot supply the randomized stride result.
