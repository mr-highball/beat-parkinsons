# Tree nut oil follow-up studies

## Macadamia nut oil in a fibromyalgia aroma comparison

Masubuchi and colleagues first published February 21, 2025. Ten fibromyalgia patients enrolled and five were analyzed in a crossover comparing topical macadamia nut oil with the same oil plus geranium essential oil. The paper’s change-from-baseline analysis did not show a clear difference between the two treatments for state anxiety (p = .09) or trait anxiety (p = .63). The raw scores in the dissertation used a different analysis and show an opposite numerical trend in state anxiety. The paper reports a rise in diastolic pressure during macadamia-only sessions and lower heart rate after both treatments. The registry's short results summary differs on those immediate cardiovascular contrasts. Both arms received topical macadamia and the trial did not enroll people with Parkinson’s. The 2021 registry and dissertation are earlier reports from the same study family. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1177/1934578X251322308) · [Earlier registry and dissertation](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#macadamia-oil-as-a-comparator-in-the-fibromyalgia-trial-registry-and-dissertation)

## Walnut oil and a delayed multiple T maze test

Nemeth and colleagues first published July 18, 2025. Rats fed walnut oil for eight weeks and controls underwent a delayed maze retest. Finish time and errors did not clearly differ between diets at the end of training or on the later memory test. Controls worsened on the within-group comparison while the supplemented group remained stable. The paper is an informative contrast to earlier positive spatial learning studies and did not measure clinical executive function. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40684931/) · [University bibliographic record](https://ucrisportal.univie.ac.at/en/publications/effects-of-walnut-oil-on-long-term-memory-of-rats-in-a-multiple-t/)

## Oral hazelnut oil and experimental gastric lesions

Akalin and colleagues (August 2025) gavaged rats with oil from cold-pressed Corylus avellana or controls and then induced gastric injury with ethanol. The authors report smaller gross lesions and improved histopathology after hazelnut oil. The primary outcome was injury, without a nociception or clinical pain measure. A later rat surgery study used oil topically inside the abdomen and scored adhesions. Neither experiment can determine the direction of clinical pain after consuming cold-pressed oil in Parkinson's. [Primary gastric study](https://doi.org/10.56941/odutip.1737767) · [Primary surgery study](https://doi.org/10.16919/bozoktip.1733385)

## Walnut oil in a printed texture-modified food

You and colleagues (June 18, 2026) used market-sourced walnut oil in a 3D-printed hydrogel containing Fu Ling Yin Zi and a blend of xanthan gum, pectin and carrageenan. Spoon tilt, fork drip and fork pressure testing classified the selected printed formulation as IDDSI level 4. The tested characteristic is food texture. The experiment did not enroll people or measure swallowing, aspiration, drooling or oil processing. ## Topical macadamia blend in carpal tunnel syndrome

Sakai observed one person with carpal tunnel syndrome across seven sessions. The clinician applied a blend of essential oils in macadamia nut oil over the affected forearm and hand and treated the shoulder and back. Qualitative checks describe easier writing, coin pickup and clothespin use over the series. Figure 1 gives right-hand grip of 23.6 kg at the first visit and 24.4 kg at the last, an increase of 0.8 kg. The discussion gives a conflicting change of 0.9 kg. The April 10, 2025 report provides no comparator, cold-press verification or isolated effect of the carrier oil.
