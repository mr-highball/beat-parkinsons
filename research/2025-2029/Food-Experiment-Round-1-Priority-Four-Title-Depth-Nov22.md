# Round 1 priority-four title-depth review and remaining originals, November 22, 2026

This audit established exact identities and local research texts for 26 priority-four claim–source pairs across 13 frozen claims. It added 19 dated files and expanded seven earlier files. The dated notes identify the original evidence examined, what it measures, how it relates to the frozen exposure and symptom, and what remains unavailable. [Exact source checks](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/priority-four-title-depth-nov22/source-route-reviews.tsv)

Six pairs were resolved at original scope. The 1989 and 1994 Sage papers concern carbidopa and levodopa treatment. The original anaesthesia survey measures perioperative experiences. The familial exercise-induced dystonia study has its own three-page scope within a collected PDF. The oxybutynin case documents episodic sweating. Amantadine regression tables directly identify the drug and distinguish freezing of gait from any lemon-balm exposure. These are source-context decisions. The Round 1 verdicts remain unchanged.
