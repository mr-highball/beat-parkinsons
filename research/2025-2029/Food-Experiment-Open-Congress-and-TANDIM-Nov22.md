Reviewed 2026-11-22. Two authenticated original books resolved 61 exact Round 1 source reviews. The [UEG 2023 publisher version](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2023-PMC10576948-UEG-Poster-Access-Gap.txt) spans 964 pages and has 1,720 numbered entries including withdrawals. The [2011 hematology congress book](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMC3127552-Hematology-Congress-Access-Gap.txt) matches the publisher's 1,755 scientific entries on pp. 1–678. The bibliographic difference between the NLM terminal page and the publisher's complete scientific sequence remains documented.

The full [TANDIM trial](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2023-PMID37969059-Staudacher-TANDIM-IBS-Whole-Diet.txt) and its [same-cohort dietary analysis](Source-Text/2025-PMID41325894-Staudacher-TANDIM-Olive-Oil-Secondary.txt) separate whole-diet benefits from ingredient-level attribution. The 59-person IBS trial did not show a clear constipation difference. The full trial favored lower anxiety for the assigned diet, while the exploratory dietary analysis reported an olive oil association with depression response and did not identify an oil-specific anxiety association.
