# Round 1 shared article and proceedings review

The November 25 review checked original publication bodies found under several frozen food and symptom searches. The table links 56 reviewed originals and the companion 2018 food-composition measurements. Dispositions describe whether each source bears on a frozen question. They do not assign an effect to the ingredient.

The 2001 nuclear-medicine volume supplies context from 303 scanned pages. The missing end of the printed span keeps the 12 checks for that record open. The other 55 originals supplied 222 exact dispositions across 64 claims. The 66 related contexts include preparation, vehicle, ingredient composition and broader outcomes. The 156 source exclusions include labels, different species, citations and diagnostic terms.
