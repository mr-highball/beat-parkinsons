# Topical almond oil and facial appearance

## Almond oil massage before facial kinesio tape

Yuk and Lim published June 25, 2025. Ten healthy older women completed a four-week one-group comparison before and after facial taping. The methods describe topical sweet almond oil massage before tape placement. Table 2 reports a decrease in the right nasolabial wrinkle score from 1.45 to 1.25 (nominal p = .046), while the other four locations did not show a statistically clear wrinkle change. Other outcomes assess skin elasticity and appearance. There is no separate oil-only or no-oil comparison and no measured facial expression task or Parkinson cohort.
