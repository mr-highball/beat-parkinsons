# Posted results from the generalized anxiety diet program

## Public generalized anxiety diet program result posting

The ClinicalTrials.gov record for EASe-GAD posted public results April 4, 2025 for the same cohort as its 2024 journal report. The posted results give mean Beck Anxiety Inventory scores of 26.2 to 11.0 in the program group and 29.3 to 26.8 on the waitlist, with 23 measured per arm at week 12. The 2024 article reports a baseline-adjusted contrast rather than simple raw change. The program incorporated individualized dietary counseling and omega-3, and neither results source isolates toasted cumin.
