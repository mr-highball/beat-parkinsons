# Fresh-derived rosemary beverage and reported mood

## Contentedness versus plain water after a visual task

The first public eye-blink and mood report appeared as a November 20, 2025 preprint and was published on January 17, 2026. It describes a fresh-derived extract and steam hydrolat beverage in healthy adults. Participants with current depression or anxiety diagnoses were excluded. The Table 1 mean contentedness changes calculated from the rounded group means were +4.30 for rosemary and +4.12 for plain water. The paper reports paired tests within each arm and no direct inference for the between-arm change. Alertness changed +8.15 versus +3.21 and calmness −0.67 versus +2.01. These are descriptive differences from table values. Depression was outside the measured outcomes. The earlier 2023 report from the same research program quotes identical sample size, sex distribution and age statistics. The reports should not be counted as independent trials without evidence of participant independence.
