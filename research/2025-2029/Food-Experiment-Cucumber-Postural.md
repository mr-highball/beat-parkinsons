# Cucumber and postural instability

## 2025 Cucumber fruit extract in lead-exposed rats

Idemudia and Enogieru, published 2025-06-10, PMID 40493089, DOI 10.1007/s10735-025-10482-8. The accessible publisher abstract describes a 28-day study in 40 rats across five groups, using aqueous Cucumis sativus fruit extract at 500 or 1000 mg/kg with lead acetate. Authors report mitigation of lead-associated motor and cerebellar changes compared with lead exposure; the abstract does not specify the individual motor tests or administration route. This lead-toxicity model is not Parkinson disease, raw cucumber eating, or quantified human postural instability. PubMed lists no abstract; the Springer publisher abstract is accessible but the full methods are subscription restricted. No directional inference for the claim is warranted.
