# Egg, bell pepper and cauliflower studies, 2025–2029

## Green bell pepper preference in Parkinson disease

Sakamoto and colleagues reported taste preferences in 49 people with Parkinson's and 101 neurological controls on June 20, 2025. Green bell pepper received mean scores of 2.16 and 2.29 out of three respectively (p = .22). This was a questionnaire with no standardized preparation, prescribed intake, or before–after symptom outcome. [Journal version of record](https://doi.org/10.1272/jnms.JNMS.2025_92-304) · [Original Table 2](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnms/92/3/92_JNMS.2025_92-304/_pdf/-char/en#page=4)

## Paprika extract and executive tasks in college students

Wadazumi and colleagues' August 27, 2025 crossover of 21 healthy college students tested paprika xanthophyll supplementation versus a control condition without placebo. Between-condition group-by-time tests were inconclusive for Trail Making Test B (p = .877) and the two Stroop interference outcomes (p = .495 and p = .581). Within-supplement comparisons improved in two tasks. The study used a different design and younger sample than the 2022 report from the same investigator group. Neither tested grilled bell pepper or Parkinson executive dysfunction. ## Registered paprika extract and eye exercise crossover

UMIN000060772 was first disclosed June 30, 2026. It plans a placebo-controlled crossover in 20 healthy students with a four-week paprika xanthophyll capsule period and a placebo period, using Trail Making B and Stroop outcomes in an extraocular exercise protocol. Its public results fields were empty at review. The placebo and eye-exercise protocol distinguish it from the 2025 report. [Official UMIN record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000069494) · [Selected registry fields](Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000060772.txt)

## Weekly egg consumption and osteosarcopenia

Sun and colleagues studied 519 community-dwelling older adults. The original abstract associates weekly egg consumption with lower odds of osteosarcopenia (OR 0.713, 95% CI 0.564 to 0.900). Gait comparisons were reported by musculoskeletal diagnosis. The publisher supplement confirms dietary-frequency collection and gives no egg-by-gait estimate. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41379306/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2025-Sun-Egg-Osteosarcopenia-Gait-Abstract-Extract.txt)

## Egg yolk oil and hemiplegia dysarthria

Kavery and Shiyamala’s deposited abstract compares standard care with standard care plus Anda thylam, an egg-yolk-derived oil, in 50 people with hemiplegia dysarthria. It reports Robertson score improvements of 11.92 versus 2.76 points, with p < .001. The full methods, administration route and individual speech measures remain open.
