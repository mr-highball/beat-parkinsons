# Tomato preparations and cognitive outcomes, 2025–2029

## Concentrated tomato paste and executive function in MITOS

López-Solís et al. randomized 47 healthy adults aged 40–55 to an open-label crossover. Forty-two completed both three-month periods, separated by a one-month washout. The supplied paste contained tomato and salt. During the intervention, participants consumed it with meals, without further cooking or with cooking, at 0.5 g/kg body weight per day (observed mean 34.6 g/day). Each participant also completed a low-lycopene comparison diet. The trial reported a single paste comparison with both serving methods pooled. ClinicalTrials.gov prespecified executive function as a secondary outcome and listed BDNF as the primary outcome. [Primary report and Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13203555/#antioxidants-15-00644-t003) · [Local licensed report](Source-Text/PMC13203555.txt) · [Local registration fields](../2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/NCT05891977.txt)

The adjusted paste-versus-comparison difference on the Modified Wisconsin Card Sorting Test Executive Function Index was +0.7 (95% CI −1.9 to +3.4, p = 0.589, q = 0.669). Categories completed, perseverative errors and total errors also had intervals spanning zero. Mean baseline categories were 5.8 and 5.9 out of 6, which leaves little room for improvement on that measure. The adjusted differences were +7.2 (95% CI 1.4 to 12.9, q = 0.022) for attention concentration and +0.8 (95% CI 0.2 to 1.3, q = 0.027) for face–name matching. The total associative-memory score gained 1.3 points within the paste period, while its adjusted between-period difference was +1.0 (95% CI −0.1 to 2.1, q = 0.168). The study contained no diagnosed Parkinson cohort and cannot settle claim `0311`. [Primary report, executive testing and Table 3](https://doi.org/10.3390/antiox15050644)

The supplement gives an adjusted executive-index difference of +2.5 (95% CI −2.8 to +7.8) using the first period only. The one-period-participant, triglyceride and BMI sensitivity estimates also span zero, as do all three plasma-lycopene per-protocol estimates. The most restrictive per-protocol estimate is +3.11 (95% CI −1.4 to +7.6; n = 28). These tables are additional analyses of the same pooled intervention. ## Cooked tomato powder in Thai congee and cognitive processing

Wattanathorn et al. randomized healthy adults aged 60–70 to control congee or the same base with 200 or 400 mg/day of processed tomato powder, with 20 per group completing both follow-ups. The team boiled cut tomatoes for 15 minutes, filtered them and oven-dried the preparation. The base included rice flakes, maltodextrin, pea fiber and salmon across groups. These arms estimate the incremental effect of the powder in that matrix. The primary outcome was auditory event-related potential. At four weeks, mean Cz P300 latency was 355.70 ms with control, 345.25 ms with the lower dose (p = 0.036) and 333.16 ms with the higher dose (p = 0.009). At eight weeks those comparisons had p = 0.203 and 0.704, respectively. The higher-dose mean was already 315.61 ms at baseline, versus 339.09 ms for control (p = 0.054), and rose over time. The study reported selected attention and recognition-task findings. Executive function in Parkinson disease was outside the outcome and population studied. [Primary report, Methods and Tables 2–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12301000/) · [Local licensed report](Source-Text/PMC12301000.txt)

Serum alpha-synuclein was lower than control in both powder groups at four weeks and in the higher-dose group at eight weeks. The mean rose from baseline in all three groups, from 35.77 to 53.23 pg/mL in control, from 35.43 to 52.41 in the lower-dose group and from 36.89 to 48.82 in the higher-dose group. PARK7 comparisons were nonsignificant. The article's conclusion names 200 and 400 g/day, while Methods, treatment groups and key tables consistently identify 200 and 400 mg/day of powder. The official TCTR20201020003 export confirmed the eight-week evoked-potential endpoint, with a listed enrollment date of January 26, 2021 after the article’s ethics date of December 20, 2020. Its planned sample was 66, compared with the article’s 63 allowed to participate and 60 completing. The previously checked mirror listed October 20, 2020 as start and enrollment 61. October 20 is the official registration date, and the origin of the mirror's enrollment count remains unverified. ## Comparison with earlier Parkinson research

A ten-week trial in diagnosed Parkinson disease reported a favorable executive subsection of the Persian MoCA with an individualized Mediterranean diet. The trial randomized dietary patterns and published no component-specific cooked tomato estimate in the available abstract. Earlier rodent experiments tested whole tomato-enriched diets for striatal dopamine preservation or isolated lycopene for neurobehavioral effects. These exposures and endpoints cannot substitute for the cooked tomato and clinical executive impairment comparison.
