# Rosemary, saffron and odor trials, 2025-2029

## Perimenopausal fatigue after a standardized saffron extract

Sultana and colleagues reported a 12-week randomized trial of 30 mg/day standardized Safr'Inside extract or placebo in 104 perimenopausal women. Table 4 gives POMS fatigue changes of −7.6 and −1.7, respectively, with an interaction p=.0003. Depression findings differed between intention-to-treat and per-protocol analyses. Registration timing and item estimates from the per-protocol analysis remain unresolved. This preprint addresses an extract and perimenopausal symptoms, with no Parkinson fatigue estimate. ## Rosemary mood review and uncertainty in pooled estimates

Aucoin and colleagues report 14 studies and 865 participants. Nine studies used inhalation and five used oral preparations. The abstract reports inconclusive pooled depression, anxiety and stress estimates and high heterogeneity. The planned oral subgroup anxiety comparison favored rosemary, with a standardized mean difference of −0.61 (95% CI −0.99 to −0.23). Anxiety is a separate endpoint. An earlier poster by the same team gives a pooled depression effect of −0.29 (95% CI −1.01 to 0.42, I²=85%, five studies). The conference plot’s week-eight result for Azizi includes a substantial baseline imbalance in the original trial. The final journal study table and forest plot remain open.
