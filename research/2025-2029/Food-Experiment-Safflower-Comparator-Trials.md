# Oral safflower oil comparator trials in 2025–2029

## Baylor professional voice user trial registration

NCT07800702 first posted on 2 September 2026. The Baylor protocol plans 68 women using their voice professionally, with 10 weeks of 3 g/day NOW-brand oral safflower oil or an omega-3 supplement. Primary listings cover vocal aerodynamics and phonation threshold pressure during repeated soft syllables. Secondary measures include self-ratings of vocal effort, tiredness, weakness and discomfort after vocal loading. The retrieved record lists not yet recruiting and has no results. No product code, extraction process, Parkinson population, dedicated intelligibility measure or swallowing measure is specified. The protocol follows related but separate Baylor singer cohorts. The repeated /pa/ outcome measures phonation threshold pressure and does not score what a listener understands.
