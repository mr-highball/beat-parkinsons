# Celery studies and hallucination outcome limits

## Standardized seed extract and recorded adverse events

The 2026 trial assigned standardized celery seed extract or placebo in metabolic syndrome. Its diaries and follow-up interviews yielded mild events in both groups. The reported categories include abdominal symptoms and headache, with no hallucination-specific ascertainment. The original paper identifies the 2023 registry. [Original result extract](Source-Text/2026-Escobedo-Gutierrez-Celery-Seed-Safety.txt) · [Dated registration](../2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/NCT06061926.txt)

## Retraction of the celery-containing dyspepsia trial

The publisher retracted the 2023 Persian-medicine dyspepsia report on April 4, 2025. The active formulation combined celery and ajwain. Its bibliographic appearance is retained for search reconciliation. Exclude its efficacy and safety claims from the evidence synthesis.
