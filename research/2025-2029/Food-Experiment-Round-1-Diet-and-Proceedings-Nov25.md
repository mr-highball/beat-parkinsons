# Round 1 diet, proceedings and coconut-oil citation trails

- The 1938 English journal digest describes rapid tremor and subjectively less fatigue during excessive coffee use. It cites a 1937 French report with a confirmed author and page trail. The French original remains an access target for the number of cases, clinical diagnoses and observations after withdrawal. The 1993 pharmacology archive independently illustrates a food oil used as a drug vehicle. The 1996 Lewy-body case series records hallucinations and motor symptoms without a culinary comparison. [Coffee and case-report lineage](../1935-1939/Source-Text/1937-Privat-de-Fortunie-Belfils-Caffeine-Intoxication-Trail.txt) · [1993 proceedings](../1990-1994/Source-Text/PMC1364582-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [1996 cases](../1995-1999/Source-Text/PMC486366-Round1-Original-Scope.txt)
- The 2025 nutraceutical review cites a zebrafish toxicology study for its black-tea extract claim. The primary reports ovarian mitochondrial transcripts after bisphenol exposure, with an unspecified commercial tea-polyphenol preparation. It supplies no black-tea movement or tyrosine hydroxylase estimate. [Review](Source-Text/PMC12248533-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Original](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-DOI915415-Dai-Tea-Polyphenols-Zebrafish-Tissue.txt)
- The 2026 exercise review cites a college-student trial of green-tea extract for fatigue resistance. The primary records a sprint-derived fatigue index rather than separately reported symptoms. The longer extract group shows higher indices after its training period. That comparison neither reproduces nor overturns the brewed-green-tea Parkinson fatigue observation. [Review](Source-Text/PMC12969489-Round1-Original-Scope.txt) · [Original](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-DOI5385-Mao-Green-Tea-Sprint-Fatigue.txt)
- Kerala practitioners reportedly used Karkataka Taila for tremor and palsy. The 2022 preliminary fieldwork and 2024 follow-up repeat the same combined-preparation context. The earlier paper reports adverse preclinical viability findings at higher tested exposures, and the later work reports favorable broad animal outcomes. The 2024 Changdar meeting poster separately reports virgin coconut oil in a mouse model, with motor tasks but without sleepiness or executive outcomes. The 2026 Urukku Velichenna report concerns a distinct hot-processed oil and gives broad preclinical findings. - The prospective registry list in the dietary-interventions overview leads to published KIM studies. The clinical trial, the bowel and microbiome report, and the public data belong to the same registered crossover. Both diets were multicomponent, so related reports do not establish an isolated effect of olive oil.
