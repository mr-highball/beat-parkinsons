# Potato sleep research and clove odor testing, 2025–2029

## Genetically proxied potato preference and insomnia

The primary Mendelian-randomization report gives an insomnia odds ratio of 1.17 (95% CI 1.10–1.25) per genetically predicted potato-preference contrast. Supplement S6 identifies two variants for the pair, with 159,579 in the preference GWAS and 453,379 in the insomnia GWAS. S7a shows inverse-variance-weighted beta .159967 (SE .032580) and gives no potato-specific Egger or weighted-median estimate. S7b gives FDR-adjusted p=.0000811. The exposure is a preference instrument with possible alternate pathways to sleep. Neither preparation nor actual serving size was observed. The outcome does not isolate clinical insomnia severity in Parkinson disease. ## Eugenol as an odor stimulus in mixture detection

Drnovšek and colleagues presented eugenol as an inhaled clove-like stimulus during a human odor-detection experiment. The results discuss detection performance approaching or falling below chance in odor mixtures. There was no ground-clove consumption or measurement of participant falls.
