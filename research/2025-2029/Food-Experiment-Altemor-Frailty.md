# Altemor trial outcomes in older adults

## Composite fall-risk and cognition results

Giovannini and colleagues first published on July 22, 2025. The primary report describes a 36-person randomized pilot of two Altemor dose sequences and an untreated comparison. The author abstract reports differences relative to control in fall-risk scores and cognition and an inconclusive dose comparison. The Silver Index is a fall-risk screen. The accessible report supplies no fall-event or fatigue estimate, and the outcome tables remain unavailable. The cohort was older adults. The manufacturer's current label does not verify the trial lot.
