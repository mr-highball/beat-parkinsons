# Cinnamon and sesame oil evidence

## Cinnamon flavored products and oral contact reactions

Kalogirou and colleagues first published in 2025. They reviewed 74 cases after cinnamon-containing gum or candies. Buccal mucosa was involved in 67 cases and lateral tongue in 50. Lesions resolved within one week of discontinuation in the included cases. An earlier case report associated similar contact allergy with food containing cinnamon. These reports capture local irritation or hypersensitivity and provide no acoustic or listener-rated speech, vocal volume or facial mobility comparison in Parkinson disease. [2025 original report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40914980/) · [2014 primary case](https://forumpediatrow.pl/files/article/609.pdf)

## Cinnamon-containing Kampo formula and apathy in a randomized comparison

Tamura and colleagues first published December 4, 2025. The open-label trial compared TJ-108 with control in Alzheimer or Lewy body dementia, with 24 and 25 participants in the efficacy groups. The primary appetite comparison was inconclusive. The NPI apathy domain declined within the active arm, while the between-group apathy changes were inconclusive. The earlier uncontrolled 2017 study had reported improvement from baseline. The randomized comparison estimates the formula effect and gives no separate cinnamon or Parkinson result. [Original randomized report](https://doi.org/10.1111/psyg.70120) · [Manufacturer formulation](https://www.tsumura.co.jp/english/products/pi/JPR_T108.pdf) · [2017 pilot](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Sesame.md#cinnamon-containing-kampo-formula-and-apathy-in-an-open-pilot)

## Cinnamon-containing formula and oral function

Ngo and colleagues first published December 26, 2025. Eleven older adults with oral frailty received Ninjin’yoeito, a 12-component extract including cassia bark. The clinician-rated oral composite includes reduced facial expression and speech problems. Its mean score fell from 5.09 to 1.36 after 12 weeks (p = .006). The article reports the combined score and the supplement provides an example questionnaire. There are no separate changes for speech or facial expression, acoustic voice amplitude, parallel comparator, or ground cinnamon exposure. [Local CC BY study, Methods and Results](Source-Text/PMC12843259.txt#L108) · [Publisher supplements](https://www.mdpi.com/article/10.3390/medicina62010048/s1) · [Earlier registration](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Sesame.md#registration-of-a-cinnamon-containing-formula-for-oral-frailty)

## Intranasal sesame oil and measured nasal outcomes

Published online July 26, 2025. Healthy young adults were randomized to sesame oil, medicated nasal oil or no nasal intervention (102 allocated and 99 completed). Nasal barrier biomarkers, respiratory symptoms, stress and sleep were assessed. Olfaction appears in background and discussion, with no verified measured threshold, discrimination or identification result. The protocol identifies CTRI/2022/06/043096. Nasal application does not test eating cold-pressed oil. [Original intranasal sesame trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40716461/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2025-Chaturvedi-Intranasal-Sesame-Scope-Extract.txt)
