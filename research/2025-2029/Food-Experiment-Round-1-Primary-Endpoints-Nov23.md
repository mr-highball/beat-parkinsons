# Round 1 primary endpoints and study independence

The November 23 review authenticates 16 original articles and resolves 39 exact checks with ten related findings linked to their claims. Pooled beverage caffeine, phytate-containing millet, and corn oil used as a medication carrier have different roles from the requested culinary exposures. Several clinical records address pain or motor symptoms in the context of different interventions. ## Turmeric motor outcomes and clinical transfer

Ramkumar's original distinguishes 36 rats in a preliminary dose screen from 24 rats in the follow-up comparison. The rotenone dose reported in the methods was 2.5 mg/kg/day. The oil volume belongs to the carrier control. Isolated oral demethoxycurcumin improved timed balance-beam passage, rod retention and hanging. None of those comparisons isolates passive rigidity or independently assigns edible sunflower oil. The revised family records preserve the actual movement measures. [Dated original scope](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID29576695-Ramkumar-Demethoxycurcumin-Rotenone-Rat-PD.txt)

Separate rodent studies give favorable open-field speed or pole-timing comparisons after isolated curcumin. In older adults outside Parkinson disease, the posted 400-m walking gains were 0.014 m/s in nine curcumin recipients and 0.103 m/s in eight placebo recipients, an observed difference of 0.089 m/s favoring placebo with no submitted between-arm analysis. The Schmitz test measured successful pellet reaches and reports incompatible sample descriptions. Successful reach counts, timed locomotion and clinician-assessed bradykinesia remain distinct outcomes. [Favorable original speed comparison](../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC10303117.txt#L148) · [Official gait results extract](../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT03085680.txt) · [Contrary reach experiment](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-Schmitz-Curcumin-Skilled-Reach-Extract.txt)

The Ghodsi randomized nanomicelle trial and Donadio observational phospholipid cohort use different preparations and clinical designs. The reported motor composites give no isolated bradykinesia result. The Round 1 culinary ground-turmeric claim 0507 retains a minimal easing signal under the scorecard and remains provisional. ## Cinnamon and pooled caffeine

Isolated cinnamaldehyde affected cell and neuronal readouts in experimental Parkinson models. Rigidity and dyskinesia appear in the article's context without a symptom-specific comparison. The adolescent caffeine survey combined coffee, green tea and other products into total intake and assessed insomnia. It does not isolate either beverage's effect on Parkinson fatigue.
