## Observations and gaps worth following

- **Olive oil and coconut oil.** The 2026 review describes an association with slower Parkinson progression. Its cited original is a baseline cross-sectional analysis in 1,053 people with self-reported idiopathic Parkinson disease. Oil frequency had a favorable association with aggregate patient-reported severity. Follow-up change and individual symptom estimates by oil preparation remain open. [2026 review scope](Source-Text/2026-PMID42646502-Parkinson-Diet-Review-Olive-and-Coconut.txt) · [2017 primary study](../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC5610862.txt)
- **Sesame oil.** The original Parkinson cannabidiol study measured symptoms and adverse events with sesame oil in the formulation. It had no oil-only comparator. Separate topical trials report pain changes after limb trauma. The 2015 trial adds a routine-care comparison and an unresolved attrition-accounting discrepancy. The 2017 trial used a paraffin control, with oil processing still unverified at abstract depth. Neither trial supplies an oral toasted sesame oil comparison in Parkinson disease. [Cannabidiol carrier](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID33381646-Parkinson-Cannabidiol-Sesame-Oil-Carrier.txt) · [2015 trauma trial](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-PMID26161326-Topical-Sesame-Oil-Trauma-Pain.txt) · [2017 original-access note](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-Sesame-Trauma-Pain-Extract.txt)
- **Cinnamon and orange.** A Parkinson diagnostic paper reports odor identification, including difficulty with cinnamon and recognition of orange. Identification performance gives no change estimate after eating cinnamon or orange. [Diagnostic study](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28776303-Cinnamon-Orange-Odor-Testing.txt)
- **Pine bark review.** Two separate trial members account for several ingredient matches. A diabetic eye-health study used a multi-ingredient supplement and canola oil as the control, while an erectile-dysfunction study included saffron in a combination supplement. Kale was a shared dietary restriction in the eye study. The original tables preserve their populations, exposures and outcomes. [Cochrane review scope](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32990945-Pine-Bark-Review-Food-and-Control-Scope.txt)
- **Rosemary, basil, sage and ginger.** Rosemary essential oil was inhaled in the cited behavioral model. Basil occurs as a botanical source of linalool. The two sage species and shell ginger discussed in the review differ from the frozen culinary herbs. [Source scope](../2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-PMID34512404-Pinene-Linalool-and-Culinary-Herb-Scope.txt)
