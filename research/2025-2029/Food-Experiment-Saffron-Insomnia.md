# Saffron extract insomnia and sleep comparisons

## SafrInside extract and Athens insomnia scores

The July 2025 randomized trial assigned 165 adults with moderate insomnia to two Safr’Inside extract groups or placebo. At four weeks 150 had completed follow-up. The combined extract-group contrast favored lower Athens Insomnia Scale scores, with an adjusted mean difference of −0.95 (95% CI −1.79 to −0.11). The individual dose groups had inconclusive multiplicity-adjusted comparisons. The preprint describes the same trial and does not add an independent sample. [Published trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12281372/) · [Local article](Source-Text/PMC12281372.txt) · [Earlier preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.05.07.25326999)

## Affron sleep disturbance and daytime impairment

In 2026 Lopresti and Smith reported 86 women aged 50–70 with low mood and poor sleep randomized to Affron or placebo for 12 weeks. The secondary PROMIS Sleep Disturbance contrast was 0.38 (95% CI −2.40 to 3.16, p = .786). Sleep-related impairment favored extract by 2.99 points as reported in the favorable direction (95% CI 0.12 to 5.86, p = .041). Depression was the primary endpoint. Table 2 gives a 2.37-point greater improvement on DASS depression with 28 mg/day of standardized extract (95% CI 0.23 to 4.51; p=.030). The extract manufacturer funded and helped design the study. The study compared extract and placebo outside Parkinson disease and did not use an insomnia severity scale. [Original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13318664/) · [Local article](Source-Text/PMC13318664.txt)

The existing Parkinson powder trial favored saffron on overall PSQI. The powder preparation and Parkinson population make it relevant to symptom transfer, while an isolated insomnia result remains unavailable. [Parkinson powder trial record](Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#saffron-powder-and-fatigue)
