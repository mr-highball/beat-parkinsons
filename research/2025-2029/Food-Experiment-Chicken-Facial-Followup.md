# Chicken and Parkinson outcomes

## 2025 Chicken avoidance and composite symptom score

Mischley and Murawska, 2025, PMID 40732955, PMCID PMC12299618, DOI 10.3390/nu17142330, Table 4, report an adjusted baseline PRO-PD total-score coefficient of -64.326 (SE 32.791, p=0.05) for reporting chicken less than one serving per month. The comparison uses different food coding and a later overlapping MVP cohort than the 2017 frequency analysis; it does not identify poaching or isolate a facial symptom. Table 4 labels N=2788, while the abstract describes 2290 individuals. Table 5 evaluates repeated scores against whole PRO-21 diet adherence, not chicken-specific change. The baseline association cannot establish a food-specific progression rate or causation.
