# Observations for 1890-1894

## Major observations

- The Blocq and Marinesco lesion case gave clinicians an anatomic lead for unilateral parkinsonism. It did not establish that idiopathic disease arose from the same pathology. [Mechanisms](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md)

## Minor observations

- Smith described pain or cramps before obvious tremor. Charcot’s vibrating-chair experiment pursued short-term relief and comfort. [Clinical sources](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md) · [Devices](../Procedures-and-Devices.md)

## Comparison with 1885–1889

- Gowers’s clinical account provided a pattern for recognizing one-sided signs. The later lesion report added a potential anatomical explanation for one secondary presentation. [Prior observations](../../1885-1889/Observations/README.md)

## Collection gaps

- Compare Charcot’s published account against surviving device and patient records. Dietary effects remain unverified. [Science Museum Group vibrating platform](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co142797/vibrating-stool-paris-france-1890-1914-vibrating-stool)


- A contemporary historical article places blackberry jam in home treatment of sore throats. Its separate account of bramble root pertains to a different preparation, and there is no patient outcome. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Blackberry-Voice.md)
