# Heravi translation on fresh coriander juice

The 1893 witness and the 1993 encyclopedia entry share the Heravi attribution. Fresh juice is explicit. Whole-leaf preparation and the modern clinical meaning of the historical symptom terms remain open. This source expands the dated history of the account while keeping both records within one lineage.
