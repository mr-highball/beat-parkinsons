# Mechanisms and models, 1890-1894

### 1893 — A focal midbrain lesion accompanies opposite-side parkinsonian tremor

Paul Blocq and Georges Marinesco described unilateral parkinsonian tremor with a focal tuberculous mass in the opposite cerebral peduncle involving the substantia nigra at autopsy. This lesion case established a localization lead for a secondary parkinsonian presentation. It could only suggest the site of pathology in idiopathic disease.

[Blocq and Marinesco, Comptes rendus de la Société de biologie, Mémoires, pp. 105–111, scan](https://archive.org/details/comptesrendusdes1893soci)
