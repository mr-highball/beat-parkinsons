# Procedures and devices, 1890-1894

### 1892 — Charcot experiments with therapeutic vibration

Charcot published an account of continuous vibration for neurologic symptoms, including a chair intended to mimic shaking during travel. He described perceived comfort or symptom relief. The source is a historical clinical account with no controlled comparator or durable outcome measurement.

[Charcot, La médecine vibratoire, Le Progrès Médical, pp. 149–151, scan](https://archive.org/details/BIUSante_90170x1892x02x16/page/149/mode/2up)
