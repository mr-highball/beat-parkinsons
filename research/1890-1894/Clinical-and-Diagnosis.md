# Clinical findings and diagnosis, 1890-1894

### 1893 — Smith describes the course of tremor and pain in a clinical lecture

Allen J. Smith summarized presentations in which aches or cramps preceded obvious tremor and tremor began in one extremity before spreading. He recorded contemporary speculation about cold, damp, and emotional shocks as causes. A clinical lecture and patient histories cannot establish these exposures as risk factors.
