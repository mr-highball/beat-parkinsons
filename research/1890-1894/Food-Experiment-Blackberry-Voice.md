# Blackberry and voice outcomes

## Historical account of blackberry jam and sore throats

William T. Fernie's account published on 3 May 1890 describes blackberry jam as a household remedy for sore throats in England. His discussion of a root decoction for whooping cough concerns a separate preparation. This is a record of customary use without patient follow-up, treatment comparisons, voice measurements or freezing information. The reference to a voice in that paragraph concerns the bittern, in a discussion of a name for the fruit.
