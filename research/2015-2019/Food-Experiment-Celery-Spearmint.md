# Food experiment evidence for celery leaf and spearmint

## Spearmint extract and sleep in healthy active adults

Falcone and colleagues first published the trial online December 7, 2018. It randomized 142 healthy, active adults to concentrated aqueous spearmint extract or placebo for 90 days. The full main article reports no significant Leeds Sleep Evaluation Questionnaire or Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index comparisons. Supplement Tables S1–S2 were unavailable in the accessible copy. This is a separate study from the older adult trial that found improved sleep initiation. The NCT03567343 protocol also describes a six-person antecedent crossover with a favorable one-week latency comparison and an inconclusive two-week comparison (p = .107). Its original conference abstract remains unlocated. The protocol's later study used a blend with green tea. [Falcone original](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30802720/) · [Author article copy](https://magistralbr.caldic.com/storage/product-files/476461568.pdf) · [Falcone registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02518165) · [Arizona protocol, page 3](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/43/NCT03567343/Prot_000.pdf)

## 2015 Celery leaf extract and blood pressure in rats

Dianat and colleagues used a hydroalcoholic celery leaf extract in rats with fructose-induced hypertension and reported decreases in blood pressure relative to untreated hypertensive animals. It is a plant-part-specific cardiovascular lead for 0578. Resting animal pressure and orthostatic symptoms in Parkinson’s are different outcomes. [Primary report, PMID 26101753](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26101753/)

## 2015 Spearmint gum compared with gum base for alertness

Allen and Smith compared spearmint gum, gum base and no chewing in their first experiment. Gum chewing increased reported alertness without a detected moderating effect of flavor. In their workday studies, the fatigue finding appeared once and did not replicate within participants. The experiments studied commercial gum and chewing in participants without Parkinson’s. The findings give a comparator for the fatigue and daytime sleepiness claims 0518 and 0373. [Primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4449949/) · [Local full text](Source-Text/PMC4449949.txt)

## 2016 Spearmint and peppermint oils in a fibrillation assay

Figure 1 of Morshedi and Nasouti identifies spearmint oil as sample 7 and peppermint oil as sample 8. Both were applied directly to purified α-synuclein. The paper's prominently reported fibrillation increases and decreases refer to other tested oils. This provides species-resolved laboratory context without human ingestion or symptom measurement. [Primary report, Figure 1](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2016/6219249) · [Local full text](Source-Text/PMC4894988.txt) · [Earlier local assay note](Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#fennel-oil-in-laboratory-and-animal-models)

## 2017 Celery extract and behavioral memory tests

Boonruamkaew and colleagues reported that orally administered *Apium graveolens* extract improved spatial and non-spatial memory outcomes in healthy mice after 30 days. The experiment used memory tasks, concentrated extract and a healthy mouse model. It supplies a related cognition lead for 0164, which specifies dried celery leaf and executive function in Parkinson’s. [Primary abstract, PMID 28205135](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28205135/)

## 2018 Whole dried celery extract and motor behavior in Parkinson-like mice

Chonpathompikunlert and colleagues authenticated the whole celery plant and extracted the dried plant with 70% methanol. In groups of eight mice exposed to MPTP, oral extract at 250 and 375 mg/kg reduced beam-crossing latency and slip counts at days 3 and 7 and reduced grid-walk foot faults versus vehicle. These doses also increased forepaw touches in a drag task. These experimental motor measures are related to claims 0275, 0716, 0800 and 0941. The study did not separately measure dystonia. The study does not report human bradykinesia severity, freezing frequency, stride length, or an isolated fresh-leaf treatment. [Primary study, Methods and Figures 1–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5859653/) · [Local full text](Source-Text/PMC5859653.txt)

## 2018 Celery leaf extract and evoked pain in animals

Bouazza and colleagues reported lower chemically induced writhing and changes in heat-evoked responses after hydroalcoholic celery leaf extract. The paper concerns experimental nociception and a solvent extract. It is an ingredient-part lead for 0672, with no fresh-leaf ingestion or clinical Parkinson pain assessment. [Primary article DOI](https://doi.org/10.3166/phyto-2018-0039) · [Author article copy](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/326164452_Anti-inflammatory_Analgesic_and_Antioxidant_Activities_of_the_Hydroalcoholic_Extract_from_Celery_Apium_graveolens_Leaves)

## 2018 Aqueous spearmint extract and drug-induced sleep in rats

Caro and colleagues gave aqueous *Mentha spicata* extract orally to rats and then measured pentobarbital-induced sleep. Figure 3 shows significantly longer induced sleep, from 42.13 to 73.64 minutes, without a significant onset effect. A separate acute toxicity observation found no overt behavioral changes during the first 24 hours. This supplies preparation-specific sedation context for 0140 and 0373. The outcome is induced sleep in rats, with no clinical insomnia or waking sleepiness measure. [Primary report, Figure 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6106973/) · [Local full text](Source-Text/PMC6106973.txt)

## 2018 Spearmint extract, sleep initiation and mood in adults

Herrlinger and colleagues randomized 90 adults with age-associated memory impairment to two doses of dried aqueous spearmint extract or placebo for 90 days. At 900 mg/day the self-rated ability to fall asleep improved relative to placebo (p = .0046). The paper found no significant pairwise placebo differences in individual mood outcomes. This provides concentrated extract context for 0140, 0518 and 0923. The outcome does not establish insomnia severity after dried culinary leaves or mood change after fresh leaves in Parkinson’s. [Primary report, Tables 4–5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5779242/)

## 2018 Spearmint extract and reactive agility in healthy adults

Falcone and colleagues randomized 142 active adults to a standardized spearmint extract or placebo. The extract improved selected stationary and multidirectional reactive-agility scores, with other tests showing no significant differences. The tasks scored sensor responses rather than incident falls or a clinical hand-dexterity scale. This is a movement-task lead for 0494 and 0652, with no fresh or dried leaf preparation and no Parkinson sample. [Primary report, Figures 3–4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6291964/) · [Local full text](Source-Text/PMC6291964.txt)

## 2018 Registration of a spearmint and green tea sleep trial

NCT03567343 was first posted June 25, 2018. The registry describes a water-extracted spearmint and green tea blend compared with microcrystalline cellulose for 30 days and lists sleep diary, tracker, mood and cognitive measures. Its later posted results and the 2021 paper give different statistical contrasts on the same study. ## 2019 Supermint drops and pain following cholecystectomy

Nematshahi, Asadi and Tajabadi describe a separate double-blind study of Supermint drops in 60 elective cholecystectomy participants and placebo in 47. Table 1 reports no significant pre-intervention or 20-minute pain difference. The figure and discussion favor mint at later times, while the Persian abstract's results sentence says placebo did better. The study's direction is recorded with this internal discrepancy. The journal issue dates to autumn 1398 (2019), while an external metadata listing displays February 2020. The product's study-lot species is unconfirmed. [Primary report, Table 1 and line graph](https://jmp.ir/files/site1/user_files_8d9738/a9151737407-A-10-1813-2-fa5ae28.pdf)

## Dried mixed herbs containing celery and dizziness in hypertension

Triyono and colleagues reported an open randomized comparison in April 2018, with 60 adults who had stage I hypertension. The two arms received a boiled or steeped version of the same six-herb preparation. The materials were oven-dried at 50 °C, and the authors identify whole celery herb with five other plants. Figure 4 places disappearance of the combined dizziness/headache symptom at 27 days for boiling and 21 days for steeping. The comparison gives neither a celery-free arm nor an isolated standing dizziness measure. [Original Methods and Figure 4](https://journalpharm.univpancasila.ac.id/index.php/jifi/en/article/download/425/347/959) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2018-Triyono-Celery-Mixture-Extract.txt)

## Registered celery seed extract and blood pressure

IRCT20130418013058N8 first registered April 22, 2018. The record describes a comparison of celery seed extract and placebo and lists blood pressure and collection of possible side effects. The retrieved fields do not specify standing dizziness or the Beck anxiety inventory. Later reporting gives a dizziness item and the registry number. ### Depression and dejection values in the original spearmint trial

Herrlinger and colleagues’ Table 4 lists placebo 1.80 to 2.10, 600 mg/day extract 1.30 to 2.11, and 900 mg/day extract 3.83 to 2.32 on the POMS depression/dejection scale over 90 days. The mixed-model treatment p-value was .0862. The two dose contrasts point in different directions and the more favorable arm started higher. Higher-dose movement cannot identify a fresh spearmint response in Parkinson disease. [Original Table 4](https://magistralbr.caldic.com/storage/product-files/2130275156.pdf)

## Rest tremor in the oral whole-celery-extract mouse study

Figure 4 of the 2018 study reports lower measured rest tremor with whole-celery methanol extract in MPTP-exposed mice. The applied severity scale scores observed behavior and the model tests neither raw celery stalk nor clinical rest tremor in Parkinson disease. A different paragraph names hallucinations among problems associated with levodopa/carbidopa. It supplies no celery hallucination case. [Original archived text and Figure 4 caption](Source-Text/PMC5859653.txt)
