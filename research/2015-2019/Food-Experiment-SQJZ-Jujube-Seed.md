# SQJZ Jujube Seed

## Jujube seed in the SQJZ protocol

The December 2017 article proposes an oral nine-component SQJZ preparation in 240 people with Parkinson disease. The planned blinded comparison specifies sachets dissolved in hot water twice daily, a two-week placebo run-in, 12 weeks of treatment and follow-up to week 24. Table 1 lists jujube seed (Semen ziziphi spinosae) at 15 g/day within the formula. The ingredient and preparation differ from dried date fruit and culinary figs. The primary nonmotor scale includes a perceptual domain. The protocol supplies no estimates for individual symptoms and gives no separately specified drooling or pain endpoint.
