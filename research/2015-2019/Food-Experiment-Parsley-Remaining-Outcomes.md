# Parsley preparation and Parkinson symptom evidence

## Parsley in a texture-adapted dysphagia recipe development programme

The authors developed 296 texture-adapted recipes and report a garlic-and-parsley chicken dish among the minced recipes. Older people with dysphagia and carers attended recipe workshops, some of which included cooking. The report does not verify each participant's consumption of that dish, parsley preparation, comparison without parsley or change in swallowing difficulty.
