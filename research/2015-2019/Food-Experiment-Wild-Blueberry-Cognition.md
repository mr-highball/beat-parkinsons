# Wild blueberry powder trial registration

## Registered BluFlow cognition and vascular outcomes

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted BluFlow on September 10, 2019. Its current record describes a randomized comparison of wild blueberry powder and placebo in healthy adults aged 65–80. It lists cognitive testing, mood and vascular measures, records actual enrollment of 53 and has no structured results. Some outcome fields changed in 2021. The linked 2023 publication describes different enrollment and analysis totals.
