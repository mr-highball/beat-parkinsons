# Pea protein breakfasts and cognitive outcome trails

## Pea protein breakfast registration and exploratory cognition

NCT03595436 first posted July 23, 2018. The completed registry reports 40 enrolled and crossover conditions of 20, 30 or 40 g pea protein, 20 g whey and an isocaloric carbohydrate control in breakfast preloads. The narrative schedules computerized memory, executive function, attention and reaction-time tests 30 minutes before and 90 and 180 minutes after breakfast. The registry gives no task names or cognitive results. The population and isolated protein preparations differ from cooked green peas in Parkinson’s.
