# Pea protein breakfasts and cognitive outcome trails

## Pea protein breakfast registration and exploratory cognition

NCT03595436 first posted July 23, 2018. The completed registry reports 40 enrolled and crossover conditions of 20, 30 or 40 g pea protein, 20 g whey and an isocaloric carbohydrate control in breakfast preloads. The narrative schedules computerized memory, executive function, attention and reaction-time tests 30 minutes before and 90 and 180 minutes after breakfast. The registry gives no task names or cognitive results. The population and isolated protein preparations differ from cooked green peas in Parkinson’s. [Official study narrative and outcomes](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03595436) · [Local text](Source-Text/NCT03595436-Pea-Protein-Exploratory-Cognition-Extract.txt)

## Buffet plate and portion memory registration

NCT03610776 first posted August 1, 2018. It compares a calibrated portion-control plate with an ordinary plate during buffet meals in a randomized crossover. It prespecifies eye tracking and meal-component portion memory, including percentage reconstruction error three hours after eating. Its later methods paper identifies boiled peas as a buffet item. The treatment assignment concerns the plate. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03610776) · [Published meal methods](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Pea-Protein-Cognition.md#boiled-peas-in-a-plate-and-portion-memory-study)
