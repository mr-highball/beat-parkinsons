# Dill tablets, dill seed and a lovage root mixture

## Oral dill tablet and sleep in premenstrual syndrome

- **2018-01. Shokrpour and colleagues.** The reported randomized study enrolled 142 participants with premenstrual syndrome, 71 per arm. The Pittsburgh sleep score at baseline was 8.95 (SD 4.5) with dill tablets and 9.1 (SD 3.3) with placebo. Table 3 lists scores of 3.7 (SD 4.1) and 8.1 (SD 3.2), respectively, after two months, with a reported between-group p value of 0.001 or lower. The table presents standard deviations before means. The methods report two dill tablets and one placebo tablet per day and leave the plant part and tablet mass unspecified. The questionnaire describes overall sleep quality in a premenstrual population. It cannot isolate insomnia or establish the effect of dried dill weed in Parkinson disease. [Original report, printed pages 65–76](https://www.atmph-specialissues.org/cms/images/Sp1.pdf).

## Boiled dill seed and postpartum anxiety

- **2019-11-09, online. Hekmatzadeh and colleagues.** In a 100-person childbirth trial, the authors compared boiled, filtered dill seed with oxytocin. At six hours after delivery they reported fewer participants in the highest anxiety categories in the dill arm. The dose was a water preparation of seed, whereas culinary dill weed refers to foliage. Labor and the comparator introduce additional differences from the frozen anxiety claim. The trial identifier IRCT201607177065N2 also appears in the companion labor report. [Original anxiety report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31705450/) · [Companion labor report](https://jccs.yums.ac.ir/files/site1/user_files_407f04/hekmatzadeh-A-10-95-1-f48d461.pdf).

## Lovage root mixture in acute cystitis

- **2015-12-24, first posted. NCT02639520.** The registered randomized phase III comparison tested BNO 1045 and fosfomycin in women with acute uncomplicated lower urinary tract infection. The main endpoint tracked additional antibiotic use. The registration and the publications concern the same cohort. [Original registry record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02639520).
- **2018-09-19, online. Wagenlehner and colleagues.** The trial randomized 659 women. The herbal preparation contained powdered lovage root, rosemary leaf and centaury herb. Of the per-protocol participants, 83.5% in the herbal arm and 89.8% in the antibiotic arm received no additional antibiotic by day 38, meeting the prespecified 15-percentage-point noninferiority margin. Acute urinary urgency contributed to the symptom assessments. The result describes a root-containing combination in infection. The [later item-level analysis](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Umbellifer-Herbs.md#lovage-root-mixture-and-urgency-severity) clarifies how the investigators assessed urgency. [Original trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6262678/).

## Coriander aerial-part extract in a Korean patent

- **2016-04-25, publication. KR20160044080A.** Kyung Hee University’s application lists Myung Sook Oh, Gunhyuk Park and 김정희 as inventors. Example 1 describes an ethanol extract of coriander aerial parts, concentrated and dried after extraction. Experimental Example 5 and Figure 8 describe shorter pole turn and descent times and longer rotarod retention with oral extract in MPTP-treated mice. The intervention schedule, group means and error bars match the motor results in Park’s [2014 dissertation](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Umbellifer-Herbs.md#coriander-aerial-part-extract-and-motor-performance-in-a-korean-dissertation). The patent also plots the ropinirole comparison. KR20170115472A (2017-10-17) and its grant KR101867238B1 (2018-06-14) repeat the disclosure and motor figure. These records represent one experiment. Oh later supervised the distinct [Lee et al. study](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Chives-Cilantro-Curry-Leaf.md#cilantro-leaf-extract-in-two-mouse-models) in the same research program. The separate 2021 Koppula study tested coriander fruit.
