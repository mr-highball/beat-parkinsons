# Flaxseed oil and pain, 2015–2019

## Flaxseed oil in a single-blind osteoarthritis comparison

Vyas and colleagues reported a 35-per-arm six-week trial of flaxseed oil capsules versus oral liquid paraffin with prescribed anti-inflammatory medication allowed. Mean 0–3 pain at rest changed from 1.60 to 0.55 with oil and 1.63 to 1.70 with placebo. Pain on movement changed from 1.71 to 0.80 and 1.60 to 1.80, respectively. The report gives discrepant dosing schedules in Methods and Discussion. The preparations differed in fill description and pressing grade was unspecified. The authors reported similar methods in a later rheumatoid arthritis paper, with independence of enrollment unverified.
