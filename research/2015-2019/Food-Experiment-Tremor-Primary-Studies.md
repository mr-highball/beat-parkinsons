# Parkinson motor scores, tremor subtype and culinary exposure

## Grouped poultry and fruit intake in a Parkinson survey

The 96-person survey found an inconclusive difference in total UPDRS III for daily combined meat, fish and poultry intake. Its corresponding comparison by tremor or rigidity motor subtype also was inconclusive. The fruit-or-vegetable tables report slightly different group sizes across the descriptive and outcome rows. Participants who ate fewer dairy products were more frequently classified as tremor dominant. The study is cross-sectional and gives no separately assessed rest-tremor severity for baked turkey or any individual fruit or vegetable. ## Butylphthalide and total UPDRS in Parkinson disease dementia

Wang and colleagues report 12-week total UPDRS of 20.28 after butylphthalide and 32.68 with donepezil in people with Parkinson disease dementia. The article gives no separately reported rest-tremor measure. This comparison of pharmaceuticals is indirect evidence for the celery question. The later Zhou trial reports a pooled tremor subtotal with unresolved table inconsistencies.
