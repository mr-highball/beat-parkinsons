# Canola oil and swallowing context, 2015–2019

## Mixed edible-oil formulation in a swallowing anecdote

A July 2015 patent application lists an oral blend containing canola alongside other oils, water, sugar and a thickener. A head or neck cancer patient described the finished blend as easy to swallow. The example gives no swallowing scale, comparison or cold-pressed specification. The product texture and mixed ingredients prevent attribution to canola oil.
