# Shiso and voice follow-up

## 2015 Hange-koboku-to and postoperative hoarseness

UMIN000018516 was first publicly disclosed on August 1, 2015. The completed randomized trial planned 120 women aged 20–75 undergoing tracheal intubation and compared preoperative Hange-koboku-to with a no-formula control. The register's design fields call the study double-blind and placebo-controlled, whereas the intervention description says the control did not take the formula. Its main results say there was no improvement in postoperative nausea or sore throat; an additional results field says hoarseness and itching did not differ. The record gives no actual enrollment, hoarseness counts, effect estimate or measurement instrument, and labels results unpublished. Hange-koboku-to is a multi-herb preparation; a 2011 Tsumura product label lists perilla herb in a dried extract with four other crude drugs, but the UMIN record does not identify a brand or batch. The study is not specific to Parkinson disease, dried culinary shiso, or voice loudness, and hoarseness is not a loudness measurement.
