# Dried dill leaf and gastrointestinal study registration

## Dill powder in the Iranian gastrointestinal trial registration

The Iranian registry first posted IRCT20120704010181N12 on May 12, 2018. The study planned 48 adults with type 2 diabetes in a blinded comparison of dill powder capsules with placebo for eight weeks. Its registered outcomes include gastrointestinal symptoms and metabolic and inflammatory laboratory measurements. The paper links to a legacy numeric path. Searching the exact registration identifier in the current registry located project 29037, with the matching title and full record. The same number from the legacy URL leads to a different registration in the current site's routing format.
