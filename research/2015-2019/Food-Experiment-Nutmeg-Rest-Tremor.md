# Nutmeg reports and tremor phenotyping

## Intention tremor after powdered nutmeg exposure

October 2018. Serrano Benavente, Pérez Ruiz and Ruiz Sánchez describe an adult presenting after intentional excess powdered nutmeg. Examination recorded intention tremor of the hands alongside other acute findings. The report follows recovery during observation. It gives neither a Parkinson diagnosis nor a quantified rest-tremor observation. The case has no comparison group and the route and severity limits keep the culinary hypothesis open. ## Marseille poison-center signs and background animal tremor

September 19, 2019. Of 31 Marseille poison-center exposure reports, 22 had sufficient data for analysis. Eleven were intentional and eleven unintentional. Powder predominated, with some whole seed. Table I groups neurological and psychiatric signs and does not enumerate individual tremor findings. Tremor in the Discussion refers to earlier animal literature. The body supplies case-provenance and symptom-reporting context, with no Parkinson rest-tremor comparison.
