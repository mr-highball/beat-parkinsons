# SHAPE Sleep Study

## Registered SHAPE sleep comparisons

First posted July 27, 2018. The University of Arkansas SHAPE trial randomized postmenopausal women among five diet groups for 16 weeks. It registers Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index change as the primary sleep endpoint and wrist-actigraphy sleep duration as a secondary outcome. The registry describes an arm receiving whey protein and a placebo fat source. Its arm description leaves the oil identity unspecified. The completed record lists 45 actual enrollees and no posted results. The dissertation reports 39 completing study procedures.
