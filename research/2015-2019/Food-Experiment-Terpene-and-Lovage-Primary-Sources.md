# Terpene and lovage primary sources, 2015-2019

## Author thesis summary of the phellandrene experiment

Piccinelli's doctoral thesis summarizes the previously published nerve injury experiment as showing reduced hyperalgesia and inhibited forced-swim immobility. The latter direction differs from the 2014 journal abstract. The public thesis file omits the article pages between printed pages 40 and 49. The summary supplies a conflicting description of the same experiment, with no independent group estimate and no ginger exposure. ## Urinary urgency severity in a single-arm lovage root blend trial

Ivanov and colleagues followed 125 women with uncomplicated urinary tract infection after a formula containing lovage root, rosemary leaves and centaury. The study had no control arm. Mean urgency severity on a 0–4 scale fell by 1.6 at day 7 and by 1.9 at day 37 (reported within-arm p<.001). Urinary frequency was a separate symptom; urgency episodes were not counted. The trial had no Parkinson cohort and no separately administered dried lovage leaf. NCT01478620 and this paper describe the same cohort, distinct from NCT02639520.
