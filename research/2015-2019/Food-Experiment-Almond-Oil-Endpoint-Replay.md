# Almond oil preparation and endpoint search

## Naringenin formulation screen distinguishes almond oil from dosed vehicle

The April 2019 naringenin study tested almond oil in a preliminary series of solubility measurements. The formulation Methods describe Capryol 90 and vitamin E as the oil phase of the optimized intranasal product. The Figure 2 caption instead names Capmul MCM, a formulation reporting inconsistency. Neither passage identifies almond oil in the dosed formulation. The observed behavioral tests do not establish an oral almond-oil effect or a patient diary outcome.
