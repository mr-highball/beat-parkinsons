## Extra virgin olive oil and elicited swallowing in rats

Ujihara, Hitomi and Ono’s June 20, 2019 physiology abstract 2P-487 reports that local pharyngolaryngeal extra-virgin olive oil elicited swallows in anesthetized rats, while ordinary olive oil and mineral oil did not. Swallows were identified from observed movement and electromyography. Sample sizes and quantitative oil-specific estimates are unavailable in the abstract. Preparation contrast is informative for the local reflex. The experiments did not use ordinary ingestion, Parkinson disease, or a clinical dysphagia outcome. [Official primary proceedings, PDF page 272](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s12576-019-00673-w.pdf#page=272)
