## Whole walnuts in bread and profile of mood tension scores

Pribis reported a double-blind crossover in young adults in 2016. Bread with whole walnut material and comparison bread without walnuts were tested over eight-week periods. POMS tension-anxiety comparisons were inconclusive for the whole sample and the sex subgroups. In the combined sample of 49 the two means were 11.2 and 11.9 (p = .507). In men the means were 9.1 and 11.2 (p = .117), and in women they were 12.6 and 12.1 (p = .735), walnut and comparison respectively. The subgroup directions differed. Male total mood improvement is a separate reported result. Walnut matrix and population limit application to cold-pressed oil in Parkinson disease.
