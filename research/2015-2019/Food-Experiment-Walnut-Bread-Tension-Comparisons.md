# Whole walnuts in bread and profile of mood tension scores source record

## Depression-dejection in the walnut bread crossover

The 2016 report (PMID **27792133**) uses the same trial cohort as the earlier cognition report. In Table 4, the mean POMS Depression-Dejection scores were 9.7 with walnut bread and 12.1 with walnut-free bread (n=49, p=.103). Estimates were also inconclusive in the men (9.4 versus 12.9, p=.132) and women (9.7 versus 11.3, p=.444). The composite mood result in men and the depression subscale have different statistical results. The cohort consisted of young adults and the food contained ground whole walnuts. ## Whole walnuts in bread and profile of mood tension scores

Pribis reported a double-blind crossover in young adults in 2016. Bread with whole walnut material and comparison bread without walnuts were tested over eight-week periods. POMS tension-anxiety comparisons were inconclusive for the whole sample and the sex subgroups. In the combined sample of 49 the two means were 11.2 and 11.9 (p = .507). In men the means were 9.1 and 11.2 (p = .117), and in women they were 12.6 and 12.1 (p = .735), walnut and comparison respectively. The subgroup directions differed. Male total mood improvement is a separate reported result. Walnut matrix and population limit application to cold-pressed oil in Parkinson disease.
