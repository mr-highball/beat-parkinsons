# Corn Oil Depression

## OCEAN posted Hamilton depression results

Thirty-six participants were assigned to each arm. The official intention-to-treat adjusted week-12 Hamilton depression means (SE) were 15.1 (0.9) with EPA/DHA, 15.7 (0.9) with EPA and 14.9 (0.9) with corn oil. The two contrasts versus corn oil gave p=.74 and p=.45 respectively. The analysis used imputation and adjustment including baseline score and clinical covariates. The registry reports four oil capsules daily but does not specify the refining grade. These outcomes compare assigned formulations in depressed heart-failure patients. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02057406?tab=results) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/NCT02057406-OCEAN-Hamilton-Results.txt)

## Published OCEAN depression report

The article reports 108 randomized patients with heart failure and major depression and 80 qualifying completers. Overall depression comparisons were inconclusive in the intention-to-treat analysis. Exploratory social-function contrasts and correlations with cognitive depressive symptoms address other constructs. The official results identify the arm-specific Hamilton values and the corn comparator. The checked publisher route exposed the author abstract; body tables remain unverified. ## Corn oil capsules in adolescent major depression

Gabbay and colleagues report 51 adolescents randomized to omega-3 monotherapy or matching corn-oil capsules. Table 2 gives 27 corn and 21 omega cases and an adjusted Children's Depression Rating Scale–Revised difference of 1.81 (95% CI −3.31 to 6.94, p=.49). The adjusted Beck difference was 0.79 (−4.27 to 5.84, p=.76). The linked registry reports 16 in an imaging scope recruited from GCO 12-1321. Those 16 should not replace the published clinical denominators or be counted as another independent trial. There was no oil-free control and no reported refining grade or Parkinson cohort. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6625364/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/PMID29985566-Adolescent-Corn-Oil-Depression.txt)

## Corn oil placebo in the later sertraline trial

The trial randomized 144 adults with major depression and coronary disease or risk factors. The 73 corn and 71 EPA assignees received 2 g/day assigned oil with sertraline 50 mg/day for ten weeks. Unadjusted Beck means changed 29.1 to 9.1 for corn and 29.9 to 11.0 for EPA. The intention-to-treat adjusted means were 10.3 versus 12.1, modeled EPA minus corn difference 1.74 (95% CI −1.04 to 4.52, p=.22). Adjusted Hamilton means 7.2 versus 8.0, difference 0.81 (−1.11 to 2.73, p=.40). A separate earlier Carney trial used an EPA/DHA formulation. The assigned comparison gives no oil-free or independently identified refining contrast. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6550340/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/PMID31163106-Corn-Oil-Sertraline.txt)

## Corn oil comparator in young adults with depressive symptoms

Ginty and Conklin randomized 23 young adults with a baseline Beck Depression Inventory score above 10 to corn-oil capsules or 1.4 g EPA plus DHA daily for 21 days. The abstract reports that 20 percent of the corn group and 67 percent of the omega-3 group no longer met the study depression threshold. It reports a significant group-by-time interaction and an inconclusive corn-arm change. The accessible abstract does not give group denominators, the exact interaction estimate, a corn grade or an oil-free comparison. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26188642/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/PMID26188642-Corn-Oil-Young-Adult-Depression.txt)

## Lahore adjunct depression trial registry

The Lahore major depression study lists 70 participants and a completed 2017 study. Adults taking antidepressants received a 500 mg corn-oil capsule or an EPA/DHA capsule daily for 12 weeks. Depression was assessed with the Beck inventory. The registration was first posted after the study and has no posted results. The original publication gives smaller analysis denominators. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03732378) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/NCT03732378-Corn-Oil-Lahore.txt)

## Lahore corn oil comparator and unresolved reporting

Yousef and colleagues compared 500 mg/day corn oil and a 500 mg/day EPA/DHA capsule alongside antidepressants for 12 weeks in adults with major depression. The report and linked registry give 70 assigned, while the analyzed tables show 30 omega and 31 corn participants. The abstract calls the corn Beck change insignificant while printing 2.32 (SD 4.43), p=.007. Its reported paired tests describe changes within groups. The published account leaves an isolated corn effect, a verified refining grade and a directly quantified adjusted between-arm effect unresolved.
