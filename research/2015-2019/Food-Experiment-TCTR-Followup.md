# Thai Clinical Trials Registry follow-up

## Thai cannabis practice cohort registration

TCTR20191231001 first registered on December 31, 2019, after the first enrollment date in the record. The observational protocol followed patients with various indications who received medicinal cannabis oil at their practitioners' discretion. It lists monthly Edmonton Symptom Assessment Scale and EQ-5D-5L assessments. The linked reports establish that the cohort included people with Parkinson's and that the formulation contained coconut oil.
