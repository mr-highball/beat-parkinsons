# Clinical and Diagnosis

### 2015 — Movement Disorder Society clinical and prodromal criteria

The MDS task force published motor parkinsonism requirements, exclusions, red flags and supportive features for clinical diagnosis. The companion research criteria estimated prodromal probability from age and risk markers. They address different stages and purposes. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26474316/) [Related primary study](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.26431)

### 2019 — Risk of phenoconversion after isolated REM sleep behavior disorder

Across 24 centers and 1,280 people with polysomnography-confirmed isolated REM sleep behavior disorder, about 6.3% per year developed a defined neurodegenerative syndrome. Outcomes included parkinsonism or dementia and participants came from specialty settings. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6391615/)
