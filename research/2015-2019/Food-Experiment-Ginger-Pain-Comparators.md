# Ginger Pain Comparators

## Ginger extract and acute exercise soreness

Thirty-six healthy women received a 60 mg ethanolic extract of dried ginger either before or after eccentric exercise, or placebo. The paper equates 60 mg extract with 2 g dried powder. Table 3 pain means at 24 hours were 57.9 before exercise, 40.0 after exercise and 65.0 with placebo, and at 48 hours were 53.7, 33.3 and 58.75. The abstract describes lower pain in the before-exercise group than the after-exercise group, the reverse of the table. Table 4 reports a group-by-time p=.0001. The paper prints unclear pairwise symbols and no complete adjusted pairwise estimates. The extract and exercise-soreness setting differ from grated ginger and Parkinson pain.
