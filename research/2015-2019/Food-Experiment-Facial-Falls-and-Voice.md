# Facial expression, falls, voice and related food outcomes

## Registered Brazilian diet and cold-pressed olive oil trial

NCT02463435 was first posted June 4, 2015. The current record lists three active arms, including olive oil with and without a diet intervention, and HADS as a secondary change outcome. The live record had no posted results. The original registry history would be needed to place the addition of HADS. The later journal outcomes belong to this trial. ## Orange and other citrus in an injurious falls cohort

Supplementary Table S4 from Sim et al. gives adjusted HR 1.01 (95% CI .99–1.04, p=.345) per 20 g/day for oranges and other citrus combined. The cohort included 1,429 older Australian women, with 568 fall-related hospitalizations over 14.5 years. Baseline FFQ excluded canned and frozen fruit and juice. The study analyzed first fall hospitalization, with no separate estimate for orange, fall count or Parkinson subgroup. It is the same study already indexed for vegetable intake and falls.
