# Tarragon recipes and facial motor assessment in a Parkinson diet trial

Phillips and colleagues first published this randomized trial on August 11, 2018. Forty-seven participants were randomized, 44 began the diets, and 38 completed eight weeks. The ketogenic plan includes a chicken recipe with 1½ teaspoons of tarragon per serving on page 77 and a salmon meal with a teaspoon of tarragon in the dill sauce on page 81. The shopping list specifies a small vial. The menu gives neither the botanical species nor dried or fresh status, and actual participant intake of these recipes is unreported. [Original dietary supplement](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC6175383.1/MDS-33-1306-s003.pdf) · [Primary trial](https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002%2Fmds.27390)

MDS-UPDRS Part III includes item 3.2 for facial expression. The clinical report publishes Part III totals without the facial-expression item or a tarragon-specific outcome. This is the same trial as the other Phillips 2018 menu annotations.
