# Post-traumatic olfactory training and its trial registration

## Post-traumatic olfactory training registration

IRCT lists registration on October 20, 2017 and labels it during recruitment. The expected dates run from July 23, 2017 to February 19, 2019. Actual first and last enrollment fields are blank. The live registry displays recruitment complete, with zero recorded updates. The February 22, 2018 WHO snapshot lists recruiting and retrospective registration. It records July 23 as the first-enrollment date, which IRCT labels expected.

The original registry gives a target of 120 participants and a fixed four-odor program with rose, eucalyptus, lemon and clove versus no training. The intervention field describes 12 weeks, while the narrative describes 16. Sniffin’ Sticks olfaction is the primary outcome and sexual satisfaction is secondary. The published study reports 25 analyzed in three groups, with 16 weeks and a rotating scent arm that includes thyme. The registration establishes what its own fields report. It leaves the timing and reason for changes from those fields to the published design unresolved.
