# Tree nut exposure and voice or behavior

## Macadamia kernel anaphylaxis after prior topical oil exposure

Leins and Smart reported in 2015 that a three-year-old with eczema developed coughing, hives, lip swelling and hoarseness severe enough to prevent speaking within one minute of chewing and spitting out a macadamia kernel. A skin-prick test to macadamia produced a 20 mm wheal. Prior exposure was through a moisturizer containing macadamia nut oil. The investigators raised the possibility of skin sensitization. The abstract gives no oil processing method, oral oil challenge, formal voice volume, speech intelligibility or Parkinson swallowing assessment. [Primary conference abstract P60](https://doi.org/10.1111/imj.12869)

## Hazelnut oil feeding in a rat reproductive study

Kati and colleagues published a 16-week rat feeding comparison of diets with hazelnut oil, canola oil or no added experimental oil in 2018. The abstract reports no significant difference in overall observed behavior or body weight across the three groups. The paper focuses on reproductive histology and hormone concentrations. No validated motor task, tremor assessment, gait freezing measure or oil-processing comparison is reported. The paper notes a 2012 conference presentation. An accessible original abstract with study results from that meeting remains to be located.
