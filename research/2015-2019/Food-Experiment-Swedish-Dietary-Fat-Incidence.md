# Dietary fat and Parkinson incidence in the Swedish National March Cohort

## 2018 conference report

Bonn and colleagues assessed dietary fat by baseline food-frequency questionnaire and linked participants to Swedish registers from 1997 through 2010. Among 43,863 participants the abstract reports 292 Parkinson diagnoses. Higher total and saturated fat were both associated with incident diagnoses in these analyses. The respective hazard ratios were 1.31 (95% CI 1.04–1.66) and 1.87 (95% CI 1.11–3.15). The reported substitution of saturated for polyunsaturated fat yielded HR 2.75 (95% CI 1.02–7.40) per 5 percentage points of energy. See presentation P20-020 in the July 2018 collection. [Original conference abstract collection](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6265811/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2018-Bonn-Swedish-Dietary-Fat-P20-020.txt)

The outcome was Parkinson incidence. The collection's potato mortality presentation P20-066 and alcohol and sleepiness presentation P20-110 used separate cohorts. The 2022 article extended follow-up in the same Swedish cohort and produced different total fat and substitution estimates.
