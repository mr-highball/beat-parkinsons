# Marjoram and olive oil symptom outcomes

## Olive oil comparator in first-episode schizophrenia

The OFFER primary symptom article compared concentrated omega-3 oil with an olive oil comparator alongside usual antipsychotic treatment for 26 weeks. The Positive and Negative Syndrome Scale gives composite results. A combined psychosis score cannot estimate visual hallucination frequency, and the active comparison gives no olive-oil effect against usual care alone. The material reviewed does not verify a refined grade. The primary report was published online in 2015 and in the February 2016 issue. [Trial protocol and comparator](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4456694/) · [Primary trial report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26679763/)

## Dried marjoram as an odor identification stimulus

Knaapila and colleagues first published November 21, 2016. Of 126 adults, 31.0% identified dried marjoram odor correctly. Among the 118 participants with linked survey responses, 42.7% reported using the herb. The identification task measured recognition of one familiar or unfamiliar stimulus in a cross-sectional sample. The study did not assign consumption, measure changes in olfactory impairment or enroll a Parkinson cohort. Chervil and tarragon appeared as multiple-choice distractors. [Institutional primary manuscript, supplementary table 3](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/4549f2bf-0663-4e7a-b8cf-f88b3010040b/content)

## Marjoram massage and rotating-shift sleep

Chang and colleagues first published July 6, 2017. The aromatherapy massage used sweet marjoram essential oil. The pre-to-post sleep questionnaire score fell in that group, while the modeled comparison between groups at the fourth post-test was −0.53 points (95% CI −2.33 to 1.273, p = .565). The reported detector-derived wake or dream duration did not show a clear group difference. Table 4 assigns p = .026 to deep sleep duration, while the Results prose assigns it to sleep duration. The comparison combines odor, massage and the care setting. It gives no dream enactment count or Parkinson sleep result. [Primary report, Tables 3–5](https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/3861273) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC5518528.txt)

## Olive oil and footprint outcomes after cerebral ischemia

The mouse report first published May 18, 2019. It compared diets rich in saturated fat, olive oil, olive oil plus DHA/EPA and a low-fat diet around a cerebral ischemia intervention. Olive oil without DHA/EPA gave no clear improvement on the reported footprint measures. The DHA/EPA blend and the low-fat diet improved hindlimb stride coordination and steps per meter relative to the saturated-fat group. These outcomes concern a focal stroke model and the report does not establish a cold-pressed oil exposure. [Primary report, Figure 8](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11051109) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC6566717.txt)

## Refined and extra-virgin olive oil cognition trial registration

NCT03824197 first posted January 31, 2019. The Auburn study compared oils high and low in phenols and planned blood-brain barrier integrity and brain activity outcomes. The registry has no posted results. The 2022 article identifies the lower-phenol comparison as refined oil and reports clinical cognition and imaging results. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03824197) · [2022 results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Marjoram-Olive-Outcomes.md#refined-olive-oil-cognition-and-task-imaging)

## Fresh marjoram extract and a potassium channel assay

The 2019 experiments prepared a methanolic extract from fresh marjoram leaves and assayed human KCNQ5 expressed in frog oocytes. Marjoram was among the extracts reported to shift the voltage dependence of channel activation. The vascular relaxation experiment used aloperine isolated from a different plant. The report supplies no human blood pressure, standing symptoms or oral marjoram comparison. [Primary paper, Figure 1 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6800379/)

## Marjoram extract and rat aortic relaxation

Badran and colleagues first published June 11, 2019. Marjoram extract relaxed isolated norepinephrine-contracted rat aortic rings, with maximal relaxation of 95 ± 3% in intact rings and 46 ± 4% after endothelial removal. The original experiment directly applied an extract to tissue. It gives no oral or standing blood pressure comparison. [Primary report, Results 3.2 and Figure 2](https://doi.org/10.3390/biom9060227) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC6627793.txt)

## Registered oral olive-oil comparator and actigraphy

First posted June 18, 2018. The registry identifies olive-oil capsules as the placebo arm in a 26-week comparison of DHA- and EPA-rich products and measures objective and subjective sleep. The register was completed without a posted result module at the November 15, 2026 replay. The 2021 report specifies refined olive oil. [Local source text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT03559361.txt) · [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03559361)

## Seated blood-pressure response to inhaled sweet marjoram oil

Kawai and colleagues tested inhaled Origanum majorana essential oil in 18 healthy resting adults across two protocols. Nine seated participants inhaled oil fragrance and plain air through a mask for six minutes each in randomized order. Over the final two minutes, mean arterial pressure fell with fragrance versus plain air (F(1,8) = 5.769, p = .043). In a separate supine group of nine, mean pressure during the last five minutes of fragrance exposure fell by 3.0 ± 2.6 mmHg versus baseline (p = .004). The supine protocol used a within-person baseline comparison. The study measured resting physiology. Standing pressure, orthostatic symptoms, Parkinson disease and ingestion of fresh leaves were outside the study design. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5336204/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2017-03-03-Marjoram-Oil-Resting-Blood-Pressure.txt)
