# Mechanisms and Models

### 2016 — Microbiota and motor deficits in α-synuclein mice

A mouse study linked intestinal microbes to motor deficits and neuroinflammation, including different effects of human-donor microbial communities. The model supports mechanistic testing. It did not measure treatment outcomes in humans. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27912057/)

### 2019 — Experimental gut-to-brain propagation of α-synuclein pathology

Kim and colleagues observed a pattern of spread and Parkinson-like changes after an experimental gut challenge in mice. Findings depended on the study model and support testing a route that may matter in some forms of disease. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31255487/)
