# Shrimp and clinical case context

## 2016 Shrimp as the content of a visual hallucination

Sakurada et al. reported visual hallucinations among patients receiving voriconazole for fungal infection (DOI 10.1691/ph.2016.6725). Table 2 describes a 70-year-old man with non-Hodgkin lymphoma who saw shrimp on day 2 of treatment; whether his eyes were open and the symptom duration were unknown. The shrimp are part of the reported visual experience, not a documented food exposure. The series does not test grilled shrimp or Parkinson disease. [Original paper, Table 2](https://storage.imrpress.com/IMR/pharmazie/application/10.1691/ph.2016.6725.pdf).
