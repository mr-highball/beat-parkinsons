# Blackberry exposures and pain outcomes

Reviewed 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

## Frozen blackberry homogenate in elite handball athletes

García-Dávila and colleagues reported 14 handball players in the 2017 supplement. Seven received 200 g of commercially obtained frozen blackberry (Rubus sp.) homogenized into 200 mL water and seven received 200 mL red-colored water. Doses were taken before lunch over 16 days around a national competition. The source compared fresh and frozen fruit for laboratory composition and selected frozen fruit for the human intervention. The species and cultivar were not identified beyond Rubus sp.

The measured human outcomes were plasma creatine kinase and urea. The between-group Mann–Whitney creatine kinase comparisons were inconclusive at all time points. Both groups had some within-group changes after competition. The paper mentions post-exercise muscle pain in its background and has no patient-rated pain or soreness measure. [García-Dávila handball article](https://ddd.uab.cat/record/181302), [local text](Source-Text/2017-Garcia-Davila-Frozen-Blackberry-Handball-Extract.txt).

## Blackberry drink and oxidative stress around handball competition

Estrada-Díaz and colleagues reported another 14-player, seven-per-arm handball study in the same supplement, also using 200 g of Rubus sp. blackberry in 200 mL water versus red-colored water. The sport, sample size, intervention and some investigators overlap with the García-Dávila report. The published schedules differ (15 versus 16 days) and participant identity could not be reconciled. Treat these as potentially overlapping until verified. The primary article reports a within-arm fall in oxidative stress from baseline to pre-competition (p=.018), using plasma d-ROMs. It did not measure pain or confirm the fruit's freezing status. [Estrada-Díaz handball article](https://ddd.uab.cat/record/181224), [local text](Source-Text/2017-Estrada-Diaz-Blackberry-Handball-Extract.txt).

The controlled [whole-frozen blackberry feeding study](Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md#frozen-whole-blackberries-in-a-metabolic-crossover) reports a separate exact food preparation bridge. Its 600 g/day intervention was evaluated for fuel oxidation, glucose and insulin after a meal, with no pain test.
