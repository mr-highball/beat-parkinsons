# Topical olive oil formulations and swallowing

## Chewable olive oil combination and reported swallowing improvement

The article describes better swallowing in its conclusion and reports only an aggregate questionnaire change in its results.

[Original publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4511916/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2015-Olive-Oil-Betaine-Xylitol-Tablet-Swallowing-Factual-Extract.txt)

## Topical olive oil formulations and postradiotherapy swallowing complaints

Uncontrolled symptom ratings included improved dryness-related swallowing. One participant with complete baseline aphagia discontinued. Neither observation isolates the oil's effect.

[Original publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5126143/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2016-Martin-Olive-Oil-Postradiotherapy-Swallowing-Factual-Extract.txt)
