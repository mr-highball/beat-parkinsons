# Peach in a Parkinson dietary plan

## Optional peach and measured motor outcomes

The low-fat diet appendix to the 2018 Phillips trial includes peach or persimmon as a choice. Printed page 56 slices the chosen fruit as a topping for a raspberry, mango and granola smoothie. Page 58 chops it into granola cereal. A different fruit-salad recipe includes peach from a container of sliced fruit in juice. The assigned diet therefore allowed whole uncooked peach, alongside substitutions and other forms, without public individual intake data. [Original low-fat plan, pp. 56–58](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC6175383.1/MDS-33-1306-s002.pdf)

The report gives composite MDS-UPDRS motor change and describes an individual low-fat participant with pre-existing postural hypotension and falls who required an increase in midodrine because falls were ongoing. There are no participant meal records for that event, no isolated peach comparison, and no peach-specific manual dexterity or postural outcome.
