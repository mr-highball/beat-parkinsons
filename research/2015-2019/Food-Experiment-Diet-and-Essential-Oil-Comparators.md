# Diet and essential oil comparators, 2015-2019

## Topical shell ginger oil after stroke and muscle EMG

Maia and colleagues applied essential oil from Alpinia zerumbet to affected calf muscles in 15 people with post-stroke spasticity. The untreated opposite leg served as a physiological comparison. The abstract describes before-and-after changes in surface electromyography of the treated muscles, with no randomized placebo contrast, clinical dystonia rating or harms data. The observed outcome is electrophysiology under topical shell ginger oil. A later review describes it as a post-stroke dystonia result with galangal. The original abstract names a different outcome and does not identify either common culinary galangal species.
