# Peanut oil comparison in visceral pain

## Rectal vehicle in a visceral pain behavior experiment

Jain and colleagues compared intrarectal peanut oil vehicle with allyl isothiocyanate in mouse studies of visceral pain. Figure 6 shows that the irritant prolonged freezing and reduced locomotion and rearing relative to the vehicle in healthy controls. Figure 8 reports more freezing with irritant than vehicle among mice pretreated with saline, p < .05. Saline was a separate injection preceding both rectal treatments. Oil preparation grade is unavailable, the oil was administered rectally, and the mice were outside a Parkinson model.
