# Grapeseed and flaxseed oil evidence

## Healthy Cookie Study registration and oral grapeseed oil

NCT02841618 first posted on July 22, 2016. The healthy-adult study uses one baked cookie daily with 10 g of grapeseed oil. The randomized phase compared a cookie containing high-oleic safflower oil. Gait speed, grip strength and a sleep questionnaire are secondary measurements for associations with blood linoleic acid. The public registry remained active, not recruiting, with no posted results in the November 13, 2026 replay. The [2022 primary report](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Flaxseed-Followup.md#healthy-cookie-dietary-fatty-acid-results) covers blood outcomes. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02841618)

## Grapeseed oil enema in experimental colitis

Hosseinzadeh and colleagues compared grapeseed oil enema with several control and active preparations after acetic-acid colitis. Table 1 gives mean microscopic lesion score 0.88 for the grapeseed-oil arm and 2.67 for untreated colitis, with different post hoc significance groups. The non-colitic animals given grapeseed oil had a mean macroscopic injury score of 1.60 compared with zero in the untreated healthy group, with a shared statistical grouping. Bowel frequency, stool consistency and constipation severity were unreported. The exposure was rectal commercial oil with no pressing method given. [Primary report, Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5506352/) · [Local licensed text](Source-Text/PMC5506352.txt)

## Injected grapeseed oil nanocapsules and oral dyskinesia

Benvegnú and colleagues first published December 5, 2017. The study compared injected haloperidol nanocapsules with grapeseed oil, fish oil or medium-chain triglyceride cores and free haloperidol. The grapeseed formulation only partly attenuated acute involuntary jaw movements relative to free drug. At the subchronic assessment all loaded formulations prevented the excess movements observed with free haloperidol. The study measures a drug-induced orofacial movement outcome in rats. Parkinson OFF time, speech intelligibility and dystonia were unmeasured. The oil was the core of an injected drug carrier. [Primary report, Fig. 3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11064-017-2444-0) · [Public report copy](https://www.academia.edu/93984790/Effects_of_Fish_and_Grape_Seed_Oils_as_Core_of_Haloperidol_Loaded_Nanocapsules_on_Oral_Dyskinesia_in_Rats)

## Topical grapeseed oil control in a sleep pilot

A 2018 research meeting abstract reported a two-week sleep comparison using a wrist application of lavender oil or grapeseed oil. Jawbone Up2 measures showed no clear differences between groups in total, light or deep sleep time. Sleep-diary measurements were also collected. The study used topical oil and the accessible abstract gives no comparison with an untreated group. [Original conference supplement](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02701367.2018.1453732)

## Resveratrol with grape juice and reported neuropsychiatric outcomes

Zhu and colleagues published 2018 results of NCT00678431 in Alzheimer disease. The study gave resveratrol, glucose and malate or placebo with commercial red grape juice. The treatment-by-visit estimate for total Neuropsychiatric Inventory score was 0.368 (95% CI −0.584 to 1.319, p = .449). Table 2 and Figure 2 report the total without separate visual hallucination results. The authors report that falls occurred in the control group and judged the events unrelated to the study. Both groups received grape juice, and neither used grapeseed oil. [Primary report, Methods and Table 3](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1016/j.trci.2018.09.009)

## High-fat breakfast and levodopa pharmacokinetics

The Yao healthy-adult crossover compared extended-release carbidopa–levodopa after a high-fat breakfast, fasting, and sprinkled on applesauce while fasting. With breakfast, the initial increase in levodopa concentration was delayed by about one to two hours, peak concentration was 21% lower and total estimated exposure was 13% higher than when fasted. The meal included eggs cooked in butter, bacon, buttered toast, hash browns and milk. No daily Parkinson OFF-time outcome or separate grapeseed oil arm was included. [Primary Methods and Table 4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4714633/)
