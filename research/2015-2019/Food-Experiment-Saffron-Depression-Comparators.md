# Saffron Depression Comparators

## Sahraian adjunct report and public abstract

Sahraian and colleagues report a four-week randomized comparison in major depression with 40 allocated participants and fluoxetine shared across groups. The author abstract reports 19 of 20 saffron-arm and 11 of 20 placebo-arm completers and an intergroup Beck Depression Inventory p = .560. It was published online November 10, 2015 and appeared in the 2016 issue. The original full body remained unavailable through the checked publisher, library and author-hosting routes. The full 2018 companion report gives different participant accounting and an intergroup p = .056. Both reports use the same trial registration. ## Linked Shiraz reports and their unresolved differences

In the January–February 2018 issue Jelodar and colleagues describe Khorasan dried stigma ground to powder and administered as one 30 mg capsule daily with fluoxetine 20 mg. Controls received placebo with the same fluoxetine. After four weeks mean Beck scores went from 22.73 (SEM 0.90) to 15.01 (0.87) and from 22.00 (0.98) to 17.73 (1.09), respectively. The reported intergroup p = .056. The original flow figure reports 20 analyzed in each arm and no recorded losses. The same registered trial family shares authors, site, 40 randomized participants, total daily dose and assessment, but the 2015 abstract reports 19 versus 11 completers and p = .560. The original registry planned six weeks and twice-daily dosing. The published and registry accounts leave attrition, exact between-arm inference and schedule differences unresolved. Neither report provides a Parkinson cohort. ## Saffron extract combination and geriatric depression scores

Akouchekian and colleagues reported on June 6, 2018 a randomized trial of 60 outpatients with major neurocognitive disorder. The intervention used capsules containing 30 mg saffron extract and 500 mg sedge, two capsules daily, with Astragalus honey and stable medicine. The comparator used starch capsules, rock candy syrup and stable medicine. Mean Geriatric Depression Scale scores at baseline were 14.6 (SD 7.9) and 14.5 (6.9), and at month two 12.2 (6.5) and 14.4 (7.1), respectively (p = .224). The abstract describes eight weeks, Methods three months and the reported visits baseline, one month and two months. The printed registration IRCT20161030255N1 was not independently resolved. No Parkinson subgroup effect or isolated ground saffron estimate was reported.
