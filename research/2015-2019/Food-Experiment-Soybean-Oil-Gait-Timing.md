# Soybean oil in an instrumented developmental mouse gait study

## Gait timing with a soybean-oil chow comparator

Carlson and colleagues compared laboratory chow whose reported fat component was soybean oil with a different long-chain-polyunsaturate diet containing hydrogenated coconut oil, DHA and arachidonic acid. These diets also differed in other ingredients and breeding exposure. The authors measured forelimb and hindlimb propulsion, swing and braking times with DigiGait. The group means showed no statistically clear difference for the three measures. Time measurements from treadmill gait do not provide stride distance. The study used neurologically typical male mice and did not specify soybean oil grade.
