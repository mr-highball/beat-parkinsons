# Chocolate Anxiety

## Brewed cocoa with caffeine and task related anxiety

Boolani and colleagues compared four drinks in a double-blind, within-person crossover in 24 adults. Cognitive-task anxiety rose after caffeine alone compared with caffeinated cocoa (treatment-by-time p = .038). Cocoa versus flavored water had no reported mood interaction. The difference is specific to cocoa plus added caffeine against caffeine alone under a mental task, and the intervention was a drink. The registered outcomes give cognition and vigor separately. No Parkinson-specific or solid dark chocolate comparison was reported.
