# Saffron and curcumin in a major-depression trial

## Original curcumin and saffron arm doses

Lopresti and Drummond first published October 1, 2016. They randomized 123 adults with major depression to placebo, low-dose curcumin extract, high-dose curcumin extract or low-dose curcumin extract plus saffron for twelve weeks. In the combined arm, curcumin extract was 250 mg twice daily and saffron was 15 mg twice daily. The 500 mg twice-daily curcumin dose was a separate arm. The pooled active arms differed from placebo on IDS-SR30 depression (p=.031), state anxiety (p<.001) and trait anxiety (p=.001). The original abstract reports no clear differences among the active arms. The trial has no isolated saffron arm or Parkinson population.
