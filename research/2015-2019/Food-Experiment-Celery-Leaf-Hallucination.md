# Celery leaf and hallucinations

## 2016 Celery root extract and mania with venlafaxine and St Johns wort

Khalid and colleagues report mania with elevated venlafaxine after a patient began celery-root extract while taking venlafaxine and St John's wort. Their proposed interaction is a hypothesis. The 2016 primary abstract and 2022 review describe one patient. The primary full case remains inaccessible. A separate 2009 venlafaxine case documented transient visual hallucinations without reported celery exposure. ## Pharmaceutical butylphthalide and the PDQ-39 combined item

Zhou and colleagues used aggregate PDQ-39 scores in their 2019 Parkinson trial. Item 33 groups distressing dreams and hallucinations in one ordinal question. The paper provides neither that individual result nor a separate visual-hallucination outcome. Its independently reported adverse events have no hallucination category. The article identifies ChiCTR1800018892, and the official registry fields remain unavailable. [Original trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6447885/) · [AMP-PD questionnaire data dictionary](https://amp-pdrd.org/pdq-39)

## Dried celery leaf powder in a thyroid case report

A case report describes a 48-year-old man who used 4 g daily of dried celery leaf powder for 45 days for weight loss while taking losartan. The authors report weight loss, sweating, exophthalmos and abnormal thyroid tests during the case. After he stopped the powder and started methimazole, laboratory results normalized. The simultaneous treatment limits inference from the dechallenge. The report gives no hallucination or Parkinson symptom measurement. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5403020/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2016-Rouhi-Boroujeni-Celery-Leaf-Case-Extract.txt)

## Celery extract powder in a separate thyroid case report

A 36-year-old woman presented with blurred vision, palpitation and other thyroid symptoms after taking 8 g per day of celery extract powder for 78 days. The plant part is unspecified. She stopped the extract and started methimazole together. The authors cite an earlier, separate dried-leaf case. This report concerns blurred vision with abnormal thyroid tests and supplies no hallucination-frequency measurement or Parkinson exposure comparison. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6528421/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2019-Maljaei-Celery-Extract-Case-Extract.txt)
