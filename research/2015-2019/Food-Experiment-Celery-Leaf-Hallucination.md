# Celery leaf and hallucinations

## 2016 Celery root extract and mania with venlafaxine and St Johns wort

Khalid et al. 2016, Postgraduate Medicine 128:682–683, PMID 27467225, DOI 10.1080/00325481.2016.1218263. Publisher abstract reports a woman stabilized on venlafaxine and St John's wort who developed mania after starting celery root extract for menopause; elevated venlafaxine was reported. The proposed interaction is a hypothesis. Full original case details were not accessible. The exposure is root extract, not dried leaf; the accessible abstract does not report hallucination modality or frequency.

## Dried celery leaf powder in a thyroid case report

A case report describes a 48-year-old man who used 4 g daily of dried celery leaf powder for 45 days for weight loss while taking losartan. The authors report weight loss, sweating, exophthalmos and abnormal thyroid tests during the case. After he stopped the powder and started methimazole, laboratory results normalized. The simultaneous treatment limits inference from the dechallenge. The report gives no hallucination or Parkinson symptom measurement. ## Celery extract powder in a separate thyroid case report

A 36-year-old woman presented with blurred vision, palpitation and other thyroid symptoms after taking 8 g per day of celery extract powder for 78 days. The plant part is unspecified. She stopped the extract and started methimazole together. The authors cite an earlier, separate dried-leaf case. This report concerns blurred vision with abnormal thyroid tests and supplies no hallucination-frequency measurement or Parkinson exposure comparison.
