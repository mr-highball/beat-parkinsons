# Further black pepper trial outcomes

Review date 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

## Registry for topical oil in chemotherapy-induced neuropathy

NCT03449303 was first posted on 2018-02-28. Its intervention description specifies a 10% dilution of six essential oils, including Piper nigrum, in jojoba. The concurrent control is jojoba. Its population was adults with breast cancer and chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy. The registry had no posted results when reviewed. The subsequent primary article reports 27 randomized participants. [ClinicalTrials.gov neuropathy record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03449303), [local text](Source-Text/NCT03449303-Mixed-Oils-Pain-Registry-Extract.txt).

The updated registry record gives 26 actual enrollees, while the linked publication reports 27 randomized. Weekly SF-MPQ-2 pain intensity was primary and daily VAS pain secondary. Registry results are absent. The journal abstract reports an inconclusive pain comparison and does not provide the raw arm-level pain estimates. The later photovoice paper describes qualitative symptom experiences and does not supply an essential-oil treatment estimate. Participant overlap is unresolved. [Official JSON](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03449303) · [Original 2023 paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmn.2022.12.008) · [Later photovoice report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42913343/)

## Piperine blend registry and ureteral-stent symptom measurement

NCT02598726 first posted November 6, 2015. It records nine actual enrollees in a single-group study of curcumin plus a standardized piperine product. A secondary measure is the Ureteral Stent Symptom Questionnaire. The current record has no posted results. No clinical effect or separate piperine comparison is available.
