# Pumpkin seed oil endpoint follow-up, 2015–2019

## Roasted and unroasted seeds in cold pressed oil production

The same primary paper cold-pressed oil from roasted seeds and from unroasted seeds. This verifies that pretreatment and pressing method can coexist and should be recorded independently. Composition and storage outcomes do not bear directly on a frozen symptom. [Primary paper, Methods and Table 2](https://www.food.actapol.net/volume16/issue3/5_3_2017.pdf) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2017-Raczyk-Cold-Pressed-Roasted-Pumpkin-Oil.txt)

## Topical pumpkin seed oil and aphthous ulcer scores

An uncontrolled 25-person study evaluated topical commercial pumpkin seed oil for aphthous ulcers. The oral lesion score fell during follow-up, which cannot distinguish treatment from natural healing. The endpoint, topical route and unspecified preparation differ from toasted edible oil and drooling. [Primary paper, OCMI and Table 3](https://file.scirp.org/Html/4-1050407_81084.htm) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2017-Sharquie-Topical-Pumpkin-Oil-Aphthae.txt)

## Intranasal pumpkin preparation and dream related questionnaire limits

The trial measured insomnia and broad sleep components after intranasal whole pumpkin in sesame oil. The standard PSQI includes dream disturbance as one question, and the trial reports aggregate domains. No separate dream result or dream-enactment ascertainment was identified. Parkinson was excluded. [Author-accessible primary full text](https://www.academia.edu/41163340/Efficacy_of_pumpkin_oil_a_Persian_medicine_product_in_the_treatment_of_chronic_insomnia_A_randomized_double_blind_clinical_trial) · [PSQI instrument](https://www.sleep.pitt.edu/psqi) · [Primary trial, Methods and Tables 2–3](https://doi.org/10.56499/jppres19.654_7.6.471) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2019-Haghjoo-Pumpkin-Insomnia-Dream-Endpoints.txt)

## Registered intranasal pumpkin preparation in insomnia

The Iranian registry first posted on September 19, 2018. It lists insomnia severity and sleep quality after intranasal administration, with Parkinson disease among the exclusions. The 2019 paper describes the actual whole-pumpkin and sesame vehicle preparation. This registration and the paper represent the same trial.
