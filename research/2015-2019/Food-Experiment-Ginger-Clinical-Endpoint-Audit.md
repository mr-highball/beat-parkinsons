# Ginger and abdominal pain in a Parkinson case

## Dai-Kenchu-To and nocturnal abdominal pain

Shiota, Nagai and Yamaguchi reported a man with Parkinson disease, Hoehn–Yahr stage III, whose nocturnal abdominal pain resolved following bedtime oral Dai-Kenchu-To. This was one uncontrolled observation. The report does not give a pain scale, dose, brand or separate follow-up measurement of constipation. Dai-Kenchu-To contains multiple ingredients, including processed ginger. The case cannot isolate the effect of grated culinary ginger. The presentation took place in November 2017. The journal issued the proceedings October 25, 2018 and J-STAGE posted them December 11, 2018. This case is presentation 36 on printed page 720.
