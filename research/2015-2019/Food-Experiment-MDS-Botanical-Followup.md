# Earlier records traced through the 2026 Movement Disorder Society congress

## Oral Eclipta and holy basil extracts in drug-induced models

Jaisin et al. tested an ethyl acetate *Eclipta prostrata* extract in SH-SY5Y cells exposed to 6-OHDA in 2016. Pretreatment improved cell survival and reduced reactive oxygen species and markers of apoptosis. The laboratory outcomes have no behavioral counterpart in this experiment. [Primary study, DOI 10.2306/scienceasia1513-1874.2016.42.259](https://www.scienceasia.org/2016.42.n4/scias42_259.pdf).

The locally archived 2018 Chahal and Sharma text comes from the publisher's version, which permits noncommercial use with attribution. [Local article text](Source-Text/2018-Chahal-Eclipta-Ocimum.txt).

Ranjithkumar and Muralidharan presented a five-arm haloperidol mouse study on October 21–22, 2016. The proceedings report lower catalepsy and higher rotarod performance and activity in the actophotometer and hole-board tests after seven days of oral ethanolic *Eclipta alba* extract at 200 or 400 mg/kg, all reported p < .001 against haloperidol alone. The abstract gives both 1 and 2 mg/kg for haloperidol, provides no group sizes or numerical effect estimates, and was the only report located for these authors and intervention. [Original proceedings TPV16/PP/006, printed page 31](https://www.ijptjournal.com/File_Folder/014287512.pdf).

On September 15, 2018, Chahal and Sharma reported Swiss albino mouse groups receiving separate oral extracts alongside haloperidol. The Methods describe aqueous decoction of the entire dried *E. alba* plant (300 or 600 mg/kg/day) and a separate 70% ethanolic extract of *Ocimum sanctum* leaves (400 or 800 mg/kg/day). Figure 1 and the text describe lower day-15 catalepsy scores at the higher doses. The prose identifies the bromocriptine reference group as the comparator for p < .001 and describes only small changes at the lower doses. The paper's terminology for units and group comparisons is unclear. *O. sanctum* denotes holy basil, *O. tenuiflorum*. This study supplies no data on fresh culinary basil and human Parkinson rigidity or postural instability. [Original article and Figure 1](https://www.jddtonline.info/index.php/jddt/article/view/1871) · [Publisher PDF](https://jddtonline.info/index.php/jddt/article/download/1871/1339) · [2024 injected Eclipta study with higher catalepsy scores](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-MDS-Botanical-Followup.md#injected-aqueous-eclipta-and-catalepsy).

## Levodopa in ground Argentine velvet beans

Nacul, Debenedetti, and Bosco's October 2016 university report measured levodopa by HPLC in powdered *Mucuna pruriens* seeds grown in Formosa and reported 5.87% by weight. The report describes analysis of locally used seeds without a clinical cohort or symptom outcome. The university published a related 2018 interview on the same preparation, which appears in the 2026 Argentine survey’s references. [October 2016 original university report](https://repositorio.ub.edu.ar/bitstream/handle/123456789/8372/UBnews_octubre_2016.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1) · [2018 investigators’ account](https://repositorio.ub.edu.ar/handle/123456789/8649) · [Later Argentine survey](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-MDS-Botanical-Followup.md#reported-velvet-bean-use-in-argentina).
