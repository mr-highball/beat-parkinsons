# Risk and Prevention

### 2015 — Vagotomy and subsequent Parkinson’s diagnosis

Danish registry comparisons suggested lower long-term incidence after truncal vagotomy in some analyses, with uncertain estimates. Historical surgery, health indications and comparison groups complicate causal interpretation.
