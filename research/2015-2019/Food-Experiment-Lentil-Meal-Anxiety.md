# Acute mood after meals containing canned lentils

## Canned lentil meals and the acute anxiety time course

The 2017 conference report from NCT02110511 describes self-rated anxiety over three hours for the same 12 healthy adults. The authors describe more anxiety and other negative mood ratings at times after lentil meals. The 5–11% range covers several mood variables, with no separate anxiety estimate or precise p value. They report no main or interaction effect for anxiety area under the curve. The publisher dates the issue to 2017 and gives a 2018 electronic date. The companion methods verify canned, whole or blended lentils, rather than home boiling from dry.
