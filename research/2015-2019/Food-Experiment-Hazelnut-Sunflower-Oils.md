# Hazelnut and sunflower oil evidence

## BENEFIC registration and first sunflower comparator report

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT02551419 on September 16, 2015. It planned PET and cognitive outcomes, including executive function, after six months of a ketogenic or control beverage. The record calls the comparator placebo. Fortier and colleagues identified it in their April 23, 2019 first-phase report as an emulsion containing refined, bleached, winterized and deodorized high-oleic sunflower oil. Fifty-two adults with mild cognitive impairment were randomized and 39 completed this phase. The 2020 combined report provides the full cognitive comparison and incorporates this phase. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02551419) · [Local registry summary](Source-Text/Trials/NCT02551419.txt) · [2019 Methods, §2.4](https://alz-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1016/j.jalz.2018.12.017) · [2020 aggregate cognitive results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#refined-sunflower-oil-comparator-in-the-benefic-cognitive-analysis)

## Shiraz major depression and sunflower oil trial registration

IRCT retrospectively registered IRCT2016041820140N3 on May 14, 2016. Sahar Foshati registered 73 adults with major depression and a comparison of 25 mL/day of sunflower oil with extra-virgin olive oil for 52 days. Blood pressure, anthropometrics and serum markers were specified outcomes. The protocol mentions the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index as confounder monitoring and does not list the depression rating scales. Processing grade and randomized arm values are unavailable in the register. The exposure, sample and investigators match the 2021 study reports. [Official IRCT record](https://en.irct.ir/trial/17869) · [Local registry summary](Source-Text/Trials/IRCT2016041820140N3.txt) · [2021 depression results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#sunflower-oil-comparator-in-the-shiraz-major-depression-trial)

## High oleic sunflower oil and executive tasks and handgrip

Jenkins and colleagues first published August 10, 2016. Sixty-five older adults received rumenic-acid-rich conjugated linoleic acid or 6 g/day of high-oleic sunflower oil for eight weeks. The sponsor patent gives sunflower-arm Trail Making B times of 93.8 to 78.4 seconds for men and 82.5 to 74.3 seconds for women. There was an improvement over visits in both groups with no clear treatment difference on Trail Making, serial subtraction, handgrip strength or handgrip fatigability. The study did not assess clinical fatigue or dexterity. It enrolled older volunteers, gave no extraction grade and did not have an oil-free arm. The patent’s cognition example is the same cohort. [Original trial report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27521997/) · [European patent, Table 2](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3474839B1/en)

## Sunflower oil aroma comparator and mood ratings in residential care

Watson reported ACTRN12614000130662 results in a 2017 Western Sydney University thesis and a subsequent journal report. The crossover used two drops of unscented sunflower oil on lapel wadding for two weeks per period as the comparison for lavender and lemon balm oils. The thesis reports NPI anxiety change of −2.88 with sunflower and −0.57 with the essential oils pooled (p = .09) and depression changes of −1.0 and −2.8 (p = .15). One Parkinson diagnosis was considered a protocol violation in the per-protocol analysis. There is no separately analyzed Parkinson subgroup, no ingestion and no refining grade. [2017 thesis, pp. 163–167](https://researchers-admin.westernsydney.edu.au/ws/portalfiles/portal/94874239/uws_44603.pdf) · [Later journal report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30670268/) · [2014 registration](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#sunflower-comparator-in-two-australian-and-new-zealand-registrations)

## Sunflower seed oil rinse registration in healthy volunteers

DRKS retrospectively registered DRKS00011922 April 5, 2017. In three adults the design used saline and sunflower seed oil in a fixed sequence and planned oral microbiome and sample comparisons. The later article reports collected saliva volumes. ## Sunflower oil frequency and overall Parkinson symptoms

Mischley, Bennett and Lau presented their CAM Care in PD baseline analysis at the 2017 MDS Congress. The poster's food-choice table associates each increase in intake-frequency category for a one-tablespoon serving of sunflower oil with 2 higher total PRO-PD points (SE 6, 95% CI −10 to 13, p = .787). It used cross-sectional self-report and did not specify oil refining, seed roast, cultivar or individual symptom estimates. The related 2017 journal article does not print a sunflower-oil row, and a check of the 2024 ten-year report and 2025 PRO diet paper did not locate an exact sunflower-oil item analysis. The 2016 meeting is a prior report from the same project, and the later cohort reports should not be counted as independent replication. [Original poster, Food Choices](https://womenwithparkinsons.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2019/11/bc2ba-mds2017diet.pdf) · [2017 congress abstract](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/the-roles-of-diet-exercise-supplements-in-parkinsons-disease-progression/) · [Local 2017 journal article](Source-Text/PMC5610862.txt) · [Later cohort follow-up](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-MVP-Followup.md)

## Sunflower oil and infant aspiration reports

Marangu and colleagues published their exogenous lipoid pneumonia case series August 7, 2018. Table 1 names repeated oral sunflower oil use in three infants and a fourth who switched from sunflower oil to liquid paraffin. Two in the sunflower-only group had silent aspiration and gastroesophageal reflux documented, while a third had reflux with no aspiration demonstrated on the recorded evaluation. The series gives no oil refining grade or comparative incidence and does not establish that the oil worsened swallowing physiology. It records pulmonary complications in an aspiration-risk population. [Local primary text, Table 1](Source-Text/PMC6079933.txt) · [Published article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6079933/)

## Registered sunflower oil rinse for medication related xerostomia

The German Clinical Trials Register posted DRKS00012459 on January 31, 2018. The completed crossover enrolled 26 participants and compared a sunflower-oil rinse with water. The registry links the eventual report by Ludwar and colleagues. This is the same study as the 2020 conference and journal reports. ## Sunflower oil as a cooking oil replacement in mild cognitive impairment

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT03727386 on November 1, 2018. The six-month randomized pilot assigned a replacement cooking oil, either sunflower or virgin coconut, to adults with mild cognitive impairment. Trail Making Test B was planned among secondary outcomes. The study excluded Parkinson’s and certain other neurological conditions and is marked completed. ClinicalTrials.gov lists 34 participants and no posted results. The 2022 thesis reports 31 randomized participants. The registry gives no extraction grade for the sunflower oil. [2022 trial results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#refined-sunflower-cooking-oil-and-executive-function-in-the-dice-pilot) · [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03727386) · [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT03727386.txt) · [UK Health Research Authority study record](https://www.hra.nhs.uk/planning-and-improving-research/application-summaries/research-summaries/the-effect-of-vegetable-oil-on-cognitive-functions-in-mci-patients/)

## DEPOXIN sunflower oil comparator registration and pilot

ISRCTN first posted ISRCTN81655012 on October 19, 2016, after the recorded start of enrollment. The 2017 pilot says recruitment began June 2013, while the registry gives March 2014. The registered intervention compares fish oil with an omega-6-rich sunflower oil emulsion for 12 weeks in children and adolescents with depression or mixed anxiety and depressive disorder. The 2017 pilot randomized 38, with 35 completers. Some participants did not receive an antidepressant. Its later clinical and laboratory papers share the registered study. [Official registry](https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN81655012) · [2017 primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13034-017-0167-2) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2016-2017-DEPOXIN-Sunflower-Registry-and-Pilot.txt) · [2020 trial results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#sunflower-oil-emulsion-in-the-depoxin-pediatric-trial)

## Sunflower carrier in the registered lavender inhalation trial

ReBEC registered the study on December 27, 2019, after first enrollment on April 24, 2018. The record names Laszlo sunflower oil in both randomized kits. The lavender kit added lavender essential oil. Participants used the vial by inhalation and placed its remaining contents on cotton near the pillow. The registration specifies the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale and reports a within-lavender depression change without an arm-specific estimate. The formulation record leaves the sunflower oil batch and refining grade unidentified. Both arms received the same sunflower carrier. [Original source](https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-5q5t5z) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2019-RBR-5q5t5z-Lavender-Sunflower-Inhalation-Extract.txt)

## Registered sunflower comparator after prostate cancer recurrence

The official record first posted November 27, 2018. It plans HADS depression and PHQ-9 alongside a primary PSA doubling-time outcome, comparing EPA with high-oleic sunflower oil. The record shows 39 enrolled and no posted results. It is distinct from the presurgery comparison NCT02333435. Participant-level overlap and refining grade remain unverified.
