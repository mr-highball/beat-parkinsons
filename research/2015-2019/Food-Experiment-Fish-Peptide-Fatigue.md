# Fish peptide trial registration and fatigue results

## Registered fish peptide fatigue study and posted results

UMIN000034278 first disclosed September 26, 2018. Its randomized, double-blind comparison assigned fish-meat peptide tablets or placebo in healthy adults over 12 weeks. Thirty-nine enrolled. On July 10, 2019, the registry reported a favorable fatigue visual analogue scale comparison with peptide tablets without providing numerical means or a confidence interval. The registry gives no fish species. The likely matching 2020 article is listed separately.
