# Optional spearmint flavor in a levodopa and carbidopa patent

## Optional flavor list and the actual capsule example

The United States patent application US20150037405A1 was published February 5, 2015. Its optional flavor language lists spearmint, peppermint and menthol separately. The actual ingredients in the first capsule example include none of these. The reported 15-subject fasted pharmacokinetic example gives levodopa concentration and timing outcomes, without an identified spearmint exposure, Parkinson diagnosis or daily OFF diary.
