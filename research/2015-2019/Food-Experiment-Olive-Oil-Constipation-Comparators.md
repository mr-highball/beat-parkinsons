# Olive Oil Constipation Comparators

## Constipation in an extra virgin olive oil based ketogenic program

Guzel and colleagues first published online November 20, 2018. Of 476 eligible children with treatment-resistant epilepsy, 389 started a ketogenic program. Mean olive oil accounted for 80–85% of fat in the described meal plan and the oil was extra-virgin. Some children used a ketogenic formula, and other diet components and medications varied. Table 4 lists constipation in 102/389 children (26.2%) and sleep disturbances in 78/389 (20.0%). There was no oil-free assignment, and those frequencies do not isolate olive oil or describe Parkinson symptoms.
