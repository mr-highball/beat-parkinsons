# Apple and dystonia search identity

## Apple manufacturer in a spiral-drawing study

Sisti and colleagues’ 2016 online publication compared spiral drawing on iPad and Wacom in people with movement disorders and controls. Methods describe Apple as the manufacturer. Brachial dystonia was a participant subgroup. No consumed fruit, comparative dietary exposure or baked apple was studied.
