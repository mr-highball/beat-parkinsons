# Corn oil comparator outcomes in mobility and oral symptom trials

## ENRGISE walking speed and fall events

The ENRGISE pilot included mobility-impaired adults aged 70 or older. Pahor and colleagues reported the main results online December 12, 2018. The published design paired fish-oil gelcaps with matched corn-oil gelcaps supplied by Epax in 0.7 g units and separately paired losartan with cellulose placebo. The fish-oil contrast covered the fish-oil-only stratum and the factorial stratum. The adjusted 12-month 400 m walking-speed difference for fish oil versus fish-oil placebo was +0.010 m/s (95% CI −0.025 to +0.044, p=.58). Walking speed gives a human mobility measure for 0324. Stride length was unreported, and the methods give the placebo supplier without refining documentation. [Primary paper and Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6748815/) · [Registry record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02676466)

The registry results first posted in September 2019 and give stratum-specific treatment-arm safety data for 0546. In the fish-oil-only stratum, the serious FALL term reports one event in one of 71 participants receiving corn-oil gelcaps and one event in one of 109 participants receiving fish oil. The same counts appear under both the all-serious and inpatient-hospitalization subcategories. They describe the same events. The journal’s separate mechanical-fall row has nine events in nine of 102 pooled placebo recipients and nine events in eight of 122 recipients classified as fish oil. The pooled placebo category includes both corn-oil and cellulose placebos. These adverse-event tables leave everyday falls frequency and any isolated oil effect unresolved. ## Corn-derived glycerol triesters in a topical xerostomia spray

Donath and colleagues randomized 24 adults with drug-induced xerostomia and documented hyposalivation to sequences of four one-day treatment periods. Table 1 gives the Aequasyal active comparator as 94.4% glycerol triesters of oxidized fatty acids derived from corn oil, with additional ingredients. The other treatments were distinct topical sprays. Table 3 gives variable dry-mouth visual analogue outcomes. The confidence intervals for adjusted four-hour dry-mouth area-under-curve differences versus the marketed comparator all cross zero. Saliva was measured to establish baseline hyposalivation. The treatment measures concern subject-rated dryness and oral symptoms following topical lubrication. This makes the formulation an exposure-boundary lead for 0091 rather than a dietary corn-oil comparison. ## UMIN mental-fatigue trial registry history

The UMIN000037848 version history shows changes to recruitment and enrollment through February 3, 2020, with no posted results update. The Japanese MHLW mirror specifies healthy adults aged 35–59 reporting fatigue during desk work, six oral corn-oil comparator capsules per day for twelve weeks, and symptom ratings around mental workload and upon awakening. A Japanese agricultural research organization’s functional-food review template lists this trial as completed. Its table is a trial inventory. Queries by the exact Japanese study title, registry number, oral oil comparator, sponsor, funder and investigator did not locate a primary results report.
