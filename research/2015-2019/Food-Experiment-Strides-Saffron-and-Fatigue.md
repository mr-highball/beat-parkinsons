# Stride, saffron and fatigue follow-up

## Marjoram aroma within a chronic insomnia program

Published 2017. The nonrandomized community-center comparison analyzed 62 older women with chronic insomnia and excluded Parkinson disease. The intervention paired sleep education, exercise, meditation and a marjoram–orange aroma necklace. The authors report a group sleep-quality comparison t = −5.10, p < .001. There was no dried oral marjoram arm or contrast separating scent, other components and center selection. [Original Korean Academy of Nursing article](https://jkan.or.kr/journal/view.php?number=2884) · [Local text](Source-Text/2017-Chun-Marjoram-Sleep-Program.txt)

## Injected crocin and rat motor outcomes

First published December 4, 2017. Crocin pretreatment was given by injection in a malathion rat model. The primary abstract lists catalepsy, open field and rotarod and broadly reports attenuation of neurobehavioral effects. The public publisher preview exposes Figure 3. Its bar-chart descent latency is roughly 58 seconds with malathion alone, about 2 seconds with vehicle, and roughly 7 to 12 seconds in the three crocin pretreatment groups. The chart annotation and full methods remain incompletely accessible. Values are visual approximations. This is an animal catalepsy proxy after injected isolated crocin. The isolated injected constituent and toxin model differ from ground saffron and clinical rigidity. [Original indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29204935/) · [Publisher preview and figure](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11356-017-0842-0/figures/3) · [Local text](Source-Text/2017-Mohammadzadeh-Crocin-Catalepsy.txt)

## Purified ellagic acid and measured paw stride length

Published in the 2015 issue. In a 6-OHDA lesion model, eight rats per group received purified ellagic acid by gavage or vehicle. Figure 2 reports longer paw stride after ellagic acid than after lesion and vehicle (p < .001). A later review lists common sage among possible plant sources of ellagic acid. Sage was not administered and the review gives no comparable culinary dose. [Original paper Figure 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27307952/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2015-Farbood-Ellagic-Acid-Stride.txt)

## Related ellagic acid stride report and overlap

Published August 2016. Figure 3 reports longer paw stride after purified oral ellagic acid versus 6-OHDA lesion and vehicle (p < .001). The treatment lasted ten days starting at day 14. The paper shares the five investigators, animal model, thesis context and closely matching rotation data with the 2015 report. Stride figures and caption counts differ. Cohort independence is unclear, so these reports carry one study-family weight. Neither administered common sage.
