# Premeal olive oil described as a stent lubricant source record

## Premeal olive oil described as a stent lubricant

In the January–February 2018 issue, Sabău and colleagues describe postoperative care in a technical report covering 162 cases with oesophageal neoplasms. Printed page 141 describes recommending grade-unspecified olive oil before meals and a carbonated drink after meals to keep an oesophageal prosthesis clear. A 2017 Romanian textbook chapter is cited for the technique. That chapter's original body, participant-level oil uptake and oil-specific clinical outcomes are unverified. The report and 2023 cohort may overlap.
