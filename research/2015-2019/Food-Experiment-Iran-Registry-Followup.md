# Iranian dietary trial registrations and results, 2015–2019

## Licorice extract syrup trial

IRCT20120305009204N3 was registered March 12, 2018. A placebo-controlled trial compared polyphenol-rich licorice syrup with placebo syrup alongside usual Parkinson’s treatment. Petramfar and colleagues reported on 39 randomized participants in a publication first released September 28, 2019, with a 2020 issue date. The public abstract describes favorable tremor and daily activity findings at six weeks. Its description of four-month motor and rigidity results says the findings were significant while giving p > .05. The printed inequality leaves those comparisons unclear. Licorice was absent from the frozen ingredient set. [Primary registration](https://irct.ir/trial/29414) · [Primary report, PMID 31574343](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31574343/)

## Curcumin nanomicelles and sleep or fatigue

IRCT20101209005352N2 was registered August 31, 2018. The 2019 study randomized 50 people with Parkinson’s to a curcumin nanomicelle formulation or placebo. At three months the authors reported a favorable overall Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index result and an inconclusive fatigue comparison (p = .42). The Results paragraph gives the placebo baseline sleep score as 9.49, while Table 2 gives 9.40. The measures and preparation differ from the frozen grated-turmeric claim about daytime sleepiness (`0821`). A 2021 meeting abstract repeats the same sample, interventions and main outcome values. [Primary registration](https://irct.ir/trial/5726) · [2019 primary report, Table 2](https://journals.sbmu.ac.ir/neuroscience/article/download/26302/4) · [Local article text and tables](Source-Text/2019-Maghbooli-Nanomicelle-Curcumin.txt) · [2021 meeting report](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jns.2021.119586)

## Nine-month curcumin nanomicelle registration

The separate June 8, 2018 registration IRCT20171123037600N1 specified a nine-month add-on comparison of nanomicelle curcumin versus placebo with MDS-UPDRS outcomes. The recruitment and formulation match the 2022 Ghodsi pilot. This is a different registration from the three-month sleep and fatigue study. [Primary registration](https://irct.ir/trial/28424) · [2022 outcome note](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#nanomicelle-curcumin-as-an-add-on)

## Mediterranean diet trials and protein timing

IRCT20141108019853N4 was registered December 5, 2018, with Parkinson’s severity and serum antioxidant capacity as outcomes. IRCT20141108019853N5 followed on January 28, 2019, with cognition as an outcome. The two reports share the location, design, enrollment, intervention and several authors. The extent of participant overlap is unclear. A separate registration, IRCT20180203038593N1, was posted January 7, 2019, for a protein distribution intervention and motor function. Recruitment is marked complete and no study-specific results were located by ID or title.
