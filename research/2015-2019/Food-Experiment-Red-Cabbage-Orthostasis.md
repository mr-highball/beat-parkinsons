# Red cabbage and orthostatic symptoms

## 2018 Assigned red cabbage in mixed vegetable diets

Peluso and colleagues assigned higher or lower amounts of five vegetables together, including 800 versus 152 g of red cabbage weekly. The outcomes cover blood, saliva and platelet markers. The study supplies no pressure or standing-symptom outcome. [Original Methods and food allocation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6196889/) · [Local original text extract](Source-Text/2018-Peluso-Mixed-Vegetables-Extract.txt)

## 2018 Fermented and pickled mixed vegetables registration

The registry compares fermented vegetables, a nonfermented vegetable arm and usual diet. Its systolic pressure results appeared in 2023. The article describes pickled cabbage or cucumber in the nonfermented arm without identifying the cabbage cultivar. The result-table arm denominators differ between the two sources. [Official record and posted results](Source-Text/Trials/NCT03407794.txt) · [2022 journal result](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Red-Cabbage-Orthostasis.md#2022-fermented-and-pickled-mixed-vegetables)
