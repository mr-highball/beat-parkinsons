# Thyme Anxiety Comparators

## Oral thyme leaf extract and elevated plus maze

Komaki and colleagues tested aqueous suspensions of a dried hydroalcoholic extract made from Thymus vulgaris leaves in male rats. The two higher tested amounts increased elevated-plus-maze open-arm entries versus saline (p<.05 and p<.01), and the highest increased open-arm time (p<.05). Locomotor and closed-arm entry comparisons were inconclusive. A rodent maze records exploratory behavior after a concentrated extract, with limited transfer to human anxiety after consuming dried leaves.
