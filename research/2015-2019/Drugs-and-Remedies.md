# Drugs and Remedies

### 2017 — Repeat-dose opicapone and a moderate meal

BIA-91067-128 first posted as NCT03116308 on April 17, 2017, after study completion in 2015. The June 2017 congress report describes 28 healthy volunteers receiving opicapone for 12 days, with the moderate meal on day 10 and day 9 as the fasted comparison. The fed-to-fasted ratios were 38.21% for peak concentration and 68.70% for area under the concentration curve through the last measurement. The authors reported no clear change in maximum or 24-hour COMT inhibition. Neither the conference report nor registry identifies the foods or compares symptoms in people with Parkinson’s. [Primary report](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/effect-of-food-on-opicapone-pharmacokinetics-and-pharmacodynamics/) · [Registry and local text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT03116308.txt) · [FDA clinical pharmacology review, Figure 6](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2020/212489Orig1s000ClinPharmR.pdf#page=23)

### 2015 — Creatine and long-term progression

The LS-1 randomized trial assigned 1,741 participants already receiving dopaminergic therapy to creatine or placebo. The study stopped for futility, with no improvement on its composite clinical progression measure. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25668262/)

### 2016 — Long-term follow-up of early versus delayed rasagiline

A follow-up of ADAGIO participants found no significant long-term differences on UPDRS scores or times to selected milestones between the original early and delayed groups. Selective participation and treatment after the randomized study limit interpretation. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27431201/)

### 2017 — Exenatide phase 2 signal

A small randomized placebo-controlled trial reported a favorable difference on the off-medication motor examination after 48 weeks of treatment and a 12-week washout. The design left persistent symptomatic effects possible and motivated phase 3 testing. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28781108/)

### 2019 — Levodopa timing and progression in LEAP

A placebo-controlled delayed-start trial found no significant difference in UPDRS change at 80 weeks between early and delayed levodopa. The result addresses disease modification over the study interval. Levodopa improved symptoms in the placebo-controlled phase. [Primary source](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1809983)
