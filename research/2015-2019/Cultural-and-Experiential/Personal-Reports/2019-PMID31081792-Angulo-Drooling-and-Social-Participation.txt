Title: Parkinson patient accounts of drooling, food spills and social participation
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6700625/
PMID: 31081792
PMCID: PMC6700625
DOI: 10.3233/JPD-181462
Publication date: 2019-05-07
Source kind: Participant experiences from support groups and informal interviews
Source depth: Original article body, section 4 and the social participation discussion
Date basis: Publisher first online date. The issue followed in July.
Design: Review with firsthand discussion material from 44 people with Parkinson disease

One participant described avoiding public settings because of food spills and drooling and connected the experience with embarrassment. Other reported experiences in the source distinguish nonmotor drooling, tremor-related spills and fear of being judged. The paper identifies these accounts as coming from support groups and informal interviews. It does not attribute the episode to a named food or assess drooling severity before and after eating. Paraphrased to retain the source context without reproducing the participant's wording.
