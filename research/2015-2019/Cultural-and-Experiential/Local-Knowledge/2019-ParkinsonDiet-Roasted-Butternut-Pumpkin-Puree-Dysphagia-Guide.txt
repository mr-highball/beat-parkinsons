The Parkinson Diet culinary resource (published 2019-02-07; revised 2026-02-26) presents a roasted butternut pumpkin puree recipe (500 g peeled/cubed butternut squash roasted with olive oil and blended to a smooth puree) specifically adapted for people with Parkinson's disease experiencing swallowing difficulties (dysphagia).

The guide instructs caregivers and patients to verify bolus consistency using International Dysphagia Diet Standardisation Initiative (IDDSI) Fork Drip and Spoon Tilt checks so the roasted squash forms a cohesive, smooth puree rather than dry, fibrous, or stringy pieces that increase choking risk. While demonstrating practical culinary utility of roasted and pureed butternut squash in dysphagia meal planning, texture modification is a compensatory swallowing safety strategy rather than evidence that butternut squash physiologically improves Parkinson's swallowing mechanics.
