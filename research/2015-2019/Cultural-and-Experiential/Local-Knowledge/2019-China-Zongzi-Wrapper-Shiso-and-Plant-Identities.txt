Title: Plant leaves for wrapping zongzi in China — shiso and botanical identity
Source kind: Local knowledge
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6907129/
PMID: 31829257
PMCID: PMC6907129
DOI: 10.1186/s13002-019-0339-7
Date basis: Published 2019-12-11.
Coverage: Factual summary of the original ethnobotanical methods, Table 1 and culinary introduction.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The study recorded 57 plant species used to wrap zongzi. Its table identifies Perilla frutescens in Liaoning, with culinary wrapper use and reported uses for colds, emesis and digestive complaints. Preparation as a wrapper supplies no measured oral perilla amount. The named health uses are customary indications.

The introductory seasoning example names Chinese jujube, Ziziphus jujuba, separately from date palm. The ginger-family plants mentioned elsewhere include Alpinia taxa. Botanical family membership establishes neither Zingiber officinale exposure nor a shared preparation. The ethnobotanical record does not include an intervention or a Parkinson symptom measure.
