El Hajjam and colleagues studied 72 farms across four sites, including Bouhouda in Taounate. They report that Ouled Frej predominantly supplies fresh fruit, while the other surveyed sites use and market much of their harvest dried. Nabout is the most common listed cultivar in the Bouhouda planting profiles and is discussed for dried autumn figs. The work identifies cross-site similarities among some cultivar names. It supplies no ingredient or symptom observations from the households of the 2009 report.

Open trail: Use the locality and cultivar distinctions in fruit-source searches. A cultivar name alone leaves the specific preservation method and individual exposure open.
