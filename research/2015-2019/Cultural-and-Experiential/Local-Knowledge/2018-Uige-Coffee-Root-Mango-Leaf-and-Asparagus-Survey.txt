Date basis: Publication date of the original Angolan ethnobotanical survey.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Reported direction: Customary uses are described without a measured Parkinson symptom direction.
Authenticity: Table 1 in the original survey identifies plant taxa, plant parts and reported uses.
Efficacy evidence: Interviewed or compiled uses give no verified treatment response or comparator.
Archive scope: Distilled original Table 1 entries and botanical identity limits.

The survey records a Coffea root preparation as a mouthwash for toothache, as distinct from drinking brewed coffee. A different listed plant provides a coffee substitute. Mango leaf preparations appear in traditional use accounts and do not identify a fruit consumption effect. The Asparagus entries name distinct or unresolved species and cannot be taken as culinary Asparagus officinalis exposure. The source retains the preparation and local context without identifying the participants.
