Title: Lower Mekong outpatient survey of reported traditional and complementary medicine use
Source kind: Local knowledge and patient-reported use survey
PMID: 26952043
PMCID: PMC4782577
DOI: 10.1186/s12906-016-1078-0
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4782577/
Date basis: Published 2016-03-08. The surveyed clinical population and findings are dated separately from any historical practice.
Archive scope: Original country-level study methods and reported use, with limits on ingredient attribution. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

Adults attending clinics for chronic conditions in Cambodia, Thailand and Vietnam answered the I-CAM-Q about provider consultations, products and self-help practices. The pooled product examples include Curcuma longa among other plants. The reported helpfulness and duration are aggregate responses for categories of treatment. The paper does not assign Curcuma usage or response to an individual Parkinson patient, distinguish dietary ground turmeric, or provide a turmeric-specific pain outcome.
