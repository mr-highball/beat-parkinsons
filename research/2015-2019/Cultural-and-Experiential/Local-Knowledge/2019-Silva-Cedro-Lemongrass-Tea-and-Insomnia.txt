The survey used formal and informal interviews and plant identification in Cedro, Goiás, Brazil. Twenty-five residents were interviewed across twenty-five households. The species table identifies capim-limão and citronela in this entry as Cymbopogon citratus, distinct from the separately described Melissa officinalis. Table 1 records the leaves prepared as tea for internal use for insomnia, calming, anxiety and depression.

The paper documents customary indications rather than symptom changes after a standardized brew. The number of informants supporting this specific indication, timing of use, daytime sleepiness, and Parkinson status are unreported. A bedtime indication gives no directional observation of daytime sleepiness. The historical clinical counterpart is research/1985-1989/Source-Text/1986-Leite-Lemongrass-Tea-Sleep-Extract.txt.
