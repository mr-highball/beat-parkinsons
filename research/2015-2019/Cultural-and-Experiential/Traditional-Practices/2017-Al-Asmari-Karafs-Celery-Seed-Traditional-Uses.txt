Title: Traditional Unani, Persian, Arab, Ayurvedic, and Uyghur uses of Apium graveolens seeds (Karafs / Tukhm-e-Karafs / Ajmod)
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5414449/
Source kind: Traditional-practice record
Publication date: 2017
Date basis: Peer-reviewed phytopharmacological review publication year (Pharmacognosy Reviews 2017; PMID:28503047, PMC5414449), supplemented by Unani and Uyghur scholarly records (PMC5871174, PMC8642661, PMC11990171).
Reviewed: 2026-11-25 UTC
Reported direction: Customary traditional uses for rheumatic and gouty body pain, menstrual pain (Usr-i-Tamth), diuresis/urinary complaints, digestive gas, and sleep disturbances.
Authenticity: Traceable peer-reviewed open-access ethnobotanical reviews (Al-Asmari et al. 2017, PMC5414449; Sultana et al. 2017, PMC5871174; Naushad et al. 2021, PMC8642661; Chen et al. 2025, PMC11990171).
Efficacy evidence: Traditional and historical practice context only; does not establish clinical Parkinson efficacy for ground culinary celery seed.

Classical Arab, Persian, Unani (Karafs / Tukhm-e-Karafs / Ajmod), Ayurvedic (Ajamoda), Uyghur, and European herbal traditions prominently use the dried ripe fruit (seed) of Apium graveolens L. as an aromatic spice and medicinal botanical. Al-Asmari et al. (2017, PMC5414449) document traditional applications for body pain, hepatic/spleen complaints, brain disorders, diuresis, and sleep disturbances. Unani gynecological manuscripts list celery seed among emmenagogue, antispasmodic, and analgesic herbs for menstrual pain (Sultana et al. 2017, PMC5871174) and as a renal diuretic and deobstruent (Naushad et al. 2021, PMC8642661), while Uyghur and European phytotherapy similarly employ celery seeds for diuresis, gout, arthritis, and uncomplicated urinary complaints (Chen et al. 2025, PMC11990171; Sarshar et al. 2018, PMID:29496563). Traditional descriptions do not separate Parkinson's diagnoses, modern neurogenic bladder urgency, or controlled culinary ground-seed dosing.
