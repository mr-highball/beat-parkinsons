Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The investigators recorded 1,590 questionnaires from 450 interviews, with 917 single-plant remedies. Two citations report an oral decoction of crushed Zingiber officinale rhizomes for common cold and cough. Olive oil appears as an added ingredient with elder or lemon preparations, with no separate attribution or oil grade.

Here local bay leaf means Pimenta racemosa. Local basil records include Ocimum gratissimum and Ocimum micranthum. The table identifies black sage as Cordia curassavica and saffron or hardi as Curcuma longa. Those names cannot establish exposure to culinary laurel, sweet basil, common sage, or true saffron. The turmeric reports, including use for afterbirth, have a different indication from the frozen claims. These are reported practices, with no measured clinical treatment response.
