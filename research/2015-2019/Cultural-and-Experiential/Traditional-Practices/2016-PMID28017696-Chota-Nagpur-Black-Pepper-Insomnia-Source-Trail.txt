The original abstract reports open and semi-structured interviews with 65 traditional practitioners in the Chota Nagpur Plateau during fieldwork from 2012 through 2015. The later Romanian military medicine review, reference 41, attributes to this study an intranasal black-pepper and honey preparation for insomnia. This is a different route from seasoning eaten as food. The recipe-level description remains secondary until the primary table can be read and it carries no verified sleep response.
