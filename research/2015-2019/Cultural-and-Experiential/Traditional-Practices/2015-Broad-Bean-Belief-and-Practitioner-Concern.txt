The Turkish report describes a belief that green broad-bean pods help Parkinson treatment. The interviewed clinician disputes the benefit claim and says the food's medication-related constituent complicates planned dosing. Tremor is discussed separately as a Parkinson symptom, without a bean–tremor comparison.

Reported direction: Claimed community benefit and contrary medication-management opinion; no measured symptom effect.
