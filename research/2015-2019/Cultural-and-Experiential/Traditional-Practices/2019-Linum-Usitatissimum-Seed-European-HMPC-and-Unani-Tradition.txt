Traditional and pharmacopoeial record:
Whole and crushed/ground Linum usitatissimum L. seeds (Lini semen) hold longstanding European Union Herbal Medicinal Products Committee (EMA/HMPC) recognition as a bulk-forming laxative for habitual constipation and as a mucilage demulcent for mild gastrointestinal discomfort (reviewed in Thumann et al. 2019, PMID:31408679). Traditional Greco-Arab (Unani) medical literature also lists flaxseed preparations among supportive botanical remedies for benign prostatic urinary complaints (reviewed in PMC9051355, PMID:35509487, 2022).
Safety and Parkinson's practice precautions: Traditional monographs explicitly require taking mucilage-rich ground flaxseed with ample liquid (typically >=150 mL per spoonful) to avoid esophageal or intestinal obstruction, and spacing ingestion apart from oral pharmaceuticals (critical for Parkinson's levodopa absorption).
