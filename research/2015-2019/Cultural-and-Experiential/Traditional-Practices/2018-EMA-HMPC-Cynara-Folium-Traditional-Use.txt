The European Medicines Agency Committee on Herbal Medicinal Products (EMA/HMPC/194014/2017, adopted 27 March 2018) establishes traditional herbal medicinal use for globe artichoke leaf (Cynara cardunculus L., syn. Cynara scolymus L., folium) strictly for symptomatic relief of digestive disorders such as dyspepsia with fullness, bloating, and flatulence. While older European and Mediterranean herbal records historically cited choleretic, bowel-regulating, and diuretic uses, HMPC explicitly declined to grant a diuretic or constipation indication, noting mild diarrhea, abdominal spasms, nausea, and heartburn among possible undesirable effects (with sporadic constipation mentions in safety logs). The monograph applies to dried leaf and leaf extracts rather than steamed or cooked edible artichoke hearts.
