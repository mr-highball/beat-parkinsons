Remedy 14 uses dau den, a Vietnamese black-bean term, with lotus seed, Erythrina and mulberry leaves and other ingredients including black sesame. Four components overlap the 2020 folk-therapy article. The exact lineage and taxonomy remain uncertain, so these texts belong to a related customary-use trail without separate clinical observations.

The 2020 text lists a neighboring cowpea recipe separately. Its general survey of 40 people concerns attitudes and use of traditional methods. It supplies no preparation-specific response or canned bean exposure. Vietnamese crop and food references apply the local black-bean term to more than one Vigna taxon.
