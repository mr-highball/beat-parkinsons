Title: Iranian Traditional Medicine Uses of Beta vulgaris (Selgh/Choghondar) for Constipation, Pain, and Psychological Complaints
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29521241/
Source kind: Published ethnomedical historical review (PMID 29521241)
Publication date: 2019
Date basis: Peer-reviewed journal publication year 2019 reviewing historical Persian/Iranian Traditional Medicine texts (historical tradition predates publication by centuries).
Reviewed: 2026-11-24 UTC
Reported direction: Traditional favorable claims for constipation, gastrointestinal/joint pain, headaches, and psychological symptoms.
Authenticity: Peer-reviewed ethnomedical literature synthesis anchored to PMID 29521241.
Efficacy evidence: Customary/traditional context only; historical citation does not establish efficacy for Parkinson's disease symptoms.

Peer-reviewed synthesis of Iranian Traditional Medicine (ITM) sources (Hamedi & Honarvar 2019, PMID 29521241) documenting traditional culinary and medicinal applications of Beta vulgaris (known as Selgh or Choghondar) for constipation, intestinal and joint pain, headaches, and psychological distress. Publication date (2019) is distinct from the centuries-old historical manuscripts summarized; traditional use records cultural persistence and does not substitute for controlled clinical evaluation in Parkinson's disease.
