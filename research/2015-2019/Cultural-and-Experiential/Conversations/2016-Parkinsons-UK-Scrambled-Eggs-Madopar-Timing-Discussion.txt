Title: Parkinson's UK discussion on timing Madopar around scrambled eggs and beans for breakfast
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/madopar/9556
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2016-12-02
Date basis: Posts #11, #17, and #19 timestamps (2016-12-02 to 2016-12-04) on public Parkinson's UK Discourse thread.

In December 2016 posts (#11, #17, #19) within a public Parkinson's UK forum thread on Madopar, a newly medicated contributor asked how to time Madopar around a daily breakfast of scrambled eggs and beans on toast, noting confusion over conflicting spacing advice and wondering about taking levodopa one hour before eating. A spouse caregiver replied that large protein-containing meals 'kill' their husband's levodopa effect, requiring dispersible rescue doses when dining out or anticipating freezing in crowds. The forum moderation team quoted page 24 of Parkinson's UK's 'Drug Treatments for Parkinson's' booklet, explaining that protein—found mainly in meat, fish, eggs, cheese, and beans—can interfere with levodopa absorption in some people and that taking medication at least 30 minutes before eating may help without eliminating necessary dietary protein. All handles and names have been omitted.
