Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Account of Daily Mixed Berries Including Blackberries Supporting Bowel Regularity
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/glucosamine-clondroitin/8205
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2015-04-21
Date basis: Public Discourse thread posts spanning 2015-03-27 to 2015-04-21 (blackberry breakfast mention in Post #9 on 2015-04-21).

In Post #9 (April 21, 2015) of a 10-post public Parkinson's UK forum thread discussing hip pain and dietary habits (/t/glucosamine-clondroitin/8205), a person with Parkinson disease reported eating a daily breakfast combining blueberries, prunes, blackberries, and raspberries, noting that this routine kept their bowel movements regular. Because blackberries were consumed alongside prunes (a known sorbitol/fiber laxative) and two other berries without specifying fresh vs frozen preparation, this firsthand report provides lived-experience context rather than isolated evidence for frozen blackberries.
