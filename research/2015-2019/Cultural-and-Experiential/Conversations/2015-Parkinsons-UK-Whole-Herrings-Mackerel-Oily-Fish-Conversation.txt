Title: Public Parkinson's forum discussion favoring fresh herrings and mackerel over fish-oil capsules
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/shark-oil/8779
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2015
Date basis: Opening post date (2015-12-14) on the public Parkinson's UK Discourse forum thread.

In a December 2015 public thread on the Parkinson's UK community forum asking where to source shark liver oil and alternative therapies for Parkinson's disease, a peer responder advised eating affordable whole oily fish instead—specifically recommending fresh mackerel, herrings (noted as inexpensive and readily available fresh), or pan-fried sprats—rather than expensive shark-oil supplements. A second contributor agreed that eating whole oily fish is preferable to costly alternative supplements, recounting prior out-of-pocket spending on unproven alternative remedies for pre-diagnosis gait symptoms that yielded no physical gait benefit. No participant reported measured Parkinson's symptom changes from eating herring.
