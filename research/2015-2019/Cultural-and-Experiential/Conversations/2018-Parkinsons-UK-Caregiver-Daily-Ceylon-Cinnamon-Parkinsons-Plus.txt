Title: Parkinson's UK caregiver account of adding daily Ceylon cinnamon alongside persistent gait and fatigue symptoms
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/recently-diagnosed-parkinsons-possible-parkinsons-plus/13814
Source kind: conversations
Publication date: 2018-06-04
Date basis: Exact public forum post timestamp (Parkinson's UK Discourse topic 13814, post #7 dated 2018-06-04).

In a 2018 Parkinson's UK discussion on atypical parkinsonism (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/recently-diagnosed-parkinsons-possible-parkinsons-plus/13814, post dated 2018-06-04), an adult child caring for an 84-year-old parent with suspected Parkinson's Plus reported stirring half a teaspoon of Ceylon cinnamon (explicitly noting Ceylon as the safer low-coumarin type) into daily breakfast along with multivitamins and seated cycling exercise. Despite routine use, the caregiver described ongoing small-step shuffling gait, unsteadiness requiring a walking stick, occasional drooling, tiredness, emotional distress, and cognitive muddling—preserving a grounded, no-miracle real-world perspective alongside preclinical optimism.
