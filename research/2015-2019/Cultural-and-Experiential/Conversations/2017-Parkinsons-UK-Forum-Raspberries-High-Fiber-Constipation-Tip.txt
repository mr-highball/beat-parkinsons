Title: Parkinson's UK Forum: High-fiber raspberries suggested alongside prune juice for PD constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/diet-exercise/9674
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2017-01-24
Date basis: Post timestamp (2017-01-24; opening thread 2017-01-21) inspected directly via Parkinson's UK Forum public thread record.

In a January 2017 Parkinson's UK Forum diet-and-exercise thread, a participant responding to another member's Parkinson's constipation difficulty specifically suggested eating raspberries because they contain "more fibre than you can shake a stick at," alongside prune juice. The commenter humorously cautioned that strong laxative foods (specifically prune juice) should be timed during optimal motor ON periods rather than PD OFF periods when mobility and clothing fasteners are harder to manage. The exchange reflects practical community awareness of raspberries as a high-fiber whole fruit in PD constipation management, but represents a peer dietary suggestion rather than a documented clinical outcome.
