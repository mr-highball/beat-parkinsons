Title: Parkinson's UK public forum firsthand mention of bottled black seed oil for Parkinson's throat catarrh and cough
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/cough/6587
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2019-11-06
Date basis: Exact post timestamp (2019-11-06) of post #34 on the public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread 'Cough' (thread opened 2013-01-16).

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussion about persistent dry tickly cough and throat clearing in Parkinson's disease (thread opened January 2013, post dated November 6, 2019), a participant experiencing what they termed 'the Parky bark' reported buying a bottle of black seed oil (Nigella sativa oil) after watching online videos and stated that it felt helpful alongside spraying sea-salt water into each nostril to clear throat catarrh and keeping throat lozenges on hand. Other contributors in the same thread noted that chronic cough in Parkinson's disease was often linked to reflux or medications such as ACE inhibitors or SSRIs. All usernames and personal details are omitted. This single uncontrolled firsthand account documents real-world PD community uptake of black seed oil (not ground or toasted culinary seed) with concurrent remedies for cough rather than any frozen Round 5 motor or non-motor efficacy endpoint.
