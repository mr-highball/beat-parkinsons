Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussions mentioning daily fresh strawberries during PD constipation management
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-any-cure/14613
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2018-07-14
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2018-07-14; strawberry reply 2018-07-15; cross-referenced corroborating thread https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-and-low-sodium/35120 opened 2023-01-25 with reply 2023-03-01).

In a July 2018 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread seeking constipation remedies compatible with PD medications, one participant reported eating abundant fruit — at least a punnet of grapes or strawberries daily — while limiting meat intake as part of their bowel-regularity routine. Other participants in the same discussion emphasized prunes, leafy greens, and prescribed osmotic laxatives (macrogol sachets), noting that severe constipation and bloating often required medical management or surgical hernia repair after straining. In a separate 2023 Parkinson's UK thread on constipation and low sodium (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-and-low-sodium/35120, reply dated 2023-03-01), another participant similarly remarked that a daily serving of strawberries seemed to help them stay regular. All personal identifiers and account handles are omitted. These uncontrolled community remarks provide real-world dietary context for fresh strawberries (not specifically frozen strawberries) alongside conventional laxative regimens, but cannot establish causal efficacy.
