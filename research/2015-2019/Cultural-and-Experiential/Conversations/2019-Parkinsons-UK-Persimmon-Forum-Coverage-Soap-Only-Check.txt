Title: Parkinson UK public forum coverage audit for persimmon confirming zero dietary symptom accounts (topical persimmon soap only)
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/parkinsons-body-smell/22028
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2019-12-15
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on the sole Parkinson UK public forum thread matching persimmon (2019-12-15).

A direct search of the public Parkinson UK Community Forum for persimmon during Round 6 review identified only a single thread (opened 15 December 2019) discussing Parkinson-associated body odor and topical Japanese persimmon soap, with zero threads or firsthand patient accounts evaluating dietary raw persimmon consumption for motor or non-motor Parkinson symptoms. All personal identifiers are omitted. This negative community-coverage check reinforces that patient experiential data on dietary raw persimmon in Parkinson disease are absent, whereas clinical gastroenterology records highlight phytobezoar risks relevant to delayed gastric emptying in older and Parkinson-affected adults.
