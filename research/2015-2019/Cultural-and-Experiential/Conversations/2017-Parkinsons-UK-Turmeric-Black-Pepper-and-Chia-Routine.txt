Title: Parkinson's UK Forum discussion on turmeric, black pepper, chia seeds and exercise routine
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/turmeric-dramatic-results/9716
Publication date: 2017-02-04
Date basis: Opening post date shown on the public forum (2017-02-04); replies reviewed through 2017-02-13.
Reviewed: 2026-11-21 UTC

A participant reported perceived "dramatic" improvements after building a daily routine centered around turmeric alongside 30 minutes of daily aerobic exercise (cycling/elliptical plus brisk walking) and other dietary additions. When another member asked which specific symptoms had improved, subsequent replies discussed adding black pepper (speculating about bioavailability/blood-brain barrier), hemp oil, vitamin D3, beetroot, blueberries, and turmeric in food rather than capsules.

Mid-thread, two other participants introduced chia seeds: one reported that chia seeds "worked wonders" personally for constipation, cramps, and muscle spasms, and another reported putting chia seeds on morning cereal and perceiving help with tremors. A later reply noted temporary walking improvement after prolonged walking accompanied by fatigue. Because exercise, multiple foods, supplements, and unverified dosing were combined without blinding or objective ratings, individual ingredient effects cannot be separated from exercise, baseline fluctuation, or expectation.
