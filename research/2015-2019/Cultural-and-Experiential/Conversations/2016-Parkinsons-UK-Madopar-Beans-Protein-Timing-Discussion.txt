Title: Public Parkinson UK forum discussion on timing beans and dietary protein around Madopar (levodopa)
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/madopar/9556
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2016-11-21
Date basis: Opening post date on public Parkinson UK Discourse thread (protein-and-beans discussion posted December 2-4, 2016).

In a November-December 2016 Parkinson UK forum thread on starting Madopar (levodopa/benserazide), a newly diagnosed participant who regularly ate eggs and beans on toast for breakfast asked how to time levodopa around dietary protein. Experienced community members advised taking levodopa 30 to 60 minutes before meals or an hour afterward, with one care partner reporting that large protein-heavy restaurant meals blunted her husband medication response. A forum moderator also quoted the Parkinson UK Drug Treatments booklet noting that dietary protein (including meat, fish, eggs, cheese, and beans) may interfere with levodopa absorption for some individuals and advising discussion of dose timing rather than eliminating protein. Limits: Groups beans together with animal proteins (eggs, meat, cheese) without distinguishing plant-legume amino acid kinetics or measuring individual OFF-time durations. All participant handles were omitted.
