Title: Parkinson's UK forum firsthand account of brewed peppermint tea providing brief interim relief for PD constipation-related abdominal pain
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/tell-me-what-worked-for-you/20827
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2019-09-15
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2019-09-15; firsthand peppermint tea reply posted 2019-09-16).

In a September 2019 Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussion on managing Parkinson's constipation and severe abdominal cramps, a participant living with Parkinson's disease and camptocormia described daily stomach cramps, constipation requiring Movicol titration, trapped wind, and a bruised-like morning abdominal pain. They reported drinking a cup of hot water or brewed peppermint tea on waking, noting that it helped in the interim but did not clear the discomfort, which returned throughout the day. All personal identifiers are omitted. This real-world firsthand report contrasts temporary warm-infusion comfort with persistent PD gastrointestinal dysmotility and does not establish clinical efficacy.
