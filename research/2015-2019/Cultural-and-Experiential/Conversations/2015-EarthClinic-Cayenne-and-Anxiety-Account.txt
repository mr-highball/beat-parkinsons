Access and follow-up: Seek independently identifiable preparation and symptom outcomes, including nonresponse and worsening reports.

The firsthand account describes an anxiety and dizziness episode followed by perceived relief after drinking water mixed with cayenne. The author also reported an earlier change in symptoms with sleep and alcohol, symptom recurrence, and worsening after further alcohol before using cayenne. The record does not establish whether change is attributable to cayenne or whether the reported culinary form matches the experiment. The page's other selected posts address different conditions and do not provide corroboration of this anxiety outcome.
