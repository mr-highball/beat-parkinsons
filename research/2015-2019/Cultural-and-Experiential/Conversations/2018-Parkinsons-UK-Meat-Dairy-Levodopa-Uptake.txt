Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Reports on Meat and Dairy Impairing Levodopa Uptake and Meal-Timing Dystonia
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/effect-of-diet-on-levadopa-uptake/13095
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2018-04-20
Date basis: Thread creation and reply dates (2018-04-20 to 2018-05-28).

Public Parkinson's UK forum thread (9 posts, April-May 2018) in which the initiating contributor reported severe impairment of levodopa effectiveness after dairy and meat meals (describing vegetable protein as less dramatic). A care partner whose spouse took Sinemet every 2 hours described restricting protein to one meal per day and planning for roughly two hours of post-meal inactivity, relying on vegetable-based dishes during active daytime hours. Another contributor reported abdominal tightness and severe head-to-toe dystonia cramps lasting 20 minutes to 2-3 hours occurring 1-2 hours after regular meals around medication times.
