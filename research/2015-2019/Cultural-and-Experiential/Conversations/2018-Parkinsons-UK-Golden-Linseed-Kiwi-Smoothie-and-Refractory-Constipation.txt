Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion of golden linseed (flaxseed), kiwi fruit smoothie, bladder pressure, and refractory constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-any-cure/14613
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2018-07-14
Date basis: Opening post date on public Parkinson's UK Discourse thread (relevant posts dated 2018-07-15 through 2019-01-25).

Reported direction: Mixed experiential accounts: two participants/caregivers reported constipation relief from golden linseed (one noting secondary urinary relief from reduced bowel pressure), one reported bowel regularity with a multi-ingredient smoothie including kiwi fruit, and another reported no dietary benefit under heavy Parkinson's medication.
Authenticity: Publicly accessible Parkinson's UK community forum thread inspected on 2026-11-26; all member names and handles omitted for privacy.
Efficacy evidence: Anecdotal community observations with multi-food and prescription co-interventions; does not establish clinical efficacy.

Thread summary (selected posts, 2018-07-15 to 2019-01-25):
- Firsthand report (2018-07-15): A participant stated that a Parkinson's nurse recommended golden linseed (1 tablespoon mixed into yogurt or sprinkled on cereal/muesli) as a natural laxative, sometimes alternated with prunes.
- Caregiver report (2018-07-16): A spouse reported that a partner with Parkinson's was advised to take 2 tablespoons of golden linseed with breakfast cereal, describing improved constipation as well as reduced urinary trouble attributed to bowel pressure on the bladder, without adding extra medication.
- Firsthand report (2019-01-22): Another participant reported staying regular with a daily pint of homemade smoothie containing spinach, parsley, ginger, apple, banana, mango or kiwi fruit, lemon juice, and lime juice (switching to bran flakes if out of smoothie).
- Contrasting no-benefit report (2019-01-25): A replying participant noted trying healthy dietary foods with little benefit, attributing persistent constipation to their Parkinson's medication load, while other participants reported needing prescription macrogol (Laxido).
