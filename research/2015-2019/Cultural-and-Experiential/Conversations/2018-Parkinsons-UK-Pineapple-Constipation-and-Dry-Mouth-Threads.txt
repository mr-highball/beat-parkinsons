Title: Parkinson UK forum mentions of fresh pineapple as a post-meal constipation tip and slow-eaten pineapple cubes for dry sore mouth
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-any-cure/14613
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2018-07-14
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson UK public forum thread 14613 (2018-07-14), paired with Parkinson UK thread 21122 (2019-10-08).

Across two public Parkinson UK Community Forum threads inspected during Round 6 review—one on constipation opened in July 2018 (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-any-cure/14613) and one on sore dry mouth opened in October 2019 (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/sore-dry-mouth/21122)—community members briefly referenced fresh pineapple outside the better-known nurse-suggested pineapple-juice tip for thick oral secretions (threads 3857 and 883). In the 2018 constipation thread, one contributor suggested fresh pineapple as a post-meal dessert (describing it as an old Asian digestive remedy) as an alternative to aloe vera, psyllium, and occasional mini-enemas without reporting isolated constipation outcomes. In the 2019 dry-mouth thread, a participant experiencing severe oral dryness and soreness noted slowly eating pineapple cubes while primarily attributing pain and sleep relief to topical oral analgesic gel. All account names and personal identifiers are omitted. Neither thread provides controlled symptom data for raw or grilled pineapple in Parkinson disease.
