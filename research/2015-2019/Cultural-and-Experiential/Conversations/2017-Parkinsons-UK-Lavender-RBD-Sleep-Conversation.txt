Title: Bedtime lavender diffusion suggested in a Parkinson's UK thread on REM sleep disorder and sleep fatigue
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/rem-sleep-disorder/9846
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2017-04-06
Date basis: Opening post dated April 6, 2017; relevant reply mentioning lavender diffusion dated August 7, 2017.

In an opening post dated April 6, 2017, a person with Parkinson's disease described severe REM sleep behavior disorder (vivid violent nightmares, leaping out of bed 3-4 times nightly despite clonazepam and prolonged-release melatonin) causing severe daytime exhaustion. In a reply dated August 7, 2017 responding to sleep-onset insomnia and Parkinson's fatigue within the thread, another participant shared six personal bedtime measures including evening caffeine avoidance, a relaxing wind-down hour, diffusing lavender essential oil in the bedroom for relaxation, and consulting a GP if persistent.
Observation and limits: The thread reflects community interest in lavender for relaxation around Parkinson's sleep disturbance, but specifies bedroom essential oil diffusion bundled with sleep hygiene rather than oral dried or brewed culinary lavender, and reports no before-and-after change in dream enactment frequency or insomnia scores. All participant handles were omitted for privacy.
