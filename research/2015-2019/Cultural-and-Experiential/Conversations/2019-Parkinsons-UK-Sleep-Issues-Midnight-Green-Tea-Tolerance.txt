Thread summary (post #10, 2019-11-07):
- Within a wider Parkinson's UK discussion of severe sleep fragmentation, nocturia, and turning stiffness, one participant diagnosed 20 years earlier described sleeping consistently well in a dark, unheated bedroom, taking levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone (Stanek) around 6 pm and again at midnight with green tea before sleeping soundly until morning.
- Contrasts directly with the 2013 Parkinson's UK report of green tea causing 4-5 nightly awakenings, underscoring dose, timing, and individual sensitivity variation.
