Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Mention of Dietary Nicotine in Peppers and Tomatoes
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/battle-briefing/9818
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2017-08-22
Date basis: Public Discourse thread post #155 timestamped 2017-08-22 within thread /t/battle-briefing/9818.

In Post #155 (August 22, 2017) of the public Parkinson's UK forum thread 'Battle briefing' (/t/battle-briefing/9818), a community member responding to a discussion of Solanaceae foods humorously remarked that they had not previously realized trace nicotine occurs in tomatoes or peppers. The exchange reflects patient-community awareness of epidemiological literature such as Searles Nielsen et al. (2013) linking edible Solanaceae to lower Parkinson disease risk, without reporting any personal symptom improvement or worsening from eating raw, grilled, or roasted bell peppers.
