Title: Parkinson's UK forum report of abdominal pain after meals containing ground black pepper
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/food-sensitivity/22123
Publication date: 2019-12-23
Date basis: Opening post date shown on the public forum.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27 UTC

One participant reporting Parkinson medication use associated episodes of severe post-meal abdominal pain with meals containing ground black pepper. The participant described the association as a suspicion and chose not to retest it. A separate gallbladder diagnosis was reported. The cause of the post-meal symptoms and the role of meal composition were unverified. The public reply did not add an independent first-person report linking pepper to pain.
This firsthand account records possible worsening after oral ground pepper. It offers no controlled or documented pepper-free comparison and does not establish a diagnosis for the abdominal pain or an effect of cracked pepper on Parkinson pain severity.
