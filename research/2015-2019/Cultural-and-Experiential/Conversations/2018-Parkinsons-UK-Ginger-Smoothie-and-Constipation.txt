Title: A ginger-containing smoothie in a Parkinson’s UK constipation discussion
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-any-cure/14613
Publication date: 2018-07-14
Date basis: Dated public opening post. The relevant reply is January 22, 2019, post 48.

A reply describes having regular bowel movements while drinking a daily smoothie containing ginger, leafy greens, fruit and citrus. The same person reports using cereal as an alternative. The opening question concerns constipation with Parkinson medicines. The smoothie reply gives no ginger-only comparison, quantity or verified diagnosis for that participant. Other replies describe responses to different interventions. This is a reported outcome for a combined food preparation.
