Title: Parkinson's UK Forum discussion on dairy reduction, protein timing and levodopa OFF periods
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/dairy-products-parkinsons/16915
Publication date: 2018-11-30
Date basis: Opening post date shown on the public forum (2018-11-30); follow-up replies reviewed through 2018-12-11.
Reviewed: 2026-11-21 UTC

A caregiver of a 75-year-old spouse diagnosed with Parkinson disease in 2011 reported that the spouse had been experiencing regular OFF periods, including a 60-to-90-minute morning OFF period. After incidentally switching breakfast from cow's milk and natural yogurt to soy milk and soy yogurt on November 11, 2018, the caregiver reported marked improvement and followed up one month later (2018-12-11) stating that cutting down on dairy products continued to yield good perceived results.

A second participant with Parkinson disease replied that taking levodopa on an empty stomach (avoiding food 1 hour before and 30 minutes after dosing) and deferring protein-rich foods—including cheese—until the evening meal had considerably reduced their wearing-off periods, while still consuming cow's milk and yogurt away from medication timing. Together the accounts illustrate lived patient/caregiver observations consistent with dietary protein competition with levodopa absorption, while remaining uncontrolled self-reports without pharmacokinetic verification or blinded comparator arms.
