Title: Culinary lavender listed among food items of interest in a Parkinson's UK forum discussion
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/food/8682
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2015-11-08
Date basis: Opening post dated November 8, 2015.

On November 8, 2015, a Parkinson's UK forum participant described reviewing online literature (including Wikipedia) for everyday culinary foods reported as neuroprotective or supportive of nervous-system health, compiling 13 candidate food items that included lavender alongside broad beans, cinnamon, coffee/tea, turmeric, green tea, hops, licorice root, velvet bean, rosemary, sage, and tomatoes, and asking peers for additions.
Observation and limits: This is a secondhand literature-curated food list rather than a firsthand consumption trial; it documents patient-community curiosity about culinary lavender for neurological health without reporting dose, preparation, or personal symptom outcomes. All participant handles were omitted for privacy.
