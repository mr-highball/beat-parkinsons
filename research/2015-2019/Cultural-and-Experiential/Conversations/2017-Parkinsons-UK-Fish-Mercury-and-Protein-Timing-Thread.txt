Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion on dietary fish mercury concerns, omega-3 alternatives, and high-protein levodopa crashes
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/diet-and-exercise-help/9730
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2017-02-10
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public community forum (2017-02-10; diet, mercury, and protein-timing replies dated 2017-02-12 through 2017-03-08).

In a February 2017 Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread, a newly diagnosed younger participant taking carbidopa/levodopa, ropinirole, and rasagiline asked about diet, daytime sleepiness, vivid dreams, and suspected daytime medication "crashes." Responding community members raised two distinct seafood- and protein-related considerations: one advised considering plant-derived omega-3 oils out of concern that dietary fish may be contaminated with mercury, and also warned that high dietary protein can impair levodopa absorption; another agreed that high-protein eating affected energy and emphasized taking levodopa either 30 minutes before or 1 hour after meals while pacing activity to manage fatigue. All personal identifiers are omitted. These views mirror clinical guidance on protein-levodopa spacing and species-selective fish mercury awareness (e.g., choosing lower-mercury light skipjack tuna or farmed trout over high-mercury predatory species) without providing controlled outcome measurements.
