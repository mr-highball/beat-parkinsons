Title: Parkinson's UK Forum: Protein Timing With Meat and Fish Around Levodopa, Weight Loss, and Soft-Meal Adaptations
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/diet-and-parkinsons/8967
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2016-03-03
Date basis: Original public thread opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK Community Forum (2016-03-03; 23 posts through 2016-03-11).

Public Parkinson's UK community discussion (23 posts, March 2016; all contributor usernames and identifying details removed) examining practical diet adjustments alongside Parkinson's medications.

Key privacy-safe observations:
- Levodopa-protein timing: Multiple contributors living with Parkinson's disease and care partners discussed restricting daytime dietary protein (including poultry, red meat, and fish) and shifting main protein meals to the evening so levodopa doses would not be blocked during active daytime hours, noting clinician/dietitian advice to space levodopa 30-60 minutes before or after protein-rich meals.
- Trade-offs and individual variation: Several participants cautioned against overly rigid protein avoidance because of unintended weight loss and fatigue, noting that not every person with Parkinson's notices acute protein interference at every meal.
- Texture and swallowing adaptations: Contributors also discussed choosing softer, moist preparations (soups, stews, flaky fish, or well-cooked tender meats) rather than dry textures when chewing fatigue or swallowing difficulty was present, alongside hydration and fiber for constipation.
