Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion citing evening salmon meals for omega-3 support amid hip aching and disrupted sleep
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/glucosamine-clondroitin/8205
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2015-04-21
Date basis: Relevant salmon-dinner post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2015-04-21; opening post dated 2015-03-27).

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened in March 2015, a member described difficulty sleeping on either side for more than two hours due to aching hips and asked peers about joint-support supplements. In April 2015 replies, one contributor recommended omega-3 for brain and joint health, and another participant with Parkinson's disease reported just having eaten salmon for dinner specifically because it is rich in omega-3 and believed to support joints, alongside a daily high-fiber berry and prune breakfast for bowel regularity. Combined with a separate 2021 Parkinson's UK dietary-timing thread (topic 29027) in which members shifted oily fish including salmon, sardines, and mackerel to 6 pm evening meals to avoid daytime levodopa interference, this discussion reflects common real-world patient motivations for eating salmon in the evening—seeking omega-3 support for brain, joint, and general wellness while keeping daytime protein lower. All personal identifiers are omitted. No objective pain, sleep, or cognitive outcomes were measured.
