Title: Parkinson's UK forum report combining Mucuna pruriens and green tea alongside high-dose thiamine for tremor
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/high-dose-thiamine/12093
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2018-02-21
Date basis: Opening post date on public Parkinson's UK Discourse thread (green tea post dated 2019-02-02).

Reported direction: Confounded favorable tremor anecdote combining green tea with Mucuna pruriens (a natural levodopa source) on top of high-dose thiamine; cannot attribute tremor change to green tea.
Authenticity: Publicly accessible Parkinson's UK community forum thread inspected on 2026-11-26; all member names and handles omitted for privacy.
Efficacy evidence: Uncontrolled single-person anecdote heavily confounded by concurrent Mucuna pruriens levodopa; contrasts with separate public forum reports of caffeine/green tea aggravating tremor.

Thread summary (post #11, 2019-02-02):
- A participant taking 3.5 g/day thiamine for 9 months reported progressive worsening of tremor and stated they were adding Mucuna pruriens and green tea, which they felt helped tremor (green tea polyphenols are sometimes co-taken informally by patients attempting peripheral DDC/COMT modulation with Mucuna, though unproven clinically).
