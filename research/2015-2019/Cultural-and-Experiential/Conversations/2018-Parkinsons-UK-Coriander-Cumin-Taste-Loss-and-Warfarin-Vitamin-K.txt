Title: Parkinson's UK public forum discussions on coriander and cumin for taste changes and leafy-coriander vitamin K caution
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/taste-budds-and-pd/5613
Source kind: conversations
Publication date: 2018-07-24
Date basis: Public forum post timestamp on Parkinson's UK Discourse (topic 5613, post #16 on 2018-07-24; companion warfarin-vitamin K post in topic 4190, post #2 on 2009-04-25).

In a public Parkinson's UK discussion on altered taste buds and reduced smell/flavor perception in Parkinson's disease (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/taste-budds-and-pd/5613, post dated 2018-07-24), a community contributor suggested seasoning meals with aromatic non-chili spices including coriander and cumin to restore flavor enjoyment for individuals who cannot tolerate chili heat (noting chili triggered migraine in a family member). In an earlier public Parkinson's UK medication-safety thread (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/vitamins/4190, post dated 2009-04-25), a forum member replying to a caregiver of a person with PD on warfarin cautioned against unmonitored dietary shifts and shared standard anticoagulation guidance listing green coriander leaves among vitamin-K-rich leafy vegetables that can attenuate warfarin activity. Neither thread reported an isolated change in Parkinson's motor or olfactory test scores.
