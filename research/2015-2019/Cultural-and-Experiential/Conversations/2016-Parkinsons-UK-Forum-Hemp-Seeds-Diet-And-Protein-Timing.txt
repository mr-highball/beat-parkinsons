Title: Parkinson's UK public forum discussions mentioning hemp seeds in daily cereal routines and protein-timing management
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/going-it-alone/8806
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2016
Date basis: Opening post date (2016-01-02) on publicly accessible Parkinson's UK Discourse community forum thread; cross-referenced with 2020-06-20 low-protein breakfast thread.

Summary of anonymized community posts (handles omitted): In a long-running Parkinson's UK community thread started in January 2016 (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/going-it-alone/8806), a member living with Parkinson's described sprinkling hemp seeds every morning on breakfast cereal together with chia seeds and brown linseed as part of a self-directed nutritional routine. No isolated symptom before-and-after change, exact gram dose, or specific hemp-seed benefit was separated from the concurrent seeds and lifestyle changes.

In a related Parkinson's UK nutrition thread from June 2020 (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/low-protein/24249), members discussed practical low-protein breakfast options and spacing food 30-60 minutes away from Madopar/levodopa doses so dietary protein would not slow medication kick-in, noting occasional inclusion of nuts/seeds in breakfast routines while keeping daytime protein modest. Across both discussions, hemp seeds appear strictly as a general culinary seed rather than a tested symptom intervention.
