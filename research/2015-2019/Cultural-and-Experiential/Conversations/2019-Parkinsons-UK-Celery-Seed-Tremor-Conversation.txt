A participant described a perceived general benefit with a celery seed extract supplement while also using mannitol and coffee. They reported an earlier emerging tremor and said they had no diagnosis. The post does not link any change in the tremor specifically to the extract, detail the posture of the tremor, or describe raw celery stalk. The conversation includes differing outcomes with other foods and bowel approaches. A linked secondary anecdote was unavailable at its posted address when checked. The account supplies experiential context around supplement use.
