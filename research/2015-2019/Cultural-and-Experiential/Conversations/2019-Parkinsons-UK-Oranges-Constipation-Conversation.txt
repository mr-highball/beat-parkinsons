Title: Different experiences with oranges and constipation in a Parkinson’s UK discussion
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/tell-me-what-worked-for-you/20827
Publication date: 2019-09-15
Date basis: The opening post is dated September 15, 2019. The relevant reports are in posts 4, 6, 7 and 11, dated September 15–19.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Reported direction: Mixed. Some participants describe relief with combined approaches. A separate participant reports no benefit from oranges.

One participant reporting Parkinson disease said constipation recurred when they omitted a regimen containing a laxative, cereal, prunes and fruit, usually oranges or tangerines. The opening poster later reported improvement after adding oranges while also taking an osmotic laxative and receiving other care. Two posts describe the same course for that participant. A third participant reported that oranges and then additional fruit did not help, and described improvement after later dietary changes.

The time courses are self-reported. The accounts give no standardized severity score or controlled orange-only comparison. The first fruit exposure varies by type. Repeated posts from one person contribute one account. A separate catalog note on celery seed and an earlier tremor covers a different post from the same thread, without independent corroboration of the orange reports.
