Title: Public Parkinson forum firsthand account of acute choking while eating raw grapefruit segments
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/hi-how-are-you-today/7235
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2016-06-12
Date basis: Post date on Parkinson UK public forum thread 7235 (2016-06-12).

In a June 2016 post on the Parkinson UK community forum (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/hi-how-are-you-today/7235), a participant with Parkinson disease recounted a frightening breakfast incident in which a large piece of raw grapefruit lodged in their throat, leaving them unable to breathe or speak and leading to transient fainting while a spouse performed back blows and abdominal thrusts. Although a single anecdotal mechanical choking episode rather than a clinical trial of swallowing physiology, it documents an important real-world texture and dysphagia hazard of eating fibrous raw grapefruit segments in people with Parkinson disease.
