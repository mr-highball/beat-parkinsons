Summary of public Cure Parkinson's HealthUnlocked thread titled 'Cruciferous Foods & PD' (posted 2018-04-09; privacy-safe summary with all member handles omitted).

Participants discussed cruciferous vegetables explicitly naming Brussels sprouts alongside broccoli, cabbage, and cauliflower in relation to Parkinson's disease and sulforaphane science summaries. Responses captured clearly diverging experiential perspectives: one participant expressed skepticism that eating cruciferous vegetables measurably altered Parkinson's motor symptoms, whereas another participant reported subjectively steadier daytime energy and noticeably better intestinal function after increasing cruciferous vegetable intake. Other commenters noted the gap between laboratory sulforaphane findings and real-world culinary portions. No controlled clinical measurements or preparation standardization (steamed vs roasted) were reported.
