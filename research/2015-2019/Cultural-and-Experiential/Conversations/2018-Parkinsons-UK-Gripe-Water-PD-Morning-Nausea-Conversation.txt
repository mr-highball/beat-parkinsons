Title: Gripe water reported ineffective for morning medication-related nausea in a Parkinson's UK discussion
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/nausea/14814
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2018-07-27
Date basis: Opening post dated July 27, 2018.

On July 27, 2018, a person taking daily Parkinson's medication posted on the Parkinson's UK forum describing six months of persistent morning nausea despite consistent timing of medication one hour after meals. The participant reported having tried gripe water alongside tonic water, ginger biscuits, and over-the-counter remedies without resolving the morning nausea, and expressed hesitation about starting prescribed domperidone.
Observation and limits: Traditional UK gripe water contains dill seed oil (alongside sodium bicarbonate and excipients), though the post does not name the commercial brand or verify dill seed oil content, does not test toasted or ground culinary dill seed, and concerns upper-GI nausea rather than constipation severity. It is preserved as an authentic no-benefit patient experiential context. All participant handles were omitted for privacy.
