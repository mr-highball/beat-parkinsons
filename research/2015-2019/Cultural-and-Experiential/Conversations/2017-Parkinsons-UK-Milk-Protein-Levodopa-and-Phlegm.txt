Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion warning against taking carbidopa-levodopa with milk and noting phlegm sensation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/affects-of-sinimet-3x-100-per-day/10047
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2017-08-05
Date basis: Public thread opening timestamp on the Parkinson's UK Community Forum (2017-08-05; additional community replies through 2025-07-13).

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened in August 2017 (with follow-up replies in July 2025), a person taking carbidopa-levodopa three times daily reported developing a phlegm cough, indigestion, and bloating over two months while taking tablets with milk. One responder warned that taking levodopa/carbidopa with any protein including milk severely restricts medication effectiveness. A later responder with 14 years of carbidopa-levodopa experience strongly echoed taking tablets with water rather than milk or protein-rich foods—describing dramatic loss of medication effect when taken with protein—and suggested the phlegm sensation could be linked to milk. Forum moderators directed readers to Parkinson's UK medication guidance and nurse advice. These accounts illustrate common patient concerns regarding milk-levodopa timing and perceived throat phlegm without controlled confirmation.
