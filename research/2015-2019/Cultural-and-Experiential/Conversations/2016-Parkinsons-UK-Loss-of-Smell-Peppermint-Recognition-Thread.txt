Title: Parkinson's UK forum account of recognizing peppermint scent despite severe PD hyposmia
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/loss-of-smell-survey-ha/8861
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2016-01-22
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2016-01-22; firsthand peppermint smell reply posted 2020-02-17).

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on Parkinson's loss of smell (opened January 2016; reply February 2020), a participant with marked hyposmia described playing a scratch-and-sniff odor game with family and failing to identify almost all test scents except peppermint, which registered clearly like a mint sweet. All personal identifiers are omitted. This experiential observation illustrates why menthol/peppermint is widely recognized even in PD hyposmia through trigeminal nerve TRPM8 cooling stimulation rather than genuine recovery of olfactory discrimination.
