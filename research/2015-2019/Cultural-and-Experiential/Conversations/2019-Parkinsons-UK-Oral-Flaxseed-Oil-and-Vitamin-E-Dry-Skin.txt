Title: Parkinson's UK forum reports on daily oral flaxseed oil and vitamin E for Parkinson's-associated dry, flaky skin
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/dry-skin/1697
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2019-06-08
Date basis: Date of first flaxseed oil post on public Parkinson's UK thread opened 2010-12-10 (follow-up caregiver post 2022-08-18).

Reported direction: Favorable off-target dermatologic anecdotes (dry/flaky skin) confounded by concurrent vitamin E and topical oil/soap changes; zero motor or non-motor Parkinson's symptom outcomes reported.
Authenticity: Publicly accessible Parkinson's UK community forum thread inspected on 2026-11-26; all member names and handles omitted for privacy.
Efficacy evidence: Anecdotal patient/caregiver skin reports with multi-product co-interventions; supplies no evidence for frozen Parkinson's neurological or autonomic symptoms.

Thread summary (selected flaxseed-oil posts):
- Firsthand report (2019-06-08): A participant with Parkinson's-related dry, itchy, flaky skin reported taking approximately 2 tablespoons of oral flaxseed oil plus one vitamin E capsule daily, alongside switching away from medicated shampoos to organic soap, natural beard oil, and coconut oil, noting markedly reduced itching and flaking.
- Caregiver follow-up (2022-08-18): A spouse of a person with Parkinson's flaking skin reported trying daily oral flaxseed oil and vitamin E together with topical olive oil scale removal, coconut oil, and colloidal oat moisturizer, describing visible improvement requiring ongoing maintenance.
- Relevance note: Demonstrates real-world PD community use of oral flaxseed oil (frequently paired with vitamin E), while highlighting that community anecdotes target dermatologic dryness rather than the 19 frozen Round 4 Parkinson's motor/non-motor symptoms.
