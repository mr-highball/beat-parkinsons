In an August 16, 2017 post (#85) on a public Parkinson's UK forum thread sharing community wellness tips, a contributor highlighted the vagus nerve as important for physical and mental wellbeing in Parkinson's and shared a self-compiled dietary checklist advising members to eat broccoli, Brussels sprouts, and cabbage alongside probiotic kefir and coffee while limiting gluten and alcohol. The post reflects lay patient interest in cruciferous vegetables and fermented foods for gut-brain health but reports no individual preparation details or objective symptom measurements.
