Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion with mixed firsthand accounts of freeze-dried Vicia faba powder (AtremoPlus)
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/atremoplus/21341
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2019-10-21
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2019-10-21; firsthand user and caregiver replies dated 2020-06-06 through 2022-02-03).

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread running from October 2019 to February 2022, members discussed AtremoPlus, a commercial freeze-dried powder derived from Vicia faba (broad beans). Early posts expressed skepticism about cost and clinical trial quality. Between June 2020 and February 2022, three participants reported favorable personal experiences: one reported slightly better tremor relief than Madopar after 9 months on 10 g twice daily with yogurt; a second reported smoother, longer-lasting ON/OFF periods after three days; and a third with 2 years of use alongside carbidopa/levodopa reported marked perceived improvements in walking, dressing, hand turning, and typing. Conversely, in January 2021, a caregiver reported that their heavily medicated spouse took the Vicia faba powder one morning and was frozen more than usual for the remainder of the day, suspecting interference with prescribed medication absorption and deciding to seek specialist advice. All personal identifiers are omitted. The thread reflects a concentrated commercial Vicia faba supplement rather than culinary cooked beans and illustrates both perceived dopaminergic benefit and unpredictable motor worsening/freezing when combined with prescription regimens.
