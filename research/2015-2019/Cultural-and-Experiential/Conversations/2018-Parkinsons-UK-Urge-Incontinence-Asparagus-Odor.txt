Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Discussion Mentioning Asparagus Urine Odor in an Urge Incontinence Thread
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/treatment-to-combat-urge-incontinence/15929
Source kind: public_forum_conversation
Publication date: 2019-01-03
Date basis: Public forum thread opened 2018-10-01; asparagus comment posted 2019-01-03 (post #19) on Parkinson's UK Community Forum

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on treatments to combat urge incontinence (thread opened 2018-10-01; relevant post #19 dated 2019-01-03; usernames omitted for privacy), community members discussed prescription bladder medications (such as solifenacin and mirabegron) and pelvic floor exercises. One participant jokingly referenced the well-known smell of urine after eating asparagus while reassuring peers during symptom discussion. No participant reported that eating steamed, grilled, or roasted asparagus either eased or worsened urinary urgency frequency, underscoring the absence of experiential urinary-urgency signal even in Parkinson's patient forums.
