Title: Parkinson's UK Forum discussion on constipation coping with prunes, figs, dates, walnuts and milled golden flaxseed
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation/20523
Publication date: 2019-08-23
Date basis: Opening post date shown on the public forum (2019-08-23); replies reviewed through 2019-08-30.
Reviewed: 2026-11-21 UTC

A participant diagnosed with Parkinson disease two years earlier reported worsening constipation despite nightly laxative use and sought dietary or practical advice. Multiple participants and caregivers shared firsthand dietary experiences alongside medical bowel regimens.

Several participants reported regular relief from dried plums (prunes) and figs: one recommended roughly six prunes or figs daily (fresh or canned); another reported eating four dried prunes every morning—noting constipation returned whenever they forgot—alongside four dates and four walnuts (explicitly uncertain whether the dates or walnuts contributed to bowel relief); a caregiver reported combining prunes, figs, bran cereal, a footstool, and a nurse-directed osmotic laxative (macrogol).

Two separate participants highlighted golden flaxseed (linseed): one caregiver reported that a bladder and bowel nurse recommended two dessertspoons daily of milled golden flaxseed on cereal with strong perceived relief, and noted an accidental dechallenge/rechallenge when constipation recurred during a hospital stay where flaxseed was omitted; another participant reported soaking one teaspoon of golden linseeds overnight in water and drinking them before food with perceived benefit. Other replies discussed hydration, toilet posture stools, probiotics, ispaghula husk, lactulose, and irrigation. Accounts are uncontrolled self-reports and frequently involved concurrent laxatives or lifestyle steps, but provide detailed real-world dosing and dechallenge observations.
