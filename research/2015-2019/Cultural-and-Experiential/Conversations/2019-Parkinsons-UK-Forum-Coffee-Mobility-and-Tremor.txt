Title: Parkinson's UK Forum discussion about black coffee, mobility and tremor
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/coffee-as-an-aid/18490
Publication date: 2019-03-20
Date basis: Opening post date shown on the public forum. Selected replies through March 26, 2019.
Source kind: Conversational
Reviewed: 2026-11-18

A caregiver attributed short-term improvements in mobility, coordination and speech to black coffee in a relative with Parkinson disease. The caregiver described marked swallowing difficulty and use of a feeding tube. A later reply reported a few successful swallows during monitoring after coffee. Changes in illness, concurrent care and baseline fluctuations prevent attribution. The account supplies no standardized swallowing assessment.

A different participant attributed worse tremor to coffee and reported switching to decaffeinated tea. The caregiver said that tremor was generally absent in the relative, so the reports concern different outcomes. Replies discussed milk and medication timing as possibilities without testing them. Dose and tremor type are unknown. The caregiver mentioned possible follow-up research without an identifiable study in the reviewed posts.
