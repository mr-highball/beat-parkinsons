Title: Parkinson's UK forum post surveying everyday culinary herbs including rosemary and sage for nerve health
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/food/8682
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2015-11-08
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2015-11-08).

In a November 2015 Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread titled 'Food', a participant shared a personal shortlist of 13 everyday culinary foods and herbs—including broad beans, turmeric, green tea, rosemary, and sage—compiled from online reading about potential neuroprotective properties, asking peers for additional food ideas without reporting clinical symptom changes. All account names are omitted. The thread illustrates lay interest in culinary Lamiaceae herbs (rosemary and sage) in Parkinson's self-care while underscoring the absence of direct PD dietary trials.
