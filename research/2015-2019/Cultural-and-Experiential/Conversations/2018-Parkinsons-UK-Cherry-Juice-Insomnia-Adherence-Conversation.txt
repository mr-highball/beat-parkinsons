Title: Parkinson's UK Forum exchange on trying cherry juice for nighttime insomnia and daytime sleepiness
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/i-am-back-after-3-years-of-absence-from-this-forum/13948
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2018-06-08
Date basis: Public thread opening post dated 2018-06-08; relevant reply reporting cherry juice experience dated 2018-07-04 (Post #11).

In a Parkinson's UK public forum thread opened on June 8, 2018 (all participant handles omitted), a member with Parkinson's disease experiencing severe nighttime sleeplessness alongside unintentional daytime dozing after starting carbidopa/levodopa reported in Post #11 (July 4, 2018) that after doctors prescribed mirtazapine 15 mg, the member had also tried drinking cherry juice after hearing about its natural melatonin content.
Reported outcome and adherence limit: The participant reported that cherry juice appeared to help for a few weeks, but noted having to drink a large volume of juice and could not sustain daily intake, discontinuing it.
Evidence and limits: Unverified single self-report confounded by concurrent mirtazapine initiation and levodopa adjustment; tests commercial liquid cherry juice rather than whole dried or raw cherries, with no objective sleep diary or scale. Preserves both perceived temporary benefit and a practical high-volume adherence barrier.
