Title: Parkinson's UK forum constipation discussion including community suggestion of daily papaya and guava
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation/20523
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2019-08-23
Date basis: Opening post timestamp (2019-08-23) and papaya/guava reply (post #19, 2019-10-04) on Parkinson's UK Community Forum topic 20523.

In an August-November 2019 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread started by a person diagnosed with Parkinson's disease for two years experiencing worsening constipation refractory to nightly laxatives, contributors shared practical bowel-management strategies. Firsthand improvements were described with daily prunes, milled golden linseed (flaxseed), water intake, toilet footstools, and prescribed lactulose. One responder recommended increasing dietary fiber and eating papaya and guava daily after meals, without reporting a personal PD before-and-after trajectory. All personal names, handles, and external promotional URLs are omitted.
