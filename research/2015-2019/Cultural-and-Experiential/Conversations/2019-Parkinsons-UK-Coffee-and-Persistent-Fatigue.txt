Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/parkinsons-fatigue/19726
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2019-06-19
Date basis: Opening timestamp on the public discussion. The two coffee accounts appear June 24 and 27.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24, original public thread
Related public thread: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/excessive-sleepiness-fatigue/18566

The opening participant described disabling fatigue and sleepiness with Parkinson disease and later reported remaining tired and liable to fall asleep after morning coffee. Another participant described a family member appearing more able to move and exercise after black coffee given with Parkinson medication. That caregiver also reported coffee use for the same person in the related public thread. Count these caregiver comments as one source family.

The firsthand report documents no perceived relief after the reported coffee experience. The caregiver described apparent activity and alertness. Specific preparation, fatigue scoring, diagnosis verification and differences in medication exposure were unavailable. The comments cannot isolate the coffee effect.
