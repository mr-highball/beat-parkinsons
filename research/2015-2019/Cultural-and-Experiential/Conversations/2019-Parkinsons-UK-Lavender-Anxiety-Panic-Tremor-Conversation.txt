Title: Fresh lavender scent mentioned in a Parkinson's UK discussion on panic attacks, anxiety, and tremor
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/panic-attacks-anxiety-and-tremor/20909
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2019-09-22
Date basis: Opening post dated September 22, 2019; relevant reply mentioning lavender dated January 23, 2023.

In an opening post dated September 22, 2019, a caregiver described a 68-year-old spouse diagnosed with Parkinson's disease six years earlier who experienced longstanding anxiety and depression alongside worsening panic attacks and increased tremor after starting citalopram. In a reply dated January 23, 2023, another forum participant shared personal non-pharmacological coping strategies for debilitating panic attacks, specifically going outdoors to smell fresh lavender and using a weighted blanket when weather allowed.
Observation and limits: This public patient exchange documents perceived calming support from smelling fresh lavender (non-oral olfactory exposure, bundled with going outside and a weighted blanket), NOT from consuming brewed or dried culinary lavender, and reports no quantified anxiety or tremor change. All participant handles were omitted for privacy.
