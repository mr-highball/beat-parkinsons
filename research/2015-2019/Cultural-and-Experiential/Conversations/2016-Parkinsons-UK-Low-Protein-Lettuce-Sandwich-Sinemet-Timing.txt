Title: Parkinson's UK public forum discussion of tomato-and-lettuce sandwiches and mixed salads for daytime low-protein levodopa meal planning
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/diet-and-parkinsons/8967
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2016-03-03
Date basis: Opening thread timestamp (2016-03-03; caregiver and patient replies dated 2016-03-10 to 2016-03-13) on public Parkinson's UK Discourse forum.

In a March 2016 Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussion gathering patient and caregiver experiences for the charity's Diet and Parkinson's booklet, a caregiver whose spouse took Sinemet every two hours explained that leaving a full one-hour medication window around meals was impossible and that dietary protein frequently blocked medication effectiveness. To keep daytime levodopa working before going out, they relied on virtually protein-free meals such as a sliced tomato-and-lettuce sandwich, mixed salad with a baked potato, or vegetable soup, reserving higher-protein foods for other times. In the same thread (March 12, 2016), an unmedicated participant experiencing swallowing difficulty and constipation reported subsisting mainly on vegetable soup and mashed potatoes rather than raw foods. All contributor handles and personal identifiers are omitted. The exchange illustrates both why low-protein leafy salads are practically compatible with levodopa timing and why texture limitations restrict raw greens for patients with dysphagia.
