Title: Parkinson's UK public forum firsthand report of lettuce from a sandwich causing nasal regurgitation during PD swallowing difficulty
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/food-coming-out-of-my-nose/19190
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2019-05-10
Date basis: Opening post timestamp (2019-05-10; replies dated 2019-05-10, 2019-05-12, and 2023-09-06) on public Parkinson's UK Discourse forum thread.

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened on May 10, 2019, a person with Parkinson's asked whether nasal regurgitation was a swallowing symptom after feeling an unusual sensation while eating a lunchtime sandwich and blowing out pieces of lettuce through their nose—noting prior episodes with fried rice and water going down the wrong way. Peer responders on May 10 and May 12, 2019 identified the symptom as dysphagia with nasal regurgitation (sharing similar experiences with peas and liquids), warned about the risk of aspiration pneumonia if food enters the airway, and recommended prompt evaluation by a GP, ENT specialist, or movement-disorder speech and language therapist (noting compensatory strategies such as the chin-tuck posture). A later reply in September 2023 echoed the need for clinical swallow assessment. All contributor names and handles are omitted. This firsthand PD account highlights real-world texture management challenges with raw leafy salad greens when oropharyngeal dysphagia is present.
