Title: HealthUnlocked: Firsthand account of naturally caffeine-free rooibos tea for mild anxiety, palpitations, and family colic use
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://healthunlocked.com/heartrhythmcharity/posts/133513970/health-benefits-of-rooibos-tea
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2016-05-04
Date basis: Opening post date (2016-05-04) verified directly from HealthUnlocked Heart Rhythm Disorders Support public page.

In a May 2016 HealthUnlocked Heart Rhythm Disorders Support thread, a daily rooibos drinker described multigenerational household use of traditional South African rooibos tea, noting their grandmother prized it and their family used weak rooibos infusions for infant colic/stomach cramps with perceived relief. Personally living with atrial fibrillation and mild anxiety, the contributor emphasized that rooibos is naturally caffeine-free (rather than chemically decaffeinated), making it a reassuring, stress-free daytime and relaxation beverage alongside or instead of coffee when avoiding stimulants. At the same time, the poster offered balanced realism, noting "Rooibos is not this super thing that will cure everything in a minute." The account illustrates real-world patient motivation for choosing brewed rooibos over caffeinated Camellia sinensis teas without providing controlled symptom evidence.
