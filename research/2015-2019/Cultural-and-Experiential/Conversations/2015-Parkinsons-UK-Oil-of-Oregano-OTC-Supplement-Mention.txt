Title: Parkinson's UK forum member noting concurrent oil of oregano supplement use with uncertain individual effect
Collection: Cultural and experiential

Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2015-02-18
Date basis: Public Parkinson's UK Community Forum Discourse JSON timestamp for post #18 (2015-02-18T05:13:59Z) within thread opened 2015-02-03.

In a February 2015 public discussion on the Parkinson's UK Community Forum, a participant with Parkinson's disease summarized their regimen of prescribed dopaminergic and gastrointestinal medications (pramipexole, amantadine, rasagiline, carbidopa/levodopa, domperidone, and omeprazole for gastroparesis) alongside several over-the-counter supplements including vitamin C, ubiquinol, glucosamine, and oil of oregano. The participant explicitly remarked that they were not sure which, if any, of the over-the-counter supplements were helping, continuing them out of reluctance to change a stable routine. All personal names, handles, and geographic references are omitted. The account involves commercial oil of oregano rather than culinary fresh or dried oregano leaves and documents neutral/uncertain real-world attribution.
