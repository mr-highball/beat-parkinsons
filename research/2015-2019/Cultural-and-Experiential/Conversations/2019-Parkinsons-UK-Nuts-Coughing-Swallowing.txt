Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Account of Coughing When Eating Whole Nuts Including Brazil Nuts
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/nuts-about-nuts/20445
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2019-08-16
Date basis: Thread creation and follow-up posts (2019-08-16 to 2019-08-26).

In an August 2019 Parkinson's UK forum thread, a contributor with Parkinson's disease reported coughing every time they tried to eat whole nuts despite having had no issue prior to diagnosis. When another member suggested smooth almond butter, the contributor replied it was not the same as eating a whole almond or Brazil nut. A peer recommended seeking referral to a Speech and Language Therapist for swallowing difficulties. Ten days later, the contributor reported successfully eating peanuts on two consecutive days without serious coughing, highlighting fluctuating dysphagia sensitivity to hard, crumbly nut textures.
