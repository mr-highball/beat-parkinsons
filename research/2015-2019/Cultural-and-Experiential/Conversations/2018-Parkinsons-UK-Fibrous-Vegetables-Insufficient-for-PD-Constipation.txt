Title: Parkinson's UK public forum discussion reporting that high-fiber diets and fibrous vegetables alone often fail to relieve PD constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation/13941
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2018-06-08
Date basis: Opening post timestamp (2018-06-08; replies inspected through 2018-06-11) on public Parkinson's UK Discourse forum thread.

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened on June 8, 2018, a person with Parkinson's asked for advice after persistent constipation continued despite adopting a high-fiber diet and taking a fiber supplement. Several respondents shared counterbalancing experiences against relying solely on dietary vegetables: one contributor who had eaten healthily lifelong noted that increasing fibrous vegetables, cereals, fruit, and water did not resolve prodromal or post-diagnosis PD constipation, prompting their Parkinson's nurse to advise a regular daily mild osmotic laxative so constipation would not impair levodopa uptake. Another commenter similarly reported being advised that extra fiber alone is often not the best solution compared with mild osmotic medication (such as Movicol) plus adequate fluids, whereas a third member emphasized combining exercise, fluids, dietary fiber, and daily prunes to keep bowels moving and support levodopa absorption. All contributor handles and personal identifiers have been omitted. These uncontrolled reports do not isolate individual bitter or cruciferous vegetables but counterbalance folk laxative assumptions in Parkinson's disease.
