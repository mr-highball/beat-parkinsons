# Grape preparations and hand function

## Concord juice and grooved pegboard in a crossover

First published online February 11, 2016. Twenty-five healthy working mothers entered a crossover comparing 355 mL/day Concord grape juice with energy- and sugar-matched placebo in two 12-week periods. In the first phase, averaged Grooved Pegboard times were 60.4 s (SE 1.9) with placebo and 63.2 s (2.3) with juice (p<.05). Treatment by phase F(1,21)=9.61, p<.01, with no clear difference in the second phase. The phase-specific result concerns juice and the healthy study population, with no raw-fruit or Parkinson effect. ## Registered grape seed extract and motor tasks

First posted May 16, 2018. The 60-person randomized trial compared CP9700 400 mg with maltodextrin in healthy adults and registered finger tapping. The 2020 Bell paper matches the intervention, design and primary ID in its Methods. The author manuscript's preliminary page gives NCT03579095, the accession for a different ginseng study. The official CP9700 registry contains no posted results.
