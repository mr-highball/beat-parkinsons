## Rosemary and daylily sleep trial and bad dream eligibility

Bloomer and colleagues published October 12, 2016. Thirty-two adults with self-reported sleep difficulty were randomized to a proprietary rosemary and daylily product or placebo. The published survey reports bad dreams among several alternative criteria for entering the study. The outcome tables report Leeds sleep dimensions and other sleep or mood measures, with no between-condition interaction established for the sleep quality measures. Individual dream frequencies and objectively scored dream enactment were unreported. Ingredient isolation, dried culinary rosemary and Parkinson disease were outside the design. [Primary report, Methods 2.1 and result tables](https://www.memphis.edu/nutraceutical/pdfs/7-2016-health-clock.pdf)
