# Parkinson and related neurological symptom outcomes

## Reported measures in a Parkinson complementary medicine survey

March 2015. An anonymous complementary medicine survey reports respondent perceptions of symptom and quality-of-life changes. The limitations identify pain as a possible treatment-use reason that was not asked, and the discussion identifies apathy as an unexamined possible adverse effect. No dried-date exposure was analyzed. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4363882/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2015-Finseth-Parkinson-CAM-Outcome-Scope.txt)

## Nocturnal sialorrhea eligibility and instrumental swallowing outcomes

April 25, 2016. The study compared 24 men with Parkinson dysphagia and 24 controls using ultrasound. Nocturnal sialorrhea is an example of an eligibility complaint. Swallowing measurements include tongue thickness, the distance and timing of hyoid-thyroid approximation, and a fluoroscopy subset assessing penetration and aspiration. The protocol gives no separate food exposure or measured change in drooling or rest tremor.
