# Chinese Clinical Trial Registry follow-up, 2015–2019

## Registered Congrong Shujing granules trial

ChiCTR prospectively registered ChiCTR-IOR-16008394 on April 30, 2016. It lists 72 participants per arm and a parallel comparison of the Congrong Shujing formula or placebo alongside antiparkinsonian medication. The primary measure is the Unified Parkinson’s Disease Rating Scale. Secondary measures include PDQ-39 quality of life and medication use. The associated 2020 publication gives a smaller cohort and reports results for this same identifier. The intervention was a prepared medicinal formula.
