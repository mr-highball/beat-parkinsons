# Oil exposure, dry mouth and swallowing outcomes

## Rice bran emulsion and a solid swallowing test

Swallowing test outcomes were inconclusive, while the separate subjective dry-mouth measure improved modestly. [2013 registration](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Rice-Bran-Saliva-Substitute-Registry.md)

## Dry mouth and subjective dysphagia in Parkinson disease

In the Parkinson cohort, subjective dry mouth and dysphagia were associated. The connection supplies clinical context for the patient-reported rinse result. [Later rinse trial](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#cold-pressed-sunflower-oil-rinse-and-subjective-swallowing)
