# Oregano exposures and primary study context

## Reported oregano use in a multiethnic Parkinson cohort

Sauerbier and colleagues reported on 139 people with Parkinson disease. Table 4 lists reported oregano use in 14 of 66 White, 9 of 34 Asian and 6 of 39 Black African and Caribbean participants. Its p value of .506 is a comparison of reported intake across those groups. Section 3.5 reports no significant differences after correction in exploratory comparisons across diets, herbs, beverages and supplements. The report gives no oregano-specific drooling estimate. The questionnaire does not identify oregano preparation or quantify its amount. The gastrointestinal domain and published analyses cannot isolate clinical drooling after fresh oregano.
