# Oregano exposures and primary study context

## Reported oregano use in a multiethnic Parkinson cohort

Sauerbier and colleagues reported on 139 people with Parkinson disease. Table 4 lists reported oregano use in 14 of 66 White, 9 of 34 Asian and 6 of 39 Black African and Caribbean participants. Its p value of .506 is a comparison of reported intake across those groups. Section 3.5 reports no significant differences after correction in exploratory comparisons across diets, herbs, beverages and supplements. The report gives no oregano-specific drooling estimate. The questionnaire does not identify oregano preparation or quantify its amount. The gastrointestinal domain and published analyses cannot isolate clinical drooling after fresh oregano. [Primary record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6312592/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/PMC6312592.txt)

Section 3.5 lists total HADS and total and domain NMSS as outcomes in its exploratory diet comparisons. It provides no oregano-specific estimate or separate HADS anxiety result for oregano. The corrected overall account and the .506 frequency comparison do not indicate whether reported oregano use was associated with more or less anxiety. [Primary report, Section 3.5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6312592/) · [Institutional copy](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10066041/1/Schrag_Dietary%20Variations%20in%20a%20Multiethnic%20Parkinson%27s%20Disease%20Cohort%20and%20Possible%20Influences%20on%20Nonmotor%20Aspects_VoR.pdf)

## Isolated carvacrol and measured safety outcomes in healthy adults

Ghorani and colleagues first published November 29, 2018. The phase I comparison measured laboratory values and spirometry at two levels of isolated carvacrol. Some values changed within groups while staying within the reported laboratory reference ranges. The abstract has no separate anxiety or psychiatric adverse-event result. The full adverse-event table remains open. [Original abstract and scope](Source-Text/2018-Ghorani-Carvacrol-Safety-Extract.txt)
