# Chicken essence and fatigue-related mood scales

## Chicken essence and routine energy or tiredness

Young, Benton and Carter reported 50 randomized and 46 completing ten days of chicken extract or placebo. POMS energetic/tired showed no treatment effect. The separate visual-analogue treatment main effect was inconclusive, and the reported treatment-by-time p value was below .07. A larger 2015 report from Benton and Young gives an energetic/tired numerical difference favoring essence, with reported p below .06 and an unclear printed F statistic. Cohort overlap remains unresolved. The soup study from the preceding period additionally found an inconclusive fatigue comparison.
