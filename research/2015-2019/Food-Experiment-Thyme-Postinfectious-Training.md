# Thyme in a rotating postinfectious smell training program

## Thyme in a rotating postinfectious smell training program

A separate postinfectious cohort had better combined smell scores after rotating odors. The schedule placed thyme in one of three blocks with other scents. The groups were allocated in order, and the contrast estimates the whole training schedule.
