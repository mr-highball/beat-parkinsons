# Oral safflower oil comparator trials in 2015–2019

## TRIHEP3 Huntington disease registration

NCT02453061 first posted on 25 May 2015. The randomized study enrolled 100 adults with early Huntington disease. The blinded six-month comparator was whole oral safflower oil at 1 g/kg/day versus oral triheptanoin. Both groups received triheptanoin in the next six months. The protocol specified caudate atrophy, the Unified Huntington’s Disease Rating Scale total motor score, functional capacity, and Problem Behaviors Assessment–Short. The publicly accessible sponsor statistical analysis plan carries a 9 January 2019 revision date. Its secondary comparisons use total scale scores. It does not prespecify separate clinical contrasts for gait, rigidity, speech, swallowing, falls or apathy. Oil extraction method and cultivar remain unspecified.
