# Spearmint Blend Conference

## Preliminary spearmint blend insomnia scores

Grandner and colleagues report 100 adults with sleep complaints randomized to placebo or a proprietary spearmint and green-tea extract blend for 30 days, with a preliminary per-protocol analysis of 93. The adjusted between-group Insomnia Severity Index improvement was 2.15 points (95% CI 0.43 to 3.87, p=.01). Group change in poor sleep category had relative risk ratio 0.16 (0.04 to 0.63, p=.009). The investigators, blend, exposure schedule, sample and endpoints tie this presentation to the later Arizona report, which analyzed 89 and reported ISI interaction p=.044. Treat the earlier abstract as preliminary reporting of the same family. The blend does not isolate spearmint and the trial did not study Parkinson disease.
