# Dietary and botanical registry leads, 2015–2019

## Rectal botanical regimen containing sesame oil

Kashikar and Rao reported the results of [CTRI/2018/09/015592](https://ctri.nic.in/Clinicaltrials/pmaindet2.php?trialid=27849) in 2019. The journal describes 23 people diagnosed with parkinsonism in an open-label pre/post series. The regimen included three botanical enemas, five rectal administrations of processed sesame oil (murchita tila taila), and a nightly purgative. All participants received the regimen. Mean scores decreased immediately after treatment from 2.56 to 0.13 for constipation, 1.04 to 0.30 for speech, 1.18 to 0.18 for gait and 3.30 to 0.78 for facial expression. The tables leave rigidity and walking speed unquantified. The changes apply to the full regimen and give no estimate for [0321, toasted sesame oil and facial expressiveness](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0321.txt), [0697, toasted sesame oil and speech intelligibility](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0697.txt), or [0994, cold-pressed sesame oil and walking slowness](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0994.txt). [Primary report, Methods and Tables 4–9, printed pp. 50–55](https://www.ijapc.com/upload/MNAPC-V-11-I3-23-p-48-59.pdf)

## Pharmaceutical butylphthalide across Parkinsonian diagnoses

ChiCTR1900023725 first registered on June 9, 2019. It plans separate placebo and pharmaceutical butylphthalide arms for Parkinson’s disease, vascular parkinsonism, multiple system atrophy and progressive supranuclear palsy. Its registered outcomes include 48-week UPDRS change, MoCA, SCOPA-COG and Epworth Sleepiness Scale. The available WHO ICTRP export and accession, investigator and grant searches supplied no results for this cohort. The mirrored record flags pending ethics approval at registration. The record differs from ChiCTR1800018892, the previously cataloged Zhou trial, and provides no effect estimate for fresh or dried celery leaf. [Primary registry](https://www.chictr.org.cn/showproj.html?proj=39890) · [Archived registry fields](https://trial.medpath.com/zh/clinical-trial/c6eb6a13c47f6153/chictr1900023725-butylphthalide-delaying-parkinson-disease-progression-mechanism) · [Zhou trial evidence](Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#pharmaceutical-butylphthalide-and-motor-symptoms)

## Protein-enriched nutritional support during rehabilitation

PRO-LEADER randomized 150 participants with Parkinson’s or parkinsonism undergoing intensive rehabilitation to hospital meals alone or meals plus a supplement with whey protein, leucine and vitamin D. The 2018 conference abstract and 2019 journal article describe the same study, NCT03124277. At 30 days the center-adjusted difference in improvement on the six-minute walk was 18.1 m (95% CI 0.9 to 35.3 m), favoring supplementation. The authors also reported a favorable secondary result for four-meter walking speed. The abstract combines the diagnostic groups. The comparison evaluates a supplement during rehabilitation. [Trial registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03124277) · [2018 conference abstract](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/protein-leucine-and-vitamin-d-enhancing-rehabilitation-pro-leader-in-patients-with-parkinsons-disease-or-parkinsonism-a-rct/) · [2019 primary journal report](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000007858)

## Banisteriopsis caapi capsule trial

RBR-6t4j85 registered in September 2018. It plans a four-week comparison of dried, pulverized *Banisteriopsis caapi* vine capsules with placebo in 40 people with Parkinson’s and mild to moderate motor fluctuations. Outcomes include MDS-UPDRS, dyskinesia, HADS and diary-recorded off time. The primary registry lists “not yet recruiting” and provides no outcome results. This botanical falls outside the frozen ingredient list. [Primary registration and outcomes](https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-6t4j85)

## Retracted probiotic capsule report

Tamtaji et al. linked IRCT2017082434497N4 to a 12-week randomized comparison of four-strain probiotic capsules and placebo in 60 people with Parkinson’s. The 2019 issue article was first available online in 2018. It reported changes in a global MDS-UPDRS score and biomarkers. The journal retracted the report in 2025. The capsule formulation supplies no result for a frozen fermented-food preparation. [Primary registration](https://irct.ir/trial/26352) · [Retracted report, PMID 29891223](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29891223/) · [2025 retraction notice](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-WHO-ICTRP-Followup.md#retraction-of-the-probiotic-capsule-report)

## Folate and vitamin B12 trial registration

TCTR20181221001 was registered December 21, 2018. The Prapokklao Hospital trial planned oral folate with vitamin B12, entacapone, and no added treatment in levodopa-treated people with Parkinson’s. The primary measure was homocysteine at six and twelve weeks. The later journal report matches the contact, institution, comparison and endpoint. [Primary registration](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/show/TCTR20181221001) · [2021 report](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-WHO-ICTRP-Followup.md#folate-and-vitamin-b12-during-levodopa-treatment)
