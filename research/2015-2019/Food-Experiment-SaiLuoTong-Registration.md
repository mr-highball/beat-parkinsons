# SaiLuoTong pilot registration and protocol

The Australian New Zealand Clinical Trials Registry lists prospective registration ACTRN12617000371392 on March 10, 2017. The 12-week trial compared the standardized ginseng, ginkgo and saffron extract formula with matched starch placebo in older adults with mild cognitive impairment. The current registry lists change in D-KEFS Trail Making Test Condition 4 among the primary outcomes and separately lists depression, anxiety and stress as secondary. The saffron constituent was administered with both other extracts. The 2018 original protocol, DOI 10.1186/s13063-018-2912-0, describes the same trial and executive test battery.
