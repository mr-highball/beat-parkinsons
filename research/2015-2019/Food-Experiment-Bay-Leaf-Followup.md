# Bay Leaf Followup

## Bay leaf powder cookies and acute digestive comfort

Khan and colleagues first published August 30, 2017. Twenty healthy adults consumed control cookies and matching carbohydrate loads in cookies containing 3% or 6% bay leaf powder, in a randomized crossover. The accessible original abstract reports no gastrointestinal discomfort over two hours. It does not provide a bowel movement, stool frequency or long-term constipation assessment. The full result tables remain access-limited. Author institution's original abstract: https://squ.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/effect-of-incorporating-bay-leaves-in-cookies-on-postprandial-gly/.
