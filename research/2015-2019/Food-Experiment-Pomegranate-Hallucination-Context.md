# Pomegranate label and reported hallucination timing

## Auditory hallucinations in the HSA bulletin

The May 2015 Singapore Health Sciences Authority bulletin on pp. 5–6 describes auditory hallucinations and delusions after a teenager used a mixed slimming capsule. The label included pomegranate and grapefruit. Testing found undeclared drugs, and pomegranate is label information alone. The bulletin notes that confidentiality changes were made to the case. The 2014 alert may describe the same incident, so these reports supply no independent second case count. [Original bulletin, pp. 5–6](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/409/ca3a0244-1518-4691-867a-15a400767b0e/adr_news_may2015_vol17_no1.pdf) · [2014 alert](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Pomegranate-Hallucination-Context.md#hallucinations-and-an-adulterated-mixed-product)

## Stroke trial and hallucinations before exposure

NCT02442804 first posted in May 2015 and results first posted August 2, 2017. The other adverse event table reports a psychotic episode with auditory hallucinations for one of eight participants assigned to oral POMx, compared with none of eight assigned placebo. The record explicitly reports that hallucinations began several days before the first supplement administration. The group assignment and event timing do not establish an effect from the supplement. The exposure was a supplement and the participants had stroke. Visual hallucination frequency, raw arils and Parkinson outcomes were outside the reported comparison.
