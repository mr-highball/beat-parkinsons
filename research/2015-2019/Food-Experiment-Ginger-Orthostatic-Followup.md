# Ginger in dietary plans and a vascular protocol

## Registered ginger drink and postprandial pressure

NCT02735486 first posted April 12, 2016. It records a completed randomized crossover in 22 healthy men with ginger drink and water, with blood pressure and flow-mediated dilation around a meal. No results are posted in the official registry. The Europe PMC thesis record ETH:701648, Ukeyima 2016, has a closely matching design and sample size; its indexed abstract ends partway through the blood-pressure result. The full thesis, confirmation of the link and the statistical and posture details remain open. ## Minced ginger in Parkinson diet plans and lightheadedness reports

The 2018 Phillips trial randomized 47 people with Parkinson disease, 44 started and 38 completed. The low-fat meal plan's stir-fried vegetables recipe on printed page 76 uses minced ginger. The ketogenic plan's low-carb chicken curry on printed page 83 uses ginger minced and blended before cooking. Individual food consumption and mechanical grating are not reported. Adverse-event Table 3 lists lightheadedness during weeks 1–4 and 5–8 in 2/23 and 4/23 low-fat participants and 2/24 and 2/24 ketogenic participants. The event is not specified as triggered by standing, and these are group safety counts. One low-fat participant with pre-existing postural hypotension and falls required a midodrine increase for ongoing falls. The authors report no link between that event and a particular meal.
