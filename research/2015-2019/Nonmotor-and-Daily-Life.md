# Nonmotor and daily life

### 2017 — Endoscopic testing detected aspiration in early Parkinson’s

Pflug and colleagues used flexible endoscopic swallowing assessment in 119 consecutive patients across Parkinson’s stages and 32 controls. They observed aspiration in 30 patients during the protocol, including people with early disease. A cross-sectional specialty cohort establishes that aspiration can occur early and leaves the community frequency and subsequent pneumonia risk unresolved. The journal issue appeared in 2018. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28828545/) · [Same-cohort dissertation and bolus-specific results](Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#water-buttered-bread-and-wheat-biscuit-in-the-hamburg-endoscopy-cohort)

### 2019 — Home fall-prevention program improved function with no overall fall reduction

The PDSAFE trial randomized 474 people at risk of falling to routine care with or without tailored physiotherapist-led home training. It found no significant overall reduction in repeat falling over six months. Balance, chair-rise performance and confidence improved, and near-falls decreased. Subgroup findings suggested differences by disease severity and need independent confirmation. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30944149/)
