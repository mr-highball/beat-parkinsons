# Isolated piperine and rodent memory

## Injected piperine in an STZ rat memory model

Published online September 20, 2017, with a January 2018 journal issue. PMID
28939403. DOI 10.1016/j.bbr.2017.09.031. The original report used a rat model
of experimental cognitive injury induced by intracerebroventricular
streptozotocin. The piperine arms received isolated compound by intraperitoneal
injection in distinct pretreatment and post-treatment schedules. The radial
arm maze figure reports six animals per group.

After injury, the 2.5 mg/kg arm recorded 6.66 ± 0.7 correct nonrepeated arm
entries compared with 3.83 ± 0.6 for the injury vehicle group. Reference errors
were 1.66 ± 0.8 compared with 5.66 ± 1.2 (U=5, p<0.05). These are reported
means and standard errors. The 5 and 10 mg/kg post-treatment groups had no
significant reference-error differences against the negative control. The
5 mg/kg pretreatment group performed more poorly than the lower-dose
pretreatment or selected post-treatment groups on the reported memory
comparisons. Dose and timing differed across contrasts. The study measured
spatial memory in rats, with no clinical sustained-attention outcome or
culinary pepper comparison.
