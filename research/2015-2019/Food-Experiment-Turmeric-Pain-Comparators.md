# Registered turmeric powder comparison for menstrual pain

## Turmeric powder added to usual pain medication registration

The official NCT04183556 record was first posted December 3, 2019. It lists a turmeric powder plus a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory comparison with the same analgesic alone in people with primary dysmenorrhea. The primary outcome is a visual analogue pain score. The public record is completed and has no posted results table. Its linked article reports outcomes separately.
