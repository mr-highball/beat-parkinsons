# Refined rice bran oil and Parkinson symptom evidence

## Hoarseness in survivors of a contaminated rice oil outbreak

The investigators compared 1,131 Japanese Yusho survivors surveyed in 2008 with a general population sample matched by age and residential area. The exposure was oil implicated in a contamination outbreak first reported in 1968. Hoarseness and dysgeusia were among the outcomes associated with Yusho in the abstract. The analysis does not isolate effects of rice bran oil from the contamination, qualify oil refinement, or report reduced voice amplitude, handwriting or a Parkinson population. [Akahane primary article abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00244-017-0486-6).
