# Cardamom endpoint evidence

## Injected cineole and haloperidol catalepsy

Figueiredo et al. published online 5 September 2019. The accessible original abstract reports that intraperitoneal 1,8-cineole 50 mg/kg and separately Hyptis martiusii leaf oil potentiated haloperidol-induced catalepsy in female mice. Exact numerical tables and full methods were unavailable through the publisher route. The induced catalepsy outcome is not clinical dystonia and neither exposure is ground green cardamom. [Original author abstract, PMID 31493462](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31493462/)
