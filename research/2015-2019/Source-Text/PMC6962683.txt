Title: Participant and Study Partner Reported Impact of Cognition on Functional Activities in Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6962683/
PMID: 31970213
PMCID: PMC6962683
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12870
Publication date: 2019-12-14
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML original body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC and PubMed metadata.
Design: Parkinson cognition and daily function report

Parkinson participants and study partners rated the impact of cognition on instrumental activities of daily living. Subjective and partner reports and neuropsychological measures address function. The study does not record visual episode rates after a food intervention.
