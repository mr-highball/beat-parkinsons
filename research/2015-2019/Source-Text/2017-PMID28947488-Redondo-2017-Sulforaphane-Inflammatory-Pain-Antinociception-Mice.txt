Title: Treatment with Sulforaphane Produces Antinociception and Improves Morphine Effects during Inflammatory Pain in Mice.
PMID: 28947488
DOI: 10.1124/jpet.117.244376
First published: 2017
Authors: Redondo A, Chamorro PAF, Riego G, Leánez S, Pol O
Journal: The Journal of pharmacology and experimental therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28947488/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical study in male C57BL/6 mice with complete Freund's adjuvant (CFA) inflammatory pain. Sulforaphane (5 and 10 mg/kg) inhibited CFA-induced mechanical allodynia and thermal hyperalgesia, enhanced local morphine antinociception, induced Nrf2/HO-1/NQO1 and mu-opioid receptor expression, and suppressed spinal/paw NOS2 and microglial activation. Limits: purified sulforaphane in non-PD mice.
