Title: Eicosapentaenoic and Docosahexaenoic Acid-Enriched High Fat Diet Delays Skeletal Muscle Degradation in Mice
Publication date: 2016-09 (journal publication)
PMCID: PMC5037530
DOI: 10.3390/nu8090543
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5037530/
Text coverage: Primary full article and P210 report lineage.

Male C57BL/6J mice received corn-oil-enriched high-fat chow, a high-fat diet enriched in EPA and DHA, or standard chow for eight weeks. Muscle RNA and protein measures, including the slow-fiber marker Tnnc1, differed with the marine-fat formulation. P210 presents a compatible comparison and markers. This muscle-tissue experiment does not measure rigidity, stride, fatigue, depression, pain or constipation. Grade and context remain distinct from eating refined corn oil in Parkinson disease.
