Aqueous lemon balm leaf extract and severe-depression screening
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jep.2015.02.007
Coverage: factual extract of primary Table 3
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Alijaniha and colleagues randomized 71 adults with benign palpitations to a freeze-dried aqueous leaf extract or placebo for 14 days. Table 3 reported severe-depression threshold counts among 55 completers: extract 3/28 to 3/28, placebo 10/27 to 6/27 (within-arm p = 1 and .12). The thresholds started imbalanced. No verified between-group change test or continuous depression severity estimate applies to this item.
