Title: Effect of guava and vitamin C supplementation on experimental gingivitis: A randomized clinical trial
PMID: 29757462
DOI: 10.1111/jcpe.12922
First published: 2018
Authors: Amaliya A, Risdiana AS, Van der Velden U
Journal: Journal of Clinical Periodontology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29757462/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized clinical trial in young nonsmoking adults assigned 200 g/d raw guava fruit, 200 mg/d synthetic vitamin C, or water during a 14-day pre-experimental and 14-day oral-hygiene-abstention experimental gingivitis protocol. Both raw guava and vitamin C significantly blunted Gingival Index rise vs control (delta GI 0.10, 0.24 vs 0.87), and guava lowered Plaque Index rise. Direct raw guava fruit human RCT, but periodontal gingivitis endpoint only; no PD motor or non-motor symptoms evaluated.
