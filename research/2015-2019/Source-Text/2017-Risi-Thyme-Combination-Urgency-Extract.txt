# Thyme in an eight-herb urinary urgency study

Source https://www.pagepressjournals.org/aiua/article/download/aiua.2017.4.293/6904/33027
Coverage original Methods and Table 1

Risi and colleagues reported 42 women with mild stress or mixed incontinence taking an eight-herb product containing thyme. Table 1 gives urgency-intensity means of 1.357 ± 1.509 before and 1.26 ± 1.481 after two months (p = .705). All eight participants with the maximum baseline urgency score remained at that score. Five of 17 with urgency improved and one worsened. The uncontrolled product study does not identify the species, part and preparation of thyme. It does not measure changes in Parkinson urgency frequency after fresh thyme.
