Title: Aubert et al. — fixed STW 5 blend in repeated stress and intestinal barrier experiments
DOI: 10.1055/s-0037-1608490
Source: https://pubblicazioni.unicam.it/retrieve/e0ff0073-c528-9bac-e053-1705fe0af019/GA%202017%20%E2%80%93%20Book%20of%20Abstracts.pdf
Member: Tu-Poster Session 2-PO-162
Companion: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7672678/ (P1127)
Publication date: 2017, matching the original meeting supplements.
Coverage: Both original abstract bodies and their matching treatment and endpoint descriptions.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18 UTC

The authors compared a fixed herbal formulation with controls in mice exposed to repeated water avoidance stress. The blend is identified as STW 5 or Iberogast. Another UEG member, P0547, explicitly lists Melissa officinalis among nine plant extracts in the formulation.

P1127 and Tu-PO-162 give the same study design and consistent results. The formulation changed several segment-specific permeability results in tissue preparations and modified stress-related colonic transit. Total transit and blood corticosterone did not show clear changes. The investigators did not separate a lemon balm arm or measure depressive severity. Do not treat the matching conference reports as independent experiments.
