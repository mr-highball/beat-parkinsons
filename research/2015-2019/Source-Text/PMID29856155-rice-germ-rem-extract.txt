Byun and colleagues randomized 30 participants to 300 mg/day fermented rice-germ-derived GABA and 10 to placebo for four weeks. Active REM share was 16.3% before treatment and 18.3% after treatment. Placebo values were 17.8% and 18.1%. Neither the REM share nor REM latency interaction was statistically significant. The general sleep latency interaction was p=.009. There was no separate dream-enactment ascertainment in the published table.
