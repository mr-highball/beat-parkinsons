Title: Effects of lavender on anxiety: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
PMID: 31655395
DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2019.153099
First published: 2019
Authors: Donelli D, Antonelli M, Bellinazzi C, Gensini GF, Firenzuoli F
Journal: Phytomedicine : international journal of phytotherapy and phytopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31655395/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis (Donelli et al., 2019, Phytomedicine, PROSPERO CRD42019130126) synthesizing 65 RCTs (n=7,993) and 25 NRSs (n=1,200) of lavender for anxiety across administration routes. Oral Silexan 80 mg/day (>=6 weeks) significantly reduced HAMA (MD -2.90, 95% CI -4.86 to -0.95, p=0.004, n=1,173) and SAS (MD -2.62, 95% CI -4.84 to -0.39, p<0.05, n=451), with high methodological heterogeneity and bias across non-oral inhalation and massage trials. Review/meta-analysis providing route-separated context.
