A randomized placebo-controlled study in adults aged 60–75 used freeze-dried blueberry powder and measured motor and cognitive performance at baseline and after 45 and 90 days. The abstract describes improvement in selected variables without providing their estimates. It separately describes a cellular assay using berry metabolites.

Frozen claim 0746 specifies raw blueberries and constipation. The member supplies neither exposure nor endpoint. Cohort overlap with separately published blueberry outcomes is possible.
