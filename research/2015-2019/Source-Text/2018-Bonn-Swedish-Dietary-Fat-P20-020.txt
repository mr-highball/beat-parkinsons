Factual extract from a conference abstract
Source: Bonn SE and colleagues. Dietary Fat Intake and Risk of Parkinson Disease in a Large Swedish Cohort. P20-020 in Nutritional Epidemiology, Current Developments in Nutrition, 2018.
PMCID of collection: PMC6265811
Original source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6265811/
Population: Swedish National March Cohort. The report describes 43,863 participants and 292 incident diagnoses during register follow-up from 1997 through 2010.
Exposure: baseline questionnaire for total, saturated, monounsaturated and polyunsaturated fat intake. The reported analyses categorized total fat and applied models for individual fatty acid groups and isocaloric substitutions.
Outcomes: incident Parkinson diagnosis. The reported HRs for high total and saturated intake were 1.31 (95% CI 1.04–1.66) and 1.87 (95% CI 1.11–3.15). Substitution of saturated for polyunsaturated fat per 5 percentage points of energy gave HR 2.75 (95% CI 1.02–7.40). No Parkinson daytime sleepiness or potato-specific estimate was reported.
Later report: PMID 35416636, PMCID PMC9288363, 2022. Treat as overlapping cohort with revised methods and follow-up.
