Phillips et al. PMID 30098269 DOI 10.1002/mds.27390. 2018 trial, 23 randomized low-fat, 24 ketogenic; 38 completed, imputation used. Low-fat supplement p.57 smoothie contains one cup strawberries, mango and yogurt. Ketogenic supplement p.100 strawberry halves 2/3 cup is one of seven optional fruit choices, with cream; menu permits fresh or frozen strawberries. Trial authors recorded meal completion checklists, not individual ingredient selections. Cognitive impairment item showed between-arm difference in changes -0.27 points favoring ketogenic; this broad item is not an isolated attention score. One low-fat participant had pre-existing hypotension and falls and medication adjustment for ongoing falls; no comparative fall count is given. Multiple diet differences preclude fruit attribution.
