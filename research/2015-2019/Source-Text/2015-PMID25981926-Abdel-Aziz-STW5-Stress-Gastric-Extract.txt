Title: Novel sequential stress model for functional dyspepsia: Efficacy of the herbal preparation STW5

Male rats underwent neonatal maternal separation, adult restraint stress or both. Randomized subgroups are described as eight animals; figure captions specify at least five preparations from different animals. Commercial STW5, nine hydroethanolic extracts including 10% Melissa leaf extract, was given orally at 5 mL/kg for two weeks versus vehicle containing 31% alcohol.

Some gastric muscle responses and plasma hormones improved. Potassium-chloride response contrasts were inconclusive, p=.27 and .08 in the adult-stress and combined models. Adrenaline relaxation improved under combined stress but was inconclusive under adult stress alone. No behavioral depression outcome is reported in the inspected Methods and Results. Hormones and gastric responsiveness are distinct from depressive symptoms.

Manufacturer funding and employee coauthors are disclosed. The UEG 2015 P1103 poster cites this model paper but uses additional doses and outcomes; shared animals or independent replication are unverified. Its indexed conclusion may cross an adjacent member boundary. Neither preparation isolates brewed lemon balm or a Parkinson outcome.
