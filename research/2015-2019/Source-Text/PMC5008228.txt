Title: Nilotinib Effects in Parkinson's disease and Dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5008228/

DOI: 10.3233/jpd-160867
Publication date: 2016-07-11
Date basis: NLM original e-publication and PMC earlier public date; separate August issue metadata
Source depth: Complete original PMC structured article body
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The 12-person Parkinson dementia or Lewy body dementia pilot compared two active nilotinib dose groups with open-label monitoring. It reports one mild hallucination event in the lower group and two in the higher group and describes broader psychotic symptoms during changes to other Parkinson medications. The event report does not classify visual modality or frequency, and the trial had no dried fig exposure.

The pilot paper cites registry NCT02281474. Thirty people were screened and 12 entered the active comparison, with five and seven in the two treatment groups. The primary comparison randomized dose in an open-label setting. Some participants reduced levodopa or stopped or reduced concurrent rasagiline during the study. The paper also discusses broader motor, cognitive and biochemical changes without a placebo control. The recorded hallucination adverse events are separate from the discussion of psychosis, which combines hallucinations, paranoia and agitation.

This trial predates and is distinct from the 75-person Georgetown NCT02954978 placebo-controlled trial and the 76-person multicenter NCT03205488 trial. The open-label extension of NCT02954978 rerandomized eligible completers, so it is part of the same parent study family.
