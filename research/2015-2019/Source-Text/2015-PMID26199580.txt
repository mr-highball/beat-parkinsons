Title: SurAsleep mixture and sleep symptoms
First published or posted: 2015-06
PMID: 26199580
PMCID: PMC4502736
Source URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4502736/
Text type: original-source evidence summary

Xie and colleagues randomized 100 adults over 50 with mild or moderate sleep complaints to a multicomponent supplement (47) or flour placebo (53) for 12 weeks. The supplement combined oat straw extract, valerian, calcium, magnesium, L-theanine and melatonin. Oat extract dose was unavailable. Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index means (SD) were 9.64 (3.54) versus 9.83 (4.21) at baseline and 4.51 (2.65) versus 9.02 (4.09) at study end. The final-score comparison had P<.001. The paper did not give a between-group change estimate. This trial measured the mixture and included no separate oat arm, rolled-oat food or Parkinson cohort.
