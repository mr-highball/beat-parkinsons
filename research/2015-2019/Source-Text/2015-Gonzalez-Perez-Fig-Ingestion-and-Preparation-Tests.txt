Title: Immediate anaphylaxis after the ingestion of fig fruit (Ficus carica)
DOI: 10.1186/2045-7022-5-S3-P167
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/2045-7022-5-S3-P167
Publication year: 2015
Coverage: Original conference supplement poster abstract including case, laboratory tests and challenge status.

The author report describes nasal or eye symptoms and substantial abdominal pain immediately following fig ingestion. The ingested fruit's preparation is unspecified in the case description. Diagnostic testing separately used raw fresh and dried fig material and reported positive results for both. The planned controlled fig challenge did not take place.

The observed food reaction and diagnostic sensitization do not establish separate clinical effects for fresh and dried intake. There is no Parkinson population or visual hallucination outcome in the report.
