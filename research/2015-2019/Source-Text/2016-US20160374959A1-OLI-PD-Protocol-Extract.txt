Title: Olive polyphenol clinical plans in patent application US20160374959A1
Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/US20160374959A1/en
First published: 2016-12-29
Coverage: published application, Example 2 paragraphs 0088–0103. Local factual extract.

The applicants describe OLI-PD, an open-label crossover plan involving 17 participants and a proprietary hydroxytyrosol-containing olive-polyphenol supplement alongside usual treatment. The plan refers to two named predecessor studies, OLIVE-PD and AD-OLIVE-PD, and lists broad clinical evaluation scales. Invalidating tremor was an exclusion in the early-stage predecessor plan. The application provides a source trail for the names and proposed evaluations. The example supplies no verified numerical rest-tremor result or registered comparison of culinary extra-virgin olive oil. An independent report or registry linking any of the named studies remains open.
