Nigella derived melanin and reflux registry outcomes
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02587910
Registration: NCT02587910
Coverage: Official registry intervention, population and endpoints

First posted October 27, 2015. The public registry identifies Melanole as a Nigella sativa extract. The planned capsules supplied a purified herbal-derived preparation during a reflux comparison against placebo. Outcomes include GERD-Q, pH-defined reflux episodes and treatment-emergent reports. The protocol supplies no Parkinson sample, assigned ground seed or measured dysphagia endpoint. Its official status is Unknown and no results are posted.
