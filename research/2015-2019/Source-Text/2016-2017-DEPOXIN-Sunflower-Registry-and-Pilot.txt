Official registry ISRCTN81655012, https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN81655012, first posted 2016-10-19.
Registered 12-week comparison of fish oil emulsion and omega-6-rich sunflower oil emulsion in ages 8–18 with depressive disorder or mixed anxiety and depressive disorder. The registry lists first enrollment in March 2014. The 2017 pilot reports June 2013 to December 2015. Both place the start before registration.
Trebatická et al., pilot first published 2017-07-05, DOI 10.1186/s13034-017-0167-2. The report identifies the same trial registry, with 38 randomized and 35 completing. The publication describes care alongside the study oils; the Discussion reports that four fish-oil and six sunflower completers did not take an antidepressant during the trial. The later 60-person report shares the registry. Refined grade was not specified.
