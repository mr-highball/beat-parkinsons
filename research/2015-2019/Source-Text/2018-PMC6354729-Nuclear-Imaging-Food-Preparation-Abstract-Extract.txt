Atterton-Evans and colleagues review four cardiac FDG-PET dietary-preparation studies for suspected sarcoidosis. One reported mixed meal includes egg, tofu, grilled chicken and vegetables. Other protocols vary food restrictions and fasting. Imaging preparation is the intervention context; neither egg nor chicken is independently assigned, and poached food, falls and fatigue are not tested outcomes.

The review cites Manabe DOI 10.1007/s12350-015-0226-0. Its original meal, cohort and outcome details remain a citation trail, not facts established by this poster.
