Title: Antihypertensive effect of the methanolic extract from Eruca sativa Mill., (Brassicaceae) in rats: Muscarinic receptor-linked vasorelaxant and cardiotonic effects.
PMID: 29913298
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2018.06.013
First published: 2018
Authors: Salma U, Khan T, Shah AJ
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29913298/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical cardiovascular pharmacology study (Salma, Khan & Shah, 2018, J Ethnopharmacol) in anesthetized normotensive and high-salt hypertensive rats testing methanolic and aqueous extracts of Eruca sativa Mill. (with quercetin and erucin detected by HPLC). Intravenous crude extract lowered mean arterial pressure (max fall 41.79 +/- 1.55% normotensive, 58.25 +/- 0.91% hypertensive, atropine-sensitive p<0.001), and oral crude/aqueous extracts lowered MAP in hypertensive rats via muscarinic-NO vasodilation and calcium channel inhibition. Limits: anesthetized rat systemic hypotension model; does not assess human Parkinson's neurogenic orthostatic lightheadedness.
