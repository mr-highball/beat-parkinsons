Title: Masticatory function parameters in patients with removable dental prosthesis.
PMID: 31123524
PMCID: PMC6527414
DOI: 10.25122/jml-2019-0028
First published: 2019
Authors: Oncescu Moraru AM, Preoteasa CT, Preoteasa E
Journal: J Med Life
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31123524/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Clinical mastication study in older adults (>=45 y) with removable dental prostheses chewing digestive biscuit, apple, and raw carrot. Raw carrot consistently required the longest chewing time and highest number of mastication cycles—especially in older and systemically impaired subjects—documenting high mechanical chewing demand of raw carrot.
