The tested intervention is Acacia catechu leaf extract. The authors discuss an earlier acetylcholinesterase screen of medicinal plants and name Myristica fragrans in that context (reference 48, Mukherjee and colleagues, 2007, Phytotherapy Research 21:1142–1145). Nutmeg was not a comparative arm in the current study. This cross-reference preserves a constituent-screening trail without attributing the Acacia cognitive findings to nutmeg or transferring them to sleep.

Claim 0774 | PMID:26949964 | The discussion traces Myristica fragrans to a cited acetylcholinesterase screening paper. The actual feeding and cognition experiments here used Acacia catechu.
