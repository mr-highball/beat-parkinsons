Title: Selenium reduces bradykinesia and DNA damage in a rat model of Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 25592015
DOI: 10.1016/j.nut.2014.07.004
First published: 2015
Authors: Ellwanger JH, Molz P, Dallemole DR, Pereira dos Santos A, Müller TE, Cappelletti L, et al.
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25592015/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled rat study (n=48 male Wistar rats) administering inorganic selenium in drinking water (11.18 ug/L) prior to paraquat exposure: prevented paraquat-induced bradykinesia on the narrow-beam test (p<0.01). Limits: inorganic drinking-water selenium (not Brazil nut or selenomethionine) in a paraquat toxin model.
