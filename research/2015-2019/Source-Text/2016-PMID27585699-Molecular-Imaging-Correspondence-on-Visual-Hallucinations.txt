This complete AJNR correspondence comments on the separate imaging review PMID26744445 and emphasizes molecular imaging in the visual hallucination differential. It supplies interpretive context without a new patient sample or fig comparison.

The article explicitly names the Winton-Brown imaging review. The recommendations discuss signs seen with dopaminergic or glucose-metabolism imaging and represent the authors' interpretation.
