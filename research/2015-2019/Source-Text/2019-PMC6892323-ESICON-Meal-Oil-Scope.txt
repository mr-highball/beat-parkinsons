Title: ESICON 2019 Abstracts
Publication date: 2019-11
Publication: Indian Journal of Endocrinology and Metabolism 23, supplement 1, S5–S83
PMCID: PMC6892323
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6892323/
Coverage: Original collection and member text. Food study titled Leukocyte, insulin, and glucose response to high-carbohydrate and high-fat meals: A comparative study.

Agarwal and colleagues studied four meals at weekly intervals in eight healthy adults. The study plan names carbohydrate, coconut oil, canola oil and sunflower oil meals. Results repeatedly name olive oil where the design listed canola oil. This inconsistency prevents attribution of that arm to either oil. The authors report post-meal blood cell counts, insulin and glucose. Coconut and sunflower are actual named meal components. Refining and pressing methods are unreported. The experiment supplies no individual Parkinson outcome for falls, balance, pain or anxiety.

Elsewhere in the collection the acronym OATs refers to oncocytic adrenocortical tumors. It does not identify rolled oats. No studied date fruit exposure was found in the context examined.
