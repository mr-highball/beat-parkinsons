Title: Dynamic functional connectivity changes in dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer's disease

Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC and PubMed metadata.
Design: Clinical functional imaging comparison in dementia

The study recruited 102 older participants from two independent contemporary samples and used an analyzed cohort after exclusions. The authors report no clear association of selected dynamic connectivity measures with visual hallucination severity in dementia with Lewy bodies. The outcome is an imaging correlation with symptoms rather than a food intervention.
