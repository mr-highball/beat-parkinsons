A healthy volunteer pharmacological challenge compares resting-state network connectivity after serotonergic and cholinergic interventions. It mentions prior visual hallucination research in discussing potential mechanisms. The exposure and outcome do not match dried fig intake or Parkinson visual episode frequency.
