Title: Relationship between Dementia Severity and Behavioral and Psychological Symptoms of Dementia in Dementia with Lewy Bodies and Alzheimer's Disease Patients

Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary cross-sectional comparison
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The study compares behavioral and psychological symptom scores in dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer disease by Clinical Dementia Rating stage. The Neuropsychiatric Inventory obtained caregiver information for the preceding month. The Lewy body group scored higher on the hallucination domain overall, with stage-specific analyses. The reported domain combines symptom characteristics and does not isolate longitudinal visual episode counts in Parkinson disease.
