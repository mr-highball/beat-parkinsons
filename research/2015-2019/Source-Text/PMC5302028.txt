Title: Effects of Safinamide on Pain in Fluctuating Parkinson's Disease Patients: A Post-Hoc Analysis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5302028/
PMID: 27802242
PMCID: PMC5302028
DOI: 10.3233/JPD-160911
Publication date: 2016-10-11 (first electronic publication; 2017 issue)
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Factual extract from the original PMC XML article body.

The secondary pooled analysis reused two randomized safinamide-versus-placebo studies. At 24 weeks, the analysis reported 23.6% lower concomitant pain-treatment use for the safinamide group relative to placebo (p=0.0421). Three items in the PDQ-39 bodily-discomfort domain supplied additional symptom information. Concomitant analgesic use and a quality-of-life subdomain are different from a standalone pain-intensity instrument. The article provides a comparator for outcome measurement without dried-date exposure.
