Title: 2018 Cumin intake in a Parkinson diet survey
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6312592/


Sauerbier et al., published 2018-12-18, PMID 30662706, PMCID PMC6312592, DOI 10.1155/2018/7274085, surveyed 139 Parkinson patients about spices and herbs used in the previous month. Cumin was checked by 11/66 White, 25/34 Asian, and 8/39 Black African/Caribbean participants. The questionnaire did not specify cumin botanical identity, toasting or dose. The study examined Hoehn–Yahr stage and nonmotor symptom scales, with no cumin-specific dyskinesia comparison. Reported individual use is not assigned treatment or evidence of change in dyskinesia.
