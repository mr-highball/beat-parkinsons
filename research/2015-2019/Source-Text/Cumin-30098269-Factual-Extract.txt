Title: 2018 Heated cumin in a mixed Parkinson diet trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175383/


Phillips et al., 2018, PMID 30098269, PMCID PMC6175383, DOI 10.1002/mds.27390, ketogenic recipe supplement S3 p.72 includes cumin added to a pan with oil and vegetables and then simmered in mixed chicken enchilada soup. It does not say seed was toasted; individual ingredient consumption is unverified. Motor complications were reported as total MDS-UPDRS IV, not cumin-specific dyskinesia severity. Diet group comparisons and individual medication changes cannot isolate the ingredient effect.
