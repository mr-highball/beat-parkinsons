Title: Assessment of the effect of sachet formulation of almond (Amygdalus dulcis L.) on diarrhea prominent irritable bowel syndrome (IBS-D) symptoms: A clinical trial.
PMID: 31331568
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2019.07.001
First published: 2019
Authors: Darvishmoghadam, Ansari, Ahmadipour, Azimipour, Esmaeilzade
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31331568/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

20-day double-blind parallel RCT in 50 adults with diarrhea-predominant irritable bowel syndrome (IBS-D) randomized to standardized almond powder sachet (40 g/d) or wheat-flour placebo sachet. None of the primary IBS-D outcomes improved; bowel movement frequency and abdominal pain severity significantly increased in the almond-treated group compared with placebo and baseline (p < 0.05), which authors attributed to fermentable oligo-fructans at 40 g/d intake. Limits: IBS-D population with visceral hypersensitivity, powdered sachet rather than whole roasted or raw almonds, abstract omits arm-level numerical means.
