Title: Metabolome based volatiles mapping of roasted umbelliferous fruits aroma via HS-SPME GC/MS and peroxide levels analyses
PMID: 30268004
DOI: 10.1016/j.jchromb.2018.09.022
Publication date: 2018-09-22
Citation: Elmassry, Kormod, Labib and Farag. Journal of Chromatography B 1099, 117–126. The issue appeared November 1, 2018.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30268004/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1570023218305920
Author institution record: https://research.asu.edu.eg/handle/123456789/187774
Coverage: Author abstract, publisher preview and author institution record. Full analytical tables remain open.

The investigators compared unroasted and roasted fruits from five spice crops. Fennel samples differed by origin in headspace estragole. The authors report lower fennel estragole after roasting and a peroxide value of 6.1 mEq O2/kg for roasted fennel. Headspace composition, apparent volatile abundance and peroxide measurements characterize the prepared food. The study included no clinical intervention or symptom outcome. The actual roasting schedule, analyzed sample-specific contrasts and post-roast amounts of individual compounds require the full tables.
