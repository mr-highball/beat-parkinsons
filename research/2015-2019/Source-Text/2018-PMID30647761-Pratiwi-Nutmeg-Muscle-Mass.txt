Title: Nutmeg extract and skeletal muscle mass in aging rats
Publication date: 2018-12-17
PMCID: PMC6311876
PMID: 30647761
DOI: 10.1155/2018/2810840
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6311876/
Verified depth: original article body and figure captions
Checked: 2026-11-22

Pratiwi and colleagues assigned 20 aging male Wistar rats to oral nutmeg extract or water for 12 weeks. The authors report a greater soleus muscle mass and changes in muscle-associated markers after treatment. The experiment used a specialized extract described as free of safrole and myristicin. Clinical fatigue, sleep, mood, constipation and Parkinson symptoms were outside the measurements.

The S280 conference report 2P-545 contains the same investigator group, animal count and age, treatment duration and soleus endpoints. It is a report of the same research, with no independent evidence count. The journal introduction attributes a general aging-related use of nutmeg to traditional communities in Sulawesi. It provides no measured symptom or independent historical account of that use.
