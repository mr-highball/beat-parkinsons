Original study scope
The antipsychotic movement-disorder review describes red rice bran oil in a haloperidol rat study. Figure 6 summarizes preclinical movement and dyskinesia-like effects. The original cited report is Naz and Shireen 2014, DOI 10.29052/IJEHSR.v2.i2.2014.82-92. That report gives a catalepsy result in opposite directions under different drug backgrounds.

Continuing evidence gaps
Keep the review and original as one evidence family. The research-grade red oil is not verified as commercial refined oil. Catalepsy is a movement proxy, vacuous chewing is dyskinesia-like, and neither establishes Parkinson dystonia or bowel outcomes.
