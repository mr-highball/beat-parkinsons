Title: IGMC poster P105 and the computed targets of date-fruit constituents

DOI: 10.1186/s12864-016-2858-0

First publication: 2016-07-20 proceedings for the 2015 meeting.
Coverage: Original complete NCBI EFetch PMC proceedings, member P105 and its Figure 40 caption. Identifiers belong to the proceedings.

The investigators selected named date-fruit constituents from the literature for computational pathway analysis. The Methods list includes luteolin, beta-glucan, apigenin, carotenoids, lutein, proanthocyanidins and stigmasterol, and the Results additionally name quercetin among shared predicted targets. Figure 40 displays annotated molecular networks for selected compounds. Cancer signaling and predicted interaction pathways were the comparison. This member reports no ingestion, measured pain outcome, Parkinson population or clinical assessment. Its reference to suggested anti-inflammatory properties is background rather than an observed symptom response.
