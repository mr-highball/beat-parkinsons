2016 — Fennel oil and anethole in drug delayed gastric emptying

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0006291X16302674
Identifier: 26915803

Asano and colleagues’ primary abstract reports that separately administered fennel oil, anethole and a multicomponent Anchu-san preparation improved clonidine-induced delayed gastric emptying in mice. Anethole did not accelerate normal gastric emptying in the reported mouse comparison. It also improved impaired gastric accommodation in rats. The oil, compound and formula are distinct exposures. The original abstract verifies the reported directions. Arm-level estimates from the full Methods and figures remain an access gap.
