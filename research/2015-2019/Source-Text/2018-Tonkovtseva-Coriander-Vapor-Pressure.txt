2018 — Inhaled coriander essential oil during relaxation
Source: https://boolt.nbgnsc.ru/download/boolt/126/7-126-2018.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of Russian Methods and Table 1.

Ten-minute systolic means changed from 156.70 to 139.10 mm Hg with coriander vapor (n = 15) and 157.10 to 154.50 with relaxation alone (n = 20). The 20-minute comparison also reached significance. At 30 minutes the difference was inconclusive. This study used inhalation and reported no standing challenge.
