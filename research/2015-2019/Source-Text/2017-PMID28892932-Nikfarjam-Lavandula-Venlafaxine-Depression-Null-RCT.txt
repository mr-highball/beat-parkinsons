Title: Comparison of Effect of Lavandula officinalis and Venlafaxine in Treating Depression: A Double Blind Clinical Trial.
PMID: 28892932
PMCID: PMC5583809
DOI: 10.7860/JCDR/2017/20657.10233
First published: 2017
Authors: Nikfarjam M, Rakhshan R, Ghaderi H
Journal: Journal of clinical and diagnostic research : JCDR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28892932/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind randomized controlled trial (Nikfarjam et al., 2017, J Clin Diagn Res, PMC5583809) enrolling 120 psychiatry clinic patients with depression randomized for 6 weeks to venlafaxine alone (control), venlafaxine plus Lavandula officinalis, or venlafaxine plus placebo. Depression scores decreased over time in all groups (p=0.001); both venlafaxine + L. officinalis (p=0.004) and venlafaxine + placebo (p=0.002) differed from venlafaxine alone, but L. officinalis did NOT differ from placebo (p=0.95). Crucial null/contrary oral lavender add-on depression trial in a double-blind placebo-controlled design.
