Symposium 12.1 uses intramuscular sesame oil as the vehicle comparator in haloperidol-treated rats and assesses brain metabolic markers. It supplies no culinary preparation, oral-oil exposure or mapped Parkinson symptom result.

T191 describes a five-week feasibility trial in 16 people with schizophrenia and elevated antigliadin antibodies; 14 completed. Gluten or rice-flour challenges were given while meals were gluten-free. Negative-symptom and attention effect-size trends were exploratory. Barley was identified as a gluten source, but boiled barley was not independently assigned and a separate anxiety result was not reported. This mixed dietary challenge does not isolate a food or establish Parkinson efficacy.
