Title: Aberrant functional connectivity of resting state networks related to misperceptions and intra-individual variability in Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 31794926
PMCID: PMC6906716
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2019.102076
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6906716/
First publication date: 2019-11-05 (Europe PMC original-search record).

The study compares perceptual task errors and response times in Parkinson groups with and without self-reported hallucinations and uses a smaller resting-state fMRI subset. Visual recognition errors, time variability and existing hallucination severity are separate from food-induced changes.
