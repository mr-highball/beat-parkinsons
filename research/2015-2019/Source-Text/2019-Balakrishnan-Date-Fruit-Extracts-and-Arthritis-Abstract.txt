Title: Ameliorative potential of Saudi Arabian date fruit (Phoenix dactylifera L.) varieties against Freund’s complete adjuvant induced arthritis in rats
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13596-019-00377-x
DOI: 10.1007/s13596-019-00377-x
Publication date: 2019-05-31
Date basis: Publisher first online date. The issue version is 2020, volume 20, pages 291–301.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Review depth: abstract

The publisher abstract identifies oral extracts of Sukkari, Ekhlass Almajmaah, Abu Minifee and Dawee date fruit in a rat arthritis model and includes an indomethacin comparison. It reports smaller paw volume and arthritis scores along with several biochemical outcomes. It supplies no stand-alone pain behavior value, between-group estimate for pain or Parkinson population. The arthritis-score components require the full Methods. The study is kept at publisher-abstract depth with the reported extract preparation and clinical transfer limits.
