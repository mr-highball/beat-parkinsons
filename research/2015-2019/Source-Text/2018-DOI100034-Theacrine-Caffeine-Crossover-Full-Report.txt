Original source scope
Twenty-four men and 26 women received the same five conditions and were assessed on the same schedule as the 2017 P36 proceedings report, suggesting one cohort. The authors' subjective ratings analysis lists greater lethargy and grogginess for the lower theacrine condition than other conditions. The lethargy condition-by-time term is p=.99 and the gender-by-condition term is p=.01 in Table 7. The condition-level observation does not establish treatment-related worsening over time. Caffeine-containing conditions generally scored better on energy, focus or alertness measures. The design supplies neither brewed tea nor clinical Parkinson fatigue evidence. Round 1 0948.
