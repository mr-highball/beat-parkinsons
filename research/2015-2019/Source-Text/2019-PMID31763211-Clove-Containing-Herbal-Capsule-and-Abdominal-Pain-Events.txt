Original source scope
A randomized placebo comparison enrolled 64 constipated postmenopausal women. The herbal capsule combined powdered cloves with more abundant ingredients including senna, raisins and other botanicals. Abdominal pain was reported in 6 of 32 assigned the formula and 2 of 32 assigned placebo.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Pain was logged as presence of a side event. The trial did not measure a standalone clove effect, pain severity or a Parkinson population. It reports improved constipation after the entire formula.
