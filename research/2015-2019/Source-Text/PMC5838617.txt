Title: Bilateral Deep Brain Stimulation of the Nucleus Basalis of Meynert for Parkinson Disease Dementia: A Randomized Clinical Trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5838617/
PMID: 29255885
PMCID: PMC5838617
DOI: 10.1001/jamaneurol.2017.3762
Publication date: 2017-12-18
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML article; local research digest

Six Parkinson disease dementia participants complete a double-blind crossover of active versus sham stimulation. The primary cognitive measures did not consistently improve. The exploratory total Neuropsychiatric Inventory contrast favored active treatment by a median five points versus sham (95% CI 2.5–8.5). Two participants reported near-cessation of visual hallucinations while stimulation was active and resurgence after it was turned off. This is the registered NCT01701544 intervention and the parent cohort of the 36-month report PMC9346244. The trial does not test food intake.
