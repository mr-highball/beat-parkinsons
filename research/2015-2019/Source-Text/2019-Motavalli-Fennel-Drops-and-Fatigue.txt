The indexed original Table 2 lists fatigue after treatment in 8 of 36 participants in each arm, with baseline 4 of 36 versus 5 of 36. This measures occurrence during dysmenorrhea. The comparator and tabular analysis remain uncertain, and the oral extract does not establish an effect of toasted culinary fennel seed or a Parkinson fatigue-severity effect.

The paper describes 72 participants assigned in groups of 36, fennel extract drops against a distilled-water placebo, for three menstrual cycles. The paper reports no significant between-group differences for accompanying symptoms. The indexed table prints p=.21 for the post-intervention fatigue row despite equal counts. No interpretation of that printed probability is needed to identify the displayed result.
