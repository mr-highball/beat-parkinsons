Title: Improvement of Endurance Based on Muscle Fiber-Type Composition by Treatment with Dietary Apple Polyphenols in Rats.
PMID: 26222548
PMCID: PMC4519157
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0134303
First published: 2015
Authors: Mizunoya W, Miyahara H, Okamoto S, Akahoshi M, Suzuki T, Do MK et al.
Journal: PloS one
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26222548/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Pair-fed controlled experiment in n=18 (9/group) 9-week-old male Fischer F344 rats fed 5% (w/w) dietary apple polyphenols vs control diet for 8 weeks. Apple polyphenols significantly upregulated plantaris muscle oxidative myosin heavy chain IIx/IIb ratio and myoglobin protein expression (p<0.05) without changing food intake or body weight. Mechanistic muscle-fiber fatigue-resistance context only.
