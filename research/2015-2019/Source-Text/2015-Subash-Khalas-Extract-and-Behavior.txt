Coverage: factual digest of Methods, behavioral results and Figures 1–3.

Six female mice per group. The supplemented APPsw/Tg2576 groups received pellets containing 2% or 4% acetone extract of dried Khalas fruit for 14 months from age four months. Tests included open field, rotarod, elevated plus maze and memory mazes. The body describes improved movement and motor coordination relative to untreated mutant mice. The Figure 1 significance markers specify comparison to wild type. Time spent exploring, locomotion and maze performance do not provide a validated apathy measure. The journal names the earlier 2014 FASEB abstract 845.4 as the same research. The earlier abstract describes a differing age at testing; the separate 2014-online oxidative report has unresolved cohort linkage.
