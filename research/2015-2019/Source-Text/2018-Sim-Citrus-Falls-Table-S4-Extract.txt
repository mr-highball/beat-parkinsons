Orange and other citrus in an injurious falls cohort

PMID: 30153877
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114518002155
Coverage: Factual extract from primary Supplement Table S4.

Supplementary Table S4 from Sim et al. gives adjusted HR 1.01 (95% CI .99–1.04, p=.345) per 20 g/day for oranges and other citrus combined. The cohort included 1,429 older Australian women, with 568 fall-related hospitalizations over 14.5 years. Baseline FFQ excluded canned and frozen fruit and juice. The study analyzed first fall hospitalization, with no separate estimate for orange, fall count or Parkinson subgroup. It is the same study already indexed for vegetable intake and falls.
