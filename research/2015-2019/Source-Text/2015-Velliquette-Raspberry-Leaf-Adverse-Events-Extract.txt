Red raspberry leaf extract and trial adverse events
Source depth Original Methods, safety results and author adverse-event supplement. DOI 10.1186/s12986-015-0025-2.
Velliquette and colleagues tested multiple extract capsules against placebo for seven days in overweight or obese adults. The 90 participants were randomized across five groups. The main outcome was postprandial triglycerides. The red raspberry leaf arm listed mild appetite loss, lower-leg aching, nausea and heartburn in the supplement. It supplies no posture-specific pressure or dizziness measurement. The authors report no product-related change in vital signs and do not present those data. Raspberry leaf extract is a distinct exposure from frozen berries.
