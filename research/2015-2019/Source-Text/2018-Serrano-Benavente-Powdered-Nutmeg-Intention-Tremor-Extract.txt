Intention tremor after powdered nutmeg exposure
October 2018
https://www.samfyc.es/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/V19n1s2.pdf
Factual extract from complete primary society conference case, printed p. 400.

Serrano Benavente, Pérez Ruiz and Ruiz Sánchez describe an adult presenting after intentional excess powdered nutmeg. Examination recorded intention tremor of the hands alongside other acute findings. The report follows recovery during observation. It gives neither a Parkinson diagnosis nor a quantified rest-tremor observation. The case has no comparison group and the route and severity limits keep the culinary hypothesis open.
