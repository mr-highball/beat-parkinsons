Wissel and colleagues compared records from eight centers. They matched 53 patients with Parkinson disease and functional neurological disorder to 53 with Parkinson disease alone. The study used depression and anxiety inventories from routine clinical practice where available. The abstract and the Results paragraph present the numbers in different symptom orders.

Table 1 explicitly labels the Beck Depression Inventory as 20.9 (SD 9.7) versus 14.1 (SD 7.6), p=.025, with data for 15 people in each group. The Beck Anxiety Inventory is 34.0 (SD 7.9) versus 16.8 (SD 7.3), p=.033, with five people in each group. The manuscript's main Results agrees with Table 1. The abstract appears to transpose the endpoint names in front of the rounded comparisons. These are chart associations with substantial missingness and no assigned food or treatment. They do not establish an ingredient effect. The contrast does show why a matched cohort's full denominator should not be carried into individual mood outcomes.

Locators: UCL manuscript pp. 6, 9–10 and 20, especially Table 1. The exact numbers above use the explicit table labels.
