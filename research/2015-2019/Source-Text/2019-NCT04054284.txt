Title: Multicomponent tea safety in diabetes
First posted: 2019-08-13
Registration: NCT04054284
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04054284
Text type: official-registry evidence summary

The completed randomized trial lists 60 participants and 12 weeks of tea use. The active-comparator tea contains Salvia officinalis and six other botanicals. The experimental arm description names ten plants while the general description says nine. The sage plant part, freshness and relative amount are unspecified. The main measure is the weekly count of adverse events. The registry identifies no pain severity measure and had no posted results at the November 17, 2026 check. Any safety comparison would be between formulations.
