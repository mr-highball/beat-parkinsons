Methods and Table 1 describe a double-blind randomized parallel pilot with 15 participants per arm. They had self-perceived cognitive decline and baseline MMSE scores of 20–27. The eight-week intervention combined saffron, Bacopa, L-theanine, vitamins and minerals. The comparator was placebo. The measured exposure is a multi-ingredient product without a separate saffron arm.

Table 2 gives Self-Rating Depression Scale means of 42.8 to 37.1 in the active group and 43.6 to 40.9 in the placebo group. Each post-treatment value carries a within-group significance marker. Neither carries the symbol for the placebo comparison. The descriptive difference in change is −3.0 points. The report supplies no confidence interval or exact between-arm p value for that difference. The active group showed separately marked differences versus placebo on MMSE and perceived stress. MMSE is a global cognitive screen, and the report does not quantify executive function separately.

The discussion refers to a crossover, while the methods, arm sizes and results describe parallel groups without a crossing sequence. The study supplies no isolated ground saffron, diagnosed Parkinson population or specific sleep, rigidity or fatigue result. The comparison framed here follows the reported assignment and the table footnotes.
