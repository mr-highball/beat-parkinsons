One hundred adolescent girls were assigned Melissa capsules or starch placebo, 50 per arm, during menstrual days for three cycles. Capsules are described as 600 mg essence twice daily. GHQ was used for eligibility; the outcome is the PSST composite and domains, without a separate depression estimate.

Table 1 total active scores at baseline and months 1–3 were 42.56, 30.72, 30.02 and 13.90; placebo 39.28, 35.24, 34.44 and 28.38. Corresponding SDs and all timepoints match the later four-arm report's Table 5. The abstract instead prints 32.72 at month 1. Psychological-domain baseline and timing conflict with the later report. Printed domain p-values also require scrutiny.

The same registry IRCT2014060717998N1 and thesis 92-6805 support an overlapping report family. The later GHQ analysis shares these identities; exact participant-level mapping remains unavailable. These papers are not independently replicated tea or Parkinson depression trials.
