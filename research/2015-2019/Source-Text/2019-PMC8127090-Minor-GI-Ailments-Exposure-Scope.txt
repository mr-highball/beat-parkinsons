Ginger appears as a traditional digestive preparation, a constituent, a combined probiotic formulation and reference 82 on functional dyspepsia. The primary Hu comparison distinguishes faster gastric emptying from inconclusive measured dyspepsia symptoms. A separate dyspepsia reference concerns Ocimum basilicum. Curcumin, cinnamon laboratory reports and proposed gastric responses to black pepper or cumin are discussed at their distinct evidence levels. Cumin and Nigella sativa should be distinguished.

Vegetable oil, olive oil, carrot and orange entries are dietary lists or components of composite patterns. They do not supply single-food Parkinson symptom comparisons. The review draws together distinct digestive indications and gives no primary graded effect for the assigned culinary and Parkinson pairings.
