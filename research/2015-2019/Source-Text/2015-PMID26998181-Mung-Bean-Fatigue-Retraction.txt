Reviewed: 2026-11-25

The title names a green-bean sports drink. The introduction and experimental preparation describe mung bean, a different exposure from the cooked immature pods called green or string beans in the frozen claim. The paper describes weighted swimming endurance and a serum assay in mice, without a measured fatigue rating in people with Parkinson disease.

The journal editors retracted the study following a compromised peer review process. The study remains discoverable for identity correction and provenance. Its numbers supply no efficacy weight for claim 0561. The retraction applies to this paper, and says nothing about how cooked green beans affect fatigue.
