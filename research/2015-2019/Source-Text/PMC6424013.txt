Design: Clinical plasma proteomics comparison

The sample includes 57 patients with dementia with Lewy bodies, 32 people with Parkinson disease without dementia and 56 controls. The comparison addresses blood protein signatures and diagnostic group differences. It does not measure fig intake or repeated visual episodes.
