Prospective Longvida registration for sleep and attention
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=370499
Registration: ACTRN12616000484448
Coverage: Official ANZCTR registration and explicit publication linkage

Registered April 13, 2016. The protocol assigned Longvida capsules or matched placebo for 12 weeks in healthy older adults and explicitly excluded a Parkinson history. Divided attention was among the primary cognitive outcomes. PSQI at four and 12 weeks was secondary, with no separate Epworth or standalone sleepiness outcome specified. The registry fields show a target of 80 and final accrual of 101. The 2020 publication describes 80 randomized. These numbers may reflect different participant-flow boundaries. The registration identifies a distinct longer trial from the earlier Cox acute and four-week experiment.
