Title: Biochanin A protects dopaminergic neurons against lipopolysaccharide-induced damage and oxidative stress in a rat model of Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 26394281
DOI: 10.1016/j.pbb.2015.09.013
First published: 2015
Authors: Wang J, He C, Wu WY, Chen F, Wu YY, Li WZ et al.
Journal: Pharmacol Biochem Behav
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26394281/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rodent Parkinson model (Wang et al., 2015, J Neuroinflammation) evaluating isolated purified isoflavone biochanin A (a phytochemical occurring in red clover, soy, and chickpeas) in intranigral LPS-injected rats. Biochanin A attenuated behavioral motor deficits, microglial activation, proinflammatory cytokines, and substantia nigra dopaminergic neuron loss. Critical limits: pure isolated pharmaceutical biochanin A rather than cooked, canned, or roasted whole chickpeas; rodent toxin model.
