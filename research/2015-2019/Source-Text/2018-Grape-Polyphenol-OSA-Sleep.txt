Source: https://doi.org/10.1093/jn/nxy005
Factual primary report summary. Registered randomized comparison of grape juice polyphenols and placebo in severe obstructive sleep apnea. Polysomnographic total sleep time changed by −10 minutes versus +15 minutes, reported p=.02. Severe daytime sleepiness, Epworth score of at least 16, was excluded. The accessible results do not identify post-treatment Epworth, frozen grapes or Parkinson effects.
