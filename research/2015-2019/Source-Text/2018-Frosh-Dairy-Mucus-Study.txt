Frosh A et al., first online 2018-09-04, PMID 30178886 DOI 10.1002/lary.27287. Primary abstract https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30178886/ . Randomized double-blind dairy vs nondairy supplements after dairy-free period among 108 ENT patients with self-reported persistent nasopharyngeal mucus. Abstract reports within-arm perceived secretion score change: dairy days 4 to 7 t(53)=-2.56 p=.01; nondairy t(53)=1.54 p=.13. Neither result alone is a between-arm effect, objective mucus secretion or intelligibility measurement.
