Original study scope
The original separates a 36-rat dose screen with a seven-day rotenone schedule from a 24-rat four-arm follow-up at 45 days. In each reported rotenone regimen the dose is 2.5 mg/kg/day, and 0.5 mL is the stated sunflower-oil vehicle volume in the control methods. The earlier local summary incorrectly used 0.5 as the toxin dose. Figures 1–3 describe faster beam crossing, greater rotarod retention and longer hang time for the isolated oral demethoxycurcumin condition compared with rotenone alone. Six animals are reported per group. Beam traversal includes balance and initiation. Rotarod and hanging reflect different motor functions. Neither phase reports passive rigidity. The rotenone and comparator conditions do not provide an independent culinary sunflower-oil comparison.

Continuing evidence gaps
Verify phase-specific arm linkage and preserve the different outcomes in claim 0507. Oral ground turmeric, clinical bradykinesia, passive clinical rigidity and an isolated refined sunflower-oil contrast remain unresolved.
