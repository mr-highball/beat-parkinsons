Title: Neuroprotective potential of Beta vulgaris L. in Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 26288473
PMCID: PMC4527062
DOI: 10.4103/0253-7613.161263
First published: 2015
Authors: Nade VS, Kawale LA, Zambre SS, Kapure AB
Journal: Indian journal of pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26288473/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled preclinical rat pharmacology study (PMC4527062; Wistar rats, n=6 per group across 6 groups per assay = 36 rats per model) testing oral methanolic extract of Beta vulgaris (MEBV 100, 200, and 300 mg/kg) and L-dopa/carbidopa (30 mg/kg) in reserpine (5 mg/kg i.p. x 5 d), haloperidol (1 mg/kg i.p.), and tacrine (2.5 mg/kg i.p.) models: MEBV significantly reduced reserpine-induced muscular rigidity, tremors, and akinesia (p<0.05 to p<0.01), restored locomotor activity and grip strength (p<0.01), decreased haloperidol-induced catalepsy (200 & 300 mg/kg; p<0.01), reduced tacrine vacuous chewing movements (200 & 300 mg/kg; p<0.01), and increased brain SOD/CAT while lowering LPO (p<0.01). Limits: acute catecholamine-depletion/receptor-blockade rodent models using concentrated methanolic extract rather than culinary beetroot in human PD.
