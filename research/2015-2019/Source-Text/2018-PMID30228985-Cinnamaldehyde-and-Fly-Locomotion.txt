Original source scope
The experiment fed purchased cinnamaldehyde to Drosophila and measured lifespan, memory and rapid negative geotaxis. Male Tau-model flies showed improved vertical climbing, while the wild-type climbing comparison gave no clear benefit.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The experiment used an isolated constituent, an Alzheimer-related insect model and a climbing task. It does not measure walking speed after ground cinnamon in Parkinson disease.
