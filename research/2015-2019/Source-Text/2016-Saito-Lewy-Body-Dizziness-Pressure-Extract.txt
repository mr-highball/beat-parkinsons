Title: Postprandial and Orthostatic Hypotension Treated by Sitagliptin in a Patient with Dementia with Lewy Bodies
Authors: Yoshihiro Saito, Joji Ishikawa, Kazumasa Harada
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5127632/
PMID: 27885251
DOI: 10.12659/AJCR.900620
Date: November 25, 2016
Coverage: factual extract of original report and case figures

A 78-year-old woman with previous ischemic stroke presented with dizziness after meals. The clinicians diagnosed dementia with Lewy bodies and explicitly describe cognitive impairment without parkinsonism. Ambulatory monitoring showed a decrease in systolic pressure from 143 mmHg to 77 mmHg by 30 minutes after meals. The systolic decrease during a separate head-up tilt was 160 to 120 mmHg. A separate 75 g glucose challenge assessed glucose handling and pressure. This single case involves clinical pharmacological management, and the original does not quantify an individual culinary exposure or a change in standing-induced lightheadedness severity.
