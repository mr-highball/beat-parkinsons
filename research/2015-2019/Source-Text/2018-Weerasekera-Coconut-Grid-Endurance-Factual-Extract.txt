Weerasekera and colleagues' 2018 article gave extra-virgin coconut oil mixed with rodent chow or compared with standard chow in an ALS mouse model. Paw-grip endurance was the time to fall from an inverted grid. The supplemented group showed longer endurance in weeks 14–16 with six animals per diet. The significance notation differs slightly between Figure 8 and the Results, and survival percentages are inconsistent across the paper. The intervention and apparatus endpoint do not estimate spontaneous falls after refined coconut oil in Parkinson disease.
