Nisshin OilliO. Manufacturer news release, January 11, 2018, page 1.

The release names Nisshin Macadamia Nut Oil in the 145 g Fresh Keep Bottle and gives the March 1, 2018 nationwide launch date. It describes oil pressed from macadamia nuts without an extraction temperature or a lot certificate. The name and container size match the Nisshin item named in patent JP2026034397A Test Example 1. The release confirms product identity and gives an extraction description without verifying cold pressing or the trial lot.
