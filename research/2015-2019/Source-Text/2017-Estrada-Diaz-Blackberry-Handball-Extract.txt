Factual extract of Spanish primary Methods and Results, checked 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

Fourteen male handball athletes were allocated seven per arm. An oral dose was 200 g blackberry (Rubus sp.) in 200 mL water each day for fifteen days. The control contained water and red coloring. The paper measured plasma d-ROMs and total antioxidant capacity at four visits before and after competition. Baseline versus precompetition d-ROMs declined within the blackberry arm (p=.018). The report does not state a frozen fruit preparation or study a pain outcome. This could be an overlapping cohort with García-Dávila given matching sport, sample size, arm sizes, closely related protocols and shared investigators. The two reports state different total exposure periods.
