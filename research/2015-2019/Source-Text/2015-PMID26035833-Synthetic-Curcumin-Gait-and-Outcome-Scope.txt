Original study scope
The transgenic mouse feeding study uses synthetic curcumin with soybean oil held equal and measures gait patterns on a treadmill. Figure 3 describes variability and swing duration, while date references are temporal. Neither whole turmeric nor fall frequency or pain was tested.

Continuing evidence gaps
The published gait result belongs to the original mouse family. Isolated culinary turmeric, oil effects and relevant symptom measures remain open.
