Title: Oculo-Visual Dysfunction in Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4927837/
PMID: 26599301
PMCID: PMC4927837
DOI: 10.3233/jpd-150686
Publication year: 2015
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The clinical review examines Parkinson visual function from acuity and eye movements to perceptions and hallucinations. It discusses overlapping diagnoses and medication context. None of these narrative observations compares dried fig intake with a Parkinson hallucination endpoint.

The authors discuss clinical evaluation of visual dysfunction and note different relationships to disease course and medication. Visual acuity, color and contrast measures are distinct from the experience or frequency of a formed hallucination.
