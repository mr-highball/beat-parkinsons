Title: Polysaccharides from Pleurotus ostreatus alleviate cognitive impairment in a rat model of Alzheimer's disease.
PMID: 27498414
DOI: 10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2016.08.008
First published: 2016
Authors: Zhang Y, Yang X, Jin G, Yang X, Zhang Y
Journal: International Journal of Biological Macromolecules
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27498414/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Preclinical D-galactose + AlCl3 AD rat model fed isolated Pleurotus ostreatus polysaccharides (POP): improved Morris water maze spatial escape latency and step-down passive avoidance, lowered AChE, A-beta, and tau phosphorylation. Spatial/avoidance memory paradigm in AD rats with extracted polysaccharides; no PD motor, attention, or executive testing.
