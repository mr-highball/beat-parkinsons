Format: Factual digest of the original material

The article reviews clinical motor and neuropsychiatric features reported with GBA variants, including cognitive, psychiatric and hallucination-related phenotypes in some cohorts. It is a synthesis of group definitions and clinical risks. It reports no independently tested food exposure or visual episode rate after dried figs.
