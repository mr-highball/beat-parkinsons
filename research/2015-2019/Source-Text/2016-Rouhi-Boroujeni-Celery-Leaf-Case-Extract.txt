Dried celery leaf powder in a thyroid case report
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5403020/
PMID: 28458701
PMCID: PMC5403020
Coverage: Original NLM article abstract and case narrative reviewed; 2016 print publication

A case report describes a 48-year-old man who used 4 g daily of dried celery leaf powder for 45 days for weight loss while taking losartan. The authors report weight loss, sweating, exophthalmos and abnormal thyroid tests during the case. After he stopped the powder and started methimazole, laboratory results normalized. The simultaneous treatment limits inference from the dechallenge. The report gives no hallucination or Parkinson symptom measurement.
