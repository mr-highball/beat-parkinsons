2019 | Carlson et al. | Digigait measurements with soybean-oil-sourced laboratory chow

Source: original full text, DOI 10.3389/fnins.2019.00072, PMID 30858795, PMCID PMC6397844, published February 25, 2019. Native Europe PMC JATS confirmed the Table 1 footnote and Figure 4 text.

The standard NIH-31 chow formula lists 44.7 g/kg fat from soybean oil. The experimental diet provides hydrogenated coconut oil, DHA, and arachidonic acid. The mice were bred under the respective diets. Diet composition and duration are not otherwise equivalent. The tested mice were adult males, and the authors report 15 per diet in treadmill gait testing.

Propulsion times were 0.101 and 0.104 seconds, p=.65, for soybean chow and experimental feed. Swing times were 0.122 and 0.123 seconds, p=.87, and braking times were 0.073 and 0.070 seconds, p=.79. These are generalized limb timings. The paper reports no separate stride length, dystonia outcome, or refining grade. Some other endpoints, including rotarod performance, differed, so the gait timing findings are specific to the named measures.
