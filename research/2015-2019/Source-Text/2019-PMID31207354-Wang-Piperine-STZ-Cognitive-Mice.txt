Title: Piperine attenuates cognitive impairment in an experimental mouse model of sporadic Alzheimer's disease.
PMID: 31207354
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2019.05.009
First published: 2019
Authors: Wang C, Cai Z, Wang W, Wei M, Kou D, Li T
Journal: The Journal of nutritional biochemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31207354/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse experiment administering i.p. piperine (2.5-10 mg/kg x 15 d) in ICV-streptozotocin sporadic Alzheimer model mice. Improved Morris water maze spatial memory and open-field outcomes. Tests rodent spatial memory with i.p. piperine, not human executive function.
