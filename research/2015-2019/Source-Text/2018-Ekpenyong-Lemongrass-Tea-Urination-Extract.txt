Title: Lemongrass tea consumption and changes in Acid-Base Balance and Electrolyte homeostasis
Publication: Ekpenyong and colleagues, December 31, 2018, Archives of Food and Nutritional Science 2:41–51
Source: https://www.foodscijournal.com/afns/article/download/afns-aid1016/pdf/4187
Coverage: factual extract of the accessible primary source

The 105 healthy adults drank doses of 2, 4 or 8 g of powdered Cymbopogon citratus leaves infused in 150 mL hot water, with day 0, 10 and 30 assessments. Table 2 lists mean 24-hour void counts for the 2 g group of 6.42, 7.08 and 6.56; the 4 g group of 5.06, 7.04 and 6.74; and the 8 g group of 6.02, 7.02 and 8.00. The baseline label 'Control' in the table denotes pretreatment measurements of each dose group. Per the table and text, all doses had higher frequency on day 10 versus baseline and the 4 and 8 g groups remained higher on day 30. This fits the previous report's identical design and frequency pattern; treat the cohort overlap as likely. The authors measured void frequency rather than urgent desire to urinate. No matched Parkinson population was included.
