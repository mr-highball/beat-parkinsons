Title: Serotonergic dysregulation is linked to sleep problems in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5964830/
PMID: 29845011
PMCID: PMC5964830
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2018.03.001
Publication date: 2018-03-02
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC metadata and PubMed.
Design: Clinical serotonin PET comparison and sleep symptoms

Fourteen Parkinson participants with sleep dysfunction, fourteen without it and twelve healthy controls underwent a serotonin-transporter imaging comparison. Binding and sleep status are the endpoints. The study does not measure visual hallucination frequency after food.
