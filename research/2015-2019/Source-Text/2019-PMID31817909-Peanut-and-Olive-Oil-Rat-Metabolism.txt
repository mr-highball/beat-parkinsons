Title: High Oleic Acid Peanut Oil and Extra Virgin Olive Oil Supplementation Attenuate Metabolic Syndrome in Rats by Modulating the Gut Microbiota
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31817909/
PMID: 31817909
DOI: 10.3390/nu11123005
Collection: Source text
Publication date: 2019-12-07
Date basis: Nutrients online publication date.
Review depth: primary_source, original abstract and results context
Checked: 2026-11-25

The oil comparison tested high-oleic peanut oil and extra-virgin
olive oil in a high-fat-diet rat model, alongside other comparison
groups. It measured features of metabolic syndrome and gut microbial
composition. The result supplies oil type and formulation specificity,
with no Parkinson participants or measured constipation, fatigue or
depression endpoint.

The 2022 dietary microbiota review (PMID 35928846, reference 135)
cites this original experiment. Its food comparison should be kept
separate from general polyphenol findings and from human
ingredient-specific decisions.
