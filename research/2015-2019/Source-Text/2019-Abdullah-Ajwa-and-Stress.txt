Abdullah N, Mokhtar RH, Marwan AA, Noh NA. Improvement of stress-induced changes related to mood and cognitive function in healthy young adults following supplementation with Ajwa dates. Ulum Islamiyyah 26:1–8, journal release 2019-04-19.
Journal DOI: 10.33102/uij.vol26no.111
Printed PDF DOI: 10.33102/ulum.2019.26.01
Primary sources: https://uijournal.usim.edu.my/index.php/uij/article/view/111 and https://uijournal.usim.edu.my/index.php/uij/article/download/111/80/389
Coverage: complete original interpretation and rendered Figures 1–2 on PDF page 7.

Twenty healthy young adults attended baseline and follow-up sessions with a laboratory stressor after six weeks of daily whole Ajwa consumption. The study had no control group. POMS tension and DASS-21 stress declined with significance marked at p<.05. The POMS vigor bar declined slightly, roughly 20 to 19 by visual reading, without a significance marker or exact printed estimate. No apathy scale was used. Fruit maturity and drying were unspecified. Acceptance in 2018 and an unverified 2017 conference title do not establish an earlier release of this full report.
