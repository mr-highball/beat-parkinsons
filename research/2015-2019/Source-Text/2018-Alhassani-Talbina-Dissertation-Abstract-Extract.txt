Title: The Effects Of A High Fiber Beta Glucan Talbina Meal On Emotional Well-Being, Health-Related Quality Of Life, And Nutritional Status Among Arab Refugees In Metropolitan Detroit
Source: https://digitalcommons.wayne.edu/oa_dissertations/2087/
Date of award: January 2018
Coverage: Factual extract from publicly accessible institutional abstract. Full dissertation is restricted to Wayne State access.

Walaa Alhassani studied 23 Syrian refugees, 14 women and nine men, using a single-blind crossover. The cereal preparation used 30 g organic hulled whole-grain barley flour cooked in 240 mL of water and provided 1 g beta-glucan. The abstract describes anxiety improvement during study participation regardless of the meal ingredients. It supplies no anxiety estimates by arm. A conference abstract from the same study reports no clear difference between the barley flour and rice flour phases. The preparation was a cooked flour meal; intact boiled barley grains and Parkinson disease were outside the design.
