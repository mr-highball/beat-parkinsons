Summer savory sprout volatiles during germination
Primary source: https://www.ptfarm.pl/download/?file=File/Acta_Poloniae/2017/2/706.pdf
PMID: 29624277
Coverage: factual extract from primary methods and Table 1.

Jarosz and Szumny sampled germinating Satureja hortensis seeds across ten days and identified headspace volatiles using solid-phase microextraction and gas chromatography–mass spectrometry. Relative p-cymene, gamma-terpinene, thymoquinone and other compounds changed across sampling days. The endpoint describes sprout chemistry. The experiment gives no human sensory performance or dried culinary dose effect.
