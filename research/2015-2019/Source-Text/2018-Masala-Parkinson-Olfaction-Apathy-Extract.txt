Masala et al., 2018. Correlation among olfactory function, motors’ symptoms, cognitive impairment, apathy, and fatigue in patients with Parkinson’s disease.
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00415-018-8913-9
Access and coverage: Publisher author abstract. Complete odour list and item-level results not established from accessible article portions.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

The study evaluated 96 people with Parkinson disease and 51 controls. Apathy and motor scores were associated with composite olfactory identification, discrimination and TDI in regression. Exposure was odor testing. The accessible abstract neither identifies a separate clove test item association nor reports dietary clove intake.
