Title: Short-term effect of add on bell pepper (Capsicum annuum var. grossum) juice with integrated approach of yoga therapy on blood glucose levels and cardiovascular functions in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus: A randomized controlled study.
PMID: 28917374
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2017.07.011
First published: 2017
Authors: Nagasukeerthi P, Mooventhan A, Manjunath NK
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28917374/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Short-term 4-day randomized controlled trial in N=50 adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus (ages 34-69 y) comparing 100 mL twice-daily sweet bell pepper (Capsicum annuum var. grossum) juice + integrated yoga therapy vs yoga therapy alone. Add-on bell pepper juice significantly lowered resting systolic blood pressure, pulse pressure, rate pressure product, and postprandial blood glucose vs yoga alone. Limits: 4-day resting supine/seated BP in T2DM; did NOT assess standing orthostatic hypotension or lightheadedness, and tested raw juice rather than grilled bell pepper.
