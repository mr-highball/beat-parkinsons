Title: Ficus carica stem bark extract and an oligosaccharide-rich fraction in a convulsion model
PMID: 31453121
PMCID: PMC6701940
DOI: 10.1016/j.jtcme.2018.01.007
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6701940/
First publication: 2018-09-28 online, 2019 October issue.
Coverage: Original full JATS body and assay descriptions.

Raafat and Wurglics identify stem bark as the plant material and report an oligosaccharide-rich active fraction. Both the crude methanol extract and the fraction reduced strychnine-evoked convulsion outcomes in mice. In a separate assay with central administration, behavior under a glycine and ethanol challenge contributed to the authors' glycine-receptor hypothesis. These are convulsion, righting-reflex and laboratory receptor-related outcomes. There is no dried culinary fruit, clinical Parkinson diagnosis or visual hallucination measurement.
