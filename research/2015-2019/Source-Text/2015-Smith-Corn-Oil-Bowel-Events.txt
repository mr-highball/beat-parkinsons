Corn oil capsules and constipation adverse events
https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=88e907176e60d0a64226fd70d87ae8bfa789aa5e&repid=rep1&type=pdf
selected methods and findings paraphrase

First published May 20, 2015. Six-month randomized older-adult trial of fish oil versus four corn-oil capsules daily. Investigator-classified possibly or definitely related constipation occurred in 1 of 18 corn-oil starters and 2 of 35 fish-oil starters. Diarrhea occurred in 2 and 4, respectively. These are the safety denominators, distinct from randomized and efficacy populations. The study does not specify corn-oil refining, test an oil-free comparator or measure Parkinson constipation severity.
