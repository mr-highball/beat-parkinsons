The 2019 article expressly identifies NCT02476708. Twelve schizophrenia outpatients were randomized. Each analyzed group in Table 1 contains five. Eight-week change in attention/vigilance was +9.20 ± 18.18 for the active arm and −6.80 ± 12.79 for placebo (Mann–Whitney Z=1.257, p=.209). Working memory had a different estimate (+10.60 ± 15.17 versus −4.00 ± 5.52, p=.028). The authors describe 180 mg/day of active curcumin and capsules made from 300 mg of a 30% formulation. The official registry describes 600 mg capsules three times daily. The publication leaves that difference unresolved. This study measured attention outside Parkinson disease and used Theracurmin capsules rather than culinary grated rhizome.

Kucukgoncu, Guloksuz and Tek, J Clin Psychopharmacol 39(2), 182–184 (2019). Primary article registration and formulation are on p. 182. Attention, working memory and composite results are in Table 1 on p. 183. The composite changes were +6.00 ± 6.28 and −3.80 ± 8.84, p=.138.
