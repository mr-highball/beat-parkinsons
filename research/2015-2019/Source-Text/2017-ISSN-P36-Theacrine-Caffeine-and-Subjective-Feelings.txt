Twenty-four men and 26 women completed five crossover conditions involving two isolated theacrine levels, caffeine alone, theacrine plus caffeine, and placebo. Grogginess and lethargy declined over time in all conditions. The authors described favorable energy and focus ratings for selected comparisons and cortisol was not the fatigue endpoint.
