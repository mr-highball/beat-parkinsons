Title: Effect of visual cues on the resolution of perceptual ambiguity in Parkinson's disease and normal aging
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5433847/
PMCID: PMC5433847
PMID: 25765890
DOI: 10.1017/s1355617715000065
Publication date: 2015-02
Date basis: NLM issue month. Electronic publication was 2015-03-13.
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary perceptual experiment
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The 27 Parkinson participants, 23 older controls and 20 younger adults judged two interpretations of an ambiguous Necker cube while visual cues and instructions varied. Outcome measures were percept dominance and alternation under passive or intentional viewing. The experiments test how participants respond to an external ambiguous image. Background discussion of hallucinations does not turn the task into a count of spontaneous visual episodes. No food was assigned.
