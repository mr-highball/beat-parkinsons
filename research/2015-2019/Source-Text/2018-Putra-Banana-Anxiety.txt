Whole banana and adolescent anxiety
https://media.neliti.com/media/publications/256608-effect-of-banana-consumption-and-walking-a48d4f39.pdf
original conference abstract paraphrase

Presented April 18–19, 2018. The original abstract describes randomization of 62 girls aged 15–17 to banana, walking, both or control. The banana-only arm received two fruits daily for 14 days. The reported Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale regression coefficient was −7.84 (p < .001). Allocation method, blinding, fruit preparation, arm-level data and full model are unavailable in the one-page abstract. The participants did not have Parkinson disease.
