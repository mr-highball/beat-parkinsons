Celery extract powder in a separate thyroid case report
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6528421/
PMID: 31143429
PMCID: PMC6528421
DOI: 10.4103/ijpvm.IJPVM_209_17
Coverage: Original NLM article case narrative reviewed; electronic publication May 6, 2019

A 36-year-old woman presented with blurred vision, palpitation and other thyroid symptoms after taking 8 g per day of celery extract powder for 78 days. The plant part is unspecified. She stopped the extract and started methimazole together. The authors cite an earlier, separate dried-leaf case. This report concerns blurred vision with abnormal thyroid tests and supplies no hallucination-frequency measurement or Parkinson exposure comparison.
