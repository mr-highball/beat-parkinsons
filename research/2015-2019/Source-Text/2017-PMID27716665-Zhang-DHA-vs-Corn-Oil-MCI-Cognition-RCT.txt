Title: Effects of DHA Supplementation on Hippocampal Volume and Cognitive Function in Older Adults with Mild Cognitive Impairment: A 12-Month Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial.
PMID: 27716665
DOI: 10.3233/JAD-160439
First published: 2017
Authors: Zhang YP, Miao R, Li Q, Wu T, Ma F
Journal: J Alzheimers Dis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27716665/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized double-blind trial (Zhang et al., 2017, J Alzheimers Dis, n=240 older adults with mild cognitive impairment) comparing DHA 2 g/day versus corn oil placebo capsules for 12 months. Full-scale IQ and several cognitive subtests improved on DHA relative to corn oil placebo; reflects active DHA supplementation against an inert oil comparator rather than refined corn oil worsening cognition.
