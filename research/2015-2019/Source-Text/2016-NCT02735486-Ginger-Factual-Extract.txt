Title: Registered ginger drink and postprandial pressure
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02735486


NCT02735486 first posted April 12, 2016. It records a completed randomized crossover in 22 healthy men with ginger drink and water, with blood pressure and flow-mediated dilation around a meal. No results are posted in the official registry. The Europe PMC thesis record ETH:701648, Ukeyima 2016, has a closely matching design and sample size; its indexed abstract ends partway through the blood-pressure result. The full thesis, confirmation of the link and the statistical and posture details remain open.
