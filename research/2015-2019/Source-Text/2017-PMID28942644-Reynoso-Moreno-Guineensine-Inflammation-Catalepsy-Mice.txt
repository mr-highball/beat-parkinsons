Title: An Endocannabinoid Uptake Inhibitor from Black Pepper Exerts Pronounced Anti-Inflammatory Effects in Mice.
PMID: 28942644
DOI: 10.1021/acs.jafc.7b02979
First published: 2017
Authors: Reynoso-Moreno I, Najar-Guerrero I, Escareño N, Flores-Soto ME, Gertsch J, Viveros-Paredes JM
Journal: Journal of agricultural and food chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28942644/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Mouse pharmacology study showing intraperitoneal guineensine (2.5-5 mg/kg i.p.) exerted strong anti-inflammatory analgesia (95.6% at 2.5 mg/kg) and independently induced acute catalepsy and CB1-independent hypolocomotion in healthy mice, though catalepsy was abolished when combined with LPS endotoxemia. Highlights opposing pharmacological effects of trace i.p. guineensine vs major oral alkaloid piperine.
