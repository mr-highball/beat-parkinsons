Text coverage: Distilled review body, included studies and ongoing-study table.

Appleton and colleagues' review lists sunflower oil comparators for the Lespérance and Lucas depression trials. The 432-person Lespérance trial used sunflower oil with 2% fish oil and recorded IDS-SR and MADRS. The Lucas whole trial enrolled 120 midlife women with psychological distress. The review abstracts a 29-person depressed subset and gives 1.5 g/day sunflower oil with 0.2% fish oil in its study table. Its general comparator paragraph groups both under 2%, so the original report governs each formulation. ACTRN12610000710022 is listed as ongoing and planned an uncharacterized sunflower arm with depression scales and BDNF. These entries are review or registry leads to separate original trials. The review gives no Parkinson executive-function outcome for these comparators.

Supplementary member review
The review distinguishes Bot's rapeseed and medium-chain triglyceride comparator from the Jazayeri rapeseed and fluoxetine comparisons. Carney 2009 used corn oil with sertraline, and the review includes additional planned or blended comparators. The primary Carney studies reported overlapping uncertainty and no clear difference on depression measures relative to omega-3 plus sertraline. Olive oil, olive oil ethyl esters and a multi-food diet containing extra-virgin olive oil occur in separate comparisons. The cod-liver oil occurrences belong to search terms in the appendices.
