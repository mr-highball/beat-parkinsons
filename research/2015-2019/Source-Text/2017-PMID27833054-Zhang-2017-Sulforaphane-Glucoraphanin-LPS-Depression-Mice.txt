Title: Prophylactic effects of sulforaphane on depression-like behavior and dendritic changes in mice after inflammation.
PMID: 27833054
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2016.10.004
First published: 2017
Authors: Zhang JC, Yao W, Dong C, Yang C, Ren Q, Ma M
Journal: The Journal of nutritional biochemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27833054/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Mouse inflammatory depression study showing sulforaphane pretreatment and dietary 0.1% glucoraphanin food attenuated LPS-induced immobility in forced swim and tail suspension tests, suppressed microglial activation and TNF-alpha, raised IL-10, and preserved brain BDNF, PSD-95, GluA1, and dendritic spine density. Limits: rodent LPS depression model rather than PD.
