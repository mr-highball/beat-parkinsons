Title: Anti-inflammatory properties and phenolic profile of six Moroccan date fruit (Phoenix dactylifera L.) varieties
Source: https://jksus.org/anti-inflammatory-properties-and-phenolic-profile-of-six-moroccan-date-fruit-phoenix-dactylifera-l-varieties/
DOI: 10.1016/j.jksus.2017.08.011
Publication date: 2017-10-01
Date basis: Journal article history. Volume 30, issue 4, pages 519–526.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Review depth: full_text

The original compares aqueous extracts from six date fruit varieties in assays and two acute animal inflammation models. The oral rat comparison records paw swelling by cultivar and time. Section 3.6 reports the largest suppression for Jihl at hour 4 and a smaller effect for Bouskri. The abstract and main results summarize different edema values, so each percentage requires its own timepoint and figure. Ear swelling and paw swelling are separate outcomes.

The introduction cites customary use in the Moroccan pharmacopoeia. It gives no patient pain trajectory and does not establish preparation, dose or era for a specific analgesic practice. The experiment has no pain rating, Parkinson disease or whole dried fruit intervention.

Reporting note. The rat Methods state a different extract amount from Fig. 4's caption. The dosage basis remains unresolved. The animal outcomes remain edema measurements.
