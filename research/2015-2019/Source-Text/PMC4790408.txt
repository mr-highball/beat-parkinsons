Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official complete PMC JATS body.

This review cites Crocus sativus and constituents in mood and sleep contexts and discusses a traditional multicomponent formula containing ginger rhizome. The cited studies vary by formulation and endpoint. No original direct ground or dried saffron or grated ginger Parkinson comparison is reported.
