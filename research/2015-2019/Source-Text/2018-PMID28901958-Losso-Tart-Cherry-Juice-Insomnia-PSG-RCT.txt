Title: Pilot Study of the Tart Cherry Juice for the Treatment of Insomnia and Investigation of Mechanisms.
PMID: 28901958
PMCID: PMC5617749
DOI: 10.1097/MJT.0000000000000584
First published: 2018
Authors: Losso JN, Finley JW, Karki N, Liu AG, Prudente A, Tipton R, Yu Y, Greenway FL
Journal: American journal of therapeutics
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5617749/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled balanced crossover trial (Losso et al., 2018, Am J Ther, PMC5617749) in adults >50 years with insomnia randomized to Montmorency tart cherry juice (240 mL twice daily) vs placebo for 2 weeks each with a 2-week washout. Eleven subjects were randomized; 3 with sleep apnea were excluded, leaving 8 completers analyzed. Compared with placebo, tart cherry juice increased polysomnography total sleep time by 84 minutes (p=0.0182) and improved PSQI habitual sleep efficiency (p=0.03), while reducing serum kynurenine-to-tryptophan ratio and prostaglandin E2 (both p<0.05) and inhibiting IDO in Caco-2 cells via procyanidin B-2. Secondary questionnaires—Insomnia Severity Index (ISI), Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS), Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II), and State-Trait Anxiety Inventory (STAI)—showed no statistically significant between-group differences; no adverse events occurred. Limits: very small completer sample (n=8), liquid tart cherry juice rather than whole dried/raw cherries, non-PD cohort.
