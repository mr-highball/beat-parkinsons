Title: Falls and Their Associated Risks in Parkinson's Disease Patients in Nigeria
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5035939/
PMCID: PMC5035939
PMID: 27667188
DOI: 10.14802/jmd.16011
Published online: 2016-09-21
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled complete NLM article text, Methods, Results and Table 3.

Farombi and colleagues analyzed 81 people with Parkinson disease in a hospital-based observational study in Ibadan, Nigeria. Interviewers collected separate fall histories over one, six and twelve months and asked about circumstances and injuries. Investigators additionally used the Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale, Tinetti balance and gait scores, and Timed Up and Go.

The paper reports fall prevalence of 42% in the Parkinson group and 14.8% in age-matched healthy adults. The abstract reports recurrence for 67.7% of fallers. Table 3 models fall occurrence. Adjusted odds ratios were 3.86 for fear of falling (95% CI 1.17 to 12.7), 1.09 per UPDRS point (1.02 to 1.16) and 1.01 for Tinetti score (0.91 to 1.12). The observational design and recalled falls limit causal interpretation. Tinetti and Timed Up and Go capture aspects of clinical mobility. The paper uses recalled fall occurrence separately.

The study provides context for the actual human endpoint. No assigned diet or raw strawberry exposure appears in the methods.
