Four groups of seven mice include two extract groups and separate normal and model control groups. The 2016 study reports better vertical-rod performance with oral Cinnamomum cassia bark extract than in haloperidol controls, each p<.05 for the two groups of seven. The extraction method, quantitative graph estimates and precise assignment timing remain unresolved. The model task addresses sensorimotor behavior. Ground culinary cinnamon, human Parkinson gait speed and walking slowness were outside the experiment. The text reports a dose-group difference on the vertical rod. Wire-hanging significance wording varies between sections. The experiment is distinct from the 2022 report attributed to Churihar.
