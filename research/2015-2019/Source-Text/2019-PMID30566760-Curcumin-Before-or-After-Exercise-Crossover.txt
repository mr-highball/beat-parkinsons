The abstract describes two timing experiments, with ten healthy men each and a double-blind crossover design. It summarizes lower soreness on days three through six with curcumin given after exercise and no clear soreness effect for the before-exercise condition. The publisher full-text route returned the abstract page and the supplementary table link returned HTTP 403. The accessible summary lacks all day-by-day values, detailed attrition and definitive linkage information for a separate 2019 paper from the same research group.

Preparation, exercise context and outcome differ from the frozen ground turmeric comparison. Keep these results separate from clinical Parkinson pain.
