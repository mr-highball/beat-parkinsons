Sixty healthy adults entered the eight-week placebo-controlled parallel trial of two supplement levels. The Profile of Mood States fatigue group-by-time term was p=.41 and the group-by-time-by-gender term was p=.79. Vigor had a transient group-by-time signal and is distinct from fatigue. The observed fatigue result is inconclusive for a treatment-specific change. This is an independent follow-up context for the acute pilot family.
