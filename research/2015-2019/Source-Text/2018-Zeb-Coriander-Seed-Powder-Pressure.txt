2018 — Two coriander seed powder blood-pressure reports
Source: https://applications.emro.who.int/imemrf/Pak_J_Pharm_Sci/Pak_J_Pharm_Sci_2018_31_5_1935_1941.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of Methods and Table 3.

Eighty participants with hyperlipidemia and cardiovascular disease were assigned to seed powder, garlic powder, both or unsupplemented control. Coriander arm systolic means were 140.00, 117.14 and 121.42 mm Hg at baseline, 40 and 60 days. Control means were 137.14, 135.71 and 132.85. The authors report a treatment-by-time effect for systolic pressure and no significant diastolic effect. No active-standing protocol was reported.
