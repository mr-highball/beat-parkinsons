Title: Five Herbs Plus Thiamine Reduce Pain and Improve Functional Mobility in Patients With Pain: A Pilot Study.
PMID: 28160759
First published: 2017
Authors: Hedaya R
Journal: Altern Ther Health Med
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28160759/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Uncontrolled 14-day pilot case series (n=13 patients with chronic musculoskeletal pain, 27 sites) of a 5-herb plus thiamine blend (Urtica dioica, Boswellia serrata, Equisetum arvense, Allium sativum, Apium graveolens + vitamin B1) lowering VAS pain from 58.04 to 23.33 (p<0.05). Uncontrolled polyherbal supplement; cannot attribute to celery stalk.
