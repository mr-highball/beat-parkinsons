Title: Grape powder and anxiety-like behavior in aged rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26022140/
PMID: 26022140
DOI: 10.1016/j.nutres.2015.05.005
Publication date: 2015-05-15
Date basis: NLM electronic date May 15, 2015. Nutrition Research 35(6), pp. 504–511, appeared in June.
Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: original study abstract

Patki and colleagues compared grape powder in water with plain water in young and aged rats. The authors report lower anxiety-like behavior in supplemented aged rats and inconclusive short- and long-term memory results. Some physiological stress indicators changed, with a more limited effect among measured brain regions.

The age comparison constitutes a different animal design from the prior oxidative-stress, ovariectomy and prolonged-stress reports. Investigators overlap across the research line. The public abstract carries no retraction marker and provides no raw whole grape or Parkinson anxiety estimate. Obtain the full tables before estimating the behavioral effect size.
