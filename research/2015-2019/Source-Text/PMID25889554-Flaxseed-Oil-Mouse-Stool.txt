Solvent-extracted flaxseed oil and animal stool outcomes
Source: https://www.academia.edu/17632001/Dual_effectiveness_of_Flaxseed_in_constipation_and_diarrhea
PMID: 25889554
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2015.03.064
Coverage: Original article available online April 15, 2015, Methods 2.1 and fecal outcomes

Palla and Gilani tested flaxseed oil and mucilage in separate animal comparisons. The Methods describe petroleum-ether extraction of ground seeds. In groups of six mice, 24-hour wet-feces proportion was 5% (SE 4) with vehicle and 38.33% (5.5) and 54.66% (6.43) at the two lower tested oil amounts. The higher tested amount yielded 6.5% (5.12), making the response nonmonotonic. An independent castor-oil challenge in the same paper used different amounts and measured diarrhea. The study used solvent-extracted oil and animal stool proxies outside Parkinson disease. [Original author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25889554/)
