Title: Therapeutic implications of ajwa dates in the inhibition of liver tissue alterations through modulation of VEGF and PTEN
DOI: 10.4103/pr.pr_119_17
Source: https://doi.org/10.4103/pr.pr_119_17
Published: 2018, Pharmacognosy Research 10(2), pp. 151–155.
Archive scope: Publisher-formatted article as indexed and publicly reproduced, including animal grouping, extract source and outcome methods. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

The authors studied a water-derived, freeze-dried extract of Ajwa date pulp in a carbon-tetrachloride mouse liver model. They describe three groups of eight over 12 weeks and report changes in tissue structure, antioxidant measures, VEGF and PTEN. The Methods print different model-agent administration frequencies for the two exposed groups. This limits attribution of any observed difference to the date extract.

The preparation uses dried pulp as an input but the intervention is an extracted fraction. The measurements address hepatic injury, without a measured symptom comparison for Parkinson disease or whole dates.
