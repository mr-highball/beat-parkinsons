Title: Chinese patients with Lewy body dementia had shorter survival and developed complications earlier than those with Alzheimer's disease.
PMID: 31489427
PMCID: PMC7930314
DOI: 10.11622/smedj.2019103
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7930314/
First publication date: 2019-09-06 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC complete original article HTML and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The retrospective cohort includes 25 people with Lewy body dementia and 31 with Alzheimer disease. Presentation and timing of survival-related complications differ by diagnosis. The source discusses clinical hallucination presence without assigning or evaluating a fig exposure.
