Title: Effects of fish and grape seed oils as core of haloperidol-loaded nanocapsules on oral dyskinesia in rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29209877/
PMID: 29209877
DOI: 10.1007/s11064-017-2444-0
Publication date: 2017-12-05
Date basis: First online publication. The journal issue is dated 2018.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: Original author abstract and previously archived full-report comparison.
Related research: research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Flaxseed-Followup.md#injected-grapeseed-oil-nanocapsules-and-oral-dyskinesia

The study injected haloperidol-loaded nanocapsules with grapeseed, fish or medium-chain triglyceride oil cores in rats. The grapeseed-core formulation partially attenuated the acute jaw-movement response relative to free haloperidol. All loaded formulations attenuated excess jaw movements in the subchronic comparison. The endpoint was experimentally induced oral dyskinesia. The treatment comparison is between drug-delivery formulations, without clinical Parkinson dystonia, consumption of oil, or an identified cold-press grade.
