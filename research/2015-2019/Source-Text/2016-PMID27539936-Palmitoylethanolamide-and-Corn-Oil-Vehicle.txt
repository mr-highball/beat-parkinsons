Original study scope
A review cites corn as a source of palmitoylethanolamide and describes a mouse exposure in which corn oil carries the compound. That experiment examined distribution after subcutaneous administration. The pain comparisons elsewhere in the review use palmitoylethanolamide formulations and do not isolate oral corn oil or dates.

Continuing evidence gaps
Follow the cited distribution and analgesia studies separately. A pharmacologic carrier cannot establish a culinary corn-oil pain effect.
