Title: Rice bran oil prevents neuroleptic-induced extrapyramidal symptoms in rats

The study assigned 16 rats to a four-group comparison crossing haloperidol and water with extracted rice bran oil or water. Oil reduced chewing stereotypies and the motor coordination deficit under haloperidol in the reported comparisons. Open-field and home-cage activity did not show clear group differences. The food intervention was solvent-extracted oil, while commercial refining grade and measured product composition were unspecified. The reported motor outcomes do not directly quantify dystonia or rigidity and the model is drug-induced, distinct from Parkinson disease.

Continuing evidence gaps
Independence across the Samad 2015, 2016 and 2017 reports remains unverified. The later reports use similar methods with symptom proxies and different neurochemical panels. Group-level clinical rigidity, dystonia and refining remain open. Keep the evidence separate from the Naz–Shireen catalepsy comparison.
