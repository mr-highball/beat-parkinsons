Factual extract of official ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, checked 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

Active arm was topical 10% blend of essential oils from Curcuma longa, Piper nigrum, Pelargonium asperum, Zingiber officinale, Mentha x piperita and Rosmarinus officinalis ct. cineole in jojoba. Control was topical jojoba. Primary/secondary record fields include pain and chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy measures. The registry does not post numerical results.

The current official API record checked November 16, 2026 gives 26 actual enrollees. The linked journal article reports 27 randomized. The registry names weekly SF-MPQ-2 pain intensity as the primary outcome and daily VAS pain as secondary. It provides no posted results. The reason for the enrollment discrepancy and the original full arm-level pain tables remain unresolved.
