Title: Metabolic patterns across core features in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30805951/
Publisher: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/ana.25453
PMID: 30805951
DOI: 10.1002/ana.25453
Publication date: 2019-02-25
Date basis: Wiley first public accepted-manuscript date; journal version of record 2019-03-22 and May issue
Source depth: Primary abstract, publication history and university full-text accession inspected; original result tables and supplement remain open
Format: Factual digest of inspected material

The authors analyzed FDG-PET and clinical core features in 171 records from the European dementia with Lewy bodies imaging repository. Their principal component and regression analyses adjust for cognitive score, age, education, sex and center. Clinical visual hallucination status showed an association with metabolic patterns including lower metabolism in parts of the frontal, posterior cingulate and parietal regions. The study examines status relative to imaging and offers no episode-frequency or dried fig intake comparison in the abstract.

EANM OP-367 (PMC7103094) names the same consortium, includes many of the same investigators and analyzes a smaller sample with different modeling. Shared participants are plausible.
