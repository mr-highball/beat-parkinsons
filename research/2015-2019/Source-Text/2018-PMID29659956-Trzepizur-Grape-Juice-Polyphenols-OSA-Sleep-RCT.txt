Title: Polyphenols Have No Impact on Endothelial Function in Patients with Obstructive Sleep Apnea: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 29659956
DOI: 10.1093/jn/nxy005
First published: 2018
Authors: Trzepizur W, Bironneau V, Recoquillon S, Priou P, Meslier N, Hamel JF, Henni S, Darsonval A
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29659956/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind randomized 1-month trial (NCT01977924) in 40 adults with severe obstructive sleep apnea randomized to 300 mg/day grape juice polyphenols capsule (n=20) vs placebo (n=20). Primary endothelial function (RHI) and blood pressure were NULL. Secondary polysomnographic total sleep time decreased -10 min vs +15 min placebo (p=0.02) without sleep-stage shifts; HR changed -1 vs +6 bpm (p=0.001). Isolated polyphenol supplement in severe OSA, not whole raw grapes or PD insomnia.
