Factual extract from Wong et al., published January 14, 2016.
PMID 27247830. DOI 10.4088/PCC.15br01856.
Primary source: https://www.psychiatrist.com/pcc/five-supplements-and-multiple-psychotic-symptoms/
Adult case with several months of auditory and visual hallucinations and no Parkinson diagnosis reported. Five supplement products were initiated around symptom onset. Table 1 lists holy basil (Tulsi) extract as a component of a multi-ingredient blend. No culinary sweet basil dose was established.
The patient stopped the supplement regimen and received psychiatric treatment. The report describes improvement but does not compare individual substances or count visual hallucination frequency.
