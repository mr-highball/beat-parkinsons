Title: Beet the Best?
PMID: 29976553
PMCID: PMC6202165
DOI: 10.1161/CIRCRESAHA.118.313131
Registry ID: NCT01684930
First published: 2018
Authors: Woessner M, VanBruggen MD, Pieper CF, Sloane R, Kraus WE, Gow AJ, et al.
Journal: Circulation research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29976553/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (PMC6202165) in n=24 patients with peripheral artery disease (PAD) and intermittent claudication combining supervised exercise with nitrate-rich vs nitrate-depleted beetroot juice: active BRJ yielded greater improvements in claudication onset distance (pain-free walking) and 6-minute walk distance. Limits: vascular claudication rather than PD.
