Title: Effect of butylphthalide on cognition and activities of daily living in Parkinson disease dementia
Source: https://www.ynbzz.com/ynbzzcn/upload/files/201604.pdf#page=24
DOI: 10.3969/j.issn.1671-6450.2016.04.006
First public issue date: 2016-04-18
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated official issue article, printed pp. 351–354

The report describes 84 people with Parkinson dementia from
Shiyan People's Hospital, recruited June 2013 to June 2015
and allocated 42 per group by random-number table. Both
groups received usual care and donepezil. The adjunct group
also received synthetic racemic pharmaceutical butylphthalide
for 12 weeks. Rest tremor is an eligibility feature, and
baseline tremor-predominant counts are 11 in the adjunct
group and 12 in the comparison group.

Table 2 prints total UPDRS baseline to week 12 of
46.32±5.23 to 20.32±5.26 with adjunct and 46.85±6.96 to
35.12±5.11 with comparator. The follow-up comparison is
reported as p<.01. The body gives no individual rest item
or tremor-subtype follow-up. The exposure is a drug capsule.
