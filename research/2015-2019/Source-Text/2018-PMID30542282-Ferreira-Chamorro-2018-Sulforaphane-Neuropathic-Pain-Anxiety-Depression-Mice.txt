Title: Sulforaphane Inhibited the Nociceptive Responses, Anxiety- and Depressive-Like Behaviors Associated With Neuropathic Pain and Improved the Anti-allodynic Effects of Morphine in Mice.
PMID: 30542282
PMCID: PMC6277937
DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2018.01332
First published: 2018
Authors: Ferreira-Chamorro P, Redondo A, Riego G, Leánez S, Pol O
Journal: Frontiers in pharmacology
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6277937/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical study in C57BL/6 mice (n=6/group) with chronic constriction injury (CCI) neuropathic pain. Repeated sulforaphane (10 mg/kg i.p., days 14-28) progressively inhibited and by day 15 completely reversed mechanical allodynia, thermal hyperalgesia, and cold allodynia (all p<0.001 vs CCI-vehicle), attenuated comorbid anxiety- and depressive-like behaviors, normalized spinal/cortical Nrf2/HO-1, and potentiated morphine anti-allodynia. Limits: injected purified compound in non-PD mice.
