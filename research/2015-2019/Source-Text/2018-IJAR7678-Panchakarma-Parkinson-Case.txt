Title: One Parkinson case during a bundled Panchakarma treatment
Source: https://www.journalijar.com/uploads/2018/09/843_IJAR-24577.pdf
DOI: 10.21474/ijar01/7678
Publication date: 2018-09
Date basis: Publisher issue and paper publication month.
Review depth: Full original article, pp. 312–318 including symptom and function tables.

The clinicians describe one patient with Parkinson disease and Kampavata. The series combines Ksheerbala oil in nasal and head applications with other massage, steam and bowel procedures, multiple oral formulations and follow-up treatment. The source records changes across the full regimen. Its summary says tremor markedly resolved, while its results table records residual slight unilateral tremor at rest. Retain the table's residual symptom. Thirty-foot walking time changed from 56 to 40 seconds in the report, with qualitative improvement in rigidity and other symptoms.

Material limits are the uncontrolled single case, co-interventions, treatment history and assessor expectations. No treatment-cycle diary, isolated sesame-oil comparator or culinary ingestion was evaluated. The article and the 2021 open-label report share personnel and a setting. Their participant overlap is unresolved.
