Title: Dietary supplements for dysmenorrhoea.
PMID: 27000311
PMCID: PMC7387104
DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD002124.pub2
First published: 2016
Authors: Pattanittum P, Kunyanone N, Brown J, Sangkomkamhang US, Barnes J, Seyfoddin V, Marjoribanks J
Journal: The Cochrane database of systematic reviews
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27000311/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Cochrane systematic review and meta-analysis (Pattanittum et al., 2016, Cochrane Database Syst Rev, PMC7387104) of 27 RCTs (n=3,101) of dietary supplements for dysmenorrhea. For Anethum graveolens seed (Heidarifar 2014), Cochrane extracted VAS pain MD -1.15 points (95% CI -2.22 to -0.08; 1 RCT, n=46) vs placebo and MD 0.13 points (95% CI -1.01 to 1.27; 1 RCT, n=47) vs NSAIDs, grading overall certainty as low or very low due to small sample size, reporting gaps, and single-trial evidence.
