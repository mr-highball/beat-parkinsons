Publication: Crichton et al., SPICE prospective randomized trial protocol, first published July 23, 2018. DOI 10.1111/1747-0080.12446, PMID 30033645.
Original: https://doi.org/10.1111/1747-0080.12446
Coverage: Selected original protocol fields. The later publication reports the outcomes independently of this protocol.

The protocol plans a double-blind comparison between ginger root powder and placebo during chemotherapy. Anxiety and depression are listed among the secondary outcomes. The trial is associated with ACTRN12616000416493, shown with a p suffix in the original manuscript. Direct ANZCTR access was unavailable during review, and its first public posting date and full list of outcomes remain unverified.
