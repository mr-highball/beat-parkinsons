Title: Four Days of Blueberry Powder Supplementation Lowers the Blood Lactate Response to Running But Has No Effect on Time-Trial Performance.
PMID: 31629347
DOI: 10.1123/ijsnem.2019-0040
First published: 2019
Authors: Brandenburg JP, Giles LV
Journal: International journal of sport nutrition and exercise metabolism
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31629347/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled 3-way crossover trial in n=14 runners testing 4 days vs 2 days blueberry powder vs placebo before an 8-km time trial. Four-day blueberry lowered post-run blood lactate (5.4 vs 6.6 mmol/L, p=0.038) and attenuated reactive strength index drop (p=0.038), but had NO effect on time-trial performance, heart rate, or ratings of perceived exertion (RPE). Limits: acute athletic exercise physiology in young runners; null perceptual fatigue.
