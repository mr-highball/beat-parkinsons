Title: Daily supplementation with mushroom (Agaricus bisporus) improves balance and working memory in aged rats.
PMID: 26475179
DOI: 10.1016/j.nutres.2015.09.012
First published: 2015
Authors: Thangthaeng, Miller, Gomes
Journal: Nutrition research (New York, N.Y.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26475179/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Eight-week dietary study randomizing aged Fischer 344 rats to 0%, 0.5%, 1%, 2%, or 5% lyophilized whole white button mushroom (Agaricus bisporus). Rats fed 0.5% and 1% A. bisporus remained significantly longer on a narrow balance beam (improved postural balance), and 0.5% (~1.5 oz/d fresh human equivalent) improved Morris water maze working memory; 2% and 5% showed no balance/memory benefit. Limits: freeze-dried raw powder in non-PD aged rats.
