Title: Rubiscolin-6, a δ-Opioid Peptide from Spinach RuBisCO, Exerts Antidepressant-Like Effect in Restraint-Stressed Mice.
PMID: 31061291
DOI: 10.3177/jnsv.65.202
First published: 2019
Authors: Mitsumoto Y, Sato R, Tagawa N, Kato I
Journal: Journal of nutritional science and vitaminology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31061291/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 7 review).

Primary Abstract:
Rubiscolin-6 (Tyr-Pro-Leu-Asp-Leu-Phe) is produced by a pepsin digest of spinach d-ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase/oxygenase (RuBisCO) and known to act as an agonist on δ-opioid receptor. Here, we showed that administration of rubiscolin-6 reduced immobility time in the tail suspension test in restraint-stressed mice without effect on locomotor activity. The antidepressant-like effect of rubiscolin-6 was blocked by a δ-opioid receptor antagonist, naltrindole. These results indicate that rubiscolin-6 exerts antidepressant-like effect through activation of δ-opioid receptor.

Verified Round 7 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Preclinical mouse pharmacology study (J Nutr Sci Vitaminol 2019) showing synthetic rubiscolin-6 reduced immobility in the tail suspension test in restraint-stressed mice via naltrindole-sensitive delta-opioid receptors without altering locomotion. Per Karasawa 2023 (PMC10298254 Section 3.2), administration was intraperitoneal (non-oral) synthetic isolated hexapeptide, not dietary cooked/steamed spinach (exposure_fit=remote/unusable; population_fit=remote).
