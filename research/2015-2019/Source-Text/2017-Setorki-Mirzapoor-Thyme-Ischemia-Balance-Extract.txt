Injected thyme extract and balance after ischemia

Source: https://brieflands.com/journals/zjrms/articles/9216
Coverage: Original Methods and Results, factual extract.

Six groups of seven Wistar rats included controls, ischemia and three thyme extract dose groups. Extract was delivered intraperitoneally after experimentally induced ischemia. The 100 and 200 mg/kg groups had significantly better rotarod balance than ischemia without extract according to Results section 3.2. No dietary dried thyme or Parkinson clinical postural outcome was studied.
