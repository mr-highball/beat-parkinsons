Title: Isolated sesamin and movement in a rat model of Parkinson disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28157651/
PMID: 28157651
DOI: 10.1016/j.biopha.2017.01.123
Publication date: 2017-01-31
Date basis: First electronic publication. The journal issue is dated April 2017.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: Original author abstract.

The investigators examined sesamin after a unilateral lesion in rats. They report model motor performance and cellular or biochemical outcomes. The assigned substance is a sesame constituent rather than toasted sesame oil, and the outcomes do not isolate reduced facial expressiveness.
