Title: Evaluation of anxiolytic and sedative effect of essential oil and hydroalcoholic extract of Ocimum basilicum L. and chemical composition of its essential oil.
PMID: 26779273
PMCID: PMC4698864
First published: 2015
Authors: Rabbani M, Sajjadi SE, Vaezi A
Journal: Research in pharmaceutical sciences
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4698864/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical study (PMC4698864) in male Syrian mice testing intraperitoneal Ocimum basilicum L. hydroalcoholic extract (100, 150, 200 mg/kg i.p.) and essential oil (200 mg/kg i.p.; 42.8% methyl chavicol, 13.0% geranial, 12.2% neral) on EPM and locomotor activity meter. Extract 150 and 200 mg/kg and essential oil 200 mg/kg significantly increased EPM open-arm time (100 mg/kg nonsignificant; open-arm entries unchanged) while reducing locomotor activity (sedative-anxiolytic profile).
