Title: EVOO intake in the ALS pilot companion nutrition abstract
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1747-0080.12567
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Hammond and colleagues, 2019, oral abstract 325, matching team and pilot arms. Among groups assigned the diet, reported EVOO intake rose from 3.9% to 18.3% of energy. The abstract reports weight change +1.2 kg compared with +0.1 kg in exercise only, p = .1. It provides no swallowing test. The matching sample size of 14 and trial design identify this as a companion nutrition report of the preceding pilot.
