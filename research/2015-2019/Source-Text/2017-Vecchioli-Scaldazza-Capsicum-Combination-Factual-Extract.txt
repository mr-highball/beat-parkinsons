Vecchioli-Scaldazza and colleagues, original Tables 1 and 2

Original source https://www.pagepressjournals.org/aiua/article/download/aiua.2017.4.296/6920

The publisher dates this study December 31, 2017. A 12-week open-label comparison in women with overactive bladder tested a mixed oral product containing Capsicum annuum, pumpkin, Polygonum ingredients, vitamins and glutamine against solifenacin. Table 2 reports urgency intensity scores changing from 3.47 to 1.94 on the combination and 3.55 to 2.65 on solifenacin, with reported post-treatment p = .0039. The paper gives conflicting enrollment counts. Abstract and methods say 45 per group while results say 50 per group entered and 42 and 48 were evaluable. Neurologic disease was excluded. The comparator and combination leave the individual pepper effect unresolved.
