Title: Efficacy of oral administration of licorice as an adjunct therapy on improving the symptoms of patients with Parkinson's disease, A randomized double blinded clinical trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31574343/
PMID: 31574343
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2019.112226
First public article date: 2019-09-28
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated author abstract with registry identity and access limits

Petramfar and colleagues randomized 39 people with Parkinson
disease to licorice root extract syrup or placebo alongside
standard management for up to six months. The abstract states
that tremor and daily activity improved at the six-week
assessment. It gives neither a tremor subtype nor an item
estimate or confidence interval. A later motor and rigidity
sentence describes a significant result while printing p>.05.

The previously cataloged registry IRCT20120305009204N3 is the
same trial. The original results table and the thesis mentioned
by the publisher remain unrecovered in this audit. The EMA
draft assessment gives total UPDRS and attrition for the same
trial, but does not provide separate rest-tremor measurements.
