Title: Efficacy of Violet oil, a traditional Iranian formula, in patients with chronic insomnia: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study.
PMID: 29217495
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2017.11.036
First published: 2018
Authors: Feyzabadi Z, Rezaeitalab F, Badiee S, Taghipour A, Moharari F, Soltanifar A, Ahmadpour MR
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29217495/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-arm double-blind randomized controlled trial in n=75 adults with chronic insomnia (Traditional Iranian Medicine Clinic, Mashhad) receiving 3 intranasal drops per nostril nightly for 30 days of Violet oil (Viola odorata in almond/sesame oil base), plain Almond oil, or 1% carboxymethyl cellulose (CMC) placebo. Baseline ISI and PSQI did not differ (p > 0.05). Post-intervention three-group differences were significant for ISI (p < 0.002) and PSQI (p < 0.001); within-group ISI and PSQI improved in all three arms including placebo (Violet oil p < 0.001, Almond oil p < 0.001, placebo p < 0.001), with Violet oil showing the largest effect. Route was intranasal drops rather than oral cold-pressed ingestion, and abstract does not establish pairwise superiority of intranasal almond oil over CMC placebo.
