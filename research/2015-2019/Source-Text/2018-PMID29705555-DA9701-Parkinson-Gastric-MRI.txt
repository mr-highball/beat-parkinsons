Title: DA-9701 on gastric motility in patients with Parkinson's disease: A randomized controlled trial
PMID: 29705555
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2018.04.018
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29705555/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1353802018302062
Registration: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03022201
Published: 2018-04-21 online, Parkinsonism & Related Disorders 54, 84–89.
Archive scope: Factual summary from the original author abstract, trial registry and published baseline MRI reanalysis. Original endpoint tables remain open.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Forty adults with Parkinson disease were randomized to DA-9701 or domperidone, and 19 per arm completed four weeks. The active-comparator change in MRI gastric emptying at two hours was 4.0 percentage points (one-sided 95% lower bound −3.7, prespecified noninferiority margin −10). The 54.5% to 61.8% result describes the change within the DA-9701 arm. The registry also specifies diary assessments of dyspepsia and constipation, whose tabulated changes are unavailable in the accessible abstract. An exploratory levodopa subset included nine participants per arm and showed no clear increase.

Cho et al. explicitly reanalyzed baseline MRI from this same trial in PMC6499432. It describes two follow-up losses, one requiring an increase in Parkinson medication after aggravation of disease. The 2018 abstract refers to no deterioration observed among its study participants. Keep the dropout history separate from completer results. The baseline reanalysis estimates associations between gastric measurements and symptoms before treatment, without another randomized contrast. NCT02775591 is a different placebo-comparator study. None gives an assigned ginger exposure.
