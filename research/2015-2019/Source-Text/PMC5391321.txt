The SGIM supplement contains a separate anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis case with visual hallucinations and a severe vitamin B12 deficiency case with imbalance. Their diagnoses, treatments and outcomes differ. No member links a measured dried fig exposure to Parkinson visual episode frequency.

Control ID 2700754 documents a visual hallucination report during anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis with later general neurological improvement. Control ID 2707619 describes fatigue and loss of balance in severe vitamin B12 deficiency, treated with injectable vitamin B12. These are different cases. Neither reports exposure to figs or treats idiopathic Parkinson disease.
