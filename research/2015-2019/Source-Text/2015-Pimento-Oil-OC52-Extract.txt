Shelly-Campbell J and Oswald S. J Pharm Pharmacogn Res 3(suppl. 1):S75, 2015. FAPRONATURA 2015, September 21–25, oral communication OC-52. Original conference abstract rechecked in publisher HTML. The model used Sprague-Dawley rats with experimentally induced monoarthritis and groups receiving pimento oil, corn oil, eugenol or methyleugenol. Sedation associated with dose was reported, with no onset times, sign counts or standardized fine motor instrument. Administration route and oil plant part are unreported. The possible oral formulation discussed by the authors is not verification of the route used. This was not a Parkinson or ground allspice study.
