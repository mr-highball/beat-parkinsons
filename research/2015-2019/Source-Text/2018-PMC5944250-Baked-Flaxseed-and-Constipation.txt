Title: A randomized trial of the effects of flaxseed to manage constipation, weight, glycemia, and lipids in constipated patients with type 2 diabetes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5944250/
DOI: 10.1186/s12986-018-0273-z
Publication date: 2018-05-08
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: Original intervention methods and Table 3.
PMID: 29780230
PMCID: PMC5944250
Trial registration: IRCT20110416006202N2

The 53-person analysis compares flaxseed baked into cookies with placebo cookies in adults with type 2 diabetes and constipation. The reported 12-week global symptom change favors flaxseed, +2.46 vs +0.41 on the study score with positive change representing improvement (p=.008). Interviewed improvement rated at least somewhat better was reported by 11 of 26 versus 2 of 27. The separate follow-up after discontinuation gives an inconclusive group comparison. The intervention is whole-seed material baked into food. It does not identify an effect of the extracted oil or a clinical Parkinson comparison.
