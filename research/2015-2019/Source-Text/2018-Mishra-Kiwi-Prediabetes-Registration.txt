Whole kiwi and prediabetes trial registration
https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=374559

March 6, 2018. ANZCTR registered the 12-week kiwifruit intervention in adults with prediabetes. The trial measures glycaemia and related metabolic effects and compares regular whole fruit consumption with control. The exact identifier links the 2022 Mishra results report. The registered medical context and outcome list supply no cognitive attention endpoint.
