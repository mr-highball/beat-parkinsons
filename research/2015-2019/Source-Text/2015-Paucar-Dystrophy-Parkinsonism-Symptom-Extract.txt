Hallucinations and rest tremor with concomitant muscular dystrophy
Publication: October 20, 2015 first online, Movement Disorders Clinical Practice 3(2), 194–196
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12247
PMID: 30713914
PMCID: PMC6353534
Source: https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/mdc3.12247
Coverage: Identity-matched original published body and clinical or assay results.

Paucar and colleagues describe a woman with genetically supported facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy and levodopa-responsive parkinsonism. Her clinical history included back pain, resting tremor and visual hallucinations. The report describes a qualitative reduction in hallucination frequency after introducing quetiapine and a tremor response to levodopa. The authors discuss idiopathic Parkinson disease as compatible with the presentation. The case has no symptom episode counts or assigned culinary exposure.
