Nigella mouth rinse and normal food counts
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2019/3619357
PMID: 31781612
PMCID: PMC6875195
DOI: 10.1155/2019/3619357
Coverage: Original Methods and complete Table 2

Hussain and colleagues randomized 54 patients receiving chemotherapy for acute myeloid leukemia. Two control-arm participants died in the first week and 52 were analyzed. Table 2 shows normal food in 27/27 nigella-oil versus 20/25 control at day 4, 27/27 versus 15/25 at day 12 and 27/27 versus 18/25 at day 18. The reported diet-category differences had p=.02, p<.001 and p=.004, respectively. At day 28 the counts were 26/27 versus 20/25, with one oil-arm participant on a liquid diet and p=.159 across categories. The table qualifies the broad statement in the abstract that all oil-arm participants could eat normal food throughout. The trial was open label and used a topical oil rinse during mucositis. Diet texture was self-reported. The tested route, mechanism and population differ from eating ground nigella seed for Parkinson dysphagia.
