Title: Caffeine reduction manual and the pooled withdrawal-syndrome item

Date basis: First electronic publication.
Review depth: Complete identified PMC structured original and public rendered Table 1, including its footnotes.

Thirty-six treatment seekers were randomized to immediate or delayed use of a caffeine reduction manual. The caffeine-use analyses and the symptom follow-up have different denominators. For 27 completers with the withdrawal item available, the reported symptom score uses the maximum rating of five categories, one labeled fatigue or drowsiness. Table 1 reports mean composite 5.2 at intake, 4.8 at end of treatment and 1.8 at 20 weeks. The 20-week change is reported relative to intake. These time points do not demonstrate a randomized isolated fatigue contrast.

Coffee, tea and other sources contributed to caffeine intake. The study supplies evidence about total use and a withdrawal composite, without a specific brewed-beverage Parkinson fatigue result. The earlier 2015 cohort received therapist-guided treatment, while this trial tested a manual-only intervention.
