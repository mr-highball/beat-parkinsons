Title: Diet quality improves for parents and children when almonds are incorporated into their daily diet: a randomized, crossover study.
PMID: 26773784
DOI: 10.1016/j.nutres.2015.11.004
First published: 2016
Authors: Burns, Zitt, Rowe, Langkamp-Henken, Mai
Journal: Nutrition research (New York, N.Y.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26773784/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-week randomized crossover trial (4-week washout) in 29 parents (35 +/- 0.6 y) and 29 children (4 +/- 0.2 y) consuming 1.5 oz (parents) or 0.5 oz (children) daily of almonds and/or almond butter vs no-almond control. Healthy Eating Index-2010 rose from 53.7 to 61.4 in both parents and children (P < 0.001). No change in daily stool frequency and minimal changes in weekly GSRS gastrointestinal symptoms were observed. Limits: mixed whole almond/butter allowance, healthy non-PD cohort, short 3-week period.
