Marjoram aroma within a chronic insomnia program
https://jkan.or.kr/journal/view.php?number=2884
selected primary methods and findings paraphrase

Published 2017. The nonrandomized community-center comparison analyzed 62 older women with chronic insomnia and excluded Parkinson disease. The intervention paired sleep education, exercise, meditation and a marjoram–orange aroma necklace. The authors report a group sleep-quality comparison t = −5.10, p < .001. There was no dried oral marjoram arm or contrast separating scent, other components and center selection.
