Title: Multimodal EEG-MRI in the differential diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease and dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4866554/
PMCID: PMC4866554
PMID: 27060340
DOI: 10.1016/j.jpsychires.2016.03.010
Publication date: 2016-03-25
Date basis: NLM first electronic publication; July journal issue
Source depth: Complete original PMC structured article body
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Seventy-two people across controls, Alzheimer disease and dementia with Lewy bodies underwent EEG and MRI. The 21-person DLB group showed higher NPI hallucination scores than the Alzheimer group. The clinical item and diagnostic-imaging grouping address a different population and outcome from food-timed Parkinson visual episodes.
