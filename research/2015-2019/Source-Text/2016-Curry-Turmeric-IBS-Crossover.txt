Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ctim.2016.04.002
Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01418066
Original article and outcome summary. A registered randomized crossover compared a curry leaf, pomegranate and turmeric decoction with placebo in diarrhea-predominant IBS. The report found no clear difference for overall IBS severity. The study tested a multi-ingredient preparation and supplies no Parkinson motor assessment or individual ingredient result.
