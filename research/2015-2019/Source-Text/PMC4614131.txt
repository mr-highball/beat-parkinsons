The review relates cognitive profiles and visual perceptual dysfunction in Parkinson dementia to large-scale neural networks. Its visual hallucination map refers to prior imaging contrasts and its discussion draws on earlier clinical and experimental studies. It does not add a new food-exposed cohort or count visual episodes after dried fig intake.
