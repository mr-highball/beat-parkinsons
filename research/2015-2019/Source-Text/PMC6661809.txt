Title: Lead optimization for promising monoamine oxidase inhibitor from eugenol for the treatment of neurological disorder: synthesis and in silico based study.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6661809/
PMID: 31384786
PMCID: PMC6661809
DOI: 10.1186/s13065-019-0552-4
Publication date: 2019-03-26
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official complete PMC JATS body.

The laboratory study uses commercial eugenol and tests derivatives in enzyme assays. The introduction cites multiple botanical sources and separate animal literature. No original arm assigned culinary nutmeg, cinnamon or oregano and no patient had an assessed anxiety or depressive symptom.
