Title: Salvia officinalis reduces the severity of the premenstrual syndrome
PMID: 30572339
DOI: 10.1159/000490104
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30572339/
Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1159/000490104
First published: 2018-12-15 online, 2019 issue
Coverage: Author abstract. Original result tables and individual pain outcomes remain open.

Abdnezhad and colleagues describe 90 students randomized to 500 mg oral sage extract or placebo daily for two menstrual cycles. The abstract reports total symptom-severity decreases of 19.84% and 23.42% in the extract group for months one and two, respectively. These are active-group changes. The abstract describes greater improvement in physical and psychological symptom groups with extract, without giving arm-specific pain estimates. The 2017 companion abstract reports unequal total symptom scores at baseline and a favorable emotional symptom comparison. Shared authors, setting, sample size and intervention support treating the reports as one likely family. No item-level pain result or fresh culinary sage exposure in Parkinson disease is available from these passages.
