Title: Visual Perceptual Organization Ability in Autopsy-Verified Dementia with Lewy Bodies and Alzheimer's Disease.
PMID: 27221597
PMCID: PMC5802394
DOI: 10.1017/s1355617716000436
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5802394/
First publication date: 2016-05-25 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The study compares visual perceptual organization in autopsy-verified Lewy body and Alzheimer dementia. Visual task performance is an experimental measure distinct from spontaneous hallucination episodes. No exposure to dried figs is assigned.
