NM01 describes processed egg-yolk supplementation and weight gain in 334 neonatal puppies. It does not test poached egg, people with Parkinson disease or falls. The collection also contains institutional names Sage Centers and Orange Park; those matches do not identify sage or orange interventions. The animal nutritional context is retained without assigning a human symptom effect.
