Early fermented perilla patent examples

Primary source: https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2019107844A1/en
Coverage: Published patent Example 2
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published June 6, 2019. Ajou University's application describes a hot-water perilla leaf extract and extracts fermented with different organisms in a mouse pentobarbital sleep test. One example gives onset latencies of 325 s with saline, 261 s with unfermented extract and 180 s with Bacillus-fermented extract. The patent is an early development record and shares institution and close assay features with later reports. Independent cohorts are unverified. It provides no culinary dried-leaf or human comparison.
