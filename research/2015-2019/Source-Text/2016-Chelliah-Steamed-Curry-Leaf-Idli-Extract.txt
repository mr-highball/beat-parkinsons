Steamed idli made with ground curry leaves
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4951439/
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

Dried ground leaves were mixed into rice and black-gram batter that was later steamed. Table 2 reports iron of 36.8 ± 0.13 mg per 100 g in the curry leaf product and 6.12 ± 0.2 mg per 100 g in control idli. The sensory panel rated mouthfeel, flavor and texture, and instruments measured firmness. Methods describe a five-point scale while Table 3 contains scores above five. The study offers a prepared food and composition measurement but no measured swallowing impairment, absorbed iron response or levodopa pharmacokinetics.
