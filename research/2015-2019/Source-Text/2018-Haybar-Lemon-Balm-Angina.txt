Lemon balm powder and depression scores in stable angina
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clnesp.2018.04.015
Coverage: factual extract of original Methods and Table 3
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Haybar and colleagues randomized 80 adults with stable angina to 3 g/day of powdered lemon balm in capsules or corn starch for eight weeks. Table 3 lists 35 and 38 completers. DASS-21 depression means fell from 8.54 to 6.46 and 6.92 to 6.71, respectively. Reported baseline-adjusted ANCOVA p < .001. Adjusted means and intervals are unavailable in the table. Previous depression and mental disorders were exclusions. The sleep and biomarker reports with overlapping authors and matching arms should not be counted as independent without resolving their registration identity. Original Table 3 was checked from the author article rendered at https://www.scribd.com/document/531022292/haybar2018.
