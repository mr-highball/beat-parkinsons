Title: Responder reanalysis of the 2014 poststroke Daikenchuto trial
PMID: 31823650
PMCID: PMC6906340
DOI: 10.1177/2515690X19889271
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6906340/
Published: 2019-12-11
Archive scope: Evidence-focused summary of the inspected original article body.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

This secondary analysis explicitly reuses all 34 participants from the 2014 Numata comparison (17 per group). Baseline characteristics refer to Numata et al. and add Neurogenic Bowel Dysfunction Score and a constipation subscale of the Gastrointestinal Symptom Rating Scale. The paper examines how higher baseline severity relates to greater observed improvement in the same treated group. These are exploratory response analyses. The new scales do not add a second independent treatment comparison or isolate ginger.
