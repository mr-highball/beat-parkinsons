Title: Apium graveolens extract influences mood and cognition in healthy mice.
PMID: 28205135
DOI: 10.1007/s11418-017-1077-6
First published: 2017
Authors: Boonruamkaew P, Sukketsiri W, Panichayupakaranant P, Kaewnam W, Tanasawet S, Tipmanee V et al.
Journal: J Nat Med
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28205135/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical study (same Thai laboratory as Chonpathompikunlert 2018) feeding healthy male mice whole-plant Apium graveolens methanolic extract (65-500 mg/kg/d x 30 d) vs vehicle, donepezil, and fluoxetine. Doses of 125-500 mg/kg significantly reduced immobility in forced swimming and tail suspension tests, improved spatial/non-spatial memory, lowered brain MAO-A and AChE activities, and reduced lipid peroxidation. Caveats: healthy non-PD mice, whole-plant methanolic solvent extract (not raw/cooked culinary celery stalk alone), and human raw-celery survey Brookie 2018 was null for depressive symptoms.
