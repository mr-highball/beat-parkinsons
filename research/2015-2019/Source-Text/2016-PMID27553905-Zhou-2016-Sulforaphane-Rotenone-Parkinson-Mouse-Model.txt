Title: Sulforaphane protects against rotenone-induced neurotoxicity in vivo: Involvement of the mTOR, Nrf2, and autophagy pathways.
PMID: 27553905
PMCID: PMC4995453
DOI: 10.1038/srep32206
First published: 2016
Authors: Zhou Q, Chen B, Wang X, Wu L, Yang Y, Cheng X
Journal: Scientific reports
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4995453/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Sixty-day rotenone-induced PD mouse study evaluating intraperitoneal sulforaphane (50 mg/kg every other day; behavioral figure caption n=3/group). Sulforaphane significantly rescued rotenone-induced loss of spontaneous hind-limb stepping/rearing in the cylinder (Fig 1d) and challenging narrow-beam traversal time (Fig 1f; p<0.05-0.01 vs rotenone), restored >50% striatal/cortical TH protein and reduced ROS/MDA while activating Nrf2/HO-1/NQO1 and mTOR/autophagy. Note: Fig 1e adhesive removal was lengthened by rotenone but NOT listed among the panels rescued in Results prose. Limits: injected purified sulforaphane, very small n=3/group in behavioral panels.
