Title: Diet rich in date palm fruits improves memory, learning and reduces beta amyloid in transgenic mouse model of Alzheimer's disease.
PMID: 26167001
PMCID: PMC4484046
DOI: 10.4103/0975-9476.159073
First published: 2015
Authors: Subash S, Essa MM, Braidy N, Awlad-Thani K, Vaishnav R, Al-Adawi S et al.
Journal: J Ayurveda Integr Med
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4484046/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical Alzheimer transgenic mouse study (Subash et al., 2015, J Ayurveda Integr Med, PMC4484046) feeding 2% or 4% Oman date palm fruit-supplemented chow to APPsw/Tg2576 mice for 14 months. Date-fed AD mice showed improved Morris water maze spatial memory, reduced anxiety-like behavior, improved rotarod motor coordination, and lower brain Abeta40/42. Limits: transgenic Alzheimer mouse model (not Parkinson dopaminergic degeneration) without clinical human confirmation.
