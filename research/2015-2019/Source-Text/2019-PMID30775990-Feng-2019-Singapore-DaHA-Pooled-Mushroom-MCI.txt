Title: The Association between Mushroom Consumption and Mild Cognitive Impairment: A Community-Based Cross-Sectional Study in Singapore.
PMID: 30775990
DOI: 10.3233/JAD-180959
First published: 2019
Authors: Feng L, Cheah IK, Ng MM, Li J, Chan SM, Lim SL
Journal: Journal of Alzheimer's disease : JAD
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30775990/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional Singapore Diet and Healthy Aging (DaHA) study in n=663 adults >=60 y. Pooled mushroom intake >2 portions/wk associated with lower MCI odds (OR 0.43, 95% CI 0.23-0.78, p=0.006). Limits: cross-sectional; pools six commonly consumed Asian mushroom species (golden, oyster, shiitake, white button, dried, canned) without isolating A. bisporus.
