Title: Postoperative cognitive dysfunction and short-term neuroprotection from blueberries: a pilot study.
PMID: 29856175
DOI: 10.23736/S0375-9393.18.12333-9
First published: 2018
Authors: Traupe I, Giacalone M, Agrimi J, Baroncini M, Pomé A, Fabiani D, et al.
Journal: Minerva anestesiologica
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29856175/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized pilot trial in n=26 elective major surgery patients assigned to 500 mL/d blueberry juice for 14 preoperative days vs control. Controls showed significant postoperative declines on Prose Memory (p<0.001), Attentional Matrices (p<0.01), and Trail Making Test Part B (p<0.01), whereas the blueberry juice arm showed no decline across these three tests. Limits: small unblinded/no-placebo perioperative pilot using juice rather than raw fruit; non-PD surgical population.
