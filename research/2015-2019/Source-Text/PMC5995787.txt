This review synthesizes clinical apomorphine studies and describes mixed neuropsychiatric observations. References 182–184 lead to earlier treatment reports. The cited observations use a prescribed drug and may reflect changes to other dopaminergic medications. The review supplies no dried fig comparison or original counted visual outcome.
