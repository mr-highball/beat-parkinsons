Title: Improvement Effect of Dewaxed Brown Rice on Constipation in Antibiotic-treated Mice.
PMID: 28652422
PMCID: PMC5566905
DOI: 10.21873/invivo.11096
First published: 2017
Authors: Inagawa H, Saika T, Nishiyama N, Nisizawa T, Kohchi C, Uenobe M
Journal: In vivo (Athens, Greece)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5566905/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Mouse study showing 50% dewaxed brown rice feed significantly improved defecation frequency, wet/dry stool weight per hour, and body weight after 27-day antibiotic-induced constipation (which halved stool output in controls). Preclinical antibiotic-constipation model.
