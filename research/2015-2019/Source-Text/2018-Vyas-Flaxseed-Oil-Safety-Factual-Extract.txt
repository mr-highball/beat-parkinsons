The randomized dyslipidemia study lists 60 allocated per arm. Table 5 tabulates 57 recipients of flaxseed oil and 55 recipients of liquid paraffin after diet restriction. Two and five participants respectively were listed as reporting constipation. These are small counts with no grading of severity or confirmation of pressing. The comparator was active. Neither arm was a Parkinson cohort.
