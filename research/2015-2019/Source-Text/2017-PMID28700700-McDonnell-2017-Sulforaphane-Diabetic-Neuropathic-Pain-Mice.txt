Title: The induction of the transcription factor Nrf2 enhances the antinociceptive effects of delta-opioid receptors in diabetic mice.
PMID: 28700700
PMCID: PMC5507309
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0180998
First published: 2017
Authors: McDonnell C, Leánez S, Pol O
Journal: PloS one
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5507309/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical study in diabetic db/db mice showing sulforaphane dose-dependently reversed mechanical allodynia, potentiated delta-opioid receptor agonist anti-allodynic effects, normalized sciatic nerve Nrf2/HO-1/NQO1, and reduced hyperglycemia. Limits: purified compound in diabetic neuropathic mice.
