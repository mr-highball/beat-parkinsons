Text coverage: original review Results and Summary of Findings 4.

The caffeine comparison reuses the 61-person Postuma 2012 capsule trial. The main Results reports post-intervention fatigue severity SMD +0.19 (95% CI −0.32 to +0.69), while the summary-table prose describes the same magnitude as lower. The interval spans zero either way. The magnitude for impact of fatigue on activities is −0.29 (95% CI −0.79 to +0.22). The parent trial instead reports its own adjusted estimates at three and six weeks. Keep this sign and endpoint distinction as a sensitivity check within the same trial family. The synthesis adds no independent trial of brewed coffee.
