Title: Significance of visual hallucinations and cerebral hypometabolism in the risk of dementia in Parkinson's disease patients with mild cognitive impairment.
PMID: 26663702
PMCID: PMC6867477
DOI: 10.1002/hbm.23080
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6867477/
First publication date: 2015-12-14 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC complete original article HTML and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The Parkinson mild cognitive impairment study classified 12 patients without visual hallucinations and nine with them, with 19 controls. It compared FDG uptake and later cognitive outcomes. Dementia progression was observed in both Parkinson subgroups. The data stratify existing hallucination status without assigning food.
