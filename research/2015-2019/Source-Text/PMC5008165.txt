Title: Syndromic parkinsonism and dementia associated with OPA1 missense mutations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5008165/
PMCID: PMC5008165
PMID: 25820230
DOI: 10.1002/ana.24410
Publication date: 2015-06-10
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary family study
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Two families with OPA1 variants had syndromic neurological findings that included parkinsonism, dementia, optic or other sensory disturbances, and mitochondrial abnormalities. An individual case had clinical visual hallucinations. Clinical descriptions and functional assays do not establish a food-timed visual episode rate or transfer directly to idiopathic Parkinson disease.
