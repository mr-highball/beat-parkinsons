Title: In vivo anti-inflammatory activity and UPLC-MS/MS profiling of the peels and pulps of Cucumis melo var. cantalupensis and Cucumis melo var. reticulatus.
PMID: 30857984
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2019.03.015
First published: 2019
Authors: Ezzat SM, Raslan M, Salama MM, Menze ET, El Hawary SS
Journal: J Ethnopharmacol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30857984/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

In vivo rat study (adult male Sprague-Dawley rats) testing oral 95% ethanol extracts of peels and pulps of Cucumis melo var. cantalupensis and var. reticulatus (25 and 50 mg/kg) 30 min before carrageenan paw edema vs indomethacin 10 mg/kg. Pulp and peel extracts inhibited paw edema at 3 h (except low-dose French cantaloupe pulp), with peel extracts reducing TNF-alpha (p<0.05), IL-1beta (p<0.001), PGE-2, and IL-6 (p<0.05). UPLC-MS/MS identified 44 phenolic compounds. Also documents Egyptian traditional use of cantaloupe for hot liver inflammation, cough, eczema, and urinary tract ulcers. Non-PD acute edema model; no nociceptive pain or motor testing.
