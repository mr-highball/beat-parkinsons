Title: Phillips 2018, local factual extract of cooked pea recipes and the falls report.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175383/
Supplement: https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC6175383.1/MDS-33-1306-s002.pdf
Population: 47 randomized people with Parkinson disease, 44 started and 38 completed.
Exposure: Low-fat plan includes frozen green peas boiled with pasta then baked in tuna casserole (printed p. 82), and a separate baked beef stroganoff recipe with peas.
Outcome: A person with pre-existing postural hypotension and falls in the low-fat arm required increased midodrine for ongoing falls. Individual cooked-pea intake, case timing and fall counts were not provided.
