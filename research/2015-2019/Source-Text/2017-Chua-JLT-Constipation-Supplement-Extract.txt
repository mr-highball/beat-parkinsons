Title: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Chinese Medicine on Nonmotor Symptoms in Parkinson's Disease
PMID: 28630780
PMCID: PMC5463168
DOI: 10.1155/2017/1902708
First public publication: 2017-05-23
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5463168/
Format: Factual extraction from the original clinical report and Supplementary Table 1.
Chua and colleagues compared the Jiawei-Liujunzi Tang granules plus usual treatment with placebo plus usual treatment. The flow shows 116 randomized and 111 who received treatment. The primary MDS-UPDRS Part I total contrast was -1.30 (95% CI -3.37 to 0.77, p=.215). A post-hoc NMSS constipation item result favored the mixture at week 16 (-2.49, 95% CI -3.75 to -1.23, p<.001).
The official supplementary table lists 11 components, none identified as Perilla leaf or seed. This outcome concerns a distinct multi-herb formula and does not apply to fresh shiso.
Registration named by the paper: ChiCTR-TRC-13003085. The official record was not independently verified in this follow-through.
