Title: Change in organosulfur compounds in onion (Allium cepa L.) during heat treatment.

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
LC/ESI-MS/MS quantification of onion S-alk(en)yl-L-cysteine sulfoxides (methiin, isoalliin, propiin, cycloalliin) across thermal cooking methods. Dry-heat/non-leaching cooking (frying, steaming, microwaving) INCREASED ACSOs by 34.2% to 568.0%, whereas boiling leached/decreased ACSOs by 32.6% to 69.4%. Bridges Nakayama 2020 onion ACSO sleep trial to dry-heat roasted onion. Companion cooking-chemistry studies (Cattivelli 2021 PMID:34066759; Rohn 2007 PMID:17263552) also show baking/roasting increases quercetin bioaccessibility and preserves stable aglycone.
