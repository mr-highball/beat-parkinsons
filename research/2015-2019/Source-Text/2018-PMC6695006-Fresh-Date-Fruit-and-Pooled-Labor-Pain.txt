Title: Effects of date fruit consumption on labour and vaginal delivery in Tabuk, KSA

The hospital trial reports 89 women assigned to fresh Rotana rutab date fruit with water (32), fruit alone (26), or no fruit (31). Table 1 lists pooled labor pain categories for 76 participants: 16 mild, 42 moderate and 18 severe. The arm tables and narrative supply no corresponding per-arm labor-pain estimate. Labor progression and neonatal outcomes do not supply the missing contrast. The background statement about pain tolerance appears outside the reported randomized results.
Evidence boundary: Whole fruit is documented as fresh. The pain categories have no arm-specific breakdown, and the population and clinical setting differ from Parkinson pain. This study leaves direction unresolved.
