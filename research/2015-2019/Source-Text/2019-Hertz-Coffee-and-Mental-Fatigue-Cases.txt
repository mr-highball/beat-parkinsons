Coverage: factual extract of original case histories and Table 1.

Two half-brothers with Parkinson disease reported different coffee histories. Table 1 records eight cups per day explicitly described as premorbid for the first case and two to three cups per day in the second case. Mental-fatigue observations were 10.5/44 and 10/44. The report supplies neither preparation-specific intake nor an assigned contrast. The first case's resting-tremor label in Table 1 differs from the absence of a documented rest tremor in the initial narrative. The table describes substantial differences in disease stage and motor score.
