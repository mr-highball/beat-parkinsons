Title: Factual extract of the 2019 results attachment for EudraCT 2007-002899-34.
Source: https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/rest/download/result/attachment/2007-002899-34/1/26590
Review basis: All five primary page images inspected; searchable extraction empty.

The trial was registered as EudraCT 2007-002899-34 for people with Parkinson disease and mild cognitive impairment. The five-page results attachment posted in 2019 states that the study assigned encapsulated dried common sage leaves or vitamin E for six weeks. The report describes computerized cognition and salivation. The referenced secondary-outcome timetable is absent, and the pages contain no handwriting or timed hand-task result. The vitamin E comparator differs from an inert placebo, although the attachment describes a placebo-controlled study. The publicly available pages cannot establish a fresh sage effect on handwriting severity.

The report gives 600 mg of dried sage leaf per capsule dose twice daily versus vitamin E at 400 IU twice daily. The six-week report describes no sleep endpoint, insomnia result or insomnia adverse event in its five pages. Tolerability language and the absence of a full adverse-event table limit any sleep safety interpretation. The only reported significant between-group cognitive result favored vitamin E for working-memory quality. The official database entry is dated June 26, 2007, the report describes March–August 2008, and the public results version is dated March 24, 2019. These are separate dates. The EU Clinical Trials Register began public operation in March 2011 and this record does not give its exact first public protocol posting date.
