Title: Dyskinesia-Hyperpyrexia Syndrome in Parkinson's Disease: A Heat Shock-Related Emergency?
PMID: 30515444
PMCID: PMC6207118
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12663
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6207118/
First publication date: 2018-09-01 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC complete original article HTML and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The case series describes three advanced Parkinson patients on intestinal levodopa-carbidopa gel with dyskinesia and high body temperature during a heatwave. Acute mental-state changes and environmental triggers are discussed. There is no fig exposure or clinical hallucination frequency comparison.
