Title: Delusional Infestation in Parkinson's Disease

DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12352
Publication date: 2016-03-31
Date basis: Electronic date in PMC. The Movement Disorders Clinical Practice issue is January–February 2017.
Source depth: Complete PMC HTML main article, including the five case narratives and modality table
Format: Original-source factual extract

Davis and colleagues described five people selected because they had both Parkinson disease and delusional infestation. The case table codes visual and tactile experiences in all five, sometimes with another sensory modality. These are descriptions of selected cases. The table reports the duration of the infestation syndrome in months, without measuring the frequency of visual episodes.

The reported resolution followed changes in antiparkinsonian or psychiatric therapy. Some cases had several changes and the paper details different possible contributors. The series has no untreated comparison, assigned food or measured dried-fig intake.
