Title: Influence of cultivation sites on sterol, nitrate, total phenolic contents and antioxidant activity in endive and stem chicory edible products.
PMID: 27575665
DOI: 10.1080/09637486.2016.1221386
First published: 2017
Authors: D'Acunzo F, Giannino D, Longo V, Ciardi M, Testone G, Mele G, Nicolodi C, Gonnella M
Journal: International journal of food sciences and nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27575665/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract Facts: Compositional comparison of smooth and curly endive (Cichorium endivia) leaves vs stem chicory across two Italian regions: sterols 106.3-176.0 mg/kg FW, total phenolics 30.8-76.1 mg GAE/100 g FW, and nitrate consistently below hazardous thresholds (also corroborated by Conversa 2021 PMC8117335). Analytical composition only; no human blood-pressure or orthostatic lightheadedness clinical testing.
