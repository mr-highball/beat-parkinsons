Title: Burnout: evaluation of the efficacy and tolerability of TARGET 1® for professional fatigue syndrome (burnout).
PMID: 25537278
DOI: 10.1177/0300060514558324
First published: 2015
Authors: Jacquet A, Grolleau A, Jove J, Lassalle R, Moore N
Journal: The Journal of international medical research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25537278/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in n=87 workers with professional burnout syndrome (44 active, 43 placebo) evaluating TARGET 1, a multi-ingredient supplement combining casozepine, taurine, Eleutherococcus senticosus, and Extramel melon SOD. Active formulation improved BMS-10, MBI-HSS, and BDI vs placebo, but multi-ingredient confounding prevents isolating melon/cantaloupe and no validated anxiety or PD apathy endpoint was isolated.
