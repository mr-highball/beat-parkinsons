Title: Evaluation of Antioxidant, Lipid Peroxidation And Toxic Effects After Apple Intake In Healthy Human Volunteers
Authors: P. Faizal, R. Manikkumar and K. T. Augusti
Reference: Indian Journal of Clinical Biochemistry 32 Supplement 1 (2017), original conference abstract P140, pages S190–S191.
Text coverage: Complete original author abstract, paraphrased.
Accessed: 2026-11-17

The report follows 25 healthy volunteers aged 18–65 before fresh
apple intake and at 45 and 90 days. The study reports 200 g per day.
Its three group labels refer to measurement times in a before-and-after
study. The abstract does not describe
a parallel untreated group. It reports higher enzyme and non-enzyme
antioxidant measures and lower oxidative stress after intake. The
authors report no observed liver or kidney laboratory toxicity.
The abstract provides no numeric effect sizes or interval estimates.
It does not study Parkinson disease or report resting tremor.

The original conference volume contains unrelated studies. P140
starts on S190 and ends on S191 before the orange study P141.
Original collection and page context: https://doi.org/10.1007/s12291-017-0733-7
