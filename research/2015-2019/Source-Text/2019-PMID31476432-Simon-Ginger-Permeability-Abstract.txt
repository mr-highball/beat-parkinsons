Title: Blood-brain barrier permeability study of ginger constituents
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31476432/
PMID: 31476432
DOI: 10.1016/j.jpba.2019.112820
First public: 2019-08-19
Coverage: Factual extract of author abstract and original bibliography. Journal issue is January 2020.

Simon and colleagues tested seven isolated ginger compounds in a laboratory artificial membrane assay. The assay identified passive permeability for 6-gingerol, 8-gingerol and 6-shogaol. It did not measure human or animal anxiety, actual concentrations in the human brain or eating grated ginger. The authors cite earlier studies across distinct endpoints, including Vishwakarma's ginger extract behavioral work and a separate isolated shogaol Parkinson model. The Semmelweis public record lists the paper; its full PDF and original assay tables remain inaccessible. A public 436-page search compilation includes the abstract and is not the original article.
