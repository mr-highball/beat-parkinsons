Gočmanac-Ignjatović et al., 2015. Spasmolytic Effect of Anethum graveolens L. Methanol Extract on Isolated Rat Ileum.
Source: https://publisher.medfak.ni.ac.rs/AMM_1/amm-stari/2015-html/2015%202-broj/Celi%20radovi%20pdf%202015-2/Marija%20Gocmanac.pdf
Access and coverage: Publisher PDF, methods and results, Acta Medica Medianae 54(2), pages 5–10.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

In isolated rat ileum in an organ bath, a methanol extract of grocery dill's dried aerial parts inhibited spontaneous and acetylcholine-induced contractions. This is an assay of gastrointestinal smooth muscle, without oral exposure, central motor function or a neurological dystonia assessment.
