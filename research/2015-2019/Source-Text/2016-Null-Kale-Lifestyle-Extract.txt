Title: Nutrition and Lifestyle Intervention on Mood and Neurological Disorders
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5871207/
Publisher: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/2156587216637539
PMID: 26976087
PMCID: PMC5871207
DOI: 10.1177/2156587216637539
First published: 2016-03-14
Coverage: Distilled from the original full article's Methods, diet and juicing instructions, edited testimonials and Results. Print issue January 2017.

The authors describe a three-month lifestyle program in which 27 people enrolled and 21 videotaped testimonials were collected. The reported conditions included mood disorders, cognitive decline, stroke, Parkinson disease and multiple sclerosis. A clinician supervised the overall program. Nutritional changes, juice, supplements, exercise, stress management and environmental changes were carried out in the same program. There is no concurrent comparison group or ingredient-specific effect estimate.

The allowed diet lists millet among various grains, extra-virgin olive oil among several permitted oils, coconut oil among permitted oils and in a protein smoothie, and basil, rosemary, oregano, thyme, cinnamon and spearmint among optional herbs and spices. Parsley, kale and celery are named in the mixed juice guidance. The instructions do not establish individual consumption, an isolated dose or preparation for the evaluated ingredient hypotheses. Fresh sage is not named in that diet and juice list.

One caregiver described preparing juice with kale and perceived more fluent speech for a person with dementia and previous minor strokes; there was no measured speech intelligibility, roasted kale, or established Parkinson disease in this report. In the Parkinson testimonials one participant described improvements in memory and cognition and reduced medication use, and another reported better walking and sleep with less nighttime screaming. Neither account establishes individual kale, celery or spearmint exposure, measured walking speed, time spent in motor OFF, sleep stage or REM sleep behavior disorder frequency. The mood testimonials and isolated descriptions of energy or pain likewise apply to the combined program. All observations are from the same paper.
