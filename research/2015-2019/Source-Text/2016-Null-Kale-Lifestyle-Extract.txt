The original Methods on pp. 69–70 describe 27 entrants and 21 completers in a three-month combined lifestyle program, without a comparison group. Kale was listed among optional green juice ingredients. In a case account on pp. 71–72, a daughter reported kale-containing juices and more fluent speech in her mother, who had dementia and previous minor strokes. There was no measured intelligibility, no roasted preparation and no documented Parkinson diagnosis in this account. Another participant with Parkinson disease reported improved sleep and the end of nighttime screaming after twelve weeks. There was no documentation of this participant's kale intake, steamed preparation, measured event frequency, dream relationship or sleep stage. The programs had multiple elements and no ingredient-specific comparison. The two accounts belong to one publication.
