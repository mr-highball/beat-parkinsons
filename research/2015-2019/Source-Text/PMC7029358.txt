Title: Family History is Associated with Phenotype in Dementia with Lewy Bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7029358/
PMID: 31771063
PMCID: PMC7029358
DOI: 10.3233/JAD-190825
Publication date: 2019-11-19
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: The journal publisher gives this first online date. The version was included in a 2020 issue.
Design: Retrospective family-history and phenotype comparison

The investigators compared 154 familial and 137 sporadic dementia-with-Lewy-bodies patients. CSF Alzheimer biomarker status differed between groups, and the study assessed first symptoms, cognitive trajectories and family history. Visual hallucination is part of the clinical diagnosis; there is no food-specific contrast.
