Venkat et al. original public health outbreak investigation
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6309180/

The 2018 publication describes a 2016 Salmonella outbreak. A restaurant case-control analysis included 21 confirmed cases and 31 well dining companions. The reported association for unfried cooked shrimp and gastrointestinal illness was OR 6.7 (95% CI 1.8 to 24.9). The identified preparation category includes boiled and sautéed examples. Samples from raw shrimp, halibut and a freezer handle shared the outbreak strain, so the investigation considered multiple sources and cross-contamination. Visual hallucinations and Parkinson status were outside the reported outcome assessment.
