Title: No Effect of Tart Cherry Juice or Pomegranate Juice on Recovery from Exercise-Induced Muscle Damage in Non-Resistance Trained Men.
PMID: 31337122
PMCID: PMC6683053
DOI: 10.3390/nu11071593
First published: 2019
Authors: Lamb KL, Ranchordas MK, Johnson E, Denning J, Downing F, Lynn A
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6683053/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, parallel placebo-controlled trial (Lamb et al., 2019, Nutrients, PMC6683053) in 36 non-resistance-trained men consuming 2x250 mL/day tart cherry juice, pomegranate juice, or placebo for 9 days around eccentric elbow flexor exercise. Neither tart cherry nor pomegranate juice differed from placebo on DOMS VAS, maximal isometric voluntary contraction, creatine kinase, or range of motion over 96 h (group and group-by-time all p>0.05). Competing null soreness trial.
