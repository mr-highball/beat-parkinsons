Title: Pimavanserin for Psychosis in Parkinson's Disease-Related Disorders: A Retrospective Chart Review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6584229/
PMID: 30924099
PMCID: PMC6584229
DOI: 10.1007/s40266-019-00655-y
Publication date: 2019-03-29
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication in original PMC metadata; PubMed lists the issue chronology.
Design: Retrospective pimavanserin chart review

Of 107 patients prescribed pimavanserin for Parkinson-related psychosis, 91 began treatment. The charts recorded clinical psychosis improvement for 69 of 91. The endpoint combines forms of psychosis, including delusions and different hallucination modalities. The uncontrolled drug review supplies no estimate for the isolated visual episode rate after food.
