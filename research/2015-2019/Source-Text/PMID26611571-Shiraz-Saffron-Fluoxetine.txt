Sahraian adjunct report and public abstract
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26611571/
PMID: 26611571
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajp.2015.10.012
Registration: IRCT2013110915334N1
Coverage: Original author abstract and publication metadata; full body access pending

Sahraian and colleagues report a four-week randomized comparison in major depression with 40 allocated participants and fluoxetine shared across groups. The author abstract reports 19 of 20 saffron-arm and 11 of 20 placebo-arm completers and an intergroup Beck Depression Inventory p = .560. It was published online November 10, 2015 and appeared in the 2016 issue. The original full body remained unavailable through the checked publisher, library and author-hosting routes. The full 2018 companion report gives different participant accounting and an intergroup p = .056. Both reports use the same trial registration.
