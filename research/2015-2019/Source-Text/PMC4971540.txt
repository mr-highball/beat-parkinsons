Title: Amyloid-Beta Positron Emission Tomography Imaging of Alzheimer's Pathology in Parkinson's Disease Dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4971540/
PMCID: PMC4971540
PMID: 27500181
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12290
Publication date: 2016-01-05
Date basis: NLM and PMC electronic date
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The autopsy-correlated PET case series reports five people with Parkinson disease dementia. Four had a hallucination item marked present at imaging, and individual histories sometimes distinguish visual symptoms from sleep phenomena. Amyloid imaging and tissue measures do not test a food or clinical episode-frequency contrast.
