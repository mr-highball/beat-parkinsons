Title: Fact‐finding survey on diagnostic procedures and therapeutic interventions for parkinsonism accompanying dementia with Lewy bodies

PMID: 30784148
PMCID: PMC6850325
DOI: 10.1111/psyg.12408
Publication date: 2019-02-19
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC and PubMed metadata.
Design: Survey of clinician practice in dementia with Lewy bodies

One hundred neurologists and 100 psychiatrists were surveyed about evaluation and treatment. Possible aggravation of visual hallucinations was frequently given as a reason to avoid increasing levodopa. The response measures reported clinical practice and concern rather than monitored visual events following food.
