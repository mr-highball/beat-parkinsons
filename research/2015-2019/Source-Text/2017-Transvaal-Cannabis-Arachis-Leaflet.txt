Transvaal Apotheek patient leaflet: Bedica THC 2%, Bedrocan THC 2%, Bediol CBD 2% / THC 1.3%, Bedrolite CBD 2% and 10% oils, for sublingual use. Authorisation date 11-12-2017, draft 05-09-2017; original https://www.transvaalapotheek.nl/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/Patient-leaflet-Cannabis-oil-1.pdf . It states its inactive ingredient is refined arachis oil. The THC-containing product side-effect section discusses possible hallucinations in association with exacerbation of psychiatric disorders by cannabis. It supplies neither an actual case nor a frequency, experimental peanut-oil comparison, or Parkinson-specific result. The oil is refined; cold pressing is not established.
