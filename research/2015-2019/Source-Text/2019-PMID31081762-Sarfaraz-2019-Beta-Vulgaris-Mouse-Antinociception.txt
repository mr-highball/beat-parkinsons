Title: Anti-nociceptive potential of lyophilized Beta vulgaris L. (Beet root) powder.
PMID: 31081762
First published: 2019
Authors: Sarfaraz S, Ikram R
Journal: Pakistan journal of pharmaceutical sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31081762/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse study evaluating oral Beta vulgaris extract in hot-plate, tail-flick, and acetic-acid writhing assays: significantly reduced nociceptive responses compared with vehicle (p<0.001). Limits: acute non-PD rodent nociception model.
