Title: Dietary supplements for dysmenorrhoea
PMID: 27000311
PMCID: PMC7387104
DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD002124.pub2
First published: 2016
Authors: Pattanittum P, Kunyanone N, Brown J, Sangkomkamhang US, Barnes J, Seyfoddin V
Journal: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27000311/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Cochrane systematic review and meta-analysis of 27 RCTs (3,101 women) of dietary supplements for dysmenorrhea. Independently extracted raw 0-10 VAS pain scores from the guava leaf extract trial (Doubova 2007): concluded there was no consistent evidence of effectiveness for guava vs placebo (MD +0.59 points, 95% CI -0.13 to 1.31; 1 RCT, 151 women), and guava was inferior to NSAIDs (MD +1.19 points, 95% CI 0.42 to 1.96; 1 RCT, 155 women), with low/very low GRADE certainty.
