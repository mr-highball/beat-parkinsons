Title: Effect of Tribulus terrestris, ginger, saffron, and Cinnamomum on menopausal symptoms: a randomised, placebo-controlled clinical trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5437055/
PMID: 28546803
PMCID: PMC5437055
DOI: 10.5114/pm.2017.67366
First online: 2017-04-26
Print issue: 2017-03
Coverage: Distilled original Methods and Results. Cited as reference 16 in the later affron trial report.

The active capsules contained ginger and cinnamon with saffron extract and Tribulus terrestris; control capsules contained starch. After four weeks the total Menopause Rating Scale mean was 13.11 in the combination arm versus 22.13 with placebo, from baseline means of 21.93 and 22.13, respectively. The authors report improvements for physical and mental components. These are multicomponent results in postmenopausal women. The report does not provide an attributable cinnamon or ginger effect on measured anxiety in Parkinson disease.
