# Isolated thymoquinone and chlorpromazine motor outcomes

Source https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27180446/
Coverage original author abstract

The March 2016 original abstract describes four rat groups of eight and reports less catalepsy with oral isolated thymoquinone than with chlorpromazine alone. Coordination and spontaneous movement were also reported as improved. The accessible abstract gives no numerical trajectory for clinical bradykinesia or ground seed intake. Drug-induced motor responses and isolated compound remain an indirect comparator.
