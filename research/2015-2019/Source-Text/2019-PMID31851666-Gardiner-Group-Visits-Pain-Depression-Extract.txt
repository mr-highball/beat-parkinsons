Title: Effectiveness of integrative medicine group visits in chronic pain and depressive symptoms: A randomized controlled trial
PMID: 31851666
PMCID: PMC6919581
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0225540
Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0225540
Published: 2019-12-18.
Text coverage: Original Methods, Results, Figure 1 and Table 5.

The report explicitly names NCT02262377. The Results and registry report 159 randomized, with 155 in baseline tables and the described intention-to-treat set after four withdrew consent. The Methods use 155 as the randomized count. The group-visit program combined in-person visits and electronic self-management support, with a primary care comparator outside a Parkinson cohort.

At week 9, the reported group-visit versus control ratios are 1.03 (95% CI 0.92–1.15) for average pain and 1.09 (0.92–1.28) for PHQ-9. At week 21, the corresponding ratios are 0.98 (0.88–1.08) and 0.89 (0.75–1.06). These are whole-program, inconclusive comparisons. Higher attendance showed exploratory associations with outcomes. This contrast does not isolate a food. The 2026 Park dietary abstract appears to use the same cohort.
