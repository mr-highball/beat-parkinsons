Text coverage: selected bilingual fields paraphrased from the public search index of the official registry, which displayed the linked record. Direct page requests returned 405.

The record lists Junjie Hu as applicant and Tao Wang as study leader. People with idiopathic Parkinson's disease and mild cognitive impairment could enter with or without deep brain stimulation under defined MoCA criteria. The non-surgical group was required to have stable dopaminergic treatment. The design plans 140 participants per group. Active and placebo groups receive two capsules three times daily for 48 weeks. The planned primary outcomes are MMSE and MoCA. MDS-UPDRS Parts III and IV, SCOPA-Cog, and PDQ-39 are among the registered secondary measures. Part IV covers motor complications including dyskinesia and motor fluctuations. This registry extract gives no item-level dyskinesia results and describes a pharmaceutical exposure without celery leaf. A 2024 report with matching design features and senior investigator, MDS congress abstract 764, describes cognition outcomes and overall adverse events. The abstract does not supply the accession or Part IV results.
