Title: Amaranthus caudatus extract inhibits the invasion of E. coli into uroepithelial cells.
PMID: 29621584
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2018.04.003
First published: 2018
Authors: Mohanty S, Zambrana S, Dieulouard S, Kamolvit W, Nilsén V, Gonzales E, et al.
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29621584/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

In vitro cellular study (Mohanty et al., 2018, J Ethnopharmacol) testing Amaranthus caudatus extract on human bladder epithelial cell lines (T24 and 5637) exposed to uropathogenic E. coli. Extract had no direct bactericidal effect but reduced uroplakin-1a and caveolin-1 mRNA and decreased E. coli adhesion and invasion. Limits: in vitro UTI infection model; does not test cooked amaranth grain or Parkinson's urinary urgency frequency.
