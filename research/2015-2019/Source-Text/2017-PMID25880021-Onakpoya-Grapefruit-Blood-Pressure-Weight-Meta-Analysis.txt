Title: The effect of grapefruits (Citrus paradisi) on body weight and cardiovascular risk factors: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials.
PMID: 25880021
DOI: 10.1080/10408398.2014.901292
First published: 2017
Authors: Onakpoya I, O'Sullivan J, Heneghan C, Thompson M
Journal: Critical reviews in food science and nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25880021/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 3 human RCTs (n=250 overweight/obese adults, including Dow 2012 whole fresh grapefruit PMID:22304836 and Silver 2011 PMID:21288350). Weight change was non-significant (MD -0.45 kg, 95% CI -1.06 to 0.16), whereas systolic blood pressure declined significantly (MD -2.43 mmHg, 95% CI -4.77 to -0.09, I2=0%). Demonstrates modest intrinsic SBP-lowering effect of grapefruit even without interacting drugs, though without direct orthostatic symptom measurement.
