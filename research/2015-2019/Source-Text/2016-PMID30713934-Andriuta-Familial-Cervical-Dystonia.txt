Title: Huntington's Disease Revealed by Familial Cervical Dystonia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6353476/
PMID: 30713934
PMCID: PMC6353476
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12311
Citation: Andriuta D, Tir M, Godefroy O, Krystkowiak P. Movement Disorders Clinical Practice. First published January 18, 2016. 3:415–416.
Coverage: PMC original full main text, two clinical narratives and supporting video captions. Distilled factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

Two relatives presented with predominant cervical dystonia and had pathogenic HTT expansions confirming Huntington disease. This report supplies a differential-diagnosis example involving dystonia. It did not test fresh lemon balm, assign food exposure or track Parkinson dystonia.
