Title: Phenoconversion from Idiopathic Rapid Eye Movement Sleep Behavior Disorder to Lewy Body Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6207131/
PMID: 30515439
PMCID: PMC6207131
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12647
Publication date: 2018-10-03
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML article body; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC metadata and PubMed.
Design: Longitudinal clinical cohort of REM sleep behavior disorder

Investigators followed 273 people with confirmed idiopathic REM sleep behavior disorder across two clinical cohorts. Converting to a defined neurodegenerative syndrome is the principal outcome. The study does not compare visual episode frequency after dried fig intake.
