Title: Comparison of Adaptive Neuroprotective Mechanisms of Sulforaphane and its Interconversion Product Erucin in in Vitro and in Vivo Models of Parkinson's Disease.
PMID: 29307179
DOI: 10.1021/acs.jafc.7b04641
First published: 2018
Authors: Morroni F, Sita G, Djemil A, D'Amico M, Pruccoli L, Cantelli-Forti G, et al.
Journal: Journal of agricultural and food chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29307179/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical Parkinson's model study (Morroni et al., 2018, J Agric Food Chem) comparing purified sulforaphane and its interconversion product erucin (derived from glucoerucin in arugula and other brassicas) in 6-OHDA-treated SH-SY5Y cells (5 uM) and an in vivo 6-OHDA mouse model. Both compounds activated Nrf2/glutathione defenses and attenuated 6-OHDA neurotoxicity in vivo via metabolic interconversion between erucin and sulforaphane. Limits: purified isolated isothiocyanate administered parenterally in a toxin-lesion rodent model rather than raw whole arugula leaves; neuroprotective biochemical/lesion outcomes do not isolate individual human PD motor or non-motor symptom severity.
