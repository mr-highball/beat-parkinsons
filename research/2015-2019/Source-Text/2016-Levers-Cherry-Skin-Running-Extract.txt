Levers et al., May 26, 2016. PMID 27231439; PMC4880859; DOI 10.1186/s12970-016-0133-z. Separate 27-runner parallel trial of freeze-dried Montmorency cherry skin capsules versus placebo. Race time was reported as 13% faster with cherry (p=.001); predicted-versus-actual pace contrast p=.091. No clear postrace soreness difference; prerun-to-recovery change at one site was smaller with placebo. Earlier P33 and P34 abstracts strongly match protocol and cohort. No Parkinson or perceived-fatigue assessment. Supplier support declared.
