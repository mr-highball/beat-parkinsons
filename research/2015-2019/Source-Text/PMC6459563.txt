The official PMC HTML contains 216 numbered titles and also carries withdrawal notices. Member 27 measures patient and caregiver knowledge. Member 222 describes a six-person sham comparison in dementia with Lewy bodies and separately cites the earlier Parkinson dementia stimulation trial. Member 225 reports one new visual hallucination after subthalamic surgery among 36 Parkinson participants. Members 22 and 23 concern pantothenate-kinase-associated neurodegeneration. These outcomes use no dried fig ingestion comparison.

Verified members

Member 22. Familial dystonia and suspected pantothenate kinase-associated neurodegeneration
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6459563/#mdc312744-sec-0069
Population or exposure: Two familial dystonia cases with the characteristic MRI sign and no genetic confirmation
Outcome: Neurological exam and MRI
Scope: Pantothenate kinase names a suspected diagnosis. There was no dietary B5 exposure.

Member 23. Dystonic storm after deep brain stimulation battery depletion
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6459563/#mdc312744-sec-0072
Population or exposure: Loss of stimulation during hardware depletion in a PKAN case
Outcome: Dystonic storm following battery depletion and subsequent improvement
Scope: The timing concerns stimulation hardware and dystonia. There is no edible pantothenate or dried fig comparison.

Member 27. Knowledge and Perception of Parkinson's disease among Patients and Caregivers
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6459563/#mdc312744-sec-0083
Population or exposure: Questionnaire development and preliminary responses from 32 patients and 28 caregivers
Outcome: Recognition of visual hallucinations, 40.6% and 39.3%, respectively
Scope: These percentages describe recognition of a possible Parkinson symptom. They do not count personal hallucination events.

Member 222. Bilateral nucleus basalis of Meynert deep brain stimulation for dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6459563/#mdc312744-sec-0667
Population or exposure: Six patients in a randomized double-blind active versus sham crossover
Outcome: No consistent primary cognitive improvement and a trend in total neuropsychiatric outcomes
Scope: Matches the Brain Stimulation report of NCT02263937. The earlier Parkinson disease dementia trial was cited as background and used a separate clinical sample.

Member 225. Psychiatric and cognitive effects of STN-DBS in PD
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6459563/#mdc312744-sec-0677
Population or exposure: Prospective pre-post evaluation in 36 Parkinson patients undergoing bilateral subthalamic stimulation
Outcome: One participant developed a visual hallucination following surgery
Scope: Surgical timeline and occurrence. The member gives no repeated visual episode count, exposure control or dried fig comparison.

Drooling and oral outcomes

The original body and the separate table of contents contain the same 237 numbered entries, including 21 notices of withdrawal. There are 216 available abstracts. Three numbered entries included a saliva-related term in the source check.

Member 70. The early-morning off study reports aggregate quality-of-life and nonmotor scores. Its saliva mention is a citation to a 2017 quality-of-life article rather than an item result in the abstract. Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6459563/#mdc312744-sec-0216
Member 125. The SNCA study reports a longitudinal dribbling item and genotype associations. Table 3 gives 48 (28.6%) at baseline and 72 (47.1%) at follow-up. Those percentages and the methods' 136 participants described as followed imply different or inconsistent analysis denominators. The reported odds ratios are genetic associations with a symptom item. This study has no ingredient or dietary intervention. Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6459563/#mdc312744-sec-0378
Member 162. The 28-person Wenzhou study reports more severe salivation in the postural-instability/gait-difficulty group than the tremor-dominant group (p=.002). The abstract gives no item magnitude, no raw-fig exposure, and no proof of which participants overlap the later 101-person article. Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6459563/#mdc312744-sec-0492
