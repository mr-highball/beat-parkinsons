Title: Celery root extract as an inducer of mania induction in a patient on venlafaxine and St John's Wort
Source: https://doi.org/10.1080/00325481.2016.1218263
PMID: 27467225
DOI: 10.1080/00325481.2016.1218263
Publication history: Accepted author version posted 2016-07-27 and final version online 2016-08-05.
Coverage: Factual extract of the original publisher abstract and identified source lineage.

The original abstract describes mania with elevated venlafaxine after initiating a celery-root extract. The patient had been stable on venlafaxine and St John's wort for depression. The authors proposed an interaction. That abstract supplies no verified hallucination subtype, frequency, onset interval or complete product details.
The later review PMID 34370637 describes oral root capsules, mania and hallucinations within 48 hours in the same patient. Its hallucination modality is unspecified. A nearby visual-hallucination description in that review concerns a separate ginseng and phenelzine case.
The primary full case and public accepted manuscript were unavailable through the checked routes. The report concerns a root extract and concurrent medications.
