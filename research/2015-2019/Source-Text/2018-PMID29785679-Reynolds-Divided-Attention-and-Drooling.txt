Title: Drooling in Parkinson's Disease: Evidence of a Role for Divided Attention
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00455-018-9906-7
PMID: 29785679
DOI: 10.1007/s00455-018-9906-7
Publication date: 2018-05-21
Coverage: Paraphrased original author abstract with the official registry and conference companion verified. The publisher publicly exposes the abstract and references.

Eighteen participants with idiopathic Parkinson disease and self-reported daytime drooling were observed at rest and during a computer language task. Within-person comparisons yielded fewer swallows (p=.002) and more episodes of drooling (p=.041) under distraction. The drooling severity comparison was inconclusive (p=.085). These are three distinct outcomes. The exposures are task and rest conditions without a dietary comparison. ISRCTN11045864 reports a target of 20 and links to the journal results. The MDS 2017 abstract 111 reports the same sample and test statistics.
