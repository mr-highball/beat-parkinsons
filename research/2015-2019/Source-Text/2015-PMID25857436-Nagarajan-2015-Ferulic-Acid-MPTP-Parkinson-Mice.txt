Title: Ferulic acid pretreatment mitigates MPTP-induced motor impairment and histopathological alterations in C57BL/6 mice.
PMID: 25857436
DOI: 10.3109/13880209.2014.993041
First published: 2015
Authors: Nagarajan S, Chellappan DR, Chinnaswamy P, Thulasingam S
Journal: Pharmaceutical biology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25857436/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Seven-day MPTP C57BL/6 mouse study showing ferulic acid (40 mg/kg/d, a phenolic acid present in brown rice, whole wheat, and oats) attenuated MPTP-induced dopaminergic neuronal loss, microglial activation, and motor balance/coordination impairments. Ubiquitous cereal compound in mouse toxin model; does not isolate cooked brown rice.
