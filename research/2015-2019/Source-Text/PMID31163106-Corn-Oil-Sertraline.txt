Corn oil placebo in the later sertraline trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6550340/
PMID: 31163106
PMCID: PMC6550340
DOI: 10.4088/JCP.19m12742
Registration: NCT02021669
Coverage: Full original June 4, 2019, Methods and Tables 1–3; matched official registry

The trial randomized 144 adults with major depression and coronary disease or risk factors. The 73 corn and 71 EPA assignees received 2 g/day assigned oil with sertraline 50 mg/day for ten weeks. Unadjusted Beck means changed 29.1 to 9.1 for corn and 29.9 to 11.0 for EPA. The intention-to-treat adjusted means were 10.3 versus 12.1, modeled EPA minus corn difference 1.74 (95% CI −1.04 to 4.52, p=.22). Adjusted Hamilton means 7.2 versus 8.0, difference 0.81 (−1.11 to 2.73, p=.40). A separate earlier Carney trial used an EPA/DHA formulation. The assigned comparison gives no oil-free or independently identified refining contrast.
