Title: Effect of olive oil consumption on aging in a senescence-accelerated mice-prone 8 (SAMP8) model

Reviewed: 2026-11-27

In accelerated-aging and resistant mouse strains, the control diet contains 70 g/kg soybean oil. The intervention diet replaces 10 g/kg of it with olive oil and retains 60 g/kg soybean oil, with the same reported ratio of energy from fat. The original describes a 24-week feeding period and passive-avoidance testing at eight weeks. The article does not report the soybean oil refining grade.

The between-diet comparison of SAMP8 mice shows a lower triglyceride value in the olive-oil substitution group and no clear passive-avoidance difference. The study separately reports between-strain comparisons on the control diet. It does not isolate an effect of soybean oil against a soybean-free diet, use levodopa, or measure daily Parkinson OFF time. Round 1 claim 0391 receives preparation and comparator context only.
