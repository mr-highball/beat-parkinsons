Title: Characteristics of facial expression recognition ability in patients with Lewy body disease

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC metadata and PubMed.
Design: Visual facial expression recognition comparison

The clinical report evaluated recognition of six facial expressions in 107 older patients with Lewy body disease. It examines perceptual task accuracy and describes visual features in the cohort. Task accuracy differs from a timed hallucination count following food intake.
