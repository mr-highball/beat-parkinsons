Hydroalcoholic curry leaf extract and slower rat transit
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26631977/
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

First available December 2, 2015. This rat study compared oral hydroalcoholic curry-leaf extract against vehicle and an injected atropine control. The authors report reduced stool frequency, greater stool consistency and slower small-intestinal transit. The original Methods describe collecting and grinding leaves, and the extraction sentence calls the powder bark. The treatment groups and Results identify it as leaf extract. The plant-part wording remains unresolved. Figure 3 measures charcoal progression after a castor-oil challenge, a different model from the earlier mouse tests. No gastric emptying, oral levodopa concentrations or Parkinson OFF-time measure was collected.
