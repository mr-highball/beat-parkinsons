Original source scope
Isolated curcumin reduced the cisplatin-associated prolongation of response latency at week five in a rat hot-plate assay. The change restored heat sensitivity toward controls. Other endpoints concerned nerve conduction and dorsal-root-ganglion morphology.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The measured phenomenon was protection against thermal hypoalgesia. This is not a measure of clinical pain relief. There was no ground turmeric or Parkinson population.
