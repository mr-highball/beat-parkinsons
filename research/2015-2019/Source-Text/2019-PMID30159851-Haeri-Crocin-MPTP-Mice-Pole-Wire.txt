Study design and findings
- 24 male BALB/c mice randomized into 4 groups (n = 6/group): MPTP (30 mg/kg x 5 days), MPTP + oral gavage crocin (30 mg/kg x 15 days), saline i.p. control, and saline i.p. + saline gavage control.
- Behavioral motor outcomes: MPTP impaired pole-test fall latency and wire-hanging duration (p < 0.001); oral crocin significantly improved pole-test performance (p < 0.05) and wire-hanging duration (p < 0.001) compared with MPTP alone.
- Histology: Crocin preserved stereologically counted substantia nigra TH-positive neurons and reduced dark neurons relative to MPTP (p < 0.001).

Relevance and limits for Parkinson's food experiments
- Additional controlled MPTP motor study of the saffron constituent crocin supporting preclinical saffron motor/bradykinesia context.
- Limits: Isolated crocin rather than whole culinary saffron; MPTP mouse model (n = 6/group); abstract-level depth (full text paywalled).
