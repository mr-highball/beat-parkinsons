Original source scope
The abstract reports 24 male BALB/c mice randomized across four groups. Isolated crocin shortened time to fall from the pole (p < 0.05) and lengthened wire-hanging time (p < 0.001) versus the model-only group.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The reported motor tasks measure time on apparatus. The abstract contains neither a natural fall count nor a ground saffron comparison. Obtain the journal article, volume 94, issue 1, pages 119–127, to inspect the test definitions and the full outcome tables.

Related motor evidence
The archived preliminary summary recorded both tasks as motor improvement. The original abstract specifies the opposite signs for the two task latencies. The investigators also report more tyrosine hydroxylase-positive neurons and fewer dark neurons with crocin than in the model-only group (p < 0.001). These observations have no fall-frequency estimate.
