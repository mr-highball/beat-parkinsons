Title: Divergent brain functional network alterations in dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer's disease

Evidence type: Primary clinical imaging study
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Resting functional MRI contrasts brain networks in dementia with Lewy bodies, Alzheimer disease and controls. Clinical characterization recorded hallucination-related features in the Lewy body group. Connectivity differences are diagnostic and mechanistic measures. No assessed dietary factor or Parkinson visual episode rate is reported.
