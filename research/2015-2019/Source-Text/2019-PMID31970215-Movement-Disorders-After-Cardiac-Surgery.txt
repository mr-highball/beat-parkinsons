Title: Post-Pump Chorea and Progressive Supranuclear Palsy-Like Syndrome Following Major Cardiac Surgery.
PMID: 31970215
PMCID: PMC6962669
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12867
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6962669/
First publication date: 2019-12-11 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC complete original article HTML and original clinical or laboratory methods.

Three cases describe chorea or a progressive supranuclear palsy-like syndrome following cardiac surgery and circulatory arrest. The clinical time course documents serious postoperative changes. There is no fruit intervention or assessment of Parkinson hallucination frequency under food.
