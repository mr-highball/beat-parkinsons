Title: White Grape Juice Elicits a Lower Breath Hydrogen Response Compared with Apple Juice in Healthy Human Subjects: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 28342683
DOI: 10.1016/j.jand.2017.01.020
First published: 2017
Authors: Erickson J, Wang Q, Slavin J
Journal: Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28342683/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover trial in 40 healthy adults consuming 12 oz white grape juice vs apple juice. White grape juice elicited significantly lower breath hydrogen AUC (5.8 vs 23.3 ppm/h, p<0.001) with no between-arm GI symptom differences. Low-FODMAP tolerance study, not constipation.
