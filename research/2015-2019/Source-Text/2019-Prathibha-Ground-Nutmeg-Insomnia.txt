Ground nutmeg in milk and one primary insomnia case
Source: https://doi.org/10.7897/2277-4343.100119
Coverage: factual extract of primary case and Table 1
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Prathibha and colleagues described a woman age 54 with longstanding primary insomnia who took a preparation of 2 g powdered Myristica fragrans boiled in milk and water nightly for 14 days. Regensburg Insomnia Scale scores in Table 1 were 21 at baseline, 18 on day 8, 10 on day 15 and 9 after a month. Daytime drowsiness was reported after one week. There was no comparator and no Parkinson diagnosis. The design cannot separate the powder from the milk preparation, natural variation or expectancy.
