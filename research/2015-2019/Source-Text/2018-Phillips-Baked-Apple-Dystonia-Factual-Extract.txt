Title: Factual extraction from Phillips et al., Low-fat versus ketogenic diet in Parkinson's disease, and the low-fat meal plan.
Trial: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175383/
Supplement: https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC6175383.1/MDS-33-1306-s002.pdf
MDS-UPDRS score sheet: https://mds.movementdisorders.org/UserFiles/New%20UPDRS%207%203%2008%20final.pdf
Publication: 2018. PMID 30098269. PMC6175383. DOI 10.1002/mds.27390.
Design: 47 randomized; 44 started; 38 completed. Eight-week comparison of low-fat and ketogenic dietary plans.
Exposure: Printed supplement pages 30, 34 and 35 schedule apple crisp. Page 69 specifies sliced apples cooked by baking as part of a mixed dish. Individual intake was not reported. Discussion says food logs were not used.
Outcome: Table 2 mean MDS-UPDRS Part IV change was -0.79 (SD 2.71) for low-fat and -1.56 (SD 2.45) for ketogenic, between-arm P=0.32. The official scale includes painful OFF-state dystonia as item 4.6. The trial does not present separate item 4.6 data or an apple-specific comparison.
Adverse effects: The reported worsening of dyskinesia in one ketogenic participant was a separate endpoint. The report does not identify baked apple exposure for that participant.
