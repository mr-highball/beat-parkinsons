Coverage: Paraphrased original review and verification of the cited dermatitis source.

The review identifies perioral dermatitis associated with severe saliva loss. Its clinical example cites the earlier Bloem report, PMID 19412723. This is a cited complication of drooling. The review's dermatology, sweating and biopsy sections do not supply a calculated saliva score or a food effect. The historical autonomic citation by Aminoff and Wilcox measures separate physiological systems.
