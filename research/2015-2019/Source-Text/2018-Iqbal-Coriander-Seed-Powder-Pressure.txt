2018 — Two coriander seed powder blood-pressure reports
Source: https://juniperpublishers.com/artoaj/pdf/ARTOAJ.MS.ID.555971.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of Methods and Table 2.

In 32 people with type 2 diabetes and hypertension, coriander seed-powder mean systolic pressure was 175.63 at baseline and 156.88 mm Hg at 40 days. Placebo was 163.75 at both times. The authors describe within-arm changes and report no significant active-versus-control comparison. Standing outcomes were unreported.
