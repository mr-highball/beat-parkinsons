Predicting mixed-meal measured glycaemic index in healthy subjects
Original factual extract of a published primary study

Ballance S and colleagues. European Journal of Nutrition. Online September 14, 2018.
PMID 30218140
DOI 10.1007/s00394-018-1813-z
Record https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30218140/
Primary paper copy https://potet.nlr.no/files/documents/Fagforum-Potet/Filer/Ballance_et_al-2018-European_Journal_of_Nutrition.pdf

In a randomized non-blind crossover, healthy fasting volunteers consumed dinner-type mixed meals consisting of poached salmon, boiled carrots and herb sauce with a carbohydrate staple. The four staples were mashed potato, pasta, rice or a glucose drink. Comparator periods used the carbohydrates alone. The protocol tracked postprandial glucose and insulin. It did not track anxiety and participants were healthy volunteers. This documents the precise salmon preparation in a human intervention.
