Original study scope
The school survey included 234 students in the analysis. Intake estimates from coffee, green tea and other items were pooled into total caffeine. Analyses used total-caffeine quartiles, with outcomes such as the Insomnia Severity Index. The paper provides genuine coffee and tea exposure context without beverage-specific fatigue estimates or a date-fruit contrast.

Continuing evidence gaps
Isolate a beverage-specific analysis and an independently measured Parkinson fatigue endpoint before assigning an effect.
