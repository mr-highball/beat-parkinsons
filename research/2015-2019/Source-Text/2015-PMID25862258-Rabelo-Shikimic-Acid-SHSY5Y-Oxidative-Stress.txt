Title: In Vitro Neuroprotective Effect of Shikimic Acid Against Hydrogen Peroxide-Induced Oxidative Stress.
PMID: 25862258
DOI: 10.1007/s12031-015-0559-9
First published: 2015
Authors: Rabelo TK, Zeidán-Chuliá F, Caregnato FF, Schnorr CE, Gasparotto J, Serafini MR, de Souza Araújo AA, Quintans-Junior LJ et al.
Journal: Journal of molecular neuroscience : MN
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25862258/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

In vitro cell-culture study (Rabelo et al., 2015, J Mol Neurosci) exposing SH-SY5Y human neuroblastoma cells to H2O2 with isolated shikimic acid (10 nM to 10 mM, originally derived from Illicium verum). Shikimic acid was non-cytotoxic and attenuated H2O2-induced intracellular reactive species and cytotoxicity. Limits: cell-only assay of an isolated compound; no animal or human PD symptom data.
