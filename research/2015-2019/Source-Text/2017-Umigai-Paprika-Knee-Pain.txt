Original abstract summary. Japanese Pharmacology and Therapeutics 45(2):223–233. Thirty-two healthy volunteers with mild knee pain randomized to 20 mg paprika oleoresin or placebo for 12 weeks. Pain VAS and total JKOM improved in both groups without a clear between-group difference. General activities subscale favored oleoresin. Exact pain table and dose formulation details remain inaccessible.
