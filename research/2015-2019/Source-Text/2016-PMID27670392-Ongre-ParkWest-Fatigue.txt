Title: Fatigue in early Parkinson's disease: the Norwegian ParkWest study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27670392/
PMID: 27670392
DOI: 10.1111/ene.13161
Publication date: 2016-09-26
Date basis: First publication online. Print issue followed in 2017.
Accessed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: distilled author abstract and cohort lineage.

The ParkWest fatigue paper reports higher Fatigue Severity Scale scores among people with early Parkinson disease than controls at baseline and one year. Mean patient scores changed from 4.4 to 4.0 over follow-up. The public results connect fatigue with other features and treatment use. The abstract supplies no coffee-stratified effect and gives no way to link the fatigue observations to the exposure measurements reported separately by Skeie et al. The full body and linked participant analysis remain open.
