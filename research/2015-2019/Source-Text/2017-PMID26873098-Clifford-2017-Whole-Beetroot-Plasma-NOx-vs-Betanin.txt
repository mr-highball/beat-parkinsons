Title: The plasma bioavailability of nitrate and betanin from Beta vulgaris rubra in humans.
PMID: 26873098
PMCID: PMC5346430
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-016-1173-5
First published: 2017
Authors: Clifford T, Constantinou CM, Keane KM, West DJ, Howatson G, Stevenson EJ
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26873098/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Human pharmacokinetic study feeding 300 g whole beetroot: significantly elevated plasma nitrate/nitrite (NOx) for 5 h, whereas betanin was completely undetectable in human plasma. Critical translational caveat for rodent studies administering high-dose oral betanin.
