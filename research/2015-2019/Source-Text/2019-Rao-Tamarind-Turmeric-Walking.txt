Original Table 3 summary. Ninety randomized to two doses of combined tamarind and turmeric extract or placebo; 89 analyzed. Day-90 walking velocity changes +0.08 and +0.11 m/s with mixtures versus +0.02 with placebo. Adjusted p=.001 and p<.0001. Nonarthritic knee pain, no whole grated spice or isolated turmeric effect.
