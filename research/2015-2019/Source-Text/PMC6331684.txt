Design: Diagnostic dopamine-transporter imaging cohort

Seventy-five people with mild cognitive impairment included 33 with probable Lewy body disease, 15 with possible Lewy body disease and 27 with Alzheimer disease. Clinical features inform the diagnostic grouping. The original compares dopamine-transporter imaging against clinical assessments rather than measuring benefit or harm after a food.
