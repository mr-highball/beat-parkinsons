Inspected extent: Complete displayed collection body. Member P10-057 describes dietary recalls in seven people with Parkinson disease taking levodopa. Eggs, oatmeal, chicken, peanuts, Greek yogurt and cereal grains appeared as protein sources. The abstract reports group diet measures and correlations with characteristics such as time since diagnosis. It gives no food-specific clinical effect or fall counts.
