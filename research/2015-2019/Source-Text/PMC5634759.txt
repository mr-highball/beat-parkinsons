Title: Kaempferia parviflora rhizome extract and Myristica fragrans volatile oil increase the levels of monoamine neurotransmitters and impact the proteomic profiles in the rat hippocampus: Mechanistic insights into their neuroprotective effects.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5634759/
PMID: 29034205
PMCID: PMC5634759
DOI: 10.1016/j.jtcme.2017.01.002
Publication date: 2017-06-15
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official complete PMC JATS body.

Separate oral arms gave nutmeg volatile oil, Kaempferia parviflora rhizome extract, fluoxetine or vehicle to rats. Table 2 reports hippocampal monoamines, with additional proteomic outcomes. Volatile oil differs from grated nutmeg and Kaempferia parviflora differs from Zingiber officinale. The experiment does not measure clinical depression, anxiety or pain.
