Title: Comparison of cold-pressing and soxhlet extraction systems for bioactive compounds, antioxidant properties, polyphenols, fatty acids and tocopherols in eight nut oils

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6045996/
First published: 2018-06-07.
Coverage: Factual extract of complete original Methods, Results and Table 3, preserving fatty-acid labels and literature attribution.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

The study compares cold pressing and Soxhlet extraction of eight nut oils. Its walnut Table 3 linoleic values are 63.56% (SD .68) cold-pressed and 62.41% (SD 1.27) Soxhlet. Phenolic and tocopherol measurements also differ across preparations; the oils are not chemically identical.

The same table labels another row simply Linolenic, reporting .11% (SD .01) cold-pressed and .57% (SD .09) Soxhlet for walnut. The nearby sentence describing approximately 12% alpha-linolenic acid attributes a profile to earlier Colic 2015 literature. It is not the current table's matched extraction measurement. Alpha-linolenic, gamma-linolenic and linoleic acid must not be interchanged.

The linoleic comparison provides a limited composition bridge to a separate walnut-oil mouse experiment reporting 55% linoleic acid. It does not establish matching lots, active-constituent causality, analgesic equivalence or a human Parkinson outcome.
