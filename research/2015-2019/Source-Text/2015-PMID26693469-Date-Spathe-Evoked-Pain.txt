Title: Antinociceptive effect of palm date spathe hydroalcoholic extract on acute and chronic pain in mice as compared with analgesic effect of morphine and diclofenac
PMID: 26693469
PMCID: PMC4685642
DOI: 10.4103/2277-9175.170239
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4685642/
Publication: 2015-11-23 electronic publication
Coverage: Verified original NCBI JATS body and separate pain assays.

The preparation is an injected extract of the inflorescence sheath, or spathe. The hot-plate results did not show a clear extract comparison against control, while the writhing assay included a favorable result at the highest tested extract level. The endpoints and their directions are distinct. No Parkinson disease model or ordinary oral dried fruit was tested.
