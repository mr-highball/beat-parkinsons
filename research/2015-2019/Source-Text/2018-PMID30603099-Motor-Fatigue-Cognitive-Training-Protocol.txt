Title: Study protocol for a randomised pilot study of a computer-based, non-pharmacological cognitive intervention for motor slowing and motor fatigue in Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 30603099
PMCID: PMC6306004
DOI: 10.1186/s40814-018-0375-4
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6306004/
First publication date: 2018-12-26 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The pilot protocol plans a computerized intervention for motor slowing and motor fatigue. It excludes participants with visual hallucinations. The eligibility field provides no measured hallucinations or food comparison.
