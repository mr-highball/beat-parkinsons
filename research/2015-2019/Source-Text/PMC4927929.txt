Title: Conversion to IPX066 from Standard Levodopa Formulations in Advanced Parkinson's Disease: Experience in Clinical Trials
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4927929/
PMCID: PMC4927929
PMID: 26444090
DOI: 10.3233/jpd-150622
Publication date: 2015-11-05
Date basis: Sage issue publication date. PubMed indexed the record in October.
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary analysis of two prospective trial conversion phases
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Two otherwise distinct extended-release carbidopa-levodopa trial programs included a six-week open-label conversion before their randomized phases. The 450 immediate-release carbidopa-levodopa starters had two discontinuations attributed to visual hallucinations (0.4%). The 110 levodopa/entacapone starters had one adverse-event discontinuation whose reported terms did not include hallucinations. These are conversion-stage discontinuation counts, not the total event incidence or a randomized comparison. The registries and randomized reports are separate within the two parent families. No food exposure was assigned.
