Title: Systemic Synuclein Sampling Study clinical study protocol version 4.0
Source: https://www.michaeljfox.org/sites/default/files/media/document/S4_Clinical%20Study%20Protocol_Version%204%200%2018_Oct_2016%20CLEAN.pdf
Record: S4-001v4.0
Document date: 2016-10-18
Date basis: Document version date on the sponsor PDF. Earliest public posting of this version is unverified.
Source depth: Original 54-page sponsor protocol, synopsis and assessment sections inspected.

The protocol plans up to 100 enrollments, aiming for 80 participants to meet its minimum specimen requirements. The target is distributed across four groups of 20. The design includes biomarker measurements in saliva and other specimens. The clinical characterization includes MDS-UPDRS and SCOPA-AUT. The protocol specifies no assignment of a food preparation or ingredient-specific drooling outcome. It is in the same S4 study family as Trials/NCT02572713.txt and the Visanji and Chahine papers.
