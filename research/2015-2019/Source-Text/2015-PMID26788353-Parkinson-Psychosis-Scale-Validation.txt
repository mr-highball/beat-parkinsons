Title: A novel scale to assess psychosis in patients with parkinson's disease.

The clinician-rated Parkinson psychosis scale evaluates hallucinations and delusions, including individual features such as frequency and disruption. The validation reports inter-rater agreement and drug-response examples. Its measurements describe psychosis assessment and provide no dried fig comparison.
