Title: Psychophysical and vasomotor evidence for interdependency of TRPA1 and TRPV1-evoked nociceptive responses in human skin: an experimental study
PMID: 29847470
DOI: 10.1097/j.pain.0000000000001298
First published: 2018
Authors: Nielsen TA, Eriksen MA, Gazerani P, Andersen HH
Journal: Pain
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29847470/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Human experimental cutaneous pain study using occluded 10% topical allyl isothiocyanate (AITC) in 12 mm forearm Finn chambers, provoking acute burning pain, heat hyperalgesia, and neurogenic vasomotor flare (inhibited 86.9% by 8% capsaicin afferent ablation, p<0.001; companion to Segerdahl 1995 PMID:7605941 and Sjolund 1999 PMID:10072015 topical mustard-oil allodynia models). Topical cutaneous chemical-burn provocation, NOT oral dietary grated horseradish in PD.
