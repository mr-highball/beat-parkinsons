Registration date: 2016-08-15
Registration timing: while recruiting
Enrollment date displayed: 2016-07-15
Target population and size: 60 children aged 3 to 14
Design: Double-blind randomized parallel trial with local oral savory gel or placebo for five days after chemotherapy-related ulceration.
Outcomes and status: Primary measure was mucositis. Secondary measure was pain using the Oucher scale. The registry lists complete recruitment. The May 2018 publication reports the results.
