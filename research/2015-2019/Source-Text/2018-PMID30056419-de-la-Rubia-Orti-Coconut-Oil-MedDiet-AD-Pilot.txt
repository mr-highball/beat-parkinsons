Title: Improvement of Main Cognitive Functions in Patients with Alzheimer's Disease after Treatment with Coconut Oil Enriched Mediterranean Diet: A Pilot Study.
PMID: 30056419
DOI: 10.3233/JAD-180184
First published: 2018
Authors: de la Rubia Ortí JE, García-Pardo MP, Drehmer E, Sancho Cantus D, Julián Rochina M, Aguilar MA, Hu Yang I
Journal: Journal of Alzheimer's disease : JAD
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30056419/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized pilot intervention (de la Rubia Orti et al., 2018, J Alzheimers Dis; same 44-patient Valencia Alzheimer cohort as PMID:28421789 Nutr Hosp 2017) assigning 44 patients with Alzheimer disease (22/arm) to a coconut-oil-enriched isocaloric Mediterranean diet (40 mL/day coconut oil) vs control for 21 days. Assessed via 7-Minute Screen (and MMSE in 2017 report), reporting improvements in episodic/semantic memory, temporal orientation, and language-construction, especially in women with mild-to-moderate AD. Limits: short 21-day pilot bundled with a Mediterranean diet, unspecified coconut oil refinement grade, no isolated attention subtest or Parkinson cohort, and contradicted by the larger 24-week VCO-AD DB-RCT (Fernando 2023 PMID:37980665, n=120 overall null).
