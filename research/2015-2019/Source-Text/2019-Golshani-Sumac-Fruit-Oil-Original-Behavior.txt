The investigators randomized 84 male Wistar rats into two separate behavioral cohorts of 42, with seven rats per group. The forced-swim cohort compares oral distilled fruit essential oil groups with saline, solvent and an active comparator. Figure 1 shows about 19 and 18 seconds of immobility in the saline and solvent arms and about 16 and 11 seconds in the two higher oil groups. The values are approximate plot readings. The paper reports an overall group result and lower immobility under the two higher exposures. Figure 2 shows more climbing in those groups. The section presents no independent locomotor test for the forced-swim cohort.

The results prose and the Figure 1 legend differ in the highest-dose significance threshold and their comparator wording. The plotted uncertainty and the printed uncertainty values cannot be aligned with confidence. Preserve the direction of the behavioral observations while leaving exact pairwise uncertainty unresolved. Distilled fruit oil, a swim behavior and a non-Parkinson rat population differ from ground sumac and scored depression in Parkinson disease.
