Design: Spatial chemical imaging of human primary visual cortex

The investigators map molecular and elemental distributions within human V1 tissue using two mass-spectrometry imaging modalities. These maps concern tissue composition and cortical layers, without a defined population with food exposure and subsequent visual episodes.
