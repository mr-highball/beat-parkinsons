Title: Oral supplementation with geranium oil or anise oil ameliorates depressed rat-related symptoms through oils antioxidant effects.
PMID: 31490773
DOI: 10.1515/jcim-2019-0028
First published: 2019
Authors: El-Shamy KA, Koriem KMM, Fadl NN, El-Azma MHA, Arbid MSS, Morsy FA, El-Zayat SR, Hosny EN, Youness ER
Journal: Journal of complementary & integrative medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31490773/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical chronic mild stress (CMS) depression study in n=80 male albino rats (8 subgroups of n=10: normal and CMS cohorts treated orally for 4 weeks with saline, geranium oil, anise oil, or venlafaxine). Oral anise oil attenuated CMS-induced reductions in sucrose preference, open-field distance/center entries, and CMS-induced increases in tail-suspension immobility and forced-swim floating time, alongside restored cortical/hippocampal SOD, GPx, GST, GR, catalase and lower MDA/NO. Verified from indexed PubMed abstract; exact group means/p-values and anise oil dose are not printed in the abstract.
