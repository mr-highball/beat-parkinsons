Title: Dietary nitrate supplementation improves sprint and high-intensity intermittent running performance.
PMID: 27777094
DOI: 10.1016/j.niox.2016.10.006
First published: 2016
Authors: Thompson C, Vanhatalo A, Jell H, Fulford J, Carter J, Nyman L, et al.
Journal: Nitric oxide : biology and chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27777094/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=36 recreationally active adults given BRJ vs placebo for 5 days: improved Stroop reaction time during cognitive demand (p<0.05) with unchanged accuracy. Limits: non-PD healthy sample.
