Title: Computer vision for automatic quality inspection of dried figs (Ficus carica L.) in real-time
Source: https://redivia.gva.es/handle/20.500.11939/6957?show=full
Source alias: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2015.11.002
DOI: 10.1016/j.compag.2015.11.002
Publication date: 2016-01
Date basis: Journal issue date from the publisher deposit, Computers and Electronics in Agriculture 120, 17–25.
Text coverage: Original authors' institutional record and abstract. Research summary.

Benalia and colleagues studied image-based inspection and sorting of dried figs from Cosenza. They compared digital image color measurements with conventional instrumental measurements and evaluated sorting based on fruit appearance. The exposure is a product sample passing through an inspection system. There were no people consuming figs or health outcomes. The report is a preparation and product quality reference.
