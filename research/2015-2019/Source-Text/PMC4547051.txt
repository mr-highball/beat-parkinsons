CHCHD10 variant analysis covers several neurodegenerative diagnostic groups. One familial frontotemporal lobar degeneration case had visual hallucinations during clinical deterioration and developed bradykinesia later. The report is a genetic and differential-diagnosis inquiry. It supplies no fig exposure or longitudinal visual episode counts for Parkinson disease.
