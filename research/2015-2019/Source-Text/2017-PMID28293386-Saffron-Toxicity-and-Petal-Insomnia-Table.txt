The 2018 saffron cardiovascular review cites Bostan and colleagues in a safety passage that lists possible insomnia. The traceable insomnia row in Bostan's adverse-event table belongs to the Basti et al. pilot trial, reference 46. It gives three events with petal extract and three with fluoxetine, reported p=1.00. The section header for that group spans several rows. Do not assign the row to the preceding post-coronary-intervention trial listed as reference 45. The original petal trial has 40 randomized outpatients, insomnia events rather than an insomnia severity outcome, no placebo arm, and no Parkinson population.

The review repeats the underlying trial. Its insomnia row supplies no second family and no attributable worsening contrast for culinary stigma powder. Other toxicology comparisons use different products, doses and endpoints.
