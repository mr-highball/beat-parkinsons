Title: Betalain-rich concentrate supplementation improves exercise performance and recovery in competitive triathletes.
PMID: 28121183
DOI: 10.1139/apnm-2016-0452
First published: 2017
Authors: Montenegro CF, Kwong DA, Minow ZA, Davis BA, Lozada CF, Casazza GA
Journal: Applied physiology, nutrition, and metabolism = Physiologie appliquee, nutrition et metabolisme
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28121183/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=22 competitive runners given 100 mg/d betalain-rich, nitrate-free beetroot concentrate vs placebo for 7 days: significantly attenuated post-exercise subjective fatigue increase (-0.05 vs +3.23 units; p=0.05) and improved 10-km time-trial performance. Shows non-nitrate beetroot betalain fraction also lowered subjective exercise fatigue.
