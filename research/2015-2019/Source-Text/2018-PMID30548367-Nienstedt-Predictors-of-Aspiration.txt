Title: Predictive clinical factors for penetration and aspiration in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/nmo.13524
PMID: 30548367
DOI: 10.1111/nmo.13524
Publication date: 2018-12-11
Date basis: Publisher online-first date. Issue publication followed in March 2019.
Source depth: Original author abstract. Full outcome tables remain open.

Flexible endoscopy in 119 Hamburg Parkinson outpatients identified swallowing abnormalities in 113/119. Specific symptom questions elicited swallowing difficulty in 12–27%, with reported sensitivity up to 50%. The adjusted odds ratio for critical dysphagia among those reporting subjective signs of aspiration was 8.6 (95% CI 3.05–26.52). These are clinical and instrumental swallowing comparisons. The author abstract gives no food-specific drooling result. Shared authors, institution and patient count suggest overlap with the two earlier Hamburg reports. Exact participant overlap remains open.
