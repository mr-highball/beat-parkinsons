2019 — Oral extracts made from dried cilantro leaves increased rat urine output
Source: https://irjpms.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/IRJPMS-V2N5P61Y19.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of Methods and Figure 1.

Researchers gave rats oral water or methanol extracts of powdered shade-dried cilantro leaves. The methanol group had the greater six-hour urine volume of the two extract treatments. The study also assessed urinary electrolytes and did not measure pressure or orthostatic symptoms.
