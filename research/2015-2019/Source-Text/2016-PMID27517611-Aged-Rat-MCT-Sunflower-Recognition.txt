Title: Cognition and Synaptic-Plasticity Related Changes in Aged Rats Supplemented with 8- and 10-Carbon Medium Chain Triglycerides
Publication date: 2016-08-12
PMID: 27517611
PMCID: PMC4982641
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0160159
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4982641/
Text coverage: Primary full text methods and reported behavioral comparisons.

Wang and Mitchell assigned 36 aged male rats to three calorie- and lipid-matched chows containing 5% C8 MCT, 5% C10 MCT or 5% sunflower oil for eight weeks. C10 improved novel-object recognition relative to sunflower. Both MCT groups had better social recognition. The comparator supplies an active oil replacement and no no-supplement group, verified cold-pressed grade, Parkinson model or executive measure. The manufacturer-affiliated experiment merits tracking as one study family.
