Title: Effect of crocetin on quality of sleep: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30477864/
PMID: 30477864
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2018.09.003
Registration: UMIN000025854
First published online: 2018-09-08
Coverage: Original investigator abstract, publisher preview and verified registry linkage. Full arm-level outcome tables pending.

Umigai and colleagues randomly assigned 30 healthy adults with mild sleep complaints to placebo and oral crocetin in two treatment sequences. The crocetin course lasted 14 days, with a separate washout. Single-channel EEG delta power favored crocetin. Reported contrasts for sleep latency, sleep efficiency, total sleep time and wake after sleep onset were inconclusive. Subjective sleepiness on rising and refreshed feeling favored crocetin. The abstract uses a sleep inventory and gives no dedicated insomnia severity comparison in Parkinson disease. The registry and publication describe the same comparison by DOI. The botanical origin of the preparation must be checked in the full methods before inferring its relationship to saffron.
