Title: ADS-5102 (Amantadine) Extended-Release Capsules for Levodopa-Induced Dyskinesia in Parkinson's Disease (EASE LID 2 Study): Interim Results of an Open-Label Safety Study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5611804/
PMID: 28777755
PMCID: PMC5611804
DOI: 10.3233/jpd-171134
Publication year: 2017
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The open-label amantadine extension records visual hallucinations in 43 participants (19.3%). These are participants with a reported adverse event during follow-up, with no concurrent food comparison. Prior trial exposure and age are relevant to interpretation.

The interim study records 45 patients with hallucinations of any type and 43 with visual events. Visual and auditory groups overlap. Treatment discontinuation associated with visual hallucinations was reported in five patients. The report is an open-label safety extension.
