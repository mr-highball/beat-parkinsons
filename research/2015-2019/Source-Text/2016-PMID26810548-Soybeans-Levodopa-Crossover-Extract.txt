Title: Effects of soybean ingestion on pharmacokinetics of levodopa and motor symptoms of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26810548/
First published online: 2016-01-06
PMID: 26810548
DOI: 10.1016/j.jns.2016.01.005
Text coverage: original author abstract. Results tables and item outcomes remain open.

Seven people with Parkinson disease and wearing-off and five healthy volunteers participated in a crossover. The study compared levodopa/carbidopa alone with the same treatment plus ground soybeans. In the Parkinson group the soybean condition gave longer reported motor ON (p=.028) and a lower modified abnormal involuntary movement score (p<.001). The abstract names UPDRS Part III and different levodopa metabolites. It gives no separate rest-tremor result or refined soybean oil exposure. The effect sizes for ON and dyskinesia and the precision of the comparisons cannot be determined from this abstract. The study evaluates ground beans during drug administration.

November 26, 2026 original follow-through.
The 2019 Rijntjes review (PMC6875167) gives roasted and pulverized Hokkaido soybean preparation and a 75-minute acute on-period extension. Its carbidopa amount and metabolite directions differ from the directly inspected original abstract. The review-specific preparation and additional duration estimate are retained at secondary depth. The original author abstract establishes a longer acute on-period without refined soybean oil or daily OFF time.

November 26 investigator thesis-summary follow-through

The Wakayama Medical University original thesis summary, pp.11–12, matches the 2016 lead author's journal title and verifies the same acute 11-g soy comparison. Seven participants with Parkinson disease and five healthy comparators took part in separate visits. In the Parkinson group the two-day comparison used levodopa/carbidopa 100/10 mg with or without soy. Clinical and blood measures were collected through 180 minutes, then ON status was self-recorded. Median ON time was 150 minutes without soy and 270 with soy, p=.028. The indexed article Table 2 shows respective interquartile ranges of 135–330 and 165–390 minutes.

The primary summary reports adjusted Parkinson 3-O-methyldopa 374.6 versus 317.0 ng/mL (p=.03), homovanillic acid 223.5 versus 267.8 ng/mL (p=.002), modified AIMS 3.9 versus 1.9 (p<.001) and UPDRS-III p=.61. Healthy-participant 3-O-methyldopa area under the curve is 627.8 versus 532.4 ng·h/mL (p=.04). The indexed article text gives no soy-by-time interaction for the motor or dyskinesia scales. The thesis summary gives no full numerical model or preparation confirmation beyond the soy exposure. These are acute ON comparisons, with no measurement of 24-hour OFF time or separate refined soybean oil.
