Title: Changes in blood pressure indices in normotensive adults after the consumption of lemongrass tea
Publication: Ekpenyong and Osim, online September 18, 2016, Journal of Coastal Life Medicine 4(10):802–808
Source: https://oaji.net/pdf.html?n=2016%2F2154-1474878502.pdf
Coverage: factual extract of the accessible primary source

The authors used the same 105-person three-dose and day 0, 10 and 30 design reported for the diuresis study. Blood pressure was taken after five minutes of seated rest. Several blood pressure indices decreased relative to baseline. The report did not measure standing balance, postural instability severity or incident falls, and participants did not have Parkinson disease.
