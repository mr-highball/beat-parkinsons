Title: Cucurbitacins extracted from Cucumis melo L. (CuEC) exert a hypotensive effect via regulating vascular tone.
PMID: 30962520
DOI: 10.1038/s41440-019-0258-y
First published: 2019
Authors: Yuan RQ, Qian L, Yun WJ, Cui XH, Lv GX, Tang WQ et al.
Journal: Hypertens Res
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30962520/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse/rat study of cucurbitacins extracted from Cucumis melo (CuEC, rich in cucurbitacin B). Intraperitoneal 1 mg/kg reduced systolic blood pressure and blunted Ang II pressor response via acetylcholine-mediated vasodilation; 2 mg/kg caused lethal endothelial barrier injury. Isolated injected cucurbitacin extract, not oral raw edible cantaloupe flesh.
