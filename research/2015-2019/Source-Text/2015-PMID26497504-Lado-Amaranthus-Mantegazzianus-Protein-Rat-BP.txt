Title: Effects of the Dietary Addition of Amaranth (Amaranthus mantegazzianus) Protein Isolate on Antioxidant Status, Lipid Profiles and Blood Pressure of Rats.
PMID: 26497504
DOI: 10.1007/s11130-015-0516-3
First published: 2015
Authors: Lado MB, Burini J, Rinaldi G, Añón MC, Tironi VA
Journal: Plant foods for human nutrition (Dordrecht, Netherlands)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26497504/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical rat feeding study (Lado et al., 2015, Plant Foods Hum Nutr) adding 2.5% (w/w) Amaranthus mantegazzianus protein isolate to basal or high-cholesterol diets in Wistar rats for 28 days. Protein isolate lowered tail-artery resting blood pressure by 18% and reduced plasma/liver lipids in cholesterol-fed rats. Limits: isolated protein from a non-standard amaranth species measuring resting rat tail blood pressure; does not evaluate cooked culinary grain or orthostatic lightheadedness.
