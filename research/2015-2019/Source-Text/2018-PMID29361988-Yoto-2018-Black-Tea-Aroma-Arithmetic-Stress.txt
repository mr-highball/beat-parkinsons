Title: Black tea aroma inhibited increase of salivary chromogranin-A after arithmetic tasks.
PMID: 29361988
PMCID: PMC5781314
DOI: 10.1186/s40101-018-0163-0
First published: 2018
Authors: Yoto A, Fukui N, Kaneda C, Torita S, Goto K, Nanjo F, et al.
Journal: Journal of physiological anthropology
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5781314/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled crossover study of inhaling Darjeeling and Assam black tea aromas vs water control during 30-min Uchida-Kraepelin arithmetic stress tasks. Both black tea aromas reduced post-stress salivary chromogranin-A compared with water, and Darjeeling aroma showed only a trend toward lower POMS tension/anxiety immediately after initial exposure. Limits: aroma inhalation only (not consumed brewed black tea), non-PD sample, subjective anxiety trend only.
