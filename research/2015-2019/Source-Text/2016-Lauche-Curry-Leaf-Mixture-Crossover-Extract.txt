Human crossover of a three-botanical decoction
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27261998/
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

The study gave a decoction of curry leaf, pomegranate and turmeric powders or placebo in a blinded crossover among 32 people with diarrhea-predominant irritable bowel syndrome. The estimated severity difference was 24.10 points (95% CI −17.12 to 65.32, p = .26). The authors report roughly one third with at least one minor event considered potentially related. The indexed report gives no clinical Parkinson outcomes. The public publisher body could not be obtained.
