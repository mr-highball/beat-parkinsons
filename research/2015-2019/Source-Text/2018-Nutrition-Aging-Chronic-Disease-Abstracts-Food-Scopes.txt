Claim 0259 | PMCID:PMC6265829 | raw orange | fall frequency
Current exact disposition: related
P01-021 reports higher stool frequency and softer stools in healthy adults assigned an orange-pomace beverage, alongside mild gas and bloating. The 2019 article identifies NCT02979496 and reports the companion trial. This is one study family for the raw-orange constipation claim, with preparation and population indirectness, and it provides no fall outcome.
