Title: A PROSPECTIVE PHARMACOLOGICAL REVIEW OF MEDICINAL HERBS, CUCUMIS MELO AND BERBERIS VULGARIS, COMMONLY USED IN THE TREATMENT OF RENAL DISEASES IN PAKISTAN.
PMID: 26647620
First published: 2015
Authors: Ullah N, Khan S, Khan A, Ahmad W, Shah Y, Ahmad L et al.
Journal: Acta Pol Pharm
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26647620/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Pharmacological and ethnobotanical review describing traditional Pakistani use of Cucumis melo fruit and especially seeds for renal disorders and diuresis; review without controlled human urinary urgency or PD clinical data.
