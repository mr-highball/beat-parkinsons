Title: Regional Structural Hippocampal Differences Between Dementia with Lewy Bodies and Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6839604/
PMID: 31524178
PMCID: PMC6839604
DOI: 10.3233/JPD-191600
Publication date: 2019-09-10
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Advance publication in original PMC metadata. The journal collection version is dated October 11.
Design: Clinical hippocampal structure comparison

MRI compares hippocampal measures in 14 dementia-with-Lewy-bodies patients and 62 people with Parkinson disease. The regional structure outcome does not estimate visual hallucination frequency after a culinary exposure.
