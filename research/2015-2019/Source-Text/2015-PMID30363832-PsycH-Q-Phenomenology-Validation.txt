Title: Validation of the Psychosis and Hallucinations Questionnaire in Non-demented Patients with Parkinson's Disease.
PMID: 30363832
PMCID: PMC6183006
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12139
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6183006/
First publication date: 2015-03-16 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC complete original article HTML and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The 20-item self-report validation separates visual misperceptions, sensory experiences and other psychosis phenomena. The paper records instrument construction and comparison with clinical assessments. It does not assign dried figs or estimate their effect on visual episodes.
