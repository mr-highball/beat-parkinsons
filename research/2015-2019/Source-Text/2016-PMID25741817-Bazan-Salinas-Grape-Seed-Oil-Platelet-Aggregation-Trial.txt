Title: Reduction of Platelet Aggregation From Ingestion of Oleic and Linoleic Acids Found in Vitis vinifera and Arachis hypogaea Oils.
PMID: 25741817
DOI: 10.1097/MJT.0000000000000185
First published: 2016
Authors: Bazán-Salinas IL, Matías-Pérez D, Pérez-Campos E, Pérez-Campos Mayoral L, García-Montalvo IA
Journal: American journal of therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25741817/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind pre-post controlled trial in 30 healthy adults (plus 4 aspirin controls) evaluating oral Vitis vinifera grape seed oil and peanut oil on ADP-induced platelet aggregation. Grape seed oil reduced platelet aggregation by 8.4 +/- 1%. Hemostasis assay only; zero neurological or PD symptom outcomes.
