Title: Modulation of dopamine metabolizing enzymes and antioxidant status by Capsicum annuum Lin in rotenone-intoxicated rat brain
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214750018307698/pdf
PMCID: PMC6700337
PMID: 31440456
DOI: 10.1016/j.toxrep.2019.07.012
First public article date: 2019-08-01
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated original Sections 2.2 and 3.1 and Figure 1

The study reports a Capsicum annuum solvent extract in a
rotenone rat model. The behavioral Methods count rearing, and
Figure 1 graphs rearing rates in groups of six. Rearing increased
under extract in several groups while the model control declined.
Other results concern dopamine metabolism and oxidative markers.
The paper mentions rest tremor in the clinical background.
The treatment differs from culinary paprika.
