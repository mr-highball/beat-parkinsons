Title: A Controlled, Randomized, Double-blind Trial to Evaluate the Effect of Vegetables and Whole Grain Powder That Is Rich in Dietary Fibers on Bowel Functions and Defecation in Constipated Young Adults.
PMID: 25853105
PMCID: PMC4384716
DOI: 10.15430/JCP.2015.20.1.64
First published: 2015
Authors: Woo HI, Kwak SH, Lee Y, Choi JH, Cho YM, Om AS
Journal: Journal of cancer prevention
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4384716/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Four-week double-blind RCT in n=96 constipated young adults (Rome II) comparing 60 g/d vegetable-grain fiber powder (chicory, broccoli, whole grains) vs rice-flakes control. Vegetable powder significantly improved stool hardness, stool amount, incomplete evacuation, straining, and evacuation frequency (1.04 +/- 0.71 vs 0.41 +/- 0.64 at week 2). Limits: multi-ingredient fiber blend; cannot isolate broccoli.
