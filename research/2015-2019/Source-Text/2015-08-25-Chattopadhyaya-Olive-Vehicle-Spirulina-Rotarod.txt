Olive oil in a spirulina and amantadine rotarod experiment
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4548915/
PMID: 26306668
PMCID: PMC4548915
DOI: 10.1186/s12906-015-0815-0
Coverage: Original Methods and results for motor coordination

Chattopadhyaya and colleagues suspended a freeze-dried spirulina extract in olive oil for oral gavage in 6-OHDA-lesioned rats. The lesion comparison used the vehicle, and other arms evaluated spirulina or amantadine. The reported motor comparisons include time before falling on a rotarod. The treatment contrasts and motor task do not isolate a culinary olive oil effect or report clinical postural instability. The article does not establish the oil processing grade.
