Title: Almond oil for patients with hyperlipidemia: A randomized open-label controlled clinical trial.
PMID: 30670262
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2018.10.013
First published: 2019
Authors: Zibaeenezhad MJ, Ostovan P, Mosavat SH, Zamirian M, Attar A
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30670262/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Open-label randomized controlled clinical trial in n=97 patients with hyperlipidemia (intervention n=49, no-intervention control n=48) administering 10 mL oral sweet almond oil twice daily (20 mL/day) for 30 days. Total cholesterol (-16.12 +/- 26.16 mg/dL, p = 0.009) and LDL-C (-20.88 +/- 18.4 mg/dL, p < 0.001) fell significantly, with no significant change in triglycerides or HDL-C. Pressing method (cold-pressed vs refined) was not specified in the abstract, and no Parkinson's motor, cognitive, sleep, pain, or bowel symptom endpoints were measured.
