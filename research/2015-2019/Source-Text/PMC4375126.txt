Title: Parkinsonism in phenylketonuria: a consequence of dopamine depletion?
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4375126/
PMCID: PMC4375126
PMID: 25614310
DOI: 10.1007/8904_2014_386
Publication date: 2015-01-23
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Evidence type: Case report
Format: Factual digest of the original material

One adult with late-diagnosed phenylketonuria had action and rest tremor and later parkinsonism. Childhood phenylalanine restriction partly improved speech. Adherence was limited after later reintroduction, and an attempt at stricter restriction was discontinued. Suspected visual hallucinations improved after pramipexole withdrawal. The later levodopa motor examination scored 32 off and 28 on. The clinician's observation, dietary history and drug sequence describe separate events. No visual episode count or dried fig comparison was made.
