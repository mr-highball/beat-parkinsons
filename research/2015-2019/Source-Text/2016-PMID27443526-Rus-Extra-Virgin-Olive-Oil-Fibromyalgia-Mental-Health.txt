Title: Extra Virgin Olive Oil Improves Oxidative Stress, Functional Capacity, and Health-Related Psychological Status in Patients With Fibromyalgia: A Preliminary Study
Source: https://doi.org/10.1177/1099800416659370
PMID: 27443526
DOI: 10.1177/1099800416659370
Publication date: 2016-07-26
Date basis: First publication online. Print issue January 2017.
Archived article: https://www.ciberdolor.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Jaen-Granada-2017.pdf
Accessed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: institutional copy of original article, Table I and Figure 3.

The three-week active comparison randomized 23 women with fibromyalgia to organic extra-virgin olive oil (11) or refined olive oil (12). The reported between-group difference in pre-to-post SF-12 mental-component change is +11.228 points (95% robust CI 3.19–25.24, adjusted p=.035). The raw start values differ between groups and the pilot is small. The mental-component score is a composite, and the investigators do not report a separate anxiety estimate or a Parkinson cohort.
