Access evidence: Exact PubMed author abstract and publisher bibliographic record. No publicly accessible matching original body was found in DOI and title searches.

The abstract describes fewer chewing stereotypies in the haloperidol-plus-oil animals than with haloperidol alone. It gives additional dopamine and oxidative-stress outcomes. The duration, movement endpoint and preparation route resemble the 2015 Samad study. Similar design does not establish either identical animals or independent samples.

Continuing evidence gaps
Retrieve Metabolic Brain Disease 32, pp. 1099–1107, including methods, model-level counts and figures. Verify cohort independence and oil processing. The accessible abstract gives no separate rigidity or dystonia measurement and no clinical Parkinson outcome.
