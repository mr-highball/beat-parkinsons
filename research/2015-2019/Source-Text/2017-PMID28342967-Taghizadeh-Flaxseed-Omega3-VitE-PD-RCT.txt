Title: The effects of omega-3 fatty acids and vitamin E co-supplementation on clinical and metabolic status in patients with Parkinson's disease: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
PMID: 28342967
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuint.2017.03.014
First published: 2017
Authors: Taghizadeh M, Tamtaji OR, Dadgostar E, Daneshvar Kakhaki R, Bahmani F, Abolhassani J, Aarabi MH, Kouchaki E
Journal: Neurochemistry International
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28342967/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. EXCLUSION CONTEXT RECORD: 12-week DB-RCT in 60 PD patients testing 1,000 mg omega-3 from FLAXSEED oil (plant ALA, NOT marine fish EPA/DHA) co-supplemented with 400 IU vitamin E vs placebo, reporting improved total UPDRS (-3.3 vs +4.4, P=0.02). Strictly excluded as unusable exposure for herring.
