Title: Acute effects of theanine, caffeine and theanine-caffeine combination on attention.
PMID: 26869148
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2016.1144845
First published: 2017
Authors: Kahathuduwa CN, Dassanayake TL, Amarakoon AMT, Weerasinghe VS
Journal: Nutritional neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26869148/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Five-way placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=20 healthy young men comparing 200 mg L-theanine, 160 mg caffeine, their combination, 1 cup brewed black tea, and water on visual reaction time (SVRT/RVRT) and N2-P300 ERPs. High-dose L-theanine (p=0.019), caffeine (p=0.043), and their combination (p=0.001) improved RVRT and N2-P300 amplitude, whereas 1 cup of brewed black tea did not significantly change RVRT (p=0.429 vs water p=0.822). Limits: single cup dose, healthy young men.
