Sunflower carrier in the registered lavender inhalation trial
Source: https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-5q5t5z
Coverage: Official registry interventions, dates and secondary outcome 13 reviewed

ReBEC registered the study on December 27, 2019, after first enrollment on April 24, 2018. The record names Laszlo sunflower oil in both randomized kits. The lavender kit added lavender essential oil. Participants used the vial by inhalation and placed its remaining contents on cotton near the pillow. The registration specifies the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale and reports a within-lavender depression change without an arm-specific estimate. The formulation record leaves the sunflower oil batch and refining grade unidentified. Both arms received the same sunflower carrier.
