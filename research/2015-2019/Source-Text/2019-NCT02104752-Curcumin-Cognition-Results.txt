Posted curcumin composite scores and unreported attention domain
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02104752?tab=results
Coverage: Factual original official record summary.

Official results first posted May 15, 2019. For 17 Theracurmin and 19 placebo participants in the MCCB table, mean composite T-scores were 36.6 and 32.9 at baseline, 36.3 and 33.8 at week 4, and 35.8 and 33.9 at week 8. The posted table does not give an attention/vigilance subscore or an adjusted between-arm change test. The registry links Wynn PMID 28965778 to the same study. These values describe a global score in schizophrenia.
