Original flaxseed omega-3 and vitamin E registration and later UPDRS revision
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/6103?revision=6103
Registration: IRCT201604035623N73
Coverage: Official first and September 2019 registry revisions compared with the 2017 accepted manuscript

The IRCT first public version dated April 7, 2016 listed total antioxidant capacity and malondialdehyde as primary outcomes, 1800 mg omega-3 plus 200 IU vitamin E versus placebo, and Naghavi clinic. It did not list UPDRS. The September 2019 public revision lists hs-CRP and neurologist-measured UPDRS, 1000 mg omega-3 plus 400 IU vitamin E, and Shahid Beheshti clinic. The 2017 accepted manuscript cites this registration and describes the later dose. The later public record cannot verify advance specification of the clinical outcome. Neither registry version supplies a separate rigidity outcome or a method of pressing the oil.
