Terminated curcumin pilot with registered attention testing
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02476708
Coverage: Factual original official record summary.

First posted June 19, 2015. The Yale trial stopped for insufficient recruitment with 12 enrolled. The protocol lists Continuous Performance Test–Identical Pairs as a secondary attention/vigilance measure and reports no structured results. The registry lists a 600-mg capsule three times daily versus placebo. A related 2019 Yale pilot publication has an unverified exact link in the available originals, and the article body and formulation comparison remain open.

Follow-up: Kucukgoncu et al., PMID 30747752, explicitly names this registration. See 2019-Kucukgoncu-Curcumin-Pilot-Attention.txt for published results and the unresolved regimen difference.
