The 119-person Parkinson imaging cohort classifies cognitive phenotypes. Table I reports presence and severity from MDS-UPDRS item 1.2. Most positive observations were illusions, unformed experiences or formed hallucinations with preserved insight. Prevalence and ordinal severity do not give a food-timed visual episode count.

Table I gives a hallucination-item prevalence of 0%, 0%, 34.88% and 14.29% across the four cognitive groups. The positive item counts mostly indicate illusions or visual experiences with preserved insight. The comparison concerns cognitive strata and resting-state connectivity at assessment, with no exposure assignment or longitudinal visual episode count.
