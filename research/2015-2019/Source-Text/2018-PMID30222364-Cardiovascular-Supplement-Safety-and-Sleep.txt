Original source scope
An uncontrolled adult prehypertension trial characterizes a proprietary herbal–mineral intervention and adverse events. Its ingredient list does not include culinary sage. Tiredness and sleep complaints were recorded during use of the actual blend.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The reported fatigue or insomnia events cannot be attributed to fresh or dried sage. No Salvia officinalis exposure was assigned.
