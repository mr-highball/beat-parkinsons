Title: Low-fat versus ketogenic diet in Parkinson's disease: A pilot randomized controlled trial
Source: https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/mds.27390
PMID: 30098269
PMCID: PMC6175383
DOI: 10.1002/mds.27390
Trial registration: ACTRN12617000027314
Publication date: 2018-08-11
Date basis: First online date in the publisher record.
Coverage: Paraphrased original methods and Tables 2–3. Publisher erratum checked.

Forty-seven people with Parkinson disease were randomized to an eight-week low-fat diet or ketogenic diet. Forty-four started and 38 completed. The clinical examiner was blinded to diet assignment. The study compared assigned meal patterns. The report gives separate adverse-event counts and total scale scores.

Table 3 records exacerbation of tremor, rigidity or both in 12/24 ketogenic and 3/23 low-fat participants during weeks 1–4, and 7/24 versus 5/23 during weeks 5–8. The report attributes two early ketogenic-arm withdrawals to these events. The combined category supplies no count for isolated rest tremor or isolated rigidity. The same ketogenic arm showed a larger reduction in the Part I nonmotor total (−4.58 versus −0.99, p<.001). The symptom and composite results address distinct outcomes.

Individual intake of any single ingredient was not assigned or measured separately. The study and conference report refer to one family. The December 17, 2018 erratum (DOI: 10.1002/mds.27596) corrects reference 13 and does not change the results.
