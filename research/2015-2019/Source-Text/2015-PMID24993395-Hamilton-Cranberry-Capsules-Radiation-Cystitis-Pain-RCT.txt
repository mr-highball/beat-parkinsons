Title: Standardized cranberry capsules for radiation cystitis in prostate cancer patients in New Zealand: a randomized double blinded, placebo controlled pilot study.
PMID: 24993395
DOI: 10.1007/s00520-014-2335-8
First published: 2015
Authors: Hamilton K, Bennett NC, Purdie G, Herst PM
Journal: Supportive care in cancer : official journal of the Multinational Association of Supportive Care in Cancer
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24993395/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled pilot trial (Hamilton et al., 2015, Support Care Cancer) in 41 men undergoing prostate external-beam radiotherapy assigned to standardized cranberry capsules (72 mg PAC/day) vs placebo. Cystitis incidence was 65% vs 90% (p=0.058, severe 30% vs 45% p=0.30); overall urinary pain/burning (dysuria) incidence was lower with cranberry (p=0.045), especially on a low-hydration regimen (p=0.038). Limits: localized radiation-induced mucosal dysuria rather than musculoskeletal/neuropathic Parkinson pain, standardized PAC extract rather than cooked whole cranberries, small pilot (n=41), contrasting with null culture-confirmed dysuria in Stonehouse 2025 (PMID:39863114).
