Title: Carrot (Daucus carota L.): Nephroprotective against gentamicin-induced nephrotoxicity in rats.
PMID: 27127313
PMCID: PMC4825426
DOI: 10.4103/0253-7613.178822
First published: 2016
Authors: Sodimbaku V, Pujari L, Mullangi R, Marri S
Journal: Indian J Pharmacol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27127313/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rat study (n=24) of oral ethanolic Daucus carota root extract (200 and 400 mg/kg) attenuating gentamicin-induced BUN/creatinine/uric-acid elevation (p<0.01); notes folkloric use of carrot for urinary/renal disorders. Acute nephrotoxicity model, not PD urinary urgency.
