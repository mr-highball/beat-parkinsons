Text coverage: Distilled member notes from the original S1–S367 supplement. The catalog's separate P-426 record describes strawberry-flavor preference.

O-4, S3. The protein-screening model pooled food-frequency predictors that included eggs. The development and external validation samples contained 1,348 LASA and 563 HELIUS participants, with development AUC 0.889 and external-validation AUC 0.856. The reference was estimated protein intake from a longer questionnaire, with no poached-egg intervention, walking-speed or falls outcome. The full PLOS ONE paper is the same analysis, PMC5965846 and PMID 29791454.

O-32, S13. The Helsinki Businessmen Study reported midlife measurements for 2,600 men assessed in 1974 and 409 surviving to 90 or older by March 2018. The abstract describes lower coffee and alcohol consumption in the longevity group and highlights smoking and BMI among adjusted predictors. The coffee preparation and Parkinson fatigue were not reported. A subsequent narrower report in the same family used 970 men and found 3.8 versus 4.1 cups daily, p = 0.13, in the unadjusted survival comparison (PMID 31612429). This difference in analytic scope limits comparison, and neither report is an independent confirmation about fatigue.

P-432, S183. A one-week dietary recall furnished an 11-component Mediterranean diet score in 53 older coronary patients, including 27 with diabetes and 26 without. Olive oil was one component. The abstract separately discusses blood pressure and metabolic measures. The reported association in the non-diabetic group uses the composite score and lacks an oil-specific coefficient or grade. The study did not assess Parkinson symptom change.
