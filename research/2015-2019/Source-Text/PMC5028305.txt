Title: The discovery of LRRK2 p.R1441S, a novel mutation for Parkinson's disease, adds to the complexity of a mutational hotspot

Source depth: Complete original PMC structured article body

The authors describe an LRRK2 Arg1441Ser carrier with Parkinson disease and visual symptoms in the course of long-term care. Genetic phenotype, medication changes and a briefly recorded antipsychotic intervention provide case context. There is no dietary exposure or episode count.
