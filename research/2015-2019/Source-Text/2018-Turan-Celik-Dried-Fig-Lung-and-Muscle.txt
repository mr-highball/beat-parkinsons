Title: The Healing Properties of Dried Figs (Ficus carica L.) Against Oxidative Stress Caused By Ethyl Alcohol in Rats
Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/330134291_The_Healing_Properties_of_Dried_Figs_Ficus_carica_LAgainst_Oxidative_Stress_Caused_By_Ethyl_Alcohol_in_Rats
Publication date: 2018-05
Date basis: Original Bulletin of Environment, Pharmacology and Life Sciences imprint 7(6), 30–36.
Text coverage: Author-uploaded original including tissue assays, Table 1 and references. Research summary.

Turan and Celik reported lung and muscle measurements in a 50-day rat study with dried fig and ethanol groups. Adding dried fig to the ethanol group lowered tissue malondialdehyde in both tissues. The antioxidant defense measures did not show a consistent recovery, and the authors concluded that the tissue response was unclear. This qualifies the liver-oriented signal in the authors' 2016 report. Both use the same named treatment groups and duration. They measure different tissues, and the inspected records do not establish whether the reports used distinct animals. Neither measures visual behavior or clinical symptoms.
