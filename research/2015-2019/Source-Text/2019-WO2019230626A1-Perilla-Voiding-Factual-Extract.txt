WO2019230626A1 and EP3804710A1, human perilla extract comparison
Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2019230626A1/en
Tables: https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3804710A1/en
Coverage: Original patent specification, human Example 2 and Tables 3–4

The December 2019 international application presents a two-period crossover in 14 healthy men aged 20–59. Each 28-day period used an oral commercial red-perilla water extract or a starch placebo. The capsules supplied 100 mg rosmarinic acid per day in the extract period. Washout was at least 28 days. A three-day voiding diary was collected before and after each period. Baseline nighttime voiding averaged 0.24 episodes, so the study began with little nocturia.

The European publication gives nighttime mean changes of +0.17 ± 0.08 on placebo and −0.07 ± 0.09 on extract. The treatment-minus-control change was −0.24 ± 0.11 episodes per night (p<.05). The daytime change was −0.52 ± 0.35 in Table 3 and −0.52 ± 0.53 in the prose, with both called nonsignificant. Table 4 found no significant total urine-volume difference. The patent gives no urgency rating or Parkinson subgroup. It identifies the commercial extract and plant variety preferences, with no verified plant part, fresh-leaf dose, or equivalent culinary exposure. Independent replication and participant-level results were not reported.
