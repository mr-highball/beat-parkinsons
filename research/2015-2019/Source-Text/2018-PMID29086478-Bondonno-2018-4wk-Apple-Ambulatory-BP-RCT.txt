Title: Flavonoid-Rich Apple Improves Endothelial Function in Individuals at Risk for Cardiovascular Disease: A Randomized Controlled Clinical Trial.
PMID: 29086478
DOI: 10.1002/mnfr.201700674
First published: 2018
Authors: Bondonno NP, Bondonno CP, Blekkenhorst LC, Considine MJ, Maghzal G, Stocker R et al.
Journal: Molecular nutrition & food research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29086478/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled crossover trial in n=30 adults at CVD risk comparing acute and 4-week intake of high-flavonoid apple (with skin) vs low-flavonoid apple flesh only. High-flavonoid apple improved endothelial FMD acutely and at 4 weeks, but produced NO significant change in 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure or arterial stiffness, and did not evaluate orthostatic lightheadedness.
