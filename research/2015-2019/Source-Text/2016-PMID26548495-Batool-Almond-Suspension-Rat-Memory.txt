Title: Repeated administration of almonds increases brain acetylcholine levels and enhances memory function in healthy rats while attenuates memory deficits in animal model of amnesia.
PMID: 26548495
DOI: 10.1016/j.brainresbull.2015.11.001
First published: 2016
Authors: Batool, Sadir, Liaquat, Tabassum, Madiha
Journal: Brain research bulletin
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26548495/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

28-day controlled rat experiment administering oral almond suspension (400 mg/kg/d) before saline or scopolamine (0.5 mg/kg) challenge. Almond improved retention in elevated plus maze, Morris water maze, and novel object recognition and elevated cortical/hippocampal acetylcholine. Corroborated by Batool 2018 (PMID 29429075, passive avoidance) and Kulkarni 2010 (PMID 20871769, 150-600 mg/kg paste). Limits: rodent amnesia models, no PD or clinical executive/attention score.
