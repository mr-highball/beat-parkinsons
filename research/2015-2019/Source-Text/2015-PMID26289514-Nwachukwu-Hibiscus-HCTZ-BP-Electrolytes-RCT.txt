Title: Effect of Hibiscus sabdariffa on blood pressure and electrolyte profile of mild to moderate hypertensive Nigerians: A comparative study with hydrochlorothiazide
PMID: 26289514
DOI: 10.4103/1119-3077.163278
First published: 2015
Authors: Nwachukwu DC, Aneke E, Nwachukwu NZ, Obika LF, Nwachukwu UI, Anigbogu CN
Journal: Nigerian Journal of Clinical Practice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26289514/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Randomized controlled trial in 80 newly diagnosed untreated mild-to-moderate hypertensive adults in Enugu, Nigeria assigned to aqueous H. sabdariffa, hydrochlorothiazide (HCTZ), or placebo for 4 weeks with 1-week withdrawal follow-up. Compared with placebo, both HCTZ and H. sabdariffa significantly reduced SBP, DBP, mean arterial pressure, and serum Na+ (P<0.001), without inducing the urinary K+ and Cl- wasting seen with HCTZ (P<0.001). BP and serum Na+ remained lower after 1-week withdrawal in the hibiscus group. No orthostatic standing BP or urinary urgency questionnaire outcomes.
