Title: Aberrant cerebral network topology and mild cognitive impairment in early Parkinson's disease.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6869566/
PMID: 25950288
PMCID: PMC6869566
DOI: 10.1002/hbm.22822
Publication date: 2015-05-06
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC complete article HTML.

The PPMI analysis compares brain-network organization among cognitive and imaging groups in early Parkinson disease. Its clinical battery includes tests used to assess attention and executive function. The analysis has no roasted turkey exposure or feeding arm.
