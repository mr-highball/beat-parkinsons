Title: Reversible Parkinsonism and Rapidly Progressive Dementia Due to Dural Arteriovenous Fistula: Case Series and Literature Review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6174482/
PMCID: PMC6174482
PMID: 30363443
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12480
Publication date: 2017-03-27
Date basis: NLM and PMC electronic date
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Format: Factual digest of the inspected material

Two patients with secondary parkinsonism from dural arteriovenous fistula had hallucinations during their vascular illness. Their timelines include symptom improvement following embolization, a recurrence after the first procedure in one case, and medication changes. There is no idiopathic Parkinson dietary comparison.

Case 1's visual and other symptoms improved following a first embolization and then recurred while off levodopa. Repeat angiography found a residual shunt. Hallucinations resolved after further fistula treatment. Case 2 reports both visual and auditory phenomena during illness. These are secondary parkinsonian presentations and clinical observations, with no food intervention or visual episode count.
