Mohan and colleagues, original animal results

Original source https://nopr.niscpr.res.in/handle/123456789/31440

The 2015 study gave an ethanolic coriander seed extract orally for 15 days in tacrine-treated rats, with five animals per group. It reports fewer induced vacuous chewing, tongue protrusions and orofacial bursts. These are excess movement outcomes after a seed extract. The study did not measure masked expression, dried cilantro leaves or patients with Parkinson disease.
