Title: Dietary nitrate improves sprint performance and cognitive function during prolonged intermittent exercise.
PMID: 25846114
DOI: 10.1007/s00421-015-3166-0
First published: 2015
Authors: Thompson C, Wylie LJ, Fulford J, Kelly J, Black MI, McDonagh ST, et al.
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25846114/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=16 male team-sport athletes given 140 mL/d BRJ vs placebo for 7 days: significantly faster Stroop/decision reaction time during intermittent sprint exercise (p<0.05) without accuracy loss. Limits: young athletic non-PD sample.
