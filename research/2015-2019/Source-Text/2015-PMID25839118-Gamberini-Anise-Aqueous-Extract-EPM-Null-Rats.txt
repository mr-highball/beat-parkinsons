Title: Effects of the aqueous extract of Pimpinella anisum L. seeds on exploratory activity and emotional behavior in rats using the open field and elevated plus maze tests.
PMID: 25839118
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2015.03.053
First published: 2015
Authors: Gamberini MT, Rodrigues DS, Rodrigues D, Pontes VB
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25839118/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical behavioral study in rats administering oral freeze-dried aqueous extract of Pimpinella anisum L. seeds (0.5, 1.0, or 2.0 g/kg p.o.) vs water control (5 mL/kg) on open-field test (OFT) and elevated plus maze (EPM). On EPM, NO alterations in open/closed arm entries or time were observed at any dose relative to controls; on OFT, 0.5 g/kg reduced rearing, 1.0 g/kg delayed exploration onset without impairing general activity, and 2.0 g/kg impaired within-session habituation. Authors explicitly concluded results failed to support anxiolytic/tranquilizing activity of aqueous anise seed extract.
