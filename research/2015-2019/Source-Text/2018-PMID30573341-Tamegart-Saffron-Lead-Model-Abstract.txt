Title: Crocus sativus restores dopaminergic and noradrenergic damages induced by lead in Meriones shawi: A possible link with Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30573341/
PMID: 30573341
DOI: 10.1016/j.acthis.2018.12.003
Publication date: 2018-12-17
Date basis: Electronic publication in the original PubMed record; print issue in 2019
Review depth: abstract
Checked: 2026-11-24

The original abstract describes oral gavage of saffron hydroethanolic extract in a lead-toxicity rodent model. The authors report preservation or recovery of tyrosine hydroxylase measures and locomotor activity relative to lead exposure. The abstract supplies no depression, insomnia, passive rigidity or fatigue comparison. The extract differs from ground saffron and the model is lead intoxication.
