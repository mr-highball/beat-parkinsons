Title: Parkinson's disease, visual hallucinations and apomorphine: A review of the available evidence
Source: https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1494594/
PMID: 27132157
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2016.04.023
Publication date: 2016-04-22
Source depth: complete author-institution review manuscript; locally distilled

The review traces the observational and clinical literature on Parkinson visual hallucinations and apomorphine. It discusses early case observations, concomitant medication and possible mechanisms. The authors identify the absence of a dedicated prospectively designed randomized efficacy trial in the evidence reviewed at that time. The cited studies remain the evidence units. This review supplies no additional patient sample, randomized outcome or dried fig comparison.
