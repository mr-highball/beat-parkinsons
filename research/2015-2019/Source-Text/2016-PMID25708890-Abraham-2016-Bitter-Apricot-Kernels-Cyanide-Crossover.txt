Title: Bioavailability of cyanide after consumption of a single meal of foods containing high levels of cyanogenic glycosides: a crossover study in humans.
PMID: 25708890
PMCID: PMC4754328
DOI: 10.1007/s00204-015-1479-8
First published: 2016
Authors: Abraham K, Buhrke T, Lampen A
Journal: Archives of toxicology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25708890/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Human crossover toxicokinetic study in n=12 healthy adults consuming foods equivalent to 6.8 mg cyanide, including bitter apricot kernels (~3,250 mg/kg total cyanide). Bitter apricot kernels produced rapid, high systemic blood cyanide bioavailability peaks (~20 min). Essential food-part safety boundary distinguishing edible Prunus armeniaca fruit flesh from toxic cyanogenic bitter kernels/amygdalin.
