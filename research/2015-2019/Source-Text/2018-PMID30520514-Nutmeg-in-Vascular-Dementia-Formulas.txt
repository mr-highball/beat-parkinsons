The review maps RouDouKou to Myristica fragrans seed and lists it in 25 Wei ZhenZhuWan with other herbs and nonplant components. Its included trials address vascular dementia and the summary reports cognition and related outcomes. A listing of nutmeg within a formulation leaves its preparation and individual attribution unresolved. The reported results cannot estimate the effect of ground nutmeg on constipation or insomnia severity.

Claim 0168 | PMID:30520514 | Tables list seed nutmeg in a multi-ingredient formulation and review dementia trials. The review does not isolate a ground nutmeg effect on constipation.

Claim 0774 | PMID:30520514 | Seed nutmeg appears in a multi-ingredient vascular dementia formulation. The review provides no separate culinary nutmeg insomnia comparison.
