Frozen red raspberry registration and metabolic outcomes
https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02479035

First posted June 23, 2015, as dated by ClinicalTrials.gov. The completed crossover registration evaluates plasma insulin, glucose, other metabolites, oxidative markers and inflammatory markers following meals with and without red raspberry. The public registry has no posted results and does not list an anxiety measure. The published Xiao 2019 report names this registration.
