Title: Beneficial effects of lycopene against haloperidol induced orofacial dyskinesia in rats

The author abstract reports fewer drug-induced orofacial dyskinetic movements and better coordination, motor activity and grip measures with isolated lycopene. It lists chewing, tongue and facial movements, rotarod, grip and narrow-beam tests. None is a separate rigidity measure. The 2018 congress abstract describes the same intervention pattern and reported outcomes. Treat it as the same candidate lineage pending full cohort details. Full tables, arm sizes, numerical effects and uncertainty by group are unavailable in the inspected abstract.

Claim 0544 asks about cooked tomato and Parkinson muscle rigidity. This report contributes isolated-constituent and animal outcome context.
