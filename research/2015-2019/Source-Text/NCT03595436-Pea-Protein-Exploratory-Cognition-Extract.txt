# Pea protein breakfast registration and exploratory cognition

Source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03595436
Coverage official study narrative and outcomes

NCT03595436 first posted July 23, 2018. The completed registry reports actual enrollment of 40 and breakfast conditions varying pea protein content alongside whey and carbohydrate. Its narrative mentions executive function, without posted cognitive outcome results. The population and isolated protein preparations differ from cooked green peas in Parkinson’s.
