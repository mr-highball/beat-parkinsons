Title: Cryptosporidium parvum Caused a Large Outbreak Linked to Frisee Salad in Finland, 2012.
PMID: 25807859
DOI: 10.1111/zph.12190
First published: 2015
Authors: Aberg R, Sjoman M, Hemminki K, Pirnes A, Rasanen S, Kalanti A, Pohjanvirta T, Caccio SM
Journal: Zoonoses and public health
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25807859/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract Facts: Epidemiological outbreak investigation in Finland (>250 gastroenteritis cases across 5 outbreaks, RR 2.66, 95% CI 1.02-6.9, p<0.01) tracing watery diarrhea and abdominal cramps to ready-to-eat raw frisee (curly endive, Cichorium endivia) salad contaminated with Cryptosporidium parvum IIdA17G1. Food-hygiene safety context for raw leafy salads; does not establish an intrinsic effect of clean raw endive on Parkinson constipation or GI motility.
