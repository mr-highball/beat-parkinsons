Coverage: Original Methods, Table 1, Results and Figure 1 caption in author-posted article; publisher confirmed identity and online date.

Three randomly assigned groups of eight female mice comprised uninduced controls, induced EAE receiving 100 microliters phosphate-buffered saline daily, and induced EAE receiving walnut oil 5 mL/kg daily by gavage. The oil was cold-pressed from dried Juglans regia kernels. Treatment began at first clinical symptoms and continued for 21 days. The 0–8 EAE scale covers tail weakness and paralysis, limb weakness and paralysis, and death. The authors report average clinical scores of 2.38 ± 0.56 with oil versus 3.00 ± 0.69 with saline, p < 0.05. They do not specify in the Results prose whether the reported average covers the entire 21-day period or a single time point. There was no olive oil group. This was an inflammatory demyelination model, not a Parkinson or dystonia experiment.
