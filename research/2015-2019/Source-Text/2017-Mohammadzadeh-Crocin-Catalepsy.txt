Injected crocin and rat motor outcomes
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29204935/
original abstract paraphrase

First published December 4, 2017. Crocin pretreatment was given by injection in a malathion rat model. The primary abstract lists catalepsy, open field and rotarod and broadly reports attenuation of neurobehavioral effects. The separate catalepsy effect size is unavailable at abstract depth. The isolated injected constituent and toxin model differ from ground saffron and clinical rigidity.

The public publisher preview exposes Figure 3. Its bar-chart descent latency is roughly 58 seconds with malathion alone, about 2 seconds with vehicle, and roughly 7 to 12 seconds in the three crocin pretreatment groups. The chart annotation and full methods remain incompletely accessible. Values are visual approximations. This is an animal catalepsy proxy after injected isolated crocin.
