Original animal study abstract

Original source https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28413647/

The original Figure 1 reports a 24.6% longer time to exhaustion after oral concentrated Nigella seed extract versus water in the 2017 rat experiment, with ten animals per group. Its statistical description calls the test paired despite separate group labels. The extraction, animal population, experimental exercise exposure and time to exhaustion differ from ground seed and reported fatigue severity in Parkinson disease. The original result supports an extract performance lead with uncertain transfer to subjective fatigue.
