Title: Effect of Multinutrient Supplementation and Food-Related Behavioral Activation Therapy on Prevention of Major Depressive Disorder Among Overweight or Obese Adults With Subsyndromal Depressive Symptoms: The MooDFOOD Randomized Clinical Trial.
PMID: 30835307
PMCID: PMC6439597
DOI: 10.1001/jama.2019.0556
Registry ID: NCT02529423
First published: 2019
Authors: Bot M, Brouwer IA, Roca M, Kohls E, Penninx BWJH, Watkins E, et al.
Journal: JAMA
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30835307/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-month multicenter randomized factorial trial (MooDFOOD) in n=1,025 overweight adults with subsyndromal depressive symptoms comparing daily multinutrient supplement (containing selenium 30 ug/d plus omega-3/folate/vitamin D/calcium) vs placebo: no reduction in major depressive episodes or depressive symptom scores (null).
