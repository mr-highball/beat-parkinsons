Butylphthalide gait and tremor composite table check
Source: https://doi.org/10.3892/etm.2019.7397
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: factual extract

Zhou and colleagues randomized 131 people with Parkinson disease to usual care with or without oral pharmaceutical DL-3-n-butylphthalide. Final follow-up included 43 for drug plus care and 60 for care only. The off-medication non-tremor UPDRS III subtotal includes gait, with no timed walk or separate gait item. The tremor subtotal groups rest and action or postural items. Original Table II repeats the complete PDQ-39 numeric row under tremor in HTML and PDF, which prevents treating those figures as reliable tremor estimates. The trial used a synthetic product with historical seed discovery, without an oral celery-stalk exposure. The registration number ChiCTR1800018892 is reported by the paper. A matching public original protocol was not verified.

Follow-up of the original PDF confirmed that motor assessments were performed in the morning after 12 hours off dopaminergic medication. The stated tremor subtotal combines items 20 and 21. Besides the duplicated PDQ-39 and tremor rows, the printed baseline sleep values in Table II duplicate the Hamilton depression baseline in Table I rather than its sleep baseline. A targeted DOI and PMID correction search did not find a published correction. The separately reported rest item remains unverified.
