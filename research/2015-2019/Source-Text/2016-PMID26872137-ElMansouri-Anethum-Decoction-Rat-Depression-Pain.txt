Title: Phytochemical Screening, Antidepressant and Analgesic Effects of Aqueous Extract of Anethum graveolens L. From Southeast of Morocco.
PMID: 26872137
DOI: 10.1097/MJT.0000000000000090
First published: 2016
Authors: El Mansouri L, Bousta D, El Youbi-El Hamsas A, Boukhira S, Akdime H
Journal: American journal of therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26872137/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical Wistar rat study (El Mansouri et al., 2016, Am J Ther) testing oral aqueous decoction of Moroccan Anethum graveolens (250 mg/kg and 1 g/kg p.o.) vs sertraline and tramadol, reporting significant antidepressant-like and analgesic activity (250 mg/kg stronger than 1 g/kg) without adverse effects. Limits: abstract does not specify plant part (seed vs aerial herb), sample sizes, behavioral tests, numeric means, or p-values; non-PD healthy rodents.
