Lemon balm and Nepeta blend in insomnia with depressive symptoms
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.imr.2018.08.001
Coverage: factual extract of primary Methods and Table 1
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Ranjbar and colleagues randomized 67 adults with insomnia and BDI > 10. With per-protocol analysis, Table 1 BDI fell from 25.86 to 16.26 versus 24.18 to 21.40 after four weeks with placebo (repeated-measures p < .001). Active capsules combine 1,000 mg lemon balm and 400 mg Nepeta menthoides. The comparison cannot allocate effects to either ingredient.
