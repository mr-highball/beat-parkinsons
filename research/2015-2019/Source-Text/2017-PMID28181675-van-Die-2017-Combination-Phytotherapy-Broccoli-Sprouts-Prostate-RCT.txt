Title: A Placebo-Controlled Double-Blinded Randomized Pilot Study of Combination Phytotherapy in Biochemically Recurrent Prostate Cancer.
PMID: 28181675
PMCID: PMC5444299
DOI: 10.1002/pros.23317
First published: 2017
Authors: van Die MD, Williams SG, Emery J, Bone KM, Taylor JM, Lusk E
Journal: The Prostate
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5444299/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week double-blind placebo-controlled feasibility RCT in n=22 men with biochemically recurrent prostate cancer taking a 4-ingredient combination (turmeric, resveratrol, green tea, broccoli sprouts) vs placebo. Well tolerated; no significant between-group differences on IPSS urinary symptoms, HADS anxiety/depression, or PSA doubling time. Bundled multi-herb pilot.
