Title: Aberrant resting-state oscillatory brain activity in Parkinson's disease patients with visual hallucinations: An MEG source-space study.
PMID: 30897434
PMCID: PMC6425119
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2019.101752
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6425119/
First publication date: 2019-03-13 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The study compares resting MEG oscillatory power between Parkinson patients with visual-only, multimodal or absent hallucinations. Frequency of neural oscillations and reported visual episode frequency are separate concepts. No fig preparation was administered.
