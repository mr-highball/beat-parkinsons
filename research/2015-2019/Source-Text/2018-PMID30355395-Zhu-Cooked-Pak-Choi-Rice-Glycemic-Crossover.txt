Title: Acute effects of non-homogenised and homogenised vegetables added to rice-based meals on postprandial glycaemic responses and in vitro carbohydrate digestion.
PMID: 30355395
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114518002489
First published: 2018
Authors: Zhu R, Liu M, Han Y, Wang L, Ye T, Lu J, et al.
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30355395/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized crossover trial (Zhu et al., 2018, Br J Nutr) in 16 healthy volunteers completing 13 acute meal sessions including non-homogenized cooked pak choi (Brassica rapa subsp. chinensis) + rice vs pure rice and homogenized variants. Non-homogenized cooked pak choi + rice lowered acute glycemic index to 71 and starch hydrolysis index to 83.8% of pure rice control, whereas homogenized pak choi showed no difference. Acute healthy-adult carbohydrate digestion test; no Parkinson's motor, GI, or non-motor symptoms evaluated.
