Title: Effects of Apium graveolens Extract on the Oxidative Stress in the Liver of Adjuvant-Induced Arthritic Rats.
PMID: 27390722
PMCID: PMC4935245
DOI: 10.3746/pnf.2016.21.2.79
First published: 2016
Authors: Sukketsiri W, Chonpathompikunlert P, Tanasawet S, Choosri N, Wongtawatchai T
Journal: Prev Nutr Food Sci
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27390722/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical study (same Thai laboratory as PMC5859653) giving whole-plant methanolic Apium graveolens extract (250, 500, 1,000 mg/kg oral x 24 d) to complete Freund adjuvant-induced arthritic rats. Reduced hepatic superoxide anion, total peroxide, and oxidative stress index and raised hepatic SOD/GPx. Measured liver oxidative biomarkers only—no behavioral pain, joint tenderness, or PD motor outcomes.
