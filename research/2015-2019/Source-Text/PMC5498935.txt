The age-stratified comparison reports memory, executive and visuoconstructional tests in 202 participants with dementia with Lewy bodies and 236 with Alzheimer disease. Visual hallucinations are a diagnostic phenotype discussed alongside cognitive results. The paper gives no Parkinson dried fig exposure or visual episode-rate comparison.
