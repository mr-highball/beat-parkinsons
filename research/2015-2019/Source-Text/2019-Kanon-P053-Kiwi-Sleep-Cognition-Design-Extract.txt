Kanon conference design for kiwi, sleep and cognition
Original 2019 P053 conference abstract. DOI 10.1111/jsr.52_12913.
Kanon and colleagues’ 2019 conference abstract describes a proposed crossover with measurements after the first and fourteenth nights, plus a washout. It lists polysomnography and mood and cognitive testing. The plan gives no results. The separate acute kiwi and later planned freeze-dried COMPASS registrations identify different measurements or time frames. The 2019 document supplies no shared registration to establish study identity.
