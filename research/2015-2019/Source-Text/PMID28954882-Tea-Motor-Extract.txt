Factual extract from PMID28954882, PMCID PMC5664303, DOI 10.1212/WNL.0000000000004568. Postuma et al. Caffeine as symptomatic treatment for Parkinson disease (Café-PD): a randomized trial. Neurology. 2017;89:1795–1803. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5664303/.
Sixty of 121 patients were assigned caffeine 200 mg twice daily and 61 matched placebo. The six-month motor examination outcome had an estimated −0.48-point difference (95% CI −3.21 to 2.25). The authors report inconclusive individual and total Part II outcomes and provide no separate numerical rest-tremor or measured walking-speed result. Part II queries daily-living tremor impact and walking or balance. The study tested capsules of caffeine in patients on stable medication.
