Title: Phillips et al. 2018, notes on scrambled egg menu options and visual hallucinations.
Primary article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175383/
Source IDs: PMID 30098269, PMC6175383, DOI 10.1002/mds.27390
Population: 47 participants randomized, 44 started and 38 completed. Participants had Parkinson disease.
Exposure: Ketogenic meal plan recipe printed p. 59 and weeks 2 and 4 day 7 menus show a spinach, cheddar and egg scramble. The low-fat plan printed p. 62 includes scrambled egg on toast. Individual meals were not documented in the outcome report.
Event: One ketogenic-arm participant experienced visual hallucinations that resolved after reducing pramipexole. No date relative to egg intake, individual food log, comparison by egg consumption or visual hallucination frequency for this participant is reported.
