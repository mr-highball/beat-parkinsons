Title: Medical Record Review to Differentiate between Idiopathic Parkinson's Disease and Parkinsonism: A Danish Record Linkage Study with 10 Years of Follow-Up

PMCID: PMC4681800

Publication date: 2015-12-03
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary chart review and record linkage
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Systematic chart review of 2,446 people in a Danish hospital register distinguished idiopathic Parkinson disease from other forms of parkinsonism. The protocol considered hallucinations unrelated to medication and timing of cognition relative to motor symptoms when adjudicating diagnoses. Those criteria do not provide a dried fig exposure or clinical visual episode measure.
