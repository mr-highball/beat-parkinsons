Title: Efficacy and safety of add-on butylphthalide in early and moderate Parkinson disease
Source: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=ChiCTR-IPR-16009395
Registry identifier: ChiCTR-IPR-16009395
First public registration: 2016-10-14
Last registry update shown: 2017-04-18
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Factual extract of official WHO ICTRP registry

The registration plans a two-arm early and moderate Parkinson
comparison with 36 participants per group and a 24-week
pharmaceutical butylphthalide add-on versus no add-on. It
records planned first enrollment on 2016-11-01. The outcomes
include the UPDRS I–III total and a portable evaluation device
without a separate resting-tremor item identified in the record.

WHO lists pending status and blank result fields. The planned
sample and registration differ from the 2019 Zhou study, whose
record is ChiCTR1800018892. The record supplies no result for a
culinary celery preparation.
