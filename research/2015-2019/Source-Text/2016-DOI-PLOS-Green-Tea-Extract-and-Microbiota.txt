Title: Long-Term Green Tea Supplementation Does Not Change the Human Gut Microbiota
Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0153134
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0153134
Publication date: 2016-04-07
Date basis: PLOS ONE publication date.
Collection: Source text
Review depth: full_text, main text, flow figure and outcome tables
Checked: 2026-11-25

Sixty-five adults began the trial and 58 provided analyzable
baseline and week-12 microbiota data. The assigned comparison
was green-tea extract in capsules and placebo capsules. The
paper reports no statistically significant treatment-by-time
interaction in microbiota diversity or body composition. This
distinguishes an inconclusive measured contrast from a positive
effect and from a claim that the two groups were equivalent.

This trial is reference 233 in the 2018 dietary microbiota review
(PMID 29886183). The participants did not form a Parkinson cohort.
The trial did not administer brewed tea or collect fatigue severity
as the mapped symptom outcome.
