NCT02598726. First posted November 6, 2015, date type estimated. Official record https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02598726.
The phase I study investigated curcumin plus a standardized piperine product in cancer patients with ureteral stents. The design is single-group and the official enrollment field gives nine actual participants. The secondary Ureteral Stent Symptom Questionnaire assesses symptoms and quality of life. The official record is completed and reports no posted results as of November 16, 2026. This is a registration without a separate clinical effect estimate for piperine or a culinary exposure.
