Study design and population
- Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, three-arm parallel-group clinical trial conducted in 90 older adults with age-associated memory impairment (AAMI; 198 screened; N = 90 randomized ITT, n = 30 per arm; 67% female; mean age 59.4 +- 0.6 years; recruited August 2013 to January 2014).
- Funded by Kemin Foods, L.C.; conducted with Biofortis Research Services/Merieux NutriSciences.
- Three participants discontinued due to adverse events; detailed safety outcomes were reported separately (Lasrado et al.).

Intervention and comparator (90 days)
- Arms: 900 mg/day dried aqueous spearmint (Mentha spicata L.) extract high in polyphenols including rosmarinic acid, 600 mg/day spearmint extract, or appearance-matched placebo (two capsules once daily with breakfast for 90 days).
- Compliance was 98.1% +- 0.9% (placebo), 99.1% +- 0.8% (600 mg/day), and 100.1% +- 0.4% (900 mg/day).

Verified cognitive, sleep, and mood outcomes (Tables 3, 4, and 5)
- Cognition (CDR System, days 0, 45, 90; MMRM change from baseline):
  - Quality of working memory: overall p = 0.0435; 900 mg/day improved vs placebo by ~15% (day 0 1.61 -> day 90 1.81 vs placebo 1.72 -> 1.82; p = 0.0469; 900 vs 600 mg p = 0.0212).
  - Spatial working memory accuracy: 900 mg/day improved vs placebo by ~9% (p = 0.0456).
  - Power of attention (msec): NULL (overall p = 0.9312).
  - Continuity of attention: NULL (overall p = 0.8881).
  - Quality of episodic secondary memory (p = 0.8082), speed of memory (p = 0.6460), quality of memory (p = 0.2296): NULL.
- Sleep (Leeds Sleep Evaluation Questionnaire, LSEQ, days 0 and 90):
  - Ease of getting to sleep: overall F(2,82) = 4.29, p = 0.0170; 900 mg/day (47.63 -> 51.11 mm) improved vs placebo (49.83 -> 45.48 mm; t(82) = 2.91, p = 0.0046, d = 0.805).
  - Alertness and behavior following wakefulness: overall F(2,82) = 3.31, p = 0.0415; 900 mg vs 600 mg t(82) = -2.52, p = 0.0137.
  - Quality of sleep (p = 0.4267) and ease of awakening from sleep (p = 0.9016): NULL.
- Mood (Profile of Mood States, POMS, days 0 and 90):
  - Vigor-activity: overall F(2,82) = 3.35, p = 0.0399; 900 vs 600 mg t(82) = -2.49, p = 0.0149, d = 0.729; 900 mg vs placebo was NOT statistically significant (t(82) = 1.87, reported p = 0.0646 [printed as 0.646 in text]).
  - Total Mood Disturbance (TMD): overall F(2,82) = 3.42, p = 0.0374; pairwise comparisons vs placebo not statistically significant.
  - Tension-anxiety (p = 0.3411), depression-dejection (p = 0.0862), anger-hostility (p = 0.1935), fatigue-inertia (p = 0.2111), confusion-bewilderment (p = 0.8448): NULL.

Relevance and limits for Parkinson's food experiments
- No pain efficacy endpoint was tested (relevant exclusion/context for 0728 spearmint / pain severity).
- Provides direct randomized human clinical evidence on sleep onset (ease of getting to sleep p = 0.0046), null attention (p = 0.9312 / 0.8881), and null fatigue-inertia (p = 0.2111) for aqueous spearmint extract in older adults.
- Limits: Sponsor-funded (Kemin Foods, L.C.); proprietary high-rosmarinic-acid dried aqueous extract capsules rather than fresh/dried culinary spearmint; non-Parkinson AAMI cohort; baseline numerical imbalances on working memory and mood.
