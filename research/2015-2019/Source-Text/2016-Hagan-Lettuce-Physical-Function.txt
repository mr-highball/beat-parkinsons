Grouped lettuce intake and later physical function
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4926850/
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: original Methods and Table 3

Hagan and colleagues followed 54,762 women in the Nurses’ Health Study. In Table 3, the combined romaine or leaf lettuce intake category at two or more servings weekly versus less than monthly had adjusted HR 0.88 (95% CI 0.83–0.93) for incident SF-36 physical impairment. The composite includes gross mobility and activity items without handwriting or fine motor testing. The observational exposure did not isolate raw romaine, and no Parkinson stratum was analyzed.
