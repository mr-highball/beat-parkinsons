ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03986307, institutional study S61809. First posted June 14, 2019.
Official current record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03986307
Coverage: Factual registration fields and linkage to original paper.

Twenty-three older adults were assigned to omega-3 or appearance-matched corn-oil capsules for 14 weeks, with resistance exercise in the final 12 weeks in both arms. The registered Other outcome Sleep uses the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index before and after the intervention. The trial lists completed status without structured results. The directly matched Dalle article, DOI 10.1093/gerona/glaa309, and its supplement report no PSQI outcome. Registration was posted after the recorded May 2019 completion. The oil processing is unreported.
