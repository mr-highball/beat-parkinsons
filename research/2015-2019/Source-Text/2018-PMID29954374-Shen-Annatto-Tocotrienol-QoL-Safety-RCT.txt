Title: A 12-week evaluation of annatto tocotrienol supplementation for postmenopausal women: safety, quality of life, body composition, physical activity, and nutrient intake.
PMID: 29954374
PMCID: PMC6022510
DOI: 10.1186/s12906-018-2263-0
Registry ID: NCT02058420
First published: 2018
Authors: Shen, Wang, Yang, Tomison, Abbasi
Journal: BMC complementary and alternative medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29954374/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-week double-blind 3-arm RCT (NCT02058420; companion bone-turnover report PMID 29330573) in 89 randomized / 87 completed postmenopausal osteopenic women assigned to placebo (430 mg olive oil/d), Low TT (300 mg/d annatto DeltaGold tocotrienol, 90% delta-/10% gamma-TT), or High TT (600 mg/d). Repeated-measures ANOVA showed no statistically significant between-group differences on any SF-36 quality-of-life domain (Table 4): Bodily Pain p = 0.761 (Placebo 71.43->72.22, Low 68.28->71.07, High 71.00->71.00), Vitality p = 0.079 (Placebo 66.29->65.97, Low 63.58->67.63, High 66.46->73.54), Mental Health p = 0.637, Physical Function, Role-Emotional p = 0.818, General Health p = 0.754. No treatment-related adverse events or liver/kidney abnormalities. Limits: isolated seed tocotrienol extract in non-PD postmenopausal women without baseline pain/fatigue/depression.
