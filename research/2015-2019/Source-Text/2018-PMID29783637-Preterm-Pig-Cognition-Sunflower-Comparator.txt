Title: A Phosphatidylserine Source of Docosahexanoic Acid Improves Neurodevelopment and Survival of Preterm Pigs
Publication date: 2018-05-18
PMID: 29783637
PMCID: PMC5986516
DOI: 10.3390/nu10050637
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5986516/
Text coverage: Primary indexed abstract.

Preterm pigs were fed formulas containing phosphatidylserine-bound DHA or a sunflower-oil placebo. Term-born pigs supplied a developmental reference. The investigators report more mature sensory responses and better novel-object recognition for PS-DHA compared with sunflower formula. The developmental comparison and different lipid interventions do not establish whether sunflower affects executive performance, whether the oil was cold-pressed, or a Parkinson-specific effect.
