Title: Protection against 1-methyl-4-phenyl pyridinium-induced neurotoxicity in human neuroblastoma SH-SY5Y cells by Soyasaponin I by the activation of the phosphoinositide 3-kinase/AKT/GSK3β pathway.
PMID: 27196724
DOI: 10.1097/WNR.0000000000000603
First published: 2016
Authors: Guo Z, Cao W, Zhao S, Han Z, Han B
Journal: Neuroreport
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27196724/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

In vitro cellular study testing purified Soyasaponin I (10-40 uM, extracted from Glycine max) in MPP+-treated SH-SY5Y cells. Increased cell viability, reduced ROS and Bax/Bcl-2 ratio, and restored phosphorylated AKT/GSK-3beta in a PI3K-dependent manner (abrogated by LY294002). Purified compound in vitro only; unusable as clinical evidence for cooked or steamed edamame.
