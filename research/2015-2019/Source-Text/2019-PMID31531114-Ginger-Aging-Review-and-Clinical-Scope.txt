Original study scope
The original ginger review tables separate laboratory, animal and human evidence. Its Parkinson rows concern isolated 6-shogaol and neural or inflammatory outcomes. Other reported human studies address different indications, including metabolic endpoints. Turmeric is a separate botanical context.

Continuing evidence gaps
Find direct graded rigidity and pain outcomes after grated ginger in Parkinson disease. Neither the constituent work nor metabolic human studies resolve those outcomes.
