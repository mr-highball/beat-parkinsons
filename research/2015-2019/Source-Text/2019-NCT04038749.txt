Title: Postpartum lactation inhibition methods and breast pain
First posted: 2019-07-31
Registration: NCT04038749
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04038749
Text type: official-registry evidence summary

The Polish study lists bromocriptine, cabergoline and a bundle of natural measures. The bundle includes sage tea, breast expression, cold or cabbage compresses and mild analgesics. The record gives no sage species, amount or fresh versus dried preparation. The primary outcomes include a 0–10 pain scale monitored through day 14. Structured fields say randomized, while the protocol narrative describes participant choice of medication. The study has unknown status and the public record supplied no posted pain estimates at the November 17, 2026 check. The design gives no separate sage comparison.
