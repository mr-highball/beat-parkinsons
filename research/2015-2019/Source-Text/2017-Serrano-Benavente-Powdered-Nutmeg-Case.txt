Intention tremor in an earlier powdered nutmeg case
https://www.revista-portalesmedicos.com/revista-medica/intoxicacion-nuez-moscada/

April 4, 2017. Serrano Benavente and Pérez Ruiz report one adult with hand intention tremor after excessive powdered nutmeg with diazepam. They report ongoing fluoxetine treatment and a positive benzodiazepine test. Two authors, the hospital, patient age, symptom timing, hand tremor and course align with a 2018 society report, making shared patient identity plausible. The reported amounts differ, and the 2018 account lists no chronic treatment and a negative drug screen. The reports have no explicit linkage and do not supply an isolated nutmeg effect, a rest-tremor comparison or a Parkinson population.
