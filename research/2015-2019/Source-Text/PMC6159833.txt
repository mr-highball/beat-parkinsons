Title: The Pentagon Copying Test and the Clock Drawing Test as Prognostic Markers in Dementia with Lewy Bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6159833/
PMID: 29996144
PMCID: PMC6159833
DOI: 10.1159/000490045
Publication date: 2018-07-11
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML article body; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC metadata and PubMed.
Design: Clinical prognostic drawing-test cohort

The authors retrospectively collected drawing measures for 103 people with probable dementia with Lewy bodies. Prognosis was compared by scores on pentagon and clock drawing. The assessments do not provide a controlled food exposure or repeated visual event outcome.
