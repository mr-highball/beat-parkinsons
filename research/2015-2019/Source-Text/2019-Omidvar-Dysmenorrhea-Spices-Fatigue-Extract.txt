Omidvar et al., 2019. Clinical trial for the management dysmenorrhea using selected spices. Complementary Therapies in Clinical Practice, 2019.
Source: https://europepmc.org/article/MED/31383440
Access and coverage: Europe PMC and corrected PubMed query, indexed author abstract and sections previewed by the publisher. Full tables were not accessible in this pass.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

Thirty-one participants with dysmenorrhea were randomized to ginger, dill seed or cumin. The abstract attributes relief of pain to dill seed and attributes a group of systemic symptom reductions including fatigue to cumin. It does not report a fatigue estimate for the dill seed arm. The exposure was seed in menstrual cycles, without culinary dill foliage or Parkinson disease.
