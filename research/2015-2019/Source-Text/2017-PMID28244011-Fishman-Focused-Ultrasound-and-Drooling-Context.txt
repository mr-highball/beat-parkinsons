Fishman and Frenkel describe worsening of speech and drooling in literature on bilateral pallidal lesions. The original Intemann report provides the event context for four patients. Siegel and Metman primarily measured gait, and Counihan and colleagues reported motor function and activities of daily living. The review authors supply no raw-fig trial or estimated drooling response. Their discussion of bilateral lesions should be read alongside the techniques and outcomes in each cited paper.
