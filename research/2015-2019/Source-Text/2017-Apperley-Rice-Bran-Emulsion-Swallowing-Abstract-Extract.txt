Title: Rice bran emulsion and a solid swallowing test
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/joor.12545
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Apperley and colleagues, July 25, 2017. Forty adults with postradiation dry mouth were invited. The article abstract says participants tried the rice-bran emulsion, cellulose saliva substitute and water in randomized order. It reports no clear difference on Test of Masticating and Swallowing Solids outcomes at first use or after seven days. The overall xerostomia score differed by product (p = .02); the emulsion improved that score slightly (p < .01 within condition). The registry identifies a multi-ingredient spray and describes a different planned allocation. There was no Parkinson or refined edible oil contrast.
