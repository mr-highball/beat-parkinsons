Lemon balm capsule trial in adolescents with premenstrual symptoms
Source: https://doi.org/10.1515/ijamh-2017-0015
Coverage: factual extract of primary abstract
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Heydari and colleagues randomized 100 adolescent participants to lemon balm capsules or placebo over three cycles. The abstract concludes depression improved, yet the numerical comparisons listed concern psychosomatic symptoms, anxiety and sleep, and social function. The depression instrument row, exact preparation and effect remain unverified from the original tables.
