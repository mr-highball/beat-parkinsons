Reviewed: 2026-11-17

One hundred adolescents were allocated 50 per arm to capsules described as Melissa essence, 600 mg twice daily during menstruation across three cycles, or starch placebo. Table 2 gives post-treatment GHQ depression means 10.72 (SD 3.69) versus 12.50 (SD 4.47), with reported p=.001. Baseline psychological means are explicitly unavailable, so this is a post-treatment comparison rather than a measured reduction. The paper calls GHQ 29 questions but enumerates four seven-item domains.

Conditional calculation using actual analysis n=50 per arm and the printed SDs gives an ordinary independent t-test p approximately .0323, unreconciled with the printed value. Actual analysis denominators and raw observations are unavailable. This calculation is a sensitivity check, not a replacement published result.

IRCT2014060717998N1 and thesis 92-6805 also identify the 2015 and 2017-online PMS reports. Matching aggregate PSST trajectories support overlap between those reports; individual membership of this GHQ analysis remains unverified. Do not count three independent cohorts. Capsule composition, registry discrepancies and transfer to brewed tea in Parkinson disease remain unresolved.
