Title: Plant-derived flavanol (−)epicatechin mitigates anxiety in association with elevated hippocampal monoamine and BDNF levels, but does not influence pattern separation in mice

Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: full_text

Thirty adult male mice were assigned in groups of 15, counterbalanced on a prior training task. One group drank water with isolated epicatechin over 14 weeks. In Figure 2, total open-field distance was similar. The center-to-periphery distance ratio was 0.119 with epicatechin and 0.097 with plain water (p<0.03). The paper also reports a higher center-time ratio. In the elevated plus maze the authors compare open and closed arm times within each group. Those within-arm tests do not supply a between-group difference. The separate pattern-separation task had no clear improvement.

The constituent occurs in cocoa and other foods. This comparison uses purified epicatechin, normal mice and exploratory behavior. There is no measurement of plain chocolate intake or clinical anxiety severity in Parkinson disease. The study is separate from the cacao-mass rat study PMID:18976895.
