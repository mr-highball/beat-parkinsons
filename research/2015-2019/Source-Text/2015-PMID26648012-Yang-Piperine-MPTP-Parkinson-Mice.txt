Title: Neuroprotective effects of piperine on the 1-methyl-4-phenyl-1,2,3,6-tetrahydropyridine-induced Parkinson's disease mouse model.
PMID: 26648012
DOI: 10.3892/ijmm.2015.2356
First published: 2015
Authors: Yang W, Chen YH, Liu H, Qu HD
Journal: International journal of molecular medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26648012/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled MPTP PD mouse experiment administering oral piperine (10 mg/kg x 15 d including 8-d pretreatment). Piperine improved rotarod motor coordination and Morris water maze spatial learning, preserved SNpc TH+ dopaminergic neurons, and reduced microglial activation, IL-1beta, oxidative stress, and Bax/Bcl-2 apoptosis. Limits: isolated piperine pretreatment in MPTP mice.
