Title: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of Ficus carica paste for the management of functional constipation
Source: https://apjcn.qdu.edu.cn/25_3_25.pdf
PMID: 27440682
DOI: 10.6133/apjcn.092015.06
Trial registration: NCT02138851
Publication year: 2016
Citation: Baek et al. Asia Pacific Journal of Clinical Nutrition 25(3), 487–496.
Text coverage: Paraphrased methods and findings from the complete journal PDF, including Figure 1 and Tables 2, 4 and 5.

The study randomized 80 people aged 19–39 with Rome III functional constipation and baseline colon transit time of at least 36 hours. Neurological disorders were exclusionary. Forty per group were analyzed by intention to treat. Seventy-four completed the eight-week intervention (36 fig, 38 placebo). The supplied Ficus carica paste was standardized to 1.7% fiber. Table 2 reports 88.8% water. The placebo consisted primarily of water, sugar and modified starch. The preparation and endpoint differ from eating intact dried figs and tracking visual hallucinations.

Table 4 (p. 491) reports mean colon transit changes of -24.5 ± 22.4 hours for fig paste and -15.0 ± 17.7 hours for placebo. The between-group mixed-model p value is .030. The displayed baseline and week-eight means are 63.7 and 38.7 hours for paste and 61.6 and 46.7 hours for placebo. Percentage reductions printed in the preceding narrative are inconsistent with those means. The figures here preserve the table's reported change columns.

Table 5 (p. 492) reports stool consistency scores up 1.1 points for paste and 0.7 for placebo (between-group P=.024), and abdominal discomfort down 0.8 and 0.3 points (P=.012). Weekly bowel movements increased 1.6 and 1.2 respectively (P=.346). Abdominal pain also did not differ clearly between groups (P=.518). No serious adverse events were reported during the eight-week study. Generalization to Parkinson, other preparations and longer use is untested here.

Page 488 identifies NCT02138851 explicitly. The earlier NCT01185431 registration has a different sponsor-study identifier, sample size and time frame. Count this paper and NCT02138851 as the same result family. The earlier record has no posted outcome estimates.
