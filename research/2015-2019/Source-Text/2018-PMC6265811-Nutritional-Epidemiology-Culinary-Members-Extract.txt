P20-147 is a walnut evidence map: 362 abstracts screened, 70 publications included, with 58 whole-walnut, three oil and ten mixed-intervention classifications. The categories total 71; overlapping coding is not explained. The three oil publications are unidentified. This is not a new symptom trial.

P20-084 models Korean dietary acrylamide exposure using 1,000 individual recalls from 2011–2013. Adult mean exposure is .076 micrograms/kg body weight/day; pregnancy-trimester estimates are .060, .054 and .046. Black pepper leads adult contributors. These are modeled exposures, not measured Parkinson symptoms.

P20-150 describes egg dishes in children's intake; P20-066 concerns mixed potato preparations and mortality. Neither isolates the frozen preparation or symptom. OR40-06 concerns diet-quality indices and PHQ-9, without an oil intervention.

OR23–03, OR28–03 and OR40–04 are present with en dashes. Native identity checks and exact titles resolve apparent missing-member gaps; PDF replacement glyphs alone are not identity evidence.
