Published pediatric potato meal trial and missing sleepiness outcome
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30844296/
Coverage: Factual summary of author abstract and study identity fields.

First published March 7, 2019. The primary abstract reports 22 children and the same six conditions as NCT02820805. It describes fresh potatoes processed into mash, frozen and microwaved, with no documented boiling step. Word recall was higher after French fries than after mash, 9.1 versus 8.2 words (p=.001). The abstract does not give acute sleepiness data. The full Methods and item-level result remain unverified.
