Title: Effect of aqueous extract of the Vigna angularis in rats subjected to an experimental model of moderate chronic kidney disease.
PMID: 27579880
DOI: 10.1590/S0102-865020160080000005
First published: 2016
Authors: Baracho NC, Monteiro NF, Borges MG, Arguelho RR
Journal: Acta cirurgica brasileira
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27579880/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical rat study (Baracho et al., 2016, Acta Cir Bras) randomizing 30 surgically induced moderate chronic kidney disease rats for 14 days to distilled water control, 5% aqueous Vigna angularis extract, or 10 mg/kg enalapril maleate. Aqueous adzuki extract increased 24-hour urine output from day 2 through day 14 vs control (p<0.01) and enalapril (p<0.05), and lowered serum urea, creatinine (p<0.01), glucose, triglycerides, VLDL, uric acid, and ALT (p<0.05) while raising HDL. Limits: nephrectomy CKD diuresis model; urine volume increase does not equate to overactive bladder or Parkinson's urinary urgency.
