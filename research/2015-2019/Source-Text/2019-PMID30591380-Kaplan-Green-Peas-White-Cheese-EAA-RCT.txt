Title: Protein bioavailability of Wolffia globosa duckweed, a novel aquatic plant - A randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 30591380
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2018.12.009
Registry ID: NCT03020225
First published: 2019
Authors: Kaplan A, Zelicha H, Tsaban G, Yaskolka Meir A, Rinott E, Kovsan J, Novack L, Thiery J
Journal: Clinical nutrition (Edinburgh, Scotland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30591380/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract & Official ClinicalTrials.gov v2 Posted Results (NCT03020225): Randomized 3-arm acute test-meal trial in 36 healthy men (12 per arm; 12 h fasted) consuming iso-protein (30 g protein) whole-food meals of intact cooked green peas, soft white cheese (animal protein reference), or cooked Wolffia globosa (Mankai) with 250 mL water; blood sampled at 0, 30, 90, 180 min. Postprandial essential amino acids (histidine, phenylalanine, threonine, lysine, tryptophan) rose significantly vs baseline (p<0.05) similarly across groups (p>0.05), whereas branched-chain amino acids (leucine/isoleucine, valine — key LNAAs competing with levodopa transport) rose significantly LESS after cooked green peas than after soft white cheese (p<0.05 between groups). Posted NCT03020225 adverse events: 0 serious or non-serious AEs across all arms over 3 h. Direct human cooked green-pea kinetic RCT showing blunted BCAA/LNAA surge relative to animal dairy even at an extreme 30 g protein load (~550 g cooked peas), with no PD levodopa co-administration or OFF-time measurement.
