The authors summarize ginger and pungent constituents in receptor studies, experimental emesis models and prior clinical comparisons. Models include drug-provoked vomiting in distinct animal species. A separate clinical citation measures gastric electrical function. Neither vomiting nor gastric electrical activity measures chronic constipation severity. The cited preparations and outcomes stay separate.
