Title: Caffeinated versus decaffeinated espresso in patients with reduced smell
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26742907/
PMID: 26742907
DOI: 10.1007/s00405-015-3879-z
Publication date: 2016-01-07
Date basis: Publisher first online date.
Coverage: Exact publisher author abstract.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

Meusel and colleagues compared caffeinated and decaffeinated espresso under double blinding in 76 people with hyposmia of sinonasal or upper respiratory causes. The publisher abstract finds no statistically clear short-term olfaction difference in the approximately 45-minute reassessment. It does not give an equivalence result or a Parkinson cohort response. The later review PMC8524700 incorrectly expands the sinonasal condition into a cardiac sinus-node term.
