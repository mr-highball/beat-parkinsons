Title: Detection and localisation of hesitant steps in people with Alzheimer's disease navigating routes of varying complexity
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6498402/
PMID: 31119037
PMCID: PMC6498402
DOI: 10.1049/htl.2018.5034
Publication date: 2019-02-20
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: The PMC original records February 20 as the advance electronic release, with an April 24 electronic issue release.
Design: Instrument evaluation of hesitant stepping

This methods paper assesses hesitation and step detection in older people navigating routes of different complexity, including people with Alzheimer disease. The walking task and signal-classification outcomes do not measure hallucinations in Parkinson disease after a dietary exposure.
