Tart cherry concentrate and pretraining fatigue
Publication: 2015
Source: https://openrepository.aut.ac.nz/server/api/core/bitstreams/c1796f16-bb67-43c6-b0e6-36412219d639/content
Coverage: Auckland University of Technology thesis dated 2015. The repository does not specify its first public posting day. Chapter 6, Table 6.3, printed page 161.

On the seven-point Samn–Perelli scale, week-one group means (SD) were 2.7 (0.6) for concentrate and 3.0 (1.3) for placebo. Week-four means were 2.5 (0.6) and 3.1 (1.4). The author reports no clear group difference and provides no fatigue p value or confidence interval. The consent appendix says assignment would be randomized. The reported methods leave implementation, arm sizes, exact concentrate quantity and masking unclear. Morehen's subsequent 11-player crossover is a separate cohort.
