Title: Naringin treatment induces neuroprotective effects in a mouse model of Parkinson's disease in vivo, but not enough to restore the lesioned dopaminergic system
PMID: 26878791
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2015.10.013
Publication date: 2015-11-04 (electronic publication)
Citation: Kim and colleagues. Journal of Nutritional Biochemistry 27, February 2016 issue.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S095528631500306X
Coverage: Author abstract and publisher study framing.

The investigators separately tested isolated naringin before or after a toxin-induced lesion in mice. In the pretreatment comparison, they report protection of dopaminergic tissue. In the post-treatment comparison, dopaminergic markers did not clearly recover even after twelve weeks. Resting tremor is cited as a clinical feature in the introduction. The available results concern tissue markers, not a tremor test. This is a separate mouse experiment from the earlier rat study, with the same research trajectory and different timing.
