Title: Efficacy and safety of avocado-soybean unsaponifiables for the treatment of hip and knee osteoarthritis: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials.
PMID: 31328413
DOI: 10.1111/1756-185X.13658
First published: 2019
Authors: Simental-Mendía M, Sánchez-García A, Acosta-Olivo CA, Vilchez-Cavazos F, Osuna-Garate J, Peña-Martínez VM, Simental-Mendía LE
Journal: International journal of rheumatic diseases
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31328413/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized placebo-controlled trials of oral avocado-soybean unsaponifiables (ASU, typically 1:2 unsaponifiable extract of avocado and soybean oils) in hip and knee osteoarthritis. Pooled VAS pain fell (WMD -9.64 mm, 95% CI -17.43 to -1.84, p = 0.02, I2 = 92%; knee OA VAS WMD -17.36 mm, p < 0.0001; hip OA null; adverse events RR 1.02, p = 0.88). CRITICAL EXPOSURE/ATTRIBUTION BOUNDARY: ASU is a standardized 1:2 avocado/soybean unsaponifiable pharmaceutical extract, NOT isolated culinary refined or cold-pressed avocado oil.
