Kidney bean yogurt and sleep in a 2018 conference report
Source: https://theicph.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/03.-Natalia-Desy-Putriningtyas.pdf
DOI: 10.26911/theicph.2018.05.03
Coverage: original conference abstract; 2018 meeting dates and reported sleep comparison

The August 2018 meeting abstract reports a before-and-after study of fermented kidney bean yogurt in 17 older adults. Its reported sleep-quality scores match the later journal report, along with investigators, study population and intervention period. The abstract provides no control group or separate kidney bean effect. It represents the same likely cohort as the 2019 article.
