Title: Evaluation of Neuroprotective Activity of Melissa officinalis in MPTP Model of Parkinson's Disease in Mice
DOI: 10.5958/0974-360X.2019.00349.4
Source: https://www.rjptonline.org/HTML_Papers/Research%20Journal%20of%20Pharmacy%20and%20Technology__PID__2019-12-5-8.html
Publication: 2019, volume 12 issue 5, pp. 2103–2108
Coverage: Publisher original methods and numerical Tables III and IV.

The test exposure was an oral whole-plant ethanol extract in a toxin model. The higher extract level had rotarod retention of 132.2 ± 34.63 versus 80.17 ± 14.34 in the model control. The corresponding locomotor readouts were 355.2 ± 62.16 and 202.8 ± 30.66. Both higher extract levels bear significance marks against model control, with the lowest level marked inconclusive. These are means and standard deviations as reported. Retention before falling from a rod and general movement during a separate recording are different endpoints from episodes of gait freezing.

The source methods report 30 mice and also list six groups of six, an unreconciled denominator. No separate freeze count, fresh culinary lemon balm intervention, or human Parkinson outcome appears in the methods.
