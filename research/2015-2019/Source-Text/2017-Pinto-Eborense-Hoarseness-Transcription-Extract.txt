Pinto thesis metadata and primary transcription
https://repositorio.ul.pt/entities/publication/75586144-cdb3-4119-9e21-4f1c10cfc8cc/full

Pinto’s transcription on thesis page 115, manuscript folio 160v lines 22–24, records a remedy intended for hoarseness using sage, pennyroyal and an herb transcribed as ssegurelha in vinegar. Pinto’s editorial Table 2, page 178, interprets segurelha as Satureja hortensis using a modern botanical reference. The historical lines leave species, part and processing state unresolved, and do not record a patient response. The university records a 2017 examination and repository availability on August 21, 2017. This dates the verified public transcription. The manuscript’s age is a separate question.
