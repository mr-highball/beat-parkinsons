Original factual extract of primary abstract, publisher sections and figures 1–3

The MPTP mouse study compared 5, 10 and 20 mg/kg/day oral isolated lycopene with model and unexposed comparators. Figure 1 narrow-beam crossing times read approximately 2.5, 8.1, 6.2, 4.8, 5.1 and 2.5 s for untreated controls, model, model plus 5 mg/kg, model plus 10 mg/kg, model plus 20 mg/kg and isolated lycopene alone respectively. The graph letters are a, b, c, d, d and a in that order. Foot-slip errors are described in the accessible result snippet but not quantified in the recovered panels. Figure 2 shows model-only hang time of about 75 s vs about 300 s in untreated controls. The lycopene arms show more hang time than model only. Figure 3 reports peripheral and central squares crossed, grooming and rearing over five minutes. The rescued model arm shows larger counts. The primary results shown do not quantify step or stride length.
