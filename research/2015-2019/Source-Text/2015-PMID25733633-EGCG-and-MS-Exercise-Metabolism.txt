Title: Metabolic response to epigallocatechin-3-gallate in relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis: a randomized clinical trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25733633/
Original article: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0002916523273050
PMID: 25733633
DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.113.075309
Publication date: 2015-01-14
Date basis: NLM electronic date preceding the March 2015 issue.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract and searchable publisher methods and results sections.

An 18-person randomized double-blind crossover in relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis compared isolated EGCG to placebo, with background glatiramer acetate. The experiment measured energy expenditure, substrate oxidation and tissue responses at rest and during bicycle exercise. Lower energy expenditure at the same work after EGCG and differences by sex are metabolic outcomes. Fatigue is a motivation for the study. The inspected original does not provide a dedicated subjective fatigue effect or a prepared green-tea Parkinson comparison.
