Kidney bean yogurt formulation and measured sleep quality
Source: https://journal.ipb.ac.id/jgizipangan/article/download/24603/18063/88713
DOI: 10.25182/jgp.2019.14.3.141-148
Coverage: original journal body; preparation Methods and sleep Table 4; conference lineage verified

First published November 26, 2019. Seventeen older adults living in a care institution received kidney bean yogurt for two weeks. Mean Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index was 8.06 (SD 3.09) initially and 4.88 (SD 1.83) afterward (paired p=.001). The preparation combined kidney bean porridge with skim milk, sugar and cultures. The study provides no untreated comparison, pinto cultivar or Parkinson group. Its outcome measures sleep quality rather than an ingredient-specific clinical insomnia effect.
