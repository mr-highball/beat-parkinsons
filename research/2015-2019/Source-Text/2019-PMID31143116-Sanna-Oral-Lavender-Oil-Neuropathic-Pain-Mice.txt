Title: Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia Mill.) Essential Oil Alleviates Neuropathic Pain in Mice With Spared Nerve Injury.
PMID: 31143116
PMCID: PMC6521744
DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2019.00472
First published: 2019
Authors: Sanna MD, Les F, Lopez V, Galeotti N
Journal: Frontiers in pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31143116/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical controlled mouse study (Sanna et al., 2019, Front Pharmacol, PMC6521744) evaluating acute oral gavage of Lavandula angustifolia essential oil (LEO, 100 mg/kg p.o.) in a spared nerve injury (SNI) neuropathic pain model. Oral LEO alleviated SNI-induced mechanical allodynia (von Frey test) comparably to pregabalin via decreased spinal pERK1/2, pJNK1, iNOS, and endocannabinoid (CB1/FAAH/MAGL) modulation, while producing antidepressant-like and anxiolytic-like actions WITHOUT impairing gross behavior, rotarod motor coordination, locomotor activity, or memory. Limits: non-PD mouse model using concentrated oral essential oil rather than brewed culinary tea.
