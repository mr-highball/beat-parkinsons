Watermelon mention and orthostatic pressure in a dementia letter
Publication: 2015-02-17
Source: https://agsjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jgs.13265
Coverage: Jallad and colleagues, Journal of the American Geriatrics Society 63(2), 418–419. First published February 17, 2015. Original publisher letter and case report.
DOI: 10.1111/jgs.13265
PMID: 25688631

The letter records that the patient's caregiver gave the watermelon episode as an example of visuoperceptual difficulty. The authors report blood pressure of 130/84 mmHg supine and 114/79 mmHg standing during medication review. They planned to taper quetiapine because of orthostatic hypotension. Ingestion and timing of the watermelon relative to pressure or symptoms were unverified. The diagnosis was posterior cortical atrophy.
