Title: Efeito tipo antimaníaco de R-(-) e S-(+)-carvona em camundongos; Antimanic-like effects of (R)-(−)-carvone and (S)-(+)-carvone in mice
Authors: Francianne Poli Nogoceke; Nogoceke and colleagues in the journal article
Date: dissertation openly available 2015-07-13 on the original UFPR record; journal article 2016
Identifiers: PMID 26970377; DOI 10.1016/j.neulet.2016.03.013; institutional handle 1884/38022
Sources: https://acervodigital.ufpr.br/xmlui/handle/1884/38022?show=full
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26970377/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the dissertation and journal abstracts. Study overlap is inferred from the matched designs.

Methylphenidate and sleep deprivation increased activity in separately described mouse models. Both carvone enantiomers attenuated the induced hyperlocomotion. The journal abstract notes reduced spontaneous movement with S-(+)-carvone in the sleep-deprivation experiment, while the dissertation abstract mentions spontaneous-movement reduction with prolonged S-(+) exposure. Neither study arm consumed toasted fruit or measured clinical apathy or handwriting.
