Lopresti and Drummond, J Affect Disord 207:188–196, first published online October 1, 2016. PMID 27723543, DOI 10.1016/j.jad.2016.09.047.
Primary abstract describes 123 adults allocated to placebo, 250 mg curcumin extract twice daily, 500 mg curcumin extract twice daily or 250 mg curcumin extract with 15 mg saffron twice daily. The 500 mg dose belonged to curcumin alone. Pooled active vs placebo IDS-SR30 p=.031; STAI state p<.001 and trait p=.001. Active groups did not clearly differ. No designated saffron-only arm or Parkinson cohort.
