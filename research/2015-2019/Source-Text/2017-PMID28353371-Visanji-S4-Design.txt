Title: The Systemic Synuclein Sampling Study: toward a biomarker for Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28353371/
PMID: 28353371
DOI: 10.2217/bmm-2016-0366
Publication date: 2017-03-29
Date basis: Online-first date. The issue followed in April.
Source depth: Original author abstract. The linked sponsor protocol was inspected separately.

Visanji and colleagues describe a design to compare alpha-synuclein across specimens within participants and across disease stages. The abstract describes design and specimen comparisons for biomarker development. It provides no food exposure or drooling result. This paper, the original sponsor protocol, the registration and the 2018 report belong to one S4 study family.
