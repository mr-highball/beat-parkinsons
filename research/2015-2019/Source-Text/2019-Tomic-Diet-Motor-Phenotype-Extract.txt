Title: Do Nutrition Habits Influence the Clinical Presentation of Parkinson's Disease?
Source: https://hrcak.srce.hr/en/232218
Original: https://hrcak.srce.hr/file/337794
First public: 2019-12-16
Depth: Factual extract from original Methods and Tables 2–5.

Tomić and colleagues surveyed 96 idiopathic Parkinson patients about grouped food intake over the prior three months. Tables 3 and 4 give an UPDRS III median of 15.0 for daily combined meat/fish/poultry versus 18.5 for the other group (p=.334); motor subtype counts for tremor versus rigidity were 22/24 and 28/22 (p=.540). For the fruit-or-vegetable groups the UPDRS III medians were 16.0 versus 17.0 (p=.487). The printed classified counts for the fruit-or-vegetable groups are 27/25 and 23/21 (p=.835); their implied group sizes 52 and 44 differ from Table 2's 53 and 43. Dairy status was associated with motor subtype at p=.040. Food groups did not identify individual cooked preparations and motor subtype is distinct from recorded change in rest-tremor severity.
