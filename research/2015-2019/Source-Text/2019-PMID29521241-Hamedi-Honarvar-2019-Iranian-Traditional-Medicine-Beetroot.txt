Ethnomedical historical review of Beta vulgaris (Selgh/Choghondar) in Iranian Traditional Medicine manuscripts documenting customary use for constipation, gastrointestinal and joint pain, headaches, and psychological symptoms. Context-only traditional record; does not establish clinical PD efficacy.
