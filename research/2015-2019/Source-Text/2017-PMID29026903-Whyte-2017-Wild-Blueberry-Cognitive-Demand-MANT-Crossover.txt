Title: The effect of cognitive demand on performance of an executive function task following wild blueberry supplementation in 7 to 10 years old children.
PMID: 29026903
DOI: 10.1039/c7fo00832e
First published: 2017
Authors: Whyte AR, Schafer G, Williams CM
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29026903/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover trial in n=21 children (7-10 y) consuming 30 g freeze-dried wild blueberry powder vs placebo tested at 3 h on a demand-varied Modified Attention Network Task (MANT). Wild blueberry produced significantly faster performance vs placebo on cognitively demanding incongruent/high-load trials and visually cued alerting trials. Limits: acute test in healthy children; powder beverage.
