Title: Distinct Effects of two Almond Cultivars on Agreeability and Gastrointestinal Motility in Healthy Subjects: more than mere Nutraceuticals.
PMID: 29557413
DOI: 10.15403/jgld.2014.1121.271.dll
First published: 2018
Authors: Diella, Di Ciaula, Lorusso, Summo, Caggiano
Journal: Journal of gastrointestinal and liver diseases : JGLD
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29557413/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Comparative healthy-volunteer study (n=24 motility subset) testing 24 g of Californian Carmel vs Apulian Filippo Cea almonds against an isovolumetric liquid test meal. Gastric emptying was similar across both almond cultivars and test meal, while H2-breath orocecal transit time was shorter after both almond cultivars. Limits: healthy volunteers, no levodopa or PD OFF-time assessment.
