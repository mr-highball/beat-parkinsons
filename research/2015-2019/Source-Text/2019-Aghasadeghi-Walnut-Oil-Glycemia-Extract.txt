First published: 2019-08-26 online; 13(2):78–82, 2019 issue.
Coverage: Complete five-page publisher PDF; printed pages 79–80 and Tables 1–3 visually checked. No separate DOI or PMID verified.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

The cold-pressed-oil comparison uses 15 g/day for three months versus no intervention. Of 50 per group, 45 remain; two oil participants withdrew for GI intolerance. Table 2 reports oil-arm fasting glucose 158.37 (SD48.16) to 137.91 (23.24), p=.005, and HbA1c 7.00 (1.08) to 6.37 (1.29), p=.001. These are within-arm comparisons, not between-group estimates.

IRCT2014022216682N1 and ethics CT.P.92.5670 match the 2016 glycemic report. Aggregate trajectories also match. Do not count independent replication. Age eligibility is 20–80 here versus 30–60 previously. Stated power is 95% versus 80%, and the BMI p-value is .323 versus .233; retain these reporting differences rather than treating them as new cohorts or independent efficacy estimates.

No Parkinson symptom or pain endpoint is measured. The publisher HTML omits the numerical tables; the authenticated PDF resolves that access gap without changing cohort independence or clinical scope.
