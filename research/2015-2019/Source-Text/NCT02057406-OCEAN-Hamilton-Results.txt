OCEAN posted Hamilton depression results
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02057406?tab=results
Registration: NCT02057406
Coverage: Official result fields first posted June 12, 2017

Thirty-six participants were assigned to each arm. The official intention-to-treat adjusted week-12 Hamilton depression means (SE) were 15.1 (0.9) with EPA/DHA, 15.7 (0.9) with EPA and 14.9 (0.9) with corn oil. The two contrasts versus corn oil gave p=.74 and p=.45 respectively. The analysis used imputation and adjustment including baseline score and clinical covariates. The registry reports four oil capsules daily but does not specify the refining grade. These outcomes compare assigned formulations in depressed heart-failure patients.
