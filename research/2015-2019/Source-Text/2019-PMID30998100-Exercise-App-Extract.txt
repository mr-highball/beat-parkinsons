The study compares an iPhone exercise application with normal
routine. Its exercise domains are gait, speech and dexterity.
Apple identifies the device. Rest tremor is a Parkinson clinical
feature in the text. There is no oral apple intervention or
individual rest-tremor result attributable to fruit consumption.
