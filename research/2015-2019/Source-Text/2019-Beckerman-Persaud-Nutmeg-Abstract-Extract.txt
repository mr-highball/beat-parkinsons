Title: Nutmeg overdose: Spice not so nice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31519286/
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2019.07.011

First public: 2019-07-19
Depth: Factual summary of the author abstract. The original main text remains inaccessible through the checked publisher and institution routes.

Beckerman and Persaud describe an emergency presentation after nutmeg overdose with psychiatric and neurological symptoms. The author abstract does not itemize the observed motor signs, describe a Parkinson population or give a tremor outcome. The description does not resolve whether tremor was documented in the unavailable case body.
