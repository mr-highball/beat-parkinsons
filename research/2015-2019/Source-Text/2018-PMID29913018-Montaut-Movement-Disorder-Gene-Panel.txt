Title: Assessment of a Targeted Gene Panel for Identification of Genes Associated With Movement Disorders

Coverage: PMC original full main text, Methods and Cohort Description. Distilled factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

A targeted 127-gene panel was evaluated in 378 people with suspected inherited movement disorders, including patients with a predominant dystonia or parkinsonism phenotype. Probable pathogenic variants were identified in 83 participants. The cohort describes phenotypes and diagnostic yield. It did not assign fresh lemon balm or compare its effect on dystonia severity.
