Acute tart cherry concentrate and mental fatigue
Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/british-journal-of-nutrition/article/montmorency-tart-cherries-prunus-cerasus-l-modulate-vascular-function-acutely-in-the-absence-of-improvement-in-cognitive-performance/ACC2C96D5820BC9CAF649B03D49D0437

Coverage: Original full report, online December 19, 2016, Methods and Table 2

Keane and colleagues used a blinded randomized crossover in 27 healthy middle-aged adults with at least 14 days washout. They compared 60 mL Montmorency concentrate diluted with 100 mL water with an energy and macronutrient-matched low-fruit cordial. A 100-mm visual analogue scale measured mental fatigue during an acute cognitive battery. The original Table 2 reports treatment p=.690 and treatment-by-repetition p=.288. At five hours the unadjusted mean was 57.20 (SE 4.21) with concentrate and 56.85 (SE 4.08) with placebo. These are acute task effects in a different population and preparation from dried cherry and Parkinson fatigue.
