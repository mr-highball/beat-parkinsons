# Injected tarragon extract and open-field travel

Original source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6937744/
Source coverage original Methods and Figure 3

Jahani and colleagues used injected ethanolic aerial-part tarragon extract in an acute mouse experiment. Figure 3 reports generally unchanged open-field distance with a reduction in travel at 100 mg/kg. The measure is total exploratory travel. It does not establish slower walking, fresh culinary exposure or a Parkinson-specific effect.
