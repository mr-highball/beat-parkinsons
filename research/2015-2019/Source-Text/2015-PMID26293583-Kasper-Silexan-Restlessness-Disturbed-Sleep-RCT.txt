Title: Efficacy of orally administered Silexan in patients with anxiety-related restlessness and disturbed sleep--A randomized, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 26293583
DOI: 10.1016/j.euroneuro.2015.07.024
First published: 2015
Authors: Kasper S, Anghelescu I, Dienel A
Journal: European neuropsychopharmacology : the journal of the European College of Neuropsychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26293583/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Multicenter double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (Kasper et al., 2015, Eur Neuropsychopharmacol) enrolling 170 adult outpatients with anxiety-related restlessness (ICD-10 R45.1, HAMA >= 18, HAMA tension and insomnia items >= 2) randomized to oral Silexan 80 mg/day or placebo for 10 weeks. HAMA decreased by 12.0 vs 9.3 marginal mean points (p=0.03 ANCOVA; responders 48.8% vs 33.3%, p=0.04), with consistent superiority across PSQI sleep disturbance, SAS, State Check, and CGI. Adverse events were comparable (33.7% Silexan vs 35.7% placebo). Excluded major neurological disorders including Parkinson's disease; tests proprietary essential oil softgel rather than dried culinary lavender.
