Registered grape seed extract and motor tasks

Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03526406
Coverage: Official first posting and motor outcome modules
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First posted May 16, 2018. The 60-person randomized trial compared CP9700 400 mg with maltodextrin in healthy adults and registered finger tapping. The 2020 Bell paper matches the intervention, design and primary ID in its Methods. The author manuscript's preliminary page gives NCT03579095, the accession for a different ginseng study. The official CP9700 registry contains no posted results.
