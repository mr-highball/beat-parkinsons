Title: PHOSPHORYLATED α-SYNUCLEIN IN THE RETINA IS A BIOMARKER OF PARKINSON'S DISEASE PATHOLOGY SEVERITY
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6146055/
PMID: 29737566
PMCID: PMC6146055
DOI: 10.1002/mds.27392
Publication date: 2018-05-08
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC metadata and PubMed.
Design: Retinal tissue and Lewy pathology comparison

The analysis examined nine Parkinson autopsies, four incidental Lewy body cases and six controls for retinal phosphorylated alpha-synuclein. The comparisons concern postmortem biomarker localization. No culinary exposure or visual episode response was measured.
