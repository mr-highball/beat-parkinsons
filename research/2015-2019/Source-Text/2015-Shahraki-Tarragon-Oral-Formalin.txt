Oral tarragon aerial extract and formalin response in fructose-fed rats
Source: https://doi.org/10.1155/2015/895417
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: original animal study Methods and formalin outcome

Shahraki and colleagues published June 11, 2015. The original methods describe shade-dried aerial parts rather than leaf only, extracted in water. Male Wistar rats in the fructose model received the preparation orally over four weeks. Formalin-evoked scores were lower than fructose-only controls in the reported phases (p < .001 as stated in the abstract). The experiment is distinct from the earlier diabetic-rat study. It gives no fresh-leaf or clinical Parkinson pain contrast. Original paper: https://downloads.hindawi.com/journals/ecam/2015/895417.pdf.
