Title: Isolated tetrahydrocurcumin and experimental chemotherapy-related neuropathy
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26102012/
PMID: 26102012
DOI: 10.1016/j.cbi.2015.06.025
Publication date: 2015-06-20
Date basis: NLM electronic publication date.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Investigator abstract and original citation traced from the later metabolomics review. Full original methods and arm estimates remain unavailable.

The investigators report an experimental neuropathy comparison in rats with isolated tetrahydrocurcumin. The abstract describes thermal and mechanical pain behaviors, conduction and other laboratory endpoints. It summarizes favorable changes in pain manifestations for the higher tested condition. Individual arm values and uncertainty are outside the available abstract.

The isolated metabolite, experimental chemotherapy-related neuropathy and behavioral assays differ from culinary ground turmeric and human Parkinson pain severity.
