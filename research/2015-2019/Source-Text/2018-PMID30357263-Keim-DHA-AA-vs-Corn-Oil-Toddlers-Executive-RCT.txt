Title: Effect of Docosahexaenoic Acid Supplementation vs Placebo on Developmental Outcomes of Toddlers Born Preterm: A Randomized Clinical Trial.
PMID: 30357263
PMCID: PMC6583023
DOI: 10.1001/jamapediatrics.2018.3082
First published: 2018
Authors: Keim SA, Boone KM, Klebanoff MA, Turner AN, Rausch J, Nelin MA et al.
Journal: JAMA Pediatr
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6583023/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized double-blind trial (Keim et al., 2018, JAMA Pediatr, PMC6583023, n=377 preterm toddlers) comparing DHA+AA oil versus corn oil placebo for 180 days. Primary Bayley cognitive composite and effortful/executive control scores were null between groups (parent-rated effortful control slightly lower in DHA+AA); non-PD pediatric placebo design.
