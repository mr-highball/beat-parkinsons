Title: Daily Consumption of Virgin Coconut Oil Increases High-Density Lipoprotein Cholesterol Levels in Healthy Volunteers: A Randomized Crossover Trial.
PMID: 29387131
PMCID: PMC5745680
DOI: 10.1155/2017/7251562
First published: 2017
Authors: Chinwong S, Chinwong D, Mangklabruks A
Journal: Evidence-based complementary and alternative medicine : eCAM
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5745680/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Open-label, randomized, controlled crossover trial (Chinwong et al., 2017, Evid Based Complement Alternat Med, PMC5745680) in 35 healthy young Thai adults (32 completed) comparing 15 mL twice daily (30 mL/day) virgin coconut oil vs 2% carboxymethylcellulose control for 8 weeks each (8-week washout). Daily VCO increased HDL-cholesterol (+5.72 mg/dL, p=0.001) without altering LDL, total cholesterol, or triglycerides; mild diarrhea was reported by some participants taking VCO with no serious adverse events. Supplies GI tolerability context (loose stools in healthy adults) rather than a controlled constipation severity trial.
