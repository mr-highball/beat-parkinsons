Title: Increased weighting on prior knowledge in Lewy body-associated visual hallucinations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6924538/
PMID: 31886459
PMCID: PMC6924538
DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcz007
Publication year: 2019
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

A laboratory visual learning task compares 17 Lewy body participants with hallucinations, 20 without and 20 controls. Prior visual templates improved disambiguation more in the hallucinator group. The task measures an effect of prior information on perception, without oral fig or clinical episode rates.

The design uses a visual-template task and separates task disambiguation from the pre-existing clinical classification. The symptom-severity association is not a within-person change after food intake.
