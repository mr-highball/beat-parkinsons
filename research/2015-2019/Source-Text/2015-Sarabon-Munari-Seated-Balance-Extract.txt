Šarabon compared cayenne mixed with kaolin and water to kaolin and water alone in healthy adults. The topical procedure was applied over the back. The preliminary report describes 30-second seated centre-of-pressure testing on a wobble board and no detected change in balance or trunk strength. It supplies no standing postural responses or falls. The written 2015 article transcribes a presentation given in November 2014.
