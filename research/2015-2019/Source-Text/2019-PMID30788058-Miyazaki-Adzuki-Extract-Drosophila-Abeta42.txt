Title: Adzuki bean (Vigna angularis) extract reduces amyloid-β aggregation and delays cognitive impairment in Drosophila models of Alzheimer's disease.
PMID: 30788058
PMCID: PMC6369114
DOI: 10.4162/nrp.2019.13.1.64
First published: 2019
Authors: Miyazaki H, Okamoto Y, Motoi A, Watanabe T, Katayama S, Kawahara SI, et al.
Journal: Nutrition research and practice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30788058/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Invertebrate preclinical study (Miyazaki et al., 2019, Nutr Res Pract, PMC6369114) testing 40% ethanol extract of Tokachi red adzuki beans (Vigna angularis, 1 mg/mL) in A-beta42-overexpressing Alzheimer's model Drosophila melanogaster. Extract reduced brain A-beta42 accumulation, attenuated climbing decline (30% vs 70% impaired at day 30), improved olfactory aversive conditioning memory, and extended mean lifespan (45.4 vs 32.8 days). Limits: invertebrate Alzheimer's model using ethanolic extract rather than cooked whole beans or Parkinson's disease pathology.
