Title: Detection of Low-Signal Pulvinar Areas Using Diffusion-Weighted Imaging in Patients with Dementia Experiencing Visual Hallucinations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5075724/
PMID: 27790244
PMCID: PMC5075724
DOI: 10.1159/000449409
Publication year: 2016
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The memory clinic series relates pulvinar visibility across MRI sequences to the presence of prior visual hallucinations in people with dementia. Time after a food exposure and Parkinson-specific episode counts were not measured.

Seventy-three patients were recruited, and two without dementia were excluded. The reported association concerns an image's visibility and a clinical history indicator.
