2016 | Sereflican et al. | Thymoquinone in rabbit traumatic facial nerve paralysis

Source: original indexed abstract, PMID 27311344 and DOI 10.1016/j.amjoto.2016.05.006, online May 24, 2016.

Twenty-four rabbits were randomly allocated to uninjured or buccal branch injury groups with no active treatment, isolated thymoquinone or methylprednisolone. Electrophysiology was observed at baseline and at two months. Both active groups showed improved nerve recovery by electrophysiology and histopathology relative to untreated injury. The authors report greater postoperative electrical amplitude, axon diameter and myelin thickness with thymoquinone than with methylprednisolone. The accessible text states no facial expression rating and gives no culinary seed exposure or Parkinson model. Full treatment route remains pending.
