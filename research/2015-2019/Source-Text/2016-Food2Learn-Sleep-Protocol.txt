Van der Wurff ISM et al. BMJ Open. 2016;6:e011790. First published July 8, 2016. PMID27401364, PMC4947785, DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2016-011790. Source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4947785/. Original protocol Methods; open sleep results checked against the official registration and same-author dissertation.

52-week krill oil versus mixed oil protocol for typically developing adolescents ages 13–15. Comparison capsule combines olive oil, corn oil, palm oil and medium-chain triglycerides in a 4:4:9:3 ratio. The sleep substudy in the second cohort, n=64 planned, specifies seven days of ActivPAL and diary and the Adolescent Sleep-Wake and Sleep Hygiene Scales at baseline and 12 months. No food-alone, Parkinson or separately assigned refined oil.

Official NCT02240264 lists completion without posted results. The author's 2018 dissertation contains the same protocol in chapter 4 and reports cognition and depression outcomes in chapters 7–8. The scoped full-text search located the named sleep scales in protocol context only. There is no verified numerical arm-specific sleep estimate in the sources checked.
