Pasteurized orange juice and bowel diary outcomes
Source: https://citrusbr.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/2019-Lima-Cesar-OJ-intervention-change-gut-microbiota.pdf
PMID: 30638420
DOI: 10.1089/jmf.2018.0080
Coverage: Original Methods, intestinal-function results, Table 1 and Discussion; factual text

Lima and colleagues first published online January 14, 2019. Ten apparently healthy women followed a 30-day baseline, 60 days of 300 mL/day commercial pasteurized 100% orange juice, and a 30-day washout. The same group went through all periods in that order. Table 1 gives mean (SD) ordinal bowel-frequency scores of 1.40 (0.69) at baseline, 1.30 (0.48) at day 30, 1.40 (0.69) at day 60, and 1.60 (0.69) at washout. The frequency scale used 1 for daily, 2 for every other day, and 3 for less than three times weekly. Baseline to day 60 stool consistency was 1.00 (0.94) to 1.20 (0.63), evacuation effort was 2.40 (0.69) to 2.20 (0.41), and painful evacuation was 3.50 (0.52) to 2.40 (0.51). The Results report no significant differences across periods for these diary outcomes. The study had no concurrent control group, enrolled healthy participants, and used pasteurized juice.
