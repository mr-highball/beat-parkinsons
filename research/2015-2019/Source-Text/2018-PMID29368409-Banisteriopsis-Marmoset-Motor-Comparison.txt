Title: The effect of Banisteriopsis caapi on the motor deficits in the MPTP-treated common marmoset model of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/ptr.6017
PMID: 29368409
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.6017
Publication date: 2018-01-24
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Publisher article methods and results
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The experiment separates B. caapi extract, the isolated compound harmine and selegiline alone or with levodopa in common marmosets with MPTP-induced motor impairment. Time-course and aggregate comparisons use motor disability, locomotor activity and dyskinesia. The extract mildly improved the motor disability score. It did not increase locomotor activity or induce dyskinesia in the tested conditions, and it did not enhance the response to levodopa. Harmine likewise did not enhance the levodopa response. Some animals already displayed action tremor after MPTP exposure. The model cannot supply a participant-reported visual hallucination frequency or a clinical Parkinson resting-tremor estimate. The 2001 extract trial and this experimental model are separate evidence families.
