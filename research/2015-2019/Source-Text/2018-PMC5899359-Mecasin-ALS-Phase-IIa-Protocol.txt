The planned trial compares two randomized Mecasin add-on groups with placebo add-on to riluzole in ALS. The protocol excludes Parkinson disease. Pain VAS, depression and fatigue measures are secondary to the total functional scale. The formulation includes Curcuma longa alongside other constituents. The corresponding result report is PMID 37257710. Count protocol, original and conference report as one KCT0001984 family. This is distinct from the 24-week KCT0003792 trial. Round 1 claim 0812.
