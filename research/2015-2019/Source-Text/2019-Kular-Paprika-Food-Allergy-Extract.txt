Title: Anaphylactic reaction to paprika: A case series in a child and adult patient without prior risk factors
Authors: H Kular, S Zadi, D Kobric and G Sussman
Date: 2019
Identifier: Allergy congress abstract TP1049, collection DOI 10.1111/all.13961
Source: https://dspace.uevora.pt/rdpc/bitstream/10174/27240/1/2019-Allergy.pdf
Coverage: Original factual extract of primary conference abstract TP1049.

A five-year-old experienced oral mucosal angioedema after snacks sharing paprika. The abstract reports a positive skin test to paprika extract and elevated paprika-specific IgE. An adult reported throat tightness with generalized itching and gastrointestinal reactions after a chickpea and paprika dish. Paprika skin testing was positive and chickpea was negative. The report does not verify smoked paprika, isolated oral challenge or speech intelligibility.
