Kiwi breakfast registration and planned cognitive tests
https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=366885

First registered November 18, 2015 after recruitment, with 20 enrolled. The registry describes a four-breakfast crossover in healthy adults and names choice reaction time, Stroop, Trail Making B, word recall and serial sevens as secondary measures at baseline, 90 and 180 minutes. The registered comparison matches the breakfast conditions and timing in the Edwards thesis. The related journal report explicitly gives this registration and reports the metabolic results. These are publications from one trial family.
