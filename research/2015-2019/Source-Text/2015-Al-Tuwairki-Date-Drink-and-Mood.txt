Al-Tuwairki DS. Effects of acute administration of a single dose of date fruit juice (Phoenix dactylifera) on the cognitive and mood performance. Alexandria Science Exchange Journal 36(1):73–79, January–March 2015.
DOI: 10.21608/asejaiqjsae.2015.2741
Primary source: https://asejaiqjsae.journals.ekb.eg/article_2741_1f8b42609b39f821f039cb9ad5ad6a30.pdf
Coverage: factual digest of indexed original, pp. 73–75 and Table 1.

Fifteen healthy adults completed a blinded, counterbalanced two-visit drink and placebo crossover. At two hours the date-drink and placebo changes respectively were +15.77 and −1.84 for alertness (p=.001), +13.69 and −1.65 for contentment (p=.001), and +13.69 and −1.57 for calmness (p=.019). The paper prints the same treated mean and SD for contentment and calmness. Placebo composition, fruit maturity and fruit cultivar were not established. Ratings are subjective mood dimensions, without a Parkinson cohort or apathy measure.
