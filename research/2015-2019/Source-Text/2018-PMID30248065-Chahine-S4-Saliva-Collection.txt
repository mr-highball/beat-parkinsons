The S4 report describes 61 adults with Parkinson disease and 21 healthy controls. Collection was completed for 57/61 and 21/21, with mean obtained saliva volumes of 4.08 and 4.92 mL, respectively. The authors report no association of volume with time elapsed since food or liquid intake, without publishing the estimate or underlying data. The obtained volume measures specimen yield. The article gives no calibrated secretion rate or named food comparison with rated drooling. Previous salivary gland treatment for sialorrhea appears in the eligibility discussion.

The article cohort totals 82. The NCT02572713 actual enrollment field is 80, while its group descriptions sum to 82. The inspected registry has no posted aggregate results. It, the sponsor protocol and the 2017 Visanji design article describe the same study family.
