Title: The effects of beetroot juice supplementation on indices of muscle damage following eccentric exercise.
PMID: 26537365
DOI: 10.1007/s00421-015-3290-x
First published: 2016
Authors: Clifford T, Bell O, West DJ, Howatson G, Stevenson EJ
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26537365/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled parallel trial in n=30 recreationally active men after eccentric drop jumps: high- and lower-dose beetroot juice significantly preserved pressure pain threshold (reduced provoked muscle pain) at 24, 48, and 72 hours post-exercise vs placebo (p<0.001). Limits: provoked exercise pain in healthy men, not PD pain.
