The Cochrane review evaluates dysphagia interventions after stroke. The excluded-studies table describes oral isolated piperine in Rofes and separate capsaicin protocols. Its criteria excluded the piperine study from synthesis based on study and outcome scope.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
