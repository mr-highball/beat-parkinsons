P95, printed pages 81–82, reviews herbal IBS trials. Its punctuation places GJS beside a Melissa/spearmint/coriander preparation. Primary sources identify that trio as Carmint and identify GJS as a different 13-herb formula. The abstract does not provide item-specific citations establishing its intended mapping; do not transfer product identity or efficacy between them.

P146 studies combinations of plant extracts in cells. P61 compares external ginger and hot-water compresses using warmth and relaxation observations, without an oral-ginger or separate clinical anxiety estimate. P227 randomizes 66 PCOS patients to cinnamon-powder capsules or placebo for 12 weeks and reports fasting insulin p=.024, HOMA-IR p=.014 and LDL p=.049. No mapped Parkinson symptom outcome is supplied. P221 is an interaction review, not a controlled food experiment.

These member abstracts retain combined products, route, population and endpoint boundaries. The existing Carmint primary extract is under 2005–2009; GJS protocol and results are under 2010–2014.
