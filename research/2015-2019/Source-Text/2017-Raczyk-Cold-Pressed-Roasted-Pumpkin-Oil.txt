Roasted and unroasted seeds in cold pressed oil production
Source: https://www.food.actapol.net/volume16/issue3/5_3_2017.pdf
PMID: 29055977
DOI: 10.17306/J.AFS.2017.0494
Coverage: factual extract from primary Methods and Table 2

M. Raczyk, A. Siger, E. Radziejewska-Kubzdela, K. Ratusz and M. Rudzińska. Acta Sci Pol Technol Aliment 16(3):293–301, 2017.
Investigators compared oils cold-pressed from roasted and unroasted Cucurbita pepo seeds. One batch was roasted at 110 °C for 40 minutes before screw pressing. Output oil was reported at 39 ± 1 °C. The reported unsaturated-fat profiles were broadly similar, while phytosterols, carotenoids, tocopherols and oxidative stability differed. The reported total carotenoid amounts were 0.29 and 0.54 mg per 100 g oil for unroasted and roasted seeds, respectively. Roasting and pressing are separately documented in this experiment, and both oils are described as cold pressed. No patient ingestion or symptom outcome was studied.
