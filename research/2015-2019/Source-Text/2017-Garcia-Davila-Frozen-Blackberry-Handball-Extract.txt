Fourteen competitive handball players, mean age 22.30 years (SD 1.83), were allocated seven per group. The experimental drink contained 200 g commercially obtained frozen blackberry homogenized with 200 mL water. Control was 200 mL red-colored water. The drinks were taken before lunch for sixteen days. The investigators performed laboratory phenolic and antioxidant measurements on fresh and frozen berries and selected frozen berries for the consumption intervention. They measured plasma creatine kinase and urea around a competition and found no significant between-group creatine kinase difference across individual timepoints including at 24, 48 or 72 hours of recovery. Mean creatine kinase changed within both arms. Human pain severity and soreness were not outcomes. The trial identifies Rubus sp., but the exact species and cultivar are absent.
