Factual extract of the primary Methods, Results and Tables 3–5, checked 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

Adults with assorted pain conditions were allocated by lot to inhale essential oil from Piper nigrum (25 adults) or sesame oil (29 adults) from coded vials for 15 minutes. No scent masking check is listed. Median numerical pain scores before exposure were 6 versus 7, and Mann–Whitney comparison p=.930. After exposure, median scores were 3 versus 6, with U=117.5 and p<.001. The test compares groups immediately after exposure. The paper does not establish a quantified PD effect or report oral intake of cracked peppercorns.
