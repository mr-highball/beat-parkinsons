Title: Effects of Long Chain Omega-3 Polyunsaturated Fatty Acids on Brain Function in Mildly Hypertensive Older Adults.
PMID: 30279397
PMCID: PMC6213246
DOI: 10.3390/nu10101413
First published: 2018
Authors: Howe PRC, Evans HM, Kuszewski JC, Wong RHX
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6213246/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized double-blind trial (Howe et al., 2018, Nutrients, PMC6213246, n=38 older adults) comparing long-chain omega-3 fish oil capsules against corn oil placebo capsules for 20 weeks. Cognitive and executive function endpoints did not differ significantly between arms; corn oil served exclusively as placebo without an oil-free control.
