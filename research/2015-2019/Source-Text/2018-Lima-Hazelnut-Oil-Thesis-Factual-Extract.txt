Lima RPA. UFPB thesis on folate and hazelnut oil. Printed pages 78 and 81 (PDF pages 80 and 83).

The thesis describes the same 40-woman, four-arm, eight-week trial as the 2017 journal report. It carries 2017 on the cover. Repository metadata gives a September 26, 2018 issue date and November 8, 2018 availability. Printed page 78 (PDF page 80) lists 25 mg of hazelnut oil per daily capsule for both oil groups. Printed page 81 (PDF page 83) gives 25 g for the same intervention, matching the article methods. It names a compounding pharmacy and says that a quality certificate was supplied. The conflicting stated quantities and unverified source certificate leave the dose, lot and pressing process open. Visual hallucination frequency was not measured.
