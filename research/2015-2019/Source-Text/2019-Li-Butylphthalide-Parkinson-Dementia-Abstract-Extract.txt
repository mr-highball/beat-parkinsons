Title: Butylphthalide soft capsules and clinical outcomes in Parkinson disease dementia
Source: https://coaa.istic.ac.cn/openjournal/periodicalArticle/0120190301148262
First public issue date: 2019, Clinical Research 27(1):76–78
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated national journal platform author abstract

The authors describe Parkinson dementia cases recruited from
August 2016 through October 2017. They report random-number
table allocation of 36 to conventional care and 37 to usual
care plus butylphthalide. The listed hospital is Zhengzhou
First People's Hospital. The abstract reports a lower
Parkinson composite score with adjunct treatment (p<.05).

Arm scores, outcome timing, a rest tremor item and a culinary
preparation are absent from the available abstract. The
full-paper route requires sign-in. This is distinct from the
2016 Shiyan report by recruitment time, location and counts.
