Title: A Retrospective Evaluation of the Frequency of Impulsive Compulsive Behaviors in Parkinson's Disease Patients Treated with Continuous Waking Day Apomorphine Pumps.
PMID: 30363495
PMCID: PMC6174511
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12416
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6174511/
First publication date: 2016-08-11 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC complete original article HTML and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The retrospective series follows 28 people with advanced Parkinson disease using waking-day apomorphine pumps. It compares impulsive-compulsive behaviors around changes in dopamine agonist treatment, with no dried fig exposure or separate episode-rate comparison for visual hallucinations.
