Title: Dietary Nitrate Increases VO2peak and Performance but Does Not Alter Ventilation or Efficiency in Patients With Heart Failure With Reduced Ejection Fraction.
PMID: 28916479
PMCID: PMC5811385
DOI: 10.1016/j.cardfail.2017.09.004
First published: 2018
Authors: Coggan AR, Broadstreet SR, Mahmood K, Mikhalkova D, Madigan M, Bole I, et al.
Journal: Journal of cardiac failure
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28916479/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=8 patients with heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF): acute beetroot juice significantly increased knee-extensor time to fatigue (+7%; p<0.05) and peak muscle power (+13%). Limits: very small non-PD sample.
