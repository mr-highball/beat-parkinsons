Bell et al., August 18, 2017. Spices in a Product Affect Emotions: A Study with an Extruded Snack Product.
Source: https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/foods/foods-06-00070/article_deploy/foods-06-00070.pdf
Access and coverage: Publisher PDF, Abstract, Methods 2.1, Results and Conclusions.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

Human consumers tasted extruded apple-based snacks with 0%, 4% or 5% of a seasoning blend. Ground clove supplied 6% of the blend along with ground cinnamon, dried ginger and nutmeg. The 5% blend was associated with an increase in the food-evoked satisfied rating. The experiment captured acute emotion labels and product liking. A separate ten-person clinical arm compared antioxidant capacity and blood glucose after 4% blend or control. No diagnosed Parkinson sample, sustained apathy score or ground-clove-only arm was reported.
