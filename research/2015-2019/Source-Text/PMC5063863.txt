The Korean study enrolls 323 Parkinson patients and 94 controls. The K-NMSS perception problems and hallucination domain is a composite, reported in 23.8% of Parkinson participants and with higher scores in advanced disease. The domain cannot identify a separate visual episode rate or a dried fig effect.
