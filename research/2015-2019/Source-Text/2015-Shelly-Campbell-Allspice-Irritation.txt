Allspice oil oral irritation and sedation in rats
Source: https://jppres.com/jppres/oc-52-the-toxicological-profiles-of-pimenta-dioica-pimento-and-two-of-its-constituents/
Coverage: Original FAPRONATURA abstract OC-52, publication p S75

Shelly-Campbell and Oswald report mucosal irritation, labored breathing and sedation in rats exposed to allspice oil or isolated eugenol. Methyleugenol caused slight sedation without observed irritation in this report. The conference abstract tests oil and constituents in an induced arthritic model. It gives no acoustic, listener-rated, articulation, or culinary ground allspice outcome.
