Title: Oral supplementation of Ocimum basilicum has the potential to improves the locomotory, exploratory, anxiolytic behavior and learning in adult male albino mice.
PMID: 25082078
DOI: 10.1007/s10072-014-1913-3
First published: 2015
Authors: Zahra K, Khan MA, Iqbal F
Journal: Neurological sciences : official journal of the Italian Neurological Society and of the Italian Society of Clinical Neurophysiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25082078/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical oral behavioral study in adult male albino mice treated orally for 7 days with Ocimum basilicum leaf extract (100 mg/mL solvent/kg body weight) vs saline control on elevated plus maze, light-dark box, open field, rotarod, and novel object recognition tests. Reported improved neuromuscular coordination, open-field and light-dark box anxiolytic/exploratory performance, and object recognition/transfer latency. Abstract omits group sizes and exact p-values.
