Title: Examining the impact of grape consumption on brain metabolism and cognitive function in patients with mild decline in cognition: A double-blinded placebo controlled pilot study.
PMID: 27856335
DOI: 10.1016/j.exger.2016.10.004
First published: 2017
Authors: Lee J, Torosyan N, Silverman DH
Journal: Experimental gerontology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27856335/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind, placebo-controlled 6-month pilot trial in 10 older adults (mean age 72.2 +/- 4.7 y) with mild cognitive decline randomized to freeze-dried whole table grape powder formulation (72 g/day) vs polyphenol-free matched placebo. Placebo showed significant FDG-PET regional cerebral metabolic decline in right posterior cingulate (p=0.01), left superior posterolateral temporal (p=0.04), and left prefrontal/cingulate cortices (p<0.01), whereas the grape arm maintained stable metabolism. Critically, NO significant between-group differences were found on the neuropsychological test battery (within active group only, regional metabolism correlated with WAIS-III Digit Span attention/working memory r=-0.69, p=0.04). Limits: tiny sample (n=10), null between-group clinical cognitive endpoints, non-PD cohort.
