Title: Metabolic treatment of syndrome linked with Parkinson's disease and hypothalamus pituitary gonadal hormones by turmeric curcumin in Bisphenol-A induced neuro-testicular dysfunction of wistar rat.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6296165/
PMID: 30582014
PMCID: PMC6296165
DOI: 10.1016/j.bbrep.2018.12.004
Publication date: 2018-12-13
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official complete PMC JATS body.

The toxin-model rat study tests isolated curcumin with olive oil used as an oral vehicle. It reports biochemical, tissue and motor-related findings, without isolated cold-pressed oil or tested culinary turmeric, fig or ginger. There is no separate Parkinson postural instability, bradykinesia, resting tremor or muscle rigidity estimate.
