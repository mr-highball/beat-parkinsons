Publisher member M255 on S182 compares Parkinson motor subtypes in 1,003 retrospective Mayo patients enrolled or seen during 1998–2016. Akinetic-rigid and mixed subtypes were more likely than tremor-dominant cases to have hallucinations at the final visit. The abstract does not specify visual modality or count episodes. The 2018 Konno paper (PMID 29406964) repeats the site, years, denominator and effect probabilities; treat these as likely overlapping analyses. The 2020 MAPT paper uses 855 individuals at the same site in the same years and cites the clinical paper. Do not pool them as independent studies.
