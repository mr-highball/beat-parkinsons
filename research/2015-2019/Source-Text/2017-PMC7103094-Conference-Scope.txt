Visual hallucination context for claim 0175

Abstract OP-367 begins on printed page S252 and continues through the top of the right column on S253. The investigators report 118 FDG-PET scans in the European dementia with Lewy bodies database. Clinical feature data were available for 79 participants, of whom 60% were classified as having visual hallucinations at imaging. The presence subgroup showed more extensive occipital hypometabolism, including after adjustment for global cognitive score. The comparison is metabolic imaging by symptom status in dementia with Lewy bodies. It supplies no visual episode rate and no dried fig intake comparison.
