Topical sesame oil and an eight-meter walk in knee osteoarthritis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31780006/
PMID: 31780006
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2019.08.017
Coverage: Primary author abstract

Askari and colleagues randomized 104 knee osteoarthritis patients to topical sesame oil or diclofenac gel three times daily for four weeks. Testing at baseline, week two and week four included time to walk eight meters. The investigators report noninferiority of sesame oil for the walking outcome at follow-up. The abstract provides no walk times, confidence interval or prespecified margin. It describes topical use and gives no cold-press designation. The comparison addresses pain-related mobility under an active comparator. The outcome table and product specifications remain open.
