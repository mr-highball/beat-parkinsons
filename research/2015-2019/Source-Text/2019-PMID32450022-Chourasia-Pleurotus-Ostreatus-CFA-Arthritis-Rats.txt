Title: Evaluation of Antiarthritic Effect of Culinary-Medicinal Oyster Mushroom Pleurotus ostreatus cv. Florida (Agaricomycetes) on Complete Freund's Adjuvant Induced Arthritis in Rats.
PMID: 32450022
DOI: 10.1615/IntJMedMushrooms.2019032636
First published: 2019
Authors: Chourasia A, Tiwari A, Ganeshpurkar A, Jaiswal A, Shrivastava A, Dubey N
Journal: International Journal of Medicinal Mushrooms
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32450022/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Rat Complete Freund's Adjuvant arthritis model treated orally with 200 or 400 mg/kg hydroethanolic extract of Pleurotus ostreatus cv. Florida vs piroxicam for 21 days. Reduced paw volume, joint diameter, and X-ray swelling (p < 0.001) and improved gripping activity; crucially, direct nociception/pain-threshold assays (von Frey, hot plate, withdrawal latency) were NOT reported in the abstract. Hydroethanolic extract, animal model.
