The two reported arms are honey syrup and water, 40 per group. The honey comparison is not a date syrup exposure. VAS pain means at 60 minutes were 7.62 versus 9.05. At 150 minutes the comparison was inconclusive (p=.426). Five of the six control rows match the companion date-syrup paper exactly in both mean and SD. Several reported baseline control characteristics are also identical. The 90-minute control row differs, and individual participant linkage is unavailable.

The two reports cannot serve as independent replications. Report the active preparations separately within the shared registration program and preserve the unresolved common-control mapping. Neither paper establishes a honey-versus-date contrast.
