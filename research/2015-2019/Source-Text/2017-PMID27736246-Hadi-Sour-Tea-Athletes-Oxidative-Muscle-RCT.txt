Title: The Effect of Green Tea and Sour Tea (Hibiscus sabdariffa L.) Supplementation on Oxidative Stress and Muscle Damage in Athletes
PMID: 27736246
DOI: 10.1080/19390211.2016.1237400
First published: 2017
Authors: Hadi A, Pourmasoumi M, Kafeshani M, Karimian J, Maracy MR, Entezari MH
Journal: Journal of Dietary Supplements
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27736246/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Double-blind RCT in 54 male soccer athletes randomized to 450 mg/day sour tea (H. sabdariffa) extract (n=18), 450 mg/day green tea extract (n=18), or maltodextrin placebo (n=18) for 6 weeks. Sour tea significantly lowered MDA (P=0.008) and raised TAC (P=0.01) vs placebo, but had NO significant effect on muscle-damage biomarkers (AST, CK, LDH) and did not measure subjective fatigue or Parkinson's symptoms.
