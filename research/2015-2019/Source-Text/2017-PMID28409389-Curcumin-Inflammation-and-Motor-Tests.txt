Sharma and colleagues examined injected isolated curcumin in a rat model of inflammatory dopaminergic injury. The abstract reports favorable general activity, rotarod, beam and grid performance with curcumin alongside biomarker changes. Those findings concern a distinct route and toxin model. The abstract supplies no direct passive-rigidity measure or separately reported velocity estimate. Sample sizes by arm, effect estimates and precise behavioral definitions remain open at abstract depth.

Scope: Retain the experimental context for claims 0359 and 0507. The isolated compound and injection route differ from the frozen turmeric preparations.
