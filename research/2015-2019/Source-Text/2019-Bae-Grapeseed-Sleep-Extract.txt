Suyeon Bae. The Impact of Ambient Scent Environment on Residents' Wellness and Their Perception of Interior Environments in Long-Term Care Facilities. University of Minnesota dissertation, May 2019. Handle 11299/206341.
Original dissertation pp. 95–101, 116–118 and 139–140. Two-week ambient bedside reed diffusion of lavender plus grapeseed carrier or carrier only in long-term care residents. Fifty-eight were randomized and 42 had paired data, 21 per arm. Table 13 complete-pair PSQI means were 5.90 to 5.19 in carrier controls and 7.14 to 5.81 with lavender. The printed placebo change and significance notation are internally inconsistent. Table 14 baseline-adjusted intervention coefficient was −0.07 (SE 0.75). The single pleasant-dream anecdote concerns a lavender participant. There was no oil-free contrast, food administration, processing grade, target Parkinson population or recorded dream-enactment frequency. Bae and Asojo 2020, PMID 32500733, appears to report the same study family.
