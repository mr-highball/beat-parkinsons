Title: Stable sulforaphane protects against gait anomalies and modifies bone microarchitecture in the spontaneous STR/Ort model of osteoarthritis.
PMID: 28778596
PMCID: PMC5571892
DOI: 10.1016/j.bone.2017.07.028
First published: 2017
Authors: Javaheri B, Poulet B, Aljazzar A, de Souza R, Piles M, Hopkinson M
Journal: Bone
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5571892/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-month study in spontaneous osteoarthritis STR/Ort mice (n=8/group) orally dosed with 100 mg/kg synthetic stabilized sulforaphane (SFX-01) vs vehicle. Prevented OA-related gait asymmetry and improved bone microarchitecture without modifying histological cartilage lesions. Non-PD OA joint model; gait symmetry rather than PD walking speed.
