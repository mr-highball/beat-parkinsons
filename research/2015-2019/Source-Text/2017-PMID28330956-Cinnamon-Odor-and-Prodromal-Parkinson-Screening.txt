Original source scope
This idiopathic REM sleep behavior disorder cohort received a 16-item smell identification test and symptom questionnaires. The authors name cinnamon and clove among the odor items and discuss familiarity. Analyses report aggregate olfaction and clinical disease conversion, and record constipation and sleep disorder context separately.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Cinnamon is a genuine sensory stimulus in a clinical test. The report has no cinnamon-specific smell score, consumption intervention, isolated constipation effect or separate dream-enactment effect from the odor.
