Title: Common-sage extract, morphine withdrawal and sedation in rats
Publication: 2015
PMID: 26337199
DOI: 10.3109/00952990.2015.1062893
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26337199/
Text type: original abstract evidence summary

Hasanein and colleagues report a hydroalcoholic common-sage extract in rats studied for morphine tolerance and dependence. Their abstract reports longer sleep duration at two extract doses in its sedation assessment. The detailed sleep protocol, units, and full group estimates were not verified in the original main body. The model and extract do not provide a human insomnia or culinary dried-sage effect.
