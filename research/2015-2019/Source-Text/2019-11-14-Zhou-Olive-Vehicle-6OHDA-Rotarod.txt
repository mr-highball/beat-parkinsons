Olive oil vehicle in a 6-OHDA mouse rotarod experiment
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6885178/
PMID: 31827703
PMCID: PMC6885178
DOI: 10.1155/2019/8169125
Coverage: Original Methods, in vivo Results and Figure 5

Zhou and colleagues assigned four groups of 15 mice. Sham and lesion comparison animals received plain olive oil by gavage. The treated lesion group received the tetramethylpyrazine analogue T-006 dissolved in olive oil. Treated mice stayed on the rotating rod longer and made fewer induced rotations than the lesion group. The oil was present in both decisive arms and no cold-pressed grade was reported. The introductory mention of clinical postural instability is distinct from the tested rodent measures.
