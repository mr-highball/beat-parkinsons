Title: Safety and tolerability of carvacrol in healthy subjects: a phase I clinical study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30486682/
PMID: 30486682
DOI: 10.1080/01480545.2018.1538233
First published online: 2018-11-29
Coverage: Factual extract of the original indexed abstract.

The study randomized healthy volunteers between two levels of isolated carvacrol for one month. It reports laboratory and spirometry outcomes. Within-dose changes included higher creatine phosphokinase in the lower-dose group and lower HDL and iron in the higher-dose group. The authors report that measured post-treatment values stayed within laboratory reference ranges. The comparison had no unexposed arm.

The accessible abstract does not provide a standardized anxiety or neurological adverse-event measure. Full adverse-event tables and an independently matched registration remain unverified. The preparation was isolated carvacrol and the cohort did not consist of people with Parkinson disease.

A 2019 report names IRCT2016080429191N1 and describes 30 healthy adults in the same two-dose, one-month design. It measured pulmonary function and white blood cells. The Methods mention questioning about clinical signs and exclusion of psychiatric disorders. The numerical results do not give anxiety. The shared principal investigator, dose, duration and research project support possible cohort overlap, without confirming the identities of all participants.
