Title: An iTRAQ-based proteomic analysis reveals dysregulation of neocortical synaptopodin in Lewy body dementias

The prefrontal-cortex proteomics study selects 19 dementia with Lewy bodies, 21 Parkinson dementia and 21 control brains from a larger pool. It compares protein abundance and synaptopodin. It does not measure dried fig intake or visual hallucination frequency.
