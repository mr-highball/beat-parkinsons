Two dose contrasts for forepaw use in the Lins thesis
Primary source: https://ri.ufs.br/bitstream/riufs/3648/1/LIVIA_CRISTINA_R_F_LINS.pdf
Coverage: factual extract from primary Experiment II methods and Figure 15.

Lins’s 2017 thesis compared two doses of isolated intraperitoneal carvacrol around a unilateral 6-OHDA lesion in Long–Evans rats. In Experiment II, the authors counted contralateral forepaw contacts with the cylinder wall. The lower-dose group differed unclearly from sham (p=.211), while the higher-dose group and lesion vehicle group each showed lower use than sham (both p<.001). The report gives an overall group contrast and sham comparisons for this measure, with no significant treatment-versus-lesion-vehicle comparison reported. This experiment is separate from the thesis’s reserpine oral-movement experiment already represented in the catalog. Forepaw contact choice does not measure handwriting size.
