Title: Cognitive effects following acute wild blueberry supplementation in 7- to 10-year-old children.
PMID: 26437830
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-015-1029-4
First published: 2016
Authors: Whyte AR, Schafer G, Williams CM
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26437830/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-way double-blind randomized crossover trial in n=21 children (7-10 y) testing 15 g vs 30 g freeze-dried wild blueberry powder vs vehicle. Improved accuracy on cognitively demanding incongruent interference trials at 3 h alongside immediate recall (1.15 h) and delayed word recognition in a dose-response pattern (30 g best). Limits: acute pediatric test; powder beverage.
