Chicken and milk in a corticobasal case history

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6095655/
Coverage: Original case history, factual extract.

Tetzloff and colleagues followed a person with progressive apraxia of speech and autopsy-confirmed corticobasal degeneration. Clinically described hypomimia was recorded by 2013. In 2015, the family reported a fixed diet that included milk and occasional chicken, with an intermittent unusual facial appearance. These are separate clinical and diet observations in the same progressive case. Processing or pasteurization of the milk, cooking method of the chicken, earlier dietary history, portion and meal timing were unreported. There is no food-specific facial response, standardized change after eating, or idiopathic Parkinson cohort. The same case has already been indexed locally for blueberries.
