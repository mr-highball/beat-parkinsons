Title: Cannabidiol versus placebo in Crohn disease with olive-oil vehicles
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28349233/
PMID: 28349233
DOI: 10.1007/s10620-017-4540-z
Publication date: 2017-03-27
Date basis: NLM electronic publication date.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Published investigator abstract and companion public registry. Full original report not authenticated.
Registration: NCT01037322

Twenty participants with active Crohn disease were randomized and 19 completed follow-up. The investigator abstract reports baseline mean disease activity scores of 337 and 308 and week-eight means of 220 and 216, for the cannabidiol and placebo groups respectively. The between-group comparison was described as inconclusive. The registry identifies olive oil in both arms. The registry and publication abstract differ on the reported drug dose.

Neither source identifies the carrier as extra-virgin olive oil, isolates a culinary oil comparison or reports Parkinson insomnia. Keep the registry and publication as one trial family.
