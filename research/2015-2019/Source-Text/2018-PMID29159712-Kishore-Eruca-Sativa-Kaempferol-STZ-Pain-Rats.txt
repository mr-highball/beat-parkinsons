Title: Effect of Kaempferol isolated from seeds of Eruca sativa on changes of pain sensitivity in Streptozotocin-induced diabetic neuropathy.
PMID: 29159712
DOI: 10.1007/s10787-017-0416-2
First published: 2018
Authors: Kishore L, Kaur N, Singh R
Journal: Inflammopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29159712/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled 30-day preclinical diabetic neuropathy study (Kishore, Kaur & Singh, 2018, Inflammopharmacology) in streptozotocin-diabetic rats starting 60 days post-STZ. Chronic oral hydroalcoholic Eruca sativa seed extract (100, 200, and 400 mg/kg/day) and kaempferol isolated from E. sativa seeds (5 and 10 mg/kg/day) significantly attenuated thermal and mechanical hyperalgesia, tactile allodynia, motor nerve conduction velocity decline, hyperglycemia, oxidative-nitrosative stress, and advanced glycation end products. Limits: non-PD diabetic rat neuropathy model using seed extract and isolated kaempferol; abstract-only inspection omits per-arm animal counts and exact p-values.
