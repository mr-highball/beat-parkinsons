Rocoto extract and anxiety ratings in medical students
Source: https://repositorio.ucsm.edu.pe/server/api/core/bitstreams/2cff5d65-2c9d-4820-9aa0-c14d09e042cb/content
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: original university thesis pages 8 and 13–16

Valencia Mercado’s Peruvian thesis randomized 70 students to hydroalcoholic Capsicum pubescens extract in ketchup or ketchup and water for ten days. Reported anxiety within-arm Friedman p-values were .779 and .967. In Table 3, extract-arm category counts sum to 39, 38 and 39, exceeding printed visit totals of 35, 32 and 32. Most extract respondents identified assignment. The within-arm tests and inconsistent table do not support a between-arm effect. Rocoto is a separate species from the frozen grounded cayenne exposure. Repository publication year is 2016.
