Title: Omega-3 and vitamin E in Parkinson disease in the Tamtaji trial
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/26350?revision=26350
Registration: IRCT2017061234497N1
First public: 2017-06-20
Coverage: Official initial and revised registry, WHO record and subsequent paper.

The 2017 first public version lists Naghavi clinic, 1800 mg omega-3 and 200 IU vitamin E, with TNF-alpha and IL-6 as the specified outcome measures. The September 2019 revision lists Shahid Beheshti, 1000 mg omega-3 and 400 IU vitamin E. The article cites the registration and identifies flaxseed-derived omega-3 at the revised amount. Neither registry version gives total or separate rest-tremor UPDRS as a prespecified result, an oil-only comparison or the method of pressing the oil. The source registry marks registration retrospective and labels 2017-04-20 as an expected start, leaving the actual date field blank. The WHO record labels that same value as first enrollment. The clinical paper describes recruitment from April to August 2017; the specific actual enrollment date remains unconfirmed.
