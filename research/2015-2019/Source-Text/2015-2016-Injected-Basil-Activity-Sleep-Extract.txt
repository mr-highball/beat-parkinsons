Factual extracts of original mouse studies.
Rabbani et al., 2015. PMID 26779273. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4698864/
The intraperitoneally administered sweet basil hydroalcoholic extract and essential oil reduced measured locomotion versus control. Elevated-plus-maze outcomes were collected separately. The publication did not measure human daytime sleepiness.
Askari et al., first published 2016-03-27. PMID 27651944. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5020426/
Injected basil preparations were given before pentobarbital. The report found greater drug-induced sleep duration and shorter latency with the hydroalcoholic extract. The intervention was not fresh whole leaves and the outcome was a drug-dependent mouse assay.
