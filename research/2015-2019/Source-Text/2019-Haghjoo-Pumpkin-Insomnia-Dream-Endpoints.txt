Elham Haghjoo, Khosro Sadeghniiat Haghighi, Fataneh Hashem Dabaghian, Asie Shojaii and Hoorieh Mohammadi Kenari. J Pharm Pharmacogn Res 7(6):471–481. First online October 14, 2019. Trial IRCT20180424039397N1.
The nasal intervention was prepared from whole pumpkin without peel in a sesame-oil base. The comparison was liquid paraffin. The paper excluded Parkinson and Alzheimer diseases. The flow diagram gives 100 randomized and 74 analyzed after two weeks, including 35 active and 39 comparator participants. Outcomes were the Insomnia Severity Index and a validated Persian Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index. Table 3 provides component-level sleep disturbance and daytime dysfunction scores. The generic Pittsburgh instrument contains a bad-dream disturbance question. A separate bad-dream score and the exact Persian item form were not published in the trial. Neither instrument establishes observed dream enactment or REM without atonia. The paper gives no toasted seed oil only arm.
