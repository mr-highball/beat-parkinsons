Registered whole macadamia nut crossover

Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03801837
Coverage: Official design and public dates
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First posted January 14, 2019. This whole-macadamia nut crossover completed November 25, 2019. Its eight-week phases and 35 completers correspond closely to the subsequent bowel conference abstract and Jones cardiometabolic report. The official record has no posted results.
