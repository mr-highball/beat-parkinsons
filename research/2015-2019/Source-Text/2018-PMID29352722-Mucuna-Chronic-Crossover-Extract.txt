
Title: Daily intake of Mucuna pruriens in advanced Parkinson's disease: A 16-week, noninferiority, randomized, crossover, pilot study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29352722/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1353802018300142
First published online: 2018-01-11
PMID: 29352722
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2018.01.014
Trial identifier: NCT02680977
Text coverage: original author abstract. Publisher body and outcome tables remain open.

Fourteen people with advanced Parkinson disease, fluctuations and dyskinesias received repeated roasted-seed powder and marketed levodopa/carbidopa in randomized crossover order. Seven discontinued the plant-powder phase prematurely. Four stopped with gastrointestinal effects and three with progressively poorer motor performance. No participants stopped during the levodopa/carbidopa phase. The authors describe similar efficacy results among those who tolerated both treatments. That comparison concerns a selected subset. The abstract mentions a subsequent plant preparation extension for participants who discontinued. The reported withdrawals and composite results give no separately specified rest-tremor estimate.

The 2017 acute report shares the registration and protocol. The acute comparison and chronic tolerability findings address different outcomes and time windows. Treat them as part of one study program until individual overlap and comparison independence can be verified.
