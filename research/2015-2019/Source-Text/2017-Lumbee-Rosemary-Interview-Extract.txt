Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6149226/
The ethnobotanical survey interviewed Lumbee participants about local medicine and a range of symptoms including tremor. Table 1 describes rosemary leaves and a stem-and-leaf tea. Its rosemary row concerns nonspecific nervous complaints and stimulant use, among other reported uses. No rosemary-specific hallucination frequency or clinical resting tremor result was measured. The article also examines extracts in cells separately from the interviews.
