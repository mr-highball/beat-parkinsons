Title: Lumbee traditional medicine: Neuroprotective activities of medicinal plants used to treat Parkinson's disease-related symptoms
PMID: 28214539
PMCID: PMC6149226
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2017.02.021
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6149226/
Publication date: 2017-02-15; print issue 2017-07-12
Coverage: Factual extract; manuscript Methods and all 32 Table 1 plant rows checked. Separate supplementary tables remain unread. Not an archived full article.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

The interview prompt included tremor, but no inspected Table 1 plant row explicitly assigns tremor, shaking, palsy or paralysis to a preparation. This does not exclude uninspected supplementary material. The rosemary row describes leaves and stem-and-leaf tea for nonspecific nervous concerns and stimulant use; no hallucination-frequency or rest-tremor outcome is measured. Lemon-balm leaf infusion and passionflower flower/fruit tea have calming or sedative uses. Plant-specific specimen vouchers were not confirmed; the study used vouchers when available and photographs otherwise. Cell-model experiments are separate from human clinical effects. PMC manuscript availability in 2018 is not first publication.
