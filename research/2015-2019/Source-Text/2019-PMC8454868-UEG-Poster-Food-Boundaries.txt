P1381 screened pediatric functional gastrointestinal studies and mentions ginger and turmeric in its broader herbal search. The abstract identifies pediatric clinical studies only for psyllium, STW 5 and peppermint oil within the IBS list. Ginger contributes no pediatric study effect in this poster.

P1396 records coffee use by questionnaire and assesses liver outcomes. Another member compares dietary patterns and records components including eggs and potatoes. Separate egg contexts include a food-elimination series in eosinophilic esophagitis and a gastric-emptying practice survey. The broccoli member reports a raw sprout intervention and colorectal mucosal endpoints. A separate poster cites purple-carrot cell work. Olive oil occurs as a vehicle and as a cited dietary intervention. Selected curcumin members address a mixed animal intervention and a separate Crohn disease trial (P1823), with no clear reduction of postoperative endoscopic recurrence at the reported time point.

The orange hits refer to names or affiliations, onion is a histologic description, Pineapple-R is the name of a pediatric pancreatitis study, and raspberry describes lesion appearance. Date-palm or date-fruit intake did not form a study exposure here. Poster-specific exposures and outcomes must stay within their own abstract boundaries.
