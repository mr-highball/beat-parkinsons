Title: Consumption of anthocyanin-rich cherry juice for 12 weeks improves memory and cognition in older adults with mild-to-moderate dementia.
PMID: 26482148
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-015-1083-y
First published: 2017
Authors: Kent K, Charlton K, Roodenrys S, Batterham M, Potter J, Traynor V, Gilbert H, Morgan O
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26482148/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled trial (Kent et al., 2017, Eur J Nutr) in 49 older adults (>=70 years) with mild-to-moderate dementia consuming 200 mL/day anthocyanin-rich sweet cherry (Prunus avium) juice vs negligible-anthocyanin control juice for 12 weeks. ANCOVA controlling for baseline showed significant improvements in the cherry juice arm for verbal fluency (an executive/lexical-retrieval measure, p=0.014), short-term memory (p=0.014), and long-term memory (p<=0.001), plus lower resting systolic blood pressure (p=0.038; DBP p=0.160) with no change in CRP or IL-6. Limits: non-PD mild-to-moderate dementia cohort, sweet cherry juice rather than whole raw or dried fruit, modest sample (n=49).
