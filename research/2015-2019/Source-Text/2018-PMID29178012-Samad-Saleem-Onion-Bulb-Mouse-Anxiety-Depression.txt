Title: Administration of Allium cepa L. bulb attenuates stress-produced anxiety and depression and improves memory in male mice.
PMID: 29178012
DOI: 10.1007/s11011-017-0159-1
First published: 2018
Authors: Samad N, Saleem A
Journal: Metabolic Brain Disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29178012/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Controlled male mouse stress study: oral Allium cepa bulb powder (200 mg/kg/day dissolved in water for 14 days) vs water control before 2-h immobilization stress significantly attenuated stress-induced anxiety-like behavior in both Elevated Plus Maze (EPM) and Light-Dark Activity (LDA) tests, reduced FST depressive-like immobility, and enhanced Morris water maze memory alongside brain SOD/CAT/GPx antioxidant restoration and AChE modulation. Preclinical whole-bulb powder model; no human PD data.
