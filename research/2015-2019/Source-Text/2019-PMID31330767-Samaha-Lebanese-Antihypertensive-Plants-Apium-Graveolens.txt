Title: Antihypertensive Indigenous Lebanese Plants: Ethnopharmacology and a Clinical Trial.

First published: 2019
Authors: Samaha AA, Fawaz M, Salami A, Baydoun S, Eid AH
Journal: Biomolecules

Ethnopharmacological survey of Lebanese herbalists and traditional healers documenting Apium graveolens among the highest-cited indigenous antihypertensive plants (relative frequency of citation RFC=0.92; clinical trial arm tested Mentha, Viola, and Urtica, NOT celery). Traditional practice context only.
