Title: Therapeutic effect of lyophilized, Kefir-fermented milk on constipation among persons with mental and physical disabilities.
PMID: 29105976
DOI: 10.1111/jjns.12189
First published: 2018
Authors: Maki R, Matsukawa M, Matsuduka A, Hashinaga M, Anai H, Yamaoka Y, Hanada K, Fujii C
Journal: Japan journal of nursing science : JJNS
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29105976/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Twelve-week open clinical study in 42 individuals with severe mental and physical disabilities and chronic constipation given 2 g lyophilized kefir-fermented milk with each meal. Daily kefir significantly reduced constipation status compared with baseline (with reduced reliance on rescue glycerol enemas/aperients in responders; some achieved complete relief while others showed no effect). Limitations: uncontrolled pre-post design, lyophilized rather than liquid plain kefir, non-PD neurological/disability cohort.
