Taso and colleagues reported in their July 23, 2018 preprint a nine-person crossover comparing 12 mg of capsinoids with placebo after cycling. Both conditions used the same high-carbohydrate meal. The respiratory exchange ratio was lower at 60, 120 and 180 minutes with capsinoids, and glycogen restoration was inconclusive. The report gives no subjective fatigue estimate.
The same participant characteristics, capsule dose, exercise protocol and overlapping investigators closely match the later journal abstract and registry. Compare the original preprint DOI 10.20944/preprints201807.0411.v1, PMID 32838710 and ISRCTN13356971 as likely three records of one study.
