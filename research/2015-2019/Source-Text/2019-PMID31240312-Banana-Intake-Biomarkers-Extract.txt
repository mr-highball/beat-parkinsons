Title: Discovery and Validation of Banana Intake Biomarkers Using Untargeted Metabolomics in Human Intervention and Cross-sectional Studies

Archive scope: Original author abstract, selected publisher text and linked official trial records. Full tables were unavailable in this pass.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

Vázquez Manjarrez and colleagues used a crossover in 12 healthy adults comparing a 240 g banana intervention with a nutritionally controlled drink comparison. The discovery measurements used urine over 24 hours. A second analysis used 78 selected participants from the distinct KarMeN observational cohort. Dopamine sulfate was one of five putative intake-marker metabolites. The reported test AUC for the preferred combination of methoxyeugenol glucuronide and dopamine sulfate was .92 for higher consumers and .87 for lower consumers. These are food-intake discrimination statistics and give no brain dopamine, apathy, depression or Parkinson disease measure.

Use NCT03581955 for the crossover and DRKS00004890 for the parent observational cohort. The registry also lists tomato exposure for the discovery program. The banana paper cannot supply outcomes for the separate tomato intervention or establish effects of banana on behavior.
