2018 | Lowell et al. | Soybean-oil placebo in an essential voice tremor crossover

Source: published full manuscript, PMID 30585335, DOI 10.1002/lary.27695, online December 25, 2018. Registry NCT01864525.

Sixteen people with essential voice tremor finished an approximately 11-week blinded counterbalanced crossover. Each active or placebo exposure lasted three weeks, with two weeks between conditions. The methods identify the oral placebo as soybean oil in matched softgels and report no supplier or processing grade.

Table 3 reports percentage amplitude modulation before/after placebo of 12.91/12.43, versus 13.49/9.35 before/after octanoic acid. The frequency modulation values are 5.57/5.35 for placebo and 5.19/3.98 for octanoic acid. Baseline-adjusted between-condition comparisons gave p=.0499 and p=.0450 respectively. Table 4 listener ratings and Table 5 Voice Handicap Index-10 did not clearly distinguish periods. Modulation of some vowels is an acoustic tremor measure. The report provides no mean connected-speech loudness, Parkinson-related hypophonia score, oil-free comparator, or separate dose response for soybean oil.
