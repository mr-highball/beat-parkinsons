Title: Protective effect of two essential oils isolated from Rosa damascena Mill. and Lavandula angustifolia Mill, and two classic antioxidants against L-dopa oxidative toxicity induced in healthy mice.
PMID: 27381452
DOI: 10.1016/j.yrtph.2016.06.024
First published: 2016
Authors: Nikolova G, Karamalakova Y, Kovacheva N, Stanev S, Zheleva A, Gadjeva V
Journal: Regulatory toxicology and pharmacology : RTP
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27381452/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical healthy-mouse biochemical study (Nikolova et al., 2016, Regul Toxicol Pharmacol) testing Rosa damascena and Lavandula angustifolia essential oils combined with L-dopa on oxidative stress biomarkers (ROS/RNS, lipid/protein oxidation) in healthy mice. Measures biochemical oxidative markers only; no Parkinson's lesion, motor scoring, dyskinesia, off-time, or culinary oral flower preparation.
