Original study scope
The registry analysis covers 1,474 people with Huntington disease. SF-36 pain and psychological symptoms are analyzed in ordinal models, with 41% reporting pain. The original does not assign dried date fruit or analyze it as a predictor of pain or apathy.

Continuing evidence gaps
Any frozen ingredient effect requires a separately assigned or traceable culinary exposure and a matched symptom estimate.
