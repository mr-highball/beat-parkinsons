Title: A Retrospective Study in Adults with Metabolic Syndrome: Diabetic Risk Factor Response to Daily Consumption of Agaricus bisporus (White Button Mushrooms).
PMID: 27193019
DOI: 10.1007/s11130-016-0552-7
First published: 2016
Authors: Calvo MS, Mehrotra A, Beelman RB, Nadkarni G, Wang L, Cai W
Journal: Plant foods for human nutrition (Dordrecht, Netherlands)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27193019/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Retrospective analysis of n=37 diverse adults with metabolic syndrome who consumed 100 g/d fresh white button mushrooms (Agaricus bisporus) for 16 weeks (with 1-month washout). Daily A. bisporus consumption DOUBLED serum ergothioneine levels from baseline alongside higher ORAC antioxidant capacity and adiponectin and lower CML/methylglyoxal. Crucial human bioavailability bridge linking culinary A. bisporus intake to systemic ergothioneine exposure.
