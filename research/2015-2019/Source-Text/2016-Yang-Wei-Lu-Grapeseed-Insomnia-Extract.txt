Yang Y, Wei K, Lü D. Chinese Medical Herald 2016;13(24):144–147. Wanfang yycyzx201624035.
Author abstract and metadata reviewed through Wanfang and an abstract mirror. Inpatients with primary insomnia in Hefei were randomized to inhale undiluted, 1% or 0.1% lavender oil, inhale grapeseed oil, or receive no treatment. Inhalation lasted ten minutes before bed each night for one week. The authors describe sleep latency, duration, efficiency and maintenance, deep and non-REM sleep, and blood pressure. On listed measures the grapeseed-only group showed no significant difference versus untreated controls (p > .05). Arm-specific counts and estimates are unavailable in the abstract. Timing and group-specific measurements from the original tables were not independently inspected. Processing, oral exposure, Parkinson disease and dream enactment are unreported.
