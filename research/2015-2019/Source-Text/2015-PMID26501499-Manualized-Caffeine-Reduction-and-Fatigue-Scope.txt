Original source scope
The randomized waitlist trial allocated 67 participants with problematic use to immediate or delayed behavioral caffeine reduction. The manual addressed multiple source products. Primary results concerned caffeine intake rather than symptom efficacy.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The discussion explicitly places prospective withdrawal-symptom trajectories outside the study. There is no separate fatigue result, fixed brewed-coffee arm or Parkinson fatigue comparison. The intake cohort is characterized in a related 2012 publication.
