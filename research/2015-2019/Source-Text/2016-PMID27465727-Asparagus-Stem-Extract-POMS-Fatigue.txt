Title: Effect of Enzyme-Treated Asparagus Extract (ETAS) on Psychological Stress in Healthy Individuals.
Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnsv/62/3/62_198/_pdf
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27465727/
PMID: 27465727
DOI: 10.3177/jnsv.62.198
Publication date: 2016
Review depth: full_text
Inspected extent: Complete eight-page publisher original, including printed page 203 Figure 5B.

Design and preparation
Twenty-five healthy volunteers completed a randomized double-blind crossover of a standardized hot-water and enzyme-processed extract of Asparagus officinalis stems and dextrin control. Each period lasted four weeks with a two-week washout. The methods report three 50 mg extract capsules per day during the extract period. The fatigue item is a POMS T score assessed after a mental arithmetic challenge. The study excluded people with extreme fatigue, serious illness or insufficient sleep.

Directional observation
The paper reports a decline from 45.36 to 41.47 T points over the extract period. The within-period significance bracket compares day 28 with day 0. Using the Figure 5B bar heights on the same vertical axis, the placebo period declined by roughly 2 T points and the extract period declined by roughly 4. The difference between those rounded changes therefore favors the extract by about 2 T points. This is a descriptive reading of the figure. The original does not report a paired between-condition confidence interval, significance test or participant-level covariance for that comparison.

Scope for claim 0837
The same botanical species and stem tissue link the extract to culinary asparagus. The laboratory challenge measures subjective fatigue, which is narrower than Parkinson fatigue in daily life. The extract underwent industrial processing. A comparable constituent profile for roasted edible shoots, or a transferable dose and clinical effect, is unresolved. The figure contributes a minimal easing signal under the shared scorecard. It supplies no direct clinical estimate for roasted asparagus.
