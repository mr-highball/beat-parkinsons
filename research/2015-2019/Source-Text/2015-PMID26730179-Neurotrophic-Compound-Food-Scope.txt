Original study scope
The polyphenol review includes tea catechins, citrus compounds, rosemary-derived carnosic or rosmarinic acid and related Salvia material. Experimental pathways and clinical references vary. Extract and constituent work does not isolate fresh rosemary for depressive symptoms or ground rosemary for constipation in Parkinson disease.

Continuing evidence gaps
Trace matched species, route and trained clinical outcomes. Do not attribute a compound finding directly to a culinary preparation.
