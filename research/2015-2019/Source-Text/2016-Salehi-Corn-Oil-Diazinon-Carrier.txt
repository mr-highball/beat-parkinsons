Title: Strain-related differences on response of liver and kidney antioxidant defense system in two rat strains following diazinon exposure
Source: https://doi.org/10.17795/zjrms-5988
Publisher PDF: https://brieflands.com/articles/zjrms-5988.pdf
DOI: 10.17795/zjrms-5988
Publication date: 2016-02-28
Text coverage: Distilled full publisher PDF, including animal groups and vehicle method.

The paper compares 30 female Wistar and 30 female Norway rats in a concentration-response study. Corn oil was the injected pesticide carrier. The controls also received the vehicle. The comparisons concern liver and kidney oxidative markers after toxicant administration. They identify neither an independent oil effect nor oral refined corn oil. The 2012 Jafari report describes similar female rat strains and brain, heart and spleen chemistry. The 2016 paper does not by itself establish independent animals across those reports. FEBS P26.55 and the 2022 five-arm study instead concern male Wistar rats and a vitamin E intervention.
