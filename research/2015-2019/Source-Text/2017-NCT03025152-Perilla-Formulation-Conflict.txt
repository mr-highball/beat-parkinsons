Registered mixed formula and fatigue after gastrectomy
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03025152
Coverage: official protocol or original paper factual summary.

First posted January 19, 2017. The current record lists post-gastrectomy cancer patients, 130 enrolled, termination after 37.7% attrition at one year, and no posted results. Secondary symptom items include physical fatigue and weakness and mental fatigue and taciturnity. The registry describes a formula with added perilla leaf and no Platycodon, at 10 g/day. The linked journal protocol conflicts on recipe, dose and placebo flavor.
