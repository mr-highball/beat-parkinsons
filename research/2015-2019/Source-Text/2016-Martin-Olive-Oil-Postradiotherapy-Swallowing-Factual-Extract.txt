Title: Topical olive oil formulations and postradiotherapy swallowing complaints
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5126143/
Format: Factual extract from the cited original.

Martin and colleagues, first online November 25, 2016. Forty post-radiotherapy patients entered and 38 completed two weeks of topical olive oil, betaine and xylitol products. The study reports improvement in seven VAS dryness or oral-function items, including swallowing due to dryness, in this uncontrolled comparison. One participant with pre-existing complete aphagia discontinued and reported inability to manage saliva. The design supplies no oil attribution, grade or Parkinson outcome.
