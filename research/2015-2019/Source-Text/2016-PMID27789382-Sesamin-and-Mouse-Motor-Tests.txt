Title: Isolated sesamin, levodopa and behavioral outcomes in a mouse Parkinson model
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27789382/
PMID: 27789382
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroscience.2016.10.042
Publication date: 2016-10-24
Date basis: First electronic publication. The journal issue is dated December 17, 2016.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: Original author abstract.

The compound was isolated sesamin in an MPTP mouse model with a levodopa arm. The abstract reports favorable rotarod retention, open-field movement and memory-related readouts. These are broad motor and behavioral surrogates, with no tested toasted sesame oil or facial expressiveness outcome.
