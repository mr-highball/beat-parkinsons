Title: How do Parkinson’s Disease patients manage Ramadan Fasting?
Source: https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/how-do-parkinsons-disease-patients-manage-ramadan-fasting/
Publication date: 2017-06-07
Citation: Kamel W, Damier P, Al-Hashel J. Movement Disorders, volume 32 supplement 2, abstract 1106.
Text coverage: Original summary of the society's complete conference abstract.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The authors describe 20 people with Parkinson disease seen at an outpatient department in Kuwait who planned to observe Ramadan in 2016. They report pre- and post-Ramadan nonmotor scores of 43.1 and 43.1, plus separate quality-of-life and clinical severity measures. The report discusses medication schedules and provides no item-level pain or apathy results, foods, cultivar or preparation records.

The authors, site, calendar year, sample and several identical group outcomes link this abstract to the probable same Kuwait cohort later reported by Kamel and colleagues in 2019, PMID 31176513. The presentations give differing strict-fast and medication descriptions. The abstract and article should not be counted as independent clinical samples.
