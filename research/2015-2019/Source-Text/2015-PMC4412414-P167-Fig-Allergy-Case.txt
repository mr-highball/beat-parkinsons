Title: Immediate anaphylaxis after the ingestion of fig fruit (Ficus carica)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4412414/
PMCID: PMC4412414
DOI: 10.1186/2045-7022-5-S3-P167
Publication date: 2015-03-30
Date basis: First publication of P167, from the 2014 Food Allergy and Anaphylaxis Meeting.
Coverage: Paraphrased complete published conference abstract.

The clinical abstract describes immediate eye and nasal symptoms and severe abdominal pain after ingesting a fig of unspecified preparation. The skin tests used both fresh raw and dried fig, with positive responses to both, and the authors report supportive serum testing. The clinical food exposure and the diagnostic extracts are separate. No double-blind food challenge was performed. The case has no reported drooling severity or Parkinson outcome.
