Title: Butylphthalide soft capsule improve cognitive function in patients with Parkinson's dementia and the effect of daily living
Source: https://js.dnzh.org/magazine/contriution/3034
Original: https://js.dnzh.org/FileUpload/PdfFile/20171228094427.pdf
Publication: 2017, Journal of International Psychiatry 44(6):1060–1063
Depth: Factual summary of original Methods and Table 1 in Chinese.

The study randomized 76 people with Parkinson disease dementia, 38 per group, to oral butylphthalide capsules or donepezil with usual treatment. At 12 weeks mean total UPDRS moved from 46.98 to 20.28 in the butylphthalide arm and 47.02 to 32.68 in the comparison arm. The motor scale is reported in aggregate without a rest-tremor item. Study site and dates differ from Zhou's later clinical report. This is an oral drug comparison without a celery food exposure.
