The smartphone motor study compares waking function with end-of-dose measures in 92 Parkinson participants. Table 2 reports visual hallucination histories in 8 of 20 people with measured morning motor benefit and 26 of 72 without (P=.797). The separate history item is prevalence, without a dietary exposure or serial visual episode count.

The prespecified motor construct compared smartphone-measured waking performance with end-of-dose performance. The study reports 20 people with morning motor improvement. The separate visual history item shows no clear difference between groups. The analysis cannot determine whether sleep, medication carryover or another factor explained the morning motor difference.
