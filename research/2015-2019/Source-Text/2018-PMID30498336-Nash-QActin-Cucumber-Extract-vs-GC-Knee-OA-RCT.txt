Title: Effectiveness of Cucumis sativus extract versus glucosamine-chondroitin in the management of moderate osteoarthritis: a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 30498336
PMCID: PMC6207263
DOI: 10.2147/CIA.S173227
First published: 2018
Authors: Nash RJ, Azantsa BK, Sharp H, Shanmugham V
Journal: Clin Interv Aging
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6207263/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized, double-blind, active-comparator multicenter trial (Nash et al., 2018, Curr Rheumatol Rev, PMC6207263, n=122 randomized / 115 analyzed adults with moderate knee osteoarthritis: 58 CSE, 57 GC) comparing 10 mg twice daily standardized aqueous/hydro-ethanolic Cucumis sativus fruit extract (Q-Actin, standardized to >1% idoBR1) versus 1350 mg twice daily glucosamine-chondroitin for 180 days. Q-Actin reduced WOMAC total score by -70.3% vs -33.7%, VAS pain by -46.3% vs -25.6%, and Lequesne Functional Index by -26.9% vs -8.1% (all p<0.05 vs comparator). Limits: concentrated standardized fresh-cucumber iminosugar extract (not pickled whole cucumber), active comparator rather than inert placebo, industry-affiliated sponsor/authors (IminoTech/Phytoquest), non-Parkinson OA pain.
