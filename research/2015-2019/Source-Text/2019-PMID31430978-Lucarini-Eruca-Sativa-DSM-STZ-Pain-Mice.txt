Title: Eruca sativa Meal against Diabetic Neuropathic Pain: An H2S-Mediated Effect of Glucoerucin.
PMID: 31430978
PMCID: PMC6721019
DOI: 10.3390/molecules24163006
First published: 2019
Authors: Lucarini E, Pagnotta E, Micheli L, Parisio C, Testai L, Martelli A, et al.
Journal: Molecules (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31430978/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Blinded controlled preclinical pain study (Lucarini et al., 2019, Molecules, PMC6721019) in male C57BL/6 mice (10 mice/group across two experimental sets, investigators blinded to treatment) with streptozotocin (STZ)-induced diabetic neuropathic pain. Oral bioactivated Eruca sativa defatted seed meal (DSM, 0.3-1 g/kg p.o., standardized to glucoerucin with myrosinase) and equimolar oral glucoerucin (100 umol/kg) dose-dependently reversed mechanical hyperalgesia and cold allodynia, with sustained efficacy across repeated 8-day dosing without tolerance. Antinociception was mediated by erucin-derived H2S release and Kv7 potassium channel activation (abolished by GSSG, hemoglobin, and Kv7 blocker XE991). No sedative or motor-impairment confound was observed. Limits: non-PD diabetic neuropathy mouse model using myrosinase-bioactivated defatted seed meal rather than raw culinary arugula leaves.
