Title: Cannabinoid Poisoning by Hemp Seed Oil in a Child.
PMID: 27299295
DOI: 10.1097/PEC.0000000000000780
First published: 2017
Authors: Chinello M, Scommegna S, Shardlow A, Mazzoli F, De Giovanni N, Fucci N, Borgiani P, Ciccacci C
Journal: Pediatric emergency care
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27299295/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Pediatric clinical case report of mild cannabinoid poisoning with transient neurological symptoms (resolving with IV hydration) in a preschool child given commercial hemp seed oil for 3 weeks; urinary THC metabolite and low-level delta9-THC contamination in the oil were confirmed. Documents real-world risk of trace THC contamination in inadequately purified hemp seed oils rather than intrinsic culinary seed-oil pharmacology.

Primary PubMed Abstract:
We report a case of mild cannabinoid poisoning in a preschool child, after 3-week ingestion of hemp seed oil prescribed by his pediatrician to strengthen his immune system. The patient presented neurological symptoms that disappeared after intravenous hydration. A possible mild withdrawal syndrome was reported after discharge. The main metabolite of Δ-tetrahydrocannabinol was detected in urine, and very low concentration of Δ-tetrahydrocannabinol was detected in the ingested product. This is, as far as we know, the first report of cannabinoid poisoning after medical prescription of hemp seed oil in a preschool child.
