Title: Ji et al. Mecasin mixed extract and pain behavior in mice
Source: https://www.rroij.com/open-access/the-effects-of-the-novel-herbal-drug-gamijakyakgamchobujatang-kcho1-onneuropathic-pain-in-mice.php?aid=86848
Publication date: 2018
Date basis: Publication year shown on the publisher page. Numeric day and month ordering is ambiguous.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Complete publisher article with Methods, Results and Figure 1.

The investigators compare the mixed KCHO-1 formula in a mouse neuropathy model. Figure 1 reports improved measured mechanical and thermal pain-related responses in mice receiving the mixture. Turmeric is one component of the product. The study supplies no component-specific effect, ground culinary turmeric arm or clinical Parkinson pain outcome. Overlap with the cited 2015 rat thesis remains unverified. The two records should not be used as two independent replications without verification. Round 1 claim 0812.
