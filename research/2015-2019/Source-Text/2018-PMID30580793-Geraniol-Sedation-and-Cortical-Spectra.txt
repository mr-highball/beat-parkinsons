Original source scope
The rat study compared isolated geraniol with controls in activity, drug-induced sleep and cortical recording experiments. Figure 3 reports a longer thiopental-induced sleeping time with geraniol (p = .040). The overall sleep-latency test was inconclusive (p = .873). The separate cortical recording result in Figure 4 reports higher relative delta power (p < .001) and lower theta power (p = .021). The results section and the figure description identify the comparison. A sentence in the discussion appears to describe reduced relative delta power, in conflict with the reported results.

Evidence boundary
This experiment concerns an isolated constituent and drug-induced sleep in rats. It did not measure clinical insomnia severity, natural sleep after eating lemongrass or fall frequency. The sedation result provides a related source for Round 1 claims 0753 and 0134. Neither claim receives a direction from this result.
