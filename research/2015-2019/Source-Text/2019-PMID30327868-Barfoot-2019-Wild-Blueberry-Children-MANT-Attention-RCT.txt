Title: The effects of acute wild blueberry supplementation on the cognition of 7-10-year-old schoolchildren.
PMID: 30327868
PMCID: PMC6768899
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-018-1843-6
First published: 2019
Authors: Barfoot KL, May G, Lamport DJ, Ricketts J, Riddell PM, Williams CM
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6768899/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Single-blind randomized parallel-group trial in n=54 healthy children (7-10 y) consuming a 200 mL wild blueberry drink (253 mg anthocyanins; ~240 g fresh equivalent) vs matched placebo. At 2 h post-dose, blueberry yielded significantly faster reaction times on 120 ms Modified Attention Network Task (MANT) trials without accuracy loss and improved AVLT short-delay/acquisition recall; reading efficiency was null. Limits: acute test in healthy children; powder drink.
