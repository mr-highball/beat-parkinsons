Published OCEAN depression report
Source: https://www.jacc.org/doi/abs/10.1016/j.jchf.2018.03.011?versioned=true
PMID: 30098961
DOI: 10.1016/j.jchf.2018.03.011
Registration: NCT02057406
Coverage: Original publisher abstract, first online August 8, 2018; separate official full results

The article reports 108 randomized patients with heart failure and major depression and 80 qualifying completers. Overall depression comparisons were inconclusive in the intention-to-treat analysis. Exploratory social-function contrasts and correlations with cognitive depressive symptoms address other constructs. The official results identify the arm-specific Hamilton values and the corn comparator. The checked publisher route exposed the author abstract; body tables remain unverified.
