van Nieuwenburg, de Groot and Smeets. August 20, 2019. The Subtle Signaling Strength of Smells: A Masked Odor Enhances Interpersonal Trust.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6710396/
Access and coverage: PMC article, Methods and Results of studies 1 and 2.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

Isolated eugenol served as the clove-like comparison odor and a mask for hexanal. Study 1 randomly assigned 90 women to odor groups in a trust game. Study 2 measured how 35 women rated pictures of other people's faces. The latter ratings were higher with masked hexanal than with eugenol alone. The effect is a comparison for adding hexanal in a sensory experiment. Facial motor function or expression produced by the participants was not measured and no culinary spice was consumed.
