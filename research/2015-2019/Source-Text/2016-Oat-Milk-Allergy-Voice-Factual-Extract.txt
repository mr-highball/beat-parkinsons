Acute dysphonia after oat milk

Source: https://www.jiaci.org/issues/vol26issue1/7-17.pdf
Coverage: Original case, factual extract.

A 70-year-old experienced acute dysphonia, vomiting, laryngeal and thoracic symptoms and hypotension within ten minutes of consuming 200 mL of oat milk. The symptoms were documented during an allergic reaction. The original report does not quantify acoustic loudness or address rolled oats in Parkinson disease.
