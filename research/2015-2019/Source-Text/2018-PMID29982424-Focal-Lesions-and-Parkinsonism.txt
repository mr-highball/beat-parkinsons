Title: Localizing parkinsonism based on focal brain lesions.
PMID: 29982424
PMCID: PMC6061866
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awy161
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6061866/
First publication date: 2018-08-01 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC complete original article HTML and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The investigators map lesions associated with parkinsonism to a shared functional network and compare it with stimulation targets. The study addresses causes of lesion-associated motor symptoms. It does not investigate a dried fig or hallucination-frequency exposure contrast.
