The postmortem study measures copper, iron, manganese and zinc fractions by Parkinson tissue region and disease group. The expected higher iron and lower copper are concentrated in the soluble substantia nigra fraction. USDA documents all four minerals in dried, uncooked figs. That food composition bridge is indirect. The brain study measures neither dietary intake nor visual episode frequency and supplies no claim direction.

The nutrient composition companion is USDA FDC 174665. Its food values do not provide absorption, tissue distribution or symptom timing in these cases.
