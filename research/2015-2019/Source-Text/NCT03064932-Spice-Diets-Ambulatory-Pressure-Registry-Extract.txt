2017 — Registered meal studies and ambulatory blood pressure
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03064932
Coverage: Factual extract of official registration fields.

First posted February 27, 2017. The crossover used four-week dietary periods with differing amounts of a spice blend. It registered ambulatory blood pressure over 24 hours. NCT03063320 is the separate registration for the nested meal challenge.
