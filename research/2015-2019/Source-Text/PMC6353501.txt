Title: First Report of Kufor-Rakeb Syndrome (PARK 9) from India, and a Novel Nonsense Mutation in ATP13A2 Gene

Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Evidence type: Primary case report
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The report identifies an ATP13A2 nonsense variant in a person with juvenile-onset Kufor-Rakeb syndrome. Visual hallucinations are part of the classic phenotype in earlier reports, while this case had no reported hallucinations. That observation is a case phenotype in genetic parkinsonism and does not test a dried fig exposure.
