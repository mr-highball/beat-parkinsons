Title: Oral mucosal contact reaction after cinnamon-flavored mints

Source locator: Case Report and Figures 1–2.

The clinicians described oral burning and mucosal lesions with cinnamon-flavored mints. Findings resolved after the mints were discontinued, recurred upon re-exposure described in the case, and resolved again after discontinuation. This is one documented contact reaction to a flavored product. It supplies no ground cinnamon exposure or speech intelligibility measurement in Parkinson disease.
