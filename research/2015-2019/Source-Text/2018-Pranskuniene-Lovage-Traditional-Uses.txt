Lovage root and leaf uses in Lithuanian interviews
Source: https://doi.org/10.1186/s13002-018-0268-x
Coverage: Factual summary of the primary report.

2018-11-20. Pranskuniene and colleagues interviewed 28 people in Samogitia. Table 1 records two reports of internal root decoction for joint pain and two reports of internal leaf tea for inflammatory diseases. The preparations and indications are separate. It does not test pain intensity after dried lovage leaf or make a clinical Parkinson comparison.
