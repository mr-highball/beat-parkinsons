Factual source extract. Phillips et al., randomized diet pilot, 2018-08-11.
Primary article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175383/
DOI: 10.1002/mds.27390. PMID 30098269.
Original plans: MDS-33-1306-s002.pdf (low fat), MDS-33-1306-s003.pdf (ketogenic).
Low-fat shopping lists explicitly identify fresh chopped basil. One separate line lists a vial of basil with no processing description. The vegetable-and-bean soup recipe calls for two tablespoons of fresh chopped leaves added after cooking.
The ketogenic caprese salad incorporates two tablespoons of fresh chopped basil into an oil dressing, with no cooking step for the leaves.
Study: 47 randomized, 44 started, 38 completed the 8-week follow-up. Day-to-day meal completion was checked. Individual basil consumption was not reported.
Results, before Table 2: 0.45-point greater reduction in the daytime sleepiness item with ketogenic allocation versus low-fat allocation. The reported significance for the Part I difference describes the composite.
Adverse Effects, after Table 3: A ketogenic participant with visual hallucinations had pramipexole reduced and symptoms resolved. The source reports no count or frequency for the hallucinations, no ingredient-specific drinking or eating history, and no basil comparison.
