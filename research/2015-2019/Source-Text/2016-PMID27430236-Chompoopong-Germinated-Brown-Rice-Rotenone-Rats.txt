Study design and findings
- Rotenone-induced Parkinson's-like rat model comparing pretreatment with germinated brown rice (GBR) against rotenone-only controls and 4-phenylbutyric acid (4-PBA) positive controls.
- GBR pretreatment significantly increased open-field ambulation (without significantly changing total mobility duration), attenuated brain apoptotic cells measured by flow cytometry (79.32% vs 85.98% in rotenone, 17.50% intact; viable cells 17.94% vs 10.79%), and lowered serum and brain TNF-alpha (p < 0.05).
- On the pole test (t-turn and t-total descent time), GBR numerically ameliorated scores compared with rotenone, whereas statistical significance for both pole metrics was reached only in the 4-PBA positive control arm.

Relevance and limits for Parkinson's food experiments
- Direct preclinical brown-rice preparation context for cooked brown rice postural instability (0471).
- Limits: Germinated brown rice pretreatment rather than cooked ordinary brown rice; rat rotenone model without direct postural-instability or human Parkinson clinical measurement; abstract-level depth (full text paywalled).
