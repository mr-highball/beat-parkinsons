The original cross-sectional study examined flexor pollicis longus
tendon thickness and hand function alongside thumb rest tremor in
Parkinson disease. The authors do not compare food exposure or cookery
as interventions. Turkey appears as institutional geography. The full
body supplies no baked-turkey dietary rest-tremor outcome.
