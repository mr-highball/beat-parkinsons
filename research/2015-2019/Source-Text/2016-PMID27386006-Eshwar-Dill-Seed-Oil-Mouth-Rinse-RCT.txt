Title: Comparison of Dill Seed Oil Mouth Rinse and Chlorhexidine Mouth Rinse on Plaque Levels and Gingivitis - A Double Blind Randomized Clinical Trial.
PMID: 27386006
PMCID: PMC4911748
DOI: 10.2174/1874210601610010207
First published: 2016
Authors: Eshwar S, K R, Jain V, Manvi S, Kohli S, Bhatia S
Journal: The open dentistry journal
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27386006/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind randomized clinical trial (Eshwar et al., 2016, Open Dent J, PMC4911748) in 90 adults comparing dill seed oil mouth rinse vs 0.2% chlorhexidine rinse over 90 days on gingival and plaque indices (no significant difference between rinses). Non-swallowed topical oral rinse measuring periodontal outcomes only; does not evaluate salivary flow or Parkinson's drooling.
