Title: Hospitalization in people with dementia with Lewy bodies: Frequency, duration, and cost implications

Source depth: Complete original PMC structured article body
Format: Factual digest of the inspected material

The South London study compares hospital admissions in 194 patients with dementia with Lewy bodies and 776 matched Alzheimer patients. Its discussion points to a separate visual hallucination research program. Admissions and costs are the evaluated outcomes, without fig or visual episode-rate data.
