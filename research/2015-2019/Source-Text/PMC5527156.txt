The diffusion tensor imaging meta-analysis combines 39 neuroimaging studies involving 1,855 participants. Its extracted outcomes are fractional anisotropy and mean diffusivity across regions. Visual hallucination status in individual subgroup descriptions does not supply a dried fig or episode-frequency comparison.
