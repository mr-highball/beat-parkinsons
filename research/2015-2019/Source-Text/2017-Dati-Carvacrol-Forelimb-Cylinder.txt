Forelimb asymmetry after isolated carvacrol in mice
Primary source: https://pure.au.dk/portal/da/publications/carvacrol-promotes-neuroprotection-in-the-mouse-hemiparkinsonian-/
PMID: 28526576
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroscience.2017.05.013
Coverage: factual extract from primary indexed abstract and university record.

Dati and colleagues reported that isolated carvacrol reduced the asymmetry of forelimb use in a cylinder test after a unilateral 6-hydroxydopamine lesion in mice. The accessible author abstract gives the direction without numeric group estimates; the publisher full article was not available during this pass. The test measures limb-use asymmetry during rearing. Handwriting kinematics, fine pen control and fresh summer savory ingestion are outside the reported measurements.
