Title: Efficacy of Cabbage Leaf Wraps in the Treatment of Symptomatic Osteoarthritis of the Knee: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 26889617
DOI: 10.1097/AJP.0000000000000352
First published: 2016
Authors: Lauche R, Gräf N, Cramer H, Al-Abtah J, Dobos G, Saha FJ
Journal: The Clinical journal of pain
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26889617/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized controlled trial in patients with Kellgren-Lawrence stage II-III knee osteoarthritis comparing 4 weeks of TOPICAL raw cabbage leaf wraps (>=2 h/day) vs topical diclofenac gel vs usual care on knee VAS pain and WOMAC. Route contrast only: external topical raw leaf poultices are strictly unusable as evidence for ingested fermented, cooked, or raw green cabbage in PD.
