Title: Dynamic Oral Texture Properties of Selected Indigenous Complementary Porridges Used in African Communities.

First published: 2019
Authors: Makame J, Cronje T, Emmambux NM, De Kock H
Journal: Foods (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31234403/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Sensory rheology study (Makame et al., 2019, Foods, PMC6617364) evaluating dynamic oral texture of 13 indigenous African complementary infant porridges (including cowpea) using a 10-person trained panel simulating infant chewing. Assessed porridge viscosity/stickiness rather than human clinical dysphagia or Parkinson's swallowing difficulty.
