The viewpoint reviews Lewy body dementia psychosis evidence and quotes two caregiver reports from a 2009 association forum. The reports concern safety, general psychosis, functioning and treatment tradeoffs. They provide no measured dried fig exposure or separate visual episode rate.

The authors quote two caregiver accounts dated 9 January 2009 from the Lewy Body Dementia Association Behavioral Issues forum. One describes concern about safety. The other reports perceived improvement in overall psychosis and daily functioning during risperidone use. Original forum opening date, full thread and visual modality remain unverified. The excerpts provide a separate personal-report provenance trail. The scholarly article contains no new treatment cohort.
