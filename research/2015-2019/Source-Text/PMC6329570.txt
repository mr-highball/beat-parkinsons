This 2016 Todd and Teale guide describes delirium screening and clinical timing, including visual and auditory signs. A 2017 version has a distinct DOI and a substantially overlapping main body, without a new clinical cohort. Neither edition measures a dietary exposure or Parkinson visual episode frequency.

The later edition is PMID 28148581 and PMCID PMC6297595, published in the February 2017 issue. The two originals share authors, title, clinical approach and the core visual and auditory screening text. Their issue-specific bibliographic identifiers remain distinct.
