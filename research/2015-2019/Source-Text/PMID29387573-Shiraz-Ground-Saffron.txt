Linked Shiraz reports and their unresolved differences
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5787996/
PMID: 29387573
PMCID: PMC5787996
Registration: IRCT2013110915334N1
Coverage: Original complete Jelodar report, Methods, Results and Figures 1–2; Sahraian author abstract; official registry

In the January–February 2018 issue Jelodar and colleagues describe Khorasan dried stigma ground to powder and administered as one 30 mg capsule daily with fluoxetine 20 mg. Controls received placebo with the same fluoxetine. After four weeks mean Beck scores went from 22.73 (SEM 0.90) to 15.01 (0.87) and from 22.00 (0.98) to 17.73 (1.09), respectively. The reported intergroup p = .056. The original flow figure reports 20 analyzed in each arm and no recorded losses. The same registered trial family shares authors, site, 40 randomized participants, total daily dose and assessment, but the 2015 abstract reports 19 versus 11 completers and p = .560. The original registry planned six weeks and twice-daily dosing. The published and registry accounts leave attrition, exact between-arm inference and schedule differences unresolved. Neither report provides a Parkinson cohort.
