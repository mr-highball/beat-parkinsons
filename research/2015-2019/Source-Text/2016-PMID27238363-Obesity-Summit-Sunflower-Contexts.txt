PO2.161 (SA745) reports a cross-sectional study of 151 Serbian students aged 15 to 19. The authors list refined sunflower oils among frequently consumed foods and oils and report group summaries for grade point average and a general intelligence battery. No oil-specific estimate or executive function test is specified. PO2.229 (SA813) includes sunflower oil in a reduced-energy Balkan menu and administers a probiotic concurrently. Its reported changes concern weight, BMI and body composition. Neither member establishes cold-pressed exposure, an oil-specific cognitive contrast or a Parkinson population. These are separate members and must not be combined.

Supplementary member review
