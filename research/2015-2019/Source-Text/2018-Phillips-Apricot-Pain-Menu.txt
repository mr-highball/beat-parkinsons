Phillips et al. 2018, PMID 30098269, DOI 10.1002/mds.27390, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175383/. Randomized 23 low-fat and 24 ketogenic for eight weeks, 38 completed, regression imputation. Each diet menu includes weekend fruit-and-cream choice permitting two apricots, sliced as necessary, alternative to seven other fruit options (low-fat printed p.93, ketogenic p.100). Low-fat apricot chicken recipe separately uses canned apricots. Participants ticked meal completion; fruit choice and individual dose are unavailable. Authors report -0.64 point ketogenic versus low-fat difference in change for broad pain-and-other-sensations item, not item p or confidence interval. No isolated apricot effect or raw exposure confirmed.
