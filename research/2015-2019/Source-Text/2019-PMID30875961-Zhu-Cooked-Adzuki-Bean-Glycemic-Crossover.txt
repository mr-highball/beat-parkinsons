Title: Acute Effects of Three Cooked Non-Cereal Starchy Foods on Postprandial Glycemic Responses and in Vitro Carbohydrate Digestion in Comparison with Whole Grains: A Randomized Trial.
PMID: 30875961
PMCID: PMC6471337
DOI: 10.3390/nu11030634
First published: 2019
Authors: Zhu R, Fan Z, Han Y, Li S, Li G, Wang L, et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30875961/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Acute randomized crossover trial (Zhu et al., 2019, Nutrients, PMC6471337) in 10 healthy adults comparing postprandial glycemic responses of domestically cooked lily bulb, lotus seed, adlay, waxy black rice, millet, and pre-soaked cooked adzuki beans. Cooked adzuki bean achieved a low glycemic index (GI 21-51) and low in vitro starch hydrolysis index (37.7-61.1%). Limits: acute metabolic test in 10 healthy adults; no Parkinson's motor, sleepiness, fatigue, or non-motor symptom outcomes measured.
