Title: Toxico-Pathological Studies of Foeniculum vulgare Plant in Mice
DOI: 10.4236/ojpathology.2018.84014
Publication date: 2018-08-14
Citation: Al-Hizab and colleagues. Open Journal of Pathology 8, 123–131.
Source: https://www.scirp.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=86639
Coverage: Published full text and clinical-sign descriptions.

The investigators prepared fresh juice from stems and leaves. The body describes an injection screen, a separate single-dose series labeled oral in Methods, and a seven-day injection comparison. Jumping and other visible signs were reported during the acute work. Intermittent convulsions and other systemic findings were reported after repeated injection. The abstract expresses the lethality estimate as mass per body weight, and the Results give the quantity as liquid volume per body weight with an injection label. The report contains inconsistencies in its descriptions of route and units. No separate resting-tremor measurement, Parkinson model or toasted fruit appears.
