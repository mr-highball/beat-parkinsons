Title: Volatile compounds and fatty acid methyl esters in raw, roasted and fried cumin seeds
Source: https://www.phytojournal.com/archives/2018/vol7issue4/PartS/7-4-10-196.pdf
Publication year: 2018
Issue: Journal of Pharmacognosy and Phytochemistry 7(4), 1125–1129.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: Full original chemistry report.

The investigators compared differently processed Cuminum cyminum samples using gas chromatography of extracted oils and fatty acid methyl esters. The results text describes a lower total volatile-oil fraction after roasting than in the condition labeled raw, alongside changes in the reported fatty acid categories. The methods also heat the sample called raw during preparation, and one table repeats the roast label on two different entries. The descriptive chemistry cannot establish equivalent exposure between roasted seed and the aqueous extract in the Parkinson mouse study. The report measured no mammalian motor outcome.
