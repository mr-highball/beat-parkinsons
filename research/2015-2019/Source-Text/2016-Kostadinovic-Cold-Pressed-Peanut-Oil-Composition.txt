Title: Physicochemical characterization and quality of cold-pressed peanut oil obtained from organically produced peanuts from Macedonian “Virginia” variety
DOI: 10.3989/gya.0369151
Publication year: 2016
Citation: Kostadinović Veličkovska, Mitrev and Mihajlov. Grasas y Aceites 67(1), e118, January–March issue.
Source: https://eprints.ugd.edu.mk/14344/1/GYA2013119_e118-0369151a.pdf
Text coverage: Publisher paper in the university repository, methods and composition findings.

The analysis specifies cold-pressed oil from the Virginia peanut variety. Oleic and linoleic acid proportions were 34.19 ± 0.01% and 36.13 ± 0.01%. The paper reports tocopherols and phytosterols and measures radical-scavenging activity in a chemical assay. This material characterization provides no dose-exposure bridge to tremor and includes no patient, animal or behavioral comparison.
