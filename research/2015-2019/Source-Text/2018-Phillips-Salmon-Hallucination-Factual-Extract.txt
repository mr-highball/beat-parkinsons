Title: Salmon preparation and visual hallucination reporting in the Phillips trial.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175383/

The Phillips eight-week diet trial randomized 47 people with Parkinson disease, with 44 starting and 38 completing. The low-fat plan shows a canned-salmon salad on printed page 64. The ketogenic plan shows a salmon fillet cooked in a pan and served with tarragon-dill cream sauce on printed page 81. The recipes do not specify grilled salmon. The trial gives no consumed-food diary at participant level. One person assigned to the ketogenic arm developed visual hallucinations that resolved after pramipexole reduction. No event timing by meal, individual salmon intake or salmon-specific visual hallucination frequency is reported.
