Title: Motor subtypes and other risk factors associated with drooling in Parkinson's disease patients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29315470/
PMID: 29315470
DOI: 10.1111/ane.12893
Publication date: 2018-01-09
Source depth: Original author abstract. Full outcome tables remain open.
Date basis: Indexed online-first date. The issue followed in May 2018.
Design: Observational Parkinson cohort in Suzhou

The authors used the UPDRS Part II saliva item and report drooling in 320/586. The adjusted odds ratio for non-tremor-dominant subtype was 1.865 (95% CI 1.137–3.060), and for male sex was 1.951 (1.326–2.869). The analysis also reports associations with daytime sleepiness, motor severity, nighttime sleep and constipation. The abstract does not specify all item cutoffs, group cross-tabulations or food exposures. This study is from a different institution from the 2019 Wenzhou abstract. No measured raw-fig association.
