McCormick et al. Original water polo crossover published November 14, 2016. PMID 27895542. DOI 10.1186/s12970-016-0151-x. The article reports retrospective registration ACTRN12616001080415.
Nine well-trained male water polo players completed randomized, blinded periods of 90 mL/day Montmorency tart cherry concentrate in water and a mixed lime, cranberry and raspberry cordial placebo, each for six days with a five-week washout. On waking, players used a 6–20 scale for perceived recovery and rated muscle soreness. A simulated match took place on the final day. Table 3 and the results text report no resolved condition, time or interaction effect for recovery or soreness, with p > .05. The report measures perceived recovery and match performance. It provides no separate clinical fatigue estimate in a Parkinson population.
