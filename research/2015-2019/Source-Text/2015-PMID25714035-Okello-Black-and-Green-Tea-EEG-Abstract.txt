Title: Green and black tea and EEG in healthy adults

Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: original abstract, including participant count and abstracted results

Eight healthy volunteers took part in a feasibility study of green and black tea and resting EEG. The abstract reports increases in several bands one hour after tea consumption and considerable individual variation. The reported theta significance between 30 minutes and one hour is attributed specifically to green tea. EEG changes are physiological observations, with no Parkinson diagnoses, bradykinesia or rest-tremor measurements in the report inspected. Serving chemistry, comparator details and raw outcomes would require the original article body.
