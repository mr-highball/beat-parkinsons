PL5 describes an animal gastrointestinal comparison with grape-derived extracellular vesicles as one comparator. The presentation does not test whole or frozen grapes, a human Parkinson population or rigidity. Vesicle preparations and comparator roles must remain distinct from the causal effect of eating fruit. This is a conference abstract, not a full clinical report.
