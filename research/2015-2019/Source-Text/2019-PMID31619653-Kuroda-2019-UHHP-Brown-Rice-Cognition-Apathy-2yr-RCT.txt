Title: Influence of Ultra-High Hydrostatic Pressurizing Brown Rice on Cognitive Functions and Mental Health of Elderly Japanese Individuals: A 2-Year Randomized and Controlled Trial.
PMID: 31619653
DOI: 10.3177/jnsv.65.S80
First published: 2019
Authors: Kuroda Y, Matsuzaki K, Wakatsuki H, Shido O, Harauma A, Moriguchi T
Journal: Journal of nutritional science and vitaminology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31619653/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twenty-four-month randomized controlled trial in n=52 healthy Japanese older adults assigned to 100 g/d ultra-high hydrostatic pressurized brown rice (UHHPBR) vs polished white rice. Over 24 months, UHHPBR significantly improved Frontal Assessment Battery (FAB) sub-item 1 change, lowered Apathy Scale scores, and shortened CADi total answer time (information processing speed) vs white rice, with no significant Zung depression reduction reported. Limits: pressurized brown rice, non-PD healthy older adults, same research group as Matsuzaki 2025.
