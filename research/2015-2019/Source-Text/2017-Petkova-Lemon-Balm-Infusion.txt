Rosmarinic acid across nine brewed lemon balm samples
Source: https://bcc.bas.bg/BCC_Volumes/Volume_49_Special_D_2017/BCC2017-49-SI-D-Juchnovski.pdf
Coverage: factual extract of Methods and Table 3
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Petkova and colleagues infused commercial and garden samples for 20 minutes at 95 °C using 1 g of dried material in 100 mL of water. HPLC values in Table 3 ranged from 2.40 to 23.10 mg of rosmarinic acid per gram of starting material. This shows a range in brewed leaf chemistry and supplies no conversion between tea and an extract dose or clinical result.
