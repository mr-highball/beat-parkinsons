Title: Effects of blueberry supplementation on blood pressure: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials.
PMID: 27654329
DOI: 10.1038/jhh.2016.70
First published: 2017
Authors: Zhu Y, Sun J, Lu W, Wang X, Wang X, Han Z, et al.
Journal: Journal of human hypertension
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27654329/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 6 blueberry RCTs (n=204): no significant effect on SBP (-0.28 mmHg, 95% CI -1.11 to 0.56) or DBP (-0.50 mmHg, -1.24 to 0.24). Context-only synthesis; no PD or orthostatic lightheadedness outcomes.
