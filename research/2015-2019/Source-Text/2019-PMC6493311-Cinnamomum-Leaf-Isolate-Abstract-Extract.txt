Kua and colleagues study ABL006 isolated from Cinnamomum osmophloeum leaves and report reduced extracellular-vesicle release in HepG2 cell experiments. The abstract does not establish a more specific chemical identity for ABL006.

Species, plant part and isolated preparation differ from ordinary ground culinary cinnamon bark. Neither oral cinnamon consumption nor clinical pain, fatigue or Parkinson disease is tested. Aquaculture cod and salmon contexts elsewhere in the collection concern the animals studied, not human food intake. Distinct abstracts cannot supply each other's exposure or endpoint.
