Title: Pimpinella anisum in the treatment of functional dyspepsia: A double-blind, randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 25767516
PMCID: PMC4354059
First published: 2015
Authors: Ghoshegir SA, Mazaheri M, Ghannadi A, Feizi A, Babaeian M, Tanhaee M, Karimi M, Adibi P
Journal: Journal of research in medical sciences : the official journal of Isfahan University of Medical Sciences
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4354059/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Full-text verified double-blind RCT (PMC4354059; IRCT2013101214980) in n=107 adults (18-65 y) with Rome III postprandial distress syndrome (PDS) functional dyspepsia randomized to ground Pimpinella anisum powder 3 g tid after meals (9 g/day, n=47) or cornstarch placebo (n=60) for 4 weeks with follow-up to week 12. Total FD severity fell from 10.6 +/- 4.1 to 7.04 (wk 2), 2.44 (wk 4), and 1.08 (wk 12) vs placebo 10.96 -> 12.30 -> 13.05 -> 13.30 (all p = 0.0001). No serious side effects; 4 withdrawals/arm. CRITICAL FULL-TEXT VERIFICATION: Abstract Conclusion contains an editorial typo stating 'postpartum depression' instead of 'postprandial distress syndrome (PDS)'; the trial strictly evaluated upper-GI dyspepsia and explicitly EXCLUDED patients with major depression, IBS, and dysphagia.
