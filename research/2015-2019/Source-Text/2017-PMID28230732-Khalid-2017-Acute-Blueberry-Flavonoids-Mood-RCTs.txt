Title: Effects of Acute Blueberry Flavonoids on Mood in Children and Young Adults.
PMID: 28230732
PMCID: PMC5331589
DOI: 10.3390/nu9020158
First published: 2017
Authors: Khalid S, Barfoot KL, May G, Lamport DJ, Reynolds SA, Williams CM
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5331589/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Two double-blind placebo-controlled acute trials (Study 1 crossover n=21 young adults 18-21 y; Study 2 parallel n=50 children 7-10 y) assessing PANAS mood 2 h after flavonoid-rich wild blueberry drink vs matched placebo. Both samples showed significant drink-by-session increases in positive affect without altering negative affect. Limits: acute 2-h affect in healthy youth, not clinical depression or PD.
