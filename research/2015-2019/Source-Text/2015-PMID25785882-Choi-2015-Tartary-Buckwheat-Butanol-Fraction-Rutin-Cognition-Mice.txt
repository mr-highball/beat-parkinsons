Title: The n-Butanol Fraction and Rutin from Tartary Buckwheat Improve Cognition and Memory in an In Vivo Model of Amyloid-β-Induced Alzheimer's Disease.
PMID: 25785882
DOI: 10.1089/jmf.2014.3292
First published: 2015
Authors: Choi JY, Lee JM, Lee DG, Cho S, Yoon YH
Journal: Journal of medicinal food
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25785882/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Factual summary: Preclinical amyloid-beta mouse model testing oral n-butanol fraction and isolated rutin from Tartary buckwheat. Both fractions alleviated A-beta-induced T-maze, object recognition, and Morris water maze learning/memory deficits and attenuated brain, liver, and kidney nitric oxide and lipid peroxidation.

Verified PubMed abstract: This study examined the beneficial effects of the n-butanol fraction and rutin extracted from tartary buckwheat (TB) on learning and memory deficits in a mouse model of amyloid β (Aβ)-induced Alzheimer's disease (AD). Learning and memory were assessed using the T-maze, object recognition, and Morris water maze tests. Animals administered Aβ showed impaired cognition and memory, which were alleviated by oral administration of an n-butanol fraction and rutin extracted from TB. Similarly, Aβ-induced increases in nitric oxide formation and lipid peroxidation in the brain, liver, and kidneys were attenuated by treatment with n-butanol fraction and rutin from TB in addition to antioxidant effects observed in control (nonAβ-treated) animals. The results of the present study suggest that the n-butanol fraction and rutin extracted from TB are protective against and have possible therapeutic applications for the treatment of AD.
