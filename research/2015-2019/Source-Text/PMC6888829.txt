Title: Skin fairness is a better predictor for impaired physical and mental health than hair redness
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6888829/
PMID: 31792316
PMCID: PMC6888829
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-019-54662-5
Publication date: 2019-12-02
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC and PubMed metadata.
Design: Cross-sectional self-reported health survey

The survey relates skin color and hair redness to self-reported physical and mental health items, including perceptual complaints. It does not use a food intervention, assign a Parkinson-specific visual outcome or measure dried fig consumption.
