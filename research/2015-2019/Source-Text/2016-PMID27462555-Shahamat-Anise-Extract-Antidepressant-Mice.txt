Title: Evaluation of antidepressant-like effects of aqueous and ethanolic extracts of Pimpinella anisum fruit in mice.
PMID: 27462555
PMCID: PMC4930539
First published: 2016
Authors: Shahamat Z, Abbasi-Maleki S, Mohammadi Motamed S
Journal: Avicenna journal of phytomedicine
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4930539/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Full-text verified preclinical study (PMC4930539) in mice evaluating intraperitoneal aqueous and ethanolic extracts of Pimpinella anisum fruit (50, 100, 200 mg/kg i.p.) versus fluoxetine (20 mg/kg) and imipramine (30 mg/kg) in forced swimming test (FST) and tail suspension test (TST). Aqueous extract 100 and 200 mg/kg reduced FST immobility by 50.96% and 60.78% (p < 0.001; 50 mg/kg nonsignificant in FST), and ethanolic extract 50, 100, and 200 mg/kg reduced FST immobility by 42.57%, 58.92%, and 65.53% (p < 0.001), increasing swimming time without increasing climbing (fluoxetine-like profile). Non-oral i.p. extract in mice outside PD.
