Title: Effect of administering kefir on the changes in fecal microbiota and symptoms of inflammatory bowel disease: A randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 30662004
PMCID: PMC6428516
DOI: 10.5152/tjg.2018.18227
First published: 2019
Authors: Yılmaz İ, Dolar ME, Özpınar H
Journal: The Turkish journal of gastroenterology : the official journal of Turkish Society of Gastroenterology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30662004/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Single-center open-label randomized controlled 4-week trial in 45 IBD patients (25 kefir 400 mL/day vs 20 control). Fecal Lactobacillus increased significantly (UC p=0.001, CD p=0.005). In Crohn disease, ESR and CRP fell, hemoglobin rose, bloating improved in weeks 3-4 (p=0.012), and feeling-good scores rose (p=0.032); abdominal pain and stool frequency were not reported as significantly improved overall.
