Title: Fatty acid supply with complementary foods and LC-PUFA status in healthy infants: results of a randomised controlled trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26169870/
PMID: 26169870
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-015-0982-2
Publication date: 2015-07-14
Coverage: Factual extract from the author's abstract, with first public date and family verified against the original publisher and trial registry.

The randomized PINGU study compared jarred complementary foods containing salmon twice weekly, rapeseed oil, or a corn-oil reference in term infants. Red cell fatty acid measurements were complete for 158 children and plasma measurements for 155. The salmon arm had higher measured EPA and DHA percentages than the corn-oil arm. Fatty acid biomarkers measure exposure and metabolism. The paper's abstract reports no Parkinson or bradykinesia endpoint.

The paper identifies NCT01487889. The developmental PINGU article reports an overlapping study family and evaluates Bayley psychomotor outcomes separately.
