Title: Patient-reported outcome measures in dysphagia: a systematic review of instrument development and validation
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5675017/
PMID: 28375450
PMCID: PMC5675017
DOI: 10.1093/dote/dow028
Publication date: 2017-03-29
Date basis: PMC online publication metadata. The issue followed in May.
Source depth: Original review body and instrument tables.

The authors extracted 34 instruments after reviewing 4,950 abstracts. The Parkinson ROMP comprises separate speech, swallowing and saliva-control constructs. The table lists 23 total items, with seven in the swallowing domain. The review's evaluation of ROMP-swallowing concerns measurement quality and does not add saliva outcomes to the underlying 2011 Kalf validation. Neither report tests a culinary exposure. Keep study instruments and measured changes separate.
