Ginger extract and open-arm time after dark-box exposure
Source: https://www.ijper.org/article/51/3s1/s159.pdf
DOI: 10.5530/ijper.51.3s.4
Coverage: Original Methods and Table 1, factual text

Fadaki, Modaresi and Sajjadian reported six groups of ten female mice in the July–September 2017 issue. Table 1 gives open-arm time means of 21.43 in the anxiety group and 58.65, 65.53 and 67.19 after 50, 100 and 200 mg/kg extract. The table does not label those units. Reported locomotor activity differed across groups. The Results describe movement at the highest dose inconsistently relative to diazepam. The Methods omit extract process, route and schedule details. This experiment gives anxiety-like behavior data in mice without a Parkinson model.
