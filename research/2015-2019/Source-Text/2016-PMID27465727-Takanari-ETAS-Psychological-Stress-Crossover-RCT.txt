Title: Effect of Enzyme-Treated Asparagus Extract (ETAS) on Psychological Stress in Healthy Individuals.
PMID: 27465727
DOI: 10.3177/jnsv.62.198
First published: 2016
Authors: Takanari J, Nakahigashi J, Sato A, Waki H, Miyazaki S, Uebaba K, et al.
Journal: Journal of nutritional science and vitaminology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27465727/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial (Takanari et al., 2016, J Nutr Sci Vitaminol) in 25 healthy volunteers consuming 150 mg/day ETAS (enzyme-treated Asparagus officinalis stem extract) or dextrin placebo for 28 days with a washout period, exposed to the Uchida-Kraepelin (U-K) arithmetic stress task. ETAS improved POMS Depression-Dejection and Fatigue ratings, improved self-reported 'Feel tired', 'Hard to get up', and 'Feel heavy' items, increased post-stress salivary sIgA, and increased total and correct answers on the U-K task, whereas serum/salivary cortisol and plasma catecholamines were unchanged. Limits: Amino Up authored/funded; local figure audit (Figure 5) notes key POMS significance markers compare within-period Day 0 vs Day 28 rather than formal between-arm crossover interactions; non-PD healthy cohort and concentrated stem extract rather than roasted/steamed spears.
