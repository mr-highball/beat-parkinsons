Title: Accidental excess nutmeg ingestion with neurological examination
Publication: 2015-01
PMID: 26219944
Source: https://www.njmonline.nl/article_ft.php?a=1536&d=1013&i=179
Text type: original-source evidence summary

Roeters van Lennep and colleagues report an acute clinical presentation after accidental excess nutmeg in food and alcohol coexposure. The symptoms include confusion, dizziness and dry mouth. The report describes the subsequent neurological examination as unremarkable. Tremor is mentioned in the general discussion of previous literature, without a documented tremor in this patient. The case includes no Parkinson comparison or inference about symptoms at ordinary culinary intake.
