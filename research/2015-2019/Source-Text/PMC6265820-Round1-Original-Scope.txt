Exact frozen claim contexts
Claim 0243 | PMCID:PMC6265820 | ground cinnamon | anxiety severity
Current exact disposition: related
P08-050 tested cinnamon extract in a DSS mouse colitis model and measured intestinal outcomes. The preparation, disease model and outcomes do not answer ground cinnamon and Parkinson anxiety.

Claim 0343 | PMCID:PMC6265820 | cold-pressed olive oil | anxiety severity
Current exact disposition: related
OR22-05 and P08-115 studied isolated hydroxytyrosol or combinations of compounds in cell-free alpha-synuclein assays. No cold-pressed olive oil preparation or Parkinson anxiety outcome was measured.

Claim 0493 | PMCID:PMC6265820 | frozen raspberry | anxiety severity
Current exact disposition: related
P08-100 used frozen raspberries in adults with overweight or obesity and measured circulating metabolites. P08-103 measured metabolic outcomes in high-fat-fed mice. Neither assessed anxiety in Parkinson disease.

Claim 0860 | PMCID:PMC6265820 | raw grape | anxiety severity
Current exact disposition: related
OR22-05 cites grapes as one food source of hydroxytyrosol and tests an isolated compound in an alpha-synuclein fibril assay. P08-077 tested freeze-dried powder on lipid endpoints in metabolic syndrome. Neither comparison measured anxiety after raw grapes in Parkinson disease.

Claim 0937 | PMCID:PMC6265820 | grated ginger | anxiety severity
Current exact disposition: related
P08-039 and P08-047 report ginger extracts in rat arthritis and mouse obesity models. The anxiety experiment in the collection instead uses a milk-derived product. No Parkinson anxiety comparison after grated ginger appears in these members.

Claim 0953 | PMCID:PMC6265820 | boiled barley | anxiety severity
Current exact disposition: related
OR06-03 used a barley beta-glucan preparation in obese mice with psychosocial stress. The comparison measured cardiac dysfunction and oxidative changes, without boiled barley or a Parkinson anxiety outcome.
