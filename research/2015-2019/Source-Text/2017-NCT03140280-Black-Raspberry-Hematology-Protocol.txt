Black raspberry powder hematology trial registration
https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03140280

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT03140280 on May 4, 2017. It lists an open-label, single-group intervention for adults with myelodysplastic syndrome or overlapping myelodysplastic and myeloproliferative disease. The oral food preparation was freeze-dried black raspberry powder in water, with 25 g per dose twice daily and a 12-week initial treatment period. The protocol and outcome posting do not identify a controlled anxiety measure. The 2022 clinical report and posted results refer to this same registration.
