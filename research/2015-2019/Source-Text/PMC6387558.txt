Title: Brain glucose metabolism in Lewy body dementia: implications for diagnostic criteria
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6387558/
PMID: 30797240
PMCID: PMC6387558
DOI: 10.1186/s13195-019-0473-4
Publication date: 2019-02-23
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC and PubMed metadata.
Design: Retrospective FDG-PET diagnostic cohort

Seventy-two patients with diverse entry diagnoses ultimately had established dementia with Lewy bodies. Cases were compared by blinded PET classification and follow-up diagnosis. Relatives sometimes reported visual symptoms without psychometric confirmation at entry. The visual symptom fractions record diagnostic presence by group, without an exposure-linked event measure.
