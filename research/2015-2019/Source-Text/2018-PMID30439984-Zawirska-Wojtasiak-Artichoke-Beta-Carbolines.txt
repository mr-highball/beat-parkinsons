Title: Bioactivity of selected materials for coffee substitute.
PMID: 30439984
PMCID: PMC6237330
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0206762
First published: 2018
Authors: Zawirska-Wojtasiak, Piechowska, Wojtowicz, Przygoński, Mildner-Szkudlarz
Journal: PloS one
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30439984/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Analytical chemistry study measuring neuroactive beta-carbolines and thermal processing toxicants in roasted chicory and roasted artichoke coffee substitutes. Roasted artichoke contained 2.5 ug/g harman and 3.1 ug/g norharman (higher than chicory) alongside markedly higher acrylamide, carboxymethyllysine, and furans. Limits: laboratory analytical assay on roasted coffee substitute; no human or animal symptom measurement.
