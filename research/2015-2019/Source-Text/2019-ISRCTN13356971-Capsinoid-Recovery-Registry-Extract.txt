ISRCTN13356971 was registered August 7, 2019. It lists enrollment and completion in 2016–2017 despite the prospective label. Nine men received capsinoids and placebo in a cycling crossover with metabolic outcomes. The matching 2018 preprint preceded registration.
The official record lists enrollment from April 15 to May 15, 2016 and completion April 16, 2017. The relevant outcomes concern fat use and carbohydrate recovery. The record supplies no patient-rated fatigue measure or posted results. Dose, sample and study details strongly match the 2018 preprint and 2020 journal abstract.
