Format: Factual digest of the original material

The 12-week confirmatory phase randomized 142 people with probable DLB. Its prespecified comparison required improvement in both cognitive and neuropsychiatric primary measures. Mean NPI-2 changes were −2.1 with placebo, −1.8 with 5 mg donepezil and −2.8 with 10 mg. Neither active arm significantly differed from placebo on the composite. The investigators documented five severe psychiatric events, including visual hallucination, in two people in the 5 mg group. That adverse-event account has a different denominator and purpose from the aggregate efficacy outcome. The paper does not give a separately attributable food effect or visual episode count.
