Title: Blueberries Improve Pain, Gait Performance, and Inflammation in Individuals with Symptomatic Knee Osteoarthritis.
PMID: 30699971
PMCID: PMC6413191
DOI: 10.3390/nu11020290
First published: 2019
Authors: Du C, Smith A, Avalos M, South S, Crabtree K, Wang W, et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6413191/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Four-month double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in 63 randomized (49 completed: 27 blueberry, 22 placebo) adults aged 45-79 y with symptomatic knee osteoarthritis taking 40 g/d freeze-dried whole blueberry powder vs placebo. Crucially, full-text tables and Results show NO statistically significant between-group differences vs placebo on WOMAC pain, stiffness, total score, or GAITRite walking velocity (BB 96.3->115.3 vs PLA 105.3->119.0 cm/s), stride length (left BB 110.2->120.7 vs PLA 116.4->121.7 cm), or support percentages; improvements were within-group pre-post changes seen in both arms, and plasma inflammatory cytokines did not differ. Limits: non-PD knee OA; null between-group contrasts.
