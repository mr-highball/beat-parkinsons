Corn oil comparator in young adults with depressive symptoms
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26188642/
PMID: 26188642
DOI: 10.1016/j.psychres.2015.05.072
Coverage: Original PubMed author abstract, first online June 27, 2015; full tables not recovered

Ginty and Conklin randomized 23 young adults with a baseline Beck Depression Inventory score above 10 to corn-oil capsules or 1.4 g EPA plus DHA daily for 21 days. The abstract reports that 20 percent of the corn group and 67 percent of the omega-3 group no longer met the study depression threshold. It reports a significant group-by-time interaction and an inconclusive corn-arm change. The accessible abstract does not give group denominators, the exact interaction estimate, a corn grade or an oil-free comparison.
