Phillips MCL and colleagues. Movement Disorders. DOI 10.1002/mds.27390. Published online August 11, 2018. Primary factual extract.
The Parkinson dietary pilot randomized 47 participants to low-fat or ketogenic plans. Forty-four began and 38 completed. The published examination results include MDS-UPDRS III group totals. The scale includes facial-expression item 3.2, whose separate results are unreported.
The ketogenic supplement schedules a chicken meal seasoned with 1½ teaspoons of tarragon on printed p. 77 and a salmon meal with one teaspoon tarragon in a dill sauce on printed p. 81. A shopping list describes a vial. The recipes do not provide species or state whether tarragon is dried or fresh. Amounts actually eaten by named participants, and outcomes by tarragon exposure, are unreported.
