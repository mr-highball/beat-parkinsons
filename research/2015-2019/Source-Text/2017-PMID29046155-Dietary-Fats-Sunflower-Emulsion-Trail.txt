Text coverage: Distilled review body and reference 79 checked against the PDF.

Chianese and colleagues summarize reference 79, the Laugerette study PMID 20303729. It compares emulsified and nonemulsified sunflower oil in rats and reports greater post-meal endotoxemia and triglyceride response for the emulsion. The human comparison concerns a mixed meal and the cellular work concerns intestinal transfer. This is a route and formulation lead for the oil and an original metabolic comparison, without a measured executive, mood, anxiety, fatigue or Parkinson outcome. The review adds no independent comparison.

Supplementary coconut oil review
Reference 81 concerns a randomized meal crossover comparing coconut, grapeseed, fish and olive oil conditions for postprandial endotoxin. The original confirms the coconut exposure and the biochemical comparison. Composition and total fat differ across some meals. The cited clinical trial supplies no Parkinson fatigue, falls or executive outcome. research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27816052-Coconut-Oil-Meal-Endotoxin-Crossover.txt holds the main trial record.
