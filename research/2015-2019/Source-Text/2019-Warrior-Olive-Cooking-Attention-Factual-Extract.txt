Cooking olive oil and attention scores in a cross-sectional cohort
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/11/8/1759
PMID: 31370174
PMCID: PMC6723078
DOI: 10.3390/nu11081759
Coverage: Original study and Table 2

Warrior and colleagues published July 31, 2019. Of 166 women in a dietary and cognition sample, 113 were living with HIV and 53 were not. The questionnaire identified usual primary cooking oil without determining its refining grade. Table 2 reports an attention-and-concentration estimate of 4.23 points (SE 1.78) for olive oil compared with other oils overall. Estimates were 5.51 (SE 2.12) among women living with HIV and 1.02 (SE 2.87) among women without HIV. The scores were adjusted for test practice and demographic factors. The exposure was observational, and no Parkinson cohort or refined-only estimate was reported.
