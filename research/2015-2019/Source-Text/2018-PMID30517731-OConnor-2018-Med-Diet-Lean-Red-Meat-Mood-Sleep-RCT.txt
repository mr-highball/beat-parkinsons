Title: Adopting a Mediterranean-Style Eating Pattern with Different Amounts of Lean Unprocessed Red Meat Does Not Influence Short-Term Subjective Personal Well-Being in Adults with Overweight or Obesity.
PMID: 30517731
PMCID: PMC7263834
DOI: 10.1093/jn/nxy235
Registry ID: NCT02573129
First published: 2018
Authors: O'Connor LE, Biberstine SL, Paddon-Jones D, Schwichtenberg AJ, Campbell WW
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30517731/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized crossover controlled feeding trial in n=41 overweight/obese adults (5 weeks per arm) adopting a Mediterranean-style eating pattern with 500 g/wk vs 200 g/wk lean unprocessed red meat (beef and pork): no significant diet-by-time differences (p>0.05) on POMS mood/fatigue subscales, SF-36 quality of life, PSQI sleep quality, or wrist actigraphy sleep parameters. Limits: non-PD cohort, beef pooled with pork.
