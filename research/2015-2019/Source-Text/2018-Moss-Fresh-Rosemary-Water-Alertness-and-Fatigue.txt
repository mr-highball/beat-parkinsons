Eighty healthy adults were randomized 40 per group to a rosemary drink or plain mineral water. The product combined an aqueous and ethanolic fresh-rosemary extract with a steam hydrolat. Subjective post-task measures were alertness and mental fatigue. The narrative describes small unfavorable fatigue and alertness estimates, with individual nominal outcomes losing significance after adjustment. The numeric values cannot be confirmed through the available original rendering. The study did not measure depression.
