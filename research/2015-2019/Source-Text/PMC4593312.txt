People with lateralized Parkinson disease and older controls walked with visual and motor conditions manipulated to distinguish explanations for veering. Veering is the measured outcome. An earlier association between gait freezing and visual symptoms appears in the introduction. Participants were not tested for a food-timed hallucination effect.
