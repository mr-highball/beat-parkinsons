Title: Efficacy of Maha-Pancha Gavya Ghrita in the Management of Epilepsy (Apasmara)
Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/344605557_Original_Research_Efficacy_of_Maha-Pancha_Gavya_Ghrita_in_the_Management_of_Epilepsy_Apasmara
Publication date: 2018-04
Date basis: Original imprint in Journal of Ayurveda Physicians & Surgeons 5(2), pages 46–48. The author uploaded the paper in 2020.
Text coverage: Author-deposited rendition of the body, methods and tables.

Usha and Singh present outcomes for 12 people treated with a multicomponent medicated ghee for two months. The published comparison for seizure frequency is within that arm. Table 3 gives a mean of 1.75 attacks per week before treatment and 0.96 after. Table 4 records three of 12 in the remission category. The article does not give a separate visual hallucination measure or a Parkinson cohort. The authors describe the compound by its traditional name and the report supplies no botanical authentication of individual ingredients.

The 2001 thesis catalogue lists Usha and Singh in the same research roles. A later summary attributed to the thesis reports exactly the same frequency and remission values for the named formulation. Preserve likely sample reuse. The companion treatment group described in the thesis summary does not supply a controlled estimate from this publication. The original thesis and batch ingredient documentation remain open.
