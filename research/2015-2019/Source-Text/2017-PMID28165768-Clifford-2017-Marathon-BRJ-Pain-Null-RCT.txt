Title: Minimal muscle damage after a marathon and no influence of beetroot juice on inflammation and recovery.
PMID: 28165768
DOI: 10.1139/apnm-2016-0525
First published: 2017
Authors: Clifford T, Allerton DM, Brown MA, Harper L, Horsburgh S, Keane KM, et al.
Journal: Applied physiology, nutrition, and metabolism = Physiologie appliquee, nutrition et metabolisme
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28165768/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled parallel trial in n=34 marathon runners taking beetroot juice vs placebo for 3 days post-race: no significant difference in muscle soreness/pain ratings (p=0.694) or functional recovery. Competing null pain trial.
