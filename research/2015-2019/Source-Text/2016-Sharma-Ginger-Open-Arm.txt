Oral ethanolic ginger extract and mouse open-arm behavior
Source: https://nopr.niscpr.res.in/bitstream/123456789/35694/1/IJEB%2054%2810%29%20664-669.pdf
PMID: 30084567
Coverage: Original Methods, Results and Figure 2, factual text

Sharma and colleagues published in October 2016. The study administered a single oral dose of ethanolic rhizome extract, 50 or 200 mg/kg, or vehicle to groups of eight mice and tested the elevated plus maze after 60 minutes. The authors report increased time in open arms with the extract (p < .01), with a stronger response at 200 mg/kg. The separately measured 60-minute locomotor activity did not differ significantly according to the text, and its numerical results were not shown. This is an extract and behavioral assay in mice outside a Parkinson model.
