Factual extract of accepted manuscript and independent label evidence.
Cornish SM, Myrie SB et al., Nutrition Research 60:87–95, 2018. PMID 30527263, DOI 10.1016/j.nutres.2018.09.005. Section 2.1 names a NOW Foods omega 3-6-9 blend, three capsules at breakfast, daily 3.0 g. Formula by fatty-acid group: 45% ALA, 26.5% linoleic plus gamma-linolenic, 17.5% oleic and 11% other. The manuscript reports no product UPC, lot, individual plant oils or pressing method.
Historic NIH DSLD label 46377 was entered 2015-06-25. In the label's two-softgel serving, pumpkin oil is 20 mg and described as cold-pressed. Its nutrient percentages match those printed in the paper. The five listed plant oils match those named in NCT02617511. This supports a product linkage but does not establish a particular lot or exact participant-level intake.
Both trial arms did 12 weeks of resistance training. Outcomes include chest and leg press, timed up and go and six-minute walking distance. No handwriting comparison or diagnosed Parkinson population is reported.
