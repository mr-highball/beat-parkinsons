Coverage: Factual extract.

114 randomized equally among oral cinnamon powder, starch and ibuprofen. Mean 0–10 VAS at 24, 48 and 72 hours for cinnamon was 4.1, 3.2 and 1.8. Placebo means were 6.1, 5.3 and 4.0. Four-hour cinnamon contrast was inconclusive. Ibuprofen means at 24, 48 and 72 hours were 2.0, 1.1 and 0.6. Registry IRCT2013122114668N2. Population had primary dysmenorrhea.
