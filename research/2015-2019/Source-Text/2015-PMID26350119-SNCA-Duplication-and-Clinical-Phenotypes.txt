Title: Autosomal dominant Parkinson's disease caused by SNCA duplications
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4820832/
PMID: 26350119
PMCID: PMC4820832
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2015.09.007
Publication year: 2015
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

SNCA duplication case histories, genetic results and a literature comparison describe motor and nonmotor presentations. Some duplication carriers had hallucinations, along with sleep and cognitive symptoms. The clinical feature is reported with genetic disease and does not measure a culinary effect.

The source distinguishes an individual index presentation and the larger compilation of reported SNCA duplication carriers. Clinical severity varied across carriers, and related cases may share lineage.
