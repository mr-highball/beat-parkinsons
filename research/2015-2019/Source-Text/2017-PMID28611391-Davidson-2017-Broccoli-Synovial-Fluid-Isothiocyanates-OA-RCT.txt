Title: Isothiocyanates are detected in human synovial fluid following broccoli consumption and can affect the tissues of the knee joint.
PMID: 28611391
PMCID: PMC5469854
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-017-03629-5
First published: 2017
Authors: Davidson R, Gardner S, Jupp O, Bullough A, Butters S, Watts L
Journal: Scientific reports
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5469854/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Proof-of-principle RCT in n=40 knee osteoarthritis patients awaiting total knee replacement randomized to a high-glucosinolate broccoli diet vs low-glucosinolate diet for 14 days pre-surgery. Detected isothiocyanates and specifically sulforaphane in plasma and knee joint synovial fluid of the high-glucosinolate group only, with 125 differentially expressed synovial fluid proteins. Establishes human joint-tissue bioavailability of dietary broccoli sulforaphane without reporting clinical pain change.
