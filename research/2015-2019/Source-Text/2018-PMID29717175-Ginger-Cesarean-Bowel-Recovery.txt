Title: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial on the efficacy of ginger in the prevention of abdominal distention in post cesarean section patients
PMID: 29717175
PMCID: PMC5931585
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-018-25200-6
Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-25200-6
Registration: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02809027
Published: 2018-05-01, Scientific Reports 8, 6835.
Archive scope: Factual summary of the original five-page publisher PDF, Figure 1 and Tables 2–3.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Eighty-nine patients per arm received Abhaibhubejhr ginger capsules or a masked placebo after cesarean delivery. Each ginger capsule contained 500 mg. The study assigned two capsules after meals three times daily for three days. The primary abdominal-distention endpoint was 18/89 versus 26/89, with reported p=.328. Table 3 reports a secondary earlier first stool, 3091.0 versus 3445.5 minutes, about 5.9 hours difference, reported p=.048. Time to first flatus was inconclusive at p=.197. Constipation adverse-event counts were 13 versus 17 and the publication gives no separate item-level statistical comparison. The measured setting is short-term postoperative recovery, and a faster first stool gives no chronic Parkinson constipation or grated-root result.
