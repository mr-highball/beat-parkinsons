Title: Motor and non-motor outcomes of continuous apomorphine infusion in 125 Parkinson's disease patients
Source: https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/220478497/Chapter_2.pdf
PMID: 26709292
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2015.11.013
Publication date: 2015-11-22
Source depth: author-institution original study chapter with methods, outcome report and supplement; locally distilled

The retrospective chart cohort used Groningen infusion starts from October 2000 through June 2014 and included 125 people with Parkinson disease. Thirty-eight had current visual hallucinations when treatment began. The authors describe baseline recurrence from weekly to several times daily. The follow-up ratings record much improvement in 14, some improvement in nine, worsening in three and unknown outcome in 12. Two of the three who worsened discontinued. The clinical rating was qualitative. Dopamine agonists were reduced, and antipsychotic and cholinesterase inhibitor use increased during follow-up.

The outcome preserves favorable and adverse trajectories plus missing data. It cannot identify the independent effect of infusion or a change in counted episodes per time interval. The 2017 cognitive cohort came from the same center and has overlapping recruitment dates. The number of shared participants remains unverified. The electronic date precedes the February 2016 issue. Chapter 2 data and visual outcome pages were inspected directly.
