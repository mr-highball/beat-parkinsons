Original source scope
The Parkinson gut dysbiosis review cites Mischley and colleagues' cross-sectional baseline analysis in 1,053 people with self-reported idiopathic Parkinson. Its list of olive oil and coconut oil reflects Table 2 food-frequency associations with a total PRO-PD symptom score, adjusted for disease duration.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The underlying questionnaire does not distinguish olive or coconut oil grade. No individual oil-associated falls, rigidity, anxiety, apathy, fatigue, constipation, dyskinesia, postural-instability or slowness estimate appears in the cited analysis. The review does not supply a dried-date apathy comparison.
