Title: Sulforaphane produces antidepressant- and anxiolytic-like effects in adult mice.
PMID: 26721468
DOI: 10.1016/j.bbr.2015.12.030
First published: 2016
Authors: Wu S, Gao Q, Zhao P, Gao Y, Xi Y, Wang X
Journal: Behavioural brain research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26721468/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse study showing repeated sulforaphane (10 mg/kg i.p.) reduced immobility in FST/TST, reduced feeding latency in novelty-suppressed feeding, increased open-field center time, and reversed chronic mild stress (CMS)-induced anhedonia (sucrose preference), depression-like, and anxiety-like behaviors alongside lower serum corticosterone, ACTH, IL-6, and TNF-alpha. Limits: injected purified sulforaphane in non-PD mice.
