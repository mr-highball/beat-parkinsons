Title: Sage extract for premenstrual symptoms in university students
IRCT: IRCT201508268801N20
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/9292
WHO mirror: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT201508268801N20
Registered: 2016-05-30
Coverage: Official English registry and WHO mirror. Numerical results unavailable.

The 90-participant target covers a randomized, triple-blind parallel comparison of oral 500 mg Salvia officinalis extract and matched placebo during part of the menstrual cycle. The registry lists total, physical and psychological symptom severity before intervention and after each of two months, plus side effects. An individual pain endpoint or separately reported pain estimate was not identified.

The original registry classifies registration as retrospective and records an expected recruitment start of 2015-09-11 with actual start and completion dates blank. The WHO mirror calls the same date first enrollment and marks recruitment complete. The sources agree on the registered date. The WHO results fields are blank. The target, setting, trial design and intervention match the Abdnezhad reports, which remain one likely trial family while explicit publication-level linkage is sought.
