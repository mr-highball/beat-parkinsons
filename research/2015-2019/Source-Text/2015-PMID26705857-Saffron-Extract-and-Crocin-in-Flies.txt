Title: Evidence of neuroprotective effects of saffron and crocin in a Drosophila model of parkinsonism

PMID: 26705857

Publication date: 2015-12-17
Date basis: PubMed electronic publication date. The journal issue appeared in January 2016.
Collection: Source text
Review depth: primary_source, original abstract and publisher bibliographic record
Checked: 2026-11-25

The report compares a methanolic Crocus sativus extract and
isolated crocin as distinct interventions in a fly model of
parkinsonism. The authors report lower mortality and favorable
locomotor performance with both in their experimental setting.
The abstract also distinguishes a secondary challenge experiment
from the main model comparisons. A fly locomotion result is not a
human rigidity, depression or fatigue effect.

The 2019 MedChemComm review (PMID 31303984, reference 146) reuses
this publication. This experiment is distinct from PMID 34224829,
which studied saffron powder in a different fly model. The complete
figures and design details of the Rao article were not accessible
through the inspected original publisher record.
