Title: Cranberry fruit powder (Flowens™) improves lower urinary tract symptoms in men: a double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled study.
PMID: 26049866
DOI: 10.1007/s00345-015-1611-7
First published: 2016
Authors: Vidlar A, Student V, Vostalova J, Fromentin E, Roller M, Simanek V, Student V
Journal: World journal of urology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26049866/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled 6-month trial (Vidlar et al., 2016, World J Urol) enrolling 124 men aged >45 years with lower urinary tract symptoms (IPSS >=8, PSA <2.5 ng/mL) randomized to 250 mg/day or 500 mg/day whole cranberry fruit powder (Flowens) vs placebo. At 6 months, both dried cranberry fruit powder arms significantly improved IPSS total scores in a dose-dependent manner (-3.1 points at 250 mg, p=0.05; -4.1 points at 500 mg, p<0.001) vs placebo (-1.5 points), with within-group improvements in Qmax, Qave, bladder volume, and post-void residual in the 500 mg arm and no hematology/chemistry safety concerns. Limits: IPSS pools storage/urgency and voiding items; male non-PD LUTS cohort; encapsulated whole fruit powder.
