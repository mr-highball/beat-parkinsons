Title: Hippocampal CA1 subfield predicts episodic memory impairment in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6500913/
PMID: 31054380
PMCID: PMC6500913
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2019.101824
Publication date: 2019-04-18
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC and PubMed metadata.
Design: Clinical hippocampal MRI and memory study

Twenty-nine Parkinson participants and eight controls were examined for links between hippocampal subfield measures and episodic memory. Cognitive and structural endpoints are distinct from waking visual episodes after food.
