Title: Crocin-I and depressive-like behavior in corticosterone-exposed mice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31614158/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0031938419304895
PMID: 31614158
DOI: 10.1016/j.physbeh.2019.112699
Publication date: 2019-10-12
Date basis: Online publication in PubMed. Physiology & Behavior volume 212 was dated December 1, 2019.
Source depth: Primary abstract and public publisher methods excerpt.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25

The original identifies male C57BL/6J mice. Isolated oral crocin-I improved some depressive-like behavioral measures after corticosterone exposure. The abstract discusses accompanying hippocampal inflammatory and oxidative outcomes. This is a chemically induced behavioral model. It gives no clinical ground-saffron comparison, Parkinson cohort or insomnia endpoint.

The 2024 crocin review (PMID 38633914, reference 68) describes this experiment as involving rats. The primary title, abstract and visible methods identify mice. The reports count once for this experiment. Any extrapolation from crocin-I to ground saffron would require exposure and outcome evidence.
