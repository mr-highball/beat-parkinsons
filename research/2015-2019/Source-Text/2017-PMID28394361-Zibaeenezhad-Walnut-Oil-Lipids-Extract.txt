Title: Effects of walnut oil on lipid profiles in hyperlipidemic type 2 diabetic patients: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial

Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Results report 100 assessed for eligibility, 96 randomized and 45 per group analyzed after cold-pressed walnut oil 15 mL/day or water placebo capsules for 90 days. Trial Design more broadly describes 100 patients; preserve those distinctions. Reported LDL treatment difference is −30.44 mg/dL (95% CI −39.14 to −21.73). HDL difference +2.28 (−.14 to +4.72), p=.06, is inconclusive.

IRCT2014030611375N2 and ethics CT-P-92-5376 differ from the 2016 glycemic trial. Reported November 2012–April 2013 study dates conflict with the registry's expected October 2013–January 2014 dates. Actual registry dates are blank.

Very similar baseline summaries to the 2016 report warrant cohort checking. Distinct protocols, sex counts and comparators prevent assuming identical or independent participants. No cognition, pain or Parkinson symptom endpoint was measured. The report is not itself verified as an oil-paper member of the 2018 evidence map.
