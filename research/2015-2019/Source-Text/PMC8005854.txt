Title: Resting-state functional reorganization in Parkinson's disease: An activation likelihood estimation meta-analysis

PMCID: PMC8005854
PMID: 28467917
DOI: 10.1016/j.cortex.2017.03.016
Publication date: 2017-04-08
Date basis: NLM and PMC electronic date
Source depth: Complete original PMC structured article body
Format: Factual digest of the inspected material

The resting-state fMRI meta-analysis compares the coordinates of reported case–control activity changes across 28 between-group contrasts. Its focus is consistency of intrinsic brain function across Parkinson imaging studies. It does not compare fig intake or visual episodes.
