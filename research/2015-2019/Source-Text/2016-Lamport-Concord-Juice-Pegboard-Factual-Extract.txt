Concord juice and grooved pegboard in a crossover

Primary source: https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/52909/1/Am%20J%20Clin%20Nutr-2016-Lamport-775-83.pdf
Coverage: Original design and pegboard estimate
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published online February 11, 2016. Twenty-five healthy working mothers entered a crossover comparing 355 mL/day Concord grape juice with energy- and sugar-matched placebo in two 12-week periods. In the first phase, averaged Grooved Pegboard times were 60.4 s (SE 1.9) with placebo and 63.2 s (2.3) with juice (p<.05). Treatment by phase F(1,21)=9.61, p<.01, with no clear difference in the second phase. The phase-specific result concerns juice and the healthy study population, with no raw-fruit or Parkinson effect.
