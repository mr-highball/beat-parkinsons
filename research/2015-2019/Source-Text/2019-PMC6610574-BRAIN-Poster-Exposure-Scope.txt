PB02-G13 describes a butanolic nutmeg extract in a mouse model of scopolamine-associated memory impairment. It reports improvement on two behavioral memory tasks and changes in hippocampal biochemical measures. The model, extract and endpoints do not supply outcomes for dietary grated or ground nutmeg, Parkinson depression, fatigue, or insomnia.
Member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6610574/#sec1562

PB02-S09 compares a diet enriched with the olive-oil constituent hydroxytyrosol against a control in mice after experimental stroke. It reports improved cerebral blood flow and hippocampal neurogenesis and describes walking distance over time. Walking distance is distinct from stride length or bradykinesia. The diet supplies an isolated constituent, and the population has experimental stroke.
Member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6610574/#sec2006
