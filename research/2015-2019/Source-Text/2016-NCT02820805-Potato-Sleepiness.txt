Registered pediatric potato meals and acute sleepiness
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02820805
Coverage: Factual summary of official registry fields.

The official registry first posted July 1, 2016. Its 22-child crossover lists six morning conditions: mashed potatoes, fried potato strips, hash browns, white rice, white beans and no meal. Each food served 50 g available carbohydrate. Secondary mood ratings include acute sleepiness at baseline and 10, 30, 60, 120 and 180 minutes. The record does not identify boil preparation or supply sleepiness results. The later Lee primary abstract is a probable match based on the institution, actual sample, six conditions, dose and timing.
