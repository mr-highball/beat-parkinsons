Title: Beneficial effects of Silexan on sleep are mediated by its anxiolytic effect.
PMID: 31121394
DOI: 10.1016/j.jpsychires.2019.04.013
First published: 2019
Authors: Seifritz E, Schläfke S, Holsboer-Trachsler E
Journal: Journal of psychiatric research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31121394/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Regression-based mediation re-analysis (Seifritz, Schlafke & Holsboer-Trachsler, 2019, J Psychiatr Res) of 212 efficacy-evaluable adults from the 10-week subsyndromal anxiety DB-RCT (same underlying cohort as Kasper 2010 PMID:20512042) comparing oral Silexan 80 mg/day vs placebo on HAMA and PSQI. While Silexan reduced HAMA (p<0.001) and PSQI (p=0.002), Sobel mediation showed 98.4% of the total sleep improvement was mediated indirectly via anxiolysis (p<0.001) with virtually zero direct hypnotic effect (1.6%, p=0.958). Confirms Silexan improves disturbed sleep secondary to anxiety relief rather than via primary sedation.
