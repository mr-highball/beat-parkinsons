Title: Preliminary swallowing results in the EVOO ALS pilot
Source: https://nahc.com.au/a-multidisciplinary-approach-to-support-management-of-nutrition-swallowing-and-quality-of-life-in-patients-with-motor-neurone-disease/
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Niven and colleagues, National Allied Health Conference, 2019. The randomized four-week pilot targeted 20% of energy as extra-virgin olive oil in two of three groups. Preliminary results cover 14 participants with MND. The authors state the combined diet and exercise group experienced less swallowing burden and fatigue than the single-intervention groups, and give SWAL-QOL 54.0 versus 68.2, p = .017. The abstract does not map each value to a prespecified group or time comparison and provides no diet-only result. An accompanying diet report describes the same enrollment and arms. No public registry ID or full arm table was verified.
