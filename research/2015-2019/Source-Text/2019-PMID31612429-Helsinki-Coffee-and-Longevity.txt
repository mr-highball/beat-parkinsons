Title: Major cardiovascular disease (CVD) risk factors in midlife and extreme longevity
Publication date: 2019-10-14 (online first)
PMID: 31612429
DOI: 10.1007/s40520-019-01364-7
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31612429/
Original PDF: https://oulurepo.oulu.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/26195/nbnfi-fe202002266545.pdf
Text coverage: Distilled original methods and Table 1. The printed issue date is 2020.

The analysis included 970 men assessed in 1974, selected to be old enough to reach age 90 by March 2018 and to have complete major risk-factor data. Of these, 244 reached age 90. Table 1 displays unadjusted coffee means of 3.8 (SD 2.4) versus 4.1 (SD 2.5) cups per day, p = 0.13. Preparation and caffeine content were unspecified. The adjusted major-risk-factor analysis concerns a different set of prespecified factors. It does not provide a coffee effect on fatigue, including in Parkinson disease.

Conference O-32 (PMC7091737) reports an overlapping broader Helsinki Businessmen Study analysis with 2,600 men and 409 cases of survival to at least 90, so do not count the studies as independent. NCT02526082 is the overarching cohort registration.
