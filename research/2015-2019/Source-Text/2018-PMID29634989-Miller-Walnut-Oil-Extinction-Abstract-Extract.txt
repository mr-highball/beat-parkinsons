Title: A mind cleared by walnut oil: The effects of polyunsaturated and saturated fat on extinction learning
PMID: 29634989
DOI: 10.1016/j.appet.2018.04.004
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29634989/
First published: 2018-04-07 online; Appetite 126:147–155, July 2018 issue.
Coverage: Factual original author-abstract extraction; complete publication and individual mood subscales remain unavailable.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Miller and colleagues describe an acute randomized between-group comparison of walnut-oil, cream and no-added-fat milkshake conditions in young adults. The laboratory task concerns threat learning and extinction. Acquisition and generalization were similar; extinction was faster under walnut oil in the author abstract. This is not a clinical Parkinson anxiety or depression outcome.

An earlier 2015 conference abstract reports 39 participants and two named conditions. A later systematic review reports 62 randomized and 59 analyzed, with three conditions. Matching investigators/task support a study-family lead, not verified participant mapping or independent replication. The review's total-mood extraction does not supply original arm-specific anxiety or depression scores.

Oil pressing, complete methods and those original symptom subscales remain open. Existing notes are reused; this structured header improves exact source retrieval without adding a new cohort or clinical finding.
