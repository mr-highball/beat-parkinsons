Anise oil and reported tiredness in the IBS trial
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jep.2016.10.083
Coverage: factual extract of primary Methods and Table 2
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Mosaffa-Jahromi and colleagues randomized 120 adults with IBS to enteric-coated anise oil, placebo or peppermint oil. The original Table 2 reports tiredness on a 0–10 scale (n = 39, 39, 38). Anise oil mean scores were 5.77 at baseline, 3.15 at four weeks and 2.74 at follow-up. Placebo was 5.25, 3.94 and 3.54. Peppermint oil was 5.50, 4.24 and 4.03. The trial table reports p < .001 for the three-group contrasts after baseline, with no independently verified adjusted pairwise contrast. The oil form and population differ from eating ground anise seed in Parkinson disease. A 2023 secondary review says tiredness did not improve. Its wording conflicts with the original symptom table and abstract.
