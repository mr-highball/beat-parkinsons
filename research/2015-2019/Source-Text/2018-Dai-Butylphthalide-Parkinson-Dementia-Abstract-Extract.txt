Title: Effect of butylphthalide soft capsules on dementia-related factors and quality of life in Parkinson disease dementia
Source: https://rs.yiigle.com/cmaid/1021660
DOI: 10.3760/cma.j.issn.1008-6706.2018.01.018
First public article date shown by publisher: 2018-01-01
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated official bilingual author abstract

The abstract describes random-number-table allocation of 90
participants with Parkinson dementia. Each group received
usual care plus donepezil. The adjunct group additionally
received butylphthalide soft capsules. The abstract gives
post-treatment total UPDRS of 33.28±2.18 versus 41.26±2.54
(p<.05). The accessible content does not provide a separately
scored resting-tremor result or an edible celery exposure.

The publisher full reading is account-gated. Recruitment
dates, participant-level overlap and individual motor tables
remain unverified.
