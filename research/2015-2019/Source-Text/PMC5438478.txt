Title: Reduction of RPT6/S8 (a Proteasome Component) and Proteasome Activity in the Cortex is Associated with Cognitive Impairment in Lewy Body Dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5438478/
PMCID: PMC5438478
PMID: 28269775
DOI: 10.3233/jad-160946
Publication date: 2017-03-01
Date basis: Publisher first publication; PMC distinguishes March 1 preliminary and March 21 electronic fields
Source depth: Complete original PMC structured article body
Format: Factual digest of the inspected material

The authors examine cortical RPT6 and proteasome activity in donated brains from Parkinson dementia, Lewy body dementia, Alzheimer disease and controls. The correlations include cognitive disease measures. Clinical visual hallucinations are background features, without an exposure analysis or frequency endpoint.
