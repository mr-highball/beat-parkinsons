Title: Dietary citrate and plasma ionized calcium: Implications for platelet donors
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28802068/
PMID: 28802068
DOI: 10.1002/jca.21575
First public: 2017-08-12
Coverage: Factual extract from the original author abstract.

Six volunteers drank orange juice or grapefruit juice and received the other beverage on a separate day. After orange juice, the authors report a 2.1% to 12.2% decrease in ionized plasma calcium in four volunteers at 30 to 60 minutes. The outcome is change in a blood measurement. The word fall refers to declining calcium, with no measured fall event, raw-orange intervention or Parkinson subgroup.
