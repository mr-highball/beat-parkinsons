Title: Oral curcumin and topical curcumin nanoparticles in osteoarthritis models
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4891896/
PMID: 27260322
PMCID: PMC4891896
DOI: 10.1186/s13075-016-1025-y
Publication date: 2016-06-03
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full structured original.

The mouse osteoarthritis work reports less cartilage deterioration with oral curcumin. The authors report no clear pain relief in that oral arm. In a separate topical curcumin nanoparticle comparison, treated animals showed less tactile hypersensitivity and more locomotor activity than vehicle-treated animals. Figure 7 gives the tactile and movement measures. The paper also reports laboratory cartilage-cell endpoints.

The positive pain-related result belongs to the topical preparation. The inconclusive oral result, topical delivery and osteoarthritis setting limit transfer to eating ground turmeric for Parkinson pain.
