Title: Cholinesterase inhibitors and memantine for Parkinson's disease dementia and Lewy body dementia: A meta-analysis

PMID: 30783428
PMCID: PMC6364145

Publication date: 2018-12-24
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC metadata and PubMed.
Design: Meta-analysis of cholinesterase inhibitors and memantine

The analysis compares drug studies in Parkinson cognitive impairment, Parkinson dementia and dementia with Lewy bodies. Outcomes include cognition, global impression, behavior and adverse events. The pooled comparisons do not count visual episodes following dried figs.
