Title: UEG Week 2015 poster presentations, culinary and herbal member abstracts
PMCID: PMC4669991
DOI: 10.1177/2050640615601623
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4669991/
First verified online: 2015-10-25 per Crossref; print metadata October 2015 without day.
Coverage: Factual extracts of original member abstracts in the complete 542-page collection. Member abstracts are not full trial reports.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

P1103 tested mixed STW5, including Melissa, in stressed rats for gastric responses. Its cited paper DOI10.1016/j.phymed.2015.03.012 is a related model trail; cohort overlap remains unverified. No isolated tea or depression contrast is reported.

P1725 compared a mixed ginger/cinnamon-containing solution with placebo for postprandial distress. The abstract describes 20 enrolled and nine active/ten placebo participants; four-week response was 44.4% versus 20.0%, p=.350. This inconclusive mixed-product result does not isolate ginger, cinnamon, pain or constipation.

P0240 used chicken or mushroom soup for imaging in ten volunteers, without allocation by soup. P1127 eliminated egg among six foods in eosinophilic oesophagitis. P1229 lists onion among dietary fructan sources. P1825 studied broccoli-sprout extract. P0017 used injected olive oil in rats; P0442 coffee charcoal in a mixture; P0444 potato as a low-FODMAP substitute. Culinary preparation and the assigned Parkinson symptom contrasts remain unmeasured.
