Title: Antioxidant and hepatoprotective properties of dried fig against oxidative stress and hepatotoxicity in rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27268385/
Source alias: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2016.06.009
PMID: 27268385
DOI: 10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2016.06.009
Publication date: 2016-06-05
Date basis: NLM electronic publication date. October print issue, International Journal of Biological Macromolecules 91, 554–559.
Text coverage: Author abstract and original bibliographic identity. Complete methods and tables remain open.

Turan and Celik described four rat groups with and without a dried-fig diet and ethanol over 50 days. Relative to ethanol alone, the fig-supplemented ethanol group had lower liver damage enzyme measures and less visible liver tissue injury according to the authors. Antioxidant measures were also reported. This is an ethanol liver model with no visual behavior endpoint. A 2018 same-author paper studies lung and muscle using the same nominal group design. The cohort relationship remains open.
