Title: Effects of inorganic nitrate and nitrite consumption on cognitive function and cerebral blood flow: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials.
PMID: 29617153
DOI: 10.1080/10408398.2018.1453779
First published: 2019
Authors: Clifford T, Babateen A, Shannon OM, Capper T, Ashor A, Stephan B, et al.
Journal: Critical reviews in food science and nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29617153/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 13 randomized clinical trials (n=297) examining inorganic nitrate/beetroot juice on cognitive function and cerebral blood flow: pooled cognitive effect was non-significant (SMD +0.06; 95% CI -0.06 to +0.18; p=0.32). Context review highlighting modest/inconsistent cognitive evidence.
