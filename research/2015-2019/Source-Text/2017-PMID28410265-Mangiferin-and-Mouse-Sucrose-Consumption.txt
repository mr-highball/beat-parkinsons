Title: Cao mangiferin chronic mild stress model and sucrose consumption
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28410265/
PMID: 28410265
DOI: 10.1097/FBP.0000000000000305
Publication date: 2017-08
Date basis: Journal issue month in NLM. An earlier online day was not confirmed.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract. The available publisher route did not provide the full article.

The original abstract reports increased sucrose consumption after isolated mangiferin in mice exposed to chronic mild stress. It also describes improved locomotor activity, reduced immobility on two tests and lower inflammatory markers. The 2025 review indexed as PMID 40696232 instead describes decreased sucrose intake when summarizing this study. The source attribution follows the original abstract and flags the review's opposite wording.

This experiment measures behavior in an animal stress model and does not use dried mango or assess a clinical Parkinson depression endpoint. Full numerical group contrasts were unavailable in this pass.
