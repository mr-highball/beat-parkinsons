Factual extract from PMID30653734, PMCID PMC6544143, DOI 10.1002/mds.27577. Paul KC et al. The association between lifestyle factors and Parkinson's disease progression and mortality. Mov Disord. 2019;34(1):58–66. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6544143/.
Lifetime caffeinated tea use was reported for four age periods. Study included 360 PD patients, 244 with active reassessment and 212 in Hoehn and Yahr progression models in Table 3. Ever versus never tea HR 0.93 (95% CI 0.51–1.71, p=.82) for progression to stage ≥3. At or above median daily cups versus below median gave HR 0.64 (95% CI 0.36–1.14). Black tea and brewing were not isolated. This is a motor-stage transition analysis distinct from walking speed or rest tremor.
