Title: Broad bean (Vicia faba) consumption and Parkinson's disease: a natural source of L-dopa to consider.
PMID: 24332774
DOI: 10.1016/j.nrl.2013.08.006
First published: 2015
Authors: Ramírez-Moreno JM, Salguero Bodes I, Romaskevych O, Duran-Herrera MC
Journal: Neurologia (Barcelona, Spain)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24332774/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Verified Round 4 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Clinical case letter (Neurologia 2015, first online Dec 2013) describing a PD patient with mild baseline ON dyskinesia on a stable regimen who developed severe generalized involuntary movements (dyskinesias), cervical dystonia, sweating, nausea, anxiety, and hypotension (ED blood pressure 90/55 mm Hg) approximately 2 hours after eating an estimated 300-400 g of freshly harvested broad beans (both raw with pod and stewed). Symptoms resolved within 48 hours after acute management and temporary pause of antiparkinsonian drugs. Limitations: n=1, mixed raw/stewed exposure, no bean L-DOPA assay, no plasma L-DOPA measurement, no rechallenge.
