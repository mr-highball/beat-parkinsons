Title: Effect of light pressure stroking massage with sesame oil on acute traumatic limb pain
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28619303/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0965229917301620
PMID: 28619303
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2017.03.004
Publication date: 2017-03-16
Checked: 2026-11-22
Review depth: abstract

Original investigators randomized 40 patients with traumatic limb pain to topical sesame-oil or paraffin massage. Pain comparisons favored sesame oil on days 6 and 9. The abstract reports no clear between-group difference in diclofenac use. The publisher's indexed result excerpt confirms the later pain comparisons. The inspected record does not establish oil roasting, oral administration or a Parkinson pain outcome.
