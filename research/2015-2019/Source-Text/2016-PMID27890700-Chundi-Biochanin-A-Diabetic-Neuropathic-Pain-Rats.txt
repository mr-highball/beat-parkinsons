Title: Biochanin-A attenuates neuropathic pain in diabetic rats.
PMID: 27890700
PMCID: PMC5192256
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaim.2016.08.001
First published: 2016
Authors: Chundi V, Challa SR, Garikapati DR, Juvva G, Jampani A, Pinnamaneni SH et al.
Journal: J Ayurveda Integr Med
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5192256/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rat study (Chundi et al., 2016, PMC5192256) showing purified biochanin A attenuated mechanical and thermal hyperalgesia in streptozotocin-diabetic rats. Remote isolated-compound rodent neuropathy model; does not test whole cooked chickpeas or Parkinson pain.
