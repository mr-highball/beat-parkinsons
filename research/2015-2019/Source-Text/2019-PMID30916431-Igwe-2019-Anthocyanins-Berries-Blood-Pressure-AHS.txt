Title: Usual dietary anthocyanin intake, sources and their association with blood pressure in a representative sample of Australian adults.
PMID: 30916431
DOI: 10.1111/jhn.12647
First published: 2019
Authors: Igwe EO, Charlton KE, Probst YC
Journal: Journal of human nutrition and dietetics : the official journal of the British Dietetic Association
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30916431/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional secondary analysis of the 2011-2012 Australian Health Survey linking estimated usual dietary anthocyanin intake from berries (including blackberries) and other plant foods to resting blood pressure. Resting population BP only; does not isolate raw blackberries or evaluate orthostatic hypotension/lightheadedness in PD.
