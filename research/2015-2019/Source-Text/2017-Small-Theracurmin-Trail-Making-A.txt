Bioavailable curcumin and Trail Making A in nondemented adults
Source: https://www.ajgponline.org/article/S1064748117305110/pdf
PMID: 29246725
DOI: 10.1016/j.jagp.2017.10.010
Coverage: Factual summary of full primary table and methods.

Published online October 27, 2017. Small and colleagues randomized 40 nondemented adults to oral Theracurmin or placebo for 18 months. Table 2 gives Trail Making A time from 32.6 to 24.9 seconds with curcumin versus 30.5 to 28.4 seconds on placebo. The reported group-by-time result was p=.04. This was a secondary timed attention and motor-speed task. The trial did not test culinary turmeric, clinical Parkinson attention impairment or an attention-specific primary endpoint.
