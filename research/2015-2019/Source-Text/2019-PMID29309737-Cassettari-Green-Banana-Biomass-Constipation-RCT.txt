Title: Combinations of laxatives and green banana biomass on the treatment of functional constipation in children and adolescents: a randomized study.
PMID: 29309737
DOI: 10.1016/j.jped.2017.10.011
First published: 2019
Authors: Cassettari VMG, Machado NC, Lourenção PLTA, Carvalho MA, Ortolan EVP
Journal: Jornal de pediatria
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29309737/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

5-arm randomized clinical trial in n=80 children and adolescents with Rome IV functional constipation comparing: (1) green banana biomass alone, (2) green banana biomass + PEG 3350 with electrolytes, (3) green banana biomass + sodium picosulfate, (4) PEG 3350 alone, and (5) sodium picosulfate alone. Primary outcome: reduction in proportion with Bristol Stool Form Scale (BSFS) 1-2. Green banana biomass alone produced statistically significant reductions in BSFS 1-2 hard stools, straining on defecation, painful defecation, and abdominal pain, though it did not increase the proportion achieving >3 bowel movements/week or reduce fecal incontinence or blood in stool. As adjunct, green banana biomass enabled laxative dose reduction in 87% with sodium picosulfate and 63% with PEG 3350, with no reported adverse effects. Limits: cooked green banana biomass (not raw ripe banana), pediatric non-PD population.
