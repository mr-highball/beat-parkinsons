Format: Factual digest of the original material

The study compared EEG measures and network activity in an alpha-synuclein transgenic mouse model and in humans with dementia with Lewy bodies. Human visual hallucinations are part of the clinical rationale. Neither the neurophysiological measures nor the animal experiment count food-timed clinical visual episodes.
