Title: Comparison of clinical features among Parkinson's disease subtypes: A large retrospective study in a single center
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29406964/
Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jns.2018.01.013
PMID: 29406964
DOI: 10.1016/j.jns.2018.01.013
Publication date: 2018-01-11
Date basis: NLM and publisher electronic publication; March 2018 issue
Source depth: Original journal abstract examined; body and result tables remain open
Format: Factual digest of the primary abstract

The Mayo retrospective cohort included 1,003 patients reviewed between 1998 and 2016. Of these, 694 had more than one visit. The authors classified four motor subtypes using the initial visit. At the final visit hallucinations were more likely relative to the tremor-dominant group in the akinetic-rigid group (reported OR 2.36, P=.005) and the mixed group (reported OR 3.28, P<.001). The abstract does not distinguish hallucination modalities or give a visual episode rate.

The 2017 American Neurological Association M255 abstract gives the same site, years, sample size, subtype proportions and hallucination significance values. Treat this as publication lineage rather than independent confirmation. A later MAPT analysis (PMID 32767721) shares site and years with a restricted 855-person sample. The earlier AAN P6.014 abstract gives a different preliminary sample and subtype proportions. The three source scopes and participant overlap require separate tracking. No dried fig comparison is reported.
