Title: Neuroprotective effect of lycopene against experimental Parkinson's disease in mice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25980996/
PMID: 25980996
DOI: 10.1016/j.neulet.2015.05.024
Publication date: 2015-05-14
Date basis: First electronic publication in PubMed. Print issue July 2015.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: abstract

The publisher describes narrow-beam walking for balance and coordination, open-field assessment for spontaneous movement, and a hang test for grip and coordination. The author abstract reports favorable changes with isolated lycopene in a mouse Parkinson model. The accessible report gives no separate resistance-to-passive-movement or clinical rigidity comparison. Full group statistics and methods remain open.

Claim 0544 asks about cooked tomato and Parkinson muscle rigidity. This report contributes isolated-constituent and animal outcome context.
