Acute tart cherry cognition trial registration
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02381860
Registration: NCT02381860
Coverage: Official registry, first posted March 6, 2015 (estimated)

This registration describes an acute tart cherry concentrate study on cerebral blood flow and cognition. It lists enrollment of 28 and completed status. Its outcome labels do not specifically name fatigue, while the 2016 paper explicitly links the record and includes a fatigue visual analogue scale in a 27-person study. The registry has no posted results.
