Title: Lean-seafood intake reduces cardiovascular lipid risk factors in healthy subjects: results from a randomized controlled trial with a crossover design
Publication date: 2015 (first electronic publication)
PMID: 26224298
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26224298/
Text coverage: Primary trial outcomes and the verified 2016 parallel proceedings descriptions.

Twenty healthy adults consumed lean-seafood and nonseafood dietary patterns in a four-week crossover with a five-week washout. The blood-sampling test meals used cod or lean beef. Relative fasting triacylglycerol difference was 0.31 mmol/L (p=.03) and postprandial difference was reported at p=.01, favoring the seafood period. The nonseafood chronic diet included egg and other proteins. The two 2016 proceedings accounts match the participant counts, design and NCT01708681 lineage. Diet effects do not isolate grilled cod or poached egg and give no separate fatigue or falls result.
