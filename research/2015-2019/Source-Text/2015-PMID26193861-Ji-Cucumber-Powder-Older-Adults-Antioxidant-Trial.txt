Title: In Vivo Antioxidant Properties of Lotus Root and Cucumber: A Pilot Comparative Study in Aged Subjects.
PMID: 26193861
PMCID: PMC12878895
DOI: 10.1007/s12603-015-0524-x
First published: 2015
Authors: Ji L, Gao W, Wei J, Pu L, Yang J, Guo C
Journal: J Nutr Health Aging
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12878895/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Pilot human biomarker intervention (Ji et al., 2015, PMC12878895, n=30 healthy older adults) administering freeze-dried cucumber powder (or lotus root powder) for 30 days and measuring blood antioxidant enzymes (SOD, MDA, vitamin C). Measured oxidative biomarkers only with zero clinical neurological, motor, or non-motor Parkinson symptom endpoints.
