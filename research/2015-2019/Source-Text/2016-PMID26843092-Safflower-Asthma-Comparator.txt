Title: Effects of a dietary intervention with conjugated linoleic acid on immunological and metabolic parameters in children and adolescents with allergic asthma
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4739392/
PMCID: PMC4739392
PMID: 26843092
DOI: 10.1186/s12944-016-0187-6
First public article date: 2016-02-03
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled original indexed abstract.

NCT01026506 compared conjugated linoleic acid in yoghurt with safflower oil in yoghurt over 12 weeks. The 29 participants had allergic asthma. The oil group had an increase in some immune markers, while pollen-allergic participants in both groups improved FEV1. The investigators reported no change in allergy symptom scores or specific IgE. They did not report isolated throat or swallowing outcomes. The control was a formulated dairy food exposure with unspecified safflower processing.
