Title: Bian and colleagues, volatile oil fractions before and after dry-frying huajiao
Original source: https://ccj.pku.edu.cn/article/info?aid=230485909
Publication: Chinese Journal of Clinical Pharmacology, 2019, issue 4, pp. 369–371 and 376
Coverage: Factual English notes from the original Chinese journal abstract.

Groups of ten mice were assigned to four raw or dry-fried volatile-oil conditions, a positive-control analgesic, or vehicle. The extract fractions were given orally. Acetic-acid writhes over 20 minutes were 19.50 ± 3.31 and 27.20 ± 3.91 for the two raw conditions, 18.40 ± 3.10 and 21.10 ± 2.85 for the dry-fried conditions, 19.20 ± 3.79 with the positive control and 49.60 ± 7.62 with vehicle. The abstract describes the active-versus-vehicle comparisons as significant. The inequality sign for the printed p-values is absent in the web rendering. A direct dry-fried-versus-raw significance test and voucher are unverified. It concerns extracted fractions in mice.

Group-count check, November 26, 2026. The original Chinese abstract enumerates six allocations and 60 mice overall. Four fraction arms and one vehicle arm provide the 50-animal subset for the cited fraction-versus-vehicle contrasts. The sixth arm is an active positive control. No direct statistical comparison of raw and dry-fried fractions is verified.

November 26 original Chinese abstract audit. In the order stated by the journal, the raw high and low groups have 19.50 ± 3.31 and 27.20 ± 3.91 writhes, and the dry-fried high and low groups have 18.40 ± 3.10 and 21.10 ± 2.85. The active comparator has 19.20 ± 3.79, and the vehicle group has 49.60 ± 7.62. Each arm contains ten animals. The abstract also calls out a hot-plate assay but supplies no numerical heat result.
