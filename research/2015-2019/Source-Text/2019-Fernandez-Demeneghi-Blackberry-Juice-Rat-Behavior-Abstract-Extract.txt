Blackberry juice and locomotor tests in Wistar rats
Source depth Original author abstract and locomotor scope. DOI 10.1080/09637486.2019.1580680.
Fernández-Demeneghi and colleagues’ 2019 abstract describes 21 days of blackberry juice in Wistar rats with maze, locomotor and forced-swim assessments. The intermediate exposure gave an anxiolytic-like result and an inconclusive locomotor comparison. The low and high exposures gave inconclusive maze contrasts. The preparation was juice, and human walking symptoms and full arm-level tables remain outside the verified record.
