Title: UEG Week 2017 poster proceedings — ingredient-specific exposure contexts
PMCID: PMC7672678
DOI: 10.1177/2050640617725676
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7672678/
First published: 2017-10-29, per publisher citation metadata.
Coverage: Member contexts checked in the complete posted poster collection. Each member remains at conference depth.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18 UTC

Lemon balm. P0547 identifies Melissa officinalis in the nine-extract STW 5 mixture. It reports tissue changes in reflux-esophagitis rats. P1127 reports segment-specific permeability and colonic-transit changes under stress with the same blend, while total transit and corticosterone did not show clear differences. P1828 reports a pooled functional dyspepsia analysis for the blend. The studies supply neither a single-leaf tea arm nor a depression measure.

Coconut oil. A dietary comparison in rats used control feed, coconut oil and evening primrose oil. The investigators report no clear gastrointestinal motility contrast between those diets. The member supplies no heated or grade-matched oil and no human Parkinson outcome.

Corn oil. A tumor model used a corn-oil control diet in comparisons involving EPA and aspirin. Tumor measures belong to the model and the oil formulation and concomitant arms prevent transfer to the culinary symptom claims.

Olive and soybean oil. P0635 grouped 388 hospital patients by intravenous lipid emulsion, including 90 soybean-based and 141 olive-based recipients. It reports a shorter stay for the olive-based group in one comparison, with other formulations and retrospective allocation. This is parenteral nutrition without a dietary symptom comparison.

Coffee. Separate members report coffee with a standardized reflux-test breakfast, coffee consumption as a clinical covariate and coffee charcoal in a herbal product. P1064 compares the constituent extracts and blend in cell and bowel preparations. These exposures and outcomes are distinct from drinking coffee for Parkinson fatigue.

Broccoli, barley and green tea. P0032 tests isolated sulforaphane in a liver fibrosis model. A separate clinical member includes barley in a skin-sensitization testing panel. P1263 tests isolated EGCG in a mouse chemotherapy mucositis model with gut histology. Treat each separately from the corresponding whole-food exposure.

Chicken, egg and salmon. One clinical series reports chicken bones among esophageal foreign bodies. A separate intake analysis includes poultry and eggs across small-intestinal bacterial overgrowth groups. An allergy member reports egg sensitization. An analytical member characterizes food microRNA, including salmon. None supplies the specified Parkinson preparation and outcome combination.
