Title: Cognitive fluctuations in Parkinson's disease dementia: blood pressure lability as an underlying mechanism
PMID: 29456869
PMCID: PMC5811960
DOI: 10.1186/s40734-018-0068-4
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5811960/
Publication year: 2018
Coverage: Complete original JATS including case and timeline.

This case describes Parkinson disease followed by cognitive impairment, formed visual experiences and marked blood pressure variability. At one observed spell, blood pressure rose after the patient was repositioned and alertness returned. The contemporaneous outcome was alertness and capacity to respond, while visual hallucinations appear in the broader neurological history. The report does not specify a food trigger, dried fig intake or a corresponding visual episode rate. The 2026 four-case report examines the proposed cognitive fluctuation mechanism in a separate publication.
