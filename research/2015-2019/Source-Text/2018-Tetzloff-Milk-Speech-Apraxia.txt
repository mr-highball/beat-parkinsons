Tetzloff KA et al., Neurocase 2018, PMID 29799310 PMC6095655 DOI 10.1080/13554794.2018.1477963. Primary report https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6095655/. In 2010 his progressive apraxia compromised intelligibility, no dysarthria; later clinical report describes fixed mixed diet including milk, oatmeal, orange juice, blueberries, toast and grapes and essentially mute examination, with parkinsonism. Autopsy corticobasal degeneration. Speech examination includes motor speech disorder scale, apraxia of speech rating scale and dysarthria ratings; no milk challenge or milk-free comparison. Species, processing, serving and relation in time not specified. Same case earlier cataloged for blueberry, not independent study.
