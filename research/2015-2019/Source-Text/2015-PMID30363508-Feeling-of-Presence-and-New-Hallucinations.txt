Title: Predictable Risk Factors for the Feeling of Presence in Patients with Parkinson's Disease.
PMID: 30363508
PMCID: PMC6178595
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12233
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6178595/
First publication date: 2015-09-16 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC complete original article HTML and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The two-year Parkinson follow-up asked 78 people about feeling of presence and hallucinations. Seventeen developed a new feeling of presence, seven of them developed subsequent hallucinations, and 26 developed first visual hallucinations. The study measures onset and sequence rather than within-person episode frequency under a fig exposure.
