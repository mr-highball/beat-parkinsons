Title: Date fruit exposure and a family series of hereditary fructose intolerance
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4859097/

Review depth: Structured original case narratives and results table.

Three adult relatives reported adverse symptoms associated with dates, and reported symptoms after honey as well. One participant, who also had treated ulcerative colitis, described episodic nonspecific abdominal pain with possible date ingestion. Two relatives underwent clinical fructose testing and had symptomatic declines in glucose. The third did not take the test. Symptoms improved after broader fructose restriction. Direct testing used fructose and did not reproduce the symptoms with a controlled fruit challenge.

Date fruit drying state, cultivar and pain-intensity changes by exposure are unreported. This clustered case series supplies context for a particular metabolic condition. It does not establish pain effects for the frozen dried-date Parkinson claim.
