Coverage: Full 239-page organizer supplement and visually checked pages S16 and S96. This text summarizes the relevant scope and member boundaries.

Abstract 41 (S96) examines food intake and sleep in 814 Bavarian adults. The survey groups coffee and black tea together and reports an association with sleep-duration categories (p=.01). It gives no separate beverage estimate, prescribed preparation, Parkinson group, fatigue severity or walking velocity result.

Abstract 316 (S16) evaluates a smartphone gait-measurement approach. The measurement comparisons include five healthy adults and 31 people with hemiplegia walking below 30 m/min. The authors describe use of the system in hospitals for several clinical populations, including Parkinson disease. The gait comparison uses no dietary exposure and is separate from abstract 41.

The review of plant-based diet patterns on S47 mentions eggs as a possible pattern component. The review of diabetes risk and dietary patterns on S89 discusses poultry in multi-food patterns. The instrument report on S13 uses Raspberry Pi hardware. These contexts do not supply culinary exposures matched to Parkinson fall frequency, walking slowness, fatigue or anxiety.
