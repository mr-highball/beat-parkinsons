Title: Effects of almond consumption on the post-lunch dip and long-term cognitive function in energy-restricted overweight and obese adults.
PMID: 28183366
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114516004463
Registry ID: NCT02360787
First published: 2017
Authors: Dhillon, Tan, Mattes
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28183366/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-week RCT (NCT02360787) in 86 overweight/obese adults on energy restriction randomized to an almond-enriched diet (AED, 15% energy) or nut-free diet (NFD), plus acute high-fat almond lunch vs high-carbohydrate lunch. Acutely, the almond lunch attenuated the post-lunch decline in memory by 57.7% vs high-carb lunch (P = 0.004), while both lunch groups had similar declines in attention. Over 12 weeks, memory and attention improved in both groups (P < 0.05) with no between-group difference between AED and NFD. Companion PMID 27807041 reported DBP reduction (-2.71 vs +0.815 mmHg, P = 0.029) in compliant participants only, with ITT null. Limits: non-PD weight-loss cohort, null chronic attention/memory contrast.
