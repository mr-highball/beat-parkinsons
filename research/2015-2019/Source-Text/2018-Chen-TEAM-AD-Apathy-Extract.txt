Chen et al., Neuropsychiatric Symptoms and Caregiver Burden in Individuals With Alzheimer’s Disease: The TEAM-AD VA Cooperative Study. First published 2018. PMID29966477, DOI 10.1177/0891988718783897. Original factual extract of primary report and baseline Table 5, https://doi.org/10.1177/0891988718783897.

The secondary analysis used baseline data only from 613 participants with Alzheimer disease. Forty-seven percent had any apathy. In Table 5, 152 had an apathy/indifference score of at least four, 78 of 362 in the mild and 74 of 251 in the moderate group. Adjusted OR 1.48 (95% CI 1.02 to 2.16) compares moderate with mild disease. The baseline report gives no association of later apathy with soybean oil or any assigned treatment.
