Title: Clinical and imaging progression over 10 years in a patient with primary progressive apraxia of speech and autopsy-confirmed corticobasal degeneration
PMID: 29799310
DOI: 10.1080/13554794.2018.1477963
Publication date: 2018; PubMed article date 2018-05-25, issue April 2018; OpenAlex gives 2018-03-04. Earliest public date unresolved.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6095655/
Text type: factual research extract, reviewed 2026-11-15

One longitudinal case presented with progressive apraxia of speech, then parkinsonism and dysarthria, with autopsy-confirmed corticobasal degeneration. The late clinical history says a fixed diet included blueberries alongside other foods, and examination described the patient as essentially mute. The patient had very mild hypomimia earlier in the disease course. The berry's preparation, cultivar, quantity, and exposure timing before symptoms are unspecified; there is no blueberry comparison. The case is not evidence of a causal blueberry effect in idiopathic Parkinson's disease.
