Acetylcholinesterase assay for watermelon seed oil
Publication: 2018
Source: https://chimie-biologie.ubm.ro/carpathian_journal/Vol_10%281%29_2018.pdf
Coverage: Oguntoye and colleagues, Carpathian Journal of Food Science and Technology 10(1), 82–94 (2018). Original special issue pages and institutional deposit https://eprints.lmu.edu.ng/1437/.

The team compared seed oils and polyphenolic fractions from several melon species using cell-free acetylcholinesterase and antioxidant assays. They report an acetylcholinesterase IC50 of 19.50 µg/mL for the Citrullus lanatus seed oil. The report contains no assigned human food exposure and no measured clinical Parkinson outcome.
