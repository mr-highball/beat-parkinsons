Title: Comparing Effectiveness of a Combined Herbal Drug Based on Echium Amoenum with Citalopram in the Treatment of Major Depressive Disorder
PMID: 29468978
DOI: 10.2174/1570163815666180219115844
Source: https://www.benthamscience.com/article/88630
Reference: Anushiravani and colleagues, Current Drug Discovery Technologies 16(2):232–238, 2019 issue; indexed in 2018.
Text coverage: Original author abstract.

The paper describes 50 adults with major depressive disorder in a randomized, active-comparator study. It compares an Echium-based compound syrup with citalopram for eight weeks. The authors report a lower Hamilton depression score in the syrup arm at week eight (p=.03). The original abstract gives no arm means, interval estimate or complete ingredient list.

Secondary reviews attribute lemon balm to the compound. The author abstract does not name Melissa. A same-title thesis is indexed, but its body was inaccessible through the located author-deposit routes. The original ingredient identity, material type, full quantitative contrast and trial registration remain open. The original report describes a mixed syrup outside Parkinson disease. It provides no isolated brewed-leaf comparison.
