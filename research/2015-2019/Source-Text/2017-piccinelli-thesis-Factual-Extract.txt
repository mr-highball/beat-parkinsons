Piccinelli's doctoral thesis summarizes the previously published nerve injury experiment as showing reduced hyperalgesia and inhibited forced-swim immobility. The latter direction differs from the 2014 journal abstract. The public thesis file omits the article pages between printed pages 40 and 49. The summary supplies a conflicting description of the same experiment, with no independent group estimate and no ginger exposure.
