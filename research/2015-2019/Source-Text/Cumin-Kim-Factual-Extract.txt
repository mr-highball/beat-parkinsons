Title: 2016 Cuminum extract in an MPTP mouse model
Source: https://tjpr.org/home/abstract.php?id=437


Kim et al., 2016-04-30, DOI 10.4314/tjpr.v15i4.14, authenticated dried Cuminum cyminum fruit, powdered it, extracted it using boiling water, then administered the aqueous extract by oral gavage to MPTP-treated mice at 100, 200 or 300 mg/kg for 21 days. Rotarod, open-field line crossings, rearing, grooming and immobility, and passive-avoidance tests were used. The authors report higher rotarod performance on day 20 with the extracts. These are movement and learning measures in a toxin model, not levodopa-induced dyskinesia. The methods contain an apparent group-name typo, MPTP + ITE 300 mg/kg, while the dosing paragraph and results call the group cumin extract. No toasted culinary seed or human exposure was tested.
