Claim 0359, grated turmeric and muscle rigidity severity. The paper introduces rigidity as a feature of Parkinson disease on page e23. The Curcuma longa material (PDF text extraction lines 138–141, references 74–78) cites a cell alpha-synuclein experiment and experiments on isolated curcumin or curcuminoids in MPTP mice. Their reported outcomes include dopaminergic neuron markers and signaling. It gives no clinical rigidity outcome, passive-resistance measure, culinary grated-turmeric intervention or claim-comparable contrast.

Bradykinesia in the review appears in clinical background and separately in a discussion of Chunghyuldan. The cited material gives no culinary ground-turmeric clinical bradykinesia contrast.
