Nocturnal sialorrhea eligibility and instrumental swallowing outcomes
https://www.e-arm.org/upload/pdf/arm-40-190.pdf

April 25, 2016. The study compared 24 men with Parkinson dysphagia and 24 controls using ultrasound. Nocturnal sialorrhea is an example of an eligibility complaint. Swallowing measurements include tongue thickness, the distance and timing of hyoid-thyroid approximation, and a fluoroscopy subset assessing penetration and aspiration. The protocol gives no separate food exposure or measured change in drooling or rest tremor.
