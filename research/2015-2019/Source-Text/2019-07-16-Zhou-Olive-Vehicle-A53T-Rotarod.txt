Olive oil comparator in the A53T rotarod study
Source: https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s13311-019-00759-8.pdf
PMID: 31313223
PMCID: PMC6985330
DOI: 10.1007/s13311-019-00759-8
Coverage: Original journal PDF, in vivo Methods and Figure 6

Zhou and colleagues studied a tetramethylpyrazine analogue in an A53T alpha-synuclein mouse model. Wild-type or transgenic mice received T-006 or olive oil vehicle for four weeks. Rotarod retention was tested in ten mice per group and improved with T-006 in the transgenic comparison. This design measures the tested compound with an oil vehicle and does not isolate olive oil or measure a human postural-instability outcome.
