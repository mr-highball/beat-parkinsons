Title: Neural correlates of attention-executive dysfunction in lewy body dementia and Alzheimer's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4784171/
PMCID: PMC4784171
PMID: 26705763
DOI: 10.1002/hbm.23100
Publication date: 2015-12-26
Date basis: NLM electronic publication. Journal issue March 2016.
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary cognitive imaging study
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Lewy body dementia, Alzheimer disease and control groups completed an attention and executive task during functional imaging. Behavioral and task-network outcomes were analyzed, while hallucination-related networks were discussed in the interpretation. There was no measured dietary exposure or visual episode rate.
