Original source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5911194/

First online December 27, 2017. Eligible paired diary data were available for 292 participants, with 582 flare and 797 nonflare assessments. The within-participant estimate for flare onset per category increase in servings of grouped spicy foods in the previous three days was OR 0.99 (95% CI 0.81–1.22). The estimate was 1.67 (1.13–2.48) in the subgroup who suspected a dietary trigger. The food contrast measures composite flare onset. Urgency was higher during flares, but there was no separate food-specific urgency estimate or smoked paprika exposure.
