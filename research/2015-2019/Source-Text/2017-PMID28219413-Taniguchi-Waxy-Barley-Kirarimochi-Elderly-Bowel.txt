Title: Effect of waxy barley, Kirarimochi, consumption on bowel movements of late-stage elderly residents at Roken nursing home.
PMID: 28219413
PMCID: PMC5319020
DOI: 10.1186/s40101-017-0131-0
First published: 2017
Authors: Taniguchi K, Komae K, Takahashi A, Yoshioka T, Sone Y
Journal: Journal of physiological anthropology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28219413/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

5-month longitudinal dietary intervention (PMC5319020) in n=28 late-stage elderly nursing home residents (mean age 88.9 y; 14 constipated, 14 non-constipated) consuming boiled Kirarimochi waxy barley mixed with rice. Among constipated residents (n=14), monthly defecation days increased significantly from baseline 7.1+/-1.6 to 8.9, 9.1, and 9.6 days across intervention months (p=0.014, 0.024, 0.030), monthly laxative-use days fell from 6.3+/-2.3 to 4.1 days (p=0.003, 0.018), and defecation-to-laxative ratio rose from 1.3 to 4.4 (p=0.007-0.033). Non-constipated residents (n=14) showed no adverse diarrhea shift. Limits: single-group longitudinal pre-post design without parallel control; elderly non-PD nursing home residents.
