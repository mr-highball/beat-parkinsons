DHA pilot conference results and registry denominators

Source: https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/preventing-levodopa-induced-dyskinesia-with-docosohexanoid-acid-in-parkinson-disease/
Coverage: Primary 2017 conference abstract and 2018 official result, factual extract.

Chung and Lobb, 2017 International Congress abstract 1347. Thirty-three participants were randomized before starting levodopa. The poster reported dyskinesia by 12 months in 3/15 on DHA and 7/15 on placebo, with no difference in peak severity stated. The later clinical registry reports 3/9 versus 6/15 for its forceplate-defined outcome among available measurements. The records share investigators, 33 randomized, the specific intervention and visiting schedule. The denominators differ and the reports should not be pooled as independent trials or assumed to report the same endpoint set. Neither tested walnut oil or alpha-linolenic acid as the active ingredient.
