Ginger in both arms of a senna-containing constipation trial
2015-02-21 (first public date in the article JATS)
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4488565/

Somi and colleagues randomized 48 adults with chronic constipation and analyzed 40 completers. Both groups received dried ginger, peppermint and licorice. Only the Lax-Asab product also contained senna. Mean weekly defecations changed from 1.8 to 4.8 with Lax-Asab and 1.7 to 2.2 with the comparator over four weeks (time-by-treatment p<.0001). The original Methods identify ginger in the product and its comparison mixture. The group contrast cannot establish a ginger effect, and the study did not test fresh grated ginger or enroll a Parkinson cohort.
