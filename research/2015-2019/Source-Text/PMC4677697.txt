Title: Clinical and Cognitive Phenotype of Mild Cognitive Impairment Evolving to Dementia with Lewy Bodies

Publication date: 2015-11-24

Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary longitudinal diagnostic cohort
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The study compares people with mild cognitive impairment who later met criteria for dementia with Lewy bodies or Alzheimer disease. Visual hallucinations were recorded at the mild cognitive impairment stage in nine of 25 Lewy body converters and in one Alzheimer converter. The authors also measured cognitive and visuospatial task performance. The clinical count is the number of patients with the symptom, not the number of episodes. No dried fig exposure was compared.
