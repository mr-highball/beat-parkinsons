Title: Dietary nitrate modulates cerebral blood flow parameters and cognitive performance in humans: A double-blind, placebo-controlled, crossover investigation.
PMID: 26037632
DOI: 10.1016/j.physbeh.2015.05.035
Registry ID: NCT01169662
First published: 2015
Authors: Wightman EL, Haskell-Ramsay CF, Thompson KG, Blackwell JR, Winyard PG, Forster J, et al.
Journal: Physiology & behavior
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26037632/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled parallel trial in n=40 healthy adults ingesting 450 mL beetroot juice vs placebo: active BRJ modulated prefrontal hemodynamic response and significantly improved serial 3s subtraction cognitive performance during sustained attentional demand. Limits: acute single-dose trial in healthy young adults.
