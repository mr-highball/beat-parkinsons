Black raspberry powder and Barrett surveillance outcomes
https://www.oncotarget.com/article/10457/text/

July 7, 2016 online. Twenty Barrett esophagus participants completed 26 weeks of lyophilized black raspberry powder with baseline comparison. Jewel fruit were frozen and then freeze-dried before administration as a suspension. The Methods report biomarkers and clinical monitoring, without a specific anxiety outcome or a concurrent untreated group. It cites the 2006 interim report and shares investigators, intervention and timing with the 2007 conference report. Exact participant overlap has not been established.
