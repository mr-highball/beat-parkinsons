Title: Neurochemical and Behavior Deficits in Rats with Iron and Rotenone Co-treatment: Role of Redox Imbalance and Neuroprotection by Biochanin A.

First published: 2017
Authors: Yu L, Wang X, Chen H, Yan Z, Wang M, Li Y
Journal: Front Neurosci

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rodent study (Yu et al., 2017, Neurotox Res, PMC5703859) examining purified biochanin A in iron plus rotenone co-treated rats. Biochanin A reduced striatal dopamine depletion, oxidative stress, and behavioral deficits. Critical limits: isolated compound dosing (not dietary whole chickpeas) in a chemical rodent model.
