Title: Molecular changes in the absence of severe pathology in the pulvinar in dementia with Lewy bodies

Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The postmortem pulvinar comparison reports differences in transcripts and selected synaptic proteins in dementia with Lewy bodies. Clinical visual hallucinations frame the rationale. Tissue findings cannot supply the exposure or episode rate in the frozen claim.

Synaptic marker measures are based on separately described postmortem cohorts and assays. Differences across transcript and protein results require keeping each measure distinct.
