Oral groundnut oil and measured paw inflammation in adjuvant arthritis
Source: https://ir.cftri.res.in/13270/
Coverage: Factual extract of primary Methods and Tables 2–4

The adjuvant arthritis study compared market groundnut oil and oil stripped in the laboratory. The paper describes the groundnut oil minor-compound content inconsistently across narrative and arm labels. Reported mean CRP was 7.60 mg/dL in the labelled native oil arm, 15.80 in stripped oil and 16.80 with adjuvant alone. The study measured inflammation and paw changes without a pain score.
