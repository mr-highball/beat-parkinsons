Akahane et al. Long-Term Health Effects of PCBs and Related Compounds: A Comparative Analysis of Patients Suffering from Yusho and the General Population. First published December 18, 2017, 2018 journal issue.
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00244-017-0486-6
Access and coverage: Publisher author abstract, survey methods and reported associations. Item-level adjusted estimate and hoarseness ascertainment detail were not available in the accessible report.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

The investigators compared 1,131 Japanese Yusho survivors surveyed in 2008 with a general population sample matched by age and residential area. The exposure was oil implicated in a contamination outbreak first reported in 1968. Hoarseness and dysgeusia were among the outcomes associated with Yusho in the abstract. The analysis does not isolate effects of rice bran oil from the contamination, qualify oil refinement, or report reduced voice amplitude, handwriting or a Parkinson population.
