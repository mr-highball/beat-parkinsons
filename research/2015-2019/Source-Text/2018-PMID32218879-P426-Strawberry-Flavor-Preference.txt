Text coverage: Curated original posters P-426 and same-page P-427 scope

P-426 interviewed 148 people aged at least 60, including 62 in a
geriatric department and 86 community participants. The authors
report that 32.9% preferred a strawberry-flavored oral nutritional
supplement, with 60.8% preferring nectar-thick liquids. Participants
reported preferences. No strawberry fruit, flavor or supplement
was randomly assigned in this interview.

P-427 is a separate study in the same proceedings page. Its falls
analysis concerns vitamin D status in admitted patients. The
adjacent results cannot be attached to strawberry flavor or to
P-426's participants. Neither poster compares raw strawberries and
recurrent falls in people with Parkinson disease.
