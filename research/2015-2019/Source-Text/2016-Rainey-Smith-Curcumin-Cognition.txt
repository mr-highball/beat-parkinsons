Turmeric curcuminoid formulation and repeated cognitive testing
Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/british-journal-of-nutrition/article/curcumin-and-cognition-a-randomised-placebocontrolled-doubleblind-study-of-communitydwelling-older-adults/83419D0C8B37ACD8BBC7C27B91FBAF58

Coverage: Factual summary of full primary table and methods.

Published online in 2016. Rainey-Smith and colleagues enrolled 160 community-dwelling older adults and analyzed 96 who satisfied eligibility and compliance requirements. The BCM-95 product combined curcuminoids and volatile turmeric oils. The attention-containing cognitive battery showed no clear differential change. The MoCA intermediate visit drove the stated three-visit interaction, while the baseline to 12-month test gave p=.25. Attrition and baseline differences matter. The study did not test ordinary grated turmeric or Parkinson attention.
