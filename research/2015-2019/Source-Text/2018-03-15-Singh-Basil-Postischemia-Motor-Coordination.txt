Sweet basil leaf extract after mouse ischemia
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11011-018-0215-5
PMID: 29546690
DOI: 10.1007/s11011-018-0215-5
Coverage: Author abstract, original publisher preview and cited predecessors

Singh, Krishan and Shri report seven days of oral sweet basil leaf extract after carotid occlusion and reperfusion in mice. The abstract specifies examination of sensorimotor deficits with a neurological severity score and reports improved motor coordination. Detailed outcome tables were not available in the accessible text. This paper cites the 2011 pretreatment report and uses a different schedule. Its abstract gives no outcome for stride length, fresh culinary basil or Parkinson disease.
