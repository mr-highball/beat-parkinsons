Factual source comparison. The published knee osteoarthritis trial describes low-dose fish oil in Sunola versus high-dose fish oil and reports a WOMAC pain comparison at two years. In a separate interview, https://www.abc.net.au/listen/programs/healthreport/best-oil-for-osteoarthritis/6792188, the lead investigator refers to the comparator diluent as canola. The wording leaves the actual plant oil unresolved without the study product specification. Fish oil dose is also part of the contrast.
