Title: Asparagus soup among texture modified foods in a dysphagia tasting
Source: https://researchprofiles.ku.dk/en/publications/acceptance-of-texture-modified-in-between-meals-among-old-adults-/
Format: Factual extract from the original record.

Okkels and colleagues, Clinical Nutrition ESPEN 25:126–132, June 2018. Thirty adults aged 70 or older with dysphagia were recruited from three nursing homes to score flavor and appearance of 20 texture-modified snacks. The original food table lists asparagus-and-chicken soup. The outcomes are food liking, based on a three-point scale, and make no clinical severity comparison after eating steamed asparagus. No Parkinson subgroup estimate was identified.
