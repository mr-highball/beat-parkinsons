Measured outcomes in the SunGold congee preload crossover
https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/10/8/1110

First published August 17, 2018. Thirty healthy Chinese adults enrolled and 28 completed all breakfast periods, with 29 at lunch. Water, rice or ripe SunGold kiwi was given before the congee meal. Glucose incremental area under the curve was smaller with the kiwi than water preload after breakfast, but there was no clear treatment difference in incremental area under the curve after lunch. The trial also measured hormonal responses, satiety and energy intake. The report supplies no separate cognitive attention outcome.
