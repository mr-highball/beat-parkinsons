Title: Improvement of memory recall by quercetin in rodent contextual fear conditioning and human early-stage Alzheimer's disease patients.
PMID: 27145228
DOI: 10.1097/WNR.0000000000000594
First published: 2016
Authors: Nakagawa T, Itoh M, Ohta K, Hayashi Y, Hayakawa M, Yamada Y
Journal: NeuroReport
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27145228/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
4-week trial in early-stage AD patients comparing quercetin-rich 'Quergold' hybrid onion powder vs 'Mashiro' white onion powder: improved Revised Hasegawa Dementia Scale (HDS-R) memory recall. Evaluates episodic memory recall in AD rather than attention, executive function, or PD symptoms.
