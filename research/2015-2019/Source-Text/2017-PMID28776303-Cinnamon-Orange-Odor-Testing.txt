Original study scope
In the olfaction section, a Parkinson smell-identification task used cinnamon and orange odors. Cinnamon was among the difficult odors. Fourteen of 20 participants identified orange. Constipation, motor signs and REM sleep behavior appear in separate clinical discussions. No study fed ground cinnamon or raw orange for symptom change.

Continuing evidence gaps
The task distinguishes the ability to identify an odor from an effect of ingesting the food. Retrieve the cited diagnostic task reports for test composition if needed, and continue the separate oral culinary and outcome questions.
