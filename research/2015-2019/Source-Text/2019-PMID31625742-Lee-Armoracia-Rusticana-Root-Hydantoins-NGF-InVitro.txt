Title: Thiohydantoin and Hydantoin Derivatives from the Roots of Armoracia rusticana and Their Neurotrophic and Anti-neuroinflammatory Activities
PMID: 31625742
DOI: 10.1021/acs.jnatprod.9b00527
First published: 2019
Authors: Lee TH, Khan Z, Kim SY, Lee KR
Journal: Journal of Natural Products
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31625742/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Phytochemical isolation and cell-culture screening study identifying thiohydantoin and hydantoin derivatives from Armoracia rusticana roots that stimulated NGF secretion in C6 glioma cells (up to 153.6%) and marginally inhibited LPS microglial NO (IC50 32.6 uM; companion to Lee 2023 PMID:37806466 phenolic glycosides in C6/BV-2 cells). Cell-only laboratory study of isolated minor root compounds; no in vivo PD evidence.
