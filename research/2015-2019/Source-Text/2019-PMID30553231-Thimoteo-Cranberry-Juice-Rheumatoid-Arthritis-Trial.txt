Title: Cranberry juice decreases disease activity in women with rheumatoid arthritis.
PMID: 30553231
DOI: 10.1016/j.nut.2018.10.010
First published: 2019
Authors: Thimóteo NSB, Iryioda TMV, Alfieri DF, Rego BEF, Scavuzzi BM, Fatel E, Lozovoy MAB, Simão ANC
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30553231/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Prospective 90-day open-label controlled study (Thimoteo et al., 2019, Nutrition) in 41 women with rheumatoid arthritis (23 consuming 500 mL/day low-calorie cranberry juice, 18 usual-diet controls). Relative to baseline, the cranberry juice group decreased composite Disease Activity Score 28 (DAS28, p=0.048) and anti-CCP antibodies (p=0.034), while systemic inflammatory biomarkers did not change and no isolated between-group VAS pain contrast is reported in the abstract. Limits: open-label non-placebo design, liquid juice rather than cooked whole cranberries, composite RA activity rather than isolated pain severity or Parkinson pain.
