The review surveys genetic, biochemical and biological-fluid markers for aging-related neurocognitive disorders. Recurrent visual hallucinations are cited as a Lewy body dementia feature. The differential timing of cognitive and motor onset is diagnostic context. No dried fig contrast or measured effect on visual episodes is supplied.
