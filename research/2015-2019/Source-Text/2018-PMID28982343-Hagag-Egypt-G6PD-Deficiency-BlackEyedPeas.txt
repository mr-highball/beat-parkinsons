Title: Study of Glucose-6-Phosphate Dehydrogenase Deficiency: 5 Years Retrospective Egyptian Study.

Journal: Endocrine, metabolic & immune disorders drug targets

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Five-year retrospective hospital series (Hagag et al., 2018, Endocr Metab Immune Disord Drug Targets) of 1,000 Egyptian pediatric patients with G6PD deficiency anemia. Dietary triggers accounted for 83.4% of hemolytic crises—led by fava beans (326 cases, 32.6%) and falafel (194, 19.4%)—with black-eyed peas linked to 20 cases (2.0%). Safety context only; not a Parkinson's symptom study.
