Title: Caffeine, theacrine, and cayenne in an acute mood and exercise supplement crossover
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03678116
Trial registration: NCT03678116
Publication date: 2018-09-19
Date basis: First trial registration posting.
Source depth: Official registry protocol and status.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27

Fourteen participants were enrolled in a completed crossover with placebo, caffeine plus theacrine, and caffeine plus theacrine plus cayenne. The registry lists mood visual analog measures including fatigue during four hours of observation. The registry has no posted results. The cayenne arm is a multi-ingredient supplement with an acute exposure.

The design is related to capsaicinoid exposure and fatigue. It does not establish a result for raw sweet bell pepper or for people with Parkinson disease.
