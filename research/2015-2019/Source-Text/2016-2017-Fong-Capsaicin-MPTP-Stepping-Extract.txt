Coverage: Original factual comparison of the 2016 primary journal abstract with the 2017 authored thesis abstract as exposed by OpenAlex. The full thesis was not publicly retrieved through the checked routes.

Both records specify pretreatment with isolated capsaicin and gait testing in a subset of male C57BL/6 mice six days after treatment began. The conference abstract describes abnormal steps and akinesia in MPTP mice and reports normalization after capsaicin, while also stating that overall gait dynamics did not change significantly with its MPTP regimen. It does not specify a numeric stride or freezing measure. The later thesis abstract gives no gait result. The records differ in their characterization of neuronal viability and cerebral inflammatory findings despite matching main design details. Cohort overlap is probable but cannot be verified without underlying Methods and tables. None of this establishes a culinary paprika exposure.
