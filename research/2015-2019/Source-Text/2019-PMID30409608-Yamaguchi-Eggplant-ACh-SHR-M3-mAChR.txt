Title: Antihypertensive effects of orally administered eggplant (Solanum melongena) rich in acetylcholine on spontaneously hypertensive rats.
PMID: 30409608
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodchem.2018.10.017
First published: 2019
Authors: Yamaguchi S, Matsumoto K, Koyama M, Tian S, Watanabe M, Takahashi A
Journal: Food chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30409608/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Preclinical hypertensive rat study administering oral lyophilized Solanum melongena powder (0.0650 mg/kg acute; 0.821 mg/kg/d chronic) to spontaneously hypertensive rats (SHRs). Significantly lowered SBP and DBP, suppressed urinary adrenaline and noradrenaline excretion, and acted via M3 muscarinic ACh receptors (M3 mAChR) to suppress sympathetic nervous activity. Preclinical cardiovascular mechanism in hypertensive rats only; does not test orthostatic hypotension or PD symptoms.
