Title: Antipsychotics for the management of psychosis in Parkinson's disease: systematic review and meta-analysis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4998940/
PMCID: PMC4998940

DOI: 10.1192/bjpo.bp.115.000927
Publication date: 2015-06
Date basis: NLM issue month. Electronic publication was 2015-07-24.
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Systematic review and meta-analysis
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Nine antipsychotic trials in Parkinson psychosis cover quetiapine, clozapine, olanzapine and pimavanserin. Effects and tolerability varied. Clozapine improved the summarized psychosis outcome versus placebo, while pooled quetiapine and olanzapine analyses did not show clear benefit. Broad psychosis scales and trial-level outcomes do not estimate a specific dried-food effect. The constituent trials provide distinct endpoints and should not be counted again as corroborating samples.
