Green and gold kiwi cereal trial registration
https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=ACTRN12615000222549

First registered March 9, 2015. The completed crossover compared green Hayward and gold SunGold kiwifruit, cereal, mixtures of fruit and cereal, and a glucose reference. Scheduled outcomes concern glycaemic potency, glycaemic index and matched meals. The registered finger-prick blood glucose outcomes and explicit identifier link to the 2016 Mishra publication.
