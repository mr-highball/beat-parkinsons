Title: EVOO diet and swallowing exercise trial protocol in ALS
Source: https://symposium.mndassociation.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/WIPS-final-141117.pdf
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Flood, Vucic, Bogaardt and Menon, 28th International Symposium on ALS/MND, 2017, abstract CW-31, pages 44–45. The work-in-progress abstract planned 45 people randomized to laryngeal exercise, diet targeting 20% of energy from extra-virgin olive oil, or their combination, for four weeks with swallowing assessment. Recruitment was underway. The abstract contains no results. Matching team, intervention and arms appear in two 2019 preliminary records.
