Title: Development and validation of a short food questionnaire to screen for low protein intake in community-dwelling older adults: The Protein Screener 55+ (Pro55+)
Publication date: 2018-05-22
PMID: 29791454
PMCID: PMC5965846
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0196406
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5965846/
Text coverage: Distilled methods and Table 2 from the original open-access article.

The model estimates whether intake was at or below 1.0 g/kg of adjusted body weight per day, using a longer food questionnaire as its reference. The development sample included 1,348 people and external validation included 563. The final model used 1,319 development records with complete responses. Relative to eating eggs less than weekly, the fitted coefficient for eggs at least three days per week was −1.480 (SE 0.262), with a shrunken coefficient of −1.361 on validation. This is a coefficient for classification of low protein intake, and preparation was unspecified. There was no poached-egg intervention, recorded fall rate or walking-speed comparison. This publication and geriatric congress abstract O-4 (PMC7091737) are the same analysis.
