Title: Habitual coffee consumption and changes in measures of adiposity: a comprehensive study of longitudinal associations
Publication date: 2017-12-21 (online first)
PMID: 29381147
DOI: 10.1038/ijo.2017.310
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29381147/
Text coverage: Distilled author abstract and cross-check against ECO T2P126.

Among up to 2,128 Danish MONICA participants, a higher concurrent change in coffee intake was associated with a small inverse change in BMI and waist circumference. The BMI estimate per additional daily cup was −0.05 kg/m² (95% CI −0.07 to −0.02), and the waist estimate was −0.23 cm (95% CI −0.34 to −0.12). Earlier coffee changes did not predict subsequent adiposity changes, and baseline intake did not consistently predict later changes. The 2018 conference report summarizes the same analysis. Fatigue, Parkinson status and a verified brewing method were outside the reported comparison.
