Title: Holy basil (Ocimum sanctum Linn.) leaf extract enhances specific cognitive parameters in healthy adult volunteers: A placebo controlled study
Source: https://ijpp.com/IJPP%20archives/2015_59_1/69-77.pdf
PMID: 26571987
Trial registry: CTRI/2010/091/006113
Publication date: 2015
Coverage: Complete IJPP original, volume 59, issue 1, pages 69–77, Methods and Tables II–III.
Original publisher PDF SHA-256: ac415cd9b8abe0b2ed79c41fce8c0e25a154aa59492ba332c12215f1fa64f46d

The study randomized 44 healthy young men. Forty completed the trial, with 20 in each arm. The participants received either 300 mg daily of ethanolic extract from dried holy basil (Ocimum sanctum, also called O. tenuiflorum) leaves or placebo for 30 days.
The investigators reported lower Stroop interference reaction time and error rates in the extract arm compared with placebo at day 30. Error rates were summarized as medians and interquartile ranges. Repeated performance also improved within both groups. The facilitation comparison was inconclusive.
The study concerns a different basil species, an ethanolic extract and healthy volunteers. It gives no estimate for dried culinary sweet basil or executive function in Parkinson disease.
