Title: Contribution of insula in Parkinson's disease: A quantitative meta-analysis study

Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The insula imaging meta-analysis considers 96 reports and maps fMRI and PET comparisons. Visual hallucination groups appear in the source-study inventory, including Ballanger's receptor PET study. The imaging synthesis has no dietary comparison or pooled visual episode rate.

Ballanger's clinical PET study (PMID 20385906) compares visual hallucination status with serotonin 2A ligand binding. The review's moderator construction combines several clinical study contrasts, and the receptor study remains a separate primary evidence family.
