Original source scope
The meta-analysis includes 25 functional-constipation trials with 2089 adults. Its description identifies dried mature citrus peel in a modified Buzhong-Yiqi-Tang mixture and names Citrus reticulata and C. sinensis. The authors rate included methods generally low and identify no double-blind placebo-controlled trial.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The clinical results pertain to varied multi-herb regimens. Individual peel species, raw orange intake and the peel-specific effect in Parkinson constipation are unresolved.
