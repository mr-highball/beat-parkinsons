Astaxanthin and sesamin mixture with attention measures

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5900571/
Coverage: Original Table 2 and Results, factual extract.

Twenty-one adults with mild cognitive impairment were recruited into a blinded supplement pilot. At 12 weeks the complex attention means changed from 102.9 to 106.4 in the supplement analyzed group and 99.3 to 109.1 in placebo. Simple attention changed from 101.7 to 98.9 and 102.9 to 104.3. Reported between-group differences in change concerned psychomotor and processing speed. The supplement contained both astaxanthin and sesamin.
