Salmon meals and timed hand dexterity in children
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30598384/
original abstract paraphrase

First published online December 22, 2018. The indexed abstract describes 205 preschool children randomized to farmed Atlantic salmon or meat meals three times weekly for 16 weeks. A nine-hole peg test measured dexterity. Full primary outcome tables remain open. Grilling and Parkinson bradykinesia were not verified.
