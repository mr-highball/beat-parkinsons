PMID 30617467; PMCID PMC6394654; DOI 10.1007/s00421-018-04058-6.
Morgan and colleagues first published January 8, 2019. In a crossover, trained cyclists took capsules of Montmorency cherry powder or dextrose before a 15 km time trial. Completion time was 1,506 seconds (SD 86) with cherry and 1,580 (SD 102) with placebo, p=.004. The outcome was sports performance, with no Parkinson fatigue scale. Capsule preparation differs from eating dried whole cherries.
