Published online 2019 and in the 2020 issue. PMID 30932750; DOI 10.1024/0300-9831/a000575.
Davis and Bellar reported a double-blind crossover in 12 healthy men. Freeze-dried Montmorency powder capsules and cellulose placebo preceded a cycle test capped at 30 minutes. Time to exhaustion was 21.46 versus 19.50 minutes (both SEM 1.51, paired p=.36). The inconclusive test addresses exercise performance and supplies no estimate of Parkinson fatigue.
