Title: Effects of Lavender Tea on Fatigue, Depression, and Maternal-Infant Attachment in Sleep-Disturbed Postnatal Women.
PMID: 26523950
DOI: 10.1111/wvn.12122
First published: 2015
Authors: Chen SL, Chen CH
Journal: Worldviews on evidence-based nursing
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26523950/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Controlled clinical trial (Chen & Chen, 2015, Worldviews Evid Based Nurs) systematically assigning 80 Taiwanese sleep-disturbed postnatal women (PSQS >= 16; n=40 lavender tea, n=40 usual postpartum care) to drink one cup of brewed lavender tea daily after inhaling its aroma for 2 weeks. ANCOVA adjusted for baseline and education showed lower Postpartum Fatigue Scale scores (F=6.281, p=0.014), lower Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale scores (F=4.731, p=0.033), and improved maternal-infant bonding (F=4.022, p=0.049) at 2 weeks; all four instruments (including PSQS sleep quality) were similar between groups by the 4-week posttest. Limits: non-blinded systematic allocation, no placebo beverage, aroma appreciation step precedes ingestion, transient 2-week effect, postpartum cohort without Parkinson's disease.
