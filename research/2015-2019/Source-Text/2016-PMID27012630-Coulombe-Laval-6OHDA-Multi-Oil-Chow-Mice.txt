Title: Partial neurorescue effects of DHA following a 6-OHDA lesion of the mouse dopaminergic system.
PMID: 27012630
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2015.11.014
First published: 2016
Authors: Coulombe K, Saint-Pierre M, Cisbani G, St-Amour I, Gibrat C, Giguère-Rancourt A et al.
Journal: J Nutr Biochem
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27012630/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical Parkinson mouse study (Coulombe et al., 2016, Neurobiol Dis) testing high-DHA versus low-omega-3 control chow (formulated with a mixed vegetable oil blend including corn/soy/safflower oils) in 6-OHDA-lesioned mice. DHA partially rescued nigrostriatal endpoints relative to the omega-3-depleted multi-oil chow, but corn oil was not isolated as a single dietary variable.
