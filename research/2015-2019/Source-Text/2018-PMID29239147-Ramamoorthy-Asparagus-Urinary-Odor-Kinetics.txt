Title: Crowdsourced Asparagus Urinary Odor Population Kinetics.
PMID: 29239147
PMCID: PMC5784735
DOI: 10.1002/psp4.12264
First published: 2018
Authors: Ramamoorthy A, Sadler BM, van Hasselt JGC, Elassaiss-Schaap J, Kasichayanula S, Edwards AY, et al.
Journal: CPT: pharmacometrics & systems pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29239147/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized crowdsourced dose-response study (Ramamoorthy et al., 2018, CPT Pharmacometrics Syst Pharmacol, PMC5784735) in 87 healthy conference attendees eating assigned numbers of asparagus spears to model urinary odor perception kinetics (estimated urinary odor half-life 4.7 hours, RSE 13.2%). Directly illustrates that human clinical studies of culinary asparagus spears and urination measure volatile sulfur odor kinetics rather than urinary urgency frequency or overactive bladder symptoms.
