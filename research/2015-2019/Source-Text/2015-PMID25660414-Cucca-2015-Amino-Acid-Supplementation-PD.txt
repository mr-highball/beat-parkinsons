Title: Amino acid supplementation in l-dopa treated Parkinson's disease patients.
PMID: 25660414
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2014.12.007
First published: 2015
Authors: Cucca A, Mazzucco S, Bursomanno A, Antonutti L, Di Girolamo FG, Pizzolato G, et al.
Journal: Clinical nutrition (Edinburgh, Scotland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25660414/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled pilot trial in n=14 protein-restricted PD patients (7 amino acid supplement twice daily timed away from levodopa vs 7 placebo for 6 months): no worsening in UPDRS III, Hoehn & Yahr, levodopa equivalent dose, or ON/OFF periods while lowering oxidized glutathione (GSSG 5.2 vs 2.4 umol/L, p=0.04). Shows timing separation mitigates amino acid-levodopa interference.
