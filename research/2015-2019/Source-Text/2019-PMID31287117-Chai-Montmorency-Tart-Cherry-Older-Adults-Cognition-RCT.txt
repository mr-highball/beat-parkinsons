Title: Effect of Montmorency tart cherry juice on cognitive performance in older adults: a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 31287117
DOI: 10.1039/c9fo00913b
First published: 2019
Authors: Chai SC, Jerusik J, Davis K, Wright RS, Zhang Z
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31287117/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled trial (Chai et al., 2019, Food Funct) in 37 cognitively normal older adults (ages 65-80) assigned to two cups/day Montmorency tart cherry juice vs energy/sugar-matched control drink for 12 weeks (companion to BP/lipid report PMID:29862410). Compared with control, tart cherry improved subjective memory contentment (MD 2.7, 95% CI 1.2-4.2, p=0.02), reduced computerized movement time (MD -10.4, 95% CI -13.4 to -7.5, p=0.03), and reduced paired associates learning errors (MD -8.5, 95% CI -12.5 to -4.5, p=0.02); within-group visual sustained attention improved vs baseline (p<0.0001) while spatial working memory was borderline (p=0.06). Limits: small older-adult non-PD sample (n=37), liquid tart cherry juice rather than whole dried/raw cherries.
