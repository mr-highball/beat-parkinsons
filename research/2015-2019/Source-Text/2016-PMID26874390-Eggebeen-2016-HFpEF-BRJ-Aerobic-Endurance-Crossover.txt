Title: One Week of Daily Dosing With Beetroot Juice Improves Submaximal Endurance and Blood Pressure in Older Patients With Heart Failure and Preserved Ejection Fraction.
PMID: 26874390
PMCID: PMC4892939
DOI: 10.1016/j.jchf.2015.12.013
First published: 2016
Authors: Eggebeen J, Kim-Shapiro DB, Haykowsky M, Morgan TM, Basu S, Brubaker P, et al.
Journal: JACC. Heart failure
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26874390/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=20 older adults with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF): 1 week of daily nitrate-rich beetroot juice significantly increased submaximal aerobic endurance (time to exhaustion) by +24% (p=0.02) and lowered resting systolic BP. Limits: HFpEF exertional intolerance rather than PD fatigue.
