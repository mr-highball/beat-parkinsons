Title: The Effect of a Chickpea-Based Persian Diet on Cancer-Related Fatigue in Breast Cancer Patients: A Semi-Experimental Study.
PMID: 31132760
DOI: 10.1159/000500017
First published: 2019
Authors: Heydarirad G, Choopani R, Pasalar M, Parvizi MM, Hajian P, Mirzaei HR
Journal: Complement Med Res
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31132760/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single-arm uncontrolled pre-post semi-experimental study (Heydarirad et al., 2019, Complement Med Res, n=40 women with breast cancer receiving routine care) evaluating daily cooked traditional Persian chickpea-based soup (Nokhodab) for 3 weeks. Mean Cancer Fatigue Scale fell from 40.72 to 33.38 (p=0.001) and Fatigue Severity Scale and VAS fatigue also declined (p<0.001). Critical limits: no control or comparator group, concurrent routine cancer therapy, multi-ingredient broth matrix, high expectancy/regression-to-the-mean risk, non-Parkinson population.
