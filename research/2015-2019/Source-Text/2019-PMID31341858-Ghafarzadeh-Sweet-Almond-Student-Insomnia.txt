Title: Investigating the Prevalence of Sleep Disorder and the Impact of Sweet Almond on the Quality of Sleep in Students of Tehran, Iran.
PMID: 31341858
PMCID: PMC6635337
First published: 2019
Authors: Ghafarzadeh, Sadeghniiat-Haghighi, Sadeghpour, Akbarpour, Amini-Behbahani
Journal: Iranian journal of public health
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31341858/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

14-day uncontrolled before-after study in 446 distributed / 442 analyzed Tehran medical sciences dormitory students (49.1% female) given 10 sweet almonds daily. Using the Insomnia Severity Index (ISI), insomnia prevalence (ISI > 7) decreased from 343/442 (77.6%; 56.1% mild, 20.4% moderate, 1.1% severe) to 306/442 (69.2%; 55.4% mild, 13.1% moderate, 0.7% severe; Chi-square = 10.891, P < 0.001; Wilcoxon signed-rank Z = -4.679, P < 0.001: 135 improved, 69 worsened, 238 ties). Limits: uncontrolled before-after design without placebo or comparator, student non-PD sample, raw/unroasted state implied as sweet almond kernel.
