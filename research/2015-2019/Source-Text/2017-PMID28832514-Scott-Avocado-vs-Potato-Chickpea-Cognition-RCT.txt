Title: Avocado Consumption Increases Macular Pigment Density in Older Adults: A Randomized, Controlled Trial.
PMID: 28832514
PMCID: PMC5622679
DOI: 10.3390/nu9090919
First published: 2017
Authors: Scott TM, Rasmussen HM, Chen O, Johnson EJ
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5622679/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled dietary trial (Scott et al., 2017, Nutrients, PMC5622679, n=40 completed healthy adults >=50 years: 20 avocado, 20 control) comparing 1 fresh avocado/day for 6 months against a low-lutein control diet of either 1 medium potato OR 1 cup chickpeas daily. No significant Group x Time interaction was observed on any CANTAB cognitive measure (Stockings of Cambridge executive planning, Rapid Visual Information Processing sustained attention, Paired Associates Learning, Spatial Working Memory). Sustained attention improved from baseline within the avocado group only, whereas PAL improved in both groups (practice effect). Critical limits: chickpeas were pooled optionally with potatoes as the low-lutein control comparator rather than isolated as an active roasted chickpea arm; non-Parkinson healthy adults.
