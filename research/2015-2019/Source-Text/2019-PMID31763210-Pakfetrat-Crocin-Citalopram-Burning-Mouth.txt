Pakfetrat and colleagues reported 47 completers with burning mouth syndrome, with 26 assigned to isolated oral crocin and 21 to citalopram over 11 weeks. At week 11, Hamilton depression means were 19.0 versus 19.4 (p=.755), anxiety means 18.0 versus 18.6 (p=.755), and burning-mouth pain 1.1 versus 1.2 (p=.981), crocin first throughout. Both groups improved from baseline. Without an untreated control or a prespecified noninferiority margin, those results cannot establish an untreated benefit or equivalence.

Continuing evidence gaps
The article has inconsistent citalopram baseline depression means in its text/Table 1 versus Table 3 (29.09 versus 29.90). Both are preserved and no between-arm change is reconstructed. This non-Parkinson trial used an isolated constituent and supplied no relevant fatigue, insomnia severity or fall outcome. A directional effect of ordinary saffron in Parkinson mood does not follow.

Round 1: 0063 depression, indirect active-comparator evidence. The ethics approval in the report is not a trial registration.
