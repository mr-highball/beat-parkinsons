Marjoram oil and neurofeedback in bruxism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6406666/
PMCID: PMC6406666
DOI: 10.3390/jcm8020158
Coverage: Original full text, Methods, outcome definitions and Figure 5 contrast

Merino and colleagues combined marjoram aroma from nasal filters with 21 neurofeedback sessions in a dental-clinic bruxism study. The exposed and unexposed trained bruxism groups did not clearly differ on postintervention perceived stress (p>.05), although the exposed trained grade differed from untrained participants. Original results describe early salivary myeloperoxidase changes and an inconclusive global divergence test. The paper proposes cognitive effects in its discussion without administering a cognitive attention test. Overlapping authors and setting link the report to the 2016 neurofeedback study, while the reported populations and session counts differ. Neither report tests oral fresh marjoram.
