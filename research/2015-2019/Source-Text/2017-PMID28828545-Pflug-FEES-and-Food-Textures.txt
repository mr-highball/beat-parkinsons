Title: Critical dysphagia is common in Parkinson disease and occurs even in early stages
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00455-017-9831-1
PMID: 28828545
DOI: 10.1007/s00455-017-9831-1
Publication date: 2017-08-21
Date basis: Publisher online-first date. Dysphagia 33:41–50 appeared in 2018.
Source depth: Original author abstract. Full original outcome tables remain open.

Pflug and colleagues compared 119 consecutive Parkinson patients with 32 controls using a flexible endoscopic swallowing assessment. Test items included water, biscuit and bread. The abstract reports aspiration in 30/119 Parkinson patients, all associated with water, and swallowing residue in 111/119 across consistencies, most frequently with bread. The materials and findings characterize swallowing, with no change in a graded drooling outcome after a named food.

The Hamburg authors and sample sizes overlap those in the 2017 Nienstedt study. The later Hamburg study of aspiration predictors also has 119 Parkinson patients. Exact participant overlap remains open. Preserve the three as likely related reports for inference.
