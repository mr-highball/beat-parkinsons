Title: Curcumin crossover and soreness after elbow exercise
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4536282/
PMID: 25921600
PMCID: PMC4536282
DOI: 10.1007/s00421-015-3170-4
Publication date: 2015-04-29
Date basis: Electronic publication in the original.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full structured original, including Figure 5 and outcomes text.

Fourteen untrained men completed a randomized crossover of branded curcumin and starch placebo around eccentric exercise. Peak palpation soreness was 5.5 versus 6.0 cm on the visual analogue scale, with no clear group-by-time interaction. Elbow-extension soreness was also inconclusive. Separate strength or laboratory measures do not establish lower pain.

This comparison concerns a concentrated formulation and induced exercise soreness. It gives no ground-turmeric or Parkinson pain result.
