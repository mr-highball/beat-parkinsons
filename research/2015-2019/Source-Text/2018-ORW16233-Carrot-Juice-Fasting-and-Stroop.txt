Title: The sustained effect of two prolonged three day intensive fasting regimes, water and carrot juice plus water, on cognition
Source: https://library.wso.at/s/orw/item/380
Repository identifier: 16233
Publication date: 2018-11-12
Date basis: The repository displays an acceptance year of 2017 and a November 2018 deposit field. An earlier public edition was not verified.
Source depth: Institutional author abstract for a 31-page osteopathy thesis. The linked public record does not expose the thesis or numerical tables.

The abstract describes 15 people and three groups: no fasting, fasting with water, or fasting with water and carrot juice. Reported Stroop assessments span baseline, the last day of fasting, and two follow-up points. It reports no main group signal across the task variants and describes time-related changes in both fasting arms. The Cognitive Failure Questionnaire was not analyzed. Arm counts, pairwise Stroop contrasts, and a separate carrot-juice effect are unavailable.

The study concerns fasting and liquid intake. It contains no roasted carrot, verified Parkinson population, or dose-response estimate for executive impairment.
