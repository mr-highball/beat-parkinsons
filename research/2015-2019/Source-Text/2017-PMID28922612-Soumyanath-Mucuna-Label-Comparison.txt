Title: Analysis of levodopa content in commercial Mucuna pruriens products using high-performance liquid chromatography with fluorescence detection

PMID: 28922612
PMCID: PMC5808387
DOI: 10.1089/acm.2017.0054
Publication date: 2017-09-18
Date basis: Online-first date. The print issue followed in February 2018.
Source depth: Original body, Table 1 and historical citation.

The authors measured six commercial products. Table 1 shows contents from 6% to 141% of each product's label claim, with none within the cited 90%–110% band. The earlier P01.36 poster from the same group reported two near their labels and four below, so keep both descriptions and avoid treating them as independent without product-level resolution. The 2022 Cohen series uses a different sampling frame and a different comparator for part of its analysis.

The article cites Singhal and colleagues for traditional accounts of Kampavata. It lists drooling among reported manifestations of the syndrome. The new quantitative observations are chemical measurements of marketed Mucuna products. They supply neither a culinary fig exposure nor a Parkinson drooling outcome.
