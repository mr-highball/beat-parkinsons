Boiled black common beans in the Clark dissertation
Source: https://mspace.lib.umanitoba.ca/handle/1993/34298
Registration: NCT02342340
Coverage: Original institutional dissertation pp. 71–73 and public registry

The University of Manitoba dates Jaime Clark's dissertation to 2019. It describes the registered crossover in which eight healthy adults ate black, navy, pinto and red kidney common beans and white rice on separate visits. The bean portions were soaked and boiled in broth, then frozen and reheated. This documents consumption of boiled black common beans. The post-meal protocol measures vascular and metabolic responses and does not include daytime sleepiness. The later journal article reports the same registered study.
