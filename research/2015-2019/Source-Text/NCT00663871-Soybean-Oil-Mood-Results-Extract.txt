Primary report of NCT00663871, https://doi.org/10.1097/PSY.0000000000000453, 2017. Primary formulation substudy https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3988126/. Posted results https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00663871?tab=results. Coverage: original factual abstract, formulation and outcomes extract.

The comparator received 2 g/day soybean oil with mint flavor and vitamin E. Participants were 272 generally healthy adults with low intake of marine fats. The final behavioral report found no clear intervention-time interactions on mood and impulsivity from questionnaires, momentary ratings or fMRI tasks. The registered endpoints and posted results measure negative affect and impulsivity rather than a separate apathy assessment. No Parkinson subgroup or processing grade was described.
