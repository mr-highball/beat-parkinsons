Coverage: factual summary of 2019 author abstract and 2015 protocol Table 2.

The nurse-led Parkinson care program increased recorded guideline adherence against usual care, with reported proportions 0.77 versus 0.58 and mean difference 0.19 (95% CI 0.16–0.22). Among eight secondary measures the reported clear contrast was for the PHQ-2 depression screen, which is different from apathy. Table 2 of the prior protocol includes pain and an adapted MDS-UPDRS apathy assessment in its care-planning workflow. A separate dried date effect is outside the arms and outcomes. The 2019 numerical abstract cannot establish what might appear in clinical subitem data.
