In a five-week oral rat comparison, the authors reported inconsistent or absent anxiety-like behavior effects across the oils studied. The olive-oil arm showed an exploratory-activity signal only at one dose. Open-field activity and elevated-plus-maze behavior are separate outcomes. The abstract does not specify pressing grade or quantify a separately reportable anxiety comparison. The animal experiment narrows the transfer question for Round 1 claim 0343.
