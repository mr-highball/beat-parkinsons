Title: Effects of oral date syrup on labor pain severity and the active phase of labor in nulliparous women

A total of 64 first-time mothers are reported, 32 per arm. The exposure was filtered date-fruit syrup versus access to plain water. Table 2 shows date-syrup versus control mean VAS scores of 7.62 versus 8.25 at 30 minutes (p=.124), and 7.59 versus 9.12 at 60 minutes (p=.001). Later rows have fewer recorded test degrees of freedom (39 at 120 minutes and 19 at 150 minutes, versus 62 earlier). The printed 90-minute control SD of 3.06 is unusually large for the reported mean of 9.35 on a 0–10 scale. The report's active-labor duration result and unadjusted later VAS comparisons require separate interpretation.

The 80-person Taavoni report, this 64-person report and the 80-person honey report cite the same registration. The English date and honey papers reproduce five of six control VAS means and SDs. The 2024 synthesis treats the date reports as overlapping. Consolidate them in one registration family. Actual participant-level overlap and reasons for distinct denominators are unreported.
