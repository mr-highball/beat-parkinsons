Nada M. Mostafa. Records of Natural Products 12(5), 480–492, 2018. DOI 10.25135/rnp.47.17.10.062. The journal issue gives September–October 2018.

The study tested Bombax ceiba leaf extract in rats. Figure 1 and section 3.1 describe four groups with six animals each and use Morris water maze and passive avoidance, which measure spatial and learned avoidance memory. The oral leaf extract was administered before and alongside the scopolamine period. This 2018 report does not include a pepper intervention or a Parkinson cohort.

Compared with the control group, scopolamine was reported to lower time in the target quadrant by 84%, platform crossings by 45.45% and avoidance latency by 91.14%. Compared with scopolamine, the treated Bombax group had 305.85% more time in the target quadrant. Compared with control, donepezil and Bombax group avoidance latencies were lower by 10.34% and 13.76%, respectively. These are reported relative changes, with no individual values in the figure. The later pepper-oil article cites this paper in four figure captions and prints the same comparator percentages. Shared comparator provenance remains unresolved.
