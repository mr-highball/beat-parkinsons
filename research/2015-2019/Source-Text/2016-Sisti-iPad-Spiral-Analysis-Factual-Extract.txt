Methods §§2.1–2.2 identify Apple Inc. as the company providing Xcode and the iPad. Participants drew with a finger on the iPad and with a stylus on the Wacom tablet. Neither assignment is dietary.
Results §§3.0–3.2 and Table 1 compare nine digital spiral indices across both devices from 31 participants. Parkinson disease and brachial dystonia identify separate reported subgroups. Degree of Severity is a composite of spiral execution and irregularity, without a dietary comparison or a baked apple dystonia measure. The paper's overall count is used without reinterpreting the individual diagnostic labels, which do not sum to the total movement-disorder group.
