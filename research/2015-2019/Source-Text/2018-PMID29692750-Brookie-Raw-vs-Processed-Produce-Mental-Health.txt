Title: Intake of Raw Fruits and Vegetables Is Associated With Better Mental Health Than Intake of Processed Fruits and Vegetables.
PMID: 29692750
PMCID: PMC5902672
DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00487
First published: 2018
Authors: Brookie KL, Best GI, Conner TS
Journal: Front Psychol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29692750/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Cross-sectional survey of 422 young adults (18-25 y, NZ/US) comparing raw vs cooked/canned/processed individual fruits and vegetables on CES-D depressive symptoms, HADS anxiety, negative mood, and positive mood. Controlling for covariates, raw carrots were inversely associated with depressive symptoms (r=-0.130, p<0.01) and negative mood (r=-0.108, p<0.05) and positively with positive mood (r=0.248, p<0.01), while anxiety was null (r=-0.085, NS). Critically, cooked/processed carrots were completely null for depressive symptoms (r=+0.017, NS), anxiety (r=+0.057, NS), and negative mood (r=+0.066, NS). Raw celery was null for depressive symptoms (r=-0.060, NS) and anxiety (r=+0.008, NS) with positive mood r=0.137 (p<0.01). Cooked/processed cauliflower was null for depressive symptoms (r=-0.035, NS). Non-PD young-adult cross-sectional survey.
