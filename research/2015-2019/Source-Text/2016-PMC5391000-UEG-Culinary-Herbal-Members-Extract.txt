Title: UEG Week 2016 poster presentations, culinary and herbal member abstracts
PMCID: PMC5391000
DOI: 10.1177/2050640616663689
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5391000/
First verified online: 2016-10-16, Crossref; print metadata October 2016.
Coverage: Factual extracts of individual original member abstracts from the complete 564-page collection.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

P0422 and P1638 describe 48 chronic-gastritis patients receiving purified oat beta-glucan or potato-starch solution for about four weeks. Gastric histology comparisons were inconclusive in P0422; P1638 reports selected blood markers. Matching design suggests a shared trial, without verified registry identity. Whole-oat or potato-dish effects on sleep or falls were not tested.

P1005 reports observational reflux associations in 248 cases and 273 controls, including an adjusted olive-oil odds ratio of .03 (95% CI .01–.20) and no reported association for daily coffee. Preparation, reverse causality and prior publication overlap remain unresolved; no constipation, anxiety, insomnia or fatigue effect is measured.

P1067 tests cranberry capsules with standard therapy, P0999 a six-food elimination including egg, P1493 an isolated spearmint constituent in laboratory cancer work, and P1356 a multi-extract product including Melissa in rat colitis. These preparations and endpoints do not isolate the frozen culinary Parkinson claims. Member abstracts are not full trial reports.
