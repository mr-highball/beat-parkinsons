Title: Analgesic, anti-inflammatory and diuretic activities of Cicer arietinum L.
PMID: 29618447
First published: 2018
Authors: Masroor D, Baig SG, Ahmed S, Ahmad SM, Hasan MM
Journal: Pak J Pharm Sci
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29618447/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rodent pharmacology screening (Masroor et al., 2018, Pak J Pharm Sci) reporting analgesic (acetic-acid writhing and hot-plate), anti-inflammatory, and diuretic activity of crude methanol seed extracts of Cicer arietinum in mice. Critical limits: concentrated solvent extract in acute rodent nociception/diuresis assays, not cooked or canned whole chickpeas in Parkinson disease.
