Preparation and endpoint scope in the kiwi breakfast article
https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/9/11/1195

October 30, 2017. Mishra, Edwards and colleagues report the four-period wheat-cereal breakfast crossover in 20 adults. Section 2.1 describes peeled green Hayward kiwifruit stored frozen, partly thawed to crush it to pulp, and frozen again in portions. The human meals used 200 g pulp in two conditions. The journal article gives glucose and appetite results, while the Edwards thesis reports the cognitive battery. The published meal and registered timing corroborate one study family. Reported fruit processing and absence of Parkinson participants limit transfer to the hypothesis about raw kiwi.
