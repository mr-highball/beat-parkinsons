Title: Cochrane poststroke urinary incontinence review and the ginger–salt moxibustion member
PMID: 30706461
PMCID: PMC6355973
DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD004462.pub4
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6355973/
Published: 2019-02-01
Archive scope: Evidence-focused summary of the inspected original article body.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The review describes a 62-person Liu 2006 comparison of ginger–salt moxibustion plus acupuncture with acupuncture alone. The measured endpoint was daytime voiding frequency after stroke. The review reports a mean difference of −5.57 voids (95% CI −7.00 to −4.14). The topical intervention combines materials and heat with a procedural program. It supplies no oral grated ginger contrast, bowel constipation measure or muscle rigidity outcome. Pain mentioned elsewhere in the review belongs to other procedures or adverse events. The original Chinese report remains a separate citation trail if its methods or scope are needed.
