Title: The effect of olfactory stimuli on the balance ability of stroke patients.
PMID: 25642050
PMCID: PMC4305536
DOI: 10.1589/jpts.27.109
First published: 2015
Authors: Gim MN, Lee SB, Yoo KT, Bae JY, Kim MK, Choi JH
Journal: Journal of physical therapy science
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25642050/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-group randomized controlled trial (PMC4305536) in n=33 male chronic stroke patients (n=11 black pepper oil [BPO], n=11 lavender oil, n=11 distilled water sham) undergoing force-platform Romberg balance tests (eyes open 1 min, eyes closed 1 min). With eyes open, BPO olfactory stimulation significantly reduced mediolateral CoP displacement Xmean (22.8+/-16.3 to 16.2+/-11.9 mm, p<0.05) and shifted Ymean (-15.7+/-12.8 to -25.5+/-12.7 mm, p<0.05), while distilled water had no significant effect; eyes-closed between-group changes were NS. Limits: inhaled essential oil, acute session, n=11/arm, post-stroke non-PD cohort.
