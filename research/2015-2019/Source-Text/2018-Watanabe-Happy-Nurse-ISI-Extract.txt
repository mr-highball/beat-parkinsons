Watanabe N and colleagues. Happy Nurse Project, J Psychiatr Res 102:72–80, online April 3, 2018. PMID 29627596, DOI 10.1016/j.jpsychires.2018.03.015, NCT02151162.
Original results pp. 73 and 76–77 embedded in the Japanese labor ministry report. Eighty nurses were randomized to fish oil or blended oral comparator containing 47% rapeseed, 25% soybean, 25% olive and 3% fish oils. Table 2 mean ISI scores at Week 13 were 5.07 (fish oil) and 6.62 (blend); group-by-time difference +2.05 (95% CI 0.34–3.76, p=.019). Weeks 26 and 52 were inconclusive (both coefficients −0.24, p=.780 and p=.786). These were among multiple secondary comparisons. The original does not identify pressing grade or assign an isolated rapeseed arm. No Parkinson cohort or dream-enactment count.
