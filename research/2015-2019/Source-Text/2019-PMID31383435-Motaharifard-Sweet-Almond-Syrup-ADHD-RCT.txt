Title: Effect of sweet almond syrup versus methylphenidate in children with ADHD: A randomized triple-blind clinical trial.
PMID: 31383435
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctcp.2019.07.008
First published: 2019
Authors: Motaharifard, Effatpanah, Karimi, Akhondzadeh, Rahimi
Journal: Complementary therapies in clinical practice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31383435/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

8-week randomized triple-blind active-comparator trial in 50 children aged 6-14 y with ADHD assigned to Persian Medicine sweet almond syrup or methylphenidate. Parent and Teacher ADHD Rating Scale scores decreased similarly in both groups with no statistically significant between-group difference (F = 2.3, p = 0.13; F = 0.57, p = 0.47). Limits: lacks placebo control, pediatric ADHD population, processed sweet almond syrup rather than raw whole almonds.
