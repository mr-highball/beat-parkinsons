Title: Pooled Analyses of Phase III Studies of ADS-5102 (Amantadine) Extended-Release Capsules for Dyskinesia in Parkinson's Disease.
PMID: 29532440
PMCID: PMC5934466
DOI: 10.1007/s40263-018-0498-4
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5934466/
First publication date: 2018-04-01 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS and original clinical or laboratory methods.

In pooled Parkinson dyskinesia trials, 18 participants assigned extended-release amantadine and three assigned placebo reported visual hallucinations. Nine of the 18 amantadine reports began within the first 30 days. These are people with drug adverse events and time to first occurrence, without episode frequency per person or a fig exposure.
