Title: 2016 Nigella oil and haloperidol-related oral dyskinesia
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27752803/


Malik et al., PMID 27752803, reported that Nigella sativa seed oil 0.2 mL per rat with haloperidol 1 mg/kg reduced extrapyramidal-like behavior and oral dyskinesia in rats. The original abstract is accessible; full methods remain unverified. Nigella sativa, often called black cumin, is a different species from Cuminum cyminum; extracted oil, antipsychotic-induced movement and rats differ from toasted cumin and levodopa dyskinesia in people with Parkinson disease. A 2018 ASENT abstract (PMID 29987760, PMC6095775), titled The Key Roles of Nigella Sativa-Oil in Regulating Microglia and Mitochondrial Activity in Parkinsonism, has overlapping authors, identical drug and oil doses, and matching behavioral outcome; treat as potentially overlapping, not independent replication.
