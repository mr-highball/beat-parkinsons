Title: Investigation of thymol effect on learning and memory impairment induced by intrahippocampal injection of amyloid beta peptide in high-fat diet-fed rats
Publication date: 2017-03-02
PMID: 28255862
DOI: 10.1007/s11011-017-9960-0
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28255862/
Text coverage: Primary indexed abstract.

One of five randomized arms (eight rats per arm) received sunflower oil by mouth after the same high-fat diet and amyloid-beta model used for the untreated model group. Another model arm received thymol in sunflower oil. The abstract states a favorable spatial-memory result for the thymol comparison, and does not give the separate oil-versus-untreated-model estimate. Full group tables and handling balance remain open. Oil grade and relevance to Parkinson executive impairment are unverified.
