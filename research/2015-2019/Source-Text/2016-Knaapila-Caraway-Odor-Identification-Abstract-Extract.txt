Title: Reported spice use and caraway in an odor identification panel
Source: https://researchportal.helsinki.fi/en/publications/pleasantness-familiarity-and-identification-of-spice-odors-are-in/
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

Knaapila and colleagues, first online in November 2016 and published in the February 2017 Appetite issue, 109:190–200. One hundred twenty-six adults aged 25 to 61 ranked 12 odors including caraway for familiarity and pleasantness, and completed odor identification. A questionnaire measured reported spice use. The original abstract describes positive associations across spice exposure and odor familiarity, identification and pleasantness. It gives no separate numerical caraway-use effect or toasted preparation. The institutional manuscript route returned an access error; the individual item table remains pending. There was no assigned exposure or Parkinson analysis.
