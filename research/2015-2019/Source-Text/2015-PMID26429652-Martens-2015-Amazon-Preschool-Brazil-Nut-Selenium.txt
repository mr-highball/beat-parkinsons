Title: Selenium status in preschool children receiving a Brazil nut-enriched diet.
PMID: 26429652
DOI: 10.1016/j.nut.2015.05.005
First published: 2015
Authors: Martens IB, Cardoso BR, Hare DJ, Niedzwiecki MM, Lajolo FM, Martens A, et al.
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26429652/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Observational comparison of n=41 preschool children in Macapa receiving 15-30 g Brazil nuts 3 d/wk in school meals vs n=88 controls in Belem: Macapa selenium intake averaged 155.3 ug/d (potentially toxic on Brazil nut days) with elevated biomarkers above reference ranges, though no clinical selenosis signs were observed; authors recommended monitoring.
