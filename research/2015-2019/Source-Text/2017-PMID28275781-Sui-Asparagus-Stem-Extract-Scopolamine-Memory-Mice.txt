Title: Aqueous extracts from asparagus stems prevent memory impairments in scopolamine-treated mice.
PMID: 28275781
DOI: 10.1039/c7fo00028f
First published: 2017
Authors: Sui Z, Qi C, Huang Y, Ma S, Wang X, Le G, et al.
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28275781/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse amnesia study (Sui et al., 2017, Food Funct) dividing 60 ICR mice into 6 groups (n=10/group) to test oral aqueous Asparagus officinalis stem extract (AEAS 1.6, 8, and 16 mL/kg) vs scopolamine control and donepezil. AEAS 8 mL/kg significantly reversed scopolamine-induced deficits in novel object recognition, Y-maze, and Morris water maze (all p<0.05), inhibited hippocampal AChE, and restored acetylcholine, BDNF, and CREB mRNA. Limits: acute cholinergic amnesia mouse model using concentrated stem extract; measures spatial/recognition memory rather than Parkinson's executive function or attention.
