Title: A Scale to Assess Activities of Daily Living in Pantothenate Kinase-Associated Neurodegeneration

Source depth: Original structured body, item-development methods and Table 2

Design: Instrument development and validation in a PKAN sample

The researchers adapted a Parkinson activities-of-daily-living instrument for a distinct condition. The scale covers salivation and drooling separately from chewing or swallowing and eating tasks. The validation involved 37 caregivers and two patients contributing directly. The salivation and drooling item had a 6.8/10 mean relevance score. Table 2 records 38 of 39 understanding the item as intended. This is a report about item performance and symptom ascertainment in PKAN. It supplies no Parkinson food comparison or graded change after raw fig.
