Corn oil capsules in adolescent major depression
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6625364/
PMID: 29985566
PMCID: PMC6625364
DOI: 10.4088/JCP.17m11596
Registration: NCT00962598
Coverage: Full original June 26, 2018, Methods and Table 2; official imaging-flow record

Gabbay and colleagues report 51 adolescents randomized to omega-3 monotherapy or matching corn-oil capsules. Table 2 gives 27 corn and 21 omega cases and an adjusted Children's Depression Rating Scale–Revised difference of 1.81 (95% CI −3.31 to 6.94, p=.49). The adjusted Beck difference was 0.79 (−4.27 to 5.84, p=.76). The linked registry reports 16 in an imaging scope recruited from GCO 12-1321. Those 16 should not replace the published clinical denominators or be counted as another independent trial. There was no oil-free control and no reported refining grade or Parkinson cohort.
