The Italian poison-center abstract includes one intentional
nutmeg exposure in an adolescent. It records emesis, abdominal
pain and headache about six hours later. The consultation was
approximately 14 hours after exposure. It does not specify a
neurological examination, a tremor assessment or Parkinson
disease, and provides no ordinary culinary comparison.

This is a separate case family from the 2019 Beckerman and Persaud
report. The DOI identifies the whole conference collection.
