Original study scope
The review separates Mediterranean dietary patterns, spice and constituent mechanisms, and Ayurvedic massage with herbalized sesame oil. It cites topical sesame oil massage comparisons after acute limb trauma. Narrative diet and inflammatory findings have no common Parkinson symptom cohort. Date matches refer to time, and the inspected material supplies no tested sage or bell-pepper exposure.

Continuing evidence gaps
Follow the two original injury trials at PMID 28619303 and PMID 26161326. The review does not isolate swallowed toasted or cold-pressed sesame oil, or measure attributable Parkinson pain, fatigue, anxiety or depression. Preserve kitchen-spice preparation and endpoint gaps.
