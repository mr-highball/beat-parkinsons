Admane and Grover compare two isolated green-tea polyphenols in laboratory prion-protein aggregation models and report differing activity. The abstract uses GTP1 and GTP2 without naming their chemical identities. Neither can be silently equated with a particular catechin.

This is a derived-constituent lead. It does not evaluate brewed tea, clinical fatigue or patients with Parkinson disease. Collection completeness supports the recorded source-context decision, not proof of a favorable or unfavorable clinical effect.
