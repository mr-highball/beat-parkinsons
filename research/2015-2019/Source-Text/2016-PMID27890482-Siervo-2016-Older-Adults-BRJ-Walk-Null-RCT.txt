Title: Dietary nitrate does not affect physical activity or outcomes in healthy older adults in a randomized, cross-over trial.
PMID: 27890482
DOI: 10.1016/j.nutres.2016.11.004
First published: 2016
Authors: Siervo M, Oggioni C, Jakovljevic DG, Trenell M, Mathers JC, Houghton D, et al.
Journal: Nutrition research (New York, N.Y.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27890482/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=19 healthy older adults (60-75 y) testing 7 days of nitrate-rich vs nitrate-depleted beetroot juice: no significant effect on 10-m walk time, grip strength, or cognitive performance despite increased plasma nitrate/nitrite. Competing null older-adult walking trial.
