Title: Phloretin attenuates behavior deficits and neuroinflammatory response in MPTP induced Parkinson's disease in mice.
PMID: 31251998
DOI: 10.1016/j.lfs.2019.116600
First published: 2019
Authors: Zhang G, Yang G, Liu J
Journal: Life sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31251998/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

MPTP-induced Parkinson disease mouse study evaluating oral phloretin (apple dihydrochalcone). Phloretin attenuated MPTP-induced motor behavioral abnormalities, restored striatal dopamine levels and tyrosine hydroxylase protein expression, and suppressed GFAP, Iba-1, iNOS, COX-2, IL-1beta, IL-6, and TNF-alpha neuroinflammation. Limits: isolated constituent in MPTP mice; abstract omits sample size and exact motor test p-values.
