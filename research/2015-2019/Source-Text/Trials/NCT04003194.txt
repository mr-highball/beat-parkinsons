NCT04003194 — Pinto Beans and Glycemic Control in Adults With Type 2 Diabetes
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04003194
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Coverage: Selected official registry fields, summarized as plain text.

First public registration and last public update: 2019-07-01.
Status: Completed. Results posted to ClinicalTrials.gov: No.
Sponsor: University of Colorado, Colorado Springs. Actual enrollment: 13.
Design: Open-label randomized crossover in adults with type 2 diabetes. Two 12-week phases separated by a four-week washout.
Interventions: Half a cup of pinto beans or green beans daily.
Registered measures: Fasting glucose, postprandial glucose and hemoglobin A1c.
The associated 2020 article DOI 10.23880/fsnt-16000211 reports gastrointestinal questionnaires, eight completers and canned preparations. No Parkinson outcome.
