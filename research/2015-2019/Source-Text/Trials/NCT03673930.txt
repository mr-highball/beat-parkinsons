Primary measure: Chronic secondary memory composite, registered as measured at 57 days.
Related secondary measures: Attention speed and accuracy composites and subjective ratings of mental fatigue from the cognitive demand battery.
Overall status: Completed. Results status: No results posted in the registry. The published results identify this trial and describe a final sample of 82 and outcome assessment on day 56. See ../PMC6950039.txt.
