Title: A randomized, non-eating-controlled crossover study to investigate the relief effect of chicken meat on muscle fatigue
Registry ID: UMIN000033042
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000037663
Registry history: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_his_list.cgi?recptno=R000037663
First published: 2018-06-19, official first disclosure. The Japanese registry history records initial entry the preceding evening and a status and analysis update in April 2020.
Coverage: Official registry factual snapshot.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

The University of Miyazaki study planned 10 healthy adults aged 20–39 in an open randomized crossover comparing 100 g of salad chicken before exercise with no test food. Change in serum creatine phosphokinase was primary. Secondary fields include a visual analogue scale, exercise duration and adverse events. The registry does not specify the VAS construct or cooking process.

The study status shows completed and publication of results shows unpublished. The actual enrollment, numerical outcomes and publication URL remain blank. The institutional study list confirms the project. Searches with its official identifiers, author and Japanese or English title did not recover a matched results paper. This registry gives no poached-chicken comparison in Parkinson disease. A differently registered 2024 study used verified chicken meat in older women and cannot supply these missing results.
