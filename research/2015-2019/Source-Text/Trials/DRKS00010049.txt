Title: NutriAct long-term dietary-pattern trial registration
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00010049
Registry ID: DRKS00010049
First public: 2016-05-12
Last registry update: 2022-05-27
Coverage: Distilled fields from the German Clinical Trials Register and the linked protocol.

This prospective multicenter parallel study assigned adults ages 50–80 with an unhealthy aging risk factor to a cardiometabolic diet pattern or general dietary counseling for 36 months. The intervention emphasized unsaturated fat, protein and dietary fiber. Gait-affecting conditions such as Parkinson disease were eligibility exclusions. The registry lists a final sample of 502 and completion on 2021-12-31.

GAD-7 was planned alongside PHQ-9 and quality-of-life instruments. The protocol (PMID 32991458) documents barley and barley flakes among supplies offered to usual care. No separate boiled barley randomization or reported GAD-7 result was verified. The 12-month publication (PMID 34656026) concerns adherence and diet composition within the same trial family.
