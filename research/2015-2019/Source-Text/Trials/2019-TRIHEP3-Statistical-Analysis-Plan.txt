The study compares triheptanoin and a control oil in Huntington disease. Its prespecified secondary assessments include change in the overall Problem Behaviors Assessment short-form score from baseline to months 6 and 12. The analysis plan uses baseline-dependent analysis of covariance with age, sex and BMI adjustment. It describes secondary tests as exploratory and does not impute missing secondary endpoint data.

The plan contains no comparative outcome estimate or separately specified apathy-item analysis. The journal paper identifies safflower oil as the control in the blinded comparison, with pressing method unspecified. Its overall behavioral comparison data were not shown, and the public registry results do not supply an apathy contrast.
