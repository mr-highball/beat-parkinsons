Title: ODYSSEI Japan Registry of Clinical Trials registration and reported outcomes
Source: https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/en-latest-detail/jRCTs071180014
Registry ID: jRCTs071180014
Publication date: 2019-01-29
Coverage: Factual extract from the official indexed English and Japanese jRCT results and the official NIPH trial portal, checked 2026-11-19. Direct English detail retrieval returned HTTP 403.

The registry displays 2019-01-29 as the jRCT registration date, 2022-04-26 as completion and 2024-07-01 as the results posting date. The earlier UMIN registration for the same research program first appeared on 2016-10-25.

The result fields distinguish 214 assigned, 212 included in safety analyses and 210 included in efficacy analyses. In the efficacy analysis, dyskinesia arose in 39/103 with adjunctive istradefylline and 44/107 with adjusted medical therapy without it. The registry reports inconclusive median time-to-onset comparisons, with medians of 1,100 versus 1,082 days. The direction of the age subgroup estimates differed by stratum. The result concerns dyskinesia onset during medication treatment. It does not estimate clinical dyskinesia severity during a ginger comparison.

The jRCT results include longer follow-up than the historic UMIN status field alone conveys. The UMIN record, jRCT results and article represent one family for evidence counting.
