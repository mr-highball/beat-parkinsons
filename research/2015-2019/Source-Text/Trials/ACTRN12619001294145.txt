Title: VEgetableS for vaScular hEaLth: The VESSEL Study
Registry identifier: ACTRN12619001294145
Source: https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=378003
Format: Selected official ANZCTR fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public posting: 2019-09-19 (prospective)
Last posted update: 2023-10-01
Design: Short crossover periods with cruciferous vegetables versus root and squash vegetables in middle-aged and older adults with mildly elevated blood pressure.
Outcomes: Ambulatory blood pressure and vascular and metabolic measures. The registry does not identify a Parkinson cohort, isolated red cabbage or standing-lightheadedness endpoint.
Linked results: Connolly et al. PMID 39218859 is the pressure report. PMID 40375391 reports additional glycaemic results in the same trial.
