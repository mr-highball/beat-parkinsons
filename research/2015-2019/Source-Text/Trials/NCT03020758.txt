Title: Effect of Dried Fruit on Cardiometabolic Risk Factors
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03020758
Source alias: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03020758
NCT ID: NCT03020758
Publication date: 2017-01-13
Date basis: First public posting as recorded by ClinicalTrials.gov. Last public update 2023-08-18.
Text coverage: Selected registry design, outcomes and result status from the official API. Factual summary.

The completed randomized crossover study enrolled 55 adults at increased cardiometabolic risk. The two four-week conditions tested a blend of dried plums, figs, dates and raisins and an energy- and nutrient-matched snack. Serum LDL cholesterol at four weeks was the registered primary outcome. The registry lists investigator masking and shows no posted results. The matching outcome paper, PMID 32513313, explicitly identifies this registration and is the same trial family.
