Status

Active, not recruiting. No posted registry results.

Study and outcomes

Healthy adults. Daily cookies provide 10 g of grapeseed oil, and randomized comparison cookies provide 10 g of high-oleic safflower oil. The secondary outcomes include correlations of linoleic acid with grip strength, gait speed, and a sleep questionnaire. The related publication PMID 35596730 reports biochemical results.
