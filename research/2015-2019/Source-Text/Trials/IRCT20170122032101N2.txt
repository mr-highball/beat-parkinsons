Title: Lemon balm tea and chamomile tea after lower-limb orthopedic surgery
Registry ID: IRCT20170122032101N2
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/28464
WHO bridge: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT20170122032101N2
First registered: 2018-01-16, while recruiting. The original registry lists the same date for its last update.
Text coverage: Official Iranian registry and WHO record inspected 2026-11-18.

The trial planned 96 participants and assigned separate lemon balm tea, chamomile tea and black tea arms using block randomization. Registered outcomes are visual analogue pain intensity, Spielberger state anxiety and satisfaction with pain management. Each is scheduled before and 30 minutes after tea administration. Depression severity and Parkinson disease are outside these registered outcomes and population.

The registry still describes recruitment and does not supply numerical results. The linked 2020 pain paper and 2023 anxiety paper report 96 participants, the same site, arms, ethics approval and trial identifier. Treat these as two outcome reports from one registered trial. Verify outcomes against the article tables.
