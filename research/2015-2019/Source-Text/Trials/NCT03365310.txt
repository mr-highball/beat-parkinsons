Title: Management of frailty in older adults with turmeric and tulsi supplements
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03365310
Registry ID: NCT03365310
Publication date: 2017-12-07
Coverage: Selected fields from the official ClinicalTrials.gov record, verified 2026-11-19.

OI-007-2017 is an open-label randomized frailty plan with 50 estimated participants. It compares a polyherbal combination containing holy basil, turmeric and ginger with standard care. It lists three-month frequency of falls as secondary to the frailty-score primary outcome. The eligibility field excludes progressive degenerative neurologic disease, including parkinsonism.

The latest posted update is 2018-04-11. Overall status remains Unknown and the registry has no posted results. The record concerns a mixed treatment in community frailty. The planned contrast leaves the culinary sweet-basil preparation and Parkinson fall frequency open.
