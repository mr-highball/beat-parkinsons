Title: NCT00938340 — posted walnut-fraction crossover results
Registry ID: NCT00938340
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00938340
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00938340
First results posted: 2018-10-29; current record updated 2023-08-21.
Coverage: Factual current official results tables; this is the same trial as the 2009 registration and 2013 report.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Twenty participants were randomized and 15 completed. Oil was received by 15, whole walnuts by 16, skins by 15 and nutmeat by 19; endpoint denominators differ.

For four-hour reactive hyperemia index, least-squares mean changes (SE) are oil +.07 (.09), skins −.26 (.09), whole walnut −.19 (.09), and nutmeat −.09 (.09), with endpoint n=14,15,15,14 respectively. Mixed-model omnibus p=.012. Pairwise oil-versus-skin p=.002, oil-versus-whole p=.011 and oil-versus-nutmeat p=.12.

The registry specifies p<.0083 for six Bonferroni comparisons: the skin comparison passes, the whole-walnut comparison does not. The earlier paper also reports Framingham RHI, a separate calculation; do not merge metric-specific results. Neither vascular indices nor serum/cell experiments establish a Parkinson symptom benefit or isolated cold-pressed-oil effect.

The nonsystematic adverse-event module, threshold zero, covers 0–360 minutes. Diarrhea is reported in 9/15 oil recipients versus 0/15 in each component condition; serious events are 0/15 in each. The oil condition supplied 51 g of solvent-extracted oil. No separate pain-coded event appears; this is not proof that pain was absent, a pain-effect estimate, or a rate applicable to usual consumption.
