Protocol
Phase 2/3 randomized parallel double-blind trial comparing encapsulated RT001, a synthetic deuterated linoleic acid ester, with encapsulated USP safflower oil in people with Friedreich ataxia. In both arms, participants took nine capsules daily for the first month and six daily thereafter. The listed main endpoint was change in maximal oxygen consumption from baseline to month 11. Registry results also report the timed one-minute walk.
