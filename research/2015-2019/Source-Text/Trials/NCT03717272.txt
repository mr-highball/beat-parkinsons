Title: Corn oil capsule placebo and sustained attention
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03717272
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

NCT03717272. First posted October 24, 2018. The official study snapshot lists completion. AEF0117 was compared with a corn oil capsule placebo in adults with cannabis use disorder. Sustained attention and sleep are among the secondary measures. Corn oil refining is unspecified and no oil-free group was assigned.
