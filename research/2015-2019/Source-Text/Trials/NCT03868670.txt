Title: Responsive neurostimulation for loss-of-control eating
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03868670
Registry ID: NCT03868670
First public: 2019-03-11
Coverage: Distilled original registry fields. Status checked 2026-11-19.

The feasibility device registration plans responsive neurostimulation for treatment-refractory obesity and loss-of-control eating. Named primary measures concern adverse events and change in frequency of loss-of-control episodes. The latest record lists six enrollees and no posted tables. The associated case report PMID 41249493 investigates surges of food preoccupation during tirzepatide treatment and makes no dietary date assignment.
