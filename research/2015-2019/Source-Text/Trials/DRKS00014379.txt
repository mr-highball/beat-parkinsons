Title: Chronic sleep loss: Does habitual coffee consumption ensure optimal performance?
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00014379
Trial: DRKS00014379
Publication date: 2018-04-04
Date basis: Initial registry registration date.
Review depth: Public registry design, recruitment and publication entries checked 2026-11-25.

The registry describes a completed, randomized, parallel comparison of caffeinated and decaffeinated coffee in healthy adults during a defined sleep-restriction and recovery protocol. Primary and secondary planned outcomes concern adenosine-receptor availability. It lists final enrollment of 36 versus the original target of 40. The exclusion criteria cover chronic neurological illness.

The registered record links the behavioral report and a later receptor-imaging abstract. Its results-table section supplies no effect estimate. Use the journal records for outcomes and keep reports from the same trial under one study family.
