2015 — Registered oral tarragon capsules in glucose intolerance

Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02330341
Identifier: NCT02330341

The registration first posted January 1, 2015. It specifies a randomized trial of Artemisia dracunculus capsules versus a calcined-magnesia placebo in 24 adults with glucose intolerance. It lists metabolic tests and blood pressure. The registered measures do not define standing pressure change, orthostatic symptoms, gait, speech or facial expressiveness. The original 2016 article represents the same participant group.
