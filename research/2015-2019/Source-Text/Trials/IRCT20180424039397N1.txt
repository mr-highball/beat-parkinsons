IRCT20180424039397N1 — Intranasal Cucurbita pepo product in a chronic-insomnia trial

Official registration: https://irct.ir/trial/31300
First public registration: September 19, 2018
Source coverage: Selected official public registry fields, paraphrased.

The registry planned a randomized, double-blinded comparison of an intranasal Cucurbita pepo preparation and liquid paraffin in adults with insomnia. The planned sample was 74, and the study assessed insomnia severity and sleep quality. Neurological diseases including Parkinson disease were exclusion criteria. The registration calls the investigational product pumpkin oil. The 2019 Methods describe peeled whole pumpkin processed in a sesame oil base. Neither record identifies toasted edible pumpkin seed oil as the intervention.
