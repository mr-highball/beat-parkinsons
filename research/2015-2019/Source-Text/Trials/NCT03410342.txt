Title: CIRCUS trial of fruit and vegetable types and vascular function
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03410342
Registration: NCT03410342
First public posting: 2018-01-25
Registry last update: 2022-03-22
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Official trial registration, baseline protocol and analysis plan.

Thirty-six participants are recorded in the completed registry. Diets compared low and higher amounts of common fruit and vegetables with a higher citrus and cruciferous diet. Carrot and tomato appear among multiple vegetables in the common diets. The study planned cognitive tasks as secondary measures, including reasoning and attention. It did not isolate a single food or study a Parkinson cohort.

No results are posted. The 2024 primary protocol gives baseline characteristics and names the tests without an executive-function contrast. The 2022 official plan describes exploratory secondary analysis. A report of the planned cognitive scores remains open.
Publisher protocol: https://doi.org/10.3390/nu16172923
Statistical analysis plan: https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/42/NCT03410342/SAP_001.pdf
