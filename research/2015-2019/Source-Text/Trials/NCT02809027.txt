The completed Chulalongkorn University study reports 178 enrolled. The first specified outcome is abdominal distention assessed by diary and examination within 72 hours. Time to first flatus or feces is listed among secondary outcomes, alongside side effects and quality of life. The public API snapshot has no posted results. The paper explicitly links this registration and reports the first-stool result in Table 3. The registry start field is June 1, 2016. First public posting followed that field, and the individual first-enrollment date was not verified.
