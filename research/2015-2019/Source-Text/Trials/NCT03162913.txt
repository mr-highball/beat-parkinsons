Title: The Effects of Strawberry Supplementation on Memory and Gait Among Over-weight Older Adults
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03162913
First posted: May 22, 2017
Last update: March 28, 2024
Retrieved: November 15, 2026
Official API rechecked: November 19, 2026
Coverage: Selected official public fields.

Completed with 47 participants and no posted results. Freeze-dried strawberry powder and control were compared. Preferred gait speed was registered as a secondary outcome. Recent falls and substantial fall-risk conditions were excluded. No prospective fall-frequency outcome is listed.
