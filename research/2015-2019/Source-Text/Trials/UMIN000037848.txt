Title: A study on the effect of eicosapentaenoic acid and docosahexaenoic acid on mental fatigue
Identifier: UMIN000037848
Official registry: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000043151
First disclosed: 2019-08-31
Registry last modified: 2020-02-03
Snapshot reviewed: 2026-11-14
Format: Plain-text factual summary of public registry fields

The Japan-based interventional study reports completed recruitment and 60 participants enrolled. The oral intervention contained eicosapentaenoic acid and docosahexaenoic acid. The comparator was corn oil without either compound, administered as six capsules daily for 12 weeks. The registration does not identify the corn oil refining grade. Prespecified fatigue ratings include a visual analogue scale before and after mental workload and another on awakening. The eligibility record excludes previously diagnosed chronic fatigue syndrome. KSO Co., Ltd. is the sponsor and Nippon Suisan Kaisha, Ltd. is the funding organization. Satoru Suzuki is the principal investigator. The registry flags publication as unpublished and gives no effect estimates, adverse events, or results citation in the visible fields.
