BRIEF SUMMARY

This is an open-label, postmarketing, observational study to document health outcomes, in
Canadian patients with advanced Parkinson's disease and long-term treatment with Duodopa
(levodopa/carbidopa intestinal gel).

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in participant's motor symptoms
    Description
      Evaluated via the Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS) Part II, III, V and
      Part IV items 33-35; items 32 & 39 modified as per MDS-UPDRS, and the Freezing of Gait
      Questionnaire (FOGQ).
    Time frame: Baseline, Day 1, Month 3, Month 6, and every 6 months thereafter up to 48 months
  -
    Measure: Change in the cognitive functions.
    Description: Evaluated via the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCa).
    Time frame: Baseline, Day 1, and every 12 months thereafter up to 48 months
  -
    Measure: Change in the Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire (PDQ-39)
    Description: Evaluated via the Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire (PDQ-39)
    Time frame: Baseline, Day 1, Month 3, and every 6 months thereafter up to 48 months
  -
    Measure: Change in caregiver burden
    Description: Evaluated via the Zarit Care Giver Burden Questionnaire.
    Time frame: Baseline, Day 1, Month 3, and every 6 months thereafter up to 48 months
  -
    Measure: Change in the non-motor symptoms.
    Description
      Evaluated via the Non-Motor symptoms Scale (NMS) and the Parkinson's Disease Sleep Scale
      (PDSS-2).
    Time frame: Baseline, Day 1, Month 3, and very 6 months thereafter up to 48 months
  -
    Measure: Change in caregiver work productivity
    Description
      Evaluated by the Work Productivity and Activity Information Questionnaire (WPAI - care
      giver)
    Time frame: Baseline, Day 1, Month 3, and every 6 months thereafter up to 48 months
  -
    Measure: Change in the participant's Healthcare Resource Utilization
    Description: Evaluated via the Health Care Resources Utilization Questionnaire (HCRU).
    Time frame: Baseline, Month 3, and every 6 months thereafter up to 48 months

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Diagnosed with advanced Parkinson's Disease

    -  Prescribed Duodopa by his/her treating physician.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Patients who were previously treated with Duodopa.

    -  Presence of other condition that, in the opinion of the treating physician,
       prohibits the patient from participating in the study or obscures the assessment of
       the treatment of advanced PD.
Healthy volunteers: No

Study population
  The patient population will consist of patients eligible for DUODOPA treatment according

  reimbursement criteria accepted by the Canadian Provincial Health Authorities or private
