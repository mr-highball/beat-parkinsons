Title: Effect of Melissa officinalis on sleep, anxiety and depression after coronary artery bypass graft surgery
Registry ID: IRCT2014071918531N1
Source: https://irct.ir/trial/16776
First registered: 2015-01-29.
Text coverage: Official registry identity, design, intervention and outcome fields.

The official entry describes retrospective registration and a target of 80 coronary artery bypass patients. It lists Melissa capsules and a starch comparator for one postoperative week, and hospital depression measured before and one week after intervention with HADS as the fourth primary outcome. Other primary outcomes are preoperative anxiety, hospital anxiety and sleep. Recruitment is complete. The estimated recruitment dates were in 2014, and actual dates are not supplied in the public record.

The registry's randomization field says not randomized and leaves its description blank. The 2015-issue depression paper describes randomized assignment. Preserve this disagreement when interpreting the comparison. The paper supplies measured HADS outcomes that the registry does not post.
