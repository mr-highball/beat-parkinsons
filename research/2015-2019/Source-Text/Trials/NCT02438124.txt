BRIEF SUMMARY

The beneficial effects of rhythmical auditory stimulation (RAS) on spatio-temporal gait
characteristics in Parkinson's disease (PD) are attested by clinical research.

Nonetheless, it is still unclear which auditory information parameters (i.e. complexity,
temporal regularity or adaptability to gait) maximize the effectiveness of RAS.

Objectives: To evaluate the beneficial effects of RAS on spatio-temporal gait
characteristics in PD patients with gait disorders according to:

  -  RAS complexity (i.e. metronome, amplitude modulated noise, music)

  -  RAS variability (i.e fixed, random, semi-random)

  -  RAS adapted in real-time to gait

Variability study:

To study the impact of RAS variability (i.e fixed, random, semi-random) in 20 PD patients
and 20 healthy controls on spatio-temporal gait characteristics with metronome, amplitude
noise and music.

Adaptability studyTo study the impact of RAS adaptability (i.e fixed, random, adapted in
real-time to gait) in 20 PD patients and 20 healthy controls on spatio-temporal gait
characteristics with metronome, amplitude noise and music.
