Title: Diet and Learning Abilities in Norwegian Youths
Study: FINS-TEENS, NCT02350322
First public posting: January 29, 2015
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02350322
API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02350322
Coverage: Factual extraction of registered intervention and outcomes.

The three arms assign school meals with fatty fish, non-seafood meat or cheese school meals, or omega-3 capsules three times weekly for 12 weeks. Attention and literacy are primary outcomes, and the Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire is a secondary mental-health outcome at 12 weeks. The structured intervention description does not give individual fish species or cooking details. The 2017 mental-health publication PMID29056893 gives the mixed fish meals and reports an emotional-problems score. The registry has no posted results.
