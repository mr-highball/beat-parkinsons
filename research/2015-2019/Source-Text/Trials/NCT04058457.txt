BRIEF SUMMARY

Patients referred to neurosurgery routinely and safely undergo deep brain stimulation
(DBS) for treatment of neurological conditions, most commonly Parkinson's disease.

The investigators have observed that respiratory problems (breathlessness) sometimes
occur subsequent to DBS of the subthalamic nucleus (STN). This study aims to determine
whether this is indeed a consequence of STN stimulation. Secondary objectives include
identification of the respiratory physiological mediators of any interoceptive
neuromodulation observed, changes in daily physical activity and MRI structural
connectivity analysis.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

A continuous cohort of Parkinson disease patients planned to undergo STN-DBS will be
approached to participate in this study (i.e. every patient will be offered participation
in the study). Over the same time period other DBS patients (GPi and VIM) will be
approached before their implantation procedure to participate as controls/comparators.
Participants will all be offered the full study, but will also be free to participate in
a smaller number of activities if so chosen.

Patients will be assessed both pre-operatively and post-operatively where they will
complete a compound respiratory questionnaire and tests of respiratory interoception and
function. Post-operatively, these tests will be carried out with stimulation ON and OFF,
the order of which will be randomised between patients.

Data on daily activity will be collected during pre- and post-operative windows, using a
wearable pedometer/heart-rate monitor.

Positive findings of respiratory neuromodulation will be correlated between patients with
structural connectivity (e.g. STN-insula.) from pre-operative MRI scans.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in breathlessness
    Description: Dyspnoea questionnaire (D12, MRC)
    Time frame: Pre-operatively and post-operatively (6-months)
  -
    Measure: Change in breathlessness
    Description
      Breathlessness ratings (modified-Borg Scale) from experimentally induced breathlessness.
      0 = none, 10 maximum. Reported every 15 seconds.
    Time frame: ON and OFF stimulation between 1 and 6 months after surgery
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in pulmonary function
    Description: Spirometry
    Time frame: ON and OFF stimulation between 1 and 6 months after surgery
  -
    Measure: Change in airways resistance
    Description: Oscillometry
    Time frame: ON and OFF stimulation between 1 and 6 months after surgery
  -
    Measure: Change in respiratory muscle strength
    Description: Maximum inspiratory/expiratory pressures
    Time frame: ON and OFF stimulation between 1 and 6 months after surgery
  -
    Measure: Change in surface electromyography of respiratory muscles
    Description: Signal analysis for evidence of tremor
    Time frame: ON and OFF stimulation between 1 and 6 months after surgery
  -
    Measure: Change in hypercapnic ventilatory response
    Description: Changes in ventilation from incrementally increased inhaled CO2
    Time frame: ON and OFF stimulation between 1 and 6 months after surgery
  -
    Measure: Change in breath-hold
    Description: Dyspnoea threshold and breakpoint
    Time frame: ON and OFF stimulation between 1 and 6 months after surgery
  -
    Measure: Change in daily activity
    Description: Pedometry
    Time frame: Pre-operatively (10 days) and post-operatively (10 days, within 6 months of surgery once recovered from surgery and programmed)
  -
    Measure: Change in daily activity
    Description: Heart rate
    Time frame: Pre-operatively (10 days) and post-operatively (10 days within 6 months of surgery once recovered from surgery and programmed)
  -
    Measure: MRI structural connectivity
    Description: DTI correlation with respiratory outcomes
    Time frame: Pre-operative scan with post-operative analysis through study completion at an average of 1 year

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Participant is willing and able to give informed consent for participation in the
       study.

    -  Male or female, aged 18 years or above.

    -  Planned for required DBS surgery

    -  Fluent in the English language

    -  For experimental group: diagnosed with Parkinson's disease

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Female who is pregnant

    -  Subject is currently participating in a clinical investigation that includes an
       active treatment arm which may affect the respiratory system.

    -  Acute respiratory problem at time of experimental session: e.g. rhinosinusitis,
       pharyngitis, asthma exacerbation
