BRIEF SUMMARY

Deep brain stimulation of the sub thalamic nucleus (STN-DBS) is an efficient treatment to
improve motor and non motor signs of Parkinson's disease (PD). In people aged more 70
years, an increased rate of side effects such as post-operative cognitive decline and/or
postural instability with falls has been suspected with less improvement in the quality
of life. Here, Investigators aim further explore the effects of STN-DBS in 50 patients
with PD people aged more 69 years in a randomized parallel controlled study.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

The effects of combined bilateral deep brain stimulation of the subthalamic nucleus and
medical treatment on the quality of life will be evaluated in 25 PD patients aged more 69
years in comparison to 25 PD patients aged more 69 years that only received medical drug
treatment.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: PDQ-39 Quality of life questionnary
    Description
      PDQ-39 : Parkinson's disease quality of life scale questionnaire with 39 questions,
      grouped into 8 domains. Each item is scored on a scale from 0 to 4, with higher scores
      indicating greater difficulties. An overall score can be calculated by averaging the
      eight domain scores. Scores range from 0 to 100 :

      0 = best quality of life 100 = worst quality of life
    Time frame: Quality of life one year after surgery
