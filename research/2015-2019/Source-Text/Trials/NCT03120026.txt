First posted: 2017-04-19
Last update posted: 2019-01-31
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated official registry and publication reconciliation

The completed randomized sarcopenia rehabilitation study enrolled 140
participants. The registry gives a short-walk gait-speed outcome and
has no posted results. The linked study report provides results for
the same trial family (PMID 32961041). It's the article Methods that
describe strawberry and vanilla flavoring in both treatment groups.
The assigned contrast was the nutrient formula versus an isocaloric
control. The registration supplies no raw-strawberry or
Parkinson-specific fall-frequency analysis.
