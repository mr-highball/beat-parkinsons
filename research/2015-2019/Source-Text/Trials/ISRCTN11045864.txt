Title: Drooling in Parkinson’s disease: a case for divided attention
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN11045864
Official registry PDF: https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/11045864
Registry ID: ISRCTN11045864
DOI: 10.1186/ISRCTN11045864
Registration date: 2016-02-01
Coverage: Selected paraphrased official registry fields, including dates, design, target sample and results link.

The register describes a within-person comparison in adults with idiopathic Parkinson disease and daytime drooling. Saliva swallows and observed drooling were planned at rest and under a concurrent language and decision task. The registry classification is cross-sectional with a target of 20, listed first enrollment on 2015-10-01, and completed status. The posted results article is the 2018 Dysphagia report, PMID 29785679, which reports 18 participants. The 2017 MDS abstract 111 supplies the same outcomes and tests. These are linked records for one study. No food was assigned.
