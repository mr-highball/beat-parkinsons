Title: Assessment of a topical date-palm product versus diclofenac gel for clinical manifestations of knee osteoarthritis
Registry ID: IRCT20180126038510N1
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/29648
Persian registration: https://fa.irct.ir/trial/29648
First registered: 2018-08-25, prospective.
Text coverage: Selected official English registry fields verified on 2026-11-19.

The record plans an unblinded randomized comparison in 94 people with knee osteoarthritis. One arm is a topical date-palm preparation and the other is diclofenac gel. The outcomes include visual analogue pain and WOMAC domains at baseline and after two and four weeks. The registry does not identify the botanical part in the topical product.

It marks recruitment complete and supplies no numerical group outcomes or confirmed analyzed count. The institute's dissertation record is a separate route to the original results. The route, clinical population and pain phenotype differ from oral dried dates in Parkinson disease.
