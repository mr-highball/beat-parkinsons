Registration: NCT04183556, The Effect of Turmeric on New Onset Primary Dysmenorrhea
First posted: 2019-12-03
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04183556
Coverage: Official protocol field and results-posting check, November 15, 2026.
The completed study compares a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug alone and with oral turmeric powder. It lists visual analogue pain in primary dysmenorrhea as the primary outcome. No results are posted. The published results are DOI 10.28982/josam.828571.
