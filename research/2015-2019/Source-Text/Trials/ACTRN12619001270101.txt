Source: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=ACTRN12619001270101
Prospective registration date: 2019-09-12.
Official WHO mirror summary. Participants with hand or knee osteoarthritis assigned canola oil, olive oil or fish oil for approximately three months. Listed canola amount is one tablespoon daily. Pain VAS is secondary to feasibility and adherence. Results availability is marked Yes but no numerical pain values or results URL is supplied. The 2021 proceedings report a matching feasibility design, with a different liquid-oil dose description.
