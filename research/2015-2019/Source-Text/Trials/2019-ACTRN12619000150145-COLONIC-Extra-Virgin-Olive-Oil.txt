Title: COLONIC extra-virgin olive oil protocol in ulcerative colitis
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=375208
Registry ID: ACTRN12619000150145
Publication date: 2019-02-01
Date basis: Original ANZCTR registration date. Registry last updated 2019-04-29.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review basis: Official registry, intervention, registered outcomes, recruitment and results sections.
Source type: Randomized parallel dietary trial protocol without reported outcomes.

The proposed comparison replaces added dietary fats with extra-virgin olive oil versus usual care in adults with ulcerative colitis and a healthy comparison population. Primary outcomes are partial Mayo disease activity and gut microbial variety or richness. Registered secondary outcomes include HADS anxiety and depression and the IBD fatigue scale. Constipation severity is not named separately in the checked registry outcomes.

The registry lists the study as not yet recruiting, has no actual enrollment or completion date and shows a target sample of 56. The 2020 Cochrane review gives an earlier estimated target of 50 and no published results. The listed target, planned measurements and actual reported outcomes are separate fields. No published outcome is linked to the inspected registry. This is separate from the completed Iranian EVOO and canola crossover, PMID 31901082. It supplies no Parkinson food-effect direction for either claim 0072 or claim 0343.
