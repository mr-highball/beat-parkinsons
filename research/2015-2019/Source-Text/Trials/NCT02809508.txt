Title: The Effect of Oily Fish and Poultry on Children's Cardiometabolic Health and Cognitive Function
Study: FiSK Junior, NCT02809508
First public posting: June 22, 2016
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02809508
API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02809508
Coverage: Factual extraction of registered intervention and outcomes.

The two arms assign a mixture of oily fish, including salmon, herring and mackerel, or mainly chicken, for lunch and dinner. At 12 weeks the primary cardiometabolic endpoints are blood pressure and fasting serum triacylglycerol. Parent-rated Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire and executive function ratings are registered other outcomes at 12 weeks. The record has no posted results or separate salmon or preparation comparison. The 2020 article PMID32529206 reports the socioemotional results.
