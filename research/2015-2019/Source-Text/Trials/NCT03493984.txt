Title: Plant Exosomes and Patients Diagnosed With Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) 17
NCT ID: NCT03493984
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03493984
API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03493984
First posted: 2018-04-11
Last update posted: 2021-03-16
Archive scope: Official registry fields and factual summary. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

Withdrawn before enrollment. Actual enrollment zero. No results are posted.

The proposed groups were assigned ginger-derived vesicles, aloe-derived vesicles, both, or placebo. The primary outcome was change on a glucose tolerance test. The registry states that the investigator left the institution before study approval. The record documents an intended metabolic comparison without observed clinical outcomes.
