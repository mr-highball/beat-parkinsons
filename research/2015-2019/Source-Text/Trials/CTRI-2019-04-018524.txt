Title: Effects of Helicobacter Pylori Infection Eradication in Patients With Parkinsons Disease
Registry ID: CTRI/2019/04/018524
Source: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=CTRI/2019/04/018524
Registration date: 2019-04-09 according to the WHO ICTRP field.
Archive scope: WHO ICTRP primary registry export, with 2026 report linkage. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

The public CTRI record in WHO ICTRP lists a randomized parallel placebo comparison with participants and investigators masked. It also describes a prevalence comparison at baseline. The unstratified target sample field is 200 and the displayed recruitment field says not yet recruiting. The registered primary fields broadly describe motor and nonmotor symptoms and quality of life at baseline and weeks 4, 8 and 12. The secondary fields describe baseline infection prevalence and symptom severity.

The later journal article cites this exact registration. Its abstract reports 80 breath tests, 34 positive screens, and 30 randomized participants. It specifies the 12-week ON-medication MDS-UPDRS Part III as the primary result. The registry snapshot and article give different levels of detail and different enrollment scopes. Obtain the original protocol and complete participant flow before interpreting enrollment changes. There are no posted result fields in the inspected WHO snapshot.
