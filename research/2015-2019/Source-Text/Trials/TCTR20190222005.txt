Title: Effects of Helicobacter Pylori Eradication on Symptoms of Parkinson's Disease with Motor Fluctuations
TCTR ID: TCTR20190222005
Source: https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/show/TCTR20190222005
Registration date: 2019-02-22. Registry last update field: 2020-01-07.
Archive scope: Factual summary of official public preview record, status, design and outcomes retrieved on 2026-11-20.

The official registry labels the registration retrospective and gives an anticipated January 2019 start. It records a single-arm, open-label, nonrandomized drug intervention, with target enrollment 40 and no populated actual-enrollment field. The plan used a two-week Helicobacter pylori eradication regimen. The primary measure is average daily hours off, recorded in symptom diaries at one month. The secondary registry measure is UPDRS motor score at one month. The record says completed, with no results posted in the registry.

The subsequent paper explicitly cites this registration and the same sponsor protocol identifier. It reports 40 enrolled, 22 testing positive and receiving eradication therapy, and 17 with successful eradication. The paper measured four weeks after the two-week course and reports additional outcomes. The paper's Methods and registration differ in the printed omeprazole amount and amoxicillin unit. The latter discrepancy remains open. The study treats infection and reports food intake as an observational covariate.
