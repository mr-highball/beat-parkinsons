Title: Randomized Daikenchuto placebo trial for functional constipation
Registry ID: UMIN000020570
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000022244
First posted: 2016-01-14
Archive scope: Summary from the official structured registration and linked publication.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The official record describes a double-blind, randomized parallel comparison. Target enrollment was 60. The plan specifies four-week symptom assessment, colonic transit and gas volume. The comparison is 5 g of Daikenchuto or placebo before meals three times daily for 28 days. The 2023 article reports 63 randomized with a global efficacy primary outcome and prints 15 g three times daily after meals. The publication and registration agree on the product, control and duration, while timing, amount and endpoint wording differ. The administered schedule is unresolved.
