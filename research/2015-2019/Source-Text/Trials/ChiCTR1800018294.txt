Format: Plain-text factual summary of the official WHO ICTRP mirror of the ChiCTR submission

The record describes an interventional, randomized parallel trial at the Affiliated Hospital of Qingdao University in China. It targeted 40 participants in each group and lists recruitment as ongoing at its September 2018 refresh. Eligible patients were aged 18–75 and undergoing laparoscopic distal gastrectomy for gastric cancer without neoadjuvant therapy. Group 1 is postoperative Chinese green tea and group 2 is listed as “no.” The entry does not specify water as the comparison beverage or identify a tea brewing procedure.

The primary outcome is gastrointestinal function recovery. Secondary fields include postoperative complications, hospital stays, hospital expenses, visual analogue pain scales, fatigue scores, and inflammatory factors. The dated record identifies the fatigue outcome but supplies no exact instrument, timing, baseline values, or effect estimates. The displayed protocol and results fields are empty. The WHO mirror reports an ethics approval date of 2018-07-25. It gives no explanation of the interval between first enrollment and that displayed date.

The original ChiCTR project route returned a hosting challenge when checked. The WHO record is the accessible official transmitted snapshot. The linked 2021 publication supplies the water-comparator details, brewing procedure, and postoperative fatigue figures.
