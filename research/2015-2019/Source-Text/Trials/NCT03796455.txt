Registered fish oil and HMB comparison in COPD
Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03796455
Registration: NCT03796455
Coverage: Factual official registration snapshot.

NCT03796455 first posted January 8, 2019 and lists six-minute walk distance as a secondary functional measure. It describes placebo oil capsules with no ingredient breakdown in the intervention text. The official record shows completion with no posted results. The linked 2024 trial publication identifies a soybean-and-corn-oil capsule comparison with maltodextrin powder.

The published supplement and current registry add the following details.

This completed randomized study compared EPA/DHA with and without HMB against a matched comparator in COPD. The registry lists actual enrollment of 54 and net whole-body protein metabolism as its primary endpoint. The registered secondary outcomes include six-minute walking performance. The publication describes 46 participants in its three groups and additionally identifies gait speed and balance tests. Separate comparative fall counts are not registered. No numerical results are posted in the registry as of retrieval. The published Supplementary Table S1 specifies equal parts soybean and corn oil in the comparator capsules.
Linked publication: PMID 39181037 and DOI 10.1016/j.clnu.2024.08.004.
