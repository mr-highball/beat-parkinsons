Title: The Effect of Superoxide Dismutase (SOD) Enzyme on Frailty and Health Related Quality of Life Among Indonesian Pre-frail Elderly: A Double Blind Randomized Controlled Trial
Registry: NCT02753582
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02753582
First posted: 2016-04-28
Record status: Unknown as displayed by the registry on 2026-11-19 UTC
Coverage: Official protocol record and results availability.

The randomized double-blind protocol plans 150 pre-frail older adults. It compares an SOD-plus-gliadin capsule identified as GliSODin against placebo over 24 weeks. The primary outcome is frailty on the FI-40 questionnaire. Quality of life, serum antioxidant status, morbidity and hospitalization are separate secondary outcomes. There are no results posted. The exposure is a capsule formulation and the study's listed outcomes concern frailty and associated measures.
