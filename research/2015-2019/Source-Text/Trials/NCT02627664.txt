BRIEF SUMMARY

The investigators prospectively enrolled 64 early PD patients (less than 3 years after
the first symptom) in order to prospectively assess the natural history of
non-dopaminergic symptoms.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Patients were evaluated at baseline and after 2 years in off drug condition. Clinical,
psychological, neuropsychological evaluations, functional respiratory evaluation,
swallowing video fluoroscopy, dysarthria, gait and axial disorders.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: dysarthria severity assessed by the BECD scale
    Description
      BECD (French battery of clinical evaluation of the dysarthria) is a validated scale for
      qualitative assessment of dysarthria severity in neurological disorders, especially PD
    Time frame: 2 years
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: respiratory insufficiency detection
    Description
      pulmonary function tests include spirometry with standard spirometer and maximal
      inspiratory and expiratory flow volume curves . At least 3 reproductible F-V curves are
      necessary. Values of FCV, FEV, peak expiratory flow, peak inspiratory flow, forced
      expiratory flow, SNIP were measured 12 hours after last levodopa intake (off drug)
    Time frame: 2 years
  -
    Measure: swallowing function
    Description
      150 mL glass of water test video fluoroscopy of swallow in off drug condition face and
      profile incidences: qualitative analysis of oral, pharyngeal, aspiration if necessary
      blindly assessed by 2 ENT experts in PD
    Time frame: 2 years
  -
    Measure: gait axial function (freezing)
    Description
      SWS test rhythmic tests for differens imposed frequencies (upper lower limb and facial)
      kinematic analysis of gait parameters by VICON (oxford metrics)
    Time frame: 2 years
  -
    Measure: Mattis scale
    Time frame: 2 years
  -
    Measure: LARS scale
    Time frame: 2 years
  -
    Measure: MADRS scale
    Time frame: 2 years
  -
    Measure: PAS scale
    Time frame: 2 years
  -
    Measure: MoCA
    Time frame: 2 years
  -
    Measure: Genetic Polymorphisme
    Description
      To evaluate of cognitive and profile correlation to the polymorphisms of COMT
      (catechol-O-methyltransferase: Val158Met COMT) of MAPT H1 / H2 (microtubule associated
      tau protein) and ApoE (Apolipoprotein-E-ε2, 3, 4 )
    Time frame: 2 years
