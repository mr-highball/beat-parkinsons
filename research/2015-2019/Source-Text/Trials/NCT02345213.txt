Title: A post-marketing clinical study of Aricept in patients with dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02345213
Trial registration: NCT02345213
Sponsor study ID: E2020-J081-419
Publication date: 2015-01-26
Date basis: First posted at ClinicalTrials.gov
Source depth: Official registration; retrieved registry had no posted results
Evidence type: Phase IV randomized study registration

The record gives 160 participants and assignment to donepezil or placebo. Global clinical change measured by CIBIC-plus is primary. The separately published 2024 article links this exact registration and reports NPI as secondary. The phase IV recruitment dates and study ID distinguish it from the phase III trial.
