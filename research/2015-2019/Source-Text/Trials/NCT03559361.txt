Registered oral olive-oil comparator and actigraphy
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03559361
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: official record with publication linkage

First posted June 18, 2018. The registry identifies olive-oil capsules as the placebo arm in a 26-week comparison of DHA- and EPA-rich products and measures objective and subjective sleep. The register was completed without a posted result module at the November 15, 2026 replay. The 2021 report specifies refined olive oil.
