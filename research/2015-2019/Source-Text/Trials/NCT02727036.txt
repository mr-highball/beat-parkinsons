Title: Pumpkin seed oil or pumpkin seeds in postmenopausal women
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02727036
Registration: NCT02727036
First public posting: 2016-04-04
Registry last update: 2018-05-03
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Official registry and links to same-cohort original evidence.

The completed Texas parallel study compares pumpkin seed oil with raw pumpkin seeds in postmenopausal women. The registry supplies no posted numerical results. The 2017 dissertation establishes cold pressing and shows unchanged oil-arm depression category counts. Depression is a single item of the menopausal symptom questionnaire.

Dissertation: ../2017-Maiya-Cold-Pressed-Pumpkin-Oil-Depression.txt
Same cohort: 2018 abstract P08-074 in PMC6265820.
The 2011 report cited in the registration predates this trial and concerns a distinct cohort.
