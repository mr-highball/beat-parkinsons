Title: Effect of flixweed and fig on irritable bowel syndrome with predominant constipation
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02559245
Source alias: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02559245
NCT ID: NCT02559245
Publication date: 2015-09-24
Date basis: First public posting as recorded by ClinicalTrials.gov. Last public update 2018-05-22.
Text coverage: Selected registry design, outcomes and result status from the official API. Factual summary.

The completed Isfahan University of Medical Sciences trial enrolled 150 adults with constipation-predominant irritable bowel syndrome. Its parallel design randomized participants to fig fruit, flixweed seed or their usual diet for four months. The registry specifies investigator masking and no intervention for the usual-diet group. It specifies 90 g/day of Ficus carica without a drying description.

The registered primary outcome was the IBS severity questionnaire. Registered secondary outcomes include quality of life, frequency of defecation and hard stool. The registry shows no posted results. The outcome paper is PMID 30318190 and represents the same trial. Visual analog scores for bowel symptoms are not measures of visual perception or hallucinations.
