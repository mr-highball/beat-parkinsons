2018 — Registered knee injection study of capsaicin

Original source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03429049
Identifier: NCT03429049

The randomized trial first posted February 12, 2018. It compared intra-articular investigational capsaicin CNTX-4975-05 with a matching injection in adults with knee osteoarthritis. The primary measure was change in knee pain with walking. The registration therefore specifies pain experienced during walking, a distinct measure from gait speed.
