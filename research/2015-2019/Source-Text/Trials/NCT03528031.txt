Title: Habitual Diet and Avocado Trial
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03528031
First posted: May 17, 2018
Last update: March 22, 2023
Retrieved: November 15, 2026
Coverage: Selected official public fields.

A completed parallel trial compared one daily whole avocado in the usual diet with low-avocado habitual diet. No posted registry results. Ancillary reports use the same allocation and do not provide a distinct avocado-oil or Parkinson daytime sleepiness comparison.
