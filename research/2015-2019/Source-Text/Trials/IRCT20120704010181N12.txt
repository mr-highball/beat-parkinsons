Title: Assessment of the effect of dill powder on gastrointestinal symptoms, glycemic control, lipid profile, serum levels of oxidant and Inflammatory markers in type 2 diabetic patients

Selected public fields checked November 14, 2026. The two-arm blinded trial planned 48 adults with type 2 diabetes in Ahvaz, Iran. It describes dill powder capsules or starch placebo for eight weeks. The listed outcomes include a gastrointestinal symptoms questionnaire, insulin and glucose or fructosamine, serum lipids, and oxidative and inflammatory markers. The 2020 report identifies the dried leaf preparation and publishes symptom distributions in Table 5.

The article's legacy address gives the numeric slug 20288. Direct look-up of this number in the current registry served IRCT2016112023861N4. A search by the exact registration identifier returned the present project 29037, which carries IRCT20120704010181N12. The current exact identifier and title were used to link the study.
