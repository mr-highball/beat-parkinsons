Boston red raspberry breakfast trial
https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03743792

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT03743792 on November 16, 2018. The record describes a randomized, double-masked crossover in adults and reports actual enrollment of six at one Boston site. The active breakfast used 25 g of freeze-dried red raspberry powder split between a drink and a buttermilk biscuit sandwich. The comparison was a control drink and sandwich.

The registered primary outcome measures psychomotor function with the Grooved Pegboard from baseline through six hours. Endothelial function measured by near-infrared spectroscopy through five hours is secondary. The registry reports study completion on April 30, 2023. As checked on November 16, 2026, it has no posted results and lists no mood or anxiety outcome. The separate NCT03879213 register in Chicago includes Profile of Mood States. An unambiguous mapping from the Boston participants to a published outcome analysis remains open.
