BRIEF SUMMARY

Reading disorders in Parkinson's Disease (PD) are poorly evaluated due to the lack of
validated tests to screen for them. They are often attributed to hand tremors associated
with the disease. In this study, the investigating team evaluated the "alouette test"
validated for dyslexia screening, in PD by comparing the results to healthy patients.

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

  -
    Label: Patients without Parkinson's Disease = control group
    Description
      Patients without Parkinson's Disease (PD) were evaluated for reading disorders using a

    Intervention names
      - Other: Alouette test (reading test)
Interventions
  -
    Type: OTHER
    Name: Alouette test (reading test)
    Description
      The alouette test was assessed at a fixed distance of 40 centimeters on a fixed surface
      to avoid errors related to hand tremors.
    Arm group labels
      - Patients with Parkinson's Disease
      - Patients without Parkinson's Disease = control group

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Score on the Alouette test
    Description
      The Alouette test is based on the precision and speed in reading a short text (279
      words). The score corresponds to the total number of words correctly read.
    Time frame: 1 day
