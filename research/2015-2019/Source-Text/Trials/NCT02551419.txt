Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.

First public posting or registration: 2015-09-16

Completed. The current registry lists actual enrollment 82 and no posted results. Experimental and placebo drinks were assigned for six months, 125 mL twice daily. The register identifies PET brain ketone uptake and plasma ketones as primary outcomes and cognitive measures including executive function as secondary. The register does not name the oil. The first report identifies the sunflower formulation and the aggregate report gives different enrollment accounting.
