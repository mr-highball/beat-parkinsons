Identification of New Biomarkers of Banana and Tomato Intake

Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03581955
Source credit: ClinicalTrials.gov, U.S. National Library of Medicine, U.S. Government database
NCT ID: NCT03581955
First posted: 2018-07-10
Text coverage: factual extract of official protocol and outcome fields.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

This completed crossover enrolled 12 healthy adults. The registry gives actual study dates March 26–June 16, 2016. The registered comparisons include acute banana, tomato and a control drink. The primary outcome is serum and urine metabolite profiles through 24 hours, with sample pools for analytical use. Summary results are not posted. The separate banana-focused paper is PMID31240312. The trial does not assess Parkinson disease or clinical apathy.
