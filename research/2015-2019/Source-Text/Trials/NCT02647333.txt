Title: Intervention With Omega Fatty Acids in High-risk Patients
Registry: NCT02647333
First posted: 2016-01-06
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02647333
Status: Completed; no posted results in the registry as checked 2026-11-19 UTC.
Text coverage: Distilled official protocol, enrollment, results status and article references.

The crossover lists actual enrollment of 40. The registered lipid profile, particle concentration and particle-size outcomes were planned for baseline and week 8. The later articles describe two seven-week intervention periods separated by nine weeks of washout. The registry links the lipid outcome and sex-specific analysis (PMIDs 33933722 and 37404855). The later inflammatory analysis PMID 40058591 uses the same crossover. The registry reports no separate fall, anxiety or swallowing outcome and supplies no evidence of cold pressing.
