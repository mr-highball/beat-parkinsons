Curcumin and Function in Older Adults

The protocol compared oral curcumin with microcrystalline cellulose placebo for approximately three months in older adults. The primary outcomes were 400-m walking speed and handgrip strength. Cognition, pain and interleukin-6 were secondary outcomes.

The results groups consisted of 9 participants receiving curcumin and 8 receiving placebo. Mean walking speed changes were +0.014 (SD 0.090) and +0.103 (SD 0.100) m/s, respectively. Mean grip strength changes were +0.63 (SD 4.90) and -0.25 (SD 3.14) kg, respectively. The cognition entry is labeled Attention & Memory and describes MMSE; mean changes were +0.33 (SD 3.94) and -2.75 (SD 5.09) points. The record gives no analysis for these between-arm comparisons. No adverse events were posted for either group.
