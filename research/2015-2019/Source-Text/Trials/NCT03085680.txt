Title: Curcumin and Function in Older Adults
Registry ID: NCT03085680
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03085680
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03085680
First posted: 2017-03-21
Results first posted: 2022-02-02
Record type: Distilled official trial registration
Protocol and analysis plan: https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/80/NCT03085680/Prot_SAP_000.pdf

The protocol recruits sedentary adults older than 65 with slower walking speed and elevated C-reactive protein. It compares oral curcumin capsules with a microcrystalline cellulose placebo over approximately three months. The primary posted outcomes are walking across 400 m and handgrip strength. Cognitive performance (MMSE), pain severity and interleukin-6 are secondary endpoints. Participants were selected by function and inflammatory marker rather than a Parkinson disease diagnosis. The exposure is a capsule formulation, with a separate journal record identifying it as Curcumin C3 Complex.

The official outcomes posted in 2022 are cataloged under that year. The linked journal publication is Mankowski et al., DOI 10.14283/jfa.2022.47, PMID 36946712. The posted results and article represent a single trial.

The previously indexed snapshot reported walking speed changes of +0.014 and +0.103 m/s, grip changes of +0.63 and −0.25 kg, and MMSE changes of +0.33 and −2.75 points for curcumin and placebo, respectively. The dated results digest also provides group dispersion and pain outcomes.
