Title: Study To Evaluate the Efficacy, Safety and Tolerability of E2027 (Hereinafter Referred to as Irsenontrine) in Participants With Dementia With Lewy Bodies

First posted: 2018-03-15

Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Verified registration and selected posted study results.

The completed phase 2 trial registered 326 participants in a randomized, masked comparison of irsenontrine and placebo in dementia with Lewy bodies. At 12 weeks, Montreal Cognitive Assessment mean change was -0.4 with irsenontrine and -0.6 with placebo (reported p=0.6909). The ordinal global outcome comparison reported an odds ratio of 1.018 (95% CI 0.695 to 1.492).

The posted Neuropsychiatric Inventory totals combine several symptoms. Mean change on NPI-12 was -2.0 with irsenontrine and -0.2 with placebo (reported p=0.6127). The posted NPI-4 and NPI-10 outcomes are also composites. The motor outcome is the total UPDRS Part III, with mean changes of +1.0 and -1.2 respectively. The posted results provide neither isolated visual hallucination frequency nor an isolated rest tremor item. No food exposure is compared.
