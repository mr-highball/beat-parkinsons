Title: Effects of Acute Red Raspberry Consumption on Vascular Function in Healthy Individuals
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02734901
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting: 2016-04-12
Status: Completed. Registry enrollment: 10. Posted results: No.
Design: Controlled crossover in healthy participants. Arms include beverages made with 200 or 400 g frozen raspberries or control.
Outcome measures: Endothelial function, pulse wave velocity, central and peripheral blood pressure, and heart rate.
Related report: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29802820/
The registry lists no standing blood pressure or orthostatic symptom outcomes.
