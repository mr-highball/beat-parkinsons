Title: Efficacy of oil pulling on drug-induced xerostomia
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00012459
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting: 2018-01-31
Last posted update: 2021-07-13
Status: Completed. Actual enrollment 26. The linked journal report had 24 completers.
Design: Randomized crossover in medication-related dry mouth. Sunflower-oil and mineral-water rinse periods with a washout. The publication identifies cold-pressed oil from roasted sunflower kernels and instructs participants to spit out the rinse.
Outcomes: Xerostomia burden and oral measures. The publication reports easier swallowing as a secondary patient-reported question.
