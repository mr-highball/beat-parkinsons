Text coverage: Official registry protocol, outcomes, results status and references.

The ENHANce protocol concerns physical functioning in older adults with sarcopenia and assigns combinations of exercise, protein and omega-3 supplementation. Peanut oil capsules are listed as the omega-3 placebo, with pressing grade unspecified. The primary outcome is change on the Short Physical Performance Battery at weeks 12 and 24. Neither the population nor the measured outcome addresses Parkinson resting tremor. The registry has no posted results. The registry references the protocol (PMID 33302879), the TEMPUS-FUGIT nested study (PMID 37752426) and the later ENHANce feasibility report (PMID 41632571). Treat the nested comparison and results as the same intervention lineage.
