Source: UMIN Clinical Trials Registry
Registration: UMIN000037387 (R000042626)
Public title: A Study to investigate the influence of diet on plasma levodopa concentration during LCIG therapy
First public posting and latest registry update: 2019-07-16
Source URL: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000042626
Coverage: Local summary of the original registry fields

The Ehime University group planned an open, non-randomized, single-arm study in Japan. Eligible adults had Parkinson’s disease and were receiving levodopa-carbidopa intestinal gel. The target sample was ten. The primary comparison was plasma levodopa concentration with and without lunch. The planned lunch contained 20–25 g protein. The entry names no individual foods and gives no separate secondary outcome.

The registry displays its original preinitiation status and contains no posted results. The matching 2021 Miyaue and colleagues publication reports results in ten patients and is summarized in research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-NHO-Clinical-Nutrition.md.
