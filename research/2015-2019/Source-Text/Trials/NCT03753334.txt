Title: EPA supplementation after biochemical recurrence of prostate cancer
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03753334
NCT ID: NCT03753334
First posted: 2018-11-27
Last update posted: 2025-12-19
Coverage: Factual extract of official registration.

This randomized, blinded pilot compares 5 g/day fish oil, including 4 g purified EPA, with 5 g/day high-oleic sunflower oil for 12 months. The registry lists 39 actual enrollments. The primary outcome is PSA doubling time. HADS depression and PHQ-9 are secondary measures scheduled through month 12. Status is active, not recruiting, with no posted results.
The population has prostate cancer after biochemical recurrence or progression. The earlier presurgery trial NCT02333435 uses a distinct protocol, with shared investigators. Person-level participation overlap is unverified. Oil refining grade and a Parkinson cohort are unspecified.
