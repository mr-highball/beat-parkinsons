Title: Reduction of the oral microbiome by oil pulling
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00011922
Record: DRKS00011922
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting or registration: 2017-04-05

Completed, retrospectively registered. Three adults used saline, then after a washout sunflower seed oil, as separate rinses. Outcomes concern the oral microbiome and sample characteristics. The resulting study measured saliva volumes. The study did not enroll a Parkinson cohort or test ingestion.
