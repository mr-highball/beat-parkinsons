Official public IRCT fields, accessed 2026-11-15 at https://en.irct.ir/trial/36888.
Registration and last update: 2019-05-10, while recruiting. Planned recruitment 2019-01-06 to 2019-09-21. Actual dates are blank.
Women 20–65 with mild or moderate depression and BMI above the threshold printed as 24/9. Antidepressant use is excluded. Randomized detailed arms: 3 g/day ground sumac powder or starch in three capsules with a weight-reduction diet for 12 weeks. The public summary separately gives six weeks.
Depression is secondary outcome 15. Beck Depression Inventory at baseline and 12 weeks. Matched publications are Hariri 2020 DOI 10.1002/ptr.6737 and Hariri 2023 DOI 10.1016/j.ctcp.2023.101734. Both name the same registry and ethics approval.
