BRIEF SUMMARY

The study assessed acute mood and cognitive effects of a drink made from date seeds in healthy young adults. The arm descriptions specify a Latin square for visit order.

ARMS AND INTERVENTIONS

Experimental, date seeds filtered coffee: drink prepared from 45 g date-seed coffee in 280 mL boiled water with coffee flavor and brown food coloring, using a filter coffee machine.
Experimental, normal filtered coffee: 6 g regular coffee in 280 mL boiled water, using a filter coffee machine.
Placebo comparator: boiled water with coffee flavor and brown food coloring.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES

Average change in CogTrack cognitive indices from baseline at 45 and 90 minutes per visit. The registry labels this as nine indices and lists attentional intensity, sustained attention, attentional fluctuation, memory retrieval speed, cognitive reaction time, working memory capacity, episodic memory capacity, and quality of memory.

SECONDARY OUTCOMES

Caffeine consumption questionnaire: change from each visit's baseline.
Caffeine research visual analogue scales: relaxed, alert, jittery, tired, tense, headache, and overall mood. Changes from baseline measured at 45 and 90 minutes post-dose.
Bond–Lader visual analogue mood scales: alertness, calmness, and contentment. Changes from baseline measured at 45 and 90 minutes post-dose.
