Title: Acute Berry Intake on Metabolic Control and Cognitive Function
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03879213
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting: 2019-03-18
Status: Completed. Registry enrollment: 30. Posted results: No.
Design: Raspberry meal crossover in older adults with overweight or obesity.
Primary outcomes: Profile of Mood States, Digit Symbol Coding, Hopkins Verbal Learning, CANTAB Paired Associates Learning, Rapid Visual Information Processing and Spatial Working Memory.
Secondary outcomes: Psychomotor and vascular function.
Related report: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41536265/
The published report enrolled two sites with 36 completers and analyzed freeze-dried raspberry powder. No Parkinson cohort was enrolled.
