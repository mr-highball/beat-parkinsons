Title: A Comparative Phase 2 Study Assessing the Efficacy of Triheptanoin, an Anaplerotic Therapy in Huntington's Disease
Publication date: 2015-05-25 (first public registry posting)
Record: NCT02453061
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02453061
Text coverage: Official registry API data, distilled.

The completed randomized study enrolled 100 adults with early Huntington disease. The first six months compared oral triheptanoin and oral safflower oil at 1 g/kg/day. Both arms then received triheptanoin for six months. The randomized oil comparison ends at the blinded phase. Primary outcome is volumetric MRI. The protocol specifies total movement and functional scales. The press grade of the safflower oil and separate dysphagia and dystonia effects remain unreported. No results module is posted in the US registry.
