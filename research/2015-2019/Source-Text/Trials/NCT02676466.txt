Format: Selected public record fields, summarized in plain text

The fish-oil placebo uses corn-oil gelcaps matched to the omega-3 gelcaps. The losartan placebo is cellulose based. Participants were assigned in three strata with a fish-oil-only, losartan-only, or factorial comparison. The publicly posted adverse-event rows give the count of people affected and at risk after randomization, with event type and seriousness classification.

Serious fall term, no co-assigned losartan:
Fish Oil Active (EG000): 1 event in 1 of 109.
Fish Oil Placebo (EG001): 1 event in 1 of 71.
The table presents the same values under ANY SAE and INPATIENT HOSP. They are repeated classifications rather than additional events. The nonserious table contains no separately reported FALL term with these groups. The listed primary mobility outcome is 400-m walking speed, representing average pace rather than step-length or ordinary-life fall frequency. Oil processing or supplier grade is absent from the registry entry. The journal report specifies Epax in Aalesund, Norway, and 0.7 g matched gelcaps.
