Title: Anti-inflammatory nutrition in fibromyalgia
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04007705
Registration: NCT04007705
Publication date: 2019-07-05
Date basis: First public registry posting.
Coverage: Official registered intervention and outcomes checked November 26, 2026.

The completed registered intervention combines a low-FODMAP phase, multiple exclusions, and counseling to increase selected fish, nuts, fruit and vegetables. Sardines appear as one of four fish options. There is no separate sardine intervention, cooking preparation, or anxiety outcome. The linked primary publication reports pain and other combined diet outcomes in fibromyalgia. The registry itself displays no results module, while journal results are available.
