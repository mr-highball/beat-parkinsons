Title: Adding stewed apricot juice to sena improves the right-side and overall colon cleansing quality for colonoscopy preparation.
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov
URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02665624
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02665624
Registry ID: NCT02665624
First published: 2017
Authors: Yasar B, Abut E, Kayadibi H, Akdogan F, Gonen C
Journal: Functional Foods in Health and Disease
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled trial (NCT02665624; Functional Foods in Health and Disease, Jan 2017, 7(1):54-67; DOI 10.31989/ffhd.v7i1.314) in N=128 outpatients undergoing elective colonoscopy randomized to natural stewed apricot juice plus senna vs senna alone. Adding stewed apricot juice significantly improved Ottawa Bowel Preparation Scale overall colon cleansing quality (p<0.001), right colon cleansing (p=0.038), and transverse colon cleansing (p=0.037), shortened total colonoscopy time (17.6 vs 22.8 min, p=0.048) and cecal intubation time (7.4 vs 11.2 min, p=0.042), improved technical ease (79.4% vs 49.2%, p<0.001), and increased patient willingness to repeat the regimen (91.2% vs 80%, p=0.037) without increasing adverse events. Limits: non-PD elective colonoscopy outpatients, acute laxative-adjunct bowel cleansing rather than chronic PD constipation severity, and stewed apricot juice rather than dry-eaten dried apricots.
