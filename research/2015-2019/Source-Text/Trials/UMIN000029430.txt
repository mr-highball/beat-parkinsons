Title: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel Group Comparison Study on Effects of Daily Ingestion of Plant Powder on Bowel Movements
Registry identifier: UMIN000029430
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000033607
Format: Selected official registry fields paraphrased for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public posting: 2017-10-06
Status: Completed. The 2019-02-28 registry version lists results as unpublished.
Design: Plant granules versus placebo for two weeks, 72 target participants with low bowel-movement frequency. Bowel frequency is primary. Stool characteristics and symptom ratings are secondary.
Linked original: Nishihira et al. 2019, DOI 10.31989/ffhd.v9i5.603, explicitly cites this registration and identifies the plant as Kumaizasa bamboo (Sasa senanensis), not Perilla. It describes a related dose-ranging trial under UMIN000024504.
