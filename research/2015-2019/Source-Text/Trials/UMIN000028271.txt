Title: Effects of carotenoid on exercise performance
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000032357
Identifier: UMIN000028271
First public disclosure: July 28, 2017
Coverage: Selected official registry fields and original report scope.

The registry specifies an eight-week placebo comparison using a paprika carotenoid supplement in young exercising men. Secondary fatigue was planned on a visual analog scale. The linked report describes 21 participants across the two arms and gives respiratory outcomes without a separately reported fatigue contrast in its accessible text.
