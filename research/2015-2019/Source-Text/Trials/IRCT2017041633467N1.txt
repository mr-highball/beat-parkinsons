The Arak University registry describes a completed randomized triple-blind parallel trial with a placebo and 64 planned participants. The primary outcome is IBS symptoms on the Gastrointestinal Symptom Rating Scale, measured before and four weeks after treatment. There is no separate registered constipation-domain endpoint. The 2018 report gives the constipation domain within Table 4. Registration followed the listed first enrollment date. The two records describe one comparison. The later three-arm study cites a separate registration.
