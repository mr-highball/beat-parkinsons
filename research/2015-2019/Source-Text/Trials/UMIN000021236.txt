Title: Neuroscientific study of aromatherapy using magnetoencephalography
Registry: UMIN000021236
Receipt: R000020500
First posted: 2016-03-01
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000020500
Official mirror: https://rctportal.mhlw.go.jp/detail/um?trial_id=UMIN000021236
Coverage: Factual English summary of registry intervention and outcome fields.

The randomized crossover planned a total of ten participants across healthy volunteers, people with epilepsy and people with Parkinson disease. It proposes exposure to grapefruit essential-oil aroma, lavender oil aroma and a synthetic lavender preparation. The planned outcomes are immediate brain-signal changes and subjective states such as calm, relaxation and alertness, with short-window adverse responses as a secondary outcome. The official record was last modified June 10, 2018. The mirror was updated June 11 and lists recruitment as closed with the study continuing. No results or Parkinson subgroup denominator were visible at the November 19, 2026 UTC check. Inhalation and planned short-window outcomes leave raw fruit and resting-tremor severity outside the intervention.
