2019 sesame presscake protocol

Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03826121
Coverage: Official trial protocol, factual extract.

First public February 1, 2019. This completed randomized trial assigned sesame oil cake extract versus placebo and registered computerized visual memory and learning outcomes. There were no results posted in the registry at the checked snapshot. The original 2021 article reported results and stated that attention was not assessed. Presscake extract differs from toasted sesame oil.
