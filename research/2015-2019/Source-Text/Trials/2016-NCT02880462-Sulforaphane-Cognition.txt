Official sulforaphane trial registration

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02880462

First posted August 26, 2016. The completed three-arm schizophrenia registry describes sulforaphane tablets at two doses and a starch placebo with the MATRICS battery. Actual enrollment was 172 and the registry planned a 24-week outcome time. The later Hei paper describes the same arm and enrollment structure and 22 weeks of treatment. The exact identifier is not displayed in that original article. Treat the registry and paper as a probable trial family. The registry does not supply a raw broccoli or Parkinson result.
