Sardine-enriched Diet for Prevention Type 2 Diabetes

Older participants with prediabetes were assigned a sardine or control diet. The sardine intervention specified 200 g per week. The record lists new type 2 diabetes, adiponectin, gut microbiota, gene expression, metabolomics, glucose homeostasis, insulin and insulin resistance outcomes. The record does not list fatigue.
