Title: Clinical Evaluation of Herbal Patch for Overactive Bladder Patients
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov
URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03532789
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03532789
Registry ID: NCT03532789
First posted: 2018-05-22
Status: Completed. No summary results were posted in the record retrieved 2026-11-13.
Coverage: selected public registry fields

Design: randomized parallel comparison of herbal and placebo patches in 66 participants with overactive bladder at EnChuKong Hospital, New Taipei City. Primary outcome was the Overactive Bladder Symptom Score. Secondary outcomes include the Urgency Severity Scale and patient perception of bladder condition. The registry gives two-week time points and names cinnamon in the study summary.
Outcome report citing this registration: PMID 33091856. The article identifies cinnamon in the active patch and includes a four-week follow-up after the two-week treatment assessment.
