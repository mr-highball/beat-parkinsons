Title: Feasibility of Acute and Chronic Concentrated Beetroot Juice Supplementation (NCT03129178)
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov
URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03129178
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03129178
Registry ID: NCT03129178
First published: 2019
Authors: ClinicalTrials.gov Study Record (Results Posted)
Journal: ClinicalTrials.gov
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Completed crossover feasibility trial (n=23 recruited; n=10 per arm analyzed) comparing 140 mL acute + 70 mL/d x 2 wk concentrated beetroot juice vs nitrate-depleted placebo; posted descriptive perceived pain means at baseline (0.2+-0.6 vs 0.2+-0.6) and acute visit (0.3+-0.8 vs 0.2+-0.6) with no formal statistical comparison.
