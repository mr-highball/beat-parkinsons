OFFICIAL TITLE

A Phase 1, Double Blind, Sponsor Open, Randomized, Placebo-controlled, Single Ascending Dose Study To Investigate The Safety, Tolerability, Pharmacokinetics And Pharmacodynamics Of Pf-06649751 In Subjects With Idiopathic Parkinson's Disease

BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a study with two sequential cohorts, each with three treatment periods. Single
doses of PF-06649751 will be tested in this study, starting at a low dose and escalating
to a dose projected to be under the current limits for drug concentration. Each cohort
will aim to achieve approximately 9 completers. Primary endpoint is safety and
tolerability, secondary endpoint is MDS-UPDRS part III.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  L-DOPA-responsiveness

    -  Hoehn & Yahr Stage II-III inclusive

    -  Experiencing motor fluctuations

    -  Stable daily dose of L-DOPA of at least 300 mg

    -  Females on non-childbearing potential and male subjects

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  History of troublesome dyskinesias

    -  History of surgical intervention for Parkinson's disease
