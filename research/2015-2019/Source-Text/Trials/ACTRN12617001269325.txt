Title: Swallowing skill training in neurodegenerative disease
Publication date: 2017-09-01 (first official registration date)
Record: ACTRN12617001269325
Source: https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=12617001269325
Text coverage: Official ANZCTR record, distilled.

A single-group study examined swallow skill training in people with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis or Huntington disease. The registry lists 54 planned participants and 38 actual participants. The program evaluated swallowing function and swallowing-related quality of life. Secondary outcomes include pharyngeal pressure, transit time, penetration or aspiration and hyolaryngeal excursion. There was no oil assignment. The registry supplies neither a safflower exposure nor a comparative safflower result.
