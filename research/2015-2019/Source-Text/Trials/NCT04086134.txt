Official overall status: UNKNOWN. No posted results at the November 15, 2026 check.

The study registers a three-arm food intervention for functional constipation. Primary outcome is stool weight. Secondary assessments include gastrointestinal symptoms on GSRS and PAC-SYM. The protocol does not isolate raw apricot or a Parkinson cohort.
