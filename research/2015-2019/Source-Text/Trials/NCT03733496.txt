REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Incidence of Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (Safety and Tolerability)
    Description: Long-term safety of VY-AADC01 as measured by Adverse Events and Serious Adverse Events
    Time frame: Up to 8 years from VY-AADC01 administration
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in AADC enzyme activity
    Description
      Continued expression of AADC as measured by [18F]-fluorodopa (F-Dopa) positron emission
      tomography (PET)
    Time frame: Up to 8 years from VY-AADC01 administration
  -
    Measure: Changes in PD medications
    Description: Change from baseline in PD medications, measured as levodopa equivalent dose
    Time frame: Up to 8 years from VY-AADC01 administration
  -
    Measure: Changes in motor function as assessed by Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS)
    Description
      Changes from baseline in motor function as assessed by UPDRS, in both "Off" and "On"
      medication states
    Time frame: Up to 8 years from VY-AADC01 administration
  -
    Measure: Changes in "Off" and "On" time as recorded by the participant in Parkinson's Disease (PD) diary
    Description
      Changes from baseline based on the PD Diary: "OFF" time; "ON" time without troublesome
      dyskinesia; "ON" time without dyskinesia; "ON" time with troublesome dyskinesia; and "ON"
      time with non-troublesome dyskinesia
    Time frame: Up to 8 years from VY-AADC01 administration
