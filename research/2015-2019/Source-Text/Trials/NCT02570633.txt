Registration: NCT02570633, Ginger Capsules for the Prophylactic Treatment of Migraine
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02570633
First posted: 2015-10-07
Coverage: Official protocol/status fields rechecked November 15, 2026.

Status completed. The registry compares a ginger extract capsule with cellulose. It names migraine attack frequency and migraine impact, serum biomarkers and resting energy expenditure. It does not list an anxiety-specific measure or a results table. The associated published article (DOI 10.1177/0333102419869319) reports the Beck Anxiety Inventory and cites this registration.
