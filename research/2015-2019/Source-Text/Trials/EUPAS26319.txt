Title: Domperidone Use and Misuse in Parkinson Disease (DUMP)
Registry ID: EUPAS26319
EMA study ID: 33284
Source: https://catalogues.ema.europa.eu/node/2154/administrative-details
Registry export: https://catalogues.ema.europa.eu/print/pdf/node/2154
Date basis: First published 2018-10-29 according to the official program export. The current catalogue version was last updated 2024-04-02.
Coverage: Selected original program and linkage fields.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The umbrella program describes French administrative data analysis, a cross-sectional clinical study, and a survey of neurologists. The registration documents prescribing, clinical indications and safety scope. It does not present a ginger intervention.

The cross-sectional component matches NCT04653584 and the 2025 primary publication PMID 40062736. The public catalogue retains the parent program's planned status. The ClinicalTrials.gov component was administratively terminated and nevertheless has the separately published 1,579-person report. The published report's separate front-matter NCT02850705 refers to a different registry.
