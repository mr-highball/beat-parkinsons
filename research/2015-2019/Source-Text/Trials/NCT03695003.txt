The completed parallel-group study registered 94 actual participants and compared a mixed sage product branded Cognivia with visually matched placebo capsules. Healthy adults ages 30 to 60 are described in the trial summary. Registered outcomes include Bond–Lader mood, state and trait anxiety, cognitive performance and a prospective-memory task. The registry has no posted summary results.

PMID 33466627 reports 94 healthy adults of the same age range, the identical branded product, placebo and key task and time-point structure. The original publication did not cite this NCT number, and no journal citation was attached to the current registry. These overlapping features support a probable study-family link. Keep one combined family and mark its exact registry linkage uncertain. The formulation combines common-sage extract and Spanish-sage essential oil. The primary article reported no clear mood effect, and neither record includes a clinical Parkinson apathy measure.
