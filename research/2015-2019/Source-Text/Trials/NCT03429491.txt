Title: Novel Nutrition Solutions for Sarcopenia
Registry identifier: NCT03429491
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03429491
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03429491
First public registration: 2018-02-12
Retrieved: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled from the official registration and linked report.

This randomized 24-week NUTRIMAL trial enrolled 108 older adults at risk of sarcopenia. It compared a control drink, a leucine-enriched protein drink and leucine-enriched protein plus long-chain omega-3. Appendicular muscle mass was the primary outcome. Secondary measures include knee extension and physical performance. The registration does not list a recurrent falls endpoint. As of retrieval it has no posted numerical results. The main publication identifies high-oleic sunflower plus corn oil in both the control and protein-only drinks. The main report, interindividual analysis and oxylipin analysis are connected trial reports.
