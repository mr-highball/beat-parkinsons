Title: The Integrate program for safe mobility in Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=376891
Registry ID: ACTRN12619000415101
First public: 2019-03-13
Coverage: Selected original Australian New Zealand Clinical Trials Registry fields.

The registry lists a tailored six-month, home-based mobility program. Prespecified primary measures include the Short Physical Performance Battery and Goal Attainment Scale. The current registry marks recruitment completed and shows the last record update on June 20, 2024. The study record describes a broader randomized evaluation. The later Allen feasibility report, PMID 40090322, gives within-person comparisons for its analyzed intervention cohort. Its published comparisons do not establish a controlled fall effect from any named food.
