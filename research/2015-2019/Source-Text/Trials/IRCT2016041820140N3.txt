Title: The effect of olive oil consumption on anthropometric indices, blood pressure, inflammatory status and oxidative stress in patients with depression
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/17869
Record: IRCT2016041820140N3
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting or registration: 2016-05-14

Retrospectively registered parallel, randomized, double-blind comparison in Shiraz, planned for 73 adults aged 18–65 with major depression. Both oils were ingested at 25 mL/day for 52 days. The comparator was sunflower oil with no processing grade specified. The registry lists anthropometric indices, blood pressure and two serum markers as primary outcomes. It describes Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index monitoring as assessment of a potential confounder. Depression severity rating scales are not listed as outcomes.
