Text coverage: Selected official trial fields and crosswalk to primary articles.

The named ACID trial registered purified Medox anthocyanins and matched placebo, with quality of episodic memory as the primary measure. The current record lists 212 actual enrollees and unknown recruitment status. It posts no registry summary results. The linked 2020 protocol identifies the ingredients as bilberry and black currant extracts. PMID 36372613 reports 106 active and 100 placebo participants and has the relevant adverse-event table. The registration also links the separate cardiometabolic and neurogenesis analyses from the same parent trial. These publications do not count as independent preparations or cohorts. No raw-blueberry comparison is assigned.
