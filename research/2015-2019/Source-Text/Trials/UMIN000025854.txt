Source: UMIN Clinical Trials Registry UMIN000025854, Effect of the Test Food Containing Crocetin on the Quality of Sleep
Registration: UMIN000025854
Official registry: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000029714
Official government portal: https://rctportal.mhlw.go.jp/s/detail/um?trial_id=UMIN000025854
First public date: 2018-01-28. The registry records a 2017-01-26 registration entry date and anticipated start of 2017-01-28.
Coverage: Selected original registry fields, Japanese title, crossover, measurements and linked results.

The randomized placebo-controlled crossover of oral crocetin targets 30 healthy adults. The specified measures are SleepScope EEG and the OSA sleep inventory. The Japanese PSQI appears among screening measures. Status is complete. The registry lists DOI 10.1016/j.ctim.2018.09.003 as the results publication, corresponding to PMID 30477864, and gives no quantitative result in its designated result field. Registration disclosure, entry and projected study start are separate dates.
