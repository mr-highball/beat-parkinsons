Title: Effect of Melissa officinalis and Lavandula angustifolia products in treatment of depression
Registry ID: IRCT2014061718126N1
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/16535
First registered: 2015-06-04.
Text coverage: Official registry design, interventions and outcomes.

The record targets 60 participants and specifies separate oral lemon balm powder, lavender and fluoxetine arms for eight weeks, with Hamilton depression as an outcome. It was publicly posted while recruitment was reported as active.

Araj-Khodaei et al. identify the same registration in PMID32620104 (2020) and report 45 participants. The paper describes retrospective registration, which can occur while recruitment is still active. The target and reported sample are separate counts. This is a registry-to-publication link for one study. All arms are active comparators, and the result does not establish equivalence or the effect of brewed lemon balm in Parkinson disease.
