Title: The Effect of Vegetable Oil on Cognitive Functions of MCI Patients
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03727386
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03727386
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting: 2018-11-01
Last posted update: 2021-06-29
Status: Completed. Actual enrollment 34. No posted results.
Design: Randomized parallel comparison of sunflower or virgin coconut oil, replacing usual cooking oil for six months in adults with mild cognitive impairment. Parkinson’s and certain other neurological conditions were exclusions. Sunflower grade was unspecified.
Outcomes: Cooking-oil use and adherence as feasibility outcomes. Secondary measures include Trail Making Test B, overall cognition, memory, fluency, speed, attention, visuospatial memory and quality of life.
