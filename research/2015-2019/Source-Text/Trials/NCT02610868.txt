Title: Open-label and single-arm study of Myobloc in the treatment of troublesome sialorrhea in adults
Registry ID: NCT02610868
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02610868
Registration date: 2015-11-20
Results first posted: 2019-11-04
Coverage: Official protocol, participant flow and posted results metadata.

The completed registry identifies 187 adults in one open-label treatment arm. Primary outcomes covered general and dental adverse events, other prespecified safety events, and a standardized behavioral safety assessment. Unstimulated salivary flow and investigator-rated drooling frequency and severity were separate secondary outcomes. The participant-flow table records 187 starting the first session, 177 the second, 50 the third and 24 the fourth.

The 2025 OPTIMYST article cites this registry and uses the same 187 participants. Later-session outcomes use the remaining subsets. The study assigned a prescription treatment and supplied no dietary comparator.
