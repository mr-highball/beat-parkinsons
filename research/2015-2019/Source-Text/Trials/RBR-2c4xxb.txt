Title: Effects of grape juice consumption improvements in functionality and cognition of individuals with Parkinson's disease who perform aquatic physiotherapy
Registry ID: RBR-2c4xxb
Source: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=RBR-2c4xxb
Primary registry: https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-2c4xxb
Registration date: 2019-10-31 in the WHO record. The same record lists first enrollment as 2019-08-01.
Archive scope: Official WHO export of Brazilian registry fields and linkage to two original reports. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

The registry states recruitment completed and describes two randomized groups of 13 with crossover between conditions. The two publications describe parallel aquatic exercise and aquatic exercise plus grape juice groups. The planned outcomes cover BDNF, oxidative stress, cognition and function. The inspected public results field is blank. Keep the registered design and reported analyses separate.

The 2020 paper cites this accession and shows 24 randomized and 19 analyzed. A 2021 paper cites the same accession, describes 24 included and gives conflicting completion and table counts. The product composition also differs across the printed reports. Treat them as a linked study family with participant and product overlap unresolved.

Publications: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32798568/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7887390/
