Title: ODYSSEI registration for adjunctive istradefylline and dyskinesia onset in Parkinson disease

Coverage: Factual extract from the official UMIN registration and history, checked 2026-11-19.

The history distinguishes record insertion on 2016-10-24 from first public disclosure on 2016-10-25. The latest UMIN version displayed is 13, dated 2019-07-05. The registry displays Terminated in the UMIN recruitment field and lists the secondary identifier jRCTs071180014. The linked Japanese registry and the journal report provide completion and results for the same trial.

The registered comparison was open-label random allocation to istradefylline with optimized medical therapy or optimized therapy without it. The study population had Parkinson disease with levodopa wearing-off and no prior dyskinesia. Time to dyskinesia onset was the primary outcome. Secondary measures included time to troublesome dyskinesia and total MDS-UPDRS scores by part. The record does not compare ginger or another culinary preparation.
