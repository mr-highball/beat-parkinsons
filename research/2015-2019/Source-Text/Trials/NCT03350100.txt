BRIEF SUMMARY

The study assessed acute effects of two Saudi date cultivars on mood and cognition in healthy volunteers. The protocol used a Latin square to counterbalance treatment order, with a screening and training visit followed by three measurement visits at weekly intervals.

ARMS AND INTERVENTIONS

Experimental, Birhi date cultivar: 48.46 g freeze-dried date fruit powder, equivalent to 115 g fresh fruit, in 150 g low-fat yogurt.
Experimental, Khassab date cultivar: 34.5 g freeze-dried date fruit powder, equivalent to 115 g fresh fruit, in 150 g low-fat yogurt.
Placebo comparator: 150 g low-fat yogurt with added sugars and fiber to match the date-containing treatments. The arm text lists 14.72 g total sugar and 0.80 g fiber.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES

CogTrack tests: simple reaction time, digit vigilance, choice reaction time, numeric working memory, spatial working memory, immediate word recall, delayed word recall, word recognition, and pattern separation. Each is recorded as change from baseline at 45, 90 and 135 minutes post-dose.
Bond–Lader visual analogue mood scales: change from baseline at 45, 90 and 135 minutes post-dose.
Profile of Mood States: change across visits 1, 2 and 3.
Blood glucose by finger prick: change from baseline at 45, 90 and 135 minutes post-dose.
