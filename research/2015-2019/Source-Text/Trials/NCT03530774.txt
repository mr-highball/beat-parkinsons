Title: Supplemental Egg Protein Intervention in Older Adults
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03530774
Registry: NCT03530774
Evidence date: 2018-05-21
Date basis: ClinicalTrials.gov first public posting.
Registry update posted: 2024-12-20
Review date: 2026-11-24
Review basis: Official structured registry record with protocol and results-status fields.

The randomized comparison assigned a dried egg-white protein supplement or an isocaloric carbohydrate supplement to community-dwelling older adults with impaired strength or function. The registry lists 54 as actual enrollment and the trial as completed. Muscle mass, short physical performance battery and grip strength were registered primary outcomes. The secondary fall-risk outcome included recalled fall counts, medical attention and injury over six months, as well as a Falls Efficacy Scale measure of confidence. A fall count and confidence about falling are distinct outcomes. The official registry has no posted results.

The linked primary report (PMID 34730461) describes 29 older women allocated in its pilot comparison, 16 intervention and 13 control. Its public abstract provides no fall-frequency comparison. The registry and report belong to one evidence family, and the participant-flow difference requires the full report or study data to reconcile. This was a protein supplement in a non-Parkinson population. The registration preserves a specific follow-up route for Round 1 claim 0329 without supplying a clinical direction.
