PENSA isolated EGCG and lifestyle trial in older adults with subjective cognitive decline

Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03978052
Source credit: ClinicalTrials.gov
NCT ID: NCT03978052
First posted: 2019-06-06
Registry last update posted: 2024-03-15
Text coverage: research summary of registered groups and outcomes.

The trial randomized APOE-ε4 carriers with subjective cognitive decline to isolated epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG) or placebo on the same multimodal lifestyle intervention. A nonrandomized reference group received healthy lifestyle recommendations. Cognitive change was primary. The University of Pennsylvania Smell Identification Test (UPSIT) was an exploratory measure. The registry lists completion and no posted results. The 2020 methods paper (DOI 10.1002/trc2.12155) describes powder sachets. The final report describes the intervention as capsules and includes 12-month UPSIT results in its supplement. See research/2025-2029/Source-Text/2025-PENSA-EGCG-Olfaction.txt.
