Registry: DRKS00013219
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00013219
Date first registered: 2017-10-25, retrospective
Status: Complete
Design: Open-label, non-randomized controlled crossover
Primary outcome: Increase in urinary DHPMA and 2-/3-MCPD after controlled fat and oil consumption.
Secondary outcome: Basal urinary excretion of the same biomarkers before exposure.
Intervention: Hazelnut oil rich in bound 2-/3-MCPD and palm fat rich in glycidyl esters. Scheduled quantitative urine collection.
Results links: DOI 10.1002/mnfr.202000735 (PMID 33079463), DOI 10.1002/mnfr.202000736 (PMID 33112049).
Exposure and outcome boundary: No urinary urgency measure. Registry ethics EA4/139/17 differs from EA4/130/17 in first primary paper.
