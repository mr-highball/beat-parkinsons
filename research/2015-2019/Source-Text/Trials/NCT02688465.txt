BRIEF SUMMARY

Parkinson's disease is not only a pathology of movements. There are many non-motor
symptoms that complicate and impair patients' quality of life. Among those disorders are
sleep disorders.

Insomnia is the most frequent symptom. Most patients report 2-5 awakenings per night and
long periods of awakening which occupy 30 to 40% of their night.

Apomorphine is a dopamine agonist that may be administered with a pump . The objective of
the study is to assess changes in the quality of sleep in Parkinson's disease patients
treated with an apomorphine pump.
