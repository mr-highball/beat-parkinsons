Snapshot date: 2026-11-19

The prospective study compares standardized saffron extract and placebo in adults with unsatisfactory sleep. The planned trial is double-blind and uses 28 days of treatment and the Insomnia Severity Index as a clinical outcome. The original publication PMID 32056539 reports the trial results. The registry is a companion of that two-arm trial, distinct from the three-arm study first registered in September 2020.
