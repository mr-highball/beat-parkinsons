Title: Dark chocolate flavanols and cognition, FlaSeCo-study
Registration: ACTRN12617000748314
Source: https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=372569
ANZCTR request number: 372569
First posted: 2017-05-22
Last updated at source: 2017-05-22
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated official registration and outcome reconciliation

The prospective registration describes high- versus low-flavanol
dark chocolate over eight weeks in adults aged 65–75. The cognitive
tests include verbal fluency and Trail Making. The record also
lists a depression scale as a secondary outcome.

The registry still displays the recruitment status entered in
2017 and includes no later results update. The same study's 2018
P-445 poster and 2020 published authors' trial report identify a
completed cohort of 100. Trial publication provides the actual
outcome evidence. The registry and reports describe one study.
