Access evidence: WHO ICTRP trial record from IRCT, exact main identifier. Mirror says last refreshed 2018-02-22.

The registry describes a double-blind phase 3 comparison in fibromyalgia and lists a 50-participant recruitment target. It identifies pain, fibromyalgia impact and fatigue as primary outcomes. Planned fatigue uses the Global Fatigue Index at baseline and weeks 2, 4, 6 and 8. Recruitment on the mirror is marked complete. The results fields on the mirror are empty. The published article reports 54 participants randomized and lists 46 in Table 2.

Continuing evidence gaps
This registration and the Shakiba article are one study family. The record does not address Parkinson disease or include an untreated comparison. Use the published report for effect estimates.
