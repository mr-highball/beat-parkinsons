Title: DL-3-n-butylphthalide Therapy for Parkinson's Disease: A Randomized, Controlled Trial
Source: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=ChiCTR1800018892
Registry identifier: ChiCTR1800018892
Original registry route: https://www.chictr.org.cn/showproj.html?proj=31742
First public registration: 2018-10-15
Date basis: WHO ICTRP registration field. Listed first enrollment is 2014-09-01.
Text coverage: Factual extract of the official WHO ICTRP record.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The record identifies an interventional parallel randomized comparison of pharmaceutical DL-3-n-butylphthalide and no add-on in China. It marks registration as retrospective and recruitment as complete. The posted target counts are 43 for the drug arm and 60 for the comparator. The primary outcomes include UPDRS, cognitive, sleepiness, sleep quality, fatigue, depression, anxiety and quality-of-life scales. The primary outcome list repeats two entries and does not give separate rest-tremor, stride-length or freezing-of-gait measurements. No tabular clinical results or public protocol are posted in the recovered WHO record.

The corresponding 2019 article, PMID 30988766, reports 131 randomized and 103 with complete follow-up. Its reported follow-up arm counts of 43 and 60 match the retrospective registry targets. These matching counts therefore describe closely linked retrospective and publication records. The registry and article evaluate a pharmaceutical compound. Neither describes a celery stalk or leaf intervention. The different ChiCTR-IPR-16009395 registration remains distinct.
