Title: Effects of combination of meat intake and resistance training on risks of sarcopenia and metabolic syndrome
Registry ID: UMIN000038253
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000043607
First published: 2019-10-09, official registry first disclosure.
Coverage: Official registry factual snapshot, including the April 15, 2025 update and its posted result links.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

The registry describes a four-group comparison of meat intake and resistance training in older participants. It records 93 enrolled and lists two results papers, PMID38888088 and PMID39569460. The linked articles give the chicken serving and compare muscle and body-composition, vascular and microbiome measures. Both address the same participants and intervention assignments. No Parkinson fatigue assessment is identified.

The registry differs from UMIN000033042, which planned a pre-exercise, single-exposure crossover in healthy young adults and still lists results as unpublished. Track the separate protocols and populations when assessing cooked-chicken evidence.
