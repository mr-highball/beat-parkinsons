Activation levels of interested regions of the brain will be measured through fMRI
technique prior and after the dry needling and active stimulation applications.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: fMRI - Activation level comparison of the motor and supplementary cortex
    Time frame: Baseline and PostStim (right after the stimulation is terminated)
  -
    Measure: fMRI - Activation level comparison of the subthalamic nucleus (STN) and basal ganglia including pedunculopontine nucleus (PPN)
    Time frame: Baseline and PostStim (right after the stimulation is terminated)
  -
    Measure: fMRI - STN, PPN and cortical connectivity alterations
    Time frame: Baseline and PostStim (right after the stimulation is terminated)

  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Diagnosis of Parkinson's Disease

    -  Rigidity

    -  Walking disorder

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Cardiac Pacemaker

    -  Psychiatric diagnosis

    -  Irregular heart/respiration rate

    -  Pregnancy

    -  Alcohol consumption

    -  Cardiovascular disease history
