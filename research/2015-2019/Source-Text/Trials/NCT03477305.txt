Title: BerryCare: A Blackberry Extension Lesson Series to Facilitate Community Engagement and Phytonutrient Intake
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03477305
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03477305
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13
First public posting: 2018-03-26
Last public update: 2023-01-19
Status: Completed. Study start 2017-01-18. Primary and study completion 2018-09-26. Results unposted.
Design: Single-group, open-label, University of Kentucky-led community engagement study with actual enrollment of 38 and five listed Kentucky sites. Adults aged 18 or older could participate. Physical or mental incapacitation excluded participation.
Intervention: Blackberry consumption in the setting of instruction on gardening, fruit harvesting, homegrown fresh berries and recipes.
Primary outcome: Blackberry consumption at baseline, six, 12 and 18 months.
Secondary outcomes: Physical function, blood pressure, lipids, hemoglobin A1c, carotenoids, weight, waist circumference, knowledge, reported fruit and vegetable intake and group cohesion at the same time points. The record does not specify the physical function instrument.
Individual participant data: No plan to share.
Related report: Koempel and colleagues 2023, DOI 10.47991/2835-6764/AJSER-140, presents 28 baseline participants from one rural senior center and describes the eight-foot walk.
