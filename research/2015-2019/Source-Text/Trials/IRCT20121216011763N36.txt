Source: https://irct.ir/trial/35715
First registration: 2019-01-25, retrospective.
Coverage: Selected factual registry fields.

Migraine study with planned start 2018-08-23, actual recruitment 2018-09-08 through 2018-11-14, and completion 2019-01-13. Primary measures include VAS pain severity, frequency, duration and MIDAS. The registry lists 44 participants. The 2020 paper reports 50 randomized and 43 analyzed. The 2024 disability article reports the same study.
