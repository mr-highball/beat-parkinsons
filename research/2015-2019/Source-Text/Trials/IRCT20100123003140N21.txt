Title: Effect of omega-3 fatty acid supplementation on inflammatory factors and lipid profile in Parkinson's disease
Registry identifier: IRCT20100123003140N21
Public registry pages: https://irct.ir/trial/29861 and https://fa.irct.ir/trial/29861
Coverage: Original factual extract of verified bilingual registry records and selected fields. Native IRCT searches and detail fetch recovered both versions.
First registration displayed: 2018-05-05 (1397/02/15), retrospective. Last listed update same day, update count zero.
Planned recruitment: 2017-04-18 through 2017-06-19. Actual recruitment dates and trial completion date blank. Recruitment marked complete.
Ethics: Tabriz University of Medical Sciences, IR.TBZMED.REC.1396.1244. Listed approval date 2018-03-11.
Registrant: Bahram Pourghassem Gargari, Health and Nutrition Faculty, Tabriz University of Medical Sciences.
Recruiting center listed: Imam Reza Hospital neurology clinic, Tabriz, Iran.
Design: Parallel, simple randomized study. Participants, clinicians and investigators masked according to the narrative.
Population: Planned 44 people with Parkinson's disease aged 50–70, 22 per assigned arm.
Control: Two capsules daily containing soybean oil, supplied by Zahravi Pharmaceutical Company. No processing grade, oil mass, or study lot stated.
Intervention: Two omega-3 capsules daily, each recorded as EPA 180 mg plus DHA 120 mg, also from Zahravi.
Primary outcomes at start and end: Serum IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α, IL-10, triglycerides, HDL cholesterol and LDL cholesterol.
Secondary outcomes: Dietary questionnaires, RAPA physical activity at baseline only, anthropometry, BMI and baseline serum albumin.
Symptom outcomes: No repeated walking, gait freezing, Parkinson motor examination, falls, apathy, sleepiness, swallowing, salivation, tremor, off time, dyskinesia, dystonia or voice endpoint was registered.
Reported results: None in these registry records.
Publication trail: Mohammadi et al. (2023), DOI 10.1016/j.nupar.2023.06.001. Same investigator, planned n44, capsule count and soybean comparator, and matching biomarker set. Abstract lists trial duration eight weeks and per capsule EPA 120 mg plus DHA 180 mg. The registry and abstract reverse the EPA and DHA allocation. IL-1β was registered and is absent from the public abstract's outcome list.
