BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a prospective observational study investigating the utility of 7 Tesla MRI to
quantify nigrosome1 signal in a cohort of individuals with recent onset Parkinson's
disease and in at-risk cohorts at a premotor state of Parkinson's disease.
