REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in "Off" Periods From Start of Treatment to Week 104
    Description
      "Off" periods describe those times when medication is not working well and a person has
      greater difficulty with movement.
    Time frame: 104 weeks
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Time-dependent Change in "Off" Time and "On" Time Accompanied By Dyskinesia Interfering with Daily Activities
    Description
      "Off" periods describe those times when medication is not working well and a person has
      greater difficulty with movement, and "On" periods describe when medication is working
      well and symptoms are controlled.
    Time frame: 104 Weeks
  -
    Measure: Time-dependent Change in Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS)
    Description: Motor evaluation
    Time frame: 104 Weeks
  -
    Measure: Time-dependent Change in Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire - 8 Items (PDQ-8)
    Description
      The PDQ-8 is a quality of life questionnaire for PD patient. This short-form version
      derived from the PDQ-39 includes 1 question from each of the domains on the PDQ-39
      (mobility, ADLs, emotional well-being, stigma, social support, cognitions, communication,
      and bodily discomfort).
    Time frame: 104 weeks
  -
    Measure: "Off" Period and "On" Period Accompanied by Dyskenesia Interfering with Daily Activities by Patient Characteristic Factor
    Description
      "Off" periods describe those times when medication is not working well and a person has
      greater difficulty with movement, and "On" periods describe when medication is working
      well and symptoms are controlled.
    Time frame: 104 weeks
  -
    Measure: Time-dependent Change in Parkinson's Disease Quality of Life Questionnaire - Carers (PDQ-Carer)
    Description
      The PDQ-Carer is a 29 item measure of health related quality of life for use with carers
      of people with Parkinson's disease.
    Time frame: 104 weeks
