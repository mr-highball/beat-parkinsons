Title: Children Food Neophobia - a Playful Intervention at a Kindergarten
Registry: NCT03513081
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03513081
First posted: 2018-05-01
Status: Completed as displayed on 2026-11-19 UTC
Text coverage: Official protocol, enrollment, outcomes and posted-results status.

The registration gives actual enrollment of 82 participants and lists children's food neophobia and eating behavior as primary outcomes. Education, repeated exposure and a reward for tasting vegetables are parts of the intervention. The register has no posted results. The separate conference abstract, P179 of PMC6157254, reports liking and intake observations, names the vegetables and carries internally inconsistent arm counts. The registry and poster are one study family. The population was children in kindergarten, and the registration contains no Parkinson or gait outcome.
