BRIEF SUMMARY

Impact of unilateral modulation of deep brain stimulation of the subthalamic nucleus on
freezing of gait in Parkinson's disease

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Unilateral modulation of subthalamic stimulation and its impact on freezing of gait in
Parkinson's disease will be studied.

In a cross-over, doubleblind, randomized study, a 30% reduction of stimulation intensity
of the less impacted side will be compared with chronic treatment with regard to
frequency and duration of freezing of gait episodes.
