Title: Effect of Potato Fiber on Appetite and Fecal Fat Excretion
Registry: NCT02957318
First posted: 2016-11-07
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02957318
Status: Completed; no posted results in the registry as checked 2026-11-19 UTC.
Text coverage: Distilled official protocol, enrollment and outcomes.

The registry lists 18 participants and compares two potato-derived fiber preparations and a control. The primary outcomes are fecal fat excretion and subjective appetite sensations. The conference abstract T1P158 and the full paper PMID 33203008 represent this crossover study. None assigns roasted potato or measures insomnia.
