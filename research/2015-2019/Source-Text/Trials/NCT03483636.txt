ClinicalTrials.gov describes a randomized crossover in healthy volunteers. The primary outcomes compare postural sway and computerized attention after drug and alcohol exposures. Secondary measures include memory and drug kinetics. The current registration lists actual enrolment of 24 and has no posted results. The journal and conference reports provide results under their own reported denominators.

The cranberry-beverage detail appears in the 2019 conference report. The registration does not evaluate dried cranberry, Parkinson disease, or depression.
