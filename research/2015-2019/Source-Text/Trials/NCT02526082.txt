Title: Long-term Follow-up of the Helsinki Businessmen Study
Registry: NCT02526082
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02526082
First posted: 2015-08-18 (registry labels the date estimated)
Status: Active, not recruiting as displayed on 2026-11-19 UTC
Text coverage: Official study design, registered primary outcome and posted-results status.

The cohort registration lists 3,490 participants and national-register total mortality as its primary outcome. The study is observational. The registry has no posted results. The 2018 O-32 meeting abstract (PMC7091737) and the separately published 970-person longevity analysis (PMID 31612429) report subsets of this cohort. They share the 1974 exposure measurements and March 2018 mortality follow-up. They provide no assigned coffee preparation and no Parkinson fatigue result.
