BRIEF SUMMARY

Patients with Parkinson's disease (PD) often display symptoms, such as constipation, due
to denervation of the cholinergic nerves in the gut. It has been hypothesized that PD
initiates in the gut years prior to diagnosis. To gain a detailed understanding of the
early stages of PD, techniques for quantification of cholinergic nerves are needed. The
PET tracer 18F-FEOBV binds specifically to the vesicular acetylcholine transporter,
situated in presynaptic cholinergic nerve terminals. The investigators will investigate
18F-FEOBV uptake in the brain and internal organs of 15 patients with moderate-stage PD
and compare to 15 healthy controls. Furthermore, findings are correlated to validated
clinical tests of the autonomic nervous system. The aim is to validate 18F-FEOBV PET/CT
as a clinical imaging modality to diagnose parasympathetic denervation in PD.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Density of vesicular acetylcholine transporter
    Description
      Average density will be determined in each group for intestine, pancreas, suprarenal
      gland, heart and relevant areas of the brain. Group differences will be calculated
    Time frame: Day 1
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Correlation to measurements of gastrointestinal denervation
    Description
      Density of vesicular acetylcholine transporter will be correlated to symptoms of
      gastrointestinal autonomic denervation (constipation, gastroparesis).
    Time frame: Day 1
  -
    Measure: Correlation to measurements of dysautonomia
    Description
      Density of vesicular acetylcholine transporter will be correlated to measurements of
      dysautonomia (orthostatic hypotension, colon transit time)
    Time frame: Day 1
  -
    Measure: Correlation to measurements of neuropsychological tests
    Description
      Cerebral density of vesicular acetylcholine transporter is correlated with
      neuropsychological test scores.
    Time frame: Day 1

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Parkinsons disease, disease duration > 4 years, Hoehn & Yahr stage 2-3

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Significant neurological diseases (e.g. stroke, tumor)

    -  Significant psychiatric disease

    -  Previous major surgery on thoracic and/or abdominal organs

    -  Significant medical disease (e.g. heart- or liver failure)

    -  Inflammatory bowel disease

    -  Gluten intolerance

    -  Diabetes

    -  Magnetic Resonance (MR) contraindications (metal in body, claustrophobia)

    -  Allergy to CT-contrast fluid

    -  Pacemaker

    -  Any current or previous cancer involving internal organs

    -  Peripheral neuropathy (except Parkinsons disease related)
