NCT02364830 | Enteric-coated anise oil in irritable bowel syndrome
Official source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02364830
First public posting and latest update: 2015-02-18
Retrieved: 2026-11-13. Selected official registry fields summarized.

The completed Shiraz University of Medical Sciences trial reports an actual enrollment of 120 patients with irritable bowel syndrome. It compares enteric-coated anise oil capsules, peppermint oil and placebo. The registered composite includes quality of life, pain, gastrointestinal symptoms, headache and tiredness. The results tab has no posted data. The investigators reported gastrointestinal outcomes in PMID 27815079 and depression outcomes in PMID 26873392.
