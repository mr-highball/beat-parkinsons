Title: Citrus Beverages and Gastrointestinal Function
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02979496
First posted: December 1, 2016
Latest record posted: October 16, 2018
Retrieved: November 15, 2026
Coverage: Selected official registry fields.

The registry lists controls and two orange pomace levels, with stool frequency as a primary outcome. A change in constipation symptom score is among the registered bowel symptom outcomes. The latest record lists the trial as completed and contains no posted results. The linked 2019 report details a two-arm healthy adult comparison. No raw sweet orange or Parkinson-specific outcome was registered.
