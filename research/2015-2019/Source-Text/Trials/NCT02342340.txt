Registered pinto crossover outcomes and absence of sleep measure
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02342340
Registration: NCT02342340
Coverage: official ClinicalTrials.gov v2 record, selected public intervention, outcome and results fields

The registry gives January 19, 2015 as its first public posting and May 13, 2016 as its last posted update, both labeled estimated. The completed study lists eight participants and no posted results. The planned primary outcome covers phytochemical absorption, metabolism and excretion. Secondary measures include pulse-wave arterial stiffness and satiety. Sleep and insomnia are absent from the archived array of registered outcomes. The institutional thesis describes actual boiled pinto consumption during the crossover. That preparation does not link to an insomnia outcome or a Parkinson cohort.
