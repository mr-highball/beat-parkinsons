Registered Brazilian diet and cold-pressed olive oil trial
Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02463435
Registration: NCT02463435
Coverage: Factual official registration fields.

NCT02463435 was first posted June 4, 2015. The current record lists three active arms, including olive oil with and without a diet intervention, and HADS as a secondary change outcome. The live record had no posted results. The original registry history would be needed to place the addition of HADS. The later journal outcomes belong to this trial.
