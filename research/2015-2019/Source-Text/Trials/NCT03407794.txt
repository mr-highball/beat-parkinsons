Title: Effects of Fermented Vegetables on Gut Microflora and Inflammation in Women
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03407794
NCT ID: NCT03407794
Format: Selected official ClinicalTrials.gov fields and posted results, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public posting: 2018-01-23
First results posting: 2023-01-11
Status: Completed. Registry lists 2018-01-07 to 2021-04-16. The journal report gives recruitment and completion in 2019.
Design: Usual diet, fermented vegetables or a nonfermented vegetable comparison, six weeks. Registry lists cabbage among several supplied options without identifying cultivar. There is no isolated red cabbage assignment.
Secondary result: Week-six median systolic pressure in mmHg (lower to upper quartile) was 104 (89–111) usual diet, 121 (108–122) fermented and 107 (104–121) nonfermented, with denominators 10, 10 and 11. The registry supplies no treatment contrast or orthostatic measure for this endpoint.
Linked publication: Galena et al., PMID 36201455, Table 3 labels 10 usual-diet, 11 fermented and 10 pickled participants. This arm-denominator discrepancy is unresolved.
