The intervention describes an isocaloric diet with canola oil and a canola-oil spread in centrally obese shift workers. The comparator continued a usual diet. The record names body composition, blood pressure, several metabolic measures and melatonin. A shift-work sleep disturbance label appears among the conditions. The registered outcome list gives no specific measure of daytime sleepiness or dream enactment.

The record lists 40 estimated participants and unknown status. No results were posted as of November 13, 2026 UTC.
