Title: Efficacy and Safety of Hou Gu Mi Xi in Patients With Spleen Qi Deficiency and Radical Gastrectomy for Gastric Cancer
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03025152
NCT ID: NCT03025152
Format: Selected official fields and original protocol comparison.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public posting: 2017-01-19 (estimated by registry)
Status: Terminated. Enrollment 130. Registry cites 37.7% attrition at one-year follow-up. No results posted.
Comparison: Hou Gu Mi Xi with rice-and-oat placebo. Registry narrative says perilla leaf was added in adapting the formulation. The linked 2019 protocol, PMID 31182140, Table 1 omits Perilla and includes Platycodon. Registered arm descriptions and protocol also differ on the amount of product.
Outcome scope: The planned composite includes abnormal stools and difficulty defecating. It gives no observed constipation endpoint. The formulation actually consumed and item-level data are unresolved.
