Title: Effect of drugs associated with gastric acid on concentration of levodopa preparations in Parkinson's disease
Registration: UMIN000036494
Registry receipt: R000041582
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000041582
First disclosed: 2019-05-01
Last declared modification: 2021-10-14 version 2
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated authoritative English and Japanese registry fields

The open, nonrandomized, self-controlled trial lists a target of
30 people with Parkinson disease already taking medicines that
affect gastric pH. The registered intervention is 14 days of
discontinuation of such medicine. The primary outcome is blood
levodopa concentration. The public record shows recruitment
ended and results unpublished. It registers neither citrus fruit
nor a separately measured resting tremor outcome.

The design differs from the older lemon-juice report and from
studies that added an acid-suppressing drug. A result publication
with this exact trial identifier remains unverified.
