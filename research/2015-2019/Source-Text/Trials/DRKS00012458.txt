DRKS lists prospective registration, the same planned and actual start on 2017-07-15 and completion on 2017-12-31. The target and final sample were 75 adults with Parkinson disease at a single Cologne center. Local ethics reference 17-194. This was a non-interventional study. Participants recorded whether dry mouth, dysphagia or excessive saliva were present each hour along with activities including eating or taking medication.

The primary registry question was the correlation among the three symptoms. The secondary question concerned factors associated with their occurrence. Food identity and ingredient-specific effect were not listed as assigned interventions or results. The July 2021 record update points to PMID 30028552. The register does not post its own results tables or protocol attachments.
