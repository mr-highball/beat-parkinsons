The mechanistic review compares aggregation and interactions of amyloid beta, tau, alpha-synuclein and TDP-43. It mentions visual hallucinations to characterize dementia with Lewy bodies and Parkinson dementia. Its comparisons concern tissue pathology and model findings, without food intake or visual episode rates.
