Title: Nitrate-Rich Vegetables Increase Plasma Nitrate and Nitrite Concentrations and Lower Blood Pressure in Healthy Adults.
PMID: 27075914
DOI: 10.3945/jn.116.229807
Registry ID: NCT02271633
First published: 2016
Authors: Jonvik KL, Nyakayiru J, Pinckaers PJ, Senden JM, van Loon LJ, Verdijk LB
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27075914/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Semirandomized acute crossover trial (Jonvik et al., 2016, J Nutr, NCT02271633) in 18 healthy normotensive young adults (11 men, 7 women; mean age 28 +/- 1 y) comparing four beverages matched to 800 mg (~12.9 mmol) dietary nitrate: sodium nitrate, beetroot juice, rocket salad (arugula) beverage, and spinach beverage. Rocket salad beverage raised plasma nitrate (peak 584 +/- 24 umol/L) and nitrite (peak 643 +/- 63 nmol/L) and lowered resting systolic blood pressure at 150 min (122 +/- 3 to 116 +/- 2 mmHg, p=0.007) and resting diastolic blood pressure at 150 min (p<0.05) and 300 min (p=0.045). Limits: seated/resting blood pressure in healthy young adults without standing orthostatic challenge, lightheadedness ratings, or Parkinson's patients; liquefied beverage rather than raw salad leaves.
