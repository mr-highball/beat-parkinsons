Chompoopong and colleagues studied germinated brown-rice pretreatment in rats exposed to rotenone and used 4-phenylbutyric acid as positive control. Open-field ambulation improved, while mobility duration did not. The brown-rice group gave shorter direction of effect for pole orientation and descent, but the only arm reported as having significant improvement in both times was the positive control. No numeric pole estimates or cooking information are available from this reading.
