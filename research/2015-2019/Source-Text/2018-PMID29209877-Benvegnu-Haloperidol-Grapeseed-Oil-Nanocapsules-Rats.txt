Title: Effects of Fish and Grape Seed Oils as Core of Haloperidol-Loaded Nanocapsules on Oral Dyskinesia in Rats.
PMID: 29209877
DOI: 10.1007/s11064-017-2444-0
First published: 2018
Authors: Benvegnú DM, Roversi K, Barcelos RCS, Trevizol F, Pase CS, Segat HJ, Dias VT, Savian AL
Journal: Neurochemical research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29209877/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Preclinical 28-day experiment in rats injected intraperitoneally with haloperidol-loaded lipid-core nanocapsules using grape seed oil, fish oil, or caprylic/capric triglycerides vs free haloperidol. Evaluated drug-delivery vehicle effect on haloperidol-induced oral dyskinesia. Parenteral antipsychotic nanocapsule excipient in rats (exposure_fit unusable for oral culinary grapeseed oil).
