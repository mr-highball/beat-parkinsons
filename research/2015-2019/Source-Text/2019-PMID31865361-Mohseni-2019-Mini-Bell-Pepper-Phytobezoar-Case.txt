Title: An Unusual Mimic of Intermittent Bowel Obstruction.
PMID: 31865361
PMCID: PMC6938214
DOI: 10.12659/AJCR.920621
First published: 2019
Authors: Mohseni M, Kruse B
Journal: The American journal of case reports
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31865361/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Clinical case report of a 42-year-old woman presenting with several months of intermittent right-sided abdominal pain and vomiting resolved after endoscopic removal of an undigested mini bell pepper phytobezoar near the terminal ileum. Mechanical foreign-body obstruction case, not functional PD constipation.
