Title: A Study of the Effects of Pistacia vera (Pistachio) Seed Oil on Working Memory as Well as Spatial Learning and Memory.
DOI: 10.22123/phj.2018.146958.1016
First published: 2019
Authors: Pak-Hashemi M, Hassanipour M, Mohammadinasab M, Kaeidi A, Shamsizadeh A, Hakimizadeh E, Allahtavakoli M, Fatemi I
Journal: Pistachio and Health Journal
Source: https://doi.org/10.22123/phj.2018.146958.1016
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Preclinical animal study published in a regional non-PubMed journal (Pistachio and Health Journal 2019;1:1-7; cited in Mandalari 2021 PMC8747606 Ref [73]) evaluating oral Pistacia vera seed oil (1 and 4 mL/kg for 21 days) in intact, healthy male Wistar rats (no PD or cognitive-impairment model) on the Y-maze continuous alternation task (short-term working memory) and Morris Water Maze (spatial learning/memory). Reported improved Y-maze alternation but strictly NULL effect on Morris Water Maze spatial memory. Cannot support a directional executive-function or attention lean for culinary pistachio oil in PD because: (1) intact non-PD, non-impaired rats (population_fit=unusable for PD deficit inference); (2) rodent spatial alternation memory is remote from human PD executive function/attention, with MWM null; and (3) full numerical tables, group n, oil processing grade, and p-values are unavailable in public OA records.
