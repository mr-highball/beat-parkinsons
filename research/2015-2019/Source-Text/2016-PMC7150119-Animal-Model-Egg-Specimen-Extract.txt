Embryonated chicken eggs are biological specimens in the reference discussion, not food assigned to patients. The source describes animal-model health and research contexts. A photo contributor named Melissa is not a lemon-balm intervention.

This preserves a derived biological-material lead while distinguishing oral food, preparation and clinical endpoint. The chapter supplies no poached-egg fall comparison or brewed-lemon-balm depression result. A whole original chapter and its separate underlying experiments must not be counted as independent trials.
