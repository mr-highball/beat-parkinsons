Baked flaxseed cookies and Rome III constipation
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30661699/
PMID: 30661699
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnesp.2018.11.002
Registration: IRCT20110416006202N2
Coverage: Original author abstract, first published November 17, 2018

The original trial randomized 77 people with type 2 diabetes and constipation among baked flaxseed, psyllium or placebo cookies twice daily for 12 weeks. It describes 10 g flaxseed or psyllium mixed into cookies and reports change in Rome III symptoms favoring active cookie groups over placebo (p=.002). The author abstract identifies seed in baked cookies. A later OTC review uses the term flaxseed oil for this citation. Keep the cookie study separate from oil comparisons. The original names the Iranian registration.
