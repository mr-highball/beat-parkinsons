Topical pumpkin seed oil and aphthous ulcer scores
Source: https://file.scirp.org/Html/4-1050407_81084.htm
DOI: 10.4236/jcdsa.2017.74029
Coverage: factual extract from primary Methods and Table 3

Khalifa E. Sharquie, Adil A. Noaimi and Taha M. Latif. J Cosmet Dermatol Sci Appl 7(4):324–335. Published December 15, 2017.
Twenty-five patients aged 9–60 years with recurrent aphthae applied a commercial oil described as 100% pumpkin seed oil topically to the lesions for three months. All received oil and there was no assigned placebo or untreated arm. Mean Oral Clinical Manifestation Index was 12.96 ± 1.42 at baseline, 2.76 ± 1.50 on day 4 and 0.72 ± 1.48 on day 8; within-group reported p < 0.0001. The article describes spontaneous resolution of many minor aphthae. Ulcer size, phenotype and recurrence were tracked. It does not measure salivary flow, clinical drooling, oral dietary oil intake or Parkinson outcomes. Seed roasting and expression were not specified.
