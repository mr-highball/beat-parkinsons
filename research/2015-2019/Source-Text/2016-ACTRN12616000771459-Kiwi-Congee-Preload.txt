SunGold and congee preload trial registration
https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=ACTRN12616000771459

First registered June 14, 2016. The registry describes meal preloads to test a blood glucose response, with insulin and other postmeal outcomes. Its visible recruitment field still says not yet recruiting. The human results appeared in the 2018 Lubransky article, which explicitly names the registry. The report supplies participant disposition independently of the stale registry field.
