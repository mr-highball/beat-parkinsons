Goubault E et al. Cardinal motor features of Parkinson's disease coexist with peak-dose choreic-type drug-induced dyskinesia. J Parkinsons Dis. 2018;8(2):323–331.
PMID 29843253. PMCID PMC6027941. DOI 10.3233/JPD-181312.
Primary source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3233/JPD-181312

The study enrolled 89 people with Parkinson disease and analyzed 87. Daily activities included cutting and eating a piece of apple among several timed activities. Rest tremor was captured just before movement while hands rested on a table. Sixty-three participants had measurable peak-dose dyskinesia. Of those, 12.7% showed measurable concomitant resting tremor. There was no assignment to eat or avoid apple, no independently identified raw preparation, and no apple-specific before/after tremor comparison. The 2019 linked article explicitly reuses a portion of the participants' data.
