Title: Can Ocimum basilicum relieve chronic unpredictable mild stress-induced depression in mice?
PMID: 28823898
DOI: 10.1016/j.yexmp.2017.08.007
First published: 2017
Authors: Ayuob NN, Firgany AEL, El-Mansy AA, Ali S
Journal: Experimental and molecular pathology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28823898/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical CUMS depression model in n=40 male Swiss albino mice (4 groups of n=10: control, 4-week CUMS, CUMS + fluoxetine, CUMS + Ocimum basilicum essential oil). O. basilicum EO alleviated CUMS behavioral and corticosterone alterations, attenuated hippocampal CA3/DG atrophy and apoptosis, and increased hippocampal BDNF, glucocorticoid receptor, and dentate gyrus neurogenesis comparable to fluoxetine. NOTE: Non-retracted 2017 Experimental and Molecular Pathology paper (distinct from retracted 2018 Metab Brain Dis paper PMID:29356981, which is strictly excluded).
