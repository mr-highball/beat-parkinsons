Title: Safranal-promoted differentiation and survival of dopaminergic neurons in an animal model of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6201804/
PMID: 30354840
PMCID: PMC6201804
DOI: 10.1080/13880209.2018.1501705
Publication: 2018-10-24 online
Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article and figures.

The investigators cultured neural stem cells with or without isolated safranal and studied the survival and function of transplanted cells in a rat lesion model. Figure 1 documents tyrosine-hydroxylase-positive differentiation and dopamine release in cultured cells. Figures 2–3 record drug-elicited rotation, graft observations and dopaminergic measures after transplantation. The rotation count decreased sooner in the safranal-conditioned group than in the control transplant group, while both groups improved over time. This is a cell-pretreatment and transplantation experiment with a motor proxy. It does not test consumed saffron powder, passive muscle rigidity, or depressive symptom severity.
