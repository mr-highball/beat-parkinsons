Kennedy et al. studied standardized Zanthoxylum armatum extract in an MCT carrier and compared it with sunflower oil in healthy adults. Table S5 gives the acute treatment main effect for cognitive-task mental fatigue as F=3.45, p=.07. Day-1 pre-dose ratings for task repetitions 1–3 were 47.79, 49.79 and 58.40 mm for placebo (42) and 55.95, 64.15 and 70.82 mm for extract (39). These groups differed at baseline.

Table S6 reports day-56 pre-dose changes from each matching day-1 pre-dose value. In the 39-per-group analysis, mean changes for repetitions 1–3 were −5.54, −1.31 and −3.77 mm for placebo, and −7.13, −10.6 and −12.9 mm for extract. The repetition 2 SEM was 3.10 mm for each arm. These are group descriptive changes. Table S6 gives the treatment main effect as F=8.01 with a rounded p=.01. The article reports F(1,76)=8.01, p=.006.

Table S6 labels the final post-dose time 6 h. The main article Methods and Figure 8 label it 5 h. The table gives a treatment-by-assessment term of p=.13. The article describes a three-factor analysis with a treatment-by-assessment term of p=.019. The two descriptions leave the model and time-label alignment unresolved. The day-56 pre-dose changes are separate from that time label.

The supplement belongs to the same 2019 study family, NCT03673930. Its participants and cognitive-task fatigue outcome differ from Parkinson fatigue. The standardized Nepalese pepper extract differs from the ground culinary Sichuan pepper in Round 1 claim 0117.
