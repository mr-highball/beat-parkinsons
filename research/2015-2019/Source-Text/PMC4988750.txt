Title: What Is the Link Between Hallucinations, Dreams, and Hypnagogic-Hypnopompic Experiences?
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4988750/
PMCID: PMC4988750
PMID: 27358492
DOI: 10.1093/schbul/sbw076
Publication date: 2016-06-29
Date basis: NLM and PMC electronic date
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The review distinguishes waking hallucinations, dreams and experiences at sleep onset or awakening. Its comparison across Parkinson disease, eye disease and schizophrenia considers phenomenology and mechanisms. No original dried fig or Parkinson visual episode-frequency result is reported.
