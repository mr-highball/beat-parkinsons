Title: Neuroimaging in Parkinson's disease dementia: connecting the dots
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6777517/
PMID: 31608325
PMCID: PMC6777517
DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcz006
Publication year: 2019
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The neuroimaging synthesis includes nine studies of Parkinson hallucination groups alongside separate dementia and cognitive analyses. Coordinate convergence and anatomical network projections address spatial localization. No underlying dried fig comparison or exposure-specific episode rate is reported.

The paper uses both coordinate-location and network mapping approaches. Its sensitivity discussion points to small study counts and clinical heterogeneity. The hallucination evidence does not constitute a separate dietary cohort.
