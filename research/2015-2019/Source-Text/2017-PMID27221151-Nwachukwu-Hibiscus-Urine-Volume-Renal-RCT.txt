Title: Does consumption of an aqueous extract of Hibiscus sabdariffa affect renal function in subjects with mild to moderate hypertension?
PMID: 27221151
PMCID: PMC10717794
DOI: 10.1007/s12576-016-0458-z
First published: 2017
Authors: Nwachukwu DC, Aneke EI, Nwachukwu NZ, Azubike N, Obika LF
Journal: Journal of Physiological Sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27221151/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract (PMC10717794 non-OA XML unavailable). Randomized controlled clinical trial in 78 newly diagnosed untreated adults with mild-to-moderate hypertension at Enugu State University Teaching Hospital, Nigeria, equally randomized to once-daily aqueous H. sabdariffa calyx extract, lisinopril, or placebo for 4 weeks. Measured weekly, H. sabdariffa significantly increased urine volume compared with placebo (P<0.001) AND increased urine volume significantly more than lisinopril (P<0.001), while also increasing creatinine clearance vs placebo (P<0.001). Limitations: hypertensive non-PD cohort; measured total urine volume/diuresis rather than OABSS urinary urgency episodes or nocturia frequency.
