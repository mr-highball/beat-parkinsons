Title: Mushrooms: A rich source of the antioxidants ergothioneine and glutathione.
PMID: 28530594
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodchem.2017.04.109
First published: 2017
Authors: Kalaras MD, Richie JP, Calcagnotto A, Beelman RB
Journal: Food chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28530594/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Analytical quantification of ergothioneine (0.15-7.27 mg/g dry weight) and glutathione (0.11-2.41 mg/g dw) across edible mushroom species including Agaricus bisporus. Confirmed measurable ergothioneine and glutathione in A. bisporus (higher in pileus than stipe and in 3rd vs 1st cropping flush), though lower per gram than oyster/porcini species. Laboratory constituent bridge.
