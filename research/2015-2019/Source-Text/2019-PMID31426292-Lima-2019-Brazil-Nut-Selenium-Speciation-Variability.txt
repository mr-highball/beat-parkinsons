Title: Selenium Accumulation, Speciation and Localization in Brazil Nuts (Bertholletia excelsa H.B.K.).
PMID: 31426292
PMCID: PMC6724122
DOI: 10.3390/plants8080289
First published: 2019
Authors: Lima LW, Stonehouse GC, Walters C, Mehdawi AFE, Fakra SC, Pilon-Smits EAH
Journal: Plants (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31426292/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Analytical chemistry study (PMC6724122) demonstrating 8-fold nut-to-nut selenium variability in commercial Brazil nuts (mean 28-49 mg/kg; predominantly selenomethionine), showing a 30 g serving can exceed 400 ug/d or 1,200 ug toxicity thresholds depending on geographic origin.
