Title: Self-assessment of oral health, dental health care and oral health-related quality of life among Parkinson's disease patients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27231151/
PMID: 27231151
DOI: 10.1111/ger.12237
Publication date: 2016-05-27
Source depth: Original author abstract and university bibliographic confirmation
Date basis: First online date. The issue is dated March 2017.
Design: Questionnaire study of 100 Parkinson participants recruited through German support groups

The abstract reports 49% xerostomia, 70% drooling, 47% dysphagia and 29% impaired oral hygiene ability. These questionnaire outcomes describe a selected sample. The oral-health impact score compares participants with and without oral symptoms. The available abstract does not supply separate dietary effects or a raw-fig comparison. The participant recruitment and outcome scope differ from the same group's clinic-based flow study and later registered diary. Person-level overlap cannot be established from the available reports.
