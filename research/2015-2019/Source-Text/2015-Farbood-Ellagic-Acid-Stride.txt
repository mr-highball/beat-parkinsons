Purified ellagic acid and measured paw stride length
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27307952/
selected primary methods and findings paraphrase

Published in the 2015 issue. In a 6-OHDA lesion model, eight rats per group received purified ellagic acid by gavage or vehicle. Figure 2 reports longer paw stride after ellagic acid than after lesion and vehicle (p < .001). A later review lists common sage among possible plant sources of ellagic acid. Sage was not administered and the review gives no comparable culinary dose.
