Title: A Randomized, Double-Blinded, Placebo-Controlled Study to Compare the Safety and Efficacy of Low Dose Enhanced Wild Blueberry Powder and Wild Blueberry Extract (ThinkBlue™) in Maintenance of Episodic and Working Memory in Older Adults.
PMID: 29882843
PMCID: PMC6024810
DOI: 10.3390/nu10060660
First published: 2018
Authors: Whyte AR, Cheng N, Fromentin E, Williams CM
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6024810/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Six-month double-blind randomized placebo-controlled 4-arm trial in n=122 older adults (65-80 y) comparing low-dose whole wild blueberry powder (500 or 1,000 mg/d), purified extract (WBE111, 100 mg/d), or placebo. Only purified extract WBE111 significantly improved RAVLT delayed recognition at 3 months and lowered SBP over 6 months, while low-dose whole powders and executive/mood outcomes did not show clear between-group benefit. Limits: low-dose encapsulated powder/extract in non-PD older adults.
