Title: Uncooked spinach options in Parkinson trial menus
Original source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175383/

Phillips et al. randomized 47 people with Parkinson disease to low-fat or ketogenic diets for eight weeks; 44 commenced and 38 completed. In the low-fat plan, the green smoothie blends 60 g spinach or kale with fruit and other ingredients without a cooking step. In the ketogenic plan, a salad mixes 60 g spinach or kale with mayonnaise and lemon juice without cooking. The plans give alternatives and the report describes checking meal completion, not a record of each participant's ingredient choice. Therefore actual individual raw spinach consumption is not established.

The trial reports MDS-UPDRS Part II and III totals, which encompass speech items, but no spinach-specific speech item, listener-rated intelligibility comparison or isolated spinach effect. One ketogenic participant needed levodopa, ropinirole and apomorphine reductions for worsening dyskinesias, with partial resolution; the event is not attributed to spinach and food intake for that person is unknown.
