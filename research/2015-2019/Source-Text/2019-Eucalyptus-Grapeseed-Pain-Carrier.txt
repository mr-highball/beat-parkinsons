Topical grapeseed carrier and animal hot-plate response
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31548590/
selected original methods and findings paraphrase

Published in 2019 online. Six rats per arm received saline, eucalyptus or a topical eucalyptus nanoemulsion with grapeseed oil as one carrier. The optimized mixture prolonged hot-plate response latency, reaching a reported 40.75 seconds at one hour. The study supplies no plain grapeseed-oil arm or ingestion, refining or Parkinson pain measure.
