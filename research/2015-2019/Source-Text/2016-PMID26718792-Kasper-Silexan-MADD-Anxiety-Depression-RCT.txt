Title: Efficacy of Silexan in mixed anxiety-depression--A randomized, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 26718792
DOI: 10.1016/j.euroneuro.2015.12.002
First published: 2016
Authors: Kasper S, Volz HP, Dienel A, Schläfke S
Journal: European neuropsychopharmacology : the journal of the European College of Neuropsychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26718792/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Multicenter double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (Kasper et al., 2016, Eur Neuropsychopharmacol) enrolling 318 adult outpatients with mixed anxiety and depressive disorder (ICD-10 F41.2, HAMA >= 18) randomized to oral Silexan 80 mg/day or placebo for 70 days (10 weeks). Co-primary endpoints favored Silexan over placebo: HAMA fell 10.8+/-9.6 vs 8.4+/-8.9 (p<0.01) and MADRS fell 9.2+/-9.9 vs 6.1+/-7.6 (p<0.001), with superior SF-36 physical/mental quality of life and daily living skills. Eructation was the only adverse event substantially more common with Silexan. Limits: proprietary oral essential oil softgel rather than brewed or dried culinary lavender; non-PD cohort.
