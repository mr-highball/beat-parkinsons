Title: Disruption of the default mode network and its intrinsic functional connectivity underlies minor hallucinations in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30536829/
PMID: 30536829
DOI: 10.1002/mds.27557
Publication date: 2018-12-10 (first electronic release per NLM, January 2019 journal issue)
Source depth: Original abstract, publisher supporting-information index and verified later methods citation

The original abstract reports structural imaging and resting-state functional MRI in 18 Parkinson participants with minor hallucinations and 14 without them. The observed differences involve gray matter, default mode network connectivity and connectivity with visual-processing areas. The authors identify progression from minor to formed hallucinations as a question for longitudinal study. The abstract reports no dried fig intake or clinical episode rates after dietary exposure.

The 2026 patient-specific brain network article explicitly reuses the clinical and Parkinson imaging data from this study. Its 2025 preprint reports the same 18 and 14 Parkinson groups. These reports are different analyses of a shared clinical sample. The publisher exposes the abstract and supplement index. The complete 2018 main text and supplement contents remain open.
