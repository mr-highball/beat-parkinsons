Title: Visual cortical excitability in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4853644/
PMID: 26541688
PMCID: PMC4853644
DOI: 10.1192/bjp.bp.114.152736
Publication year: 2015
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The secondary comparison selected 16 dementia with Lewy bodies participants and 19 controls with both imaging and stimulation measurements. Average early visual responses to checkerboards and resting perfusion were similar, while the relationship between stimulation threshold and visual response differed. The authors cite the earlier stimulation dataset. No oral fig comparison was made.

The study incorporates earlier cortical stimulation and imaging observations and should be linked to the cited reports. The group difference in the relationship between measured variables is distinct from the absence of a clear average early visual response difference.
