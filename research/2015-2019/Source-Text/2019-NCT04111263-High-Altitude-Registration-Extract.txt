Registered fiber and polyphenol mixture during simulated altitude
First posted: 2019-10-01
Registration: NCT04111263
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04111263
Coverage: Official current record, public posting dates and outcome fields

The crossover trial assigned a fiber/polyphenol blend or matched placebo during simulated high versus low altitude. The current record reports actual enrollment of 33, start on October 6, 2019 and study completion on November 5, 2022. Its listed primary outcome was intestinal permeability. The secondary Profile of Mood States endpoint included the depression/dejection subscale. No structured results are posted. The cranberry-extract ingredient was identified in the later published research report.
