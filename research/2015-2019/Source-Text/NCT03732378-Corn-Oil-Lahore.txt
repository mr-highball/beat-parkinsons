Lahore adjunct depression trial registry
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03732378
Registration: NCT03732378
Coverage: Official registry, first posted November 6, 2018; current record

The Lahore major depression study lists 70 participants and a completed 2017 study. Adults taking antidepressants received a 500 mg corn-oil capsule or an EPA/DHA capsule daily for 12 weeks. Depression was assessed with the Beck inventory. The registration was first posted after the study and has no posted results. The original publication gives smaller analysis denominators.
