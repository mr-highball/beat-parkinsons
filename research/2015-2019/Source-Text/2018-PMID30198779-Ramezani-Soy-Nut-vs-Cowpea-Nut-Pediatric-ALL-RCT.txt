Title: The Effect of Soy Nut Compared to Cowpea Nut on Body Weight, Blood Cells, Inflammatory Markers and Chemotherapy Complications in Children with Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia: A Randomized Controlled Clinical Trial.
PMID: 30198779
DOI: 10.1080/01635581.2018.1495240
First published: 2018
Authors: Ramezani N, Moafi A, Nadjarzadeh A, Yousefian S, Reisi N, Salehi-Abargouei A
Journal: Nutrition and cancer
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30198779/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week active-comparator randomized trial (Ramezani et al., 2018, Nutr Cancer) in 56 children with B-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia on maintenance chemotherapy randomized to 30 g/day soy nut (n=29) vs cowpea nut powder (n=27) without a placebo or no-legume control arm. Energy/protein intake, weight, BMI, RBC/Hb/Hct, physical activity, and fatigue improved more in the soy nut arm than the cowpea nut arm (p<0.05). Limits: active-comparator design cannot determine whether cowpea itself improved, worsened, or had no effect relative to no intervention; roasted powder in pediatric leukemia differs from canned black-eyed peas in adult Parkinson's disease.
