Title: The correlation between clinical characteristics and radionuclide salivagram findings in patients with brain lesions
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5773434/
PMID: 29354567
PMCID: PMC5773434
DOI: 10.5535/arm.2017.41.6.915
Publication date: 2017-12-28
Source depth: Original article body and reported results.

Park and colleagues used a retrospective brain-lesion cohort with documented dysphagia. The analyzed group was 35 and eleven had salivary aspiration on the salivagram. The reported adjusted association for cognitive examination score was an odds ratio of 0.760 per point (95% CI .625–.923). The Results say one salivagram-positive patient had a negative finding for aspiration on videofluoroscopy. The Discussion groups eleven as having findings on both tests. Whether this reflects the use of penetration together with aspiration needs verification against the table definitions.

Internal aspiration differs from saliva visible outside the mouth. The study supplies no raw-fig comparison or separate estimate for Parkinson drooling.
