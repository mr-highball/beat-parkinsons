Title: Effects of Luo Han Guo on throat complications associated with tracheal intubation: a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 31187674
PMCID: PMC6683879
DOI: 10.1177/0300060519853656
First published: 2019
Authors: Tan HL, Liang YK, Li YM, Qiu LY, Huang R, Guo L, et al.
Journal: The Journal of international medical research
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6683879/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized trial in n=203 postoperative tracheal-intubation patients comparing Luo Han Guo decoction (n=102) against black tea as active control (n=101) for VAS throat soreness at 2-48 h. Luo Han Guo outperformed black tea at 12, 24, and 48 h, but because there was no inert placebo arm, black tea absolute analgesic efficacy cannot be estimated.
