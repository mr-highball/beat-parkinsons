Title: Concord grape juice, cognitive function, and driving performance: a 12-wk, placebo-controlled, randomized crossover trial in mothers of preteen children.
PMID: 26864371
DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.115.114553
First published: 2016
Authors: Lamport DJ, Lawton CL, Merat N, Jamson H, Myrissa K, Hofman D, Chadwick HK, Quadt F, Wightman JD, Dye L
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26864371/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial (NCT01411631) in 25 healthy working mothers aged 40-50 y consuming 355 mL/day Concord grape juice (777 mg polyphenols) vs energy-/sugar-/taste-matched placebo for two 12-week periods with 4-week washout. Concord grape juice significantly improved immediate spatial memory and 25-min driving simulator performance (n=17 subsample matching lead-vehicle speed/direction, complex everyday executive/visuomotor task), with enduring carryover in arm 1. However, sustained attention (RVIP) showed no significant benefit, and Grooved Pegboard was slower in phase 1 (63.2 vs 60.4 s, p<0.05). Limits: healthy middle-aged female sample, juice rather than whole frozen/raw grapes, carryover across washout.
