Original source scope
The primary compares fly food containing powdered dried fermented grape skin with standard food in PINK1-model and control flies. The standard-fed mutant group had roughly 50% abnormal wing posture, with a lower proportion at higher supplementation. The paper separately discusses olive oil in a general Mediterranean-diet description and uses figure numbers.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Grape preparations are related but wing posture, activity and ATP do not isolate clinical rigidity after frozen grapes. Olive oil has dietary background only, without a cold-pressed comparison or bradykinesia measurement. Fig. denotes illustrations rather than fruit.
