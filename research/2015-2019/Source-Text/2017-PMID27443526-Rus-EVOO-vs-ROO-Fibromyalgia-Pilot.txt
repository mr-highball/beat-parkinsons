Title: Extra Virgin Olive Oil Improves Oxidative Stress, Functional Capacity, and Health-Related Psychological Status in Patients With Fibromyalgia: A Preliminary Study.
PMID: 27443526
DOI: 10.1177/1099800416659370
First published: 2017
Authors: Rus A, Molina F, Ramos MM, Martínez-Ramírez MJ, Del Moral ML
Journal: Biological Research for Nursing
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27443526/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
3-week randomized preliminary pilot in 23 women with fibromyalgia comparing 50 mL/day organic EVOO vs refined olive oil (ROO). Reported significant pre-post between-group differences favoring EVOO on Fibromyalgia Impact Questionnaire (FIQ), SF-12 MCS-12, protein carbonyls, and lipid peroxidation. Tiny 3-week pilot now directly unreplicated in the 10x larger, 6-month DB-RCT Durán González 2026 (PMC13301056, n=250/190 completed, null on FIQR and SF-36).
