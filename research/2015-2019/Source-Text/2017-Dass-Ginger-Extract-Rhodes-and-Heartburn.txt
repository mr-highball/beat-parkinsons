Title: Implementing standardised Rhodes index to measure the efficacy of ginger extract in pregnancy induced nausea and vomiting
DOI: 10.18203/2319-2003.ijbcp20173273
Source: https://www.ijbcp.com/index.php/ijbcp/article/view/1947/0
Original PDF: https://www.ijbcp.com/index.php/ijbcp/article/download/1947/1557/7191
Date basis: 2017-07-22 electronic publication, August journal issue 6(8), pp. 1932–1936.
Coverage: Factual extract of the complete original study, methods, results and discussion.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The study followed 30 pregnant participants with nausea or vomiting from 4 to 16 weeks' gestation. They received an oral ginger extract for seven days. The Rhodes Index declined from 14.43 at baseline to 1.17 at day 7 as reported in the discussion. There was no concurrent comparison arm. Four participants reported heartburn. The original gives an abstract significance threshold of p<.005 and a less precise threshold of p<.05 in the discussion.

The 2021 pharmacology abstract CPO-9 reports matching cohort size, gestational range, ginger extract regimen, outcome method and four heartburn events. Treat the two reports as one likely family, with an earlier 2015 publication lead unresolved. The clinical outcome differs from constipation severity in Parkinson disease.
