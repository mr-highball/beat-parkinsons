Ekmekci H, Kaptan H. Camptocormia and deep brain stimulation: The interesting overlapping etiologies and the therapeutic role of subthalamic nucleus-deep brain stimulation in Parkinson disease with camptocormia. Surgical Neurology International. 2016;7(Suppl 4):S103–S107. First published 2016-02-10.
DOI: 10.4103/2152-7806.176130
PMID: 26958425
PMCID: PMC4765245
Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4765245/
Coverage: factual summary of complete original case and discussion.

The original describes one Parkinson patient with slight resting tremor and moderate to sometimes severe low-back pain with camptocormia, prior lumbar disease and surgery. Pain varied with posture and improved after stimulation by the clinician report. Pain-scale wording does not furnish a clear before/after difference. Tremor was described qualitatively. Dietary exposures were not assessed.
