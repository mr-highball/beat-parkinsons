# Evening meal with lentils and next-morning sleepiness

Source https://worldsleepcongress.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/WS19-Poster-abstracts-by-category.pdf
Coverage original World Sleep poster abstract

The original World Sleep 2019 Sher abstract at Board 091 and printed p. 934 describes 20 healthy adults tested after two evening meals. A lighter meal contained lentils, feta and other vegetables, and the heavier comparator used a different mixed menu. The next-morning Epworth comparison was inconclusive (t = 0.76, p = .45). Meal energy and ingredients differed, canning was unreported and the study did not enroll a Parkinson cohort.
