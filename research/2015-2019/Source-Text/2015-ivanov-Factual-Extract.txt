Urinary urgency severity in a single-arm lovage root blend trial
2015-09-22
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40816-015-0008-x

Ivanov and colleagues followed 125 women with uncomplicated urinary tract infection after a formula containing lovage root, rosemary leaves and centaury. The study had no control arm. Mean urgency severity on a 0–4 scale fell by 1.6 at day 7 and by 1.9 at day 37 (reported within-arm p<.001). Urinary frequency was a separate symptom; urgency episodes were not counted. The trial had no Parkinson cohort and no separately administered dried lovage leaf. NCT01478620 and this paper describe the same cohort, distinct from NCT02639520.
