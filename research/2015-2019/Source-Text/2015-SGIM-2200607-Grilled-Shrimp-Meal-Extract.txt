Original SGIM clinical vignette 2200607
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4405523/

A vignette by Metcalf and Dubuske reports lip swelling after a mixed meal containing crab dip and grilled shrimp in a 42-year-old. The abstract reports a negative shellfish skin-prick test and discusses later thyroid and immune findings. It records a meal history and leaves food-specific causation unresolved. A separate vignette in the same collection describes visual hallucinations in an older patient with a different presentation and no shrimp exposure. The grilled-shrimp vignette reports neither Parkinson disease nor hallucinations.
