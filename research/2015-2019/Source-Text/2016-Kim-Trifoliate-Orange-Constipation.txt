Title: Effects and Safety of Aqueous Extract of Poncirus fructus in Spinal Cord Injury with Neurogenic Bowel
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5055929/
Identifiers: PMID 27738444, DOI 10.1155/2016/7154616
First published: September 25, 2016
Coverage: Factual extract from Methods, adverse events, and Tables 2 and 3.

The open before-and-after study enrolled 31 adults with spinal cord injury; 25 completed the analysis. Participants took 1,600 mg/day of aqueous immature Poncirus trifoliata fruit extract for 14 days, with no concurrent comparator. Constipation score moved from 4.60 to 3.48 (p = .04). Total transit moved from 57.4 to 41.2 hours (p < .05). The study identified seven people with loose stool or diarrhea considered related to treatment. No raw sweet orange exposure was assigned and the study did not include a Parkinson cohort.
