Lipid curcumin and the Flanker attention task
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3233/NHA-170029
PMID: 29951592
DOI: 10.3233/NHA-170029
Coverage: Factual summary of full primary table and methods.

Published online March 25, 2018. Thirty-nine adults aged 45–74 were assigned to Longvida 2000 mg/day or placebo for 12 weeks. Table 3 gives identical rounded flanker inhibitory control and attention means for curcumin and placebo, 8.3 to 8.5, arm-by-time p=.97. Improved scores over time in both groups do not support an added effect in this comparison. This course used a formulated supplement in healthy aging.
