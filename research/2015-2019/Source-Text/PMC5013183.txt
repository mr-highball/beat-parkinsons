Title: Dopamine Activation Preserves Visual Motion Perception Despite Noise Interference of Human V5/MT
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5013183/
PMCID: PMC5013183
PMID: 27605607
DOI: 10.1523/jneurosci.4452-15.2016
Publication date: 2016-09-07
Date basis: PMC and NLM issue date
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The 12-volunteer visual-motion experiment uses dopamine-drug sessions and focal transcranial magnetic stimulation to compare discrimination under transient visual noise. The volunteers did not form a clinical Parkinson hallucination cohort. The task does not test dried figs or visual hallucination episodes.
