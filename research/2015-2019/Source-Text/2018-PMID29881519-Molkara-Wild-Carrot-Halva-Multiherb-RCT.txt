Title: Effects of a food product (based on Daucus carota) and education based on traditional Persian medicine on female sexual dysfunction: a randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 29881519
PMCID: PMC5984011
DOI: 10.19082/6577
First published: 2018
Authors: Molkara T, Akhlaghi F, Ramezani MA, Salari R, Vakili V, Kamalinejad M et al.
Journal: Electron Physician
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29881519/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Three-arm randomized trial (n=96 women with sexual dysfunction, 8 wk) testing traditional Persian wild carrot halva (Daucus carota mixed with several herbs and honey) with or without TPM education, improving FSFI pain and sexual domains (p<0.001). Bundled polyherbal honey confection using wild carrot for dyspareunia, non-transferable to culinary carrot root in PD.
