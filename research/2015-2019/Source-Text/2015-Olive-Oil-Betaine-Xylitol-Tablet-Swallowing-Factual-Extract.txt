Title: Chewable olive oil combination and reported swallowing improvement
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4511916/
Format: Factual extract from the cited original.

First online March 14, 2015. Thirty-four adults used the olive oil, betaine and xylitol preparation and sorbitol control in randomized order. Periods lasted one week, separated by a one-week washout. Aggregate questionnaire means were 9.41 at baseline, 8.97 on control and 8.15 on active (active versus baseline p = .0015 in the Results). The conclusion describes subjective swallowing improvement, but separate item values and an item-level test are not reported. Unstimulated and stimulated saliva comparisons were inconclusive, p = .88 and .53. Oil grade and Parkinson-specific results are unavailable.
