Original study scope
The review assesses repeat radiography in chiropractic and manual care for structural change and safety. Imaging, clinical pain and treatment indications are the relevant contexts. No ground or fresh sage, date-fruit assignment or culinary fall effect was examined.

Continuing evidence gaps
Any frozen ingredient effect requires a separately assigned or traceable culinary exposure and a matched symptom estimate.
