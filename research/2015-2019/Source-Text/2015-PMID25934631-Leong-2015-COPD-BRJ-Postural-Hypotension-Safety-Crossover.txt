Title: A double blind randomized placebo control crossover trial on the effect of dietary nitrate supplementation on exercise tolerance in stable moderate chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
PMID: 25934631
PMCID: PMC4423518
DOI: 10.1186/s12890-015-0057-4
First published: 2015
Authors: Leong P, Basham JE, Yong T, Chazan A, Finlay P, Barnes S, et al.
Journal: BMC pulmonary medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25934631/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover trial in n=19 analyzed COPD patients (35 screened/tested with initial open test dose): 3 days of BRJ did not improve incremental shuttle walk distance vs placebo; during the initial unblinded test dose, mean systolic BP fell ~10 mmHg at 1 h and 1/35 participant experienced symptomatic postural hypotension and was excluded before randomization. Important safety observation for orthostasis monitoring, not a controlled between-arm worsening test.
