Title: An Acute Dose of Specific Grape and Apple Polyphenols Improves Endurance Performance: A Randomized, Crossover, Double-Blind versus Placebo Controlled Study.
PMID: 28829368
PMCID: PMC5579710
DOI: 10.3390/nu9080917
First published: 2017
Authors: Deley G, Guillemet D, Allaert FA, Babault N
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28829368/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=48 physically active men (mean 31 +/- 6 y) taking 500 mg combined grape and apple polyphenols vs placebo the evening before and 1 h before intense cycling. Polyphenols increased time to exhaustion by +9.7% (p<0.05) and delayed time to maximal perceived exertion by +12.8% (p<0.05) without altering heart rate or pain. Limits: bundled with grape polyphenols (cannot isolate apple) and young healthy athletes.
