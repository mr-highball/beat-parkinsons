Star anise and green anise infusion in infant poisoning
Source: https://scielo.isciii.es/scielo.php?pid=S1130-01082016001200015&script=sci_arttext
PMID: 26875753
Coverage: factual extract from primary case

Pablo Obando-Pacheco and colleagues. Revista Española de Enfermedades Digestivas 108(12):819–821, 2016. DOI 10.17235/reed.2016.3964/2015.
A four-month-old had severe coagulopathy, acute liver injury, nonketotic hypoglycemia, moderate metabolic acidosis, seizures, and nystagmus. Clinical history disclosed daily consumption of a homemade infusion containing both star anise and green anise Pimpinella anisum for about two months. A series of alternative etiologies was investigated. The exposure was a mixture and the ingredient-level contributions cannot be isolated. The severe acute neurologic presentation offers a differential distinct from recurrent visual hallucinations in an adult Parkinson cohort. No comparison is made for culinary ground Pimpinella or a visual-hallucination frequency measure.
