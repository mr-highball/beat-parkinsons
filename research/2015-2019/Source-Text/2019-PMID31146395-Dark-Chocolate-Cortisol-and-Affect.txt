Title: Effect of Polyphenol-Rich Dark Chocolate on Salivary Cortisol and Mood in Adults
Publication date: 2019-05-29
Publication: Antioxidants 8(6), 149
PMID: 31146395
PMCID: PMC6616509
DOI: 10.3390/antiox8060149
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3921/8/6/149
Coverage: Original article methods, subjective mood results, Figure 2 and limitations.

Twenty-six adults completed four weeks of either polyphenol-rich dark chocolate or a low-polyphenol dark chocolate comparator. Participants with neurological conditions were excluded. The high-polyphenol comparison showed lower salivary cortisol measures. The between-group treatment-by-time comparison for positive affect was p=.13 and for negative affect p=.14. Negative affect improved within one arm, with p=.02, without a clear between-arm effect. The PANAS composite includes several affect terms and provides no separate anxiety severity measure. Both groups ate dark chocolate. This does not estimate anxiety effects from adding plain dark chocolate in Parkinson disease.
