Title: Daikenchuto stool frequency in a retrospective clinical chart review
PMID: 29693001
PMCID: PMC5859840
DOI: 10.1155/2018/1296717
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5859840/
Published: 2018-03-05
Archive scope: Evidence-focused summary of the inspected original article body.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The study reviewed 33 patients treated with Daikenchuto in clinical practice. Twenty-two used 7.5 g daily and 11 used 15 g daily. The article reports a median of two bowel movements per week before treatment and medians of 5, 5.5, 5 and 8 in weeks one through four. The available observations are not a fixed randomized cohort over time. There was no untreated comparison. Reports of one loose stool and one watery stool belong to adverse events. Abdominal pain appears in safety reporting with zero events and has no efficacy comparison here. The formula combines processed ginger with ginseng and Japanese pepper, so the stool data cannot attribute an effect to ginger.
