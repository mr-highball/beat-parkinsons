Linked trial: ISRCTN95931417, as reported in the companion paper PMID 30325021
Coverage: Factual extract of Methods, Results and Table 4

The phase II feasibility trial randomized 133 men with elevated PSA or previous negative biopsy in a three-by-three factorial design. The tea assignment was at least three cups per day (about 600 mL) made from supplied Camellia sinensis green tea bags, 600 mg/day of EGCG from green tea leaf extract capsules, or matching capsule placebo. The beverage assignment was open label. Each tea group was also randomized to a lycopene intervention or placebo. One hundred thirty-two attended the six-month visit.

Table 4 reports fatigue during the preceding month in 2 (5%) beverage participants, 10 (24%) extract capsule participants, and 7 (18%) tea-placebo participants. The table gives minimum group denominators of 41, 41 and 39, respectively. These are descriptive adverse symptom counts. The article provides no baseline-adjusted fatigue severity comparison, matched beverage placebo, or Parkinson subgroup. The pooled counts give no separate fatigue comparison by the nested lycopene assignment. The lower count in the brewed beverage group and the higher count in the concentrated capsule group are opposite descriptive signs from a single experiment, with different preparations. They are context for Round 1 claim 0948.
