Title: Psychotropic and anti-dementia treatment in elderly persons with clinical signs of dementia with Lewy bodies: a cross-sectional study in 40 nursing homes in Sweden
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5816356/
PMID: 29454305
PMCID: PMC5816356
DOI: 10.1186/s12877-018-0740-4
Publication date: 2018-02-17
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC metadata and PubMed.
Design: Cross-sectional nursing-home cohort and medication records

Forty Swedish nursing homes contributed clinical sign and dispensing data. The groups included 440 residents with recorded dementia and 154 without it. The clinical instrument asks about observable visual hallucinations among multiple Lewy body signs. The analysis compares medication use and signs, with no change in visual event frequency after an ingredient.
