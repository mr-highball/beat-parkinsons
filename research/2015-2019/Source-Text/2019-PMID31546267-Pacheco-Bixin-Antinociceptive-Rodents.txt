Title: Antinociceptive and Anti-Inflammatory Effects of Bixin, a Carotenoid Extracted from the Seeds of Bixa orellana.
PMID: 31546267
DOI: 10.1055/a-1008-1238
First published: 2019
Authors: Pacheco, Gasparin, Jesus, Sotomaior, Ventura
Journal: Planta medica
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31546267/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled acute-pain and inflammation study administering oral bixin (extracted from Bixa orellana seeds) to male Wistar rats (15 or 30 mg/kg p.o.) and Swiss albino mice (27 or 53 mg/kg p.o.). Both doses significantly reduced formalin-test flinches in both neurogenic and inflammatory phases and reduced acetic-acid writhing in mice without altering open-field locomotor activity (ruling out sedation); 30 mg/kg also prolonged hot-plate latency and reduced carrageenan paw edema and MPO activity. Limits: isolated seed carotenoid in acute rodent nociception assays.
