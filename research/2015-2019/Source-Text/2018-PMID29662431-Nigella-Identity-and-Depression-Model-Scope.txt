Original study scope
The review treats black cumin and nutmeg flower as names of Nigella sativa, distinct from culinary cumin and nutmeg. The depression section includes hydroalcoholic extract and isolated thymoquinone in an animal behavioral model. It does not show a Parkinson result for toasted nigella seeds.

Continuing evidence gaps
