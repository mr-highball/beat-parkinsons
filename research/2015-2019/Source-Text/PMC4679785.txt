The investigators combine dopamine transporter SPECT and cardiac MIBG imaging in a differential diagnosis of dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer disease. The baseline clinical-characteristics table reports hallucination presence by diagnostic group alongside other clinical features. Diagnostic discrimination and clinical presence do not measure food exposure or episode frequency in Parkinson disease.
