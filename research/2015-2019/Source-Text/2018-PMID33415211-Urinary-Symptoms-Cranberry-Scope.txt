Original study scope
This review separates psychosocial burden from management of male lower urinary tract symptoms. Its cranberry section cites dried powder and extract comparisons on urinary symptoms and flow, including a randomized six-month powder study in 42 men.

Continuing evidence gaps
Anxiety and depression in urinary symptom epidemiology do not establish cranberry effects on depression. The cited cranberry trials report urinary outcomes, and the article supplies no grape anxiety comparison.
