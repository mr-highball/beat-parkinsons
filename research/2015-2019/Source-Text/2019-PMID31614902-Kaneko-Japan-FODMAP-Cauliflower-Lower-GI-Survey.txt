Title: Observational Study on Knowledge and Eating Habits with Respect to Low- and High-FODMAP Foods in Medical Checkup Populations in Japan.
PMID: 31614902
PMCID: PMC6835958
DOI: 10.3390/nu11102436
First published: 2019
Authors: Kaneko H, Tsuboi H, Yamamoto S, Konagaya T
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31614902/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Cross-sectional survey of 1,024 Japanese medical checkup examinees modeling high-/low-FODMAP food habits against lower gastrointestinal problems (LGIP: pain and irregular stools). Cauliflower ranked among foods associated with lower LGIP odds (alongside soybeans OR 0.48, seaweed, shellfish, green beans) rather than worsening LGIP, though observational and non-PD.
