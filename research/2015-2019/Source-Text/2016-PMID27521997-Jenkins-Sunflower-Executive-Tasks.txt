Title: Effects of rumenic acid rich conjugated linoleic acid supplementation on cognitive function and handgrip performance in older men and women
Publication date: 2016-08-10 (Europe PMC electronic publication)
PMID: 27521997
DOI: 10.1016/j.exger.2016.08.004
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0531556516302492
Text coverage: Distilled investigator report and published primary result summary.

Jenkins and colleagues randomized 65 older adults to rumenic-acid-rich conjugated linoleic acid or high-oleic sunflower oil at 6 g/day for eight weeks. The sunflower group contained 12 men and 23 women. No significant differential changes were found in Trail Making, Serial Sevens or handgrip fatigue. Previously cataloged Trail Making B figures are within-comparator summaries. They do not establish an oil effect independent of practice, a cold-pressed product or a Parkinson population.
