Title: Default mode network differences between rigidity- and tremor-predominant Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5375103/
PMCID: PMC5375103
PMID: 27266635
DOI: 10.1016/j.cortex.2016.04.021
Publication date: 2016-05-06
Date basis: NLM and PMC electronic date
Source depth: Complete original PMC structured article body
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Resting-state fMRI compared 17 rigid, 15 tremor-predominant Parkinson participants and 24 controls. The experimental outcomes concern network connectivity and motor-subtype differences. No dried fig intake or clinical visual episode outcome is measured.
