Title: Cerebrospinal Fluid Alzheimer's Disease Biomarkers Across the Spectrum of Lewy Body Diseases: Results from a Large Multicenter Cohort

Source depth: Complete original PMC structured article body
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The European multicenter cohort measures cerebrospinal fluid biomarkers among 375 participants with dementia with Lewy bodies, 55 with Parkinson dementia and 164 with Parkinson disease. Its outcomes concern biomarker-defined copathology and cognitive measures, without a dried fig or timed visual episode comparison.
