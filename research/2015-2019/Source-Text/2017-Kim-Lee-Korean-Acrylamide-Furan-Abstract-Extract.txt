Title: Dietary exposure of the Korean population to acrylamide and furan: Total diet study
DOI: 10.4172/2476-2059-C1-002
Source: https://www.longdom.org/conference-abstracts-files/2476-2059-C1-002-008.pdf
First public presentation: June 5–7, 2017, food-safety conference; an earlier publication day is unverified.
Coverage: Factual original one-page conference abstract, page 39; DOI identifies the collection, not an independently verified article DOI.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Kim and Lee summarize a Korean total-diet study using national intake records from 2008–2013 and approximately 1,200 food/preparation combinations. Estimated average acrylamide exposure is .086 micrograms/kg body weight/day; furan exposure is .210. Black pepper accounts for 18.2% of estimated acrylamide exposure.

The 2018 nutrition abstract P20-084 reports a different adult estimate and pregnancy strata. Shared authorship/program context is not independent replication; denominator and preparation definitions require reconciliation before treating the estimates as contradictory.

These are food-contaminant concentration/intake models, not measured Parkinson symptoms, clinical toxicity thresholds or a pepper intervention. Pepper preparation and individual-level uncertainty remain open. Do not substitute a similarly named acrylamide derivative studied as a drug for dietary acrylamide.
