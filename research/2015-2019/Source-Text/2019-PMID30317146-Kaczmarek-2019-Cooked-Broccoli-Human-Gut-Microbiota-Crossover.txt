Title: Broccoli consumption affects the human gastrointestinal microbiota.
PMID: 30317146
PMCID: PMC8025206
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2018.09.015
First published: 2019
Authors: Kaczmarek JL, Liu X, Charron CS, Novotny JA, Jeffery EH, Seifried HE
Journal: The Journal of nutritional biochemistry
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8025206/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled feeding randomized crossover trial (two 18-d periods, 24-d washout) in n=18 healthy adults consuming 200 g/d cooked broccoli + 20 g/d raw daikon radish vs control. Broccoli shifted gut beta-diversity (p=0.03), lowered Firmicutes by 9% (p=0.05), and increased Bacteroidetes by 10% (p=0.03) and Bacteroides by 8% (p=0.02). Mechanistic gut context for cooked broccoli; no constipation or clinical bowel symptoms reported.
