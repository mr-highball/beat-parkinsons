Title: Effects of blueberry supplementation on measures of functional mobility in older adults.
PMID: 25909473
DOI: 10.1139/apnm-2014-0247
First published: 2015
Authors: Schrager MA, Hilton J, Gould R, Kelly VE
Journal: Applied physiology, nutrition, and metabolism = Physiologie appliquee, nutrition et metabolisme
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25909473/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Six-week partially randomized trial in n=20 older adults (>60 y; 13 consuming 2 cups/d frozen blueberries vs 7 carrot-juice control) evaluating grip strength, simple RT, adaptive gait, and executive function. Mixed-model ANCOVA showed significant between-group improvement vs carrot juice ONLY for step errors during a challenging dual-task adaptive gait test (independent of gait speed); usual gait speed, dual-task gait speed, and single-task step errors changed only within-group pre-post in the blueberry arm without between-group superiority. Limits: very small, partially randomized non-PD sample using active carrot-juice control; no between-group gait speed or stride-length gain.
