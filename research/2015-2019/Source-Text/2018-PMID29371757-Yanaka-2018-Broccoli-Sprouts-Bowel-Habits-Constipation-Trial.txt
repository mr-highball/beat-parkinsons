Title: Daily intake of broccoli sprouts normalizes bowel habits in human healthy subjects.
PMID: 29371757
PMCID: PMC5773831
DOI: 10.3164/jcbn.17-42
Registry ID: UMIN000021207
First published: 2018
Authors: Yanaka A
Journal: Journal of clinical biochemistry and nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5773831/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled semi-open-label intervention trial (UMIN000021207) in n=48 healthy Japanese adults with mild constipation (modified Constipation Scoring System CSS >2) assigned to 20 g/d raw sulforaphane-rich broccoli sprouts (4.4 mg/g SGS, n=24) or alfalfa sprouts (0 mg SGS, n=24) for 4 weeks, zero dropouts. Broccoli sprouts significantly reduced total CSS from 7.25 +/- 2.83 to 5.17 +/- 3.27 (within-group p=0.0017; sustained at 4-wk washout 5.46 +/- 2.57) and shortened defecation-attempt duration score from 0.96 +/- 0.62 to 0.58 +/- 0.58 (p=0.0077), whereas alfalfa sprouts showed no significant change (5.75 +/- 2.71 to 4.83 +/- 3.07, NS). Limits: raw sprouts rather than mature florets, non-PD adults, semi-open allocation with slightly higher baseline CSS in BS group.
