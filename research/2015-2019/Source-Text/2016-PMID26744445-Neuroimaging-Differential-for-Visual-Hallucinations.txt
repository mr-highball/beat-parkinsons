Title: Distinguishing Neuroimaging Features in Patients Presenting with Visual Hallucinations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7960303/
PMID: 26744445
PMCID: PMC7960303
DOI: 10.3174/ajnr.a4636
Publication year: 2016
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete official PMC HTML.

The completed AJNR review organizes causes of visual hallucinations by clinical presentation and characteristic imaging. Parkinson and Lewy body disease are part of a broader clinical differential. It summarizes imaging across conditions and does not present a fig study.

The method uses clinical neuroimaging to distinguish neurological and ophthalmic presentations. The separate comment by Jack and McCarthy (PMID 27585699) expands the molecular imaging discussion.
