Title: Dietary Supplement Use in Gastrointestinal Symptom Management and Effect on Hopelessness Levels in Patients With Irritable Bowel Syndrome.
PMID: 30973435
DOI: 10.1097/HNP.0000000000000324
First published: 2019
Authors: Erdogan Z, Kurçer MA
Journal: Holistic nursing practice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30973435/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional survey of n=127 Turkish IBS outpatients documenting high dietary supplement/herbal use (73.2%), significantly predicted by nausea and decreased stool passage (R=0.409, p<0.0001), with apricot commonly reported for constipation self-management. Observational usage survey without interventional efficacy comparison.
