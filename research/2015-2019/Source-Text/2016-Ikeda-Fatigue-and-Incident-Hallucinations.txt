Coverage: factual summary of original Methods and Tables 1–3.

Of 63 followed patients selected without hallucinations at baseline, 31 reported first visual hallucinations and six also reported auditory hallucinations. The Brief Fatigue Inventory mean in Table 1 was 36.96 versus 24.25 for the incident-hallucination and comparison groups. The per-score-point adjusted odds ratio was 1.027 (95% CI 1.001–1.053). The prose direction for general fatigue, mood and relations conflicts with Table 3. This is an observational analysis of incident symptoms, without a dried fig intervention, a dietary predictor or an episode-rate comparison.
