Title: Black Beans, Fiber, and Antioxidant Capacity Pilot Study: Examination of Whole Foods vs. Functional Components on Postprandial Metabolic, Oxidative Stress, and Inflammation in Adults with Metabolic Syndrome.
PMID: 26225995
PMCID: PMC4555112
DOI: 10.3390/nu7085273
First published: 2015
Authors: Reverri EJ, Randolph JM, Steinberg FM, Kappagoda CT, Edirisinghe I, Burton-Freeman BM
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26225995/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled crossover trial (Reverri et al., 2015, Nutrients, PMC4555112) in 12 adults with metabolic syndrome comparing a whole black bean meal vs fiber-matched and antioxidant-matched control meals over 5 hours postprandially. Black beans lowered postprandial insulin vs both controls (p<0.0001) and modestly enhanced plasma antioxidant capacity (meal x time p=0.002). Metabolic biomarker trial without gastrointestinal, fatigue, or neurological symptom scales.
