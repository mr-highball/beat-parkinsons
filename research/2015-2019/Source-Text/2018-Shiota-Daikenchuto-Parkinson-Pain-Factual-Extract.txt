Title: Parkinson disease abdominal pain relieved in one case during Dai-Kenchu-To treatment

The presentation reports a 78-year-old man with Parkinson disease, Hoehn–Yahr stage III, with previous oral levodopa treatment. Nocturnal and early-morning epigastric pain was eased by food and later extended into the lower abdomen. The workup included upper endoscopy and abdominal CT. Constipation had worsened despite previous laxative use, and CT showed retained colonic stool. After bedtime oral Dai-Kenchu-To, nocturnal abdominal pain was reported to have disappeared and late-night eating stopped. The report does not quantify pain, give the brand or dose, document a separate post-treatment constipation outcome, or isolate a component of the herbal regimen. The neighboring meeting abstracts describe other patients and outcomes.
