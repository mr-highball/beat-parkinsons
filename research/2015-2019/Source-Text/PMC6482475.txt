Title: Antioxidant properties of saffron and its constituents in Alzheimer and Parkinson research
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6482475/
PMID: 29564976
PMCID: PMC6482475
DOI: 10.2174/1570159X16666180321095705
Publication: 2019 issue; NLM indexed this record in March 2018
Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article and references.

The review distinguishes whole stigma, extract and isolated constituents, then summarizes cell and animal neuroprotection research and clinical cognitive studies in Alzheimer disease. Its discussion of depression points to prior capsule and crocin add-on trials in major depression. The authors also speculate about transfer to Alzheimer comorbidity. The reported Alzheimer comparator outcomes are cognitive or functional and do not establish a ground-saffron depression result in Parkinson disease. The Parkinson sections emphasize experimental neuroprotection, dopamine-related markers and model observations. They supply no separately measured fatigue comparison from dried saffron.
