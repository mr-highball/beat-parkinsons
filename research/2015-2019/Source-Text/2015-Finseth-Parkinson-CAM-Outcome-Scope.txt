Reported measures in a Parkinson complementary medicine survey
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4363882/

March 2015. An anonymous complementary medicine survey reports respondent perceptions of symptom and quality-of-life changes. The limitations identify pain as a possible treatment-use reason that was not asked, and the discussion identifies apathy as an unexamined possible adverse effect. No dried-date exposure was analyzed.
