Parkinson-related reports with separate scopes

Member 1189 (publisher PDF page 548) reports worse motor performance and greater nigrostriatal vulnerability in diabetic mice under a neural injury challenge. The later full report adds other diabetes and signaling comparisons. The overlapping comparison belongs to the same research line. See research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32666590-Diabetes-Dopaminergic-Vulnerability.txt.

Member 1257 (publisher PDF page 579) reports an investigational triple receptor agonist and motor and tissue outcomes in a Parkinson mouse model. A 2019 report describes follow-up preclinical work on the same drug and a different injury model. See https://diabetesjournals.org/diabetes/article/68/Supplement_1/1810-P/59199/1810-P-Effect-of-HM15211-a-Novel-Long-Acting-GLP-1. These reports were screened separately from the conference's studies of particular foods.
