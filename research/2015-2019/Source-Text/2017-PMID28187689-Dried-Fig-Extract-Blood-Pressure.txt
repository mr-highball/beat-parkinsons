Title: Evaluation of antihypertensive potential of Ficus carica fruit
Source: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.1080/13880209.2017.1278611
Source alias: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28187689/
PMID: 28187689
PMCID: PMC6130763
DOI: 10.1080/13880209.2017.1278611
Publication date: 2017-02-10
Date basis: Publisher online DOI deposit, Pharmaceutical Biology 55(1), 1047–1053.
Text coverage: Original publisher article and tables inspected. Research summary.

The investigators prepared an aqueous methanol extract from authenticated dried ripe fig fruit. They measured blood pressure in normal rats and rats with glucose-induced hypertension, and tested heart contraction and rate in isolated hearts. The higher tested concentration gave lower pressures in the rat experiments and the isolated preparation showed reduced contraction and rate. Fruit processing and route matter for interpretation. This was a solvent extract and animal study, with no whole-fruit diet or Parkinson visual outcome.
