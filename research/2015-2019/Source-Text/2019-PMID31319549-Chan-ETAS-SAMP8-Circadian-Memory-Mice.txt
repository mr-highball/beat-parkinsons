Title: A Standardized Extract of Asparagus officinalis Stem (ETAS®) Ameliorates Cognitive Impairment, Inhibits Amyloid β Deposition via BACE-1 and Normalizes Circadian Rhythm Signaling via MT1 and MT2.
PMID: 31319549
PMCID: PMC6683278
DOI: 10.3390/nu11071631
First published: 2019
Authors: Chan YC, Wu CS, Wu TC, Lin YH, Chang SJ
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31319549/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical aging mouse study (Chan et al., 2019, Nutrients, PMC6683278) feeding 3-month-old SAMP8 mice ETAS (Asparagus officinalis stem extract, 200 or 1,000 mg/kg/day) for 12 weeks vs SAMP8 control and SAMR1 normal control. ETAS improved active avoidance memory performance, reduced brain APP/BACE-1 and A-beta deposition, increased suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) neuron counts, and normalized melatonin receptor MT1 and MT2 expression. Limits: non-PD senescence/Alzheimer's mouse model using standardized stem extract; measures circadian receptor histology and avoidance memory rather than clinical insomnia or Parkinson's symptoms.
