Title: A phase I trial of mushroom powder in patients with biochemically recurrent prostate cancer: Roles of cytokines and myeloid-derived suppressor cells for Agaricus bisporus-induced prostate-specific antigen responses.
PMID: 25989179
PMCID: PMC5685188
DOI: 10.1002/cncr.29421
First published: 2015
Authors: Twardowski P, Kanaya N, Frankel P, Synold T, Ruel C, Pal SK
Journal: Cancer
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5685188/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Phase I dose-escalation trial in n=36 men with biochemically recurrent prostate cancer consuming white button mushroom (A. bisporus) powder (4-14 g/d). Well tolerated with zero dose-limiting toxicities; 11% achieved PSA complete/partial response (2 CR lasting 30-49 mo, 2 PR) and 36% had any PSA decline at 3 months alongside lower MDSCs. Did NOT report urinary urgency, IPSS, or LUTS symptom endpoints.
