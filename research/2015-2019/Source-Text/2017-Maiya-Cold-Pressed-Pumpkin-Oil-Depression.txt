The randomized open-label study assigned 27 postmenopausal participants to cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil or raw pumpkin seeds for 12 weeks. Figure 6a displays the individual depression item. In the oil group, 11 of 13 were rated absent and two of 13 mild at both baseline and follow-up. This is an unchanged aggregate distribution, which does not establish unchanged individual trajectories.

The seed comparator shows four absent and three mild at each time, totaling seven despite a caption n of 14. The denominator is unexplained and the source gives no item-specific between-arm test. The total of 17 menopausal symptoms improved more within the oil group and is a separate outcome. The trial does not measure Parkinson depression.

Trial identity: NCT02727036 and conference abstract P08-074, PMC6265820, describe this same Texas cohort. This family is distinct from the 2011 Gossell-Williams trial.
