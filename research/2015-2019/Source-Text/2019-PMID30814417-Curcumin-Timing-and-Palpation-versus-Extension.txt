Twenty-four healthy men were assigned in equal groups to branded curcumin before or after eccentric exercise, or placebo. Figure 5 separates soreness elicited by palpation and by extending the elbow. The post-exercise group had lower palpation soreness at day three against both comparison groups and lower extension soreness at day four against placebo, as reported in the main text. The pre-exercise condition did not show a clear soreness advantage over placebo. The concentration and torque results answer different questions. Individual plotted pain values are not presented as transcribed exact means here.

The comparison is single-blind with eight people per group. The study measures exercise-induced soreness after a concentrated formulation, and supplies no clinical pain comparison after eating ground turmeric. A separate 2019 crossover paper from the same research group supplies related timing evidence, and participant overlap has not been definitively reconciled.
