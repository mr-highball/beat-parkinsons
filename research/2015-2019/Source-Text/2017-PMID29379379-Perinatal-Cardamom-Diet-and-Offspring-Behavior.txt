Original source scope
The study assigned dams high dietary proportions of Elettaria cardamomum or a control diet. Offspring assessment included growth, developmental timing, shuttle-box behavior, maze learning and tissue chemistry. The maze findings differed by task.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Perinatal exposure, developmental changes and learning tasks do not estimate depressive symptoms after household ground cardamom in adults or in Parkinson disease.
