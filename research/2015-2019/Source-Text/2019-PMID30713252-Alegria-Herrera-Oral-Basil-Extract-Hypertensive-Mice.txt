Title: Effect of Ocimum basilicum, Ocimum selloi, and Rosmarinic Acid on Cerebral Vascular Damage in a Chronic Hypertension Model.
PMID: 30713252
DOI: 10.1248/bpb.b18-00574
First published: 2019
Authors: Alegría-Herrera E, Herrera-Ruiz M, Román-Ramos R, Zamilpa A, Santillán-Urquiza MA, Aguilar MI, Avilés-Flores M, Fuentes-Mata M, Jiménez-Ferrer E
Journal: Biological & pharmaceutical bulletin
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30713252/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical chronic angiotensin-II hypertension mouse model (12 weeks i.p. AGII) evaluating 6 weeks of ORAL Ocimum basilicum ethyl-acetate extract (Oba-EtOAc), Ocimum selloi extract, and rosmarinic acid. Oral O. basilicum extract attenuated AGII-induced corticosterone elevation, proinflammatory cytokines (IL-1beta, IL-6, TNF-alpha, MCP-1), EPM anxiety-like behavior, and Morris water maze cognitive deficits.
