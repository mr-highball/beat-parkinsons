Title: Rytary approval package, hallucination event definitions in the IPX066 studies
Source: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2015/203312Orig1s000MedR.pdf
Original labeling: https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2015/203312s000lbl.pdf
Publication date: 2015
Date basis: Original FDA public approval package year. The package contains reviews prepared before publication.
Source depth: Primary regulatory case narratives and tables, and historical FDA label section 5.4
Format: Factual digest of the original material

ADVANCE-PD, study B09-02, corresponds to NCT00974974. During open-label conversion, FDA reviewer Table 65 lists three of 450 early terminations with a hallucinations term. The subsequent 2015 conversion-paper summary lists two of 450 discontinuations with the specific visual hallucinations preferred term. Different coding breadth can account for different totals. The available reports do not provide a one-to-one patient crosswalk between those tallies.

In the double-blind maintenance phase, the 2015 label reports hallucinations or psychosis in 9 of 201 extended-release participants and 2 of 192 immediate-release participants. This is a combined person-level count. In a maintenance narrative, the regulatory review describes a participant in the extended-release arm who developed visual hallucinations and anxiety, withdrew, and then improved. The narrative does not give a series of discrete visual episodes or establish the visual proportion of the combined label count. Table 66 and the narrative appear on printed pages 122–123 of the clinical review.

ASCEND-PD, study B09-06, corresponds to NCT01130493. Table 70 covers open conversion and Table 73 covers the 91-person randomized crossover, which measured off time and recorded confusional state as an adverse-event category. Those distinct treatment and phase denominators cannot be combined with the 201/192 arms of ADVANCE-PD. The 2015 conversion article links both cohorts without creating a third trial.

The 2015 FDA record documents adverse events associated with drug formulations and phases in the approval package. It supplies no culinary dried fig exposure or estimate of Parkinson visual hallucination episodes per unit time.
