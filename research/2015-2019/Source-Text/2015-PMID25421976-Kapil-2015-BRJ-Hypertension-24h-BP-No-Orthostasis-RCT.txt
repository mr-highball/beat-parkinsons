Title: Dietary nitrate provides sustained blood pressure lowering in hypertensive patients: a randomized, phase 2, double-blind, placebo-controlled study.
PMID: 25421976
PMCID: PMC4288952
DOI: 10.1161/HYPERTENSIONAHA.114.04675
Registry ID: NCT01405898
First published: 2015
Authors: Kapil V, Khambata RS, Robertson A, Caulfield MJ, Ahluwalia A
Journal: Hypertension (Dallas, Tex. : 1979)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25421976/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

4-week double-blind randomized placebo-controlled parallel trial (PMC4288952) in n=68 hypertensive adults (64 analyzed) receiving 250 mL/d nitrate-rich (~6.4 mmol) vs nitrate-depleted beetroot juice: lowered clinic BP by 7.7/2.4 mmHg (p<0.001/0.050), 24-h ambulatory BP by 7.7/5.2 mmHg (p<0.001), and home BP by 8.1/3.8 mmHg. Explicitly reported zero serious adverse events and no adverse effects (no dizziness/orthostatic symptoms) other than expected beeturia and fecal discoloration; flagged oxalate kidney stone caution. Limits: hypertensive non-PD adults without neurogenic orthostatic hypotension.
