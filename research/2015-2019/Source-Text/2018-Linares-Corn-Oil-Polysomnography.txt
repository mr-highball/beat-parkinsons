Original Methods and Tables 2–3: 27 healthy volunteers selected and randomized by initial order; one withdrew and 26 were analyzed after both overnight polysomnography visits. Cannabidiol dissolved in corn oil and equal corn oil without cannabidiol were encapsulated identically. No refining grade reported. Screening PSQI total 4; screening scale is not a post-treatment outcome. Table 3 provides 389.3 versus 388.1 minutes of total sleep, 13.4 versus 9.8 minutes of sleep onset latency, 46.2 versus 47.10 minutes awake after sleep onset, and 85.3% versus 87.1% sleep efficiency, all placebo/carrier only versus added drug, and all reported nonsignificant. This design cannot isolate corn oil because both arms received it. No insomnia patient cohort and no Parkinson subgroup.
