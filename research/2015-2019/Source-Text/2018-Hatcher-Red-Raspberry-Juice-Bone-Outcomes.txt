Red raspberry juice and bone outcomes in postmenopausal women
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6265829/

The 2018 author abstract reports a six-month randomized comparison in 57 postmenopausal women with osteopenia. Thirty were assigned two ounces of red raspberry juice concentrate mixed with water, and 27 received a matched placebo. The report examines bone biochemical markers and density, with fruit species, cultivar and mood or anxiety effects unreported. A possible earlier thesis under a matching title is an open publication history lead. The result does not substantiate the separate 16-week mixed berry and mood claim.
