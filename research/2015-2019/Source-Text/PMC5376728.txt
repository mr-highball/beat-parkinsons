Title: Niemann-Pick type C disease - the tip of the iceberg? A review of neuropsychiatric presentation, diagnosis and treatment

This review describes psychiatric and movement manifestations of Niemann–Pick disease type C, with visual hallucinations discussed in its differential diagnosis. It does not compare a dietary exposure or episodes in idiopathic Parkinson disease.
