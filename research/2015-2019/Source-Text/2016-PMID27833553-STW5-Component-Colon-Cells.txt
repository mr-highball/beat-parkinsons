Title: Fixed STW 5 and STW 5-II compared with individual extracts in colon cells
First published: 2016-10-26
PMID: 27833553
PMCID: PMC5080345
DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2016.00393
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5080345/
Archive scope: Original formulation table, Results and figure captions summarized.

The primary laboratory study compared two fixed herbal combinations and constituent extracts in a colon epithelial cell model. The formulation table identifies lemon balm as the KVIII constituent at 10% of STW 5 and 15% of STW 5-II. The combinations reduced several induced inflammatory readouts, with differences by concentration and marker. The paper reports limited effects for most isolated extracts and identifies Chelidonium as the only single extract significantly reducing IL-8 in the tested comparison. The data therefore do not isolate a general lemon balm explanation for combination effects. They also provide no clinical outcome, brewed preparation or depression measure.
