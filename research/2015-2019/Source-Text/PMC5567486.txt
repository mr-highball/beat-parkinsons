The clinical comparison used a five-odor identification and sensitivity array in Parkinson groups and healthy controls. The study's diagnosis-related odors are distinct from the five-item Australian test mentioned in discussion, which lists cinnamon. An odor's ability to distinguish a group is a diagnostic result. This clinical comparison did not test consumption of ground cinnamon for impaired smell.
