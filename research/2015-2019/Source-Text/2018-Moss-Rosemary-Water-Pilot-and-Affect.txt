Moss M, McMullon M, McDonald H. A Pilot Study of the Acute Ingestion of No. 1 Rosemary Water: Evidence of Cognitive, Physiological and Subjective Effects in Healthy Adults. Advances in Chemical Engineering and Science. 2018;8:190–203. Published 2018-07-26.
DOI: 10.4236/aces.2018.83013
Primary source: https://nrl.northumbria.ac.uk/id/eprint/35197/1/No_1_Rosemary_Water_Paper_1.pdf
Coverage: factual summary of full institutional original, Methods and Table 2.

Twenty healthy adults were allocated to a commercial carbonated extract/hydrolat drink or carbonated water. Table 2 positive-affect mean change was +1.40 compared with +0.20; the corresponding negative-affect changes were −1.80 versus −0.90. Calmness changed +10.00 in both groups, although the narrative describes a greater calmness change for rosemary. The report presents exploratory effect sizes without significance tests. No depression outcome or confirmed fresh-leaf exposure was supplied.
