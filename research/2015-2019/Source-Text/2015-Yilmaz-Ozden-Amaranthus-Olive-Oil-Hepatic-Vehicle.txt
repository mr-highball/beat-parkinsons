Title: Protective effect of Amaranthus lividus L. on carbon tetrachloride induced hepatotoxicity in rats
Source: https://doi.org/10.5505/tjb.2015.05935
DOI: 10.5505/tjb.2015.05935
Publication date: 2015
Journal: Turkish Journal of Biochemistry 40(2), 125–131
Author-deposited primary text: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/276240475
Text coverage: Distilled original paper as deposited by the authors, including the treatment-group and vehicle passages.

The seven-group liver study included three control groups of five animals each and four injury groups of seven animals each. The three controls received injected olive oil without the toxicant. The injury groups received the toxicant in olive oil. Oral Amaranthus extract, silymarin or carrier assignments created comparisons on hepatic markers and tissue effects. The vehicle was shared, and the paper does not report oil grade or measure anxiety.

FEBS poster P18.29 reports a five-group version with the same liver endpoint family, shared investigators and similar treatment conditions. The article adds extract-only controls. The seven-group paper and five-group poster are closely related and independence is unverified. The 2014 electronic renal report is a distinct publication with different outcome scope and uncertain cohort overlap.
