Pooled fish and olfactory impairment in the Blue Mountains cohort

Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114515001257
Coverage: Primary full article, Tables 3 and 4
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published June 16, 2015. The 145-item food questionnaire pooled fish and the eight-odor San Diego test assessed identification in older community adults. For top versus bottom fish-intake quartile, adjusted odds ratios in 1,331 participants were 0.65 (95% CI 0.44–0.96) for prevalent impairment, 0.50 (0.31–0.82) for mild, and 0.86 (0.51–1.45) for moderate or severe. Over five years in 667 reexamined participants the corresponding incident estimate was 1.23 (0.75–2.02). Total and long-chain omega-3 intake did not show an independent association. The pooled exposure, different prevalence and incidence results, and non-Parkinson population leave baked cod unresolved.
