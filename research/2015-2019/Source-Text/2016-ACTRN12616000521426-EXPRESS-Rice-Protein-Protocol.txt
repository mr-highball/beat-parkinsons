Title: EXPRESS rice-protein and whey-protein comparison with a shared exercise program
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=370522
Registration: ACTRN12616000521426
Universal trial number: U1111-1182-0484
Publication date: 2016-04-21
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Original ANZCTR record and companion journal protocol.

The planned older-adult comparison used two reconstituted powders, rice protein and whey protein, each with the same exercise program. The protocol specifies a physical-performance battery, Timed Up and Go and walking speed among outcomes. Rice variety and culinary grain preparation were not identified. The registry last updated December 9, 2016 and still displays recruiting status. The matched 2021 original outcome abstract confirms that a comparison was reported.
