Title: Case Report: Severe Hematological, Muscle and Liver Toxicity Caused by Drugs and Artichoke Infusion Interaction in an Elderly Polymedicated Patient.
PMID: 28901251
DOI: 10.2174/1574886312666170912163746
First published: 2018
Authors: Campos, Machado, Costa, Lino, Correia
Journal: Current drug safety
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28901251/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Clinical case report of 1 polymedicated elderly man with diabetes, hypertension, and gout (taking metformin, gliclazide, enalapril/HCTZ, amlodipine, aspirin, diazepam, colchicine, clonixin) who consumed ~1.5 L Cynara cardunculus infusion and developed distal muscle pain, severe anemia, and biochemical cholestasis after agricultural work. Extensive workup ruled out autoimmune/infectious causes; authors attributed toxicity to CYP3A4/2C9 inhibition potentiating colchicine toxicity, with complete spontaneous recovery. Limits: single polymedicated drug-interaction case with large infusion volume.
