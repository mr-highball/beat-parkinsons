Title: Banisteriopsis caapi, a Forgotten Potential Therapy for Parkinson's Disease?
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6353393/
PMCID: PMC6353393
PMID: 30713897
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12242
Publication date: 2015-10-06
Date basis: NLM and Wiley electronic publication. Journal issue 2016.
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Evidence type: Historical clinical viewpoint
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Purified harmine, single-vine Banisteriopsis caapi extract and customary mixed-plant ceremonial beverages are distinct preparations. Historical harmine reports had inconsistent tremor results. The viewpoint cites a 30-person 2001 controlled trial in which the extract improved a composite motor score while tremor worsened, and describes one transient visual hallucination. The primary 2001 paper labels that event as hallucinations of unspecified modality. A favorable historical use account, the trial and its later retelling are not independent demonstrations of the same effect. There is no dried fig intervention.
