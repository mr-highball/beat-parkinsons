Of 107 randomized participants, 106 entered the efficacy analysis, 53 in each group. TOLEDO's main endpoint was daily off time, not recurrent visual hallucinations. The report lists two hallucination adverse-event cases in each arm in the neuropsychiatric summary and separately lists a visual hallucination withdrawal during apomorphine treatment. These counts should not be added without an event-level linkage. Moderate psychosis was excluded, while very mild visual phenomena with insight were allowed. The trial is NCT02006121, distinct from the registered refractory visual hallucination study NCT02702076.
