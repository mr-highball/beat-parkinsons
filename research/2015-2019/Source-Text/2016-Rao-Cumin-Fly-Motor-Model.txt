Text coverage: selected facts paraphrased from the primary report, Methods and Figure 1. The public author-posted paper indicates December 2016 with a day placeholder and pages 1–11 from pre-pagination.

The study ground cumin seed from a market, extracted it in water, separated the solids and concentrated the liquid to a dried residue. Adult male Drosophila exposed to rotenone for seven days received medium supplemented with an aqueous cumin extract or a separate cinnamon extract. Locomotor performance was the proportion passing a climbing threshold. The higher cumin extract groups improved climbing in Figure 1B. Other groups used separately acquired isolated cuminaldehyde or cinnamaldehyde, not mixed spice powder. Figure 1's caption specifies 25 and three replicates, whereas the general Methods text states 50 flies per replicate and three replicates. Preserve the distinction and uncertainty. No levodopa challenge, human Parkinson diagnosis, or daily OFF-duration assessment was performed.
