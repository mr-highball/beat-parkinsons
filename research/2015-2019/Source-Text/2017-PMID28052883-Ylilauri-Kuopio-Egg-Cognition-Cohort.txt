Title: Association of dietary cholesterol and egg intakes with the risk of incident dementia or Alzheimer disease: the Kuopio Ischaemic Heart Disease Risk Factor Study.
PMID: 28052883
DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.116.146753
First published: 2017
Authors: Ylilauri MP, Voutilainen S, Lönnroos E, Mursu J, Virtanen HE, Koskinen TT
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28052883/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Prospective population-based Kuopio Ischaemic Heart Disease Risk Factor Study (n=2,497 dementia-free Finnish men aged 42-60 y followed 21.9 y; n=480 with 4-year neuropsychological re-examination). Incident dementia/AD hazard ratios per +0.5 egg/d crossed unity (HR 0.89, 95% CI 0.78-1.01, null), while higher baseline egg intake correlated with better 4-year performance on Trail Making and Verbal Fluency tests in men regardless of APOE4 status. Male-only non-PD observational cohort without preparation specificity.
