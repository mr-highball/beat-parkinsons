Title: Whole Red Grape Juice Reduces Blood Pressure at Rest and Increases Post-exercise Hypotension.
PMID: 28853994
DOI: 10.1080/07315724.2017.1331385
First published: 2017
Authors: Neto MM, da Silva TF, de Lima FF, Siqueira TMQ, Toscano LT, de Moura SKMSF, Silva AS
Journal: Journal of the American College of Nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28853994/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind randomized 28-day trial in 26 adults with hypertension (14 red grape juice 100-150 mL/day vs 12 control) assessed before/after 60-min treadmill exercise. Overall resting BP and post-exercise hypotension did NOT differ significantly in the experimental group; only post-hoc subgroup with controlled baseline BP showed resting SBP reduction (133.3 to 114.6 mmHg, p=0.02) and potentiated post-exercise BP dip. No orthostatic lightheadedness measured.
