Title: Postprandial serum endotoxin in healthy humans is modulated by dietary fat in a randomized, controlled, cross-over study
Publication date: 2016-11-05
PMID: 27816052
PMCID: PMC5097840
DOI: 10.1186/s12944-016-0357-6
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5097840/
Text coverage: Original trial's randomized meal composition and clinical scope.

Isoenergetic test meals supplied lower total fat with olive oil or higher fat emphasizing DHA-rich fish oil, grapeseed oil or coconut oil. The reported saturated-fat coconut meal produced higher postprandial serum endotoxin than the n-3 meal. The inflammation markers and timing do not establish an executive, fatigue or fall effect. Processing grade is unspecified and no Parkinson patient outcome is reported. The later Chianese review summarizes this family.
