Title: Effects of Psidium guajava Linn. leaf extract in Japanese subjects with knee pain: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel pilot study
PMID: 29687303
DOI: 10.1007/s40520-018-0953-6
First published: 2018
Authors: Kakuo S, Fushimi T, Kawasaki K, Nakamura J, Ota N
Journal: Aging Clinical and Experimental Research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29687303/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Twelve-week double-blind placebo-controlled parallel pilot RCT in 53 Japanese adults with untreated knee pain randomized to 1 g/d Psidium guajava LEAF extract or placebo. At week 12, JKOM knee pain/stiffness subcategory and VAS knee pain were lower in the guava leaf extract group than placebo. STRICT PLANT-PART SEPARATION: Intervention was concentrated guava LEAF extract (distinct quercetin-glycoside/tannin/sesquiterpene composition), NOT raw culinary guava fruit; non-PD osteoarthritis knee pain cohort.
