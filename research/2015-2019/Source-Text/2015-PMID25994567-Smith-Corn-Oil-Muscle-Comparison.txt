Title: Fish oil-derived n-3 PUFA therapy increases muscle mass and function in healthy older adults
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4480667/
PMID: 25994567
PMCID: PMC4480667
DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.114.105833
Publication date: 2015-05-20
Text coverage: Full original paper, including randomized and completer counts, muscle results, and Table 3.

The six-month trial randomized 60 healthy older adults, 20 to corn oil and 40 to fish oil. Fifteen and 29 completed, respectively. Fish oil improved thigh muscle volume, handgrip and a combined strength measure relative to the comparator. Those outcomes did not measure daily-life falls. The paper mentions falls as clinical background.

Treatment was initiated by 18 corn-oil recipients and 35 fish-oil recipients. Table 3 lists adverse events for those groups. It includes isolated symptoms, diagnoses and injuries, but no separate fall outcome or rate. Cause of the reported dislocated shoulder in the fish-oil arm is not given. The adverse-event table does not establish zero falls or a corn-oil-specific fall effect.
