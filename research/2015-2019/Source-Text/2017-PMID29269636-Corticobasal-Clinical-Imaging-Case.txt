Title: Diagnosing Corticobasal Syndrome Based on the Presence of Visual Hallucinations and Imaging with Amyloid Positron Emission Tomography.
PMID: 29269636
PMCID: PMC5849562
DOI: 10.2169/internalmedicine.8534-16
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5849562/
First publication date: 2017-12-21 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The case discusses visual hallucinations as a diagnostic clue in corticobasal syndrome with amyloid imaging and an alternative Lewy body explanation. The observed course and imaging are a differential diagnosis, without dietary exposure or a measured after-fig change.
