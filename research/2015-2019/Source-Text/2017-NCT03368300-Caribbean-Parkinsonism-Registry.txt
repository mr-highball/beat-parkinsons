Caribbean parkinsonism registry and dietary survey scope
https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03368300

First posted December 11, 2017. The ClinicalTrials.gov record describes characterization of typical and atypical parkinsonism in the French Caribbean and Guyana, with a secondary objective concerning dietary exposures in the three regions. The currently displayed overall status is unknown, and the record has no separate posted results. The published 2022 and 2026 analyses use this study identifier. The registry does not identify a kiwi exposure or a fruit intervention.
