COMBAT cranberry powder and planned depression questionnaire
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/editorial/retrieveFile/21f15c36-e587-4ecd-858f-d2ae68f1b3ce/35927
Registration: ISRCTN76069316 and NCT03679533
Coverage: Public original protocol v4 dated May 7, 2019, pages 20 and 24; first public upload date unavailable

The COMBAT protocol schedules the PHQ-9 depression questionnaire at screening and after a 12-week comparison of freeze-dried cranberry powder and matched placebo in healthy older adults. The main registry outcome list does not name PHQ-9. ClinicalTrials.gov first posted September 20, 2018 and ISRCTN registration dates to November 13, 2018. The 2022 article randomized 29 to cranberry and 31 to placebo. The body and complete supplementary data document cognition, perfusion and other measures, with no reported PHQ-9 comparison. The 2023 biochemical report explicitly names the same registry identifiers. Whether the questionnaire was collected and analyzed remains open.
