Title: Postprandial Hypotension and Coma Following Subarachnoid Hemorrhage in a Patient with Parkinson's Disease
Authors: Jun Watanabe, Jun Maruya, Kenjyu Hara, Keiichi Nishimaki
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26884093/
DOI: 10.1016/j.jstrokecerebrovasdis.2016.01.019
Date: February 13, 2016 online; April 2016 issue, volume 25 pages e58–e61
Coverage: factual extract of publicly available primary abstract

A 79-year-old woman with Parkinson disease developed after-meal hypotension and reduced consciousness on postoperative days seven to nine following aneurysm clipping. The publication describes cessation of the episodes after midodrine was administered. The authors discuss possible cerebral autoregulation impairment in the setting of vasospasm, alongside autonomic dysfunction. The indexed report does not supply the meal composition, body position or a severity scale for orthostatic lightheadedness.
