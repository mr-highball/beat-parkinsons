Title: Effects of thymol on amyloid-β-induced impairments in hippocampal synaptic plasticity in rats fed a high-fat diet
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29339105/
PMID: 29339105
DOI: 10.1016/j.brainresbull.2018.01.008
Publication date: 2018-01
Date basis: PubMed electronic article date. Print issue March 2018, volume 137, pages 338–350.
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled official PubMed author abstract, distinct from the 2017 behavior paper. Full original figures and protocols remain open.

Asadbegi and colleagues assigned 72 male Wistar rats to nine groups. The abstract lists a standard-diet control, standard diet with phosphate-buffered saline and oil vehicle, high-fat diet with saline, high-fat diet with amyloid, high-fat diet with amyloid and oil vehicle, and four thymol arms under different regimens. The abstract calls the vehicle oil and leaves its identity and preparation unspecified. The 2017 separate five-arm paper named sunflower as its vehicle.

The 2018 article's outcomes are hippocampal field potentials after stimulation, including measures of long-term potentiation, and biochemical markers. The abstract describes worse synaptic responses after high-fat feeding plus amyloid and attenuation with thymol. It gives no separate oil-versus-no-oil estimate, whole-animal executive outcome or Parkinson group. Even if the oils match across the two papers, comparing their numbers requires the separate methods, treatment schedules and cohort identities.
