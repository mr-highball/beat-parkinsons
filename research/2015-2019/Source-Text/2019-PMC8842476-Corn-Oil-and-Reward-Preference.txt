Title: British Neuroscience Association abstracts — corn-oil vehicle and flavored milk preference in separate members
PMCID: PMC8842476
DOI: 10.1177/2398212819855490
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8842476/
First published: 2019-05-23 in publisher citation metadata.
Coverage: Original individual member contexts in the complete posted collection.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18 UTC

PT080 reports an injected corn-oil control and a chemically induced perimenopause model using the same vehicle. A related member describes the same group structure and interventions with additional measures. PT080 reports higher percentage correct alternation in controls than in induced and aged groups. The comparison does not independently assign corn oil and does not use an oral food exposure. Keep potential cohort overlap open.

A separate object-recognition methods member reports a liquid preference test conducted in a separate mouse cohort. Soy and almond milk were preferred over strawberry-flavored and chocolate milk. The strawberry flavor was used in the preference test and did not determine the automated-versus-manual recognition comparison. This study design does not provide a raw-strawberry effect or fall-related clinical outcome.
