Title: Effect of the auricular acupoint associated with physical exercise in elderly people
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1016/j.jams.2018.05.003
PMID: 29753704
DOI: 10.1016/j.jams.2018.05.003
Publication date: 2018-05-16
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Original full journal text, methods and Table 4.

Twenty-two older adults were randomized to exercise alone or the same exercise with whole mustard seeds used as auricular acupressure material. Group sizes were 11 each. The authors report no significant additional effect on mobility, balance, or fear of falling. Table 4 reports between-group p=.12 for Timed Up and Go, p=.66 for concern about falling, and p=.31 for the Berg Balance Scale. Both groups improved within groups.

These are proxy measures. The trial does not compare oral ground mustard or count falls in a Parkinson cohort.
