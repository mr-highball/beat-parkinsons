Title: Single-arm Daikenchuto comparison before and after treatment for abdominal bloating
PMID: 27069528
PMCID: PMC4812817
DOI: 10.1016/j.curtheres.2015.04.002
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4812817/
Published: 2015-05-04
Archive scope: Evidence-focused summary of the inspected original article body.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Ten participants with constipation and bloating received the same Daikenchuto formulation after suspending as-needed laxatives. Breath testing divided the sample into four with small intestinal bacterial overgrowth and six without. Both subgroups received the intervention. The article describes a randomized open trial in its methods, while the registry specifies a nonrandomized, open, uncontrolled single arm and the paper reports no treatment allocation between groups. The article states 15 g daily for 14 days and the registry lists 1.5 g daily for 17 days. The administered schedule cannot be reconciled from these documents.

The median bloating visual-analog score moved from 76 to 30 (p=.005). The constipation subscale decreased while stool frequency and stool form showed no clear changes. The face-scale abdominal pain comparison yielded p=.039 and the GSRS abdominal pain subscale was inconclusive. The publication excluded Parkinson disease. The formula uses processed ginger, ginseng and Japanese pepper, with no individual ingredient contrast.
