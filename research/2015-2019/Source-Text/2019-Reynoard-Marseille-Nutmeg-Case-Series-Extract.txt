Marseille poison-center signs and background animal tremor
September 19, 2019 · PMID 31543391 · DOI 10.1016/j.lpm.2019.08.016
https://www.em-consulte.com/showarticlefile/1326067/main.pdf
Factual extract from original publisher body, Methods, Table I and Discussion.

Of 31 Marseille poison-center exposure reports, 22 had sufficient data for analysis. Eleven were intentional and eleven unintentional. Powder predominated, with some whole seed. Table I groups neurological and psychiatric signs and does not enumerate individual tremor findings. Tremor in the Discussion refers to earlier animal literature. The body supplies case-provenance and symptom-reporting context, with no Parkinson rest-tremor comparison.
