Title: Long-Term Dietary Supplementation of Pomegranates, Figs and Dates Alleviate Neuroinflammation in a Transgenic Mouse Model of Alzheimer's Disease
PMID: 25807081
PMCID: PMC4373715
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0120964
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4373715/
First publication date: 2015-03-25
Coverage: Complete original JATS, preparation, separate diet arms and reported endpoints.

The study began with fresh fruit, froze it, ground it to powder, and incorporated each fruit separately at 4% by weight into chow. The methods describe frozen and ground material rather than ordinary sun-dried figs. Five groups of 16 animals included wild-type control, transgenic control and separate pomegranate, fig and date arms of APPsw/Tg2576 mice. The diets were evaluated after 15 months.

Measured outcomes included circulating inflammatory markers and brain inflammatory and related laboratory outcomes. The reported direction varies by biomarker and fruit, with some lower inflammatory markers in the fig arm. These outcomes do not measure visual hallucinations, and overlapping investigators, diet and model mean this publication cannot be assumed independent of the behavioral report.
