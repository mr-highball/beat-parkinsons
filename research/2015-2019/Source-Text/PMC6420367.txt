Title: 18F‐AV‐1451 uptake differs between dementia with lewy bodies and posterior cortical atrophy
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6420367/
PMID: 30615804
PMCID: PMC6420367
DOI: 10.1002/mds.27603
Publication date: 2019-01-07
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in the original PMC and PubMed metadata.
Design: Clinical tau PET comparison

Investigators compared 33 dementia-with-Lewy-bodies patients, 18 posterior cortical atrophy patients and 100 cognitively unimpaired controls. Clinical assessment identified visual hallucinations in 13 of the Lewy body group and two of the atrophy group. The PET contrast concerns tracer uptake and syndrome differentiation. The clinical counts describe symptom presence.
