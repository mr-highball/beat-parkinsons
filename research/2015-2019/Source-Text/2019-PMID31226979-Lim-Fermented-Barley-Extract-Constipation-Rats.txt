Title: Laxative effects of triple fermented barley extracts (FBe) on loperamide (LP)-induced constipation in rats.
PMID: 31226979
PMCID: PMC6587300
DOI: 10.1186/s12906-019-2557-x
First published: 2019
Authors: Lim JM, Kim YD, Song CH, Park SJ, Park DC, Cho HR
Journal: BMC complementary and alternative medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31226979/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled rat experiment (PMC6587300, n=48 Sprague-Dawley rats, 6 groups of 8) testing oral triple-fermented barley extract (100-300 mg/kg x 6 d) in loperamide-induced spastic constipation. Increased GI charcoal transit ratio and colonic mucosal goblet cells while reducing retained fecal pellets. Limits: fermented extract in rodent loperamide model, not cooked barley in PD.
