Title: Effect of Guava in Blood Glucose and Lipid Profile in Healthy Human Subjects: A Randomized Controlled Study
PMID: 27790420
PMCID: PMC5071920
DOI: 10.7860/JCDR/2016/21291.8425
First published: 2016
Authors: Kumari S, Rakavi R, Mangaraj M
Journal: Journal of Clinical and Diagnostic Research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27790420/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Six-week 3-arm RCT in 45 healthy young MBBS students (15/arm): Group A 400 g/d ripe guava fruit with peel, Group B 400 g/d ripe guava pulp without peel, Group C control. Both guava arms reported significant reductions in BMI and seated blood pressure (p<0.05); peeled pulp lowered total cholesterol, triglycerides, and LDL-C (p<0.05) and raised HDL-C, whereas with-peel arm increased FPG, total cholesterol, and triglycerides (p<0.05). Direct ripe guava fruit exposure, but normotensive young student cohort with seated BP/metabolic endpoints only and no standing orthostatic challenge, lightheadedness, or PD symptom outcomes.
