Title: MRI of the Swallow Tail Sign: A Useful Marker in the Diagnosis of Lewy Body Dementia?
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7963704/
PMCID: PMC7963704
PMID: 28705819
DOI: 10.3174/ajnr.a5274
Publication date: 2017-07-13
Date basis: NLM electronic date; PMC September issue
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Format: Factual digest of the inspected material

The multicenter study examines the MRI swallow-tail sign in 97 participants across dementia, mild cognitive impairment and control groups. The imaging sign concerns nigrosome visibility, while visual hallucinations appear among clinical features of Lewy body dementia. There is no dried fig exposure contrast or visual episode count.
