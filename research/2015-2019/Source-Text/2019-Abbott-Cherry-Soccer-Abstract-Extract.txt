Abbott W, Brashill C, Brett A, Clifford T. Original author abstract. PMID 31188696. DOI 10.1123/ijspp.2019-0221. PubMed lists electronic publication October 28, 2019 and journal issue February 2020. OpenAlex reports June 12, 2019. The first electronic day remains unresolved.
Ten male professional soccer players entered a randomized blinded crossover of tart cherry juice and a cherry-flavored calorie-matched control around 90-minute matches. The abstract reports no clear treatment-by-time effect on aggregate subjective well-being (p = .874) or soreness (p = .808). Questionnaire wording, individual fatigue or energy estimates and detailed dosing were not recovered from the publisher body. The abstract establishes athlete recovery context only.
