Source: Mu X, Yuan X, Du LD, He GR, Du GH. Antagonism of quercetin against tremor induced by unilateral striatal lesion of 6-OHDA in rats.
Journal: Journal of Asian Natural Products Research (2016)
Identifiers: PMID:26217978 | DOI:10.1080/10286020.2015.1057576
Source URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26217978/
Access basis: Official PubMed XML abstract (paywalled full text).

Study summary and reported findings
- Evaluated the anti-tremor effects of isolated quercetin in a unilateral striatal 6-hydroxydopamine (6-OHDA)-lesioned rat model of Parkinson's disease.
- Quercetin showed no effect on apomorphine-induced contralateral rotations, but significantly attenuated muscle tremor in 6-OHDA-lesioned rats, decreasing tremor burst frequency in a dose- and time-dependent manner.
- The authors noted the anti-tremor effect may involve the serotonergic system and requires further mechanistic confirmation.

Relevance and limits for Parkinson's food experiments
- Direct preclinical Parkinson's tremor model demonstrating attenuation of 6-OHDA rat muscle tremor burst frequency by isolated quercetin.
- Limits: Tests purified quercetin rather than a specific whole culinary food (quercetin occurs widely across apples, onions, capers, tea, and berries, so it cannot isolate raw apple consumption alone); exact dosing schedule, route, group sizes, and numerical burst-frequency tables require full-text access.
