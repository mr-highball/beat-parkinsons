Title: Hypermanganesemia with Dystonia 1: A Novel Mutation and Response to Iron Supplementation.
PMID: 31970220
PMCID: PMC6962664
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12861
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6962664/
First publication date: 2019-11-12 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC complete original article HTML and original clinical or laboratory methods.

The inherited hypermanganesemia and dystonia case describes psychosis and a course after oral iron. The physiological context and treatment are distinct from Parkinson disease and dried fig exposure. It supplies no food-attributed rate of visual episodes.
