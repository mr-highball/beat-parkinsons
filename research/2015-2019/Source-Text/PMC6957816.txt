Title: Differences in early and late pattern-onset visual-evoked potentials between self- reported migraineurs and controls

Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Design: Visual-evoked response comparison in migraine

Investigators compared visual evoked potentials in 29 women with self-reported migraine and 31 healthy comparison participants. Perceptual symptom discussion and cortical responses concern migraine, with no ingestion-linked Parkinson visual outcomes.
