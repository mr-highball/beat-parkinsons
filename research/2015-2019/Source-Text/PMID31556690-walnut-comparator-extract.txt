Title: Trial of Essential Oils to Improve Sleep for Patients in Cardiac Rehabilitation
Citation: McDonnell B, Newcomb P. J Altern Complement Med. 2019 Dec;25(12):1193–1199. First published September 26, 2019.
PMID: 31556690
DOI: 10.1089/acm.2019.0222
Source: https://www.liebertpub.com/doi/pdf/10.1089/acm.2019.0222
Text type: factual outcome extraction

The randomized crossover analyzed 42 complete participants. They used bedside cotton balls on a jojoba base with either 100% grocery La Tourangelle walnut oil or an equal-part lavandin, bergamot and ylang-ylang blend. Each condition lasted five nights, separated by one week. The walnut and blend global Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index means were 8.0 and 4.9 (reported p = .0001). Table 2 ranks sleeping difficulty from the need to use the bathroom as 2.42 (SD 1.011) for walnut and 2.18 (SD 1.126) for the blend (Wilcoxon p = .046). Bad dreams were 0.42 (SD .917) and 0.25 (SD .534), an inconclusive comparison. Pain interrupting sleep was 0.62 (SD 1.114) and 0.61 (SD 1.039), also inconclusive. The study did not measure urinary urgency, dream enactment or oil ingestion.
