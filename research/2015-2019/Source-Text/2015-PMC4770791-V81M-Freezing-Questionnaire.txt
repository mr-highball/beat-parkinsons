Title: The V81M variant of tyrosine hydroxylase is associated with more severe freezing of gait in Parkinson's disease
PMID: 26732803
PMCID: PMC4770791
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2015.12.015
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4770791/
Publication: 2015-12-23 electronic publication, February 2016 issue
Coverage: Original article body and Figure 1 results.

The study measured genetic variant status and gait freezing rather than testing a food. Among participants already experiencing freezing, 13 V81M homozygotes had higher questionnaire scores than 67 participants with one or two wild-type alleles after accounting for disease duration (reported p = .05). The expanded analysis including nonfreezers was inconclusive (p = .11, data not shown). Figure 1 describes group means and SEM. The evidence concerns a questionnaire score and genotype. The body contains no fresh lemon balm exposure or episode-frequency response.
