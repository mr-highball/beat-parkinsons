Original study scope
The authors tested isolated cinnamaldehyde in MPTP mice and a cell model. In the animal comparison Figures 2 and 3 show neuronal staining and autophagy-related outcomes. Clinical rigidity and dyskinesia appear as general Parkinson or treatment context. Neither outcome was measured separately, and the study did not assign culinary cinnamon.

Continuing evidence gaps
Separate the cinnamon constituent, animal and cellular endpoints from culinary preparation and human symptom scores.
