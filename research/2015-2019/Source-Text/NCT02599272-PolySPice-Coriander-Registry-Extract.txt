2015 — PolySPice registry and coriander seed in curry
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02599272
Coverage: Factual extract of official registration fields.

First posted November 6, 2015. Randomized crossover of curry meals with different amounts of a spice blend. The narrative mentions 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure. Its structured outcomes omit that measure. The published Methods identify the coriander as dried seed powder.
