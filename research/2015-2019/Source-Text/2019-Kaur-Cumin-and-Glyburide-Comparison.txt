Cumin extract and glyburide in diabetic rats
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31348760/

July 26, 2019. Kaur and colleagues evaluated Cuminum cyminum seed ethanolic extract, glyburide and a combination in streptozotocin-diabetic rats. The combination group used 600 mg/kg extract and 2.5 mg/kg glyburide, while the reference glyburide group used 5 mg/kg. The authors report different glyburide pharmacokinetics between these groups and reductions in glucose measures. The unequal glyburide doses complicate the exposure comparison. No levodopa, dietary ground cumin or Parkinson OFF duration was studied.
