Title: N,N,N-trimethyl chitosan modified flaxseed oil based mucoadhesive neuronanoemulsions for direct nose to brain drug delivery
PMID: 30201564
DOI: 10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2018.09.032
Publication date: 2018-09-08
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30201564/
Text coverage: Author abstract and accepted-manuscript scope.

Pardeshi and Belgamwar investigated a flaxseed-oil-based carrier delivering ropinirole to mice through the nose. The study measured the drug in plasma and brain and described greater brain exposure with the modified carrier. The formulation uses processing and coingredients beyond the named cooking oil. The accessible study reports drug distribution. It calls for separate future behavioral and toxicity studies. It gives no oral flaxseed-oil contrast or Parkinson rest-tremor result. Behavioral results from other records returned by the manuscript search are separate studies.
