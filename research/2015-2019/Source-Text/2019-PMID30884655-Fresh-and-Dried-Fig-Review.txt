Arvaniti and colleagues reviewed composition, processing, antioxidant assays and health studies for fresh and dried fig. Cultivar, growing conditions and processing differ across reports. The deposited references include dried-fruit chemistry, simulated digestion and animal models alongside related foods and analytical methods. The review is useful for locating original studies. Its indexed abstract gives no distinct human Parkinson estimate or hallucination outcome.
