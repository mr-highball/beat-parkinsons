Title: Quantitative Evaluation of Chinese Herb Medicine in the Treatment of Sialorrhea and Frequent Nighttime Urination in Patients with Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5412142/
PMID: 28484503
PMCID: PMC5412142
DOI: 10.1155/2017/3045260
First published: 2017-04-18
Coverage: Distilled original methods and results or protocol fields, with article identity checked against PubMed.

The original randomized comparison allocated 96 people with Parkinson disease to Lian-Se formula or a five-component comparison granule. Methods list Chinese date in the comparison granule, citing an earlier Parkinson herbal study with the same placebo terminology. The original does not resolve a species or establish Phoenix dactylifera. Its outcomes were saliva, frequency of nighttime urination and autonomic measures. The eligible population excluded people with pain symptoms, so the report contains no clinical pain comparison for the listed ingredient.
