Title: Physiological Effect of Ataulfo Mango (Manguifera Indica) Beverage in Exercise
Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03182543
Native API receipt: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03182543
Identifier: NCT03182543, UACJ-ICB-2017-01
Date: 2017-06-09 first posted; actual completion 2017-04-30
Coverage: factual extract of the registry arms, dates and endpoints

The completed three-condition randomized crossover enrolled 19 male university students. The registered arms gave 200 g Ataulfo pulp in water, 160 g pulp plus 40 g peel in water, or water only. The participants began cycle exercise testing two hours later. Registered measurements concerned plasma antioxidants and oxidation, blood lactate and postprandial glucose. A fatigue scale or time-to-exhaustion outcome is absent from the specified outcomes. The official record has no posted results and the references included in it are background publications. The form of untreated pulp beyond its appearance in the drink is unspecified.
