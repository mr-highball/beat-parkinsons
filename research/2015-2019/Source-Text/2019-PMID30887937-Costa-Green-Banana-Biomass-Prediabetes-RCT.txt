Title: Beneficial effects of green banana biomass consumption in patients with pre-diabetes and type 2 diabetes: a randomised controlled trial.
PMID: 30887937
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114519000576
First published: 2019
Authors: Costa ES, França CN, Fonseca FAH, Kato JT, Bianco HT, Freitas TT
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30887937/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

24-week parallel RCT in n=113 middle-aged adults with prediabetes or T2DM randomized to nutritional support + 40 g/d green banana biomass (~4.5 g RS, n=62) vs diet alone (n=51). Within the green banana biomass arm, diastolic BP (p=0.010), HbA1c (p=0.0001), fasting glucose (p=0.021), and BMI decreased. Measures cardiometabolic endpoints, not orthostatic lightheadedness in PD.
