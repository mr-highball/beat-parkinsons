In the presentation on future marine and vegetable sources of fatty acids, Sandberg identifies sunflower oil and corn oil as sources of linoleic acid. The laboratory comparison discussed in that same passage was EPA plus DHA in a high-fat diet versus a corn-oil high-fat diet. The proceedings supply a source-of-nutrient mention for sunflower oil. They do not show a sunflower treatment or measurement in this member. The fish fatty acid, linseed, canola and corn contexts cannot supply a separate sunflower effect.
