The randomized, double-blind crossover enrolled 16 people with essential voice tremor. The Methods identify soybean oil as the matching oral softgel comparator. The soybean oil refining grade is unspecified. There was no alternate oil-free or no-treatment arm. A Parkinson diagnosis was excluded.

The active-minus-comparator result favored octanoic acid on the amplitude and frequency of acoustic modulation. The soybean comparator's own mean acoustic values also fell, by 0.48 and 0.22 percentage points. Those changes lack a separate comparison for exposure attribution. Blinded perceptual comparisons did not establish an overall difference. These measurements describe voice tremor after a short study period. The results do not establish oil-induced worsening or a limb rest tremor effect. The article and posted NCT01864525 results are one crossover family.
