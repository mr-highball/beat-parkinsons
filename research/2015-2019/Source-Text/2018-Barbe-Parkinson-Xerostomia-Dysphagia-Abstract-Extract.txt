Title: Dry mouth and subjective dysphagia in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30028552/
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Barbe and colleagues, first published in 2018, Oral Diseases 25(1):282–289. Participants with Parkinson disease reported dry mouth, swallowing difficulty and drooling on visual analog scales and in daily diaries. The original abstract reports an association of swallowing complaints with dry mouth and drooling. This is a concurrent symptom association and does not measure how oil changes swallowing. A 2020 independent crossover compares rinses in medication-related dry mouth.
