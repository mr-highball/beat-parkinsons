Original source scope
The 15-person healthy adult crossover compared a branded isolated theacrine capsule with placebo. Fatigue was reported to fall by 6.7% with theacrine and rise by 5.8% with placebo (p<.05). The report's Results subheading says single-blind while the design diagram and figure captions say double-blind. The journal reports nine participants in the separate seven-day open-label component, allocated across two exposure levels. The early abstract and later patents report six participants for the lower exposure. The reporting overlap cannot be added as replication. The crossover fatigue estimate identifies the same family as P49 and two patent reports. It provides a constituent contrast in a healthy acute setting for claim 0948, whose exposure is brewed green tea and whose outcome is Parkinson fatigue severity.
