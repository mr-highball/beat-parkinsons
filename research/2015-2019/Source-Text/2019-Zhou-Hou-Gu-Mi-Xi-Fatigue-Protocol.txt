Linked gastrectomy protocol gives a different formula
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6558912/
Coverage: official protocol or original paper factual summary.

Published June 10, 2019. The journal protocol explicitly identifies NCT03025152. Table 1 lists ten herbs including Platycodon and no perilla. It gives 30 g/day of diet formula, including 10.1 g of herbal materials. The placebo differs slightly in taste and smell. The protocol contains planned fatigue items without results, and does not resolve the administered formula or establish a separate perilla effect.
