The BNA proceedings contain the full P-W082 poster abstract and a separate pattern-glare hallucination study. P-W082 classified 24 Parkinson participants with hallucinations and 26 without, plus 13 controls, and compared attention-task performance and computational drift rates. The later paper analyzed different denominators. The poster reports group status and perceptual processing without a dried fig exposure or a visual episode-rate comparison.

Proceedings member P-W082 compares a computational component of an attention task in groups defined by hallucination status. It reports 24 patients with hallucinations, 26 without and 13 controls. The related journal article (PMID 29560902) analyzed 20, 25 and 12, respectively. The journal paper identifies the same 24 and 26 initial enrollment counts and reports exclusion of four and one due to missed task responses. Shared investigators and the same attention task and modeling approach strongly support reuse. The control crosswalk remains open. P-M019 is a separate 343-person pattern-glare and anomalous-perception experiment, with no Parkinson diagnosis or food comparison.
