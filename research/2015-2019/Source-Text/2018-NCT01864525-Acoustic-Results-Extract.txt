2018 | NCT01864525 | Official posted crossover results

Source: ClinicalTrials.gov API. First results posted August 20, 2018.

The baseline and follow-up entries give percent modulation by condition. Placebo means are 12.91 to 12.43 for acoustic amplitude and 5.57 to 5.35 for frequency. Octanoic acid means are 13.49 to 9.35 for amplitude and 5.19 to 3.98 for frequency. Each condition has 16 participants under a crossover design. Adjusted period contrasts are p=.0499 and p=.0450 respectively. The official record identifies placebo as an inactive capsule, with no contents listed. The original paper duplicates the values and identifies its softgel placebo as soybean oil. A mean vocal loudness or Parkinson hypophonia measure is absent from the posted results.
