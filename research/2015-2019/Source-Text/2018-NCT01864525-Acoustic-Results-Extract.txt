Acoustic measures are percentages and lower means represent less acoustic modulation. Under placebo, amplitude went from 12.91 to 12.43 and frequency from 5.57 to 5.35. Under octanoic acid, amplitude went from 13.49 to 9.35 and frequency from 5.19 to 3.98. The baseline-adjusted models gave p=.0499 for amplitude and p=.0450 for frequency across conditions. Both conditions included the same 16 crossover participants.

Blinded perceptual scores favor octanoic acid for the sustained vowel (1.53 versus 1.25 on a 0–3 scale for better voice ratings) and placebo for sentences (1.63 versus 1.19). The posted auditory comparison has p=.7172. The inactive material is identified as soybean oil in the journal publication. The design lacks an oil-free control. The trial addresses acoustic changes in essential voice tremor. Separate Parkinson hypophonia, limb rest tremor and refining grade were outside the measured comparison.
