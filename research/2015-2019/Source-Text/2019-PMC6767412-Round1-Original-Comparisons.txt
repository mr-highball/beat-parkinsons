The original mouse study induces anosmia and compares multi-odor training, steroid treatment and control conditions. The trained group found buried food more quickly at the third test. Cinnamon was one of four odors rather than a consumed treatment. The experiment evaluates olfactory recovery and molecular markers; it supplies no food effect on mood, fatigue or pain.
