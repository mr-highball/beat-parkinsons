Title: True ileal digestibility of legumes determined by dual-isotope tracer method in Indian adults.
PMID: 31374575
PMCID: PMC6766447
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/nqz159
First published: 2019
Authors: Kashyap S, Varkey A, Shivakumar N, Devi S, Reddy B H R, Thomas T et al.
Journal: Am J Clin Nutr
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6766447/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Dual stable-isotope human amino acid digestibility study (Kashyap et al., 2019, Am J Clin Nutr, PMC6766447) measuring true ileal indispensable amino acid (IAA) digestibility of cooked whole chickpeas (Cicer arietinum) and yellow peas in healthy adults. Mean true ileal IAA digestibility of dehulled/cooked chickpea was ~74.6% (notably lower than animal proteins such as egg/meat ~90-95%), providing mechanistic nutritional context for lower postprandial large neutral amino acid flux than animal protein, without testing Parkinson levodopa pharmacokinetics or OFF time.
