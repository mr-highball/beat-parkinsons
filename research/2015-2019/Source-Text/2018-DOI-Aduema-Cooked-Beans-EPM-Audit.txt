Title: Using the Elevated plus Maze Task in Assessing Anxiety and Fear in Swiss White Mice
DOI: 10.19080/JCMAH.2018.06.555678
First published: 2018
Authors: Aduema W, Osim EE, Nwankwo AA
Journal: Journal of Complementary Medicine & Alternative Healthcare (Juniper Publishers)
Source: https://juniperpublishers.com/jcmah/pdf/JCMAH.MS.ID.555678.pdf
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Non-PubMed Juniper Publishers rodent study (30 adult Swiss white mice, n=10/group assigned to normal chow, 50% w/w cooked beans, or 0.2 mg/50g 5-HTP diet for 4 weeks) assessed in a 5-minute elevated plus maze (EPM), cited in PMC11422034 as anxiolytic evidence for Vigna unguiculata. Direct full-text PDF audit revealed severe, disqualifying methodological and reporting flaws: (1) taxonomic conflation defining 'Common bean (Vigna unguiculata)' via Phaseolus vulgaris varieties ('navy, black, kidney and pinto beans') with zero botanical authentication or cooking method; (2) glaring numerical/statistical contradictions—cooked-bean centre square duration (25.35 +/- 4.61 s) was LOWER than control (28.71 +/- 5.76 s) yet claimed 'significantly higher (P<0.001)', grooming duration (13.76 +/- 2.34 vs 19.44 +/- 3.25 s) claimed p<0.001 in text vs p<0.05 in abstract despite non-significant t-ratio, open-arm entries were numerically lower (3.75 +/- 0.63 vs 4.20 +/- 0.63), and reported means imply n=7-8 rather than n=10; (3) scrambled figure captions (Figs 3-6 mislabeled) and text admitting a 4th experimental group was omitted; (4) manual unblinded scoring and predatory venue. Rated reliability_fit=unusable; companion motor paper (DOI:10.19080/PBSIJ.2018.08.555729) similarly misinterpreted higher foot-slip frequency as improved coordination and contained garbled citations.
