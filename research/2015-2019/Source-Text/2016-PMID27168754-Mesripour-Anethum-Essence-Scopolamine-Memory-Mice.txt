Title: The effects of Anethum graveolens essence on scopolamine-induced memory impairment in mice.
PMID: 27168754
PMCID: PMC4852659
First published: 2016
Authors: Mesripour A, Rafieian-Kopaei M, Bahrami B
Journal: Research in pharmaceutical sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27168754/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse study (Mesripour et al., 2016, Res Pharm Sci, PMC4852659) administering intraperitoneal Anethum graveolens essence (100 mg/kg i.p.) in scopolamine-impaired male Balb/c mice on novel object recognition. Dill essence prevented scopolamine recognition deficit (RI 40+/-5.5% vs -15.5+/-3.0% scopolamine, 50+/-5.8% control) in a tamoxifen-sensitive manner. Limits: non-oral intraperitoneal route, plant part unspecified, acute cholinergic recognition memory proxy rather than PD attention or executive function.
