The cannabinoid review compares different receptor-active drugs, cannabis preparations and trials across Parkinson motor and nonmotor outcomes. Adverse-event reports include visual hallucinations, while the underlying trials concern chemically distinct exposures. The review supplies no dried fig or visual episode-frequency result.
