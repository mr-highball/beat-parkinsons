The authors describe a short FFQ covering 17 food types, an extended version covering 84 food items across 16 categories, and a 149-food reliability and validity version. The accessible abstracts do not enumerate the items or say which version a later cardiac rehabilitation study applied. The cardiac rehabilitation original cites this methodological work in section 2.11 and gives an aggregate diet score. The study's questionnaire or item-level coding is still required to determine whether date fruit was explicitly captured.
