The MR colonography phantom experiment places excised pig colon
segments in containers with soybean oil for imaging. The methods
use air, water or an imaging agent within the segment. The
experiment has no oral oil administration, oil grade comparison
or patient symptom effect.

The maxillofacial ultrasound collection includes a patient with a
shrimp anatomical fragment lodged in the floor of the mouth. The
case reports foreign-body detection without identifying the cooking
method or a bowel effect. A separate gallbladder imaging study uses
strawberry dot sign as a name for radiologic morphology.
