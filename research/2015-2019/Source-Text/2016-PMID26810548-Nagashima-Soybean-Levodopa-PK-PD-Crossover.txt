Title: Effects of soybean ingestion on pharmacokinetics of levodopa and motor symptoms of Parkinson's disease--In relation to the effects of Mucuna pruriens.
PMID: 26810548
DOI: 10.1016/j.jns.2016.01.005
First published: 2016
Authors: Nagashima Y, Kondo T, Sakata M, Koh J, Ito H
Journal: Journal of the neurological sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26810548/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Crossover clinical study in 7 Parkinson's disease (PD) patients with wearing-off and levodopa-induced dyskinesia (mean age 67.1 +/- 7.9 y, disease duration 10.7 +/- 3.5 y, H&Y 3.0 +/- 0.6, mean daily levodopa 378.6 mg/d) and 5 healthy volunteers comparing single-dose levodopa/carbidopa (100/10 mg, LD/CD) alone vs LD/CD + 11 g ground mature soybeans (LD/CD/soy) over 3 hours with a 1-week washout. Independently appraised as Adjusted Low Risk of Bias in Boelens Keun et al. 2021 (PMC8634393, Tables 2 & 5) and graded C in Sukmueng et al. 2026 (PMID:42489905). Results: LD/CD/soy significantly prolonged self-reported ON-period by 80% (270 vs 150 min, p = 0.028) and significantly lowered modified Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale (mAIMS) dyskinesia severity (estimated marginal mean [EMM] 1.9 vs 3.9, p < 0.001). Unadjusted plasma levodopa and 3-O-methyldopa (3-OMD) AUCs did not differ significantly, whereas covariate-adjusted EMM of plasma 3-OMD significantly decreased in both PD patients (317.0 vs 374.6 ng/mL, p = 0.03) and healthy controls, and EMM of homovanillic acid (HVA) increased in PD, consistent with partial peripheral COMT inhibition. Limitations: small non-randomized single-dose crossover (n=7 PD), unblinded clinical ratings, acute 3-hour ON window rather than 24-hour daily off time, and 11 g ground mature soybean (~4 g protein) differs in maturity, matrix, and protein load from culinary steamed/cooked immature green edamame (~18.5 g protein per cup shelled).
