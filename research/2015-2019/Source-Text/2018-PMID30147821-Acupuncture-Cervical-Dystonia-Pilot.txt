Original study scope
The five-person open-label pilot added acupuncture between stable botulinum toxin sessions for idiopathic cervical dystonia. The authors report subjective improvement and describe feasibility, without a matched sage dietary group. Participants and treatment differ from the frozen Parkinson questions.

Continuing evidence gaps
Any frozen ingredient effect requires a separately assigned or traceable culinary exposure and a matched symptom estimate.
