Title: Mastication of Nuts under Realistic Eating Conditions: Implications for Energy Balance.
PMID: 29865169
PMCID: PMC6024662
DOI: 10.3390/nu10060710
First published: 2018
Authors: McArthur, Mattes, Considine
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29865169/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized crossover mastication study in 50 healthy adults (ages 18-52 y) measuring chewing forces and expectorated bolus particle size distribution for almonds, walnuts, and pistachios under varying eating conditions. Chewing forces did not differ across nuts; bolus particle size differed by nut type. Companion Peyron 2004 (PMID 15218050, n=10) similarly measured expectorated nut bolus particles. Limits: healthy normal-dentition adults, no dysphagia or PD swallowing assessment.
