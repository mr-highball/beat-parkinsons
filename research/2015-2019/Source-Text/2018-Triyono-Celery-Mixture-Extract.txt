Triyono et al., 2018, DOI 10.35814/jifi.v16i1.425. Factual extract from https://journalpharm.univpancasila.ac.id/index.php/jifi/en/article/download/425/347/959
Open randomized comparison in 60 adults with stage I hypertension. Both arms used the same six herbs, including whole celery herb; the raw materials were oven-dried at 50 °C. Assigned preparation was boiling or steeping. Figure 4 gives 27 versus 21 days for disappearance of the combined dizziness/headache symptom. Neither ingredient attribution nor a standing-related dizziness result can be read from the comparison.
