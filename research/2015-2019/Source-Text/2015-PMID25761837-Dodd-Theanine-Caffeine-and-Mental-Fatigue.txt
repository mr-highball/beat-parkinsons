Original source scope and evidence boundary
Twelve habitual and 12 nonhabitual caffeine consumers completed a four-condition crossover with placebo, caffeine, L-theanine and the combination. Table 2 reports no statistically clear mental-fatigue treatment effect (F=2.61, p=.08) or treatment-by-consumer interaction (F<1). Unadjusted mean fatigue rose from 35.83 to 52.75 mm on placebo and 30.58 to 45.17 on the blend in consumers. Nonconsumer values were 30.50 to 48.25 on placebo and 31.67 to 37.33 on the blend. These descriptive condition means do not supply an adjusted blend contrast. Overall mood, attention and neurovascular findings are separate. The trial used isolated constituents and healthy volunteers, leaving the exact Parkinson beverage comparisons open.
