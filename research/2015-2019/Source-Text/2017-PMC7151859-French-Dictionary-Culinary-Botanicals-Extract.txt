Coverage: Complete 105-page original dictionary section, including separate food and plant entries.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Entries identify coffee, cinnamon, clove and Crocus sativus. The citronnelle entry uses Melissa officinalis as a vernacular referent while also naming Cymbopogon citratus. A common name alone cannot identify the botanical material or transfer findings between these plants.

The dictionary provides definitions and citation leads, not a newly conducted intervention or a preparation-specific Parkinson symptom comparison. French word fragments resembling sage or orange do not automatically refer to those foods. Genuine botanical entries remain related context; lexical collisions remain separate source-eligibility decisions. No clinical effect estimate follows from a definition.
