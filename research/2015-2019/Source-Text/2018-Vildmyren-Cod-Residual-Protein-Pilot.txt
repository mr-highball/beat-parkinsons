Title: Daily Intake of Protein from Cod Residual Material Lowers Serum Concentrations of Nonesterified Fatty Acids in Overweight Healthy Adults: A Randomized Double-Blind Pilot Study
Publication date: 2018-06 (journal publication)
DOI: 10.3390/md16060197
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/1660-3397/16/6/197
Text coverage: Primary journal results and methods.

Cod presscake and presscake combined with soluble stickwater protein were compared with placebo over eight weeks. The presscake-only finding concerns nonesterified fatty acids and glucose/insulin measures, while the mixed protein arm showed no corresponding pattern. Participants were otherwise healthy adults with overweight or obesity. Nordic poster P482 reports the related early analysis. The trial compares processed cod-protein preparations and does not measure Parkinson fatigue or grilled fillets.
