Measured outcomes after a frozen red raspberry breakfast
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/oby.22406

First published February 14, 2019. In a randomized single-blind crossover, 21 adults with prediabetes and insulin resistance plus 11 metabolically healthy adults consumed meals with 0, 125 or 250 g whole frozen red raspberry. Half the fruit was served in a beverage and half with cereal. The study reported lower two-hour insulin area under the curve at both doses in the prediabetes group. The full methods and results address metabolic, inflammatory and oxidative outcomes. They supply no mood or anxiety measure, and the cohort did not target Parkinson disease.
