Title: Horseradish extract promotes urinary bladder carcinogenesis when administered to F344 rats in drinking water
PMID: 28165151
DOI: 10.1002/jat.3434
First published: 2017
Authors: Cho YM, Hasumura M, Imai T, Takami S, Nishikawa A, Ogawa K
Journal: Journal of Applied Toxicology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28165151/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Toxicology bioassays (2-wk, 13-wk promotion, 104-wk chronic, 32 male F344 rats/group) administering horseradish extract (AITC-rich food additive, 0.005%-0.04% in drinking water). Chronic intake >0.01% (5 mg/kg/d) induced simple urinary bladder urothelial hyperplasia (without primary neoplasia), and post-initiation intake promoted bladder papilloma/carcinoma. High-dose rodent urothelial irritation/safety caveat; no human urinary urgency outcomes.
