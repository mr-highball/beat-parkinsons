Cranberry juice and cell-free MAO-A inhibition
First published: September 20, 2017
Journal: Food & Function 8(11), 4187–4193
Authors: Guillermo Cásedas, Francisco Les, María Pilar Gómez-Serranillos, Carine Smith and Víctor López
DOI: 10.1039/C7FO01205E
PMID: 29038797
Source: https://aplicaciones.ciencia.gob.es/teseo-rest/api/documento/download/public/46646
Coverage: Original journal article reproduced in the author's deposited thesis, PDF pages 95–101. Publisher metadata dates the journal article. The thesis defense date is a separate event.

Commercial cranberry and bilberry juices were freeze-dried for laboratory testing. The authors describe inhibition of MAO-A activity in a microplate assay and give no species or tissue source for the enzyme in Methods 2.6.2. Results 3.4 reports a cranberry IC50 of 223.96 µg/mL, while Table 2 prints 0.1850 ± 0.04471 and inconsistent units between heading and row. The source does not reconcile these values. No participant consumed cranberry in this assay and no depression symptoms were measured. A separate paper by overlapping authors applied pomegranate juice to human adipose-tissue preparations. The original 2019 chapter bibliography remains unavailable.
