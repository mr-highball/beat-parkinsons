Measured glucose and appetite after kiwi and cereal
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC5048185/fullTextXML

First published September 14, 2016. Twenty adults took part in the randomized within-person meal comparison. Two 40 g available-carbohydrate meal conditions contained 200 g green Hayward or gold SunGold kiwi with wheaten cereal. The full Methods and Results describe capillary glucose and self-rated appetite and satiety. These reported outcomes leave cognitive attention unmeasured.
