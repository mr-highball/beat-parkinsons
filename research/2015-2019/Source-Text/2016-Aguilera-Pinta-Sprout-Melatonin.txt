Pinta bean sprouts and plasma melatonin in rats
https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2016/fo/c5fo01538c

January 20, 2016. Aguilera and colleagues identify the seed as Phaseolus vulgaris var. Pinta. The methods describe ethanolic extraction of six-day sprouts, solvent evaporation and reconstitution before oral gavage. In the first experiment, plasma melatonin after 90 minutes was about 29 pg/mL in fasted rats receiving extract, 16% higher than the freely fed chow control and 2.9 times the fasting comparator. This is a comparison against separate groups, without a sleep or insomnia measurement. The measured exposure was a sprout extract and its identity as the same culinary bean in the frozen claim is unresolved.
