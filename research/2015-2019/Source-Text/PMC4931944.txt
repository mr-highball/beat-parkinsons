The 167-person dementia with Lewy bodies cohort relates MRI hippocampal volume to survival. The authors report frequency of hallucinations as a sensitivity covariate, with no dedicated episode-rate estimate. The paper does not evaluate dried fig intake.

The clinical characterization and regression models are separate. Recurrent formed visual hallucinations are one diagnostic feature, and the authors include hallucination frequency as a covariate in a survival sensitivity analysis. The reported adjusted probability value concerns the hippocampal-volume survival relationship, not a change in hallucination frequency.
