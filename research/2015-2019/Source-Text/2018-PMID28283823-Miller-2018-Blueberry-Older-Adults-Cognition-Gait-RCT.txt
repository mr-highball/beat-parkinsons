Title: Dietary blueberry improves cognition among older adults in a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 28283823
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-017-1400-8
First published: 2018
Authors: Miller MG, Hamilton DA, Joseph JA, Shukitt-Hale B
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28283823/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Ninety-day double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in n=37 older adults (aged 60-75 y; 13 men, 24 women) consuming 24 g/d freeze-dried whole blueberry powder (~1 cup fresh equivalent) vs placebo. Blueberry significantly improved executive function via reduced task-switching switch cost (p=0.033, partial eta2=0.09) and fewer CVLT repetition errors (p=0.031, partial eta2=0.126), whereas NO improvement in gait or balance was observed. Limits: healthy non-PD older adults; freeze-dried powder.
