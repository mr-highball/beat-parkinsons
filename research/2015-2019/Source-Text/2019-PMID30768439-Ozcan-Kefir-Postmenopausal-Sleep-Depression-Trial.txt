Title: Effects of Kefir on Quality of Life and Sleep Disturbances in Postmenopausal Women.
PMID: 30768439
DOI: 10.1097/HNP.0000000000000310
First published: 2019
Authors: Özcan H, Oskay Ü, Bodur AF
Journal: Holistic nursing practice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30768439/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Clinical intervention study in postmenopausal women evaluating daily kefir consumption on sleep disturbances, depression, and quality of life. Reported favorable improvements in sleep disturbances, depression, and QOL, though limited by open non-PD postmenopausal sample and balanced by null/conflicting subsequent RCTs (Mohsenpour 2023, Noori 2025, Cannavale 2023, Bakirhan 2026, Lawrence 2025).
