Title: A Randomized Placebo-Controlled Trial of Omega-3 and Sertraline in Depressed Patients With or at Risk for Coronary Heart Disease
Publication date: 2019 (first electronic publication)
PMID: 31163106
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31163106/
Text coverage: Original indexed abstract and clinical outcomes.

This later Carney trial compares another EPA regimen with corn oil while both groups receive sertraline. Its population includes people with coronary disease or elevated coronary risk. The ten-week mean baseline-adjusted BDI-II scores were 10.3 on the corn-oil comparator and 12.1 on EPA (p=.22). Hamilton values were 7.2 and 8.0 (p=.40). Remission comparisons were imprecise. Those relative groups and the concurrent antidepressant do not isolate a corn-oil effect in Parkinson disease.
