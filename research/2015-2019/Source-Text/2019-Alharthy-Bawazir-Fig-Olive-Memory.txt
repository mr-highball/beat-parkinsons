Alharthy and Bawazir, Pharmacophore 2019;10(4):62–71.
Source: https://doi.org/10.51847/9wdz9jy
Coverage: Paraphrased primary exposure, design and endpoints.

Fifty male rats were assigned to five groups, ten per group. An oral dried fig and olive oil mixture was used in two arms over four weeks. The study compared the mixture in a scopolamine-induced amnesia model with control and a rivastigmine comparison. Maze outcomes and hippocampal acetylcholinesterase were reported. No fig-only food arm, clinical Parkinson cohort, or visual hallucination frequency estimate was reported.
