Title: Proton-independent activation of acid-sensing ion channel 3 by an alkaloid, lindoldhamine, from Laurus nobilis.
PMID: 29277899
PMCID: PMC5825300
DOI: 10.1111/bph.14134
First published: 2018
Authors: Osmakov DI, Koshelev SG, Andreev YA, Dubinnyi MA, Kublitski VS, Efremov RG, et al.
Journal: British journal of pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29277899/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Electrophysiological laboratory study (Osmakov et al., 2018, Br J Pharmacol, PMC5825300) characterizing lindoldhamine isolated from Laurus nobilis leaves in Xenopus laevis oocytes. At physiological pH >=7.4, lindoldhamine caused sustained proton-independent activation of rat and human ASIC3 (an acid-sensing ion channel linked to peripheral nociception) and potentiated human ASIC3 transient currents. Provides opposing mechanistic nuance alongside Osmakov 2019 (PMID:31540492) ASIC1a inhibition; no whole-leaf or human pain data.
