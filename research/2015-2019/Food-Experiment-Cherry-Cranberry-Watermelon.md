# Cherry, cranberry and watermelon trial records, 2015–2019

## Raw watermelon and resting blood pressure

NCT03380221 was first posted December 21, 2017 and still listed recruiting when checked in November 2026. It estimates 40 participants. The March 2019 article explicitly names the registration and reports 33 completers, all of whom received the fresh diced watermelon condition first and the cookie condition second. Systolic pressure changed from 127 to 125 mmHg in the watermelon phase and from 124 to 129 mmHg in the cookie phase. The article’s Results and Table 1 show no clear within-watermelon change, although its abstract calls the reduction significant. Neither standing pressure nor lightheadedness was measured. The controlled comparison involved adults with overweight or obesity without a Parkinson cohort. [Trial registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT03380221.txt) · [Primary article, Results and Table 1](Source-Text/PMC6470521.txt#L138)

## Registered cranberry powder cognitive feasibility study

NCT03679533 was first posted September 20, 2018 and lists 60 adults. The registered study, COMBAT, examines freeze-dried cranberry powder, cognition and brain perfusion. The authors also identify ISRCTN76069316 in their report. These records and the 2022 results describe one cohort. [ClinicalTrials.gov snapshot](Source-Text/Trials/NCT03679533.txt) · [ISRCTN](https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN76069316) · [2022 results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cherry-Cranberry-Watermelon.md#cranberry-powder-and-measured-attention)

## Registered tart cherry concentrate cognition study

NCT04021342 was first posted July 16, 2019. Its official record lists actual enrollment of 56, with no results posted. The 2022 publication reporting fatigue and cognition is a companion analysis of the same study, whose primary outcome was systolic blood pressure. [Local registry snapshot](Source-Text/Trials/NCT04021342.txt) · [2022 companion results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cherry-Cranberry-Watermelon.md#tart-cherry-concentrate-and-mental-fatigue)

## Registered watermelon juice and vascular outcomes in older women

NCT03626168 was first posted August 10, 2018. It describes an oral watermelon juice comparison in postmenopausal women with actual enrollment of 21. The public results are for serum lycopene, vascular endothelial function and arterial stiffness. The later articles report vascular and cognitive measurements in 16 women and share this registration. [Local official record fields](Source-Text/Trials/NCT03626168.txt) · [Official posted results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03626168?tab=results) · [2021 original reports](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cherry-Cranberry-Watermelon.md#watermelon-juice-cognition-and-vascular-outcomes-in-postmenopausal-women)
