# Chicken and Parkinson outcomes

## 2017 Chicken frequency and composite symptom score

Mischley et al., 2017, PMID 29081890, PMCID PMC5610862, DOI 10.1155/2017/6405278, Table 2, cross-sectionally modeled self-reported symptom severity against food-frequency categories. For chicken (4-ounce serving), the age-, sex-, years-since-diagnosis- and income-adjusted coefficient per one-category increase was +13.4 (SE 9.8; 95% CI -5.8 to 32.5; p=0.171) on total PRO-PD. The result was inconclusive; it is not a measured longitudinal slope, isolated cooking method, or facial-expression-specific estimate. The authors call this progression in the article, but the design is cross-sectional.
