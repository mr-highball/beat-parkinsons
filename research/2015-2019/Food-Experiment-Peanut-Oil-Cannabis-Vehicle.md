# Peanut oil in a medicinal cannabis vehicle

## Refined peanut oil in a pharmacy cannabis preparation

A Transvaal pharmacy patient leaflet, authorised December 11, 2017, describes sublingual THC- and CBD-containing medicinal oils and identifies refined arachis (peanut) oil as the vehicle. In its discussion of possible cannabis-related psychiatric effects, it includes hallucinations. This is product information, not a report that a particular patient experienced hallucinations; the leaflet does not attribute any effect to peanut oil or identify a cold-pressed oil. It does not supply a Parkinson-specific hallucination frequency.
