# Paprika preparations and knee pain, 2015–2019

## Paprika oleoresin knee pain trial

The 2017 report by Umigai and colleagues randomized 32 adults with mild knee pain to paprika oleoresin or placebo for 12 weeks. Its original English abstract reports within-group improvement on pain VAS and the total Japanese Knee Osteoarthritis Measure (JKOM), with no clear between-group difference. A general-activities subscale favored the extract. The numeric pain table and full body remain publisher-gated. This evaluates an oleoresin product in healthy volunteers with knee pain, without a culinary ground-paprika or Parkinson comparison. ## UMIN registration of paprika pigment and knee pain

UMIN000019995 lists registration on 30 November 2015 and disclosure on 30 November 2016. It specifies healthy adults aged 45–69 with knee pain, a target of 92, and a JKOM outcome at baseline, four and eight weeks. The listed paprika intervention has a daily extract amount of 40 mg, and the duration fields differ between groups. Completed status supplies no measured results. Differences in sample target, preparation amount and timepoints leave the identity link to the 2017 publication unresolved.
