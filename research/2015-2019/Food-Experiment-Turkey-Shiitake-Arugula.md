# Turkey, shiitake, and arugula evidence

## 2016 Rocket salad beverage and blood pressure in healthy adults

Jonvik and colleagues compared four nitrate sources in an acute crossover in 18 healthy adults. Systolic pressure after the rocket salad beverage fell from 122 to 116 mm Hg at 150 minutes (p = .007). It did not change after the sodium nitrate comparator at that time. The study used a beverage and assessed blood pressure and nitrate markers. It did not measure Parkinson's fatigue, standing lightheadedness, or falls. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27075914/) · [Official trial record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02271633)

## 2016 Fresh salad rocket aroma ratings

Bell and colleagues used a trained sensory panel and chemical analysis to compare seven Eruca sativa accessions. Aroma and flavor attributes varied by accession and were related to measured compounds. This tested perception of rocket during tasting. It gives no measure of a change in a participant's olfactory function after repeated or acute consumption. The publisher's electronic publication was in 2016, ahead of the 2017 issue. [Primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5066371/)

## 2017 Whole shiitake in two intestinal obstruction cases

The report describes two people without teeth who consumed whole shiitake without using dentures. Uncut mushroom pieces were found in small-bowel obstructions. The two patients needed surgery and recovered. This evidence concerns chewing and downstream bowel obstruction. The report does not establish a roasted preparation or a change in Parkinson's swallowing difficulty. It appeared online in 2017 and in a 2019 issue. [Primary full report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6688735/)

## 2018 Isolated erucin in a Parkinson mouse model

The study compared erucin and sulforaphane in a 6-OHDA cell model and in lesioned mice. After injected treatment, both compounds partially reduced apomorphine-induced rotations relative to lesioned vehicle controls (p < .05). The study also reported changes in tyrosine hydroxylase and oxidative-stress measures. The compound and induced rotation outcome are indirect context for arugula and do not establish raw leaf exposure, falls, facial movement, fatigue or olfaction. [Primary report and Figure 7](https://cris.unibo.it/bitstream/11585/622676/11/acs.jafc.7b04641.pdf)
