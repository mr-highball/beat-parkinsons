# Curcumin capsules and swallowing complaints in oral fibrosis

## Curcumin capsules and swallowing complaints in oral fibrosis

Ara and colleagues described a single-blind, randomized comparison in 100 adults with stage 2 oral submucous fibrosis. At six months, the curcumin capsule group reported swallowing difficulty in 0/50 compared with 4/50 on placebo. Both groups had 4/50 at baseline. Table 1 prints p<.001 for the within-group curcumin comparison, which is inconsistent with the small number of changes and should not be interpreted as a reliable significance estimate. Swallowing difficulty was recorded as present or absent. The intervention and disease differ from grated turmeric and Parkinson swallowing severity.
