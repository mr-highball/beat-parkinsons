# Caffeine, L-theanine and fatigue across comparisons

The 2005 and 2007 crossover studies in the prior publication periods include positive mental-fatigue results with isolated caffeine. The 2007 study used four matched drink conditions, and the ingredients were added to a base with the tea powder removed. Its outcomes measured short-term task fatigue and separate tiredness ratings. [Habitual-use comparison](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID15678363-Haskell-Caffeine-Fatigue-Habitual-Use.txt) · [Four-condition task trial](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2007-PMID18006208-Caffeine-Theanine-Four-Condition-Fatigue.txt)

The 2015 four-condition study reports a favorable descriptive pattern for the blend and an inconclusive treatment term for mental fatigue. Its broader mood and attention outcomes cannot supply a standalone fatigue effect. The 2014 instant coffee study measured postural stability following stroke. These reports clarify why coffee, isolated caffeine and extracted tea compounds should remain distinct in Parkinson symptom reviews.
