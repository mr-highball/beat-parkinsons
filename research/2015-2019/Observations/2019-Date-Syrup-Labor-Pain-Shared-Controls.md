# Date syrup, mixed food access and shared controls

The previous period's Gonabad studies included date fruit in an offered-food or carbohydrate-choice group. The food-access article gives later-stage labor-pain differences for the group. The companion setting supplies labor progress and analgesic use with different counts. Participant overlap and per-food effects remain open. [Earlier pain outcomes](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-ZJRMS93776-Food-Choices-and-Labor-Pain.txt) · [Earlier choice experiment](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMID23304677-Rahmani-Carbohydrate-Choices-and-Labor.txt)

This period's Khorramabad articles report two separately published comparisons of date syrup with water and a third report of honey syrup and water. They share one registration. The two English reports reproduce five control mean and SD pairs despite different active ingredients. The Persian report has a smaller stated denominator and diverging late values. Treat all three as a single registration family while retaining their individual observations and uncertainty about the exact participants.
