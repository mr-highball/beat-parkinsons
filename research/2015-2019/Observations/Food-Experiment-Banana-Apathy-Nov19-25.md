# Banana intake, sensory exposures and apathy

The later feeding crossover measures markers after actual oral banana and the rat study uses a shared chip as an assay target. Separate marmoset handling and feeding uses are procedural. The community conversation documents perceived nausea relief from alternative snacks and leaves apathy open.
