# Separate food exposures, endpoints, and control groups

A [2018 curcumin experiment](../Source-Text/2018-PMID29339457-Curcumin-Nociception-and-Rotarod-Scope.txt) records an inconclusive rotarod control in healthy mice after injection and reduced nociceptive behavior in separate assays. The earlier [2014 saffron constituent experiment](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID24401376-Crocin-and-Forced-Swim-Behavior.txt) measures immobility. A subsequent [2017 saffron constituent experiment](../Source-Text/2017-PMID29299203-Saffron-Crocin-and-Experimental-Memory.txt) measures platform learning. The time-related behavioral outcomes require their particular tasks and comparison conditions for interpretation.

The [2018 citrus peel review](../Source-Text/2018-PMID29552087-Citrus-Peel-in-Mixed-Herbal-Constipation-Trials.txt) identifies dried Chenpi within an eight-ingredient formula. Different species sources and regimen modifications matter for comparison with later orange-pomace research. The [2018 synucleinopathy review](../Source-Text/2018-PMID29508455-Synucleinopathy-Autonomic-Care-and-Olive-Oil-Advice.txt) lists extra-virgin olive oil as practice advice and cites a controlled study of another food.
