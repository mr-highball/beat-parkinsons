# Crocin motor tasks and saffron fall frequency

The 2018 mouse original's abstract separates the direction of two motor tasks. Isolated crocin shortened the reported pole time to fall and lengthened wire-hang time. Both are timed tasks. Full-text access is still needed to check the task definitions and outcome tables. [Crocin abstract assessment](../Source-Text/2019-PMID30159851-Haeri-Crocin-MPTP-Mice-Pole-Wire.txt)

A 2010 Parkinson study in the preceding period distinguishes recalled fall counts from fear of falling. Two frequent fallers reported little fear. Each source needs its actual event definition before transfer to a fall-frequency claim. [Parkinson mobility and falls context](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2010-PMID20157723-Fear-and-Fall-Frequency.txt)

The later saffron stigma-extract study measured time to a forced rotarod fall in animals. Its form and endpoint add context independent of isolated crocin. The [ground-saffron scorecard](../../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/scorecards.tsv) remains open for spontaneous fall frequency in people with Parkinson disease.
