# 2015 visual event definitions and botanical preparations

The 2015 papers provide four materially different ways to record visual symptoms. [Medication conversion](../Source-Text/PMC4927929.txt) records withdrawal attributed to a visual adverse event. [Diagnostic follow-up](../Source-Text/PMC4677697.txt) records whether people had a visual symptom at the mild cognitive impairment stage. [A cross-sectional comparison](../Source-Text/PMC4483492.txt) uses a preceding-month hallucinations domain. [A cue experiment](../Source-Text/PMC5433847.txt) times responses to an ambiguous external image. Evidence of a symptom, reports from a time window and experimental percept switching cannot be treated as an interchangeable visual episode rate.

The [2013 ADVANCE-PD](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID23485610-ADVANCE-PD-Randomized-Maintenance.txt) and [2014 ASCEND-PD](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID25306200-ASCEND-PD-Randomized-Crossover.txt) articles separate randomization and conversion. The 2015 report associates two visual adverse-event withdrawals with its cohort of 450 immediate-release starters, in advance of randomization. The [2016 nilotinib comparisons](2016-Visual-Outcome-Definitions-and-Nilotinib-Lineage.md) use other stages and distinct adverse-event definitions. None of these observations provides a clinical food exposure contrast.

The [phenylketonuria case](../Source-Text/PMC4375126.txt) separates earlier dietary restriction, suspected hallucinations on pramipexole, and a later motor response to levodopa. The [2015 viewpoint](../Source-Text/PMC6353393.txt) distinguishes historical alkaloid treatment, use of a mixed ceremonial beverage and the [2001 single-vine trial](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2001-Serrano-Duenas-Banisteriopsis-Parkinson-Trial.txt). The trial shows why a motor total can improve while a particular motor symptom worsens. Its original describes one transient hallucination in the extract arm without modality. The viewpoint calls it visual. The [1999 ceremonial record](../../1995-1999/Cultural-and-Experiential/Ceremonial-Accounts/1999-UDV-Hoasca-Research-Setting.txt) and [2018 experiment](../Source-Text/2018-PMID29368409-Banisteriopsis-Marmoset-Motor-Comparison.txt) describe different populations, preparations and outcomes.

The [AES member crosswalk](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-AES-Selected-Visual-and-Parkinsonism-Abstracts.txt) distinguishes the 2014 society members from the 2015 compiled issue, and identifies the repeated Dravet cohort. Complete proceedings access remains open. Twenty-six reviewed 2015 source pairs supply no measured dried fig and Parkinson visual episode comparison.
