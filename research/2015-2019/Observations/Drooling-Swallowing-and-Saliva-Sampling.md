# Drooling, swallowing and saliva sampling

The [Sohag cohort](../Source-Text/2018-PMID30416335-Mohamed-Subtype-Drooling-and-Swallowing.txt) observed more sialorrhea in the tremor-dominant group and more instrumental dysphagia in the gait-dominant group. The direction of the saliva association differs from the larger [Suzhou comparison](../Source-Text/2018-PMID29315470-Mao-Drooling-and-Motor-Subtypes.txt) of tremor-dominant and non-tremor-dominant patients. The small Sohag gait-dominant subgroup and different group definitions limit that comparison.

The [Zhejiang videofluoroscopy study](../Source-Text/2016-Xie-Videofluoroscopy-and-Drooling.txt) relates reported saliva loss most consistently to food leaking through the lips. The [Hamburg drooling analysis](../Source-Text/2017-PMID29178420-Nienstedt-Aspiration-and-Drooling.txt) finds limited overlap with aspiration. Its likely cohort companions study [different test consistencies](../Source-Text/2017-PMID28828545-Pflug-FEES-and-Food-Textures.txt) and [aspiration predictors](../Source-Text/2018-PMID30548367-Nienstedt-Predictors-of-Aspiration.txt).

The S4 [sponsor protocol](../Source-Text/2016-S4-001v4-Saliva-Biomarker-Protocol.txt), [design paper](../Source-Text/2017-PMID28353371-Visanji-S4-Design.txt), [registry](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT02572713.txt) and [feasibility report](../Source-Text/2018-PMID30248065-Chahine-S4-Saliva-Collection.txt) describe one biomarker study. The [ROMP validation from the prior period](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID21704797-Kalf-ROMP-Saliva-Validation.txt) distinguished perceived saliva control from swallowing and speech. These later cohorts add distinct measurements of clinical drooling, swallowing and sample collection. The related measurements do not establish a raw-fig effect.
