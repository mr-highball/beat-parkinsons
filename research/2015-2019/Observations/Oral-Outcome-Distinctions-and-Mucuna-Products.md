# Oral outcomes and marketed Mucuna preparations

The [Kalf validation](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMID21704797-Kalf-ROMP-Saliva-Validation.txt) in the previous period distinguished speech, swallowing and saliva control. The [Patel instrument review](../Source-Text/2017-PMID28375450-Patel-Dysphagia-Instrument-Review.txt) evaluates how the swallowing part was developed. The [Park salivagram study](../Source-Text/2017-PMID29354567-Park-Salivagram-and-Swallowing.txt) instead observes salivary aspiration in a brain-lesion cohort, with an open discrepancy about how positivity on both tests was counted.

The [focused-ultrasound review](../Source-Text/2017-PMID28244011-Fishman-Focused-Ultrasound-and-Drooling-Context.txt) cites three different measures from original surgery studies. The [2001 surgical series](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2001-PMID11235949-Intemann-Pallidotomy-and-Drooling.txt) reports increased drooling in four patients alongside speech changes. The [related gait study](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2000-PMID10681077-Siegel-Pallidotomy-and-Gait.txt) measured walking. The [separate Counihan series](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2001-PMID11274323-Counihan-Pallidotomy-Followup.txt) measured motor off time and activities of daily living. These sources distinguish adverse symptoms from total motor change.

The [Singhal account](../../2000-2004/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2003-PMID12915075-Singhal-Kampavata-Saliva-and-Mucuna.txt) associates Mucuna with an Ayurvedic syndrome that includes reported drooling. The [2012 poster](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMC3373868-Soumyanath-Mucuna-P36.txt), [2017 article](../Source-Text/2017-PMID28922612-Soumyanath-Mucuna-Label-Comparison.txt), and [2022 product study](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-PMID35939305-Cohen-Mucuna-Product-Assays.txt) compare preparation contents and use different labeling or reference comparisons. Keep possible overlap between the first two and distinguish the assays from the [seed-powder crossover](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15548480-Mucuna-Crossover-Extract.txt). There is no ingredient-specific drooling comparison among these reports.
