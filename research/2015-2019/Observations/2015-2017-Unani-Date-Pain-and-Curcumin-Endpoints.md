# Unani date descriptions and experimental turmeric pain endpoints

The [2015 Unani review](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2015-Azhar-Unani-Date-Fruit-and-Pain.txt) reports analgesic use, backache and sciatica indications, and a separate headache caution. Its tooth pain accounts use other plant parts. The [2014 Spanish inventory](../../2010-2014/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2014-Rivera-Date-Fruit-and-Pain-Spain.txt) gives a separate cultural context. Neither source establishes a patient-level response with a specified drying state. The [2016 family case series](../Source-Text/2016-PMID27186159-Date-Fruit-and-Fructose-Intolerance.txt) describes possible abdominal pain in a different population. Keep the historical indications and clinical account with uncertain attribution distinct.

The [older lipid-formulation abstract](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID25400173-Lipid-Curcumin-and-Arthritis-Pain-Abstract.txt) concerns arthritis. The [2015 isolated metabolite abstract](../Source-Text/2015-PMID26102012-Tetrahydrocurcumin-and-Experimental-Neuropathy.txt) concerns chemotherapy-related neuropathy. Both report animal pain behaviors and leave the ground turmeric Parkinson comparison unresolved.
