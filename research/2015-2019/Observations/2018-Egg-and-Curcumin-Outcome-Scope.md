# Mobility measures and actual falls

The [earlier Parkinson cohort in this period](../Source-Text/2017-PMID28777755-Amantadine-Extension-Visual-Adverse-Events.txt) explicitly recorded fall adverse events. The [earlier-period olfaction study](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID28360659-Olfactory-Function-and-Verbal-Memory-in-Parkinson.txt) measured a different endpoint. The [2018 solid-lipid curcumin trial](../Source-Text/2018-PMID29951592-Curcumin-Balance-and-Fatigue.txt) and [whole-egg dietary trial](../Source-Text/2018-PMID30041437-Whole-Egg-Protein-and-Muscle-Composition.txt) add randomized balance and tissue-composition tests with no clinical fall counts. The [egg-white trial registration](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT03530774.txt) explicitly lists recalled falls and confidence about falling as separate measurements. Check both fields before using a registry as an evidence bridge.
