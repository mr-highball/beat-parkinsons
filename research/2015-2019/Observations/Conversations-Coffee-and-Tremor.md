# Public conversation on coffee, mobility and tremor

A caregiver reported better mobility and speech after coffee in a relative without prominent tremor. A different participant reported worse tremor. The accounts concern different outcomes. [2019 forum record](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2019-Parkinsons-UK-Forum-Coffee-Mobility-and-Tremor.txt)

The earlier survey also documented perceived worsening in some respondents. Its acute oral caffeine challenge found no clear increase in measured tremor. A 2019 cross-sectional study associated prior coffee use with lower rest-tremor scores. These results concern different exposure timings and comparisons.
