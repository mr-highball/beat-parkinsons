# Visual endpoints and dried fig constituents in the 2017 source group

The [BNA poster collection](../Source-Text/PMC7545776.txt) and [later attention-task paper](../Source-Text/2017-PMID29560902-Parkinson-Visual-Perception-and-Drift-Rates.txt) use the same starting Parkinson group sizes. The paper accounts for five omitted Parkinson participants and reports one fewer control. The modeled evidence accumulation rate is a task result measured in groups classified by hallucination status. A participant crosswalk for controls and the earlier behavioral study remains open.

The [ANA issue record](../Source-Text/PMC7159532.txt) and [2018 Mayo abstract](../Source-Text/2018-PMID29406964-Parkinson-Motor-Subtypes-and-Hallucinations-Abstract.txt) report overlapping cohort features and hallucination presence at the final visit. The [2020 MAPT analysis](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7480915.txt) uses a restricted cohort from the same site and years. The [child neurology issue component](../Source-Text/2017-ANA-Child-Neurology-Meeting-Supplement-Scope.txt) has a different publication identifier and its complete article body remains open.

The [2017 EANM member](../Source-Text/2017-PMC7103094-Conference-Scope.txt) shows a relationship between visual hallucination status and PET findings in dementia with Lewy bodies. Its [2019 consortium follow-up](../Source-Text/2019-PMID30805951-European-Lewy-Body-Imaging-Abstract.txt) uses a larger imaging dataset and a different approach to adjustment. Participant overlap and the later original tables remain open.

The [USDA dried fig nutrient record](../Source-Text/2019-FDC174665-Dried-Fig-Pantothenic-Acid.txt) now documents iron, zinc, copper and manganese in the same food entry as the earlier magnesium and pantothenate results. The [2017 postmortem mineral analysis](../Source-Text/PMC5647261.txt) measures brain tissue rather than intake. Together the records identify a constituent lead. They do not estimate the direction of a food effect on hallucination frequency.

The [visual evoked response study](../Source-Text/PMC5472540.txt) examined visual physiology in Parkinson participants who reported no hallucinations. The [morning motor study](../Source-Text/PMC6174513.txt) records a visual hallucination history item, and the [vascular case series](../Source-Text/PMC6174482.txt) describes symptomatic trajectories during treatment of secondary parkinsonism. The endpoints differ from the [later medication-phase evidence](Apomorphine-Treatment-Changes-and-Visual-Events.md). The [original source index and exact coverage](../../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/fig-priority-six-2017-nov19-95/README.md) document the batch's other comparisons.
