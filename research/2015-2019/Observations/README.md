- Clinical and prodromal criteria formalized different uses for motor signs and risk markers. A larger multicenter REM sleep behavior disorder study quantified later parkinsonism or dementia in a specialist cohort. [Clinical and diagnosis](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md)
