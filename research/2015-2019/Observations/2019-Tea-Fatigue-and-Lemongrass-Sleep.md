# Beverage fatigue outcomes and customary sleep use

ProDiet extends the preceding period's [acute green tea crossover](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-Watanabe-Brewed-Green-Tea-and-Task-Fatigue.txt) to six months with a supplied tea bag beverage. The crossover assessed the degree of fatigue after a typing task in five healthy adults. ProDiet recorded whether participants reported fatigue among adverse symptoms in men at elevated prostate cancer risk. Its beverage count was lower than placebo and its extract capsule count was higher. Neither used the Parkinson Fatigue Severity Scale comparison reported in the [2015 single-arm pilot](../Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#green-tea-and-fatigue-in-parkinsons-disease). The [2019 Cedro interviews](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Local-Knowledge/2019-Silva-Cedro-Lemongrass-Tea-and-Insomnia.txt) document the customary use of brewed lemongrass leaves for trouble sleeping and calming. The older [1986 coded comparison](../../1985-1989/Source-Text/1986-Leite-Lemongrass-Tea-Sleep-Extract.txt) asked about morning experience. Waking sleepiness occurred slightly more often after the active brew in the published raw counts. The household practice, reported waking symptom and daytime sleepiness severity in Parkinson disease describe different observations.
