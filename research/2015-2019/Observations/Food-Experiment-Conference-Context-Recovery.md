# Genuine food context and archive identity

The earlier period's [clove presentation](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2011-PMC6863370-Clove-Odor-Stimulus-Abstract-Extract.txt) uses clove oil as an odor stimulus in an oxytocin experiment. It remains a real ingredient lead even though the intervention, route and outcome differ from culinary clove. A different endpoint does not erase the exposure, and its presence does not establish a clove treatment effect.

The [2018 nutrition collection](../Source-Text/2018-PMC6265811-Nutritional-Epidemiology-Culinary-Members-Extract.txt) adds genuine walnut-oil, egg, potato and black-pepper contexts. The oil evidence map does not identify its three oil publications. The apparent missing oral abstracts were en-dash identity variants; exact native identifiers and member titles resolve access parity. Orange-colored vegetables and calendar dates remain different meanings of the frozen foods.

The two nuclear-medicine deposits preserve [volume-7 metadata](../Source-Text/2018-PMC6231292-Nuclear-Collection-Identity-Extract.txt) and [printed volume-17 food-preparation context](../Source-Text/2018-PMC6354729-Nuclear-Imaging-Food-Preparation-Abstract-Extract.txt). Matching complete contents and original pages support one publication with a metadata defect. Exact provider keys remain separate; two deposits do not create two independent studies.

The [Cinnamomum leaf isolate](../Source-Text/2019-PMC6493311-Cinnamomum-Leaf-Isolate-Abstract-Extract.txt) and [green-tea polyphenols](../Source-Text/2019-PMC6738495-Green-Tea-Polyphenols-Abstract-Extract.txt) add laboratory leads. Unknown chemical labels, species and plant-part differences prevent assuming ordinary culinary exposures or clinical symptom effects.

The [Korean acrylamide abstract](../Source-Text/2017-Kim-Lee-Korean-Acrylamide-Furan-Abstract-Extract.txt) opens a contaminant trail. Unlike an efficacy trial, it estimates exposure from foods. Later Singapore reports add preparation-specific measurements; they do not retroactively establish a Parkinson outcome.
