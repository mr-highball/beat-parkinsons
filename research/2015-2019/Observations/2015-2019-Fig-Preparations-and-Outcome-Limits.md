# Fig preparations and outcome limits

The Isfahan trial measured bowel symptoms in adults with constipation-predominant irritable bowel syndrome. The randomized fig comparison benefited some reported bowel outcomes. Pain severity and C-reactive protein did not clearly change. The result abstract and registration identify figs, and the original processing description remains open. [Isfahan report](../Source-Text/2018-Pourmasoumi-Fig-IBS.txt) · [Registered design](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT02559245.txt)

The rat studies broaden the earlier preparation context. An extract from dried fig fruit lowered blood pressure, while dietary dried fig gave favorable liver markers in an ethanol model. The same investigators later found a mixed response across lung and muscle antioxidant markers using a matching design. Whether the same animals contributed to both tissue reports is unresolved. [Research summaries](../Food-Experiment-Fig-Clinical-and-Model-Traces.md#dried-fig-preparations-in-animal-studies)

The preceding period includes actual whole dried fruit in an adult crossover with lipid and mineral outcomes, as well as enzyme assays of processed material. The newer observations add tissue and bowel measurements. Neither period gives a controlled Parkinson visual episode comparison.
