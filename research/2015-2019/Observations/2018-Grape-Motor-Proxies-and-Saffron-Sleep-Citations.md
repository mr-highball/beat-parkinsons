# Grape preparations, motor proxies and saffron sleep citations

The [2018 grape-skin fly study](../Source-Text/2018-PMID30248358-Fermented-Grape-Skin-and-Fly-Motor-Phenotypes.txt) measures abnormal wing posture, locomotor activity and muscle energy. The preceding period's [2013 wild-grape cellular study](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID23485458-Wild-Grape-Amurensin-and-Rotenone-Cells.txt) instead measures cellular injury and autophagy after an isolated constituent. The [2009 red grape fly study](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID19929256-Whole-Grape-Knotweed-and-Climbing.txt) uses a mixed whole-grape and Japanese knotweed extract and measures climbing in a distinct model. The dissimilar exposures and motor tasks limit an inference about rigidity.

The [2017 safety table](../Source-Text/2017-PMID28293386-Saffron-Toxicity-and-Petal-Insomnia-Table.txt) repeats insomnia counts in the [earlier petal trial](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-PMID17174460-Saffron-Petal-Fluoxetine.txt), with three reports per arm. The [2009 mouse experiment](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID19142981-Saffron-Safranal-and-Induced-Sleep.txt) uses drug-induced sleep and distinguishes injected stigma extract, crocin and safranal. The [2018 narrative review](../Source-Text/2018-PMID29952263-Saffron-Cardiovascular-Review-and-Sleep-Scope.txt) brings the threads together, so preserving each original avoids treating these as directly opposing clinical results.
