# Sucrose consumption after isolated mangiferin

The [2014 observations](../../2010-2014/Observations/2014-Mangiferin-Behavior-and-Food-Attribution.md) distinguish the inflammatory mouse model from clinical depression. The [2017 Cao author abstract](../Source-Text/2017-PMID28410265-Mangiferin-and-Mouse-Sucrose-Consumption.txt) instead studies chronic mild stress and reports increased sucrose consumption. The later review's opposite description can lead a reader to infer the wrong direction from the same data.

The original source was accessible at author-abstract depth. The [2022 study](../../2020-2024/Observations/2022-Mangiferin-Model-Comparisons.md) reports different behavioral outcomes in a third model.
