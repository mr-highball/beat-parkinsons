# 2019 saffron citation, dietary oil cohort and clove event observations

The 2019 saffron metabolite review's reference 110 is an induced-seizure article. The complete 2006 article cites a separate 2004 proceedings paper that contains forced-swim observations and different administration and endpoints. Recording both originals keeps the citation distinct from the mood hypothesis.
