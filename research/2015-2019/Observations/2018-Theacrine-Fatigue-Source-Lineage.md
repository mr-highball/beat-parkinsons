# Theacrine and fatigue across the 2010–2014 and 2015–2019 periods

The [2014 pilot abstract](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMC4271659-P49-Theacrine-Pilot-and-Fatigue.txt), [2016 full pilot](../Source-Text/2016-PMID27164220-Theacrine-Pilot-Fatigue-Full-Report.txt), [2018 filing](../Source-Text/2018-US20180104248A1-Theacrine-Caffeine-Patent-Fatigue-Lineage.txt) and [2019 continuation](../Source-Text/2019-US20190374546A1-Theacrine-Caffeine-Continuation-and-Fatigue.txt) preserve the same acute fatigue result from a 15-person healthy-adult crossover. The later report adds information about a nine-person open-label component. It also contains inconsistent descriptions of blinding. The different records add provenance to one trial family.

The [eight-week comparison](../Source-Text/2016-PMID26766930-Theacrine-and-Eight-Week-Fatigue.txt) provides a separate test of isolated theacrine and found no group-by-time difference on its fatigue scale. The [2017 conference report](../Source-Text/2017-ISSN-P36-Theacrine-Caffeine-and-Subjective-Feelings.txt) and [2018 journal article](../Source-Text/2018-DOI100034-Theacrine-Caffeine-Crossover-Full-Report.txt) describe the same-sized, matching five-condition trial. Its lower-dose condition had higher overall lethargy ratings, with an inconclusive condition-by-time comparison. These are separate observations on acute subjective fatigue, perceived lethargy and a longer-term mood scale. A brewed tea comparison in Parkinson disease remains open for Round 1 claim 0948.
