# Saffron combinations and sage identity in earlier sources

The [eight-week 2016 pilot](../Source-Text/2016-Cicero-Saffron-Combination-Cognition-and-Depression.txt) compared a saffron-containing multi-ingredient product with placebo. Depression scores improved within both arms. Table 2 marks between-group differences for global cognition and perceived stress only. The [later Bacopa review](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-PMID38671841-Bacopa-Review-and-Saffron-Combination-Citation.txt) covers several exposures and symptoms. The original study separates the depression comparison from those other outcomes.

The [Valencian ethnopharmacology field paper](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2017-PMID28790914-Valencian-Sage-Taxa-Pain-and-Fatigue-Uses.txt) records topical Spanish-sage uses for headache and fatigue. The subspecies and preparations differ from the canonical oral garden-sage claim. The earlier [2012 extract record](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMID23017402-Sage-Rosemary-Extracts.txt) concerns preparation chemistry and cell outcomes. The dated records establish different kinds of observations and leave clinical fall frequency unresolved.
