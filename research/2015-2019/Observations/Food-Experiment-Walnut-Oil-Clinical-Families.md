# What the clinical identity checks change

Compared with the [2003 lipid abstract](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2003-PMID12934760-Zibaeenezhad-Walnut-Oil-Lipids-Abstract-Extract.txt), later reports provide more original methods and registered outcomes, but still do not test a Parkinson symptom. The [2013 original](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID23616506-Berryman-Walnut-Fractions-Extract.txt) establishes solvent extraction rather than cold pressing. Its [later posted results](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT00938340-Results-2018.txt) require metric-specific and multiplicity interpretation.

The Iranian glucose and lipid studies have distinct registrations, correcting a preliminary uncertainty about protocol identity. This establishes two named protocols while leaving participant overlap unresolved. Registration and paper dates conflict, and a subsequent glycemic report belongs to the earlier named family. Similar summary values are a reason to investigate, not proof of independent or shared participants.

These checks prevent counting conference summaries, registrations, companion papers and later reviews as independent evidence. They do not transform lipids, HbA1c, endothelial function or cell assays into pain, cognition or another Parkinson symptom.
