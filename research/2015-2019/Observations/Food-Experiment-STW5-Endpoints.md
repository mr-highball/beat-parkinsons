# Gastric stress and depression measurements

Following the earlier mixed-tincture clinical comparison, the 2015 study measures gastric muscle responses and hormones in stressed rats. Some comparisons improve and others remain inconclusive. These endpoints cannot replace measured depression. The cited poster adds doses and outcomes, leaving actual sample overlap unresolved.
