# Food exposure and fatigue attribution

The 2018 proceedings add a traceable historical attribution about black seed and fatigue. They add no participant comparison. The 2019 fennel trial adds an original measured fatigue-occurrence comparison. Both reported groups have the same frequency after treatment, and the registration leaves comparator uncertainty.

The earlier 2014 fennel capsule study reported changes in menstrual weakness. The 2019 result addresses occurrence in a different preparation and trial setting. The separate 2007 fatigue table remains open. These differences matter when comparing the indirect signals with the frozen Parkinson fatigue-severity outcome.
