# Visual outcome definitions and nilotinib trial lineage

The 2016 nilotinib pilot compared two active-dose groups in 12 people with Parkinson dementia or dementia with Lewy bodies. It recorded mild hallucination adverse events and described broader psychotic symptoms while some Parkinson medicines changed. The registry and paper identify the same pilot, with different wording about the population and outcomes. [Pilot paper](../Source-Text/PMC5008228.txt) · [Original registration](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/NCT02281474.txt).

The later 75-person Georgetown trial recorded a nonserious hallucination report in placebo and a serious report in the higher nilotinib group. It reported these as adverse-event categories without a separate visual episode measure. The 63-person open-label extension follows completers of that parent trial. The independent 76-person NILO-PD study found little central exposure and no change in measured dopamine metabolites attributable to treatment. These trials add comparison groups and distinct analyses. They do not measure a dried fig exposure. [Georgetown registration](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT02954978.txt) · [Placebo-controlled report](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC6990742.txt) · [Extension](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC8048914.txt) · [Independent NILO-PD trial](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7737147.txt).

The Georgetown protocol names grapefruit juice as a drug interaction restriction. A separate crossover in healthy participants measured increased serum nilotinib exposure with double-strength juice. Both records provide an indirect lead for the raw grapefruit claim. Neither measures Parkinson resting tremor after eating raw fruit. [Crossover digest](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID19948946-Grapefruit-Juice-and-Nilotinib.txt) · [Trial protocol](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/78/NCT02954978/Prot_SAP_000.pdf).

The 2016 sources also distinguish reported adverse events, clinical hallucination presence, severity items and frequency used as a covariate. These complement the [2017 visual outcome observations](2017-Research-Visual-Endpoints-and-Fig-Constituents.md). The [published caregiver accounts](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Personal-Reports/2016-LBDA-Caregiver-Reports-Quoted-in-Clinical-Viewpoint.txt) give a different perspective on safety and daily function.
