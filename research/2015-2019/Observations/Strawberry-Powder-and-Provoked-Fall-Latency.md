# Strawberry powder and provoked fall latency

The 2015 study measured how long normally aging rats stayed on three instruments. The strawberry powder arm was shorter than blueberry on the stationary rod and longer on the wide plank. Strawberry and control shared significance letters on the rod, rotarod and plank. Fig. 1 shows that the numeric direction also varies by apparatus against control. The discussion's pooled language about berries hides that distinction. [2015 primary study](../Source-Text/2015-PMID26392037-Strawberry-and-Blueberry-Fall-Latency.txt).

The earlier extract diets covered different models or outcomes. The 1999 aging interval used dried aqueous extracts and favored blueberry over strawberry in the rod and rotarod tests. The 2006 irradiation study reported spatial retention for strawberry extract and no fall assessment. The 2015 powder uses the whole fruit preparation, with a different dose, test details and cohort. Clinical falls still require observed event counts over a time window.
