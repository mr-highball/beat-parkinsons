# Lemon balm dose and formulation boundaries

The cell study tests both the older nine-extract formula and six-extract variant and reports limited effects for single extracts. Later GI comparisons use the whole formula, so a product effect cannot be assigned to lemon balm alone.

- [Fixed STW 5 and STW 5-II compared with individual extracts in colon cells](../Source-Text/2016-PMID27833553-STW5-Component-Colon-Cells.txt)
