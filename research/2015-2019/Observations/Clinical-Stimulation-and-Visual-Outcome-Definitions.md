# Clinical stimulation and visual outcome definitions

The 2019 proceedings separate knowledge of visual symptoms, postoperative onset and measures collected in a randomized stimulation trial. The North East interview is a planned secondary outcome in the six-person dementia-with-Lewy-bodies trial. The published exploratory NPI hallucination subscale gives a median paired difference of zero. The aggregate NPI results describe a broader psychiatric outcome. The earlier six-person Parkinson dementia crossover is a separate trial with a different population. The selected clinical papers also distinguish baseline symptom recognition or disease status from a treatment-related event. The retrospective pimavanserin study uses clinical psychosis improvement. The FDG-PET and tau PET studies record presence of visual symptoms as part of diagnostic profiling. The preceding period's clinical procedures and case observations depend on outcome definition and ascertainment, too.
