# Registered whole chicken and ingredient measurements

A 2018 registration compares salad chicken with no food during an exercise study and gives no posted outcome values. A separate 2019 registration concerns several weeks of resistance training and chicken in older participants; both of its result papers appeared in 2024. The 2019 composition study reports dipeptide amounts in chicken parts and retention under sealed retort conditions. This supports a constituent link to earlier drink experiments without establishing the amount consumed after open poaching or a symptom outcome.

The 2018 Parkinson diet survey collected chicken intake and a sleep/fatigue domain but left the clinical comparison by chicken intake unreported. The extract literature and whole-food records therefore resolve different preparation details while leaving the frozen exposure and outcome pairing open.
