# Food experiment sleep and attention comparisons

## Zanthoxylum extract and drug-induced sleep in mice

The high extract dose shortened drug-induced loss of righting. The onset cell and narrative disagree on significance. The assay, plant preparation and population limit transfer to insomnia after eating toasted Sichuan pepper.
