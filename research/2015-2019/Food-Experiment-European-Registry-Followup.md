# European dietary and botanical registrations, 2015–2019

## Ropinirole meal study sponsor summary

The EU register first posted the study summary for EudraCT 2006-006209-94 on July 2, 2016. The report confirms 25 fed and 25 fasted pharmacokinetic observations at the comparable dose, with fed-to-fasted ratios of 1.20 (90% CI 1.12–1.28) for AUC and 1.44 (90% CI 1.34–1.56) for peak concentration. Estimated time to peak was 3.01 hours later (90% CI 2.00–4.01). These results are from the same study in the 2008 FDA review. ## Aerosolized capsaicin and reflex cough force

DRKS00010719 was registered June 28, 2016 for a comparison of voluntary and capsaicin-evoked peak cough flow in people with and without neurogenic dysphagia. The registry links the 2022 paper, which included three people with Parkinson’s. It reported no Parkinson-specific effect estimate. The chemical challenge was aerosolized cayenne extract, and the endpoint was cough flow. [Primary registration](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00010719/details) · [2022 results and preparation assessment](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#aerosolized-cayenne-extract-and-airway-protection)

## Vitamin K2 mitochondrial biomarker trial

DRKS00019932 was registered December 19, 2019. PD-K2 planned a randomized comparison of one week of menaquinone-7 versus placebo in groups with genetic Parkinson’s, sporadic Parkinson’s and healthy controls. The primary endpoint is a magnetic resonance spectroscopy estimate of cerebral ATP and phosphocreatine. Prasuhn and colleagues published the protocol January 11, 2021. The public record contains no results, and an identifier and title search verified only the protocol. [Primary registration](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00019932/details) · [2021 primary protocol](https://doi.org/10.3389/fneur.2020.592104)

## QuinoMit Q10 formulation in the MitoPD protocol

DRKS00015880 was registered November 15, 2018. The planned comparison assigned participants in four genetic strata to a QuinoMit Q10 fluid or placebo for six months. The registry targeted 84 participants and prespecified MDS-UPDRS III as the primary endpoint, with secondary measures of mood, cognition, fatigue, motor complications and timed mobility. Prasuhn and colleagues’ August 23, 2019 protocol specifies 156 mg/day of coenzyme Q10 and excludes soy allergy. The manufacturer lists soy lecithin as an ingredient of the named commercial formulation. Neither the trial protocol nor the registry provides the placebo recipe or verifies the commercial formula for the trial batch. Soy lecithin is a phospholipid ingredient, and the documents give no separately assigned soybean oil exposure. The registry lists recruitment as planned and has no results.
