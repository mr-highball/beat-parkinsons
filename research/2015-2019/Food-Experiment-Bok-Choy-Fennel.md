# Bok choy and fennel seed evidence

## Fennel seed extract in a 6-OHDA rat model

Nakisa and colleagues published the study in the summer 2015 issue of the Journal of Birjand University of Medical Sciences. Female rats received a methanolic extract of fennel seeds by gavage. Repeated exposure for 14 days before and 14 days after intracerebroventricular 6-OHDA was reported to improve rotarod and wire-grasp tests on day 14 (p < .05). The 14-day regimen started after the lesion had no clear effect on those tests. These tests capture coordination and grasp in a lesion model. Parkinson postural responses, freezing episodes and toasted seeds were outside the experiment. [Original paper, English abstract and Persian full text](https://www.academia.edu/121270248/Assessment_of_Foeniculum_Vulgare_L_methanolic_extract_effect_on_a_model_of_Parkinson_s_disease_induced_by_ICV_injection_of_6_hydroxydopamin_in_female_rats_using_behavioral_tests) · [Regional bibliographic record](https://www.virascience.com/en/article/164154/)

## Bok choy in a Taiwanese prospective falls questionnaire

Lin and colleagues reported a prospective community cohort in 2016. Among 953 Taiwanese postmenopausal women followed for one year, 183 fell and 25 sustained low-impact fractures. The food questionnaire grouped bok choy with spinach, chrysanthemum greens, broccoli and green peppers as dark-green vegetables. The paper reports fruit intake among dietary predictors of falls and a combined vegetable association with fragility fracture. It gives no separate estimate for bok choy, preparation methods or an effect on postural testing in Parkinson’s. [Original Methods and results tables](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6140300/)

## Fennel essence and locomotor behavior in reserpine rats

Nemati and colleagues published February 18, 2018. In non-ovariectomized rats, the reserpine-only group II averaged 146.75 for bar posture and 4.25 for open field. Group III, given fennel essential oil with reserpine, averaged 17.67 and 332.17. Group IV received levodopa/carbidopa and averaged 77.75 and 32.50. The published figures annotate comparisons to untreated group I, so the oil versus reserpine values remain descriptive here. Resting tremor, timed OFF and observed freezing did not appear among the outcomes, and the exposure was concentrated oil. [Original report, Methods and Table 1](https://brieflands.com/journals/jjnpp/articles/67391) · [Indexed source extract](Source-Text/2018-Nemati-Fennel-Oil-Reserpine-Motor-Tests.txt)

## Fennel capsules and daytime dysfunction in a placebo trial

Afiat and colleagues published December 31, 2018. Fifty postmenopausal women completed 12 weeks in a randomized comparison of a commercial anethole-standardized fennel capsule in sunflower oil against a sunflower-oil placebo. Mean Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index daytime dysfunction was 0.36 to 0.25 with fennel and 0.28 to 0.16 with placebo (week 12 comparison p = .439). This component includes difficulty staying awake during daytime activities and reduced enthusiasm. Table 1 does not report the wakefulness item separately. The preparation and population differ from toasted fennel seed and Parkinson’s. [Original Methods and Table 1](https://e-jmm.org/DOIx.php?id=10.6118/jmm.2018.24.3.204)
