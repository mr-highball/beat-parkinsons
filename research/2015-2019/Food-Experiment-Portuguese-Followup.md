# Portuguese-language dietary studies, 2015–2019

## Levodopa and dietary protein conference record

Silva and colleagues’ September 2018 report concerns interactions between levodopa and dietary protein. Crossref and OpenAlex identify the work and DOI. The author’s publication record identifies the 22nd Brazilian Congress of Nutrology. The DOI, publisher abstract page and Crossref record yielded no usable abstract text. Cohort, preparations and symptom measures remain unresolved. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0038-1674793) · [OpenAlex W2893982418](https://openalex.org/W2893982418)

## Protein intake and disease stage in São Paulo

Guerdão and colleagues published a cross-sectional study of 31 people aged 60 or older with Parkinson’s at São Paulo’s Hospital do Servidor Público Estadual on January 31, 2019. One 24-hour recall yielded mean protein intake of 1.22 g/kg/day, with 50.17 g in the first three meals and 38.37 g in the last three meals. Table 5 gives p = .189 for total protein by disease stage and p = .358 by disease duration. The comparisons concern dietary protein totals and broad clinical categories. The published tables leave individual foods, levodopa timing relative to individual foods, and symptom-specific change unresolved. [Primary article, Methods and Tables 3–5](https://acervomais.com.br/index.php/saude/article/download/219/216) · [OpenAlex W2914642797](https://openalex.org/W2914642797)
