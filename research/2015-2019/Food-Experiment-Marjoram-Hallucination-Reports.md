# Marjoram Hallucination Reports

## Regulatory review of marjoram preparations and clinical safety

The European Medicines Agency reviewed defined medicinal preparations of Origanum majorana including dried flowering shoots as tea and a topical extract. Its 2016 review reported no documented overdose, mental impairment or abuse case in the sources assessed. It gives no study estimate of hallucination incidence, and its traditional-use record supplies no fresh-leaf Parkinson comparison. This review has a different exposure and source scope from the 1970 historical mention.
