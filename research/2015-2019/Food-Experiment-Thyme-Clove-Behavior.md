# Thyme Clove Behavior

## Isolated beta caryophyllene and the MPTP pole task

Viveros-Paredes and colleagues compared isolated beta-caryophyllene with MPTP model controls. Oral pretreatment gave a pole-task time of 5.7 ± 1.8 seconds versus 16.5 ± 1.7 with MPTP alone. The paper identifies the compound in several botanicals, including clove leaf and stem oil. This is a pre-exposure isolated compound result on a rodent bradykinesia-like assay, without ground flower-bud clove or clinical Parkinson treatment.
