# Clinical and experimental outcome boundaries

## Synthetic paradol and experimental ischemic deficit

March 19, 2015. Gaire and colleagues cite a Parkinson dopamine-agonist adverse-effects review in their introductory discussion. The original experiments test synthetic 6-paradol in cellular assays and focal cerebral ischemia in mice, with a neurological deficit composite. The models and outcomes give no comparison of ground ginger and Parkinson dyskinesia. The citation leaves the source review’s full body unresolved.
