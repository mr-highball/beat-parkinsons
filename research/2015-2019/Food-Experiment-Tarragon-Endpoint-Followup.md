# Tarragon endpoint follow-up

## Russian tarragon and systolic pressure in glucose intolerance

The April 20, 2016 randomized double-blind study assigned 24 people with impaired glucose tolerance equally to Russian tarragon or placebo for 90 days. The primary abstract reports systolic pressure falling within the tarragon group from 120.0 ± 11.3 to 113.0 ± 11.2 mmHg (p < .05). It does not give the placebo systolic values, a between-arm change estimate, standing measurements or orthostatic symptoms. The publicly indexed preparation is an assigned supplement. The later registry results give the two week-12 endpoint means and label the tests within each arm. ## Tarragon in odor enrichment for muscarinic receptor knockout mice

Chan and colleagues included tarragon in a rotating odor-enrichment program presented for 24 hours per odor during a 40-day period. The authors say each named fragrance was used once and do not give an individual dose or a complete calendar for the odors. M1/M3 double-knockout male mice investigated the two test esters similarly before enrichment (3.2 versus 3.3 seconds, n = 13, p = .94). After enrichment the mice spent longer investigating C8 than C7 (9.0 versus 3.5 seconds, n = 6, p < .001). Mice housed with an empty odor container still showed no clear discrimination (4.1 versus 3.4 seconds, n = 5, p = .72). These are comparisons between test odors within conditions. This is related to 0982 with an olfactory behavior outcome and a tarragon-containing odor regimen. The study leaves tarragon's individual effect and any benefit of ingestion unresolved.
