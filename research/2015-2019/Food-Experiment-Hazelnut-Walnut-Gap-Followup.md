# Hazelnut and walnut oil outcome follow-up

## Hazelnut oil in a registered urinary biomarker study

DRKS00013219 was entered retrospectively October 25, 2017. The German Federal Institute for Risk Assessment registered a nonrandomized, open-label crossover of hazelnut oil and palm fat in 12 adults. Urinary contaminant biomarkers after exposure and their pre-exposure excretion were the registered outcomes. The registry links two resulting reports. Its ethics number EA4/139/17 differs from EA4/130/17 given in the first article. Matching investigators, exposures, dates and linked publications support the study match. This registration does not supply urinary urgency results for 0397. ## Walnut oil as the comparator in a cardiac rehabilitation sleep trial

McDonnell and Newcomb first published September 26, 2019. In a randomized double-blind crossover, 42 outpatients supplied complete data after placing cotton balls containing a three-essential-oil blend or grocery La Tourangelle 100% walnut oil on a jojoba base at the bedside for five nights, followed by a one-week washout and the other exposure. The global PSQI mean was 8.0 during walnut oil and 4.9 during the blend condition (p = .0001). For getting up to use the bathroom at night, the 0–3 item means were 2.42 and 2.18, respectively (Table 2 Wilcoxon p = .046). This individual item came from a seven-day recall questionnaire and Table 2 reports no adjustment across its nine items. Bad dream item means were 0.42 and 0.25, with no clear difference. Sleep latency and duration also showed no clear differences. The report provides no stand-alone daytime sleepiness result or motor dream-enactment measure. Daytime urinary urgency, nocturnal bathroom-related sleep interruption and a report of bad dreams describe different outcomes. This comparator supports a related assessment for 0664, where the report leaves effects of orally consumed cold-pressed oil unresolved. The article does not give lot or extraction method. A distributor brochure predating the study describes the brand's listed walnut oil as roasted and expeller-pressed. The lot distinction remains unresolved.
