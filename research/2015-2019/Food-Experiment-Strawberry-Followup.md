# Strawberry and fisetin follow-up, 2015–2019

## Isolated fisetin in dopaminergic cells and MPTP mice

Pamela Maher’s July 10, 2017 review reports experiments from her lab in Figures 2–4. Isolated fisetin at 5 or 10 µM improved survival of MN9D dopaminergic cells exposed to 6-OHDA or paraquat. The mouse experiment used oral 10 or 25 mg/kg fisetin starting one day before MPTP exposure and continuing for seven days. Figure 3 reports six mice per treatment and approximately 70% and more than twofold increases in striatal dopamine relative to MPTP alone at the lower and higher doses, respectively. Figure 4 reports preservation of striatal tyrosine hydroxylase immunoreactivity at 25 mg/kg. The tested formulation was isolated fisetin in a vehicle. The article estimates a strawberry equivalent from a food composition value and uses the 2012 Renoudet case for clinical context. Neither part provides a new fruit exposure or handwriting outcome for claim `0349`. Related 2008 patent data come from the same investigator, with uncertain cohort overlap.
