# A mixed-herb multiple-sclerosis fatigue trial

## Distilled cinnamon-containing mixture and fatigue scores

Namjooyan and colleagues published a three-month double-blind trial on 26 April 2019. Sixty adults with multiple sclerosis were randomized equally, and 51 completed. The 15 cc/day formulation was made by soaking two parts ajwain and one part Iranian borago for 24 hours and one part cinnamon for 72 hours, then distilling. The text also calls for four capsules daily without explaining their relationship to the stated volume. Its introduction discusses Cinnamomum verum/zeylanicum bark generally, but the exact cinnamon material in the mixture is not independently authenticated. The mixture was not a comparison of ground culinary cinnamon.

The fatigue-severity means in Table 2 were 3.729 at baseline and 2.213 at month three for the mixture, versus 3.520 and 5.066 for placebo. The paper reports p=.353 for the overall group term and p<.001 for the group-by-time interaction. The authors report nine withdrawals but list reasons totaling ten; printed SD values and confidence interval widths in Table 2 are internally inconsistent. These reporting limitations, multiple ingredients, distillation and the non-Parkinson population prevent a cinnamon-specific inference.
