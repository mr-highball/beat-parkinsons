# Sage-containing teas in controlled trial records

## Postpartum care bundle and registered breast pain

Sage tea belongs to a multicomponent care program in NCT04038749. The trial lists a 0–10 pain scale and gives conflicting descriptions of allocation. Comparative effects and the exact tea preparation remain open. [Dated registry text](Source-Text/2019-NCT04038749.txt)

## Sage in a mixed-tea safety comparator

NCT04054284 names common sage in a seven-herb control tea. The clinical outcomes distinguish overall adverse-event counts from blood-glucose measures and do not specify clinical pain scores. No results were posted when reviewed. [Dated registry text](Source-Text/2019-NCT04054284.txt)
