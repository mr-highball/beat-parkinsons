# Rosemary extract in a mixed-supplement case

## Multiple supplements and visual hallucinations

Wong and colleagues reported visual and auditory hallucinations in an adult taking five supplements, one listing rosemary extract. Symptoms improved after the supplements were stopped and antipsychotic treatment began. The report does not identify Parkinson disease or isolate rosemary, fresh leaves, or hallucination frequency. It is the same 2016 case already cataloged for holy basil.
