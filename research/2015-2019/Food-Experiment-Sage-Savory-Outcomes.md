# Sage and summer savory symptom studies

## Sage extract and footprint recovery after peripheral nerve injury

El Gabbas and colleagues published a controlled mouse sciatic nerve injury experiment on June 24, 2019. The oral ethanolic sage leaf extract groups recovered on the sciatic functional index. The index uses paw print length and toe spread during walking. It gives no separate stride-length, rigidity, dystonia, fall or handwriting measure. The injury affects a peripheral nerve and the exposure was extracted leaf. [Primary paper, Methods and Figure 6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6613031/) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC6613031.txt)

## Summer savory extract in an immobilization stress model

Moghaddam and Asl Rousta reported six groups of eight rats in the Mehr 1395 issue of Daneshvar Medicine (September–October 2016). The hydroalcoholic extract increased open-arm entry and time in the elevated plus maze in stressed rats at both studied doses (p < .01). The higher dose shortened immobility in the forced swim test (p < .05). The publication's public Persian abstract gives no fatigue measure. Its forced-swim result also differs in direction from the 2021 tail-suspension report, in which the extract attenuated an abnormally low immobility time after cisplatin. Both results are sensitive to the model and task. [Original Persian abstract](https://www.magiran.com/p1599687) · [Cisplatin tail suspension report](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#summer-savory-extract-and-tail-suspension-after-cisplatin)

## Summer savory extract and locomotion after cisplatin

The July 29, 2019 Kumburovic paper reports separate open-field and elevated-plus-maze movement measures in the previously cataloged cisplatin rat study. The middle dose attenuated the decline in distance moved and time moving. The lower and higher doses generally left activity below control values, and the extract-only group had no clear difference from control. Five rats were analyzed per group. These tests measure activity while awake and provide no sleep, fatigue, freezing, rigidity, falls or dystonia result.
