# Rosemary and lemon balm evidence

## Abdominal massage and constipation in people with Parkinson disease

McClurg and colleagues published the pilot in January 2016. Thirty-two people with Parkinson disease were randomized to lifestyle advice with or without daily abdominal massage for six weeks. Gastrointestinal ratings and bowel diaries showed improvement in both groups, with an inconclusive between-group comparison. This is a clinical comparison for the massage procedure in the mixed-oil literature. The study assigned no rosemary exposure. [Primary randomized pilot](https://doi.org/10.1093/ageing/afw005)

## Lemon balm extract and locomotor activity in MPTP mice

Reddy, Saravanan and Praveen reported an MPTP mouse experiment in May 2019. Mice received oral ethanolic whole-plant extract before and after the model exposure. Rotarod retention and general movement counts improved at the two higher tested levels compared with model controls. Table IV lists actophotometer readings of 202.8 for model controls, 331.5 and 355.2 in the two higher groups, and a nonsignificant contrast at the lowest level. The Methods report 30 mice and also specify six groups of six, leaving the stated sample accounting inconsistent. No task captured sustained abnormal postures, handwriting, or episodes of freezing during walking. [Original article, Methods and Tables III–IV](https://doi.org/10.5958/0974-360X.2019.00349.4)

## Rosmarinic acid and overnight urinary frequency in a patent study

The international patent application WO2019230626A1 was published December 5, 2019. Its examples include a rat feeding comparison and a human comparison of rosmarinic-acid-containing perilla extract. In the human example, the reported treatment–control difference in change in nighttime urination was −0.24 episodes per night (p < .05). Daytime frequency and daily urine volume contrasts were inconclusive. Table 3 and the prose give different standard errors for the daytime contrast. The commercial red-perilla water extract and starch conditions each lasted 28 days in 14 healthy men. The low baseline nighttime frequency was 0.24 episodes. [Dated factual text](Source-Text/2019-WO2019230626A1-Perilla-Voiding-Factual-Extract.txt) The application reports no urgency scale or Parkinson subgroup. Perilla extract and lemon balm tea are different exposures that share rosmarinic acid. [Original international application](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2019230626A1/en) · [English publication, Tables 3–4](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3804710A1/en)

## Rosmarinic acid across nine brewed lemon balm samples

Petkova and colleagues infused commercial and garden samples for 20 minutes at 95 °C using 1 g of dried material in 100 mL of water. HPLC values in Table 3 ranged from 2.40 to 23.10 mg of rosmarinic acid per gram of starting material. This shows a range in brewed leaf chemistry and supplies no conversion between tea and an extract dose or clinical result. [Local factual text](Source-Text/2017-Petkova-Lemon-Balm-Infusion.txt) · [Primary source](https://bcc.bas.bg/BCC_Volumes/Volume_49_Special_D_2017/BCC2017-49-SI-D-Juchnovski.pdf)

## Lemon balm powder and depression scores in stable angina

Haybar and colleagues randomized 80 adults with stable angina to 3 g/day of powdered lemon balm in capsules or corn starch for eight weeks. Table 3 lists 35 and 38 completers. DASS-21 depression means fell from 8.54 to 6.46 and 6.92 to 6.71, respectively. Reported baseline-adjusted ANCOVA p < .001. Adjusted means and intervals are unavailable in the table. Previous depression and mental disorders were exclusions. The sleep and biomarker reports with overlapping authors and matching arms should not be counted as independent without resolving their registration identity. Original Table 3 was checked from the author article rendered at https://www.scribd.com/document/531022292/haybar2018. [Local factual text](Source-Text/2018-Haybar-Lemon-Balm-Angina.txt) · [Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clnesp.2018.04.015)

## Aqueous lemon balm leaf extract and severe-depression screening

Alijaniha and colleagues randomized 71 adults with benign palpitations to a freeze-dried aqueous leaf extract or placebo for 14 days. Table 3 reported severe-depression threshold counts among 55 completers: extract 3/28 to 3/28, placebo 10/27 to 6/27 (within-arm p = 1 and .12). The thresholds started imbalanced. No verified between-group change test or continuous depression severity estimate applies to this item. Original Table 3: https://www.academia.edu/93800917/Heart_palpitation_relief_with_Melissa_officinalis_leaf_extract_Double_blind_randomized_placebo_controlled_trial_of_efficacy_and_safety. [Local factual text](Source-Text/2015-Alijaniha-Lemon-Balm-GHQ.txt) · [Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jep.2015.02.007)

## Lemon balm capsule trial in adolescents with premenstrual symptoms

Heydari's original proof Table 2 reports lower post-treatment GHQ depression under capsules, 10.72 versus 12.50. Baseline psychological means are unavailable and the reported p=.001 does not reconcile with the ordinary t-test implied by 50 per arm and the printed SDs. The preparation and population differ from brewed tea in Parkinson disease. [Original-table factual extract](Source-Text/2018-Heydari-Lemon-Balm-PMS.txt) · [Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1515/ijamh-2017-0015)

The [2015 PSST report](Source-Text/2015-PMID26339667-Akbarzadeh-Melissa-PMS-Extract.txt) and [2017-online four-arm report](Source-Text/2017-Akbarzadeh-Melissa-Education-PMS-Extract.txt) share all active and placebo total-score trajectories, registry and thesis. Domain tables conflict on baseline and timing. All three papers share the [retrospective registration](../2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/IRCT2014060717998N1.txt); individual membership of the GHQ analysis remains unverified. The related reports contribute one overlapping family, with distinct questionnaires and unresolved reporting discrepancies.

## Lemon balm and Nepeta blend in insomnia with depressive symptoms

Ranjbar and colleagues considered 67 adults with insomnia and BDI > 10. The original flow diagram reports 54 randomized, 27 per arm, and 45 completing the four-week study. In the per-protocol analysis, Table 1 BDI fell from 25.86 to 16.26 versus 24.18 to 21.40 after four weeks with placebo (repeated-measures p < .001). Active capsules combine 1,000 mg lemon balm and 400 mg Nepeta menthoides. The comparison cannot allocate effects to either ingredient. [Local factual text](Source-Text/2018-Ranjbar-Lemon-Balm-Nepeta.txt) · [Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.imr.2018.08.001)
