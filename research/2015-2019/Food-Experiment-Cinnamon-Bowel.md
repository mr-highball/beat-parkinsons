# Cinnamon bowel outcomes, 2015–2019

## Cassia bark water extract in loperamide-treated rats

Sebai and colleagues measured 24-hour stool outcomes in groups of seven rats with drug-induced constipation. At 300 mg/kg aqueous cassia bark extract, the reported mean pellet count was 48.35 versus 40.23 under loperamide alone and reported fecal water was 37.91% versus 22.43%, both marked p<.05. The lower dose lacks the Table 5 mark for a significant contrast against the constipated control. The reported fecal-water percentages do not reconcile directly with the group mean wet and dry weights under the usual ratio formula. This remains a constipation-model result for a water extract.
