# Tart cherry drinks and recovery outcomes in athletes

## Water polo crossover and perceived recovery

McCormick and colleagues first published November 14, 2016. Nine young male water polo players completed blinded crossover periods of 90 mL/day of Montmorency concentrate diluted in water or a mixed-fruit cordial placebo, six days per condition. Table 3 reports inconclusive tests for total quality of recovery and muscle soreness. Recovery was a rating of how well athletes felt they had recovered, and the report gives no separate clinical fatigue result. The article reports retrospective registration ACTRN12616001080415. ## Soccer crossover and subjective well-being

Abbott and colleagues’ 2019 author abstract describes a randomized blinded crossover in ten male professional soccer players. The drinks were tart cherry juice and a cherry-flavored isocaloric control. The abstract reports an inconclusive treatment-by-time comparison for subjective well-being (p = .874) and soreness (p = .808). Full methods and any separate fatigue item remain open. The electronic and print dates differ, and the 2019 sources disagree on the electronic day.
