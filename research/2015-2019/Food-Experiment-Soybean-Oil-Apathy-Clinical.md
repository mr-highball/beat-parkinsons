# Soybean-oil comparisons and behavioral outcomes

## Oral soybean oil and negative affect in the Pittsburgh trial

The 2017 randomized report of NCT00663871 compares fish oil with soybean oil capsules in 272 healthy midlife adults with low marine-fat intake. The 2014 methods report identifies 2 g/day soybean oil, with mint flavor and a small amount of vitamin E in the placebo capsules. The final behavioral report found no clear group-by-time interactions for negative affect or impulsivity across questionnaire and momentary ratings. The results first posted in 2021 likewise list those outcomes and do not report a separate apathy severity scale. ## Baseline apathy domains in the TEAM-AD cohort

The 2018 TEAM-AD baseline analysis included 613 participants with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer disease. Any apathy or indifference was reported in 47%. A total of 152 of 613 (25%) had a score of at least 4, including 78 of 362 with mild disease and 74 of 251 with moderate disease. The adjusted odds ratio for the higher-severity category in moderate versus mild disease was 1.48 (95% CI 1.02 to 2.16). This shows that the trial cohort had a separately recorded apathy domain at baseline. It supplies no analysis by soybean-oil assignment or later treatment visit.
