# Cooked green peas and Parkinson falls

## Cooked green pea recipes and falls in a mixed diet trial

Phillips et al. randomized 47 people with Parkinson disease to eight weeks of low-fat or ketogenic meal plans. Forty-four started and 38 completed. In the low-fat plan, printed page 82 lists tuna casserole made with frozen green peas boiled with pasta and then baked. A further low-fat pea recipe is baked beef stroganoff. The published procedures tracked completion of meals without individual food diaries. [Primary randomized trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6175383/) · [Low-fat meal plan](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC6175383.1/MDS-33-1306-s002.pdf)

One person in the low-fat arm with pre-existing postural hypotension and falls required increased midodrine for ongoing falls. This case supplies no number of falls, observation comparison or timing relative to a meal. The results do not document consumption of either pea recipe by that person.
