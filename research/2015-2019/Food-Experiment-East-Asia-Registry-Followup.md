# Japanese and Korean dietary and botanical registrations, 2015–2019

## Scallop-derived plasmalogen food

UMIN000018102 was first disclosed June 26, 2015. The open single-arm trial planned to follow 20 people with Parkinson’s disease or parkinsonian syndromes for six months, using a test food containing scallop-derived plasmalogen. The primary outcome was UPDRS, with Hoehn and Yahr stage, Schwab and England activities of daily living, and PDQ-39 as secondary measures. The completed registration gives no results. [UMIN trial record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000020950)

## Grapefruit and lavender aroma exposure

UMIN000021236, receipt R000020500, was first disclosed March 1, 2016. The double-blind randomized crossover targeted ten participants across Parkinson’s, epilepsy, and healthy volunteers. Investigators planned an immediate magnetoencephalography and subjective assessment after grapefruit essential oil, lavender essential oil, or a synthetic lavender aroma. Neither enrollment by diagnosis nor results are posted. The route and endpoints differ from consuming raw grapefruit for [0009, rest tremor](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0009.txt) and [0853, shortened stride](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0853.txt). [UMIN trial record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000020500) · [MHLW trial mirror](https://rctportal.mhlw.go.jp/detail/um?trial_id=UMIN000021236)

## Caffeine absorption challenge

UMIN000022187 was first disclosed May 6, 2016. Parkinson’s and control groups each received a single isolated caffeine dose. The target sample was 20 and the outcome was serum caffeine and metabolite concentrations for six hours. The registry marks it completed with no posted outcome and provides no symptom measure for the frozen brewed or decaffeinated coffee claims. [UMIN trial record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000025560)

## Mashiningan constipation series

In 2016, Nakae et al. reported a one-month Japanese clinical series of 23 people with Parkinson’s and constipation. Eighteen reported an increased bowel movement frequency, including 13 of 15 who had no prior laxative and five of eight who switched from an existing laxative. Three reported diarrhea. The uncontrolled before-and-after design and multicomponent formula limit attribution. The authors and design differ from the terminated [UMIN000013879 randomized crossover](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-East-Asia-Registry-Followup.md#mashiningan-and-daiokanzoto-for-constipation). [Primary report, DOI 10.3937/kampomed.67.131](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/kampomed/67/2/67_131/_article/-char/ja/)

## Rikkunshito and apathy

UMIN000030857 was first disclosed January 17, 2018. The target was 30 participants with Parkinson’s and apathy for six months of usual treatment with or without a ginger-containing Kampo formula. Appetite was primary, and motor score, fatigue, quality of life, mood, and nutritional measures were secondary. The registry marks this follow-up trial terminated and gives no outcomes. [UMIN trial record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000033932)

## Yokukansankachimpihange and sleep

CRIS first registered KCT0002869 on May 15, 2018. The protocol proposed a double-blind randomized trial of 34 people with Parkinson’s and sleep disturbance, comparing the nine-ingredient Yokukansankachimpihange preparation with placebo for 12 weeks. The primary measure was SCOPA-S, with polysomnography and other sleep, mood, and biomarker measures secondary. The formulation includes citrus peel and licorice root. The sources give the study plan and no outcome estimate. [CRIS trial record](https://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/search/detailSearch.do/15380) · [Published protocol, PMID 29953013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6039679/)

## HH368 added to acupuncture

CRIS first registered KCT0003444 on January 23, 2019. The 60-person assessor-blinded randomized pilot planned six weeks of the multicomponent HH368 formula plus acupuncture and physician consultation versus acupuncture and consultation alone. The primary endpoint was total MDS-UPDRS. The CRIS record marks the trial complete and provides no posted results. The investigators' 2019 conference abstract describes the protocol, and their 2022 article identifies the registration and reports outcomes. [CRIS trial record](https://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/search/detailSearch.do/18829) · [Investigator protocol abstract](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/effectiveness-and-safety-of-a-herbal-medication-hh368-for-clinical-symptoms-of-idiopathic-parkinsons-disease-a-randomized-controlled-pilot-trial-protocol/) · [2022 results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-WHO-ICTRP-Followup.md#modified-ukgansan-with-acupuncture)

## Lunch and intestinal levodopa infusion

UMIN000037387 was first disclosed July 16, 2019. The target was ten people with Parkinson’s receiving levodopa–carbidopa intestinal gel. Investigators planned to compare plasma levodopa with and without lunch containing 20–25 g of protein. The public status remains preinitiation, with no posted results. The planned comparison is relevant to meal and medication interactions. [UMIN trial record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000042626)

## WIN-1001X botanical extract

CRIS first registered KCT0004536 on December 11, 2019. The double-blind phase II record planned 188 people with early Parkinson’s for three doses of WIN-1001X or placebo, with MDS-UPDRS as a clinical endpoint. The separate formulation paper describes an ethanol extract of *Polygala tenuifolia*, *Angelica tenuissima*, and *Dimocarpus longan* in animal and cell studies. The registry provides no clinical outcome. [CRIS trial record](https://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/search/detailSearch.do/15370) · [ClinicalTrials.gov record, NCT04220762](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04220762) · [Primary formulation and model study](https://sejong.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/extract-of-polygala-tenuifolia-angelica-tenuissima-and-dimocarpus/)
