# Flaxseed Constipation Origins

## Solvent-extracted flaxseed oil and animal stool outcomes

Palla and Gilani tested flaxseed oil and mucilage in separate animal comparisons. The Methods describe petroleum-ether extraction of ground seeds. In groups of six mice, 24-hour wet-feces proportion was 5% (SE 4) with vehicle and 38.33% (5.5) and 54.66% (6.43) at the two lower tested oil amounts. The higher tested amount yielded 6.5% (5.12), making the response nonmonotonic. An independent castor-oil challenge in the same paper used different amounts and measured diarrhea. The study used solvent-extracted oil and animal stool proxies outside Parkinson disease. ## Baked flaxseed cookies and Rome III constipation

The original trial randomized 77 people with type 2 diabetes and constipation among baked flaxseed, psyllium or placebo cookies twice daily for 12 weeks. It describes 10 g flaxseed or psyllium mixed into cookies and reports change in Rome III symptoms favoring active cookie groups over placebo (p=.002). The author abstract identifies seed in baked cookies. A later OTC review uses the term flaxseed oil for this citation. Keep the cookie study separate from oil comparisons.
