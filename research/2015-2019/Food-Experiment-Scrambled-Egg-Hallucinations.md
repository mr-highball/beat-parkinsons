# Scrambled egg and visual hallucinations

## 2018 Meal plans and a reported visual hallucination event

In the Phillips eight-week pilot diet trial, 47 people with Parkinson disease were randomized, 44 started and 38 completed. One participant assigned to the ketogenic diet developed visual hallucinations that resolved after reducing pramipexole. The adverse-event section gives neither a date relative to meals nor a visual hallucination frequency measure. Six participants across both groups changed medication during the trial. [Primary trial, Methods and adverse effects](https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/mds.27390)

The ketogenic meal plan schedules a spinach and cheddar egg scramble in weeks two and four. The low-fat plan separately schedules scrambled egg on toast. Neither plan confirms what the participant took at a particular meal. The trial reports composite nonmotor scores and selected individual items, without an egg-specific hallucination comparison.
