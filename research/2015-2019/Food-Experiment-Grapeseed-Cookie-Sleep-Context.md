# Grapeseed cookie program and sleep registry fields

The Healthy Cookie Study, NCT02841618, first posted in 2016, describes a two-week oral grapeseed oil cookie and a safflower-oil comparator. The current record includes a secondary correlation between blood linoleic acid and an unspecified sleeping questionnaire, but has no posted results. The linked dissertation and 2022 report detail blood lipid findings, not arm-level sleep or dream-enactment outcomes. The study concerns healthy adults and does not identify refining of the supplied grapeseed oil. Its single-arm pilot and randomized phase have inconsistent pilot counts between the registry and dissertation.
