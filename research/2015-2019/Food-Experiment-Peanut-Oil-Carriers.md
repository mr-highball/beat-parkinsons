# Peanut Oil Carriers

## Arachis oil placebo in a vitamin D depression comparison

The November 22, 2018 report compared vitamin D capsules against placebo containing arachis oil. In Table 2 the mean Beck Depression Inventory-II change after four months was −1.5 (SD 4.3, n=206) in the vitamin D arm and −1.9 (SD 4.1, n=202) in the placebo arm. The adjusted between-arm result was inconclusive. Table 3 prints 208 for the overall vitamin D group where Table 2 and the main results give 206. The study supplies no oil-free comparison, oil dose or processing grade, and participants were recruited for low vitamin D outside Parkinson disease.
