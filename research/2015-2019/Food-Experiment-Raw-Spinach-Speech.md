# Raw spinach and speech

## Uncooked spinach options in Parkinson trial menus

Phillips et al. randomized 47 people with Parkinson disease to low-fat or ketogenic diets for eight weeks; 44 commenced and 38 completed. In the low-fat plan, the green smoothie blends 60 g spinach or kale with fruit and other ingredients without a cooking step. In the ketogenic plan, a salad mixes 60 g spinach or kale with mayonnaise and lemon juice without cooking. The plans give alternatives and the report describes checking meal completion, not a record of each participant's ingredient choice. Therefore actual individual raw spinach consumption is not established. [Low-fat plan, printed p. 55](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC6175383.1/MDS-33-1306-s002.pdf) · [Ketogenic plan, printed p. 80](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC6175383.1/MDS-33-1306-s003.pdf).

The trial reports MDS-UPDRS Part II and III totals, which encompass speech items, but no spinach-specific speech item, listener-rated intelligibility comparison or isolated spinach effect. One ketogenic participant needed levodopa, ropinirole and apomorphine reductions for worsening dyskinesias, with partial resolution; the event is not attributed to spinach and food intake for that person is unknown.
