# Black pepper use in long-term care

## Alternating exposure and aspiration pneumonia

Yamaguchi and colleagues included 32 of 35 long-term inpatients. Two groups exchanged pepper-use conditions after 60 days, with 120 days of observation. The abstract reports 28 patients with fever and 11 who received antibiotics for aspiration pneumonia overall. It reports 11 fever cases and two antibiotic cases during the pepper-use condition. The authors report lower antibiotic frequency and duration. The publicly available abstract gives no paired event table, preparation, route, or Parkinson subgroup. The complete paper remains subscriber-gated. [Primary Japanese abstract, February 2018](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jbes/69/1/69_13/_article/-char/ja/)
