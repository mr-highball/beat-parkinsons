# Restricted diet, fatigue and scurvy

## Fatigue and scurvy with a restricted fried chicken and pasta diet

A conference case describes worsening fatigue and low vitamin C after months of a highly restricted diet centered on fried chicken and pasta. It supplies no isolated poultry comparison or Parkinson outcomes.
