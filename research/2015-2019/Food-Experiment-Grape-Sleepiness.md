# Grape preparations and sleep outcomes, 2015–2019

## Grape juice polyphenols and polysomnographic sleep time

The 2018 NCT01977924 paper compares grape-juice polyphenols and placebo over one month in people with severe obstructive sleep apnea. Sleep time changed by −10 minutes versus +15 minutes, with reported p=.02 for the contrast. Epworth scores of 16 or above were excluded. No post-treatment daytime-sleepiness estimate was identified. Sleep duration is measured separately from daytime sleepiness. The intervention was a supplement and the participants did not have a defined Parkinson cohort. [Original report](https://doi.org/10.1093/jn/nxy005) · [Local factual summary](Source-Text/2018-Grape-Polyphenol-OSA-Sleep.txt)

## Grape juice runner crossover and pre-exercise Epworth ratings

Toscano and colleagues first published the runner crossover online in 2019. Fourteen runners completed the grape juice and matched-beverage visits. They filled out Epworth before both exhaustion tests to characterize prior sleepiness. The table gives 7.1 versus 5.8, with p=.13 between visits. The questionnaire serves as pre-exercise context, without a longitudinal clinical sleepiness estimate. Both beverage form and study population differ from the frozen grape claim.
