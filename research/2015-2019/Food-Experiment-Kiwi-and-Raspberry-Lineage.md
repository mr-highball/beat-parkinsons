# Kiwifruit and raspberry study lineages and outcomes

## Kiwi breakfast registration and planned cognitive tests

First registered November 18, 2015 after recruitment, with 20 enrolled. The registry describes a four-breakfast crossover in healthy adults and names choice reaction time, Stroop, Trail Making B, word recall and serial sevens as secondary measures at baseline, 90 and 180 minutes. The registered comparison matches the breakfast conditions and timing in the Edwards thesis. The related journal report explicitly gives this registration and reports the metabolic results. These are publications from one trial family. [Original source](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=366885) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2015-ACTRN12615001259538-Kiwi-Breakfast-Registry.txt)

## Preparation and endpoint scope in the kiwi breakfast article

October 30, 2017. Mishra, Edwards and colleagues report the four-period wheat-cereal breakfast crossover in 20 adults. Section 2.1 describes peeled green Hayward kiwifruit stored frozen, partly thawed to crush it to pulp, and frozen again in portions. The human meals used 200 g pulp in two conditions. The journal article gives glucose and appetite results, while the Edwards thesis reports the cognitive battery. The published meal and registered timing corroborate one study family. Reported fruit processing and absence of Parkinson participants limit transfer to the hypothesis about raw kiwi. [Original source](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/9/11/1195) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2017-Mishra-Kiwi-Breakfast-Frozen-Pulp-Methods.txt)

## Black raspberry powder and Barrett surveillance outcomes

July 7, 2016 online. Twenty Barrett esophagus participants completed 26 weeks of lyophilized black raspberry powder with baseline comparison. Jewel fruit were frozen and then freeze-dried before administration as a suspension. The Methods report biomarkers and clinical monitoring, without a specific anxiety outcome or a concurrent untreated group. It cites the 2006 interim report and shares investigators, intervention and timing with the 2007 conference report. Exact participant overlap has not been established. [Original source](https://www.oncotarget.com/article/10457/text/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2016-Kresty-Barrett-Black-Raspberry-Monitoring.txt)

## Caribbean parkinsonism registry and dietary survey scope

First posted December 11, 2017. The ClinicalTrials.gov record describes characterization of typical and atypical parkinsonism in the French Caribbean and Guyana, with a secondary objective concerning dietary exposures in the three regions. The currently displayed overall status is unknown, and the record has no separate posted results. The published 2022 and 2026 analyses use this study identifier. The registry does not identify a kiwi exposure or a fruit intervention. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03368300) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2017-NCT03368300-Caribbean-Parkinsonism-Registry.txt)

## Frozen red raspberry registration and metabolic outcomes

First posted June 23, 2015, as dated by ClinicalTrials.gov. The completed crossover registration evaluates plasma insulin, glucose, other metabolites, oxidative markers and inflammatory markers following meals with and without red raspberry. The public registry has no posted results and does not list an anxiety measure. The published Xiao 2019 report names this registration. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02479035) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2015-NCT02479035-Frozen-Raspberry-Metabolic-Registry.txt)

## Measured outcomes after a frozen red raspberry breakfast

First published February 14, 2019. In a randomized single-blind crossover, 21 adults with prediabetes and insulin resistance plus 11 metabolically healthy adults consumed meals with 0, 125 or 250 g whole frozen red raspberry. Half the fruit was served in a beverage and half with cereal. The study reported lower two-hour insulin area under the curve at both doses in the prediabetes group. The full methods and results address metabolic, inflammatory and oxidative outcomes. They supply no mood or anxiety measure, and the cohort did not target Parkinson disease. [Original source](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/oby.22406) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2019-Xiao-Frozen-Raspberry-Breakfast-Outcomes.txt)

## Green and gold kiwi cereal trial registration

First registered March 9, 2015. The completed crossover compared green Hayward and gold SunGold kiwifruit, cereal, mixtures of fruit and cereal, and a glucose reference. Scheduled outcomes concern glycaemic potency, glycaemic index and matched meals. The registered finger-prick blood glucose outcomes and explicit identifier link to the 2016 Mishra publication. [Original source](https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=ACTRN12615000222549) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2015-ACTRN12615000222549-Kiwi-Cereal-Glycaemia.txt)

## Measured glucose and appetite after kiwi and cereal

First published September 14, 2016. Twenty adults took part in the randomized within-person meal comparison. Two 40 g available-carbohydrate meal conditions contained 200 g green Hayward or gold SunGold kiwi with wheaten cereal. The full Methods and Results describe capillary glucose and self-rated appetite and satiety. These reported outcomes leave cognitive attention unmeasured. [Original source](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC5048185/fullTextXML) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2016-Mishra-Kiwi-Cereal-Glycaemia-Appetite.txt)

## SunGold and congee preload trial registration

First registered June 14, 2016. The registry describes meal preloads to test a blood glucose response, with insulin and other postmeal outcomes. Its visible recruitment field still says not yet recruiting. The human results appeared in the 2018 Lubransky article, which explicitly names the registry. The report supplies participant disposition independently of the stale registry field. [Original source](https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=ACTRN12616000771459) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2016-ACTRN12616000771459-Kiwi-Congee-Preload.txt)

## Measured outcomes in the SunGold congee preload crossover

First published August 17, 2018. Thirty healthy Chinese adults enrolled and 28 completed all breakfast periods, with 29 at lunch. Water, rice or ripe SunGold kiwi was given before the congee meal. Glucose incremental area under the curve was smaller with the kiwi than water preload after breakfast, but there was no clear treatment difference in incremental area under the curve after lunch. The trial also measured hormonal responses, satiety and energy intake. The report supplies no separate cognitive attention outcome. [Original source](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/10/8/1110) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2018-Lubransky-Kiwi-Congee-Glycaemia-Appetite.txt)

## Whole kiwi and prediabetes trial registration

March 6, 2018. ANZCTR registered the 12-week kiwifruit intervention in adults with prediabetes. The trial measures glycaemia and related metabolic effects and compares regular whole fruit consumption with control. The exact identifier links the 2022 Mishra results report. The registered medical context and outcome list supply no cognitive attention endpoint. [Original source](https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=374559) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2018-Mishra-Kiwi-Prediabetes-Registration.txt)
