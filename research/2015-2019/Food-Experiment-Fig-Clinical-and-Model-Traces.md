# Fig fruit exposure and symptom outcomes, 2015–2019

## Dried figs and olive oil in a scopolamine memory model

Alharthy and Bawazir studied five groups of ten male rats. Animals in relevant arms received an oral formulation made from dried figs and olive oil over four weeks in a scopolamine amnesia model. The authors report improvements on maze behavior and lower hippocampal acetylcholinesterase activity. The food appeared in a mixture, and the model and outcomes concern memory and cholinergic effects. The experiment supplies no visual hallucination outcome and cannot establish a whole dried fig effect in Parkinson disease. ## Dried fig and functional gastrointestinal outcomes

Pourmasoumi et al. randomized 150 adults with constipation-predominant irritable bowel syndrome to fig, flixweed, or a control. The national registry record NCT02559245 lists bowel symptoms and quality of life. The report described favorable bowel outcomes and no difference on C-reactive protein or abdominal pain severity. The protocol reports a fig serving before breakfast and lunch. This gastrointestinal trial gives no neurological event frequency.
