# Broad bean extract trial conference report

## Mashhad trial of fava bean extract and levodopa

Nikkhah and colleagues presented the Mashhad Parkinson’s trial at the Iranian Congress of Neurology, May 12–15, 2015. The abstract on printed page 129 reports 30 participants, 20 men and 10 women, aged 51–81. It compares fava bean (*Vicia faba*) extract with carbidopa against conventional levodopa with carbidopa. At one week the authors report no significant between-treatment differences for rigidity, chair rising, gait or postural instability. They report better rest tremor and freezing outcomes with conventional levodopa, and fewer adverse effects during the extract treatment. The abstract supplies no arm-level estimates. Investigators, site, age range, sample, treatments and results match the 2023 full report, so the two reports are counted as one trial.
