# Corn oil and the registered PSQI outcome

## Corn oil capsules and PSQI during resistance exercise

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT03986307 on June 14, 2019. The registry specifies 14 weeks of omega-3 or corn-oil control and 12 weeks of exercise in both groups, with pre/post Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index as an Other outcome. The related Dalle primary report matches institutional study identifier S61809 and the 23-person protocol. The journal and original supplement do not give PSQI results. The registry lists completed status and contains no posted results. The oil treatment comparison does not use an oil-free arm or study Parkinson insomnia.
