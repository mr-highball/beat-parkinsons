# Movement and Rehabilitation

### 2016 — Low-intensity physiotherapy and occupational therapy in PD REHAB

In a pragmatic randomized trial of 762 people with mild-to-moderate disease, a low-intensity course of physiotherapy and occupational therapy did not meaningfully improve activities of daily living at three months. The result applies to the service and intensity tested. [Primary source](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2482646)

### 2017 — High-intensity treadmill feasibility in early disease

SPARX phase 2 found that the high-intensity arm met its prespecified nonfutility threshold over six months and was feasible in its selected early-stage group. The study was designed to decide whether a definitive trial was warranted. First published online in December 2017. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29228079/)

### 2019 — Remotely supervised aerobic cycling

In Park-in-Shape, home cycling attenuated worsening of off-medication motor scores compared with home stretching at six months in participants with mild disease. Follow-up was limited and the study leaves persistence of the effect open. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31521532/)
