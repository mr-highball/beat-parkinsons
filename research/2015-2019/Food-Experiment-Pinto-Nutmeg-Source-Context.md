# Pinto bean sleep and nutmeg motor source context

## Pinta bean sprouts and plasma melatonin in rats

January 20, 2016. Aguilera and colleagues identify the seed as Phaseolus vulgaris var. Pinta. The methods describe ethanolic extraction of six-day sprouts, solvent evaporation and reconstitution before oral gavage. In the first experiment, plasma melatonin after 90 minutes was about 29 pg/mL in fasted rats receiving extract, 16% higher than the freely fed chow control and 2.9 times the fasting comparator. This is a comparison against separate groups, without a sleep or insomnia measurement. The measured exposure was a sprout extract and its identity as the same culinary bean in the frozen claim is unresolved. ## Intention tremor in an earlier powdered nutmeg case

April 4, 2017. Serrano Benavente and Pérez Ruiz report one adult with hand intention tremor after excessive powdered nutmeg with diazepam. They report ongoing fluoxetine treatment and a positive benzodiazepine test. Two authors, the hospital, patient age, symptom timing, hand tremor and course align with a 2018 society report, making shared patient identity plausible. The reported amounts differ, and the 2018 account lists no chronic treatment and a negative drug screen. The reports have no explicit linkage and do not supply an isolated nutmeg effect, a rest-tremor comparison or a Parkinson population.
