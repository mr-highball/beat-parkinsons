# Walnut-oil clinical reports and study families

The [2003 author abstract](../2000-2004/Source-Text/2003-PMID12934760-Zibaeenezhad-Walnut-Oil-Lipids-Abstract-Extract.txt) concerns lipids, with full methods still unavailable. The [2013 component trial](../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID23616506-Berryman-Walnut-Fractions-Extract.txt) supplies actual Soxhlet extraction in Methods. This corrects the preliminary assumption that its preparation was unspecified. The [2009 registration](../2005-2009/Source-Text/Trials/NCT00938340.txt) and [2018 posted results](Source-Text/Trials/NCT00938340-Results-2018.txt) belong to that same trial, with endpoint-specific denominators and multiplicity rules. They do not form independent replications or a clinical pain comparison.

The [2016 glucose report](Source-Text/2016-PMID28115966-Zibaeenezhad-Walnut-Oil-Glucose-Extract.txt) explicitly names [IRCT2014022216682N1](../2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/IRCT2014022216682N1.txt). The [2017 lipid report](Source-Text/2017-PMID28394361-Zibaeenezhad-Walnut-Oil-Lipids-Extract.txt) names [IRCT2014030611375N2](../2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/IRCT2014030611375N2.txt). Those registrations have distinct ethics approvals, designs and expected recruitment periods. Each is retrospective, actual recruitment fields are blank, and each publication conflicts with its registration's expected dates.

Very similar published baseline summaries prompted a participant-overlap concern. Correctly separating the registrations prevents silently treating them as one protocol. It also does not prove that participants were independent. Differences in sex counts, comparator, dates and dose units stay explicit. A 2019 glycemic publication names the 2016 registration and ethics approval; its relationship and discrepant age eligibility remain an open companion-report check.

The [2018 walnut map](Source-Text/2018-PMC6265811-Nutritional-Epidemiology-Culinary-Members-Extract.txt) reports three oil publications without identities. Plausible older oil papers cannot be assigned to those three slots without a verified inventory. Its category counts also leave possible overlap unresolved. The later 2021 review has different eligibility and a later search end, so it cannot authenticate the original map's members.

Metabolic and vascular endpoints remain related context. None of these human reports measures the frozen Parkinson pain or cognition claim. The separate [mouse pain and preparation bridge](Observations/Food-Experiment-Walnut-Oil-Pain.md) supports only the smallest favorable lean under this experiment's thresholds. These clinical reports neither confirm nor disprove that lean. Miller's full mood paper and preparation details remain an access trail; no repeated failed route is represented as an exhausted investigation.
