## Cold pressed walnut oil and measured fatty acid composition

Al Juhaimi's 2018 Table 3 reports walnut linoleic acid at 63.56% cold-pressed versus 62.41% Soxhlet. Its separate Linolenic row is .11% versus .57%. The approximately 12% alpha-linolenic profile in nearby prose belongs to cited earlier literature, rather than this matched experiment. The earlier local attribution to Table 3 was incorrect. These acids and source attributions remain distinct. The 2019 comparison gives linoleic acid 55.19–56.71% in cold-pressed oils and 54.47–55.93% in Soxhlet oils, alongside minor-constituent differences. This supports limited relevance to the separate mouse pain oil reporting 55% linoleic acid. It does not establish oil-lot identity, causal ingredients or clinical analgesic equivalence. It also does not identify the pressing method or separate mood scores in Miller's experiment.
