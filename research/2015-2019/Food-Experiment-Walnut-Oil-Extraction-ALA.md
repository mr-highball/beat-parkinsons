## Cold pressed walnut oil and measured fatty acid composition

Al Juhaimi and colleagues published June 7, 2018, comparing cold pressing and Soxhlet extraction of eight nut oils. Table 3 reports substantial alpha-linolenic acid in Juglans regia oil, around twelve percent, with the cold-pressed comparison directly measured. This establishes the constituent's presence in a culinary preparation and overlaps the approximate content reported for the acute walnut oil extinction experiment. It does not establish the pressing method of that experiment’s lot, constituent causality, or an anxiety outcome.
