# Fiber and polyphenol mixture trial registration

## Registered mood measures during simulated altitude

The first public posting of NCT04111263 was October 1, 2019. The crossover study registered depression/dejection within a Profile of Mood States secondary outcome, with intestinal permeability as the primary outcome. The current record reports 33 enrolled and no structured results. The later paper identifies cranberry extract as one ingredient in the intervention. ## Cranberry juice and cell-free MAO-A inhibition

Cásedas and colleagues tested freeze-dried commercial juice in a laboratory MAO-A assay. The preparation was cranberry juice, and the paper did not identify the enzyme's tissue or species source. Its prose and table give discordant IC50 values and units. The assay supplies no clinical mood outcome. The separate human adipose work used pomegranate juice.
