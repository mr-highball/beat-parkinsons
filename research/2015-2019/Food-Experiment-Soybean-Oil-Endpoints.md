# Soybean oil comparator and clinical endpoints

## Vascular aging trial preparation and eligibility

Bowman and colleagues' protocol and baseline report for NCT01953705 first published 29 March 2019. Nordic Naturals supplied a soybean-oil placebo softgel matched to the fish-oil product. The trial enrolled 102 adults aged 75 and older with cerebral white matter lesions and low omega-3 status. Section 2.3 expressly names Parkinson’s disease among the clinical exclusion examples. This paper reports baseline characteristics and describes the same sample followed in the 2024 results.

## Timed gait at baseline

For the 30-foot course, participants walked 15 feet, turned and walked back without running. The mean of three trials was recorded. In 99 participants, the blinded baseline means were 15.5 seconds in group A and 16.4 seconds in group B (p=.29, Table 2). Allocation labels and timings were reported at baseline. The 2024 report and its exploratory supplement give no follow-up gait result. The protocol and all follow-up reports describe one study.
