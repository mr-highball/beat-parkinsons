# Cranberry Depression Protocol

## COMBAT cranberry powder and planned depression questionnaire

The COMBAT protocol schedules the PHQ-9 depression questionnaire at screening and after a 12-week comparison of freeze-dried cranberry powder and matched placebo in healthy older adults. The main registry outcome list does not name PHQ-9. ClinicalTrials.gov first posted September 20, 2018 and ISRCTN registration dates to November 13, 2018. The 2022 article randomized 29 to cranberry and 31 to placebo. The body and complete supplementary data document cognition, perfusion and other measures, with no reported PHQ-9 comparison. The 2023 biochemical report explicitly names the same registry identifiers. The later thesis and journal reports confirm that participants in the baseline analysis completed PHQ-9 screening. Collection after treatment and depression estimates by randomized arm remain open.
