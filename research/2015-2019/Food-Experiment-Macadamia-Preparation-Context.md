# Macadamia oil in the named retail and meal formulations

## Dated launch record for the named retail oil

Nisshin OilliO. Manufacturer news release, January 11, 2018, page 1.

The release names Nisshin Macadamia Nut Oil in the 145 g Fresh Keep Bottle and gives the March 1, 2018 nationwide launch date. It describes oil pressed from macadamia nuts without an extraction temperature or a lot certificate. The name and container size match the Nisshin item named in patent JP2026034397A Test Example 1. The release confirms product identity and gives an extraction description without verifying cold pressing or the trial lot. ## Registered whole macadamia nut crossover

First posted January 14, 2019. This whole-macadamia nut crossover completed November 25, 2019. Its eight-week phases and 35 completers correspond closely to the subsequent bowel conference abstract and Jones cardiometabolic report. The official record has no posted results.
