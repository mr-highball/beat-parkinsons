## Injected cardamom seed extract and rodent nociception

Rahmanian, Rahmanian and Jahromi’s March 2019 author abstract reports longer tail-flick latency and diminished formalin-phase responses at some doses of intraperitoneal hydroalcoholic cardamom seed extract. The title calls the animals rats and the abstract calls them mice. Exact group data and species remain open pending the full original. This treatment is far removed from ground culinary cardamom and clinical Parkinson pain.
