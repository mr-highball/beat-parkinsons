# A controlled oral cinnamaldehyde experiment

## Cinnamaldehyde alone and with diazepam in mice

Churihar and colleagues first published December 24, 2019, in a 2020 issue. Separate cinnamaldehyde groups did not clearly change elevated-plus-maze tests versus vehicle. The abstract places p < .05 beside its no-effect sentence, while the full Results and Table 1 distinguish the standalone contrasts from significant comparisons involving diazepam. The combination's open-arm time comparison supplies a drug interaction result rather than a standalone cinnamon effect.
