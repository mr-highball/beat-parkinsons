# Turmeric Pain Models

## Injected curcumin and inflammatory nociception in mice

El Nebrisi and colleagues tested purified curcumin in mouse inflammatory-pain assays and electrophysiology in oocytes. After injected curcumin, formalin hind-paw licking declined in the later phase (p<.001) with no clear early-phase contrast. The higher tested dose also reduced writhing after a separate challenge. These behavioral findings apply to isolated injected curcumin and nonhuman nociception.
