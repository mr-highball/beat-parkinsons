# Leafy grain search replay evidence, 2015–2019

## Buckwheat product substitution and self-reported attention in gluten sensitivity

The 19-person randomized crossover tested a six-week replacement diet with buckwheat pasta, hardtacks, biscuits and flakes in non-celiac gluten sensitivity. On a self-reported attention item the control-period score fell from 4.8 ± 1.5 to 3.9 ± 0.7, reported within-period p < 0.05. The buckwheat product period yielded a nonsignificant improvement trend on the broader questionnaire. The article gives no isolated between-period attention test, formal attention-task outcome, or toasted-groat exposure.
