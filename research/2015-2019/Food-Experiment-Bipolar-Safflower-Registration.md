# Safflower and olive oils in a registered placebo combination

## Safflower and olive oils in a registered placebo combination

NCT03541031 first posted May 30, 2018. Its intervention description names a multinutrient product and fish oil against a double placebo made with olive and safflower oils. The official record has no structured results. The published report explicitly gives this trial identifier. Safflower's own effect and pressing grade are unmeasured.
