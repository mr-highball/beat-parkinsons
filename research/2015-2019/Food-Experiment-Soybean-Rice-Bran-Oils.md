# Food experiment evidence for soybean and rice bran oil

## Extracted rice bran oil and haloperidol motor outcomes

Samad’s paper first appeared January 2, 2015. Sixteen male Wistar rats were divided among water or haloperidol crossed with water or orally administered rice bran oil for five weeks. The oil was extracted using Hu et al.’s solvent-extraction reference. Its grade, the solvent chosen from that reference and a product constituent assay are unspecified. Oil reduced haloperidol-associated vacuous chewing and rotarod impairment. Open-field and home-cage activity showed no clear pairwise differences. The report studies a neuroleptic model of tardive dyskinesia and coordination, with no measured dystonia, fall rate or Parkinson gait. [Primary full report, Figures 1–4](https://www.fda.gov.tw/tc/includes/GetFile.ashx?id=f636694852860381089) · [Publication date and index](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28911693/) · [Dated original scope](Source-Text/2015-PMID28911693-Samad-Rice-Bran-Oil-Motor-Outcome-Scope.txt)

The 2016 serotonin report describes the same author, species, oral routes, doses, schedule and temporal coordination pattern, and adds striatal serotonin and a pharmacological behavioral challenge. The 2017 dopamine/oxidative markers report again uses the same schedule and chewing endpoint and gives a different neurochemical panel. Cohort independence is unverified. These reports cannot be counted as independent confirmation without sample reconciliation. ## Refined soybean oil placebo in an exploratory sleep patent

A patent family first published October 1, 2015 describes a crossover in 16 adults in their 20s to 40s reporting poor sleep. Its Test Example 2 compares phosphatidylserine with a refined soybean oil soft-gel placebo for five days each, followed by a phosphatidylserine plus fermented milk period. Mean night awakenings were 0.9, 0.8 and 0.6 respectively, and sleep latency was 24.2, 22.0 and 19.5 minutes. The first two periods did not differ clearly. The separate Test Example 1 measures PSQI daytime dysfunction with a fermented milk and a different placebo. The soybean oil comparison reports no daytime sleepiness outcome or Parkinson sample. ## Garcinia leaf extraction into rice bran oil

Sae-Lim and colleagues’ March 6, 2019 study compared edible oils as solvents for *Garcinia cowa* leaves. The selected rice bran oil preparation carried added chamuangone and was evaluated in cultured cells for cytotoxicity. This method is cited by the 2024 rat study. It identifies a specific enriched formulation but gives no matching raw or refined lot for the later experiment and no Parkinson symptom data.
