# Cucumber and postural instability

## 2018 Raw cucumber option in low-fat Parkinson trial menu

Phillips et al., 2018, PMID 30098269, PMCID PMC6175383, DOI 10.1002/mds.27390, randomized 47 people with Parkinson disease to low-fat or ketogenic diet plans for eight weeks. The low-fat plan Supplement S2 printed page 64 offers salmon salad with chopped cucumber, uncooked in the preparation, or courgette as a substitute. The diet is a mixed meal plan, and participants' individual choices and cucumber amounts were not published. The study assessed MDS-UPDRS III but reported the total motor score, not a cucumber-specific postural-instability item. No isolated cucumber assignment, postural comparison, or claim direction can be inferred. The protocol monitored completion of planned meals, not food diaries.
