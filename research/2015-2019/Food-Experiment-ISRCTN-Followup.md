# ISRCTN dietary registrations and outcomes, 2015–2019

## Instant coffee and attention meeting results

Sharma and colleagues presented the DE-CAFF study at the May 2016 Association of British Neurologists meeting. The linked abstract describes a blinded crossover in 30 healthy older adults and six people with dementia with Lewy bodies. Caffeinated drink improved choice reaction time and Stroop task performance in healthy older adults, while digit span decreased and the attentional blink was delayed. The abstract reported no clear effect yet in the dementia group. It provided no result for Parkinson’s disease dementia or fatigue severity. The later Parkinson’s crossover by the same research group used dissolved caffeine tablets in instant decaf and measured acute attention. Its matching clinical question and different methods make it a related report with uncertain overlap. [2016 meeting abstract](https://doi.org/10.1136/jnnp-2016-315106.64) · [2014 registration](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#instant-coffee-and-attention) · [2022 Parkinson’s crossover](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8799544/)

## Ketone ester and exercise endurance

ISRCTN16599164 was registered December 7, 2018. It records an acute randomized crossover in people with early Parkinson’s using a ketone ester and carbohydrate drink versus a taste-matched, isocaloric carbohydrate drink before cycle-ergometer testing. The final paper assessed time at a fixed cycling cadence. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN16599164) · [2020 results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#ketone-ester-and-exercise-endurance-results)

## Ketone ester and cerebral ATP

ISRCTN10531043 was registered December 7, 2018. The Oxford study used a before–after comparison of cerebral ATP by phosphorus magnetic resonance spectroscopy in early Parkinson’s following an acute ketone ester drink. The registry records 10 enrolled and its basic report lists 11 completing. The primary measures were biomarkers. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN10531043) · [2021 basic results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#ketone-ester-and-cerebral-atp-results)

## Ketone ester and overall symptom severity

ISRCTN64294760 was registered December 31, 2018. The Oxford protocol planned 20 participants and a randomized four-week comparison of a ketone ester drink with taste-matched placebo. It specified off-medication MDS-UPDRS, gait and activity monitoring, and a fatigue scale. The register links the protocol and an ethics summary. Searches by identifier and title found an exercise study with its own registration, ISRCTN16599164. Symptom results for ISRCTN64294760 remain unverified. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN64294760) · [Health Research Authority summary](https://www.hra.nhs.uk/planning-and-improving-research/application-summaries/research-summaries/a-ketone-drink-to-alleviate-the-symptoms-of-pd-117/)

## Primary care nutrition support for older adults

ISRCTN76863664 was registered September 25, 2017. The mixed-population feasibility study compared nurse-led nutrition care, the same care with oral nutrition supplements as needed, and usual care. Parkinson’s was among the qualifying nutritional risks. ISRCTN12415161, registered September 6, 2019, is the subsequent STREAM trial, which also lists Parkinson’s among several possible qualifying conditions and compares a care package with usual care. The interventions depend on participants’ nutritional assessments. [Feasibility registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN76863664) · [STREAM registration](https://www.isrctn.com/api/trial/ISRCTN12415161/format/default) · [Feasibility results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#nutrition-support-feasibility-results)

## Ghana Mucuna trial interim report

The October 2018 Movement Disorder Society late-breaking abstract LBA 16 identified PACTR201611001882367, the Ghana trial cited by the later Tanzanian TraPCAf protocol. At the interim report, 26 people had been randomized to *Mucuna pruriens* powder or levodopa plus benserazide. Investigators described improvement in postural instability and dysphagia in selected powder recipients and shortening of ON time in some patients. These interim observations lack comparative estimates. The primary registry and November 2026 WHO ICTRP export give November 21, 2016 as the registration date. [Primary registration](https://pactr.samrc.ac.za/TrialDisplay.aspx?TrialID=1882) · [2018 conference abstract](https://www.mdscongress.org/Congress-2018-Files/Abstracts/Late-BreakingAbstracts.pdf#page=24) · [Final results](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#ghana-mucuna-trial-final-results)
