# Summer savory preparation and endpoint search

## Summer savory sprout volatiles during germination

Jarosz and Szumny sampled germinating Satureja hortensis seeds across ten days and identified headspace volatiles using solid-phase microextraction and gas chromatography–mass spectrometry. Relative p-cymene, gamma-terpinene, thymoquinone and other compounds changed across sampling days. The endpoint describes sprout chemistry. The experiment gives no human sensory performance or dried culinary dose effect. [Primary Polish Pharmaceutical Society report, Methods and Table 1](https://www.ptfarm.pl/download/?file=File/Acta_Poloniae/2017/2/706.pdf) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2017-Jarosz-Summer-Savory-Sprout-Volatiles.txt)

## Forelimb asymmetry after isolated carvacrol in mice

Dati and colleagues reported that isolated carvacrol reduced the asymmetry of forelimb use in a cylinder test after a unilateral 6-hydroxydopamine lesion in mice. The accessible author abstract gives the direction without numeric group estimates; the publisher full article was not available during this pass. The test measures limb-use asymmetry during rearing. Handwriting kinematics, fine pen control and fresh summer savory ingestion are outside the reported measurements. [Primary indexed abstract and university deposited publication record](https://pure.au.dk/portal/da/publications/carvacrol-promotes-neuroprotection-in-the-mouse-hemiparkinsonian-/) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2017-Dati-Carvacrol-Forelimb-Cylinder.txt)

## Two dose contrasts for forepaw use in the Lins thesis

Lins’s 2017 thesis compared two doses of isolated intraperitoneal carvacrol around a unilateral 6-OHDA lesion in Long–Evans rats. In Experiment II, the authors counted contralateral forepaw contacts with the cylinder wall. The lower-dose group differed unclearly from sham (p=.211), while the higher-dose group and lesion vehicle group each showed lower use than sham (both p<.001). The report gives an overall group contrast and sham comparisons for this measure, with no significant treatment-versus-lesion-vehicle comparison reported. This experiment is separate from the thesis’s reserpine oral-movement experiment already represented in the catalog. Forepaw contact choice does not measure handwriting size. [University thesis, Experiment II, Methods and Figure 15](https://ri.ufs.br/bitstream/riufs/3648/1/LIVIA_CRISTINA_R_F_LINS.pdf) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2017-Lins-Carvacrol-Experiment-II-Forepaw-Use.txt)

## Hoarseness recipe in a Portuguese manuscript transcription

Pinto’s transcription on thesis page 115, manuscript folio 160v lines 22–24, records a remedy intended for hoarseness using sage, pennyroyal and an herb transcribed as ssegurelha in vinegar. Pinto’s editorial Table 2, page 178, interprets segurelha as *Satureja hortensis* using a modern botanical reference. The historical lines leave species, part and processing state unresolved, and do not record a patient response. The university records a 2017 examination and repository availability on August 21, 2017. This dates the verified public transcription. The manuscript’s age is a separate question.
