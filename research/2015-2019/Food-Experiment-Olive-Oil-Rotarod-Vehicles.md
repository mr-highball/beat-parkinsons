# Olive Oil Rotarod Vehicles

## Olive oil in a spirulina and amantadine rotarod experiment

Chattopadhyaya and colleagues suspended a freeze-dried spirulina extract in olive oil for oral gavage in 6-OHDA-lesioned rats. The lesion comparison used the vehicle, and other arms evaluated spirulina or amantadine. The reported motor comparisons include time before falling on a rotarod. The treatment contrasts and motor task do not isolate a culinary olive oil effect or report clinical postural instability. The article does not establish the oil processing grade. ## Olive oil comparator in the A53T rotarod study

Zhou and colleagues studied a tetramethylpyrazine analogue in an A53T alpha-synuclein mouse model. Wild-type or transgenic mice received T-006 or olive oil vehicle for four weeks. Rotarod retention was tested in ten mice per group and improved with T-006 in the transgenic comparison. This design measures the tested compound with an oil vehicle and does not isolate olive oil or measure a human postural-instability outcome. ## Olive oil vehicle in a 6-OHDA mouse rotarod experiment

Zhou and colleagues assigned four groups of 15 mice. Sham and lesion comparison animals received plain olive oil by gavage. The treated lesion group received the tetramethylpyrazine analogue T-006 dissolved in olive oil. Treated mice stayed on the rotating rod longer and made fewer induced rotations than the lesion group. The oil was present in both decisive arms and no cold-pressed grade was reported. The introductory mention of clinical postural instability is distinct from the tested rodent measures.
