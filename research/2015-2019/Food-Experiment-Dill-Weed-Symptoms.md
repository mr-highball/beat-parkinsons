# Dill weed symptom evidence

## Isolated intestinal contractions with a dill methanol extract

In isolated rat ileum in an organ bath, a methanol extract of grocery dill's dried aerial parts inhibited spontaneous and acetylcholine-induced contractions. This is an assay of gastrointestinal smooth muscle, without oral exposure, central motor function or a neurological dystonia assessment. [Gočmanac-Ignjatović full article](https://publisher.medfak.ni.ac.rs/AMM_1/amm-stari/2015-html/2015%202-broj/Celi%20radovi%20pdf%202015-2/Marija%20Gocmanac.pdf).

## Dill seed and comparator fatigue in dysmenorrhea

Thirty-one participants with dysmenorrhea were randomized to ginger, dill seed or cumin. The abstract attributes relief of pain to dill seed and attributes a group of systemic symptom reductions including fatigue to cumin. It does not report a fatigue estimate for the dill seed arm. The exposure was seed in menstrual cycles, without culinary dill foliage or Parkinson disease. [Indexed dysmenorrhea author abstract](https://europepmc.org/article/MED/31383440).
