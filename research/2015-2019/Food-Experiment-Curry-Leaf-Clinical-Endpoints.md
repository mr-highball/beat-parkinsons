# Curry leaf preparation and measured outcomes

## Hydroalcoholic curry leaf extract and slower rat transit

First available December 2, 2015. This rat study compared oral hydroalcoholic curry-leaf extract against vehicle and an injected atropine control. The authors report reduced stool frequency, greater stool consistency and slower small-intestinal transit. The original Methods describe collecting and grinding leaves, and the extraction sentence calls the powder bark. The treatment groups and Results identify it as leaf extract. The plant-part wording remains unresolved. Figure 3 measures charcoal progression after a castor-oil challenge, a different model from the earlier mouse tests. No gastric emptying, oral levodopa concentrations or Parkinson OFF-time measure was collected. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26631977/) · [Local text extract](Source-Text/2015-Ramasamy-Curry-Leaf-Slower-Rat-Transit-Abstract-Extract.txt)

## Human crossover of a three-botanical decoction

The study gave a decoction of curry leaf, pomegranate and turmeric powders or placebo in a blinded crossover among 32 people with diarrhea-predominant irritable bowel syndrome. The estimated severity difference was 24.10 points (95% CI −17.12 to 65.32, p = .26). The authors report roughly one third with at least one minor event considered potentially related. The indexed report gives no clinical Parkinson outcomes. The public publisher body could not be obtained. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27261998/) · [Local text extract](Source-Text/2016-Lauche-Curry-Leaf-Mixture-Crossover-Extract.txt)

## Steamed idli made with ground curry leaves

Dried ground leaves were mixed into rice and black-gram batter that was later steamed. Table 2 reports iron of 36.8 ± 0.13 mg per 100 g in the curry leaf product and 6.12 ± 0.2 mg per 100 g in control idli. The sensory panel rated mouthfeel, flavor and texture, and instruments measured firmness. Methods describe a five-point scale while Table 3 contains scores above five. The study offers a prepared food and composition measurement but no measured swallowing impairment, absorbed iron response or levodopa pharmacokinetics. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4951439/) · [Local text extract](Source-Text/2016-Chelliah-Steamed-Curry-Leaf-Idli-Extract.txt)

## Published curry leaf combination crossover in diarrhea-predominant IBS

The 2016 primary paper reports the crossover comparing curry leaf, pomegranate and turmeric as a mixture with placebo. Thirty-two were randomized, and 11 withdrew. The reported difference for overall irritable bowel severity was inconclusive.
