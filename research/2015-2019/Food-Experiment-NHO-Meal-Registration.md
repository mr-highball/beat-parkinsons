# Meal timing and continuous intestinal levodopa

## Meal and intestinal levodopa registration

The 2019 Ehime University registration planned an afternoon comparison with and without lunch during levodopa-carbidopa intestinal gel infusion in ten people with Parkinson’s. The planned meal contained 20–25 g protein. The record gives no named foods. The 2021 paper reports the same comparison in ten participants.
