# Corn oil and Parkinson’s symptom follow-up

## Corn oil vehicle in PCB gait and balance experiments

Colter and colleagues exposed pregnant mice to polychlorinated biphenyls in corn oil or the vehicle alone and examined offspring by genotype. PCB-treated wild-type animals had the shortest strides in freely moving gait analysis, with a genotype-by-treatment interaction (p<.01). Challenging-beam slips relative to the same-genotype vehicle controls increased in one genotype and decreased in another. The gait and balance comparisons therefore test PCB exposure on a shared corn-oil background. Slips on a beam and falls in daily life have different definitions. This record retains the measured gait and balance endpoints for claims 0324 and 0546. [Primary report, Figures 4 and 7](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5857246/)

## Japanese oral corn oil mental fatigue trial registration

The completed 2019 UMIN study enrolled 60 adults. The randomized comparison assigned EPA and DHA or six corn oil comparator capsules per day for 12 weeks and specified fatigue ratings around a mental workload and on awakening. The registry lists the results as unpublished and does not report an effect estimate. The administering protocol targeted generally healthy adults. The exposure arm and prospective measures leave a results-retrieval lead for Parkinson fatigue claim 0694.
