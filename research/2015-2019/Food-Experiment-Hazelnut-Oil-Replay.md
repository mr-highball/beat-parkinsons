# Hazelnut oil preparation and movement-related research

## Roasted kernel oils in cultured skeletal muscle cells

Terruzzi and colleagues separated Ordu and Tonda Gentile hazelnut kernel oils from oils made from their cuticles. The kernels were roasted first, then ground and hydraulically pressed. Oil left the press near 41 °C. Cuticle oils used supercritical carbon dioxide at 50 °C. The researchers added each oil to cultured mouse C2C12 muscle cells, with untreated medium and commercial olive oil comparisons. Figure 5 shows higher end-of-differentiation myosin heavy chain for Tonda kernel oil. The corresponding levels were similar in untreated cells and the Ordu kernel and both cuticle treatments. Section 3.3 and Figure 7 distinguish increased myotube diameter after Ordu kernel oil from increased length after both cuticle oils. The findings measure growth and differentiation in culture. They provide no direct muscle tone, contractile or Parkinson rigidity outcome and do not test oral intake.
