# Black lentils in a rectal Parkinson intervention

## Black lentils in a rectal Parkinson intervention

The August 11, 2017 conference supplement reports 15 participants with idiopathic Parkinson's. Eight received a rectal sesame-oil preparation containing black lentils, Withania and Mucuna, and seven received sesame oil alone over 16 days. The authors report within-arm improvement in total MDS-UPDRS (p<.002) and its motor activities component (p<.01), with reported tremor and spontaneity changes. The abstract includes no rigidity-specific score or between-group treatment estimate. The 2024 report repeats group sizes, ingredients and results, so it is treated as the same cohort. The contrast differs in route, preparation and cointerventions from boiled lentils. ## Botanical identity of black lentils

The abstract does not provide a botanical binomial or voucher for black lentils. A separate 2015 pharmacognostic report names Masha in its Mashadi Taila as Phaseolus radiatus. Another published Ayurvedic intervention identifies Masha as Phaseolus mungo, commonly called black gram. Those reports concern other formulations and leave the Pathak batch and membership in Lens culinaris unverified.
