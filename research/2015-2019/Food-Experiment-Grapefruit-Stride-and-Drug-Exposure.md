# Grapefruit fragrance and gait terms in a physiology conference

## Grapefruit fragrance and blood pressure

Poster 1P-546 by Kawai and colleagues studied inhaled grapefruit essential oil in 18 healthy participants. Eight had a family history of hypertension and ten did not. Diastolic pressure rose from baseline during inhalation in the first group, while the presentation reports no change in the other group. It measured cardiovascular variables with participants supine. The stride-length measurements in poster 1P-306 belonged to a separate rat ankle osteoarthritis study using intra-articular hyaluronic acid. [Original meeting PDF, poster 1P-546 on S187 and poster 1P-306](https://jps.biomedcentral.com/track/pdf/10.1007/s12576-019-00673-w.pdf)
