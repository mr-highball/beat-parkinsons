# A cinnamon-containing mixture registered for endometriosis fatigue

## Gynoclear trial registration and linked protocol

ANZCTR prospectively registered ACTRN12619000807156 on 3 June 2019. The current record, last updated 9 October 2023, reports completion with 93 participants and last data collection on 28 August 2023. The placebo-controlled trial enrolled women aged 18–45 with surgically diagnosed endometriosis; no BMI criterion was found in the official record. It specified six oral Gynoclear capsules daily for 12 weeks, each containing 92 mg cinnamon along with five other botanical ingredients. The 2021 protocol identifies the cinnamon as Cinnamomum cassia. Neither source establishes an isolated culinary ground-cinnamon contrast.

The primary endpoint was pelvic pain. Secondary fatigue was specified using an endometriosis daily diary and Fatigue Severity Scale. The linked protocol describes daily 0–10 fatigue ratings and a nine-item FSS. Its abstract gives a two-month treatment period, while the current registry specifies 12 weeks. The registry lists the 2021 protocol but no numeric fatigue results; an outcome-publication search did not identify an authenticated matched report. It is one registered trial family, not evidence of a measured effect of cinnamon or in Parkinson disease.
