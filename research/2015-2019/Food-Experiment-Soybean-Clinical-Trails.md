# Soybean oil and clinical comparators

## Registered soybean oil comparator in the Tabriz Parkinson trial

The Iranian Registry of Clinical Trials lists IRCT20100123003140N21 as retrospectively registered on 5 May 2018. It records ethics approval IR.TBZMED.REC.1396.1244 and the Imam Reza Hospital neurology clinic in Tabriz as the recruiting site. The planned sample was 44 people with Parkinson's disease, with 22 per arm. The study specified simple randomization and described blinding of participants, treating clinicians and investigators. Its placebo arm was two soybean-oil capsules daily supplied by Zahravi Pharmaceutical Company. The registry does not give the oil mass, grade or specific lot.

Primary registered outcomes were IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α, IL-10, triglycerides, HDL cholesterol and LDL cholesterol. Physical activity was planned at baseline only, using the Rapid Assessment of Physical Activity questionnaire. The protocol also listed diet, anthropometry, BMI and baseline albumin. The registry marks recruitment complete. Actual dates, arm completion counts and results are absent. Recruitment dates of 18 April through 19 June 2017 were estimates.

The author, sample size, outcome set and soybean-oil comparator connect this registration to the 2023 Mohammadi paper. The registry gives 180 mg EPA and 120 mg DHA per experimental capsule. The paper abstract reverses those amounts. Both sources report the same combined 300 mg per capsule. The registry names the recruiting clinic and the paper gives a Valiasr Hospital author affiliation. These fields record different roles and the site's actual recruitment still needs primary confirmation.
