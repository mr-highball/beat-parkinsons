# Bell pepper source follow-through

## Oral Capsicum extract and rearing in rotenone-exposed rats

Ogunruku et al. first published this rat study on August 1, 2019. In their rotenone model, the cylinder test measured the number of rears in five minutes. The paper reports higher rearing after two higher doses of oral Capsicum annuum fruit extract and improvement with extract plus levodopa (reported p<.05). The cultivar is unspecified and its extract contained capsaicin and dihydrocapsaicin. Figure 1 shows rearing counts over five days. Rearing and its model do not provide human walking speed, and the exposure was fruit extract rather than a culinary preparation. [Primary paper, section 3.1 and Figure 1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214750018307698/pdf)

## Tianjin conference report of sweet peppers and sarcopenia

Xuena Wang and colleagues reported 2,537 older adults from the Tianjin Chronic Low-grade Systemic Inflammation and Health cohort in the proceedings of the 14th China Nutrition Science Congress and 11th Asia Pacific Conference on Clinical Nutrition, held September 19–22, 2019 in Nanjing. Sarcopenia prevalence was 15.53%. At ≥2–3 chili intakes per week versus less than weekly, the adjusted odds ratio was 0.60 (95% CI 0.39–0.89, trend p<.01). The authors reported a similar direction for sweet pepper without statistical significance and did not publish the sweet pepper odds ratios or separate gait or handgrip associations. The contents list the abstract at page 705 and the body spans printed pages 655–656. [Primary conference proceedings](https://static.medmeeting.org/Upload/user/500041/file/20190925/20190925105026_5555.pdf) · [Conference organizer](https://en.cnsoc.org/pEvents/421901206.html)

The March 2021 journal report has overlapping authors, the same named cohort and matching broad methods. It analyzes 2,451 participants and reports a significant sweet pepper association using almost-never intake as the reference. Participant-level overlap and the reason for the different analysis samples are unverified. Use the two publications as related reports within the same analysis family. [2021 report](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Bell-Pepper-Replay.md#sweet-peppers-and-older-adult-sarcopenia-in-the-tianjin-journal-report)

## Capsinoid crossover preprint and muscle recovery

Taso and colleagues reported in their July 23, 2018 preprint a nine-person crossover comparing 12 mg of capsinoids with placebo after cycling. Both conditions used the same high-carbohydrate meal. The respiratory exchange ratio was lower at 60, 120 and 180 minutes with capsinoids, and glycogen restoration was inconclusive. The report gives no subjective fatigue estimate. [Original source](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/201807.0411) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2018-Taso-Capsinoid-Recovery-Preprint-Extract.txt)

## Capsinoid cycling trial registration

ISRCTN13356971 was registered August 7, 2019. It lists enrollment and completion in 2016–2017 despite the prospective label. Nine men received capsinoids and placebo in a cycling crossover with metabolic outcomes. The matching 2018 preprint preceded registration.
