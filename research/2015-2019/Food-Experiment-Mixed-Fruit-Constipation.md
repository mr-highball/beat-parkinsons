# Registered mixed fruit trial in functional constipation

## FruGut symptom and stool outcomes

The official NCT04086134 record was first posted on September 11, 2019 and gives an October 25, 2019 start date. It specifies stool weight as the primary outcome and gastrointestinal symptom assessments including GSRS and PAC-SYM. The current registry has no posted results. A doctoral thesis abstract and later original congress reports describe results from the same trial.
