# Extra virgin olive oil and lycopene versus water for dry mouth

## Extra virgin olive oil and lycopene versus water for dry mouth

A water-controlled trial measured swallowing complaints after an extra-virgin olive oil and lycopene oral spray. The reported item comparison was inconclusive. The higher initial symptoms on active and the combined product limit interpretation of the larger raw decline.
