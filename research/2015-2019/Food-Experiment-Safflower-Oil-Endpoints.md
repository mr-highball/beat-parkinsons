# Safflower oil and neurologic outcomes in 2015–2019

## Safflower supplementation and open-field behavior in rats

Sousa’s 2015 UFCG undergraduate thesis compared adult male Wistar rats given standard feed and safflower oil or water, with sedentary and treadmill-trained groups. Open-field crossings averaged 74.3 (SEM 5.1) with sedentary water, 60.5 (7.4) with sedentary safflower, 102.3 (10.2) with exercised water and 76.3 (14.5) with exercised safflower. The report found higher crossings with exercise plus water than with either sedentary group. Rearing was also higher with exercise plus water than in the other groups. Sedentary safflower showed more grooming than sedentary water and more elevated-plus-maze open-arm entries. The authors interpreted individual assays in different anxiety directions. Fecal boli showed no statistically significant group difference. Oil extraction grade is unspecified. Open-field activity, grooming and maze exploration speak to movement and stress in healthy animals. Apathy, Parkinsonian bradykinesia and constipation require different measures.

[Sousa, UFCG thesis](https://dspace.sti.ufcg.edu.br/handle/riufcg/9155)

## Safflower comparator in older adult resistance training

Da Boit et al. published the trial online on 16 November 2016 before its 2017 issue. Older adults undertook lower-limb resistance training for 18 weeks alongside oral capsules containing 3 g/day safflower oil or fish oil. Table 3 labels the safflower-placebo means for the four-metre walk as 2.22 to 1.99 seconds in men and 2.43 to 2.25 seconds in women. Corresponding fish oil means were 2.12 to 1.87 seconds and 2.36 to 2.20 seconds. The authors reported no group, sex or interaction effects for walk or chair-rise time. All participants held all three standing positions for the full ten seconds, so the study observed a balance ceiling. The sample-size labels on Table 3 (23 placebo and 27 fish) differ from the abstract intervention counts (27 placebo and 23 fish). Values above follow the Table 3 labels. Both groups exercised. The study leaves the oil-alone effect, cold-press grade and Parkinson outcomes unresolved.

[Da Boit et al., article](https://doi.org/10.3945/ajcn.116.140780) · [NCT02843009](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02843009)

## Friedreich ataxia RT001 registration

The phase 2/3 Friedreich ataxia trial first posted on 25 September 2019. The placebo was encapsulated USP safflower oil, with processing grade unspecified. The study tested a synthetic deuterated linoleic acid ester against safflower oil in inherited ataxia. The [2022 results](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Oil-Endpoints.md#friedreich-ataxia-safflower-walking-and-falls-results) give arm-level walking and fall data.
