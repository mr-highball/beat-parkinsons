# Bowel outcomes and selected motor or nonmotor follow-up

## Corn oil capsules and constipation adverse events

First published May 20, 2015. Six-month randomized older-adult trial of fish oil versus four corn-oil capsules daily. Investigator-classified possibly or definitely related constipation occurred in 1 of 18 corn-oil starters and 2 of 35 fish-oil starters. Diarrhea occurred in 2 and 4, respectively. These are the safety denominators, distinct from randomized and efficacy populations. The study does not specify corn-oil refining, test an oil-free comparator or measure Parkinson constipation severity. ## Corn and coconut oil crossover and bowel events

First published September 10, 2018. An independent crossover used breads and muffins delivering about 54 g of corn or coconut oil per day in four-week periods. One listed corn-period constipation event was deemed possibly, probably or definitely related to treatment. The coconut related-event list lacks constipation, while total arm-specific constipation counts are unavailable. The 7 of 24 and 8 of 25 counts describe any treatment-emergent event, respectively. The original reports no refining grade or Parkinson constipation rating. [Original randomized report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6168703/) · [Local text](Source-Text/PMC6168703.txt)

## Corn and coconut oil crossover registration

First posted June 28, 2017. Completed randomized corn versus coconut oil lipid and metabolic crossover. No registry results are posted. The separate 2018 primary report has selected adverse events. Registration and publication refer to the same participants. [Official ClinicalTrials.gov record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03202654) · [Local text](Source-Text/2017-NCT03202654-Corn-Coconut-Bowel-Scope.txt)

## Water dispersion and eight-hour Parkinson OFF time

First published January 17, 2019. The open fixed-order study of 18 participants compared usual tablets with oral dosing from crushed tablets in neutral-pH water every 5–10 minutes. The publication reports 43% less time OFF in an eight-hour assessment window. The patent clinical example likewise gives 2.20 versus 1.26 hours OFF. Its generic oil list includes soybean, while the actual clinical dispersion used water. The EU results link the paper and document that water preparation.
