# Raspberry clinical source history

## Red raspberry juice and bone outcomes in postmenopausal women

The 2018 author abstract reports a six-month randomized comparison in 57 postmenopausal women with osteopenia. Thirty were assigned two ounces of red raspberry juice concentrate mixed with water, and 27 received a matched placebo. The report examines bone biochemical markers and density, with fruit species, cultivar and mood or anxiety effects unreported. A possible earlier thesis under a matching title is an open publication history lead. The result does not substantiate the separate 16-week mixed berry and mood claim. ## Black raspberry powder hematology trial registration

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT03140280 on May 4, 2017. It lists an open-label, single-group intervention for adults with myelodysplastic syndrome or overlapping myelodysplastic and myeloproliferative disease. The oral food preparation was freeze-dried black raspberry powder in water, with 25 g per dose twice daily and a 12-week initial treatment period. The protocol and outcome posting do not identify a controlled anxiety measure. The 2022 clinical report and posted results refer to this same registration.
