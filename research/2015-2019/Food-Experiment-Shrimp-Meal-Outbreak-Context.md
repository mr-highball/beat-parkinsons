# Shrimp and verified meal context

## Cooked shrimp in an Arizona gastrointestinal outbreak

The 2018 publication describes a 2016 Salmonella outbreak. A restaurant case-control analysis included 21 confirmed cases and 31 well dining companions. The reported association for unfried cooked shrimp and gastrointestinal illness was OR 6.7 (95% CI 1.8 to 24.9). The identified preparation category includes boiled and sautéed examples. Samples from raw shrimp, halibut and a freezer handle shared the outbreak strain, so the investigation considered multiple sources and cross-contamination. Visual hallucinations and Parkinson status were outside the reported outcome assessment. [Venkat et al. original public health outbreak investigation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6309180/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2018-Venkat-Cooked-Shrimp-Outbreak-Extract.txt)

## Grilled shrimp in a vignette within the 2015 SGIM proceedings

A vignette by Metcalf and Dubuske reports lip swelling after a mixed meal containing crab dip and grilled shrimp in a 42-year-old. The abstract reports a negative shellfish skin-prick test and discusses later thyroid and immune findings. It records a meal history and leaves food-specific causation unresolved. A separate vignette in the same collection describes visual hallucinations in an older patient with a different presentation and no shrimp exposure. The grilled-shrimp vignette reports neither Parkinson disease nor hallucinations.
