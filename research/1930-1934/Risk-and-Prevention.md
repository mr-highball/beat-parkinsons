# Risk and prevention

### 1933 — Trauma as a proposed contributor to paralysis agitans

A contemporary JAMA discussion cited French and German case series in which clinicians attributed some cases to preceding trauma. The reports are a record of an early hypothesis. Retrospective attribution and the possibility that an injury led to recognition of already present symptoms limit causal interpretation. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1933.02740200036013)
