British Medical Journal Supplement, 2 June 1934, vol 1, issue 3830, pp. 273–274, South Wales and Monmouthshire Branch. Visual review: right column of p.273 and continuation top left of p.274. J. T. Rice Edwards described 50 consecutive hemorrhoid injection cases and 45 replies: 25 complete and 20 partial cures. Two methods were discussed: direct injection using carbolic acid in glycerin and indirect injection around the area with 5% carbolic acid in almond oil or another solution. Outcomes are not separated by injected formulation. A face-related plastic-surgery presentation occurs separately in the left column of p.273. No oral almond oil, Parkinson, measured voice intensity or facial expression. Eight scanned pages were retrieved and passed native OCR; only relevant printed pp.273–274 visually checked.
