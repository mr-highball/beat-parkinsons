Title: Behandeling van Scheurbuik met lever
Author: J. A. Slot
Source: https://archive.nsgh.nl/gtni-deel-lxx-1930
Publication date: 1930
Date basis: Geneeskundig Tijdschrift voor Nederlandsch-Indië volume LXX. The article footnote gives a received date of August 26, 1929.
Text coverage: Distilled from the original article. Title on physical PDF page 148 and relevant case and discussion on physical page 149, printed page 141.

In an article primarily about treating scurvy with liver, Slot recounts Sonius's earlier recommendation of strawberries for sprue. Slot and another physician observed a postpartum patient with persistent diarrhoea and other findings they interpreted as sprue. The treatment course involved fruit and an attempted liver extract that the patient did not tolerate. The author reported recovery during the nutritional treatment without documenting strawberries as the administered fruit. The case cannot isolate a particular fruit or establish its mechanism. No Parkinson disease or fall-frequency outcome was reported.
