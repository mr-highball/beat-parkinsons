# Drugs and remedies

### 1930 — Hyoscine, hiamine and stramonium in chronic encephalitis

A British Medical Journal clinical address reported partial symptomatic relief with injected hyoscine for chronic encephalitis with a parkinsonian syndrome, with less perceived benefit by mouth and practical difficulty arranging repeated injections outside institutions. The author described hiamine results as unsatisfactory and dried stramonium results as preliminary. This is a practice account about secondary parkinsonism. [Source](https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/1/3604/186.full.pdf)

### 1932 — Four antimuscarinic preparations in postencephalitic parkinsonism

R. G. Anderson’s Edinburgh thesis describes a hospital series of 65 cases and compares hyoscine, stramonium, belladonna, and genoscopolamine against symptoms following encephalitis lethargica. The repository summary confirms the comparison and population. Its drug-by-symptom findings require extraction from the thesis scan. [Source](https://era.ed.ac.uk/items/0d93d8ef-0201-4f1d-bb40-d859b8d014ac)
