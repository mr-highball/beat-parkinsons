# Procedures and devices

### 1933 — Extrapyramidal tract surgery for athetosis and dystonia

Tracy J. Putnam reported experimental surgery for involuntary movements in athetosis and dystonia and discussed competing explanations for their origin. This adjacent work documents the movement-disorder rationale for later tract surgery. Its cases concern different syndromes and offer no direct evidence of benefit in Parkinson’s disease. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneurpsyc.1933.02240090074005)
