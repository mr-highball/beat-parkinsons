# Clinical features and diagnosis

### 1930 — Blood sugar, calcium and hematology in postencephalitic parkinsonism

J. Hühnerfeld investigated blood sugar and calcium, sedimentation rate and blood counts in postencephalitic parkinsonism. The 1930 original record verifies the measured variables, with full results requiring access to the German article. These measurements are laboratory values rather than a food intervention. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1159/000164303)

### 1931 — Clinical and autopsy comparisons of parkinsonism

Keschner and Sloane selected ten brains from people diagnosed with parkinsonism during life and compared clinical histories with histology across cases classified as encephalitic, idiopathic, or arteriosclerotic. Three long-preserved specimens stained poorly, especially in the globus pallidus. The report illustrates early attempts to distinguish similar motor presentations through anatomy and the technical limits of those comparisons. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneurpsyc.1931.02230050087005)
