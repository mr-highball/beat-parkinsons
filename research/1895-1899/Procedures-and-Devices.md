# Procedures and devices, 1895-1899

### 1895 — Althaus reports improvement with electrical treatment

A contemporary clinical digest reproduces an account by Julius Althaus of applying galvanic current to people diagnosed with shaking palsy and reporting symptomatic improvement. Patient selection, durability, and comparative results are unclear.

[Moir’s 1895 clinical digest, Electrical Treatment in Paralysis Agitans, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5142957/)
