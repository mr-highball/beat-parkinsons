The digitized edition contains complex oil formulations. Candidate wording on page 418 includes हस्तकम्पं and शिरःकम्पं, provisionally glossed as hand/head shaking. Adjoining formulations have distinct boundaries. Ingredient words in one must not be assigned automatically to the shaking passage in another.
