# Drugs and remedies, 1895-1899

### 1899 — Borate, hyoscyamine, and physical modalities show mixed case outcomes

Louis Lamarche’s thesis describes patients given combinations of sodium borate, hyoscyamine, baths, and massage. One patient reported improvement after borate and hyoscyamine, and another showed no improvement with subsequent electricity. The case narratives cannot attribute improvement to an individual remedy.
