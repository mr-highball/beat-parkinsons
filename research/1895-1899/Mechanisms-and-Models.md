# Mechanisms and models, 1895-1899

### 1895 — Brissaud proposes the substantia nigra as a disease site

Édouard Brissaud’s published lectures reviewed proposed sites of lesions and used Blocq and Marinesco’s focal lesion case to argue that the *locus niger* might be the anatomical substrate of Parkinson’s disease. The lecture was a reasoned inference from a mimic and earlier observations. It did not include a confirmatory series of idiopathic cases.
