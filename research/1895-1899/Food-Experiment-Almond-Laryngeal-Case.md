# Almond oil in a historical laryngeal case

## Intralaryngeal almond oil as a menthol carrier

Brandis and Thissen reported this case on August 7, 1897. A man with obstructing laryngeal disease received intralaryngeal menthol carried in almond oil with other medical treatments. His condition worsened before tracheotomy and other procedures. Follow-up in May 1897 described a good voice and loss of hoarseness. Multiple interventions and the clinical course prevent attributing the change to the carrier oil. Oil pressing, food consumption, Parkinson diagnosis and acoustic voice volume were unreported.
