# Observations for 1895-1899

## Major observations

- Brissaud advanced the substantia nigra hypothesis explicitly in relation to Parkinson’s disease, on evidence that still centered on a focal lesion mimic. [Mechanisms](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md)

## Minor observations

- Claims about electricity and multi-remedy care continued. The two sources record perceived improvement and a negative electrical outcome, with limited follow-up. [Devices](../Procedures-and-Devices.md) · [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md)

## Comparison with 1890–1894

- Blocq and Marinesco’s lesion observation became the basis of Brissaud’s broader anatomical hypothesis. The combination of pharmacologic and physical remedies extended symptom-focused experimentation beyond vibration alone. [Prior observations](../../1890-1894/Observations/README.md)

## Collection gaps

- Retrieve Althaus’s original article and extract comparable outcomes from other 1890s dissertations. No controlled dietary evidence has been collected.

## Dietary supplement original follow-through, November 15, 2026

The case associates a menthol carrier with airway delivery and later voice-quality recovery after several additional interventions. Pressing and measured vocal volume were unreported.
