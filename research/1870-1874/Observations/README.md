# Observations for 1870-1874

[Remedy reports and outcome verification](1871-Remedy-Reports-and-Outcome-Verification.md) compares the 1871 remedy anecdote with a separate clinical laboratory case.

## Major observations

- The Salpêtrière lectures make rigidity and slowness more explicit as separable clinical findings. Jones’s cases show the breadth of contemporary usage of the term. [Clinical sources](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md)

## Minor observations

- The same investigator recorded both benefit and failure with a hemlock preparation. His reports of electrical treatment provide a baseline for later skepticism about durability. [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) · [Devices](../Procedures-and-Devices.md)

## Comparison with 1865–1869

- Trousseau’s repetitive-movement bedside example and Charcot’s initial tremor distinction became part of a more explicit account of how slowness, rigidity, and tremor can vary independently. Case definitions still differed between authors. [Prior observations](../../1865-1869/Observations/README.md)
