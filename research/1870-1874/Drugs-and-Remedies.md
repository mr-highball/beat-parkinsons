# Drugs and remedies, 1870-1874

### 1873 — Hemlock had mixed results in a clinical lecture

C. Handfield Jones reported improvement after *succus conii* (hemlock preparation) in some patients and lack of benefit in another. Treatments varied, and follow-up was incomplete. Hemlock is poisonous and the historical reports establish no safe or effective use.

[Handfield Jones, British Medical Journal, part 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2293445/)
