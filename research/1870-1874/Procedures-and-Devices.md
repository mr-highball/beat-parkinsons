# Procedures and devices, 1870-1874

### 1873 — Galvanism in Handfield Jones’s case series

Handfield Jones described perceived benefit in some patients treated with galvanic electrical current. Case outcomes were described qualitatively and additional remedies were used. The reports cannot establish a device effect.

[Handfield Jones, British Medical Journal, part 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2293445/)
