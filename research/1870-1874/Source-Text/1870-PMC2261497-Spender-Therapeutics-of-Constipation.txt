Text coverage: Selected paraphrase from both columns of the original page image.

The letter begins at the foot of the left column and continues at the top of the right column. Spender recalls recommending iron with an extract of aloes in an earlier Medical Times and Gazette paper. He compares his approach with a colleague's, notes possible adjuncts, and discusses tolerability and frequency of dosing. The letter reports professional correspondence without an independently measured comparison of treatment response.

The term walnut appears twice elsewhere on the page as a description of size in adjacent clinical reports. One describes a cerebellar tumor and the other a clot in a different patient. These neighboring passages do not describe walnut ingestion. The constipation letter does not mention consuming fig or walnut.

The 2022 walnut–fig syrup article cites the letter in its walnut-laxative background. The 1870 page identifies a separate medicinal practice. The citation does not verify historical fig or walnut efficacy.
