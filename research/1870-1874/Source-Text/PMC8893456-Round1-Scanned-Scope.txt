The original historical miscellany appeared in volume 1, issue 12, pages 653–692. Its case anecdotes, therapy recipes, and copied reports provide evidence of historical use. The notes preserve the exposure and indication without repeating quantities, preparation instructions, or unverified efficacy claims.
