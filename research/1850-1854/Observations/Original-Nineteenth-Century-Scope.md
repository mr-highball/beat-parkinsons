# Egg and trout in an 1851 history of chemistry

The [1851 account of the work of William Prout](../Source-Text/1851-PMC5929050-Round1-Source-Scope.txt) discusses chemical observations on raw, incubating and boiled eggs and comparative urine chemistry including trout. These topics establish historical ingredient mentions, with no dietary assignment or Parkinson outcome. Dates in the article describe chronology.
