# History and context, 1865-1869

### 1865 — Sanders uses the English eponym in a differential diagnosis

William R. Sanders described a young man with dystaxia and compared the presentation with classic shaking palsy. He used the phrase *Parkinson’s disease* while proposing terms to distinguish the classic syndrome from mimics. The 1865 paper documents English usage within this archive’s requested starting window. An 1861 French publication by Charcot and Vulpian used *maladie de Parkinson* before Sanders.
