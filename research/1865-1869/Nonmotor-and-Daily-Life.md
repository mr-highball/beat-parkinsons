# Nonmotor symptoms and daily life, 1865-1869

### 1868 — Trousseau records fatigue and reported late memory changes

Trousseau recorded fatigue after severe shaking and described later memory and intellectual changes in his lecture on shaking palsy. He also reported pneumonia as the cause of death in three patients he followed. These observations offer leads on symptom burden and complications. Their frequencies and causes cannot be estimated from the lecture.
