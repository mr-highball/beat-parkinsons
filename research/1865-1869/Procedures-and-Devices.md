# Procedures and devices, 1865-1869

### 1868 — Sanders summarizes conflicting reports of electricity and galvanism

Sanders’s textbook chapter records improvement attributed to continuous electrical current in some reports and a lack of improvement in four cases observed by William Gull. The chapter lists variation across modalities and patient groups. The historical diagnoses and absence of standardized outcomes prevent a reliable comparison.
