# Drugs and remedies, 1875-1879

### 1879 — Barium chloride fails in two further cases

Saint-Léger described Brown-Séquard’s reported recovery in one patient given barium chloride and subsequent lack of benefit in Brown-Séquard’s later use and in two of Saint-Léger’s own cases. The uncontrolled observations show failure to reproduce the initial report. The substance is toxic.

[Saint-Léger, clinical thesis, chapter VII, scan](https://archive.org/details/b22353598/page/110/mode/2up)

### 1879 — Saint-Léger summarizes mixed responses to historical drugs

The thesis records reported temporary benefit with bromide of camphor and relief in some patients given hyoscyamine. It also records increased shaking attributed to strychnine and silver nitrate. These reports combine small uncontrolled cases and contemporary citations. Their outcomes and adverse effects were not systematically measured.

[Saint-Léger, clinical thesis, chapter VII, scan](https://archive.org/details/b22353598/page/110/mode/2up)
