# Nonmotor symptoms and daily life, 1875-1879

### 1879 — Saint-Léger documents pain and feeding dependence

The clinical thesis specifically investigated pain, documenting its character and timing, and described difficulty bringing food to the mouth as the motor disorder progressed. The symptoms were reported in selected cases and are distinct from evidence about effects of food on the disease.
