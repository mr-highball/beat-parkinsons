# Procedures and devices, 1875-1879

### 1879 — Electrical effects judged transient in two patients

Saint-Léger reported that local static electrical stimulation stopped tremor during application in two patients. Tremor returned when the intervention stopped. He also questioned whether earlier reports of electrical cures had described the same disorder.
