# History and context, 1875-1879

### 1877 — Sigerson translates Charcot’s lectures into English

George Sigerson translated the first volume of Charcot’s Salpêtrière lectures. Lectures V–VII made Charcot’s bedside descriptions and differential diagnosis available in an English edition. The date records dissemination of earlier teaching.

[Charcot and Sigerson, Lectures on the Diseases of the Nervous System, scan](https://archive.org/details/lecturesondiseas01char)

### 1879 — Saint-Léger publishes a photographic clinical study

Paul de Saint-Léger used photographs and facsimiles in a clinical thesis. The plates, including a series of images of the patient Pierre D., document hand and body posture. The photographs record selected positions at individual moments.

[Saint-Léger, Paralysie agitante (maladie de Parkinson), scan](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/pp3abfbv)
