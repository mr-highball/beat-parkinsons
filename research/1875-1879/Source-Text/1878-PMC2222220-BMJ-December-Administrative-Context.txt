Title: British Medical Journal issue 939 administrative context and ingredient terms
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2222220/
PMCID: PMC2222220
Publication date: 1878-12-28
Full bound-volume scan: https://archive.org/details/sim_british-medical-journal_july-6-december-28-1878_2
Text coverage: Distilled text from the complete December 28 issue, including the administrative material and member lists. Physical PDF pages 957–1019. Identified printed page 970 at physical page 982 by visual comparison. The last frame is blank.

Printed page 970 opens with hospital and dispensary management and includes Paris correspondence and association business. The following page carries council and association material. The Orange and Le Sage matches in the member list are surnames, and saffron appears in the place name Saffron Walden. Neither strawberry nor Fragaria appears in the dated issue. A strawberry-tongue reference elsewhere in the bound volume describes a clinical sign. No identified section supplies a culinary exposure comparison or a fall endpoint for these ingredients.
