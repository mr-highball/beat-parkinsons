# Observations for 1900-1904

## Major observations

- The period contains a detailed thesis on unilateral presentations and their mimics, as well as a possible secondary case following febrile illness. [Clinical sources](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md) · [Risk and prevention](../Risk-and-Prevention.md)

## Minor observations

- Bury’s report links relief of tremor and other symptoms with hyoscine. It also provides a lead on familial clustering. [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md)

## Comparison with 1895–1899

- Lamarche’s cases included hyoscyamine amid several treatments. Bury focused on a related but distinct alkaloid, hyoscine, and reported improvement in two family members. The diagnoses and patient-level timing were recorded with methods too limited for comparative conclusions. [Prior observations](../../1895-1899/Observations/README.md)

## Collection gaps

- Locate additional longitudinal reports on antimuscarinic exposure and follow-up of unilateral cases. Food-effect evidence was not identified.
