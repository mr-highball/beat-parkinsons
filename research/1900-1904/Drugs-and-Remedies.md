# Drugs and remedies, 1900-1904

### 1902 — Bury reports improvement after hyoscine in two related patients

Judson S. Bury described two cases of paralysis agitans in the same family with improvement after hyoscine, also called scopolamine. A contemporary digest attributes reduced tremor, restlessness, and heat sensations to the drug and notes concern about toxicity. Two related cases cannot establish comparative efficacy or inheritance.

[Bury, The Lancet, original report](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(01)70026-8)
