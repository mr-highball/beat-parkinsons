# Risk and prevention, 1900-1904

### 1904 — Parkinsonian symptoms after febrile illness in Burma

J. Good reported tremor, reduced facial expression, and slow movement in a 22-year-old after a febrile episode diagnosed clinically as malaria. No parasites were seen on the reported blood examination, and he had received quinine. The chronology documents a possible secondary presentation, with infection and other causes unconfirmed.
