# Clinical findings and diagnosis, 1900-1904

### 1901 — Michaud studies unilateral presentations and look-alikes

Louis Michaud’s Lyon thesis examines one-sided paralysis agitans and compares it with post-hemiplegic tremor, hemichorea, and hemiathetosis. He emphasized the difficulty of diagnosis when typical posture and gait changes were absent. Historical labels and autopsy interpretation need comparison against modern criteria.
