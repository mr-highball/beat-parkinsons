Title: Anise scent in a seventeenth-century sleep recipe
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Historical household manuscript, library reading copy

Original locator: Folger Shakespeare Library V.a.452, manuscript p. 318, reading-copy PDF pp. 371–372.
Publication date: 1675 (approximate)
Date basis: Folger manuscript catalog date used for the inspected original. The page is undated and the reading copy is a modern transcription.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25

In the section on sleep, the compiler lists anise placed by pillows so that the sleeper can smell it. The surrounding material describes remedies for sleep difficulty during illness and several distinct preparations. This entry is about scent, with no measurement of pain or intake of ground anise seed.

Reported direction: Intended improvement in sleep, without a corresponding reported patient result.
Authenticity: Folger's reading copy and page heading verify the spelling, route and context. The modern historical study cites the same page.
Efficacy evidence: No clinical diagnosis, comparison, response measure or adverse-outcome assessment.
