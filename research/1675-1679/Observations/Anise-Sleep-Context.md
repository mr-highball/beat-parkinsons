# Anise scent and the preceding historical collection

The 1664 printed remedy uses a mixed distillate for shaking palsy. The manuscript catalogued around 1675 documents scent near the sleeper's pillow for illness-related sleep difficulty. Different exposure routes and symptom descriptions keep those uses distinct. The separately inspected Boyle manuscript adds an anise sleep recipe with rosewater and a neighboring external nutmeg and rosemary preparation.
