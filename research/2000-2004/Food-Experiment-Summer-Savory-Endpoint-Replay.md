# Summer savory preparation and endpoint search

## Topical summer savory extract in a rabbit rhinosinusitis model

Uslu and colleagues published a rabbit experiment in 2003 using topical aqueous Satureja hortensis extract after induction of rhinosinusitis. The original report gives lower nitric oxide metabolites and nitric oxide synthase activity, with histological findings consistent with less local inflammation. The primary abstract specifies topical administration. It supplies no quantitative odor detection, discrimination or identification score. This model examines nasal inflammation after a local extract exposure and does not establish smell improvement after eating dried herb.
