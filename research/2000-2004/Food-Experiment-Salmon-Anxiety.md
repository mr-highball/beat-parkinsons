# Salmon and anxiety follow-up

## Fish advice and mood trial in men with angina

Ness and colleagues, 2003. This randomized follow-up compared advice to eat fatty fish, with fish-oil capsules available as an alternative, against no fish advice in 452 men with angina. The paper lists salmon among the fatty fish. The six-month adjusted difference on the Derogatis Stress Profile anxiety scale was +0.82 points for the advice group (95% CI −0.57 to 2.22, higher scores indicate worse anxiety). The analysis did not estimate a salmon-specific or preparation-specific effect. This was a cardiac cohort. [Primary report, PMID 12608739](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12608739/)
