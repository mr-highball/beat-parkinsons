# Facial expression, falls, voice and related food outcomes

## Summer savory essential oil and isolated intestinal muscle

Hajhashemi et al. report reduced contractions in isolated rat ileum after summer savory essential oil. The tested smooth muscle and concentrated oil do not give clinical rigidity or effects of fresh summer savory leaves. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10904162/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2000-Hajhashemi-Savory-Ileum-Extract.txt)

## Tomato powder and striatal dopamine in mice

The original Suganuma report assigned diet with lyophilized tomato powder. Its reported monoamine measurements show attenuation of the induced striatal dopamine decline. It gives no clinical or behavioral rigidity result and its powder differs from cooked tomato. [Original source](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnsv1973/48/3/48_3_251/_pdf) · [Local text](Source-Text/2002-Suganuma-Tomato-MPTP.txt)
