# Soybean oil comparator and clinical endpoints

## Registered soybean oil blend in the Alzheimer pilot

NCT00090402 first posted on 26 August 2004. It identifies a soybean-oil placebo in a 12-month randomized trial of fish oil and alpha-lipoic acid in Alzheimer’s disease. The registry results posted 26 February 2010 and the later original paper identify fish oil at 5% in the soybean-oil capsules. The registered trial, posted results and 2013 paper describe the same 39-person study.
