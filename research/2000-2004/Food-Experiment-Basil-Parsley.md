# Basil and parsley evidence

## Parsley seed extract and rat colon water absorption

Kreydiyyeh and colleagues published in September 2001. An aqueous parsley seed extract in the perfusion buffer significantly reduced net water absorption by rat colon compared with control. The reduction was consistent with an effect on sodium absorption. The experiment used a seed extract applied to the tissue and measured fluid transport. Fresh leaf intake and stool symptoms were outside its design. Table 1 gives 0.254 ± 0.058 mL/cm² net absorption for controls and 0.061 ± 0.010 for the extract, eight rats per group.
