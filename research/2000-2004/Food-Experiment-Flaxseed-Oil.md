# Flaxseed oil evidence

## Flaxseed oil in a rat odor discrimination diet

Greiner and colleagues reported a two-generation feeding experiment in February 2001. The omega-3-adequate diet contained flaxseed oil. Second-generation rats fed the omega-3-deficient comparison diet made more errors acquiring several two-odor discriminations and had 82% less DHA in the olfactory bulb. The treatment contrast was developmental omega-3 adequacy. It did not test treatment of a diagnosed smell impairment, Parkinson’s or a verified cold-pressed oil. [Original methods and results](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0031938400004376) · [PubMed primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11274681/)
