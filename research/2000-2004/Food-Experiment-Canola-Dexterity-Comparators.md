# Canola oil and motor task comparators

## Canola oil and finger-tapping BOLD in the 2003 experiment

Noseworthy and colleagues scanned healthy volunteers while they tapped bilaterally to a paced 2 Hz task. Brain BOLD signal was lower at 40 and 100 minutes after a meal with 25 or 50 mL added canola oil. The supplement-only control did not show a clear change. Tapping was the imaging stimulus. The report supplies no separate speed, accuracy or function outcome for clinical hand dexterity, no Parkinson cohort and no verified processing grade.
