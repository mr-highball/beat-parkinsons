# Nutmeg and paprika evidence

## Nutmeg preparations in experimental diarrhea

Grover and colleagues’ 2002 study compared crude nutmeg suspension and two fractions in experimental diarrhea. The crude suspension and petroleum-ether fraction decreased loose stools in the challenge model. The aqueous preparation did not show the same effect. The petroleum-ether fraction also prolonged barbiturate-induced sleeping time. The study measured induced diarrhea and drug-induced sleep in animals. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12616960/)

## Red pepper powder and abdominal pain in functional dyspepsia

Bortolotti and colleagues compared encapsulated red pepper powder with placebo in people with functional dyspepsia in 2002. Reported dyspepsia and epigastric pain scores fell with repeated use. The report also describes withdrawals attributed to pain after early red pepper exposure. The product and pain site differ from the frozen culinary paprika claim. [Primary trial](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-2036.2002.01280.x)
