# Rapeseed oil and pain, 2000–2004

## Rapeseed oil within a Mediterranean diet for rheumatoid arthritis

Sköldstam and colleagues compared a Cretan-style Mediterranean diet with a usual diet in people with rheumatoid arthritis. The intervention included olive and rapeseed oils and formulated spreads alongside changes to several other foods. Mean pain VAS decreased from 32 to 20 on the intervention and increased from 31 to 34 in the control group. The reported comparison was p=.006. The change is attributable to the dietary program under the trial contrast. Pressing grade and an oil-specific contribution are unresolved.
