# Food experiment evidence for soybean and rice bran oil

## Refining methods and rice bran oil constituents

Krishna and colleagues’ 2001 processing study measured oryzanol across refining steps. Alkali refining removed 83–95% under its tested conditions. Degumming, dewaxing, bleaching, deodorization and the tested physical refining step retained much more. A later physical-refining study measured smaller but significant reductions in commercial process samples, from 1,599–1,666 to 933–960 mg oryzanol per 100 g of oil. These experiments give constituent concentrations and do not test neurological or bowel symptoms. The refining process matters when comparing isolated oryzanol, unrefined oil and commercial refined oils.
