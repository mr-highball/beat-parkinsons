# Fennel seed clinical endpoint follow-up

## Slovak poison surveillance of contaminated fennel tea

The Slovak regulator described the Czech withdrawal of a fennel tea product contaminated with Datura and summarized the poison center’s field sampling and calls. Four of 20 samples tested positive. Consumers reported fatigue, difficulty expressing themselves, disorientation and visual disturbance with pupil dilation. The bulletin does not identify formed visual hallucinations or a Parkinson cohort. The 2002 conference account already cataloged for ground fennel reports the same event, so the bulletin adds source detail without creating an independent safety observation.
