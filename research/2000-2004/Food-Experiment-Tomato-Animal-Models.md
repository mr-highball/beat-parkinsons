# Tomato preparations in Parkinson models, 2000–2004

## Freeze-dried tomato concentrate in MPTP mice

Suganuma et al. gave male mice a diet containing 20% freeze-dried tomato powder made from concentrated juice for four weeks before toxin exposure. The tomato diet attenuated the striatal dopamine reduction measured shortly after MPTP. Changes in dopamine metabolites DOPAC and HVA did not reach significance. The experiment measured biochemical outcomes and body weight. Executive function and stride length were outside the reported design.
