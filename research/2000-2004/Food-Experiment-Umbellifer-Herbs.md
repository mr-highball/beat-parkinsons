# Dill and acute throat symptoms

## Dill allergy case

- **2000-05. Chiu and Zacharisen.** A patient with allergic rhinitis reported tongue and throat swelling, oral itching, hives and gastrointestinal symptoms after eating foods cooked with dill. The report describes a separate reaction with airborne exposure and a positive skin test to fresh dill. The exposure report leaves the culinary part and amount uncertain. The skin test confirms exposure to fresh material by a different route. The case concerns allergy and did not measure Parkinson dysphagia or the severity of difficulty swallowing fresh dill leaves. [Original case](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1081-1206(10)62523-9) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10831013/).
