# Cumin bioavailability patent families

## Cumin bioavailability patent family first published in 2003

Public patent examples identify other-drug interaction leads. The first family became public in 2003. The original US versions describe rifampicin in rats and contain claimed CNS examples. Duplicate continuation and national publications are not separate evidence contributions. [US example text](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20040052873A1/en) [Original source](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003075685A2/en) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2003-CSIR-Cumin-Bioavailability-Patent-Family.txt)

## Separate cumin fraction patent family first published in 2004

The second family's first public version appeared in 2004 and the US examples report effects with some other drugs. There is no preparation-matched Parkinson observation or levodopa comparison in the reviewed material.
