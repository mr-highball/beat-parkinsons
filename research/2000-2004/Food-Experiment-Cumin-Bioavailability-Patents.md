# Cumin bioavailability patent families

## Cumin bioavailability patent family first published in 2003

Public patent examples identify other-drug interaction leads. The first family became public in 2003. The PCT example 4 table describes rifampicin in rats. The original graphical tables in example 5 name alprazolam and haloperidol. An accessible Chinese family publication repeats the other-drug examples. No reviewed example used levodopa or a clinical OFF-time outcome. Continuations and national copies describe the same evidence family. [US example text](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20040052873A1/en) · [PCT original, examples 4 and 5](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003075685A2/en) · [Chinese family publication](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN1649513A/en) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2003-CSIR-Cumin-Bioavailability-Patent-Family.txt)

## Separate cumin fraction patent family first published in 2004

The second family's first public version appeared in 2004. Its original example 5 graphical tables report other drugs, including alprazolam and haloperidol. These are patent examples on extract fractions, with no preparation-matched Parkinson observation or levodopa comparison. Individual European graphical tables and national Australian and South African bodies remain unverified.
