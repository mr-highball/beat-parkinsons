# Myrcene and drug-induced sleep, 2000–2004

## Injected isolated myrcene and pentobarbital sleep in mice

Do Vale and colleagues measured the time mice lost their righting reflex after pentobarbital. Table 3 reports 40.9 minutes for controls (SEM 5.1; n=14), 64.0 minutes after injected isolated myrcene at 100 mg/kg (SEM 10.2; n=10; p<.05) and 106.6 minutes at 200 mg/kg (SEM 14.1; n=10; p<.01). Rotarod performance also declined at those two doses. In Table 4 the 25 mg/kg group entered open arms less often than controls, while the 50 mg/kg group did not show that change. The paper's abstract calls the reduction a highest-dose effect, which conflicts with the displayed dose rows. The authors found no evidence of anxiolysis. The study examined a drug-induced loss of righting in mice. It gives no estimate of spontaneous insomnia, Parkinson symptoms or the effects of eating a myrcene-containing food.
