The [Singhal historical account](Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2003-PMID12915075-Singhal-Kampavata-Saliva-and-Mucuna.txt) separates traditional syndrome descriptions from measured symptom response. The surgical reports record [gait](Source-Text/2000-PMID10681077-Siegel-Pallidotomy-and-Gait.txt), [adverse drooling](Source-Text/2001-PMID11235949-Intemann-Pallidotomy-and-Drooling.txt) and [separate motor follow-up](Source-Text/2001-PMID11274323-Counihan-Pallidotomy-Followup.txt).

The [2001 swallow reminder study](Source-Text/2001-PMID11340797-Marks-Drooling-and-Swallow-Cueing.txt) preserves the original author abstract and the open body-access gap for numerical drooling effects.

The [date-waste mushroom citation](Source-Text/2002-Jwanny-Date-Waste-Mushroom-Polysaccharide-Citation.txt) records the coauthor's verified journal and page locator for the antitumor study. The article body remains open.

The [2001 SGIM label survey](Source-Text/2001-PMID11357836-SGIM-Herb-Supplement-and-Food-Scopes.txt) and [2003 emergency-department survey](Source-Text/2003-PMID14535236-ACEP-Herbal-Knowledge-and-Scope.txt) address marketed herbal products and knowledge of indications. The separate [rat gastric-emptying experiment](Source-Text/2001-PMID11876024-Ginger-Extract-Rat-Gastric-Emptying.txt) and [human gastric electrical comparison](Source-Text/2003-PMID14534370-Ginger-Gastric-Electrical-Rhythm.txt) measured distinct physiological endpoints.

The [Indian Phalgu root monograph](Source-Text/2001-API-Phalgu-Dried-Root-Botanical-Identity.txt) designates dried Ficus hispida root. The [2001 apasmāra thesis record](Source-Text/2001-Usha-Apasmara-Thesis-Identity.txt) traces a likely shared study family with the [2018 treatment report](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-Usha-Singh-Mahapanchagavya-Epilepsy-Series.txt).

The [2004 Rabat–Salé doctoral survey](Source-Text/2004-Zinedine-IMIST-Dried-Fig-Ochratoxin-Thesis.txt) reports an ochratoxin A detection-limit result and an unidentified chromatogram peak in five fig samples. [Comparison with later retail samples](Observations/README.md)

The [2000 hallucination and proceedings notes](Observations/2000-Hallucination-and-Proceedings-Access.md) compare clinical measurements and record missing archival pages.

The [2001 visual histories and publication scope notes](Observations/2001-Visual-History-and-Conference-Scope.md) link the EANM proceedings, the later perfusion report, and diagnostic and medication sources.

The [visual processing and clinical measurement notes](Observations/2002-2004-Visual-Processing-and-Clinical-Outcome-Scope.md) compare visual reasoning, diagnostic classification and behavioral ratings. [Moretti primary follow-up](Source-Text/PMC10833835.txt) · [Kuopio diagnostic prevalence](Source-Text/PMC1738491.txt).

The [2004 Lewy body dementia expert report](Source-Text/2004-PMID14693108-Lewy-Body-Dementia-Expert-Review.txt) gives historical diagnostic context for the [2005 classification and visual symptom records](../2005-2009/Observations/2005-Conference-Scope-and-Diagnostic-Outcomes.md).

The open-label [rivastigmine](Source-Text/2001-PMID11748755-Rivastigmine-Parkinson-Psychosis.txt), [tacrine or donepezil](Source-Text/2001-PMID11768630-Cholinesterase-Cognition-and-Motor.txt), and [quetiapine](Source-Text/2004-PMID15090935-Quetiapine-Parkinson-Psychosis.txt) papers report distinct psychosis, cognition or motor outcomes. [2006 evidence comparison](../2005-2009/Observations/2006-Visual-History-Treatment-and-Diagnostic-Comparisons.md).

Two 2004 FP-CIT papers use [clinical diagnosis groups](Source-Text/2004-PMID15210531-FP-CIT-Clinical-Diagnosis-Comparison.txt) and [statistical image maps](Source-Text/2004-PMID15528096-FP-CIT-Statistical-Mapping-Comparison.txt). [Comparison with the autopsy series and symptom measures](../2005-2009/Observations/2007-Visual-History-Symptom-Measurement-and-Proceedings.md).

The [six-month Spheramine pilot report](Source-Text/2004-PMID14766394-Spheramine-Six-Month-Pilot.txt) describes the initial clinical follow-up of six participants. [Comparison with the later pilot and randomized reports](../2005-2009/Observations/2008-Visual-Event-Definitions-and-Spheramine-Lineage.md).

The [2002 multiple system atrophy report](Source-Text/2002-PMID12040988-Infestation-in-MSA.txt) describes infestation delusion in a distinct diagnostic population. [Comparison with Parkinson case reports](../2005-2009/Observations/2009-Visual-Symptom-Definitions-and-Proceedings-Identity.md).

The [2002 temporal pathology study](Source-Text/2002-PMID11844739-Formed-Visual-Hallucinations-and-Temporal-Pathology.txt) compares formed visual hallucination phenotypes with regional Lewy body findings.

The [2001 single-vine extract trial](Source-Text/2001-Serrano-Duenas-Banisteriopsis-Parkinson-Trial.txt) reports distinct motor total, tremor and adverse-event observations. The [2003 enzyme and tissue study](Source-Text/2003-Banisteriopsis-MAO-and-Striatal-Slices.txt) examines a separate experimental question.

The [Rush hallucination cohort](Source-Text/2001-PMID11739829-Parkinson-Hallucination-Followup-Abstract.txt) tracked Parkinson symptoms with sequential interviews. [Later outcome comparisons](../2015-2019/Observations/Psychosis-Remission-and-Visual-Symptom-Status.md).

The [banana pulp enzyme study](Source-Text/2000-PMID11087516-Banana-Pulp-Oxidation-and-Salsolinol-Abstract.txt) measures salsolinol oxidation outside the body.

The [clinical fig allergy and laboratory cross-reactivity record](Source-Text/2003-PMID12859455-Fresh-and-Dried-Fig-Cross-Reactivity.txt) separates fresh and dried preparations in the assay.

The [2003 Taounate ethnobotanical excerpt](Source-Text/2003-PMID12738079-Taounate-Ethnobotany-Excerpt.txt) verifies the earlier paper cited for a fig digestive use. Its original fig table remains open.

The [2004 diabetes congress food members](Source-Text/2004-PMC7096100-EASD-Food-Exposure-Contexts.txt) distinguish meat-derived laboratory reagents, coffee, tomato juice and oil formulations. The [related marine-fat feeding publication](Source-Text/2004-PMID15736913-Corn-Oil-and-Marine-PUFA-Adiposity.txt) records adiposity outcomes in mice.

The [2002 author follow-up](Source-Text/2002-Hansen-Macadamia-Kernel-Toxicosis-Tremor-Scope.txt) distinguishes reported whole-kernel field tremor from the four-dog observation and links the same veterinary series.

The [2000 sweet-fennel pamphlet trace](Source-Text/2000-Ostad-Sweet-Fennel-Toxicity-Pamphlet-Trace.txt) and [2001 essential-oil toxicology study](Source-Text/2001-PMID11448553-Fennel-Oil-Toxicity-and-Movement.txt) share three investigators. The original pamphlet remains open. [Comparative observations](../2020-2024/Observations/Fennel-Preparation-and-Resting-Tremor.md).

Two human crossover studies tested [unprocessed grapefruit](Source-Text/2000-PMID11103749-Unprocessed-Grapefruit-and-Felodipine.txt) and [fresh grapefruit pulp](Source-Text/2002-PMID12040751-Grapefruit-Pulp-and-Calcium-Antagonists.txt) with cardiovascular drugs. A separate [cabergoline interaction case report](Source-Text/2002-PMID12465083-Cabergoline-and-Itraconazole-Cases.txt) tested itraconazole. [Later clinical and preparation observations](../2020-2024/Observations/Grapefruit-Preparation-and-Tremor.md).
