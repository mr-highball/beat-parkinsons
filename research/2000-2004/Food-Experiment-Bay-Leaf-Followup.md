# Bay leaf symptom evidence

## Sedation and motor impairment with leaf essential oil

In April 2002, Sayyah, Valizadeh and Kamalinejad reported sedation and motor impairment at anticonvulsant doses of *Laurus nobilis* leaf essential oil in mice evaluated in seizure models. The primary abstract gives no walking speed, step length, sleep stage or dream-behavior result. The 2003 primary paper from the same group reported moderate sedation at anti-inflammatory doses of leaf essential oil in separate rodent experiments. These preparations and endpoints give adverse-direction motor and sleep context for 0032, 0154 and 0360. [2002 primary report](https://doi.org/10.1078/0944-7113-00113) · [2003 primary report](https://doi.org/10.1002/ptr.1197)
