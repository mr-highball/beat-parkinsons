# Mustard seed and caraway research

## Nasal mustard constituent and odor thresholds

Jacquot, Monnin and Brand compared detection thresholds with and without prior nasal allyl isothiocyanate exposure in 20 healthy volunteers. Detection improved for phenylethyl alcohol and butanol (p < .027 and p < .05, respectively). Thresholds describe a short laboratory effect after nasal exposure to a constituent. The study supplies no estimate for eating ground mustard or for people with Parkinson’s. [Primary article, Figure 1 and Results](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/biologies/item/10.1016/j.crvi.2004.02.004.pdf)
