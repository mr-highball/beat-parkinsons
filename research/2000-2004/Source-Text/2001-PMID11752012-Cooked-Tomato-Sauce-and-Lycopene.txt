Title: Oxidative DNA damage in prostate cancer patients consuming tomato sauce-based entrees as a whole-food intervention
Publication date: 2001-12-19
PMID: 11752012
DOI: 10.1093/jnci/93.24.1872
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11752012/
Text coverage: Original presurgery whole-food report and measured circulating response.

A short presurgery whole-food intervention provided cooked tomato sauce-based entrees for patients with prostate cancer. Mean serum lycopene rose from 638 to 1,258 nM (p<.001) and tissue concentrations also rose. Related endpoints concerned oxidative DNA damage and markers of prostate disease. The comparison does not isolate an executive or rigidity effect in Parkinson disease.
