Title: Dopamine transporter loss visualized with FP-CIT SPECT in the differential diagnosis of dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15210531/
PMID: 15210531
DOI: 10.1001/archneur.61.6.919
Publication date: 2004-06
Date basis: Archives of Neurology June issue
Source depth: Primary author abstract
Format: Original-source factual extract

The paper compares tracer imaging with clinical diagnoses in 164 people, including 23 with Lewy body dementia, 34 with Alzheimer disease, 38 with Parkinson disease, 36 with Parkinson disease and dementia, and 33 controls. Tracer uptake and visual ratings of scans distinguished Lewy body dementia from Alzheimer disease more readily than the groups containing Parkinson disease. Scan readings and visual symptoms are distinct. The abstract provides no episode-frequency or measured food comparison.
