Title: Antihypertriglyceridemic effect of walnut oil
PMID: 12934760
DOI: 10.1177/000331970305400404
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12934760/
First published: July–August 2003 issue; earlier exact public day unverified.
Coverage: Factual author-abstract extraction; complete methods and tables unavailable in this review.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Zibaeenezhad and colleagues describe 60 adults with hyperlipidemia: 29 assigned encapsulated walnut oil and 31 placebo for 45 days, with oil intake 3 g/day. The abstract reports triglycerides 19%–33% below the oil group's baseline, p<.05, without significant changes in other measured lipids.

A between-group estimate, oil extraction method and complete adverse-event analysis are not available here. The abstract's earlier whole-walnut pilot is a separate study, not another arm of this trial. No Parkinson symptom, cognition or pain outcome is reported. This publication has not been verified as one of the three oil papers in the 2018 evidence map.
