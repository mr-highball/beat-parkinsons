Title: Analgesic and anti-inflammatory activity of the leaf essential oil of Laurus nobilis Linn.
PMID: 12916069
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.1197
First published: 2003
Authors: Sayyah M, Saroukhani G, Peirovi A, Kamalinejad M
Journal: Phytotherapy research : PTR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12916069/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical rodent study (Sayyah et al., 2003, Phytother Res) testing Laurus nobilis leaf essential oil in mouse/rat tail-flick, formalin pain, and formalin edema assays. Essential oil reduced nociceptive responses comparably to morphine/piroxicam, but the authors explicitly documented a moderate sedative effect at effective doses, matching companion motor-impairment/sedation findings in Sayyah et al. 2002 (PMID:12046861). Limits: concentrated volatile essential oil (not ground culinary bay leaf) and overt sedative/motor confounding in reflex pain assays.
