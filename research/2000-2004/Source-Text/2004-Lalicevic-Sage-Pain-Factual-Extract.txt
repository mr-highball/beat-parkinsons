Title: Comparison of benzydamine hydrochloride and Salvia officinalis as an adjuvant local treatment to systemic nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug in controlling pain after tonsillectomy, adenoidectomy, or both: an open-label, single-blind, randomized clinical trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3964578/
Identifiers: PMID 24672091, PMCID PMC3964578, DOI 10.1016/j.curtheres.2004.07.002
Publication year: 2004
Coverage: Factual extract from the original Methods and Results.

The assessor-blind, open treatment comparison included 420 people after tonsil and/or adenoid surgery, 278 children and 142 adults. Both arms received ibuprofen or diclofenac and used a local adjunct of benzydamine or common-sage infusion. In children, the benzydamine group reported less moderate or severe pain at postoperative days 1 and 4 (p < .01), and days 2 and 7 (p < .001). No inert comparator, fresh-leaf food exposure or Parkinson cohort was included.
