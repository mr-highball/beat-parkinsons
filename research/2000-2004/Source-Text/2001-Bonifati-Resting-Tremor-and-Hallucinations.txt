Resting tremor and visual hallucinations in parkin-linked parkinsonism
PMID 11561042
DOI 10.1136/jnnp.71.4.531
https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/71/4/531.full.pdf

October 2001. On page 532, Bonifati and colleagues report that the sister in a parkin-linked family had mild bilateral resting upper-limb tremor and a three-year history of visual hallucinations and confusion at age 89. Recorded medication included levodopa, amantadine and biperiden. Her brother had no tremor at examination. The paper gives no episode count, serial tremor estimate, or edible fig comparison. The indexed original journal PDF supplied the clinical pages.
