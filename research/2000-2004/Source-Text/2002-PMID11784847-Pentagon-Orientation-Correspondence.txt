Della Sala and colleagues described clear rotation errors in 7 of 134 people with Alzheimer disease and questioned the earlier comparison's scoring. Ala replied that the figures in the earlier study had not included the original models. The reply illustrated six model–copy pairs to distinguish differences in orientation of the printed model from actual copying errors. Ala retained the pathology-confirmed comparison under its original scoring and stated that the revised review supported no conclusion about rotational errors in either group.

The letter's initial summary gives a Lewy body denominator of 13, whereas the original includes 17. Use the original counts and its separately reported stricter and looser scoring analyses. The reply reuses the original participants. The letter's 134-person Alzheimer series concerns a separate copying outcome. Neither contribution measures visual hallucination frequency or fig intake.
