Title: Rivastigmine for dementia associated with Parkinson's disease

DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa041470
Sponsor trial identifier: CENA713B2311
Publication date: 2004-12-09
Date basis: Original journal publication
Source depth: Complete journal article with Table 3 and the tolerability results
Evidence type: Primary randomized double-blind trial
Format: Factual digest of original material

EXPRESS randomized 541 people with Parkinson disease dementia to oral rivastigmine or placebo for 24 weeks. Table 3 reports the number of people with the general adverse-event term hallucinations, 17 of 362 (4.7%) on rivastigmine and 17 of 179 (9.5%) on placebo, with a reported P value of 0.04. That table does not specify the sensory modality or the number of episodes per person. It does not use a dietary comparison.

Adverse-event tremor was reported in 37 of 362 (10.2%) versus 7 of 179 (3.9%), reported P=0.01. Between-group differences on the formal UPDRS motor and tremor-related scores had P values of 0.83 and 0.84, respectively. Adverse-event reporting and examination scores are distinct endpoints.

The later visual-hallucination subgroup analysis, PMID:16960863, groups participants by baseline visual hallucinations to examine dementia treatment response. The executive-function analysis, PMID:20950329, and the clinical-profile comparison, PMID:24174923, also reuse EXPRESS. The early hallucination trial PMC8413762 is a separate study.
