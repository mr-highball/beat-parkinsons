Title: Effects of heavy particle irradiation and diet on amphetamine- and lithium chloride-induced taste avoidance learning in rats
PMID: 12384235
DOI: 10.1016/S0006-8993(02)03263-8
Publication: Brain Research, volume 953, pages 31–36, October 25, 2002
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12384235/
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original author abstract. The full figures and allocation details remain open.

Rats received 2% blueberry extract, 2% strawberry extract or control diet before 1.5 Gy iron-ion irradiation. Both berry regimens preserved the experimental acquisition of amphetamine-induced conditioned taste avoidance after exposure compared with irradiated rats on the control diet. Lithium chloride-induced taste avoidance did not differ by irradiation or diet. The outcome is conditioned learning after drug challenges. This is distinct from the later operant-response experiments and did not measure falls.
