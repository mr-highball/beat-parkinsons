Seventeen healthy women entered a controlled feeding study. One fat source remained assigned within each participant while the two manganese periods crossed over. Figure 1 reports ten in the corn-oil arm and seven in the cocoa-butter arm. Table 1 reports ten corn-oil and six cocoa-butter participants for the tracer analysis. The later EFSA opinion describes six corn-oil and ten cocoa-butter participants. The original figure and table control the allocation labels, and their differing denominators are recorded separately.

Neurological examination included limb rigidity and related signs. The investigators reported no signs or symptoms of neurological impairment in any diet group. Fine and gross nondominant-hand steadiness differences pointed in opposite directions on a separate instrument. Depression and aches appear on the indexed examination schedule, but arm-specific depression severity and pain severity results are not shown. The sample and manganese design leave the frozen Parkinson questions open.
