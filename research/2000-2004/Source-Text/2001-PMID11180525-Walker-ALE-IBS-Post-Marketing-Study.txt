Title: Artichoke leaf extract reduces symptoms of irritable bowel syndrome in a post-marketing surveillance study.
PMID: 11180525
DOI: 10.1002/1099-1573(200102)15:1<58::aid-ptr805>3.0.co;2-r
First published: 2001
Authors: Walker, Middleton, Petrowicz
Journal: Phytotherapy research : PTR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11180525/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

6-week uncontrolled post-marketing surveillance study evaluating standardized artichoke leaf extract (ALE) in a subgroup of dyspeptic patients with concomitant IBS symptoms. Reported significant reductions in IBS symptom severity, favorable physician/patient effectiveness ratings, very good tolerability, and 96% rating ALE equal or superior to prior therapies. Limits: observational uncontrolled post-marketing surveillance, leaf extract, abstract lacks exact sample size and numerical symptom scores.
