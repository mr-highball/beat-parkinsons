Title: Effects of medium-chain fatty acids and oleic acid on blood lipids, lipoproteins, glucose, insulin, and lipid transfer protein activities
Publication date: 2004-04
PMID: 15051598
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/79.4.564
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15051598/
Text coverage: Distilled primary abstract and corresponding 2022 review table.

In a randomized blinded crossover, 17 healthy young men replaced part of their usual fat intake with medium-chain triglycerides or sunflower oil containing 89.4% oleic acid. The contrasted outcomes concern blood lipids, glucose and related metabolic markers. MCT produced higher total, LDL and VLDL cholesterol, triglycerides and glucose than the sunflower oil period. Neither the original abstract nor the corresponding review table supplies cognitive, anxiety or Parkinson outcomes. The review's cognitive trials concern other comparisons.
