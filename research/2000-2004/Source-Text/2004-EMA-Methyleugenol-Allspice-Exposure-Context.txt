Working Party on Herbal Medicinal Products position paper, March 2004.
Public source https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/position/working-party-herbal-medicinal-products-final-position-paper-use-herbal-medicinal-products-containing-methyleugenol_en.pdf

The position paper lists Pimenta dioica leaf and Pimenta officinalis oil as separate entries in its constituent table. Its discussion of CNS effects refers to isolated methyleugenol. Species nomenclature, plant part and extraction matter when using these table entries for exposure identification. The document supplies regulatory context for the compound and leaves the two ground-allspice symptom outcomes unmeasured.
