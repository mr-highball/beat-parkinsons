Original source scope and evidence boundary
The narrative review summarizes alertness, fatigue, vigilance and withdrawal research. It describes opposed interpretations of caffeinated versus decaffeinated coffee studies, including augmentation and withdrawal relief. The 2014 animal microglia article cites this review in its introduction. The review is secondary evidence and the cited caffeine, coffee and decaf designs need comparison with the already archived cohorts before allocating individual votes for brewed coffee or Parkinson fatigue.
