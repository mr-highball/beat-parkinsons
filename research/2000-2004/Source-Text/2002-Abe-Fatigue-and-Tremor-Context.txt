Fatigue scores during dopamine agonist add-on treatment

2001–2002 date discrepancy. The volume is indexed as 2001, the original prints 2001/2002 and Wiley dates first publication September 28, 2002. Abe and colleagues compare five weeks of adjunctive pergolide in 25 and bromocriptine in 16 people receiving levodopa/carbidopa. Fatigue changed from 5.1 to 4.4 in the pergolide group (paired p < .05) and from 4.8 to 4.7 in the bromocriptine group (reported nonsignificant). The report does not clearly give a between-arm change estimate. Baseline pergolide fatigue SD is 0.7 in the prose/figure and 1.0 in Table 2. Mean doses also differ between the Methods and Table 2. Total UPDRS motor scores do not provide a separate rest-tremor result. The original does not evaluate culinary fig or apple.
