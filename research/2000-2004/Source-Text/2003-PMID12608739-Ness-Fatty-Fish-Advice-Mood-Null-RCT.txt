Title: Advice to eat fish and mood: a randomised controlled trial in men with angina
PMID: 12608739
DOI: 10.1080/1028415021000056069
First published: 2003
Authors: Ness AR, Gallacher JE, Bennett PD, Gunnell DJ, Rogers PJ, Kessler D, Burr ML
Journal: Nutritional Neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12608739/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Adversarial null mood RCT: 452 men with angina randomized to dietary advice to eat more fatty fish vs no fish advice for 6 months (83% completed Derogatis Stress Profile). Despite increased fish intake, there was NO benefit on depression (adjusted diff +1.29, 95% CI -0.29 to +2.88, non-significantly higher in fish arm) or anxiety (+0.82, 95% CI -0.57 to +2.22).
