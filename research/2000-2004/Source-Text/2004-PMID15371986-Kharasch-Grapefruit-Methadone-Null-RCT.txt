Title: Role of hepatic and intestinal cytochrome P450 3A and 2B6 in the metabolism, disposition, and miotic effects of methadone.
PMID: 15371986
DOI: 10.1016/j.clpt.2004.05.003
First published: 2004
Authors: Kharasch ED, Hoffer C, Whittington D, Sheffels P
Journal: Clinical pharmacology and therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15371986/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized 4-way crossover trial in healthy volunteers showing selective intestinal CYP3A inhibition by grapefruit juice slightly lowered EDDP/methadone ratio but had NO significant effect on methadone plasma concentrations, clearance, or clinical miotic effects (corroborated for oral transmucosal fentanyl in PMID:15329598).
