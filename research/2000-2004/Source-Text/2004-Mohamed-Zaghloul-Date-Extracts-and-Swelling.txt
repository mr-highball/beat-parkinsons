Title: In vivo evaluation of antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity of different extracts of date fruits in adjuvant arthritis
Source: https://journal.pan.olsztyn.pl/pdf-97818-30268?filename=30268.pdf
Publication: 2004, Polish Journal of Food and Nutrition Sciences 13(4), pp. 397–402
Coverage: Original publisher PDF, Methods and Tables 2–3.

The rat study compared methanol and water extracts of the edible Zaghloul date flesh and a separate methanol extract of seeds with an arthritic control. Table 2 reports 67.8%, 61.3% and 35.5% lower paw swelling, respectively. The design lists eight rats per group. The main outcome is change in paw thickness. Inflammatory and antioxidant biomarkers appear separately.

The measured changes concern swelling and laboratory markers. The study has no pain behavior endpoint, whole dried fruit comparison or Parkinson population.
