Title: Patients with vascular dementia due to microvascular pathology have significant hippocampal neuronal loss
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1737900/
Source alias: https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/72/6/747.full.pdf
PMID: 12023418
PMCID: PMC1737900
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.72.6.747
Publication date: 2002-06
Date basis: JNNP 72(6), 747–751, June issue
Text coverage: Publisher original article, pages 747–751, inspected. Research summary.

Kril and colleagues reviewed 54 cases and compared hippocampal tissue from four people with small-vessel dementia, five with Alzheimer disease and seven controls. The selection process excluded two reviewed cases with Lewy body dementia in the set with small-vessel disease and other concurrent pathology. The analyzed comparisons concern hippocampal volume and CA1 neuronal counts. The article supplies no dedicated Parkinson visual hallucination comparison or food exposure.
