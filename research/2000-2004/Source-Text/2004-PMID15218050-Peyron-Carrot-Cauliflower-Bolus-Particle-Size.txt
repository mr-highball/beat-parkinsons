Title: Particle size distribution of food boluses after mastication of six natural foods.
PMID: 15218050
DOI: 10.1177/154405910408300713
First published: 2004
Authors: Peyron MA, Mishellany A, Woda A
Journal: J Dent Res
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15218050/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Mastication physiology study in 10 healthy dentate adults chewing 3 nuts and 3 raw vegetables (carrot, cauliflower, radish) until self-estimated swallow readiness. Vegetable boluses retained much larger particle sizes than nuts with tightly conserved bolus texture targets prior to swallowing; baseline oral-processing context for raw carrot and raw cauliflower, not a PD clinical trial.
