Insomnia and falls in two multidrug Lewy body cases
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8353899_Alleviation_of_multiple_abnormalities_by_galantamine_treatment_in_two_patients_with_dementia_with_Lewy_bodies

July–August 2004 issue. The complete author copy on pages 216–217 describes resolved insomnia in the first case after multiple medicines were given. The second patient had unsteady gait, falls and insomnia at presentation. The report describes sleep at discharge and no further falls noted amid concurrent medication changes including galantamine, quetiapine, sertraline and melatonin. It supplies no fall-rate estimate, isolated comparator, or sage ingestion.
