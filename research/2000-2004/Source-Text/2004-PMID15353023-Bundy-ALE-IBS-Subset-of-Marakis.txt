Title: Artichoke leaf extract reduces symptoms of irritable bowel syndrome and improves quality of life in otherwise healthy volunteers suffering from concomitant dyspepsia: a subset analysis.
PMID: 15353023
DOI: 10.1089/acm.2004.10.667
First published: 2004
Authors: Bundy, Walker, Middleton, Marakis, Booth
Journal: Journal of alternative and complementary medicine (New York, N.Y.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15353023/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Post-hoc subset analysis of 208 adults with concomitant IBS drawn from the Marakis 2002 open dose-ranging ALE trial (same cohort family as PMID 12587688; do not count independently). After 2 months of standardized ALE (320 or 640 mg/d), IBS incidence fell 26.4% (p < 0.001), self-reported usual bowel pattern shifted significantly away from alternating constipation/diarrhea toward normal (p < 0.001), NDI total symptom score fell 41% (p < 0.001), and NDI QoL improved 20%. Limits: uncontrolled before-after subset of open trial, leaf extract, pooled bowel pattern rather than isolated constipation severity.
