Hallucination history and motor signs in a Lewy pathology cohort
PMID 12810766
DOI 10.1136/jnnp.74.7.852
https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/74/7/852.full.pdf

July 2003. Colosimo and colleagues selected 38 patients from 276 brains with Parkinson pathology. Visual hallucinations were recorded at some point in 24 of 38 patients, including 8 of 17 without recorded cognitive impairment and 16 of 21 with late dementia. Twenty-nine patients had resting tremor and all had akinesia and rigidity. The full case histories and Table 1 give clinical occurrence without episode frequencies or a quantified bradykinesia comparison. Salmon is a cited author surname. No salmon food exposure was evaluated.
