Title: Cognitive performance is associated with glucose regulation in healthy elderly persons and can be enhanced with glucose and dietary carbohydrates.
PMID: 10966906
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/72.3.825
First published: 2000
Authors: Kaplan RJ, Greenwood CE, Winocur G, Wolever TM
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10966906/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

4-morning randomized crossover trial in n=20 healthy elderly adults (10 M, 10 F, age 60-82 y) consuming 50 g carbohydrate as glucose, potatoes, or boiled barley vs placebo after an overnight fast, with cognitive testing at 15, 60, and 105 min. Overall cognitive performance did NOT differ across the test foods. Poor baseline memory or beta-cell function correlated with carbohydrate-associated improvement on paragraph recall and visuomotor tasks for all three carbohydrates (r > -0.50, p<0.03), but explicitly NOT on the attention task. Limits: no main effect of barley vs placebo and null on attention; non-PD cohort.
