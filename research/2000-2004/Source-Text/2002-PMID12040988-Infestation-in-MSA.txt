Title: Delusional ectoparasitic infestation in multiple system atrophy
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12040988/
PMID: 12040988
DOI: 10.1007/s00115-002-1273-8
Publication date: 2002-04
Date basis: Der Nervenarzt 73(4), 380–383, April issue per NLM. The 2009 review's reference instead gave 378–381.
Source depth: Primary author abstract. The Springer PDF URL returned HTML; the article body is pending.
Format: Original-source factual extract

Kumbier and Kornhuber described delusional infestation as a possible early presentation of multiple system atrophy. The reported improvement concerned a fixed infestation delusion under olanzapine. The abstract discusses how non-medication-associated hallucinations affect the diagnostic distinction. It provides no confirmed Parkinson cohort, defined visual episode frequency or dried-fig consumption data.
