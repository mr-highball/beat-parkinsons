Title: Absorption of kaempferol from endive, a source of kaempferol-3-glucuronide, in humans.
PMID: 15164116
DOI: 10.1038/sj.ejcn.1601916
First published: 2004
Authors: DuPont MS, Day AJ, Bennett RN, Mellon FA, Kroon PA
Journal: European journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15164116/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract Facts: 24-hour human pharmacokinetic study in 8 healthy adults (4M, 4F) fed endive (Cichorium endivia) supplying 9 mg kaempferol. Kaempferol reached mean plasma Cmax 0.1 uM at Tmax 5.8 h (predominantly as kaempferol-3-glucuronide; 1.9% urinary excretion over 24 h), indicating absorption from distal small intestine/colon. Human bioavailability study only; measured zero clinical or neurological symptom outcomes.
