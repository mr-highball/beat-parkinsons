Title: Occipital hypoperfusion in Parkinson's disease without dementia: correlation to impaired cortical visual processing
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1738406/
Source alias: https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/74/4/419.full.pdf
PMID: 12640053
PMCID: PMC1738406
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.74.4.419
Publication date: 2003-04
Date basis: NLM and the publisher DOI deposit identify the April issue, 74(4), 419–422.
Text coverage: Publisher original article, pages 419–422, inspected. Research summary.

Abe and colleagues compared 28 Parkinson participants without dementia with 17 controls. The full eligibility methods explicitly exclude patients with visual hallucinations. A subset of 25 Parkinson participants and 14 controls completed Raven's coloured progressive matrices. The study reports lower perfusion in bilateral occipital and posterior parietal regions and a correlation between matrix scores and perfusion in a right visual association area within the Parkinson group. The outcome concerns visual reasoning and perfusion. The exclusion prevents using this comparison to estimate visual episode frequency. No dietary exposure was measured.
