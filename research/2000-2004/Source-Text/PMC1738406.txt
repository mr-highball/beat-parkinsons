Title: Occipital hypoperfusion in Parkinson's disease without dementia: correlation to impaired cortical visual processing
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1738406/
PMID: 12640053
PMCID: PMC1738406
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.74.4.419
Publication date: 2003-04
Date basis: NLM and the publisher DOI deposit identify the April issue, 74(4), 419–422.
Source depth: Primary author abstract. Complete article remains pending.

Abe and colleagues compared 28 Parkinson participants without dementia with 17 controls. They reported lower blood flow in bilateral occipital and posterior parietal regions. In the Parkinson group a visual reasoning score correlated with perfusion in a right visual association area. The reported measure is performance on Raven's coloured progressive matrices. The abstract gives no event frequency for visual hallucinations and no fig comparison. The original full methods and symptom coding remain open.
