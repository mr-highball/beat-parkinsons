Pain and fatigue ratings during a brain stimulation comparison
2001 · PMID 11459878 · PMC6762641 · DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.21-15-j0003.2001
https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/21/15/RC157.full.pdf
Factual extract from complete original four-page article, Methods, Results and Table 2.

Strafella and colleagues assessed eight healthy adults during prefrontal and occipital stimulation sessions. The main comparison uses PET dopamine binding. Table 2 separately tabulates fatigued-to-rested ratings and a pain-to-no-pain item before and after stimulation. Occipital fatigue ratings are 2.00 to 2.16 and prefrontal 1.25 to 2.08. Occipital pain ratings are 1.63 to 1.75 and prefrontal 1.13 to 1.50. The original outcome table concerns acute stimulation in healthy adults. These ratings provide symptom context without a cherry or date exposure.
