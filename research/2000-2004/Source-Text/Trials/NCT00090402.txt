Title: Fish Oil and Alpha Lipoic Acid in Treating Alzheimer's Disease
ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT00090402
First posted: 2004-08-26 (estimated). Results first posted: 2010-02-26 (estimated).
Primary registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00090402
API record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00090402
Coverage: Selected registry identity and exposure fields. Native official download verified. Posted results are summarized in the separate 2010 snapshot.

Population: 39 participants with Alzheimer's disease.
Design: Three randomized groups of 13, followed for 12 months.
Interventions: Soybean-oil placebo capsule plus lipoic-acid placebo, fish-oil concentrate plus lipoic-acid placebo, or fish-oil concentrate plus alpha-lipoic acid.
Composition clarified in results and 2013 primary article: The oil placebo contained soybean oil mixed with 5% fish oil and lemon flavor.
Linked paper: Shinto et al., PMID 24077434, DOI 10.3233/JAD-130722.
