BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a database study. Medical records will be reviewed in order to document a new or
pending diagnosis of Parkinson's disease and in order to learn more about the disease
process. Other than the time that it takes to complete a medical record authorization
form, no other human subject participation will be needed.
