Title: Fresh and dried fig extract cross-reactivity in clinical fig allergy
PMID: 12859455
DOI: 10.1046/j.1365-2222.2003.01704.x
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12859455/
First publication: 2003-07.
Coverage: Original investigator abstract. Individual clinical histories and full methods remain open.

Five people with oral allergy syndrome or anaphylaxis after fig ingestion and one with symptoms from Ficus benjamina exposure were assessed. In the tested sera, dried Ficus carica extract inhibited binding to fig material by 63–97%, and fresh extract by more than 95%. These laboratory percentages express IgE cross-reactivity. They do not count how many people experienced symptoms after each preparation. This paper links sensitivity to fresh and dried fig and gives no clinical visual hallucination frequency.
