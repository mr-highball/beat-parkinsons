Title: Effects of barley intake on glucose tolerance, lipid metabolism, and bowel function in women.
PMID: 14624940
DOI: 10.1016/s0899-9007(03)00182-5
First published: 2003
Authors: Li J, Kaneko T, Qin LQ, Wang J, Wang Y
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14624940/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

4-week randomized crossover trial (1-month washout) in n=10 healthy young women (mean age 20.4+/-1.3 y) comparing a high-barley high-fiber diet against a macronutrient-matched standard diet. Barley intake significantly increased stool volume and significantly lowered total cholesterol, LDL-C, and triglycerides, with no difference in glucose tolerance. Limits: small sample (n=10), healthy young non-PD women.
