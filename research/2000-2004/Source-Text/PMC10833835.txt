The study followed 68 outpatients across five dementia categories without a parallel control group. The Parkinson dementia group comprised three patients. The paper reports a 45% visual-hallucination figure linked to behavioral subitems, without a denominator that can be mapped to an integer participant count in that group. Treat it as the reported figure rather than a verified participant proportion.

The authors report improvement in hallucinations, delusions and anxiety during follow-up in the Parkinson dementia group, using behavioral ratings. They provide no separately counted visual episode rate. The medication exposure, small subgroup and absent parallel control limit interpretation. The study does not evaluate dried figs.
