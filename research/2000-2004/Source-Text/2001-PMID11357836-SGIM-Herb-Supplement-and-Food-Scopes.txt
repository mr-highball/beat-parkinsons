The official supplement covers printed pp. 23–233. A survey of 20 online herbal weight-loss products found ginger listed in nine (45%), as recorded in the table on p. 115. It catalogs labels rather than administering the products or measuring patient outcomes. The neighboring end-of-life survey measures pain and anxiety in different participants. Its results cannot be attributed to ginger.

A separate reflux survey lists avoidance of grouped trigger foods including coffee. Caffeine is used as a medication in other case material. A dietary history documents avoidance of animal products including eggs, and another member refers to reproductive egg donation. Salmon-colored rash is a clinical description. BARLEY is an author surname in an education abstract. None identifies a symptom effect after the frozen preparations.
