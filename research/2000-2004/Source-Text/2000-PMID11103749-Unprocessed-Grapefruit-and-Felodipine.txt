Title: Grapefruit-felodipine interaction: effect of unprocessed fruit and probable active ingredients
PMID: 11103749
DOI: 10.1067/mcp.2000.110774
Publication date: 2000-11
Citation: Bailey DG, Dresser GK, Kreeft JH, Munoz C, Freeman DJ and Bend JR. Clinical Pharmacology and Therapeutics 68:468–477.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11103749/?dopt=Abstract
Coverage: Factual extract from the author abstract. Full intervention tables and analyzed denominator remain unverified in this extract.

The randomized crossover compared felodipine pharmacokinetics with water, commercial grapefruit juice, homogenized fresh segments, and an extract of the parts remaining after segments were removed. Across the three grapefruit preparations, the abstract reports felodipine exposure of about three times the water comparison on average. Peak concentration rose and half-life was unchanged. The groups of grapefruit compounds varied by preparation. This is measured fruit-preparation context for felodipine. Cabergoline, levodopa and resting tremor were outside the comparison.
