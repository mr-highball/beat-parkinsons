Title: Anaphylactic reaction to the ingestion of raw onion. A case report
Authors: A Arena, C Cislaghi and P Falagiani
Primary journal PDF: https://www.elsevier.es/en-revista-allergologia-et-immunopathologia-105-pdf-download-10017984
Identifiers: PMID 11270091
Date: September–October 2000, volume 28 issue 5, pages 287–289
Coverage: factual extract of the full original case and test format

A 44-year-old woman recalled repeated immediate reactions after raw or lightly cooked onion, with tolerance of well-cooked onion and symptoms from raw contact or vapors. Her serum onion-specific IgE was 3.7 kU/L. In vitro, her serum reacted to unheated extract, whereas onion extract heated for 30 minutes at 100 °C did not elicit a positive result. The researchers avoided live oral or skin provocation with onion because of her previous reactions. The study reports allergy rather than speech or Parkinson disease.
