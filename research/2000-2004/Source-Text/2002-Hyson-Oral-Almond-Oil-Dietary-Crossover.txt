Oral almond oil and whole almonds in a dietary crossover
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11925464/
PMID: 11925464
DOI: 10.1093/jn/132.4.703
Coverage: factual extract from original article and dietary comparisons.

Hyson and colleagues analyzed 22 healthy adults who completed both six-week crossover periods, after two withdrawals. Whole almonds or almond oil replaced part of the usual dietary fat. The oil and dry-roasted almonds came from the same California nonpareil almond lot. Mean oil provision was 35 ± 2 g/day, consumed in a beverage. Both periods produced similar measured plasma lipid changes and neither changed the study’s indices of LDL oxidation. The extraction grade is unreported. A footnote on p. 703 explicitly links a 1999 FASEB meeting abstract from the same study. The abstract is an open source trail. Parkinson disease, medication response and the four target symptoms were outside the trial.
