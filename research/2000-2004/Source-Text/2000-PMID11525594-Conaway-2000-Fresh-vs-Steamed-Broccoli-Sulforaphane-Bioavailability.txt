Title: Disposition of glucosinolates and sulforaphane in humans after ingestion of steamed and fresh broccoli.
PMID: 11525594
DOI: 10.1207/S15327914NC382_5
First published: 2000
Authors: Conaway, Getahun, Liebes
Journal: Nutrition and cancer
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11525594/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled crossover trial in n=12 healthy men ingesting 200 g fresh (raw) vs steamed broccoli after 48-h glucosinolate washout. Pre-hydrolysis ITC content was virtually identical (1.1 vs 1.0 umol/g wet wt), but 24-h urinary ITC recovery was 32.3 +/- 12.7% for fresh broccoli vs 10.2 +/- 5.9% for steamed broccoli (urinary SFN-NAC 25.8 +/- 13.9 vs 6.9 +/- 2.5 umol). Demonstrates ~3x greater sulforaphane bioavailability from raw broccoli with active plant myrosinase, while cooked/steamed broccoli still yields ~1/3 bioavailability via gut microbial hydrolysis.
