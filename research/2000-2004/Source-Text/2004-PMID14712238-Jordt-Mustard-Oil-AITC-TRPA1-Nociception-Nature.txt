Title: Mustard oils and cannabinoids excite sensory nerve fibres through the TRP channel ANKTM1
PMID: 14712238
DOI: 10.1038/nature02282
First published: 2004
Authors: Jordt SE, Bautista DM, Chuang HH, McKemy DD, Zygmunt PM, Högestätt ED
Journal: Nature
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14712238/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Landmark mechanistic study demonstrating that allyl isothiocyanate (AITC, the pungent mustard oil liberated from sinigrin upon grating horseradish root) directly depolarizes primary nociceptive sensory neurons by activating TRPA1 (ANKTM1), mediating acute local burning pain, neurogenic inflammation, and thermal/mechanical hypersensitivity. Mechanistic nociception study; does not assess systemic chronic PD pain.
