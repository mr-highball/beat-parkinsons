Visual hallucination prevalence in dementia with Lewy bodies
PMID 11971046
DOI 10.1136/jnnp.72.5.602
https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/72/5/602.full.pdf

May 2002. Doubleday and colleagues report current visual hallucinations in 71% of 41 selected dementia-with-Lewy-bodies cases. They excluded cases whose hallucinations coincided with changes in antiparkinsonian medication. None of the 26 selected Alzheimer’s disease comparators had a history of visual hallucinations. Pages 602–603 concern symptom prevalence and eligibility. They give no episode frequency or fig or salmon food comparison. The indexed original journal PDF supplied the full report.
