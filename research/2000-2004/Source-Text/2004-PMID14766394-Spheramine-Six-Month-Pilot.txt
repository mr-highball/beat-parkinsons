Title: Implantation of Spheramine in advanced Parkinson's disease (PD)
Source: https://www.imrpress.com/journal/FBL/9/1/10.2741/1217
PMID: 14766394
DOI: 10.2741/1217
Publication date: 2004-01-01
Date basis: On the journal page and printed in Frontiers in Bioscience 9, 592–602
Source depth: Complete publisher PDF, 11 pages
Format: Original-source factual extract

Bakay and colleagues reported the six-month follow-up of six adults with advanced Parkinson disease in an open-label unilateral implantation pilot. They assessed motor measures and drew safety information from scheduled assessments and elicited adverse events. The results report one small asymptomatic hemorrhage without neurological deficit. The inspected six-month report does not give a specific visual hallucination count. The subsequent primary report from the same pilot does, so silence in the earlier publication is not evidence of zero visual events.

The later 2008 review cites this report and the 2005 primary report for the same pilot. See NCT00761436 for the distinct single-group registry record. The study does not measure dried-fig intake.
