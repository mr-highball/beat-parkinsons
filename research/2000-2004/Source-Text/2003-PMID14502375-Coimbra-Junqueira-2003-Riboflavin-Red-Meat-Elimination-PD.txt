Title: High doses of riboflavin and the elimination of dietary red meat promote the recovery of some motor functions in Parkinson's disease patients.
PMID: 14502375
DOI: 10.1590/s0100-879x2003001000019
First published: 2003
Authors: Coimbra CG, Junqueira VB
Journal: Brazilian journal of medical and biological research = Revista brasileira de pesquisas medicas e biologicas
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14502375/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Uncontrolled open-label study of 31 sporadic PD patients (19 completed 6 months) given high-dose riboflavin (30 mg every 8 h = 90 mg/d) simultaneously with elimination of all red meat: mean modified Hoehn & Yahr motor capacity rose from 44% to 71% (p<0.001). Severely confounded by simultaneous high-dose riboflavin, lack of control arm, and 39% attrition (attribution to red meat elimination unusable).
