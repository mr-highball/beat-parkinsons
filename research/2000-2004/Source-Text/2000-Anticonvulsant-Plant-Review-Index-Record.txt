The 2005 cumulative index identifies Melissa officinalis at volume 22, page 526, under epilepsy/hysteria. That is an index statement, not a verified original treatment, preparation or response. Historical hysteria terminology must not be equated with a measured modern depression outcome.

Publisher body access remained unavailable through the checked routes; a university-hosted PDF was a different chapter and was rejected as a substitute. Keep the original page, botanical context and cited primary experiments open. No clinical effect or independent cohort is inferred.
