Title: Aromas of rosemary and lavender essential oils differentially affect cognition and mood in healthy adults.
PMID: 12690999
DOI: 10.1080/00207450390161903
First published: 2003
Authors: Moss M, Cook J, Wesnes K, Duckett P
Journal: The International journal of neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12690999/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled trial (Moss et al., 2003, Int J Neurosci) in 144 healthy adults assigned to ambient inhalation of Lavandula angustifolia oil, rosemary oil, or odorless control during the CDR computerized cognitive battery. Inhaled lavender aroma produced decrements in working memory performance and slower reaction times on memory and attention tasks vs control. Non-oral ambient olfactory exposure cannot transfer to ingested culinary lavender.
