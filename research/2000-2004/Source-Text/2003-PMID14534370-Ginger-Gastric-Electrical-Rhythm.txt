Title: Healthy adult ginger root experiment and gastric electrical rhythm
PMID: 14534370
DOI: 10.1124/jpet.103.053421
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14534370/
Published: 2003-10-08
Archive scope: Factual note from the investigators' publicly indexed primary abstract.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The investigators' abstract reports 22 healthy volunteers and blinded ginger root or placebo under experimentally elevated blood sugar. The mean percentage of recording time in the normal electrical frequency band during the challenge was 77.3% after ginger and 66.0% after placebo. Ginger also reduced the induced change in tachygastria. A separate challenge with misoprostol showed no clear ginger effect against placebo. These measurements are derived from gastric electrical activity. They do not establish a gastric-emptying or constipation effect. Preparation details and full tables remain open.
