Soybean oil versus butter and gastric retention
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10652918/
selected original methods and findings paraphrase

Published in 2000. Twenty-seven functional dyspepsia patients received solid meals with soybean oil or butter on separate days. Reported retention at 90 minutes was 0.600 versus 0.648, paired p > .05. The abstract inconsistently labels one mention of the retention variable. There was no no-fat arm, refined-oil verification, levodopa or Parkinson daily OFF measurement.
