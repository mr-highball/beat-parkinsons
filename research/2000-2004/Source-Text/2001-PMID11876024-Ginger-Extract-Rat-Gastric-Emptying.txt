Title: Acetone ginger extract and delayed gastric emptying in rats
PMID: 11876024
DOI: 10.1358/mf.2001.23.9.662137
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11876024/
Published: 2001-11
Archive scope: Factual note from the investigators' publicly indexed primary abstract.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The author abstract reports that two higher amounts of an acetone ginger extract improved emptying of a test meal in rats under a chemically induced delay. It also reports a separate combination with antioxidant vitamins. The measured endpoint is gastric emptying in an experimental model. Abdominal discomfort and bloating are mentioned in interpretation, with no measured patient severity. The abstract provides no colonic stool endpoint, grated-ginger comparison, Parkinson group or complete tables.
