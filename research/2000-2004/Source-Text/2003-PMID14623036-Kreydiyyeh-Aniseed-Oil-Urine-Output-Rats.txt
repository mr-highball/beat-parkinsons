Title: Aniseed oil increases glucose absorption and reduces urine output in the rat.
PMID: 14623036
DOI: 10.1016/j.lfs.2003.07.013
First published: 2003
Authors: Kreydiyyeh SI, Usta J, Knio K, Markossian S, Dagher S
Journal: Life sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14623036/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical physiological transport study in rats using hydrodistilled Pimpinella anisum essential oil. Aniseed oil increased jejunal glucose absorption and Na+-K+ ATPase activity, had NO effect on colonic water absorption or colonic Na+-K+ ATPase, and when added to drinking water reduced urine volume while increasing renal Na+-K+ ATPase activity (antidiuretic mechanism). Measures physiological renal urine volume in healthy rats rather than clinical overactive bladder or urinary urgency frequency.
