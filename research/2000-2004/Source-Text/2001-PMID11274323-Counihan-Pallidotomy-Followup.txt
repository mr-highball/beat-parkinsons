Title: Outcomes following staged bilateral pallidotomy in advanced Parkinson disease
Source: https://www.neurology.org/doi/10.1212/WNL.56.6.799
PMID: 11274323
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.56.6.799
Publication date: 2001-03-27
Source depth: Original author abstract and publisher issue.

The Massachusetts General Hospital series assessed 14 patients with advanced Parkinson disease up to one year after staged bilateral surgery. The abstract reports a 67% reduction in motor off time and a 24% reduction in off-state activities-of-daily-living scores. It lists visual-field and transient-confusion events. A drooling-specific outcome is unreported at this depth. This is separate from the UCLA series reporting four saliva-loss events.
