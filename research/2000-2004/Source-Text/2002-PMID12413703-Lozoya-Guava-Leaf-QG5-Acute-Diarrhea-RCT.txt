Title: Intestinal anti-spasmodic effect of a phytodrug of Psidium guajava folia in the treatment of acute diarrheic disease
PMID: 12413703
DOI: 10.1016/s0378-8741(02)00185-x
First published: 2002
Authors: Lozoya X, Reyes-Morales H, Chávez-Soto MA, Martínez-García Mdel C, Soto-González Y, Doubova SV
Journal: Journal of Ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12413703/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Three-day double-blind RCT in adult patients with acute diarrheic disease (50 active / 50 placebo) administering oral QG-5 capsules (500 mg q8h standardized guava LEAF extract, 1 mg quercetin equivalent). Reduced duration of acute diarrheal abdominal pain/spasm without significant changes in liquid stool frequency or consistency vs placebo (per Daswani 2017 summary). Strictly guava LEAF extract in acute infectious diarrhea, NOT raw guava fruit or PD pain/constipation.
