2001 combined extract and knee osteoarthritis

Altman and Marcussen randomized 261 patients with knee osteoarthritis and moderate-to-severe pain to a concentrated standardized extract of Z. officinale and Alpinia galanga or placebo. Among 247 evaluable participants, standing-pain responders were 63% versus 50% (p=.048) after six weeks. Mean standing pain fell by 24.5 versus 16.4 mm (p=.005), while gastrointestinal adverse events were more frequent with extract. The two-botanical formulation and osteoarthritis endpoint limit transfer to the frozen exposure and population. The primary abstract was verified. Original trial abstract.
