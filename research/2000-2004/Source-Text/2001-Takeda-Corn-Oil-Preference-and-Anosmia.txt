Corn oil preference in mice with induced smell loss
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11487096/
PMID 11487096. DOI 10.1016/S0024-3205(01)01180-8.
Coverage: original author abstract factual extract.

Published July 6, 2001. Takeda and colleagues compared corn-oil preference in mice with normal or experimentally blocked olfaction. Both groups showed conditioned preference for a place associated with undiluted corn oil. In a two-bottle test, normal mice preferred concentrations from 1% to 10%, while 1% and 3% were not preferred in the model group. Smell loss preceded the oil-choice test. The study measured food preference and does not test whether eating a refined oil changes the severity of smell loss. The author abstract leaves arm denominators and processing method open.
