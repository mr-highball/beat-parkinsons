Sunflower oil placebo in a lemon balm agitation trial
Source: https://www.psychiatrist.com/jcp/aromatherapy-safe-effective-treatment-management-agitation/
PMID: 12143909
DOI: 10.4088/JCP.v63n0703
Coverage: Original journal results summary and PubMed author abstract

Ballard and colleagues assigned 72 residents with severe dementia and clinically important agitation to topical lemon balm essential oil or sunflower oil placebo. At four weeks, at least a 30 percent decrease on the Cohen-Mansfield Agitation Inventory was reported in 21 of 35 assessed participants in the active arm and 5 of 36 in the comparator. The article reports 35 percent and 11 percent mean reductions, respectively. The contrast varies the topical preparation and contains no sunflower-free untreated arm. It gives no oral refined sunflower oil, Parkinson anxiety or depression result.
