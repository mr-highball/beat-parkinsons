The clinical review discusses reported visual hallucinations in Parkinson disease and how medication exposure may contribute to psychotic symptoms. It mentions differing medication outcomes and points to underlying studies. It does not provide a new controlled visual-frequency experiment or a comparison of dried figs. The observations synthesized here cannot be counted as additional independent patients.
