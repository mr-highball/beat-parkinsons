Title: Visual hallucinations in Lewy body disease relate to Lewy bodies in the temporal lobe
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11844739/
PMID: 11844739
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awf033
Publication date: 2002-02
Date basis: Journal issue month; original abstract
Source depth: Primary author abstract; body remains open
Evidence type: Clinicopathological cohort with visual phenotype
Format: Factual digest of the inspected source

The study used longitudinal clinical records and regional pathology in 63 cases with Lewy bodies, including 29 DLB cases, 18 Parkinson dementia cases and 16 with parkinsonism without dementia. Well-formed visual hallucinations were associated with Lewy body burden in the amygdala and parahippocampus. The visual phenotype and its timing differ from an event-frequency response to food.
