Title: Rivastigmine in the treatment of parkinsonian psychosis and cognitive impairment: preliminary findings from an open trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11748755/
PMID: 11748755
DOI: 10.1002/mds.1204
Publication date: 2001-11
Date basis: Movement Disorders November issue
Source depth: Primary author abstract. Publisher direct request returned HTTP 403.
Format: Original-source factual extract

The uncontrolled rivastigmine study included 12 people with advanced Parkinson disease. The authors reported improvement in hallucinations, sleep disturbance, caregiver distress and cognition. The abstract does not specify a visual episode count, capture time or placebo comparison. This is a medication study without a dried-fig arm.
