Cumin seed aqueous extract and wet stool output
https://pjsir.org/multidisciplinary-archive/Volume%2045%202002/Issue%204/Article%209%20Vol%2045%20Issue%204%202002%20pp%20256-258.pdf
selected original methods and findings paraphrase

Published in 2002, volume 45 issue 4. In intact young rats with four per group, both oral aqueous seed-extract groups produced more wet stool than saline. Figure 1 shows approximately six for the lower extract arm and eight to nine for the higher arm, with the control curve at zero. The small preclinical extract experiment gives no verified whole-ground-spice dose or Parkinson constipation severity.
