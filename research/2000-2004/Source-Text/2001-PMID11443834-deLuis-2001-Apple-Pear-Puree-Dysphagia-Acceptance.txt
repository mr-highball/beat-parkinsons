Title: [Evaluation of a dessert in patients with deglutition changes, one more step in advanced basic feeding].
PMID: 11443834
First published: 2001
Authors: de Luis DA, Cabezas G, Rojo S, Terroba C, Aller R, Izaola O et al.
Journal: Nutricion hospitalaria
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11443834/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Hospital acceptability survey of n=47 patients with dysphagia (including n=19 with chronic neurological disease: Alzheimer, vascular dementia, and Parkinson disease) evaluating organoleptic acceptance of a commercial instant stewed apple-and-pear pureed dessert. Measured palatability/acceptance only; did not evaluate swallowing safety or efficacy vs comparator.
