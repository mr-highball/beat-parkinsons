Kreydiyyeh, Usta and Copti. Food and Chemical Toxicology, 2000.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10930696/
Access and coverage: Original author abstract and exposed text of the article reproduction. No human comparison was reported.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

Aqueous clove and cinnamon extracts inhibited Na+/K+-ATPase activity in rat jejunal preparations and reduced intestinal alanine absorption. The absorption substrate was alanine. Neither levodopa pharmacokinetics nor the duration of motor OFF periods was measured. This assay leaves the effects of ground clove in a meal unresolved.
