Title: Effects of repeated ingestion of grapefruit juice on the single and multiple oral-dose pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of alprazolam.
PMID: 10907671
DOI: 10.1007/s002130000438
First published: 2000
Authors: Yasui N, Kondo T, Furukori H, Kaneko S, Ohkubo T, Uno T, Osanai T, Sugawara K
Journal: Psychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10907671/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Two-study clinical investigation (8 healthy volunteers single-dose 0.8 mg + 11 anxiety patients steady-state 0.8-2.4 mg/day) consuming 600 mL/day grapefruit juice for 7-10 days. Grapefruit juice altered NEITHER alprazolam plasma levels NOR psychomotor function NOR clinical status, reflecting alprazolam high inherent oral bioavailability. Important substrate-specific null comparator.
