Title: Macadamia nut toxicosis in dogs
Publication date: 2002-04
Citation: Steven R. Hansen. Veterinary Medicine 97(4), April 2002.
Source: https://www.aspcapro.org/sites/default/files/x-toxbrief_0402.pdf
Provider key: AGR:IND23274061
Text coverage: Original author article, Figure 1, animal exposure text and two case narratives.

The article reviews 48 calls to the same poison center from 1987 to 2001 and describes two subsequent call records, one from 2000 and one from 2001. The later dog had owner-reported tremor with ataxia and breathing difficulty. Kernel consumption was reported. The article also revisits four dogs experimentally exposed to roasted kernels in the author's 2000 study (PMID 10670081). The dogs became weak and had difficulty standing. The author reports no observed tremor in that four-dog experiment and offers muscle weakness as a possible explanation for the field tremor reports.

Treat the overlapping poison-center series and repeated four-dog experiment as one lineage for this claim. The observations involved nuts, a nonhuman acute toxicity setting and no recorded rest-tremor phenotype. The report gives no comparison with expressed macadamia oil.
