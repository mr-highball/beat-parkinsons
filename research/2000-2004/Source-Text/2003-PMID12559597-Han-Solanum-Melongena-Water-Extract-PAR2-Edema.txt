Title: The aqueous extract of Solanum melongena inhibits PAR2 agonist-induced inflammation.
PMID: 12559597
DOI: 10.1016/s0009-8981(02)00377-7
First published: 2003
Authors: Han SW, Tae J, Kim JA, Kim DK, Seo GS, Yun KJ
Journal: Clinica chimica acta; international journal of clinical chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12559597/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Mouse inflammation model administering oral Solanum melongena water extract (SMWE; 1-100 mg/kg) 1 h prior to PAR2-agonist hindpaw injection. Doses of 5-100 mg/kg inhibited paw edema, vascular permeability, MPO activity, and TNF-alpha. Measured acute inflammatory paw edema only — no behavioral nociception/pain threshold test, no cooked whole eggplant, and no PD model.
