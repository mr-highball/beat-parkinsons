Factual extract from original JNNP pp. 315–324 including Table 1.

The study compares handwriting adjustment under experimental distortion of visual feedback across Parkinson, older-control and younger-control groups. Table 1 for the 11 Parkinson participants distinguishes baseline presence of action tremor from tremor at rest. Stroke size and adaptation are the outcomes measured during the experiment. Baseline symptom flags do not quantify resting-tremor severity, and visual-feedback conditions do not measure hallucination frequency. The named food was outside the reported experimental exposure.
