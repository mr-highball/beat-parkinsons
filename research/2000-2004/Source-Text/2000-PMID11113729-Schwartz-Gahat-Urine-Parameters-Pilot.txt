Title: Gahat: a Napalese cure for urolithiasis?
PMID: 11113729
DOI: 10.1016/s0090-4295(00)00840-2
First published: 2000
Authors: Schwartz BF, Schenkman N, Nguyen R, Stoller ML
Journal: Urology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11113729/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Uncontrolled 2-day before-after study (Schwartz et al., 2000, Urology) in 8 healthy non-stone-forming volunteers ingesting 8 oz pureed 'Gahat' (labeled Vigna unguiculata, though Nepalese Gahat commonly refers to horse gram Macrotyloma uniflorum) three times daily. No statistically significant changes occurred in 24-hour urine pH, calcium, citrate, phosphate, sodium, oxalate, uric acid, magnesium, or volume (slight volume rise attributed to added fluid intake). Limits: tiny uncontrolled 2-day sample (n=8), botanical identity uncertainty, and no urinary urgency frequency outcome.
