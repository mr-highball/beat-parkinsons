Title: Long-term evaluation of the effect of quetiapine on hallucinations, delusions and motor function in advanced Parkinson disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15090935/
PMID: 15090935
DOI: 10.1097/00002826-200401000-00010
Publication date: 2004-01
Date basis: Clinical Neuropharmacology January–February issue
Source depth: Primary author abstract verified with the publisher abstract
Format: Original-source factual extract

The uncontrolled study enrolled 35 people with Parkinson disease and disabling psychiatric symptoms and assessed them at 1, 3 and 12 months after starting quetiapine. The authors report improvement in the psychiatric assessment and distinguish hallucinations from delusions. The abstract does not disaggregate visual modality or report a separately counted episode frequency. Dopaminergic regimens often changed over follow-up. No dried-fig exposure was compared.
