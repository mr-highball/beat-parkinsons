Tremor scores after thalamotomy and publication lineage
PMID 12271163
DOI 10.3348/kjr.2002.3.3.180
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2713882/

July–September 2002 issue. Baik and colleagues assessed 15 people with medically refractory parkinsonian tremor before and about four months after unilateral thalamotomy. On the 0–4 tremor scale, three patients scored 2, ten scored 3 and two scored 4 before surgery. On follow-up thirteen scored 0 and two scored 1. Scale anchors include action and postural tremor, so they do not isolate rest tremor. The paper reports imaging measures and does not quantify rigidity or examine edible fig or sage. The 2007 journal notice identifies the 2003 European Journal of Radiology paper as the same study.
