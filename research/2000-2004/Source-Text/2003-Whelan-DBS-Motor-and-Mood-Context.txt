Baseline bradykinesia and mood review before stimulation
PMID 14617713
DOI 10.1136/jnnp.74.11.1543
https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/74/11/1543.full.pdf

November 2003 issue. The original PDF states first published November 14. Whelan and colleagues compare language test performance in five stimulation patients and 16 nonsurgical Parkinson’s disease controls. Table 1 lists bradykinesia as the leading motor symptom in two of the five surgical patients. The baseline evaluation reported no indication of anxiety or depression in the surgical patients. There were no anxiety severity estimates or food comparisons, and the treatment outcomes were linguistic rather than bradykinesia scores. Source pages 1544–1545.
