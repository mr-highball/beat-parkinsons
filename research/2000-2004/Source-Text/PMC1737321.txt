Title: Pentagon copying is more impaired in dementia with Lewy bodies than in Alzheimer's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11254771/
Publisher: https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/70/4/483
PMID: 11254771

Source depth: Primary author abstract and indexed publisher methods and discussion excerpts. Complete article remains pending.
Format: Original-source factual extract with access boundary

Ala and colleagues examined available cognitive screening records for patients with subsequently confirmed dementia with Lewy bodies or Alzheimer disease. The first qualifying screening scores represented mild-to-moderate dementia. Using original grading criteria, 2/17 Lewy body patients and 16/27 Alzheimer patients made acceptable interlocking-pentagon copies. The authors discussed impairment in visuospatial construction as a potential additional diagnostic feature. That comparison measures task performance. The accessible sections do not establish a dried-fig exposure or a visual hallucination episode count.
