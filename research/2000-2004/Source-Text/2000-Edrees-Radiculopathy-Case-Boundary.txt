Factual extract from original case p. 586 and answers pp. 591–592.

The assigned Edrees and Lee case describes leg weakness and pain before diagnosis of ruptured aortic aneurysm. Its report has no fall-frequency count or examination of resting tremor. Sweetened orange juice and a resting-tremor finding occur in different cases printed in the same collection. The exact title, answer pages and authors separate those terms from this patient.
