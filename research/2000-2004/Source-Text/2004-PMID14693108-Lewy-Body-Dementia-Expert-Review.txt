The review describes the overlapping clinical and pathological features of dementia with Lewy bodies and dementia in Parkinson disease. It discusses drug sensitivity and cognitive and neuropsychiatric outcomes using the prior literature. The abstract does not provide an original Parkinson visual episode count or a fig comparison.
