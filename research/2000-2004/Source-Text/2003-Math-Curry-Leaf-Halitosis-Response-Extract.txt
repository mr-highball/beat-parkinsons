Fresh curry leaves in an informal oral observation
Source: https://www.bmj.com/rapid-response/2011/10/29/curry-leaves-and-halitosis
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

The authors' April 22, 2003 rapid response describes ten male volunteers aged 30–52 who held fresh curry leaves in the mouth. They report reduced bad breath in these previously unpublished observations and suggest more saliva. They supply no comparator, numerical salivary flow, swallowing measure or voice result. The page URL reflects an archive path, and its signed response gives the publication date.
