Title: Artichoke leaf extract reduces mild dyspepsia in an open study.
PMID: 12587688
DOI: 10.1078/094471102321621287
First published: 2002
Authors: Marakis, Walker, Middleton, Booth, Wright
Journal: Phytomedicine : international journal of phytotherapy and phytopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12587688/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

2-month open-label randomized dose-ranging postal trial in 516 enrolled / 454 completed adults with self-reported mild dyspepsia allocated to standardized artichoke leaf extract (ALE) 320 mg/d or 640 mg/d. Global Nepean Dyspepsia Index score fell ~40% from baseline in both arms with no primary between-dose difference, while relief of State-Trait Anxiety Inventory (STAI) state anxiety (secondary outcome) was significantly greater with 640 mg/d vs 320 mg/d (P = 0.03). Health-related QoL improved from baseline in both arms. Limits: open-label postal design, no placebo control, leaf extract rather than steamed artichoke flower bud, abstract omits exact STAI arm means.
