October 2004 issue. The University of Barcelona thesis includes the matching original article chapter, from title to Table I and figures. Dalfó and colleagues compared eight pure diffuse Lewy body disease cases, twelve with coexisting Alzheimer-type pathology and five controls. Table I provides demographic, pathology and postmortem fields. Recurrent hallucinations are discussed as a diagnostic feature without episode outcomes for these tissue donors. Salmon is a cited author’s surname. The original issue predates the 2006 electronic date displayed on PMC.
