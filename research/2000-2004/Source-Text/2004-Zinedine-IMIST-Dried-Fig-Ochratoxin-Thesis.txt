Title: Zinedine doctoral thesis on food mycotoxins, dried fig sampling section
Source: https://toubkal.imist.ma/server/api/core/bitstreams/02b36d2f-20a3-41b8-afba-50e8380e5497/content
Publication date: 2004-10-30
Date basis: Date on the original defense title page. The institutional repository copy is a later deposit.
Text coverage: Original 162-page doctoral thesis. The dried fruit methods are on PDF page 68, the fig results and qualifications on pages 98–99, and Table 15 on page 100.

Zinedine describes a Rabat–Salé market series of 24 dried fruit samples, including five fig samples. The narrative reports ochratoxin A below the method’s detection limit for the five fig samples. Table 15 lists measured concentrations for raisins and prunes. The text describes an earlier-eluting chromatogram peak in the fig samples and explicitly leaves its molecular identity hypothetical. The peak cannot be counted as confirmed ochratoxin A or assigned a concentration.

The 2007 journal report describes a separate July–August 2005 retail sampling campaign with 20 dried fig samples. The doctoral study was already defended in October 2004, and the food panel and denominator differ. Neither study reports what any individual consumed or a visual outcome.
