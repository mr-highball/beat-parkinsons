Original study scope
The original abstract reports neurological assessment and occupational pesticide histories in 310 men, most of them orchardists. Rest tremor, rigidity and other signs contribute to the study classification. Orchard work describes recruitment and pesticides are the measured exposure.

Continuing evidence gaps
