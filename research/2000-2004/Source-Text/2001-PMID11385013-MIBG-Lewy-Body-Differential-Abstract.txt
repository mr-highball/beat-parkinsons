Title: Cardiac 123I-meta-iodobenzylguanidine (MIBG) uptake in dementia with Lewy bodies: comparison with Alzheimer's disease
PMID: 11385013
PMCID: PMC1737405
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.70.6.781
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11385013/
Publication year: 2001
Coverage: Original NLM abstract, with full print body still open.

The abstract compares cardiac tracer uptake in 11 people with dementia with Lewy bodies, 10 with Alzheimer disease and 10 age-matched controls. It reports lower heart-to-mediastinum uptake ratios in the Lewy body dementia group. The summary describes a diagnostic imaging outcome without a food exposure or visual hallucination rate. The detailed diagnostic criteria and original tables require the full body.
