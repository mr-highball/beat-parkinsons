Title: Cardiac 123I-meta-iodobenzylguanidine (MIBG) uptake in dementia with Lewy bodies: comparison with Alzheimer's disease

Publication date: 2001-06
Date basis: JNNP 70(6), 781–783, June issue.
Text coverage: Original article body and Table 1 inspected in the author's uploaded copy. Research summary.

Watanabe and colleagues compared cardiac tracer uptake in 11 people with dementia with Lewy bodies, 10 with Alzheimer disease and 10 age-matched controls. Heart-to-mediastinum tracer uptake ratios were lower in the Lewy body dementia group. Table 1 records a positive entry for hallucination for ten of the 11 participants in that group. The column is labeled for hallucination generally. The introduction describes visual hallucinations as a feature of the syndrome. The table gives clinical presence and the study measures cardiac imaging. Neither measures visual episode frequency or a fig exposure.
