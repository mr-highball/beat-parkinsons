Original source scope
The six-week pilot randomized university personnel to fish oil (16) or olive oil (14). The separately observed 63-person no-treatment group was outside that random assignment. Both intervention groups' Perceived Stress Scale means fell within groups. The between-group change was significant for fish oil versus no treatment. Fish oil versus olive oil and olive oil versus no treatment were inconclusive. Reference 21 in PMID 33763446 compresses the result and is less specific about that distinction. The olive oil's cold-pressed grade was not established here. Perceived stress differs from a structured Parkinson anxiety or insomnia outcome. Round 1 0343 and 0897.
