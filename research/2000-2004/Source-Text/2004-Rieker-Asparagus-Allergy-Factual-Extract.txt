Title: Asparagus allergy and acute swallowing difficulty
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15146878/
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Rieker and colleagues, Hautarzt 55(4):397–398, April 2004. The original German study has an indexed English abstract.
A 55-year-old cook developed symptoms consistent with occupational sensitization and reported shortness of breath and difficulty swallowing after eating asparagus. Allergy investigation showed evidence of immediate and delayed sensitization. The accessible abstract gives no preparation or standard severity measurement. Parkinson disease is not reported.
