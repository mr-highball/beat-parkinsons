Suganuma et al. — Effect of tomato intake on striatal monoamine level in a mouse model of experimental Parkinson's disease
Source: https://doi.org/10.3177/jnsv.48.251
Primary PDF: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnsv1973/48/3/48_3_251/_pdf
Publication: J Nutr Sci Vitaminol. 2002;48(3):251–254. PMID 12350086.
Coverage: Factual extract of preparation, measured outcomes and Figure 2.

Four groups of male mice received basal or tomato-enriched feed and either saline or MPTP. The tomato preparation was freeze-dried from concentrated juice and replaced part of the cornstarch at 20% by weight. It was fed for 28 days before toxin administration. The figure reports group sizes 10, 5, 5 and 5. The tomato diet attenuated the loss of striatal dopamine after MPTP. Its effects on striatal DOPAC and HVA did not reach statistical significance. Neither executive tasks nor stride length were reported.
