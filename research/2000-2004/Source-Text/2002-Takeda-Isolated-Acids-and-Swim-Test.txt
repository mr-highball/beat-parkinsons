Takeda H et al. Rosmarinic acid and caffeic acid produce antidepressive-like effect in the forced swimming test in mice. Eur J Pharmacol. 2002;449:261–267.
DOI: 10.1016/S0014-2999(02)02037-X
PMID: 12167468
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12167468/
Coverage: factual summary of original author abstract.

Two isolated compounds were tested in mice. Forced-swim immobility declined, with no clear change in spontaneous motor activity at the reported active doses. The abstract supplies no clinical depressive-symptom scale or culinary Rosemary intervention. Exact study-level overlap with the same group's Perilla investigation is unverified.
