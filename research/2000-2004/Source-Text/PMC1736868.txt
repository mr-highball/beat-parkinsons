Title: Progressive dementia and gait disorder in a 78 year old woman

Tagliati and colleagues describe a patient with mixed tremor, gait dysfunction and progressive cognitive changes. The patient was initially treated for suspected Parkinson disease. Visual hallucinations and worsening tremor were reported following a levodopa increase and resolved after withdrawal. The final diagnosis was gliomatosis cerebri. The case is a clinical differential and temporal medication observation. It cannot estimate visual episode frequency in confirmed Parkinson disease and supplies no dried-fig comparison.
