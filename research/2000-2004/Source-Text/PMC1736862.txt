Title: What clinical features are most useful to distinguish definite multiple system atrophy from Parkinson's disease?
Source: https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/68/4/434.full.pdf
PMID: 10727478
PMCID: PMC1736862
DOI: 10.1136/jnnp.68.4.434
Publication date: 2000-04
Date basis: JNNP 68(4), 434–440
Source depth: Complete publisher main article and tables
Format: Original-source factual extract

Wenning and colleagues compared 100 Parkinson disease cases with 38 multiple system atrophy cases using retrospective clinical notes and pathologically confirmed diagnoses. The diagnostic models separated observations in the first five years from observations across the disease course. Medication-associated psychiatric side effects were interpreted as a diagnostic feature within a composite clinical pattern. The paper does not provide a standalone visual hallucination episode-rate comparison or a culinary exposure.
