The study investigated drooling and swallowing measures for Parkinson disease. The authors described a speech and language therapy strategy incorporating reminders to swallow and a separate clinical treatment strategy. The available abstract confirms the intervention comparison and distinct measures for saliva, swallowing and drooling. Numerical treatment estimates and the details of participant assignment remain open at this source depth. Subsequent references describing a six-person cueing group refer to this study and do not establish another cohort. No ingredient comparison was specified.
