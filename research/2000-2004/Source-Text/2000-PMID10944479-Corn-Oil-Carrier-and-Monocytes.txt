Title: Effects of lycopene and lutein supplementation on surface molecules on blood monocytes from healthy male nonsmokers
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10944479/
Original article: https://academic.oup.com/jid/article-pdf/182/Supplement_1/S11/17998865/182-Supplement_1-S11.pdf
PMID: 10944479
DOI: 10.1086/315910
Publication date: 2000-09
Date basis: NLM Journal of Infectious Diseases 182 supplement 1, pp. S11–S15.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract, methods and outcome discussion in the publisher article.

Two clinical comparisons use carotenoid capsules in corn oil and matching corn-oil-only capsules. The main experimental variables are lycopene and lutein, and the outcomes are plasma carotenoids and monocyte surface molecules, including HLA-DR. The oil grade is not identified, the carrier is shared, and there is no oil-free group, depression scale or Parkinson cohort. The immune findings do not determine an oil effect.
