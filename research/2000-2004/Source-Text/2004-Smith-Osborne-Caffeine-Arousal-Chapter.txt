Original source scope and evidence boundary
This is the immediate reference numbered 106 in PMID 24522465. The chapter discusses task persistence, vigilance, subjective tiredness and differing performance results. It cites Bruce and colleagues (PMID 3741730), whose nine-person crossover used isolated caffeine after abstinence and reported lower tiredness, and Zwyghuizen-Doorenbos and colleagues (PMID 2296626), whose accessible original summarizes objective alertness and vigilance. The chapter provides no independent cohort, brewed tea or coffee comparison for Parkinson fatigue. Preserve the separation from the 2002 A. Smith review cited by the 2014 microglia paper.
