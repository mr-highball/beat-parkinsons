Dépatie and Lal's review tabulates psychiatric observations during apomorphine treatment in Parkinson disease. Table 1 includes reports of transient visual symptoms that resolved during treatment, reports of visual symptoms after treatment began, and series reporting no new psychiatric disturbance. Baseline susceptibility, advanced Parkinson disease, other medicines and inconsistent outcome coding limit attribution. Visual hallucinations, pseudo-hallucinations, visual delusions, broadly coded psychosis and absence of recorded symptoms appear as distinct descriptions. No common time-based visual episode outcome permits a pooled comparison.
