Original source scope and evidence boundary
Forty-nine participants with solid tumors entered a dose-escalation study of an oral caffeinated green tea extract. The original Results and Table 4 report mild and moderate fatigue in multiple cohorts, and describe one participant who stopped after fatigue and nausea. The report also records other adverse events, including tremor, with a discrepancy between the grade in one case narrative and the extracted table. The study has no placebo arm, brewed tea exposure, Parkinson cohort or Parkinson fatigue scale. The varied dose cohorts and toxicity grades do not identify a unique fatigue count that can be compared with the frozen claim. The later fibroid paper cites this trial in its safety discussion. This primary adverse-event lead is contextual for claim 0948.
