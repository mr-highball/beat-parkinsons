Title: Oxidation of salsolinol by banana pulp polyphenol oxidase and its kinetic synergism with dopamine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11087516/
PMID: 11087516
DOI: 10.1021/jf000293f
Publication date: 2000-11, journal issue.
Text coverage: Paraphrased original NLM abstract. Complete methods and results remain open.

The laboratory study measures oxidation of salsolinol by banana pulp polyphenol oxidase. Reaction measurements vary with pH and the presence of dopamine in the reaction medium. This is an enzyme assay of banana material and supplies neither measured edible fig composition nor concentrations or symptom outcomes after human ingestion.
