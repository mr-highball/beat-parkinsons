Inhaled eugenol and sensory ratings in healthy students
Source: https://doi.org/10.2114/jpa.19.35
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: original Methods, Results and Figure 2

Masago and colleagues recorded EEG and immediate sensory adjective ratings in 13 healthy students exposed to lavender, chamomile, sandalwood and eugenol odors. Eugenol ranked behind lavender in a derived comfort factor. Alpha-1 EEG activity changed after eugenol exposure. The study measured odor perception and electrophysiology without clinical anxiety or apathy assessments, isolated culinary ground clove, or Parkinson disease.
