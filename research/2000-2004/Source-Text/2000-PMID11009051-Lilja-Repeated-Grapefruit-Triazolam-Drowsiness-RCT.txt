Title: Effect of grapefruit juice dose on grapefruit juice-triazolam interaction: repeated consumption prolongs triazolam half-life.
PMID: 11009051
DOI: 10.1007/s002280000156
First published: 2000
Authors: Lilja JJ, Kivistö KT, Backman JT, Neuvonen PJ
Journal: European journal of clinical pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11009051/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized 4-phase crossover trial in 12 healthy volunteers taking triazolam 0.25 mg with water, single 200 mL normal-strength, single double-strength, or 3-day t.i.d. double-strength grapefruit juice. Triazolam AUC increased +53% (p<0.01), +49% (p<0.01), and +143% (p<0.001), with half-life prolonged +54% (p<0.001) during repeated intake. Repeated grapefruit significantly WORSENED subjective drowsiness (p<0.05), subjective overall drug effect (p<0.05), and DSST psychomotor performance (p<0.05).
