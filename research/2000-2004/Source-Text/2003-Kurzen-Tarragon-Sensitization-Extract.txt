2003 — Tarragon skin sensitization during a mugwort airway case

Primary source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1046/j.1610-0387.2003.02034.x
Identifier: PMID 16285482 · 10.1046/j.1610-0387.2003.02034.x

The German primary report describes a florist who developed subglottic edema after occupational contact with mugwort (Artemisia vulgaris). Prick and scratch tests later showed marked sensitization to both mugwort and French tarragon. The authors reported no oral allergy syndrome to the cross-reactive exposures in this patient. The clinical episode and the diagnostic tarragon skin result refer to separate exposures. No tarragon ingestion challenge, speech intelligibility measure or Parkinson participant was described in the accessible original abstract.
