Title: The effects of a normal protein diet on levodopa plasma kinetics in advanced Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15036167/
PMID: 15036167
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2003.10.004
Citation: Simon N, Gantcheva R, Bruguerolle B, Viallet F. Parkinsonism & Related Disorders. 2004 Mar. 10(3):137–142.
Coverage: Author abstract from PubMed. Local factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

In 20 participants with advanced Parkinson disease, the authors compared morning dosing with a low-protein breakfast against noon dosing with a standard-protein lunch. Each person's formulation and dose matched across the two observations. Peak concentration and time to peak did not differ significantly. Area under the concentration curve was higher at noon (reported p < 0.001), which the authors attributed to the higher pre-dose trough.

Meal and time of day differed together. The study measured plasma pharmacokinetics, leaving clinical ON/OFF, rest tremor, daytime sleepiness and isolated culinary preparations unmeasured in the abstract.
