Title: Fibromyalgia syndrome improved using a mostly raw vegetarian diet: an observational study.
PMID: 11602026
PMCID: PMC57816
DOI: 10.1186/1472-6882-1-7
First published: 2001
Authors: Donaldson MS, Speight N, Loomis S
Journal: BMC Complement Altern Med
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11602026/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single-arm observational diet study (n=30 enrolled, n=20 analyzed at 7 mo) testing a mostly raw vegetarian diet bundling raw fruits, salads, carrot juice, tubers, nuts, seeds, and dehydrated barley grass juice in fibromyalgia. Mean FIQ fell 46% and 7/8 SF-36 subscales improved, EXCEPT SF-36 bodily pain did not significantly improve. Uncontrolled bundled multi-food diet in non-PD fibromyalgia.
