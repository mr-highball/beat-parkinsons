Title: Randomized, double-blind, crossover challenge study in 53 subjects reporting adverse reactions to melon (Cucumis melo).
PMID: 11080722
DOI: 10.1067/mai.2000.110467
First published: 2000
Authors: Rodriguez J, Crespo JF, Burks W, Rivas-Plata C, Fernandez-Anaya S, Vives R et al.
Journal: J Allergy Clin Immunol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11080722/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled food challenge (DBPCFC) crossover study in 53 consecutive adults reporting adverse reactions to melon (Cucumis melo). Clinical reactivity was confirmed in 19/53 (36%): oral allergy syndrome (n=14; oral/pharyngeal itching or throat symptoms) and life-threatening systemic anaphylaxis with respiratory symptoms and hypotension in 2/53. Reflects IgE-mediated fruit allergy in sensitized individuals, not Parkinson dysphagia or neurogenic orthostatic hypotension.
