Title: Association of L-DOPA with recovery following Ayurveda medication in Parkinson's disease
PMID: 10930594
DOI: 10.1016/S0022-510X(00)00329-4
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10930594/
Publication date: 2000-06-15 journal date; earlier online day unverified
Coverage: Original author abstract; complete article and tables not inspected
Source kind: Small prospective multicomponent clinical comparison
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Eighteen parkinsonian participants received a milk-based mixture of Mucuna/Hyoscyamus seeds and Withania/Sida roots, with (n=13) or without (n=5) preliminary cleansing. Authors report better tremor response in the former group and worsening salivation in both.

No numerical rest-tremor comparison, random allocation or isolated herb effect is established here. Levodopa was detected in the mixture.

Open trails: Original tables, baseline comparability, harms and commentary PMID11305284.
