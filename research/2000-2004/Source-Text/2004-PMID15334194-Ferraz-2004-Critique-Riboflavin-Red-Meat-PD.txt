Title: Comments on the paper "High doses of riboflavin and the elimination of dietary red meat promote the recovery of some motor functions in Parkinson's disease patients. C.G. Coimbra and V.B.C. Junqueira. Brazilian Journal of Medical and Biological Research, 36: 1409-1417, 2003".
PMID: 15334194
DOI: 10.1590/s0100-879x2004000900002
First published: 2004
Authors: Ferraz HB, Quagliato EA, Rieder CR, Silva DJ, Teive HA, Barbosa ER, et al.
Journal: Brazilian journal of medical and biological research = Revista brasileira de pesquisas medicas e biologicas
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15334194/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Published critical letter pointing out major methodological flaws in Coimbra & Junqueira 2003 (unblinded, uncontrolled design, simultaneous riboflavin and red meat withdrawal, selective attrition, and lack of validated UPDRS motor ratings), concluding red meat elimination in PD is unproven.
