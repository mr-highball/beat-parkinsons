Title: Value of 99mTc-ECD SPET for the diagnosis of dementia with Lewy bodies

PMID: 12271417
DOI: 10.1007/s00259-002-0919-x
Publication date: 2002-07-31
Date basis: Publisher online date; volume 29, 1342–1348
Source depth: Primary publisher and NLM abstracts. Full item tables remain open.
Format: Original-source factual extract

Pasquier and colleagues retrospectively compared brain perfusion in 34 patients with probable dementia with Lewy bodies and 28 with probable Alzheimer disease. Bilateral occipital hypoperfusion was present in 15 of 26 patients with DLB and visual hallucinations. All eight DLB patients without hallucinations had left and right indices at or above the reported threshold. The comparison concerns an imaging pattern and symptom history.

The 2001 PS_51 abstract on p. 1087 used the same tracer, matching diagnostic group sizes and overlapping authors, but reported 25 rather than 26 DLB patients with visual hallucinations. Both reports identify 15 DLB patients with visual hallucinations below the bilateral threshold. These features make cohort overlap likely. Confirmation of participant-level linkage and the reason for the count difference require the complete journal paper. Neither accessible comparison reports dried-fig exposure.
