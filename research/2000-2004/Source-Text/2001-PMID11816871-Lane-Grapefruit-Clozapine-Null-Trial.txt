Title: Repeated ingestion of grapefruit juice does not alter clozapine's steady-state plasma levels, effectiveness, and tolerability.
PMID: 11816871
DOI: 10.4088/jcp.v62n1010
First published: 2001
Authors: Lane HY, Jann MW, Chang YC, Chiu CC, Huang MC, Lee SH, Chang WH
Journal: The Journal of clinical psychiatry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11816871/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Clinical trial showing repeated grapefruit juice ingestion does NOT alter steady-state plasma clozapine/norclozapine levels, efficacy, or tolerability (corroborated by PMID:10954351 and PMID:11791889), confirming grapefruit does not disrupt CYP1A2-metabolized clozapine used for PD psychosis.
