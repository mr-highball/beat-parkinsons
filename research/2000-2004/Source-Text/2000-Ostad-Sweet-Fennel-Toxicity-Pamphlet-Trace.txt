The regulator identifies a short 2000 report about the sweet variety of Foeniculum vulgare. Its narrative attributes abnormal movement, tremor, fasciculation and drowsiness after high-dose fennel extract to that source. The numerator, denominator, exact preparation, dose groups and assessment protocol for this pamphlet remain unverified. Three investigators also appear on a 2001 journal article on fennel oil and acute toxicity. Both reports are kept in an uncertain shared lineage until the original pamphlet can distinguish their experimental animals and comparisons. The evidence concerns toxicity observations, with no separately identified Parkinson resting tremor or culinary seed exposure.
