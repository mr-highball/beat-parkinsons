Visual hallucination status in a diagnostic comparison
PMID 11606666
DOI 10.1136/jnnp.71.5.583
https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/71/5/583.full.pdf

November 2001. Table 2 on page 585 in Yoshita and colleagues gives presence or absence of visual hallucinations for each of 14 clinically diagnosed dementia-with-Lewy-bodies patients. The study compares cardiac MIBG uptake and autonomic features with probable Alzheimer-type dementia. It gives no hallucination episode rate or edible fig or salmon exposure. The final table row has a merged token in the indexed PDF rendering, so this review does not tally the number with hallucinations.
