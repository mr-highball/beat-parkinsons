Title: Conventional roasting and cumin volatile aldehydes
Source: https://ir.cftri.res.in/30/
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

Behera, Nagarajan and Rao, Food Chemistry 87, pages 25–29, 2004. Conventional roasting and microwave heating altered the volatile oil profile of Cuminum cyminum seeds. The authors analyzed distilled oil with gas chromatography and mass spectrometry. The abstract reports better retention of characteristic aldehydes with microwave treatment than with conventional roasting. The full tables were not obtained from the institutional record. No food ingestion, measured dose or cognitive outcome was studied.
