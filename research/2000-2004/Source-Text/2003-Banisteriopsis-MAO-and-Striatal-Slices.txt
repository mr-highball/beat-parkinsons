Title: Activities of extract and constituents of Banisteriopsis caapi relevant to parkinsonism
Source: https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/portal/en/publications/activities-of-extract-and-constituents-of-banisteriopsis-caapi-re/
DOI: 10.1016/S0091-3057(03)00129-1
Publication date: 2003
Date basis: Author-affiliated university publication metadata
Source depth: Primary author-affiliated article abstract
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The laboratory study compares the plant extract and constituents using monoamine oxidase assays in liver homogenate and dopamine measures in rat striatal slices. Extract and harmaline showed concentration-dependent inhibition of MAO-A and little activity against MAO-B in the reported assay. The extract increased measured dopamine release in a slice condition. These are assay results from tissue preparations. They establish neither human exposure at the tested conditions nor a symptom effect. The authors cite the previous clinical trial as motivation and use a different experimental population.
