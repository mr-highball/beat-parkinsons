Title: Staged bilateral pallidotomy for treatment of Parkinson disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11235949/
PMID: 11235949
DOI: 10.3171/jns.2001.94.3.0437
Publication date: 2001-03
Source depth: Original author abstract. The original patient tables remain open.

The UCLA series included 12 people with medically intractable Parkinson disease. Eleven received staged bilateral surgery and one received simultaneous bilateral surgery. The abstract reports four patients with worsened speech and increased drooling following the bilateral stage. It separately states that near-complete three-month follow-up was available for eight. The denominator and timing for the event count should be checked in the full tables before treating it as a risk estimate. The exposure is neurosurgery. It supplies no culinary comparison.
