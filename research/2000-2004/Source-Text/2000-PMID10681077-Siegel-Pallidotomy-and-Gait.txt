Title: Effects of bilateral posteroventral pallidotomy on gait of subjects with Parkinson disease
Source: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/775954
PMID: 10681077
DOI: 10.1001/archneur.57.2.198
Publication date: 2000-02
Source depth: Original article and gait figure description.

Siegel and Metman studied 11 Parkinson patients in a surgical before-and-after series. Walking speed changed from 0.214 to 0.440 body heights per second and stride length from 0.24 to 0.47 body heights (both reported p = .03). The report concerns quantitative gait. It does not provide a separately rated drooling outcome. The 2017 focused-ultrasound review included this citation near its discussion of drooling, for which the Intemann series supplies a separate original observation.
