Takeda H et al. Identification of rosmarinic acid as a novel antidepressive substance in the leaves of Perilla frutescens Britton var. acuta Kudo (Perillae Herba). Jpn J Psychopharmacol. 2002;22:15–22.
PMID: 11917505
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11917505/
Coverage: factual summary of original author abstract.

Water extract and selected fractions from the Perilla species reduced mouse forced-swim immobility. The authors also report effects of isolated rosmarinic acid. The tested leaf genus was Perilla, and there was no clinical depression measurement. The full original Methods and tables were unverified in the accessible abstract route.
