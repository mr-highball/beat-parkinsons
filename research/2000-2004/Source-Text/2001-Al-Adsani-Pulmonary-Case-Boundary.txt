Factual extract from original case p. 127 and answers pp. 137–138.

Al-Adsani and colleagues describe one patient with back pain and pulmonary findings involving malignancy and tuberculosis. The assigned case does not measure a food effect or report drooling for this patient. A neighboring angioedema case includes drooling language. Separate title, case pages and answers establish that those are separate patients.
