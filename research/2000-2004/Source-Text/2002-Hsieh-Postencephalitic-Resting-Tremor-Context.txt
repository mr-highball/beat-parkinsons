Hsieh, Lue and Lee describe one child who developed resting tremor and bradykinesia in an encephalitic illness. The reported signs improved over approximately three weeks. Drooling appears in the introductory description of parkinsonian symptoms and is not documented as a finding in this child. The case offers no dietary intervention or estimate for idiopathic Parkinson disease.
