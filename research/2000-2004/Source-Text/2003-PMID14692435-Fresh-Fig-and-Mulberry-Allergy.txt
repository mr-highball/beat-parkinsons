Title: Fig and mulberry cross-allergy
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14692435/
PMID: 14692435
DOI: 10.1016/s1081-1206(10)61520-7
Publication date: 2003-11
Citation: Caiaffa et al. Annals of Allergy, Asthma & Immunology 91(5), 493-495.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: Original author abstract. Full report remains to be obtained.

The abstract describes three patients with histories of food allergy after fresh Ficus carica and separately reports clinical or laboratory evidence of mulberry sensitization in all three. The participants had multiple other sensitizations. The authors offer cross-reactivity as an explanation. These are selected allergy cases without a population denominator, a Parkinson cohort, or a tremor outcome. This report supplies raw food safety context and does not estimate the probability of allergy or an effect on Parkinson symptoms.
