Title: The feasibility of a kinematic measure of lip closure during meaningful speech
Authors: Carter and colleagues
Primary original abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11197518/
Identifiers: PMID 11197518, DOI 10.1080/09638280050207866
Date: December 2000
Coverage: factual extract of the primary abstract prepared 2026-11-14

The investigators studied three healthy men aged 69. Each repeated a sentence that includes an apple and blackberry pie six times. Retroreflective markers tracked the position of the lips. The study used lip separation around eight bilabial events to characterize mechanical closure. The abstract supplies no acoustic intensity, portion or ingestion of blackberry, freezing history or Parkinson disease sample. The record surfaced in literature searches because the test sentence names blackberry.
