Title: The use of ascorbigen in the treatment of fibromyalgia patients: a preliminary trial.
PMID: 11056415
First published: 2000
Authors: Bramwell B, Ferguson S, Scarlett N, Macintosh A
Journal: Alternative medicine review : a journal of clinical therapeutic
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11056415/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

One-month open-label pilot trial in n=12 female fibromyalgia patients taking 500 mg/d blend (100 mg ascorbigen + 400 mg broccoli powder). Reported 20.1% decrease in FIQ physical impairment (p=0.044; rebounding 2 wk post-treatment p=0.028), 17.8% decrease in total FIQ (p=0.016), and a non-significant trend toward higher tender-point pain threshold in n=10 (p=0.059). Limits: open-label, uncontrolled, bundled with ascorbigen.
