Title: Dietary supplementation with fruit polyphenolics ameliorates age-related deficits in behavior and neuronal markers of inflammation and oxidative stress
Publication: Society for Neuroscience Abstracts and Proceedings, November 2, 2002
Source: https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=139919
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated investigators' technical abstract in the USDA publication archive

Galli and colleagues describe aged Fischer 344 rats eating 2% blueberry, blackcurrant, boysenberry or cranberry extract. Preliminary motor and hippocampal HSP70 results favored blueberry and cranberry. The abstract does not assign strawberry or orange. It describes ten weeks of feeding and both young and old controls, while the 2005 article presents its aged-rat diet-group data after eight weeks at first behavioral testing. The matching title, team, four extracts and result pattern make this a possible precursor to PMID 23598603. Participant-level animal identifiers are unavailable.
