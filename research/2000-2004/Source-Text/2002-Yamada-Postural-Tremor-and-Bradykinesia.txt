Postural tremor and bradykinesia in a treated parkinsonism case
PMID 11784836
DOI 10.1136/jnnp.72.1.111
https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/72/1/111.full.pdf

January 2002. On page 111 in Yamada and colleagues, a 41-year-old man with parkinsonism had mild bradykinesia and postural hand tremor. The exam explicitly records absence of resting tremor. Following trihexyphenidyl the report describes improvement in gait, rigidity and bradykinesia, with total UPDRS motor score changing from 33 to 18 at four weeks. No individual bradykinesia score or food comparison is given. The indexed original PDF supplied the clinical pages.
