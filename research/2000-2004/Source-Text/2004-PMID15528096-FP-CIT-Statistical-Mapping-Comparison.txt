Title: The application of statistical parametric mapping to 123I-FP-CIT SPECT in dementia with Lewy bodies, Alzheimer's disease and Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15528096/
PMID: 15528096
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2004.06.045
Publication date: 2004-11
Date basis: NeuroImage November issue
Source depth: Primary author abstract
Format: Original-source factual extract

The analysis compared image patterns in Lewy body dementia, Alzheimer disease, Parkinson disease and controls. Striatal tracer binding was reduced in the Lewy body dementia and Parkinson groups and the two groups were not distinguishable on this measure. Visual inspection means assessment of the scan. The paper shares investigators with the earlier 2004 clinical imaging report. Their participant overlap remains unverified at abstract depth. No visual episode-frequency or measured food comparison is reported.
