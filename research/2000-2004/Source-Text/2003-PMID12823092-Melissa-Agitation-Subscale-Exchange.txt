Archive scope: Primary letter and investigator reply on journal page 732.

Snowdon questioned how the report assigned verbal and nonaggressive behaviors to the agitation subscales and asked for item counts, baseline subscale values or possible score ranges. Ballard maintained that small differences in scoring methods could not explain the overall contrast and planned to clarify the profile of improvement. The response supplies no new cohort or corrected item-level table. The account concerns the original 2002 aromatherapy cohort. Social withdrawal, activity and agitation should retain their own outcome labels.
