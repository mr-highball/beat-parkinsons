Title: Pharmacokinetics of carvone and menthol after administration of peppermint oil and caraway oil containing enteric formulation
Authors: H Mascher, Ch Kikuta and H Schiel
Date: 2002
Identifiers: PMID 12244893; DOI 10.1046/j.1563-258x.2002.02068.x
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12244893/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the primary English abstract.

Sixteen healthy male volunteers received a fixed peppermint–caraway oil combination in enteric and immediate-release formats. Each period administered a total of 180 mg peppermint oil and 100 mg caraway oil. The analysis compared systemic exposure to the native oils' constituents menthol and carvone. The relative area-under-the-curve confidence interval was 79–119% for carvone and 97–105% for menthol. Mean carvone plasma peaks were 14 ng/mL for each preparation and peaked at 2.5 hours with enteric coating versus 1.3 hours with immediate release. A different drug’s pharmacokinetics and Parkinson symptoms were not outcomes.
