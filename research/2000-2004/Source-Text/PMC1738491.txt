Rahkonen and colleagues sampled 700 people aged at least 75 years in Kuopio and examined 601. Of the 137 classified with clinical dementia, 20 had probable and 10 had possible dementia with Lewy bodies. The combined prevalence was 5.0% in the examined population. Two Parkinson dementia diagnoses appear separately under other dementia categories. The researchers did not use prospective follow-up and could not verify the neuropathology of clinically classified pure Lewy body dementia. The rendered signs in Table 4 fail reconciliation with the diagnostic totals. A checked image is needed to recover a symptom numerator. The study provides diagnostic prevalence and clinical feature coding, with no observed episode rate or fig comparison.
