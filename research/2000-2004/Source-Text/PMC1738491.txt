Title: Dementia with Lewy bodies according to the consensus criteria in a general population aged 75 years or older

The Kuopio study examined 601 older adults. It classified 20 as probable and 10 as possible dementia with Lewy bodies. The combined prevalence was 5.0% in the examined population. Diagnostic prevalence is distinct from symptom occurrence and within-person episode frequency. The abstract provides no separate visual-hallucination measure or fig comparison.
