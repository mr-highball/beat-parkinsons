Original source scope and evidence boundary
The investigators compare habitual nonconsumers against habitual caffeine users after overnight abstinence. One experiment uses differently flavored drinks carrying caffeine or placebo, and the other compares acute caffeine and placebo during reaction-time and mood assessment. The abstract reports improved self-rated alertness in both groups. Other mood, reinforcement and performance effects were concentrated among habitual consumers, and some nonconsumers showed worse performance. The abstract does not provide a separate mental-fatigue score. This supplies a withdrawal interpretation alongside the differing Haskell comparisons without identifying a brewed-coffee Parkinson effect.
