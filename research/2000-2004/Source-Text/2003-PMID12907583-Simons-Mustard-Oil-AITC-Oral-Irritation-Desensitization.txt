Title: Oral irritation by mustard oil: self-desensitization and cross-desensitization with capsaicin
PMID: 12907583
DOI: 10.1093/chemse/28.6.459
First published: 2003
Authors: Simons CT, Carstens MI, Carstens E
Journal: Chemical Senses
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12907583/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Human oral psychophysical crossover study applying 0.125% allyl isothiocyanate (mustard oil, 40 uL) sequentially to one side of the tongue at 1-min intervals. Acute oral burning irritation declined across successive trials and demonstrated significant self-desensitization after a 10-min hiatus as well as bidirectional cross-desensitization with capsaicin. Demonstrates acute local lingual chemesthesis followed by rapid desensitization, NOT chronic systemic PD pain modulation.
