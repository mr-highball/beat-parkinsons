The printed article gives almond oil as the vehicle for its nonaqueous carbidopa/levodopa suspension. It has no patient group or measured clinical outcome. The concentration shown in the printed article title (20 mg/mL) differs from the official archive index and issue contents listing (250 mg/mL). This note does not reproduce the formula or preparations.
The 2010 Handbook of Extemporaneous Preparation, Table 14.2, describes the 2002 oil formula as suggested and notes that the storage-duration claim lacks supporting validation. Its separate summary of Allwood 1987 covers a buffered suspension made with Keltrol, not an almond-oil stability test. No exposure grade or Parkinson motor outcome is provided.
