Dystonia and rigidity in slowly progressive aphasia cases
PMID 11831416
DOI 10.1177/153331750201700107
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11530107_Slowly_progressive_aphasia_with_striatal_involvement

2002. Nagaratnam and colleagues describe four progressive aphasia cases with striatal signs on pages 18–22. Case 1 developed limb and axial rigidity with neck dystonia. Case 2 had increased limb tone and dystonic right-hand posture. Case 3 developed rigidity. The retrospective cases include no sage exposure or symptom comparisons by food. The publisher-supplied preview gave the complete article narrative and citations. Figures were unavailable in the readable rendering.
