The original October 2003 forum supplement gives the first verified public period. The PMC page's collection metadata displays a later date. Abstract 88 uses a convenience survey of 837 emergency-department patients, family members and visitors. It reports 37% correct answers on ginger indications and 11% correct on ginger adverse effects. The study records knowledge of herbal uses rather than what the participants consumed or experienced.

SAGE in another member refers to a behavioral baseline measure. Orange is part of place or affiliation names. An unrelated skin study names poison sumac, which is a different botanical exposure from culinary Rhus coriaria. Those reports cannot be joined to the herb survey's ginger questions.
