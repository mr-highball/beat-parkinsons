The review surveys differential diagnosis in young onset dementia. Its differential table lists visual hallucinations, extrapyramidal findings and fluctuation as features of Lewy body dementia. These are diagnostic clues. The paper does not supply an original Parkinson visual-frequency outcome or a dried-fig comparison.
