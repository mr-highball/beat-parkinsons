Chinese patent example uses almond oil in a mixed oral snoring spray
Primary source: https://patents.google.com/patent/CN100364498C/zh
Coverage: factual extract from the later granted family specification and original publication metadata.

Chinese patent application CN1535708A was published October 13, 2004. The later granted Chinese-language specification CN100364498C includes almond oil with olive, sunflower, mint and sesame oils in a worked oral spray formulation. It proposes lubricating the throat and tongue to reduce snoring noise. The accessible family specimen supplies no reported comparative human data on vocal loudness and no evidence of cold-pressed oil. Snoring during sleep, deliberate vocal sound production and consuming an isolated culinary oil are different exposures or endpoints.
