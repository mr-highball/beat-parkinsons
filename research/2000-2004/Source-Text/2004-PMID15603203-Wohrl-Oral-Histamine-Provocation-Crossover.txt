Title: Histamine intolerance-like symptoms in healthy volunteers after oral provocation with liquid histamine.
PMID: 15603203
First published: 2004
Authors: Wöhrl S, Hemmer W, Focke M, Rappersberger K, Jarisch R
Journal: Allergy and asthma proceedings
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15603203/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover in 10 healthy women challenged with 75 mg pure liquid histamine (noting sauerkraut among histamine-rich foods up to 500 mg/kg), provoking acute headache, diarrhea, flushing, or transient hypotension in 5/10 participants. Pure liquid histamine provocation — not whole dietary sauerkraut — providing biogenic-amine intolerance context.
