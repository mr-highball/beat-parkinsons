Title: Structure-activity relationships of seco-prezizaane terpenoids in gamma-aminobutyric acid receptors of houseflies and rats.
PMID: 11937345
DOI: 10.1016/s0968-0896(02)00011-1
First published: 2002
Authors: Kuriyama T, Schmidt TJ, Okuyama E, Ozoe Y
Journal: Bioorganic & medicinal chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11937345/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Receptor-binding pharmacology study (Kuriyama et al., 2002, Bioorg Med Chem) testing 13 seco-prezizaane terpenoids from Illicium species (including I. verum) against [3H]EBOB binding at GABA-A receptors. Veranisatin A (rat brain IC50 = 271 nM) and anisatin (rat brain IC50 = 282 nM) acted as potent picrotoxin-site non-competitive GABA-A antagonists, explaining the convulsant mechanism of both adulterated I. anisatum and high-dose I. verum veranisatin extracts.
