Title: Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India, Phalgu root botanical identity
Source: https://www.portal.pcimh.gov.in/product_details/9a0327f5-e0cb-4df4-8bf5-57b91d367677
Original volume rendition: https://www.ayurveda.hu/api/API-Vol-3.pdf
Publication date: 2001
Date basis: Government pharmacopoeia publication record, part I volume III.
Text coverage: Original volume contents and monograph 68 (index page 151, PDF page 166). Adjacent monograph 67 treats the fruit.

The root monograph identifies standardized Phalgu as dried root of Ficus hispida. The volume separately specifies a fruit monograph for the same botanical species. This establishes the designated modern raw material for the root term and neither assigns every vernacular use nor identifies a historical specimen. Kew’s Medicinal Plant Names Services records phalgu against several taxa, including Ficus carica, Ficus hispida and Ficus palmata.
