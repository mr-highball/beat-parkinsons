Title: Food allergy and IgE sensitization caused by spices: CICBAA data (based on 589 cases of food allergy)
Authors: D-A Moneret-Vautrin and colleagues
Date: 2002-04
Identifiers: PMID 12078423
Sources: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12078423/
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11297522_Food_allergy_and_IgE_sensitization_caused_by_spices_CICBAA_data_based_on_589_cases_of_food_allergy
Coverage: Original factual extract of the abstract and case descriptions in the primary manuscript copy.

The clinical databank contained 589 food-allergy cases, including a subgroup with spice investigations. The abstract lists two caraway cases among ten allergies related to the mugwort–celery–spice syndrome. The manuscript body labels case 283 “cumin (caraway)” and reports oral symptoms after cumin, positive caraway skin testing and negative caraway RAST. Case 77 describes shock after a combination including celery and cumin-containing sausage. Repeat celery ingestion reproduced shock, and caraway appears in a basophil assay. There is no documented standardized oral challenge to an isolated botanically verified caraway preparation in either case. The report gives no phonation, acoustic intensity or speech intelligibility result.
