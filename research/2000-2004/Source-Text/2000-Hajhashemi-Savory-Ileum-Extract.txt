Summer savory essential oil and isolated intestinal muscle
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10904162/
PMID: 10904162
DOI: 10.1016/S0378-8741(99)00209-3
Coverage: Factual original abstract extract.

Hajhashemi et al. report reduced contractions in isolated rat ileum after summer savory essential oil. The tested smooth muscle and concentrated oil do not give clinical rigidity or effects of fresh summer savory leaves.
