Title: Banana leaf dressing for skin graft donor areas.
PMID: 12880730
DOI: 10.1016/s0305-4179(03)00049-4
First published: 2003
Authors: Gore MA, Akolekar D
Journal: Burns : journal of the International Society for Burn Injuries
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12880730/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled split-area clinical trial in n=30 skin-graft patients comparing topical banana leaf dressing vs vaseline gauze on donor-site wound pain and healing. Uses topical banana leaves rather than ingested raw/baked banana fruit; inapplicable to dietary intake.
