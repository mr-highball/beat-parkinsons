Leenders and Oertel review parkinsonian diagnosis, motor manifestations and the role of positron emission tomography. In the differential diagnosis, visual hallucinations without medication attribution accompany early or rapidly progressive dementia as a clinical clue. Illustrative imaging and diagnostic history concern a different endpoint from visual episode frequency. The review supplies no new controlled visual outcome comparison and no dried-fig intake measurement.
