2000 extract crossover with osteoarthritis

Bliddal et al. compared ginger extract, ibuprofen and placebo in a double-blind, double-dummy crossover in people with hip or knee osteoarthritis. The overall ranking favored ibuprofen, but the ginger–placebo comparison across the full crossover was unclear. An exploratory first-period analysis favored ginger and ibuprofen over placebo. Review as an indirect extract result with a neutral full-crossover ginger–placebo finding. The primary abstract was verified, and full methods on preparation and sequencing are unverified.
