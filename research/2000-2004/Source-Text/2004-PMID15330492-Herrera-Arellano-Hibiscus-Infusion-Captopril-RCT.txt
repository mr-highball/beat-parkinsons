Title: Effectiveness and tolerability of a standardized extract from Hibiscus sabdariffa in patients with mild to moderate hypertension: a controlled and randomized clinical trial
PMID: 15330492
DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2004.04.001
First published: 2004
Authors: Herrera-Arellano A, Flores-Romero S, Chávez-Soto MA, Tortoriello J
Journal: Phytomedicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15330492/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Randomized controlled trial in 90 hypertensive adults (75 analyzed: 39 hibiscus, 36 captopril) comparing daily pre-breakfast aqueous infusion of 10 g dried H. sabdariffa calyces in 0.5 L water (9.6 mg anthocyanins) against captopril 25 mg twice daily for 4 weeks. Hibiscus infusion reduced SBP from 139.05 to 123.73 mmHg (ANOVA P<0.03) and DBP from 90.81 to 79.52 mmHg (P<0.06), statistically indistinguishable from captopril (effectiveness 78.95% vs 84.38%, P>0.560; 100% tolerability). A natriuretic urinary electrolyte effect was observed in the hibiscus group. Follow-up 2007 DB-RCT vs lisinopril (PMID:17315307, n=171 analyzed) confirmed -17.14/-11.97 mmHg BP reduction and plasma ACE inhibition (P=0.0001).
