Title: Broad bean (Vicia faba)--a natural source of L-dopa--prolongs "on" periods in patients with Parkinson's disease who have "on-off" fluctuations.
PMID: 10634260
DOI: 10.1002/1531-8257(200001)15:1<164::aid-mds1028>3.0.co;2-e
First published: 2000
Authors: Apaydin H, Ertan S, Ozekmekçi S
Journal: Movement disorders : official journal of the Movement Disorder Society
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10634260/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Verified Round 4 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Open-label clinical case series in Istanbul (Mov Disord 2000;15(1):164-166) enrolling 8 PD patients with ON-OFF motor fluctuations who had previously reported favorable motor responses to cooked broad beans. Only 3 of 8 kept usable motor diaries during 1-3 months of consuming cooked fresh broad-bean pod meals (Turkish culinary preparation with olive oil, onions, optional dill, alongside yogurt and regular PD medications). All 3 diary completers documented prolonged daily ON time and reduced daily OFF time compared with baseline diaries; 1 patient reduced carbidopa/levodopa intake. Crucially, the authors noted (p. 166) that some patients reported no clinical benefit from cooked dry seeds. Limitations: n=3 usable diaries (5/8 missing), selection for prior responders, open-label, concurrent PD therapy, non-standardized dosing.
