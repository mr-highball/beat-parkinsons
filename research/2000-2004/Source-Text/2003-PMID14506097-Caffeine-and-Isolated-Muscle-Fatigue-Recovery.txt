Title: Effects of caffeine on mouse skeletal muscle power during recovery from fatigue
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14506097/
PMID: 14506097
DOI: 10.1152/japplphysiol.00696.2003
Publication date: 2003-09-23
Date basis: NLM electronic date before the February 2004 issue.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract.

The study compares recovery measures in isolated mouse limb muscles after fatigue. At the lower caffeine concentration presented as physiologically relevant, force, work and net power changes were inconclusive for both muscle types. At the much higher concentration, net power after fatigue moved in opposite directions in the two muscles, with a transient increase in the fast muscle and a decrease in soleus. This is a recovery protocol, separate from endurance during stimulation and from perceived fatigue after coffee.
