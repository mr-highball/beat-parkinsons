Title: Usha’s 2001 Apasmara thesis and the later Mahapanchagavya series
Source: https://www.ayurvedlibrary.com/thesissearch.php
Library identifier: 2667
Publication date: 2001
Date basis: Institute of Teaching and Research in Ayurveda Central Library catalogue year.
Text coverage: Original library record and the later Caraka edition’s thesis citation. The original thesis body remains open.

The library identifies the study by Usha K. Satyanarayana with Gurdip Singh as guide. The later Caraka chapter attributes a comparison of Mahapanchagavya ghrita and Vachadi vati to that thesis. Its cited seizure-frequency values and remission percentage match the later treatment series published by Usha and Singh. This is a likely shared study family. The thesis body is required to verify the comparison-arm denominators, balancing, participant overlap and case-level definitions.
