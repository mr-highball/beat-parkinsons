Title: Grapefruit juice--felodipine interaction in the elderly.
PMID: 10945313
DOI: 10.1067/mcp.2000.107524
First published: 2000
Authors: Dresser GK, Bailey DG, Carruthers SG
Journal: Clinical pharmacology and therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10945313/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized crossover single-dose (n=12) and steady-state (n=6) clinical trial in healthy ELDERLY adults aged 70-83 years taking felodipine ER 5 mg with 250 mL grapefruit juice vs water. Mean felodipine AUC was 2.9-fold and Cmax 4.0-fold higher with grapefruit juice in both phases (high interindividual variability). Systolic and diastolic blood pressures were significantly LOWER with grapefruit juice in the single-dose phase (steady-state BP equilibrated via concentration-response curve), while heart rate was significantly HIGHER in both phases (greater and more prolonged at steady state). Authors warn elderly patients taking felodipine against concomitant grapefruit ingestion.
