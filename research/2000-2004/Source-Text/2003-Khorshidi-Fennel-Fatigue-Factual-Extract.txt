Topic: Fennel essential oil and menstrual fatigue severity
Source: https://applications.emro.who.int/imemrf/Iran_J_Pharm_Res/Iran_J_Pharm_Res_2003_2_2_89_93.pdf
Coverage: Original WHO-hosted article, Methods and Table 1, factual extract
DOI: 10.22037/ijpr.2010.18
Reported fatigue categories in order of none, mild, moderate and severe: placebo 12.3, 36.8, 38.6 and 12.3 percent. 1% fennel oil 23.7, 40.0, 32.7 and 3.6 percent. 2% fennel oil 33.3, 44.5, 18.5 and 3.7 percent. The authors report an overall fatigue p-value of .08. The main outcome was pain. Oil was given as needed across menstrual cycles. No toasted seed or Parkinson fatigue comparison was conducted.
