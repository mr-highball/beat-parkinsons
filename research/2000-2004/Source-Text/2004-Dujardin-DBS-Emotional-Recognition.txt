Emotional recognition after stimulation and cited symptoms

February 2004 issue. The identity-matched author-hosted journal PDF covers pages 202–208. Twelve nondemented people with Parkinson disease received bilateral subthalamic stimulation and completed facial-emotion recognition tasks and mood/executive assessments. Page 202 discusses hallucinations and a prior report of behavioral change that includes apathy. The current group has no hallucination-frequency or apathy-severity comparison and no fig or date-fruit exposure.
