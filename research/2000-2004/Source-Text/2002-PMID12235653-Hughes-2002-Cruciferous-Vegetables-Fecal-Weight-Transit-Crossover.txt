Title: Effect of vegetables, tea, and soy on endogenous N-nitrosation, fecal ammonia, and fecal water genotoxicity during a high red meat diet in humans.
PMID: 12235653
DOI: 10.1207/S15327914NC421_10
First published: 2002
Authors: Hughes R, Pollock JR, Bingham S
Journal: Nutrition and cancer
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12235653/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized 15-day controlled dietary crossover in n=11 men on high red-meat diets with or without 400 g/d mixed vegetables (134 g broccoli, 134 g Brussels sprouts, 134 g peas). Vegetable supplementation increased fecal weight (p<0.001), which correlated with shorter colonic transit time (r=0.594, p<0.0001). Multi-vegetable mix; does not isolate broccoli.
