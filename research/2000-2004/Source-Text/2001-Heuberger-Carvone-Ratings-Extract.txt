Title: Effects of chiral fragrances on human autonomic nervous system parameters and self-evaluation
Authors: Eva Heuberger and colleagues
Date: 2001-03
Identifiers: PMID 11287388; DOI 10.1093/chemse/26.3.281
Source: https://academic.oup.com/chemse/article-pdf/26/3/281/9754053/260281.pdf
Coverage: Original factual extract of the Results and mood, calmness and alertness figures.

Twenty volunteers underwent separate sessions for two carvone and two limonene enantiomers. The (+)-carvone inhalation session yielded no statistically significant differences between the tested intertrial score changes in alertness, calmness or mood. The corresponding p values ranged from .179 to .955 for alertness, .350 to .711 for calmness, and .304 to .643 for mood. This enantiomer yielded higher systolic and diastolic blood pressure during exposure. Clinical apathy and effects after ingesting caraway were not measured.
