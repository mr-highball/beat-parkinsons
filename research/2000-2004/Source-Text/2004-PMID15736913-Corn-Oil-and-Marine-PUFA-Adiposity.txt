Title: Omega-3 PUFA of marine origin limit diet-induced obesity in mice by reducing cellularity of adipose tissue
Publication date: 2004-12
PMID: 15736913
DOI: 10.1007/s11745-004-1345-9
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15736913/
Text coverage: Distilled author abstract and comparison with the original EASD congress material.

The published study compares high-fat formulations in C57BL/6J mice with different EPA and DHA fractions and base fatty acids. The abstract reports lower epididymal fat and reduced fat-cell cellularity when marine lipid is incorporated into some formulations. For the corn-oil identities and group weights, the companion congress abstract 662 explicitly identifies oil matrices and substitutions. Cohort overlap is plausible given the investigators, species, formulations and outcomes, but the published abstract alone is insufficient to map each conference arm to its full-publication counterpart. The effect pertains to marine concentrate in a formulated diet, not the independent effect of ordinary refined corn oil.
