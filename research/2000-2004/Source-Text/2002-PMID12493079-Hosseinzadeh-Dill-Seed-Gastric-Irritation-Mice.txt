Title: Effects of Anethum graveolens L. seed extracts on experimental gastric irritation models in mice.
PMID: 12493079
PMCID: PMC140031
DOI: 10.1186/1471-2210-2-21
First published: 2002
Authors: Hosseinzadeh H, Karimi GR, Ameri M
Journal: BMC pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12493079/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse study (Hosseinzadeh, Karimi & Ameri, 2002, BMC Pharmacol, PMC140031) evaluating oral and intraperitoneal aqueous and ethanolic extracts of Anethum graveolens seeds in HCl/ethanol-induced gastric mucosal ulcer and pylorus-ligation models. High oral seed extract doses matched sucralfate for mucosal protection and lowered gastric acidity (i.p. LD50 3.04 g/kg aqueous, 6.98 g/kg ethanolic). Limits: acute gastric acid/ulcer model in healthy mice; does not evaluate colonic motility, stool frequency, or constipation severity.
