2002 — Concentrated coriander leaf and branch extract case
Source: https://www.e-lactancia.org/media/papers/Coriander-WHO2002.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of original case narrative.

A 28-year-old lactating woman reported severe diarrhea and abdominal pain after a week of a concentrated aqueous preparation of coriander leaves and branches. Later symptoms included fluid loss. The case gives no measured blood pressure change, standing protocol or Parkinson outcome.
