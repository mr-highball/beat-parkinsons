Title: Efficacy of artichoke leaf extract in the treatment of patients with functional dyspepsia: a six-week placebo-controlled, double-blind, multicentre trial
PMID: 14653829
DOI: 10.1046/j.1365-2036.2003.01767.x
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1046/j.1365-2036.2003.01767.x

Published: 2003-11-20 online, Alimentary Pharmacology & Therapeutics 18, 1099–1105.
Archive scope: Factual text from original Methods and Table 3 on page 1103.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Holtmann and colleagues assigned artichoke leaf extract or placebo for six weeks to adults with functional dyspepsia. The authors analyzed 244 of 247 participants, 129 active and 115 placebo. They report greater improvement for global dyspepsia in the extract arm. Table 3 records a narrower result for the separate epigastric pain item. The mean summed improvements were 7.7 (SD 5.5) and 6.7 (SD 5.7), respectively, p=.1786. Individual symptom results differ from the global result.

This is an independent artichoke-only comparison cited by Giacosa. Different products, populations and outcome scales prevent subtraction of its effect from the 2015 blend trial. The 247-person conference abstract DOI 10.1016/S0016-5085(03)80909-5 uses overlapping investigators, design and sample size. Before treating it as another cohort, verify the endpoint totals and participant provenance.
