Rest tremor and subthalamic oscillation measurements
https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/24/50/11302.full.pdf

December 15, 2004. Amirnovin and colleagues recorded neuronal activity in 11 patients with Parkinson disease during surgery. Some patients had rest tremor, with little or no active tremor during the cued joystick task. The reported contrasts are neuronal oscillations during movement-related conditions. The study does not quantify a dietary change in rest-tremor severity or include fig or apple ingestion.
