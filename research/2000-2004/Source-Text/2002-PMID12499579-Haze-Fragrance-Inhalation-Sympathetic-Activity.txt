Title: Effects of fragrance inhalation on sympathetic activity in normal adults.
PMID: 12499579
DOI: 10.1254/jjp.90.247
First published: 2002
Authors: Haze S, Sakai K, Gozu Y
Journal: Japanese journal of pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12499579/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Human physiological study measuring systolic blood pressure low-frequency (SBP-LF) sympathetic amplitude and plasma catecholamines during essential oil fragrance inhalation in healthy adults. Pepper essential oil inhalation increased SBP-LF sympathetic amplitude 1.5- to 2.5-fold vs odorless solvent (p<0.05) and raised plasma adrenaline 1.7-fold (p=0.06). Mechanistic autonomic biomarker via essential oil inhalation; does not test oral cracked pepper or clinical orthostatic lightheadedness.
