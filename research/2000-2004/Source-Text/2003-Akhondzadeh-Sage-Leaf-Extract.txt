Akhondzadeh and colleagues, J Clin Pharm Ther 28:53–59, February 2003. PMID 12605619, DOI 10.1046/j.1365-2710.2003.00463.x.
Original full paper describes oral Salvia officinalis dried-leaf alcoholic extract versus placebo for 16 weeks in mild to moderate Alzheimer's disease. ADAS-cog and CDR-SB favored extract in the published comparisons. The abstract describes 42 randomized and Results report 39 randomized and 30 completing. Agitation was reported in one extract and six placebo participants, p=.09. These figures leave exact trial denominator reconciliation and any clinical agitation effect unresolved. Pain, Parkinson motor symptoms and consumption of fresh or ground household sage were not evaluated.
