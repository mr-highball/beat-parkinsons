Title: [An epidemic of epileptic seizures after consumption of herbal tea].
PMID: 12014242
First published: 2002
Authors: Johanns ES, van der Kolk LE, van Gemert HM, Sijben AE, Peters PW, de Vries I
Journal: Nederlands tijdschrift voor geneeskunde
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12014242/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Public health outbreak investigation (Johanns et al., 2002, Ned Tijdschr Geneeskd) in the Netherlands documenting 63 individuals developing malaise, nausea, and vomiting 2-4 hours after drinking a commercial star anise herbal tea; 22 required hospitalization and 16 suffered generalized tonic-clonic seizures. Morphologic inspection and NMR analysis confirmed contamination with Japanese star anise (Illicium anisatum) and the potent non-competitive GABA-A antagonist anisatin. Critical adult and pediatric adulteration safety record.
