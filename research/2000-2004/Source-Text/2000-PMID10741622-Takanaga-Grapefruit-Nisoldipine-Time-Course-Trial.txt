Title: Relationship between time after intake of grapefruit juice and the effect on pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of nisoldipine in healthy subjects.
PMID: 10741622
DOI: 10.1067/mcp.2000.104215
First published: 2000
Authors: Takanaga H, Ohnishi A, Murakami H, Matsuo H, Higuchi S, Urae A, Irie S, Furuie H
Journal: Clinical pharmacology and therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10741622/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Clinical pharmacokinetic-pharmacodynamic study in 8 healthy volunteers taking oral nisoldipine with water vs 200 mL grapefruit juice concurrently (G0) or 14-96 h after 7 days t.i.d. grapefruit juice. Coadministration (G0) caused significant reductions in BOTH systolic and diastolic blood pressure and headaches (3/8 at G0, 2/8 at 14 h, 1/8 at 38 h vs 0/8 at 72-96 h); elevated nisoldipine plasma concentrations persisted up to 72 hours.
