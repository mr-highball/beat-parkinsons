Advice to eat fish and mood in men with angina
Original factual extract of a published primary study

Ness AR and colleagues. Nutritional Neuroscience. 2003;6(1):63–65.
PMID 12608739
DOI 10.1080/1028415021000056069
Record https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12608739/
Primary study copy https://www.academia.edu/120206759/Advice_to_Eat_Fish_and_Mood_A_Randomised_Controlled_Trial_in_Men_with_Angina

Randomization assigned 229 men to fish advice and 223 to comparison groups without fish advice. The advice encouraged fatty fish and allowed fish-oil capsules as a substitute. The full methods mention salmon among fatty fish species. The report records no species-specific consumption estimate and no controlled cooking method.

Derogatis Stress Profile anxiety at six months gave an unadjusted difference of +1.94 (95% CI 0.03 to 3.85) in 392 follow-up respondents and a difference adjusted for age and baseline anxiety of +0.82 (95% CI −0.57 to 2.22) in 377 participants with baseline and follow-up data. Positive values indicate greater anxiety in participants assigned fish advice. This cardiac cohort does not identify a distinct Parkinson subgroup.
