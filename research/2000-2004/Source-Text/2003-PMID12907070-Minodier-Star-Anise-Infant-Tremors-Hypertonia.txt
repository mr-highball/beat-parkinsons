Title: [Star anise poisoning in infants].
PMID: 12907070
DOI: 10.1016/s0929-693x(03)00274-4
First published: 2003
Authors: Minodier P, Pommier P, Moulène E, Retornaz K, Prost N, Deharo L
Journal: Archives de pediatrie : organe officiel de la Societe francaise de pediatrie
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12907070/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Pediatric case series (Minodier et al., 2003, Arch Pediatr) describing 2 infants under 6 months presenting with tremors/spasms, hypertonia, hyperexcitability, nystagmus, and vomiting after star anise tea given for colic; contamination of Chinese star anise (Illicium verum) with Japanese star anise (Illicium anisatum / I. religiosum) was confirmed in one case. Documents acute neurotoxic tremor/hypertonia differential caused by adulterated star anise tea rather than culinary PD modulation.
