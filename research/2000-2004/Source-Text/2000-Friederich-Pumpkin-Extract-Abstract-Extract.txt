M. Friederich, C. Theurer and G. Schiebel-Schlosser. Prosta Fink Forte capsules in the treatment of benign prostatic hyperplasia. Multicentric surveillance study in 2245 patients. Forsch Komplementärmed Klass Naturheilkd 7(4), 200–204, August 2000. DOI 10.1159/000021344. PMID 11025395.

The author abstract describes 2,245 patients treated with a commercial pumpkin seed extract for 12 weeks. It reports a 41.4% decrease in I-PSS and a 46.1% improvement on the study’s life-quality measure. More than 96% reported no undesired effects in the article summary. The author abstract does not enumerate hypotension, posture, event counts by type or comparators. The full original body remains unverified.

The European Medicines Agency’s table for the 1998 Schiebel-Schlosser and Friederich report lists exactly 2,245 participants and the same I-PSS and life-quality changes. It describes an ethanolic seed extract and lists hypotension among adverse event types without a specific count. These publications may overlap and should not be counted as independent. The regulator table is not the original 2000 event data. Neither source establishes cold-pressed culinary oil or a Parkinson-specific orthostatic endpoint.
