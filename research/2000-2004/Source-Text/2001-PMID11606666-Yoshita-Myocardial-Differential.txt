Yoshita and colleagues at Kanazawa studied 14 people with probable dementia with Lewy bodies and 14 with probable Alzheimer-type dementia. Participants underwent cardiac tracer imaging and orthostatic assessment. Cardiac uptake was attenuated in the Lewy body group.
