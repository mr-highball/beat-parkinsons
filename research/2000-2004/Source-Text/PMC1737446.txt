The referred diagnostic series compared 25 confirmed sporadic Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease cases with 19 Alzheimer disease and 12 dementia with Lewy bodies cases. The clinical review treated nonhallucinatory visual disturbances and visual hallucinations as separate signs. Table 2 records visual hallucinations over the course of disease in 9/25, 2/19 and 7/12, respectively. The smaller nonspecific hallucination counts in the initial-signs table are a separate measure. Referral for suspected Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease conditions the sample. There was no dietary comparison or count of recurrent visual episodes per patient.
