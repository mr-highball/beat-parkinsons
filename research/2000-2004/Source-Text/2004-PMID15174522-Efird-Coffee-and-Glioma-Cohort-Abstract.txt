Title: Coffee intake and malignant glioma incidence in a managed-care cohort
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15174522/
PMID: 15174522
DOI: 10.1023/B:NEON.0000024746.87666.ed
Publication date: 2004-05
Date precision: The journal and PubMed specify May 2004.
Text coverage: Author abstract and corresponding original 2005 conference member. Main journal tables were unavailable.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18

The managed-care cohort included 133,811 adults in Northern California followed for incident malignant primary adult-onset glioma. Coffee intake came from an earlier member questionnaire. The author abstract reports a coffee trend probability of .05 and approximately 70% higher glioma risk for the heaviest reported intake category. The accessible author abstract does not supply the confidence interval for that contrast or consistently identify brewing methods.

Conference abstract 93 in the 2005 neuro-oncology collection repeats the same cohort size, outcomes and main comparison. It is a later report of the same cohort. Neither source measured fatigue severity or enrolled a defined Parkinson cohort.
