Title: Effectiveness of artichoke extract in preventing alcohol-induced hangovers: a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 14662662
PMCID: PMC280580
First published: 2003
Authors: Pittler, White, Stevinson, Ernst
Journal: CMAJ : Canadian Medical Association journal = journal de l'Association medicale canadienne
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14662662/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled randomized crossover trial in 15 healthy adults (18-65 y) given 3 capsules standardized globe artichoke (Cynara scolymus) extract vs inert placebo immediately before and after alcohol exposure (1-week washout). Primary hangover severity scores and secondary mood profile questionnaire, cognitive performance tests, and sleep duration showed no statistically significant differences between artichoke extract and placebo. Adverse events were rare and mild. Limits: very small n=15 acute alcohol-hangover model.
