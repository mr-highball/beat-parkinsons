Cumin bioavailability patent family first published in 2003
https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2003075685A2/en

September 18, 2003 public PCT application WO2003075685A2, priority March 12, 2002. Its US continuation records US20040052873A1, US20060141080A1 and US7514105B2 provide readable examples. Example 4 concerns rifampicin pharmacokinetics in rats. The US text also lists a CNS example table in which one cumin preparation is reported with an alprazolam change and no haloperidol enhancement. These are inventor reports about other drugs and concentrated preparations. The reviewed US examples give no levodopa test, Parkinson cohort or daily OFF assessment. Several national and PCT graphical tables still require verification.
