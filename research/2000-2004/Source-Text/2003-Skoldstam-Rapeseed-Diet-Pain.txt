Original Methods and Table 2 summary. Adults with rheumatoid arthritis received Cretan-style dietary guidance or continued an ordinary diet. The intervention allowed olive and rapeseed oils for preparation and canola-based spreads. Pain VAS changed from 32 to 20 versus 31 to 34, with the printed comparison p=.006. The assigned dietary pattern changed multiple foods. No separately changed rapeseed comparison or verified cold pressing.
