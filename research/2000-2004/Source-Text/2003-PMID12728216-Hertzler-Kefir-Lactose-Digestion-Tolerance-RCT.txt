Title: Kefir improves lactose digestion and tolerance in adults with lactose maldigestion.
PMID: 12728216
DOI: 10.1053/jada.2003.50111
First published: 2003
Authors: Hertzler SR, Clancy SM
Journal: Journal of the American Dietetic Association
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12728216/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized block crossover trial in 15 adults with lactose maldigestion consuming 20 g lactose portions of 2% milk, plain/flavored kefir, and yogurt. Plain kefir sharply reduced breath hydrogen AUC vs milk (87 vs 224 ppm*h, p<0.001) and lowered flatulence 54-71%, with negligible abdominal pain and diarrhea across treatments.
