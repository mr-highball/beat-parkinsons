The cross-sectional Veterans Health Administration study compared SF-36 health-related quality-of-life measures across chronic conditions, with 14,530 respondents identified in the Parkinson group. The full methods use Agent Orange as a veteran eligibility category. They mention the survey's diet and activity module without analyzing raw orange consumption and falls. The original provides no fall-frequency comparison attributable to the frozen exposure.
