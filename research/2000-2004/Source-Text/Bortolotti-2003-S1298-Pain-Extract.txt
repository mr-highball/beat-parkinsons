Bortolotti M, Morselli C, Romagnoli R, Amadini C, Miglioli M. Effect of red pepper on patients with irritable bowel syndrome. Gastroenterology. 2003;124(4 suppl):A190. S1298. DOI 10.1016/S0016-5085(03)80952-6. Adjacent page PDF https://www.gastrojournal.org/article/S0016508503809538/pdf.
After a two-week washout, 10 adults with IBS and without functional dyspepsia all received 2.5 g/day of red pepper powder for five weeks. The regimen used five gastroresistant 0.5 g pills daily, 1.75 mg capsaicin in total. Weekly abdominal pain diary scores were compared with the pretreatment week. The week-five pain score was 1.71 ± 0.5 versus pretreatment 3.08 ± 0.6 on a five-point scale, p<.05 within group. There was no randomized concurrent comparator and no cultivar or Parkinson population. This pilot used different methods from the later 50-person trial.
