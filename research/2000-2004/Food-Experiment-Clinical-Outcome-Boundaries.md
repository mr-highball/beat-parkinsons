# Clinical and experimental outcome boundaries

## Emotional recognition after stimulation and cited symptoms

February 2004 issue. The identity-matched author-hosted journal PDF covers pages 202–208. Twelve nondemented people with Parkinson disease received bilateral subthalamic stimulation and completed facial-emotion recognition tasks and mood/executive assessments. Page 202 discusses hallucinations and a prior report of behavioral change that includes apathy. The current group has no hallucination-frequency or apathy-severity comparison and no fig or date-fruit exposure. [Original source](https://www.ursulakhess.com/resources/DBDKHBD04.pdf) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2004-Dujardin-DBS-Emotional-Recognition.txt)

## Insomnia and falls in two multidrug Lewy body cases

July–August 2004 issue. The complete author copy on pages 216–217 describes resolved insomnia in the first case after multiple medicines were given. The second patient had unsteady gait, falls and insomnia at presentation. The report describes sleep at discharge and no further falls noted amid concurrent medication changes including galantamine, quetiapine, sertraline and melatonin. It supplies no fall-rate estimate, isolated comparator, or sage ingestion. [Original source](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8353899_Alleviation_of_multiple_abnormalities_by_galantamine_treatment_in_two_patients_with_dementia_with_Lewy_bodies) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2004-Holm-Galantamine-Sleep-and-Falls.txt)

## Rest tremor and subthalamic oscillation measurements

December 15, 2004. Amirnovin and colleagues recorded neuronal activity in 11 patients with Parkinson disease during surgery. Some patients had rest tremor, with little or no active tremor during the cued joystick task. The reported contrasts are neuronal oscillations during movement-related conditions. The study does not quantify a dietary change in rest-tremor severity or include fig or apple ingestion. [Original source](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/24/50/11302.full.pdf) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2004-Amirnovin-Subthalamic-Oscillations-and-Rest-Tremor.txt)

## Cortical receptor assays and the Lewy body case table

October 2004 issue. The University of Barcelona thesis includes the matching original article chapter, from title to Table I and figures. Dalfó and colleagues compared eight pure diffuse Lewy body disease cases, twelve with coexisting Alzheimer-type pathology and five controls. Table I provides demographic, pathology and postmortem fields. Recurrent hallucinations are discussed as a diagnostic feature without episode outcomes for these tissue donors. Salmon is a cited author’s surname. The original issue predates the 2006 electronic date displayed on PMC.
