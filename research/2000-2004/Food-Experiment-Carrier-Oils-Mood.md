# Almond and grapeseed carriers in symptom studies

## Almond carrier inhalation during radiotherapy

Graham and colleagues randomized 313 radiotherapy patients to three inhaled preparations, including an unfragranced carrier group, a scented preparation in carrier and a pure essential-oil mix. The original abstract reports no meaningful between-group depression finding on HADS. An independent study description identifies the unfragranced carrier as cold-pressed sweet almond oil. The accessible primary abstract does not specify processing. There is no dietary oil or no-oil comparison, and original arm-level depression estimates remain unavailable. ## Grapeseed as a shared massage carrier

Lemon compared plain grapeseed-oil massage with individually selected essential oils in the same grapeseed carrier in 32 psychiatric patients. The abstract reports improvement on depression and anxiety measures in the essential-oil group. The two groups share oil and massage, and the primary body and processing grade remain unverified. An existing paper uses a different grapeseed mixture in Gua Sha.
