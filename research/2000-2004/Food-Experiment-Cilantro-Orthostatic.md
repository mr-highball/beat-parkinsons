# Cilantro, blood pressure and orthostatic symptoms

## 2002 — Concentrated coriander leaf and branch extract case

Zabihi and Abdollahi reported severe diarrhea and stomach pain in a 28-year-old lactating woman after seven days of taking roughly 200 mL per day of a 10% aqueous extract of coriander leaves and branches. Fifteen days after the initial presentation, the patient reported fluid loss and other symptoms, which the authors attributed to adrenal dysfunction. The case does not report a measured blood pressure change, posture-provoked lightheadedness, a control or Parkinson disease. The concentrated extract exposure and timing are distinct from culinary use.
