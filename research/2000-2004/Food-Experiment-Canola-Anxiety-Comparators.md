# Canola and rapeseed oil anxiety comparator studies

## Developmental high-GLA canola comparison

Wainwright and colleagues compared borage oil against an engineered high-gamma-linolenic-acid canola oil. The GLA-matched diets were fed before conception, through gestation and lactation, and after weaning. The original author abstract reports no significant differences in elevated-plus-maze behavior in adult male offspring. It provides no numerical maze contrast and does not identify the oil as ordinary cold-pressed canola or test Parkinson’s.
