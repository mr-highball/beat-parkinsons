# Grapefruit, cabergoline and stride outcomes

## 2004 Japanese pharmacology meeting

The original index identifies a 2004 presentation by Nagai, Nakatsuka, Yabe, Moritoyo and Nomoto on grapefruit juice, clarithromycin and cabergoline blood concentrations. The record gives supplement page S172. The authors’ grant report confirms the original program entry. The presentation body is still pending. Study participants, dose, comparator and any clinical outcome cannot be recovered from the bibliographic record. [Japan Science and Technology Agency](https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=200902258943783917) · [Author grant report, presentation 16](https://mhlw-grants.niph.go.jp/system/files/2005/057141/200500885B/200500885B0002.pdf)

The February 2005 primary abstract uses the same author group and reports a small measured grapefruit juice comparison. The relation between their underlying samples remains open. [Later primary abstract and chart](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Grapefruit-Stride-and-Drug-Exposure.md)
