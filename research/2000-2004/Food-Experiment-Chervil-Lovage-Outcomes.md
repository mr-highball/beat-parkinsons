# Chervil and lovage leaf outcomes

## 2000 Chervil herb and root antioxidant assays

Fejes and colleagues compared standardized aqueous extracts from the herb and root of garden chervil, *Anthriscus cerefolium*. The herb extract performed better in the in vitro antioxidant assays. An assay used rat brain homogenate to measure induced lipid peroxidation. These measurements do not assess pain, cognition, attention or a Parkinson symptom after eating chervil. In a 2003 report, the group characterized activity of chervil herb flavonoids and root lignans and found lower radical-scavenging activity for herb essential oil. The reports cover laboratory assays and extraction fractions. [2000 primary report](https://doi.org/10.1016/s0378-8741(99)00171-3) · [2003 primary report](https://ishs.org/ishs-article/597_26/)
