# Filbertone in oils made from raw and roasted hazelnuts

The June 2002 author abstract reports a nearly constant filbertone enantiomeric ratio in oils from unroasted hazelnuts of different origins. The cold-press comparison concerns this ratio. The abstract gives no absolute concentration, sample count or numeric ratio. Original tables remain inaccessible. [2002 author abstract](https://aocs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1007/s11746-002-0527-1)

The March 2003 author abstract describes two batches of oils from roasted and unroasted nuts. It reports generally greater filbertone estimates with roasting and detection limits in the micrograms-per-liter range for each enantiomer. The available abstract gives no individual concentrations or sample counts. [2003 author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12696926/)

The separate 1999 paper describes an oil adulteration assay. Later results below quantification in some unroasted-kernel samples permit amounts below the limit. Oil lots, preparations and analysis methods vary. These are composition studies without human uptake or tremor measurements.
