# Seafood and Parkinson’s symptom evidence

## Oily fish advice and mood in angina

Ness and colleagues randomized 452 men with angina to advice to eat more fatty fish or no fish advice. The adjusted estimates were 1.29 points higher for depression (95% CI −0.29 to 2.88) and 0.82 points higher for anxiety (−0.57 to 2.22) in the advice group. Higher scores indicate worse symptoms. Follow-up covered about 83% of participants. The wider DART2 trial permitted fish-oil capsules as a substitute, and the AHRQ evidence report records trout and sardines among the fatty fish options. The study gives no estimates by species or cooking method.
