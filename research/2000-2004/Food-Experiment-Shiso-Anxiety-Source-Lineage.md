## Injected rosmarinic and caffeic acids in mouse conditioned fear

Takeda and colleagues tested individual compounds in a mouse conditioned fear paradigm. Injected rosmarinic acid at 0.25–4 mg/kg had a U-shaped reduction in defensive freezing duration. Injected caffeic acid at 1–8 mg/kg also reduced duration. The abstract reports no effect on spontaneous motor activity at effective doses. This is an anxiety-associated behavior after injection and supplies no fresh-shiso intake comparison, Parkinson diagnosis or gait-freezing estimate.
