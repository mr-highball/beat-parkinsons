# Recovered Parkinson surgery report

## Symptom reports after subthalamic stimulation in apomorphine users

Varma and colleagues followed seven people with advanced Parkinson disease. Two developed postoperative visual hallucinations and needed treatment. The authors describe less drooling and some relief of off-period pain in the two people still using infusion pumps. Two people stopped morphine previously used for off-period pain. The paper gives qualitative clinical observations for drooling and pain in a surgery and medication context. It supplies no food comparison.
