# Shiso and perilla follow-up

## Travel health formula with perilla and fatigue claims

Published April 21, 2004. The original Chinese application names perilla generically among eight materials in a processed travel-health formula, and makes an anti-fatigue claim. It describes animal pharmacology of the mixture and does not separate dried leaf or test Parkinson fatigue. Europe PMC displays the 2003 filing date for this hit. [Original source](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN1490047A/zh) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2004-CN1490047A-Perilla-Travel-Fatigue.txt)

## 2004 Seed-containing capsules and reported bowel outcomes

The Chinese patent published September 15, 2004 describes a multi-herb capsule for hemorrhoids and constipation. Perilla is specified as fruit or seed. The applicant reports 216 people with constipation within a 256-person treated series and an average 3.5 days to resolution. No parallel comparator or perilla-only response is reported. [Original patent application](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN1528378A/zh)
