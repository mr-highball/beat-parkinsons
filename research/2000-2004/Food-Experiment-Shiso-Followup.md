# Shiso and perilla follow-up

## Travel health formula with perilla and fatigue claims

Published April 21, 2004. The original Chinese application names perilla generically among eight materials in a processed travel-health formula, and makes an anti-fatigue claim. It describes animal pharmacology of the mixture and does not separate dried leaf or test Parkinson fatigue. Europe PMC displays the 2003 filing date for this hit.
