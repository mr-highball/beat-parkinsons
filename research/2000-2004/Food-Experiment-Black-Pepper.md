# Black pepper essential oil and swallowing

## Salad dressing observation in a 2004 patent

Ebihara and colleagues’ patent application was published on October 28, 2004. Its fourth experiment describes three people with dysphagia from the untreated group of the aroma study. They first ate vegetable salad with oil dressing, then the same preparation with black pepper essential oil. All three said the second salad was easier to eat. The order was fixed and the patent gives no objective swallowing score or Parkinson diagnosis. The oral exposure was essential oil. [Original patent, experiment 4 and Table 2](https://patents.google.com/patent/JP2004300103A/ja)

The patent's 100 aroma-study completers match the arm ages and attrition counts of the 105-person poststroke trial published in 2006. These are very likely reports of the same study. The salad observation is additional information about a small subset.
