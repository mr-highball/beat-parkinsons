## Major observations

- [Controlled fetal graft trials](../Procedures-and-Devices.md) did not confirm overall clinical benefit on the primary outcomes. Graft survival and benefit in some subgroups could coexist with persistent graft-associated dyskinesia.
- [Subthalamic and pallidal stimulation](../Procedures-and-Devices.md) had larger multicenter evidence and a broader U.S. indication. Five-year subthalamic follow-up showed enduring improvement in some motor outcomes alongside worsening in speech, gait, balance, and cognition.
- [ELLDOPA](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) found clinical differences favoring levodopa after brief withdrawal and a dopamine transporter imaging difference in the opposite direction. An effect on disease progression remained unresolved. CALM-PD found distinct symptomatic benefits and risks for starting with levodopa or a dopamine agonist.
- [Genetic and tissue studies](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md) identified DJ-1, PINK1, and LRRK2 variants and proposed stages of alpha-synuclein pathology. An early [GBA association](../Risk-and-Prevention.md) connected Gaucher disease biology with a susceptibility hypothesis.
- [Diet cohorts](../Food-and-Nutrition.md) recorded lower incidence with coffee or caffeine in several populations and higher incidence with dairy among men in one analysis. Differences by sex, dietary measurement, and residual confounding matter. An acute Mucuna study addressed symptom response and duration in treated patients.

## Minor observations

- The eugenol odor study measures immediate comfort and EEG patterns. It supplies an exposure-specific physiological record without a Parkinson anxiety or apathy outcome. [Original experiment](../Food-Experiment-Clove-Remaining-Outcomes.md#inhaled-eugenol-and-sensory-ratings-in-healthy-students)

- The Ethiopian case-control study tied a distinct legume, grass pea, to neurolathyrism. This differs from the earlier kidney bean reports in plant species, preparation conditions and neurologic outcome. [Lentil and kidney bean evidence](../Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md)
- [Acupuncture, coenzyme Q10, and neurotrophic factor delivery](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) produced different levels of evidence and divergent outcomes. GDNF results also differed between a blinded ventricular trial and an open putaminal study.
- [Constipation before diagnosis and later dementia](../Nonmotor-and-Daily-Life.md) extended longitudinal attention to nonmotor burden. Nursing, caregiver, voice, swallowing, and fall studies addressed outcomes outside routine motor scales. [Care studies](../Community-and-Care.md) · [Rehabilitation studies](../Movement-and-Rehabilitation.md)
- Six Parkinson’s participants in an uncontrolled Dai-kenchu-to study reported more frequent bowel movements and easier defecation. The study used a combined formula, and its statistical tests pooled two diagnoses. Its online publication in December 2004 preceded the February 2005 issue. [Primary study note](../Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#dai-kenchu-to-and-constipation)
- [Pergolide-related valve findings and reports of pathological gambling](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) raised safety questions beyond initial trial efficacy.
- [Prospectively recorded smoking](../Risk-and-Prevention.md) showed an inverse association with incidence, while a [nicotine patch trial](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md) found no short-term symptomatic effect. The studies address different exposures, populations, and outcomes.

## Comparison with [1995–1999](../../1995-1999/Observations/README.md)

- The earlier subthalamic stimulation series showed broader motor effects than thalamic tremor stimulation. Multicenter and five-year studies described persistence, domain-specific limits, and surgical harms. Sham controls also tested the promising graft observations from 1985–1994.
- The earlier SNCA and parkin reports showed distinct inherited forms. Later genetic studies expanded that set and described differing pathology among carriers of a single gene’s variants. These family findings address questions different from the earlier twin concordance estimate.
- The German retrospective coffee association received prospective follow-up. Earlier total dietary vitamin E findings were mixed, and the new cohorts separated food intake from supplements. The small controlled Mucuna crossover improved on the earlier open study for single-dose response, leaving longer-term outcomes open.
- Ropinirole and pramipexole moved from placebo comparisons to trials of initial treatment strategy. The later trials added longer-term trade-offs among motor complications, symptom control, adverse effects, and quality of life.

- Ogoshi’s survey asked participants which foods they found difficult to swallow. The later author account identifies baked sweet potato and gives no Parkinson-specific result. [Survey source trail](../Food-and-Nutrition.md#2000--japanese-survey-of-foods-reported-difficult-to-swallow)
- The 1999 Toneyama survey measured symptoms after usual meals. The 2000 Toyota Memorial series used several contrast boluses and observed tracheal entry only during unthickened cup drinking in its selected clinical series. Test completion by bolus and the diagnosis of the three affected participants remain open. [1999 survey](../../1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Japanese-Swallowing-Followup.md#post-meal-choking-and-food-records-at-toneyama) · [2000 series](../Food-Experiment-Japanese-Swallowing-Followup.md#sugar-solution-agar-and-cookie-in-the-toyota-memorial-series)

- An earlier Japanese videofluoroscopy report used a single barium liquid. In 2004, Nagaya's group compared sugar-water contrast by cup with contrast jelly by spoon. Thirteen Parkinson participants aspirated the liquid, and none of those 13 aspirated the jelly during imaging. The comparison varied the delivery method as well as the texture. [Earlier Japanese report](../../1995-1999/Food-Experiment-JSDR-Legacy-Early.md#1995-videofluoroscopy-with-a-single-barium-liquid) · [2004 comparison](../Food-Experiment-JSDR-Legacy-Early.md#2004-comparison-of-contrast-jelly-with-sugar-water)

- The 1998 Wisconsin manometry report names mashed potato in its protocol and publishes pressure results for water swallows. The 2002 Milan bedside study reports cough separately after solid and liquid intake but leaves the test foods unnamed. Neither report gives a potato-specific swallowing outcome. [Wisconsin manometry](../../1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#mashed-potato-in-a-wisconsin-parkinson-manometry-study) · [Milan bedside study](../Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#solid-and-liquid-bedside-swallows-in-early-parkinson-disease)

- The earlier controlled parsley diet measured biochemical responses to leaf consumption. This period’s colon perfusion study examined water absorption with an aqueous parsley seed preparation in rats. The route and plant part differed from the dietary comparison. [Earlier leaf study](../../1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Basil-Parsley.md) · [Colon perfusion study](../Food-Experiment-Basil-Parsley.md)

- Spinach extract improved the normalized peg-running learning curve after the previous period’s inconclusive rod balance comparison. A separate study measured conditioned blinking, extending motor learning rather than demonstrating spontaneous facial change. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Leafy-Grains.md)

- The four blinded zucchini food challenges introduce a distinct IgE-associated ingestion route after the earlier bitter-fruit poisoning and skin-contact observations. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md#2000-zucchini-challenge-in-four-patients-with-suspected-allergy)

- The Czech inspection and Slovak poison centre describe a ground-fennel tea incident involving separately identified thorn-apple seeds. The episode provides evidence about contaminated products and helps avoid attributing visual disturbance, somnolence or lethargy to the fennel itself. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Bok-Choy-Fennel.md)

- The lemon balm crossover studies found different patterns across attention accuracy, memory and alertness. The computerized button-response tasks leave progressive handwriting changes unmeasured. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Rosemary-Lemon-Balm.md#lemon-balm-and-computerized-reaction-tasks)

- The oily fish advice trial moved both mood estimates toward higher scores with broad intervals. Trout and sardines were among options, and capsules were permitted. Compared with the prior period’s fixed chitosan intake, this trial estimates the effect of advice rather than a single food exposure. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Trout-Sardine-Shrimp.md#oily-fish-advice-and-mood-in-angina)

- The rat odor-discrimination experiment used actual flaxseed oil to construct an omega-3-adequate diet across two generations. The measured result concerns learned odor discrimination after prolonged dietary deficiency and leaves treatment of Parkinson hyposmia open. [Dated research](../Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md)

The Alzheimer sage extract trial favored the extract on composite cognition, with no separate apathy measure. The earlier sage tolerability account described a urinary response. [Sage leaf extract and cognition](../Food-Experiment-Sage-Savory-Outcomes.md#sage-leaf-extract-and-cognition-in-alzheimer-disease)

Chervil herb and root aqueous extracts behaved differently in laboratory antioxidant comparisons. A subsequent fraction analysis is related to plant composition and supplies no symptom test. [Chervil and lovage symptom outcomes](../Food-Experiment-Chervil-Lovage-Outcomes.md#2000-chervil-herb-and-root-antioxidant-assays)

One canine macadamia report observes field tremor without reproducing it in four dogs experimentally exposed to kernels. A second series observes hindlimb paresis after kernels. These acute animal observations do not indicate a Parkinson effect from pressed oil. [Local source](../Food-Experiment-Macadamia-Hazelnut-Followup.md#macadamia-kernels-and-acute-movement-signs-in-dogs)

- A 2002 trial used oral corn oil alongside formal fatigue and unstimulated whole-saliva measures. A separate 2003 canine study adds measured smell thresholds, with exercise conditioning changing interpretation. [Evidence notes](../Food-Experiment-Corn-Oil-Comparator-Trails.md)

## Food experiment follow-up, November 14, 2026

- The early leaf-oil rodent studies documented sedation and nonspecific motor impairment in seizure and inflammation research. The measured exposures were concentrated essential oil. [sedation and motor impairment with leaf essential oil](../Food-Experiment-Bay-Leaf-Followup.md#sedation-and-motor-impairment-with-leaf-essential-oil)
- The [almond-oil dentifrice study](../Food-Experiment-Almond-Oil-Followup.md#almond-oil-dentifrice-in-an-adolescent-brushing-study) measured stimulated salivary flow after oral surface exposure. It provides a historical comparator for the later whole-almond chewing study, with route and drooling severity still unmatched.

- An isolated allspice constituent adds mouse forced-swim and tail-suspension results to food-level evidence. The behavioral and hippocampal endpoints have distinct meanings. [Primary outcome note](../Food-Experiment-Paprika-Allspice-Endpoints.md#isolated-eugenol-in-mouse-immobility-tests)

- The adverse acute rotarod finding after injected essential oil and scattered weaker forelimb grip after oral extract answer different motor questions. The 90-day gavage study recorded a nonmonotonic female grip signal with comparable global motor activity. An animal can change a selected strength or coordination measure while gross movement is similar. These records sharpen the 0504 phenotype search to skilled manipulation and reduce the likelihood of mistaking rotarod, seizure or aggregate activity for dexterity. In the same window, Rochefort exposed adult mice to many odors, including tarragon, and detected longer recognition retention. Plant ingestion, detection threshold and single-odor contribution remain separate questions. [Ribnicky and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fct.2003.11.002) · [Sayyah and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15325732/) · [Rochefort and colleagues](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6758329/)

- The early functional dyspepsia trial found lower later epigastric pain scores with ground pepper capsules and recorded two active-arm withdrawals for pain. A distinct IBS pilot used a pretreatment comparison. [Original trial and pilot](../Food-Experiment-Oral-Chili-Pain.md)

- Fatty-fish advice with optional capsule substitution provided a six-month anxiety comparison in a cardiac population. The trial pooled fatty fish. [Fish advice and mood trial](../Food-Experiment-Salmon-Anxiety.md)

- A tomato concentrate diet partly preserved a dopamine endpoint in toxin-treated mice while two dopamine metabolites had nonsignificant results. Clinical and behavioral cognitive outcomes were outside the design. [Tomato animal studies](../Food-Experiment-Tomato-Animal-Models.md#freeze-dried-tomato-concentrate-in-mptp-mice)

- A research hit about blackberry pie used those words to elicit articulator movement. A separate mixed-berry case recorded throat swelling after ingestion, with uncertain individual trigger. Neither source measured voice volume after fruit. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Blackberry-Voice.md)

- The concentrated leaf-and-branch case extends the earlier seed literature to a different plant part and records fluid loss after gastrointestinal symptoms. Standing blood pressure was unreported. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Cilantro-Orthostatic.md)

- The reported ability to tolerate thoroughly cooked onion in one allergic person adds an individual preparation contrast to the previous cooking and challenge literature. Its laboratory heating results and respiratory symptoms from cooking vapor address different routes or assays. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Onion-Speech.md)

- A six-week rat diet containing ground Cuminum cyminum shortened time to a first marker-colored stool. This anchors the whole-gut endpoint in the culinary preparation and does not measure stomach emptying or levodopa response. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Ground-Cumin-OFF-Time.md)

- The primary rapid response records oral contact with fresh leaves and an informal halitosis observation. Its suggestion of salivation lacks a measured flow rate. [Dated evidence](../Food-Experiment-Curry-Leaf-Clinical-Endpoints.md)

- The Slovak 2001 bulletin adds confirmed product sampling and a distinction between visual disturbance and formed hallucinations to the existing account of the same contamination incident. [Fennel seed follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Fennel-Seed-Clinical-Endpoints.md)

- The commercial Anethum tablet trial adds an adverse complaint loosely related to fatigue alongside the existing dill food allergy record. Both leave specific preparation and standardized Parkinson outcomes unresolved. [Dill follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Dill-Weed-Symptoms.md)

- Measured plasma carvone after peppermint and caraway oil capsules differs from short-term carvone odor ratings. The spice allergy cases retain cumin and caraway naming ambiguities. [Caraway follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Caraway-Replay.md)

- Rat absorption experiments identified a potential transport distinction between an aqueous clove preparation and food. Alanine transport did not provide information about levodopa exposure or OFF timing. [Clove outcomes](../Food-Experiment-Clove-Remaining-Outcomes.md)

- A separate 2001 orange-versus-eugenol study recovered an odor-quality P3 contrast and attention-instruction effects. The 1998 and 2001 results concern odor-evoked physiology and do not resolve cognitive direction for the food claim. [Allspice outcome follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Allspice-Endpoint-Replay.md)

- The florist case distinguishes a serious clinical episode after mugwort and diagnostic skin sensitization to tarragon. It supplies no clinical speech result following tarragon. [Tarragon outcome follow-up](../Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Motor-and-Face.md)
