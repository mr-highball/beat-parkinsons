The two earliest clinical comparisons took different routes. One used an oral alcoholic extract and cognition scales, and the other applied oil in lotion and measured agitation. The brief scoring exchange stays with the topical cohort.

The [experiment source review](../../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/lemon-balm-depression-nov18-20/README.md) links the related work, reports the original source depth and preserves the open clinical and publication trails. The preparation, diagnosis and endpoints determine whether each result bears on a frozen claim.
