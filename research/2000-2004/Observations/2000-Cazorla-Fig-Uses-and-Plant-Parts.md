# Common fig food and preparation records in the Cazorla area

The [2000 field thesis](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2000-Cazorla-Fresh-and-Dried-Fig-Practices.txt) distinguishes common fig food use from regional bibliographic indications. Fresh fruit and dried fruit are different preparations. The [1998 clinical allergy case](../../1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Edible-Fig-Adverse-Contexts.md) likewise distinguishes fresh and dried exposure in one symptom history. Its symptoms and context differ from the interviews. The [later Lucania account](../../2005-2009/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2005-PMID15978757-Lucania-Fig-and-Linden-Use.txt) attributes body shaking to a linden indication. The common Fig records considered here supply no comparative resting-tremor observation.
