# Visual histories and archived publication scope in 2001

Pasquier's [conference abstract](../Source-Text/PMC7101766.txt) recorded visual hallucination history in 25 of 34 people with probable dementia with Lewy bodies. The [later publication](../Source-Text/2002-PMID12271417-Pasquier-Lewy-Perfusion.txt) recorded 26 of 34. The shared authors, diagnostic sample sizes, tracer and 15 low-perfusion observations suggest overlapping samples. The full journal article is needed to check why the affected count differs.

The [retrospective diagnostic comparison](../Source-Text/PMC1737446.txt) distinguished impaired vision and hallucinations. Table 2 describes affected patients over the disease course. The [pentagon study](../Source-Text/PMC1737321.txt) measured visuospatial performance. Those measurements address different questions.

The [2001 apomorphine review](../Source-Text/PMC1408306.txt) brings together reports of visual symptoms emerging, resolving or absent during treatment. The [1990 primary series](../../1990-1994/Source-Text/1990-PMID2313313-Frankel-Apomorphine.txt) distinguishes symptoms during motor off periods from adverse reports during treatment. The 2001 review reuses that earlier cohort. Neither the imaging comparisons nor the drug series directly test dried-fig intake.

The [book-review catalog](../Source-Text/2001-JNNP-Book-Review-Index.tsv) records 25 individually accessioned notices at two JNNP page locators. The books have separate publication identities. Twenty-five PDF routes returned access errors, so these entries retain their original review depth. The EANM DOI object ends at p. 1263, while NLM's broader pagination extends to p. 1298.
