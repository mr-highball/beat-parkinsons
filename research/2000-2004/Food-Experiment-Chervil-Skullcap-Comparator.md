## Chervil as a possible comparator in a skullcap crossover

Wolfson and Hoffmann reported in March–April 2003 on 19 healthy volunteers given several skullcap conditions and a placebo. The original author abstract describes self-reported anxiety, energy and cognition. It supplies no objective executive-function test, chervil preparation or isolated chervil result. A later dissertation quotes a trial author’s 2008 personal communication identifying chervil as the placebo. The original Methods, placebo contents, plant part and result table remain open.
