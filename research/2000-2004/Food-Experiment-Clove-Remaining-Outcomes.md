# Ground clove and Parkinson symptom evidence

## Aqueous clove extract and rat jejunal alanine absorption

Aqueous clove and cinnamon extracts inhibited Na+/K+-ATPase activity in rat jejunal preparations and reduced intestinal alanine absorption. The absorption substrate was alanine. Neither levodopa pharmacokinetics nor the duration of motor OFF periods was measured. This assay leaves the effects of ground clove in a meal unresolved. [Kreydiyyeh primary article abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10930696/).


## Inhaled eugenol and sensory ratings in healthy students

Masago and colleagues recorded EEG and immediate sensory adjective ratings in 13 healthy students exposed to lavender, chamomile, sandalwood and eugenol odors. Eugenol ranked behind lavender in a derived comfort factor. Alpha-1 EEG activity changed after eugenol exposure. The study measured odor perception and electrophysiology without clinical anxiety or apathy assessments, isolated culinary ground clove, or Parkinson disease.
