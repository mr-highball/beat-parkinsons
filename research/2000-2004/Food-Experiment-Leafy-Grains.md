# Leafy vegetable and grain evidence, 2000–2004

## Spinach extract and motor learning in aged rats

Bickford and colleagues studied repeated running across a runway with irregularly spaced pegs. Aged rats on spinach-extract chow improved more on the normalized learning curve than controls (p < .001), and the paper found no difference in actual running times. This complements the 1999 study with a learning outcome. The preparation was dietary extract and the rats had no induced Parkinson model. A 2002 study by Cartford and colleagues also found better conditioned eyeblink learning in aged rats fed spinach-enriched chow. Conditioned blinking is an experimentally learned reflex and supplies no measure of spontaneous facial expressiveness in Parkinson’s.
