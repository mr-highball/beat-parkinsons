# Tree nut exposure and movement or airway findings

## Macadamia kernels and acute movement signs in dogs

Hansen and colleagues reported 29 canine poison-center cases in February 2000, with weakness, ataxia, tremor and occasional stiffness after whole macadamia kernel exposure. Four dogs given roasted kernels experimentally developed marked weakness and difficulty standing on their rear legs. The same investigator's 2002 follow-up states that tremor was absent in those four dogs and suggests weakness as one explanation for tremor in the field reports. In a separate Australian series in March 2000, McKenzie and colleagues documented acute hindlimb paresis, recumbency and joint pain in 13 dogs after raw or roasted kernels. The species, food preparation and acute poisoning context differ from human Parkinson disease. Neither series measured Parkinson bradykinesia, rest tremor, freezing, rigidity or dexterity. [Hansen primary article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10670081/) · [Author follow-up](https://aspcapro.org/sites/default/files/x-toxbrief_0402.pdf) · [McKenzie article abstract](https://eurekamag.com/research/003/837/003837700.php)

## Roasted and raw hazelnut food challenges

Skamstrup Hansen and colleagues published a double-blind placebo-controlled roasted hazelnut challenge in February 2003. Five of 17 birch-pollen-allergic participants with established raw hazelnut allergy reacted to the roasted kernels, all with oral allergy syndrome and generally mild symptoms. The challenge compares whole kernel preparations and provides no result for expressed toasted oil, clinical swallowing difficulty in Parkinson disease or isolated voice volume. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1034/j.1398-9995.2003.23959.x)
