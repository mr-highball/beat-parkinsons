# Allspice constituent and depression-like behavior

## Isolated eugenol in mouse immobility tests

Irie and colleagues published two rodent behavioral outcomes in June 2004. The primary abstract describes activity comparable to imipramine in the forced swim and tail suspension tests. Both compounds induced hippocampal brain-derived neurotrophic factor. Metallothionein-III expression was reported for eugenol. The public abstract gives no numeric immobility contrast or equivalence test. These experiments address performance in induced immobility tests and isolated eugenol. The food preparation and depressive symptoms of people with Parkinson disease remain unmeasured.
