# History and Context for 2000–2004

### 2000 — Incorporation of the Michael J. Fox Foundation

The foundation’s public filing records its 2000 incorporation as an organization dedicated to Parkinson’s research. This retrospective institutional record documents the founding date. [Original source](https://www.michaeljfox.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2023%20Final%20990%20Public%20Disclosure%20Copy.pdf)

### 2003 — Global Declaration on Parkinson’s Disease

Parkinson’s Europe records the launch of the Global Declaration in Mumbai in December 2003 following a World Health Organization working group. The organizer’s retrospective account connects the declaration with the 1997 awareness day and patient charter. [Original source](https://parkinsonseurope.org/campaigns/world-parkinsons-day/world-parkinsons-day-a-brief-history/)
