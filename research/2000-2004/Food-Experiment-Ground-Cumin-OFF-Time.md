# Ground cumin and food transit in rats

## Ground culinary cumin shortened time to marked stool in rats

Platel and Srinivasan compared individually assigned dietary spices in adult female Wistar rats. The *Cuminum cyminum* arm contained 1.25% ground spice for six weeks. Figure 1 reports 570 ± 22 minutes from marker feeding to the first marked fecal pellet with cumin and 780 ± 25 minutes in the corresponding control (mean ± SEM, eight per group, p < .05). This was a whole-gut endpoint. It leaves gastric emptying, levodopa absorption and hours of Parkinson OFF time unmeasured.
