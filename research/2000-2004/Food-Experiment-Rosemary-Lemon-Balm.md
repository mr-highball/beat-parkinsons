# Rosemary and lemon balm evidence

## Lemon balm and computerized reaction tasks

Kennedy and colleagues reported two acute placebo-controlled crossover studies in healthy young adults in 2002 and 2003, each with 20 participants. The 2002 extract improved attention accuracy at the middle level and produced some memory decrements. The paper reports no clear change in its attention-speed composite or component tasks. The 2003 study used dried leaf and found time- and dose-dependent changes in computerized memory, attention and alertness. These tasks included button responses and do not quantify letter size, handwriting progression, intelligibility or gait. [2002 original report](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0091-3057(02)00777-3) · [2003 original report](https://www.nature.com/articles/1300230)

## Extract capsules and acute self-rated mood

The [2002 Figure 3 review](Source-Text/2002-PMID12062586-Kennedy-Lemon-Balm-Mood-Extract.txt) found smaller drops in calmness after some doses than with placebo. It also preserves reduced alertness and the null contentedness finding. Acute Bond–Lader ratings and a capsule crossover in healthy adults give indirect context for the brewed lemon balm and depressive-symptom question.
