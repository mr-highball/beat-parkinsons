# Hazelnut preparation and oral symptoms

## Roasted hazelnut challenge in birch pollen allergy

Hansen and colleagues (2003) challenged 17 birch pollen-allergic patients with confirmed allergy to raw hazelnuts using whole roasted hazelnuts under double-blind, placebo-controlled conditions. Five of 17 reacted to the roasted challenge. Reported reactions were mild and associated with oral allergy syndrome. The clinical experiment tested whole roasted nuts and reports no effect of toasted oil on a swallowing scale. It documents a preparation and disease difference for 0481.
