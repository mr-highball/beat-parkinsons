# Fennel preparations and fatigue outcomes

## Fennel essential oil and menstrual fatigue severity

Khorshidi et al. randomized 60 participants, aged 17–25, to sequences of 1% fennel essential oil, 2% oil and placebo across three menstrual periods. The report lists seven treatment-program discontinuations. Table 1 gives moderate or severe fatigue in 50.9% with placebo, 36.3% with 1% oil and 22.2% with 2% oil, calculated from the published categories. The authors report an overall fatigue p=.08. The direction applies to a small menstrual-symptom trial of oil, with differential discontinuation and possible rescue treatment.
