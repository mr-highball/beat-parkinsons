# Fennel preparations and fatigue outcomes

## Fennel essential oil and menstrual fatigue severity

Khorshidi et al. randomized 60 participants, aged 17–25, to sequences of 1% fennel essential oil, 2% oil and placebo across three menstrual periods. The report lists seven treatment-program discontinuations. Table 1 gives moderate or severe fatigue in 50.9% with placebo, 36.3% with 1% oil and 22.2% with 2% oil, calculated from the published categories. The authors report an overall fatigue p=.08. The direction applies to a small menstrual-symptom trial of oil, with differential discontinuation and possible rescue treatment. ## Fennel drops in fixed-order menstrual cycles

The 2004 author abstract reports less menstrual fatigue and lethargy across treatment cycles, attributing a stronger effect to fennel. The 60 participants completed an untreated cycle followed by two fennel and two chamomile cycles. A fixed order and no concurrent placebo limit causal interpretation.
