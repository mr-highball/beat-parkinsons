# Almond oil dentifrice and salivary flow

## Almond oil dentifrice in an adolescent brushing study

Aguiar and Saliba compared the almond oil dentifrice Titoil with a low-abrasive dentifrice in 80 male military recruits aged 18. After 28 days, Graph 1 and its results text report no significant difference in stimulated salivary flow between products. The study measured saliva produced during a chewing-gum test and gives no spontaneous drooling outcome, culinary oil ingestion, cold-press grade or Parkinson cohort.
