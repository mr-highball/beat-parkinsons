# Piperine and upper-gut transit

## Isolated piperine and animal transit

Bajad and colleagues reported slower solid gastric emptying in rats and slower gastrointestinal transit in mice after oral isolated piperine. Gastric emptying of liquids was inconclusive at the cited lower exposure. The experiment concerns a black pepper constituent given as a compound. The different route and physiology matter when comparing it with an earlier inconclusive human powder pilot or later reports of bowel-proxy improvement in lesion models.
