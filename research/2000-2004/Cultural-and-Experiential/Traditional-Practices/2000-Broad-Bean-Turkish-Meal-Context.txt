The original describes Mediterranean and Turkish consumption of fresh broad-bean pods and dry seeds. Its recipe includes olive oil, onions and optional dill; the three documented patients also ate yogurt. The authors describe longer ON periods reported by medicated patients; some also reported no clinical benefit from cooked dry seeds (p.166). These are preparation-specific accounts, not a tremor comparison.

Reported direction: Favorable motor experiences with fresh meals; some dry-seed nonresponse.
Authenticity: Original three-page article inspected; this context reuses its existing clinical accession.
Efficacy evidence: Selected prior responders, eight enrolled, three usable diaries, continued medications; no isolated food or quantified rest-tremor effect. No new cohort.
Open trail: Earlier symposium report remains unlocated; dry-seed preparation details and participant overlap are unspecified.
