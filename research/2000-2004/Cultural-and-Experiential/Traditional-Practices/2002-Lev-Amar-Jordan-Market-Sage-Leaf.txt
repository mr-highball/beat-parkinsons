Community and context: The authors interviewed sellers in selected Jordanian medicinal markets and classified 304 collected substances. Their sage entry describes locally sourced Salvia fruticosa leaf and seller-reported use for intestinal illnesses and pain, with hemorrhage also listed.

Reported direction: Intended relief of intestinal pain. Preparation, intake, dosing and clinical results are unspecified in the inspected row.
Authenticity: The author abstract establishes the survey design and years. The original table names the species, plant part and voucher.
Efficacy evidence: The seller-reported uses supply no treated-patient results, comparison or confirmed Parkinson diagnosis. Salvia fruticosa is a separate species from the Salvia officinalis used in the later experiment.
