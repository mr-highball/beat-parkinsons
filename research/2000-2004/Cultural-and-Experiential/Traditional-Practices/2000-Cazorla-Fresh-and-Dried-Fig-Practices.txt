Date basis: Year on the University of Jaén doctoral thesis title page. The cited observations are field accounts, and the exact origin of the practices is unverified.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24 UTC
Location inspected: Title page and the Ficus carica results and discussion entry, printed pages 182–184.

Exposure and reported symptom: The entry records common fig as a food and household accounts of gathering and drying figs. The same species entry lists toothache and laxative uses from cited regional literature. Those uses do not establish ingestion of raw fruit or a clinical response.
Reported direction: Uses and intended indications are documented. The entry gives no comparative direction for rest tremor.
Efficacy evidence: Food preparation accounts and bibliographic medicinal indications have different provenance. The entry gives no clinical tremor diagnosis, measured change or Parkinson subgroup. Fresh and dried fruit are separate exposures.
Summary: Cazorla-area interviews document collecting and drying common figs. The species entry cites other regional reports on toothache and laxative uses. It reports no raw-fig and rest-tremor response.
Authenticity: Original institutional archive of the thesis. Personal details of individual informants are omitted.
