Community/context: Tuzlacı and Aymaz reported medicinal plants used in Gönen, Balıkesir, Turkey. The abstract distinguishes 73 wild and 11 cultivated species.

Reported direction: Indications include hemorrhoids, rheumatic, gastric and kidney complaints at aggregate survey level. The abstract does not pair these indications to an individual culinary plant, preparation or outcome.

Authenticity: Original abstract and bibliographic identity checked. It is one of two sources for the Molecules 2016 historical discussion of Turkish preparations.
Efficacy evidence: Counts of plants and reported indications are practice evidence. No measured symptom trajectory or Parkinson cohort appears in the accessible scope.
Open trail: Species and preparation tables still need an accessible original. No species-level inference was imported.
