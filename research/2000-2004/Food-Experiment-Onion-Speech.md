# Onion exposure and speech intelligibility

## Raw or lightly cooked onion allergy and reported tolerance after thorough cooking

Arena, Cislaghi and Falagiani reported a 44-year-old woman with acute reactions after raw or lightly cooked onions in the September–October 2000 journal issue. She reported tolerance of well-cooked onion and sensitivity to steam from cooking. Serum onion-specific IgE was 3.7 kU/L. Her serum bound unheated onion extract, while a laboratory extract heated at 100 °C for 30 minutes gave a negative result. The researchers avoided in vivo tests with onion. Neither the reaction history nor laboratory results measured voice or intelligibility in Parkinson disease.
