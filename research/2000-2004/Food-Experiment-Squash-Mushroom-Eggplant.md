# Squash, mushroom, eggplant, and zucchini evidence

## 2000 Zucchini challenge in four patients with suspected allergy

Reindl and colleagues used double-blind placebo-controlled food challenges in four patients who had reported allergic symptoms after zucchini. Symptoms included oral reactions, nausea, diarrhea or itching. The study used skin testing and IgE testing to distinguish potential cross-reactivity and reported that some zucchini allergens could remain active after heating. This is food-specific evidence about allergy in a selected population. It supplies no Parkinson's, dream enactment, or executive-function outcomes. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10932084/)
