# Food experiment evidence for sumac and cardamom

## 2013 Cardamom and ginger gelatin trial registration for dry mouth

NCT01915966, first posted August 5, 2013, compares a gelatin made with cardamom, ginger and orange juice with chamomile and lemon infusion in terminally ill adults with dry mouth. It lists dryness-related speech difficulties in a secondary assessment and excludes motor dysphagia with swallowing coordination problems. No results were posted in the registry. This gives a search lead for 0455, 0527 and 0748. It cannot resolve Parkinson speech intelligibility, voice volume or motor dysphagia. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01915966)

## 2014 Sumac extract and experimental diarrhea

Janbaz and colleagues tested a crude *Rhus coriaria* extract in mice with castor oil-induced diarrhea and intestinal fluid accumulation and in isolated rabbit jejunum. They found suppression of diarrhea and secretion in mice and relaxation of spontaneous and induced contractions in rabbit tissue. This provides a plausible direction to investigate for 0210. The exposure was an extract and the in vivo outcome was chemically induced diarrhea. It supplies no estimate of constipation after ingesting ground sumac in Parkinson’s. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.3329/bjp.v9i4.20879) · [Author institution](https://scholars.aku.edu/en/publications/pharmacological-basis-for-the-medicinal-use-of-rhus-coriaria-in-h-3/)
