# Virgin coconut oil in an allergic-rhinitis thesis

The Universiti Sains Malaysia repository dates Zainuddin's thesis to 2012. The 2013 thesis abstract collection, PMC3743989, records the same author, preparation, rhinitis population and comparison of oral virgin coconut oil plus an antihistamine versus antihistamine alone that appear in the 2016 full report. The abstract describes rhinitis symptom scores and more reported side effects in the oil group. It gives no measured fatigue result. The thesis, collection member and later paper are treated as one study.
