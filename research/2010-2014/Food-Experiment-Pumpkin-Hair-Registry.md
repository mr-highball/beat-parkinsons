# Pumpkin capsule trial registration

## Registered pumpkin capsule hair study and endpoint gaps

NCT01852487 was first posted in May 2013 after its reported completion in December 2012. Its sponsor, investigator, site, indication, duration and dose point to the 2014 publication reporting pumpkin seed oil capsules in men with hair loss. The registry lists 80 enrolled. The paper reports 76 randomized. Registry text repeats the capsule dose in both arms while the paper describes a placebo control. Read the original paper for actual treatment and measured seated pressure.

The official record lists scalp photographs and self-assessment as primary and secondary outcomes, with phototrichogram analysis as another outcome. Its results fields are empty. The registry supplies no standing measurement, lightheadedness scale or processing grade.
