# Spearmint species and regulated oil context

## Yerbabuena listing and essential-oil identity

The July 2014 INVIMA list gives Mentha spicata for the Yerbabuena leaf and stem entry. Its preparations column calls the standardized menthol and menthone essential oil Mentha piperitae L. The warning column includes Parkinson disease among the conditions named for an additional restriction on essential oil. The entry records no traceable patient case, culinary fresh-leaf consumption or daily OFF result. Earlier publication of the warning has not been established. ## Spearmint in the recombinant MAO-B assay

Mazzio and colleagues first published online August 8, 2012. Table 1 lists *Mentha spicata* as sample S64, in the recombinant human MAO-B IC50 interval above 0.2 and below 0.4 mg/mL. The extracts used ethanol and the method used selegiline for positive-control validation. The source leaves the spearmint sample’s part and fresh state unresolved. An enzyme assay does not measure levodopa wearing-off duration. The shiso, cilantro and marjoram notes refer to this same original assay.
