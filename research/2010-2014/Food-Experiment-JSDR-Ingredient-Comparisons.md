# Ingredient and texture comparisons in early Japanese swallowing proceedings

## 2010 full paper on udon and white rice

Iida, Katsumata and Fujishita's full paper first appeared online September 7, 2010. The videofluoroscopy study compared barium-marked udon noodles and cooked white rice in 22 healthy volunteers aged 19–38. The same author team had presented the food comparison in 2005. Mean chewing counts were 16.2 for udon and 19.5 for rice, and eating times were 17.7 and 24.7 seconds, respectively (both p < .05). In Figure 4 the forming bolus reached the vallecular category or farther before the swallow in 16 of 22 udon observations versus seven of 22 rice observations. Mean numbers of partitioned swallows did not differ significantly. The investigators noted that the images could not reliably distinguish active stage 2 transport from gravity-related spillage. The experiment measured healthy swallowing and provides no Parkinson food-specific estimate. ## Green tea thickener guide and healthy yogurt comparison

The 2010 title of Fusa and colleagues' guide names green tea as the liquid for comparing thickener preparation in clinical practice. A coauthor's university bibliography corroborates the guidebook series. The original abstract requires member access, and the title gives no clinical endpoint or Parkinson cohort. The same issue lists Ishida and Murakami's endoscopic-depth comparison of yogurt and jelly by position and swallowing method in healthy people. The title describes the healthy population, with no result values or Parkinson group.
