# Fresh ginger and pain

## Fresh ginger drink and conflicting menstrual-pain reports

The 2013 study enrolled 120 students with dysmenorrhea into a fresh-ginger drink group and a control group. The paper did not describe randomized allocation, blinding, or a matched control drink. It gives different baseline pain values in two tables and conflicting severity results between the figure and prose. The reported improvement in the ginger arm gives no reliable between-group estimate. The preparation used fresh ginger in warm water, with grating unspecified.

A 2015 paper under different author names repeats the earlier abstract and key methods and outcome data. Study independence remains unverified.
