# Banana, eggs, fatigue and related symptom evidence

## Mixed fish and mobility after hip fracture

Posted January 24, 2014. Planned 16 weeks of salmon, cod and herring versus meat after hip fracture. New Mobility Score was the primary outcome and falls were planned weekly. The record lists 14 participants and termination for insufficient recruitment. The registry has no posted outcomes. It gives no isolated salmon or Parkinson bradykinesia estimate. [Official ClinicalTrials.gov registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02045355) · [Local text](Source-Text/2014-NCT02045355-Mixed-Fish.txt)

## Oregano extract and mouse anxiety behavior

First published online December 21, 2010. In a light–dark box, injected oregano extract at 60 mg/kg increased time in the light compartment by 35% versus vehicle (p = .046). The oral repeat-dose experiment measured forced-swim behavior. The two experiments differ in route and outcome. Human dried-oregano anxiety and Parkinson OFF time were outside the study. Overlap with the related patent remains unresolved. [British Journal of Nutrition original](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114510004940) · [Local text](Source-Text/2010-Mechan-Oregano-Anxiety.txt)
