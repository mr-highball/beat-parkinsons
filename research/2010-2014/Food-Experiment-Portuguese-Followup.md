# Portuguese-language dietary studies, 2010–2014

## Dietary iron and clinical findings in Porto Alegre

Chaves’s PUCRS dissertation, defended March 28, 2011 and listed as publicly available June 2, 2011, compared 52 older people with Parkinson’s and 30 controls. The study collected a 24-hour recall and a three-day food record and summarized intake as nutrients. In the Parkinson’s group, Table 8 gives dietary iron versus serum iron r = .265 (p = .058), ferritin r = .052 (p = .712), and transferrin r = .256 (p = .067). Table 9 compares serum markers with UPDRS II and III, including serum iron versus UPDRS III r = −.018 (p = .898). Table 10 compares serum markers across tremor-dominant, akinetic-rigid and mixed presentations. The tables contain no estimates linking individual foods or dietary iron to a Parkinson’s symptom. [Repository record, public availability](https://tede2.pucrs.br/tede2/handle/tede/2643?mode=full) · [Dissertation, Tables 8–10](https://tede2.pucrs.br/tede2/bitstream/tede/2643/1/431555.pdf) · [OpenAlex W129131192](https://openalex.org/W129131192)

Chaves also coauthored a later biomarker study in 40 Parkinson’s patients and 46 controls recruited at the University Hospital of Santa Maria. Its methods describe blood biomarkers and clinical scores. Its cohort differs in setting, sample counts and recruitment from the Porto Alegre dietary study. [2016 primary article](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0146129)

## Diet diaries and levodopa timing at Santa Casa

Morais and colleagues evaluated 36 adults aged 60 or older at the Santa Casa Movement Disorders and Dementia Clinic. Twenty-three usable three-day diet diaries entered the intake analyses. Table 2 gives mean fiber intake of 13.5 g/day in 14 men and 13.6 g/day in nine women. Table 3 relates nutrient totals to anthropometry and other nutrients. The paper discusses constipation in relation to prior research. It gives no individual food or fiber intake estimate against measured constipation or another Parkinson’s symptom. The September 2013 issue entered SciELO on November 4, 2013. [Primary article, Tables 1–3](https://www.scielo.br/j/rbgg/a/ZHGwgRKnwsKt7wby5kMM44y/?lang=pt) · [Local article text](Source-Text/2013-Morais-Porto-Alegre-Diet-Diaries.txt) · [OpenAlex W1971777657](https://openalex.org/W1971777657)

Fracasso and colleagues reported a separate analysis in the June 2013 issue of *Revista Chilena de Nutrición*. Among 34 patients, 16 (47.1%) took levodopa with meals or without the recommended interval, and the mean protein intake was 70.9 g/day. The article reports exposure and dose timing, with no comparison of individual food or meal timing against observed symptom response. Both publications share three authors, the same clinic, October 2010 to August 2011 recruitment and ethics project 10.681. Treat the reports as overlapping. The publications give no patient-level mapping. [Primary article, Methods and Results](https://www.scielo.cl/scielo.php?pid=S0717-75182013000200001&script=sci_arttext)
