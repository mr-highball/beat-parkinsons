# Turmeric preparation and symptom outcomes

## Speech fluency and motor complications in the ZAZ2 trial

Pan and colleagues’ 2011 Parkinson trial compared a soluble 14-ingredient ZAZ2 granule containing *Curcuma longa* with a granule containing five other botanicals. Table 3 reports nonfluent speech at 13 weeks as 0.65 ± 0.33 in the ZAZ2 group and 1.02 ± 0.36 in the comparison group, with respective baseline scores of 1.08 ± 0.74 and 1.12 ± 0.59. The week-13 between-group comparison is marked p < .01. The outcome is a secondary rating of nonfluent speech and gives no acoustic voice volume. Table 2 gives conventional UPDRS Part IV scores of 2.7 ± 1.3 and 3.0 ± 1.4 at week 13 (p < .05), without hours of daily OFF, falls, handwriting or freezing frequency. A lower insomnia/nightmare rating also provides no measure of daytime sleepiness. ## Whole turmeric capsules and working memory

Lee and colleagues’ 2014 factorial trial assigned 48 adults with untreated prediabetes to capsules containing whole turmeric, cinnamon, both, or placebo at a standardized breakfast. The authors report working memory scores of 2.6 before and 2.9 after turmeric on a three-point scale (p = .05). The acute test covered six hours and the authors did not specify a fresh grated preparation. Working memory overlaps with attention, and the trial did not have a separate attention endpoint or Parkinson cohort. [Primary report, methods and results](https://apjcn.nhri.org.tw/server/APJCN/23/4/581.pdf)

## Registered curcumin cognition comparison in schizophrenia

First posted April 4, 2014. This eight-week trial assigned a Theracurmin formulation or placebo in schizophrenia. The primary MCCB composite includes a wider cognitive battery with an attention and vigilance domain. The study population and formulation differ from the Parkinson grated-turmeric claim. The official results subsequently posted in 2019 give composite scores only. [Original record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02104752) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2014-NCT02104752-Curcumin-Cognition-Protocol.txt)

## Solid lipid curcumin and digit vigilance in older adults

First published online October 2, 2014. Cox and colleagues randomized 60 healthy older adults to a 400-mg Longvida formulation or placebo. One-hour digit vigilance accuracy favored the formulation in the baseline-adjusted analysis (p = .046) and was inconclusive after adding demographics (p = .168). The difference was no longer apparent at three hours. After four weeks, the serial-three working-memory task favored treatment with demographic adjustment (p = .044). The published formulation and healthy cohort leave grated turmeric and Parkinson attention untested.
