# Pulse foods and cognitive outcomes

## Registered pulse enriched foods and cognition

NCT01495832 was first posted December 20, 2011. The completed parallel trial assigned adults age 50–80 with overweight or obesity to pulse-enriched or comparison foods for 12 weeks. Cognitive function was part of the study protocol. Peas appear in the list of pulses participants had to be able to eat. The recorded assignment does not specify a separate cooked green-pea food, and the registry gives no cognitive results. Exact registration searches did not locate the cognitive publication. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01495832)
