# HAL and French-language dietary research

## 2010 and 2011 levodopa videofluoroscopy reports

The American Academy of Neurology program lists Viallet, Baudo, Teston, Gayraud and Sert for poster P04.138 on April 14, 2010. It uses the same English title as the later French conference record. The program establishes the earlier presentation listing. The full 2010 abstract and its sample size remain unverified. The AAN announced a general February 17 online release for regular abstracts, but the release of this specific abstract could not be confirmed. [Official 2010 program, P04.138](https://www.aan.com/globals/axon/assets/6849.pdf) · [AAN abstract release notice](https://www.aan.com/globals/axon/assets/6988.pdf)

The bilingual HAL notice cites the April 26–29, 2011 Journées de Neurologie de Langue Française in Paris, p. 99. HAL records an April 26, 2017 deposit and provides the abstract without an attached full report. The abstract describes 22 people with Parkinson’s, an overnight medication withdrawal followed by an acute levodopa ON assessment, and videofluoroscopy using liquid and solid boluses. Mean motor UPDRS scores were 40 OFF and 14 ON. The authors report significant improvement across the three swallowing phases and the total 24-item dysphagia score. The abstract gives no ingredients, preparation details or separate food effect. The study measured the levodopa comparison. The 2009 pilot used 14 items and ten participants. Sample overlap and a matching full journal publication remain unverified.
