# Kidney bean and lentil allergy, 2010–2014

## Kidney bean and lentil allergy tests

Rougé and colleagues’ first-online August 2010 case report describes severe allergy after white kidney bean and identifies putative bean allergens. The case details beyond the publisher preview remain unavailable. Kasera and colleagues’ 2011 clinical study found positive skin tests to kidney bean in 78 of 355 and to lentil in seven of 355 people selected for asthma or allergic rhinitis and a history of legume allergy. Skin-test sensitization does not measure the effect of eating these foods.
