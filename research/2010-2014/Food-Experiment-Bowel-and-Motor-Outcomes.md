# Bowel outcomes and selected motor or nonmotor follow-up

## Corn oil and olive oil feeding crossover

First published electronically October 23, 2014. Fifty-four adults entered the lipid efficacy analysis of two 21-day corn-oil and extra-virgin olive-oil feeding conditions (four tablespoons daily). The adverse-event section reports constipation in three participants during the olive-oil period. It presents events exceeding a frequency threshold and treats the arm lists differently from a complete table. The exact corn-arm constipation count and severity are unavailable. Three corn-period diarrhea events are reported. No oil-free comparator or Parkinson cohort. [Original crossover paper adverse-event results](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1933287414003444) · [Local text](Source-Text/2014-Maki-Corn-Oil-EVOO-Bowel-Events.txt)

## Corn and olive oil crossover registration

First posted August 20, 2013. Completed randomized dietary oil comparison in adults with hypercholesterolemia. No registry results are posted. The lipid publication provides a narrative adverse-event report for the same cohort. It gives no structured constipation severity comparison. [Official ClinicalTrials.gov record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01925716) · [Local text](Source-Text/2013-NCT01925716-Corn-Olive-Bowel-Scope.txt)

## Corn oil comparator in an older adult function trial

First posted March 4, 2011. Fish oil and muscle function comparison with corn-oil capsules as comparator. The registry posts no results. The original 2015 article reports safety counts in starters. The study did not enroll a Parkinson constipation cohort. [Official ClinicalTrials.gov record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01308957) · [Local text](Source-Text/2011-NCT01308957-Corn-Capsule-Bowel-Scope.txt)

## Salmon meals and daytime functioning

Published May 2014. Ninety-five forensic inpatients were assigned to salmon or meat meals three times weekly. The trial examined nocturnal sleep and a daytime functioning rating. It did not use an Epworth daytime sleepiness outcome or test trout. This is an outcome-specific reread of the original already listed in the period catalog. [Original sleep and daytime functioning report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4013386/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2014-Hansen-Salmon-Daytime-Function.txt)
