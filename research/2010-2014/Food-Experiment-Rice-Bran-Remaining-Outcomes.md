# Refined rice bran oil and Parkinson symptom evidence

## Trained sensory panel and fresh rice bran oil flavour

Eight trained panellists tracked the intensity of fresh rice bran, canola, corn and palm olein oils during eating, using soybean oil as the maximum reference. The authors characterize the rice bran oil as having moderate overall flavour intensity that quickly subsided. Nine trained assessors described a sweet flavour and rounded aroma. Rating the oil stimulus is distinct from measuring a change in olfactory impairment. The recorded preparation was unheated, and fresh does not specify crude, refined or cold-pressed grade. [Hayakawa primary article](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/cookeryscience/47/6/47_333/_article/-char/en).
