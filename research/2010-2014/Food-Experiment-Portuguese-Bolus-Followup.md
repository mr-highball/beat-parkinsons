# Portuguese-language Parkinson swallowing studies, 2010–2014

## Earlier public abstract of the Parkinson wafer and water study

Aretuza Zaupa Gasparim's December 16, 2010 published thesis abstract identifies her 2007 Universidade Tuiuti do Paraná dissertation and a Parkinson sample of 38. The investigator, sample size, disease staging, test approach and airway outcomes align with the 2011 journal report that identifies the wafer and water preparations. The 2010 abstract and 2011 article represent companion publications. The university's public TEDE surname search and the national BDTD exact-name search returned zero records on November 12, 2026. The separate Pergamum catalog timed out. The full 2007 dissertation and its original public release date remain open. ## Creamy yogurt and water in Parkinson videofluoroscopy

Sebben and colleagues reviewed 20 videofluoroscopy exams from patients with diagnosed Parkinson disease in Passo Fundo, Brazil. The charts were from 2007–2011 and the report appeared in the 2013 issue. The pasty bolus was creamy Danet yogurt mixed with barium and the liquid bolus was water with barium. The authors omitted solids from their analysis because the reports showed no elicited swallow for solids. The study gives no identity for the excluded solid. Figures 3–4 show laryngotracheal aspiration in three patients with the liquid contrast and four with the yogurt contrast. Laryngeal penetration is shown in eleven patients with liquid and nine with yogurt. Endpoint denominators vary across the charts. The results measure instrumental airway entry during those preparations. They supply no baked-potato choking result. [Primary report, printed pp. 293–297, Methods and Figures 3–4](https://d1xe7tfg0uwul9.cloudfront.net/sbgg.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/2013-4.pdf#page=49) · [Publisher article](https://ggaging.com/details/129/pt-BR/achados-videofluoroscopicos-na-degluticao-de-pacientes-com-doenca-de-parkinson)

## French bread and a pudding-texture grape preparation during swallowing therapy

The University of São Paulo released Marcela Maria Alves da Silva's thesis on October 21, 2014. The six men with Parkinson disease received conventional swallowing therapy, with three also receiving electromyographic biofeedback. Videofluoroscopy used a half slice of pão francês 1 cm thick, 10 mL of a pudding-texture bolus made from diet grape drink powder, water and thickener, and 10 mL of a liquid water bolus. All three preparations included Bariogel barium contrast. Tables 7–8 give individual oral and pharyngeal residue grades for each texture at baseline, three months and six months. Case 3 had persistent oral residue with bread and vallecular residue with liquid. Table 9 compares the therapy groups and times for residue outcomes. The thesis supplies no by-food aspiration or choking count and tests no baked potato. The 2021 article reproduces the same six participants and their oral intake, dysphagia severity and quality of life scores.
