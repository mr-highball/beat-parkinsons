# Parkinson neurobehavior review context, 2010–2014

## Blonder and Slevin on emotional dysfunction

The 2011 review (PMID 21876260, PMC3177157, DOI 10.3233/BEN-2011-0329) describes clinical apathy prevalence, scales and longitudinal reports. It discusses rigidity in Parkinson’s motor phenotypes and fatigue in depression assessment. Sage appears as a cited surname. Dates refer to time or string matches within other words. The authors do not compare sage or date-fruit intake with symptom severity. The local source extract contains front matter. The full author manuscript and published version were verified separately. ## Bugalho and Oliveira-Maia on impulse control disorders

The review appeared in its 2013 issue after online publication in 2012 (PMID 23242359, PMC5215623, DOI 10.3233/BEN-129019). It summarizes mixed executive-function comparisons in Parkinson’s impulse-control disorders, discusses visual hallucinations alongside dopaminergic treatment and cites apathy among symptoms associated with disease laterality. Pain appears among general nonmotor symptoms. The carrot match comes from a metaphorical reference title, fig from figure notation and date from temporal phrasing. The reviewed clinical comparisons do not assign those foods.
