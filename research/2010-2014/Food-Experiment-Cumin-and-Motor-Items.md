# Cumin, edible oils and specific motor outcomes

## Corn oil comparator and Parkinson motor scale scope

Published in 2014. In 24 people with Parkinson disease, DHA plus EPA was compared with equicaloric corn oil over six months. The accessible abstract reports inconclusive comparisons for total UPDRS and Hoehn and Yahr score. The individual resting tremor item and oil-refining grade could not be verified. The full paper and item tables remain open. [Original journal abstract and comparator documentation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.baga.2014.03.004) · [Local text](Source-Text/2014-Pomponi-Corn-Oil-Tremor-Scope.txt)

## Sesame seed oil and movement outcomes in lesioned mice

First published November 17, 2011. Sesame seed oil was examined in 6-hydroxydopamine-lesioned mice. The paper evaluates rotational behavior and locomotor or biochemical outcomes after pretreatment. A clinical rigidity scale is outside its model, and cold pressing was not verified. [Original animal study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22089932/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2011-Ahmad-Sesame-Seed-Oil-Motor-Items.txt)
