# Drugs and Remedies

### 2012 — Opicapone absorption after a high-fat meal

Almeida and colleagues first published the phase 1 study on December 18, 2012. The food sub-study compared one dose of opicapone after overnight fasting with the same dose after a high-fat, high-calorie meal in 12 healthy male volunteers. Drug exposure decreased with the meal. The same 12-person single-dose comparison appears in a 2017 report, which gives fed-to-fasted ratios of 31.73% for peak concentration and 47.11% for area under the concentration curve through the last measurement. The accessible records identify the meal class without specifying its ingredients. The study measured drug pharmacokinetics in healthy volunteers. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40262-012-0024-7) · [2017 report and repeat-dose study](../2015-2019/Drugs-and-Remedies.md#2017--repeat-dose-opicapone-and-a-moderate-meal)

### 2013 — Pramipexole delayed-start trial

PROUD tested early versus delayed pramipexole in early disease. At 15 months the groups showed no significant difference on clinical scores or dopamine transporter imaging, despite symptomatic benefit during the placebo-controlled phase. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23726851/)

### 2013 — Inosine target engagement in SURE-PD

The randomized dose-ranging study demonstrated that inosine raised urate in blood and cerebrospinal fluid. Its main questions concerned target engagement, safety and tolerability. It provided the rationale for a larger progression trial. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3940333/)

### 2014 — Initial medication choice in PD MED

An open-label pragmatic trial randomized 1,620 people to start levodopa, a dopamine agonist or an MAO-B inhibitor. Patient-rated mobility modestly favored levodopa over the pooled levodopa-sparing groups. Treatment discontinuation due to adverse effects differed by drug class. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24928805/)

### 2014 — High-dose coenzyme Q10 in early Parkinson’s

The phase 3 QE3 randomized trial was stopped for futility. Changes in total UPDRS provided no evidence of benefit at the tested doses. The result directly revisited encouraging signals from an earlier small trial. [Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2014.131)
