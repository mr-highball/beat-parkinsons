# Clinical and experimental outcome boundaries

## Visual hallucination prevalence and Parkinson sign criteria

January 14, 2010 online. Goto and colleagues report visual hallucinations in 16 of 19 selected dementia-with-Lewy-bodies patients and no reports among the Alzheimer comparators. Thirteen of 19 had parkinsonism by criteria including rest tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia or impaired postural reflexes. A patient could meet the motor criterion with any one of these findings. The study reports imaging discrimination and gives no episode counts, isolated tremor score or fig exposure. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7964226/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2010-Goto-DLB-Hallucination-Diagnostic-Context.txt)

## Orthostatic blood pressure falls and baseline rigidity

January 19, 2010 issue. Goldstein and colleagues assessed 23 people with Parkinson disease for olfaction and autonomic function. The cohort had baseline cogwheel rigidity. The relevant fall refers to a drop in systolic blood pressure during Valsalva testing or orthostasis. The paper uses composite parkinsonism scores. It gives no frequency of body falls, rigidity-specific change or basil intervention. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2809034/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2010-Goldstein-Autonomic-Pressure-and-Rigidity.txt)

## Planned depression and pain domains in the FACETS trial

June 16, 2010. The full protocol for the multiple-sclerosis FACETS trial prespecifies a group fatigue-management intervention. Secondary measures include the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale and the bodily-pain dimension of the SF-36v2. The design contains no rosemary or date-fruit intervention and the protocol reports no treatment direction for either measure. The identity is ISRCTN76517470. [Original source](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-2377-10-43) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2010-Thomas-FACETS-Protocol-Mood-and-Pain.txt)

## FACETS registration and nonculinary intervention scope

March 17, 2010 first registered, submitted February 2. ISRCTN76517470 identifies a completed multicenter group self-management trial in adults with multiple sclerosis and fatigue. The assigned program combines cognitive behavioral and energy-effectiveness approaches compared with current local practice. The protocol and later findings share this identifier and provide no culinary comparison. [Original source](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN76517470) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2010-ISRCTN76517470-FACETS-Registration.txt)

## FACETS results for measured depression and pain domains

May 2013 online and October 2013 issue. The complete final report for ISRCTN76517470 randomized 164 people. Table 2 reports a four-month fatigue-severity contrast favoring the program. The Secondary outcomes section reports no statistically significant arm difference in HADS or the SF-36 subscales other than Vitality at either follow-up. The original compares behavioral treatment in multiple sclerosis without rosemary, dates or Parkinson participants. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3786656/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2013-Thomas-FACETS-Mood-and-Pain-Results.txt)

## Bone pain and rest tremor in the Gaucher registry

November 2010 online, with a 2011 issue. Rosenbloom and colleagues report baseline bone-pain presence in 25 of 42 type 1 Gaucher cases with parkinsonism and 202 of 320 matching controls with data, p=.65. Table 7 uses survey responses for 31 of 68 eligible parkinsonism cases and separates rest from active-motion tremor. These are bone symptom and observed movement descriptions, without standardized food-related pain or tremor change and without date or fig consumption data. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4662380/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2010-Rosenbloom-Gaucher-Bone-Pain-and-Tremor.txt)
