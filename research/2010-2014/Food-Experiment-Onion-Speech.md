# Onion exposure and speech intelligibility

## Onion input to flavonol calculations in a Parkinson incidence study

Gao and colleagues used Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study food-frequency data in a prospective analysis of incident Parkinson disease. The 1986 men's questionnaire omitted onions, so the team used the 1990 questionnaire onion field to derive baseline flavonol intake. This is evidence of onion intake being collected in a Parkinson-incidence cohort. The paper supplies no estimate for prepared onion and no speech outcome following diagnosis. ## Onion bulb medicines in the European Medicines Agency assessment

The EMA report summarizes oral uses recorded for cough, bronchitis, asthma and tonsillitis and lists commercial preparations, including an ethanolic bulb extract in a syrup. It distinguishes those preparations from raw or heat-treated culinary bulb in the available human studies. Those studies did not substantiate well-established medicinal use, and the report contains no cooked bulb–intelligibility comparison in Parkinson disease.
