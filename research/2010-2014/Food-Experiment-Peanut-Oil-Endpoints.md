# Peanut oil comparison in colon nociception

## Intracolonic vehicle in a spinal nociception experiment

Mitrovic and colleagues used peanut oil as the vehicle comparison for two chemical irritants in mice. Figure 1 compares spinal c-Fos after intracolonic challenge. The irritants increased this neuronal signaling measure relative to the vehicle. The report gives no food-grade or oral oil comparison and assesses colon nociceptive signaling outside Parkinson disease. It is a distinct study from the 2015 study led by Jain, which included observed pain-related behaviors.
