# Japanese Parkinson nutrition and swallowing reports, 2010–2014

## 2014 diet modifications and dysphagia in Hokkaido Parkinson outpatients

Matsushima and colleagues presented P-099-5 on May 24, 2014 at the 55th Japanese Neurological Society meeting in Fukuoka. J-STAGE released the supplement on December 18, 2014. The survey covered meal adjustments, assistive utensils, dysphagia and health-care costs. Of 245 consenting patients with Parkinson disease, the analysis included 237 after excluding five public-assistance recipients and three institutional residents. Compared with patients below modified Hoehn and Yahr stage 3, those at stage 3 or above reported higher dysphagia scores (p=.045), more meal adjustments (p=.005) and more assistive utensils (p=.008). The abstract identifies no foods or preparation-specific symptoms. [Primary abstract, PDF p. 245, printed p. S243](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/clinicalneurol/54/Supplement/54_S1/_pdf/-char/ja#page=245), [official program](https://www.neurology-jp.org/sokaitiho/neuro2014/09_program/031.pdf), [supplement release record](https://doi.org/10.5692/clinicalneurol.54.S1).

The 2016 cross-sectional publication reports the same team, setting and 237-person analytic sample. The 2017 and 2018 follow-ups report additional survey waves. The 2014 poster and the 2016 cross-sectional article represent the same baseline evidence.
