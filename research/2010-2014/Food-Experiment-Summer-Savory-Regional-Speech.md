# Summer savory in a medieval Serbian source survey

## Separate savory and speech-related field mustard records

Jarić and colleagues published their analysis of the Chilandar Medical Codex on 17 June 2011. Table 1 identifies *Satureja hortensis* root, including fresh root subsequently cooked with honey, for traditional non-speech indications. Its distinct field-mustard row mentions speech in febrile states. The source reports historical prescriptions, not a Parkinson clinical observation or measured speech effect.
