# Sumac preparations and urinary outcomes

## Terminated fragrant sumac bark combination urgency trial

Registered January 19, 2010. The randomized eight-week comparison planned a three-day diary with urgency, urination and incontinence. Combined capsules supplied pumpkin seed oil, aqueous fragrant sumac bark and hop extract. The sponsor summary signed June 29, 2012 gives seven active and two placebo recipients. The trial terminated for slow recruitment and presents no efficacy analyses. The first public date of the sponsor summary has not been verified. The later 117-person uncontrolled 2019 report studied the same product in a distinct cohort. The exposure is fragrant sumac bark rather than culinary Rhus coriaria fruit.
