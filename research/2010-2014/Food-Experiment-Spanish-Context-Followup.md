# Spanish evidence follow-up, 2010–2014

## Early report of impulse-control outcomes in Parkinson disease

Martí Andrés and colleagues reported 25 impulse-control cases among 107 people with Parkinson disease at the November 19–23, 2013 Spanish Neurology Society meeting. The Vall d’Hebron team used a structured psychiatric interview. The conference abstract groups impulse-control outcomes and gives no count for a named food exposure. The similar title, institution, team and measures link it to the later 115-person paper. The extent of participant overlap is unreported.
