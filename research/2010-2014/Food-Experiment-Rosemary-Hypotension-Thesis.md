## Rosemary essential oil and primary hypotension dissertation

Universidad Complutense de Madrid records Luis Fernando Fernández Martín’s February 21, 2012 defense. Its author summary gives 30 selected primary hypotension patients in one pharmacy and reports a pressure rise during rosemary essential-oil use that reversed after withdrawal. The later overlapping research group’s journal article reports 32 recruited. The design, author and setting suggest a shared study lineage, with enrollment/analysis counts and study identification unresolved. The file linked by the university returned HTTP 403. No orthostatic symptom estimate or fresh leaf exposure is established from the original abstract.
