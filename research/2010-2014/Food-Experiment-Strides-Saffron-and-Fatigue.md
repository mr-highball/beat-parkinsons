# Stride, saffron and fatigue follow-up

## Virgin coconut oil and chemotherapy fatigue scores

First published August 27, 2014. In the 60-person analyzed sample, mean EORTC fatigue scores in the virgin coconut oil arm went from 49.34 at baseline to 45.88 at cycle six. Controls went from 36.38 to 47.26. The authors report no significant between-group symptom score differences at any cycle and no baseline-adjusted fatigue contrast. Baseline imbalance, withdrawals, concurrent chemotherapy and lack of clinical Parkinson data limit transfer. The descriptive changes point away from worsening.
