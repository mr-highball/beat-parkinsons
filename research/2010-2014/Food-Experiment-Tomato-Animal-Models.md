# Tomato constituents in Parkinson models, 2010–2014

## Isolated lycopene and neurobehavior in rotenone models

Kaur et al. reported that oral lycopene was associated with improvements in neurobehavioral abnormalities in rotenone-treated rats. Liu et al. independently used a four-arm mouse experiment and a water-maze test, with favorable neurobehavioral and tissue-marker results in lycopene-treated toxin-exposed animals. The accessible author abstracts lack numerical cognitive endpoint group contrasts. Both interventions used isolated lycopene. The cognitive measures and Parkinson models differ from the clinical executive-function outcome and cooked food in claim `0311`.
