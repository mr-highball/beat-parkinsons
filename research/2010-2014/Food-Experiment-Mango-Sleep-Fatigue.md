# Mango fruit, sleep and fatigue

## Bedtime mango and nocturnal cramps

Abe reported a 79-year-old woman who ate a mango before bed every day for about a month with the intention of sleeping well. She reported painful cramps during sleep and had serum potassium of 5.5 mmol/L. On stopping mango, cramps resolved and potassium at the next admission was 4.8 mmol/L. The author suspected potassium overload. The article gives no cultivar, preparation or quantified fruit weight, no sleep-onset or insomnia-severity measurement, and no Parkinson diagnosis. The stated reason for eating mango is patient recall. The observed outcome was cramps with a change in serum potassium after stopping. [Abe primary case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3471402/) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC3471402-Abe-Mango-Nocturnal-Cramps.txt)

## An agricultural profile repeats insomnia and fatigue uses

The Government of India post-harvest profile dated 2013 lists mango among customary uses for insomnia and mental fatigue on the page labeled section 7.8. It provides no source observations, species verification beyond the crop profile, protocol, patient counts or symptom results for those assertions. The nearby pulp-and-honey preparation is described in connection with bronchitis. It cannot be assigned to insomnia or fatigue from this document.
