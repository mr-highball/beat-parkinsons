# ISRCTN dietary and botanical registrations, 2010–2014

## Ashwagandha root powder

ISRCTN31871098 was registered June 7, 2010. The randomized placebo-controlled trial planned to enroll 30 adults in Pakistan with mild-to-moderate Parkinson’s and motor fluctuations on levodopa. It specified modified UPDRS at baseline and after four weeks of adjunctive *Withania somnifera* root powder or placebo. The register marks the study completed and gives no final sample size or outcomes. Searches by registry ID, acronym WiS-PD, scientific title and investigator did not identify a verified results report. Ashwagandha is outside the frozen vocabulary. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN31871098)

## Instant coffee and attention

ISRCTN78129879 was registered June 6, 2014. The crossover protocol specifies caffeinated versus decaffeinated instant coffee, with attention as its primary endpoint and timed walking as a secondary endpoint. Eligibility included Parkinson’s disease dementia or dementia with Lewy bodies. The linked 2016 meeting abstract reports 30 healthy older adults and six people with dementia with Lewy bodies. A separate estimate for Parkinson’s disease dementia remains unavailable. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN78129879) · [2016 meeting results](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#instant-coffee-and-attention-meeting-results)
