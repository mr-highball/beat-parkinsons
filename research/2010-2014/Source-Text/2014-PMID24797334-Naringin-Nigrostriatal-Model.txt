Title: Naringin protects the nigrostriatal dopaminergic projection through induction of GDNF in a neurotoxin model of Parkinson's disease
PMID: 24797334
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2014.03.006
Publication date: 2014-04-01
Citation: Leem E and colleagues. Journal of Nutritional Biochemistry 25:801–806.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24797334/
Coverage: Author abstract.

In a rat model of toxin-related nigrostriatal injury, the investigators administered isolated naringin by injection and reported effects on dopaminergic-neuron preservation, GDNF and inflammatory markers. These are tissue and pathway observations. The abstract does not report raw grapefruit exposure, a separate tremor measure, or a human response. A later same-program mouse study separates protection from restoration after an established lesion.
