Forward and backward digit span in a sleep laboratory
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3577106/

August 2012. A 48-hour inpatient protocol included controlled observation, polysomnography and repeated digit span. The authors report day-to-day improvement on backward span among people with Parkinson disease already taking dopaminergic medication, with no clear forward-span improvement. Meals followed customary schedules. The study estimates neither fig intake effects nor hallucination-episode or tremor outcomes.
