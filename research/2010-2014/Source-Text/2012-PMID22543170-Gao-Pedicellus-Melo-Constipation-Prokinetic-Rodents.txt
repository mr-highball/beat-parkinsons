Title: Pharmacological basis for the medicinal use of muskmelon base (Pedicellus Melo.) for abdominal distention and constipation
PMID: 22543170
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2012.04.025
First published: 2012
Authors: Gao Y, Cai RL, Xie C, Lin YL, Zhang L, Qi Y
Journal: Journal of Ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22543170/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Preclinical mouse, rat, and ex vivo guinea-pig study of ethanol extract of bitter non-edible melon stalk/pedicel (Pedicellus Melo) given rectally or intracolonically (4-26 mg/kg), showing atropine-sensitive cholinergic prokinetic acceleration of intestinal transit and colonic contractility. Strictly non-edible toxic/bitter fruit stalk (pedicel) administered rectally in rodents, NOT oral raw honeydew melon flesh.
