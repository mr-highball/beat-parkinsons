Title: Does coffee enriched with chlorogenic acids improve mood and cognition after acute administration in healthy elderly? A pilot study

The 39-person double-blind crossover in healthy older adults compared caffeinated soluble coffee, regular soluble decaf, chlorogenic-acid-enriched soluble decaf and a flavored placebo. The mean acute mental-fatigue VAS changes were −6.20, +9.24, +1.40 and +13.30 respectively. Caffeinated soluble coffee favored less mental fatigue versus placebo and regular decaf in exploratory tests. Enriched versus regular decaf was inconclusive at p=.074. These unadjusted multiple comparisons address an acute, formulation-specific mental-fatigue endpoint. There is no ordinary brewed coffee Parkinson fatigue comparison.
