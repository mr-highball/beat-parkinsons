Agah et al. Cumin Extract for Symptom Control in Patients with Irritable Bowel Syndrome. Middle East J Dig Dis. 2013 Oct;5(4):217–222.
PMID 24829694. PMCID PMC3990147.
Original: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3990147/
Coverage: Factual exposure, patient-flow and adverse-event extract from the primary report.

In this uncontrolled case series the oral cumin drops contained 2% essential oil. The abstract reports 57 enrolled, and the analysis included 50. The first Results sentence instead reports 28 included. Five withdrew during treatment, including two for severe nausea and one for severe abdominal pain, and two did not complete later follow-up. Discussion mentions that some patients experienced dizziness after consumption but gives no count, posture, blood pressure during symptoms, or reporting interval. The preparation was essential oil and the patients had IBS. No control arm, ground culinary cumin exposure or Parkinson orthostatic symptom outcome was present.
