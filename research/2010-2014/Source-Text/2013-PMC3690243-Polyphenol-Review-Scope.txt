Title: Polyphenols: Multipotent Therapeutic Agents in Neurodegenerative Diseases
Publication year: 2013
PMID: 23840922
PMCID: PMC3690243
DOI: 10.1155/2013/891748
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3690243/
Coverage: Original review body, sections 1–12

The June 2013 review describes green tea polyphenols and isolated EGCG in the context of neurodegenerative mechanisms. Some examples are cellular or animal studies. The narrative discusses rosmarinic acid as a major rosemary polyphenol, citing laboratory mechanisms. Fatigue occurs as a symptom of multiple sclerosis in the introduction. The review supplies no independent brewed green tea fatigue measurement or fresh rosemary depression comparison. This is related compound and ingredient context with no sign transferred to the Round 1 clinical claims.
