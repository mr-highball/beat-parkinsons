Rosemary extract shared by both registered MsFLASH capsule arms
2010-08-10 (first public posting)
https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01178892

The registry identifies a factorial trial of omega-3 or placebo and separately assigned activity programs. Both capsule descriptions list rosemary extract together with lemon flavor and other excipients. Depressed mood is one secondary outcome. The trial does not assign rosemary separately and the capsule description supplies no dosage of rosemary. The 2013 omega-3 paper is the matching outcomes source.
