Title: Effects of Ficus carica paste on loperamide-induced constipation in rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22178225/
PMID: 22178225
DOI: 10.1016/j.fct.2011.12.001
Publication date: 2011-12-09, first electronic publication. The journal issue is dated 2012.
Text coverage: Paraphrased original abstract. Complete main text and result tables remain open.

The original abstract describes four weeks of fig-paste feeding in a rat model of drug-induced constipation. The authors report treatment-group increases in fecal output, water content and transit length, and compare colon histology. These controlled observations concern induced bowel dysfunction in rats.

The 2016 adult paste trial cites this study, whose animal cohort and endpoint are separate. The abstract does not provide the complete component and preparation comparisons needed to generalize to intact dried figs or to a human visual symptom.
