Title: Effect of rotigotine on sleep and quality of life in Parkinson's disease patients: post hoc analysis of RECOVER patients who were symptomatic at baseline
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21790503/
PMID: 21790503
DOI: 10.1517/14656566.2011.604031
Publication date: 2011-07-27
Date basis: NLM first electronic date
Source depth: Primary author abstract; full item tables remain open
Evidence type: Exploratory analysis of the original RECOVER randomization
Format: Factual digest of the inspected source

The paper reuses the RECOVER trial and describes post hoc subsets based on baseline symptom burden. The abstract gives sleep and quality-of-life items that favored rotigotine, mainly pain and mobility-related items. It supplies no separate hallucination-item estimate and notes the trial did not have statistical power for subgroup analyses. This is a reanalysis of the same randomization and not a dried-fig comparison.
