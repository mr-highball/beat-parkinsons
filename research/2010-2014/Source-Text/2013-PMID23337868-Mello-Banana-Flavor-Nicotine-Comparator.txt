Title: Mello et al. — banana-flavored pellets in a nicotine and concurrent nicotine–cocaine experiment
PMID: 23337868
PMCID: PMC3656370
DOI: 10.1038/npp.2013.25
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3656370/
First published: 2013-01-21 online according to the publisher and Crossref, June 2013 issue. PMC citation metadata gives 2013-02-06.
Coverage: Complete posted original including food, drug schedules, controls and reported response outcomes.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18 UTC

Five previously cocaine-experienced rhesus monkeys performed reinforcement tasks. The report describes the banana-flavored pellet diet and banana-flavored pellets used in food sessions. The drug comparisons involve nicotine and concurrent nicotine plus cocaine under chronic buspirone. The authors report reduced nicotine and combination responding at selected doses. Their abstract reports no clear change in food-maintained responding.

The food is a shared flavored comparator without a fruit-matched control arm. Drug, preparation, species and endpoint boundaries prevent using the result for an effect of eating raw banana. An earlier conference record and the cocaine-only paper may share animals from the same research program, with overlap still unverified.
