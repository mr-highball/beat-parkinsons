Title: Fluoxetine versus other types of pharmacotherapy for depression.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11513554/
PMID: 24353997
PMCID: PMC11513554
DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD004185.pub3
Publication date: 2013-07-17
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC complete article HTML.

This systematic review compares fluoxetine with other depression treatments. Its included saffron studies include a petal capsule comparison and a separate stigma preparation comparison with fluoxetine in depressed outpatients. It does not supply a culinary saffron trial or Parkinson insomnia or fatigue measures.
