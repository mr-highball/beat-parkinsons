Probiotic oil drops and stimulated salivary flow

Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22368259/
Coverage: Author abstract and separately identified secondary vehicle descriptions
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published February 27, 2012. In 42 randomized periodontal patients stimulated salivary flow increased in both groups by day 15. The trial compared Lactobacillus salivarius WB21 oil drops with placebo. The accessible original abstract neither specifies the oil nor reports absolute or adjusted between-arm flow estimates. Two later reviews identify soybean oil as the vehicle and one describes a soybean-oil placebo. The primary Methods are still inaccessible, and refining remains unverified. Increased secretion does not establish drooling, and the trial gives no Parkinson result.
