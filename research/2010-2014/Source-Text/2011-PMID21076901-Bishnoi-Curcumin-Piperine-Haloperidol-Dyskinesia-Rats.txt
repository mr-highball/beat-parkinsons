Title: Protective effect of curcumin and its combination with piperine (bioavailability enhancer) against haloperidol-associated neurotoxicity: cellular and neurochemical evidence.
PMID: 21076901
DOI: 10.1007/s12640-010-9229-4
First published: 2011
Authors: Bishnoi M, Chopra K, Rongzhu L, Kulkarni SK
Journal: Neurotoxicity research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21076901/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Rat study of chronic haloperidol-induced orofacial dyskinesia (5 mg/kg i.p. x 21 d). Co-administration of piperine enhanced the protective effect of 25 mg/kg curcumin (not 50 mg/kg) against haloperidol orofacial dyskinesia and striatal oxidative/inflammatory injury. Bundled with curcumin in antipsychotic tardive model, not isolated black pepper or PD levodopa-induced dyskinesia.
