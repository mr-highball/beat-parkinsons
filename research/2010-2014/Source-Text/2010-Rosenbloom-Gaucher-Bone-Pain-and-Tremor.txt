Bone pain and rest tremor in the Gaucher registry
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4662380/

November 2010 online, with a 2011 issue. Rosenbloom and colleagues report baseline bone-pain presence in 25 of 42 type 1 Gaucher cases with parkinsonism and 202 of 320 matching controls with data, p=.65. Table 7 uses survey responses for 31 of 68 eligible parkinsonism cases and separates rest from active-motion tremor. These are bone symptom and observed movement descriptions, without standardized food-related pain or tremor change and without date or fig consumption data.
