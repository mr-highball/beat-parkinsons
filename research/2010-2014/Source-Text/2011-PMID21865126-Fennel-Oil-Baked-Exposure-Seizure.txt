Title: Epileptic seizure induced by fennel essential oil
PMID: 21865126
DOI: 10.1684/epd.2011.0451
Publication date: 2011-09
Citation: Skalli and Soulaymani Bencheikh. Epileptic Disorders 13(3), 345–347.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21865126/
Coverage: Original author abstract. The case history beyond the abstract remains open.

A clinical pharmacovigilance report describes a generalized tonic-clonic seizure in a person with previously controlled epilepsy after eating cake that contained a quantity of fennel essential oil the authors could not establish. The exposure was an oil in a baked food. The report documents a temporal adverse-event association in one patient. It gives no Parkinson diagnosis, sustained resting-tremor assessment or measured toasted-seed exposure.
