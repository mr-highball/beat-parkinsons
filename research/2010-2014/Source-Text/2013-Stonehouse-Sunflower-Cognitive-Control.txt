High-oleic sunflower comparator and working-memory reaction time
Source: https://doi.org/10.3945/ajcn.112.053371
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: primary publication and registered comparator

Stonehouse and colleagues first published March 20, 2013. The randomized study analyzed 176 healthy adults given DHA-rich oil or an oral high-oleic sunflower oil comparator over six months. The working-memory reaction-time active-minus-comparator estimate was −0.36 SD (95% CI −0.58 to −0.14). This contrast identifies relative supplement performance. The oil-free counterfactual, pressing grade and Parkinson-specific executive outcome were absent. Original record ACTRN12610000212055.
