Title: Combining functional features of whole-grain barley and legumes for dietary reduction of cardiometabolic risk: a randomised cross-over intervention in mature women.
PMID: 24063257
DOI: 10.1017/S000711451300305X
First published: 2014
Authors: Tovar J, Nilsson A, Johansson M, Björck I
Journal: Br J Nutr
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24063257/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized crossover trial (Tovar et al., 2014, Eur J Clin Nutr, n=46 overweight adults) testing a multifunctional diet combining whole-grain barley, brown beans, and chickpeas for 4 weeks, reporting modest seated diastolic BP and cardiometabolic improvements. Bundled multi-food diet with seated BP only; cannot isolate roasted chickpeas or orthostatic hypotension.
