Oral ginger capsule crossover and dyspepsia symptoms
2011-01-07
https://www.wjgnet.com/1007-9327/full/v17/i1/105.htm

Hu and colleagues compared a single dose of encapsulated ginger with placebo in an 11-person randomized crossover study of functional dyspepsia. Median gastric half-emptying was 12.3 and 16.1 minutes, respectively, with a reported p≤.05. The authors found no significant differences in the recorded gastrointestinal symptom scores. Gastric emptying and pain or discomfort are distinct outcomes. The brief study used capsules and did not enroll a Parkinson cohort.
