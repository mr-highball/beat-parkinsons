Title: Grape powder and anxiety-like behavior in a rat ovariectomy model

Patki and colleagues compared freeze-dried table grape powder in water with water alone in female rats with and without ovariectomy. The paper reports lower anxiety-like behavior and improved short-term memory with grape powder in the model group. Its long-term memory comparison was inconclusive. The preparation and model are different from raw grape consumption and clinical Parkinson anxiety.

The investigators overlap with the Allam paper and cite it for preparation and prior findings. The female ovariectomy model is a distinct design from Allam's oxidative-stress study. The consulted publisher record had no linked notice retracting this report. This observation is indirect context with no automatic transfer to Round 1 claim 0860.
