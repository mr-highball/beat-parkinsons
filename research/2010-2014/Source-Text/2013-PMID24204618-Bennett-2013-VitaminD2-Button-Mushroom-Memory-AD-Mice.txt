Title: Vitamin D2-enriched button mushroom (Agaricus bisporus) improves memory in both wild type and APPswe/PS1dE9 transgenic mice.
PMID: 24204618
PMCID: PMC3799746
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0076362
First published: 2013
Authors: Bennett L, Kersaitis C, Macaulay SL, Münch G, Niedermayer G, Nigro J
Journal: PloS one
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3799746/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Seven-month feeding study in wild-type and APPswe/PS1dE9 Alzheimer transgenic mice showing UV-enriched vitamin D2 Agaricus bisporus (~54 IU/kg) improved learning/memory and lowered amyloid plaque load and GFAP vs vitamin D-deficient control. Preclinical dementia model.
