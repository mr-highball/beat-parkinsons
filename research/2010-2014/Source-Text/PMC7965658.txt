This review of dopamine-transporter SPECT compares Parkinson disease and other parkinsonian syndromes, including dementia with Lewy bodies. Visual hallucinations figure in clinical differential diagnosis and in cited studies. The imaging review evaluates neither fig intake nor episode frequency after a culinary exposure.
