Original source scope
A counterbalanced placebo comparison recruited 23 healthy adults and analyzed 18 complete task records. Isolated caffeine increased button presses by 0.20 per second (SE 0.03, p <0.001). The effortful choice coefficient was -0.06 log-odds (SE 0.14, p=0.66).

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The session used a capsule. Coffee intake and abstention characterize participants. Fatigue was considered as a possible task or session effect, with no separate fatigue severity scale. Button pressing and effort decisions do not estimate fatigue after brewed coffee in Parkinson disease.
