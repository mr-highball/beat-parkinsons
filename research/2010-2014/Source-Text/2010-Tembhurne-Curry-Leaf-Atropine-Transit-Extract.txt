Atropine arm and overlap with the 2009 mouse report
Source: https://sphinxsai.com/sphinxsaivol_2no.1/pharmtech_vol_2no.1/PharmTech_Vol_2No.1PDF/PT%3D51%20%28322-326%29.pdf
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

The same authors describe the same specimen voucher and all five means and SDs for vehicle and extract-only arms as in their 2009 verapamil paper. Table 1 gives 40.53 ± 2.44% charcoal transit with atropine, 61.01 ± 2.96% with atropine plus ethanol leaf extract, and 58.50 ± 1.62% with atropine plus petroleum-ether leaf extract. The two papers are linked as one evidence family for their overlapping arms. Neither measured a dose of ground culinary leaf, gastric emptying or levodopa response.
