Title: Effect of oral carbohydrate intake on labor progress: randomized controlled trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3521887/
PMID: 23304677
PMCID: PMC3521887
Publication date: 2012-11-01
Date basis: NLM electronic date.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Complete original PMC structured article, Methods and Results, Tables 1–2.

The source reports 190 entrants and 177 participants analyzed, 87 in the carbohydrate group and 90 controls. Participants offered carbohydrate could choose dates with water or light tea, or orange juice. There is no randomized comparison separating those choices. Stage two was shorter in the offered-carbohydrate group, and analgesic use did not differ clearly. The paper reports analgesic use and labor progression without a date-stratified pain-intensity result.

A paper from the same Gonabad setting with overlapping authors describes 87 intervention and 88 control participants and reports a pooled food-access pain outcome. Participant-level overlap remains unresolved.
