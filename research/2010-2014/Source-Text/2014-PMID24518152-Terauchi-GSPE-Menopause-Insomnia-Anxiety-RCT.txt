Title: Effects of grape seed proanthocyanidin extract on menopausal symptoms, body composition, and cardiovascular parameters in middle-aged women: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot study.
PMID: 24518152
DOI: 10.1097/GME.0000000000000200
First published: 2014
Authors: Terauchi M, Horiguchi N, Kajiyama A, Akiyoshi M, Owa Y, Kato K, Kubota T
Journal: Menopause (New York, N.Y.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24518152/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 8-week trial in 96 middle-aged women (91 completers) with menopausal symptoms taking 100 or 200 mg/day grape seed proanthocyanidin extract (GSPE) vs placebo. High-dose 200 mg GSPE reduced Athens Insomnia Scale at 8 weeks and both doses reduced HADS anxiety and BP at 4 weeks. Strictly isolated grape-seed proanthocyanidin extract (remote from whole raw/frozen grapes; directly contrasts with OSA grape-juice polyphenol sleep-time reduction in Trzepizur 2018 PMID:29659956).
