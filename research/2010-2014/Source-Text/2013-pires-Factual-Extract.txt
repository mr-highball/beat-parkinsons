Carvacryl acetate and the conflicting marble-burying report
2013-09-12
https://www.academia.edu/28869971/Anxiolytic_like_effects_of_carvacryl_acetate_a_derivative_of_carvacrol_in_mice

Pires and colleagues injected synthetic carvacryl acetate in mice. The Results report greater open-arm behavior and 47%, 52%, 55% and 74% fewer marbles buried across the reported doses versus vehicle. The abstract says marble burying increased. The original figure caption is accessible but the bar graphic remained unavailable. The conflict applies to the marble assay. These exposures and proxy behaviors differ from eating dried thyme or oregano and clinical anxiety.
