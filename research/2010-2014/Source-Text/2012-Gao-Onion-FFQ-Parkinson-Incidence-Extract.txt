Title: Habitual intake of dietary flavonoids and risk of Parkinson disease
Authors: Xiang Gao and colleagues
Primary article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3320056/
Identifiers: DOI 10.1212/WNL.0b013e31824f7fc4, PMCID PMC3320056
Date: 2012
Coverage: factual extract of dietary exposure methods and outcome family

The analysis used questionnaires from the Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study to estimate habitual dietary flavonoids in relation to incident Parkinson disease. The men's 1986 FFQ did not ask about onion. The authors used the following questionnaire in 1990 to account for onion in the baseline flavonol estimate. The reported exposure was nutrient or pooled dietary consumption and no separately modeled cooked onion contrast for diagnosed Parkinson symptoms was made. No speech endpoint was collected for that analysis.
