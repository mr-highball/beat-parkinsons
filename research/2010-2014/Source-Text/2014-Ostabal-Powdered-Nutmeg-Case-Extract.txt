Title: Serious powdered nutmeg intoxication
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24998027/
Original: https://www.medintensiva.org/en-download-pdf-S0210569114001363
PMID: 24998027
DOI: 10.1016/j.medin.2014.04.011
First public: 2014-07-02
Depth: Factual summary of the original clinical letter, pages 527–528, from the publisher's indexed PDF.

An adult presented with hallucinations, headache and tachycardia after excessive powdered nutmeg intake and developed status epilepticus requiring intensive care. The authors reported negative testing for alcohol and other substances. The clinical description gives no tremor phenotype, Parkinson diagnosis or comparator. The seizure is a different outcome from rest tremor. This case has a different age, hospital and clinical course from the later Axarquía intention-tremor reports.
