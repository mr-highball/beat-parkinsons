Kaur, Chauhan and Sandhir — Protective effect of lycopene on oxidative stress and cognitive decline in rotenone induced model of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21484267/
Publication: Neurochem Res. 2011;36:1435–1443. DOI 10.1007/s11064-011-0469-3.
Coverage: Primary author abstract fact extract. Full results tables unavailable in inspected preview.

The investigators compared outcomes in a rotenone rat model with an oral isolated lycopene intervention. The author abstract reports favorable neurobehavioral and motor changes after concurrent administration and describes changes in oxidative stress and mitochondrial measurements. The abstract provides no numerical comparison or confidence interval for an executive-function outcome. The exposure was isolated lycopene, not prepared food.
