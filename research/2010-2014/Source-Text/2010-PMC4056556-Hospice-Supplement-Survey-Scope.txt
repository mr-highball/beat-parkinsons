Title: Soliciting an herbal medicine and supplement use history at hospice admission
PMID: 20557233
PMCID: PMC4056556
DOI: 10.1089/jpm.2009.0378
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4056556/
Date basis: June 2010 journal issue.
Coverage: Factual extract of the original methods, results and survey tables.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Twenty specialists completed the survey. They ranked products for intake history based on prevalence of use, interactions and adverse effects. Table 1 summarizes prior studies given to respondents as reference material. Tables 2 and 5 distinguish questionnaire options from the final appraisal, which included ginger. Spearmint, flaxseed oil and green tea appear among reference materials or questionnaire products. A respondent suggestion lists grape-seed extract. Product listings do not supply new patient outcomes. The sage matches did not establish a culinary sage item in the survey.
