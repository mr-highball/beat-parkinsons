Title: Multiple medication use in older patients in post-acute transitional care: a prospective cohort study

Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Verified NLM original PMC XML body and methods. Factual extract.

The post-acute transitional-care cohort examined medication count, frailty, and clinical outcomes in older adults. Cranberry juice and other supplements without classification codes were excluded from medication counts. Depressive symptoms were part of wider clinical assessments. This does not provide a dried cranberry or Parkinson depressive-symptom contrast.
