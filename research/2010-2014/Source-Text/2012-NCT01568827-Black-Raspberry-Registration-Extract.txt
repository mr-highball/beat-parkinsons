Black raspberry slurry crossover registration
Official registration and planned primary outcome scope
NCT01568827 first posted April 2, 2012. Ten overweight or obese men were assigned treatment sequences of a freeze-dried black raspberry powder slurry and water around a test meal. The prespecified outcomes concern inflammation and tolerability. Posture-specific blood pressure and lightheadedness measures are unspecified. Registration and later results belong to one cohort.
