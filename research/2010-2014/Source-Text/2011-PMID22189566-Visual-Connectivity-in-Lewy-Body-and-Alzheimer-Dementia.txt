Title: Functional connectivity in cortical regions in dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3708629/
PMID: 22189566
PMCID: PMC3708629
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awr327
Publication year: 2011
Text coverage: Paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

Resting-state imaging compared dementia with Lewy bodies, Alzheimer dementia and healthy controls. Visual hallucinations were among the core features used to classify the Lewy body cases. The analysis mapped connectivity between selected brain regions and did not assign or measure a fig exposure or its association with repeated visual episodes.
