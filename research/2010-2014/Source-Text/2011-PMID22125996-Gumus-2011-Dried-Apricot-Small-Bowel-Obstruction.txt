Title: An unusual cause of small bowel obstruction: dried apricots.
PMID: 22125996
First published: 2011
Authors: Gümüs M, Kapan M, Onder A, Tekbas G, Yagmur Y
Journal: JPMA. The Journal of the Pakistan Medical Association
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22125996/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Surgical clinical case report of a 56-year-old man presenting with acute small-bowel obstruction requiring resection and end-to-end anastomosis after swallowing a dried apricot that passed the pylorus and swelled in intestinal fluid to obstruct the terminal ileum. Mechanical phytobezoar obstruction safety caveat (especially pertinent in PD with impaired chewing/swallowing and dysmotility), distinct from functional constipation.
