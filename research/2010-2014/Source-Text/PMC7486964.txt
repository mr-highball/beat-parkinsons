Title: Cerebral glucose metabolic features of Parkinson disease and incident dementia: longitudinal study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7486964/
PMCID: PMC7486964
PMID: 21571793
DOI: 10.2967/jnumed.111.089946
Publication date: 2011-05-13
Date basis: Publisher or NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Primary longitudinal clinical PET cohort
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Twenty-three people with Parkinson disease and 27 controls had baseline PET. Six Parkinson participants were later diagnosed with dementia. Lower glucose metabolism in visual association and posterior cingulate regions was associated with subsequent dementia. The discussion cites hallucination work, while the measured longitudinal event was dementia diagnosis. The dataset does not test a food exposure or separate visual hallucination recurrence.
