Title: Effect of tart cherry juice (Prunus cerasus) on melatonin levels and enhanced sleep quality
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22038497/
PMID: 22038497
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-011-0263-7
First published: 2011-10-30
Coverage: Distilled author abstract. Print issue December 2012.

Twenty healthy volunteers participated in a blinded placebo-controlled crossover with a seven-day concentrated tart-cherry juice period. The abstract reports more total sleep time and greater sleep efficiency with juice, and higher total urinary melatonin metabolite. It does not provide a clinical Parkinson fatigue comparison or test dried cherry as food.
