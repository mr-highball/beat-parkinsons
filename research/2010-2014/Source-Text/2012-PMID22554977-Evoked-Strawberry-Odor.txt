Title: Olfactory hallucinations elicited by electrical stimulation via subdural electrodes: effects of direct stimulation of olfactory bulb and tract

Conference abstract: https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/olfactory-hallucinations-elicited-by-electrical-stimulation-via-subdural-electrodes--effects-of-direct-stimulation-of-olfactory-bulb-and-tract

Kumar and colleagues studied 16 children with focal epilepsy undergoing clinical electrode mapping. Eleven reported a smell during electrical stimulation. Nine perceived an unpleasant smell and two a pleasant smell. The primary table documents a perceived sweet strawberry smell in one child after electrical stimulation. No fruit or fruit odorant was administered and the study did not measure fall frequency. The phrase records the quality of a perception induced by stimulation.

The society abstract and journal manuscript describe the same cohort and the same counts. The identified abstract explains one occurrence of the search term in the AES 2012 meeting material. Other members of the 492-page supplement require separate inspection.
