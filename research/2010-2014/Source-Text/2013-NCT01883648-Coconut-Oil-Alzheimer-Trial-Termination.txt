Title: Study to Evaluate Coconut Oil for Alzheimer's Disease
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01883648
Trial registration: NCT01883648
Collection: Source text
Publication date: 2013-06-21
Date basis: First public ClinicalTrials.gov posting.
Review depth: registry record and current structured trial status
Checked: 2026-11-25

The registry planned a coconut-oil beverage versus placebo
beverage comparison in Alzheimer disease, with cognitive scores
as the primary outcome. It records 21 actual participants and
termination for limited funding and enrollment. The public registry
currently has no posted results.

The 2024 natural-products review (PMID 38755627) includes this
registration in its trial table. Registration and termination provide
no estimate of efficacy. Falls in Parkinson disease were not a
reported endpoint.
