Cardamom aroma and measured salivary secretion
Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jsdr/14/2/14_134/_pdf
DOI: 10.32136/jsdr.14.2_134
Coverage: Original Japanese article with author English summary

Ito and Yamamura included 43 healthy adults across two experiments. Cardamom essential oil was presented on an odor strip near the nostrils. In experiment II the authors reported mean saliva weight of 0.182 g during cardamom exposure, 0.130 g at rest and 0.118 g with odorless jojoba. Black pepper oil gave 0.175 g. The paper reports higher secretion with aromatic oils and no clear difference between cardamom and black pepper. The intervention was aroma and the outcome was saliva collected by cotton. The reference to a shorter swallowing reflex comes from separate black-pepper literature. No clinical swallowing outcome or ingestion of ground cardamom was measured.
