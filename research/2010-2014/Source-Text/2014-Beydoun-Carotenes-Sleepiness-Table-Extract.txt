Beydoun et al. Serum nutritional biomarkers and sleep in NHANES. PLOS One. Published 2014 Aug 19.
DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0103490. PMID 25137304. PMCID PMC4138077.
Original: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0103490
Coverage: Factual Methods and Table 4 summary.

NHANES 2005–06 contributed 2,459 adults aged 20–85, with 2,346 included in the Table 4 selection models. The sleepiness factor comes from responses to survey items. With serum nutritional biomarkers entered together, Model 2 sleepiness coefficients per 1 SD were −0.09 ± 0.02 for alpha-carotene (p=.002) and +0.09 ± 0.03 for beta-carotene (p=.003). Model 4's combined carotenoid estimate was +0.07 ± 0.03 (p=.029), above the .010 familywise threshold. The cross-sectional biomarker associations may reflect multiple foods, supplements and metabolism. The study did not assign raw carrot or measure a Parkinson-specific change in daytime sleepiness.
