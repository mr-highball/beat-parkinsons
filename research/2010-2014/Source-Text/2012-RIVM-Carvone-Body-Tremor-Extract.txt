Date: dossier dated 2012-09-21; ECHA public consultation opened 2012-10-23

Coverage: Original factual extract of section 4.2.1.1 of the submitted regulatory dossier. The 2000 draft assessment report and individual animal observations were not separately obtained.

In a summarized OECD 401 acute oral rat study, a single 2,000 mg/kg exposure to carvone with unspecified isomer ratio induced hunched posture and lethargy, with occasional body tremor reported in two animals. There was no death or reported necropsy abnormality in that test. The preparation was isolated test material, with no dietary spice exposure. The dossier does not specify the body tremor’s rest versus movement state or a scaled severity comparison. This study differs from the NTP’s later-cited chronic d-carvone mouse model and from the separate acute literature report cited by NTP.
