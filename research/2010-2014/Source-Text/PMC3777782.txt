Title: A three dimensional anatomical view of oscillatory resting-state activity and functional connectivity in Parkinson's disease related dementia: an MEG study using atlas-based beamforming
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3777782/
PMCID: PMC3777782
PMID: 24179762
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2012.11.007
Publication date: 2012-11-17
Date basis: Publisher or NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Primary MEG analysis of previously reported comparison groups
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The study reanalyzes recordings from 13 Parkinson patients with dementia and 13 without dementia. Atlas-based beamforming estimates resting power and connectivity, with slowing and reduced connectivity in the dementia group. Visual hallucinations are mentioned in clinical background, with no separate group endpoint for their frequency. The analysis uses previously described participants.
