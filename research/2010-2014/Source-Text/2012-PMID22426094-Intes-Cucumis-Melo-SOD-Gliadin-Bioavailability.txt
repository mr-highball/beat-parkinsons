Title: Intestinal cell targeting of a stable recombinant Cu-Zn SOD from Cucumis melo fused to a gliadin peptide
PMID: 22426094
DOI: 10.1016/j.jbiotec.2012.02.019
First published: 2012
Authors: Intes L, Bahut M, Nicole P, Couvineau A, Guette C, Calenda A
Journal: Journal of Biotechnology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22426094/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Mechanistic study of recombinant Cu-Zn superoxide dismutase (SOD) cloned from cantaloupe (Cucumis melo) fused to a wheat gliadin peptide, showing that protective gliadin/lipid formulation is required to sustain intestinal epithelial interaction rather than rapid luminal elimination/proteolysis. Directly explains why Round 1/3 proprietary palm-oil-coated high-SOD cantaloupe concentrate (Extramel) RCTs cannot be transferred onto raw unencapsulated honeydew melon (Cucumis melo Inodorus) flesh.
