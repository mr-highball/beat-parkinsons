Title: Sex and exercise interact to alter the expression of anabolic androgenic steroid-induced anxiety-like behaviors in the mouse.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4127168/
PMID: 24768711
PMCID: PMC4127168
DOI: 10.1016/j.yhbeh.2014.04.008
Publication date: 2014-04-21
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official complete PMC JATS body.

The mouse study compares steroid exposure with matched injected sesame-oil vehicle under different exercise conditions. The measured behaviors include startle and anxiety-related responses. There is no dietary exposure to cold-pressed or toasted sesame oil or a fall outcome.
