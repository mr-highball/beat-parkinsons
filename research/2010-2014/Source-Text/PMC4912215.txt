Title: Sympathetic noradrenergic before striatal dopaminergic denervation: relevance to Braak staging

A person initially evaluated for pure autonomic failure reported visual hallucinations by the second follow-up year and worsening by the fourth year, as cognitive and motor findings evolved. The authors subsequently considered probable DLB. Imaging and autonomic measurements track different components of the course. The visual account records timing and perceived worsening with no comparable episode-count windows or food intervention.
