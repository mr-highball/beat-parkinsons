The authors examined tarragon essential oil in rodent evoked-pain tests. The abstract reports favorable responses compared with controls after injected oil. The published group tables and complete denominators remain unverified. This is separate from the ethanol-extract study PMID 26079854.

Claim 0285 concerns fresh tarragon taken orally for pain in people with Parkinson disease. This experiment uses essential oil, injection, and evoked animal behavior. Those exposure, route, population, and endpoint differences limit transfer.
