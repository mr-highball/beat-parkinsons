Title: Acute toxicity report for a multi-ingredient weight-loss supplement
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24501816/
PMID: 24501816
Publication year: 2013
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: Primary author abstract.

The clinicians reported anxiety and other sympathomimetic signs after excess use of a multicomponent supplement listing cayenne, black pepper, caffeine and other stimulant ingredients. Follow-up mass spectrometry did not confirm an initial immunoassay indication of amphetamine. The case does not separate cayenne or ground pepper from the other exposures and does not provide a Parkinson anxiety comparison.
