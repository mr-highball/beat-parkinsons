Oral olive oil and flaxseed oil for constipation during hemodialysis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25238699/
PMID: 25238699
DOI: 10.1053/j.jrn.2014.07.009
Coverage: Official author abstract with trial results, dissertation metadata

The original author abstract reports 50 hemodialysis participants assigned 17 to mineral oil, 16 to olive oil and 17 to flaxseed oil. Four-week Rome III mean scores changed from 10.5 to 4.1, 10.3 to 3.2 and 9.6 to 6.0, respectively, with a group-by-time p = .15. An inconclusive comparison against an active laxative is neither equivalence nor evidence against olive oil. The verified abstract does not identify the oil grade. The publisher body and UNIFESP thesis full text were not retrieved through the checked endpoints. The university metadata identifies the original dissertation, defended in 2014 and made available in its repository in 2015.
