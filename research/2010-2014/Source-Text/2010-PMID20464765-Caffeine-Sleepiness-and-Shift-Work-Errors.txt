Original source scope
The Cochrane review evaluated 13 randomized trials of caffeine interventions for errors or cognitive performance in shift-work or jet-lag contexts. The Philip row uses caffeinated and decaffeinated instant coffee.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Driving and attention outcomes do not establish a Parkinson fatigue effect. The primary driving papers report fatigue separately and share the young cohort. The 2010 review is a citation trail, not an independent efficacy vote.
