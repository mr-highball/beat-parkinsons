Title: Influence of electroacupuncture on p38-mitogen activated protein kinase in substantia nigra cells of rats with Parkinson disease model
Publication date: 2013-04-01
PMID: 23819239
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23819239/
Text coverage: Distilled original investigator abstract indexed by Europe PMC.

The investigators report 32 male rats in four groups. Sunflower-oil emulsion without rotenone was the injected sham; model arms received toxin in the oil. The abstract reports tissue markers for the toxin and electroacupuncture comparisons. It does not give an independently quantified sunflower-versus-untreated cognitive or rigidity effect. Oil processing and an oral dietary or Parkinson patient comparison are unverified.
