Title: Visual and general hallucination entry counts in RECOVER and its extension
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00474058?tab=results
Companion results: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00519532?tab=results
Publication date: 2010-04
Date basis: Separate first public posting dates for the two results modules below
Source depth: Official registry results JSON, adverse event term and group denominators
Format: Factual extraction of posted primary results

The randomized RECOVER trial registration was first posted May 16, 2007. Results were first posted April 12, 2010. The serious-adverse-event table lists one affected participant and one event under visual hallucination in the rotigotine safety group (1/191). The placebo safety group lists zero events (0/96). The article reports randomization of 190 versus 97 and describes withdrawal after the visual event. Safety counts and randomization counts are separate denominators.

The RECOVER open-label extension registration was first posted August 22, 2007. Its results first appeared April 2, 2010. In the extension's single rotigotine group, the posted table lists 11 of 84 participants and 22 events under the general hallucination term. The entry does not disaggregate visual from other modalities. The extension enrolled participants from the parent study and lacks a parallel placebo group. Neither registry provides a dried-fig comparison.
