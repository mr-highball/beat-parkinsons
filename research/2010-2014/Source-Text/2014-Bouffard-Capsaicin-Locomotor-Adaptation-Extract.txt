Title: Tonic Pain Experienced during Locomotor Training Impairs Retention Despite Normal Performance during Acquisition
Authors: Jason Bouffard and colleagues
Date: 2014-07-09
Identifiers: PMID 25009252; PMCID PMC4087202; DOI 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.5303-13.2014
Source: https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/34/28/9190.full.pdf
Coverage: Original factual extract of the primary full report.

Thirty healthy volunteers completed a two-day treadmill adaptation protocol with an ankle perturbation. Fifteen participants trained during day-one cutaneous pain from a local capsaicin preparation, and both groups were pain-free on day two. Baseline gait and initial adaptation did not differ clearly. The pain group showed weaker next-day retention of ankle-trajectory adaptation. The authors measured the trajectory error rather than a decline in natural stride length or the number of Parkinson gait-freezing episodes.
