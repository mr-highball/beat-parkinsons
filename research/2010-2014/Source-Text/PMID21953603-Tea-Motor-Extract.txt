Table 1 and Results. Twenty-five idiopathic PD participants received oral caffeine tablets with weekly dose escalation starting at 100 mg twice daily. Secondary comparisons with baseline were per protocol. The first two weeks gave a reported change of −1.18 rest tremor points (SD 2.4, p=.01). Some withdrawals cited increased or newly noticed tremor. The Timed Up and Go baseline mean was 10.39 seconds (SD 3.04) and the main report did not give its postbaseline estimates. Patient-rated PDQ-39 mobility changed by −2.8 points (SD 3.4, p=.01) among those tolerating 200 mg twice daily. The article describes 25 patients, 17 tolerated that dose and three finished six weeks. Intervention was isolated caffeine and lacked a concurrent control.
