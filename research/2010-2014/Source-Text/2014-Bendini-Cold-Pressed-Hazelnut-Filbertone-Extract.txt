Title: Quality evaluation of sunflower and hazelnut cold-pressed oils by a sensory approach
Source: https://extranet.innovhub-ssi.it/allstd/risg/2014-RISG%202.pdf
First public issue date: 2014, Rivista Italiana delle Sostanze Grasse 91(2), starting p. 103
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated original Tables II and VI

The investigators profiled commercial and sample cold-pressed
oils. Three cold-pressed hazelnut oils made from raw kernels
(H1, H4, H6) have filbertone below the table's quantification
limit. Four made from roasted kernels (H2, H5, H7, H8) give
values 0.04, 0.06, 0.06 and 0.06, and a mixed raw-and-roasted
oil (H3) gives 0.04 ± 0.01. The unit is mg internal-standard
1-penten-3-one equivalents per kg of oil, so these are
semi-quantitative signals, not direct filbertone masses.

Cold pressing and pre-press roasting are separate
preparation features. A below-quantification result permits
some compound below the limit. Different analytical methods
and lots can yield different detections. The food profiles do
not measure human uptake or motor outcomes.
