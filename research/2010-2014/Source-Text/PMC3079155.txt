Title: Dementia with Lewy Bodies versus Alzheimer's Disease and Parkinson's Disease Dementia: A Comparison of Cognitive Profiles

Date basis: Publisher or NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Clinical neuropsychological comparison
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The investigators compared 10 people with probable DLB, 76 with probable Alzheimer disease and 17 with Parkinson dementia. The groups completed cognitive tests, with fluctuating Parkinson participants tested during the on phase. Hallucinations appear in the description of DLB. The comparison concerns cognitive performance and does not quantify repeated visual experiences or assign a food exposure.
