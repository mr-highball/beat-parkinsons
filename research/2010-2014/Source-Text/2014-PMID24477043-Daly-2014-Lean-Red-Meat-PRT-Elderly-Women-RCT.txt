Title: Protein-enriched diet, with the use of lean red meat, combined with progressive resistance training enhances lean tissue mass and muscle strength and reduces circulating IL-6 concentrations in elderly women: a cluster randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 24477043
DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.113.064154
First published: 2014
Authors: Daly RM, O'Connell SL, Mundell NL, Grimes CA, Dunstan DW, Nowson CA
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24477043/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

4-month cluster RCT in n=100 elderly women (60-90 y) across 15 retirement villages receiving progressive resistance training 2x/wk + vitamin D3 1000 IU/d, randomized to ~160 g cooked lean red meat 6 d/wk (n=53) vs pasta/rice control (n=47). Protein intake 1.3 vs 1.1 g/kg/d (p<0.05). RT+Meat improved total lean mass (+0.45 kg; 95% CI 0.07-0.84), leg lean mass (+0.22 kg), muscle strength (+18%; 95% CI 0.03-0.34), IGF-I (+10%), and lowered IL-6 (-16%; all p<0.05); blood pressure and lipids unchanged; no separate gait-speed benefit reported in abstract.
