Aniseed essential oil and CNS drug challenge in mice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22926042/
PMID: 22926042
Coverage: factual extract from primary abstract and indexed methods

I. Samojlik and colleagues. Fitoterapia 83(8):1466–1473, first online August 2012. DOI 10.1016/j.fitote.2012.08.012.
The study pretreated mice orally for five days with a seed essential oil and subsequently challenged separate groups with central nervous system-active drugs. The source reports shorter pentobarbital-induced sleeping time and fewer open-arm entries in the diazepam elevated-plus-maze condition and greater midazolam-induced motor impairment. The available indexed full-text passage says no hypnotic effect was observed during the oil pretreatment itself. Rotating-rod performance after midazolam and drug-induced sleep or righting behavior are separate from daily falls, dexterous hand use, insomnia, daytime sleepiness, or REM sleep behavior. These were drug interaction experiments with essential oil and no levodopa or Parkinson cohort.
