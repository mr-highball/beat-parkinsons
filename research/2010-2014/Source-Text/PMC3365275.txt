Format: Factual digest of the original material

The comparison included 17 people with DLB and 19 controls, with perfusion available for 15 and 19. Caregivers provided a past-month visual hallucination rating combining frequency and severity. In DLB the combined score correlated inversely with V4 perfusion (rho −0.52, p = 0.04). The visual-task BOLD responses did not show a significant association with that score. The contrast concerns imaging and a composite rating without an assigned food exposure.

The publisher data supplement confirms an exploratory between-group BOLD motion contrast in V5/MT (Table DS1, p = 0.02) and the separate within-DLB association of V4 perfusion with the combined visual hallucination rating (Figure DS4). The latter scatterplot does not show a count of visual events over time.
