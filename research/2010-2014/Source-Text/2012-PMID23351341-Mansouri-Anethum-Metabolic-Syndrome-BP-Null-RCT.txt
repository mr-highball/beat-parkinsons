Title: The effect of 12 weeks Anethum graveolens (dill) on metabolic markers in patients with metabolic syndrome; a randomized double blind controlled trial.
PMID: 23351341
PMCID: PMC3555834
DOI: 10.1186/2008-2231-20-47
First published: 2012
Authors: Mansouri M, Nayebi N, Keshtkar A, Hasani-Ranjbar S, Taheri E, Larijani B
Journal: Daru : journal of Faculty of Pharmacy, Tehran University of Medical Sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23351341/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (Mansouri et al., 2012, Daru, PMC3555834) in 24 adults with metabolic syndrome (n=12 oral Anethum graveolens extract, n=12 placebo for 12 weeks). Between-group differences were NULL for blood pressure, fasting blood glucose, waist circumference, triglycerides, and HDL-C (only within-group TG decreased from baseline, p=0.01). Confirms no resting blood pressure signal and no orthostatic or Parkinson's symptom evaluation.
