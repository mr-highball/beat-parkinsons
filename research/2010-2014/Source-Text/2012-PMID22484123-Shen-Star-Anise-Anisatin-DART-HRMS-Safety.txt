Title: Rapid control of Chinese star anise fruits and teas for neurotoxic anisatin by Direct Analysis in Real Time high resolution mass spectrometry.
PMID: 22484123
DOI: 10.1016/j.chroma.2012.03.058
First published: 2012
Authors: Shen Y, van Beek TA, Claassen FW, Zuilhof H, Chen B, Nielen MW
Journal: Journal of chromatography. A
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22484123/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Analytical food-safety study (Shen et al., 2012, J Chromatogr A) validating Direct Analysis in Real Time high-resolution MS for detecting neurotoxic anisatin (>1,000-fold higher in Illicium anisatum than I. verum), noting that adulterated star anise teas cause epilepsy, hallucinations, and nausea, and confirming none of 8 retail teas tested contained significant anisatin. Relevant safety/differential context for visual hallucinations and neurological toxicity.
