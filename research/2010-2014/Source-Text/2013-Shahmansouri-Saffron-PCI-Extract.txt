Shahmansouri N et al. J Affect Disord 155:216–222, first online November 16, 2013. PMID 24289892, DOI 10.1016/j.jad.2013.11.003.
The original abstract describes a 40-person randomized double-blind, six-week comparison of saffron capsules (30 mg/day) with fluoxetine (40 mg/day) for mild to moderate depression following percutaneous coronary intervention. It reports p=.62 for difference in Hamilton improvement, p=.67 for response and p=1.00 for remission. There was no inactive placebo. Full formulation and interval estimates were unavailable. The study population is distinct from Parkinson disease.
