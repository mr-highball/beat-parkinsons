Homeopathic celery preparation and a hoarseness report

Primary source: https://ccrhindia.ayush.gov.in/sites/default/files/site/default/files/annual_reports/Annual-report2012-23.pdf
Coverage: Official annual-report period and primary table
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

The Central Council for Research in Homoeopathy's 2012–2013 annual report describes 6C and 30C Apium graveolens preparations in a randomized, double-blind proving in healthy volunteers. One prover in its reported symptom table had hoarseness with throat pain, tearing and dry cough. Group-specific counts and measured vocal loudness were unavailable. The fresh-leaf identity is unverified. The placement follows the report's stated period because its first online date is unverified. The 2014–2015 report from the same centers repeats related symptoms, with individual overlap undetermined.
