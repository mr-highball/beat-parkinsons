The study uses oral ethanolic tarragon extract PMI-5011 in a chemically induced diabetes mouse model. Table III compares untreated and extract-treated diabetic mice at the final assessment, with n=7–10 per group. Paw withdrawal latency was 15.6 versus 12.8 seconds and tail-flick latency 4.8 versus 3.2 seconds. Mechanical response threshold was 144.8 versus 119.3 g and tactile threshold 0.8 versus 1.1 g. The published direction for each outcome is toward the non-diabetic group. The figures describe stimulus-evoked sensory responses without a spontaneous pain endpoint.

This paper distinguishes the high-fat diet experimental cohort previously reported in PMID 20396384. The mouse models are different, while the formulated extract and research program overlap. The frozen claim concerns fresh oral tarragon and Parkinson pain. These experiments do not use that preparation or population.
