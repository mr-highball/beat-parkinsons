The article separates sensed presence and passage phenomena, formed visual hallucinations, and night-time confusional states. Section 4 explains that the general Neuropsychiatric Inventory hallucination item combines several modalities with frequency and severity, creating limitations for Parkinson-specific visual outcomes. The authors review competing visual, neurotransmitter and sleep explanations. Sections 7–8 propose that persistently active default-mode processing and disrupted attention networks can contribute to visual experiences. The network observations in this discussion refer to cited studies. The galley reports no food comparison or dried fig measurement.
