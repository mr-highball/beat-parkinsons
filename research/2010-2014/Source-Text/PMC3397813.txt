Title: Cerebral perfusion differences between drowsy and nondrowsy individuals after acute sleep restriction.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3397813/
PMID: 22851804
PMCID: PMC3397813
DOI: 10.5665/sleep.1994
Publication date: 2012-08-01
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC complete article HTML.

Participants recorded coffee in a study diary and avoided it on scan days. The experiment compares sleep restriction with a rested condition using eye-video drowsiness and perfusion imaging. It does not compare coffee consumption or measure Parkinson fatigue.
