Coverage: Factual summary of the original review and traditional-use section.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The review describes isolated curcumin, derivatives and reported turmeric uses. Its neuropharmacology sections combine distinct experimental and clinical endpoints. The names Indian saffron and yellow ginger here refer to Curcuma longa. They do not identify Crocus sativus or Zingiber officinale exposures. No culinary date or fig comparison appears. Isolated curcumin results are related leads for turmeric with processing, population and endpoint differences.
