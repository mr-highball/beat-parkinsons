Swallowing history and pharyngeal muscle pathology
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3358551/

June 2012. This autopsy study compared eight pathologically confirmed Parkinson cases and four controls. Four of eight cases had a swallowing impairment on a UPDRS II item. Atrophic pharyngeal fibers comprised 27.6% among cases with dysphagia versus 16% among cases without. The study reports a composite motor score and swallowing history, with no separate diet comparison or quantified drooling or rest-tremor response.
