Title: Flavonoid-rich apples and nitrate-rich spinach augment nitric oxide status and improve endothelial function in healthy men and women: a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 22019438
DOI: 10.1016/j.freeradbiomed.2011.09.028
First published: 2012
Authors: Bondonno CP, Yang X, Croft KD, Considine MJ, Ward NC, Rich L et al.
Journal: Free radical biology & medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22019438/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled 4-way acute crossover trial in n=30 healthy men and women comparing flavonoid-rich apple (with skin), nitrate-rich spinach, apple + spinach, and energy-matched control. Flavonoid-rich apple acutely augmented plasma nitric oxide, improved brachial artery FMD, and lowered resting systolic blood pressure (p<0.05). Limits: acute single-meal resting supine/seated BP in healthy non-PD adults; no standing orthostatic BP or lightheadedness measurement.
