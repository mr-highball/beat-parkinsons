Oregano extract and mouse anxiety behavior
https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114510004940
selected methods and findings paraphrase

First published online December 21, 2010. In a light–dark box, injected oregano extract at 60 mg/kg increased time in the light compartment by 35% versus vehicle (p = .046). The oral repeat-dose experiment measured forced-swim behavior. The two experiments differ in route and outcome. Human dried-oregano anxiety and Parkinson OFF time were outside the study.
