Title: Falls and the falls efficacy scale in Parkinson's disease
PMID: 20157723
DOI: 10.1007/s00415-010-5475-x
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20157723/
Publication: 2010-02-16 electronic publication, July issue
Coverage: Original author abstract. Complete methods and numerical tables remain open.

The authors interviewed and examined 102 people with idiopathic Parkinson disease. Participants reported falls over the previous three months and answered a falls efficacy questionnaire. The abstract reports that fall counts and freezing of gait correlated positively. It also describes two very frequent fallers with low reported fear, showing why self-reported confidence and actual fall count need to remain separate. The study abstract gives no fresh lemon balm or associated change in freezing episodes.
