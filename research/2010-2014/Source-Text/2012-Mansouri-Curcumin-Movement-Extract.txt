Mansouri and colleagues. Online March 15, 2012. PMID 22418789, DOI 10.1007/s12031-012-9727-3.
The author-shared full original shows intraperitoneal isolated curcumin begun before homocysteine exposure in rats. Figure 1 and text report better open-field distance and velocity relative to the exposed group without curcumin. The exact vehicle control and the timing of injury induction are described inconsistently. Numerical estimates cannot be read reliably from the plots. These outcomes were preclinical and did not test oral ground turmeric in Parkinson disease.
