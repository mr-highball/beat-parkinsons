Factual summary
Sections IX.B and XII separate mood, sensory properties and cognitive performance. The cited Martin experiment classifies participants by initial anxiety and reports metabolites. The cited Scholey crossover changes cocoa flavanol content. Its original manuscript reports a mental-fatigue signal only at the lower of two flavanol doses and no clear change in state anxiety. Background mentions of other foods and botanical sources are not symptom trials.
