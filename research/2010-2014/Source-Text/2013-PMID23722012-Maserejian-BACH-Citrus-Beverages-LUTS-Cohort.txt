Title: Intake of caffeinated, carbonated, or citrus beverage types and development of lower urinary tract symptoms in men and women.
PMID: 23722012
PMCID: PMC3676152
DOI: 10.1093/aje/kws411
First published: 2013
Authors: Maserejian NN, Wager CG, Giovannucci EL, Curto TM, McVary KT, McKinlay JB
Journal: American journal of epidemiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23722012/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Prospective 5-year Boston Area Community Health (BACH) cohort (n=4,144 men and women). Coffee/caffeine increased LUTS and urgency progression, whereas CITRUS JUICE intake was associated with 50% LOWER odds of LUTS progression in men (p=0.02) and no worsening in women. Directly opposes retrospective interstitial cystitis citrus-trigger surveys.
