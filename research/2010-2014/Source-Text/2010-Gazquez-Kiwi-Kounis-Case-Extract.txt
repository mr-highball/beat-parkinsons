Kiwi exposure in an allergic coronary case
PMID 20461972. Original case 1 and case series table.
Gázquez and colleagues’ 2010 series of five allergic coronary presentations identifies kiwi in case 1. A 66-year-old man with prior kiwi oral allergy experienced urticaria, vomiting, chest pain and hypotension after eating a piece of kiwi. His diagnostic testing included a positive prick test. No numerical pressure or posture-specific test is reported for this patient. A pressure of 64/39 in the paper belongs to case 2 after ibuprofen. The kiwi case is acute and outside a Parkinson cohort.
