Title: Assessment report on Allium cepa L., bulbus
Author: European Medicines Agency, Committee on Herbal Medicinal Products
Agency original: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/herbal-report/final-assessment-report-allium-cepa-l-bulbus_en.pdf
Identifier: EMA/HMPC/347195/2011
Date: 2012-03-27
Coverage: factual extract of assessed traditional preparations, clinical evidence and efficacy conclusion

The report documents customary oral use for cough, bronchitis, asthma and tonsillitis. It presents marketed products by processing method, including an ethanol extract in syrup. Section 4 reviews human studies from cardiovascular, metabolic and other clinical domains and identifies differences between raw bulb, cooked bulb, and extracts. Because of preparation and methodological gaps, the clinical evidence did not support well-established medicinal use. The report contains no measured intelligibility effect of cooked onion in Parkinson disease.
