Title: Clinical characteristics of patients with familial amyotrophic lateral sclerosis carrying the pathogenic GGGGCC hexanucleotide repeat expansion of C9ORF72

Source depth: University repository copy of publisher PDF
Evidence type: Primary familial ALS cohort and clinical pedigrees
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The Italian analysis includes 141 familial index cases. Cognitive impairment was more common among the C9ORF72 expansion cases than among participants with other ALS-related mutations (46.7% and 9.1%). Three index cases with associated behavioral-variant frontotemporal dementia had prominent hallucinations and delusions. The case material concerns an ALS/FTD phenotype and gives no uniform visual episode count or Parkinson food comparison.
