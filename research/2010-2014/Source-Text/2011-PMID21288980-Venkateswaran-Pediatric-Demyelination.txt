The observational study followed 213 children with confirmed pediatric acquired demyelinating syndromes and used DNA from 166 controls. In this article PD-ADS abbreviates pediatric demyelination, and dystonia occurs in the background and screening rationale for certain mutations. Genetic associations and later multiple sclerosis diagnoses were the study outcomes. No fresh lemon balm exposure, Parkinson dystonia severity rating or food comparison appears in the cohort.
