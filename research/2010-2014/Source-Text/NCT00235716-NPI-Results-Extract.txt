TEAM-AD clinical outcomes and registry results. Primary paper PMID24381967, DOI 10.1001/jama.2013.282834, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4109898/. Results posted 2014-01-29, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00235716?tab=results. Coverage: selected original facts from trial methods, posted NPI outcome, and the later baseline report.

The study assesses treatment of Alzheimer disease in 613 participants. Soybean oil supplied the vitamin E placebo and the published sources do not state refining grade. The posted outcome is the overall NPI frequency times severity sum. For the mean change averaged across measured visits, the registry lists +0.79 (SE 1.00) with vitamin E and +2.26 (SE 1.01) with dual placebo. The baseline analysis from PMID29966477 documents an NPI apathy domain, including any apathy in 47% and a score of at least four in 152 of 613. That baseline publication does not estimate subsequent apathy by treatment.
