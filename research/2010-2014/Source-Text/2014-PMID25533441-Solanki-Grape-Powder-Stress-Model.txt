Title: Grape powder in a rat model of prolonged psychological stress

Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: full original article

Solanki and colleagues compared freeze-dried grape powder with water in a rat stress model. The authors report less anxiety- and depression-like behavior and fewer memory-test errors in stressed animals receiving powder. These outcomes are proxy measurements in an experimental trauma setting. The paper does not give a Parkinson population or a raw grape intervention.

The article cites the now-retracted Allam study as prior work and shares investigators, including Patki and Salim. Its own stress-model comparison is separate. The consulted journal and archive listings did not display a linked retraction for this report.
