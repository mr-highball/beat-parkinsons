Olive oil grades and mouse fecal microbiota
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30011688/
PMID: 30011688
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2014.07.030
Coverage: Primary author abstract with diet comparators

Hidalgo and colleagues compared four mouse diets: standard chow and three high-fat diets containing extra-virgin olive oil, refined olive oil or butter. Culture, 16S profiling and sequencing of selected bands indicated distinct bacterial changes, with the extra-virgin olive-oil profiles furthest from the butter profiles. The accessible original author abstract does not give a constipation score, stool frequency, Parkinson model or individual oil effect on symptoms. The original full quantitative tables remain open.
