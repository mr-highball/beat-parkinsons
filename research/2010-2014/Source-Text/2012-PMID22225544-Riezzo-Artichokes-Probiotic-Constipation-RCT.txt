Title: Randomised clinical trial: efficacy of Lactobacillus paracasei-enriched artichokes in the treatment of patients with functional constipation--a double-blind, controlled, crossover study.
PMID: 22225544
DOI: 10.1111/j.1365-2036.2011.04970.x
Registry ID: NCT01212146
First published: 2012
Authors: Riezzo, Orlando, D'Attoma, Guerra, Valerio
Journal: Alimentary pharmacology & therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22225544/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover trial (NCT01212146; companion microbiology PMID 21447019 Valerio 2011) in 20 adults with functional constipation (3M/17F, 38.8 +/- 14.4 y) consuming 180 g/d ordinary prepared globe artichokes or Lactobacillus paracasei IMPC 2.1-enriched artichokes for 15 days each. 80% preferred probiotic-enriched over ordinary artichokes (P = 0.011) and satisfactory relief VAS was higher during probiotic artichokes (P = 0.0014). Compared with baseline, GSRS constipation dropped from 13.9 +/- 0.9 to 10.2 +/- 0.8 with ordinary artichokes and 8.3 +/- 0.9 with probiotic artichokes (P = 0.032 probiotic vs baseline), and Bristol chart cluster scores shifted 3.3 +/- 1.2 -> 2.9 +/- 1.3 -> 2.2 +/- 1.2 (P = 0.009 probiotic vs baseline). Limits: lacks an artichoke-free control arm; ordinary-artichoke improvement from baseline is descriptive without a standalone printed p-value.
