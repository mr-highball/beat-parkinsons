# Millet pain evidence

## Contaminated millet flour and acute poisoning

Patel and colleagues documented eight people from two families after eating bavta millet flour bread and vegetables. All reported severe abdominal pain within hours; chemical testing found monocrotophos in the flour, and clinical and laboratory findings indicated organophosphate poisoning. This acute contaminant event is not an estimate of pain after uncontaminated boiled millet or in Parkinson disease. [2012 original report, Methods and Results](https://hrcak.srce.hr/file/129346)
