Title: Piperine reverses chronic unpredictable mild stress-induced behavioral and biochemical alterations in rats.
PMID: 24401942
PMCID: PMC11488929
DOI: 10.1007/s10571-014-0025-1
First published: 2014
Authors: Mao QQ, Huang Z, Zhong XM, Xian YF, Ip SP
Journal: Cellular and molecular neurobiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24401942/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled rat chronic unpredictable mild stress (CUMS) experiment (PMC11488929). Piperine significantly reversed CUMS-induced reductions in sucrose consumption and increases in forced swim test immobility while restoring hippocampal and frontal cortical 5-HT and BDNF levels. Limits: isolated piperine in rodent stress model.
