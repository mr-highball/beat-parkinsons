March 17, 2010 first registered, submitted February 2. ISRCTN76517470 identifies a completed multicenter group self-management trial in adults with multiple sclerosis and fatigue. The assigned program combines cognitive behavioral and energy-effectiveness approaches compared with current local practice. The protocol and later findings share this identifier and provide no culinary comparison.
