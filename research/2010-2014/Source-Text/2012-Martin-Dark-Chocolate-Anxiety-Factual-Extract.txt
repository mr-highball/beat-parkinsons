Publication: Martin et al., Nutrients 4(6), 554–567. First published June 20, 2012. DOI 10.3390/nu4060554.
Coverage: Original Methods and Results section 3.2, factual extract.
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/4/6/554

The study assigned 90 healthy adults to 74% dark chocolate, a chocolate-covered wafer, or cheese and crackers. The original Results section found no statistically clear acute change in state anxiety after dark chocolate in either habitual anxiety subgroup. The abstract suggests a more favorable result in the lower-anxiety subgroup. The authors report manufacturer affiliations. Results do not estimate Parkinson anxiety or an ongoing course of symptoms.
