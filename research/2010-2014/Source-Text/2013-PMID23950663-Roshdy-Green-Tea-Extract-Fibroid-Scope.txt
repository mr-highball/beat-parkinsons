Original source scope and evidence boundary
Thirty-nine participants were randomized, with 22 assigned decaffeinated standardized green tea extract and 17 assigned placebo. Six withdrew from placebo before follow-up and 33 completed. The authors report fibroid volume, symptom composite, disease-specific quality of life and hemoglobin. Mean hemoglobin rose from 11.7 to 12.4 in the extract arm and fell from 12.95 to 12.5 in placebo, with p=.05 reported for the group contrast. A separate fatigue estimate is absent. The authors state that no adverse effects were reported and separately describe three amenorrhea observations in the extract arm. Discussion of fatigue in an earlier oncology dose-escalation trial comes from reference 60 (PMID 11251015). The fibroid outcomes cannot establish an effect of brewed green tea on Parkinson fatigue.
