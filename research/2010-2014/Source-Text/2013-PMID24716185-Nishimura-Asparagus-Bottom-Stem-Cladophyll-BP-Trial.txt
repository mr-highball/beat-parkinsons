Title: Improvement of Blood Pressure, Glucose Metabolism, and Lipid Profile by the Intake of Powdered Asparagus ( Lú Sŭn) Bottom-stems and Cladophylls.
PMID: 24716185
PMCID: PMC3924997
DOI: 10.4103/2225-4110.119728
First published: 2013
Authors: Nishimura M, Ohkawara T, Kagami-Katsuyama H, Sato H, Nishihira J
Journal: Journal of traditional and complementary medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24716185/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Ten-week open randomized two-arm comparative trial without placebo (Nishimura et al., 2013, J Tradit Complement Med, PMC3924997) allocating 28 healthy Japanese volunteers (27 completed: 13 men, 14 women) to 6 g/day Asparagus officinalis cladophyll powder or bottom-stem powder. Bottom-stem powder significantly lowered resting systolic and diastolic blood pressure, fasting glucose, and total cholesterol from baseline; cladophyll powder lowered resting DBP, FPG, left CAVI, and T-CHO. Limits: no placebo control arm, discarded cladophyll/bottom-stem powders rather than grilled culinary spears, resting BP in healthy adults without standing orthostatic testing or PD cohorts.
