Title: A multi-center, double-blind, randomised study of the Lavender oil preparation Silexan in comparison to Lorazepam for generalized anxiety disorder.
PMID: 19962288
DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2009.10.006
First published: 2010
Authors: Woelk H, Schläfke S
Journal: Phytomedicine : international journal of phytotherapy and phytopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19962288/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Multicenter double-blind randomized active-controlled trial (Woelk & Schlafke, 2010, Phytomedicine) comparing 6 weeks of oral Silexan 80 mg/day vs lorazepam 0.5 mg/day in adults with generalized anxiety disorder (baseline HAMA ~25+/-4; pooled review PMID:21170695 reports 37 lorazepam vs Silexan arm, N=77 total). HAMA decreased comparably by 11.3+/-6.7 points (45%) with Silexan and 11.6+/-6.6 points (46%) with lorazepam, with similar improvements in psychic/somatic anxiety, SAS, PSWQ-PW, SF-36, CGI, and sleep diaries, and no sedative or abuse effects with Silexan. Limits: no placebo arm, low lorazepam comparator dose (0.5 mg/day), standardized oral essential oil rather than culinary tea, non-PD GAD cohort.
