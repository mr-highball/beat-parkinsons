Planned depression and pain domains in the FACETS trial
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-2377-10-43

June 16, 2010. The full protocol for the multiple-sclerosis FACETS trial prespecifies a group fatigue-management intervention. Secondary measures include the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale and the bodily-pain dimension of the SF-36v2. The design contains no rosemary or date-fruit intervention and the protocol reports no treatment direction for either measure. The identity is ISRCTN76517470.
