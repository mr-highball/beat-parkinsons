Title: Changes Caused by Fruit Extracts in the Lipid Phase of Biological and Model Membranes
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11483-010-9175-y
DOI: 10.1007/s11483-010-9175-y
First public article date: 2010-09-03
Issue year: 2011
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original Results and model-membrane scope

The authors compare strawberry, apple and chokeberry fruit extracts
in red cells and lipid models. The outcome differs by probe and
membrane region. In the egg-derived phosphatidylcholine model the
reported laurdan general polarization is −0.205 ± 0.041 for
strawberry extract and −0.251 ± 0.005 for control. The results also
describe minor hydrophobic-region changes. In their overall summary,
the authors identify apple extract as inducing the greatest changes.

The 2009 EBSA report P-557 and this publication share the investigators,
three fruit extracts and several endpoints. The reports are counted
together for this review. These assays provide material chemistry
and molecular mechanisms without an oral dose comparison or a
human mobility outcome.
