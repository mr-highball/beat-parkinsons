Factual extract of the primary Methods and Results, checked 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

Double-blind randomized oral crossover in 23 healthy adults measured cerebral blood flow and behavioral endpoints following placebo, trans-resveratrol 250 mg, and the same resveratrol dose with 20 mg purified piperine, on visits at least one week apart. After 40 minutes the participant completed task cycles including digit vigilance by rapid visual information processing and serial subtraction. No significant treatment effects were reported for behavioral outcomes. The combination increased cerebral blood-flow measures during task performance. No culinary pepper-fruit arm, piperine-only arm or Parkinson subgroup was identified.
