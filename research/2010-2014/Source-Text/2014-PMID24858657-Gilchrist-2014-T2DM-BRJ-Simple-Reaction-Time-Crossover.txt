Title: Dietary nitrate supplementation improves reaction time in type 2 diabetes: development and application of a novel nitrate-depleted beetroot juice placebo.
PMID: 24858657
DOI: 10.1016/j.niox.2014.05.003
First published: 2014
Authors: Gilchrist M, Winyard PG, Fulford J, Anning C, Shore AC, Benjamin N
Journal: Nitric oxide : biology and chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24858657/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=27 older adults with type 2 diabetes given 250 mL nitrate-rich vs nitrate-depleted beetroot juice daily for 14 days: significantly improved simple reaction time (attention/vigilance speed) by 13.9 ms (95% CI 3.8 to 24.0 ms; p=0.009); four other CANTAB cognitive tests were not significantly changed. Limits: non-PD diabetic cohort, single positive cognitive subtest.
