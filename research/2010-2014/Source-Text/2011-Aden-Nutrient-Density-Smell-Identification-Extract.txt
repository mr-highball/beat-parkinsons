The original Results report mean Brief Smell Identification Test scores of 7.0 (SD 2.5) in the newly diagnosed Parkinson group and 9.4 (SD 2.0) in controls, p<.001. The group totals were 87 and 28. Participants used three-day food records and 24-hour recalls. In the combined sample, the Table 4 association between folate density and B-SIT was 0.063 (unstandardized coefficient, p=.022) after adjustment for age, sex, group and BMI. Table 4 also reports associations for protein energy share (p=.045), magnesium density (p=.012) and phosphorus density (p=.029). The study modeled nutrients, with no separate roasted kale estimate. The 2016 Umeå report used a different analysis, and individual overlap remains unquantified.
