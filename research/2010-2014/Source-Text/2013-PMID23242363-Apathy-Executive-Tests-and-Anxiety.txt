Title: Apathy and Related Executive Syndromes in Dementia Associated with Parkinson's Disease and in Alzheimer's Disease
PMID: 23242363
PMCID: PMC5215721
DOI: 10.3233/BEN-129023
Source: https://files01.core.ac.uk/download/pdf/194994195.pdf
Publication: Behavioural Neurology 27 (2013), pp. 515–522
Coverage: Verified complete publisher-formatted original in the CORE public copy. The separately saved PMC text is abstract-only.

The clinical comparison selected 61 participants without marked depression, including 29 with dementia associated with Parkinson disease and 32 with Alzheimer disease. The investigators distinguished self and informant apathy scales, clinician judgment, executive tasks and Neuropsychiatric Inventory subscales. They observed apathy in 14 of the 29 Parkinson dementia participants. The four-group comparison includes a separate anxiety subscale. It is a cross-sectional behavioral comparison and has no identified food intervention.

Salmon is an author surname in a cited Huntington disease article. The paper does not compare culinary salmon preparations or anxiety severity after salmon exposure.
