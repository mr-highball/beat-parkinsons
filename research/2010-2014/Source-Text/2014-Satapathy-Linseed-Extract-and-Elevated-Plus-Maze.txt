Title: Evaluation of anxiolytic potential of Linum usitatissimum oil in Wistar rats
Source: https://www.japtronline.com/index.php/JOAPR/article/view/33
Publication date: 2014-09-24
Date basis: Publisher journal landing page, volume 2, issue 3, pp. 5–9.
Original: https://media.neliti.com/media/publications/320040-evaluation-of-anxiolytic-potential-of-li-201c69d4.pdf
Coverage: Full journal original, Methods and Table 2.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

The laboratory used a petroleum-ether oil extract administered orally to healthy Wistar rats. The extract differs from cold-pressed culinary oil. Four groups of six in the elevated plus maze included vehicle, two extract levels and diazepam. Table 2 reports mean ± SEM open-arm time of 27 ± 9.372 seconds for vehicle and 200.5 ± 5.809 and 230.25 ± 6.884 seconds for extract, with p<.05 reported for the comparisons with vehicle.

The abstract and Methods give different animal weights. The abstract mentions entries and time, while Table 2 gives time alone. A separate locomotor contrast is unavailable. The potential behavioral sign concerns an extracted material in non-Parkinson animals.
