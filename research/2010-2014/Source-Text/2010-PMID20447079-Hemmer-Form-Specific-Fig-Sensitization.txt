Title: Identification of Bet v 1-related allergens in fig and other Moraceae fruits
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20447079/
PMID: 20447079
DOI: 10.1111/j.1365-2222.2010.03486.x
Publication date: 2010-03-11
Date basis: Publisher's first online date.
Coverage: Paraphrased original author abstract.

The sensitization study tested 188 people with and without birch pollen allergy. Within the isolated birch pollen allergy subgroup of 85, the authors report positive skin tests in 78% for fresh fig and 10% for dried fig. About two-thirds of birch-allergic participants who tested positive to fig reported symptoms after eating fresh figs. A separate weeping-fig-sensitized subgroup showed a different preparation pattern. Positive skin tests and retrospective symptoms are distinct observations. The accessible abstract supplies no separate estimate for drooling or a Parkinson group. Keep the full case symptom detail and preparation tables open.
