Mixed fish and mobility after hip fracture
https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02045355
selected public registry fields

Posted January 24, 2014. Planned 16 weeks of salmon, cod and herring versus meat after hip fracture. New Mobility Score was the primary outcome and falls were planned weekly. The record lists 14 participants and termination for insufficient recruitment. The registry has no posted outcomes. It gives no isolated salmon or Parkinson bradykinesia estimate.
