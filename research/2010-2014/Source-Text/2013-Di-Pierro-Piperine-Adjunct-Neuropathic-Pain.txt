Di Pierro F and Settembre R. J Pain Res 6:497–503, July 2013. PMID 23861596. PMCID PMC3704545. DOI 10.2147/JPR.S48432.
Primary full paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3704545/
Primary erratum: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3762669/

The open randomized study assigned background dexibuprofen to all groups. Comparators were background only, background with lipoic acid, or background with the combined piperine, lipoic-acid and curcumin phytosome product. 141 enrolled, 135 completed. Authors reported lower 0–10 pain ratings in the combination arm. No piperine-specific effect is identified, and the authors acknowledge the absence of a curcumin-only comparator. The August 26 erratum corrects trademark wording without reporting different clinical values. No Parkinson cohort or cracked pepper was tested.
