Title: Effect of banana consumption on faecal microbiota: a randomised, controlled trial.
PMID: 21524710
DOI: 10.1016/j.anaerobe.2011.03.018
First published: 2011
Authors: Mitsou EK, Kougia E, Nomikos T, Yannakoulia M, Mountzouris KC, Kyriacou A
Journal: Anaerobe
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21524710/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

60-day 3-arm randomized controlled trial in n=34 healthy women (age 19-45 y, BMI 24-30 kg/m2, no GI disease) consuming twice daily before meals either 1 medium ripe banana, 1 cup banana-flavored drink, or 1 cup water (control). Bifidobacterial counts increased non-significantly in the banana arm; fecal SCFAs did not differ overall. Gastrointestinal symptom records showed significantly lower bloating in the banana group vs controls on days 26-35 (p=0.009) and days 51-60 (p=0.010), and daily ripe banana consumption had no adverse effects on evacuation patterns. Limits: healthy non-constipated young women, non-PD.
