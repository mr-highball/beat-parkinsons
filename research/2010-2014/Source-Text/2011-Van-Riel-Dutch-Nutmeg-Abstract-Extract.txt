Factual extract from complete original conference abstract 133.

Van Riel and colleagues described 48 exposures in 37 people. Ground powder predominated, there were also whole-seed and coexposure cases, and three reports involved unexpectedly excess spice in food. The complete original abstract characterizes the overall course and leaves the individual tremor counts and posture unspecified. This is the Dutch series shown in the conference collection; its DOI identifies the collection and 133 the member abstract.
