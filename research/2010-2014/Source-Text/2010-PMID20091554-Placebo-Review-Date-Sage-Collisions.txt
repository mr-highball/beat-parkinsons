Original study scope
This Cochrane review compares placebo interventions to no treatment in multiple conditions. The reports include pain and other clinical outcomes. The French term sage-femme in a trial blinding account identifies an assessor profession, and study dates are chronological.

Continuing evidence gaps
The original intervention tables do not give sage or edible-date effects on any of the five frozen Parkinson symptoms.
