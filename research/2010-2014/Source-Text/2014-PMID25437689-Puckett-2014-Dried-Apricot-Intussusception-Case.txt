Title: Intussusception caused by dried apricot: A case report.
PMID: 25437689
PMCID: PMC4276074
DOI: 10.1016/j.ijscr.2014.11.016
First published: 2014
Authors: Puckett Y, Nathan J, Dissanaike S
Journal: International journal of surgery case reports
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25437689/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Surgical clinical case report of a 56-year-old woman requiring celiotomy and enterotomy for distal ileal small-bowel obstruction and intussusception caused by a lead point of swallowed swollen dried apricots after large intake. Mechanical obstruction safety warning regarding unchewed dried apricots.
