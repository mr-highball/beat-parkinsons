Title: Lipid-formulated curcumin compared with free curcumin in experimental arthritis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25400173/
PMID: 25400173
DOI: 10.1002/ejp.620
Publication date: 2014-11-17
Date basis: NLM electronic publication date, with 2015 journal issue.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Investigator abstract and verified citation in a later review. Complete publisher body was not recovered.

The abstract describes an animal arthritis experiment comparing curcumin in solid lipid nanoparticles with free curcumin. The reported measures include paw withdrawal thresholds, thermal responses, joint hyperalgesia and mobility. The authors summarize favorable results for the formulated compound over free curcumin. The abstract does not give all arm-level values or the full comparison methods.

This is a separate experiment from earlier Arora neuropathy work. It reports a pain-related result in arthritis. Its drug formulation and population give no culinary ground-turmeric estimate for Parkinson pain.
