Injected clove oil and immobility or movement in mice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23462195/
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: original journal abstract

Mehta and colleagues first published March 4, 2013. Intraperitoneal clove oil reduced immobility in the tail-suspension assay at the tested doses (p < .01), while the highest dose increased measured locomotion (p < .05). Rotarod latency decreased at the lowest dose and increased at the highest. These are experimentally evoked behaviors with a motor confound, without culinary exposure or a Parkinson apathy scale.
