Title: Species attribution in the common fig review's paralysis and fever entries
Source: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.3109/13880209.2014.892515
PMID: 25017517
DOI: 10.3109/13880209.2014.892515
Publication date: 2014-07-14
Date basis: First online date in PubMed. The issue is November 2014.
Source depth: Published review Table 5 and its Khanom reference checked against the original printed source table.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25

Table 5 of the Ficus carica review assigns dried fruit entries for fever and paralysis in Bangladesh to Khanom and colleagues. The cited original (PMID 10830501) labels the plant as Ficus religiosa and specifies fruit in its usage table. The original gives no rest-tremor phenotype or observed treatment response. The secondary entries therefore cannot provide common-fig evidence for Parkinson rest tremor. Both citations point to the same historical use attribution, whose botanical identity must be taken from the original entry.
