Acute speech loss in a clove oil and alcohol coexposure
Primary source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/0310057X1204000223?download=true
PMID: 22417049
Coverage: factual extract from primary correspondence, case pages 365–366. The text below records source facts and scope.

The March 2012 clinical letter by Dyrbye and colleagues reports a 67-year-old man found confused and unable to speak following a clove oil and alcohol coexposure. The initial Glasgow Coma Scale components were E1, M3, and V1. The authors attributed the acute episode to the combined exposure and report full recovery. The correspondence PDF DOI identifies the full multi-letter correspondence item, and PMID 22417049 identifies this case specifically. The reported acute loss of speech occurred with depressed consciousness and combined intoxication. Parkinson speech intelligibility, a standardized speech measure, and ground allspice were outside this case.
