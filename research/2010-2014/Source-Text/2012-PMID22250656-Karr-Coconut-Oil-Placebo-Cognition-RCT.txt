Title: Omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids and cognition in a college-aged population.
PMID: 22250656
DOI: 10.1037/a0026945
First published: 2012
Authors: Karr JE, Grindstaff TR, Alexander JE
Journal: Experimental and clinical psychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22250656/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled trial (Karr et al., 2012, Exp Clin Psychopharmacol) in 41 healthy college-aged adults assigned to 4 weeks of fish oil (n=20) vs coconut oil placebo (n=21), evaluated on RAVLT, Stroop, Trail Making Test (TMT), and PANAS. Coconut oil placebo showed a TMT practice/learning gain while RAVLT stages 6-7 declined relative to fish oil; authors attributed the TMT change to a learning effect. Active comparator design with young healthy adults and unspecified oil grade supplies no Parkinson attention direction.
