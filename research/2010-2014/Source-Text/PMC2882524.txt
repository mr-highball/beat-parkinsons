The review reuses the sleep comparison of 14 left-onset and 17 right-onset Parkinson participants with 17 chronically ill controls from PMID 18797256. The studies of spatial perception and sleep use different tests. In Figure 12 the review reverses the sleep-item scale for frequency display. Nocturnal hallucination ratings are not a separately counted visual-episode outcome for dried figs.
