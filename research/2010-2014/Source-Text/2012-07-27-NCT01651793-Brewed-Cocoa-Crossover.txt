Registered brewed cocoa crossover and posted cognitive and vigor outcomes
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01651793
Registration: NCT01651793
Coverage: Official registration and results plus linked original paper

The study record was first posted July 27, 2012 and lists completed status and posted results. It registers a crossover of brewed cocoa, cocoa with added caffeine, flavored water, and caffeinated flavored water. The registered main outcomes include serial subtraction and Bakan-task performance, and the secondary mood result is POMS vigor. The 2017 linked report additionally analyzes anxiety under a cognitive challenge. Registered and published outcomes should be kept distinct. This study used drinks and had no Parkinson participants.
