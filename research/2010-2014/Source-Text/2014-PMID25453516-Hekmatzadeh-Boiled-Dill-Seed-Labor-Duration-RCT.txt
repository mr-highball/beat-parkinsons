Title: A randomized clinical trial of the efficacy of applying a simple protocol of boiled Anethum Graveolens seeds on pain intensity and duration of labor stages.
PMID: 25453516
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2014.10.007
First published: 2014
Authors: Hekmatzadeh SF, Bazarganipour F, Malekzadeh J, Goodarzi F, Aramesh S
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25453516/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized clinical trial (Hekmatzadeh et al., 2014, Complement Ther Med) enrolling 153 women in active labor allocated to a single oral dose of boiled filtered Anethum graveolens seeds (10 g in 100 mL water) vs routine care. Boiled dill seed significantly shortened labor stages (except second stage in primiparous women) and improved cervical dilatation and effacement at 1-4 hours; however, the indexed abstract does not report a separate pain-intensity estimate between arms. Limits: obstetric labor progression trial without separately reported pain-intensity statistics or Parkinson's outcomes.
