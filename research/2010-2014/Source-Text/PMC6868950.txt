Title: Progression of brain atrophy in the early stages of Parkinson's disease: a longitudinal tensor-based morphometry study in de novo patients without cognitive impairment
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6868950/
PMCID: PMC6868950
PMID: 24453162
DOI: 10.1002/hbm.22449
Publication date: 2014-01-22
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Evidence type: Primary longitudinal imaging
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Patients recruited with de novo parkinsonism underwent clinical screening and structural MRI with follow-up. The analysis focused on progression of brain atrophy in early diagnosed Parkinson disease without cognitive impairment. Motor symptoms and medication timing characterize the cohort. Brain volume change does not measure a dried fig exposure or Parkinson visual episode frequency.
