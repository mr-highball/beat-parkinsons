Title: Effect of herbal extract granules combined with probiotic mixture on irritable bowel syndrome with diarrhea: study protocol for a randomized controlled trial
PMID: 21978382
PMCID: PMC3198690
DOI: 10.1186/1745-6215-12-219
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1745-6215-12-219
First published: 2011-10-06
Text coverage: factual extract of original protocol and Table 1

The protocol describes Gwakhyangjeonggisan (GJS), supplied as Gwakjungsan granule (GJG) by Hanpoong, as a water extract with starch and lactose. Table 1 lists 13 botanical ingredients including Agastache rugosa and Perilla frutescens. Melissa officinalis, Mentha spicata and Coriandrum sativum are absent from that explicit composition table. Names and plant-part labels should follow the printed source when transcribed.

The factorial trial plans active or placebo GJS with active or placebo Duolac7S, a seven-strain probiotic mixture, for eight weeks. GJG is dissolved in water before administration. This is a manufactured extract preparation. The protocol identifies NCT01342718 and IRB KHNMC-OH-IRB 2010-011. It reports first randomization on 2011-04-25. The 2013 Ko report explicitly cites this protocol as reference 13, forming one study family.

The protocol contains planned IBS outcomes and supplies no observed depressive symptom result.
