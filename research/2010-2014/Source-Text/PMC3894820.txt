The four-year prospective study follows newly observed impulse control disorders during dopamine agonist treatment in Parkinson disease. It measures timing, medication exposures and clinical risk factors for compulsive behaviors. These outcomes differ from visual hallucination frequency and have no dried fig exposure comparison.
