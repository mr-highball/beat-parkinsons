Canola oil in an oral olive oil challenge
Source: https://doi.org/10.19070/2332-2799-140003
DOI: 10.19070/2332-2799-140003
Coverage: Original publisher case report PDF

Karam and colleagues published August 21, 2014. The report describes a 25-year-old patient with prior throat irritation, coughing and other symptoms after olive oil exposure. An inhalation challenge used raspberry dressings containing either canola or extra-virgin olive oil and produced no reported reaction. A later oral challenge used graded blends. An oral dressing containing 60% extra-virgin olive oil and canola was followed by throat and eye pruritus, shortness of breath and a reported 7% decline in FEV1. The report uses canola as a diluent and comparator in its examination of olive oil sensitivity. It does not give a canola-only oral voice measurement, processing grade or a Parkinson cohort.
