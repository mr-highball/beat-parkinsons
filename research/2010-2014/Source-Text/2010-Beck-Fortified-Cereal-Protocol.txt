Gold kiwi with fortified cereal trial protocol
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2823703/

January 26, 2010. The prospective Beck protocol describes 89 randomized participants, women with low iron stores, assigned two gold kiwifruit or a banana with the same iron-fortified cereal and milk for 16 weeks. Its endpoints are iron status, ascorbate, carotenoids and other physiological or intake measures. The registration identifier links the August 2010 report. Cognitive attention is outside the listed outcomes.
