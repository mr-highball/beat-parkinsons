The original book chapter covers distinct topical products, named diseases, illustrations and references. The food names and outcomes occur in separate contexts. Its narrative claims and cited preparations do not establish food efficacy for the frozen pairs.

Claim 0033 | PMCID:PMC7498916 | The chapter discusses topical ozonated sunflower oil in skin conditions and cites an ozonated-oil gastric-injury assay. The altered topical preparation and outcomes provide no culinary sunflower oil estimate for this Parkinson symptom.
Claim 0259 | PMCID:PMC7498916 | Orange peel describes skin appearance in the chapter. It is neither a fruit exposure nor a comparison of fall frequency.
Claim 0329 | PMCID:PMC7498916 | The chapter mentions avoiding eggs in atopic dermatitis and separately uses egg to refer to an oocyte. The clinical food mention supplies no dietary egg comparison for falls.
Claim 0348 | PMCID:PMC7498916 | Chicken occurs in the clinical history of chickenpox. The chapter does not evaluate eating chicken for Parkinson fatigue.
