Original source scope
The study compared isolated piperine in five groups of developing mice, with a nociceptive-response test among the behavioral measures. The abstract summarizes behavioral findings across tests and supplies no separable pain contrast or constipation result. The experimental piperine exposure does not identify a cracked black pepper or Parkinson effect. PMID 34471461 reference 170. Round 1 claims 0227 and 0312.
