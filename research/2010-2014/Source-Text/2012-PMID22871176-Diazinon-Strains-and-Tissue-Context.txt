Title: The role of oxidative stress in diazinon-induced tissues toxicity in Wistar and Norway rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22871176/
PMID: 22871176
DOI: 10.3109/15376516.2012.716090
Publication date: 2012
Text coverage: Distilled original author abstract and bibliographic identity. Full methods remain open.

Female Wistar and Norway rats were compared after acute pesticide exposure, with brain, heart and spleen biochemical endpoints. The reported changes concern oxidative stress enzymes and related tissue markers. The abstract does not identify the vehicle or give a mobility endpoint. This design differs from the male Wistar vitamin E intervention in FEBS poster P26.55. The related female strain comparison reported in 2016 has liver and kidney endpoints and a documented corn-oil vehicle. Shared animals between the two female strain reports are unverified.
