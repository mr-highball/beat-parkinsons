Measured stride length in a multimodal exercise pilot
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3263544/

October 30, 2011. The nonrandom pilot analyzed 19 trained and 10 control participants. The stride-length group-by-time interaction was p = .005 and the trained group improved after six months. The overall clinical model did not show the same interaction (p = .535) and did not isolate rigidity. This is an exercise comparison.
