Title: Pharmacological basis for the medicinal use of muskmelon base (Pedicellus Melo.) for abdominal distention and constipation.
PMID: 22543170
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2012.04.025
First published: 2012
Authors: Gao Y, Cai RL, Xie C, Lin YL, Zhang L, Qi Y
Journal: J Ethnopharmacol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22543170/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical ICR mouse, SD rat, and guinea pig study of ethanol extract of muskmelon base/pedicel (Pedicellus Melo, fruit stalk; single rectal/intracolonic doses 4-46 mg/kg). Accelerated gastric emptying, intestinal transit, and proximal colonic contractility via atropine-sensitive cholinergic signaling; MTD 400 mg/kg. Documents Ming-dynasty Chinese traditional use of Pedicellus Melo rectal suppositories for abdominal distention and constipation. Critical plant-part and route boundary: non-edible fruit pedicel administered rectally, NOT oral raw cantaloupe flesh.
