Title: Hibiscus sabdariffa L. tea (tisane) lowers blood pressure in prehypertensive and mildly hypertensive adults
PMID: 20018807
DOI: 10.3945/jn.109.115097
First published: 2010
Authors: McKay DL, Chen CY, Saltzman E, Blumberg JB
Journal: Journal of Nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20018807/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial in 65 prehypertensive and mildly hypertensive adults (aged 30-70 years, not taking antihypertensive medication). Participants consumed three 240-mL servings/day of brewed Hibiscus sabdariffa tisane or matching placebo beverage for 6 weeks. Brewed hibiscus tea significantly lowered systolic blood pressure (-7.2 +/- 11.4 vs -1.3 +/- 10.0 mmHg, P=0.030), with greater reductions among participants with higher baseline SBP (r=-0.421, P=0.010). Diastolic BP (-3.1 +/- 7.0 vs -0.5 +/- 7.5 mmHg, P=0.160) and mean arterial pressure (-4.5 +/- 7.7 vs -0.8 +/- 7.4 mmHg, P=0.054) trended lower. No orthostatic tilt-table readings, standing BP drops, or Parkinson's disease outcomes were measured.
