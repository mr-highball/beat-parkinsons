The 2007 Zhao comparison used ginger-partitioned moxibustion for chemotherapy-associated leukopenia. The review table lists constipation within a group of gastrointestinal toxicity outcomes including nausea, vomiting and diarrhea, and it gives no constipation-specific contrast. The separate 2006 Liu and Wang reference compares topical ginger-salt-partitioned moxibustion for poststroke urination. Both exposures combine heat with a partition, with no oral culinary ginger comparison.
