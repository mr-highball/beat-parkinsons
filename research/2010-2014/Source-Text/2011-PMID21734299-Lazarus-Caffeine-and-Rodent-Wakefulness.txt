Factual summary
The original reports rodent wakefulness and locomotor activity after isolated caffeine, with sleep and wake measured separately from locomotor activity. General background mentions fatigue. The experiment did not measure fatigue severity, use brewed coffee, compare poached chicken or study Parkinson patients.
