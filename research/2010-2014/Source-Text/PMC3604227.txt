Title: Drug induced parkinsonism caused by the concurrent use of donepezil and risperidone in a patient with traumatic brain injuries
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3604227/
PMCID: PMC3604227
PMID: 23526695
DOI: 10.5535/arm.2013.37.1.147
Publication date: 2013-02-28
Date basis: NLM electronic and journal issue date
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary clinical case
Format: Factual digest of the original material

A person with traumatic brain injury developed parkinsonian signs during combined risperidone and donepezil treatment. The report describes no observed auditory or visual hallucinations at rehospitalization despite other behavioral symptoms. Dopamine transporter imaging and improvement after withdrawal inform the differential for drug-induced parkinsonism. The case did not test dried figs or a clinical visual episode frequency.
