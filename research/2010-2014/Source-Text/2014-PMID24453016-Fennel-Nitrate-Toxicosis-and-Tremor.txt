Title: Outbreak of fatal nitrate toxicosis associated with consumption of fennels (Foeniculum vulgare) in cattle farmed in Campania region (southern Italy)
PMID: 24453016
DOI: 10.1007/s11356-014-2520-9
Publication date: 2014-01-23
Citation: Costagliola and colleagues. Environmental Science and Pollution Research 21, 6252–6257.
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11356-014-2520-9
Coverage: Author abstract and Table 1 in a public copy of the primary article.

Fifteen cows in a single outbreak developed muscular tremor and systemic illness after eating market waste from grown fennel. The authors found nitrate in the feed plant material and in samples from the cattle. Table 1 reports nitrate concentrations of 4,672.2 ppm for green plant material and 2,915.2 ppm for the white part, on a dry-weight basis. This is a source for a contaminant-associated tremor in cattle. The article provides no isolated seed exposure, disease-matched comparison or resting-tremor phenotype.
