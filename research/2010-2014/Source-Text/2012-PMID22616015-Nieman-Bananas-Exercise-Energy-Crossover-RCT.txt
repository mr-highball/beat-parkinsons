Title: Bananas as an energy source during exercise: a metabolomics approach.
PMID: 22616015
PMCID: PMC3355124
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0037479
First published: 2012
Authors: Nieman DC, Gillitt ND, Henson DA, Sha W, Shanely RA, Knab AM
Journal: PloS one
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22616015/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

2-arm randomized crossover trial (PMC3355124) in n=14 trained cyclists completing two 75-km cycling time trials while consuming raw Cavendish bananas or a 6% carbohydrate sports drink (0.2 g/kg carbohydrate every 15 min). Cycling performance time (p=0.258) and rating of perceived exertion (14.6 vs 14.4, p=0.613) did not differ between bananas and carbohydrate drink, with higher plasma dopamine after bananas and slightly more fullness/bloating. Limits: no water-only control arm; acute athletic exertion in non-PD adults.
