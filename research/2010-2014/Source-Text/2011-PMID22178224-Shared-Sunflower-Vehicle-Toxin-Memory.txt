Title: Behavioral effects of oral subacute exposure to BDE-209 in young adult mice: a preliminary study
Publication date: 2011-12-09 (online first)
PMID: 22178224
DOI: 10.1016/j.fct.2011.12.002
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22178224/
Text coverage: Primary indexed abstract.

Young adult mice received BDE-209 or a zero-dose control in sunflower oil. The toxin comparison gave lower anxiety-related behavior and delayed spatial learning after BDE-209. Both arms had oil, so exposure to the contaminant is the difference. Neither an oil-free comparator, a pressing grade nor an executive outcome in Parkinson disease is established.
