Title: Endurance after caffeine in maximally and submaximally stimulated mouse soleus
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12576-012-0247-2
PMID: 23292763
PMCID: PMC10717149
DOI: 10.1007/s12576-012-0247-2
Publication date: 2013-01-06
Date basis: NLM and publisher electronic publication.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Author abstract on the original publisher page and NLM. PMC returned identity metadata without the body.

Isolated mouse soleus exposed to caffeine showed 17.6% lower endurance at maximal stimulation and 19.2% higher endurance at submaximal stimulation. The direction changes with the tissue activation condition. These are measured muscle-endurance outcomes in an isolated preparation, not reports of human fatigue or a result after a brewed beverage. Full trial figures and sample-specific estimates remain an access question.
