Clary sage and peanut oil in a 2013 symposium abstract
Source: https://www.iseoils.com/images/documents/boa/ISEO_book-of-abstract_2013.pdf
Coverage: 44th ISEO dated meeting abstract book, numbered child BA29 only

Abstract BA29 in the September 8–12, 2013 Budapest meeting book reports a 30-person dermal study comparing clary sage in peanut oil with peanut oil alone alongside separate inhalation experiments. It provides no oil-specific clinical depression or anxiety estimate. A thesis dated January 2013 and the 2020 journal abstract share investigators, sample size, formulation and comparator in the dermal experiment. Individual overlap is unverified. The book year and meeting dates are verified. The posting date of the current online copy is unverified.
