Pigeon WR, Carr M, Gorman C and Perlis ML. Original pilot crossover, first online May 3, 2010. PMID 20438325, PMCID PMC3133468, DOI 10.1089/jmf.2009.0096.
Fifteen older adults with insomnia completed randomized periods of a proprietary Montmorency tart cherry and apple juice mixture and placebo. Treatments each lasted two weeks. The secondary MFI means in Table 1 were 49.1 (SD 12.5) at baseline, 44.1 (9.4) after placebo, and 46.2 (11.5) after the cherry beverage. The reported interaction was inconclusive, without exact interval or p value in the table. No dried whole cherries or Parkinson population.
