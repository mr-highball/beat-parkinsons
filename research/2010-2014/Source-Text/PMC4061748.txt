The paper compares sleep outcomes across multiple system atrophy, dementia with Lewy bodies, progressive supranuclear palsy and other atypical syndromes. Visual hallucinations are mentioned as clinical features and in cited medication intolerance. Sleep observations and case citations do not establish a dried fig or idiopathic Parkinson episode rate.
