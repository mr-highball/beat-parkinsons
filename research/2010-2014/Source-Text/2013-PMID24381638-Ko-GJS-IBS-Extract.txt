Text coverage: factual extract of original full report

Ko and colleagues screened 64 IBS-D patients, randomized 53 and retained 48 through completion. The four randomized arms had 13, 14, 14 and 12 participants. They used active or placebo Gwakjungsan granule and active or placebo Duolac7S for eight weeks. The GJS product is a water extract with starch and lactose. The cited 2011 protocol gives its 13-herb composition.

Results 3.2 reports higher responder proportions for the three active combinations than double placebo. Weekly adequate relief and individual bowel symptom scores had no significant between-group differences. IBS quality of life and its subdomains, including dysphoria, also had no significant between-group difference. Methods define responders by at least 50% symptom reduction, while Figure 3 defines them by adequate relief during at least half the weekly assessments, agreeing with the earlier protocol. Preserve that internal definition discrepancy.

This publication and the 2011 protocol are one trial family. The primary product composition differs from the three-herb Carmint formula. It provides no Melissa-specific or depressive symptom result.
