Title: Eicosapentaenoic acid versus docosahexaenoic acid in mild-to-moderate depression: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 22910528
DOI: 10.1016/j.euroneuro.2012.08.003
First published: 2013
Authors: Mozaffari-Khosravi H, Yassini-Ardakani M, Karamati M, Shariati-Bafghi SE
Journal: European neuropsychopharmacology : the journal of the European College of Neuropsychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22910528/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial (Mozaffari-Khosravi et al., 2013, Eur Neuropsychopharmacol) in 81 outpatients with mild-to-moderate depression (62 analyzed) randomized for 12 weeks to 1 g/day EPA, 1 g/day DHA, or coconut oil placebo adjunct to maintenance medication. EPA significantly outperformed coconut oil placebo on 17-item HAM-D (p=0.002), with 6 treatment responders on EPA vs 0 responders on coconut oil placebo or DHA. Demonstrates lack of clinical antidepressant response to low-dose coconut oil comparator in non-PD depression.
