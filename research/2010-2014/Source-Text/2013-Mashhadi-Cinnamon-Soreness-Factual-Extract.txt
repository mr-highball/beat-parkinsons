Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3665015/
PMID: 23717759
Coverage: Factual comparison and source limitations.

Sixty athletes assigned to placebo, oral cinnamon powder or ginger powder. Forty-nine completed. The Results and abstract report p < .10 for cinnamon versus placebo on muscle soreness. The conclusion states a clearer ginger observation than cinnamon. The report also gives conflicting intervention durations. No Parkinson population or separate Parkinson pain endpoint.
