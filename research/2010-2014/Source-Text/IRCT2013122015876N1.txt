Coverage: factual extract of official English and Persian registry and WHO export retrieved 2026-11-16.

The randomized rheumatoid arthritis adjunct trial compares an olive and fig semi-solid formula plus usual therapy with usual therapy. The registry records a target sample of 58 and completed recruitment. February 20, 2014 is the expected recruitment start. Actual start and end are blank, although WHO shows the expected value under date of first enrollment. Published reports describe September 2014 to August 2015 sampling and 72 entrants with 56 completers.

The registry specifies 15 mL twice a day. Both linked publications report 15 g three times a day. Outcomes recorded in the registry include DAS28 disease activity, joint pain, urine color and viscosity, fasting glucose, blood lipid profile and diarrhea monitoring. Visual hallucinations and Parkinson disease are outside the specified outcome and population. No result posting was located in the retrieved pages.
