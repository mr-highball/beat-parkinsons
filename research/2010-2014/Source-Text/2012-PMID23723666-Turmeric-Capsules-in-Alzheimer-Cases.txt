Original source scope and evidence boundary
Three Alzheimer dementia cases received oral turmeric powder in capsules. The clinicians report changes in behavioral symptoms and caregiver distress using the NPI-Q and report cognition results that varied between cases. Background medication and repeated clinical observations limit attribution. The investigators report no adverse reactions in the observed clinical or routine laboratory data. They do not provide a fall count, Parkinson cohort, isolated gait outcome or matched ground turmeric food preparation. This case series is the human turmeric source cited by PMID 25025046. The case series and its review are one underlying source.
