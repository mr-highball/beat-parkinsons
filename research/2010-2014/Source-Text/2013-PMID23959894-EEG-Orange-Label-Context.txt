This study compares auditory EEG measures in healthy human volunteers and macaques. The original methods describe auditory stimuli and the results describe neurophysiological responses. Orange denotes a curve in Figure 3B, and rise and fall describe sound timing. The population, exposure and outcomes are outside the frozen Parkinson claim about raw orange intake and falls.
