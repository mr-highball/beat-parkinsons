Title: Crocin and forced-swim behavior in rats
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3927874/
PMID: 24401376
PMCID: PMC3927874
DOI: 10.1186/2008-2231-22-16
Publication date: 2014-01-08
Date basis: First journal publication.
Review basis: Complete original text via NLM structured PMC record. Methods and Figures 1–3 inspected.
Source type: Controlled animal experiment.

Thirty male Wistar rats were randomized into five groups of six, with saline and imipramine comparisons. Purified crocin from saffron stigma was administered by injection. The observer scoring immobility in the forced-swim test was unaware of allocation. The three crocin groups had less immobility than saline, with Tukey p<.01 at the lower and higher tested levels and p<.05 at the middle level. The behavioral result did not show a dose-response ordering. CREB and BDNF protein findings differed from their corresponding transcript findings.

For Round 1 claim 0063, this is an indirect result on behavior in rats after purified crocin. The forced-swim endpoint differs from diagnosed depression, and injected purified material differs from ground saffron by mouth. The 2017 saffron-crocin water-maze paper cites this work but measured learning and memory in a separate sample.
