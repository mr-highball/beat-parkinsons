Title: Increase in plasma concentrations of geranylgeranoic acid after turmeric tablet intake by healthy volunteers
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2872231/
PMID: 20490321
PMCID: PMC2872231
DOI: 10.3164/jcbn.09-109
Publication date: 2010-04-10
Date basis: First online date in the original record.
Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: full_text

Seven healthy volunteers received formulated turmeric tablets with milk or milk alone in an exposure comparison. The original used liquid chromatography and mass spectrometry to estimate circulating geranylgeranoic acid (GGA). It reports higher plasma GGA within two hours of tablet intake, persisting through four hours and returning toward baseline by eight hours. The milk-only comparison did not show the same rise. The study provides a human constituent-exposure observation for a formulated tablet. It measured neither pain nor falls and did not assign a culinary turmeric preparation in Parkinson disease.
