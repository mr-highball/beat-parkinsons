Title: Possible involvement of GABAergic and nitriergic systems for antianxiety-like activity of piperine in unstressed and stressed mice.
PMID: 25149996
DOI: 10.1016/j.pharep.2014.05.008
First published: 2014
Authors: Gilhotra N, Dhingra D
Journal: Pharmacological reports : PR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25149996/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse experiment evaluating isolated piperine (5, 10, 20 mg/kg i.p.) in unstressed mice and mice subjected to 6-h immobilization stress using elevated plus maze, light-dark box, and social interaction tests (diazepam positive control). Piperine produced significant anxiolytic-like effects comparable to diazepam in both unstressed (via increased brain GABA / nNOS inhibition) and stressed mice (via decreased plasma nitrite / iNOS inhibition). Limits: intraperitoneal isolated piperine in rodents, not oral ground black pepper in PD.
