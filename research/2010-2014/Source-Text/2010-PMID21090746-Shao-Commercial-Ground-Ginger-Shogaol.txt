Title: Measured 6-shogaol in three commercial ground ginger powders
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3446752/
PMID: 21090746
PMCID: PMC3446752
DOI: 10.1021/jf1029256
Publication date: 2010-11-23
Date basis: First online date in PubMed. Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry 58(24), 12608–12614.
Source depth: Original full analytical paper, including Figure 4 and Table 4.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25

The paper measured eight ginger compounds in 11 retail products using liquid chromatography and electrochemical detection. Products 9–11 were ground dried ginger rhizome. Table 4 reports 6-shogaol at 145.62 ± 2.71, 115.67 ± 13.76 and 149.54 ± 8.47 mg per 100 g of product, respectively. The sample means span 1.157–1.495 mg/g. Values describe physical product composition. The three powders were not tested for clinical effects.

The direct chemical measurements provide an exposure bridge between culinary ground ginger and the isolated 6-shogaol comparison in the WO2024101890A1 animal family. The patent's ethanol extract and the retail powders were not compared chemically, and this study cannot establish absorption, a dose equivalence or a human response.
