The authors examine the NIMH Research Domain Criteria framework, comparing explanations for hallucinations across clinical and nonclinical groups. The worked example emphasizes auditory verbal hallucinations. A conceptual extension to other modalities cannot establish the effect of dried fig intake on visual hallucination frequency in Parkinson disease.
