Title: Corn oil ethyl ester comparator and cognitive testing
Source: https://academic.oup.com/milmed/article-abstract/179/4/396/4160759
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Dretsch and colleagues, Military Medicine, April 2014, 179(4):396–403. Original abstract.
Seventy-eight deployed soldiers were randomized for 60 days, 44 receiving EPA plus DHA and 34 receiving corn oil ethyl esters. The abstract describes no statistically significant arm difference across the cognitive battery and psychological-health indices. The abstract gives no attention-specific estimate by arm. An omega-3 index association with daytime sleepiness concerns a separate analysis.
The comparator was chemically esterified rather than an identified culinary oil grade. There was no separate control without oil and no Parkinson cohort.
