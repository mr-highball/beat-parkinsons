Title: Spezielle Arzneimitteltherapie in der Schwangerschaft
PMCID: PMC7271212
DOI: 10.1016/B978-3-437-21203-1.10002-0
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7271212/
Date basis: 2012 print edition, pp. 33–574. Electronic release 2013-08-09.
Coverage: Factual summary of the complete book chapter, including the pregnancy nausea and herbal subsections.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Sections on pyridoxine and ginger and on ginger products describe oral antiemetic studies in pregnancy. The cranberry entry describes urinary indications. The sage entry distinguishes Salvia officinalis oils and alcoholic preparations from culinary amounts. The cinnamon citation concerns glucose and lipids in type 2 diabetes. The 2025 expression of concern for that study is at https://doi.org/10.2337/dc25-ec09.

One orange juice example concerns a mixture used in the context of labor induction, whereas Agent Orange is a separate herbicide topic. Oregano occurs as a possible ingredient in a smoked drug mixture. The chapter does not report measured effects for the assigned culinary Parkinson symptom pairs.
