Diabetic rat intestinal transit and colonic contractility
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21849153/
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

In male Wistar rats with streptozotocin-induced diabetes, curry leaf extract was reported to increase propagation of a charcoal meal and improve an acetylcholine response in isolated distal colon. The study also measured blood glucose and intestinal oxidation markers. The indexed abstract describes small-intestinal transit without a separate gastric emptying test.
