Title: Intestinal microbial metabolism of phosphatidylcholine and cardiovascular risk
Publication date: 2013-04-25
PMID: 23614584
DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1109400
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23614584/
Text coverage: Primary results for the measured oral challenge and separate clinical cohort.

The experimental meal included two hard-boiled eggs and labeled phosphatidylcholine. Microbial suppression with antibiotics attenuated expected TMAO production after this meal. The cardiovascular association was estimated separately using a clinical cohort and circulating TMAO, with adjusted context distinct from the meal. The source supplies an actual boiled egg exposure but no comparison isolating poaching, neurologic effects, fall frequency or a Parkinson group.
