Title: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of the effect of dried purple carrot on body mass, lipids, blood pressure, body composition, and inflammatory markers in overweight and obese adults: the QUENCH trial.
PMID: 23746205
DOI: 10.1139/cjpp-2012-0349
First published: 2013
Authors: Wright OR, Netzel GA, Sakzewski AR
Journal: Can J Physiol Pharmacol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23746205/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (QUENCH trial) in 16 overweight/obese men (mean age 53.1 y) testing dried purple carrot (118.5 mg/d anthocyanins, 259.2 mg/d phenolic acids) for 4 weeks. Completely null for blood pressure, CRP, body mass, appetite, and LDL/total cholesterol; HDL slightly lower (p<0.05); liver enzymes unchanged. Small non-PD RCT showing null vascular/inflammatory change.
