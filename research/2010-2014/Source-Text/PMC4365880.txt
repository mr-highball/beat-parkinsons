The review separates psychotic symptoms by dementia diagnosis and discusses formed visual experiences, other hallucinations and delusions. The Parkinson and DLB sections describe published clinical and imaging cohorts and cholinergic and serotonergic hypotheses. Its cited comparisons include perceptual tasks and presence or absence of symptoms. The review supplies no additional participant sample or dietary contrast.
