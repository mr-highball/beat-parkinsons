Title: Early-Onset L-dopa-Responsive Parkinsonism with Pyramidal Signs Due to ATP13A2, PLA2G6, FBXO7 and Spatacsin Mutations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6005705/
PMCID: PMC6005705
PMID: 20669327
DOI: 10.1002/mds.23221
Publication date: 2010-09-08
Date basis: Wiley first publication; 2010-09-15 issue
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Complex early-onset parkinsonism family series
Format: Factual digest of the original publication

The case series examines five families with complex early-onset parkinsonism and reuses previously reported clinical descriptions. Visual hallucinations appear in the background on earlier ATP13A2 follow-up. One FBXO7-family case developed psychosis during levodopa treatment, prompting clinicians to withdraw it. The authors give no visual episode count for this case or a dietary comparison.
