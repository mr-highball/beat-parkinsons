Title: Clinical benefits of gluten free diet in Parkinson's disease. A case report
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/S1590-8658(14)60162-6
DOI: 10.1016/S1590-8658(14)60162-6
Publication: 2014, Digestive and Liver Disease 46 supplement, P.02.3, S56–S57
Depth: Original congress abstract across two pages.

Jafrancesco and Filippetti describe a person with established Parkinson disease and possible gluten sensitivity without characteristic villous changes. Levodopa had already improved tremor, rigidity and walking. After the broader dietary change, the authors describe improved overall motor symptoms and reported OFF time, with medication changes documented later. They supply no separate rest-tremor outcome or assigned cooked wheat berry exposure. This is distinct from the Di Lazzaro silent-celiac case.
