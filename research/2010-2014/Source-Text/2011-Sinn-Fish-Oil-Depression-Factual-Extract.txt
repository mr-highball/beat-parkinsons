Publication: Sinn et al., British Journal of Nutrition. First published September 20, 2011. PMID 21929835, DOI 10.1017/S0007114511004788.
Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114511004788
Coverage: Original trial Methods, Geriatric Depression Scale results in Table 3 and Figure 2.

Fifty older adults with mild cognitive impairment entered the randomized comparison of EPA-rich capsules, DHA-rich capsules, and safflower oil. Forty completed. Six-month differences in change on the Geriatric Depression Scale relative to safflower oil were −1.40 (95% CI −2.47 to −0.32) for DHA and −1.23 (−2.37 to −0.09) for EPA. The analysis removed one improving control participant. The EPA result's p value differs slightly across the original table and figure. The study used concentrated fish oils, with no test of poached trout or a clinical Parkinson depression cohort.
