Title: Sage leaf aqueous and butanol extracts and animal pain behaviors
Publication: 2010-10
PMID: 20860437
DOI: 10.3109/13880200903530763
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20860437/
Text type: original abstract evidence summary

Qnais and colleagues report that aqueous and butanol extracts of Salvia officinalis leaves altered hot-plate responses in mice and reduced formalin-related paw licking in rats. The studies use extracts and induced pain behaviors. The accessible peer-reviewed abstract gives no sample sizes, detailed group means or clinical findings. The publisher full body was unavailable during the check.
