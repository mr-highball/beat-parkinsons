Original source scope
Randomized double-blind crossover pilot with 39 healthy older adults. It compared caffeinated coffee, two decaffeinated coffees with differing chlorogenic acid and residual caffeine, and placebo. Paper results and discussion report lower acute mental fatigue with caffeinated coffee. The high-chlorogenic acid decaf comparison against conventional decaf is described as a tendency for mental fatigue, with a stronger alertness result. This refines the apparent certainty in the 2020 review PMID 33374338, reference 327. Neither alertness nor task-provoked acute fatigue in healthy people establishes a chronic Parkinson fatigue response with an ordinary brewed coffee preparation. Camfield PMID 24349389 is a separate cohort. Round 1 0121.
