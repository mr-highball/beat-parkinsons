Title: An approach to the phytochemical profiling of rocket [Eruca sativa (Mill.) Thell].
PMID: 23794443
DOI: 10.1002/jsfa.6286
First published: 2013
Authors: Villatoro-Pulido M, Priego-Capote F, Álvarez-Sánchez B, Saha S, Philo M, Obregón-Cano S, et al.
Journal: Journal of the science of food and agriculture
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23794443/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Analytical phytochemical profiling study (Villatoro-Pulido et al., 2013, J Sci Food Agric) of four Eruca sativa (rocket) leaf accessions quantifying total leaf glucosinolates (14.02-28.24 umol/g dry weight, with glucoraphanin up to 52% of total), variable endogenous myrosinase hydrolysis activity, flavonol phenolics, and carotenoids (16.2-275 ug/g, mainly lutein). Bridges leaf glucosinolate-myrosinase chemistry to raw arugula, but measures no animal or human clinical symptoms.
