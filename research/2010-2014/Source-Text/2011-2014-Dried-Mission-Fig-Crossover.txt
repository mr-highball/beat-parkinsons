Dried Mission fig crossover: main and ancillary reports
Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3169356/ and https://doi.org/10.1017/S1368980014001566
Coverage: Selected factual methods and endpoints from author reports.

The 2011 paper reports 102 randomized and 83 in the paired lipid comparison. Whole dried California Mission figs were supplied at 120 g/day, approximately 12–15 figs, for five weeks within a ten-week crossover. The study supplied calorie replacement guidance, measured serum lipids and diet, and found no differential LDL, HDL or triglyceride concentrations at its corrected threshold. The ancillary paper, first online in 2014 and in print in 2015, examined data from 88 participants in the same 2008 trial. It reported greater intakes of some minerals but no clear blood mineral changes. Both reports concern the same cohort. Visual hallucination frequency and Parkinson diagnosis were outside the reported analyses.
