Title: A Retrospective Review of Supratherapeutic Modafinil Exposures

DOI: 10.1007/s13181-010-0017-6
Publication date: 2010-04-01
Date basis: Publisher first online date
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Poison-center chart review
Format: Factual digest of the original publication

The investigators retained 87 single-agent modafinil cases from a poison-center database. Two had hallucinations, and one clinical narrative explicitly describes visual hallucinations following a large unintended exposure. This measures adverse presentation after pharmaceutical exposure, without a Parkinson cohort or dried-fig contrast.
