Title: DNA damage and repair activity after broccoli intake in young healthy smokers
Publication date: 2010-08-16 (online publication)
PMID: 20713433
DOI: 10.1093/mutage/geq045
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20713433/
Text coverage: Primary indexed abstract and dietary crossover comparison.

Twenty-seven young healthy smokers completed a 250 g/day steamed broccoli or control diet crossover with ten-day periods. Oxidized DNA lesions in blood mononuclear cells fell by 41% (95% CI 10% to 72%) and resistance to induced strand breaks increased by 23% (95% CI 13% to 34%). Measured repair enzyme activity and expression were unchanged. This is a preparation-matched exposure with a laboratory endpoint and no Parkinson falls assessment.
