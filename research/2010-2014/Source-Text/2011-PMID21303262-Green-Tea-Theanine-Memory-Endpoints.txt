Original source scope and evidence boundary
Ninety-one people with mild cognitive impairment entered a randomized double-blind placebo comparison of LGNC-07, a green tea extract and L-theanine formulation. The author abstract reports delayed recognition p=.0572. Positive memory and selective attention subgroup results concern 11 versus 9 participants in the lower baseline cognitive score stratum. A 24-person EEG subgroup supplies cognitive alertness measurements. A separately measured tiredness or Parkinson fatigue result is absent from both checked summaries. The EMA assessment cites Park in its trial description and spells the name Parker in the later conclusion. Treat the meeting abstract and journal report as a likely same-cohort report, pending methods comparison.
