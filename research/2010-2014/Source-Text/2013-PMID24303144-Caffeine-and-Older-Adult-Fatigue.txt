Title: Assessment of caffeine supplementation, mood and muscular strength in older adults
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3835022/
PMID: 24303144
PMCID: PMC3835022
DOI: 10.1002/phy2.72
Publication date: 2013-08-29
Date basis: NLM electronic date.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Complete original PMC structured article, Methods and individual mood and exertion results.

Twelve healthy older adults completed a double-blind randomized crossover of isolated caffeine and placebo. The Brunel Mood State Inventory recorded a distinct fatigue subscale. The authors found a gain in vigor (p=.009) but no clear substance-by-time difference on fatigue. Separate ratings of perceived exertion were inconclusive after repeated knee extensions. Vigor, subjective fatigue and exertion are separate measures. The sample is small and the controlled caffeine exposure does not represent ordinary brewed coffee or Parkinson fatigue.
