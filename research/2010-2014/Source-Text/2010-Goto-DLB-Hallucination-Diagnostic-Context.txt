Visual hallucination prevalence and Parkinson sign criteria
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7964226/

January 14, 2010 online. Goto and colleagues report visual hallucinations in 16 of 19 selected dementia-with-Lewy-bodies patients and no reports among the Alzheimer comparators. Thirteen of 19 had parkinsonism by criteria including rest tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia or impaired postural reflexes. A patient could meet the motor criterion with any one of these findings. The study reports imaging discrimination and gives no episode counts, isolated tremor score or fig exposure.
