Salmon meals and daytime functioning
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4013386/
selected methods and findings paraphrase

Published May 2014. Ninety-five forensic inpatients were assigned to salmon or meat meals three times weekly. The trial examined nocturnal sleep and a daytime functioning rating. It did not use an Epworth daytime sleepiness outcome or test trout. This is an outcome-specific reread of the original already listed in the period catalog.
