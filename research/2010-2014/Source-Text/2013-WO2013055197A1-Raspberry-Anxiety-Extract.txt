Raspberry powder and reported anxiety during smoking cessation
Primary source: https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2013055197A1/es
Coverage: Factual extract of original patent application, numbered examples 10 and 18–23.

The April 2013 international patent application reports two adults starting in its mild-anxiety category and in its no-anxiety category at the end of a 14-day raspberry-powder regimen. Other very small groups received zeolite, combination products or varenicline. The record describes cessation counseling and assessments timed around quitting, without a randomized, blinded control, raw anxiety scores or Parkinson population. The powder is prepared from dried juice. This is an uncontrolled self-reporting lead with substantial co-intervention and time confounding.
