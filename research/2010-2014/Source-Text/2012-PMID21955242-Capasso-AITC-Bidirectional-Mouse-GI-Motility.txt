Title: Modulation of mouse gastrointestinal motility by allyl isothiocyanate, a constituent of cruciferous vegetables (Brassicaceae): evidence for TRPA1-independent effects
PMID: 21955242
PMCID: PMC3372844
DOI: 10.1111/j.1476-5381.2011.01703.x
First published: 2012
Authors: Capasso R, Aviello G, Romano B, Borrelli F, De Petrocellis L, Di Marzo V
Journal: British Journal of Pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21955242/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Preclinical mouse in vivo and isolated-gut study demonstrating bidirectional, route- and region-dependent GI motility effects of isolated allyl isothiocyanate (AITC): inhibited isolated ileal contractions and slowed upper GI transit after i.p. dosing, while contracting isolated colon and accelerating upper GI transit after intragastric dosing (TRPA1-antagonist insensitive). Preclinical isolated-constituent non-PD study with mixed GI direction.
