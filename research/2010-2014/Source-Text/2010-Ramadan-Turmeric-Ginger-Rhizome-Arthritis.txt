Ramadan and colleagues compared orally administered turmeric and ginger rhizome powders in a rat arthritis model. Both reduced paw oedema and several inflammatory measures relative to the arthritis comparison. Turmeric generally performed better for paw oedema in the tested conditions, with larger differences when treatment started earlier. The paper also measured body weight, immune markers and oxidative measures.

Figure 1 and the results describe the swelling outcome. The paper defines its recovery percentage using hind-paw oedema. Pain is discussed as an arthritis concern, but the experimental result is swelling rather than a pain score or pain behavior measure. Neither preparation was studied in a Parkinson pain model or clinical sample. The ginger preparation was powder rather than grated rhizome.
