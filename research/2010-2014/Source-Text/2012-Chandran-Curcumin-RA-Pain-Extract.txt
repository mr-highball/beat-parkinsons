Chandran and Goel, first published online March 9, 2012. PMID 22407780. Primary full report Table 5 and Methods checked. Randomized pilot of three active arms in rheumatoid arthritis enrolled 45 and analyzed 38 completers. Pain VAS means fell from 68.57 to 27.50 with BCM-95 curcuminoids (n=14), 77.25 to 34.29 with curcuminoids and diclofenac (n=12), and 78.25 to 39.17 with diclofenac (n=12). The reported significance tests are within arm. The study does not estimate the effect of ground turmeric on Parkinson-related pain.
