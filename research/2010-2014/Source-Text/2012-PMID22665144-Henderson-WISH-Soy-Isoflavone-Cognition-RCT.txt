Title: Long-term soy isoflavone supplementation and cognition in women: a randomized, controlled trial.
PMID: 22665144
PMCID: PMC3369523
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0b013e318258f822
First published: 2012
Authors: Henderson VW, St John JA, Hodis HN, Kono N, McCleary CA, Franke AA
Journal: Neurology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22665144/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Women's Isoflavone Soy Health (WISH) Class I double-blind, placebo-controlled randomized trial (PMC3369523) in 350 healthy postmenopausal women (aged 45-92 y; 313 in ITT cognitive analysis, ~90% adherence) randomized to 25 g/day isoflavone-rich soy protein (91 mg/d aglycone isoflavones: 52 mg genistein, 36 mg daidzein, 3 mg glycitein) vs milk-protein-matched placebo for 2.5 years. Primary endpoint (global cognition composite of 14 neuropsychological tests): NULL between groups (mean standardized difference +0.11, 95% CI -0.13 to 0.35). Secondary factors: modest visual memory improvement (+0.33 SD, 95% CI 0.06-0.60), but NULL on all 3 other cognitive factors (including executive/frontal function and attention/working memory) and individual tests. Directly opposes extrapolating soy isoflavone cognition claims to cooked edamame in PD.
