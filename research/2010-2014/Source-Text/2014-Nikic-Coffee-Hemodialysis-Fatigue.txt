Public source https://europepmc.org/articles/PMC4026941
PMID 24895603, PMC4026941, DOI 10.1155/2014/707460

First published 2014-05-05. Habitual Coffee Consumption Enhances Attention and Vigilance in Hemodialysis Patients. Methods classified habitual black coffee use and estimated caffeine per typical brewed cup. Table 2 reports FACIT fatigue means and standard deviations for 67 coffee users and 19 nonusers. The study was cross-sectional in hemodialysis patients and did not assign coffee. Data above use the original Table 2 group labels.

Nikić and colleagues studied 86 adults on hemodialysis in Serbia. Sixty-seven reported habitual black coffee and 19 reported no coffee use. Table 2 reports mean FACIT fatigue scores of 30.1 (SD 10.2) and 29.2 (SD 11.6), respectively, p=.745. Higher scores indicate less fatigue. Coffee intake was self-reported. The cross-sectional comparison leaves confounding and the effect of brewed coffee in Parkinson’s unresolved.
