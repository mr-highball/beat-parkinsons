Title: Severe Myoclonic Epilepsy in Infancy – Adult Phenotype with Bradykinesia, Hypomimia, and Perseverative Behavior: Report of Five Cases
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3214946/

Publication date: 2011-03-26
Date basis: NLM electronic date and PMC metadata; provider assigns 2010
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML main body
Evidence type: Adult Dravet syndrome phenotype series
Format: Factual digest of the original publication

In five adults with a history of Dravet syndrome, motor slowness, hypomimia and perseveration resembled features seen in other disorders. One narrative describes recurring hallucinatory symptoms in adulthood and no indication of hallucinations during a later examination. The article does not classify those experiences by sensory modality or compare diagnosed Parkinson participants receiving dried figs.
