Title: Effects of a ketogenic diet on the quality of life in 16 patients with advanced cancer: A pilot trial
PMID: 21794124
PMCID: PMC3157418
DOI: 10.1186/1743-7075-8-54
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3157418/
First published: 2011-07-27.
Coverage: Factual original Methods, Table 3 and Results/Figure 4 narrative extraction; graph heights not digitized.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Sixteen advanced-cancer patients entered an uncontrolled ketogenic-diet pilot; five completed. The offered shake contains flaxseed, canola, walnut, MCT, grape-seed, argan and pumpkin-seed oils. Individual walnut-oil amount, processing and intake are not established.

EORTC QLQ-C30 symptom trajectories are reported for seven evaluable patients. Pain and fatigue increased, whereas insomnia improved. The authors discuss progression of advanced cancer; five patients progressed and two died during the study. Digestive symptoms and emotional functioning also varied. Neither the recipe nor an uncontrolled trajectory isolates walnut oil or another ingredient.

This preserves genuine human food-plus-symptom context, including an unfavorable pain trajectory, without assigning causation. It is not a Parkinson comparison or an independent trial of each listed oil. A suggested menu and actual individual exposure remain distinct.
