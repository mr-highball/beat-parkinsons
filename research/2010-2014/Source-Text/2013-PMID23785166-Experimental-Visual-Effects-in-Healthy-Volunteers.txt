Title: Activation of serotonin 2A receptors underlies the psilocybin-induced effects on α oscillations, N170 visual-evoked potentials, and visual hallucinations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6618596/
PMID: 23785166
PMCID: PMC6618596
DOI: 10.1523/jneurosci.3007-12.2013
Publication year: 2013
Text coverage: Paraphrased findings checked against complete official PMC HTML.

Fifteen healthy volunteers were analyzed in a randomized double-blind within-person comparison of pharmacologically induced visual experiences and electrophysiologic measures. Receptor antagonism altered the induced effects in that setting. The study does not test dried fig, typical Parkinson symptoms, or clinical visual episode rates.

Seventeen healthy volunteers enrolled and fifteen were included in the statistical analysis. Changes in the electrophysiologic measures are reported for the acute experimental sessions. The comparison uses a pharmacologic challenge and concerns induced experiences.
