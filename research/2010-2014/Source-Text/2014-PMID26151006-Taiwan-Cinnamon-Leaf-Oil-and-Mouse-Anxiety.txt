Original source scope
The Taiwanese Cinnamomum osmophloeum linalool chemotype supplied hydrodistilled leaf oil. Oral oil and isolated linalool conditions increased mouse open-arm time compared with corn-oil controls. Open-field movement did not differ significantly.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The tested species, plant part, extraction and doses differ from ground culinary cinnamon. The mice were not a Parkinson population. Botanical presence and human customary oral exposure need independent characterization.
