Title: [Apparent life-threatening event in infants: think about star anise intoxication!].
PMID: 21652187
DOI: 10.1016/j.arcped.2011.03.024
First published: 2011
Authors: Perret C, Tabin R, Marcoz JP, Llor J, Cheseaux JJ
Journal: Archives de pediatrie : organe officiel de la Societe francaise de pediatrie
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21652187/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Clinical toxicology case report (Perret et al., 2011, Arch Pediatr) of a 2-month-old infant presenting with central cyanosis, limb tetany, opisthotonos, horizontal nystagmus, extensor hypertonia, and seizure-like events after ingesting three 200 mL bottles of star anise colic infusion (4-5 fruits/bottle). LC-MS confirmed urinary anisatin 22 hours post-ingestion and measured 7,800 ug/kg anisatin in the star anise fruit (exceeding the 1,000 ug/kg Swiss limit), proving Illicium anisatum contamination — explicitly noting ground/broken fruits make visual distinction impossible.
