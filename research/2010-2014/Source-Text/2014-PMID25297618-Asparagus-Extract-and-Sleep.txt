Title: Effects of enzyme-treated asparagus extract on heat shock protein 70, stress indices, and sleep in healthy adult men
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25297618/
Original: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnsv/60/4/60_283/_pdf
PMID: 25297618
DOI: 10.3177/jnsv.60.283
Publication year: 2014
Coverage: Author abstract and original publisher report.

Two double-blind comparisons used an enzyme-treated asparagus extract in healthy men. One comparison evaluated autonomic and heat-shock-protein measures. The second was a crossover with hormonal and sleep measures. Total sleep time did not differ significantly overall. Physical fatigue was explicitly among the visual analog questionnaire items. The result section identifies appetite as the significant visual analog outcome and says the remaining items were inconclusive. Separate sleep subgroups had different results. No numerical physical-fatigue contrast or roasted-asparagus comparison in Parkinson disease is reported.
