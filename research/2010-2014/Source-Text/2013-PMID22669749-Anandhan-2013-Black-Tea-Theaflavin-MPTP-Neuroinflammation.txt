Title: Therapeutic attenuation of neuroinflammation and apoptosis by black tea theaflavin in chronic MPTP/probenecid model of Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 22669749
DOI: 10.1007/s12640-012-9332-9
First published: 2013
Authors: Anandhan A, Essa MM, Manivasagam T
Journal: Neurotoxicity research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22669749/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Non-retracted chronic MPTP/probenecid mouse PD model (male C57BL/6 mice; 35 days; oral theaflavin 10 mg/kg 1 h before MPTP/p) reporting reduced nigral neuroinflammation (IL-1beta, IL-6, TNF-alpha, GFAP), reduced apoptosis (Bax/Bcl-2), and improved catalepsy and akinesia behavioral tests. Limits: preclinical mouse model of isolated theaflavin rather than brewed black tea in human PD; distinct from retracted PMID:22634505 which is excluded.
