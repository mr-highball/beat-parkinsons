The review summarizes anticoagulation interactions for ginger products and distinct caffeine or enzyme contexts for coffee and green tea. Chicken liver appears among examples of high-tyramine products. A paragraph links an orange-juice and aluminum-antacid combination to severe constipation without a corresponding controlled orange-fruit exposure in the review. The potato paragraph concerns solanine in unripe or green potatoes. Raspberry appears in dietary oxalate guidance. No sweet potato exposure was identified. These are product, formulation, and co-exposure leads without a measured comparison for the frozen clinical endpoints.
