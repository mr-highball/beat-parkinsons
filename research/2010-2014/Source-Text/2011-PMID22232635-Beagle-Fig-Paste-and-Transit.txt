Title: Effects of Ficus carica paste on constipation induced by a high-protein feed and movement restriction in beagles
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3251757/
PMID: 22232635
PMCID: PMC3251757
DOI: 10.5625/lar.2011.27.4.275
Publication date: 2011-12-19, first electronic publication.
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings from complete PMC JATS.

Fifteen dogs passed through successive baseline, induced-constipation and fig-paste periods. The induced conditions included a high-protein diet and movement restriction and were retained during the later paste period. During the paste period the study reports higher fecal weight and shorter measured colonic transit. The design uses the same animals in fixed sequential conditions, with no concurrent treatment-free group in the paste period.

The study concerns bowel outcomes under an experimental animal condition. It is linked as a precursor to the controlled 2016 human fig-paste publication. Neither the route and material nor clinical endpoints establish an effect of whole dried figs on Parkinson visual symptoms.

The Results subsection includes one inconsistent description of which period was shortest. The same subsection gives 18 hours with paste and 30 hours during induced constipation, and the Discussion identifies the baseline period as fastest. It reports a clearer left-colon comparison and no clear difference in the right or rectosigmoid segments. Preserve these measurement boundaries.
