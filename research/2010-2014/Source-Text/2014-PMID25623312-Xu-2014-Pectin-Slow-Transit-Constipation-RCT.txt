Title: [Clinical benefits after soluble dietary fiber supplementation: a randomized clinical trial in adults with slow-transit constipation].
PMID: 25623312
First published: 2014
Authors: Xu L, Yu W, Jiang J, Li N
Journal: Zhonghua yi xue za zhi
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25623312/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized placebo-controlled trial in n=80 adults with slow-transit constipation comparing 24 g/d soluble dietary pectin vs maltodextrin placebo for 4 weeks. Pectin significantly shortened colonic transit time (60.2 +/- 11.2 h vs 79.4 +/- 11.7 h placebo and 80.3 +/- 9.5 h baseline, p<0.01), reduced clinical constipation symptom scores vs baseline and placebo (both p<0.05), increased fecal Bifidobacterium and Lactobacillus (p<0.05), and reduced total Clostridium (p<0.05). Limits: non-PD adults; PubMed abstract does not specify whether pectin was derived from apple or citrus, so treated as constituent context only.
