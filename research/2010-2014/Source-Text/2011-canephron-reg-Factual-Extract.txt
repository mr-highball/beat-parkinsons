Single-arm Canephron urinary symptom trial registration
2011-11-23 (first public posting)
https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01478620

ClinicalTrials.gov identifies this as a 125-participant, open-label, single-arm study of oral Canephron N for uncomplicated urinary tract infection. Results first posted October 13, 2014. The official API reports 0 of 125 with investigator-attributed adverse drug reactions and marks secondary urinary outcomes as NOT_POSTED. The separate Ivanov article supplies urgency severity results. Its preparation uses lovage root with other botanicals.
