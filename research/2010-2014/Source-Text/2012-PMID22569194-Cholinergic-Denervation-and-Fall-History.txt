Original source scope
This Parkinson imaging cohort compared 101 participants without dementia with 29 controls. Within Parkinson, low thalamic activity was associated with more fall history (10/18 versus 17/83 in the normal range, Table 3).

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The exposure is regional cholinergic denervation. The fall-history comparison contains no ground sage intervention and does not establish a food effect.
