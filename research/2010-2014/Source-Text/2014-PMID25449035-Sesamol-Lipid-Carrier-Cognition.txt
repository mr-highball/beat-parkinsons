Sachdeva and colleagues compared sesamol and a solid-lipid nanoparticle formulation in a rat model of cognitive impairment. The authors report favorable changes in maze performance, inflammatory markers and oxidative-stress measurements. The carrier comparison reportedly had stronger effects on some oxidative measures.

This is a separated ingredient constituent and a pharmaceutical formulation. The abstract has no cold-pressed or toasted sesame-oil arm, no Parkinson population, and no fall frequency or clinical rigidity measure. Treat the formulation study as a distinct line from sesame oil used only as a carrier component in other studies.
