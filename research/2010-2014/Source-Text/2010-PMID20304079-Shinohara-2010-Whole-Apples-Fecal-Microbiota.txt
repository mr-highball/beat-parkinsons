Title: Effect of apple intake on fecal microbiota and metabolites in humans.
PMID: 20304079
DOI: 10.1016/j.anaerobe.2010.03.005
First published: 2010
Authors: Shinohara K, Ohashi Y, Kawasumi K, Terada A, Fujisawa T
Journal: Anaerobe
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20304079/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Human feeding study in n=8 healthy adults consuming 2 whole apples/day for 2 weeks. Fecal Bifidobacterium increased significantly (p<0.01) with concurrent reductions in Lecithinase-positive clostridia including C. perfringens and fecal sulfide/ammonia. Limits: small uncontrolled sample in healthy volunteers; gut microbiota and fecal chemistry endpoints without clinical constipation symptom score.
