Title: Investigating the in vitro bioaccessibility of polyphenols in fresh and sun-dried figs (Ficus carica L.)
DOI: 10.1111/ijfs.12258
Source: https://ifst.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ijfs.12258
Publication year: 2013
Coverage: Primary publisher summary with cultivar, comparison and reported assay findings.

The experiment compares fresh and sun-dried Sarilop and Bursa siyahi figs using simulated gastrointestinal digestion. The response variables include antioxidant assay values, proanthocyanidins and named phenolic compounds in a dialysed fraction. Some measures increased after drying in yellow figs. The authors did not detect the examined anthocyanins in the dialysed dried-fig fraction. These assay findings vary by cultivar and analyte and do not establish blood, brain or clinical symptom exposure.
