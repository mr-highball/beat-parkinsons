Searles Nielsen and colleagues added a grouped dietary
analysis from the same case-control study reported in 2013
(PMID 23661325, DOI 10.1002/ana.23884). The pooled cucumber,
cantaloupe, zucchini and yellow winter squash frequency had
an adjusted relative risk of 1.00 (95% CI 0.72–1.38) per
additional daily consumption frequency, as presented by
the authors. The combined measure includes covariate
adjustment for diet, demographics, tobacco and caffeine.

This is a disease-occurrence comparison. It gives no
cantaloupe-specific, raw preparation or rest-tremor estimate.
The reply is part of the same study family as the 2013 paper.
