Title: Anti-inflammatory activity of ethanol extract derived from Phaseolus angularis beans.
PMID: 21821108
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2011.07.048
First published: 2011
Authors: Yu T, Ahn HM, Shen T, Yoon K, Jang HJ, Lee YJ, et al.
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21821108/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical macrophage and mouse gastritis study (Yu et al., 2011, J Ethnopharmacol) evaluating ethanol extract of Phaseolus angularis (adzuki bean). Extract suppressed NO and PGE2 release, iNOS/COX-2 expression, NF-kappaB/AP-1/CREB, and Syk/p38/TAK1 signaling in TLR-activated macrophages, and orally ameliorated EtOH/HCl-induced gastric lesions in mice. Also documents East Asian (Korea, China, Japan) ethnopharmacological use for edema and inflammation of joints, appendix, kidney, and bladder. Limits: no behavioral nociceptive or pain severity assay, no cooked whole-bean arm, and no Parkinson's model.
