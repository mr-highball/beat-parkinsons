Title: Intake of whole apples or clear apple juice has contrasting effects on plasma lipids in healthy volunteers.
PMID: 23271615
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-012-0489-z
First published: 2013
Authors: Ravn-Haren G, Dragsted LO, Buch-Andersen T, Jensen EN, Jensen RI, Németh-Balogh M et al.
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23271615/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Five-period 4-week dietary crossover trial in n=23 healthy volunteers comparing whole apples (550 g/d), apple pomace (22 g/d), cloudy apple juice (500 mL/d), clear apple juice (500 mL/d), and no supplement. Whole apple and pomace tended to lower LDL cholesterol whereas clear juice raised LDL; blood pressure did not differ across treatments, and no PD symptom endpoints were collected.
