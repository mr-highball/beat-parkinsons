2012 spearmint gum and measured six-minute saliva production
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3312213/
PMID: 22505903
PMCID: PMC3312213
DOI: 10.1155/2012/569327
Coverage: Original full text, Methods and Table 2

In the 2012 Karami Nogourani report, mean salivary flow for the spearmint-flavored gum condition rose from 0.85 to 4.03 mL/min, a reported paired difference of 3.18 mL/min with 95 percent confidence interval 2.65 to 3.70. The reported six-minute comparison among flavors had p = .091. The study did not include an unflavored-gum condition. Its later spearmint peak differs from the 2011 description, while its team and design may overlap the earlier report. The source gives no authenticated spearmint leaf exposure or clinical drooling count.
