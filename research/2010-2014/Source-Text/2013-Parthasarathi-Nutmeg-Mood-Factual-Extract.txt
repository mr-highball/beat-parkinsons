Title: Out of the cupboard and into the clinic: Nutmeg-induced mood disorder
Source: https://www.mdedge.com/psychiatry/article/78788/addiction-medicine/out-cupboard-and-clinic-nutmeg-induced-mood-disorder
First published: December 2013
Coverage: Factual extract from the original case report.

A 50-year-old man described severe dysphoria, hopelessness and psychotic symptoms in recurrent episodes temporally associated with deliberate high-dose nutmeg misuse. The authors reconsidered a prior bipolar diagnosis and attributed the recent presentation to a substance-induced mood disorder. Symptoms reportedly resolved within three days. The exposure was self-reported and the report provides no ordinary dietary comparator, processing detail, symptom scale or Parkinson cohort.
