Title: Effects of five Ayurvedic herbs on locomotor behaviour in a Drosophila melanogaster Parkinson's disease model
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4310928/
PMCID: PMC4310928
PMID: 25091506
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.5199
Publication date: 2014-08-04
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary model experiment
Format: Factual digest of the original material

PINK1-mutant and wild-type fruit flies received individual botanical preparations, a five-herb combination, levodopa or comparison feed. The preparations were a commercial Mucuna pruriens powder, Withania somnifera, Centella asiatica, Sida cordifolia and Bacopa monnieri. Bacopa improved mutant climbing but worsened wild-type climbing. Centella also reduced wild-type climbing, and the other individual herbs yielded no significant change versus untreated flies in the reported comparisons. Neither the tested preparations nor the climbing endpoint matches dried figs and clinical visual hallucination frequency.
