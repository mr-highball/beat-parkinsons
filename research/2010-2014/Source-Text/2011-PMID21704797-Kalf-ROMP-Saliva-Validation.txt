Title: Reproducibility and validity of patient-rated assessment of speech, swallowing, and saliva control in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21704797/
PMID: 21704797
DOI: 10.1016/j.apmr.2011.02.011
Publication date: 2011-07
Coverage: Paraphrased original author abstract.

This instrument-development study enrolled 129 people with Parkinson disease and 49 with atypical parkinsonism. Sixty completed a repeat assessment and another 118 were independently evaluated by clinicians blinded to the patient ratings. The abstract reports three domains for speech, swallowing and saliva control, with subscale repeatability intraclass correlations of .83 to .92. The range covers all three domains. The abstract alone does not identify a saliva-only coefficient. ROMP–Saliva preserves perceived saliva problems as a measure separate from measured salivary flow and spontaneous swallow counts. No food was assigned.
