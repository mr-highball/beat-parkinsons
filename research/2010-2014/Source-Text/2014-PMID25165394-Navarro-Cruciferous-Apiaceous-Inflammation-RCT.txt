Title: Cruciferous vegetables have variable effects on biomarkers of systemic inflammation in a randomized controlled trial in healthy young adults.
PMID: 25165394
PMCID: PMC4195422
DOI: 10.3945/jn.114.197434
First published: 2014
Authors: Navarro SL, Schwarz Y, Song X, Wang CY, Chen C, Trudo SP et al.
Journal: J Nutr
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25165394/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized 4-period crossover controlled feeding trial in 63 healthy young adults (32 men, 31 women) comparing basal vegetable-free diet, 1x cruciferous (7 g/kg), 2x cruciferous (14 g/kg), and cruciferous+apiaceous (7+4 g/kg) diets for 14 d each. IL-6 fell 19-20% on 2xC and 1xC+A vs basal, while IL-8 rose 16% on 1xC+A and CRP/TNF-alpha/sTNFRI-II were null overall. Bundled multi-vegetable diets measuring serum cytokines in young healthy adults; no isolated cauliflower or clinical PD symptoms.
