Wood discusses dyskinesia in the comparison of dopamine receptor agonist treatment with levodopa. The author separately identifies ginger, peppermint and chamomile as complementary approaches discussed for agonist-related nausea. The publisher's bibliography links ginger to antiemetic evidence. The accessible material provides no ground ginger intervention with a dyskinesia outcome. The recommendation and the drug-associated motor outcome should remain separate.
