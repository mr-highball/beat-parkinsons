Title: Gastrointestinal Tolerance to Daily Canned Chickpea Intake.
PMID: 26067078
DOI: 10.3148/cjdpr-2014-018
First published: 2014
Authors: Dahl WJ, Hanifi A, Zello GA, Tyler RT
Journal: Can J Diet Pract Res
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26067078/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized crossover tolerance trial (Dahl et al., 2014, Can J Diet Pract Res; same n=12 healthy young adult cohort as microbiota report Fernando et al. 2010 PMID:21831757) comparing 3 weeks each of 200 g/day canned chickpeas (providing ~11 g/day dietary fiber), 5 g/day raffinose, and control diet. Daily stool frequency did not change significantly on canned chickpeas (1.7 +/- 0.3 vs 1.5 +/- 0.3/day; baseline already normal). Mild flatulence (1.0 +/- 0.2 vs 0.4 +/- 0.1, p<0.001) and mild bloating (0.2 +/- 0.1 vs 0.0, p<0.001) increased slightly, whereas diarrhea, abdominal pain, and reflux did not differ. Limits: very small sample (n=12), healthy non-constipated adults, short 3-week phase.
