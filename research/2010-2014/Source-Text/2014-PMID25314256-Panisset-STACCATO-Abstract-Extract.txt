Title: Serotonin Toxicity Association with Concomitant Antidepressants and Rasagiline Treatment: Retrospective Study (STACCATO)
PMID: 25314256
DOI: 10.1002/phar.1500
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25314256/
Publisher: https://accpjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/phar.1500
First published: 2014-10-14 online. Pharmacotherapy 34(12):1250–1258.
Coverage: Original author abstract, NLM investigator roster and publisher metadata. Journal body and cohort flow remain open.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18 UTC

The retrospective multicenter phase IV study compared Parkinson patient records for rasagiline with antidepressants, rasagiline without antidepressants, and antidepressants plus other dopaminergic medications. Adjudicators applied the Hunter criteria to recorded events. No case of serotonin toxicity was identified by this review of medical records. The abstract's overall count of 1,504 differs from the sum of its reported group counts (471 + 511 + 525 = 1,507). The paper body is needed to reconcile the analysis populations and whether there is overlap or a reporting error.

Melissa appears as a given name in the original study group's roster. The abstract evaluates drug groups and serotonin toxicity. It provides no outcome for brewed lemon balm or depressive symptom improvement. Zero observed cases in this cohort does not establish safety for an unstudied drug or herbal combination.
