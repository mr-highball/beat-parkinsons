Title: Topical Trikatu versus an active topical comparator for mosquito-bite symptoms
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24559814/
PMID: 24559814
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2013.08.014
Publication date: 2013-09-01
Coverage: Original author abstract, randomized within-person design, intervention ingredients and results.

Thirty healthy adults received a topical blend of black pepper, another Piper species and ginger on one arm and a camphor, menthol and eucalyptus reference product on the other. Pain was a secondary outcome among localized bite symptoms. The authors found no statistically clear differences at the assessed time points. The comparison is for two topical preparations and does not isolate black pepper fruit or a Parkinson outcome.
