Claim 0025 | PMID:28156288 | The full book uses sage for publisher citations or editorial praise. No Salvia preparation is linked to clinical pain, fatigue, falls or apathy.
Claim 0121 | PMID:28156288 | The Greenland smoking ethnography on PDF pp. 233–237 describes coffee during smoking routines. The energy-drink survey on pp. 196–201 explicitly separates energy drinks from coffee. Neither member assigns brewed coffee or measures Parkinson fatigue.
Claim 0133 | PMID:28156288 | No lemon balm, Melissa officinalis or balm mint exposure is reported anywhere in the original proceedings. Mood and coping are covered independently.
Claim 0138 | PMID:28156288 | PDF p. 820 lists cod among sources of vitamin D in a Greenland review. PDF p. 845 refers to Pacific cod in a regional habitat description. Neither provides a grilled-cod comparison or a fatigue outcome.
Claim 0167 | PMID:28156288 | The wild berry paper on PDF p. 799 cites animal cognition and memory research using blackberry extract. The local ingredient preparation is a cited extract; no frozen blackberry pain result is recorded.
Claim 0259 | PMID:28156288 | The childhood allergy paper on PDF p. 305 groups citrus as a skin sensitization category and relates it to attack histories. It leaves the member's specific citrus species and the role of raw orange unresolved. Different pages use orange in an idiom or a product name. There is no fall-frequency result.
Claim 0329 | PMID:28156288 | The childhood allergy paper on PDF pp. 303–306 distinguishes hen egg during allergen testing and reported respiratory exacerbation. The Swedish food cohort on pp. 423–429 aggregates egg intake with dietary and lipid analyses. Neither member tests poached egg or Parkinson falls.

Claim 0493 | PMID:28156288 | The cross-project Alaska Native coping analysis on PDF pp. 393–394 reports raspberry gathering among several practices and foods.
