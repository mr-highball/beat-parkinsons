Title: Effect of legumes as part of a low glycemic index diet on glycemic control and cardiovascular risk factors in type 2 diabetes mellitus: a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 23089999
DOI: 10.1001/2013.jamainternmed.70
First published: 2012
Authors: Jenkins DJ, Kendall CW, Augustin LS, Mitchell S, Sahye-Pudaruth S, Blanco Mejia S et al.
Journal: Arch Intern Med
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23089999/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled trial (Jenkins et al., 2012, Arch Intern Med, n=121 adults with type 2 diabetes) assigning >=1 cup/day mixed cooked legumes (beans, chickpeas, lentils) vs high-wheat-fiber diet for 3 months. Seated systolic blood pressure fell by -4.5 mmHg (p<0.001) and diastolic BP by -3.1 mmHg (p<0.001) vs wheat-fiber control. Critical limits: bundled multi-legume dietary pattern (not isolated roasted chickpeas), seated resting BP in T2DM rather than orthostatic standing BP or lightheadedness in Parkinson disease.
