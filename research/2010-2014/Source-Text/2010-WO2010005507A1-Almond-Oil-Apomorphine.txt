Patent WO2010005507A1, first published 2010-01-14.
Public source https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2010005507A1/en

The patent describes six people with Parkinson’s receiving topical apomorphine. It names bitter-almond kernel oil among the carrier ingredients, with processing unspecified. The example table reports total motor scores, although its illustrative UPDRS form includes voice and facial expression items. A separate example gives qualitative observations of speech and facial expression. It leaves the relationship to the six cases and an interpretable comparison unresolved. The later US20200030228A1 application repeats the six case examples. This formulation provides clinical context for the ingredient but leaves the effect of oral cold-pressed oil unanswered.
