Authors: Sinisalo M, Enkovaara AL, Kivistö KT

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Published clinical case report (Eur J Clin Pharmacol 2010; 66(4):427-428; doi:10.1007/s00228-009-0776-7; note verified PMID is 20072844) documenting unexplained liver enzyme elevation / possible hepatotoxicity temporally linked to heavy rooibos tea consumption in a patient with low-grade B-cell malignancy, normalizing after discontinuing rooibos. Systemic hepatic adverse-event caution; does not document direct worsening of Parkinson motor or non-motor symptoms.
