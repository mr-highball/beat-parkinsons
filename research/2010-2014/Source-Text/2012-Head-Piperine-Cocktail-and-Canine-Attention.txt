Piperine-containing cocktail and canine spatial attention
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4006672/

Head and colleagues, 2012. Eighteen aged beagles were assigned in balanced groups of nine. The treatment provided two daily capsules containing black pepper extract standardized to 95% piperine with green tea extract, curcumin, N-acetylcysteine and R-lipoic acid. Each capsule used 17 mg of the standardized pepper extract. Seven cocktail dogs and six placebo dogs completed variable-distance landmark testing. Total errors favored the cocktail (t(11) = 4.34, p = .001), as did reported accuracy across distances (F(1,9) = 20.7, p = .001). There was no treatment arm isolating pepper. This canine aging experiment did not enroll human Parkinson patients or test cracked culinary pepper.
