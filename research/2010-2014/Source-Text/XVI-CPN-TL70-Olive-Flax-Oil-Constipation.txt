Early Brazilian oil and constipation conference comparison
Source: https://sonesp.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Resumos-XVI-CPN-2011.pdf#page=44
Coverage: Original 2011 congress TL-70, official abstract book printed page 44

Grilli, Lima and Cuppari presented a four-week randomized comparison in hemodialysis. Of 47 people found constipated, 33 began and 29 completed, with nine mineral-oil, 11 olive-oil and nine flaxseed-oil completers. This report started at 8 mL/day with titration. Reported flaxseed-arm stool consistency changed from 1.56 to 2.06, p=.06. Hard or fragmented stools were reported less often in all groups. The date, starting dose and denominators differ from the later trial registry and journal article. The relationship between cohorts has not been established.
