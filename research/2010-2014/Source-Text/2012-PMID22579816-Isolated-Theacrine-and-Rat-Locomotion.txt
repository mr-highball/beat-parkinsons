Original source scope
Isolated injected theacrine increased activity at the larger of the tested systemic exposures in rats. The behavioral assays tested locomotor activation and receptor contributions. Repeated administration did not show locomotor sensitization or tolerance in that experiment. This is mechanistic context for 0948. The study does not measure human fatigue, assign Parkinson disease treatment or compare brewed green tea. PMID 33392238 reference 24.
