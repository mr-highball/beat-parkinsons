Title: Influence of vitamin D mushroom powder supplementation on exercise-induced muscle damage in vitamin D insufficient high school athletes.
PMID: 24117183
DOI: 10.1080/02640414.2013.847279
First published: 2014
Authors: Shanely RA, Nieman DC, Knab AM, Gillitt ND, Meaney MP, Jin F
Journal: Journal of sports sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24117183/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Six-week randomized placebo-controlled trial in vitamin D-insufficient high school athletes given UV-exposed Portobello (A. bisporus) mushroom powder (600 IU/d vitamin D2) vs placebo before eccentric exercise. Serum 25(OH)D2 rose 9.9-fold while 25(OH)D3 dropped 28%, with NO group differences in skeletal muscle function or exercise-induced muscle damage.
