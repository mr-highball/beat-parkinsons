May 2013 online and October 2013 issue. The complete final report for ISRCTN76517470 randomized 164 people. Table 2 reports a four-month fatigue-severity contrast favoring the program. The Secondary outcomes section reports no statistically significant arm difference in HADS or the SF-36 subscales other than Vitality at either follow-up. The original compares behavioral treatment in multiple sclerosis without rosemary, dates or Parkinson participants.
