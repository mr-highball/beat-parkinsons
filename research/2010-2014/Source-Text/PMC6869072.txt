Title: The role of dysfunctional attentional control networks in visual misperceptions in Parkinson's disease.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6869072/
PMID: 23760982
PMCID: PMC6869072
DOI: 10.1002/hbm.22321
Publication date: 2013-06-13
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC complete article HTML.

The 22-person Parkinson study uses a bistable percept task and several MRI measures to investigate visual misperceptions. The task errors differ from counts of spontaneous visual hallucinations. Figure references provide no edible-fig exposure.
