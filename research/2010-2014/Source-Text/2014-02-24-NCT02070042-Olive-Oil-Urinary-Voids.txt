Extra virgin olive oil capsules as an overactive bladder comparator
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02070042
Registration: NCT02070042
Coverage: Official registry design, interventions and results status

The February 24, 2014 first-posted registry compares omega-3 with extra-virgin olive oil placebo capsules in women with overactive bladder. Both groups were assigned oxybutynin. The main outcome was the change in daily void count at three and six weeks. The registry lists terminated for lack of participation, 31 actual enrollments, and no posted results when checked November 16, 2026. The trial neither assigned oil against an oil-free control under otherwise identical treatment nor supplied a Parkinson-specific result.
