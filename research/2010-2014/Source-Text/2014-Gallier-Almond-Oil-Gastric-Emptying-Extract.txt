Factual extract of the primary abstract, Gallier et al., Food & Function 2014;5:2410–2419.
PMID 25066699. DOI 10.1039/c4fo00335g.
Growing rats were given preparations of almond lipids. Magnetic resonance spectroscopy followed gastric emptying of a marker. Reported half-emptying times were 172 ± 11 minutes for almond oil, 194 ± 17 for crushed whole almonds and 157 ± 9 for almond milk. The oil value was described as intermediate among the tested preparations. The abstract does not establish oil pressing grade, a daily OFF outcome, or a drug exposure contrast.
Full text and complete oil preparation methods were not verified in this retrieval.
