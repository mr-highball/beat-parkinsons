The original author abstract reports motor and cognitive deficits in a rat Parkinson model and improvement with isolated oral lycopene. It gives more detailed biochemical measurements than behavioral results. The inspected publisher page provides no complete behavioral methods or Figure-level group estimates. Distinct from the Liu mouse report. The behavioral endpoints and rigidity specificity remain open.

Claim 0544 asks about cooked tomato and Parkinson muscle rigidity. This report contributes isolated-constituent and animal outcome context.
