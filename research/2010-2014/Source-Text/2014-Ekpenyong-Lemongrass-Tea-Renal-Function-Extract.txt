Title: Effect of lemongrass tea consumption on estimated glomerular filtration rate and creatinine clearance rate
Publication: Ekpenyong, Daniel and Antai, online October 16, 2014; January 2015 issue
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25440110/
Coverage: factual extract of the accessible primary source

The primary report gives 105 healthy adults (55 men and 50 women), randomized across three dose groups taking 2, 4 or 8 g leaf powder infusion daily for 30 days. A separate no-tea group is absent. Serum and urine indices were compared with baseline on days 10 and 30. Estimated filtration and creatinine clearance fell by day 30 in all three dose groups. Participants, sex counts, ages, timing and doses closely match the companion diuresis report. Filtration estimates do not measure urinary urgency.
