Primary abstract summary. The authors report a blinded 15-week comparison for osteoarthritis with problems completing WOMAC forms. Original pain and comparator methods tables remain inaccessible through the checked publisher route. The canola oil comparator and inconclusive COAT pain result are described in the source-level extraction, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4494689/. This comparison does not isolate effects of the oil against an inactive group.
