Original study scope
This animal study compares individual curcuminoids after a Parkinson-related lesion. Behavioral and tissue outcomes include spontaneous locomotor counts and oxidative markers. Curcumin and demethoxycurcumin improved the reported locomotor measure, while bisdemethoxycurcumin did not show that improvement.

Continuing evidence gaps
The isolated compounds and broad locomotor proxy leave ground or grated turmeric, clinical bradykinesia or rigidity, and Parkinson fall frequency open.
