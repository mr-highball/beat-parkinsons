Title: Does grape seed oil improve inflammation and insulin resistance in overweight or obese women?
PMID: 23506314
DOI: 10.3109/09637486.2013.775228
First published: 2013
Authors: Irandoost P, Ebrahimi-Mameghani M, Pirouzpanah S
Journal: International journal of food sciences and nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23506314/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized 8-week parallel trial in 44 overweight/obese women comparing 15% energy from culinary grape seed oil vs sunflower oil on a weight-loss diet. Grape seed oil lowered hs-CRP compared with sunflower oil (p<0.03) and reduced within-group HOMA-IR and TNF-alpha. Assessed strictly metabolic/inflammatory blood biomarkers; ZERO Parkinson, cognitive, sleep, pain, fatigue, autonomic, or motor symptoms measured.
