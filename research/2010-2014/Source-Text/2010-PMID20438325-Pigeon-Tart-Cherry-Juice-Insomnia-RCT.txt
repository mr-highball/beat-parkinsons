Title: Effects of a tart cherry juice beverage on the sleep of older adults with insomnia: a pilot study.
PMID: 20438325
PMCID: PMC3133468
DOI: 10.1089/jmf.2009.0096
First published: 2010
Authors: Pigeon WR, Carr M, Gorman C, Perlis ML
Journal: Journal of medicinal food
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3133468/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled crossover pilot trial (Pigeon et al., 2010, J Med Food, PMC3133468) in 15 otherwise healthy older adults with chronic insomnia comparing 2 weeks of a proprietary Montmorency tart cherry and apple juice blend (CherryPharm, 8 oz twice daily) vs placebo beverage with a 2-week washout. Compared with placebo, the tart cherry beverage produced statistically significant reductions in Insomnia Severity Index (ISI) severity and minutes awake after sleep onset (WASO; 62-minute improvement noted in Losso et al. 2018), whereas sleep latency, total sleep time, and sleep efficiency did not differ significantly vs placebo; secondary MFI fatigue scores were inconclusive. Effect sizes were modest to negligible and smaller than standard CBT-I or hypnotics. Limits: small sample (n=15), proprietary tart cherry-apple blend rather than whole dried or raw cherries, older adults without diagnosed Parkinson disease.
