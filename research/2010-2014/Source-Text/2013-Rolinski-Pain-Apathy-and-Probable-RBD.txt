Pain and apathy by probable REM sleep behavior disorder status
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3995329/

First online November 2013. Of 475 people with early Parkinson disease, the authors compared those with and without probable REM sleep behavior disorder. Table 3 reports pain in 76.8% without and 85.0% with probable RBD, and apathy in 13.5% without and 22.8% with probable RBD. These are baseline symptom-presence associations. The study does not assign food exposures.
