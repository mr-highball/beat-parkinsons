Title: Effects of a gliadin-combined plant superoxide dismutase extract on self-perceived fatigue in women aged 50-65 years.
PMID: 21044831
DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2010.09.006
First published: 2011
Authors: Houghton CA, Steels EL, Fassett RG, Coombes JS
Journal: Phytomedicine : international journal of phytotherapy and phytopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21044831/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in n=38 women (aged 50-65 y) with self-perceived fatigue comparing 500 mg/d gliadin-combined plant/melon superoxide dismutase (SOD) extract vs placebo. Found NO within- or between-group differences (all p>0.05) in Multidimensional Fatigue Inventory (MFI) general fatigue, physical fatigue, mental fatigue, reduced activity, or reduced motivation (closest proxy to apathy), nor in oxidative stress markers. Limits: non-PD women; coated SOD/gliadin extract rather than raw cantaloupe.
