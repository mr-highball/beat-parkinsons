Peanut oil applied to tape and acute skin pain
Source: https://www.magonlinelibrary.com/doi/10.12968/jowc.2011.20.8.380
Coverage: Factual extract of the publisher primary abstract

Peanut oil was one of five solvents compared against a dry tape condition using a blinded acute procedural pain assessment. The authors report an overall p value of .39 for the visual analog scale and give no separate peanut-oil estimate or preparation grade.
