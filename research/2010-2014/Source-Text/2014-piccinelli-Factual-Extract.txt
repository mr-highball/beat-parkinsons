Isolated alpha-phellandrene in a nerve injury model
2014-03-25
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24661285/

Piccinelli and colleagues studied isolated alpha-phellandrene or limonene and Schinus fruit essential oil in rats with nerve injury. The journal abstract reports reduced mechanical and cold pain-like responses. It also states forced-swim immobility increased while describing an antidepressant-like effect. The overlapping 2013 meeting abstract repeats the wording. The later author thesis says immobility was inhibited. The journal figure remains unavailable. The reported preparations did not include ginger.
