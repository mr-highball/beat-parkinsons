Title: A Jerte valley cherry product provides beneficial effects on sleep quality. Influence on aging
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12878997/
PMID: 23732552
PMCID: PMC12878997
DOI: 10.1007/s12603-013-0029-4
Publication year: 2013
Coverage: Distilled methods, tables and results from the original crossover article.

Thirty volunteers in three age strata consumed a cherry formulation and a cherry-flavored comparison drink in five-day periods, with a one-week washout. Per dose the formulation combined 18.85 g pitted freeze-dried cherries from four cultivars with maltodextrin and ascorbic acid. Both preparations were consumed twice per day. The study recorded actigraphic sleep and activity, urinary melatonin metabolite and serum cytokines. It reports sleep changes during the cherry period. The study used a formulated exposure outside Parkinson disease and did not measure fatigue severity. The same team's related cherry-product publications need participant-level matching before they can be counted as independent corroboration.
