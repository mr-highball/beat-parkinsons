Title: The effects of daily consumption of grapefruit on body weight, lipids, and blood pressure in healthy, overweight adults.
PMID: 22304836
DOI: 10.1016/j.metabol.2011.12.004
First published: 2012
Authors: Dow CA, Going SB, Chow HH, Patil BS, Thomson CA
Journal: Metabolism: clinical and experimental
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22304836/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Six-week randomized controlled trial in 74 overweight adults consuming half a fresh Rio-Red grapefruit 3x daily (1.5 fresh grapefruits/day, n=42) vs low-bioactive citrus-free control (n=32). Between-group differences in weight, BP, and lipids were non-significant; within the fresh grapefruit arm, systolic BP dropped -3.21 +/- 10.13 mmHg (p=0.03), waist circumference -2.45 cm (p=0.0002), and LDL-C -18.7 mg/dL (p<0.001). Direct whole raw grapefruit RCT, no PD or orthostatic symptom endpoints.
