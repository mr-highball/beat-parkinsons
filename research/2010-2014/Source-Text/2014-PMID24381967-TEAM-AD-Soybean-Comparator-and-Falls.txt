Date basis: January 2014 journal issue. The earlier online day is unresolved in this record.
Review basis: Complete JAMA trial report, Methods, disclosures and Table 3, with the Cochrane intervention description and prior local formulation summary.
Source type: Four-arm randomized controlled clinical trial in Alzheimer disease.

TEAM-AD randomized 613 people with Alzheimer disease to vitamin E, memantine, both or matching placebos. The vitamin E placebo was a soybean-oil capsule. The memantine and dual-placebo arms used that companion capsule. The report does not specify the oil's refining grade or compare it with habitual intake. Background care and other trial assignments matter.

Table 3 gives ordinary fall adverse event counts and the separate rounded percentage of participants with an event. In trial arm order, vitamin E, memantine, combined treatment and dual placebo recorded 58 (20%), 38 (17%), 64 (23%) and 48 (20%). Serious fall event counts were 16 (10%), 16 (10%), 14 (8%) and 18 (10%), respectively. The authors report serious falls p=.89 across groups. Event counts should not be treated as counts of unique participants.

The review records the two memantine strata of the same cohort, so count TEAM-AD once. The results estimate differences among treatment regimens in Alzheimer disease. No culinary refined-soybean-oil Parkinson fall-frequency estimate is available for claim 0609.
