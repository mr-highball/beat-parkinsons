Title: Dietary intake of melatonin from tropical fruit altered urinary excretion of 6-sulfatoxymelatonin in healthy volunteers.
PMID: 23252791
DOI: 10.1021/jf300359a
First published: 2013
Authors: Johns NP, Johns J, Porasuphatana S, Plaimee P, Sae-Teaw M
Journal: Journal of agricultural and food chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23252791/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Human crossover study in n=30 healthy volunteers consuming six tropical fruits individually with 1-week washouts. Banana consumption significantly increased urinary 6-sulfatoxymelatonin (aMT6-s, a circulating melatonin biomarker) by 180% (p=0.001; pineapple +266% p=0.004; orange +47% p=0.007). Limits: endocrine biomarker only; no clinical sleep, insomnia, or PD outcome measured.
