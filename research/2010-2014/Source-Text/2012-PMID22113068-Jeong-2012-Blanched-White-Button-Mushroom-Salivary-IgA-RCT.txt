Title: Dietary intake of Agaricus bisporus white button mushroom accelerates salivary immunoglobulin A secretion in healthy volunteers.
PMID: 22113068
DOI: 10.1016/j.nut.2011.08.005
First published: 2012
Authors: Jeong SC, Koyyalamudi SR, Pang G
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22113068/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

One-week RCT in n=24 healthy volunteers consuming 100 g/d blanched white button mushrooms (A. bisporus) vs normal diet. Salivary IgA secretion rate rose 53-56% at weeks 1-2 (p<0.0005) without changing osmolality or reporting drooling/sialorrhea symptoms.
