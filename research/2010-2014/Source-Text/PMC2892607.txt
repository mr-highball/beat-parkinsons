The paper summarizes cortical Lewy body clinicopathology and describes work distinguishing presynaptic aggregates from conspicuous Lewy bodies. It reports mostly null correlations of broad body counts with clinical findings and cites regional temporal associations for formed visual hallucinations. Regional pathology, disease classification and episode frequency are distinct. No food intervention is described.
