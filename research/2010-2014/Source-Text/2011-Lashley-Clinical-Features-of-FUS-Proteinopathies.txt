Case-specific apathy and hallucinations in FUS proteinopathies
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3170529/

July 2011. The clinical histories and Table 2 report behavioral features in frontotemporal degeneration with FUS pathology. Apathy and visual hallucinations occur in patient histories and cannot be assigned to the same exposure or interpreted as a food response. The study compared proteinopathies using clinical, genetic and postmortem assessments.
