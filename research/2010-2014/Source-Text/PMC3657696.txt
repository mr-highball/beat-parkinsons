The study describes seven PLA2G6-associated cases with differing ages of onset. One adult case had transient visual hallucinations early in a progressive dystonia-parkinsonism syndrome and later had a cortical biopsy. The case description gives timing but no recurrence estimate. Its diagnosis, tissue findings and treatment history do not supply an idiopathic Parkinson food comparison.
