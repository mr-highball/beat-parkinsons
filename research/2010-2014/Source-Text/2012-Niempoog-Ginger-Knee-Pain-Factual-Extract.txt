Publication: Niempoog, Pawa and Amatyakul. The efficacy of powdered ginger in osteoarthritis of the knee. Journal of the Medical Association of Thailand 95 Suppl 1, S59–S64, January 2012. PMID 23964445.
Source: https://thaiscience.info/Journals/Article/JMAT/10971310.pdf
Coverage: Factual description of original methods and reported KOOS outcome. Exact arm table values unavailable in the accessible rendering.
The blinded trial randomized 60 adults to dry powdered ginger capsules or placebo. After eight weeks, 26 and 23 completed respectively. The original reports no clear advantage on the KOOS pain comparison. The clinical setting was knee osteoarthritis.
