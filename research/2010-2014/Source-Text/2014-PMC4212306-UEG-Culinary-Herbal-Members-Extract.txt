Title: UEG Week 2014 poster presentations, culinary and herbal member abstracts
PMCID: PMC4212306
DOI: 10.1177/2050640614548980
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4212306/
First verified online: 2014-10-19 per Crossref; print metadata October 2014 without day.
Coverage: Factual extracts of original member abstracts in the complete 474-page collection. Member abstracts are not full trial reports.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

P0991 tested STW5 and individual extracts in cell and rat intestinal preparations. Lemon balm and mixed extracts reduced a cell-injury marker, while isolated lemon balm did not affect the tested rat-ileum contractions. P1558 examined mixed STW5 and individual extracts in 575 intestinal muscle strips from 114 surgical patients; region and layer affected responses. Neither establishes a brewed clinical depression result.

P0470 supplied all participants a chicken sandwich before acid-versus-water gastric challenge. P1080 used orange juice as a diagnostic carrier. P1624 tested a mixed sausage/egg breakfast and coffee in a laboratory acid model. P1473 involved salmon nasal-cartilage extract in ulcerative colitis. P1701 compared mashed-potato meal conditions in 12 healthy adults for gastric and vascular endpoints. The assigned fatigue, falls, anxiety, constipation or insomnia effects cannot be isolated from these contexts.
