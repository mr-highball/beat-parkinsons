Title: Plasma potassium concentration and content changes after banana ingestion in exercised men.
PMID: 23182013
PMCID: PMC3499889
DOI: 10.4085/1062-6050-47.6.05
First published: 2012
Authors: Miller KC
Journal: Journal of athletic training
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23182013/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled crossover kinetics study (PMC3499889) in exercised men ingesting 0, 1, or 2 servings of bananas after 60 min of exercise in heat. Measured plasma potassium and glucose kinetics only; did not measure clinical muscle cramping or pain outcomes.
