Sage mouth rinse registration in palliative cancer
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02067572
Registration: NCT02067572
Coverage: Official registry fields and history list, with published trial control

First posted February 20, 2014 (estimated registry date). The current study record was posted February 20, 2017. It describes a completed parallel randomized comparison of saline and sage tea-based mouth rinses over four days in advanced cancer palliative care. The registry reports 88 actual participants and actual primary and study completion on September 8, 2016.

The current primary outcome fields give clinician-rated oral mucositis (OMAS), patient-perceived oral comfort including EORTC QLQ-OH17, and the dental clinical evaluation. All have a four-day timeframe. The secondary measure is general symptom experience by ESAS at four days. The official record history shows changes to outcome measures between the 2014 and 2017 versions. The first version’s exact outcome wording remained unavailable through the checked history routes. The record has no posted results.

The linked 2021 trial report (PMID 33586003) omitted comparative numerical ratings for mouth or swallowing pain from its analysis because they were uncommon at baseline. The 2023 publication (PMID 37072843) describes oral fungal isolates from the same project, with no randomized sage-versus-saline pain analysis.
