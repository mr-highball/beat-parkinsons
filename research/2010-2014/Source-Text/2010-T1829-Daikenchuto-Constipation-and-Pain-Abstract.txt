Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The conference members list the same eligibility, 12-week phases, two active doses, ongoing sennoside and main results as the 2010 journal paper PMID 27942295. Both indicate improved bloating and abdominal pain after the added formula and no clear change in stool frequency. The journal article assigned 22 people to two active doses and uses each patient as their own control for its before–after comparisons. The conference and journal versions are consistent with one study family.

NCT00522093 names the same hospital, formula, population, phase lengths and outcomes. Its plan specifies a single lower-dose arm, nonrandom allocation and an estimated 60 participants, whereas the paper reports two active arms and 22 participants. The link to the published protocol is plausible and unconfirmed.
