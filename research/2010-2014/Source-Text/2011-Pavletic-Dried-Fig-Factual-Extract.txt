Title: Pavletic, dried fruit and elevated potassium
PMID: 21907077
PMCID: PMC3932298
DOI: 10.1016/j.psym.2011.01.011
Publication: Psychosomatics, 2011, 52(5), 494–495
Original source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3932298/
Coverage: Factual notes from available author manuscript.

The first raised potassium measurement came from psychiatric study screening of a 36-year-old who volunteered as a healthy control. After the value normalized, a later raised result followed reported unusually large dried fig intake the preceding evening. The case associates the repeated laboratory result with a dietary history. It describes no visual hallucination or Parkinson comparison.
