Dark chocolate crossover

Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22584952/
Coverage: Original Methods, Table 1 and supplemental scope
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published May 15, 2012. Twenty-six participants with moderate, nonfluctuating Parkinson disease received 200 g of 80.1% cocoa dark chocolate and cacao-free white chocolate in a randomized investigator-blinded crossover. The composite UPDRS III contrast at one hour was −0.54 (95% CI −1.50 to 0.42, p=.258). Its period effect was 1.46 (0.50–2.42, p=.004). The full main report gives no separate speech item or intelligibility result. The supplement concerns blood analytes. The registration first posted in 2009 and is distinct from the unreported one-week protocol.
