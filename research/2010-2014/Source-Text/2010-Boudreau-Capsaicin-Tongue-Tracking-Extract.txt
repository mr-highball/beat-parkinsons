Title: The effects of training time, sensory loss and pain on human motor learning
Authors: S A Boudreau and colleagues
Date: 2010-05-20 first online
Identifiers: PMID 20492438; DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2842.2010.02103.x
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20492438/
Coverage: Original factual extract of the primary abstract.

A short-duration human tongue task tracked a moving target by varying tongue-protrusion force on a transducer. Capsaicin-induced intraoral pain reduced performance gains and led to both overshoots and undershoots and delayed responses. The separate long-training comparison in the article addresses session duration. These data do not evaluate speech intelligibility, an ingested food or Parkinson disease.
