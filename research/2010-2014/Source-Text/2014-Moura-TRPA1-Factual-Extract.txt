Title: The blockade of transient receptor potential ankirin 1 (TRPA1) signalling mediates antidepressant- and anxiolytic-like actions in mice
Authors: Juliana Cavalcante de Moura and colleagues
Identifiers: PMID 24846744, PMCID PMC4241094, DOI 10.1111/bph.12786
First published: 2014-05-21 (Wiley first-published field)
Original source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4241094/
Coverage: Full primary body, Results for elevated plus maze and Figure 2. Factual extract.

In Figure 2E, oral cinnamaldehyde 50 mg/kg alone produced no clear elevated-plus-maze open-arm time change (t(13)=0.28, p>.05). It prevented the increased open-arm exploration induced by the TRPA1 antagonist HC030031 when used as a pretreatment. Separate open-field data reported no change in spontaneous ambulation with the compounds. This pharmacological interaction in mice does not show that or in which direction ground culinary cinnamon changes anxiety in people with Parkinson disease.
