Title: Meal-related choking and Parkinson disease, original Japanese abstract O-220
Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jjspen/25/1/25_1_290/_pdf/-char/en#page=17
Publication date: 2010
Date basis: Volume 25 issue 1, page 306, original proceedings.
Coverage: Official J-STAGE publisher PDF, individual member O-220. Complete published member text. No patient-level cross-tabulation was published in the member.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

The series identifies 15 acute choking events. Five patients had Parkinson disease and one implicated food was shiitake. Chronic aspiration pneumonia was excluded. The pooled results do not show which patient had shiitake, whether the mushroom was roasted, or a symptom difference attributable to it.

The report groups incidence estimates by diagnosis using hospital admissions as denominators. The Parkinson figure is 1.8%, compared with 1.2% for dementia, 0.9% for psychiatric disorders and 0.1% for cerebrovascular disease. Diagnoses overlap. The combined analysis includes arrivals from outside the hospital and events on the wards. Neither the number nor its p value is specific to shiitake.
