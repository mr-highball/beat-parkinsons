Title: Lavender oil preparation Silexan is effective in generalized anxiety disorder--a randomized, double-blind comparison to placebo and paroxetine.
PMID: 24456909
DOI: 10.1017/S1461145714000017
First published: 2014
Authors: Kasper S, Gastpar M, Müller WE, Volz HP, Möller HJ, Schläfke S, Dienel A
Journal: The international journal of neuropsychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24456909/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Multicenter double-blind, double-dummy randomized controlled trial (Kasper et al., 2014, Int J Neuropsychopharmacol) enrolling 539 adults with DSM-5 generalized anxiety disorder (HAMA >= 18) randomized for 10 weeks to oral Silexan 160 mg/d, Silexan 80 mg/d, paroxetine 20 mg/d, or placebo. HAMA fell 14.1+/-9.3 (160 mg/d) and 12.8+/-8.7 (80 mg/d) vs 11.3+/-8.0 (paroxetine) and 9.5+/-9.0 (placebo); both Silexan doses beat placebo (p<0.01) while paroxetine showed a trend in FAS (p=0.10). Silexan also reduced depressive symptoms and improved SF-36 health-related quality of life with placebo-like adverse event rates (0.006-0.008 AEs/day vs 0.008 placebo, 0.011 paroxetine). Limits: standardized oral essential oil softgel rather than brewed/dried culinary lavender; non-PD GAD cohort.
