Title: Salivary α-synuclein and DJ-1: potential biomarkers for Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3122368/
PMID: 21349902
PMCID: PMC3122368
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awr015
Citation: Devic I, Hwang H, Edgar JS, et al. Brain. First published February 24, 2011. 134:e178.
Coverage: Original PMC journal report and Figure 2. Distilled factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

The study compared unstimulated resting saliva samples from 24 people with Parkinson disease and 25 healthy controls. Group differences in the two candidate biomarkers did not reach statistical significance. The report describes lower α-synuclein and higher DJ-1 tendencies, with limited score availability for the motor correlations. The concentrations are specimen measures, without an estimate of clinical drooling severity or an administered fig exposure.
