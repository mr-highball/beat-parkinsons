Title: Ergocalciferol from mushrooms or supplements consumed with a standard meal increases 25-hydroxyergocalciferol but decreases 25-hydroxycholecalciferol in the serum of healthy adults.
PMID: 22623385
DOI: 10.3945/jn.112.159764
First published: 2012
Authors: Stephensen CB, Zerofsky M, Burnett DJ, Lin YP, Hammock BD, Hall LM
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22623385/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Six-week RCT in n=38 healthy adults comparing untreated vs UV-treated white button mushrooms (A. bisporus, 8.8 or 17.1 ug/d ergocalciferol) vs vitamin D2 supplement. UV mushrooms raised serum 25(OH)D2 (+12.7-13.8 nmol/L) but proportionally decreased 25(OH)D3 (-10.4 to -20.6 nmol/L), yielding no net total 25(OH)D gain and no falls/clinical symptom endpoints.
