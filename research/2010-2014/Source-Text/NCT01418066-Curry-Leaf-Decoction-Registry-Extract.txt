Ground curry leaf mixture in a registered digestive trial
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01418066
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

First posted August 16, 2011. This double-blind crossover registered a tea decoction made from curry leaves, pomegranate and Curcuma powders, compared against a different placebo tea. The planned measures included bowel and abdominal symptoms, quality of life, anxiety, depression and adverse events. The official record has no posted results module. It supplies no levodopa interaction, OFF time, dysphagia, handwriting or voice measure.
