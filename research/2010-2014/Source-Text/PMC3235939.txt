Title: Positive FP-CIT SPECT (DaTSCAN) in Clinical Alzheimer's Disease – An Unexpected Finding?
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3235939/
PMCID: PMC3235939
PMID: 22545039
DOI: 10.1159/000330470
Publication date: 2011-09-21
Date basis: Publisher or NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Primary clinical diagnostic case series
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The report describes three people initially assessed for an Alzheimer-type presentation with abnormal dopamine transporter imaging. One case had uncontrolled visual hallucinations without evident motor parkinsonism at examination. Another described brief experiences closer to misperceptions. The paper illustrates uncertainty in clinical classification. It does not follow visual episode rates under dried fig exposure.
