Title: Visual hallucinations and altered visual information processing in Parkinson disease and dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20063433/
PMID: 20063433
DOI: 10.1002/mds.22919
Publication date: 2010-01-08
Date basis: Verified electronic publication
Source depth: Primary abstract; full article remains open
Evidence type: Primary clinical event-related potential comparison
Format: Factual digest of the original abstract

The study compared 11 Parkinson dementia patients with visual hallucinations and six without them, 24 DLB patients and 21 Alzheimer patients. The visual P3 response was slower in the hallucinatory Parkinson dementia and DLB groups than in the Alzheimer group. Auditory P3 latencies were comparable across the patient groups. Visual and auditory task latencies are separate from spontaneous visual event frequency or a food response.
