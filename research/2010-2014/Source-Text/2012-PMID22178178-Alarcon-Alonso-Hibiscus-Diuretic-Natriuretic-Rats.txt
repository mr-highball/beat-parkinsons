Title: Pharmacological characterization of the diuretic effect of Hibiscus sabdariffa Linn (Malvaceae) extract
PMID: 22178178
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2011.12.005
First published: 2012
Authors: Alarcón-Alonso J, Zamilpa A, Aguilar FA, Herrera-Ruiz M, Tortoriello J, Jimenez-Ferrer E
Journal: Journal of Ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22178178/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Experimental in vivo rat and isolated kidney perfusion study of standardized aqueous H. sabdariffa calyx extract (56.5 mg/g delphinidin-3-O-sambubioside, 20.8 mg/g cyanidin-3-O-sambubioside, quercetin, rutin, chlorogenic acid). Demonstrated dose-dependent diuretic, natriuretic (ED50=86 mg/kg, Emax=0.9 mEq/100 g/5 h), and potassium-sparing activity, plus +48% increased renal filtration in situ (additive with furosemide) mediated via endothelial nitric oxide release.
