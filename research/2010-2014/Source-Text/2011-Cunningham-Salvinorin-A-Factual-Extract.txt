The review identifies the source species as Salvia divinorum. Section V.B.1 summarizes variable rodent antinociceptive results with isolated salvinorin A, including weak or absent responses after some systemic experiments and signals following direct central delivery. Some experiments use analogs. These observations concern route, assays and receptor activity of a distinct species and compound. No human Parkinson comparison of eating fresh common sage or clinical pain severity is supplied.
