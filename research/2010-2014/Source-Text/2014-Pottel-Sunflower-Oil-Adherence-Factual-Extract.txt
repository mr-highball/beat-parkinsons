Title: High oleic sunflower oil and ability to swallow an assigned dose
Source: https://d-nb.info/1107449421/34
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Pottel and colleagues, October 7, 2014. Cancer radiotherapy study NCT01596933 randomized 91 people, and 85 began taking the assigned product (43 echium and 42 high-oleic sunflower). Body weight change was the primary outcome.
The results on page 6 list inability to swallow oil without identified reason in 2.9% of echium nonadherence reasons and 0% of sunflower reasons. Another 2.9% in the echium group reflected tracheotomy. These percentages do not give trial-arm dysphagia incidence or severity. Oil refining is not verified, no oil-free arm was assigned, and the population did not have Parkinson disease.
