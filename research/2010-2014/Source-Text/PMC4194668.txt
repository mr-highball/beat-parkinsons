Title: Lewy- and Alzheimer-type pathologies in Parkinson's disease dementia: which is more important?
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4194668/
PMCID: PMC4194668
PMID: 21596773
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awr031
Publication date: 2011-05-04
Date basis: Publisher or NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Primary retrospective clinicopathological cohort
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The brain-bank analysis included 56 pathology-confirmed Parkinson cases. Dementia classification drew on recorded clinical courses, with visual hallucinations and fluctuating confusion noted as clinical context. The reported associations concern combined Lewy and Alzheimer-type pathology and dementia. Twenty-three cases had been partially assessed in a prior cohort analysis. The study provides no food-linked symptom change or matched event-count endpoint.
