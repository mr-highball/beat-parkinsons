Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Mollik reports 2010–2012 interviews about traditional plant knowledge for central-nervous-system conditions in Bangladesh's Rajbangshi community. The inventory includes Ocimum gratissimum. It is a botanical citation lead, not an assigned preparation or a measured efficacy comparison for a specific plant.

O. gratissimum must not be silently equated with ordinary culinary O. basilicum. The abstract does not establish a Parkinson depression or other frozen symptom effect. Related tomato-derived lectin contexts in the collection are research materials, not tomato consumption. Named investigators elsewhere are not evidence of lemon-balm exposure.
