Title: Combined omega-3 dietary oils and memory in aged mice
Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0036861
PMID: 22662127
PMCID: PMC3360741
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0036861
Publication date: 2012-05-25
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full text, including publisher article methods and diet composition table.

The study used two cohorts of mice to examine behavior and laboratory endpoints. Both diets contained high-oleic sunflower oil with rapeseed and palm oils. The experimental diet included tuna oil as part of the fat blend. The authors reported better spatial recognition in older animals on the experimental diet alongside changes in laboratory measures.

The diet comparison does not isolate sunflower oil or identify it as refined or cold-pressed. The study did not evaluate depressive symptoms or anxiety in people with Parkinson disease.
