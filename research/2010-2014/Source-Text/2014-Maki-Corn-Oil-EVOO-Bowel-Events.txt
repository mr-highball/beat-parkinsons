Corn oil and olive oil feeding crossover
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1933287414003444
selected methods and findings paraphrase

First published electronically October 23, 2014. Fifty-four adults entered the lipid efficacy analysis of two 21-day corn-oil and extra-virgin olive-oil feeding conditions (four tablespoons daily). The adverse-event section reports constipation in three participants during the olive-oil period. It presents events exceeding a frequency threshold and treats the arm lists differently from a complete table. The exact corn-arm constipation count and severity are unavailable. Three corn-period diarrhea events are reported. No oil-free comparator or Parkinson cohort.
