The review synthesizes research on cortical spreading depression in migraine aura, vascular and trigeminal theories, imaging and animal experiments. A slowly progressing visual aura and neurophysiological depolarization are separate from formed hallucinations in Parkinson disease. There is no fig trial or measured food-associated visual symptom frequency.
