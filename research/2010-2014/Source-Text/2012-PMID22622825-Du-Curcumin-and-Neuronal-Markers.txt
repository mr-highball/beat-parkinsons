Original source scope
The intervention used isolated curcumin in a Parkinson rat model. Striatal dopamine and DOPAC partially recovered. Tyrosine hydroxylase-positive cells and iron staining shifted toward controls. Figure 2 reports six per group.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The rotation test identifies the model. The Results give no comparative rotation or bradykinesia effect. The source studies a turmeric constituent, with no ground spice intervention. It gives no toasted cumin exposure or postural-instability comparison.
