Title: Phytosome curcumin and regional soreness after downhill running
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4074833/
PMID: 24982601
PMCID: PMC4074833
DOI: 10.1186/1550-2783-11-31
Publication date: 2014-06-18
Date basis: Published structured original.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full structured original, including Figure 3 and outcomes text.

The single-blind randomized pilot assigned 20 healthy, moderately active men to a curcumin phytosome or placebo around a downhill running test. Nineteen participants completed the trial, with nine in the formulation group and ten on placebo. At 48 hours, the summed pain score was 23.3 versus 30.6, p=.06. The two anterior-thigh contrasts were reported as significant. Other regional contrasts and the combined pain outcome were inconclusive. The study separately measured magnetic-resonance evidence of muscle injury and blood markers.

The comparison concerns a formulation, induced soreness and healthy adults. The small sample and multiple region-specific comparisons limit inference. The experiment does not measure a culinary ground turmeric effect on Parkinson pain.
