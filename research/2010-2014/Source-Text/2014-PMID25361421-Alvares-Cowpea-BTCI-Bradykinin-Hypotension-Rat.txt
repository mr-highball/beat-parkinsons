Title: Bowman-Birk protease inhibitor from Vigna unguiculata seeds enhances the action of bradykinin-related peptides.
PMID: 25361421
PMCID: PMC6271500
DOI: 10.3390/molecules191117536
First published: 2014
Authors: da Cunha Morales Álvares A, Schwartz EF, Amaral NO, Trindade NR, Pedrino GR, Silva LP, et al.
Journal: Molecules (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25361421/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Biochemical and acute anesthetized-rat cardiovascular study (da Cunha Morales Alvares et al., 2014, Molecules, PMC6271500) testing purified black-eyed pea trypsin/chymotrypsin inhibitor (BTCI) complexed with infused bradykinin peptides. Parenteral BTCI protected bradykinin from cleavage and potentiated bradykinin-induced vasodilation and acute hypotension. Limits: injected purified protease inhibitor plus exogenous bradykinin in rats; cannot extrapolate to oral canned black-eyed peas or Parkinson's orthostatic lightheadedness.
