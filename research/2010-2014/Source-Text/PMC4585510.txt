The study compares thalamic connectivity and microstructure in dementia with Lewy bodies, focusing on fluctuating cognition and alertness. A visual hallucination or parkinsonism can satisfy part of the clinical classification, but the reported analysis concerns cognitive fluctuation and imaging. No food challenge is reported.
