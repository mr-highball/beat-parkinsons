Early Malik report on black seed oil and drug-induced movements
Original World Congress of Neurology poster abstract 1491 page e112
Malik and colleagues’ 2013 conference abstract reports black seed oil and haloperidol exposure in rats over six weeks. It describes oral dyskinesia, other movement observations and astrogliosis. Group sizes, formulation details and numerical motor estimates remain unavailable in this abstract. The same author team and outcomes appear in the 2016 publication PMID 27752803, suggesting one study family. Identical animals remain unverified. The preparation used oil and the outcomes came from drug exposure in rats.
