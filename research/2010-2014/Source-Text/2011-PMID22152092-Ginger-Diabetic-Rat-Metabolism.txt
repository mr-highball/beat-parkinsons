Title: Diabetic rat carbohydrate metabolism and the earlier IUPS ginger poster
PMID: 22152092
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114511006635
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22152092/
Published: 2011-12-12
Archive scope: Factual note from the investigators' publicly indexed primary abstract.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The author abstract reports oral aqueous ginger extract administered for 30 days in a diabetic rat model. Whole extract was tested at the same three amounts described in the 2009 poster. Outcomes include plasma glucose, organ glycogen and glycolytic enzyme activity. The abstract reports plasma glucose decreases of 38% and 68% at days 15 and 30 in the highest amount group. The complete tables are needed to verify the comparator for those percentages. Stool outcomes, pain, rigidity and anxiety were outside the reported measurements.

The 2009 poster and this 2011 full-journal report have overlapping lead authors, exposure schedules and a metabolic outcome. Tissue panels differ. Treat the reports as possibly overlapping until the complete methods and animal lineage can be compared.
