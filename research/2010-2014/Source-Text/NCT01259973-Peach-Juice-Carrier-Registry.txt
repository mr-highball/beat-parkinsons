Factual extract of the official ClinicalTrials.gov record.
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01259973
First posted: 2010-12-15
Status: UNKNOWN
Results posted in the 2026-11-15 snapshot: No.
The study compares haloperidol, risperidone and placebo in healthy volunteers. Peach juice is a shared carrier, with no untreated or juice-free arm assigned.
The planned Simpson–Angus assessment includes drug-induced postural signs. Tapping is among the planned psychomotor tests. This registration gives no raw peach comparison or measured result in idiopathic Parkinson disease.
