Title: Assessment of mild dementia with amyloid and dopamine terminal positron emission tomography
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3102241/
PMCID: PMC3102241
PMID: 21555336
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awr089
Publication date: 2011-05-09
Date basis: Publisher or NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML main body
Evidence type: Primary neurochemical PET and clinical classification comparison
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Seventy-five people with mild dementia received clinical and PET-based classifications. About 35% differed between approaches, with agreement kappa 0.39. The 14 people clinically classified with Lewy body dementia included 29% with a discordant PET classification. Spontaneous visual hallucinations were a diagnostic feature considered with other signs. The measured contrast is between classification methods, and there is no dried fig or episode-frequency comparison.
