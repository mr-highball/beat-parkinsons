Title: Comparing clinical profiles in Alzheimer's disease and Parkinson's disease dementia

DOI: 10.1159/000351861
Publication date: 2013-09-11
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Secondary analysis of trial databases
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The secondary analysis compares clinical profiles from the IDEAL Alzheimer trial, the four-trial ADENA program and the EXPRESS Parkinson disease dementia trial. Caregiver-rated NPI-10 totals and changes were available for IDEAL and EXPRESS. ADENA contributes cognitive and global outcomes without its own NPI-10 data in this analysis. The comparison reuses the original trials and supplies no dry fig arm or visual hallucination episode count.
