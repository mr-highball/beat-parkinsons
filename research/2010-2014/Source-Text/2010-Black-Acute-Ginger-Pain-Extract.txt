2010 single dose after eccentric exercise

Black and O’Connor first published this separate crossover report on 28 October 2010. In 27 volunteers, a single 2-g ginger dose or placebo at 24 and 48 hours after induced elbow-flexor injury gave no overall pain difference at 45 minutes post-dose. A subset taking ginger first showed lower pain the following day. This timing-specific subset result does not override the overall acute result and is distinct from 11-day preloading. The reports differ in dose schedule, randomization and exercise protocol. Individual participant overlap has not been verified. The publisher abstract and explicit early online date are accessible.
