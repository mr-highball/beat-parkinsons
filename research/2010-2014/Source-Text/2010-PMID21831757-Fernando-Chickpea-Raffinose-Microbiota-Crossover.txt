Title: Diets supplemented with chickpea or its main oligosaccharide component raffinose modify faecal microbial composition in healthy adults.
PMID: 21831757
DOI: 10.3920/BM2009.0027
First published: 2010
Authors: Fernando WM, Hill JE, Zello GA, Tyler RT, Dahl WJ, Van Kessel AG
Journal: Benef Microbes
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21831757/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Microbiota companion report (Fernando et al., 2010, Br J Nutr; same n=12 healthy adult crossover cohort as Dahl et al. 2014 PMID:26067078) testing 200 g/day canned chickpeas vs 5 g/day raffinose vs control diet for 3 weeks each. Chickpea and raffinose diets modulated fecal Bifidobacterium and reduced putrefactive Clostridium clusters, without direct clinical Parkinson or constipation symptom severity testing.
