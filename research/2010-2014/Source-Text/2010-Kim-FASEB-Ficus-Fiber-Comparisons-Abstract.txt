Title: Effect of Ficus carica on Functional Constipation
Source: https://faseb.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1096/fasebj.24.1_supplement.lb348
DOI: 10.1096/fasebj.24.1_supplement.lb348
Publication date: 2010-04-01, publisher's first publication date.
Citation: Kim et al. Experimental Biology 2010. FASEB Journal 24(S1), lb348.
Text coverage: Paraphrased complete published meeting abstract.

This double-blind study randomized 20 patients with modified Rome III functional constipation to a Ficus carica intervention or a fiber comparison for two weeks. The fig group alone was reported to improve from 44.4 to 28.9 hours in colon transit (P=.027) and from 2.8 to 5.2 bowel movements weekly (P=.004). The abstract states that the arms did not differ significantly across the defecation symptoms. It provides no exact between-arm transit estimate and does not establish an advantage over fiber.

The April 2010 publication predates the registered July 2010 start of NCT01185431 and reports a smaller study and shorter duration. The 2016 80-person paste paper names NCT02138851 and used a separate placebo comparison. This meeting record should be preserved as its own observed comparison. Individual participant overlap has not been established.
