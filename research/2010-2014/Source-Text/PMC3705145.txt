Title: Cohort study of prevalence and phenomenology of tremor in dementia with Lewy bodies

Format: Factual digest of the original material

The diagnostic comparison included 67 people with dementia with Lewy bodies, 111 with Parkinson disease and 34 with essential tremor. Table 1 records whether visual hallucinations were present at baseline and follow-up, including benign experiences. In DLB, 49 of 67 had them at baseline and 62 of 67 at follow-up. The Parkinson group had none at baseline and 31 of 111 at follow-up. This concerns presence by visit, with no food-linked visual episode count. A separate blinded rum challenge used orange juice in both the exposure and control. Tremor improved with alcohol in essential tremor, with no significant improvement in the Parkinson or Lewy body groups. The levodopa and primidone comparisons and the chronic period had separate designs.
