Original study scope
The original Methods and Figure 2 use oral corn oil as the common vehicle for C-DIM compounds in MPTP mice and as the vehicle-only treatment in the lesioned control arm. A shorter hind-limb stride in vehicle mice and improvement in selected C-DIM arms are compound contrasts. Open-field rearing showed no clear between-group change in the reported comparison.

Continuing evidence gaps
Gavage with shared corn oil does not isolate the effect of refined or cold-pressed edible oil. No pain endpoint was measured.
