The study compares phosphorylated alpha-synuclein inclusions in skin specimens in Parkinson disease and other parkinsonian presentations. Its clinical context includes visual hallucinations as a feature of Lewy body disease. The measured diagnostic tissue endpoint cannot establish an effect on food-associated visual episodes.
