Title: Neuroprotective effect of sulforaphane in 6-hydroxydopamine-lesioned mouse model of Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 23518299
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuro.2013.03.004
First published: 2013
Authors: Morroni F, Tarozzi A, Sita G, Bolondi C, Zolezzi Moraga JM, Cantelli-Forti G
Journal: Neurotoxicology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23518299/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Four-week 6-OHDA intrastriatal-lesioned PD mouse model testing sulforaphane (5 mg/kg twice weekly). Sulforaphane significantly ameliorated 6-OHDA-induced apomorphine rotations and motor coordination deficits, protected nigral dopaminergic neurons against DNA fragmentation/caspase-3 apoptosis, and enhanced brain glutathione and ERK1/2 survival signaling. Limits: purified compound in rodent toxin model.
