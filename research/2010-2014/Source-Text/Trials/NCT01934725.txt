The observational SECRETO study is listed as active, not recruiting. Estimated enrollment is 1,200 for the broader registry. There are no results posted in the registry at access. ESOC25-2386 and the 2026 journal paper describe a nested 598-person case-crossover analysis of caffeinated beverage timing and cryptogenic ischemic stroke onset. The three records identify one study family. The registry population and outcome are distinct from Parkinson symptom change.
