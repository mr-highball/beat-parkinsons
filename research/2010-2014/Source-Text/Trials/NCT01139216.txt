Title: Daikenchuto (TU-100), transit and rectal sensation in women with constipation
NCT ID: NCT01139216
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01139216
First posted: 2010-06-08
Archive scope: Summary from the official structured registration and linked publication.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The completed masked parallel comparison enrolled 45 women. The three groups received placebo or TU-100 at 7.5 or 15 g daily, divided into three administrations, for 28 days. The official API protocol lists scintigraphic colonic transit, rectal compliance, sensation and bowel function assessments. The official registry snapshot has no posted results. The 2013 original abstract reports mostly inconclusive group differences. These are two records for the same cohort.
