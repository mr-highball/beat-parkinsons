Title: Daikenchuto for abdominal bloating with constipation
Registry ID: UMIN000008070
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000009507
First posted: 2012-06-01
Archive scope: Summary from the official structured registration and linked publication.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The official record specifies an unblinded single arm with no random assignment or control and a target of 20 people. The planned primary measure was GSRS. Secondary measures include breath hydrogen, bowel movements, stool form, other abdominal symptoms and fullness. The registered schedule is 1.5 g per day for 17 days. Ten participants appear in the linked 2015 article, which states 15 g per day for 14 days and uses different randomization wording. Both records identify the same trial. The administered schedule is unresolved.
