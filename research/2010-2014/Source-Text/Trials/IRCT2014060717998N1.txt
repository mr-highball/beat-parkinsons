Title: Melissa officinalis capsules and health education for premenstrual symptoms in school students
Registry ID: IRCT2014060717998N1
Source: https://irct.ir/trial/16427
First registered: 2014-08-16, explicitly retrospective.
Coverage: Factual extract of official registry retrieved HTTP 200 on 2026-11-17.

The summary describes 200 students in four parallel groups, capsule treatment and education with respective controls, three months of intervention and GHQ and PSST questionnaires. The recruitment field says complete. Investigator blinding is single blind, whereas later papers describe double blinding.

Structured target enrollment is 800, despite 200 in the summary. Ages 76–72 and expected start 2004-01-21 are implausible metadata and are not accepted as participant ages or a verified study start. Actual dates are blank. The papers describe 2013–2014 recruitment and share thesis 92-6805. These fields must remain distinct from verified actual enrollment and results. See the 2015, 2017-online and Heydari reports under 2015–2019; their participant overlap is only partly established.
