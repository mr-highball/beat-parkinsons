NCT01767922 — Multicentre, Double-blind Study Versus Placebo on Impact and Safety of Extramel 140 IU
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01767922
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Coverage: Selected official registry fields, summarized as plain text. Publication-based clarifications are identified.

First public registration: 2013-01-15. Last public update: 2013-01-18.
Status: Completed. Results posted to ClinicalTrials.gov: No.
Sponsor: Robertet SA. Enrollment: 61 actual.
Intervention: Oral Extramel melon concentrate or excipient placebo in adults reporting stress and fatigue.
Outcomes: Perceived Stress Scale primary, physical and intellectual fatigue, pain and quality of life secondary. The 2014 report DOI 10.3390/nu6062348 gives results. No Parkinson or raw fruit arm.
