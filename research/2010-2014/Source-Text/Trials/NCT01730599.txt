BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a longitudinal study in patients with Parkinson's Disease (PD) carriers of a
genetic mutation - substitution of gly with ser in position 2019 (G2019S) in the
leucine-rich repeat kinase 2 (LRRK2) gene. The purpose of this study is to explore the
association between genetic mutations in the known genes and their influence on disease
manifestation over few years of follow up
