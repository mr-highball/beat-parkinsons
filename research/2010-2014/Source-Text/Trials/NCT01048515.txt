The registry describes a randomized placebo-controlled crossover of isolated caffeine after eight hours of abstinence in older adults. Its primary outcome is ergometer cycling endurance. The secondary fatigue measure is a Borg exercise effort rating at five minutes and exhaustion. Other planned measures include postural stability, walking speed, biochemical outcomes and withdrawal symptoms. The registry lists PMID 16081625 and 16886964 as background publications. A matching 2011 report uses the eight-hour-abstinence comparison and identifies no clear endurance or perceived-effort result, with increased postural sway. Compare actual study dates and group characteristics before assuming no participant overlap. Neither the registry nor the separate publication isolates brewed coffee or a Parkinson fatigue scale.
