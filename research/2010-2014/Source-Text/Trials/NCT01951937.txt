Title: Fish Intervention and Learning Abilities

Publication date: 2013-09-27
Coverage: Selected fields from the official ClinicalTrials.gov record, verified 2026-11-19.

The German FINS-547 study is marked completed. The latest public update was 2022-08-22. The registry describes random allocation of preschool children to frequent salmon meals or a habitual comparison diet, and lists actual enrollment as 200. Cognitive performance appears as the primary outcome and fatty acid and nutrient biomarkers as secondary outcomes. The displayed registration does not list a standalone peg-test outcome or a cooking method. No summary results are posted.

The linked journal article identifies NCT01951937, reports 205 children randomized and calls the comparator meat meals. It reports no clear difference on the nine-hole peg test. The full results table and preparation details remain open. The registry and report represent one trial family. See research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-Demmelmair-Salmon-Dexterity.txt.
