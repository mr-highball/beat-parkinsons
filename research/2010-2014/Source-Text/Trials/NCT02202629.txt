ARMS AND INTERVENTIONS

Four single-exposure beverage arms. One specifies a cherry-flavored test beverage and the remaining three specify the same type of drink with fruit, vegetable and herbal extracts. The public registry does not identify each extract.

OUTCOMES

Primary: Change in cognitive function and fatigue during extended Cognitive Demand Battery performance and change in long-term declarative memory. The record specifies one, three and six hours after consumption.
Secondary: Executive function, frontal cerebral hemodynamic measures during task performance, episodic memory by immediate word recall, and mood ratings.
