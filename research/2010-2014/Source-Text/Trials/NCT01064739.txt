Source: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT01064739, Renal Salt Handling in Postural Tachycardia Syndrome Following Dietary Dopa Administration
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01064739
First registration posting: February 8, 2010
First public results: July 31, 2013
Coverage: original summary of selected registration and participant-flow fields, verified November 12, 2026

Vanderbilt University tested a fixed-sodium diet with and without fresh fava beans in a fixed sequence. Fourteen participants completed the control diet day. The second day added 100 g of fresh fava beans at both breakfast and lunch. Thirteen participants completed that day. The registry records one withdrawal after blood was observed in the participant’s urine on the fava bean day. The completed cohort was made up of healthy volunteers. Parkinson’s disease was an exclusion criterion.

Primary publication: Garland et al., DOI 10.3945/ajcn.112.048470, PMID 23553159, PMC3628380.
