Title: The Better Resiliency Among Veterans With Omega-3's (BRAVO) Study
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01901887
Data endpoint: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01901887
Format: Selected official registry fields summarized for this catalog.
Registry: NCT01901887
First posted: 2013-07-17 (estimated in the registry).
Last posted: 2018-06-12.
Status: Terminated for low recruitment. Actual enrollment 125.
Design: Randomized masked parallel comparison in adults at risk of suicidal behavior.
Intervention and comparison: A drink containing omega-3 fatty acids versus a matched drink with macadamia nut oil substituted. Pressing grade is unspecified.
Planned outcomes: Suicidal behaviors and thinking, negative affect, PTSD symptoms and cognition. The registry gives no Parkinson or movement endpoint and posts no trial outcome results.
Family note: This trial differs from the RRIPP-3 infantry trainee study (NCT02908932). The BRAVO cognitive-test-methods paper (PMID 27074873) does not establish an oil-specific tremor effect.
