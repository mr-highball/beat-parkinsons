NCT00102596 — Essential tremor formulation results
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00102596
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Coverage: Selected official results fields, summarized as plain text.

First public registration: 2005-01-31.
First public results: 2012-02-01.
Results groups: Cellulose-based 1-octanol (CEL) and soybean-oil-embedded 1-octanol (SOY).
Fixed-dose primary analysis: 10 participants in each period.
Normalized mean tremor amplitude at 90 minutes: CEL 0.670 (SE 0.082); SOY 0.685 (SE 0.160).
Normalized mean tremor amplitude at 360 minutes as currently posted: CEL 0.894 (SE 0.223); SOY 0.122 (SE 0.098). The 2011 journal report describes a 33.2% reduction with the soybean formulation at six hours. The figures do not match as posted, and the discrepancy remains unresolved.
The registry pools adverse events across 1-octanol formulation and dose groups. Sedation and worsened tremor each affected two of 15 participants, without formulation attribution.
