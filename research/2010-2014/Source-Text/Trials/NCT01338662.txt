BRIEF SUMMARY

The purpose of this study is to compare the onset time and severity of dyskinesia in
amantadine or Dopamine agonist initial treated groups in Parkinson's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

1. Dopamine agonist can delay the risk of dyskinesia by initiating treatment rather
     than levodopa. Amantadine is typical antidyskinetic drug. There is no data about
     comparison of risk of dyskinesia in amantadine and dopamine agonist by initiating
     treatment.

  2. Prospective , randomized, open label study compare the onset time and severity of
     dyskinesia between groups randomized assigned order of amantadine and dopamine
     agonist
