Title: Effects of Nucleus Basalis of Meynert Stimulation on Cognitive Disorders in Dementia With Lewy Bodies
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01340001
NCT ID: NCT01340001
Publication date: 2011-04-21
Source depth: original ClinicalTrials.gov API registration; locally distilled

First posted 2011-04-21, last posted update 2018-04-30. The French record lists completed status, actual enrollment of six and no posted registry results. Free recall of the Free and Cued Selective Reminding Test is the planned primary active versus off comparison. The journal report PMID33199437 identifies this registration. The clinical endpoint and population are distinct from dietary exposure and a counted visual episode rate.
