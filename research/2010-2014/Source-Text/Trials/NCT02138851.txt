Protocol YGF-CON-FIG2. The randomized double-masked eight-week parallel study enrolled 80 people. It compares supplied fig paste and placebo in functional constipation and specifies change in colon transit time as the primary outcome. Eligibility includes ages 19–39 and excludes neurological disorders. The registry dates the study start to 2013-06. The registry's recorded primary completion month is also 2013-06, while the paper reports recruitment through December 2013. The dates are retained as reported.

The publication cites this NCT accession on printed p. 488 and supplies the outcomes. Link PMID 27440682 and DOI 10.6133/apjcn.092015.06 as the same study. The registry record supplies protocol information without separate result estimates.
