Title: Edible Plant Exosome Ability to Prevent Oral Mucositis Associated With Chemoradiation Treatment of Head and Neck Cancer
NCT ID: NCT01668849
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01668849
API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01668849
First posted: 2012-08-20
Last update posted: 2022-08-09
Archive scope: Official registry fields and factual summary. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

Completed. Sixty enrolled. No results are posted.

The experimental arm uses grape extract, also described as grape powder delivering plant vesicles. The comparator field lists standard management with analgesics and mouthwash. The primary outcome is oral mucositis pain during chemoradiation. The registry gives no comparative outcome and covers a different preparation, clinical cause, and population from a symptom claim in Parkinson disease.
