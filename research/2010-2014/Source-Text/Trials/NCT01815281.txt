BRIEF SUMMARY

The purpose of this research study is to evaluate safety and effectiveness of Foot
Mechanical stimulation to improving Gait and Gait Related Disorders in Parkinson Disease
and Progressive Supranuclear Palsy both stable and with motor fluctuation.

CONDITIONS

- Idiopathic Parkinson's Disease
- Progressive Supranuclear Palsy

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Diagnosis of idiopathic PD or PSP by UK Brain Bank criteria,

    -  Able to walk 25 feet unassisted or with minimal assistance;

    -  On stable doses of Parkinson's medications for at least 2 weeks prior to study
       onset;

    -  Endurance sufficient to stand at least 20 minutes unassisted per patient report.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Other significant neurological or orthopedic problems.
