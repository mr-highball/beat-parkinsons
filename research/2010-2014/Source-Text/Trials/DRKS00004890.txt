Title: The Karlsruhe Metabolomics Project
Registry ID: DRKS00004890
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00004890
First posted: 2013-04-10 according to the official registry.
Source kind: Trial registry factual extract.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

The complete, single-center German non-interventional study records blood and 24-hour urine metabolomic profiles and measurements including dietary recall. It does not assign banana intake. The 2019 banana biomarker paper selected 78 people from the parent KarMeN sample for its cross-sectional validation. Treat this validation as distinct from that paper's 12-person randomized feeding crossover. Neither study reports a clinical apathy contrast.
