NCT02210767: Effects of Walnuts on Central Blood Pressure, Arterial Stiffness Indices, Lipoproteins, and Other CVD Risk Factors.
Official source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02210767
First posted: 2014-08-07. Current record checked 2026-11-16; last update posted 2023-08-21.
Status: completed; 46 actual enrollment; no posted results.

Randomized crossover interventions: walnut diet, approximately 2 ounces of walnuts daily; a walnut-free fatty-acid-matched diet; and a lower-alpha-linolenic-acid diet replacing that fatty acid with oleic acid. Current primary outcome is change in central blood pressure at the ends of the three diet periods; secondary measures include ambulatory blood pressure, arterial stiffness, lipids, inflammation, gut microbiome and glucose. No dystonia or muscle-spasm outcome is listed. These current fields do not establish the exact content of the first posted version. The 2019 original reports published results.
