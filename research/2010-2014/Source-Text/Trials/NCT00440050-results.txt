Coverage: Original factual extract of the registry fall result and journal intervention and safety details.

The official registry maps EG000 to placebo and EG001 to algal DHA. The nonserious fall row records 33 events and 33 affected participants among 164 placebo recipients and 42 events and 42 affected participants among 238 DHA recipients. The journal table gives 20.1% and 17.6%, respectively (p=.60). The paper reports corn or soy oil as the matched placebo. Separate oil allocation, oil grade, and Parkinson outcomes were unavailable. The registry and paper describe one trial.
