Title: Walnut oil and blood glucose in adults with type 2 diabetes
Registry ID: IRCT2014022216682N1
Source: https://irct.ir/trial/15547
WHO bridge: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT2014022216682N1
First registered: 2014-03-19, retrospectively registered.
Coverage: Factual current official primary registry extraction; historical versions not recovered.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Target enrollment is 100. The record describes a randomized, single-blind parallel trial of oral walnut oil 15 g/day for 90 days versus no intervention, with fasting glucose and HbA1c primary and weight secondary. Ethics identifier is CT-P-92-5670.

Expected recruitment is March 1–16, 2014; actual recruitment fields are blank. The WHO first-enrollment field reflects the expected date and does not verify actual enrollment.

The 2016 report PMID28115966 names this registration and ethics approval but says recruitment occurred May–June 2013. Preserve the date conflict. The later 2019 glycemic report uses the same identifiers and is a companion-family lead rather than independent replication. The distinct 2017 lipid registration is IRCT2014030611375N2. No Parkinson, cognition or pain outcome is registered here.
