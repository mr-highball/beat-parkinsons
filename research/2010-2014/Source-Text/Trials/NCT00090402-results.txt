Title: Fish Oil and Alpha Lipoic Acid in Treating Alzheimer's Disease — official results extract
ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT00090402
Results first posted: 2010-02-26 (estimated).
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00090402?tab=results
API record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT00090402
Coverage: Original factual extract of explicit arm compositions and the adverse event terms Falls and Dizziness.

The placebo contained soybean oil with 5% fish oil and lemon flavor. Participants took three 1-gram capsules daily and a separate alpha-lipoic acid placebo.
The fish-oil arm received fish oil and an alpha-lipoic acid placebo. The combined arm received fish oil and active alpha-lipoic acid.
Nonserious falls, assessed systematically according to the registry, affected 2 of 13 in the soybean-oil-blend arm (two events), 1 of 13 in the fish-oil-only arm (one event) and 0 of 13 in the combined arm.
Dizziness affected 1 of 13, 2 of 13 and 2 of 13, respectively.
The cohort had Alzheimer's disease. Falls were recorded as adverse events in a small pilot, without a Parkinson population or a comparison of refined oil against no oil.
