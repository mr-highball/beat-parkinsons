BRIEF SUMMARY

Non-Ergot Dopamine agonists are meanwhile the drugs of first-choice in the treatment of
Parkinson's disease. The receptor profile of the non-ergot dopamine-agonist piribedil is
unique. In addition to agonistic effects on dopaminergic D2- and D3-receptors piribedil
has adrenergic alpha-2A- and alpha-2C-receptors antagonisic properties. There is evidence
from the literature that the antagonistic properties of piribedil are correlated with an
improvement of cognitive function and vigilance parameters in parkinson's disease. The
aim of the present non-interventional study is to investigate the safety and efficacy of
piribedil during long-term therapy of patients with M. Parkinson under consideration of
cognitive functions and quality of life.
