Title: Efficacy and Safety of Sage Tablets Compared to Placebo in Menopausal Patients With Hot Flushes and Sweating
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02032862

Text coverage: Selected official registration fields.

The registry describes a randomized masked tablet comparison over 12 weeks using an extract from fresh sage leaves. The primary outcome was change on a hyperhidrosis severity measure. Secondary measures included the Menopause Rating Scale and modified DemTect. The record lists 107 actual participants, with termination attributed to insufficient recruitment. No summary results are posted. Extraction and menopausal outcomes differ from dried culinary sage and diagnosed Parkinson apathy.
