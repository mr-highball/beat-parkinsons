Title: Evaluation of moxibustion therapy for urge urinary incontinence after stroke
Registry ID: ISRCTN44706974
DOI: 10.1186/ISRCTN44706974
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN44706974
Registry export: https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/44706974
Date basis: First public registration 2013-12-31. Submission 2013-12-05.
Coverage: Selected official registration fields and public protocol linkage.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The registered pilot planned to assign 120 participants to routine care alone or routine care plus one of two topical ginger-salt moxibustion methods. Mean volume per void was the primary measure, with related urinary and quality-of-life outcomes. The registration marks the study completed and no longer recruiting and lists the 2014 protocol as the study output. The public export has no posted results.
