BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a extended long-term safety study in Parkinson's disease patients who have motor
response complications on levodopa therapy and completed 12 weeks administrations of
KW-6500 in 6500-004 study. The safety and efficacy of long-term subcutaneous
self-injections of KW-6500 are evaluated.
