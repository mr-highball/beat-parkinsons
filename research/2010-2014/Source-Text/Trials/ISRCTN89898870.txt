Title: PREDIMED-Plus cardiovascular prevention trial
Registration: ISRCTN89898870
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN89898870
Registry DOI: 10.1186/ISRCTN89898870
Official PDF snapshot: https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/89898870
Sponsor project: https://www.predimedplus.com/en/project/
Registration date: 2014-07-24
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated official registration and sponsor statements

The trial randomized older adults with overweight or obesity and metabolic
syndrome to an unrestricted Mediterranean diet or an energy-restricted
Mediterranean diet with physical activity and behavioral support. Both
groups received extra-virgin olive oil and tree nuts. The sponsor reports
6,874 participants. The registry records completion on 2024-12-22.

The registry includes incident Parkinson disease among many secondary
outcomes in a cardiovascular prevention study. The exposure is shared
between treatment groups. There is no Parkinson rest-tremor severity
outcome in the verified registration. The November 2026 follow-up did
not authenticate a separate incident-Parkinson results paper. The
registry lists reports on other outcomes.

The official registry distinguishes submission on 2014-05-28 from
registration on 2014-07-24. The two dates appear interchangeably in
some secondary publications. The accessible registry PDF is an earlier
snapshot than the indexed May 2026 online record.
