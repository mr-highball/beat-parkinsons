Title: Herbal extract granules combined with a probiotic mixture for diarrhea-predominant irritable bowel syndrome
Registry ID: NCT01342718
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01342718
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01342718
First posted: 2011-04-27, date typed estimated in current API; first submitted 2011-04-25.
Coverage: Factual extract of current official registry and matched protocol/result identity. No registry results posted; historical versions unavailable.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

The four-arm trial assigns GJS or placebo with Duolac7S probiotic or placebo for eight weeks. The registry lists 3 g GJS granules three times daily and two probiotic capsules daily. Its 11 listed herbal ingredients omit ginger and jujube, which appear among 13 rows in protocol Table 1. This documentation discrepancy does not establish their absence from the administered product.

Current enrollment is 48 actual, while the 2013 paper reports 53 randomized and 48 completing. Protocol targets at least 60; the later paper describes a planned 64. The protocol says first randomization occurred April 25, two days before public posting. Submission and posting dates are distinct.

Responder wording differs across registry, protocol, paper Methods and figure caption. Weekly adequate relief, a symptom-reduction threshold and the proportion of affirmative weeks cannot be silently equated. The original protocol and result belong to this family; they identify a different product from Carmint. Earlier registry versions remain inaccessible.
