Title: n-3 PUFA for Vascular Cognitive Aging
ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT01953705
First posted: 2013-10-01 (estimated).
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01953705
API record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01953705
Coverage: Selected official registration fields, as retrieved natively.

The randomized comparison lists a soybean-oil placebo and a fish oil concentrate.
The primary endpoint was white matter hyperintensity volume on MRI. Other registry outcomes include brain atrophy and cognitive tasks.
The official public registry contains no results module. The 2019 protocol and 2024 primary paper report the same 102-person study.
Protocol: PMID 30934894, DOI 10.3390/nu11040735.
Results: PMID 39088212, DOI 10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.26872.
