A Sardine Diet Intervention Study to Assess Benefits to the Metabolic Profile in Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus Patients

The trial compared a sardine diet and a control diet in 35 participants with type 2 diabetes. Glycated hemoglobin at six months was the primary outcome. Other recorded outcomes included glucose, insulin, blood pressure, inflammatory markers, circulating lipids and gut microbiota. The record does not list fatigue.

This plain-text snapshot records selected official protocol and results-availability fields.
