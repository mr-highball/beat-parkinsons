Food2Learn, NCT02240264. Official registry source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02240264. Public first posted date September 15, 2014 (estimated); last update April 6, 2017. Completed; final completion August 2016 (actual). No structured results section.

Sleep duration and sleep quality were registered at 12 months, using ActivPAL and diary, Adolescent Sleep-Wake Scale and Adolescent Sleep Hygiene Scale. Trial compares krill oil with placebo in typically developing adolescents. The publication connected to the record is the 2016 protocol PMID27401364; that protocol names mixed olive, corn, palm and medium-chain triglyceride oils in the placebo. No corn-only, Parkinson or insomnia group.
