Title: Whole Ajwa dates and intestinal microbiota in healthy adults
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02288611
Registry ID: NCT02288611
First public: 2014-11-11
Coverage: Distilled original registry fields. Status checked 2026-11-19.

A randomized crossover planned 22 healthy adults and compared whole Ajwa dates with maltodextrin and dextrose. The public record classifies masking as none. The published report (PMID 26428278) calls the trial single-masked, and reports a daily abdominal pain diary. The registry identifies microbiota composition as primary and does not publish diary results. This is the same cohort as the 2015 original.
