Title: Integrative medicine group visits for patients with chronic pain and depression
Registry ID: NCT02262377
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02262377
First public registry date: 2014-10-13.
Text coverage: Official registry fields and matched original outcomes paper.

The completed randomized comparison tests integrative group medical visits against primary care. The registry reports actual enrollment of 159 and lists chronic pain, depression, pain self-efficacy and pain medication use among primary outcomes. Posted results first appeared on August 28, 2019. The intervention combines multiple forms of care, with no randomized cinnamon arm.

The original clinical analysis is DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0225540. A later dietary abstract is a likely secondary analysis of this cohort.
