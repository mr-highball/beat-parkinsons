Title: Melissa tea and psychological outcomes after burns
Registry ID: IRCT201205198177N4
Source: https://irct.ir/trial/8624
WHO bridge: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT201205198177N4
First registered: 2013-08-22, after the recorded 2013-07-06 enrollment start.
Text coverage: Official IRCT registration and WHO bridge, matched to the original paper.

The registry targets 36 hospitalized burn patients and compares commercial Melis lemon balm tea with black tea, twice daily for 20 days. It lists depression measured by a Beck questionnaire on day 1 and day 20 as a primary outcome. Patients with prior depression were excluded. The 2016 Chehroudi report prints this trial ID and describes replacement of participants discharged before completing follow-up. Its depression categories and reported test lack the details needed to reconstruct a comparative severity estimate.

Original paper: https://doi.org/10.5812/traumamon.33630. Outcome review: research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#brewed-lemon-balm-and-depression-after-burn-injury.
