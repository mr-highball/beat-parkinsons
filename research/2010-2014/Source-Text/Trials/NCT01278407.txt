Title: A study of E2020 in patients with dementia with Lewy bodies, followed by a long-term extension phase
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01278407
Trial registration: NCT01278407
Sponsor study ID: E2020-J081-341
Publication date: 2011-01-17
Date basis: First posted at ClinicalTrials.gov
Source depth: Official registration, protocol fields and posted confirmatory results
Evidence type: Registry for the phase III donepezil trial

The record gives 142 participants and lists three confirmatory randomized arms. The week-12 primary outcomes are change in MMSE and change in NPI-2, which combines hallucinations and cognitive fluctuation. The registry identifies an extension phase. The confirmatory publication and extension report share this trial lineage. The earlier 140-participant phase II trial is a separate enrollment.
