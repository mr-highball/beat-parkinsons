REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in cumulative daily doses of Sifrol®
    Description: Changes in the doses between visits are evaluated using Wilcoxon's signed rank test
    Time frame: up to 8 weeks
  -
    Measure: Changes in cumulative daily doses of L-dopa
    Description: Changes in the doses between visits are evaluated using Wilcoxon's signed rank test
    Time frame: up to 8 weeks
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change from Baseline in Short Parkinson Evaluation Scale (SPES) total score
    Time frame: Baseline, after 8 weeks
  -
    Measure: Change from Baseline in SPES subscales
    Description
      Psychopathological disturbances, Activities of daily living, Evaluation of motor
      function, Tremor, Resting Tremor, Postural tremor, Complications of therapy
    Time frame: Baseline, after 8 weeks
  -
    Measure: Change from Baseline in Snaith-Hamilton-Pleasure-Scale total score
    Description: patients with SPES depression >= 2
    Time frame: Baseline, after 8 weeks
  -
    Measure: Change from Baseline in Tremor Impact Scale (TIS-D) total score
    Time frame: Baseline, after 8 weeks
  -
    Measure: Change from Baseline in global Parkinson's Disease (PD) symptoms
    Description
      Akinesia, anhedonia, depression, dyskinesia, motor fluctuation, cognitive disturbances,
      rigor and tremor
    Time frame: Baseline, after 8 weeks
  -
    Measure: Global assessment of efficacy by investigator on a 5-point scale
    Time frame: after 8 weeks
  -
    Measure: Global assessment of tolerability by investigator on a 5-point scale
    Time frame: after 8 weeks
  -
    Measure: Number of patients with adverse events
    Time frame: up to 8 weeks
  -
    Measure: Number of patients with adverse drug reactions
    Time frame: up to 8 weeks
