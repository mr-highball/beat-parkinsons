BRIEF SUMMARY

The purpose of this study is to collect kinematic motion data from subjects with movement
disorders such as Parkinson's disease (PD) and essential tremor (ET) to develop and
validate algorithms for quantifying motor symptoms such as tremor, bradykinesia (slowed
movements), dyskinesias (sudden, involuntary movements), gait, and balance during
standardized tasks and/or activities of daily living.
