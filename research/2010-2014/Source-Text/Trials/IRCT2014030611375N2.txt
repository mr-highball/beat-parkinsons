Title: Walnut oil and lipid profiles in hyperlipidemic type 2 diabetes
Registry ID: IRCT2014030611375N2
Source: https://irct.ir/trial/11639
WHO bridge: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT2014030611375N2
First registered: 2014-04-09, retrospectively registered.
Coverage: Factual current official primary registry extraction; historical versions not recovered.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

The record targets 90 participants in a randomized double-blind parallel trial of walnut oil 15 mL/day for 90 days versus water-placebo capsules. Primary outcomes are triglycerides, LDL, HDL and total cholesterol; secondary outcomes include BMI and blood pressure. Ethics identifier is CT-P-92-5376.

Expected recruitment is October 10, 2013–January 10, 2014; actual fields are blank. The matched 2017 report PMID28394361 instead describes November 2012–April 2013 study dates. Its Results give 100 assessed, 96 randomized and 90 analyzed, while Trial Design broadly describes 100 patients. Preserve recruitment and target/randomization/analysis distinctions.

This registration differs from IRCT2014022216682N1 in identity, ethics and design. Similar published baseline summaries warrant checking participant overlap; separate protocol identifiers alone do not establish independent participants. The record does not register a Parkinson symptom, cognition or pain outcome.
