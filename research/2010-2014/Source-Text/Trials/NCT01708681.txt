Title: Lean Seafood Intake and Postprandial Metabolism
Publication date: 2012-10-17 (first public registry posting)
Record: NCT01708681
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01708681
Text coverage: Official registry API data, distilled.

The registry lists an interventional crossover comparing lean-seafood and nonseafood protein sources. It identifies postprandial lipids as the primary outcome and records total enrollment as 27. The primary publication reports 20 participants in its summarized analysis. This is one study family. The recorded protocol supplies no Parkinson fatigue or fall result.
