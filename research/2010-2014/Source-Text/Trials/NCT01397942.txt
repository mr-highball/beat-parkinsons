Title: Maximising the Taste and Health Value of Plant Food Products
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01397942
NCT ID: NCT01397942
Format: Selected official ClinicalTrials.gov fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public posting: 2011-07-20 (estimated by the registry)
Last public update: 2015-03-11 (estimated by the registry)
Status: Completed. Listed start May 2011, primary completion December 2014 and completion January 2015.
Design: Three parallel diets for adults with type 2 diabetes. Two arms specify 500 g daily of bitter/strong or mild/sweet vegetables and cabbages. A habitual Nordic diet is the control.
Outcomes: Glucose-tolerance insulin sensitivity and blood/urine biomarkers. The record describes a two-year outcome window and does not list blood pressure or standing symptoms. No tabular results were posted.
Linked publication: Thorup et al., PMID 34073610, records a 12-week intervention and secondary ambulatory blood pressure measurements. Registration and publication give different scopes for timing and endpoints.
