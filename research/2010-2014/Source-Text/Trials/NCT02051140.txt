Title: The Effect of Dietary Strawberry Supplementation on Older Adults
Publication date: 2014-01-31 (first public registry posting)
Record: NCT02051140
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02051140
Text coverage: Distilled official trial registration and outcome fields.

The study registered 54 participants and compared 12 g of freeze-dried strawberry powder twice daily for 90 days with a strawberry-flavored placebo powder. The registry lists cognition measures as primary and gait at follow-up as secondary. The current official JSON has no separate results module. The 2021 publication reports a 37-person per-protocol analysis. Its outcomes distinguish gait and postural sway from the Falls Efficacy Scale International, a measure of concern about falling. No observed fall-frequency comparison was reported.
