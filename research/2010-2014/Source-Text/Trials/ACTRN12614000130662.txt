Title: Essential Oils for Agitation Management in Older Adults
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=365702
Record: ACTRN12614000130662
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting or registration: 2014-02-04

Prospectively registered residential-care crossover. Unscented sunflower oil was the comparison for lavender and lemon balm essential oils. Two drops on lapel wadding remained in place for at least two hours a day, with two weeks per intervention and a two-week washout. Planned assessments included the Neuropsychiatric Inventory and Cohen-Mansfield Agitation Inventory. No oral oil exposure.
