Registered high-oleic sunflower comparator and cognitive battery
Source: https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=335243
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: official registry selected fields

The 2010 ANZCTR record ACTRN12610000212055 registered oral high-oleic sunflower oil as a comparator for DHA with cognitive measurements including working memory, Stroop and digit vigilance. Listed sunflower oil exposure was 2.25 g/day in capsules. Pressing grade was unspecified. The registration and Stonehouse 2013 publication describe one cohort.
