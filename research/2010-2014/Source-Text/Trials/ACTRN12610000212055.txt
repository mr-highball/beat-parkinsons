Title: Effects of docosahexaenoic acid supplementation on cognitive performance in healthy adults
Registry ID: ACTRN12610000212055

First registered: 2010-03-16
Record type: Distilled official Australian New Zealand Clinical Trials Registry registration

The prospective registration describes a six-month randomized trial in healthy adults aged 18 to 45. The active comparator consists of oral capsules with 2.25 g/day of high-oleic sunflower oil. The record lists working memory, long-term memory, processing and reaction times, Stroop and digit vigilance measurements. It provides no cold-pressing documentation. The comparison is DHA against an active oil, with no control condition that separately identifies a sunflower oil effect.

The registry's DHA dose line is printed with an ambiguous value and unit. Use the study's own Methods for verified doses. The registry explicitly links the single Stonehouse et al. publication, PMID 23515006.
