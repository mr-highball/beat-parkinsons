Title: The Effect of Macronutrients in the Diet on Digestive and Cardiovascular Health
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01510223
Data endpoint: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01510223
Format: Selected registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Sponsor: University of Limerick
Investigators: Oonagh Markey and Amir Shafat
First posted: 2012-01-16
Last posted: 2012-01-16
Overall status: Completed
Enrollment: 24 actual
Dates: Start August 2010, primary completion May 2011, study completion July 2011.
Study design: Randomized, participant-masked parallel comparison with three five-day dietary periods and healthy adults aged 18–35.
Intervention: High-fat dietary supplement with either 90 g olive oil, an olive-oil plus fish-powder blend, or 74.82 g olive oil and 15.18 g macadamia nut oil. Low-fat milkshake periods served as a comparison and deadaptation.
Primary outcome: Gastric emptying of a high-fat test meal by carbon-13 octanoic acid breath testing.
Other registered measures: Mouth-to-caecum transit by inulin breath testing, appetite, energy intake and substrate use.
Public results: No registry results. Trial outcomes by macadamia arm remain unverified.
