Title: Grape-seed proanthocyanidins and quality of life in menopausal women
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000010985
Registry: UMIN000009353
Publication date: 2012-11-19
Date basis: Official UMIN first public disclosure.
Coverage: Original registration, later update and linked article identity.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

The official UMIN record names three parallel randomized blinded arms for eight weeks, with 100 mg, 200 mg or no proanthocyanidin. Quality of life is the primary registered domain. The planned allocation total was 120. The linked 2014 article abstract reports 96 randomized and 91 completing. The completed registry, last updated September 26, 2015, provides no sleep-related numerical results, no Athens Insomnia Scale comparison and no actual group enrollment counts.

The registry directly links PMID 24518152. It corroborates the original trial's registration and intervention lineage. The primary results abstract gives a within-high-dose change only, without a placebo-relative AIS value. An extract tablet and a refined grapeseed oil preparation are materially different.
