BRIEF SUMMARY

Parkinson Disease is one of the most common neurodegenerative illnesses. First degree
relatives of Parkinson patient are in high risk for developing the disease. We will try
to detect early changes in brain metabolism before the appearance of Parkinson symptoms.

Participants: first degree relatives of diagnosed Parkinson patients that carry a gene
mutation in either LRRK2 or GBA genes.

The examination results will be given to the participants by a doctor from the neurology
department.
