The official record lists two dietary experiments. The acute crossover includes traditional canola, high-oleic canola, soybean, high-linoleic safflower, and coconut oils served in banana bread. The longer experiment compares provided food containing traditional canola oil against provided food containing a mixture intended to represent a typical Western diet. The comparator intervention name reads “safflower oil” while the description specifies a mixture.

The record names vascular measures and walking distance, claudication and cognitive function in the study description. The intervention used a peripheral arterial disease cohort. The record lists 53 actual participants, completed, and no registry results posted. The companion 2015 dissertation reports selected results for its 50-person eight-week comparison.
