Title: Deep Brain Stimulation for Patients With Dementia With Lewy Bodies
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02263937
NCT ID: NCT02263937
Publication date: 2014-10-13
Source depth: original ClinicalTrials.gov API registration; locally distilled

ClinicalTrials.gov lists first posting on 2014-10-13, last posted update 2016-10-31 and actual enrollment of six. The record is completed and has no posted registry results. The protocol expresses active/sham stimulation as ON versus OFF, with cognitive tests and clinical fluctuation assessment as primary outcomes. The North East Visual Hallucinations Interview and Neuropsychiatric Inventory are secondary measures. The 2019 conference report and 2020 journal report identify the same pilot. A registered endpoint alone supplies no outcome direction.
