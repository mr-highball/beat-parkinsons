Title: Effect of Perilla Extract on Improvement on Gastrointestinal Discomfort
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01931930
NCT ID: NCT01931930
Format: Selected official ClinicalTrials.gov fields paraphrased for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public posting: 2013-08-30 (estimated by the registry)
Status: Completed. July–November 2012, with 47 recorded participants and no tabular results.
Design: Randomized and double-blind comparison of perilla extract and a placebo in adults with gastrointestinal discomfort.
Outcomes: Daily gastrointestinal symptoms, constipation questionnaires, stool consistency, frequency and adverse events.
Linked original: PMID 24885816 enrolled 50 and reports 47 completers. The registry names maltodextrin for the placebo and the paper lists tapioca starch. The different placebo descriptions remain unresolved.
