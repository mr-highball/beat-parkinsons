BRIEF SUMMARY

Documentation of the effect of an direct or overlapping switch from another dopamine
agonist to Sifrol® on motor function, psychopathological disturbances and mood,
assessment of the reasons for the switch and the reasons for using Sifrol®, equivalent
doses at the end of the switch and tolerability of Sifrol® in ambulatory patients
suffering from idiopathic Parkinson's disease under routine conditions
