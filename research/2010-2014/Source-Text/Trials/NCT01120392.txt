BRIEF SUMMARY

Treatment with Nintendo wii is higher to physical therapy conventional in the quality of
life and motor performance in patients with Parkinson's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

The aim of this study is to assess if the treatment of rehabilitation with Nintendo wii
is higher to the conventional in the quality of life and motor performance in patients
with Parkinson's disease.
