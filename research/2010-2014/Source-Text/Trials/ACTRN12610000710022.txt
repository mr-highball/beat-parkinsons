ACTRN12610000710022. Submitted 2010-08-20. Registered 2010-08-26. Last update shown 2011-02-15. Sponsor: The Melbourne Clinic.
Adults 18–65 with major depression. Target 90. Eight-week masked comparison of DHA 260 mg/day, EPA 360 mg/day and sunflower oil 2 g/day. Where possible background antidepressants continued at a fixed dose. Planned follow-up put all arms on DHA.
Primary outcome: change in the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale from baseline to eight weeks. Secondary outcome: BDNF. Dates and target are planned. Official recruitment status field says not yet recruiting. Actual enrollment and results are unavailable here. The summary describes three arms and a common open-label extension; the design field gives crossover. The oil grade is unspecified.
