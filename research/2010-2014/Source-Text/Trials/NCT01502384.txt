BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a longitudinal study in healthy 1st degree relatives of patients with Parkinson's
Disease (PD) carriers of a genetic mutation in genes that are known to increase the risk
for PD. The purpose of this study is to explore the association between genetic mutations
in the known genes and early preclinical symptoms such as motor, sensory, autonomic,
behavioral and cognitive functions.
