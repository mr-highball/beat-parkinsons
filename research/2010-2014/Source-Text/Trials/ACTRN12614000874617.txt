Title: DHA fish oil in older adults with mild cognitive impairment
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=366439
Record: ACTRN12614000874617
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting or registration: 2014-08-14

Completed randomized trial. Seventy-two adults aged 60–90 were enrolled. The planned comparison was DHA and EPA capsules or three sunflower oil capsules daily with breakfast for 12 months. Cognitive tests included Trail Making A and B, with Geriatric Depression Scale and Geriatric Anxiety Inventory among the well-being measures. The registry gives no oil extraction or refining grade.
