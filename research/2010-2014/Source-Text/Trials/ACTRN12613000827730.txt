Title: Rice bran oil emulsion and planned solid swallowing tests
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=364612
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

ACTRN12613000827730, first registered July 26, 2013. The protocol describes a rice bran oil, soy lecithin, propylene glycol and water oral spray for radiotherapy-related dry mouth. Comparators were a cellulose saliva substitute and flavored water. The planned outcomes include the Test of Masticating and Swallowing Solids immediately and after a week. Inability to safely swallow solids is an exclusion. The 2017 paper by Apperley and colleagues reports the linked formulation trial. Its abstract describes all three products per participant, whereas the original registry describes allocation between two active-versus-water sequences.
