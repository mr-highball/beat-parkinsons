Title: Registered corn oil comparator and Epworth daytime sleepiness
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01216982
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

NCT01216982, first posted October 7, 2010, last posted March 9, 2011. A randomized military mood study assigned omega-3 ethyl esters or gelatin capsules containing one gram of corn oil each. The primary psychometric battery includes Epworth Sleepiness Scale. The register lists unknown status and no posted results in the November 2026 snapshot. The oil refining method was unspecified and there is no oil-free control. The direct identity between this registration and a potentially related 2014 report on deployed soldiers remains unverified.
