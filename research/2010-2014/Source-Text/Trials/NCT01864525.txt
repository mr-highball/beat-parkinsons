NCT01864525 | Effects of Octanoic Acid for Treatment of Essential Voice Tremor

Source: official ClinicalTrials.gov API record. First study posting 2013-05-29. Results first posted 2018-08-20. Sponsor Syracuse University, sponsor ID 370955-3.

The essential voice tremor crossover lists octanoic acid and an inactive matched capsule without specifying its contents. The primary outcomes are percent modulation of acoustic amplitude and fundamental frequency, computed from voice recordings. Listeners rated tremor severity in paired vowel and sentence samples as a secondary assessment. The results record lists 16 participants per within-person condition. The published study confirms the inactive capsule consisted of soybean oil and repeats the outcome values.
Sources: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01864525 and https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01864525
