Title: Effects of Octanoic Acid for Treatment of Essential Voice Tremor
Registry ID: NCT01864525
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01864525
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01864525
First posted: 2013-05-29
Results first posted: 2018-08-20
Record type: Distilled official trial registration

The Syracuse University crossover study assigned an active octanoic acid capsule and an inactive matching capsule in alternate periods, each approximately three weeks. The registration concerns essential voice tremor and specifically excludes Parkinson disease. Its principal outcomes are acoustic modulation of amplitude and fundamental frequency. A secondary outcome uses blinded listening ratings of paired vowel and sentence recordings.

The registration calls the comparator inactive. The investigators' final paper identifies the matching softgels as soybean oil. The published comparison and official results describe one cohort of 16. See the separate 2018 results and PMID 30585335 in the 2015–2019 bucket.
