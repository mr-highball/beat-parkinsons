Title: Polyunsaturated fatty acids in child nutrition, German PINGU study
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01487889
Registry ID: NCT01487889
Publication date: 2011-12-08
Coverage: Selected fields from the official ClinicalTrials.gov record, verified 2026-11-19.

The public record gives 2011-12-08 as the first-posted date and marks the field as estimated. It lists completion and actual overall enrollment of 214. The primary outcome is infant fatty acid status. Secondary outcomes include visual evoked potentials and Bayley II development at ten months, as well as nutritional and growth measures. The registry displays no posted summary results.

The source population is infants starting complementary foods. The 2015-online biomarker publication explicitly cites this ID. The 2019-online developmental publication names the PINGU study and reports the three matching feeding arms. These publications belong to one trial family and use different outcome and analysis denominators.
