Heart failure depression OCEAN registration
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02057406
Registration: NCT02057406
Coverage: Official registry, first posted February 7, 2014 (estimated)

OCEAN randomized adults with heart failure and major depressive disorder to a mixture of EPA and DHA, nearly pure EPA or corn-oil capsules for 12 weeks. The registry names Hamilton Depression Rating Scale and Beck Depression Inventory II outcomes. The posted results give 108 randomized evenly across three arms and were first released June 12, 2017. No separate no-oil group was assigned.
