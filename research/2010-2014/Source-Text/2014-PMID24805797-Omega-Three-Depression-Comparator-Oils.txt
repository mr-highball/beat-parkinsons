Original study scope
The depression meta-analysis lists trial controls, including olive oil or its ethyl esters, sunflower oil, coconut oil and a corn-oil mixture. It compares omega-3 interventions with control preparations. Neither placebo-arm symptom values nor aggregate treatment contrasts identify an effect of assigned culinary oil versus an appropriate oil-free comparator.
