Title: Effects of omega-3 supplementation on neurocognitive functioning and mood in deployed soldiers
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24690964/
PMID: 24690964
DOI: 10.7205/MILMED-D-13-00395
Publication date: 2014-04
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Original author abstract and explicitly attributed secondary numerical lead.

The author abstract describes 44 and 34 participants in two arms and a corn-oil ethyl-ester comparator. It reports an association between the omega-3 index and daytime sleepiness, without providing a corn-ester group effect or the original Epworth table. The target exposure was not refined culinary corn oil and the participants were not a Parkinson cohort.

A subsequent review reports Epworth values of 10.7 ± 4.5 for omega-3 and 10.0 ± 4.3 for the comparator, p=.247. It lists n=106, compared with 78 across the two groups in the original abstract. Analysis population and time point are not confirmed. This is a secondary numerical lead, not independent evidence or an original outcome check.
