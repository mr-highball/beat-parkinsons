Title: Unsaturated fat intakes and mental health outcomes in young women from the Australian Longitudinal Study on Women's Health
Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/S1368980014000561
PMID: 24717118
DOI: 10.1017/S1368980014000561
Publication date: 2014-04-09
Date basis: Published online in 2014. Print issue followed in 2015.
Accessed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: publisher full text and adjusted regression table.

The cross-sectional analysis included 7,635 young Australian women. The adjusted odds ratio for self-reported diagnosed anxiety per additional gram of estimated dietary oleic acid was 1.02 (95% CI 1.00–1.05, p=.046). The upper and lower bounds are rounded in the original. Estimates for non-diagnostic anxiety symptoms and the SF-36 mental-health measure were near null for oleic acid. The dietary model estimates a nutrient across foods, gives no pressing grade, and cannot assign these findings to olive oil or symptom severity in Parkinson disease.
