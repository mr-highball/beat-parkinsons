Title: Preparation and evaluation of functional foods in adjuvant arthritis
DOI: 10.3989/gya.130811
Source: https://grasasyaceites.revistas.csic.es/index.php/grasasyaceites/article/download/1393/1389/1387
Publication: 2012-12-30, volume 63 issue 4, pp. 394–402
Coverage: Original journal PDF, intervention table, Figure 1 and Table 5.

The rat experiment compares jam formulations containing isolated date-fruit extract, fenugreek extract, fish oil, evening primrose oil, or combinations. Jam occupies 23% of the diets described in the original formulation table. Table 5 reports swelling of 0.10 cm (SE 0.013) for the date-extract jam and 0.21 cm (SE 0.015) for the arthritic control. The table reports 52% inhibition. Fish-oil plus date-extract jam is a separate combined arm.

The measured outcome is paw swelling in an arthritis model. The article does not report pain behavior or a response to whole dried dates. The formulation study cites the 2004 separate extract experiment and adds a distinct preparation comparison.
