Original source scope and evidence boundary
The randomized double-blind crossover compares caffeine plus L-theanine with placebo. The experiment favors the blend for task switching, while reported subjective alertness and intersensory attention did not clearly change. The abstract does not provide a separate fatigue contrast. The ingredients match the 2010 Giesbrecht blend, and Rycroft and De Bruin appear on both reports. Assessment timing and tasks differ. Cohort overlap is unverified, and the study does not establish an independent coffee, brewed green tea or Parkinson fatigue result.
