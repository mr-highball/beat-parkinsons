Title: Anti-apoptotic and anti-inflammatory effect of Piperine on 6-OHDA induced Parkinson's rat model.
PMID: 22819561
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2012.03.018
First published: 2013
Authors: Shrivastava P, Vaibhav K, Tabassum R, Khan A, Ishrat T, Khan MM
Journal: The Journal of nutritional biochemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22819561/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled 6-OHDA PD rat experiment evaluating isolated piperine (10 mg/kg body weight). Piperine attenuated striatal lipid peroxidation, preserved GSH, suppressed cytochrome-c/caspase-3/caspase-9/TNF-alpha/IL-1beta, reduced apomorphine-induced contralateral rotations, and improved narrow-beam crossing and rotarod motor coordination/balance. Limits: isolated piperine in rodent neurotoxin model, not culinary cracked/ground pepper in human PD.
