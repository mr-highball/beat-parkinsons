Title: Caffeine consumption and risk of dyskinesia in CALM-PD.
PMID: 23339054
PMCID: PMC3608707
DOI: 10.1002/mds.25319
First published: 2013
Authors: Wills AM, Eberly S, Tennis M, Lang AE, Messing S, Togasaki D, et al.
Journal: Movement disorders : official journal of the Movement Disorder Society
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3608707/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Observational cohort analysis of the CALM-PD trial and CALM Cohort extension examining baseline self-reported coffee intake and time to dyskinesia in Parkinson disease using adjusted Cox models. Compared with <4 oz/d coffee, adjusted HR for dyskinesia was 0.61 (95% CI 0.37-1.01) for >12 oz/d and 0.73 (95% CI 0.46-1.15) for 4-12 oz/d (p-trend=0.05). Limits: observational baseline coffee (not brewed black tea) with 95% CIs crossing 1.0 and contrasting with later randomized Café-PD trial dyskinesia increase.
