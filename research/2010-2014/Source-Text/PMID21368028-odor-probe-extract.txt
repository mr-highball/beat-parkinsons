Title: Memory for the Order of Events in Specific Sequences: Contributions of the Hippocampus and Medial Prefrontal Cortex
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3081724/
PMID: 21368028
PMCID: PMC3081724
DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.4202-10.2011
Publication: 2 March 2011. Journal of Neuroscience 31, 3169–3175.
Text coverage: Original factual extraction from Methods, Figure 3 and Table 1.

In separate mouse groups the authors compared the effects of prefrontal and hippocampal lesions with sham procedure on odor sequence memory. Table 1 summarizes several odor pairs in each probe category. Tarragon figured as N in the training procedure and was probed with orange, cilantro and onion. The pooled within-sequence preference index was 0.28 ± 0.04 for sham, 0.01 ± 0.06 after hippocampal lesions and -0.08 ± 0.05 after medial prefrontal lesions. The pooled novelty analysis had p = .271 for the group comparison. Multiple odor pairs and no intervention assignment by tarragon distinguish these endpoints from a tarragon efficacy test.
