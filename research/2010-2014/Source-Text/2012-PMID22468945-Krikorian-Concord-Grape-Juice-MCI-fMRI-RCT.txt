Title: Concord grape juice supplementation and neurocognitive function in human aging.
PMID: 22468945
DOI: 10.1021/jf300277g
First published: 2012
Authors: Krikorian R, Boespflug EL, Fleck DE, Stein AL, Wightman JD, Shidler MD, Sadat-Hossieny S
Journal: Journal of agricultural and food chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22468945/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 16-week trial in 21 older adults (68-90 y) with mild cognitive impairment consuming Concord grape juice vs placebo. Grape juice significantly reduced semantic interference on memory tasks (frontostriatal/executive interference control) and produced relatively greater right-hemisphere anterior/posterior activation on fMRI. Limits: small MCI sample (n=21), juice matrix, no PD cohort.
