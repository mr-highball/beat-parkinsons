Title: Effect of Carum carvi, a herbal bioenhancer on pharmacokinetics of antitubercular drugs: A study in healthy human volunteers
Authors: Naiyma Choudhary and colleagues
Date: 2014-04 to 2014-06 issue
Identifiers: PMID 24741485; PMCID PMC3980549; DOI 10.4103/2229-3485.128027
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3980549/
Coverage: Original factual extract of Methods and Table 2.

The single-dose, open-label two-period crossover enrolled 20 healthy men. Every participant first received the fixed combination of rifampicin, isoniazid and pyrazinamide, then repeated it with 100 mg of caraway extract after a ten-day washout. The extraction and standardization method is attributed to Sachin et al. (PMID 19734267). Plasma HPLC measurements yielded 0–24-hour areas under the curve of 40.11 then 53.01 for rifampicin, 17.72 then 22.87 for isoniazid and 107.65 then 137.71 for pyrazinamide. This is a fixed-order human comparison for these three drugs. P-glycoprotein activity, levodopa exposure and motor symptoms were not measured.
