Coverage: Original PMC text, Figures 2–3, Tables 5 and 7 and discussion. Distilled factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

The postmortem series included 92 people, including 17 classified with Parkinson disease and nine with dementia with Lewy bodies. The investigators observed pathological staining in submandibular gland and lower esophageal tissue. Denominators differ by specimen availability and additional sections. The discussion distinguishes poor clearing of saliva from overproduction. The design gives a tissue comparison, without a prospectively measured drooling outcome or a food exposure.
