The formulation combines Bala (Sida cordifolia), cow's milk and sesame oil. The investigators compared the full formula in rats exposed to quinolinic acid, measuring oxidative-stress biomarkers, membrane enzyme measures and histology. The group description in Methods and the enumerated groups in the dose-finding phase differ. Preserve the phase and table denominators and avoid pooling the phases as an additional independent experiment.

The publication describes traditional use for central nervous system complaints and insomnia. PMID 25829772 cites it when introducing sesame-oil use. Its actual comparison is the full formula. No separate sesame-oil-only arm isolates its role, no toasted kitchen oil was given and no Parkinson daily off time, sleep symptom or clinical neurological outcome was measured.
