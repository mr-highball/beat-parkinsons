Café-PD, ClinicalTrials.gov NCT01738178. First public posting November 30, 2012 (registry date type estimated).
Official registry JSON: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01738178
Linked original: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5664303/ (PMID 28954882, published 2017).
Coverage: official current status, enrollment, intervention and secondary outcomes checked November 16, 2026, plus the linked original report.

The trial compares caffeine capsules and placebo in Parkinson disease. The registry includes fatigue among individual MDS-UPDRS items planned for six-month assessments. The current registry reports 119 actual enrollment and no posted results. The linked publication reports that 121 patients were randomized, with 118 available for the primary analysis. The source of the count difference is unresolved.

The publication says it examined individual MDS-UPDRS items and prints a favorable daytime somnolence comparison at six months, attenuated later. Daytime somnolence is distinct from fatigue severity. No fatigue-specific group result was located in the main text or main tables. The authors direct readers to online supplemental tables; the fatigue item remains an original data or supplement lead.
