Title: Baseline reports in people seeking caffeine-dependence treatment
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3642245/
PMID: 22369218
PMCID: PMC3642245
DOI: 10.1037/a0027246
Publication date: 2012-02-27
Date basis: First electronic publication.
Review depth: Complete identified PMC structured original and intake symptom description.

Ninety-four treatment seekers underwent an intake assessment for caffeine consumption and dependence symptoms. Participants recalled previous abstinence symptoms and impairment. The report gives a symptom distribution and functional impairment with the diagnoses derived from interview. It does not provide a randomized trial fatigue effect.

The 2015 electronic publication PMID 26501499 describes the intervention participants as drawn from the previously documented intake sample. This baseline paper supplies cohort and reported-withdrawal context. It can be linked to the randomized intervention without counting a separate trial family. No prospective fatigue trajectory in diagnosed Parkinson disease was measured.
