Factual summary
The original studies isolated sodium benzoate and reports changes in DJ-1 and other Parkinson-related protein markers mainly in cultured cells. Whole cinnamon is discussed as a precursor. Parkinson rigidity and rest tremor are listed as manifestations. The experiment did not compare ground cinnamon or raw fig and did not grade passive rigidity or rest tremor.
