Title: Jangra mangiferin mouse behavior contrasts
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25064341/
PMID: 25064341
DOI: 10.1016/j.ejphar.2014.07.031
Publication date: 2014-07-23
Date basis: First electronic publication in the NLM record.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract. Publisher redirects did not provide the full article.

The authors report that isolated mangiferin pretreatment improved sucrose preference and social interaction in mice with an experimental inflammatory challenge. They also report less immobility on two behavioral tests and changes in inflammation and oxidative stress markers. The abstract reports selected favorable contrasts without the full group statistics.

The compound is linked to mango plant material, including bark and stem. The assigned compound, pretreatment design and behavioral proxy outcomes do not establish an effect of ingested dried mango flesh, clinical depression, or Parkinson disease.
