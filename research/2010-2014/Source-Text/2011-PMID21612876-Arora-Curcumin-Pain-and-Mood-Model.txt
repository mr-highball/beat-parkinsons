Title: Curcumin ameliorates reserpine-induced pain-depression dyad
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21612876/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0306453011001363
PMID: 21612876
DOI: 10.1016/j.psyneuen.2011.04.012
Publication date: 2011-05-25
Date basis: First electronic publication in PubMed. Print issue November 2011.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: abstract

The author abstract describes separate mechanical withdrawal and forced-swim measures in a rat pain and mood model. It reports less pain-related hypersensitivity and behavioral improvement with injected isolated curcumin. The mechanical thresholds differ from self-reported pain severity. The model concerns drug-induced pain and depression rather than a clinical Parkinson cohort.

Claim 0812 concerns ground turmeric and Parkinson pain severity. This separate study gives a preclinical pain proxy. The exposure, route and population limit transfer. Full results with arm sizes, separate threshold contrasts, variance and any motor confounding assessment remain open. The review PMID:22895696 cites this experiment.
