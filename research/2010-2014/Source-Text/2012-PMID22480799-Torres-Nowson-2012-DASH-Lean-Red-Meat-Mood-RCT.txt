Title: A moderate-sodium DASH-type diet improves mood in postmenopausal women.
PMID: 22480799
DOI: 10.1016/j.nut.2011.11.029
First published: 2012
Authors: Torres SJ, Nowson CA
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22480799/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

14-week RCT in n=95 postmenopausal women comparing a moderate-sodium DASH diet including daily lean red meat vs a healthy diet control: anger improved between groups (p<0.05), while within-study combined correlation showed higher red meat intake correlated inversely with POMS depression (r=-0.21, p<0.05). Limits: whole-diet intervention, observational correlation for red meat vs depression, non-PD cohort.
