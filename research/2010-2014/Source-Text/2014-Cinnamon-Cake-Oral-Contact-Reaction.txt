Title: Treatment of cinnamon contact allergy – case description
Source: https://forumpediatrow.pl/files/article/609.pdf
Publication date: 2014
Citation: Magazyn Stomatologiczny, issue 4, pp. 56–57.
Coverage: Full original Polish case report and figure captions, distilled.

The patient developed a hard-palate blister shortly after eating cake containing cinnamon. When the blister ruptured within 24 hours, the lesion was reported to be very painful and interfered with speech and food intake. The product was an actual culinary preparation.
The report leaves the species, quantity, physical form of the cinnamon and other cake ingredients unverified. The case does not document a controlled rechallenge or cinnamon-specific diagnostic result. The suspected contact reaction supplies an oral pain safety context, with attribution to cinnamon and transfer to Parkinson pain unresolved.
