Title: Safranal enhances non-rapid eye movement sleep in pentobarbital-treated mice.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6493539/
PMID: 22632633
PMCID: PMC6493539
DOI: 10.1111/j.1755-5949.2012.00334.x
Publication date: 2012-05-28
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC complete article HTML.

This mouse study combined isolated safranal and an experimental sedative context. EEG and EMG capture non-REM latency, duration, stage transitions and sleep activity. The measured outcomes concern a constituent and a laboratory sleep model. They do not estimate ground-saffron effects on Parkinson insomnia or depression.
