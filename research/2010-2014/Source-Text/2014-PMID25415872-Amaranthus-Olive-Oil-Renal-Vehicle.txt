Title: Carbon tetrachloride-induced kidney damage and protective effect of Amaranthus lividus L. in rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25415872/
PMID: 25415872
DOI: 10.1177/0748233714555390
Publication date: 2014-11-20
Date basis: First electronic publication in NLM. The journal issue is June 2016.
Text coverage: Distilled original author abstract, including stated administration route and effects.

The study tested oral water extract of Amaranthus lividus in male Wistar rats with an induced kidney injury. Olive oil carried the injected toxicant. Antioxidant and histological results concern renal injury and the plant-extract interventions. There is no reported oral cold-pressed olive oil anxiety comparison. An overlapping author team's 2015 publication and 2011 poster describe liver findings with similar intervention schedules. The abstract alone does not establish whether the kidney and liver studies used the same animals.
