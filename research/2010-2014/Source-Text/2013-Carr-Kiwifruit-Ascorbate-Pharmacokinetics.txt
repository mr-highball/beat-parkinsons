Fresh Sungold fruit and plasma and urinary ascorbate
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3847741/

November 11, 2013. Nine healthy men took 1.5 Sungold kiwifruit without the skin or approximately 200 mg tablet vitamin C in different sessions. One participant did not complete the tablet session. Table 1 reports fruit versus tablet plasma ascorbate AUC of 237 versus 220 h × micromoles per liter (p = .483) and urinary AUC of 856 versus 618 h × micromoles per millimole of creatinine (p = .004). Here concentration denotes an analyte. No attention performance or Parkinson cohort was studied.
