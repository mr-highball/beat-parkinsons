This open-label extension enrolled 84 participants from the randomized RECOVER study. The authors reported that 79% completed one year and that hallucinations occurred as treatment-emergent adverse events in 13%. The abstract groups hallucinations across modalities. The official NCT00519532 results enumerate 11 affected participants and 22 events, also under a general hallucination term. They describe the same extension group.

The separately reported randomized parent trial and its visual-hallucination serious adverse event belong to the double-blind phase. The extension had no parallel control group and did not measure dried fig exposure. The full publication's item tables and visual subtype coding remain to be verified.
