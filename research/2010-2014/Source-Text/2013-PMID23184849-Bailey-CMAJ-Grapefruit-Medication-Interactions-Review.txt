Title: Grapefruit-medication interactions: forbidden fruit or avoidable consequences?
PMID: 23184849
PMCID: PMC3589309
DOI: 10.1503/cmaj.120951
First published: 2013
Authors: Bailey DG, Dresser G, Arnold JM
Journal: CMAJ : Canadian Medical Association journal = journal de l'Association medicale canadienne
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23184849/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Authoritative CMAJ clinical pharmacology review documenting >85 medications interacting with grapefruit (fresh fruit and juice) via irreversible intestinal CYP3A4 inactivation, with >43 capable of serious adverse events including symptomatic/orthostatic hypotension, dizziness, syncope, excessive sedation, QT prolongation/torsades de pointes, and neurotoxicity—explicitly encompassing PD-relevant medications such as quetiapine, pimavanserin-class CYP3A4 antipsychotics, domperidone, ergot dopamine agonists, felodipine/nifedipine, tamsulosin/silodosin, sildenafil, triazolam, and oxycodone.
