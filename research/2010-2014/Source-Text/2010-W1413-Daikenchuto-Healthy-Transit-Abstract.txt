The conference abstract describes 60 healthy volunteers randomized to two daily amounts of Daikenchuto or placebo for five days. Six-hour colonic filling, right colon emptying and general stool outcomes are separate outcomes. Design, investigators, enrollment and endpoints match the full-journal abstract and registry. Count the conference and journal versions as one study.
