Khasnavis and Pahan tested ground Ceylon cinnamon, identified in Reagents as Cinnamomum verum, in young MPTP-exposed mice. Cinnamon 100 mg/kg/day in 0.5% methylcellulose was orally gavaged starting three hours after the last toxin injection; MPTP controls received vehicle. This is early post-insult treatment, without a delayed established-disease treatment comparison.

At seven days, Figure 8 reports favorable rotarod and open-field measures, including horizontal activity, movement time, distance and rest time, with six mice per group. The source supplies no human walking-speed estimate. General activity and coordination are related to, but do not equal, gait velocity. Parkin/DJ-1 and dopaminergic-marker findings are mechanistic support, not additional clinical outcomes.

The separate sodium-benzoate arm used 50 mg/kg/day with a sodium-formate control. Benzoate is described as a downstream metabolite; its isolated-arm results must not be assigned to cinnamon. The 2019 MPTP and 2020 A53T reports are later experiments from the same research program, not independent human replication.
