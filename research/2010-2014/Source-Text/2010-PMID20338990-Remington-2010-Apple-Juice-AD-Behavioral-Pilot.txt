Title: Apple juice improved behavioral but not cognitive symptoms in moderate-to-late stage Alzheimer's disease in an open-label pilot study.
PMID: 20338990
PMCID: PMC10845592
DOI: 10.1177/1533317510363470
First published: 2010
Authors: Remington R, Chan A, Lepore A, Kotlya E, Shea TB
Journal: American journal of Alzheimer's disease and other dementias
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20338990/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Open-label 1-month clinical pilot in n=21 institutionalized adults with moderate-to-severe Alzheimer disease consuming two 4-oz glasses of apple juice daily. No change occurred in cognitive Dementia Rating Scale or ADCS-ADL scores, but institutional caregivers reported an approximate 27% improvement (p<0.01) in Neuropsychiatric Inventory (NPI) behavioral and psychotic symptoms, with the largest reductions in anxiety, agitation, and delusions. Limits: uncontrolled single-arm design, Alzheimer disease rather than PD, and apple juice rather than baked or raw whole apple.
