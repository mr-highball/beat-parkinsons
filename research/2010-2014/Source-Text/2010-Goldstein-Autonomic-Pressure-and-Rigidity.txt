Orthostatic blood pressure falls and baseline rigidity
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2809034/

January 19, 2010 issue. Goldstein and colleagues assessed 23 people with Parkinson disease for olfaction and autonomic function. The cohort had baseline cogwheel rigidity. The relevant fall refers to a drop in systolic blood pressure during Valsalva testing or orthostasis. The paper uses composite parkinsonism scores. It gives no frequency of body falls, rigidity-specific change or basil intervention.
