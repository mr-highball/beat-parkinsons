Title: An interesting case that included visual hallucinations in a patient with severe hypomagnesaemia and hypocalcaemia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3233929/
PMID: 22669770
PMCID: PMC3233929
DOI: 10.1136/bcr.10.2011.5051
Publication date: 2011-12-06
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML case report; PMC JATS supplies front matter; local research digest

The original case describes a sudden, hours-long episode of visual hallucinations with severe hypomagnesemia and hypocalcemia. The case presentation says the hallucinations had already resolved when blood tests were ordered the following day. Later overall symptoms resolved during magnesium and active vitamin D treatment. Distinguish the first visual episode from general improvement during the admission. The patient had no reported Parkinson diagnosis and no documented dried fig exposure.
