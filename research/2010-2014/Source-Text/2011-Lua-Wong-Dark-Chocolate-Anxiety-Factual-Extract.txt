Publication: Lua and Wong, ASEAN Journal of Psychiatry 12(2), July–December 2011.
Coverage: Original published Methods and Table 4, factual extract.
Source: https://www.aseanjournalofpsychiatry.org/articles/can-dark-chocolate-alleviate-anxiety-depressive-and-stress-symptoms-among-trainee-nurses-a-parallel-openlabel-study.pdf

The open-label randomized comparison included 47 nursing students, 25 with dark chocolate and 22 with mineral water. Post-intervention medians were 5 versus 7 for HADS anxiety (p = .001) and 10 versus 16 for DASS anxiety (p < .001). Duration was three days. The groups knew their food assignment, the control lacked a matched snack, and the sample did not include people with Parkinson disease. Some table ranges are internally inconsistent.
