Apathy scale and quality of life domains in Parkinson disease
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4344415/

First available in 2014. In the 107-person retrospective Parkinson analysis, apathy was associated with greater impairment in PDQ-39 quality of life (r = .241). Trait anxiety accounted for the most independent variation in the overall association. The report also examines the bodily discomfort domain. It supplies mood and quality-of-life context without a separate food or pain-severity comparison.
