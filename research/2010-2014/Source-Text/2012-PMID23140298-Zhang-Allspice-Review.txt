Narrative review (PMC3891794) of Jamaican pepper / allspice (Pimenta dioica [L.] Merr., Myrtaceae) phytochemistry, ethnobotany, and pharmacology. Key fruit essential-oil and aqueous-extract constituents include eugenol, gallic acid, and ericifolin. Documents Caribbean and Central American traditional uses (Jamaican hot tea for colds, dysmenorrhea, and dyspepsia; Costa Rican use for dyspepsia and diabetes; Guatemalan topical crushed berries for bruises, sore joints, and myalgia; Cuban medicine and British Pharmacopoeia 1898 pimenta preparations). Reports no human randomized clinical trials of isolated ground allspice for Parkinson's disease motor or non-motor symptoms.
