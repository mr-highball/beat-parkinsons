Title: Guineensine is a novel inhibitor of endocannabinoid uptake showing cannabimimetic behavioral effects in BALB/c mice.
PMID: 24412246
DOI: 10.1016/j.phrs.2013.12.010
First published: 2014
Authors: Nicolussi S, Viveros-Paredes JM, Gachet MS, Rau M, Flores-Soto ME, Blunder M
Journal: Pharmacological research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24412246/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical pharmacology study characterizing guineensine, a minor N-isobutylamide constituent of Piper nigrum, as a nanomolar endocannabinoid uptake inhibitor (EC50=290 nM). In BALB/c mice, isolated guineensine dose-dependently induced cannabimimetic tetrad behaviors including strong catalepsy, hypolocomotion, hypothermia, and analgesia (catalepsy and analgesia blocked by CB1 antagonist rimonabant). Competing minor-constituent preclinical signal; trace in culinary pepper.
