Table 1 prints baseline means and SD of 6.65 ± 2.76 for fennel and 6.39 ± 2.71 for control. End-point values are 2.88 ± 2.20 and 6.39 ± 2.71. The comparison did not test toasted seed or Parkinson fatigue.

November 26 original and extraction reconciliation

The original author abstract (PMID 25085020) gives 40 participants randomized per group. The Cochrane extraction describes 38 controls analyzed and 40 in the fennel arm. Its characteristics table identifies self-rated weakness on a 0–10 cm visual scale. The original Table 1 control result of 6.39 is retained over the abstract's 6.65. The original is an unblinded comparison against no medication for menstrual weakness.
