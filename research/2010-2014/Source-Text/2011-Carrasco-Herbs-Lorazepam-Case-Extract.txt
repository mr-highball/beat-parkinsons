Title: Self-directed valerian and passionflower with lorazepam: case extract
Source: Carrasco et al., Medicina Naturista 5(2):67–70; https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/3695419.pdf
Publication date: 2011
Coverage: Full Spanish case and discussion; factual paraphrase

The patient reported hand tremor, dizziness and sedation after valerian-root and passionflower-aerial-part products with prescribed lorazepam. Earlier infusions and later dry extracts differed. Tremor was not observed during examination. Follow-up improved after herbs stopped while lorazepam continued. Multiple substances and anxiety prevent component-specific attribution; no Parkinson diagnosis or rest-tremor score is reported.

Related prior report: PMID 19441067, DOI 10.1002/ptr.2847, 2009, same distinctive case; not an independent event. These identifiers belong to the earlier report, not this Spanish article. Its full body remains uninspected. The suggested interaction is not confirmed causation. Participant identifying details and amounts are omitted.
