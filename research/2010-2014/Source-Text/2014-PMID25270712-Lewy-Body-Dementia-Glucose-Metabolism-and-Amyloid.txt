Title: Regional glucose metabolic reduction in dementia with Lewy bodies is independent of amyloid deposition.
PMID: 25270712
PMCID: PMC4835511
DOI: 10.1007/s12149-014-0911-0
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4835511/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2014-10-01
Coverage: complete PMC JATS.

The PET study contrasts patterns of regional glucose metabolism and amyloid labeling in Lewy body dementia and Alzheimer disease. Hallucinations appear among the clinical features in the diagnosis and case table. The imaging comparison identifies no dried fig exposure or diet-linked symptom measure.
