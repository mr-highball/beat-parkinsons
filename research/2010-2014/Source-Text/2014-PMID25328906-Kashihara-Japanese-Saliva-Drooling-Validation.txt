The validation compares distributions from 365 Japanese and 876 English-language assessments. The combined patient-reported item 2.2 covers saliva and drooling. Table 4 shows score zero for 186 Japanese and 341 English-language assessments, with two and seven missing, respectively. The table separately reports chewing and swallowing. This cohort comparison validates a translation and does not attribute group differences to diet or assign raw fig.
