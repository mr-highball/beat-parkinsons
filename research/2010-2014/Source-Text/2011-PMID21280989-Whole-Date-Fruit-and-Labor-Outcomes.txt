Title: The effect of late pregnancy consumption of date fruit on labour and delivery
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21280989/
PMID: 21280989
DOI: 10.3109/01443615.2010.522267
Publication date: 2011-01
Date basis: Journal of Obstetrics and Gynaecology 31(1), pp. 29–31. NLM issue month.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract

The prospective study compared 69 late-pregnancy whole date-fruit consumers with 45 nonconsumers. Spontaneous onset occurred in 96% versus 79% (p=.024) and mean latent-phase duration was 510 versus 906 minutes (p=.044). The summary reports induced or augmented labor and cervical findings. It does not identify a date preparation by drying state or report standalone pain scores. A later review lists the whole fruit as Tamer. Shorter latent phase and pain severity are different endpoints.
