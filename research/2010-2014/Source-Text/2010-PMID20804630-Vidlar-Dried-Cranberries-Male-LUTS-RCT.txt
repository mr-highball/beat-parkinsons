Title: The effectiveness of dried cranberries ( Vaccinium macrocarpon) in men with lower urinary tract symptoms.
PMID: 20804630
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114510002059
First published: 2010
Authors: Vidlar A, Vostalova J, Ulrichova J, Student V, Stejskal D, Reichenbach R, Vrbkova J, Ruzicka F
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20804630/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized open-label controlled trial (Vidlar et al., 2010, Br J Nutr) enrolling 42 older men with LUTS, elevated PSA, negative prostate biopsy, and chronic non-bacterial prostatitis assigned to 1,500 mg/day dried powdered cranberries (n=21) vs no-cranberry control (n=21) for 6 months. The dried cranberry group showed statistically significant improvements in IPSS, quality of life, urinary flow rates, post-void residual urine volume, and lower total PSA at day 180, with no significant improvement in controls. Limits: small sample (n=42), unblinded no-placebo control, non-PD male LUTS cohort, pooled IPSS rather than isolated urgency count.
