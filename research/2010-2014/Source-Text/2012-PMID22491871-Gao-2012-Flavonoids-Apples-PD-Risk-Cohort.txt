Title: Habitual intake of dietary flavonoids and risk of Parkinson disease.
PMID: 22491871
PMCID: PMC3320056
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0b013e31824f7fc4
First published: 2012
Authors: Gao X, Cassidy A, Schwarzschild MA, Rimm EB, Ascherio A
Journal: Neurology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22491871/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Prospective cohort analysis of Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study (>130,000 participants; 805 incident PD cases over 20-22 years). Higher dietary flavonoid intake (notably anthocyanins and berries/apples in food-based analyses) was inversely associated with PD risk, especially in men. Disease-incidence epidemiology only; does not measure symptom severity in diagnosed PD.
