Title: The influence of the polyunsaturated fatty acids on body weight and anxiolytic-like behavior in female rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23321284/
PMID: 23321284
DOI: 10.1179/1476830512Y.0000000007
Publication date: 2012-12-04
Date basis: PubMed electronic release December 4, 2012. Journal issue published in February 2013.
Coverage: Original author abstract.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

Female Wistar rats were assigned to standard chow or chow enriched with fish, linseed or soybean oil for thirty days. The authors describe longer open-arm time with the linseed diet versus regular chow. Weight did not differ across the compared diets in the author abstract. The indexed abstract provides no group means, sample size, oil processing, matched constituent exposure or independent locomotor result. This is a separate rodent diet comparison with an anxiety-like behavioral endpoint and no Parkinson anxiety measure.
