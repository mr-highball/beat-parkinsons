Title: Elevated blood harmane concentrations in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3915406/?report=reader
PMCID: PMC3915406
PMID: 24300779
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuro.2013.11.005
First public article date: 2013-12-01
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated full original author manuscript review

This study compares 113 people with Parkinson disease and 101
controls. Mean log blood harmane was 0.59 versus 0.27 in the
source's reported scale (p<.001). The adjusted association with
Parkinson case status had OR 2.54 (95% CI 1.55–4.16).

In the chart-derived subgroups, the 35 tremor-predominant and 13
postural-instability/gait-difficulty cases had mean log results
of 0.80 and 0.72 (p=.72). The authors cite small subgroups and
recommend a continuous tremor measure for future investigation.
The samples were generally nonfasting. No baked-turkey intake or
assigned exposure is measured, and the phenotype comparison
does not give a separate rest-tremor severity estimate.
