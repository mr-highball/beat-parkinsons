Pain and loss of interest items in a Parkinson fluctuation instrument
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3153935/

August 9, 2011. Eleven patients with Parkinson motor and nonmotor fluctuations and eleven caregivers participated in instrument development. The appendix asks about painful sensations, loss of interest in prior activities and the timing of each relative to levodopa. The item content identifies the constructs and gives no food or efficacy comparison.
