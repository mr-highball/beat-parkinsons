Source depth: Full original poster abstract P01.36.

The investigators tested six marketed preparations. The poster reports two near their labels and four substantially below the labels, with two below 10% of the listed amount. A later same-team article tested six preparations and reports a different distribution around label claims. The repeated sample count, investigators and assay goal make shared material plausible, with product-level overlap unverified. The poster measures constituent content, without patient or drooling outcomes.
