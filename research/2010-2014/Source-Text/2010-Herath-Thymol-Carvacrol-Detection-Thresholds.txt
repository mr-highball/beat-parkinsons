Sensory panel thresholds for isolated thymol and carvacrol
Primary source: https://vuir.vu.edu.au/15840/1/Rupika_Herath_2010.pdf
Coverage: factual extract from institutional primary thesis and Table 4.22.

Herath’s 2010 Victoria University thesis measured isolated odorant thresholds for a food-packaging sensory panel. Table 4.22 reports detected concentrations of 0.01 ppm thymol and 1.00 ppm carvacrol. Its estimated 50%-detection thresholds were 0.21 ppm and 2.62 ppm, respectively. The same table reverses the compound assignments of Bitar’s published sunflower-oil comparison in its literature column. The figures in the Bitar original are carvacrol 30.97 mg/kg and thymol 124 mg/kg. The two original within-study rankings differ under their study conditions. Neither tested dried summer savory or treatment of olfactory impairment.
