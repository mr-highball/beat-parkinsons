2011 spearmint gum comparison and salivary flow timing
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3556288/
PMID: 23372599
PMCID: PMC3556288
Coverage: Original full report, Methods and Results

Karami-Nogourani and colleagues studied five sugar-free gum flavors in 15 dental students over six days. Collected saliva increased while subjects chewed compared with unstimulated samples. This report describes an early peak across flavors and no significant flavor differences in the three-to-six-minute window. Cinnamon and spearmint flavors raised saliva pH. A 2012 report shares the volunteer count, scheduled windows, named flavors and three authors, but describes a later spearmint flow peak. The reports may overlap. Neither provides a dried-leaf exposure, Parkinson cohort or a measure of lost saliva.
