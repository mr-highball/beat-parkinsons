Sakaue M, Maeda K, Oba M, Tojo H, Ito J. Japan Journal of Aromatherapy 14(1):8–14. Issued March 31, 2014. J-STAGE posted the archival copy March 31, 2026.

The Japanese methods identify Macadamia integrifolia oil from Meadows. A separate experiment randomized 14 healthy nurses, seven per group, to topical macadamia or coconut oil and measured glucose after an oral glucose load. The comparison with the same group on a no-massage day showed lower 90-minute glucose after topical macadamia oil. This is a within-group comparison, without a reported between-oil estimate for that result. Other substudies tracked blood fatty acids after application or described an eight-week blended oil and lifestyle program. The measured effects do not describe eating macadamia oil, Parkinson disease, or dexterity. The press process and product lot were unspecified.
