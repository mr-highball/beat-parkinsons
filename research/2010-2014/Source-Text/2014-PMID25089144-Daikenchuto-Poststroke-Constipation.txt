Title: Randomized add-on Daikenchuto comparison for poststroke constipation
PMID: 25089144
PMCID: PMC4095989
DOI: 10.1155/2014/231258
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4095989/
Published: 2014-06-25
Archive scope: Evidence-focused summary of the inspected original article body.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Thirty-four people with poststroke functional constipation were assigned to existing bowel care or existing care plus Daikenchuto, 17 per arm, for four weeks. The intervention is a Japanese Kampo formula containing processed ginger, ginseng and Japanese pepper. The addition was open label. Constipation Scoring System mean fell from 8.0 to 6.0 in the formula arm, with the reported two-way comparison p<.01. The source reports favorable differences for stool frequency, incomplete evacuation and assistance. Painful evacuation and abdominal pain are separate items in the instrument, and the overall score does not specify their effect.

The 2019 Arita report uses the same randomized cohort and adds secondary response analyses. The comparison identifies no independent contribution of ginger or Parkinson outcome.
