Title: Comparison of IPX066 with carbidopa–levodopa plus entacapone in advanced PD patients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25306200/
PMID: 25306200
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2014.08.004
Trial registration: NCT01130493
Publication date: 2014-08-15
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Primary abstract retrieved from NLM
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The ASCEND-PD trial converted patients from a regimen with entacapone and then randomized the sequence of a double-blind crossover. Ninety-one were randomized and 84 completed it. The diary-defined waking off time was 24.0% on IPX066 and 32.5% on the entacapone regimen in the report. Confusional state is one listed adverse-event term. The abstract supplies no visual hallucination episode counts for the randomized groups.

The 2015 shared conversion paper discusses the earlier open-label stage separately. It reports one discontinuation for digestive adverse effects among 110 entacapone-regimen conversion participants. Keep the two phases and ADVANCE-PD's different parent population distinct.
