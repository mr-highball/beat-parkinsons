Title: Effect of Talbinah food consumption on depressive symptoms among elderly individuals in long term care facilities, randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 23493965
PMCID: PMC3593710
DOI: 10.2147/CIA.S37586
First published: 2013
Authors: Badrasawi MM, Shahar S, Abd Manaf Z, Haron H
Journal: Clinical interventions in aging
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23493965/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-week randomized crossover clinical trial (PMC3593710) in n=30 institutionalized depressed older adults (21 men, 9 women) in Malaysia comparing daily Talbinah (cooked barley syrup prepared with milk and honey) vs habitual diet. Within-period paired comparisons showed reductions during Talbinah on Geriatric Depression Scale (GDS-R: 6.6+/-3.1 to 3.7+/-2.7, p<0.001; control 4.4 to 4.77, p=0.453), DASS-Depression (17.1 to 7.9, p<0.001; control 11.1 to 8.6, p=0.053), POMS Depression-Dejection (19.5 to 10.0, p=0.001), POMS Fatigue (9.8 to 6.4, p=0.005), and POMS Tension-Anxiety (11.7 to 7.9, p=0.002; DASS-Anxiety p=0.100). Limits: bundled formula containing barley, milk, and honey (cannot isolate barley alone); baseline period imbalance and paired within-period reporting; non-PD cohort.
