Green tea extract in a valproate-exposed developing mouse model was evaluated against a set of motor, nociceptive, exploratory, learning and other measures. The abstract gives a broad behavioral finding, without a dedicated fatigue comparison, Parkinson population or brewed tea arm. It is not the same experiment as the isolated piperine comparison.
