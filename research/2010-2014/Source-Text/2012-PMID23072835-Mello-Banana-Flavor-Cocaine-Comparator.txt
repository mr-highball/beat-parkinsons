Title: Mello et al. — banana-flavored reinforcement in the chronic buspirone and cocaine experiment
PMID: 23072835
PMCID: PMC3547196
DOI: 10.1038/npp.2012.202
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3547196/
First published: 2012-10-17 online, February 2013 issue.
Coverage: Complete posted article through its references. Factual extraction from the subjects, procedures and food-maintained response passages.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18 UTC

Five adult rhesus monkeys performed food and cocaine reinforcement tasks. The paper identifies a vitamin-C-fortified grain-based banana-flavored diet and the banana-flavored grain pellets delivered as rewards. Chronic buspirone was compared against saline. Food-maintained responding generally showed a smaller or less consistent change than cocaine-maintained responding under the studied conditions.

The study varies drug conditions and uses food-maintained responding as a comparator. It supplies a food flavor and formulation, without establishing raw fruit content or a banana effect on anxiety, apathy or dyskinesia. Related 2011 and 2013 reports use similar tasks and preparations. Participant or animal independence remains open.
