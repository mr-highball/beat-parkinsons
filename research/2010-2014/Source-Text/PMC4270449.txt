Title: Quantitative electroencephalogram utility in predicting conversion of mild cognitive impairment to dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4270449/
PMCID: PMC4270449
PMID: 25129239
DOI: 10.1016/j.neurobiolaging.2014.07.009
Publication date: 2014-07-15
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary longitudinal EEG cohort
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The study follows 47 mild cognitive impairment participants and compares EEG patterns with subsequent dementia classification. Visual hallucination status is recorded in the clinical tables along with REM sleep behavior and cognition. EEG measures and symptom presence serve diagnostic prediction rather than exposure assignment or visual event frequency in Parkinson disease.
