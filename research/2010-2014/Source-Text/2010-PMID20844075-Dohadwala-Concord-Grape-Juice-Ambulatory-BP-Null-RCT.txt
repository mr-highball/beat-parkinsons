Title: Effects of Concord grape juice on ambulatory blood pressure in prehypertension and stage 1 hypertension.
PMID: 20844075
PMCID: PMC2954442
DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.2010.29905
First published: 2010
Authors: Dohadwala MM, Hamburg NM, Holbrook M, Kim BH, Duess MA, Levit A, Titas M, Chung WB, Vincent FB, Caiano TL
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20844075/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover trial (NCT00302809) in 64 unmedicated adults with prehypertension or stage 1 hypertension consuming 7 mL/kg/day 100% Concord grape juice vs matched placebo for 8 weeks each. Primary 24-hour ambulatory systolic BP, diastolic BP, and stress-induced BP were completely NULL (only secondary nocturnal SBP dip changed +1.4% vs -2.3%, p=0.005). No orthostatic symptoms reported.
