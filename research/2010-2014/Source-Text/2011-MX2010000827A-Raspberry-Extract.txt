Raspberry powder in a nicotine-withdrawal mouse experiment
Primary source: https://patents.google.com/patent/MX2010000827A/en
Coverage: Factual extract of original patent application.

The July 2011 Mexican patent application describes spray-dried Rubus idaeus juice and reduced closed-arm time in a raised-maze test after induced nicotine withdrawal. It also describes a powder comparison in mice without nicotine. The exposure is dehydrated juice and the measure is mouse behavior, with no Parkinson or frozen-fruit group. The 2013 application cites this antecedent. Related mouse claims in the two disclosures do not establish independent replication.
