Title: A nucleoprotein-enriched diet suppresses dopaminergic neuronal cell loss and motor deficit in mice with MPTP-induced Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25303858/
PMID: 25303858
DOI: 10.1007/s12031-014-0432-2
Publication date: 2014-10-11
Coverage: Factual extract from the original author abstract. The full numerical outcome data are open.

The authors compared diets with and without an isolated nucleoprotein fraction obtained from salmon soft roe in a mouse Parkinson model. The supplemented group showed less locomotor impairment and higher dopaminergic neuron staining. The abstract does not give a numeric locomotion comparison or analyzed group counts.

The tested material was a processed fraction from roe. The study gives a general animal movement signal rather than a clinical bradykinesia measure after consumption of grilled salmon.
