Title: Anxiolytic-like effect of Illicium verum fruit oil, trans-anethole and related compounds in mice.
PMID: 24919985
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.5190
First published: 2014
Authors: Miyagawa M, Satou T, Yukimune C, Ishibashi A, Seimiya H, Yamada H, Hasegawa T, Koike K
Journal: Phytotherapy research : PTR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24919985/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse inhalation study (Miyagawa et al., 2014, Phytother Res) testing vapor inhalation of hexane-extracted Illicium verum fruit oil (4 uL/L air) vs isolated trans-anethole (1 uL/L air). Whole I. verum fruit oil inhalation did NOT produce an anxiolytic-like effect (null), whereas isolated trans-anethole vapor showed anxiolytic-like activity (p<0.05). Non-oral inhalation route and null whole-fruit oil finding.
