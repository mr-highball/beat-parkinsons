Original source scope
The US cohort model used 2352 observations. The odds of slow 20-foot walking for high versus low Mediterranean diet score were 0.706 before the cognition covariate and 0.753 after it (95% CI 0.540–1.049). The Israeli cohort modeled disability.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The score includes the ratio of monounsaturated to saturated fat. It has no separately measured cold-pressed olive-oil exposure. A continuous-score sentence says increased risk while Table 3a gives lower odds. No ingredient-specific Parkinson walking estimate is available.
