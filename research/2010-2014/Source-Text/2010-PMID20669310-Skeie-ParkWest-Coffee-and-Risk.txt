The incident ParkWest analysis included 212 cases and 175 matched controls. Table 2 gives habitual coffee of 3.37 versus 3.83 cups per day and a diagnosis odds ratio of 0.86 per additional cup in the reported regression (p=.007). The motor subtype coffee contrast was inconclusive (p=.09). The full report does not estimate fatigue by coffee consumption or distinguish the frozen brewing preparation.
