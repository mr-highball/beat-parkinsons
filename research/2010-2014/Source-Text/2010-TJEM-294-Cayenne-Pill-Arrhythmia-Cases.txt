Title: Two arrhythmia reports following cayenne weight-loss capsules
Source: https://turkjemergmed.com/abstract/294
Original pages: https://turkjemergmed.com/full-text-pdf/294/tur
Publication date: 2010
Date basis: Turkish Journal of Emergency Medicine, volume 10, issue 3, pages 133–136.
Review basis: Complete Turkish original with English abstract, case sections and discussion.
Source type: Emergency medicine case series.

The report describes two adults who developed symptomatic tachyarrhythmias while using a cayenne-containing weight-loss product. The diagnoses reported were supraventricular tachycardia and acute atrial fibrillation. Both case descriptions report chest pain and palpitations. These cases document timing and clinical observations. They provide no controlled comparison, independent composition assay or estimate of event frequency.

The 2012 case from the overlapping investigator group describes a distinct adult and an ischemic outcome. These reports are retained as formulation-specific safety context. They do not estimate pain severity after ground cayenne is eaten or any effect in Parkinson disease.
