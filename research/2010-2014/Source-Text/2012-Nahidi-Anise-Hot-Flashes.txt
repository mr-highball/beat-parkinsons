Anise ethanol extract capsules and menopausal hot flashes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3813162/
PMID: 24250540
Coverage: factual extract from primary Methods and results

Fatemeh Nahidi and colleagues. Iranian Journal of Pharmaceutical Research 11(4):1079–1085, autumn 2012. The article describes clinical research in Qazvin in 2009.
The methods clarify that powdered Pimpinella anisum seeds were extracted with ethanol, solvent removed, and the extract combined with potato starch. Seventy-two women enrolled in a double-blind trial with four weeks of capsule treatment or starch placebo. Enrollment and outcome collection focused on menopausal hot flash frequency and severity. The report describes sensitivity testing before the trial and discusses sleep disruption as a consequence of hot flashes, with no separate insomnia, daytime sleepiness or dream-enactment estimate. The population, extract, and outcome all differ from frozen ground-seed claims.
