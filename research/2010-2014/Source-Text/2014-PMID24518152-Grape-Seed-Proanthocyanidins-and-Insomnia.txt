Title: Grape-seed proanthocyanidin extract and Athens Insomnia Scale in a menopause pilot
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24518152/
Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1097/GME.0000000000000200
PMID: 24518152
DOI: 10.1097/GME.0000000000000200
Publication year: 2014
Issue: September 2014, volume 21(9), pages 990–996.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: Original author abstract and verified journal citation. The full outcome tables remain unavailable.

The authors randomized 96 women with menopausal symptoms to oral grape-seed extract tablets with two doses of proanthocyanidins or placebo. Ninety-one completed the eight-week pilot. The abstract reports a decrease in the Athens Insomnia Scale at week eight within the higher-dose group. It gives no placebo-relative sleep estimate or uncertainty for that comparison. The trial used proanthocyanidin extract in tablets. It did not test refined grapeseed oil or a Parkinson population.
