Nine people with Parkinson disease and 15 controls performed cued hand movement, speech and combined tasks during imaging. The combined-task comparison measured brain activation patterns and movement or speech responses. Figure 2 uses orange to depict an orofacial motor-cortex activation. The discussion cites earlier work on walking while talking and falls. The study assigned no raw orange intake and produced no fall-frequency estimate for it.
