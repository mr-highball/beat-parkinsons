Title: Fluctuations in cognition and alertness vary independently in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3946909/
PMCID: PMC3946909
PMID: 24151106
DOI: 10.1002/mds.25707
Publication date: 2013-10-22
Date basis: NLM electronic publication. Journal issue January 2014.
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary repeated-measures clinical study
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Thirteen participants with DLB and 64 with Parkinson disease underwent repeated cognitive testing and objective daytime alertness assessment. DLB showed greater within-day variation in digit span, with no difference in alertness fluctuation between the full patient groups. Average alertness and fluctuation are separate, and group differences change with the comparison used. The study neither assigns fig intake nor counts food-associated visual episodes.
