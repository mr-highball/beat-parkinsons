Title: Effects of fermented brown rice on the intestinal environments in healthy adult.
PMID: 21921425
DOI: 10.2152/jmi.58.235
First published: 2011
Authors: Nemoto H, Ikata K, Arimochi H, Iwasaki T, Ohnishi Y, Kuwahara T
Journal: The journal of medical investigation : JMI
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21921425/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized 2-week crossover trial (12-week washout) in n=36 healthy adults consuming 21 g/d Aspergillus oryzae-fermented brown rice (FBRA) vs control. Well tolerated, but no significant in vivo changes in fecal bifidobacteria, organic acids, pH, or bowel outcomes. Limits: healthy non-constipated adults, fermented product.
