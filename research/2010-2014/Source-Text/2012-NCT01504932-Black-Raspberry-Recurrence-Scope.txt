Appalachian oral cancer black raspberry registry OSU-06132
https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01504932

First posted January 6, 2012. The OSU-06132 registry reports 42 enrollees, a lozenge arm and a non-berry biomarker comparison. It describes administration of the Insomnia Severity Index, Brief Fatigue Inventory and Head and Neck Cancer Inventory. The primary registered outcome is time to recurrence. There are no posted summary results or separate anxiety measure.
