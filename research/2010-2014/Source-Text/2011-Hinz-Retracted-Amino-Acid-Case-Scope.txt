Retracted amino acid medication case and exposure identity
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3068871/

February 28, 2011. The article describes a selected case treated with levodopa and several amino acids or cofactors. Agent Orange appears as a historical exposure in the case narrative. The report has no fruit exposure contrast or quantified fall-frequency or pain comparison for the frozen hypotheses. The journal retracted the case in December 2020, DOI 10.2147/IJGM.S296324.
