Title: Cayenne weight-loss pill exposure, chest pain and a reported coronary event
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3284873/
PMID: 22264348
PMCID: PMC3284873
DOI: 10.1186/1865-1380-5-5
Publication date: 2012-01-20
Date basis: Official PMC publication date.
Review basis: Complete original case presentation, figures and discussion.
Source type: Single clinical case report.

An adult presented with acute chest pain and findings consistent with inferior myocardial infarction after five days of oral cayenne-containing weight-loss pills. The original records electrocardiographic changes, a subsequent troponin rise and patent coronary arteries on angiography. Clinicians suspected vasospasm. The paper reports improvement after acute clinical treatment and discontinuation. The report lacks an isolated withdrawal test, product-content verification and a comparison group.

This is an adverse event signal in a concentrated product context. Chest pain during a possible ischemic event is distinct from pain severity after ground culinary cayenne in Parkinson disease. The paper cites the 2010 two-case arrhythmia report, which involved different presentations and patients.
