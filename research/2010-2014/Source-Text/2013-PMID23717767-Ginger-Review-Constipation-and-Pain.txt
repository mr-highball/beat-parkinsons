Title: Ginger health and physical activity review: digestive indications and pain citations
PMID: 23717767
PMCID: PMC3665023
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3665023/
Published: 2013-04
Archive scope: Evidence-focused summary of the inspected original article body.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The narrative review lists constipation among digestive indications for ginger. It supplies no controlled constipation estimate or Parkinson subgroup. Its exercise-pain discussion cites the comparison of raw and heat-treated ginger preparations after eccentric elbow-flexor exercise (PMID 20418184). That independent original used ginger in capsules and recorded provoked muscle pain. Cumin appears in an antioxidant comparison rather than a bowel-symptom comparison.
