Title: Randomized clinical trial of a phytotherapic compound containing Pimpinella anisum, Foeniculum vulgare, Sambucus nigra, and Cassia augustifolia for chronic constipation.
PMID: 20433751
PMCID: PMC2874511
DOI: 10.1186/1472-6882-10-17
Registry ID: NCT00872430
First published: 2010
Authors: Picon PD, Picon RV, Costa AF, Sander GB, Amaral KM, Aboy AL, Henriques AT
Journal: BMC complementary and alternative medicine
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2874511/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Full-text verified single-blind crossover RCT (PMC2874511; NCT00872430) in n=20 chronic constipation adults comparing a 5-day 4-herb Brazilian commercial laxative tea (Pimpinella anisum fruits, Foeniculum vulgare fruits, Sambucus nigra flowers, and Cassia angustifolia [Senna] flowers) versus placebo tea separated by a 9-day washout. Colonic transit time fell to 15.7 h (95% CI 11.1-20.2) vs 42.3 h (95% CI 33.5-51.1, p < 0.001); evacuations/day and bowel perception improved (p < 0.001 / p = 0.01); WHOQOL-Bref unchanged (p = 0.2); mild potassium decrease and diarrhea/colic noted. CRITICAL ATTRIBUTION LIMITATION: Contains stimulant anthraquinone laxative Senna (Cassia angustifolia); authors explicitly state carminative herbs (such as fennel/anise) are traditionally added to reduce Senna/Sambucus cramping, so laxative efficacy cannot be attributed to Pimpinella anisum.
