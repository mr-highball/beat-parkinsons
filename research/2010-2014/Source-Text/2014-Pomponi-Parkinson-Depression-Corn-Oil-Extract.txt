Title: DHA effects in Parkinson disease depression
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.baga.2014.03.004
DOI: 10.1016/j.baga.2014.03.004
Publication: 2014-06, Basal Ganglia 4(2), 61–66. An earlier day-level publication date was not verified.
Coverage: Factual extract from the original publisher abstract and available Methods text.

The double-blind trial randomized 24 people with mild to moderate Parkinson disease. The intervention combined DHA with EPA for six months; the energy-matched comparison used corn oil. The abstract reports a 50% or greater Hamilton Depression Rating Scale score reduction in 75% of the DHA/EPA group and 25% of the corn-oil group. Neither UPDRS nor Hoehn–Yahr progression showed a clear group effect in the reported comparison. The accessible original extract does not supply the exact analyzed denominators or arm estimates for mean Hamilton change. This design provides no sunflower oil arm.
