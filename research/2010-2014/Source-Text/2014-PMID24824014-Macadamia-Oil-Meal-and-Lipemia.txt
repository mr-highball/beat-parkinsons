Title: Acute effects of monounsaturated fat on postprandial lipemia and gene expression in first-degree relatives of subjects with type 2 diabetes
PMID: 24824014
DOI: 10.1038/ejcn.2014.64
Publication date: 2014-05-14
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24824014/
Publisher: https://www.nature.com/articles/ejcn201464
Text coverage: Original abstract at two publisher routes. Full methods and outcomes tables were unavailable.

Pietraszek and colleagues compared 17 people with first-degree relatives with type 2 diabetes and 17 controls without a family history of diabetes. Participants in both groups consumed the same high-MUFA meal using macadamia nut oil. Postmeal lipids, glucose, insulin and other circulating markers did not differ significantly between the family-history groups. Several measured genes responded differently in muscle and adipose tissue. This is a comparison of population groups given the same oil, with no independent oil-free contrast, no stated pressing grade and no separate motor or tremor outcome.
