Original source scope and evidence boundary
Forty-four young adults entered a counterbalanced randomized double-blind crossover comparing the combined L-theanine and caffeine drink with a matched placebo drink. The primary report gives a treatment effect on the task-demand tiredness item, F(1,38.8)=5.13, p<.05, and no clear effort or difficulty differences. Figure 2 shows a greater reduction in the first post-drink period. The trial handles an acute task rating. Neither constituent is isolated, and there is no brewed green tea or Parkinson group. The original observation counters a broad assertion that all tea-derived exposures worsen fatigue. It has no independent transferable sign for the frozen whole-beverage claim.
