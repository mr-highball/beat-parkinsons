Title: Beans and diabetes: Phaseolus vulgaris preparations as antihyperglycemic agents

Review depth: abstract
Checked: 2026-11-25

The historical review's indexed abstract discusses customary bean-pod use for diabetes and describes contradictory results across historical and later reports. Its proposed food-component role concerns diabetes and is based on broad fiber and enzyme considerations. It gives no Parkinson symptom comparison or evidence for cooked green beans on fatigue.

The EMA's final assessment cites this review in its historical introduction and relays a 1608 Dodoens attribution for a different urinary use. The available author abstract leaves the exact early passage, edition and attribution unresolved. Keep the original historical edition as a separate retrieval question.
