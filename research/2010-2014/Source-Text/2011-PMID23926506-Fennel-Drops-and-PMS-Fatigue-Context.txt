Title: Comparing the Effects of Echinophora-platyloba, Fennel and Placebo on Pre-menstrual Syndrome
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3719290/
PMCID: PMC3719290
PMID: 23926506
Registration: IRCT138810263078N1
Citation: Delaram and colleagues, Journal of Reproduction & Infertility 12(3), pp. 221–226.
Publication date: 2011
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Original randomized study, Methods and Table 2.

Ninety students with premenstrual syndrome were assigned to fennel extract drops, a separate herb, or sterile-water placebo. The groups began with 30 each. Two fennel and three placebo participants did not complete treatment. The emotional domain pools fatigue and reduced energy with other items. Table 2 lists domain change 7.6 ± 4 in the fennel group and 5.4 ± 7.5 in placebo, with a three-group p=.29. The paper notes incomplete responses. This is a pooled endpoint and the p-value is not a fennel-only pairwise test.

The study gives no fatigue-only contrast, does not confirm toasted seed or a culinary preparation and does not enroll a Parkinson cohort. It is independent of the menstrual-weakness comparison already used in Round 1.
