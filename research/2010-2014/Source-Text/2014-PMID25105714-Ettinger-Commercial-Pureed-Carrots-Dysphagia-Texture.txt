Title: Characterizing commercial pureed foods: sensory, nutritional, and textural analysis.
PMID: 25105714
DOI: 10.1080/21551197.2014.927304
First published: 2014
Authors: Ettinger L, Keller HH, Duizer LM
Journal: J Nutr Gerontol Geriatr
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25105714/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Descriptive sensory, nutritional, and rheological analysis (line spread and back extrusion) of 4 commercial pureed carrot brands intended for dysphagia diets (noting PD/stroke dysphagia context). Found wide inter-brand textural variability requiring texture standards for safe swallowing; laboratory food-science study, not a clinical intervention.
