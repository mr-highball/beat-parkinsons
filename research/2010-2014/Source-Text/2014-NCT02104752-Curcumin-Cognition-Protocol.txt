Registered curcumin cognition comparison in schizophrenia
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02104752
Coverage: Factual original official record summary.

First posted April 4, 2014. This eight-week trial assigned a Theracurmin formulation or placebo in schizophrenia. The primary MCCB composite includes a wider cognitive battery with an attention and vigilance domain. The study population and formulation differ from the Parkinson grated-turmeric claim. The official results subsequently posted in 2019 give composite scores only.
