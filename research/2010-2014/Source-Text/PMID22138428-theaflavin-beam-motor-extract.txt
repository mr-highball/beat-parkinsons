Original factual extract from sections 2.1 and 4.3 and figure legends 1–3

The chronic mouse study used commercial theaflavin dissolved in water orally by gavage at 10 mg/kg for 35 days. A pilot selected 10 mg/kg. Compared with chronic MPTP/probenecid model controls, the treated group had longer rod retention, more open-field movement and rearing, shorter narrow-beam crossing time and fewer foot slips. The beam and open-field text reports p < .05 for these comparisons and Figure 3 presents means and SD from six mice per group. Methods give 12 per group and state all mice performed behavioral tests. The published text gives no explanation for the smaller figure caption denominator. Brewed tea, straight-line walking speed and rest tremor were outside the experimental contrast.
