Pengelly et al., first publication 2011-08-30, DOI 10.1089/jmf.2011.0005, PMID 21877951. Original Methods identify 28 healthy nonsmokers aged 65–90, a blinded randomized crossover, five separate single-dose sessions of zero or 750, 1500, 3000, and 6000 mg 100% Turkish-origin dried rosemary leaf powder mixed into 458 mL tomato juice; some doses exceed normal diet. Original Results concern cognitive battery and subjective mood, state no serious adverse events, and do not present a quantified rest-tremor or hallucination result.
