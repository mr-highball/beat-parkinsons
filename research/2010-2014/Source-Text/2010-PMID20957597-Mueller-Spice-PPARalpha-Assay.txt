Mueller, Beck and Jungbauer compared 34 plant extracts in an in-vitro PPAR alpha transactivation assay. The investigators list saffron among extracts with low to moderate activity, alongside nutmeg, paprika, various peppers and other extracts. Separately tested piperine and capsaicin had higher reported activity. The abstract frames receptor activity as a possible contribution to lipid metabolism.

The readout is laboratory receptor transactivation. It supplies no saffron fatigue measure, Parkinson population, dietary treatment, or measured symptom benefit. The abstract does not specify a formulation that maps to dried culinary saffron. The later narrative review cites this same result and supplies no independent cohort.
