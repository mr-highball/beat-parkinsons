Title: Nightmares that mislead to diagnosis of reactivation of PTSD.
PMID: 23556065
PMCID: PMC3615167
DOI: 10.3402/ejpt.v4i0.18714
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3615167/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2013-04-01
Coverage: complete PMC JATS.

The case uses polysomnography to distinguish persistent nightmares and REM-related motor phenomena from recurrent PTSD symptoms. The workup specifically reports absence of visual hallucinations and other neurological signs at assessment. There was no Parkinson fig exposure comparison.
