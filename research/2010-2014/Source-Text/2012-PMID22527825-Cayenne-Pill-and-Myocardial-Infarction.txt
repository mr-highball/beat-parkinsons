Title: Acute myocardial infarction reported during cayenne weight-loss pill use
Source: https://www.springermedizin.de/a-case-of-acute-myocardial-infarction-due-to-the-use-of-cayenne-/8566934
Publisher DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00508-012-0163-8
PMID: 22527825
DOI: 10.1007/s00508-012-0163-8
Publication date: 2012-04-20
Date basis: Publisher article's first online date.
Review basis: Original author abstract and bibliographic record. Complete methods and case details were unavailable in the inspected routes.
Source type: Single clinical case report.

Investigators reported acute myocardial infarction in an adult using cayenne-containing weight-loss pills. The original abstract gives the sequence at the level of a case report, with no assay of product contents, comparison group or effect estimate. An earlier conference listing with the same report title and investigators may represent the same patient. It does not add another case.

This report differs from the separate January 2012 case documented in PMID 22264348. Neither supplies a frozen ground-cayenne Parkinson pain comparison.
