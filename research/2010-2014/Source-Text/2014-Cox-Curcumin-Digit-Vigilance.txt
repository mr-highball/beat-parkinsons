Solid lipid curcumin and digit vigilance in older adults
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0269881114552744
Coverage: Factual original results and statistical model summary.

First published online October 2, 2014. Cox and colleagues randomized 60 healthy older adults to a 400-mg Longvida formulation or placebo. One-hour digit vigilance accuracy favored the formulation in the baseline-adjusted analysis (p = .046) and was inconclusive after adding demographics (p = .168). The difference was no longer apparent at three hours. After four weeks, the serial-three working-memory task favored treatment with demographic adjustment (p = .044). The published formulation and healthy cohort leave grated turmeric and Parkinson attention untested.
