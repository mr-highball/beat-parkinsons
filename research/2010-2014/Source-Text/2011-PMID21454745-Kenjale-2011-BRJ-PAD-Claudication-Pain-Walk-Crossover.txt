Title: Dietary nitrate supplementation enhances exercise performance in peripheral arterial disease.
PMID: 21454745
PMCID: PMC3119136
DOI: 10.1152/japplphysiol.00071.2011
First published: 2011
Authors: Kenjale AA, Ham KL, Stabler T, Robbins JL, Johnson JL, Vanbruggen M, et al.
Journal: Journal of applied physiology (Bethesda, Md. : 1985)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21454745/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover trial (PMC3119136) in n=8 patients with PAD comparing 500 mL beetroot juice vs placebo: active BRJ delayed claudication pain onset time by 18% and increased peak walking time by 17% alongside reduced diastolic BP. Limits: very small non-PD vascular sample.
