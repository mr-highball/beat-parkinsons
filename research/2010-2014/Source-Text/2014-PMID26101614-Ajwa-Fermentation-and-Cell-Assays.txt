Title: The impact of date palm fruits and their component polyphenols, on gut microbial ecology, bacterial metabolites and colon cancer cell proliferation
PMID: 26101614
PMCID: PMC4473134
DOI: 10.1017/jns.2014.16
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26101614/
Published: 2014-10-08, Journal of Nutritional Science 3, e46.
Archive scope: Official NLM text, Methods and outcome sections. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

The authors collected Tamr-stage Ajwa fruit and compared digested whole-fruit extract with a polyphenol-rich extract in laboratory fermentation using human donor faecal cultures. They also tested an intestinal cancer cell line. The preparations differed in composition and were studied in separate batch experiments. The reported microbial and cell outcomes reflect the laboratory systems, without a dietary human pain or apathy outcome.

The 2015 human crossover (PMID 26428278, NCT02288611) cites this work as a precursor. Its comparable ingredient lineage and separate human design warrant distinct records. The human trial measured abdominal symptoms and recorded mood in a diary. The laboratory study did not use those outcome measures.
