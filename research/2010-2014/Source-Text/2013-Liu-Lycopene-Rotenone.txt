Liu, Wang, Pan, Ding and Lu — Effect of lycopene on oxidative stress and behavioral deficits in rotenone induced model of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24175569/
Publication: Zhongguo Ying Yong Sheng Li Xue Za Zhi. 2013;29(4):380–384.
Coverage: Authors' English abstract fact extract. Full Chinese results tables unavailable in inspected sources.

Forty C57BL/6 mice were randomized to control, lycopene, rotenone, or rotenone plus lycopene, ten per group. The supplement was isolated lycopene administered orally. The investigators used a WT-200 water maze and report that lycopene reversed deficits in toxin-treated mice. They also reported changes in oxidative stress indicators and TH, alpha-synuclein and LC3-B staining. The accessible abstract supplies no numerical cognitive contrast or executive test score.
