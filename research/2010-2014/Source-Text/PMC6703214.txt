Title: Characterization of 3,4-methylenedioxymethamphetamine (MDMA) enantiomers in vitro and in the MPTP-lesioned primate: R-MDMA reduces severity of dyskinesia, whereas S-MDMA extends duration of ON-time
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6703214/
PMCID: PMC6703214
PMID: 21562283
DOI: 10.1523/jneurosci.1171-11.2011
Publication date: 2011-05-11
Date basis: NLM journal issue date at reported precision
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML main body
Evidence type: Laboratory pharmacology and primate movement-disorder experiment
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The experiment compared isolated drug enantiomers in a primate model and rated movement outcomes and observed psychosis-like behaviors during levodopa testing. The R enantiomer affected the disabling subset of the behavior measure without a significant change in the duration of any such behavior. These observer ratings do not establish what the animals saw or how often people with Parkinson disease hallucinate. Dried fig intake was not studied.
