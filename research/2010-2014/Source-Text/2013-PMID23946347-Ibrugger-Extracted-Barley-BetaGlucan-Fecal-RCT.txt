Title: Extracted oat and barley β-glucans do not affect cholesterol metabolism in young healthy adults.
PMID: 23946347
DOI: 10.3945/jn.112.173054
First published: 2013
Authors: Ibrügger S, Kristensen M, Poulsen MW, Mikkelsen MS, Ejsing J, Jespersen BM
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23946347/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized single-blind 4-period crossover trial (3-wk periods) in n=14 young healthy adults comparing control vs 3.3 g/d extracted oat, barley, and mutant barley beta-glucans in beverage and yogurt. Extracted barley beta-glucans did NOT alter fecal wet or dry weight, stool frequency, fecal pH, or cholesterol relative to control. Demonstrates that purified isolated barley beta-glucan extract may not replicate intact cooked whole-barley bowel effects.
