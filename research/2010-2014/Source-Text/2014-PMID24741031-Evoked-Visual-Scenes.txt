Title: Seeing scenes: topographic visual hallucinations evoked by direct electrical stimulation of the parahippocampal place area
Source: https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:163297
Primary article PDF: https://www.jneurosci.org/content/jneuro/34/16/5399.full.pdf
PMID: 24741031
DOI: 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.5202-13.2014
Publication date: 2014-04-16
Date basis: Original journal issue and institutional repository
Source depth: Primary body on printed pages 5399–5405
Format: Factual digest of the original material

In one person undergoing evaluation for refractory epilepsy, functional MRI and intracranial EEG helped identify visual scene-selective cortex. Direct electrical stimulation at selected medial temporal sites evoked recognizable indoor or outdoor scenes. More lateral stimulation distorted face perception. Position and stimulus determine the observations. This clinically elicited case clarifies the difference between formed visual experiences and perceptual distortions. It does not evaluate spontaneous visual episode frequency, a Parkinson cohort or a fig exposure.

The 2014 Journal Club commentary (PMID 25186734) interprets this same case. Count the commentary and original as one observation family.
