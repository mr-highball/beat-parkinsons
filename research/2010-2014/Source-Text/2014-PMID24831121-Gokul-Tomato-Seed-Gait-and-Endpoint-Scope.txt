The preclinical experiment tested an aqueous extract of separated tomato seeds and examined gait measures in a mouse neurotoxicity model. The reported forepaw stride-length difference favored the extract during the model exposure, with improvements in some other gait measures. Motor performance and an anxiety-like assay are distinct from examiner-rated rigidity or an executive-function measure. The source offers neither a cooked tomato comparison nor Parkinson patient outcomes. It clarifies how the extract cited in the later antioxidant review relates to the frozen ingredient.
