Title: Extended-release carbidopa-levodopa compared with immediate-release carbidopa-levodopa in Parkinson's disease with motor fluctuations
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23485610/
PMID: 23485610
DOI: 10.1016/S1474-4422(13)70025-5
Trial registration: NCT00974974
Publication date: 2013-02-26
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Published primary abstract
Format: Factual digest of the original material

ADVANCE-PD registered 471 people, of whom 450 entered conversion and 393 were randomized to double-blind maintenance. The randomized arms were extended-release and immediate-release carbidopa-levodopa. The adjusted difference in daily off time was approximately 1.17 hours favoring the extended-release arm. In conversion, 23 of 450 withdrew for adverse events, and 13 for insufficient efficacy.

The 2015 conversion article (PMID 26444090) specifies two withdrawals attributed to visual hallucinations in this conversion cohort. The main randomized report's abstract does not provide an arm-specific visual hallucination event table. Keep conversion withdrawals and randomized safety outcomes separate. These publications share one parent trial and do not compare foods.
