Sabzghabaee et al., Dental Research Journal 2012;9(2):198–202, PMID 22623938, DOI 10.4103/1735-3327.95236. The full article describes a randomized double-blind 40/40 trial of 1% Satureja hortensis essential oil gel versus gel vehicle in complete denture wearers with Newton type II/III denture stomatitis. A 1 cm gel layer was applied twice daily to denture base and palatal mucosa for 14 days. Clinical palatal erythema and Candida cultures were assessed; no voice, intelligibility, Parkinson subgroup, or fresh-leaf ingestion outcome appears.
