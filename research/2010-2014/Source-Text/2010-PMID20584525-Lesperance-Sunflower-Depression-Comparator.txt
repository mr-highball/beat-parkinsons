Title: The efficacy of omega-3 supplementation for major depression: a randomized controlled trial
Publication date: 2010-06-15 (online first; 2011 print issue)
PMID: 20584525
DOI: 10.4088/JCP.10m05966BLU
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20584525/
Text coverage: Distilled investigator report and published primary result summary.

Lespérance and colleagues randomly assigned 432 outpatients with a major depressive episode to eight weeks of EPA plus DHA or sunflower capsules containing 2% fish oil. The reported full-cohort IDS-SR difference favored the omega-3 intervention by 1.32 points (95% CI −0.20 to 2.84); the interval includes zero. The comparison does not isolate sunflower oil and does not measure Parkinson executive performance or an oil-specific anxiety effect. The registration ISRCTN47431149 describes the same trial.
