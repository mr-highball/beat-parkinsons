Title: Renal failure in dementia with lewy bodies presenting as catatonia.
PMID: 23466522
PMCID: PMC3573819
DOI: 10.1159/000346594
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3573819/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2013-01-18
Coverage: complete PMC JATS.

A case traces visual hallucinations in the history of dementia with Lewy bodies and later catatonia during renal failure. The admission investigation concerns metabolic complications, medication and altered movement or responsiveness. It does not identify dried fig exposure or quantify visual hallucinations in relation to it.
