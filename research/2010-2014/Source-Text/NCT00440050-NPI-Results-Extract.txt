Official results of NCT00440050 first posted 2010-08-30, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00440050?tab=results. Primary study PMID21045096 (PMC3259852, DOI 10.1001/jama.2010.1510). Coverage: original factual extract of randomized population, comparison and Neuropsychiatric Inventory outcome.

In this Alzheimer trial, 402 participants were randomized to algal DHA or the capsules described in Methods as corn or soy oil. The registry lists mean 18-month total NPI change 2.93 (SD 13.62) for placebo and 5.09 (SD 15.08) for DHA. The publication gives p=.11 for total NPI rate of change. The reported 2025 reanalysis, PMID40843066, examined agitation or aggression. The public outputs do not give an individual apathy or oil-specific arm result.
