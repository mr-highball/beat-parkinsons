Preliminary three-oil dialysis comparison
Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/271607674_A_DOUBLE-BLINDED_RANDOMIZED_CONTROLLED_TRIAL_ON_THE_EFFECT_OF_FLAXSEED_AND_OLIVE_OILS_ON_CONSTIPATION_OF_HEMODIALYSIS_PATIENTS_PRELIMINARY_RESULTS
DOI: 10.1016/j.krcp.2012.04.610
Coverage: Original 2012 published meeting abstract, Kidney Research and Clinical Practice 31(2):A88

The investigators reported 36 randomized dialysis patients, 11 assigned mineral oil, 11 flaxseed oil and 14 olive oil, with four weeks of treatment at an initial 4 mL/day. The percentages of reported symptoms declined in the flaxseed arm, but that column has no significance markers and no item-specific between-arm test is supplied. The investigators and comparison resemble the later 50-person article. Whether participants overlap is unresolved, so the reports cannot be counted as independent replications.
