Eid NS and colleagues. Author abstract on the impact of date fruit on growth of gut microbiota and large-intestinal health. ISAPP Student and Fellows Association conference abstract book, New York, June 11–14, 2013.
Primary source: https://isappscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/SFA_program_Abstract_booklet_2013.pdf
Coverage: factual extract from printed conference identity and author abstract.

The conference abstract describes a crossover in 22 healthy volunteers with whole dates and a carbohydrate control, each for 21 days, and a 14-day washout. The population, durations, investigators and gastrointestinal focus match the later NCT02288611 study report. The abstract contains no apathy measure or mood result for this trial.
