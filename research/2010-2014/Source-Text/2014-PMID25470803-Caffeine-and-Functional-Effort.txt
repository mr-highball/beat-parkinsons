Title: The effect of caffeine ingestion on functional performance in older adults
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25470803/
PMID: 25470803
DOI: 10.1007/s12603-014-0474-8
Publication date: 2014-12
Date basis: Journal of Nutrition, Health and Aging 18(10), pp. 883–887.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract.

The crossover in 19 healthy older adults reports improvement in selected functional tests, including timed up-and-go and six-minute walking, after isolated caffeine. Readiness to invest mental effort increased within the caffeine visit. Physical readiness and effects by sex were inconclusive. The author summary does not establish a measured fatigue-severity effect. Author and visit-design overlap with the 12-person 2013 study leaves participant-level independence unresolved.
