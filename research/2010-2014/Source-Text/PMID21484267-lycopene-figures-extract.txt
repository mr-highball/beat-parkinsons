Original factual extract of primary abstract and figures

The experiment tested isolated oral lycopene at 10 mg/kg over 30 days in a rotenone rat model. The abstract describes reduced motor and cognitive abnormalities with lycopene. At day 30, the model-only and lycopene-plus-model bar estimates in Fig. 1 are about 25 and 10 s in panel A and 4.4 and 2.0 score units in panel B. Fig. 2 shows approximately 28 and 7.5 s. The model-only day-30 bars carry number signs and lycopene-plus-model bars carry asterisks. The preview has no prose figure captions to identify the test or symbols. Its reference list includes an article on narrow-beam testing. The generic graph axes do not verify stride values or the exact test used.
