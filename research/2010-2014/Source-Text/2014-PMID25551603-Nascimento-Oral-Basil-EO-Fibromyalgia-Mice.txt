Title: Cyclodextrin-complexed Ocimum basilicum leaves essential oil increases Fos protein expression in the central nervous system and produce an antihyperalgesic effect in animal models for fibromyalgia.
PMID: 25551603
PMCID: PMC4307262
DOI: 10.3390/ijms16010547
First published: 2014
Authors: Nascimento SS, Araújo AA, Brito RG, Serafini MR, Menezes PP, DeSantana JM, Lucca W, Alves PB, Blank AF, Oliveira RC, Oliveira AP, Albuquerque RL, Almeida JR, Quintans LJ
Journal: International journal of molecular sciences
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4307262/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Full-text verified preclinical chronic pain study (PMC4307262) in mice (n=8/group across 6 groups = 48 mice) using an acid-saline fibromyalgia non-inflammatory chronic muscle pain model. Daily ORAL (p.o.) Ocimum basilicum leaf essential oil (LEO: 68.96% linalool, 13.09% geraniol, 6.12% 1,8-cineole) at 25 mg/kg or LEO/beta-cyclodextrin complex (25, 50, 100 mg/kg p.o.) significantly reduced mechanical hyperalgesia (von Frey, p < 0.001 vs vehicle) without impairing rotarod motor coordination or grip strength (unlike tramadol 4 mg/kg i.p.), and increased Fos activation in descending pain-inhibitory nuclei (PAG, NRM, LC; p < 0.05).
