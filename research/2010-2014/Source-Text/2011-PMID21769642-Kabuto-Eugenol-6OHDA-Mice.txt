Title: Effects of zingerone [4-(4-hydroxy-3-methoxyphenyl)-2-butanone] and eugenol [2-methoxy-4-(2-propenyl)phenol] on the pathological progress in the 6-hydroxydopamine-induced Parkinson's disease mouse model.
PMID: 21769642
DOI: 10.1007/s11064-011-0548-5
First published: 2011
Authors: Kabuto H, Yamanushi TT
Journal: Neurochemical research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21769642/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical 6-OHDA mouse Parkinson's model testing isolated eugenol (sourced as clove/spice constituent, not Pimenta dioica fruit) and zingerone given orally daily for 4 weeks alongside i.p. L-DOPA after intracerebroventricular 6-OHDA injection. Post-lesion eugenol did NOT improve L-DOPA-induced abnormal behaviors and surprisingly enhanced the 6-OHDA-induced depletion of striatal dopamine and its metabolites, contrasting with the authors' earlier pretreatment protection. Authors explicitly cautioned that timing relative to PD onset reversed eugenol's striatal dopamine effect.
