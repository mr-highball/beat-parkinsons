Gold kiwi with fortified cereal reported iron outcomes
https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/british-journal-of-nutrition/article/gold-kiwifruit-consumed-with-an-ironfortified-breakfast-cereal-meal-improves-iron-status-in-women-with-low-iron-stores-a-16week-randomised-controlled-trial/D5D398498909A7188DDECCF3850564EA

First published August 23, 2010. Beck and colleagues randomized 44 participants to two gold kiwifruit and 45 to banana with the same cereal and milk. The final analysis included 33 and 36 respectively. Median ferritin changes were 10.0 and 1.0 micrograms per liter (comparison p < .001). The final samples also differed in soluble transferrin receptor change. The study measured iron biomarkers and recorded dietary and health fields. There was no attention task. The thesis also describes other studies of iron, health and fatigue, which do not provide a kiwi-arm attention contrast.
