Title: Isolated CNS Whipple disease with normal brain MRI and false-positive CSF 14-3-3 protein: a case report and review of the literature

PMCID: PMC3500470

DOI: 10.1002/brb3.97
Publication date: 2012-10-01
Date basis: Publisher or NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Primary clinical case with postmortem diagnosis
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The recorded course includes visual hallucinations, cognitive decline and motor decline. The postmortem investigation established a different disease context, isolated central nervous system Whipple disease. The symptom narrative gives neither an episode rate nor a comparison with dried figs, and it cannot be treated as idiopathic Parkinson evidence.
