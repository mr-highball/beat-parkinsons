Title: Role of Monoaminergic System in the Etiology of Olive Oil Induced Antidepressant and Anxiolytic Effects in Rats
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3725699/
PMID: 23936669
PMCID: PMC3725699
DOI: 10.1155/2013/615685
First public: 2013-07-10
Coverage: Factual extract from methods, results and Figure 2 in the original public full text.

Perveen and colleagues compared repeated extra-virgin olive oil administration with a control condition over 27 days in male Wistar rats. Methods give 0.25 mL/kg daily and specify water for controls, whereas the Figure 2 caption calls the controls saline-treated. Processing temperature, composition, blinded scoring and an energy-matched oil comparator are unreported. Figure 2 gives n=5 in its caption and reports longer time in open arms of the elevated plus maze following olive oil, at p<.05. The paper provides no interval estimate for the difference. The rats had no Parkinson model. Open-arm exploration in the maze and the separate forced-swim result do not establish a clinical anxiety effect. The discussion cites the Pitozzi aging-rat study, which used a different test and different animals.
