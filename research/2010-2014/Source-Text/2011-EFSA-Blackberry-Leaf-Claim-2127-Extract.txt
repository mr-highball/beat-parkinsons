Title: EFSA Journal scientific opinion 2248, blackberry claim ID 2127
Corporate author: EFSA Panel on Dietetic Products, Nutrition and Allergies
Official original: https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/2248
Accessible publisher PDF in DTU repository: https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/files/6351778/prod11324395893836.Scientific.pdf
Identifiers: DOI 10.2903/j.efsa.2011.2248
Date: 2011-06-30
Coverage: factual extract of the panel conclusion and submitted claim in appendix C, prepared 2026-11-14

The appendix lists Rubus fructicosus L. (the spelling used in the source), common name blackberry, under claim 2127. The submission describes respiratory comfort and mentions the mouth, throat, pharynx and vocal cords. The proposed conditions specify dried leaves, including traditional consumption in cough drops. The panel concluded the submitted items lacked pertinent human evidence for substantiation. The opinion does not report a trial of fruit or measurement of voice sound pressure.
