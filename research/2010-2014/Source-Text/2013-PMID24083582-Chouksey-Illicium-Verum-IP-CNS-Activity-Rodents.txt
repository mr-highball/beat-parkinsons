Title: Central nervous system activity of Illicium verum fruit extracts.
PMID: 24083582
DOI: 10.1016/S1995-7645(13)60155-8
First published: 2013
Authors: Chouksey D, Upmanyu N, Pawar RS
Journal: Asian Pacific journal of tropical medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24083582/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rodent study (Chouksey, Upmanyu & Pawar, 2013, Asian Pac J Trop Med) testing Soxhlet n-hexane, ethyl acetate, and methanol extracts of Illicium verum fruit administered intraperitoneally (200 mg/kg i.p., 1/10 of 2,000 mg/kg acute toxicological dose; n=6/group) in male albino rats and mice. All three i.p. extracts (methanol most prominent) prolonged phenobarbitone sleeping time, reduced spontaneous locomotor activity, and produced anxiolytic-like effects without impairing rotarod muscle coordination. Limits: non-oral intraperitoneal route, solvent extracts rather than culinary ground spice, non-PD healthy rodents, abstract lacks numeric means/SDs and p-values.
