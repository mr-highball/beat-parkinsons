Corn oil comparator and Parkinson motor scale scope
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.baga.2014.03.004
selected original methods and findings paraphrase

Published in 2014. In 24 people with Parkinson disease, DHA plus EPA was compared with equicaloric corn oil over six months. The accessible abstract reports inconclusive comparisons for total UPDRS and Hoehn and Yahr score. The individual resting tremor item and oil-refining grade could not be verified.
