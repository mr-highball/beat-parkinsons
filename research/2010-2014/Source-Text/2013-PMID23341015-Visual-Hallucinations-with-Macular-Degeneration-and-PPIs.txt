Title: Oral proton pump inhibitors disrupt horizontal cell-cone feedback and enhance visual hallucinations in macular degeneration patients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3588886/
PMID: 23341015
PMCID: PMC3588886
DOI: 10.1167/iovs.12-11091
Publication year: 2013
Text coverage: Paraphrased findings checked against complete official PMC HTML.

Five people with advanced wet macular degeneration reported newly apparent or enhanced light-dependent patterned hallucinations during prescribed proton pump inhibitor exposure. The clinical cases had no Parkinson history. A separate retinal experiment addressed a possible mechanism for those drugs, with no dried fig exposure.

Three patients had new hallucinations and two reported a change from an existing pattern. The case table reports reversibility in three. The table and small selected case series do not provide a comparative incidence estimate.
