The pediatric respiratory-allergy paper distinguishes skin-test sensitization from reported respiratory exacerbation assessed with elimination and provocation. The reported allergen categories include hen egg, chicken, pooled cereals such as peeled barley and citrus. Its first and second groups have different respiratory conditions. Combined cereal or citrus categories carry no separate preparation-specific estimate. The paper examines respiratory allergy in children, with no Parkinson falls, fatigue or anxiety outcome.

The distinct Swedish cohort in the same volume estimates associations of egg intake with lipid measures. Count its results separately from the pediatric allergy paper. The frozen experiment preparations and symptoms remain open.
