Title: Effect of lycopene on oxidative stress and behavioral deficits in a rotenone Parkinson model
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24175569/
PMID: 24175569
Publication date: 2013-07
Date basis: Indexed Chinese Journal of Applied Physiology issue, volume 29(4), pages 380–384.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: abstract

The author abstract describes four mouse groups assigned ten each, a water-maze assessment and separate brain biochemical and histology outcomes. It summarizes improvement in broad cognitive and motor deficits with oral isolated lycopene. Neither the original abstract nor indexed records inspected provide a separate rigidity result. The Chinese full text and underlying behavioral estimates remain an access gap.

Claim 0544 asks about cooked tomato and Parkinson muscle rigidity. This report contributes isolated-constituent and animal outcome context.
