Coverage: Factual extract.

Acute injection of sesame oil, compared with injected saline, reduced the percentages of open-arm entries and open-arm time in rat elevated plus maze testing. Partial attenuation by antagonist within the model. The paper reports an injection vehicle, no food preparation or clinical anxiety.
