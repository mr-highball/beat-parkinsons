High cocoa chocolate and anxiety in a chronic fatigue crossover
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3001690/
PMID: 21092175
PMCID: PMC3001690
DOI: 10.1186/1475-2891-9-55
Coverage: Original masked crossover report, Methods and Table 3

Ten adults with severe chronic fatigue syndrome completed randomized eight-week exposures to high-cocoa chocolate and a simulated low-polyphenol iso-caloric chocolate, separated by two weeks. The active bar contained 85% cocoa solids. In Table 3, median HADS anxiety was 9.5 to 6 during active treatment (within-period p = .01) and 6 to 9 during simulated chocolate (p = .03). These are paired tests within each period. The report gives no direct between-treatment change estimate, and the starting medians differed by 3.5 points. The pilot has no Parkinson cohort and excluded DSM-IV psychiatric comorbidity.
