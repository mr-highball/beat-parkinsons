Title: Anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and antipyretic activities of virgin coconut oil.
PMID: 20645831
DOI: 10.3109/13880200903062614
First published: 2010
Authors: Intahphuak S, Khonsung P, Panthong A
Journal: Pharmaceutical biology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20645831/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rodent pharmacology study (Intahphuak et al., 2010, Pharm Biol) testing oral virgin coconut oil in rat ear/paw edema, chronic granuloma, yeast hyperthermia, and mouse acetic-acid writhing assays. Oral VCO showed moderate anti-inflammatory, antipyretic, and antinociceptive (writhing) effects, corroborated by Zakaria et al. 2011 (PMID:21454992) in dried/fermented VCO writhing, formalin, and hot-plate assays. Limits: non-Parkinson rodent nociception assays that do not establish clinical human Parkinson pain efficacy.
