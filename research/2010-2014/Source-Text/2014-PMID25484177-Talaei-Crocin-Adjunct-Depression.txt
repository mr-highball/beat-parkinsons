Title: Isolated crocin as an adjunct to SSRIs in a pilot major depression trial
First published: 2014-11-26 online. Journal issue: 2015-03-15.
PMID: 25484177
DOI: 10.1016/j.jad.2014.11.035
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25484177/
Archive scope: Authenticated full author abstract. Journal full tables remain an access lead.

Forty adults with major depressive disorder were assigned to isolated crocin or placebo in addition to a prescribed SSRI for four weeks. The author abstract reports a mean BDI reduction of 17.6 with crocin and 6.15 with placebo. The authors also report comparisons favoring crocin on BAI and GHQ, alongside compliance, small-sample, short-duration and self-report limitations. This is an add-on trial of isolated crocin. It cannot identify what a serving of ground saffron would do in Parkinson disease. The 2026 crocin systematic review includes this cohort.
