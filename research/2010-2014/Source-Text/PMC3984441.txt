Title: fMRI resting state networks and their association with cognitive fluctuations in dementia with Lewy bodies

Format: Factual digest of the original material

Resting functional connectivity was measured in 16 individuals with fluctuating dementia with Lewy bodies and 17 age-matched controls. The paper discusses overlap of hallucinations and cognitive fluctuations. Its primary contrasts concern functional connectivity and cognitive fluctuation rather than response to food or Parkinson visual episode frequency.
