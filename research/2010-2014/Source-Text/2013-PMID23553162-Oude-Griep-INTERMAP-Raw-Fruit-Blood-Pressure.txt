Title: Association of raw fruit and fruit juice consumption with blood pressure: the INTERMAP Study.
PMID: 23553162
PMCID: PMC3628377
DOI: 10.3945/ajcn.112.046300
First published: 2013
Authors: Oude Griep LM, Stamler J, Chan Q, Van Horn L, Steffen LM, Miura K
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23553162/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional INTERMAP observational study (PMC3628377, NCT00005271, n=4,680 adults age 40-59 y across Japan, China, UK, US). Pooled raw fruit intake was not consistently associated with BP; among East Asian banana consumers, banana intake was inversely associated with diastolic BP (-1.01 mmHg, 95% CI -1.88 to -0.02). Observational resting BP epidemiology; does not evaluate orthostatic lightheadedness.
