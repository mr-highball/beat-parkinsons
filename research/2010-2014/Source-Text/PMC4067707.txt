Title: Screening for impaired cognitive domains in a large Parkinson's disease population and its application to the diagnostic procedure for Parkinson's disease dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4067707/
PMCID: PMC4067707
PMID: 24987404
DOI: 10.1159/000362124
Publication date: 2014-05-23
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary clinical cognitive screening
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The cognitive screening study compares MMSE and MoCA scores in 304 Parkinson patients. It tests the sensitivity and specificity of cognitive-domain items in assessing dementia. The design does not measure dried fig consumption or hallucination frequency.
