Title: Relation between the rheological properties and the swallowing characteristics of vegetable juices fortified with carrot puree.
PMID: 22451381
DOI: 10.1271/bbb.110576
First published: 2012
Authors: Moritaka H, Sawamura S, Kobayashi M, Kitade M, Nagata K
Journal: Biosci Biotechnol Biochem
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22451381/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Rheology and swallowing dynamics study testing vegetable juices fortified with 0-30% carrot puree. Higher carrot puree concentration increased viscosity/yield stress (>=17.5%) and significantly slowed maximum pharyngeal bolus velocity. Texture-modification physiology in non-PD subjects; does not evaluate clinical PD dysphagia severity.
