Title: Efficacy of Safoof-e-Rasha in Parkinsonian tremors
Source: https://ccrum.res.in/writereaddata/UploadFile/HJUM%2C%20Volume%206%20%281%29%20Jan%E2%80%93Mar%202011_1627.pdf
Citation: Ahangar, Hakim and Siddiqui. Hippocratic Journal of Unani Medicine 6(1):23–27.
Publication date: January–March 2011
Coverage: Original methods, botanical composition and Tables 3–4 verified in the Council's issue.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

This open series followed 15 clinically selected people with parkinsonism over 60 days on a multi-ingredient Unani formulation. The researchers counted upper-limb oscillations and separated activation states. Table 3 gives mean resting frequency of 4.8 Hz initially and 1.7 Hz at day 60, with postural frequency 7.8 and 3.0 Hz. Table 4 reports qualitative pill-rolling improvement in four of the 11 people with that baseline feature. The reported values measure oscillation frequency and a qualitative change, with no parallel control, masked assessment, or reported uncertainty around the mean changes.

The composition includes henbane (Hyoscyamus niger), aloe, pellitory, asafoetida and lettuce seed. Ajwain Khurasani in this original denotes henbane. Tukhm-e-Kahu denotes lettuce seed. Neither common fig nor peepal appears in this formula. The effect of any component, diagnostic subtype and reproducibility in a controlled comparison remain open. The 2023 review retells this cohort.
