Title: Yersinia enterocolitica O:9 infections associated with bagged salad mix in Norway, February to April 2011.
PMID: 21596007
First published: 2011
Authors: MacDonald E, Heier B, Stalheim T, Cudjoe K, Skjerdal T, Wester A
Journal: Euro surveillance : bulletin Europeen sur les maladies transmissibles = European communicable disease bulletin
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21596007/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Primary Abstract:
In March 2011, the Norwegian Institute of Public Health identified a possible outbreak involving 21 cases of Yersinia enterocolitica O:9 infection with similar MLVA-profiles. Preliminary results of epidemiological and microbiological investigations indicate bagged salad mix containing radicchio rosso (also known as Italian chicory) as a possible source. As a result of the investigation, bagged salad mixes of a specific brand were voluntarily withdrawn from the market by the producer.

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Epidemiological outbreak report (Euro Surveill 2011) documenting 21 cases of Yersinia enterocolitica O:9 infection in Norway linked to commercial bagged salad mixes containing radicchio rosso, prompting voluntary product recall. Contextual food-safety hygiene record for raw ready-to-eat radicchio salads; no PD symptom data.
