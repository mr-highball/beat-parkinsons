Title: Free Radical Scavenging and Analgesic Activities of Cucumis sativus L. Fruit Extract.
PMID: 21264095
PMCID: PMC3019374
DOI: 10.4103/0975-1483.71627
First published: 2010
Authors: Kumar D, Kumar S, Singh J, Narender, Rashmi, Vashistha B et al.
Journal: J Young Pharm
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3019374/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rodent analgesia study (Kumar et al., 2010, J Young Pharm, PMC3019374) evaluating aqueous Cucumis sativus fruit extract (250 and 500 mg/kg) in Swiss albino mice using acetic-acid writhing and tail-immersion tests against diclofenac sodium. Aqueous fruit extract significantly reduced nociceptive responses in a dose-dependent manner (p<0.01). Limits: acute nociception mouse model using fresh fruit extract rather than pickled whole cucumber in Parkinson disease.
