This double-blind trial randomized 68 participants with moderate to severe Alzheimer disease to saffron extract capsules or memantine for one year. The preparation section identifies Saffrotin dried extract in capsules. Cognitive and functional measures are the study efficacy outcomes.

Table 4 on printed page 357 reports four fatigue events in the 34-person extract arm and five in the 34-person memantine arm, with reported p=1.00. These sparse adverse-event counts under an active comparison leave fatigue severity unclear. The population and preparation differ from Round 1 claim 0485.
