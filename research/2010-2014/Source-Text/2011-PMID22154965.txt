Title: Sage leaf hydroalcoholic extract and evoked responses in mice
First online: 2011-12-02 according to PubMed
PMID: 22154965
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2011.11.042
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22154965/
Text type: original abstract evidence summary

Rodrigues and colleagues administered hydroalcoholic extract of Salvia officinalis leaf orally to mice before induced nociceptive challenges. The abstract reports fewer writhing behaviors and reductions in selected other evoked responses. Full group estimates and details of the starting leaf preparation remain unverified. These are extract responses in laboratory animals. The report supplies no human or Parkinson estimate.
