Sesame seed oil and movement outcomes in lesioned mice
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22089932/
selected original methods and findings paraphrase

First published November 17, 2011. Sesame seed oil was examined in 6-hydroxydopamine-lesioned mice. The paper evaluates rotational behavior and locomotor or biochemical outcomes after pretreatment. A clinical rigidity scale is outside its model, and cold pressing was not verified.
