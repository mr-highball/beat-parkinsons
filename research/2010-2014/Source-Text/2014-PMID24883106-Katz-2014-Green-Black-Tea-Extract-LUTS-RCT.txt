Title: A green and black tea extract benefits urological health in men with lower urinary tract symptoms.
PMID: 24883106
PMCID: PMC4003843
DOI: 10.1177/1756287214526924
First published: 2014
Authors: Katz A, Efros M, Kaminetsky J, Herrlinger K, Chirouzes D, Ceddia M
Journal: Therapeutic advances in urology
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4003843/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled 12-week trial in 46 randomized (40 completed) men aged 30-70 y with lower urinary tract symptoms (AUAss 8-24) testing 500 or 1,000 mg/d of a proprietary green + black tea extract blend (AssuriTEA) vs placebo. The 1,000 mg blend reduced composite AUAss by 34.5% from baseline to week 12 (p=0.008) and improved average flow rate vs placebo (p=0.002). Limits: blended green+black tea extract capsule rather than brewed black tea; composite male LUTS score rather than isolated urgency episodes.
