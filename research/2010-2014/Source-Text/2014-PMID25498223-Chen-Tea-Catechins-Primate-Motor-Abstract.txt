Title: Tea polyphenols and motor outcomes in a nonhuman Parkinson model

Review depth: original abstract and publisher preview

The authors report improved aggregate motor impairment and dopaminergic injury measures after an oral tea polyphenol fraction in nonhuman primates with chemically induced parkinsonism. EGCG predominated in the fraction. The abstract separately reports molecular and cell findings. This was an experimental fraction and a nonhuman model, with no brewed black tea comparison and no result specific to bradykinesia or rest tremor in the accessible abstract. Group sizes and motor subscores were not recovered from the accessible preview. This records a constituent/model lead only.

Citation trail: Figueira et al., PMID 27784225, reference 188. Round 1 context: brewed black tea claim 0082. The review and underlying experiment are one source family for this observation.
