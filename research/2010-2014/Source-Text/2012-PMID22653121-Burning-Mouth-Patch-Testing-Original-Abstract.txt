Title: Clinically relevant patch test results in patients with burning mouth syndrome
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22653121/
PMID: 22653121
DOI: 10.1097/DER.0b013e31824a625e
Publication date: 2012
Coverage: Original PubMed author abstract. Allergen-specific tables remain open.

The retrospective report covers 195 patients and 75 oral-series patch tests. Twenty-eight tested positive for one or more allergens. The report summarizes both symptom improvement and persistent symptoms after advice.
The accessible abstract does not state a cinnamon-specific test count, preparation or outcome. Aggregate patch-test and follow-up figures cannot be attributed to cinnamon alone.
