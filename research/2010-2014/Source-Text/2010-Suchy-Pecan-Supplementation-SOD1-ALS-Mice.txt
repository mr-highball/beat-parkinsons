Title: Dietary Supplementation with Pecans Delays Motor Neuron Pathology in Transgenic Mice Expressing G93A Mutant Human Superoxide Dismutase-1.
First published: 2010
Authors: Suchy J, Lee S, Ahmed A, Shea TB
Journal: Current Topics in Nutraceutical Research
Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/285889404_Dietary_supplementation_with_pecans_delays_motor_neuron_pathology_in_transgenic_mice_expressing_G93A_mutant_human_superoxide_dismutase-1
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Preclinical transgenic mouse study (Curr Top Nutraceut Res 2010;8(1):45-54; not indexed in PubMed) feeding 0.05% dietary pecans to G93A mutant human SOD1 transgenic mice (a familial ALS spinal motor-neuron degeneration model, NOT a Parkinson's nigrostriatal dopaminergic model). Reported delayed motor-neuron functional decline, increased motor neuron survival, reduced mutant SOD1 aggregation, and decreased reactive gliosis. Unusable for directional PD motor-symptom inference because SOD1 ALS motor-neuron loss is pathophysiologically distinct from PD basal-ganglia bradykinesia/tremor/rigidity and full statistical tables are unavailable in public OA records.
