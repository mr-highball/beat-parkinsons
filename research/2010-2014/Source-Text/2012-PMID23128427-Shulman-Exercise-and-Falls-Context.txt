The Parkinson trial compared higher- and lower-intensity treadmill exercise with stretching and resistance exercises. Sixty-seven participants completed the trial. Outcomes included six-minute walk distance, fitness and muscle strength. Orange cones marked the walking route on p. 184. Falls references include safety measures and the Falls Efficacy Scale, a measure of perceived ability to avoid falling. No trial arm assigned raw orange and the report supplies no fruit-specific fall-frequency estimate.
