European Medicines Agency, Committee on Herbal Medicinal Products. Assessment report on Cucurbita pepo L., semen. Final report EMA/HMPC/136022/2010 dated November 20, 2012. The cover also carries 2013 copyright.

The report distinguishes comminuted seed, two alcohol extracts and fatty oil as separate preparations. The row for Schiebel-Schlosser and Friederich 1998 describes an uncontrolled 2,245-patient prostate surveillance study using the ethanolic extract Prosta Fink Forte, with a drug-to-extract ratio of 15–25:1 and 92% ethanol as solvent. The adverse-event column names gastrointestinal complaints, hypotension and allergy. It gives 96% with no adverse effects and provides no count, definition, orthostatic measurement, baseline or comparator for hypotension. Section 5.3 discusses the 1998 and 2000 reports with mild gastrointestinal complaints in no more than 4% and does not separately quantify hypotension.

Both reports have matching sample and outcome patterns. The available 2000 abstract omits event-specific details. The earlier original has not been recovered. This is a regulator attribution for an ethanolic seed extract, with unresolved incidence and reporting scope. Cold-pressed oil exposure and Parkinson orthostatic symptoms remain unmeasured.
