Primary Abstract Facts (OA XML unavailable on EPMC/NCBI): Analytical chromatography screen of fava beans, green peas, and green beans aiming to quantify L-dopa and carbidopa. Abstract reports quantitative concentrations only for fresh/dry sprouted fava beans (1.4-2.6 mg/mL) without supplying validated cooked green-bean or green-pea values; claiming endogenous synthetic-drug carbidopa in plants lacks mass-spectrometric verification (contrasted with Tesoro 2023 validated LC-ESI/MS/MS showing only trace 0.02-2.34 ug/g dw L-dopa in Phaseolus vulgaris). No human PD clinical testing.
