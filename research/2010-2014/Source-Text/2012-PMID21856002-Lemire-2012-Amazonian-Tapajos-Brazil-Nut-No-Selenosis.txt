Title: No evidence of selenosis from a selenium-rich diet in the Brazilian Amazon.
PMID: 21856002
DOI: 10.1016/j.envint.2011.07.005
First published: 2012
Authors: Lemire M, Philibert A, Fillion M, Passos CJS, Guimarães JRD, Barbosa F, et al.
Journal: Environment international
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21856002/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional field survey of n=448 residents (ages 15-87 y) across 12 Lower Tapajos River Amazonian communities consuming a traditional diet rich in Brazil nuts, fish, and game: despite blood selenium 103.3-1,500.2 ug/L (median 228.4 ug/L) exceeding conventional toxicity cutoffs, nurse exams found no dermal, nail, hair, or garlic-breath selenosis signs.
