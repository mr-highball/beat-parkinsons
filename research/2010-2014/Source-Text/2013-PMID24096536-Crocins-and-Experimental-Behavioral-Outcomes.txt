Title: Crocins and behavioral outcomes in a ketamine rat model
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24096536/
PMID: 24096536
DOI: 10.1007/s00213-013-3293-4
Publication date: 2013-10-06
Date basis: NLM electronic publication date, with 2014 journal issue.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Investigator abstract.

The investigators assess isolated crocins in a ketamine rat model aimed at schizophrenia-like behavioral deficits. Reported outcomes include locomotor behavior and cognitive measures. A later treatment-resistant depression review cites this work when discussing saffron.

The model, isolated preparation and behavioral endpoints supply no ground saffron and Parkinson depression comparison. These results are distinct from isolated-crocin work specifically measuring depression-like behavior in Parkinson-model mice.
