Cardamom case number and reported symptoms

Source: https://smj.org.sa/content/smj/32/2/171.full.pdf
Coverage: Original Table 5, verified visually, factual extract.

Al-Braik and colleagues, Saudi Medical Journal 32(2):171–176, 2011. Table 5 p.174 lists cardamom under case 27 and reports finger numbness in the herb and prescription drug interaction section. Low blood pressure, high blood pressure and dizziness are cases 9, 17 and 13, respectively, with multiple herbs entered in the exposure column. Those rows do not identify cardamom specifically. The survey does not establish an orthostatic-lightheadedness case attributable to ground cardamom. This table is screened as contextual exposure identification only.
