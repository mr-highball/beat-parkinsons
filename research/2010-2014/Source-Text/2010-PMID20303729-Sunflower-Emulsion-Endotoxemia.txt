Title: Emulsified lipids increase endotoxemia: possible role in early postprandial low-grade inflammation
Publication date: 2010-03-20 (online first; 2011 journal issue)
PMID: 20303729
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2009.11.011
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20303729/
Text coverage: Distilled original abstract plus the exposure identification in Chianese and colleagues' full review.

The original Laugerette abstract reports higher endotoxemia and triglycerides after emulsified lipid than nonemulsified lipid in rats. The full Chianese review identifies the experimental oil as sunflower oil. The original also reports a human mixed-meal study and epithelial experiments. Human results do not isolate sunflower oil or compare clinical cognitive performance. Unverified processing grade and the lack of a measured Parkinson clinical outcome limit transfer. The original full-text extraction and formulation details remain open.
