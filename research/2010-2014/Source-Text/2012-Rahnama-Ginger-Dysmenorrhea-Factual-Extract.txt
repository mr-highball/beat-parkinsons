Publication: Rahnama et al. BMC Complementary and Alternative Medicine, 2012, 12:92. DOI 10.1186/1472-6882-12-92. PMID 22781186, PMC3518208.
Source: https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6882-12-92
Coverage: Primary abstract outcome description and study context.
Oral ginger root powder and placebo were compared in university students with primary dysmenorrhea. The original abstract reports lower visual analogue pain severity with ginger for both administration schedules (p = .015 and .029). Original table-level values were not verified in this pass. The exposure was encapsulated powder and the population had menstrual pain.
