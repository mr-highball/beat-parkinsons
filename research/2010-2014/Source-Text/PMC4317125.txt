Title: Deep brain light stimulation effects on glutamate and dopamine concentration

Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Evidence type: Primary rat experiment
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The paper tests optical stimulation of the subthalamic nucleus in a rat model and reports microdialysis measurements of striatal dopamine and glutamate. These are laboratory neurotransmitter outcomes. The experiment does not administer a fig preparation or measure clinical visual hallucinations.
