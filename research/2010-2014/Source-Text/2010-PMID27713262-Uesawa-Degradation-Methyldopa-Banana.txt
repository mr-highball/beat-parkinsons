Title: Degradation of Methyldopa by Banana.
PMID: 27713262
PMCID: PMC4033963
DOI: 10.3390/ph3030441
First published: 2010
Authors: Uesawa Y, Mohri K
Journal: Pharmaceuticals (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27713262/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

In vitro chemical compatibility study (PMC4033963) mixing methyldopa (a structural levodopa analog) with banana pulp supernatant at 30 C: yellow discoloration appeared within 30 seconds accompanied by rapid enzymatic drug degradation. Supports a polyphenol oxidase catechol-degradation mechanism but provides no human PD motor outcome.
