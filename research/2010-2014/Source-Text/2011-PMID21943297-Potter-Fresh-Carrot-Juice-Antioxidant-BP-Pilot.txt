Title: Drinking carrot juice increases total antioxidant status and decreases lipid peroxidation in adults.
PMID: 21943297
PMCID: PMC3192732
DOI: 10.1186/1475-2891-10-96
First published: 2011
Authors: Potter AS, Foroudi S, Stamatikos A, Patil BS, Deyhim F
Journal: Nutr J
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21943297/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single-arm uncontrolled 90-day pilot study of 16 fl oz/day freshly squeezed carrot juice in adults without diet modification. Increased plasma total antioxidant capacity (p<0.05) and lowered malondialdehyde (p<0.05); borderline non-significant systolic BP reduction (p=0.06) and no diastolic BP, lipid, insulin, or CRP change (p>0.1). Uncontrolled non-PD pilot; no orthostatic blood-pressure or symptom measurement.
