Title: The MAPT p.A152T variant is a risk factor associated with tauopathies with atypical clinical and neuropathological features
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3657164/
PMCID: PMC3657164
PMID: 22595371
DOI: 10.1016/j.neurobiolaging.2012.04.006
Publication date: 2012-05-16
Date basis: Publisher or NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Primary neuropathology and sequencing series
Format: Factual digest of the original material

One patient initially diagnosed clinically with Parkinson dementia experienced visual hallucination during a rapidly worsening course that included fluctuating consciousness and myoclonus. The paper places the clinical description in an atypical tauopathy series and includes a separate screen of pathologically confirmed idiopathic Parkinson cases. Neither the individual course nor the genetic screen estimates visual recurrence under a culinary exposure.
