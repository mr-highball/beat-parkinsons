Title: Central nervous system activity of an aqueous acetonic extract of Ficus carica L. in mice

Coverage: Factual extract from the original Methods, generalized behavior, rotarod, seizure outcomes and Figure 1.

The introduction refers to leaf extract and the collection and extraction sections specify dried aerial parts. The researchers administered the prepared extract orally at 250 or 500 mg/kg in male Swiss albino mice. Groups were six per procedure and each animal was tested once. Oral extract reduced observed spontaneous activity and alertness over two hours, prolonged ketamine-induced sleep, and reduced rotarod time compared with vehicle. The seizure experiments evaluated induced convulsions. Reduced activity and shorter rotarod time do not measure Parkinson resting tremor. The tests concern a distinct extract and several experimental endpoints, with no raw fig intake or Parkinson population.
