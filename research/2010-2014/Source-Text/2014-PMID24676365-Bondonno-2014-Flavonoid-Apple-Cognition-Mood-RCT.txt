Title: The acute effect of flavonoid-rich apples and nitrate-rich spinach on cognitive performance and mood in healthy men and women.
PMID: 24676365
DOI: 10.1039/c3fo60590f
First published: 2014
Authors: Bondonno CP, Downey LA, Croft KD, Scholey A, Stough C, Yang X et al.
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24676365/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled 4-way acute crossover trial in n=30 healthy men and women comparing energy-matched control, flavonoid-rich whole apple (with skin), nitrate-rich spinach, and apple + spinach. Although flavonoid-rich apple acutely augmented plasma S-nitrosothiols/RXNO nitric oxide status, it produced NO significant effect on Cognitive Drug Research (CDR) computerized cognitive performance (including attention/executive domains) or acute mood vs control. Direct human null counterweight for acute whole-apple cognition and mood claims.
