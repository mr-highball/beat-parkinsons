Title: Whole beetroot consumption acutely improves running performance.
PMID: 22709704
DOI: 10.1016/j.jand.2011.12.002
First published: 2012
Authors: Murphy M, Eliot K, Heuertz RM, Weiss E
Journal: Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22709704/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=11 recreationally fit adults ingesting 200 g baked whole beetroot (>=500 mg nitrate) vs isocaloric cranberry relish before a 5-km treadmill time trial: whole baked beetroot significantly increased running velocity over the final 1.8 km (+5%; p=0.02) and lowered rating of perceived exertion (RPE; p=0.04). Crucial whole-food cooked beetroot exposure bridge.
