The role of natural products in the discovery of new drug candidates for the treatment of neurodegenerative disorders I: Parkinson's disease

PMID: 20874702
DOI: 10.2174/187152711794480483
First publication: 2011-03 (verified issue month)

The Parkinson-focused review discusses natural-product discovery, active extracts and constituents, historical use and proposed mechanisms for cognition, neurovascular function and neuroprotection. The inspected abstract does not report a dried-saffron and fatigue outcome comparison. It has a separate author order, DOI and pagination from Part II on Alzheimer disease.
