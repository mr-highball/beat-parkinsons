Mazzio et al. original Methods and Table 1
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3521852/#T1

Mazzio and colleagues first published online August 8, 2012. Table 1 lists Mentha spicata as sample S64, in the recombinant human MAO-B IC50 interval above 0.2 and below 0.4 mg/mL. The extracts used ethanol and the method used selegiline for positive-control validation. The source leaves the spearmint sample’s part and fresh state unresolved. An enzyme assay does not measure levodopa wearing-off duration. The shiso, cilantro and marjoram notes refer to this same original assay.
