Published black raspberry slurry crossover
Author abstract indexed in 2013 and journal issue in 2016
Sardo and colleagues’ author abstract is indexed in 2013 and appeared in a 2016 journal issue. The open-label crossover in ten overweight men compared consuming a 45 g freeze-dried black raspberry powder slurry with periods without the slurry. Postprandial IL-6 exposure was lower after the slurry. CRP and TNF-alpha yielded inconclusive results. The abstract supplies no blood pressure by treatment or posture-specific symptom estimate.
