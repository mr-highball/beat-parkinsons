Title: Date fruit extract is a neuroprotective agent in diabetic peripheral neuropathy in streptozotocin-induced diabetic rats: a multimodal analysis.

PMCID: PMC3236446
DOI: 10.1155/2011/976948
First published: 2011
Authors: Zangiabadi N, Asadi-Shekaari M, Sheibani V, Jafari M, Shabani M, Asadi AR et al.
Journal: Oxid Med Cell Longev
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3236446/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rat peripheral neuropathy study (Zangiabadi et al., 2011, Evid Based Complement Alternat Med, PMC3236446) reporting aqueous Phoenix dactylifera fruit extract preserved sciatic motor nerve conduction velocity and exploratory open-field activity in streptozotocin-diabetic rats. Peripheral diabetic neuropathy rodent model; does not test central Parkinson motor signs.
