Original randomized meal crossover methods and results

Original source https://doi.org/10.1186/1475-2840-10-78

The 2011 crossover randomized nine healthy participants to two breakfasts with powdered Cinnamomum zeylanicum spice capsules or flour placebo. Post-meal brachial pressures were measured in triplicate while supine. The report gives an inconclusive time-by-supplement comparison for systolic and diastolic pressure. No paired standing measure or lightheadedness result was collected. The authors identify NCT01350284.
