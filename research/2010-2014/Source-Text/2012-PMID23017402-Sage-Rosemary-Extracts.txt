Title: Phytochemical profile of Rosmarinus officinalis and Salvia officinalis extracts and correlation to their antioxidant and anti-proliferative activity
Publication date: 2012-07-31 (online first)
PMID: 23017402
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodchem.2012.07.091
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23017402/
Text coverage: Distilled author abstract from the original PubMed record. The 2013 issue date and the publisher access restriction are recorded separately.

The study profiled rosemary and common-sage extracts by chromatography and mass spectrometry and compared NMR measurements of triterpenic acids with chromatography. It examined anti-proliferative activity in cancer cells and antioxidant activity. The comparison is product chemistry and a cell assay. Oral dose, culinary preparation and an effect on Parkinson symptoms are unavailable. The separate IPLeiria P33 cites this article. Its printed antidiabetic body cannot be used as a replication of the plant assays.
