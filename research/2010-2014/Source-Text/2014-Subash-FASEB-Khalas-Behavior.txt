Coverage: factual summary of author conference abstract, matching later full paper.

APPsw/Tg2576 mice received 2% or 4% date preparations in feed. The author abstract describes multiple motor, exploration and spatial learning assays. It mentions different test ages in the methods and comparison description. The full 2015 behavioral paper expressly describes the 2014 presentation as the same work. Neither dates as an ordinary food nor apathy severity in Parkinson disease is established by these outcomes.
