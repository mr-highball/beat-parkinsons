Title: Interaction between plant polyphenols and the erythrocyte membrane

Issue date: 2012-03

Text coverage: Curated original comparison and linked EBSA poster P-603

Cyboran and colleagues study extracts from leaves of strawberry,
apple and blackcurrant in red-cell assays. The paper describes
changes to membrane properties and protection against oxidation
during the laboratory test. The earlier EBSA 2011 P-603 poster
(PMID 21847795, printed page S196) examines strawberry leaves in
red-cell and model-membrane assays and reports surface-region
changes. The two reports share researchers, leaf preparation and
several assays, so they are retained as one likely family.

The study involves leaves applied to a model. It gives no comparison
for eating raw strawberries and no Parkinson movement or fall data.
