Title: The Effects of Lavandula Angustifolia Mill Infusion on Depression in Patients Using Citalopram: A comparison Study.
PMID: 24578844
PMCID: PMC3918201
DOI: 10.5812/ircmj.4173
First published: 2013
Authors: Nikfarjam M, Parvin N, Assarzadegan N, Asghari S
Journal: Iranian Red Crescent medical journal
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24578844/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled trial (Nikfarjam et al., 2013, Iran Red Crescent Med J, PMC3918201) of 80 adult outpatients with moderate-to-severe major depression randomized to citalopram 20 mg twice daily alone or citalopram plus two cups daily of brewed Lavandula angustifolia infusion (5 g dried shoots in boiling water with sugar) for 8 weeks. Mean 24-item HAM-D was lower in the lavender infusion add-on group at 4 weeks (15.2+/-3.6 vs 17.5+/-3.5, p<0.05) and 8 weeks (14.8+/-4.0 vs 16.8+/-4.6, p<0.01). Most common adverse events were nausea (12.8%) and confusion (10%), with no significant between-group difference in adverse events. Limits: open-label add-on design without placebo infusion, added sugar, non-PD psychiatric cohort.
