# Human motor results after antipsychotic doses carried in peach juice

Original source https://www.academia.edu/94500606/Secondary_nonmotor_negative_symptoms_in_healthy_volunteers_after_single_doses_of_haloperidol_and_risperidone_a_double_blind_crossover_placebo_controlled_trial
Source coverage original Methods, Results and Table 2

Mas and colleagues first published this APSEP result on October 7, 2013. The official registry links the report and the paper independently matches the crossover study site and three arms. Thirty healthy volunteers enrolled and 25 completed. Each condition, including the placebo, used 250 mL peach juice as carrier. In Table 2 and the results, risperidone impaired dominant-hand tapping versus placebo and haloperidol (both post hoc p < .05, overall p < .0009). Both antipsychotics performed worse than placebo on the fine-motor task (overall p = .002). Actual juice intake was held constant. The results estimate drug effects without a raw-fruit contrast.
