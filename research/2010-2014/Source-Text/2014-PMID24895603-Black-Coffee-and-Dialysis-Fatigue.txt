The original single-centre cross-sectional cohort's Table 2 reports FACIT fatigue 30.1 ± 10.2 among 67 habitual black-coffee users and 29.2 ± 11.6 among 19 nonusers, p=.745. Preparation and treatment assignment are unverified. The dialysis comparison does not establish equivalence or a Parkinson effect.

The paper's separate cognitive and sleep outcomes are different endpoints. This is the same study already cataloged in Food-Experiment-Coffee-Followup.md for claim 0121. No replication is counted.
