First public February 8, 2012. Usual care versus nutrition program in 210 malnourished adults aged at least 60. Fortified oatmeal was part of the intervention hospital menu along with fortified desserts, dietary supplements, calcium-vitamin D and counseling. Three-month falls: 10 versus 24 participants and 16 versus 41 incidents. First-fall HR 0.41 (95% CI 0.19–0.86). Rolled processing, separate oat amount and Parkinson outcomes unreported.
