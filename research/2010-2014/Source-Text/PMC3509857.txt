The case traces early-onset parkinsonism before recognition of Gaucher disease and describes multiple treatment changes over follow-up. Benign visual hallucinations are reported in the discussion of levodopa withdrawal, along with loss of response. The text does not identify an independent visual event rate, isolate a drug effect from disease course or measure dried fig intake.
