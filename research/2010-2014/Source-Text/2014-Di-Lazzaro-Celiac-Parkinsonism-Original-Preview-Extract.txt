Gluten withdrawal in a case of parkinsonism and celiac disease

Primary source: https://publires.unicatt.it/en/publications/dramatic-improvement-of-parkinsonian-symptoms-after-gluten-free-d-9/
Coverage: Original case opening, outcome table unavailable
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First online January 25, 2014. The accessible original describes a 75-year-old with walking difficulty, instability, fatigability and baseline UPDRS II 8 and III 19. The reproduction stops before treatment and Table 1. The published title and a later review describe improved parkinsonism after gluten withdrawal in celiac disease, while the complete primary course and freezing outcomes remain unverified here. The case does not isolate rye bread. The overlapping authors' 2017 celiac cortical-excitability study is not a documented follow-up of this case.
