Original source scope and evidence boundary
Reference 150 in the 2013 Huntington botanical review (PMID 24347915) describes an isolated-sesamol rat comparison. The investigators identify the water maze and elevated plus maze as memory tasks here and report improved memory performance after pre-treatment. The abstract gives no anxiety-scale result, anxiety-specific maze readout or oral culinary oil exposure. It reports no clinical freezing or dystonia result. The 2009 motor report (PMID 19504387) shares authors and model class. Welfare, sampling and cohort lineage remain unverified because the Wiley PDF route did not supply the original tables.
