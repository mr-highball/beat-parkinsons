Title: Phytoconstituents and diuretic activity of Cymbopogon citratus leaf infusions in humans
Publication: Ekpenyong, Daniel and Akpan, September 2014, Journal of Coastal Life Medicine 2(9):704–713
Source: https://www.academia.edu/83649592/Phytoconstituents_and_diuretic_activity_of_Cymbopogon_citratus_leaf_infusions_in_humans
Coverage: factual extract of the accessible primary source

One hundred five normotensive adults, 55 men and 50 women, received 2, 4 or 8 g of powdered Cymbopogon citratus leaves in approximately 150 mL hot water once daily. The paper compares baseline with day 10 and day 30 without a separate untreated comparison. The trial recorded 24-hour urination frequency on charts and 12-hour collected urine volume. Figure 1 shows increased void frequency compared with baseline for every dose at day 10 and the 4 and 8 g groups at day 30. The dose cohorts and timing closely match the 2014 online renal function, 2016 blood pressure and 2018 acid-base papers. These reports should not be counted as independent replications without participant linkage. No urge sensation, inability to defer voiding or Parkinson cohort was assessed.
