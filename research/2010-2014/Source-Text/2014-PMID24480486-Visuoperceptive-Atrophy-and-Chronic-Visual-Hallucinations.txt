Title: Visuoperceptive region atrophy independent of cognitive status in patients with Parkinson's disease with hallucinations.
PMID: 24480486
PMCID: PMC3983409
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awt360
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3983409/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2014-01-29
Coverage: DOI-verified complete PMC HTML.

A structural MRI study compares 25 people with Parkinson disease and current chronic visual hallucinations with 25 without hallucinations, matched for age and cognitive status. The authors assess visuoperceptive regions and separate cognition from hallucination group status. This comparison does not quantify a change in visual episode frequency after a documented food intake.
