Title: Saffron in the treatment of patients with mild to moderate Alzheimer’s disease: a 16-week, randomized and placebo-controlled trial

The original randomized 46 people with probable mild to moderate Alzheimer disease to dried ethanol-derived stigma extract in capsules or visually matched placebo for 16 weeks. Cognition and dementia staging are efficacy outcomes.

Table 2 reports fatigue in one of 23 extract recipients and two of 23 placebo recipients, with reported Fisher p=1.00. The table counts adverse events and provides no change in fatigue severity or Parkinson comparison. Read alongside the distinct active-comparator report without combining participants across the two papers.
