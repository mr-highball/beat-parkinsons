Title: Impact of beta-glucan on the faecal microbiota of polypectomized patients: a pilot study.
PMID: 21515398
DOI: 10.1016/j.anaerobe.2011.03.025
First published: 2011
Authors: Turunen K, Tsouvelakidou E, Nomikos T, Mountzouris KC, Karamanolis D, Triantafillidis J
Journal: Anaerobe
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21515398/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-month randomized controlled pilot trial (n=33 enrolled, n=20 completers, mean age 57.6 y, polypectomized patients) comparing 125 g/d bread with 3 g/d barley-derived beta-glucan vs placebo bread. Reported reduced bloating and abdominal pain scores on days 30-37 vs placebo and increased acetate molar ratio at day 90 (p=0.018), with no change in fecal moisture or pH. Limits: barley beta-glucan added to bread, high attrition, non-PD.
