Title: Chemical composition, acute toxicity, and antinociceptive activity of the essential oil of a plant breeding cultivar of basil (Ocimum basilicum L.).
PMID: 21157680
DOI: 10.1055/s-0030-1250607
First published: 2011
Authors: Venâncio AM, Onofre AS, Lira AF, Alves PB, Blank AF, Antoniolli AR, Marchioro M, Estevam Cdos S, de Araujo BS
Journal: Planta medica
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21157680/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical antinociceptive study in Swiss mice (n=8/group) testing subcutaneous Ocimum basilicum L. (cv. Maria Bonita) essential oil (50, 100, 200 mg/kg s.c.; LD50 = 532 mg/kg). Reduced acetic-acid writhing by 48-78% at all doses, increased hot-plate latency by 37-52% at 50 mg/kg (reversed by naloxone), and reduced formalin paw-licking time by 38% (phase 1) and 75% (phase 2) at 200 mg/kg. Non-oral essential oil in mice.
