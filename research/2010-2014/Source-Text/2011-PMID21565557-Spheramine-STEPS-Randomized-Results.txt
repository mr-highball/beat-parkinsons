Title: Intrastriatal transplantation of microcarrier-bound human retinal pigment epithelial cells versus sham surgery in patients with advanced Parkinson's disease: a double-blind, randomised, controlled trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21565557/
PMID: 21565557
DOI: 10.1016/S1474-4422(11)70097-7
Publication date: 2011-05-10
Date basis: NLM electronic date; June issue of Lancet Neurology
Source depth: Primary author abstract; direct ScienceDirect request returned HTTP 403
Format: Original-source factual extract

The study identifies NCT00206687. It reports 35 participants in the intervention arm and 36 in sham surgery. The 12-month mean change in off-medication motor score was −10.5 with cell transplantation and −10.1 with sham surgery, with a between-group p value of 0.90. The randomized study differs in design and participants from the six-person unilateral pilot.

The abstract reports similar overall adverse-event rates and more procedure- or cell-attributed events in the intervention group, mostly neurologic or psychiatric. It does not break out visual hallucinations or episode frequency. The complete arm-specific adverse-event table remains to be verified. No dried-fig intake or other culinary exposure was assessed.
