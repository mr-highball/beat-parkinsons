Title: The changing face of Parkinson's disease-associated psychosis: a cross-sectional study based on the new NINDS-NIMH criteria
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2891710/
PMID: 20437542
PMCID: PMC2891710
DOI: 10.1002/mds.22839
Publication year: 2010
Text coverage: Paraphrased findings checked against complete official PMC HTML.

Structured interviews of 116 consecutive Parkinson outpatients covered the preceding month. The authors report 42% with hallucinations in any modality, including 16% visual and 35% nonvisual with overlap. These are proportions of patients, and neither dried fig intake nor a food-linked change in episode frequency was measured.

The interview records minor symptoms such as sensed presence, delusions and hallucinations by modality. Overlapping visual and nonvisual categories cannot be added to infer an overall prevalence. The measures describe the preceding month.
