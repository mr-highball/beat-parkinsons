Title: Glucosinolates from pak choi and broccoli induce enzymes and inhibit inflammation and colon cancer differently.
PMID: 24714741
DOI: 10.1039/c3fo60676g
First published: 2014
Authors: Lippmann D, Lehmann C, Florian S, Barknowitz G, Haack M, Mewis I, et al.
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24714741/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse study (Lippmann et al., 2014, Food Funct) feeding mice glucosinolate-poor vs glucosinolate-enriched pak choi (Brassica rapa subsp. chinensis) and broccoli diets for 4 weeks in an AOM/DSS inflammation-driven colon carcinogenesis model. GSL-rich pak choi generated 1-methoxyindolyl-3-methyl-histidine adducts, upregulated colonic Nrf2 targets (Nqo1, Gstm1, Srxn1, GPx2) and AhR target Cyp1a1, and reduced colitis/tumor counts. Preclinical oncology/colitis biomarker study without constipation severity, bowel transit, or Parkinson's neurological endpoints.
