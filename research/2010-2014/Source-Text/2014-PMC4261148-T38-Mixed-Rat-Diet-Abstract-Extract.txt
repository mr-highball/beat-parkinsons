The rodent diet combined standard chow, potato chips, chocolate-chip cookies, peanut butter and powdered chocolate milk. The presentation concerns obesity susceptibility and striatal and behavioral measures. It supplies no isolated potato effect, roasted-potato comparison, insomnia measure or human Parkinson outcome. Individual ingredients cannot be assigned the response to this mixed exposure.
