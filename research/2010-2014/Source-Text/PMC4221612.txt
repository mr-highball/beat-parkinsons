The investigators compare 34 monoallelic GBA Parkinson patients with 60 comparison patients. Persistent hallucinations were operationalized by a UPDRS thought-disorder rating above one over more than six months. The reported hazard ratio was 5.0 with p=0.01. Table 1 shows nine and four individuals meeting that sustained threshold respectively. The time-to-criterion event uses a composite item and persistence across visits, not counted visual episodes. The study supplies no dietary exposure comparison.
