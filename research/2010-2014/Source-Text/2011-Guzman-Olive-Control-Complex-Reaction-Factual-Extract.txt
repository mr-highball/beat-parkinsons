Olive oil control and complex reaction time in soccer players
Source: https://www.jssm.org/volume10/iss2/cap/jssm-10-301.pdf
PMID: 24149875
Coverage: Original publisher PDF and Table 1

Guzman and colleagues published June 1, 2011. Twenty-four female soccer players received 3.5 g daily of DHA-rich fish oil or olive oil over four weeks, 12 in each arm. Table 1 gives complex reaction time in the olive-oil comparator as 0.682 (SD 1.132) before and 0.715 (SD 0.159) after. It reports a group-by-time interaction p=.004. The abstract labels the approximate 0.7 values as milliseconds. Both the unusual unit and baseline spread appear in the original and require clarification. Oil processing grade, an oil-free comparison and a clinical Parkinson attention outcome were unavailable.
