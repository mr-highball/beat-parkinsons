The review discusses visual, auditory, tactile and olfactory experiences separately, contrasts early untreated observations with longitudinal treated cohorts, and surveys pharmacologic and disease-related risk factors. Reported prevalence estimates come from earlier studies with different designs. The review adds no independent participant series or ingredient comparison.
