Title: Digested polyphenols from wild and commercial blackberry extracts in a cell model

Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: original abstract in the author institution's record and NLM

The authors compared simulated digests of extracts from two wild species, Rubus brigantinus and Rubus vagabundus, and a commercial blackberry variety in a neurodegeneration cell model. They report protection for both wild species under the study conditions, whereas the commercial extract was ineffective. This species- and preparation-dependent observation measured a cellular response. It does not measure pain, Parkinson symptoms, or a human reaction to a culinary serving.
