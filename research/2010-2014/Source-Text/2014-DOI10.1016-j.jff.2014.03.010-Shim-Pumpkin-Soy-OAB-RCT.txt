Title: A randomized double-blind placebo-controlled clinical trial of a product containing pumpkin seed extract and soy germ extract to improve overactive bladder-related voiding dysfunction and quality of life
DOI: 10.1016/j.jff.2014.03.010
First published: 2014
Authors: Shim B, Jeong H, Lee S, Hwang S, Moon B, Storni C
Journal: Journal of Functional Foods
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jff.2014.03.010
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal (Exclusion Audit):
Double-blind placebo-controlled 12-week RCT (Journal of Functional Foods 2014;8:111-117; n=120 women aged 35-70 y with OAB) testing 500 mg/day of a proprietary two-ingredient extract blend (Go-Less / Cucuflavone) containing 87.5% oil-free water-soluble pumpkin seed extract (EFLA 940) PLUS 12.5% soy germ isoflavone extract (SoyLife). Reported significant reductions in urination frequency, urgency, incontinence, and nocturia vs placebo. Strictly excluded from monotherapy pumpkin seed and pumpkin seed oil attribution because soy germ phytoestrogens are co-formulated and the pumpkin component is an oil-free water-soluble extract rather than culinary roasted whole seeds or pressed pumpkin seed oil (attribution_fit=unusable).
