Mythri and colleagues, British Journal of Nutrition 106:63–72, online April 8, 2011. PMID 21473798, DOI 10.1017/S0007114510005817.
The full original describes boiled whole turmeric powder in drinking water before MPTP injury. The primary reported measures concern glutathione, complex I activity, protein nitration and neuronal tissue. The authors report no apparent general-activity change during supplementation without publishing activity data. No formal clinical bradykinesia, rigidity or pain result.
