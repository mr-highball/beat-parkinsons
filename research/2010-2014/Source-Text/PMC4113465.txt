Title: Antecollis and levodopa-responsive parkinsonism are late features of Dravet syndrome
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4113465/
PMID: 24850485
PMCID: PMC4113465
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0000000000000521
Publication date: 2014-05-21
Date basis: NLM electronic publication date. Journal issue 2014-06-17.
Source depth: Complete official PMC article body
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Twelve adults with genetically documented Dravet syndrome underwent a prospective motor examination. Eleven had parkinsonism. The motor scale correlated with age, with no corresponding association with seizure frequency reported. Neck posture and gait impairment had several forms. Two severely affected participants who received levodopa showed improvement in slowness or rigidity. This intervention experience is uncontrolled.
