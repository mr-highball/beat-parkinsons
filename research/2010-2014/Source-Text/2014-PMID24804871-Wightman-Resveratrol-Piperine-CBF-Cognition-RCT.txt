Title: Effects of resveratrol alone or in combination with piperine on cerebral blood flow parameters and cognitive performance in human subjects: a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, cross-over investigation.
PMID: 24804871
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114514000737
First published: 2014
Authors: Wightman EL, Reay JL, Haskell CF, Williamson G, Dew TP, Kennedy DO
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24804871/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=23 healthy adults comparing placebo, trans-resveratrol (250 mg), and trans-resveratrol (250 mg) + piperine (20 mg). Co-supplementation with piperine increased frontal cortex cerebral blood flow during cognitive tasks relative to placebo and resveratrol alone, but cognitive task performance, mood, and blood pressure were completely unaffected. Bundled with resveratrol and null on cognition.
