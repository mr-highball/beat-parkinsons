Title: Beverage coadministration and observed tremor in a rat seizure model
First online: 2014-12-03
PMID: 28911389
DOI: 10.1016/j.jfda.2014.07.012
Source: https://www.fda.gov.tw/tc/includes/GetFile.ashx?id=f636694426583419404
Text type: original-source evidence summary

Karmakar and colleagues used a rat seizure model and varied beverages given with carbamazepine. In Table 3, mean tremor-onset time (SEM) was 2.60 (0.18) minutes with black tea, 2.94 (0.43) with grapefruit juice and 1.40 (0.21) with water, each in drug-treated groups of nine rats. The stated table significance symbols compare with the challenge-only group, whose mean was 0.83 (0.09), rather than testing the beverage groups against the water group. The outcome is induced seizure tremor onset. It does not estimate Parkinson rest-tremor severity following ordinary brewed black tea or raw grapefruit.
