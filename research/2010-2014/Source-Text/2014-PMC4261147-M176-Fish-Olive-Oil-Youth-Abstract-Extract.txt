Title: ACNP Monday M176, fish oil versus olive-oil placebo in depressed youth
PMCID: PMC4261147
DOI: 10.1038/npp.2014.280
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4261147/#sec526
First public date: 2014-12-03, original journal metadata.
Coverage: Factual extract of original M176 abstract in complete Monday M1–M270 collection.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Sixty medication-free depressed youth with a parent with bipolar I disorder were randomized to fish oil or olive-oil placebo for 12 weeks. The abstract reports depression-scale improvement of 61% versus 55%, p=.39; remission 46% versus 54%, reported p=.1; CGI-I response 64% versus 36%, p=.04. These are separate endpoints with differing comparisons. Both groups improved over time.

An untreated comparison is absent and the olive-oil grade is unspecified. No separate anxiety, insomnia or postural-instability outcome is supplied. Publication overlap with other McNamara cohorts remains an open trail. This abstract does not isolate a causal benefit of culinary olive oil.
