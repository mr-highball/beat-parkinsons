Title: Multicomponent Parkinson diet and symptoms in one case
First published: 2012-06-25
PMID: 22846082
DOI: 10.1089/jmf.2012.0060
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22846082/
Text type: original published abstract evidence summary

Renoudet and colleagues describe one patient with Parkinson disease whose baseline multifood diet included extra-virgin olive oil. The initial diet did not prevent described clinical decline. The patient later added foods enriched in fisetin and N-hexacosanol. At six months after the additions, the clinician described apparent resolution of several symptoms including dystonia, with little tremor improvement. The abstract does not grade rest tremor or dystonia, verify a fresh-chive exposure, isolate olive oil, or include a comparison group. The original full main body remains an access lead.
