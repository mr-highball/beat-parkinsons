2012 — Multi-food diet and physician-observed dystonia in one Parkinson case
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22846082/
Coverage: Factual extract limited to the primary published abstract

The case concerns one Parkinson outpatient who adopted a multi-food diet. The clinician described apparent resolution of dystonia at six months after adding foods rich in fisetin and N-hexacosanol. The abstract gives no separate dystonia rating, comparison group or named fresh-chive exposure. The full clinical narrative is still an access lead.
