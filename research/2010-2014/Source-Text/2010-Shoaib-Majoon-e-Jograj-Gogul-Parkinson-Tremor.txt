Title: Therapeutic evaluation of Majoon-e-Jograj gogul in Parkinson’s disease
Source: https://ccrum.res.in/writereaddata/UploadFile/HJUM%2C%20Volume%205%20%284%29%20Oct%E2%80%93Dec%202010_1598.pdf
Citation: Shoaib, Mohsin and Siddiqui. Hippocratic Journal of Unani Medicine 5(4):1–14.
Publication date: October–December 2010
Coverage: Original composition table, methods and tremor results verified in the Council's issue.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

The authors report a before-and-after clinical series of 40 patients receiving a multi-ingredient Unani formulation. Clinical selection uses resting or pill-rolling tremor, rigidity and other findings. The investigators counted upper-limb oscillations briefly using a stopwatch and report measured tremor frequency of 7.1 ± 1.5 Hz initially and 1.2 ± 1.1 Hz at the end. The four temperament groups are classifications within the treated series. The source presents no parallel untreated or matched placebo arm. Tremor frequency and tremor severity are different measures, and the reported frequency result does not stratify all activation states.

The original composition lists peepal (Ficus religiosa), as well as almond, mint, ginger, black pepper and other ingredients. Peepal is a different species from common fig (Ficus carica). The original does not isolate its effect or any other ingredient's effect. Individual trajectory, stable-medication verification, and a separable causal contrast remain open. The 2023 review's description of Majoon-e-Jograj Gogul cites this original and refers to the same clinical series.
