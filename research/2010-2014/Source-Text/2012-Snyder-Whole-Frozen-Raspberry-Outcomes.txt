Whole frozen red raspberry and measured human outcomes
https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/obj/thesescanada/vol2/OTU/TC-OTU-33687.pdf

Snyder’s thesis title page says fall 2010. The University of Toronto record for handle 1807/33687 lists issuance and public availability on November 29, 2012. In the original thesis, 24 healthy adults completed a four-week exposure to individually quick-frozen whole red raspberries and eight took part in acute comparisons. The chronic phase had no parallel untreated group. Methods and appendices describe oxidative biomarkers and a general symptom questionnaire without a specific anxiety measure. The Methods list second-phase portions of 1, 2 or 4 cups, while the participant instructions print 1, 2 or 3. The two OpenAlex identities link to this one thesis.
