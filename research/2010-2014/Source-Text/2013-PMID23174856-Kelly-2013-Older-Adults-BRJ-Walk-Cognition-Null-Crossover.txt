Title: Effects of short-term dietary nitrate supplementation on blood pressure, O2 uptake kinetics, and muscle and cognitive function in older adults.
PMID: 23174856
DOI: 10.1152/ajpregu.00406.2012
First published: 2013
Authors: Kelly J, Fulford J, Vanhatalo A, Blackwell JR, French O, Bailey SJ, et al.
Journal: American journal of physiology. Regulatory, integrative and comparative physiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23174856/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=12 healthy older adults (60-70 y) taking concentrated BRJ vs nitrate-depleted placebo for 2.5 days: reduced resting systolic/diastolic BP, but no significant effect on 6-minute walk distance, O2 uptake kinetics, or cognitive tests (serial subtractions, RVIP). Competing null trial.
