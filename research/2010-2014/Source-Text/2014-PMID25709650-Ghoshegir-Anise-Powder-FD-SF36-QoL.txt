Title: Pimpinella anisum in modifying the quality of life in patients with functional dyspepsia: A double-blind randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 25709650
PMCID: PMC4333517
First published: 2014
Authors: Ghoshegir SA, Mazaheri M, Ghannadi A, Feizi A, Babaeian M, Tanhaee M, Karimi M, Adibi P
Journal: Journal of research in medical sciences : the official journal of Isfahan University of Medical Sciences
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4333517/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Full-text verified SF-36 quality-of-life companion report (PMC4333517; same n=107 cohort as PMC4354059, IRCT2013101214980) testing 9 g/day ground Pimpinella anisum fruit powder (n=47) vs cornstarch placebo (n=60) for 4 weeks in postprandial distress syndrome (excluding major depression and IBS). Baseline PCS (167 vs 159, p = 0.1) and MCS (165 vs 172, p = 0.1) were balanced. At week 12, anise improved PCS to 251 vs placebo 141 (p = 0.0001) and MCS to 233 vs placebo 135 (p = 0.0001), with domain gains >5 points across all 8 domains (BP +53, GH +34, SF +28, MH +24, VT +23, PR +20, ER +15, PF +10). Generic SF-36 mental health in dyspepsia patients without clinical depression provides contextual mood support rather than direct clinical depression/anxiety rating.
