Title: Ouafi and Bounaga study of Deglet Nour date fruit fractions and mouse nociception
Source: https://cms.idpc.ae/uploads/cn10iilffu0mvhtrhk4g/2024/7/193fc0cd-73a8-40a4-873b-7ce5c1b0878c.pdf#page=442
Publication year: 2014
Date basis: Printed date of the Fifth International Date Palm Conference, March 16–18, 2014, and publication year in the Cadi Ayyad University library catalog.
Bibliographic record: https://ucarech.uca.ma/open-research/ouvrage.php?id=168
Citation: Ouafi S, Bounaga N. Proceedings of the Fifth International Date Palm Conference, edited by Zaid A and Alhadrami GA. Khalifa International Date Palm Award. ISBN 978-9948-22-868-4, pp. 441–444.
Text coverage: Original summary of the complete conference paper, including the rendered methods, Table 1 and Figure 2.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The investigators collected 22 specimens of Phoenix dactylifera Deglet Nour fruit in the Biskra region in 2012. The abstract names Adrar, while the Methods and conclusion identify Biskra. The paper describes phenolic fractions from fruit, including anthocyanins and glycosides. Its animal study involved 40 albino mice in total and counted abdominal writhes after an induced stimulus. The preparations were isolated fractions given by gastric administration. The paper does not report a comparison of whole dried fruit.

Table 1 gives mean writhes of 21 ± 3.90 in the control group, 10.8 ± 0.6 in the reference group and 5.4 ± 0.3 and 5.5 ± 0.3 for the two fractions. It labels those fractions anthocyanins with C-glycosides and heterosides. The table and figure report 32.5% protection for the reference and 66.3% for each fraction. Applying the printed formula to the listed means gives approximately 48.6%, 74.3% and 73.8%. The Methods name Feldene as the reference, while the abstract, table and figure name aspirin. Those discrepancies leave the reported comparative magnitude and active-control identity unresolved. Group-specific sample sizes, the meaning of the reported spread and checks for changes in movement were not found in the paper.

The lower counts are an observed direction in an acute animal assay. The report does not identify a Parkinson cohort, clinical pain outcome, whole dried-date exposure or an apathy measure. An attributed customary abdominal-pain practice is cataloged separately under Cultural and Experiential.
