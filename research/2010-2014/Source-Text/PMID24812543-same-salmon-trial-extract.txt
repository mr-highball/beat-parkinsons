Fish consumption, sleep, daily functioning and heart-rate variability
Original factual extract of a published primary study

Hansen AL and colleagues. Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine. 2014;10(5):567–575.
PMID 24812543
PMCID PMC4013386
DOI 10.5664/jcsm.3714
Record https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24812543/

The Wisconsin forensic-facility randomized trial involved 95 adult men. Farmed Atlantic salmon was served three times a week for 23 weeks and compared with meals based on chicken, pork or beef. The article reports objective sleep and heart-rate variability and subjective sleep quality and daily functioning. The recruitment, July 2008 baseline, intervention season, sample, facility, meals and arms match the separate Hansen state-trait anxiety publication PMID 25431880. Treat the two papers as reports from the same trial and use the latter article for the anxiety comparisons.
