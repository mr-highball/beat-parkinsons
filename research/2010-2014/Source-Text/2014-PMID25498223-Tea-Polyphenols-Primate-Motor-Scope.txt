Title: Tea polyphenols alleviate motor impairments, dopaminergic neuronal injury, and cerebral alpha-synuclein aggregation in MPTP-intoxicated parkinsonian monkeys

Related research: research/2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7140513-Round1-Original-Scope.txt

The authors report that an oral tea-polyphenol preparation favored broader motor performance and dopaminergic measures in a primate Parkinson model. The cited review describes the preparation as green-tea polyphenols. The abstract does not give a separately identifiable bradykinesia contrast, arm-level analyzed counts, or the clinical effect of brewed black tea. Keep the primate evidence separate from the human green-tea delayed-start trial and the animal black-tea theaflavin reports.
