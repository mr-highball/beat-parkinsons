Title: Sequence variants in eukaryotic translation initiation factor 4-gamma (eIF4G1) are associated with Lewy body dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3580022/
PMCID: PMC3580022
PMID: 23124435
DOI: 10.1007/s00401-012-1059-4
Publication date: 2012-11-04
Date basis: Publisher or NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Primary familial clinicopathology and genetic series
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Records and neuropathology compare carriers of EIF4G1 variants. Selected case courses describe later delusions and hallucinations after motor or cognitive changes. Some narratives combine perceived presence, speech and visual material. They do not provide matched episode windows, an ingredient contrast or an estimate that separates the symptom modalities.
