Project report on eugenol and abnormal movement
Source: https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/en/report/KAKENHI-PROJECT-20500714/20500714seika/
Coverage: official dated final project report and full PDF; relationship to 2011 journal abstract

The public KAKEN page dates the fiscal-2010 final report to January 26, 2012. The project describes isolated eugenol and zingerone in a mouse Parkinson model. The recorded motor sign was abnormal hind-limb extension after levodopa treatment, which the compounds did not suppress. The report also describes less favorable striatal dopamine measurements after treatment. It gives no gait, walking-speed or ground-spice comparison. The 2011 journal abstract has overlapping authors and design. Its full behavioral and neurochemical tables remain unavailable. [Full government report PDF](https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/ja/file/KAKENHI-PROJECT-20500714/20500714seika.pdf).
