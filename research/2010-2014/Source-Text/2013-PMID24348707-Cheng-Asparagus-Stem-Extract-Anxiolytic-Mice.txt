Title: Evaluation of anxiolytic-like effect of aqueous extract of asparagus stem in mice.
PMID: 24348707
PMCID: PMC3853311
DOI: 10.1155/2013/587260
First published: 2013
Authors: Cheng L, Pan GF, Sun XB, Huang YX, Peng YS, Zhou LY
Journal: Evidence-based complementary and alternative medicine : eCAM
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24348707/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled preclinical behavioral study (Cheng et al., 2013, Evid Based Complement Alternat Med, PMC3853311) in male ICR mice randomly divided into 5 groups receiving oral aqueous extract of Asparagus officinalis L. stems (AEAS 0.6, 1.5, or 3.0 g/kg, equivalent to 100, 250, and 500 mg/kg sarsasapogenin), water control, or diazepam 3.0 mg/kg p.o. In the elevated plus maze (EPM), AEAS at 1.5 and 3.0 g/kg significantly increased percentage of open-arm time without altering total arm entries (ruling out gross locomotor confounding), and in the Vogel conflict test increased punished licks to 177% and 174% of control while lowering serum cortisol and elevating brain serotonin. Limits: non-PD mouse model using concentrated aqueous stem extract standardized to sarsasapogenin rather than steamed culinary asparagus spears.
