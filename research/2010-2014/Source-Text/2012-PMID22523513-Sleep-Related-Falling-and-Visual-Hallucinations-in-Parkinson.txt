Title: Sleep-related falling out of bed in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3325432/
PMID: 22523513
PMCID: PMC3325432
DOI: 10.3988/jcn.2012.8.1.51
Publication year: 2012
Text coverage: Paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

Thirteen of 50 Parkinson patients reported sleep-related falling out of bed. The original reports more people with a history of visual hallucinations among those who fell. Five fallers with hallucinations had used quetiapine, and the groups differed in some medication use. This is a patient-history association with sleep-related falling, with no dried fig exposure or episode-rate comparison.
