Schmitz AE and colleagues, Biol Trace Elem Res 158:399–409 online April 11, 2014, PMID 24723215, DOI 10.1007/s12011-014-9951-5. Original paper Figure 1 and UFSC thesis Figure 5. Six young-mouse groups tested experimental dietary curcumin, inhaled manganese, combinations and control. Both curcumin-alone groups, manganese alone and both combined groups performed poorer skilled forepaw pellet reaching than control. Beam walking and spontaneous open-field activity showed no clear difference. The paper methods state eight per group, while the figure legend gives N=18. Actual analyzed sample is unresolved. The thesis and paper appear to report the same experiment. The isolated dietary constituent, model and reaching task differ from clinical bradykinesia after ground turmeric.
