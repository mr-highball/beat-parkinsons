Title: Efficacy and safety of standardized extract of Trigonella foenum-graecum L seeds as an adjuvant to L-Dopa in the management of patients with Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 23512705
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.4969
First published: 2014
Authors: Nathan J, Panjwani S, Mohan V, Joshi V, Thakurdesai PA
Journal: Phytotherapy research : PTR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23512705/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract:
The objective of this study is to evaluate disease modifying efficacy and safety of a standardized extract of Trigonella foenum-graecum L, Fenugreek (IBHB) (family Fabaceae) as a nutritional adjuvant to Levo-dopa (L-Dopa) in Parkinson's disease (PD) patients. We conducted double-blind placebo-controlled proof of concept clinical study of IBHB capsules (300 mg, twice daily) with matching placebo for 6 months of period in 50 patients of PD stabilized on L-Dopa therapy. The efficacy outcome measures were the scores of Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS - total and its subsections), and Hoehn and Yahr (H&Y) staging at baseline and end of 6-months treatment duration. Safety evaluation included haematology, biochemistry, urinalysis parameters and adverse event monitoring. Total UPDRS scores in IBHB treatment (0.098%) showed slower rise as opposed to steep rise (13.36%) shown by placebo. Further, Clinically Important Difference for total UPDRS scores and scores of motor subsection of UPDRS was found to be 5.3 and 4.8, respectively, in favour of IBHB treatment. Similar improvement was shown by IBHB in terms of H&Y staging as compared with placebo. IBHB was found to have excellent safety and tolerability profile. In conclusion, IBHB can be useful adjuvant treatment with L-Dopa in management of PD patients.

Verified Round 4 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Double-blind, placebo-controlled proof-of-concept RCT (Phytother Res 2014;28(2):172-178; industry-affiliated co-authors from Indus Biotech Pvt. Ltd.) in 50 PD patients stabilized on L-DOPA therapy. Participants received standardized hydroalcoholic fenugreek seed extract IBHB (300 mg capsules twice daily = 600 mg/day) or matching placebo for 6 months alongside L-DOPA. Total UPDRS scores rose much more slowly with IBHB (+0.098%) than placebo (+13.36%), yielding a Clinically Important Difference (CID) of 5.3 points for Total UPDRS and 4.8 points for the UPDRS Motor subsection (Part III) favoring IBHB, with parallel Hoehn & Yahr staging improvement and excellent safety/tolerability (hematology, biochemistry, urinalysis, AEs). Limitations: proprietary standardized seed extract (not culinary ground/toasted seed), small proof-of-concept n=50, single center, industry sponsorship, exact between-group p-values, ITT/dropout counts, and individual UPDRS subscores (tremor, rigidity, bradykinesia, gait, dyskinesia, OFF time, non-motor) are not separately reported in the public record, and no independent replication has been published.
