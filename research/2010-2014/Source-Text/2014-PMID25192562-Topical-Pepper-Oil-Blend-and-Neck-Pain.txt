Title: A topical essential-oil blend for neck pain
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25192562/
PMID: 25192562
DOI: 10.1089/acm.2013.0453
Publication date: 2014-09-05
Coverage: Original author abstract, topical intervention, comparator and reported outcome scope.

Sixty adults with neck pain were randomized to cream with black pepper, marjoram, lavender and peppermint oils or unscented cream. The authors report lower visual-analog pain scores in both groups and changes in pressure tolerance in the blend arm. The abstract does not give a separate black pepper effect or a direct comparative VAS estimate. The multicomponent topical preparation and neck pain population differ from oral cracked black pepper and Parkinson pain.
