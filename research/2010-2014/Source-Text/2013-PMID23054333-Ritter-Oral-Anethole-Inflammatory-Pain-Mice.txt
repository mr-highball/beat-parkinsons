Title: Antihypernociceptive activity of anethole in experimental inflammatory pain.
PMID: 23054333
DOI: 10.1007/s10787-012-0152-6
First published: 2013
Authors: Ritter AM, Domiciano TP, Verri WA, Zarpelon AC, da Silva LG, Barbosa CP, Natali MR, Cuman RK et al.
Journal: Inflammopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23054333/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse study (Ritter et al., 2013, Inflammopharmacology) evaluating oral pretreatment with isolated anethole (125, 250, 500 mg/kg p.o.; GC-MS confirmed 98.1% anethole from star anise oil) in acute carrageenan and persistent CFA inflammatory paw models. Oral anethole inhibited paw edema, mechanical hypernociception, MPO activity, TNF-alpha, IL-1beta, and IL-17 without altering PGE2 hypernociception or liver AST/ALT/histology over 7 days. Limits: isolated purified anethole (shared across anise, fennel, and star anise) in non-PD mouse paw inflammation, not whole culinary ground I. verum or human PD pain.
