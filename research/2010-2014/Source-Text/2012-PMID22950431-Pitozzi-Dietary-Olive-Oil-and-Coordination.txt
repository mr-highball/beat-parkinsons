Title: Long-term dietary extra-virgin olive oil rich in polyphenols reverses age-related dysfunctions in motor coordination and contextual memory in mice: role of oxidative stress

Review depth: abstract

The investigators fed ageing mice a diet containing polyphenol-rich extra-virgin olive oil and observed motor coordination, memory and biochemical outcomes after extended treatment. The abstract reports less age-associated loss of rotarod coordination with the dietary treatment. It does not specify cold-press status or report a separate Parkinson bradykinesia outcome or arm-level effect estimates.
