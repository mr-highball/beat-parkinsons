Later coronary depression EPA trial registration
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02021669
Registration: NCT02021669
Coverage: Official registry, first posted December 27, 2013 (estimated)

The study compares 2 g/day corn oil versus 2 g/day EPA for ten weeks. Both groups receive sertraline 50 mg/day. The primary outcome is Beck Depression Inventory II change from baseline. The current record gives 144 randomized and results first posted August 6, 2019. The enrollment period and registration distinguish this from the earlier Carney trial.
