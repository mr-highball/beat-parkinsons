Fresh curry leaf chewing and saliva pH
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3807960/
Coverage: Factual extract of the original public document.

Sixty healthy volunteers were allocated to chew fresh tulsi, mint or curry leaves and then expectorate. Mean saliva pH increased shortly after chewing across groups. Immediate mint-versus-curry comparisons gave p < .01, and a 30-minute tulsi-versus-curry comparison gave p < .05. Later saliva pH comparisons were inconclusive. The measurements concern pH and tongue coating. There was no reported flow rate, bolus transit, swallowing safety or voice volume measure.
