Title: The antioxidant level of Alaska's wild berries: high, higher and highest
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3751288/
PMID: 23977647
PMCID: PMC3751288
DOI: 10.3402/ijch.v72i0.21188
First public article date: 2013-08-05
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original food processing and literature boundaries

The investigators compare laboratory antioxidant-capacity measures
for Alaska wild berries and their preparations. Their four species
in the home-processing comparison are lingonberry, bog blueberry,
salmonberry and highbush cranberry. The article describes gathering
and preparing wild berries in a subsistence and food-access context.

The discussion mentions strawberries in other laboratory comparisons
and in the Devore human cognition and Joseph rat studies. These
citations supply strawberry leads, with no strawberry processing arm
in the article's own four-berry comparison. The chemistry assay
does not measure individual clinical benefit or falls.
