Cayenne capsules and repeated running sprints
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22130402/
PMID: 22130402
DOI: 10.1519/JSC.0b013e3182429ae5
Coverage: Primary paper abstract and original thesis methods

Opheim and Rankin reported a placebo-controlled crossover in 19 healthy male athletes. Six commercial cayenne capsules per day supplied 3 g cayenne and 25.8 mg capsaicin for seven days. The [original thesis methods](https://vtechworks.lib.vt.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/0344840d-4e07-4afd-87e6-e251cf12751f/content) identify the GNC Nature's Fingerprint product and a toasted wheat flour capsule comparator. Testing covered 15 electronically timed 30-meter maximal sprints. Fastest and average sprint times and measured fatigue showed no between-treatment difference. The combined rating of gastrointestinal symptoms was 6.3 times higher on cayenne. The protocol tested maximal running in trained adults. It did not assess everyday walking speed, a Parkinson cohort, or cayenne added to meals.
