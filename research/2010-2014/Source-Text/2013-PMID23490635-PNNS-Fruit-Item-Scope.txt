The French PNNS questionnaire validation lists vegetables as item 8 and fruit as item 9 in the combined fruits-and-vegetables recommendation. The item scores track entire food categories. The article supplies no separate date-fruit intake estimate. The PACAPh protocol cites this validation for its aggregate nutrition questionnaire. The protocol independently lists the EQ-5D-5L pain domain but does not assign or measure a separate date-fruit exposure.
