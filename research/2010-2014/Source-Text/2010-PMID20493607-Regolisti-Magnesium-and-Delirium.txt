Page 168 lists delirium with hallucinations during a period of neurologic deterioration, with no modality or episode rate. Clinical signs and electrolyte abnormalities improved overall during intravenous magnesium treatment. Table 1 on page 169 reports serum magnesium of 0.42 mEq/L before and 1.48 mEq/L after infusion. The later clinical follow-up records changes in serum electrolytes and drug exposure. It does not separately track visual episodes. The NLM electronic date is May 20, 2010, and the printed note says May 21. There is no Parkinson or dried fig exposure in the case.
