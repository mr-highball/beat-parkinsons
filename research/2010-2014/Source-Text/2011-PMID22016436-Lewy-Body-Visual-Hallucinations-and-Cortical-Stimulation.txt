Title: Visual hallucinations in dementia with Lewy bodies: transcranial magnetic stimulation study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3227808/
PMID: 22016436
PMCID: PMC3227808
DOI: 10.1192/bjp.bp.110.090373
Publication year: 2011
Text coverage: Paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

Cortical stimulation in 21 dementia with Lewy bodies patients and 19 controls found no overall group difference in phosphene threshold or response rate. Within patients both measures correlated with the past-month hallucination frequency-by-severity score. Six patients and no controls reported transient complex images during stimulation. This study does not measure a dried fig effect or spontaneous Parkinson episode rates.

The past-month score combines frequency and severity, while the acute stimulation outcome records induced responses. The group-level null and within-group correlations answer different questions.
