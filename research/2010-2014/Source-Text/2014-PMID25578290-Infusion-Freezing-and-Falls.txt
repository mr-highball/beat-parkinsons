The prospective open-label report included five Parkinson patients whose disabling freezing was described as unresponsive to prior levodopa treatment. Under continuous intestinal levodopa-carbidopa infusion, the abstract reports a 14% improvement on a freezing questionnaire and a fall-frequency score change from 3 to 0 at six months. A timed walking assessment was unchanged. This is an uncontrolled drug-delivery observation without lemon balm exposure. The abstract does not establish a counted frequency of freezing episodes.
