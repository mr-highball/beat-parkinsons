Title: Effect of crocetin from Gardenia jasminoides Ellis on sleep: a pilot study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20537515/
PMID: 20537515
DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2010.03.025
Publication: 2010-09 issue
Coverage: Factual extract from the original indexed investigator abstract.

Kuratsune and colleagues recruited 21 healthy men reporting mild sleep complaints for a double-blind placebo-controlled crossover. The original report identifies Gardenia jasminoides as the botanical source of purified crocetin. It reports fewer actigraphy-detected waking episodes (p=.025) with crocetin. The subjective St Mary's Hospital sleep questionnaire result is described as a tendency relative to pretreatment, with no numerical between-treatment estimate in the indexed abstract. Full arm-level endpoints and the analysis denominator remain open. This study did not intervene with culinary saffron or examine Parkinson insomnia severity.
