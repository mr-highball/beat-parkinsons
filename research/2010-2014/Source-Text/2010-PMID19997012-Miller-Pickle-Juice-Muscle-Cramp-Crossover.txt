Title: Reflex inhibition of electrically induced muscle cramps in hypohydrated humans.
PMID: 19997012
DOI: 10.1249/MSS.0b013e3181c0647e
First published: 2010
Authors: Miller KC, Mack GW, Knight KL, Hopkins JT, Draper DO, Fields PJ et al.
Journal: Med Sci Sports Exerc
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19997012/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized crossover laboratory experiment (Miller et al., 2010, Med Sci Sports Exerc, n=10 hypohydrated healthy men) comparing ~74 mL dill pickle juice versus deionized water immediately upon electrically induced flexor hallucis brevis muscle cramp. Pickle juice shortened cramp duration by 49.1 +/- 14.6 s (84.6 +/- 18.5 vs 133.7 +/- 15.9 s, p<0.05) too rapidly (~85 s) to be explained by plasma electrolyte changes, supporting an acetic-acid-mediated oropharyngeal reflex. Limits: liquid brine (not eaten solid pickles), n=10 healthy athletes, peripheral electrically induced cramp rather than Parkinson dystonia or pain.
