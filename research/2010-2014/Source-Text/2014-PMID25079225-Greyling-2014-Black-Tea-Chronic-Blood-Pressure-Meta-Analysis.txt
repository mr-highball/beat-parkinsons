Title: The effect of black tea on blood pressure: a systematic review with meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 25079225
PMCID: PMC4117505
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0103247
First published: 2014
Authors: Greyling A, Ras RT, Zock PL, Lorenz M, Hopman MT, Thijssen DH, et al.
Journal: PloS one
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4117505/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 11 placebo-controlled RCTs (12 arms, n=378 adults, >=1 week of 4-5 cups/d black tea as sole intervention): regular black tea intake reduced SBP by -1.8 mmHg (95% CI -2.8 to -0.7, p=0.0013) and DBP by -1.3 mmHg (95% CI -1.8 to -0.8, p<0.0001). Context-only synthesis showing opposite BP direction to acute pressor response; no PD or OH symptom data.
