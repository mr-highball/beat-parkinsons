Original study scope
The original Figure 4 and Figure 5 climbing comparisons show partial recovery with isolated curcumin in younger paraquat-exposed flies and no comparable rescue in later-life flies. The outcome is negative geotaxis, quantified by climbing height in a fixed interval. The design separates fly age and curcumin timing.

Continuing evidence gaps
Fly movement outcomes give stage-dependent motor context, with no human rigidity or ground-turmeric comparison.
