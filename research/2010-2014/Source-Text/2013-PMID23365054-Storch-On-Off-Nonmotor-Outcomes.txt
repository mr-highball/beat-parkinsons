Title: Nonmotor fluctuations in Parkinson disease: severity and correlation with motor complications
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23365054/
PMID: 23365054
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0b013e318285c0ed
Publication date: 2013-01-30
Date basis: PubMed electronic article date.
Coverage: Paraphrased original structured abstract. Full results tables remain open.

The investigators compared ten specified nonmotor measures across motor-defined on and off periods in 100 people with advanced fluctuating Parkinson disease. Seven were more frequent and severe off. Dysphagia, excess sweating and bladder urgency were exceptions in the authors' analysis. The ten measures included dysphagia and did not include a drooling item. No ingredient comparison was reported.
