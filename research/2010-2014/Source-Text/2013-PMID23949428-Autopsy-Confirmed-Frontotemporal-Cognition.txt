Title: Presenting neuropsychological testing profile of autopsy-confirmed frontotemporal lobar degeneration.
PMID: 23949428
PMCID: PMC4559565
DOI: 10.1159/000353860
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4559565/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2013-08-16
Coverage: complete PMC JATS.

The diagnostic study compares initial neuropsychological testing in autopsy-confirmed frontotemporal lobar degeneration and Alzheimer disease. A motor scale helps describe extrapyramidal features. The endpoints are cognitive testing and pathological classification rather than an ingredient or visual episode frequency.
