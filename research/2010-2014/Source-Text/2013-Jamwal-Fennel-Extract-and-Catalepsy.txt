Title: Antidepressant Activity of Methanolic Extract of Foeniculum Vulgare (Fennel) Fruits in Experimental Animal Models
DOI: 10.7324/JAPS.2013.3912
Publication date: 2013-09-30
Citation: Jamwal, Kumar and Rana. Journal of Applied Pharmaceutical Science 3(9), 65–70.
Source: https://japsonline.com/admin/php/uploads/1050_pdf.pdf
Coverage: Primary publisher PDF, methods and Table 3 reviewed.

The paper used separate forced-swim, norepinephrine and haloperidol preparations. The haloperidol arm tested oral methanolic fennel-fruit extract at 500 mg/kg with groups of six mice. In Table 3, duration of imposed bar posture on day seven was 125.17 ± 2.44 seconds with the extract and 185.83 ± 2.27 seconds with haloperidol alone, mean ± SEM. The table reports p<0.001 for the comparison and gives 89.00 ± 2.38 seconds for the fluoxetine comparator. Data for days three and five point in the same direction for catalepsy. A separate norepinephrine challenge had two deaths in each of six mice with or without extract. The motor endpoint was time in a posture imposed by the experimenter, with no resting tremor score or roasted culinary exposure.
