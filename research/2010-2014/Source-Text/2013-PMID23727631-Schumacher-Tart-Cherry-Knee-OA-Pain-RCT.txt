Title: Randomized double-blind crossover study of the efficacy of a tart cherry juice blend in treatment of osteoarthritis (OA) of the knee.
PMID: 23727631
DOI: 10.1016/j.joca.2013.05.009
First published: 2013
Authors: Schumacher HR, Pullman-Mooar S, Gupta SR, Dinnella JE, Kim R, McHugh MP
Journal: Osteoarthritis and cartilage
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23727631/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled crossover trial (Schumacher et al., 2013, Osteoarthritis Cartilage) in 58 non-diabetic adults with Kellgren grade 2-3 knee osteoarthritis consuming two 8 oz bottles/day of tart cherry juice blend vs placebo for 6 weeks each (1-week washout). Withdrawals were 5 on cherry juice (4 adverse events) and 7 on placebo (3 adverse events). In ITT analysis, WOMAC scores declined significantly within the cherry period (p<0.01) and not within placebo (p=0.46), but the BETWEEN-TREATMENT difference was NOT statistically significant (p=0.16); walking time, acetaminophen rescue use, and plasma urate were unaffected, though hsCRP declined vs placebo (p<0.01). Crucial competing null between-group clinical osteoarthritis pain and walking-time trial.
