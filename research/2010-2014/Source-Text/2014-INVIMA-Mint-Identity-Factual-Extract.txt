INVIMA. Listado de plantas medicinales aceptadas con fines terapéuticos. July 2014, PDF page 35.

The July 2014 INVIMA list gives Mentha spicata for the Yerbabuena leaf and stem entry. Its preparations column calls the standardized menthol and menthone essential oil Mentha piperitae L. The warning column includes Parkinson disease among the conditions named for an additional restriction on essential oil. The entry records no traceable patient case, culinary fresh-leaf consumption or daily OFF result. Earlier publication of the warning has not been established.
