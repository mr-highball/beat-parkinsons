Injected isolated beta-caryophyllene in mouse behavioral assays
2014-06-13
https://accurateclinic.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Caryophyllene-a-CB2-receptor-agonist-produces-multiple-behavioral-changes-relevant-to-anxiety-and-depression-in-mice-20214.pdf

Bahi and colleagues compared injected isolated beta-caryophyllene in an olive-oil vehicle with the corresponding vehicle control. Open-arm time increased (p=.045), and forced-swim and tail-suspension immobility decreased (p=.021 and p=.015). These are acute mouse behaviors and reflect an isolated constituent. The study supplies no rosemary, cinnamon or oregano ingestion comparison and no Parkinson clinical result.
