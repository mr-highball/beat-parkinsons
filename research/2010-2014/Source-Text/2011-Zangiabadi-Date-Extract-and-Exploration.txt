Coverage: factual summary of full original, including sections 2.2–2.4, 3.2–3.3, Table 1 and Figures 1–2.

Male Wistar rats in an induced diabetic peripheral neuropathy model received an aqueous extract of fresh pitted Bam dates. The study reported more arena travel and rearing in the extract group than the diabetic sham group (p<.05). Time spent in center or perimeter had no clear difference, and grooming was lower in diabetic groups than healthy controls. Motor nerve conduction velocity was higher with extract than in sham. The diabetic-sham and healthy means in Results were 38.50 and 51.36 m/s. The stated reduction of 133.4% conflicts with the means and with the 25% reduction in Discussion. Methods say eight per group; Table 1 labels sham n=9 while its footnote says n=8. This test does not measure apathy, Parkinson disease, or whole dried fruit as food.
