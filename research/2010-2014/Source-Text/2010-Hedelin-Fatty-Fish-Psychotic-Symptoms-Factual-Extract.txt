Title: Fatty fish and psychotic like symptom categories
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-244X-10-38
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

Hedelin and colleagues, May 26, 2010. Analysis included 33,623 Swedish women, aged 30–49 at enrollment. Food-frequency data at study enrollment were linked with a later questionnaire measuring positive psychotic-like experiences. The fatty fish analysis pools herring and mackerel with salmon-type fish; the authors report relative risk 0.81 (95% CI 0.66–0.98) for intermediate intake and 1.90 (1.34–2.70) at high intake for the high-symptom classification. The cohort is observational. It does not provide a grilled-salmon or Parkinson visual-hallucination-frequency comparison.
