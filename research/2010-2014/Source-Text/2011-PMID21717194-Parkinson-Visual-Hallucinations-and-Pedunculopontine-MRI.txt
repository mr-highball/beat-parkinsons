Title: The pedunculopontine nucleus is related to visual hallucinations in Parkinson's disease: preliminary results of a voxel-based morphometry study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3251778/
PMID: 21717194
PMCID: PMC3251778
DOI: 10.1007/s00415-011-6149-z
Publication year: 2011
Text coverage: Paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The retrospective structural MRI included 13 non-demented Parkinson patients with visual hallucinations and 16 without, plus 13 Parkinson dementia cases with hallucinations and 11 dementia with Lewy bodies cases. The non-demented Parkinson contrast found a pedunculopontine-region gray matter difference. The thalamic difference appeared in the combined comparison with dementia included. The exposure was clinical group status and the result was structural imaging.

The comparisons used retrospective grouping. The visual group definition required well-formed images of people or animals for at least four weeks. The anatomical contrast does not measure events after an exposure.
