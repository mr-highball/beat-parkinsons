Title: Selected original abstracts from the AES 2014 Annual Meeting
Source: https://aesnet.org/education/annual-meeting/aes-abstract-search
Publication date: 2014
Date basis: Original society member pages give the meeting and publication year as 2014. The consolidated journal supplement was published in January–February 2015.
Source depth: Five complete original society abstract pages. Selected crosswalk, with no coverage claim about the remaining proceedings.
Format: Factual digest of the material inspected

1.112. Antecollis and levodopa-responsive parkinsonism are late features of Dravet syndrome.
Original: https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/antecollis-and-levodopa-responsive-parkinsonism-are-late-features-of-dravet-syndrome
Clinical motor examinations found parkinsonism in 11 of 12 adults with genetically confirmed Dravet syndrome. The participant group and reported values match the earlier 2014 original article (PMID 24850485). The abstract contributes no independent cohort or dry fig comparison.

1.317. Eslicarbazepine acetate as adjunctive therapy for refractory partial-onset seizures: Analysis of cognitive adverse events.
Original: https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/eslicarbazepine-acetate-as-adjunctive-therapy-for-refractory-partial-onset-seizures--analysis-of-cognitive-adverse-events
The pooled analysis covered 1,447 participants in epilepsy trials and 1,705 in non-epilepsy trials. It reports two visual hallucination events on placebo and two differently coded psychotic-disorder reports on active drug in the epilepsy trials. The abstract cannot supply an overall Parkinson or dried fig comparison.

2.115. Bizarre visual hallucinations in a child with PRES syndrome.
Original: https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/bizarre-visual-hallucinations-in-a-child-with-pres-syndrome
The report describes a child with posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome and observed visual episodes temporally associated with epileptic activity on EEG. It addresses a different clinical population and a time-linked event measure. No dietary challenge was made.

2.287. Equivalence among generic AEDs (EQUIGEN), single-dose study.
Original: https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/equivalence-among-generic-aeds-%28equigen%29---single-dose-study
The society uses this member as its sample citation for the supplement. The original concerns lamotrigine formulation bioequivalence in people with epilepsy.

3.181. Accuracy of EEG interpretation without a reference standard.
Original: https://aesnet.org/abstractslisting/accuracy-of-eeg-interpretation-without-a-reference-standard
A university catalogue locates this study on supplement page 471. The member evaluates a model for repeated EEG readings with no reference standard.
