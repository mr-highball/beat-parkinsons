Title: Anethum graveolens: An Indian traditional medicinal herb and spice.

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Ethnopharmacological and phytochemical review (Jana & Shekhawat, 2010, Pharmacogn Rev, PMC3249919) detailing Ayurvedic traditional uses of Anethum graveolens seeds (carminative, stomachic, diuretic) and explicitly contrasting dill seed phytochemistry (predominantly carvone plus coumarins, flavonoids, phenolic acids) against dill herb/foliage (predominantly alpha-phellandrene, limonene, dill ether, myristicin). Key botanical basis for separating dill seed from dill weed.
