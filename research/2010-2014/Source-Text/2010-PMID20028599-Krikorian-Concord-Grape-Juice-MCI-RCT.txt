Title: Concord grape juice supplementation improves memory function in older adults with mild cognitive impairment.
PMID: 20028599
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114509992364
First published: 2010
Authors: Krikorian R, Nash TA, Shidler MD, Shukitt-Hale B, Joseph JA
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20028599/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled 12-week pilot trial in 12 older adults (mean age 78.2 y) with mild cognitive impairment consuming Concord grape juice vs matched placebo. Concord grape juice significantly improved CVLT verbal learning, with non-significant trends for verbal and spatial recall. Critically, there was NO appreciable effect on depressive symptoms (Geriatric Depression Scale null) and no effect on body weight or waist circumference; fasting insulin rose slightly in the juice arm. Limits: very small sample (n=12), juice matrix, no PD cohort.
