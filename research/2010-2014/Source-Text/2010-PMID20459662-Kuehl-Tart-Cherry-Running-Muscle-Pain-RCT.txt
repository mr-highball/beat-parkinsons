Title: Efficacy of tart cherry juice in reducing muscle pain during running: a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 20459662
PMCID: PMC2874510
DOI: 10.1186/1550-2783-7-17
First published: 2010
Authors: Kuehl KS, Perrier ET, Elliot DL, Chesnutt JC
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2874510/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial (Kuehl et al., 2010, J Int Soc Sports Nutr, PMC2874510) in 54 healthy endurance runners (36 male, 18 female, mean age 35.8+/-9.6 years) consuming 355 mL tart cherry juice vs placebo cherry drink twice daily for 7 days before and on the day of a 26.3 km relay race. Post-race 100 mm VAS muscle pain increase was significantly smaller with tart cherry juice (12+/-18 mm) than placebo (37+/-20 mm, p<0.001). Limits: acute exercise-induced muscle pain in healthy runners rather than chronic Parkinson pain, liquid juice blend rather than whole dried fruit.
