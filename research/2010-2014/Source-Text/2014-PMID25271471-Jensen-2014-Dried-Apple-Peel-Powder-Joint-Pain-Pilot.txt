Title: Consumption of dried apple peel powder increases joint function and range of motion.
PMID: 25271471
PMCID: PMC4224039
DOI: 10.1089/jmf.2014.0037
First published: 2014
Authors: Jensen GS, Attridge VL, Benson KF, Beaman JL, Carter SG, Ager D
Journal: Journal of medicinal food
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25271471/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Open-label uncontrolled 12-week clinical pilot in n=12 healthy adults with moderate loss of joint range of motion (ROM) and associated chronic joint pain consuming 4.25 g/day dried apple peel powder (DAPP). Dual digital inclinometry ROM improved across 12 weeks and 100-mm Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) joint pain scores significantly decreased vs baseline (p<0.05 at 4 weeks; p<0.01 at 8-12 weeks), alongside increased serum cellular antioxidant protection (CAP-e) and in vitro COX-2 and lipoxygenase inhibition. Limits: small uncontrolled single-arm pilot, non-PD chronic joint pain, dried peel powder rather than whole raw apple.
