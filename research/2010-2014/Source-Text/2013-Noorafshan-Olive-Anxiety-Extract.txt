Whole olive oil and water vehicle in stressed rats
Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3699679/
PMID: 23833558
DOI: 10.5607/en.2013.22.2.96
Coverage: Factual original body extract.

Noorafshan et al. included an oral olive-oil gavage and a distilled-water gavage among chronically stressed male rats. Time and entries in the plus-maze open arms were lower in both stressed vehicle groups than in the untreated non-stressed comparison. The authors give no separately established olive-oil versus water anxiety contrast, oil grade or Parkinson context.
