The synthesis describes alpha-synuclein, tau and amyloid pathology across Parkinson disease dementia and Lewy body disorders. It describes hallucination presence and cognitive profiles as differentiating features and cites previous risk-factor reports. It does not measure a food exposure or new visual episode rate.
