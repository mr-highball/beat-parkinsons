Title: Vitamin K absorption and kinetics in human subjects after consumption of 13C-labelled phylloquinone from kale
PMID: 20420753
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114510001182
First published: 2010
Authors: Novotny JA, Kurilich AC, Britz SJ, Baer DJ, Clevidence BA
Journal: British Journal of Nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20420753/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Human pharmacokinetic trial in 7 healthy volunteers consuming a single 400 g serving of 13C-labelled kale (156 nmol phylloquinone) with 30 g vegetable oil. Plasma 13C-phylloquinone peaked at 6-10 h (mean 2.1 nmol/L in 6 responders) with mean bioavailability of 4.7% and plasma/tissue half-times of 8.8 and 215 h. Establishes direct human vitamin K absorption from whole kale relevant to warfarin consistency.
