Gait results and resting tremor eligibility in early Parkinson disease
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3437031/

August 2012. Twenty-two patients with early Parkinson disease and 22 controls underwent instrumented gait assessment and short-latency afferent inhibition testing. The Parkinson group showed shorter stride length (p = .008). Significant resting tremor was a screening exclusion for inhibition testing. The reported gait and attention analyses offer no fig intake, visual hallucination frequency or tremor-change effect.
