Black raspberry formulation and patient questionnaires in the Uhrig thesis
https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file/send?accession=osu1416961549&disposition=inline

Ohio State dissertation, 2014, OhioLINK accession osu1416961549. The full thesis describes 112 participants across OSU-06132 and OSU-07085, with a comparison and 4 g or 8 g per day lyophilized black raspberry groups using oral preparations. Printed pp. 48–49 identify the cultivar and formulation. Pages 65–69 describe its questionnaires. General mood appears in a fatigue-interference item and distress appears in an insomnia item. Pages 107–112 report an inconclusive insomnia contrast, with no separate anxiety outcome. The registrations identify two protocols, and the combined thesis is a shared publication.
