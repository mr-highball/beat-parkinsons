Title: Modeled dietary contaminant exposure attributable to strawberries among other foods
PMID: 23140444
PMCID: PMC3551655
DOI: 10.1186/1476-069X-11-83
Authors: Vogt et al.
First published: 2012-11-09
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3551655/
Coverage: full original Methods and Results, including Appendix tables. Local factual extract.

California household food-frequency data were linked to national food-contaminant residue measurements and benchmarks. The investigators estimated exposures attributed to food categories. Strawberries appeared among the contributors to contemporary pesticide intake for some age groups and among the fruit sources in the endosulfan comparison. Amount consumed and residue concentrations jointly determined the modeled ranking. This was neither a controlled strawberry feeding intervention nor an analysis of neurological events. The outcomes were modeled exposure levels rather than observed Parkinson symptoms or falls.
