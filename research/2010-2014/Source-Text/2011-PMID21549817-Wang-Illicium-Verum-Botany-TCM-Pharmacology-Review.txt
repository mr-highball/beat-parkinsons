Title: Illicium verum: a review on its botany, traditional use, chemistry and pharmacology.
PMID: 21549817
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2011.04.051
First published: 2011
Authors: Wang GW, Hu WT, Huang BK, Qin LP
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21549817/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Ethnopharmacological review (Wang et al., 2011, J Ethnopharmacol) synthesizing Illicium verum Hook. f. botany, Traditional Chinese Medicine uses (dispelling cold, regulating Qi, relieving pain), phytochemistry (trans-anethole, shikimic acid, seco-prezizaane sesquiterpenes), pharmacology, and documented adulteration with toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) and poisonous I. lanceolatum. Identifies no human clinical efficacy trials for neurological or Parkinson's symptoms.
