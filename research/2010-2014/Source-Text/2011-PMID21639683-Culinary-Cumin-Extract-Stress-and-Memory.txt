Title: Koppula and Choi on Cuminum cyminum extract, stress biomarkers and memory
Source: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.3109/13880209.2010.541923
PMID: 21639683
DOI: 10.3109/13880209.2010.541923
Publication date: 2011-06-03
Date basis: Electronic publication date printed by the journal.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full published original, pages 702–708.

An aqueous extract of culinary cumin fruit was tested in rats. The forced-swim stress experiment measured urinary vanillylmandelic acid and ascorbic acid. The extract attenuated stress-related marker changes under the reported conditions. A separate scopolamine experiment measured conditioned avoidance learning or recall. These are distinct outcomes. No stand-alone anxiety symptom or anxiety-like behavioral outcome was measured. The preparation also differs from toasted culinary cumin, with no Parkinson population. Reference 49 in PMID 34235220 is this Cuminum study. The mixture tested in that 2021 paper used a different species, Bunium persicum. Round 1 claim 0083.
