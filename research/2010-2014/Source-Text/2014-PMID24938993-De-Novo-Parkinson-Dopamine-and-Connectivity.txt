Title: Dopaminergic modulation of resting-state functional connectivity in de novo patients with Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 24938993
PMCID: PMC6869560
DOI: 10.1002/hbm.22561
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6869560/
Publication date carried by the Europe PMC search: 2014-06-17
Coverage: DOI-verified complete PMC HTML.

The study correlates striatal dopamine transporter imaging with resting-state connectivity in 23 drug-naive people with de novo Parkinson disease. Hallucinations appear in the rationale for differential dopaminergic effects reported elsewhere. The analysis does not test a fig exposure or a clinical visual hallucination endpoint.
