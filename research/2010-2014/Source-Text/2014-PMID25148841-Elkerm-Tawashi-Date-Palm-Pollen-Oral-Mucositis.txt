Title: Date palm pollen as a preventative intervention in radiation- and chemotherapy-induced oral mucositis: a pilot study.
PMID: 25148841
DOI: 10.1177/1534735414547110
First published: 2014
Authors: Elkerm Y, Tawashi R
Journal: Integr Cancer Ther
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25148841/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Small pilot study (Elkerm & Tawashi, 2014, n=20 oncology patients) testing swish-and-swallow date palm pollen (DPP) suspension for chemoradiation-induced oral mucositis and associated swallowing pain. Critical limits: uses date palm pollen (male reproductive grains, NOT dried date fruit) for radiation-mucositis odynophagia rather than neurogenic Parkinson dysphagia.
