Title: Effects of a kefir supplement on symptoms, colonic transit, and bowel satisfaction score in patients with chronic constipation: a pilot study.
PMID: 25599776
DOI: 10.5152/tjg.2014.6990
First published: 2014
Authors: Turan İ, Dedeli Ö, Bor S, İlter T
Journal: The Turkish journal of gastroenterology : the official journal of Turkish Society of Gastroenterology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25599776/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Prospective uncontrolled 4-week clinical pilot in 20 consecutive adults with Rome II functional chronic constipation (10 normal transit, 10 slow transit) consuming 500 mL/day plain probiotic kefir. Kefir significantly increased stool frequency (p<0.001), improved stool consistency (p=0.014), decreased laxative consumption (p=0.031), accelerated colonic transit on repeat marker study in the slow-transit subgroup (p=0.013), and improved VAS bowel satisfaction (p<0.001); straining trended better (p=0.18). Primary limitation: uncontrolled single-arm before-after design (no placebo arm), non-PD cohort.
