Original study scope
The authors measured mouse forced-swim and elevated-plus-maze behavior after a TRPA1 antagonist, isolated cinnamaldehyde and associated controls. Oral cinnamaldehyde alone showed no detected effect on the described behavioral outcomes and blocked the antagonist effect. Intracerebroventricular grape oil was a formulation control in the central antagonist comparison. Raw grapes were not studied.

Continuing evidence gaps
The actual materials are a cinnamon constituent and an injected grape-derived oil; neither provides the frozen culinary pain or raw-grape anxiety contrast.
