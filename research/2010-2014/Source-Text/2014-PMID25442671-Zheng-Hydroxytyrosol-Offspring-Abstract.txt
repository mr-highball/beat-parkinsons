Title: Maternal hydroxytyrosol and cognitive outcomes in a prenatal stress model
PMID: 25442671
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2014.10.006
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25442671/
Publication date: 2014-11-12
Date basis: PubMed electronic publication. Journal of Nutritional Biochemistry 26(2), pp. 190–199, February 2015 issue.
Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: original abstract

The authors tested isolated hydroxytyrosol in pregnant rats subjected to restraint stress. They report improvements in the offspring's learning and memory task performance relative to the stress condition, with related hippocampal and oxidative measures. The exposure was a constituent present in olive oil rather than a comparison of edible oils. The model, developmental stage and measured outcomes differ from postural instability and bradykinesia in Parkinson disease.
