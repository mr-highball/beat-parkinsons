Depressive scores in a trial with rosemary extract in both capsule arms
2013-08-26 (online publication)
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4072122/

Cohen and colleagues randomized 355 women with vasomotor symptoms to omega-3 fish oil or placebo capsules, each containing rosemary extract and natural lemon oil. The PHQ-8 score changed by 0 points in the omega-3 arm and −0.8 in the comparator (p=.10). The trial estimates the omega-3 capsule contrast and leaves the contribution of the shared rosemary ingredient unidentified.
