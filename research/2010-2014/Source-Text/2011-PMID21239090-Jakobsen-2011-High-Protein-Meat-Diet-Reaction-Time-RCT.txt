Title: Effect of a high protein meat diet on muscle and cognitive functions: a randomised controlled dietary intervention trial in healthy men.
PMID: 21239090
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2010.12.010
Registry ID: NCT00621231
First published: 2011
Authors: Jakobsen LH, Kondrup J, Zellner M, Tetens I, Roth E
Journal: Clinical nutrition (Edinburgh, Scotland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21239090/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-week RCT (NCT00621231) in n=23 healthy young men (19-31 y) comparing a high-protein meat diet (3.0 g protein/kg/d) vs usual protein (1.5 g/kg/d): the high-protein group significantly improved reaction time alongside increased plasma BCAAs and phenylalanine; no adverse effects observed. Limits: extreme protein dose (3.0 g/kg/d), pooled meat (not beef alone), small young male sample, conflicts with null older-adult cognitive RCT Formica 2020.
