Title: Efficacy and safety of ginger-salt-indirect moxibustion for urge urinary incontinence after stroke
PMID: 25335962
PMCID: PMC4208054
DOI: 10.1136/bmjopen-2014-006326
Registration: ISRCTN44706974
Source: https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/4/10/e006326
Date basis: 2014-10-21 electronic publication.
Coverage: Factual extract of the complete original protocol and registered endpoint linkage.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The prospective protocol describes three arms of 40 poststroke participants. One is routine care, and the others add different topical moxibustion methods with ginger and salt. Mean volume per void at week 4 is the primary outcome. Mean void frequency, incontinence-related quality of life and adverse events are separate endpoints. The paper supplies no trial outcomes or constipation measure.

The author group cites PMID 17036477 as prior work with different enrollment and arms. The matching 2013 registration carries the exact new protocol ID and lists the protocol without results.
