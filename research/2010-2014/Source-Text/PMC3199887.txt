The investigators analyzed 41 people with probable or possible DLB. They compared 21 acute with 20 chronic presentations and separately grouped the initial complaints by cognitive features. Table 3 summarizes the distribution of symptoms within initial and subsequent symptom categories. The method describes onset and symptom mix rather than the number of recurring visual episodes per person. No dietary intervention was studied.
