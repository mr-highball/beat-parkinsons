Oat bran comparator in a coffee-fruit cognition protocol
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01965795
Coverage: Factual original official record summary.

First posted October 18, 2013. The completed trial compares coffee fruit concentrate with an oat-bran Nutrim comparator in 39 healthy adults. The protocol lists cognitive assessments, with no posted results or separate manual dexterity measure. Its oat-bran product and active comparator cannot estimate a clinical hand outcome after cooked oats. The exposure has no turmeric or curcumin.
