Title: Epileptic seizures caused by accidental ingestion of sage (Salvia officinalis L.) oil in children: a rare, exceptional case or a threat to public health?
PMID: 22353306
DOI: 10.1016/j.pediatrneurol.2012.01.009
First published: 2012
Authors: Lachenmeier DW, Walch SG
Journal: Pediatric neurology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22353306/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Clinical toxicology analysis (Pediatr Neurol 2012) documenting tonic-clonic epileptic seizures precipitated by accidental oral ingestion of Salvia officinalis essential oil in young children, driven by thujone and camphor neurotoxicity. Key seizure/neurotoxicity safety caveat for concentrated sage preparations.
