Title: A double-blind, placebo-controlled, cross-over study to establish the bifidogenic effect of a very-long-chain inulin extracted from globe artichoke (Cynara scolymus) in healthy human subjects.
PMID: 20591206
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114510001571
First published: 2010
Authors: Costabile, Kolida, Klinder, Gietl, Bäuerlein
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20591206/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled crossover RCT in 32 healthy adults consuming 10 g/d very-long-chain inulin (VLCI) extracted from globe artichoke (Cynara scolymus) vs maltodextrin for two 3-week periods (3-week washout). VLCI significantly increased fecal bifidobacteria, lactobacilli, and Atopobium and reduced Bacteroides-Prevotella without changing fecal SCFAs. Well tolerated with no adverse GI symptoms except significantly higher mild-to-moderate bloating. Limits: isolated inulin extract in healthy volunteers without constipation severity or PD endpoint.
