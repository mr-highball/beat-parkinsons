Registered anise extract trial measured menopausal hot flashes
Source: https://irct.ir/trial/4583
Registration: IRCT201103284317N4
Coverage: selected original registry fields

IRCT201103284317N4, official page 4583. Registration date shown May 15, 2011. Expected recruitment dates December 21, 2009–September 21, 2010; actual recruitment and completion dates blank in the retrieved public record. The English translation calls the product anise abstract. It planned 72 participants and 330-mg capsules three times daily for four weeks compared with matched starch placebo. Primary outcomes were hot flash frequency and severity. This record does not list a prespecified insomnia, somnolence, dream enactment or Parkinson outcome. The publication citing this registration in 2022 describes a May 2018–February 2019 enrollment window and an eight-week intervention. Linkage requires clarification.
