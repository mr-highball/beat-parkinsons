Title: Curcumin and a turmeric formulation in brain tumor cell-line comparisons
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22944718/

DOI: 10.1515/1553-3840.1614
Publication date: 2012-08-10
Date basis: NLM electronic publication date.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original author abstract plus the study description in the 2016 review.

The original compares curcumin and the branded Turmeric Force formulation in glioblastoma and medulloblastoma cell lines, alone and in combinations with anticancer drugs. The endpoints are cytotoxicity and cell death. The formulation is different from household ground turmeric. This original links the review's botanical and laboratory context without supplying a pain or Parkinson outcome.
