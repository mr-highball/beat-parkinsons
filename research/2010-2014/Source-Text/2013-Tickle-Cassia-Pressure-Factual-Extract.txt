Tickle thesis, abstract and pressure results

Original source https://studylib.net/doc/10969735

The May 2013 thesis reports 26 adults aged 40–65 who completed beverage comparisons with and without ground cassia cinnamon. Blood pressure was compared at baseline and repeatedly over four hours. No time point showed a clear between-condition difference. The study did not report an active-standing challenge or a Parkinson lightheadedness outcome. A 2013 conference abstract has the same title and overlapping investigators.
