Title: Abnormal visual gain control in a Parkinson's disease model
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4119403/
PMCID: PMC4119403
PMID: 24718285
DOI: 10.1093/hmg/ddu159
Publication date: 2014-04-09
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete structured PMC article text
Evidence type: Primary model experiment
Format: Factual digest of the original material

Drosophila expressing an LRRK2 variant underwent retinal and brain visual-response recordings. The investigators measured contrast responses and visual gain, including comparisons using LRRK2 kinase inhibitors in fly food. A stimulus-evoked electrophysiological response is distinct from hallucinations in humans. No dried fig preparation was evaluated.
