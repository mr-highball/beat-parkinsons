Published 2011-07-19. Twelve-week randomized fish oil versus mixed-oil placebo comparison in 68 medical students. Modeled log Beck Anxiety Inventory comparison −0.23 (95% CI −0.44 to −0.011, p=.04). Species of fish in the preparation unverified, no whole fish preparation or Parkinson disease.
