Title: REM sleep behavior disorder and REM sleep without atonia as an early manifestation of degenerative neurological disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3656587/
PMCID: PMC3656587
PMID: 22328094
DOI: 10.1007/s11910-012-0253-z
Publication date: 2012-04
Date basis: NLM journal issue year, month or date as available
Source depth: Complete structured PMC main body
Evidence type: Review with an original illustrative clinical course
Format: Factual digest of the original material

An aging-study participant developed formed visual hallucinations before observed parkinsonism. Dream enactment and fluctuating cognition were recorded separately, and visual experiences recurred later in the Lewy body dementia course. Medication changes and disease progression occurred during follow-up. The illustrative history gives no dietary comparison or count of episodes over a common window.
