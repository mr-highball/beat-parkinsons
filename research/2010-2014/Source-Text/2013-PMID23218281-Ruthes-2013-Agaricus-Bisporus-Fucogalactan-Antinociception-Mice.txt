Title: Agaricus bisporus fucogalactan: structural characterization and pharmacological approaches.
PMID: 23218281
DOI: 10.1016/j.carbpol.2012.08.071
First published: 2013
Authors: Ruthes AC, Rattmann YD, Malquevicz-Paiva SM, Carbonero ER, Córdova MM, Baggio CH
Journal: Carbohydrate polymers
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23218281/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse pharmacology study of purified Agaricus bisporus fucogalactan (EFP-Ab). Significantly inhibited both neurogenic and inflammatory phases of formalin-induced pain licking (inflammatory phase ID50 36.0 mg/kg, 95% CI 25.8-50.3) and reduced sepsis lethality and iNOS/COX-2 expression. Limits: purified injected/extracted polysaccharide in acute mouse formalin pain; human whole-mushroom RCTs showed no SF-36 pain benefit.
