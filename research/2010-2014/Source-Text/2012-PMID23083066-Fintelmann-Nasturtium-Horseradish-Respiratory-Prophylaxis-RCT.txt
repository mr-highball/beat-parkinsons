Title: Efficacy and safety of a combination herbal medicinal product containing Tropaeoli majoris herba and Armoraciae rusticanae radix for the prophylactic treatment of patients with respiratory tract diseases: a randomised, prospective, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase III trial
PMID: 23083066
DOI: 10.1185/03007995.2012.742048
First published: 2012
Authors: Fintelmann V, Albrecht U, Schmitz G, Schnitker J
Journal: Current Medical Research and Opinion
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23083066/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Phase III multicenter double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (EudraCT 2010-023227-26; n=351 randomized, 344 ITT, 84 days) of fixed 2-herb Angocin (3x2 or 2x2 tabs/d vs placebo) for respiratory tract infection prophylaxis. Reduced post-day-7 RTI incidence (13.3% high-dose vs 25.6% placebo, trend p=0.0171) with good tolerability. Bundled 2-herb combo for viral/bacterial RTIs; no PD motor or non-motor endpoints.
