Title: Daikenchuto colonic transit and bowel diaries in healthy adults
PMID: 20378829
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20378829/
Published: 2010-04-08
Archive scope: Investigators' indexed abstract, separately published W1413 and official trial registration. The journal's complete article remains inaccessible through the attempted publisher route.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Sixty healthy adults were randomized to placebo or one of two Daikenchuto granule doses for five days. The investigators report a lower ascending colon emptying time in one active arm versus placebo. The omnibus first filling measure at six hours had no individually clear dose-versus-placebo difference, and 24-hour colonic transit, gastric emptying and stool frequency or consistency were inconclusive. The paper reports one participant with elevated creatine phosphokinase after treatment. The intervention was the whole Daikenchuto formula. The design, dose groups, five-day course, enrollment and registered outcome set link the author reports to NCT00871325. The distinct NCT01139216 studied women with functional constipation.
