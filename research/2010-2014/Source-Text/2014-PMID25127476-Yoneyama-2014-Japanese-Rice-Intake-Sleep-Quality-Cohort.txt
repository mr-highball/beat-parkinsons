Title: Associations between rice, noodle, and bread intake and sleep quality in Japanese men and women.
PMID: 25127476
PMCID: PMC4134283
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0105198
First published: 2014
Authors: Yoneyama S, Sakurai M, Nakamura K, Morikawa Y, Miura K, Nakashima M
Journal: PloS one
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4134283/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional study in n=1,848 Japanese adults (20-60 y) using diet history questionnaire and PSQI. Higher total rice consumption was associated with lower odds of poor sleep (PSQI >5.5; Q5 vs Q1 adjusted OR 0.54, 95% CI 0.37-0.81, p-trend=0.015), whereas noodles were associated with worse sleep. Observational and did not separate brown rice from polished white rice.
