The isolated oral constituent reduced the later inflammatory response in mice at the higher tested exposure, with no statistically clear effect in the early phase or at the lower tested exposure. The separate neuropathic model reported less thermal hypersensitivity and mechanical allodynia. The controls, timing and model matter for interpreting the contrast.
Beta-caryophyllene occurs in black pepper and other plants. The study does not test cracked pepper or clinical Parkinson pain.
