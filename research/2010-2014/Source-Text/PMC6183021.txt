Title: Sleep Attacks in Patients With Parkinson's Disease on Dopaminergic Medications: A Systematic Review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6183021/
PMCID: PMC6183021
PMID: 30363881
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.12063
Publication date: 2014-09-01
Date basis: NLM electronic publication
Source depth: Complete official PMC HTML article body
Evidence type: Systematic review
Format: Factual digest of the original material

The review synthesizes sleep attacks and sudden sleep onset reported during dopaminergic therapy. Its cases include visual and auditory hallucinations coded separately among co-occurring symptoms. The primary outcome is sleep attacks. Co-occurring case reports, with concurrent medication and variable ascertainment, do not establish a food-associated visual episode rate.
