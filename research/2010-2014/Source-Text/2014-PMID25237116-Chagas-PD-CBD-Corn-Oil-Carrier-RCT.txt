Title: Effects of cannabidiol in the treatment of patients with Parkinson's disease: an exploratory double-blind trial.
PMID: 25237116
DOI: 10.1177/0269881114550355
First published: 2014
Authors: Chagas MH, Zuardi AW, Tumas V, Pena-Pereira MA, Sobreira ET, Bergamaschi MM et al.
Journal: J Psychopharmacol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25237116/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled exploratory trial (Chagas et al., 2014, J Psychopharmacol, n=21 patients with Parkinson disease randomized to placebo, CBD 75 mg/day, or CBD 300 mg/day for 6 weeks) in which gelatin capsules of pure corn oil served as the placebo and shared CBD dissolution carrier across all three arms. Aggregate UPDRS motor/total scores and PDQ-39 communication subscores (0.00 placebo vs 9.52 CBD 300 mg, p=0.531) showed no significant differences, while PDQ-39 total wellbeing improved with CBD 300 mg vs corn-oil placebo. Because every arm received corn oil with no oil-free comparator, the trial cannot isolate any effect of refined corn oil on PD OFF time, dyskinesia, dystonia, voice, or facial expression.
