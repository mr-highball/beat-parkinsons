Title: Brazilian geriatric neuropsychiatry congress, Caprara coffee and Parkinson case-control presentation
PMCID: PMC5619206
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5619206/
Publication: Dementia and Neuropsychologia 7(3):316–360, issue labeled July–September 2013. Exact first online day unverified; later archive deposit is distinct from issue date.
Coverage: Factual extract of original member abstract in the complete 45-page proceedings.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

The presentation reports retrospective coffee use in 113 Parkinson patients and 139 controls, with genotyping in 107 patients and all controls. Never drinking coffee was reported in 26.7% versus 4.3%, p=.00004. This concerns disease association and APOE context, with recall and selection limits. Coffee preparation is unspecified. Fatigue was not measured; disease association does not establish a symptom treatment effect.
