The European Medicines Agency Committee on Herbal Medicinal Products (HMPC) Final Assessment Report on Cucurbita pepo L., semen (EMA/HMPC/136022/2010, adopted 20 November 2012) documents more than three decades of continuous European traditional herbal use of whole pumpkin seeds, comminuted seeds, soft extracts, and fatty seed oil for lower urinary tract symptoms associated with benign prostatic hyperplasia or an overactive bladder. Traditional Central European dosing commonly uses 10 to 20 g/day of whole or chewed seeds or equivalent seed preparations. HMPC classified Cucurbitae semen under Traditional Use rather than Well-Established Use because placebo-controlled clinical evidence remained inconclusive at the time of assessment, and required medical exclusion of serious urological pathology. Safety review noted mild gastrointestinal discomfort, occasional allergic reactions, and theoretical attention to hemodynamic/hypotension mentions in older literature without establishing clinically relevant orthostatic hypotension in PD.
