Abu-Rabia's account draws on published sources and interviews with healers and patients. It lists Foeniculum vulgare under the Arabic name Shawmar. Its preparation entry describes a drink made by boiling leaves. The fruit is separately described as calming and antispasmodic in cited sources. The fennel section gives no identifiable firsthand response or diagnosed Parkinson patient. A neighboring entry about convulsions and facial paralysis belongs to a different plant. The collection's wide falij and nervous-disorder scope cannot supply a separate resting-tremor result for fennel or a match to toasted seed.
