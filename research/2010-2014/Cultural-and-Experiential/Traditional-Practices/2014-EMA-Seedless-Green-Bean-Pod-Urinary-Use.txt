Title: Seedless green bean pods in the European traditional-use assessment
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/herbal-report/final-assessment-report-phaseolus-vulgaris-l-fructus-sine-semine_en.pdf
Source kind: Traditional-practice documentation in an herbal regulatory assessment
Publication date: 2014-01-17
Date basis: EMA lists 17 January 2014 as first publication of the final report EMA/HMPC/317317/2012. The document was adopted on 12 November 2013, following a separate consultation draft.
Source locator: EMA/HMPC/317317/2012, pp. 7–10, 14 and 16. Publication record at https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/herbal/phaseoli-fructus-sine-semine.
Community/context: The EMA committee compiled European medicinal market history and prior published accounts. Its historical summary attributes a diuresis description to a 1608 work by Dodoens. That attribution is recorded through the assessment and the historical edition remains unverified here.
Exposure and reported symptom: An oral herbal infusion of comminuted seedless pods of Phaseolus vulgaris L. The listed use concerns traditional urinary tract flushing in minor urinary complaints. The plant part and infusion differ from whole cooked snap beans.
Reported direction: The historical use was intended to increase urine volume. Other possible uses and preparations appear in the report and belong to separate evidence questions.
Authenticity: The agency report and its publication date are documented. The assessment reports older sources indirectly. It is an institutional account of medicinal practice.
Efficacy evidence: The indication rests on longstanding use. The report establishes neither clinical efficacy for the urinary use nor measured effects on Parkinson fatigue, urinary urgency or urinary frequency. Urine volume is a distinct endpoint from urgency and void count.
Related research: research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID33022693-Green-Bean-Questionnaire-and-Aggregated-Frailty-Fatigue.txt and research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md. The cohort and Parkinson pilot studied different exposures and outcomes.
Access and follow-up: Confirm the older Dodoens reference in its original edition. The EMA page lists a later periodic review of the monograph. Record any new clinical observations under their own date and study family.
