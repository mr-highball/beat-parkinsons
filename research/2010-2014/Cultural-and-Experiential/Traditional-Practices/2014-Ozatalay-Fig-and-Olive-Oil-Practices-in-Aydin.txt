The Aydin fieldwork records a local account that figs help bowel movements, and another describes eating fig after soaking it in olive oil for stomach pain. A cited regional folk medicine account on the preceding page also describes fig soaked in olive oil for constipation. These reports refer to the combined preparation or to unspecified fig forms. Olive oil grade is unreported. The article also discusses fig's ceremonial role in asure and distinguishes fruit from leaf and latex uses. The fieldwork and earlier cited literature describe different traditions, with no symptom change measures, Parkinson diagnosis or rest tremor observation.
