Community/context: Interviews with 136 informants in Elazığ, Turkey. The field survey records preparation, administration and locally identified plants.

Exposure: Table 2 distinguishes sweet basil leaf decoction for stomach ache, lemon balm aerial-part decoction as a sedative, and Mentha spicata leaf decoction for cold or vomiting. The Rhus coriaria row and discussion distinguish culinary sour seasoning from reported customary medicinal uses. The separately cited use of Mentha longifolia for constipation is neither the same species as spearmint nor a finding of this survey.

Reported direction: Intended relief within each plant-specific tradition. Sumac is present as a spice, with no reported change in the frozen constipation, dystonia or depression outcomes.
Authenticity: Original author copy and NLM metadata matched by DOI and article identity. Local reports and the article's prior-literature column were read separately.
Efficacy evidence: Preparations and intended uses are documented without controlled or patient-level symptom measurements for Parkinson disease.
Open trail: Retain region, specimen and actual exposure when comparing with other records; do not pool the sites or species.
