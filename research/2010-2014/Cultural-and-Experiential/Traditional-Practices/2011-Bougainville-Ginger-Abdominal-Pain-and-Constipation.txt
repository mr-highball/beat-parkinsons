Title: Siwai and Buin medicinal-plant survey — reported ginger indications
Source kind: Traditional practices
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3245962/
PMID: 22004894
PMCID: PMC3245962
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2011.09.052
Date basis: 2011-10-06 online publication preceding the 2012 journal issue.
Coverage: Factual summary of the survey design and Zingiber officinale entry in Table 1.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The original ethnobotanical field survey in the Siwai and Buin districts reports Zingiber officinale rhizome in a row that includes crushed and concoction preparations and topical, oral and massage routes. The indications group centipede bites with abdominal pain, constipation and loss of appetite in a typhoid context, and the row identifies several villages.

The shared row does not assign one preparation or route to each indication. It records local use, without a comparison group, individual response measurement or confirmation of a Parkinson diagnosis. It preserves the named symptoms as cultural evidence of use and a lead for comparison.
