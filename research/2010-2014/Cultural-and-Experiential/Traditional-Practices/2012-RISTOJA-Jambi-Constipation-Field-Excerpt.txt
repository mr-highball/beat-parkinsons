Title: RISTOJA Jambi field-survey excerpt records a constipation preparation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Traditional practice, primary field-survey excerpt
Source: https://www.badankebijakan.kemkes.go.id/repositori/id/eprint/5470/
Original scan: https://www.badankebijakan.kemkes.go.id/repositori/id/eprint/5470/1/Arsip%20Tekstual_011%20RISTOJA%202012%20JAMBI.pdf
Publication date: 2012
Date basis: Year on the official project-report record. The executive summary dates fieldwork to November 2012.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Location inspected: Four-page public scan, executive summary printed pages vii–viii and printed page 25, row 31.

Exposure and reported symptom: Printed page 25 records a preparation for difficulty defecating, labeled susah berak. The two local plant names are batang kendung and katumeh. The entry describes the leaves taken as a vegetable preparation. The excerpt gives no scientific species names for these two plants or the community attribution for that specific entry.
Reported direction: The survey records the intended traditional use. It supplies no controlled outcome, reported change in constipation severity or Parkinson subgroup.
Evidence and limits: The provincial summary describes questionnaires with five traditional practitioners selected by chain referral in each of five communities, including Anak Dalam. The figures in the summary describe the provincial survey. They do not attribute the constipation row to a specific community or establish efficacy.
Summary: An official Jambi field report records constipation use for two locally named leaves. Botanical identity is unresolved, and the four-page public excerpt gives no measured treatment effect. The cultural entry is searchable by constipation and is not attributed to a cataloged ingredient.
