Title: Traditional suggestion of ginger for stomach irritation after tea
Source: https://doi.org/10.4103/2225-4110.124326
Original article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4032838/
Publication date: 2014
Date basis: Publication of the review reporting the suggestion. The historical text that it cites is a separate source.
Source type: Traditional practice described in a narrative review
Exposure: Ginger mentioned alongside tea ingestion
Symptom topic: Stomach irritation after tea
Reviewed: 2026-11-27

The author discusses gastrointestinal irritation after tea, especially on an empty stomach, and attributes a possible use of ginger to unnamed traditional Chinese medicine experts. The author separately cites the historical Bencao Gangmu for irritation attributed to tea. The article does not identify a particular historical ginger recipe, grade, dose or recorded participant response.

This documents the recommendation as attributed practice. It supplies no prospective comparison or evidence of an effect on Parkinson symptoms.
