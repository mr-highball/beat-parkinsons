Reported direction: The caregiver perceived less fatigue and more participation in ordinary tasks after a week of combined changes.

The caregiver judged fatigue from household activity and gives no fatigue scale, tea-only period or otherwise comparable observations. The presence and stage of Parkinson disease are reported in a forum context and were not independently verified. The inseparable changes leave the direction of brewed green tea alone open.
