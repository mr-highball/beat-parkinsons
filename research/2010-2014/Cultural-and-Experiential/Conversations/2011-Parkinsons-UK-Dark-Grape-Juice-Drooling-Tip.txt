Title: Public Parkinson forum reports of nurse-recommended dark grape juice for drooling and thick saliva
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/where-to-go-for-advice/883
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2011-09-05
Date basis: Post date on Parkinson UK public forum thread 883 (2011-09-05), corroborated by throat-clearing thread 3212 (2012-08-24).

In a September 2011 Parkinson UK discussion on early symptom management (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/where-to-go-for-advice/883), a participant experiencing nighttime drooling recounted that a Parkinson nurse recommended sipping dark grape juice or pineapple juice and noted that it seemed subjectively helpful while acknowledging uncertainty about any underlying chemistry. A separate August 2012 thread on throat clearing and thick secretions (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/throat-clearing/3212) similarly shared diluted black grape juice and diluted pineapple juice as low-risk practical tips passed along by peers or clinicians. These reports reflect informal patient/nurse coping strategies for liquid grape juice rather than clinical trial evidence for raw grapes.
