Title: Public Parkinson's UK forum discussions mentioning fresh melon among hydrating soluble-fiber fruits for constipation and bloating
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-and-parkinson/3694
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2013
Date basis: Opening post date (2013-06-07) on Parkinson's UK public forum thread t/constipation-and-parkinson/3694, cross-referencing companion thread t/stomach-bloatimg/1164 (opened 2013-03-20).

In a June 2013 Parkinson's UK forum thread opened by a newly diagnosed person with Parkinson's experiencing constipation while starting ropinirole (Requip), a community member suggested eating hydrating fresh fruit—specifically naming melon alongside peaches and apricots—plus drinking warm water for constipation prevention, while other replies discussed prunes, dietary fiber, exercise, magnesium citrate, and macrogol (Movicol). In a companion March 2013 Parkinson's UK thread on stomach bloating (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/stomach-bloatimg/1164), a community participant similarly suggested fresh or tinned melon alongside peaches, apricots, dates, and mangoes as soluble-fiber fruits perceived not to interfere with levodopa/benserazide (Madopar) absorption. Neither thread specified honeydew versus cantaloupe or watermelon, and neither reported an isolated honeydew before-and-after bowel-symptom comparison or medication-timing measurement.
