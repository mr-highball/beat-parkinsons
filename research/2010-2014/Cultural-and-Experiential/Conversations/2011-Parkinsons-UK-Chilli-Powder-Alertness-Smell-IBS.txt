Date basis: Original public thread opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK Community Forum (2011-08-30; 12 posts through 2011-09-02).

Public Parkinson's UK community discussion (12 posts, August-September 2011; all usernames and identifying details removed) initiated by a person with Parkinson's disease asking whether others experienced unexpected reactions to chili powder.

Key privacy-safe observations:
- Transient arousal/medication-timing impression: The opening contributor reported repeatedly feeling markedly 'hyper' or energized after adding generous chili powder to meals and subjectively delaying their next Parkinson's medication dose, wondering whether chili acted as a stimulant or affected medication response.
- Compensatory seasoning for hyposmia: Another contributor noted preferring strongly seasoned foods with chili and garlic because Parkinson's-related loss of smell made blander meals taste flat.
- Gastrointestinal intolerance: Another contributor cautioned that irritable bowel symptoms made chili powder intolerable, illustrating divergent individual GI tolerability alongside unverified alertness anecdotes.
