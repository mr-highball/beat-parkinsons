Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Discussion on Red Meat, Other Protein Sources, and Levodopa Effectiveness
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/protein-and-levadopa/3778
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2011-08-20
Date basis: First post timestamp on public Discourse thread (2011-08-20 to 2011-08-21).

Public Parkinson's UK forum thread (32 posts, initiated August 20, 2011) asking whether different protein sources including red meat, white meat, dairy, eggs, nuts, and beans impair levodopa efficacy differently. Contributors described markedly heterogeneous personal experiences: one person reported feeling Madopar worked better without protein and suspected meat was the worst offender while nuts seemed more tolerable; another reported no noticeable Madopar effect when taken close to meals and ongoing variability even with 1-hour spacing; a third reported eating large amounts of tuna without medication loss whereas milk porridge blocked medication completely; another expressed skepticism that dietary protein meaningfully reduced dopamine transport. Care partners also described spacing Sinemet between meals per clinical advice without being certain of individual impact.
