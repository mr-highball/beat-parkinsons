Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Discussion: Selective Preservation of Vanilla Scent on Diagnostic Parkinson's Sniff Testing
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/sense-of-smell-taste/3759
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2010-07-21
Date basis: Original thread creation date on Parkinson's UK public community forum (relevant post dated 2010-07-22).

Public Parkinson's UK community thread on smell and taste loss (topic 3759, initiated 2010-07-21; contributor identities removed). Contributors exchanged varied experiences with Parkinson's hyposmia and selective odor loss; in a reply dated 2010-07-22, one member described undergoing a 10-odor diagnostic sniff test and identifying only two scents—eucalyptus and vanilla—while failing eight others. Other contributors noted that smell loss appears heterogeneous across individuals and typically persists over time. Context only: balanced experiential counterpoint to topic 2579, showing vanilla is used diagnostically in olfactory testing rather than as an oral symptom remedy.
