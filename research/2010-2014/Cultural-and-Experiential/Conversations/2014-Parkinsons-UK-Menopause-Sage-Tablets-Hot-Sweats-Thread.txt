Title: Parkinson's UK forum firsthand account of sage tablets easing menopausal hot sweats in a person with PD
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/parkinsons-and-menopause/7431
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2014-05-26
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2014-05-26; firsthand sage tablet reply posted 2014-08-20).

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread discussing overlapping Parkinson's and menopausal symptoms (opened May 2014; reply August 2014), a participant who could not use hormone replacement therapy after a prior hysterectomy reported trying over-the-counter sage tablets for menopausal hot sweats, noting that while the sweats did not disappear completely, they became far less severe. Other contributors in the thread discussed anxiety, depression, sweating, and tremor exacerbation during menopause without evaluating sage for those symptoms. All personal identifiers are omitted. This account reflects real-world patient use of commercial sage supplements for vasomotor sweating rather than controlled PD symptom evidence.
