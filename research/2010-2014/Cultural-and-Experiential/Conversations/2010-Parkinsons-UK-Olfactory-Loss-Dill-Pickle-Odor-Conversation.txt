Title: Public Parkinson's forum discussion noting selective loss of dill-pickle odor recognition in PD
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/sense-of-smell-taste/3759
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2010
Date basis: Opening post date (2010-07-21) on the public Parkinson's UK Discourse forum thread.

In a July 2010 public thread on the Parkinson's UK forum exploring hyposmia and ageusia in Parkinson's disease, participants compared personal experiences of losing smell and taste, including undergoing clinical sniff tests. One contributor summarized published olfactory-identification findings noting that people with Parkinson's disease were selectively less able than controls to identify specific odors—explicitly citing dill pickle alongside licorice, coconut, banana, cherry, and soap—while sometimes retaining lemon recognition. Another participant humorously remarked on hoping to still detect pickle aroma at lunch. The thread reflects lived experience of dill aroma loss in PD without reporting any symptomatic effect of consuming dill weed.
