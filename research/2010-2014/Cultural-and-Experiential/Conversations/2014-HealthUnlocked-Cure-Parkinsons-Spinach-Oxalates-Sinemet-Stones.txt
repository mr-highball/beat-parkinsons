Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2014-05-21
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on HealthUnlocked Cure Parkinson's public forum (2014-05-21; replies through 2015-03-24; cross-referencing companion oxalate discussion opened 2024-01-30 at https://healthunlocked.com/cure-parkinsons/posts/150483145/oxalates).

In a public HealthUnlocked Cure Parkinson's thread opened May 21, 2014, a person with Parkinson's asked whether high-oxalate foods worsened PD symptoms, reporting several days when Parkinson symptoms felt noticeably worse and carbidopa/levodopa (Sinemet) seemed less effective after eating large amounts of spinach or lentils the prior evening, and speculating about oxalate effects on mitochondria and kidney stones. A responding PD member disagreed regarding spinach, noting that protein-rich legumes/lentils interfered with their Sinemet (attributable to competitive neutral amino acid transport), whereas eating home-grown spinach in the evening did not produce a comparable noticeable drop in Sinemet response. A decade later, in a January 30, 2024 Cure Parkinson's thread on dietary oxalates (https://healthunlocked.com/cure-parkinsons/posts/150483145/oxalates; 18 replies), contributors shared analytical tables flagging both spinach (682-2,902 mg/100 g reported range) and Swiss chard among the highest-oxalate leafy greens; one member recounted experiencing painful kidney stones after eating a heavy summer harvest of spinach and emphasized generous hydration to flush urinary oxalate. All usernames and personal details are omitted. Together these threads preserve adverse, neutral, and kidney-stone cautionary perspectives within the PD community.
