Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Exchange on Eating Blueberries for Digestive Upset and Brain Stimulation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/lactose-intolerence-and-parky/7432
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2014-05-26
Date basis: Public Discourse thread posts spanning 2014-05-26 to 2014-06-02.

Public Parkinson's UK forum thread (12 posts, May 26 to June 2, 2014) started by a person with Parkinson disease experiencing prolonged diarrhea, bloating, gas, and abdominal discomfort. Another forum member recommended eating blueberries for digestive support and stated that blueberries "stimulate the brain." The original poster noted an elderly relative ate blueberries daily and later reported starting blueberries alongside broader dietary caution after colonoscopy preparation, noting bowel urgency had settled somewhat while awaiting biopsy results.
