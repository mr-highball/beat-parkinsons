Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Conversation: Melon as Soluble-Fiber Fruit for Stomach Bloating and Madopar Timing
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/stomach-bloatimg/1164
Source kind: Public forum conversation
Publication date: 2013-03-20
Date basis: Published forum reply date (thread started 2013-03-19; melon reply posted 2013-03-20).

Public Parkinson's UK forum discussion (12 posts, 2013-03-19 to 2013-03-20) on Parkinson's-associated stomach bloating, slowed gut motility, and constipation. After several participants linked bloating to slowed GI transit or dopaminergic drugs and noted reliance on soft foods or macrogol/domperidone, one participant reported reducing severe bloating by avoiding gluten and choosing soluble-fiber fruits such as melon, peaches, apricots, dates, and mangoes over insoluble fiber, adding that these fruits did not appear to disturb morning Madopar absorption while explicitly cautioning that this approach may not work for everyone.
