Privacy-safe summary: Across a long-running Parkinson's UK constipation thread discussing psyllium, dietary fiber, linseed, polyethylene glycol (macrogol), and enemas, perspectives on bulk fiber were sharply divided:
- Favorable chia seed anecdote (posted 2016-01-21): One contributor reported taking a teaspoon of chia seeds after trying other laxatives and attributed strong relief of both constipation and cramps/spasms to chia seeds. (Note: editorial cross-check suggests this contributor may overlap with the later 2017 turmeric/black-pepper/chia forum thread archived locally, so independence across Parkinson's UK chia anecdotes is uncertain.)
- Safety warnings on slow-transit PD bowel dysmotility: Multiple other contributors and a caregiver explicitly cautioned that PD slows colonic peristalsis so adding bulk fiber without adequate osmotic therapy can compact stool and precipitate bowel obstruction; one caregiver described a family member requiring emergency sigmoidectomy for complete bowel blockage/volvulus before stabilizing on macrogol (polyethylene glycol).

Evidentiary context: Highlights why non-PD chronic constipation trial signals for chia seeds cannot be assumed universally safe or effective across severe slow-transit PD constipation without clinical PD testing.
