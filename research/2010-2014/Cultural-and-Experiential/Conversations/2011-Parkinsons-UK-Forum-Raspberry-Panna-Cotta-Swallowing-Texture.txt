Title: Parkinson's UK Forum: Caregiver notes raspberry panna cotta eases swallowing via texture rather than whole fruit
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/trouble-swallowing-try-raspberry-panna-cotta/6190
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2011-09-22
Date basis: Opening post timestamp (2011-09-22) inspected directly via Parkinson's UK Forum public thread record.

In a September-October 2011 Parkinson's UK Forum thread (5 posts), a caregiver reported that their mother with Parkinson's disease had marked difficulty swallowing liquids and foods except raspberry panna cotta, describing it as "just the right combo of liquid and substance" and calorie-dense against weight loss. Subsequent replies (2011-09-25 and 2011-09-28) clarified that the benefit derived from smooth semisolid food texture and consistency (noting mousse or similar puddings worked similarly, whereas jelly/mixed textures were trickier), rather than any pharmacological effect of raspberries themselves. Indeed, intact raw or frozen whole raspberries contain small fibrous seeds that require chewing and are distinct from smooth strained dairy desserts; the thread provides useful practical PD dysphagia context without evidence that frozen whole raspberries ease or worsen swallowing difficulty.
