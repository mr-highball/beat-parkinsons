Title: Parkinson's UK forum recipes using cooked tomatoes for fatigue days, taste loss, and low-protein levodopa meals
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/pd-friendly-recipes/3088
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-05-05
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2012-05-05; chopped-tomato recipe posts 2012-05-07 and 2012-05-08; cross-referencing low-protein meal thread https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/non-low-protein-diet/49795 reply dated 2026-08-06).

In a May 2012 Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread exchanging PD-friendly recipes, participants shared practical cooked dishes built around tinned chopped tomatoes — including a soft one-pot chicken, onion, carrot, optional courgette (zucchini), and chopped-tomato stew cooked early in the day before fatigue sets in and frozen in portions, as well as a bacon-mushroom-tomato rice dish. When another member questioned whether everyday recipes could help PD, the recipe contributor explicitly clarified that the dishes were meant to remain appetizing and textured for people experiencing reduced taste rather than to improve Parkinson's symptoms. In an August 2026 thread on low-protein daytime eating around levodopa (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/non-low-protein-diet/49795, post #3), another participant remarked that they wanted more PD-friendly low-protein meal ideas because they were getting tired of relying on avocado and tomatoes. All personal identifiers are omitted. These discussions show how cooked and raw tomatoes fit ordinary PD meal routines without claiming symptom modification.
