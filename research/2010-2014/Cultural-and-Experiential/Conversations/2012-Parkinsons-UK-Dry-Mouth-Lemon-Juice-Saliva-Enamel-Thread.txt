Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion of lemon juice for PD dry mouth, enamel erosion risk, and transition to drooling
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/dry-mouth/4171
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-07-10
Date basis: Opening post and replies timestamped 2012-07-10 on Parkinson's UK Community Forum topic 4171.

In a July 2012 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened by a person with Parkinson's disease taking pramipexole and amantadine who experienced severe dry mouth when speaking, a responder cautioned from personal experience that nocturnal dry mouth leaves teeth vulnerable to rapid acid enamel erosion and noted that a drop of lemon juice (alongside sugar-free gum, which can loosen bowels) is believed to stimulate saliva. The opening poster replied that they planned to try the lemon strategy without posting follow-up results. Another caregiver in the same thread noted that their spouse initially suffered severe morning dry mouth before later developing persistent drooling, illustrating why salivary stimulants must be weighed carefully in PD.
