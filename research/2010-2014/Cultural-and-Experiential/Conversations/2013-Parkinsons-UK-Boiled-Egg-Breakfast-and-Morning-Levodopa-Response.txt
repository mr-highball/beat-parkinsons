Title: Parkinson's UK caregiver report on boiled eggs at breakfast blunting morning co-careldopa relief
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/starting-sinemet/6743
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2013-11-30
Date basis: Post #42 timestamp (2013-11-30) on public Parkinson's UK Discourse thread.

In a November 30, 2013 post (#42) within a public Parkinson's UK discussion on starting co-careldopa/Sinemet, a family caregiver asked whether dietary protein interferes with levodopa absorption, reporting that their mother experienced no symptom relief from her 8:00 am co-careldopa dose taken simultaneously with a breakfast of boiled eggs. The caregiver noted advising home carers to stop serving boiled eggs with the morning tablet to test whether separating egg protein improved morning medication effectiveness. All contributor handles and personal identifiers have been omitted.
