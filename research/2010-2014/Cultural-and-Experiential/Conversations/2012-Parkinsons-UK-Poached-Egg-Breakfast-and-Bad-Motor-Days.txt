Title: Parkinson's UK firsthand reports linking poached egg breakfasts and egg salads to worse motor days
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/hi-everyone/1773
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-03-24
Date basis: Posts #24-#25 timestamps (2012-03-24) on public Parkinson's UK Discourse thread.

In March 24, 2012 posts (#24 and #25) on a public Parkinson's UK forum thread discussing protein redistribution guidance, one person with Parkinson's described eating a light non-protein breakfast on weekdays with stable control, while repeatedly having a 'bad day' on Saturdays after eating bacon and eggs or poached eggs for breakfast—prompting them to shift eggs to Friday evening instead. A second contributor immediately replied that they felt markedly unwell about an hour after eating a chicken-and-egg salad. Both accounts reflect real-world experiences of daytime animal-protein meals coinciding with reduced levodopa responsiveness. All personal names, handles, and email addresses have been omitted.
