Public Parkinson's UK community discussion (11 posts, April-May 2012; all usernames and identifying details removed) examining whether cod liver oil helps joint aches and stiffness in people with Parkinson's disease.

Key privacy-safe observations:
- Mixed pain/stiffness impressions: One contributor asked whether cod liver oil helped arthritic joint pain alongside Parkinson's; several members replied that they took cod liver oil or omega-3 fish oil capsules for general joint/cardiovascular health with either modest subjective benefit or no noticeable change in Parkinson's symptoms, while noting medical advice to verify vitamin A/D and medication interactions.
- Exposure distinction for Round 3 review: Contributors also exchanged lighthearted regional culinary remarks contrasting eating cod versus haddock fillets, underscoring a recurring real-world gap: patient discussions of 'cod' for pain almost always refer to high-fat, vitamin-A/D-rich cod liver oil capsules rather than lean baked, poached, or grilled Atlantic cod muscle fillet.
