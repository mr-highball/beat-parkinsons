Title: Parkinson's UK forum thread discussing fresh or tinned peaches and mangoes as soluble-fiber breakfast fruits alongside Madopar
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/stomach-bloatimg/1164
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2013-03-19
Date basis: Opening post and reply dates on public Parkinson's UK Discourse thread (2013-03-19 to 2013-03-20).

In a March 2013 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussion opened by a caregiver asking about persistent abdominal bloating in a spouse taking Parkinson's medications, members shared contrasting digestive experiences. One participant described progressive digestive slowing requiring a soft-food diet plus daily macrogol (Movicol) sachets and senna for constipation, while another linked severe bloating to wheat/gluten and reported relief after avoiding gluten and increasing fruit intake. In a follow-up tip (2013-03-20), that participant recommended fresh or tinned peaches, mangoes, melon, apricots, and dates as soluble-fiber fruits that did not appear to interfere with morning Madopar absorption, contrasting them with insoluble fiber. All personal identifiers and handles are omitted. The exchange reflects practical community perceptions regarding peaches and mangoes for PD gastrointestinal comfort and levodopa meal timing, without isolated attribution or clinical measurement.
