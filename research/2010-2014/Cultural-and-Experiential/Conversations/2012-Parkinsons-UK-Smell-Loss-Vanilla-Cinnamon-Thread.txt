Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Discussion: Loss of Ability to Smell Vanilla and Cinnamon in Parkinson's Hyposmia
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/smell/2579
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-12-17
Date basis: Original thread creation date on Parkinson's UK public community forum (posts December 2012).

Public Parkinson's UK community thread on sense of smell (topic 2579, initiated 2012-12-17; contributor identities removed). In response to a member wondering whether probiotic yogurt improved their sense of smell, another contributor with Parkinson's noted that clinical testing had revealed severe hyposmia—specifically being unable to detect vanilla, cinnamon, or other familiar pleasant odors—while humorously noting one practical upside in unpleasant-odor environments. Context only: lived-experience account showing that vanilla aroma is frequently lost in PD hyposmia, highlighting a practical boundary when interpreting laboratory inhaled-vanilla aromatherapy trials.
