Title: Parkinson's UK forum thread on spacing Christmas roast turkey and daytime protein around levodopa doses
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/food/7979
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2014-12-23
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public community forum (2014-12-23; turkey-timing advice 2014-12-23; follow-up replies through 2015-01-09).

In a December 2014 Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on food experiments and symptom control, the opening poster reported that "bad days" coincided with eating protein early in the day while testing ways to balance meals and medications. A community responder explicitly cautioned that dietary protein in the stomach interferes with levodopa absorption and advised timing holiday turkey at least one hour away from levodopa doses (while also warning against combining St. John's Wort with rasagiline). In follow-up replies through January 2015, another participant using a transdermal patch reported that Christmas dinner had not noticeably upset their symptoms, while a third contributor taking Sinemet confirmed that high-protein or heavy morning foods blunted medication onset and emphasized trial-and-error individuality. All personal names and handles are omitted. Together with the archived 1998 PARKINSN listserv report on Thanksgiving turkey leftovers, the thread reflects heterogeneous real-world levodopa-protein sensitivity.
