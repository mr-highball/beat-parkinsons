Title: Public Parkinson UK forum thread sharing tremor-friendly mushy peas and pressure-cooked red kidney bean recipes
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/pd-friendly-recipes/3088
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-05-05
Date basis: Opening post date on public Parkinson UK Discourse thread (relevant replies posted May 6 and May 13, 2012).

In a May 2012 Parkinson UK community thread exchanging practical PD-friendly meal ideas, one participant humorously recommended microwaved mushy peas with seasoning specifically because mashed peas stay on the fork and are easy to eat on a shaky day with hand tremor or dexterity difficulty. Later in the same thread (May 13, 2012), another participant shared a home recipe for Jamaican rice-and-peas prepared with soaked, pressure-cooked dry red kidney beans cooked until soft with coconut cream and thyme. Limits: Everyday mealtime sharing without symptom-rating endpoints; reinforces real-world preference for soft or mashed textures over loose whole peas during tremor episodes. All participant handles were omitted.
