Title: Parkinson's UK forum mention of daily unroasted in-shell peanuts in a PD incontinence discussion
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/effects-of-pd/1181

Publication date: 2012-07-07
Date basis: Post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public Community Forum thread (2012-07-07).

In a July 7, 2012 Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussion initiated on the prevalence of urinary incontinence in Parkinson's disease, one contributor posted a brief personal recommendation suggesting that individuals without peanut allergy try eating peanuts daily, specifically preferring non-roasted in-shell ('monkey') peanuts over roasted peanuts and stating that this helped them. No dose, duration, specific urinary urgency frequency measure, concurrent bladder or dopaminergic medication details, or replication by other members was recorded. All handles and personal details are omitted.
