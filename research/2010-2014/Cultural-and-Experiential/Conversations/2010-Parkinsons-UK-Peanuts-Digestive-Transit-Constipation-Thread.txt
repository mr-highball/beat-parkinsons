Title: Parkinson's UK forum firsthand account of peanuts stimulating bowel transit for PD constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation/79
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2010-04-16
Date basis: Post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public Community Forum thread opened 2010-04-15 (relevant firsthand reply dated 2010-04-16).

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened on April 15, 2010 by a caregiver seeking advice for a spouse with four days of Parkinson's constipation despite macrogol and senna, a member living with Parkinson's disease replied on April 16, 2010 describing lower digestive transit as a persistent symptom. After trying conventional dietary measures, the participant reported that eating a generous handful of peanuts approximately every second day appeared to stimulate the digestive tract and helped them manage constipation, while adding an explicit caution regarding peanut allergies and individual dietary problems. Preparation (roasted vs boiled vs raw), exact gram dose, concurrent laxative regimen, and validated stool-frequency or Bristol Stool Scale measurements were not reported. All personal names and account handles are omitted. This single uncontrolled observation provides patient-experience context without establishing efficacy.
