Title: Parkinson's UK forum firsthand account of undiluted lemon juice for thick nighttime throat phlegm
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/phlegm-at-night-keeping-me-up/2733
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2013-05-20
Date basis: Opening post and reply timestamped 2013-05-20 (follow-up 2013-06-25) on Parkinson's UK Community Forum topic 2733.

In a May-June 2013 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on difficulty clearing thick, sticky throat phlegm at night that disrupted sleep, one participant shared a firsthand coping tip of taking a few squirts of undiluted bottled lemon juice, stating that it worked for them. Other participants discussed nasal breathing strips and suspected levodopa-related thick oral secretions. No standardized swallowing, drooling, or sleep scores were recorded, and all personal identifiers are omitted.
