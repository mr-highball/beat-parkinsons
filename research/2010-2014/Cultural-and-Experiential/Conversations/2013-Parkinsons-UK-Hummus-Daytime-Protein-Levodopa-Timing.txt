Title: Parkinson's UK Forum discussions on using chickpea hummus as a light daytime meal during levodopa protein redistribution

Thread locators: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/protein-and-carbohydrate-balance/4479 (opened 2013-04-10; 2 posts), cross-referenced with https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/dairy-products-parkinsons/16915 (opened 2018-11-30; 3 posts) and https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/wearing-off/45475 (opened 2025-06-17; 21 posts). All contributor usernames and personal names removed.

Privacy-safe summary: Across three public Parkinson's UK threads on protein-levodopa interactions and wearing-off, contributors repeatedly cited small servings of chickpea hummus as a manageable daytime lunch choice during protein redistribution—keeping breakfast very low in protein, eating a modest lunch such as vegetable soup or crackers with hummus and salad, and reserving meat, fish, or cheese for the evening meal. In the 2013 and 2018 threads, contributors reported improved daytime levodopa reliability after shifting heavy animal/dairy protein to evening while retaining light lunchtime hummus; in the 2025 thread, however, an early-onset contributor taking four-hourly carbidopa-levodopa still experienced shrinking ON windows (from 5+ hours down to ~3 hours) despite spacing medication by an hour around porridge and lunchtime hummus.

Evidence and limits: Uncontrolled self-reports involving commercial or homemade hummus (chickpeas plus tahini/oil) and concurrent medication adjustments; confirms neither OFF-time rescue nor predictable acute levodopa blockade from modest chickpea servings.
