Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion on PD acid reflux, transient Colpermin relief, and peppermint cordial for bloating
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/parkinsons-and-acid-reflux/81
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-05-15
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2012-05-15; Colpermin and peppermint cordial replies posted 2016-01-30 and 2018-09-02).

In a multi-year Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on Parkinson's disease and acid reflux (opened May 2012 with replies through September 2018), participants discussed slowed gastric emptying, lower esophageal sphincter weakness, and reflux choking episodes. In January 2016, a caregiver reported that their partner's acid reflux received only very temporary relief from lansoprazole, Gaviscon, and Colpermin (enteric-coated peppermint oil capsules), resolving instead after reducing dietary wheat, yogurt, and coffee. In September 2018, another member noted that diluted peppermint-flavored cordial seemed to help when feeling bloated. All account names are omitted. Together with a separate 2009 Parkinson's UK reflux thread listing peppermint among common dietary reflux triggers, these community accounts echo clinical cautions about LES relaxation and GERD worsening.
