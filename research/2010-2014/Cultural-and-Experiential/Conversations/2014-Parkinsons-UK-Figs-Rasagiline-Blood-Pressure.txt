Title: Parkinson's UK participant reports elevated blood pressure after figs while taking rasagiline
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/recently-diagnosed/7556/9
Publication date: 2014-07-08
Date basis: Opening post date in the public thread. The fig account is reply 9, posted July 11, 2014.
Related discussion: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/dat-scan-results/7273/45
Related discussion basis: The same public participant described a similar medication and blood-pressure episode in a May 9 reply. Count these mentions as one reported experience.

A participant attributed high blood pressure to taking rasagiline after eating figs. The fig preparation, quantity, tyramine content, medication dose, timing interval and measured blood-pressure values are unspecified. The account gives no independent verification or comparison, and it does not describe a change in rest tremor from eating figs.

Other participants discussed their own experiences with the medication and disagreed about how dietary advice applied. A U.S. rasagiline product label separately describes an interaction concern for foods with very high tyramine content. That labeling does not identify the exposure in this account or verify its cause. See sections 5.1 and 7.7 at https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=fdebcedc-1b5e-4803-9617-58d705cda726.
