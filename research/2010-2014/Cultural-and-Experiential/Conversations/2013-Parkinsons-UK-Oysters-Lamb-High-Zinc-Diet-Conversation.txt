Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion naming oysters and lamb as high-zinc foods (contrasted with a 2020 oyster-shell calcium tremor post)
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/cortisol/3372
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2013-06-02
Date basis: Direct post timestamp (2013-06-02, post 3) in public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread 3372 (with companion boundary observation from thread 24397 dated 2020-07-08).

In a June 2013 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread discussing hormones and diet (topic 3372), a participant listed high-zinc foods—explicitly naming red meats such as beef and lamb alongside oysters and lobster—while remarking lightheartedly on checking whether oysters carried any drawbacks before continuing to eat them. Public forum searches yielded very few direct discussions of eating cooked oysters for Parkinson symptoms; notably, a July 2020 arm-tremor thread (topic 24397) mentioned taking two tablets of *oyster shell calcium* alongside propranolol, amantadine, Avena sativa, full-spectrum CBD oil, and exercise. All usernames are omitted. Together, these threads confirm limited firsthand dietary oyster symptom reports in PD communities and underscore keeping oyster-shell calcium supplements separate from cooked oyster meat.
