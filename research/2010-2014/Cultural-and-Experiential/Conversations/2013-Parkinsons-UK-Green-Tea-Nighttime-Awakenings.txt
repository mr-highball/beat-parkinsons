Thread summary (2 posts, 2013-01-10 to 2013-01-15):
- Opening post (2013-01-10): A participant who normally slept well described waking 4-5 times per night and struggling to fall back asleep since recently starting to drink green tea, stating they planned to stop green tea and asking whether others experienced similar effects.
- Reply (2013-01-15): Another member noted that although green tea has less caffeine than black tea, it still contains enough caffeine to disrupt sleep, particularly when consumed close to bedtime.
