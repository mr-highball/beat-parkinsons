Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Survey of Habitual Tea and Coffee Consumption and Taste Changes
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/what-did-you-drink-tea-or-coffee-or-both/3365
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2011-10-27
Date basis: Public Discourse thread posts spanning 2011-10-27 to 2011-11-06.

Public Parkinson's UK forum thread (14 posts, October 27 to November 6, 2011) asking people with Parkinson disease about their pre-diagnosis and current tea versus coffee intake. Respondents reported diverse lifelong habits ranging from exclusively black tea with milk (up to 6 cups/day) or Earl Grey to exclusively coffee or water. Multiple contributors noted that neither tea nor coffee tasted the same after developing Parkinson disease and shifted toward water, while another noted caffeinated coffee helped "kickstart" alertness for work. Contributors did not isolate brewed black tea as a targeted treatment for a specific motor symptom.
