Title: Parkinson's UK forum mixed firsthand reports contrasting vegetable proteins vs lentils impairing Sinemet absorption
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/protein-and-levadopa/3778
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2011-12-28
Date basis: Post timestamp of the lentil-specific firsthand report on Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened 2011-08-20 (posts span 2011-08-20 to 2012-09-15).

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussion initiated on August 20, 2011 asking whether different dietary protein sources (meat, dairy, eggs, nuts, beans) affect levodopa differently, participants shared notably divergent firsthand experiences. On August 29, 2011, two contributors reported that soft cheese, milk, and eggs severely blunted their levodopa response whereas vegetable proteins and tuna seemed well tolerated. Conversely, on December 28, 2011, a contributor diagnosed with Parkinson's disease roughly 16 years earlier who experienced pronounced slow gastric emptying wrote that food in the stomach completely blocked their Sinemet response (though not pramipexole or tolcapone), adding that protein was the main trigger and that among protein-rich foods they personally found lentils the worst culprit alongside heavy homemade brown bread, leading them—with dietitian guidance—to avoid daytime protein and eat a double portion at night while dosing Sinemet 20 minutes before small meals. All names and handles are omitted. This balanced patient-experience record highlights individual variability in gastric emptying and protein sensitivity without establishing a population-level clinical effect of lentils on daily OFF time.
