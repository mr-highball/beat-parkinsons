Title: Parkinson's UK public forum caregiver account of improved arm swing during a 7-day cabbage soup diet, with peer attribution to meal-medication timing
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/carbohydrates-in-diet/7591
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2014-07-22
Date basis: Opening post timestamp (2014-07-22; replies dated 2014-07-22 to 2014-08-21) on public Parkinson's UK Discourse forum thread.

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened on July 22, 2014, a caregiver reported that after she and her husband followed a 7-day high-fiber, nil-carbohydrate cabbage soup diet for pre-holiday weight loss, her husband unexpectedly showed improved walking mobility, renewed arm swing not seen for years, and less late-night shuffling. Responding members on July 22-23, 2014 noted that food composition, protein, and meal timing strongly affect intestinal absorption of Parkinson medications, with another contributor observing better medication response when cutting wheat and potatoes for constipation; the charity's moderation team replied on August 21, 2014 that clinical research linking carbohydrate restriction directly to Parkinson drug efficacy was lacking. All personal identifiers and handles are omitted. Cabbage variety was unspecified, and total diet restriction severely confounds any attribution to cabbage.
