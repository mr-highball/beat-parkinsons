Date basis: Opening post date (2014-10-16) of public Parkinson's UK Discourse thread (with earlier incidental mention 2012-10-05 and live full-forum audit verified 2026-11-26).

A full-index search of the public Parkinson's UK Community Forum Discourse archive on 2026-11-26 returned only two threads ever mentioning 'parsnip' (memory-failure/7818, opened 2014-10-16, and ralnea-xl/1346, opened 2012-10-05), both strictly lighthearted wordplay or casual banter rather than dietary symptom accounts. Public web-index searches of Reddit r/Parkinsons and HealthUnlocked Cure Parkinson's similarly surfaced parsnip only inside broad whole-food, MIND-diet, or umbellifer root-vegetable grocery lists without any standalone trial or patient-reported symptom change. All personal identifiers and handles are omitted.
