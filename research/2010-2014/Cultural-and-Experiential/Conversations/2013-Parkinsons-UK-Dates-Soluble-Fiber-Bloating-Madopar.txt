Privacy-safe summary: In a Parkinson's UK thread opened by a care partner asking about persistent abdominal bloating in a spouse with Parkinson's disease, multiple contributors attributed bloating and constipation to Parkinson's-related gastrointestinal slowing, with one member noting reliance on soft foods, four sachets/day of Movicol (polyethylene glycol), and nighttime senna. Another contributor who reported resolving severe bloating by eliminating gluten and increasing fruit specifically highlighted fresh or tinned peaches, melon, apricots, dates, and mangoes as soluble-fiber fruits—contrasting them with insoluble fiber that could worsen bloating—and remarked that these fruits did not appear to interfere with Madopar (levodopa-benserazide) absorption at breakfast.

Evidence and limits: Unverified personal observation bundling dates with other low-protein soluble-fiber fruits and gluten avoidance; complements existing Parkinson's UK threads on dates for constipation (2011 and 2019) while preserving the counterbalance that many PD patients require pharmacologic osmotic laxatives.
