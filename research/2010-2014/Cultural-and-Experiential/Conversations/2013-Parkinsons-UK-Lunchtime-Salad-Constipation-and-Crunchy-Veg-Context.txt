Title: Parkinson's UK public forum discussion of lunchtime salads, olive oil, and diet adjustments for PD constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-and-parkinson/3694
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2013-06-07
Date basis: Opening post timestamp (2013-06-07; follow-up salad/olive-oil firsthand report dated 2014-08-13) on public Parkinson's UK Discourse forum thread.

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened on June 7, 2013 by a newly diagnosed patient struggling with constipation and fatigue on ropinirole, participants exchanged varied dietary strategies alongside frustration over persistent lack of bowel movements. In an August 13, 2014 reply (#20), one member reported becoming free of laxatives and constipation after eliminating wheat, adding generous olive oil to a daily lunchtime salad, and switching breakfast cereal to granola, nuts, and seeds—a multi-factor change that cannot isolate any single salad vegetable such as radish, romaine, or radicchio. Other threads on the same forum (e.g., thread 13941, 2018) report zero constipation relief from increased fibrous vegetables alone, while Swedish qualitative research in older adults with Parkinson-like motor eating difficulties (Forsberg et al. 2022) notes that hard, crunchy raw vegetables (including radish) become difficult to chew and handle as motor and swallowing deficits progress. All personal identifiers are omitted.
