Title: Public Parkinson UK forum discussion on peas, beans, lentils, high-fiber diets, and FODMAP tolerance for constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation/2386
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-10-03
Date basis: Opening post date on public Parkinson UK Discourse thread (replies span 2012-10-03 to 2020-04-30).

In a public Parkinson UK forum thread initiated on October 3, 2012 asking for help with refractory Parkinson constipation after prunes, psyllium, senna, and macrogol lost effectiveness, participants shared contrasting firsthand dietary experiences. On November 4, 2012, one participant with Parkinson disease reported becoming free of constipation by avoiding chocolate and coffee, drinking more water, and eating more peas, beans, lentils, and brown bread, noting that dietary adjustment took roughly two weeks to take effect. Conversely, on April 30, 2020, another participant reported that high-fiber dietary strategies eventually lost effectiveness and coincided with IBS bloating and trapped wind, leading them to adopt a low-FODMAP diet alongside psyllium husk and prescription bowel therapies. Limits: Unverified self-reported multi-food changes without dose, preparation specifics (canned vs steamed vs cooked), or controlled comparison; all participant handles were omitted.
