Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Report: Low Blood Pressure, Dizziness, and Nausea After Increasing Curcumin Complex Dose
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/has-anyone-tried-curcumin/7932
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2014-11-30
Date basis: Original thread creation date on Parkinson's UK public community forum (posts November-December 2014).

Public Parkinson's UK community thread (topic 7932, initiated 2014-11-30; contributor identities removed). A person with Parkinson's reported tolerating one capsule daily of an 875 mg Curcumin Complex supplement, but developing repeated bouts of low blood pressure, dizziness, and nausea after increasing to the bottle-recommended two capsules daily, asking whether curcumin caused the drop or whether it was coincidental. A Parkinson's UK staff reply explained that curcumin research in Parkinson's remained mainly at the laboratory stage without large clinical trials proving dietary or supplement efficacy, and noted that intrinsic Parkinson's autonomic blood-pressure fluctuations make single-person attribution uncertain. Context only: unfavorable/adverse personal report balancing favorable turmeric anecdotes; does not establish causality.
