Title: Parkinson's UK forum culinary discussion sharing sautéed leek and potato soup as an approachable meal
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/pd-friendly-recipes/3088/2
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-05-05
Date basis: Public post timestamp on Parkinson's UK Community Forum (2012-05-05).

In a May 2012 Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on Parkinson's-friendly home recipes, a contributor shared a simple Cullen skink soup made by frying leeks in butter with chopped potatoes as one of three manageable regular dishes, noting the physical effort of peeling vegetables. No therapeutic or symptom-modifying claim was made for leeks, and broader searches across Parkinson's UK Community Forum confirmed that leek rarely appears except in ordinary meal recipes (with most keyword hits being spelling variants of 'leak'). All personal identifiers and handles are omitted.
