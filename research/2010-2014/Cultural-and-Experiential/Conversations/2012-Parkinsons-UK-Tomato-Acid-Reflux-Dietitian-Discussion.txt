Title: Parkinson's UK forum thread on gastroparesis, acid reflux, and dietitian advice regarding tomatoes
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/parkinsons-and-acid-reflux/81
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-05-15
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2012-05-15; follow-up replies through 2016-01-30).

In a May 2012 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on Parkinson's disease and acid reflux, a participant taking daily omeprazole summarized an explanation from their registered dietitian: slowed gastric motility and weakened esophageal sphincter tone in PD cause food and acid to linger in the stomach, promoting bloating, fullness, and upward reflux into the throat. They relayed the dietitian's guidance that acidic or reflux-provoking foods — specifically naming tomatoes, citrus foods, chocolate, and alcohol — as well as large meals can worsen symptoms for some people, while emphasizing that individual triggers differ and patients benefit from personalized dietitian assessment. Subsequent replies (2012-2016) described silent reflux, choking/coughing sensations, variable response to PPIs, and individualized dietary adjustments. All personal identifiers are omitted. This provides practical PD tolerability context, especially when considering evening or bedtime tomato consumption.
