Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion reporting red-meat protein blunting Madopar/entacapone efficacy and wearing-off meal timing
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/entacapone-comtess/3178
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2011-11-02
Date basis: Direct firsthand post timestamp (2011-11-02; follow-up wearing-off & meal-timing discussion dated 2026-01-18) in public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread 3178.

In a publicly accessible Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on entacapone (Comtess) and Madopar absorption (topic 3178, opened October 2011 with active wearing-off replies continuing into January 2026), a participant reported in November 2011 that despite clean tolerance of entacapone, medication absorption rate remained inconsistent and singled out red-meat protein as their most troublesome trigger: repeatedly finding their levodopa ineffective with poor mobility after enjoying a heavy red-meat meal, inferring obstruction of gut absorption and blood-brain barrier transport. In January 2026 follow-up posts on the same thread, participants discussing hourly pre-dose wearing-off noted that shifting protein later in the day or spacing lighter daytime meals helped some people manage fluctuations depending on individual response. A separate February 2025 Parkinson's UK thread (topic 43658) also noted practical fine-motor difficulty cutting lamb chops at a restaurant. All personal identifiers are omitted.
