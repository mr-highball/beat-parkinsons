In an August 2011 public thread on the Parkinson's UK Community Forum, a participant with Parkinson's disease sought peer advice after being prescribed prochlorperazine for dizziness and vertigo and noticing a package warning against use in Parkinson's disease. A community responder strongly urged contacting a Parkinson's nurse or neurologist about medication safety and added a brief secondhand remark that an acquaintance with vertigo used lemongrass, noting uncertainty as to whether it was taken in capsule form or as a tea, and reiterating that a Parkinson's specialist should be consulted before trying anything. All personal identifiers and handles are omitted. This thread confirms minimal community discussion of lemongrass on Parkinson's UK Discourse and supplies no firsthand Parkinson's outcome, standardized preparation, or directional evidence.
