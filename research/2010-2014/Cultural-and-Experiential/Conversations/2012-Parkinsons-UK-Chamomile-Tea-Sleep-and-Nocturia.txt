Title: Parkinson's UK discussion noting evening chamomile tea trial and nocturia trade-off
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/hello-to-everyone/5366
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-11-04
Date basis: Opening post dated November 4, 2012 (bucket 2010-2014); relevant chamomile tea reply posted November 9, 2012.

In a November 2012 Parkinson's UK welcome thread, a newly diagnosed participant asked for suggestions to manage early-morning awakening after two to three hours of sleep. On November 9, 2012, another participant with Parkinson's disease described similar poor sleep exacerbated by Parkinson-related bladder problems (waking twice nightly to urinate), noting that fluid restriction helped nocturia and that poor sleep worsened next-day general slowness and forgetfulness. The participant wrote that they planned to try chamomile tea and report back on whether it helped sleep or simply increased nighttime urination. No follow-up efficacy outcome was posted, highlighting a practical bedtime fluid-load trade-off for people with Parkinson's nocturia.
