Title: Parkinson's UK public forum thread asking about cold-pressed hempseed oil in Parkinson's disease
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/hempseed-oil-anyone-tried/7632
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2014
Date basis: Opening post publication date (2014-08-04) on publicly accessible Parkinson's UK Discourse forum thread (replies September 2014).

Summary of anonymized thread (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/hempseed-oil-anyone-tried/7632, 3 posts, August-September 2014, handles omitted): The opening poster explicitly specified non-intoxicating cold-pressed culinary hempseed oil from Cannabis sativa seeds (rich in essential fatty acids) and asked whether anyone had noticed positive effects in Parkinson's disease management.

One community member replied that they had not tried it yet but had read online claims and was willing to try it. A third participant—reporting as a family caregiver for an uncle with Parkinson's—stated that the uncle experienced no benefit at all from hempseed oil, and on the contrary appeared to feel worse, with the effects of his other Parkinson's medications seeming less noticeable while consuming hempseed oil. Neither exact oil dose, pressing verification, concomitant levodopa/fat-meal timing, nor specific motor scales were reported, making causality unresolved while documenting a real-world unfavorable observation.
