Title: Parkinson's UK Forum no-benefit and uncertain self-reports after multi-month coconut oil use for PD and memory
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/coconut-oil/1356
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2013-01-17
Date basis: Primary thread opening post and replies dated 2013-01-17 (TID 1356); companion thread replies dated 2013-11-18 to 2013-12-04 (TID 6900).

In a Parkinson's UK public forum thread opened January 17, 2013 (all participant handles omitted), a member described watching Dr. Mary Newport's Alzheimer's coconut oil memory videos and planning regular coconut oil intake for Parkinson's neuroprotection. In Post #2 (January 17, 2013), another participant with Parkinson's explicitly reported no benefit after prolonged use: having tried coconut oil for 4 months (and a gluten-free diet for 6 months), 'neither did anything for me.'
In a separate companion Parkinson's UK thread opened November 18, 2013 (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/coconut-oil/6900, Post #2 dated November 23, 2013), a member diagnosed 9 years earlier who ate roughly 3 teaspoons/day of organic coconut oil on toast noted uncertainty about any Parkinson's benefit ('I don't know if it has helped with my PD') while finding it helpful for dry mouth, dry nose, skin, and hair.
Observation and limits: Essential counterweight to publicized caregiver testimonials (such as Newport 2024), documenting multi-month no-benefit and uncertain firsthand Parkinson's experiences with unverified oil grades and no formal cognitive or motor testing.
