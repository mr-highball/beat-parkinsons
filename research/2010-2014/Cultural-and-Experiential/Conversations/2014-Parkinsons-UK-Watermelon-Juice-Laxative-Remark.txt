Title: Parkinson's UK forum remark mentioning watermelon juice as an informal laxative
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/duodopa-nhs-says-no/7540
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2014-07-01
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2014-07-01; watermelon juice remark posted 2015-01-13, post #59).

In a multi-month public Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussion opened in July 2014, a participant returning from travel in the tropics remarked in January 2015 (post #59) that they joked about retiring with a steady supply of watermelon juice as a laxative. No intake quantity, duration, concurrent laxatives, or measurable bowel outcomes were reported, and the comment referred to liquid juice rather than raw fruit portions. All personal identifiers are omitted. While consistent with watermelon's high water and FODMAP (fructose/mannitol) content that can loosen stools or trigger bloating in sensitive individuals, this casual remark provides no clinical efficacy evidence for PD constipation.
