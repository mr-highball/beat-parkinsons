Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion mentioning dried mangoes, dried apricots, and prune juice for constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/horrible-taste-in-my-mouth/1099
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2013-06-20
Date basis: Opening post and reply dates on public Parkinson's UK Discourse thread (2013-06-20 to 2013-06-21).

In a June 2013 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread initiated by a participant starting ropinirole therapy and experiencing persistent constipation alongside an unpleasant taste in the mouth, a replying participant with Parkinson's recommended maintaining physical activity, vegetables, fluids, and fruit, specifically noting that prune juice, dried apricots, or dried mangoes work very well for some types of constipation. The original poster replied the next day noting plans to add dried fruit and prunes to their shopping list. All personal identifiers and member handles are omitted. This informal community exchange reflects practical patient use of dried mango alongside other high-fiber dried fruits for bowel regularity, without controlled dosing, isolated exposure, or objective stool outcomes.
