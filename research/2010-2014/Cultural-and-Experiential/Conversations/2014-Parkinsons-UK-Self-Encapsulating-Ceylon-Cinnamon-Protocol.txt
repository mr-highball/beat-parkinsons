Title: Parkinson's UK public forum discussion on self-encapsulating organic Ceylon cinnamon after 2014 mouse news
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/what-i-have-been-doing-to-manage-pd/7409
Source kind: conversations
Publication date: 2014-07-30
Date basis: Exact public forum post timestamps (Parkinson's UK Discourse topic 7409, posts #25-#26 dated 2014-07-23 to 2014-07-30).

Following July 2014 media coverage of the Rush University MPTP mouse cinnamon study, a caregiver on the Parkinson's UK forum shared the press release (post dated 2014-07-23) and a young-onset PD participant replied on 2014-07-30 that they had purchased organic Ceylon cinnamon and empty gelatin capsules to incorporate ground cinnamon into their daily regimen alongside concurrent lifestyle changes and planned MAO-B inhibitor adjustments. No isolated baseline-to-follow-up Parkinson's symptom measurement attributable to cinnamon was reported, illustrating how preclinical rodent news rapidly drives human self-supplementation without controlled clinical data.
