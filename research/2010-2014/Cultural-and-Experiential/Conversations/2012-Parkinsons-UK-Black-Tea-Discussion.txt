Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Thread Discussing Black Tea, Neuroprotection Claims, and Adding Milk
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/black-tea/6053
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2012-11-07
Date basis: First and final post timestamps on public Parkinson's UK Discourse thread (2012-11-07).

Public Parkinson's UK forum thread (5 posts on November 7, 2012) initiated by a community member exploring epidemiologic reports linking black tea intake to lower Parkinson disease risk and preclinical autophagy papers. Another member asked whether "black tea" meant a tea variety or tea served without milk, and the thread noted the botanical processing distinction along with popular concern that adding milk might attenuate tea polyphenols. No contributor reported personal symptom improvement or worsening after drinking brewed black tea.
