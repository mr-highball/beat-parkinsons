Public Parkinson's UK community discussion (17 posts, October 2012; all usernames and identifying details removed) regarding persistent Parkinson's-related constipation.

Key privacy-safe observations:
- Favorable multi-factor chili anecdotes: One contributor living with Parkinson's reported achieving regular daily bowel movements after adding a daily raw chili pepper alongside beetroot and increased fluids, and the opening contributor noted that eating a hot spicy curry sometimes helped relieve constipation.
- Limited dietary reliability: Other participants emphasized that Parkinson's slow-transit constipation frequently failed to respond to dietary adjustments alone and required scheduled osmotic or stool-softening medications (such as macrogol/Movicol), highlighting high inter-individual variability and multi-ingredient confounding in self-reported chili remedies.
