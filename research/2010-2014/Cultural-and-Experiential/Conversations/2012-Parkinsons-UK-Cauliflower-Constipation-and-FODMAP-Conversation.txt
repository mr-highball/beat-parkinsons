Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Conversation: Spiced Cauliflower Bhaji Constipation Relief and Later Low-FODMAP Bloating Experience
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation/2386
Source kind: Public forum conversation
Publication date: 2012-10-07
Date basis: Published forum reply date for cauliflower report (2012-10-07; thread spanned 2012-10-03 to 2020-04-30).

Public Parkinson's UK forum conversation (17 posts, 2012-10-03 to 2020-04-30) on refractory Parkinson's constipation. In a 2012-10-07 follow-up, a participant diagnosed with PD in 2008 reported that eating a spiced cauliflower bhaji and mushroom bhaji curry the prior evening resolved acute constipation (confounded by curry spices, oil, and mushrooms). Elsewhere in the same thread, participants underscored serious constipation risks including bowel obstruction, and a 2020 responder reported that high-fiber diets stopped working and triggered IBS-like bloating and trapped gas, leading them to adopt a low-FODMAP diet (which typically limits cauliflower) alongside prescription secretagogues.
