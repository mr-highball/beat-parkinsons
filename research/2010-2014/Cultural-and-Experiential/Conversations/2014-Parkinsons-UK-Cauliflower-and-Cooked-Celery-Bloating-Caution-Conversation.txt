Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Conversation: Gastroparesis Bloating Guidance Avoiding Cauliflower and Cooked Celery
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/burning-feet-palpitations-bloating-and-frequency/7043
Source kind: Public forum conversation
Publication date: 2014-01-06
Date basis: Published forum reply date citing cauliflower and cooked celery avoidance (2014-01-06; thread opened 2014-01-03).

Public Parkinson's UK forum thread (39 posts, started 2014-01-03) discussing severe stomach bloating, gastroparesis, wearing-off, and urinary frequency in advanced Parkinson's. On 2014-01-06, a contributor shared gastroparesis diet guidance warning that high-fat and fibrous foods slow gastric emptying and risk phytobezoar formation, specifically listing cauliflower, cabbage, raw fruits and vegetables, and cooked celery as foods to avoid in favor of softer vegetables, while transparently noting they had personally relied on smaller, more frequent meals rather than strict vegetable elimination.
