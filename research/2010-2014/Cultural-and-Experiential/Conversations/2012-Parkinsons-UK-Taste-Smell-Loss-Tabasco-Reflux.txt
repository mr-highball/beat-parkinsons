Title: Parkinson's UK Forum: Using Tabasco and Chili Sauces for Parkinson's Hyposmia vs Acid Reflux Intolerance
Collection: Cultural and experiential

Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-02-02
Date basis: Original public thread opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK Community Forum (2012-02-02; 16 posts through 2012-02-23).

Public Parkinson's UK community discussion (16 posts, February 2012; all usernames and identifying details removed) concerning diminished taste and smell in Parkinson's disease.

Key privacy-safe observations:
- Sensory workaround for hyposmia: Multiple participants and care partners reported adding pungent pepper condiments (including Tabasco pepper sauce, sweet chili sauce, and strong spices) to otherwise bland meals so trigeminal heat made food enjoyable despite Parkinson's-related loss of smell.
- Upper-GI adverse trade-off: The opening contributor noted that while strong flavors were tempting when taste was muted, spicy foods provoked troublesome acid reflux, limiting practical tolerance.
- Relevance to Round 3 review: Clearly separates culinary flavor compensation via oral trigeminal pungency from biological restoration of olfaction.
