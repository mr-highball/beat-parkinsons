Title: Parkinson's UK forum post reporting perceived smell improvement after large doses of probiotic yogurt
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/smell/2579
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2012-12-17
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2012-12-17).

On December 17, 2012, a participant on the public Parkinson's UK forum posted asking whether others had experienced any change in sense of smell, reporting a perceived improvement in their own olfaction that they tentatively attributed to taking a very large dose of concentrated probiotic yogurt. Subsequent replies discussed living with Parkinson-related anosmia (such as inability to smell cinnamon or vanilla) without corroborating any smell recovery from yogurt. All contributor names and handles are omitted. This isolated, uncontrolled self-report lacks objective smell testing, strain/dose details, or independent confirmation and serves only as an experiential hypothesis lead.
