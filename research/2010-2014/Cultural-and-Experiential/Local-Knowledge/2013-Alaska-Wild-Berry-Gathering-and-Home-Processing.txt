Title: Alaska wild berry gathering and home processing in the Dinstel paper
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3751288/
Published: 2013-08-05
Date basis: Publication of the inspected report
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Source kind: Local knowledge described in a published extension research report

Dinstel and colleagues describe gathering wild berries as part of
Alaskan subsistence and food culture. They report consumption of
fresh berries and preserving berries for later use through home
preparations such as juices, jam, sauces and dried fruit. This
description comes from the authors' contextual account.

The authors' own laboratory comparison concerns four identified
wild berry species. Their strawberry mentions point to separate
scientific studies. The article supplies no strawberry-specific
traditional treatment account, no individual symptom trajectory
and no Parkinson fall observation. The associated primary extract
preserves the measured sample and preparation boundaries.
