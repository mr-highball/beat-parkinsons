The bulletin calls a product made by opening fresh figs and sun-drying the fruit chriha. It reports a 2009 processing test in Bouhouda, Taounate, and describes a separate storage practice using aromatic plants. The name is evidence for a dried fruit preparation in this context. Later survey entries group it with several parts or preparation codes and leave individual exposures unspecified.
