Gharibzahedi et al. (Journal of Food Science and Technology, 2014; PMID:24426045; PMC3857422) characterized walnut oils from three traditional Iranian Juglans regia growing regions (Toyserkan, Chaboksar, Karaj), documenting 50.2-51.4% linoleic acid and 10.5-12.0% alpha-linolenic acid alongside cultivar-dependent differences in phenolics, tocopherols, and hydroperoxide/secondary oxidation products. This regional food-science record highlights traditional Persian walnut-oil culinary heritage while showing that polyunsaturated walnut oils are prone to rancidity and compositional variability—and provides no clinical Parkinson symptom evidence.
