# Food2Learn registration and planned sleep measures

## Registered adolescent sleep measures in Food2Learn

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT02240264 on September 15, 2014. Its current record lists the trial as completed in August 2016, with no posted results. It specifies sleep duration by ActivPAL and diary and sleep quality by two adolescent questionnaires at 12 months. The later protocol identifies the comparator as a multiple-oil capsule including corn oil. The registry and protocol represent one trial family.
