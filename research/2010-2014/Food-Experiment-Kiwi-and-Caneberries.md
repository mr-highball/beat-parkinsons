# Kiwifruit, blackberry and raspberry studies

## Oral allergy cases after kiwifruit and raspberry

The 2013 Polish report describes systemic reactions after kiwifruit ingestion and documents positive testing with fresh kiwifruit. Ciprandi and Tosca’s 2014 report describes anaphylactic shock after raspberry ingestion during a walking outing. In each case the observed outcome is allergy, which differs from a change in Parkinson gait, attention or orthostatic symptoms. [Primary kiwifruit case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3834723/) · [Primary raspberry case](https://www.eurannallergyimm.com/anaphylactic-shock-to-raspberry/)

## Blackberry controlled feeding registration

NCT01932879 first posted August 30, 2013. It planned a high-fat controlled-feeding crossover of blackberry intake and a calorie-matched control, with substrate oxidation and a glucose tolerance test as endpoints. The registry reports 35 enrolled and no posted results. The subsequent article distinguishes 27 enrolled in its reported analysis cohort, 17 for calorimetry and 24 for the meal challenge, and provides the actual fruit preparation. The registry and report belong to one study family. ## Kiwi exposure in an allergic coronary case

Gázquez and colleagues’ 2010 series of five allergic coronary presentations identifies kiwi in case 1. A 66-year-old man with prior kiwi oral allergy experienced urticaria, vomiting, chest pain and hypotension after eating a piece of kiwi. His diagnostic testing included a positive prick test. No numerical pressure or posture-specific test is reported for this patient. A pressure of 64/39 in the paper belongs to case 2 after ibuprofen. The kiwi case is acute and outside a Parkinson cohort. [Original source](https://www.jiaci.org/issues/vol20issue2/10.pdf) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2010-Gazquez-Kiwi-Kounis-Case-Extract.txt)

## Black raspberry slurry crossover registration

NCT01568827 first posted April 2, 2012. Ten overweight or obese men were assigned treatment sequences of a freeze-dried black raspberry powder slurry and water around a test meal. The prespecified outcomes concern inflammation and tolerability. Posture-specific blood pressure and lightheadedness measures are unspecified. Registration and later results belong to one cohort. [Source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01568827) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2012-NCT01568827-Black-Raspberry-Registration-Extract.txt)

## Published black raspberry slurry crossover

Sardo and colleagues’ author abstract is indexed in 2013 and appeared in a 2016 journal issue. The open-label crossover in ten overweight men compared consuming a 45 g freeze-dried black raspberry powder slurry with periods without the slurry. Postprandial IL-6 exposure was lower after the slurry. CRP and TNF-alpha yielded inconclusive results. The abstract supplies no blood pressure by treatment or posture-specific symptom estimate. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23982695/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2013-Sardo-Black-Raspberry-Crossover-Abstract-Extract.txt)

## Blackberry flower buds and background hoarseness use

Martins and colleagues’ 2013 poster describes traditional external use of Rubus ulmifolius preparations for hoarseness. Its experiment compares flower-bud preparations in antioxidant assays. The oral uses discussed concern other conditions. The report supplies no clinical voice outcome, berry ingestion comparison or Parkinson cohort. This source helps distinguish the background use from the measured in vitro work.
