# Registered corn oil comparator and Epworth daytime sleepiness

## Registered corn oil comparator and Epworth daytime sleepiness

The official protocol names corn oil and a planned daytime sleepiness scale. Results and linkage to the 2014 report remain open. [Possible related report](Food-Experiment-Corn-Oil-Ethyl-Ester-Comparator.md)

[Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01216982) · [Local source text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT01216982.txt)

### A related military cohort and the protocol number

PMID 22748167 reports a deployed servicemember biomarker analysis. Its author report names approval M-10023, which matches the sponsor ID in the registration. That supports a link to the same program. The 2014 intervention report (PMID 24690964) gives a post-treatment biomarker–sleepiness relationship and a comparator described as corn-oil ethyl esters. The registered product is described as corn oil. The exact treatment materials and the registered arm-level Epworth outcome remain unresolved.
