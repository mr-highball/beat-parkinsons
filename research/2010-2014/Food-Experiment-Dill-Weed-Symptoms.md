# Dill weed symptom evidence

## Dill pickle brine and cycling endurance

Nine euhydrated healthy men underwent a crossover with dill pickle brine, hypertonic saline and deionized water. Times to exhaustion were 77.4 ± 5.9, 77.4 ± 4.0 and 75.7 ± 3.2 minutes, respectively, with P=.40. The researchers strained commercial dill pickle brine. The plant part, freshness and actual dose of dill in the brine were not specified. Cycling time to exhaustion measures performance in this setting and does not provide a Parkinson fatigue severity score. [Peikert full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3975776/).
