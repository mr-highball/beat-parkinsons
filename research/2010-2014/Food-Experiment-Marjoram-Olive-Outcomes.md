# Marjoram and olive oil symptom outcomes

## Extra-virgin olive oil in a Parkinson motor case

Renoudet and colleagues first published June 25, 2012. The patient's basic diet had included extra-virgin olive oil for years while the disease progressed. Following the addition of foods rich in fisetin and hexacosanol, the authors reported improved hypomimia, dystonia, micrographia, arm swing and retropulsion. They did not report a measured stride length. The case gives no exposure change isolating olive oil and supplies no evidence about refined oil. ## Olive oil comparator and sleep diaries in primary insomnia

Cornu and colleagues studied adults with chronic primary insomnia. The oral olive-oil placebo arm recorded mean diary sleep efficiency of 81.5% in week one and 85.1% in week four. The active multi-ingredient arm recorded 80.9% and 82.8%. Diary evolution and Leeds sleep scores were inconclusive between arms. The actigraphy subset also showed similar changes. Oil grade was unspecified, and there was no inert comparator for the oil itself.
