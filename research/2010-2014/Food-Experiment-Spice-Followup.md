# Spice evidence follow-up, 2010–2014

## Zingerone and eugenol after 6-OHDA lesion

Kabuto and Yamanushi first published the journal article on July 19, 2011. In 6-OHDA-lesioned mice, oral isolated zingerone or eugenol given alongside injected levodopa failed to improve levodopa-related abnormal behaviors and increased depletion of striatal dopamine and its metabolites. Earlier constituent pretreatment studies reported protection of striatal dopamine. The timing, preparations, and model limit application to human culinary ginger or clove. The grant report lists related presentations in October 2009 and May 2010. The individual conference abstracts were unavailable for checking which results appeared at each meeting. [2011 primary abstract, PMID 21769642](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21769642/) [Earlier zingerone study, PMID 16018576](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16018576/) [Earlier eugenol study, PMID 17329831](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17329831/) [Investigator grant report](https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/ja/file/KAKENHI-PROJECT-20500714/20500714seika.pdf)

The investigators’ May 30, 2011 grant report describes two minutes of observation after levodopa dosing. From week three, several lesioned mice stretched or stiffened their hindlimbs just after the injection. The report states that zingerone and eugenol failed to suppress the observed behavior. It provides no counts by arm, dyskinesia severity scores or stride measurements. The matching protocol and outcome link the grant report to the journal study. ## Isolated piperine in 6-OHDA rats

Shrivastava and colleagues first published online on July 20, 2012. Isolated piperine reduced apomorphine-provoked rotations and improved narrow-beam and rotarod outcomes in a 6-OHDA rat model. The abstract gives no separate route or detailed movement estimates. A related 2009 conference abstract reports oral piperine and narrow-beam outcomes. Neither report measures human medication off time after ground black pepper for `0129`. [Primary abstract, PMID 22819561](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22819561/) [2009 conference outcome](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Spice-Followup.md#piperine-conference-abstract-in-6-ohda-rats)


## Oral curcuminoids and motor tasks in MPTP mice

Ojha and colleagues published in 2012. Oral curcuminoids improved rotarod and open-field performance and preserved striatal dopamine in MPTP mice. The study used turmeric constituents and model movement tasks. It supplies no grated turmeric rigidity or ground turmeric bradykinesia estimate. [Primary abstract, PMID 22527634](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22527634/)

## Isolated ginger constituent in MPTP mice

Park and colleagues first published this study on July 1, 2013. Oral isolated 6-shogaol shortened pole-test movement times in MPTP mice relative to vehicle-treated model mice. Time to turn fell from 164.44% to 108.02% of control and landing time fell from 127.86% to 105.01% of control (both p < .01). The Methods and figure caption differ on dosing timing and the test cutoff. The pole task measures movement speed in the model. The study gives no estimate of muscle rigidity after grated ginger for `0626`. [Primary report, Methods and Figure 4, PMID 23811724](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4003157/)

## Nigella seed extract in chlorpromazine-treated rats

Sandhu and Rana published in 2013. Their oral ethanolic seed extract groups had mean catalepsy scores of 0.780 and 0.587 versus 1.727 in rats given chlorpromazine alone (both p < .001). This acute behavioral assay models antipsychotic-induced motor effects. It gives no culinary nigella seed estimate for Parkinson disease. [Primary report, Table 1](https://innovareacademics.in/journal/ijpps/Vol5Suppl3/7604.pdf)

## Capsaicin as a probe in a levodopa dyskinesia mouse model

González-Aparicio and Moratalla first published on October 17, 2013. In 6-OHDA-lesioned mice treated with levodopa, oleoylethanolamide reduced dyskinetic movements. Pretreatment with isolated capsaicin blocked that reduction and the associated changes in two molecular markers. This receptor-probe comparison supplies no clinical dyskinesia effect from consuming ground cayenne for `0640`. [Primary abstract, PMID 24140894](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24140894/)

## Isolated thymoquinone and rotations in 6-OHDA rats

Sedaghat and colleagues published online on January 31, 2014. Oral isolated thymoquinone before 6-OHDA lesion reduced apomorphine-induced turning at the higher tested dose. The study also reported neuronal and oxidative outcomes. The drug-provoked turning comparison does not measure clinical bradykinesia after eating nigella seeds. [Primary report, PMID 24734075](https://brieflands.com/journals/ijpr/articles/125415) [Local report](Source-Text/PMC3985249.txt)

## Project report on eugenol and abnormal movement

The public KAKEN page dates the fiscal-2010 final report to January 26, 2012. The project describes isolated eugenol and zingerone in a mouse Parkinson model. The recorded motor sign was abnormal hind-limb extension after levodopa treatment, which the compounds did not suppress. The report also describes less favorable striatal dopamine measurements after treatment. It gives no gait, walking-speed or ground-spice comparison. The 2011 journal abstract has overlapping authors and design. Its full behavioral and neurochemical tables remain unavailable.
