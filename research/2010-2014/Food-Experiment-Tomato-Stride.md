# Tomato and stride follow-up

## Published figures in the early isolated lycopene studies

Kaur and colleagues administered 10 mg/kg oral lycopene for 30 days in a rotenone rat model. The author's abstract reports reversal of behavioral deficits and increased acetylcholinesterase activity in the striatum. Publisher Fig. 1A plots day-30 totals of roughly 25 s with rotenone and 10 s with rotenone plus lycopene. Fig. 1B plots roughly 4.4 versus 2.0 score units. Fig. 2 plots roughly 28 versus 7.5 s. The publisher archive shows the original figures without explanatory captions or Methods. The visible axes are generic and cannot establish the specific tasks or compare stride lengths.
