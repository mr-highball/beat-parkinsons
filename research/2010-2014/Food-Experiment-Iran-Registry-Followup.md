# Iranian dietary trial registration, 2010–2014

## Fava bean extract compared with levodopa

IRCT2013060313570N1 was registered July 23, 2013. The study compared a levodopa-containing extract of *Vicia faba* with a conventional levodopa regimen and measured blood levodopa and clinical effects. The 2023 full report confirms this identifier. The frozen bean statements use other species.
