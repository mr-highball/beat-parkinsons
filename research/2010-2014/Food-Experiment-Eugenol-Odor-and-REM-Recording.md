# Eugenol odor pulses during sleep recordings

## Olfactory response during REM and slow wave sleep

Barnes, Chapuis, Chaudhury and Wilson, PLOS ONE, March 23, 2011. DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0018130. In a separately recorded group of untrained rats, eugenol and carvone odors were used as short olfactory stimuli. Additional rats had electromyography and piriform cortex field potential recording to permit odor delivery during wake, REM and slow-wave sleep. The pooled odor response in piriform cortex was greater in wake and REM than in slow-wave sleep. The study used EMG to classify sleep state, with no eugenol-specific estimate of loss of REM atonia or dream enactment. The conditioned fear freezing assessment also differs from sleep motor behavior. The route and exposure do not correspond to ingested ground allspice.
