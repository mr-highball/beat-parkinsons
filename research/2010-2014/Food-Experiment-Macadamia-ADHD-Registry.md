# Registered macadamia oil comparator

## Macadamia oil comparator in an ADHD registry

NCT02156089 first posted June 5, 2014. Its April 3, 2015 version names oral macadamia oil in the comparator smoothie for adults with ADHD. The primary measure concerns the ventral striatal imaging response during a reward task. Trial outcomes do not identify hand dexterity as a measured endpoint. The status is terminated, and the official record has no posted results. The registered pressing method is unspecified. The trial was separate from the pending Japanese healthy-adult vigilance study.
