# Rosemary Voice

## Rosemary containing spray and pooled acute throat symptoms

Ben-Arye and colleagues compared a mixture of rosemary, oregano, mint and two eucalyptus oils against placebo spray in 60 adults with upper respiratory illness. At 20 minutes 19/26 versus 14/34 improved at least one point on their worst symptom from sore throat, hoarseness and cough, p=.019. Table 2 prints a control heading of n=35, although the prose and percentages use 34. The three-day between-group comparison was inconclusive at p=.42. No isolated hoarseness or voice sound-pressure level estimate was reported. The journal citation uses 2011, with an online record first appearing in 2010. Either date falls in this period.
