# Kiwifruit and raspberry study lineages and outcomes

## Whole frozen red raspberry and measured human outcomes

Snyder’s thesis title page says fall 2010. The University of Toronto record for handle 1807/33687 lists issuance and public availability on November 29, 2012. In the original thesis, 24 healthy adults completed a four-week exposure to individually quick-frozen whole red raspberries and eight took part in acute comparisons. The chronic phase had no parallel untreated group. Methods and appendices describe oxidative biomarkers and a general symptom questionnaire without a specific anxiety measure. The Methods list second-phase portions of 1, 2 or 4 cups, while the participant instructions print 1, 2 or 3. The two OpenAlex identities link to this one thesis. ## High-risk oral cancer black raspberry registry OSU-07085

First posted November 10, 2011. OSU-07085 enrolled 70 in the ClinicalTrials.gov record. The registered objectives cover safety and tolerability, dose and formulation effects on oral tissue uptake, biomarkers and their persistence. The public record shows no posted summary results. Uhrig includes this protocol in the combined dissertation analysis. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01469429) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2011-NCT01469429-Black-Raspberry-Dose-Delivery.txt)

## Appalachian oral cancer black raspberry registry OSU-06132

First posted January 6, 2012. The OSU-06132 registry reports 42 enrollees, a lozenge arm and a non-berry biomarker comparison. It describes administration of the Insomnia Severity Index, Brief Fatigue Inventory and Head and Neck Cancer Inventory. The primary registered outcome is time to recurrence. There are no posted summary results or separate anxiety measure. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01504932) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2012-NCT01504932-Black-Raspberry-Recurrence-Scope.txt)

## Black raspberry formulation and patient questionnaires in the Uhrig thesis

Ohio State dissertation, 2014, OhioLINK accession osu1416961549. The full thesis describes 112 participants across OSU-06132 and OSU-07085, with a comparison and 4 g or 8 g per day lyophilized black raspberry groups using oral preparations. Printed pp. 48–49 identify the cultivar and formulation. Pages 65–69 describe its questionnaires. General mood appears in a fatigue-interference item and distress appears in an insomnia item. Pages 107–112 report an inconclusive insomnia contrast, with no separate anxiety outcome. The registrations identify two protocols, and the combined thesis is a shared publication.
