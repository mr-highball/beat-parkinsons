# Corn oil and fatigue, 2010–2014

## Corn oil comparator in the OFAMS multiple sclerosis trial

Torkildsen and colleagues randomized 92 people with relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis to omega-3 capsules or corn oil, analyzing 91. The Fatigue Severity Scale comparison was inconclusive at six months (p=.97) and over 24 months (p=.57). Both groups started interferon beta-1a after six months. The main report gives no arm mean FSS changes and no corn-oil refining grade. This active-oil comparison leaves the effect against no oil open.
