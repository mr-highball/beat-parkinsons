# Black pepper and clinical outcomes

Review completed on 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

## Oral pepper fruit and sustained attention

Lindheimer, Loy and O'Connor published this randomized, double-blind crossover on 2013-08-01. Forty young adults with low perceived energy completed all three visits. Each participant swallowed capsules with 2.0 g of dried *Piper nigrum* fruit blend (53 mg/g piperine), 3.1 g of rice flour, or 1.7 g of rosemary, on different days while wearing a noseclip. The authors did not describe the dried fruit as cracked.

The 16-minute dual task measured recall-free sustained attention at baseline and at 60 and 90 minutes. Mean primary task reaction time in milliseconds (SD) was 587.3 (65.6), 601.6 (57.8) and 613.3 (67.3) on the pepper visit and 607.9 (74.0), 599.5 (75.4) and 592.5 (69.1) on the placebo visit. Condition-by-time interactions were nonsignificant for all cognitive measures (p > .05). Response speed means diverged between exposure visits and the trial did not establish an attention effect. The participants did not represent a PD cohort. [Lindheimer attention article](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1089/jmf.2012.0216), [local text](Source-Text/PMID23905652-Black-Pepper-Attention-Extract.txt).

## Piperine with resveratrol and digit vigilance

Wightman and colleagues published the trial online on 2014-05-07. Twenty-three healthy adults took placebo, trans-resveratrol 250 mg alone, and trans-resveratrol 250 mg with 20 mg piperine in a randomized, double-blind crossover. After 40 minutes, the participants performed repeated cognitive tasks that included rapid visual information processing to detect digit sequences. The authors reported no significant treatment effects on cognitive test outcomes. The combined capsule increased frontal cerebral blood flow measures during tasks. The trial did not administer piperine alone or involve a PD population. [Wightman piperine resveratrol article](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114514000737), [local text](Source-Text/PMID24804871-Piperine-Digit-Vigilance-Extract.txt).

## Piperine-containing cocktail and canine spatial attention

The controlled canine cocktail comparison reports improved landmark-task accuracy. The study included five active ingredients and has no pepper-only arm, so the result remains mixture-level animal evidence. ## Mixed rosemary species and acute energy ratings

The Lindheimer study’s other active capsule contained Rosmarinus officinalis and R. eriocalyx. The report gives rosemary moisture content but leaves species proportions and drying method unspecified. Forty young adults with low energy each received two of three conditions, yielding 26 rosemary, 26 pepper and 24 placebo sessions. The tabulated rosemary mental-fatigue means were 135.1, 112.4 and 130.2 from baseline through the repeated assessments, compared with 132.5, 135.3 and 138.0 on placebo. The reported treatment-by-time mood comparisons remained above .05. The exploratory fatigue effect and the combined species do not establish fresh rosemary and depression. [Publisher full original](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1089/jmf.2012.0216) · [Expanded factual text](Source-Text/PMID23905652-Black-Pepper-Attention-Extract.txt)

## Piperine in a neuropathic pain adjunct trial

Di Pierro and Settembre’s July 2013 open trial enrolled 141 people with lumbar sciatica or carpal tunnel syndrome, with 135 completers. The arms used dexibuprofen alone, with lipoic acid, or with the curcumin, lipoic acid and piperine combination. The authors report greater pain reduction with the combination. A curcumin-only arm or piperine-only arm was absent. The later erratum concerns trademarks.
