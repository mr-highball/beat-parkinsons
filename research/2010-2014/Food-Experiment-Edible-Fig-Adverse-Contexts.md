# Fig and olive mixture trial registration

## Iranian registration for fig and olive adjunct therapy

IRCT2013122015876N1 records registration on March 3, 2014, a target of 58 and completed recruitment. February 20, 2014 is the expected recruitment start and the actual date fields are blank. The WHO export labels the same value as a date of first enrollment. The trial papers report sampling from September 2014 to August 2015 and 72 entrants and 56 completers. The randomized intervention registry lists 15 mL twice daily, while the publications describe 15 g three times daily. These differences are unresolved. The registered outcomes include rheumatoid arthritis disease activity, joint pain, urine measures, glucose, lipids and diarrhea. The registry has no visual hallucination measure or public result located.
