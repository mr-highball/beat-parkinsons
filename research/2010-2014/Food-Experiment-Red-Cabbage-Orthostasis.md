# Red cabbage and orthostatic symptoms

## 2011 MAXVEG registration

The Danish diabetes registration compares two mixed cabbage-and-vegetable diets and a habitual diet. It lists glucose tolerance and laboratory outcomes. The later report identifies red cabbage in both active diets and reports 24-hour pressure as a secondary measure. The registry and paper describe different endpoint and timing scopes.
