# Safflower comparator and registered anxiety scores

## Safflower comparator and registered anxiety scores

ACTRN12614000390684 was registered April 10, 2014. Outpatients with anxiety or mood symptoms already taking DHA entered an eight-week-per-period DHA versus safflower oil crossover. The registry lists the Hamilton Anxiety Scale and other psychiatric measures, no washout and final accrual of 60. The oil grade is unspecified. A later abstract has matching investigator, product and design but lists 47 participants and does not identify the placebo or registry ID.
