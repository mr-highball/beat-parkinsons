# Olive oil and anxiety-like behavior

## Whole extra-virgin olive oil and elevated plus-maze time

Perveen and colleagues treated male Wistar rats with extra-virgin olive oil for 27 days. Figure 2 reports more time in the open arms of the maze at p<.05 with n=5 in its caption. The methods describe a water control, while the caption calls it saline. This small study gives no cold-press processing details or energy-matched comparator. It cites a separate aging-rat experiment whose results and figure show a longer light–dark transition latency with high-phenol oil, while its abstract describes reduced latency. The distinct rat samples and outcomes cannot be pooled as a clinical effect.
