# Controlled tongue and walking tasks after local capsaicin

## Tongue force tracking during intraoral capsaicin pain

In a separate 2010 report, Boudreau and colleagues compared tongue-tip somatosensory conditions during a brief tongue force-tracking task. The capsaicin condition had smaller overall performance gains, with more overshoot and undershoot errors and delayed responses. The paper also describes a separate longer training comparison of session duration. Tongue force and timing are distinct from speech intelligibility. Individual overlap with the 2007 report has not been established. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20492438/) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2010-Boudreau-Capsaicin-Tongue-Tracking-Extract.txt)

## Topical capsaicin and retention of a treadmill adaptation

Bouffard and colleagues had 30 healthy participants learn to walk with a force perturbation linked to one ankle. The topical capsaicin group learned during ankle pain and was retested pain-free the next day. Initial gait parameters and adaptation were comparable by group, with poorer retention of the adapted ankle trajectory on the next day. A later same-team trial maintained pain at both sessions and did not find clear interference with overall performance improvement. These report different experimental conditions and do not test normal stride shortening or freezing episodes. [2014 primary report](https://www.jneurosci.org/content/34/28/9190) · [2016 follow-up](https://doi.org/10.1155/2016/8539096) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2014-Bouffard-Capsaicin-Locomotor-Adaptation-Extract.txt)


## Smoked and unsmoked paprika consumed by healthy adults

Tormo and colleagues reported additional glutathione-cycle and enzyme-expression analyses online in 2012. They describe the same university cohort, La Vera smoked and Novelda unsmoked paprika preparations, and crossover schedule as the earlier Campillo trial. The authors cite that trial for the protocol. The Campillo catalog entry covers blood pressure and the exposure metabolite. Oxidized glutathione decreased and reduced glutathione increased in paired samples after smoked paprika. The overlapping methods indicate a companion report from the feeding experiment. The published methods give no measures of gait, handwriting, resting tremor, freezing, or intelligibility.
