## Pumpkin seed oil capsules and seated pressure in a placebo trial

A placebo-controlled alopecia trial measured seated pressure after rest. Mean systolic changes were −1.2 and +1.0 mmHg (p = 0.496), and diastolic changes were −0.1 and −0.8 mmHg (p = 0.813). The branded capsule had no verified pressing grade, and the trial did not ascertain standing-triggered lightheadedness.
