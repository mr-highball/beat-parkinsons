# Cilantro and curry leaf extract studies

## Curry leaf extracts and drug-induced movements in rats

Patil and colleagues’ paper first published December 2, 2011 used an oral ethanol leaf extract and its alkaloid fraction in rats treated chronically with haloperidol. The paper reports improved locomotor activity and fewer involuntary oral movements. A separate April 30, 2012 study led by Patil used methanol leaf extract in reserpine-treated rats and an acute haloperidol catalepsy assay in mice. At 120 minutes the mouse control averaged 256.7 seconds on the bar, compared with 110.0 seconds at the highest tested extract level. The extract value of 113.0 seconds belongs to the 150-minute reading. The later petroleum ether and alkaloid fractions from the same lead author prolonged catalepsy in a haloperidol mouse assay. The different formulations and protocols leave the clinical questions open. ## Injected cilantro leaf extract and anxiety-like behavior

Harsha and Anilakumar reported more open-arm activity in an elevated plus maze after injecting a hydroalcoholic *Coriandrum sativum* leaf extract into mice. The higher extract levels also increased open-field movement, which complicates interpreting maze activity as an anxiety-specific effect. The study used an injected extract in mice without induced Parkinsonism. [Primary report, 2012](https://greenpharmacy.info/index.php/ijgp/article/view/253)
