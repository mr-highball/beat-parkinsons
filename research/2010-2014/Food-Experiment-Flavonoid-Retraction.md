# Retracted apigenin and luteolin report

## Original report with retracted status

Patil and colleagues' mouse-model paper first published July 31, 2014. The paper tested isolated apigenin and luteolin and is indexed as retracted. A later Parkinson supplement review cites it through an intermediate flavonoid review. The retraction removes the paper as a source of evidence direction. The original identity is retained for citation reconciliation.
