# Food experiment evidence for 2010–2014

## Dark chocolate crossover

In a single-dose, investigator-blinded crossover study of 26 people with moderate Parkinson’s, participants received dark or cocoa-free white chocolate on separate occasions. The between-treatment difference in the composite UPDRS motor score was inconclusive. The study did not test anxiety, voice volume, or speech intelligibility as individual outcomes. [Trial abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22584952/)

First published May 15, 2012. Twenty-six participants with moderate, nonfluctuating Parkinson disease received 200 g of 80.1% cocoa dark chocolate and cacao-free white chocolate in a randomized investigator-blinded crossover. The composite UPDRS III contrast at one hour was −0.54 (95% CI −1.50 to 0.42, p=.258). Its period effect was 1.46 (0.50–2.42, p=.004). The full main report gives no separate speech item or intelligibility result. The supplement concerns blood analytes. The registration first posted in 2009 and is distinct from the unreported one-week protocol. [Primary controlled crossover](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22584952/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2012-Wolz-Dark-Chocolate-Motor-Table-Extract.txt)

## Olive and flaxseed oils in hemodialysis

A four-week randomized trial compared mineral, olive, and flaxseed oils in patients with constipation receiving hemodialysis. Constipation scores improved over time in all arms, with no statistically clear group-by-time interaction. This is a bowel-outcome comparison in a different clinical population and does not establish a Parkinson’s-specific direction. [Ramos et al., online 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25238699/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2014-09-17-Ramos-Oils-Hemodialysis-Constipation.txt) · [Thesis metadata](https://bdtd.ibict.br/vufind/Record/UFSP_b8c05e48867504e6f920c753c899a567)

## Corn oil as the comparison in a Parkinson’s depression trial

Pomponi and colleagues randomized 24 people with mild to moderate Parkinson’s to DHA plus EPA or an equicaloric amount of corn oil for six months. The report states that 75% of those assigned DHA plus EPA and 25% of those assigned corn oil had at least a 50% decrease on the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale. Changes in total UPDRS and Hoehn and Yahr scores did not clearly differ. The comparison estimates a difference between the two treatments. The accessible article text gives no oil-free control or corn oil processing grade. The publisher Results preview records 96 screened and all 24 randomized completing. It leaves exact responder denominators, arm means and the complete outcome table unresolved. The improvement in some corn oil recipients is indirect evidence for `0205` because the study cannot attribute it to the oil. [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.baga.2014.03.004)

## Corn oil carrier in the 2014 cannabidiol trial

Chagas and colleagues randomized 21 people with Parkinson’s among two cannabidiol doses in corn oil or corn oil capsules without cannabidiol. Six-week changes in total UPDRS and its displayed subscores did not differ clearly. Table 2 also reports a quality-of-life physical-discomfort domain with no clear between-group difference (p = 0.292). The study gives no standalone pain measurement for `0278`. The randomized comparison estimates the effect of adding cannabidiol to the shared carrier. The oil grade and effect of consuming it alone remain undetermined.
