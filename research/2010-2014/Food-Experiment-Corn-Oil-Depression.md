# Corn Oil Depression

## Later coronary depression EPA trial registration

The study compares 2 g/day corn oil versus 2 g/day EPA for ten weeks. Both groups receive sertraline 50 mg/day. The primary outcome is Beck Depression Inventory II change from baseline. The current record gives 144 randomized and results first posted August 6, 2019. The enrollment period and registration distinguish this from the earlier Carney trial. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02021669) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/NCT02021669-Corn-Oil-Sertraline.txt)

## Heart failure depression OCEAN registration

OCEAN randomized adults with heart failure and major depressive disorder to a mixture of EPA and DHA, nearly pure EPA or corn-oil capsules for 12 weeks. The registry names Hamilton Depression Rating Scale and Beck Depression Inventory II outcomes. The posted results give 108 randomized evenly across three arms and were first released June 12, 2017. No separate no-oil group was assigned. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02057406) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/NCT02057406-OCEAN-Corn-Oil.txt)
