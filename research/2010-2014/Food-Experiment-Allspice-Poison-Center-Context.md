# Allspice oil in pediatric exposure records

## Allspice oil in poison-center records

A 2014 toxicology conference abstract reported 234 pediatric single-substance exposures coded as allspice oil in a poison-center system. The most commonly listed effects were oral irritation, cough and vomiting. It does not identify the botanical species or report all symptoms or visual hallucination counts. These are reports involving concentrated oil, not a comparison of ground allspice consumption in people with Parkinson disease.
