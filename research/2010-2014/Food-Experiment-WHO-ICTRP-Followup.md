# Botanical and nutritional trial outcomes, 2010–2014

## Yokukansan in Lewy body dementia

Two reports by Iwasaki et al., in 2011 and 2012, identify UMIN000001511 and describe the same four-week open-label cohort. The first report gives 63 enrolled, 54 in efficacy analysis and 60 in safety analysis. Mean Neuropsychiatric Inventory total score decreased by 12.5 points (p < .001), with favorable within-person changes in hallucinations, anxiety and several other subscales. The 2011 letter reports a small MMSE improvement, while the 2012 article reports no significant MMSE change. The published results describe the pooled Lewy body dementia cohort. Although the protocol allowed Parkinson’s disease dementia, a separate estimate for that diagnosis was unavailable. The product was a multi-herb extract. [Primary registry](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000001811) · [2011 report](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1532-5415.2011.03373.x) · [2012 report](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1479-8301.2012.00413.x)

## Yokukansan for Parkinson neuropsychiatric symptoms

Hatano et al. reported a 12-week open-label study in 25 people with Parkinson’s, first published October 30, 2013. Median Neuropsychiatric Inventory total score fell from 12 to 4 (p = .00003). The hallucination, anxiety and apathy subscales improved within participants. UPDRS Part III and Hoehn and Yahr scores showed no clear change. The authors reported hypokalemia below 3.5 mEq/L in two participants. The outcome schedule overlaps with the 76-person target in Tsumura’s Phase IV record jRCT1080220598, but no shared accession or other unambiguous link was verified. The intervention was a multicomponent Kampo extract. [Primary report, PMID 24169925](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24169925/) · [Phase IV registry](https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/latest-detail/jRCT1080220598)

## Vitamin D3 and Parkinson disability scales

Suzuki et al. identified UMIN000001841 in their 2013 randomized study of vitamin D3 versus placebo. The primary report compared one year of 1,200 IU daily vitamin D3 in 56 participants with placebo in 58 participants. The mean modified Hoehn and Yahr score changed by +0.02 with supplementation and +0.33 with placebo (between-group p = .005). The trial examined a vitamin capsule and a global disability scale. [2009 registration note](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-WHO-ICTRP-Followup.md#vitamin-d3-supplementation-registration) · [Primary report, PMID 23485413](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23485413/)
