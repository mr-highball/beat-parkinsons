# Carrot and sleepiness-related measurements

## Serum carotene and sleepiness

Beydoun and colleagues studied serum nutritional markers in NHANES 2005–06. Table 4 Model 2 uses 2,346 respondents and estimates opposite sleepiness associations for alpha-carotene (−0.09 ± 0.02 per SD, p=.002) and beta-carotene (+0.09 ± 0.03, p=.003). Model 4 gives +0.07 ± 0.03 for pooled carotenoids (p=.029), above the study's familywise threshold of .010. The outcome is a survey-derived factor. Serum markers can reflect many foods and metabolic differences. The study did not compare raw carrot or measure a Parkinson outcome.
