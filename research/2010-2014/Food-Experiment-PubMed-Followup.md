# Food experiment PubMed follow-up

## Asparagus and a jejunal levodopa tube

Schrader and colleagues’ case letter appeared online on October 8, 2010. PubMed indexes it under asparagus, bezoars, and Parkinson’s drug therapy and supplies no abstract. A later case series lists two cases from this letter, including a 69-year-old man who developed epigastric pain and nausea during continuous jejunal levodopa infusion. Its table describes undigested asparagus at a knotted jejunal tube with ulcers, with normal pump operation and flushing at presentation. The original full text remains an access gap. Steamed asparagus and swallowing difficulty (`0433`), steamed asparagus and drooling (`0647`), and roasted asparagus and fatigue (`0837`) are outside the available case evidence. [Primary letter, PMID 20933456](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20933456/) · [Shimamura and colleagues, Table 2](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/clinicalneurol/59/3/59_cn-001195/_pdf)

The publisher preview and full-text route did not supply the original letter during the November 2026 recheck. Hannover Medical School's 2010 bibliography lists the letter and points to the DOI. A later clinical article by Titova and Chaudhuri reproduces the original bezoar image as its Figure 2. The reproduced image and Shimamura's case table corroborate a device complication. The food preparation, symptom assessments and complete descriptions in the 2010 letter remain unavailable. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.parkreldis.2010.09.004) · [Institutional bibliography](https://www.mhh.de/fileadmin/mhh/bibliothek/pdf/jahresbibliografie_2010.pdf) · [Titova and Chaudhuri, Figure 2](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/316110832_Intrajejunal_levodopa_infusion_therapy_for_Parkinson%27s_disease_practical_and_pragmatic_tips_for_successful_maintenance_of_therapy)

## Cola and a gastric bezoar during levodopa infusion

Stathis and colleagues described a 70-year-old man with Parkinson’s disease who deteriorated abruptly during intestinal levodopa therapy. Endoscopy found a gastric bezoar around the tube. The bezoar had dissolved at repeat endoscopy four days after the team administered cola with a liquid diet and substituted oral Parkinson’s medicines. The replacement procedure found hardened gel blocking the inner tube. The report attributes the motor deterioration to that blockage. Cola is outside the frozen ingredient set. [Primary letter, PMID 24909683](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24909683/) · [Case text hosted by the lead author](https://uoa.academia.edu/PantelisStathis)
