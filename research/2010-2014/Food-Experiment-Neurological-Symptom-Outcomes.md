# Parkinson and related neurological symptom outcomes

## Retracted amino acid medication case and exposure identity

February 28, 2011. The article describes a selected case treated with levodopa and several amino acids or cofactors. Agent Orange appears as a historical exposure in the case narrative. The report has no fruit exposure contrast or quantified fall-frequency or pain comparison for the frozen hypotheses. The journal retracted the case in December 2020, DOI 10.2147/IJGM.S296324. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3068871/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2011-Hinz-Retracted-Amino-Acid-Case-Scope.txt)

## Pain and loss of interest items in a Parkinson fluctuation instrument

August 9, 2011. Eleven patients with Parkinson motor and nonmotor fluctuations and eleven caregivers participated in instrument development. The appendix asks about painful sensations, loss of interest in prior activities and the timing of each relative to levodopa. The item content identifies the constructs and gives no food or efficacy comparison. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3153935/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2011-Kleiner-Fisman-Pain-and-Loss-of-Interest-Items.txt)

## Measured stride length in a multimodal exercise pilot

October 30, 2011. The nonrandom pilot analyzed 19 trained and 10 control participants. The stride-length group-by-time interaction was p = .005 and the trained group improved after six months. The overall clinical model did not show the same interaction (p = .535) and did not isolate rigidity. This is an exercise comparison. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3263544/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2011-Vitorio-Multimodal-Exercise-and-Stride.txt)

## Swallowing history and pharyngeal muscle pathology

June 2012. This autopsy study compared eight pathologically confirmed Parkinson cases and four controls. Four of eight cases had a swallowing impairment on a UPDRS II item. Atrophic pharyngeal fibers comprised 27.6% among cases with dysphagia versus 16% among cases without. The study reports a composite motor score and swallowing history, with no separate diet comparison or quantified drooling or rest-tremor response. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3358551/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2012-Mu-Pharyngeal-Muscle-Study-Outcomes.txt)

## Forward and backward digit span in a sleep laboratory

August 2012. A 48-hour inpatient protocol included controlled observation, polysomnography and repeated digit span. The authors report day-to-day improvement on backward span among people with Parkinson disease already taking dopaminergic medication, with no clear forward-span improvement. Meals followed customary schedules. The study estimates neither fig intake effects nor hallucination-episode or tremor outcomes. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3577106/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2012-Scullin-Sleep-and-Digit-Span-Outcomes.txt)

## Gait results and resting tremor eligibility in early Parkinson disease

August 2012. Twenty-two patients with early Parkinson disease and 22 controls underwent instrumented gait assessment and short-latency afferent inhibition testing. The Parkinson group showed shorter stride length (p = .008). Significant resting tremor was a screening exclusion for inhibition testing. The reported gait and attention analyses offer no fig intake, visual hallucination frequency or tremor-change effect. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3437031/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2012-Rochester-Gait-and-Tremor-Eligibility.txt)

## Pain and apathy by probable REM sleep behavior disorder status

First online November 2013. Of 475 people with early Parkinson disease, the authors compared those with and without probable REM sleep behavior disorder. Table 3 reports pain in 76.8% without and 85.0% with probable RBD, and apathy in 13.5% without and 22.8% with probable RBD. These are baseline symptom-presence associations. The study does not assign food exposures. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3995329/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2013-Rolinski-Pain-Apathy-and-Probable-RBD.txt)

## Apathy scale and quality of life domains in Parkinson disease

First available in 2014. In the 107-person retrospective Parkinson analysis, apathy was associated with greater impairment in PDQ-39 quality of life (r = .241). Trait anxiety accounted for the most independent variation in the overall association. The report also examines the bodily discomfort domain. It supplies mood and quality-of-life context without a separate food or pain-severity comparison. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4344415/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2014-Jones-Apathy-Mood-and-Quality-of-Life.txt)

## Case-specific apathy and hallucinations in FUS proteinopathies

July 2011. The clinical histories and Table 2 report behavioral features in frontotemporal degeneration with FUS pathology. Apathy and visual hallucinations occur in patient histories and cannot be assigned to the same exposure or interpreted as a food response. The study compared proteinopathies using clinical, genetic and postmortem assessments. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3170529/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2011-Lashley-Clinical-Features-of-FUS-Proteinopathies.txt)

## Visual hallucinations and apathy in a PSP-like case

September 2012. The clinical case describes apathy and visual hallucinations during the course of a syndrome with PSP-like features. The report includes later pathology and clinical context. It supplies a case history without a measured food exposure or a controlled symptom contrast. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8029232/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2012-Kasahata-Hallucinations-and-Apathy-in-a-PSP-like-Case.txt)

## Tremor phenotype and combined depression or apathy in Ghana and Italy

July 2014. Table 2 reports a tremor-dominant phenotype in 68 of 91 Ghanaian patients and 94 of 182 Italian patients. The combined depression or apathy row is 13 of 91 versus 30 of 182. The study examines clinical and levodopa-related motor differences and leaves the effects of particular foods and isolated apathy unresolved. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4163032/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2014-Cilia-Phenotype-in-Ghana-and-Italy.txt)
