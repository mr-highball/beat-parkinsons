## Oral bay leaf extract and movement in ethanol exposed rats

The first public EP2792364A1 document appeared October 22, 2014. Its experimental section describes an aqueous extract of Laurus nobilis leaves, spray dried and given orally before ethanol. Relative to ethanol controls the authors report increased open-field distance and duration of movement. These are overall locomotor measures during acute exposure to ethanol. They are different from gait speed after ground leaves in Parkinson disease. Companion national patent records refer to the same family. Publication of overlapping animal results as a journal article remains unverified. [Patent primary example and figures](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP2792364A1/en)
