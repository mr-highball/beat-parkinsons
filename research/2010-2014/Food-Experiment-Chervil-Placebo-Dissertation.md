## Dissertation reports chervil placebo identity

Christine Brock’s University of Westminster dissertation describes the earlier Wolfson–Hoffmann trial in Chapter 2 and cites an author’s 2008 personal communication identifying chervil (Anthriscus cerefolium) as the comparator. The dissertation is copyright 2012. The earlier trial article did not disclose composition according to the dissertation. This is an ingredient-identity lead, with no independent effect estimate or confirmed fresh leaf.
