# Curcumin formulation and rheumatoid arthritis pain

## BCM-95 curcumin and rheumatoid arthritis pain

Chandran and Goel first published on March 9, 2012. The single-blind randomized pilot assigned 45 people with active rheumatoid arthritis to BCM-95 curcuminoids, diclofenac or both for eight weeks. Thirty-eight completed the study. The primary outcome was DAS28 disease activity. Mean pain VAS in Table 5 changed from 68.57 to 27.50 in 14 curcumin completers, 77.25 to 34.29 in 12 combination completers, and 78.25 to 39.17 in 12 diclofenac completers. The reported pain tests compare each arm with its own baseline. The formulation, rheumatoid arthritis cohort and absence of an inactive placebo limit inference about ground turmeric and Parkinson pain.
