# Rosemary capsule comparators, 2010–2014

## Rosemary extract shared by both registered MsFLASH capsule arms

The registry identifies a factorial trial of omega-3 or placebo and separately assigned activity programs. Both capsule descriptions list rosemary extract together with lemon flavor and other excipients. Depressed mood is one secondary outcome. The trial does not assign rosemary separately and the capsule description supplies no dosage of rosemary. The 2013 omega-3 paper is the matching outcomes source. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01178892) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2010-msflash-reg-Clinical-Source-Extract.txt)

## Depressive scores in a trial with rosemary extract in both capsule arms

Cohen and colleagues randomized 355 women with vasomotor symptoms to omega-3 fish oil or placebo capsules, each containing rosemary extract and natural lemon oil. The PHQ-8 score changed by 0 points in the omega-3 arm and −0.8 in the comparator (p=.10). The trial estimates the omega-3 capsule contrast and leaves the contribution of the shared rosemary ingredient unidentified.
