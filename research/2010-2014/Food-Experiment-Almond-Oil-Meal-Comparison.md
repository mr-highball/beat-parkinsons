# Almond oil in a randomized meal comparison

## Oral oil, almonds, butter, flour and vehicle

In January 2011 Mori and colleagues reported a five-condition randomized, single-blind crossover in 14 adults with impaired glucose tolerance. Each breakfast included orange juice and farina. The assigned farina contained almond oil, whole almonds, almond butter, defatted almond flour or none of these. The products came from the same harvest. The methods specify dry roasting for the whole almonds and give no pressing method for the oil. [Original Methods and Table 2](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1743-7075-8-6)

Morning glucose incremental area under the curve was lower after the oil meal than after flour or vehicle, and post-lunch area was lower after whole almonds than after oil or butter. The measurements concern metabolic responses to different forms in a non-Parkinson cohort. The study measured neither vocal intensity nor facial expression.
