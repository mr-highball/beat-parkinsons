# Blackberry and voice outcomes

## Submitted blackberry leaf wording for the throat and vocal cords

The EFSA Panel on Dietetic Products, Nutrition and Allergies published its opinion on 30 June 2011. Claim ID 2127 identifies blackberry as Rubus fructicosus L. in the submission and proposes comfort of the throat, pharynx and vocal cords. The submitted conditions refer to dried leaves, including a cough-drop preparation. The panel did not find pertinent human data in support of the submitted wording. This was an evaluation of claim substantiation. It contains no direct fruit comparison or measurement of Parkinson-related voice volume.
