# Fish-oil capsules and depressive symptoms

## Marine oil capsules and depressive symptoms in older adults with mild cognitive impairment

Sinn and colleagues published online September 20, 2011. Fifty older adults with mild cognitive impairment entered a randomized comparison of EPA-rich oil, DHA-rich oil and safflower oil. Forty completed. Table 3 reports six-month contrasts versus safflower oil on the Geriatric Depression Scale of −1.40 points for DHA (95% CI −2.47 to −0.32) and −1.23 for EPA (−2.37 to −0.09). The investigators excluded one control participant whose score improved from 9 to 3. The EPA p value is .04 in Table 3 and .03 in Figure 2. The active interventions were concentrated fish oils with no poached trout arm. The trial concerns mild cognitive impairment, with no Parkinson subgroup.
