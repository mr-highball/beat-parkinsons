# Fennel seed clinical endpoint follow-up

## Oral fennel fruit extract and imposed bar posture in mice

Singh and colleagues tested a methanol extract of fennel fruits in haloperidol-treated mice, with six per arm. Table 3 puts day-seven imposed posture duration at 125.17 ± 2.44 seconds with the extract and haloperidol versus 185.83 ± 2.27 seconds with haloperidol alone, means ± SEM (reported p < .001). Fluoxetine was another active arm. The bar assay records how long the imposed posture is held. Spontaneous resting oscillations and dystonia were not scored, and the test used an extract.
