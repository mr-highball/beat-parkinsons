# Japanese dietary and botanical registrations, 2010–2014

## Omega-3-enriched formula and weight loss

UMIN000008057 was first disclosed June 6, 2012. This completed single-arm Parkinson’s study planned to enroll 25 participants and assess body weight and UPDRS after 12 weeks of an oral omega-3-enriched medical formula. The registry gives no outcomes and the intervention combines multiple nutrients. [UMIN trial record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000009489)

## Rikkunshito and appetite

UMIN000009626 was first disclosed April 1, 2013. It registers the randomized crossover comparing usual Parkinson’s medication with and without rikkunshito. The registry's results field remains unpublished. The investigators' 2017 paper explicitly identifies this registration, so the protocol and report represent one cohort. The tested formula included ginger alongside seven other ingredients. [UMIN trial record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000011294) · [2017 outcome note](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#rikkunshito-and-gastrointestinal-symptoms)

## Mashiningan and Daiokanzoto for constipation

UMIN000013879 was first disclosed June 1, 2014. A randomized, assessor-blinded crossover was planned for 50 people with Parkinson’s and constipation resistant to prior laxatives. Four weeks of each Kampo formula were to be compared on the Constipation Assessment Scale, with Bristol stool form and adverse effects as secondary measures. The registry marks the trial terminated and provides no outcomes. A separate Japanese 23-person clinical series used a different design and research group. [UMIN trial record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000016133) · [2016 series](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-East-Asia-Registry-Followup.md#mashiningan-constipation-series)
