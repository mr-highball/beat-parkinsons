# Nutmeg reports and tremor phenotyping

## California poison-center report and unresolved body access

2011. The later journal abstract retains the 119 California exposures and 86/33 intent counts from the earlier proceedings. The authors associate intentional exposure with more agitation and tachycardia. Tremor subtype and Parkinson diagnosis are unspecified at this access depth. The 2009 meeting table and a later Illinois paper give different under-13 counts for the California series. The journal body remains an open route for the case definitions, ages and signs. ## Dutch poison-center abstract and exposure forms

2011. Van Riel and colleagues described 48 exposures in 37 people. Ground powder predominated, there were also whole-seed and coexposure cases, and three reports involved unexpectedly excess spice in food. The complete original abstract characterizes the overall course and leaves the individual tremor counts and posture unspecified. This is the Dutch series shown in the conference collection; its DOI identifies the collection and 133 the member abstract.
