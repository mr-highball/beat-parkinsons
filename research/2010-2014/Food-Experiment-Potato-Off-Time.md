# Potato intake and Parkinson motor fluctuations

## Potato menu options and ordinal waking day OFF categories

January 2011 issue. The four-week nonrandom comparison had 12 participants in the plant-food menu and 13 in the usual omnivorous menu, with protein redistributed in both. Both groups could eat potatoes. Table 1 offers bread or potatoes at lunch and dinner in the usual menu, while Table 3 lists baked or boiled potatoes three to four times weekly in the plant menu. Tables 5 and 6 report waking-day OFF categories none, 1–25%, and 26–50%. At baseline counts were 1, 10, and 1 versus 6, 6, and 1 (Fisher p = .091). At four weeks they were 2, 10, and 0 versus 6, 5, and 2 (Fisher p = .054). These are cross-sectional distributions at each visit. Participants chose their menu, and the authors used a .01 threshold. The report does not identify which patients chose baked rather than boiled potatoes, quantify intake of each preparation, or give daily hours OFF by ingredient.
