# Food and oral-care leads in the 2011 NHO program

The 65th meeting was held October 7–8, 2011. The organizer makes the schedules and presentation titles public. Its separate abstract book was issued in print and the sales page is closed. The National Diet Library identifies the printed series as Z19-2753. [Official program](https://www.convention-w.jp/nms65/prog.html) · [Abstract-book notice](https://www.convention-w.jp/nms65/abst.html) · [Print catalog](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I000008338786)

## Olive oil and sodium bicarbonate in oral care

The program lists 菅 清美 of NHO Matsue Medical Center as the presenter of P4-38-3, on oral care using 2% sodium bicarbonate solution and olive oil for pronounced dry mouth in a patient with Parkinson. The title gives no oil grade, application protocol, comparator, dry-mouth measurement or swallowing result. The adjacent P4-38-4 title names tea tree oil mixed with olive oil for oral care in patients unable to take food orally, with diagnoses unspecified. [Day two poster program, page 49](https://www.convention-w.jp/nms65/img/pro006.pdf#page=49)

## Meal exposure and plasma levodopa

Risa Mizuta of NHO Tokushima presented O-70-9 on meals and plasma levodopa in advanced Parkinson. The title names no food or result. Her later report at the same hospital describes meal-timed measurements in 22 patients. Participant overlap with the 2011 presentation remains unknown. [Day two oral program, page 21](https://www.convention-w.jp/nms65/img/pro007.pdf#page=21) · [2012 primary abstract summary](Food-Experiment-NHO-Early.md#meal-protein-and-levodopa-in-advanced-parkinson-disease)
