# Soybean oil and measured motor outcomes

## Registered oral comparator and acoustic tremor endpoints

NCT01864525 first posted May 29, 2013. The registered essential-voice-tremor crossover compared octanoic acid with a matched inactive capsule and prespecified measures of acoustic amplitude and frequency modulation and listener ratings. The public record does not identify the oil. The 2018 results and same-study paper report the same values. The paper identifies the placebo as soybean oil. ## Soybean oil substitution and stepping recovery after spinal cord injury

Figueroa and colleagues assigned female rats to isocaloric feed with approximately 6% dry-weight soybean oil or menhaden oil before sham surgery or spinal cord injury. The injured soybean-oil comparator group reached a plateau with uncoordinated plantar stepping. On the transformed Basso–Beattie–Bresnahan score at one week, the soybean-oil group averaged 1.53 versus 5.47 for menhaden oil. At eight weeks the means were 8.20 and 11.88, respectively. Figure 3 measures ordinal stepping and coordination, with no stride distance or Parkinson model. Oil processing is unspecified.
