# Chickpea and recommended food score

## 2011 Korean recommended food score legume items

Kim et al., first online 2011-06-29, DOI 10.1038/ejcn.2011.120, PMID 21712839, Table 1 of the author-posted primary article: the Korean 47-point recommended food score has four legume items: soybeans/cooked soybeans, miso soup/soybean paste, tofu and soy milk. Each recommended food is scored for at least weekly consumption. This is an instrument development and oxidative-stress study, not a Parkinson or sleep intervention. Lee et al. 2023 cite this paper when describing the Korean score. A news report describing the Lee cohort lists the same four items; without the Lee full questionnaire, the exact application cannot be checked directly. Chickpeas or canning do not appear in Kim Table 1 legume items.
