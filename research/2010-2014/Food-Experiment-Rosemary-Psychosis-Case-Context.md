# Rosemary-containing mixtures and psychosis: 2011 conference reports

## A mixed-product case with visual hallucinations

Tomruk and colleagues described a 41-year-old woman who developed visual and auditory hallucinations and delusions after taking a rosemary-containing weight-loss mixture with other botanicals. Her symptoms improved after antipsychotic treatment. The conference report does not identify a Parkinson diagnosis, fresh culinary rosemary, a hallucination frequency or an ingredient-specific effect. A separate abstract by the same group describes two women and possible participant overlap remains unresolved.
