# Bell pepper source follow-through

## Dihydrocapsiate symptom checklists in a four-week trial

Galgani and Ravussin first published this distinct parallel trial on September 8, 2010, following their 2009 acute crossover. Seventy-eight healthy participants were allocated to placebo, 3 mg/day or 9 mg/day of dihydrocapsiate for 28 days. The investigators collected symptom checklists before and after the laboratory assessments on days 1 and 28, on interim visits days 8 and 15, and asked about symptoms 72 hours after the last test. Day 1 postdose dry mouth was reported by 32%, 66% and 44% in placebo, 3 mg and 9 mg groups (p=.04). Predose day 28 leg cramps occurred in 12% of the 9 mg group and in no participants in the other arms (p=.04). No separate sleepy, alert or tired estimates are published. ## Pepper and wolfberry patent for drowsiness

The Korean patent application published March 27, 2012 describes a combination of pepper powder and wolfberry intended to reduce drowsiness and improve concentration. Bell pepper is among the possible Capsicum sources, and the claims describe formulations by capsaicinoid content. The public document does not supply a verifiable clinical outcome comparison or a grilled bell pepper intervention in people with Parkinson disease. The assertion concerns a mixed powdered product. [Original Korean patent as indexed](https://patents.google.com/patent/KR20120029542A/en)

## Japanese safety-trial records for dihydrocapsiate

The public 2012 EFSA opinion describes two 2009 Soiken Clinic safety reports by Y. Kajimoto. A four-week randomized double-blind comparison allocated 70 healthy men to a beverage with 3 mg or 9 mg dihydrocapsiate daily or placebo (23, 23 and 24). A symptom questionnaire was collected on the first and last days. The publicly available opinion describes overall tolerability and does not list a sleepiness item or between-group estimate. The original safety reports were not available in the reviewed public sources. [Official EFSA opinion](https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.2903/j.efsa.2012.2812)
