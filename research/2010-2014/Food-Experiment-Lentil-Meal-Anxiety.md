# Lentil preparation and the registered crossover

## Canned lentil meals and appetite in the registered crossover

Anguah and colleagues first published October 8, 2014. Twelve healthy adults completed six meal and enzyme assignments. Meals used canned lentils, served whole or blended, with a no-lentil comparison. The original Methods specify 42 g of canned lentils per 200 kcal and at least 84 g per lentil breakfast. This article reports appetite and glycemic comparisons, and the 2017 conference abstract separately reports mood from the same trial. ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT02110511 on April 10, 2014.
