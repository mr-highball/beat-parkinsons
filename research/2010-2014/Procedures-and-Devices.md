# Procedures and Devices

### 2010 — Pallidal versus subthalamic stimulation

A randomized trial of 299 people found similar motor improvement at 24 months for the two stimulation targets. Medication reduction, depression and selected cognitive measures differed, making target and outcome selection important. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20519680/)

### 2013 — Deep brain stimulation earlier in motor complications

EARLYSTIM randomized 251 selected participants with early motor complications. Stimulation plus medication improved quality of life at two years compared with medication alone. Procedure or device-related serious events occurred. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23406026/)

### 2013 — Double-blind trial of intestinal levodopa gel

A double-dummy trial found about 1.9 additional hours of daily off-time reduction with intestinal gel compared with immediate-release oral treatment over 12 weeks. Tube and procedure-related events were common. First published online in December 2013. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24361112/)
