# Terpene and lovage primary sources, 2010-2014

## Carvacrol open-arm percentages in the companion thesis

Melo's institutional dissertation reports mean open-arm time percentages of 23.61 in the control group and 39.09, 49.14 and 44.00 across the three oral isolated-carvacrol groups in Figures 7–10 and accompanying text. For the middle group the adjacent text gives both n=9 and n=10. The overlap in investigator, dose and outcomes makes the connection to the 2009 journal experiment plausible. A matched cohort has yet to be established. This is a mouse behavior result rather than a clinical food exposure. [Original source](https://repositorio.ufc.br/handle/riufc/2358) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2010-melo-thesis-Factual-Extract.txt)

## Oral isolated beta-caryophyllene in mouse pain assays

Klauke and colleagues administered purified beta-caryophyllene in olive oil. The five mg/kg dose reduced late formalin responses, while early responses were inconclusive and the lower dose did not clearly improve late phase. The Results and Figure 1 caption print different p-value thresholds. After nerve injury, repeated oral doses reduced mechanical hypersensitivity, with thermal latency improved at one dose only. Tail-flick testing did not find a significant effect. The procedures tested evoked responses in mice and did not include cinnamon or pepper consumption. [Original source](https://www.for926.uni-bonn.de/kunden/for926/content/e229/e1348/e1361/2013_Klauke_EurNeuropsychopharmacol.pdf) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2013-klauke-Factual-Extract.txt)

## Carvacryl acetate and the conflicting marble-burying report

Pires and colleagues injected synthetic carvacryl acetate in mice. The Results report greater open-arm behavior and 47%, 52%, 55% and 74% fewer marbles buried across the reported doses versus vehicle. The abstract says marble burying increased. The original figure caption is accessible but the bar graphic remained unavailable. The conflict applies to the marble assay. These exposures and proxy behaviors differ from eating dried thyme or oregano and clinical anxiety. [Original source](https://www.academia.edu/28869971/Anxiolytic_like_effects_of_carvacryl_acetate_a_derivative_of_carvacrol_in_mice) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2013-pires-Factual-Extract.txt)

## Injected isolated beta-caryophyllene in mouse behavioral assays

Bahi and colleagues compared injected isolated beta-caryophyllene in an olive-oil vehicle with the corresponding vehicle control. Open-arm time increased (p=.045), and forced-swim and tail-suspension immobility decreased (p=.021 and p=.015). These are acute mouse behaviors and reflect an isolated constituent. The study supplies no rosemary, cinnamon or oregano ingestion comparison and no Parkinson clinical result. [Original source](https://accurateclinic.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Caryophyllene-a-CB2-receptor-agonist-produces-multiple-behavioral-changes-relevant-to-anxiety-and-depression-in-mice-20214.pdf) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2014-bahi-Factual-Extract.txt)

## Isolated alpha-phellandrene in a nerve injury model

Piccinelli and colleagues studied isolated alpha-phellandrene or limonene and Schinus fruit essential oil in rats with nerve injury. The journal abstract reports reduced mechanical and cold pain-like responses. It also states forced-swim immobility increased while describing an antidepressant-like effect. The overlapping 2013 meeting abstract repeats the wording. The later author thesis says immobility was inhibited. The journal figure remains unavailable. The reported preparations did not include ginger. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24661285/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2014-piccinelli-Factual-Extract.txt)

## Single-arm Canephron urinary symptom trial registration

ClinicalTrials.gov identifies this as a 125-participant, open-label, single-arm study of oral Canephron N for uncomplicated urinary tract infection. Results first posted October 13, 2014. The official API reports 0 of 125 with investigator-attributed adverse drug reactions and marks secondary urinary outcomes as NOT_POSTED. The separate Ivanov article supplies urgency severity results. Its preparation uses lovage root with other botanicals.
