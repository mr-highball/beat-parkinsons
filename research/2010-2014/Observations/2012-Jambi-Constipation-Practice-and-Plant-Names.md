# Jambi constipation account and botanical names

The [2012 provincial field report excerpt](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2012-RISTOJA-Jambi-Constipation-Field-Excerpt.txt) documents community-based ethnomedicine in Jambi. In printed page 25, row 31, difficulty defecating is the intended indication for a leaf preparation under the names batang kendung and katumeh. The scan supplies no Latin names or individual outcomes. Its executive summary covers five ethnic groups and does not identify which group reported the pictured table entry.

The record can be found through the symptom and place. Its local plant names provide leads to compare against original plant inventories. The [later literature review](../../2025-2029/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2026-Jambi-Ginger-and-Turmeric-Cultural-Review.txt) concerns blood-pressure uses and cannot identify the species in the constipation row.
