# Food flavors and odors in reinforcement and detection tasks

The banana pellet reports describe a grain-based flavored reward in nonhuman primates. The orange conditioning reports use a scent to reinstate lever responding in rats. The orange reports share the test statistics, and animal overlap in the banana study family remains open. The preparation, route and experimental assignment determine which comparisons the reports can inform. The 2012 proceedings distinguish clove as a test odor from strawberry flavor as a shared reward. The later odor paper omits clove in its stated methods and results. The later reinforcement paper describes the milkshake recipe and separates reward-associated learning from motivation.
