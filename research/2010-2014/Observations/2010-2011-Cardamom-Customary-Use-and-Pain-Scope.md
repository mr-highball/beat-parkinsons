# Cardamom customary use and pain outcomes

The West Yorkshire and Kani field reports record different preparations and pain complaints. The first describes chewing seeds for toothache. The second describes swallowing fruit powder for headache or rheumatism and records a separate mixed preparation. The numbers refer to people reporting practices, with no measured symptom response. [West Yorkshire record](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2010-Pieroni-West-Yorkshire-Cardamom-Toothache.txt) · [Kani record](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2011-Ayyanar-Kani-Cardamom-Powder-Pain.txt)

Compared with the general 1812 Malabar account, these interviews identify preparation and indication more precisely. The 2015 original crude-extract assays and 2019 dietary-powder edema abstract answer separate questions. They provide neither an isolated human Parkinson pain effect nor a contradiction of a specific community report.
