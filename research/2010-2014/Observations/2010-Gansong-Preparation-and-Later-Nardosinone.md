# The Gansong preparation and the later nardosinone model

The Hunan standard distinguishes dried root and rhizome, decoction and external use. Its references to fullness, digestive symptoms and toothache concern customary indications. The original text supplies no measured symptom change. [2010 Gansong monograph](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2010-Hunan-Gansong-Root-Monograph.txt)

The earlier-period field report on Central Italy identifies a different oral preparation and separately describes topical fig latex. Both records show why the plant part, route and indication belong in the evidence record. [2005 Central Italy fig record](../../2005-2009/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2005-Guarrera-Central-Italy-Fig-Fruit-and-Latex.txt)

In 2026 a rat study tested an isolated Gansong constituent for levodopa-induced dyskinesia. Sunflower oil was the injection vehicle for model preparation. The customary use, isolated drug exposure and matched vehicle each have a different role in the source record.
