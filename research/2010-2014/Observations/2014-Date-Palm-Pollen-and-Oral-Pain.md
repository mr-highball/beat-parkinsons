# Date palm pollen and oral pain

The [2014 pilot](../Source-Text/2014-PMID25148841-Date-Palm-Pollen-and-Mouth-Pain.txt) compares a pollen suspension with standard care during cancer treatment. Mean day-29 oral pain was 0 versus 3.0. The sequential groups received different cancer regimens, and whole-fruit exposure was outside the study. Compare the [2015–2019 date pain review](../../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Date-Pain-Followup.md) and the [later date fruit case series](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27186159-Date-Fruit-and-Fructose-Intolerance.txt) by plant part and population. The [2026 animal pollen study](../../2025-2029/Source-Text/2026-PMID42635914-Date-Palm-Pollen-and-Colitis-Endpoints.txt) gives distinct colitis endpoints and does not replicate the oral pain finding.
