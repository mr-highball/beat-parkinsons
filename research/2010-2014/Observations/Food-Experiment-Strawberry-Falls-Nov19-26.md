# Strawberry as a modeled dietary source

The 2012 California model relates dietary reports and measured residue categories to estimated contaminant intake. It adds genuine fruit context to the period's Parkinson search returns. The modeled ranking concerns exposure, and the article does not test a clinical falls outcome. [Contaminant analysis](../Source-Text/2012-PMID23140444-Vogt-Strawberry-Contaminant-Estimates.txt)
