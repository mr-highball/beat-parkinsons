# Lewy body visual symptom measures in the 2012 source group

The [DLB imaging study](../Source-Text/PMC3365275.txt) used a caregiver rating that combines visual hallucination frequency and severity. The rating correlated with lower V4 perfusion. The same rating showed no significant association with BOLD responses to the visual tasks. The publisher supplement confirms the V4 correlation figure and the separate between-group motion-task response table. The earlier [2006 before-and-after SPECT comparison](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-PMID16567718-Donepezil-and-Visual-Hallucination-Perfusion.txt) also reports a visual symptom rating. It had no placebo group, and the apparent increase in occipital perfusion did not reach significance after imaging correction. Both studies retain useful visual phenotype measurements.

The [2012 exploratory donepezil trial](../Source-Text/PMC3504981.txt) distinguishes baseline people with visual symptoms, the general hallucination item, and the NPI-2 composite of hallucinations and cognitive fluctuation. Mean NPI-2 change favored the active treatment in the exploratory trial. The [confirmatory trial](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC4338565.txt) and [postmarketing trial](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC11578010.txt) did not reproduce a significant placebo-adjusted composite change. These trials had separate enrollment, and the postmarketing trial chose a different primary measure. The [open-label extension](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC4338564.txt) follows the phase III cohort after the randomized comparison. The confirmatory report also describes visual hallucination among severe adverse events. [Phase III registration](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT01278407.txt) · [Phase IV registration](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT02345213.txt).

Two clinical histories show why diagnosis and symptom measurement matter. An [illustrative aging-study history](../Source-Text/PMC3656587.txt) describes formed visual experiences and later motor features. A [Whipple disease case](../Source-Text/PMC3500470.txt) pairs visual hallucinations with cognitive and motor decline in a different disease context confirmed at autopsy. Neither provides a comparison of visual event frequency after eating dried figs.

The [AES issue record](../Source-Text/PMC3280453.txt) confirms a December 2011 meeting and a 2012 supplement. Its full member content remains open. The [source inventory](../../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/fig-priority-six-2012-nov19-100/source-receipts.tsv) distinguishes that metadata access from complete main-body reading.
