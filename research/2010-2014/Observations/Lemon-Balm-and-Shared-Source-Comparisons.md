- [Blinded Melissa oil and donepezil agitation comparison](../Source-Text/2011-PMID21335973-Burns-Melissa-Oil-Donepezil-Agitation.txt)

Compared with the earlier topical trial, the 2011 study used a different primary agitation scale, a three-arm design and nose clips for outcome raters. The observed differences cannot resolve the effect of a brewed preparation.
