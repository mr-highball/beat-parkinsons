# Spice receptor activation and arthritis endpoint fit

The [2009 gastroparesis citation review](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID19115465-Gastroparesis-Ginger-Citation-Scope.txt) traced ginger to distinct gastric-emptying and gastric electrical measurements. The [2010 arthritis study](../Source-Text/2010-Ramadan-Turmeric-Ginger-Rhizome-Arthritis.txt) adds different rhizome and animal-model evidence. Reported recovery is based on paw swelling. Neither gastric measures nor paw swelling establish a Parkinson pain result.

The [2010 receptor study](../Source-Text/2010-PMID20957597-Mueller-Spice-PPARalpha-Assay.txt) reports low to moderate activity for a saffron extract. The [2011 narrative review](../Source-Text/2011-Preventive-Medicine-Chronic-Inflammation-Spice-Scopes.txt) repeats the same underlying result. It broadens the mechanistic leads while leaving the human fatigue outcome untested.
