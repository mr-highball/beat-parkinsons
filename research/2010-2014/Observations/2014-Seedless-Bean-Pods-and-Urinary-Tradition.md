# Seedless bean pods in the European traditional-use assessment

The final 2014 assessment preserves an earlier European tradition of an infusion prepared from seedless green bean pods. The intended effect was greater urine volume for urinary tract flushing. The report records the claim and the preparation history. Its efficacy conclusion rests on traditional use. The earlier 1608 attribution was read through the assessment, and the historical edition remains a separate retrieval task. [Dated practice record](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2014-EMA-Seedless-Green-Bean-Pod-Urinary-Use.txt)

Later dietary studies used ordinary food categories and measured frailty or fatigue components. The separate Parkinson pilot offered whole green beans among several foods and did not give a bean-specific symptom result.
