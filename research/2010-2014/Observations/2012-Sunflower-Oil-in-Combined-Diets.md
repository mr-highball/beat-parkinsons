# Sunflower oil in combined diets

The [2012 dietary mouse experiment](../Source-Text/2012-PMID22662127-Combined-Oil-Diet-and-Mouse-Memory.txt) included high-oleic sunflower oil in both comparison diets. Its reported memory results are outcomes of the combined diet. The [2022 review](../../2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-PMID36117650-Omega3-Depression-Review-Combined-Oil-Diet.txt) later cited the experiment in its discussion of omega-3 fatty acids. The original adds no stand-alone sunflower oil estimate for Parkinson depression or anxiety.
