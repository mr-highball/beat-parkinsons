# Food texture and food attribution in choking records

The 2010 original choking series identifies individual foods and underlying diagnoses in separate summaries. It cannot identify which diagnosis corresponds to the shiitake event. Subsequent food-texture work describes modified preparations under laboratory conditions. It does not supply the missing attribution or a comparison in people with Parkinson disease.

This distinction lets the catalog retain a clinically relevant safety observation while leaving the preparation-specific symptom hypothesis open. The official Japanese abstract supplies the published counts and the boundaries of the sample.
