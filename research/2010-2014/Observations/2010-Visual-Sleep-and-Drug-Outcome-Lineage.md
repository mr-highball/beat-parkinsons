# 2010 visual symptoms, sleep ratings and drug outcome lineage

The [2008 side-of-onset cohort](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/PMC2630478.txt) compared 14 left-onset and 17 right-onset Parkinson participants with 17 controls who had chronic health conditions. It used patient-reported nocturnal hallucination frequency. In the [2010 review](../Source-Text/PMC2882524.txt), Figure 12 reversed the scale for display and reused those participants. The [six-year cohort](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID15642908-Hallucinations-and-Sleep-Followup.txt) separates a concurrent dream association from prediction of later hallucinations.

[RECOVER](../Source-Text/PMC3072524.txt) reports an individual sleep-scale item for distressing hallucinations, a broader perception item and a visual adverse-event withdrawal. The [registry results extract](../Source-Text/2010-RECOVER-Registry-Visual-Adverse-Events.txt) identifies one serious visual event in the randomized phase and 22 events among 11 affected participants for the general hallucination term in the open-label extension. The [post hoc analysis](../Source-Text/2011-PMID21790503-RECOVER-Post-Hoc-Sleep-Items.txt) reuses the randomized cohort. These measurements and populations must be kept separate.

The [temporal pathology study](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2002-PMID11844739-Formed-Visual-Hallucinations-and-Temporal-Pathology.txt) and [perfusion comparison](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID16344511-Visual-Hallucinations-and-Perfusion.txt) associate regional findings with different visual phenotypes. The [2010 tissue review](../Source-Text/PMC2892607.txt) also records null results for broader Lewy body counts. The [2008 plaque study](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2008-PMID18185940-Parkinson-Plaques-and-Lewy-Body-Load.txt) supplied partial earlier observations for 23 of 56 Parkinson cases in [Compta 2011](../Source-Text/PMC4194668.txt).

The [inherited dystonia-parkinsonism series](../Source-Text/PMC3657696.txt) and [adult epilepsy case series](../Source-Text/PMC3214946.txt) describe symptoms in other disorders. The [2010 toxicology report](../Source-Text/PMC3550295.txt) discusses several chemical syndromes with neurologic signs. The [2010 neuropathology proceedings](../Source-Text/PMC8094762.txt) remain open at issue-metadata depth. For claim 0175, these sources clarify symptom definitions and source lineage. The dried-fig exposure remains unresolved.
