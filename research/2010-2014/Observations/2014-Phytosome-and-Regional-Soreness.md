# A curcumin phytosome and regional soreness

The [2014 pilot](../Source-Text/2014-PMID24982601-Phytosome-Curcumin-and-Regional-Soreness.txt) observed lower pain in two thigh regions and an inconclusive total pain score at 48 hours. The [2015 crossover](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-PMID25921600-Curcumin-Crossover-and-No-Clear-Soreness-Effect.txt) measured arm soreness with different exercise and a different formulation. Its measured soreness did not show a clear between-condition interaction. Both studies leave ground turmeric and clinical Parkinson pain unresolved.
