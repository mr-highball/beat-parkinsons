# Date analgesia, preparation and observation

The preceding period's mouse study fed a diet containing whole dried Zahedi dates and measured evoked thermal latency. No clear date-versus-chow difference was established. This period's Spanish synthesis documents a different kind of observation. Its historical and ethnobotanical records describe intended fruit analgesia, including postpartum pain, with the drying procedure and patient outcome unspecified. The late-pregnancy cohort recorded labor progression and induction without a reported independent pain measure.
