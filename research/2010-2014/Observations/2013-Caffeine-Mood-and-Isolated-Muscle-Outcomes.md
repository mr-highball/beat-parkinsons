# Caffeine, perceived fatigue and isolated muscle outcomes

The previous period's older-adult crossover reported lower exertion and longer endurance after caffeine following 48 hours of abstinence. The corresponding outcomes depend on preparation and regimen. In this period's 12-person study, a distinct mood fatigue subscale remained inconclusive while vigor improved. A later 19-person study gives timed function and readiness-to-invest-effort results. The participants may overlap and the functional measurements do not establish a fatigue trajectory. Isolated mouse-muscle results vary with activation and recovery. The 2003 report had inconclusive recovery-force and power effects under the lower caffeine condition. The 2013 report found lower endurance under maximal activation and longer endurance under submaximal activation. These sources preserve both signs without transferring them to habitual brewed coffee or subjective Parkinson fatigue.
