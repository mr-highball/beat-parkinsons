# Isolated mangiferin and mouse behavior

The [2014 Jangra author abstract](../Source-Text/2014-PMID25064341-Mangiferin-and-Mouse-Affect-Tests.txt) reports more sucrose preference and social interaction and less immobility following isolated mangiferin in a mouse inflammation model. The publication connects the compound to mango plant material. Stem and bark chemistry does not establish a clinical effect from eating dried mango flesh.

The [2017 observations](../../2015-2019/Observations/2017-Mangiferin-and-Sucrose-Direction.md) track the independent chronic stress experiment and a later discrepancy in the review literature.
