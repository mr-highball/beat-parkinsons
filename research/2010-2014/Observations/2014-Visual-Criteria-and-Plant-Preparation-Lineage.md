# 2014 visual criteria and plant preparation lineage

The [GBA Parkinson cohort](../Source-Text/PMC4221612.txt) records time to a sustained clinical thought-disorder threshold. The [quantitative EEG cohort](../Source-Text/PMC4270449.txt) records visual symptom presence while studying diagnostic conversion. The [original stimulation case](../Source-Text/2014-PMID24741031-Evoked-Visual-Scenes.txt) records visual scenes elicited in one person undergoing epilepsy evaluation. The methods distinguish persistence, presence and experimental elicitation. The [2015 endpoint comparisons](../../2015-2019/Observations/2015-Visual-Event-Definitions-and-Botanical-Preparations.md) add withdrawal counts and an ambiguous-image task.

The [FDA approval package](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-FDA-NDA203312-Levodopa-Phase-and-Hallucination-Events.txt) separates ADVANCE-PD conversion from randomized maintenance. The source uses broader hallucination coding for conversion than the 2015 paper's visual term. In maintenance, the label's combined report and a separate visual withdrawal narrative concern the same randomized family. ASCEND-PD has its own conversion and crossover stages.

The [Ayurvedic practice record](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2014-Ayurvedic-Botanical-Use-in-Parkinson-Research.txt) attributes traditional use as described in a research publication. The [fly study](../Source-Text/PMC4310928.txt) shows different directions by plant and genotype on climbing. The [1995 open HP-200 study](../../1995-1999/Source-Text/1995-PMID9395621-HP200-Mucuna-Open-Study.txt), [2004 crossover](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2004-PMID15548480-Mucuna-Crossover-Extract.txt) and [2018 tolerability study](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID29352722-Mucuna-Chronic-Crossover-Extract.txt) concern distinct preparations or measurements. The 2017 acute and 2018 chronic studies share a registration and must retain their cohort and phase boundaries.

In the Hoasca project, [1994 platelet binding](../../1990-1994/Source-Text/1994-PMID7892432-Hoasca-Platelet-Transporter.txt), [1996 psychological assessment](../../1995-1999/Source-Text/1996-PMID8596116-Hoasca-Ceremonial-Cohort.txt) and [1999 pharmacokinetics](../../1995-1999/Source-Text/1999-PMID10404423-Hoasca-Ceremonial-Pharmacokinetics.txt) describe different outputs. The [2005 paper](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-PMID16149329-Hoasca-Metabolizer-Reanalysis.txt) expressly reuses the 1999 profiles. Some other subject overlap remains unresolved. These comparisons supply no measured intake of dried figs.
