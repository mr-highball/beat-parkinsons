# Experimental formulations and clinical endpoints

The [2008 saffron assays](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2008-PMID18496783-Saffron-Quality-Market-Samples-and-Receptor-Binding.txt) identify variation in market samples. The [2013 crocin experiment](../Source-Text/2013-PMID24096536-Crocins-and-Experimental-Behavioral-Outcomes.txt) addresses different questions about isolated constituents and behavior. Neither supplies a human food comparison.

The [2014 arthritis abstract](../Source-Text/2014-PMID25400173-Lipid-Curcumin-and-Arthritis-Pain-Abstract.txt) reports a favorable related result for formulated curcumin. The [2016 full original](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27260322-Oral-and-Topical-Curcumin-Osteoarthritis-Pain.txt) distinguishes a favorable topical result and an inconclusive oral pain comparison. The models and formulations differ from dietary ground turmeric and Parkinson pain.
