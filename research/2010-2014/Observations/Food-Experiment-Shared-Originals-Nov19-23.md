# Food exposures and procedural uses in gastrointestinal abstracts

The 2013 poster proceedings report faster emptying for intragastric soy milk than cow milk in unexplained dyspepsia, with no reported difference in symptom scores. The orange–lemon imaging series measures a transient change in duct diameter. Its preceding 2012 title points to a possible earlier report of the same series. The cinnamon and benzoate exclusion series follows an elemental diet and involves childhood orofacial disease.

The 2014 full paper classifies the 404 endoscopies as a retrospective series. Intravenous soybean oil and egg lecithin appear as anesthetic excipients, while sensitization measures describe the patient group. Compared with the preceding period's isolated rubiscolin study, these examples add human measurements in gastrointestinal and procedure contexts. The route, preparation and population still govern which Parkinson questions each observation can inform.
