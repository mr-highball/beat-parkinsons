# Visual symptom reports, diagnosis and images in the 2011 source group

The [DLB onset and SPECT study](../Source-Text/PMC3199887.txt) records acute and chronic presentation and the distribution of initial complaints. A [longitudinal autonomic case](../Source-Text/PMC4912215.txt) instead follows visual symptoms from onset to reported worsening. The [earlier interview study](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-PMID16473980-Visual-Hallucination-Relative-Frequency-Interview.txt) directly asked selected participants about the frequency and duration of recurring visual hallucinations. These approaches measure complaint mix, a single clinical trajectory and recalled episode characteristics.

Two reports illustrate uncertainty in patient stratification. The [amyloid and dopamine-terminal PET comparison](../Source-Text/PMC3102241.txt) found clinical and scan classifications differed in about a third of the sample. The [positive dopamine-transporter scan cases](../Source-Text/PMC3235939.txt) describe visual hallucinations and brief visual misperceptions under an Alzheimer-type presentation. The [postmortem Parkinson series](../Source-Text/PMC4194668.txt) studied dementia and documented that 23 of its 56 cases had appeared in an earlier analysis.

The [Parkinson longitudinal PET cohort](../Source-Text/PMC7486964.txt) measured dementia conversion. Earlier [task-related fMRI](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID19755518-Image-Recognition-and-Visual-Hallucination-Groups.txt), [longitudinal MRI](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID19965847-Clinical-Visual-Hallucination-Groups-and-Atrophy.txt) and [visual and auditory response timing](../Source-Text/2010-PMID20063433-Visual-and-Auditory-Evoked-Response-Comparison.txt) used hallucination status to group participants and measured distinct outcomes.

The [later DLB reanalysis](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-PMID26541688-Lewy-Body-Cortical-Excitability-and-Visual-Response.txt) explicitly selected participants with data from both the [2011 stimulation report](../Source-Text/2011-PMID22016436-Lewy-Body-Visual-Hallucinations-and-Cortical-Stimulation.txt) and the [2012 MRI report](../Source-Text/PMC3365275.txt). The overlapping subset links stimulation thresholds to checkerboard BOLD response. The reanalysis contributes a different comparison within shared data.

The [long-term Gaucher-associated case](../Source-Text/PMC3509857.txt) records benign visual symptoms during a course with multiple treatment changes. In the [primate comparison](../Source-Text/PMC6703214.txt), a compound changed the disabling subset of observed psychosis-like behavior. Both records leave the effect of eating dried figs on Parkinson visual episode frequency unresolved.
