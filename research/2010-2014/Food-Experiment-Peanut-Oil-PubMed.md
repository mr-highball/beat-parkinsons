# Peanut oil in a human acute pain comparison

## Peanut oil applied to tape and acute skin pain

Van Schaik and Rövekamp compared five applied solvents with a dry tape condition in adult volunteers using a double-blinded design and a visual analogue pain scale. Peanut oil was one of the solvents. The abstract reports an overall p value of .39 for the VAS comparison and gives no peanut-specific estimate or processing grade. This is a local skin exposure and acute procedural pain. The 2001 study that preceded it also used arachis oil and focused on tape removal speed, without a patient-reported pain result in its abstract.
