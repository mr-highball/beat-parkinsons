# Macadamia oil and mood outcomes

## Macadamia oil in the BRAVO comparison beverage

The July 17, 2013 registration describes a matched beverage containing macadamia nut oil as the comparison for an omega-3 beverage. The endpoints include negative affect and symptom instruments in adults at suicide risk. No oil-free comparison or specified cold-pressed grade appears in the registration. The record shows Terminated and no posted results. A 2016 design paper describes the same trial, so it does not supply an independent results cohort.
