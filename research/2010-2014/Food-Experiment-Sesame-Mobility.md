# Sesame Mobility

## Oral sesame seeds and timed mobility in knee osteoarthritis

Eftekhar Sadat and colleagues compared standard care alone with standard care plus oral sesame seeds at 40 g daily for two months in 50 patients with knee osteoarthritis, 25 per group. Timed Up and Go scores fell within each group (both p=.001). The reported post-treatment comparison was p=.002. The abstract omits group-specific times and the magnitude of the between-group difference. This test combines rising, walking, turning and sitting. The exposure was sesame seed and the clinical setting was knee osteoarthritis. Effect estimates and full methods remain open.
