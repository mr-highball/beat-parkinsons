# Grape-derived interventions and sleep, 2010–2014

## Registered grape polyphenol study in obstructive sleep apnea

NCT01977924 first posted 7 November 2013. The comparison assigns grape-juice polyphenols or placebo in severe obstructive sleep apnea, with vascular function as the primary outcome. ClinicalTrials.gov currently displays unknown recruitment status and no posted results. The primary publication separately reports measured outcomes from the named trial.
