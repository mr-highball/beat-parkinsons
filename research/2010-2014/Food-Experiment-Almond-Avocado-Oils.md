# Almond oil in a Parkinson massage patent

## Sweet almond oil in a proposed massage formula

The original December 25, 2013 Chinese filing CN103463243A lists sweet almond oil as the carrier for rosemary, marjoram, nutmeg, geranium and basil essential oils. Some sections of the machine translation misleadingly render the carrier as *Semen pruni armeniacae*. The Chinese abstract, example and claim identify sweet almond oil. The patent describes a topical formulation and names Parkinson symptoms in its rationale. It gives no enrolled patients, control group or measured outcomes. The citing 2024 application CN119185479A reports rotarod and gait tests after inhalation of a different seven-oil mixture in mice, without almond oil.
