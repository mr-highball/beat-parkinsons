# Green Tea Nasal Pathology

## Short term green tea extract and nasal pathology in mice

Chan and colleagues first published online September 30, 2010. Mice received water or concentrated standardized green tea extract by gavage over 14 weeks. At the 250 and 500 mg/kg dose levels, olfactory epithelial atrophy was 4/10 and 4/10 in male mice versus 0/10 vehicle, and olfactory nerve atrophy was 5/10 and 7/10 versus 0/10. The report calls both differences significant at these doses. The extract contained 48.4% EGCG by weight. Histology among animals after concentrated extract gives no odor threshold or identification measurement and no clinical brewed-tea comparison. The related NTP program later reported a distinct two-year cohort.
