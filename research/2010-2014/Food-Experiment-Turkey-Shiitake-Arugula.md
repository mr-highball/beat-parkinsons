# Turkey, shiitake, and arugula evidence

## 2014 French surveillance of dermatitis after shiitake

Boels and colleagues described 15 reported cases from the French poison-centre system. The cases chiefly followed raw shiitake consumption and presented with delayed cutaneous symptoms. The conference abstract and journal paper cover the same series. They did not evaluate tremor or balance in Parkinson's. [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.3109/15563650.2014.923905) · [Conference proceedings](https://doi.org/10.3109/15563650.2014.906213)

## 2014 Anaphylaxis after arugula and goat cheese salad

Ruiz Garcia and colleagues described a 35-year-old woman with urticaria, angioedema and bronchospasm after arugula and goat cheese salad. The evaluation included prick-by-prick testing with raw Eruca sativa and other vegetables. It concerns a suspected food allergy in one person, with no Parkinson diagnosis, fatigue score, falls assessment or clinical smell test. [Primary report](https://www.jiaci.org/issues/vol24issue6/8-18.pdf) · [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25668902/)

## 2014 Registration for the rocket salad beverage crossover

NCT02271633 first posted October 22, 2014. The crossover registered sodium nitrate, beetroot juice, spinach, and rocket salad as exposures, with plasma nitrate/nitrite, resting blood pressure and oral flora outcomes. No registry results were posted in the retrieved record. The 2016 primary report gives the completed vascular comparisons.
