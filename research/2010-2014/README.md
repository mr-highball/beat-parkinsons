# 2010-2014

The [2013 registration](Source-Text/Trials/ISRCTN44706974.txt) and [2014 protocol](Source-Text/2014-PMC4208054-Ginger-Salt-Poststroke-Urinary-Protocol.txt) document a prospective ginger–salt moxibustion comparison for poststroke urinary incontinence. The public registry lists the protocol and no results.

The [Bougainville field survey](Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2011-Bougainville-Ginger-Abdominal-Pain-and-Constipation.txt) documents a traditional ginger use that lists abdominal pain and constipation in a typhoid context.

Original conference screens cover the [2010 European Students’ Conference](Source-Text/2010-PMC3476935-European-Students-Conference-Food-Scope.txt), [2012 FEBS poster block](Source-Text/2012-PMC7163973-FEBS-Poster-Culinary-Exposure-Scope.txt) and [2014 Indian pharmacology oral papers](Source-Text/2014-PMC4308838-Indian-Pharmacology-Oral-Papers-Food-Scope.txt). The notes distinguish measured exposures from comparators, vehicles and neighboring abstracts.

Clinical and reference sources include the [hospice supplement questionnaire](Source-Text/2010-PMC4056556-Hospice-Supplement-Survey-Scope.txt), [ginger-partitioned moxibustion review](Source-Text/2011-PMC3136359-Moxibustion-Ginger-Partition-and-GI-Endpoints.txt), [herbal–drug interaction review](Source-Text/2011-PMC3252717-Herbal-Drug-Interaction-Food-Scope.txt), [pregnancy therapy chapter](Source-Text/2012-PMC7271212-Pregnancy-Herbal-Chapter-Scope.txt), [gait review word context](Source-Text/2012-PMC3613197-Gait-Review-Ginger-Word-Context.txt) and [curcumin neuropharmacology review](Source-Text/2013-PMC3744901-Curcumin-Review-Taxon-and-Endpoint-Scope.txt).

The [2011 metabolic study](Source-Text/2011-PMID22152092-Ginger-Diabetic-Rat-Metabolism.txt) and [2009 IUPS poster](../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMC10722647-IUPS-Poster-Food-Exposures.txt) share researchers and an extract schedule. Animal cohort overlap remains unresolved.

The [healthy adult transit study](Source-Text/2010-PMID20378829-Daikenchuto-Healthy-Colonic-Transit.txt) and its [W1413 abstract](Source-Text/2010-W1413-Daikenchuto-Healthy-Transit-Abstract.txt) match the same 60-person registered comparison. [T1829](Source-Text/2010-T1829-Daikenchuto-Constipation-and-Pain-Abstract.txt) and the [chronic constipation article](Source-Text/PMC5139733.txt) share phases and outcomes. The link to the single-arm registration remains uncertain.

The [registered Daikenchuto transit comparison](Source-Text/Trials/NCT01139216.txt) and [2013 author abstract](Source-Text/2013-PMID23451764-Daikenchuto-Transit-and-Rectal-Sensation.txt) document mostly inconclusive transit and stool outcomes. The [2012 bloating registration](Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000008070.txt) specifies a single uncontrolled arm. A [2013 ginger review](Source-Text/2013-PMID23717767-Ginger-Review-Constipation-and-Pain.txt) lists customary digestive uses and cites a distinct exercise-pain trial. The [2014 poststroke trial](Source-Text/2014-PMID25089144-Daikenchuto-Poststroke-Constipation.txt) measured an add-on effect of the complete formula.

The [STEPS randomized results abstract](Source-Text/2011-PMID21565557-Spheramine-STEPS-Randomized-Results.txt) identifies the sham-controlled comparison and its motor endpoint. [Pilot and pathology lineage](../2005-2009/Observations/2008-Visual-Event-Definitions-and-Spheramine-Lineage.md).

The [2012 RECOVER extension abstract](Source-Text/2012-RECOVER-Open-Label-Extension.txt) reports general hallucinations in the open-label group. Its participant proportion aligns with the [posted results](Source-Text/2010-RECOVER-Registry-Visual-Adverse-Events.txt).

The [2010 visual symptom observations](Observations/2010-Visual-Sleep-and-Drug-Outcome-Lineage.md) trace the RECOVER reports, nocturnal hallucination ratings, diagnostic differences and the open congress issue.

The [2011 visual phenotype and imaging observations](Observations/2011-Visual-Phenotypes-and-Imaging-Lineage.md) distinguish presentation, recurrence, diagnostic classification and imaging signals, and verify reuse of stimulation and MRI data.

The [2012 DLB and visual symptom source group](Observations/2012-Lewy-Body-Hallucination-Measures.md) separates visual ratings, symptom composites, adverse events and the older imaging comparison. The [phase III registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT01278407.txt) links the subsequent confirmatory publication.

The [2014 clinical visual criteria and plant preparation observations](Observations/2014-Visual-Criteria-and-Plant-Preparation-Lineage.md) compare the GBA clinical cohort, stimulation case, 2014 Ayurvedic records and adjacent trial phases.

The [ADVANCE-PD](Source-Text/2013-PMID23485610-ADVANCE-PD-Randomized-Maintenance.txt) and [ASCEND-PD](Source-Text/2014-PMID25306200-ASCEND-PD-Randomized-Crossover.txt) primary abstracts identify the two randomized levodopa comparison families. The [2014 epilepsy meeting members](Source-Text/2014-AES-Selected-Visual-and-Parkinsonism-Abstracts.txt) cross-reference the [original Dravet cohort](Source-Text/PMC4113465.txt) and the separately published 2015 issue.

The [serotonin PET comparison](Source-Text/2010-PMID20385906-Parkinson-Visual-Hallucinations-and-Serotonin-PET.txt) distinguishes formed visual hallucinations from PET binding in two groups of Parkinson patients. The later [2014 nilotinib pilot registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT02281474.txt) tracks different outcomes.

The [2010 clinical magnesium case](Source-Text/2010-PMID20493607-Regolisti-Magnesium-and-Delirium.txt) documents delirium with hallucinations during a period of acute electrolyte disturbance. [Comparative visual outcome and citation observations](../2020-2024/Observations/Visual-End-Points-and-Magnesium-Citation-Follow-Through.md).

The [2011 electrolyte case](Source-Text/PMC3233929.txt) reports a visual hallucination episode that stopped before clinical replacement. [Comparison of visual symptom timing](../2020-2024/Observations/Dried-Fig-Magnesium-and-Visual-Symptom-Timing.md).

An [active-fiber comparison in 20 patients](Source-Text/2010-Kim-FASEB-Ficus-Fiber-Comparisons-Abstract.txt) reports within-arm changes. The earlier publication date and study size distinguish this conference record from the 40-person and 80-person paste registrations. The [canine fig-paste model](Source-Text/2011-PMID22232635-Beagle-Fig-Paste-and-Transit.txt) and [rat fig-paste abstract](Source-Text/2011-PMID22178225-Rat-Fig-Paste-Constipation-Abstract.txt) supply separate measured animal outcomes.

The [Parkinson interview study](Source-Text/2010-PMID20437542-Parkinson-Psychosis-Interviews-and-Visual-Outcomes.txt) separates past-month visual and nonvisual hallucinations. Original comparisons report [pedunculopontine and thalamic MRI findings](Source-Text/2011-PMID21717194-Parkinson-Visual-Hallucinations-and-Pedunculopontine-MRI.txt), [stimulus-evoked visual experiences in Lewy body dementia](Source-Text/2011-PMID22016436-Lewy-Body-Visual-Hallucinations-and-Cortical-Stimulation.txt), [connectivity by dementia group](Source-Text/2011-PMID22189566-Visual-Connectivity-in-Lewy-Body-and-Alzheimer-Dementia.txt), and [history of sleep-related falling](Source-Text/2012-PMID22523513-Sleep-Related-Falling-and-Visual-Hallucinations-in-Parkinson.txt). The [Onofrj review](Source-Text/PMC5215646.txt) describes measurement boundaries. Separate studies inspect [patterned visual hallucinations during proton pump inhibitor exposure](Source-Text/2013-PMID23341015-Visual-Hallucinations-with-Macular-Degeneration-and-PPIs.txt) and [acute experimental visual experiences in healthy volunteers](Source-Text/2013-PMID23785166-Experimental-Visual-Effects-in-Healthy-Volunteers.txt). [Earlier fig and visual observations](Observations/Fig-Preparation-and-Older-Visual-Phenotypes.md).

Two separately registered functional constipation trials studied fig paste. [2010 40-person protocol](Source-Text/Trials/NCT01185431.txt) · [2014 80-person protocol](Source-Text/Trials/NCT02138851.txt) · [2016 controlled comparison](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27440682-Fig-Paste-and-Functional-Constipation.txt).

A [terminated masked sage-tablet trial](Source-Text/Trials/NCT02032862.txt) registered sweating and menopause outcomes using a fresh-leaf extract.

New [diazinon strain evidence](Source-Text/2012-PMID22871176-Diazinon-Strains-and-Tissue-Context.txt) records the female rat biochemical comparison. The [Pitozzi aging-rat olive oil study](Source-Text/2010-PMID20070918-Pitozzi-Olive-Oil-Light-Dark-Box.txt) records a direction discrepancy between Figure 2 and its abstract. The [Amaranthus renal paper](Source-Text/2014-PMID25415872-Amaranthus-Olive-Oil-Renal-Vehicle.txt) identifies injected olive oil as the vehicle and was posted online in 2014.

The [full 2011 FEBS poster supplement](Source-Text/2011-PMC7164111-FEBS-Poster-Food-and-Parkinson-Context.txt) distinguishes food composition, isolated compounds, oil substrates and separate Parkinson biomarker comparisons.

The [2011 SGIM food and coffee contexts](Source-Text/2011-PMC6730977-SGIM-Coffee-and-Ingredient-Contexts.txt) distinguish symptom-driven use and case histories. The [separate erratum](Source-Text/2011-PMC3181309-SGIM-Omitted-Abstracts.txt) supplies the omitted members.

The [2012 online-first rosemary and common-sage study](Source-Text/2012-PMID23017402-Sage-Rosemary-Extracts.txt) profiles plant extracts and tests cell and chemical responses. The later IPLeiria proceedings cite it.

The [BRAVO registry](Source-Text/Trials/NCT01901887.txt) used a macadamia-oil comparison drink. A [2014 family-history meal study](Source-Text/2014-PMID24824014-Macadamia-Oil-Meal-and-Lipemia.txt) gave the same oil to both participant groups.
