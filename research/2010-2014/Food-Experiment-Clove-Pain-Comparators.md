# Clove Pain Comparators

## Injected clove essential oil and mouse hot-plate response

Hosseini, Kamkar Asl and Rakhshandeh published in summer 2011. In 50 male mice assigned to saline or one of four concentrations of clove essential oil injected intraperitoneally, the authors reported higher percentage maximal possible effect in the hot-plate assay for 5%, 10% and 20% preparations versus saline. Baseline latencies did not show significant differences. The exposure was injected essential oil and the outcome a thermal nociceptive response.
