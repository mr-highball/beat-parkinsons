# Fortified oatmeal in a hospital falls intervention

## Oatmeal within a randomized nutrition package and recorded falls

Neelemaat and colleagues first published the results on February 8, 2012. The trial randomized 210 malnourished hospitalized adults aged at least 60 to a nutrition program or usual care. During hospitalization, the program included enriched meals with fortified oatmeal and desserts. Other components included oral supplements, calcium and vitamin D, and dietitian support. Over three months, 10 participants in the intervention group and 24 in usual care fell, with 16 and 41 events, respectively. The reported hazard ratio for first fall was 0.41 (95% CI 0.19–0.86). Oatmeal was a component with unspecified grain processing and individual intake. The comparison estimates the effect of the assigned package. The paper reports trial registration NTR476 and ISRCTN29617677, created January 27, 2006.
