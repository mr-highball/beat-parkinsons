# Summer savory voice follow-up, 2010–2014

## Market survey separates summer savory from hoarseness entries

Amiri and Joharchi’s 2013 survey documents medicinal plants sold in Mashhad, Iran. In its Table 1, entry 153 assigns *Satureja hortensis* aerial parts to reported digestive uses, not hoarseness. The same long table mentions hoarseness for other plants, including *Alyssum alyssoides* and *Acacia senegal*. A whole-document co-occurrence of summer savory and hoarseness therefore does not establish an herb-specific use, let alone measured intelligibility or voice volume.
