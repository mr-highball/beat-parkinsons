## Prospective acidic fruit cohorts and urgency incontinence

Townsend and colleagues published online August 10, 2012. Two US Nurses’ Health Study cohorts contributed 65,168 women to incidence analyses. The adjusted pooled relative risk for incident urgency incontinence was 1.07 (95% CI 0.76–1.50) for the highest versus lowest intake quintile, with p for trend .8. The report analyzes citrus, plums or grapes, apples, berries and tomato products. It does not analyze raw peach intake separately. Urgency incontinence contacts in this study differ from counts of urgency episodes without leakage in Parkinson disease. A newer case-control paper found a positive association for a pooled high-acid-food item.
