# Ginger and menstrual pain

## Ginger capsules and an active menstrual-pain comparator

The trial was first published in November 2014 and appeared in the June 2015 issue. The 122 participants with primary dysmenorrhea received ginger capsules or mefenamic acid for two cycles. Mean pain on a 100 mm scale was 43.49 versus 39.01 after the first cycle and 38.19 versus 33.75 after the second. The between-group comparisons were inconclusive. The ginger group had longer menses in both treatment cycles.

The study used an active comparator and specified no equivalence margin. It contributes a human pain comparison for a capsule preparation and menstrual pain.
