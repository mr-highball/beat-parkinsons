# Tarragon and oregano

## Oral summer savory leaf powder knee osteoarthritis trial registration

IRCT2013112515542N1 registered December 17, 2013 while recruiting for a double-blind trial targeting 60 adults with knee osteoarthritis. It compares dried *Satureja hortensis* leaf powder capsules with starch capsules over two months. The primary measures include visual analogue pain scores, TNF-α and IL-1β. A 2015 paper by registry contacts Mozaffari-Khosravi and Asadi matches the center, cohort size, intervention and pain endpoint. [Official IRCT registration](https://irct.ir/trial/14789) · [Local registry summary](Source-Text/IRCT2013112515542N1.txt) · [2015 outcome report](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#oral-summer-savory-powder-and-knee-osteoarthritis-pain)

## Oregano extract crossover EEG and overnight polysomnography

A 2010 patent and Boeijinga and colleagues’ 2012 conference abstract describe the same randomized crossover experiment in 20 healthy young men. The patent reports that 17 finished four dose conditions including placebo, separated by at least five days. The 60 mg condition had a 21.2% increase in P300 amplitude over placebo after two hours, and the 120 mg condition raised alpha-1 and beta-1 EEG power. Overnight polysomnography showed no apparent change in sleep architecture. The human preparation was a standardized oregano extract with no confirmed species and no whole dried leaf comparator. The reports supply neither a measure of clinical daytime sleepiness nor a Parkinson-specific result for claim 0842. [2010 patent, example 1 and Figure 4](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2010094761A2/en) · [2012 IPEG conference abstract, printed page 58](https://www.ipeg-society.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/IPEG-2012-Conference-Abstract-Book.pdf)

## Tarragon oil, mouse nociception and acute toxicity

Maham and colleagues injected tarragon essential oil in mice. The 100 and 300 mg/kg groups had lower paw-licking scores in both formalin test phases. Higher tested doses prolonged hot-plate latency and decreased acetic acid-induced writhing. Separate acute toxicity observations above 1000 mg/kg included abnormal gait, ataxia and reduced activity. These observations are indirect for tarragon and pain (0285) and for postural instability (0018). They do not supply a result on eating fresh leaves, human balance or Parkinsonian pain. [September 2013 online primary article, PMID 24074293](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.3109/13880209.2013.824007)

## Tarragon extract in healthy adults registered in 2014

NCT02088203 was first posted March 14, 2014, enrolled 64 participants and completed in May 2018. Its protocol includes a soy-protein tarragon complex and general adverse-event collection. The public registry has no posted results and does not register a dedicated sleepiness, fatigue or orthostatic lightheadedness outcome. The study concerns a different preparation and a healthy population. No result is available for claims 0563, 0822 or 0871. [Official registry record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02088203) · [Public protocol](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/03/NCT02088203/Prot_SAP_000.pdf)

## Summer savory seed fractions and evoked nociception

2011-11-16. Hajhashemi, Zolfaghari and Yousefi compared summer savory seed oil and two seed extracts in mouse experiments. All three preparations reduced acetic-acid-induced abdominal responses (p < .001). Oil and polyphenolic extract affected the late formalin phase, while the hydroalcoholic extract affected both phases. The study varied administration route across preparations and tested seed fractions, not fresh leaves. The rodent outcomes provide indirect context for claim 0659. [Primary publisher abstract](https://doi.org/10.1159/000333555)
