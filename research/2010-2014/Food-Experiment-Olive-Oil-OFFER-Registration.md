## Olive oil comparator for psychiatric symptoms in OFFER

The official NCT02210962 record was first posted August 7, 2014. The study compared long-chain omega-3 fatty acids with an olive oil placebo in first-episode schizophrenia. It prespecified total PANSS and its positive, negative and general domains and relapse. Olive oil grade, visual hallucination frequency and a comparison isolating olive oil are unreported. The later clinical article reports broad symptom change and shares this trial. [Official record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02210962) · [Trial publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26679763/)
