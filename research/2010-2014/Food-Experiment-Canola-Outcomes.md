# Canola and rapeseed oil outcome follow-up

## Rapeseed oil mixture in a nurse anxiety trial registration

- First posted May 30, 2014. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT02151162 was a randomized study of omega-3 capsules and mindfulness-based stress management among hospital nurses. The oil contrast enrolled 80 participants. The sponsor's ClinicalTrials.gov record displayed no results under the API receipt dated November 14, 2026. [Trial registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02151162)
- The subsequent protocol describes a blended capsule containing 47% rapeseed oil as the comparator in the oil contrast, and the 2018 report provides anxiety outcomes. The protocol and results belong to the same trial family as this record. [Local protocol and results](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Canola-Outcomes.md#anxiety-outcomes-with-a-rapeseed-oil-blend-in-hospital-nurses)
