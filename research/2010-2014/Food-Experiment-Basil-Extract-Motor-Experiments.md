# Basil Extract Motor Experiments

## Sweet basil extract and motor coordination before mouse ischemia

Bora, Arora and Shri report an oral ethyl acetate sweet basil extract before 15 minutes of bilateral carotid occlusion and 24-hour reperfusion in mice. The abstract reports less motor coordination impairment on the inclined beam versus injured controls without displaying group-level estimates. The 2009 poster with the same authors describes 10 minutes of occlusion and both methanol and ethyl acetate leaf extracts. These reports may be related. No measured stride length, clinical Parkinson population or fresh food intake is provided.
