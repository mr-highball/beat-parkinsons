# Kiwi feeding studies

## Gold kiwi with fortified cereal trial protocol

January 26, 2010. The prospective Beck protocol describes 89 randomized participants, women with low iron stores, assigned two gold kiwifruit or a banana with the same iron-fortified cereal and milk for 16 weeks. Its endpoints are iron status, ascorbate, carotenoids and other physiological or intake measures. The registration identifier links the August 2010 report. Cognitive attention is outside the listed outcomes. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2823703/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2010-Beck-Fortified-Cereal-Protocol.txt)

## Gold kiwi with fortified cereal reported iron outcomes

First published August 23, 2010. Beck and colleagues randomized 44 participants to two gold kiwifruit and 45 to banana with the same cereal and milk. The final analysis included 33 and 36 respectively. Median ferritin changes were 10.0 and 1.0 micrograms per liter (comparison p < .001). The final samples also differed in soluble transferrin receptor change. The study measured iron biomarkers and recorded dietary and health fields. There was no attention task. The thesis also describes other studies of iron, health and fatigue, which do not provide a kiwi-arm attention contrast. ## Fresh Sungold fruit and plasma and urinary ascorbate

November 11, 2013. Nine healthy men took 1.5 Sungold kiwifruit without the skin or approximately 200 mg tablet vitamin C in different sessions. One participant did not complete the tablet session. Table 1 reports fruit versus tablet plasma ascorbate AUC of 237 versus 220 h × micromoles per liter (p = .483) and urinary AUC of 856 versus 618 h × micromoles per millimole of creatinine (p = .004). Here concentration denotes an analyte. No attention performance or Parkinson cohort was studied.
