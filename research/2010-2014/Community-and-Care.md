# Community and care

### 2011 — Uneven access to neurologists in Medicare claims

Willis and colleagues found that 58% of more than 138,000 Medicare beneficiaries with Parkinson’s saw a neurologist during a four-year claims window. Women and beneficiaries from racial groups other than White had lower odds of receiving neurologist care. Neurologist care was associated with better outcomes, with illness severity, geography and selection into specialist care limiting causal inference. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3162639/)

### 2014 — Racial disparity in deep brain stimulation use

Chan and colleagues analyzed U.S. inpatient discharges from 2002–2009. Black patients accounted for a smaller share of Parkinson’s deep brain stimulation procedures than of Parkinson’s discharges overall. The study examined insurance and hospital characteristics as possible contributors. Discharge data cannot determine who met clinical eligibility, received a referral, or chose surgery. [Primary source](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/1812406)
