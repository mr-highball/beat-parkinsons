# Squash, mushroom, eggplant, and zucchini evidence

## 2014 Purified cucurbitacin E in a dopaminergic model

Arel-Dubeau and colleagues studied cucurbitacin E isolated from Ecballium elaterium in differentiated PC-12 cells. Cell death after MPP+ exposure decreased, and autophagic flux changed. The compound did not rescue the cells from the measured oxidative stress. The experiments did not involve zucchini food, food preparation, dream enactment, or executive testing.
