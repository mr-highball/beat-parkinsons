# Seafood and Parkinson’s symptom evidence

## Whole trout meals in healthy men

Hallund and colleagues compared daily 150 g portions of farmed rainbow trout raised on marine or vegetable feed with chicken over eight weeks. The full article reports 68 completers. The marine-fed trout group had higher erythrocyte omega-3 levels, and the paper reported no clear group differences in most cardiovascular measures. The trial did not evaluate the frozen Parkinson’s symptom endpoints. The companion proteomics paper used 30 participants drawn from this trial. Its findings were exploratory biomarkers from the same participants. [2010 primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20594395/) · [Companion primary report, first online 2015](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114514004152) · [Registration](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Trout-Sardine-Shrimp.md#farmed-trout-intervention-registration)

## Sardine diet trial registration

NCT02294526 first posted on November 19, 2014. The completed study enrolled 35 adults with type 2 diabetes, with a sardine diet arm and a control diet arm. Outcomes included glycemic measures, inflammatory biomarkers, blood pressure, and gut microbiota. The registry has no posted results and lists no fatigue outcome.
