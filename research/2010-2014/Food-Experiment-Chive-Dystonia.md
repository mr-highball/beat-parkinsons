# Chives, related Allium species and dystonia

## 2012 — Multi-food diet and physician-observed dystonia in one Parkinson case

Renoudet and colleagues reported a single outpatient with idiopathic Parkinson’s disease. After a broad low-animal-fat eating pattern had failed to prevent decline, the regimen was supplemented with dietary sources of fisetin and N-hexacosanol. At six months the treating neurologist described apparent resolution of dystonia and several other signs, with little change in tremor. The primary abstract describes outpatient observations without a parallel comparison or standardized dystonia rating and gives no evidence of a defined fresh-chive exposure. The public journal and PubMed views supplied the abstract. The detailed case narrative and ingredient list remain an access lead.
