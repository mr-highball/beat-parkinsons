# Corn oil and Parkinson’s symptom follow-up

## Parkinson cognition communication and mobility during the 2014 CBD trial

Chagas and colleagues randomized 21 people with Parkinson’s to two doses of cannabidiol or placebo for six weeks. Corn oil carried the active CBD and was also the oral placebo. Refinement grade and an oil-free allocation were absent from the methods. In Table 2, baseline-minus-end Parkinson’s Disease Questionnaire communication changes were 0.00, 0.00 and 9.52 for placebo, CBD 75 mg and CBD 300 mg, with omnibus p=.531. Cognition changes were 13.57, 14.29 and 7.14, with p=.332. Mobility changes were 4.17, 5.71 and 19.64, with p=.106. Unified Parkinson’s Disease Rating Scale parts I and II were reported only as totals in the treatment table.

Use the communication domain for the speech intelligibility trail (0459), the cognition domain for attention (0237), and the mobility domain for stride (0324) and falls (0546). Salivation, speech and motivation may contribute to parts I or II of the original UPDRS. Those totals give no item-level information for drooling (0091), speech or apathy (0862). The tables do not give stride measurement, prospectively counted falls, or oral-oil effects independent of the drug comparison. This is the same cohort already annotated for physical discomfort and UPDRS. ## Corn oil comparator and sustained attention performance

McNamara and colleagues analyzed 33 healthy boys aged 8–10 after an eight-week randomized comparison of corn oil and two algal DHA doses. Their identical-pairs continuous performance task found no statistically significant group difference or time-by-dose interaction for accuracy, commission errors, discriminability or reaction time. Imaging showed differences in activation, and the investigators separately reported an inverse correlation of erythrocyte DHA with reaction time. Neither finding estimates a change in behavioral attention attributable to ingesting corn oil. The task directly measures sustained attention, while the population, active comparator and unreported oil refinement limit transfer to Parkinson’s claim 0237.
