# Beverage coadministration and induced tremor

The 2014 Karmakar original includes black tea, grapefruit juice and water in rat groups receiving carbamazepine. Table 3 reports different observed onset times for induced tremor. Significance is given against the challenge-only comparator. The relevant beverage-versus-water uncertainty remains unpublished in that table. The study provides no rest-tremor change in Parkinson disease. [Original-source summary and comparator definitions](Source-Text/2014-PMID28911389.txt)
