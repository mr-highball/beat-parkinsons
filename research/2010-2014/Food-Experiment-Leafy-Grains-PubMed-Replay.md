# Leafy grain search replay evidence, 2010–2014

## Tartary buckwheat products and peripheral neuropathy in a diabetes case series

Five men who took Tartary buckwheat products for diabetes developed peripheral neuropathy with motor and sensory signs. The abstract labels part of the presentation dyskinesia, while listing limb weakness, numbness, and nerve conduction changes. Four reported hoarseness. Product preparation and dose remain unverified and no Parkinson dyskinesia or speech-intelligibility outcome was separately reported. This is an adverse clinical signal with a distinct neurological syndrome and uncertain exposure attribution.
