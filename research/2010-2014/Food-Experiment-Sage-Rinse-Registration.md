# Sage rinse registration

## Sage mouth rinse registration in palliative cancer

The official 2014 registration identifies the completed comparison of sage tea mouth rinse with saline over four days in advanced cancer. The currently posted outcomes cover oral mucositis, oral comfort, clinical dental assessment and general symptoms. The registry history records an outcome-module revision in 2017, and the original wording remains open. No results are posted in the registry. The published pain ratings and companion papers are recorded in their publication period.
