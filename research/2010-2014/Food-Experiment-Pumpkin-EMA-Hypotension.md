# Hypotension mention in the pumpkin seed regulator assessment

## Hypotension mention in the pumpkin seed regulator assessment

The regulator’s clinical table lists hypotension in an uncontrolled 2,245-patient ethanolic seed-extract study. It gives no count by event type, standing test or comparator. The safety section does not quantify hypotension separately. The extract and cold-pressed oil are distinct preparations.
