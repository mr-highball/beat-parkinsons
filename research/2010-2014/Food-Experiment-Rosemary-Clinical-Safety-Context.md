# Rosemary exposure and clinical outcomes

## Multi-ingredient event in pharmacy surveillance

Necyk and colleagues published a pharmacy active-surveillance study on 28 March 2014. Its Table 4 includes a 16-year-old who reported shallow breathing, fatigue and convulsions while using RespirActin, a product listing rosemary among many other ingredients. The investigators judged the event possibly caused by the natural health product. The record does not identify fresh rosemary leaves, dose, an isolated rosemary effect, Parkinson disease or visual hallucinations. The product-level assessment is not proof that rosemary caused the event.
