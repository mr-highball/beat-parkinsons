# Walnut and vegetable-oil feeding trial registration

## Registered walnut and fatty-acid-matched diets

NCT02210767 was first posted on August 7, 2014. The current record describes a randomized crossover trial comparing a whole-walnut diet with a walnut-free fatty-acid-matched diet and a lower-alpha-linolenic-acid diet. Its primary outcome is change in central blood pressure; the listed outcomes do not include dystonia or spasm. The record reports 46 enrolled and no posted results. The registration is dated by first posting; the fields summarized here reflect the current record.
