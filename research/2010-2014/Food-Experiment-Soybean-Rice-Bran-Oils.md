# Food experiment evidence for soybean and rice bran oil

## Essential tremor under two octanol formulations

Nahab and colleagues first reported NCT00102596 on May 19, 2011. In ten participants in the fixed-dose crossover, mean normalized spiral tremor amplitude at 90 minutes was 0.670 with cellulose-formulated 1-octanol and 0.685 with soybean-oil-formulated 1-octanol. Figure 4 reports improvement from baseline with both and the authors describe a longer effect with the soybean formulation. The soybean preparation also altered drug pharmacokinetics. Table 5 pools adverse events, including sedation and worsening tremor, across formulations, and the text reports a transient gait-instability episode without identifying its formulation. Registry results posted February 1, 2012 and give a 360-minute soybean mean that differs from the paper's reported six-hour reduction. The 2013 octanoic acid trial found no difference on the primary 80-minute tremor-power outcome, with differences favoring the active drug at later times. It used soybean oil in the active preparation. Neither study estimates an oil-only effect on Parkinson rest tremor, daytime sleepiness or falls. [2011 primary report, Figure 4 and Table 5](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s13311-011-0045-1.pdf) · [Official results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00102596?tab=results) · [Local results snapshot](Source-Text/Trials/NCT00102596-results.txt) · [2013 primary trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3653213/)

## Soybean oil compared with hydrogenated fat in rat movement tests

Teixeira and colleagues first published October 8, 2011. Rats were assigned feeds enriched with 20% soybean oil, lard or hydrogenated vegetable fat. Hydrogenated fat increased orofacial dyskinesia and, together with lard, reduced locomotor activity relative to the soybean diet. An exercise intervention modified some outcomes. Pase and colleagues’ 2014 study compared soybean oil, hydrogenated fat and standard chow over age and reported the highest orofacial dyskinesia and anxiety-like measures under hydrogenated fat. These were rat food comparisons without induced Parkinson disease. Unverified refining grade and active comparator effects limit translation to levodopa-related dyskinesia or walking speed. [2011 primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22004982/) · [2014 primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24625052/)

## Physical refining and measured rice bran oil constituents

The 2014 commercial refining study measured significant reductions in phytosterols and oryzanol between crude and finished rice bran oil. Oryzanol fell from 1,599–1,666 to 933–960 mg per 100 g. The earlier laboratory study reported little loss at its physical refining step and substantially larger losses after alkali refining. The degree of retention depended on the tested process. These studies measured oil composition rather than clinical outcomes.
