# Tarragon endpoint follow-up

## Russian tarragon extract and anaerobic capacity

The February 26, 2014 randomized double-blind crossover gave ten recreationally trained men an aqueous Russian tarragon extract or placebo before creatine in both periods. Nine supplied exercise data. Table 3 reports no clear group-by-time interaction for peak power (p = .92), mean power (p = .27) or total work (p = .27) over five days. Mean power and total work improved over time in the combined data. The study measured short sprint performance without a clinical fatigue scale or dried culinary leaf intervention. This is indirect exercise context for 0822. [Primary plain text, Table 3](Source-Text/PMC3975968.txt#L189) · [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1186/1550-2783-11-6)

## Tarragon in odor enrichment after neonatal naris occlusion

Kato and colleagues reopened the occluded naris in mice at postnatal day 19 and presented 21 grocery-sourced odors in rotation for 42 days. Tarragon was one of these odors. The testing stimuli were paprika and cinnamon. Intact mice (n = 14), reopened mice without enrichment (n = 11) and reopened mice with enrichment (n = 8) all investigated cinnamon longer than in the previous paprika trial. Compared with intact controls, the normalized discrimination index was lower in reopened mice without enrichment and showed no clear difference in the enriched reopened group. Figure 4 does not establish the individual tarragon effect. This is related to 0982 through the behavior and odor exposure. [November 2012 primary paper, Methods and Figure 4](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0048431) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC3486849.txt)

## Russian tarragon tinctures and rat exploratory behavior

Table 30 of Ifrat N. Zilfikarov’s 2011 pharmaceutical monograph, reposted online in 2018, reports open-field and burrow-reflex outcomes after intragastric administration of tarragon tinctures prepared with 40% or 70% ethanol. Exploratory scores rose from 2.9 in controls to 3.8 and 3.4, while search scores fell from 3.9 to 2.4 and 2.6. The authors describe moderate sedation and give no timed sleep outcome. The differing rat behavioral measures provide context for the later grooming and social interaction study. Overlap with Aglarova and Zilfikarov's 2006 article and Aglarova's 2006 thesis remains unverified.
