# Clinical and Diagnosis

### 2014 — Olfaction and dopamine transporter imaging in PARS

The Parkinson Associated Risk Syndrome study used smell screening followed by dopamine transporter imaging in participants without diagnosed Parkinson’s. A transporter deficit was more common in the hyposmic group. The study tested an enrichment strategy for following people at elevated risk. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25298306/)
