# Canola and safflower oil evidence

## Food questionnaire used in the Iranian oil study trail

The 2010 Tehran Lipid and Glucose Study food-group validation grouped liquid oils. The adjusted and deattenuated comparison of the second questionnaire with recalls for liquid oil was 0.03 in men and 0.35 in women. The later Parkinson cohort papers describe a 147-item version. The validation publications cannot establish the exact oil questions used in the Parkinson study. [2009 nutrient validation](../2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Canola-Safflower.md#nutrient-validation-of-the-tehran-food-questionnaire) · [2010 full text, Tables 1 and 4](Source-Text/PMC3900814.txt) · [Tabriz study trail](../2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Canola-Safflower.md#cooking-oil-exposures-in-the-tabriz-approved-parkinson-reports)

## Cold-pressed turnip rapeseed oil and resting blood pressure

Palomäki and colleagues reported 37 completers in a Finnish crossover of cold-pressed turnip rapeseed oil and butter. Resting systolic pressure averaged 138.5 mmHg after each period. Mean diastolic pressure was 81.3 after oil and 81.8 after butter, with no clear difference. The population had metabolic syndrome, and the study used turnip rapeseed oil without a standing or lightheadedness outcome. The 2018 postprandial report shares the trial ID and concerns metabolic responses. [Primary full text](Source-Text/PMC3017527.txt) · [Official record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01119690) · [Later report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5935965/)

## Registered canola feeding study in peripheral arterial disease

NCT01250275 first posted on November 30, 2010 and lists completed without results on the registry. The acute experiment lists high-linoleic safflower oil as one of five banana-bread oil arms. In the eight-week comparison, the registry names a comparator intervention “safflower oil” but describes a mixture representing the Western diet. The 2014 protocol and 2015 dissertation likewise describe the eight-week comparator as a blend and specify intended treadmill walking and cognitive tests. The thesis reports selected trial results. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01250275) · [Selected registry fields](Source-Text/Trials/NCT01250275.txt) · [Primary protocol](https://doi.org/10.2147/OAJCT.S70576) · [Dated results note](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Canola-Safflower.md#canola-feeding-study-results-in-peripheral-arterial-disease)

## Maternal safflower oil and exploration in offspring rats

Rangel’s 2014 university dissertation compared offspring from dams fed a diet containing safflower oil during gestation and lactation with a control group. The safflower group spent more time in open arms and showed more exploration in the elevated plus maze (reported p < .05). The project assessed developmental exposure, with no Parkinson model or verified cold-pressed grade. A 2022 paper with investigators in common reports developmental reflex and memory outcomes. Cohort identity remains unverified. [Original dissertation, pp. 30–46](https://dspace.sti.ufcg.edu.br/bitstream/riufcg/9838/3/RENATA%20COSTA%20RANGEL%20-%20TCC%20BACHARELADO%20EM%20NUTRI%C3%87%C3%83O%20CES%202014.pdf) · [2022 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36029874/)
