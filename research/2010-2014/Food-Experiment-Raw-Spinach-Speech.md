# Raw spinach and speech

## Communication interviews and a spinach gardening example

Baylor et al. analyzed interviews with 44 adults with communication disorders from seven diagnostic groups, including Parkinson disease. A participant with stroke mentioned giving a family member advice about planting spinach; other participants with Parkinson disease described speech clarity difficulties. The spinach reference is a conversation topic, not a meal, exposure, or comparison. The paper does not test a change in speech intelligibility after consuming spinach. [Primary author manuscript, PMID 21813820](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC3296484.1/PMC3296484.1.txt).
