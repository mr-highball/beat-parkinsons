# Tarragon odor methods follow-up

## Tarragon in serine racemase mouse odor probes

DeVito and colleagues used commercial tarragon at 1% by weight in sand as one of two odor sequences. A chocolate piece rewarded digging during familiarization. The unbaited probes paired tarragon with orange for within-sequence order, cilantro for between-sequence order, and onion for novelty. Figure 2 pools multiple odor-pair trials. Mutants and controls had opposing within-sequence preferences, while the pooled novelty result did not clearly differ. The study does not identify the tarragon pair’s contribution and specifies no dietary exposure. ## Tarragon in mouse lesion and sham odor probes

DeVito and Eichenbaum used the same tarragon pairings in a separate hippocampal lesion, medial prefrontal lesion and sham experiment. For the pooled within-sequence odor probes, preference indices were 0.28 ± 0.04 (sham), 0.01 ± 0.06 (hippocampal lesion), and −0.08 ± 0.05 (prefrontal lesion). Across pooled novelty trials, the between-group p value was .271. This distinguishes odor-order memory and familiarity recognition under brain lesions. The tarragon comparisons remained pooled with other pairs.
