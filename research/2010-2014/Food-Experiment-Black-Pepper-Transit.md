# Pepper extract and model gastrointestinal behavior

## Crude black pepper extract and mixed bowel outcomes

Mehmood and Gilani studied crude black pepper extract and isolated piperine in excised tissue and animal assays. The primary abstract reports both stimulatory and inhibitory activity under different assay conditions, including stimulation in isolated ileum and antidiarrheal effects in mice. It supplies no clinical estimate of constipation severity, and neither the crude extract nor the purified alkaloid is cracked peppercorns in ordinary meals.
