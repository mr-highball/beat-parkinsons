# Movement and Rehabilitation

### 2012 — Tai chi versus resistance training or stretching

A 195-person randomized trial found better limits of stability with tai chi than with resistance training or stretching. Falls were less frequent than in the stretching group during the intervention. The endpoints address functional balance. [Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1107911)

### 2012 — Treadmill intensity and resistance training

A randomized study found different functional benefits across lower-intensity treadmill, higher-intensity treadmill and stretching plus resistance training. The treadmill arms improved fitness and the resistance arm improved strength. The study compared active exercise approaches. First published online in November 2012. [Primary source](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/1389386)

### 2014 — Home occupational therapy and daily activities

A randomized trial found better self-rated performance on personally important daily activities after individualized home occupational therapy than usual care. The endpoint captures perceived function over short-term follow-up. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24726066/)
