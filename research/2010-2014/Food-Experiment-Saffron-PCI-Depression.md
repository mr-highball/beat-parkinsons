# Saffron capsules in a post-coronary intervention trial

## Saffron capsules and depression after coronary intervention

Shahmansouri and colleagues first published online November 16, 2013. They randomized 40 adults with mild to moderate depression after coronary intervention to saffron capsules or fluoxetine for six weeks. The author abstract gives no clear between-arm difference in Hamilton Depression Rating Scale improvement (p=.62), response (p=.67) or remission (p=1.00). The small active-comparator design has no inactive placebo and supplies no evidence of equivalence. Capsule processing, full effect estimates and applicability to Parkinson depression remain unverified.
