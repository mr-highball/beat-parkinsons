# Fish-derived omega-3 and measured anxiety

## Marine omega-3 blend and Beck anxiety in students

In a randomized 12-week comparison in 68 medical students, Kiecolt-Glaser and colleagues reported a favorable Beck Anxiety Inventory difference with fish oil relative to a mixed-oil placebo. Table 5 gives a modeled log difference of −0.23 (95% CI −0.44 to −0.011, p = .04). The report identifies a commercial EPA-predominant formulation. The fish species and effects of a baked fish meal are outside the reported comparison. Other trials of pooled oily fish and Parkinson questionnaire foods have different groupings and preparations.
