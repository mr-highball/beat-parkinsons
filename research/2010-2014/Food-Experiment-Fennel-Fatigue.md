# Fennel preparations and fatigue outcomes

## Fennel capsules and menstrual weakness scores

Ghodsi and Asltoghiri randomized 80 students to fennel soft capsules or no medication over three months. Table 1 prints weakness 6.65 to 2.88 with fennel and 6.39 to 6.39 in controls (end-point p=.001). The abstract describes the control end-point as 6.65, which Table 1 prints as the fennel baseline. The no-treatment control and self-report leave expectation effects open. Weakness is adjacent to fatigue, and the capsules and population differ from toasted fennel and Parkinson.
