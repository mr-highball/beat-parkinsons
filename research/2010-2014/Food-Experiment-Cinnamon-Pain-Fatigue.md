# Cinnamon, muscle soreness and fatigue source checks

## Cinnamon powder and exercise-related muscle soreness

Mashhadi and colleagues reported 60 female martial athletes assigned to cinnamon powder, ginger powder or placebo mixed with food. Forty-nine completed. The article reports the cinnamon comparison inconsistently. Its abstract and Results give p < .10, and its Conclusion describes insufficient support for cinnamon. The Methods and Results disagree on study duration. This provides culinary-form context for soreness, with an inconclusive comparison and no Parkinson participants. ## Curcumin fatigue and cinnamon references in a glutathione review

Section 9.4.2 of the deposited Morris manuscript attributes the mouse fatigue experiment to curcumin. Cinnamaldehyde is discussed separately in relation to antioxidant enzymes. This resolves the source's broad term match for ground cinnamon and Parkinson fatigue.
