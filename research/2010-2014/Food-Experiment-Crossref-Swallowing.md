# Swallowing sources from Crossref citations, 2010–2014

## Earlier report of the Campinas lemon juice and biscuit cohort

Luchesi, Kitamura and Mourão's September 25, 2013 report followed 24 people with idiopathic Parkinson disease and 27 with ALS in a Campinas clinic between 2006 and 2011. In the clinical and endoscopic protocol, the thin bolus was green-dyed lemon juice. Water and Thicken-easy made the nectar, honey and pudding consistencies. The solid bolus was half a cornstarch biscuit. The Parkinson group received recommendations for bolus changes and exercises. The assessments pool foods and interventions. The matching 24-person Parkinson sample, investigators, clinic, study period and protocol indicate likely cohort overlap with the later Parkinson-only publication. [Primary report and license](https://www.scielo.br/j/codas/a/VKMDwRww4kq7bqjWMzgYtpP/?lang=en) · [Local CC BY article text](Source-Text/2013-Luchesi-Campinas-Dysphagia.txt) · [Later publication](Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#lemon-juice-and-cornstarch-biscuit-in-serial-swallowing-assessments)

Table 1 and the results text give a median increase of 2.5 Functional Oral Intake Scale levels in the Parkinson group. The same table gives the observed range as −6 to +1 and defines positive changes as improvement. Figure 4 plots changes for 24 cases, also with a maximum increase of one. Its caption says 27. The article’s numerical and graphical presentations cannot all describe the same distribution as printed.
