# Canola oil comparator and pain, 2010–2014

## Canola oil in a shea-extract osteoarthritis trial

The original Cheras study identifies a 15-week blinded randomized osteoarthritis comparison and discusses incomplete WOMAC data. The original pain table and the carrier processing method remain inaccessible. The linked trial identifier is ACTRN12606000162516. An independent extraction identifies canola oil as comparator and reports no clear end-of-study difference on the COAT pain subscale. This is an active-comparator context with no oil-free control. Publisher-deposited public date fields give 2010, although Crossref created its record in 2009.
