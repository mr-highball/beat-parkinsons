# Isolated eugenol sedation and oral capsaicin behavior

## Eugenol and barbiturate-induced loss of righting reflex

Sharma and colleagues tested intraperitoneal isolated eugenol at 100 mg/kg in preclinical assays. In the rat pentobarbital assay, the criterion was time between loss and return of the righting reflex. The paper reports longer induced sleep time relative to control (p=.002). Anxiety measures gave no clear effect and mouse rotarod endurance was lower at 30 minutes, with no clear effect later. The endpoint is a barbiturate response after injected compound exposure. Natural sleep, REM behavior, clinical insomnia, and ground allspice were outside the tests. [Primary methods and Table 1](https://www.nepjol.info/index.php/HREN/article/view/7137/5752) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3126/hren.v10i3.7137)

## Repeated oral capsaicin and anxiety-like behavior in intact rats

Choi and colleagues exposed rats with intact oral sensory nerves to 1 mL of 0.02% isolated capsaicin in the oral cavity daily for ten days. Treated rats spent less time in and made a smaller percentage of entries to the open arms of the elevated plus maze. Stress-induced corticosterone elevation persisted longer, with similar basal levels. In forced swim, swimming decreased and struggling increased, while immobility duration did not differ. The study concerns an isolated pepper constituent and experimental behaviors. It supplies a higher-anxiety lead for claim 0035, with no assigned smoked paprika or Parkinson population.
