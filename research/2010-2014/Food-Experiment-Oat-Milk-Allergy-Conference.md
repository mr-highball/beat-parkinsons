# Oat milk allergy at the 2013 EAACI meeting

## 2013 conference case and later journal report

Abstract 1615 at the 2013 EAACI/WAO congress describes a 70-year-old man with hypertension and rheumatoid arthritis who developed an allergic reaction ten minutes after consuming 200 mL of oat milk. The poster reports a 45-kDa immunoblot band and does not list dysphonia. The 2016 journal report explicitly identifies the conference presentation of the same case and reports dysphonia and a 48-kDa serpin. The two reports count as one patient. Neither report measures vocal loudness or Parkinson disease.
