# Rapeseed oil and attention comparator studies

## Rapeseed comparator in a child EPA attention study

The 2010 Gustafsson EPA trial of children with ADHD reports a teacher-rated inattention comparison favoring EPA (p = .04), while overall Conners scores were inconclusive. The investigator’s institutional thesis describes rapeseed oil in the comparator formulation. The trial compares assigned formulations, with no verified refined rapeseed-only effect or Parkinson context. Recipe identity from the later thesis and measured outcome from the original paper belong to the same study.
