# Cinnamon constituent and a laboratory anxiety assay

## Isolated cinnamaldehyde and an oral intervention interaction

Moura and colleagues' mouse experiment tested oral cinnamaldehyde alone and before a TRPA1 antagonist. Cinnamaldehyde on its own produced no clear change in the elevated plus maze. It prevented the open-arm exploration change induced by the antagonist. The interaction does not establish an anxiogenic effect of cinnamon in the absence of the antagonist, and there was no ordinary food preparation or human clinical outcome. ## Water-soluble cinnamon registry outcome boundary

The 2011 prediabetes registration NCT01301521 was completed and has results. Its planned and posted outcomes concern a soluble cinnamon extract and glucose. Consent-form references to anxiety do not supply a separate anxiety measurement. [Official outcomes and posted results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01301521?tab=results)
