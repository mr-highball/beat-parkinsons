# Bok choy and fennel seed evidence

## Fennel fruit extract and reduced rotarod retention in mice

Singh and colleagues reported a fennel fruit Soxhlet extract in 2010. Healthy mice given the extract had lower rotarod retention than saline controls, with significant reductions in grip across the tested doses. Within extract-treated groups, the higher doses produced less impairment than lower doses. The study was a short animal experiment using a concentrated extract and did not measure freezing of gait or clinical balance. [Original report, Table 1](https://pharmacologyonline.silae.it/files/newsletter/2010/vol1/66.Singh.pdf)

## Seizure after a cake containing fennel essential oil

Skalli and Soulaymani Bencheikh reported a woman with previously controlled epilepsy who had a prolonged generalized seizure after eating several pieces of cake containing fennel essential oil. The report describes 45 minutes of unconsciousness and leaves the amount of oil uncertain. This case involved concentrated oil, epilepsy and an acute seizure. Its outcomes do not establish a daytime sleepiness or resting tremor effect from culinary seed. [Original case report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21865126/)
