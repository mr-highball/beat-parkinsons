# White mustard seed observations

The 1826 pamphlet and 1827 review document dissension about a popular whole-seed remedy with adverse and inconclusive accounts. The earlier indexed 1815–1819 period supplies the historical descriptions of Parkinson disease. The mustard report adds a separate account of customary practice without establishing that any participant had the condition or that anyone counted falls.
