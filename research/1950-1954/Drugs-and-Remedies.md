# Drugs and remedies

### 1950 — Lysivane for parkinsonism

A New Zealand clinical series reported varying improvement with lysivane among 16 people with parkinsonism. Most were treated as outpatients and some hospitalized participants also received physiotherapy. The investigators noted drowsiness and acknowledged that expectation could contribute to early perceived improvement. [Source](https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/2/4678/558.full.pdf)
