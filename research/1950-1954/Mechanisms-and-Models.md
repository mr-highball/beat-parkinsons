# Mechanisms and models

### 1953 — Brainstem inclusions and tangles across parkinsonian syndromes

Greenfield and Bosanquet examined brainstem pathology in 19 idiopathic cases, 10 cases with a history of encephalitis and five of uncertain cause. They found Lewy-type inclusions in all typical idiopathic cases in their series and neurofibrillary tangles in nine of the postencephalitic cases. The authors observed exceptions, recognized age differences across groups and asked for a larger series. [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC503142/)
