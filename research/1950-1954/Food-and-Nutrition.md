# Food and nutrition

### 1950 — Coffee and drowsiness during lysivane therapy

In their 16-case lysivane report, clinicians described coffee as one way they managed transient post-dose drowsiness. This incidental clinical observation concerns a reported drug adverse effect. The study did not isolate caffeine exposure or measure a change in Parkinson’s motor symptoms from coffee. [Source](https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/2/4678/558.full.pdf)
