# Risk and prevention

### 1954 — Parkinsonian symptoms during chlorpromazine treatment

Hans Steck described parkinsonian states that arose during chlorpromazine treatment. This is clinical evidence that medication exposure can precede a recognizable parkinsonian syndrome. The indexed report addresses secondary parkinsonism and offers no estimate of idiopathic Parkinson’s disease risk. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/13201893/)
