# Observations for 1950–1954

## Major observations

Independent follow-up of artery occlusion found only temporary tremor relief and limited sustained rigidity benefit in a four-case series. A separate report identified parkinsonian states appearing during chlorpromazine exposure. An autopsy series found different predominant brainstem changes in idiopathic and postencephalitic groups. ## Minor observations

The Leeds practitioner survey and bedside descriptions recorded signs across a broader population than the surgical and drug series. A lysivane case series also recorded coffee use for post-dose drowsiness. The latter report had no dietary comparator. ## Injected drug vehicle versus dietary exposure, November 15, 2026

The original 1950 psychiatric paper establishes an injected peanut-oil drug vehicle and a reported visual event in a patient with recurrent mania. The timing follows withdrawal of the active drug. The earlier 1947 case concerned topical exposure, and the 1953 oral poisoning series has no verified visual-hallucination result. These are separate records and exposures. ## Food identity in historical sources

The fig inventory distinguishes common fig accessions from entries identified only to Ficus. Anjir and closely related names occur across both groups. Later folk and research references can be searched with those names while preserving species and preparation uncertainty.
