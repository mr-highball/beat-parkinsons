# Clinical features and diagnosis

### 1952 — Signs, symptom progression and a Leeds-area survey

A British Medical Journal account incorporated a practitioner survey identifying 288 people with broadly defined parkinsonism in Leeds and nearby districts. It described early changes in handwriting and dexterity, asymmetry, and oculogyric crises in postencephalitic cases. Practitioner reporting and historical diagnostic groupings limit comparisons with modern population incidence. [Source](https://www.bmj.com/content/bmj/1/4750/153.full.pdf)
