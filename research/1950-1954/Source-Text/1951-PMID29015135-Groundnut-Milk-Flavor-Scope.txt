The archive supplies all ten printed pages 172–181. The local digitization and visual inspection identify a brief report on changing the flavor of groundnut milk. Nutmeg was among several ingredients added to boiled groundnut milk in attempts to mask its flavor. The paragraph favors other additions, and the taste comparison does not isolate nutmeg or measure constipation.
