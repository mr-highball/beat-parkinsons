Ebin reviews treatment options, surgical reports and observed
rest-tremor outcomes. The literature discussion attributes to
Bird and Meyers a neuromuscular-blocking drug suspended in
peanut oil and wax. It describes their findings for tremor
and rigidity as unfavorable for the formulation.

The account concerns a drug formulation. It supplies no
independent oral peanut-oil group or grade. The cited 1949
clinical and electromyography paper is PMID 18130078. Its
full original was subsequently authenticated in a digitized journal issue.
The 1949 authors observed reduced tremor amplitude under drug treatment
and unchanged incidence and frequency. The published interpretation was
little overall tremor benefit. The study has no oil-only comparison.
