# Visual hallucinations in a historical trial with a peanut-oil carrier

## Visual hallucinations after withdrawal of an injected active drug

Rowntree, Nevin and Wilson's February 1950 article describes peanut oil as an injectable drug vehicle on page 47. On page 62, the authors report auditory and visual hallucinations during recurrent mania in one patient two days after the active drug was withdrawn. The study supplies no oil-specific treatment estimate or pressing grade. The outcome occurred in the setting of psychiatric illness and recent exposure to a drug. It cannot identify a food effect in Parkinson disease.
