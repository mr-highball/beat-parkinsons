In the printed inventory, entries 141121–141133 are classified as common fig, Ficus carica. The records use the local name Anjir and variants such as Anjir-i-zard and Anjir-i-suja, and distinguish cultivated and wild forms and fruit descriptions. Neighboring entries also use Anjir for plants identified only to Ficus. The name and its variants are useful for searching historical sources. The shared name alone does not identify a cultivar or confirm a species when the original record is ambiguous. The inventory supplies no preparation as a remedy or measured neurological response.
