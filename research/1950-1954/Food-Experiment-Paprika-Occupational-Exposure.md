# Paprika Occupational Exposure

## Occupational inhalation during paprika processing

Donald Hunter described respiratory illness among Hungarian workers who split dried Capsicum fruit and removed the internal ribs. Late-season fruit carried fungal growth described as Mucor stolonifer, and splitting dispersed its material into workroom dust. Hunter reported chronic cough, lung damage and bronchiectasis in affected workers and reported no comparable illness among workers grinding or handling the prepared pepper. He considered the fungus the probable cause while acknowledging that spores in sputum did not prove causation. This occupational account supplies no dietary comparison, Parkinson population or dyskinesia measurement.
