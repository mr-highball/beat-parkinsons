# Procedures and devices

### 1953 — Anterior choroidal artery ligation

Irving Cooper described artery ligation for parkinsonism and other involuntary movements. This initial surgical report helped motivate larger investigations of effects on tremor and rigidity. A later independent series in this folder supplies a contemporaneous check on reproducibility. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01562492)

### 1954 — A small independent series finds inconsistent benefit from artery occlusion

Rand, Stern and Orr reported disappointing overall results in four operations. Tremor stopped temporarily in three cases and rigidity improved for a sustained period in two. The small uncontrolled series could neither identify the mechanism nor support confident predictions of benefit for individual patients. [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1532184/)
