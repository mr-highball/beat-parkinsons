# Historical nutmeg observations

## Cushny reports on drowsiness with crude nutmeg

Cushny’s original April 1908 proceedings paper summarizes Wallace’s collection of earlier poison cases and presents pharmacological context. Among the historical accounts are drowsiness and stupor after crude or grated nutmeg. This documentation describes poisoning after unusually large exposures. It supplies no systematic comparison of ordinary food use or clinical Parkinson sleepiness and should not be counted as a new patient cohort separate from Wallace’s cases.
