# Drugs and remedies, 1905-1909

### 1909 — Stewart presents hyoscine as symptomatic treatment

Purves Stewart’s teaching article described symptomatic management with hyoscine and discussed formic acid as a previously tried remedy. The article is a contemporary clinical account and provides no comparative estimate of treatment benefit.

[Stewart, The Hospital, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5200314/)
