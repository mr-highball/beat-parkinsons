# Clinical findings and diagnosis, 1905-1909

### 1909 — Harris describes a young patient with unilateral signs

Wilfred Harris reported a 27-year-old with a rigid face, a left-sided tremor, arm stiffness, and an asymmetric gait. He considered a tumor as an alternative explanation. Modern diagnostic confirmation and longitudinal follow-up were unavailable.

[Harris, Proceedings of the Royal Society of Medicine, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2046555/)
