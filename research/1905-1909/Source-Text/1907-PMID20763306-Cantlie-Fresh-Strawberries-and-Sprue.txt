Cantlie describes dietary management for tropical sprue and reports that in an unspecified subset of patients, symptoms improved within a few days on a temporarily all-fresh-strawberry regimen after prior attempts had failed. The article does not give a denominator, a prespecified outcome scale, or a controlled comparison for this observation. Exposure is fresh whole fruit and the clinical context is gastrointestinal disease. No fall, balance, or Parkinson outcome is measured in the strawberry passage.
