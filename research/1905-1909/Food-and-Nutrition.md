# Food and nutrition, 1905-1909

### 1909 — A whisky invitation illustrates temporary tremor suppression

Stewart described a patient whose tremor abated when he was invited to take a glass of whisky. The anecdote appears in a discussion of voluntary action briefly suppressing rest tremor, with no measured alcohol exposure or comparator. The direction of any dietary alcohol effect remains undetermined.

[Stewart, The Hospital, p. 512, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5200314/)
