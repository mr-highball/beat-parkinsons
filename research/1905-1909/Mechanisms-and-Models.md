# Mechanisms and models, 1905-1909

### 1906 — Parathyroid anatomy study reports a negative result

R. L. Thompson compared the number, position, size, and structure of parathyroid glands in nine paralysis agitans autopsies with findings in other autopsies. He found no distinguishing morphological change. The study addressed tissue structure and left gland function unresolved.

[Thompson, Journal of Medical Research, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2099914/)
