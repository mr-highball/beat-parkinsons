# Observations for 1940–1944

## Major observations

Pharmacological work sought to identify which component of the Bulgarian regimen produced its claimed effects. Price and Merritt highlighted weak support for superiority of the regional preparation and documented concurrent care as a source of bias. [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md)

## Minor observations

The 1942 study chose two patients for intensive observations and separated tremor and rigidity as outcomes. Putnam reported pyramidal tract surgery in people with unilateral resting tremor, and the Meyers team later acknowledged a technical concern with early deep recordings. ## Comparison with 1935–1939

[Previous observations](../../1935-1939/Observations/README.md)

The 1938 belladonna comparison raised the question of whether the geographic source mattered. Reports in this bucket tested the alkaloids more directly and questioned whether geographic origin explained the effect. The 1941 report makes exercise, nursing and occupational therapy visible as co-interventions.
