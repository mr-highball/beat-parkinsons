# Clinical features and diagnosis

### 1940 — Anatomical differential diagnosis of idiopathic and postencephalitic disease

Rudolf Klaue’s German monograph examined the clinical and anatomical distinction between paralysis agitans and postencephalitic parkinsonism. The publisher record establishes the comparative focus and original date. The full paper is needed to verify case counts and extract pathology by clinical group. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01813986)

### 1940 — Clinicopathological study of presumed acute encephalitis

A clinicopathological series reviewed 100 cases diagnosed as acute encephalitis. This adjacent evidence provides context for the challenges of defining an antecedent illness when reconstructing postencephalitic parkinsonism. The selected hospital cases do not estimate the risk of later idiopathic Parkinson’s disease. [Source](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/archneurpsyc/article-abstract/648594)
