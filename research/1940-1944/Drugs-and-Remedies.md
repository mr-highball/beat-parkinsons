# Drugs and remedies

### 1940 — Pharmacology and clinical effects of belladonna alkaloids

H. Vollmer examined belladonna-root alkaloids in the historical Bulgarian treatment. The paper attributes the activity of the multi-part regimen to its belladonna component. This early pharmacological attribution concerns a toxic medicinal plant and historical symptomatic claims. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneurpsyc.1940.02280060002001)

### 1941 — Bulgarian belladonna wine and standardized belladonna alkaloids

Price and Merritt compared the Bulgarian preparation with United States Pharmacopeia belladonna alkaloids. They reported that prior investigations with English, Italian, and German roots had failed to support the claimed superiority of Bulgarian belladonna. They also documented nursing care, exercise, and occupational therapy as co-interventions in some reports. Wine served as a preparation vehicle. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1941.02820310007004)

### 1942 — Drug effects on tremor and rigidity examined separately

Loman, P. G. Myerson and A. Myerson selected two patients with parkinsonism for intensive observations after examining a wider group. They compared established and newer drugs and explicitly sought objective evidence for separate effects on tremor and rigidity. The two selected cases limit generalization. The publisher preview verifies the design and leaves drug-specific results for full-text extraction. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneurpsyc.1942.02290030057004)
