# Procedures and devices

### 1940 — Pyramidal tract surgery for unilateral paralysis agitans

Tracy Putnam reported tract section for unilateral parkinsonian resting tremor. The article connects fluctuating tremor and the effort to relieve it without severe functional impairment, continuing his earlier surgery research in other movement disorders. The publisher’s excerpt does not give a reliable outcome count. The full report is needed to quantify benefits and neurological harms. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneurpsyc.1940.02280110024002)

### 1941 — Early caudate electrical recordings

Russell Meyers obtained electrical recordings from the human caudate nucleus and cerebral cortex during work on parkinsonism. The investigators’ 1949 follow-up says the initial apparent differences could have been influenced by recording technique. The 1941 report is an archival lead cited by Meyers himself in the later primary study. [Investigator follow-up](https://jnnp.bmj.com/content/jnnp/12/2/111.full.pdf)
