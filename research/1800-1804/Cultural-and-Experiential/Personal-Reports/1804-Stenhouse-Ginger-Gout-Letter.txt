Stenhouse credited regular ginger use with less pain during his gout attacks. He described continued weakness after the attack. His vapor-bath self-experiment appears later in the letter and remains a separate intervention. The source supplies no standardized ginger preparation or comparator.

Reported direction: Less pain during the gout attack and ongoing subsequent debility.
Authenticity: Physician self-report reprinted in a directly inspected original issue.
Efficacy evidence: One personal account with unverified diagnosis, preparation and co-exposures.
Open trail: Verify the earlier newspaper printing and preceding notices mentioned in the letter.
