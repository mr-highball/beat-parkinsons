Title: Stenhouse’s gout letter in Blegborough’s 1804 article
PMCID: PMC5674314
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5674314/
Published: 1804-07-01
Archive scope: Complete seven-page original print object, volume 12, pages 62–68. Distinct letter beginning on page 66.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Blegborough introduces an enclosed letter from Stenhouse and identifies an earlier Edinburgh Courant print on January 9. On page 66, Stenhouse reports daily use of ginger over three years and relief during the painful stage of what he called gout. He says the weakness after the attack remained prolonged. The letter then switches to his personal experiment with heat, which is a separate exposure. The ginger preparation, measured changes and modern diagnosis are unspecified. The later letter’s cited January printing remains uninspected.
