Title: Common parsley, culinary uses and reported fits in an 1804 botanical description
PMCID: PMC5674414
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5674414/
Published: 1804-12-01
Archive scope: Complete seven-page original print object, volume 12, pages 554–560. Printed pages 557–558 visually checked.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Printed page 557 identifies the culinary common or garden parsley as Apium petroselinum, with other historical names. It is next to a distinct smallage or celery entry. Page 558, attributing the discussion to Woodville, describes traditional root and seed uses for urinary symptoms and the disputed report of aggravated fits in people subject to them. It supplies no individual case description, verified preparation or graded rigidity change. The older Latin naming, urinary descriptions and fits must be read as separate contexts.
