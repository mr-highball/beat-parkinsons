# Almond oil and historical clinical practice

## Hassall on local oil use in respiratory practice

Printed pages 151 to 152 discuss olive oil or almond oil as local respiratory preparations for dry mucosa and mucus clearance. The aphonia and hoarseness paragraph directly above concerns glycerine. The almond-oil paragraph does not describe a Parkinson cohort, a patient-specific loudness outcome, oral consumption, or how the oil was obtained.
