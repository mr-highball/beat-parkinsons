# Drugs and remedies, 1885-1889

### 1888 — Heimann reports limited benefit and adverse responses in Berlin

Anton Heimann summarized trials of multiple remedies in Charité neurology patients. He reported no meaningful benefit with several agents, including curare and potassium bromide in individual cases, and described occasional short-term tremor reductions with other drugs. He also recorded marked disturbances following one treatment. The reports are uncontrolled and offer no safety or efficacy basis for those historical remedies.
