# Clinical findings and diagnosis, 1885-1889

### 1886 — Mann revisits his previously reported case and reviews electricity

Edward C. Mann revisited the combined electricity and drug case he had published in 1881 and discussed an earlier electrical cure claim by J. Russell Reynolds. The 1886 article is evidence of circulation and interpretation of those reports. The repeated case should be counted once when reviewing outcomes.

[Mann, Chicago Medical Journal and Examiner, scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9876515/)

### 1888 — Gowers describes laterality, gait, and differential diagnosis

William R. Gowers summarized his clinical experience in a neurology manual. His chapter describes unilateral onset, spread to other limbs, short quick steps, and contrasts with senile tremor. He also describes cases in which rigidity and posture were more informative than shaking. The material is descriptive and clinically selected.

[Gowers, A Manual of Diseases of the Nervous System, vol. II, pp. 589–608, scan](https://archive.org/details/b21932128_0002/page/589/mode/2up)
