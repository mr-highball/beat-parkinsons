# Procedures and devices, 1885-1889

### 1888 — Gowers cites twenty unsuccessful electrical-treatment cases

Gowers reported that he agreed with Berger’s experience in twenty patients who did not improve with electrical treatment. The historical textbook gives neither a controlled comparison nor standardized outcome measures. The account documents a substantial negative series against the period’s isolated claims of recovery.
