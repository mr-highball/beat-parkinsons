# Procedures and devices

### 1960 — Stereotactic pallidal thermolesions in 81 cases

Svennilson and colleagues evaluated thermal lesions in the pallidal region in 81 parkinsonism cases. They reported changes in tremor and rigidity related to where the lesion was placed. The surgical series contributes clinical and anatomical detail without a randomized comparator. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0447.1960.tb07606.x)
