# Clinical features and diagnosis

### 1960 — Neurological syndrome with severe postural hypotension

Shy and Drager described a clinicopathological syndrome involving postural hypotension and autonomic failure. The historically named syndrome later became part of the multiple system atrophy spectrum, an important differential for parkinsonism with marked autonomic dysfunction. The original series defines a phenotype in selected cases. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneur.1960.03840110025004)

### 1964 — Progressive supranuclear palsy distinguished clinically and at autopsy

Steele, Richardson and Olszewski described a progressive disorder with predominantly vertical gaze palsy, axial rigidity, pseudobulbar signs and brainstem degeneration. They considered that older diagnoses of arteriosclerotic parkinsonism may have included similar cases. The small series laid out a differential diagnosis that helps interpret historical case mixtures. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1001/archneur.1964.00460160003001)
