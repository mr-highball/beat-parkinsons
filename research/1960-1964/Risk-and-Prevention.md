# Risk and prevention

### 1961 — Prediction that Parkinson’s disease would largely disappear by 1980

Poskanzer and Schwab published a prediction based on their epidemiological interpretation of parkinsonism and a proposed relationship to epidemic encephalitis. The claim is recorded as a historical causal hypothesis. See the [1975–1979 observations](../1975-1979/Observations/README.md) for later evidence against the forecast. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14488169/)
