# Mechanisms and models

### 1960 — Depleted striatal dopamine in human parkinsonian brains

Herbert Ehringer and Oleh Hornykiewicz compared brain catecholamines in postmortem specimens across neurological groups. They found marked depletion of dopamine in the caudate and putamen in Parkinson’s disease and postencephalitic parkinsonism. The postmortem association supplied a biochemical rationale for replacement therapy and left the initiating causes unresolved. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01485901)
