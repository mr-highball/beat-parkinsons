The September 1960 volume 18(3) print issue gives the filing period. The SciELO digitization timestamp is 2013-12-05.

This contemporary Portuguese synopsis names the 1960 NEJM article and describes its five low-magnesium cases as having nutritional or absorption deficits or chronic alcohol use. It reports a reduction in tetany after parenteral magnesium. Normalizing magnesium did not prevent subsequent delirium in the account. The synopsis does not specify hallucination modality or episode counts. Treat it as secondary reporting on the same five patients, with clinical detail and timing still dependent on the NEJM original.
