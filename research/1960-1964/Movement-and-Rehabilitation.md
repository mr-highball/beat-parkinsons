# Movement and rehabilitation

### 1962 — Physiotherapy methods for Parkinson’s disease

A New England Journal of Medicine clinical article described methods and argued for the value of physiotherapy in Parkinson’s disease. This is a contemporary account of supportive rehabilitation. Controlled estimates of the benefit of particular protocols cannot be inferred from its practice discussion. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM196204262661709)
