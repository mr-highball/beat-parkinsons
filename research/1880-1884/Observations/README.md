# Observations for 1880-1884

## Major observations

- Hammond’s American textbook distinguished paralysis agitans from other movement disorders, and Breslau theses tested proposed pathological and metabolic signs. Gürtler did not reproduce urinary phosphate excess in his three cases. [Clinical source](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md) · [Mechanisms](../Mechanisms-and-Models.md)

## Minor observations

- Mann’s claim of recovery involved multiple treatments and a potentially different diagnosis. Erlenmeyer recorded distinct sleep, sweating, and tremor responses, including a failed drug trial and reported toxicity. [Remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md)

## Comparison with 1875–1879

- Saint-Léger documented transient relief and questioned some electrical cures. Mann’s paper gives a new recovery claim with similar diagnostic uncertainty, while Erlenmeyer separates short-lived symptom responses from long-term change. [Prior observations](../../1875-1879/Observations/README.md)
