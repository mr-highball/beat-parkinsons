# Mechanisms and models, 1880-1884

### 1882 — Gürtler finds no consistent urinary phosphate excess

Gustav Gürtler measured urinary constituents in three people with paralysis agitans and found no excess phosphate excretion in his series. One patient had polyuria. He cautioned against drawing conclusions about nervous-tissue metabolism from urinary phosphate and nitrogen ratios. The series gives an early negative replication of a proposed metabolic feature.

[Gürtler, Ueber Veränderungen im Stoffwechsel unter dem Einfluss der Hypnose und bei der Paralysis agitans, scan](https://archive.org/details/IA41543101_0076)

### 1882 — Wienskowitz documents a postmortem case without a gross focal brain finding

Oscar Wienskowitz’s Breslau thesis combines a prolonged clinical case history with postmortem findings. He described severe late immobility and found no clear difference in the gross size or shape of examined brain regions. The autopsy and methods do not rule out focal microscopic disease.

[Wienskowitz, Beitrag zur Lehre von der Paralysis agitans, scan](https://archive.org/details/IA40153704_0276)
