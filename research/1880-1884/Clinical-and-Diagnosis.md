# Clinical findings and diagnosis, 1880-1884

### 1881 — Hammond teaches paralysis agitans alongside similar disorders

William A. Hammond’s American neurology treatise distinguished paralysis agitans from multiple cerebrospinal sclerosis and chorea using the movement pattern, sensory findings, and clinical history. His description reflects bedside diagnosis and an era-specific disease classification.
