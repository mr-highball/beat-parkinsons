Title: Indische spruw (Aphthae tropicae), eene monographie
Author: C. L. van der Burg
Source: https://archive.nsgh.nl/gtni-deel-xxi-1881
Publication date: 1880
Date basis: Monograph title leaf reads Batavia, Ernst & Co., 1880. The society archive catalogs the enclosing journal volume as 1881.
Text coverage: Distilled from the society volume, especially printed pages 62–65. Verified the imprinted year and the strawberry passage on the scanned pages. Physical PDF page 83 is printed page 63.

Van der Burg reports personally seeing clinical improvements under the fruit-based practice of A. Sonius and then using mixed fruit regimens himself. He cautiously attributes the earliest regular local use to Sonius. He notes that he had not seen a formal publication by Sonius explaining the method.

The account places strawberries and mulberries in water early in some regimens. It says they were often tolerated and could also be irritating, which the author attributed to their seeds. Other foods and drinks were used, and the detailed pediatric case on the following page initially received peaches, apricots and cucumber water. That case cannot be assigned to the strawberries. There is no denominator, isolated strawberry comparison, Parkinson cohort or fall outcome.
