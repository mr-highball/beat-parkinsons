# Tarragon odor follow-up

## Tarragon and thyme in an olfactory reversal task

Roddick and colleagues assessed 18 successive odor pairs in 3xTg-AD mice and B6129F2/J controls. Pair 7 presented tarragon and thyme odorants diluted in mineral oil. Odor delivery was through a sampling port. The investigators randomized the rewarded identity and reversed its assignment after acquisition. The downloadable study records give mean discrimination and reversal errors of 7.40 and 7.47 in the 15 Alzheimer-model mice and 7.83 and 15.22 in the 18 controls for pair 7. Three model mice and one control made at most one error on that reversal. These are descriptive reconstructions from the author data using the published error definition. Group differences combine genotype, age and experience accumulated in a fixed sequence. The authors' inferential results do not isolate tarragon from thyme or an odor-pair position effect. The long-term retest was on pair 18. There was no eating arm and the target disease was Alzheimer’s.
