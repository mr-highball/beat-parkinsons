# Procedures and Devices

### 2022 — Continuous subcutaneous foslevodopa-foscarbidopa

A 12-week phase 3 randomized trial in advanced disease found more on time without troublesome dyskinesia and less off time with continuous infusion compared with oral immediate-release treatment. Infusion-site reactions and discontinuations were more frequent with the infusion. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36402160/)

### 2023 — Globus pallidus focused ultrasound

A sham-controlled trial in selected participants found a higher three-month clinical response rate with unilateral focused ultrasound ablation. Some participants developed gait, speech or visual adverse effects. The technique creates a permanent lesion. [Primary source](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2202721)

### 2024 — Adaptive stimulation feasibility

In four people with Parkinson’s and implanted stimulators, a blinded crossover feasibility study found improvements in the most bothersome symptom with personalized adaptive stimulation. The small sample and specialized setup limit generalization. [Primary source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-024-03196-z)

### 2024 — Fecal microbiota transplantation trials diverge

The small GUT-PARFECT trial reported a favorable motor-score change with donor transplant versus autologous control. A separate randomized Finnish study found no advantage on its primary symptom outcome. Controls, delivery and protocols differed, so the results leave efficacy unresolved. [Primary source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11056595/) [Related primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11287445/)
