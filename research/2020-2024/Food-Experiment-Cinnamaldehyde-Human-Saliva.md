## Cinnamaldehyde rinse and saliva compared with vehicle

Houghton and colleagues posted a preprint in June 2020 and published the peer-reviewed report online July 10. Healthy adults rinsed with different sensory compounds and vehicle. Figure 1's whole-mouth results are from six participants. Cinnamaldehyde did not increase flow beyond its propylene glycol vehicle, although the rinse and vehicle both increased from unstimulated levels. Menthol and nonivamide increased flow beyond their vehicle contrasts. The preprint and journal are one study family. The experiment measures oral secretion after isolated compounds, with no ground cinnamon, oral escape, or Parkinson drooling.
