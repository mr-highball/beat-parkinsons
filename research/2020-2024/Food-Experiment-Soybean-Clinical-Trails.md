# Soybean oil and clinical comparators

## Soybean oil comparator in the Tabriz Parkinson trial

Mohammadi and colleagues' publisher record dates online publication to 6 August 2023. The four-author report lists Tabriz University of Medical Sciences and the Valiasr Hospital neurology department. It describes 44 Parkinson participants divided between eight weeks of marine omega-3 and a placebo of two soybean-oil soft capsules daily. The linked 2018 IRCT record identifies Zahravi Pharmaceutical Company as the soybean capsule supplier. The available sources give no soybean oil grade, oil mass or specific lot. They do not report actual recruitment dates or the number analyzed per arm.

The published results concern weight, BMI, lipid measures and circulating IL-6, TNF-α and IL-10. The abstract reports no statistically significant adjusted between-group changes and no change in total cholesterol. The registered physical activity measure was the Rapid Assessment of Physical Activity questionnaire at baseline only. No gait, dyskinesia, resting tremor, off-time diary, fall, swallowing, saliva, voice, sleepiness, dystonia or apathy result appears in the accessible report or registered outcome list. The full report required publisher access. No companion clinical outcomes report was verified.

The paper and registry share the investigator, planned sample size, intervention and comparator structure and biomarker outcomes. The registry records EPA 180 mg and DHA 120 mg, while the abstract gives EPA 120 mg and DHA 180 mg per active capsule. IL-1β was registered and omitted from the abstract. The registry's stated recruitment site is Imam Reza Hospital. The paper gives the neurologist's Valiasr Hospital affiliation. The registered trial's ethics identifier, design, counts and authors distinguish it from the observational 120-Parkinson/50-control diet cohort with an Isfahan recruitment trail.
