# Kiwi feeding studies

## Whole kiwi and prediabetes reported outcomes

February 25, 2022. The parallel trial compares two peeled whole kiwifruit with bottled water daily for 12 weeks. There were 34 randomized and 32 completers. The primary measures were HbA1c and plasma ascorbate. The reported group separation favored kiwi for plasma vitamin C and the HbA1c comparison was inconclusive. Other outcomes include blood pressure and dietary and metabolic measures. This is a separate trial from the kiwi breakfast-pulp cognition crossover. No objective attention task or Parkinson group is reported. [Original source](https://www.lidsen.com/journals/rpn/rpn-02-01-006) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2022-Mishra-Whole-Kiwi-Prediabetes-Outcomes.txt)

## Golden kiwi single-arm duration discrepancy

Published in a 2020 supplement with the J-STAGE public record released February 22, 2021. Twenty-two young adults consumed two golden kiwifruit daily without a parallel control. The abstract and results describe six weeks, while the Methods on S356 describe four weeks. Outcomes include anthropometry, inflammation, lipids and blood pressure. The comparison gives no standalone effect estimate and does not measure attention. [Original source](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnsv/66/Supplement/66_S356/_article) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2020-Yang-Golden-Kiwi-Single-Arm.txt)
