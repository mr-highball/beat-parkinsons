# Pirot survey: earlier supplementary savory records

## A 2023 source for the Pirot savory reports

Marković and colleagues’ broader Pirot survey was published online on 27 July 2023. Its supplementary plant table records five reports of dried *Satureja hortensis* seasoning; the three sore-throat reports belong to *S. montana*. The 2024 savory-focused report draws on the same 631-informant survey, so these are not independent observations. Neither source tests fresh-leaf intake or Parkinson speech.
