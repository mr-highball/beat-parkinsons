# Anise seed symptom and safety evidence, 2020–2024

## Repeated oral anise seed extract and observed drowsiness in mice

Only the highest repeated-dose arm in a 28-day study of an oral aqueous extract from powdered Pimpinella seeds had drowsiness and reduced spontaneous motor activity in Table 4. The report records group-level observations and gives no affected count or daytime severity measurement. This is a safety signal for concentrated extract in mice. [Primary journal Methods and Table 4](https://asjp.cerist.dz/en/downArticle/255/11/2/173040) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2021-Fenaghra-Khali-Anise-Drowsiness.txt)

## Hot flash outcome and registration discordance in the Tehran trial

The 2022 paper reports eight-week hot flash results for ethanol-extract capsules and states that no side effects were reported. No sleepiness, insomnia or dream enactment measure is given. The cited 2011 registry lists a four-week treatment and a decade-earlier expected recruitment interval. [Primary article and declared protocol link](https://brieflands.com/journals/semj/articles/117385) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2022-Zare-Anise-Hot-Flashes-Registry-Discordance.txt)

## Perioperative tea blend measured anxiety after surgery

The study compares a chamomile, passionflower and Pimpinella tea blend with lemon-flavored tea in elective surgery. The abstract reports anxiety and attitudes to surgery. It does not report measured sedation or daytime sleepiness, and the ingredients were assigned together. [Original study abstract and publication metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39514279/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2024-Zampieri-Perioperative-Anise-Blend.txt)


## Early public listing of the National Institute of Unani Medicine anise trial

The 2023 parliamentary record establishes a public listing of the named NIUM randomized study. The NIUM supervisor list identifies Arfa Begum. A 2025 review citing the dissertation reports results only in aggregate and has ambiguous adverse-event reporting. The 2026 articles give methods and results separately.
