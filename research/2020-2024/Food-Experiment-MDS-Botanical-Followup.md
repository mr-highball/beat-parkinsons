# Earlier botanical studies traced through the 2026 Movement Disorder Society congress

## Hydroalcoholic Eclipta extract and MPP-induced motor changes

Bhatia, Singh and Nehru first published their study on November 3, 2020. They reported fewer behavioral deficits in male Wistar rats pretreated with a hydroalcoholic *Eclipta alba* extract before intrastriatal MPP⁺, along with cell experiments. Figure 4 compares six groups and reports n = 10. The plotted MPP⁺ group reached about 32 seconds on rotarod and 21 seconds in catalepsy. One comparison group marked as improved versus MPP⁺ reached about 76 seconds and 10 seconds, respectively. These are figure estimates. The public article abstract and figure do not specify the dose and route of extract or fully identify the numbered arms. This experiment used a plain extract before toxin exposure in a rat model. [Original journal article, PMID 33145730](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11356-020-11452-1) · [Original Figure 4 image](https://media.springernature.com/full/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1007%2Fs11356-020-11452-1/MediaObjects/11356_2020_11452_Fig4_HTML.png) · [Figure caption](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Effects-of-EA-MEx-pre-treatment-on-MPP-induced-behavioral-alterations-in-rats-a-Rotarod_fig4_345267282).

## Injected aqueous Eclipta and catalepsy

Adegoke et al. published an antipsychotic pharmacology study on January 4, 2024. In Table 3, mice received intraperitoneal aqueous *Eclipta alba* extract at 100, 200 or 400 mg/kg and haloperidol 2 mg/kg. At 60 minutes each combination group had a mean catalepsy score of 3.33 ± 0.17 compared with 1.25 ± 0.11 with haloperidol alone and 0.17 ± 0.11 with vehicle. At 120 minutes the respective combination scores were 2.17 ± 0.21, 2.17 ± 0.21 and 3.17 ± 0.21, compared with 0.67 ± 0.17 with haloperidol alone. The table's significance marks use vehicle as comparator, and the Methods and Table 3 report n = 5 and n = 6, respectively. In a separate novelty assay, injected extract lowered spontaneous locomotion compared with vehicle. The observed catalepsy direction differs from the oral preparations tested in 2014 and 2018. Route, preparation, animal protocol and comparator differ as well. [Original article](https://journaljamps.com/index.php/JAMPS/article/view/663) · [Local licensed full text, Table 3](Source-Text/2024-Adegoke-Eclipta-Aqueous-Extract.txt#L714) · [Publisher PDF archived January 21, 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20240121205609id_/https://journaljamps.com/index.php/JAMPS/article/download/663/1318) · [2014 oral rat study](../2010-2014/Food-and-Nutrition.md#2014--eclipta-leaf-extract-and-drug-induced-movement-in-rats) · [2018 oral mouse study](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-MDS-Botanical-Followup.md#oral-eclipta-and-holy-basil-extracts-in-drug-induced-models).

## Pueraria tuber extracts in haloperidol-treated rats

Vasava et al. published a seven-arm experiment with six male Wistar rats per group on December 30, 2022. With haloperidol 1 mg/kg intraperitoneally for 15 days, the oral interventions were ethanolic *Pueraria tuberosa* tuber extracts at 150 and 300 mg/kg or aqueous extracts at 200 and 400 mg/kg. With 300 mg/kg ethanolic extract, day-14 rotarod time was 55.833 ± 5.269 seconds versus 32.166 ± 2.714 seconds with haloperidol alone (Table 3), and day-15 catalepsy time was 72.580 ± 3.125 versus 138.785 ± 2.753 seconds (Table 5). The 400 mg/kg aqueous extract gave 50.500 ± 5.319 and 91.51 ± 3.475 seconds, respectively. These results concern pharmacological extract preparations and drug-induced motor impairment. [Primary article, Tables 3 and 5](https://doi.org/10.54085/ap.2022.11.2.37) · [Publisher PDF](https://www.ukaazpublications.com/publications/?download_id=10230&sdm_process_download=1) · [Later Friedreich ataxia fly study](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-MDS-Botanical-Followup.md#pueraria-extract-and-larval-locomotion-in-friedreich-ataxia).

## Dayak onion bulb solvent fractions

Lubis et al. collected fresh *Eleutherine bulbosa* bulbs in North Sumatra, Indonesia and used the Medanense Herbarium for morphological authentication. In their February 2024 report, chemical screening, thin-layer chromatography and FTIR characterized fractions from dried bulb powder. The authors report extract yields of 11% for ethanol, 10% for hexane and 16.7% for ethyl acetate. These are solvent extraction yields. The tests classify constituents and functional groups without identifying an exact methylanthraquinone isomer or quantifying eleutherin. The study measured chemistry without a Parkinson model or patient outcomes. [Original report, DOI 10.5530/pj.2024.16.12](https://www.phcogj.com/article/2213) · [Local licensed article text](Source-Text/2024-Lubis-Dayak-Onion-Phytochemical-Profile.txt) · [2026 docking and LC–MS follow-up](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-MDS-Botanical-Followup.md#dayak-onion-docking-and-bulb-chemistry).

## Levodopa nasal spray patent and excipient identity

Novaken's CN117503703A, published February 6, 2024, and WIPO counterpart WO2025098365A1, published May 15, 2025, cover intranasal levodopa formulations. The Chinese application gives dodecyl beta-D-maltoside as the preferred absorption enhancer and lists chitosan and other pharmaceutical alternatives. It also names microcrystalline cellulose–carboxymethyl cellulose and polysorbate 80. The applications and the later XJN010 public reports share the sponsor and some investigators, but no public source establishes the exact relationship between a patent example and the clinical product. The patent family identifies no individual culinary oil or food intervention. [Chinese application](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN117503703A/en) · [WIPO publication](https://patentscope.wipo.int/search/en/detail.jsf?docId=WO2025098365) · [Dated clinical report](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-MDS-Botanical-Followup.md#xjn010-nasal-spray-and-fed-oral-comparator).

## Velvet bean with levodopa in a Mexican case

Botello-Villagrana and Martinez-Ramirez published an eight-session, single-patient comparison on October 1, 2021. The 42-year-old woman had motor fluctuations and dyskinesia. With 10 g or 20 g of *Mucuna pruriens* and different levodopa regimens, the reported mean times to ON were 47.3 and 39.7 minutes, respectively, and ON duration averaged 214.3 and 241.0 minutes. The levodopa/carbidopa-only session ended when she had not reached ON by 120 minutes. Dyskinesia severity was similar across the bean conditions, and nausea, confusion and visual hallucinations were reported during 20 g sessions. This uncontrolled case with differing concomitant levodopa doses gives no population effect estimate. [Mexican original, figures 1–2 on p. 182, DOI 10.24875/RMN.21000010](https://www.revmexneurociencia.com/portadas/rmn_21_22_5.pdf) · [Earlier Argentine seed analysis](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-MDS-Botanical-Followup.md#levodopa-in-ground-argentine-velvet-beans).

## Bacopa extract and quality of life in a Brazilian pilot

Fuentes dos Santos and colleagues first published this pilot on February 15, 2023. Twenty people with Parkinson disease were assigned to a commercial *Bacopa monnieri* dry extract at 225 mg/day or 450 mg/day or placebo for 90 days. The original abstract reports no clear group difference in parkinsonian symptoms or emotional, social or systemic PDQL domains. It separately reports a time-dependent percentage change in the emotional domain at 450 mg/day. The accessible primary text does not give the effect estimate or a registration identifier. The MDS review table identifies the extract as Cognitus and reports no clear MDS-UPDRS motor difference. No frozen statement names this plant. [Primary report, PMID 36800346, DOI 10.1089/jmf.2022.0106](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1089/jmf.2022.0106) · [2026 review's source table](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/0988_1474_000516_3.jpg).

## Olive oil carrier in a Canadian cannabinoid pain study

Di Luca et al.'s May 8, 2023 report randomized eight people with Parkinson disease and bothersome pain to three oral cannabis extracts with THC:CBD ratios of 10:10 (n = 3), 18:0 (n = 2) and 1:20 (n = 3). All preparations used the same olive oil carrier. The study ended early for logistical reasons. Table 2 gives mean changes in King's Parkinson Disease Pain Scale of −8.0, −14.0 and −1.3 and mean changes in MDS-UPDRS Part III of −2.3, −10.5 and +6.3. These are descriptive changes in small active-treatment groups. Table 3 lists one report of constipation and one of anxiety in the 10:10 group. The grade of olive oil was unspecified and there was no oil-free group. The study therefore gives no independent estimate for [0072, extra-virgin olive oil and constipation](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0072.txt) or [0343, cold-pressed olive oil and anxiety](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/statements/0343.txt). The Canadian registry lists 15 planned patients and two centers. The published dose-finding study enrolled eight at a single center under the same protocol identifier and used the same three formulation ratios. [Primary article, Tables 2–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10354611/) · [ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03639064](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03639064) · [Health Canada registry](https://clinical-trials-search.canada.ca/en/clinical-trial/215421-parkinson-s-disease).
