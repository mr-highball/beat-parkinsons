# Olive Oil Rotarod Vehicles

## Olive oil vehicle in a later rat motor experiment

Chen and colleagues tested T-006 in MPTP mice and 6-OHDA rats. In the rat comparison, the T-006 and L-DOPA treatments used olive oil and sham and lesion groups received equivalent oil volumes. The authors report longer rotarod retention and greater open-field distance with T-006 in lesioned rats. No cold-pressed identity or clinical postural-instability outcome was established. The distinct MPTP experiment used a saline vehicle for rasagiline and should not be pooled with this rat contrast.
