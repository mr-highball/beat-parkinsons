# MEDI PD Constipation

## Official registration of MEDI-PD constipation outcomes

The MEDI-PD registration first posted December 24, 2020 and reports study completion June 7, 2022. The current record gives 46 actual enrollees. The registered primary endpoint is final-minus-baseline change in the GSRS constipation syndrome. The study timeline includes a two-week baseline and an eight-week randomized intervention. Secondary outcomes include stool frequency, stool form and laxative use. The record shows no posted summary results. The separate journal protocol describes Mediterranean diet counseling with cooking and dressing oil advice, and the 2024 result reports include 36 analyzed participants. ## Published participant flow and dietary supplement results

Rusch and colleagues first published the main article on September 2, 2024. Figure 1 shows 46 assessed, one excluded before randomization and 45 randomly assigned, 21 to standard care and 24 to Mediterranean diet plus standard care. Four and five, respectively, did not receive their assigned intervention. The paper labels the remaining 17 and 19 in each arm as its intention-to-treat analysis. Median constipation change was −0.8 in control and −0.5 with dietary counseling, p=.600. The [original supplement](https://www.mdpi.com/article/10.3390/nu16172946/s1) reports reflux, indigestion and diarrhea in Table S1. Table S2 reports group-by-week p=.004 for oleic acid intake, a whole-diet nutrient estimate. It gives no olive-oil quantity or grade. Table S5 reports a HAM-A group-by-week p=.745, leaving a between-arm change unclear. The diet pattern and constipation care cannot identify a separate effect of oil, dark chocolate, or another food.
