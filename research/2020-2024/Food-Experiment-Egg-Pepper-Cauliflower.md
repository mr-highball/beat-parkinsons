# Egg, bell pepper and cauliflower studies, 2020–2024

## Pooled cruciferous vegetables and anxiety in healthcare workers

Göbel and Doğan Güney surveyed 153 healthcare workers. Table 5 gives a Spearman correlation of −.231 (p = .004) between a combined cauliflower, cabbage and broccoli food item and DASS-21 anxiety. The numerical coding of reported food-consumption status could not be established. Cauliflower alone, roasting and Parkinson disease were outside the comparison. [Original article](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/2840831)

## Eggs and cauliflower in Parkinson diet studies

Hegelmaier and colleagues' two-week Parkinson diet menu lists eggs and cauliflower, alongside many other foods. The study reports composite motor results. The underlying scale includes speech, gait and postural stability, without separate egg intake, culinary preparation or item-level comparisons. Cauliflower's proposed outcomes are anxiety and drooling. They were outside the reported motor subscale. In Gatti and colleagues' observational Nutri-Park study, the example dinner lists two eggs as one possible protein portion. Walking speed was measured, and an analysis of reported protein timing included falls, with no egg-specific estimate. [Hegelmaier Appendix A](Source-Text/PMC7072275.txt#L285) · [Nutri-Park results and example menu](Food-Experiment-French-Reference-Followup.md)

## Egg recipe provision and physical performance

Van den Heuvel and colleagues first published the Bournemouth trial on September 1, 2020. The questionnaire separately asked about poached and scrambled egg in one section and pooled boiled with poached egg in another. Scrambled eggs were among the provided recipe examples. The Short Physical Performance Battery included an 8-foot walk, standing balance and chair stands. The paper reports no clear intervention group difference on the physical performance battery at either follow-up and gives no walking-speed estimate for a specific preparation. These participants were community-dwelling older adults. The study had no Parkinson-specific symptom comparison. [Primary full text](Source-Text/PMC8145475.txt) · [Dated registration](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Egg-Pepper-Cauliflower.md#egg-recipe-trial-registration-and-early-results)

## Withdrawn high-protein egg diet comparison

NCT04264013 was first posted February 11, 2020. It proposed comparing a high-protein egg diet with a high-protein diet without eggs among older adults participating in exercise. Listed outcomes cover protein intake, lean mass, strength, vascular health and fat mass. The official record says withdrawn due to funding, with zero participants and no results. [Official record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04264013) · [Selected registry fields](Source-Text/Trials/NCT04264013.txt)

## Cauliflower and oral measures in a Geneva Parkinson cohort

Fleury and colleagues' 2021 congress abstract P38 pooled cauliflower and broccoli as one food-frequency item and reported an association with salivary bacterial composition and unspecified chronic constipation measures. It reports no separate cauliflower value. The February 2021 journal article from the same center, with matching Parkinson and control sample sizes and stage, reports median Sialorrhea Clinical Scale scores of 1.5 for 20 Parkinson participants and 0 for 20 controls (p < .001). It also reports an anxiety measure. Neither report links its clinical anxiety or drooling scores to cauliflower intake. The reports are treated as probably overlapping. [Congress abstract P38](https://www.mdpi.com/2514-183X/5/3/22) · [Journal article, Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8150470/) · [Earlier local dietary notes](Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md)

## Red bell pepper extract and Trail Making in older adults

Shirai and Wadazumi's January 1, 2022 article describes a four-week before–after study of oral xanthophyll capsules extracted from red bell pepper in 31 adults. The Trail Making Test B comparison between visits was p = .039 before the exercise session and p = .061 after exercise. All 31 received the supplement, so repeated testing and time could contribute to changes. The 2022 UMIN registration was public February 1, after the paper. The registry cites an identical title and enrollment and marks results published. It is counted as the same study. The experiment used an extract, and no grilled pepper or Parkinson executive-function comparison was reported. [Primary full text, Figure 6](Source-Text/PMC8775015.txt) · [Official UMIN record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000052574) · [Selected registry fields](Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000046785.txt)

## Isolated egg white protein and alpha-synuclein in vitro

Vaneyck and colleagues' February 2, 2021 paper reports that preformed hen egg white lysozyme fibrils shortened the lag phase of alpha-synuclein aggregation in buffer, while the comparison fibrils from bovine beta-lactoglobulin did not. The study used isolated proteins and gives no evidence of exposure to the fibrils after eating cooked eggs, human symptom effects or Parkinson risk after consumption. [Primary full text](Source-Text/PMC7949133.txt)

### Original pilot cohort and Trail Making values

Shirai and Wadazumi’s 31-person single-arm pilot is the same cohort as UMIN000046785. For Trail Making B, means fell from 50.1 to 45.2 seconds before exercise (p = .039) and from 42.2 to 41.7 seconds after exercise (p = .061). The prose uses stronger wording for the latter than its p-value supports. The protocol paired repeated tasks and exercise with extract supplementation in all participants. [Original licensed text](Source-Text/PMC8775015.txt) · [Official registry record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000052574)
## Paprika xanthophyll with exercise in two student experiments

The 2021 JPFSM program abstract 464 describes 13 university students tested under five conditions. Trail Making B improved before versus after dual-task exercise with a carbohydrate mouth rinse whether or not paprika xanthophyll was added. Stroop improved following several conditions, including exercise alone. The separate 2022 program abstract 450 reports 17 students and seven conditions. Only its three-component regimen improved Trail Making before versus after testing at night. Neither report gives a direct statistical comparison isolating the addition of paprika xanthophyll. [2021 original program](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jpfsm/10/6/10_398/_pdf/-char/en) · [2022 original program](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jpfsm/11/6/11_427/_pdf) · [Local original factual extracts](Source-Text/2021-2022-Paprika-Cognition-Proceedings-Factual-Extract.txt)

## Paprika xanthophyll and mouth-rinse adult registration

UMIN000054104 was first disclosed on April 15, 2024. It registers a separate parallel study in 40 healthy midlife adults with cognitive testing, paprika xanthophyll and carbohydrate mouth rinse. The record marks recruitment completed and results unpublished. The age group, design and timeline distinguish it from the university student abstracts.
