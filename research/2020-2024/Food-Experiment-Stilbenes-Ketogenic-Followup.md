# Coconut oil and MCT caregiver reports

## 2023 and 2024 caregiver reports on coconut oil and MCT oil

Mary Newport presented a collection of 288 unsolicited reports at the July 2023 Alzheimer’s Association International Conference in Amsterdam. The abstract was published December 25, 2023. The journal website and Crossref date the expanded article April 26, 2024. Its PDF gives April 30, 2024 and misdates the Amsterdam poster to 2024. The poster number P3-582, sample size and outcomes identify the same collection. [Author’s conference report and poster](https://coconutketones.com/aaic-2023-poster-presentations/) · [Conference abstract](https://doi.org/10.1002/alz.079090) · [Article record](https://doi.org/10.18103/mra.v12i4.5316) · [Crossref dates](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.18103/mra.v12i4.5316)

Table 3 classifies 5.9% of the reports as Parkinson’s with Alzheimer’s or other memory impairment. The author did not verify diagnoses or administer symptom tests. Table 1 groups caregiver descriptions of less tremor, stiffness, excessive sleep and pain, and better gait, balance, energy and cognition across diagnoses. Figure 2 gives pooled outcome totals. The paper does not link these reports to the Parkinson subgroup or to a particular oil or grade. The narrative oil-use proportions are 72% coconut oil alone, 26% both coconut and MCT oils, and 2% MCT oil alone. Figure 2 gives different proportions of 71.8%, 25.7% and 3.5%. The individually described participant had early-onset Alzheimer’s disease.
