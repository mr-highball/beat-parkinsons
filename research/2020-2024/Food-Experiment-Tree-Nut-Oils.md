# Tree nut oil clinical and experimental results

## Macadamia oil as the RRIPP-3 active comparator

Marriott and colleagues (2021) enrolled 555 young military officers and compared encapsulated macadamia nut oil with concentrated krill oil during training. The intention-to-treat analysis did not show significant treatment group differences for the executive-control tests, Spielberger State Anxiety Inventory (p = .8611), the Profile of Mood States elated/depressed scale (p = .2103), or the measured resilience, fatigue and sleep-related outcomes. Sleep-related measures do not diagnose insomnia or capture REM dream enactment. The paper reports a high level of capsule nonadherence. There is no comparison with no oil, no cold-pressed formulation specified and no Parkinson cohort. [Local primary text, Results and Appendix B](Source-Text/PMC8228047.txt#L158) · [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8228047/) · [Earlier registration and protocol](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#rripp-3-macadamia-oil-comparator-protocol)

The comparison assigned macadamia oil to 279 participants and krill oil to 276, with 245 completing the study. The original marksmanship measure was intended to capture visual psychomotor performance, but scoring changed and the authors could not test the planned comparison adequately. The four-choice serial reaction-time group-by-time result was inconclusive (p=.7068). This is an attention and reaction-time measure. The trial supplies no fine hand dexterity result and leaves preparation grade unspecified. [Original Outcome Measures and Results](Source-Text/PMC8228047.txt) · [Study registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02908932)

## Macadamia oil and colitis stool consistency in mice

Shan and colleagues first published September 30, 2024, before the March 2025 issue. Pressed macadamia oil by gavage lowered the composite disease activity index in a dextran sulfate sodium mouse model. The score combines body mass change, stool consistency, and stool bleeding. The oil extraction used a hydraulic press, with cold-press temperature unverified. The study models acute colitis and loose stool, with no constipation or Parkinson outcome. [Primary report, Methods and Figure 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11832958/)

## Cold pressed walnut oil and fly climbing after rotenone

Avallone and colleagues (September 2024) fed adult male flies a diet containing unrefined cold-pressed Juglans regia walnut oil, rotenone, both, or neither. Oil alone had no clear locomotor effect at day three. The paper reports more rapid climbing after oil plus toxin than with toxin alone at both day three and day seven. The combined arm remained between the toxin-alone and untreated groups at day seven. This is a locomotor task in an invertebrate toxin model. The study measured neither clinical slowness, falling episodes, dystonia, rigidity nor dyskinesia. [Local primary text, Figure 3 and Section 3.1](Source-Text/PMC11397333.txt#L105) · [Primary article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39275038/)

## Walnut oil and survival in a rotenone cell model

Avallone, Franchini and colleagues published a separate cell experiment in December 2024. In HMC3 cultures, two walnut oil cotreatment levels increased reported cell viability by 12% and 17% versus rotenone alone at the reported time point. At higher oil-only concentrations, viability fell. A companion neuronal assay and conditioned-media work provide mechanistic results. This is the same research program as the fly paper with a different experiment and no behavioral outcomes. [Local primary text, Figure 2](Source-Text/PMC11643845.txt#L130) · [Primary article](https://www.mdpi.com/1420-3049/29/23/5718)

## Walnut oil and scopolamine memory models

Liao and colleagues (2020) reported shorter Morris water maze escape latency after oral walnut oil in scopolamine-treated mice. Qian and colleagues (2024) compared aqueous enzymatic extraction with cold pressing in mice. The abstract reports shorter water-maze escape latency and greater active avoidance with the enzyme-extracted oil than with the cold-pressed oil. Group estimates and the cold-pressed oil versus vehicle contrast remain open pending the full article. Additional independent scopolamine reports use memory and avoidance assays. These endpoints and models cannot establish clinical executive function in Parkinson's. [Local 2020 primary text, Figure 1](Source-Text/PMC7180932.txt#L70) · [2024 preparation comparison](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejlt.202300233) · [Separate 2024 Oil Crop Science report](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ocsci.2024.09.003) · [2023 Food & Function report](https://doi.org/10.1039/D3FO01893H) · [2024 Chinese primary journal record](https://opaj.napstic.cn/periodicalArticle/0120241100806720)

## Hazelnut challenge swallowing symptoms and product distinction

In a Dutch adult diagnostic study first published in 2021, the assessment criteria for a blinded hazelnut challenge include difficulty swallowing as a subjective allergic symptom. Whole nut was used in the open challenge stage, and the study did not test toasted oil or report a Parkinson-specific swallowing effect. A separate Italian pediatric investigation of the Cor a 15 oil-body protein used serum recognition and clinical histories of reactions to roasted nut foods, not oral administration of expressed oil. [Adult primary study](https://doi.org/10.1111/all.15166) · [Pediatric primary study](https://doi.org/10.1111/pai.13579) · [Earlier gourmet oils study](../1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#gourmet-oils-and-residual-allergen-binding)

## Hazelnut oil as a topical comparison for tactile sensitivity

Samain-Aupic and colleagues (2023) compared a hazelnut seed base oil with the same oil plus added aromatic compounds in 44 healthy women. Touch detection and finger spatial discrimination improved only in the perfumed-oil group after daily application for 30 days. Both groups received hazelnut oil. The study applied oil to skin and did not measure Parkinson symptoms, bulbar function, hand motor dexterity or clinical pain. [Local primary text](Source-Text/PMC10310855.txt) · [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10310855/)

## Macadamia oil as a comparator in the fibromyalgia trial registry and dissertation

The completed crossover UMIN000043490 first became public on March 3, 2021, following the December 2020 last follow-up recorded in the registry. Its plan includes pain, depression and State-Trait Anxiety Inventory (STAI) measures. The posted result reports no clear STAI difference between treatments and provides no numerical anxiety, pain or depression results. The linked 2025 journal article identifies topical macadamia nut oil as the base in both arms. [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000043490.txt) · [Official record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000042893) · [Government mirror](https://rctportal.mhlw.go.jp/detail/um?trial_id=UMIN000043490)

Masubuchi’s Japanese dissertation for the university’s 2021 academic year reports the same five analyzed participants and compares topical macadamia oil with the same oil plus geranium. In the return-visit raw score analysis, state anxiety averaged 53.8 in the macadamia-only condition versus 58.6 with geranium (p = .06), and trait anxiety averaged 59.1 versus 63.2 (p = .12). Section 2.3.2 reports no clear three-month STAI change in either condition. The university’s exact public posting date and reuse license remain unverified. The registry, dissertation and journal article describe one study family. [University dissertation, Sections 2.3.2 and 2.3.3, Figures 7 and 8](https://iuhw.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2000126/files/32206AR20.pdf) · [University academic-year abstract](https://iuhw.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2000126/files/32206AR20_youshi.pdf) · [2025 journal report](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#macadamia-nut-oil-in-a-fibromyalgia-aroma-comparison)

An earlier 2020 conference abstract by the same investigators describes one fibromyalgia patient receiving geranium aromatherapy and gives little change in STAI score after one month. The abstract does not identify the carrier oil or establish whether the case was included in the later crossover. [Primary abstract, P7-26](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/amjsphcs/30/0/30_800/_pdf/-char/ja)

## Topical macadamia blend in cervical myelopathy

Ishihata reported one person with cervical spondylotic myelopathy who had eight weekly aromatherapy sessions. A blend of essential oils used macadamia nut oil as a topical carrier. The recorded right-hand grip averaged 17.8 kg in week one and 27.3 kg in week eight. The subjective hand-sensation rating combined numbness, difficulty gripping, fine tasks and other sensations. The report gives no separate dexterity score, untreated comparison, oil-only intervention or pressing method. The journal issue appeared October 10, 2022 and J-STAGE posted it June 20, 2025. [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2022-Ishihata-Macadamia-Hand-Factual-Extract.txt) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.69380/rahos.16.2_35)

### State anxiety in the oral macadamia comparator

The RRIPP-3 state anxiety analysis was inconclusive between krill oil and macadamia oil, with intent-to-treat p = .8611 for the analysis reported alongside Figure 5 and a per-protocol interaction p = .6262 in Appendix C. In the per-protocol macadamia arm the least-squares means were 32.5, 32.0 and 33.7 at the three assessments. The trial tested a training population with substantial attrition and gave no oil-free contrast or verified cold-press processing. [Original trial, Figure 5 and Appendix C](Source-Text/PMC8228047.txt)

## Hoarseness after roasted hazelnut challenges

The 2021 Turin study included 27 children with confirmed hazelnut allergy and ten sensitized tolerant children. Table 1 lists hoarseness three times in individual symptom descriptions after supervised finely ground roasted whole-hazelnut challenges. The comparison of raw and roasted oil-body proteins was a separate laboratory procedure. The report supplies no edible cold-pressed oil challenge, acoustic vocal intensity or Parkinson hypophonia outcome.
