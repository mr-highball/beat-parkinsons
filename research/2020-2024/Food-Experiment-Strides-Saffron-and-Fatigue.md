# Stride, saffron and fatigue follow-up

## Parkinson baseline fish anxiety coconut fatigue and oat balance

The December 27, 2024 publication gives graphical cross-sectional correlations. Non-fried fish and self-reported anxiety have a negative plotted sign. Coconut oil and fatigue have a negative sign. Oatmeal and balance have a near-zero inverse sign. The two printed copies show identical matching colors. No published numerical coefficient, pairwise confidence interval, food species or oil grade appears for these cells. The published oat field is oatmeal, not the separate questionnaire variable for cooked oats. ## Virgin coconut oil and acute infection symptom reporting

First published January 23, 2024. Fatigue was among daily signs and symptoms. The Results plot a combined resolution measure and report no clear overall group difference. The paper includes a participant flow discrepancy between the total reported complete and arm-specific withdrawals. It provides no distinct fatigue severity estimate or Parkinson population. [Original randomized COVID-19 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10808878/) · [Local text](Source-Text/PMC10808878.txt)

## Brown rice protein hydrolysate and physical performance

First published August 20, 2020. Forty older adults were randomized 30 to rice protein hydrolysate and 10 to maltodextrin. The secondary SPPB total, which includes standing balance, gait and chair stands, favored the hydrolysate (p = .04); chair stand p = .02. The original does not separately report the standing balance comparison. The isolated enzymatically processed protein differs from cooked whole brown rice. [Original 12-week randomized study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7555431/) · [Local text](Source-Text/PMC7555431.txt)

## Brown rice protein trial registration

First posted June 30, 2020. The completed comparison evaluates a brown-rice protein hydrolysate for digestive discomfort in older adults. Results are not posted in the current registry. The 2020 article reports a physical-performance composite as a secondary measure. Registration and article concern the same cohort. [Official ClinicalTrials.gov record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04450979) · [Local text](Source-Text/2020-NCT04450979-Rice-Balance-Scope.txt)

## Cooked common beans and rodent cognitive tasks

Volume 39(1), June 2024. The mouse study tests cooked, air-dried, ground market Phaseolus vulgaris at 50 percent of a feed formulation using scopolamine and control conditions. Behavioral measures include water maze, object recognition and beam walking. The food tissue is not identified as immature green pods and the scopolamine assignment in Methods conflicts with a group label. It gives no human executive outcome. [Original Nigerian Journal of Physiological Sciences article](https://ojshostng.com/index.php/njphysiologicalsciences/fr_CA/article/download/3096/2137/10547) · [Local text](Source-Text/2024-Uket-Cooked-Common-Bean-Cognition.txt)

## Long-chain omega-3 and olfactory threshold outside Parkinson

First published 2022. In 58 people with postviral olfactory dysfunction, randomized addition of an EPA and DHA supplement to olfactory training gave more improvement on the odor threshold component than training alone. Source marketing and Methods specify long-chain EPA/DHA capsules. They do not establish cold-pressed flaxseed oil or its plant alpha-linolenic acid effect. Postviral olfactory injury and Parkinson hyposmia also differ. [Original Rhinology study](https://www.rhinologyjournal.com/Rhinology_issues/manuscript_2958.pdf) · [Local text](Source-Text/2022-Hernandez-Omega-3-Olfaction.txt)

## Soybean oil as a dietary comparator in a rotenone model

First published June 14, 2024. Soybean oil at 5% by weight served as the control dietary fat in the rotenone model. Perilla and fish-oil diets replace it and vary dose. Pole, cylinder and open-field outcomes do not measure human OFF time. The soybean comparator receives no isolated no-oil comparison. [Original perilla comparison methods and Figure 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11561828/) · [Local text](Source-Text/PMC11561828.txt)

## Saffron cognition review and outcome scope

The review first appeared August 4, 2021. Section 3.2.8 identifies saffron-extract dementia trials using global cognition and dementia measures. The retrieved body gives no separate executive-function result in Parkinson disease. The local full text supports exclusion of a direct inference while preserving the Alzheimer study trail. [Recovered 2021 source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8445631/) · [Local text](Source-Text/PMC8445631.txt#L559)

## Brown rice hydrolysate outcomes by physical test

The primary randomized human trial allocated 30 older adults to rice protein hydrolysate and 10 to maltodextrin. Figure 4 supplies the overall Short Physical Performance Battery and chair-stand changes at four weeks, including p=.04 and p=.02 respectively. The analyzed denominators differ from allocated counts. The separately usable standing-balance estimate is absent. The primary gut-discomfort endpoint was not met. The randomized comparison used a processed protein hydrolysate, with no cooked-whole-rice or Parkinson postural outcome.
