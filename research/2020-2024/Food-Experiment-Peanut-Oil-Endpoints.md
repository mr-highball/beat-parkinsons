# Peanut oil in Parkinsonian motor and REM sleep models

## Rigidity and dystonic postures in levodopa-treated primates

Herrero and colleagues describe rigidity after MPTP in all three monkeys later given subcutaneous 7-nitroindazole dissolved in peanut oil. Two developed dystonia during MPTP exposure, and the individual narratives describe dystonic postures during levodopa treatment. Figure 2 gives combined dyskinesia severity and duration after the drug in oil. There is no separate post-dose rigidity or dystonia estimate, and the drug effect cannot be distinguished from its vehicle by this design. ## Oral peanut oil vehicle and REM sleep without atonia

Kam and colleagues compared gavaged peanut oil vehicle with DORA-12 in aged PS19 tauopathy mice. In the nine with an REM sleep behavior disorder phenotype, the drug condition had lower REM electromyographic area, lower percentage of REM without atonia and lower REM-to-non-REM electromyographic area ratio, with paired p values .02, .004 and .002 in Figure 7. This experiment used actual electromyographic measures rather than inferring dream enactment from time spent asleep. It tested a drug relative to the vehicle, with no matched oil-free treatment, no reported oil grade and a tauopathy model.
