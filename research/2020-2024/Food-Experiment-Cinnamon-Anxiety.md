# Anxiety measurement in a Parkinson-model cinnamon study

## Cinnamon extract and maze outcomes after brain injury

The Qubty primary abstract reports an inconclusive anxiety comparison after oral cinnamon extract in a traumatic injury mouse study. The publicly displayed original figures identify elevated-plus-maze testing and open-arm times across four groups. The full article body is subscription gated. These are extract and injury-model outcomes rather than clinical anxiety or ground cinnamon. [Original abstract and figures](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12031-020-01688-4) · [Local factual figure extract](Source-Text/2020-Qubty-Cinnamon-TBI-Figure-Extract.txt)

## Locomotion and the reported anxiety scope in MPTP mice

Jiao and colleagues used oral isolated cinnamaldehyde in an acute MPTP model. The abstract describes reduced anxiety. Figure 2 reports open-field crossings and rearing and an inconclusive urination comparison. No separately interpretable avoidance or center-time comparison is reported in that figure. General movement improvement in this model does not isolate anxiety behavior or bear directly on eating ground cinnamon.
