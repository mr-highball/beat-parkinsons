# Squash, mushroom, eggplant, and zucchini evidence

## 2020 Zucchini and Muscat pumpkin in a Parkinson diet menu

Hegelmaier and colleagues reported a vegetarian diet pilot in 16 people with Parkinson's. The appendix lists zucchini, Muscat pumpkin, and the literal menu entry “auberges,” whose ingredient identity the source does not confirm. The intervention bundled foods and included an additional bowel-cleansing group. Global UPDRS III changes do not isolate any ingredient or preparation. The article supplies no zucchini-specific executive or dream-enactment outcome and no butternut-specific voice result. [Existing local primary text, Appendix A](Source-Text/PMC7072275.txt)

## 2020 Bitter zucchini clinical poisoning report

The primary case describes a 66-year-old woman presenting after eating bitter zucchini, with gastrointestinal symptoms attributed to cucurbitacin poisoning. The report concerns an acute toxic exposure. Its abstract provides no Parkinson's cohort or assessment of dream enactment and executive performance. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32668470/)

## 2020 Registration for the Purdue mushroom feeding study

NCT04259229 first posted on February 6, 2020. The randomized controlled Mediterranean-diet comparison added or omitted mushrooms and registered cognitive function among the study's outcomes. The registered population was middle-aged and older adults outside a Parkinson-specific trial. The companion cardiometabolic and cognitive reports are from the same trial. [Official registry with version history](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04259229?tab=table)

## 2020 Roasted button mushroom aroma in a trained panel

The researchers compared raw and roasted white, crimini, and portobello Agaricus bisporus using a chemically trained panel. They scored the intensity of aroma, flavour, and taste attributes for each mushroom product. This provides preparation-specific food sensory evidence. The task did not measure smell impairment following ingestion. The publisher reports first online publication in November 2020, ahead of the 2021 issue. [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lwt.2020.110596) · [USDA publication record](https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/publications/publication/?seqNo115=371919)

## 2020 White button mushroom capsules and cognition

Zajac and colleagues randomized 436 healthy older adults among standard freeze-dried mushroom capsules, vitamin D2-enriched mushroom capsules, vitamin D3, and placebo over 24 weeks. The report found no significant treatment effects on the tested cognitive or mood outcomes. The authors excluded the first cohort's underdosed vitamin D2-enriched arm from analysis after a manufacturing error. This supplies null cognitive context for a dried powder in capsules. [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC7766163.txt) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12123847)

## 2021 Cucurbita pepo seed extracts in rodent models

Saleem and colleagues administered methanolic Cucurbita pepo seed extract in a haloperidol rat model and reported changes in motor behaviour and tissue markers. Nain and colleagues used ethanolic extract of C. pepo seeds in a separate scopolamine rat model, with increased passive-avoidance latency and reduced water-maze escape latency reported at 400 mg/kg. The first study addresses drug-induced motor impairment and the second addresses memory in a dementia model. Neither used culinary zucchini fruit or measured Parkinson's dream enactment or executive symptoms. [Primary Parkinson-model abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33759084/) · [Primary cognitive-model report](https://brieflands.com/journals/jrps/articles/146964)

## 2022 Butternut squash skin contact case

Shah and Feldman described transient palm tightness, dryness, and scaling in a 28-year-old man after holding butternut squash during peeling. Tightness returned with direct hand reexposure. This extends the 1994 preparation-contact observation, with no voice outcome or roasted ingestion challenge. The original article is openly readable under CC BY-NC. [Primary case report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9210002/)

## 2022 White button mushroom in an amyloid mouse model

An amyloid precursor protein transgenic mouse study examined a diet supplemented with white button mushroom. Supplemented animals had better spatial-memory performance and reduced measures of plaque formation. The pathology, supplemented preparation, and spatial task differ from the proposed Parkinson's executive-function comparison. [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC9599624.txt) · [Primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9599624/)

## 2022 Registration for the whole cremini cognition trial

NCT05559112 first posted September 29, 2022. The registry title emphasizes vitamin D status, immune function, and inflammatory status. The 2025 paper links its UV-exposed whole-cremini and cognitive comparison to this registration. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05559112?tab=table) · [Later trial results](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md#2025-whole-uv-treated-cremini-and-cognitive-performance)

## 2024 Pooled mushroom consumption and executive outcomes in EPIC Norfolk

Cha and colleagues related a pooled mushroom food-frequency item to cognitive tests. Higher consumption was associated with better scores on some executive and other cognitive measures after adjustment for measured covariates. The authors describe a cross-sectional analysis, acknowledge residual confounding and reverse causality, and do not identify species, preparation, or Parkinson diagnoses in the analytic dataset. [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC10857520.txt) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu16030353)

## 2024 Cooked button mushroom volatile aroma measurements

The laboratory study compared raw, steamed, boiled, and baked Agaricus bisporus using GC-MS and an electronic nose. Cooking changed measured volatiles and the instrument could discriminate preparation profiles. These measurements characterize the mushroom stimulus. They provide no clinical measure of an eater's olfactory impairment or change after consumption. [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC10930934.txt) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.3390/foods13050685)

## 2024 Zucchini and butternut preparation contact case

Ravichandran and colleagues described dermatitis following contact with zucchini during cooking, with a prior similar skin reaction after handling butternut squash pulp. The patient reported no systemic or cutaneous symptoms from oral consumption of either food. That history concerns one case and contains no acoustic, executive, or dream outcome. [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC11178144.txt) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.60359)

## 2024 White button and oyster mushrooms in a randomized feeding study

Uffelman and colleagues randomized healthy adults to eight weeks of a controlled Mediterranean-style diet with mushrooms or breadcrumbs. Sixty adults completed the trial. The intervention supplied fresh white button mushrooms on four days and oyster mushrooms on three days per week, with raw, sautéed or microwaved use permitted. Both groups improved on list learning and story memory. Delayed list recall improved in the control group only, with a reported time-by-group interaction of p = .005. Digit span and coding did not change. The design combined species, allowed different preparations, and did not test roasted button mushroom in people with Parkinson's. [Local article text, Tables 4–5](Source-Text/PMC11311880.txt) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.3390/foods13152319) · [Trial registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04259229)
