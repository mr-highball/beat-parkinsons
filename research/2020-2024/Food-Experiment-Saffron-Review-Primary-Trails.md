# Original experiments cited in the saffron reviews

## Crocin and depressive-like behavior in a Parkinson mouse model

Tang and colleagues tested isolated crocin in a mouse model and reported improvement on a forced-swim phenotype. The author abstract does not give the group-level estimate or uncertainty. The intervention is a saffron constituent and the outcome is a behavioral proxy. [Original author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32495180/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2020-PMID32495180-Tang-Crocin-Mouse-Depression.txt)

## Whole saffron in feed and climbing in Parkinson-model flies

Inoue used saffron suspended in water and mixed into fly feed. Isolated crocetin was tested separately. Both treatments attenuated loss of climbing in the two fly strains at selected measurements, while life span improved in one strain. The preparation and assay details do not establish ground-stigma effects on rigidity, fatigue, depression or insomnia. [Original methods and Figures 1–2](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/352972188_Saffron_ameliorated_motor_symptoms_short_life_span_and_retinal_degeneration_in_Parkinson%27s_disease_fly_models) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2021-PMID34224829-Inoue-Saffron-Fly-Food.txt)

## Crocin capsules and motor totals in a human Parkinson trial

Soleymani reported 53 completers of 60 randomized, with a favorable within-group change in MDS-UPDRS Parts II and III after isolated crocin. The abstract supplies no item-level rigidity result or adjusted between-group estimate. [Author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39025034/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2024-PMID39025034-Soleymani-Crocin-Motor-Totals.txt)

## Human and nonhuman studies in the 2024 review bibliographies

The [Bej review](Source-Text/2024-PMID38446350-Bej-Saffron-Review-Citations.txt) cites an original non-Parkinson depression trial using an ethanol extract of dried stigma. The [Abdian review](Source-Text/2024-PMID39577115-Abdian-Saffron-Review-Citations.txt) separately cites petal, non-Parkinson mood studies and an [uncontrolled multiple sclerosis syrup study](Source-Text/2020-PMID32716906-Ashtiani-Saffron-Syrup-Fatigue.txt). The latter reports a baseline fatigue mean above the stated scale range. Its direction of within-group change is preserved with that reporting limit. None of the reviews adds an independent clinical observation.
