# Pinto and common-bean studies with sleep ascertainment

## Incidental sleep observations in a white bean extract trial

February 5, 2020. Wang et al. randomized 120 adults with obesity to white kidney bean seed extract capsules or maltodextrin placebo for 35 days. Analysis comprised 56 and 58 participants, respectively. The Results state that no sleep abnormalities were observed in either arm, without a sleep instrument, ascertainment schedule, baseline insomnia information or separate event counts. This is an incidental observation using extract in a different population. The September 2020 corrigendum concerns the plant-origin discussion and leaves the sleep observation as printed. ## PROMED sleep questions and actual supplied bean categories

December 23, 2022. The design article describes a questionnaire on sleep quantity and quality for a whole-diet pilot. Table 1 lists canned pink and white beans, dried small red beans and other pulses among foods provided. The intervention visual-guide supplement uses generic habichuelas and shows mixed bean images without a pinto designation. Supplement images cannot establish cultivar or individual ingestion. The protocol provides no sleep results. The official supplementary document is https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC10100940.1/mmc1.docx. ## Cooked pinto meal crossover and metabolic room consent

November 8, 2023. Casperson's randomized crossover compares a cooked whole-pinto meal, a pinto-flour meal and a shrimp control. Rice, tomatoes and spices are shared meal components. Whole beans are described as cooked; the cultivar and boiling method are not specified. The registered endpoint is substrate use after the meal. The completed record lists 36 actually enrolled and no posted results. The September 2024 consent projects about 28 and gives meal, appetite-rating and metabolic-room procedures. It instructs participants to remain awake for a short measurement period, with no sleep scale or insomnia endpoint. The original consent was posted in 2025 at https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/08/NCT06123208/ICF_000.pdf. The trial first posted in 2023. ## PROMED pilot outcome abstract and unreported sleep results

June 14, 2024. This report identifies PROMED and the same intervention program. Forty-four adults were randomized and the per-protocol report gives 15 and 13 by group. It reports cardiometabolic, blood-pressure, diet-quality and depressive-symptom outcomes, with no sleep estimate. The named multiple food and counseling components and absent individual pinto intake preclude a boiled-pinto effect estimate. Retain the sleep questionnaire reporting trail separately from the published pilot outcomes. [Original source](https://doi.org/10.2337/db24-133-OR) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2024-Mattei-PROMED-Outcome-Abstract-Extract.txt)

## Adverse event reporting in a second white bean extract trial

June 3, 2024. Jäger and colleagues randomized 90 adults with overweight or moderate obesity to two white kidney bean extract formulations or placebo for twelve weeks. The original paper reports 19 adverse events, with no insomnia or sleep disturbance named in its published summary. Sleep is not a measured endpoint there. These context records involve extracted material and clinically distinct participants. The absence of a named adverse event does not quantify an insomnia change.
