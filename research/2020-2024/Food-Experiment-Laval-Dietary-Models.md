# Laval dietary models, 2020–2024

## 2021 enteric tissue analysis of the 2017 Thy1 cohort

Lamontagne-Proulx and colleagues published this analysis on November 24, 2021. Section 2.1 identifies the plasma and tissue as collected for the previously reported Coulombe Thy1-αSyn study. The investigators found that the high-DHA feed preserved enteric tyrosine hydroxylase-positive neuron density and increased myenteric GLP-1 and Nrf2. The study measured tissue and plasma outcomes. Its mention of bowel transit and fecal output refers to other studies. It gives no measured constipation or bowel-transit effect for the diet groups. ## 2024 dissolved trehalose, maltose and sucrose in PrP-A53T mice

Pradeloux and colleagues presented this transgenic study as NeuroQuébec abstract 98 in February 2024 and published the full article on September 30. Mice received plain water or water with 2 percent weight per volume of one disaccharide from three to 9.5 months of age. None of the three sugars measurably changed open-field activity, the anxiety and nesting proxies, or the reported bowel outcome in the transgenic mice. The bowel measures were 30-minute fecal pellet count, wet mass and water content. Trehalose did preserve tyrosine hydroxylase staining in central and enteric tissues. This cohort used continuous oral intake in a transgenic model. The 2012 Gué study tested a shorter gavage regimen after a toxin lesion.
