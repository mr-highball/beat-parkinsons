# Salmon and anxiety follow-up

## FiSK Junior mixed oily-fish meals and internalizing symptoms

Teisen and colleagues, 2020. The Danish FiSK Junior trial randomized 199 healthy children aged 8–9 to approximately 12 weeks of oily fish or poultry. Of these, 197 completed the trial. Provided fish included salmon dinner fillets and mixed lunch products with salmon or other oily fish. Compared with poultry, the fish group had a lower parent-rated Strengths and Difficulties Questionnaire internalizing score, −0.63 points (95% CI −1.11 to −0.16), and a lower total difficulties score, −0.89 (−1.60 to −0.18). The broad socioemotional composite difference was −0.13 (−0.26 to .01). Internalizing covers emotional symptoms and peer problems. The study supplied no preparation-stratified, salmon-specific or anxiety-only estimate. ## Parkinson diet scores and fish components in Fox Insight

Fox and colleagues, December 6, 2022. The study used 1,205 participants reporting Parkinson's disease or another parkinsonian disorder, 1,145 of whom reported idiopathic Parkinson's disease. Table 2 relates overall Mediterranean and MIND diet scores to individual symptoms including anxiety. Supplementary Table S4 relates individual diet components to total patient-reported Parkinson symptom severity. For the total score, the MEDI fish or shellfish item at three or more meals per week had a coefficient of −31.0 (95% CI −88.9 to 27.0, Holm-adjusted p = .90). The MIND nonfried fish item coefficient was −93.6 (−164 to −23.6, Holm-adjusted p = .08). This provides a fish-component comparison for the total score. The study provides no fish-component estimate for anxiety and no separate salmon or poached-salmon exposure.
