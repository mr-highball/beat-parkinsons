## Fatigability attention and anxiety in the same canola comparator preprint

Table 1 in the exploratory preprint reports change in physical Pittsburgh Fatigability Scale of −1.6 ± 5.2 for the canola comparison and +0.1 ± 3.6 for ketone ester (p = .711). Grip changes were +0.6 versus −1.0 kg (p = .728). Trail Making Test A changed by −1.6 versus −5.8 seconds (p = .422), and POMS tension-anxiety by −0.73 versus +1.75 points (p = .211). These exploratory measures compare active ketone treatment with a canola-containing comparator in healthy older adults, without a separately assigned oil-free arm.
