# Raspberry clinical source history

## Black raspberry hematology article and reported tolerability

June 30, 2022. Dong and colleagues identify NCT03140280 and report 15 participants in a paired before-and-after analysis of blood and epigenetic measures after three months of black raspberry powder. The article describes no adverse events during treatment. It does not give counts for depression, insomnia or confusion. The later official results use an 18-person adverse-event denominator and a 52-week assessment period. The available texts do not establish a reconciled reporting window or clinical attribution. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9271408/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2022-Dong-Black-Raspberry-Hematology-Report.txt)

## Black raspberry hematology registry safety events

November 4, 2022 first posted results for NCT03140280. The adverse-event module reports a 52-week time frame and one dietary intervention group with 18 at risk. Depression, insomnia and confusion each list one affected participant and one event under systematic other adverse events. The registry extract gives no individual linkage, occurrence dates, baseline change, comparison group or attribution of these entries to raspberry. The published three-month 15-participant analysis describes no adverse events and uses a different denominator and stated scope. No anxiety effect can be inferred for this trial or for frozen red raspberry in Parkinson disease.
