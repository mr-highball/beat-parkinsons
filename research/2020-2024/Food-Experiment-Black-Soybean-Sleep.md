# Black Soybean Sleep

## Black soybean extract and task day drowsiness in the 2021 crossover

Akagi and colleagues' issue is dated June 20, 2021 in the [Japanese science index](https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/en/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=202102282653702133). The [publisher contents](https://www.lifescience.co.jp/yk/yk21/yke2106.html) list pages 953–964. The June 29 sponsor release reproduces the authors' conference abstract. It describes 24 healthy adults reporting usual fatigue, a randomized crossover with four weeks per condition, and comparisons of black soybean seed coat extract capsules and dextrin placebo around two hours of computer work and a recovery period. The author abstract reports less subjective drowsiness with extract and omits numerical contrasts. The journal body and registry identity remain open. This is a Glycine max extract and an acute task rating in healthy volunteers. ## Patent tables for the likely 2021 drowsiness crossover

The Japanese patent JP2023036051A was published March 13, 2023. Its [US counterpart](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20230124668A1/en) followed on April 20. The authors, 24 randomized volunteers, dosing, four-week crossover periods and task schedule give strong reason to suspect overlap with the 2021 journal crossover, pending direct article verification. One dropout left 23 complete participants. In US Table 3, drowsiness VAS means after four weeks are 48.7 versus 61.0 before the workload and 71.7 versus 80.0 at two hours of workload (extract versus placebo, paired p<.05 at each point). The baseline-to-week-four change before workload in Table 4 is −8.7 versus +0.9 and has no significance mark. The narrative and tables use different dispersion labels. These are time-specific subjective ratings with sponsor involvement and multiple comparisons. A distinct [earlier patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/JP2021187785A/ja) reports a smaller parallel experiment. Trial identity remains unresolved for the crossover, so the 2021 report and this 2023 family should be counted once provisionally. ## Public registration for the later sleepiness on rising trial

The UMIN page lists a registered date and anticipated start of June 5, 2023, last follow-up December 17, 2023 and study information disclosure December 16, 2024. The dates distinguish registration from documented public disclosure. It describes a parallel placebo-controlled study in healthy adults and specifies 12-week sleepiness on rising from the OSA-MA as the primary outcome. PSQI components and other sleep measures are secondary. The 2025 article supplies composition and results. This is a different protocol from the four-week, task-day crossover reported in 2021. ## Three arm soybean extract sleep comparison reported in a patent

The Taiwan application was published July 1, 2024. The [international counterpart](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2024142285A1/en) followed July 4 and JP7771432B2 follows the same patent family. The 12-week double-blind comparison reports 69 participants in three arms, with 21, 25 and 23 assigned to placebo, lower-dose and higher-dose black soybean seed coat extract. Sixty-five were evaluable for sleep. OSA-MA was assessed on waking. The patent reports no significant between-group differences on the sleep factors, despite within-arm changes. Enrollment and study design differ from the later 64-participant two-arm paper, while participant independence remains unverified. This is an extract study in healthy adults, without a Parkinson daytime sleepiness outcome or boiled common-bean exposure.
