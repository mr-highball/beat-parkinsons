# Fennel seed and handwriting

## 2020 Fennel seed in a mixed Parkinson diet

Hegelmaier et al., published 2020-02-06, PMID 32041265, PMCID PMC7072275, DOI 10.3390/cells9020376, supplied 16 people with Parkinson disease an ovo-lacto vegetarian diet for two weeks; ten also received bowel cleansing. Table 3 lists fennel seed among nuts and seeds used in the menu but gives no grinding, individual ingestion or dose. The reported UPDRS III total includes motor items but no separate handwriting result. Mixed foods and other procedures prevent attribution to fennel.

## 2020 Fennel seed extract and hind-paw grip in mice

Maqbool et al., first published 2020-12-13, PMID 33598155, PMCID PMC7866564, DOI 10.1002/fsn3.2033: six mice in each group received standard chow or chow containing 200 mg/kg methanolic extract from ground Foeniculum vulgare seeds after sciatic nerve crush. Figure 2 reports changes in hind-paw grip force and gait-related sciatic function, not forelimb dexterity or writing; the population is a peripheral injury model, not Parkinson disease. Extract and ground whole seed are distinct exposures. This is not evidence of a direction for human handwriting.
