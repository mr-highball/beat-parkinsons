# Clinical and Diagnosis

### 2023 — α-synuclein seed amplification in PPMI

A cross-sectional analysis of cerebrospinal fluid in the Parkinson’s Progression Markers Initiative reported high overall agreement with clinical case and control status. Positivity varied by genetic and clinical subgroup, including lower detection in some LRRK2-associated disease. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37059509/)

### 2024 — Testing a biological staging proposal in existing cohorts

Researchers applied the neuronal α-synuclein disease integrated staging system to data from PPMI and two trial cohorts. This tests whether biological and functional measures can organize research populations. Broader validation and how to handle assay-negative disease remain open. [Primary source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41531-024-00789-w)
