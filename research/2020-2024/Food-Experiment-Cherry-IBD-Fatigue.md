# Registered cherry concentrate comparison for ulcerative colitis

## Ulcerative colitis fatigue outcomes in the registration

NCT05486507 first posted August 3, 2022. The trial registers cherry concentrate and a comparator for ulcerative colitis, with inflammatory bowel disease fatigue among the reported follow-up outcomes. The current official record shows completed and no registry-posted results. Its linked original journal report publishes the fatigue comparison.
