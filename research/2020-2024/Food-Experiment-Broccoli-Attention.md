# Broccoli-related preparations and attention

## Registered glucoraphanin and cognitive processing in older adults

The entry first posted December 5, 2020 and lists symbol coding, symbol search and digit span among the primary outcomes in a 144-person glucoraphanin trial in healthy older adults. The linked 2022 Nouchi article explicitly names this registration. Registry results were posted June 12, 2023. The protocol measures task performance after capsules, without a raw mature broccoli or Parkinson clinical contrast. [Local factual extract](Source-Text/Trials/2020-UMIN000042666-Glucoraphanin.txt) · [Official Japanese registry UMIN000042666](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000048062)

## Attention and vigilance during adjunct sulforaphane in schizophrenia

Published in 2022. The study randomized 172 people with first or early schizophrenia to two levels of a glucoraphanin and myrosinase tablet or placebo alongside risperidone. Table 2 gives estimated 22-week attention and vigilance changes of 2.56, 5.38 and 6.28 on high-dose, low-dose and placebo, respectively. The omnibus comparison was inconclusive (p = .263). The overall cognitive score was also inconclusive (p = .693). The report used a supplement, with no raw broccoli or Parkinson sample. The registry linkage is probable from matching trial details rather than an identifier printed in the article. [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2022-Hei-Sulforaphane-Attention-Factual-Extract.txt) · [Hei and colleagues, original Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11205988/)

## Glucoraphanin capsules and task-specific cognitive performance

First published July 29, 2022. The randomized trial assigned 144 healthy older adults to glucoraphanin capsules or placebo for 12 weeks. Table 2 gives symbol-search mean changes of 1.99 versus 1.29, reported adjusted p = .03. Coding (p = .83) and Stroop (p = 1) were inconclusive after adjustment. The authors classify symbol search as processing speed. Capsule plant source was not specified in the checked methods. The patients, preparation and tasks do not establish clinical Parkinson attention effects. [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2022-Nouchi-Glucoraphanin-Processing-Factual-Extract.txt) · [Nouchi and colleagues, original Table 2](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnagi.2022.929628)

## Broccoli among allowed foods in a pediatric attention diet

First posted July 9, 2024. The completed Cairo ADHD study compared an oligoantigenic diet with a food regimen without the restrictions. Broccoli was one of several permitted vegetables. The protocol lists parent ADHD ratings and a continuous performance task. Broccoli quantity, preparation and a separate broccoli comparison were unspecified. The entry has no posted results. [Local factual extract](Source-Text/Trials/2024-NCT06491836-Attention-Diet.txt) · [Official pediatric oligoantigenic diet registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06491836)

## Registered pediatric sulforaphane and inattention in France

First posted September 19, 2024. The Robert Debré protocol specifies eight weeks of sulforaphane capsules or placebo, with parent-rated inattention and a sustained-attention task. It was Recruiting at the official November 2026 update, with no posted results. Location and protocol distinguish it from the 2025 Tehran methylphenidate adjunct report. This comparison does not test raw broccoli or Parkinson disease. [Local factual extract](Source-Text/Trials/2024-NCT06594536-Sulforaphane-Inattention.txt) · [Official French pediatric trial registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06594536)
