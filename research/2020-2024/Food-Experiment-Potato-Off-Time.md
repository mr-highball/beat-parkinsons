# Potato Off Time

## Registered Parkinson dose optimization and motor complications

N-DOSE assigns nicotinamide riboside dose regimens in Parkinson disease. The completed registry lists 81 actual enrollees and no posted results. It registers MDS-UPDRS part IV in its assessment battery. The registry's outcome description is for the total part IV scale. It does not specify a potato intervention or an analysis involving a dietary potato exposure. ## Measured potato salad intake in a standardized Parkinson lunch

July 16, 2020. Participants ate the same lunch with sausage, cold potato salad and apple puree. Section 2.4 describes weighing components before and after the meal. Table 4 reports mean potato salad consumed of 232 g in advanced Parkinson disease (n = 21), 291 g in early Parkinson disease (n = 20), and 255 g in controls (n = 23). Parkinson participants were evaluated during their usual medicated ON state. The primary outcome was meal energy intake, leaving daily OFF hours and an independently assigned potato intervention outside the comparison.
