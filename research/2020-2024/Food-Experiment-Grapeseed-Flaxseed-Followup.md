# Grapeseed and flaxseed oil evidence

## Cold-pressed grapeseed oil in a rat spatial learning experiment

Berahmand and colleagues first published May 11, 2020. Their supplier's Vitis vinifera seed oil was made by cold pressing and delivered orally by gavage. In the scopolamine model, timing groups receiving oil spent more time in the target quadrant of a Morris water maze than scopolamine alone (p < .01). The oil-only and saline control groups did not clearly differ in target-quadrant time. The authors report no swimming-speed, fall-count or skilled-limb measure. The experiment addresses spatial learning after scopolamine in rats. [Primary report, Methods and Figure 2](https://apb.tbzmed.ac.ir/PDF/apb-10-430.pdf) · [Local licensed text](Source-Text/PMC7335983.txt)

## Grapeseed oil comparator in a hospitalized adult sleep trial

NCT04648215 first posted December 1, 2020. Hospitalized adults were randomized to lavender oil on gauze, grapeseed oil on gauze or usual nursing care. The two registered outcomes are sleep quality and duration on a hospital sleep scale. The record lists 108 enrolled and termination due to unavailable subjects, with no posted results. Grapeseed oil was used near the participant to supply the comparison aroma. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04648215)

## Healthy Cookie Energy Study registration and grapeseed oil

NCT04677920 first posted December 21, 2020. This independent single-arm pilot registers a baked grapeseed oil cookie in healthy adults, two weeks of exposure, and sleep quality, walking speed and grip strength in secondary analyses correlating those measures with cardiolipin. The November 13, 2026 replay shows active, not recruiting, and no posted results. Gait speed, grip strength and sleep quality differ from falls, hand dexterity and Parkinson insomnia. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04677920)

## SNACk dietary oil and insomnia trial registration

NCT04677946 first posted December 21, 2020. The single-arm study plans eight weeks of linoleic-acid-rich cookies for 16 overweight adults with insomnia symptoms. It plans the Insomnia Severity Index, Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, PROMIS Sleep, actigraphy, EEG, gait speed and grip strength. The registry does not identify the cooking oil. Two independent trials from the same program name grapeseed oil but do not establish the SNACk recipe. The latest record remains not yet recruiting, with an estimated December 2027 start and no posted results. [Official registration and history](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04677946?tab=history)

## Healthy Cookie dietary fatty acid results

The 2020 Cole dissertation and 2022 journal paper describe actual oral grapeseed oil cookies and high-oleic safflower oil controls from NCT02841618. The randomized phase used 10 g of Pompeian grapeseed oil in the daily cookie for two weeks. The final report found changes in fatty acid and cardiolipin measurements. The dissertation and published results do not give gait-speed, sleep or grip outcomes from the registration. Oil pressing and refining details for the study lot were not reported. The dissertation, earlier conference abstract and journal report are the same program and should not multiply its randomized comparison. [Cole dissertation](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file/send?accession=osu1606329528422527&disposition=inline) · [2022 primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9540417/) · [Local licensed text](Source-Text/PMC9540417.txt)

## Oral grapeseed oil carrier and stool consistency in colitis rats

Lazcano-Silveira and colleagues tested carbon 60 dissolved in grapeseed oil against untreated DSS colitis in 2022. The intragastric formulation reduced the composite disease activity index (p < .05), which uses looser stool and rectal bleeding scores. The design included a healthy control, untreated colitis and a combined intervention. An oil-only arm and constipation measures were absent. [Primary Methods, Table 1 and Figure 2](https://www.dovepress.com/getfile.php?fileID=82368)

## Flaxseed oil in the UFRGS maternal separation experiments

The Souza master's dissertation was defended in 2023 and accessioned to the UFRGS public repository August 3, 2024. The thesis identifies Pazze flaxseed oil in an isocaloric rodent chow containing soybean oil. Both catalogued Pazze varieties are cold pressed, and the exact experimental variant was unspecified. The flaxseed-rich diet attenuated an elevated-plus-maze anxiety measure after maternal separation. The light-dark result did not reproduce that change. The annex reports diet main effects of p = .595 for open-field crossings and p = .976 for rearings, and p = .726 for freezing during fear extinction. The journal report also identifies increases in some amygdala inflammatory measures. The matching authors, design and endpoints identify one study family. The model concerns early life stress and fear extinction, with no Parkinson diagnosis, clinical bradykinesia or executive-function measure.
