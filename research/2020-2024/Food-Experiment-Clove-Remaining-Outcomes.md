# Ground clove and Parkinson symptom evidence

## Clove oil gargle and postoperative throat symptoms

The randomized trial assigned an oil gargle or tap-water gargle before anesthesia and tracheal intubation. One hundred thirty-four adults completed follow-up. At six hours, postoperative sore throat occurred in 26.9% versus 50.7%, with P=.0049. Grades 2 and 3 combined were less frequent at 24 hours (4.5% versus 16.4%, P=.0249). Grade 3 combines severe throat symptoms with hoarseness. These published outcomes concern postoperative throat morbidity. The exposure was gargled clove oil and there was no reported separate speech intelligibility, vocal sound pressure or Parkinson endpoint. [Lomi publisher study report](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2950553424000145).


## Clove capsule adjunct and a mislabeled Beck table

Sadeh and colleagues report randomizing 60 adults with depression to an oil-reduced clove bud extract preparation with starch in capsules or matched placebo, with citalopram in both groups. Methods identify Beck depression testing. Table 2 prints anxiety, but its values align with Beck depression described in the surrounding Results and Figure 1. Week six means were 7.0 and 10.9 (reported p = .009), with earlier visit comparisons inconclusive. The paper contains no separate anxiety scale or motivational item outcome. The article prints IRCT20190309042979N1 without a verified public registry record. The publisher’s 2020 volume 14 issue record and PDF route conflict with the 2019 volume 13 PDF footer. ## Registered inhaled eugenol and daytime sleepiness evaluation

The May 11, 2023 registry concerns smell training and neuromodulation for Long COVID. Eugenol is among the odorants and the Epworth Sleepiness Scale is a secondary outcome. The official record was Recruiting with no posted results in this check. The administration route is inhalation and the protocol has no isolated consumed ground clove exposure. ## Dysarthria after concentrated clove oil in a single case

AlBaazi and Hassoun reported that a man with diabetes experienced abdominal symptoms after a third exposure to concentrated clove oil, then fasciculations, weakness, dysarthria and dysphagia roughly two weeks later. His clinical and electrophysiologic findings resolved over about two weeks after presentation. The authors proposed toxicity based on timing, without testing eugenol or otherwise verifying causation. The report concerns concentrated oil and a motor-neuron-like presentation. It has no ground-clove exposure, Parkinson diagnosis or listener-based intelligibility measurement. ## Clove derived caryophyllene and cognitive failures

Published September 21, 2021. Bell et al. assigned older adults with memory concerns to one of two active doses of capsules containing standardized clove-derived beta-caryophyllene and rice-bran oil. Methods report 59 enrolled and 52 completed, 26 per arm. The abstract prints 29 per arm alongside total 52. Over eight weeks, mean Cognitive Failures Questionnaire total moved from 39 ± 16 to 27 ± 11 in the higher-dose group (within-group p≤.005) and 39 ± 12 to 33 ± 11 in the lower-dose group. These self-reports lack a placebo arm, and Parkinson disease was excluded. BrainCare funded the study and two investigators disclosed ties to it. No objective executive-function task or ground-clove effect was estimated.
