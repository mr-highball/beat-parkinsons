# Retrospective hazelnut oil registration

## Registered capsule mass and unresolved dose conflict

NCT04523532 first posted August 21, 2020 and links PMID 29046732 as a result of the same 40-person study. It specifies 25 mg daily in the hazelnut-oil arms. This is a retrospective administrative record, published after the intervention and the 2017 paper. The earlier 2016 registry gives no quantity. The 2018 Lima and 2021 Chaves theses each contain both 25 mg and 25 g descriptions. The Lima thesis cites Freitas and Naves as the source of its fatty-acid values, which match a scaled generic hazelnut lipid profile. The compounding certificate, batch fill and pressing remain unverified. The cohort and exposure conflict are relevant to source identity. No hallucination item or Parkinson population was reported.
