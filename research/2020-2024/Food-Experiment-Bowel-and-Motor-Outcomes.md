# Bowel outcomes and selected motor or nonmotor follow-up

## Rosemary oil massage and defecation reports

Published August 24, 2020. In adults with constipation, 15 received abdominal rosemary-oil massage and 15 peppermint-oil massage for 30 days. Figures 3 and 4 indicate defecation-benefit counts of 12 and 9, respectively. The benefit definition, allocation and adjusted group comparison are unclear. A pie chart percentage is not a reliable within-arm response rate. No sham massage, oral ground rosemary or Parkinson population. [Original publisher PDF](https://www.ikprress.org/index.php/PCBMB/article/download/5303/4919/9758) · [Local text](Source-Text/2020-Pratheebha-Rosemary-Constipation.txt)

## Cinnamon-containing Kampo and measured apathy

First publicly prepublished January 23, 2023. Fourteen participants with mild cognitive impairment or mild Alzheimer disease received 12-ingredient Ninjin’yoeito for 24 weeks. Median NPI apathy was zero at both baseline and week 24 with no significant change. Apathy was not required for entry and the floor limits inference. Neither cinnamon nor Parkinson patients were tested in a separate arm. [Original FRAMINGO study Table 4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9986705/) · [Local text](Source-Text/PMC9986705.txt)

## DopaFuse micropump and reported OFF time

First published May 2, 2024. A distinct 16-person open study reported day-15 OFF time lower by 1.72 hours (SE 0.37, p = .0004) versus conventional tablet treatment. The original device report identifies the paste vehicle as Miglyol 812 N medium chain triglyceride. Multiple aspects of the drug administration change concurrently; this comparison does not supply an isolated culinary oil effect. [Original device methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-78145-4) [Original Movement Disorders abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38698639/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2024-Olanow-DopaFuse-OFF.txt)

## DopaFuse registry and study family

First posted March 2, 2021. Completed 16-participant study with no posted registry results. Registration and 2024 clinical report concern the same formulation delivery comparison. This cohort differs from the earlier water-dispersion study. [Official ClinicalTrials.gov record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04778176) · [Local text](Source-Text/2021-NCT04778176-DopaFuse.txt)

## EU posted results for the water dispersion

The trial protocol was first entered September 29, 2014, and EU results were first published May 19, 2021. The results say the tablet was dispersed in 50 mL neutral-pH water. The registry reports pharmacokinetic results and links the 2019 report for this same 18-person clinical cohort. Neither trial entry nor results compare oral soybean oil.
