# ISRCTN dietary registrations and outcomes, 2020–2024

## Ketone ester and exercise endurance results

Norwitz and colleagues reported results for ISRCTN16599164 on September 30, 2020. In the randomized crossover, 14 adults with early Parkinson’s cycled at 80 rpm for 24 ± 9% longer after ketone ester plus carbohydrate than after an isocaloric carbohydrate drink (p = 0.027). The lab measure reflects acute exercise endurance. The intervention was a formulated ester and carbohydrate beverage. [Primary publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7556340/) · [2018 registration](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#ketone-ester-and-exercise-endurance)

## Ketone ester and cerebral ATP results

The ISRCTN10531043 basic results posted January 12, 2021 reported βATP and NAD(H) one hour after ketone ester exposure. For βATP the smallest nominal p value was 0.05 in the right temporoparietal cortex, above the report’s Bonferroni threshold of 0.004. These are imaging biomarkers measured in a before–after study without a control arm. [Primary results](https://www.isrctn.com/editorial/retrieveFile/be4788e1-0b84-431f-ad44-a68b8e26bc15/36051) · [2018 registration](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#ketone-ester-and-cerebral-atp)

## Low-carbohydrate ketogenic diet pilot

ISRCTN80586209 was registered May 5, 2020 and links the February 18, 2022 results paper by Tidman and colleagues. Sixteen adults with Parkinson’s participated in a 12-week single-group low-carbohydrate, healthy-fat ketogenic diet pilot. The abstract reports significant pre–post improvement in anxiety scores and UPDRS Part I. The design provides no concurrent control estimate or ingredient-specific comparison. The first registration projected 40 participants. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN80586209) · [Primary report abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35179078/)

## Hydrogen-rich water crossover

ISRCTN17961308 was registered May 10, 2023. The randomized, participant-blinded crossover compares four weeks of hydrogen-rich water with four weeks of placebo water, with a one-week washout. Registered outcomes include manual dexterity, cognition, mood, and mobility. The investigator’s 2025 University of Kent thesis identifies the study by registry ID. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN17961308) · [Thesis result note](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#hydrogen-rich-water-thesis-results)

## Mucuna powder in the TraPCAf protocol

ISRCTN77014546 was registered April 13, 2023. The registered TraPCAf project includes a planned two-site Tanzanian randomized trial of *Mucuna pruriens* powder versus levodopa/carbidopa in early Parkinson’s. The double-blind phase II design targets about 90 participants and motor UPDRS noninferiority, with quality of life as a secondary outcome. The protocol references the separate Ghana trial PACTR201611001882367 for methods. Searches by title and investigator identified the protocol but did not verify Tanzanian trial results. The powder uses a different botanical species from fava or broad beans (*Vicia faba*). [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN77014546) · [Published protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10585779/) · [Ghana results](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#ghana-mucuna-trial-final-results)

## Probiotic K10 in Parkinson’s and Alzheimer’s

ISRCTN13536327 was registered August 22, 2023. It describes a randomized placebo-controlled comparison of K10 capsules, with separate Parkinson’s and Alzheimer’s groups. The Parkinson’s measures include MDS-UPDRS and PDQ-39 at baseline, 45 days and 90 days. The registry lists 166 total across both conditions and links a trial website. No verified Parkinson’s subgroup result was identified by registry ID, formulation or title. K10 is a formulated supplement. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN13536327)

## Nutrition support feasibility results

The ISRCTN76863664 feasibility summary posted January 18, 2022 lists Parkinson’s in 15 of 520 participants with baseline health-condition data. It reports recruitment and nutritional-risk measures without a separate Parkinson’s outcome comparison. Related qualitative publications describe the acceptability and implementation of the nutrition support package. [Posted feasibility report](https://www.isrctn.com/editorial/retrieveFile/e7061a83-d521-4aed-bef4-ef2249461ee4/34110) · [2021 qualitative study](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-021-01572-z) · [2024 qualitative study](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12875-024-02623-x) · [2017 and 2019 registrations](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#primary-care-nutrition-support-for-older-adults)
