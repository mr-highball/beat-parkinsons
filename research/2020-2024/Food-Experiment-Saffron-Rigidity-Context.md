# Ground saffron and rigidity outcomes

## Injected safranal and catalepsy in a Parkinson model

The April 29, 2024 report used MPTP-treated mice and injected isolated safranal intraperitoneally. For the catalepsy test, the authors timed return of the hindlimbs to the supporting surface after positioning them on a raised support. Figure 3A reports shorter hindlimb release time after safranal compared with the untreated MPTP group (n = 8 per group). The authors use this assay as a stiffness proxy. The experiment supplies no culinary ground saffron, oral food comparison, or clinical rigidity score.
