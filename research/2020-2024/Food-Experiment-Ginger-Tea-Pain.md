# Ginger tea and pain ratings

## Ginger tea and post-dose menstrual-pain ratings

This 50-student quasi-experiment used 120 mL ginger tea twice daily for two menstrual days. The reported two-hour between-group intensity comparisons favored the tea group (p = 0.002 for the first morning and p < 0.001 for the other three occasions). The report has no randomized, masked or matched-beverage contrast and gives no clean between-group change estimate in Table 2. It does not specify herb processing or establish a Parkinson pain result.

The issue is dated 2019. The article gives February 10, 2020 as its online publication date, the first public date verified here.
