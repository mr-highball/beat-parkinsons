# Potato sleep trials, 2020–2024

## Combined potato preparations and sleep efficiency

NCT05994313 was first posted on 16 August 2023. The protocol assigns a 12-week diet with baked, boiled or mashed potatoes versus refined starches, with sleep efficiency as a primary outcome. The official registry gives actual completion 1 May 2025 and no posted results in the 15 November 2026 retrieval. A separate report of blood-pressure outcomes does not furnish the sleep estimate. The trial pools preparations and is not registered as a trial in Parkinson disease. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05994313) · [Official protocol](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/13/NCT05994313/Prot_SAP_ICF_000.pdf) · [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05994313.txt)

## Evening roasted potato comparison and overnight glycemia

Devlin and colleagues assigned evening mixed meals with boiled, roasted, boiled-and-cooled potato or basmati rice in a crossover with adults with type 2 diabetes. The term “nocturnal” in the title relates to glucose. The report provides no clinical insomnia comparison. The article was posted online in October 2020 before appearing in its 2021 issue.
