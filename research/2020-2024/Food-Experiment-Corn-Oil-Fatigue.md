# Corn oil and fatigue, 2020–2024

## Registered corn oil comparison for exercise fatigue

UMIN000039127 first disclosed 18 January 2020 and registered a double-blind comparison of EPA-rich fish oil with corn oil over eight weeks in healthy adults aged 50–59. The primary outcome is exercise-induced fatigue rated on a visual analogue scale. Posted results on 26 July 2021 report 20 participants and no group difference, without numerical estimates. This is a separate trial from UMIN000037848 on fatigue around desk work. Oil grade and Parkinson fatigue were unreported.
