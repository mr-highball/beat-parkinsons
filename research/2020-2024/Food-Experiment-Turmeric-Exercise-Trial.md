# Registered turmeric formulation and exercise soreness study

## Registered exercise soreness outcome

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT04946981 on July 1, 2021 before the reported July 12 enrollment start. The randomized, masked crossover included a proprietary turmeric formulation and placebo in moderately active adults. The current registry lists 45 enrolled and measures quadriceps soreness during two squats on a 100 mm visual analog scale through 72 hours. It does not separately list the percent recovery from maximum soreness to 72 hours. The registry had no posted summary results at the November 15 check. The separately published journal results cover the same cohort.
