# Fennel seed clinical endpoint follow-up

## First conference report on fennel tea and isolated gastric muscle

Annahazi and Schemann’s conference abstract NGS15049-91 reports that fennel tea relaxed guinea pig fundus and corpus strips and increased spontaneous antral contraction amplitude. Antral tone and frequency showed no detected changes. The closely matching setup, exposure range and endpoint pattern appear in the group’s 2025 full report. Count this as an earlier public report of the study family. A 2023 follow-up abstract under DOI 10.1111/nmo.14637 specifically investigated nerve and nitric oxide contributions.

## Fennel fruit extract in loperamide delayed stomach emptying

Choi and colleagues studied oral fennel fruit extract in a mouse model of loperamide-induced dysmotility. Figure 7 reports less stomach dye retention and reduced stomach weight in treated mice compared with loperamide controls. Figure 9 separately reports restored small-intestinal dye progression. The public May preprint and June 6 journal paper represent one study. The tested extract and drug-induced model leave the effect of culinary toasted fennel on levodopa and Parkinson OFF time open.
