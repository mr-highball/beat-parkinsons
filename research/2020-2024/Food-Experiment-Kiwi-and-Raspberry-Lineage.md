# Kiwifruit and raspberry study lineages and outcomes

## POLYAGE registry and distinction between registered and reported outcomes

First posted April 16, 2020 and updated September 16, 2021. The completed ClinicalTrials.gov record reports 90 enrolled and lists cocoa flavanols, berry anthocyanins, their combination and a control. Brachial flow-mediated dilation is primary, with Stroop and Tower of London change listed secondarily. Anxiety is absent from the displayed outcomes. The 2026 anxiety article reports 59 people in three active arms. The record does not explain that difference, and has no posted registry results. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04348162) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2020-NCT04348162-POLYAGE-Registry.txt)

## Dried fruit composition of the POLYAGE mixture

December 21, 2021 online, in the 2022 issue. Section 2.3 specifies dried red currants 33.3%, blackcurrants 33.3%, raspberries 16.7% and blueberries 16.7%. Participants added their formulation to a selected beverage or breakfast food. The cognitive paper and the later anxiety report share the POLYAGE registration. No raspberry-only or frozen raspberry arm was tested. [Original source](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/14/1/1) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2021-Garcia-Cordero-POLYAGE-Ingredient-Composition.txt)

## Registered green kiwifruit constipation and wellbeing outcomes

First registered May 24, 2021. The open-label KINGS comparison assigns two green kiwifruit daily for four weeks or maltodextrin in adults with functional constipation or constipation-predominant irritable bowel syndrome. The registration centers on abdominal pain and includes fatigue and psychological measures. It provides no named attention task, Parkinson population or posted article results. [Original source](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=381723) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2021-ACTRN12621000621819-KINGS-Registry.txt)

## Mood and fatigue outcomes in the complete KINGS thesis

The University of Otago displays October 2, 2024 as published or e-published. The thesis deposit date was not independently verified. The original complete thesis Methods on printed pages 298–299 and Table 5.7 on pages 302–303 report wellbeing, mood and total fatigue after fresh green kiwi and maltodextrin in adults with constipation. Total MFSI-SF has a reported between-arm p=.57, with a displayed contrast and interval whose signs need clarification. The table gives no attention-specific result or attention task. KINGS has registration ACTRN12621000621819, separate from the Massey sleep and cognition study. ## Annonaceae exposure and cognition in a Caribbean parkinsonism cohort

October 10, 2022 online. The analysis includes 180 of 305 people with degenerative parkinsonism, including 74 classified as Parkinson disease and 106 atypical. Attention and working memory were assessed through digit span. Fruit or juice exposure above the study threshold was associated with membership in the severe clinical cluster (reported OR 3.76, 95% CI 1.13–15.18). The result describes a combined clinical profile with retrospective consumption and selected neuropsychological participation. The exposure comprises soursop, sweetsop and custard apple, with no kiwi comparison. A later Parkinson-only geographic analysis uses the same registry and Caribbean group size. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10092620/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2022-Cleret-de-Langavant-Annonaceae-Cognition.txt)

## Green and gold kiwi protein digestion registration and results

The registry dates its first public posting to April 22, 2020, following the April 21 retrospective registration date used by the paper. It reports results first posted November 4, 2021. The posted outcome modules concern whole-body net protein balance and skeletal-muscle protein metabolism using stable-isotope methods. The record links the Park crossover and gives no separate attention outcome. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04356573) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2020-NCT04356573-Kiwi-Protein-Kinetics-Registry.txt)

## Measured amino acid outcomes in the green and gold kiwi crossover

First published September 24, 2020. Twelve healthy older adults consumed two Hayward green or two Hort 16A gold kiwifruit daily for 14 days and again with 100 g of ground beef in a crossover. Early plasma essential amino acids rose faster with green fruit, with treatment-by-time interactions for essential and branched-chain amino acids and leucine. Total essential amino acid absorption and measured protein kinetics did not differ by fruit. The study did not measure attention. [Original source](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00394-020-02363-5) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2020-Park-Kiwi-Protein-Kinetics-Outcomes.txt)
