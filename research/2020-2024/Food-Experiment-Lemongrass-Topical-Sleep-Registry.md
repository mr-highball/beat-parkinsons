# Lemongrass oil in a topical sleep product

## Registered multicomponent lotion and sleep quality

NCT06146712 was first posted November 27, 2023, after its listed October 2 completion. This single-group study enrolled 35 adults and registered changes in the 28-item Sleep Quality Scale, Fitbit-measured sleep time and wearable sleep score. Its topical lotion combined melatonin and magnesium with ingredients including *Cymbopogon flexuosus* oil and citral. The public registration has no posted results; it lists neither polysomnography nor a separate REM sleep behavior or dream-enactment outcome. The uncontrolled, multicomponent topical preparation does not estimate effects of brewed lemongrass or an individual ingredient.
