# Caffeine and Parkinson motor outcomes

## Short caffeine trial and total motor responder status

In 2024 Hamdan and colleagues reported a double-blind trial of 50 mg isolated caffeine twice daily, or starch placebo, for 21 days. Twelve of 15 caffeine completers and two of 12 placebo completers exceeded the prespecified four-point total MDS-UPDRS III improvement threshold (p = .004, OR 20, 95% CI 2.77–144.31). Baseline motor scores differed across arms. Tea was among the beverages participants were told to avoid. No bradykinesia result is available separately from the composite. [Original report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11391974/)
