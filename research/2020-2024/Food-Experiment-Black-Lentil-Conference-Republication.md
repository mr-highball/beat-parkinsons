# Later report of the black-lentil Parkinson cohort

## Later report of the black-lentil Parkinson cohort

The 2024 proceedings item P03.01 repeats the 2017 comparison of eight participants receiving sesame oil prepared with black lentils and other botanicals and seven receiving sesame oil alone. Reported whole-scale and component p-values and symptom descriptions match the 2017 findings. The intervention was rectal. No separate rigidity value or independent between-arm estimate is printed. This is a second report of the same 15 participants.
