# Lemongrass preparation and symptom evidence

## Concentrated leaf extract capsules in chronic kidney disease

Sixty-four patients with stage 3 chronic kidney disease were randomized in Thailand to capsules containing 900 mg/day of spray-dried aqueous Cymbopogon citratus leaf extract or placebo over 90 days. The investigators used clinical safety and renal biomarker endpoints. A Thai ministry research summary describes a matching 64-person, 900 mg/day project and reports increased 24-hour urine volume after ten days in the extract group, without a numerical between-arm difference. These measures and home blood pressure documentation are distinct from a urinary urgency measure. The registry identifier TCTR20230720001 is printed in the original journal Methods. Indexed registry access did not yield a readable full record. This is a concentrated oral capsule preparation in kidney disease, without brewed tea as the intervention or a Parkinson cohort. ## Topical mixed compress plus balance exercise in one older adult

One older person with joint pain and balance difficulty received a topical warm ginger and lemongrass mixture and performed balance exercise for seven days. The Berg Balance Scale rose from 8 at baseline to 30 on day seven. No comparator separated the botanical compress from exercise or warming. Parkinson disease was not reported for this participant, the lemongrass species is not given, and no oral preparation or counted falls endpoint was used. A closely matching institutional training paper describes the same participant and setting. ## Lemongrass fragrance notes and dream emotionality

Martinec Nováková and colleagues published May 6, 2024. Twenty healthy adults each selected a fragrance, then underwent scent and odorless sessions in balanced order. Two participants selected different mixed products whose listed fragrance notes include lemongrass. The pooled analysis after REM awakenings found no clear differences in dream emotionality or post-sleep affect. The study supplies neither an estimate for either specific cartridge nor observed dream enactment, oral tea or a Parkinson comparison. ## Lemongrass iced tea before an exercise-associated reaction

In one 2022 case, a child consumed lemongrass iced tea and potato chips before exercise and developed urticaria and dysphonia. Allergy testing and the history of symptoms again after potato exposure favor a competing explanation. The original does not establish lemongrass attribution or measure acoustic loudness. The exposure was a beverage consumed alongside another food, outside Parkinson disease.
