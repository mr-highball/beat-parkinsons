# Cardamom in a multi-ingredient dental pain tablet source record

## Cardamom in a multi-ingredient dental pain tablet

Shinde and colleagues published June 25, 2022. In a randomized split-mouth active comparison, 22 orthodontic patients each had a premolar-extraction site assigned to Analdent or aceclofenac. The multi-ingredient tablet lists 15 mg cardamom seed powder with other plant powders. Table 3 gives mean VAS at 8 hours 5.95 versus 5.14 (p = .383), and at 72 hours 0.86 versus 1.18 (p = .525). The main text gives a conflicting 8-hour aceclofenac number. This trial compared active products and supplied no cardamom-only or untreated arm. The authors give CTRI/2020/03/024296, with independent registry confirmation pending. [Original Methods and Tables 2–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9871148/)
