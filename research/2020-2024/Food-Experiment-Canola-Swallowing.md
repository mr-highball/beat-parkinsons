# Rapeseed-containing foods and bolus properties, 2020–2024

## Rapeseed-containing texture-modified food and bolus testing

Stading's 2021 study compared model foods as regular, timbale and gel textures. Five healthy volunteers contributed oral processing measures, with extensional tests performed using saliva substitute. The methods list rapeseed oil in the texture-modified food preparation. Chews-to-swallow and rheological differences accompany a change in product texture. The study neither varies rapeseed oil alone nor measures Parkinson dysphagia severity or a verified cold-pressed product.
