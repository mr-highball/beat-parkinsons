# Dietary exposure and symptom evidence

## Pepper-containing supplement and urgent voids

Published December 14, 2020. In an uncontrolled 12-week study, 238 women with overactive bladder completed treatment with a mixture containing pepper extract, pumpkin seed extract, vitamins, glutamine and resveratrol. The mixture supplied 10 mg pepper extract per tablet, with two tablets daily. Three-day diary urgent voids changed from 4.71 ± 1.28 to 2.53 ± 1.22 per day (reported within-group p < .0001). Neurogenic bladder or neurological abnormality was excluded. There was no comparison group or pepper-only arm. The table's separately printed change in overall voids does not agree with its displayed means. ## Self-reported hot pepper sensitivity in veterans with bladder pain

First online August 24, 2022. In the veterans questionnaire, sensitivity to spicy or ethnic foods was reported by 94/266 participants with interstitial cystitis or bladder pain syndrome, 6/68 with another pelvic pain condition, and 6/91 controls (p < .001). Hot pepper counts were 80/266, 5/68 and 5/91. These are recalled effects on grouped urinary or bladder symptoms. The study did not isolate urgency frequency and did not document smoked paprika ingestion or Parkinson disease as the study population.
