## Celery extract and sleep quality in older adults registration

Iran’s official IRCT record 75723, IRCT20240217061029N1, gives March 4, 2024 as its prospective registration date. It displays recruitment complete, without actual recruitment dates or quantitative results in the checked record. The plan targets 70 adults aged 60 or older, randomized to dry celery extract capsules or cornstarch capsules before bedtime for four weeks. The Persian intervention gives cornstarch in mg, correcting an English summary's mL inconsistency. Neither record establishes fresh culinary leaf as the exposed plant part. The prespecified primary outcome is overall Pittsburgh sleep score, without a separately specified waking sleepiness or Parkinson measure.
