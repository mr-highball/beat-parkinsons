# Olive oil as a comparator in a Parkinson mouse model

The 2023 Theranostics article randomized an MPTP mouse experiment that included intragastric olive oil alone, model control, an active pharmaceutical comparator, and fullerene dissolved in olive oil at multiple concentrations. The article does not identify the oil as cold pressed. [Local original](Source-Text/PMC10526674.txt) · [Publisher text and Figure 4](https://www.thno.org/v13p4936.htm)

Figure 4 and its results distinguish the formulation and oil vehicle. The authors report no significant oil-only changes in the prevention-phase pole turn/descent and rotarod measures. Sample sizes vary across measures. These results address mouse bradykinesia-related performance and coordination. They leave human bradykinesia, clinical postural instability, and a specific culinary preparation unresolved.

The 2025 carbon nanoparticle review describes the mixture. It points to the same olive-oil experiment and belongs to the same evidence family. [Review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12330272/)
