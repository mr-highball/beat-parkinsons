# Cranberry extract and related depression measures

## Cranberry anthocyanins and mouse test behavior

Wang and colleagues first published the berry extract study on June 14, 2024. The cranberry extract groups showed no detected change in either tail-suspension or forced-swimming immobility compared with vehicle in Figure 2. Blueberry and black mulberry had different findings in at least one assay. These mouse behavior measures leave dried cranberries and human depression unresolved. ## Preliminary altitude mixture and depression comparison

The October 2024 conference issue includes an abstract on NCT04111263. Its interpretation suggested that a fiber and polyphenol mixture containing cranberry could attenuate depression at simulated high altitude. That reading uses comparisons involving low-altitude placebo. The 2025 full paper supplies the direct high-altitude treatment comparison.
