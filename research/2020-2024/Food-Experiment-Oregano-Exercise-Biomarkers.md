# Oregano exercise biomarkers

## Dried oregano powder and exercise biomarkers

Shirvani and colleagues reported this randomized comparison on December 28, 2022. Twenty-four healthy male soldiers received a 500 mg capsule of dried, ground Origanum vulgare plant material or starch after a combat-readiness test. The report does not specify the plant part. Essential oil distilled for chemical profiling came from a separate sample.

Creatine kinase showed a group-by-time interaction (p < 0.0001). Levels were higher in the starch group at 60 and 120 minutes (both p < 0.0001). The study measured blood biomarkers and supplied no clinical anxiety or subjective fatigue scores.
