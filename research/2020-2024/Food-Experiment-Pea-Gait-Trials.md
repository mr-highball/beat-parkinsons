# Pea Gait Trials

## Mixed pea protein and peptide powder in a sarcopenia gait composite

First posted 2024-07-12. A blinded prototype powder trial enrolled people aged 50–70 with sarcopenia and knee or hip osteoarthritis. The 12-week intervention includes soy isolate and peptides, wheat protein, pea protein and pea peptide, with aloe and excipients in both arms. The Short Physical Performance Battery includes gait speed, chair stand and balance. The registry shows completed status and no posted results as captured 2026-11-16. The study assigns a protein mixture and a composite, without an isolated cooked green-pea or Parkinson gait estimate. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06499012) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2024-NCT06499012-Pea-Blend-SPPB.txt)

## Mixed plant protein and six meter gait testing in sarcopenia

First posted 2023-05-22. People with sarcopenia are assigned 12 weeks of whey isolate, a soy isolate, pea isolate and brown-rice protein mix, or nonprotein placebo. Six-meter gait speed is specified as a physical-performance measure. Registry status remains unknown and no results are posted as captured 2026-11-16. The protein blend cannot isolate the effect of cooked green peas. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05869305) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2023-NCT05869305-Pea-Mix-Gait.txt)
