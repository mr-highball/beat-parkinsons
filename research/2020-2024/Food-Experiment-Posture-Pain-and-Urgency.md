# Posture, pain, urinary urgency and related outcomes

## Dyskinesia in a case receiving palmitoylethanolamide and luteolin

Brotini reported a Parkinson patient with peak-dose leg and trunk dyskinesia. The report describes resolution after four months of adding a palmitoylethanolamide and luteolin product to carbidopa/levodopa. Earlier ropinirole and levodopa comparisons make this a multi-treatment clinical course. The uncontrolled case gives no separate luteolin or thyme exposure, and no ingredient-attributable dyskinesia change. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8794485/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2021-Brotini-Luteolin-Dyskinesia-Extract.txt)

## Spinach extract thesis and planned balance assessment

Losada Zafrilla's thesis gives balance as objective 9 on printed p. 75 and lists two balance visits and NCT04612127 on p. 95. No balance result was located in the body. The thesis design diagram labels maltodextrin and the official registry lists sucrose. Both documents concern the same trial of spinach extract with strength training. The repository records public availability January 17, 2024, following the November 2023 defense and institutional deposit. ## Soybean and corn oil comparator in COPD walking outcomes

Engelen et al. randomized 46 people with COPD. The original report's abstract identifies placebo soybean and corn oil capsules plus maltodextrin powder, and lists six-minute distance, four-metre gait speed and postural balance among secondary outcomes. It gives no clear functional finding or oil-specific arm estimates for stride length. The authors' public repository record and publisher abstract are available. The original tables and supplement outcomes remain unverified. [Original source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39181037/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2024-Engelen-Oil-Comparator-Walking-Extract.txt)

## Danshen root urgency results in a placebo comparison

Shin et al. randomized 136 men with lower urinary tract symptoms to two doses of Salvia miltiorrhiza root extract or placebo. In Table 1 the 12-week urgency changes were −0.20, −0.38 and +0.03, with p = .1619 and .2917 versus placebo. The 800 mg arm had an earlier six-week urgency contrast at p = .0263. The abstract's general urgency wording should be read against the outcome window and the individual results. Salvia miltiorrhiza root differs from the ground common-sage leaf in claim 0593. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11723278/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/PMC11723278.txt)
