# Leafy grain search replay evidence, 2020–2024

## Swiss chard and buckwheat juice blend in dietary magnesium reduction

The authors gavaged a dried and reconstituted juice of Swiss chard leaves plus buckwheat aerial parts to healthy male rats under reduced dietary magnesium. The blend increased CSF magnesium. Novel-object recognition showed a group effect at p = 0.0266 and no treatment-by-time interaction at p = 0.237. The Results identify low-magnesium vehicle as the significant recognition comparator, while the Figure 7 caption marks normal-diet control. A blend and a memory outcome give limited context for the toasted-groats attention claim. ## Boiled spinach and pastry in an Italian food-contamination investigation

An investigation recovered boiled spinach and spinach-stuffed pastry leftovers after anticholinergic poisoning. Chemical analysis identified atropine and scopolamine, with the contaminant plant species still uncertain. This is a clinically relevant example of contamination surviving food preparation. The series supplies no Parkinson-specific visual hallucination frequency or clean-spinach comparison. [Primary article held by University of Milan](https://air.unimi.it/retrieve/f98dce64-0ab5-42be-be13-0a2ac622ba56/1-s2.0-S0956713524000513-main.pdf) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2024-Caprai-Spinach-Tropane-Contamination.txt)

## Persian barley water in a multicomponent hyperthyroidism case

The 2024 case describes qualitative anxiety improvement while a woman with uncontrolled hyperthyroidism received a package of Persian barley water, lemon balm tea and lifestyle modification. The abstract gives neither a standardized anxiety score nor an isolated barley or Parkinson comparison.
