# Asparagus and measured salivary outcomes

## Asparagus oral liquid trial and open original access

Yan and Wang are indexed in a later systematic review as an original 2022 trial in Heilongjiang Medical Science 45(3):94–95. The review describes 28 burning-mouth patients and fresh Asparagus officinalis oral liquid plus mecobalamin versus a different conventional-drug regimen. Its reported unstimulated salivary-flow difference is +0.20 mL/min (95% CI 0.10 to 0.29), with high risk of bias. The original report and manufacturing details remain unavailable. The reported endpoint is saliva output, and the trial did not establish drooling after steamed asparagus in Parkinson’s.
