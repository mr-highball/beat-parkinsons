# Mango and cilantro PubMed replay follow-up

## FruktBAR protocol includes mango in rotating smoothies

The September 2022 randomized trial protocol lists four optional commercial combinations. Two contain mango. Participants receiving opioid agonist treatment were to receive one 250 ml bottle per day for 16 weeks while controls continued usual care. The trial specifies the three-item fatigue scale as a secondary endpoint. It assigned the mixed-food intervention as a whole. ## Coriander in a mixed supplement before hypoglycemic syncope

A 2024 report describes a 67-year-old man with insulin autoimmune syndrome confirmed by an insulin autoantibody level above 300 U/mL. Episodes mostly occurred during fasting, with recent capillary glucose readings of 52 and 64 mg/dL during episodes and a history of falls. The patient had used an oral four-herb supplement containing coriander roughly two years before symptoms, along with an unrelated two-week omeprazole exposure four weeks before symptom onset. The authors favored omeprazole on timing and listed the supplement as a possible alternative. The authors did not specify coriander plant part, a culinary drying method or dose. The report has no active-standing protocol, treatment attribution to coriander alone or Parkinson cohort.
