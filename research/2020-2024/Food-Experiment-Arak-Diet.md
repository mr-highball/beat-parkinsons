# Dietary questionnaire and Parkinson case status in Arak

## 2023 dietary patterns and food coding

Shokri-Mashhadi and colleagues analyzed dietary patterns in 105 newly diagnosed Parkinson cases and 215 controls. The article first appeared online on November 23, 2023. In adjusted comparisons of the highest and lowest pattern-score tertiles, the reported odds ratios were 0.002 for the traditional pattern, 0.314 for the healthy pattern, 0.282 for the light pattern and 7.26 for the Western pattern. These are odds of diagnosis in a case-control sample. The reported values do not estimate any food-specific symptom effect. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10072-023-07204-x)

The publisher’s supplement identifies boiled potato in the potato category. Fries and potato chips are assigned to fast food and salty snack groups. It pools green olives with olive oil and lists a separate unspecified vegetable oil group. Its food codes distinguish raw and cooked carrots and raw and fried onions. Coffee, tea and pepper lack a specific preparation in the supplement. Fruits include figs, dates and strawberries. The supplement provides group definitions and factor loadings, without participant-level intake, food-by-symptom models or clinical outcomes by food. [Publisher’s supplementary tables](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs10072-023-07204-x/MediaObjects/10072_2023_7204_MOESM1_ESM.docx)

Two 2026 reports use the same ethics approval, matching sample sizes and overlapping investigators. They give additional comparisons by diagnosis and should be counted with the original as one study family. [2026 companion analyses](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Arak-Diet.md)
