# Saffron extracts and insomnia measures outside Parkinson disease

## Affron and the insomnia severity index

Lopresti and colleagues randomized 63 adults with self-reported poor sleep in 2020. Fifty-five provided analysis data. After 28 days, the Insomnia Severity Index group-by-time comparison favored standardized Affron extract over placebo (p = .017). Table 2 gives an extract-group mean of 11.74 averaged across days 7–28. Figure 2 presents the individual follow-up visits. The methods specify 28 days. The brief summary gives an inconsistent duration of eight weeks. [Original study, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7849671/)

## Affron and the insomnia symptom questionnaire

A different four-week study from Lopresti and colleagues published in 2021 randomized 120 adults with unsatisfactory sleep to two Affron extract groups or placebo. Its original abstract reports improved Insomnia Symptom Questionnaire total score and classifications in addition to the primary subjective sleep-quality result. It supplies no Parkinson population or ground saffron arm. [Original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34438361/)

## Extract and subjective sleep quality in primary sleep complaints

In a 2021 trial by Pachikian and colleagues, 66 adults with mild to moderate sleep complaints were assigned saffron extract or maltodextrin. The Insomnia Severity Index determined eligibility. Outcomes used actigraphy and sleep questionnaires including the Leeds questionnaire and PSQI. These are useful sleep outcomes with a different scale and preparation.
