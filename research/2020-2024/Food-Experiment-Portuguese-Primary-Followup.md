# Primary Portuguese-language diet research

## Nutrient recalls and breakfast levodopa timing at Univates

Renata Gabriela Wermann and Patricia Fassina published their descriptive study in *Destaques Acadêmicos* on October 28, 2024. The seven participants, four men and three women with Hoehn and Yahr stages 1–3, were recruited from a university extension program in Rio Grande do Sul and assessed from March to May 2024. Three 24-hour recalls were summarized as nutrient totals. Table 2 records that all seven took levodopa while fasting, 60–90 minutes before breakfast. Table 3 reports mean dietary iron of 11.93 mg/day in men and 11.61 mg/day in women, and mean fiber of 20.17 and 24.50 g/day, respectively. The article gives no item-level food data, participant-level symptom comparisons or measurements of a medication response to a food. The similarly titled Univates meeting abstract is a protocol. The 2024 sample and authors are distinct from the [September 2018 Silva and colleagues abstract](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Portuguese-Primary-Followup.md#levodopa-and-dietary-protein-proceedings-archive). [Univates journal record](https://www.univates.br/revistas/index.php/destaques/article/view/3857), [primary report, Methods and Tables 1–3](https://univates.br/revistas/index.php/destaques/article/download/3857/2480/13688), [Univates protocol abstract, p. 193](https://www.univates.br/editora-univates/media/publicacoes/424/pdf_424.pdf#page=193)

## Diet recalls and constipation in Blumenau

Daniela Falchetti Samulewski, Michel Seemann, Samuel Faganello, Luciane Azevedo and Daniela Ewald assessed 18 people with Parkinson’s disease from the SuperAção Parkinson group in Blumenau, Brazil, using a 24-hour diet recall, Rome III constipation questions, a dysphagia questionnaire and a nutritional assessment. Eleven of 18 met the constipation criteria and 16 of 18 screened negative for dysphagia risk. Fourteen of 18 ate less fruit than recommended, 13 of 18 ate less vegetables, 15 of 18 consumed less fiber, and 11 of 18 drank less milk. Eight of 18 ate more meat than recommended. The abstract reports symptom prevalence and dietary intake separately. It provides no food-specific comparison for constipation or dysphagia. [2021 FURB teaching proceedings, p. 59](https://www.furb.br/web/upl/arquivos/202111241009400.Ensino.pdf?20260227232500=#page=59), [FURB program showing the same project in two tracks](https://www.furb.br/web/upl/arquivos/202111101507320.Apresentacoes_banners2.pdf?20220525184144=)

## Water and fiber by constipation status at UNIFESP

Cristiane Siviero Scorza’s UNIFESP thesis was defended March 9, 2023, and publicly available April 13, 2023. It compared 31 people with Parkinson’s disease with 33 controls and used a food-frequency questionnaire and bowel and medication measures. In the Parkinson group, median fiber intake was 37 g in the 22 participants with constipation and 32 g in the nine without constipation (p = .78). Median water intake per kg was 18 versus 26, respectively (p = .094). Table 7 reports correlations of the increase in levodopa dose with water intake per kg (rho −.32, p = .08) and fiber intake (rho .23, p = .22). Table 5 reports that 17 of 31 people with Parkinson’s reported an improvement in motor symptoms after bowel evacuation. The comparisons address total fiber and water intake and provide no outcome estimate for a named food. The cross-sectional results do not establish the effect of changing intake. ## Combined prebiotic fibers and motor behavior in mice

João Gabriel Falcão de Moraes’s UFPE report is dated August 26, 2024 and was made available in the repository November 25, 2024. It reports three groups of ten male mice. In the rotenone model, a combined oral fructooligosaccharide and galactooligosaccharide preparation was given during model induction. The combination increased rotarod latency and open-field crossings relative to the rotenone group. The values reported in the results text and Figure 4 caption disagree, so the direction is clearer than the precise significance values in this version. Two related 2023 presentations are identifiable by title in the research group’s bibliography, but their abstracts and outcome data were unavailable. The 2024 report, 2025 thesis and 2026 journal article use the same ethics protocol, model, preparation and some identical results. Treat them as potentially overlapping reports. The fiber combination was not a named food and the tests assessed mouse motor behavior.
