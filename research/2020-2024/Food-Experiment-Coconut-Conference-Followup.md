# Virgin coconut oil, conference and clinical outcome follow-up

## Hospitalized infection trial registration

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT04849637 on April 19, 2021. The plan listed 74 participants, comparing virgin coconut oil plus hospital usual care with usual care. Primary measures are hospital stay and overall symptom resolution. The record does not specify fatigue as a separate outcome and has no posted results. The later original article also identifies Philippine Health Research Registry PHRR210303-003392. [Official NCT04849637 record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04849637) · [Philippine registry record](https://adnu.herdin.ph/index.php/registry?cid=3392&layout=details&view=research)

## Hospitalized infection trial results

Alejandria and colleagues reported the open-label randomized trial on May 15, 2024 (PMID 38812763, PMC11132283, DOI 10.47895/amp.vi0.7498). Thirty-nine inpatients received virgin coconut oil plus usual care and 38 received usual care alone. Mean time to overall symptom resolution was 6.8 versus 6.74 days (p=.91). Adverse events occurred in 10/39 versus 2/38 participants (reported RR 4.87, 95% CI 1.14–20.79). The article provides no separate fatigue estimate. This is a different study from the 76-person COVID-19 cohort PMID 38282651. The same-issue editorial PMID 38812765 describes fatigue in a separate lagundi study. [Original article and tables](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11132283/) · [Original editorial](https://actamedicaphilippina.upm.edu.ph/index.php/acta/article/view/10339)

## P90 virgin coconut oil and motor observations in mice

Changdar and colleagues' P90 conference abstract reports better grip, gait, kyphosis, hindlimb clasping and pole activity in virgin coconut-oil treatment groups than in untreated rotenone-model mice. The source reports qualitative comparisons, with no numerical effect estimates, treatment quantities, administration route or fatigue measure. The publisher lists January 30, 2024 as first publication online, with a December 2023 issue and October 2023 meeting. An adjacent P84 abstract describes a model with overlapping authors without the oil intervention. Participant or animal overlap cannot be verified.

The ICMR 2023 selection list names the related project IIRPIG-2023-0001508. Selection was conditional and the record gives no experimental outcome or verified cohort link to P90. The institutional 2021–2022 rat project is another lead, with species different from P90. The proceedings contain the only verified source-specific outcome for P90 in this pass.
