# Thyme Anxiety Comparators

## Wild thyme gastrointestinal trial registration

The current record reports an actual February 1, 2024 start, lists 75 estimated participants, crossover allocation and investigator-only masking, and names intestinal microbiome composition as the sole primary outcome. The public fields do not identify an anxiety outcome. The later article explicitly links the same accession but reports 178 randomized in a parallel, double-blind comparison and treats gastrointestinal symptom score as primary. The paper attributes differences to registry formatting, and the checked public files do not establish how the design and size records align.
