# COMBAT cranberry phenolic metabolite analysis

Tosi and colleagues first published the COMBAT secondary analysis on June 28, 2023. It uses the original randomized cohort of 60 healthy adults aged 50–80, with 29 assigned freeze-dried cranberry powder and 31 assigned placebo for 12 weeks. The intervention delivered two 4.5 g sachets daily. The authors analyzed phenolic metabolites in plasma and urine before and after treatment. The main report and supplementary tables address product composition, laboratory measurements and metabolite profiles. They report no PHQ-9 score or depression comparison by randomized arm.
