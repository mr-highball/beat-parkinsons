# Risk and Prevention

### 2023 — Parkinson’s incidence after service at Camp Lejeune

A retrospective cohort of veterans found more Parkinson’s diagnoses among those stationed at Camp Lejeune than at Camp Pendleton. The comparison used residence at the base as a proxy for contaminated-water exposure. The study could not isolate trichloroethylene from coexposures. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37184848/)
