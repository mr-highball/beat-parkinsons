# Bundled Oil Symptoms

## Registration of stratified diabetes self-management

The WHO ICTRP mirror gives July 16, 2022 as registration and July 17 as planned first enrollment. It marks registration prospective and lists a target of 30 per arm. Primary fields include cognition, self-management and fasting glucose. A broad mental-health field appears under secondary outcomes, without an anxiety scale or a separate depression instrument in the WHO snapshot. The mirror was refreshed April 3, 2023. The originating ChiCTR site returned a verification page during review, so the original revision history and July-level outcome detail are unverified. [Original source](https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=ChiCTR2200061991) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/Trials/ChiCTR2200061991.txt)

## Earliest public protocol for the stratified program

The protocol dated October 21, 2022 identifies ChiCTR2200061991. It names anxiety and depression as secondary hypotheses for a 12-week support program and specifies the Zung Self-Rating Anxiety Scale and Geriatric Depression Scale. The assessment prose lists weeks 0, 13 and 24. The publication establishes a named measurement plan by October 2022. It provides no outcome estimates. [Original source](https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-2150228/v1/71a80a98-4172-47a0-86d7-e5b27c6f627b.pdf?c=1676672068) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2022-Stratified-Diabetes-Self-Management-Protocol-Preprint.txt)

## Published protocol with optional cooking oils

The 2023 protocol for ChiCTR2200061991 studied a combined self-management program in older adults with type 2 diabetes and mild cognitive impairment. Table 2 offers olive, peanut, walnut, soybean and rapeseed oils as optional choices under dietary advice. The design assigns the support program and gives no separate oil arm, processing grade, intake or ingredient-specific adherence. The planned anxiety and depression instruments apply to the full program. See the [2025 results](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Bundled-Oil-Symptoms.md#anxiety-in-the-stratified-self-management-comparison).
