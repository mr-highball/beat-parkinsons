## Fennel extract nanosuspension and peripheral motor recovery

The 2023 primary report evaluated a fennel extract nanosuspension after sciatic nerve injury in mice. Methods 2.7.3 and Results 3.2 describe an ipsilateral hind-limb grip and sciatic functional index. These outcomes concern peripheral recovery and the extract differs from ground culinary seed. The authors do not assess hand writing size or parkinsonian micrographia. Independence from earlier related extract experiments requires checking their cohorts. [Open primary paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2218-1989/13/3/391)
