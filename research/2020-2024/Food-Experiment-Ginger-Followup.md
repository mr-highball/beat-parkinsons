# Ginger preparations in Parkinson’s research, 2020–2024

## Dried ginger in Duzhong Fang motor tests

Li and colleagues published their mouse study on July 23, 2021. Duzhong Fang was a four-herb ethanol extract made from *Eucommia ulmoides*, Dendrobium, Rehmanniae Radix and dried ginger in a raw weight ratio of 200:2:3:3. The study administered the extract by gavage across the MPTP model induction period. There was no ginger-only arm. [Primary report, preparation and experimental design](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.2147/JIR.S316314)

The authors’ abstract uses the term dyskinesia. Figure 1E–H reports shorter pole descent time, longer rotarod retention, greater grip strength and higher traction scores with the extract versus MPTP alone (10 mice per group for behavioral tests). The methods give no levodopa challenge, involuntary-movement severity score or stride-length measurement. The study supplies a mixed-extract motor result for the evidence trail on `0271` and `0566`. [Primary report, Figure 1 and Behavioral Tests](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8315774/#f0001)

Shen and colleagues’ subsequent article, first published July 18, 2024, cites the previous motor work and evaluates the same named four-herb preparation in a rotenone mouse model. Its primary abstract reports motor and cognitive improvements with the combined formula. The toxin models distinguish the two experiments. Neither study isolates the effect of ginger. [2024 primary abstract, PMID 39019600](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ddt/18/4/18_2024.01035/_article/-char/en)
