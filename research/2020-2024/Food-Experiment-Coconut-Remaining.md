# A ketogenic diet, sleep behavior and speech observations

Tidman and colleagues published a 24-week uncontrolled follow-up on June 13, 2024. Seven Parkinson's participants continued from a 12-week pilot; the paper says participants received Phillips ketogenic educational materials, submitted food logs and had ketosis monitored. The researchers did not publish ingredient-level logs. In the main interview account, participant 008 described possible slowing of episodes they called REM sleep behavior disorder but still about two episodes per week, a recent injury to a bed partner, and no clear overall change attributed to the diet. The supplement records the same participant's companion perceiving less vivid dreaming and movement. Neither account supplies a quantitative baseline frequency or confirms polysomnographic diagnosis. These descriptions are one case with different perspectives, not two independent observations or evidence that episodes worsened. [Primary article, qualitative results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11457624/) · [Supplement](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC11457624.1/INMT_A_2352394_SM0001.docx) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/PMID38869924-coconut-qualitative-extract.txt)

The main article's statement about drinking regular coconut or avocado oil belongs to participant 019, not 008. Their oil brand, processing and actual choice are not established, and the article does not identify which oil participant 008 consumed. A companion of participant 013 reported better talking and facial expression, without a standardized intelligibility assessment or a linked oil intake. No causal conclusion about virgin coconut oil or refined coconut oil can be drawn from either case. The main trial is a multi-food intervention without a control. [Article results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11457624/)

The Phillips 2018 ketogenic meal plan lists virgin coconut oil in several recipes, with multiple other foods; the low-fat group used a different diet. Participants marked meal completion rather than reporting ingredient-specific amounts. Total MDS-UPDRS motor scores include a combined speech item but are not a listener-scored intelligibility measure; no separate relevant item estimate is given. Oil grade in the frozen 0963 hypothesis is refined, not the specified virgin oil.
