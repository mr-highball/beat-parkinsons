# Safflower oil comparators and anxiety outcomes

## Safflower and olive comparator in a bipolar feasibility trial

Results were first released as a June 29, 2023 preprint. The May 24, 2024 article identifies NCT03541031 and reports 69 randomized and 49 analyzed. Its comparison was a multinutrient product plus fish oil versus both olive- and safflower-oil placebos in bipolar outpatients. The Hamilton Anxiety row prints coefficient −1.274, SE 2.754 and p=.646. The confidence interval printed as −4.264 to 6.812 centers on +1.274, exposing a sign discrepancy. No independent safflower contrast is available. ## Reported anxiety scores in a safflower comparator crossover

In the posted Glut1-deficiency crossover results, adult PROMIS anxiety T-score change was +0.5 (SE 1.45, n=25) on safflower oil and +3.1 (SE 1.49, n=23) on triheptanoin. Their adjusted difference was 2.6 points (95% CI −0.7 to 5.9, p=.1138). The active-comparator and period changes leave safflower versus no oil unresolved. The trial used an unspecified oil grade in another disorder.
