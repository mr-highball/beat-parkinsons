# Parkinson nutrition and feeding reports from the 2023 and 2024 NHO meetings

## 2023 catechin roll for contracted hands

Nakabe and colleagues compared a hand-held catechin roll with nonwoven gauze, each used for one week, in six Parkinson patients with hand contractures. On day seven, odor-meter readings decreased in four with the roll, while three had less maceration or fewer skin problems. The authors discuss green tea in the preparation and possible effects of moisture absorption. The roll was held in the hand. The study measured local skin and odor outcomes and gives no brewed tea, olfaction, handwriting or fatigue comparison. The presenting hospital confirms the October 20–21, 2023 meeting. [Primary abstract P1-31-6](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000204087.pdf#page=283) · [Presenting hospital's meeting record](https://yanai.hosp.go.jp/tayori/file/036.pdf)

## 2023 and 2024 energy intake and hypoglycemia

Tani and colleagues reported an 80-year-old patient with Hoehn and Yahr stage V Parkinson’s, low body weight and recurrent nighttime hypoglycemia documented by continuous monitoring. After the team increased energy intake, weight rose from 30.5 to 33.1 kg and albumin from 3.0 to 3.5 g/dL. Hypoglycemia improved. Dyskinesia persisted despite concurrent adjustment of Parkinson medication. The abstract gives no food composition and cannot isolate the effect of energy intake from other care. A separate Parkinson case at the same meeting used high-energy liquid supplements and an unnamed trace-element drink during medication ON periods, alongside extensive care for a pressure wound. It reports healing without a separate nutrition effect or a Parkinson symptom endpoint. [Primary case P1-106-2](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000204087.pdf#page=427) · [Primary case O1-9-3](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000204087.pdf#page=646)

At the June 2023 neurology meeting, the same Utano group used a six-month window before death in 99 Parkinson patients. It reported severe hypoglycemia in 24, with the mean interval from the lowest reading to death reported as 23.5 days. Mean energy intake on the index day was 538.3 kcal versus 911 kcal (P < .001). This earlier report and the three-year analysis draw on records from the same hospital and calendar period with different eligibility and follow-up definitions. [2023 neurology abstract Pj-087-1, printed S316](https://web.archive.org/web/20250315065017id_/https://www.neurology-jp.org/Journal/public_pdf/63_supplement_04.pdf#page=80)

In a separate retrospective study, Park and colleagues found severe hypoglycemia, defined as glucose at or below 50 mg/dL, in 27 of 80 deceased Parkinson patients during the three years before death. Recorded mean energy intake on the index day was 637.6 kcal in the hypoglycemia group and 932.4 kcal in the other group (P = .001). Their February 2024 JSPEN abstract gives the same Parkinson cohort counts and energy means and adds 12 severe events among 98 patients with other diagnoses (12.2% versus 33.8%, P < .001). The team later expanded the analysis through March 2024, reporting 32 of 106 Parkinson patients and 15 of 122 patients with other diagnoses (P = .001). The reports cover overlapping samples from one hospital. Their analyses provide no ingredient-level comparisons or causal estimates. [2023 NHO abstract P1-106-3](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000204087.pdf#page=427) · [2024 JSPEN abstract P-28-2](https://web.archive.org/web/20250323185258id_/https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/6/Supplement1/6_761/_pdf/-char/ja#page=253) · [2024 NHO abstract P2-131-4](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000223895.pdf#page=640) · [Hospital study disclosure](https://utano.hosp.go.jp/section/clinical_info_disclosure.html)

## 2023 and 2024 oral feeding after aspiration procedures

Ishida and colleagues reviewed 31 Parkinson patients who received percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy. Fourteen could still eat orally before placement, and five were able to eat orally for at least one month afterward. The abstract gives 32 months (95% CI 15–113) as the median from gastrostomy to death or tracheostomy. Their earlier JSPEN and neurology abstracts at the same hospital describe the same analysis of 30 of 33 patient charts. Fifteen patients ate by mouth before gastrostomy, six afterward, and two for at least four months. The median and interval were also 32 months (95% CI 15–113). The NHO analysis used a different count and oral-intake threshold. The abstracts do not identify foods. [2023 NHO abstract P1-107-3](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000204087.pdf#page=429) · [2023 JSPEN abstract P-04-8](https://web.archive.org/web/20240917213122id_/https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/5/Supplement1/5_744/_pdf/-char/ja#page=37) · [2023 neurology abstract Pj-065-5, printed S296](https://web.archive.org/web/20250315065017id_/https://www.neurology-jp.org/Journal/public_pdf/63_supplement_04.pdf#page=60)

In a separate 2023 report, Shimizu describes two Parkinson patients eating three unrestricted meals after total laryngectomy for aspiration prevention. The title refers to three cases, while the body describes seven patients across diagnoses. In 2024, Motozaki and colleagues at Ishida’s hospital described aspiration-prevention surgery in 18 patients with progressive neurological disease, including two with Parkinson’s. One Parkinson patient maintained small amounts of oral intake with tube feeding over the longer term. These reports concern surgical management and do not specify named foods or ingredient-specific outcomes. [2023 primary abstract P2-213-1](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000204087.pdf#page=626) · [2024 primary abstract P2-131-8](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000223895.pdf#page=641)

## 2023 levodopa concentrations and gastrointestinal context

Oda and colleagues compared 12 antibody-positive and 28 antibody-negative Parkinson patients after a fasting levodopa/benserazide dose. Mean peak concentration was 8.5 versus 11.9 nmol/mL (P = .04), and mean time to peak was 68.8 versus 40.7 minutes (P = .03). The area under the concentration curve did not differ. The same lead author, group sizes and estimates appear in a 2021 abstract, which also reports no detectable effect on these measures for levodopa/carbidopa. The exposure was *H. pylori* serostatus. A separate 18-patient 2023 report on intestinal levodopa gel found a different time profile of the 3-O-methyldopa-to-levodopa ratio in 11 participants with afternoon motor worsening versus seven without it (P = .004). Neither report measured a food exposure. [2023 primary abstracts P1-107-5 and P1-107-4](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000204087.pdf#page=430) · [Earlier primary abstract Pj-21-6, printed p. S318](https://neurology-jp.org/Journal/public_pdf/61_supplement_04.pdf)

## 2024 flavored oral care during feeding decisions

Sugiyama described a woman in her 70s with Hoehn and Yahr stage V Parkinson’s, severe dysphagia and aspiration pneumonia. A flavored oral-care gel was among several measures to address thirst, hunger and discomfort while the team and family discussed nutrition and feeding goals. Distress complaints decreased and the patient expressed a wish to pursue oral feeding. The abstract does not identify a flavor, isolate the effect of the gel or report that oral feeding resumed. [Primary abstract P2-216-5](https://nho.hosp.go.jp/files/000223895.pdf#page=804)
