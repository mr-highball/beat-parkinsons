# Flaxseed oil evidence

## Oral capsule oil and coordination in rats with hyperammonemia

A 2023 primary report found longer rod retention under oral flaxseed oil than under the hyperammonemia control, with an inconclusive omnibus comparison for general open-field activity. The oil contained 43% alpha-linolenic acid by chemical assay. The investigators give no verified pressing method or resting-tremor outcome. [Primary methods and motor results](Source-Text/2023-PMID37960203-Flaxseed-Oil-Hyperammonemia-Motor-Tests.txt).

## Japanese flaxseed oil and cognition trial registration

UMIN000039901 first became public March 24, 2020. The planned randomized, double-blind study compared equal pouches of flaxseed and corn oils for 12 weeks in 60 healthy adults aged 65–80. The registry lists cognition at baseline, six and 12 weeks and reports enrollment of 60 and completion. Its publication field had not been updated when checked. The March 2023 original publication gives the same identifier and study design. [Original registry](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000045507) · [Selected local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000039901.txt) · [Published results](#flaxseed-table-oil-and-executive-function-in-older-adults)

## Plasma alpha-linolenic acid and individual Parkinson symptoms

In the 2021 Yoo study of 38 people with early Parkinson’s, supplementary Table S2 provides item-level correlations for plasma alpha-linolenic acid (ALA). Age-adjusted correlations were small for examiner-rated speech (ρ = .014, p = .936), handwriting (ρ = .094, p = .581), finger taps (ρ = −.179, p = .289), resting tremor (ρ = .059, p = .728) and body bradykinesia or hypokinesia (ρ = −.230, p = .171). Rigidity correlated inversely in the age-adjusted analysis (ρ = −.331, p = .046) and had a weaker result in the sex-adjusted analysis (ρ = −.278, p = .096). Rising from a chair correlated inversely in both models, as a separate item. The study compared circulating fatty acids and did not identify an effect of consuming flaxseed oil. Several correlated comparisons and the cross-sectional design limit interpretation. [Primary supplement, Table S2](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art:10.1038%2Fs41598-021-92029-x/MediaObjects/41598_2021_92029_MOESM1_ESM.pdf) · [Local licensed supplement](Source-Text/2021-Yoo-PUFA-Parkinson-Supplement.txt) · [Earlier cohort note](Food-Experiment-Oil-Followup.md#plasma-fatty-acids-and-motor-and-nonmotor-symptoms)

## Roasted flaxseed and sleep measurements in young adults

Kanchan and colleagues published a single-group before-and-after pilot November 14, 2022. Forty-six of 50 selected students completed the study of roasted brown whole flaxseed. Table 1 reports Epworth Sleepiness Scale means of 11.50 to 11.32 (p = .023), while the Results text describes no statistically clear change. The same table gives global Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index means of 9.61 to 8.74 (p = .635), and polysomnography sleep latency of 14.8 to 10.39 minutes (p < .001). The study had no comparison group, did not use flaxseed oil and did not enroll people with Parkinson’s. [Primary study, Methods and Table 1](https://www.bibliomed.org/mnsfulltext/140/140-1659695982.pdf)

## Dietary omega-3 imbalance and mouse olfactory behavior

Soubeyre and colleagues published June 11, 2022. The study used dietary blends that varied alpha-linolenic acid and long-chain omega-3 supply from other oils. The lower-ALA diet reduced electroolfactogram amplitudes. The low- and high-omega-3 groups showed no clear differences in the tested olfactory behaviors. This study used a different developmental dietary design from the earlier rat experiment and included no flaxseed oil. [Primary methods and results](https://doi.org/10.1080/1028415X.2022.2082642) · [Earlier rat experiment](../2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#flaxseed-oil-in-a-rat-odor-discrimination-diet)

## Whole flaxseed and flaxseed oil in an acute inflammatory challenge

Livingston and colleagues published August 11, 2023. Mice received diets containing ground whole flaxseed, flaxseed oil or basal diet before a saline or LPS injection. In the LPS groups, the whole-seed diet partially attenuated selected inflammatory changes, while the oil showed limited effects. Neither diet clearly changed sickness or nest-building behavior. The experiment shows why a whole-seed finding cannot establish an oil result, even with an ALA-rich seed and oil from the same intervention program. The study did not test sleep, a human motor measure or a Parkinson disease model. [Primary report and Figures 2–4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10459276/) · [Local licensed text](Source-Text/PMC10459276.txt)

## Flaxseed table oil and executive function in older adults

Ogawa and colleagues published March 21, 2023. Sixty healthy older adults received flaxseed table oil or corn oil for 12 weeks. Table 3 reports a phonemic fluency score change of +0.30 versus +0.03 (p = .049), with lower baseline fluency in the flaxseed arm (2.50 versus 2.80, p = .032). The Frontal Assessment Battery total (p = .142) and motor series subtest (p = .140) showed no clear differences. The motor series is a learned movement sequence. It does not directly assess clinical hand dexterity or bradykinesia. The trial reports multiple cognitive outcomes, and the supplier funded the study. The article names NIPPN and describes table-oil use but gives no product name or lot. The supplier identifies cold-pressed commercial products, which does not verify the study lot. [Original Tables 1 and 3](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/15/6/1499) · [Local licensed primary text](Source-Text/PMC10056498.txt) · [Supplier oil specification](https://www.nippn.co.jp/pro/functional_material/flaxseed/index.html) · [Trial registration](#japanese-flaxseed-oil-and-cognition-trial-registration)

## NEUROBIS linseed blend registry follow-up

The official CTIS record for 2023-507715-35-02 lists Italy as recruiting, recruitment from August 8, 2025 and estimated trial completion May 1, 2027. No results were posted when checked November 13, 2026. The 9:1 sesame and linseed oil comparison remains part of a trial of a cannabis extract in Parkinson’s, Alzheimer’s disease and amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. It lists Parkinson subgroup UPDRS evaluation and supplies no isolated oil contrast. A third-party dated reading shows the public registry was already visible November 27, 2024, which supports retaining the registration in this bucket. ## Examiner-rated rest tremor versus body bradykinesia

The same early-Parkinson cohort gives near-zero adjusted associations between plasma ALA and resting tremor and weak inverse estimates for body bradykinesia. Supplementary Table S2 presents age and sex adjustments separately; neither bradykinesia result is a clear statistical contrast on its own. The study found no clear association between dietary PUFA intake and plasma concentrations among participants with Parkinson disease. Two separately registered trials assigned flaxseed-derived omega-3 with vitamin E and found favorable total clinical scores. The smaller trial's examiner motor result was near its threshold and the individual symptom results were unreported. In aggregate these add weak motor context to the bradykinesia hypothesis without supplying the same direction for rest tremor or an isolated oil result.
