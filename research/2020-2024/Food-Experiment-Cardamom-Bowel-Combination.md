## Cardamom in a five plant bowel formulation

Moazzam and colleagues published April 22, 2022. The open-label trial enrolled 26 adults with irritable bowel syndrome, and 22 completed 45 days. Table 1 specifies Elettaria cardamomum fruit or seed ground into a formulation with amla, Terminalia, coriander seed and willow-derived material. Table 3 gives mean total IBS-SSS 229.50 at baseline and 123.76 at 45 days (reported p < .0001). The scale includes abdominal pain, distention, bowel habit and interference. Item-level pain means and a comparison arm are not reported. The article identifies CTRI/2020/03/023790. The combined oral product and pooled outcome cannot isolate ground cardamom for Parkinson pain. [Primary report, Methods and Tables 1 and 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9618389/)
