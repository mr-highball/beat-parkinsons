# Refined rice bran oil and Parkinson symptom evidence

## Registered rice oil control in a swallowing crossover

This crossover protocol compares inhaled cypress essential oil with a rice oil control in older people with swallowing impairment. The primary outcome is the repetitive saliva swallowing test and the key secondary is the modified water swallowing test. Oil grade, product lot and a Parkinson-specific subgroup are not provided in the registry. The subsequent peer-reviewed trial identifies the product. [Official UMIN record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000060177).
