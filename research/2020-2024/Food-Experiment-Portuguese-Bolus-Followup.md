# Portuguese-language Parkinson swallowing studies, 2020–2024

## Mashed potato in a Pará endoscopic swallowing assessment

Barbosa and colleagues assessed 24 people with Parkinson disease at a university hospital in Pará between July and October 2019. The March 19, 2020 report lists milk-free mashed potato as the pasty test food, with filtered water and a salted cracker as separate test foods. The standard amount was 15 g of mashed potato when there was no contraindication. The authors did not specify how the potatoes were cooked or how many participants received each food. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.25248/reas.e2929.2020) · [Primary methods, p. 4](https://acervomais.com.br/index.php/saude/article/download/2929/1581#page=4)

Table 4 reports 3 normal, 7 mild, 10 moderate and 4 severe results across the 24 patients. Moderate indicates penetration without aspiration and severe indicates aspiration. The table gives one classification per patient across the assessment. It does not identify the bolus associated with any event, report results by food or test baked potato. The published methods identify an Excel source worksheet and provide no public file. Ethics identifier CAAE 12245919.0.0000.0017 identifies the cohort for further follow-up. [Primary results, Table 4, p. 8](https://acervomais.com.br/index.php/saude/article/download/2929/1581#page=8)

An earlier eight-person report by the same team gives questionnaire and clinical observations without a named food. Cohort overlap remains unverified. [2019 Pará poster](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Portuguese-Bolus-Followup.md#earlier-pará-questionnaire-and-choking-observations)

## Later journal report of the Bauru biofeedback cohort

Arone and colleagues' November 8, 2021 report, DOI `10.1590/2317-6431-2021-2542`, describes a half slice of pão francês, pudding-texture thickened diet grape drink and liquid water, all with Bariogel barium contrast. The study presents oral intake, dysphagia severity and quality of life by therapy group and follow-up time. The 2014 thesis gives additional per-texture residue scores. The matching protocol, participant details and clinical scores identify one cohort. The journal article supplies no food-level choking or aspiration counts. It is licensed CC BY-NC. [Primary journal article, Methods and Tables 1–2](https://www.scielo.br/j/acr/a/9GJjydf4Q7t7CSXt5xwFDFD/?lang=pt) · [2014 thesis with residue tables](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Portuguese-Bolus-Followup.md#french-bread-and-a-pudding-texture-grape-preparation-during-swallowing-therapy)

## Choking by consistency before and after swallowing therapy in one patient

Sales and colleagues' December 21, 2023 case report assessed an 82-year-old man with Parkinson disease before and after two months of swallowing therapy. Tables 3–5 record clinical choking with liquid, pasty and solid consistencies before therapy. After therapy choking was absent during the liquid and pasty assessments and persisted during the solid assessment. The report does not identify the ingredients in the assessment boluses. These observations reflect one patient receiving an exercise program and supply no result for baked potatoes or for confirmed airway obstruction. [Primary article, Tables 3–5](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/8f46/cee5651769f8d237b19eae1f4b43c8aee2d0.pdf) · [Publisher article record](https://clium.org.uk/index.php/editions/article/view/2632)

## Dysphagia, disease stage and age in Pará

Amaral Moraes and colleagues' May 14, 2024 report describes 60 people with Parkinson disease at Hospital Ophir Loyola in Belém. The researcher asked the questions in person and entered responses electronically. The validated Brazilian Portuguese 15-item SDQ-DP classified 46 of 60 participants at risk of dysphagia using a total score of at least 11. Table 3 gives risk-positive counts of 0/2, 11/20, 17/18, 11/13 and 7/7 from Hoehn and Yahr stages 1.5 through 4 (overall p = .0027). Figure 2 reports an association with age (p = .0005). The SDQ-DP groups example foods within items, while this report only presents composite screening risk. It provides no item-level counts, food intake measure or food challenge. The authors cite an earlier food-frequency study from the same hospital. The earlier study collected data in 2019 under a different protocol, and the reports give no participant linkage. ## Later journal report of the mixed-diagnosis dysphagia cohort

Rabelo and Dantas's December 31, 2024 abstract describes the same 35-person sample and assessment methods as the 2016 USP thesis. Their results summarize agreement between clinical and nasoendoscopic scores across stroke, Alzheimer's disease and Parkinson disease. Both documented PDF routes returned HTTP 403 during this review. The source remains a likely companion publication pending comparison of the full reports.
