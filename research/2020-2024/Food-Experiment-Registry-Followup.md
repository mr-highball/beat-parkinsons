# Dietary trial follow-up, 2020–2024

## Brazilian Mediterranean diet counseling registration

ReBEC RBR-36q469s was first registered September 21, 2024. Its first public version describes Mediterranean diet education for people with Parkinson’s and constipation at Hospital Ophir Loyola in Brazil. The original registry describes double blinding. The published methods identify blinded outcome assessors and analysts and state that participants and counseling staff knew the allocation. The 2026 preprint and journal report describe the same 38-person study. [Original ReBEC registration](https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-36q469s/1) · [Current ReBEC results](https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-36q469s) · [2026 outcome note](../2025-2029/Food-and-Nutrition.md#2026--mediterranean-diet-counseling-for-constipation)

## German registration of a high-fiber dietary pilot

DRKS00034528 was retrospectively registered on June 27, 2024. Eleven pairs of people with Parkinson’s and their unaffected household partners were enrolled for a four-week high-fiber dietary intervention, including apples and lactulose. The registry specifies motor and gastrointestinal measures. The register still has no linked results report. The 2024 preprint and 2025 journal publication identify this same registry ID and cohort. [Primary registration](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00034528/details) · [2024 preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.09.09.24313184) · [Published result note](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md#apples-and-lactulose-in-a-high-fiber-diet)

## Australian prebiotic powder study

ACTRN12623001186640 was registered November 16, 2023. It describes a randomized, blinded first phase of OM002 prebiotic powder versus placebo in people with Parkinson’s, followed by an open-label period. Registered outcomes include constipation and cognition. The Michael J. Fox Foundation’s 2025 pipeline report identifies the active constituent as 2′-fucosyllactose. The Australian trial remains listed as not yet recruiting in the record last updated December 8, 2024. Searches by identifier, title and formulation yielded no verified result. DRKS00027061 is a separate study of 2′-fucosyllactose and short-chain fatty acid capsules. [Primary registration](https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=386783) · [Pipeline report](https://zenodo.org/records/15604012/files/2025%20Parkinson%27s%20Priority%20Therapeutic%20Clinical%20Pipeline%20Report.pdf?download=1) · [German trial note](Food-Experiment-European-Registry-Followup.md#short-chain-fatty-acid-and-prebiotic-trial)

## Medium-chain triglyceride crossover registration

ISRCTN13221188 was registered March 8, 2023. The protocol describes a randomized-order comparison of a standard diet, the same diet with medium-chain triglyceride (MCT) oil, and a ketogenic diet. The plain-language description also adds oil to the ketogenic phase. The registered measures include the Parkinson’s Disease Fatigue Scale, Epworth Sleepiness Scale, Parkinson’s Disease Sleep Scale, and Parkinson’s Disease Cognitive Rating Scale. The record lists completion on December 31, 2025. On November 12, 2026 the registry’s results and actual enrollment fields were empty. The investigators’ recruitment deck describes preliminary observations and allows either coconut oil or MCT oil. [Primary registration](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN13221188) · [Registry results fields](https://www.isrctn.com/api/trial/ISRCTN13221188/format/who) · [Preliminary observations](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md#preliminary-observations-from-the-mct-crossover)

## Iranian registration of the saffron powder trial

The March 5, 2024 IRCT revision describes one 100 mg first-class Sargol saffron tablet or lactose placebo daily for 12 weeks, with a target of 86 participants. It lists DASS-21, Parkinson sleep and cognition measures, the MDS-UPDRS and other outcomes. The registry’s first archived version with PFS-16 for fatigue dates to 2025, after receipt of the outcomes manuscript. The later record lists recruitment complete with actual dates unfilled. The published protocol names dried-stigma powder and the Mojtahedi supplier. [Original registration](https://irct.ir/trial/75378?revision=296350) · [Published protocol](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-093167) · [Registry history](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Saffron-Registry-History.md)

## Iranian registration of the Tehran saffron capsule trial

IRCT20090117001556N145 was prospectively registered August 15, 2022. The eight-week comparison added saffron or placebo capsules to stable usual Parkinson’s treatment in adults with mild to moderate depression. The registered outcomes include Hamilton depression scores and MDS-UPDRS Part III. The official record confirms the capsule regimen and provides no further preparation detail. The 2024 publication cites this registration. [Primary registration](https://irct.ir/trial/65247) · [Outcome note](Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#saffron-capsules-and-depressive-symptoms)

## Curcumin with and without resistance training

IRCT20191231045968N1 was first registered July 20, 2020. Four planned arms separated curcumin capsules, resistance training, their combination, and placebo, with ten participants targeted in each arm. The registry identifies a UPDRS score and metabolic outcomes. Recruitment is listed as complete. No results linked to this trial were located by registry ID or title. [Primary registration](https://irct.ir/trial/45058)

## Trehalose and Parkinson motor and nonmotor symptoms

IRCT20181022041407N10 was registered August 25, 2024. The comparison assigned trehalose or sucrose for 12 weeks. Registered endpoints include UPDRS, a nonmotor scale, DASS, MMSE, and PDQ-39. Recruitment is listed as complete and no results linked to this trial were located by ID or title. [Primary registration](https://irct.ir/trial/78559)

The separate NCT05355064 registration from Italian sponsor Neuromed IRCCS first posted May 2, 2022. It planned a single open-label group taking 4 g/day of oral trehalose (CITOPLAS) for 24 weeks and included idiopathic Parkinson’s and LRRK2-associated Parkinson’s. MDS-UPDRS, MMSE and MoCA were secondary endpoints. The registry now marks recruitment status unknown and supplies no results. A search by trial ID, product and sponsor yielded no matching outcome publication. [Primary registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05355064) · [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05355064.txt)

## Oral caffeine PET study in LRRK2 carriers

The NCT05009199 registration first posted August 17, 2021. The completed phase I PET study enrolled three people with a pathogenic LRRK2 variant and without manifest Parkinson’s. It planned measurement of striatal A2A receptor occupancy and plasma pharmacokinetics after repeated oral caffeine. The registry supplies no symptom endpoints, prepared coffee exposure or posted results. [Primary registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05009199) · [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05009199.txt)

## Brazilian counseling registry dates and masking

The first public registration records September 21, 2024 and lists February 5, 2024 as first enrollment. It describes a cross-sectional screening phase and a later randomized phase, so the dates do not establish when randomization started. The registry describes a double-blind crossover. The published methods describe a parallel, delayed-start comparison with blinded outcome assessors and analysts. Participants and counselors knew the allocation. The 2026 ReBEC results are from the same cohort as the preprint and journal report. [Latest registry results](https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-36q469s). [Original source](https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-36q469s/1) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/Trials/RBR-36q469s.txt)

## German pilot registration follow-through and outcome scope

The first and latest posting is June 27, 2024. The official record distinguishes 22 enrolled from a target sample value of 100. The motor outcome is UPDRS III alongside gastrointestinal measures, with no rest-tremor item specified. Both groups underwent the diet and differed in lactulose use. The official results field remains empty. [Original registry](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00034528/details) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/Trials/DRKS00034528.txt)
