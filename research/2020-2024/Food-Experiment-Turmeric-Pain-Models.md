# Turmeric Pain Models

## Isolated curcumin and tactile allodynia in rats

Pastrana-Quintos and colleagues studied tactile allodynia after spinal nerve ligation in rats. Oral and intrathecal curcumin prevented the development of tactile allodynia in a dose-dependent manner in this model. The study measures responses to von Frey stimulation after laboratory nerve injury. It uses isolated curcumin and supplies no whole-spice comparison or Parkinson symptom result. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7076866/) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/PMID32171311-Curcumin-Tactile-Allodynia.txt)

## Combined ginger and turmeric extracts and rat joint pain

Abdel-Rahman and colleagues compared Manjarix, prepared from ginger and turmeric powder extracts, with controls in a rat model of osteoarthritis. The authors report reduced pain-related findings and joint swelling. The combined formulation prevents attribution to either plant, and the animal model measures osteoarthritis-related rather than clinical Parkinson pain. The accessible original abstract does not establish a clean ground-spice contrast.
