# Oral chili and gastrointestinal pain

## Ground red chili and separate pain and burning outcomes

2020-12-04. In a double-blind randomized crossover, 15 healthy adults and 15 people with nonerosive reflux disease consumed commercial ground Capsicum frutescens or placebo in capsules. The reflux group’s postprandial abdominal burning scores were 2.2 ± 2.5 versus 0.3 ± 0.8 (p < .05). Abdominal pain was collected separately and was inconclusive, as were both pain and burning comparisons in the healthy volunteers.
