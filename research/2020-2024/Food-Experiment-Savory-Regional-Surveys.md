# Regional summer savory preparations

## Dagestan survey distinguishes fresh leaf juice and dried herb

Aliev and colleagues published November 18, 2023. The field survey lists summer savory as Satureja hortensis. Page 55 records fresh leaf juice applied to bee stings, dried herb in meat sausages and an infusion of unspecified freshness for other complaints. The record gives traditional preparation and use, with no observed Parkinson voice-volume or speech outcome. ## Pülümür survey records dried leaf for digestive indications

Doğan published July 29, 2024. Table 1 identifies Satureja hortensis by voucher MARE17749 and records internally used dried leaves for appetite and carminative indications in the Pülümür region. The authors report traditional use. Fresh use, measured speech and Parkinson outcomes were unreported. The field sample differs from the Serbian Pirot respondents.
