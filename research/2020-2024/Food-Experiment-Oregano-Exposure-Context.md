# Oregano exposures and primary study context

## Oregano in a shared Parkinson diet and reported levodopa doses

Hegelmaier and colleagues list oregano among spices in the two-week diet, which was shared by the dietary-only and diet-plus-enema Parkinson groups. Table 4 prints differences in levodopa-equivalent daily dose at one year of +83.3 mg in the dietary-only group and -56.1 mg in the diet-plus-enema group. The narrative instead describes +100 and -36 mg/day. The two presentations are kept separate. Daily medication dose is distinct from daily OFF duration. The study provides no oregano quantity, fresh-leaf comparison or participant-level oregano attribution. [Primary record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7072275/) · [Local source text](Source-Text/PMC7072275.txt)

## Oregano softgel and a registered drug interaction comparison

The registry first posted November 19, 2024. The planned comparison uses a 180 mg oregano oil softgel and a cocktail of caffeine, dextromethorphan, losartan, midazolam and omeprazole, with a target enrollment of 16. Its primary outcome is a midazolam exposure ratio. Levodopa and the frozen Parkinson outcomes are outside the registered comparison. The record was recruiting with no results posted in the checked API snapshot. [Primary record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06693960) · [Local source text](Source-Text/NCT06693960-Oregano-Drug-Cocktail-Registry.txt)

## Registered oregano mouthwash and odor outcomes

The March 3, 2021 first-posted registry compares an oregano essential-oil mouthwash, chlorhexidine mouthwash and water in a halitosis study. Its registered main measures are tongue and floss odor scores. The completed record had no posted results in the checked snapshot. The exposure and outcome differ from eating fresh oregano and measuring clinical drooling. [Primary record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04779502) · [Local source text](Source-Text/NCT04779502-Oregano-Mouthwash-Registry.txt)
