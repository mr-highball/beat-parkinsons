# Oral safflower oil comparator trials in 2020–2024

## Baylor randomized pilot of singing self-perception

Perrine et al. first published this separate pilot on 27 October 2021. During September to December 2020, twenty college singers were randomized to fish oil or 3 g/day NOW-brand safflower oil orally for ten weeks during regular instruction. Seven oil and nine fish-oil participants completed the protocol. In Table 2 the safflower group's Singing Voice Handicap Index-10 fell from 7.00 to 6.29, versus 8.75 to 6.89 with fish oil. The time-by-group interaction in Table 3 had nominal p=0.046 and the authors correctly describe it as nonsignificant at their Bonferroni threshold of 0.017. The across-group time effect was p=0.005. The safflower group's Reflux Symptom Index fell from 5.14 to 4.14 and the fish-oil group's rose from 5.56 to 6.00, with interaction p=0.509. The reflux instrument includes a swallowing question, but the report gives no arm-level item result. The pilot did not collect direct voice measurements. Regular training and an active-oil comparison limit attribution. Product identifiers and processing remain unknown.

## Baylor college singers registration

NCT05141045 first posted on 2 December 2021, after its recorded February–May 2021 study period. The registry lists 43 participants, random allocation, a double-blind design, and Evaluation of Ability to Sing Easily (EASE) as the primary outcome. The later explicit study report identifies the same NCT number, describes alternate assignment by participant number, and uses Singing Voice Handicap Index (SVHI) as the primary outcome. Interpret the results using the executed study's allocation and retain the primary outcome discrepancy. The [2020 randomized pilot](#baylor-randomized-pilot-of-singing-self-perception) predated this registration's study period and the later paper refers to that pilot as prior work.

## Baylor alternating-allocation safflower comparator and voice range

Perrine et al. first published the NCT05141045 report online on 30 October 2023. The report describes alternation by odd and even participant number in a double-blind comparison. Eighteen safflower and seventeen fish-oil participants completed the protocol. The oil was NOW-brand, 3 g/day orally with the evening meal for 10 weeks during ordinary college singing instruction. The brand alone cannot verify the trial product, seed processing or oil grade.

Table 4 shows maximum singing sound pressure level change of −1.23 dB (95% CI −6.81 to 4.25) on safflower, versus +0.53 dB (−0.16 to 1.67) on fish oil. The reported comparative change was +1.81 dB (−2.24 to 5.85), p=0.5571. The lowest-pitch sound pressure range increased by 1.50 dB on safflower (−1.00 to 4.00) and fell 2.47 dB with fish oil (−5.15 to 0.21). The reported between-arm p=0.0469 is nominal across several exploratory voice measures. This within-range acoustic measure, the maximum volume, and speech intelligibility answer different questions.

Table 2 shows safflower-group SVHI change of −3.72 (95% CI −10.3 to 2.8) versus −15.59 (−20.8 to −10.3) on fish oil, with reported between-arm p=0.0118. The registered EASE outcome had p=0.9713 for the comparison. Total reflux symptom change on safflower was +0.33 (−2.4 to 3.1). The between-arm RSI estimate was −3.27 (−5.6 to −1.0) and the source reports p=0.0792. Preserve the reported interval and p-value without converting them into a swallowing verdict. The reflux instrument includes a swallowing item and the paper reports a total. Neither the acoustic nor the reflux result identifies Parkinson’s speech intelligibility or clinical dysphagia.

## TRIHEP3 initial total motor results

The September 2021 European Huntington's Disease Network abstract and presentation report early results from the [2015 TRIHEP3 registration](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Comparator-Trials.md#trihep3-huntington-disease-registration). The original presentation reports unadjusted change in Huntington total motor score through the six-month blinded phase of +0.4 (SD 4.4, n=50) with safflower versus +1.6 (SD 4.7, n=45) with triheptanoin, p=0.215. Higher scores indicate more impairment. The later trial Methods identify the placebo as safflower. Scores from month six onward reflect open-label triheptanoin in both randomized groups. The investigators' parallel REV-HD placebo comparison used a separate study and is not an arm of the six-month safflower comparison.

[Mochel et al., conference abstract](https://doi.org/10.1136/jnnp-2021-EHDN.128) · [Investigators' presentation](https://ehdn.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/J01.pdf)

## TRIHEP3 European posted results

The first European results version posted on 21 April 2022. The structured page lists the six-month caudate endpoint but no interpretable numeric effect estimate. It shows diarrhea in two of fifty per displayed group, a 5% nonserious-event reporting threshold, no listed serious events, and a 48-hour timeframe. The arm description mixes the randomized safflower comparator and the external REV-HD comparator NCT02336633. The [2024 paper](#trihep3-total-motor-and-psychiatric-outcomes) lists four unrelated serious events across its study period and 86 one-year completers, whereas the EU disposition displays 100 completed. Keep these source windows and labels visible. No separate item results for the named symptoms appear in the EU structured table.

## TRIHEP3 total motor and psychiatric outcomes

Mochel et al. first published their full Huntington study on 30 December 2024. In Table 3, the six-month adjusted UHDRS total motor changes were +0.7 (95% CI −0.4 to 2.0) on safflower and +1.3 (0.0 to 2.7) on triheptanoin. The active-minus-safflower difference was +0.6 (−1.0 to 2.5). The authors considered these secondary outcomes exploratory. The UHDRS motor examination includes gait, tandem walking, the pull test, dysarthria, body bradykinesia and arm rigidity. PBA-s includes an apathy item. The investigators reported no clear group difference on the PBA-s composite and provided no item-level apathy data. The available paper does not give comparative gait, reactive balance, speech, rigidity, bradykinesia, swallowing, or fall-specific outcomes. It lists anonymized data as available by request from qualified investigators. The journal's separately linked supplementary files could not be retrieved in this pass. The comparator and disease leave the Parkinson hypotheses and safflower cold-press grade unresolved.
