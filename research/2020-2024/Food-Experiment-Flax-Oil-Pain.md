# Flaxseed oil and pain, 2020–2024

## Flaxseed oil in a single-blind rheumatoid arthritis comparison

Vyas and colleagues reported 70 participants randomized to flaxseed oil or placebo with nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory medicines. Table 3 gives 0–3 pain-at-rest means of 1.97 to 0.93 versus 1.94 to 1.94 and movement pain means of 1.94 to 0.90 versus 2.00 to 2.10, with reported intergroup p<.001. Some printed differences diverge from the displayed values, and reporting of oil processing, dose, allocation and comparator composition is incomplete. The same authors reported a similarly sized osteoarthritis trial. Treat these two reports as potentially related when weighing replication. [Primary trial, December 2020, Table 3](https://ijhcr.com/index.php/ijhcr/article/download/397/436/1004) · [Source summary](Source-Text/2020-Vyas-Flax-Oil-RA.txt)

## Flaxseed versus corn oil after anterior cruciate ligament surgery

Tang and colleagues randomized 142 participants to flaxseed oil or corn oil capsules for two years. At follow-up, the Knee Injury and Osteoarthritis Outcome Score pain component averaged 87.6 with flax and 87.3 with corn (p=.96), with higher scores indicating a better state. Other composite and sport measures favored flax; the pain-specific comparison remained inconclusive. Processing grade and a Parkinson population were absent. [Primary paper, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9641443/) · [Source summary](Source-Text/2022-Tang-Flax-Oil-ACL.txt)

## Flaxseed oil versus vitamin E for cyclic mastalgia

Godazandeh and colleagues enrolled people with cyclic breast pain and analyzed 51 assigned to flaxseed oil and 49 to vitamin E. Pain declined in both arms, with an inconclusive group comparison over follow-up (p=.142). An inactive control was unavailable, and oil pressing grade was unspecified. [Primary report, 2021](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40780-020-00186-4) · [Source summary](Source-Text/2021-Godazandeh-Flax-Oil-Mastalgia.txt)

## Flaxseed formulation conflict in endometriosis pain

Khalajinia and colleagues reported lower pain under the flaxseed intervention than placebo. The November 2024 paper uses the term flaxseed oil in parts of the report, while its preparation method describes an ethanol-extracted and dried flaxseed preparation. The paper also gives unreconciled capsule and flaxseed quantities. The matching Iranian registry gives oil in the title and encapsulated flaxseed in the arm description. Neither gives a verifiable cold-pressed oil specification.
