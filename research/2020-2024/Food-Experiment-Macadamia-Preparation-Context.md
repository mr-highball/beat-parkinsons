# Macadamia oil in the named retail and meal formulations

## Summit supplier specification and extraction method

Summit Oil Mill, macadamia nut oil product specification. On-page update field November 2021, accessed November 15, 2026.

The supplier specifies pressing as the extraction method, food use, and a typical palmitoleic acid share of 23%. Its cosmetic names list Macadamia ternifolia and Macadamia integrifolia. No oil lot, pressing temperature, or trial-specific certificate appears on the page. The 23% is compatible with the range in patent JP2026034397A Test Example 2, but the patent supplies no product code for a direct lot link. The supplier's displayed update is the earliest dated version verified in this review. ## Whole macadamia nuts and bowel outcomes

First published May 29, 2020. Thirty-five adults crossed over between eight weeks of usual diet and a mix of raw and roasted whole macadamia nuts supplying 15% of calories. Bowel frequency showed no clear difference. Bristol stool type had p=.049 but the direction and phase values were unreported. The nut matrix retained fiber and did not test oil. The 2023 Jones body and supplements did not supply the missing bowel values. [Original bowel conference abstract](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7258298/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2020-Heskey-Macadamia-Bowel-Abstract-Extract.txt)
First published May 8, 2023. The paper identifies NCT03801837 and reports 35 completers in the matching whole-nut crossover. Its report and supplements give cardiometabolic, adiposity and lipid subgroup outcomes without Bristol values or bowel-frequency results. This is a companion from the same family, without an independent bowel estimate.
