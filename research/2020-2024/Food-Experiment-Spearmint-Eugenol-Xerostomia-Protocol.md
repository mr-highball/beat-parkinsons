## Spearmint oil and eugenol in a salivary flow protocol

NCT06313905 first posted March 15, 2024. The protocol compares the EVADRY oral spray containing spearmint herb oil, eugenol and other ingredients with mint-flavored water in Sjögren syndrome. Its primary outcome is collected unstimulated whole-saliva flow and its secondary outcomes include subjective xerostomia and oral-health scores. The checked official record lists unknown recruitment status and no posted results. Its regimen and endpoints do not isolate eugenol, ground clove or brewed spearmint. [Official study record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06313905)
