# Registered capsaicin and gait learning studies

## Registered age comparison after experimental leg pain

First posted 2022-07-25. The official record accepts healthy volunteers and compares motor learning and retention after experimental pain in younger and older adults. It also lists digit-span measures. The 2026 paper explicitly names this registration and supplies the local capsaicin and heat procedure. No results are posted in the registry at review. The registration and the paper are one study family.

[Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05471557) · [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05471557.txt)

## Registered pain and context changes during gait learning

First posted 2022-11-23. The registered comparison measures motor learning and later retention in healthy young adults after experimental pain assigned in different sessions. No results are posted. This protocol is close to the Jackson 2025 paper, but an exact cross-identifier remains unverified.

[Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05626582) · [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05626582.txt)
