# Spinach in attention and cognition comparisons

## Red spinach extract and Stroop performance

Haynes and colleagues reported a crossover of red spinach extract and maltodextrin in ten healthy resistance-trained men. Seven days of exposure produced no significant Stroop performance difference in the exercise protocol. The concentrated red-spinach product and healthy exercise setting differ from cooked Spinacia oleracea in Parkinson’s. The primary paper and NCT04292106 registration share the Stroop method and exercise outcomes. ## Registry for optional spinach and perinatal attention

NCT05890014 first posted June 5, 2023. The intervention offers one or two choices per day from a list including spinach, fruit and dark chocolate, versus continuing usual intake. The secondary measures include sustained attention and executive functioning. The registry assigns a food pattern and does not specify a cooked spinach arm or ingredient-specific exposure contrast. The linked thesis independently verifies several attention outcomes.
