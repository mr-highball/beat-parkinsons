# Kagoshima Parkinson cohort in 2020–2024

## Sarcopenia and forward trunk posture in the Kagoshima project

On 28 September 2024, Toshiro Uchi and colleagues presented a posture and sarcopenia analysis of community-dwelling older people with Parkinson's. They recruited 97 people from October 2022 through March 2024 and analyzed 58 after exclusions. Thirteen met sarcopenia criteria and 45 did not. Reported gait speed was 0.7 ± 0.3 versus 0.9 ± 0.3 m/s (p=.024). The between-group trunk-distance comparison was 13.3 ± 9.6 versus 9.2 ± 5.4 cm (p=.11). An age- and sex-adjusted logistic model associated greater C7-to-wall distance with sarcopenia (OR 1.117, 95% CI 1.002–1.245, p=.045).

The shared Kagoshima ethics approval 220137 and October 2022 recruitment start link the project to the [2025 poster and 2026 tea report](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Kagoshima-Tea-Cohort.md#kagoshima-green-tea-conference-analysis). Participant overlap remains unverified. The 2024 report gives no tea exposure or fatigue or olfactory outcome.
