# Paprika preparations and reported pain, 2020–2024

## Joint pain reports in a paprika carotenoid trial

The randomized bone-turnover study assigned 50 participants per arm. Fifty participants in the extract group and 49 in the placebo group received intervention. The report lists one versus four adverse joint-pain events and eight versus two headaches. The examining physician judged the events unrelated. The report does not separately define a denominator for these narrative safety counts or present pain severity as an efficacy outcome. The carotenoid extract trial and the earlier knee pain oleoresin report have different designs and enrollment.
