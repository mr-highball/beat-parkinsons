# Banana, eggs, fatigue and related symptom evidence

## Ambon bananas and hospital anxiety scores

Published July 30, 2020. Sixty female schizophrenia inpatients with anxiety were assigned to five groups of 12. Mean HARS from baseline to day 14 was 36.33 to 27.08 for controls, 33.17 to 20.17 and 33.92 to 19.25 for new inpatients receiving two or three bananas daily, and 33.08 to 18.83 and 34.33 to 18.67 for previously treated inpatients receiving two or three. The post-treatment comparisons favor banana arms, with Table 3 reporting p < .001. Randomization is unclear. Antipsychotic history defines groups, and baseline HARS means differ. Preparation details and transfer to Parkinson disease are uncertain. [Original journal article and Tables 2 and 3](https://medicopublication.com/index.php/ijphrd/article/download/10253/9595/19335) · [Local text](Source-Text/2020-Setyarini-Ambon-Banana-Anxiety.txt)

## Cavendish bananas and tension anxiety

Published December 2022. An open randomized trial assigned 13 healthy adults to two Cavendish bananas daily and seven to no bananas for two weeks. The between-group change rate for the POMS2 tension–anxiety subscale did not show a clear difference. Outcome-driven responder subgroups do not provide a randomized effect estimate. Dole supplied the fruit. Healthy-volunteer selection and the small sample limit the comparison. [Original Journal of Food and Nutrition Research article](https://pubs.sciepub.com/jfnr/10/12/2/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2022-Kobayashi-Banana-Tension-Anxiety.txt)

## Egg intake and fracture-producing falls

Published March 13, 2023. In 300 fracture-producing fall cases and 590 controls, mean weekly egg portions were 2.03 ± 1.52 and 2.21 ± 1.41 (unadjusted p = .07). The reported multivariable model did not provide an egg estimate. Egg preparation was unspecified. This comparison concerns falls with fractures in older adults. Diet recall and confounding limit the direction. [Original Journal of Health Population and Nutrition article Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10009923/) · [Local text](Source-Text/PMC10009923.txt)

## Parkinson baseline eggs fish oils and spices

Published December 27, 2024. Baseline heatmap tiles show near-zero inverse egg frequency and falls, small inverse non-fried fish and subjective slow movement, small positive pooled cooking oils and fatigue, and small inverse pooled spices and freezing. The oil item groups sunflower, safflower, canola and vegetable oils. Individual food preparation, species or ingredient proportions are unresolved. There are no printed pair-specific coefficients or uncertainty intervals for these comparisons. Colors measured on two copies of the same heatmap give approximate signs only. [Original MVP 10-year summary pages 64–65](https://img1.wsimg.com/blobby/go/14919326-8cbc-4a3e-88d4-0f609019e178/MVP%20Study%2010-Year%20Summary-c634e33.pdf#page=64) · [Local text](Source-Text/2024-MVP-Eggs-Fish-Oils-Spices.txt)

## Sunflower meal and registered fatigue timing

First posted January 30, 2020. Double-blind randomized crossover compared a high-saturated-fat meal with a high-oleic sunflower oil meal. The current record specifies MFSI-SF sampling on admission before meals and at 18 and 30 months. No results are posted. Madison 2020 published postmeal attention; companion reports cover metabolism and inflammatory markers. The registered timing does not establish an acute postmeal fatigue comparison. Refining grade is unspecified. [Official ClinicalTrials.gov registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04247763) · [Local text](Source-Text/2020-NCT04247763-Sunflower-Fatigue.txt)

## Marjoram oil inhalation and nurses anxiety

Published November 17, 2023. Sixty intensive-care nurses were randomized and 57 completed. A two-hour inhalation of 3% Origanum majorana oil versus almond oil gave no clear between-group state anxiety or anxiety visual analog scale difference. The separate stress VAS changed by −0.67 versus −0.16 (p = .026). The exposure differs in species, route and concentration from dried common oregano. [Original Frontiers in Psychiatry trial](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2023.1287282/full) · [Local text](Source-Text/2023-Lee-Marjoram-Anxiety.txt)

## Isolated oral piperine and experimental pain

Dhargawe and colleagues published independent experimental comparisons in 2020. In a six-rat-per-arm tail-flick experiment, orally administered isolated piperine at 50 mg/kg yielded 6.71 ± 0.26 seconds at 60 minutes compared with 2.40 ± 0.17 seconds with gum acacia (reported p = .004). Other tests differ in species and method. This is a reflex after a concentrated isolated constituent, with no culinary pepper, Parkinson population or pain severity scale. [Primary methods and results](https://www.phcogres.com/sites/default/files/PharmacognRes-12-2-176.pdf) · [Local text](Source-Text/2020-Dhargawe-Piperine-Pain.txt)
