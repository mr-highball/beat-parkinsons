# UEG 2021 original poster section and food evidence

The institutional copy of the UEG 2021 abstract issue includes the publication's entire poster section. The review screened the complete span for the thirteen ingredients in 25 frozen Parkinson questions. Ten pairs provide related context and 15 have unrelated terminology or absent exposures. [Dated source record](Source-Text/2021-PMC8487351-UEG-Poster-Original-Scope.txt) · [Verified packet](../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/ueg-2021-original-poster-audit-nov25/README.md)

Poster P0156 tracked habitual oats in people with celiac disease undergoing a mixed-grain gluten challenge. The later full paper reports the same groups and outcome. The association concerns nausea and vomiting, with possible tolerance and selection differences. It supplies no prepared oats intervention for Parkinson falls, insomnia or rigidity. [2023 full report](Source-Text/2023-PMID37674120-Celiac-Gluten-Challenge-and-Oats.txt)

Poster P0193 compares plain and post-fermented green tea extracts with placebo using body composition and microbial outcomes. The matching workshop abstract uses the same 120 randomized participants and analysis. Poster P0652 uses observed recorded onion intake in steatosis and repeats the values in an earlier nutrition meeting report. These studies have specific preparations or composite exposures and do not supply a separate Parkinson fatigue or rigidity comparison. [2021 poster record](Source-Text/2021-PMC8487351-UEG-Poster-Original-Scope.txt) · [Parallel onion abstract](Source-Text/2021-PMC8180753-Dietary-Patterns-Onion-and-Steatosis.txt)

The UEG 2025, UEG 2022, FEBS and AES originals have independent page and member boundaries. Copies or previews outside those boundaries leave the corresponding pairs open. The exact iron-deficiency case was located in the authors' university index without an attached article. The P2 review's public PDF locator opens a subscription preview with an abstract and figures.
