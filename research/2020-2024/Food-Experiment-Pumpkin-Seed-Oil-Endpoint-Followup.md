# Pumpkin seed oil endpoint follow-up, 2020–2024

## Parkinson dietary trial supplementary motor outcome resolution

The recovered supplementary Figure S2 displays total UPDRS III at the start and end of a 14-day intervention, with groups identified as diet plus enema or diet alone. The appendix names pumpkin seed oil among four oils. There is no separately displayed hand-task score, handwriting, dyskinesia, saliva or ingredient-specific intake. ## Oral pumpkin seed oil and tongue histology with orlistat

Oral commercial oil was studied alongside orlistat in rats. The report measures tongue tissue and cellular proliferation. Salivation appears as background physiology, without an exposure-specific drooling or salivary flow result. [Primary paper Methods and microscopy](https://ejh.journals.ekb.eg/article_77437_320d584bc6f090faa30252c8f620beda.pdf) · [Local source text](Source-Text/2020-Kassab-Orlistat-Tongue-Pumpkin-Oil.txt)

## Pumpkin fruit extract in a salivary gland stress model

The Methods explicitly remove seeds and peel, freeze-dry the remaining pumpkin fruit and extract it with ethanol for oral gavage. The study measures rat salivary-gland histology, tissue markers and stress-related behavior, with no saliva flow or clinical drooling estimate. Its seed-free preparation and endpoints distinguish it from pumpkin seed oil.
