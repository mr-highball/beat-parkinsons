# Registered mixed sleep formulation

## Sleep registry and the flavored comparison beverage

The first public registration lists actigraphic sleep efficiency and PSQI. The record names an inert placebo but does not specify flavorings. The associated article identifies lemongrass, peppermint and mint flavoring and describes 64 randomized with 58 completing follow-up. No separate REM behavior measure is registered. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05609890) · [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05609890.txt)
