## Green cardamom extract in a tail flick experiment

Habibiyan, Dehghani and Ahmadi’s 2020 conference proceedings, printed pp. 111–114, report longer thermal tail withdrawal latency after higher doses of intraperitoneal green cardamom fruit extract. The lowest tested dose showed no significant difference. Their conclusion contains pain threshold wording opposite to the observed latency direction. The abstract identifies male rats and the paper does not supply a ground culinary exposure or a clinical symptom.
