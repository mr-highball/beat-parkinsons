# Ginger smell and stiffness outcomes, 2020–2024

## Ginger in a four-aroma postviral olfactory training trial

Qiao and colleagues randomized 172 people with post-COVID smell loss to 24 weeks of training with four aromatics including ginger, or conventional odor training. The original reports no clear overall TDI contrast and six-month response rates of 43.90% and 43.21%. The publisher deposited online publication in October 2024 ahead of the February 2025 issue. The exposure was inhaled as part of a mixture regimen, with no standalone ginger or oral comparison. ## Ginger extract and shoulder hardness in adults

Higashikawa and colleagues assigned 100 generally healthy adults reporting eye fatigue or shoulder stiffness to ginger extract or placebo. They found no clear group difference in mechanically measured shoulder hardness. The subjective shoulder-stiffness finding favored ginger in women below 51 in a subgroup of the trial. The shoulder measure and extract are distinct from passive-resistance rigidity in Parkinson disease after grated ginger.
