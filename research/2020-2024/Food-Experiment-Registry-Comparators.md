# Food experiment study registrations

First public posting dates and later publication links are shown for the registered cohorts.

## Mediterranean diet and mushroom trial registration

First posted 2020-02-06. Completed without posted tabular results. The 2024 outcome paper links this record to button and oyster mushrooms in a feeding study. The registration and publication describe one cohort. [Official study registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04259229) · [Local registry summary](Source-Text/Trials/NCT04259229.txt)

## Infant blueberry powder trial registration

First posted 2021-08-16. Completed without posted results. Two 2025 papers link this record to infant freeze-dried blueberry powder versus placebo. The allergic-symptom paper collected caregiver constipation responses and gives no published arm comparison. [Official study registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05006989) · [Local registry summary](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05006989.txt)

## Canola and coconut mixed meal trial registration

First posted 2022-01-26. Completed without posted tabular results. The June 2025 outcome report links this record to the randomized four-meal crossover and supplies postprandial vascular and metabolic outcomes without an orthostatic symptom estimate. [Official study registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05208346) · [Local registry summary](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05208346.txt)

## Vitamin D-enriched whole mushroom trial registration

First posted 2022-09-29. Completed without posted tabular results. The September 2025 cognitive publication identifies this registration and reports the ultraviolet-treated fresh cremini group and breadcrumb control. The short title describes vitamin D and immune outcomes. [Official study registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05559112) · [Local registry summary](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05559112.txt)

## Nested blueberry powder and stool microbiome registration

First posted 2023-04-18. Completed with results posted. The August 2026 report identifies the substudy as nested within NCT05735587 and gives microbiome and short-chain-fatty-acid outcomes. The nested cohort is one part of the larger trial and adds no independent constipation comparison. [Official study registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05817383) · [Local registry summary](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05817383.txt)
