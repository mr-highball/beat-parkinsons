# Celery leaf oil composition

## Butylphthalide in shade-dried celery leaf essential oil

Foudah and colleagues collected *Apium graveolens* leaves in Al-Kharj, Saudi Arabia, shade-dried and ground them, and obtained a hydrodistilled essential oil. The paper reports a 0.03% volume-to-weight oil yield and 3-butylphthalide at 5.36% of the oil composition in Table 1. This is a result for the distilled fraction of a dried-leaf sample. The study did not administer fresh leaf as food or measure levodopa response or dyskinesia.
