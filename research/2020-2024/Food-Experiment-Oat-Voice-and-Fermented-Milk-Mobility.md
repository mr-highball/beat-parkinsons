# Oat allergy and fermented milk mobility records

## Independent 2020 oat milk dysphonia case

The 2020 report's second case was a 62-year-old man with ischemic heart disease. Oropharyngeal itching, dysphonia and dyspnea appeared within five minutes of oat milk, followed by urticaria. The first patient, age 14, had oral itching and erythema without reported dysphonia. The report cites the separate 2013/2016 case in a 70-year-old man. These observations concern acute allergy to oat milk. They give no acoustic loudness measure, Parkinson cohort or rolled-oat comparison. [Original source](https://www.jiaci.org/revistas/vol30issue3_5-5.pdf) · [Companion record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31932273/)

## Strain-assigned fermented milk in sarcopenia

First public August 2, 2021. The completed protocol used two daily bottles of Yakult 300 LIGHT containing Lactobacillus casei Shirota for 12 weeks. The posted results include walking speed, chair-stand and Timed Up and Go, SPPB, handgrip, microbiota and biomarkers. No named fall-count outcome is present. The product is a strain-assigned fermented milk, and the record does not describe kefir grains. Inconsistent arm-label references in the protocol remain unresolved. No numerical efficacy estimate is used here. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04985877) · [Companion record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04985877)
