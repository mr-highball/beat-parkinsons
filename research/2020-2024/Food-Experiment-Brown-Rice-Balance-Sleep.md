# Brown rice, postural instability and dream enactment

## Lower-dose fermented rice-germ GABA and REM share

Yoon, Byun and Shin first published June 28, 2022. They compared 75 mg/day of a natural GABA product derived from unpolished rice germ with placebo for four weeks in adults with insomnia symptoms. Table 1 reports a change from 16.9% to 16.6% REM sleep in the active arm versus 18.9% to 20.5% on placebo (interaction p = .9173). The periodic limb movement index interaction was also inconclusive (p = .9442). Periodic limb movements and proportion of sleep spent in REM do not measure dream enactment or quantify REM without atonia. The food matrix and population also differ from claim 0379. ## Cooked ground brown rice and climbing in an iron fly model

Uthman and colleagues first published September 5, 2024, before the 2025 issue. During a 15-day experiment, a ground Danboto brown-rice diet was compared with a ground white-rice diet and other controls, with and without iron exposure. The brown-rice feed contained 1% weight/volume ground grain and replaced half the base sweetener, as did white rice. Table 1 specifies cooking the diets at 100 °C. Figure 2A reports better negative geotaxis with brown rice plus iron than iron alone. Separate iron plus levodopa groups also improved, and despite wording in the discussion, the design lists no arm combining rice plus levodopa. The assay measures whether flies climb 8 cm within six seconds, with no analogous human postural examination. Methods give 20 flies per group and three replicates for climbing (n = 60), while the figure caption refers to three replicates of 25 fly heads (n = 75). This discrepancy leaves the behavioral denominator uncertain. ## Brown rice after prior iron exposure in a related fly program

Yankuzo and colleagues first published November 26, 2024. Three investigators overlapped with the earlier fly paper. The abstract describes a different treatment schedule. Flies first received ten days of iron or control feed and then five days with brown rice, white rice, levodopa or standard feed according to assignment. The authors report better motor and cognitive function with brown rice in iron-exposed flies than iron or white rice comparisons. The indexed report does not establish the exact preparation, test or group size. The ten-plus-five-day protocol differs from the earlier paper's concurrent exposure design. A direct postural endpoint remains unresolved for claim 0471. [Original abstract and publication history](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39588846/)

## Registered brown-rice protein hydrolysate sleep study

ClinicalTrials.gov first posted NCT06267586 on February 20, 2024. The paper cites its February 2 submission date. The completed study reports 60 participants enrolled and lists placebo with three doses of a plant-based peptide product. The registry defines adverse event safety as primary, and wearable estimates of minutes spent in sleep phases as secondary. It does not list dream enactment, REM electromyography or a Parkinson subgroup. The 2026 sleep paper describes a 250 mg subgroup and identifies the ingredient as a brown-rice protein hydrolysate.
