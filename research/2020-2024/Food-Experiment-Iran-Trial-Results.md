# Iranian dietary trial results, 2020–2024

## Mediterranean diet and cognitive or global symptom scales

Paknahad and colleagues reported two ten-week randomized Mediterranean diet comparisons, each with 80 enrolled and 70 completing the trial. The cognition report described favorable changes in the total MoCA and several domains. The report on disease severity and serum antioxidant capacity described a favorable total UPDRS comparison. The studies tested a whole dietary pattern. Common enrollment and design across reports warrant checking cohort overlap before pooling. Both were first published online in 2020. [Cognition report, PMID 32444045](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32444045/) · [Severity report, PMID 32319358](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32319358/) · [2018 and 2019 registrations](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Iran-Registry-Followup.md#mediterranean-diet-trials-and-protein-timing)

## Nicotine-rich dietary pattern and protein timing

IRCT20210618051612N1 was prospectively registered July 13, 2021. The original English and Persian pages show the same posting and last-update date, with an update count of zero. The investigators planned block randomization in Sari among 45 people over age 50 receiving stable dopaminergic treatment. Both active diets targeted 20 μg of nicotine per day from food. The redistribution arm planned 10 g of protein through the afternoon snack and 1 g/kg of body weight after the evening meal. The control diet matched calories. The intervention fields in both languages omit the foods, recipes and portions, and the registry's XML has blank protocol and results fields. The ethics identifier is IR.TUMS.MEDICINE.REC.1400.290, approved June 16, 2021. [English registration](https://irct.ir/trial/56993) · [Persian registration](https://fa.irct.ir/trial/56993) · [Official registry XML](https://irct.ir/trial/56993/xml?download=1).

Amiri et al. published results online September 30, 2024. All 45 randomized participants completed the 12 weeks, with 15 in each arm. The motor endpoint was the UPDRS Part III total. The nicotine-rich diet alone and the nicotine-rich diet with protein redistribution lowered the score by 1.47 and 1.95 points versus control, respectively, with p < .001 reported for the active-versus-control comparisons. The two active arms did not differ significantly. Serum cotinine changed in both active arms versus control, and the corresponding marginal mean differences for serum α-synuclein were −52.82 and −175.85. The abstract and publicly available journal sections provide no UPDRS item estimates. The study compared complete diets and supplies no food-specific treatment estimate. [Primary results, PMID 39353253](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39353253/) · [Publisher preview and project number 53161](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0967586824003849).

The full article, intervention menus, and individual motor scores remained unavailable on November 12, 2026. The journal and ScienceDirect PDF routes returned HTTP 403. ## Probiotic capsules and bowel movement frequency

IRCT20170608034390N11 was registered May 14, 2022. The 2023 trial randomized 30 people with Parkinson’s and constipation and analyzed 27 after eight weeks. Table 3 shows median weekly bowel movements of four with the probiotic capsule and two with placebo (p = .02). Complete evacuation, laxative use and motor scores did not clearly differ. The capsule trial does not establish the effect of kefir, yogurt or other fermented foods.
