# Arachis oil adverse-event profile

## UK arachis oil pharmacovigilance access

The MHRA's April 2023 disclosure directed readers to its arachis oil Yellow Card profile. The letter provides no event counts. The former public archive route timed out when retried, and the current official page rendered its interpretation guidance without the reaction table in this environment. The profile remains an open data access trail. Exposure processing, route and individual visual-event results are unverified.
