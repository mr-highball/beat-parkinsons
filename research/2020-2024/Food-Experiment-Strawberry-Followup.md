# Strawberry and fisetin follow-up, 2020–2024

## Oral isolated fisetin and motor asymmetry in rotenone rats

Alikatte and colleagues first published online on January 29, 2020, before the 2021 issue. In a rotenone rat model, they gave isolated fisetin orally at 10 or 20 mg/kg for 25 days. The primary abstract reports better forelimb-use symmetry in the cylinder test and attenuation of changes in dopamine, mitochondrial enzymes and oxidative markers. The accessible abstract gives no arm-level cylinder values. This is a rodent motor test using an isolated compound. [Primary report, PMID 31992104](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31992104/)

## Nanoemulsified fisetin and motor tests in rotenone rats

Kumar and colleagues first published online on July 23, 2020. They compared oral fisetin in a self-nanoemulsifying drug delivery system with unformulated fisetin in a rotenone rat model. The primary abstract reports higher oral bioavailability and more favorable results for locomotor activity, motor coordination and catalepsy with the formulated compound, with p < .05. This was a formulation comparison in a separate animal study. It provides neither a raw fruit arm nor a handwriting outcome. [Primary report, PMID 32710995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32710995/)

## Oral isolated fisetin and motor tasks in MPTP mice

Chen and colleagues published on December 14, 2020. Three randomized groups of eight male mice received isolated fisetin plus MPTP, vehicle plus MPTP, or vehicle alone. The methods report oral fisetin at 100 ng/kg for 30 days and daily MPTP injections for five days starting on day 25. The ng unit is as printed. Figure 1 reports better open-field distance and velocity, pole performance, and wire-hang scores with fisetin relative to MPTP alone. The study also assessed striatal tyrosine hydroxylase and gut microbiota. The endpoints and preparation provide indirect context for claim `0349`.
