# Thai Clinical Trials Registry follow-up

## Eight-week Buriram cannabis extract trial

TCTR20210303005 first registered on March 3, 2021, ahead of its March 18 first enrollment date. Kanjanarangsichai and colleagues first published the results online on October 22, 2022. The trial randomized 40 participants with Parkinson's to sublingual cannabidiol-enriched cannabis extract or placebo, with 36 analyzed after eight weeks. The primary report identifies olive oil as the carrier in both arms. [Official registry XML](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/export/xml/TCTR20210303005) · [Primary report, PMID 36743777](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9894020/)

The adjusted total UPDRS difference was +4.32 points for the extract versus placebo (95% CI −3.68 to +13.32 as printed, p = .290). UPDRS III, timed up and go, repeated sit to stand, gait speed, anxiety, and depression showed no clear between-arm difference. EQ-5D-5L favored placebo, with a difference of −0.14 for the extract (95% CI −0.23 to −0.04). The randomized contrast estimates adding cannabis extract to olive oil. Neither arm isolates the oil's effect or uses it as a culinary intervention. [Primary report, Table 1](https://doi.org/10.25259/JNRP-2022-6-19)

## Cannabis oil versus rice bran oil

Tangrungruengkit and colleagues reported the six-week trial at the Japanese Society of Neurology meeting on May 20, 2021. TCTR20210807002 first registered on August 7, 2021, after the study's listed June 2020 enrollment start and September 2020 completion. The active intervention was 1:1 CBD-to-THC medicinal oil. The registry names rice bran oil as the placebo. The active oil's carrier was unspecified in the registry and meeting report. Fifteen people were randomized and 13 completed the trial. [Meeting program, Pe-15-1](https://www.neurology-jp.org/neuro2021/program/pdf/j_19.pdf) · [Primary abstract, p. S374](https://www.neurology-jp.org/Journal/public_pdf/61_supplement_04.pdf) · [Official registry XML](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/export/xml/TCTR20210807002)

The meeting abstract reported no clear differences for total MDS-UPDRS (p = .926), motor complications (p = .705), or PDQ-8 (p = .969). The registry summary gives a different total MDS-UPDRS p-value (.461) and records two withdrawals related to tolerability and one possible acute kidney injury in the cannabis arm. Its results-date field precedes the registration date, so the May meeting is the independently dated public outcome source. The two-arm comparison gives no isolated estimate for culinary rice bran oil. [Primary abstract, p. S374](https://www.neurology-jp.org/Journal/public_pdf/61_supplement_04.pdf) · [Registry results summary](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/export/xml/TCTR20210807002)

## Twelve-week Buriram cannabis extract registration

TCTR20230204001 first registered on February 4, 2023, ahead of its February 17 first enrollment date. The 60-person randomized study named Thai MoCA delayed recall at 12 weeks as primary and specified sublingual olive oil in both arms. The registry lists completion and leaves the result and publication fields empty. The authors' journal article reports a November 2024 presentation. The venue archive confirms the meeting dates, and the study's conference abstract and program entry remain unverified. The journal outcome appeared in April 2025. [Official registry XML](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/export/xml/TCTR20230204001) · [Taipei venue archive](https://www.ticc.com.tw/wSite/sp?bId=4192&ctNode=318&mp=2&xdUrl=%2FwSite%2Fap%2Fcp_Activity.jsp) · [Later outcome](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-TCTR-Followup.md#twelve-week-buriram-cannabis-extract-outcome)

## Thai cannabis practice cohort outcomes

The investigators presented an early report at the 2021 APHA meeting. The registry lists aggregate results dated February 19, 2023. Stienrut and colleagues published the fuller primary report on July 17, 2024. The cohort enrolled 21,284 people across 30 Thai hospitals with different indications. The paper identifies the prescribed formulation as cannabis and coconut oil. [2019 registration](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-TCTR-Followup.md#thai-cannabis-practice-cohort-registration) · [2021 meeting abstract](https://apha.confex.com/apha/2021/meetingapi.cgi/Paper/507664?filename=2021_Abstract507664.pdf&template=Word) · [Primary report, PMID 39144529](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11324265/)

In the paper's model for one daily drop over four weeks, the Parkinson subgroup (n = 134) had lower ESAS pain scores (−1.08, 95% CI −1.63 to −0.52) and lower anorexia scores (−0.48, 95% CI −0.88 to −0.08). The estimate for the row labeled insomnia was −0.38 (95% CI −0.88 to +0.12). The authors used ESAS drowsiness for that row. The uncontrolled, practitioner-selected exposure precludes isolating a causal effect of coconut oil or cannabis. [Primary report, Table 5](https://doi.org/10.1159/000540153)

## Buriram olive-oil vehicle and motor item outcomes

First published October 22, 2022. The Buriram trial randomized 40 people with Parkinson disease and analyzed 19 in the cannabinoid arm and 17 in placebo after eight weeks. Both sublingual preparations used olive oil of unreported grade. The adjusted between-arm estimates for UPDRS III, timed up and go and gait velocity were 1.28 points (95% CI −3.86 to 6.41), 0.49 seconds (−1.08 to 2.06), and −0.06 m/s (−0.17 to 0.06). Anxiety and depression comparisons were inconclusive. These are contrasts for added cannabinoid extract. Separate clinical postural instability, rest tremor and bradykinesia scores are not shown. The trial has registry identifier TCTR20210303005. ## Official Buriram registry outcome fields

The official public export records March 3, 2021 as registration and March 18 as enrollment start, with target 40. The planned primary measure was total UPDRS at eight weeks. Secondary measures include timed up and go, 10-meter walking speed, repeated sit-to-stand, quality of life, anxiety and depression on Thai HADS, safety and laboratory tests. The register names active CBD/THC extract and placebo. The article separately establishes sublingual olive oil in both preparations. The export provides no vehicle, grade, posted results or version history. The recorded registration date is the earliest verified timing field.
