Title: Effect of food sources of nitrate, polyphenols, L-arginine and L-citrulline on endurance exercise performance: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials.
PMID: 34965876
PMCID: PMC8715640
DOI: 10.1186/s12970-021-00472-y
First published: 2021
Authors: d'Unienville NMA, Blake HT, Coates AM, Hill AM, Nelson MJ, Buckley JD
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34965876/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 118 randomized controlled trials (d'Unienville et al., 2021, J Int Soc Sports Nutr, PMC8715640) evaluating food sources of nitrate, polyphenols, L-arginine, and L-citrulline on endurance exercise performance. Among nitrate-rich foods, small ergogenic benefits were seen for beetroot but NOT for red spinach (Amaranthus leafy extract), Swiss chard, or rhubarb. Highlights both the distinction between leafy nitrate supplements and culinary amaranth grain and the lack of consistent human ergogenic efficacy.
