Original study scope
The review separately presents basil and rosemary essential oil aromas, a multi-oil blend containing cinnamon, and an acute coffee fragrance exposure. Designs include healthy or occupational contexts and outcomes such as sleepiness, fatigue or mood. Each odor and comparator has its own study row.

Continuing evidence gaps
Olfactory stimulation and multi-oil exposure are separate from ordinary culinary intake. No constituent or smell comparison yields one of the six frozen Parkinson estimates.
