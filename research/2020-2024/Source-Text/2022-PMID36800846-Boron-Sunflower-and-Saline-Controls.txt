Title: Investigation of the pharmacological, behavioral, and biochemical effects of boron in parkinson-indicated rats
Publication date: 2022-08-31
PMID: 36800846
DOI: 10.14715/cmb/2022.68.8.3
Source: https://cellmolbiol.org/index.php/CMB/article/view/4486
Original PDF: https://cellmolbiol.org/index.php/CMB/article/download/4486/2237/10646
Text coverage: Complete primary paper, route descriptions, and visually verified Figures 5–8.

Both oil and saline control groups contained six rats. The abstract describes saline as subcutaneous; the detailed methods describe intraperitoneal saline and subcutaneous sunflower seed oil. Figures 5–7 give shared significance letters for the two control groups on general motion analysis, square crossings and rotarod persistence. The marked forelimb comparisons in Figure 8 share control letters too. The abstract describes the catalepsy test as the exception to the reported toxin group differences, but the article does not give a separately resolved oil-versus-saline catalepsy estimate. This route and outcome evidence supplies no direction for dietary cold-pressed oil and Parkinson executive function or rigidity.
