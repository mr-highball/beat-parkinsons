Title: Oral capsaicinoids and precision in a challenged swallow task
Publication date: 2021-01-15
PMCID: PMC8423940
PMID: 33449304
DOI: 10.1007/s13311-020-00996-2
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8423940/
Verified depth: original article including exposure definitions and outcome tables
Checked: 2026-11-22

Cheng and colleagues studied healthy volunteers in three separate experiments. The source material was quantified capsaicinoids from a red pepper sauce suspended in water. The short preconditioning comparison favored greater precision in a visually cued swallow task. Continuous exposure did not show the same improvement. Other outcomes included pharyngeal pressure and upper esophageal sphincter relaxation. These instrumented measures and exposure settings do not supply symptom severity after eating ground cayenne in Parkinson disease.
