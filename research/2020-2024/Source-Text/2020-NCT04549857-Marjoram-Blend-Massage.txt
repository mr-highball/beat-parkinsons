Registry for blended marjoram oil hand massage
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04549857
Registration: NCT04549857
Coverage: Official ClinicalTrials.gov protocol and publication linkage

First posted September 16, 2020. The trial assigned a blend of cedarwood, sweet marjoram and sweet orange oils in almond oil, almond oil alone, or usual care. The aromatic products were applied by hand and wrist massage in adults with rheumatoid arthritis. The protocol lists PSQI and Epworth sleepiness. It has no stated REM dream-enactment endpoint. A 2023 report expressly identifies this registration.
