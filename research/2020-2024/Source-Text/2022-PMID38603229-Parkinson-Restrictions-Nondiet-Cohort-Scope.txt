Original study scope
The original Parkinson study compares reported symptom burden around pandemic restrictions in Ireland. The outcome context is restriction and service disruption, rather than assigned herbs. SAGE is the publisher. The publisher dates the online article October 2022 and the issue is from 2023.

Continuing evidence gaps
No individually measured fresh, dried or ground sage exposure links to fatigue, pain or rigidity in the original analysis.
