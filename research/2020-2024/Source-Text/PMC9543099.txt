Title: Neuropathological studies of serotonergic and noradrenergic systems in Lewy body disease patients with delusion or depression
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9543099/
PMID: 35695782
PMCID: PMC9543099
DOI: 10.1111/pcn.13436
Publication date: 2022-06-28
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

Researchers compare serotonergic and noradrenergic findings in 24 Lewy body disease donors with clinical histories emphasizing depression or delusions. This tissue analysis does not measure an oral fig contrast or count visual episodes.
