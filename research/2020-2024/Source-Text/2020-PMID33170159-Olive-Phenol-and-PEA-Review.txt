Original study scope
This overview discusses palmitoylethanolamide and either olive-derived hydroxytyrosol or Bacopa extracts. It identifies extra-virgin olive oil as one dietary source of hydroxytyrosol and reviews mechanisms and animal exposures.

Continuing evidence gaps
A proposal for a supplement and experimental observations using isolated phenols do not give a whole-oil comparison or an attributable Parkinson anxiety or insomnia outcome.
