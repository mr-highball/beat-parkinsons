Coverage: Distilled text from the identity-matched original PubMed Central article and PubMed metadata.

Original four-week rat feeding comparison. Table 1 describes the recipes, Table 2 the assigned diet composition, and Table 9 the growth and stool measurements.

Both soup powders contained 5 g parsley and 5 g olive oil per 100 g. The parsley was freeze-dried as part of preparation. The three assigned diets contained 10.00, 8.76 and 8.74 g corn oil per 100 g, respectively. The mixtures were fed at 20% of the assigned diet, alongside changes in casein, starch and cellulose. Mean fecal weights were 1.16, 2.46 and 2.42 g/day (overall p = .0003). Mean fecal moisture was 21.29%, 25.05% and 25.33%. The report gives no independent comparison of any of the three named ingredients. Oil grade, whole fresh-parsley exposure, human population and Parkinson constipation severity remain unresolved.
