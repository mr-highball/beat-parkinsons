Title: Chronic feeding with 3% dried raw blueberries (V. corymbosum) reduces apomorphine-induced rotations and striatal dopaminergic loss in hemiparkinsonian rats.
PMID: 33648289
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2020.110066
First published: 2021
Authors: Parra-Paz VG, Calderón-Sauri A, Granados-Patrón D, Cuevas-Carbonell SG, García-López D, Dawn-Ojeda A, et al.
Journal: Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33648289/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical intrastriatal 6-OHDA hemiparkinsonian rat study feeding 3% lyophilized raw Vaccinium corymbosum blueberries (2 weeks pre- and 3 weeks post-lesion) vs standard diet. Reduced total apomorphine-induced contralateral rotations by 46% (p<0.05; days 14 and 21 p<0.001) and spared striatal TH immunoreactivity (p<0.05), whereas forepaw cylinder asymmetry reduction was non-significant and microgliosis was unchanged. Limits: preclinical rat neurotoxin model; non-specific motor asymmetry/rotation proxy.
