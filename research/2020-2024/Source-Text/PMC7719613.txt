Title: Alpha-Synuclein Oligomers and Neurofilament Light Chain in Spinal Fluid Differentiate Multiple System Atrophy from Lewy Body Synucleinopathies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7719613/
PMID: 32557811
PMCID: PMC7719613
DOI: 10.1002/ana.25824
Publication date: 2020-08-01
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: CSF differential biomarker comparison

The study compares alpha-synuclein oligomers and neurofilament light in CSF among multiple system atrophy, Lewy body synucleinopathy and control groups. Biomarker discrimination is the outcome. No fig ingestion and no exposure-linked visual hallucination count were measured.
