Title: Leaf extract capsules and state anxiety before orthopedic surgery
DOI: 10.1016/j.hermed.2021.100532
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2210803321001123
Publication date: February 2022 issue, Journal of Herbal Medicine 31, 100532. Available metadata does not establish the exact first public date.
Coverage: Original author abstract and posted publisher excerpts. Full result tables and trial registration remain unverified.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18 UTC

The trial enrolled 80 orthopedic surgery candidates and compared oral leaf extract capsules with starch placebo administered before surgery. The original abstract reports no baseline anxiety difference and a lower anxiety score in the extract group at the next morning assessment. The authors used the Spielberger instrument. Numeric group means and a depression outcome are unavailable in the inspected abstract.

This preoperative extract trial has different authors, sample and treatment from the Saidi postoperative tea study. Preserve the timing, placebo, formulation and anxiety-specific outcome. Neither design estimates depressive symptom change in people with Parkinson disease.
