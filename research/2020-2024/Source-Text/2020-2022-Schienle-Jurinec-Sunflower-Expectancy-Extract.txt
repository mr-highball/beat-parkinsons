Factual extract from three primary publications by Jurinec and Schienle.
2020 DOI 10.1016/j.jad.2020.08.087, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33065817/: 126 adults randomized to CBT, CBT plus sunflower oil presented as a natural remedy, or waiting. Greater reduction was reported in the combined intervention.
2021 DOI 10.2147/PRBM.S294940, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7912085/: same two course arms with 82 participants returning at three months. BDI-II clinical cutoff comparison 74% below cutoff for CBT plus the placebo treatment and 53% for CBT alone.
2022 DOI 10.1016/j.conctc.2022.100948, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9228278/: separate 60-person randomized study. The oil was disclosed as placebo and linked to relaxation homework. BDI-II changes −7.93 with CBT plus open-label placebo versus −5.47 with CBT (p=.001). Group difference in improvement to the three-month visit p=.052, with differential attrition.
Neither evidence family isolates sunflower oil from changes in instruction and adherence. The reports do not provide a refining grade or a Parkinson population.
