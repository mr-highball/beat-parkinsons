Title: Chinese herbal formula GCNY-medicated serum in a microglial cell experiment
PMID: 36188472
PMCID: PMC9515651
DOI: 10.3389/fnins.2022.990040
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9515651/
Date basis: 2022-09-14 original journal publication.
Coverage: Factual summary of the complete original laboratory paper and formula methods.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The GCNY formula contains dried Zingiber officinale alongside several other plant materials. The study evaluates the medicated-serum mixture in human microglial cells. It measures experimental oxidative stress endpoints. Constipation occurs in the background as a proposed use, and the experiment does not measure stool, symptoms, or an isolated effect of ginger.
