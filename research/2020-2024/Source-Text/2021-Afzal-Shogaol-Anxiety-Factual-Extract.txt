Coverage: Original Tables 1–2 and Methods summarized as facts. This extract does not reproduce or translate licensed article text.

The study compared injured mice receiving saline with injured mice receiving intraperitoneal 6-shogaol and other comparison groups. Table 1 gives mean open-arm entries of 2.10 in injured controls and 6.00, 6.60 and 7.70 across the lower to higher 6-shogaol arms (six per arm). The lowest-dose significance marker conflicts with the Results narrative, which calls this result inconclusive. Table 2 reports lower ambulation in all three treated arms than in injured controls despite a written claim of increased ambulation. The study measured behavior following traumatic brain injury in mice and does not give a clinical anxiety scale or effects of oral grated ginger.
