Title: Neural substrates in Parkinson's Disease psychosis: A systematic review
Source: https://doi.org/10.1101/2022.12.23.22283889
DOI: 10.1101/2022.12.23.22283889
Publication date: 2022-12-24
Date basis: first medRxiv posting date in original JATS and repository record
Source depth: complete official medRxiv preprint JATS; local research digest

The December 2022 review included 18 articles covering 291 Parkinson psychosis participants and 433 comparison participants. Psychosis includes hallucinations or delusions and the reported outcomes concern imaging. The 2024 Brain Communications paper is an expanded version of the same registered review, CRD42020221904. Neither version records oral dried figs or food-timed visual episode counts.

The official medRxiv JATS contains the complete released preprint body. The published review at PMC11538965 incorporates searches through January 25, 2023. Count the two records as one evolving evidence synthesis.
