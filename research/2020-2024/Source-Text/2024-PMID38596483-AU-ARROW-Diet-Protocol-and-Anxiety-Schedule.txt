Text coverage: original protocol, Dietary Counselling, Table 1, and Table 2.

The protocol compares a combined lifestyle intervention with education among older people at increased dementia risk. Olive oil is one part of the MIND diet recommendations, alongside exercise, cognitive training and health monitoring in the intervention. The depression, anxiety and stress scale is scheduled at baseline and months 6, 12, 18 and 24. The design does not directly compare cold-pressed oil or isolate its contribution to anxiety. There are no efficacy results in the protocol.
