The protocol dated October 21, 2022 identifies ChiCTR2200061991. It names anxiety and depression as secondary hypotheses for a 12-week support program and specifies the Zung Self-Rating Anxiety Scale and Geriatric Depression Scale. The assessment prose lists weeks 0, 13 and 24. The publication establishes a named measurement plan by October 2022. It provides no outcome estimates.
