Cinnamon and orange odor cues in the Veranopolis protocol
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8679106/
PMID: 34911720
PMCID: PMC8679106
DOI: 10.1136/bmjopen-2021-054423
Coverage: original protocol full text, Table 1 and odor-testing methods

First published December 15, 2021. The Veranópolis community Parkinson protocol specifies 16 odor cues in a standardized smell identification battery, including cinnamon and orange. The cues are smelled during assessment. Other instruments in the schedule address falls and sleep symptoms separately. The protocol provides no consumed cinnamon or orange intervention and no odor-item results. The PROBE-PD registry names the same site and an extended observation period, with precise overlap with this protocol not established. [Related registered cohort](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05677529).
