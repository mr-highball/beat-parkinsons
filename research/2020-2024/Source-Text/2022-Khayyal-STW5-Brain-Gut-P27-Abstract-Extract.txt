Title: The efficacy of STW 5 in irritable bowel syndrome partly involves serotonin as a mediator in the brain-gut axis
DOI: 10.1055/s-0042-1749277
Source: https://doi.org/10.1055/s-0042-1749277
Original meeting PDF: https://www.thieme-connect.com/products/ejournals/conferencepdf/086141/10.1055/s-00000101.pdf
Publication: Zeitschrift fur Phytotherapie 43(S01):S41–S42, P27, June 2022. Publisher-deposited Crossref online date 2022-06-13; metadata creation is later and does not independently prove first access on that day.
Coverage: Factual extract of complete indexed original P27 meeting member, with official poster inventory and DOI identity corroboration.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Khayyal, Gamal and Mohsen describe a nine-herb hydroalcoholic STW5 product at 1 and 2.5 mL/kg, with an itopride comparator, in a rodent bowel-stress model. Gastrointestinal measures, brain/colon serotonin and oxidative markers are reported. The member says one week of stress and also refers to a two-week experimental period; the timing needs reconciliation. No mood or depression outcome is named in this abstract.

The topic and investigators overlap the 2021 Gamal paper, but the abstract provides no explicit participant/animal mapping. Its doses, comparator and experimental timing cannot be assigned to the earlier paper without primary linkage. It supplies neither an independently established new cohort nor an isolated lemon balm effect.
