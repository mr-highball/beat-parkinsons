Title: Effects of a Functional Food Made with Salvia hispanica L. (Chia Seed), Amaranthus hypochondriacus L. (Amaranth), and an Ethanolic Extract of Curcuma longa L. (Curcumin) in a Rat Model of Childhood Obesity.
PMID: 38890948
PMCID: PMC11171659
DOI: 10.3390/foods13111720
First published: 2024
Authors: Rivero-Salgado GM, Zamudio SR, Fregoso-Aguilar TA, Quevedo-Corona L
Journal: Foods (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38890948/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical rat study (Rivero-Salgado et al., 2024, Foods, PMC11171659) feeding male Wistar rat pups in a small-litter hypercaloric childhood obesity model a bundled functional food containing chia seed, Amaranthus hypochondriacus flour, and ethanolic Curcuma longa (curcumin) extract for 7 weeks. Barnes maze spatial learning and memory did not differ between any groups (null), while non-ambulatory grooming time was higher in hypercaloric functional-food rats than standard-diet controls. Limits: three-ingredient bundled mixture (chia + amaranth + curcumin) cannot isolate amaranth; null cognitive effect and non-PD juvenile obesity model.
