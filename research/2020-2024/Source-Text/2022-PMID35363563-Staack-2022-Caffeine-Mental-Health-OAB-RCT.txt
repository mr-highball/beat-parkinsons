Title: The Impact of Caffeine Intake on Mental Health Symptoms in Postmenopausal Females with Overactive Bladder Symptoms: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial.
PMID: 35363563
DOI: 10.1089/jwh.2021.0467
Registry ID: NCT02180048
First published: 2022
Authors: Staack A, Distelberg B, Moldovan C, Belay RE, Sabaté J
Journal: Journal of women's health (2002)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35363563/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Prospective double-blind placebo-controlled crossover RCT (NCT02180048) in 81 randomized (56 completed, mean age 69.2 y) postmenopausal women with overactive bladder comparing 200 mg/d caffeine, 400 mg/d caffeine, and placebo capsules for 1 week each. Moderate caffeine (400 mg/d) significantly decreased Beck Anxiety Inventory scores vs placebo (p<0.05), whereas Beck Depression Inventory, Insomnia Severity Index, and Perceived Stress Scale were unaffected; 2 participants withdrew at 400 mg/d for headache/nausea. Limits: isolated caffeine capsules in non-PD women with OAB.
