Title: Nutritional Composition, Efficacy, and Processing of Vigna angularis (Adzuki Bean) for the Human Diet: An Overview.

First published: 2022
Authors: Wang Y, Yao X, Shen H, Zhao R, Li Z, Shen X, et al.
Journal: Molecules (Basel, Switzerland)

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Narrative review (Wang et al., 2022, Molecules, PMC9506481) summarizing nutritional composition (starch, lysine-rich protein, polyphenols, polysaccharides), processing methods, dual food-medicine heritage in Asia, and mostly preclinical antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, antidiabetic, renoprotective, and HFD-cognitive findings for Vigna angularis. Identifies no human Parkinson's disease or symptom-specific clinical trials.
