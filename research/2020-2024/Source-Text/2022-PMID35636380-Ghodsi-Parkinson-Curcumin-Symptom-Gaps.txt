Title: Ghodsi et al. Parkinson curcumin pilot and missing symptom-item results
PMID: 35636380
DOI: 10.1016/j.clineuro.2022.107300
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35636380/
Published: 2022-05-18 online.
Coverage: Original author abstract and indexed article identity. Full item-level result tables remain unverified.

The triple-blind randomized add-on pilot assigned 60 people with Parkinson disease equally to nanomicelle curcumin or placebo and followed them for nine months alongside usual treatment. MDS-UPDRS and PDQ-39 scores did not show a between-group difference at individual visits in the author abstract. Part III had a group-by-time p=.04, with no significant post-hoc visit contrast reported. These aggregate results do not establish the direction of separate pain or other abnormal sensations, rigidity, handwriting, daytime sleepiness, or other individual items. The intervention is formulated curcumin and cannot authenticate ground turmeric.

Round 1 claim 0812 remains open for an attributable clinical Parkinson pain measurement. Sought access detail: original results and methods including whether the individual pain and discomfort items were collected and compared by arm.
