Title: Antiadhesive activity of hydroethanolic extract from bean pods of Phaseolus vulgaris (common bean) against uropathogenic E. coli and permeability of its constituents through Caco-2 cells monolayer.
PMID: 33746003
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2021.114053
First published: 2021
Authors: Popowski D, Pawlowska KA, Deipenbrock M, Hensel A, Kruk A, Melzig MF, Piwowarski JP, Granica S
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33746003/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract Facts: Documents pharmacopeial Phaseoli pericarpium (Phaseolus vulgaris bean pods) traditionally used as a diuretic (first reported in 1608) and antidiabetic herbal tea. Hydroethanolic pod extract (ethanol:water 7:3, rich in rutin and soyasaponin derivatives) inhibited adhesion of uropathogenic Escherichia coli (strain NU14) to human T24 bladder epithelial cells concentration-dependently (0.5-2 mg/mL) without cytotoxicity, with Caco-2 permeability of rutin and soyasaponins. In vitro bladder-cell assay of solvent pod extract; does not test steamed culinary green beans or Parkinson neurogenic urinary urgency.
