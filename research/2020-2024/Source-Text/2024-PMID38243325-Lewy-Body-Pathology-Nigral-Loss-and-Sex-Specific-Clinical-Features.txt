Title: Sex differences for clinical correlates of substantia nigra neuron loss in people with Lewy body pathology
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10797801/
PMID: 38243325
PMCID: PMC10797801
DOI: 10.1186/s13293-024-00583-6
Publication year: 2024
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The multicenter clinical-pathological analysis relates substantia nigra cell loss and clinical features by sex. Hallucinations are self- or caregiver-reported clinical features. Tissue loss and symptom history cannot identify a fig-related episode effect.

The clinical features were recorded across contributing centers. The constituent clinical and tissue measures were used to examine apparent differences by sex.
