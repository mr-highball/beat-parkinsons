Title: Effect of a 12-Week Almond-Enriched Diet on Biomarkers of Cognitive Performance, Mood, and Cardiometabolic Health in Older Overweight Adults.
PMID: 32340150
PMCID: PMC7230374
DOI: 10.3390/nu12041180
First published: 2020
Authors: Coates, Morgillo, Yandell, Scholey, Buckley
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32340150/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-week parallel RCT in 128 older overweight/obese adults (50-80 y; n=63 almond-enriched diet providing 15% energy as whole natural raw California almonds vs n=65 isocaloric nut-free carbohydrate snacks). No significant group x time interactions on any cognitive composite or test (Table 3: Attention RT p = 0.347, Attention Accuracy p = 0.264, Working Memory RT p = 0.725, Working Memory Accuracy p = 0.148, Long-Term Memory p = 0.318, Executive Function Accuracy p = 0.693, Peg and Ball planning/execution/errors p = 0.852/0.444/0.786) or on any mood/sleepiness score (Table 4: POMS TMD p = 0.644, Tension p = 0.991, Depression p = 0.827, Fatigue p = 0.826, Vigor p = 0.163, Bond-Lader Alert p = 0.067, Calm p = 0.832, Karolinska Sleepiness Score p = 0.892). Triglycerides and systolic BP decreased significantly. Limits: cognitively healthy older non-PD adults without baseline mood/sleep disorders.
