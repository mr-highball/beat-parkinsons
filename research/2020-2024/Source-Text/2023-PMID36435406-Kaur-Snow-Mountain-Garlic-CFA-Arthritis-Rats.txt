Title: Validation of traditional claims of anti-arthritic efficacy of trans-Himalayan snow mountain garlic (Allium ampeloprasum L.) extract using adjuvant-induced arthritis rat model.
PMID: 36435406
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2022.115939
First published: 2023
Authors: Kaur B, Kumar N, Patel MK, Chopra K, Saxena S
Journal: Journal of Ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36435406/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Botanical-Boundary Appraisal:
Oral aqueous extract of trans-Himalayan Snow Mountain Garlic (Allium ampeloprasum bulb cultivar / elephant garlic, 250-500 mg/kg x 28 d) ameliorated CFA-induced arthritis edema and hyperalgesia in Wistar rats. BOTANICAL BOUNDARY: Snow Mountain Garlic / elephant garlic is a wild bulb cultivar of A. ampeloprasum chemically distinct from culinary garden leek pseudostem/leaves (A. ampeloprasum var. porrum), so it does NOT establish oral culinary leek analgesia (exposure_fit = remote, claim_effect = none).
