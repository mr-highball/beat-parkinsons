Title: Atypical Presentations of Huntington Disease-like 2 in South African Individuals
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11233840/
PMID: 38725192
PMCID: PMC11233840
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.14052
Publication date: 2024-05-09
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

This clinical series describes cognitive and motor manifestations in genetically characterized Huntington disease-like 2, including atypical presentations of bradykinesia or rigidity. These are a separate disorder and genotype; there is no dried fig or Parkinson visual hallucination comparison.
