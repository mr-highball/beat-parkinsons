Title: A Non-Randomized Trial Investigating the Impact of Brown Rice Consumption on Gut Microbiota, Attention, and Short-Term Working Memory in Thai School-Aged Children.
PMID: 36501207
PMCID: PMC9738792
DOI: 10.3390/nu14235176
First published: 2022
Authors: Gruneck, Marriott, Gentekaki
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9738792/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Non-randomized dietary trial in n=127 Thai school-aged children consuming Sinlek brown rice vs control. Found NO significant differences in Psychomotor Vigilance Test (PVT, sustained attention), Corsi block-tapping working memory, or gut microbiota abundance; age explained cognitive variation. Limits: non-randomized, healthy children.
