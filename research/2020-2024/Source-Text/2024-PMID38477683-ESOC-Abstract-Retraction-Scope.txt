The notice withdraws one abstract about using an external apixaban control arm to reproduce PACIFIC-AF trial results. The authors found that their matching analysis did not meet their standards. The notice identifies that abstract by title and does not refer to the Hamburg coffee presentation O036/808. The linked book contains independent presentations, so apply the withdrawal only to the named member.
