Title: Self-Reported Visual Complaints in People with Parkinson's Disease: A Systematic Review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9108577/
PMID: 35001897
PMCID: PMC9108577
DOI: 10.3233/JPD-202324
Publication date: 2022-01-04
Date basis: first public pre-press date in original publisher JATS; issue release 2022-04-05
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The systematic review compiles patient-reported visual complaints across studies and distinguishes them from functional visual tests. It documents heterogeneity in phenotyping and does not identify an intake comparison using dried figs or a defined change in visual episode frequency after food.
