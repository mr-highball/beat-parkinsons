The SUPERBRAIN protocol plans multidomain cognitive and nutritional interventions in older people at dementia risk. It describes vegetable advice as part of a broader dietary pattern and also uses exercise and cognitive training. Executive function is one cognitive domain. The inspected recipe and methods do not identify an assigned cooked tomato comparison.
