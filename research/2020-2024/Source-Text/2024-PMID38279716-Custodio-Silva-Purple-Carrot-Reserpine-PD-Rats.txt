Title: Purple Carrot Extract Exhibits a Neuroprotective Profile in th e Nigrostriatal Pathway in the Reserpine-induced Model of Parkinson 's Disease.
PMID: 38279716
DOI: 10.2174/0118715249260445231226112021
First published: 2024
Authors: Custódio-Silva AC, Beserra-Filho JIA, Soares-Silva B, Maria-Macêdo A, Silva-Martins S, Silva SP et al.
Journal: Cent Nerv Syst Agents Med Chem
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38279716/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical Parkinsonism study in 6-month-old male rats exposed to progressive subcutaneous reserpine (0.01 mg/kg x 28 d preventive phase, then 0.1 mg/kg every other day x 23 d protective phase). Chronic oral purple carrot root extract (400 mg/kg/d) vs vehicle significantly attenuated motor disabilities: reduced bar catalepsy duration (rodent akinesia/bradykinesia assay) and decreased vacuous oral movement frequency, accompanied by preserved tyrosine hydroxylase (TH+) immunoreactivity in SNpc and VTA. Transferability caveats: anthocyanin-rich purple carrot root extract (400 mg/kg) rather than culinary orange raw/steamed carrot; rat reserpine model; oral movements model tardive/orofacial dyskinesia rather than levodopa-induced dyskinesia.
