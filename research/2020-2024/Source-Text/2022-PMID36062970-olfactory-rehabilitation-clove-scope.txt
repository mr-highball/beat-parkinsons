Title: Interventions for the treatment of persistent post-COVID-19 olfactory dysfunction
Publication year: 2022
PMID: 36062970
PMCID: PMC9443431
DOI: 10.1002/14651858.cd013876.pub3
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9443431/
Coverage: Factual extract of the original article body. Round 1 food exposure and outcome scope.

Trial descriptions list clove among multiple odors used for post-COVID smell training. These are scent exposures, sometimes with several other oils or scents, rather than oral culinary ground clove. No separate effect of clove is estimated for the mapped Parkinson pain or anxiety symptoms. Trout appears in the appendix search filters for animal studies, and there is no studied trout or date-fruit intervention.
