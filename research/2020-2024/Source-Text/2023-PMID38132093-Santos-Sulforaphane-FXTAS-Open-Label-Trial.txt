Title: Open-Label Sulforaphane Trial in FMR1 Premutation Carriers with Fragile-X-Associated Tremor and Ataxia Syndrome (FXTAS).
PMID: 38132093
PMCID: PMC10741398
DOI: 10.3390/cells12242773
First published: 2023
Authors: Santos E, Clark C, Biag HMB, Tang SJ, Kim K, Ponzini MD et al.
Journal: Cells
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38132093/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

24-week open-label pilot trial of purified sulforaphane supplement in 15 older adults (11 completers) with Fragile X-associated tremor/ataxia syndrome (FXTAS). Safe/tolerable with mitochondrial biomarker shifts, but NO clinical cognitive/motor measures remained significant after multiple-comparison correction. Non-PD, uncontrolled, and purified sulforaphane (not cauliflower food).
