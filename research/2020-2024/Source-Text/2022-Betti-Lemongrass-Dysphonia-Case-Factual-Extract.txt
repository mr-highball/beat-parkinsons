Betti and colleagues, primary case series, Case 1

Original source https://doi.org/10.23750/abm.v93iS3.13071

In one 2022 case, a child consumed lemongrass iced tea and potato chips before exercise and developed urticaria and dysphonia. Allergy testing and the history of symptoms again after potato exposure favor a competing explanation. The original does not establish lemongrass attribution or measure acoustic loudness. The exposure was a beverage consumed alongside another food, outside Parkinson disease.
