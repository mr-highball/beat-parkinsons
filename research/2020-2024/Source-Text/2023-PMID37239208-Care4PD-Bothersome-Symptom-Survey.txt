Of 1,437 returned questionnaires, 1,269 were usable. Figure 1 analyzes 906 respondents without device therapy who ranked up to three bothersome symptoms. Those meeting the study's suspected advanced Parkinson criteria more frequently selected the combined gait disorder with falls item (reported p = .007). The figure's color key uses orange for the group without suspected advanced disease. The item is a bother ranking rather than a fall-episode count, and the group color has no fruit-exposure meaning.
