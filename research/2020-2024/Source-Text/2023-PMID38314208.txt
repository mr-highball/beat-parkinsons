Title: Acute toxicity by Salvia officinalis with intestinal sub-occlusion
First published: 2023-09-30
PMID: 38314208
PMCID: PMC10832869
DOI: 10.12865/CHSJ.49.03.17
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10832869/
Text type: original-source evidence summary

Rapino and colleagues report one patient with abdominal pain rated 6 on an 11-point scale, diarrhea, fever and intestinal obstruction-like findings after reportedly eating a large amount of common sage plant material. The report provides no measured amount, preparation or freshness. There were concurrent medications and the clinical course included stopping the plant exposure and receiving treatment. The case documents the timing and measured pain at presentation. It does not isolate the cause or show what happens after normal culinary intake or in Parkinson disease.
