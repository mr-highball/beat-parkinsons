Title: Crossover Analysis of the Astringent, Antimicrobial, and Anti-inflammatory Effects of Illicium verum/Star Anise in the Oral Cavity.
PMID: 34195261
PMCID: PMC8181057
DOI: 10.1155/2021/5510174
First published: 2021
Authors: Assiry AA, Karobari MI, Bhavikatti SK, Marya A
Journal: BioMed research international
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34195261/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover clinical trial (Assiry et al., 2021, Biomed Res Int, PMC8181057) in 50 adults comparing Illicium verum mouthwash vs placebo rinse (21 days each, 14-day washout). Mouthwash significantly lowered gingival index, papillary bleeding index, and oral microbial count (p<0.05). Only identified human RCT of I. verum, but uses non-swallowed topical oral rinse and evaluates periodontal indices only — no ingestion and no Parkinson's or neurological symptom endpoints.
