Title: Fruits and their impact on the gut microbiota, gut motility and constipation.
PMID: 34505614
DOI: 10.1039/d1fo01125a
First published: 2021
Authors: Katsirma Z, Dimidi E, Rodriguez-Mateos A, Whelan K
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34505614/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Narrative/mechanistic review of fruits, gut microbiota, motility, and constipation. Summarizes that prunes, raisins, and apple fiber isolate have been shown to increase fecal weight in humans, and apple fiber isolate, kiwifruit, fig paste, and orange extract reduce gut transit time, while emphasizing limited evidence on which fruit constituent predominates. Context review only.
