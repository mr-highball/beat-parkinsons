OC5 reports the six-month BENEFIC cognitive trial, NCT02551419, with a high-oleic sunflower-oil placebo supplied by Abitec and a ketogenic MCT intervention. This is a report of the same MCI trial as the final cognitive analysis. That analysis specifies refined oil and supplies individual switching and error results. The conference aggregate claim is an active-comparator finding in mild cognitive impairment. It gives no cold-pressed preparation, oil-free group or Parkinson executive response.
