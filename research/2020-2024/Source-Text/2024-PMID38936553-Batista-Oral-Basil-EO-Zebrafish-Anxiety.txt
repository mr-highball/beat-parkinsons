Title: Anticonvulsant and anxiolytic-like potential of the essential oil from the Ocimum basilicum Linn leaves and its major constituent estragole on adult zebrafish (Danio rerio).
PMID: 38936553
DOI: 10.1016/j.neuint.2024.105796
First published: 2024
Authors: Batista FLA, de Araújo SMB, de Sousa DB, Sobrinho FBC, de Lima Silva MG, de Oliveira MRC, da Costa RHS, Rodrigues LB, Bezerra FS, de Azevedo DV, Vieira-Neto AE, Magalhães FEA, de Menezes IRA
Journal: Neurochemistry international
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38936553/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical adult zebrafish (Danio rerio) study administering oral Ocimum basilicum L. leaf essential oil and its constituent estragole. Reported non-toxic anxiolytic-like effects (including against alcohol-withdrawal anxiety) involving GABAA and 5-HT1/2A/2C/3A/3B receptors and anticonvulsant attenuation of PTZ seizures.
