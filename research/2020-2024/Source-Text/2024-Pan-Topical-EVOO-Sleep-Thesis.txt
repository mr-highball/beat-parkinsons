Pan, Mei-Hua. August 2024 university thesis. Trial NCT05466045.
Source: https://irlib.ntunhs.edu.tw/retrieve/22065/112NTCN0563052-001.pdf
Coverage: factual text from Methods, Figure 4-4, Table 4-7 and registry appendix.

The thesis compared extra-virgin olive oil with saline applied to itchy skin twice daily over 28 days in adults receiving hemodialysis. It reports 81 participants, with 42 in the topical oil group and 39 in the saline group. The registry estimated enrollment at 86. PSQI was collected at baseline and days 14 and 28. Figure 4-4 gives baseline and four-week means of 10.31 and 7.17 with oil and 9.33 and 9.38 with saline. Table 4-7 reports day-28 adjusted within-arm coefficients of −2.863 (95% CI −4.851 to −0.874, p=.005) for oil and +0.126 (−0.202 to +0.455, p=.451) for saline. The table does not provide a direct between-arm coefficient. This topical trial measured broad sleep quality and pruritus outside Parkinson disease.
