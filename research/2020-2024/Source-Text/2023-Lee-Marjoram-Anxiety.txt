Marjoram oil inhalation and nurses anxiety
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2023.1287282/full
selected methods and findings paraphrase

Published November 17, 2023. Sixty intensive-care nurses were randomized and 57 completed. A two-hour inhalation of 3% Origanum majorana oil versus almond oil gave no clear between-group state anxiety or anxiety visual analog scale difference. The separate stress VAS changed by −0.67 versus −0.16 (p = .026). The exposure differs in species, route and concentration from dried common oregano.
