Title: Omega-3 Supplementation Improves Isometric Strength But Not Muscle Anabolic and Catabolic Signaling in Response to Resistance Exercise in Healthy Older Adults
Publication date: 2020 (online publication; 2021 issue)
PMID: 33284965
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33284965/
Text coverage: Original publication abstract and verified prior P233 conference comparison.

Twenty-three older adults received omega-3 or corn oil during a resistance-training trial. The final report gives isometric knee extensor changes of +12.2% versus −1.4% (arm interaction p=.015). The conference report gives −0.3% in the corn-oil arm. Dynamic leg press strength improved across conditions. The different reports share an underlying trial. The final paper contrasts oils during a concurrent training program in healthy older adults and separately measures strength, without a rigidity, gait or Parkinson outcome.
