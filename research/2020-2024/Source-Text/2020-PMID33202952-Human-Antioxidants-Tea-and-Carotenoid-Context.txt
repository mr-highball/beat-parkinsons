Original study scope
The review separates tea epidemiology, circulating carotenoids and isolated polyphenols across conditions. Black tea is discussed in relation to risk of diagnosis and not a bradykinesia treatment outcome. Circulating lycopene, curcumin and resveratrol do not establish a culinary intervention.

Continuing evidence gaps
Find exact preparation-specific human symptom endpoints independent of disease risk or nutritional biomarkers.
