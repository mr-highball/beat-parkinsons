POLYAGE registry and distinction between registered and reported outcomes
https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04348162

First posted April 16, 2020 and updated September 16, 2021. The completed ClinicalTrials.gov record reports 90 enrolled and lists cocoa flavanols, berry anthocyanins, their combination and a control. Brachial flow-mediated dilation is primary, with Stroop and Tower of London change listed secondarily. Anxiety is absent from the displayed outcomes. The 2026 anxiety article reports 59 people in three active arms. The record does not explain that difference, and has no posted registry results.
