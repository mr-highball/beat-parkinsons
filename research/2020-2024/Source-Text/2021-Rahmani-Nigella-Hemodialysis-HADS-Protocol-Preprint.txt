Title: Effect of nigella sativa oil supplementation on kidney function tests, glycemic parameters, oxidative stress, inflammation, quality of life and depression status in diabetic hemodialysis patients
Source: https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-61604/v1

Source depth: Full publisher-rendered protocol text.

The protocol plans a randomized oil versus paraffin comparison in diabetic adults undergoing hemodialysis. The planned sample is 46 and the original protocol identifies HADS among its measured instruments. The matching clinical report describes 20 and 21 analyzed in the metabolic outcomes report. No analyzed HADS denominator or arm result is reported in the inspected sources. The shared trial identifier, setting, intervention and protocol establish cohort linkage. The separate public versions of the protocol do not increase the number of trials.
