Shayani Rad et al., first posted 2022-11-05, PMID36334223, DOI 10.1007/s10787-022-01083-y. Factual extract from the primary abstract https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10787-022-01083-y.
Randomized crossover in 50 adults with hypertension, 1.34 g/day celery seed extract in addition to usual medication. The abstract reports improvement on a dizziness item among several Beck anxiety inventory items (p < .001), without giving a clear between-arm estimate, item values or orthostatic context. Full tables require access.
The earlier IRCT20130418013058N8 registration also identifies this cohort. The intervention was seed extract, and Parkinson disease and dried leaf were outside the compared groups.
