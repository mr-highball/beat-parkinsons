Original Rezaeyan abstract and study allocation
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33957160/
PMID 33957160

First online May 3, 2021. The original abstract describes 25 adults with post-traumatic olfactory impairment, allocated nine to no training, nine to fixed training and seven to rotating training for 16 weeks. The trained groups reportedly improved on smell testing, particularly identification. A later scoping review attributes cardamom, saffron and cinnamon to the rotating regimen. The original abstract omits individual odors, and two reviews differ on the first-month thyme attribution. This evidence does not isolate an ingredient’s contribution or evaluate culinary use in Parkinson’s.
