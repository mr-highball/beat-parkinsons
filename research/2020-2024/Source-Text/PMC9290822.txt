Title: Bergamot rehabilitation against agitation in dementia (BRAINAID)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9290822/
PMID: 34435395
PMCID: PMC9290822
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.7223
First published: 2021-08-26
Coverage: Distilled original methods and results or protocol fields, with article identity checked against PubMed.

The prospective protocol compares a topical cream containing defurocoumarinized bergamot oil against matching empty cream in severe dementia. Agitation is primary and the MOBID-2 pain assessment is secondary. The subsequent pilot attributes this protocol to NCT04321889. Published protocol dates and study visits are timing information. No date-palm exposure is specified.
