Factual extract from original Methods and Tables 2–3.

A Swiss cross-sectional self-report survey classified pregnant participants by the presence of mental disorders or symptoms (MDS). Table 3 counts raspberry-leaf tea use in 150/329 respondents overall, including 76/172 with MDS and 74/157 without MDS (p = .552). The table concerns prevalence of use. Table 2 separately reports group counts for pharmaceutical raspberry-leaf preparations. Neither table estimates change in a separately scored anxiety outcome attributable to raspberry-leaf tea. The frozen fruit and Parkinson population are outside this study.
