Title: Meat and mental health: A meta-analysis of meat consumption, depression, and anxiety.
PMID: 34612096
DOI: 10.1080/10408398.2021.1974336
First published: 2023
Authors: Dobersek U, Teel K, Altmeyer S, Adkins J, Wy G, Peak J
Journal: Critical reviews in food science and nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34612096/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 20 observational studies (n=171,802 participants): meat consumers had lower depression (Hedges g=0.216, p<0.001) and lower anxiety (g=0.17, p=0.02) compared with meat abstainers, with stronger effects in higher-rigor studies. Limits: observational, non-PD, pooled meat vs abstention, confounding/reverse causality possible.
