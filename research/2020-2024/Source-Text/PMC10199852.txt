Title: Sex differences in dementia with Lewy bodies: an imaging study of neurotransmission pathways
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10199852/
PMID: 36826477
PMCID: PMC10199852
DOI: 10.1007/s00259-023-06132-4
Publication date: 2023-02-24
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The imaging study compares subregional dopaminergic findings in 123 probable dementia with Lewy bodies participants and 78 controls. Imaging outcomes and clinical phenotype by sex are separate from a culinary exposure and a visual episode-frequency outcome.
