The S69 Nascimento abstract reviews six drooling instruments for people with Parkinson disease. It identifies ROMP-Saliva as the only candidate classified as recommended under the applied MDS criteria and identifies open measurement-property gaps. The matching five-author journal publication gives the same six instruments and recommendation. Treat both reports as one review family.

Other pages include separate work on salivary substitutes, swallowing and airway rehabilitation. These studies have distinct populations and measurements. The supplement supplies no raw-fig Parkinson drooling comparison.
