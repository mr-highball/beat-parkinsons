Title: 56-year-old man - increased heart rate - weakness - intense sweating - horseradish consumption - Dx?
PMID: 36179141
DOI: 10.12788/jfp.0476
First published: 2022
Authors: Lehmann CU, Puckett AL, Betz BO
Journal: The Journal of Family Practice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36179141/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Clinical case report diagnosing horseradish-induced vasovagal syncope (historically known as 'Seder syncope') in a 56-year-old man who developed epigastric burning, tachycardia/weakness, intense diaphoresis, and brief syncope shortly after consuming pungent horseradish. Attributed to potent mucosal TRPA1/vagal afferent reflex stimulation by isothiocyanates. Uncontrolled single non-PD case report, directly counterbalancing the small supine DBP rise reported in Gregersen 2013.
