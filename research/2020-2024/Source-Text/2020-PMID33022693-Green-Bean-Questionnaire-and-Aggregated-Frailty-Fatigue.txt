Original source scope
The cohort's food-frequency validation names string beans. The body reports lower incidence of the fatigue component for total vegetables, a distinct outcome from the frailty composite. The comparison of five or more versus less than two daily vegetable servings reports HR 0.83 (95% CI 0.78–0.88).

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
