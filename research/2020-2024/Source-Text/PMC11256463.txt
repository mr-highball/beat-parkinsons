Title: Assessing serum anti-nuclear antibodies HEp-2 patterns in synucleinopathies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11256463/
PMID: 39026277
PMCID: PMC11256463
DOI: 10.1186/s12979-024-00453-0
Publication date: 2024-07-18
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The investigators compare HEp-2 autoantibody patterns in samples from Parkinson disease, multiple system atrophy and dementia with Lewy bodies. The serum or plasma immunofluorescence patterns do not measure oral fig exposure or count hallucinations after food.
