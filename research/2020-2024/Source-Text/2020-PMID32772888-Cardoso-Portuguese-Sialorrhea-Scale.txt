Title: Cross-cultural adaptation and validation of the Swallowing Disturbance Questionnaire and the Sialorrhea Clinical Scale in Portuguese patients with Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32772888/
PMID: 32772888
DOI: 10.1080/14015439.2020.1792979
Publication date: 2020-08-10
Date basis: article date in the PubMed and Europe PMC records. The journal issue appeared in December 2021.
Source depth: original author abstract and original translated forms. The validation tables remain unavailable.

Cardoso and colleagues adapted two distinct questionnaires in European Portuguese. The validation involved 75 people with Parkinson disease and 65 matched comparison participants. The author abstract reports internal consistency of 0.85 and an AUC of 0.704 for the translated Sialorrhea Clinical Scale, with a floor effect. The swallowing questionnaire has separate estimates and domains.

The institution's published sialorrhea form distinguishes daytime accumulation, overnight saliva, saliva escape and effects on function. The publisher-linked Figshare annex contains the separate swallowing questionnaire, including solid-food chewing examples. These are assessment items and do not describe a tested raw-fig exposure. The original institutional article file is listed publicly and its download requires authorization.
