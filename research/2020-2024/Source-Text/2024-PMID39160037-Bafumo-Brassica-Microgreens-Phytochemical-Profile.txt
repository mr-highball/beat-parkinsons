Title: Optimal Brassicaceae family microgreens from a phytochemical and sensory perspective.
PMID: 39160037
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2024.114812
First published: 2024
Authors: Bafumo RF, Alloggia FP, Ramirez DA, Maza MA, Fontana A, Moreno DA et al.
Journal: Food Res Int
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39160037/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Comparative HPLC-MS/MS study of 14 Brassica microgreens showing cauliflower microgreens ranked highest in ascorbic acid and total phenolics with highest sensory acceptance, whereas broccoli and radish microgreens yielded the highest isothiocyanate levels. Confirms distinct phytochemical profile of cauliflower vs broccoli.
