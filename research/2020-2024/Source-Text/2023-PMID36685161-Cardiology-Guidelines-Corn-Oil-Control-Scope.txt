Original study scope
The full guideline cites the STRENGTH trial, which compared a fatty-acid formulation with corn oil in a cardiovascular population. Its reported primary outcome is cardiovascular, and dates are temporal. The comparison does not measure pain after refined culinary corn oil.

Continuing evidence gaps
Use the original trial for the comparator, conditions and measured endpoints. No frozen pain outcome can be attributed to the control oil.
