Title: The role of olive oil and its constituents in mental health: a scoping review
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39696776/
PMID: 39696776
PMCID: PMC12240357
DOI: 10.1017/S000711452400299X
Publication date: 2024-12-19
Date basis: First public article indexed ahead of print.
Text coverage: Distilled author abstract and indexed reference trail. The full included-study audit remains open.
Accessed: 2026-11-26

The review included 49 studies across human experimental, observational and animal designs, including studies that used olive oil as a comparator. The reported observational directions varied: higher rates of mental illness in five reports, lower rates in eight, and no association in five. The aggregate favorable experimental description covers anxiety or depression, so separate primary endpoints, study families, and each oil exposure need verification. Its indexed references include the human acute typing-stress imaging study PMID 33841804.
