Title: Efficacy, acceptability, and tolerability of all available treatments for insomnia in the elderly: a systematic review and network meta-analysis.
PMID: 32521042
DOI: 10.1111/acps.13201
First published: 2020
Authors: Samara MT, Huhn M, Chiocchia V, Schneider-Thoma J, Wiegand M, Salanti G, Leucht S
Journal: Acta psychiatrica Scandinavica
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32521042/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Systematic review and network meta-analysis (Samara et al., 2020, Acta Psychiatr Scand) of 53 RCTs (n=6,832 older adults >65 years, mean age 75) evaluating pharmacological and non-pharmacological insomnia treatments. Overall confidence was very low due to small per-comparison samples and sparse network connectivity; among disconnected non-pharmacological pairwise comparisons, limited evidence suggested tart cherry juice outperformed control alongside acupressure, auricular acupuncture, and mindfulness-based stress reduction, with insufficient overall evidence to establish comparative superiority. Context-only review; does not isolate whole dried cherry or Parkinson insomnia.
