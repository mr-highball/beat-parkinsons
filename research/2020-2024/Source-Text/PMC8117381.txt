Title: Use of Antipsychotics in Patients with Behavioral and Psychological Symptoms of Dementia: Results of a Spanish Delphi Consensus
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8117381/
PMID: 33176326
PMCID: PMC8117381
DOI: 10.1159/000510866
Publication date: 2020-11-11
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PubMed.
Design: Clinical Delphi consensus about antipsychotic use in dementia

The work reports expert judgments on behavioral and psychological symptoms and antipsychotic treatment. Visual hallucination is a clinical factor in treatment selection. The consensus gives no observed food-associated symptom change.
