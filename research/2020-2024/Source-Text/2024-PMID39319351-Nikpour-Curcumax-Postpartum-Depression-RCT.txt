Title: The effect of curcumax on postpartum women's depression: a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 39319351
PMCID: PMC11420044
DOI: 10.3389/fpsyt.2024.1302174
First published: 2024
Authors: Nikpour F, Ansari S, Abedi P, Jahanfar S, Sharifat N, Hooshmand G
Journal: Frontiers in psychiatry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39319351/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

8-week randomized placebo-controlled trial (PMC11420044, IRCT20210822052254N1, n=124 postpartum women) of Curcumax (ginger + turmeric + black pepper) vs placebo. Lowered Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale scores at weeks 4 and 8 (p<0.0001). Bundled 3-spice capsule; cannot attribute to black pepper alone.
