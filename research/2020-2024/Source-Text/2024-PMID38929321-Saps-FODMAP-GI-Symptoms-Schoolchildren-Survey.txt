Title: Gastrointestinal Symptoms and Fermentable Oligosaccharides, Disaccharides, Monosaccharides, and Polyols Sources in Schoolchildren-A Pilot Study.
PMID: 38929321
PMCID: PMC11201681
DOI: 10.3390/children11060742
First published: 2024
Authors: Saps M, Velasco-Benitez CA, Velasco-Suarez DA, Alvarez-Baumgartner M, Balda AN, Arrizabalo S
Journal: Children (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38929321/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional pediatric questionnaire survey (Saps et al., 2024, Children, PMC11201681) of 208 schoolchildren aged 8-18 years rating perceived gastrointestinal symptom triggers across 20 FODMAP-rich foods. Overall 38.0% reported GI symptoms (abdominal pain 33%, distension 24%, nausea 23%); black beans were cited by 11% as a perceived FODMAP trigger (behind milk 12%). Limits: cross-sectional pediatric self-report of abdominal intolerance without constipation scoring, preparation detail, or adult/Parkinson's cohorts.
