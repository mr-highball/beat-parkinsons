Intranasal white tea extract and odor tasks in stressed mice
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7875552/

December 15, 2020. Hu and colleagues tested intranasal white tea extract in a chronic unpredictable mild stress mouse model. Figure 3 used 10 mice per group and assessed buried-food latency, odor investigation and aversive-odor avoidance. The authors report that high-dose latency was around 130 seconds compared with 280 in the stressed control group and that treated mice improved on other odor measures. The extract contained a high EGCG fraction by the paper's analysis. White tea processing, intranasal delivery and stress-induced odor impairment distinguish this positive model comparison from ingested brewed green tea and Parkinson olfactory impairment.
