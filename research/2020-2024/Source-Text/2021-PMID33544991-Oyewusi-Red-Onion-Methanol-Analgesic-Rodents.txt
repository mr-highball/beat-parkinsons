Title: Anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects of methanol extract of red cultivar Allium cepa bulbs in rats and mice.
PMID: 33544991
DOI: 10.1515/jbcpp-2020-0080
First published: 2021
Authors: Oyewusi AJ, Oridupa OA, Saba AB, Oyewusi IK, Olukunle JO
Journal: Journal of Basic and Clinical Physiology and Pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33544991/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Preclinical rat and mouse analgesia assays: oral methanol extract of Nigerian red cultivar Allium cepa bulb (200-800 mg/kg) inhibited carrageenan paw edema, early-phase formalin paw-licking, and significantly prolonged hot-plate (p < 0.01) and hot-water tail-flick (p < 0.05) thermal pain reaction times. Concentrated methanol extract in rodents; opposite direction to human IBS FODMAP visceral pain triggers.
