Primary-study factual extract for PMID32875616. Lüth et al. Age at onset of LRRK2 p.Gly2019Ser is related to environmental and lifestyle factors. Mov Disord. 2020;35(10). DOI 10.1002/mds.28238. https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mds.28238.
Median onset in affected LRRK2 carriers who drank black tea was 58 years (IQR 48–67) versus 52 (46.5–57), p=.0024. Among idiopathic PD participants, onset was 57 versus 49 years, p=.0034. The clinical-presentation section compares scales for tobacco use. The published black tea comparison addresses age at onset and gives no effect estimate for walking or tremor severity. Brewing variables were not analyzed.
