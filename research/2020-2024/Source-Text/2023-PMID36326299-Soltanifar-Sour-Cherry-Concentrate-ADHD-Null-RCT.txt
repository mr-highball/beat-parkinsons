Title: The Effect of Adding Sour Cherry Concentrate to The Usual Treatment of Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder In 6 To 12 Years Old Children.
PMID: 36326299
DOI: 10.1177/10870547221129307
First published: 2023
Authors: Soltanifar A, Moharreri F, Bakhtiari E, Hosseinpour A
Journal: Journal of attention disorders
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36326299/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized clinical trial (Soltanifar et al., 2023, J Atten Disord) in 70 children (ages 6-12) with ADHD comparing 75 mL twice-daily sour cherry concentrate added to routine treatment vs routine treatment alone for 2 weeks. No statistically significant between-group differences occurred on Conner Parent Rating Scale cognitive impairments (p=0.317), hyperactivity/impulsivity (p=0.525), oppositional behavior (p=0.986), or ADHD index (p=0.451). Adverse events in the sour cherry arm included abdominal pain (28.6%) and asthenia (31.4%). Competing null attention/behavioral trial with GI tolerability signal in children.
