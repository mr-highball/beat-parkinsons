Original factual extract from the primary abstract, preview, outcome figure and supplement

The study compared green, semi-fermented and fully fermented tea extracts from Hadong. The Introduction identifies fully fermented tea as black tea. The available Methods describe powdered leaves extracted in distilled water at a reported 1:10 ratio at 121 °C for up to 15 min and subsequently freeze-dried. The abstract's 25 μg/mL measurements refer to cell-free assays. Figure S1 shows all three tea extracts at 100 mg/kg/day administered orally over four weeks following model induction. The diagram also shows untreated and model controls and a levodopa comparator. The tea branches specify an oral route, while the overall intervention period is generically labeled as a sample injection.

Original Fig. 4A gives a seconds-valued motion test. Approximate bar readings are 300 s untreated control, 55 s MPTP, 330 s levodopa, 340 s green tea, 320 s semi-fermented tea and 300 s fully fermented tea. MPTP alone is labeled b. Each other bar is labeled a. The motion-test figure therefore shows improvement for the fully fermented extract arm, along with the semi-fermented and green arms. The abstract refers generally to fermented tea, whereas the Highlights specifically credit semi-fermented tea. The graph gives no basis for a unique semi-fermented result on that test. The figure only labels the outcome Motion test (s). The apparatus and full test protocol are unavailable in the recovered materials.

Supplement Table S1 gives theanine 1171.80 ± 19.96 (a), 1183.02 ± 45.18 (a) and 638.90 ± 6.35 (b), and GABA 27.96 ± 0.52 (c), 93.95 ± 1.15 (a) and 39.42 ± 0.12 (b) for green, semi-fermented and fully fermented tea, respectively. Values are triplicate means ± SD. Different letters indicate p < .05. The table does not list units. The figure and supplement offer no direct evidence for walking velocity or rest tremor. The October 2023 P08-370 conference abstract repeats the three Hadong categories and several outputs without separating behavioral arms.
