Title: A multicentre study on grey matter morphometric biomarkers for classifying early schizophrenia and parkinson's disease psychosis

The multicenter classification study compares structural imaging across Parkinson psychosis and schizophrenia categories. Hallucinations contribute to broad clinical grouping; no dried fig ingestion or timed visual outcomes are measured.
