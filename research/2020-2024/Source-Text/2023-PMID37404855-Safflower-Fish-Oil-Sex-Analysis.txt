Title: Sex-specific responses in glucose-insulin homeostasis and lipoprotein-lipid components after high-dose supplementation with marine n-3 PUFAs in abdominal obesity: a randomized double-blind crossover study
Publication date: 2023-06-19
PMID: 37404855
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2023.1020678
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37404855/
Text coverage: Distilled author abstract of the linked secondary analysis.

The study reports the same 16/23 sample, seven-week periods and safflower versus fish-oil regimens as PMID 33933722. It evaluates sex-specific changes in lipoproteins and indicators of glycemic regulation. The abstract describes different female and male glucose-insulin responses after fish oil, without evidence of the same sex-specific effect after safflower oil. This is a secondary analysis of NCT02647333 and supplies no new independent symptom observation.
