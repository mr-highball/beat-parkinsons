EPO-049 compares resting EEG microstates in 19 Parkinson patients with hallucinations and 20 matched patients. EPO-553 compares task-evoked EEG in 23 Parkinson patients with minor hallucinations and 23 without. Resting microstate transitions and evoked processing are different measures. The posters contain no assigned or measured dried fig intake.

EPO-049 reports differences in some microstate transitions with broadly similar duration, occurrence and coverage. EPO-553 studies time-dependent visual categorization and related EEG responses. Each is a complete released meeting abstract rather than a separate full underlying article.
