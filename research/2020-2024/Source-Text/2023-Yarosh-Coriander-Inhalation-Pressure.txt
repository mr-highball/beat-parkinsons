2023 — Ten- and twenty-minute coriander oil inhalation
Source: https://www.mediasphera.ru/issues/voprosy-kurortologii-fizioterapii-i-lechebnoj-fizicheskoj-kultury/2023/2/1004287872023021022
Coverage: Factual extract of primary Tables 1 and 4.

After 10 minutes of relaxation, the vapor group changed from 156.04 to 141.46 mm Hg systolic (n = 26). Relaxation alone changed from 155.26 to 152.00 (n = 42). The reported between-group p = .0006. The 20-minute arm contrast was inconclusive (p = .13). The study has no oral or postural protocol.
