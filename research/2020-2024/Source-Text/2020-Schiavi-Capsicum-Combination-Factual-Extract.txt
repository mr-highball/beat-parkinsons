Original source https://www.clinicsinsurgery.com/open-access/efficacy-and-safety-of-integrative-and-complementary-therapy-ict-based-7051.pdf

Published December 14, 2020. In an uncontrolled 12-week study, 238 women with overactive bladder completed treatment with a mixture containing pepper extract, pumpkin seed extract, vitamins, glutamine and resveratrol. The mixture supplied 10 mg pepper extract per tablet, with two tablets daily. Three-day diary urgent voids changed from 4.71 ± 1.28 to 2.53 ± 1.22 per day (reported within-group p < .0001). Neurogenic bladder or neurological abnormality was excluded. There was no comparison group or pepper-only arm. The table's separately printed change in overall voids does not agree with its displayed means.
