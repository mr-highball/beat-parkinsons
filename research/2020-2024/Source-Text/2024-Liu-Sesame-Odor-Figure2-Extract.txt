Sesame oil used to measure odor identification

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11219208/
Coverage: Original Methods and section 3.3 and Figure 2
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

Published June 25, 2024. Among 36 participants with Parkinson disease and seven with multiple system atrophy, correct identification of the smelled sesame oil item was 44.4% and 100%, respectively (p=.010). Group totals imply 16/36 and 7/7 if there was no missing item data. The oil was a diagnostic stimulus, with no oral intake, cold-press specification or treatment effect.
