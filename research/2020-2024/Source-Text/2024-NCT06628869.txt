Title: Fructanase crossover with oatmeal in both periods
First published or posted: 2024-10-08
Registration: NCT06628869
Source URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06628869
Text type: original-source evidence summary

First posted October 8, 2024. Thirty healthy adults completed the randomized crossover. The protocol compares fructanase and placebo during an inulin challenge. PMID 42580594 confirms the oatmeal challenge was shared by both periods. No separate registry results were posted at the November 17, 2026 check. The 2026 journal paper reports the sleep measure and belongs to the same trial.
