Design: Clinical hearing and cognition comparison

The study examined 112 participants with cognitive impairment and subjective hearing loss, comparing hearing classification with cognitive tests, MRI and amyloid PET. The sensory phenotype is auditory. No edible fig contrast or timed visual hallucination outcome appears.
