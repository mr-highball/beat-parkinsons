Title: Effect of Date Fruit Consumption on the Glycemic Control of Patients with Type 2 Diabetes: A Randomized Clinical Trial.
PMID: 36079749
PMCID: PMC9458144
DOI: 10.3390/nu14173491
First published: 2022
Authors: Butler AE, Obaid J, Wasif P, Varghese JV, Abdulrahman R, Alromaihi D et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9458144/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled trial (Butler et al., 2022, PMC9458144, n=61 completed adults with type 2 diabetes) comparing 60 g/day whole date fruit versus raisins for 12 weeks. Seated systolic and diastolic blood pressure did not differ significantly between groups or from baseline (p>0.05), alongside neutral glycemic control. Direct whole-date human RCT showing null resting BP; zero orthostatic standing BP or Parkinson lightheadedness endpoints.
