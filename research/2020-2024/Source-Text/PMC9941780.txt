Title: Donepezil-Induced Complex Multimodal Hallucinations: Two Cases and a Review of the Literature
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9941780/
PMID: 36824578
PMCID: PMC9941780
DOI: 10.1159/000526159
Publication date: 2022-09-19
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

Two original cases report complex hallucinations around donepezil exposure. One describes cessation after withdrawal, and the other recurrence after re-exposure. These are medication time courses in dementia with no dried fig contrast or Parkinson visual episode assessment.

Case 1 describes complex visual experiences emerging after the drug and stopping after withdrawal. Case 2 describes both visual and auditory experiences, resolution on withdrawal and recurrence on re-exposure. These individual clinical courses do not provide a Parkinson food-frequency comparison.
