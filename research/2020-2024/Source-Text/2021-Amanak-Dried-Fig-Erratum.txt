Amanak — Erratum to Effects of Dry Figs on Primary Dysmenorrhea Symptoms, Perceived Stress Levels and the Quality of Life
Publisher document: https://prhsj.rcm.upr.edu/index.php/prhsj/article/download/2660/1374/9955
Publisher record: https://prhsj.rcm.upr.edu/index.php/prhsj/article/view/2660
Published: 2021-03-29. P R Health Sci J. 2021;40(1):59. PMID 33876922.
Coverage: Factual paraphrase of the correction and primary Figure 1 verification.

The erratum corrects participant counts in the original English and Spanish abstracts and Methods. The reported analysis population was 99 rather than 115 from the original abstract. The corrected allocation was 107 students total: placebo 36, cinnamon 36, dried fig 35. The analysis denominators were placebo 32, cinnamon 34, dried fig 33. Exclusions before randomization numbered 123 and the chronic-disease exclusion subtotal was 21. The corrected counts agree with the original Figure 1, which also records postallocation exclusions of four, two and two by arm. The one-page correction identifies no change to outcome results or food exposure. The corrected text supplies no Parkinson or hallucination assessment.
