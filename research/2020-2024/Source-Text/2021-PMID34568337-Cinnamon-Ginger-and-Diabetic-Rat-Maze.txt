Original study scope
Six animals per arm received cinnamon or ginger aqueous extract alone or together in a streptozotocin-diabetes model. Combined extracts increased open-arm duration from 11.99 to 17.80 seconds versus untreated diabetic rats, p=0.004. The separate arms rose without significant comparisons. The combined arm also decreased closed-arm entries, a measure that can depend on activity.

Continuing evidence gaps
The individual arms and combination must remain separate. The rodents had diabetes rather than parkinsonism, the exposure was gavaged extract, and plus-maze behavior differs from clinical anxiety. The combination does not isolate either culinary ingredient.
