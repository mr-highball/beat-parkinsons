Title: Interindividual variability in response to protein and fish oil supplementation in older adults
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8977999/
PMID: 35191215
PMCID: PMC8977999
DOI: 10.1002/jcsm.12936
Publication date: 2022-02-21
Registry identifier: NCT03429491
Text coverage: Distilled full secondary analysis from the original randomized NUTRIMAL family.

The analysis considers how individual changes in appendicular lean mass, strength, physical performance and triglycerides vary across the same three assigned groups as the 2021 primary trial. Authors report similar proportions of participants across estimated categories of clinically meaningful improvement and worsening. The analysis shares the parent trial and its high-oleic sunflower and corn oil comparator. It does not provide an isolated corn-oil or prospective falls estimate.
