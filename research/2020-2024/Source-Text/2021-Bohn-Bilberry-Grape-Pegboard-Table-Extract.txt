Bilberry and grape juice with pegboard testing

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8607697/
Coverage: Original Methods and Table 2
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published November 22, 2021. Sixty older men with subjective memory complaints entered a randomized comparison of bilberry and red grape juice with placebo for nine weeks. The dominant-hand Grooved Pegboard change comparison gave p=.41. The mixed juice and non-Parkinson population leave raw grape dexterity unresolved. The null estimate does not establish an opposing direction.
