Title: Head Drop and Trunk Flexion as an Early Manifestation of Anti-IgLON5 Disease.
PMID: 36704078
PMCID: PMC9847274
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13575
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9847274/
First publication date: 2022-10-18 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: Official PMC complete article page, including body and displayed study observations.

The case follows posture and gait changes, later cognitive symptoms and visual hallucinations in anti-IgLON5 disease. The diagnostic workup provides a parkinsonism differential. There is no dried fig exposure or food-linked visual hallucination frequency measurement.
