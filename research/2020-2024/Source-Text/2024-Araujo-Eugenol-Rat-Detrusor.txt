Eugenol and isolated rat bladder contraction
PMID: 39765337. PMCID: PMC11710953. DOI: 10.5213/inj.2448326.163.
Source: https://www.einj.org/upload/pdf/inj-2448326-163.pdf
Coverage: original factual Methods and Results.

Published December 31, 2024. Araújo and colleagues found concentration-dependent inhibition of phasic and tonic contractions in isolated rat detrusor strips. The paper reports complete suppression at the highest tested 3 mM bath concentration for the potassium and carbachol stimuli. The laboratory comparison uses isolated eugenol and measures muscle force. Urinary urgency episodes and ground clove intake were not measured.
