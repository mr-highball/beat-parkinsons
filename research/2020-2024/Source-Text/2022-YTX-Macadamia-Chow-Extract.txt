Factual extract: Figure 5/Table 1 describes six male rats per group, 14 days on custom food followed by 15 days on food and once-daily oral YTX-7739 or vehicle. Macadamia food contained 0.21 g macadamia oil and 0.029 g soybean oil per g total food. The matched high-fat comparator used lard plus soybean oil, not macadamia oil. Drug or vehicle was suspended in 0.5% methylcellulose and administered by gavage; oil was in chow, with free food access. Food palmitoleic acid was 58 times standard chow. Tissue and plasma C16 desaturation were the readouts. The Results report attenuation at 10 mg/kg by 89% in plasma, 61% in brain and 74% in skin, relative to the drug response on standard chow; at 30 mg/kg peripheral attenuation was approximately 50% and brain difference was not significant. The paper does not establish a cold-pressed grade, the amount each rat ate, Parkinson patients, levodopa treatment or a dyskinesia severity outcome. PD in its figure label means pharmacodynamic, not a Parkinson disease outcome.
