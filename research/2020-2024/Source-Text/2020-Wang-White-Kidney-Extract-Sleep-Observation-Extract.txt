Incidental sleep observations in a white bean extract trial
February 5, 2020 · PMID 32180941 · PMC7063375 · DOI 10.1002/fsn3.1299
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7063375/
Factual extract from original Methods and Results and scope of later corrigendum.

Wang et al. randomized 120 adults with obesity to white kidney bean seed extract capsules or maltodextrin placebo for 35 days. Analysis comprised 56 and 58 participants, respectively. The Results state that no sleep abnormalities were observed in either arm, without a sleep instrument, ascertainment schedule, baseline insomnia information or separate event counts. This is an incidental observation using extract in a different population. The September 2020 corrigendum concerns the plant-origin discussion and leaves the sleep observation as printed.
