Original study scope
The three-case report describes complex care for chronic Lyme disease or post-treatment symptoms and coexisting conditions. Cinnamon, clove and oregano occur in a combined oil product. Cranberry extract was one of several agents discussed in a different care setting. Turmeric and broccoli-derived preparations appear as concurrent supplements or mechanistic discussion. Shrimp paste is on a dietary restriction list. Dates refer to chronology.

Continuing evidence gaps
Case-level changes cannot isolate any individual culinary ingredient or separate a Parkinson symptom effect. The shrimp list provides no boiled-shrimp consumption contrast or bowel outcome.
