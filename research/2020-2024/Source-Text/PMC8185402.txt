Design: Experimental retinal imaging in an Alzheimer model

The investigators used injected fluorescent ARCAM-1 to assist visualization of amyloid-containing retinal deposits in APP/PS1 mice and controls. The imaging signal measures deposits. It supplies no observation of culinary fig intake or human visual hallucinations.
