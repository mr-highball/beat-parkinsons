Title: Two apples a day lower serum cholesterol and improve cardiometabolic biomarkers in mildly hypercholesterolemic adults: a randomized, controlled, crossover trial.
PMID: 31840162
PMCID: PMC6997084
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/nqz282
First published: 2020
Authors: Koutsos A, Riccadonna S, Ulaszewska MM, Franceschi P, Trošt K, Galvin A et al.
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31840162/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled crossover trial in n=40 mildly hypercholesterolemic adults (23 women, 17 men; mean age 51 y) consuming 2 whole Renetta Canada apples/day vs sugar- and energy-matched control beverage for 8 weeks each (4-week washout). Whole apples lowered total and LDL cholesterol and ICAM-1 and improved microvascular vasodilation, with no blood pressure difference and no PD symptom endpoints.
