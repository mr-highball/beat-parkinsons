Title: Topography of cortical thinning in the Lewy body diseases
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7016450/
PMID: 32059167
PMCID: PMC7016450
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2020.102196
Publication date: 2020-01-31
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: Cortical imaging and clinical comparison

The analysis compares 21 participants with dementia with Lewy bodies, 16 with Parkinson cognitive impairment, and 24 cognitively normal Parkinson participants. MRI cortical thickness and amyloid PET are the measured contrasts. Clinical visual features are not timed against food ingestion.
