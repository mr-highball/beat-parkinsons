Title: Bedtime banana and milk intake on sleep and biochemical parameters.
PMID: 39209357
PMCID: PMC11389809
DOI: 10.6133/apjcn.202412_33(4).0002
First published: 2024
Authors: Keser MG, Yuksel A, Ekmekci AH, Vatansev H
Journal: Asia Pacific journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39209357/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-arm parallel controlled clinical trial (PMC11389809) in n=21 patients with primary insomnia allocated to 1 portion bedtime banana, 200 mL whole-fat bedtime milk, or habitual control. Sleep was evaluated via Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) and polysomnography (PSG) alongside serum BDNF, leptin, and ghrelin. PSQI scores significantly decreased after intervention in both the banana and milk groups (p<0.05). On PSG, total sleep time increased significantly from baseline in the milk group only (not reported significant for banana), and serum ghrelin fell in the milk group. Limits: small total sample (n=21 across 3 arms), open-label snack, non-baked raw banana, non-PD primary insomnia.
