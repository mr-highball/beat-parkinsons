Title: Phytochemical Composition and Health Benefits of Figs (Fresh and Dried): A Review of Literature from 2000 to 2022
PMID: 37299587
PMCID: PMC10255635
DOI: 10.3390/nu15112623
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10255635/
First publication date: 2023-06-03
Coverage: Complete review JATS, including search methods, tables and the cognitive evidence trail.

The review separates cultivar, fruit part and processing in its chemistry synthesis. Its cognitive evidence table points to the Subash transgenic Alzheimer-model mouse study using a fig-supplemented diet. This is a citation to an existing experiment, not an independent cognitive comparison. The reported outcomes concern memory, learning, motor coordination and anxiety-related behavior. For preparation and animal-level data, inspect the original methods and clarify overlap with the separate fig biomarker report. The review does not report a direct human Parkinson visual-hallucination comparison.
