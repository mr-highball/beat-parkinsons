Source: https://annexpublishers.co/full-text/JNH/10104/Efficacy-of-Standard.php
Published: 2023-08-29
Format: Original methods and results, paraphrased.
Design: Randomized double-blind comparison in 60 adults with occasional constipation, 56 days. Twenty-nine ginger and 26 placebo participants completed. The paper reports CTRI/2022/01/039843.
Outcome: PAC-SYM and PAC-QoL favored the extract at days 28 and 56 and stool frequency favored it on days 14, 28 and 56. Stool consistency was inconclusive at each visit.
Limits: The manufacturer's 280 mg capsule is described as partly cellulose in Methods. The paper's extract and gingerol amounts are inconsistent. The study was manufacturer funded and used neither fresh grated root nor a Parkinson population.
