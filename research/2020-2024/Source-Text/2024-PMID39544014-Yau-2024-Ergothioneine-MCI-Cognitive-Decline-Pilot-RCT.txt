Title: Investigating the efficacy of ergothioneine to delay cognitive decline in mild cognitively impaired subjects: A pilot study.
PMID: 39544014
DOI: 10.1177/13872877241291253
Registry ID: NCT03641404
First published: 2024
Authors: Yau YF, Cheah IK, Mahendran R, Tang RM, Chua RY, Goh RE
Journal: Journal of Alzheimer's disease : JAD
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39544014/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

One-year double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled pilot trial (NCT03641404) in n=19 older adults (>=60 y) with mild cognitive impairment taking 25 mg ergothioneine 3x/week vs placebo. Ergothioneine improved Rey Auditory Verbal Learning Test (RAVLT) learning ability and stabilized plasma neurofilament light chain (NfL), whereas placebo showed rising NfL and no cognitive improvement, with normal safety labs. Limits: very small pilot (n=19), isolated ergothioneine capsules, verbal learning rather than PD attention/executive outcomes.
