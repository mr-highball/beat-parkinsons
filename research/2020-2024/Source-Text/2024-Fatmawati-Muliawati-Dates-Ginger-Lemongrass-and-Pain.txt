Title: Ginger, lemongrass and date drink and menstrual pain
Original title: Efektivitas Rebusan Jahe Sereh Kurma Dalam Menurunkan Derajat Nyeri Dismenore
Source: https://jurnalmadanimedika.ac.id/JMM/article/view/356
Original PDF: https://jurnalmadanimedika.ac.id/JMM/article/download/356/198/
DOI: 10.36569/jmm.v14i02.356
Publication date: 2024-01-10
Date basis: Article-specific publication date. Its issue is dated December 2023 and the issue listing is dated 2023-12-31.
Citation: Fatmawati and Muliawati. Jurnal Kesehatan Madani Medika 14(2), 230-235.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: Original Indonesian methods and Tables 1-3.

Twenty students with primary dysmenorrhea received a drink blending dates with a boiled ginger and lemongrass preparation. The design used one group measured before and after delivery of the mixture. Table 2 records 14 of 20 with moderate or severe pain before and 3 of 20 afterward. The group table gives 3 without pain, 14 mild and 3 moderate afterward. The authors report a Wilcoxon statistic Z = -4.008, with p displayed as .000. The drying status of the dates is unreported. Exposure to ginger or date fruit was not varied independently and timing of the normal cycle may influence the result. This study does not measure pain after solely consuming grated ginger or dried date fruit in people with Parkinson disease.
