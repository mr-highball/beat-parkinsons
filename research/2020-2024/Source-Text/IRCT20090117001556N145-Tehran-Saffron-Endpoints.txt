Tehran registry and the accessible motor total
Primary source: https://irct.ir/trial/65247
Coverage: factual extract of the registry and accessible publication.

The official IRCT20090117001556N145 record <https://irct.ir/trial/65247> describes an eight-week, double-blind trial in PD with mild-to-moderate depression: 15 mg saffron capsules twice daily plus stable usual PD therapy versus placebo. Its primary registration names Hamilton Depression Rating Scale and MDS-UPDRS Part III at weeks 0, 4 and 8; the secondary-outcomes section is empty. The registry does not identify a Sargol grade or confirm a culinary ground-stigma preparation. It is already cataloged at `research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md#iranian-registration-of-the-tehran-saffron-capsule-trial`.

The related 2024 Lalezari publication, DOI 10.1016/j.hermed.2024.100968, reports 52 participants and a composite Part III interaction p=.424. It also lists other depression scales and Hoehn–Yahr as secondary measures in the abstract, despite the empty registry secondary section. No individual 3.1 speech or other item results are accessible in the abstract. Part III speech evaluates volume as well as other characteristics, so even an item score would not isolate voice volume; the total result is weaker still. The registration belongs to the same trial as the article.
