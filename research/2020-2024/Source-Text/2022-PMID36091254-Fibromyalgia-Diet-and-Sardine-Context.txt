Title: Fibromyalgia trial of a combined dietary program including fish advice
Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2022.856216/full
PMID: 36091254
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2022.856216
Publication date: 2022-08-15
Coverage: Original results, outcomes and linked official registration.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

Forty-six women completed a three-month combined dietary program or general healthy-eating control. The study reported improvements in pain and several other patient-reported measures. The registration promoted four fish options including sardines, along with other simultaneous dietary components. Anxiety was discussed as background and was not measured as a separate outcome. Sardines and cooking preparation were not assigned separately. The population had fibromyalgia and the paper does not provide a baked sardine anxiety comparison.
