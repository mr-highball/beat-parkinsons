Title: Targeting Nrf2 and NF-κB Signaling Pathways in Inflammatory Pain: The Role of Polyphenols from Thinned Apples.
PMID: 37513248
PMCID: PMC10385557
DOI: 10.3390/molecules28145376
First published: 2023
Authors: Interdonato L, Ferrario G, Cordaro M, D'Amico R, Siracusa R, Fusco R et al.
Journal: Molecules (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37513248/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled rat carrageenan paw-edema inflammatory pain experiment administering thinned apple polyphenol (TAP) extract orally at 5 and 10 mg/kg 30 min before and 1 h after subplantar carrageenan injection. Oral TAP reduced paw edema, neutrophil infiltration, mast cell degranulation, oxidative stress, thermal and mechanical hyperalgesia, and spinal microglial nociception markers via Nrf2/NF-kB modulation. Limits: non-PD acute inflammatory pain rat model and unripe thinned apple extract (sample size not stated in abstract).
