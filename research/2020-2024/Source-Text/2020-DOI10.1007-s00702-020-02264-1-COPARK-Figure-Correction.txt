Title: Correction to: Excessive buccal saliva in patients with Parkinson's disease of the French COPARK cohort
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00702-020-02264-1
DOI: 10.1007/s00702-020-02264-1
Publication date: 2020-10-21
Source depth: Publisher correction notice and replacement Figure 2 caption
Date basis: Publication online October 21, 2020. The correction appears in volume 128 in 2021.
Design: Erratum to the COPARK observational cohort article

The original Figure 2 caption assigned its fills to the wrong groups. The correction specifies unfilled segments for no excessive saliva, singly hatched segments for sialorrhea, and crosshatched segments for drooling. The corrected legend applies to the nonfluctuator and on/off groups. This correction belongs to the same result as the original cohort publication and adds no independent participants, dietary contrast or intervention.
