Title: Betanin improves motor function and alleviates experimental Parkinsonism via downregulation of TLR4/MyD88/NF-κB pathway: Molecular docking and biological investigations.
PMID: 37244180
DOI: 10.1016/j.biopha.2023.114917
First published: 2023
Authors: ElSayed MH, Atif HM, Eladl MA, Elaidy SM, Helaly AMN, Hisham FA, et al.
Journal: Biomedicine & pharmacotherapy = Biomedecine & pharmacotherapie
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37244180/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse study (n=28 male Swiss albino mice across 4 groups) testing betanin (50 and 100 mg/kg) against rotenone-induced parkinsonism: betanin improved pole/locomotor motor performance and reduced neuroinflammatory markers. Limits: purified betanin in rotenone mice; translational gap to culinary beetroot.
