Original source scope
Mean analytical piperine across the studied samples was 4,418 mg per 100 g in black pepper, 28 in powdered curry, 3.4 in retorted curry, 4.3 in instant noodles and 4.2 in cup noodles. These are measurements in sample material. They do not determine piperine released in a mouthful, mucosal exposure, particle effects, or matching to the Rofes nectar study. No swallowed-bolus safety or clinical study was performed. Use as a preparation bridge question for Round 1 claim 0124.
