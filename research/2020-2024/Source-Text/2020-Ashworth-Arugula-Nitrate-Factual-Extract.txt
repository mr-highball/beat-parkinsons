May 2020 online. Thirty healthy young adults in crossover periods with fresh nitrate-rich vegetables including arugula, or beetroot juice. Post-lunch pressure fell during both conditions; no clear fasting change or between-intervention difference. No orthostatic challenge, falls measure or arugula-only arm.
