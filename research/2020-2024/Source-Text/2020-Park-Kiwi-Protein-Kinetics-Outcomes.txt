Measured amino acid outcomes in the green and gold kiwi crossover
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00394-020-02363-5

First published September 24, 2020. Twelve healthy older adults consumed two Hayward green or two Hort 16A gold kiwifruit daily for 14 days and again with 100 g of ground beef in a crossover. Early plasma essential amino acids rose faster with green fruit, with treatment-by-time interactions for essential and branched-chain amino acids and leucine. Total essential amino acid absorption and measured protein kinetics did not differ by fruit. The study did not measure attention.
