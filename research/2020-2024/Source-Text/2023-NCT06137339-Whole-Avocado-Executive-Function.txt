Registered whole avocado and executive testing in older adults
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06137339
Coverage: Factual official protocol and endpoint summary

First posted November 18, 2023. AvoCog plans 70 adults aged 65–85, whole avocado daily for 12 weeks versus usual diet, executive-task MRI and NIH Toolbox outcomes. Parkinson disease is excluded. The official August 22, 2024 update showed Unknown status without posted results when checked November 16, 2026.
