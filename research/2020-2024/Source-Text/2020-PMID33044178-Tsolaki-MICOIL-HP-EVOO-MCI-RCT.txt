Title: A Randomized Clinical Trial of Greek High Phenolic Early Harvest Extra Virgin Olive Oil in Mild Cognitive Impairment: The MICOIL Pilot Study.
PMID: 33044178
DOI: 10.3233/JAD-200405
First published: 2020
Authors: Tsolaki M, Lazarou E, Kozori M, Petridou N, Tabakis I, Lazarou I
Journal: Journal of Alzheimer's Disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33044178/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
12-month randomized pilot trial (MICOIL, Greece) in older adults with MCI assigned to 50 mL/d High Phenolic Early Harvest EVOO (HP-EH-EVOO), 50 mL/d Moderate Phenolic EVOO (MP-EVOO), or Mediterranean diet instructions alone. Both EVOO arms outperformed MeDi-only on ADAS-cog (p = 0.003), Digit Span (p = 0.006), and Letter Fluency (p = 0.003), with HP-EH-EVOO superior across nearly all domains. Strictly EVOO (NOT refined olive oil), non-PD MCI cohort, baseline APOE4 imbalance (p = 0.001).
