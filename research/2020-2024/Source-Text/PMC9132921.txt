Title: The macular inner plexiform layer thickness as an early diagnostic indicator for Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9132921/

PMCID: PMC9132921

The optical coherence tomography study measures inner retinal layers and diagnostic performance in Parkinson participants and controls. Visual hallucinations appear as context for visual dysfunction, without a food intervention or visual symptom comparison following intake.
