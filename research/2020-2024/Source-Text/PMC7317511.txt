Title: Prodromal Dementia With Lewy Bodies: Clinical Characterization and Predictors of Progression
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7317511/
PMID: 32048343
PMCID: PMC7317511
DOI: 10.1002/mds.27997
Publication date: 2020-02-11
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: Longitudinal prodromal Lewy body dementia and Alzheimer comparison

The cohort included 73 people with MCI and Lewy body features and 124 with MCI associated with Alzheimer disease. Visual hallucinations were recorded in 37 of 73 in the Lewy body group, and symptom presence also informed group definition. The follow-up modeled time to dementia, not an incidence rate after dried fig ingestion.
