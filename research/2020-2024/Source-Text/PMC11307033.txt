The authors use the previously reported multicenter patient and caregiver survey to investigate factors associated with quality of life in probable dementia with Lewy bodies. The treatment-needs paper is part of the same source family. The derived questionnaire analysis does not record a dried fig exposure or isolate recurrent food-timed visual events.
