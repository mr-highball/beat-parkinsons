Title: Vestibular Sensory Conflict During Postural Control, Freezing of Gait, and Falls in Parkinson's Disease
PMID: 36373942
PMCID: PMC9673158
DOI: 10.1002/mds.29189
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9673158/
Publication: 2022-08-15 electronic publication, November issue
Coverage: Verified original NCBI JATS body and Tables 1–2.

The cross-sectional study tested 92 people with Parkinson disease during modified Romberg sensory conflict. Table 2 records freezing in 11 of 33 participants who failed condition 4 and 2 of 59 who passed. The abstract's percentage for the normal subgroup does not reconcile with the two of thirteen freezers shown in the table. The adjusted association between Romberg failure and freezing gives an odds ratio of 7.96 with a reported interval of 1.28 to 49.4 after accounting for motor severity, age and cognition. The fall comparison was inconclusive.

This assessment measures postural sensory conflict and the presence of freezing. It has no food exposure or post-exposure change in freezing episodes. The Romberg task differs from the later ARO SU212 rotary vestibulo-ocular reflex comparison. Cohort overlap is unresolved.
