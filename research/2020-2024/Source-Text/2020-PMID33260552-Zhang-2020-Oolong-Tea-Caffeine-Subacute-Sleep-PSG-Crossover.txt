Title: Subacute Ingestion of Caffeine and Oolong Tea Increases Fat Oxidation without Affecting Energy Expenditure and Sleep Architecture: A Randomized, Placebo-Controlled, Double-Blinded Cross-Over Trial.
PMID: 33260552
PMCID: PMC7760339
DOI: 10.3390/nu12123671
First published: 2020
Authors: Zhang S, Takano J, Murayama N, Tominaga M, Abe T, Park I, et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7760339/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=12 healthy men consuming oolong tea (100 mg caffeine/dose), isolated caffeine (100 mg/dose), or placebo at breakfast and lunch for 14 days each, with day-14 24-h calorimetry and polysomnography. Both oolong tea and caffeine increased fat oxidation without impairing polysomnographic sleep architecture when consumed only at breakfast and lunch. Limits: oolong tea/caffeine (not black tea), daytime-only dosing, small non-PD male sample.
