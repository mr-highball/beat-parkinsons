Zhang and colleagues analyzed 39,717 adults aged 18 years and older in NHANES 2005–2020 using two 24-hour recalls and a 49-deficit frailty index. In the fully adjusted Model 3, a reported intake of at least 0.42 cup-equivalent per day of the other vegetables group, compared with no intake, had an odds ratio of 0.78 (95% CI 0.60–1.01). Below that median the odds ratio was 0.93 (95% CI 0.71–1.22). The same higher-intake comparison had an unadjusted odds ratio of 0.56 (95% CI 0.49–0.65). The adjusted estimates are imprecise and the population is a general adult sample.

The article uses Food Patterns Equivalents Database groupings. The published USDA vegetable methods report places green beans in the broad other vegetables group. It distinguishes them from the dry bean, pea and lentil group and from dark-green vegetables. The study reports the group-level frailty index. Separate intake, cooking and fatigue estimates for green beans are unavailable in the article. It supplies context about broad food-group exposure for claim 0561.
