Oral fungal testing linked to the sage rinse project
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12903-023-02950-y
PMID: 37072843
PMCID: PMC10111683
DOI: 10.1186/s12903-023-02950-y
Registration: NCT02067572
Coverage: Primary full body and cohort relationship

Published April 18, 2023. The original report names the Oral Health in Advanced Cancer project and registration NCT02067572. The observational laboratory analysis used archived isolates from 45 advanced-cancer patients in the underlying 88-person project. The authors evaluated 56 fungal isolates and recovered Candida albicans in 40 isolates, 39 susceptible to fluconazole in the reported test.

The analysis describes species and laboratory susceptibility. The article gives no randomized sage-rinse and saline pain estimate. It is a linked report of the project, with different analyzed material and outcomes from the 2021 symptom trial.
