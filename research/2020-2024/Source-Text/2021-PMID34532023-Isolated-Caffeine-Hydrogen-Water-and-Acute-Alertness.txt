Original source scope
Sixteen healthy habitual coffee drinkers took randomized crossover drinks containing isolated caffeine, hydrogen-rich water, their combination or control after sleep deprivation. The VAS alertness interaction was p=.095.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The intervention does not assign brewed coffee. The study measures alertness and task outcomes rather than a stand-alone Parkinson fatigue estimate.
