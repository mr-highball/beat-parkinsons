Black raspberry hematology registry safety events
https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03140280

November 4, 2022 first posted results for NCT03140280. The adverse-event module reports a 52-week time frame and one dietary intervention group with 18 at risk. Depression, insomnia and confusion each list one affected participant and one event under systematic other adverse events. The registry extract gives no individual linkage, occurrence dates, baseline change, comparison group or attribution of these entries to raspberry. The published three-month 15-participant analysis describes no adverse events and uses a different denominator and stated scope. No anxiety effect can be inferred for this trial or for frozen red raspberry in Parkinson disease.
