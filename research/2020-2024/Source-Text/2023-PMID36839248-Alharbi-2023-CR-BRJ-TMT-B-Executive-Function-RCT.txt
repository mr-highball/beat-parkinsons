Title: Caloric Restriction (CR) Plus High-Nitrate Beetroot Juice Does Not Amplify CR-Induced Metabolic Adaptation and Improves Vascular and Cognitive Functions in Overweight Adults: A 14-Day Pilot Randomised Trial.
PMID: 36839248
PMCID: PMC9962072
DOI: 10.3390/nu15040890
First published: 2023
Authors: Alharbi M, Chiurazzi M, Nasti G, Muscariello E, Mastantuono T, Koechl C, et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36839248/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

14-day parallel pilot RCT in n=29 overweight/obese middle-aged and older adults randomized to caloric restriction + high-nitrate beetroot juice (n=15) vs caloric restriction alone (n=14): CR+BRJ significantly improved Trail-Making Test Part B (TMT-B, executive set-shifting; p=0.012), microvascular flux (p=0.03), and NO-dependent endothelial function (p=0.02); resting BP did not differ (p>0.05). Limits: non-PD, open-label add-on to CR, small pilot.
