Topic: Reported anxiety scores in a safflower comparator crossover
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02960217?tab=results
Coverage: Primary source facts and outcome boundary
In the posted Glut1-deficiency crossover results, adult PROMIS anxiety T-score change was +0.5 (SE 1.45, n=25) on safflower oil and +3.1 (SE 1.49, n=23) on triheptanoin. Their adjusted difference was 2.6 points (95% CI −0.7 to 5.9, p=.1138). The active-comparator and period changes leave safflower versus no oil unresolved. The trial used an unspecified oil grade in another disorder.
