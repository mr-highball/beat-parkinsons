# Rapeseed meal and selective attention in a healthy crossover

Source https://lup.lub.lu.se/student-papers/search/publication/9089334
Coverage original institutional author abstract

Shen’s Lund university record first made the study abstract available in 2022. Seventeen healthy participants consumed five breakfast conditions, including bread with rapeseed oil and bread without added lipid. The author abstract reports no significant selective-attention or working-memory difference. The no-added-lipid meal had faster psychomotor reaction times than each lipid-containing meal. The repository gives no public full thesis, oil refining method or separated response-time estimates. General response speed leaves the named clinical attention effect open.
