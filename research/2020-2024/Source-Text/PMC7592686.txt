Design: Clinical management review of visual dysfunction in Parkinson disease

The review distinguishes ocular deficits, diplopia, perceptual changes and hallucinations. It discusses visual symptoms and relevant management measures, without an original cohort comparing visual episode rates after dried fruit or figs.
