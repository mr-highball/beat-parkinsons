Title: Therapeutic effects of HESA-A (a herbal-marine compound) in acute organophosphorus pesticide poisoning
Authors: Seyed Reza Mousavi and colleagues
Date: 2020-05 to 2020-06 issue
Identifiers: PMID 32523878; PMCID PMC7256278; related registration IRCT138811062083N2 as cited in article
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7256278/
Coverage: Original factual extract of clinical Methods and Tables 1 and 3.

The intervention was the combination powder HESA-A given by nasogastric tube in addition to standard care. It contains material from Carum carvi, king prawn and celery within a composite formulation. Dysarthria appears among alternative baseline signs contributing to eligibility for moderate poisoning. The paper does not give a baseline dysarthria count or a follow-up speech result. Fasciculations are categorized among poisoning symptoms and summarized in a baseline table. Reported clinical outcomes concern supportive treatment, intubation, intensive care, hospitalization and mortality. The design does not isolate ground caraway, measure rest tremor or test Parkinson speech.
