Text coverage: Original erratum, one page. Same trial as PMID 33320461.

The published correction standardizes the English and Spanish abstracts and methods to the 99 students included in analysis: 32 placebo, 34 cinnamon and 33 dried fig. It gives 107 randomized (36 placebo, 36 cinnamon, 35 dried fig) and 123 excluded at screening. It corrects participant quantities. No revised effect estimate is published in the notice.
