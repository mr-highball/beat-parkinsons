Soybean and corn oil comparator in COPD walking outcomes
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39181037/
PMID: 39181037
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2024.08.004
Registration: NCT03796455
Coverage: Factual original abstract and trial link.

Engelen et al. randomized 46 people with COPD. The original report's abstract identifies placebo soybean and corn oil capsules plus maltodextrin powder, and lists six-minute distance, four-metre gait speed and postural balance among secondary outcomes. It gives no clear functional finding or oil-specific arm estimates for stride length. The authors' public repository record and publisher abstract are available. The original tables and supplement outcomes remain unverified.
