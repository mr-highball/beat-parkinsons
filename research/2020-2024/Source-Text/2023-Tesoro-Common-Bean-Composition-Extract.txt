Levodopa chemistry in common bean samples
Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1002/jms.4952
Coverage: factual extract of analytical methods and scope.

Tesoro et al. <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37401097/>, DOI 10.1002/jms.4952, published July 2023, used LC–ESI/MS/MS to measure L-dopa in uncooked *Phaseolus vulgaris* fresh/dried seeds and pods, including a commercial dried black-bean comparator. Across tested matrices reported values ranged 0.020 ± 0.005 to 2.34 ± 0.05 µg/g dry weight; that range is not an estimate for a cooked serving. This is analytical composition, not exposure-response, clinical PD, sleep, or balance. Existing detailed catalog `research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md` flags an older 0.25% common-bean claim whose citations did not support the value; a separate 2018 UV assay gave higher values in different dried seed accessions. Do not combine divergent assays or extrapolate to boiled or canned beans.
