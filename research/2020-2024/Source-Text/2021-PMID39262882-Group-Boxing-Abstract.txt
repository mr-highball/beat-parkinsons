Title: A Retrospective Analysis of Group-Based Boxing Exercise on Measures of Physical Mobility in Patients With Parkinson Disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39262882/
PMID: 39262882
PMCID: PMC11384840
DOI: 10.1177/15598276211028144
First public: 2021-07-10
Coverage: Factual extract from author abstract and publisher metadata. Full body remains an access gap.

Sonne and colleagues retrospectively followed 68 people involved in Rock Steady Boxing. The author abstract reports balance, Timed Up and Go, chair stand and gait speed comparisons at repeated follow-up. The publisher dates first online release to July 10, 2021. The accessible PubMed Central XML contains metadata and the abstract without an article body. The PDF link returned HTML and the publisher requires access. The abstract describes an exercise intervention and no named food comparison.
