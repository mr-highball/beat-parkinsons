Title: A Retrospective Analysis of Group-Based Boxing Exercise on Measures of Physical Mobility in Patients With Parkinson Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11384840/
PMID: 39262882
PMCID: PMC11384840
DOI: 10.1177/15598276211028144
Publication date: 2021-07-10
Coverage: Selected factual text distilled from the original PMC article HTML and embedded figures or tables; supplementary files are separate.

The authors analyzed 68 Parkinson participants from four Rock Steady Boxing sites. Program records had baseline and at least one subsequent measure. The four measures were balance, timed mobility, chair stands and gait speed. Within-group improvements appeared on several balance, mobility or strength measures through 18 months. The 10-meter walk had an inconclusive comparison at six and twelve months. At 24 months the two reported comparisons each had eight participants and were inconclusive.

There was no parallel comparison group, individually recorded class adherence, counted fall endpoint, dedicated rigidity measure or sage exposure. Mobility and balance scores provide fall-risk context. Attrition, baseline selection and variation between sites limit interpretation.

The official PMC HTML supplied the methods, results text and embedded outcome tables missing from the PMC XML export. The narrative and Table 1 give different values for some comparisons and disagree about the named test. This extract does not resolve those discrepancies.
