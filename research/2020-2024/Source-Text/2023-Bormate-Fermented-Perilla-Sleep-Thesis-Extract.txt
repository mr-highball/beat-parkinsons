Fermented perilla extract and rodent sleep

Primary source: https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/detail?nodeId=T16655272
Coverage: Thesis author abstract and contents
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

In the February 2023 thesis summary, oral Bacillus subtilis-fermented Perilla frutescens var. acuta extract increased NREM sleep and slow-wave activity in rodent recordings. A pentobarbital induction assay reportedly showed shorter sleep onset and longer sleep. The adenosine A1 antagonist DPCPX attenuated effects. This is part of the closely matching 2024 journal development family. Complete numerical results and independent cohort identity are unverified.
