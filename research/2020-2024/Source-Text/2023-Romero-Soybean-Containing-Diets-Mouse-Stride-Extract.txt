2023 | Romero et al. | Matched soybean oil content in two interventions with measured strides

Source: DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-36818-0, PMCID PMC9977963, March 1, 2023. Full methods and Figure 8.

Adult male Angelman model mice and control genotypes ate a standard chow, a safflower oil-rich diet, or an anhydrous milk-fat-rich diet for the gait assay. The custom energy compositions both list soybean oil at 630 kcal/kg. The added safflower oil or anhydrous milk fat contributes 2,430 kcal/kg in the respective formulations. The comparison holds the soybean-oil amount constant and gives no oil refining grade.

The CatWalk instrument measured paw stride length and step counts. The genetic model on standard feed had strides approximately 12–15% longer than comparator mice and took fewer steps. The safflower-plus-soybean oil diet brought both measures toward wild-type values. The milk-fat-plus-soybean-oil diet left model gait measures resembling standard-feed values. The observed differences are associated with the two blends and this model, with no isolated soybean oil intervention.
