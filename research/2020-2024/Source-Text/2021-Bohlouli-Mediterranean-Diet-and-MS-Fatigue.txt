Title: Bohlouli and colleagues on Mediterranean-style diet adherence and fatigue in multiple sclerosis
Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/S000711452100307X
DOI: 10.1017/S000711452100307X
Publication date: 2021
Coverage: Verified original randomized trial at the published fatigue and intervention scope.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

The report compares six months of a modified Mediterranean diet with a traditional diet in relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis. The published analysis reports adjusted mean Modified Fatigue Impact Scale changes of −8.5 and +6.4, with p<.001. Its dietary description includes olive oil as part of the pattern. It supplies no isolated canola-oil exposure, refining grade or Parkinson fatigue estimate. The study uses a separately recruited multiple-sclerosis cohort.
