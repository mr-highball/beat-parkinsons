Title: Effect of Fruit Smoothie Supplementation on Psychological Distress Among People Receiving Opioid Agonist Therapy
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05229770
Source API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05229770
Open from: 2022-02-08, last posted update 2025-08-08
Access date: 2026-11-14 in America/New_York
Coverage: factual extract of official registration record

The record is completed. It compares a 250 ml daily serving of rotating commercial fruit smoothies, in addition to standard opioid agonist treatment, with standard treatment without smoothie. The structured secondary outcome specifies an FSS-3 fatigue assessment at week 16 and prints a 0–18 scale range, which differs from the published methods’ description of three 1–7 items. No results module was posted at access. The published protocol and outcome report provide the mango-containing recipes and reported clinical outcomes.
