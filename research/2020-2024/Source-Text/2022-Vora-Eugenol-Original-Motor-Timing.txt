Original eugenol beam results by treatment timing
Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ddt/16/4/16_2022.01026/_pdf
PMID: 36002316
DOI: 10.5582/ddt.2022.01026
Coverage: original J-STAGE PDF; Methods and Figures 2 to 4, advance publication record

Advance publication August 24, 2022. Vora and colleagues compared isolated oral eugenol by treatment timing in a parkinsonian mouse model. The original narrow-beam result shows faster crossings in the pretreatment comparisons and slower crossings in the post-treatment comparisons. The paper describes one model-only control group and different observation schedules. It does not describe a separate time-matched model-only group for each schedule, which limits causal interpretation of the timing contrast. Other motor tasks and group contrasts differ by dose and endpoint, and the abstract emphasizes high-dose worsening of akinesia. The beam time is a model behavior. It gives no human walking-speed comparison, effect for ground allspice, or measurement of culinary exposure.
