Title: Effects of cooking methods on total isothiocyanate yield from cruciferous vegetables.
PMID: 33133569
PMCID: PMC7590320
DOI: 10.1002/fsn3.1836
First published: 2020
Authors: Wang Z, Kwan ML, Pratt R, Roh JM, Kushi LH, Danforth KN et al.
Journal: Food Sci Nutr
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33133569/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Analytical HPLC study measuring isothiocyanate (ITC) yield from cauliflower, broccoli, cabbage, and kale across raw and 6 cooking methods. Light cooking (steaming, stir-frying, microwaving) increased mean ITC yield ~4-fold, whereas heavy boiling/stewing decreased yield 58%. Shows wide inter-vegetable ITC variation and preparation sensitivity without clinical endpoints.
