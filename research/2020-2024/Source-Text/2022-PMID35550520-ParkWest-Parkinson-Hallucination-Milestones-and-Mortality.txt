Title: Parkinson's disease clinical milestones and mortality
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9098431/
PMID: 35550520
PMCID: PMC9098431
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-022-00320-z
Publication year: 2022
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The ParkWest cohort uses predefined clinical thresholds for onset of hallucinations and other disease milestones, then analyzes mortality. Reaching a clinical threshold is distinct from changes in repeated visual episodes. The cohort does not compare dried fig intake.

Its clinical milestone uses a threshold on existing rating scale items. The timing of first threshold-crossing is analyzed with other milestones in follow-up of the same population-based cohort.
