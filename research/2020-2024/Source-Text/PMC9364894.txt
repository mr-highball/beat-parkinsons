Title: Confirmation of 123I-FP-CIT SPECT Quantification Methods in Dementia with Lewy Bodies and Other Neurodegenerative Disorders
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9364894/
PMID: 32198310
PMCID: PMC9364894
DOI: 10.2967/jnumed.119.239418
Publication date: 2020-03-20
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML article body; local research digest

Date basis: PubMed electronic date. The official PMC HTML labels the November 2020 issue.
Design: Neuropathology-referenced SPECT methods comparison

The retrospective study evaluates three dopamine transporter SPECT quantification approaches against neuropathology in neurodegenerative syndromes. Hallucination information is clinical characterization. The compared test methods cannot estimate a food effect.
