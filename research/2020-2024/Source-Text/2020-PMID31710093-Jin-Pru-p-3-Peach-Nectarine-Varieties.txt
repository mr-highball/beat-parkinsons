Title: Selection of Pru p 3 hypoallergenic peach and nectarine varieties.

Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Allergological screening report (2020) quantifying heat-stable Pru p 3 lipid transfer protein content across peach and smooth-skinned nectarine cultivars (Prunus persica var. nucipersica), confirming shared Rosaceae Pru p 3 allergenicity between peaches and nectarines alongside cultivar-dependent variation. Safety context for both raw and grilled peach/nectarine claims.
