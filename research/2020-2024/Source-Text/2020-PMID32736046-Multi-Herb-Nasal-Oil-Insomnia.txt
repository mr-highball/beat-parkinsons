Combined nasal botanical oils versus sesame oil in chronic insomnia
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32736046/
PMID: 32736046
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2020.113116
First published online: 2020-07-28
Coverage: Original investigator abstract factual extract. Complete article tables pending.

Taherzadeh and colleagues randomized 50 patients with primary chronic insomnia to a nasal preparation containing extracts from violet, saffron and lettuce seeds or a sesame-oil nasal comparator. The authors describe the combination as based on Traditional Persian Medicine. The primary outcome was eight-week change in the Insomnia Severity Index. ISI improved from baseline in both arms (p=.001 in the combination arm, p<.01 in the sesame arm). The accessible abstract supplies no direct between-arm ISI estimate or uncertainty. Differences in co-ingredients, route and population prevent attributing the observation to oral ground saffron. The study includes no Parkinson-specific analysis.
