Title: Posterior Cortical Atrophy phenotype in a GBA N370S mutation carrier: a case report.
PMID: 33435912
PMCID: PMC7802182
DOI: 10.1186/s12883-020-02023-5
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7802182/
First publication date: 2021-01-12 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

The report documents a posterior cortical atrophy phenotype in a GBA variant carrier. Overt visual hallucinations were absent during the reported follow-up despite visuospatial difficulties and mild parkinsonian features. The negative observation describes the phenotype and has no dried fig exposure.
