Title: Grape seed polyphenol extract and cognitive function in healthy young adults: a randomised, placebo-controlled, parallel-groups acute-on-chronic trial.
PMID: 31942838
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2019.1708033
First published: 2022
Authors: Bell L, Whyte AR, Lamport DJ, Spencer JPE, Butler LT, Williams CM
Journal: Nutritional neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31942838/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled acute-on-chronic 12-week trial in 60 healthy young adults (18-30 y) receiving 400 mg/day purified grape seed polyphenol extract (GSPE, n=30) vs placebo (n=30). Linear marginal models revealed NO consistent pattern of cognitive or PANAS mood benefits acutely (2-6 h) or chronically (6-12 wk), with isolated reaction-time/tapping changes offset by placebo gains in memory/RT. Also isolated seed extract (remote from whole frozen/raw grape).
