Title: Parkinson's disease and schizophrenia interactomes contain temporally distinct gene clusters underlying comorbid mechanisms and unique disease processes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10899210/
PMID: 38413605
PMCID: PMC10899210
DOI: 10.1038/s41537-024-00439-3
Publication date: 2024-02-27
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The researchers integrate protein-interaction and transcriptomic data to identify gene clusters shared or distinguished across Parkinson disease and schizophrenia. A comorbidity mechanism inferred from networks does not supply a measured food exposure or clinical episode count.
