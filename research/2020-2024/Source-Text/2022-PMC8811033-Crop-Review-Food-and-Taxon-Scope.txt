The crop table distinguishes barley and multiple millet species from foods and plants in separate categories. Blackberry appears in a fruit and nutrient summary. The potato names identify sweet potato and Ethiopian potato, not culinary Solanum tuberosum. Wild ginger means Siphonochilus aethiopicus in the crop table. Zingiber officinale appears separately in a list of antioxidant botanicals.

The lemongrass entry identifies Cymbopogon flexuosus. Mentha spicata appears in a phytochemical context. Cinnamomum verum, Curcuma longa and Crocus sativus are listed as distinct botanicals. The source provides no paired Parkinson symptom comparison with these preparations. The customary uses and nutritional facts do not indicate measured clinical efficacy.
