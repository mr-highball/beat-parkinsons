Peanut oil vehicle in dopamine nanoparticle treatment
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8551202/
PMID: 34707203
PMCID: PMC8551202
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-021-00764-y
Coverage: Original Methods and Results with local article archive

The October 27, 2021 original used peanut oil as the oily phase in a dopamine and soy-lecithin nanoparticle preparation in a reserpine rat model. Treatment arms received formulated dopamine, bee venom or levodopa separately. The design did not assign a peanut-oil-only symptom comparison or specify refining or cold pressing. Fatigue and pain occur in introductory clinical background. The reported experimental outcomes are laboratory measures without a peanut-oil-specific fatigue or pain result.
