Title: Effects of onion extract containing concentrated cysteine sulfoxides on sleep quality: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, crossover study.
PMID: 33282442
PMCID: PMC7708608
DOI: 10.1007/s10068-020-00829-0
First published: 2020
Authors: Nakayama Y, Makita M, Nozaki S, Kikuchi Y
Journal: Food Science and Biotechnology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33282442/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
5-day double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover RCT in 30 healthy adults dissatisfied with their sleep, consuming onion extract tablets containing concentrated S-alk(en)yl-L-cysteine sulfoxides (ACSOs) vs placebo. Significantly decreased objective sleep latency and significantly increased NREM delta power and first-sleep-cycle delta power per minute vs placebo, alongside significantly lower salivary alpha-amylase stress marker. Limitations: short 5-day crossover, concentrated onion-extract tablets rather than whole roasted onion, non-PD adults without clinical insomnia diagnosis, single Japanese research group without independent replication, full text non-OA in Food Sci Biotechnol (PMC7708608 body unavailable via OA XML).
