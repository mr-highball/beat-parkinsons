Title: Filizzola and colleagues, preliminary avocado oil supplementation and adolescent body composition
Source: https://revistamundodasaude.emnuvens.com.br/mundodasaude/article/view/1600
Publication: October 25, 2024
Coverage: Factual original Methods and Results.

Thirty-four participants started and 30 completed. The higher avocado-oil arm contained nine, the lower arm 12 and the mineral-oil comparison nine. Results cover anthropometry, resting pressure and body composition. The paper does not identify the oil pressing method or include an adverse-event table. It calls the results preliminary and shares ethics code 61242122.3.0000.0062 with the later ReBEC registration.
