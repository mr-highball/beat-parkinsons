Title: Scientific opinion on the tolerable upper intake level for selenium.
PMID: 36698500
PMCID: PMC9854220
DOI: 10.2903/j.efsa.2023.7704
First published: 2023
Authors: EFSA Panel on Nutrition, Novel Foods and Food Allergens (NDA), Turck D, Bohn T, Castenmiller J, de Henauw S, Hirsch-Ernst KI, et al.
Journal: EFSA journal. European Food Safety Authority
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36698500/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

European Food Safety Authority (EFSA NDA Panel; PMC9854220) scientific opinion lowering the adult tolerable upper intake level (UL) for selenium from 300 to 255 ug/day (based on LOAEL 330 ug/d for clinical alopecia in SELECT) and explicitly identifying regular Brazil nut consumers as a population at risk of exceeding the UL.
