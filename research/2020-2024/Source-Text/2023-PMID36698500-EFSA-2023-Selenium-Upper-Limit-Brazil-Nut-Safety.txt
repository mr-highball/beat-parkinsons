European Food Safety Authority (EFSA NDA Panel; PMC9854220) scientific opinion lowering the adult tolerable upper intake level (UL) for selenium from 300 to 255 ug/day (based on LOAEL 330 ug/d for clinical alopecia in SELECT) and explicitly identifying regular Brazil nut consumers as a population at risk of exceeding the UL.

The 24-person analyzed chicken comparison uses enriched versus nonenriched chicken and is distinct from the selenium supplementation adverse-event studies. The latter report mild-to-moderate fatigue RR 1.09 (99% CI 0.95–1.26) in SELECT. That estimate is for selenium supplementation, with no separate coffee or food effect.

Claim 0121 | PMID:36698500 | Coffee intake appears as an adjustment variable in summarized studies. The reported fatigue analysis concerns a separate selenium exposure, with no fatigue estimate for brewed coffee.
Claim 0348 | PMID:36698500 | The panel summarizes a 10-week selenium-enriched chicken-breast trial in overweight adults. Body composition and metabolic outcomes were recorded. Poached preparation and fatigue were not measured in that comparison.
Claim 0502 | PMID:36698500 | The selenium opinion discusses some toxicity symptoms, including pain, but the reviewed source does not identify dried date fruit as an exposure in a pain comparison.
Claim 1000 | PMID:36698500 | The background names broccoli grown with selenium enrichment and discusses chemical speciation. No steamed broccoli comparison or fall-frequency outcome is presented.
