Title: Therapeutic potential of blackberry extract in the preventing memory deficits and neurochemical alterations in the cerebral cortex, hippocampus and cerebellum of a rat model with amnesia.
PMID: 36735154
DOI: 10.1007/s11011-023-01175-w
First published: 2023
Authors: de Mello JE, Luduvico KP, Dos Santos A, Teixeira FC, de Souza Cardoso J, de Aguiar MSS et al.
Journal: Metabolic brain disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36735154/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Five-group male rat scopolamine-induced amnesia experiment testing 10 days of oral Rubus sp. blackberry extract (100 and 200 mg/kg) vs donepezil (5 mg/kg) and saline on object-recognition memory and cortical/hippocampal/cerebellar neurochemistry. Assessed recognition memory rather than attention, executive function, or PD motor symptoms.
