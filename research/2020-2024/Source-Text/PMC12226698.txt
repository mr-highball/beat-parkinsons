Title: Disentangling the acute subjective effects of classic psychedelics from their enduring therapeutic properties
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12226698/
PMID: 38743110
PMCID: PMC12226698
DOI: 10.1007/s00213-024-06599-5
Publication date: 2024-05-14
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The review considers whether subjective experiences during classic psychedelic use explain subsequent changes in psychiatric outcomes. The exposures and acute perceptual changes pertain to pharmacologic agents; the review contains no dried fig challenge in Parkinson disease.
