Title: Lemon balm and Nepeta aqueous extracts in a reserpine mouse model
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/ptr.7457
PMID: 35470926
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.7457
Publication date: 2022-04-26
Text coverage: Publisher's original abstract and supplement listing. The article's numerical single-arm outcome tables remain to be checked.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18

The experiments use oral aqueous extracts of Melissa officinalis and Nepeta menthoides in mice exposed to reserpine. Separate herb, saline and drug arms were included along with a low-dose combined herb arm. The stated endpoints were open-field activity, forced-swim and tail-suspension immobility, and brain catalase. The design uses one week of pretreatment before reserpine. This is a pharmacologic behavioral model.

The abstract reports favorable behavioral and catalase findings for the combination and calls the effect synergistic. It does not provide the separate lemon-balm outcome results or an interaction estimate needed to verify the synergy claim. The reported mean rosmarinic acid content is 6.42 mg/g of dried lemon balm extract and 11.03 mg/g of dried Nepeta extract.

The separate acute experiment published in 2026 cites this extraction method but uses a different design. Its single-dose findings cannot fill the missing 2022 single-herb arm comparisons.
