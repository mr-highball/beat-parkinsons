Title: Diet and the gut microbiome in patients with Parkinson's disease
PMID: 38649365
PMCID: PMC11035608
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-024-00681-7
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11035608/
Publication year: 2024
Coverage: Complete original JATS including eligibility, food instrument and analysis.

The final analysis includes 85 people with Parkinson disease with diet information and qualifying stool sequencing data. The Diet History Questionnaire II was used to estimate diet quality, dietary fiber and added sugar. The published results compare measures of microbiome diversity and composition across dietary exposures. They do not provide an ingredient-specific dried fig result or a clinical visual hallucination measure. Richer food instruments can identify underlying diet context even when the published analysis reports only aggregate exposures.
