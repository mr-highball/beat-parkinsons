Title: Artichoke (Cynara Scolymus) Methanolic Leaf Extract Alleviates Diethylnitrosamine-Induced Toxicity in BALB/c Mouse Brain: Involvement of Oxidative Stress and Apoptotically Related Klotho/PPARγ Signaling.
PMID: 36556233
PMCID: PMC9781370
DOI: 10.3390/jpm12122012
First published: 2022
Authors: Cicek, Genc, Yeni, Kuzucu, Cetin
Journal: Journal of personalized medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36556233/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

14-day controlled BALB/c mouse study (n=32, 8/group) testing oral Cynara scolymus methanolic leaf extract (0.8 and 1.6 g/kg/d) against diethylnitrosamine (DEN 100 mg/kg) brain toxicity. High-dose extract restored open-field ambulatory activity (58 +/- 1.87% vs 46 +/- 1.09% DEN, p < 0.05), horizontal movement (4529 vs 2012, p < 0.05), total distance (5949 vs 1501 cm, p < 0.05), and resting time (42% vs 54%, p < 0.05), while restoring brain MDA, TAC, Klotho, PPAR-gamma, and Bcl-2/Bax/caspase-3. Limits: methanolic leaf extract, DEN hepatoneurotoxin rather than dopaminergic PD model, general open-field locomotion proxy.
