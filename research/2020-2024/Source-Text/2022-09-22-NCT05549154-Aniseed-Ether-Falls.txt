Aniseed ether in a vitamin D comparator with registered falls
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05549154
Registration: NCT05549154
Coverage: Official registry protocol fields and posted results status

The registered kidney-disease trial assigns different doses of vitamin D2 and a matching placebo capsule. The listed placebo excipients are aniseed ether and refined vegetable oil. Low- and high-dose regimens also use placebo capsules for masking at specified visits. Incidence of falls after six months is a secondary outcome based on participant reports of number and reason. As checked November 16, 2026, there were no posted results. The protocol addresses vitamin D and does not establish the aniseed ether source, ground seed intake, an isolated anise contrast or a Parkinson sample.
