The comparator in weeks zero to four was placebo. Later both groups received DA-9701, derived from Corydalis tuber and Pharbitis seed. Supplementary Table 4 gives four-week within-group p-values above .999 and .655 for stool frequency and .329 and .914 for Bristol distribution, ordered drug-first then placebo-first. Separate within-group p-values do not establish an interaction or an equivalence result. The upper abdominal burning or pain diary result is also within-group, with p=.029 and .216. At four weeks, Supplementary Table 6 gives two constipation adverse events among 65 on DA-9701 and zero among 65 on placebo, without an item-specific estimate. Preserve this safety observation separately from the bowel efficacy measures.

The registry lists bowel movement frequency, Bristol stool scale and epigastric pain among secondary outcomes. The official results module supplies no tables. This supplement belongs to NCT02775591. NCT03022201 compared DA-9701 with domperidone and retains a separate diary access gap. The placebo trial assigned neither ginger nor dried dates.
