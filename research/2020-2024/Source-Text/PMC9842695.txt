Title: Neuropsychological endpoints for clinical trials in methylmalonic acidemia and propionic acidemia: A pilot study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9842695/
PMID: 36659999
PMCID: PMC9842695
DOI: 10.1016/j.ymgmr.2022.100953
Publication date: 2023-01-08
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The pilot measures selected neuropsychological domains in methylmalonic and propionic acidemia to inform potential trials. The metabolic conditions and measured tests differ from habitual fig intake and recurrent visual hallucinations in Parkinson disease.
