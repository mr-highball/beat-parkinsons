Title: Apple juice relieves loperamide-induced constipation in rats by downregulating the intestinal apical sodium-dependent bile acid transporter ASBT.
PMID: 37129213
DOI: 10.1039/d3fo00510k
First published: 2023
Authors: Zhu Q, Iwai R, Okaguchi T, Shirasaka Y, Tamai I
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37129213/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Experimental loperamide-induced constipation study in rats. Twice-daily oral apple juice for 7 days restored fecal pellet weight and number close to saline controls, increased fecal total bile acids from day 4, and downregulated ileal apical sodium-dependent bile acid transporter (ASBT / slc10a2) mRNA and bile-acid transport activity; Caco-2 cell experiments implicated apple procyanidins in ASBT downregulation. Limits: rat loperamide model (sample size absent in abstract) and apple juice rather than baked whole apple.
