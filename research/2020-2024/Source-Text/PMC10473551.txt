The commentary interprets the same Okkels cohort published under DOI 10.1093/brain/awad192. It describes no clear cholinergic PET difference by hallucination status. The commentary is a derivative report, and neither gives an oral dried fig contrast.

The primary study contrasts 25 participants with dementia with Lewy bodies, 15 with Parkinson disease and 15 controls. The North-Eastern Visual Hallucinations Interview provided a binary endpoint. See the separately cataloged primary article PMID37279796.
