Title: Protein Society proceedings on food-derived proteins and plant assays
First published: 2022-12-26
PMCID: PMC9794021
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9794021/
Archive scope: Verified complete original body or conference collection. Selected passages summarized.

Complete posted Protein Society abstract collection with food-derived materials in individual basic science members. ABS094 purified black-seed proteins for molecular characterization by protein sequencing and mass spectrometry. ABS341 tested how extraction pH changes lentil seed protein nanofibrils. An enzyme-design abstract compared laboratory lysozymes with hen egg-white lysozyme. ABS403 studied bacterial proteins in tomato wilt and a separate cystatin abstract used tomato protein elements as a model. These are laboratory materials and preparation leads. The selected members do not test the corresponding foods for Parkinson symptoms.
