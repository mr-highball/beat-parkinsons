Title: Donepezil for dementia with Lewy bodies: meta-analysis of multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled phase II, III, and, IV studies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11578031/
PMID: 38439217
PMCID: PMC11578031
DOI: 10.1111/psyg.13101
Publication date: 2024-03-04
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The meta-analysis uses data from three double-blind randomized donepezil comparisons in dementia with Lewy bodies. The NPI-2 combines hallucinations and cognitive fluctuations. This drug outcome is broader than visual episode frequency and contains no dried fig comparison.
