Title: Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia) syrup as an adjunct to standard care in patients with mild to moderate COVID-19: An open-label, randomized, controlled clinical trial.
PMID: 37663382
PMCID: PMC10474914
DOI: 10.22038/AJP.2022.21606
First published: 2023
Authors: Qaraaty M, Bahrami M, Azimi SA, Hashem-Dabaghian F, Saberi S, Abbas Zaidi SM, Sahebkar A, Enayati A
Journal: Avicenna journal of phytomedicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37663382/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Open-label randomized controlled trial (Qaraaty et al., 2023, Avicenna J Phytomed, PMC10474914, IRCT20110907007511N4) in 84 enrolled (81 analyzed: 41 lavender syrup, 40 control) Iranian outpatients with mild-to-moderate COVID-19 receiving oral Lavandula angustifolia aerial-part hydroethanolic extract syrup (9 mL twice daily, standardized to 2.38 ug/mg rosmarinic acid) + standard care vs standard care alone for 21 days. Between-group reductions favored lavender syrup for cough (n=66, p=0.024) and acute viral anosmia (n=43: change 6.6+/-2.93 vs 4.3+/-4.07, p=0.043, no difference by weeks 5-7), but were NULL for fatigue (n=74: 5.15+/-2.88 vs 4.37+/-3.54, p=0.246), malaise (p=0.828), diarrhea (p=0.635), and Hamilton anxiety score (3.10+/-6.79 vs 2.66+/-5.19, p=0.35). Limits: open-label, baseline sex imbalance (70.9% vs 40% male, p=0.007), acute viral COVID-19 anosmia distinct from neurodegenerative PD olfactory loss.
