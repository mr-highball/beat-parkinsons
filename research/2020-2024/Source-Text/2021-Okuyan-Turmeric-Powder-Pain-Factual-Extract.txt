Publication: Okuyan et al., Journal of Surgery and Medicine 5(7), 715–717 (2021). DOI 10.28982/josam.828571. NCT04183556.
Coverage: Original trial methods and outcomes, Tables 1–2.
Source: https://jsurgmed.com/article/download/828571/5938

The analysis included 75 participants using naproxen alone and 74 using naproxen plus turmeric powder. Table 2 reports pain-score reductions of 5.6 and 7.0 respectively, p < .001. The report describes no blinding or matching powder. Its Table 1 includes an anomalous spread statistic. The population had primary dysmenorrhea, distinct from Parkinson disease.
