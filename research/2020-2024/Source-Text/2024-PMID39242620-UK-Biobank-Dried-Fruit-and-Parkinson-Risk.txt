Title: Identifying potential causal effects of Parkinson's disease: a polygenic risk score-based phenome-wide association and Mendelian randomization study in UK Biobank
PMID: 39242620
PMCID: PMC11379879
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-024-00780-5
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11379879/
Publication year: 2024
Coverage: Complete original JATS including analysis and interpretation.
Dietary questionnaire definition: https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1319

The article uses UK Biobank exposures in a polygenic risk score screen and evaluates selected associations using Mendelian randomization. The investigators report a positive association for a dried-fruit intake variable and Parkinson disease. UK Biobank field 1319 counts pieces of generic dried fruit, giving prunes, apricots and raisins as its unit examples. This instrument does not identify a fig-specific intake variable. The outcome is Parkinson diagnosis or risk rather than visual hallucination frequency after diagnosis.

The 2024 and 2026 dietary risk analyses use UK Biobank input data. Treat the possible overlap in source cohorts and instruments as an independence limitation. Genetic proxy analyses do not provide a randomized comparison of eating dried figs.
