Title: Black carrot anthocyanins exhibit neuroprotective effects against MPP+ induced cell death and cytotoxicity via inhibition of oxidative stress mediated apoptosis.
PMID: 34776632
PMCID: PMC8554904
DOI: 10.1007/s10616-021-00500-4
First published: 2021
Authors: Zaim M, Kara I, Muduroglu A
Journal: Cytotechnology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34776632/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

In vitro cellular neuroprotection study extracting acylated cyanidin-based anthocyanins from black carrots (Daucus carota ssp. sativus var. atrorubens) and co-incubating human dopaminergic SH-SY5Y cells (2.5-100 ug/mL) with 0.5 mM MPP+. Concentrations of 50-100 ug/mL preserved metabolic activity, reduced membrane damage, scavenged ROS, and inhibited MPP+-induced apoptosis without cytotoxicity. In vitro cell model using black-carrot anthocyanins absent in standard orange culinary carrots.
