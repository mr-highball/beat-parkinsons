Title: Extracted short-chain oat fiber tolerability trial
First published or posted: 2024-12-18
Registration: NCT06739941
Source URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06739941
Text type: original-source evidence summary

First posted December 18, 2024. This completed dose study focused on gastrointestinal tolerance of extracted short-chain oat fiber. The registry had no separate results posting at the November 17, 2026 check. The linked June 2026 paper reports exploratory daily sleep duration. The preparation is extracted fiber rather than whole oat grain.
