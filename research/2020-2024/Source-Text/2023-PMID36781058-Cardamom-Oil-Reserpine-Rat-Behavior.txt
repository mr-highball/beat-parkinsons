Title: Cardamom oil ameliorates behavioral and neuropathological disorders in a rat model of depression induced by reserpine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36781058/
PMID: 36781058
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2023.116254
Publication date: 2023-02-11
Date basis: PubMed electronic publication date before the May issue.
Collection: Source text
Review depth: primary_source, abstract and publisher methods excerpt
Checked: 2026-11-25

Investigators used a reserpine-induced depressive-behavior paradigm in
male rats and administered oral cardamom oil. Fluoxetine was an active
reference. The publisher methods excerpt reports 50 animals overall.

The abstract reports improvement on both forced-swim behavior and
open-field locomotion. The animal finding has a favorable observed
sign for the behavioral readout. Increased ability or tendency to move
can also influence forced-swim performance. The experimental oil is
not culinary ground cardamom, and the study did not measure Parkinson
depression in humans. The two outcomes are from one experimental family.

Related claim: 0777, ground cardamom and depressive symptom severity.
