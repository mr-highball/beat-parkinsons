Title: Phantom Boarder Relates to Experimentally‐Induced Presence Hallucinations in Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10105112/
PMID: 37070043
PMCID: PMC10105112
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13684
Publication date: 2023-02-13
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

Conflicting sensorimotor stimulation examines sensed presence among different Parkinson symptom groups. In the four phantom-boarder cases, three reported complex visual hallucinations, separately from phantom-boarder episodes. The experiment neither changes food intake nor records visual episodes after dried figs.
