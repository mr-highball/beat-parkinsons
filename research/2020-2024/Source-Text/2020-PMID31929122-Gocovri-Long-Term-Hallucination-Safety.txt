Title: EASE LID 2: A 2-Year Open-Label Trial of Gocovri (Amantadine) Extended Release for Dyskinesia in Parkinson's Disease.
PMID: 31929122
PMCID: PMC7242830
DOI: 10.3233/jpd-191841
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7242830/
First publication date: 2020-01-01 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

EASE LID 2 follows 223 participants receiving open-label extended-release amantadine for Parkinson dyskinesia. Hallucination adverse events and time to onset were tracked; the common named event was reported in 24.2% of participants. The extension re-enrolled participants from earlier trials and also enrolled a deep-brain-stimulation cohort. NCT02202551 is a medication safety study without a dried fig exposure or controlled food comparison.
