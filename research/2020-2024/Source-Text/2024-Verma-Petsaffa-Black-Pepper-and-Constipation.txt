Title: Evaluation of Petsaffa tablets in adults with constipation, gas and acidity
Source: https://ijapr.in/index.php/ijapr/article/download/3207/2794/7794
DOI: 10.47070/ijapr.v12i4.3207
Publication date: 2024-05-04
Date basis: The original displays May 4 as publication and identifies the April 2024 journal issue.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: full_text

The open-label before-after study evaluated 120 adults over 14 days without a comparator. The proprietary oral mixture contained Piper nigrum alongside senna and several other plants. It did not isolate cracked pepper or report a diagnosed Parkinson subgroup.

The original reports a within-person mean reduction in constipation score of 10.62 points (95% CI 10.15–11.08). The frequency table records 2 participants with one daily bowel movement at baseline and 81 at day 14. Without separate arms, these observations cannot distinguish the black pepper from the other ingredients, time effects, or the effects of the study context. The abstract says no adverse events occurred, while the Results section describes two events and states that the investigators did not attribute them to the tablets.
