Title: Fig-and-almond and sweet-almond syrups with methylphenidate in pediatric ADHD
Registry ID: IRCT20220406054433N1
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/62774
Other official routes: https://irct.ir/trial/62774, https://fa.irct.ir/trial/62774, and https://irct.ir/trial/62774/xml?download=1
Coverage: Selected fields from the exact official registration, retrieved with native FPC bilingual search and detail.

First registration and last update: 2022-04-23 (1401/02/03). Registration timing: while recruiting. Update count: 0. Recruitment status: complete. Expected start: 2022-04-21. Actual recruitment start and trial completion are blank in the inspected HTML. Ethics identifier: IR.IUMS.REC.1400.1264.
Design: Double-blind block-randomized study in children aged 6–14 with ADHD. Target sample 120, 40 per arm. All three arms planned methylphenidate. Placebo was an inactive syrup. The sweet almond arm used a Tehran University of Medical Sciences pharmacy preparation made from sweet almonds and raisins, 5 mL three times daily for 12 weeks. The fig-and-almond arm used a distinct commercial NIAK syrup identified by Iranian Food and Drug Administration registration 8953162594580741. The public intervention text does not specify the fig input's fresh/dried status or provide ingredient proportions.
Primary: ADHD severity via Teacher and Parent ADHD Rating Scale at baseline and 4, 8 and 12 weeks.
Secondary: Checklist of drug side effects based on CTCAE v4.03 (2010), every two weeks after treatment began. BMI and clinical findings at 4, 8 and 12 weeks. The registration does not identify named tremor or drooling items, subtypes or grading plans.
The 2025 article (PMID 42021791, PMCID PMC13097199) names the commercial syrup Tinlose and reports secondary adverse-event tallies for tremor and drooling, which belong to this same trial.
