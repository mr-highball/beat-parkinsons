Title: A Comparison of Dry Bean and Pea Consumption on Serum Cholesterol: A Randomized Controlled Trial in Adults with Mild Hypercholesterolemia.

Primary Abstract Facts (OA XML restricted on EPMC/NCBI; GI detail previously extracted in 2024-Bell-Bean-Rotation-Stool-Factual-Extract.txt): Single-blind 3-arm parallel RCT (n=60/group, 6 wk) in adults with mild hypercholesterolemia assigned to 120 g/d cooked dry beans (black, great northern, navy, pinto), 120 g/d cooked dry peas (mixture of yellow and green), or white parboiled rice. Peas did not significantly alter LDL-C (-0.11, 95% CI -0.29 to 0.07), blood pressure (SBP -1.39, 95% CI -5.18 to 2.40; DBP -1.89, 95% CI -4.65 to 0.88), glucose, or insulin vs rice; gastrointestinal effects were transient and most often unrelated to study foods. Tests dry split/whole yellow+green pea mix rather than steamed garden green peas, and excludes PD.
