# Red spinach extract and Stroop performance

Source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8227312/
Coverage original Methods and Results

Haynes and colleagues reported a crossover of red spinach extract and maltodextrin in ten healthy resistance-trained men. Seven days of exposure produced no significant Stroop performance difference in the exercise protocol. The concentrated red-spinach product and healthy exercise setting differ from cooked Spinacia oleracea in Parkinson’s. The primary paper and NCT04292106 registration share the Stroop method and exercise outcomes.
