Title: The phenomenology of pareidolia in healthy subjects and patients with left- or right-hemispheric stroke
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10926141/
PMID: 38468958
PMCID: PMC10926141
DOI: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e27414
Publication date: 2024-02-29
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The study elicits recognizable forms in ambiguous stimuli from stroke patients and controls. Counts in a stimulus task measure perceptual interpretation, with lesion hemisphere as a grouping variable. The counts do not represent spontaneous Parkinson visual hallucinations after dried figs.
