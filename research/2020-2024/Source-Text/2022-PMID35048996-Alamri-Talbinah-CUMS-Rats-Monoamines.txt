Title: Talbinah (Barley Porridge): neurotransmitters modulatory effect and antidepressant-like action in experimentally depressed rats.
PMID: 35048996
DOI: 10.26355/eurrev_202201_27769
First published: 2022
Authors: Alamri HS
Journal: European review for medical and pharmacological sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35048996/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled rat experiment (n=32 adult rats in 4 groups: control, CUMS model, fluoxetine, Talbinah) showing Talbinah (barley porridge) restored CUMS-altered urinary serotonin, dopamine, and noradrenaline over 30 days. Limits: urinary monoamine biomarkers in rodents using multi-ingredient Talbinah porridge.
