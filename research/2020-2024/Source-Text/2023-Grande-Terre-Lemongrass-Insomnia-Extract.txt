Coverage: Original survey Methods and Tables 1 and 2

The original Grande-Terre ethnopharmacology survey first published February 2, 2023. Cymbopogon citratus accounts for two of 18 remedy reports in the combined stress and insomnia category. Table 2 records a bedtime leaf infusion in that plant's own row group. It also records a separate bedtime preparation for Lippia alba. These counts represent reported traditional use and instructions. The survey supplies no treated and control groups, measured change in insomnia or dream enactment, preparation using minced leaves, or Parkinson cohort.
