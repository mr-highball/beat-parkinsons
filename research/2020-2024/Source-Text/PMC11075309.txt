Title: Neuroinflammation is associated with Alzheimer's disease co-pathology in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11075309/
PMID: 38715119
PMCID: PMC11075309
DOI: 10.1186/s40478-024-01786-z
Publication date: 2024-05-07
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The study examines brain tissue markers of microglial and astrocytic activity across dementia with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer co-pathology. It relates microglial reactivity to amyloid and phosphorylated tau without measuring oral dried fig intake or visual event frequency.
