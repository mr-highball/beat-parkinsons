Coverage: Factual extract of the original article body. Round 1 food exposure and outcome scope.

The review describes barley fiber and diets containing barley, lentils as pulses, olive oil in dietary patterns and curcumin as a turmeric constituent. The quoted dietary and mechanistic findings cannot isolate a prepared food effect on the mapped Parkinson symptoms. The cited Tsang study of dark chocolate used an active chocolate comparator. Its original PANAS between-group results were inconclusive. Do not count the review as a replication. No studied dried-date fruit exposure appears in the screened text.
