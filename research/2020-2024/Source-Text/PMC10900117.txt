Title: Continuous subcutaneous foslevodopa/foscarbidopa infusion for the treatment of motor fluctuations in Parkinson's disease: Considerations for initiation and maintenance
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10900117/
PMID: 38419617
PMCID: PMC10900117
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2024.100239
Publication date: 2024-02-10
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

This clinical guidance describes continuous subcutaneous foslevodopa and foscarbidopa for motor fluctuations and draws on the existing trial reports. Hallucination discussion concerns dopaminergic treatment and adverse-event management. It does not report an independent food-exposure cohort.
