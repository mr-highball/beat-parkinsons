Title: Complementary and alternative medicines used by cancer patients to cope with chemotherapy-induced constipation.
PMID: 32379648
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctcp.2020.101108
First published: 2020
Authors: Toygar İ, Yeşilbalkan ÖU, Kürkütlü M, Aslan A
Journal: Complementary therapies in clinical practice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32379648/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional descriptive survey of n=214 adult Turkish chemotherapy patients evaluating CAM use for chemotherapy-induced constipation. Overall 31.8% used CAM (97.1% phytotherapy), and apricot was the single most frequently used agent (39.4%). Prevalence survey only; does not measure comparative symptom change or isolate PD constipation.
