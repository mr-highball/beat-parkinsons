Title: Standardized saffron stigma extract and Insomnia Severity Index in adults reporting poor sleep
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7849671/
PMID: 32056539
PMCID: PMC7849671
DOI: 10.5664/jcsm.8376
Publication year: 2020. Issue date: 2020-06-15.
Checked: 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-19
Coverage: Original full article factual extract, Methods, Table 2, Figure 2 and disclosures.

Lopresti and colleagues randomized 63 adults with poor sleep and analyzed 55. Diagnosed neurological disease, including Parkinson disease, was an exclusion. The intervention was a standardized oral extract from Crocus sativus stigmas, Affron, against matched placebo tablets. Methods document 14 mg per tablet twice daily for 28 days and a marker specification above 3.5% Lepticrosalides, a combined measure that includes safranal and crocin isomers. The research report's short summary has a conflicting duration. The Methods, measurement timeline, main abstract and prospective registration ACTRN12619000863134 support 28 days.

The primary Insomnia Severity Index model favored extract on the time-by-group interaction (F[1,53]=6.07, p=.017). Table 2 gives baseline and aggregate days 7–28 means of 15.75 and 11.74 in 28 extract recipients and 14.74 and 13.46 in 27 placebo recipients. The 11.74 is a follow-up average, not labeled a day-28 mean in Table 2. Diary sleep duration and latency were inconclusive between groups. The authors described largely self-reported outcomes. The extract supplier funded the project. The study tests a measured insomnia outcome with a processed stigma preparation and provides no whole-powder comparison or Parkinson insomnia estimate.
