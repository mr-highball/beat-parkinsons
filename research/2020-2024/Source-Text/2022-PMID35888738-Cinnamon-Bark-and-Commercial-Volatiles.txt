Title: Volatile composition of authenticated cinnamon bark and commercial preparations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9322727/
PMID: 35888738
PMCID: PMC9322727
DOI: 10.3390/metabo12070614
Publication date: 2022-07-01
Date basis: First electronic publication.
Review depth: Full PMC structured original. Methods and volatile-analysis results.

The investigators compared authenticated bark from cinnamon taxa and commercial products using multiple analytical methods. Headspace volatile analysis detected several aromatic alcohols. The authors did not detect linalool in their own samples and cited prior reports of linalool in C. verum oil. The reported nondetection applies to this method and sample set.

Round 1 claim 0243. The tested botanical samples vary from oils measured in other studies. Together these analyses establish variable occurrence, without a clinically interpretable dietary dose, transfer to anxiety or Parkinson symptom effect.
