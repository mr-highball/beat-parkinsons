Patent tables for the likely 2021 drowsiness crossover
Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/JP2023036051A/ja
Coverage: Original Japanese patent disclosure and corresponding US Tables 3–5

The Japanese patent JP2023036051A was published March 13, 2023. Its [US counterpart](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20230124668A1/en) followed on April 20. The authors, 24 randomized volunteers, dosing, four-week crossover periods and task schedule give strong reason to suspect overlap with the 2021 journal crossover, pending direct article verification. One dropout left 23 complete participants. In US Table 3, drowsiness VAS means after four weeks are 48.7 versus 61.0 before the workload and 71.7 versus 80.0 at two hours of workload (extract versus placebo, paired p<.05 at each point). The baseline-to-week-four change before workload in Table 4 is −8.7 versus +0.9 and has no significance mark. The narrative and tables use different dispersion labels. These are time-specific subjective ratings with sponsor involvement and multiple comparisons. A distinct [earlier patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/JP2021187785A/ja) reports a smaller parallel experiment. Trial identity remains unresolved for the crossover, so the 2021 report and this 2023 family should be counted once provisionally.
