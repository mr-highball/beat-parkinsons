Title: Portal vein thrombosis and abdominal pain after reported date seed powder consumption
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8075688/
PMID: 33959162
PMCID: PMC8075688
DOI: 10.1155/2021/6668722
Publication date: 2021-04-17
Date basis: Peer-reviewed article publication. A December 2020 manuscript belongs to the same case.
Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.06872
Reviewed: 2026-11-24 UTC
Review depth: Structured original case narrative and clinical findings.

An older adult reported taking date seed powder for chronic knee pain for about two weeks. New severe abdominal pain was graded 8/10 at presentation. CT showed portal-system thrombosis. The article describes a temporal association and hypothesizes a contribution from seed powder. No isolated effect on knee pain, rechallenge or population comparison was reported.

This observation concerns powdered seeds, a single case and a diagnosed acute condition. It provides no result for the flesh of dried dates in people with Parkinson disease.
