Original study scope
This retrospective analysis of an adult medical cannabis program examined longitudinal changes in controlled-substance prescriptions in people with chronic noncancer pain. Program enrollment and prescription use defined the main comparisons.

Continuing evidence gaps
The data are about cannabinoids and receipt of prescriptions. They do not isolate sage or culinary dates, or measure their effects on Parkinson pain or insomnia.
