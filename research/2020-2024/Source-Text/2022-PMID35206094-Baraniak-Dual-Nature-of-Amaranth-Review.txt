Comprehensive review (Baraniak & Kania-Dobrowolska, 2022, Foods, PMC8871380) covering nutritional, traditional Mesoamerican/Andean culinary-ceremonial heritage (Aztec, Mayan, Incan use of A. hypochondriacus, A. cruentus, and A. caudatus / kiwicha), and largely preclinical/metabolic literature on grain amaranth. Confirms absence of human Parkinson's disease symptom trials.
