Registry comparator composition for the saw palmetto urinary trial
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000052929
Registration: UMIN000046386
Coverage: Official Japanese registry disclosure and linked primary paper

The public UMIN record was disclosed June 1, 2022, last modified June 17, and lists completed status. It plans 50 middle-aged men with an IPSS primary outcome, randomized for 12 weeks to saw palmetto or placebo listed as polyethylene glycol-400. The linked original 2024 article reports 54 participants and olive-oil placebo capsules. The formulation discrepancy remains unresolved and the registration provides no direct result for extra-virgin olive oil. [Linked paper](https://ffhdj.com/index.php/ffhd/article/view/1312)
