Title: MRI data-driven clustering reveals different subtypes of Dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9859778/
PMID: 36670121
PMCID: PMC9859778
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-023-00448-6
Publication date: 2023-01-20
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The structural imaging analysis distinguishes data-driven atrophy patterns in dementia with Lewy bodies and compares their clinical profiles. The subtypes are grouped by imaging and diagnosis, with no dried fig intervention or prospective visual episode frequency outcome.
