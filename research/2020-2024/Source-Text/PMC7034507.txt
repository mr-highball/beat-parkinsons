Title: Cognition in multiple system atrophy: a single-center cohort study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7034507/
PMID: 32031752
PMCID: PMC7034507
DOI: 10.1002/acn3.50987

Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: Clinical multiple-system-atrophy cohort

This study characterizes cognitive and behavioral features across multiple system atrophy subtypes and evaluates the relationship to orthostatic hypotension. The examination and diagnosis differ from a food-timed Parkinson visual hallucination study.
