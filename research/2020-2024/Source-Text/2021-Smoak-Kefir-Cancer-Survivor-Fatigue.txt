Postexercise kefir and cancer survivor fatigue across two reports
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34519716/
PMID: 34519716
DOI: 10.1249/MSS.0000000000002690
Coverage: Original journal abstract and original institutional dissertation abstract

Smoak and colleagues followed 24 cancer survivors in a structured 12-week exercise program with kefir after three weekly sessions or exercise alone. The journal abstract reports fatigue improving 39.3% with p=.017, without identifying the exact statistical contrast or flavor. The institutional dissertation abstract describes closely matching methods and 19 participants, with a Piper fatigue group-by-time p=.002. The dissertation was created in 2019 and embargoed to December 2021. Overlapping authors and protocols suggest an earlier or overlapping cohort; it should not be counted as replication. A later review reports p=.030 for the journal cohort's interaction and p=.017 for pooled time, which remains unverified against the gated original tables. Nonrandomized assignment, exercise, lack of a sensory-matched placebo and no established plain formulation limit attribution. [Institutional dissertation record](https://digscholarship.unco.edu/dissertations/644/).
