Miller et al., PMID 33468271, DOI 10.1017/S0007114521000222, first online 2021-01-20. Registered NCT02051140. n=37 completers, 18 strawberry and 19 placebo, 24 g/day freeze-dried powder 90 days. No significant effect on Attention Network Test, task switching, trail making or digit span. Entry fall history excluded participants with falls in past year; FES-I fear of falling p=.518, not number of falls. No group gait, postural sway improvement.
