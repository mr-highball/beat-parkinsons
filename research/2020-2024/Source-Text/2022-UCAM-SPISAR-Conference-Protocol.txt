The abstract describes a planned randomized comparison of spinach leaf extract and exercise with a comparator among adults over 50. It states that recruitment had begun and lists balance among secondary outcomes. It provides no outcome estimate. Investigators, exposure, timeline and protocol scope match the registered SPISAR study, so this is part of the same trial family.
