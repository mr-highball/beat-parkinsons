Title: The efficacy of curcumin-piperine co-supplementation on clinical symptoms, duration, severity, and inflammatory factors in COVID-19 outpatients: a randomized double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 35668500
PMCID: PMC9167899
DOI: 10.1186/s13063-022-06375-w
First published: 2022
Authors: Askari G, Sahebkar A, Soleimani D, Mahdavi A, Rafiee S, Majeed M
Journal: Trials
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35668500/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

14-day double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (PMC9167899, IRCT20121216011763N46, n=46 COVID-19 outpatients) of 1,000 mg curcumin + 10 mg piperine daily vs placebo. Reported greater improvement in weakness (p=0.025) with no between-group difference on CRP or other clinical indices. Bundled curcumin-piperine in acute COVID-19.
