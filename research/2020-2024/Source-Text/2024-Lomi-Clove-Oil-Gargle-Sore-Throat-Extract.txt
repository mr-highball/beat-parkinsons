Lomi, Jena, Mitra and Misra. Effect of preoperative clove oil gargle on postoperative sore throat in adults after surgery. JCA Advances, 2024.
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2950553424000145
Access and coverage: Publisher primary abstract and study definitions. Independent hoarseness item counts or acoustic data were not accessible.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

The randomized trial assigned an oil gargle or tap-water gargle before anesthesia and tracheal intubation. One hundred thirty-four adults completed follow-up. At six hours, postoperative sore throat occurred in 26.9% versus 50.7%, with P=.0049. Grades 2 and 3 combined were less frequent at 24 hours (4.5% versus 16.4%, P=.0249). Grade 3 combines severe throat symptoms with hoarseness. These published outcomes concern postoperative throat morbidity. The exposure was gargled clove oil and there was no reported separate speech intelligibility, vocal sound pressure or Parkinson endpoint.
