S. A. Gutiérrez-Romero and colleagues. Effect of a nutraceutical combination on sleep quality among people with impaired sleep: a randomised, placebo-controlled trial. Scientific Reports 14:8062. Published April 5, 2024. PMID 38580720, PMCID PMC10997602, DOI 10.1038/s41598-024-58661-z.

Sixty-four adults with impaired sleep were randomized. Thirty-one active and 27 placebo participants completed six weeks. The active product included tea-derived L-theanine and lemon balm, valerian and saffron extracts. The comparator sachet used lemongrass, peppermint and mint flavors. Methods distinguish the flavorings from the active botanical ingredients. Participants dissolved the sachets in water. The report does not verify brewed Cymbopogon leaf or a dose of botanical lemongrass actives.

Figure 3 and Results report an inconclusive sleep-efficiency contrast (p = 0.49). Mean global PSQI changed −3.11 versus −3.86 points in the active and placebo arms (p = 0.41). Table 3 lists one nightmare adverse-event report among 35 active assignments and none among 29 placebo assignments. There is no clinical REM sleep behavior or dream-enactment measure. Specific sleep-disorder and neurological histories were excluded. NCT05609890 is the linked registry.
