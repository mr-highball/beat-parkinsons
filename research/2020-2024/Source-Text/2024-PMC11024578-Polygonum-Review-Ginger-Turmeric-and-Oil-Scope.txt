A customary preparation pairs ginger with Polygonum hydropiper and is described in a food-poisoning context. A separate antioxidant comparison uses extracts of kesum, ginger and turmeric. The coconut-oil passage mentions a fatty-acid source separately from Polygonum trials. The constipation indication in a plant-use table concerns Polygonum and does not give an effect for culinary ginger or coconut oil.
