Title: Effect of Aromatherapy on Postoperative Pain: Randomized Clinical Trial
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06294769
Registry: NCT06294769
Publication date: 2024-03-06
Date basis: First public posting.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27
Source depth: Official registry intervention and outcome fields.

Grapeseed vegetable oil applied as the carrier and comparison for lavender aromatherapy with usual postoperative care. The registry does not establish a cold-pressed preparation or an ingested oil arm.

The registry shows completed and had no posted results on the review date. It gives no direct comparison of cold-pressed grapeseed oil consumed orally for Parkinson pain. Related context for Round 1 claim 0338.
