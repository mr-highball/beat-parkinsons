Abstract 7 describes a small-expanded TBP allele with an SCA17 movement phenotype and visual hallucinations. Abstract 32 evaluates the pharmacologic pantothenate-kinase modulator BBP-671 in healthy volunteers. Blood acetyl-CoA is measured after the drug, without testing dietary pantothenate or dried figs. These are separate participants and outcomes.

The separate registry NCT04836494 lists the intended exposure as BBP-671, a synthetic kinase modulator, with safety and pharmacokinetic outcomes. No registry results were posted by the review date.

Abstract 88 compares tongue and pharyngeal swallowing pressure in 13 people with early Parkinson disease and 14 controls. Oral tongue pressure during ordinary saliva swallows differed (p=0.03), while the effortful swallowing task and pharyngeal comparisons were inconclusive. The study measures pressure.

Abstract 113 reports drooling in 38% of the Parkinson group with comorbid PTSD and 60% of the group without PTSD (p=0.04) from a retrospective series of 176 veterans. It records binary charted symptom presence. A separately published conference abstract has the same title and authors (DOI 10.1212/WNL.98.18_supplement.632). Exact sample overlap cannot be resolved from the available records. Neither presentation compares raw fig.
