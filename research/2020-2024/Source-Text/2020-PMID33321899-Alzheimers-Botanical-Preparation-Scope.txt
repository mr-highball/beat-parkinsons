Original study scope
The review covers Indian herbal medicine in Alzheimer disease, including cardamom as a formula member, cinnamon constituents, sage and turmeric. Winter cherry refers to ashwagandha. The asparagus reference names Asparagus racemosus rather than the culinary species. Date phrases indicate chronology.

Continuing evidence gaps
The reviewed formulas and species should remain separate. They give no matching frozen Parkinson efficacy estimate and should be linked to original botanical, preparation and symptom sources before transfer.
