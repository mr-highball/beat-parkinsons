Title: Neuropsychological Comparison of Patients With Alzheimer's Disease and Dementia With Lewy Bodies

The analysis compares neuropsychological profiles across 168 Alzheimer and 169 Lewy body dementia participants. The test scores and diagnostic groups supply no food exposure or measure of visual hallucination episode change.
