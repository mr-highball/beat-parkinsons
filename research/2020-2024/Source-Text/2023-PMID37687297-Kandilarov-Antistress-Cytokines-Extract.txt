Title: Effect of Plant Extracts Combinations on TNF-α, IL-6 and IL-10 Levels in Serum of Rats Exposed to Acute and Chronic Stress
PMID: 37687297
DOI: 10.3390/plants12173049
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/12/17/3049
First published: 2023-08-24 online. Plants 12(17):3049.
Coverage: Original body, results, assay figures, protocol and acknowledgments.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18 UTC

This report measures serum cytokines in acute cold stress and chronic unpredictable stress models. It compares two dry extract blends and separate plant extracts, including Melissa. Both blends reduced TNF-α and IL-6 in the chronic model compared with the stressed control. Antistress I also lowered IL-10 in that model. The single Melissa arm is not among the reported significant reductions for the three chronic cytokine comparisons. The text reports a nonsignificant tendency toward higher chronic IL-10 for Melissa alone. Blend results cannot be assigned to that arm.

The 2017 online behavioral article and this report share investigators, the Antistress formulations, chronic model details, the stated project number and extract supplier. A shared or overlapping chronic experiment remains possible. Keep the cytokine comparisons with their actual exposures and do not count them as independent replication of a depression effect. The acute cold-stress group is a separate model. The report gives no clinical depressive severity result.
