Krikorian et al., PMID 37892506, DOI 10.3390/nu15204431, 2023-10-19, NCT02751866. Randomized double-blind placebo control, 13 g/day freeze-dried whole-fruit strawberry powder for 12 weeks in overweight middle-aged adults with subjective cognitive difficulties. CVLT intrusion scores lower with strawberry after baseline adjustment, F(1,27)=5.69 p=.02. This is interference during learning and recall, interpreted by authors as executive control, not a specific attention task or Parkinson outcome. Primary https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/15/20/4431.
