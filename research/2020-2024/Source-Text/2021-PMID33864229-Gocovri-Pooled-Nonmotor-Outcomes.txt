Title: Effects of Gocovri (Amantadine) Extended Release Capsules on Non-Motor Symptoms in Patients with Parkinson's Disease and Dyskinesia.
PMID: 33864229
PMCID: PMC8140024
DOI: 10.1007/s40120-021-00246-3
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8140024/
First publication date: 2021-04-17 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

The pooled post hoc analysis uses EASE LID and EASE LID 3 data, NCT02136914 and NCT02274766. At week 12 the MDS-UPDRS hallucinations and psychosis item was 0.2 points higher after Gocovri than after placebo in the adjusted analysis (P < 0.001). The scored item combines experiences and cannot be treated as an independent food effect or a count of visual episodes. This analysis overlaps the parent randomized trial populations, which also contributed extension participants.
