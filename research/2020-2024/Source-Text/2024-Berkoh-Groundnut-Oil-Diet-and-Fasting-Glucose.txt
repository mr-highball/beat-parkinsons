Title: Effect of Tropical Vegetable Oil Consumption on Lipid Profile and Glycaemic Control in Type 2 Diabetics
DOI: 10.3923/tmr.2024.274.284
Publication date: 2024-07
Citation: Berkoh and colleagues. Trends in Medical Research 19, 274–284.
Source: https://space.uenr.edu.gh/items/aa611d4f-0670-45c7-bea5-203a14b11a16
Text coverage: Institutional author abstract and issue information. The linked full-body download timed out during this checkpoint.

Sixty-two outpatients with type 2 diabetes on metformin took part in an open prospective dietary comparison. The groundnut-oil arm numbered 16 and the group without supplemental oils numbered eight. Two other arms received palm or coconut oil. The authors report no significant fasting-glucose change from baseline to week four across the groups. Groundnut-arm triglycerides rose from baseline (within-arm p=.020). The abstract does not identify pressing grade or describe clinical episodes of low glucose, shaking or resting tremor.
