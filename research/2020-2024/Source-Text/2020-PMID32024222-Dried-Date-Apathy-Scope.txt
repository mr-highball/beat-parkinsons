The ICICLE-PD cohort used repeated caregiver-based neuropsychiatric ratings after Parkinson diagnosis. The analysis reports apathy in 24.7% of the Parkinson group versus 5.6% of controls at one analyzed visit. This observational clinical comparison does not test dates.
