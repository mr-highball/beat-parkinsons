Title: Synergistic Pain-Reducing Effects of Bixa orellana (Chronic® and Chronic In®) and Cannabidiol-Rich Cannabis sativa Extracts in Experimental Pain Models.
PMID: 39770552
PMCID: PMC11678258
DOI: 10.3390/ph17121710
First published: 2024
Authors: Santos, Carvalho, Gonçalves, Gomes, Colares
Journal: Pharmaceuticals (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39770552/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled Wistar rat pain study evaluating oral granulated Bixa orellana seed oil (Chronic, 72.0 +/- 1.0% delta-tocotrienol; 400 mg/kg p.o.) and injectable nanodispersion (Chronic In, 10 mg/kg i.m.) alone and combined with CBD-rich Cannabis sativa extract across acetic-acid writhing, formalin, hot-plate, and cold-water tail withdrawal tests. Isolated oral Chronic Bixa orellana oil produced statistically significant pain reductions (p < 0.001) in writhing and both phases of the formalin test vs water control, though smaller than the CBD combination. Limits: concentrated seed oil extract in acute rat pain models.
