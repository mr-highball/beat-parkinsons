Coverage: factual extract of two fatigue endpoints from Supplementary File 4, with original public registry linked.

The acute double-blind crossover used 300 mg mango leaf extract or placebo in generally healthy adult participants (114 in the study summary). The main intervention effect gave F=1.04, p=.309 for mental fatigue on a task-related visual scale, and the intervention-by-time term gave F=1.18, p=.308. For POMS Fatigue–Inertia, the corresponding findings were F=.86, p=.354 and F=.40, p=.669. Means (SE) for POMS Fatigue–Inertia at 300 minutes were 42.45 (.60) with extract and 43.30 (.59) with placebo. Mental Fatigue in repetition 1 had means 69.81 (1.58) and 68.85 (1.59), respectively, at that time. The comparison concerns leaf extract in a cognitive challenge, with no person with Parkinson disease or mango flesh contrast identified.
