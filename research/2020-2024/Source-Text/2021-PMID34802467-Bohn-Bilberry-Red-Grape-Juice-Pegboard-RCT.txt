Study design and findings
- 9-week double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled dietary intervention in 60 older men (age >= 67 years) with subjective memory impairment assigned to a 50/50 bilberry and red grape juice blend or an isocaloric placebo juice.
- Outcomes: Cambridge Cognition Test Battery (CANTAB) visual memory, Grooved Pegboard hand dexterity and motor speed tests, and plasma inflammation/tissue-damage/polyphenol biomarkers.
- Behavioral result (NULL): Compared with placebo, Grooved Pegboard hand dexterity, motor speed, and selected CANTAB memory scores were unaffected by the bilberry/red grape juice intervention.
- Biomarkers: Plasma LDH decreased (median 362 to 346 U/L), inflammatory markers (EGF, IL-6, IL-9, IL-10, TNF-alpha) declined significantly more vs placebo, and plasma p-coumaric acid rose 116% (2.2 to 4.7 uM/L).

Relevance and limits for Parkinson's food experiments
- Direct randomized double-blind human null trial on Grooved Pegboard hand dexterity relevant to 0712 (raw grape / hand dexterity impairment).
- Limits: Mixed 50/50 bilberry and red grape juice rather than isolated raw grape; non-Parkinson older men with subjective memory impairment.
