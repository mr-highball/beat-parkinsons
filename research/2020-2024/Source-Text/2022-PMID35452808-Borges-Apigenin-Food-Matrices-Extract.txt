The food comparisons include a parsley drink, parsley in
yogurt and chamomile tea. For circulating apigenin-4′-
glucuronide the authors report peaks at about four, six
and two hours, respectively. The paper also reports
urinary metabolites after isolated apigenin. The paper
distinguishes compound forms, food matrices and metabolite
identities.

The additional parsley urine study describes 11 healthy men.
Repeated tests and reused earlier observations give 17
intakes. NCT03526081 reports overall registered enrollment
of 17. The repeated-intake denominator is distinct from
the number of people. Neither this experiment nor the
registry records celery stalk, Parkinson or resting tremor.
