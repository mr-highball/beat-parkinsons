Title: Clinical characteristics of Lewy body dementia in Chinese memory clinics
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8010972/
PMID: 33789600
PMCID: PMC8010972
DOI: 10.1186/s12883-021-02169-w
Publication date: 2021-03-31
Source depth: PMC original full body from structured article text; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC article metadata and PubMed.
Design: Multicenter memory-clinic record review

Nine Chinese memory clinics contributed 8,405 records, including 455 patients with Lewy body dementia. The report compares clinical characteristics, cognition and imaging findings across diagnoses. The analysis does not establish a temporal culinary exposure.
