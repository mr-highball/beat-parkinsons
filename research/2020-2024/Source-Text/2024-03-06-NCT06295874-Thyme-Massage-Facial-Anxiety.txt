Thyme oil in a massage blend and a registered facial anxiety scale
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06295874
Registration: NCT06295874
Coverage: Official registry protocol arms, descriptions and posted results status

The intensive-care registry compares massage with a lavender, thyme and eucalyptus oil blend in almond oil against massage with almond carrier alone. It registers a Facial Anxiety Scale, which rates anxiety by matching an expression category, alongside pain and comfort measures. The scale addresses anxiety. The protocol gives no Parkinson hypomimia endpoint, consumption of fresh thyme or thyme-only contrast. No results were posted when checked November 16, 2026.
