Title: Sleep and physical activity in healthy 8-9-year-old children are affected by oily fish consumption in the FiSK Junior randomized trial
PMID: 33515093
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-021-02490-7
First published: 2021
Authors: Vuholm S, Teisen MN, Mølgaard C, Lauritzen L, Damsgaard CT
Journal: European Journal of Nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33515093/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Pre-specified exploratory sleep/activity report from FiSK Junior (NCT02809508; n=199 healthy Danish 8-9-year-olds randomized to ~300 g/week pooled oily fish [salmon, herring, mackerel, trout] vs poultry for 12 weeks). Accelerometry showed lower weekly sleep-duration variability (-6.0 min, 95% CI 0.8 to 11.1) and slightly shorter weekend sleep-onset latency (-3.6 min, 95% CI 1.0 to 6.3) vs poultry, alongside higher sedentary time (+9.4 min/day). Does not isolate herring, enrolls healthy children without insomnia, and contrasts with null sleep findings in FINS-KIDS (PMC6213752).
