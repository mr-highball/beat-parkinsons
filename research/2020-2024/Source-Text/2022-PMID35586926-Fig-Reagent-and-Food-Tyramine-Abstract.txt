Title: Quantification of tyramine in different types of food using novel green synthesis of ficus carica quantum dots as fluorescent probe
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35586926/
PMID: 35586926
DOI: 10.1002/bio.4291
Publication date: 2022-05-31 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: original abstract via NLM; full text not verified

The study uses fig fruit to make a fluorescent probe and applies it to aged cheese, cured meat, sausage, pickled olives and canned fish. The authors report a calibration range of 5–400 ng/mL and a quantitation limit of 1.68 ng/mL. Those values describe the method. The abstract gives no fig tyramine concentration or clinical outcome.

The individual food concentration table and fig cultivar or processing basis require the full paper. Upstream search accession and DOI were checked with NLM and the publisher route. The paper is kept at abstract depth until the complete original can be checked.
