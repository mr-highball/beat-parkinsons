Title: An Elderly Man With Chaotic Sleep Behaviors and Rapidly Progressive Ataxia.
PMID: 36118510
PMCID: PMC9464990
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13537
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9464990/
First publication date: 2022-09-11 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: Official PMC complete article page, including body and displayed study observations.

The case describes unusual sleep behavior, rapid ataxia, cognitive decline and visual hallucinations during a progressive neurological illness. It supplies a differential-diagnosis and temporal context. No dried fig intake or Parkinson food comparison was recorded.
