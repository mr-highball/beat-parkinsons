Title: Nanoemulsion-based piperine to enhance bioavailability for the treatment of LPS-induced depression-like behaviour in mice.
PMID: 37591360
DOI: 10.1016/j.neulet.2023.137441
First published: 2023
Authors: Basavaraju SM, Mudhol S, Peddha MS, Ud Din Wani S, Krishna KL, Mehdi S
Journal: Neuroscience letters
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37591360/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse experiment testing oral nanoemulsion-encapsulated piperine (5 and 10 mg/kg/d x 14 d) in LPS-induced depression-like behavior. Reversed LPS-induced immobility in TST and FST while increasing hippocampal BDNF and monoamines. Limits: engineered nanopiperine formulation in acute rodent LPS model.
