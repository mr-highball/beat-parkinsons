Title: Randomized controlled trial of sulforaphane and metabolite discovery in children with Autism Spectrum Disorder.
PMID: 34034808
PMCID: PMC8146218
DOI: 10.1186/s13229-021-00447-5
Registry ID: NCT02561481
First published: 2021
Authors: Zimmerman AW, Singh K, Connors SL, Liu H, Panjwani AA, Lee LC
Journal: Molecular autism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8146218/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Fifteen-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (NCT02561481) in n=57 randomized (n=45 analyzed: 22 SF vs 23 placebo) children aged 3-12 y with ASD. Primary clinician OACIS was non-significant (d=0.21, 95% CI -0.46 to 0.88), while secondary caregiver Aberrant Behavior Checklist improved (d=-0.96, 95% CI -1.73 to -0.15). Well tolerated; non-serious side effects included rare insomnia, irritability, and taste/smell intolerance.
