Title: Four odorants for olfactory training are enough: a pilot study.
PMID: 39242414
PMCID: PMC11564259
DOI: 10.1007/s00405-024-08930-4
First published: 2024
Authors: Power Guerra N, Kruschwitz E, Krautwurst D, Hummel T
Journal: European archives of oto-rhino-laryngology : official journal of the European Federation of Oto-Rhino-Laryngological Societies (EUFOS) : affiliated with the German Society for Oto-Rhino-Laryngology - Head and Neck Surgery
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39242414/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled 3-month olfactory-training pilot in n=40 patients with olfactory dysfunction and n=60 normosmic controls comparing 7-odorant training (adding stewed apple, balm, and mint to rose, lemon, eucalyptus, clove) vs standard 4-odorant training. Both training arms improved TDI scores similarly, with no added olfactory benefit from the 3 extra odorants including stewed apple; inhaled odorant training, not ingested baked apple, and not a PD cohort.
