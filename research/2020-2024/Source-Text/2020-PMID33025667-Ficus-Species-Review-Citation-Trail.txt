Title: Ficus plants: State of the art from a phytochemical, pharmacological, and toxicological perspective
PMID: 33025667
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.6884
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ptr.6884
NLM record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33025667/
Publication year: 2020 (online publication; journal issue 2021).
Coverage: Publisher abstract and visible bibliography, with the linked Parkinson-model article reviewed separately.

The review spans multiple Ficus species and plant parts. Its bibliography cites an oral petroleum ether leaf-extract experiment under Ficus religiosa, not the common fig Ficus carica. The primary paper uses rodent motor measures such as catalepsy and rotarod time. This citation supplies a botanical distinction even though the broader review title includes anti-Parkinson activity.

The bibliography separately identifies common-fig food chemistry, fresh fruit allergy and a functional-constipation paste trial. These publications concern different exposures and endpoints. The review record supplies no original dried-fig assessment of visual hallucinations.
