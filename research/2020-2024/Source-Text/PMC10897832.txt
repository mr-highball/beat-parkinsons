In the published article, there was an error in Figure 2A as published. In Figure 2A the P value of the placebo group is wrongly written as P < 0.001. The corrected Figure 2 and its caption appear below.

Figure 2 Average difference between sleep measures derived by wrist actigraphy. (A) sleep efficiency, (B) WASO, (C) Sleep onset latency, (D) total sleep time. Values are expressed as Mean ± SD. A “P” value less than 0.05 (P < 0.05) is considered as statistically significant.

The authors apologize for this error and state that this does not change the scientific conclusions of the article in any way. The original article has been updated.
