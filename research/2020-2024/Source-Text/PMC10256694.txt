Title: Substantia nigral dopamine transporter uptake in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10256694/
PMID: 37296236
PMCID: PMC10256694
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-023-00534-9
Publication date: 2023-06-09
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The DLB and mild cognitive impairment imaging analysis relates lower regional dopamine transporter uptake to recorded hallucination status and other clinical features. Association with occurrence is distinct from change in episode frequency after eating, and food exposure is not measured.
