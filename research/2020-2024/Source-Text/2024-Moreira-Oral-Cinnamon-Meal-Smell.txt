Oral ground cinnamon and ratings of meal smell
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11463819/
PMID: 39383145
PMCID: PMC11463819
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0311501
Coverage: original journal Methods and Table 3, full body

Moreira and colleagues published this randomized crossover on October 9, 2024. Nineteen men with type 2 diabetes completed meal sessions with ground Cinnamomum cassia in water or capsules and a control meal. After eating, participants rated breakfast-smell pleasantness. Table 3 reports medians of 7.8 for control, 7.3 and 6.9 for the two powder sessions, and 7.3 and 7.9 for the capsule sessions. The authors report all palatability comparisons as p at least .80. The endpoint measures response to the meal and provides no detection threshold, clinical olfactory impairment score or Parkinson comparison. The journal cites ReBEC RBR-98tx28b. Its current registration details were not verified separately in this packet.
