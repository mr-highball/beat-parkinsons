Original source scope
The review links flax-derived omega-3 plus vitamin E to a prior randomized Parkinson study reporting total UPDRS. Raw oats and unrefined barley are listed as dietary fiber sources. A separate probiotic comparison describes abdominal pain and defecation.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The cited flax intervention uses a combination, has no reported cold-press grade, and does not give separate matched outcomes for the frozen scopes. Fiber-source examples leave cooked oats for dyskinesia and boiled barley for anxiety unresolved. No dried date fruit and pain comparison.
