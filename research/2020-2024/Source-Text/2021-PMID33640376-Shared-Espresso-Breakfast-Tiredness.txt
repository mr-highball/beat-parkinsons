Title: Italian breakfast in mind: The effect of caffeine, carbohydrate and protein on physiological state, mood and cognitive performance
Source: https://ricerca.uniba.it/bitstream/11586/381886/5/1-s2.0-S0031938421000639-main_.pdf
PMID: 33640376
DOI: 10.1016/j.physbeh.2021.113371
Publication date: 2021-02-26
Citation: Manippa V, Lupo R, Tommasi L, Brancucci A. Physiology & Behavior 234:113371.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete university-deposited primary article, including design and Table 2.

The crossover enrolled 40 young adults. Both sessions included bitter espresso and either a whole-wheat or a protein croissant. The assigned comparison concerns the croissant. Pooled tiredness on the visual analogue scale was 50.31 before breakfast and 42.52 afterward (reported p=.030). The article did not identify a tiredness difference by croissant type. It has no coffee-free breakfast or time control and does not establish a separate caffeine or coffee effect. The study enrolled no Parkinson participants and measured short-term tiredness.
