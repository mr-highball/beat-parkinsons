Title: Impact of black seed oil on hemodialysis patients
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05822661
Trial registration: NCT05822661
Publication date: 2023-04-21
Date basis: First posted in ClinicalTrials.gov.
Source depth: Trial registration with no posted results on 2026-11-27.

The registry describes black seed oil versus placebo in patients receiving hemodialysis, with estimated enrollment of 60. Endothelin-1 and oxidative-stress markers are listed alongside quality of life and fatigue severity. A separate depression or HADS outcome is not listed. The last posted update on April 21, 2023 leaves the current recruitment status unknown.

This is a distinct registration from the published Nigella hemodialysis cohort that planned HADS. It documents a different planned comparison and supplies no observed change in fatigue or depressive symptoms. Black seed oil administered to patients undergoing hemodialysis also differs from the frozen toasted-seed exposure in Parkinson disease.
