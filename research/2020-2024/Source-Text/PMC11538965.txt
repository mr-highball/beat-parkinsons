The published review reports 29 articles and 1,367 participants, of whom 514 had psychosis and 853 did not. It shares PROSPERO CRD42020221904 with the 2022 preprint. Imaging comparisons include heterogeneous hallucination and delusion definitions and no dried fig ingestion outcome.
