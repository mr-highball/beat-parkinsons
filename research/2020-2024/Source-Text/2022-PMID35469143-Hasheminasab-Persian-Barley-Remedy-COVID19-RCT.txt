Title: Efficacy of the barley-based remedy, a Persian medicine formula, in coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) hospitalized patients: An open-labeled randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 35469143
PMCID: PMC9020837
DOI: 10.1016/j.aimed.2022.04.001
First published: 2022
Authors: Hasheminasab FS, Azimi M, Khodadoost M, Chouban B, Shakeri N, Ghasemi S
Journal: Advances in integrative medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35469143/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Open-label randomized controlled trial (PMC9020837, n=70 randomized, n=65 analyzed) in hospitalized COVID-19 patients comparing conventional care plus a 3-herb traditional Persian decoction (Hordeum vulgare + Ziziphus jujuba + Cordia myxa) vs conventional care alone. Reported lower VAS fatigue on days 3-6 (p=0.01-0.03). Limits: bundled 3-herb decoction cannot isolate barley; open-label acute viral illness, non-PD.
