SHAPE dissertation sleep tables and comparator identity
Source: https://scholarworks.uark.edu/etd/3853/
Registration: NCT03603041
Coverage: University dissertation identity, original Chapter 5 Methods and Table 7 checked in a publicly indexed copy

The December 2020 Hawley dissertation identifies soybean oil capsules in the whey plus placebo fat arm. Thirty-nine women completed the study procedures. In Table 7, mean global Pittsburgh sleep scores changed from 7.7 to 6.3 in the whey-plus-soybean arm and from 6.7 to 4.6 in the whey-only arm. The table leaves a dedicated adjusted comparison between those two arms unresolved. Oil refining is unspecified. This postmenopausal sleep trial does not provide a Parkinson insomnia result. The original body, published 2020 abstract and 2021 follow-up consistently identify soybean oil despite the earlier corn label.
