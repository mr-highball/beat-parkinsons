Title: Characterizing the heterogeneous metabolic progression in idiopathic REM sleep behavior disorder
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7322340/
PMID: 32570206
PMCID: PMC7322340
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2020.102294
Publication date: 2020-05-26
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: Metabolic PET comparison across sleep and movement phenotypes

The cohort comprises 19 people with idiopathic REM sleep behavior disorder, 38 with Parkinson disease and 19 controls. Relative metabolic imaging differences and inferred disease trajectories are the endpoints, distinct from visual hallucination frequency following ingestion.
