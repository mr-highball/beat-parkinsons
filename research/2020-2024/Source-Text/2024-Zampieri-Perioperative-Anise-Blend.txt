Perioperative tea blend measured anxiety after surgery
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39514279/
PMID: 39514279
Coverage: factual extract from primary report abstract

Victória Cosel Zampieri and colleagues. Journal of Medicinal Food 28(3):266–271, first online November 8, 2024, issue 2025. DOI 10.1089/jmf.2024.0170.
The researchers randomized 210 adults in an elective surgery context to a tea containing chamomile, passionflower and Pimpinella anisum or a lemon-flavored comparison tea. The primary abstract describes a postoperative anxiety assessment using DASS-21, with no statistically significant anxiety difference; a positive sentiments result concerned perception of surgery. Describing the intervention as a sedative tea in the abstract did not establish measured sleepiness or sleep physiology. No individually assigned anise exposure or ground seed comparator was reported. Per-arm preparation and formal general adverse-event ascertainment have not been verified from full text.
