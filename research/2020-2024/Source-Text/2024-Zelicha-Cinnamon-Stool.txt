Source: https://ajcn.nutrition.org/article/S0002-9165%2824%2900008-X/fulltext
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2024.01.008
First online: 2024-01-25
Format: Original methods and results, paraphrased.
Design: Eighteen adults with obesity and prediabetes took 4 g/day of Cinnamomum burmannii powder capsules or matched maltodextrin placebo for four weeks each with a two-week washout.
Outcome: Daily stool count was included in digestive symptom logging. The main results did not find a clear treatment-by-time contrast for stool counts. Supplemental arm values were not recovered here. Clinical constipation and preparation transfer remain unresolved.
