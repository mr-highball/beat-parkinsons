Title: The behavioral effects of gestational and lactational benzo[a]pyrene exposure vary by sex and genotype in mice with differences at the Ahr and Cyp1a2 loci
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8763354/
PMID: 34890772
PMCID: PMC8763354
DOI: 10.1016/j.ntt.2021.107056
Publication date: 2021-12-07
Text coverage: Distilled full author manuscript including vehicle, rotarod outcome, and group interpretation.

The developing-mouse comparison administered the tested pollutant in corn-oil-treated cereal to exposed animals and the same vehicle to controls. Investigators measured latency to fall or slip from a rotarod. The paper describes shorter latencies in exposed female mice and other outcomes that depend on sex and genotype. Corn oil was shared across the exposure contrast. Rotarod times do not give everyday fall frequency, an oil-only comparison, or a Parkinson clinical result.
