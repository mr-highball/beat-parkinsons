Title: Behavioral and Neurochemical Effects of Extra Virgin Olive Oil Total Phenolic Content and Sideritis Extract in Female Mice
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/1420-3049/25/21/5000
PMID: 33126727
PMCID: PMC7663189
DOI: 10.3390/molecules25215000
First public: 2020-10-28
Coverage: Factual extract from the original methods and Figure 2 results.

In the two EVOO phenolic-extract arms of a five-group study, female mice received 10 or 30 mg/kg by intraperitoneal injection for seven days. The other arms tested separate Sideritis extracts or vehicle. Figure 2 reports 7–9 animals per group. Both olive phenolic doses increased entries into the center of the open field compared with vehicle (p=.020 and .024), and both increased vertical activity (p=.001 for each comparison). The lower dose also increased total horizontal counts (p=.001). The mobility effects make it hard to assign increased center visits to anxiety alone. This was a phenolic fraction, a nonculinary route and a mouse exploration assay without a Parkinson model.
