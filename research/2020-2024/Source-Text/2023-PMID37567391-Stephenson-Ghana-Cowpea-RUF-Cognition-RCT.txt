Title: Effect of Peanut Paste-based Ready-to-use School Meals With and Without Milk on Fluid Cognition in Northern Ghana: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 37567391
PMCID: PMC10579044
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2023.08.001
Registry ID: NCT04349007
First published: 2023
Authors: Stephenson KB, Wegner DR, Hershey TG, Doty T, Davis E, Steiner-Asiedu M, et al.
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37567391/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

One-year randomized controlled trial (Stephenson et al., 2023, Am J Clin Nutr, PMC10579044, NCT04349007) in 871 rural Ghanaian schoolchildren (n=795 endline) comparing peanut/milk ready-to-use food (PM-RUF), peanut/cowpea RUF (PC-RUF, n=292), and fortified millet porridge (FP, n=297) on NIH Toolbox fluid cognition tests. Compared with FP, PC-RUF showed NO significant improvement on executive function (DCCS), attention/inhibitory control (FICA), processing speed (PCPS), or working memory (LSWM) (only PM-RUF improved DCCS and PCPS). Limits: pediatric school-meal RCT bundling cowpea with peanut paste; null cognitive result.
