Hooper et al., online June 14, 2021. PMID 34126996; PMCID PMC8204440; DOI 10.1186/s12970-021-00449-x.
Thirteen resistance-trained men completed a crossover of proprietary tart cherry extract powder capsules and rice-flour placebo. At 24 hours grip change was +1.7 kg with extract and −2.0 kg with placebo, p=.017. The soreness comparisons and pairwise jump comparisons were inconclusive. The authors described central-fatigue interpretation as speculative; no perceived fatigue scale is reported. Sponsor supplied the capsules.
