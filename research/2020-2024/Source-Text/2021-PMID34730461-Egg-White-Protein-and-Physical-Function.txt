Title: Egg-white protein supplementation and physical function in older women
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/02601060211051592
Source abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34730461/
PMID: 34730461
DOI: 10.1177/02601060211051592
Registry: NCT03530774
Evidence date: 2021-11-03
Date basis: First online journal publication, before December 2022 issue.
Review basis: Publisher and NLM original author abstract, linked to official registry. The full article is restricted, and direct retrieval of the publisher supplement returned HTTP 403.
Source type: Randomized double-blind human pilot trial.

The original report describes 29 community-dwelling older women with low strength or function. Sixteen received dried egg-white protein and 13 an isocaloric maltodextrin supplement for six months. The authors identify no clear between-group differences among published abstract outcomes. Protein intake, handgrip and arm curls improved within the egg-white group. These within-arm findings do not isolate a supplement effect. Under-recruitment and dropout limited the comparisons.

The registry specifies a fall-frequency and falls-efficacy secondary outcome and has no posted results. Fall counts, attrition by arm for that outcome, and the relationship between the registry's 54 enrolled and the publication's 29 randomized women remain unresolved. The source does not assign poached eggs or enroll on the basis of Parkinson diagnosis. Keep it as a single linked family for claim 0329.
