Original study scope
In the clinical MDS Non-Motor Rating Scale study, participants with Parkinson disease were measured for nonmotor symptoms and fluctuations. Medication and rating characteristics are distinct from a culinary exposure. Temporal dates and references containing Sage do not represent measured foods.

Continuing evidence gaps
No dry date or culinary sage comparisons were assigned for the nine mapped questions.
