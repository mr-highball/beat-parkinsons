Title: Protective effect of Vigna unguiculata extract against aging and neurodegeneration.
PMID: 33024055
PMCID: PMC7732273
DOI: 10.18632/aging.104069
First published: 2020
Authors: Tripodi F, Lombardi L, Guzzetti L, Panzeri D, Milanesi R, Leri M, et al.
Journal: Aging
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33024055/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Multi-model preclinical aging and neurodegeneration study (Tripodi et al., 2020, Aging, PMC7732273) evaluating aqueous extract of 1-hour boiled Vigna unguiculata (black-eyed pea/cowpea) seeds. Extract inhibited recombinant alpha-synuclein fibrillization in vitro, lowered alpha-synuclein toxicity and membrane binding in yeast and SH-SY5Y cells, extended yeast chronological lifespan and Drosophila melanogaster lifespan (40.09 vs 31.82 days at 0.2%), and reduced age-dependent cephalic dopaminergic neuron loss in a C. elegans human alpha-synuclein PD model (mean 3/4 neurons lost in mock vs 2/4 at 0.2% and 1/4 at 0.5%). Limits: cellular and invertebrate survival/neurodegeneration models only; no mammalian or human motor, behavioral, or clinical Parkinson's symptom endpoints were assessed.
