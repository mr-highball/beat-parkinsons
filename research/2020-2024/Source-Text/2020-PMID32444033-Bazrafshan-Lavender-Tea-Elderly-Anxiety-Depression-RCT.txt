Title: The effect of lavender herbal tea on the anxiety and depression of the elderly: A randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 32444033
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2020.102393
First published: 2020
Authors: Bazrafshan MR, Jokar M, Shokrpour N, Delam H
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32444033/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single-blind randomized controlled trial (Bazrafshan et al., 2020, Complement Ther Med) in 60 older adults (30 intervention, 30 no-placebo control) in Larestan, Iran. Intervention participants brewed 2 g Lavandula tea bags as a decoction twice daily (morning and night) for 2 weeks. Compared with control, brewed lavender tea reduced Beck Depression Inventory scores (post-intervention 16.33+/-1.49 vs 18.33+/-1.84; MD -2.00, 95% CI -2.86 to -1.13, p<0.001) and Spielberger State-Trait Anxiety Inventory scores (state MD -6.40, 95% CI -9.43 to -3.36; trait MD -4.13, 95% CI -7.66 to -0.60). Limits: single-blind with no placebo tea control, short 2-week duration, community elderly rather than diagnosed Parkinson's disease.
