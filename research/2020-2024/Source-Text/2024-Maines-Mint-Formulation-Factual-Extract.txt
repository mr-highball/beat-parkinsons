Title: Factual extract of Maines et al. 2024 oral formulation.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39097116/

Maines and colleagues first published this report in August 2024. The formulation study tested carbidopa and levodopa oral solutions with several flavors including mint. Long-term follow-up concerns two children with sepiapterin reductase deficiency. The indexed abstract provides no mint species or individual flavor assignment, and does not report daily OFF time in Parkinson disease. The repository full publisher text is restricted.
