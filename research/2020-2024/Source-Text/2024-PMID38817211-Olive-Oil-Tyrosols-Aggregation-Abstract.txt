The original abstract compares isolated olive-oil polyphenols and related molecules in a cellular aggregation model and a C. elegans model. The authors report lower α-synuclein aggregation in worms exposed to selected compounds. Across the tested compounds, effects differed.
Continuing gap: The accessible abstract and publisher metadata give aggregation and membrane-permeability outcomes. The full original comparison and any separately measured motor result remain unverified. Purified compounds are a different exposure from ordinary cold-pressed olive oil, and the abstract contains no clinical PD bradykinesia comparison.
