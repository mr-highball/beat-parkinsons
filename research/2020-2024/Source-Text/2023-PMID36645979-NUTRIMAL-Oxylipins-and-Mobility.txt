Title: Oxylipin status before and after long-chain omega-3 supplementation and skeletal muscle biology in older adults at risk of sarcopenia
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36645979/
PMID: 36645979
DOI: 10.1016/j.plefa.2022.102531
Publication date: 2023-01-14
Text coverage: Distilled author abstract. Compared the reported control recipe and NUTRIMAL identity with the main trial.

This exploratory analysis correlated baseline oxylipins with measures of muscle biology and physical function in a 49-participant NUTRIMAL subset. The parent randomized trial supplied the intervention data. The comparator drink combined high-oleic sunflower and corn oil. Baseline examples include a positive association of 12-HETrE with gait speed (r = 0.38, p = 0.007) and a negative association with single-leg stance duration (r = −0.39, p = 0.006). Those associations refer to different endpoints at baseline. They do not estimate an oil effect. The abstract reports no relationship between changes in measured oxylipins and changes in skeletal muscle parameters.

The abstract describes an inverse relationship between 5-HETrE and several endpoints, yet prints a positive handgrip coefficient of +0.48. The printed sign is preserved pending verification of the original table. This report is a secondary analysis of NCT03429491, without a separate fall-frequency or Parkinson outcome.
