Title: Imaging tau burden in dementia with Lewy bodies using [18F]-AV1451 positron emission tomography
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8209140/
PMID: 33631469
PMCID: PMC8209140
DOI: 10.1016/j.neurobiolaging.2020.11.006
Publication date: 2020-11-14
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: PubMed first electronic date. PMC structured metadata carries the 2021 issue date.
Design: Tau PET and clinical dementia comparison

The imaging sample comprises 10 people with dementia with Lewy bodies, 14 healthy controls and 27 in the Alzheimer group. The cited pathology literature discusses visual hallucination occurrence. The study measures tracer patterns and cognition, without an ingestion-linked visual outcome.
