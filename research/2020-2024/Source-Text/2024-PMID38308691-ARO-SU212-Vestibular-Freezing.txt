The proposed recruitment is 64 Parkinson participants. The preliminary comparison in the abstract covers 13 freezers and 20 nonfreezers. Its assessments use a freezing provocation protocol and MDS-UPDRS items 2.13 and 3.11. The investigators report lower vestibulo-ocular reflex gain among freezers at 0.32 Hz during rotary testing. The abstract gives no numerical gain difference, uncertainty estimate or adjusted result. Imaging and independence from dopaminergic denervation remain later analyses. The comparison concerns vestibular performance by existing freezing status. No fresh lemon balm comparison or exposure-induced change in freezing episodes was measured.

The adjacent SU211 diagnostic study uses exploratory eye and head movement data in 16 Parkinson participants and seven controls. It has a different endpoint and is not merged with SU212. Cohort overlap between the two is unresolved.
