Akgor MC et al. 2023-10-12, J Clin Med 12:6488, PMID 37892628, PMC10607881, DOI 10.3390/jcm12206488. Full primary XML Methods and Results retrieved. Cross-sectional online survey n=1118 asked about 125 foods and perceived headache or abdominal-pain triggers. Authors report association of perceived apricot trigger with medication-overuse headache group p=.001; do not interpret as incidence of reaction among all apricot consumers or as apricot pain-severity change. Food preparation, challenge and Parkinson status not established. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10607881/.
