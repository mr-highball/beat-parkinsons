Title: Anticalcifying effect of Daucus carota in experimental urolithiasis in Wistar rats.

First published: 2020

Journal: J Ayurveda Integr Med

Preclinical Wistar rat study of hydroethanolic Daucus carota root extract in ethylene-glycol/ammonium-chloride urolithiasis, reducing calcium oxalate kidney deposition and normalizing urinary/serum markers; notes traditional Ayurvedic Ashmari bhedan (calculus destruction) and diuretic folklore. Renal stone model, not PD bladder urgency.
