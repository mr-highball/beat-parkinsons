Title: Parkinson disease and chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy with celiac disease
Source: https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/parkinsons-disease-and-chronic-inflammatory-demyelinating-polyneuropathy-with-celiac-disease-improvement-in-neuropathy-but-not-parkinsons-symptoms-after-intravenous-immunoglobulin-and-gluten-free/
Publication: 2020 MDS Virtual Congress, abstract 824
Depth: Factual summary of original primary clinical abstract.

The case involved gait-predominant Parkinson disease, biopsy-confirmed celiac disease and inflammatory polyneuropathy. The neuropathy improved following immunoglobulin and a gluten-free diet. Parkinson symptoms remained on their clinical course with usual treatment and later dyskinesia. The report has no separately rated rest tremor or comparison of wheat berry intake.
