Original Isfahan registration and outcome list
Primary source: https://irct.ir/trial/75378?revision=296350
Registry ID: IRCT20121216011763N61
Revision ID: 296350

Coverage: factual extract from the official archived revision.

The March 5, 2024 public IRCT revision, 296350, lists a target of 86 participants. It names DASS-21, the MDS-UPDRS, Parkinson sleep and cognitive measures, and quality of life. Its outcome list omits the standalone Parkinson Fatigue Scale. The broad registered MDS-UPDRS also includes a fatigue item. Expected recruitment dates were June 21, 2024 to April 21, 2025. Actual recruitment dates were unfilled in the checked history.
