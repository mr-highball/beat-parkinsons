Dyskinesia in a case receiving palmitoylethanolamide and luteolin
Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8794485/
PMID: 35096476
Coverage: Factual extract from original Case Presentation.

Brotini reported a Parkinson patient with peak-dose leg and trunk dyskinesia. The report describes resolution after four months of adding a palmitoylethanolamide and luteolin product to carbidopa/levodopa. Earlier ropinirole and levodopa comparisons make this a multi-treatment clinical course. The uncontrolled case gives no separate luteolin or thyme exposure, and no ingredient-attributable dyskinesia change.
