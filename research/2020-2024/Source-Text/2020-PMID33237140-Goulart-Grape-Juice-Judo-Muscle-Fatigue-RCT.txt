Title: Effects of grape juice consumption on muscle fatigue and oxidative stress in judo athletes: a randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 33237140
DOI: 10.1590/0001-3765202020191551
First published: 2020
Authors: Goulart MJVC, Pisamiglio DS, MÖller GB, Dani C, Alves FD, Bock PM, Schneider CD
Journal: Anais da Academia Brasileira de Ciencias
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33237140/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover 14-day trial in 20 judo athletes consuming purple grape juice vs placebo before simulated combat. Upper-limb strength was higher with grape juice (p=0.003) with reduced lipid/DNA oxidative damage. Acute athletic strength/redox study, remote from chronic Parkinson fatigue.
