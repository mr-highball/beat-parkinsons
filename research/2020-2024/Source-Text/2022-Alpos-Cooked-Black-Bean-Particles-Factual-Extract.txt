Alpos and colleagues, primary indexed report

Original source https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ifset.2021.102892

The journal issue and university listing are January 2022. An earlier precise public date has not been verified. The original abstract reports 22 participants chewing samples of cooked black beans for up to 30 seconds. PEF-treated beans without added calcium gave smaller particles at 18 and 24 seconds than two calcium-containing preparations (p < .05). The abstract does not establish an observed swallow or clinical swallowing difficulty. Participant overlap with the earlier 17-person study remains unverified.
