Title: Patterns of tau, amyloid and synuclein pathology in ageing, Alzheimer's disease and synucleinopathies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12073977/
PMID: 39531734
PMCID: PMC12073977
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awae372
Publication date: 2024-11-12 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

Autopsy groups with Alzheimer disease, dementia with Lewy bodies, mixed disease, Parkinson disease dementia and controls were compared on regional tau, amyloid and synuclein burden. The design measures neuropathology by diagnostic group; dietary fig consumption and episode timing are absent.
