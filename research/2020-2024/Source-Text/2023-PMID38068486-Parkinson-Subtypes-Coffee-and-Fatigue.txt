Original study scope
The investigators compared nonmotor symptom subtypes in a Parkinson cohort. Their measures include the Fatigue Severity Scale, apathy and a caffeine exposure category defined in cups of coffee per week. Caffeine use was reported by symptom subtype.

Continuing evidence gaps
Subtype profiles are observational. The reported between-subtype exposure difference does not isolate a fatigue contrast by brewed coffee preparation and timing. The source contains no culinary date comparison for apathy or pain.
