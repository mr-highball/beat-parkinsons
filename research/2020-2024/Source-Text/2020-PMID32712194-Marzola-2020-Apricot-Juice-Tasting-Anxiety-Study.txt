Title: A tasting experiment comparing food and nutritional supplement in anorexia nervosa.
PMID: 32712194
DOI: 10.1016/j.appet.2020.104789
First published: 2020
Authors: Marzola E, Cavallo F, Pradella P, Brustolin A, Abbate-Daga G
Journal: Appetite
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32712194/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Two-day tasting experiment in n=33 inpatients with anorexia nervosa and n=39 healthy controls comparing 50 mL natural apricot juice vs apricot-flavored medical nutritional supplement. Visual Analogue Scale anxiety did not meaningfully improve from apricot juice; eating-disorder acute hedonic/disgust protocol rather than PD anxiety or dried apricot consumption.
