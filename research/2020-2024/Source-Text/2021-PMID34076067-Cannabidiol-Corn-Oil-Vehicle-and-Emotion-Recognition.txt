Title: Cannabidiol formulation trial using corn oil as a vehicle
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8827362/
PMID: 34076067
PMCID: PMC8827362
DOI: 10.1590/1516-4446-2020-1684
Publication date: 2021-05-28
Date basis: Electronic publication, with 2022 journal issue.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full text, verified in the NCBI PMC structured original.

The double-blind trial assigned 45 healthy volunteers among cannabidiol powder, cannabidiol in corn oil, and cornstarch placebo. The corn-oil formulation reached peak cannabidiol concentration sooner than the powder. The facial-emotion recognition comparison was inconclusive. There was no treatment arm with corn oil alone and the oil grade was unspecified.

The trial estimates medication formulation effects and measures acute emotion recognition. It gives no standalone refined corn oil comparison or depressive-symptom assessment in Parkinson disease.
