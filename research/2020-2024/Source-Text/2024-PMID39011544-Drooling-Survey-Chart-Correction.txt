Title: Corrigendum to recommendations for improving recognition and treatment of sialorrhea in Parkinson's disease
PMID: 39011544
PMCID: PMC11247352
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2024.100250
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11247352/
Publication: 2024-04-18
Coverage: Corrigendum and both original and corrected Figure 1A assets.

The original 2023 panel article, PMID 38021341, used results from the existing 382-response Parkinson's Europe questionnaire. Its published figure displayed placeholders where the severity percentages should have appeared. The 2024 correction restores the numbers for normal, slight, mild, moderate, severe and unavailable responses. The mild, moderate and severe categories are 25.9%, 34.8% and 27.2%. These round to the values already given in the original survey report.

The correction describes the same respondent cohort and does not supply a new survey, exposure or efficacy measure.
