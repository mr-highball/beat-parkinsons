The review distinguishes sweet orange fruit, bitter orange forms, citrus peel compounds and byproducts. A ginger-rhizome example concerns plant proteases in a food-processing context. Mango and mango leaves occur in separate food byproduct and plant composition discussions. The endpoints do not measure clinical Parkinson symptoms following the frozen exposures.
