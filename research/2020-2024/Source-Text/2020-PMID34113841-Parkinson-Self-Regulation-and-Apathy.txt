Original study scope
The authors examined goal-directed behavior, self-efficacy, self-regulation and imaging in mild Parkinson disease. Apathy and fatigue were recorded as clinical features and potential correlates.

Continuing evidence gaps
The findings concern motivational and clinical measures under usual conditions. They provide no fresh-sage dietary comparison for either apathy or fatigue.
