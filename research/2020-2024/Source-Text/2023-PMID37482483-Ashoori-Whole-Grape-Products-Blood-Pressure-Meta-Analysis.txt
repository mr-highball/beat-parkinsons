Title: The effect of whole grape products on blood pressure and vascular function: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 37482483
DOI: 10.1016/j.numecd.2023.05.001
First published: 2023
Authors: Ashoori M, Soltani S, Kolahdouz-Mohammadi R, Moghtaderi F, Clayton Z, Abdollahi S
Journal: Nutrition, metabolism, and cardiovascular diseases : NMCD
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37482483/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PROSPERO CRD42022379231) of 30 human RCTs evaluating whole grape products and grape juice on blood pressure and vascular function. Pooled SBP declined modestly (WMD -3.17 mmHg, 95% CI -5.36 to -0.99, p=0.004, I2=64%, low certainty); subgroup analysis showed whole grape products (raisins/powder) lowered SBP (-2.69 mmHg, p=0.01) while grape juice subgroup was NULL. No significant effects on DBP, endothelial function, or heart rate; no orthostatic hypotension or lightheadedness reported.
