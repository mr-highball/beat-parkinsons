Saudi randomized trial, original Table 2

Original source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9517329/

Table 2 of the 2022 Saudi randomized trial reports day-ten general fatigue in 1/17 placebo participants and 1/25, 2/37 and 3/26 across the ground-seed groups. The study reports no clear between-group symptom finding. Attrition changes the denominators and the placebo baseline fatigue cell is internally inconsistent. The protocol tested capsules in acute COVID and measured fatigue presence. This is separate from the Egyptian oil trial.
