Title: Effects of daily almond consumption for six months on cognitive measures in healthy middle-aged to older adults: a randomized control trial.
PMID: 33448906
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2020.1868805
Registry ID: NCT03093896
First published: 2022
Authors: Mustra Rakic, Tanprasertsuk, Scott, Rasmussen, Mohn
Journal: Nutritional neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33448906/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

6-month single-blind 3-arm RCT (NCT03093896) in 60 healthy middle-aged to older adults (50-75 y) randomized to 1.5 oz/d almonds (n=19), 3 oz/d almonds (n=24), or 3.5 oz/d macronutrient-matched snack mix control (n=17). Primary and secondary CANTAB cognitive outcomes showed no significant difference in change over time among the three groups. Within-group improvements at month 6 were noted only in the 3 oz/d almond arm for visuospatial working memory (p = 0.023), visual memory/learning (p = 0.017), and spatial planning/working memory (p < 0.001), with no improvement in controls. Limits: null between-group interaction, small arms, cognitively intact non-PD adults.
