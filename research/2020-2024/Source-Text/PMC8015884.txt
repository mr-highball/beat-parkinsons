Title: Neuropsychiatric Symptoms Are Associated with Dementia in Parkinson's Disease but Not Predictive of it
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8015884/
PMID: 33816668
PMCID: PMC8015884
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13151
Publication date: 2021-02-18
Source depth: PMC original full article HTML; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC article metadata and PubMed.
Design: Longitudinal Parkinson disease cohort

Neuropsychiatric and cognitive findings were recorded in 328 Parkinson participants at baseline. Of 202 participants without dementia followed for up to four years, 51 developed dementia. Participant, caregiver and clinician instruments supplied different observations. The strongest neuropsychiatric predictor was the total NPI score, with AUC 0.66 (95% CI 0.57–0.75). Hallucination questionnaire scores added little predictive information for dementia. These analyses do not count visual episodes against food intake.
