Rodent eugenol motor tests and the related UFC dissertation
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32353201/
PMID: 32353201
DOI: 10.1111/bcpt.13425
Coverage: original journal abstract and author-associated institutional dissertation record; full journal access unresolved

The journal article first published April 30, 2020. It describes selected behavioral outcomes in hemiparkinsonian rats receiving isolated eugenol, alone or with levodopa. The institutional dissertation describes a closely related model and names open-field exploration, rotarod and apomorphine-induced rotation. A timed gait or stride assay is not identified. The university dates the dissertation to February 6, 2018. Its first repository availability is unverified. Exact cohort overlap and the full journal tables remain unresolved. These reports give no culinary allspice or clinical Parkinson walking-speed comparison. [University record and dissertation](https://repositorio.ufc.br/handle/riufc/40992).
