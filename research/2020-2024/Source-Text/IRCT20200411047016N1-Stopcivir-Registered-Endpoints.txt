IRCT registration of a multi ingredient syrup in COVID-19
Source: https://irct.ir/trial/47044
Coverage: factual extract from original public registry record. Identifier IRCT20200411047016N1.

The Iranian Registry of Clinical Trials posted IRCT20200411047016N1 on May 31, 2020, while recruiting. It planned standard care with or without Stopcivir, a mixed syrup containing Satureja hortensis and several other ingredients. Registered outcomes were hospital stay, dyspnea and oxygen saturation. The record displays an empty secondary-outcomes section and lists no smell or taste outcome. It has no separate summer-savory arm. The publicly identified record supplies no trial estimate for olfaction.
