Title: Aflatoxins and ochratoxin A in dried fruits from Morocco
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37778435/
Publisher abstract: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S027323002300171X
PMID: 37778435
DOI: 10.1016/j.yrtph.2023.105503
Publication date: 2023-09-29
Date basis: NLM electronic publication date. December 2023 print issue of Regulatory Toxicology and Pharmacology 145, article 105503.
Text coverage: Primary author abstract and publisher summary. Complete sampling methods, commodity counts and results tables remain open.

Mannani and colleagues report 180 samples in the dried-food aflatoxin survey and 210 in the ochratoxin A survey, which also includes grape juice. They report aflatoxins in 23.3% and ochratoxin A in 53.3% of dried fig samples. The abstract gives no separate fig denominator or sampling dates. It reports 4.7% co-occurrence for the whole sample set, with no fig-specific co-occurrence value. Its dietary intake calculation estimates exposure rather than observing a clinical response.
