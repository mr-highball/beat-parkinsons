Title: Effects of curcumin and fish oil on motor behavior and biochemical parameters in mice exposed to rotenone
Authors: Barbara da Silva Alves and colleagues
Identifier: DOI 10.4025/actascibiolsci.v46i1.69047
Publication year: 2024
Source: https://periodicos.uem.br/ojs/index.php/ActaSciBiolSci/article/download/69047/751375157765/
Coverage: Original publisher PDF, pp. 3–4, Experimental design, Table 1 and Behavioral tests.

The paper dissolved curcumin in soy oil. Oral soy oil was given to both the conventional control and rotenone-only groups. The randomized five-group protocol assigns the 50 mg/kg oral dose to curcumin, without specifying an oil refinement grade or standalone soybean oil efficacy contrast. The mouse endpoints were open-field activity, beam walking, pole performance, foot-fault and biochemical measurements. Levodopa-induced dyskinesia was background, not an assessed response.

The 2026 curcumin review (PMID 41878330 / PMC13006684, Table 1) places soybean oil under the intervention column with 50 mg/kg for Alves et al. The primary assignment is curcumin. Seven per group appears in the primary biochemical sampling methods, while the randomized protocol began with ten per group.
