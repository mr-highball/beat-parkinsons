Title: Rahmani et al. 2022, published trial of Nigella sativa oil in diabetic hemodialysis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9033343/
PMID: 35463059
PMCID: PMC9033343
DOI: 10.1155/2022/2753294
Registration: IRCT20200411047027N1
Publication date: 2022
Coverage: Factual extract from the published trial results and same-cohort protocol.

Trial: randomized double-blind comparison in adults with diabetes receiving hemodialysis. Assignment was 23 per group. The intervention was oral Nigella sativa oil 2 g/day for 12 weeks and the control was paraffin oil.
Published outcomes: glycemic, inflammatory and oxidative-stress measures. The same trial's 2022 protocol (PMID 35120579, PMC8815251) includes HADS depression. The article provides no HADS estimate.
Relevance: same cohort as the registration and protocol. A depression result remains pending. The exposure and population differ from toasted whole seed in Parkinson disease.

November 26 original-results follow-through. The matched paper reports 20 oil and 21 comparator participants analyzed, from the initial 23 per group. The Methods calls the test oil cold pressed. The endpoint listing and original tables cover glucose, lipids, inflammation and oxidative markers without a separately reported HADS depression comparison. The identical registration links the Research Square posting and published study protocol to this cohort. Those documents specify HADS but do not supply outcomes. The results paper offers data on request, and no independently public HADS table was identified.
