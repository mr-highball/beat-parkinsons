Title: The antidepressant effects of lavender (Lavandula angustifolia Mill.): A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled clinical trials.
PMID: 33549687
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2021.102679
First published: 2021
Authors: Firoozeei TS, Feizi A, Rezaeizadeh H, Zargaran A, Roohafza HR, Karimi M
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33549687/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis (Firoozeei et al., 2021, Complement Ther Med) pooling 17 RCTs of Lavandula angustifolia on depression severity. Overall SMD -0.66 (95% CI -0.85 to -0.46, p<0.001, I2=68.2%); oral administration subgroup SMD -0.56 (95% CI -1.07 to -0.05, p=0.032, I2=85.2%). High heterogeneity and variable trial quality noted; no Parkinson's disease trials included.
