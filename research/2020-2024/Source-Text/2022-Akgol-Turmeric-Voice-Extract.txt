Turmeric use in a vocal hygiene questionnaire
Primary source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/365309111_Determining_Knowledge_Level_Attitude_Behaviors_Regarding_Vocal_Hygiene_and_Use_of_Medical_Drugs_and_Herbal_Medicinal_Products_for_Vocal_Health_in_Second_Level_Professional_Voice_Users
PMID: 36372675
DOI: 10.1016/j.jvoice.2022.09.015
Coverage: Factual original Table 3 and methods extract.

Akgöl et al. included 406 professional or amateur voice users. Table 3 records turmeric products in 69 of 253 people who answered the herbal-use items. The authors compare use by occupation and describe hoarseness care. They did not measure a turmeric-specific acoustic change, identify a ground preparation or report a Parkinson cohort. First online November 10, 2022 even though the issue is dated 2025.
