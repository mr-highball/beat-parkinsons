Soybean oil label in the published early SHAPE report
Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2475299123075273
PMCID: PMC7257115
DOI: 10.1093/cdn/nzaa040_009
Registration: NCT03603041
Coverage: Original journal conference abstract first public May 29, 2020; full abstract checked

The May 2020 abstract describes 34 participants across five groups. The six-person placebo fat arm received whey protein and 4.3 g/day soybean oil. The authors report an overall Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index change and a sleep-efficiency result of p = .06. Group-matched outcomes and denominators link this record to the ISBNPA description, which differs in oil label and some reported aggregate results. The published abstract includes the incomplete identifier NCT0303041. The official SHAPE record with matching investigator, title and arm design is NCT03603041.
