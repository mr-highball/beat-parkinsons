Hei and colleagues, original Table 2

Original source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11205988/

Published in 2022. The study randomized 172 people with first or early schizophrenia to two levels of a glucoraphanin and myrosinase tablet or placebo alongside risperidone. Table 2 gives estimated 22-week attention and vigilance changes of 2.56, 5.38 and 6.28 on high-dose, low-dose and placebo, respectively. The omnibus comparison was inconclusive (p = .263). The overall cognitive score was also inconclusive (p = .693). The report used a supplement, with no raw broccoli or Parkinson sample. The registry linkage is probable from matching trial details rather than an identifier printed in the article.
