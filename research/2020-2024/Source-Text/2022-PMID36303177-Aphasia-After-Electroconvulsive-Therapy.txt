Title: Electroconvulsive therapy combined with lithium developed reversible pure anomic aphasia: a case report.
PMID: 36303177
PMCID: PMC9615263
DOI: 10.1186/s12888-022-04323-1
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9615263/
First publication date: 2022-10-27 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

The report describes reversible anomic aphasia and delirium after electroconvulsive therapy combined with lithium. Auditory and visual phenomena occurred during the acute treatment course. This case is not a Parkinson culinary exposure comparison and has no dried fig evidence.
