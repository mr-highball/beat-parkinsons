Title: The protective effect of mushroom consumption on depressive symptoms in Korean population.
PMID: 36536111
PMCID: PMC9763420
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-022-26549-5
First published: 2022
Authors: Park SK, Oh CM, Ryoo JH, Jung JY
Journal: Scientific reports
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9763420/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Longitudinal Korean cohort (n=87,822) assessing CES-D >=16 depressive symptoms by pooled mushroom frequency. Consumption >=1 serving/mo associated with lower adjusted HR (>=3/wk HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.80-0.93). Limits: observational confounding; Korean dietary mushrooms pool multiple species (shiitake, oyster, enoki, oak) without isolating A. bisporus.
