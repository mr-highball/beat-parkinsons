Title: The Effect of Baru (Dypterix alata Vog.) Almond Oil on Markers of Bowel Habits in Hemodialysis Patients.
PMID: 34135977
PMCID: PMC8175138
DOI: 10.1155/2021/3187305
First published: 2021
Authors: Schincaglia RM, Pimentel GD, Peixoto MDRG, Cuppari L, Mota JF
Journal: Evidence-based complementary and alternative medicine : eCAM
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8175138/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Single-blind 12-week randomized clinical trial (PMC8175138) in n=35 hemodialysis patients comparing 5 g/day oral baru almond oil (Dipteryx alata Vog., Fabaceae) capsules versus mineral oil capsules on bowel habits. CRITICAL BOTANICAL EXCLUSION: Baru (Dipteryx alata) is a Brazilian Cerrado legume seed unrelated to sweet almond (Prunus dulcis, Rosaceae). Included solely to prevent botanical misattribution to culinary sweet almond oil.
