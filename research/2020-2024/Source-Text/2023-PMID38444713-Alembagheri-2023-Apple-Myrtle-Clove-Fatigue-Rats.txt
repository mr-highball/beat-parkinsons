Title: Antifatigue Effects of the Aqueous Extracts of Myrtle Berries, Apple and Clove: An Animal Study.
PMID: 38444713
PMCID: PMC10912872
DOI: 10.5812/ijpr-140323
First published: 2023
Authors: Alembagheri A, Hajimehdipoor H, Khoramjouy M, Esmaeili S, Faizi M
Journal: Iranian journal of pharmaceutical research : IJPR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38444713/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Animal study grounded in Iranian Traditional Medicine (ITM), which classifies Malus domestica (apple), Myrtus communis (myrtle), and Syzygium aromaticum (clove) as brain and heart tonics for fatigue. In rat weight-loaded forced swimming (WLFS) testing, aqueous apple extract significantly increased swimming duration to exhaustion (~1.8-fold vs control; myrtle ~2.7-fold; p<0.05 to p<0.0001 across active groups). Limits: non-PD rat forced swimming model and aqueous extract rather than raw whole apple.
