Title: Effects of cinnamon extract on complications of treatment and eradication of Helicobacter pylori in infected people
Source: https://www.herbmedpharmacol.com/Article/jhp-22459
DOI: 10.15171/jhp.2020.08
Publication date: 2020-01
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: Complete publisher original, Methods and Table 2 on printed page 58.
Trial registration: IRCT20121114011469N3
Original full text: https://herbmedpharmacol.com/PDF/jhp-22459

The trial enrolled 98 adults receiving the same standard therapy for H. pylori and assigned 49 per arm to additional cinnamon extract or a starch capsule. The original does not identify the cinnamon species or quantify a plain culinary powder. In Table 2, reported constipation counts at baseline, day 7 and day 14 are 11, 8, and 6 with extract and 18, 18, and 15 with control. The reported comparison of reductions gives p=.10. Baseline prevalence differs, the outcome is a count rather than a severity scale and some printed percentages or changes do not reconcile with counts. The abstract groups constipation among significant reductions, conflicting with Table 2. The same table reports fewer people with epigastric pain over treatment, 39 to 12 vs 43 to 24 (reported p=.02), an exploratory outcome during concurrent treatment. Neither result establishes the frozen preparation in a Parkinson cohort.
