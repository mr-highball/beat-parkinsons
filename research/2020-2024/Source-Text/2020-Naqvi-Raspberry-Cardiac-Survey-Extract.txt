Raspberry consumers in a cardiac blood pressure survey
Source depth Original cardiac survey Methods and Table 3. DOI 10.22159/ajpcr.2020.v13i7.37782.
Naqvi and colleagues report 73 survey responses among 100 cardiac patients. Table 3 lists five raspberry consumers. Two were prehypertensive and three hypertensive, and the comparison reports p = .710. The survey gives no isolated preparation, orthostatic assessment or treatment comparison. The same report includes separate laboratory fruit extracts that do not describe what the survey patients consumed.
