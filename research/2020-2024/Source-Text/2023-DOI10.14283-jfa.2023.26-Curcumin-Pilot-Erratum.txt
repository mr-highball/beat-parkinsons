Title: Erratum to the Curcumin C3 Complex physical-function pilot
Source: https://www.jfrailtyaging.com/7464-erratum-to-effects-of-curcumin-c3-complex-on-physical-function-in-moderately-functioning-older-adults-with-low-grade-inflammation-a-pilot-trial.html
DOI: 10.14283/jfa.2023.26
Published online: 2023-05-29
Citation: Mankowski et al., Journal of Frailty & Aging 12(3), 252 (2023)
Record type: Distilled original publisher correction

The authors correct the identity of the inactive comparator in their physical-function pilot. The trial used microcrystalline cellulose. The original manuscript mislabeled it as nitrocellulose. The published correction gives no revised outcomes. The composition agrees with ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03085680. This correction and the primary article describe the same trial.
