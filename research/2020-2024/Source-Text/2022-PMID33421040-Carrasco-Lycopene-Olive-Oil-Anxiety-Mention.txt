Title: Effects of Lycopene-enriched, Organic, Extra Virgin Olive Oil on Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia: A Pilot Study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33421040/
PMID: 33421040
Publication date basis: November 2022 journal issue. An earlier first-publication date is unverified.
Coverage: Factual extract from the author abstract and publisher landing page.

Carrasco and colleagues studied ten men with benign prostatic hyperplasia and ten men described as healthy. Both groups took a commercial lycopene-enriched organic EVOO product for 30 days, 20 mL daily containing 0.4 mg/mL lycopene. The abstract names anxiety among the study aims and emotional health among the measures repeated before and after the intervention. It reports sleep and selected prostate and antioxidant results, with no numerical anxiety result. The publisher landing page describes access as restricted. The two groups received the same combined product. There was no lycopene-only, oil-free or Parkinson comparison.
