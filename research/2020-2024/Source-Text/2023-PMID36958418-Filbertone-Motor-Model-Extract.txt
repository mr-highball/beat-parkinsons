Title: Activation of ROS-PERK-TFEB by filbertone ameliorates neurodegenerative diseases via enhancing the autophagy-lysosomal pathway
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36958418/
PMID: 36958418
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2023.109325
First public article date: 2023-03-22
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated author abstract and primary author dissertation Part II

Park and colleagues used isolated filbertone in an MPTP mouse
model, with ten mice per group in the behavioral figure.
The article abstract and the corresponding dissertation
Figure 6 describe improved rotarod fall latency and treadmill
running distance compared with the model group. The
dissertation supplies matching experimental context and
does not constitute another independently enrolled study.

The treatment is a constituent, and the behavioral tasks
are distinct from human resting tremor. The work contains no
cold-pressed hazelnut-oil comparator. The journal full text
was not independently authenticated at this checkpoint.
