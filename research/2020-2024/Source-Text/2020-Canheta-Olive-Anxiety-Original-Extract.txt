Title: Traditional Brazilian diet and extra virgin olive oil reduce symptoms of anxiety and depression in individuals with severe obesity
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32675019/
PMID: 32675019
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2020.05.046
Registration: NCT02463435
First public: 2020-06-10
Coverage: Factual original abstract and source-family scope.

Canheta et al. followed adults with severe obesity in three active arms for 12 weeks. The oil-only arm had a reported 38.23% within-arm decrease in HADS anxiety prevalence (p=.019), with decreases of 45.83% and 33.33% in the traditional-diet and combined arms. The primary companion report verifies the parent's 52 mL/day cold-pressed extra-virgin oil supplied unheated. The exact anxiety table was unavailable. An original published letter reports post-treatment prevalence favored the active diet alone. It questions using within-arm tests as an oil effect, and it gives no verified baseline-adjusted anxiety estimate. An author reply exists without a body recovered. The cohort has no matched Parkinson comparison.
