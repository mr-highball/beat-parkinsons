Title: Umami and Other Taste Perceptions in Patients With Parkinson's Disease

The Parkinson taste study measured thresholds for sweet, sour, bitter, salty, and umami stimuli. The smell test was shortened to 14 items by omitting turpentine and clove. Rigidity is described as a reason to adapt the taste-test application. Neither the specific rigidity response nor clinical use of ground clove was measured.
