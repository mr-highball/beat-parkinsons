Title: Bertholletia excelsa Seeds Reduce Anxiety-Like Behavior, Lipids, and Overweight in Mice.
PMID: 34072024
PMCID: PMC8198383
DOI: 10.3390/molecules26113212
First published: 2021
Authors: Frausto-González O, Bautista CJ, Narváez-González F, Hernandez-Leon A, Estrada-Camarena E, Rivero-Cruz F, et al.
Journal: Molecules (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34072024/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical study (PMC8198383) in hypercaloric-diet mice: nonpolar hexane extract of Bertholletia excelsa seed (SBHX 30 and 300 mg/kg) reduced anxiety-like behavior vs vehicle. Limits: nonpolar chemical extract (not whole raw/roasted nut), rodent obesity model, directly contrasted by null whole-Brazil-nut rat anxiety study Apolinario 2023.
