Title: Neurochemical, neurobehavioral and histochemical effects of therapeutic dose of l-dopa on striatal neurons in rats: Protective effect of virgin coconut oil.
PMID: 32707436
DOI: 10.1016/j.biopha.2020.110473
First published: 2020
Authors: Shehata AM, Ahmed-Farid OA, Rizk HA, Saber SM, Lashin FM, Re L
Journal: Biomedicine & pharmacotherapy = Biomedecine & pharmacotherapie
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32707436/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rat experiment (Shehata et al., 2020, Biomed Pharmacother) in 40 adult rats randomized to control, oral virgin coconut oil (1.42 mL/kg/day), levodopa/carbidopa (100/10 mg/kg/day), or VCO plus levodopa/carbidopa for 30 days. Levodopa induced open-field behavioral abnormalities, aggressive behavior, striatal oxidative stress, and GFAP neurodegeneration in non-parkinsonian rats, which VCO co-treatment minimized. Limits: healthy-rat drug-toxicity model rather than nigrostriatal Parkinson model; abstract omits numeric motor/dyskinesia/tremor scores and does not test clinical human PD symptoms.
