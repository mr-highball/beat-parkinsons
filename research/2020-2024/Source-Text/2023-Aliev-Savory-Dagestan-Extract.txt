Ethnobotany of Dagestan, 2023-11-18. DOI 10.32859/era.26.66.1-63. Original pp. 1 and 55.
Aliev and colleagues published November 18, 2023. The field survey lists summer savory as Satureja hortensis. Page 55 records fresh leaf juice applied to bee stings, dried herb in meat sausages and an infusion of unspecified freshness for other complaints. The record gives traditional preparation and use, with no observed Parkinson voice-volume or speech outcome.
