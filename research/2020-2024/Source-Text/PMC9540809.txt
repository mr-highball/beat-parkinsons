Title: A Longitudinal Study of Plasma pTau181 in Mild Cognitive Impairment with Lewy Bodies and Alzheimer's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9540809/
PMID: 35318733
PMCID: PMC9540809
DOI: 10.1002/mds.28994
Publication date: 2022-03-23
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The cohort compares biomarker trajectories across mild cognitive impairment with Lewy bodies and Alzheimer categories and applies the North-East Visual Hallucinations Inventory. Ratings are higher in probable Lewy body cases than in the Alzheimer-type group. The later plasma paper reuses pTau181 measurements. Neither gives a dried fig intervention or food-timed visual episodes.

The 146 participants include 56 probable MCI-LB, 22 possible MCI-LB, 44 MCI-AD and 24 controls. The 2023 plasma study in PMC10755229 incorporates the pTau181 previously collected from this research programme. Do not treat the biomarker measurements as independent.
