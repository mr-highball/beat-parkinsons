The Daily Habit Scale validation tested 179 Parkinson participants and includes separate questions about fast food and taking painkillers. These questions measure habits rather than category-specific date intake or pain severity. The study has no dried-date comparison.

The validated scale records whether people report habitual behavior. Further data would need separate food items and a Parkinson pain endpoint to test the frozen exposure.
