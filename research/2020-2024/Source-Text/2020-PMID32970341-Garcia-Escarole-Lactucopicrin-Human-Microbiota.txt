Title: Human Gut Microbiota Metabolism of Dietary Sesquiterpene Lactones: Untargeted Metabolomics Study of Lactucopicrin and Lactucin Conversion In Vitro and In Vivo.
PMID: 32970341
DOI: 10.1002/mnfr.202000619
First published: 2020
Authors: Garcia CJ, Beltran D, Tomas-Barberan FA
Journal: Molecular nutrition & food research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32970341/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract Facts: In vitro human fecal fermentation and in vivo healthy-volunteer urinary metabolomics study following escarole (Cichorium endivia) salad intake. Demonstrated extensive gut-microbiota hydrogenation/dehydroxylation of lactucopicrin and lactucin into dihydrolactucopicrin, tetrahydrolactucopicrin, and deoxylactucin phase-II glucuronide/sulfate conjugates in human urine, with large inter-individual microbiota-driven variability. Pharmacokinetic/microbiota conversion study without clinical symptom endpoints.
