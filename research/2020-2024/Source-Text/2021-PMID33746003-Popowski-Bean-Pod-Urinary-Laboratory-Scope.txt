Popowski and colleagues report a laboratory evaluation of a bean-pod extract and selected constituents. The original abstract describes bacterial adhesion and transport of selected constituents in a cell model. The paper's rationale concerns traditional urinary use. These endpoints characterize a laboratory comparison, with no participant-reported urinary urgency, void count, infection outcome or clinical Parkinson symptom measurement.

The preparation is a pod extract. It does not represent an ordinary cooked green bean exposure or the traditional water infusion. The institutional PDF link returned an HTML record during this review. The exact original abstract and bibliographic record establish only the described scope.
