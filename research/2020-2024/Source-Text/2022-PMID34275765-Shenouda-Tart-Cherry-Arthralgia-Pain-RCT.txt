Title: Effect of Tart Cherry on Aromatase Inhibitor-Induced Arthralgia (AIA) in Nonmetastatic Hormone-Positive Breast Cancer Patients: A Randomized Double-Blind Placebo-Controlled Trial.
PMID: 34275765
DOI: 10.1016/j.clbc.2021.06.007
First published: 2022
Authors: Shenouda M, Copley R, Pacioles T, Lebowicz Y, Jamil M, Akpanudo S, Tirona MT
Journal: Clinical breast cancer
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34275765/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial (Shenouda et al., 2022, Clin Breast Cancer) enrolling 60 nonmetastatic hormone-positive breast cancer patients with aromatase inhibitor-induced arthralgia (48 analyzed: 23 tart cherry, 25 placebo; 2 withdrew due to diarrhea and 10 were noncompliant). Participants consumed 1 oz/day Montmorency tart cherry concentrate (~50 tart cherries) in 8 oz water vs syrup placebo for 6 weeks and recorded 0-100 mm VAS pain diaries. Tart cherry achieved a 34.7% mean pain decrease vs 1.4% with placebo (Mann-Whitney U, p=0.034). Limits: 20% attrition/noncompliance (2 diarrhea withdrawals), liquid concentrate rather than dried whole cherries, non-PD endocrine-therapy arthralgia cohort.
