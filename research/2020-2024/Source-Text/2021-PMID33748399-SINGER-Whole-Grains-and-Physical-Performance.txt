Title: SINgapore GERiatric intervention study to reduce physical frailty and cognitive decline pilot
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7958306/
PMID: 33748399
PMCID: PMC7958306
DOI: 10.1002/trc2.12141
Publication date: 2021-03-15
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Original feasibility trial, Methods, diet components and Results.

Seventy participants with early frailty were randomized to two multidomain interventions, 36 and 34. Sixty-eight completed the pilot. Whole-grain rice, including brown rice, appears alongside other grains in dietary guidance. The study also assigns broader exercise, cognition and vascular components. It reports walking and strength assessments and supplies no randomized brown-rice contrast or separate clinically measured Parkinson postural-instability result.
