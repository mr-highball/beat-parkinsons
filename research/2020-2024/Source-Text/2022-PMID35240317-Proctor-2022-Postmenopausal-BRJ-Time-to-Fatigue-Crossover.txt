Title: Inorganic nitrate supplementation and blood flow restricted exercise tolerance in post-menopausal women.
PMID: 35240317
PMCID: PMC9062890
DOI: 10.1016/j.niox.2022.02.004
First published: 2022
Authors: Proctor DN, Neely KA, Mookerjee S, Tucker J, Somani YB, Flanagan M, et al.
Journal: Nitric oxide : biology and chemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35240317/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=13 postmenopausal women: acute nitrate-rich beetroot juice significantly prolonged time to fatigue during high-intensity cycling by +61.8+-56.5 s vs placebo (p=0.03). Limits: acute exercise-induced fatigue in non-PD women.
