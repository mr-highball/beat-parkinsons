PRKN family report and green tea history
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neurology/articles/10.3389/fneur.2020.582323/full

October 9, 2020. Table 1 describes two members of a Han Chinese family with early-onset Parkinson disease and PRKN variants. In the green tea row, one patient reported never consuming it and the other reported quitting. Olfactory dysfunction is absent for both in the nonmotor symptom portion of the table. The authors did not assign green tea, report brew type or dose, provide a change score for smell, or estimate a relationship with green tea. This observation identifies recorded exposure history and olfactory status in the same report.
