Perilla seed oil and constipation ratings in athletes and sedentary adults
Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114522001805
Coverage: factual extract of primary methods and constipation results
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

The 2022 first-online Kawamura trial randomized 36 female athletes to perilla seed-oil jelly or placebo. In the analysis using each participant’s lowest treatment-period score, the constipation contrast favored 9 g/day versus placebo (p = .020), with a less clear 3 g/day comparison (p = .073). The groups received unequal jelly schedules. Fresh shiso leaf and Parkinson constipation were untested.
