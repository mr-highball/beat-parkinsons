Title: Effects of Gocovri amantadine extended-release capsules on motor aspects of experiences of daily living in people with Parkinson's disease and dyskinesia
PMID: 34024025
PMCID: PMC8571461
DOI: 10.1007/s40120-021-00256-1
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8571461/
Publication: 2021
Coverage: Primary pooled trial analysis, Methods, Tables 1 and 2, and the text describing Figure 3.

The authors pooled two phase 3 amantadine trials, NCT02136914 and NCT02274766. The modified intention-to-treat groups had 97 assigned amantadine and 90 assigned placebo. The MDS-UPDRS item 2.2 findings numerically favored amantadine at week 12 and were inconclusive on this item. In Table 2, which lists placebo first and uses available week-12 observations, 17/35 participants with baseline scores of at least 2 moved below 2 with amantadine, compared with 11/37 with placebo. Among people with baseline scores below 2, 5/47 and 7/50, respectively, moved to 2 or above. The overall item model and the exploratory threshold groups address different summaries.

This is a secondary analysis of a prescription medication in Parkinson disease with dyskinesia. It reuses both registered clinical cohorts and supplies no dietary fig comparison.
