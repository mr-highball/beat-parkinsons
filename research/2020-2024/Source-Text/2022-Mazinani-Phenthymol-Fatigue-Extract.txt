Mazinani et al., 2022. Phenthymol acute COVID-19 study. AJHS/Medicina Balear 37(1), printed pages 91–96.
Source: https://ibdigital.uib.es/greenstone/sites/localsite/collect/medicinaBalear/import/volums/2022/AJHS_Medicina_Balear_2022v37n1.pdf
Access and coverage: Institutional volume PDF, abstract, intervention methods and Table 1.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

The paper describes oral thyme, ajwain, unspecified Anethum graveolens powder and honey alongside usual treatment versus a starch and sugar syrup placebo alongside usual treatment. Table 1 lists the fatigue counts 16/30 placebo and 10/30 formula with P=.118, despite abstract/narrative language of significant improvement. No validated fatigue severity scale or dill component comparison is presented. The paper also prints an incongruous January 2016 onset date for the COVID study and its abstract and body describe the group structure differently.
