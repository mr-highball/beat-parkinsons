Amone and colleagues randomized 96 healthy older adults to a standardized grape juice extract or placebo, with acute and longer cognitive testing. The reported comparisons include favorable cognitive or attention results. The outcomes listed in the methods are cognitive tests, with no Hamilton anxiety scale result. The report discloses industry funding and an author employed by the ingredient manufacturer.

The same branded preparation appears in related literature, but the enrollment, investigators and registration identify a separate study from the 2017 trial. The report cannot serve as a repeat test of the earlier anxiety result. Raw table grapes and Parkinson disease are outside the design.
