Original study scope
The exploratory Alzheimer prevention paper names foods in diet patterns, nutrition tables and a comparative household meal-cost section. Soybean oil reflects the household comparison. None of these descriptions supplies assigned servings or separate Parkinson symptom changes.

Continuing evidence gaps
Use distinct primary data for the named food and exact endpoint. Affordability and diet-category lists do not measure clinical effects.
