The anise, licorice and banana Sniffin’ Stick model discriminated good and poor smell with development AUC .95 and validation AUCs at least .90. The combined odor stimulus model does not estimate an isolated banana effect on apathy. The Oxford and other modeled groups are distinct from the Taiwanese repeated-measures cohort.
