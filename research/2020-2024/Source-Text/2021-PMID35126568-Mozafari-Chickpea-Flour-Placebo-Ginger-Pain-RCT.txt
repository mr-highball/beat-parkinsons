Title: Effect of Zingiber officinale Roscoe rhizome (ginger) capsule on postpartum pain: Double-blind randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 35126568
PMCID: PMC8765508
DOI: 10.4103/jrms.JRMS_544_20
First published: 2021
Authors: Mozafari S, Esmaeili S, Momenyan S, Zadeh Modarres S, Ozgoli G
Journal: J Res Med Sci
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8765508/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized controlled trial (Mozafari et al., 2021, PMC8765508) of ginger capsules for postpartum pain in which encapsulated chickpea flour served strictly as the inert placebo comparator. Demonstrates chickpea flour used as an inactive control rather than an analgesic intervention; provides no evidence of cooked chickpea worsening or easing Parkinson pain.
