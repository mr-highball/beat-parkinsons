Sunflower meal and registered fatigue timing
https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04247763
selected public registry fields

First posted January 30, 2020. Double-blind randomized crossover compared a high-saturated-fat meal with a high-oleic sunflower oil meal. The current record specifies MFSI-SF sampling on admission before meals and at 18 and 30 months. No results are posted. Madison 2020 published postmeal attention; companion reports cover metabolism and inflammatory markers. The registered timing does not establish an acute postmeal fatigue comparison. Refining grade is unspecified.
