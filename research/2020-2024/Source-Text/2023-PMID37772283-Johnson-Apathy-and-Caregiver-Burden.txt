Title: Predictors of Burden in Carers of Patients with Impulse Control Behaviors in Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10525061/
PMID: 37772283
PMCID: PMC10525061
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13824
Citation: Johnson D, Townsend L, David AS, Askey-Jones S, Brown R. Movement Disorders Clinical Practice. First published July 26, 2023. 10:1360–1367. ISRCTN82636004.
Coverage: Original main text, patient and caregiver measures and Tables 2–4. Distilled factual summary.
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

The cross-sectional analysis uses baseline assessments from 45 Parkinson patient–caregiver pairs in a cognitive-behavioral therapy trial for impulse-control behaviors. The Neuropsychiatric Inventory apathy/indifference subscore had a Spearman correlation of .49 with caregiver burden (p = .001). Apathy did not remain in the final regression model. The outcome correlation does not estimate a change in patient apathy. The dietary behaviors included in the broader clinical inventories do not identify date fruit or a fruit intervention.
