Title: Neuroaspis PLP10, a nutritional formula rich in omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids with antioxidant vitamins including gamma-tocopherol in early Parkinson's disease: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
PMID: 34607196
DOI: 10.1016/j.clineuro.2021.106954
First published: 2021
Authors: Pantzaris M, Loukaides G, Paraskevis D, Kostaki EG, Patrikios I
Journal: Clinical Neurology and Neurosurgery
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34607196/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. EXCLUSION CONTEXT RECORD: 30-month DB-RCT in 40 early PD patients testing Neuroaspis PLP10 (multi-component liquid oil containing 810 mg EPA + 4,140 mg DHA + 1,800 mg GLA + 3,150 mg LA + vitamin A + vitamin E + 760 mg gamma-tocopherol) vs virgin olive oil, reporting slower UPDRS progression (P<=0.05). Strictly excluded as unusable exposure for cooked/canned herring because multi-PUFA + high-dose gamma-tocopherol/vitamin A/E formula cannot isolate whole fish or herring.
