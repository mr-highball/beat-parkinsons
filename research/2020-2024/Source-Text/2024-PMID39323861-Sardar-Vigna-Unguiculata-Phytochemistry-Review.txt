Title: Unveiling the therapeutic and nutritious potential of Vigna unguiculata in line with its phytochemistry.
PMID: 39323861
PMCID: PMC11422034
DOI: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e37911
First published: 2024
Authors: Sardar H, Hadi F, Alam W, Halawani IF, Alzahrani FM, Saleem RA, et al.
Journal: Heliyon
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39323861/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Comprehensive narrative review (Sardar et al., 2024, Heliyon, PMC11422034) summarizing Vigna unguiculata phytochemistry, traditional African and Asian ethnomedicinal seed uses (diuretic, constipation, stomach strengthening, boils, urinary/hematuria complaints) vs distinct leaf/root uses (chest pain, epilepsy, headaches), and preclinical literature including non-PubMed rodent reports (Tazin 2014 methanolic extract writhing; Akinpelu 2017/Adegbuyi 2019 wild subsp. dekindtiana aerial parts; Aduema 2017/2018 cooked bean Swiss mice). Confirms lack of human Parkinson's trials and highlights species/plant-part heterogeneity.
