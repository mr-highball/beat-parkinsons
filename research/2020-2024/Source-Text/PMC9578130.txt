The full review describes ginger extracts and isolated constituents in laboratory models. It cites oral zingerone in a lesion model, in which the observed post-levodopa abnormal motor behavior persisted and dopaminergic neuron recovery was poorer after co-treatment. This account is from Kabuto and Yamanushi and gives no independent comparison. Huh and colleagues measured tight junction and enteric neuron outcomes with ginger or 6-shogaol. The anxiety discussion cites preclinical background. Cinnamon or its derivatives appear as mechanistic comparators and a research proposal, without a tested culinary cinnamon and named symptom contrast.
