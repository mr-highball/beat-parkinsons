Title: Sulforaphane content across steamed Brussels sprout samples
Source: https://easdl.org/_PR/view/index.php?aidx=38491&bidx=3475
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

Ji-Sang Kim, October 31, 2023. The author directly measured sulforaphane and allyl isothiocyanate in Brussels sprouts under several steaming and ultrasound conditions. In the steaming experiment, sulforaphane increased to three minutes and then declined with longer heating. The measurements are food composition and have no participant pharmacokinetic or dyskinesia outcome.
