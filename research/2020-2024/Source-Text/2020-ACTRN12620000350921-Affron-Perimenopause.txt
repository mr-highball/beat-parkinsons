Original ACTRN12620000350921 trial registration
Public source https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=379418

Trial registration ACTRN12620000350921. Submitted 2020-03-06. First listed 2020-03-12. Actual first enrollment 2020-04-03 and last 2020-05-12. Last update recorded as 2021-10-14. The registry threshold is a total Greene Climacteric Scale score of at least 16; the article describes a score greater than 16. Outcomes also include PANAS, SF-36 and several Greene subscales. The registry lists the later article without a separate results table. Official record reviewed 2026-11-15.

The ANZCTR record lists prospective registration on March 12, 2020, and first enrollment on April 3, 2020. It specifies standardized affron saffron extract, 14 mg twice daily for 12 weeks, and explicitly excludes Parkinson’s and other neurological diseases. The main outcome is the total Greene Climacteric Scale at baseline and weeks 4, 8 and 12. The psychological subscale is secondary. The registry says allocation was unconcealed, while the paper describes sequence concealment. The final registered enrollment is 86, and the publication listed in the registry is the same trial.
