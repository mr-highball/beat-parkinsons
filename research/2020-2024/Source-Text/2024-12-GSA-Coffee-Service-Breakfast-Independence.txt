Coffee service within a redesigned nursing-home breakfast
Source: https://doi.org/10.1093/geroni/igae098.1951
DOI: 10.1093/geroni/igae098.1951
Coverage: Original publisher abstract and complete conference PDF, printed page 596

A before-and-after project in four nursing homes included 104 residents and 134 staff. The breakfast changes offered bread, fillings, coffee and tea and encouraged residents to prepare their own sandwiches. The authors report more observed engagement and independence at breakfast, with no clear staff behavior change. The multi-component routine with no independently assigned coffee exposure provides no Parkinson-specific effect or patient fatigue comparison.
