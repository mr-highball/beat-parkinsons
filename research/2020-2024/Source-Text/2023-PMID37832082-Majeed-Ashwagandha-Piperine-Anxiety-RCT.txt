Title: A standardized Ashwagandha root extract alleviates stress, anxiety, and improves quality of life in healthy adults by modulating stress hormones: Results from a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study.
PMID: 37832082
PMCID: PMC10578737
DOI: 10.1097/MD.0000000000035521
First published: 2023
Authors: Majeed M, Nagabhushanam K, Mundkur L
Journal: Medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37832082/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

60-day double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (PMC10578737, n=54 randomized, n=50 completers) of 500 mg Ashwagandha root extract + 5 mg piperine vs placebo in healthy adults with mild-to-moderate stress/anxiety. Improved PSS, GAD-7, QoL, and CANTAB multitasking/concentration. Bundled with Ashwagandha (500 mg); cannot attribute effects to the 5 mg piperine bioenhancer.
