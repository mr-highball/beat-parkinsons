Dysarthria after historical methylmercury exposure
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10748001/
PMID: 38133424
PMCID: PMC10748001
DOI: 10.3390/toxics11121023
Coverage: full journal article; exposure Methods and examiner Table 5

The 2023 journal publication reports the same 327-person comparison as the preprint and identifies the study as a reanalysis of earlier survey participants. Table 5 lists examiner-rated dysarthria in 24.9% of the exposed residents versus 1.6% of controls. The survey also lists participant-reported difficulty speaking in a separate table. Industrial contamination and historical geography define the exposure groups. The study gives no species-specific seafood exposure, grilled sardine comparison or Parkinson speech result. It is distinct from the Bordeaux contaminated-sardine botulism outbreak.
