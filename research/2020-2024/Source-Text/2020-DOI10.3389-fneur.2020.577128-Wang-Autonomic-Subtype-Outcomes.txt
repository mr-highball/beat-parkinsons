Title: Association Analyses of Autonomic Dysfunction and Sympathetic Skin Response in Motor Subtypes of Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neurology/articles/10.3389/fneur.2020.577128/full
DOI: 10.3389/fneur.2020.577128
Publication date: 2020-11-03
Source depth: Publisher original article body and Tables 1–3
Date basis: Publisher publication date.
Design: Observational comparison of 101 people with Parkinson disease

The study included 51 participants with postural instability/gait difficulty, 33 with tremor-dominant disease and 17 intermediate. Table 3 reports a median gastrointestinal autonomic domain score of 4 versus 2 for the two main subtype groups (p<.001). This seven-item composite does not provide a separate salivation item estimate. A 28-person 2019 conference abstract by overlapping authors at the same Wenzhou institution reports a salivation-specific comparison. The two versions have different sample sizes and outcome reporting, and possible sample overlap is unresolved. This paper provides no measured raw-fig effect or independent confirmation of that item.
