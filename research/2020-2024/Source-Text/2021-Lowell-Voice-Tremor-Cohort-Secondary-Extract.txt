2021 | Lowell et al. | Repeated voice tremor assessments in the earlier crossover cohort

Source: publisher original abstract, PMID 33864634, DOI 10.1002/lary.29558, published April 17, 2021.

The 16-person cross-over sample and the mean age and first voice handicap characteristics match the earlier Lowell trial. The secondary analysis summarizes 18 repeated acoustic measures per variable. Mean frequency of amplitude oscillation was 4.67 Hz. The report identifies more severe essential voice tremor with more affected vocal tract structures and stronger response to the study drug. Self-reported speaking effort and voice handicap were also recorded. The accessible report gives no habitual voice volume or soybean-oil-specific effect estimate.
