Design: Visual-cortex stimulation and magnetic resonance spectroscopy study

Sixteen healthy people were recruited. Accelerated but not single-session stimulation reduced GABA+ for up to a day, and basic tested vision and cognition changed little. The reference to hallucinations in other studies concerns damaged visual pathways. This study supplies neither Parkinson clinical symptoms nor a food contrast.
