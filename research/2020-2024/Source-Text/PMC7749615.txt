Title: Assessing differential effects of single and accelerated low-frequency rTMS to the visual cortex on GABA and glutamate concentrations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7749615/
PMID: 32964685
PMCID: PMC7749615
DOI: 10.1002/brb3.1845
Publication date: 2020-09-23
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: Visual-cortex stimulation and magnetic resonance spectroscopy study

Sixteen healthy people were recruited. Accelerated but not single-session stimulation reduced GABA+ for up to a day, and basic tested vision and cognition changed little. The reference to hallucinations in other studies concerns damaged visual pathways. This study supplies neither Parkinson clinical symptoms nor a food contrast.
