Title: A 3-Month Comparison of Blueberry and Date Consumption among Older Adults — protocol and recruitment preprint
DOI: 10.2196/preprints.64013
Source: https://preprints.jmir.org/preprint/64013
Date basis: 2024 preprint metadata.
Archive scope: Public author abstract and registry linkage. The manuscript requires account access. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

The authors identify Deglet Noor pitted dates as the isocaloric comparator for frozen blueberries. The public abstract reports 61 participants ages 70–94, with 53 percent assigned blueberries. It describes the recruitment and planned blood pressure, physical function, falls and cognition analyses. It does not report comparative treatment results, a distinct apathy or pain scale, or a Parkinson cohort.

This preprint, the publicly filed protocol and NCT05358210 refer to the same trial. Their dates and access scopes are preserved separately. The registry lists the study as completed without posted results.
