Title: Delta- and mu-opioid pathways are involved in the analgesic effect of Ocimum basilicum L in mice.
PMID: 31837414
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2019.112471
First published: 2020
Authors: Bae AH, Kim G, Seol GH, Lee SB, Lee JM, Chang W, Min SS
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31837414/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse pain study administering Ocimum basilicum L. essential oil (i.p. or inhalation) across formalin, acetic acid, thermal plantar, and carrageenan inflammatory pain models plus rotarod motor coordination testing. Exerted analgesic effects across all pain models (thermal latency increased at high dose) with no rotarod motor-coordination impairment; formalin analgesia was significantly antagonized by delta-opioid (naltrindole), mu-opioid (naloxone), and L-arginine pretreatment. Non-oral essential oil in rodent models.
