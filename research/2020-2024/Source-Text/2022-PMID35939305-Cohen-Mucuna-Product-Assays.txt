Title: Levodopa content of Mucuna pruriens supplements in the NIH Dietary Supplement Label Database
Source: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2795169
PMID: 35939305
DOI: 10.1001/jamaneurol.2022.2184
Publication date: 2022-08-08
Source depth: Original research letter and table.

Sixteen of 20 identified products were analyzed. One had no detectable levodopa and the other 15 varied markedly in their measured content. For products with a declared extract amount, the table compares measured levodopa with an estimate based on authenticated seeds. The displayed percentages are measured divided by estimated, distinct from the 2017 paper's percentages of product levodopa label claims. One sampled unit of each product allows no batch-to-batch estimate. The study measured constituents without participant outcomes or a drooling assessment.
