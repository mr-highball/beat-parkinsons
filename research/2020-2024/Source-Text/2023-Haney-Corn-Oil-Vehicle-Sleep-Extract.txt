Corn oil vehicle held constant in the phase 2a sleep comparison
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10287566/
Coverage: Factual summary of primary report and outcome.

First published June 8, 2023. The phase 2a report identifies pharmaceutical-grade corn oil as the common vehicle for AEF0117 and its matching placebo in cannabis use disorder. Extended Data Figure 7 gives treatment p=.0071 for actigraphy snooze duration and p=.0237 for number of wake bouts. Figure 8 gives no clear subjective waking-often difference between either dose and placebo despite an interaction. Refining is unreported. The comparison holds corn oil constant and gives no independent corn-oil or Parkinson insomnia result.
