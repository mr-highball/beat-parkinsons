Title: Effects of date fruit (Phoenix dactylifera L.) on labor and delivery outcomes: a systematic review and meta-analysis

The review synthesizes eight studies and evaluates labor-stage duration, Bishop score and cesarean delivery. It reports a shorter active phase in three trials (mean difference −109.3 minutes, 95% CI −196.32 to −22.29, I²=89%) and a higher Bishop score in two trials (mean difference 2.45, 95% CI 1.87 to 3.04). The mixed and heterogeneous obstetric findings are not a pain-severity comparison. No independent pain estimate is contributed by this review.
