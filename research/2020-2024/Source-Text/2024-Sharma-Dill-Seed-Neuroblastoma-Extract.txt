Sharma et al., 2024. Neuroprotection by Anethum graveolens (Dill) Seeds and Its Phytocompounds in SH-SY5Y Neuroblastoma Cell Lines and Acellular Assays.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11241255/
Access and coverage: Primary article Introduction, Results and Materials and Methods.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

Sharma and colleagues exposed SH-SY5Y human neuroblastoma cells to hydrogen peroxide and assayed hexane and ethyl acetate extracts of dill seeds and selected constituents. The assays reported differences in oxidative stress markers, mitochondrial potential and other cellular or biochemical endpoints. The introduction mentions Parkinson disease among neurodegenerative diseases. The experiments did not identify a Parkinson population, a Parkinson-specific symptom, or culinary dill leaf exposure.
