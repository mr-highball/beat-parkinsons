Title: TMS-evoked potentials unveil occipital network involvement in patients diagnosed with Parkinson's disease within 5 years of inclusion
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11443052/
PMID: 39349492
PMCID: PMC11443052
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-024-00793-0
Publication date: 2024-09-30
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

TMS-evoked potentials compare cortical responses after stimulating motor, prefrontal and visual regions in Parkinson disease and comparison participants. Occipital network responses are evoked physiology. The study does not measure meal-associated visual hallucinations or record dried fig exposure.
