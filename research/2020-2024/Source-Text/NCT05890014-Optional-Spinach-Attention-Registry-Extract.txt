# Registry for optional spinach and perinatal attention

Source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05890014
Coverage official arms and secondary measures

NCT05890014 first posted June 5, 2023. The intervention offers one or two choices per day from a list including spinach, fruit and dark chocolate, versus continuing usual intake. The secondary measures include sustained attention and executive functioning. The registry assigns a food pattern and does not specify a cooked spinach arm or ingredient-specific exposure contrast. The linked thesis independently verifies several attention outcomes.
