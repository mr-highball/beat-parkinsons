Title: Distinct grey and white matter changes are associated with the phenomenology of visual hallucinations in Lewy Body Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11208453/
PMID: 38926597
PMCID: PMC11208453
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-65536-w
Publication year: 2024
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The MRI comparison includes 28 people with Lewy body disease and visual experiences, separating minor and complex phenomena in ratings. It associates regional structure with symptom characteristics. It contains no food exposure or time-linked change after dried fig.

The clinical group included 16 dementia with Lewy bodies and 12 Parkinson dementia cases. Visual phenomena are characterized by type and severity before the imaging comparison.
