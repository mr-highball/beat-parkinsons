Title: Cardioprotective and hypotensive mechanistic insights of hydroethanolic extract of Cucumis melo L. kernels in isoprenaline-induced cardiotoxicity based on metabolomics and in silico electrophysiological models.
PMID: 38348351
PMCID: PMC10859416
DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2023.1277594
First published: 2023
Authors: Wahid M, Saqib F, Abbas G, Shah S, Alshammari A, Albekairi TH et al.
Journal: Front Pharmacol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38348351/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rat study of hydroethanolic extract of Cucumis melo seed kernels showing NO-dependent vasorelaxation, negative inotropic/chronotropic atrial effects, hypotensive response in normotensive rats, and cardioprotection against isoprenaline. Plant-part caveat: seed kernels, not edible raw cantaloupe flesh, and no orthostatic blood pressure testing.
