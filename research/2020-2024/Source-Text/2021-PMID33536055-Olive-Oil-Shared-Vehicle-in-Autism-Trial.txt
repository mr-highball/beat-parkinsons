The randomized study enrolled 150 people with autism aged 5–21. The original specifies olive oil in the two active cannabinoid formulations. The registration identifies the matching placebo as olive oil with flavorings. The assigned groups therefore provide cannabinoid comparisons with olive oil shared across arms. The primary outcomes concern disruptive behavior. The original reports no anxiety contrast independently attributable to olive oil and supplies no Parkinson population or culinary olive oil comparison.

This trial, its 2022 sleep analysis (PMID 35884990), and the review trail (PMID 35453548) represent one study family. The source adds exposure-design context for claim 0343.
