Title: Mouthwash Formulas for Xerostomia and Malodour
Authors: Ebrar İnal and colleagues
First posted: 2024-01-12
DOI: 10.21203/rs.3.rs-3821986/v1
Source: https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-3821986/v1
Coverage: Original factual extract of the preprint's Materials and Table 1.

The investigators purchased Corylus avellana seed oil from FitoBio, Istanbul. Table 1 assigns it to four formulations alongside cinnamon leaf oil and a carboxymethyl cellulose solution. The group spans 2–4% of hazelnut seed oil. The preparations were made as mouthwash with other components. The Materials do not identify the seed oil's pressing or roasting history. An introductory passage discusses cold-pressed oils generally.

The preprint measured physical properties and cell-based outcomes. It did not measure saliva output, drooling, swallowing function or speech in people. It supplies formulation provenance for the same research group's 2026 ex vivo wetting report.
