Original study scope
The proceedings contain unrelated toxicology cases. Coffee serves as a caffeine reference and exposures involve other caffeine products. A foraging event initially described as wild onion was attributed to a different plant. Orange can be a color or a hospital place name, Sage and Melissa are people, and poison sumac is not culinary sumac.

Continuing evidence gaps
Retain the species and exposure distinctions. Direct Parkinson outcomes after ordinary coffee or edible onion remain untested in these abstracts.
