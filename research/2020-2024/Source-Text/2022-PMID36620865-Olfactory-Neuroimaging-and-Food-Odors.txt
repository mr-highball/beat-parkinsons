Original study scope
The review summarizes functional near-infrared spectroscopy during odor perception. Oils, culinary odors and food terms occur in odor and test-stimulus contexts. Measured brain activation after an odor does not give a symptom change after consumption.

Continuing evidence gaps
Verify the individual odor study and whether it tested food use or only stimulus response. A food-specific Parkinson symptom effect remains open.
