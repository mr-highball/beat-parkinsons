Title: The effects of Anethum graveolens (dill) supplementation on lipid profile and glycemic control: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 33624557
DOI: 10.1080/10408398.2021.1889459
First published: 2022
Authors: Mousavi SM, Beatriz Pizarro A, Akhgarjand C, Bagheri A, Persad E, Karimi E, Wong A, Jayedi A
Journal: Critical reviews in food science and nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33624557/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis (Mousavi et al., 2022, Crit Rev Food Sci Nutr) pooling 7 RCTs (n=330) of oral Anethum graveolens supplementation on cardiometabolic markers (LDL -15.64 mg/dL p=0.001, insulin -2.28 uU/mL p=0.001, HOMA-IR -1.06 p=0.01; null TC, TG, HDL, FBS). Confirms human metabolic RCT literature does not assess Parkinson's motor, neuropsychiatric, sleep, or autonomic endpoints and mixes leaf/herb/seed preparations.
