Title: Self-reported food intolerances in an Indian population: Need for individualization rather than a universal low-FODMAP diet.
PMID: 38034051
PMCID: PMC10684987
DOI: 10.1002/jgh3.12981
First published: 2023
Authors: Abraham P, Dhoble P, Desai D, Joshi A, Gupta T
Journal: JGH Open
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38034051/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Cross-sectional intolerance survey of 400 healthy Indian adults and 204 IBS patients evaluating high-FODMAP foods. Food intolerance was more frequent in IBS (72.1% vs 44.8%, p<0.00001); cauliflower intolerance was specifically reported more often in the IBS-diarrhea subgroup than IBS-constipation. Supports individualized GI tolerance rather than universal cauliflower benefit or harm in PD constipation.
