Title: Comparison of Associations between MIND and Mediterranean Diet Scores with Patient-Reported Outcomes in Parkinson's Disease — fatigue and ingredient scope
PMID: 36501214
PMCID: PMC9739738
DOI: 10.3390/nu14235185
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9739738/
Published: 2022-12-06.
Archive scope: Original Methods, symptom regression table, and cohort identification. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

The Modifiable Variables in Parkinsonism study contributed 1,205 online survey participants. The paper estimates cross-sectional associations between overall diet scores and the previous week's patient-reported symptoms. Higher scores were associated with lower fatigue ratings. The reported coefficients are −0.675 for Mediterranean score and −1.83 for MIND score, with different scale inputs and a self-selected cohort. The paper includes respondents with different forms of parkinsonism and does not verify every diagnosis clinically.

The regression input is a combined diet score, and the article supplies no bell-pepper exposure or preparation-specific fatigue comparison. The observational comparison also does not establish that eating an individual component changes fatigue.
