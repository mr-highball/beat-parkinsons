Title: Incremental Doses of Nitrate-Rich Beetroot Juice Do Not Modify Cognitive Function and Cerebral Blood Flow in Overweight and Obese Older Adults: A 13-Week Pilot Randomised Clinical Trial.
PMID: 35268027
PMCID: PMC8912345
DOI: 10.3390/nu14051052
First published: 2022
Authors: Babateen AM, Shannon OM, O'Brien GM, Okello E, Smith E, Olgacer D, et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35268027/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

13-week double-blind randomized placebo-controlled dose-ranging parallel trial in n=62 overweight/obese older adults (60-75 y) testing three BRJ nitrate schedules vs nitrate-depleted placebo: no statistically significant improvements in COMPASS cognitive/executive/attention battery or cerebral blood flow despite sustained biomarker increases. Major competing null chronic older-adult RCT.
