Title: Bay leaf extract and oral environment trial identity
Source: https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=202102227018503584
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

Kuniyoshi and colleagues, Japanese Pharmacology and Therapeutics 49(11):1913–1924, November 20, 2021. Japanese science index and author institution https://www.tokiwaph.co.jp/en/2021-11-29/ identify a randomized double-blind placebo-controlled study of oral Laurus nobilis extract in healthy people. Original numeric outcomes were not accessible through the identified records. An existing patent records extract and salivary weight; independence of this trial and the patent cohort remains unverified. There is no confirmed clinical drooling or ground bay leaf result.
