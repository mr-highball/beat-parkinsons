Title: True Digestibility of Tryptophan in Plant and Animal Protein.
PMID: 39307282
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2024.09.014
First published: 2024
Authors: Kashyap S, Devi S, Pasanna RM, Preston T, Kurpad AV
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39307282/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Human dual-isotope tracer study (CTRI/2017/11/010468, CTRI/2018/03/012265, CTRI/2020/04/024512) measuring true ileal tryptophan (Trp, an LNAA) digestibility of 2H-intrinsically labeled animal vs plant proteins in healthy humans: whole egg (n=6) 91.4% +/- 2.6%, egg white (n=6) 89.7% +/- 2.5%, chicken meat (n=6) 95.9% +/- 2.2%, goat milk (n=7) 92.8% +/- 2.9%, versus whole mung bean (n=6) 67.6% +/- 3.7%, chickpea (n=5) 72.6% +/- 2.3%, yellow pea (n=5) 72.5% +/- 2.2%, and dehulled mung bean (n=6) 74.5% +/- 4.4%. Animal protein (including whole egg) true LNAA digestibility was significantly higher than plant legumes (p < 0.05), reinforcing why whole cooked eggs match meat/dairy in postprandial LNAA kinetics while legumes show slower/lower LNAA release.
