The investigators assay 172 baseline plasma samples, with repeated samples in 55. The second recruitment cohort uses the previously reported cardiac MIBG method, and a prior p-tau181 result is reused. Visual hallucinations are rated as present or absent for clinical classification, without a dried fig exposure.

This plasma paper combines participants recruited through two longitudinal protocols and explicitly cites the earlier cardiac MIBG methods and p-tau181 analysis. The overlapping recruitment base precludes treating reports as independent cohorts. The originals do not give exact person-by-person overlap with the separately published 95-person analysis.
