Title: Daily Consumption of High-Polyphenol Olive Oil Enhances Hippocampal Neurogenesis in Old Female Rats
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36416641/
PMID: 36416641
DOI: 10.1080/27697061.2022.2144540
First public: 2022-11-23
Coverage: Factual extract from the original author abstract. Group estimates and figures remain open.

Kimizoglu and colleagues assigned 34 older female Sprague Dawley rats to distilled water, low-polyphenol olive oil or high-polyphenol extra-virgin olive oil by oral gavage for six weeks. The abstract reports no statistically clear difference between groups in anxiety-like open-field behavior or spatial memory (p>.05). It gives no group means, uncertainty intervals or exact p values for those comparisons. Selected neurogenesis markers improved with high-polyphenol oil. The marker results and the behavioral comparisons are separate. The animals had no Parkinson model, and the abstract does not identify the pressing conditions.
