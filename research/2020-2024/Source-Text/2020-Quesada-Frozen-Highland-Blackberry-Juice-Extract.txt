Factual extract of Methods and Results checked 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

Thirteen healthy adults in a randomized crossover consumed a 50% v/v microfiltered tropical highland blackberry drink or water, with optional sugar, on a high-fat/high-carbohydrate diet challenge. Both trial periods were completed by all 13. At each of three meals, subjects drank 250 mL of their assigned beverage. The cultivar Vino of Rubus adenotrichos was stored frozen at −20 °C before processing with crushing, enzymes and microfiltration. Participants received the processed drink, not whole frozen fruit. The measurements were lipid, glucose and antioxidant-enzyme endpoints. The study did not assess pain, discomfort or a PD-specific outcome.
