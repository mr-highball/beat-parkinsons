Title: A Novel Trial of Mirabegron and Behavioral Modification Including Pelvic Floor Exercise for Overactive Bladder in Parkinson's Disease (MAESTRO).
PMID: 36425049
PMCID: PMC9682970
DOI: 10.7759/cureus.31818
Publication year: 2022
First public date basis: NLM and original journal record.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9682970/
Coverage: Complete article-body context reviewed 2026-11-22.

MAESTRO studied mirabegron plus behavioral training versus placebo plus training for overactive bladder in Parkinson disease. The primary bladder symptom endpoint was unmet. Date and dated are diary and consent fields. No dried dates were assigned and no date-specific pain contrast was measured.
