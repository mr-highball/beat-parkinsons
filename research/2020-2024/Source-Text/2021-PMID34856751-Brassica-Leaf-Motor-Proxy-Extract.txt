Title: Anti-Parkinson’s evaluation of Brassica juncea leaf extract and underlying mechanism of its phytochemicals
Source: https://www.imrpress.com/journal/FBL/26/11/10.52586/5007
First published online: 2021-11-30
PMID: 34856751
DOI: 10.52586/5007
Text coverage: publisher article, Methods 5.9.1 and Figure 1.

The study compared oral ethanol leaf extract with vehicle, haloperidol control and a levodopa-carbidopa comparison in rats. Group size was six. The favorable behavioral results used bar catalepsy, grid hang and horizontal bar tests. The protocol measured imposed-posture immobility as a rigidity proxy and used separate strength and coordination tests. A separately measured resting tremor result and toasted mustard-seed exposure are absent. The reported leaf phytochemicals and docking results are preparation or mechanism leads.
