Original roasted kale tea Methods and Tables 1–2
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8423870/

The article first appeared online August 10, 2021. The investigators prepared a tea material using repeated roasting and drying of kale leaves. Chemical profiling identified 25 aroma compounds in raw kale and 37 in the roasted tea material. Two trained assessors sniffed separated compounds at a gas chromatography port. The investigators listed 18 aroma-active compounds, including two dimethylpyrazines with roasted notes and a dilution factor of 256. The measurements characterize aroma of the prepared product. The assessors were not studied for a change in clinical smell loss after eating kale.
