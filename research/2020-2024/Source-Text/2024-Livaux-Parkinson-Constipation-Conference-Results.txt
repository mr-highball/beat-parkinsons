Title: Livaux crossover in Parkinson constipation, conference outcomes
Source: https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/therapeutic-restoration-of-faecalibacterium-prausnitzii-in-parkinsons-disease-using-targeted-fructooligosaccharides-a-double-blind-placebo-controlled-cross-over-trial/

Checked: 2026-11-22

Access evidence: Original Movement Disorders Society 2024 abstract 753 rechecked. Earlier abstract and presentation date are dated in the linked catalog synthesis.

Twelve people with Parkinson disease and functional constipation were randomized to the order of a proprietary gold kiwifruit-derived product and matching placebo. The reported complete bowel movements were 2.2 per week in both periods (difference 0.0, 95% CI −0.7 to +0.8, p=.90). Spontaneous bowel movements, one secondary outcome, were 3.8 versus 3.3 per week (difference +0.5, reported 95% CI 0.0 to +1.0 after rounding, p=.047). The abstract reports no clear PAC-SYM difference. It cannot give an estimate for an isolated symptom of freezing, postural instability or impaired attention after eating raw kiwi.

Continuing evidence gaps
The one cohort has two conference reports and an official registration. Do not sum them as replications. Confirm any full paper, prespecified analysis, attrition between registry accrual and randomization, and the interpretation of the secondary bowel result across multiple endpoints.
