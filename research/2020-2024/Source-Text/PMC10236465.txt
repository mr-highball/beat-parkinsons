Title: Brain dysfunction in gait disorders of Caribbean atypical Parkinsonism and progressive supranuclear palsy patients: A comparative study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10236465/
PMID: 37247501
PMCID: PMC10236465
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2023.103443
Publication date: 2023-05-25
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

Clinical ratings, recorded gait and structural MRI are compared in 16 Caribbean atypical parkinsonism cases, 15 PSP cases and 17 controls. The study does not track dried figs or the frequency of visual episodes.
