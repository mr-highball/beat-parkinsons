Steamed ginger extract and postpartum pain
Source: https://www.jmatonline.com/PDF/334-340-PB-13831.pdf
DOI: 10.35755/jmedassocthai.2023.04.13831
Coverage: Original April 2023 paper, Methods and Table 3

Jaiarree and colleagues report 99 women after first vaginal delivery, 33 in each steamed ethanolic ginger extract, placebo and paracetamol group. Perineal pain mean ratings for ginger versus placebo were 3.97 versus 6.30 at 24 hours, 2.48 versus 6.09 at 48 hours, and 1.42 versus 2.61 at 72 hours. Each overall comparison is reported at p<.001, with favorable post-hoc ginger comparisons. Uterine comparisons were inconclusive. The Methods describe ginger assignment in even weeks and placebo or paracetamol in odd weeks. The open design and allocation schedule leave time-related confounding possible. The trial used steamed extract, with no Parkinson cohort.
