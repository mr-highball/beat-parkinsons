Constipation domains in the original Parkinson gastrointestinal scale
June 16, 2021 · PMID 34133059 · DOI 10.1002/mds.28675
https://movementdisorders.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mds.28675
Factual extract from original validation paper and Cambridge thesis §3.5.4 and Appendix B.

The original Gastrointestinal Dysfunction Scale for Parkinson’s Disease has 12 items. Its constipation domain includes bowel frequency, straining, stool hardness and incomplete evacuation. The development thesis specifies six-month recall. NCT07882108 lists the GIDS-PD at baseline and follow-up during a 12-week ginger extract intervention. The registration does not specify item-level contrasts, a change to recall, or posted results. The scale validation and trial are distinct datasets.
