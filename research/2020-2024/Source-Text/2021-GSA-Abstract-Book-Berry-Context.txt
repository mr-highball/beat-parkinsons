Printed p. 864 (file page 867). Courtney Millar, Alyssa Dufour, Marian Hannan and Shivani Sahni report the Framingham community cohort abstract on mixed-berry flavonoid intake and depressive symptoms. Mixed blueberries, blackberries and raspberries are grouped. No individual blueberry preparation or bowel endpoint appears. The adjusted change in CES-D was −1.06 points per 250 mg/day increase in mixed-berry flavonoids (SE 0.61, P=.08) in 1,278 adults with longitudinal measures. Neither the enrollment context nor outcome was Parkinson constipation.

Printed p. 20 (file page 23). Constipation appears as a symptom measured before and after home-based palliative care for older adults. Printed p. 791 (file page 794). Constipation medicines are one exposure class in a pharmacoepidemiologic fall-related-injury study of older adults. These abstracts report different populations and interventions from the mixed-berry presentation.
