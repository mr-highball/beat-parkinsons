Title: Factors associated the worsening of symptoms and quality of life in women with urinary incontinence
Source: https://periodicos.ufn.edu.br/index.php/disciplinarumS/article/download/3959/2922/0
DOI: 10.37777/dscs.v22n3-008
Date evidence: issue labeled 2021; accepted February 10, 2022. Exact public posting date unresolved.
Coverage: Factual extract from the original Methods, Results and Table 5.

The Brazilian cross-sectional survey included 43 women, 20 with urinary incontinence. Of the 20, five reported stress, four urgency and 11 mixed incontinence. Overall impact was assessed using the ICIQ-SF and used to form eight mild and 12 severe cases. One participant in each group reported pineapple once or twice weekly; the other 18 reported no pineapple. The percentages printed in the table use the pooled denominator of 20. The report gives no pineapple-specific statistical test, urgency-only pineapple data, raw preparation or Parkinson comparison.
