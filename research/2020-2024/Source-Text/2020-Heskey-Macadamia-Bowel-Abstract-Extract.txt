Whole macadamia nuts and bowel outcomes

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7258298/
Coverage: Author conference abstract
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published May 29, 2020. Thirty-five adults crossed over between eight weeks of usual diet and a mix of raw and roasted whole macadamia nuts supplying 15% of calories. Bowel frequency showed no clear difference. Bristol stool type had p=.049 but the direction and phase values were unreported. The nut matrix retained fiber and did not test oil. The 2023 Jones body and supplements did not supply the missing bowel values.
