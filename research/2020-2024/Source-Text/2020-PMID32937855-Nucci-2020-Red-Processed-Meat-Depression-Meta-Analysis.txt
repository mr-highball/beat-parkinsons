Title: Red and Processed Meat Consumption and Risk of Depression: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
PMID: 32937855
PMCID: PMC7559491
DOI: 10.3390/ijerph17186686
First published: 2020
Authors: Nucci D, Fatigoni C, Amerio A, Odone A, Gianfredi V
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32937855/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 17 observational studies (n=241,738 participants): higher red and processed meat intake was associated with modestly higher risk of depression (pooled ES 1.08; 95% CI 1.04-1.12; p<0.001). Limits: observational, pools processed and unprocessed red meat, non-PD, directly opposes meat-vs-abstainer meta-analyses.
