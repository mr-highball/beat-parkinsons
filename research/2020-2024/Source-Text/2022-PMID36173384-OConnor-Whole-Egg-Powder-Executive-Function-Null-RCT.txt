Title: The effects of 9 months of formulated whole-egg or milk powder food products as meal or snack replacements on executive function in preadolescents: A randomized, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 36173384
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/nqac281
First published: 2022
Authors: O'Connor PJ, Chen X, Coheley LM, Yu M, Laing EM, Oshri A
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36173384/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind, placebo-controlled, 3-arm parallel randomized trial (NCT03739424) in 122 preadolescents (aged 9-13 y, 58 females) comparing 9 months of macronutrient-matched food replacements containing whole-egg powder vs milk powder vs gelatin placebo on 3 primary NIH Toolbox-Cognitive Battery executive function outcomes (mental flexibility, working memory, selective attention/inhibitory control). Results: whole-egg powder had NULL effect on all three executive function and selective attention measures compared with placebo (only milk powder improved selective attention/inhibitory control by +5.8 T-score, p=0.049, d=0.44). Directly counters whole-egg executive/attention efficacy claims despite choline hypothesis; preadolescent population and powdered matrix also differ from cooked whole eggs in PD.
