Original study scope
A SARS-CoV-2 smell identification comparison used standardized odor items, including clove and cherry. The reported proportions reflect correct recognition of odors in antibody-positive and antibody-negative participants.

Continuing evidence gaps
The odor cards are genuine ingredient-related sensory stimuli. The comparison does not assess clove consumption and pain, cherry consumption and fatigue, or date consumption and pain in Parkinson disease.
