Title: Brazil Nut (Bertholletia excelsa H.B.K.) Consumption in Energy-Restricted Intervention Decreases Proinflammatory Markers and Intestinal Permeability of Women with Overweight/Obesity: A Controlled Trial (Brazilian Nuts Study).
PMID: 39025334
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2024.07.016
First published: 2024
Authors: Silveira BKS, Silva AD, Rocha DMUP, Waskow K, Martino HSD, Bressan J, et al.
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39025334/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled trial in n=46 women with obesity consuming 8 g/d Brazil nuts vs control within an energy-restricted diet: lowered lactulose/mannitol intestinal permeability ratio and inflammatory markers. Did not measure clinical constipation, bowel frequency, or PD symptoms.
