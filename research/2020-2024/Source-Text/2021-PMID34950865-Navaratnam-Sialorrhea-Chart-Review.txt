Title: Natural history and patterns of treatment change in Parkinson's disease: A retrospective chart review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8671728/
PMID: 34950865
PMCID: PMC8671728
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2021.100125
Publication date: 2021-12-08
Date basis: first online date in original JATS. The collection is dated 2022.
Source depth: factual extract of original chart review, Figure 1C image and Supplementary Table E2

The authors abstracted 203 Parkinson patient charts from 18 clinics. Figure 1C plots charted worsening of non-neuromotor symptoms at index and post-index. Its sialorrhea bars read 7% and 6%. The separate dysphagia bars read 2% and 9%. These rounded chart summaries contain no paired individual drooling score change.

Supplementary Table E2 reports four patients (2%) with drug-related sialorrhea in its separate adverse-event accounting. That table and Figure 1C measure different events. The observational chart design supplies no raw-fig intake, randomized food exposure or change in graded drooling after a culinary intervention.
