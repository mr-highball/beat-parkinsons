Title: Effect of 4 weeks daily wild blueberry supplementation on symptoms of depression in adolescents.
PMID: 32151287
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114520000926
First published: 2020
Authors: Fisk J, Khalid S, Reynolds SA, Williams CM
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32151287/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled 4-week trial in n=64 healthy adolescents (12-17 y) consuming daily wild blueberry drink (~253 mg anthocyanins) vs matched placebo. Wild blueberry significantly reduced self-reported Mood and Feelings Questionnaire (MFQ) depressive symptoms vs placebo (p=0.02; reported 95% CI -6.71, -5.35), while RCADS anxiety and transient affect showed no between-group difference. Limits: healthy non-PD adolescents; reconstituted wild blueberry powder drink.
