Title: Effect of Docosahexaenoic Acid and Eicosapentaenoic Acid Supplementation on Sleep Quality in Healthy Subjects: A Randomized, Double-Blinded, Placebo-Controlled Trial.
PMID: 36235788
PMCID: PMC9573173
DOI: 10.3390/nu14194136
First published: 2022
Authors: Yokoi-Shimizu K, Yanagimoto K, Hayamizu K
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9573173/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled trial (Yokoi-Shimizu et al., 2022, Nutrients, PMC9573173, n=66 healthy Japanese adults >=45 years) comparing DHA/EPA fish oil capsules against corn oil placebo capsules for 12 weeks. OSA-MA Factor III (frequent dreaming/sleep quality subscale) improved in the DHA/EPA arm relative to corn oil placebo. Because corn oil functioned strictly as the omega-3-free active-trial placebo with no oil-free control, between-arm divergence reflects active omega-3 contrast rather than corn oil worsening dream enactment.
