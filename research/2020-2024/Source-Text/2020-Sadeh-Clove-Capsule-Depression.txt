Clove capsule adjunct and a mislabeled Beck table
Source: https://pjmhsonline.com/2020/july-sep/1635.pdf
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: original Methods, Table 2 and publisher chronology

Sadeh and colleagues report randomizing 60 adults with depression to an oil-reduced clove bud extract preparation with starch in capsules or matched placebo, with citalopram in both groups. Methods identify Beck depression testing. Table 2 prints anxiety, but its values align with Beck depression described in the surrounding Results and Figure 1. Week six means were 7.0 and 10.9 (reported p = .009), with earlier visit comparisons inconclusive. The paper contains no separate anxiety scale or motivational item outcome. The article prints IRCT20190309042979N1 without a verified public registry record. The publisher’s 2020 volume 14 issue record and PDF route conflict with the 2019 volume 13 PDF footer. Cataloged provisionally under the publisher’s assigned issue and flagged for first-public-date recovery. Official issue page: https://pjmhsonline.com/published-issues/july-sep-2020/20-9-1635.
