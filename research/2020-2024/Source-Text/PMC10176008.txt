Original open-label Phase I crossover in healthy subjects, NCT03718806.

The assigned exposure was zoliflodacin taken fasting or with a standardized high-fat breakfast containing eggs, butter, potatoes, bacon, bread, orange juice and whole milk. Back and puncture-site pain are adverse-event entries. The menu and methods contain no dried date-fruit exposure.
