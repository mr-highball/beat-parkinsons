Title: The importance of nutritional status on clinical outcomes among both ICU and non-ICU patients with COVID-19
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9021035/
PMID: 35623817
PMCID: PMC9021035
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnesp.2022.04.016
First published: 2022-04-21
Coverage: Distilled original methods and results or protocol fields, with article identity checked against PubMed.

The observational report evaluates nutritional risk scores and clinical features in 400 people admitted with COVID-19. Chest pain is a reported clinical characteristic and a regression covariate. Admission, death and discharge dates define hospitalization time. The nutritional screen does not identify intake or assignment of dried Phoenix dates.
