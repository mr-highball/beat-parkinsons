Primary six-month pearl millet feeding analysis

Original source https://doi.org/10.1017/S000711452100180X

First published June 3, 2021. The analysis comes from a randomized six-month Indian adolescent feeding study comparing iron-biofortified pearl millet against conventional pearl millet. Accelerometry data were collected from 130 children over six-day periods at baseline and follow-up. Adjusted light activity was 22.3 minutes per day higher with iron-biofortified millet (95% CI 1.8–42.8, p = .034). This measures general activity. The paper does not make a dexterity comparison, and the two arms both received pearl millet.
