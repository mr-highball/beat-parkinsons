Coverage: Factual Methods, Results, Discussion and Figure 2 description from the original publisher PDF.

The authors describe five groups of ten in an ovariectomy and drug model. The Methods identify female ICR mice and elsewhere use rat wording. The forced-swim methods describe time to resting. Figure 2 captions an immobility outcome but labels the axis only as time. Approximate bar heights in seconds are 118 in normal control, 57 in negative control, and 105, 72 and 63 for the low, middle and high extract groups. Results describe increased immobility and claim a dose pattern that does not match the bar order. Discussion describes shortening and reverses the normal and negative control order. Figure 3 gives no clear spontaneous activity contrast. These inconsistencies leave the behavioral direction unresolved. The experiment did not administer whole toasted culinary pepper or study Parkinson depression.
