PROMED pilot outcome abstract and unreported sleep results
June 14, 2024 · DOI 10.2337/db24-133-OR · NCT03975556
https://doi.org/10.2337/db24-133-OR
Factual extract from primary ADA abstract 133-OR with matched design and registration.

This report identifies PROMED and the same intervention program. Forty-four adults were randomized and the per-protocol report gives 15 and 13 by group. It reports cardiometabolic, blood-pressure, diet-quality and depressive-symptom outcomes, with no sleep estimate. The named multiple food and counseling components and absent individual pinto intake preclude a boiled-pinto effect estimate. Retain the sleep questionnaire reporting trail separately from the published pilot outcomes.
