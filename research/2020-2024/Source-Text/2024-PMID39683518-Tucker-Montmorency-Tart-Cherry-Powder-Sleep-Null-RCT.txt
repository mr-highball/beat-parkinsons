Title: Commonly Used Dose of Montmorency Tart Cherry Powder Does Not Improve Sleep or Inflammation Outcomes in Individuals with Overweight or Obesity.
PMID: 39683518
PMCID: PMC11644614
DOI: 10.3390/nu16234125
First published: 2024
Authors: Tucker RM, Kim N, Gurzell E, Mathi S, Chavva S, Senthilkumar D, Bartunek O, Fenton KC
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11644614/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial (Tucker et al., 2024, Nutrients, PMC11644614) in 34 adults with overweight or obesity (BMI 32.1+/-7.0 kg/m2) and self-reported sleep issues comparing 500 mg/day Montmorency tart cherry powder capsules vs placebo taken 1 hour before bed for 14 days. Objective sleep parameters (total sleep time, deep sleep, REM sleep duration, nocturnal sleep, nap duration via Zmachine/Fitbit), subjective insomnia/sleep scores (PSQI, Sleep Quality Scale, Insomnia Severity Index), and inflammatory cytokines (CRP, TNF-alpha, IL-6, IL-8, IL-10, IL-17A) showed NO statistically significant treatment effects (all p>0.05). Crucial competing null trial testing encapsulated dried tart cherry powder in adults with sleep complaints.
