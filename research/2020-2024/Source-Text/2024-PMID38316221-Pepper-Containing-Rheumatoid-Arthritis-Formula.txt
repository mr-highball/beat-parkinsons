Title: Qurs-e-Mafasil versus celecoxib in rheumatoid arthritis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38316221/
PMID: 38316221
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2024.117833
Publication date: 2024-02-03
Coverage: Original author abstract with comparative pain estimates and intervention constituents.

The study randomized 70 adults with rheumatoid arthritis to a multicomponent Unani formula or celecoxib. Fifty completed the per-protocol analysis. The formula included black pepper fruit along with other herbs. The final between-arm pain VAS difference was 3.00 on a 100 mm scale (95% CI −1.82 to 7.84), with earlier intervals also crossing zero. The comparison concerns the complete formula against an active drug and does not isolate cracked pepper or clinical Parkinson pain.
