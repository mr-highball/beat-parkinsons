Coverage: Original Section 2 and Table 3, food texture experiment.

Yao and colleagues compared raw shiitake with samples steamed, boiled, air fried or oven baked. The texture procedure states ten replicate measurements per group. Table 3 compares raw hardness 2007.33 ± 10.97 with 1803.00 ± 244.42 after five minutes of oven baking and 1012.62 ± 251.53 after twenty minutes, using the printed hardness column unit. Raw and five-minute hardness share a significance letter, while the twenty-minute row does not. Chewiness decreases at the longer oven times. The absolute magnitude of the reported adhesiveness force-time measure rises. The paper gives a percentage summary that does not explain all printed absolute entries, so the numerical interpretation here follows the table.

These are contrasting preparation attributes measured on food samples. The researchers did not measure chewing by people, bolus behavior in the throat, airway outcomes, or Parkinson swallowing difficulty. Lower instrumental hardness alone cannot supply a clinical sign.
