Title: UEG 2022 posters P0541 and P0542 on distinct fixed formulations
First published: 2022-10-09
PMCID: PMC9548091
DOI: 10.1002/ueg2.12295
Source: https://doi.org/10.1002/ueg2.12295
Archive scope: Complete individual abstracts P0541 and P0542 from the publisher-rendered collection. Selected members only. The whole accessible poster collection requires additional ingredient-by-member reconciliation.

P0541 studies a fixed probiotic, lemon balm extract and vitamin B2 mixture (BAY3295079) in a simulated colonic system using material from six healthy donors. Microbial composition, metabolites and model barrier measures changed under the mixture. Inflammatory readouts differed by donor. The study was funded by Bayer. The abstract discusses stress and digestive discomfort as a rationale. Participants did not receive the mixture and clinical stress or depression was not measured.

P0542 concerns a different product, the six-extract STW 5-II mixture. It reports fermentation and barrier-related measurements in vitro. Melissa is one component according to the separate primary human trial formulation description (PMCID PMC11064020). Distinguish STW 5-II from the nine-extract STW 5 and the probiotic blend. These selected proceedings supply no isolated brewed preparation or Parkinson clinical endpoint.
