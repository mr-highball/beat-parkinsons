Shiva Saeri and colleagues. Journal of Food Biochemistry 44(9):e13391. First published July 21, 2020. DOI 10.1111/jfbc.13391, PMID 32696531. AGR:IND607109293 and PMID:32696531 refer to the same study.

In propylthiouracil-induced hypothyroid rats, six-week interventions included two doses of a preparation combining Cyperus rotundus, Crocus sativus, Piper nigrum and Boswellia serrata with honey. The author abstract reports favorable Morris water maze and passive avoidance performance and oxidative-marker changes. A later secondary report gives six months, while the primary abstract states six weeks.

The accessible original abstract does not specify the individual component proportions, verified culinary preparations, detailed administration methods or exact between-arm numerical effects. The publisher PDF route was access-limited. These are combined-intervention memory results in a non-Parkinson animal model. Black pepper and saffron effects cannot be attributed independently.
