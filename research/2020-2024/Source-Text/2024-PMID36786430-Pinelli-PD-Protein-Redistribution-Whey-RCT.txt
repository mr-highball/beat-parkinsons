Title: Can we add whey protein supplementation in patients with Parkinson's disease without interfering with levodopa response?
PMID: 36786430
DOI: 10.1080/00207454.2023.2178433
First published: 2024
Authors: Pinelli G, Siri C, Ranghetti A, Cereda E, Barichella M, Pezzoli G, Ferrazzoli D, Frazzitta G
Journal: International Journal of Neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36786430/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Adversarial qualifying PD clinical trial: single-blind randomized trial in 51 hospitalized PD patients undergoing 4-week intensive rehabilitation on a standard protein-redistribution diet randomized to twice-daily whey protein oral formula (n=26) vs magnesium control (n=25). Both arms improved UPDRS-III, TUG, and 6MWT with no between-group difference and stable dopaminergic therapy. Demonstrates that structured protein redistribution and meal timing can mitigate protein-levodopa interference, tempering unspaced high-protein meal risks.
