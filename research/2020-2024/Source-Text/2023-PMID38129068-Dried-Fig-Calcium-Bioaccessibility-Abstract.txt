Title: A comparison of the bioaccessible calcium supplies of various plant-based products relative to bovine milk
PMID: 38129068
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2023.113795
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38129068/
Author repository: https://nottingham-repository.worktribe.com/output/28423268
First publication date: 2023-12-03 (electronic publication in NLM record).
Coverage: Original NLM and author repository abstracts. The complete methods and product tables remain open.

The investigators compared 25 plant products with bovine milk using static simulated digestion. Dried figs were among the tested products whose measured calcium bioaccessibility was below 10%. The abstract describes several potential matrix or fortification explanations for different products. It does not supply a dried fig clinical symptom estimate or a direct absorption comparison in people. The exact product preparation and table-level result need the original body.
