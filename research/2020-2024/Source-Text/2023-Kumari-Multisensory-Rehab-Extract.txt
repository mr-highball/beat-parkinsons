Kumari and colleagues published on July 12, 2023. Fifteen people entered an unblinded, multisensory post-COVID smell and taste rehabilitation program with camera feedback; 11 completed objective follow-up. Kale appears among optional bitter foods. The report gives no count or preparation for kale exposure and no kale-specific arm. Olfactory identification and subjective measures improved during the whole program. The design leaves individual ingredient effects unmeasured and involved post-COVID cases.
