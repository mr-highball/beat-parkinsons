Cooked kidney beans in a laboratory fermentation assay
https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2023.1334623/full

January 8, 2024. Table 1 and section 3.3.5 compare boiled, roasted and grilled kidney beans and lentils in a digestion and culture assay using fecal material from three healthy donors. The reported cooking differences for legumes are between boiling and grilling and differ by donor. The study reports microbial outcomes in the assay and does not identify a pinto cultivar or test sleep or Parkinson outcomes.
