Coverage: Indexed publisher Table 3 and author manuscript thesis section 6.3, Table 2 (printed pp. 280–281). The direct journal PDF remained inaccessible. The thesis award date of June 8, 2023 does not establish its first public availability.

The study randomized 51 healthy adults aged 18–30 to standardized ginger root powder capsules (n = 29, 1.2 g/day, 84 mg/day gingerols and shogaols) or microcrystalline cellulose (n = 22) for 14 days. The DASS-21 anxiety medians (ranges) in the thesis were 2 (0–8) at baseline and 0 (0–4) after ginger, versus 0 (0–14) and 1 (0–6) with placebo. The median of individual changes was −1 (−6 to 2) versus 0 (−8 to 2). Between-arm change gave Mann–Whitney U = 286.0, z = −0.7, p = .511 in the manuscript and p = .51 in the indexed final table. The manuscript alone gives eta-squared = .01. The table supplies no mean difference or confidence interval for this outcome. The low baseline scores and short duration limit inference. In the thesis, 17 of 27 ginger and 4 of 18 placebo respondents correctly guessed assignment (p = .014). This cohort differs from the separate chemotherapy study and supplies no comparison of fresh grated ginger in people with Parkinson disease.
