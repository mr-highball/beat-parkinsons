Title: The combined effect of amyloid-β and tau biomarkers on brain atrophy in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7363702/
PMID: 32674011
PMCID: PMC7363702
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2020.102333
Publication date: 2020-07-02
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: CSF and MRI analysis in probable dementia with Lewy bodies

The study related amyloid and tau markers to atrophy in 86 probable cases. The visual rating scales record radiologic atrophy, and visual hallucinations are clinical context. Neither variable measures an ingestion-linked visual episode response.
