Pomegranate peel in a botanical alertness trial

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8336296/
Coverage: Original Methods, Table 3 and Figure 1
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

Published July 4, 2021. Thirty-six healthy adults were randomized to a four-botanical blend including pomegranate peel, caffeine or placebo. Day-21 predose mean alertness was 7.58 (SD .84) on blend, 6.16 (1.81) on caffeine and 6.42 (1.49) on placebo. Follow-up comparison with placebo gave p=.03, while the group-by-session interaction was p=.07. The blend, healthy population and MAPSS alertness subscale leave raw arils and Parkinson daytime sleepiness unresolved.
