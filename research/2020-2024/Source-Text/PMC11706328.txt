Title: Distinct transcriptional alterations distinguish Lewy body disease from Alzheimer's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11706328/
PMID: 38916996
PMCID: PMC11706328
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awae202
Publication date: 2024-06-25 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The postmortem comparison classifies Lewy body disease, Alzheimer disease and controls and contrasts regional transcriptional alterations. Tissue gene-expression profiles provide no ingestive exposure or Parkinson visual episode-rate comparison.
