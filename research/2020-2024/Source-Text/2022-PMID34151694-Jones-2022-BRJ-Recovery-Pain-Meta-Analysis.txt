Title: The Effect of Nitrate-Rich Beetroot Juice on Markers of Exercise-Induced Muscle Damage: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Human Intervention Trials.
PMID: 34151694
DOI: 10.1080/19390211.2021.1939472
First published: 2022
Authors: Jones L, Bailey SJ, Rowland SN, Alsharif N, Shannon OM, Clifford T
Journal: Journal of dietary supplements
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34151694/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 6 RCTs evaluating beetroot juice on post-exercise pain/recovery: significant increase in pressure pain threshold (less pain sensitivity) at 48 h (SMD 0.58; p=0.03) and 72 h (SMD 0.61; p=0.02). Context meta-analysis.
