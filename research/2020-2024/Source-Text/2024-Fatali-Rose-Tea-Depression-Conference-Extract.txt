Title: Evaluation of the effect of rose essential oil and its comparison with the effect of lemongrass tea on reducing stress and depression in gynecology assistants
Authors: Somaye Fatali, Atefeh Moridi and Mohammad Farjami
Source: https://publish.kne-publishing.com/index.php/tim/article/download/16695/15620/
Reference: First National Congress of Persian Medicine, May 29–31, 2024. Traditional and Integrative Medicine proceedings, printed p. 90. Proceedings DOI 10.18502/tim.v9is1.16695.
Text coverage: Complete original author abstract.

The crossover abstract reports a tea comparison with rose oil inhalation. The title, background and first period describe lemongrass. The second-period description and keywords use lemon balm. The text names the Beck II instrument for stress and depression together. It does not give a separate depression score, arm difference or sample size. It reports no significant tea-versus-rose comparison, a period effect with p<.05 and a carryover test with p=.074.

The author group and topic are compatible with the IRCT lemon-balm and rose registration. The abstract cites neither its identifier nor its enrolled count and describes one-week periods and washout where the registry gives two weeks. Species, timing and mapping of the reported outcome remain unresolved. This abstract does not establish a lemon-balm-specific depression effect.
