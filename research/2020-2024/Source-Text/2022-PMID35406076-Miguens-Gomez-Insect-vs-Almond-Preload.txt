Title: Effect of an Acute Insect Preload vs. an Almond Preload on Energy Intake, Subjective Food Consumption and Intestinal Health in Healthy Young Adults.
PMID: 35406076
PMCID: PMC9002463
DOI: 10.3390/nu14071463
First published: 2022
Authors: Miguéns-Gómez, Sierra-Cruz, Rodríguez-Gallego, Beltrán-Debón, Blay
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35406076/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Acute 3-day crossover in healthy young adults comparing liquid insect preload, liquid almond preload (both 20 g protein), and vehicle before buffet meals. VAS ratings showed lower post-preload sleepiness and tiredness after the insect preload compared with the almond preload, with no almond-vs-vehicle worsening reported. Limits: acute liquid protein drink active comparator, not roasted almonds or clinical daytime sleepiness.
