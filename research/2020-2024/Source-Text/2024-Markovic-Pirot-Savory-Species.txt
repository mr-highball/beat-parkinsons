Primary original research article, openly readable publisher PDF: https://www.nbpi.org.rs/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Marija-Markovic-et-al.-Traditional-use-of-plants-from-the-genus-Satureja-in-the-Pirot-District.pdf

Methods pp.154–155: questionnaire across 144 villages, 631 informants; 73 respondents made 78 reports concerning Satureja. Table 2 p.158 and text: eight mentioned Satureja hortensis: five dry plant for spice, one infusion diabetes, one infusion disease prevention, one unknown use. 65 respondents made 70 reports for S. montana; sore throat accounted for three infusion reports. Exact species contrast refers to this study and does not rule out S. hortensis throat use elsewhere. No fresh-herb meal, Parkinson, voice intensity, listener intelligibility, or treatment outcome is established. Terms describe reported uses, not effectiveness.
