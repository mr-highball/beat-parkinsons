Title: Treatment with Blackberry Extract and Metformin in Sporadic Alzheimer's Disease Model: Impact on Memory, Inflammation, Redox Status, Phosphorylated Tau Protein and Insulin Signaling.
PMID: 38430352
DOI: 10.1007/s12035-024-04062-2
First published: 2024
Authors: de Mello JE, Teixeira FC, Dos Santos A, Luduvico K, Soares de Aguiar MS, Domingues WB et al.
Journal: Molecular neurobiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38430352/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Five-group male rat intracerebroventricular streptozotocin (STZ) sporadic Alzheimer disease model comparing oral blackberry extract (100 and 200 mg/kg) and metformin (150 mg/kg) on memory, brain inflammation, redox status, p-tau, and insulin signaling. Non-PD Alzheimer rat model testing spatial/recognition memory rather than PD motor or attention endpoints.
