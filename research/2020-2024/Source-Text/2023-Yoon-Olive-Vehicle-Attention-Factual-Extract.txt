Olive oil vehicle and measured complex attention
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10383185/
PMID: 37513652
PMCID: PMC10383185
DOI: 10.3390/nu15143234
Coverage: Original full Methods and Table 3

Yoon and colleagues randomized 72 community participants aged 51–82 to hydroxytyrosol-rich desert olive tree pearls or cornstarch placebo for 12 weeks. Both products were gel granules dipped in olive oil. In Table 3, where lower complex-attention scores are better, the pearl group (n=35) changed from 19.1 to 13.5 and the placebo group (n=34) changed from 12.2 to 11.3. The adjusted group-by-time interaction was p=.049. The simple-attention and reaction-time interactions were p=.116 and p=.857. Different baseline scores, multiple endpoints and the common oil vehicle limit interpretation. The comparison tests the pearl formulation, with no oil-free group, verified refining grade or Parkinson subgroup.
