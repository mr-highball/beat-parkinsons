Salter D, Swamy S, Salis KM, Deep DK, Nadig P. A botanical extract blend of Mangifera indica and Sphaeranthus indicus combined with resistance exercise training improves muscle strength and endurance over exercise alone in young men: a randomized, blinded, placebo-controlled trial. Frontiers in Nutrition 11:1393917; published 2024-05-03. PMID 38765822; PMC11099261; DOI 10.3389/fnut.2024.1393917. Full original https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2024.1393917/full . Registered as CTRI/2018/12/016641 in the article; original registry history not checked in this extract.

The water-soluble botanical mixture comprises Sphaeranthus indicus flower-head extract and Mangifera indica stem-bark extract, 2:1, not mango fruit. At one study center, 120 previously untrained healthy men aged 19–29 were randomized, n=30 per arm, to 425 mg daily plus one resistance-exercise set, 850 mg daily plus one set, placebo plus one set, or placebo plus two sets, for 56 days. Ninety-nine completed: 26, 25, 25 and 23 respectively. Both ingredients and training were combined; neither plant was isolated.

Table 2: bench-press one-repetition maxima at baseline and day 56 (mean ± SD, kg) were 52.1 ± 10.1 and 71.5 ± 17.5 for 425 mg; 51.6 ± 8.9 and 74.2 ± 16.3 for 850 mg; 53.2 ± 15.3 and 62.0 ± 15.3 for the one-set placebo; 53.5 ± 11.0 and 66.1 ± 11.6 for the two-set placebo. The time-by-group interaction was p<.001. Table 3 reports covariate-adjusted day-56 differences versus one-set placebo: 425 mg 10.6 ± 3.0 kg, 95% CI 2.6–18.6, p=.003; 850 mg 13.8 ± 3.0 kg, 95% CI 5.7–21.8, p<.001.

Table 4: bench-press repetitions until failure, mean ± SD at baseline/day 56, were 6.7 ± 1.1/13.4 ± 3.8, 6.6 ± 0.8/13.9 ± 2.2, 6.6 ± 1.0/11.3 ± 2.9 and 6.6 ± 1.0/11.4 ± 2.0 for the four arms above. Day-56 omnibus Kruskal–Wallis p=.003; the 850 mg group differed from both placebo groups, while the table does not mark 425 mg as different from either at this time point. These are measured performance outcomes, not a validated subjective fatigue rating, clinical symptom or evidence about whole mango fruit or Parkinson disease.

The 2021 IUPHAR abstract Sub-Code-1223 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9045588/ has shared investigators, intervention, 120 participants and the same four treatment/exercise arms; it reports results were to be presented. This strongly supports a single study family, though the abstract does not print the trial registry identifier. The paper distinguishes a prior 40-person trial of a similar formulation in previously trained men; do not count it as the same cohort.
