Title: Brain Training and Sulforaphane Intake Interventions Separately Improve Cognitive Performance in Healthy Older Adults, Whereas a Combination of These Interventions Does Not Have More Beneficial Effects: Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 33503851
PMCID: PMC7912304
DOI: 10.3390/nu13020352
First published: 2021
Authors: Nouchi R, Hu Q, Saito T, Kawata NYDS, Nouchi H, Kawashima R
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7912304/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week 2x2 factorial double-blind RCT in n=144 healthy older adults comparing brain training vs active control game and sulforaphane capsules vs placebo. Main effect of sulforaphane significantly improved processing speed (Symbol Search F=9.05, adj p<0.05) and working memory (Backward Span F=8.44, adj p<0.05) with higher urinary SFN-NAC (p=0.008), without significant effects on other cognitive domains or mood states in this trial. Limits: supplement capsules in non-PD healthy older adults.
