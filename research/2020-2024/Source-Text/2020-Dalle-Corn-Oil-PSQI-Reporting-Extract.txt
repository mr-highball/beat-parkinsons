Dalle et al. Omega-3 supplementation and resistance exercise in older adults. J Gerontol A Biol Sci Med Sci. Online first 2020 Dec 7.
PMID 33284965. PMCID PMC7907485. DOI 10.1093/gerona/glaa309. ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03986307.
Original: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7907485/
Supplement: https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC7907485.1/glaa309_suppl_supplementarty_material.pdf
Coverage: Factual trial identity, comparator and missing outcome verification.

The paper's ethics identifier S61809, 23 enrollees and 14-week supplementation plus 12-week resistance exercise directly match the official registry. The comparator used 1,100 mg corn oil in each of three daily capsules without a stated refining method. The original and its seven-page supplement report muscle, strength, inflammatory and functional outcomes. Neither supplies the registered PSQI contrast. Neurodegenerative disease was excluded. No separated corn-oil effect or Parkinson outcome is reported.
