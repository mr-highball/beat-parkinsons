Title: Dietary nitrate supplementation effect on dynamic cerebral autoregulation in normoxia and acute hypoxia.
PMID: 32151227
PMCID: PMC8985441
DOI: 10.1177/0271678X20910053
First published: 2022
Authors: Horiuchi M, Rossetti GM, Oliver SJ
Journal: Journal of cerebral blood flow and metabolism : official journal of the International Society of Cerebral Blood Flow and Metabolism
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32151227/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover study in n=12 healthy adults: acute beetroot juice did not impair dynamic cerebral autoregulation during blood pressure challenges. Limits: small healthy non-PD sample.
