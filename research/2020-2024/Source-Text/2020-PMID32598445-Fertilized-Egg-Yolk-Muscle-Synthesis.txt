Title: Effects of Fortetropin on the Rate of Muscle Protein Synthesis in Older Men and Women: A Randomized, Double-Blinded, Placebo-Controlled Study
Publication date: 2020 (online publication; 2021 issue)
PMID: 32598445
PMCID: PMC7756695
DOI: 10.1093/gerona/glaa162
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7756695/
Text coverage: Primary paper and corresponding C4 proceedings results.

Twenty older adults were assigned fertilized egg-yolk proteolipid complex or energy- and macronutrient-matched cheese powder for 21 days. Published average synthesis rates across eligible muscle proteins were 18% higher in the supplement group. The final counts were 32/38 myofibrillar, 33/44 sarcoplasmic and 12/17 mitochondrial proteins favoring the supplement, which differ from C4 conference counts. This is a tissue synthesis outcome for a processed extract. It does not establish fall frequency or walking slowness for poached eggs or people with Parkinson disease.
