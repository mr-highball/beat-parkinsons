Title: Safety and efficacy of dietary freshwater clam (Corbicula fluminea) extract in clinical research
DOI: 10.31989/ffhd.v12i6.928
First published: 2022
Authors: Shimazoe T, Kobayashi D, Kawashiri T, Chijimatsu T, Umeki M, Mochizuki S
Journal: Functional Foods in Health and Disease 12(6):325-339
Source: https://ffhdj.com/index.php/ffhd/article/view/928
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Peer-reviewed open-access double-blind parallel randomized controlled clinical trial (published 2022-06-23; DOI 10.31989/ffhd.v12i6.928; Functional Foods in Health and Disease 12(6):325-339).
Design & Participants: Prospective double-blind parallel RCT in healthy volunteers with relatively high liver test values; 34 volunteers analyzed across 3 arms: placebo (2 softgels/day), normal-dose freshwater clam (Corbicula fluminea) extract (FCE, 2 softgels/day x 12 weeks for sleep evaluation), and high-dose FCE (10 softgels/day x 18 weeks for liver safety evaluation).
Sleep & Fatigue Results: 12 weeks of normal-dose FCE (2 softgels/day) significantly improved sleep quality on the Oguri-Shirakawa-Azumi sleep inventory middle-aged and aged version (OSA-MA) for both sleep onset and sleep maintenance compared with placebo, and resulted in longer sleep duration than placebo. The same normal dose tended to reduce subjective fatigue (non-significant trend).
Liver Safety Results: High-dose FCE (10 softgels/day) significantly reduced AST, ALT, and gamma-GTP at 12 and 18 weeks and ferritin at 18 weeks with favorable safety.
Round 3 transferability notes: Concentrated freshwater Corbicula fluminea extract softgels (not culinary cooked marine or freshwater whole clams) in non-PD healthy volunteers.
