Solvent-extracted sesame oil and rat catalepsy
https://ijpsdronline.com/index.php/journal/article/download/3656/928
selected original methods and findings paraphrase

Published September 30, 2022. The sesame seed oil was extracted with petroleum ether in a Soxhlet apparatus. In chlorpromazine-treated rats, oral oil groups had lower block-catalepsy scores and longer rotarod retention than the drug-only control. Figure comparisons used six animals per group. Rotarod retention is a performance proxy and the laboratory solvent extraction differs from cold pressing. This is an animal drug-induced model.
