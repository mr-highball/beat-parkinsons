Title: Safety Assessment of Supplementation with Cymbopogon citratus Stapf. (Lemongrass) Extract in Patients with Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 3
Publication: Wongmanit and colleagues, 2023, Pharmacognosy Journal 15(6):976–986
Source: https://phcogj.com/sites/default/files/PharmacognJ-15-4-976.pdf
Coverage: factual extract of the accessible primary source

Sixty-four patients with stage 3 chronic kidney disease were randomized in Thailand to capsules containing 900 mg/day of spray-dried aqueous Cymbopogon citratus leaf extract or placebo over 90 days. The investigators used clinical safety and renal biomarker endpoints. A Thai ministry research summary describes a matching 64-person, 900 mg/day project and reports increased 24-hour urine volume after ten days in the extract group, without a numerical between-arm difference. These measures and home blood pressure documentation are distinct from a urinary urgency measure. The registry identifier TCTR20230720001 is printed in the original journal Methods. Indexed registry access did not yield a readable full record. This is a concentrated oral capsule preparation in kidney disease, without brewed tea as the intervention or a Parkinson cohort.
