Primary Methods and Tables 1 and 3 summary. The oral mixture included cardamom fruit or seed and several other agents. The study is single-arm, and aggregate IBS-SSS went from 229.50 to 123.76 at day 45. The score combines bowel dissatisfaction with pain, abdominal distention and other items. The report discusses stool symptoms without a controlled constipation subtype or validated ingredient-level severity estimate.
