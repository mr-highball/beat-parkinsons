Soybean oil comparator and subjective sleep quality
Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jcbn/76/1/76_24-105/_pdf
Coverage: Factual summary of the primary report.

First online August 24, 2024, print issue 2025. The healthy-volunteer trial assigned 23 participants per group to soy-derived lysolecithin or capsules providing 4.2 g/day soy oil, with unspecified refining. One placebo participant withdrew and Table 4 gives n=22 and 23. Sleep-quality VAS mean ± SE was 80.8 ± 3.2 at baseline and 80.7 ± 3.6 at week eight with oil, versus 76.5 ± 4.1 and 77.5 ± 3.8 with lysolecithin. One drowsiness report occurred in the lysolecithin arm. Neither the VAS nor that event is a comparative daytime sleepiness endpoint in Parkinson disease. No oil-free control was included.
