Title: Telerehabilitation during social distancing for people with Parkinson's disease: a retrospective study.
PMID: 36527572
PMCID: PMC9759049
DOI: 10.1007/s13760-022-02160-3
Publication year: 2022
First public date basis: NLM and original journal record.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9759049/
Coverage: Complete article-body context reviewed 2026-11-22.

The retrospective telerehabilitation comparison included 57 online-training and 29 nonexercising participants with Parkinson disease. It examined quality of life, walking, posture and freezing. Date is a registry field. Neither arm tests dried dates or a dated fruit pain endpoint.
