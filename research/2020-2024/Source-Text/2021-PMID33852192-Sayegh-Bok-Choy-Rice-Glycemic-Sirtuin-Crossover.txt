Title: Inter-Individual Variation in Postprandial Glycemic Responses in Women Co-Ingesting Green Leafy Vegetables with a Carbohydrate Meal: Interactions with the Sirtuin System.
PMID: 33852192
DOI: 10.1002/mnfr.202000923
First published: 2021
Authors: Sayegh M, Henderson J, Farquharson AJ, Horgan G, Ranawana V, Drew JE
Journal: Molecular nutrition & food research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33852192/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Crossover metabolic feeding study (Sayegh et al., 2021, Mol Nutr Food Res) in 31 women consuming rice alone, rice with bok choy, or rice with spinach (75 g available carbohydrate) on separate occasions. Co-ingesting bok choy with rice produced NO statistically significant reduction in postprandial glycemic response overall (high inter-individual variation associated with baseline sirtuin gene-expression subgroups). Measured only acute metabolic and whole-blood gene-expression biomarkers with no Parkinson's or symptom endpoints.
