Title: In vitro protein bioaccessibility and human serum amino acid responses to white bread enriched with intact plant cells.
PMID: 36257266
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodchem.2022.134538
First published: 2023
Authors: Perez-Moral N, Saha S, Pinto AM, Bajka BH, Edwards CH
Journal: Food Chem
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36257266/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized crossover human postprandial study (Perez-Moral et al., 2023, Food Funct) comparing bread incorporating intact cellular chickpea flour versus disrupted chickpea flour and white wheat bread. Intact chickpea plant cell walls restricted in vitro protein/starch bioaccessibility and attenuated postprandial serum amino acid responses relative to broken cells. Mechanistic food-matrix study only; no Parkinson disease or levodopa OFF-time outcomes.
