Registered green kiwifruit constipation and wellbeing outcomes
https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=381723

First registered May 24, 2021. The open-label KINGS comparison assigns two green kiwifruit daily for four weeks or maltodextrin in adults with functional constipation or constipation-predominant irritable bowel syndrome. The registration centers on abdominal pain and includes fatigue and psychological measures. It provides no named attention task, Parkinson population or posted article results.
