Title: Modifying gut integrity and microbiome in children with severe acute malnutrition using legume-based feeds (MIMBLE): A pilot trial.
PMID: 34095882
PMCID: PMC8149470
DOI: 10.1016/j.xcrm.2021.100280
First published: 2021
Authors: Calder N, Walsh K, Olupot-Olupot P, Ssenyondo T, Muhindo R, Mpoya A, et al.
Journal: Cell reports. Medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34095882/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized open-label pilot trial (Calder et al., 2021, Cell Rep Med, PMC8149470) in 58 Ugandan children hospitalized with severe acute malnutrition comparing standard WHO feeds vs legume-enriched feeds containing cowpea flour (n=20). Cowpea-enriched feeds preserved microbiome richness and butyrate-producing taxa during antibiotic therapy, with no significant differences in mortality, weight gain, or diarrhea. Limits: acute pediatric malnutrition inpatient setting; no constipation or Parkinson's symptom endpoints.
