Title: Repeated caffeine intake suppresses cerebral grey matter responses to chronic sleep restriction in an A1 adenosine receptor-dependent manner: a double-blind randomized controlled study with PET-MRI

The PET-MRI analysis includes 36 healthy adults, 19 assigned caffeinated coffee and 17 assigned decaffeinated coffee during five days of restricted sleep. The comparison concerns brain imaging changes and their association with baseline adenosine receptor availability. The investigators report group differences in the direction of structural changes during restricted sleep with caffeine. They do not report a Parkinson fatigue estimate in this analysis.

The trial identifier matches Baur's behavioral report. Conference abstract 156 used the same team's coffee schedule, sleep restriction and A1 PET method in nine healthy participants. Treat it as a likely overlapping preliminary report. The 2021 conference abstract itself does not give the trial registration or participant-level mapping.
