Narayan et al. Daytime neurocognitive performance with nightly cannabidiol in primary insomnia. Psychopharmacology.
First published 2024 Aug 17, issue 2025 Feb. DOI 10.1007/s00213-024-06674-x. PMCID PMC11774964.
Original: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00213-024-06674-x
Official supplement: https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs00213-024-06674-x/MediaObjects/213_2024_6674_MOESM1_ESM.docx
Coverage: Factual trial methods, outcomes and supplemental participant flow.

This paper uses the same ACTRN12620000070932 cohort as the main sleep report DOI 10.5664/jcsm.10998. The supplement documents 44 starting the corn-oil run-in, 34 randomized, 32 completing dosing and 30 analyzed, 15 per arm. Eight placebo responders were excluded before randomization. The Methods display a unit inconsistency in the active CBD amount. The stated concentration and dosing volume give 150 mg, also the amount in the abstract and main report. Cognitive task comparisons yielded no clear difference (all p>.05). Selected patient-reported states and dry mouth differed. The investigators report that inclusion of 32 completers did not change the overall conclusions. Both randomized groups received corn oil and its refining status is unreported.
