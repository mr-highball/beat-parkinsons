Title: Effect of Triheptanoin on Caudate Atrophy and Motor Scores in Patients With Early-Stage Huntington Disease: A Phase II Study

DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0000000000210194
First public article date: 2024-12-30
Journal issue: 2025-01-28
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled indexed abstract and public main-paper excerpts, the official study design and the archived Table 3 extraction. The journal supplement remains a separate access lead.

TRIHEP3 randomized 100 adults with early Huntington disease. The blinded first six months compared 1 g/kg/day of triheptanoin and the same dose of oral safflower oil. Both randomized groups then received open-label triheptanoin. A separate cohort from REV-HD supplied an external one-year comparison. The trial reported no resolved six-month difference for caudate atrophy (p=.723).

Table 3 reports an adjusted change in total UHDRS motor score of +0.7 (95% CI −0.4 to 2.0) in the safflower group and +1.3 (0.0 to 2.7) on triheptanoin. The active-minus-safflower difference was +0.6 (−1.0 to 2.5). Higher scores indicate more impairment. The motor scale includes a dystonia item, and the paper gives no item-level estimate. No separate swallowing endpoint is reported, and the safflower pressing method is unreported.

The official 2022 results upload uses the same trial ID. It lists a limited adverse-event window and a comparator description that also references REV-HD. The journal safety reporting over the full study differs from that upload. These are companion reports, with distinct reporting windows and one primary randomized cohort.
