Title: Metabolic network alterations as a supportive biomarker in dementia with Lewy bodies with preserved dopamine transmission
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10881642/

PMCID: PMC10881642

The study focuses on FDG PET metabolic networks in 22 probable or possible DLB participants with little evidence of dopamine transporter loss. Metabolic classification provides no dietary contrast, visual episode count or association with dried figs.
