Title: Vitamin C-Rich Guava Consumed with Mungbean Dal Reduces Anemia and Increases Hemoglobin but not Iron Stores: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Food-to-Food Fortification in Indian Children
PMID: 39481541
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2024.10.042
First published: 2024
Authors: Rani V, Moretti D, Khetarpaul N, Thankachan P, Zimmermann MB, Melse-Boonstra A
Journal: The Journal of Nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39481541/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Seven-month parallel RCT (NCT01191463) in 200 rural Indian schoolchildren aged 6-10 y (46% anemic, 71% iron-deficient) assigned daily school meals of mungbean dal alone (3.0 mg iron) or mungbean dal plus fresh raw guava fruit (~170 mg vitamin C; 3.2 mg iron). Primary outcome (body iron stores) was null (mean treatment effect +0.65 mg/kg, 95% CI -0.34 to 1.63, p=0.197). Secondary hematologic outcomes improved: hemoglobin +3.7 g/L (95% CI 1.6-5.6, p=0.001) and anemia prevalence -51% (95% CI -74 to -10, p=0.022). Direct raw guava fruit exposure, but non-PD pediatric cohort with hematologic biomarkers only and no fatigue, motor, or neurological symptom outcomes.
