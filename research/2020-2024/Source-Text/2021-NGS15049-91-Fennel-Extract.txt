2021 — First conference report on fennel tea and isolated gastric muscle

Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/nmo.14244?campaign=woletoc
Identifier: NGS15049-91

Annahazi and Schemann’s conference abstract NGS15049-91 reports that fennel tea relaxed guinea pig fundus and corpus strips and increased spontaneous antral contraction amplitude. Antral tone and frequency showed no detected changes. The closely matching setup, exposure range and endpoint pattern appear in the group’s 2025 full report. Count this as an earlier public report of the study family. A 2023 follow-up abstract under DOI 10.1111/nmo.14637 specifically investigated nerve and nitric oxide contributions.
