Title: Large-scale network dysfunction in α-Synucleinopathy: A meta-analysis of resting-state functional connectivity
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8904227/
PMID: 35259574
PMCID: PMC8904227
DOI: 10.1016/j.ebiom.2022.103915
Publication date: 2022-03-05
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The meta-analysis synthesizes resting imaging connectivity differences in Parkinson disease and other synucleinopathies. Its inclusion criteria concern patient-control imaging contrasts. It does not measure dried fig intake or a visual event trajectory.
