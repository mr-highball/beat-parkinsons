Title: Psidium Exotic and Native Species from Brazil Abolish Depression-like Behavior and Oxidative Stress induced by Corticosterone in Mice
PMID: 39191412
DOI: 10.1055/a-2404-3294
First published: 2024
Authors: Zeni ALB, Dalmagro AP, Junges LH, Cavichioli N, Sasse OR
Journal: Planta Medica
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39191412/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Twenty-one-day mouse behavioral study administering oral aqueous LEAF extract of Psidium guajava (10 mg/kg/d) vs fluoxetine in corticosterone-induced depression-like behavior (tail suspension and splash tests; companion to Cavichioli 2022 PMID:36227271). Leaf extract reduced immobility time and brain oxidative stress. Strictly guava LEAF extract in non-PD stress mice, NOT raw guava fruit.
