Title: Effects of a traditional herbal formula containing Melissa officinalis, Pimpinella anisum, and Rosa damascena on anxiety and depression in patients with constipation-predominant irritable bowel syndrome (IBS-C): A double-blind randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 38937193
DOI: 10.1016/j.explore.2024.05.011
First published: 2024
Authors: Azimi M, Shahrbabaki HKD, Raeiszadeh M, Eslami O
Journal: Explore (New York, N.Y.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38937193/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind RCT in n=96 enrolled (88 completed: 43 intervention, 45 cornstarch placebo) adults with Rome IV constipation-predominant IBS (IBS-C) receiving 500 mg twice daily for 4 weeks of a 3-herb Traditional Persian formula (MPR: Melissa officinalis, Pimpinella anisum, and Rosa damascena). Reported HADS anxiety Cohen's d = -1.19 (95% CI -4.49 to 2.13) and HADS depression Cohen's d = -1.93 (95% CI -4.32 to 2.79) — both 95% confidence intervals span zero, AND the intervention is a 3-herb mixture that cannot isolate Pimpinella anisum.
