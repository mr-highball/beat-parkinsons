Title: Intravascular large B-cell lymphoma presenting as rapidly progressive dementia and stroke: A case report.

The case describes intravascular large B-cell lymphoma with rapidly progressive dementia and stroke. Visual and auditory hallucinations were not reported in that patient's course. The comparison is diagnostic and contains no relevant food exposure.
