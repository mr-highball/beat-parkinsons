Abdominal massage with extra virgin olive oil and constipation
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8479287/
PMID: 34604396
PMCID: PMC8479287
DOI: 10.30476/ijcbnm.2021.88206.1495
Coverage: Original full article Sections 2 and 3 and Table 2

The single-blind trial randomized 54 older nursing-home residents to abdominal massage with extra-virgin olive oil, massage with water, or no added massage. Table 2 mean Constipation Assessment Scale scores were 5.62 to 2.06 for oil massage, 5.05 to 3.11 for water massage and 4.44 to 5.22 for control, with a post-treatment three-group p ≤ .001. The oil was applied to the abdomen and the treatment included massage. It supplies no estimate for oral extra-virgin olive oil or a Parkinson-specific effect.
