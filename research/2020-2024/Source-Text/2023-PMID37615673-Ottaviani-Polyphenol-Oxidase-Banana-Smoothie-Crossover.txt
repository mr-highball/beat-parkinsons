Title: Impact of polyphenol oxidase on the bioavailability of flavan-3-ols in fruit smoothies: a controlled, single blinded, cross-over study.
PMID: 37615673
DOI: 10.1039/d3fo01599h
First published: 2023
Authors: Ottaviani JI, Ensunsa JL, Fong RY, Kimball J, Medici V, Kuhnle GGC
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37615673/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Single-blind controlled human crossover study evaluating polyphenol oxidase (PPO) activity in freshly prepared fruit smoothies on flavan-3-ol bioavailability. High-PPO banana smoothies substantially reduced flavan-3-ol bioavailability relative to low-PPO berry controls. Demonstrates in vivo human PPO enzymatic activity from banana but tests flavan-3-ols rather than levodopa or PD motor outcomes.
