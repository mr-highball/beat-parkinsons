Title: Blueberry juice augments exercise-induced neuroprotection in a Parkinson's disease model through modulation of GDNF levels.
PMID: 35321527
PMCID: PMC8935512
DOI: 10.1016/j.ibneur.2022.03.001
First published: 2022
Authors: Castro SL, Tapias V, Gathagan R, Emes A, Brandon TE, Smith AD
Journal: IBRO neuroscience reports
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8935512/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical intrastriatal 6-OHDA rat PD model comparing voluntary running, blueberry juice (BBJ) alone, combined running + BBJ, and control for 4 weeks pre- and post-lesion. Blueberry juice consumption ALONE had NO ameliorative effect on rotational behavior or nigrostriatal dopamine neuron loss, though it augmented exercise-induced protection via nigral GDNF. Limits: preclinical rodent juice model showing null monotherapy effect.
