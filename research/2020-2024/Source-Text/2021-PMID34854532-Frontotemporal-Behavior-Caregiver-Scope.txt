Original study scope
The study relates behavioral and clinician or caregiver assessments to burden in 558 participants with frontotemporal lobar degeneration spectrum disorders. Apathy and disinhibition were associated with caregiver burden.

Continuing evidence gaps
This comparison is clinical and observational but involves no exposure to fresh sage or dates. It cannot provide a food effect on Parkinson apathy, rigidity or falls.
