PROMED sleep questions and actual supplied bean categories
December 23, 2022 · PMID 37181130 · PMC10100940 · DOI 10.1016/j.cdnut.2022.100022 · NCT03975556
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10100940/
Factual extract from original design and Table 1 plus four official supplemental guide panels.

The design article describes a questionnaire on sleep quantity and quality for a whole-diet pilot. Table 1 lists canned pink and white beans, dried small red beans and other pulses among foods provided. The intervention visual-guide supplement uses generic habichuelas and shows mixed bean images without a pinto designation. Supplement images cannot establish cultivar or individual ingestion. The protocol provides no sleep results. The official supplementary document is https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC10100940.1/mmc1.docx.
