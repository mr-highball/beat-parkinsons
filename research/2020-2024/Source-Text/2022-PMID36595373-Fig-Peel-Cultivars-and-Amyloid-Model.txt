Title: Fig peel cultivar extracts in an amyloid nematode model
PMID: 36595373
DOI: 10.1089/jmf.2021.K.0190
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36595373/
First publication: 2022-12-29 online, January 2023 issue.
Coverage: Original investigator abstract and matching publisher record. The publisher labels the body restricted.

The investigators compared ethanol extracts of Peggy Red and green fig peels. In an amyloid-expressing Caenorhabditis elegans strain, the Peggy Red extract was reported to reduce amyloid aggregation and paralysis. The paper reports different total measured phenolic contents for the two peels. This is a cultivar and laboratory model comparison using extracted peel, without a dietary dried-fig exposure or an outcome of hallucinations.
