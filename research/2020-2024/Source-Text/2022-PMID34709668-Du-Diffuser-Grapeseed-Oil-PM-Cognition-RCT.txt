Title: The impact of emissions from an essential oil diffuser on cognitive performance.
PMID: 34709668
DOI: 10.1111/ina.12919
First published: 2022
Authors: Du B, Schwartz-Narbonne H, Tandoc M, Heffernan EM, Mack ML, Siegel JA
Journal: Indoor air
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34709668/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Double-blind between-subject simulated-office study in 59 university students exposed via ultrasonic indoor diffuser to lemon essential oil vs deionized water or airborne particulate matter generated from unscented grapeseed oil with/without HEPA filtration. Airborne oil particulate emissions shortened reaction time while worsening response inhibition and memory sensitivity. Strictly INHALED indoor aerosol/PM exposure (exposure_fit unusable for oral culinary refined/cold-pressed grapeseed oil).
