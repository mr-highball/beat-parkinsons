Title: Visual hallucinations in Parkinson's disease: spotlight on central cholinergic dysfunction
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11788216/
PMID: 39252645
PMCID: PMC11788216
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awae289
Publication date: 2024-09-10 (first public electronic publication)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

This review synthesizes visual hallucination studies involving cholinergic dysfunction, Lewy pathology, visual attention and distributed brain circuits. It describes reports in medication-naive patients and an earlier levodopa challenge with no elicited episodes. The underlying observations are medication and mechanism leads, with no dried fig comparison.
