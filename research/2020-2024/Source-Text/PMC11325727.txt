Title: Efficacy of therapeutic intervention with NanoBEO to manage agitation and pain in patients suffering from severe dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11325727/
PMID: 39148533
PMCID: PMC11325727
DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2024.1417851
First published: 2024-08-01
Trial: NCT04321889
Coverage: Distilled original pilot protocol, enrollment and results.

Thirty residents with severe dementia were randomized, 14 to NanoBEO topical cream and 16 to placebo cream. Cohen-Mansfield Agitation Inventory was the main endpoint; Italian MOBID-2 pain was secondary. The active arm started at higher pain scores. Figure 6 and the authors' analysis report a favorable pain comparison after one week, with an initial imbalance complicating interpretation. The study uses a topical bergamot preparation. Its outcomes provide no date-fruit result.
