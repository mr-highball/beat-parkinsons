Title: Prevotella enterotype associates with diets supporting acidic faecal pH and production of propionic acid by microbiota
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11109898/
PMCID: PMC11109898
PMID: 38779015
DOI: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e31134
First public article date: 2024-05-11
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original Methods and seasonal food reporting

The authors reanalyzed dietary and microbiota data from 57 healthy
adults who completed a three-day food diary before sampling.
Strawberry consumption was highest in the June–July observations
when compared with April–May. The analysis relates habitual diet
patterns to gut measurements and does not report a strawberry-only
effect estimate, a fall outcome or a Parkinson-specific population.
