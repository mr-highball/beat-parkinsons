Title: Salivary α-Synuclein as a Candidate Biomarker of Parkinsonism in 22q11.2 Deletion Syndrome
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11233847/
PMID: 38661486
PMCID: PMC11233847
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.14046
Publication date: 2024-04-25
Date basis: electronic publication metadata in original PMC article
Source depth: factual extract of full original abstract, methods and results

Fanella and colleagues compared ten 22q11.2 deletion syndrome participants with parkinsonism, ten with the syndrome without parkinsonism, and ten healthy comparison participants. The outcome was total α-synuclein concentration in collected saliva. The concentration was lowest in the group with parkinsonism (p=0.007 overall), with a reported classification AUC of 0.86 against the syndrome comparison group.

Participants provided saliva by passive drooling. This describes sample collection. The study provides no graded drooling endpoint or food exposure comparison. The population has a specific genetic syndrome and the exploratory cross-sectional study cannot establish how a dietary change would affect saliva control.
