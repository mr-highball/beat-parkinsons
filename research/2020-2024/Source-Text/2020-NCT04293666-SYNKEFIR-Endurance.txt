Registered exercise endurance comparison for formulated kefir
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04293666
Registration: NCT04293666
Coverage: Official registry fields and linked primary paper Methods and Results

First posted March 3, 2020. Trial records 16 healthy men assigned to two 28-day product periods plus washout and specifies treadmill endurance and blood response. Matching enrollment, crossover, five DSM strains and sponsor product strongly suggest the 2021 Lee report is the result publication, though the paper does not identify the NCT number. The registry says freeze-dried. Lee Methods describe heating, maltodextrin and spray drying. The trial gives 11.76 ± 1.92 versus 9.08 ± 1.92 minutes to exhaustion. The Results give p=.0048 for this comparison, while the abstract gives p=.0004. Neither report defines a separate patient-reported fatigue outcome or plain milk kefir intervention. [Original article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7996501/).
