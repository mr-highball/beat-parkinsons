Coverage: Full original abstract collection. Relevant member P-14, skin experiment.

Azevedo Silva and colleagues used a sunflower oil and squalane formulation in a laboratory skin experiment. The poster varies cannabinoid concentrations and reports inflammation and keratinocyte outcomes. The oil is a formulation component. Refining and pressing methods are unreported. There is no isolated food-oil effect or separately measured Parkinson symptom. The scope is related ingredient context for oral culinary sunflower oil claims.

No studied dried cherry or dried date fruit exposure was identified in the collection.
