The review separates gingerol, shogaol, galanolactone and ginger-extract studies. It describes mechanistic receptor findings and heterogeneous clinical nausea or vomiting reports. Adjacent constipation text describes an antiemetic drug effect, not an outcome measured after culinary ginger. Grape-seed extract appears in a separate experimental context. The cited nausea and laboratory outcomes are distinct from constipation severity in Parkinson disease.
