The cross-sectional Geisinger analysis grouped 122 oldest-old community participants by reported Pittsburgh Fatigability Scale score. Table 2 compares 98 participants with a score of at least 15 and 24 with a score below 15. The adjusted Greens and Beans means were 1.8 ± 0.2 and 3.0 ± 0.4, respectively, with p = 0.019. Total Vegetables means were 3.5 ± 0.1 and 4.1 ± 0.3, with p = 0.066. The models adjusted for age group, sex, BMI and the number of medical conditions.

HEI-2015 scores dark-green vegetables and mature legumes in Greens and Beans. The USDA guide classifies string bean pods as Other Vegetables and distinguishes raw, cooked, frozen and canned forms. The separate Total Vegetables measure aggregates many foods. The study has no item-level association for cooked green beans and no Parkinson-specific fatigue comparison. Claim 0561 retains an open verdict. The Nurses' Health Study item-level supplementary tables remain a separate source-access question.
