Title: Association of Egg Intake With Alzheimer's Dementia Risk in Older Adults: The Rush Memory and Aging Project.
PMID: 38782209
PMCID: PMC11347793
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2024.05.012
First published: 2024
Authors: Pan Y, Wallace TC, Karosas T, Bennett DA, Agarwal P, Chung M
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782209/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Prospective cohort analysis of the Rush Memory and Aging Project (PMC11347793; n=1,024 older adults, mean age 81.4 +/- 7.2 y, 6.7-year follow-up, 280 incident AD cases; 578 brain autopsies). Eating >1 egg/wk (HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.34-0.83) and >=2 eggs/wk (HR 0.53, 95% CI 0.35-0.81) was associated with lower incident Alzheimer's dementia risk and lower autopsy AD pathology, with 39% mediation via dietary choline. Assesses incident Alzheimer's dementia in non-PD elders rather than PD executive/attention symptom severity or specific egg preparation.
