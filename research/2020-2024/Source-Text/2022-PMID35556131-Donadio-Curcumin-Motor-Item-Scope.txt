Original study scope
The one-year Parkinson cohort compared 19 people taking phospholipid-formulated curcuminoids with 14 untreated people. Table 2 reports mean worsening of the OFF motor score of 5 versus 11 points. The displayed p values compare follow-up with baseline within each group. The groups selected different treatment strategies and differed in motor status and levodopa at baseline. A regression model included clinical covariates. The published motor result is aggregate MDS-UPDRS Part III.

Continuing evidence gaps
The published tables do not provide separately reported passive rigidity, bradykinesia or rest tremor estimates. Neither arm assigns grated culinary turmeric. The observed change in the composite can guide source discovery for claim 0359 and does not give a symptom-specific direction.
