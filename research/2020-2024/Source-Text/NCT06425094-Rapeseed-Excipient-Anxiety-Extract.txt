# Hydrogenated rapeseed excipient and planned anxiety scores

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06425094
Source coverage official registration arm descriptions and secondary outcomes

NCT06425094 first posted May 22, 2024. The detailed intervention record lists hydrogenated rapeseed oil in the active microalgae formulation and leaves it out of the maltodextrin placebo description. The secondary GAD-7 measurements were scheduled at baseline, two weeks and four weeks. Formulation ingredients vary together. Hydrogenation and use as an excipient limit application to culinary cold-pressed oil.
