Title: I123-FP-CIT (DaTSCAN) SPECT beyond the Most Common Causes of Parkinsonism: A Systematic Review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11145110/
PMID: 38693679
PMCID: PMC11145110
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.14055
Publication date: 2024-05-01
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML publication; local research digest

The systematic review summarizes dopamine transporter imaging in atypical and less common movement disorders, including disorders outside idiopathic Parkinson disease. It evaluates clinical utility of imaging rather than food-linked symptom frequency.
