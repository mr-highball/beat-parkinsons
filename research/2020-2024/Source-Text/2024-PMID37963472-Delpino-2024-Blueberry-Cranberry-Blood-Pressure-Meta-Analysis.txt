Title: The effects of blueberry and cranberry supplementation on blood pressure in patients with cardiovascular diseases: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized clinical trials.
PMID: 37963472
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.8069
First published: 2024
Authors: Delpino FM, Dos Santos FS, Flores TR, Cerqueira HS, Santos HO
Journal: Phytotherapy research : PTR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37963472/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 17 RCTs of blueberry and/or cranberry supplementation in cardiometabolic populations: pooled blood pressure effects were statistically neutral (SBP -0.81 mmHg, 95% CI -2.26 to 0.63; DBP -0.15 mmHg, -1.36 to 1.05). Context-only synthesis; no PD or orthostatic hypotension symptom outcomes.
