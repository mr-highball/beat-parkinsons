Sesame oil used to measure odor identification

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8072018/
Coverage: Original community survey and odor substitution Methods
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

Dong and colleagues substituted sesame oil for coffee in a 16-item smell test of rural Chinese older adults. They attribute sesame and soy sauce replacements to Shu and Yuan. The 2007 first-online Shu and Yuan abstract confirms five modified odors but names no sesame item. The experiment assessed recognition of odor delivered to the nose and did not measure a change from eating the oil.
