Title: The Inhibition of Mitogen-Activated Protein Kinases (MAPKs) and NF-κB Underlies the Neuroprotective Capacity of a Cinnamon/Curcumin/Turmeric Spice Blend in Aβ-Exposed THP-1 Cells
PMID: 38138438

Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38138438/

Archive scope: Factual extract from the official PMC text, including Methods, Results, chemical characterization and funding.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

Maugeri and colleagues used a mixture of Cinnamomum cassia bark extract and two turmeric-root extracts. Chemical testing identified curcuminoids and cinnamon constituents, including cinnamaldehyde. In amyloid-beta-exposed THP-1 cells, blend exposure reduced oxidant signal, IL-1β and IL-6 readouts and phosphorylation of MAPK and NF-κB pathway components relative to the amyloid challenge. The 2024 FEBS P-14-005 abstract describes the same blend, cell system and measured series. Treat the presentations as one laboratory study family.

The blend work does not separate cinnamon from turmeric. Cell concentrations and pathway endpoints provide no oral dose, patient symptom measurement, or transfer to ground cinnamon or culinary turmeric in Parkinson disease. The article and abstract declare industry funding. Source anchors include Results 2.1–2.6, Materials and Methods, and Funding.
