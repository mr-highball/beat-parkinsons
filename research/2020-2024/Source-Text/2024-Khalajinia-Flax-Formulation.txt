Article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11694585/
Registry: https://en.irct.ir/trial/40499 (IRCT20190625044004N1, registration date 2021-06-28).
Methods and registry comparison. The report states flaxseed oil in some sections, but describes powdered seeds processed by serial ethanol extraction and drying to produce capsules. Its capsule schedule and flaxseed quantity are not reconciled. The registry uses oil in the title and 6 g/day encapsulated flaxseed in the intervention field, without pressing. Reported pain improvement does not identify an oil effect.
