Official ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06505694, first posted 2024-07-17, current version checked 2026-11-15: randomized multi-ingredient tincture including organic black pepper fruit versus placebo; enrolled 80, completed, no posted results. Primary measurements include sleep-quality and well-being questionnaires; secondary measures are Fitbit sleep duration and stages. No Parkinson subgroup or dream-enactment frequency is specified in the protocol. Other active botanicals and L-theanine preclude isolation of pepper.
