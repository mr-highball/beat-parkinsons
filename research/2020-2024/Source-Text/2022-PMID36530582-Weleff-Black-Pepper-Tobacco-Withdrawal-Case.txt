Title: Black Pepper (Piper nigrum) for Tobacco Withdrawal: A Case Report.
PMID: 36530582
PMCID: PMC9757938
DOI: 10.1155/2022/5908769
First published: 2022
Authors: Weleff J, Dore S, Anand A, Barnett BS
Journal: Case reports in psychiatry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36530582/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Single clinical case report (PMC9757938) describing an incarcerated patient who inhaled combusted black pepper to self-medicate tobacco withdrawal, reporting reduced craving and withdrawal-associated anxiety. Uncontrolled n=1 self-report using combusted inhalation; not dietary ground pepper or PD.
