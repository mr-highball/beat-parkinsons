Dry sage extract and sleep symptoms after menopause
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7114003/
PMID: 32318472
PMCID: PMC7114003
DOI: 10.4103/jfmpc.jfmpc_913_19
Registration: IRCT2015111713940N2
Coverage: Original abstract, Methods and Table 3

The original article reports 66 menopause participants assigned to common sage extract tablets or placebo and states that 59 completed. The group counts in Table 3 are 31 and 27, which sum to 58. The abstract reports PSQI mean scores of approximately 9.4 before and 5.6 after treatment with extract. Table 3 appears to reverse the mean and dispersion in its displayed entries. It reports a lower post-treatment score with extract than with placebo. This trial measured global sleep quality after an extract and had no Parkinson population or culinary whole-leaf arm.
