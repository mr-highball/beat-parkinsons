Title: Ocular fixations and presaccadic potentials to explain pareidolias in Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 32954309
PMCID: PMC7425388
DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcaa073
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7425388/
First publication date: 2020-06-04 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

The study compares eye fixations and presaccadic potentials during an ambiguous visual task. Pareidolias are responses to stimuli, measured under test conditions. That comparison gives perceptual context but does not measure changes in spontaneous visual hallucination frequency after eating dried figs.
