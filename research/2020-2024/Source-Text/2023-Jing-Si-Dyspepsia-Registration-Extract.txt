Jing Si herbal formula and sleep in functional dyspepsia

Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05948215
Coverage: Official intervention, dates, and outcome modules
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First posted July 17, 2023. IRB110-232-A lists 26 adults, an eight-herb formula with perilla leaves and planned day-28 PSQI. Its public registration followed registered 2021 study dates. Duration was four weeks. The matching 2024 Liu publication gives outcome data. The registry does not establish an amount of perilla leaf or dried source material.
