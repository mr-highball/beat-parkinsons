Title: Expectation Cues and False Percepts Generate Stimulus-Specific Activity in Distinct Layers of the Early Visual Cortex

The experiment manipulates expectation cues and measures stimulus-specific activity across cortical layers. The induced false percept task is not a Parkinson food-exposure study and does not follow spontaneous visual hallucinations after meals.
