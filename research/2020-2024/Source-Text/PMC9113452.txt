Title: Neuropsychological Performance in Autoimmune Limbic Encephalitis: Evidence from an Immunotherapy-Naïve Cohort
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9113452/
PMID: 35136904
PMCID: PMC9113452
DOI: 10.1093/arclin/acac001
Publication date: 2022-02-05
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The retrospective series assesses 69 immunotherapy-naive autoimmune limbic encephalitis patients and compares neuropsychological and clinical features. The patient population and outcome assessments do not track dried fig intake or Parkinson visual episode frequency.
