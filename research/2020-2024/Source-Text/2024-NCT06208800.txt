Title: Multicomponent inhalation and sleep in older adults
Registration: NCT06208800
First posted: 2024-01-17
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06208800
Text type: official-registry evidence summary

The record compares a morning and evening essential-oil inhalation program with no intervention. It lists clary sage and musk sage among the evening oils and registers questionnaire and wearable sleep measures. The linked report PMID 42011111 describes clary sage (musk sage) as one ingredient. The registry had no posted results at the November 17, 2026 check. The planned effect is for the full inhalation program.
