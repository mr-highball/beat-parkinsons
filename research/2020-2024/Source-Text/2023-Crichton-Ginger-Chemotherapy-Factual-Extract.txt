A double-blind controlled trial compared encapsulated standardized ginger root powder (51) with placebo (52) in adults receiving chemotherapy. The Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale anxiety score was collected 12–24 hours after chemotherapy, without a per-cycle pre-chemo anxiety value reported in Table 6. At cycles 1, 2 and 3, the post-treatment means were 4.30 versus 5.14, 4.51 versus 4.79, and 4.78 versus 5.41. The reported between-group means were −0.84 (95% CI −2.05 to 0.37), −0.28 (−1.59 to 1.03), and −0.63 (−2.15 to 0.89). The repeated-measures time-by-group comparison gave p = .352. The results are inconclusive for a difference in anxiety in this setting.
