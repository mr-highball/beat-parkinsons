The trial randomized 56 adolescents with depression and a parent with bipolar I disorder. Forty-two completed follow-up. The primary depression-scale contrast between fish oil and olive-oil capsules was inconclusive in the journal report (p = 0.15). Secondary global clinical ratings favored fish oil. Safety and tolerability ratings did not show a group difference. The poster reported that both arms improved and the simple between-arm depression contrast was inconclusive (p = 0.42); a covariate-adjusted model was more favorable to fish oil. The journal primary outcome governs the primary comparison.

The matching investigator group, enrollment, dose, duration, population and olive-oil comparison indicate an overlapping study family. Olive oil had no comparison against an inert oil-free control. The work does not identify a culinary grade, a Parkinson population, or ingredient-attributable results for the frozen symptoms.
