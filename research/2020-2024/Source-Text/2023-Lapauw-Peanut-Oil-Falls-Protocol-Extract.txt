Peanut oil placebo and planned falls in TEMPUS FUGIT

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10523729/
Coverage: Original intervention and procedural outcome table
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

Published September 26, 2023. The TEMPUS-FUGIT protocol incorporates randomized ENHANce sarcopenia participants into its longitudinal gut–muscle follow-up. It describes a 1 g peanut-oil omega-3 placebo and recording of falls and healthcare use. The 2026 family feasibility report and its publisher Supplementary Materials 3, 4, 6 and 10–12 address physical performance, safety and sarcopenia outcomes without a verified falls contrast. This is one trial family. Refinement was unreported and the feasibility report lists Parkinson disease among screening exclusions.
