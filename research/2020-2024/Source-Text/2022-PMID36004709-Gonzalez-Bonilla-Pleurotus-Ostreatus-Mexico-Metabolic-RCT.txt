Title: Dietary Supplementation with Oyster Culinary-Medicinal Mushroom, Pleurotus ostreatus (Agaricomycetes), Reduces Visceral Fat and Hyperlipidemia in Inhabitants of a Rural Community in Mexico.
PMID: 36004709
DOI: 10.1615/IntJMedMushrooms.2022044837
First published: 2022
Authors: González-Bonilla A, Meneses ME, Pérez-Herrera A, Armengol-Álvarez D, Martínez-Carrera D
Journal: International Journal of Medicinal Mushrooms
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36004709/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
3-month RCT in 30 rural Mexican adults (Puebla) comparing regional healthy diet + 1 kg/wk Pleurotus ostreatus (4 x 250 g/wk, n=15) vs regional diet alone (n=15). Assessed visceral fat, lipids, fasting glucose, and blood pressure—improving metabolic indices without assessing any neurological, mood, GI motility, or PD symptoms. Also notes pre-Hispanic culinary consumption of edible mushrooms in Mexico.
