Spearmint-oil blend and salivary flow in a dry-mouth trial registration
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06313905
Registration: NCT06313905
Coverage: Official registry protocol, outcome definitions and results status

University Tunis El Manar planned a randomized double-blind trial of 180 adults with Sjögren syndrome and xerostomia, 90 per arm. The experimental oral spray EVADRY contains spearmint herb oil (Mentha spicata, 10 mg per 100 mL bottle) among several ingredients. The comparator is mint-flavored water. Both arms were to use five sprays daily. The primary measure is unstimulated saliva collected for 15 minutes, with change at three months planned. The registration lists estimated enrollment and dates. No study results were posted when checked on November 16, 2026. The study design cannot isolate the effect of spearmint oil. Parkinson drooling and consumption of dried spearmint leaves were outside the protocol.
