Cuerda-Ballester et al. Improvements in gait and balance in patients with multiple sclerosis after treatment with coconut oil and epigallocatechin gallate. A pilot study. Food Funct. 2023.
First available: 2023-01-03
PMID: 36594273
DOI: 10.1039/D2FO02207A
Trial: NCT03740295
Primary source: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2023/fo/d2fo02207a
Coverage: factual original Methods and Figure 2.
The study randomized 51 participants with multiple sclerosis. The active Mediterranean diet provided extra-virgin coconut oil, 60 mL/day, and 800 mg/day EGCG as stated by the article. The comparison used other fats and placebo capsules. The reported mean Berg values were 49 ± 9.6 to 52 ± 6.9 for the combination (within-arm p<.001) and 43 ± 16 to 43 ± 17 for comparison (within-arm p=.387). Balance confidence was inconclusive. No between-arm change estimate was verified. The registration gives 600 mg/day EGCG. The trial cannot attribute the balance result to an isolated oil or to the refined grade.
