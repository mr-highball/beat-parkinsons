Registered Parkinson dose optimization and motor complications
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05589766
Registration: NCT05589766
Coverage: Annual and original registry date metadata; first posted October 21, 2022

N-DOSE assigns nicotinamide riboside dose regimens in Parkinson disease. The completed registry lists 81 actual enrollees and no posted results. It registers MDS-UPDRS part IV in its assessment battery. The registry's outcome description is for the total part IV scale. It does not specify a potato intervention or an analysis involving a dietary potato exposure.
