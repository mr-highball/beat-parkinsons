Primary Table 2 walking data summary. In adults with chronic knee pain, 80-meter walking time after 90 days changed by −11.0 seconds (SD 26.1) under water-dispersible turmeric extract and −6.8 seconds (SD 23.3) under placebo. The reported Mann–Whitney comparison is p=.0011. Median changes were −0.2 and 0 seconds; dispersion is wide. The corrigendum concerns a pain value and leaves walking unchanged. No Parkinson cohort or grated rhizome.
