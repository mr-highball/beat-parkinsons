Factual extract from primary abstract and official registered comparator, checked 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

The single-blind study stratified 27 participants by initial pain and allocated 13 to active oil and 14 to vehicle. The protocol delivered the topical intervention every eight hours for six weeks. Pain was measured weekly by SF-MPQ-2 and daily by VAS. The primary abstract reports no significant pain or quality-of-life difference over seven weeks and a positive pain signal with concomitant pain medication (p=.001). Accessible text supplies no magnitude or assessment of that subgroup's robustness. The protocol identifies a topical six-botanical preparation with black pepper oil in jojoba and plain jojoba as the comparator. The complete article tables were inaccessible.
