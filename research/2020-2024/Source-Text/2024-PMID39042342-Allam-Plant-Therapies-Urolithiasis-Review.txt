Title: Plant-based therapies for urolithiasis: a systematic review of clinical and preclinical studies.
PMID: 39042342
DOI: 10.1007/s11255-024-04148-9
First published: 2024
Authors: Allam EAH, Sabra MS
Journal: Int Urol Nephrol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39042342/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

PRISMA systematic review (64 studies, 2021-2023) of plant extracts for kidney stones/urolithiasis, citing Cucumis melo extracts (predominantly seeds/aerial preparations in rat ethylene-glycol models) as reducing experimental stone formation; no human overactive bladder or PD urinary urgency trials.
