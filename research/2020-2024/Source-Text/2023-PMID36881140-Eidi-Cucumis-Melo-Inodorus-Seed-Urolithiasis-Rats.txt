Title: Anti-urolithiatic effect of Cucumis melo L. var inodorous in male rats with kidney stones
PMID: 36881140
DOI: 10.1007/s00240-023-01418-6
First published: 2023
Authors: Eidi M, Ashjazadeh L
Journal: Urolithiasis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36881140/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Thirty-eight-day rat ethylene-glycol kidney stone model comparing oral hydroethanolic extract of Cucumis melo var. inodorus SEEDS against potassium citrate. Reduced urinary calcium/oxalate and renal crystal deposits while raising urinary pH, magnesium, and citrate. Strictly non-flesh melon SEED extract in a rodent nephrolithiasis model; does not test raw honeydew flesh or PD urinary urgency.
