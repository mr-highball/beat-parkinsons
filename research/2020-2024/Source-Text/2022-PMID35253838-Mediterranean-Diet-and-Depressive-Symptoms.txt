Original source scope
Boston Puerto Rican cohort assesses dietary-pattern adherence and CES-D scores. The adapted individual food questionnaire includes mango. Higher adherence was associated with lower scores, while baseline adherence did not predict the five-year CES-D trajectory.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Mango is a genuine measured dietary item but no estimate is isolated for mango, its drying state or Parkinson depression. The overall dietary-pattern association cannot be decomposed into a mango effect.
