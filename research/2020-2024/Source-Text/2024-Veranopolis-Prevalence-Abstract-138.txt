Veranopolis conference prevalence report and missing odor results
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10854369/
PMCID: PMC10854369
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13966
Coverage: numbered conference abstract 138 in the original 2024 supplement

The February 9, 2024 supplement contains abstract 138 describing 3,470 people screened in Veranópolis and an estimated Parkinson prevalence of 1.64% (95% CI 1.25–2.12). The reported age cutoff differs from that in the 2021 protocol. The abstract gives no cinnamon or orange identification result and no food-exposure comparison. Supplement DOI and PMCID identify the collection. Abstract number 138 identifies this presentation.
