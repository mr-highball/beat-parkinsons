Title: Selective 5HT3 antagonists and sensory processing: a systematic review

Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The human-study review compares sensory processing and pharmacologic evidence and cites earlier uncontrolled ondansetron reports in Parkinson hallucinosis. It identifies the ongoing TOP HAT trial as separate. Neither medicine nor the reviews establish a dried fig exposure or episode result after food.

The reported seven- and 16-person historical treatment series are uncontrolled and may share participants. The separately registered TOP HAT comparator NCT04167813 does not have posted results in the registry, and its original outcome combines clinical aspects of hallucinations.
