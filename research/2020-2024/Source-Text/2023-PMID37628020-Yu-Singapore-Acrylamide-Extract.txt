The total-diet analysis measures acrylamide by LC-MS/MS across 385 main-meal foods and 386 snack foods with 14 cooking methods. Estimated exposure is .165 micrograms/kg body weight/day for general consumers and .392 at the 95th percentile. Foods outside main meals contribute 57.4% and 54.6% respectively.

The reported detection limit is 1 microgram/kg. Lower/middle-bound treatments of undetected measurements use zero or half the limit; below detection does not establish chemical absence. Food concentrations differ widely by preparation and food type.

Table S16 lists black pepper, no cooking, 403.8 micrograms/kg. Its 226 respondents are dietary-recall eaters, not analytical replicates. Pepper-only estimated exposure among eaters is .0040 mean and .0126 at the 95th percentile, micrograms/kg body weight/day. Whole/ground form, prior processing and pepper-specific analytical replicate count are unreported. No cooking does not establish raw material. The 2024 overview's 404 ppb rounds the same result, not independent replication. No Parkinson symptom was measured.
