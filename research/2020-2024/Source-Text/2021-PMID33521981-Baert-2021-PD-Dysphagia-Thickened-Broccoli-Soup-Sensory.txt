Title: Dysphagia management in Parkinson's disease: Comparison of the effect of thickening agents on taste, aroma, and texture.
PMID: 33521981
DOI: 10.1111/1750-3841.15595
First published: 2021
Authors: Baert, Vlaemynck, Beeckman
Journal: Journal of food science
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33521981/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Paired-comparison sensory and rheology study in PD patients (n=36-47) and controls (n=31-41) evaluating gum-based vs starch-based (potato starch, quinoa flour) thickeners added to broccoli soup. Starch-based thickeners preserved more aroma/taste intensity (68-70% preference) than gum thickener, though all thickeners reduced volatile release. Broccoli soup was only the vehicle; no contrast testing raw broccoli on dysphagia severity.
