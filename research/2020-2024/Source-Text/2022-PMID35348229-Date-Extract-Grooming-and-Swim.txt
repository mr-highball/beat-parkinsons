Title: Antidepressant-like effects of aqueous extracts of miswak and date palm on depression-like behaviors in a chronic stress rat model
PMID: 35348229
DOI: 10.1111/jfbc.14164
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/jfbc.14164
Publication: 2022-03-29
Coverage: Original article Methods, outcome-specific Results and Figures 2–4.

The study administered a water extract of dried date fruit and tested a separate miswak preparation and a combination in male rats exposed to chronic unpredictable mild stress. The extracts differ from whole dried dates. The figures report 12 rats per group. The date-only comparison is distinct from the miswak-only and combined arms.

In the date-only group, grooming lasted 22.9 ± 2.9 s versus 13.6 ± 1.3 s in stressed controls (means ± SEM, p < .05 per Figure 4d). The date-only swim immobility values were 164.8 ± 19.5 s versus 216.5 ± 12.6 s and the authors report an inconclusive contrast. The separate combination and miswak-only groups showed shorter immobility with significance marks. The date-only comparisons for open-field crossings and time in the closed arm were also inconclusive.

Grooming is one stress-model behavior. The study did not use an effort choice or apathy measure, a Parkinson disease model, or oral whole dried fruit. Neither the grooming result nor the abstract's broad description establishes a Parkinson apathy direction.
