Title: Menopause management: A manual for primary care practitioners and nurse practitioners
Publication date: 2022-07-22
Publication: Journal of Mid-Life Health 13, supplement 1
PMCID: PMC9490892
DOI: 10.4103/jmh.jmh_85_22
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9490892/
Coverage: Full original clinical manual, nutrition and sleep sections.

The nutrition recommendations list lentils, oranges and oily fish such as salmon among broader food choices. The lifestyle text also names coffee when discussing caffeine, dietary context and sleep. These are general recommendations for menopausal care rather than an assigned exposure study. They supply no specific preparation, Parkinson population or separate estimate for the mapped symptoms.

The egg passages concern ova and donor eggs. They do not concern eating eggs. No studied preparation of date fruit was identified in the text.
