Title: An intensive neurorehabilitation programme with sEMG biofeedback to improve swallowing in idiopathic Parkinson's disease (IPD): A feasibility study
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1460-6984.12824
PMID: 36478035
DOI: 10.1111/1460-6984.12824
Publication date: 2022-12-08
Date basis: First online publication. The journal issue appeared in 2023.
Coverage: Paraphrased original methods, Results and Table 3.

Twelve people with Parkinson disease and dysphagia enrolled, and ten completed a single-group swallowing rehabilitation study. Assessments before, after and at three-month follow-up combined endoscopy, self-ratings and interviews. Vallecular residue after saliva and cracker swallowing declined in the reported comparisons and remained lower at follow-up. Water and yogurt residue had no clear change. The ROMP–Saliva self-rating showed a small, statistically inconclusive change, and five people described improved saliva control in interviews. The residue scale was not specifically validated for secretions. These comparisons evaluate a rehabilitation program and standardized test boluses, with no raw fig assigned.
