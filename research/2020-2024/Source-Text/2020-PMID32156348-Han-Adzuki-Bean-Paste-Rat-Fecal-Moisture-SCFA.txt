Title: Dietary adzuki bean paste dose-dependently reduces visceral fat accumulation in rats fed a normal diet.
PMID: 32156348
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2019.108890
First published: 2020
Authors: Han KH, Ohashi S, Sasaki K, Nagata R, Pelpolage S, Fukuma N, et al.
Journal: Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32156348/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical rat feeding study (Han et al., 2020, Food Res Int) feeding healthy normal-diet rats control, 30%, or 58.9% adzuki bean (Vigna angularis) paste (14.5% fiber, 4.0% resistant starch) for 28 days. Both paste diets lowered energy intake and visceral fat, increased fecal lipid/acidic sterol excretion, increased cecal acetic/propionic/n-butyric acids, lowered cecal pH, and increased microbial alpha-diversity (all p<0.05). Cecal content and fecal moisture were higher in the 58.9% paste group vs control (p<0.05). Limits: healthy non-constipated rats without constipation induction, transit time, or stool frequency scoring.
