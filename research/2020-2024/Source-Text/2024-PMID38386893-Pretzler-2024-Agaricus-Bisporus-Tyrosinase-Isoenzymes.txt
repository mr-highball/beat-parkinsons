Title: Mushroom Tyrosinase: Six Isoenzymes Catalyzing Distinct Reactions.
PMID: 38386893
DOI: 10.1002/cbic.202400050
First published: 2024
Authors: Pretzler M, Rompel A
Journal: Chembiochem : a European journal of chemical biology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38386893/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Biochemical characterization of all six Agaricus bisporus polyphenol oxidase/tyrosinase isoenzymes (abPPO1-6) catalyzing phenolic/catecholic oxidation including L-DOPA reactions. Laboratory enzyme reference explaining why heat denaturation during cooking/roasting separates culinary button mushrooms from raw tyrosinase-enriched extracts.
