Topic: Safflower and olive comparator in a bipolar feasibility trial
Source: https://etuaptmumk.net/index.php/EJ/article/download/19/15
Coverage: Primary source facts and outcome boundary
Results were first released as a June 29, 2023 preprint. The May 24, 2024 article identifies NCT03541031 and reports 69 randomized and 49 analyzed. Its comparison was a multinutrient product plus fish oil versus both olive- and safflower-oil placebos in bipolar outpatients. The Hamilton Anxiety row prints coefficient −1.274, SE 2.754 and p=.646. The confidence interval printed as −4.264 to 6.812 centers on +1.274, exposing a sign discrepancy. No independent safflower contrast is available.
