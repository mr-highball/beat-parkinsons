Title: Attenuation of inflammatory response in the EAE model by PEGlated nanoliposome of pistachio oils.
PMID: 32781342
DOI: 10.1016/j.jneuroim.2020.577352
First published: 2020
Authors: Jebali A, Noroozi Karimabad M, Ahmadi Z, Khorramdel H, Kaeidi A, Mirzaei M
Journal: Journal of neuroimmunology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32781342/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Primary Abstract:
The aim of this study was to investigate the effect of PEGlated nanoliposome of pistachio unsaturated oils (PEGNLPUOs) to attenuate the inflammatory response in the EAE model by modulating of NFKB and oxidative stress signaling pathway. Real-time PCR demonstrated that the administration of 10%v/v PEGNLPUOs significantly decreased the expression level of AKT1, MAPK, and NFKB genes from NFKB signaling pathway and MGST1, NOS2, and HO-1 genes from oxidative stress signaling pathway. This study showed that the administration of pistachio oil and PEGNLPUOs at a concentration of 10%v/v decreased the number and percentage of Th1(CD4+) and increased Th2(CD8+) cells.

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Preclinical neuroimmunology study (J Neuroimmunol 2020) administering 10% v/v pistachio unsaturated oil and PEGylated nanoliposome formulations (PEGNLPUOs) in an experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis (EAE, multiple sclerosis) model, measuring only Th1/Th2 cell ratios and NF-kB/AKT1/MAPK/HO-1 mRNA expression. Excluded from directional PD claims: non-PD demyelinating autoimmune model, pharmaceutical nanoliposome/non-culinary matrix, and zero clinical or behavioral symptom endpoints.
