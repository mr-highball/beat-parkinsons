Factual extract of the primary English abstract and article text, checked 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

A single-arm clinical course involved 50 adults with mean age 48.48 ± 14.31 years and baseline PSQI above 5. They received oral Kleeb Bua Daeng, which combines Piper nigrum, Centella asiatica and Nelumbo nucifera, in two 400 mg capsules after breakfast and again before bed. Mean PSQI (SD) was 12.22 (3.12) at baseline and 8.66 (3.23), 7.12 (3.04), 6.06 (2.75) and 5.30 (2.61) after weeks 1, 2, 3 and 4. The report lists nine individuals with adverse reactions during the first three weeks and five during week four. The report does not identify a concurrent comparator or a PD cohort and provides no effect estimate for isolated pepper.
