Title: Whole grape powder and the gut microbiome in healthy adults
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8619073/
PMID: 34836220
PMCID: PMC8619073
DOI: 10.3390/nu13113965
Publication date: 2021-11-06
Date basis: Electronic publication in the original.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full structured original.

A pilot study analyzed 19 healthy adults. After a four-week dietary standardization phase, participants continued the diet with four weeks of added whole grape powder. The pre–post analysis found a higher Shannon diversity index at follow-up (p=.04) and reported higher relative abundance of Lachnospiraceae UCG-010.

The study has no concurrent control group. It measured microbial and metabolic endpoints, with no anxiety outcome, raw grape assignment or Parkinson cohort. A later migraine and irritable bowel syndrome study cites the microbiome result as background. It does not repeat the grape intervention.
