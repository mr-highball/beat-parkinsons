Original study scope
The Iranian Sniffin Sticks adaptation used odor familiarity and identification in a healthy sample. Cardamom was among the substituted cues and saffron appears as a response option. Familiarity percentages and distractor choices identify the role of foods without measuring effects of eating them.

Continuing evidence gaps
Seek oral food exposure data and change in Parkinson smell or other symptoms. Test familiarity is a measurement context, not a treatment comparator.
