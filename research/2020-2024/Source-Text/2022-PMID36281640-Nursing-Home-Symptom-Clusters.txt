Original study scope
The analysis groups newly admitted nursing-home residents by health profile and self-reported suicidal ideation. Clinical assessment items include pain and sleep-related or functional features.

Continuing evidence gaps
The health profiles are observational and give no sage or date exposure contrast, including for pain, insomnia and fatigue in Parkinson disease.
