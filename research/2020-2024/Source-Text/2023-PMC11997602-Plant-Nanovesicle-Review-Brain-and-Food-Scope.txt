Title: Plant-derived nanovesicles for brain disorders
PMID: 40242551
PMCID: PMC11997602
DOI: 10.1016/j.fmre.2023.09.007
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11997602/
Date basis: 2023-12-05 electronic publication. The issue was published in March 2025.
Coverage: Factual extract of the complete primary publication and relevant ingredient contexts.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The review identifies carrot, blueberry, ginger and orange as sources of isolated plant material or vesicles in different experimental settings. Carrot-derived material appears in preclinical neuroprotection work. The ginger and curcumin, ginger and aloe, and grape registrations concern different populations and endpoints. These preparations are not culinary foods and give no direct Parkinson symptom estimate for the assigned exposure.
