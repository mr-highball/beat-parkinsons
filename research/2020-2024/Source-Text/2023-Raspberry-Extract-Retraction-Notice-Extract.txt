Factual synopsis of the publisher's complete notice.

The notice retracts Chen et al. 2022, DOI 10.1155/2022/9943598. The publisher reports identifying one or more signs of systematic interference in publication. The possibilities listed concern scope, description of the research, availability of data, citations, coherence or relevance of material, and peer review. The notice does not specify which signs applied individually. The publisher says it cannot assure the content's reliability and did not investigate author awareness or involvement. The notice supplies publication-integrity evidence for the same study, without a separate biological experiment.
