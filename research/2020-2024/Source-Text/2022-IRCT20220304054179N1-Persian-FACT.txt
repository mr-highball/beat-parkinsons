The official recorded registration date is April 13, 2022. A separate first-public-posting date could not be verified. The registry describes a four-seed mixture with a 10% mixture comparator and prespecified postprandial fullness, pain and burning symptom outcomes. The WHO entry uses caraway wording, whereas the identifier-matched publication labels common cumin by its Latin name. The registry summary and article also differ on allocation ratio and reference ingredients. The registry has no structured results, while the trial report has published results. Actual enrollment dates are unavailable.
