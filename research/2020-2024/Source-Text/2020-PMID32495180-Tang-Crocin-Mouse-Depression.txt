Title: Crocin Reverses Depression-Like Behavior in Parkinson Disease Mice via VTA-mPFC Pathway
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32495180/
PMID: 32495180
DOI: 10.1007/s12035-020-01941-2
First public: 2020-06-03
Coverage: Factual extract from the author abstract. Group-level estimates and original full methods remain open.

Tang and colleagues tested isolated crocin in a mouse model of Parkinson-related depressive-like behavior. Approximately 60% of mice in the induced model showed the forced-swim phenotype used by the investigators. After ten days of crocin treatment, the abstract describes a favorable behavioral comparison and changes in dopamine-associated circuitry and synaptic markers. It gives no group counts or uncertainty for the behavior. The authors did not compare milled edible stigma or assess human depression. The Kaur and Gupta review's registered bibliography identifies this paper as a distinct underlying experiment.
