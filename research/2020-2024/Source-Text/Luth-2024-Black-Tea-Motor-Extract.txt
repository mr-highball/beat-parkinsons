The versions of Lüth and colleagues' longitudinal analysis in 2024 and 2025 report the same Fox Insight LRRK2 black tea association (−0.51, SE .22, p=.028) and PPMI Non-Online corroboration (−0.41, SE .18, p=.026) with total MDS-UPDRS Part II scores. Two idiopathic PD patient samples provided inconclusive comparisons. The habitual caffeinated black tea item concerns lifetime intake. The models provide no individual walking speed or tremor estimate. The earlier 2024 MDS abstract 448 used broader clinical endpoints and did not report a black-tea coefficient.
