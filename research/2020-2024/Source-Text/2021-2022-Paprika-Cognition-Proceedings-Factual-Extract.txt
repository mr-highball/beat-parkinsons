Scope: Two separately reported primary conference experiments using paprika xanthophyll with exercise or mouth rinse.

2021 JPFSM, volume 10 issue 6, abstract 464. Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jpfsm/10/6/10_398/_pdf/-char/en
Yamashiro, Kim and Wadazumi studied 13 university students across five conditions. The paprika xanthophyll comparison used 9 mg/day for four weeks. The reported pre-post Trail Making B improvements occurred with dual-task exercise plus carbohydrate mouth rinse in the presence or absence of paprika. Stroop improvement was also reported for exercise alone. The report supplies no between-condition paprika-specific effect estimate.

2022 JPFSM, volume 11 issue 6, abstract 450. Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jpfsm/11/6/11_427/_pdf
Kim and Wadazumi studied 17 university students in a late-night randomized crossover with seven conditions. Only the complete combination of dual-task exercise, carbohydrate mouth rinse and paprika xanthophyll was reported to improve Trail Making before versus after the test (p < .05). The abstract gives no direct effect of adding paprika to the otherwise identical condition, effect size, or confidence interval. The study uses a supplement and no Parkinson cohort. The 2024 registration UMIN000054104 concerns a separate planned middle-aged adult population.
