Title: Abdominal pain coverage in a prebiotic bar pilot in Parkinson disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9938693/
PMID: 36801916
PMCID: PMC9938693
DOI: 10.1038/s41467-023-36497-x
Registration: NCT04512599
First public: 2023-02-18
Coverage: Paraphrased original clinical methods and Table 1.

The 20-person open-label Rush study compared baseline and 10-day measurements after a prebiotic fiber bar. The publication lists resistant starch, rice-bran terminology, resistant maltodextrin and inulin. The published clinical intervention does not identify date paste or whole dried dates as assigned exposures. The methods include abdominal discomfort and pain in an intake of gastrointestinal symptoms. Table 1 gives an overall gastrointestinal score of 35.85 before and 27.65 after treatment (p=.09). The treated subgroup's total decreased from 41.8 to 22.7 (n=10, p=.01). These totals combine symptoms. No separate abdominal pain estimate appears in the inspected methods and results.

The Rush bar's published identity and trial registration are distinct from the Stanford and Sorridi fiber-bar cohorts. Recovering an item-level pain record would clarify the Rush trial while leaving a separate dried-date comparison open.
