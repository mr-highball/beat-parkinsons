Original study scope
The original review concerns spinal cord injury. Its discussion of Panchakarma describes abhyanga self-massage with sesame oil under a traditional rationale and cites a separate Ayurvedic overview. Study-level interventions are heterogeneous.

Continuing evidence gaps
The oil reference concerns topical massage and tradition, without an isolated consumption comparison or a Parkinson cohort. Pain and depression observations from the broader spinal cord injury literature cannot be assigned to the oil. Anxiety and fatigue effects of a frozen sesame preparation remain open.
