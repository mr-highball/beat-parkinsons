Title: Effects of flaxseed supplementation on functional constipation and quality of life in a Chinese population: A randomized trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32229443/
DOI: 10.6133/apjcn.202003_29(1).0009
Publication date: 2020-03
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: Original PDF methods, flow diagram and Table 2 symptom results.
PMID: 32229443
Original full text: https://apjcn.qdu.edu.cn/9_2019-0275_61_67.pdf

The clinical study randomized 90 adults with functional constipation in a two-to-one comparison of flaxseed flour and lactulose. The outcome tables include 60 flour and 28 lactulose participants. At four weeks, the reported median reduction in overall Wexner constipation score was 7 points (IQR 4–9) versus 5 points (IQR 3–7), with reported p=.017 for the difference in changes. Both arms actively addressed constipation. This comparison concerns seed flour in a non-Parkinson population, leaving oil extraction and the frozen Parkinson symptom untested.
