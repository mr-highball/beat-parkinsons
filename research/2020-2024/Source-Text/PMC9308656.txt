Title: CAG Somatic Instability in a Huntington Disease Expansion Carrier Presenting with a Progressive Supranuclear Palsy‐like Phenotype
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9308656/
PMID: 35510647
PMCID: PMC9308656
DOI: 10.1002/mds.29035
Publication date: 2022-05-05
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The original case reports atypical parkinsonism, behavioral changes, visual hallucinations and a preference for sweets in a Huntington expansion carrier. The preference is not a measured dried fig intake, and the clinical report has no food-timed comparison.
