Title: Assessment of heterogeneity among participants in the Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative cohort using α-synuclein seed amplification: a cross-sectional study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10627170/
PMID: 37059509
PMCID: PMC10627170
DOI: 10.1016/S1474-4422(23)00109-6
Publication date: 2023-05
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Verified NLM original PMC XML body and tables. Factual extract.

The cross-sectional Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative analysis included a prodromal group with REM sleep behavior disorder, confirmed by polysomnogram, and used a REM sleep behavior disorder screening questionnaire. It reports no clear association between questionnaire score and biomarker status in the studied groups. It does not test caraway, toasted preparation, or a food-associated change in dream enactment frequency.
