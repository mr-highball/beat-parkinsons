Zang and colleagues reported a 22-year-old who described painful stomatitis, increased saliva, fatigue and dizziness after eating kiwi. A clinician inferred allergic contact stomatitis. Kiwi avoidance and multiple treatments began together.
The article was accepted June 20, 2022, and the exact first public day remains unresolved. The reported tongue enlargement and oral lesions were accompanied by difficulty eating. The report gives no objective salivary or orthostatic measurements, dose, cultivar or challenge confirmation. Recovery cannot be assigned to an individual intervention.
