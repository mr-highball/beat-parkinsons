Title: Food-derived antioxidant ergothioneine improves sleep difficulties in humans
DOI: 10.1016/j.jff.2022.105165
First published: 2022
Authors: Katsube M, Watanabe H, Suzuki K, Ishimoto T, Tatebayashi Y, Kato Y, Murayama N
Journal: Journal of Functional Foods
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jff.2022.105165
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Four-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled human trial evaluating 20 mg/d food-derived ergothioneine vs placebo in adults with sleep difficulties (findings corroborated in Okumura et al. 2025 PMC12138820 from the same Kanazawa/Suntory group). Reported significant improvement in subjective PSQI-J sleep difficulty and objective EEG sleep architecture: increased NREM N2 stage, decreased N1 stage, and reduced wake after sleep onset (WASO) frequency. Limits: isolated 20 mg/d ergothioneine supplement rather than culinary A. bisporus, non-PD adults, full publisher PDF paywalled (details verified via Crossref metadata and Okumura 2025 open-access full text).
