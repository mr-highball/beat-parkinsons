Title: Bilateral nucleus basalis of Meynert deep brain stimulation for dementia with Lewy bodies: A randomised clinical trial
Source: https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10096484/
PMID: 32334074
DOI: 10.1016/j.brs.2020.04.010
Publication date: 2020-04-22
Source depth: complete 28-page journal pre-proof from the author institution; locally distilled

The six-person dementia-with-Lewy-bodies crossover compares six weeks of active with sham bilateral nucleus basalis stimulation, with an intervening washout. The primary cognitive comparisons showed no consistent improvement. The prespecified North East Visual Hallucinations Interview appears in the methods. Five participants had analyzable Neuropsychiatric Inventory (NPI) data. Table 2 reports NPI total medians of 21 in sham and 9 in active stimulation (median within-participant difference -13, p = 0.066). The hallucinations subscale medians were 0 and 0 (median within-participant difference 0, p = 0.655). That exploratory subscale is distinct from a separately counted visual episode outcome. The individual interview table was not found in the recovered journal pre-proof.

The registered trial is NCT02263937. Movement Disorders Clinical Practice 2019 abstract 222 reports the same six-person crossover. The cited 2018 Parkinson disease dementia crossover (PMID29255885, NCT01701544) supplies earlier background from a separate trial. The study tests stimulation in dementia with Lewy bodies, with no dried fig exposure. The PDF cover, interview methods, analysis-set notes and Table 2 were checked on PDF pages 1, 10, 13 and 25.
