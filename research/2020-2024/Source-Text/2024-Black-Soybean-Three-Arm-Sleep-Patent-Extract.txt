Three arm soybean extract sleep comparison reported in a patent
Source: https://patents.google.com/patent/TW202426025A/en
Coverage: Original patent Methods and Figures 3–6; earliest public family disclosure located

The Taiwan application was published July 1, 2024. The [international counterpart](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2024142285A1/en) followed July 4 and JP7771432B2 follows the same patent family. The 12-week double-blind comparison reports 69 participants in three arms, with 21, 25 and 23 assigned to placebo, lower-dose and higher-dose black soybean seed coat extract. Sixty-five were evaluable for sleep. OSA-MA was assessed on waking. The patent reports no significant between-group differences on the sleep factors, despite within-arm changes. Enrollment and study design differ from the later 64-participant two-arm paper, while participant independence remains unverified. This is an extract study in healthy adults, without a Parkinson daytime sleepiness outcome or boiled common-bean exposure.
