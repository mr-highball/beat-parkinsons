Title: Examining the health effects and bioactive components in Agaricus bisporus mushrooms: a scoping review.
PMID: 32653808
DOI: 10.1016/j.jnutbio.2020.108453
First published: 2020
Authors: Blumfield M, Abbott K, Duve E, Cassettari T, Marshall S, Fayet-Moore F
Journal: The Journal of nutritional biochemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32653808/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Scoping review synthesizing human health studies and bioactive density (ergothioneine, beta-glucans, ergosterol, vitamin D2, chitin, flavonoids) of Agaricus bisporus, noting strongest human evidence for UVB-mediated vitamin D2 bioavailability and sparse replication for clinical disease outcomes. Context-only secondary synthesis.
