Title: The short-term effect of dark chocolate flavanols on cognition in older adults: A randomized controlled trial (FlaSeCo)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32229139/

First public article date: 2020-03-27
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Author manuscript, indexed abstract and 2018 original poster P-445

The randomized trial included 100 older adults. Both groups
received dark chocolate, with differing flavanol amounts. The
published comparisons for Trail Making A and B have p=.93 and
p=.66 respectively. The accessible original poster reports a
depression scale and describes no change in depression. Baseline
depression scores also appear in the manuscript.

The preparation contrast concerns two chocolates rather than
chocolate intake versus none. A separately reported anxiety
severity outcome was not found in the checked report. The 2018
abstract, the 2020 paper and the 2017 registration describe one
study and cannot be counted as three observations.
