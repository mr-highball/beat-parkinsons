Rhus javanica gall extract and attention tasks

Source: https://ffhdj.com/index.php/ffhd/article/download/1213/1932/8012
Coverage: Original Tables 3–5, factual extract.

Eighty people were randomized and 68 completed six weeks of medicinal Rhus javanica gall extract or cornstarch. Adjusted ASRS score was 35.14 versus 38.73, p = .006. Number-count accuracy and time were inconclusive at p = .67 and .24. Tower of Hanoi time p = .03 and accuracy p = .09. Culinary Rhus coriaria fruit and Parkinson disease were outside the comparison.
