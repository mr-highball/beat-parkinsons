Watermelon seed in an experimental three-seed mixture
Publication: 2020
Source: https://www.journals.innovareacademics.in/index.php/ajpcr/article/download/36549/21843
Coverage: Anitha Nandagopal and Mir Amjad Ali Khan, Asian Journal of Pharmaceutical and Clinical Research 13(3), 121–125. Original issue year 2020. January 18 is labeled acceptance in the version of record. First publication day unverified.
DOI: 10.22159/ajpcr.2020.v13i3.36549

Methods identify the ingredients as extracts of Prunus amygdalus, Arachis hypogaea and Citrullus lanatus seeds. Figures 1 and 2 compare the mixture with a chlorpromazine control and report less catalepsy and higher activity. The figures report six animals per group. No arm isolates watermelon, and the preparation differs from uncooked watermelon flesh. The locomotor outcome is an animal proxy.
