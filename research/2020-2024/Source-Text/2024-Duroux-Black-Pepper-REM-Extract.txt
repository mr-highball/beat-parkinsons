# Black pepper essential oil in an adult sleep comparator

Original source https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jocd.16179
Source coverage original sleep Methods and Table 5

Duroux and colleagues first published on January 11, 2024. Thirty-two adults used each of three facial creams for one bedtime week. The pepper cream contained 0.0025% Piper nigrum essential oil. Pepper and tea-tree weeks were counterbalanced and the unscented control was always week two. Noncontact sleep staging gave mean REM duration of 75.8 minutes versus 72.4 with unscented cream. The paper reports no significant pepper-to-unscented sleep change and gives no exact REM p-value for that comparison. It measured no dream-enactment episodes or REM without atonia and used a different route from culinary pepper.
