Title: A methodological approach for botulinum neurotoxin injections to the longus colli muscle in dystonic anterocollis: A case series of 4 patients and a literature review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7448884/
PMID: 33099344
PMCID: PMC7448884
DOI: 10.1016/j.jocn.2020.08.025
Publication date: 2020-08-26
Source depth: Original article body and case outcome table
Date basis: Electronic date in the provider record, with a later journal issue.
Design: Four-case clinical series and literature review of interventions for dystonic neck flexion

The fourth case had early-onset cerebellar degeneration and anterocollis. The individual reported improved swallowing and less drooling after the posture treatment, with improvement in activities affected by neck position. These were case observations without a quantitative drooling scale, a control group, Parkinson disease, or an assigned food. This case distinguishes reported changes in saliva loss and swallowing from unrelated procedure mentions and remains a low-fit observation for Parkinson food claims.
