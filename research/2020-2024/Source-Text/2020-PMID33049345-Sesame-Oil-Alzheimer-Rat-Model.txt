The investigators used sesame oil in a chemically induced rat
Alzheimer model. They report favorable memory and learning readouts
and several biochemical comparisons. The original abstract mentions
the open-field and water-maze assessments. It supplies no diagnosed
human Parkinson population, rigidity measure or fall frequency.

The 2024 natural-products review (PMID 38755627, reference 32) gives a
conflicting year in its reference list. The original DOI, early date
and later issue identify one rodent experiment. This record gives
its actual model and outcomes without assigning a Parkinson sign.
