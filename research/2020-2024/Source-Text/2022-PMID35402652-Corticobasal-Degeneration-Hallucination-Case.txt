Title: REM Sleep Behavior Disorder and Visual Hallucinations in a Pathologically Confirmed Case of Corticobasal Degeneration.
PMID: 35402652
PMCID: PMC8974869
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13403
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8974869/
First publication date: 2022-01-06 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: Official PMC complete article page, including body and displayed study observations.

A pathology-confirmed corticobasal degeneration case showed REM sleep behavior disorder and visual hallucinations. The case challenges a simple inference from hallucination phenotype to synuclein pathology. It supplies no dried fig exposure or dietary contrast in Parkinson disease.
