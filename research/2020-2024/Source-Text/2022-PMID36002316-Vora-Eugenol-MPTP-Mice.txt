Title: Effects of eugenol on the behavioral and pathological progression in the MPTP-induced Parkinson's disease mouse model.
PMID: 36002316
DOI: 10.5582/ddt.2022.01026
First published: 2022
Authors: Vora U, Vyas VK, Wal P, Saxena B
Journal: Drug discoveries & therapeutics
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36002316/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical MPTP mouse Parkinson's model testing 7-day oral pretreatment versus 7-day oral post-treatment with isolated eugenol (25, 50, 100 mg/kg/day) on actophotometer, beam-walking, catalepsy, rearing, rotarod, brain lipid peroxidation, and reduced glutathione. Eugenol pretreatment reversed MPTP motor dysfunction and oxidative stress, whereas post-treatment at high dose AGGRAVATED MPTP-induced akinesia and augmented lipid peroxidation while further attenuating reduced glutathione. Authors concluded eugenol showed preventive but not curative benefit and demonstrated dose/timing-dependent directional conflict.
