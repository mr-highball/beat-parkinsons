Title: Delivery of Polyphenols in Gum as an Anti-Caries Agent
Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05133557
API record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05133557
Date: 2021-11-24 first posted, actual completion 2015-11-30
Coverage: factual extract of registered interventions, dates and outcomes, prepared 2026-11-14

The University of Kentucky registered a randomized crossover comparison of xylitol gum and xylitol gum with blackberry extract. Actual enrollment was 50. The registry's arms describe 2 g gum with 1 g xylitol and, in the active arm, 100 mg blackberry extract. There were four 20-minute gum periods and a one-week crossover interval. The primary endpoint is the change in bacterial counts in saliva after the four pieces, at seven hours. An enamel bacterial count is secondary. No results are posted in this record. The preparation amounts differ from the 2022 journal abstract, which describes 700 mg xylitol and 50 mg freeze-dried blackberry powder per piece. The registry offers no voice outcome.
