Title: Warning on False or True Morels and Button Mushrooms with Potential Toxicity Linked to Hydrazinic Toxins: An Update.
PMID: 32751277
PMCID: PMC7472412
DOI: 10.3390/toxins12080482
First published: 2020
Authors: Lagrange E, Vernoux JP
Journal: Toxins
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7472412/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Toxicology review noting Agaricus bisporus contains the water-soluble hydrazine agaritine (~200+ ug/g fresh) which is weakly carcinogenic in rodents when raw/extracted (Hashida 1990 PMID:2132000) and degrades substantially with boiling/cooking; advises avoiding raw or poorly cooked button mushrooms. Safety/preparation context only.
