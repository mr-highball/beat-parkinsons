Title: The Impact of Epigallocatechin Gallate and Coconut Oil Treatment on Cortisol Activity and Depression in Multiple Sclerosis Patients.
PMID: 33920655
PMCID: PMC8073508
DOI: 10.3390/life11040353
Registry ID: NCT03740295
First published: 2021
Authors: Platero JL, Cuerda-Ballester M, Sancho-Cantus D, Benlloch M, Ceron JJ, Peres Rubio C, García-Pardo MP, López-Rodríguez MM
Journal: Life (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8073508/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Companion report from the same 51-participant multiple sclerosis pilot trial (Platero et al., 2021, Life, PMC8073508; NCT03740295, same cohort as Platero 2020 PMID:31979305 and Cuerda-Ballester 2023 PMID:36594273) testing 60 mL/day coconut oil PLUS 800 mg/day EGCG on an isocaloric diet vs placebo for 4 months. Reported reduced Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II) depression scores and abdominal fat with increased serum albumin and unchanged total cortisol in the combined intervention arm. Limits: unusable attribution for isolated virgin coconut oil due to co-administration with 800 mg/day EGCG in MS rather than Parkinson disease.
