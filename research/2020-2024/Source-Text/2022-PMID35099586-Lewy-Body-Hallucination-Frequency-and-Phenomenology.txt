Title: Visual hallucinations in Lewy body disease: pathophysiological insights from phenomenology
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9217885/
PMID: 35099586
PMCID: PMC9217885
DOI: 10.1007/s00415-022-10983-6
Publication year: 2022
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The study combines symptom interviews and network imaging in 35 analyzable people with Lewy body disease. It codes hallucination content, duration and frequency separately. Imaging correlations are linked to characteristics of reported phenomena, without a dietary exposure.

The complete study separates simple and complex visual phenomena. The analyzed sample includes 19 dementia with Lewy bodies and 16 Parkinson dementia cases. Nine reported illusions, nine sensed presence, twelve passage phenomena and thirty-two complex hallucinations. Categories can overlap.
