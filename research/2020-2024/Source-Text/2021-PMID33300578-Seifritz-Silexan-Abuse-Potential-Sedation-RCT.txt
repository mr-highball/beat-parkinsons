Title: No Abuse Potential of Silexan in Healthy Recreational Drug Users: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 33300578
PMCID: PMC7968617
DOI: 10.1093/ijnp/pyaa064
First published: 2021
Authors: Seifritz E, Möller HJ, Volz HP, Müller WE, Hopyan T, Wacker A, Schläfke S, Kasper S
Journal: The international journal of neuropsychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33300578/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Phase I double-blind, 5-way crossover randomized controlled trial (Seifritz et al., 2021, Int J Neuropsychopharmacol, PMC7968617) in 40 randomized (34 evaluable) healthy CNS-depressant users given single oral doses of Silexan 80 mg, Silexan 640 mg, lorazepam 2 mg, lorazepam 4 mg, and placebo. Both Silexan doses (up to 8x therapeutic dose) matched placebo on drug-liking and sedative VAS scales and differed significantly from lorazepam (p<0.001), confirming absence of sedative or abuse liability.
