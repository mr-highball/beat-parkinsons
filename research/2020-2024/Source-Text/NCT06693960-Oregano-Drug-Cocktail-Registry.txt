Oregano softgel and a registered drug interaction comparison
Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06693960
Coverage: factual extract of first posted record and interventions.

The registry first posted November 19, 2024. The planned comparison uses a 180 mg oregano oil softgel and a cocktail of caffeine, dextromethorphan, losartan, midazolam and omeprazole, with a target enrollment of 16. Its primary outcome is a midazolam exposure ratio. Levodopa and the frozen Parkinson outcomes are outside the registered comparison. The record was recruiting with no results posted in the checked API snapshot.
