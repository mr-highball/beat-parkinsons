Corn oil sham comparison in a mouse olfactory training experiment
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32074755/
PMID 32074755. DOI 10.3760/cma.j.issn.1673-0860.2020.02.013.
Coverage: original author abstract factual extract.

Published February 7, 2020. Zhou and colleagues randomized 31 mice to a corn-oil sham injection, induced olfactory loss without training, or induced loss plus odor training. The day-28 buried-pellet times were 26.63 ± 7.60 s in sham controls, 176.63 ± 61.06 s in the injured untrained arm, and 67.79 ± 32.54 s in the injured trained arm. The reported differences concern injury and training, with no oil-free control or feeding intervention. Refining and Parkinson status are outside the comparison.
