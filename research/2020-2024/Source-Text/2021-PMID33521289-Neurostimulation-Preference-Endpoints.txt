Original study scope
The study uses threshold interviews to elicit the risks people with Parkinson disease would tolerate and how long they would wait for hypothetical neurostimulation benefits. Pain and motor status were used to describe patient baselines and options.

Continuing evidence gaps
The hypothetical device tradeoffs do not involve fresh sage or culinary dates, or provide food-linked changes in pain or falls.
