Turmeric extract and motor tasks in lesioned rats
https://doi.org/10.1007/s13596-021-00606-2
Original primary abstract paraphrase

First published in September 2021. The original abstract describes orally administered Curcuma longa extract in a 6-hydroxydopamine rat model and reports improvement in selected motor and cognitive tasks. The full Methods and outcome-specific numeric tables were inaccessible at review. This was an extract and a rodent behavioral comparison, without a clinical bradykinesia or dietary ground-spice result.
