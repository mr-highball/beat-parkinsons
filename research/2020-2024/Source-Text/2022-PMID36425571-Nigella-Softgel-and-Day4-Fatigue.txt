The open-label 2022 seed-oil softgel comparison reports day-four fatigue in 12/30 versus 25/30, adjusted OR 0.15 (95% CI 0.04–0.48). Its endpoint is fatigue presence on a particular day. The acute-illness setting, formulation and reporting limit transfer to ground culinary nigella and Parkinson fatigue severity. The comparison uses the Baraka oil-only arm and usual therapy. Baseline fatigue was recorded in 28/30 and 30/30 respectively. The separate vitamin D3 and combination groups are not counted as additional independent trials. The historical branded product is identified as nigella seed oil. The methods' total and frequency descriptions differ, so the daily amount is unresolved. This finding gives no direct dose or effect estimate for cooking with ground nigella or Parkinson disease.
