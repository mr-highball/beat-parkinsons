The nutrition meeting report compares dietary records in a liver disease cohort grouped by simple steatosis and NASH. The NASH group had lower recorded onion intake by the study's relative diet-index measure (0.02 versus 0.07, p=0.006). The abstract reports group differences in potatoes and dairy butter and identifies Russian Science Foundation support. It did not assign onion intake and cannot establish direction of causation. It provides no specific raw preparation or Parkinson rigidity outcome.

UEG 2021 P0652 reports identical food values, p values and an overlapping investigator group. Keep the records in the same source family, with no independent replication credited.
