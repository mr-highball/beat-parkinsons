Tosin and colleagues analyzed 331 of the 336 participants in the STEADY-PD III trial, pooling the randomized isradipine and placebo arms. They classified incident scale items at the 12- and 24-month visits and grouped participants by whether they started separate symptomatic treatment during an interval. The symptomatic-treatment grouping was observational.

In year two, the saliva and drooling item was newly endorsed more often among participants who began symptomatic treatment (reported p=0.043). The total year-two sample was 148, split into 62 who started treatment and 86 who did not. The figure removes people who had already endorsed each item from that item's denominator. The reported comparison concerns new endorsement. It supplies no item-level test of severity change, dose response, or raw-fig exposure. The exact item numerator and denominator for each treatment group are not tabulated.
