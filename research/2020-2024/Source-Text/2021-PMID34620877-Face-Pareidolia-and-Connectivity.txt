Title: Decreased frontotemporal connectivity in patients with parkinson's disease experiencing face pareidolia.
PMID: 34620877
PMCID: PMC8497472
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-021-00237-z
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8497472/
First publication date: 2021-10-07 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

The comparison uses face pareidolia and associated frontotemporal connectivity in Parkinson disease. Mistaking ambiguous stimuli for faces is the controlled task. This differs from spontaneous recurrence and does not compare dried fig intake.
