Title: Prominent Prolongation of Cortical Silent Period Induced by Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation in Creutzfeldt-Jakob Disease.
PMID: 33362525
PMCID: PMC7747086
DOI: 10.1159/000510395
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7747086/
First publication date: 2020-09-01 (Europe PMC original-search record).

A rapidly progressive Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease case developed visual hallucinations along with cognitive and motor changes. The study measures an electrophysiological cortical silent period. This disease and procedure context gives no dried fig exposure or Parkinson episode-rate outcome.
