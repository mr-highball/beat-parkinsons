Title: Cannabidiol for Rapid Eye Movement Sleep Behavior Disorder.
PMID: 33754375
DOI: 10.1002/mds.28577
First published: 2021
Authors: de Almeida CMO, Brito MMC, Bosaipo NB, Pimentel AV, Tumas V, Zuardi AW et al.
Journal: Mov Disord
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33754375/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Phase II/III double-blind randomized controlled trial (de Almeida et al., 2021, Mov Disord, n=33 patients with Parkinson disease and REM sleep behavior disorder) comparing oral CBD (75-300 mg/day in corn oil) versus matching corn-oil placebo capsules for 14 weeks. Primary RBD frequency and clinical global outcomes did not differ between CBD and corn-oil placebo (secondary sleep satisfaction favored CBD at weeks 4-8). Corn oil was solely a shared carrier/placebo with no oil-free arm, providing zero causal attribution for refined corn oil on dream enactment, dyskinesia, or dystonia.
