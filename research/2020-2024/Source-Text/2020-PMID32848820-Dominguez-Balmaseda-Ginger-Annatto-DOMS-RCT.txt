Title: Effect of a Blend of Zingiber officinale Roscoe and Bixa orellana L. Herbal Supplement on the Recovery of Delayed-Onset Muscle Soreness Induced by Unaccustomed Eccentric Resistance Training: A Randomized, Triple-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial.
PMID: 32848820
PMCID: PMC7396658
DOI: 10.3389/fphys.2020.00826
Registry ID: NCT03961022
First published: 2020
Authors: Dominguez-Balmaseda, Diez-Vega, Larrosa, San Juan, Issaly
Journal: Frontiers in physiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32848820/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized triple-blind placebo-controlled trial (NCT03961022; companion gut-microbiota report PMID 35226949, n=34) in 33 young male athletes (17 ReWin(d), 16 placebo) testing ReWin(d), a 1:0.75 (w/w) polyherbal blend of whole Zingiber officinale rhizome powder and Bixa orellana L. seed powder (333 mg capsules) after 1-h eccentric plyometric exercise. Significant group interaction favored the blend for lower VAS muscle pain at 48 h (p = 0.004) and preserved lower-limb half-squat power at 24 h (p = 0.049), with no CK interaction. Limits: bundled with ginger (known analgesic in DOMS), cannot isolate annatto seed powder.
