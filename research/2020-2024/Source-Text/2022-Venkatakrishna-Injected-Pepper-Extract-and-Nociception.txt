Venkatakrishna K et al. Viphyllin, a standardized black pepper seed extract, in murine pain models. J Pain Res. First published 2022-02-05. PMID 35153513; PMCID PMC8827412; DOI 10.2147/JPR.S351513.
Original: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8827412/

This manufacturer-affiliated animal study evaluates a black pepper seed extract standardized to at least 30% β-caryophyllene. Injected extract increased response latency in thermal assays. Figure 4 tail-flick comparisons use six mice per group and report higher latency than vehicle from 30 to 90 minutes. The mice are a different experimental group and species from the 2022 diabetic-rat oral extract report. The route, extract, reflex endpoint and non-Parkinson model do not establish a clinical effect of cracked black pepper.
