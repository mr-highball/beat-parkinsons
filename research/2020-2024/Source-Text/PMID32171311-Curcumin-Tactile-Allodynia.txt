Title: Isolated curcumin and tactile allodynia in rats
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7076866/
PMID: 32171311
PMCID: PMC7076866
DOI: 10.1186/s12906-020-2867-z
Coverage: Original March 14, 2020 article, behavioral tests and results

Pastrana-Quintos and colleagues studied tactile allodynia after spinal nerve ligation in rats. Oral and intrathecal curcumin prevented the development of tactile allodynia in a dose-dependent manner in this model. The study measures responses to von Frey stimulation after laboratory nerve injury. It uses isolated curcumin and supplies no whole-spice comparison or Parkinson symptom result.
