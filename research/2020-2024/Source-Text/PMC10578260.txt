Title: Screening Tools for Sarcopenia in Mild to Moderate Parkinson's Disease: Assessing the Accuracy of SARC-F and Calf Circumference

PMCID: PMC10578260

Publication date: 2023-07-12

Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The cross-sectional study evaluates SARC-F and calf circumference against a sarcopenia reference assessment. It does not measure dried fig consumption or repeated visual hallucinations during a defined dietary interval.
