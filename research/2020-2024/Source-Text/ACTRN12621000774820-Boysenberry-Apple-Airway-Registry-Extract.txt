Title: The effect of BerriQi Boysenberry and apple product on lung function following ozone exposure
Original registry: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=381761
Identifier: ACTRN12621000774820
Date: registered 2021-06-21; last updated 2022-10-28
Coverage: factual extract of public registry fields, prepared 2026-11-14

The protocol plans randomized crossover use of food capsules made with freeze-dried, milled boysenberry and apple. The registry gives an intended product amount and dosing on days before and after a chamber visit. It lists pulmonary spirometry and respiratory symptom outcomes, including a visual-analogue lung-health score and the Chronic Respiratory Questionnaire. The registry aims for 80 healthy adults and shows no actual accrual, completed arm counts or results. An investigator and the product are also in the 2025 Lomiwes journal record. The registry's planned exposure cannot substitute for the article's administered formulation, and includes no clinical or acoustic measure of voice loudness.
