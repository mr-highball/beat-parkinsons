Title: Possibility for Prevention of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Dementia Using Three Kinds of Brown Rice Blends after High-Pressure Treatment.
PMID: 35327240
PMCID: PMC8947517
DOI: 10.3390/foods11060818
First published: 2022
Authors: Nakamura, Ikeuchi, Araki
Journal: Foods (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8947517/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week randomized parallel-group trial in n=24 adults comparing high-pressure-treated boiled blend of super-hard brown rice, wax-free black rice, ordinary brown rice, black rice bran, and rice oil (WFBSK) vs polished white rice. WFBSK improved Cognitrax language memory (p<0.05) and lowered postprandial insulin (p<0.05). Multi-rice anthocyanin-enriched blend; does not isolate plain brown rice.
