Title: Co-Treatment with the Herbal Medicine SIP3 and Donepezil Improves Memory and Depression in the Mouse Model of Alzheimer's Disease.
PMID: 35422218
DOI: 10.2174/1567205019666220413082130
First published: 2022
Authors: Liu QF, Choi H, Son T, Kim YM, Kanmani S, Chin YW, Kim SN, Kim KK et al.
Journal: Current Alzheimer research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35422218/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical Alzheimer's mouse study (Liu et al., 2022, Curr Alzheimer Res) combining SIP3 — a bundled 3-herb extract of Santalum album, Illicium verum, and Polygala tenuifolia — with donepezil in APP/PS1 transgenic AD mice, improving Morris water maze, passive avoidance, and depressive-like behavior vs donepezil alone. Heavily bundled 3-herb + drug combination in an AD model cannot attribute effects to culinary ground I. verum or Parkinson's disease.
