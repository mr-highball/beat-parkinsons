ANZCTR ACTRN12620000070932. Cannabidiol treatment for insomnia (CBD-INS).
Registered 2020-01-29, last updated 2023-02-03. Universal Trial Number U1111-1246-0810.
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=12620000070932
Scope: Factual summary of the official registration.

The study protocol lists randomized parallel CBD-in-corn-oil and identical oil vehicle primarily to test insomnia severity and other sleep outcomes. The registry intervention describes 200 mg CBD in 2 mL and three weeks of dosing. The listed primary outcome timepoints refer to two weeks. The published double-blind trial describes two weeks of randomized exposure and 150 mg CBD in 1.5 mL after a one-week placebo run-in. The placebo carries no cannabinoids and uses corn oil in both descriptions. No separate untreated or no-corn-oil arm is specified. This is an insomnia study rather than a Parkinson trial.

Primary linked report DOI 10.5664/jcsm.10998: https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.10998
