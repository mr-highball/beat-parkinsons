2020 — Acute mixed-spice meal pilot measured endothelial dilation
Source: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/fo/c9fo02438g
Coverage: Factual extract of Methods and vascular result.

NCT03064958 compared 0, 2 and 6 g of a spice blend in a meal challenge. The 6 g arm had a smaller postprandial reduction in flow-mediated dilation than the no-spice comparator. Blood pressure was a baseline characteristic. Coriander was one of several ingredients.
