Title: Neuroprotective and anti-amnesic effects of Laurus Nobilis essential oil against scopolamine-induced memory deficits in mice brain.
PMID: 37689325
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2023.117151
First published: 2024
Authors: Smach MA, Hafsa J, Ben Abdallah J, Charfeddine B, Limem K
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37689325/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical amnesia study (Smach et al., 2024, J Ethnopharmacol) testing Tunisian Laurus nobilis essential oil (100 mg/kg; rich in 1,8-cineole and alpha-terpinyl acetate) in scopolamine-treated mice. Essential oil improved Morris water maze and Y-maze spatial/working memory scores (p<0.05 vs scopolamine) and attenuated brain oxidative stress and acetylcholinesterase elevation. Limits: concentrated essential oil in an acute muscarinic-blockade mouse amnesia model; does not assess human Parkinson's executive function or ground culinary bay leaf.
