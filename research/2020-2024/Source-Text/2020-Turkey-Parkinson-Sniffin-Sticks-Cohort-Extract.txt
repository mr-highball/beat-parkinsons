Cinnamon and orange identification in a Turkish Parkinson cohort
Source: https://www.noropsikiyatriarsivi.com/sayilar/113/npa_c57s3_216-221.pdf
PMID: 32952424
PMCID: PMC7481972
DOI: 10.29399/npa.23070
Coverage: Original journal PDF Methods and diagnostic group comparisons

The September 2020 report compared 31 people with idiopathic Parkinson disease and 31 controls. The 12-item smell identification method includes cinnamon and orange among the odor cues. Parkinson participants scored lower on the screening test (p<.001). The article reports anosmia in 90% versus 54.8% of controls (p=.005). The study contrasts existing olfaction and examines cognition. Cinnamon and orange were presented as test scents; the design does not assign or measure eating either food.
