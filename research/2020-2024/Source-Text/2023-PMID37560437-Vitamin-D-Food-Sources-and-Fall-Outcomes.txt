Original study scope
The EFSA opinion lists egg food groups and vitamin-D-fortified orange juice in separate dietary or bioavailability contexts. Its falls analysis reviews direct vitamin D supplementation, including varied doses and outcome definitions in older adults.

Continuing evidence gaps
Neither the egg intake estimates nor fortified-juice reports provide an egg or orange fall-frequency result in Parkinson disease. Do not assign the supplement comparisons to the foods.
