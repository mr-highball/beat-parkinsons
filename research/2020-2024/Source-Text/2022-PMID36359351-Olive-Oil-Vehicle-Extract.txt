Nadeev and colleagues began with 35 CD-1 mice. The vehicle control arm (n=9) received injected olive oil, and the model arm (n=26) received rotenone dissolved in olive oil. The report then divides the animals among six groups and examines experimental exercise or inhalation conditions. The authors report benefits in some beam and cylinder measurements and no clear survival difference. The experiment assigns neither a cold-pressed oral oil arm nor an oil-free control for estimating the oil's contribution. Outcomes concern locomotion, coordination, histology and survival. The report supplies no anxiety scale or effect estimate for consuming olive oil.

The 2023 congress abstract S9.664 describes the same mouse line, vehicle, toxin and experimental comparisons. Retain the 2022 paper and 2023 presentation as one study family. Source anchors include Methods, animal model and group allocation, and Results, beam and cylinder tests.
