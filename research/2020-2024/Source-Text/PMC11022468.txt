Title: Which Comprehensive Geriatric Assessment (CGA) instruments are currently used in Germany: a survey
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11022468/
PMID: 38627620
PMCID: PMC11022468
DOI: 10.1186/s12877-024-04913-6
Publication date: 2024-04-17
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The investigators survey German geriatric departments about which standardized instruments they use. The unit of observation is the department's assessment practice. No participants are compared on dried fig intake or Parkinson visual hallucinations.
