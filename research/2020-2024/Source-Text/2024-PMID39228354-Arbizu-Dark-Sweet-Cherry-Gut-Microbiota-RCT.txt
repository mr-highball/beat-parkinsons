Title: Effect of dark sweet cherry (Prunus avium) supplementation on the fecal microbiota, metabolic endotoxemia, and intestinal permeability in obese subjects: a single-blind randomized trial.
PMID: 39228354
DOI: 10.1039/d4fo01650e
First published: 2024
Authors: Arbizu S, Mertens-Talcott SU, Talcott S, Noratto GD
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39228354/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (Arbizu et al., 2024, Food Funct; companion cohort to PMID:40077655) in 40 obese adults (19 dark sweet cherry drink + 3 g cherry powder twice daily, 21 placebo) for 30 days. Dark sweet cherry modulated select fecal taxa (higher Roseburia intestinalis, Turicibacter, Bacteroides vulgatus, Clostridium leptum; lower Blautia, Alistipes shahii, Bilophila) without altering lactulose/mannitol intestinal permeability or LBP, and did not evaluate clinical constipation severity or stool frequency. Non-PD obese cohort; gut microbiota shifts do not establish Parkinson constipation benefit.
