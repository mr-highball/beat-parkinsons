Title: Effectiveness of a 6-week specific rehabilitation program combining education and exercises on walking capacity in patients with lumbar spinal stenosis with neurogenic claudication: a randomized controlled clinical trial protocol.
PMID: 36572884
PMCID: PMC9793648

First public date basis: NLM and original journal record.

The randomized lumbar stenosis protocol assigns exercise plus education or education alone. Walking capacity is primary and back and leg pain are secondary planned outcomes. There are no reported results in the protocol and no dried-date intervention.
