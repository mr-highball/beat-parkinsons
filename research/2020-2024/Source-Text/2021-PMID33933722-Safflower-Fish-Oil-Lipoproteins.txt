Title: Changes in lipoprotein particle subclasses, standard lipids, and apolipoproteins after supplementation with n-3 or n-6 PUFAs in abdominal obesity: A randomized double-blind crossover study
Publication date: 2021-04-03 (online first)
PMID: 33933722
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2021.03.040
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33933722/
Original: https://bora.uib.no/bora-xmlui/bitstream/handle/11250/2991546/1-s2.0-S0261561421001874-main.pdf
Text coverage: Distilled author abstract and comparison with the registered protocol and institutional original paper.

The report lists 16 women and 23 men in a double-blind crossover. Two seven-week periods were separated by a nine-week washout. The daily safflower dose supplied 15 g linoleic acid in women and 20 g in men, whereas fish oil supplied 3 or 4 g EPA plus DHA. Compared within and between interventions, the safflower group showed a 4.4% lower total cholesterol and a 4.3% lower total LDL particle concentration. Fish oil produced the larger triglyceride change. Those are biomarker contrasts, without Parkinson symptom outcomes. The congress report's oil quantities and subgroup counts use a different reporting basis. Link T4P140, NCT02647333 and subsequent analyses as one reported trial family.
