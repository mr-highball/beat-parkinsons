Publication: Aucoin et al., EASe-GAD randomized waitlist-controlled pilot. First published online September 24, 2024. PMID 39316026, NCT05573672.
Source: https://opus.lib.uts.edu.au/bitstream/10453/189132/2/1779857.pdf
Coverage: Original study design and anxiety results, factual extract.
Fifty women with generalized anxiety disorder were assigned to a combined diet counseling and omega-3 intervention or waitlist. The original published baseline-adjusted Beck Anxiety Inventory contrast at 12 weeks was −13.72 points (95% CI −18.54 to −8.90). Anxiety was secondary and unblinded. The report supplies neither a toasted-cumin assignment nor a recalled exposure linked to an anxiety result.
