Title: Acute cognitive performance and mood effects of coffeeberry extract in a randomized placebo-controlled crossover
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/15/11/2418
PMID: 37299382
PMCID: PMC10254646
DOI: 10.3390/nu15112418
Publication date: 2023-05-23
Date basis: Nutrients electronic publication date.
Checked: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Original publisher article, Methods, Results sections 3.1–3.2 and Figure 3

In a placebo-controlled crossover in healthy adults, the 100 mg coffeeberry extract gave more mental fatigue on the Cognitive Demand Battery at 60 minutes, compared with placebo (Sidak-adjusted p=.025). The primary 120-minute mental-fatigue comparison was inconclusive, as were the 300 mg dose and the separate Energy and Fatigue Scale. The authors caution about the number of outcomes. A caffeine positive-control beverage was a separate exposure. The isolated coffee-fruit extract did not test regular brewed coffee or Parkinson disease. This response limits a general extrapolation across coffee-derived preparations.
Follow-up: Compare the earlier cited coffeeberry trial across extract doses and measure timing.
