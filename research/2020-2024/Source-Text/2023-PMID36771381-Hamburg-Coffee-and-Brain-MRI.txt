Title: Association between Coffee Consumption and Brain MRI Parameters in the Hamburg City Health Study
Publication date: 2023-01-28
Publication: Nutrients 15(3), 674
PMID: 36771381
PMCID: PMC9919011
DOI: 10.3390/nu15030674
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/15/3/674
Coverage: Original article methods and Table 2, with the conference comparison traced to the same cohort.

Mayer and colleagues analyzed 2,316 participants in the Hamburg City Health Study using food-frequency records for caffeinated coffee and structural MRI at the study visit. In the adjusted analysis relative to less than one daily cup, the 3–4 cup group had lower peak width of skeletonized mean diffusivity (beta −0.591, p=.028) and higher average cortical thickness (beta 0.018, p=.015). White matter hyperintensity load did not differ clearly across the coffee groups in the adjusted model. Only seven participants drank decaffeinated coffee exclusively. No separately reported fatigue severity comparison or analysis restricted to Parkinson disease was supplied.

The later ESOC O036/808 abstract reports the same cohort and outcomes. The conference report does not list its covariates and gives different p-values for the same associations. Combine both reports under a single study family and use the full article estimates for the model described here. No clinical symptom direction is transferred.
