Title: Content-based clustering of hallucinations across sensory modalities in a large online survey
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11452635/
PMID: 39367045
PMCID: PMC11452635
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-69798-2
Publication date: 2024-10-04
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The online study groups respondents by reported hallucination content across senses and derives four clusters. Its survey outcome is a profile of content and experiences. It supplies no Parkinson-specific dried fig exposure or food-timed event count.
