Title: The Effect of DHA Supplementation on Cognition in Patients with Bipolar Disorder: An Exploratory Randomized Control Trial.
PMID: 32155883
PMCID: PMC7146155
DOI: 10.3390/nu12030708
First published: 2020
Authors: Ciappolino V, DelVecchio G, Prunas C, Andreella A, Finos L, Caletti E et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7146155/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized double-blind trial (Ciappolino et al., 2020, Nutrients, PMC7146155, n=31 euthymic bipolar disorder patients) comparing DHA 1250 mg/day against corn oil placebo capsules for 12 weeks. Executive function tests (TMT, BACS, fluency) showed largely null between-group differences except a single emotion-inhibition subtest; corn oil was strictly the placebo capsule.
