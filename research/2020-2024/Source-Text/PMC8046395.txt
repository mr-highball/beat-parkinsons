Title: Effects of medicinal plants and flavonoids on Parkinson's disease: a review on basic and clinical evidences
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8046395/
PMID: 33880344
PMCID: PMC8046395
DOI: 10.34172/apb.2021.026
Publication: 2020-07-01 first published online; 2021 issue
Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article and Tables 3–5.

The review places a direct experimental rigidity score in the section for Nigella sativa and thymoquinone. Its saffron section instead describes saffron extracts, crocin and crocetin in models, including dopamine-cell outcomes and a hanging test. A traditional smooth-muscle relaxation description does not measure Parkinson rigidity. Its turmeric section covers curcumin-related dopamine, strength, inverted-screen time and stride outcomes. Those reports address preparations other than grated turmeric and do not isolate passive muscle rigidity. The review aids original-source discovery and contains no new original human comparison of the frozen preparations.
