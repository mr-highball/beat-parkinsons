Title: Evaluation of the beneficial effects of ETAS® on normal aging or mild cognitive impairment subjects: A pilot randomized controlled trial
DOI: 10.56499/jppres23.1742_12.1.154
First published: 2024
Authors: Mikhailichenko N, Lai T, Wei JC, Takahashi T, Takanari J, Goto K
Journal: Journal of Pharmacy & Pharmacognosy Research
Source: https://doi.org/10.56499/jppres23.1742_12.1.154
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-month randomized, placebo-controlled pilot clinical trial (Mikhailichenko et al., 2024, J Pharm Pharmacogn Res) in 30 older adults with normal aging or mild cognitive impairment allocated to 1,000 mg/day ETAS (standardized Asparagus officinalis stem extract, n=15) or dextrin placebo (n=15). Compared with placebo, the ETAS group showed statistically significant improvements on the Frontal Assessment Battery (FAB, a validated executive function instrument), Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE), and Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS), with a trend toward improvement on the Clock Drawing Test (CDT) and slight blood HSP70 elevation; no adverse events were reported. Limits: small pilot (n=30), Amino Up co-authorship, abstract-level numeric effect sizes/p-values and separate HADS-A vs HADS-D subscale breakdowns not printed in the abstract, non-PD aging/MCI cohort, and concentrated stem extract rather than grilled/roasted/steamed culinary asparagus spears.
