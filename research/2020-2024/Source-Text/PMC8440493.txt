Title: MRI quantitative susceptibility mapping of the substantia nigra as an early biomarker for Lewy body disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8440493/
PMID: 34033185
PMCID: PMC8440493
DOI: 10.1111/jon.12878
Publication date: 2021-05-25
Source depth: PMC original full body from structured article text; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC article metadata and PubMed.
Design: Quantitative susceptibility MRI comparison

The imaging sample comprised 36 probable dementia-with-Lewy-bodies cases, 15 probable mild cognitive impairment with Lewy-body features, 11 idiopathic REM sleep behavior disorder cases and 102 controls. Diagnostic features and MRI susceptibility measurements provide phenotype context. The study does not measure the frozen food or outcome contrast.
