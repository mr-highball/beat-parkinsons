Duffy VB, Hayes JE, Sharafi M. J Agric Food Research 2, 100044 (2020). DOI 10.1016/j.jafr.2020.100044.
The same 97-person study as the archived Brussels sprouts annotation used 4 g frozen-thawed kale and additional vegetables. Participants rated flavor in-mouth with nose blocked and released. They could spit or swallow. There was no assigned roasted-kale exposure, Parkinson sample, or measure of a change in olfaction after consumption. The sensory method therefore cannot estimate an effect on olfactory impairment.
