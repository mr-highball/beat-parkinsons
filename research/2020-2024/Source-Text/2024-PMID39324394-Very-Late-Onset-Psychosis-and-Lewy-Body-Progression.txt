Title: Characteristics associated with progression to probable dementia with Lewy bodies in a cohort with very late-onset psychosis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11496220/
PMID: 39324394
PMCID: PMC11496220
DOI: 10.1017/s0033291724001922
Publication year: 2024
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

Records of 2,204 people diagnosed with very late-onset psychosis are followed for dementia outcomes. Visual hallucinations and other Lewy body features at onset are predictors. The follow-up outcome is dementia, without a dietary exposure or Parkinson episode-rate contrast.

The original compares time to dementia and considers competing risk of death. Symptom ascertainment and the definition of dementia with Lewy body features are material to interpretation.
