Abstract OP-066 on printed page S28 retrospectively analyzes gastric emptying imaging in 276 selected children under five. The younger and older groups receiving orange juice included 11 and 44 children, respectively. The milk groups for those ages included 102 and 99 children. The authors found no difference between meal groups at ages one to three. At ages three to five, they reported a longer half-time and lower emptying percentage for milk than for orange juice.

The groups came from clinical imaging records. The comparison concerns gastric emptying, orange juice and a pediatric population. It supplies no raw-orange bowel or fall-frequency comparison in Parkinson disease.
