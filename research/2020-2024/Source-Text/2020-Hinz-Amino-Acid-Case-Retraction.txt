Retraction of the amino acid medication case report
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7751832/

December 16, 2020. The journal retracted its 2011 Parkinson amino acid case, DOI 10.2147/IJGM.S16621. Its investigation did not obtain requested ethics and consent information, study protocols, raw data or supporting records. The journal also noted competing-interest and verification concerns. This notice and original case belong to one study history.
