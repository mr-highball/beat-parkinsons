Title: Guava with an institutional supplementary meal improves iron status of preschoolers: a cluster-randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 33385184
DOI: 10.1111/nyas.14556
First published: 2021
Authors: Roy Choudhury D, Nair Krishnapillai M, Nagalla B, Vijaya Kankipati R, Ghosh S, Buwade J
Journal: Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33385184/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

140-day 3-arm cluster RCT (CTRI/2014/09/004983, n=399 preschoolers age 24-48 mo across 28 preschools in Telangana, India) assigning 25 g/d guava, banana, or cucumber with institutional meals. No impact was observed on cognitive development or growth across arms; guava improved iron status vs banana and cucumber.
