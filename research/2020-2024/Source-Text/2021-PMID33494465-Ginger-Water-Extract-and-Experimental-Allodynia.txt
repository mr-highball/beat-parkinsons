Title: Ginger rhizome water extract and chemotherapy-related hypersensitivity in mice
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/1420-3049/26/3/548

Publication date: 2021-01-21
Date basis: First publication date in the original publisher record.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full publisher article, methods, results and Figure 2.

The study compares an orally administered ginger rhizome water extract with water in a mouse model of oxaliplatin-associated hypersensitivity. Figure 2 includes 10 controls and seven animals per extract arm. Cold responses improved at the reported 60-minute check. The pressure-response threshold showed a difference from 30 through 60 minutes. Both effects had faded by 90 minutes. The naive-mouse comparison found no clear effect on either response.

The experimental responses concern evoked hypersensitivity. The extracted preparation and chemotherapy model leave fresh grated ginger and pain severity in people with Parkinson disease open. The 2022 follow-up from an overlapping author group uses isolated shogaol and refers back to this publication.
