Title: Sodium Oxybate in Alcohol-Responsive Essential Tremor of Voice: An Open-Label Phase II Study.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10615702/
PMID: 37448353
PMCID: PMC10615702
DOI: 10.1002/mds.29529
Publication date: 2023-07-14
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official complete PMC JATS body.

The open-label trial tests sodium oxybate for alcohol-responsive essential voice tremor. The authors describe a distinct earlier group with laryngeal dystonia and co-occurring dystonic voice tremor. The current experiment does not test lemon balm or Parkinson dystonia.
