Design: Clinical review of central nervous system involvement in myopathies

The review collects CNS manifestations in selected inherited muscle diseases. Neuropsychiatric findings are relevant for differential diagnosis. It does not test exposure or visual episode frequency in a Parkinson clinical sample.
