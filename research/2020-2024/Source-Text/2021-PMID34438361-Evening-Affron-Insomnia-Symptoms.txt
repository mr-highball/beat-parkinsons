Title: Evening standardized saffron extract and insomnia symptoms in adults with poor sleep
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34438361/
PMID: 34438361
DOI: 10.1016/j.sleep.2021.08.001
First published online: 2021-08-11
Coverage: Factual extract from the original investigators' indexed abstract. Companion registration ACTRN12620000973910.

In a separate 28-day, three-arm trial, 120 adults with unsatisfactory sleep were randomly assigned to evening standardized saffron extract (14 or 28 mg) or placebo. Self-rated sleep quality from the Pittsburgh Sleep Diary was the primary endpoint. The abstract reports higher diary sleep quality and favorable secondary Insomnia Symptom Questionnaire totals and classifications for extract. It reports no clear differences in several other sleep measures and supplies no numerical ISQ contrasts for the individual arms. Diagnosis of Parkinson disease was not the target. The extract and the 2019-registered two-arm Affron trial are distinct study records. The 2020 registry entry and this 2021 article represent the same study.
