Coverage: Methods and Table 2, factual extract.

In a double-blind randomized trial, 110 patients with migraine were assigned to dried ginger extract tablets or starch placebo in addition to propranolol, with 51 and 52 completing respectively. The Beck Anxiety Inventory means at baseline were 17.4 and 17.5. At 90 days the means were 17.2 and 17.1 (reported p = .84). The report used anxiety as a measured covariate for migraine. The source does not give an isolated effect in a Parkinson population or test grated ginger.
