Title: Snacking on Whole Almonds for Six Weeks Increases Heart Rate Variability during Mental Stress in Healthy Adults: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 32575387
PMCID: PMC7353422
DOI: 10.3390/nu12061828
Registry ID: NCT02907684
First published: 2020
Authors: Dikariyanto, Smith, Chowienczyk, Berry, Hall
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32575387/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

6-week parallel RCT (ATTIS study, NCT02907684; companion cardiometabolic report PMID 32412597, n=107: 51 whole roasted almonds 20% energy vs 56 control muffins in adults aged 30-70 y at above-average CVD risk). During acute mental stress (Stroop color-word test, n=105), high-frequency HRV power reflecting parasympathetic cardiac regulation increased with whole roasted almonds vs control (mean difference +124 ms^2, 95% CI 11 to 237, p = 0.031); companion report showed improved FMD (+4.1%) and LDL-C (-0.25 mmol/L) and higher nighttime VLF-HRV (+337 ms^2) with no between-group blood pressure difference. Limits: physiological autonomic HRV proxy rather than clinical anxiety or orthostatic symptom scale.
