Original study scope
The original contrasts carpal tunnel cases and comparison participants on vitamin D, calcium-related measures and reported symptoms. Anxiety and fatigue are measured in that clinical setting. Sardine consumption and raw grape consumption were not tested to explain those outcomes.

Continuing evidence gaps
Any frozen ingredient effect requires a separately assigned or traceable culinary exposure and a matched symptom estimate.
