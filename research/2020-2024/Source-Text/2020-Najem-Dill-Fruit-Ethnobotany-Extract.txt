Najem et al., 2020. Overview of some toxic plants for medicinal use in the central Middle Atlas, Morocco.

Survey entries describe dill fruit, voucher 2041/IS/N°38, used orally for gastric complaints. The right-hand table fields are literature synthesis. The dill row credits Ouahdi 2010 for toxicity and convulsions associated with prolonged use. A narrative passage lists hallucinations across several plants without assigning that outcome to the dill row. No identified original report in this search verifies a visual hallucination event or rate after consuming fresh dill foliage.
