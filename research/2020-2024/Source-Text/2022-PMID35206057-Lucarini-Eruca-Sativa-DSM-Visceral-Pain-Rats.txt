Title: Beneficial Effects of Eruca sativa Defatted Seed Meal on Visceral Pain and Intestinal Damage Resulting from Colitis in Rats.
PMID: 35206057
PMCID: PMC8870774
DOI: 10.3390/foods11040580
First published: 2022
Authors: Lucarini E, Micheli L, Pagnotta E, Matteo R, Parisio C, Toti A, et al.
Journal: Foods (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35206057/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Blinded controlled preclinical visceral pain study (Lucarini et al., 2022, Foods, PMC8870774; 6 rats/group, researcher blinded to treatment) testing oral Eruca sativa defatted seed meal (DSM, 121.00 +/- 2.00 umol/g glucoerucin and 7.00 +/- 0.30 umol/g glucoraphanin) at 0.3-1 g/kg acutely and repeatedly over 14 days in DNBS-induced colitis rats. Oral E. sativa DSM significantly reduced visceral hyperalgesia (abdominal withdrawal reflex to colorectal distension, p<0.01 across 1-3 mL distension volumes) via H2S-dependent Kv7 channel activation and lowered colon damage, mast cell infiltration, and enteric glial GFAP reactivity without sedative confound. Limits: non-PD rat colitis visceral pain model using seed meal rather than raw arugula leaves.
