Verified depth: original PDF, all 347 labeled pages S1–S347, with 741 heading boundaries including section headings. Mapped member text inspected in column order. Three figures for a relevant poster remain unverified.

Printed S284, right column, contains conference poster 212, a retrospective 111-person medical cannabis series. The authors describe an olive-oil extract and another oral form. The abstract reports aggregate response and pain ratings without an olive-oil-only control, separate estimates by formulation, or individual Parkinson endpoints. The title, its investigator group and pain-session placement match the official program. The paper supplies context about an olive-oil preparation vehicle alongside active cannabis exposure. It does not attribute symptom effects to culinary olive oil.
