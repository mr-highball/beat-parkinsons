Title: Chemical variability of Ceylon cinnamon wood-bark oil

Review depth: Author abstract and PMC identity metadata. The retrieved XML lacked a body.

Chemical analyses characterized Cinnamomum verum leaf and wood-bark oils from two Brazilian settings. The abstract reports 14.0% linalool in the Santa Inês bark-oil chemotype and predominant cinnamaldehyde at 89.3% in the São Luís bark-oil chemotype. These values describe oil composition. The tested antioxidant and antifungal assays concern a different endpoint.

Round 1 claim 0243. This identifies a plausible constituent link for some bark oil preparations. It provides no linalool enantiomer or ingestion measurement in ordinary ground spice, no anxiety measure and no dose match to the high-linalool Taiwanese leaf oil or isolated oral linalool mouse study.
