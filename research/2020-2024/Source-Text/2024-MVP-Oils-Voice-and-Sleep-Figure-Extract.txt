Coverage: Factual original baseline figure extract.

The investigator report's duplicate baseline PD-only heatmaps show a small inverse association between unspecified olive-oil frequency and self-reported anxiety. They also show a very small inverse coconut-oil and dyskinesia association, a near-zero positive turkey and broad speech association, and a near-zero inverse generic spice and insomnia association. The source gives no numerical result with pairwise uncertainty, adjustment, individual spice, cooking method, oil grade or an intelligibility-specific speech measure. The report's positive canned fruit and falls association applies to its own pooled exposure.
