Title: Open-label Ksheerbala procedures with or without concurrent levodopa
Source: https://medicaljournals.stmjournals.in/index.php/RRJoASYN/article/view/2525
Publication date: 2021-11-01
Date basis: Publisher article metadata, volume 8 issue 3.
Review depth: Publisher author abstract and public reference list. Native PDF is subscription-restricted.

Thirty people described as Kampavata or Parkinson disease were assigned to two open-label groups. Both underwent Shirobasti and Nasya with Ksheerbala Taila for 14 days. One group continued fixed-dose levodopa and the other did not receive it. The reported follow-up extends to 45 days. The abstract lists favorable changes in different modified-UPDRS categories, including Kampa in the group without levodopa. It does not give exact item estimates, variance, allocation procedures, diagnostic detail or safety results.

The article describes a shared multi-component procedure. The between-group contrast changes medication status and does not isolate the oil or procedures. Daily off time was not identified as an outcome. No food comparison or advice about changing medication follows from the report. Related 2018 authorship and intervention do not establish whether participants overlap. Obtain the full methods, tables and exact trial registration before asserting a separate cohort.
