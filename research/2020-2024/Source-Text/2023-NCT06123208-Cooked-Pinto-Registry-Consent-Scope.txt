Cooked pinto meal crossover and metabolic room consent
November 8, 2023 · NCT06123208
https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06123208
Factual extract from official current registration and original consent pp. 1–2.

Casperson's randomized crossover compares a cooked whole-pinto meal, a pinto-flour meal and a shrimp control. Rice, tomatoes and spices are shared meal components. Whole beans are described as cooked; the cultivar and boiling method are not specified. The registered endpoint is substrate use after the meal. The completed record lists 36 actually enrolled and no posted results. The September 2024 consent projects about 28 and gives meal, appetite-rating and metabolic-room procedures. It instructs participants to remain awake for a short measurement period, with no sleep scale or insomnia endpoint. The original consent was posted in 2025 at https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/08/NCT06123208/ICF_000.pdf. The trial first posted in 2023.
