Title: Different effect of hypo- and hypermetabolism on cognition in dementia with Lewy bodies: are they coupled or independent?
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10764327/
PMID: 38172188
PMCID: PMC10764327
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-023-00622-w
Publication date: 2024-01-03
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The study compares cerebral hypo- and hypermetabolic patterns and their relationships to baseline cognitive performance in 55 participants with dementia with Lewy bodies. The underlying scans and cognitive scores do not give a dried fig comparison or clinical visual episode incidence.
