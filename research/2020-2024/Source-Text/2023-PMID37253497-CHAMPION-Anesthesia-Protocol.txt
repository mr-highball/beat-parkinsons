Title: CHAMPION protocol for anesthesia during Parkinson deep brain stimulation
PMID: 37253497
PMCID: PMC10255000
DOI: 10.1136/bmjopen-2023-071726
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10255000/
First publication: 2023-05-30
Coverage: Original NCBI PMC JATS article body checked and summarized.

The registered noninferiority trial NCT05550714 plans general anesthesia versus conscious sedation during bilateral subthalamic stimulation surgery. The primary endpoint is the fraction with high-quality microelectrode recordings, defined by normalized root mean square greater than 2.0. The protocol specifies complications and later motor measures including UPDRS III. It supplies no dried-date comparison. The 2026 principal report is a later publication from the same trial, rather than a second cohort.
