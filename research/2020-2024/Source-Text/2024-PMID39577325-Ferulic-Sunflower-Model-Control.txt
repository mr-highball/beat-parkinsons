Title: Ferulic acid mitigated rotenone toxicity-evoked Parkinson in rat model by featuring apoptosis, oxidative stress, and neuroinflammation signaling

Twenty-four male rats were included. The study reports an injected sunflower oil control and motor and cerebellar outcomes in a rotenone model with or without ferulic acid. The worse motor performance concerned the toxin contrast. The comparison gives no separate executive outcome, oral sunflower administration or identification of a cold-pressed grade.
