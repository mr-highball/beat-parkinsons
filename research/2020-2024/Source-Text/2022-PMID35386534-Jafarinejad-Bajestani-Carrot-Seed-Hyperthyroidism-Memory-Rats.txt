Title: The Effects of Seed Extract of Carrot on Memory, Nerve Conduction Velocity, and Serum Thyroxin in Rats.
PMID: 35386534
PMCID: PMC8977616
DOI: 10.4103/abr.abr_178_20
First published: 2022
Authors: Jafarinejad Bajestani M, Hadjdzadeh MA, Yousefi M, Hosseini M, Yousefvand S
Journal: Adv Biomed Res
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35386534/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical Wistar rat study (n=24) testing carrot seed extract (200 and 400 mg/kg) reporting elevated serum T4 (moderate hyperthyroidism), unchanged nerve conduction velocity, and significantly IMPAIRED spatial and passive avoidance memory (p<0.05). Plant-part caveat (seed extract) and directly opposite cognitive direction to Vasudevan 2006.
