Title: The effect of curcumin-piperine supplementation on lipid profile, glycemic index, inflammation, and blood pressure in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus and hypertriglyceridemia.
PMID: 39165011
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.8304
First published: 2024
Authors: Hosseini H, Bagherniya M, Sahebkar A, Iraj B, Majeed M, Askari G
Journal: Phytotherapy research : PTR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39165011/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

12-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT in n=72 patients with T2DM and hypertriglyceridemia receiving 500 mg curcuminoids + 5 mg piperine vs placebo. Reported improved energy/fatigue subscale (p=0.024) but NO between-group difference on DASS-21 anxiety/depression/stress items or blood pressure (p>0.05). Bundled with curcuminoids; cannot isolate black pepper/piperine.
