Review depth: Full publisher article, methods, results and Figure 2.

Figure 2 uses six animals in each of three groups in a chemotherapy-associated hypersensitivity model. The higher-dose isolated shogaol group showed lower cold and mechanical hypersensitivity at the reported early follow-up through three hours. The lower-dose group had no clear difference. The study compares injected isolated shogaol with its carrier.

The result concerns evoked responses in a mouse model. It supplies no estimate for eating grated ginger or for Parkinson pain. The paper cites the 2021 ginger water-extract experiment from an overlapping author group. Figure 2 concerns a different preparation and comparison. The two papers share this experimental line and should not be counted as separate human confirmations.
