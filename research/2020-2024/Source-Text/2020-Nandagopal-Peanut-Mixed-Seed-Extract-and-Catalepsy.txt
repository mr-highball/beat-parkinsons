Title: Neuroprotective effect of polyherbal formulation in Parkinson's animal model
DOI: 10.22159/ajpcr.2020.v13i3.36549
Publication year: 2020
Citation: Nandagopal and Khan. Asian Journal of Pharmaceutical and Clinical Research 13(3), 121–125.
Source: https://www.journals.innovareacademics.in/index.php/ajpcr/article/download/36549/21843
Text coverage: Original paper, exposure groups, behavioral methods and Figures 1–3.

The experiment assigned 30 rats to five groups. The oral intervention combined methanolic seed extracts from almond, peanut and watermelon. Compared with the chlorpromazine-only group, the combined extract produced lower block catalepsy scores and greater actophotometer activity. The study also measured tissue biomarkers. The authors mention resting tremor in the introduction. The experimental behavioral outcomes shown in Figures 1 and 2 are catalepsy and general activity. Peanut oil was not administered. No single-seed extract arm or separately reported resting-tremor outcome was identified.
