Title: Blueberry Supplementation in Midlife for Dementia Risk Reduction.
PMID: 35458181
PMCID: PMC9031005
DOI: 10.3390/nu14081619
Registry ID: NCT02751866
First published: 2022
Authors: Krikorian R, Skelton MR, Summer SS, Shidler MD, Sullivan PG
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9031005/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (NCT02751866) in 33 enrolled (27 completed) overweight insulin-resistant middle-aged adults (50-65 y) with subjective cognitive decline taking daily whole blueberry powder vs placebo. Blueberry improved executive lexical access (phonemic COWA F(1,24)=10.67, p=0.003), reduced memory intrusion/interference errors (p=0.04), reduced daily encoding difficulty (p=0.03), and lowered fasting insulin (p=0.04); BDI depression scores did not differ (F(1,24)=0.39, p=0.53). Limits: non-PD midlife SCD sample; freeze-dried powder.
