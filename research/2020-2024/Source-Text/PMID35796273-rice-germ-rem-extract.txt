Yoon, Byun and Shin compared 75 mg/day of a natural GABA product derived from unpolished rice germ with placebo for four weeks. REM percentage shifted 16.9 to 16.6 in the active arm and 18.9 to 20.5 in controls, interaction p=.9173. The interaction for periodic limb movements was p=.9442. Those endpoints do not quantify dream enactment or REM without atonia.
