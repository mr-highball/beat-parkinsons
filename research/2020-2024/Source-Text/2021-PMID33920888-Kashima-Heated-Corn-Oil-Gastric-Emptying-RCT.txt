Title: Heated Corn Oil and 2,4-Decadienal Suppress Gastric Emptying and Energy Intake in Humans.
PMID: 33920888
PMCID: PMC8071276
DOI: 10.3390/nu13041304
First published: 2021
Authors: Kashima H, Honma A, Kamimura S, Nishimura S, Sano T, Matsumoto S et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8071276/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Human crossover trials (Kashima et al., 2021, PMC8071276, n=10 and n=17 healthy adults) comparing thermally oxidized/heated corn oil (and its degradation aldehyde 2,4-decadienal) against unheated refined corn oil. Heated corn oil significantly slowed gastric emptying and suppressed subsequent energy intake compared with non-heated corn oil (p<0.05). Mechanistic gastric-emptying context relevant to levodopa transit, but contrasts thermally degraded vs unheated corn oil rather than refined corn oil vs an oil-free control in Parkinson disease.
