Zhang and colleagues, primary indexed report

Original source https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.29208

First online September 7, 2022. The cross-sectional study included 71,640 adults free of diagnosed Parkinson disease. The authors considered questionnaire-defined probable REM sleep behavior disorder alongside four other features. The adjusted odds ratio for at least two versus no features comparing extreme modified Alternate Healthy Eating Index quartiles was 0.64 (95% CI 0.49–0.85). The abstract names daytime sleepiness and constipation as individually associated with the score. It does not provide a raw carrot contrast or a count of dream-enactment episodes. The original supplement and item-level questionnaire data remain separate follow-through.
