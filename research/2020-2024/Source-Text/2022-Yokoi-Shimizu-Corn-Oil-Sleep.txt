Yokoi-Shimizu, Yanagimoto and Hayamizu. DHA and EPA supplementation and sleep quality in healthy adults. Nutrients. 2022 Oct 5;14(19):4136.
DOI 10.3390/nu14194136. PMID 36235788. PMCID PMC9573173.
Original article: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/14/19/4136
Scope: Factual treatment and sleep outcome summary from the complete journal article.

The double-blind, parallel 12-week trial randomized 66 generally healthy adults who reported poor sleep, with 33 per arm. Corn oil capsules served as comparator for DHA/EPA-containing fish oil. The report calls the active fish oil refined. That designation does not identify how the corn oil was produced. Mat-based sleep results at week 12 covered 32 people per arm.

Table 6 reports sleep efficiency (%; mean ± SD) from baseline to week 12 of 95.6 ± 2.9 to 96.0 ± 2.6 in the fish oil arm and 96.3 ± 3.1 to 93.7 ± 9.4 in the corn oil arm. The endpoint comparison was p=.018. The corn oil group had larger endpoint variability. Sleep latency had p=.062 and actual sleep time p=.468 at week 12. Subjective initiation and maintenance of sleep in Table 4 had an endpoint p=.351. This comparison cannot distinguish a fish oil effect from a corn oil effect and does not measure insomnia severity in Parkinson disease.
