Title: The protective effect of biochanin A against rotenone-induced neurotoxicity in mice involves enhancing of PI3K/Akt/mTOR signaling and beclin-1 production.
PMID: 32977283
DOI: 10.1016/j.ecoenv.2020.111344
First published: 2020
Authors: El-Sherbeeny NA, Soliman N, Youssef AM, Abd El-Fadeal NM, El-Abaseri TB, Hashish AA et al.
Journal: Ecotoxicol Environ Saf
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32977283/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse rotenone Parkinson model (El-Sherbeeny et al., 2020, Life Sci) testing isolated biochanin A (10-40 mg/kg). Biochanin A improved motor performance, striatal dopamine, and antioxidant/anti-apoptotic markers. Critical limits: purified compound rather than whole cooked/canned/roasted chickpeas; preclinical mouse toxin model.
