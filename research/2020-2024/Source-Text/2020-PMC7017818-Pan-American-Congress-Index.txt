The contents DOI presents presentation numbers, titles and authors. It is distinct from the companion articles with the same short title. The original text for entries 43, 142, 143 and 156 was inspected through the companion DOI 10.1002/mdc3.12905, PMCID PMC7017830, pages S13–S111.

Entry 43 compares externally cued balance training, training without cues, and a general education group in the same 154-person trial as entry 42. Its freezing outcome belongs to that training trial. Entry 142 tests a combination of phenolic acids in a toxin-induced rodent model, with locomotor and biochemical measures. Entry 143 analyzes genotype against a freezing item score in humans. Entry 156 lists a coauthor given name Melissa and tests nilotinib. These separate reports provide no fresh lemon balm comparison for freezing episodes.
