Group definitions from the repository
G1 mature control; G2 mature supplemented; G3 aged control; G4 aged supplemented.
Fresh basil leaf homogenate was incorporated into standard chow. The resulting chow pellets were oven-dried before administration. The mice were female C57BL/6.
