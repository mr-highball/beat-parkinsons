Title: Impact of combined plant extracts on long COVID: An exploratory randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 39488240
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2024.103107
First published: 2024
Authors: Lukkunaprasit T, Satapornpong P, Kulchanawichien P, Prawang A, Limprasert C, Saingam W
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39488240/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

7-day double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (TCTR20230131004, n=66 long COVID adults) of a 5-plant extract (Citrus aurantifolia, Tiliacora triandra, Cannabis sativa, Alpinia galanga, Piper nigrum; 4,500 mg/d) vs placebo. Lowered moderate-to-severe fatigue (RR 0.25, 95% CI 0.08-0.81). Bundled 5-plant extract including Cannabis; cannot attribute to black pepper.
