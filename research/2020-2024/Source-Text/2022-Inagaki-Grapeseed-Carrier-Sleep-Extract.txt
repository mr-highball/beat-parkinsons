Inagaki T and colleagues. Showa University Journal of Medical Sciences 34(2):95–101, first released June 28, 2022. DOI 10.15369/sujms.34.95.
Original full report, Methods and Figure 5. Random allocation of 42 nurses to rose essential oil diluted to 1% in grapeseed oil (n=20) or grapeseed oil only (n=22) in a single 15-minute neck and shoulder massage after a night shift. Brand Neal's Yard Remedies and plant Vitis vinifera reported. Sleep-difficulty change on PSQI-J was −0.60 ± 0.1 versus −0.23 ± 0.1 (mean ± SE, p < .05). Evaluation next morning used a prior-month scale. The contrast tested addition of rose with oil and massage in both groups. No oil-free group, food exposure, refining method, Parkinson population or separate dream behavior measurement was reported.
