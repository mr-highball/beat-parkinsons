Title: Patient-Reported Symptoms in the Global Multiple System Atrophy Registry
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9547130/
PMID: 36247899
PMCID: PMC9547130
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13544
Publication year: 2022
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete official PMC HTML.

The Global Multiple System Atrophy Registry survey records patient-reported symptoms, including a proportion who answered that they saw things that were absent. It examines association with levodopa history and discusses diagnostic uncertainty. It does not track fig consumption or episode counts.

People with a history of multiple system atrophy participated through the registry. A reported diagnosis and online symptom answer are different from a clinically observed rate of hallucinations. The authors note that hallucinations can prompt reconsideration of the diagnosis.

The parallel symptom questions record drooling in 172 participants (47%) and difficulty swallowing in 226 (62%) among 365 survey respondents. The responses are from a registry of multiple system atrophy. They describe perceived symptom presence without a graded Parkinson drooling or raw-fig comparison.
