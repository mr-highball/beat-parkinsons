MIND meals with extra virgin olive oil and planned attention measures
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06437860
Registration: NCT06437860
Coverage: Official structured registry protocol

First posted May 31, 2024. The NURISH protocol compares meals following a MIND dietary pattern with healthy American-pattern meals in middle-aged adults. The MIND plan includes extra-virgin olive oil. Registered attention outcomes include flanker accuracy, reaction time and event-related P3 measures. Multiple ingredients change together. The protocol gives no isolated refined olive oil intervention, Parkinson cohort or posted results.
