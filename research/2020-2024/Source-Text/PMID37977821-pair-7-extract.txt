Pair 7 of 18 was inhaled tarragon versus thyme. Aldrich odorants were diluted in heavy mineral oil and were presented at the olfactometer sampling port. The rewarded odor was assigned randomly per mouse. Following acquisition, the rewarded and unrewarded identities were reversed. The threshold for completion was 85% correct in the task. The pair-specific result compares performance between genotypes on both odors. The reported long-term retest used pair 18, isoamyl propionate versus linalool, so it did not give tarragon retention.

Raw-data reconstruction follows the data handling in code/3xTg-AD-serial-reversal.qmd. Map each animal to genotype using data/geno clean.csv. Read data/csvs for nonexcluded manual blocks and data/auto for automated entries. Use OP 7 or REV 7, follow the author's correction of animal ID 1297 to 1927, and count errors as valid positive and negative trials minus hits and correct rejections. The author's spreadsheet treats SS trials as invalid and leaves their correctness missing. The 33 animals provided both pair 7 phases. Every automated pair 7 row identified tarragon, while the manually logged pair number is confirmed by Table 2 and data/odour_pairs.csv.
