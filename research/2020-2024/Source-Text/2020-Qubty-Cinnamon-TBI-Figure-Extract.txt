Title: Orally Administered Cinnamon Extract Attenuates Cognitive and Neuronal Deficits Following Traumatic Brain Injury
Authors: Doaa Qubty and colleagues
Identifiers: PMID 32901372, DOI 10.1007/s12031-020-01688-4
First published: 2020-09-08; journal issue January 2021.
Original source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12031-020-01688-4

Access: Publisher preview has public abstract and original figures. Full body and Methods tables remain subscription-gated. Factual extract from original report and displayed figures.

Oral cinnamon extract was provided before and after experimental traumatic brain injury in mice. Figure 1 places elevated plus maze among the behavioral tests. Figure 2a displays open-arm time for control (10), TBI (11), cinnamon (10), and TBI+cinnamon (12). Figure 2d also displays open-arm time, with a different sample count sequence (10, 9, 9, 9); separate protocol details for those panels could not be reconciled from the preview. The abstract reports no significant between-group anxiety difference. The source gives indirect anxiety evidence without an estimate for Parkinson disease or ground cinnamon.
