Title: Bixin Attenuates Experimental Autoimmune Encephalomyelitis by Suppressing TXNIP/NLRP3 Inflammasome Activity and Activating NRF2 Signaling.
PMID: 33362775
PMCID: PMC7756000
DOI: 10.3389/fimmu.2020.593368
First published: 2020
Authors: Yu, Wu, Li, Deng, Liu
Journal: Frontiers in immunology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33362775/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled C57BL/6 mouse experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis (EAE) study administering intragastric bixin. Bixin attenuated EAE neurological clinical severity, CNS demyelination, microglial activation, and pro-inflammatory cytokines via NRF2 activation and TXNIP/NLRP3 inflammasome suppression. Limits: autoimmune demyelinating MS model rather than PD motor symptoms.
