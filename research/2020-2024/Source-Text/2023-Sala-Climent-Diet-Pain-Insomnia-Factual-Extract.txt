Publication: Sala-Climent et al., Frontiers in Nutrition, July 13, 2023. DOI 10.3389/fnut.2023.1205526, PMID 37521415, PMC10381948.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10381948/
Coverage: Original Methods and pain and insomnia results. Supplement identity checked separately.
Forty-five people with rheumatic chronic pain received dietary advice and were offered a powder containing turmeric and black pepper. Pain improved in 30 participants. Of those 30, 27 reported improved insomnia scores, compared with 5 of the 15 with worse pain. The study had no control arm. Figure 3 labels the exploratory turmeric-score association with pain at p < .10. A provider-supplied supplement instead belongs to the Du et al. kidney-disease sarcopenia study DOI 10.3389/fmed.2023.1188971. The intended supplement and food-specific causal effects remain unverified.
