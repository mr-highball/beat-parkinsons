Title: Corn oil and Soybean oil effect as vehicles on behavioral and oxidative stress profiles in developmentally exposed offspring mice.
PMID: 38615729
DOI: 10.1016/j.physbeh.2024.114548
First published: 2024
Authors: Berroug L, Essaidi O, Laaroussi M, Malqui H, Anarghou H, Bellali F et al.
Journal: Physiol Behav
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38615729/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical developmental toxicology vehicle study (Berroug et al., 2024, Behav Brain Res) administering daily oral gavage of corn oil or soybean oil versus water to pregnant/lactating dams and offspring mice. Corn oil gavage vehicle was associated with lower offspring locomotion, higher anxiety-like indices, and elevated brain oxidative markers relative to water. Important preclinical gavage-vehicle methodological caution, but non-Parkinson developmental rodent gavage rather than adult culinary refined corn oil consumption.
