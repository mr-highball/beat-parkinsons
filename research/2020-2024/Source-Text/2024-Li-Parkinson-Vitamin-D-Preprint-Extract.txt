Publication: Li et al., Research Square preprint, first posted December 16, 2024.
Source: https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-5145248/v1
Coverage: Original preprint identity, study design and clinical outcome scope.

The registered clinical comparison (NCT06539260) enrolled people with Parkinson disease and randomly assigned 30 with low vitamin D to isolated vitamin D3 or placebo. The 2025 journal version describes the same trial cohort. Depression and anxiety measures appear in the baseline analysis. Separate arm-level treatment results for those outcomes were unavailable in this original public report.
