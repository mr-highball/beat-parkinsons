The CIRCUS crossover trial planned to compare fruit and vegetable types in people with prehypertension. The protocol reports 39 randomized. The registry gives actual enrollment of 36. The protocol and registration describe the same trial family and use different reporting units. Preserve both reported denominators.

The planned outcomes include Stroop, numerical reasoning and verbal reasoning tasks. This publication reports baselines. The ClinicalTrials.gov record was completed, last updated March 22, 2022, and had no posted results when checked November 27, 2026. The publication and registration give no isolated roasted-carrot estimate or Parkinson executive-function result. Round 1 claim 0325 remains open.

Carrots were part of the low-produce comparator and the usual-produce test condition. The protocol allowed raw, mixed cooked, juice and soup options.
