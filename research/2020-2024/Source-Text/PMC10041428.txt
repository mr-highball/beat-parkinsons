The clinical comparison includes Parkinson disease with cognitive impairment, Alzheimer disease and frontotemporal dementia. It relates Neuropsychiatric Inventory outcomes to caregiver distress and other clinical variables. Those domain ratings are separate from counted visual episodes after eating dried figs.
