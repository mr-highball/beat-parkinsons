Title: Bioactivity-directed isolation, characterization, and quantification of an anxiolytic flavonoid from Brassica oleracea L.
PMID: 33660867
DOI: 10.1111/jfbc.13608
First published: 2021
Authors: Kaur D, Shri R, Kamboj A
Journal: Journal of food biochemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33660867/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Bioactivity-directed fractionation of hydroalcoholic Brassica oleracea (broccoli floret) extract in mice across Elevated Plus Maze, hole-board, and mirror chamber models. Both broccoli floret extract and isolated kaempferol-3-O-beta-D-glucoside (quantified at 0.061% w/w in florets by HPTLC) demonstrated significant anxiolytic-like activity. Limits: preclinical mouse anxiety assays using extract/constituent rather than human PD raw broccoli.
