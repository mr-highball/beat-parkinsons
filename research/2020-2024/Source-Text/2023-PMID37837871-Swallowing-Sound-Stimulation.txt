Title: Swallowing sound index after neck stimulation in Parkinson disease
PMID: 37837871
DOI: 10.1016/j.jns.2023.120831
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37837871/
First publication: 2023-10-10 online, November issue.
Study family: jRCTs062220013.
Coverage: Original journal abstract and linked official registry. Full article and its underlying figure values are open.

The analysis includes 25 participants in the single-arm, eight-week cervical interferential stimulation study. The swallowing sound index from electronic stethoscope analysis remained unchanged during follow-up. The baseline index was lower than in healthy controls and higher than in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. The authors report associations with normal EAT-10 status and C-reactive protein and no correlation with the other measured indicators, including videofluoroscopic findings. Those analyses are distinct from the cohort's provoked cough and water-bolus measures. No fig intervention or drooling-severity result is reported by this abstract.
