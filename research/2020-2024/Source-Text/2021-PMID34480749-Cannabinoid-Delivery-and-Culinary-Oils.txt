Original study scope
The pain-focused formulation review discusses named cooking oils as carriers. The 12-person healthy-volunteer crossover compared drug absorption across preparations including sesame oil, measuring exposure of the drug. It does not isolate sesame pain relief or the other frozen oil-symptom pairs. Date is a time expression.

Continuing evidence gaps
Use original pharmacokinetic comparators for carrier effects. A clinical oil-only comparison with matching preparation and Parkinson endpoint remains open.
