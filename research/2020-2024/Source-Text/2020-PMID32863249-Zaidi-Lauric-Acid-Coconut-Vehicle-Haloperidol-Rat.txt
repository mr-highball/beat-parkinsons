Title: Lauric acid: Its role in behavioral modulation, neuro-inflammatory and oxidative stress markers in haloperidol induced Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 32863249
First published: 2020
Authors: Zaidi AA, Khan MA, Shahreyar ZA, Ahmed H
Journal: Pakistan journal of pharmaceutical sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32863249/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rat study (Zaidi et al., 2020, Pak J Pharm Sci) in haloperidol-induced parkinsonian rats testing isolated lauric acid (0.66 and 1.32 mg/kg for 35 days) vs levodopa (30 mg/kg) and vehicle coconut oil (1 mL/kg, group D). Isolated lauric acid attenuated striatal oxidative stress, TNF-alpha/NF-kappaB/IL-8, and decline on rotarod and beam-walking tests relative to diseased controls, while whole coconut oil served only as the vehicle arm without isolated favorable motor reporting vs diseased control. Limits: acute haloperidol catalepsy/parkinsonism rat model testing isolated lauric acid rather than culinary refined or virgin coconut oil.
