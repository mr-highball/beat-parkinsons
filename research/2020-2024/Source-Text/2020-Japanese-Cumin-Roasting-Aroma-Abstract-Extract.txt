Title: Dry roasted cumin retains cuminaldehyde in the odor fraction
Source: https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1390853649779015296
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

Japanese original article abstract, March 9, 2020, Bulletin of Jissen Women's University Faculty of Human Life Sciences 57:9–14, CiNii CRID 1390853649779015296. The authors analyzed cumin using gas chromatography and odor detection. Cuminaldehyde contributed approximately 70% of odor-active components in the unheated comparison and around 80% in profiles of samples dry roasted or cooked in oil. These are relative odor-fraction proportions, with no estimate of whole-food intake, total retained mass, absorption or clinical response.
