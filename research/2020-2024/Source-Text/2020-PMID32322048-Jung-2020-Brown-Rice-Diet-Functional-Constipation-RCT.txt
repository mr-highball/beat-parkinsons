Title: Effects of rice-based and wheat-based diets on bowel movements in young Korean women with functional constipation.
PMID: 32322048
PMCID: PMC7606135
DOI: 10.1038/s41430-020-0636-1
Registry ID: NCT01933100
First published: 2020
Authors: Jung SJ, Oh MR, Park SH, Chae SW
Journal: European journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7606135/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Four-week open-label, parallel-group randomized controlled trial (NCT01933100) in n=39 randomized (n=35 analyzed) young Korean women (mean age 21.8 y) with functional constipation assigned to cooked brown rice-based diet (BRD, n=13), white rice-based diet (WRD, n=13 randomized/11 analyzed), or wheat-based diet (WD, n=13/11). Total colonic transit time (TCTT) decreased significantly with BRD (42.9 +/- 26.0 to 26.4 +/- 17.9 h, within p=0.032; change -16.5 +/- 8.1 h) compared with WRD (35.4 to 42.2 h, change +6.8 +/- 2.1 h; between-group p=0.028). Weekly stool frequency rose within BRD (3.4 +/- 1.1 to 5.0 +/- 1.1, p=0.002; between-group p=0.520). Limits: open-label diet, young non-PD women.
