Title: Association of Visual Impairment With Neuropsychiatric Symptoms of Dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11830047/
PMID: 39148160
PMCID: PMC11830047
DOI: 10.1097/wno.0000000000002235
Publication date: 2024-08-16
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The analysis covers 624 participants with dementia and distinguishes reported visual impairment from measured near acuity. Neuropsychiatric outcomes are questionnaire measures and not meal-linked observations. There is no dried fig exposure or Parkinson-specific treatment effect in this analysis.
