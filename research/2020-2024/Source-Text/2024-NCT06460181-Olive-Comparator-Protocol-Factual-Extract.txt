Olive oil control in a registered attention and exercise study
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06460181
Registration: NCT06460181
Coverage: Official protocol fields and posting date

First posted June 14, 2024. Healthy recreationally active women were assigned astaxanthin in an olive-oil softgel or an olive-oil placebo for four weeks. The registered cognitive outcomes include psychomotor vigilance, task switching and flanker performance. Both arms contain oil with no isolated refined-oil comparison or Parkinson subgroup.
