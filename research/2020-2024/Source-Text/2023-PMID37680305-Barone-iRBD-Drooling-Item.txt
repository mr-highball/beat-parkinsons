Title: Neurologic and psychiatric features of impending neurodegeneration in iRBD
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10480303/
PMID: 37680305
PMCID: PMC10480303
DOI: 10.1016/j.prdoa.2023.100216
Publication date: 2023-08-25
Date basis: electronic publication in original JATS
Source depth: factual extract of original results and Supplementary Data 1 item aggregates

The study enrolled 65 people with isolated REM sleep behavior disorder at baseline. Thirty-three completed the study assessments and were analyzed, including eight who developed one of several neurodegenerative diagnoses. The paper reports a group difference at the first follow-up in the MDS-UPDRS Part II saliva and drooling item (p=0.013 before and p=0.027 after adjustment).

The original workbook, which matches the JATS supplement checksum, labels the baseline and follow-up drooling columns M and Z on the MDS2 sheet. Nonblank item entries give baseline means of 0.286 for seven people in the group that developed neurodegeneration and 0.087 for 23 in the other group. Follow-up item means are 0.75 from four entries and 0 from nine entries, respectively. These are available-cell descriptive means. The article does not give the exact analytic denominator for the adjusted drooling test in its main results. The reduced item coverage and mixed diagnoses limit interpretation.

The study compares clinical evolution without testing raw fig or other food exposure.
