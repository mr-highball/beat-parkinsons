The retrospective RECOVER cohort included 560,752 adults, with 57,616 positive and 503,136 negative SARS-CoV-2 tests. Recorded abdominal pain showed the largest absolute excess in the Delta-period comparison at 85.3 additional patients per 1,000. The same excess was not identified for the ancestral-period comparison. Figure 3 reports the excess burden under the paper's follow-up definition. Abdominal pain is incident health-record coding in this design. Clinical pain severity, Parkinson symptom response and dietary date exposure are outside the comparison.
