PP0004 studies sage and other herbal extracts in a parietal cell model. PP0058 records coffee intake in a hiatal-hernia cohort, while PP0083 compares dietary advice that includes caffeine reduction. PP0100 uses eggs within a multi-food elimination diet. PP0260 uses reconstituted egg white in a procedure. PP0195 describes ingested foreign bodies, including chicken bones. PP0358 studies a grape-seed extract in intestinal cell experiments. PP0457 describes onion among avoided foods in Crohn disease.

In PP0462, 220 participants with IBS or functional dyspepsia answered a food-trigger survey. Figure and text distinguish self-reported gastrointestinal symptoms from intervention results. The survey mentions lentils, cauliflower, onion, watermelon, grapes and a grouped cabbage, kale and fennel item. PP0366 mentions barley in background about gluten and compares clinical celiac groups. PP1280 uses orange in the name of a laboratory reagent. Date matches concern timing and administrative dates.

PP1101 is the TANDIM randomized diet trial. PP1110 compares dietary patterns in diverticular disease. Both mention olive oil in a broader pattern. The full TANDIM article gives a separately measured but inconclusive constipation comparison, and an improvement in anxiety for the whole diet. The same-cohort post hoc analysis did not identify a food-specific anxiety association.
