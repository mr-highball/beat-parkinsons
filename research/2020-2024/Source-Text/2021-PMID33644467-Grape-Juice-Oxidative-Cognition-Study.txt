Both groups undertook aquatic exercise and one also received grape juice. Table 3 does not show a clear group-by-time interaction on the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (p=.23). Table 4 reports a group-by-time interaction for a blood protein oxidation marker. This is distinct from an isolated cognitive, anxiety, or motor symptom effect of the juice. Preserve the reported uncertainty and internal differences in descriptive and table entries.

The 2021 Results say 24 included and 18 completed, while Table 2 lists nine exercise-only and ten exercise-plus-juice participants. Its baseline means for age and UPDRS motor score exactly match those in the 2020 report. Both publications cite RBR-2c4xxb. They are linked reports, with analysis-set differences, product composition and individual participant overlap still to be reconciled. These discrepancies preclude counting them as independent confirmation.
