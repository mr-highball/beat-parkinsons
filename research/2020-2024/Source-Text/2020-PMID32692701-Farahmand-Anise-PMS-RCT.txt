Title: Could Anise decrease the intensity of premenstrual syndrome symptoms in comparison to placebo? A double-blind randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 32692701
DOI: 10.1515/jcim-2019-0077
First published: 2020
Authors: Farahmand M, Khalili D, Ramezani Tehrani F, Amin G, Negarandeh R
Journal: Journal of complementary & integrative medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32692701/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled RCT in n=67 college students (18-35 y) with premenstrual syndrome randomized to 110 mg tid (330 mg/day) Pimpinella anisum capsules vs starch placebo for 10 days per cycle across two consecutive menstrual cycles. GEE analysis reported reductions in total Premenstrual Symptoms Screening Tool (PSST) score to 13.9 (p < 0.001) after cycle 1 and 9.8 (p < 0.001) after cycle 2 (note abstract prints reversed CI signs '16.5,-11.4' and '12.4,-7.3'). Composite PMS scale does not isolate anxiety, depression, or Parkinson's symptoms.
