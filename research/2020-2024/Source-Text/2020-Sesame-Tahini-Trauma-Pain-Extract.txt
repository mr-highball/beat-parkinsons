2020 topical oil from tahini

Gholami and colleagues studied oil extracted from tahini in acute traumatic limb pain. The article describes tahini produced using roasted sesame, but the tested product was applied topically with massage. Both groups received matched care including massage. The original states 120 initially included and 90 analyzed (49 sesame, 41 control). Thirty were lost or excluded, including exclusions for NSAID use. It reports greater pain improvement after 48 hours with tahini-derived oil. The study's experimental exposure has the closest verified ingredient processing in this trail, with a route and pain population different from the frozen claim. The trial identifier printed by the report is IRCT20171017036838N1. The repository PDF gives the July 2020 issue. Original full text · NLM record.
