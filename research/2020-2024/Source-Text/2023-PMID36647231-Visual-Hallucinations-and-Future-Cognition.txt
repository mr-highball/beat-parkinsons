Title: Risk of Cognitive Impairment in Patients With Parkinson's Disease With Visual Hallucinations and Subjective Cognitive Complaints.
PMID: 36647231
PMCID: PMC10329922
DOI: 10.3988/jcn.2022.0186
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10329922/
First publication date: 2023-01-02 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

A prospective Parkinson analysis evaluates baseline visual hallucinations and subjective cognitive complaints as predictors of later cognition. The NMSS perception domain records frequency and severity over the prior four weeks. The modeled outcome is cognitive impairment during follow-up and the study does not compare dried fig intake.
