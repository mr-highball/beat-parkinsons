Coverage: factual extract of Sections 3.5–3.6, Figure 2, Appendix B and Annex B, prepared 2026-11-14

Seventy-nine adults with Parkinson disease were enrolled in two outpatient centers in Recife. The investigators administered the 98-item food-frequency questionnaire and a 24-hour food recall. Mango appears as a standalone fruit item in Annex B (printed page 100). Daily mango intake was reported by 31.3% of women in Results and Figure 2 (printed page 52). The questionnaire separates fruit juice. The mango item does not collect cultivar, culinary preparation, serving temperature or time before bed.

For Parkinson staging and severity, the Methods use Hoehn–Yahr and explicitly only domains II and III of the original 42-item UPDRS. The full instrument in Appendix B includes a part IV other-complication item 41 covering insomnia or hypersomnolence, with a pooled yes/no response. This lies outside the domains the Methods say were applied. Some movement-quality anchors within the appendix use fatigue or decrement during motor testing, without rating clinical fatigue as a separate endpoint in the results. No public mango-specific analysis estimates a sleep or fatigue outcome. The linked 2022 report is based on this dissertation.
