Title: Treatment of Parkinson's disease in Iranian ancient medicine.
PMID: 37064313
PMCID: PMC10090798
DOI: 10.1002/hsr2.1192
First published: 2023
Authors: Nobari HJ, Rashedi J
Journal: Health science reports
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37064313/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Historical/ethnomedical review of Parkinson's disease ('Ra'sha') in Iranian ancient medicine describing a multi-herb powder containing Rosa damascena (100 g), Amomum cardamom (100 g), Cinnamomum iners (100 g), Myrtus communis (100 g), and Centaurea calcitrapa (100 g). Historical multi-herb formulation naming Amomum rather than monotherapy culinary Elettaria cardamomum.
