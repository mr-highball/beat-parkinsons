Source: https://research.chalmers.se/publication/523784/file/523784_Fulltext.pdf
DOI: 10.1111/jtxs.12598
Methods summary. Five healthy adults provided chewing and bolus information after texture-modified foods. The recipes include rapeseed oil. Some rheological measurements used saliva substitute. The study measures bolus material properties and oral processing, with neither an isolated rapeseed contrast nor clinical dysphagia severity.
