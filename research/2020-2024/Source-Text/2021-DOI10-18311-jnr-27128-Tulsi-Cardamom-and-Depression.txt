The full randomized report gives post-treatment Geriatric Depression Scale mean ranks 11.98 for the aqueous tulsi–cardamom blend and 29.03 for green tea, 20 older adults per arm. U=29.5 and Z=−4.634, reported p<.001. The product contained both plants, and the report estimates a post-treatment rank contrast rather than a between-group change. It supplies no isolated ground cardamom or Parkinson depression estimate.

Reported preparation combined ground grocery cardamom and tulsi powder in boiled water, then filtered the mixture. It used an active green-tea-extract comparator matched for color. Random allocation is stated. The sequence method, allocation concealment and independent ingredient effects are not established by the report. There were no reported dropouts. The authors describe 11 participants without depressive symptoms and nine with mild symptoms after the blend. The comparator had fourteen with mild and six with severe symptoms after treatment.
The blend and oil animal report are distinct. Oral essential-oil work measures forced swim behavior while also changing open-field locomotion. The clinical Crohn trial gave cardamom to both arms while varying wormwood.
