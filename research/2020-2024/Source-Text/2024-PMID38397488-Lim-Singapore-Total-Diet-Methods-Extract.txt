The methods paper discusses acrylamide concentrations of 404 ppb in black pepper, 478 in roasted potato, 377 in roasted sweet potato, 363 in stir-fried pea and 349 in potato chips/crackers. These are food concentrations, not doses, symptom changes or verified values for every consumer preparation.

Reference 40 is the 2023 Singapore acrylamide study, DOI 10.3390/foods12163022. The Korean reference 69 concerns heterocyclic amines; it must not be presented as the primary acrylamide source. The linked Singapore papers share a total-diet program.

The original 2023 supplement Table S16 reports black pepper at 403.8 micrograms/kg with no cooking, explaining the rounded 404 ppb here. Its 226 people are survey eaters, not analytical replicates. Whole/ground form and prior processing remain unknown. No Parkinson symptom, pain, dysphagia or insomnia is measured; no clinical direction follows from this concentration ranking alone.
