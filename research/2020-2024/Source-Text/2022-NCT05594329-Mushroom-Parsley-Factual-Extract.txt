Parsley in a mushroom meal protocol with finger tapping
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05594329
Registration: NCT05594329
Coverage: Official structured registry protocol

First posted October 26, 2022. The acute older-adult crossover registers mushroom meals and cognitive tasks including simple and complex finger tapping. The recipe descriptions list parsley as a background ingredient. There is no separately assigned parsley arm, confirmed fresh preparation comparison or Parkinson subgroup. The retrieved official record has no posted results.
