Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11600120/
Coverage: Original Methods and gastrointestinal outcomes, Figure 4, summarized as facts.
The trial assigned participants to meals using several cooked bean types, peas or rice. Pinto beans were among the bean choices. The bean group more often reported increased stool frequency at week one and softer stools at weeks one and three than did the rice group. Bloating was also more often reported at week one. Outcomes pooled all bean types and give no pinto-alone or Parkinson constipation severity estimate.
