Title: A Comprehensive Review of the Pharmacology, Chemistry, Traditional Uses and Quality Control of Star Anise (Illicium verum Hook. F.): An Aromatic Medicinal Plant.
PMID: 37959797
PMCID: PMC10648513
DOI: 10.3390/molecules28217378
First published: 2023
Authors: Zou Q, Huang Y, Zhang W, Lu C, Yuan J
Journal: Molecules (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37959797/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Comprehensive review (Zou et al., 2023, Molecules, PMC10648513) cataloging 201 identified constituents of Illicium verum Hook. f., traditional warming/analgesic/culinary uses, quality control against adulterants, and preclinical pharmacology of star anise oil and shikimic acid. Confirms absence of human clinical trials of oral culinary star anise for Parkinson's motor, cognitive, affective, sleep, or autonomic symptoms.
