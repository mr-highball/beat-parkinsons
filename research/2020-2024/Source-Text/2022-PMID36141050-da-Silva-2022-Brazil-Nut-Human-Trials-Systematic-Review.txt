Title: Effects of Regular Brazil Nut (Bertholletia excelsa H.B.K.) Consumption on Health: A Systematic Review of Clinical Trials.
PMID: 36141050
PMCID: PMC9498495
DOI: 10.3390/foods11182925
First published: 2022
Authors: da Silva A, Silveira BKS, de Freitas BVM, Hermsdorff HHM, Bressan J
Journal: Foods (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36141050/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review (PMC9498495) of 24 human clinical studies (9 RCTs, 15 non-randomized/before-after) of Brazil nut consumption published 2008-2022: confirmed selenium status/GPx increases and highlighted Cardoso 2016 as the sole human cognitive trial (MCI), while noting adverse/pro-oxidant effects when Se doses exceeded upper limits in obesity trials and zero Parkinson disease trials.
