Valid paired Children's Sleep Habits Questionnaire measures were available in the first treatment period for 131 participants, including 45 assigned placebo, 42 assigned purified cannabinoids and 44 assigned whole-plant extract. Mean total scores changed by −1.4, −2.9 and −2.3 points, respectively, with p = 0.63. The first-period comparisons for questionnaire totals and subscales were inconclusive. The report also describes 107 participants with valid paired measures for both periods.

This is a companion analysis of the Aran autism trial (PMID 33536055). The original methods and registry establish that olive oil was shared across comparator and active arms. The questionnaire findings describe the cannabinoid comparisons. Oil grade, an oil-free comparison and a Parkinson insomnia outcome are absent. Claim 0897 retains an open rating.
