Title: Nootropic Effects of C. melo and C. lanatus seed extracts.
PMID: 33376745
PMCID: PMC7746445
DOI: 10.1155/2020/8823038
First published: 2020
Authors: Wahid S, Alqahtani A, Khan RA
Journal: Biomed Res Int
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33376745/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse study testing ethanol extract of Cucumis melo seeds (50, 100, 200 mg/kg) on Morris water maze, stationary rod, and passive avoidance vs diazepam and imipramine. Reported memory-enhancing effects in healthy mice. Plant-part caveat: evaluated C. melo seeds (non-flesh part), NOT raw edible cantaloupe flesh, and did not model Parkinson disease.
