Title: Bixa orellana ethyl acetate fraction and its isolated compound ellagic acid attenuate the progression of MIA-induced osteoarthritis in rat knees.
PMID: 37839112
DOI: 10.1016/j.biopha.2023.115644
First published: 2023
Authors: Santiago, Ataíde, de Araújo Morais, da Silva Lima, Dos Santos Martins
Journal: Biomedicine & pharmacotherapy = Biomedecine & pharmacotherapie
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37839112/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

28-day controlled rat monosodium iodoacetate (MIA) knee osteoarthritis study testing oral ethyl acetate fraction of Bixa orellana leaves (50, 100 mg/kg/d) and isolated ellagic acid (50 mg/kg/d). Both reduced joint inflammation, clinical joint discomfort, and allodynia comparably to indomethacin while improving radiological/histopathological cartilage preservation. Limits: leaf fraction (different plant part from seed), rat OA model.
