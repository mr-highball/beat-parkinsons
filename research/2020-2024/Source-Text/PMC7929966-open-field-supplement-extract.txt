Coverage: Factual extraction of the open-field tests and fecal-bolus results, with parent article diet composition.

The aged control feed used sunflower oil as its fat basis. The alternate diets included 6% whole walnuts and one group had enriched housing. Diets differed in several nutrients, and the sunflower oil processing grade was unavailable. In Supplementary Table 9, mean open-field fecal boli were 3.0 ± 0.5 in aged sunflower controls, 3.2 ± 0.5 with walnuts, 2.7 ± 0.6 with walnuts and enriched housing, and 2.7 ± 0.4 in young sunflower controls (SEM). The overall F=0.2151 and p=.8856; adjusted comparison p-values for the aged control versus walnut and walnut-plus-enrichment groups were .9836 and .9543. Supplementary Table 1D gives omnibus p=.3047, .3641, .9713 and .2002 for distance, speed, turn rate and stop fraction, respectively. The parent text refers to S3 for droppings, which appears in Table 9 of the deposited file. These animal proxies estimate neither Parkinson anxiety severity nor an isolated sunflower-oil effect.
