Original study scope
The investigators gavaged sodium benzoate in a methylcellulose vehicle in normal and A53T mice and measured tyrosine hydroxylase, dopamine and open-field activity. Cinnamon is the cited precursor rather than the exposure administered in these comparisons.

Continuing evidence gaps
The locomotor assays do not measure passive rigidity or dyskinesia. Figure labels do not identify fig exposure, and the motor study contains no sage intervention. The linked erratum concerns images in Figure 5C.
