Title: Oral nitrate supplementation to enhance pulmonary rehabilitation in COPD: ON-EPIC a multicentre, double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomised parallel group study.
PMID: 32376732
DOI: 10.1136/thoraxjnl-2019-214278
First published: 2020
Authors: Pavitt MJ, Tanner RJ, Lewis A, Buttery S, Mehta B, Jefford H, et al.
Journal: Thorax
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32376732/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Multicenter UK double-blind placebo-controlled parallel RCT (ON-EPIC; 165 COPD patients randomized, 122 completed analysis) administering 140 mL nitrate-rich vs nitrate-depleted beetroot juice prior to pulmonary rehabilitation sessions: active BRJ significantly increased incremental shuttle walk distance by +30 m (95% CI 10 to 40 m; p=0.027) and reduced systolic BP without serious adverse events. Limits: COPD rather than PD; concentrated juice.
