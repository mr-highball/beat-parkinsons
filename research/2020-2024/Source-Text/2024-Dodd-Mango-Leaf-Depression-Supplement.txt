The study compared a single 300 mg mango leaf extract capsule with matched placebo in generally healthy adults. Depression–Dejection was a Profile of Mood States subscale measured while studying acute cognitive task effects.

For the separate depression-dejection analysis, Supplementary File 4 reports intervention F=0.72, p=.398 and intervention-by-time F=0.31, p=.731. Post-dose mean score (SE) at 300 minutes was 43.68 (0.36, n=112) after leaf extract and 44.22 (0.36, n=113) after placebo. Both groups had similar pre-dose means (43.95 and 44.05, respectively). Measurements of short-term mood in the enrolled population do not quantify Parkinson depressive symptoms or test dried mango fruit.
