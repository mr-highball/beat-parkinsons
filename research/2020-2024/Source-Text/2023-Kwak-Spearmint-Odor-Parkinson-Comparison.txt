Spearmint odor recognition in comparative parkinsonism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10846967/
Supplement: https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC10846967.1/jmd-23105-Supplementary-Table-4.pdf
Coverage: Factual summary of the primary report and visually verified Supplementary Table 4.

First online October 6, 2023. Kwak and colleagues enrolled 78 people with Parkinson disease and 31 with drug-induced parkinsonism. The disease groups differed in age. Supplementary Table 4 reports correct spearmint identification of 53.8% in the Parkinson group and 76.9% in the drug-induced group (chi-square p = .038, Holm-adjusted p = .721). The table gives group headers of 78 and 27, but 76.9% cannot be obtained from a whole-number numerator out of 27. It gives no item-level counts or explanation and does not specify age adjustment for the item. Spearmint was a scent in the identification test. The study provides no brewed intake, no oil or leaf preparation and no therapeutic change after exposure.
