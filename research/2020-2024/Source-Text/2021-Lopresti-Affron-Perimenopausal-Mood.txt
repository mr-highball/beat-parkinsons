Title: The Effects of a Saffron Extract (affron) on Menopausal Symptoms in Women during Perimenopause
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8408316/
PMID: 34463070
PMCID: PMC8408316
DOI: 10.6118/jmm.21002

First online 2021-06-02. August 2021 issue. DOI 10.6118/jmm.21002. Matching registration ACTRN12620000350921. Product supplied by Pharmactive Biotech Products and extracted from Crocus sativus stigma. Table 2, Figure 2 and the Results text distinguish the total score interaction, percentage-change test and post-hoc depression comparison. Seventy-six completed all study requirements; 82 provided at least the week-four data used in self-report analysis. One participant in the active group withdrew with mild digestive complaints and one in placebo with nausea. The original registry excludes neurological disease including Parkinson’s.

Lopresti and Smith randomized 86 perimenopausal women to 14 mg affron twice daily or matched placebo for 12 weeks. The standardized stigma extract contained more than 3.5% Lepticrosalides by the reported specification. The analysis included 82 participants. The registered total Greene Climacteric Scale group-by-time comparison gave p=.078, and the separate baseline-to-week-12 percentage-change comparison gave p=.027. The psychological subscale group-by-time p value was .032. A post-hoc depression subscore comparison found reductions of 32% and 9%, p=.028. The extract preparation, population and scale differ from the frozen Parkinson claim.

In the SF-36 Results, the extract arm's energy/fatigue score improved over time (within-arm p=.001). Between-group changes were inconclusive for every SF-36 subscale. The cited 2017 study that gave ginger and cinnamon used a four-herb combination in a separate postmenopausal cohort (PMID 28546803).
Fatigue follow-up, 2026-11-25. The primary SF-36 table shows the energy/fatigue group mean increasing from 40.73 to 49.63 with extract and from 40.00 to 44.39 with placebo. The between-group trajectory is inconclusive (p=.521, reported d=.22). The nominal within-extract p=.001 cannot establish an attributable improvement. The preparation is a standardized extract and participants did not have Parkinson disease. The source supplies non-Parkinson comparator context for claim 0485.
