Original study scope
A Parkinson cohort analyzed early health outcomes by reported military service history. Treatment, clinical and functional outcomes reflect group histories.

Continuing evidence gaps
The exposure is military service. It does not provide an attributable change in Parkinson pain or fall frequency following sage or dates.
