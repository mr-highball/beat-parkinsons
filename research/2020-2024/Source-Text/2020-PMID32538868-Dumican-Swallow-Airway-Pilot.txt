Title: Predicting Airway Invasion Using Screening Tools and Laryngeal Kinematics in People with Parkinson's Disease: A Pilot Study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7458512/
PMID: 32538868
PMCID: PMC7458512
DOI: 10.3233/JPD-202044
Publication date: 2020-06-06
Source depth: Original JATS, methods, outcome table and publisher date
Date basis: Publisher first published online June 6. PMC also records a July 28 publication flag for the issue version.
Design: Observational diagnostic pilot involving 14 people with Parkinson disease and self-reported swallowing symptoms

The investigators evaluated 135 swallows. Forty percent of observed swallows met their abnormal penetration-aspiration scale threshold. A salivation question was one of the screening possibilities during enrollment. No count of enrolled people who endorsed that question is reported in the analyzed outcomes. The regression compares swallowing questionnaires and instrumental variables against airway invasion. The measured event is a swallow rather than a patient-level drooling episode. The article supplies no graded or separately estimated effect for drooling and no raw-fig exposure.
