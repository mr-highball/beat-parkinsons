Kefir powder and registered physical fatigue in healthy adults
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000056148
Registration: UMIN000049551
Coverage: Official UMIN study design, posting history and outcome fields

First disclosed November 18, 2022. Seventy-five healthy adults were planned for 12 weeks of 1.0 g or 0.4 g kefir powder or placebo. Physical fatigue visual analog rating is secondary and sleep quality is primary. The record updated November 21, 2025 lists the results as unpublished. Registered powder and the healthy population limit directness for plain liquid kefir and Parkinson fatigue.
