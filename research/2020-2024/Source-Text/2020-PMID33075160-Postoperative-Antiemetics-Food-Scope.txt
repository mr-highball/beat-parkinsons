The review measures postoperative nausea and vomiting and separately summarizes pharmacologic adverse effects. Phillips 1993 had a ginger arm outside the comparison extracted for the network. Gulhas 2003 is listed as awaiting classification. The review assigns the constipation and extrapyramidal summaries to drug comparisons. The ginger leads concern perioperative nausea alone. Parkinson motor symptoms, pain and anxiety remain separate outcomes.

Soybean oil appears in an allergy eligibility clause. The original text does not assign it for dystonia, bradykinesia or dyskinesia. The occurrence of grape and orange terms supplies no dietary anxiety or constipation intervention. Read each review member independently when following a candidate exposure to its actual outcomes.
