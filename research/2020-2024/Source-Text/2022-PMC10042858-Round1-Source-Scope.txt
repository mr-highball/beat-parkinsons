Claim 0118 | dried basil | fall frequency | related. The two-week rotenone mouse experiment used isolated intraperitoneal ursolic acid, a constituent named in basil leaves. It improved rotarod fall latency and open-field movement. The study did not include edible basil or count spontaneous falls.
Claim 0128 | fresh rosemary | depressive symptom severity | related. The two-week rotenone mouse experiment used intraperitoneal isolated ursolic acid at 10 mg/kg and found less forced-swim immobility in the cotreatment group. Rosemary leaves are named as a natural source. The study did not administer dried rosemary, and the behavioral proxy was not a Parkinson depression assessment.
