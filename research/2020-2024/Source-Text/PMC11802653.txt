Title: Examining Agreement in Psychotic Symptom Assessment: Insights from Parkinson's Disease Dementia Dyads
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11802653/
PMID: 39382196
PMCID: PMC11802653
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.14225
Publication date: 2024-10-09
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML publication; local research digest

The study compares Parkinson dementia patient and caregiver ratings in 21 dyads. Agreement for the SAPS-PD summary score is ICC 0.464, with poor agreement on six of nine items. The source is directly relevant to whose report a symptom scale captures, but neither reporter supplies a food-timed dried fig comparison.

For the most reliable response, the clinician selected the patient in 71.4% of dyads after considering available information. This is a measurement and reporting comparison.
