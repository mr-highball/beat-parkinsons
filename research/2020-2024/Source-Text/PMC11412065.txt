Title: Bálint syndrome in dementia with lewy bodies: A new phenotypic variant with progression implications?
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11412065/
PMID: 39309256
PMCID: PMC11412065
DOI: 10.1016/j.neurop.2024.100171
Publication date: 2024-07-31
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The clinical report evaluates Bálint syndrome and whether this visuospatial phenotype relates to disease progression in dementia with Lewy bodies. Recurrent visual hallucinations are part of the clinical context. Visuospatial test failures do not count food-timed visual events.
