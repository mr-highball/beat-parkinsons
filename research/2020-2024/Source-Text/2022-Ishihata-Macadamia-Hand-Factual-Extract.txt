Title: Aromatherapy in a cervical spondylotic myelopathy case
Source: Ishihata, The Journal of Holistic Sciences 16(2), 35–52. Issued October 10, 2022. J-STAGE release June 20, 2025.
DOI: 10.69380/rahos.16.2_35
Original: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/rahos/16/2/16_35/_pdf
Coverage: English factual extract from the original Japanese report, with methods and grip/subjective outcomes. No open reuse license verified.

One participant with cervical spondylotic myelopathy received eight weekly treatments. The carrier for a topical essential oil blend was macadamia nut oil. During the regimen, the right-hand grip was 13.8 kg at the first measurement. Mean grip was 17.8 kg in the first week and 27.3 kg in the eighth week. The subjective right-hand sensation measure combined difficulty with fine tasks, numbness and difficulty gripping together with other sensations. The article does not give a separate fine-dexterity score. The treatment included manual techniques and essential oils. The case has no comparator or carrier-only arm. The authors do not specify an oil pressing method, ingestion or a Parkinson diagnosis.
