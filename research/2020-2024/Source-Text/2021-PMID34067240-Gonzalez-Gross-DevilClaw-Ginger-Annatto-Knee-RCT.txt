Title: Analysis of Effectiveness of a Supplement Combining Harpagophytum procumbens, Zingiber officinale and Bixa orellana in Healthy Recreational Runners with Self-Reported Knee Pain: A Pilot, Randomized, Triple-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial.
PMID: 34067240
PMCID: PMC8196851
DOI: 10.3390/ijerph18115538
First published: 2021
Authors: González-Gross, Quesada-González, Rueda, Sillero-Quintana, Issaly
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34067240/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

4-week pilot randomized triple-blind placebo-controlled trial in 28 middle-aged male recreational runners (13 intervention, 15 placebo) with self-reported knee discomfort taking 2 g/d of a 3-herb mixture combining Harpagophytum procumbens, Zingiber officinale, and Bixa orellana. Knee thermograms showed deeper temperature reduction (p < 0.05) and leg fat mass decreased (p = 0.037), with no safety differences. Limits: three-herb blend without isolated annatto arm; thermography rather than standalone VAS pain contrast.
