The review summarizes work on the chemical composition and pharmacology of Zanthoxylum volatile oils. Section 3.1 cites Bian and colleagues' 2019 mouse study and characterizes dry frying as increasing the reported analgesic findings. The available primary author abstract describes enhanced effects after processing as a trend and documents significance for the volatile-oil groups versus the blank control. The review does not supply a direct raw-versus-dry-fried statistical comparison or the underlying table.

This is a secondary account of the same Bian experiment. It does not establish an independent observation, the missing original botanical voucher, or a pain outcome after culinary toasted pepper in Parkinson disease.
