Combined perilla and ginger extracts in functional dyspepsia
2020. March issue; Crossref records April 2020 and supplies no online date.
https://www.mednews.care/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/effectiveness-of-a-nutraceutcal-supplement-containing-highly-standardized-perilla-and-ginger-extracts-in-patients-with-functional-dyspepsia.pdf

Di Pierro and colleagues described 65 treated adults with functional dyspepsia. Seven stopped after the first week without a response and were omitted from the final 58-person single-group analysis. Enteric tablets provided 375 mg ginger extract and 150 mg perilla extract per tablet, taken twice daily for two weeks. Table II combined diarrhea and constipation in one score. The mean was 4.7 (SD 1.4) at baseline and 1.5 (SD 1.3) at week two, p<.01 versus baseline. The text reports 38 with constipation and 15 with diarrhea, with no separate effect estimate for either subgroup. The retrospective series had no comparison group and no separately assigned ginger exposure.
