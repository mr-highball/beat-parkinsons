Title: Evaluation of the anti-cholelithiasis activity of vinegar-soaked Ficus carica fruit in adults
Source: https://www.pjps.pk/uploads/2025/01/1736487684.pdf
PMID: 39799455
Publication date: 2024-11
Date basis: First indexed issue, November–December 2024. The publisher-hosted PDF path is dated later.
Citation: Bukhari M et al. Pakistan Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences 37(6):1411–1419.
Coverage: Factual summary from the full published report and Tables 3–4.

The 50-person randomized comparison used vinegar-soaked fig fruit and ursodiol in adults with gallstones. The investigators recorded pain, bloating and digestive discomfort independently of ultrasound. Table 4 gives one aggregate adverse event in the fig arm and four in the active-drug arm, 25 participants each, without naming a tremor event or using a Parkinson assessment. The clinical context and preparation differ from raw fig and Parkinson disease.
