Title: Brazil Nut (Bertholletia excelsa H.B.K) Retards Gastric Emptying and Modulates Enteric Glial Cells in a Dose-Dependent Manner.
PMID: 33301378
DOI: 10.1080/07315724.2020.1852981
First published: 2022
Authors: Almeida PP, Thomasi BBM, Costa NDS, Valdetaro L, Pereira AD, Gomes ALT, et al.
Journal: Journal of the American Nutrition Association
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33301378/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled rat study (n=27 male Wistar rats; n=9/group) fed standard diet vs 5% or 10% wt/wt Brazil nut for 8 weeks: 10% Brazil nut significantly increased gastric residue (retarded gastric emptying; p=0.0008), reduced colonic GFAP immunoreactivity (p=0.04), and reduced myenteric ganglia area (p=0.0155), whereas 5% increased colonic GFAP (p=0.0016); abstract did not report significant worsening of constipation transit. Limits: healthy rodents, high 10% dietary fraction.
