Title: Monoamine Oxidase-B Inhibitors for the Treatment of Parkinson's Disease: Past, Present, and Future
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8925102/
PMID: 34957948
PMCID: PMC8925102
DOI: 10.3233/JPD-212976
Publication date: 2021-12-21
Date basis: first public pre-press date in original publisher JATS; issue release 2022-02-15
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The review compares motor outcomes and adverse-event rates under monoamine oxidase-B inhibition, including visual hallucination reports with adjunctive drug use. It does not record dried fig consumption or food-timed visual episodes.
