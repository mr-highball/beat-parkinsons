Title: A comprehensive study of extracted pectin from Ficus carica fruit
Source: https://doi.org/10.31838/ecb/2022.11.04.006
Original PDF: https://epa.oszk.hu/02200/02286/00097/pdf/EPA02286_chemical_bulletin_2022_04_041-049.pdf
DOI: 10.31838/ecb/2022.11.04.006
Publication year: 2022
Citation: Kuthuru and Annammadevi. European Chemical Bulletin 11(4), 41-49.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Text coverage: Full original. Acute toxicity methods and Tables 4-6.

The study characterized pectin from common fig fruit and describes oral safety screens in healthy rats. The acute surveillance checklist names tremor among possible toxicity signs. Table 4 reports no overt toxicity for the listed groups, without separate tremor event counts, severity, or a standardized rest-tremor assessment. The article describes the tested material alternately as pectin and alcoholic fruit extract and prints different dose units in the methods and Table 4. Its small groups and inconsistent preparation and dose reporting limit interpretation. This is safety surveillance rather than a treatment test, and supplies no efficacy direction for raw fig and Parkinson rest tremor.
