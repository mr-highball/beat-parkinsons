Title: Oral Dysbiosis and Inflammation in Parkinson’s Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8150470/
PMID: 33646178
PMCID: PMC8150470
DOI: 10.3233/JPD-202459
Publication date: 2021
Coverage: Paraphrased original PMC JATS methods, results and Table 1.

The cross-sectional study examined 20 people with Parkinson disease and 20 controls. Table 1 reports median Sialorrhea Clinical Scale scores of 1.5 versus 0 and median paraffin-stimulated flow of 1.6 versus 6 mL/min, both p<.001. The group had higher symptom scores alongside lower measured flow. The two outcomes were obtained differently, and the sample was predominantly early-to-middle stage. Paraffin was a saliva-collection stimulus rather than a dietary treatment. The microbiome association and clinical contrast give no response to fig intake.
