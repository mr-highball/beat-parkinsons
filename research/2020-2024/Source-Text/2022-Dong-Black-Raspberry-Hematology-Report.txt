Black raspberry hematology article and reported tolerability
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9271408/

June 30, 2022. Dong and colleagues identify NCT03140280 and report 15 participants in a paired before-and-after analysis of blood and epigenetic measures after three months of black raspberry powder. The article describes no adverse events during treatment. It does not give counts for depression, insomnia or confusion. The later official results use an 18-person adverse-event denominator and a 52-week assessment period. The available texts do not establish a reconciled reporting window or clinical attribution.
