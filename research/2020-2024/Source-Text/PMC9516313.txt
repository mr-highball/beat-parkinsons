In the published article, there was an error in Table 2 as published. Subheading rows of the table, indicating the name of each criteria set, were not shown in the proper format. Additionally, a subheading row showing the title of each clinical category, including nfaPPA, svPPA, and lvPPA, under “Gorno-Tempini PPA criteria” was missing. The corrected Table 2 and its caption appear below.
