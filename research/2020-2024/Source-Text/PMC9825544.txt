Title: Psychiatric symptoms of frontotemporal dementia and subcortical (co-)pathology burden: new insights
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9825544/
PMID: 35136978
PMCID: PMC9825544
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awac043
Publication date: 2022-02-08
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

Postmortem pathological findings and clinical neuropsychiatric symptoms are compared across frontotemporal lobar degeneration categories. The pathologic phenotype includes behavioral measures instead of a dried fig exposure or longitudinal Parkinson visual endpoint.
