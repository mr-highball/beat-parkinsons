Original study scope
Table 1 of this opioid-withdrawal review lists oral corn oil as the carrier for CBD in several trials. Its Parkinson crossover member compares CBD with placebo during a public-speaking challenge and assesses anxiety and tremor. Those results are tied to the CBD contrast. The review's constipation, fatigue, mood and pain contexts do not give corn-oil-specific effects. Publication was online in 2021 and in the journal issue in 2022.

Continuing evidence gaps
The exact documents provide no randomized corn oil comparison or specification of refined culinary corn oil. Keep the Parkinson challenge member separate from other diagnoses and candidate withdrawal endpoints.
