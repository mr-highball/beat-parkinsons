Abstract 4 follows 26 pimavanserin recipients, including 21 with Parkinson disease, using neuropsychiatric and quality-of-life measures. Abstract 36 compares 3-day dietary records among 24 Parkinson outpatients grouped by sarcopenia. These are separate studies. The diet study analyzes overall protein intake without a named dried fig exposure or a visual hallucination outcome.

Abstract 8 is a retrospective clinical series of Parkinson sialorrhea. Response combines physician judgment or patient perception with tolerability. The abstract lists 197 patients, 27 absent from follow-up, 115 favorable first responses and 54 primary nonresponses. The printed counts and percentages do not consistently clarify the denominator. The study contains no raw-fig comparison.

Abstract 76 follows 350 people with Parkinson disease grouped by reported physical activity. It reports a within-active-group change for the MDS-UPDRS saliva and drooling item (p=0.014) without publishing the item's signed estimate. The image for Table 1 gives selected between-group changes for other items and omits saliva and drooling. A within-group p-value does not establish the between-group direction.

Abstract 100 reports dry mouth among cannabis users in a cross-sectional clinical sample. The exposure and the adverse-event measure differ from raw fig and graded drooling. The review records gaps in the numbering of the official supplement.
