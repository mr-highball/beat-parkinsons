Title: Cruciferous vegetable and isothiocyanate intake and multiple health outcomes.
PMID: 34929422
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodchem.2021.131816
First published: 2022
Authors: Li N, Wu X, Zhuang W, Wu C, Rao Z, Du L et al.
Journal: Food Chem
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34929422/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Umbrella review of 57 systematic reviews/meta-analyses across 24 health outcomes for cruciferous vegetable and isothiocyanate intake. Pooled cruciferous intake was inversely associated with depression risk and all-cause mortality (-10% per 100 g/d), but 68% of included evidence was low quality, exposures pooled all crucifers without isolating cauliflower, and no PD symptom endpoints were evaluated.
