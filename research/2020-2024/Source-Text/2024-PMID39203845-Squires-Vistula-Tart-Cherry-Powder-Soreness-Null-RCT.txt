Title: Acute Dosing Strategy with Vistula Tart Cherries for Recovery of Strenuous Exercise-A Feasibility Study.
PMID: 39203845
PMCID: PMC11357489
DOI: 10.3390/nu16162709
First published: 2024
Authors: Squires E, Walshe IH, Dodd A, Broadbelt E, Hayman O, McHugh MP, Howatson G
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11357489/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized, placebo-controlled pilot trial (Squires et al., 2024, Nutrients, PMC11357489) in 22 recreationally active young adults (12 spray-dried Vistula tart cherry extract, 10 calorie-matched placebo) dosed acutely for 4 days starting on the day of 40 maximal eccentric elbow flexor contractions. No statistically significant between-group differences were found at 24, 48, or 72 h for VAS muscle soreness, pain pressure threshold, maximal voluntary contraction, range of motion, or limb girth (all p>0.3). Competing null powdered tart cherry soreness trial.
