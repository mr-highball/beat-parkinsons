Title: Ethnic Differences in Atypical Parkinsonism-is South Asian PSP Different?
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11542300/
PMID: 39113437
PMCID: PMC11542300
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.14182
Publication year: 2024
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The retrospective case series describes progressive supranuclear palsy phenotypes in people of South Asian descent. Visual hallucinations prompted consideration of alternative diagnoses in some cases. It supplies no reported fig exposure or diet-linked Parkinson episode rate.

The authors examine alternative explanations for individual clinical features. The case series notes visual symptoms in some patients alongside other atypical features.
