Title: Spastic Paraplegia Type 30 Associated with Levodopa‐Responsive Parkinsonism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10450240/
PMID: 37635774
PMCID: PMC10450240
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13815
Publication date: 2023-07-07
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

A spastic paraplegia type 30 case describes well-formed visual hallucinations that later settled without treatment for the hallucinations. The clinical phenotype and medication history do not include dried fig intake or a counted interval for visual episodes.
