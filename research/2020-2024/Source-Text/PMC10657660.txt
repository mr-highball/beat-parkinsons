The original reports any psychotic features in 27 of 70 early Parkinson participants and three of 74 controls. The 10PDQ identifies sensory type separately from frequency of the worst psychotic experience, and CAARMS scores combine frequency and duration. The study does not track dried fig intake or separately count visual episodes after meals.

The 10PDQ's first five items distinguish kinds of experiences. Later items rate the worst experience across kinds and do not give visual-only frequency. Source Table 1 reports domain ratings. Figure 1 compares proportions experiencing the separately described subtypes.
