EPR-016 assesses liquid meal and octreotide blood pressure responses in autonomic failure, including eight Parkinson patients within a sample of 67. EPR-203 compares FDG PET and questionnaire or task measures in 11 Parkinson patients with visual symptoms and nine without. Neither member assigns dried figs, and the two outcome sets come from separate patients.

The PET abstract describes relative hypermetabolism and a questionnaire association without correction for multiple comparisons. This abstract does not quantify recurrent spontaneous episodes after a food exposure. The hemodynamic abstract measures a different outcome in a different group.

EPR-093 retrospectively compares 99 patients in a lysosomal-pathway genetic subgroup with 36 patients in a mitochondrial-pathway subgroup. The poster describes higher prevalence of a set of nonmotor symptoms that includes sialorrhea in the lysosomal group without providing a sialorrhea-specific estimate. The exposure is the genetic classification. The official EAN supplement includes ten withdrawn entries.
