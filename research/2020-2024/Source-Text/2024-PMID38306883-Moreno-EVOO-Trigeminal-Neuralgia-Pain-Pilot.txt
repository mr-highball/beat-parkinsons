Title: Daily olive oil intake is feasible to reduce trigeminal neuralgia facial pain: A pilot study.
PMID: 38306883
DOI: 10.1016/j.nutres.2024.01.005
First published: 2024
Authors: Moreno C, Percival SS, Kelly DL, Neubert JK
Journal: Nutrition Research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38306883/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
16-week open-label parallel pilot RCT (NCT05032573; 4-wk baseline + 12-wk intervention) in 52 randomized (42 completed) female adults with trigeminal neuralgia assigned to 60 mL/day EVOO vs usual diet. Within the EVOO group, Penn Facial Pain Scale severity and general/orofacial interference fell significantly from baseline (usual-diet control unchanged), whereas between-group comparison vs control did NOT reach clear statistical significance for pain severity and only trended for orofacial interference (p = 0.05). Unblinded pilot, high attrition (10/52), non-PD cranial neuralgia.
