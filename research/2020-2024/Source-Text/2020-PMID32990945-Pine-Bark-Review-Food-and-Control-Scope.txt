Original study scope
The Cochrane review included 27 trials of pine bark extract and reports very uncertain findings. Its diabetic eye trial member combines grape seed, green tea and turmeric among many ingredients and uses canola oil capsules as the control. Kale is named in a shared dietary restriction. An erectile-dysfunction trial member used Crocus sativus in a three-component supplement. Cinnamon is named as a proanthocyanidin dietary source, without cinnamon intervention. Dates are temporal.

Continuing evidence gaps
Keep the diabetic visual outcomes, erectile-dysfunction comparison, dietary guidance and pine bark estimates within their separate trial members. They provide no attribution to a culinary preparation or one of the frozen Parkinson outcomes.
