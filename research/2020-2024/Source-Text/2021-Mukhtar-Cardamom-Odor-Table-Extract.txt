# Recognition of cardamom essence in a Parkinson smell test

Source https://nja.pastic.gov.pk/PJCIS/index.php/PJNS/article/download/3641/3584
Coverage original Methods and Table 4

Mukhtar and colleagues used ten odorants in a Pakistani case-control smell identification test. Original Table 4 reports correct identification of a commercial cardamom essence in 36 of 87 Parkinson cases and 49 of 58 controls (p < .001). The article PDF lists publication December 31, 2021 and the journal record lists posting January 19, 2022. A different 2022 natural cardamom smell test reports an inconclusive case-control difference. The tests measured recognition of presented odors with distinct procedures. They did not study the effect of eating ground cardamom.
