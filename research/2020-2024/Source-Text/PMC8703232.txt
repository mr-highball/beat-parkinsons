Title: Zonisamide improves axial symptoms in dementia with Lewy bodies with parkinsonism: Post hoc analysis of clinical trials
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8703232/
PMID: 34988303
PMCID: PMC8703232
DOI: 10.1016/j.ensci.2021.100384
Publication date: 2021-12-07
Source depth: PMC original full body from structured article text; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC article metadata and PubMed.
Design: Post hoc analysis of two clinical trials

This analysis pools prior Japanese zonisamide trials JapicCTI-122040 and JapicCTI-152839 to evaluate axial motor symptoms in dementia with Lewy bodies and parkinsonism. Visual hallucinations provide clinical background. The pooled motor contrast supplies no dried fig or separately counted visual episode evidence.
