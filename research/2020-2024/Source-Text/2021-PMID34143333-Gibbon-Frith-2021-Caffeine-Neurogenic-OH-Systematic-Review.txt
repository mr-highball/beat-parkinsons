Title: The effects of caffeine in adults with neurogenic orthostatic hypotension: a systematic review.
PMID: 34143333
PMCID: PMC8212790
DOI: 10.1007/s10286-021-00814-5
First published: 2021
Authors: Gibbon JR, Frith J
Journal: Clinical autonomic research : official journal of the Clinical Autonomic Research Society
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8212790/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review evaluating caffeine in adults with neurogenic orthostatic hypotension (nOH, including PD/MSA/PAF): identified zero clinical trials evaluating caffeine monotherapy on nOH symptoms or orthostatic BP, finding only multi-agent/combination or non-nOH data. Confirms absence of clinical evidence linking caffeinated beverages to nOH lightheadedness relief or worsening.
