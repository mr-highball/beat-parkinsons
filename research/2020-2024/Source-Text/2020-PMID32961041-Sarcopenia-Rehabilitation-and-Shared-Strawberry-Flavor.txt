Title: Improving rehabilitation in sarcopenia: a randomized-controlled trial utilizing a muscle-targeted food for special medical purposes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7749532/
PMCID: PMC7749532
PMID: 32961041
DOI: 10.1002/jcsm.12532
ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT03120026
First public article date: 2020-09-22
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original Methods and Results, including the primary gait outcome

Rondanelli and colleagues randomized 140 hospitalized older adults
with sarcopenia to rehabilitation plus a whey, leucine and vitamin D
formula or rehabilitation plus isocaloric maltodextrin. The analysis
included 127 participants (64 and 63). Participants in either assigned
group could receive a strawberry- or vanilla-flavored version. The
article does not quantify fruit in the flavoring or report outcomes
separately by flavor.

The between-group difference in monthly change in 4-meter gait speed
favored the nutrient formula by 0.063 m/s/month (95% CI 0.043 to 0.082,
p<.001). Gait and secondary function scores are mobility measures.
The report provides no spontaneous fall-frequency comparison or
Parkinson-specific estimate for the flavored products. The assigned
contrast estimates the effect of the formula during rehabilitation.
