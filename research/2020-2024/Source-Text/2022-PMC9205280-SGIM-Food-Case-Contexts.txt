One arrhythmia case (polymorphic ventricular tachycardia) reports prior use of a supplement containing raspberry ketones and green coffee bean extract. The product and case establish a coexposure, without identifying an isolated cause or a comparison of brewed coffee or whole raspberries.

One folate-deficiency case reports a restricted diet that sometimes included a grape or olive. Two separate case members report potatoes or potato chips in restricted diets, one after altered taste and one with multiple nutritional deficiencies. The abstracts give no single-ingredient intervention. Distinguish whole fruit and mixed dietary contexts from oils and formulations.

A skin infection case reports environmental exposure to shrimp during cleanup. A separate infection case describes suspected raw poultry as an occupational source, and other cases mention animal contact. These are route-specific contexts. They do not establish effects of an eaten ingredient or a Parkinson symptom outcome.
