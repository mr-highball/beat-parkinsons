Review depth: Original PMC structured article, Table 2, Labor pain severity, limitations and original references 29, 58, 62 and 64

Four publications were eligible for the labor-pain discussion. References 62 and 64 report shared participants. Reference 29, the fresh Rutab Tabuk trial, reports pooled pain categories without the between-group estimate required for the review. The two independently observed favorable comparisons used date syrup during labor, from references 58 and 64. The review could not pool labor pain because the available effect estimates were insufficient. Its results on labor duration and delivery cannot substitute for pain severity.
