Gingerol enriched extract and mechanical pain in rats
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11118883/
PMID: 38790607
PMCID: PMC11118883
DOI: 10.3390/antiox13050502
Coverage: Original April 23, 2024 full text, Methods 2.3 and Figure 1

Shen and colleagues assigned 36 male rats across sham and spinal-nerve-ligation groups, with two groups receiving a supercritical-fluid ginger extract standardized to 20% gingerols. The Methods describe four weeks of administration and the abstract describes five weeks for the overall study. Treated injured rats had higher mechanical withdrawal thresholds than untreated injured animals at the end. Figure 1 supplies the comparisons with no exact means table. The extract and animal nerve injury differ from grated ginger and Parkinson pain.
