Title: Serum proBDNF Is Associated With Changes in the Ketone Body β-Hydroxybutyrate and Shows Superior Repeatability Over Mature BDNF: Secondary Outcomes From a Cross-Over Trial in Healthy Older Adults.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8417778/
PMID: 34489682
PMCID: PMC8417778
DOI: 10.3389/fnagi.2021.716594
Publication date: 2021-08-20
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official complete PMC JATS body.

In a 15-person healthy older-adult crossover, oils were served with coffee and cream. Sunflower oil is among the assigned oral comparisons. Outcomes are serum proBDNF, mature BDNF and ketone response, with no depression severity estimate. Coffee is shared across the conditions.
