Title: Baked potato cultivar and sensory texture
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2304-8158/9/4/451
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

April 7, 2020. Foods 9(4):451. Methods and Figure 3.
The study compared five potato varieties across cooking methods. Six trained panelists rated sensory traits by consensus. The baked condition used foil-wrapped oven cooking at 218 degrees Celsius. Descriptors included differences in firmness or creaminess across cultivars.
The panel evaluated the food product. No clinical swallowing comparison or Parkinson cohort was reported.
