Dietary gingerol extract and open-field behavior in diabetic neuropathy
First published online: 2024-02-01
DOI: 10.1016/j.nutres.2024.01.014
PMID: 38402829
PMCID: PMC11466295
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11466295/
Coverage: Original author manuscript Methods 2.1 and 2.3, Results 3.1 and Figure 1

Male rats were assigned to control, diabetic neuropathy, or diabetic neuropathy with dietary gingerol-enriched extract. The extract was mixed into chow. The authors report greater open-field center duration and frequency of entries than in the untreated neuropathy group. Figure 1 describes 6–8 observations per group. The Methods describe eight weeks of assigned diet and Results 3.1 references five weeks of supplementation. Movement can influence the observed open-field measures. This model contains no clinical anxiety assessment or Parkinson disease.
