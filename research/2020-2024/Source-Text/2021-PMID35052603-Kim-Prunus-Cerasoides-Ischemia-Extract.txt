The botanical source was authenticated and a voucher retained. Section 2.1 describes ethanol extracts of dry stems and leaves and lists identified compounds, including prunetin. In the animal comparison the extract was injected intraperitoneally in separate pre- and post-stroke designs after random group assignment. The parallel cell experiments assessed extract or selected constituents using neuroglobin and oxidative stress measurements.

Figure 5 reports lower infarct volumes with pretreatment at days one and three compared with vehicle. The post-treatment comparison in Figure 6 also reports lower infarct volume. Table 2 separates neurological subtests. The reported mean Zea–Longa scores were 1.6 for vehicle (n=5) and 1.3 for extract (n=7), while other scores differed by task. The behavioral battery concerns short-term focal injury and sensorimotor deficits.

The study supplies a route-specific precursor to the later oral vascular dementia research. Its tissue and composite neurological outcomes leave apathy open. Injected concentrated stem and leaf material in a mouse stroke model does not establish an effect of eating dried cherry in people with Parkinson disease.
