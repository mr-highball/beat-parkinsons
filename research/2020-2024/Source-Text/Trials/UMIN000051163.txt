Carbonated and tap water contrast boluses in Parkinson disease

Source: UMIN Clinical Trials Registry, UMIN000051163, receipt R000058349
Official record: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000058349
English record: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000058349
Sponsor: National Center of Neurology and Psychiatry, Japan
First public disclosure: 2023-05-29
Coverage: original English summary of selected registration fields, checked 2026-11-12

The registered open randomized crossover targets 50 people with Parkinson disease who need videofluoroscopic swallowing assessment in clinical care. It compares a 10 mL tap-water barium preparation with a 10 mL carbonated-water barium preparation. The two groups take them in opposite sequences. Dementia is an exclusion.

The primary measures cover liquid properties and videofluoroscopic outcomes, including the Penetration–Aspiration Scale. Secondary measures include Japanese versions of the Dysphagia Handicap Index and Swallowing Disturbance Questionnaire, the Eating Assessment Tool (EAT-10) and a preference survey. No culinary ingredient or habitual beverage use is being compared.

The May 2026 status has a separate dated summary in the 2025–2029 research period. The related NCNP record UMIN000051942 carries a different receipt number and status.
