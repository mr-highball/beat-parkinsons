Title: The BRoccoli In Osteoarthritis (BRIO study) - A randomised controlled feasibility trial to examine the potential protective effect of broccoli bioactives, (specifically sulforaphane), on osteoarthritis
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov
URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03878368
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03878368
Registry ID: NCT03878368
First published: 2024
Authors: Davidson R, Watts L, Beasy G, Saha S, Kroon P, Cassidy A, Clark A, Fraser W
Journal: medRxiv (preprint; NCT03878368 / ISRCTN11629849)
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Two-center, double-blind, parallel-group, randomized placebo-controlled dietary feasibility trial (NCT03878368 / ISRCTN11629849; n=24 enrolled) in adults with radiographic knee osteoarthritis (Kellgren-Lawrence 2-3, baseline pain >=4/10) receiving high-glucoraphanin broccoli soup or sensory-matched control soup (no sulforaphane) for 12 weeks. Mean WOMAC pain score (0-20 scale) decreased by 4.2 points (95% CI: 1.03, 7.38) with broccoli soup vs placebo, encompassing the minimal clinically important difference, with similar improvement patterns in other pain/function measures and 92% acceptability; 3 related AEs (2 expected). Limits: small feasibility preprint, cooked high-glucoraphanin broccoli soup rather than raw florets, knee OA rather than PD pain.
