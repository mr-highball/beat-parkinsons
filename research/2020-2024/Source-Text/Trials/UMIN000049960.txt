The listed objective is to assess constipation-related effects from two weeks of dark chocolate. The registry describes healthy adult women with infrequent bowel movements and a chocolate comparison. The entry describes planned observations. The linked results publication (PMID 40636155, DOI 10.12938/bmfh.2024-049) states that the trial ran in June–July 2015, before the registry record was publicly disclosed. The registry and results paper describe the same study family.
