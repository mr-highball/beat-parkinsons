Title: Effects of daily perilla oil intake on athletes
Registry identifier: UMIN000044882
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000051251
Format: Selected official registry fields paraphrased for this catalog.

Status: Completed. 48 target, 40 enrolled in the registry.
Design: Four parallel arms listed for women athletes over eight weeks. Two perilla seed-oil arms, one olive-oil arm and a placebo jelly arm. Bowel symptom comparison is secondary to gut and metabolic measures.
Linked original: PMID 35705194 lists three arms and 36 randomized, with no olive-oil result reported. The register dates its result and first journal fields July 15, 2021. The linked paper first appeared online June 16, 2022. Earlier registry date fields and the difference in arms and counts are unresolved. The registry supplies no separate olive-oil constipation outcome.
