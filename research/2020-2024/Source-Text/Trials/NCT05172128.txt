Title: Effect of Blueberry Supplementation on Alzheimer's Biomarkers
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05172128

NCT ID: NCT05172128
First posted: 2021-12-29
Results first posted: 2024-09-19
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Selected official registration and results fields.

The completed single-arm study used freeze-dried blueberry powder in mild cognitive impairment and lists 12 actual enrollees. Primary outcomes concern blood biomarkers. The posted safety results record two participants among ten at risk with constipation, using nonsystematic adverse-event assessment. The related journal report describes one resolving after a temporary stop and one resolving spontaneously. The registry and article refer to the same exposure series. There was no concurrent comparator or dedicated clinical Parkinson constipation severity measure.
