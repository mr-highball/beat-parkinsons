BRIEF SUMMARY

Patients with neurological or psychiatric symptoms or pre-existing disease will be
sampled (blood for serum and DNA or saliva for DNA) at inclusion and at M6 (blood) and
M12 (blood) depending on their pathology. Sampling will be done either at hospital if
patient is on site for routine care or at home if no consultation is scheduled at
hospital.
