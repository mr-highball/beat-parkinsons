ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    1. PD was diagnosed according to the criteria of Movement Disorders and Parkinson
       Disease Group of Chinese Society of Neurology of Chinese Medical Association.

    2. Olfactory score was 1 standard deviation lower than the corresponding age norm;

    3. no medication plan in the past four weeks or no change in medication plan in the
       past four weeks, which can be maintained until the end of the experiment; 4.40-75
       years old, with normal vision, hearing and language understanding and expression
       ability;

  5.Good cooperation of patients

  Exclusion Criteria:

    1. Parkinson&#39;s syndrome and Parkinson&#39;s plus syndrome caused by cerebrovascular
       disease, encephalitis, trauma, drugs, etc.;

    2. Neuropsychiatric diseases that may affect the sense of smell, such as depression,
       anxiety, schizophrenia, etc.;

    3. History of nasal disease or surgery, such as rhinitis, sinusitis, nasal septum
       deviation, etc. Upper respiratory tract infection in the past 3 weeks;

    4. Long-term smoking and occupations that inhale chemical irritants for a long time.
