Fujita swallowing CT protocol in Parkinson disease

Source: UMIN Clinical Trials Registry, UMIN000050721, receipt R000057788
Official record: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000057788
Sponsor: Fujita Health University
First public disclosure: 2023-03-30
Coverage: original English summary of selected registration fields, checked 2026-11-12

The registry describes a single-arm, open, nonrandomized diagnostic study using 320-row area-detector CT to examine laryngeal closure during swallowing in people with Parkinson disease. Participants had received a swallowing evaluation and were taking three meals orally. The target sample size was 60. The registered imaging procedure does not name a culinary ingredient.

Primary measures were laryngeal closure timing and laryngeal vestibule volume. Other planned assessments included swallowing structure motion, hyoid and larynx displacement, upper esophageal sphincter opening, pharyngeal volume, laryngeal penetration and aspiration, EAT-10 and PILL-5, and correlations with motor and nonmotor severity and imaging markers.

The registry's November 3, 2026 results have a separate dated summary in the 2025–2029 research period.
