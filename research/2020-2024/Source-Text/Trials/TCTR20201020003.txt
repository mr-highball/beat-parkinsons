Coverage: Original factual extract of official intervention, dates, outcomes and results fields.

Three arms are a color- and smell-matched placebo and congee combined with 200 or 400 mg/day of the preparation called tomato-pomace extract in the registry. The primary outcome is N100 and P300 event-related potentials at eight weeks. Secondary outcomes include a computerized cognitive battery, biomarkers including serum alpha-synuclein and DJ-1, and adverse effects. No distinct executive-function primary measure or Parkinson condition is listed. The public results fields are blank. The 2025 article cites this ID and describes a boiled tomato powder. Its ethics-approval date of December 20, 2020 falls before the enrollment date listed in the official registry. The paper describes 63 eligible adults and 60 completions. The registry target is a planned count, and participant histories are unavailable.
