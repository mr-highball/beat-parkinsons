Registered pain and context changes during gait learning

Official record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05626582
First public posting: 2022-11-23
Selected public registry fields, paraphrased.

The registered comparison measures motor learning and later retention in healthy young adults after experimental pain assigned in different sessions. No results are posted. This protocol is close to the Jackson 2025 paper, but an exact cross-identifier remains unverified.
