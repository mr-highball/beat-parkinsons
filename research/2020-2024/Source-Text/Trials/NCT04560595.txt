The completed remote trial enrolled 109 people who wanted to reduce caffeine and reported problems related to use. Participants were randomized to immediate or delayed access to a reduction manual. The results report caffeine consumption, acceptability, retention, standardized caffeine-related problems, anxiety, sleep, reflux and gastrointestinal measures.

The 19 standardized problem items are combined on a 0–57 scale. Posted mean scores were 17.85 and 19.23 at baseline and 5.52 and 6.28 at the respective seven-week follow-up. Each group's results are aligned to its treatment schedule. These scores do not provide a separate fatigue result. The registry publishes individual scales for several other domains. It does not establish brewed coffee, brewed tea, or Parkinson diagnosis as assigned exposure and population.
