NCT06353984. First posted April 9, 2024. Latest posted update February 18, 2026. Status active, not recruiting. Estimated enrollment 114 in registry and no posted results.
Three food-program comparisons include nutritionally balanced MiniMeal products, reference products, and habitual diet. The Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale is a secondary outcome at nine weeks. The later registered protocol describes a mango or passion fruit drink within a complete program and diagnosed neurodegenerative disease exclusion. The fruit choice was not independently randomized.
