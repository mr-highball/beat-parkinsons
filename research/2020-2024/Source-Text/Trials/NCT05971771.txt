Registry title: Study to Assess Impact of Dietary Supplement on Sleep Health

Registry status is Unknown, with 40 planned participants and no posted results. The active preparation combines a saffron extract and magnesium citrate in a powder. The placebo intervention is registered as softgels. The Insomnia Severity Index is a secondary outcome. Differences in both content and delivery limit attribution to saffron even if a comparison is subsequently published.
