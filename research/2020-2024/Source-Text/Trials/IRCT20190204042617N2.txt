Title: Lemon balm herbal tea and mental health in nurses caring for patients with COVID-19
Registry ID: IRCT20190204042617N2
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/56623
WHO bridge: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT20190204042617N2
First registered: 2021-06-30.
Text coverage: Official trial registry fields reviewed 2026-11-17.

The registry describes a parallel randomized double-blind comparison with a planned sample of 88 nurses. The arms receive lemon balm tea bags or black tea bags for 21 days. It lists separate DASS-21 depression, anxiety and stress measurements at baseline and day 21. The compared teas may differ in sensory qualities. The record states masking without an outcome that verifies whether it succeeded.

The registry was posted during recruitment. The sources checked do not supply actual enrollment or a verified depression result. The registered comparison concerns nurses rather than people with Parkinson disease. The WHO mirror contains conflicting 21-day and 28-day listings for other measures. Keep the DASS-21 depression outcome open.
