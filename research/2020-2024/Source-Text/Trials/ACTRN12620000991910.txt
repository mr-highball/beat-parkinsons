Title: Gold kiwifruit-derived prebiotic for constipation in Parkinson disease
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/ACTRN12620000991910.aspx

Registration number: ACTRN12620000991910
Date registered: 2020-10-01
Checked: 2026-11-22
Review depth: registry
Access evidence: Exact ANZCTR trial record with matching WHO ICTRP identifier. ANZCTR reports last update 2022-12-16.

The planned crossover comparison used Livaux, a gold kiwifruit-derived prebiotic capsule, versus matched placebo in people with Parkinson disease and constipation. The primary endpoint was weekly complete bowel movements. The registry states that recruitment stopped early and records final enrollment of 14 against a target of 20. It gives recruitment difficulty and resource limitations as reasons. At the recorded update the data were being analyzed. The inspected record does not supply a numerical effect for the primary endpoint.

Continuing evidence gaps
Two same-cohort conference reports discuss 12 randomized participants and give an inconclusive primary comparison. The 14 accrued in the registry and 12 randomized in the abstracts are different denominators. A complete outcomes report or tabulated dataset remains open. The intervention is a processed supplement. The planned bowel endpoint cannot establish a whole-fruit effect on postural instability. Do not count the WHO mirror and ANZCTR as separate trials.
