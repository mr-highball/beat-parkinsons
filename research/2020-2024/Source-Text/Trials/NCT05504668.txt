Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05504668
First posted: 2022-08-17, after reported actual completion in February 2022.
Trial registry summary. The Mind Focus botanical combination includes material labeled cardamom without stated species, plant part or grind. Registered outcomes principally address cognition, mood, urinary metabolites and microbiome. The registry lists 174 enrolled and no posted summary results. The 2023 paper separately analyzed 128 participants.
