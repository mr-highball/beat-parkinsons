BRIEF SUMMARY

The term perceptual awareness refers to subjective experience... or the phenomenology
associated with the processing of a sensory stimulus. The term metacognition refers to
our ability of introspection, knowledge and control of our own cognitive processes. The
objective of this research is to establish the contribution of the basal ganglia and
thalamus to the perceptual awareness and meta-cognition, using deep brain stimulation
coupled with electroencephalography.
