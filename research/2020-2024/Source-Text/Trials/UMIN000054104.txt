Title: Effects of dual-task exercise combined with mouth rinse and paprika xanthophyll ingestion on cognitive function
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000061789
Identifier: UMIN000054104 (receipt R000061789)
First public disclosure: April 15, 2024
Latest modified on visible record: October 10, 2024
Retrieved: November 15, 2026
Coverage: Factual extract from public Japanese clinical trial registry.

This single-blind randomized parallel registry record gives 40 enrolled healthy adults and lists paprika xanthophyll 9 mg/day and a 6% carbohydrate mouth rinse. The outcomes listed include cognitive testing and regional oxygen saturation. The registry lists completed recruitment, marks results unpublished, and supplies no outcome values. The registered population and parallel design differ from the two earlier student conference abstracts.
