Ten participants were assigned across two crossover sequences, five per order. One placebo-first participant was lost during washout. The registry reports exposure denominators of 9 after supplement and 10 after placebo for these outcomes. These are crossover condition denominators.
The outcome module lists three secondary Multidimensional Fatigue Index subscales at one week. Each is scored 4–20, with higher scores indicating greater fatigue. Values below are the registry's least-squares means with standard errors.

Subscale	Supplement baseline (SE)	Supplement 1 week (SE)	Placebo baseline (SE)	Placebo 1 week (SE)
General fatigue	15.1 (1.0)	14.0 (1.0)	14.7 (1.0)	13.4 (1.0)
Physical fatigue	11.1 (1.3)	9.5 (1.3)	10.3 (1.3)	11.3 (1.3)
Mental fatigue	11.7 (1.0)	12.4 (1.0)	12.5 (1.0)	11.7 (1.0)

No inferential fatigue comparison is posted in these registry outcome objects. The 2026 peer-reviewed report gives its own crossover model estimates. These reports describe the same trial.
