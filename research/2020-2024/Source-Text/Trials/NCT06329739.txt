BRIEF SUMMARY

The genetic landscape of Parkinson's disease (PD) is characterised by rare high
penetrance pathogenic variants causing familial disease, genetic risk factor variants
driving PD risk in a significant minority in PD cases and high frequency, low penetrance
variants, which contribute a small increase of the risk of developing sporadic PD. This
knowledge has the potential to have a major impact in the clinical care of people with
PD.

The goal of this observational study is to evaluate the impact of genetic mutation on
behavior and cognition in PD patients.

Patients will be assessed over time using test, questionnaire and standardised clinica
scales. An initial assessment and annual follow-up assessments will be carried out for 5
years.

Researchers will compare data collected from patients with genetic mutation versus
patients without mutation.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Montreal Cognitive Assessment
    Description
      Cognitive impairment change from baseline until 5 years in Montreal Cognitive Assessment
      (MoCA; min. 0, max. 30, higher score means better outcome)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Beck Depression Inventory
    Description
      Depressive symptoms change from baseline to 5 year (BDI-II, min 0, max 63, higher score
      means worse outcome)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: State-Trait Anxiety Inventory
    Description
      Anxiety symptoms change from baseline to 5 year (STAI, min 20, max 80, higher score means
      worse outcome)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Questionnaire for Impulsive-Compulsive Disorders in Parkinson
    Description
      Impulsivity change from baseline to 5 years (QUIP, min 0, max 112, higher score means
      worse outcome)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index
    Description: Sleep Quality change (PSQI, min 0, max 21, higher score means poorer sleep quality)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire-8
    Description
      Quality of life change from baseline to 5 years (PDQ-8, min 0, max 100, higher score
      means worse outcome)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory 2-RF
    Description
      Personality change from baseline to 5 years (MMPI-2-RF, cut-off: T>65 for clinical
      scales)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
