Title: Effect of Ficus carica and Juglans regia product on functional constipation in older adults
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/50369
Registry credit: Iranian Registry of Clinical Trials
Registration number: IRCT20200714048108N1
First posted: 2020-09-19
Registry last update: 2020-09-19
Status: Recruitment complete
Text coverage: Selected official English registry fields. Contact information omitted.

The registry marks the trial as registered while recruiting. It planned a randomized parallel design enrolling 90 older adults, with 45 in each group. The arms specify fig–walnut syrup and lactulose syrup for two weeks. Some text calls lactulose a placebo, while the intervention table also identifies it as a constipation remedy. It is a named active comparator.

The two listed primary outcomes are gastrointestinal pain and constipation. Measurements are scheduled at baseline, the last treatment session and thirty days after the session. The 2022 published report prints this registration number and reports assessments at baseline, two weeks and four weeks under a four-week treatment description. The official page provides no separate numerical outcome dataset. See DOI 10.34172/cjmb.2022.32 for the results and methodological differences.
