Title: ErdBEHR strawberry nutrition intervention registration
Registry identifier: DRKS00026998
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00026998
German original: https://drks.de/search/de/trial/DRKS00026998
First public registration: 2022-10-19 (retrospective)
Retrieved: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Distilled official registration fields and comparison with the linked results paper.

Five parallel arms compare a low-fresh-strawberry active control against higher amounts of fresh strawberries, with separate arms adding freeze-dried fruit and in the highest combined regimen, capers in olive oil. The trial enrolled community volunteers aged 50–80 years. LDL cholesterol and high-sensitivity C-reactive protein are the registered primary biomarkers. No fall-frequency endpoint is listed. Chair-rise performance appears in the paper as a prespecified secondary outcome from the 2021 ethics application. The retrospective registry, the main results and the microbiome secondary analysis all describe the same trial family.

Results publication: DOI 10.59368/agingbio.20240020, first published February 2024.
Microbiome analysis: PMID 39772703 and DOI 10.1128/spectrum.01913-24, first published January 2025.
