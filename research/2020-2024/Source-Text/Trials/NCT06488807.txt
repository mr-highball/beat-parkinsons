Perioperative olfactory enrichment randomized trial

Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06488807
Source credit: ClinicalTrials.gov
NCT ID: NCT06488807
First posted: 2024-07-05
Registry last update posted: 2025-05-14
Text coverage: research summary of registered interventions and outcomes.

This perioperative protocol compares multi-odor enrichment with sham and measures postoperative delirium and other neurocognitive outcomes. Green-tea scent is one of the olfactory stimuli, and olfactory performance is also measured. The record gives no tea-only contrast, beverage ingestion arm, or Parkinson cohort. The registry snapshot lists recruiting status and no posted results.
