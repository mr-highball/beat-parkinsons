Title: Plant Exosomes +/- Curcumin to Abrogate Symptoms of Inflammatory Bowel Disease
NCT ID: NCT04879810
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04879810
API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04879810
First posted: 2021-05-10
Last update posted: 2022-11-03
Archive scope: Official registry fields and factual summary. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

Completed. Four enrolled. No results are posted.

The three parallel groups were assigned ginger-derived vesicles, curcumin, or both. The primary outcome was change in inflammation on colonoscopy. The official title and detailed description specify inflammatory bowel disease, including refractory disease. The structured condition field says “Irritable Bowel Disease”. This discrepancy cannot establish an irritable bowel syndrome trial. The register does not provide a measured outcome for culinary ginger, constipation, or Parkinson disease.
