Title: A Long-term Safety Study of Cannabidiol Oral Solution (GWP42003-P, CBD-OS) in Patients With Rett Syndrome
Publication date: 2020-02-05 (first public registry posting)
Record: NCT04252586
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04252586
Text coverage: Distilled official registration, arm and outcome descriptions.

The completed single-group extension enrolled 21 participants with Rett syndrome for cannabidiol oral solution. The formulation lists strawberry flavoring among several excipients. It assigns no raw fruit supplement or fruit-free comparator. The planned endpoints include systematic treatment-emergent adverse events. A separately posted result gives fall reports for the single group.
