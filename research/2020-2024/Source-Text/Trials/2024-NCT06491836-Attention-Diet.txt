Official pediatric oligoantigenic diet registration

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06491836

First posted July 9, 2024. The completed Cairo ADHD study compared an oligoantigenic diet with a food regimen without the restrictions. Broccoli was one of several permitted vegetables. The protocol lists parent ADHD ratings and a continuous performance task. Broccoli quantity, preparation and a separate broccoli comparison were unspecified. The entry has no posted results.
