The University of Debrecen lists its study ID as DE RKEB/IKEB 5622-2020. The record gives 2023-06-01 as the actual start date and an estimated enrollment of 62. The registry's current overall status is Unknown. The design is a single, open-label ginger-supplementation group with no comparison group. The brief summary describes 540 mg twice daily.

The two primary measures are functional dyspepsia symptom severity (NDI) and body fat content at four weeks. The secondary quality-of-life measure is EQ-5D at four weeks and includes generic pain/discomfort and anxiety/depression domains. ClinicalTrials.gov posts no numerical results. The record has a derived PubMed link to PMID 40474983, whose investigators report an eight-week tolerability analysis. This registration does not supply an isolated anxiety estimate, Parkinson pain or constipation. Registry timepoints and instruments must be compared with the actual publications.
