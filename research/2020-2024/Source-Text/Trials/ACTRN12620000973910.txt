Date registered: 2020-09-28
Date last updated and results provided in the captured public record: 2022-03-28
Snapshot date: 2026-11-19

The prospectively registered study enrolled 120 adults reporting poor sleep and compared two standardized saffron extract doses with placebo for 28 days. The registry records diaries and questionnaire outcomes and plans salivary melatonin and cortisol. Its results listings name the 2021 original report DOI 10.1016/j.sleep.2021.08.001, PMID 34438361. These records describe one trial. The register identifies the extract manufacturer as the funding source.
