Official healthy-volunteer saliva registration

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04997889

First posted August 10, 2021. The protocol describes a preliminary three-formulation saliva experiment in 21 healthy adults and a subsequent herbal versus commercial comparison targeting 112 adults. The 2024 report matches the first phase and the 2026 report names this registration for its healthy safety phase. The same registration does not establish shared participants. Cumin seed extract is combined with ginger in an artificial saliva product, with no toasted-cumin ingestion or swallowing endpoint.
