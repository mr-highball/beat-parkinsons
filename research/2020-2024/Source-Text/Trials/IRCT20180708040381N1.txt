Title: The relationship between date consumption and the severity of dysmenorrhea in women with regular menstrual cycles
Registry ID: IRCT20180708040381N1
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/61664
Persian registration: https://fa.irct.ir/trial/61664
First registered: 2022-02-05, retrospective.
Text coverage: Selected official English registry fields verified on 2026-11-19.

The record describes consecutive menstrual cycles with and without whole Mazafati date exposure, an analyst-masked visual analogue pain assessment and a 2011 recruitment window. The summary refers to 26 students and the structured target field lists 43. Documented enrollment, arm or cycle denominators and the date fruit drying stage are unresolved.

The registry lists completed recruitment. The checked record supplies the planned pain outcome without presenting numeric outcome data. Menstrual pain outside Parkinson disease is a different clinical phenotype from the frozen claim.
