Registered age comparison after experimental leg pain

Official record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05471557
First public posting: 2022-07-25
Selected public registry fields, paraphrased.

The official record accepts healthy volunteers and compares motor learning and retention after experimental pain in younger and older adults. It also lists digit-span measures. The 2026 paper explicitly names this registration and supplies the local capsaicin and heat procedure. No results are posted in the registry at review. The registration and the paper are one study family.
