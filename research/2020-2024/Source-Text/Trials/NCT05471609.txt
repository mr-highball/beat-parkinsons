BRIEF SUMMARY

Achieving sustained blood levels of carbidopa/levodopa has been a challenge in the
treatment of PD and although levodopa remains the most commonly used drug, motor
fluctuations remain a major disability especially in advanced Parkinson's disease. The
aim of this study is to determine the efficacy of a novel oral carbidopa-levodopa
formulation in achieving a sustained blood level of levodopa and carbidopa in normal
volunteers.
