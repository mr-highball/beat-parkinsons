Start: 2024-01-22. Completed: 2024-12-18. Actual enrollment: 57.
Results posted: No, verified in the November 15, 2026 official API snapshot.

The registry compares fermented oat gurt and nonfermented oat porridge during three-week periods in a crossover trial. The GHQ-12 is a secondary mental health measure. The public outcome fields do not specify a separately reported insomnia measure. A later conference abstract and journal article describe the same cohort of 56 analyzed adults.
