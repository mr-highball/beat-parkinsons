BRIEF SUMMARY

This study is double-blinded placebo controlled followed by an open-label extension
placebo-to-active switch to estimate the efficacy of Talineuren. The investigational
Medicinal Product (IMP) is administrated 48 times intravenously as an add-on therapy to
the standard of care Parkinson medication.

Talineuren is a liposomal formulation containing GM1 (monosialotetrahexosylganglioside)
as the pharmacological active substance.

The results of this pilot study are essential for the sample size calculation of a
subsequent larger phase II/III trial.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

The ganglioside lipid GM1 (monosialotetrahexosylganglioside) has attracted attention in
scientific literature as a promising neuroprotective agent. Research suggests that GM1
ganglioside holds promise not only in the treatment of neurodegenerative disorders like
Parkinson disease (PD) and Alzheimer's disease but also in promoting nerve regeneration
post-injury. Furthermore, investigations into its potential to improve cognitive function
and memory underscore its versatility as a therapeutic agent. Numerous clinical studies
have demonstrated its therapeutic potential in treating (PD) patients.

Talineuren (TLN) represents a novel approach to harnessing the therapeutic benefits of
GM1. TLN is a liposomal formulation, comprising GM1 as its pharmacologically active
ingredient, which is expected to cross the blood-brain barrier more efficiently as free
GM1 and therefore is able to deliver more GM1 to the brain. This innovative composition
is designed to optimize the neuroprotective effects of GM1.

Study Description:

This study is designed as a double-blinded, placebo-controlled trial with open-label
placebo-to-active switch to evaluate the efficacy of TLN in PD management. The
investigational Medicinal Product (IMP), TLN, is weekly intravenously administered a
maximum of 48 times as an add-on therapy alongside patients' current standard-of-care PD
medication. Talineuren, encapsulating GM1 within liposomes, is anticipated to facilitate
enhanced delivery and bioavailability of the neuroprotective agent, GM1.

Objectives:

The primary objective is to estimate differences between PD patients treated with
Talineuren and those receiving placebo regarding their average MDS-UPDRS part III score
in the defined "off" medication state (part of the Levodopa challenge test (LCT); motor
symptoms evaluated by a physician) over a study period of 6 months.

Secondary objectives are to obtain the change from baseline and variance of TLN and
placebo and compare these between the groups for :

  -  MDS-UPDRS total score (part I + part II + part III "off" + part IV)

  -  MDS-UPDRS part I (non-motor symptoms in daily life)

  -  MDS-UPDRS part II (motor symptoms in daily life)

  -  MDS-UPDRS part III "on medication"

  -  MDS-UPDRS total part IV (motor complications)

  -  Proportion of patients meeting or exceeding the minimum clinically important
     difference (MCID) in motor and non-motor symptoms (MDS-UPDRS) and quality of life
     (PDQ-39) over time

  -  Quality of life (PDQ-39, EQ-5D-5L)

  -  Mental condition (MoCA)

  -  Parkinson medication (LEDD)

  -  Digital biomarker: voice analysis

Research objectives (biomarkers):

-Assessment of literature-described biomarkers (prognostic, predictive, monitoring and/or
response biomarkers) pre- and post-TLN or placebo intervention.

Through evaluation and statistical analysis, this study seeks to elucidate the
therapeutic potential of TLN in addressing the multifaceted challenges of Parkinson's
disease. By providing insights into treatment efficacy, medication usage, symptom
management, and quality of life improvements, our findings aim to inform future
advancements in PD management and enhance patient care.

The results of this pilot study are essential for the sample size calculation of a
subsequent larger phase II/III trial.

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

Arm groups
  -
    Label: Talineuren
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description
      Participants receive standard of care PD treatment + Talineuren i.v. weekly starting with
      a dose escalation of 12 mg, 60 mg and 360 mg Talineuren followed by 720 mg Talineuren for
      21 weeks (24 infusions, 24 weeks in total) during the double-blind phase. Participants
      may continue in an open-label extension phase and receive 720 mg of Talineuren i.v.
      weekly for additional 24 infusions (24 weeks).
    Intervention names
      - Drug: Talineuren
  -
    Label: Placebo
    Type: PLACEBO_COMPARATOR
    Description
      Participants receive standard of care PD treatment + placebo (0.9% NaCl) i.v. weekly for
      24 infusions (24 weeks) in the double-blind phase. Participants may continue in an
      open-label extension phase and will receive Talineuren i.v. weekly starting with a dose
      escalation of 12 mg, 60 mg and 360 mg Talineuren followed by 720 mg Talineuren for 21
      infusions (24 infusions, 24 weeks in total).
    Intervention names
      - Drug: Placebo

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Movement Disorder Society Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale score
    Description
      The Movement Disorder Society Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS) will
      be used by the patient and physician to evaluate various symptoms of Parkinson disease
      including non-motor and motor experiences of daily living and motor complications.

      The MDS-UPDRS Scale consists of 4 parts:

        -  Part 1 (Nonmotor aspects of experiences of daily living) with 13 items.

        -  Part 2 (Motor aspects of experiences of daily living) with 13 items.

        -  Part 3 (Motor examination) with 18 items.

        -  Part 4 (Motor complications) with 6 items.

      Each item is rated with 0=normal, 1=slight, 2=mild, 3=moderate, 4=severe. The lower the
      score, the fewer / less severe the symptoms.
    Time frame: Baseline, week 25 (primary endpoint)
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Parkinson disease Quality of Life Questionnaire score
    Description
      Parkinson disease Quality of Life Questionnaire (PDQ-39) will be completed by the
      patient. The proportion of patients reaching or exceeding the minimal clinical important
      difference (MCID) will be assessed.

      This questionnaire consists of 39 items in 8 dimensions with 0 = perfect health, 100 =
      worse health.
    Time frame: Baseline, week 25, week 49
  -
    Measure: Montreal Cognitive Assessment score
    Description
      Patient's mental condition is assessed using the Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA) by
      the physician together with the patient where the patient can reach a score of 0-30. A
      final total score of 26 and above is considered normal.
    Time frame: Baseline, week 25, week 49
  -
    Measure: Change in Levodopa equivalent daily dose
    Description: Levodopa equivalent daily dose (LEDD) in [mg] will documented at each study visit.
    Time frame: Through study completion, an average of 56 weeks
  -
    Measure: Movement Disorder Society Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale score
    Description
      The Movement Disorder Society Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS) will
      be used by the patient and physician to evaluate various symptoms of Parkinson disease
      including non-motor and motor experiences of daily living and motor complications.

      The MDS-UPDRS Scale consists of 4 parts:

        -  Part 1 (Nonmotor aspects of experiences of daily living) with 13 items.

        -  Part 2 (Motor aspects of experiences of daily living) with 13 items.

        -  Part 3 (Motor examination) with 18 items.

        -  Part 4 (Motor complications) with 6 items.

      Each item is rated with 0=normal, 1=slight, 2=mild, 3=moderate, 4=severe. The lower the
      score, the fewer / less severe the symptoms.
    Time frame: Baseline, week 5, 9, 13, 17, 21, 25, 29, 33, 37, 41, 45, 49, 53
  -
    Measure: EQ-5D-5L
    Description
      The EQ-5D-5L is a standardized, preference-based instrument used to assess health-related
      quality of life in clinical studies. It comprises five dimensions: mobility, self-care,
      usual activities, pain/discomfort, and anxiety/depression. Each dimension is rated on
      five levels of severity ranging from no problems to extreme problems. This structure
      allows for a detailed description of a participant's health state and improves
      sensitivity compared to earlier versions. Health states are converted into a single
      utility index using country-specific value sets, enabling use in economic evaluations
      such as cost-utility analyses. In addition, the instrument includes a visual analogue
      scale, where participants rate their overall health on a scale from 0 to 100.
    Time frame: Baseline, week 25, week 49
  -
    Measure: Change from Baseline in Voice-Based Disease Severity Prediction Score
    Description
      Speech samples will be recorded using the Virtuosis AI voice assessment platform. At each
      assessment, participants will provide a speech recording of at least 30 seconds in
      response to a standardized question. Automated algorithms will quantify speech features
      related to voice quality, prosody, articulation, fluency, and speech rate. Based on raw
      acoustic measures (e.g., dB and Hz), corresponding z-scores, and their position relative
      to Parkinson's disease-specific thresholds, an overall voice score will be generated.
      Changes in the overall voice score over time will be assessed.
    Time frame: Baseline to week 53 (measured twice weekly)
Other outcomes

    Measure: Number of IMP-related adverse events
    Description
      Incidence and severity of treatment-emergent AEs (TEAEs) from the start of the treatment
      until the safety visit (week 56) using CTCAE criteria (V6.0).

           procedures of the trial that can be improved for the subsequent trial.

        -  Failure proportion of IMP-administration: Proportion of visits that need to be
           rescheduled because no vein for IMP-administration is found.
    Time frame: Screening to week 53
  -
    Measure: Mean values of Biomarkers
    Description

      at baseline and end of intervention, and to compare the obtained values in between
      treatment groups
    Time frame: Baseline, week 25, week 49
  -
    Measure: Number of Participants With Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs)
    Description
      Treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) will be assessed from the start of treatment
      until the primary endpoint visit at Week 25. TEAEs will be summarized by incidence and
      severity.
    Time frame: From the date of the first administration (week 1) to primary endpoint visit (25 weeks)
  -
    Measure: Absolute Neutrophil Count (ANC)
    Description

      during, and after infusions to assess changes in neutrophil levels associated with
      treatment.
    Time frame: Week 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 13, 17, 21

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Informed consent as documented by signature.

    -  Male and female subjects, aged 30 to 85 years.

    -  Confirmed PD according to British brain bank criteria.

    -  Hoehn and Yahr Stage 0 - 2.5 on medication and on stimulation (DBS, if applicable).

    -  Stable dopaminergic PD treatment (including DBS) for a month at least.

    -  Absence of dementia confirmed by cognitive testing (MoCA ≥24).

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Previous treatment with Talineuren (i.e. participants from NEON trial are not
       allowed)

    -  Contraindications to the drug classes of standard of care medication and the
       investigational medicinal product, (e.g., known hypersensitivity or allergy) and
       contraindications as listed in the product information of marketed products.

    -  Known / suspected diagnosis of hereditary fructose intolerance.

    -  Prior Campylobacter-jejuni-gastroenteritis or prior occurrence of
       Guillain-Barré-Syndrome.

    -  Women who are pregnant or breast feeding, or planning to become pregnant during the
       course of the trial or in the 12 weeks following the trial.

    -  Lack of safe contraception, defined as: Female participants of childbearing
       potential, not willing to use double method of contraception (hormonal and
       mechanical) for the entire study duration. Note: Female participants who are
       surgically sterilised / hysterectomised or post-menopausal for longer than 2 years
       are not considered as being of child bearing potential.

  Male participants, not using and not willing to use a medically reliable method of
  contraception for the entire study duration, such as condoms or who are not using any
  other method considered sufficiently reliable by the investigator in individual cases.

    -  Inability to follow the procedures of the trial, e.g., due to language problems,
       psychological disorders etc. of the participant.

    -  Participation in another trial with an investigational drug within the 30 days
       preceding and during the present trial.

    -  Enrolment of the investigator, his/her family members, employees and other dependent
       persons.

    -  Subject has an atypical parkinsonian syndrome or secondary parkinsonism.

    -  Patients in adjustment of deep brain stimulation (DBS) parameters

    -  Patients with clinically significant concomitant diseases (e.g., renal failure,
       hepatic dysfunction, cardiovascular disease, infections, etc.) that are not under
       stable control.

    -  Known or suspected non-compliance, drug or alcohol abuse.

    -  Patients with comorbidity that may interfere with the course of the trial.

    -  Patients with an established diagnosis of clinically relevant bone marrow
       dysfunction affecting leukopoiesis or with active hematological malignancies or with
       a clinically relevant immunodeficiency.

    -  Concomitant use of myelotoxic (e.g. cytostatic agents) and/or other drugs with
       clinically relevant immunosuppressive effects (e.g. calcineurin inhibitors) and/or
       drugs associated with a clinically relevant impairment of granulopoiesis.

    -  Patients who are not considered to be eligible to participate in clinical trial by
       the investigator.
