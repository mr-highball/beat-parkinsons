BRIEF SUMMARY

Compares the patients' scores of UPDRS-III before and six-month after receiving deep
brain stimulation for Parkinson's disease under MAC or AAA anesthesia.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Compares the patients' scores of UPDRS-III before and six-month after receiving deep
brain stimulation for Parkinson's disease under MAC or AAA anesthesia. Besides, the
research also compares the adverse events, LED, dosage of anesthetic drugs, wake-up time,
surgical time, Hoehn&Yahr, PQD-39, MMSE, MoCA, HAMA, HAMD and patients' satisfaction
between the two groups mentioned above.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Age greater than 18 years old

    -  Diagnosis of primary Parkinson's disease

    -  Parkinson's disease-related symptoms remain difficult to control with appropriate
       anti-parkinsonian medications.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Refusal to Participate in the research

    -  Patients with severe comorbidities such as severe sleep apnea syndrome and metal
       disorder

    -  Previous functional neurosurgery: DBS, thalamic incision, etc.
