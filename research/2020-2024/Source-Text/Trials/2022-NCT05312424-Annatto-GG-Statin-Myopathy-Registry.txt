Title: Annatto-derived GG for Statin-associated Myopathy
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov
URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05312424
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05312424
Registry ID: NCT05312424
First published: 2022
Authors: Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center Investigators
Journal: ClinicalTrials.gov
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Recruiting triple-blind parallel 3-arm RCT (target n=95; started 2022-07-15) comparing placebo (300 mg olive oil), low-dose annatto-derived geranylgeraniol (150 mg/d GG), and high-dose GG (300 mg/d) for 3 months in adults with statin-associated muscle symptoms. Primary outcomes: Statin-Associated Muscle Symptom Clinical Index (SAMS-CI: muscle pain, weakness, tiredness, cramps) and Brief Pain Inventory (BPI); secondary outcomes include loaded 50-m walk and lower-body strength. No results posted.
