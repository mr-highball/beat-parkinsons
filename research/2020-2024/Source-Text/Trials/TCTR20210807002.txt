TCTR20210807002 — Efficacy and Safety of Medical Cannabis in Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/export/xml/TCTR20210807002
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Coverage: Selected official XML fields, summarized as plain text. Personal contact details omitted.

Registry first posted: 2021-08-07.
Enrollment start: 2020-06-01. Completion: 2020-09-11.
Recruitment status: Completed. Actual enrollment: 15. Completed: 13.
Arms: Eight assigned to medical cannabis oil with CBD and THC in equal proportions. Seven assigned to rice bran oil placebo with matched appearance and titration. Duration up to six weeks.
Outcomes: Total MDS-UPDRS was primary. Part III motor examination, Part IV motor complications and safety interview were secondary.
Registry result: Reported mean score changes 47.3 and 49.3 in active and placebo groups, with p = 0.461. Two active arm withdrawals related to side effects and one possibly related acute kidney injury were noted.
The adverse event field mentions drowsiness, dizziness and dry mouth without by-arm counts. Refining grade, dose in grams and the active carrier are unreported.
The registry results date field is 2021-02-15, earlier than registration. The May 2021 conference abstract is the independently dated public report and gives a different total MDS-UPDRS p-value.
Individual participant data: Registry describes future release on IPDShare after publication. No dataset or protocol link was provided.
