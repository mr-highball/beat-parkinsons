2020 — Posted blood pressure values for the tarragon capsule trial

Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02330341?tab=results
Identifier: NCT02330341

Official results first posted October 8, 2020. At week 12 the registry gives mean systolic values of 113 ± 11 mmHg for extract and 125 ± 8 mmHg for placebo. The reported p values are explicitly respective within-arm baseline-to-endpoint comparisons (p=.017 and .082), without a baseline-adjusted between-arm estimate. The posture was not described in the outcome field, and orthostatic lightheadedness was unmeasured. The registry also lists constipation among nonserious adverse events in 1 of 12 extract participants and 2 of 12 placebo participants. These data update the same study family as the 2016 report.
