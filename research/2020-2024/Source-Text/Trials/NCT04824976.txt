The registry describes a six-week-period crossover comparing freeze-dried blueberry powder and placebo in adults with functional gastrointestinal disorders. The registered primary measure is the Gastrointestinal Symptom Rating Scale (GSRS). The registry lists 55 estimated enrollees, unknown current status, and no posted results. PMID 37242279 names this NCT identifier and reports 43 evaluable participants. The paper calls global relief primary and provides the constipation GSRS result separately. Its signed difference is -0.3 points (95% CI -0.7 to 0.2), p=0.19. The preparation is whole freeze-dried fruit powder, and the study does not specify a Parkinson cohort. Both documents describe one trial family.
