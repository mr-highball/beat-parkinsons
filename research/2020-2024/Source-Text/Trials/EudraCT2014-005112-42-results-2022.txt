Text coverage: original factual transcription and summary of selected public reporting fields.

The structured report names oral safflower oil as the placebo, 1 g/kg/day, and triheptanoin as the other oil. The title and blinding description identify the six-month randomized comparison and six-month open-label triheptanoin phase. The comparator-arm description also refers to the external REV-HD placebo group NCT02336633 for a separate one-year comparison.

The only structured result endpoint is six-month caudate atrophy. Both value cells display 50 alongside a result type of number (not applicable). This is not a usable caudate change estimate. The statistical section describes the prespecified ANCOVA and lists a less-than-or-equal-to 0.05 field. The later primary article provides the six-month effect estimate and p=0.723.

The safety table describes systematic adverse-event collection and displays a 48 hour timeframe. Serious adverse events are recorded as 0/50 and 0/50. Diarrhea is the only listed nonserious event, 2/50 and 2/50. The table displays a 5% reporting threshold. The report has no separate motor, apathy, gait, fall, speech or swallowing outcome. The 2024 paper reports 86 one-year completers, four serious adverse events unrelated to the trial product and 330 nonserious events across the full study. The registry display calls 50 per arm completed and supplies different event counts. Use the paper and registry as separate reports within one study, keeping the observation windows and comparator label ambiguity visible.
