The completed dose-range trial reports 60 enrolled and randomized capsules of placebo or PeptiSleep at 250, 500 or 1,000 mg/day. Registry primary outcome is safety and tolerability via adverse event reporting. Wearable estimates of time in light, deep and REM sleep are secondary among multiple outcomes. No dream enactment, REM muscle tone measurement or Parkinson subgroup is identified. A related 2026 article presents a subset of the original cohort.
