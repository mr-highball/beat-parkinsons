Brewed lemon balm and depression in a registered crossover
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000062988
Coverage: official registry factual snapshot
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Official study UMIN000055134, first public 2024-08-02. Planned enrollment 42 adult women with premenstrual symptoms. Registered intervention 120 mL lemon balm tea daily versus hot water in a crossover. Daily PMS symptoms are primary. DASS-21 depression, anxiety and stress are secondary. Final follow-up is recorded as 2025-01-31. The 2026-11-17 check verified that the published results field says unpublished and the actual enrollment, participant flow and numerical outcome fields remain blank. Register history: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_his_list.cgi?recptno=R000062988.

Hoshi University’s ethics working-group record dated 2024-05-15 approves amendment 2024-12, referencing original protocol 2023-25 for the same crossover. This supplies protocol history. The national trials mirror describes the same registered study. No identity-matched primary outcome paper was retrieved from the institution’s repository. Official ethics record: https://www.hoshi.ac.jp/laboratory/ethic_result/. Registry mirror: https://rctportal.mhlw.go.jp/en/detail?trial_id=UMIN000055134.
