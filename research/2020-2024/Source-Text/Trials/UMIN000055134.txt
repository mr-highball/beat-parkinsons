Brewed lemon balm and depression in a registered crossover
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000062988
Coverage: official registry factual snapshot
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Official study UMIN000055134, first public 2024-08-02. Planned enrollment 42 female students with premenstrual symptoms. Registered intervention 120 mL lemon balm tea daily versus hot water in a crossover. DASS-21 depression, anxiety and stress are secondary outcomes. Final follow-up is recorded as 2025-01-31. As of 2026-11-15 the results are listed as unpublished and the results fields are blank. Register history: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_his_list.cgi?recptno=R000062988.
