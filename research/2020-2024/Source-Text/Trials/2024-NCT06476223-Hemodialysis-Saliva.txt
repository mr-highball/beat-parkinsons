Official hemodialysis saliva registration

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06476223

First posted June 26, 2024. The crossover design compares a cumin-ginger artificial saliva and placebo in people with hemodialysis-associated xerostomia. Protocol 0763/66 matches the later 2026 report's review-board identifier and design. The US registry showed Unknown recruitment status and no posted results. The primary report describes the clinical result separately.
