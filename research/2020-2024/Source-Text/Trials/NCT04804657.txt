ARMS AND INTERVENTIONS

The active arm description lists two 600 mg Cognivia capsules before cycling. The intervention description lists an aqueous Salvia officinalis extract and a preparation containing Salvia lavandulaefolia essential oil encapsulated with gum acacia. The comparator used visually similar maltodextrin capsules.

OUTCOMES

Primary: Percent correct on a reverse-digit immediate memory test at stages of the cycling protocol.
Secondary: Heart rate, 1–10 rating of perceived exertion, reaction time, Stroop accuracy and Stroop reaction time.
