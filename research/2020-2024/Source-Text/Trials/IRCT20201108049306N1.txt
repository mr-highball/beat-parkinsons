Title: Evaluation of the effect of ginger as an adjunct therapy along with SSRIs to reduce anxiety in patients with generalized anxiety disorder
Registry ID: IRCT20201108049306N1
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/53072
URL: https://en.irct.ir/trial/53072
Registration date: 2021-01-29
Coverage: Selected official English trial fields. Rechecked November 20, 2026.

The planned 80 adults with generalized anxiety were allocated to ginger rhizome powder capsules or starch capsules in addition to sertraline or citalopram. The planned Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale assessments were at baseline and days 14, 28, 56 and 84. The registration establishes the planned measurement, without numerical group results. The matching journal article DOI is 10.2174/0126660822258340231025171755.

The official English record displays recruitment complete and identifies registration as prospective. The registration and last-update labels both show January 29, 2021. No group outcome values are posted in the verified English record. The corresponding Persian registry request timed out during this recheck.
