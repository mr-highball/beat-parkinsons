Registration: IRCT20201108049306N1, ginger alongside SSRIs in generalized anxiety disorder
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/53072
Registration date: 2021-01-29
Coverage: Selected official trial fields. Reviewed November 15, 2026.

The planned 80 adults with generalized anxiety were allocated to ginger rhizome powder capsules or starch capsules in addition to sertraline or citalopram. The planned Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale assessments were at baseline and days 14, 28, 56 and 84. The registration establishes the planned measurement, without numerical group results. The matching journal article DOI is 10.2174/0126660822258340231025171755.
