Title: Dietary Supplement to Alleviate Stress in Subjects With Mild to Moderate Stress
Registry ID: NCT06672965
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06672965
First posted: 2024-11-04
Data snapshot: 2026-11-17, ClinicalTrials.gov official API.

The registry lists actual conduct June 19 to July 30, 2023, 60 enrolled, randomized parallel allocation, and participant, investigator and outcome assessor masking. It was submitted October 21, 2024, after study completion. The comparison is the capsule combination of lemon balm extract, magnolia and L-theanine versus placebo. The 30-day primary outcomes include Hamilton anxiety and depression scores and perceived stress. Mood states and restorative sleep are secondary. No results are posted to this registry.

Hester et al. identify this record in their 2025 journal paper. Several descriptions differ, including trial dates, the older conference masking description and stated depression scale range. The registration is a historical record of the specified design and outcomes.
