BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a, open-label, single-arm 8-week investigation of levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone
in the treatment of early Parkinson's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This study will enroll subjects who have a diagnosis of PD with Hoehn-Yahr stage 1.5-3.0
and assess the impact of low dosage of levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone treatment on the
motor function and quality of life among PD patients.

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

Arm groups
  -
    Label: levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description
      levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone is a combination drug consisting of levodopa, carbidopa,
      and entacapone. Each tablet contains a 1:4 ratio of carbidopa to levodopa and 200 mg of
      entacapone. The optimum daily dosage of levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone must be determined
      by careful titration in each patient.
    Intervention names
      - Drug: levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone
Interventions
  -
    Type: DRUG
    Name: levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone
    Description
      On Day 1, subjects will be treated with levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone. Based on previous
      regimen. The dosage of levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone will be equal to the previous total
      daily dose of LD, and the frequency of administration of levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone is
      three or four times a day.

      Levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone could be titrated in next 2 weeks according to the
      investigator's judgment, based on individual clinical response and tolerability.

      After 2-week titration, the dose of levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone will remain as constant
      as possible to the end of study.
    Arm group labels
      - levodopa/carbidopa/entacapone
    Other names
      - Stalevo

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change From Baseline in Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS) III Score
    Description
      The UPDRS is a standardized assessment scale used to measure the patient's disease state.
      UPDRS Part III measures the motor function of the patient. A higher score indicates
      greater disability.
    Time frame: Baseline, Week 8
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change From Baseline in UPDRS II Score
    Description
      The UPDRS is a standardized assessment scale used to measure the patient's disease state.
      UPDRS Part II measures the patient's activities of daily living. A higher score indicates
      greater disability.
    Time frame: Baseline, Week 8
  -
    Measure: Change From Baseline in Health-related Quality of Life Assessed Using the 39-item Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire (PDQ-39)
    Description
      The PDQ-39 instrument is used to assess quality of life in individuals with PD. A lower
      score indicates better quality of life.
    Time frame: Baseline, Week 8
  -
    Measure: Incidence of dyskinesia and wearing off
    Description
      Dyskinesia and wearing off are common motor complications. A motor complications patient
      questionnaire will be record.
    Time frame: Baseline, Week 8

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Male or female and greater from 30 to 80.

    -  Diagnosis of idiopathic PD according to the MDS PD Diagnostic Criteria (2015).

    -  Hoehn-Yahr stage of 1.5-3.0 ("open" stage).

    -  Not on anti-PD medication or stable on anti-PD medication for at least 30 days.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Diagnosis of atypical Parkinsonian syndrome, vascular Parkinsonism or drug-induced
       Parkinsonism.

    -  History of surgery within 6 months.

    -  Alcoholism, drug abuse, or severe cognitive impairment (including severe Alzheimer's
       disease)

    -  Psychiatric illness, epilepsy, pregnancy and breastfeeding, and clinically
       significant concomitant illnesses

    -  Participation in another clinical trial within 2 months.

    -  With dyskinesia.

    -  Any other condition that, in the opinion of the investigator, makes them ineligible
       for enrolment.
