Official US registration and arm descriptions

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04937530

The US entry first posted June 24, 2021 and specifies 960 mg oral safflower oil capsules in the comparator arm of a randomized RT001 study. The primary endpoint is change in the 28-item progressive supranuclear palsy rating scale at 48 weeks. This composite contains gait-related items. The registration supplies neither a separate walking-speed result nor the oil pressing method. The US entry shows Unknown recruitment status and no posted results. The matching EU protocol provides an earlier public record.
