Title: Evaluation of safety and effect of Anethum graveolens Linn. (Dill) extract beverage against stress in working age volunteers

Selected public fields, checked November 14, 2026. The study enrolls adults aged 20–55 with Thai DASS-21 above 10. The registered arms consist of six weeks of daily placebo beverage or dill extract beverage at either of two doses. The Thai DASS-21 is the primary outcome at six weeks. Secondary outcomes include the Thai Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, mood questionnaire, physical fitness, working memory, event-related potentials and laboratory markers. The registry did not name an individual fatigue scale, a finger dexterity task, the botanical part, or a fresh or dried culinary leaf preparation. The structured results summary, outcomes, posting date and result URL are empty.
