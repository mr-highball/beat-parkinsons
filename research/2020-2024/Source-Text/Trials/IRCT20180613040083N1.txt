Title: Evaluation of lemon balm capsules on cognitive impairment in depressed patients treated with ECT
Registry ID: IRCT20180613040083N1
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/58948
First registered: 2022-06-10.
Text coverage: Official IRCT fields and matched original publication.

The registry targets 80 adults with depression undergoing electroconvulsive therapy. It specifies lemon balm capsules against placebo and a primary Mini-Mental State Examination cognitive score. Its official reader shows recruitment complete and gives no actual dates; the WHO mirror still showed recruiting. The registry does not specify a separate measure of depressive symptom severity.

Nikfarjam et al. identify this trial on printed page 99 of the 2025 journal article, DOI 10.34172/jsums.931. The study reports 70 analyzed, 35 per arm. The registered and published records are one comparison.
