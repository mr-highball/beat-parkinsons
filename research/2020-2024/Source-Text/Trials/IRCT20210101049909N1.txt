IRCT20210101049909N1 | Thyrophyte herbal composition in primary hypothyroidism
Official source: https://irct.ir/trial/56663
First public registration: 2021-06-18, prospective as displayed.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13. Selected English registry fields summarized.

The registered parallel comparison targeted 80 overweight adults with primary hypothyroidism in Mashhad. It compared levothyroxine plus a mixture containing Nigella sativa, ajwain and lime against levothyroxine plus placebo over eight weeks. The registry includes weight, biochemical variables, Beck depression score and WHO quality of life. Recruitment is listed as complete. The publication is PMID 39086868.
