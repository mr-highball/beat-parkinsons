Official frailty trial registration and arm description

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06645847

First posted October 17, 2024. The registered placebo contains canola oil and is compared with a ketone ester in older adults with frailty. Protocol endpoints include a fatigue scale and leg-press fatigue testing. The record shows Recruiting and no posted results. It supplies no oil grade, zero-oil comparison or Parkinson enrollment.
