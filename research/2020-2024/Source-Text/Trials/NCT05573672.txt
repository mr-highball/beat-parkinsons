Registration: NCT05573672, Dietary Counselling Plus Omega-3 Supplementation in the Treatment of Generalized Anxiety Disorder
First posted: 2022-10-10
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05573672
Coverage: Selected official design and outcome fields, reviewed November 15, 2026.
The trial lists an August 12, 2022 start and randomized parallel comparison between individualized counseling plus omega-3 and a waitlist. The feasibility trial measured anxiety. No separately assigned or consumed toasted-cumin comparison is described. The same study's first official results submission became public April 4, 2025.
