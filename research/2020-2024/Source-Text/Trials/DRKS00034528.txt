Title: PD Diet Pilot Study
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00034528/details
Registration: DRKS00034528
First public registration and last update: 2024-06-27
Depth: Factual summary of the official English and German registry record.

The retrospectively registered intervention assigned a high-fiber diet with daily apples to people with mild Parkinson disease and their unaffected household partners. Participants also received lactulose, at a higher amount in the Parkinson group. The nonrandomized record reports 22 enrolled, with a target sample field of 100. It lists UPDRS III motor function and gastrointestinal outcomes as secondary. There is no separately identified rest-tremor item or apple-only arm. The registry's results field was empty at the November 17, 2026 check. The 2024 preprint and 2025 paper identify the same registration. The public data plan describes request-only access beyond specified minimal and laboratory data.
