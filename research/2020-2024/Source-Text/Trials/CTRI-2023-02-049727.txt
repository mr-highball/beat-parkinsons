Coverage: local plain-text description of original public registry fields. Outcome results are reported separately in DOI 10.3389/fnut.2026.1892442.

The randomized open-label study planned to compare 60 grams of whole raw almonds per day in divided portions with isocaloric millet or pulse snacks. Both groups were planned for six months. The target was 208 participants. Participants were adults with an elevated Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index or Epworth Sleepiness Scale score. The inclusion range was 30–55 years.

The listed exclusions included parasomnias, several other major sleep disorders, cognitive impairment and major neurological disorders. The main endpoint was sleep efficiency from baseline to endpoint. Planned secondary sleep endpoints included polysomnographic REM latency and percentage of sleep in REM, Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, Epworth Sleepiness Scale and actigraphic sleep duration in a subset. Dream enactment was not a listed outcome.

This registration is the same study family as the 2026 Desai publication. The publication describes five months of intervention and adults aged 21–55. The article's abstract reports 175 participants, and its CONSORT figure depicts 209 randomized with 175 in the analysis.
