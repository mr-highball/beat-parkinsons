Title: Enhancement of calmness and mood following supplementation with lemon balm during periods of cognitive demand in an adult population
Registry ID: NCT06183372
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT06183372
Protocol and analysis plan: https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/72/NCT06183372/Prot_SAP_000.pdf
First posted: 2023-12-27. The registry records a 2023-12-02 actual start and the attached protocol is dated 2023-10-30.
Text coverage: Official registration, complete four-page protocol, and original 2026 paper linkage.

Lemcog compared a single lemon balm extract capsule with placebo in adults with moderate subjective stress. The primary measure was a nine-point calmness rating at baseline and five hours. Secondary measures include mental fatigue, temporary mood ratings, recall, task switching, attention and cardiovascular outcomes. Pages 3–4 of the plan specify the overall calmness endpoint and exploratory cognitive and mood endpoints. The record gives 123 enrolled, completion in July 2024, and no posted registry results. The published allocation and analysis include 106 people. The endpoint list contains no separate depressive symptom score.

Original result: DOI 10.1177/20451253261415706. See research/2025-2029/Source-Text/2026-PMID41782777-Mathews-Lemcog-Extract.txt.
