IRCT20200217046523N17 | Nigella extract adjunct in major depression
Official source: https://irct.ir/trial/56618
First public registration: 2021-06-19, prospective as displayed.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13. Selected English registry fields summarized.

The parallel trial targeted 50 adults with major depression already receiving sertraline in Tehran. It assigned nigella extract or starch placebo in addition to sertraline. Registered outcomes were DASS-21 depression and serum BDNF, measured before and at six weeks. Recruitment is listed as complete. The 2022 publication reports 52 participants and a ten-week assessment.
