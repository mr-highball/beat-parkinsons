Title: Thyme among inhaled olfactory training odorants
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05855369
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

NCT05855369. First posted May 11, 2023. Snapshot last posted April 2, 2026. Recruiting in the official record.
The long-COVID smell rehabilitation protocol uses a series of 16 odorants including thyme. Olfaction is primary and sleepiness and cognition are secondary. The exposure is sniffed multicomponent training. It does not assign fresh culinary thyme consumption.
