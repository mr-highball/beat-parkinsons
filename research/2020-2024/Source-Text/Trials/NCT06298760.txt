NCT06298760 | Reflexology or knee massage with black cumin oil and fatigue
Official source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06298760
First public posting: 2024-03-07. Latest public update: 2024-03-12.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13. Selected official API fields summarized.

The completed, unmasked randomized comparison lists actual enrollment of 150 people with knee osteoarthritis. Its detailed design lists five groups of 30, including nigella oil and placebo procedures for reflexology and knee massage, and a routine care group. Follow-up was six weeks. The registered fatigue measure was the Fatigue Severity Scale. No numerical results are posted to ClinicalTrials.gov. The publication is PMID 39642267.
