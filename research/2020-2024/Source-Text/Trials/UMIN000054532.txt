The effect of test foods containing plant extracts on cognitive function

The registry describes a randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind parallel comparison, with 40 participants listed. The primary outcome is standardized composite memory at 24 weeks. Secondary outcomes include other cognitive domains, MoCA-J, a dementia checklist, the MIRUDAKE eye-tracking assessment and CogEvo at 12 and 24 weeks. The registration uses a general plant-extract description. The separate July 2026 primary paper identifies the test capsules as a mixture of Curcuma longa extracts.

This plain-text snapshot records selected official protocol and results-availability fields.
