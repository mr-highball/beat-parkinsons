Title: Investigating the effect of Melissa officinalis on stress, anxiety, depression and sleep quality of elderly women
Registry ID: IRCT20150519022320N32
Source: https://fa.irct.ir/trial/71909
URL: https://en.irct.ir/trial/71909
First registered: 2023-08-11.
Text coverage: Official Persian and English registry versions and WHO mirror.

The registration describes a four-week randomized unblinded comparison in a planned 90 older women. The Persian title names lemon balm and the intervention uses a variant spelling of the Persian common name for lemon balm in the description of 400 mg branded capsules. The English record and WHO mirror instead say cucumber in the intervention field. Starch is the comparator. The original Persian wording supports lemon balm as the intended exposure. The product composition has not been verified beyond the registry.

The register lists psychological and sleep questionnaires, including depression, without a separately identified instrument or numerical result for that outcome. It marks recruitment complete but gives no actual enrollment or completion date. No matching primary results were retrieved in the completed targeted searches. This is a capsule trial in older women.
