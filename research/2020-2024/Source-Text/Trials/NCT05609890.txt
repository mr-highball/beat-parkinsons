NCT05609890. Novel Formulation for Sleep Among People With Poor Sleep Quality. First posted November 8, 2022. Official study endpoint https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05609890.

The study is marked completed with 58 actual enrollment. No results module is posted. Both groups were to receive sachets. The active formulation includes saffron, tea extract, lemon balm and valerian. The registry describes inert excipients for the comparator and does not list flavorings. Primary outcome is change in sleep efficiency across six weeks. Secondary outcomes include PSQI, SF-36, salivary cortisol, creatinine and ALT. There is no outcome for dream enactment or REM without atonia.

The linked 2024 primary report PMID 38580720 identifies 64 randomized and 58 completers. It specifies lemongrass, peppermint and mint flavors in comparator material and lists adverse events. The publication and registration represent one trial.
