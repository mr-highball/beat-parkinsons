Title: Fatty Acid Modulation of Brain Function in Older Adults
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06192407
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT06192407
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting: 2024-01-05
Last posted update: 2026-11-04
Status: Recruiting at verification. Target enrollment 60. No posted results.
Design: Diet crossover in healthy adults aged 65–75. The high-oleic supplement is entirely hazelnut oil. The high-palmitic blend contains palm, peanut and virgin olive oils. Each experimental diet follows a controlled run-in.
Outcomes: N-back working memory accuracy, task-related fMRI activation and resting-state functional connectivity after each diet. The record provides no Parkinson-specific endpoint.
