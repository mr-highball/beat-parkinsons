Title: Investigation of Benegut on Immediate GI Discomfort Relief Caused by Acute Overfeeding in Healthy Subjects
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05603416
NCT ID: NCT05603416
Format: Selected official ClinicalTrials.gov fields paraphrased for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public posting: 2022-11-02
Status: Unknown at retrieval. Last update posted 2023-03-17, recorded enrollment 30. The registry has no tabular results.
Design: Randomized and blinded crossover of a perilla extract and placebo after a large meal in healthy adults.
Outcomes: Overall gastrointestinal discomfort is primary. Individual gastrointestinal symptoms, follow-up symptoms and adverse events are secondary.
Linked reports: The 2025 preprint and 2026 paper report one cohort. Their main texts report similar bowel movements across visits without publishing the data. They test acute leaf extract and general meal discomfort.
