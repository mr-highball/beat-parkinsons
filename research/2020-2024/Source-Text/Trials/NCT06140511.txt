The public registration describes an observational cohort with and without REM sleep without atonia. Its registered primary measure is enrollment of 600 participants across 18 months. At this check the registry reports unknown recruitment status and no posted results.

The 2026 methods article identifies clinical findings to assess during phenotyping, including visual hallucinations. The registration and methods publication do not report a completed food comparison or symptom frequency following dried fig ingestion.
