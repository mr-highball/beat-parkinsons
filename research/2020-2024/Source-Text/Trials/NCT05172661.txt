BRIEF SUMMARY

The present study will characterize exercise model effects (integrated model vs.
consecutive model) of physical-cognitive exercise on dual-task walking control in
Parkinson's disease with mild cognitive impairment.

CONDITIONS

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

Arm groups
  -
    Label: Integrated training model
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description
      Participants in this group will accept postural and cognitive training at the same time,
      e.g. naming animals while standing on the foam.

      Each training session will last for 70 minutes, twice a week, and 12 sessions in total.
    Intervention names
      - Behavioral: Integrated physical-cognitive training
  -
    Label: Consecutive training model
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description
      Participants in this group will accept postural and cognitive training separately and for
      identical durations (both 30 minutes).

      Each training session will last for 70 minutes, twice a week, and 12 sessions in total.
    Intervention names
      - Behavioral: Consecutive physical-cognitive training
Interventions
  -
    Type: BEHAVIORAL
    Name: Integrated physical-cognitive training
    Description
      Perform a postural task and a cognitive task at the same time (dual-task model) 70
      minutes/session*2 sessions/week*6 weeks
    Arm group labels
      - Integrated training model
  -
    Type: BEHAVIORAL
    Name: Consecutive physical-cognitive training
    Description
      Perform a postural task and a cognitive task separately for identical durations 70
      minutes/session*2 sessions/week*6 weeks
    Arm group labels
      - Consecutive training model

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  a diagnosis of idiopathic Parkinson's disease

    -  have decreased cognitive functions but do not interfere with functional independence

    -  Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA): 21-25

    -  able to walk independently without walking devices for at least 10 meters and with
       the ability to turn 180°

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  a diagnosis of dementia

    -  other diseases that may influence cognitive functions or walking performance

    -  a history of brain surgery

    -  modification of the medication during the exercise intervention
Healthy volunteers: No
