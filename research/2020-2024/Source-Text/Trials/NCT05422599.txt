Title: Evaluation of a Lemon Balm Extract on Sleep Quality in Healthy Adults
Registry ID: NCT05422599
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05422599
First posted: 2022-06-16
Data snapshot: 2026-11-17, ClinicalTrials.gov official API.

The registry lists 99 actual participants with sleep complaints, completed status and no posted results. It compares 300 mg aqueous lemon balm extract capsules with maltodextrin over 28 days. Sleep quality is the primary outcome. The DASS-21 depression domain is a secondary outcome.

The 2023 original conference abstract P135 names the registry and describes the study during data analysis. It provides no estimate. A 2026 author thesis abstract describes a similar four-week comparison and improvement in broad mood in both groups, without citing this registry ID or providing a separate depression contrast. The thesis body was not available through the institutional record. Confirmation of study identity and the separately registered depression result remains open.
