Status

Not yet recruiting. Estimated start December 2027. No posted results.

Study and outcomes

The single-arm trial plans daily high-linoleic-acid cookies for eight weeks in 16 adults with insomnia symptoms. The registered oil is unnamed. Outcomes include the Insomnia Severity Index, Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, PROMIS Sleep, actigraphy, sleep EEG, gait speed and grip strength.
