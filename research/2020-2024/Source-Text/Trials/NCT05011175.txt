REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Age
    Description: Average age of participants enrolled
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported age of Parkinson's Disease Diagnosis
    Description: Average age of diagnosis with Parkinson's Disease for enrolled participants.
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported education level
    Description
      Number of participants reporting 1. less than 12 years of school, 2. completed high
      school, 3. completed bachelor degree, 4. completed graduate/advanced degree.
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported living situation
    Description
      Number of participants reporting 1.living alone, 2. living with others 3. living in a
      healthcare facility
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported number of years taking medicine for Parkinson's Disease
    Description: Average number of years participants reported taking medication for Parkinson's Disease
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported brain stimulation treatment
    Description
      Number of participants reporting they have undergone deep brain stimulation for
      Parkinson's Disease.
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported stage of Parkinson's Disease
    Description
      Average reported stage of Parkinson's Disease participants (1. mild with no symptoms, 2.
      mild with symptoms, 3. moderate, 4. advanced, able to walk 5. advanced, unable to walk).
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported physical help requirement
    Description
      Number of participants reporting 1. they need help from another person during the day or
      2. they do not need help from another person during the day.
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported walking ability
    Description
      Number of participants reporting 1. ability to walk unaided, 2. ability to walk with an
      assistive device, or 3. requiring assistance to walk
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported mobility level
    Description: Participants reported mobility level ranging from independent to total assist required.
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Self-reported current exercise habits survey
    Description
      3 question survey component evaluating the exercise habits of individuals with
      Parkinson's Disease using a Likert scale from 0-5 where 5 indicated a higher levels of
      exercise.
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
  -
    Measure: Perceived levels of intensity
    Description
      7 question survey evaluating the level of intensity of exercise of individuals with
      Parkinson's Disease using a Likert scale from 0-5 where 5 indicated higher levels of
      intensity.
    Time frame: Reported at baseline
