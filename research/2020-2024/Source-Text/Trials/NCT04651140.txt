BRIEF SUMMARY

This trial is expected to recruit patients with primary Parkinson's disease and give them
four consecutive weeks of aerobic exercise to observe its effect on Parkinson's disease
and explore its mechanism

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This trial is expected to recruit patients with primary Parkinson's disease who are
distributed into tremor group and stiff group and give them the same kind of four
consecutive weeks of aerobic exercise to observe its effect on Parkinson's disease and
explore its mechanism

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Movement Disorder Society-United Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS)
    Description
      to assess the motor and nonmotor function of PD,0-200 scores,the higher scores mean lower
      function
    Time frame: changes of MDS-UPDRS score from baseline at four weeks
  -
    Measure: Montreal Cognitive Assessing(MoCA) score
    Description: to assess the cognitive function of PD,0-30 scores,the higher scores mean better function
    Time frame: changes of MoCA score from baseline at four weeks
  -
    Measure: The Epworth Sleeping Scale(ESS)
    Description: to assess the degree of drowsiness, 0-24 scores, the higher scores mean severe drowsiness
    Time frame: changes of ESS score from baseline at four weeks
  -
    Measure: Non-Motor Symptom Scale (NMSS)
    Description
      to assess the non-motor of PD , 0-120 scores, the higher scores mean severe non-motor
      damage
    Time frame: changes of NMSS score from baseline at four weeks
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: cerebral blood variation by Head MRI
    Description: to assess the cerebral blood variation by head MRI
    Time frame: changes of the cerebral blood from baseline at four weeks
  -
    Measure: cerebral blood flow velocity
    Description
      cerebral blood flow velocity is recorded using transcranial Doppler and a
      servo-controlled plethysmograph respectively.
    Time frame: changes of the cerebral blood flow velocity from baseline at four weeks
  -
    Measure: the level of arterial blood pressure
    Description
      arterial blood pressure is recorded using transcranial Doppler and a servo-controlled
      plethysmograph respectively. Both systolic and diastolic pressures will be assessed
      during the study period.
    Time frame: changes of the arterial blood pressure from baseline at four weeks
  -
    Measure: the concentration of microRNA in peripheral blood by gene chip
    Description: to test the the concentration of microRNA in peripheral blood
    Time frame: changes of microRNA from baseline at four weeks

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Participants were diagnosed primary PD according to the 2015 MDS diagnostic criteria

    -  Participants are between 55 and 65 years old

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Severe heart

    -  liver dysfunction

    -  kidney dysfunction

    -  severe cognitive dysfunction

    -  severe anxiety and depression

    -  history of mental illness

    -  history of cerebral infarction or ischemic attack in the past three months

    -  acute myocardial infarction or acute coronary in the past three months

    -  Parkinson's Pulse syndrome

    -  medication history (drugs that improve blood circulation in the brain and increase
       the excitability of the nervous system)
