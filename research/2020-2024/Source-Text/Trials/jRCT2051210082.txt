Title: Japanese phase 3 drug trial in people with chronic sialorrhea

Coverage: Selected fields from the official indexed Japanese and English registry pages and matched publication, verified 2026-11-19. Direct detail retrieval returned HTTP 403.

The public record lists first registration on 2021-09-13 and results posting on 2025-11-14. It identifies a neurological sialorrhea population and a primary outcome of change in unstimulated salivary flow at four weeks. The English record counts 50 people with Parkinson disease among 57 in the group A baseline result tabulation. The publication separately reports 58 treated in group A and 34 in the broader group B.

In the posted result, group A's adjusted salivary flow change was −0.08 g/min (95% CI −0.10 to −0.06) against a prespecified response threshold. The registry confirms the journal article describes the same clinical program. The outcome and exposure do not measure fig-associated drooling severity or date-associated pain.
