Title: Effects of consumption of the test food on the oral cavity environment in healthy subjects
Source: UMIN Clinical Trials Registry

Last registry update: 2021-11-02
Accessed: 2026-11-14
Coverage: selected public registry fields, summarized in plain text

Design: randomized, double-blind parallel-group comparison of an active chewable tablet and placebo in healthy Japanese adults. Participants were instructed to dissolve three tablets without chewing after the baseline examination, then dissolve three once daily through the four-week assessment. The registry calls the active product a chewable tablet. It does not name the botanical or its amount.
Population: healthy Japanese adults 20 or older with relatively high baseline periodontosis-related bacteria. Target enrollment 40. Actual enrollment 44.
Sponsor: ORTHOMEDICO Inc. Funder: Tokiwa Phytochemical Co., Ltd. Collaborating clinics: Horiuchi Dental Clinic, Takara Clinic and Minami-machi Clinic.
Primary outcome: count of periodontosis-related bacteria.
Secondary outcomes: periodontal assessment, caries-related bacteria, halitosis, oral moisture, saliva volume, saliva pH, high-sensitivity CRP, salivary IgA and investigator-designed questionnaires. The register gives no questionnaire item list.
Registry status: completed. The current registration lists results as unpublished, with no posted estimates or linked journal article. The history records the enrollment update in March 2021 and the final status update on November 2, 2021.

Cross-source linkage: The 2021 Kuniyoshi trial, 2022 sponsor filing G1435 and the clinical example in WO2023026502A1 align on sponsor or clinical network, endpoint pattern, four-week duration, three orally dissolved tablets and 44 participants where reported. Two reported gum P values (.028 and .038) match the patent Tables 1 and 3. The filing names Laurus nobilis leaf extract at 108 mg/day. This identifies a strong likely match. The journal results tables and the specific presence of the constipation item in them remain to be verified.
