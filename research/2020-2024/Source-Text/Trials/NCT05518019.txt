Title: Flaxseed oil as inactive ingredient in sleep study
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05518019
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

NCT05518019. First posted August 26, 2022. The record lists study completion. The hemp extract and terpene softgel lists organic flaxseed oil as an inactive component. The protocol includes self-report and wearable sleep measurements.
All described active exposure is a combined product. The oil pressing method is unspecified. The record does not assign isolated flaxseed oil.
