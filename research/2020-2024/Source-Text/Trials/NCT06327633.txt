Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06327633
First posted: 2024-03-25. Retrieved: 2026-11-15. Detailed follow-up: 2026-11-16.
Official trial registration summary. Sixty adults with type 2 diabetes and mild cognitive impairment. Open-label randomized assignment to six-odor training with rose, lemon, clove, eucalyptus, coffee and cinnamon or control. Three olfaction outcomes are threshold, identification and memory on OLFACT, and global cognition on the MoCA is primary. Completed without posted results. Two linked papers labeled as results, PMID 29500313 and 31221697, were published in 2018 and 2019, before the reported 2024 start. The 2025 Chao paper, PMID 40908892, matches the sponsor center, design, enrollment, six odors, schedule and cognition outcome. The accessible abstract supplies no registry identifier. The study evaluates inhaled odor training, not intake or single-odour effects.
