BRIEF SUMMARY

It is a phase II randomized, parallel, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multi-center
clinical trial of the efficacy and safety of WD-1603 Carbidopa-Levodopa Extended-Release
Tablets in patients with Parkinson's disease. The objective of the study is to access the
safety and efficacy of WD-1603 carbidopa-levodopa extended-release tablets in patients
with Parkinson's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Eligible subjects of the study will be randomly assigned into four groups at a ratio of
1:1:1:1: three treatment groups and one placebo group. The subjects will take trial drugs
orally three times a day, in the morning before meals, and the second and third
medications will be taken after meals, once every 5 hours.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Changes in the Sum of MDS-UPDRS-II and MDS-UPDRS-III From the Baseline to the 27th Day.
    Description
      The Movement Disorder Society-Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS)
      assesses Parkinson's disease impairment. Part II (Motor Experiences of Daily Living) has
      13 items; Part III (Motor Examination) has 33 scores from 18 items. Each item is rated
      0-4 (0=normal, 1=slight, 2=mild, 3=moderate, 4=severe). Part II total 0-52; Part III
      total 0-132. Higher scores = greater severity. Combined score = sum of Part II and III
      totals, range 0-184; higher = worse motor impairment. Change from baseline = Day 27 score
      minus baseline. Change scores range -184 to +184; negative = improvement, positive =
      worsening.
    Time frame: 27 days- from the baseline to the 27th day

    Description
      The Movement Disorder Society-Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS) Part

      slight, 2 = mild, 3 = moderate, 4 = severe). The Part III total score ranges from 0 to

      baseline is calculated separately for Day 14 and Day 27 as the score at each time point
      minus the baseline score. Change scores range from -132 to +132. A negative change
      indicates improvement in motor impairment, while a positive change indicates worsening.
    Time frame: Baseline to Day 14 and Baseline to Day 27.

  -
    Measure: AUC0-τ and AUC0-∞ of Levodopa and Carbidopa Following WD-1603 Administration on Day 28.
    Description
      Plasma concentrations of levodopa and carbidopa were measured on Day 28 at pre-dose (0
      hour) and 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 9 hours after the second dose. The area under the
      plasma concentration-time curve from time 0 to τ (AUC0-τ) was calculated using the linear
      trapezoidal method, where τ is the dosing interval of 5 hours. The area under the plasma
      concentration-time curve from time 0 extrapolated to infinity (AUC0-∞) was calculated as
      AUC0-t + Clast/λz, where AUC0-t is the AUC from time 0 to the last measurable
      concentration (Clast), and λz is the terminal elimination rate constant. AUC values are
      presented in ng*h/mL for both levodopa and carbidopa. Higher AUC values indicate greater
      systemic exposure.
    Time frame: 1 day- on the 28th day
  -
    Measure: Levodopa Plasma Concentration Fluctuation Index of WD-1603.
    Description
      Plasma levodopa concentrations were measured on Day 28 at pre-dose (0 hour) and 0.5, 1,
      2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 9 hours after the second dose. The fluctuation index was calculated as
      (Cmax - Cmin) / Cavg, where Cmax is the maximum plasma concentration, Cmin is the minimum
      plasma concentration, and Cavg is the average plasma concentration calculated as AUC0-τ /
      τ, with τ = 5 hours. A higher fluctuation index indicates greater fluctuation in plasma
      levodopa concentration.
    Time frame: Day 28: 0, 0.5,1, 2, 3, 4,5, 6, 9 hours post-dose
  -
    Measure: Adverse Events (AEs) in Patients With Parkinson's Disease Treated With WD-1603.
    Description
      Adverse events (AEs) refer to any untoward medical occurrence in participants, which may
      present as symptoms and signs, diseases, or abnormal laboratory findings, and may not
      necessarily have a causal relationship with the study drug. An event was recorded as an
      AE if at least one of the following criteria was met: (1) accompanying symptoms and
      signs; (2) examination results requiring treatment measures (such as surgical
      intervention); or (3) examination results leading to changes in the dosing schedule of
      the study drug (such as dose change, dosing delay, or discontinuation). The number and
      percentage of participants with AEs are reported.
    Time frame: 28 days-from baseline to the 28th day.
  -
    Measure: Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II) Scale of WD-1603 in Patients With Parkinson's Disease.
    Description
      BDI-II scores are collected for safety assessment. BDI-II is a 21-item self-administered
      survey which is scored on a scale of 0-3 in a list of four statements arranged in
      increasing severity about a particular symptom of depression. Total score of 0-13 is
      considered minimal range, 14-19 is mild, 20-28 is moderate, and 29-63 is severe.
    Time frame: 28 days-from baseline to the 28th day.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    1. Male/female patients with early Parkinson's disease who are over 30 years old and
       under 75 years old (including cut-off values).

    2. Able to understand and willing to sign an informed consent form (ICF) voluntarily.

    3. The diagnosis of Parkinson's disease complies with idiopathic Parkinson's disease
       (2015 version of MDS Parkinson's disease diagnostic criteria).

    4. Modified Hoehn and Yahr Scale≥1, ≤2.5 points.

    5. Agree to use medically acceptable contraceptive methods throughout the study and
       within 1 month after completing the study.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    1. Have a history of severe allergic reactions or allergies to levodopa or carbidopa.

    2. Pregnancy or breastfeeding.

    3. Diagnosed as atypical Parkinson's disease or any known secondary Parkinson's
       syndrome.

    4. The investigator believes that the placebo treatment cannot be tolerated.

    5. Acute psychosis or hallucinations, using any antipsychotic to treat psychosis or
       clinically obvious depression.

    6. History of epilepsy or epilepsy.

    7. The history of narrow-angle glaucoma.

    8. Subjects with a history of malignant melanoma.

    9. Patients with obvious cognitive impairment.

   10. The investigator believes that there are clinically significant medical or surgical
       diseases and patients who are not suitable for participating in clinical trials.
