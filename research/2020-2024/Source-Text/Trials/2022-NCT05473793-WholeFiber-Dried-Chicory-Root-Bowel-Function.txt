Official ClinicalTrials.gov v2 API inspection (Round 6 review): Parallel, double-blind, placebo-controlled 4-week trial (n=160, RECRUITING, hasResults=False, start 2022-08-22) evaluating three doses of sliced, dried chicory ROOT particles (WholeFiber, ~3 mm, high natural chicory-root inulin/fiber) versus rice puff placebo on bowel function satisfaction, ease of defecation, and incomplete emptying. Critical boundary verification: confirms this registry trial tests dried chicory ROOT, NOT culinary radicchio leaves, and has no posted results.
