Title: Amantadine for Neuroenhancement in Acute Patients Study (ANNES)
Registry ID: NCT05479032
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05479032
Registry API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05479032
Registry first posted: 2022-07-28
Registry update posted: 2024-02-01
Checked: 2026-11-19
Companion protocol: PMID 37608315, DOI 10.1186/s12883-023-03345-w
Other registration: EudraCT 2022-002418-18

The registration describes a single-group phase II pilot for adults with disorders of consciousness. The principal measure is a change of at least three Glasgow Coma Scale points between baseline and 120 hours after starting amantadine. The registry returned unknown overall status and no posted results at this check.

The companion protocol discusses hallucinations as a possible safety event for medication adjustment. The registration measures consciousness and supplies neither observed Parkinson visual hallucination rates nor a fig exposure.
