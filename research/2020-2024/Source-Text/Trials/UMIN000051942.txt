Videofluoroscopy protocol for carbonated and tap water contrast boluses

Source: UMIN Clinical Trials Registry, UMIN000051942, receipt R000057259
Official record: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000057259
Sponsor: National Center of Neurology and Psychiatry, Japan
First public disclosure: 2023-08-18
Coverage: original English summary of selected registration fields, checked 2026-11-12

The registry targets 50 people with Parkinson disease referred for contrast swallowing evaluation. It describes random assignment to opposite sequences of two 10 mL barium boluses, one prepared with tap water and the other with carbonated water. The registry's basic design field says parallel, while the intervention fields describe both boluses in each sequence. The assessment is open and excludes people with dementia.

Videofluoroscopy is the primary endpoint. Secondary measures include liquid property tests, dysphagia questionnaires and a visual scale concerning carbonated drinks. The registered exposure is the barium test bolus. The August 2025 termination has a separate dated summary in the 2025–2029 research period. The related NCNP record UMIN000051163 carries a different receipt number and status.
