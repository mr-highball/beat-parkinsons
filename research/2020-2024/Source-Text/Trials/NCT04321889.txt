Title: BRAINAID topical bergamot oil and severe dementia
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04321889
Registry ID: NCT04321889
First public: 2020-03-25
Coverage: Distilled original registry fields. Status checked 2026-11-19.

Parallel randomized blinded study of defurocoumarinized bergamot formulation and placebo for severe dementia. The registry lists agitation as primary and pain as secondary. The public record's target was 134 and current posted status is unknown, with no results table. The published 30-participant pilot (PMID 39148533) is separate from the registry's enrollment plan. Its study drug was a topical bergamot cream.
