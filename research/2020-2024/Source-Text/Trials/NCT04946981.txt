Givaudan France Naturals sponsored the randomized, masked, placebo-controlled crossover in moderately active adults using the Turmipure Gold turmeric formulation. The register specifies masking of participants, care providers, investigators and outcome assessors. The primary measure is the change in perceived quadriceps muscle soreness during two squats on a 0–100 mm visual analog scale across 72 hours. Listed secondary measures include serum creatine kinase, isometric and isokinetic function, jump power, knee range of motion, a composite wellness instrument and perceived exertion during exercise. Percentage recovery from maximum soreness at 72 hours is not listed separately in this current outcomes module.

The registry gives 45 as the actual enrollment. The linked original article includes 44 participants (34 male, 10 female) and documents one additional male who withdrew after randomization before taking either product. These records describe one trial. The original journal report was published October 3, 2025 as PMID 41040018, PMCID PMC12498372, DOI 10.1080/15502783.2025.2568048.

The registered intervention is a branded extract formulation. The exercise-recovery cohort supplies no estimate of ground turmeric on Parkinson pain.
