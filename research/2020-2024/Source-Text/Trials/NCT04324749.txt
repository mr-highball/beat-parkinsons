Title: Healthy Prebiotic and Postbiotic Effects of Peanuts and Peanut Butter: College Intervention Trial
Registry: NCT04324749
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04324749
First posted: 2020-03-27
Status: Completed as displayed on 2026-11-19 UTC
Text coverage: Official trial registration, outcome list and results status. Compare the main report's stated formulation.

The ARISTOTLE protocol registers roasted peanuts, peanut butter and a control supplement with similar macronutrient proportions in healthy young adults. It reports 90 enrolled and lists assessments of cognition and memory, anxiety and depression, stress coping and several biological measures. The registry does not name peanut oil in the control intervention description. The 2021 original paper (PMID 34656952) identifies its peanut-oil-based spread and analyzes 63 participants. The registry has no posted results. The regimen does not address Parkinson disease or resting tremor.
