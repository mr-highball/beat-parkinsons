Title: Effects of Blueberries in Older Adults — protocol v1.3 and dried-date comparator
NCT ID: NCT05358210
Source: https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/10/NCT05358210/Prot_000.pdf
Protocol date: 2022-03-17. Registry first posted 2022-05-03.
Archive scope: Official 32-page protocol, outcome schedule, intervention and safety sections, checked against the public register on 2026-11-20.

The study compares whole frozen blueberries against an isocaloric serving of dried dates over 12 weeks in adults aged 70 or older. The primary registry outcomes cover ambulatory blood pressure, orthostatic hypotension, lower-extremity function and cognition. The protocol separates falls, other secondary measures and spontaneous symptom or adverse-event reporting. It does not name a dedicated apathy or pain-severity scale. The version history shows removal and reinstatement of cardiac ectopy monitoring.

The completed registry lists 61 participants and no posted summary results. The distinct 2024 protocol and recruitment preprint specifies Deglet Noor dates. Results require a separate verified report. A blueberry-versus-date contrast cannot isolate a date effect against neither food.
