Strain-assigned fermented milk in sarcopenia

Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04985877
Coverage: Official registration and posted outcome fields, factual extract.

First public August 2, 2021. The completed protocol used two daily bottles of Yakult 300 LIGHT containing Lactobacillus casei Shirota for 12 weeks. The posted results include walking speed, chair-stand and Timed Up and Go, SPPB, handgrip, microbiota and biomarkers. No named fall-count outcome is present. The product is a strain-assigned fermented milk, and the record does not describe kefir grains. Inconsistent arm-label references in the protocol remain unresolved. No numerical efficacy estimate is used here.
