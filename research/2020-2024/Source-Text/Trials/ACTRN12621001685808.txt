Title: The acute effects of dried kiwifruit on sleep onset, metabolites and cognition in men
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=382485
Format: Selected official registry fields, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
First public posting: 2021-12-09
Status displayed: Not yet recruiting. First anticipated enrollment: 2022-02-28. No posted results or participant data sharing.
Design: Planned crossover in men with poor sleep comparing whole freeze-dried green kiwifruit, a sugar control and a flavor control, with drinks consumed at dinner.
Outcomes: Sleep onset and sleep quality, metabolites, mood, and computerized cognitive performance the following morning using COMPASS.
Related record: ACTRN12621000046808 is the separate earlier trial of fresh and dried kiwi and sleep.
No Parkinson disease or raw kiwifruit arm is listed.
