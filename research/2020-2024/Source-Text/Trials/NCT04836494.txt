Title: Phase 1 safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetics study of BBP-671
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04836494
NCT ID: NCT04836494
First posted: 2021-04-08
Registry last update posted: 2023-12-21
Posted summary results: No
Source depth: original ClinicalTrials.gov protocol and current result status; local research digest

CoA-101 compares oral BBP-671 and placebo in healthy volunteers and separately enrolled patients with propionic or methylmalonic acidemia. The registry records actual enrollment of 79 and termination after slow recruitment, with no results posted. The 2022 Pan American congress abstract reports an earlier healthy-volunteer phase and measured acetyl-CoA target engagement. The trial does not administer dietary pantothenate, dried figs, or a Parkinson visual symptom intervention.
