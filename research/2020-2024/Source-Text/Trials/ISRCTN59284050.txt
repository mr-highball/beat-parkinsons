Title: A wearable device for people with Parkinson’s disease to help with drooling and other symptoms of Parkinson’s
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN59284050
Official registry PDF: https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/59284050
Registry ID: ISRCTN59284050
DOI: 10.1186/ISRCTN59284050
Registration date: 2022-05-04
Coverage: Selected paraphrased official registry fields, including the results and publications section.

The record marks the study completed and lists a first-enrollment date of 2022-03-28. Its planned comparison enrolled from a wider device evaluation, with 300 targeted for the formal wristband-versus-smartphone cueing sequence and 3,000 for the wider group. The primary measure is ROMP–Saliva assessed before and after each cueing condition. Self-reported drooling frequency, severity and duration are secondary measures. The cited study protocol is PMC9955579, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0280727. The official registry snapshot lists the protocol and project site and leaves the results field unset. The enrollment targets are not achieved sample counts. The study compares prompting methods without a food exposure.
