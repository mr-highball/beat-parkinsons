Title: German registry record for freezing of gait and vibrotactile cueing in Parkinson disease
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00034584
Registry ID: DRKS00034584
First public: 2024-07-24
Coverage: Selected original German Clinical Trials Register fields.

The prospective record plans to study 30 people with Parkinson disease using repeated walking tasks and vibrotactile cueing. Primary outcomes are electroencephalographic differences between conditions, with secondary hemodynamic and gait measures. The reported recruitment status was planned. The companion protocol, PMID 42495269, names Raspberry Pi hardware as part of its synchronization setup. The record identifies no raspberry consumption or assigned food intervention.
