BRIEF SUMMARY

PD Frontline (part of RAPSODI GD) is an online study observational study for People with
Parkinson's. The aims are to better understand the genetic nature of Parkinson's and to
connect participants to clinical trials and studies, particularly those with a genetic
focus.

To do this, participants are offered genetic sequencing for two common genetic risk
factors for Parkinson's Disease, the GBA1 and LRRK2 gene, with an at-home sample
collection kit. Return postage is free.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

PD Frontline is an online one-time questionnaire based study with the purpose of
identifying potential candidates for gene targeted trials and observational studies. The
study also seeks to better characterise GBA1 risk factors for Parkinson's Disease.

Once the questionnaire is completed, the participant is sent a sample collection kit in
the post for use in genetic testing. The genes tested for are GBA1, the most common risk
factor gene for Parkinson's, and LRRK2.

Once tested and where consent is in place, the participant is informed of their results
and kept up-to-date with any studies or trials for which they may be eligible.
Individuals identified to have genetic variants are contacted by the team to discuss, all
participants are sent a report via their study profile.

Participants are kept up-to-date with any study outcomes via newsletters.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Formal diagnosis of Parkinson Disease

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Confirmation that the individual carries a GBA1 variant (GBA positive). These
       individuals will be referred to sister-study, RAPSODI.

    -  History of Parkinsonism (progressive supranuclear palsy, multiple system atrophy,
       traumatic, manganese toxicity, postencephalitic, vascular PD; drug induced PD;
       corticobasal degeneration)

    -  Individuals with another neurological disorder including: dementia, movement
       disorders, and motor neurone disease.

    -  Individuals on drugs known to be associated with parkinsonism, including neuroleptic
       agents (Amisulpride, Chlorpromazine Hydrochloride, Flupenthixol, Fluphenazine
       Hydrochloride, Haloperidol, Methotrimeprazine, Levomepromazine, Olanzapine,
       Oxypertine, Pericyazine, Perphenazine, Pimozide, Pipotiazine, Prochlorperazine,
       Promazine Hydrochloride, Reserpine, Risperidone, Sulpiride, Thioridazine,
       Trifluoperazine, Zuclopenthixol acetate, Zotepine), plus Bupropion, , Lithium,
       Methyldopa, Metoclopramide and Sodium Valproate).
