An Open Multi-centre Investigation to Evaluate the Performance of the Paragit Sleeve With build-in sEMG Sensors, Kinetic Sensors, and Smart-textile to Measure and Monitor Motor Symptoms and Treatment-induced Dyskinesia in Patients With Parkinson's Disease

BRIEF SUMMARY

This investigation is undertaken to:

  -  Evaluate the accuracy and reliability of the device in measuring and recording motor
     symptoms associated with Parkinson's Disease (PD), and

  -  To assess the safety and tolerability of the device in patients with PD.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Tremor
    Description
      Tremor will be assessed using the device, measured with superficial EMG [Volts], and
      inertial measurement unit [m/s2]. Correlation with clinical gold-standard (MDS-UPDRS)
      will be studied.
    Time frame: During the clinical assessment, and for the following 24h.
  -
    Measure: Bradykinesia
    Description
      Bradykinesia will be assessed using the device, measured inertial measurement unit
      [m/s2]. Correlation with clinical gold-standard (MDS-UPDRS) will be studied.
    Time frame: During the clinical assessment, and for the following 24h.
  -
    Measure: Rigidity
    Description
      The device will assess rigidity, measured with superficial EMG [Volts], and inertial
      measurement unit [m/s2]. Correlation with clinical gold-standard (MDS-UPDRS) will be
      studied.
    Time frame: During the clinical assessment, and for the following 24h.
  -
    Measure: Dyskinesia
    Description
      The device will assess rigidity, measured with superficial EMG [Volts], and inertial
      measurement unit [m/s2]. Correlation with clinical gold-standard (MDS-UPDRS) will be
      studied.
    Time frame: During the clinical assessment, and for the following 24h.
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Safety
    Description
      Safety of the Paragit Sleeve by assessment of device deficiencies and adverse events,
      non-serious and serious, rated for causality.
    Time frame: During the clinical assessment , and for the following 24h.
  -
    Measure: Usability
    Description: The usability of the device will be studied using a questionnaire.
    Time frame: During the clinical assessment , and for the following 24h.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    1. Parkinson disease: Clinically diagnosed

    2. Age ≥ 50 (not in the childbearing aged as defined by Statistics Denmark, 15-49)

    3. Experiencing at least two of following symptoms and/or side effect on a daily basis:

         -  Rigidity

         -  Tremor

         -  Bradykinesia

         -  Dyskinesia (treatment side effect)

    4. Provision of informed consent, i.e., the subject must be able to:

         -  Read and understand the Patient Information and Consent Form

         -  Sign the Patient Information and Consent Form.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    1. Known allergy/hypersensitivity to any material in direct contact with the skin
       (stainless steel, silicone and polyester)

    2. MMSE <24

    3. Other neurological diseases
