Design: randomized, masked, supplement-placebo crossover with one week per condition and a two-week washout. Participants were adults age 50 or older with insomnia.
Version scope: This extract records the current API fields and actual posting dates. The version-by-date public pages did not yield historical field content during this review.

Current registry arm descriptions, paraphrased
Active: a 60 mL supplement with 5 g green tea extract, 1 g chicory extract, and 5 g collagen peptides. The registry lists sugar, erythritol, 2% potassium sorbate, and 1% flavor.
Placebo: 60 mL water with sugar, erythritol, 2% potassium sorbate, and 1% flavor.

Protocol and consent records
Protocol/SAP: https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/84/NCT05323084/Prot_SAP_000.pdf
Consent: https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/84/NCT05323084/ICF_001.pdf
The protocol is dated December 16, 2021. Both files show December 23, 2021 IRB approval in registry metadata and September 17, 2023 upload timestamps. Upload dates do not establish first public availability.
Protocol p. 6 lists 5 g green tea extract, 1 g chicory extract, 5 g collagen peptides, 7 g of an equal sugar and erythritol mix, 0.2% potassium sorbate, and 0.1% flavor. It describes 60 mL water added during preparation and a finished 53 mL bottle. One bottle is specified as a dose. The listed active and placebo lots were 2008-1 and 2008-2, prepared at the LSU Food Incubator on August 17, 2020. The protocol's named lots had a planned August 17, 2022 expiry. The public documents reviewed do not verify the actual lots taken at individual visits.
Protocol p. 1 specifies decaffeinated green tea containing epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG). The consent calls out chicory, decaffeinated green tea powder, and another tea component. No quantitative caffeine, EGCG, theanine, or chicory constituent assay was located.
The finished dose volume in the protocol and its potassium sorbate and flavor percentages differ from the registry description. The three primary ingredient masses agree. The underlying distinction in product makeup remains unresolved.
