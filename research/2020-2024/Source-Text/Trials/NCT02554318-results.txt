NCT02554318 — Fermented Soybean Supplementation Among Active Pulmonary Tuberculosis Patients With Standard Therapy in Indonesia
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02554318
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Coverage: Selected official registration and results fields, summarized as plain text.

First public registration: 2015-09-18. First posted results and last public update: 2022-06-21.
Status: Completed. Results posted to ClinicalTrials.gov: Yes.
Sponsor: University of Giessen. Actual enrollment: 147.
Design: Randomized parallel comparison in pulmonary tuberculosis, 75 assigned to standard therapy plus cooked fermented soybeans and 72 to standard therapy alone.
Food: Cooked soy tempeh, 166.5 g/day, for two months.
Secondary outcome, six-minute walk change at two months: +49.67 m (SD 58.49, n=64) in the tempeh group and +25.75 m (SD 56.85, n=63) in controls.
Other events: Vomiting in 1 of 75 in the tempeh group and 0 of 72 controls. The event was recorded through non-systematic assessment.
The report does not measure stride length or include Parkinson disease.
