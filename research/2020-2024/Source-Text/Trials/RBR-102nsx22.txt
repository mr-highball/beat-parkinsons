Title: The effect of rosemary use in decreasing symptoms of Common Mental Disorders in prison workers and persons deprived of their liberty
Source: Brazilian Registry of Clinical Trials (ReBEC)
URL: https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-102nsx22
Registry ID: RBR-102nsx22
Registration and approval date: 2022-06-24
Coverage: selected public registry fields summarized in English

Design: five groups of ten adults, including workers and people in prison. The registration describes three rosemary capsule levels, a group taking concurrent medication for anxiety, and a control group.
Outcome report: Sztormowski and colleagues, DOI 10.9734/ejmp/2022/v33i121110.
