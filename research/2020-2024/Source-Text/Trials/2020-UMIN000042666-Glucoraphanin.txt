Original source https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000048062

The entry first posted December 5, 2020 and lists symbol coding, symbol search and digit span among the primary outcomes in a 144-person glucoraphanin trial in healthy older adults. The linked 2022 Nouchi article explicitly names this registration. Registry results were posted June 12, 2023. The protocol measures task performance after capsules, without a raw mature broccoli or Parkinson clinical contrast.
