Study design: parallel randomized placebo comparator
Randomized and analyzed: CBD oil n=21, placebo n=19
Placebo composition: the structured record does not identify the oil. The posted WVU protocol specifies avocado oil with the possibility of food coloring and plans peppermint-flavored oil for both groups. The linked 2022 and 2023 reports identify avocado oil, and the 2023 paper specifies mint flavor in both groups. The protocol and papers supply no avocado producer or processing grade.
Secondary assessment time in registry: four hours after baseline

Digit Symbol Substitution Test correct count endpoint mean (SD), 60 seconds in the registry and associated protocol: CBD 74.5 (8.1), placebo 71.8 (12.1), adjusted registry p=.6331
Trail Making B endpoint seconds mean (SD): CBD 29.7 (8.9), placebo 37.1 (15.9), adjusted registry p=.0347
