Title: The effect of date seed powder supplementation on nutritional, oxidative, inflammatory and exercise outcomes in runners
Registry ID: IRCT20150205020965N9
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/53070
First registered: 2021-02-19, prospective.
Last update: 2022-06-18.
Text coverage: Selected fields verified from the official English registration on 2026-11-19.

The randomized masked study targeted 36 recreational runners and used coded date-seed powder and placebo. Both groups undertook interval training. The registry lists oxidative, inflammatory and exercise outcomes, including malondialdehyde. Its English outcome fields do not list a standalone pain or soreness measure. It marks recruitment complete and provides no numerical results.

The published protocol PMID 35769196 includes muscle pain as a primary measure. The 2022 publication PMID 35832028 reports the original visual analogue pain comparison and the 2023 companion PMID 36881179 reports inflammation and muscle damage. All three papers cite the same registry identity. The protocol and companion papers are linked to one randomized seed-powder trial family.
