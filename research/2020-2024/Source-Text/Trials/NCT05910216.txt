BRIEF SUMMARY

This is a randomized clinical trial in an online format, which will only include people
with PD randomly divided into two groups:

Group 1 - Social Telerehabilitation with Dance and Music Group 2 - Social
Telerehabilitation with Multimodal Therapeutic Exercise and Music

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

Arm groups
  -
    Label: Group Dance & Music (Online)
    Type: EXPERIMENTAL
    Description
      Social Telerehabilitation with Dance & Music (Online): The dance intervention will be
      instructed by a physiotherapist and dance professional, who will follow a structure based
      on the materials of the Dance for Parkinson's (USA) ® method
    Intervention names
      - Other: Dance & Music (Online)
  -
    Label: Group Multimodal Therapeutic Exercise & Music (Online)
    Type: ACTIVE_COMPARATOR
    Description
      Social Telerehabilitation with Multimodal Therapeutic Exercise & Music (Online): The
      intervention with multimodal therapeutic exercise will be instructed by a
      physiotherapist, who will follow a structure based on a previous study and Physical
      Therapy Guidelines for People with PD
    Intervention names
      - Other: Multimodal Therapeutic Exercise & Music (Online)
Interventions
  -
    Type: OTHER
    Name: Dance & Music (Online)
    Description
      This is a social telerehabilitation with dance and music. It will include artistic form,
      imageries using scenic arts, imageries using aspects of theater and storytelling,
      movement improvisation, choreography and sequence of movements, complex dance movements,
      fluidity of movement or connection between one movement and another, rhythmicity and/or
      movement built with music. It will include instructions for motor learning, music, and
      socialization
    Arm group labels
      - Group Dance & Music (Online)
  -
    Type: OTHER
    Name: Multimodal Therapeutic Exercise & Music (Online)
    Description
      This is a social telerehabilitation with multimodal therapeutic Exercise and Music. It is
      restricted to the execution of movements within series and standardized and fixed
      repetitions, with a background music. It will not include dance. It will include
      instructions for motor learning, music, and socialization.
    Arm group labels
      - Group Multimodal Therapeutic Exercise & Music (Online)

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Assessment of Quality of life
    Description
      Quality of life measured by Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire (PDQ-39). Scores range from
      0-100 (100 = maximum level of problems).
    Time frame: Change from Pre to Post treatment (12 weeks) and FOLLOW-UP (after 4 weeks of study ending)
  -
    Measure: Assessment of Well-being
    Description
      Well-being measured by the scale Positive and Negative Affect Scale (PANAS). scores range
      from 10 - 50, a higher score indicates more of a positive affect.
    Time frame: Change from Pre to Post treatment (12 weeks) and FOLLOW-UP (after 4 weeks of study ending)
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Assessment of Motor symptoms severity
    Description
      Motor symptoms severity measured by the scale Unified Parkinson's disease rating scale,
      motor subscale (UPDRS III). Part III: Motor examination: 18 items. Score range: 0-132, 32
      and below is mild, 59 and above is severe.
    Time frame: Change from Pre to Post treatment (12 weeks) and FOLLOW-UP (after 4 weeks of study ending)
  -
    Measure: Assessment of Functional lower extremity strength
    Description
      Functional lower extremity strength measured by the Five times sit to stand test (FTSTS).
      The time to complete the test is measured, where lower time to perform sit to stand
      represents better functional lower extremity strength
    Time frame: Change from Pre to Post treatment (12 weeks)
  -
    Measure: Assessment of Symptoms of Anxiety
    Description
      Symptoms of Anxiety measured by the Parkinson Anxiety Scale (PAS). Scores range from 0-48
      (48= maximum level of problems).
    Time frame: Change from Pre to Post treatment (12 weeks)
  -
    Measure: Assessment of Attention and Memory Recognition
    Description
      Executive function by means attention and memory recognition measureb by Online Attention
      Test (OAT) and Recognition Memory Test (RMT).
    Time frame: Change from Pre to Post treatment (12 weeks)
  -
    Measure: Feasibility (self-perceptions)
    Description
      Self-perceptions (feasibility) of technology usability, socialization and benefits of
      interventions based on a structured interview.
    Time frame: Change from Pre to Post treatment (12 weeks)

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Clinical diagnosis of idiopathic PD according to the clinical criteria of the London
       Brain Bank Criteria (2006);

    -  Age between 40-90 years;

    -  A minimum score of 18 in the Montreal Cognitive Assessment;

    -  Drug therapy/exercise stable for at least four weeks before the study starts;

    -  Agreement to participate in the study according to the Informed Consent Form.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Auditory and/or visual disturbances that compromise understanding simple commands;

    -  Access to the internet and a device to access online classes;

    -  A house member and/or caregiver at home for supervision during the entire
       experiment, including evaluations and interventions. For patients scoring less than
       2.5 points on the modified Hoehn and Yahr Scale (indicating mild disease impairment,
       independence in daily living activities, and no risk of falls), the requirement for
       household member supervision may be waived.
