BRIEF SUMMARY

The goal of this observational study is to evaluate the long-term effect of DBS-STN on
cognitive and behavioral outcomes in Parkinson's disease (PD) patients.

PD patients will be assessed over time using tests, questionnaires and standardized
clinical scales. An initial assessment (T0) and annual follow-up assessments will be
carried out for 5 years.

Researchers will compare data collected from patients with DBS versus patients with best
medical therapy.

  -
    Measure: Long-term characterization of DBS-STN on cognitive outcomes
    Description
      Cognitive impairment change from baseline until 5 years in Montreal Cognitive Assessment

      assessments up to 5 years]
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Beck Depression Inventory
    Description

      means worse outcome)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: State-Trait Anxiety Inventory
    Description
      Anxiety symptoms change from baseline to 5 year (STAI, min 20, max 80, higher score means
      worse outcome)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Questionnaire for Impulsive-Compulsive Disorders in Parkinson
    Description
      Impulsivity change from baseline to 5 years (QUIP, min 0, max 112, higher score means

    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
    Measure: Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index
    Description: Sleep Quality change (PSQI, min 0, max 21, higher score means poorer sleep quality)
    Time frame: Annual assessments up to 5 years
  -
