The first public registration records September 21, 2024 and lists February 5, 2024 as first enrollment. It describes a cross-sectional screening phase and a later randomized phase, so the dates do not establish when randomization started. The registry describes a double-blind crossover. The published methods describe a parallel, delayed-start comparison with blinded outcome assessors and analysts. Participants and counselors knew the allocation. The 2026 ReBEC results are from the same cohort as the preprint and journal report. [Latest registry results](https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-36q469s).

The preprint body and final journal abstract belong to the same underlying family. See research/2025-2029/Source-Text/2026-Fernandez-Mediterranean-Counseling-Preprint.txt. Olive oil was measured within overall dietary adherence and has no isolated constipation estimate.
