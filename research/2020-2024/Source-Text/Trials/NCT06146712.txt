NCT06146712, A Single-Group Study to Examine the Efficacy of a Melatonin Lotion With Magnesium to Improve Sleep Quality and Quantity. Official ClinicalTrials.gov API https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT06146712, accessed 2026-11-16.

First submitted 2023-11-18, first posted 2023-11-27, last posted update 2023-11-27; completed, actual start 2023-08-07, actual completion 2023-10-02. Interventional single-group, unmasked, actual enrollment 35; eligible adults 18–59 without listed chronic sleep conditions. Sponsor Asutra. Registered intervention is a nighttime topical lotion containing melatonin, magnesium and multiple components including Cymbopogon flexuosus oil and citral. There is no separate lemongrass or placebo arm and no brewed ingestion.

Primary outcomes are baseline to week 3 change in the 28-item Sleep Quality Scale (SQS), and sleep time and a sleep score from Fitbit Inspire 3. Secondary outcome is self-reported product efficacy. The registry does not list polysomnography, a REM sleep behavior disorder measure, or witnessed dream enactment. Fitbit sleep-stage estimation is not polysomnographic confirmation of REM without atonia. The current record has no results section; no outcome estimates can be inferred.
