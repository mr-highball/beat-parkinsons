Title: Efficacy of Deutetrabenazine to Control Symptoms of Dysphagia Associated with HD
Publication date: 2020-03-10 (first public registry posting)
Record: NCT04301726
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04301726
Text coverage: Official registry API data, distilled.

In this randomized study for Huntington disease with dysphagia, the eight-week comparison is oral deutetrabenazine tablets versus matching placebo tablets. The registry lists instrumental and non-instrumental swallowing measures. Thirty-six participants were enrolled versus 48 planned in the published trial summaries. The registry shows termination because the study drug became unavailable and no posted results module. No dietary oil was assigned. The endpoint belongs to this trial even when it appears beside TRIHEP3 in the trial updates.
