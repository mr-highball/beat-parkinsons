Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05585762

First posted October 19, 2022. The healthy older-adult trial lists a calorie-, appearance- and taste-matched nonketone beverage and registered tolerance, safety and ketone outcomes. The public arm description does not identify canola. Its published exploratory outcomes identify the comparison beverage as canola-containing and give physical fatigability outcomes. Those results appear in the 2025 bucket.
