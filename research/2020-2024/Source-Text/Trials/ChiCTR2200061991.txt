Registration of stratified diabetes self-management
Source: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=ChiCTR2200061991
Coverage: selected official WHO registry fields; ChiCTR original blocked by verification challenge

The WHO ICTRP mirror gives July 16, 2022 as registration and July 17 as planned first enrollment. It marks registration prospective and lists a target of 30 per arm. Primary fields include cognition, self-management and fasting glucose. A broad mental-health field appears under secondary outcomes, without an anxiety scale or a separate depression instrument in the WHO snapshot. The mirror was refreshed April 3, 2023. The originating ChiCTR site returned a verification page during review, so the original revision history and July-level outcome detail are unverified.
