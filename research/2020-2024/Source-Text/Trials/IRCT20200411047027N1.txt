IRCT20200411047027N1 | Nigella sativa supplementation during diabetic hemodialysis
Official source: https://irct.ir/trial/48113
First public registration: 2020-05-31
Retrieved: 2026-11-13. Selected English registry fields summarized.

The randomized parallel trial compares nigella oil with placebo in people with diabetes receiving hemodialysis. The registry and published protocol cover renal function, glycemic and inflammatory measures, quality of life and depression. The publicly accessible evidence includes the protocol in PMID 35120579. A result for the depression comparison remains unverified.
