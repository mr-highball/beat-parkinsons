Title: The Effect of Fish Oil Supplementation on Vocal Performance
Publication date: 2021-12-02 (first public registry posting)
Record: NCT05141045
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05141045
Text coverage: Official registry API data, distilled.

The completed study enrolled 43 singers and lists oral omega-3 supplementation and safflower oil at 3 g/day. Its primary outcome is change in Evaluation of Ability to Sing Easily. The registry has no posted results module. The published comparison reports an aggregate Reflux Symptom Index without the individual swallowing item. It describes alternate allocation and 35 completers. The companion 2021 pilot recruited a separate sample. Neither report studied dysphagia in Parkinson disease, and safflower oil's pressing grade remains unverified.
