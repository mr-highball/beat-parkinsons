Official Iranian registry record

Original source https://www.irct.ir/trial/71772

The official registry marks this MIND diet comparison prospective and gives August 7, 2023 for registration. It gives August 19 as the initial enrollment date, while the later protocol lists October 12 as the planned start. WHO's 2023 snapshot shows recruiting and the current Iranian record shows complete. The study assigns multi-food diets rather than a lentil preparation. [WHO registry snapshot](https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT20181111041611N8)
