Official Title: Evaluating the Factors That Influence Glycemic Response to Wild Rice and Wild Rice Blends in Humans (EFGW) Study.
Lead Sponsor: University of Manitoba | First Posted: 2023-08-04 | Actual Start: 2023-07-14 | Status: COMPLETED | Posted Results: No.
Design: Randomized single-blind crossover trial, actual enrollment n=20 healthy adults comparing 140 g cooked stovetop whole wild rice vs white rice, brown rice, and 15% wild / 85% brown rice blends. Primary outcome: glycemic response; secondary: palatability and subjective appetite.
Verified Round 7 Appraisal: Completed acute glycemic/appetite study in healthy adults without posted results or clinical neurological/GI motility endpoints.
