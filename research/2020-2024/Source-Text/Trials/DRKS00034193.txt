Title: German registry record for immersive virtual reality upper-limb training in Parkinson disease
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00034193
Registry ID: DRKS00034193
First public: 2024-05-16
Coverage: Selected original German Clinical Trials Register fields.

The prospective record compares upper-extremity virtual reality exercises with conventional upper-extremity training in inpatient rehabilitation. The planned primary measures are the Action Research Arm Test and Jebsen–Taylor Hand Function Test at baseline and four weeks. The completed record shows a final sample of 58. The companion result report is PMID 41508050. Its exposure is rehabilitation, with no date or fig fruit assignment.
