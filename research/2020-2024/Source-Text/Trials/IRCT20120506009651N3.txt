IRCT20120506009651N3 | Aniseed oral powder for gastrointestinal symptoms during COVID-19
Official source: https://irct.ir/trial/10212
First public registration: 2022-04-04, prospective as displayed.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13. Selected English registry fields summarized.

The parallel trial targeted 225 adults with confirmed COVID-19 and gastrointestinal symptoms. The registry describes two weeks of aniseed oral powder with usual care versus usual care. Gastrointestinal symptoms are the registered outcomes. The primary 2024 paper describes a double-blind trial with a placebo powder and reports a symptom-level result for abdominal pain.
