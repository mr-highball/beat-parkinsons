Lavender Aromatherapy for Sleep in Hospitalized Adult Patients

Official source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04648215
Record ID: NCT04648215
First public posting: 2020-12-01
Last posting: 2023-05-06
Verified: 2026-11-13 via ClinicalTrials.gov API
Coverage: Selected official study fields, summarized

Status

Terminated due to unavailable subjects. Recorded enrollment 108. No posted results.

Study and outcomes

Three groups receive lavender oil on gauze, grapeseed oil on gauze, or usual care. Registered sleep outcomes use the Verran and Snyder-Halpern sleep scale on study days 3 and 4. Grapeseed exposure is nonoral.
