Title: Study of measurement on body temperature, exhaled gas, and autonomic nerve of admission for functional foods
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000063079
Format: Selected official registry fields summarized for this catalog.
Registry: UMIN000055203, receipt R000063079.
Institution: Okayama Prefectural University.
Public disclosure: 2024-08-23. Last modified: 2025-03-23.
Status: Completed. Eight healthy adults enrolled, eligible ages 18–29.
Design: Single-blind, nonrandomized crossover with bread alone, two macadamia oil and bread configurations, and soybean oil with bread.
Planned outcomes: Body temperature, gas exchange, autonomic measures, and visual analogue scores for sleepiness, fatigue and hunger up to 150 minutes after the test meal. Previous-night sleep and wake time and Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index were collected as secondary information.
Registry results: No posted outcome data. The companion report for the two experiments presents results for the matching eight-participant experiment.
