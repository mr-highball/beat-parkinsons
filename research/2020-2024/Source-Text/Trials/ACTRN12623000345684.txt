Title: A Double-Blind, Randomised Placebo-Controlled Feasibility Trial Assessing Oral Cannabis for The Relief of Fibromyalgia Symptoms
Acronym: CANN-RELIEF
First submitted: March 15, 2023
Registration date: April 3, 2023
Last update shown: April 6, 2025
Population: adults with fibromyalgia
Design: randomized, double-blind, parallel feasibility trial
Active arm: oral CBD and THC combination oil
Registered comparator: medium-chain triglyceride oil with small amounts of walnut and avocado oil
Comparator route: oral, intended before bedtime, with matched titration
Primary emphasis: feasibility, tolerability and adherence
Relevant measurements: adverse effects and sleep quality
Avocado oil preparation, per-ingredient amount and standalone contrast: unspecified
Linked results: https://doi.org/10.1155/prm/7311235
