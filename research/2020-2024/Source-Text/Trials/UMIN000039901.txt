Status: Completed. Target and actual enrollment: 60. Anticipated start: 2020-09-01.
Design: Randomized, double-blind, parallel trial in healthy adults aged 65–80. Daily unlabeled 3.7 g pouches of flaxseed oil or corn oil for 12 weeks.
Planned outcomes: Cognitive testing at baseline and weeks six and 12. Secondary outcomes include food frequency, depression, quality of life and physical condition.
Results: The registry field reads unpublished and supplies no numerical results. The March 21, 2023 journal report explicitly identifies UMIN000039901.

Relevance: Oral flaxseed oil and cognitive tests outside Parkinson’s. The specific grade and cold-pressing history of the oil used in this trial are not reported.
