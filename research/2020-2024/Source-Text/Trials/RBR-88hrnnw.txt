Title: Effect of Rosemary on sleep, mood status and cardiovascular risk in emergency nursing: intervention study
Source: Brazilian Registry of Clinical Trials (ReBEC)
URL: https://ensaiosclinicos.gov.br/rg/RBR-88hrnnw
Registry ID: RBR-88hrnnw
Registration and approval date: 2022-09-23
Date first enrollment: 2022-10-03
Coverage: selected public registry fields summarized in English

Design: interventional, quasi-experimental study of emergency nursing workers. Oral dried rosemary extract was given in a single-group design. The registry prespecifies comparisons over time for sleep, mood and cardiovascular outcomes.
Related reports: Carolina Renz Pretto's 2023 UFSM thesis, PMID 41020583 (sleep and cortisol), PMID 41861393 (cardiovascular measures). These reports cover the same 2022 cohort.
