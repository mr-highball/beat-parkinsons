BRIEF SUMMARY

In this research study the researchers want to learn more about brain activity related to
speech perception and production.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Speech production is disrupted in a number of neurological diseases that involve the
basal ganglia, including Parkinson's disease (PD) and dystonia. The investigators will
use a novel experimental approach and combination of analytic techniques to elucidate the
contribution of neural activity in cortical-basal ganglia circuits to the hierarchical
control of speech production, in subjects undergoing deep brain stimulation surgery.
