Title: Effects of celery powder on glycemic and metabolic outcomes in type 2 diabetes
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/45851
Record: IRCT20100223003408N8
First registration: 2021-03-01
Coverage: Factual extract of the Iranian Registry of Clinical Trials original.

The record plans 50 participants and compares dried celery stalk-and-leaf powder with starch, with a hypocaloric diabetic diet in both arms. Fasting glucose is primary. Other registered outcomes include weight and laboratory measures. The posted record contains no hallucination or psychiatric outcome field.
The registry labels the study registered while recruiting and shows planned dates beginning before registration, with no actual first enrollment listed. The 2023 publication describes retrospective registration. It links the same ID and reports 36 participants analyzed. Weekly safety calls were general, without published hallucination ascertainment.
