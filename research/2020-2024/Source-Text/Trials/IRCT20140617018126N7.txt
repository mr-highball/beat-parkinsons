Title: Evaluation of Lavandula angustifolia and Melissa officinalis products as nasal sprays in mild to moderate depression
Registry ID: IRCT20140617018126N7
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/66309
First registered: 2022-11-02.
Text coverage: Official IRCT registered interventions and outcomes.

The protocol targets 80 participants in four randomized parallel arms: lemon balm nasal spray, lavender nasal spray, their combination and water placebo. It specifies Hamilton depression assessment at baseline and weeks three and six. The official reader marks recruitment complete; actual dates and separately posted results remain unavailable. This protocol assigns intranasal exposure in mild to moderate depression.
