Title: Transcranial alternating current stimulation for improving motor and non-motor symptoms of Parkinson's disease
Source: https://www.chictr.org.cn/hvshowproject.html?id=226895&v=1.0
Registry ID: ChiCTR2300071969
First public: 2023-05-30
Coverage: Selected official first-version Chinese Clinical Trial Registry identity and date fields.

The public version 1.0 record identifies a randomized, double-blind controlled stimulation study for Parkinson motor and nonmotor symptoms. Its stated registration and first version dates are May 30, 2023. The later trial report, PMID 42046417, identifies the same registration and compares active stimulation with sham during rehabilitation. The historical registration is a plan and supplies no date fruit intervention or efficacy result.
