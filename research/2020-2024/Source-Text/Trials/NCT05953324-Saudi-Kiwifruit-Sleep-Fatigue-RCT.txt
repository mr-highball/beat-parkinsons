Title: The Effects Of Kiwifruit Consumption On Sleep Quality, Fatigue And BMI Of Saudi Adults
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov
URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05953324
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05953324
Registry ID: NCT05953324
First published: 2022
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified ClinicalTrials.gov Registry Record (NCT05953324, inspected Round 5):
Status: COMPLETED (Actual Start: 2022-01-01; Actual Completion: 2022-04-30; Results Posted: False). Design: Interventional, randomized, parallel, open-label trial (actual enrollment n=26 Saudi adults/students). Intervention: 2 medium raw Hayward green kiwifruits 1 hour before bedtime nightly for 6 weeks (42 days; 84 kiwifruits total) vs non-kiwifruit control. Primary outcomes: Sleep quality, Fatigue, and Body mass index. Critical appraisal: Completed in April 2022 without posted registry results or indexed peer-reviewed publication on PubMed, representing a potential publication-bias gap for both sleep quality and fatigue endpoints.
