Title: Melissa Officinalis L. Supplementation for Emotional Health and Sleep Disturbance
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov
URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05602688
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05602688
Registry ID: NCT05602688
First posted: 2022-11-02
Study dates: 2023-01-03 to 2023-04-30
Status: Completed. Results were not posted in the registry record retrieved on 2026-11-13.
Coverage: selected public registry fields

Design: randomized placebo-controlled parallel comparison in adults with emotional distress or poor sleep. The intervention used oral lemon balm extract with a phospholipid carrier. Primary outcomes were changes in depression, anxiety, stress and sleep quality over three weeks.
Outcome report: PMID 37927585, PMC10620697, DOI 10.3389/fphar.2023.1250560.
