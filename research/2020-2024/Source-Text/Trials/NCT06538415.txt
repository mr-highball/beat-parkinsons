Cooked black beans in a mixed pulse skin trial
Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06538415
Coverage: selected official registry fields.

This skin-microbiome study first posted August 5, 2024. The ten-week intervention alternates a cooked tri-bean blend containing kidney, pinto and black beans with cooked black lentils. The comparator consumes cooked white rice. The study does not isolate black beans and its population and outcomes do not address the frozen Parkinson sleep and postural endpoints. No summary results were posted in the retrieved registry snapshot. Official registry <https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06538415>
