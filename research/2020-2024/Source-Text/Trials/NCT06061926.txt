Title: Effect of Celery Seed on the Components of Metabolic Syndrome, Insulin Sensitivity and Insulin Secretion
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06061926
NCT ID: NCT06061926
First posted: 2023-09-29
Last update posted: 2024-10-23
Coverage: Factual extract of official registry fields and linkage to the original publication.

Actual start is listed as May 20, 2023. The trial compares celery seed extract with placebo in metabolic syndrome. The arm description and subsequent original results use two 75 mg capsules per day. A separate registry description uses inconsistent wording about the total. Registered primary outcomes are metabolic measures and blood pressure. No hallucination outcome is listed.
The registry shows unknown current status and no posted results. The journal report PMID 41599709 identifies this registration and presents results for 28 randomized participants.
