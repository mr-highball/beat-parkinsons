IRCT20150713023190N10 | Cumin extract after abdominal surgery
Official source: https://irct.ir/trial/59216
Retrieved: 2026-11-13
Coverage: Selected English-language registry fields summarized from the official study record.

Registration and latest displayed update: 2021-10-24. The record shows registration while recruiting and now lists recruitment complete.
Population: Adults aged 18 to 60 scheduled for abdominal surgery under general anesthesia at Ali Ibn Abi Taleb Hospital in Rafsanjan, Iran. Planned enrollment was 70.
Design: Two parallel randomized groups. The cumin group was to receive an oral 250 mg extract capsule four hours after surgery and a second capsule one hour later. The comparator group was to receive starch capsules on the same schedule. Allocation was to account for surgery type and sex.
Outcomes: Time to first flatus and first defecation, abdominal bloating and pain, nausea and vomiting. Bowel activity was to be checked every two hours during the first 24 postoperative hours.
Publication: PMID 38965524 reports 74 randomized and 72 analyzed. The paper is cataloged in this period's Source-Text/PMC11229490.txt.
