Registry title: Effects of a Standardised Saffron Extract Supplementation on Symptoms Associated With Perimenopause in Healthy Women

The completed record lists 104 enrolled and no posted results. The comparison is Safr'Inside capsules versus placebo in perimenopause. Overall perimenopause symptom severity is primary and sleep quality and mood are secondary. The registry does not establish a result for insomnia severity or for ground saffron in Parkinson disease.

Author preprint DOI 10.64898/2026.10.12.26365297 supplies separate sleep outcomes for this same trial. The registry has no results module.
