NCT05494164 | Nigella sativa for selected outcomes in chronic rhinosinusitis
Official source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05494164
First public posting: 2022-08-09. Latest public update: 2024-04-17.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13. Selected official API fields summarized.

The completed parallel trial lists actual enrollment of 102 adults and compares nigella nasal oil plus standard care with standard care. The registry describes sesame in the drop formulation. Primary measures include SNOT-22 physiological symptoms and the Sleep Quality Scale. Secondary measures include SNOT-22 sleep and emotional domains and treatment satisfaction. No numerical results are posted to ClinicalTrials.gov.
