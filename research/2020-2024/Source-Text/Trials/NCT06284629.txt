BRIEF SUMMARY

The overall aim of the trial is to compare the efficacy of using wearables and
patient-reported outcome measures versus standard care in enhancing the timeliness of
referrals to advanced monitoring of treatments for people with Parkinson's Disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

The study is a multicentre, parallel group, randomized controlled trial, investigating
the value of using wearable devices and electronic patient-reported outcome measurements
to improve clinical decision-making related to people with Parkinson's Disease.

Data from a wearable device and a mobile app will be compared to current standard of
care.

75 participants will be recruited from the uptake area of the Movement Disorder Clinics
(MDC) at Rigshospitalet Glostrup (RHG) and 5 private practice neurologists in Denmark.
They will equally be randomized to one of two groups: Intervention or Standard of Care.

Each participant will take part of the trial for 6 months, in which they will have 3
study visits:

  -  Baseline

  -  Clinical visits at month 3 and 6

  -  Final visit after 6 months

They will be asked to wear the device for 6 months. We will then compare decisions made
related to changes in medication and referrals to advanced treatment.

    Intervention names

Interventions
  -
    Type: DEVICE
    Name: Neptune
    Description
      Neptune is a software algorithm that utilizes data from wrist-worn sensors to track
      Parkinson motor symptoms and displays them in an easy to understand graph. Neptune Care
      is a mobile app that includes a patient portal, where patients can view their motor data,
      and a physician portal, where the treating physician can view the objective motor data
      prior to a clinical visit.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Differences between groups in proportion of patients referred to evaluation for advanced treatment.
    Description: Differences in number of referrals based on access to digital measurements
    Time frame: 6 months
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Differences in questionnaire data from baseline (inclusion visit) to end-of-intervention.
    Description: Global Sleep Assessment Scale (GSAQ). Every question is scored from 0-3 (highest worst)
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: Differences in questionnaire data from baseline (inclusion visit) to end-of-intervention.
    Description: Non-Motor Symptom Scale (NMSS). Scored from 0-360 (highest worst)
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: Changes in type of, frequency and dose of medication (calculated Levodopa Equivalent Dose, LED, by use of Tomlinson's scale)
    Description: Levodopa Equivalent Dose, LED, by use of Tomlinson's scale.
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: Differences in questionnaire data from baseline (inclusion visit) to end-of-intervention.
    Description
      Movement Disorders Society - Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS). Scored
      from 0-52 (UPDRS-II), 0-132 (UPDRS-III) and 0-44 (UPDRS-IV) (highest worst)
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: Differences in questionnaire data from baseline (inclusion visit) to end-of-intervention.
    Description: Parkinson's Disease Questionnaire (PDQ-39). Scored from 0-100 (highest worst)
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: Differences in questionnaire data from baseline (inclusion visit) to end-of-intervention.
    Description: EuroQol Questionnaire (EQ5D-5L). Scored from 0-1 and 0-100 (highest best).
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: Differences in questionnaire data from baseline (inclusion visit) to end-of-intervention.
    Description
      Patient Global Impressions Scale - Improvement score (measurements of QoL) (PGI-I). Every
      question is scored from 1-7 (highest worst)
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: System Usability Scale
    Description
      The System Usability Scale (SUS) is a simple, ten-statement questionnaire used to
      evaluate the usability of a system or product. It is scored from 0-100.
    Time frame: Completed at last visit of the study
  -
    Measure: Referrals
    Description: Clinician confidence in referral decision (5-point Likert scale) and reasons for referral
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: Medication adjustment
    Description: Clinician confidence in changes in oral medication based on wearable data
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: Symptom burden
    Description
      Differences in wearable-based symptom burden (OFF/DYS vs ON time) between groups at the
      time of referral.
    Time frame: 6 months
  -
    Measure: Referral time
    Description: Differences between groups in time from inclusion to referral
    Time frame: 6 months

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Diagnosed with Idiopathic Parkinson's Disease

    -  Taking five or more doses of levodopa daily, or 4 doses levodopa and a
       COMT-inhibitor

    -  Experiencing fluctuations in motor function with regards to OFF time and/or
       dyskinesia.

    -  Able and motivated to utilize a digital product, either alone or assisted by a
       caregiver.

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Consistent use of a wheelchair or walker which may interfere significantly with the
       remote monitoring of PD motor symptoms.

    -  Lack of understanding and proficiency in the Danish language.

    -  Inability to use wearable devices due to non-compliance, physical and/or skin
       sensitivity.

    -  Suffering from severe psychiatric disorders.

    -  Otherwise not qualified to receive advanced treatment (e.g., advanced cancer, severe
       heart failure).

    -  Already undergoing advanced treatment.

    -  Diagnosed with atypical or secondary parkinsonism.

    -  Other patient groups otherwise deemed ineligible by the project manager.
