Official French pediatric trial registration

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06594536

First posted September 19, 2024. The Robert Debré protocol specifies eight weeks of sulforaphane capsules or placebo, with parent-rated inattention and a sustained-attention task. It was Recruiting at the official November 2026 update, with no posted results. Location and protocol distinguish it from the 2025 Tehran methylphenidate adjunct report. This comparison does not test raw broccoli or Parkinson disease.
