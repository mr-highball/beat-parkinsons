The scientific title names flaxseed oil and the summary says flaxseed oil extract. The structured intervention section names flaxseed in divided capsule doses. It names local pharmacist preparation and supplies no oil pressing method, chemical identity or branded product. The report methods instead describe a dried hydroalcoholic extract.

The randomized population had endometriosis, with pain recorded by symptom. The matched article supplies favorable comparisons for the flax arm but cannot verify a cold-pressed culinary exposure. See ../2024-PMID39759908-Khalajinia-Oral-Chamomile-Endometriosis-Pain-RCT.txt for the original table.
