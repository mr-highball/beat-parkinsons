BRIEF SUMMARY

To evaluate the efficacy and safety of tenofovir disoproxil fumarate in the treatment of
Parkinson's disease. The changes of motor symptoms and the occurrence of adverse
reactions in early Parkinson's disease patients who took tenofovir disoproxil fumarate
and did not take Tenofovir disoproxil fumarate at different time points were compared.
