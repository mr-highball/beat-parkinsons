Title: Mind Your Nose odor-memory trial
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=12622000116729
Registry ID: ACTRN12622000116729
First registered: 2022-01-24
Text coverage: factual registration and same-trial linkage.

The prospectively registered study evaluates odor-memory training in older adults with subjective cognitive decline using a visual-memory comparison. The 2025 publication (DOI 10.1002/trc2.70120) explicitly names this registration. It reports tea sachets used as odor cues and does not name the varieties or administer tea as a beverage.
