Title: Safety evaluation and clinical effects of a combined Khao Kum rice and dill extract in transient ischemic attack and ischemic stroke
Registry: Thai Clinical Trials Registry
Identifier: TCTR20201224001
Official record: https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/show/TCTR20201224001
Official XML: https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/export/xml/TCTR20201224001
Public registration date: 2020-12-24
Review date: 2026-11-14 US Eastern time
Text type: original factual extract of verified registry fields

The Thai registry planned first enrollment on March 15, 2021 for 60 participants. The three interventions are matched placebo and combined rice and dill extract at 600 and 1,200 mg/day. The listed primary outcome is hematological and clinical chemistry changes at six weeks. The listed secondary outcomes include six-week NIHSS score, serum biomarkers, safety and side effects, electrocardiography, and stool measures. Neither earlier NIHSS time points nor distinct item estimates are supplied. The export displays enrollment by invitation and a results completion field dated December 29, 2022. The results summary, public results date and results-link fields are blank. The related primary paper is PMID39599732, DOI 10.3390/nu16223946, first public November 19, 2024.
