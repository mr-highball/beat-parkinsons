Title: Analysis of changes in the gut microbiome, and dosing of dopaminergic therapy under supplementation of short-chain fatty acids and prebiotics as dietary supplements in the context of PD
Registry identifier: DRKS00027061
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00027061
Format: Selected official registry fields paraphrased for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-17 UTC
First public registration: 2021-11-19 (retrospective)
Design: Three active groups receiving 2-fucosyllactose, a short-chain fatty acid combination, or both. No Perilla exposure is specified.
Outcomes: Microbial diversity and metabolites are primary. Stool consistency and defecation habits using a standardized questionnaire are secondary.
Discrepancies: The registry allocation field says nonrandomized even though its narrative says randomized. It lists 72 target and 80 final. The two linked reports describe a randomized cohort of 72 with 71 participants in the bowel report's plotted groups. These differences remain unresolved.
Linked papers: PMID 41350593 (2025) and PMID 42315784 (2026), two reports from one study family.
