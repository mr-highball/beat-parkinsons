Title: An Investigation of the Impact of Fruit on the Gut Microbiota and Its Metabolites: Connections to Human Health
Source: ClinicalTrials.gov
URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05063929
Official API: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05063929
Registry ID: NCT05063929
First posted: 2021-10-01
Status: Completed. The official registry record showed no posted results on 2026-11-19 UTC.
Coverage: selected public registry fields matched to primary report.

Randomized crossover with actual enrollment 40. The registry contrasts two cup-equivalents of fruit per day with restriction to about one-half cup. Its three structured primary measures cover fecal microbiome composition, processing speed and vascular health over eight weeks. No structured balance or falls endpoint is listed. The 2025 original full report specifies the six-fruit menu and reports balance as one of its additional measurements. Group assignment varied the amount of the fruit combination. No fruit was randomized individually.

Outcome report: PMID 41081007, PMCID PMC12508899, DOI 10.1016/j.cdnut.2025.107544. The trial and the 2025 article describe one cohort.
