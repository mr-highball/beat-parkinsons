The registry describes a blinded, randomized parallel comparison in children aged 6–11 with ADHD at Roozbeh Hospital. Both arms were to receive methylphenidate with either sulforaphane or placebo for eight weeks. ADHD Rating Scale severity, assessed by a parent or teacher at weeks 0, 4 and 8, is the registered primary outcome. The target sample was 50 and recruitment was marked complete. No numerical outcomes are posted in the inspected registration.
The setting, age range, design, comparator and rater details make this a plausible registration for the later clinical report PMID 41604557. The publication abstract reports 70 randomized and describes the sulforaphane amount per day, while the registry lists an amount per kilogram per day.
