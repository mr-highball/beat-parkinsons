Title: RT001 in Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis
Publication date: 2021-02-21 (first public registry posting)
Record: NCT04762589
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04762589
Text coverage: Official registry API data, distilled.

The completed, randomized phase 2 study enrolled 43 participants with ALS. The comparator capsules contain 960 mg safflower oil. People took three capsules three times daily for four weeks and then three capsules twice daily during the next twenty weeks. The other group received the same schedule of RT001. Primary outcome is change in total ALSFRS-R at 24 weeks. Its swallowing item is one component of the score. The registry gives no separate item estimate and has no posted results module. Oil pressing method was unreported.
