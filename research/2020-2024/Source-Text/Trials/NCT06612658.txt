ClinicalTrials.gov v2 record verified: NCT06612658 (status: UNKNOWN; hasResults: False; start: 2024-10-01; enrollment: 40). Intervention: proprietary encapsulated Capsicum extract beadlet standardized to 2% capsaicinoids (Capsimax) vs placebo in crossover design. Primary outcomes: resistance exercise performance and isometric strength. Secondary outcomes: energy expenditure, nitric oxide release, skeletal muscle oxygenation, subjective pain, rating of perceived exertion (RPE), and reaction time. No posted results.
