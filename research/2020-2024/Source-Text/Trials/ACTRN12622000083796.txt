First registered: 2022-01-21.
Coverage: Factual current official registration, last updated 2026-09-23; historical formulation versions and trial results unverified.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

The current comparator description lists cold-pressed walnut oil with avocado, rosemary oleoresin, sage and medium-chain triglycerides, without proportions. This is a mixed placebo vehicle, not a separately assigned walnut-oil trial. Primary outcome is total ESAS symptom distress at day 14; individual items including pain and adverse events are planned through days 7–28.

The record states recruiting, 128 accrued of a target 150. Accrual is not an analyzed outcome. No results or isolated oil estimate are reported in the inspected record. The 2024 protocol identifies this trial but does not list the oil ingredients.

Protocol recruitment start August 8, 2022 differs from the registry's first-participant date December 14, 2022; event definitions and the discrepancy remain unresolved. Earlier MedCan trials are distinct. Current formulation text does not verify every past participant's exposure or a Parkinson pain effect.
