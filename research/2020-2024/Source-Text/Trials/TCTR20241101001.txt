Coverage: Original factual extract of official trial fields and public results statement.

The intervention is pepper leaves prepared by air-drying, further drying and coarse grinding and packaged in 2 g tea bags. The active comparator uses similarly prepared chamomile flowers. The primary outcome is the Insomnia Severity Index on days 7, 14 and 21. Sleep quality on the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index and stress on ST-5 are secondary outcomes on the same dates. The public results field describes improvement in sleep and stress in general terms and supplies no group-level estimates. The results-date field gives October 31, 2024, one day before the registration date. The 2025 article identifies Piper nigrum leaves, supplies numeric insomnia results and does not study ground pepper fruit or a separate Parkinson cohort.
