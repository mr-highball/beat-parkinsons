ClinicalTrials.gov registry record NCT05130359 (first posted 2021-11-23; actual start 2022-09-01; actual completion 2025-05-31; status COMPLETED; HasResults: False; industry sponsor TCI Co., Ltd.; actual enrollment n=56). Designed to test banana peel extract over 4 weeks with primary outcomes including PSQI, Insomnia Severity Index (ISI), sleep monitoring, serum serotonin, and morning salivary melatonin. No results are posted on ClinicalTrials.gov and intervention is banana peel extract rather than baked or raw banana pulp.
