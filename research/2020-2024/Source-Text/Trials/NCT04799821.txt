Title: The Role of Walnut Consumption on Sleep Quality
Date first posted: 2021-03-16
Last registry update: 2023-11-29
Status: Completed, no results posted.
Registry enrollment: 100, type ACTUAL.
Design: Open-label randomized crossover, eight-week periods and two-week washout. Daily walnuts at dinner vs nut abstention.
Registered primary outcomes: Actigraphy-derived sleep onset latency, sleep efficiency, wake after sleep onset and sleep duration, and urinary 6-sulfatoxymelatonin.
Registered secondary outcomes: BMI, body fat, waist measurement, food cravings and well-being.
Published outcome distinction: The 2025 article reports 76 participants and provides Epworth Sleepiness Scale estimates. The public registry outcome list does not enumerate Epworth.
