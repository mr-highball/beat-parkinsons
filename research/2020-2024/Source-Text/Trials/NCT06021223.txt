ClinicalTrials.gov v2 record verified: NCT06021223 (status: COMPLETED; hasResults: False; start: 2023-08-22; enrollment: 189 healthy adults). Interventions: high-dose chicken extract supplement, low-dose chicken extract supplement, or placebo. Primary outcomes: Cogstate Brief Battery reaction time/sustained attention, Deary-Liewald reaction time, psychomotor vigilance test, Trail Making Test, POMS/VAS fatigue, vigor, and motivation, ASL/BOLD fMRI, metabolomics, plasma carnosine/anserine. Secondary outcome: modified PSQI sleep quality. No results posted as of verification.
