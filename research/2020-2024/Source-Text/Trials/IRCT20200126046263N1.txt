Registration: IRCT20200126046263N1
First registered: 2020-02-18
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/45399
Coverage: Selected original registry protocol fields. Reviewed November 15, 2026.

The public protocol names migraine, ginger powder tablets and starch placebo for three months, with 108 target participants. Registered outcomes are migraine impact, intensity and frequency. No anxiety outcome appears among the listed primary or secondary outcomes. Helli et al., DOI 10.5812/jjnpp-120449, cite this registration and describe dried extract tablets, propranolol in both arms and a Beck Anxiety Inventory measurement.
