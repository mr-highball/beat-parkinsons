Text coverage: Selected official registration fields and comparison with linked clinical reports.

In children with autism, the registration describes three groups and assesses date consumption alongside usual behavior and nutritional care. Posted primary outcome plans concern oxidative stress and stool microbiota. Secondary measures include standardized developmental and autism domains. The registry lists completed status and 120 actual participants, with nonrandomized allocation and no masking. It does not post group results.

The linked behavior and gastrointestinal articles report 131 completers and describe random allocation. The group's later biomarker article identifies Ajwa dates and uses this NCT number. Link records to a single trial family, leaving its enrollment and allocation discrepancy unresolved. The planned scales and observed behavioral activity do not establish Parkinson apathy severity or an independently measured change in voluntary motivation in Parkinson disease.
