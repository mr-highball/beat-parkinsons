2021 — Registered Russian tarragon bar palatability and absorption study

Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05130593
Identifier: NCT05130593

The registry first posted November 23, 2021. The protocol tested a bar with a soy-protein Russian tarragon complex for palatability and assayed a tarragon-associated compound in plasma. The registered intervention concerns a formulated bar, and the protocol does not collect clinical smell, facial expression or walking outcomes.
