BRIEF SUMMARY

Adults with Parkinson's Disease experience an array of challenges and changes in daily
living, behaviors, and functioning throughout the progression of their condition. The
investigators want to learn more about the effects of Parkinson's Disease on individuals'
social behavior and quality of life (QoL) compared to people of the same age without
Parkinson's Disease.
