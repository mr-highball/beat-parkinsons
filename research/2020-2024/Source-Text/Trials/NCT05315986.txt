Registry title: Exploration of Relationships Between Sleep, Gut Health and Cognition

The trial lists standardized Safr'Inside saffron extract in a gummy, a matched placebo gummy and enhanced sleep education. Feasibility outcomes include headband use and quality, along with Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, Insomnia Severity Index and objective sleep measures. The registry shows an estimated sample of 48 and status Unknown. It posts no results. This is a trial lead for a processed extract in people with poor sleep.

Journal publication matched on 2026-11-25. The official API still shows the original 2022 record with no posted registry results. The 2025 Food & Function paper names this NCT explicitly and reports 52 randomized participants, with 26 per group. The article reports the Insomnia Severity Index time-by-treatment interaction as p = 0.3 and the global Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index interaction as p = 0.02. The registry lists PHQ-9 change, and the paper reports PHQ-9 at baseline. The paper and registry are one trial family.
