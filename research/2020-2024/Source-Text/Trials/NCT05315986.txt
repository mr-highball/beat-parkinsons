Registry title: Exploration of Relationships Between Sleep, Gut Health and Cognition

The trial lists standardized Safr'Inside saffron extract in a gummy, a matched placebo gummy and enhanced sleep education. Feasibility outcomes include headband use and quality, along with Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, Insomnia Severity Index and objective sleep measures. The registry shows an estimated sample of 48 and status Unknown. It posts no results. This is a trial lead for a processed extract in people with poor sleep.
