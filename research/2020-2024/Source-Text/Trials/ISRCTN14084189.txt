Title: Effect of StressZen on stress, mood and cortisol in students
Registry ID: ISRCTN14084189
Source: https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN14084189
First assigned: 2024-01-26.
Text coverage: Official ISRCTN trial record and PDF.

The randomized crossover compared a blend of lemon balm, elderberry and sacred basil with placebo in healthy students. Final enrollment was 42. Registered outcomes include Profile of Mood States, perceived stress and cortisol. The record and sources checked provide no separate depression contrast or independently assigned brewed lemon balm exposure. No Parkinson population is specified.
