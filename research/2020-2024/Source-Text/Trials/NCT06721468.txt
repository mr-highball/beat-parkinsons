Trout Consumption in Young Children and Families and Brain Health

The protocol describes sensory testing of three strains of trout and repeated-exposure groups, some with child-centered nutrition phrases or nutrition education. A puzzle-game comparison is also recorded. The project measures liking, intake, cognition and change in emotion score at 12 weeks. The record provides no results and no Parkinson cohort.

This plain-text snapshot records selected official protocol and results-availability fields.
