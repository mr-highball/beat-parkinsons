BRIEF SUMMARY

Purpose of the study is the validity and reliability of the Turkish version of the
Swallowing Disturbance Questionnaire (SDQ-T), so that it would be used as an assessment
tool for Turkish Parkinson Disease's (PD) patients. Despite being translated and
validated in many languages, there is no validated Turkish version of SDQ to measure the
severity of dysphagia in PD.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Parkinson's disease (PD) patients who suffered from dysphagia have reported significantly
reduced quality of life. The swallow function is a multidirectional process that depends
on complex neuro-muscular network. The function can be evaluated using instrumental, such
as fibreoptic endoscopic evaluation of swallowing (FEES), videofluoroscopy, or well
structured questionnaires. There are many questionnaire and scales for assessment of
dysphagia in PD but very few have been validated. The study will assess the construct
validity and reliability of the Turkish version of Swallowing Disturbance Questionnaire.
The investigators developed the Turkish version of this questionnaire (SDQ-T), according
to the cross-cultural adaptation guidelines. Two translators translated the SDQ into
Turkish and a native English language speaker reverse-translated it into English. The
back translation was sent to the original author for proofreading. The SDQ questionnaire
consisted of 15 basic questions related with dysphagia symptoms in PD patients.

STUDY GROUPS AND INTERVENTIONS

Arm groups
  -
    Label: Study group
    Description
      The group consists of Parkinson's disease patients with dysphagia complaints. The
      participants will be given the Turkish version of the Swallow Disturbance Questionnaire
      (SDQ-T), which consisted of 15 questions and two scales evaluated with the fiberoptic
      endoscopic evaluation of swallowing. After two weeks, all participants will be given the
      SDQ-T for sampling.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Turkish Version of Swallow Disturbance Questionnaire
    Description
      Turkish version of the questionnaire consists of 15 questions about dysphagia and
      swallowing complaints. The minimum score is 0 and maximum score is 44,5. The lower scores
      mean a normal swallowing function, higher scores mean worst swallowing functions.
    Time frame: Baseline
  -
    Measure: Turkish Version of Swallow Disturbance Questionnaire
    Description
      Turkish version of the questionnaire consists of 15 questions about dysphagia and
      swallowing complaints. The minimum score is 0 and maximum score is 44,5. The lower scores
      mean a normal swallowing function, higher scores mean worst swallowing functions.
    Time frame: 2 weeks
  -
    Measure: Penetration and aspiration scale
    Description
      The scale consists of 8 levels for assessment of penetration and aspiration. The scores
      are from 0 to 8. Low score is better, higher score is worse.
    Time frame: Baseline
  -
    Measure: Yale Pharyngeal Residue Severity Rating Scale
    Description
      The scale consists of 5 assessment units and two localisations for pharyngeal residue.
      The scores are from 1 to 5 each localisation. Low score is better, higher score is worse.
    Time frame: Baseline
Secondary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Subject demographics
    Description: The median values of age and ratio of gender in all groups
    Time frame: Baseline
