BRIEF SUMMARY

To determine the effects of Lee Silverman Voice Treatment BIG on dynamic balance in
Parkinson's patients

DETAILED DESCRIPTION

This study will be conducted to compare the effects of Lee Silverman Voice Treatment BIG
and conventional physiotherapy on dynamic balance in patients of PD. Patients will be
recruited according to inclusion criteria and data will be collected from Riphah
rehabilitation center, National Hospital Lahore. Dynamic gait index will be used as data
collection tool to test dynamic balance of patients. Patients will be allocated to
intervention groups by randomization. After approval from ethical committee, data
collection will be started and informed consent will be taken from all patients.
