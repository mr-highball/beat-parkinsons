Title: Cumin essential oil cognitive pilot registration
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000057216
Format: Factual extract from the cited record.

UMIN000050328, receipt R000057216, first publicly disclosed May 31, 2024. The record describes 12-week randomized oral cumin essential oil versus placebo in older adults. Cognition is the primary outcome and biomarkers are secondary. The reported final follow-up is in 2023, before public disclosure. Registry numerical outcome fields are empty in the record checked. The 2026 Ito article reports results for the same registered cohort.
