REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (UPDRS)
    Description
      UPDRS has four parts: part I (nonmotor experiences of daily living, total score=16); part
      II (patient motor experience of daily living, total score=52); part III (clinician motor
      examination, total score=56); part IV (complications, total score=23). The total score of
      all the four parts is147. Higher scores means a worse outcome.
    Time frame: Two years postoperatively
  -
    Measure: Burke Fahn Marsden's Dystonia Rating Scales(BFMDRS)
    Description
      BFMDRS has two parts: BFMDRS movement part (BFMDRS-M) and BFMDRS disability part
      (BFMDRS-D). The total score of BFMDRS-M is 120 and the total score of BFMDRS-D is 30.
      Higher scores means a worse outcome.
    Time frame: Two years postoperatively
