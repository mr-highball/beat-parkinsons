Title: Fisetin to Improve Physical Function in Stage I-III Breast Cancer Survivors
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05595499
NCT ID: NCT05595499
First posted: 2022-10-27
Last update posted: 2026-07-17
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated official registration

The randomized placebo-controlled study evaluates isolated fisetin
after chemotherapy for breast cancer. The registration showed
recruiting status, estimated enrollment of 88 and no posted results.
The primary outcome is change in six-minute walk distance. Its
published protocol (PMID 41835341) includes a recalled fall count in
the physical activity questionnaire. Neither source reports trial
outcomes or studies raw strawberries or a Parkinson population.
