The official record describes a randomized 24-week, 2:1 compound versus placebo comparison with a primary MCCB composite outcome. It reports actual enrollment of 125 and has no registered outcome results. The detailed description identifies Third Jiangyin Hospital and the structured location field identifies Second Xiangya Hospital in Changsha. It does not partition enrollment between these sites.

The separate Chen and Huang publications both cite this registration (PMID 40133020 and PMID 39832347). The 2020 NCT04521868 record names two different sites in separate fields and has no posted results. Chen analyzed 42 long-stay patients at Jiangyin and reports no clear MCCB composite or subscore difference. The exact attention values remain unavailable. Huang analyzed 77 patients at Xiangya and used the PANSS cognitive factor, with no separate attention measure in the paper or supplement. Participant overlap remains unresolved, so the reports contribute one linked family of uncertain independence. Preparation and population differ from the frozen claim. The registration remains linked to both reports for provenance without treating them as independent trials.
