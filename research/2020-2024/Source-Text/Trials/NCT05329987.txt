BRIEF SUMMARY

This observational trial aims to evaluate the effect of Rehabilitation on Postural
Transfers (PTs) in subjects affected by Parkinson Disease. The PTs are evaluated by an
inertial sensor (a device composed by an accelerometer, a gyroscope and a magnetometer)
attached to the subjects. The data obtained by the inertial sensor are kinematic (e.g.
acceleration and angular speed) and spatiotemporal parameters (e.g. time to completion
and velocity). Additional clinical evaluations are carried out at the beginning and end
of the rehabilitative intervention.

REGISTERED OUTCOME MEASURES

Primary outcomes
  -
    Measure: Change in execution time of Postural Transfers
    Description
      The execution time is measured via an inertial measurement unit attached to the back of
      the patient
    Time frame: Change from Baseline at 10 weeks
  -
    Measure: Change of execution velocity of Postural Transfers
    Description
      The execution velocity is measured via an inertial measurement unit attached to the back
      of the patient. Specifically the velocity are measured as the Root Mean Square of

    Time frame: Change from Baseline at 10 weeks

    Measure: Change of antero-posterior acceleration during Postural Transfers execution

      to the back of the patient.
    Time frame: Change from Baseline at 10 weeks

    Measure: Change of superior-inferior acceleration during Postural Transfers execution

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility criteria
  Inclusion Criteria:

    -  Subjects with Idiopathic Parkinson's Disease;

    -  Subjects should be in a positive therapeutic window or in the ON phase during the
       evaluations;

    -  Subjects must be able to walk at least 10 meters independently with or without any
       walking aid

  Exclusion Criteria:

    -  Non-clinically stable subjects;

    -  Subjects in OFF phase during the evaluations;

    -  Other neurological conditions (e.g. stroke, peripheral neuropathies, etc.);

    -  Other pathologies that can negatively influence the subject's walking ability (e.g.
       surgery at the lower limbs in the previous 6 months, important deformities at the
       lower limbs, etc.)
