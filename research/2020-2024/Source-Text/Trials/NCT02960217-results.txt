The double-blind crossover compared UX007 (triheptanoin) with a safflower-oil comparator in Glut1 deficiency syndrome. The trial recorded 44 participants in the enrollment field. The disease and endpoints differ from idiopathic Parkinson disease.

The adverse-events module records constipation in 1 of 43 participants in the double-blind UX007 exposure group (EG000), 1 of 42 in the double-blind placebo exposure group (EG001), and 3 of 33 in the open-label UX007 group (EG002). The double-blind placebo was safflower oil. These are exposure-group adverse-event counts and give no constipation severity or no-oil contrast. The frequency threshold for the other-adverse-events table is 5%.
