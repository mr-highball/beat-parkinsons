Registry ID: UMIN000039127
https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000044623
First disclosed 2020-01-18. Results update 2021-07-26.
Official registry summary. Twenty healthy adults ages 50–59 in an eight-week double-blind EPA-rich fish oil vs corn oil comparison for exercise fatigue. The primary outcome is VAS subjective fatigue after exercise. The register reports no group difference and supplies no arm numbers. Separate trial from UMIN000037848.
