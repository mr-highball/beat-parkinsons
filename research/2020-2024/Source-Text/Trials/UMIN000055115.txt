UMIN000055115: Effects of exercise and green tea consumption interventions on physical and mental function in older people

Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000062960
Source credit: UMIN Clinical Trials Registry and registering investigators
Record: R000062960
First public posting: 2024-08-07
Public version used: https://rctportal.mhlw.go.jp/s/detail/um?trial_id=UMIN000055115
Text coverage: selected official registration facts, reformatted and summarized
Sponsor: Kagoshima University
Funding: ITO EN Ltd

Parallel cluster-randomized trial. Participants blinded. Target enrollment 80 community-dwelling older adults. The 24-week comparison planned green tea powder versus placebo powder. Both arms had weekly group sessions and monthly exercise classes. Registered physical function includes grip and lower limb strength, gait speed, physical performance and balance. Other measures include body composition, cognitive function, frailty, depression, sleep and physical activity, and urinary biomarkers.
