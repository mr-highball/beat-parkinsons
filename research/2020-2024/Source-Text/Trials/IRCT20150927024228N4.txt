Title: Melissa, anise and rose formula for constipation-predominant irritable bowel syndrome
Registry ID: IRCT20150927024228N4
Source: https://irct.ir/trial/56072
WHO bridge: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT20150927024228N4
First registered: 2021-06-14.
Text coverage: Official IRCT and WHO trial fields, matched to the 2024 author report.

The prospective register describes a double-blind four-week randomized capsule comparison. The active formulation contains aqueous lemon balm and anise extracts with rose powder. The comparator is cornstarch. The primary registry list includes depression and anxiety by questionnaire, IBS symptom severity and quality of life at baseline and four weeks. The registry gives 2021-10-07 for planned first enrollment. The author report describes recruitment beginning 2021-07-17, also after registration.

The 2024 outcome report identifies this registration and gives HADS depression, as well as anxiety, for 88 completers out of 96 randomized. Its depression interval crosses zero. The gastrointestinal companion reports the same randomized and completed counts and covers different outcomes of the same likely trial family. The exposure is a mixture in capsules.
