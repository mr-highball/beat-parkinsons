Title: Lemon balm tea and Damask rose aromatherapy in gynecology residents
Registry ID: IRCT20220821055767N1
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/65402
WHO bridge: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT20220821055767N1
First registered: 2023-09-24, retrospectively.
Text coverage: Official trial registry fields reviewed 2026-11-17.

The record describes an unblinded randomized crossover, target enrollment 40, with two weeks of lemon balm tea versus two weeks of rose essential-oil inhalation and a two-week washout. Beck Depression Inventory scores are measured before and after intervention. The official page marks recruitment complete. Actual enrollment and posted results are unavailable.

A 2024 author conference abstract uses conflicting lemongrass and lemon balm wording and differs in period and washout duration. A 2025 same-author journal abstract describes anxiety in 20 residents. Their identity as reports of this registration remains provisional. The registered depression contrast and its precision remain open.
