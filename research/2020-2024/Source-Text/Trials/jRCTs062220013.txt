Title: Swallowing function with current sensory stimulation for Parkinson's disease
Trial ID: jRCTs062220013
Source: https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/en-latest-detail/jRCTs062220013
First posted: 2022-05-09.
Results posted: 2024-07-31.
Coverage: Official English registry, intervention, participant flow and results.

One open-label arm enrolled 27 people with Parkinson disease and 25 finished eight weeks of neck interferential current stimulation. The registry reports improved prevalence of normal provoked cough at the end of treatment. EAT-10 status also improved in the single-arm observations. The summary reports no statistically detectable change in videofluoroscopic penetration or aspiration at the end of treatment or after the next eight weeks, and no pneumonia events in the observation window. It reports no procedure-attributed skin or neurological adverse events.

Nakamori and colleagues' protocol PMID 37342176 and swallowing-sound article PMID 37837871 refer to this same registry family. Related reports on a water-bolus test and baseline rigidity are linked in the local choking follow-up. Cough, symptom questionnaires, bolus aspiration and drooling are different outcomes. There was no raw fig exposure.
