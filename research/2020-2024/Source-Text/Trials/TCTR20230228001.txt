The Thai registry describes a randomized comparison of the enhanced recovery pathway alone and the same pathway plus ginger. It lists 160 planned enrollees and 500 mg Abhaibhubejhr ginger capsules taken two at a time. It describes the main endpoint as duration to return of postoperative bowel function, measured in hours or days, and a food-quantity secondary endpoint. Both fields also carry a registry timeframe label of 12 months after the intervention. The publication reports perioperative hours for its efficacy measurements. The export status is Unknown, a completion-date field is populated, and the public outcome and adverse-event fields are blank. The paper cites this identifier and reports 160 randomized with 80 per arm. It provides the results in Table 2.
