ClinicalTrials.gov Official Protocol Summary (NCT06561750; Actual Start: 2023-11-01; Actual Completion: 2024-01-30; Status: COMPLETED; hasResults: False; Enrollment: 12 actual):
Randomized parallel trial in 12 young physically inactive women undergoing a BIODEX eccentric exercise protocol to induce delayed-onset muscle soreness, randomized to 12 g pure green rooibos tea brewed in 1200 mL freshly boiled water (~6 cups/day) vs 1200 mL water control over 72 hours. Registered primary outcomes: change from baseline to 72 hours in serum creatine kinase, 10-point perceived muscle soreness scale, Perceived Recovery Status (PRS) scale, TNF-alpha, and IL-6; secondary outcomes: quadriceps swelling and vertical squat-jump contractile function.

Verified Round 6 Critical Appraisal:
Although marked COMPLETED in January 2024, the official registry contains NO posted results (hasResults=False) and PubMed/web searches through November 2026 identified no published outcome article. Unblinded taste/water comparator limitations are also noted in the protocol description. Protocol-only registry record (kind=registry_without_results; claim_effect=none).
