Title: Botanical iron extract for adults with anemia or iron-deficiency anemia
Registration: NCT06096103
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06096103
Official structured record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT06096103
First posted: 2023-10-23
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated official registration and identified study report

The study compares an iron-standardized botanical extract with and
without a vitamin C botanical formulation and a placebo in adults with
anemia. Registered outcomes concern blood counts, iron measures,
subjective well-being and laboratory safety. The official structured
record did not contain posted results on the access date.

The 2025 primary paper identifies the study materials as curry leaf
extract with or without amla, gives this registration and cross-lists
CTRI/2023/10/059283. It reports no adverse events. The trial does not
specifically measure rest tremor, and the enrolled population does not
address Parkinson symptoms. The intervention is an extract rather
than cooked curry leaf.

Publication: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12640369/
