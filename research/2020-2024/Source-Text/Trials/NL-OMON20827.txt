The Dutch national archive cross-references the original Netherlands
Trial Register identifier NL9851 and a follow-up ToetsingOnline record
52339, listed as NL-OMON52339. The ethics identifier is NL78755.056.21.
The individually archived English and Dutch records identify the same
study. The archived registration reports 100 enrolled and shows
recruitment status as of the archive version.

This is an open-label randomized crossover trial with a 12-week
anti-inflammatory diet period and a 24-week washout. It includes
people with Parkinson disease and other diagnoses. The primary
outcome is global functioning via OQ-45. The Parkinson assessment
includes the MDS-UPDRS Part III examination and a non-motor symptoms
questionnaire. The archived record does not specify a separate rest
tremor result, olive-oil grade, or raw apple assignment.

The later investigator protocol reports 107 enrolled overall, including
49 people with Parkinson disease. It gives last follow-up on 2025-09-05
and analysis ongoing when posted in June 2026. Those figures refer to
the later protocol report, while 100 and the recruitment label come
from the older registry snapshot.
