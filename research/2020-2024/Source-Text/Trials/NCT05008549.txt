Title: A Study to Assess the Synergistic Effects of a Cranberry Beverage on Cognitive Function
Registry: NCT05008549

First posted: August 17, 2021
Last update: July 7, 2023
Retrieved: November 15, 2026
Status: Completed with 25 enrolled and no posted results.
Coverage: Selected public registry fields verified from the official source.

The six-period crossover compared reconstituted cranberry concentrate alone, matched placebo, cranberry with lemon balm, cranberry with tart cherry powder, cranberry with L-theanine, and L-theanine with placebo. Each condition lasted seven days. Outcomes planned included COMPASS accuracy and speed composites, total Profile of Mood States disturbance, and salivary cortisol. The record gives no standalone depression result and does not assign dried cranberry or cooked whole fruit.
