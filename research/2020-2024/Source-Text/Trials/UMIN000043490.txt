Title: Study on clinical application of aromatherapy for patients with fibromyalgia
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000042893
Government mirror: https://rctportal.mhlw.go.jp/detail/um?trial_id=UMIN000043490
Format: Selected registry fields and results, summarized for this catalog.
Retrieved: 2026-11-13 UTC
Registry: UMIN000043490 (receipt R000042893)
Institution: International University of Health and Welfare Shioya Hospital
Public disclosure: 2021-03-03. Registry entered: 2021-03-02.
Anticipated start: 2019-12-18. Last follow-up: 2020-12-10.
Recruitment status: Completed. Target enrollment: 6. Reported enrollment: 10.
Design: Open randomized crossover with two three-month treatment periods. Participants applied oil with and without geranium essential oil in opposite sequences. The dissertation and journal paper identify the base as macadamia nut oil.
Planned outcomes: Pain intensity, pain disability, EuroQol, depression, State-Trait Anxiety Inventory, salivary biomarkers, blood pressure and heart rate.
Reported results: Registry reports no clear difference in anxiety scores between treatment conditions, no clear pre/post blood pressure changes and a clear heart-rate reduction in the geranium condition alone. It provides no numerical anxiety estimates or depression and pain results.
Related primary publication: https://doi.org/10.1177/1934578X251322308
Relevance: No Parkinson population, oil-free arm or oral oil exposure.
