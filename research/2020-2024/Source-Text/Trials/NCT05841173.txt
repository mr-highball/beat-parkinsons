Title: PanTrek Study
Registry ID: NCT05841173
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05841173
First posted: 2023-05-03
Data snapshot: 2026-11-17, ClinicalTrials.gov official API.

The five-arm study registers arms involving exercise, diet and a mixed plant formulation with lemon balm, ginseng and ketones. It includes a secondary Hamilton Depression Rating Scale change at 12 weeks. The official status is unknown and no results are posted. The assigned interventions do not provide an isolated brewed lemon balm comparison.
