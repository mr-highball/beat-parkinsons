Title: Affron standardized saffron extract for adults with low mood
Registry ID: ACTRN12623001358639
Source: https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=ACTRN12623001358639
First registered: 2023-12-21.
Text coverage: Official ANZCTR registration fields, enrolment status and linked results publication verified 2026-11-19.

The prospective registration predates the recorded first enrolment of 2024-05-01. The screening population was adults aged 18–70 with subclinical low mood by DASS-21. Neurological conditions, including Parkinson disease and Alzheimer disease, were excluded. The twelve-week parallel comparison used 28 mg/day of Affron standardized saffron extract in tablets versus matching placebo. Target enrolment was 200. ANZCTR shows 202 enrolled and completed recruitment.

The registered primary endpoint is the DASS-21 depression subscale. Sleep disturbance, sleep-related impairment, anxiety, stress and wellbeing were separately recorded outcome domains. The registry links DOI 10.1016/j.tjnut.2025.05.024 as the published study result. The registration date and the results-link posting date are distinct.
