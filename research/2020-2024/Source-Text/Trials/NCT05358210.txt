Text coverage: Selected official registration fields and planned outcomes.

Sixty-one adults aged 70 or older enrolled in the completed randomized parallel trial. It assigned frozen blueberries versus an isocaloric dried-date comparator for 12 weeks, with separate guidance about other foods. Whole dried dates are an assigned exposure in this study despite the blueberry title. The registry does not name the date cultivar.

The planned measures include ambulatory blood pressure, orthostatic hypotension, lower-extremity function, cognition and secondary self-reported falls. There is no named apathy or pain severity endpoint and the registration does not specify a Parkinson cohort. No summary results or matched article are supplied in the registry. If outcomes are recovered, their direction is a blueberry-versus-date contrast, with no arm receiving neither food.
