Official smell-training protocol and sleepiness outcome

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05855369

The May 11, 2023 registry concerns smell training and neuromodulation for Long COVID. Eugenol is among the odorants and the Epworth Sleepiness Scale is a secondary outcome. The official record was Recruiting with no posted results in this check. The administration route is inhalation and the protocol has no isolated consumed ground clove exposure.
