Title: Results of a long-term safety study of cannabidiol oral solution in Rett syndrome
Publication date: 2022-08-08 (first official results posting)
Record: NCT04252586
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04252586?tab=results
Text coverage: Distilled official results, adverse-event group and formulation context.

The registry reports two participants affected by a nonserious Fall event among 21 at risk in the GWP42003-P group. The safety window spans the previous randomized trial baseline through the open-label extension visit at day 767. The nonserious reporting frequency threshold is 5%. The table counts affected people. It gives no recurring fall rate or details allocating falls to a particular treatment phase.

The drug solution includes strawberry flavoring, with no verified fruit content or separate fruit comparison. The observations illustrate a clinical fall report in a single-arm study in another neurologic condition.
