The January 2022 original used voucher-documented Mentha spicata subsp. spicata (GA-20-001). The investigators used dried aerial portions and an acetone and water extract. Figure 6 measured L-DOPA in oxidatively challenged rat-cell culture supernatant and reported no clear effect on that measure. The assay does not measure levodopa content in fresh leaves, Parkinson medication response or daily OFF time.
