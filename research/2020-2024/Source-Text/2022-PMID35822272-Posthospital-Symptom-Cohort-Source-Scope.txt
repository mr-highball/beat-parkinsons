Original study scope
The follow-up study records persistent symptoms and quality of life after COVID hospitalization. Its dates track clinical events, and SAGE names the publisher. The patient cohort did not receive a sage or date fruit contrast.

Continuing evidence gaps
The pain, fatigue and sleep findings remain in the post-infection cohort.
