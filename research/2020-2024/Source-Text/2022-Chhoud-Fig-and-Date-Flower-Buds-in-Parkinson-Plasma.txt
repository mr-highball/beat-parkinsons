Title: Phytochemical and bioactivities of male flower buds of fruit trees from southern Tunisia in Parkinson plasma
Source: https://doi.org/10.1007/s42250-022-00430-4
Full original: https://digibug.ugr.es/handle/10481/99298
DOI: 10.1007/s42250-022-00430-4
Publication date: 2022-07-15
Citation: Chhoud R et al. Chemistry Africa 5:1337–1350.
Coverage: Factual summary from the complete university-deposited original, sections 2.5–2.8 and 3.3 and Figures 4–6.

Investigators tested extracts from male flower buds of common fig, date palm and pistachio on isolated plasma from four people with Parkinson disease, one at each of four Hoehn and Yahr stages, and one healthy donor. The extracts were applied in the laboratory. Assay triplicates do not increase the number of participants. The outcomes were superoxide dismutase, glutathione reductase and a lipid-peroxidation marker. The report describes SOD changes across tested samples and a fig-related GR increase in some conditions for the stage-two donor. The data concern extracted male flower buds, with no edible fruit assigned and no measured tremor or other symptom outcome.
