The review concerns rosmarinic acid, a compound found in rosemary and other plants. Its abstract discusses depression and Parkinson disease as separate neurological contexts. The bibliography identifies an isolated rosmarinic acid and perilla mouse swim-behavior study. The accessible abstract and bibliography do not report a fresh culinary rosemary intervention with a depressive symptom result in Parkinson disease. The body remains pending for claim 0128.
