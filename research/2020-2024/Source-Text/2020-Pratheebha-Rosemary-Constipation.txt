Rosemary oil massage and defecation reports

selected methods and findings paraphrase

Published August 24, 2020. In adults with constipation, 15 received abdominal rosemary-oil massage and 15 peppermint-oil massage for 30 days. Figures 3 and 4 indicate defecation-benefit counts of 12 and 9, respectively. The benefit definition, allocation and adjusted group comparison are unclear. A pie chart percentage is not a reliable within-arm response rate. No sham massage, oral ground rosemary or Parkinson population.
