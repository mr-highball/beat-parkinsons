Title: Canola comparator and caregiver rated daytime sleepiness
Source: https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=pmc&id=9516351
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

Boone and colleagues, Early Human Development, 2022. Official NLM manuscript, Methods and Table 2. PMID 35644107, PMCID PMC9516351, NCT01683565.
The Preemie Tots trial randomized 31 very preterm toddlers aged 18 to 38 months with autism symptoms to fish/borage omega 3-6-9 or canola oil placebo for 90 days. This analysis of protocol sleep measures was secondary and not prespecified. The canola oil grade was unspecified.
Caregiver daytime sleepiness scores for active and canola groups were 1.48 and 1.47 at entry and 1.46 and 1.36 at study end. The adjusted active-minus-canola change was +0.06 (95% CI −0.13 to 0.25, p = .55). Four children lacked end values for this comparison. There was no oil-free arm or Parkinson cohort.
The 2018 primary trial report, PMCID PMC6251698, is the same cohort.
