The study compares fecal microbial profiles across Lewy body dementia, Parkinson disease, isolated REM sleep behavior disorder and control groups. Visual hallucinations characterize the dementia syndrome in the introduction. The reported models compare microbial communities and recorded covariates, with no itemized dried fig exposure or hallucination rate linked to intake.
