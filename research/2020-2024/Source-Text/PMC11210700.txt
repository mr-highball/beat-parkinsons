Title: In vivo and in vitro effects of crocetin and its amide derivative on acrylamide-induced neurotoxicity
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11210700/
PMID: 38948176
PMCID: PMC11210700
DOI: 10.22038/AJP.2023.22316
Publication: 2024 issue
Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article, gait and oxidative stress results.

The investigators measured rat gait and cortical oxidative biomarkers after exposure to acrylamide and compared crocetin with a structurally related amide. Crocetin improved the experimental gait score under the tested conditions and the amide comparator did not clearly change it. The cell work measures cellular injury or oxidative response. The report tests isolated compounds, a different injury model and different outcomes. It contains no measured depressive or fatigue outcome, whole ground or dried saffron arm, or fresh rosemary assignment.
