Original study Methods and section 3.4
Source: https://pubs.sciepub.com/jfnr/10/12/2/index.html
DOI 10.12691/jfnr-10-12-2
Checked: 2026-11-15
Coverage: Factual extract.

Open parallel randomized assignment to bananas (n=13) or no bananas (n=7), two weeks in healthy Japanese adults. No clear between-group difference in POMS2 fatigue–inertia or vigor–activity change. Only confusion–bewilderment differed. Improvement-defined subgroup is post hoc. No apathy severity or Parkinson disease.
