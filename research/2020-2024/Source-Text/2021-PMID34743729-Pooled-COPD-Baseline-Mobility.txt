Title: Disturbances in branched-chain amino acid profile and poor daily functioning in mildly depressed chronic obstructive pulmonary disease patients
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12890-021-01719-9
PMID: 34743729
PMCID: PMC8573879
DOI: 10.1186/s12890-021-01719-9
Publication date: 2021-11-07
Source depth: Full primary article on the publisher site.

The 2021 analysis pools baseline physical-function data across four COPD studies, including NCT03796455. It groups 78 participants by depression status. Walking distance and gait assessments are baseline comparisons, without an assigned oil contrast or a cohort-specific intervention denominator. This report is linked context and supplies no independent efficacy trial for the oil. The comparison includes 27 participants classified as depressed and 51 as nondepressed. The original ranges include measured walking distance, daily function and quality of life. The marginal walking-distance comparison by depression status does not identify stride length or a refined culinary oil effect.
