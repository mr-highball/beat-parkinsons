Title: Whole egg consumption and cognitive function among US older adults.
PMID: 34841618
DOI: 10.1111/jhn.12970
First published: 2022
Authors: An R, Li D, McCaffrey J, Khan N
Journal: Journal of human nutrition and dietetics : the official journal of the British Dietetic Association
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34841618/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Nationally representative cross-sectional analysis of NHANES 2011-2014 (n=2,816 US older adults aged >=60 y; 57% egg consumers averaging 34 g/d). Tested whole egg consumption against CERAD Word List Learning/Delayed Recall, Animal Fluency, Digit Symbol Substitution Test (DSST; attention/processing speed), and composite cognitive z-score. Results: completely NULL — neither whole-egg consumer status nor daily quantity was associated with any cognitive test score after multivariable adjustment.
