The final study compared plasma metabolites in 368 people with and without Parkinson disease, stratified by LRRK2 mutation status. Plasma caffeine was 71% lower in the Parkinson group overall after adjustment. Related metabolites and trigonelline were also lower. The association was stronger among LRRK2 mutation carriers. The paper describes multiple possible explanations, including differences in caffeine consumption or handling and reverse causation.

The poster described 376 people and a larger initial analyte set. The author group, consortium, four comparison groups and key analyte pattern link the poster to the final series. Keep these as one research family. The final article identifies disease status. It does not establish a brewed-coffee intervention or a fatigue outcome for Round 1 claim 0121.
