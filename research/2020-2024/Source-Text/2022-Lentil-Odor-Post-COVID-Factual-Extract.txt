Title: Lentil odor and persistent smell problems after COVID-19
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8886082/

A 2022 exploratory survey included 47 participants with persistent post-COVID taste or smell problems. Eleven described a rubber-like or elastic odor in legumes, pulses and brown bread as a group. One report specifically mentions beans, peas and lentils, alone or in soup. The study records altered odor perception of foods. It does not identify confirmed boiling, an intervention assigning lentil consumption, an objective smell score or a Parkinson cohort.
