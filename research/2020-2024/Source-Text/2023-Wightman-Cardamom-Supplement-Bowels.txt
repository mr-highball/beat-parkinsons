Factual supplement summary. Thirteen active and ten placebo bowel free-text comments are printed for day 90. Two active and six placebo comments mention constipation or being blocked up. One placebo comment describes improvement. The displayed comments are a selected subset, with no verified arm-specific prevalence, baseline or severity score. The adverse-event table separately lists zero versus two constipation reports. The intervention combines multiple botanicals and states neither the cardamom species nor the grind.
