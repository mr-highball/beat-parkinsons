Title: The graded multidimensional geometry of phenotypic variation and progression in neurodegenerative syndromes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11788217/
PMID: 39018014
PMCID: PMC11788217
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awae233
Publication date: 2024-07-17 (electronic publication date in original JATS)
Source depth: complete PMC JATS

The observational NACC data include 39 cognitive, behavioral and clinical measures across longitudinal profiles of Alzheimer and frontotemporal disorders. The study analyzes trajectories in phenotypic space without a dried fig intervention or food-timed Parkinson hallucination outcome.
