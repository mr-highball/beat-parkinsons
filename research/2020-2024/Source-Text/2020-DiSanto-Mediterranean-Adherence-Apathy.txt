Mediterranean diet score and apathy during quarantine
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7591702/
PMID: 33173523
PMCID: PMC7591702
DOI: 10.3389/fpsyt.2020.578628
Coverage: Original full text and Table 4

Di Santo and colleagues interviewed 126 older adults with subjective cognitive decline or mild cognitive impairment. Twelve met the Apathy Evaluation Scale threshold. MEDAS includes olive-oil intake within a wider diet score. Table 4 gives an unadjusted odds ratio of 3.02 (95% CI 0.86–10.63, p=.085) and an adjusted ratio of 2.76 (0.83–9.21, p=.099) for diet nonadherence and apathy. These contrasts are inconclusive. The Results paragraph calls its list of apathy associations significant, which differs from the reported diet comparison. The study does not estimate a refined olive-oil effect or measure a Parkinson group.
