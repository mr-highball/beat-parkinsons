Title: A Randomised Placebo-Controlled Study of Purified Anthocyanins on Cognition in Individuals at Increased Risk for Dementia

Text coverage: Verified complete publisher-format article deposited in the university repository, with Table 4 adverse-event rows.

The ACID trial compared purified anthocyanins sourced from bilberry and black currant with matched placebo in 206 older adults at increased risk for dementia. The primary 24-week memory outcome had adjusted mean difference 1.4 points (95% CI -0.9 to 3.7, p=0.23). The article separately examines change slopes. Table 4 reports one mild constipation event in the anthocyanin group (106 participants) and two in the placebo group (100 participants). All adverse events totalled nine versus seven (p=0.66 across event types). The three constipation reports are sparse safety events. They do not supply a dedicated severity comparison, raw-blueberry ingestion, or a Parkinson subgroup.

Trial registration: NCT03419039. The official registration's enrollment field is 212. The outcome article reports 206 included, so use the published outcome denominators.
