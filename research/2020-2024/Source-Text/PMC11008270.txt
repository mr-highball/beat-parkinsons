Title: A review on Gaucher disease: therapeutic potential of β-glucocerebrosidase-targeted mRNA/saRNA approach
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11008270/
PMID: 38617529
PMCID: PMC11008270
DOI: 10.7150/ijbs.87741
Publication date: 2024-03-17
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

This review examines treatment research for inherited glucocerebrosidase deficiency and its relevance to Gaucher disease. The disease and candidate therapy discussion is separate from an oral culinary exposure or a visual hallucination outcome in Parkinson disease.
