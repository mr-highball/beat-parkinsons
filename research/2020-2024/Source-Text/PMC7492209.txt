Title: Effects of Purified Anthocyanins in People at Risk for Dementia: Study Protocol for a Phase II Randomized Controlled Trial

Text coverage: Verified NLM original PMC XML article body and trial protocol methods. Factual extract.

The ACID protocol plans an episodic-memory comparison in older adults at increased risk for dementia. The product contains purified anthocyanins supplied from bilberry and black currant. The authors discuss possible constipation or stool color changes as reasons treatment allocation might become apparent. This is a prospective adverse-effect concern, not a measured constipation result. The trial uses neither raw blueberries nor a Parkinson cohort. Registry NCT03419039.
