The name Chinese date refers to jujube, not date palm. The review attributes a fruit-powder and ginger combination for indigestion or stomach pain to earlier reports. It supplies no measured effect isolated to ginger. Brine shrimp occur as assay organisms in a toxicity context, without a culinary shrimp comparison.
