Different motor outcomes with Cucurbita maxima extract

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10060065/
Coverage: Original full study, Tables 4 and 5, factual extract.

Wahid, Alqahtani and Khan, Behavioural Neurology, March 22, 2023. DOI 10.1155/2023/7509937. In mice receiving ethanol extract of Cucurbita maxima seed, Table 4 shows longer rotating-rod retention in higher-dose groups than control. At day 21, Table 5 shows faster stationary-rod arrival at the lower two extract doses and slower arrival at the highest dose (64.1 versus 26.3 seconds in the control). This shows discordance across tasks, doses and test days. Timed stationary-rod arrival is a learning and coordination measure. There is no stride-length, culinary oil or Parkinson cohort comparison.
