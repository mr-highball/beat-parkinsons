Title: Laryngopharyngeal reflux disease in a series of Kashmiri patients at a tertiary care setting
Authors: Syed Waseem Abbas, Majid Ul Islam Masoodi, Viqar Khursheed Mir
Original journal PDF: https://www.ijorl.com/index.php/ijorl/article/download/3396/1955
Identifiers: DOI 10.18203/issn.2454-5929.ijohns20220465
Date: March 2022 issue
Coverage: factual extract of the full primary Methods and Tables 2, 3, 5

The study followed 130 participants with laryngopharyngeal reflux. An interviewer obtained diet information before the participants received lifestyle changes, antacids and proton pump inhibitors. Baseline mean onion consumption score in Table 2 was 1.74, without a specification of cooking. Baseline mean hoarseness rating was 0.92 and Table 5 gives a four-week mean of 0.32 for the uniform treatment program. Results contain no paired analysis of onion and the voice outcome. Printed p=.45 and .49 for two overall dietary-score associations with laryngeal edema do not support the authors' label of significance. No Parkinson subtype or speech intelligibility outcome is identified.
