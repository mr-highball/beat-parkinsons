Title: Improved method for preparation and purification of recombinant α-synuclein: high-mannose-type free N-glycan prepared from an edible bean (Vigna angulari, Azuki bean) inhibits α-synuclein aggregation.
PMID: 35293991
DOI: 10.1093/bbb/zbac040
First published: 2022
Authors: Kosaka S, Katsube M, Maeda M, Kimura Y
Journal: Bioscience, biotechnology, and biochemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35293991/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cell-free in vitro assay (Kosaka et al., 2022, Biosci Biotechnol Biochem) purifying recombinant human alpha-synuclein from E. coli and testing high-mannose free N-glycan prepared from azuki bean (Vigna angularis). The isolated azuki N-glycan inhibited recombinant alpha-synuclein amyloid aggregation in vitro. No cellular, animal, or human Parkinson's clinical or symptom endpoints were evaluated; purified N-glycan does not equal whole cooked or boiled adzuki beans.
