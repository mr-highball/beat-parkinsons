Original source scope
Nine Parkinson participants with lateral axial dystonia were followed around prism use. The report gives a VAS time test of p=.005 and about 18.6% change versus baseline pooled across follow-up visits, including the post-withdrawal visit.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
