Title: Alleviation of Pain, PAIN Interference, and Oxidative Stress by a Novel Combination of Hemp Oil, Calamari Oil, and Broccoli: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Trial.
PMID: 37375558
PMCID: PMC10304948
DOI: 10.3390/nu15122654
First published: 2023
Authors: Carlisle C, Polley K, Panda C, Barron K, Hamrock M, Dominique A
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10304948/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT in n=25 chronic pain adults seeking chiropractic care (n=12 active hemp oil + calamari oil + broccoli supplement vs n=13 mineral oil placebo). Intervention associated with 52% pain intensity decrease, improved pain interference including sleep quality, and 29.4% lower PBMC ROS. Limits: multi-ingredient supplement bundling phytocannabinoids and omega-3s with broccoli; cannot attribute to broccoli alone.
