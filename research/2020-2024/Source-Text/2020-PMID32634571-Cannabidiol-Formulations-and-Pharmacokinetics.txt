Institutional publication record: https://openscholar.huji.ac.il/avidomb/publications/pharmacokinetic-investigation-synthetic-cannabidiol-oral-formulations-healthy

The reported crossover compared cannabidiol administered as powder, in sesame oil and in a self-emulsifying formulation in 12 healthy men. The abstract reports no clear difference in overall cannabidiol exposure between the latter two formulations. The exposure was a drug formulation. Neither the abstract nor the inspected preview describes an oil-only arm, cold-pressed culinary grade or depressive-symptom measurements in Parkinson disease.

The comparison verifies the carrier exposure described in the related review. The full report remains a route for verifying secondary outcomes and formulation details.
