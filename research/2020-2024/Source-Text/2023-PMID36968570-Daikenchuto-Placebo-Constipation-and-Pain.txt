Title: Randomized placebo comparison of Daikenchuto for chronic constipation
PMID: 36968570
PMCID: PMC10037039
DOI: 10.1002/jgh3.12870
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10037039/
Published: 2023-02-08
Archive scope: Evidence-focused summary of the inspected original article body.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

Of 67 screened, 63 people were randomized to Daikenchuto or placebo for four weeks (29 and 34). The primary global treatment score was 1.7 versus 1.0 (p=.021). Stool form favored the formula. Changes in spontaneous bowel movements, straining and incomplete evacuation were inconclusive. The abdominal pain change was −0.59 versus −0.13 (p=.063). Bloating changed by −0.60 versus 0.07 (p=.032). These are distinct endpoints in chronic constipation outside Parkinson disease.

The article describes 15 g three times per day after meals. UMIN000020570 specifies 5 g three times per day before meals for each assigned product. The sources disagree on the amount, timing and planned outcome wording. The amount actually administered remains unresolved. The reported preparation contains processed ginger, ginseng, Japanese pepper and maltose. The trial cannot estimate any one ingredient's effect.
