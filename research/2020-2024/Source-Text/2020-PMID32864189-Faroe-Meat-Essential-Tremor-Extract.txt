Title: Meat consumption and meat cooking practices in essential tremor: a population-based study in the Faroe Islands

The study compared 27 people with essential tremor and 200 controls.
Total current meat intake averaged 120.0 versus 113.6 g/day
(medians 89.0 versus 95.8, p=.90). Table 2 lists 14 intake categories,
including separately described chicken, fish and seabird preparations.
No individual category differed after correction for the 14 comparisons.
The comparison of meat cooking doneness was also inconclusive.

Among essential-tremor cases, blood harmane versus total current meat
intake gave Spearman rho −0.17 (p=.39). The study does not identify
baked turkey among the intake categories or estimate Parkinson
rest-tremor severity. The cited earlier Faroe blood analysis belongs
to the same population, rather than an independent dietary sample.
