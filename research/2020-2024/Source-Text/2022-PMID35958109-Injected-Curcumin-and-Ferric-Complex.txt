Original study scope
MPTP mice received intravenous free curcumin or intravenous ferric-curcumin coordination polymers, with separate saline and levodopa arms. The formulated compound had the strongest pole, open-field and rotarod findings. Free curcumin produced only slight favorable rotarod changes as reported.

Continuing evidence gaps
Falls from a forced rotarod are an assay of motor coordination, distinct from daily fall frequency. Pole timing can indicate motor function in mice. Route, formulation and endpoint prevent attribution to eating ground turmeric.
