Turmeric extract pain formula with planned PSQI daytime dysfunction
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06247813
Registration: NCT06247813
Coverage: official protocol including ingredient amounts and prespecified PSQI component

First posted 2024-02-08. The biomarker-selected inflammation formula for chronic pain contains turmeric extract 400 mg, black-pepper preparation 10 mg, ashwagandha 500 mg and olive-leaf extract 250 mg. Sleep is a secondary outcome, including the PSQI daytime dysfunction component. No separate Epworth comparison, Parkinson cohort, or grated-turmeric-only arm is specified. Registry status was unknown and no results were posted as captured 2026-11-16. The other biomarker-directed combinations do not furnish a randomized isolated turmeric comparison.
