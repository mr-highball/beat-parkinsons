Title: Effect of cold arid high-altitude environment on bioactive phytochemical compounds of organically grown Brassicaceae vegetables for nutri-health security in mountainous regions.
PMID: 38987575
PMCID: PMC11237107
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-64926-4
First published: 2024
Authors: Shukla S, Kumar N, Bhardwaj P, Pandita P, Patel MK, Thakur MS et al.
Journal: Sci Rep
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38987575/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Phytochemical RP-HPLC field analysis of Brassicaceae vegetables demonstrating contrasting metabolite profiles: high-altitude cabbage was enriched in sulforaphane (8.94 ug/mg) and kaempferol, whereas cauliflower was characterized predominantly by indole-3-carbinol (1.31 ug/mg) rather than sulforaphane. Confirms cauliflower cannot be equated with broccoli-sprout sulforaphane supplements.
