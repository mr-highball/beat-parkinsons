Registered avocado and mango meals with cognitive testing
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05353790
Coverage: Factual official protocol and endpoint summary

First posted April 29, 2022. The completed trial compared an avocado-and-mango diet with energy-matched controls in 82 adults with prediabetes. Registered secondary outcomes include Trail Making, Stroop and verbal fluency. The registry had no posted results. The protocol assigns whole fruits.
