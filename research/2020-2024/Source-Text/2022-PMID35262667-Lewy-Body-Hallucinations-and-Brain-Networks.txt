Title: Functional and structural brain network correlates of visual hallucinations in Lewy body dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9246710/
PMID: 35262667
PMCID: PMC9246710
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awac094
Publication year: 2022
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The original compares functional and structural networks across Lewy body dementia hallucination groups. Clinical ratings distinguish degrees of frequency and severity. Imaging and clinical associations do not test eating dried figs.

The Neuropsychiatric Inventory supplies frequency and severity ratings. A neuropsychiatric score and the network's direction of association are separate observations from exposures and daily event counts.
