Title: STN-DBS does not increase the risk of sialorrhea in patients with advanced Parkinson's disease
PMID: 35768423
PMCID: PMC9243012
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-022-00348-1
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9243012/
Publication: 2022-06-29
Coverage: Original article, clinical eligibility, incident case definition and model results.

The retrospective study included 88 patients receiving stimulation and 44 managed with medication. The outcome was incident sialorrhea, defined using UPDRS item 6 at a threshold of at least 2. Incidence rates were 49.2 and 43.7 per 1,000 person-years, respectively. The adjusted hazard ratio for stimulation was 1.4 (95% CI 0.7–2.8). The reported multivariable association with dysphagia was 3.5 (95% CI 1.6–7.8).

The study estimates recorded onset in nonrandomized groups, with no objective flow measurement or raw-fig comparison. The wide interval permits differences in either direction.
