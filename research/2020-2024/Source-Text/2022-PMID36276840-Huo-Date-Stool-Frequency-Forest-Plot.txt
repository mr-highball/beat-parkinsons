Coverage: Publisher Methods, Table 1, Results, and original Figure 3 reviewed. Figure values cross-checked against Eid's published Table 2.

The review includes 11 trials. Its stool-frequency forest plot contains four studies, comprising two kiwi studies, one orange-juice study and the previously published Eid whole Ajwa crossover. The date estimate in Figure 3 is a mean difference of 0.19 (95% CI −0.17 to 0.55, p = .30). The forest plot uses 22 observations in each column. Eid reported 22 enrolled and 21 completers in a crossover and reported grouped post-period means of 1.40 and 1.21 (SD 0.71 and 0.50). The width of the review's interval is consistent with treating the periods as independent groups. It does not supply a paired crossover interval. The Eid abstract and Table 2 specify a baseline comparison for the significant test, while the Results prose describes the comparison as against control.

The pooled kiwifruit subgroup estimate comes from separate trials. Figure 3 gives p = .65 for a test of subgroup differences. The data provide an indirect comparison across fruit groups, with no head-to-head comparison between dates and kiwi. One kiwi study contributes 97.9% of the overall fixed-effect weight in the figure. The 2015 dates study is a healthy-adult trial and the meta-analysis contributes no independent dried-date group or Parkinson pain or apathy estimate.
