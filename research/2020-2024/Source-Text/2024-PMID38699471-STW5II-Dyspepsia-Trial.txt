Title: Eight-week randomized trial of six-extract STW 5-II for functional dyspepsia
First published: 2024-05-02
PMID: 38699471
PMCID: PMC11064020
DOI: 10.1002/jgh3.13054
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11064020/
Archive scope: Primary publication methods, outcomes and study lineage verified.

The eight-week blinded trial was conducted in 2004–2005 and included 272 people in the intention-to-treat population. The 2024 paper identifies historical German authority submission 4021138 and study code STW 5-II/212-D-03-III-V. It gives no EudraCT ID. The six-extract formulation contains Melissa officinalis and five other extracts. The predefined repeated-measure gastrointestinal responder rate was 61.2% on product and 45.1% on placebo (p=.008). The change in the secondary single-time-point mean Gastrointestinal Symptom Score was inconclusive (p=.072 in the main text). Rome IV is a post hoc subset of the same cohort. The sponsor funded the trial. Gastrointestinal outcomes in this clinical population carry no isolated lemon balm effect or direct evidence for Parkinson depressive symptoms.
