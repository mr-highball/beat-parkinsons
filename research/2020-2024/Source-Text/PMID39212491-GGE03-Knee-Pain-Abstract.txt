Steamed ginger extract and mild knee osteoarthritis
Source: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2024/fo/d4fo01640h
PMID: 39212491
DOI: 10.1039/d4fo01640h
Coverage: Original publisher abstract first published August 30, 2024; full tables pending

Baek and colleagues report 100 people with mild knee osteoarthritis randomized to steamed ginger extract GGE03 or placebo for 12 weeks. The author abstract says VAS pain and K-WOMAC scores favored the extract. The checked publisher body routes remained gated, leaving analysis denominators, numerical estimates and registration unresolved. The knee trial is distinct from the separate GGE03 dyspepsia trial and uses a processed extract.
