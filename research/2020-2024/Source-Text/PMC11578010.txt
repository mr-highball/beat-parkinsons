Title: Efficacy and safety of donepezil in patients with dementia with Lewy bodies: results from a 12-week multicentre, randomised, double-blind, and placebo-controlled phase IV study

Evidence type: Primary randomized postmarketing DLB trial
Format: Factual digest of the original material

This phase IV comparison randomized 160 people, 79 to placebo and 81 to donepezil. Difference in the primary global clinical rating was inconclusive (p = 0.408). NPI-2 change differed by −0.2 points (95% CI −1.48 to 1.01, p = 0.710). The paper reports a favorable change in a secondary cognitive domain and a separate adjusted post hoc cognitive comparison. These outcomes, the combined hallucination and fluctuation measure, and the food-specific visual frequency claim answer different questions.
