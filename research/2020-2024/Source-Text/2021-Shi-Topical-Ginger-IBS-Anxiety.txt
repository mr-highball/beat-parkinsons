Topical fresh ginger juice in a mixed irritable bowel syndrome intervention
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34491647/
PMID: 34491647
DOI: 10.13703/j.0255-2930.20200904-k0001
Coverage: Original author abstract and bibliographic date, full body pending

Shi and colleagues published September 12, 2021. The author abstract describes 150 adults randomized equally to fire needling plus topical acupoint therapy, topical acupoint therapy alone or oral pinaverium bromide. It reports 14, 16 and 13 dropouts, respectively. The topical plaster mixed fresh ginger juice with a multicomponent herbal powder. The abstract reports higher IBS quality-of-life anxiety-related item scores with topical treatment than oral medication at days 7 and 28 (p<.05) without numerical item values. This is a shared or combined topical exposure in IBS, with no isolated fresh-ginger group or Parkinson anxiety scale. The original article body remains open.
