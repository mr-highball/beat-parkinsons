Riby LM, Edwards S, McDonald H, Moss M. The impact of a rosemary containing drink on event-related potential neural markers of sustained attention. PLoS One. 2023;18:e0286113. First published 2023-06-01.
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0286113
PMID: 37262036
PMCID: PMC10234515
Primary source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0286113
Coverage: original Methods, analyses and pilot outcome scope.

Forty-eight healthy adults, 32 women, reported mean age 30.7 years, received a commercial rosemary beverage or water and performed a visual oddball task. People with current depression or anxiety diagnoses were screened out. The results include cognitive performance and ERP amplitudes. The authors cite Northumbria data DOI 10.25398/rd.northumbria.22819886. This paper provides no depression result. Its participant and method details match the related 2025–2026 eye-blink and mood report closely; independent cohorts are not established.
