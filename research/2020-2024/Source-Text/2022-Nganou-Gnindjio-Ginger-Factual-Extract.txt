Title: Ambulatory blood pressure in a single-arm diabetes study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35039142/


Nganou-Gnindjio et al. first published online January 14, 2022. Twenty-one adults with type 2 diabetes were analyzed after six weeks of add-on powdered ginger extract capsules. The primary abstract reports reductions of at least 5% in daytime diastolic pressure, daytime mean arterial pressure and 24-hour diastolic pressure. These measures are averaged by time period. No controlled comparison, standing-challenge result or lightheadedness severity by posture is reported in the accessible abstract. The full outcome tables and precise safety findings remain open.
