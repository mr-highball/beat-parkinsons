Title: A Diet Containing Animal Source Protein as Fresh, Lean Beef Is More Well Liked and Promotes Healthier Eating Behavior Compared with Plant-Based Alternatives in Women with Overweight.
PMID: 39224138
PMCID: PMC11367550
DOI: 10.1016/j.cdnut.2024.104415
Registry ID: NCT02614729
First published: 2024
Authors: Braden ML, Gwin JA, Leidy HJ
Journal: Current developments in nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39224138/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled feeding crossover trial (NCT02614729) in n=17 women with overweight (2 servings/d fresh lean beef vs plant equivalents x 7 d each): satiety and total 24-h energy intake did not differ; lean beef was rated higher in flavor/texture/acceptability (p<0.05) and modestly lowered ad libitum carbohydrate/sugar intake. No PD symptom endpoints.
