Title: Anti-depressant like effects of quercetin 4'-O-glucoside from Allium cepa via regulation of brain oxidative stress and monoamine levels in mice subjected to unpredictable chronic mild stress.
PMID: 31368414
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2019.1587247
First published: 2021
Authors: Singh V, Chauhan G, Shri R
Journal: Nutritional Neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31368414/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Bioactivity-guided mouse study: Allium cepa outer scale extract (ACE) and isolated quercetin-4'-O-glucoside (20 mg/kg) reversed UCMS depressive-like behavior (FST, SPT, OFT) with activity comparable to fluoxetine via MAO-A inhibition and brain serotonin restoration. Inedible outer-scale extract/isolated glycoside in mice.
