Title: Association between socio-demographic factors, lifestyle, eating habits and hypertension risk among middle-aged and older rural Chinese adults.
PMID: 38161126
DOI: 10.1016/j.numecd.2023.11.012
First published: 2024
Authors: Wan S, Pan D, Su M, Wang S, Wang Y, Xu D, et al.
Journal: Nutrition, metabolism, and cardiovascular diseases : NMCD
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38161126/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Cross-sectional epidemiological study (Wan et al., 2024, Nutr Metab Cardiovasc Dis) analyzing 10,111 rural Chinese adults aged 35-75 years (hypertension prevalence 34.32%) from 2012-2017. In multivariable logistic regression, habitual adzuki bean consumption (alongside fruits and pork liver) was inversely associated with resting hypertension prevalence. Limits: cross-sectional self-reported diet without preparation detail, Parkinson's stratum, or orthostatic blood pressure / lightheadedness outcomes.
