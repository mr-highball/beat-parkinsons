The systematic review searched through December 2021. Figure 4 and Table 2 pool two studies of insomnia severity with 118 participants. The reported standardized effect favors saffron at 0.53, with a 95% confidence interval of −0.05 to 1.11 and I² = 59%. The estimate is inconclusive. The two source comparisons are the Lopresti evening Affron trial, DOI 10.1016/j.sleep.2021.08.001, and the Taherzadeh intranasal combination trial with saffron, sweet violet and lettuce seed, DOI 10.1016/j.jep.2020.113116. They differ in preparation, administration route, and attribution.

Six studies with 308 participants contributed to sleep quality (SMD 0.89, 95% CI 0.10 to 1.68, I² = 90%, rated very low certainty by the review). Five studies with 220 participants contributed to sleep duration (SMD 0.57, 95% CI 0.21 to 0.93, rated moderate certainty). The discussion cites 494 participants overall whereas the abstract cites 431. Use the endpoint-specific denominators in Table 2. No population in the review was identified as having Parkinson disease. The pooled estimate is secondary context for claim 0218; the component trials are the evidence units.
