Title: Lack of effect of tart cherry concentrate dose on serum urate in people with gout.
PMID: 31891407
PMCID: PMC10687352
DOI: 10.1093/rheumatology/kez606
First published: 2020
Authors: Stamp LK, Chapman P, Frampton C, Duffull SB, Drake J, Zhang Y, Neogi T
Journal: Rheumatology (Oxford, England)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10687352/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized, placebo-controlled dose-finding trial (Stamp et al., 2020, Rheumatology, PMC10687352; ACTRN12615000741583) in 50 adults with gout (serum urate >0.36 mmol/L) randomized to placebo or 7.5, 15, 22.5, or 30 mL twice-daily tart cherry juice concentrate for 28 days. Tart cherry concentrate had NO statistically significant effect on 28-day serum urate AUC, urinary urate excretion, or gout flare frequency (p=0.76); 24 adverse events occurred (1 hyperglycemia possibly related). Important competing null gout trial.
