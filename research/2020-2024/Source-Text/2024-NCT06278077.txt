Title: Neurexan multicomponent insomnia trial
First published or posted: 2024-02-26
Registration: NCT06278077
Source URL: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06278077
Text type: original-source evidence summary

First posted February 26, 2024. The completed Germany trial enrolled 81 adults with short-term insomnia. It assigns a four-component homeopathic tablet containing diluted Avena sativa D2 or placebo. Polysomnographic sleep efficiency over 14 days is the primary outcome. No results were posted at the November 17, 2026 check. The planned comparison estimates the combined formulation. It includes no rolled-oat food or Parkinson group.
