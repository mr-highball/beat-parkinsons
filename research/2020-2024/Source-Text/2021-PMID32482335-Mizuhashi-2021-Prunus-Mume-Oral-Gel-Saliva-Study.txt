Title: Effectiveness of oral moisturizing gel and flavor on oral moisture and saliva volume: A clinical study.
PMID: 32482335
DOI: 10.1016/j.prosdent.2020.02.031
First published: 2021
Authors: Mizuhashi F, Koide K, Toya S
Journal: The Journal of prosthetic dentistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32482335/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Six-arm clinical study (n=36 healthy adults and n=20 adults with oral dryness) testing moisturizing oral gels with Japanese apricot (Prunus mume) acid flavor or scent. Distinct botanical species (Prunus mume, not culinary Prunus armeniaca) and topical dry-mouth gel rather than ingested apricot for PD drooling.
