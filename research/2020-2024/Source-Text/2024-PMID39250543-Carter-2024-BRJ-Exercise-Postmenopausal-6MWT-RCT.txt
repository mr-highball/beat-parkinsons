Title: Preworkout dietary nitrate magnifies training-induced benefits to physical function in late postmenopausal women: a randomized pilot study.
PMID: 39250543
PMCID: PMC11687826
DOI: 10.1152/ajpregu.00150.2024
First published: 2024
Authors: Carter SJ, Blechschmid TH, Baranauskas MN, Long EB, Gruber AH, Raglin JS, et al.
Journal: American journal of physiology. Regulatory, integrative and comparative physiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39250543/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

8-week two-arm randomized pilot trial (PMC11687826) in n=24 postmenopausal women comparing preworkout beetroot juice + circuit exercise (BRJ+EX) vs exercise alone (EX): BRJ+EX produced significantly greater 6-minute walk test gains (+40+-23 m vs +8+-25 m; p=0.003). Limits: non-PD women, open-label vs exercise alone.
