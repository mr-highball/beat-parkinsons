Title: Reliability and Validity Study of a Turkish Version of the Sialorrhea Clinical Scale for Parkinson's Disease (SCS-TR)

PMID: 36911563
PMCID: PMC9999215
DOI: 10.29399/npa.28053
Publication date: 2022-12-05
Date basis: online date in the publisher PDF and official PMC metadata. The issue appeared in 2023.
Source depth: factual extract of full original and Tables 1–4

Genç and Sapmaz Atalar report 41 people with Parkinson disease and 31 comparison participants. Table 1 gives Spearman 0.848 against the MDS-UPDRS saliva and drooling item, 0.723 against the Drooling Frequency and Severity Scale, and 0.701 against the NMSQ saliva item. Reported alpha is 0.881. The scale includes day and night symptoms, severity, frequency, eating, speech and social impact.

The validation compares instruments and groups. The observed scale correlations do not estimate a food or drooling treatment effect. There is no raw-fig intervention or outcome.
