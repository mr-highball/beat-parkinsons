Title: Exposure factors and clinical characteristics associated with Parkinson's disease in GBA1 variant carriers: A Chinese GBA1-PD intrafamilial survey
Authors: Xuxiang Zhang, Yuwen Zhao, Li Jiang and colleagues
Publication: Parkinsonism & Related Disorders 130 (2025), 107212; online 19 November 2024
PMID: 39581168
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2024.107212
Sources: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39581168/ ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1353802024012240
Coverage: Publisher abstract and introduction; full Methods, tables and supplements not verified.

The intrafamilial survey used the Chinese Parkinson's Disease & Movement Disorders Multicenter Database and Collaborative Network. It included 59 nonmanifesting GBA1 carriers, 62 controls and 107 GBA1-associated Parkinson cases, 81 of the latter in the early stage. Exposure factors included tea consumption, pesticides/solvents, smoking and alcohol; logistic models assessed their associations with Parkinson disease. Compared with carriers and controls, early-stage GBA1-associated Parkinson cases had more severe hyposmia and other listed prodromal symptoms. The abstract gives adjusted odds ratios for pesticides/solvents and smoking against Parkinson disease, not a tea-versus-hyposmia contrast. It does not specify tea type, preparation, quantity, smell test, or tea-specific estimate. The publisher introduction confirms the separate exposure and symptom comparisons. Absence of a tea–olfaction analysis from the inaccessible full tables cannot be asserted.

This multicenter GBA1 intrafamilial survey differs in stated setting and design from Zhu et al. 2025, a 20-patient cfDNA methylation study in Qinghai; neither accessible abstract provides a participant crosswalk, so individual overlap is not settled.
