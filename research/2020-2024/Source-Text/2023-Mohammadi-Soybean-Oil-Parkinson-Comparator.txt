Coverage: Original factual extract of the public English and French abstracts, affiliations and Methods outline. Full article access requires a subscription. Crossref returns September 2023 issue metadata. OpenAlex derives 2023-08-05 with no repository full text. The explicit publisher website date controls.

Population: 44 people with Parkinson's disease.
Author affiliations: Tabriz University of Medical Sciences and Valiasr Hospital neurology in Tabriz, Iran. The public abstract does not provide actual enrollment dates or the number completing each arm.
Trial duration: Eight weeks.
Comparator: Two soybean-oil soft capsules a day. Processing grade and oil mass are not stated. The linked registration IRCT20100123003140N21 identifies Zahravi Pharmaceutical Company as the supplier.
Active comparator: Two omega-3 capsules a day, each with 120 mg EPA and 180 mg DHA in the abstract. The linked registration reverses these amounts for a 300 mg total in both sources.
Measurements: Weight and height, triglycerides, HDL cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, total cholesterol, and circulating IL-6, TNF-α and IL-10 at both visits.
Public Methods outline: Anthropometry, diet and physical activity are grouped under one Methods heading. The registration lists RAPA at baseline only, with no follow-up physical activity endpoint.
Reported results: After adjustment for initial values and confounders, the abstract reports no statistically significant between-arm changes. Total cholesterol did not significantly change within or between groups. Nominal within-arm changes were described for the omega-3 group.
Endpoint fit: The public record gives no Parkinson motor, nonmotor, gait, fall, speech, swallowing, salivation, apathy or daytime sleepiness effects. No companion motor outcomes were verified.
Registry link: https://irct.ir/trial/29861. The registry matches the number of Parkinson participants, intervention and placebo composition, investigator and outcome set, and supplies planned clinic provenance with a date distinction. It lists IL-1β as a primary endpoint absent from this abstract.
