The abstract describes sesame oil given concurrently during manganese exposure in rats. It reports movement and laboratory outcomes. Time before falling from a rotarod is a model performance measure and does not give a human fall count. The abstract does not quantify clinical rigidity, dystonia or pain in Parkinson disease.
