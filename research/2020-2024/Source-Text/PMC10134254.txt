Title: Efficacy of low carbohydrate and ketogenic diets in treating mood and anxiety disorders: systematic review and implications for clinical practice
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10134254/
PMID: 37066662
PMCID: PMC10134254
DOI: 10.1192/bjo.2023.36
Publication date: 2023-04-17
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The review identifies 12 heterogeneous low-carbohydrate or ketogenic studies with incomplete reporting of carbohydrate exposure. Mood or anxiety outcomes in these diet comparisons do not establish that dried fig was tested or that Parkinson visual hallucinations changed.
