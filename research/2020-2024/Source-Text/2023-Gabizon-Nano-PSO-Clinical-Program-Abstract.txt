Ruth Gabizon described the Nano-PSO GranaGard program at the PICAD and MeCoND conference on February 11, 2023. Printed proceedings page 96 says that patients with several neurological conditions, including Parkinson disease, were receiving the formulation. It gives no Parkinson cohort size, comparison, outcome estimate or trial identifier. The numerical verbal outcome refers to a separate multiple sclerosis study. The proceedings provide a possible clinical program lead and leave its relationship to NCT05142085 / INNN0121 unverified.
