Human randomized crossover feeding trial in older adults comparing cooked lean beef vs plant/alternate protein foods: confirmed brisk, high-magnitude postprandial essential amino acid and leucine/phenylalanine plasma kinetics after cooked beef meals. Provides direct culinary exposure bridge for cooked beef LNAA load.
