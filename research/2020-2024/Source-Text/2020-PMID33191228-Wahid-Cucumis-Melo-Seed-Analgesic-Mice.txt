Title: Analgesic, anti-inflammatory and toxic effects of ethanol extracts of Cucumis melo and Citrullus lanatus seeds.
PMID: 33191228
First published: 2020
Authors: Wahid S, Khan RA, Feroz Z, Ikram R
Journal: Pak J Pharm Sci
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33191228/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse study evaluating ethanol extracts of Cucumis melo seeds (50, 100, 200 mg/kg) in Eddy hot-plate, tail-flick, and hind-paw edema assays. Reported analgesic and anti-inflammatory activity attributed to seed cucurbitacins A/B/E; 200 mg/kg increased passivity/sedation. Plant-part caveat: tested melon seeds rather than raw edible cantaloupe pulp.
