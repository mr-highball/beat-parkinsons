OP-592 uses cardiac MIBG imaging in 95 people with mild cognitive impairment. Abnormal scans occur in 22 of 37 probable Lewy body cases and normal scans in 38 of 43 Alzheimer-type cases, matching the later main cohort paper. Visual hallucinations contribute to the clinical classification. Cardiac tracer findings neither record dried fig exposure nor count recurrent visual episodes after eating.
