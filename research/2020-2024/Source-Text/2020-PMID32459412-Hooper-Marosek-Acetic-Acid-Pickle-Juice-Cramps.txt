Title: Quantitative Analysis of the Acetic Acid Content in Substances Used by Athletes for the Possible Prevention and Alleviation of Exercise-Associated Muscle Cramps.
PMID: 32459412
DOI: 10.1519/JSC.0000000000003595
First published: 2020
Authors: Hooper Marosek SE, Antharam V, Dowlatshahi K
Journal: J Strength Cond Res
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32459412/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Analytical and mechanistic study (Hooper Marosek et al., 2020, J Strength Cond Res) quantifying acetic acid content across athletic cramp remedies including dill pickle brine, supporting the hypothesis that vinegar-derived acetic acid activating oropharyngeal TRP/ASIC receptors—rather than cucumber-specific electrolytes or phytochemicals—drives rapid reflex cramp inhibition.
