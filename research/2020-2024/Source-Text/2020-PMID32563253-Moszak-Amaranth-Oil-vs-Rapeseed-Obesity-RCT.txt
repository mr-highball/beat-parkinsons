Title: Comparison of the effect of rapeseed oil or amaranth seed oil supplementation on weight loss, body composition, and changes in the metabolic profile of obese patients following 3-week body mass reduction program: a randomized clinical trial.
PMID: 32563253
PMCID: PMC7305596
DOI: 10.1186/s12944-020-01330-7
First published: 2020
Authors: Moszak M, Zawada A, Juchacz A, Grzymisławski M, Bogdański P
Journal: Lipids in health and disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32563253/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-week randomized double-blind inpatient weight-reduction trial (Moszak et al., 2020, Lipids Health Dis, PMC7305596) in 81 obese adults comparing 20 mL/day amaranth seed oil vs 20 mL/day rapeseed oil vs calorie-restricted diet control. Measured weight loss, body composition, and lipid/glucose parameters; no blood pressure superiority, gastrointestinal, or neurological symptom outcomes.
