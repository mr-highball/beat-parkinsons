Factual scope from the original abstract, Methods and Figures 1–2. The paper describes Sprague-Dawley rats exposed to chronic unpredictable mild stress and comparison groups receiving red raspberry extract. It describes sucrose preference, open-field activity, elevated plus maze and forced swimming. In Figure 2, open-arm entries and time are reported as animal observations. The experiment used an extract in a stress model. Publication status precludes using the reported effects to support or oppose a raspberry anxiety claim. The numeric efficacy results are intentionally omitted from this factual synopsis.
