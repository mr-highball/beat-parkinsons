Title: Extra virgin coconut oil (Cocos nucifera L.) intake shows neurobehavioural and intestinal health effects in obesity-induced rats.
PMID: 37318515
DOI: 10.1039/d3fo00850a
First published: 2023
Authors: Araújo de Vasconcelos MH, Tavares RL, Dutra MLDV, Batista KS, D'Oliveira AB, Pinheiro RO, Pereira RA, Lima MDS
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37318515/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rodent experiment (Araujo de Vasconcelos et al., 2023, Food Funct) in 32 male Wistar rats on control or cafeteria-obese diets receiving 3,000 mg/kg/day extra-virgin coconut oil (EVCO) vs water gavage for 8 weeks. EVCO reduced energy intake/weight gain, showed antidepressant-like behavioral effects, protected hippocampal neurons, increased fecal lactic acid bacteria, and shifted colonic macrophages from M1 to M2 in obese rats (complementing Yeap et al. 2015 PMID:25452773, Edem et al. 2021 PMID:34881837, Asuku 2022 PMID:38243570, and Kucukkatirci Baykan et al. 2024 PMID:39446626). Limits: non-Parkinson diet-induced obesity/stress rodent models; no human Parkinson mood or bowel clinical evidence.
