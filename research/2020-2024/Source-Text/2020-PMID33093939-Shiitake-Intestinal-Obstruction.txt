The two cases describe small-bowel obstruction after swallowing pieces of shiitake. One case involved partially sliced mushroom and the other involved incompletely chewed uncut mushroom. The report does not establish the cooking method or a Parkinson population. The outcome was downstream intestinal obstruction. No comparative swallowing severity, aspiration or pharyngeal function was measured.

The article cites an independent 2017 two-case report. Keep the reports as distinct clinical case families. Neither report supplies the isolated roasted-shiitake comparison in Round 1 claim 0244.
