Title: Intake of Flavonoids and Flavonoid-Rich Foods and Mortality Risk Among Individuals With Parkinson Disease: A Prospective Cohort Study.
PMID: 35082171
PMCID: PMC8967390
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0000000000013275
First published: 2022
Authors: Zhang X, Molsberry SA, Yeh TS, Cassidy A, Schwarzschild MA, Ascherio A et al.
Journal: Neurology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35082171/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Prospective cohort survival analysis in NHS and HPFS participants with Parkinson disease (n=1,251 PD cases). Higher flavonoid intake (including apples/berries/red wine) before and after diagnosis was associated with lower all-cause mortality in men with PD. Mortality endpoint only; does not isolate raw or baked apple effects on specific PD symptoms.
