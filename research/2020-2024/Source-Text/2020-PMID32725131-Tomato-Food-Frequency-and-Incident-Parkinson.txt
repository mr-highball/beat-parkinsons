Original source scope
The prospective cohorts use food-frequency measurements to estimate dietary nicotine. Tomatoes and processed tomato products appear as distinct questionnaire foods. The analysis counts newly diagnosed Parkinson disease.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The study estimates disease incidence rather than rigidity after consuming cooked tomatoes. Processed tomato products combine forms and do not define a cooking intervention.
