Title: Cooked Adzuki Bean Reduces High-Fat Diet-Induced Body Weight Gain, Ameliorates Inflammation, and Modulates Intestinal Homeostasis in Mice.
PMID: 35782919
PMCID: PMC9241564
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2022.918696
First published: 2022
Authors: Zhao Q, Liu Z, Zhu Y, Wang H, Dai Z, Yang X, et al.
Journal: Frontiers in nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35782919/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse study (Zhao et al., 2022, Front Nutr, PMC9241564) feeding 15% steamed/cooked adzuki bean powder to high-fat-diet mice (n=7/group) for 12 weeks. Cooked adzuki bean reduced body weight gain, hepatic triglycerides, serum IL-6, TNF-alpha, and LPS while modulating gut microbiota composition. Limits: metabolic/obesity mouse model; no constipation, bowel transit, stool frequency, or neurological/Parkinson's symptom endpoints were assessed.
