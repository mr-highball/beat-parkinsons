The case is post-stroke rehabilitation. The primary abstract describes
internal formulations combined with manual and external therapies. It
reports that weakness, speech and gait improved during a 20-day course
without a comparison group. Ingredient-level attribution cannot be
drawn from that treatment bundle or from a single trajectory.

The later Siddha neuroscience review (PMID 40124133, Table 2) interprets
the case as involving external gingelly (sesame) oil and Maha Narayana
Thailam. The primary treatment section identifies Dhanvantra Mukkoottu
Ennai and Ulunthu Thylam. It does not provide their composition or identify
plain sesame oil as a standalone exposure. The two publications describe
one case and differ in treatment labels. No culinary sesame-oil exposure
or mapped Parkinson symptom was evaluated.
