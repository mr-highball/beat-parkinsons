Title: Selected motor symptoms in a PLA2G6-associated neurodegeneration case series
PMID: 37403138
PMCID: PMC10320940
DOI: 10.1186/s13023-023-02780-9
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10320940/
First publication: 2023-07-05
Coverage: Original NCBI PMC JATS article body checked and summarized.

The series describes pediatric and adult phenotypes of genetically confirmed PLA2G6-associated neurodegeneration. Section 3 and Table 3 describe seven adult cases from five families. Six had hypokinesia and three had hand tremor among their initial presenting symptoms. The hand-tremor tally does not define resting-tremor severity. Illustrations and supplemental imaging are diagnostic material. There is no raw fig comparison.
