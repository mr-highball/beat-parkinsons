Inhaled basil oil and forced-swim immobility in stressed mice
Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2020.569711/full
PMID: 33061923
DOI: 10.3389/fpsyt.2020.569711
Coverage: Original publication, Methods, Figure 1 and 2021 corrigendum

Ayuob and colleagues allocated 40 adult male mice to four groups. Following chronic unpredictable mild stress, inhalation of Ocimum basilicum essential oil was associated with shorter forced-swim immobility versus untreated stressed mice (p < .001, ten per group). The experiment also tested open-arm behavior and measured corticosterone and olfactory bulb tissue. Forced-swim immobility is an experimental behavior, with clinical apathy, Parkinson disease and eating dried basil leaves outside the design. The linked 2021 correction changes author affiliations.
