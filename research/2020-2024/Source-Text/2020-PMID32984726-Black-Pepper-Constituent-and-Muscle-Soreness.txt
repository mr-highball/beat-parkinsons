Twenty healthy young men took part in a randomized, double-blind crossover of a proprietary liposomal black-pepper-derived beta-caryophyllene formulation versus starch around an exercise challenge. Table 1 reports day-4 resting forearm soreness of 2.50 ± 0.95 and 3.75 ± 1.48 on a ten-point scale, respectively, with p=.003. The table does not report corresponding between-arm tests at 24 or 48 hours. The day-4 strength comparison between the groups was inconclusive (p=.76).
All authors list an affiliation with the product provider. The exact product and exercise soreness outcome cannot be transferred directly to cracked culinary pepper or clinical Parkinson pain.
