Eckert's original letter describes post-treatment anxiety prevalence favoring the diet-only active comparator and questions inference from separate within-arm tests. The original arm table remains inaccessible, so the between-arm effect size and how it accounts for baseline are unresolved. The authors' published reply was indexed and its body was not recovered.
