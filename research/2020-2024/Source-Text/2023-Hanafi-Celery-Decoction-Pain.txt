Methods and Table 2: two gout case reports in Temanggung, Central Java, with celery leaf and stalk preparation boiled and strained, plus nursing pain management. Both reported pain scale 5 before and 2 after. Uric acid was 8.0 to 4.0 mg/dL and 7.8 to 4.1 mg/dL. No control was described. The methods propose seven days while the stated case-collection dates are June 29 through July 1, 2023; the timing of the post-treatment assessment is not resolved in the report. The preparation is a boiled liquid, not fresh leaf consumed, and the patients were not reported to have Parkinson disease.
