Title: Dietary pattern and tested hyposmia in community cohorts
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7713746/

The 2020 Molsberry study selected participants without diagnosed Parkinson disease. Repeated food-frequency questionnaires contributed to dietary indices. The alternate Mediterranean score includes legumes as one component. In a subset, the investigators assessed hyposmia using a 12-item Brief Smell Identification Test and a cohort reference threshold. Overall diet-index adherence did not show an association with hyposmia. The published comparison does not identify lentils or boiling, and it concerns a community cohort without diagnosed Parkinson disease at selection.
