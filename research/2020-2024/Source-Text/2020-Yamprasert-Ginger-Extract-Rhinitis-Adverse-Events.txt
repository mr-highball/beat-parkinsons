Constipation adverse events in a ginger extract versus loratadine trial
2020-04-20
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7171779/

Yamprasert and colleagues randomized 80 people with allergic rhinitis to ginger extract or loratadine. Six-week Table 6 lists constipation in 0 of 36 ginger and 3 of 36 loratadine participants. Eructation was reported in 26 and 6, respectively. No inactive arm or constipation severity endpoint was included. This is a comparison of reported adverse events in rhinitis. The separate official NCT02576808 registry first posted October 15, 2015, lists the study complete and has no registry results. The journal article supplies the observed outcomes.
