Title: Abnormal interhemispheric resting state functional connectivity in Parkinson's disease patients with impulse control disorders.
PMID: 34272398
PMCID: PMC8285494
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-021-00205-7
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8285494/
First publication date: 2021-07-16 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

The imaging comparison studies interhemispheric connectivity and impulse-control disorders in Parkinson disease. Severe visual hallucinations interfering with assessment were an exclusion criterion. The study did not measure a hallucination response to dried figs.
