Title: Date palm spathe extract reverses chronic stress-induced changes in dendritic arborization in the amygdala and impairment of hippocampal long-term potentiation.
PMID: 37315214
DOI: 10.1002/syn.22278
First published: 2023
Authors: Hadipour M, Meftahi GH, Jahromi GP
Journal: Synapse
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37315214/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rat chronic stress study (Hadipour et al., 2023) reporting date palm spathe (Tarooneh, floral bract) extract reversed stress-induced dendritic remodeling and anxiety-like behaviors. Distinct non-fruit plant part (spathe, not dried date fruit) in rodents.
