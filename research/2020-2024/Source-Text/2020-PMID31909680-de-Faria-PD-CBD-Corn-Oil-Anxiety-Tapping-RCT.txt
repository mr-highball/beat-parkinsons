Title: Effects of acute cannabidiol administration on anxiety and tremors induced by a Simulated Public Speaking Test in patients with Parkinson's disease.
PMID: 31909680
DOI: 10.1177/0269881119895536
First published: 2020
Authors: de Faria SM, de Morais Fabrício D, Tumas V, Castro PC, Ponti MA, Hallak JE et al.
Journal: J Psychopharmacol
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31909680/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized double-blind crossover trial (de Faria et al., 2020, J Psychopharmacol, n=24 patients with Parkinson disease) administering oral CBD 300 mg dissolved in corn oil versus matching corn-oil placebo before a Simulated Public Speaking Test. CBD attenuated speech-induced anxiety and tremor amplitude compared with the corn-oil vehicle, whereas finger-tapping dexterity was measured under the shared carrier design. Because corn oil was strictly an inactive solvent/placebo in both conditions with no oil-free control, it supplies zero attributable evidence that dietary refined corn oil worsens or eases PD anxiety or hand dexterity.
