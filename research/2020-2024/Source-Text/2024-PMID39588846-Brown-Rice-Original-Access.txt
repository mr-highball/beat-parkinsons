Title: First publication and source access for the brown-rice fly study
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39588846/
PMID: 39588846
DOI: 10.1139/apnm-2024-0110
Publication date: 2024-11-26
Date basis: NLM article date. The journal issue is dated January 2025.
Coverage: Original abstract and verified bibliographic identity.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

Brown rice is compared with white rice and other groups in a Drosophila iron exposure model. The article abstract reports a favorable fly climbing result. The complete methods and outcome tables remain inaccessible through the original publisher and institutional routes checked. Cooking and grinding, group sizes and overlap with the related author group's study PMID39235924 remain unresolved. A fly climbing result does not provide a Parkinson clinical postural assessment.
