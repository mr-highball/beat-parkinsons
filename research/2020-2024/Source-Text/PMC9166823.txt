Title: How to account for hallucinations in the interpretation of the antidepressant effects of psychedelics: a translational framework
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9166823/
PMID: 35348806
PMCID: PMC9166823
DOI: 10.1007/s00213-022-06106-8
Publication date: 2022-03-29
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The paper discusses separating acute psychedelic perceptions from later psychological treatment effects. The proposed framework concerns pharmacologic exposures and does not address frequency of visual hallucinations after dried figs in Parkinson disease.
