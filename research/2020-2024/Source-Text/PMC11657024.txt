Title: Cholinergic Basal Forebrain Integrity and Cognition in Parkinson's Disease: A Reappraisal of Magnetic Resonance Imaging Evidence

Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS

The reappraisal compares MRI-derived cholinergic basal forebrain measures with cognition in Parkinson disease. Regional measurements and cognitive outcomes do not test eating dried figs or repeated visual hallucinations.
