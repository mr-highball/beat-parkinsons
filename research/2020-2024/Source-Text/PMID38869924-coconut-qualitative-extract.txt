Original source scope
Seven people continued a single-group ketogenic intervention. Oil use enters individual narratives. Constipation occurred and participants described several different ways to address it. Reports of energy and fatigue concern the whole diet or different oils and appear without comparative estimates.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap

Text type: factual extract, reviewed 2026-11-15

Seven people continued a nonrandomized ketogenic study to 24 weeks. Participant 008 reported possible slowing of sleep behavior episodes but continuation at about two per week, a recent bed-partner injury, and perceived little overall change. In the supplement the companion reported less vivid dreaming and less movement. Baseline episode frequency, diagnosis by polysomnography, and participant 008's oil type or intake are not reported. Participant 019 separately described drinking regular coconut or avocado oil; processing and actual choice are not specified. Participant 013's companion described improved talking and facial appearance, without a listener-rated intelligibility measure or recorded individual coconut-oil use. The diet varied many ingredients; there is no comparator arm.
