ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05597189. Clinical Study for Palliative/Preventive Treatment of Chronic Back Pain.
First posted 2022-10-27; first enrollment date recorded as 2022-09-26. Completed 2023-07-31. Last update posted 2023-11-27.
Study page: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05597189
Official structured record: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05597189
Coverage: factual extract of current official design, status, interventions and outcome fields.

The registry records actual enrollment of 135 and three groups: low and high botanical extract versus control. Pain is the primary outcome. Secondary outcomes include Beck depression and a PROMIS-29 evaluation at baseline and weeks 4, 8 and 12. The available registry fields give no specific BDI-II subgroup threshold, outcome values or structured results. A later trial publication identifies rosemary extract, ashwagandha root extract and sesame seed extract in the investigational product.
