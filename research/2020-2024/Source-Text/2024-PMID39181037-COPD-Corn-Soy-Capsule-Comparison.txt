Text coverage: Distilled NLM author abstract, official registration and complete publisher supplementary tables. Verified the DOCX cell boundaries. Deposited main text and tables require access, so group-level physical performance results await the main paper.

The ten-week comparison reported 16 participants receiving the comparator, 16 receiving EPA/DHA and 14 receiving EPA/DHA plus HMB. Supplemental Table S1 specifies 2 g total soybean and corn oil at 50:50 for comparator capsules, with maltodextrin also assigned. The study does not give a refining grade. The trial is in COPD. The abstract lists six-minute walking distance, four-metre gait speed and postural balance, and gives no separate group-level estimates for them. The registry has no posted numerical results or specified fall-frequency outcome.

Supplemental Table S3 gives adjusted postintervention depression scores of 5.218 for the corn–soy comparator, 4.757 for EPA/DHA and 6.242 for EPA/DHA plus HMB. Differences versus comparator were −0.461 (95% CI −2.142 to 1.221) and +1.024 (−0.713 to 2.762), respectively. Anxiety differences versus comparator were −0.213 (−1.941 to 1.514) and −1.066 (−2.830 to 0.698). Intervals span zero. The two depression point estimates lie on different sides of the comparator. No group isolates corn oil, and the measured symptoms are in a COPD population.
