Sunflower oil placebo and composite well-being
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12144-022-04206-4
DOI: 10.1007/s12144-022-04206-4
Registration: DRKS00028118
Coverage: Original body first published January 5, 2023, Methods, Figure 2 and Results

Schienle and Jurinec assigned 30 adults with depression to 0.15 mL/day sunflower oil presented as a natural remedy and 37 to standard waiting for 21 days. The five ratings, including relaxation, were averaged into a well-being composite. Its group-by-time F(3,195) was 46.59 (p<.001). Group mean BDI-II change was −3.30 with the placebo instructions plus oil and −0.05 with standard waiting (p<.001). The report provides no separate anxiety-severity score or relaxation treatment contrast, no neutral-suggestion oil arm and no oil processing grade. The registered relaxation item and composite outcome should be distinguished.
