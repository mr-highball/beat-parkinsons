Title: The herbal preparation STW 5 affects serotonergic pathways in the brain and colon as well as stress parameters in experimental irritable bowel syndrome
PMID: 34817922
DOI: 10.1111/nmo.14301
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nmo.14301
First published: 2021-11-24, official publisher; April 2022 issue.
Publisher preview: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356537874_The_herbal_preparation_STW_5_affects_serotonergic_pathways_in_the_brain_and_colon_as_well_as_stress_parameters_in_experimental_irritable_bowel_syndrome
Coverage: Factual extract of original publisher abstract, funding/conflict fields and original figure captions in the publisher preview. Full Methods and numerical tables unavailable.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Rats underwent one hour of restraint daily for 14 days while receiving oral STW5. The abstract reports increased brain serotonin, decreased colonic serotonin and fecal output, and changes in plasma CRF and colon inflammation under stress. The authors report protection against these changes with the mixed preparation.

The abstract supplies no dose, group denominator or numerical treatment estimate. Preview figure captions identify eight rats per plotted group and one normal-control group receiving 2.5 mL/kg STW5. These partial captions do not establish the complete dose schedule or sample total. No depression-specific behavioral result is available; whether the full paper includes additional tests remains open. A separate 2022 poster describes doses that cannot be transferred to this paper without verified linkage. The 2015 gastric model is cited as background, with actual animal overlap unresolved.

Funding comes from Eva Pharma and Steigerwald/Bayer; one author is an Eva Pharma employee. These are biochemical and gastrointestinal findings, without a verified single-herb, brewed-tea, human or Parkinson depression comparison.
