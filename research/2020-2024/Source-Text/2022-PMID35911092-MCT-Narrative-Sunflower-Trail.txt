The review's direct sunflower mention is the Table 1 reference 42 crossover of medium-chain triglycerides and high-oleic sunflower oil. Healthy participants substituted 70 g/day of fat in the controlled comparison. The table explicitly shows that cognition was not measured. It lists changes in circulating lipids and glucose, referring to Tholstrup PMID 15051598. The review's cognitive MCT studies are independent rows and must not be combined with this sunflower comparison.
