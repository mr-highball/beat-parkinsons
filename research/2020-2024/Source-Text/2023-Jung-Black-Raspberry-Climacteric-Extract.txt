Factual extract from the original Methods, Results and Table 4.

A randomized double-blind parallel pilot assigned 15 men with andropause symptoms to unripe black raspberry extract and 15 to placebo for 12 weeks. The active group’s mean total Aging Male's Symptoms score was 45.3 at baseline and 38.3 at week 12, compared with 48.1 and 40.7 for placebo. Table 4 gives p = .967 for the between-group total-score comparison. The paper separately reports IPSS urinary outcomes and serum measures. The table does not report individual anxiety-item results. The extract formulation, male climacteric population and aggregate endpoint leave the frozen whole-fruit Parkinson anxiety question open.
