Coverage: Complete 161-page dissertation. Chapter 4 and Table 10 checked against the existing trial family.

The 2020 dissertation and 2022 paper belong to the same randomized NCT02841618 comparison. The dissertation's Table 10 lists 10.1 g grapeseed oil per cookie compared with high-oleic safflower oil. Oil refining is unspecified and the source reports no sleep result. The different cookie registrations supply no reported insomnia estimate.

Eighty-four participants were randomized to the two-week cookie comparison. Chapter 4 reports fatty-acid and cardiolipin outcomes. The listed 10.1 g amount is from the recipe table. It is near the nominal 10 g dose in other reports. Appendix B is a separate open pilot sharing the registration, and supplies no randomized sleep outcome. The distinct NCT04677920 and NCT04677946 trials offer no posted sleep results on the checked registry date. The latter does not identify its oil as grapeseed.
