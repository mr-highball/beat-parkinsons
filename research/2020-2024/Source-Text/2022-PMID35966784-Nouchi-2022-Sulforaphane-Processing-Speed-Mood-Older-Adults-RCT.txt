Title: Effects of sulforaphane intake on processing speed and negative moods in healthy older adults: Evidence from a randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 35966784
PMCID: PMC9372582
DOI: 10.3389/fnagi.2022.929628
Registry ID: UMIN000042666
First published: 2022
Authors: Nouchi R, Hu Q, Ushida Y, Suganuma H, Kawashima R
Journal: Frontiers in aging neuroscience
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9372582/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Twelve-week double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (UMIN000042666) in n=144 healthy older adults (60-80 y, mean 66.8 y) randomized to glucoraphanin/sulforaphane capsules vs placebo. Sulforaphane improved Symbol Search processing speed (change +1.99 vs +1.29, adj p=0.03) and reduced negative mood on POMS2 Total Mood Disturbance (change -0.11 vs +1.60, t=2.15, adj p=0.03; supplementary Anger-Hostility p=0.02 and Confusion-Bewilderment p=0.02), with elevated urinary SFN-NAC (adj p<0.01). All three executive-function tests were null (Stroop adj p=1, reverse Stroop adj p=1, Trail Making Test adj p=1), as were Coding (adj p=0.83) and working memory LNS (adj p=1). Limits: supplement capsules in healthy non-PD older adults.
