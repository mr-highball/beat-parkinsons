Title: Modified Olfactory Training Is an Effective Treatment Method for COVID-19 Induced Parosmia
Source: https://doi.org/10.1002/lary.30101
PMID: 35257391
PMCID: PMC9088368
DOI: 10.1002/lary.30101
First published: 2022-03-07
Text coverage: factual extraction from Methods, Figure 1 and Table III.

Altundag and colleagues followed 38 people who chose multiscent training for postinfectious parosmia and 37 who declined it. Green tea was one of four odors in the final phase of a 12-odor program. Sniffin' Sticks combined odor threshold, discrimination and identification into the TDI score. Higher TDI indicates better olfactory performance.

Table III reports mean TDI changing from 12.3 to 27.9 in the training group and from 10.7 to 14.0 in the comparison group. Baseline between-group p=.462 and nine-month between-group p<.001. The training group was already at 21.4 at six months, before the green-tea scent phase. The groups were self-selected. These results reflect the overall scent program and recovery period, with no isolated estimate for the tea odor, oral intervention or Parkinson population.
