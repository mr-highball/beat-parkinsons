Original study scope
The surgical comparison studied acellular dermal matrix graft versus primary fascial repair after an anterolateral thigh flap. Outcomes concern recovery and donor-site morbidity, including pain and first ambulation.

Continuing evidence gaps
Ambulation timing and perioperative pain are surgery outcomes. The comparison does not involve fresh sage, culinary dates or Parkinson-related pain.
