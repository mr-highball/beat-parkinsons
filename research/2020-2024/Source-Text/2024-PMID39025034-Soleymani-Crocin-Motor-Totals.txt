Title: The effect of crocin on movement disorders and oxidative DNA damage in Parkinson's disease: Insights from a randomized controlled trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39025034/
PMID: 39025034
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2024.107051
First public: 2024-07-01
Coverage: Factual extract from the author abstract. Original outcome tables remain open.

Soleymani and colleagues randomized 60 people with idiopathic Parkinson disease, with 53 completing the eight-week comparison. The active intervention used isolated crocin in capsules. The accessible abstract reports within-arm improvement on MDS-UPDRS Parts II and III (p<.05). It does not give an adjusted between-group rigidity, tremor or depression item estimate. The reported urinary oxidative DNA damage marker did not change clearly. Whole culinary saffron was not assigned. An aggregate motor score and within-arm change leave the direction for individual rigidity uncertain.
