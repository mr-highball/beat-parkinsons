Title: Randomised Clinical Trial Study: The Combination of Vitamin D and Curcumin Piperine Attenuates Disease Activity and Pro-inflammatory Cytokines Levels Insystemic Lupus Erythematosus Patients.
PMID: 38299416
DOI: 10.2174/0115733971276106231226071002
First published: 2024
Authors: Wahono CS, Susianti H, Hakim AL, Rosita F, Pratama MZ, Rahman PA
Journal: Current rheumatology reviews
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38299416/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

3-month double-blind 3-arm RCT (NCT05430087, n=45 female SLE patients) comparing vitamin D3, curcumin+piperine, and combined vitamin D3+curcumin+piperine. Fatigue Severity Scale improved similarly between vitamin D alone and curcumin+piperine alone, with greatest improvement in the triple combination. Bundled supplement.
