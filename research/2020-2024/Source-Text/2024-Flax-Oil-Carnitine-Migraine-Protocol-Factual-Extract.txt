Title: Flaxseed oil and carnitine sleep protocol in migraine
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11463146/
Format: Factual extract prepared from the cited record.

2024 protocol, IRCT20121216011763N57. PMID 39380085, PMCID PMC11463146.
The study in women with migraine planned an oral combination of flaxseed oil and L-carnitine against matched placebos. The protocol included Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index. It provides no outcome scores.
The 2025 results publication, PMID 40082970, reports other trial outcomes. It belongs to the same study.
