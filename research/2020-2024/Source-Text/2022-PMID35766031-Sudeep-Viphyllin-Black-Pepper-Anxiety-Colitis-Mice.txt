Title: Viphyllin™, a standardized extract from black pepper seeds, mitigates intestinal inflammation, oxidative stress, and anxiety-like behavior in DSS-induced colitis mice.
PMID: 35766031
DOI: 10.1111/jfbc.14306
First published: 2022
Authors: Sudeep HV, Venkatakrishna K, Raj A, Reethi B, Shyamprasad K
Journal: Journal of food biochemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35766031/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse experiment evaluating oral standardized Piper nigrum seed extract (Viphyllin, 30% beta-caryophyllene) at 50 and 100 mg/kg in dextran sodium sulfate (DSS)-induced colitis mice. Oral pretreatment reversed colitis symptoms, inhibited colonic NLRP3 inflammasome activation, improved brain antioxidant status, and significantly mitigated DSS-induced anxiety-like behavior. Limits: standardized seed extract in rodent colitis model, not ground black pepper in PD.
