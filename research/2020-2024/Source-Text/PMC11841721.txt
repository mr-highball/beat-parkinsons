Title: Clinical Utility and Safety of an Ultrasonic Head Stimulator in Dementia With Lewy Bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11841721/
PMID: 39588702
PMCID: PMC11841721
DOI: 10.1097/wad.0000000000000652
Publication date: 2024-11-26
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

Twelve dementia with Lewy bodies participants enter a single sequence of four placebo weeks, eight active ultrasound weeks and four follow-up weeks. The study evaluates a device and clinical tolerability. It includes no oral dried fig comparison and cannot estimate a culinary effect.
