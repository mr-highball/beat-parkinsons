Title: Aggravated behavioral and neurochemical deficits and redox imbalance in mice with enhanced neonatal iron intake: improvement by biochanin A and role of microglial p38 activation.
PMID: 31050314
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2019.1611021
First published: 2021
Authors: Li Y, Liu Y, Xu Y, Chen H, Yan Z, Wang X
Journal: Nutr Neurosci
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31050314/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse Parkinson model (Li et al., 2021, Neurotox Res) evaluating isolated biochanin A in neonatal iron plus MPTP-treated mice. Biochanin A attenuated rotarod, pole-test, and swim-test motor deficits and nigral dopaminergic loss. Critical limits: purified isoflavone compound rather than culinary chickpea food matrix; rodent toxin model.
