Title: Formononetin Exerts Neuroprotection in Parkinson's Disease via the Activation of the Nrf2 Signaling Pathway.
PMID: 39598753
PMCID: PMC11596823
DOI: 10.3390/molecules29225364
First published: 2024
Authors: Wang X, Kang N, Liu Y, Xu G
Journal: Molecules (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11596823/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Invertebrate C. elegans alpha-synuclein (NL5901) and MPP+ (BZ555) PD model using sulforaphane as an Nrf2 agonist positive comparator showing improved worm motility/thrashing ('dyskinesia') and dopaminergic neuron vitality. Invertebrate assay does not model human levodopa-induced dyskinesia.
