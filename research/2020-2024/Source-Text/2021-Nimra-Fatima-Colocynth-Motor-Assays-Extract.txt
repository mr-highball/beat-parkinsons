Bitter colocynth and motor behavior in rats
Publication: 2021-06-30
Source: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/356214693_Investigation_of_Neuroprotective_and_Antiparkinson%27s_Activity_of_Citrullus_colocynthis_Fruit
Coverage: Fariya Nimra and Nishat Fatima, Journal of Drug Vigilance and Alternative Therapies 1(2), 78–88. Original issue identifies June 30, 2021. Identity-matched public full article.
DOI: 10.52816/jdvat.2021.1204

The investigators evaluated aqueous and ethanolic bitter colocynth fruit extracts in haloperidol-treated rats. The paper reports modified catalepsy, locomotor activity and orofacial movement measures. Citrullus colocynthis is taxonomically distinct from culinary watermelon, Citrullus lanatus. These original motor outcomes describe a different plant and an animal model.
