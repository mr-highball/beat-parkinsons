Title: Supplementation of the maternal diet with Brazil nut (Bertholletia excelsa H.B.K.) prevents cognitive impairment in the offspring of obese mothers.
PMID: 38345286
DOI: 10.1017/S2040174424000023
First published: 2023
Authors: Apolinário LF, Silva AT, Rosa AP, Oliveira CDS, Lira C, Guerra JPCDS, et al.
Journal: Journal of developmental origins of health and disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38345286/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical study feeding maternal Wistar rats a high-fat or standard diet supplemented with 5% whole Brazil nut: prevented short-term memory impairment in obese-dam offspring at day 30, but produced NO alterations in offspring anxiety behavior at 30 or 90 days. Competing null whole-nut animal anxiety finding.
