Title: Drooling rating scales in Parkinson's disease: A systematic review
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34583888/
PMID: 34583888
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2021.09.012
Publication date: 2021-09-21
Date basis: electronic publication indexed by PubMed
Source depth: primary journal author abstract and separately verified congress presentation

Nascimento and colleagues identify six clinical scales and examine their measurement properties and clinical utility. The author abstract classifies only ROMP-Saliva as recommended under the review's MDS criteria. It describes incomplete testing of reliability, validity and responsiveness in the underlying literature. This is an instrument review and provides no evaluated raw-fig exposure.

The congress report on S69 of the 11th ESSD supplement preserves the same five authors, six scales and recommendation. The journal tables are a remaining access target.
