Title: Evaluation of the effect of carrot seed (Daucus Carota) in women of fertile age with hypoactive sexual desire disorder: A randomized double-blind clinical trial.
PMID: 33183662
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2020.102543
First published: 2020
Authors: Sadeghi S, Bahrami R, Raisi F, Rampisheh Z, Ghobadi A, Akhtari E
Journal: Complement Ther Med
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33183662/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (n=68 randomized, 60 completed) of Daucus carota seed capsules (500 mg tid x 12 wk) in women with hypoactive sexual desire disorder, improving FSFI total and dyspareunia pain domain (p<0.001). Plant-part and outcome boundary: carrot seed capsules for sexual dyspareunia, NOT culinary steamed/raw carrot root for PD pain.
