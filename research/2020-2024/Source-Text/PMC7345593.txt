Design: Retrospective dementia diagnosis and symptom comparison

The study compares clinical investigation delays, diagnostic features and treatments of Lewy body and other dementias, and also considers Parkinson dementia. It distinguishes visual and nonvisual symptoms in clinical records. Those records provide no repeated visual event estimate after a culinary exposure.
