Whole kiwi and prediabetes reported outcomes
https://www.lidsen.com/journals/rpn/rpn-02-01-006

February 25, 2022. The parallel trial compares two peeled whole kiwifruit with bottled water daily for 12 weeks. There were 34 randomized and 32 completers. The primary measures were HbA1c and plasma ascorbate. The reported group separation favored kiwi for plasma vitamin C and the HbA1c comparison was inconclusive. Other outcomes include blood pressure and dietary and metabolic measures. This is a separate trial from the kiwi breakfast-pulp cognition crossover. No objective attention task or Parkinson group is reported.
