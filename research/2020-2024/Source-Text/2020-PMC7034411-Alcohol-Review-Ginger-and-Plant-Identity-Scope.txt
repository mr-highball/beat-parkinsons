The history section identifies barley and broom millet among fermentation ingredients. Table 2 cites water extracts of Zingiber officinale and Thymus vulgaris in an alcohol-related study in mice. The recorded outcomes are preclinical biochemical measures, without an isolated effect on Parkinson symptoms.

The named red sage is Salvia miltiorrhiza or Salvia przewalskii, distinct from Salvia officinalis. Indian winter cherry is a name for Withania somnifera. The orange-red wording describes pigment. The indexed matching bell-pepper context does not identify a culinary Capsicum annuum exposure.
