Injected marjoram leaf extract and mouse memory tasks
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12906-023-03994-x
PMCID: PMC10199469
DOI: 10.1186/s12906-023-03994-x
Coverage: Original full text, Methods and Figure 3

Wagdy and colleagues assigned eight Swiss albino mice per group to saline, injected LPS, hydroalcoholic marjoram leaf extract plus LPS, or extract alone. In the model arm versus the extract-plus-LPS arm, novel object discrimination ratios were 0.298 ± 0.011 and 0.545 ± 0.042 (p<.001), and Y-maze alternation values were 53.03 ± 3.032% and 68.87 ± 3.901% (p<.05). The unchallenged extract group had greater novel-object discrimination than saline. Both tasks assess aspects of memory in a neuroinflammation model. There was no separate sustained-attention test, oral fresh-leaf exposure or Parkinson cohort.
