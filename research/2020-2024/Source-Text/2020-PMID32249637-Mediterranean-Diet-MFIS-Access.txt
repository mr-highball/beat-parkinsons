Publication date: 2020-04-06
Date basis: Publisher posting date for the accepted author version. Version of record posted April 16, 2020.
Coverage: Exact publisher author abstract and author-version metadata. Full thesis and outcome model remain unverified.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

The publisher identifies the source thesis as a Shiraz University of Medical Sciences PhD project 96-16241. The abstract reports a fatigue comparison at p=.001. NICE derives an unadjusted end-of-study difference of −0.70 on the Modified Fatigue Impact Scale with a 95% interval from −5.34 to 3.94. These are distinct reported quantities and the original model has yet to be verified. The study compares dietary patterns in multiple sclerosis. The preparation, quantity and independent effect of refined canola oil remain unavailable.
