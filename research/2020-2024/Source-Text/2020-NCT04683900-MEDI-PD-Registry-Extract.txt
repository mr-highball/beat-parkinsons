Official registration of MEDI-PD constipation outcomes
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04683900
Registration: NCT04683900
Coverage: Official ClinicalTrials.gov record and protocol outcome fields

The MEDI-PD registration first posted December 24, 2020 and reports study completion June 7, 2022. The current record gives 46 actual enrollees. The registered primary endpoint is final-minus-baseline change in the GSRS constipation syndrome. The study timeline includes a two-week baseline and an eight-week randomized intervention. Secondary outcomes include stool frequency, stool form and laxative use. The record shows no posted summary results. The separate journal protocol describes Mediterranean diet counseling with cooking and dressing oil advice, and the 2024 result reports include 36 analyzed participants.
