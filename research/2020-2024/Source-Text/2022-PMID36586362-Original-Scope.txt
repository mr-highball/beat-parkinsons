In a retrospective Parkinson imaging study, the matched impulsive-compulsive behavior groups had mean Apathy Scale scores 16.2 versus 15.9, p=.76. The exposure classification is behavioral, with matching on dopamine agonist dose among other factors. Nothing in the comparison tests dried-date consumption.
