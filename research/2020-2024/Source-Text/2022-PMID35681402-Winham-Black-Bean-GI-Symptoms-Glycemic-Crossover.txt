Title: Black Bean Pasta Meals with Varying Protein Concentrations Reduce Postprandial Glycemia and Insulinemia Similarly Compared to White Bread Control in Adults.
PMID: 35681402
PMCID: PMC9180063
DOI: 10.3390/foods11111652
Registry ID: NCT05182190
First published: 2022
Authors: Winham DM, Thompson SV, Heer MM, Davitt ED, Hooper SD, Cichy KA, et al.
Journal: Foods (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35681402/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized crossover trial (Winham et al., 2022, Foods, PMC9180063, NCT05182190) in 18 normoglycemic adults (8 men, 10 women) comparing boiled whole black beans (196.6 g providing 28.2 g dietary fiber and 50 g available carbohydrate) against white bread and three black bean flour pastas. Whole black beans lowered 3-hour postprandial glucose and insulin iAUCs vs white bread, while 10-12 h postprandial gastrointestinal questionnaires showed NO statistically significant differences in stool frequency, stool consistency, bloating, or flatulence across treatments. Directly tests whole boiled black beans on acute GI tolerability with a null bowel-symptom result; non-PD healthy adults and acute single-meal crossover rather than chronic constipation therapy.
