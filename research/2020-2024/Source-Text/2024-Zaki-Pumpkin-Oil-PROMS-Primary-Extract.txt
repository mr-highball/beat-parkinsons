Combined cold-pressed oil and Solcoseryl in oral mucositis

Primary source: https://www.jidmr.com/journal/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/27-D24_3129_Ghada_M_Ahmad_Egypt-Clin.pdf
Coverage: Original full body, registry linkage based on study characteristics
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

Zaki et al., issue posting September 28, 2024. Forty participants with cancer-treatment mucositis were randomized to a topical formula with 10% cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil, 10% Solcoseryl and paraffin or to benzydamine, 17 analyzed per group. The paper does not print NCT04303312, but the unique title, investigator, setting and endpoints strongly indicate the same trial. Its formulation, two-week duration and actual enrollment differ from several registered fields. PROMS means for combination were 81.76, 46.59 and 24.29 versus 72.06, 77.29 and 82.35, with follow-up comparisons p=.0003 and p<.0001. Three combination participants died of leukemia-associated internal hemorrhage, without reported assignment of causality, and three controls withdrew with burning. PROMS includes mouth pain and speaking restriction, without a verified item estimate. The topical multi-component intervention does not test a toasted culinary oil effect on Parkinson speech intelligibility.
