Title: Amantadine use in the French prospective NS-Park cohort
PMID: 38578434
DOI: 10.1007/s00702-024-02772-4
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00702-024-02772-4
Publication: 2024-04-05
Coverage: Author abstract, bibliography and institutional reference. Complete outcomes and tables remain open.

The cohort measured medication use, motor complications and nonmotor characteristics. The authors report amantadine use in 1,585 of 12,542 participants and more frequent freezing among characteristics associated with starting the medication. Reported follow-up improvements concern dyskinesia and motor fluctuations. The abstract lists freezing in its analysis plan without reporting a separate freezing treatment estimate. The author byline includes Melissa as a given name. It does not constitute evidence for an intake of lemon balm.
