Title: Acute pain impairs retention of locomotor learning
Authors: Samuel R Jackson and colleagues
Date: 2024-02-21 first online
Identifiers: PMID 38381551; PMCID PMC11305642; DOI 10.1152/jn.00343.2023
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11305642/
Coverage: Original factual extract of primary Methods and Results.

Healthy young subjects learned a treadmill step-length asymmetry through distorted visual feedback. The pain experiment compared capsaicin cream plus warming on the lower leg with no stimulus. A separate experiment used warming without the constituent. Pain did not clearly impair initial pattern acquisition or baseline stepping. The pain group reproduced less of the learned asymmetry the next day while pain-free, whereas the heat-alone comparison did not show this result. The measure was a learned asymmetry, with no diagnosis of gait freezing or natural stride shortening.
