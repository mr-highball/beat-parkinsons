Review depth: Original journal abstract, the same investigators' P-23 abstract, and the original trial protocol. The version-of-record result tables were unavailable through the tested routes.

Thirty adults with ALS were randomized and 24 completed follow-up. The 12-week trial compared two doses of the mixed formulation with placebo alongside riluzole. The investigators' P-23 abstract reports an inconclusive between-group pain VAS result (p=.916). The reported functional-scale effects concern the primary ALS outcome and should not stand in for a pain comparison. The protocol excludes Parkinson disease and the assigned product is a multicomponent extract. It cannot isolate ground turmeric. The protocol and P-23 are members of the same randomized family, distinct from the 24-week phase IIb study. Round 1 claim 0812.
