The Nurses Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study analysis included 47,679 participants. A subset of 17,400 was surveyed for additional prodromal features. Excessive daytime sleepiness was defined using an Epworth Sleepiness Scale score of at least 10.
Higher whole-pattern aMED and AHEI scores were associated with less excessive daytime sleepiness in the prodromal-feature analyses. Nuts and legumes form a combined AHEI component. The pattern association does not isolate canned lentils or estimate a Parkinson treatment effect.
