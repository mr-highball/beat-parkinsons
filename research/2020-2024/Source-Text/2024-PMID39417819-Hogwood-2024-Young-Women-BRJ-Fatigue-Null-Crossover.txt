Title: The effects of inorganic nitrate supplementation on muscular power and endurance across the menstrual cycle.
PMID: 39417819
DOI: 10.1152/japplphysiol.00323.2024
First published: 2024
Authors: Hogwood AC, Ortiz De Zevallos J, Kruse K, Buckley M, De Guzman J, DeJong Lempke A, et al.
Journal: Journal of applied physiology (Bethesda, Md. : 1985)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39417819/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=10 healthy young women: acute BRJ did not improve neuromuscular fatigue recovery and slightly reduced peak power/velocity relative to placebo. Competing null/unfavorable exercise trial.
