Title: Electroacupuncture for motor dysfunction and constipation in patients with Parkinson's disease: a randomised controlled multi-centre trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9860357/
PMID: 36691434
PMCID: PMC9860357
DOI: 10.1016/j.eclinm.2022.101814
First published online: 2023-01-13
Trial identifier as reported: ChiCTR1800019517
Text coverage: factual extraction of Methods and Table 2.

The multicenter trial randomized 166 people with Parkinson disease, 83 per arm, to electroacupuncture plus usual medication or usual medication with a waitlist. Participants could distinguish the assigned treatments. Outcome assessors were blinded. There was no sham treatment or dietary intervention.

The original Table 2 reports median weekly spontaneous bowel movements at week 12 of 3.0 (IQR 2.5–4.0) versus 0.5 (0.0–2.3), p=.001, from 25 and 26 participants with constipation data. Mean constipation severity scores were 8.0 and 10.3, with an unadjusted endpoint contrast of −2.3 (95% CI −4.0 to −0.6). The baseline severity scores also differed. The same table gives adjusted changes of −2.1 and −1.9 but prints an adjusted difference of −1.9 (95% CI −3.2 to −0.6). Those entries are inconsistent and the adjusted contrast is not verified. The publication names ChiCTR1800019517. A 2019 protocol by a similarly named group lists ChiCTR1900021053 and must be kept separate unless their relationship is verified.

The Park 2026 review supplement lists this trial as a Parkinson bowel intervention and identifies a separate ginger-tablet trial conducted in multiple sclerosis. The electroacupuncture result has no ingredient exposure and cannot grade a ginger claim.
