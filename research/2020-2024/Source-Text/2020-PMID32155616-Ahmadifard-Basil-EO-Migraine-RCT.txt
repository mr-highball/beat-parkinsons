Title: The Efficacy of Topical Basil Essential Oil on Relieving Migraine Headaches: A Randomized Triple-Blind Study.
PMID: 32155616
DOI: 10.1159/000506349
First published: 2020
Authors: Ahmadifard M, Yarahmadi S, Ardalan A, Ebrahimzadeh F, Bahrami P, Sheikhi E
Journal: Complementary medicine research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32155616/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized triple-blind placebo-controlled clinical trial in n=144 patients diagnosed with migraine stratified into 4 parallel arms (n=36/arm): topical basil (Ocimum basilicum) essential oil 2%, 4%, 6%, or matched placebo applied every 8 hours for 3 successive months alongside uniform background acetaminophen (325 mg q12h). Pain intensity (VAS) and attack frequency were assessed at baseline and weeks 2, 4, 8, and 12 using marginal GEE models. Significant dose-by-time reductions favored basil essential oil over placebo for both VAS pain intensity (p < 0.001) and attack frequency (p < 0.001). Exposure is topical concentrated essential oil in migraine rather than oral fresh culinary basil in PD, and abstract does not tabulate raw group VAS means.
