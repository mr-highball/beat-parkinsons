Original study scope
The review names asparagus, onion and cranberry as dietary sources of quercetin and then discusses isolated quercetin and delivery systems in neurodegeneration. A food source list and nanoparticle result are different exposure contexts. The complete original yields no relevant brewed green tea comparison.

Continuing evidence gaps
No estimated contribution from the three food preparations or link to the frozen Parkinson fatigue, mood, bradykinesia or rigidity outcome is reported. The publisher dates this article 7 December 2022 and the journal issue is from 2023 (https://www.benthamscience.com/article/125400).
