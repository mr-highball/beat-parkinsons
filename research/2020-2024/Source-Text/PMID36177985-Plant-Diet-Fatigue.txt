Plant diet quality and separately reported fatigue
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9745455/
PMID: 36177985
PMCID: PMC9745455
DOI: 10.1002/jcsm.13077
Coverage: Primary body Table 3 and complete original supplement, Tables S1 and S4

Sotos-Prieto and colleagues published this prospective Nurses' Health Study analysis on September 30, 2022, in women aged 60 years and older. Table 3 reports 34,493 fatigue cases. The adjusted fatigue hazard ratio comparing the highest with lowest quintile of the healthful plant-based diet index was 0.79 (95% CI 0.77–0.82). The unhealthful index estimate was 1.16 (1.12–1.20). Both trend p values were less than .001. Supplement Table S1 groups caffeinated or decaffeinated coffee with tea. Table S4 evaluates broad plant or animal food groups against composite frailty. The paper gives fatigue as its own measure for diet patterns and supplies neither a separate coffee–fatigue estimate nor a Parkinson-specific comparison.
