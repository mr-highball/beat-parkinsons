Title: Continuous cholinergic-dopaminergic updating in the nucleus accumbens underlies approaches to reward-predicting cues

First public article date: 2022-12-24
Accessed: 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Curated original Figure 1 and behavioral Methods

The mouse neuroscience study uses strawberry milkshake as a reward
paired with a visual cue. Early task training associates that cue
with access to the milkshake. Approaches to the touchscreen and
reward collection are behavioral measures. The investigators study
cholinergic and dopaminergic signaling during learning.

The paper does not assign whole strawberries or compare the reward
with a strawberry-free food. It supplies the young-mouse reference
cited by the later stroke and task-salience posters. An approach to a
reward supplies no spontaneous-fall or Parkinson resting-tremor
measure.
