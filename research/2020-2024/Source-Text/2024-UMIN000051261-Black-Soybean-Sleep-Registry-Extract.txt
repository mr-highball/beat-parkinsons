Public registration for the later sleepiness on rising trial
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000058456
Registration: UMIN000051261
Coverage: Official registry metadata and outcome fields

The UMIN page lists a registered date and anticipated start of June 5, 2023, last follow-up December 17, 2023 and study information disclosure December 16, 2024. The dates distinguish registration from documented public disclosure. It describes a parallel placebo-controlled study in healthy adults and specifies 12-week sleepiness on rising from the OSA-MA as the primary outcome. PSQI components and other sleep measures are secondary. The 2025 article supplies composition and results. This is a different protocol from the four-week, task-day crossover reported in 2021.
