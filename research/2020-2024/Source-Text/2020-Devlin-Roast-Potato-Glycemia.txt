Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33069511/
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2020.09.049
Format: Original methods and outcome scope, paraphrased.
Population: Adults with type 2 diabetes.
Comparison: Four mixed evening meals in crossover periods with servings of boiled, roasted, or boiled-then-cooled potato, or basmati rice.
Outcome scope: Glucose and insulin responses, including overnight glucose. The report does not measure clinical insomnia.
