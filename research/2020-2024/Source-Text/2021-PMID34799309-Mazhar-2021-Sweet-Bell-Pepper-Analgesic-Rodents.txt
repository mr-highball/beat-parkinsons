Title: Analgesic and anti-inflammatory potential of four varieties of bell pepper (Capsicum annum L.) in rodents.
PMID: 34799309
First published: 2021
Authors: Mazhar N, Baig SG, Ahmed S, Ul Hasan MM, Palla A, Ishrat G
Journal: Pakistan journal of pharmaceutical sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34799309/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled rodent analgesic and anti-inflammatory study (10 groups, n=7/group, N=70) testing oral 95% ethanol extracts (200 and 400 mg/kg) of all four sweet bell pepper colors (Capsicum annuum L.: green, yellow, orange, red) against vehicle control and aspirin standard in acetic acid writhing, tail immersion, hot-plate nociception, and carrageenan rat paw edema assays (with acute toxicity testing up to 3,000 mg/kg showing zero mortality). All four sweet bell pepper extracts significantly increased hot-plate pain threshold and markedly reduced carrageenan paw edema and nociceptive behavior. Limits: non-PD acute nociception rodent model and fresh ethanolic extracts rather than roasted whole sweet bell pepper in human PD.
