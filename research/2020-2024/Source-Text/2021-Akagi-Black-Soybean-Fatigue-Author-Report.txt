Black soybean extract and task day drowsiness in the 2021 crossover
Source: https://www.fujicco.co.jp/corp/upload/pr_210629.pdf
Coverage: Contemporaneous author report and publisher issue record; journal numerical tables inaccessible

Akagi and colleagues' issue is dated June 20, 2021 in the [Japanese science index](https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/en/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=202102282653702133). The [publisher contents](https://www.lifescience.co.jp/yk/yk21/yke2106.html) list pages 953–964. The June 29 sponsor release reproduces the authors' conference abstract. It describes 24 healthy adults reporting usual fatigue, a randomized crossover with four weeks per condition, and comparisons of black soybean seed coat extract capsules and dextrin placebo around two hours of computer work and a recovery period. The author abstract reports less subjective drowsiness with extract and omits numerical contrasts. The journal body and registry identity remain open. This is a Glycine max extract and an acute task rating in healthy volunteers.
