In O036/808, Mayer and colleagues grouped 2,316 Hamburg City Health Study participants by daily coffee consumption. The 3–4 cup group had lower peak width of skeletonized mean diffusivity (p=.019) and higher cortical thickness (p=.008) than the less-than-one-cup group in the reported conference analysis. Neither structural marker measures fatigue. Coffee brewing method and a Parkinson fatigue contrast were unreported. The January 2023 journal paper has the same cohort, authors and imaging endpoints, with different adjusted p-values. It is the same cohort family.
