Title: Avocado oil: Production and market demand, bioactive components, implications in health, and tendencies and potential uses.
PMID: 34146454
DOI: 10.1111/1541-4337.12784
First published: 2021
Authors: Cervantes-Paz B, Yahia EM
Journal: Comprehensive reviews in food science and food safety
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34146454/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Comprehensive food-science review of avocado oil (Persea americana) extraction methods (cold pressing, centrifugation, solvent/supercritical extraction), fatty acid/phytochemical retention across refined vs cold-pressed grades, and health literature. Explicitly concludes that proposed health effects of avocado oil on chronic diseases have been demonstrated through in vitro and animal studies, NOT in human clinical trials, confirming the lack of human neurological or PD clinical evidence for culinary avocado oil.
