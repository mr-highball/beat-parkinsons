Title: HIIT-The Track exercise crossover and symptom measurement
PMID: 37371330
PMCID: PMC10296509
DOI: 10.3390/brainsci13060849
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10296509/
First publication: 2023-05-24
Coverage: Original NCBI PMC JATS article body checked and summarized.

Four people with idiopathic Parkinson disease took part in a randomized-order single-case crossover of stationary cycling intervals and continuous aerobic exercise. Each exercise period lasted four weeks and a washout separated them. The paper presents repeated motor scores, disability stage, cognitive function, BDI-II mood scores and biomarkers. Mood scores were low at baseline. Varied within-person responses and fluctuations during non-training periods limit attribution. Apathy appears in the discussion as a candidate modifier for future studies, with no dedicated apathy scale reported. The report and registry NCT05357638 belong to the same exercise family.
