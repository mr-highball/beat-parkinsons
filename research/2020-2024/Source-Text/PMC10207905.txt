Title: Pharmacogenomics—a New Frontier for Individualized Treatment of Parkinson's Disease

This review examines genetic variation in medication response and adverse effects, particularly with levodopa. The comparisons concern drug-specific pharmacogenomics without an observed dried fig exposure, timing, or visual episode count.
