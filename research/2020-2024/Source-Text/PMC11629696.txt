Title: Gba1 E326K renders motor and non-motor symptoms with pathological α-synuclein, tau and glial activation

The model paper links the Gba1 E326K variant to enzyme activity, substrate accumulation, behavior and tissue pathology. Nonmotor test outcomes in the experimental model do not record human visual hallucinations or dried fig intake.
