The study analyzes interviews with 12 spouses of people with Parkinson disease who did not identify as primary caregivers. Participants reported how roles, losses and coping shifted around diagnosis and as the condition progressed. The qualitative interviews did not assign sage or dried dates or assess a dietary symptom contrast.
