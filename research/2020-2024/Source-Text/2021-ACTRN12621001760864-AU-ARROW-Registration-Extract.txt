Official ANZCTR registration ACTRN12621001760864. First registration December 23, 2021. Registry update shown October 18, 2025.
The AU-ARROW trial compares a 24-month multidomain lifestyle intervention with general education in individuals at higher risk of dementia. Lifestyle elements include dietary advice, exercise, cognitive training and health monitoring. Global cognition is the primary endpoint. The registry site links a protocol publication and shows no posted comparative results as checked November 16, 2026.
