Borges et al., April 20, 2022. Absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion of apigenin and its glycosides in healthy male adults.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35452808/
Access and coverage: Original author abstract and linked ClinicalTrials.gov protocol NCT03526081.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

Healthy male adults consumed dried powdered parsley leaves in yogurt in one intervention. The reported apigenin-4′-glucuronide plasma time to maximum was six hours after the yogurt formulation and four hours after the parsley drink. Other preparations included chamomile or isolated compound. The measurements concern metabolites, without levodopa kinetics or a dyskinesia, handwriting or swallowing outcome.
