Page S186 describes a patient taking lithium and fluoxetine who recently used a caffeinated workout supplement and presented with a seizure. The report is a clinical vignette in a multiexposure setting and supplies no isolated caffeine or coffee contrast.

Page S250 reports kidney injury in a patient taking a combined analgesic powder containing acetaminophen, aspirin and caffeine. The case authors attributed the findings to aspirin exposure. The product's caffeine is a documented constituent in a mixture.

Page S355 discusses stimulant ingredients, including caffeine, in a report about a preworkout supplement and suspected cardiovascular effects. Its discussion does not supply a controlled isolated caffeine exposure or an estimate for brewed coffee. The collection separately uses coffee-ground emesis to describe appearance. None of these members measures fatigue under a brewed coffee comparison in Parkinson disease.
