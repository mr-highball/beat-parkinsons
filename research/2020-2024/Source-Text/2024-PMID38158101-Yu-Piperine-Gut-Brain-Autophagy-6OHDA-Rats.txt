Title: Piperine promotes PI3K/AKT/mTOR-mediated gut-brain autophagy to degrade α-Synuclein in Parkinson's disease rats.
PMID: 38158101
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2023.117628
First published: 2024
Authors: Yu L, Hu X, Xu R, Zhao Y, Xiong L, Ai J
Journal: Journal of ethnopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38158101/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled 6-OHDA PD rat experiment testing oral piperine on gut-brain PI3K/AKT/mTOR autophagy and alpha-synuclein clearance in both substantia nigra and colon. Reported improved autonomic movement and gastrointestinal dysfunction alongside reduced alpha-synuclein aggregation and preserved dopaminergic neurons. Limits: isolated piperine in 6-OHDA rats; abstract does not report specific symptom-level gait/rigidity/constipation values.
