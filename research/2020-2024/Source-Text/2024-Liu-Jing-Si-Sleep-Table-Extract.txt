Jing Si herbal formula and sleep in functional dyspepsia

Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39256061/
Coverage: Primary Methods and Tables 1–2 from indexed original
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First electronically available September 10, 2024. Thirteen participants per arm received 15 mL/day of an eight-herb concentrate or placebo for four weeks. Table 2 assigns the tea a median PSQI of 14 (IQR 7.5–19) to 12 (6.5–17), within-arm p=.207. Placebo changed from 11 (7–16) to 10 (7.5–14.5), p=.141. The baseline comparison gave p=.472. No adjusted between-arm PSQI change was identified. The measure covers overall sleep quality and the intervention does not isolate perilla leaf. Population was functional dyspepsia.
