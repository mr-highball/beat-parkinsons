Original source scope
The matched observational study includes cinnamon, coffee and cloves among 12 odor-identification sticks. Seven participants with mild illness identified 6 ± 3 odors versus 10 ± 1 in seven controls (p=.028).

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The measured contrast is a total correct odor score across health groups. Smell stimulation is distinct from ingestion and provides no odor-specific treatment effect, no Parkinson cohort, and no cinnamon, coffee or clove fatigue or pain result.
