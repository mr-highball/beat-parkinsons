Original study scope
The mixed-method study interviewed 50 people with early Parkinson disease and nine relatives. Table 3 describes one person's morning slowness around their first coffee in the bradykinesia section. Coffee preparation and before-and-after fatigue were not reported.

Continuing evidence gaps
The coffee statement is a firsthand timing account, without an attributable fatigue effect. There is no date or fig exposure comparison.
