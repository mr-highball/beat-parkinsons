Title: Motor networks, but also non-motor networks predict motor signs in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10685308/
PMID: 37972450
PMCID: PMC10685308
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2023.103541
Publication date: 2023-11-11
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The analysis uses data from two cohorts totaling 88 Parkinson participants and relates motor and nonmotor network connectivity to off-medication motor examination scores. Visual-network involvement predicts a motor outcome and supplies no evidence about food-timed visual hallucinations.
