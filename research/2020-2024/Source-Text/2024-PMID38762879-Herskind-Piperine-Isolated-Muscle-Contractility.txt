Title: Piperine enhances contractile force in slow- and fast-twitch muscle.
PMID: 38762879
DOI: 10.1113/JP285995
First published: 2024
Authors: Herskind J, Ørtenblad N, Cheng AJ, Pedersen P, Overgaard K
Journal: The Journal of physiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38762879/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Ex vivo physiology study in isolated rodent skeletal muscles and chemically skinned single muscle fibers. Piperine bound myosin, increased myofibrillar Ca2+ sensitivity, potentiated low-frequency twitch force, and partially recovered low-frequency force after eccentric fatiguing contractions. Limits: ex vivo isolated rodent muscle fibers; does not test oral cracked black pepper or human clinical fatigue.
