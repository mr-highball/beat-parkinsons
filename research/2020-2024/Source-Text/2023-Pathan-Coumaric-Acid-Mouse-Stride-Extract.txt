Isolated p-coumaric acid and mouse stride length

Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0976500X221150837
Coverage: Original Methods and Figure 4, factual extract.

Pathan and colleagues, Journal of Pharmacology and Pharmacotherapeutics 13(4):364–374, first online February 22, 2023, issue dated December 2022. DOI 10.1177/0976500X221150837. Its original Figure 4 reports longer stride at day 21 for the two higher oral isolated compound groups versus untreated haloperidol exposure (p < .05 and p < .01), with six animals per group. The compound occurs in roasted pumpkin seed oil at concentrations measured in micrograms per gram. The animal experiment used purified compound at much higher exposures and does not establish that ordinary oil has the same effect. This study measures stride and adds a mechanistic candidate without resolving preparation, effective exposure or human transfer.
