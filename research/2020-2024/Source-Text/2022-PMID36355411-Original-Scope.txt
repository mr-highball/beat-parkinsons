Title: Older patients with COVID-19 and neuropsychiatric conditions: A study of risk factors for mortality.
PMID: 36355411
PMCID: PMC9759137
DOI: 10.1002/brb3.2787
Publication year: 2022
First public date basis: NLM and original journal record.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9759137/
Coverage: Complete article-body context reviewed 2026-11-22.

The original mortality cohort enrolled 191 older COVID-19 inpatients with neurological or psychiatric conditions. Six percent had parkinsonism. Methods and risk tables concern clinical factors and in-hospital mortality. The study has no food assignment or date-fruit pain measurement.
