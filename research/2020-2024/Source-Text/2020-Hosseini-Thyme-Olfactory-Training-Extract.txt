PMID 33350991

The 2020 report describes 16 weeks of fixed four-odor training for post-traumatic smell loss. Original Methods specify inhaled Thymus vulgaris extract alongside three other odorants. The article reports eight trained and eight untreated participants. Mean threshold, discrimination and identification composite scores were 12.42 to 18.35 and 12.59 to 13.06, respectively. The Results report a follow-up between-group composite difference (p<.033). This tests the complete scent regimen in a post-traumatic cohort. Participant overlap with the Rezaeyan imaging series remains unresolved.
