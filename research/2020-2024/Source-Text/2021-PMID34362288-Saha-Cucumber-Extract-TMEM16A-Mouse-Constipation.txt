Title: Cucumis sativus extract elicits chloride secretion by stimulation of the intestinal TMEM16A ion channel.
PMID: 34362288
PMCID: PMC8354179
DOI: 10.1080/13880209.2021.1949357
First published: 2021
Authors: Saha T, Aoun J, Sarkar P, Bourdelais AJ, Baden DG, Leblanc N et al.
Journal: Pharm Biol
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8354179/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mechanistic and mouse transit study (Saha et al., 2021, PMC8354179) isolating an HPLC-purified fraction from peeled raw Cucumis sativus fruit that activated intestinal TMEM16A chloride channels in vitro and increased charcoal-meal GI transit in loperamide-constipated mice (67.3% vs 44.0%, p<0.05, n=3-4 mice/group). Critical limits: tiny animal group sizes (n=3-4), purified raw-cucumber HPLC fraction rather than pickled whole cucumber, zero human bowel trials.
