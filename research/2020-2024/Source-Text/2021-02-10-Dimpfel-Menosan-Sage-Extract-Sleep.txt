Title: Fresh sage tip extract and sleep outcomes after menopause
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7881233/
PMID: 33615001
PMCID: PMC7881233
DOI: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2021.e05910
Coverage: Full original paper, Results 3.2 and sleep questionnaire comparisons

The trial randomized 80 menopausal women and lists 78 in its intention-to-treat population. The published per-protocol comparison has 22 sage recipients and 26 controls. Four weeks of a fresh sage-tip ethanolic extract gave favorable results on secondary sleep-quality and psychosomatic sleep questionnaire domains. The single sleep item on the menopause rating scale was a trend. Dream recall, refreshed-after-sleep ratings and sleep–wake regulation did not show a clear difference. The preparation was a processed fresh-tip extract and the cohort was selected for menopausal symptoms.

### Secondary fatigue observation and reporting gap
Trial registration as reported by the paper: EudraCT 2016-005033-77
Reviewed: 2026-11-22 UTC

Section 3.2 reports a between-group POMS fatigue result favoring the fresh sage-tip extract (p=.029). It gives values of 2.19 ± 1.34 and 1.47 ± 0.99 in the description, without an arm-by-time fatigue table or a confidence interval for the between-group estimate. The separate exhaustion item on the sleep questionnaire lacked a clear between-group effect. The paper reports many secondary comparisons. This trial enrolled menopausal women and used a manufactured ethanolic extract.

Research gap. Recover the protocol and statistical analysis plan for designation and adjustment of the POMS fatigue measure, plus arm-level data and its uncertainty if available. The exact EudraCT public results route did not supply the missing table in this check. Seek an independent comparison of fresh culinary sage with a separate Parkinson fatigue measure before upgrading the direction.
