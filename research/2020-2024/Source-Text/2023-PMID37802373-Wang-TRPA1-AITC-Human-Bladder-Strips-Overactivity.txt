Title: H2O2 enhances the spontaneous phasic contractions of isolated human-bladder strips via activation of TRPA1 channels on sensory nerves and the release of substance P and PGE2
PMID: 37802373
DOI: 10.1016/j.freeradbiomed.2023.10.001
First published: 2023
Authors: Wang H, Zhao M, Liu J, Liu L, Liu H, Ding N
Journal: Free Radical Biology & Medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37802373/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Ex vivo organ-bath study of isolated human detrusor strips from 26 cystectomy patients demonstrating that allyl isothiocyanate (AITC) mimics H2O2 by activating TRPA1 channels on bladder sensory afferents, releasing substance P and PGE2 and enhancing spontaneous phasic bladder contractions (blocked by TRPA1 antagonist HC030031). Mechanistically links AITC/TRPA1 activation to detrusor overactivity (opposing infectious-UTI prophylaxis claims; contrasted with Gratzke 2009 PMID:18468780 showing AITC relaxes precontracted human urethral strips ex vivo).
