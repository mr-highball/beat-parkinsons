Title: True Digestibility of Tryptophan in Plant and Animal Protein.
PMID: 39307282
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2024.09.014
First published: 2024
Authors: Kashyap S, Devi S, Pasanna RM, Preston T, Kurpad AV
Journal: J Nutr
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39307282/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Human dual stable-isotope protein digestibility study (Kashyap et al., 2024, J Nutr) measuring true ileal tryptophan digestibility across plant and animal proteins. Chickpea true ileal tryptophan digestibility was 72.6%, substantially below animal proteins (~90-96%), illustrating reduced amino-acid bioavailability from intact legume matrices relative to animal protein; no Parkinson disease or levodopa OFF-time endpoints.
