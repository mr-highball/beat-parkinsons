Title: Dietary fiber pilot comparisons using pea and orange fiber snacks in adult female twins
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9171781/
PMID: 35533274

DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2123411119
Publication date: 2022-05-09
Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: full_text

The original describes two open-label single-group feeding pilots with different fibers. Twelve female dizygotic twin pairs entered the orange-fiber pilot. Nine pairs had also participated in the pea-fiber pilot. The snack used a processed fiber preparation from orange pulp. The investigators measured fecal microbiota, carbohydrate-utilization genes and plasma protein profiles. They report substrate-specific microbial changes. A separate between-group constipation or raw whole orange outcome was outside the design. The partially overlapping groups also preclude treating the pilots as independent replications of an orange effect.

The 2026 polysaccharide review PMC13090150 calls the research two parallel clinical trials. The original clarifies the single-group designs and overlap. Round 1 scope: 0878.
