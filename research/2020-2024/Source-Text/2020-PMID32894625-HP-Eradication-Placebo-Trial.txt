Title: Helicobacter pylori Eradication in Parkinson's Disease: A Randomized Placebo-Controlled Trial
PMID: 32894625
DOI: 10.1002/mds.28248
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32894625/
First published: 2020-09-07. December 2020 issue, 35(12), pp. 2250–2260.
Archive scope: Original author abstract and official ClinicalTrials.gov design. Full article, subgroup results and flow tables remain open. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

Eighty people were randomized to a one-week eradication regimen or placebo. The reported analysis set has 67 people, 32 assigned treatment and 35 placebo. The primary week-12 ON-medication motor score contrast leaned 2.6 points toward placebo, with the confidence interval crossing zero at −0.4 to 5.6. The original abstract reports no clear improvement in motor, nonmotor or quality-of-life measures at weeks 12 or 52. The main result does not compare food exposure.

NCT02108704 matches the investigator, country, randomized design and prespecified ON-medication motor endpoint. The official record reports actual enrollment of 75 and no posted results. Preserve the distinct counts of 75 in the register, 80 randomized in the paper and 67 analyzed until the primary flow diagram or archival update explains them. NCT02112812 is a different study family with levodopa onset and on-duration measures.

Registry: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02108704
