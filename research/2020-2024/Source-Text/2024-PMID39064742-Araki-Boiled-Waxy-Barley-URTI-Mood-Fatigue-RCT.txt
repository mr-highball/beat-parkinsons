Title: Effects of Chronic Barley Consumption on Upper Respiratory Tract Symptoms in Japanese Healthy Adults: A Randomized, Parallel-Group, Controlled Trial.
PMID: 39064742
PMCID: PMC11279517
DOI: 10.3390/nu16142298
First published: 2024
Authors: Araki R, Ishikawa C, Kawasaki T, Kobori T, Shoji T, Takayama Y
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39064742/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

8-week investigator-blind randomized parallel-group controlled trial (PMC11279517, UMIN000049817) in n=62 randomized (n=54 analyzed: 27 vs 27) healthy Japanese adults consuming 100 g/d packaged boiled/cooked Daishimochi waxy barley (1.8 g beta-glucan) vs 100 g/d cooked white rice. Primary domains: WURSS-21 daily symptoms and POMS 2 mood. Barley significantly reduced cumulative days "feeling tired" (WURSS-21 severity >=5/7: 13 vs 52 days, p<0.0001) and yielded a more favorable POMS 2 Tension-Anxiety change (p<0.05; week-8 final median p=0.856) and non-significant trend toward lower POMS 2 Fatigue-Inertia (p=0.083). POMS 2 Depression-Dejection did not differ significantly between groups. Limits: open-label visible foods, 8/62 excluded per-protocol, unadjusted multi-item WURSS testing, healthy non-PD adults.
