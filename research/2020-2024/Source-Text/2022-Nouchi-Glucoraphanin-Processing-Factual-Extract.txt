Nouchi and colleagues, original Table 2

Original source https://doi.org/10.3389/fnagi.2022.929628

First published July 29, 2022. The randomized trial assigned 144 healthy older adults to glucoraphanin capsules or placebo for 12 weeks. Table 2 gives symbol-search mean changes of 1.99 versus 1.29, reported adjusted p = .03. Coding (p = .83) and Stroop (p = 1) were inconclusive after adjustment. The authors classify symbol search as processing speed. Capsule plant source was not specified in the checked methods. The patients, preparation and tasks do not establish clinical Parkinson attention effects.
