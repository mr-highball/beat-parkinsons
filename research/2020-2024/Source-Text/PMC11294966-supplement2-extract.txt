Coverage: Original factual extract of eTables 2–3 and supplement method boundaries.

Independent oil assays in eTable 2 identify placebo lots 1302405 (March 2015), 1301313 (October 2015), 1401313 (September 2016 and March 2017), and 171735 (September 2017 through March 2019). Across eight placebo assays the table labels EPA as 0.02–2.00 mg/mL and DHA as 0.97–1.98 mg/mL. The supplement gives no oil extraction or refining description. It designates Parkinson disease among the exclusions in eTable 1.
The executive z-score outcome averages Trail Making Test B and WAIS digit-symbol scores. In eTable 3 the annual coefficient comparing marine omega-3 with placebo is −0.03844 (95% CI −0.1179 to 0.04133, p=.35). The supplement gives no gait or walking change estimate. The parent article reports injurious falls separately.
