UMIN000053271. Research on the swallowing function improvement effect of inhalation of cypress essential oil. Registry date January 5, 2024 and public disclosure March 31, 2024.
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000060177
Access and coverage: Official registry fields for disclosure, design, intervention and endpoints.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

This crossover protocol compares inhaled cypress essential oil with a rice oil control in older people with swallowing impairment. The primary outcome is the repetitive saliva swallowing test and the key secondary is the modified water swallowing test. Oil grade, product lot and a Parkinson-specific subgroup are not provided in the registry. The subsequent peer-reviewed trial identifies the product.
