Title: Efficacy and safety of fermented Prunus mume vinegar on fatigue improvement in adults with unexplained fatigue: A randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 36438753
PMCID: PMC9682036
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2022.990418
First published: 2022
Authors: Choi JI, Lee YL, Lee SY
Journal: Frontiers in nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36438753/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind placebo-controlled 8-week RCT in n=80 adults with unexplained fatigue testing 70 mL/day fermented Prunus mume (Japanese apricot / ume) vinegar vs placebo. Distinct botanical species (Prunus mume, NOT culinary apricot Prunus armeniaca) and fermented vinegar matrix; fatigue outcomes were null between groups after accounting for placebo response.
