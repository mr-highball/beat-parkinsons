Registered long COVID herbal formula and sleep quality

Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05668104
Coverage: Official design and outcome modules
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First posted December 29, 2022. IRB111-232-A lists 32 enrolled and a crossover in long-COVID gut-brain conditions with day-28 PSQI. The narrative names perilla leaf in its eight-herb formula. The arm description calls the liquid packet a 15 mg tablet, leaving amount and form uncertain. The record marks the study completed, with no posted outcome. Different design, IRB ID and dates separate it from the 2024 publication for NCT05948215. Participant-level overlap was not established.
