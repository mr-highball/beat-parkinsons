The open-label 2021 oil trial randomized 183 (91 oil and 92 usual care). Table 2 reports 6.3 ± 4.1 days of lethargy or fatigue in 62 participants with that symptom versus 5.4 ± 3.2 days in 56 controls with the symptom, p=.18. The numerical duration was longer with oil. The estimate is inconclusive and covers a symptomatic subset during acute illness. The trial flow reports four and six losses in the oil and control groups. The printed Table 2 labels the total as 173. The overall recovery result does not establish a symptom-duration reduction. The paper lists three mild gastrointestinal reports and one hospitalization for suspected severe nausea and vomiting in the oil group. The report does not establish overlap or confirm causality. The preparation was oral seed oil. Ground seed and Parkinson disease were outside its scope.
