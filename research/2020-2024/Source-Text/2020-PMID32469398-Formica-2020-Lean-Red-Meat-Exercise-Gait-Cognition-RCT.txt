Title: Effect of lean red meat combined with a multicomponent exercise program on muscle and cognitive function in older adults: a 6-month randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 32469398
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/nqaa104
First published: 2020
Authors: Formica MB, Gianoudis J, Nowson CA, O'Connell SL, Milte C, Ellis KA, et al.
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32469398/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

24-week parallel RCT (ACTRN12613001153707; protocol PMC4529719) in n=154 healthy community-dwelling older adults >=65 y undertaking 3 d/wk multicomponent resistance training randomized to 2x80 g cooked lean red meat (participant choice of lean beef, lamb, or veal cuts) on training days vs carbohydrate control (pasta/rice). Primary outcomes (total/leg lean mass, muscle strength, global/executive cognition) and sit-to-stand/4-square step tests did not differ between groups. Secondary outcomes favored Ex+Meat for gait speed (+0.05 m/s; 95% CI 0.00 to 0.11; p=0.042), arm lean mass (+0.07 kg; p=0.029), and muscle density (+1.0%; p=0.015), whereas carbohydrate+Ex showed greater net gains in working memory/learning at 12 wk (SD 0.24; p=0.011) and 24 wk (SD 0.27; 95% CI 0.06 to 0.49; p=0.007). Limits: non-PD older adults, beef pooled with lamb/veal, secondary gait endpoint.
