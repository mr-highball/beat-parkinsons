Title: Effects of Short-Term Intake of Montmorency Tart Cherry Juice on Sleep Quality after Intermittent Exercise in Elite Female Field Hockey Players: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 36011907
PMCID: PMC9408103
DOI: 10.3390/ijerph191610272
First published: 2022
Authors: Chung J, Choi M, Lee K
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9408103/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (Chung et al., 2022, Int J Environ Res Public Health, PMC9408103) in 19 elite female field hockey players (10 tart cherry juice, 9 placebo) consuming Montmorency tart cherry juice five times over 48 hours after intermittent exhaustive exercise. Actigraphy showed significant group-by-time interaction effects favoring tart cherry juice for total time in bed (TTB, p=0.015), wake after sleep onset (WASO, p=0.044), and movement index (MI, p=0.031), with no significant group-by-time differences in melatonin or cortisol. Limits: tiny athletic sample (n=19), 48-hour post-exercise window, liquid juice rather than dried or raw whole fruit, non-PD population.
