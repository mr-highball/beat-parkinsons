Original study scope
The open-label Parkinson study administered purified cannabidiol in an oral sesame-oil solution. Thirteen people received the formulation and ten contributed efficacy data. The paper reports motor and nonmotor measures, including a prespecified rest-tremor score without a clear end-of-treatment change. A table lists turmeric as a concomitant agent for an individual. No unsupplemented oil arm or matched oil control identifies the carrier effect. The isolated adverse-effect discussion cannot establish sesame-oil attribution.

Continuing evidence gaps
Sesame oil grade and cold pressing are unverified. Anxiety, depression and rigidity cannot be attributed to the carrier. The turmeric co-use supplies no independent dose, preparation or outcome. Preserve the two leads without treating the carrier or concomitant use as controlled exposures.
