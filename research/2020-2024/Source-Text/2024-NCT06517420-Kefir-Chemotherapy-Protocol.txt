Cow milk kefir registered during chemotherapy cycles
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06517420
Registration: NCT06517420
Coverage: Official study registration and outcomes with thesis linkage

First posted July 24, 2024. The 84-participant randomized study compares supplemental kefir after four chemotherapy cycles with usual care in breast or colorectal cancer. Symptom management and quality of life are registered. The thesis by the named investigator is consistent with the arms and sample and reports aggregate physical burden. The registry itself has no posted results as of November 16, 2026. [Thesis result text](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Kefir-Fatigue.md#chemotherapy-kefir-thesis-and-physical-symptom-burden).
