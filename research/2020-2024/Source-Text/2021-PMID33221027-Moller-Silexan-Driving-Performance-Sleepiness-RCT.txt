Title: Silexan does not affect driving performance after single and multiple dose applications: Results from a double-blind, placebo and reference-controlled study in healthy volunteers.
PMID: 33221027
DOI: 10.1016/j.jpsychires.2020.10.028
First published: 2021
Authors: Möller HJ, Volz HP, Seifritz E, Müller H, Kenntner-Mabiala R, Kaussner Y, Schoch S, Kasper S
Journal: Journal of psychiatric research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33221027/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo- and lorazepam-controlled crossover trial (Moller et al., 2021, J Psychiatr Res) in healthy adults (Part 1 n=50; Part 2 n=25) testing oral Silexan 80 mg (single & 7-day multiple doses) and 160-320 mg single doses vs placebo and lorazepam 1 mg on alcohol-calibrated driving simulator lane position (SDLP), driving errors, and sleepiness. Silexan 80 mg was equivalent to placebo after single (95% CI -1.43 to +1.38 cm) and 7-day dosing (-1.45 to +0.79 cm; equivalence margin +/-2 cm), and 160-320 mg beat lorazepam by -8.58 to -5.42 cm (p<0.001), with placebo-like sleepiness and driving error profiles. Confirms oral lavender oil is non-sedating and neither impairs nor enhances objective sustained attention/psychomotor driving performance vs placebo.
