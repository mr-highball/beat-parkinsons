Original study scope
The official 657-page proceedings contain abstract 000741 on open-label kefir feasibility in 52 ICU patients with an intact gastrointestinal tract. Only 14 provided paired stool samples. Changes in richness were inconclusive overall, and falls or fatigue in Parkinson disease were not measured. PDF page 199 has a separate enteral diet containing decaffeinated coffee flavor. Cherry seed toxicity is distinct from edible cherry. Sage, Melissa, orange and Turkey have unrelated uses.

Continuing evidence gaps
Trace the kefir cohort's later study report and keep feasibility, microbiome and clinical outcomes separate. No matching Parkinson evidence for the frozen food pairs arises from the proceedings.
