Title: Zahidi date flesh and date seed powder in a pilot biomarker crossover
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10534174/
PMID: 37780309
PMCID: PMC10534174
DOI: 10.1016/j.fochx.2023.100834
Publication date: 2023-08-09
Date basis: NLM electronic publication.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24 UTC
Review depth: Structured full original methods and results.

Ten participants took part in a pilot crossover of Zahidi date flesh and seed powder. The Methods describe 28-day exposures and washout. Table 1 measures 9.1% moisture in the sampled flesh. The report assigns lower hs-CRP and several other biomarker findings to the seed intervention. The printed table gives before-and-after blood values and the article supplies no flesh-specific pain or apathy outcome.

This trial tests food constituents and blood markers. It does not measure pain during dried date consumption or change in Parkinson apathy.
