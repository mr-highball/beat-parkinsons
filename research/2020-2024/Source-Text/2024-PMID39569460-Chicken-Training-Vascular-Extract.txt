Fujie and colleagues explicitly identify this work as a secondary analysis of UMIN000038253. It compares the same four resistance-exercise and chicken groups in older women. The Methods identify steamed breast as the high-protein food. Some muscle composition or quality and vascular indices differed. The increase in carotid–femoral pulse-wave velocity was greater with resistance training alone than with the other arms. Several muscle-strength or load indices did not show an added advantage from chicken over training alone. The detailed report gives the actual comparison and outcome for each measure.

Use the original article and the June 2024 Uchida report as complementary measures from one cohort. These comparisons do not measure perceived fatigue severity in Parkinson disease. Changes in muscle size or vascular stiffness do not substitute for that missing endpoint.
