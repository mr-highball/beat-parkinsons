Title: The efficacy of oral Lavandula angustifolia Mill. essential oil on menopausal symptoms, serum lipid profile, and cortisol concentration in postmenopausal women: A triple-blind, randomized, controlled trial.
PMID: 38754638
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2024.103050
First published: 2024
Authors: Haramshahi M, Babaie S, Shahnazi M, Kafil B, Farshbaf-Khalili A, Naghdi M
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38754638/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Triple-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (Haramshahi et al., 2024, Complement Ther Med; follow-up letters PMID:40300466/40345901) in 72 postmenopausal women aged 50-65 randomized to oral Lavandula angustifolia essential oil softgel 80 mg/day or placebo for 60 days. Total Greene menopause scores fell identically in both arms (27.4+/-6.3 to 17.7+/-4.9 vs 27.4+/-7.1 to 17.6+/-5.1), with NO significant between-group differences on anxiety, depression, psychological, somatic/physical, or vasomotor domains, serum cortisol, or lipids (only sexual dysfunction differed, adjusted MD -0.35, p=0.039). Important null oral lavender essential oil trial.
