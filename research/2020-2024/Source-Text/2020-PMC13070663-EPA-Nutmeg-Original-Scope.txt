European Psychiatry published the abstract collection on pages S283–S589. The structured PMC body contains 808 separately identified members. Searching member bodies for culinary nutmeg, Myristica, mace and named constituents located one nutmeg case in subarticle297. Its introductory paragraph describes gastrointestinal folk use without a case-level bowel observation. The actual case describes mixed insomnia during acute mania after high, unspecified intake. Drug treatment and the mania make the time course hard to attribute. This member is the same case already distilled in 2020-EPA-Nutmeg-Mania-and-Insomnia.txt.

Claim 0168 | PMCID:PMC13070663 | The only nutmeg poster describes high-use psychiatric toxicity. Its introductory gastrointestinal use reference supplies no measured bowel comparison.

Claim 0774 | PMCID:PMC13070663 | Member subarticle297 reports insomnia during acute mania after unusually high nutmeg use. Preparation and baseline sleep observations are unspecified.
