Ramanaiah I, Sudeep HV, Shyamprasad K. Viphyllin, a standardized black pepper extract, in diabetic rats. Diabetes Metab Syndr Obes. First published 2022-06-15. PMID 35733641; PMCID PMC9207258; DOI 10.2147/DMSO.S366609.
Original: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.2147/DMSO.S366609

Male Wistar rats with a diabetes model were compared with untreated diabetic and non-diabetic groups. After six weeks, Figure 4 bars are approximately 1.4 seconds in the diabetic control, 3.4 to 3.8 seconds in the extract groups, and 4.5 seconds in the non-diabetic control. Values are visually approximate, not published exact means. All extract bars are marked p<.05 versus untreated diabetes, n=5 per group. The manufacturer-affiliated experiment also reports metabolic and electrophysiology changes. The intervention is an oral extract rich in β-caryophyllene, with no culinary cracked pepper or human Parkinson pain rating.
