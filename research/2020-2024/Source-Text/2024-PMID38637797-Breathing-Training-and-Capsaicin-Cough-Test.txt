Title: Diaphragmatic breathing, reflux-related cough and anxiety outcomes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11027235/
PMID: 38637797
PMCID: PMC11027235
DOI: 10.1186/s12931-024-02783-5
Publication date: 2024-04-18
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Review depth: Original full-text methods and results.

Sixty adults were randomized to usual care with or without diaphragmatic breathing training. The investigators report a favorable eight-week GAD-7 comparison for training in the intention-to-treat analysis (p=.036). Capsaicin was an inhaled cough-sensitivity probe. Both arms underwent the measurement. No culinary pepper exposure or anxiety effect attributable to capsaicin was assigned.
