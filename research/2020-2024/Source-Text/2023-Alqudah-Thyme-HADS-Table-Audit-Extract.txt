Consistency check on the thyme anxiety tables
Original Tables II–III and arithmetic consistency audit
Alqudah et al. randomized 106 university students in 2023. In Table II, normal-range anxiety counts were 27 of 53 with dried thyme and 30 of 53 with starch at baseline. After one month the counts were 40 and 31, respectively. The printed mild range overlaps the moderate and severe ranges. Table III prints mean baseline thyme HADS-A 7.33, SD 0.8, while Table II lists six observations of at least 15. Those six alone require a sample SD of at least sqrt(6 × (15 − 7.33)^2 / 52) = 2.60, before counting any remaining observations. The unchanged published mean and SD belong to the same stated measurement and cannot jointly fit the threshold counts. The control mean change of 0.23 and marginal SDs of 0.9 and 0.4 also imply a paired t-statistic magnitude of at least 1.29 at n = 53, inconsistent with p = .71 under the stated calculation. The directional category counts are descriptive. Participant-level data or a published correction are needed to reconcile the inferential results. The cohort was university students. The official registry lists actual enrollment 86 and triple masking, while the article reports 106 and double blinding. The record was first posted after completion and currently has no results.
