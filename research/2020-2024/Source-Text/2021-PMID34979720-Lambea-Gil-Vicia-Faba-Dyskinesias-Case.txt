Title: Levodopa-Induced Dyskinesias Related to Vicia faba Ingestion in a Parkinson's Disease Patient.
PMID: 34979720
DOI: 10.4103/0028-3886.333436
First published: 2021
Authors: Lambea-Gil A, María-Ángeles, Requena-Calleja, Horna-Cañete L
Journal: Neurology India
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34979720/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Verified Round 4 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Clinical case letter (Neurol India 2021) describing a PD patient on a stable prescribed dopaminergic regimen who developed generalized levodopa-induced dyskinesias, sweating, tachycardia, and anxiety approximately 90 minutes after eating fresh sauteed broad beans (Vicia faba) on two consecutive days (positive repeated challenge). Limitations: n=1 case report, no plasma L-DOPA or bean assay, anxiety occurred secondary to acute dyskinetic/hyperdopaminergic episode.
