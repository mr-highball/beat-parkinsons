Case 2 was a child with pollen-related oral allergy and described throat itching after several raw fruits including raw apricot. Skin prick-by-prick used fresh apricot peel/pulp; the separate oral food challenge that produced abdominal pain used raw celery. Do not transfer celery-associated pain to apricot. No Parkinson diagnosis or fruit-specific pain-severity estimate. https://www.all-imm.com/index.php/aei/article/view/28/6
