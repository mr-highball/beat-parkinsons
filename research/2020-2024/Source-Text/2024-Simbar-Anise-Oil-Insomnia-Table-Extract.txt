Anise oil in a combined premenstrual insomnia comparison

Primary source: https://www.eurekaselect.com/article/144661
Coverage: Publisher first-online date and indexed original Table 5
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published November 29, 2024, assigned to a 2026 issue. In a randomized PMS trial 32 combination and 35 control participants completed follow-up. The softgel contained anise, evening primrose and clary sage oils, with sunflower oil control. Table 5 means for insomnia from baseline across two cycles were 5.19, 3.56, 2.44 versus 5.46, 2.86, 2.23. The report lists between-group p=.810, no significant group-by-time interaction and a time effect p<.001. No treatment component can be isolated. The oil blend and population differ from ground anise seed and Parkinson insomnia.
