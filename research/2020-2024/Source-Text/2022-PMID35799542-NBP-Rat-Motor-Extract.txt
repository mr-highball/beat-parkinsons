Title: Butylphthalide and motor test outcomes in rotenone-exposed rats
PMID: 35799542
PMCID: PMC9241398
DOI: 10.4103/1673-5374.343892
Publication chronology: public author report available in 2022, journal issue January 2023.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9241398/
Coverage: original methods and Figure 1. Local factual extract.

The experiment evaluates isolated DL-3-n-butylphthalide in rats. The authors report favorable results on rod retention, beam testing and forelimb grip after treatment, alongside biochemical observations. Resting or quiescent tremor appears in the introduction as a clinical feature rather than as an individual measured outcome. The experiment does not compare whole celery, fresh stems or human dietary exposure.
