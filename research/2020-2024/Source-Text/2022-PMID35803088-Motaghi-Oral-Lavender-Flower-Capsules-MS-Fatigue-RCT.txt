Title: Lavender improves fatigue symptoms in multiple sclerosis patients: A double-blind, randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 35803088
DOI: 10.1016/j.msard.2022.104000
First published: 2022
Authors: Motaghi N, Tajadini H, Shafiei K, Sharififar F, Ansari M, Sharifi H, Sarhadynejad Z, Tavakoli-Far F et al.
Journal: Multiple sclerosis and related disorders
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35803088/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial (Motaghi et al., 2022, Mult Scler Relat Disord) in 48 adults with relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis (MFIS >= 25, EDSS <= 6; n=24 per arm) randomized to oral capsules containing 600 mg dried lavender flowers three times daily (1,800 mg/day) or 600 mg cornstarch placebo for 60 days. Baseline Modified Fatigue Impact Scale (MFIS) scores were similar (40.56+/-7.63 vs 41.43+/-7.89, p=0.706). After 60 days, MFIS fell sharply in the oral dried lavender flower group to 7.04+/-4.91 vs 38.17+/-9.78 in placebo (p<0.001). Limits: single-center trial with an unusually large effect magnitude needing independent replication; multiple sclerosis rather than Parkinson's disease; encapsulated supplemental dose.
