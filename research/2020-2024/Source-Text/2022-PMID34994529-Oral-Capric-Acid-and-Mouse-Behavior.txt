Original source scope
Mouse experiments compared isolated capric acid administered orally with vehicle. Acute low exposure increased locomotor activity; repeated exposure had more variable behavioral results, across anxiety-like and depression-related measures.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
Coconut oil is discussed as a source of medium-chain fats. The exposure was isolated capric acid, and activity is an unsuitable stand-in for patient-rated fatigue. No virgin coconut oil or Parkinson fatigue outcome.
