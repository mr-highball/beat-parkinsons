Title: Author correction to the Qamar review of dopaminergic imaging and nonmotor symptoms
Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41531-023-00464-6
PMID: 36737446
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-023-00464-6
Publication date: 2023-02-03
Source depth: Original author correction.

The publisher corrects the initials in a coauthor's name in the 2017 review, DOI 10.1038/s41531-016-0006-9. The notice amends author attribution. It describes no new clinical evidence and no change to the review discussion of gastric or other nonmotor symptoms.
