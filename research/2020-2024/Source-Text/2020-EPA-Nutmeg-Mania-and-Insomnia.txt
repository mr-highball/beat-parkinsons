Title: Myristica fragrans abuse and mania, a case report
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13070663/#subarticle297
Publication date: 2020-09-04
Source depth: complete original conference member, local research digest

One psychiatric case describes unusually high nutmeg use as a stimulant in the two weeks before an acute manic presentation. The clinicians report auditory hallucinations and mixed insomnia, with partial improvement during drug treatment. They do not identify a dose, culinary preparation, route, baseline sleep scale, or Parkinson diagnosis. Nutmeg attribution is the authors' clinical hypothesis. The member is adverse-use context and belongs to the original European Psychiatry supplement.
