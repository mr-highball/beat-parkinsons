Title: Increasing Contrast Improves Object Perception in Parkinson's Disease with Visual Hallucinations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7780942/
PMID: 33426159
PMCID: PMC7780942
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13104
Publication year: 2020
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete official PMC HTML.

The psychophysical experiment adjusts the contrast of visual stimuli in Parkinson participants with and without hallucinations. Discrimination under the task improves with higher contrast. The outcome is object identification, and it supplies no change in clinical hallucination frequency or fig exposure.

Visual tests in the complete paper measure contrast thresholds for letter or object discrimination. The paper does not report a longitudinal clinical trial of hallucination reduction after adjusting contrast.
