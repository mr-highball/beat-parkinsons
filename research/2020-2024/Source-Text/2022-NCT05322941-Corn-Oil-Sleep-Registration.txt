Registered phase 2b corn oil comparator and sleep scale
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05322941
Coverage: Factual summary of official registry fields.

First posted April 12, 2022. A separate 333-participant phase 2b study compared AEF0117 dose groups with corn-oil placebo for 12 weeks in cannabis use disorder. The Medical Outcomes Study Sleep Scale is an Other outcome. Completed, without structured registry results. The sponsor later released a comparison. The trial does not give an oil-free arm or enroll people for Parkinson insomnia.
