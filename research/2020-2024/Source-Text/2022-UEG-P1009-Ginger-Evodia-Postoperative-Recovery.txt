Title: UEG poster P1009 — topical ginger and Evodia after gastrointestinal anastomosis
PMCID: PMC9548091
DOI: 10.1002/ueg2.12295
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1002/ueg2.12295
Date basis: Published 2022-10-09. Poster P1009, printed p. 1084.
Coverage: Complete numbered publisher abstract in the official 620-page poster PDF. This factual extract concerns P1009 only.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The poster describes 100 gastric cancer patients randomly assigned after gastrointestinal anastomosis. Enhanced-recovery care was common to both groups. Intervention patients also received heated topical dried ginger and Evodia. The poster reports earlier first postoperative defecation and lower abdominal-pain and bloating scores on days 3 and 5. It provides threshold p values without group means.

The matching features support a common trial with the earlier 2022 journal report at 2022-Zhang-Ginger-Evodia-Postoperative-First-Stool.txt. The original article adds salt as a pack ingredient and reports the exact mean times. Both concern the complete topical intervention and postoperative recovery. They do not give a separate oral ginger result.
