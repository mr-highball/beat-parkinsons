Title: Hippocampal network hyperexcitability in young transgenic mice expressing human mutant alpha-synuclein
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7859835/
PMID: 33347975
PMCID: PMC7859835
DOI: 10.1016/j.nbd.2020.105226
Publication date: 2020-12-30
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: PubMed first electronic date. PMC structured metadata carries the 2021 issue date.
Design: Transgenic mouse electrophysiology study

Two alpha-synuclein mouse lines were examined for early hippocampal network excitability. The outcome is a laboratory physiological phenotype, without measured dried fig intake or clinical visual hallucinations.
