Title: Effect of Vigna angularis on High-Fat Diet-Induced Memory and Cognitive Impairments.
PMID: 33021429
DOI: 10.1089/jmf.2019.4644
First published: 2020
Authors: Choi JM, Lee SI, Cho EJ
Journal: Journal of medicinal food
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33021429/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse study (Choi, Lee & Cho, 2020, J Med Food) feeding mice a high-fat diet (HFD) for 16 weeks with oral Vigna angularis extract administered at 100 and 200 mg/kg for 4 weeks. Compared with HFD control, both VA 100 and VA 200 groups showed improved spatial and recognition memory ratios on T-maze novel route exploration, novel object recognition, and shorter escape latency on Morris water maze. Limits: abstract-only inspection lacks arm sample sizes, numeric means/SDs, p-values, and extraction details; non-Parkinson's HFD obesity model; tests spatial/recognition memory rather than attention or executive function.
