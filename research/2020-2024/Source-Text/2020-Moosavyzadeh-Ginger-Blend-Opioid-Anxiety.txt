Ginger-containing blend and anxiety in opioid maintenance
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32109133/
Reviewed: 2026-11-15
Coverage: factual extract

The trial randomized 81 people after assisted opioid detoxification to Hab-o Shefa or placebo. The abstract reports 41 completers and a favorable treatment-by-time Hamilton anxiety contrast (reported F = 2.58, p = .035). The product contains four herbs including ginger. The comparison is for the mixture. It yields no separately attributable ginger or Parkinson effect.
