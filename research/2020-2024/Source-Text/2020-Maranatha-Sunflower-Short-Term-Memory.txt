Title: Pengaruh Minyak Biji Bunga Matahari (Helianthus annuus L) Terhadap Memori Jangka Pendek pada Mahasiswa Fakultas Kedokteran
Publication date: 2020 thesis date; public repository deposit 2024-04-17
Study identifier: MARANATHA-32721
Source: https://repository.maranatha.edu/32721/
Public abstract: https://repository.maranatha.edu/32721/1/1710032_Abstrak_TOC.pdf
Text coverage: Distilled original bilingual abstracts. Methods and results chapters are access restricted.

Simbolon's undergraduate thesis reports 15 male medical students in a single-group before-and-after study of four sunflower seed oil capsules daily for 29 days. Image recall rose from 7.67 to 9.67 (paired p=.0015), number recall from 9.20 to 10.47 (p=.004) and word recall from 19.27 to 22.60 (p=.0025). No separate no-oil comparison estimates repeated testing or time effects. The publicly accessible abstract does not identify the oil's pressing grade or distinguish executive manipulation from short-term recall. The cohort was not recruited for Parkinson disease.
