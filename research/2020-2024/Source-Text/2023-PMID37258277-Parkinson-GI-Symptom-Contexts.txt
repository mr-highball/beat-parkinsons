Coverage: Original NCBI PMC JATS article body checked and summarized.

The review separates upper gastrointestinal dyspepsia, gastric emptying, and lower gastrointestinal constipation or defecatory dysfunction. Its dyspepsia discussion includes upper abdominal pain and its management discussion includes general meal size, texture and fat advice. Neither the full body nor the treatment table describes a dried date preparation or a Parkinson pain-severity comparison under dates. The general dietary references can be followed for specific reported foods and outcomes.
