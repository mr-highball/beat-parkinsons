Napavichayanun and colleagues, primary indexed report

Original source https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eujim.2024.102396

The 2024 study followed laboratory and nonhuman preparation work with a small clinical study of three mixed cumin and ginger oral formulations in 21 healthy adults. It measured salivary stimulation and safety. This corresponds closely to the preliminary phase of NCT04997889. Clinical swallowing evaluation, culinary spice consumption and Parkinson disease were outside the accessible account.
