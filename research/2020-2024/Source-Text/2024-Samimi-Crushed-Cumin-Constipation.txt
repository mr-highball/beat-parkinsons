Crushed cumin within a randomized digestive symptom trial
https://brieflands.com/journals/jjnpp/articles/148163
selected original methods and findings paraphrase

First published September 22, 2024. The trial randomized 67 adults with functional dyspepsia and 55 completed. The active sachets used coarsely ground common cumin, anise, fennel, ajwain and sugar. The oat-and-sugar comparator included 10% of the active mix by weight. Adjusted GSRS constipation scores were 4.74 versus 7.98 at week one (p = .049), and 4.77 versus 7.43 at week two (p = .152). The narrow comparison was one of multiple symptom outcomes. It helps establish a ground-spice context without isolating cumin, and participants did not have diagnosed Parkinson disease. The paper gives IRCT20220304054179N1 as an author-reported registration.
