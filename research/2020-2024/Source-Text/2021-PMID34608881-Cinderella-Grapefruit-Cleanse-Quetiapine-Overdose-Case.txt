Title: Grapefruit Juice Cleanse Mimicking Quetiapine Overdose: Case Report and Review of Literature.
PMID: 34608881
DOI: 10.1097/JCP.0000000000001469
First published: 2021
Authors: Cinderella MA, Morell B, Munjal S
Journal: Journal of clinical psychopharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34608881/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Published clinical case report and literature review (Journal of Clinical Psychopharmacology 2021) documenting a grapefruit juice cleanse precipitating toxicity mimicking quetiapine overdose via intestinal CYP3A4 inhibition. Highly pertinent to Parkinson disease because quetiapine is widely prescribed for PD psychosis and overdose causes marked sedation/somnolence and alpha-1-mediated orthostatic hypotension.
