Computational saffron target and docking preprint
Source: https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-143357/v1
DOI: 10.21203/rs.3.rs-143357/v1
Coverage: Original full preprint HTML, Methods, Results and Conclusion; posted January 13, 2021

Gong and colleagues used network pharmacology and molecular docking to explore possible saffron constituent and Parkinson-related target relationships. The full Methods, Results, Discussion and Conclusion were accessible in the Research Square version 1 page. Targets and docking models are computational predictions. The work includes no participant intervention, culinary preparation comparison, or measured depressive symptom outcome.
