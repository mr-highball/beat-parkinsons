Preliminary report of mood during hypobaric exposure
Publication: October 2024 issue, Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise 56(10S), p. 1066
DOI: 10.1249/01.mss.0001061788.15259.cc
Registration: Same study family as NCT04111263
Source: https://doi.org/10.1249/01.mss.0001061788.15259.cc
Coverage: Indexed original conference abstract and publisher-deposited Crossref metadata; exact first public day unverified

The conference abstract describes 13 healthy young adults in the crossover with low-altitude placebo, high-altitude placebo and a high-altitude mixture that included cranberry. Its preliminary interpretation suggested the mixture could attenuate depression scores at high altitude, based on comparisons involving low-altitude placebo. The final 2025 paper reports no detected difference in POMS depression between the two high-altitude conditions.
