COMBAT cranberry phenolic metabolite analysis
Published online: 2023-06-28
Authors: Nicole Tosi and colleagues
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2023.113187
PMID: 37689939
Registration: NCT03679533; ISRCTN76069316
Source: https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/92547/7/FOODRES_Final_ms.pdf
Supplement: https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0963996923007329-mmc1.docx
Coverage: Published report, supplementary tables and figure legends

COMBAT randomized 60 healthy adults aged 50–80, 29 to cranberry powder and 31 to placebo. Two 4.5 g sachets per day supplied the assigned powder for 12 weeks. The analysis quantified up to 13 plasma metabolites and 67 urinary metabolites and characterized three phenolic metabotypes. Supplementary Tables S1–S3 describe product composition, the metabolite assay and urine metabolites before and after dosing. Figures S1–S2 describe metabolite models. These reports supply no baseline PHQ-9 comparison by arm or randomized depression result. This is the original COMBAT participant cohort, also reported by Flanagan.
