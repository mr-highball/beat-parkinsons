Title: Central crocin and thermal response in sleep-deprived rats
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7502193/
PMID: 32963719
PMCID: PMC7502193
DOI: 10.32598/bcn.11.2.144.3
Publication: 2020-05-01
Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article, Results and Figure 2.

The investigators randomized rats into five groups of seven, including a sleep-deprived control and two centrally delivered isolated crocin groups. The hot-plate outcome was withdrawal latency, a measure related to experimentally induced pain. Figure 2 supplies mean latency 4.2 in sleep-deprived controls and 7.60 and 8.14 in the two crocin groups, with each crocin contrast reported p<.0001 versus sleep-deprived controls. The intervention was an isolated compound delivered centrally and the context was sleep deprivation. The dependent variable was pain response. The study did not measure insomnia severity, depressive symptoms or daily fall frequency, and it did not study consumed ground saffron in Parkinson disease.
