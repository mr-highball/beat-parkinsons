Commercial watermelon juice and resting pressure in a pilot crossover
Source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-nutrition-society/article/impact-of-watermelon-juice-on-blood-pressure-in-young-healthy-adults-a-pilot-randomised-controlled-crossover-trial/48A18A288E2DC14D06B82B32A7E2B7EE
DOI: 10.1017/S0029665123000393
Coverage: Original conference report published March 8 2023

Hussein and colleagues randomized 10 healthy adults in a crossover with commercial watermelon juice and water. At two hours the reported difference in systolic pressure change was −13.1 mmHg (95% CI −24.09 to −2.11, p=.02). The treatment-by-time test gave p=.91. The two estimates describe different comparisons. The report did not test raw watermelon, pressure upon standing or orthostatic lightheadedness. A distinct 21-person watermelon juice crossover, linked to NCT03626168, did not find a clear longer-term ambulatory pressure difference.
