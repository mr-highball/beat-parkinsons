Chavanelle V et al. Effects of Totum-63 on glucose homeostasis and postprandial glycemia: a translational study. Am J Physiol Endocrinol Metab. Published May 3, 2021.
PMID 33938234. PMCID PMC8285600. DOI 10.1152/ajpendo.00629.2020.
Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8285600/

The initial human arm followed 14 overweight men without a concurrent control. The product combines olive leaf, bilberry, artichoke, chrysanthellum and black pepper extracts. The clinical outcomes address glucose metabolism. Headache and low back pain are named among adverse events. The original says none was considered serious and related to the supplement, with no event counts by category or pain-severity comparisons. There was no Parkinson sample, no cracked black pepper assignment and no component-only contrast.
