The review discusses rosemary extracts or their compounds in mechanistic models, including proposed antidepressant activity; clinical migraine and depression examples concern lavender. It cites the original Kennedy crossover (PMID 20937617), which involved Spanish sage essential oil and acute cognitive-task fatigue in healthy adults. The cited Wightman study used Greek mountain tea. Some orange oil was used with other scents in cognition studies. Its cinnamon references concern essential-oil cytotoxicity or background. These are distinct exposures and outcomes.
