Title: Ingestion of an ample amount of meat substitute based on a lysine-enriched, plant-based protein blend stimulates postprandial muscle protein synthesis to a similar extent as an isonitrogenous amount of chicken in healthy, young men.
PMID: 34881688
DOI: 10.1017/S0007114521004906
First published: 2022
Authors: Kouw IWK, Pinckaers PJM, Le Bourgot C, van Kranenburg JMX, Zorenc AH, de Groot LCPGM et al.
Journal: Br J Nutr
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34881688/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized double-blind parallel trial (Kouw et al., 2022, Am J Clin Nutr, n=24 healthy young adults) comparing postprandial plasma amino acid kinetics after 40 g protein from a lysine-enriched wheat-and-chickpea meat substitute versus 40 g chicken breast fillet. Postprandial plasma leucine and total essential amino acid (EAA) increments were significantly lower after the wheat-chickpea blend than after chicken (p<0.001). Contextual amino-acid kinetic contrast only; blends wheat with chickpea and does not test canned chickpeas or Parkinson levodopa absorption.
