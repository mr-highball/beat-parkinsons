Title: Severe cholinergic terminal loss in newly diagnosed dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://academic.oup.com/brain/article/146/9/3690/7190553
PMID: 37279796
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awad192
Publication date: 2023-06-05
Source depth: original publisher methods and results sections including clinical ratings and group contrast; local research digest

The imaging report compares 25 newly diagnosed dementia with Lewy bodies participants, 15 Parkinson participants and 15 healthy controls. The visual hallucination interview is used to record presence or absence. In the dementia with Lewy bodies group, voxel-wise analyses do not find a clear relationship between tracer uptake and hallucination presence. This is the same study discussed in the commentary at PMC10473551. It has no dried fig exposure or food-timed clinical episode-rate measure.
