Title: A biphasic response to blueberry supplementation on depressive symptoms in emerging adults: a double-blind randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 38300292
PMCID: PMC11139700
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-023-03311-9
Registry ID: NCT04647019
First published: 2024
Authors: Velichkov M, Bezur Z, van Reekum CM, Williams CM
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11139700/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (NCT04647019) in n=60 emerging adults (mean age 20.0 y) with moderate-to-severe depressive symptoms evaluating acute (2 h) and 6-week daily wild blueberry drink vs matched placebo. Acutely at 2 h, blueberry improved PANAS-X positive affect (p=0.026) and task-switching accuracy (p=0.025). However, after 6 weeks, blueberry was significantly inferior to placebo on primary BDI-II depression (5.8 pts worse; 95% CI 0.8-10.7, p=0.023), PHQ-9 (p=0.040), GAD-7 anxiety (7.5 vs 5.4, p=0.026), and SHAPS anhedonia (p=0.029); chronic executive function (p=0.99) and PANAS-X fatigue (acute p=0.41, chronic p=0.76) were null. Limits: young non-PD sample; freeze-dried wild blueberry powder.
