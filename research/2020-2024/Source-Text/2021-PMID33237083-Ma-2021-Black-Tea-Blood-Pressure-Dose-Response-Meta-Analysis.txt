Title: The effect of black tea supplementation on blood pressure: a systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 33237083
DOI: 10.1039/d0fo02122a
First published: 2021
Authors: Ma C, Zheng X, Yang Y, Bu P
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33237083/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis of 13 RCTs (22 arms) of black tea supplementation in adults: pooled SBP fell by -1.04 mmHg (95% CI -2.05 to -0.03, p=0.04) and DBP by -0.59 mmHg (95% CI -1.05 to -0.13, p=0.01), especially >7 days. Context-only synthesis of systemic BP without orthostatic lightheadedness outcomes.
