Curcumin dose in lesioned rats and motor performance
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10360947/
selected original methods and results paraphrase

First published in June 2023. The animal study used >98% isolated curcumin by gavage. Figure 1 reports improvements in fixed- and accelerating-speed rotarod and open-field distance after the higher exposure compared with lesioned controls. It tests a concentrated isolate and motor tasks. It does not measure culinary ground turmeric or separately rated clinical bradykinesia.
