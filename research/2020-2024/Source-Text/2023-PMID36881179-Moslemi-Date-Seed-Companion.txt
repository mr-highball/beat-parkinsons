Title: Effectiveness of supplementation with date seed (Phoenix dactylifera) on inflammatory markers, muscle damage, and BDNF following high-intensity interval training
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36881179/
PMID: 36881179
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-023-03125-9
First public: 2023-03-07
Registration: IRCT20150205020965N9
Coverage: Paraphrased original author abstract and publisher record. Full article tables remain open.

Moslemi and colleagues describe the same registered 36-person exercise trial, with date-seed powder and wheat-bran comparator over two weeks. The companion paper reports lower inflammatory and muscle-damage markers across measurements. Supplement-by-time tests include creatine kinase p=.045, LDH p=.040 and IL-6 p=.047. The BDNF comparison was inconclusive (p=.160). These markers are distinct from the pain VAS measured in the 2022 companion. Matching registration, ethics record, dose, ages and intervention schedule establish one trial family.

The 2022 paper supplies the acute exercise recovery pain result. The preparation is ground seed and the population consists of active adults. A whole dried-fruit comparison in diagnosed Parkinson disease remains open. Obtain the full 2023 article to check biomarker tables, uncertainty and analysis denominators.
