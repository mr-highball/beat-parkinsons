P0024 lists Salvia officinalis among several hydroethanolic extracts in Weleda Amara. Excised-tissue findings concern the combined product. No isolated culinary sage and patient outcome were studied.

P0198 reports dietary-fiber assays with raw or heated oats, broccoli, onion and potato. It includes several preparations of these foods and reports composition rather than symptom measures.

P0771 reports coffee and green-tea intake in a 157-person case-control study of fatty-liver outcomes. P0998 surveys use of sunflower and olive oil among students. Neither provides a Parkinson symptom comparison.

P0541 examines a lemon-balm extract mixture in a nonclinical preparation. P0542 concerns a separate mixture. P1009 studies external ginger and Evodia after gastric cancer surgery and reports bowel and pain outcomes. P0010 uses an egg-derived material in a nonclinical model. Each member needs its own scope and comparator.
