Title: 地域在住高齢パーキンソン病患者のサルコペニアと体幹前傾姿勢
English title translation: Sarcopenia and forward trunk posture in community-dwelling older people with Parkinson's disease
Authors: Toshiro Uchi, Kazuki Nakanishi and colleagues, including Harutoshi Sakakima
Source: https://jsnpt.or.jp/storage/files/shares/program22.pdf
Meeting: 22nd Annual Meeting of the Japanese Society of Neurological Physical Therapy, Fukuoka International Congress Center, 28–29 September 2024
Presentation: 28 September 2024, O7-3, printed page 172
Text coverage: Original English factual extract prepared from the Japanese conference abstract

From October 2022 to March 2024 the researchers recruited 97 community-dwelling people with Parkinson's. The analysis retained 58 participants, mean age 74.2 years (SD 5.7) and 51.7% men. The abstract lists age 65 years or younger, prior stroke, MMSE below 24 and prior spinal surgery as exclusions. Assessments took place during the medication ON state.

The study applied Asian Working Group for Sarcopenia 2019 criteria. Thirteen participants had sarcopenia and 45 did not (22.4% prevalence). The sarcopenia group was older, 78.2 versus 73.1 years (p=.007). Reported skeletal muscle index was 5.9 versus 7.0 kg/m² (p=.001) and gait speed was 0.7 ± 0.3 versus 0.9 ± 0.3 m/s (p=.024). Trunk forward posture was measured as horizontal distance from C7 to a wall. Mean distance was 13.3 ± 9.6 versus 9.2 ± 5.4 cm (p=.11). UPDRS II was associated with distance (p=.018). An age- and sex-adjusted logistic analysis reported a sarcopenia odds ratio of 1.117 for trunk distance (95% CI 1.002–1.245, p=.045).

Kagoshima University ethics approval 220137 also appears in the 2025 Sakakima green tea poster and the 2026 Sakakima article. The 2024 gait and posture abstract gives no green tea, fatigue, or olfactory measurement. The shared approval and overlapping recruitment dates link the studies at the project level. Participant overlap remains unverified.
