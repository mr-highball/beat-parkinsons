2022 — Fresh and dried French tarragon volatile profiles

Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lwt.2022.114006
Identifier: 10.1016/j.lwt.2022.114006

Hazarika and colleagues compared freshly harvested leaves of two French tarragon cultivars with multiple drying and freezing methods. Freezing and shade drying retained more of the measured volatile material, and lyophilization shifted the relative compound profile, including a lower estragole proportion. The study distinguishes the analytical exposures hidden by a generic dried-leaf label. Its methods include instrumental taste measurements. It does not study facial expressiveness or speech.
