Injected sesame oil as comparator in a critical illness mobility protocol
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06592144
Registration: NCT06592144
Coverage: Official registry protocol fields

First posted September 19, 2024. The TESTO-TRIAL compares intramuscular testosterone with intramuscular sesame oil in critically ill adults. The primary endpoint counts ventilator-free days and secondary measures include the Timed Up and Go test. There are no posted results in the retrieved registry record. The protocol identifies sesame oil as an injection comparator. It gives no Parkinson cohort, oral oil arm or stated cold-press preparation.
