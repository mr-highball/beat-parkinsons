Design: Systematic review of brain stimulation

The review traces studies of nucleus basalis of Meynert stimulation, including an earlier randomized six-person Parkinson dementia study. Two participants in that report experienced immediate visual symptom improvement. This is a secondary report of the same participants and an electrical stimulation exposure.
