Registered soy lysolecithin trial and placebo description
Source: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000060435
Coverage: Factual summary of the official registry.

UMIN000053685, R000060435, first disclosed February 22, 2024. The official record lists protocol fixation February 17, 2017 and last follow-up August 16, 2017, more than six years before disclosure. It describes healthy adults aged 40–74 without sleep problems, two randomized capsule groups for eight weeks, a primary memory scale and mental-function VAS among secondary endpoints. The public record does not identify the placebo oil or list a separate sleepiness outcome. Its results section marks the study unpublished. The later paper identifies the soy-oil placebo and reports sleep-quality scores.
