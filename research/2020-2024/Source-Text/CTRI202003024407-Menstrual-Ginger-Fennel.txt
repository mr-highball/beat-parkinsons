Registered crossover of ginger and fennel for menstrual pain
Source: https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=CTRI%2F2020%2F03%2F024407
Registration: CTRI/2020/03/024407
Coverage: Official WHO ICTRP copy of March 31, 2020 registration, current fields

The registry plans 378 participants, a randomized crossover with participant, investigator and assessor blinding, and three treatment conditions. It names a Neotea Thirikadum capsule described as Zingiber officinale, fennel and gram-flour placebo. The named study product formulation is not specified beyond this identification. Enrollment is listed from July 1, 2020. The linked 2026 paper reports 384 participants and uses both crossover and separate-group descriptions.
