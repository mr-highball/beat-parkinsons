Title: Delusional Infestation in Parkinson's Disease Secondary to Piribedil Escalation: An Uncommon Case Report
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10917070/
PMID: 38449964
PMCID: PMC10917070
DOI: 10.7759/cureus.53631
Publication date: 2024-02-05
Date basis: Electronic date in PMC
Source depth: Complete PMC HTML case report
Format: Original-source factual extract

The patient reported a crawling sensation and an infestation belief approximately two weeks after piribedil escalation. The case narrative describes showing fibers as alleged evidence and reports no other psychotic complaint. The authors did not separately ascertain a visual hallucination or measure visual episode frequency. The infestation delusion gradually disappeared about two weeks after piribedil withdrawal while the other Parkinson regimen initially remained unchanged. The assessment concerns a temporal drug association in one case, with no dried-fig comparison.
