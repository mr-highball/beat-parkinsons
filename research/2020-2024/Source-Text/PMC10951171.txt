Title: Impact of Istradefylline on Levodopa Dose Escalation in Parkinson's Disease: ISTRA ADJUST PD Study, a Multicenter, Open-Label, Randomized, Parallel-Group Controlled Study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10951171/
PMID: 38227133
PMCID: PMC10951171
DOI: 10.1007/s40120-023-00574-6
Publication date: 2024-01-16
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The 37-week open-label trial compares istradefylline with a strategy without istradefylline and measures progression in levodopa requirements. Motor and nonmotor scores and adverse-event observations belong to those medication strategies. The trial neither assigns figs nor estimates food-timed visual episode frequency.
