Title: Contribution of Katsura-uri (Japan's Heirloom Pickling Melon, Cucumis melo var. conomon) at the Completely Ripe Stage to Diabetes Control
PMID: 32612089
DOI: 10.3177/jnsv.66.261
Publication year: 2020
Citation: Sasaki and colleagues. Journal of Nutritional Science and Vitaminology 66, 261–266.
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32612089/
Coverage: Primary author abstract.

Ten volunteers with diabetes drank ripe Katsura-uri juice and an equivalent weight of muskmelon juice. The reported glucose area under the curve was 16 ± 5 versus 55 ± 17 hour-mg/dL for the pickling-melon and muskmelon preparations, respectively. The abstract reports p<0.05. The study addresses a liquid fruit comparison across distinct cultivars and measures postprandial glucose.
