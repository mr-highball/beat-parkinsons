Factual text from Methods 4.1 and 4.5 and Table 3. Samples were mature Pimenta dioica fruit and leaves from trees in Puebla, Mexico, with voucher FCME 18114. Plant parts were hydrodistilled separately. The methods describe drying recovered oils with sodium sulfate. Fruit drying and grinding are outside this extraction description. The mouse experiment used the isolated fruit and leaf oils and evaluated acute toxicity. Mortality was a recorded endpoint. Visual hallucinations and handwriting were outside the study design. Triglyceride and antimicrobial experiments were additional separate outcomes.
