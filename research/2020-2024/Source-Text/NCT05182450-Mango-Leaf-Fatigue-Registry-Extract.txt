NCT05182450. First public posting January 10, 2022. Official registry https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05182450. Coverage: factual extract of official methods and endpoint fields.

The crossover compares acute mango leaf extract and placebo in healthy adults. Accuracy of attention is the primary outcome. POMS and task-related mental fatigue are among the separate listed secondary outcomes. The Dodd et al. report (PMID 38665302) identifies this registration. The official registry has no tabulated results in the reviewed snapshot.
