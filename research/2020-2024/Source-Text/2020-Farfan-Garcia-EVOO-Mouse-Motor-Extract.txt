Title: Extra-virgin olive oil and motor measures in an MPTP mouse model
DOI: 10.1177/2397847320922939
Authors: Farfán-García et al.
Publication year: 2020
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/2397847320922939
Coverage: original Methods, Results and Discussion. Local factual extract.

The researchers randomized 40 mice in groups of eight. Extra-virgin olive oil, corn oil, levodopa or saline were compared in the toxin model, with an additional healthy control group. The Methods specify intraperitoneal oil administration. Olive oil was associated with longer time on the rotating rod versus the toxin plus saline group on days two through four and more spontaneous movement over that interval. Forelimb grip or hanging measures and tissue staining supplied separate results. The discussion observes acute tremor after toxin exposure. No separately scored rest-tremor contrast, oral oil comparison or clinical participant appears in the experiment. The corn-oil group supplies a separate oil comparator without establishing a dietary effect.
