Title: Effect of Beetroot Juice Supplementation on Muscle Soreness and Performance Recovery after Exercise-Induced Muscle Damage in Female Volleyball Players.
PMID: 37686795
PMCID: PMC10490293
DOI: 10.3390/nu15173763
First published: 2023
Authors: Hemmatinafar M, Zaremoayedi L, Koushkie Jahromi M, Alvarez-Alvarado S, Wong A, Niknam A, et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37686795/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=12 female athletes: beetroot juice significantly lowered visual analogue scale (VAS) muscle soreness ratings during post-exercise recovery vs placebo (p<0.05). Limits: small athletic sample.
