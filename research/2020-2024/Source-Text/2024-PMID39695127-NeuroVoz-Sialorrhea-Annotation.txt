Date basis: electronic publication in original JATS
Source depth: factual extract of corpus design, Table 4 and Figure 7 description

Mendes-Laureano and colleagues recorded speech from 54 patients with Parkinson disease and 58 comparison speakers. Sialorrhea and dysphagia are distinct binary annotations in the patient metadata alongside indicators for vocal tremor and other symptoms. The scheme encodes presence, absence and missing data. These annotations accompany a speech corpus. The study supplies no graded drooling change, salivary flow measurement or dietary comparison.
