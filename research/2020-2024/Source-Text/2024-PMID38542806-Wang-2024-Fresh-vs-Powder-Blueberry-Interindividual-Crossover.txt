Title: Inter-Individual Responses to a Blueberry Intervention across Multiple Endpoints.
PMID: 38542806
PMCID: PMC10975049
DOI: 10.3390/nu16060895
First published: 2024
Authors: Wang Y, Haskell-Ramsay C, Gallegos JL, Lodge JK
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10975049/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

One-week single-blind randomized crossover RCT in n=37 healthy adults comparing 160 g/d whole fresh blueberries, 20 g/d freeze-dried blueberry powder, and cellulose placebo. Observed wide inter-individual variability across vascular (-141% to +525%) and cognitive (-114% to +96%) endpoints with NO consistent response between or within participants for fresh or powdered blueberry. Limits: short 1-week trial in healthy non-PD adults.
