The review's specific sunflower comparisons concern tissue endpoints in rats, including peripheral blood DNA damage (reference 59) and mitochondrial membrane composition and oxidation in nonbrain tissues (reference 216). Its bibliography also links jaw bone loss and liver telomere studies. These are genuine dietary oil exposures but their cited endpoints do not report Parkinson executive function. The broader human neurology material elsewhere in the review does not describe the same exposure comparison.
