The official PMC HTML contains 206 numbered member headings. The EASE LID 2 subgroup, CVT-301 drug safety analysis and pimavanserin chart review report different medication exposures and measures. Separate SCA17 and MPAN cases describe complex or visual hallucinations. The PKAN report names a disorder and gene pathway, without a dietary B5 arm or dried fig comparison.

Inspected member reports

Huntington Disease (HD)-Like Presentation of Spinocerebellar Ataxia 17 (SCA17) in a Patient with 45 CAG Repeats
Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7017830/#mdc312905-sec-0031
Exposure or diagnosis: TBP repeat expansion case with complex comorbidity
Outcome: New complex hallucinations and delusions
Scope: SCA17 diagnostic comparison without original fig or frequency outcome.

Reduced Dyskinesia and OFF Time after switch to ADS-5102
Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7017830/#mdc312905-sec-0113
Exposure or diagnosis: Immediate-release amantadine switched to extended-release amantadine in 24 Parkinson DBS participants
Outcome: 6 of 24 had a general hallucination adverse event as reported by 25%
Scope: Post hoc open-label EASE LID 2 subgroup, NCT02202551. The displayed outcome is participants with adverse events, not repeated visual episodes.

Man Carrying a Diagnosis of Parkinson's Disease with a Prolonged History of Stuttering Speech
Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7017830/#mdc312905-sec-0132
Exposure or diagnosis: Atypical PANK2-associated neurodegeneration case
Outcome: Clinical history and motor diagnosis
Scope: Pantothenate kinase is part of the disorder name. No dietary pantothenate comparison.

Mitochondrial Membrane Protein-Associated Neurodegeneration (MPAN) Caused by a Heterozygous C19orf12 Mutation
Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7017830/#mdc312905-sec-0215
Exposure or diagnosis: C19orf12-associated disease clinical case
Outcome: Visual hallucinations in multi-system disease course
Scope: There is no Parkinson dried fig contrast or exposure-linked episode count.

An Unusual Presentation of a Misidentification Syndrome in a Patient with Lewy Body Dementia
Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7017830/#mdc312905-sec-0259
Exposure or diagnosis: Clinical Lewy body dementia case
Outcome: Misidentification delusion and visual hallucinations
Scope: Outcome descriptions are separate from food and include diagnostic rather than intake comparisons.

CVT-301 Utilization in Clinical Studies
Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7017830/#mdc312905-sec-0344
Exposure or diagnosis: Inhaled levodopa in four trials
Outcome: Hallucination adverse-event percentages and a visual-event discontinuation mention
Scope: Short comparison reports 1.5% versus 1.3% on placebo. The longer 2.2% versus 1.6% comparison uses an observational group. Percentages describe people reporting events, rather than event rates.

Delayed Diagnosis of Patients with Neurodegeneration with Brain Iron Accumulation
Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7017830/#mdc312905-sec-0500
Exposure or diagnosis: Diagnostic referral and disease recognition
Outcome: Diagnostic timing in NBIA
Scope: Pantothenate-kinase terminology also appears in cited drug and disease papers, with no edible B5 contrast.

Chart Review Examining Outcomes in Patients Using Pimavanserin
Original member: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7017830/#mdc312905-sec-0514
Exposure or diagnosis: Six medication-treated clinical cases, four Parkinson and two dementia with Lewy bodies
Outcome: Overall clinician impression of hallucination and delusion severity after three months
Scope: Uncontrolled mixed-diagnosis series, with no separate visual episode rate.

Medication-Induced QTc Prolongation in Parkinson's Disease-Related Psychosis: A Literature Review

Exposure or diagnosis: Literature about antipsychotic medication
Outcome: Medication cardiac safety and QTc
Scope: Related clinical management context, without primary visual episode counts or food.
