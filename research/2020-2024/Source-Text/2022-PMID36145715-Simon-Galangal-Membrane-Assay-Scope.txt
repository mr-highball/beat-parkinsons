Title: Stability Study of Alpinia galanga Constituents and Investigation of Their Membrane Permeability by ChemGPS-NP and the Parallel Artificial Membrane Permeability Assay
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4923/14/9/1967
PMID: 36145715
PMCID: PMC9502528
DOI: 10.3390/pharmaceutics14091967
First published: 2022-09-18
Institutional copy: https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A1702098/FULLTEXT01.pdf
Coverage: Original full-text institutional deposit, Methods and Table 1.

Simon and colleagues used artificial intestinal and blood–brain membrane assays and a chemical comparison model to study compounds isolated from greater galangal, Alpinia galanga. Three stable compounds, p-hydroxybenzaldehyde, trans-p-coumaryl alcohol and p-coumaryl aldehyde, cleared the selected artificial blood–brain permeability cutoff. Galanganol A and B did not meet that cutoff. The authors flag three other apparently permeable compounds because degradation prevents reliable interpretation.

The plant, compounds and data differ from the 2019 Zingiber officinale study by the same team. The simulated-membrane comparisons provide no clinical concentrations, human symptom direction or original ginger permeability values.
