Title: Opicapone Efficacy and Tolerability in Parkinson's Disease Patients Reporting Insufficient Benefit/Failure of Entacapone.
PMID: 33163567
PMCID: PMC7604656
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13094
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7604656/
First publication date: 2020-10-21 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

This clinical audit follows opicapone use in people who previously had insufficient benefit from entacapone or could not tolerate it. The report tabulates treatment-emergent hallucinations and discusses psychosis in the context of dopaminergic medication. It supplies no evidence involving dried fig exposure or a Parkinson episode-rate comparison by diet.
