Reviewed: 2026-11-20

This narrative review concerns antiviral research and contextual traditional uses. Ginger rhizome, basil aerial parts and sweet orange appear in its botanical discussions. It mentions customary anti-arthritis use for ginger and contains no measured pain comparison for grated rhizome. The traditional constipation indication is attached to Phyllanthus niruri, a different taxon. It cannot be transferred to ginger, basil or orange. The review provides no measured Parkinson constipation outcome for any of the three foods.
