Title: The efficacy of Allium ampeloprasum L. in reducing neutrophil recovery time in childhood cancer with febrile neutropenia: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 38023416
PMCID: PMC10658031
First published: 2023
Authors: Bordbar M, Vahidifar M, Zarshenas MM, Haghpanah S, Zareifar S, Shahriari M
Journal: American Journal of Blood Research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38023416/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Pediatric oncology DB-RCT (IRCT2015051615666N2; n=97 febrile neutropenic children) giving 500 mg twice daily lyophilized A. ampeloprasum leaf extract vs placebo x 7 d: shortened neutrophil recovery time (4.02 vs 6.38 d, p < 0.001) with good tolerability. Pediatric hematologic endpoint only; zero neurological, mood, GI, or PD symptoms.
