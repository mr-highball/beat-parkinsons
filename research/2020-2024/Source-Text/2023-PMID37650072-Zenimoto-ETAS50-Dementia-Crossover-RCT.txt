Title: Effect of a Standardized Extract of Asparagus officinalis Stem (ETAS®50) on Cognitive Function, Psychological Symptoms, and Behavior in Patients with Dementia: A Randomized Crossover Trial.
PMID: 37650072
PMCID: PMC10465245
DOI: 10.1155/2023/9960094
First published: 2023
Authors: Zenimoto T, Takahashi M
Journal: Evidence-based complementary and alternative medicine : eCAM
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37650072/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial (Zenimoto & Takahashi, 2023, Evid Based Complement Alternat Med, PMC10465245) in 27 analyzed patients with mild-to-moderate dementia receiving 300 mg/day ETAS50 (Asparagus officinalis stem extract) for 12 weeks and placebo for 12 weeks. Caregiver-rated NPI-Q-a total severity (0.56 +/- 1.72 vs -0.67 +/- 2.34, p=0.045), Agitation (p=0.015), and Dysphoria/Depression (-0.04 +/- 0.04 vs +0.15 +/- 0.09, p=0.045) favored ETAS50, whereas patient self-rated Zung Self-Rating Depression Scale (SDS) was NULL (-0.37 +/- 4.56 vs -2.07 +/- 6.70, p=0.327). All other relevant outcomes were NULL or non-significant: NPI-Q-a Anxiety (p=0.646), Apathy (p=0.058 trend), Hallucinations (p=0.428), Clock Drawing Test (p=0.827), and MMSE (p=0.420). Limits: non-PD dementia cohort, discordant caregiver vs self-rated depression results, standardized stem extract rather than culinary asparagus spears.
