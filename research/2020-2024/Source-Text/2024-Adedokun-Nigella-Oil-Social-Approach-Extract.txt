2024 | Adedokun et al. | Nigella oil and sociability in ampicillin-exposed mice

Source: primary full report, PMID 38379607, DOI 10.1016/j.ibneur.2024.01.008, February 1, 2024.

The five groups include saline, ampicillin only, oil only, concurrent ampicillin and oil, and oil subsequent to ampicillin. Figure 3 reports five observations per group for the sociability index although the initial cohort has 40 animals. The concurrent and subsequent oil groups spent more of the task time near an unfamiliar mouse than the ampicillin-only group. The significance markings give p<.0001 for these comparisons. This is exploratory proximity toward another animal after antibiotic exposure. The paper does not report Parkinson apathy, seed powder, or an effort-based motivation measure.
