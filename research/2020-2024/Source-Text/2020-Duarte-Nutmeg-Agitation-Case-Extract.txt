Agitated behavior in an accidental nutmeg case
January 6, 2020 · PMID 31962059 · DOI 10.20344/amp.12541
https://www.actamedicaportuguesa.com/revista/index.php/amp/article/download/12541/5846/0
Factual extract from first online original report, with April 2021 issue pp. 298–300.

Duarte and colleagues report an adult who described a large nutmeg exposure before disorganized speech, agitation and aimless running. The case resolved after observation and treatment. The product grind is not specified, and the clinical narrative gives no tremor measurement or posture. Agitated running is a different observation from rest tremor. This remains an acute individual safety context.
