Original study scope
This Cochrane review separates conservative, physical and surgical approaches for central neurological bowel dysfunction. Its ongoing-study tables give a gold kiwifruit supplement crossover in Parkinson constipation and a separate Mediterranean-diet intervention that includes cooking and dressing with olive oil. The diet protocol also allows varied fruits and juices without an isolated orange arm.

Continuing evidence gaps
The gold-kiwifruit supplement protocol does not measure the frozen postural-instability outcome. A current registry summary reports early stopping. Whole-diet protocol outcomes cannot isolate olive oil, and ordinary raw orange, dates or coffee are not independently compared for the selected symptoms.
