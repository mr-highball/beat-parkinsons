Title: Delusional Infestation Secondary to the Dopamine Agonist Ropirinole in a Patient With Parkinson's Disease: A Case Report With an Outline of the Biology of Psychosis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7899248/
PMID: 33633909
PMCID: PMC7899248
DOI: 10.7759/cureus.12880
Publication date: 2021-01-23
Date basis: Electronic date in PMC
Source depth: Complete PMC HTML case report
Format: Original-source factual extract. The title retains the article's spelling of ropinirole.

This case describes a person with Parkinson disease reporting both seeing and feeling ants after addition of ropinirole for restless legs. The caregiver stopped the medication, and the source reports that scratching abated by 24 hours with complete resolution by 72 hours. The narrative has no controlled challenge, separately counted visual episodes or dried-fig exposure.
