The review discusses ginger, cumin, turmeric, extra-virgin olive oil, parsley, rosemary and saffron in different nutrition or laboratory contexts. It distinguishes constituents including parsley-derived psoralen, curcumin, and saffron compounds from their culinary sources. Orange juice occurs in an example of food fortification. Orange also appears as a color descriptor. The broad neurological and metabolic summaries give no isolated frozen culinary Parkinson comparison.
