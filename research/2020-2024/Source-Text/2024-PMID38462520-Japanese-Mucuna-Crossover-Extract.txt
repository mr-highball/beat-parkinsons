Title: Japanese Mucuna pruriens (Hasshou Beans) Showed Fast-acting and Long-lasting Effects in Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11557210/
Publisher: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/internalmedicine/63/20/63_3171-23/_article
First published online: 2024-03-11
PMID: 38462520
PMCID: PMC11557210
DOI: 10.2169/internalmedicine.3171-23
Text coverage: factual extraction of original PMC article, Methods, Results and Tables 3–4.

Seven people with Parkinson disease received a Japanese roasted and ground Mucuna seed preparation and levodopa/carbidopa on separate days. The treatments contained different measured quantities of levodopa. The plant preparation produced longer mean ON duration, 356.4 versus 162.1 minutes (p=.03). Time to ON was 40.0 versus 53.6 minutes (p=.30). Best UPDRS III totals favored the plant preparation, median 2 versus 8 (p=.02), while total-score change from baseline was similar (p=.90). Those are distinct comparisons. The report lists a Mann–Whitney U test for this crossover table. The plant preparation increased measured levodopa exposure. The study did not report a separate resting-tremor comparison. It supplies short-term aggregate motor, dyskinesia and pharmacokinetic evidence from a small sample.
