Title: Chronic almond nut snacking alleviates perceived muscle soreness following downhill running but does not improve indices of cardiometabolic health in mildly overweight, middle-aged, adults.
PMID: 38260074
PMCID: PMC10800814
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2023.1298868
First published: 2023
Authors: Siegel, Rooney, Marjoram, Mason, Bowles
Journal: Frontiers in nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38260074/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

8-week randomized crossover trial (King's College London, ethics ID 21319; distinct site/cohort from San Diego Rayo 2024) in 25 mildly overweight middle-aged adults (35.1 +/- 4.7 y, 11M/14F) consuming 57 g/d whole shelled unskinned raw almonds vs 86 g/d unsalted pretzels prior to a 30-min downhill treadmill run. Cumulative 72-h AUC muscle soreness during vertical jump was reduced by ~24% with almonds vs pretzels (p < 0.05) alongside better vertical-jump maintenance (p < 0.05), while CK and isokinetic torque did not differ (p > 0.05). Pre-post 8-week POMS-2 mood states (Table 4) and VAS well-being (Table 5) showed no significant time x trial interactions: Total Mood Disturbance p = 0.635, Tension-Anxiety p = 0.325, Depression-Dejection p = 0.456, Fatigue-Inertia p = 0.198, Vigor-Activity p = 0.135, VAS Anxiety p = 0.642, VAS Alertness p = 0.511. Limits: acute exercise soreness, raw almonds, non-PD cohort.
