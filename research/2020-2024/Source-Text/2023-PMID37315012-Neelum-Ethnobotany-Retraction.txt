The editors withdrew PMID 34324561 after identifying concerns about authorship, competing interests and peer review across a series of submissions. The notice records an author disagreement. Citation and botanical identity can be tracked for provenance. Reported uses require independent corroboration and carry no evidence weight for the experiment.
