Title: Sensitivity of the Communicative Participation Item Bank for Measuring Patient-Reported Outcomes After Treatment of Unilateral Vocal Fold Immobility.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8446893/
PMID: 34529046
PMCID: PMC8446893
DOI: 10.1001/jamaoto.2021.2358
Publication date: 2021-11-01
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official PMC complete article HTML.

The clinical study evaluates voice treatment in 25 analyzed participants with unilateral vocal fold immobility. One person refers to ordering coffee while illustrating improved communication and mentions tiring in longer conversations. No observed coffee consumption or Parkinson fatigue comparison was reported.
