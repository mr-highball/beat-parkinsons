Golden kiwi single-arm duration discrepancy
https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnsv/66/Supplement/66_S356/_article

Published in a 2020 supplement with the J-STAGE public record released February 22, 2021. Twenty-two young adults consumed two golden kiwifruit daily without a parallel control. The abstract and results describe six weeks, while the Methods on S356 describe four weeks. Outcomes include anthropometry, inflammation, lipids and blood pressure. The comparison gives no standalone effect estimate and does not measure attention.
