Title: Two-year longitudinal follow-up of visual illusions and hallucinations in Parkinson's disease

Source depth: complete conference abstract body, original MDS member 1436; local research digest

The Movement Disorders congress archive identifies this abstract as member 1436, volume 37 supplement 2. Follow-up symptom grouping used the SCOPA-PC, and the VIPD-Q recorded vision complaints. The member gives the same baseline group counts and major symptom transition percentages as the March journal report. It describes concurrent decisions on eye care, antipsychotics and dopaminergic therapies.

The abstract refers to the earlier baseline retina paper and gives the same study design and follow-up pattern as the journal publication. Count the three reports as one baseline cohort and its follow-up, with separate measurement stages. There is no dried fig exposure contrast.
