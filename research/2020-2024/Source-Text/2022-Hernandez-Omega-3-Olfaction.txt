Long-chain omega-3 and olfactory threshold outside Parkinson
https://www.rhinologyjournal.com/Rhinology_issues/manuscript_2958.pdf
selected primary methods and findings paraphrase

First published 2022. In 58 people with postviral olfactory dysfunction, randomized addition of an EPA and DHA supplement to olfactory training gave more improvement on the odor threshold component than training alone. Source marketing and Methods specify long-chain EPA/DHA capsules. They do not establish cold-pressed flaxseed oil or its plant alpha-linolenic acid effect. Postviral olfactory injury and Parkinson hyposmia also differ.
