Registered facial photography endpoints in the topical almond oil trial
Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06571721
Registry ID: NCT06571721
Coverage: factual extract from official arms and outcome modules.

NCT06571721 was first posted August 26, 2024. It registered topical almond oil alone, vitamin E added to almond oil, and tretinoin in a castor-oil formulation. The primary outcome is photographic wrinkle-severity change. Secondary outcomes concern pigmentation, skin hydration, sebum, tolerability and transepidermal water loss. The registered protocol does not identify cold pressing or measure voluntary or spontaneous facial movement. The separate cold-pressed almond oil registration NCT05011461 has two arms and a 12-week photographic outcome.
