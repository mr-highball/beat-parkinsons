Text coverage: Distilled original methods and Figure 2, including a check of the plot image. The university thesis repository embeds the published paper.

Male mice received standard chow or diets containing 40% by weight of high-oleic sunflower oil or palm-kernel oil for eight weeks. Figure 2 reports seven animals per diet and age group, an overall diet term p < 0.05, and a diet-by-age term p = 0.087. Its caption describes impairment specifically for the saturated diet in adolescent mice. The sunflower group bars are close to the standard-diet bars, and the figure gives no sunflower-versus-standard post hoc estimate or significance marker. Preserve this qualification alongside the broader discussion statement that both fats impaired working spatial memory. Related hippocampal electrical and molecular measurements responded differently by fat type.

There is possible lineage overlap with ECO T1P75, including investigators, age grouping, oil formulation and maze task. The saturated comparator is described differently, and exact shared animal identifiers are unavailable. Neither report demonstrates a cold-pressed sunflower or Parkinson executive outcome.
