Title: Dynamic network impairments underlie cognitive fluctuations in Lewy body dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8854384/
PMID: 35177652
PMCID: PMC8854384
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-022-00279-x
Publication date: 2022-02-17
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

This imaging comparison measures changing brain states associated with cognitive fluctuation in Lewy body dementia. The fluctuation and imaging endpoints do not measure intake-associated visual episodes or dried fig exposure.
