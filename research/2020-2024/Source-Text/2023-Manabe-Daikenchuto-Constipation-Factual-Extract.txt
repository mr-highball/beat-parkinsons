Topic: Chronic constipation under the complete Daikenchuto formula
Source: https://doi.org/10.1002/jgh3.12870
Coverage: Primary source facts and outcome boundary
Manabe and colleagues randomized 63 adults with chronic constipation in 2023 to the complete Daikenchuto product or placebo. The primary overall treatment-effect rating favored Daikenchuto (1.7 ± 1.2 vs 1.0 ± 1.2, p=.021). The main paper reports better stool form and some lower gastrointestinal symptom measures. This constitutes evidence for the formula in chronic constipation, with no isolated Zanthoxylum or toasted-pepper arm. It supplements the earlier uncontrolled Parkinson observations.
