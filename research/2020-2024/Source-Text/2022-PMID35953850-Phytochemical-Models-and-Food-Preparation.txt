Original study scope
The review discusses blueberry in animal models, grape skin or resveratrol, isolated olive polyphenols and curcumin. The olive-polyphenol worm study measured movement and alpha-synuclein accumulation, and the blueberry model measured behavioral or molecular outcomes separate from bowel function. Date occurrences are chronological.

Continuing evidence gaps
Exploratory cellular and animal results supply no distinct effects for cold-pressed or extra-virgin olive oil, whole blueberry, raw grapes or ground turmeric on the frozen symptoms.
