Title: Beneficial effects of blueberry supplementation on the components of metabolic syndrome: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
PMID: 35445669
DOI: 10.1039/d1fo03715c
First published: 2022
Authors: Azari H, Morovati A, Pourghassem Gargari B, Sarbakhsh P
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35445669/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 21 blueberry RCTs (>=2 weeks) reporting statistically significant pooled reductions in systolic and diastolic blood pressure alongside improved lipids and insulin in metabolic syndrome populations. Context-only synthesis contrasting with neutral Delpino 2024 and Zhu 2017 meta-analyses; no orthostatic lightheadedness outcomes.
