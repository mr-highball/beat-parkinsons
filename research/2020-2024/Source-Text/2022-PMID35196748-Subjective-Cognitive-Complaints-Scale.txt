Title: Reliability and Validity of the Subjective Cognitive Complaints Questionnaire for Parkinson's Disease (SCCQ-PD).
PMID: 35196748
PMCID: PMC8926760
DOI: 10.3988/jcn.2022.18.2.171
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8926760/
First publication date: 2022-02-14 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

The study validates a subjective cognitive complaints instrument in Parkinson disease. It uses the timing of cognitive impairment or hallucinations in exclusions designed to distinguish Lewy body dementia. The criteria do not supply an episode rate or dietary exposure.
