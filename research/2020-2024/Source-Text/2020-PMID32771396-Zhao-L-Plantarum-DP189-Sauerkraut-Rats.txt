Title: Antidepressant-like effects of Lactobacillus plantarum DP189 in a corticosterone-induced rat model of chronic stress.
PMID: 32771396
DOI: 10.1016/j.bbr.2020.112853
First published: 2020
Authors: Zhao Y, Yang G, Zhao Z, Wang C, Duan C, Gao L
Journal: Behavioural brain research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32771396/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Preclinical corticosterone-stressed rat study administering 1.0x10^9 CFU/day of isolated probiotic strain Lactobacillus plantarum DP189 (originally cultured from Chinese sauerkraut) by gavage for 21 days, reporting reduced sucrose-preference anhedonia, improved spatial memory, and decreased hippocampal neuronal apoptosis. Isolated probiotic monoculture in rats does not isolate whole dietary fermented green cabbage or human PD depression/apathy.
