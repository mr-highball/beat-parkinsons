Title: A combination of Tropaeolum majus herb and Armoracia rusticana root for the treatment of acute bronchitis
PMID: 37167822
DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2023.154838
First published: 2023
Authors: Albrecht U, Stefenelli U, Stange R
Journal: Phytomedicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37167822/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Phase IV double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (n=384 adults: 195 active vs 189 placebo) of fixed 2-herb Angocin (Tropaeolum majus herb + Armoracia rusticana root) for acute bronchitis over 10 days. Accelerated Bronchitis Severity Score recovery from day 3 to day 10 including cough, mucus, and infectious bronchitis chest pain. Bundled 2-herb respiratory infection product; cannot isolate grated horseradish or generalize to PD pain/motor symptoms.
