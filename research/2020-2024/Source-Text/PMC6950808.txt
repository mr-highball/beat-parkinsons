Title: Hypomagnesaemia induced recurrent cerebellar ataxia: an interesting case with successful management
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6950808/
PMID: 31934342
PMCID: PMC6950808
DOI: 10.1186/s40673-019-0110-9
Publication date: 2020-01-08
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS article; local research digest

Initial ataxia and vertigo improved during intravenous magnesium replacement. Three months later, nighttime hallucinations of unspecified modality were reported with recurrent motor symptoms and a measured serum magnesium concentration of 0.9 mg/dL. Overall symptoms improved during repeat replacement and proton pump inhibitor cessation. The original does not separately quantify hallucination resolution, frequency, or a Parkinson or dietary fig exposure.
