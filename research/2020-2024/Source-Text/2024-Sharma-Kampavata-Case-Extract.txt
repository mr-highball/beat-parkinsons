Title: Ayurvedic management of Kampvata: selected case-report facts
DOI: 10.21760/jaims.9.6.54
Source: https://jaims.in/jaims/article/download/3515/5321?inline=1
Publication date: 2024-06 issue; exact first online day unverified
Coverage: Original case, management, results and discussion; factual paraphrase

The report describes resting upper-limb tremor and a combined Ayurvedic course. It includes ashwagandharishta, Mucuna powder, other formulations and external procedures. Authors report symptomatic improvement and Hoehn–Yahr stage 3 to 1, without a separate numerical tremor outcome or comparator. Discussion instead calls the ashwagandha product churna. Species/composition, independent Parkinson diagnosis and concomitant conventional medication are not established. Generic ethics text says no human/animal research despite the human case. These reporting limits do not identify an effective or ineffective individual component. Participant identifiers and dosing details are omitted.
