Mixed pea protein and peptide powder in a sarcopenia gait composite
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06499012
Registration: NCT06499012
Coverage: official protocol including arms, outcomes and update history

First posted 2024-07-12. A blinded prototype powder trial enrolled people aged 50–70 with sarcopenia and knee or hip osteoarthritis. The 12-week intervention includes soy isolate and peptides, wheat protein, pea protein and pea peptide, with aloe and excipients in both arms. The Short Physical Performance Battery includes gait speed, chair stand and balance. The registry shows completed status and no posted results as captured 2026-11-16. The study assigns a protein mixture and a composite, without an isolated cooked green-pea or Parkinson gait estimate.
