Original study scope
The investigators assay herbal extracts and products for chemical antioxidant properties. Turmeric is among the assayed extracts. Cinnamon and ginger appear in introductory discussion of other products. The experiment does not compare cinnamon or ginger exposure and patient pain scores.

Continuing evidence gaps
Analytical extract results leave ground cinnamon, grated ginger or ground turmeric effects on Parkinson pain unmeasured.
