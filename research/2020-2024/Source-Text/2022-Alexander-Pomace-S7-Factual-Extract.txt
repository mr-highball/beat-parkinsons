Title: Bowel habits, faecal microbiota and faecal bile acid composition of healthy adults consuming fruit pomace fibres
Source: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114522002951
Supplement: https://static.cambridge.org/content/id/urn:cambridge.org:id:article:S0007114522002951/resource/name/S0007114522002951sup001.docx
First published: September 14, 2022
Coverage: Factual extract from Results and supplementary Table S7.

In the orange crossover, weekly bowel movement frequency had product-by-time p = .46. For at least one day without a bowel movement in a week, Table S7 gives product p = .004 and product-by-time p = .40. The article narrative gives product p = .002. Week 0 counts were 38/89 for orange juice and 32/86 by the reported cells for juice with added pomace, whose heading says n = 84. Week 1 counts were 49/89 versus 33/84; week 4 counts were 33/89 versus 26/84. This product main effect includes periods with a baseline difference and cannot establish differential treatment change. The experiment provides no raw fruit or Parkinson constipation result.
