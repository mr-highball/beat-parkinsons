Title: Movement Disorders and Other Neurologic Impairment Associated With Hypomagnesemia: A Systematic Review
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10547470/
PMID: 37795503
PMCID: PMC10547470

Publication date: 2023-10-02
Source depth: complete original PMC HTML; corresponding JATS contains front matter only; local research digest

The review identifies 60 patients with neurological symptoms during hypomagnesemia. Inclusion required movement or eye movement abnormalities and clinical reversal following correction. The resulting series cannot estimate the probability of improvement without that selection, or a visual hallucination treatment effect. References 41 and 42 describe hallucinations without specifying the sensory modality in the original cases. Reference 4 remains at publisher preview depth. Do not count this synthesis as additional independent case evidence. It contains no dried fig feeding or Parkinson episode-frequency comparison.
