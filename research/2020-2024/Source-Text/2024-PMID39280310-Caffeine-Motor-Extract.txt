Title: Motor improvement in Parkinson's disease patients receiving caffeine adjuvants
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11391974/
PMID: 39280310
PMCID: PMC11391974

First public article date: 2024-07-03

Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated original article and table extract

The randomized study gave isolated caffeine or starch placebo for
21 days with 15 and 12 participants, respectively. The trial restricted
tea and other caffeinated beverages and assessed participants during
their stable symptomatic treatment. The source does not specify a
separate measurement setting by medication state.

The study reports its motor threshold using the total UPDRS Part III
score. Participants exceeding the threshold number 12/15 on caffeine
and 2/12 on placebo (OR 20.00, 95% CI 2.77 to 144.31, p=.004).
The groups differed in baseline motor severity categories (p=.043).
The methods describe amplitude scoring during the resting tremor
item, but the available manuscript and tables give no isolated
rest-tremor estimate. The source calls the instrument MDS-UPDRS
III while describing a score range of 0–108.

The body reports four of 15 caffeine arm adverse-event reporters and
zero of 12 placebo. Table 6 instead uses four of 12 as the event
denominator for caffeine. Reported symptoms include sleep difficulty,
palpitations, frequent urination and anxiety. No tremor-specific
adverse-event count is provided.
