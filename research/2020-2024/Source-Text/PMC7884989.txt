Title: Nucleus Basalis of Meynert Stimulation for Lewy Body Dementia: A Phase I Randomized Clinical Trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7884989/
PMID: 33199437
PMCID: PMC7884989
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0000000000011227
Publication date: 2020-11-16
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML original article; locally distilled

The French phase I pilot assessed 30 people with Lewy body dementia for eligibility and analyzed six who underwent surgery. It compared three months of active with three months of sham stimulation in randomized order. The free recall primary comparison was inconclusive, with a median active-minus-sham difference of -0.5, IQR -3.5 to 3.33, p = 0.80. Broader cognitive and PET findings and postoperative events were reported. The report supplies no separately assessed post-food visual hallucination frequency. The French registry NCT01340001 is distinct from the UK NCT02263937 crossover and the earlier Parkinson dementia crossover.
