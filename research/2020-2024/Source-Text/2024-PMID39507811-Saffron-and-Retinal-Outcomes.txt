Original study scope
The review identifies dried saffron stigma and related compounds and discusses oral supplementation in age-related macular degeneration. Its study contrasts principally assess retinal or visual function and do not provide a Parkinson mood cohort or measured fatigue or fall-frequency outcomes.

Continuing evidence gaps
Saffron gives a genuine exposure lead. Retina outcomes and theoretical neuroprotection do not determine Parkinson depression, fatigue or falls.
