Title: Safety and Efficacy of Using Nuts to Improve Bowel Health in Hemodialysis Patients.
PMID: 32001127
DOI: 10.1053/j.jrn.2019.10.002
First published: 2020
Authors: Lambert, Bird, Borst, Fuller, Wang
Journal: Journal of renal nutrition : the official journal of the Council on Renal Nutrition of the National Kidney Foundation
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32001127/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

10-week nonrandomized within-subject repeated-measures pragmatic clinical trial in 20 hemodialysis patients (median age 67 y, 51% male) consuming 40 g/d raw almonds for 4 weeks, followed by 2-week washout and 4-week control period. Primary efficacy outcome constipation (Bristol Stool Form Scale and POS-S renal symptom score) showed statistically significant improvements at weeks 2, 3, 4, and 10. Quality of life (P = 0.030) and overall symptom burden (P = 0.002) also improved, with no significant change in predialysis potassium (P = 0.21) or phosphate (P = 0.16). Limits: nonrandomized sequential design, small n=20 hemodialysis cohort, abstract omits exact constipation mean scores and p-values.
