Title: Anti-obesity effects of Beta vulgaris and Eruca sativa-based extracts.
PMID: 39120436
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.8291
First published: 2024
Authors: Markou M, Katsouda A, Papaioannou V, Argyropoulou A, Vanioti M, Tamvakopoulos C, et al.
Journal: Phytotherapy research : PTR
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39120436/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical metabolic study (Markou et al., 2024, Phytother Res) feeding wild-type high-fat-diet mice extracts of Beta vulgaris and Eruca sativa for 8 weeks. Extracts attenuated weight gain, adipose mass, and hepatic steatosis, but failed to improve glucose tolerance and had NO effect on blood pressure. Limits: metabolic obesity mouse study using vegetable extracts; null BP outcome and no neurological or Parkinson's symptom measures.
