# Recurrent fainting reported after chili ingestion

Source https://redcien.com/index.php/redcien/article/download/40/39
Coverage original case and journal posting metadata

Villagrán Ruíz de la Peña and colleagues report a 50-year-old woman who briefly fainted while seated after eating mole poblano and did not fall to the floor. The nearest ambulatory reading was 95/60 mmHg with pulse 62, versus 100/58 and 78 at the previous reading. The half-hour cuff schedule may miss the nadir, and the measured diastolic readings differ from the prose’s stated decrease. The case reports no episodes during six months of avoiding chili and three subsequent syncopal episodes after resumption. The journal assigns the issue to 2019 and lists public posting November 29, 2021. The food preparation does not confirm ground cayenne, and the case gives no Parkinson fall frequency.
