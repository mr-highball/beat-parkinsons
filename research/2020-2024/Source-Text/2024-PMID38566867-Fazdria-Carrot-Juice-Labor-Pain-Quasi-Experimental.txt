Quasi-experimental study in primigravida stage-1 labor pain (dark chocolate n=30, carrot juice n=30, control n=30) claiming carrot juice lowered Numeric Pain Rating Scale scores. Full-text PMC10983098 inspection revealed severe internal reporting contradictions rendering directional results unusable: text erroneously states '90 individuals with T2DM were randomized to dark chocolate treatments' in a labor study, and contradicts follow-up timing across sections (30 hours in Abstract, 60 minutes in Results, 30 minutes in Methods, and 8-week intervention in Discussion).
