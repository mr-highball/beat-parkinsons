Title: Fig leaf extract in acute and prolonged stress behavior in rats
PMID: 38741409
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38741409/
Original journal PDF: https://pjps.pk/uploads/2024/01/1705318177.pdf
First publication: 2024-01 journal issue.
Coverage: Original journal body and figures.

Khaliq and colleagues compared standard conditions, unpredictable stress and stress with Ficus carica leaf extract. Acute and longer-term comparisons cover different results. Figure 6 and the Results report inconclusive acute immobility, while Figure 7 favors longer novel-object exploration relative to untreated acute stress (p<.01). Figure 8 reports longer light-box time in the chronic stress comparison (p<.05 versus untreated chronic stress). Figure 11 has a time-to-platform axis and an inconsistent ambulatory caption. The author discussion also reports increased general activity, which can influence several tasks. The extract comes from leaves and gives no culinary dried fruit, Parkinson or visual hallucination comparison.
