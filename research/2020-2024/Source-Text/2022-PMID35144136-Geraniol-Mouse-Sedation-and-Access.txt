Original source scope
The mouse study compared isolated geraniol given through nonculinary routes with control conditions. It reports reduced locomotion, altered EEG activity and facilitated general anesthesia. The laboratory results implicate inhibitory activity in a thalamic region involved in arousal. The publisher's accessible sections distinguish the locomotor observations from the anesthesia experiments.

Evidence boundary and access
The accessible original abstract and excerpts do not give a complete verified set of effect estimates for the final publication. The results concern pharmacologic sedation and anesthetic response, with no Parkinson population, culinary lemongrass exposure, clinical insomnia measure or naturally occurring fall rate.
