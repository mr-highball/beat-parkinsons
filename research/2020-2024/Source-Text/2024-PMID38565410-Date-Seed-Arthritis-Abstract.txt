Title: Polar and nonpolar Sewy date-seed extracts in a rat arthritis model
PMID: 38565410
DOI: 10.1016/j.jep.2024.118138
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38565410/
First publication: 2024-03-31 online, July issue.
Coverage: Original experiment abstract. Full outcome tables and route details require the article body.

The investigators examined polar and nonpolar Phoenix dactylifera seed extracts in a rat model of complete Freund's adjuvant-induced arthritis. The abstract reports smaller paw swelling and less inflammatory-cell infiltration and describes comparisons of inflammatory markers and joint histology. It provides no separate measured pain-response result. The abstract describes customary date-seed tea use for arthritis as the rationale; it offers no firsthand patient outcomes under tea. Extracts from the seed, a model of arthritis and inflammatory markers leave the dried fruit and human Parkinson pain comparison open.
