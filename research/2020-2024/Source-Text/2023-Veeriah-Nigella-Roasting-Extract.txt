2023 | Veeriah et al. | Superheated steam and convection roasting of Nigella sativa

Source: original methods and chemical findings, PMID 37970392, DOI 10.1002/fsn3.3655, published online September 6, 2023.

Results distinguish unheated, steam-roasted and convection-roasted seed material. The study used chemical measurements of selected constituent signals, oil quality and antioxidant assays. The retention of thymoquinone varied with roasting process. Table 4 detected thymoquinone in all studied steam-roasted seed samples, while it was undetected after the longest, highest-temperature convection process tested. The chemical comparison cannot establish constituent amounts in untested culinary toasts or a clinical physiological effect. No Parkinson outcome was measured.
