Apathy ratings in a cannabis preparation carried in olive oil
Source: https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-1672277/v1/da9bbb9b-0a04-4fc3-b9ee-5011ff9da62c.pdf
DOI: 10.21203/rs.3.rs-1672277/v1
Coverage: Original 2022 preprint and 2023 version of record

Palmieri and Vadalà first posted May 26, 2022, and the matching article appeared in January 2023 (PMID 36655645). Both versions describe 30 Alzheimer patients receiving a cannabinoid preparation formulated with olive oil. The journal Methods specify sublingual administration and omit the oil grade. Figure 1 depicts lower caregiver-rated apathy during the uncontrolled treatment course. The analysis reports an unpaired test despite baseline and follow-up observations, and the text says one participant stopped while also saying everyone completed. The report supplies no standalone olive-oil arm or Parkinson patients. The preprint and published report describe the same cohort.
