Title: Locally delivered curcumin hydrogel and thermal withdrawal in rats
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7385839/
PMID: 32782446
PMCID: PMC7385839
DOI: 10.1177/1559325820929555
Publication date: 2020-07-27
Date basis: Electronic publication in the original.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Complete structured original including figure captions.

The paper compares a local curcumin-containing hydrogel with relevant carrier and untreated conditions in an animal neuropathic pain model. Figure 4 tracks thermal withdrawal latency through seven days. The authors report a favorable change in the withdrawal assay with the active formulation. Histology is reported as a separate endpoint. The outcome reflects withdrawal behavior under experimental nerve injury.

The hydrogel review cites this animal comparison as its pain example. It supplies no clinical pain result, no orally consumed ground turmeric and no Parkinson cohort. The direction observed in the assay remains specific to the formulation and model.
