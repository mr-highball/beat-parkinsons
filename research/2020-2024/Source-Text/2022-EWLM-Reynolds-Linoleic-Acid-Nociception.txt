Title: Excessive dietary linoleic acid promotes plasma accumulation of pronociceptive lipid mediators and thermal nociceptive hypersensitivity
Source: https://workshop-lipid.eu/pictures/Abstract%20book%208EWLM.pdf#page=14
Publication date: 2022
Date basis: Eighth European Workshop on Lipid Mediators abstract book, June 29 to July 1, 2022.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Review depth: full_text
Format: Original conference member scope digest.

Reynolds and colleagues compare custom diets rich in linoleic or oleic acid in rats. The standard corn oil was replaced in both arms. The linoleic-acid-rich diet was associated with shorter thermal withdrawal latency and a different pattern of plasma lipid mediators. The sample count and a numerical group estimate for thermal withdrawal are absent in the abstract.

The authors, diet replacement, testing age, thermal endpoint and lipid results overlap with the 2022 Autonomic Nervous System abstract. Treat both presentations as a likely shared experiment. The comparison concerns custom triglyceride mixtures and nociceptive response. It leaves refined corn oil and clinical pain severity in Parkinson disease unassessed.
