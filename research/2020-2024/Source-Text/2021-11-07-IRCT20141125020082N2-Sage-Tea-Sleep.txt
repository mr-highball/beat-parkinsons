Sage tea and sleep quality trial registration
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/59723
Registration: IRCT20141125020082N2
Coverage: Direct official registry, enrollment, design and outcome fields

The prospective record was first registered November 7, 2021 and currently shows recruitment complete with no recorded revisions. It plans 128 students, assigning dormitory rooms to reduce exchange of intervention materials. The registry describes 10 g common sage tea versus 10 g brown-sugar tea. Its primary outcome is PSQI sleep quality, with a value of less than 6 printed in the timepoint field. The record plans recruitment December 2021 to February 2022 and does not give actual dates. The original 2024 report instead describes October to December 2022 and intervention bags with 10 g sage plus 3 g brown sugar versus 13 g brown sugar. Its outcome table gives group means without a responder count for the registry threshold. These discrepancies limit the protocol-to-result interpretation.
