Title: A mini review of polysaccharides from Zanthoxylum bungeanum Maxim
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39486707/
DOI: 10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2024.137007
PMID: 39486707
Publication date: 2024-10-30
Source depth: Original author abstract and publication metadata.
Reviewed: 2026-11-27

The review summarizes extraction, structure and biological findings for polysaccharide fractions from Zanthoxylum bungeanum. The author abstract includes fatigue among proposed applications. Its abstract does not supply the animal group sizes, preparation details for the cited fatigue experiment or the numerical effect on fatigue.

The cited Zhou and Yang 2023 experiment is a separate leaf-polysaccharide study in exercise-fatigued mice. A bilingual independent reference identifies that report in China Food Additives 34(7), pages 234–239. The original results and methods remain unverified. Neither the abstract nor the citation measures ground culinary pericarp and Parkinson fatigue. The two documents are citation lineage, not two independent outcomes.
