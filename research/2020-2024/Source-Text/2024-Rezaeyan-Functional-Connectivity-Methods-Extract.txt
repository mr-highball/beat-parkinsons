Original full Methods and cohort attribution
PMID 39626165, PMC11615909, DOI 10.1177/19714009241303129
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11615909/

First online December 3, 2024. The original Methods identify the same participants as the earlier structural MRI report, PMID 33957160. The 25 analyzed patients had post-traumatic anosmia, with nine untrained, nine in fixed training and seven in the modified regimen. The Methods list thyme in fixed training and in the first month of the modified regimen, cardamom in the second month, and saffron and cinnamon in the third. These were sniffed odor mixtures and sequences. The study compares regimens and does not provide an individual odor's effect, a culinary exposure or a Parkinson cohort. It cites IRCT2017102034225N1, a record that lists a different two-arm exposure plan.

The 2024 Results give a follow-up composite smell score difference for fixed training versus control (p=.023) and an odor identification difference for modified training versus control (p=.044). The modified arm has seven participants in Methods and Table 1 and six in the olfactory outcome table. Those estimates apply to whole training regimens.
