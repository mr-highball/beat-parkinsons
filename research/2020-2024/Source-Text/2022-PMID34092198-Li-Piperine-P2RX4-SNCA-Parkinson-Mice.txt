Title: Piperine promotes autophagy flux by P2RX4 activation in SNCA/α-synuclein-induced Parkinson disease model.
PMID: 34092198
PMCID: PMC9037522
DOI: 10.1080/15548627.2021.1937897
First published: 2022
Authors: Li R, Lu Y, Zhang Q, Liu W, Yang R, Jiao J
Journal: Autophagy
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34092198/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled transgenic PD mouse study (PMC9037522; Thy1-SNCA mice overexpressing human alpha-synuclein). Oral piperine (25, 50, 100 mg/kg/d x 6 weeks; 100 mg/kg/d through 12 months for motor progression) promoted P2RX4-mediated autophagosome-lysosome fusion, degraded pathological olfactory-bulb alpha-synuclein, attenuated buried-food olfactory deficits, prolonged rotarod latency, and significantly shortened pole-test descent time (explicitly defined in Methods as "used to evaluate the degree of bradykinesia"). Limits: isolated high-dose oral piperine in transgenic mice, not dietary cracked black pepper in human PD.
