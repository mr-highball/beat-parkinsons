Title: Two-year longitudinal follow-up of visual illusions and hallucinations in Parkinson's disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35296961/
PMID: 35296961
DOI: 10.1007/s00415-022-11074-2
Publication date: 2022-03-16
Source depth: original author abstract and journal metadata; local research digest; complete article remains open

Researchers contacted the earlier 82-person Parkinson cohort two years after baseline. Telephone interviews reclassified visual illusions and hallucinations and asked about visual complaints, eye treatment, antipsychotic use and dopaminergic changes. In the baseline hallucination group, the journal abstract reports 42% no longer in either visual symptom group, 32% with hallucinations alone, 21% with both and 5% with illusions alone. For baseline illusions, it reports 43% with neither, 48% with illusions alone and 9% with both. These are author-reported percentages within the analyzed groups. The baseline total is the contact pool.

The study reports symptom-state transitions and potential co-treatment. It does not supply visual episodes per time unit following dried fig intake. The congress report describes the same follow-up study. Publisher: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00415-022-11074-2
