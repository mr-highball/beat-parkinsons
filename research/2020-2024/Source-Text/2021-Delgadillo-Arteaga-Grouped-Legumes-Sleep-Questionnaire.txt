Grouped legume preference and sleep quality in students
https://www.renhyd.org/renhyd/article/download/1361/873/8500

November 17, 2021. Delgadillo-Arteaga and colleagues surveyed Mexican university students during COVID-19 restrictions. Table 2 gives an odds ratio of 1.59, 95% CI 1.22–2.08, for the beans, chickpeas and lentils preference item. Footnote a defines the item as dislike. A separate meal item pooling rice, pasta and beans gives 1.07, 95% CI 0.87–1.32. The available responses do not isolate pinto intake, boiling, an insomnia severity effect or a Parkinson group.
