The 439-page 18th EuGMS collection contains P-728, an older patient's reported attempt to use banana peel on a finger lesion before a squamous cell carcinoma diagnosis. A separate taste study tested serving coffee, tea or malted drinks with milk or supplements in older people referred for poor oral intake. A nursing-home poster discusses cranberry products, anticoagulant interactions and the limits of infection prevention evidence. P-862 reviews ginger and weight or fat outcomes. These selected members each involve a different question. None measures an assigned Parkinson symptom with its frozen preparation.
