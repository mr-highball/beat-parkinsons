Source: Kousha M, et al. Simultaneous treatment with cells and rosemary extract ameliorates 6-OHDA-induced toxicity in the hippocampus of mice.

Identifiers: PMID:38903548 | PMCID:PMC11186544 | DOI:10.4103/JAPTR.JAPTR_319_23
Source URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11186544/

Study design and findings
- Bilateral 6-OHDA lesion model (note title states mice while methods state 5 groups of male Wistar rats; species reporting discrepancy noted): lesion control, sham, oral rosemary extract (50 mg/kg from 2 weeks pre-lesion to 8 weeks post-lesion), adipose stem-cell transplant (CE), and combined CE + rosemary extract (CE+R).
- Combined CE+R and CE alone shortened spatial-memory escape latency and increased target-quadrant time at 4 and 8 weeks versus lesion controls, while CE+R upregulated hippocampal NGF, BDNF, NT3, NT4, GPx, and SOD and lowered MDA and GFAP-positive cells.

Relevance and limits for Parkinson's food experiments
- Indirect preclinical rosemary-extract hippocampal-memory context in a 6-OHDA model.
- Limits: Title-versus-methods species mismatch (mice vs Wistar rats); primary cognitive improvement reported for stem-cell + rosemary combination rather than isolated culinary rosemary; no human Parkinson symptom outcomes.
