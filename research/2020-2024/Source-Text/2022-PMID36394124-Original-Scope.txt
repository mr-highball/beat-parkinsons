Title: Safety and effectiveness of cannabinoids to Danish patients with treatment refractory chronic pain-A retrospective observational real-world study.
PMID: 36394124
PMCID: PMC10107230
DOI: 10.1002/ejp.2054
Publication year: 2022
First public date basis: NLM and original journal record.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10107230/
Coverage: Complete article-body context reviewed 2026-11-22.

This uncontrolled retrospective cannabinoid prescribing study screened 826 eligible chronic pain patients. It reported at least 30% pain improvement for 17% and 10% of the eligible cohort at the two follow-ups using the authors' intention-to-treat estimates. Attrition was substantial. It did not test dried dates or a dried-date comparison in Parkinson disease.
