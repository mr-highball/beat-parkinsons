Title: The Impact of Coconut Oil and Epigallocatechin Gallate on the Levels of IL-6, Anxiety and Disability in Multiple Sclerosis Patients.
PMID: 31979305
PMCID: PMC7070654
DOI: 10.3390/nu12020305
Registry ID: NCT03740295
First published: 2020
Authors: Platero JL, Cuerda-Ballester M, Ibáñez V, Sancho D, Lopez-Rodríguez MM, Drehmer E, Ortí JER
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7070654/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Pilot randomized controlled trial (Platero et al., 2020, Nutrients, PMC7070654; NCT03740295 cohort, same 51 MS participants as Platero 2021 PMID:33920655 and Cuerda-Ballester 2023 PMID:36594273) assigning 51 adults with multiple sclerosis on an isocaloric Mediterranean diet to 60 mL/day coconut oil PLUS 800 mg/day epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG) vs placebo for 4 months. State anxiety (STAI) and functional disability (EDSS) decreased in the combined intervention group while IL-6 fell in both groups. Limits: unusable attribution for isolated coconut oil because coconut oil was bundled with high-dose 800 mg/day EGCG and a Mediterranean diet in non-PD multiple sclerosis patients.
