Title: Evaluation of olive oil effects on human stress response by measuring cerebral blood flow
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/fsn3.2099
PMID: 33841804
PMCID: PMC8020963
DOI: 10.1002/fsn3.2099
Publication date: 2021-02-16
Text coverage: Distilled original methods, results, Tables 2 and 3, and data availability.
Accessed: 2026-11-26

Seventeen healthy students underwent typing tasks after bread alone or bread with one of three extra-virgin olive oils. Frontal oxyhemoglobin was lower with oil at one and two hours in the reported comparison. The text reports no comparable immediate or occipital change. Researchers interpreted the imaging signal as a possible stress response. The study does not give a validated anxiety symptom estimate, Parkinson population or cold-pressing specification. Manufacturer-affiliated investigators participated. The paper reports no declared conflicts, and the individual data were not shared.
