Title: Accuracy of Cardiac Innervation Scintigraphy for Mild Cognitive Impairment With Lewy Bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8205462/
PMID: 33883238
PMCID: PMC8205462
DOI: 10.1212/WNL.0000000000012060
Publication date: 2021-04-21
Date basis: electronic appearance in PubMed history, ahead of original JATS print issue
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The prospective study compares cardiac MIBG in 95 people with mild cognitive impairment. Scans are abnormal in 22 of 37 probable and two of 15 possible Lewy body cases, and normal in 38 of 43 Alzheimer-type cases. The group sizes and probable-Lewy and Alzheimer-type scan counts match EANM 2020 abstract OP-592, marking a single diagnostic cohort. Visual hallucinations are a diagnostic feature. Food intake and episode-rate change are not measured.
