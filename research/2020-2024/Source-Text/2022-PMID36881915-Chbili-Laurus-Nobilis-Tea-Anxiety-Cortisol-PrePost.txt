Title: Evaluation of daily Laurus nobilis tea consumption on anxiety and stress biomarkers in healthy volunteers.
PMID: 36881915
DOI: 10.12871/000398292022343
First published: 2022
Authors: Chbili C, Maoua M, Zaouali M, Selmi M, Kacem I, Mrizek N, et al.
Journal: Archives italiennes de biologie
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36881915/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Uncontrolled 10-day pre-post study (Chbili et al., 2022, Arch Ital Biol) in 30 healthy Tunisian volunteers (aged 20-57 y) drinking once-daily strained infusion of 5 g dried Laurus nobilis leaves in 100 mL boiled water. Day 11 plasma cortisol fell from 93.5 +/- 43.01 to 72.23 +/- 25.37 ng/mL (p=0.001), with significant pre-post reductions in PSS (p=0.006) and STAI anxiety (p=0.002). Limits: uncontrolled single-arm before-after design from the same Sousse research group and identical 30-person 5 g/100 mL x 10-day active regimen as Chbili et al. 2026 (DOI:10.1038/s41598-026-73555-y), so cohort independence is uncertain (do not double-count as an independent family); note PubMed DOI metadata currently resolves to an unrelated article.
