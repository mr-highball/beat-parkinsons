Original study scope
The original Rush Memory and Aging Project analysis included 682 older adults after clinical parkinsonism, treated Parkinson disease and missing covariate exclusions. It relates dietary carotenoid and vitamin estimates to a four-domain global parkinsonian score rather than an oil-specific comparison. A separate cited Parkinson trial (PMID 28342967) assigned flaxseed-derived omega-3 plus vitamin E, so keep that combined product separate from this cohort and from cold-pressed flaxseed oil alone.

Continuing evidence gaps
Read the separate registered oil plus vitamin E experiment and maintain separate exposure attribution. The observational data cannot answer an isolated flaxseed-oil rigidity or bradykinesia question.
