Source: https://www.przeglepidemiol.pzh.gov.pl/pdf-181025-101586?filename=Comparison+of+the+effect.pdf
Coverage: Original methods and Tables IV–V, factual extract.

The randomized study allocated 120 burn inpatients to oral ginger capsules, starch placebo or a prayer group. Ginger capsule species and manufacturing method were not specified. The Spielberger anxiety between-arm differences (ginger minus placebo) were +1.22 at admission, −0.89 on day 3 and +1.06 on day 6, with p = .761, .933 and .910. The report provides no group-by-time interaction for anxiety. The placebo frequency wording is unclear. The study does not give a Parkinson comparison or verify grated ginger ingestion.
