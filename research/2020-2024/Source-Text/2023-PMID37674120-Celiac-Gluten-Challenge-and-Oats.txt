The article reports 815 celiac disease patients' presentation histories and 74 treated patients undergoing a three-day gluten challenge. The challenge used wheat, barley, rye or a mixture. Of the 74 challenged patients, 14 reported vomiting or nausea. The comparison of habitual gluten-free oats in Table 2 was 64% in the symptomatic group and 92% among those without these symptoms (p=0.017). Oat use was observed before the challenge and could reflect differences in tolerance or avoidance. The study did not randomly assign oat use. The exposure, outcome and population differ from the frozen Parkinson questions about falls, insomnia, muscle rigidity and anxiety.
