AlBaazi and Hassoun. Spontaneous remission of motor neuron disease, proposed relation to clove oil. Journal of Medical Case Reports and Case Series 3(2). Published 2022 Feb 21.
DOI 10.38207/JMCRCS/2022/FEB03020211.
Original author report: https://www.acquaintpublications.com/get/1-JMCRCS2022012401%20Galley_Proof-1645446988.pdf
Scope: Factual narrative of the publicly readable original clinical case.

A 60-year-old man with diabetes reported three drinks containing concentrated clove oil. He experienced abdominal pain and diarrhea after the third drink. Approximately two weeks later, he developed limb weakness, fasciculations, dysarthria and dysphagia. The examination described nasal speech and tongue fasciculations. The authors initially described a progressive muscular atrophy-like syndrome, followed by clinical and electrophysiologic recovery about two weeks after presentation. They proposed toxicity based on the timing, but the oil and its eugenol content were not measured and the single case cannot establish cause. The exposure was concentrated oil. The report does not document ground clove, Parkinson disease or a listener-based intelligibility test.
