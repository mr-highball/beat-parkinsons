Title: Effect of Curcumin on Severity of Functional Dyspepsia: a Triple Blinded Clinical Trial

The trial included 75 participants with Rome III functional dyspepsia.
It compared adjunctive curcumin in 39 participants against placebo in
36 participants. Both groups received famotidine. The investigators
used the Hong Kong dyspepsia questionnaire at baseline, after one
month of treatment and after one additional month of follow-up.

The primary abstract reports no statistically significant difference
between the groups in dyspepsia severity. Severity improved within the
curcumin group. A within-group comparison does not isolate the effect
of curcumin. No basil arm, ordinary turmeric preparation, idiopathic
Parkinson cohort, swallowing-specific measure or constipation outcome
is reported in the primary abstract.

Citation trail: Reference 29 of the Thai-Mediterranean viewpoint,
PMID 37334427. The viewpoint mentions basil and turmeric together,
but the cited study directly tests only the curcumin add-on.
