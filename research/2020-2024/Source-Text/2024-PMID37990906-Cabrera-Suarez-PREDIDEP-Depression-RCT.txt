Title: Mediterranean diet-based intervention to improve depressive symptoms: analysis of the PREDIDEP randomized trial.
PMID: 37990906
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2023.2283290
First published: 2024
Authors: Cabrera-Suárez B, Hernández-Fleta JL, Molero P, Lahortiga-Ramos F, Sayon-Orea C, Sánchez-Villegas A
Journal: Nutritional Neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37990906/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Two-year multicenter single-blind secondary-prevention RCT (PREDIDEP, NCT03081065; n = 196 recovered MDD patients: 103 Mediterranean diet enriched with EVOO vs 93 usual-care control). Primary outcome (depression recurrence risk via Cox regression) showed NO significant between-group difference. Secondary Beck Depression Inventory (BDI) subsyndromal scores improved within MD+EVOO at 4 mo (-2.15, 95% CI -4.00 to -0.29) and 8 mo (-2.42, -4.17 to -0.67) and favored MD+EVOO between groups at 20 mo (-3.35, -6.08 to -0.61). Companion HRQoL paper (Cabrera-Suárez 2023, Exp Gerontol, PMID:36933773) reported higher 2-y SF-36 Vitality (+9.00, p = 0.020) and borderline Mental Health (+6.79, p = 0.055) with null MCS (p = 0.462). Whole-diet bundled intervention; cannot isolate EVOO alone.
