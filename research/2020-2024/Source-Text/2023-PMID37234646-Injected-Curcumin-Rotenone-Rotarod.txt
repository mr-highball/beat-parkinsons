Original study scope
Rotenone-exposed mice received intraperitoneal purified curcumin or DMSO. The treated mice traveled farther in an open field and remained longer on the rotarod. Figure 1 uses separate locomotion and performance-time outcomes. Sunflower oil is reported as the rotenone control vehicle.

Continuing evidence gaps
Time to fall from a rotating rod differs from daily fall frequency, and open-field distance does not test passive rigidity. Sunflower oil is a vehicle, not the randomized culinary treatment for rigidity. No ginger exposure is tested.
