Mixed corn and canola oil control during bed rest
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05679596
Coverage: Factual summary of official registry fields.

First posted January 11, 2023. The older-adult bed-rest protocol compares ketone monoester with an energy-matched control containing corn oil, canola oil and fructose. Sleep quality and disturbances are secondary measures. The completed record has no structured results. The comparator combines oils, their processing is unreported and the protocol does not isolate corn oil or study Parkinson insomnia.
