Title: Effect of green banana and pineapple fibre powder consumption on host gut microbiome.
PMID: 39246394
PMCID: PMC11378528
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2024.1437645
First published: 2024
Authors: Chong CW, Liew MS, Ooi W, Jamil H, Lim A, Hooi SL
Journal: Frontiers in nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39246394/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

14-day controlled human study (PMC11378528) in n=60 adults (n=20 green banana powder [GBP] 10.75 g/d supplying 5 g fiber, n=20 pineapple fiber powder 7.41 g/d, n=20 placebo 10.75 g/d). GBP improved bowel movement regularity and normalized stools (79% normal stool vs 65% control at day 7; Bristol types 3-4 by day 14). On exploratory sleep items, participants reporting "fairly bad" sleep disruptions fell from 20% to 11% at day 7 in the GBP group, without significant change in average sleep quality or duration. Limits: green banana powder rather than whole raw/baked banana, short 14-day follow-up, healthy non-PD adults.
