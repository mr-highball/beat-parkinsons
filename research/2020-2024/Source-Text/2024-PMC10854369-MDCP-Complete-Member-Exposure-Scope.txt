Coverage: Original PMC HTML proceedings provide 204 separately numbered abstracts, ending at number 225. The member numbers are nonconsecutive. All displayed members were screened as separate records.

Abstract 96 reports dietary caffeine from 24 sources. Of the enrolled sample, 67 completed the questionnaire. Total weekly caffeine intake was similar between Parkinson and control respondents (p=.62). The abstract proposes microbiome analyses and reports no brewed-coffee fatigue effect. Abstract 154 reports a separate Machado-Joseph disease survey with coffee, tea and mate in a combined caffeine measure. Its outcome is age at onset, with an inconclusive comparison across caffeine groups (p=.40).

Abstract 143 includes a distinct corn-oil group in a five-arm mouse experiment on zinc-oxide-associated toxicity. It reports endocrine, inflammatory and histology outcomes. There is no refined corn-oil effect estimate for a mapped human Parkinson symptom.

Date refers to elapsed time, follow-up or another calendar field. Fig refers to an illustration, Plum and Millet to author names, Chard to a cited author, and Turkey to a study location. The original clinical supplement contains separate reports for sialorrhea, fatigue, psychiatric outcomes and diet. Their outcome findings cannot be assigned to an ingredient from another abstract.
