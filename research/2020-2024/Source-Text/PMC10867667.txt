Title: The impact of psychiatric comorbidity on Parkinson's disease outcomes: a systematic review and meta-analysis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10867667/
PMID: 38361749
PMCID: PMC10867667
DOI: 10.1016/j.lanepe.2024.100870
Publication date: 2024-02-09
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The review includes 55 reports and assesses relationships between psychiatric comorbidity and later Parkinson outcomes. Psychosis definitions and prognostic outcomes are broader than recurrent visual hallucinations. None of its pooled comparisons tests eating dried figs.
