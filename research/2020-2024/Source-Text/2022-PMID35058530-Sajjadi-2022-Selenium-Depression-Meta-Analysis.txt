Title: The role of selenium in depression: a systematic review and meta-analysis of human observational and interventional studies.
PMID: 35058530
PMCID: PMC8776795
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-022-05078-1
First published: 2022
Authors: Sajjadi SS, Foshati S, Haddadian-Khouzani S, Rouhani MH
Journal: Scientific reports
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35058530/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of selenium supplementation trials for depressive symptoms reporting a small pooled reduction (WMD -0.37) driven largely by small, postpartum, or deficiency-prone cohorts and contradicted by the large older-adult RCT Rayman 2006. Context review; no Brazil nut trials included.
