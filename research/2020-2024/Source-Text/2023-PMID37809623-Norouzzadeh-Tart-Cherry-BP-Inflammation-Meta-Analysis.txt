Title: Dose-dependent effect of tart cherry on blood pressure and selected inflammation biomarkers: A GRADE-assessed systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 37809623
PMCID: PMC10559679
DOI: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2023.e19987
First published: 2023
Authors: Norouzzadeh M, Hasan Rashedi M, Shahinfar H, Rahideh ST
Journal: Heliyon
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10559679/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

GRADE-assessed systematic review and meta-analysis (Norouzzadeh et al., 2023, Heliyon, PMC10559679) of 21 RCTs evaluating tart cherry (Prunus cerasus) on blood pressure, heart rate, and inflammatory markers. Pooled effects on resting systolic/diastolic blood pressure, heart rate, hs-CRP, and IL-6 were statistically non-significant (p>0.05), consistent with Eslami et al. 2022 (PMID:35108661, 7 RCTs n=201) and Moosavian et al. 2022 (PMID:36038032, 10 RCTs); tart cherry modestly reduced serum CRP (WMD -0.39 mg/L, p=0.024) and TNF-alpha (-0.14 pg/mL, p=0.026). Context-only synthesis; resting BP is distinct from orthostatic hypotension or standing lightheadedness.
