Title: Psychosis in neurodegenerative disease: differential patterns of hallucination and delusion symptoms
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8041322/
PMID: 33501939
PMCID: PMC8041322
DOI: 10.1093/brain/awaa413
Publication year: 2021
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete official PMC HTML.

The retrospective 372-case autopsy cohort compares hallucination and delusion characteristics across confirmed neuropathologies. Presence, content and distress are distinct charted features. The cohort does not relate an identified food exposure to episodes.

The 372 records include 111 Alzheimer disease, 59 Lewy body disease with Alzheimer disease, and several neuropathologically specified frontotemporal disease groups. The study compares recorded characteristics across diagnoses.
