Commercial kefir and planned myeloma fatigue measures
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04530812
Registration: NCT04530812
Coverage: Official registration, arms, enrollment and named outcome tools

First posted August 28, 2020. Roswell Park randomized 13 multiple myeloma participants to daily commercial kefir or usual diet for three months. The detailed plan names patient-reported fatigue and the EORTC QLQ-C30 and myeloma module. There were no posted registry results at the November 16, 2026 capture. Exact NCT, sponsor and intervention searches found secondary mentions without a verifiable primary results paper. No specified plain product or Parkinson population.
