Registered onion capsule mass and extract amount
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05666752
Registration: NCT05666752
Coverage: Official registration first posted December 28, 2022; compared with 2024 original Methods

The registration specifies four capsules per day and labels their total 1,200 mg/day in both groups. The published Methods give 249.9 mg onion peel extract powder, 49.2 mg cellulose and 0.9 mg silica per active capsule. Those components total 300 mg per capsule. Four capsules contain 999.6 mg of extract powder, which explains the reported 1,000 mg/day active extract. The instrument assessed upper respiratory symptoms, including self-rated hoarseness, without a speech intelligibility measurement. The registered preparation was heated-water peel extract.
