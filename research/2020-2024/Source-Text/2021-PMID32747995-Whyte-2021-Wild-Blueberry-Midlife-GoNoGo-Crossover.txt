Title: Improved metabolic function and cognitive performance in middle-aged adults following a single dose of wild blueberry.
PMID: 32747995
PMCID: PMC7987603
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-020-02336-8
First published: 2021
Authors: Whyte AR, Rahman S, Bell L, Edirisinghe I, Krikorian R, Williams CM, et al.
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7987603/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover trial in n=35 cognitively healthy middle-aged adults (40-65 y) consuming a 1-cup equivalent wild blueberry drink vs placebo with breakfast. Wild blueberry improved executive inhibition on Go/No-Go (fewer errors on cognitively demanding invalid No-Go trials and faster reaction times at 4 h and 8 h), maintained AVLT word recognition, and attenuated 2-h postprandial glucose/insulin. Limits: acute 8-h test in non-PD adults; powder drink.
