Psychotherapy waiting period registry and relaxation endpoint
Source: https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00028118
Registration: DRKS00028118
Coverage: Official DRKS registration, August 9, 2022, and current version

The retrospective registration lists 67 adults with depression, randomly assigned to daily placebo or standard waiting over three weeks. Actual study dates March 14 to June 30, 2022 precede registration. BDI-II is the primary measure. Weekly ratings of energy, sleep quality, concentration, well-being and relaxation are the listed secondary measurements. The registry calls the intervention placebo; the linked article identifies oral sunflower oil and a positive suggestion.
