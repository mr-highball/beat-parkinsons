Title: The effect of Origanum majorana tea on motor and non-motor symptoms in patients with idiopathic Parkinson's disease: A randomized controlled pilot study
Source: https://www.prd-journal.com/article/S1353-8020%2821%2900307-2/abstract
PMID: 34455218
DOI: 10.1016/j.parkreldis.2021.08.013
First posted online: 2021-08-24 in the journal issue record. PubMed gives 2021-08-25.
Coverage: Factual extract of the primary abstract and publisher Methods and Results preview.

Sixty people with idiopathic Parkinson disease were randomized for 30 days. Twenty-eight marjoram and 23 placebo participants completed follow-up. The authors analyzed completers, following two withdrawals in the marjoram arm and seven in the placebo arm. The exposure was a filtered infusion of dried Origanum majorana leaves.
The abstract reports lower total Non-Motor Symptoms Scale and Beck Depression Inventory scores within the tea arm (p < .0001 for each). The motor examination changed from 18.76 to 16.52 (p=.069), which the authors did not interpret as a clear motor improvement. Anxiety appears among examples in the introduction. A separate anxiety score, item contrast and complete tables were unavailable.
The publisher Methods preview lists CEFMS 29/2019. The 2025 biomarker report gives CEFMS 23/2019 and cites the 2021 paper. Its later timing and different measurements do not establish individual-participant overlap.
