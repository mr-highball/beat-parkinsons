Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article, ethnomedical account and intervention tables.

The clinical section cites active and placebo comparisons of saffron preparations in major depression. The source mixes extract, dried material and different floral parts across examples. The separately checked active trial methods and outcomes resolve the preparation and dosing for those comparisons. Lab examples include effects of extracts and compounds on animal behaviors. There is no original ground-saffron study for a separately measured clinical Parkinson outcome in this review.

The ethnomedical section describes historical Iranian use of a saffron-water preparation for insomnia, and separate practices for pain and other symptoms. The 2011 source it cites identifies the insomnia practice as external use. The same paragraph series mentions olive oil as a component of a topical gynecological preparation. It gives no olive-oil anxiety or insomnia comparison, sesame-oil anxiety, pain or depression comparison, or dried-date fruit pain comparison.
