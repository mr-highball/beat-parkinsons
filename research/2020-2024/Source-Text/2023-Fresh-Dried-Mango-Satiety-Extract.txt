Title: Effects of fresh vs dried mango consumption on satiety and postprandial glucose in healthy adults
Authors: Candice Stamper, Sama Safadi, Andrew Gehr, Pia Asuncion, Mee Young Hong
Primary paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10374964/
Registration: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03956602
Identifiers: PMID 37520177, PMCID PMC10374964, DOI 10.1016/j.metop.2023.100253, NCT03956602
Date: 2023-07-18 online
Coverage: factual extract of the primary Methods and outcomes, prepared 2026-11-14

In 34 healthy adults, fresh mango, dried mango and white bread were offered as separate 100 kcal snacks in a crossover comparison. The Methods name Tommy Atkins, Kent or Keitt for the prepackaged fresh fruit. The authors describe a randomized crossover in the abstract, although their Methods explicitly put white bread first. The test examined satiety ratings and capillary glucose for 90 minutes after intake. It measured neither insomnia nor fatigue severity and had no Parkinson cohort. The 2022 meeting abstract described the same comparison. The NLM article's CC BY-NC-ND license fell outside the research archive's full-text rule, so the local record is a factual extract.
