Public KIM protocol and olive oil guidance
Source: https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-2626068/v1/e5c22279-420b-4e96-bb0e-16e36abd4ca6.pdf?c=1678485571
DOI: 10.21203/rs.3.rs-2626068/v1
Registration: NCT05469997
Coverage: author protocol selection; posting page and dietary intervention methods

The March 6, 2023 author protocol gives the dietary guidance planned for the KIM Parkinson crossover. The ketogenic Mediterranean phase encouraged olive oil alongside multiple plant foods and offered dietitian-prepared meal plans. It gives no olive-oil processing grade, dose or supply commitment. The protocol states that branded medium-chain triglyceride oil would be supplied in the supplemented phase and specifies titration. These are plans from the same registered cohort as the later outcomes. Individual olive intake cannot be measured from the plan.
