Title: Resting tremor history and a deep brain stimulation localization case
PMID: 37458360
PMCID: PMC10555643
DOI: 10.3171/CASE23100
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10555643/
First publication: 2023-07-10
Coverage: Original NCBI PMC JATS article body checked and summarized.

The primary case describes longstanding Parkinson disease with an initial asymmetric resting tremor and a UPDRS part III motor score of 46. The report uses intraoperative motor activation and imaging to explain why stimulation findings could mislocalize a lead. The illustrations show electrode position and stimulation responses. No fig intervention or independently scored post-fig rest-tremor outcome appears in the original clinical case.
