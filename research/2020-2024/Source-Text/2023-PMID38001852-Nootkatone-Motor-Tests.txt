Title: Astrocytic Nrf2 Mediates the Neuroprotective and Anti-Inflammatory Effects of Nootkatone in an MPTP-Induced Parkinson's Disease Mouse Model
PMID: 38001852
PMCID: PMC10669233
DOI: 10.3390/antiox12111999
Publication date: 2023-11-13
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10669233/
Coverage: Full paper, including the motor-test methods and reported intervention responses.

An isolated grapefruit-derived compound was injected before toxin exposure in a mouse model. The motor outcomes were time on the rotarod and time to descend a pole. The authors also assessed dopaminergic tissue, inflammation and oxidative markers. The compound-related motor differences are described in these task outcomes. The design supplies no raw grapefruit contrast or separate resting-tremor assessment.
