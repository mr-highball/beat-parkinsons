Coverage: factual extract of the mango and three sleep items, verified against both HTML responses fetched 2026-11-15 UTC.

Among 403 university students, the unadjusted Pearson correlation for mango frequency (variable 20) with semester weekend sleep duration (14) was −0.057; with holiday weekend sleep duration (15), −0.094; with the sleep-time composite (16), −0.085. The authors mark the latter two at nominal one-tailed p < .05. Each number appears in the mango row and the reciprocal matrix cell. The preprint PDF truncates the table at the right edge. Results use self-reported durations and fruit frequency, with no clinical insomnia scale, preparation-specific comparison or Parkinson group.
