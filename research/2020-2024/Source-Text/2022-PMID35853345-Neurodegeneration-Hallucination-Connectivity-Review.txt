Title: A systematic review on resting state functional connectivity in patients with neurodegenerative disease and hallucinations
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9421441/
PMID: 35853345
PMCID: PMC9421441
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2022.103112
Publication year: 2022
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The review synthesizes resting-state connectivity studies in neurodegenerative conditions with hallucinations. Eleven eligible articles contribute imaging comparisons with heterogeneous symptoms. It does not identify a fig exposure or causal dietary outcome.

The paper notes differences in clinical populations, hallucination instruments and regional connectivity approaches. The eleven articles do not constitute a dietary synthesis.
