NCT04299217. First public posting March 6, 2020. Official registry https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04299217. Coverage: selected factual endpoint and study linkage fields from the official API.

The registered study examines acute mango leaf extract against placebo and includes POMS among the listed mood outcomes, with stress and behavioral measures. It is the trial identified by Wightman et al., PMID 32717999. The primary paper reports no clear POMS or cognitive-battery mental fatigue change. No tabulated results are posted in the registry reviewed here.
