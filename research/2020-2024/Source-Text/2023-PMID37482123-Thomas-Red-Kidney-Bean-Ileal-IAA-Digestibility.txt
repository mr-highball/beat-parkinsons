Title: Determination of Indispensable Amino Acid Digestibility of the Red Kidney Bean in Humans Using a Dual Stable Isotope Tracer Method.
PMID: 37482123
DOI: 10.1016/j.tjnut.2023.07.008
Registry ID: NCT04118517
First published: 2023
Authors: Thomas DJ, Riley C, Devi S, Kurpad AV, Preston T, Francis R, Chambers B, Brown O
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37482123/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract Facts: Human dual stable isotope tracer study (n=10 healthy adults; 5M, 5F) feeding intrinsically 2H-labeled cooked red kidney beans (Phaseolus vulgaris) in a Jamaican meal alongside 13C-spirulina standard. Mean true ileal indispensable amino acid (IAA) digestibility was 79.4 +/- 0.5% (range 69.0% threonine to 85.7% lysine; extrapolated DIAAS 0.63). Confirms substantially lower and slower ileal amino acid absorption from cooked red kidney beans compared with animal proteins (>90-95%), explaining why direct PD OFF-time worsening cannot be assumed without PD clinical testing.
