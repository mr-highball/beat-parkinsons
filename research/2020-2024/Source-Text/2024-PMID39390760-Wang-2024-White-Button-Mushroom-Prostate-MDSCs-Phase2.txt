Title: Reduction of myeloid-derived suppressor cells in prostate cancer murine models and patients following white button mushroom treatment.
PMID: 39390760
PMCID: PMC11467013
DOI: 10.1002/ctm2.70048
Registry ID: NCT04519879
First published: 2024
Authors: Wang X, Ma S, Twardowski P, Lau C, Chan YS, Wong K
Journal: Clinical and translational medicine
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11467013/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Bidirectional mouse + human translational study (from suspended Phase II trial NCT04519879) showing 3 months of white button mushroom tablets reduced circulating PMN-MDSCs and increased CD8+ T/NK cells in prostate cancer patients. Measured immunologic/PSA markers only; no urinary urgency or LUTS outcomes.
