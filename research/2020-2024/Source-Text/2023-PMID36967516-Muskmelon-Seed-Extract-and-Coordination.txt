Title: Neuropharmacological evaluation of different species of Curcubitaceae seeds extract in experimental animals

Publication date: 2023-01
Citation: Sayyar, Afroz and Khan. Pakistan Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences 36(1), 223–229.

Author copy: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/369184550_Neuropharmacological_evaluation_of_different_species_of_Curcubitaceae_seeds_extract_in_experimental_animals
Coverage: Author abstract and full report including Tables 1 and 5.

The study compared ethanol seed extracts from Cucumis melo var. flexuosus, a netted muskmelon identified as var. reticulatus, and Santa Claus melon under a 60-day oral regimen. The abstract reports changes in exploratory activity, describes motor coordination as unaffected on the rotating rod, and describes depressed general behavior at the highest doses. Table 1 lists spontaneous activity, alertness, awareness, responses, reflexes and grip strength. Table 5 shows time on the rod, although the Methods describe falls. At 30 minutes the reported netted muskmelon extract values at the lowest and highest doses were 362.32 ± 1.56 and 284.34 ± 3.12 seconds against 335.14 ± 1.23 seconds in control, with neither extract entry marked by a significance symbol. The table and procedure leave measurement and analysis details uncertain. These seed extracts and behavioral assays leave the raw-flesh resting-tremor question open.
