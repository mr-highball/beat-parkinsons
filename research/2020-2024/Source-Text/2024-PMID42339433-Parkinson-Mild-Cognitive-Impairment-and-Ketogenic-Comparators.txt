Original source scope
The review discusses cognitive symptoms and reports several ketogenic preparations used in mild cognitive impairment, including one diet with coconut oil. Separate paragraphs address Parkinson apathy, falls and sleep in other intervention contexts.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The coconut oil finding does not isolate refined oil or establish clinical Parkinson executive function, instability, falls, sleepiness, apathy or dream enactment effects. The review provides no dried date fruit result.
