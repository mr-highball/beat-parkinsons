Title: Black Soybean and Adzuki Bean Extracts Lower Blood Pressure by Modulating the Renin-Angiotensin System in Spontaneously Hypertensive Rats.
PMID: 34359440
PMCID: PMC8305714
DOI: 10.3390/foods10071571
First published: 2021
Authors: Jeong EW, Park SY, Yang YS, Baek YJ, Yun DM, Kim HJ, et al.
Journal: Foods (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34359440/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical rat study (Jeong et al., 2021, Foods, PMC8305714) testing black soybean and adzuki bean (Vigna angularis) extracts (250 and 500 mg/kg/day) in spontaneously hypertensive rats (SHR). Adzuki extract lowered resting systolic blood pressure by 17-19% and diastolic blood pressure by 11-13% via renin-angiotensin-aldosterone modulation. Limits: resting hypertension rodent model using concentrated extract; does not evaluate orthostatic blood pressure drops or Parkinson's orthostatic lightheadedness.
