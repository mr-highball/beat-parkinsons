Title: Berger et al. — orange juice nanovesicles and intestine outcomes in diet-induced obesity
PMID: 32953937
PMCID: PMC7481887
DOI: 10.1016/j.omtm.2020.08.009
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7481887/
First published: 2020-08-14, issue collection 2020-09-11.
Coverage: Journal methods, comparative intestinal results and Figure 4. ISEV2020 member PF01.04 reviewed separately.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18 UTC

The investigators compared orange juice-derived nanovesicles with controls in cell experiments and in mice exposed to a high-fat, high-sucrose diet. Treated mice received the separated vesicle fraction. The journal reports changes in jejunal villus size, lipid storage and gene expression. Cecum weight and total intestine and colon length did not show clear changes after the vesicle treatment.

An ISEV2020 abstract by overlapping investigators identifies the same isolated preparation and gut model. Treat the publications as likely companion reports while sample overlap remains unverified. The intervention differs from eating raw orange, and the study does not give a clinical Parkinson symptom outcome.
