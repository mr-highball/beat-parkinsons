Original study scope
The original mouse study tests a 22-component commercial paste in aluminum-chloride neurotoxicity. Ingredients include cinnamon, greater cardamom and clary sage. Several anxiety- and depression-like assays moved in a favorable direction for the blend; the tests also depend on activity.

Continuing evidence gaps
Cinnamon is genuine mixed-exposure context and neither its anxiety contribution nor apathy was isolated. Greater cardamom and clary sage must first match the frozen culinary botanical identity. Neither is treated as the corresponding ordinary culinary spice or fresh sage.
