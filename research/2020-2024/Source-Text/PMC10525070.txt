Title: Continuous Subcutaneous Infusion Delivery of Apomorphine in Parkinson's Disease: A Systematic Review

Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML publication; local research digest

The systematic review separates off-time trials, selected patients with existing hallucinations, and observational worsening reports. A TOLEDO extension follows the same trial family. The therapy is subcutaneous medication and the review has no dried fig comparison.

The registry NCT02702076 has a separate neuropsychiatric primary endpoint and is different from the TOLEDO trial NCT02006121. The PubMed main trial report PMID30055903 and the later open-label phase share the TOLEDO family.
