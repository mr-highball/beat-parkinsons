Ambon bananas and hospital anxiety scores
https://medicopublication.com/index.php/ijphrd/article/download/10253/9595/19335
selected methods and findings paraphrase

Published July 30, 2020. Sixty female schizophrenia inpatients with anxiety were assigned to five groups of 12. Mean HARS from baseline to day 14 was 36.33 to 27.08 for controls, 33.17 to 20.17 and 33.92 to 19.25 for new inpatients receiving two or three bananas daily, and 33.08 to 18.83 and 34.33 to 18.67 for previously treated inpatients receiving two or three. The post-treatment comparisons favor banana arms, with Table 3 reporting p < .001. Randomization is unclear. Antipsychotic history defines groups, and baseline HARS means differ. Preparation details and transfer to Parkinson disease are uncertain.
