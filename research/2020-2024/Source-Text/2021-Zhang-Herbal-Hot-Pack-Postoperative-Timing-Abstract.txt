Title: Timing of topical herbal heat after gastrointestinal anastomosis
Original title: 中药热奄包辰时热熨法对胃肠吻合术后患者胃肠功能恢复的影响
Source: https://www.hunanzyzz.com/ch/reader/view_abstract.aspx?file_no=202106039
Date basis: Published in the 2021 June issue of Hunan Journal of Traditional Chinese Medicine.
Coverage: Factual extract of the original journal abstract. Pack composition and actual recruitment intervals await full-text confirmation.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

This randomized postoperative comparison describes 150 patients in three equal groups. The two active groups received a topical herbal hot pack at different times of day, and the control group received usual postoperative care. The abstract reports earlier first postoperative stool in both active arms, with the selected-time arm showing the larger improvement. Pain and bloating outcomes also favored the selected-time condition. The abstract gives no exact stool timing means.

The lead author and hospital match the 2022 ginger and Evodia paper. The sample and timing comparison differ, and the publicly posted abstract does not specify the pack ingredients. It cannot independently confirm the effect of ginger or the participant lineage.
