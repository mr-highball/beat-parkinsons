Title: Latin American cohort comparison of apathy and other neuropsychiatric symptoms
PMID: 37427840
PMCID: PMC10776811
DOI: 10.1002/alz.13384
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10776811/
First publication: 2023-07-10
Coverage: Original NCBI PMC JATS article body checked and summarized.

The 10/66 cross-sectional analysis included 11,768 older adults. The diagnosis groups comprised 704 with parkinsonism alone, 229 with parkinsonism and dementia, 844 with dementia alone and 9,991 controls. An informant answered separate Neuropsychiatric Inventory Questionnaire items for apathy, depression and other symptoms. The analyses compared symptom prevalence and burden by diagnosis and allowed for demographic and clinical covariates. Apathy and hallucinations were more prevalent in the parkinsonism with dementia group than in dementia alone. This is a diagnostic and caregiver-observation comparison without a recorded dried-date exposure.
