2022 | Huang and Running | Saliva and a commercial soybean oil emulsion

Source: original full article, DOI 10.1093/chemse/bjac013, PMID 35809054, PMCID PMC9270849.

The oral test combined Crisco vegetable soybean oil with water and color. Participants swished and then expectorated the preparation. The emulsion layer was inspected in photographs. The investigators included 62 participants in the sensory study. They attempted to use the expectorated volume to approximate salivary flow. Almost all recovered less than the administered mixture. They concluded that incomplete recovery prevented estimation of the amount of saliva secreted. Their final analyses concern emulsion stability and perceived food qualities. The study gives no clinical drooling endpoint, oral consumption arm, or oil processing specification.
