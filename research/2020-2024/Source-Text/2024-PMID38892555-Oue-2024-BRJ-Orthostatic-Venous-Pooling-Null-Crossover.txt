Title: Effect of Acute Dietary Nitrate Supplementation on the Changes in Calf Venous Volume during Postural Change and Skeletal Muscle Pump Activity in Healthy Young Adults.
PMID: 38892555
PMCID: PMC11174609
DOI: 10.3390/nu16111621
First published: 2024
Authors: Oue A, Iimura Y, Miyakoshi Y, Ota M
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38892555/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized crossover physiological study in n=15 healthy adults evaluating acute BRJ vs placebo during orthostatic tilt/positional change: beetroot juice did not alter supine-to-upright calf venous pooling or provoke orthostatic intolerance. Limits: healthy young adults without autonomic failure.
