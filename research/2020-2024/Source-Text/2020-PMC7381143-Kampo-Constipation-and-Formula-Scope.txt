Title: Kampo Medicine for Various Aging-Related Symptoms: A Review of Geriatric Syndrome
PMID: 32766269
PMCID: PMC7381143
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2020.00086
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7381143/
Date basis: 2020-07-15 electronic publication, original journal header.
Coverage: Factual extract of the original full review, constipation section, formula table and cited comparisons.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The constipation section distinguishes Daikenchuto after stroke, a responder analysis of that same trial, a different chronic-constipation comparison, and Daiokanzoto. The processed ginger listed in Daikenchuto belongs to a multi-ingredient formulation. Its effect cannot be isolated from ginseng and pepper. The formula table also lists cinnamon bark in other Kampo preparations. Those formulations and the reported outcomes do not establish a separate effect of culinary ginger or cinnamon. Perioperative recovery and chronic symptom outcomes are distinct.
