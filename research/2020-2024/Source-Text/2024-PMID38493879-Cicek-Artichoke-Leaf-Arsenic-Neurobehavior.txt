Title: AuNPs with Cynara scolymus leaf extracts rescue arsenic-induced neurobehavioral deficits and hippocampal tissue toxicity in Balb/c mice through D1R and D2R activation.
PMID: 38493879
DOI: 10.1016/j.etap.2024.104417
First published: 2024
Authors: Cicek, Hacimuftuoglu, Yeni, Kuzucu, Genc
Journal: Environmental toxicology and pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38493879/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

21-day BALB/c mouse study testing oral Cynara scolymus leaf extract (1.6 g/kg) and leaf-synthesized gold nanoparticles against sodium arsenite (10 mg/kg/d) hippocampal neurotoxicity. Treatments downregulated TNF-alpha and IL-1beta, reduced alterations in D1/D2 dopamine receptor expression, and improved water-maze learning/memory and locomotor coordination. Limits: leaf extract in arsenic-toxicity mice, no PD or human symptom endpoint.
