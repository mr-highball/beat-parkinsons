Leri and colleagues compared different molar ratios of purified oleuropein aglycone and hydroxytyrosol in human SH-SY5Y neuroblastoma cells challenged with amyloid-beta 1–42 oligomers. The selected test mix contained 37.5 micromolar of each compound. Its autophagy marker results favored that comparison over the same total molar amount as individual compounds. Figure 4 indicates a timing boundary. A 24-hour pretreatment was associated with less oxidative stress and declining cell-surface oligomer signal after challenge. Simultaneous addition and treatment after a 24-hour oligomer challenge did not significantly reduce reactive oxygen species under the tested conditions. Figure 5 compares the mitochondrial measures in pretreated and oligomer-challenged cells. Figure 6 tracks p62 under a proteasome inhibitor challenge.

FEBS poster P-02.2-07 has the same four authors, purified compounds, amyloid challenge, autophagy and oxidative endpoints. Count it and this journal article within the same experiment family unless material independent runs are established. These are cell-culture comparisons in an Alzheimer-related model. The methods do not administer or compare oils of any extraction grade and do not assess constipation or anxiety in Parkinson disease.
