Round 1 full publication review. The archive retains the focal exposure and outcome boundaries for each queued claim.

The original Parkinson nutraceutical review distinguishes ingredients, compounds and biological mechanisms in tables on oxidative stress, protein aggregation and cell injury. It describes isolated crocin, gallic acid, palmitoylethanolamide and tomato model diets without a matched culinary symptom estimate.

Claim 0417 | ground saffron | muscle rigidity severity | related
The saffron passage traces isolated crocin to cellular stress and apoptosis markers. It supplies no ground saffron rigidity score.
Claim 0430 | ground sumac | bradykinesia severity | related
Sumac is named as a possible gallic acid source. The relevant assay measures alpha-synuclein aggregation rather than slow movement after ground sumac.
Claim 0544 | cooked tomato | muscle rigidity severity | related
The original cites tomato-enriched model diets and striatal markers. The reviewed outcome is not a rigidity score after cooked tomato.
Claim 0559 | raw fig | rest tremor severity | excluded
Claim 0628 | cold-pressed peanut oil | muscle rigidity severity | related
Peanut oil is one source of palmitoylethanolamide. The cited comparisons administer the isolated compound, leaving cold-pressed oil and rigidity untested.
