Peters et al., 2023. Effects of Culinary Spices on Liking and Consumption of Protein Rich Foods in Community-Dwelling Older Adults. Nutrients 2023;15(5):1172.
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/15/5/1172/pdf?version=1677654776
Access and coverage: Publisher PDF, sections 2.4 and 3 and Appendix A, matched to ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05175391.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

One hundred community-dwelling volunteers participated in the meat or vegetarian comparison. Dill and other spices were actually served in the seasoned chicken salad and in the seasoned chickpea and rice salad. Appendix A lists dill without specifying fresh or dried. Study outcomes were meal liking, flavor and protein consumption. Parkinson disease, hand dexterity and the other five frozen symptom outcomes were not reported as endpoints.
