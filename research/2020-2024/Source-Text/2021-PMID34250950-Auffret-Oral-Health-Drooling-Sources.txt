The review distinguishes oral dryness, salivary production, difficulties clearing saliva, swallowing and saliva loss. Its supplementary table indexes original dental and oral cohorts with different outcomes and populations. Some cohorts used patient reports and others measured salivary flow. The reduced-flow observations and the drooling and dry-mouth estimates come from named studies with different definitions. Food texture and oral hygiene suggestions in the review are practical context. No reviewed comparison of raw fig and graded Parkinson drooling is identified.
