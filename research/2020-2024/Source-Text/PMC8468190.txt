Title: Substantively Lowered Levels of Pantothenic Acid (Vitamin B5) in Several Regions of the Human Brain in Parkinson's Disease Dementia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8468190/
PMID: 34564384
PMCID: PMC8468190
DOI: 10.3390/metabo11090569
Publication date: 2021-08-25
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The study analyzes nine brain regions from nine neuropathologically confirmed Parkinson disease dementia donors and nine controls. Table 2 reports about 40% lower pantothenic acid in case cerebellum, substantia nigra and medulla. Primary visual cortex does not show a clear case–control difference. The comparison measures tissue concentration without dried fig intake or recurrent visual hallucination counts.
