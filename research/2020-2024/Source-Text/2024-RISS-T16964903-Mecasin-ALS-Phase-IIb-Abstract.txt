The dissertation describes a 24-week blinded Mecasin plus riluzole comparison in ALS. The abstract reports 39 assigned participants (19 active, 20 placebo) and names pain VAS among secondary outcomes. The later 2026 paper reports 50 randomized and 43 in its efficacy analysis. Duration, phase, institution, treatment and endpoint profiles give a plausible shared lineage, but the counts differ and individual overlap cannot be authenticated from the accessible materials. Keep the thesis linked without treating it as an independent pain experiment. No thesis pain estimate is extracted.
