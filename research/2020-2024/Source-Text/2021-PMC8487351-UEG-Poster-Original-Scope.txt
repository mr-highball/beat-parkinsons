Screen hits were inspected in their poster context.

P0030 used a scrambled-egg scintigraphic test meal in 27 sedentary controls and 19 professional cyclists. The comparison concerns exercise intensity and gastric emptying. Egg preparation was shared and falls were outside the measured outcome.

P0156 and P0157 concern celiac disease. The first reports 14 of 74 patients with nausea or vomiting on a short gluten challenge and less frequent habitual gluten-free oats among those patients. P0157 has similar reported oat use in groups with and without persistent symptoms on a gluten-free diet. P0156 and the later article PMID 37674120 have matching study counts, challenge and comparison. They belong to one source family. P0590 estimates total gluten from diaries and constituent assumptions across several grains in an IBS population. It is not an isolated barley anxiety result.

P0193 reports 120 randomly assigned adults with obesity and 105 completers. The extract, fermented extract and placebo groups did not differ in weight, BMI, waist or reported body-fat-change comparisons. Within the fermented-extract group alone, reported fat percentage and fat-free mass changed. No tested brewed tea preparation, fatigue scale or Parkinson population is given. The 2021 EHMSG workshop report matches the sample, arms and findings and is the same trial.

P0584 includes coffee as one of several screening predictors and gives no isolated coffee effect on fatigue. P0675 reports an observational association with liver-test outcomes. P0606 reports a cross-sectional constipation association for the category of antiparkinson agents (adjusted odds ratio 1.9, p=0.025), with indication and exposure limits. It gives useful clinical context while supplying no food contrast.

P0652 compares dietary records in 156 people with simple steatosis and 22 with NASH. It describes lower recorded onion intake in NASH. Related AS Nutrition 2021 abstract PMC8180753 reports the same food values and population and is treated as the same family. Onion preparation and Parkinson rigidity are outside the stated measurements. P0902 gives a diluted orange syrup comparator in a study of endoscopic visualization. It is a flavored test solution, distinct from raw orange or a study of constipation and falls.
