Botanical-variety exclusion audit (Jalal et al., 2020, Nutrients, PMC7692137): tested a boiled aqueous Phaseolus vulgaris extract in 6-OHDA-lesioned Parkinson's rats. Direct full-text verification (Section 2.6) confirmed the investigators boiled fresh GREEN BEAN pods rather than mature dry/cooked black beans (Phaseolus vulgaris black turtle bean). Excluded from directional attribution to cooked/boiled/canned black beans.
