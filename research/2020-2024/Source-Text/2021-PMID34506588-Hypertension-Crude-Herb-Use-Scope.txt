Original study scope
The Malaysian cross-sectional study investigates people's use of crude herbs for hypertension and health-related quality of life. The introductory evidence lists culinary basil and ginger in other samples, and saffron occurs in a cited discussion of muscle soreness. A plant described as asparagus in the original table is Vigna unguiculata, a different food identity.

Continuing evidence gaps
The paper does not establish separate amounts or transferable symptom scores for the named culinary exposures in Parkinson disease. Its survey and outside source statements are different evidence contexts.
