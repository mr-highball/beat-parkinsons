Title: Recovery of Forearm and Fine Digit Function After Chronic Spinal Cord Injury by Simultaneous Blockade of Inhibitory Matrix Chondroitin Sulfate Proteoglycan Production and the Receptor PTPσ
Authors: Adrianna J. Milton and colleagues
Year: 2023
PMID: 37606910
PMCID: PMC10698859
DOI: 10.1089/neu.2023.0117
Source: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1089/neu.2023.0117
Coverage: Original factual extract of the publisher's Methods and Figures 4–5.

Rats with partial cervical spinal cord injury received pharmacological treatment or controls. The investigators used Nutella hazelnut spread mixed with sunflower oil as the oral gavage matrix. Drug-containing and relevant drug-free oral groups shared the matrix. The text identifies sunflower oil as the thinning ingredient. It does not identify isolated hazelnut oil or report the spread lot's processing history.

On recorded cereal manipulation tests, the IBB forelimb scale rated grip positions, dexterous adjustments of digits, and wrist use. The combination therapy reached a significant improvement relative to controls by 17 weeks after injury and remained significant at 24 weeks. The article gives p=.1601 for the oral inhibitor alone in the figure caption at the late time point. The comparison concerns the drugs after spinal injury. It does not test a hazelnut-oil effect or handwriting size.
