Title: Systemic administration of the di-apocarotenoid norbixin (BIO201) is neuroprotective, preserves photoreceptor function and inhibits A2E and lipofuscin accumulation in animal models of age-related macular degeneration and Stargardt disease.
PMID: 32255762
PMCID: PMC7185133
DOI: 10.18632/aging.103014
First published: 2020
Authors: Fontaine, Monteiro, Fournié, Brazhnikova, Boumedine
Journal: Aging
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32255762/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse models of blue-light retinal damage and Abca4-/- Rdh8-/- Stargardt/AMD degeneration testing i.p. and 6-month oral 9'-cis-norbixin (BIO201, derived from Bixa orellana seeds). Norbixin preserved scotopic/photopic electroretinogram amplitudes and reduced ocular A2E accumulation. Limits: ocular photoreceptor degeneration assay, unrelated to Parkinson's visual hallucinations.
