Oral pumpkin seed oil and tongue histology with orlistat
Source: https://ejh.journals.ekb.eg/article_77437_320d584bc6f090faa30252c8f620beda.pdf
DOI: 10.21608/ejh.2020.25027.1258
Coverage: factual extract from primary Methods and tissue findings

Amira Adly Kassab, Khalid Ahmed Ahmed Moustafa and Amal A. A. Abd-El-Hafez. Egyptian Journal of Histology 43(4):975–987, 2020.
Fifty male albino rats received assigned conditions covering controls, orlistat, and oral oil plus orlistat. The commercial liquid oil came from El Captain, and oral co-intervention lasted four weeks. The investigators evaluated tongue papillae with light and scanning electron microscopy and quantified immunostaining of a cellular proliferation marker. Oil coadministration was associated with less tissue disruption after orlistat. Salivation is mentioned in the background as an oral function. The article describes no salivary-flow assay, observed drooling count, swallowing or Parkinson assessment. Seed roasting and expression are unverified.
