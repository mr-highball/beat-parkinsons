Title: 2022 FEBS Open Bio poster collection, food exposure scope
PMCID: PMC9260650
DOI: 10.1002/2211-5463.13440
Source: https://research.unl.pt/ws/portalfiles/portal/76398782/FEBS_Open_Bio_2022_Posters_1_.pdf
Published: 2022-07-07.
Coverage: 270 consecutive published pages, pp. 67–336. The 798 numbered entries include withdrawals. On p. 310 the publisher prints one withdrawal and one separately titled poster under P-05.2-003. Both entries were kept separate.

P-01.4-003 describes the in-vitro inflammatory readouts of a hydroxytyrosol derivative. It identifies virgin olive oil as a natural source of hydroxytyrosol. P-01.4-012 measures cell-assay properties of thyme and sage extracts. The abstract calls the material sage without verifying the culinary species or preparation. P-01.5-003 tests oregano essential oil in a zebrafish behavior model. P-02.2-014 tests antimicrobial activity of aromatic plant extracts, including rosemary and oregano. P-03.3-016 describes corn oil as a laboratory vehicle in a liver-fibrosis experiment.

P-02.1-027 studies cultivated potato and related plant lines in plant disease. P-04.1-053 studies potato-derived enzyme inhibitors. P-02.1-017, P-04.1-098 and P-04.4-022 study tomato in plant disease, enzyme characterization or transgenic horticulture. P-05.1-014 studies raspberry-seed byproducts and P-05.1-020 compares juice and wine in laboratory digestion and cell models. P-05.1-021 compares compositions of basil leaves. P-05.3-002 studies barley amylose in a materials experiment and P-06.2-007 studies grain composition under growing conditions.

Date hits record chronology or line breaks inside words such as candidate and elucidate. An egg reference describes parasite eggs or laboratory egg development. The screened volume did not yield a verified kefir exposure. The verified exposures and constituents are useful for identity and mechanism context. The meeting posters provide no isolated culinary effect on the mapped Parkinson symptoms.
