Title: Effect of dietary nitrate ingestion on muscular performance: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
PMID: 33554654
DOI: 10.1080/10408398.2021.1884040
First published: 2022
Authors: Alvares TS, Oliveira GV, Volino-Souza M, Conte-Junior CA, Murias JM
Journal: Critical reviews in food science and nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33554654/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 34 RCTs assessing dietary nitrate (predominantly beetroot juice): statistically significant improvement in muscular endurance / fatigue resistance (SMD 0.31; p<0.0001). Context review (non-PD exercise trials).
