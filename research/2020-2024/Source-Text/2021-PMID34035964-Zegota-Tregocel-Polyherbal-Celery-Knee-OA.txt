Title: Prospective, Multicenter Evaluation of a Polyherbal Supplement alongside Standard-of-Care Treatment for Mild Knee Osteoarthritis.
PMID: 34035964
PMCID: PMC8124005
DOI: 10.1155/2021/5589597
First published: 2021
Authors: Żęgota Z, Goździk J, Głogowska-Szeląg J
Journal: Adv Orthop
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34035964/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Open-label single-arm multicenter study (n=107 knee osteoarthritis patients, 36 wk) of polyherbal Tregocel (curcuminoid, Harpagophytum, Boswellia, Apium graveolens extract, Zingiber) alongside standard care. Median VAS pain dropped from 60 to 21 mm and 6MWT rose 26 m (p<0.001). Heavily bundled 5-ingredient commercial supplement in non-PD knee OA; cannot isolate celery stalk.
