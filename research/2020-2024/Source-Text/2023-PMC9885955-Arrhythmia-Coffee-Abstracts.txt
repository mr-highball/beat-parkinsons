Title: Journal of Arrhythmia oral presentations on coffee and cardiac outcomes
First published: 2023-01
PMCID: PMC9885955
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9885955/
Archive scope: Verified complete original body or conference collection. Selected passages summarized.

Oral presentations in the Journal of Arrhythmia supplement. OP-162-1-GE used self-reported UK Biobank coffee amount and subtype and followed incident cardiac arrhythmias. The presenters reported subtype and intake differences for incident rhythm outcomes. OP-163-1-GE asked a related question in people with existing cardiac conditions and included mortality. Cohort and endpoint distinctions matter. The subtype comparison in the full companion article uses UK Biobank data; these publications cannot serve as independent replications. Neither selected member measures Parkinson symptoms.
