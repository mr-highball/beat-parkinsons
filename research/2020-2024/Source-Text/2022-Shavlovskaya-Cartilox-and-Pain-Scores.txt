Thirty per arm were assigned exercise with or without Cartilox, a blend of collagen, hyaluronic acid, boswellia, curcumin and piperine. Participants could use topical NSAIDs as needed. Table 4 reports day-14 rest pain 1.43 (SD 0.12) versus 2.00 (0.57) and walking pain 1.67 (0.54) versus 2.07 (0.88), with both arms recorded as zero at day 30 for each measure. The narrative reports more than 50% improvement in 28 (95.5%) versus 26 (86.7%); 28 of 30 is 93.3%. It also describes a slight early worsening reported by blend recipients. Neither the worsening nor improvement can be assigned to piperine. There was no cracked culinary exposure or Parkinson population.
