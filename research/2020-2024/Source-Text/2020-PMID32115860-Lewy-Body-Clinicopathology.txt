Title: Association between clinical symptoms and post-mortem neuropathology in dementia with Lewy bodies.
PMID: 32115860
PMCID: PMC7065117
DOI: 10.1111/ggi.13853
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7065117/
First publication date: 2020-03-01 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

An autopsy case links a clinical Lewy body dementia course to neuropathology. Occasional visual hallucinations were reported before donepezil treatment and were described as resolving after treatment in this patient. The longitudinal case gives medication and pathology context and documents neither dried fig ingestion nor a food-related episode rate.
