Early public listing of the National Institute of Unani Medicine anise trial
Source: https://sansad.in/getFile/loksabhaquestions/annex/1711/AU4081.pdf?source=pqals
Coverage: factual extract from public institutional listing

India Ministry of Ayush response to Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 4081, dated March 24, 2023. Annexure III number 16 publicly lists the National Institute of Unani Medicine study Effect of Anisoon in Menopausal Women with Psychological Symptoms: A Randomized Placebo-Controlled Study. This is a project listing and does not disclose results. NIUM supervisor record identifies Arfa Begum. A later review by lead supervisor Arshiya Sultana and colleagues cites Begum and Sultana (2023), Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, as a dissertation and lists aggregate DASS and MKI outcomes. The review table records three 667-mg capsules daily and marks side-effect or ADR reporting as reported, without naming an event. The 2026 direct preparation and clinical publications describe about 4 g/day and no adverse events. Full dissertation and public official trial ID have not been verified. The NIUM protocol's 2022 CTRI REF number is not sufficient by itself to establish public registration.
