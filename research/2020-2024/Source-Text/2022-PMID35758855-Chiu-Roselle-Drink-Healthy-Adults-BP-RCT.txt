Title: Improvement on blood pressure and skin using roselle drink: A clinical trial
PMID: 35758855
DOI: 10.1111/jfbc.14287
First published: 2022
Authors: Chiu HF, Liao YR, Shen YC, Han YC, Golovinskaia O, Venkatakrishnan K, Hung CC, Wang CK
Journal: Journal of Food Biochemistry
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35758855/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized crossover clinical trial in 39 healthy (non-hypertensive) adults consuming 200 mL/day roselle (Hibiscus sabdariffa) drink (1.96 g GAE phenolics and 1.65 g C3G anthocyanins/100 mL) vs placebo beverage for 6 months. Roselle drink significantly lowered blood pressure compared with pre-intervention values and elevated antioxidant enzymes (TEAC, GSH, SOD, GSH-Px, GSH-Rd). Important for orthostatic transferability because BP reduction occurred in healthy non-hypertensive adults, though orthostatic standing BP and PD symptoms were not assessed.
