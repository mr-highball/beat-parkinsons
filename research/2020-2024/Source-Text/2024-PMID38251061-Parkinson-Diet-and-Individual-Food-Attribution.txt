Original study scope
The review cites the baseline Mischley survey for olive and coconut oil and total PRO-PD. The flaxseed comparison combines derived omega-3 with vitamin E and reports a total UPDRS score. Eggs appear in diet descriptions. The Mediterranean constipation studies test diet patterns, not an isolated oil exposure. Dates are temporal.

Continuing evidence gaps
Use the original cohort and randomized reports for the actual outcome and denominator. Oil grade, single-food attribution and individual symptom estimates remain open.
