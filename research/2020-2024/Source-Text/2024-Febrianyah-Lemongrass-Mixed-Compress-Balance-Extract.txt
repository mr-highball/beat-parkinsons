Title: Balance Disorders in the Elderly with the Implementation of Ginger Lemongrass Warm Compress Therapy and Balance Exercise at UPT PSTW Jember
Publication: Jauhari Ahmad Febrianyah and Dian Ratna Elmaghfuroh, October 30, 2024, Curatio 1(1):35–43
Source: https://journal.idscipub.com/curatio/article/download/303/284
Coverage: factual extract of the accessible primary source

One older person with joint pain and balance difficulty received a topical warm ginger and lemongrass mixture and performed balance exercise for seven days. The Berg Balance Scale rose from 8 at baseline to 30 on day seven. No comparator separated the botanical compress from exercise or warming. Parkinson disease was not reported for this participant, the lemongrass species is not given, and no oral preparation or counted falls endpoint was used. A closely matching institutional training paper describes the same participant and setting.
