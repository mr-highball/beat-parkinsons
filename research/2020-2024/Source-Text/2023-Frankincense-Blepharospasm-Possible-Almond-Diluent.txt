Title: Possible almond diluent during topical frankincense use in focal dystonia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10214279/
PMID: 37252049
PMCID: PMC10214279
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajoc.2023.101848
Coverage: factual extract from two primary cases and Discussion.

An April 11, 2023 primary case report describes two people with benign essential blepharospasm, a focal dystonia, who used topical frankincense. One stopped botulinum toxin injections and the other's average treatment interval increased from 3.5 to eight months. The authors state that cottonseed oil, almond oil or coconut oil was used as diluent after local burning. They do not map the diluents to specific people or outcome periods. The observations are uncontrolled, involve ongoing or previous botulinum treatment, and do not isolate an almond-oil effect or establish ingestion, processing grade or Parkinson-related dystonia.
