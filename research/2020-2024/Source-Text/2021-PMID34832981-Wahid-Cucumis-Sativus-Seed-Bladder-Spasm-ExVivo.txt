Title: Cucumis sativus L. Seeds Ameliorate Muscular Spasm-Induced Gastrointestinal and Respiratory Disorders by Simultaneously Inhibiting Calcium Mediated Signaling Pathway.

Authors: Wahid M, Saqib F, Ahmedah HT, Gavris CM, De Feo V, Hogea M et al.

Ex vivo isolated tissue pharmacology study (Wahid et al., 2021, PMC8624829) showing crude ethanolic Cucumis sativus seed extract relaxed isolated rabbit/rat gastrointestinal and urinary bladder smooth muscle strips via calcium-channel/muscarinic pathways. Remote ex vivo seed extract assay; does not test pickled cucumber flesh or human Parkinson urinary urgency.
