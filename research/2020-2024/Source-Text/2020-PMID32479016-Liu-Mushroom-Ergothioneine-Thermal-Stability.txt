Title: A Study on the Antioxidant Properties and Stability of Ergothioneine from Culinary-Medicinal Mushrooms.
PMID: 32479016
DOI: 10.1615/IntJMedMushrooms.2020033625
First published: 2020
Authors: Liu Q, Huang Y, Wang S
Journal: International Journal of Medicinal Mushrooms
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32479016/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Analytical stability study demonstrating that purified mushroom ergothioneine has strong light, acid-base, and thermal stability, while noting that prolonged high-temperature heating of Pleurotus extract (P. citrinopileatus) can partially decrease ergothioneine content and Cu2+ accelerates loss. Contextualizes culinary cooking transfer.
