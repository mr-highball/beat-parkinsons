Corn oil and time to a combined motor phenotype
Source: https://ijpsr.com/bft-article/development-of-parkinsons-disease-rat-model-in-two-weeks-using-corn-oil-and-rotenone-induction/?view=fulltext
DOI: 10.13040/IJPSR.0975-8232.14(4).1921-33
Coverage: Original April 2023 issue, groups, behavioral results and animal replacement

Kumar and colleagues reported a rat experiment with nominal groups of five. The combined corn-oil and rotenone group reached the composite motor phenotype in 14.00 days (SD 0.71), compared with 20.21 (0.37) with rotenone alone, p<.0001. The phenotype combined bradykinesia, postural instability and rigidity. The oil-only group did not exhibit the composite during the reported observation period. The report states that three animals in toxin groups died and were replaced. Time to a combined phenotype, the experimental toxin, unspecified oil processing and animal accounting leave separate bradykinesia severity in humans unresolved.
