Reported sleep estimates after blended marjoram oil massage
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmn.2023.02.009
DOI: 10.1016/j.pmn.2023.02.009
Registration: NCT04549857
Coverage: Original publication Methods results and trial identifier

Lu and colleagues randomized 102 adults with rheumatoid arthritis, and 95 completed. At one week Table 3 gives a PSQI change contrast between the oil blend massage and usual care of −1.19 points (95% CI −2.35 to −0.02, p=.046). The study includes a separate almond-oil massage arm. The oil blend combined three aromas, and PSQI and ESS results do not count dream enactment. The study supplies no effect of eating fresh marjoram in Parkinson disease.
