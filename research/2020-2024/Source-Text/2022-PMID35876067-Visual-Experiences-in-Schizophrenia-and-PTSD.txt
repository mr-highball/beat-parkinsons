Title: Visual phenomenology in schizophrenia and post-traumatic stress disorder: an exploratory study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9345685/
PMID: 35876067
PMCID: PMC9345685
DOI: 10.1192/bjo.2022.544
Publication year: 2022
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

This 56-person exploratory study compares visual experience descriptions in schizophrenia, post-traumatic stress disorder and the combination. The measures concern psychiatric groups and trauma-related contexts. No Parkinson fig intake or comparable clinical episode rate is measured.

The reported groups contain 19 participants with schizophrenia, 17 with post-traumatic stress disorder and 20 with both diagnoses. The authors discuss limitations of the available instruments for particular features.
