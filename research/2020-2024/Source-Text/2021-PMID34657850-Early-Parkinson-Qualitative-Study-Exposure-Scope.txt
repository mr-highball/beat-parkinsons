Original study scope
The qualitative study reports symptom burden in 35 people with early Parkinson disease. Fig. refers to figures and Sage to publishing identity. Participants described symptoms, and the study did not vary raw fig or culinary sage in tests of drooling, fatigue or motor outcomes.

Continuing evidence gaps
The companion cultural note covers participant experience. Clinical ingredient results require a separate assigned or measured exposure.
