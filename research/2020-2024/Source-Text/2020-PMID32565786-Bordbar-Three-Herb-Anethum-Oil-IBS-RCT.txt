Title: Efficacy and Safety of a Novel Herbal Medicine in the Treatment of Irritable Bowel Syndrome: A Randomized Double-Blinded Clinical Trial.
PMID: 32565786
PMCID: PMC7273440
DOI: 10.1155/2020/8213082
First published: 2020
Authors: Bordbar G, Miri MB, Omidi M, Shoja S, Akhavan M
Journal: Gastroenterology research and practice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32565786/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind randomized controlled trial (Bordbar et al., 2020, Gastroenterol Res Pract, PMC7273440) comparing a bundled 3-herb essential oil softgel (Zataria multiflora + Trachyspermum ammi + Anethum graveolens, 180 mg oil BID x 2 weeks) vs hyoscine butylbromide in irritable bowel syndrome, showing higher global IBS-SSS/IBS-AR/IBS-GAI response rates (87%/83.9%/75% vs 37.9%/27.5%). Bundled 3-herb essential oil formulation cannot isolate toasted dill seed or evaluate PD constipation severity.
