Buriram olive-oil vehicle and motor item outcomes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9894020/
PMID: 36743777
PMCID: PMC9894020
DOI: 10.25259/JNRP-2022-6-19
Coverage: primary full Methods and Results, Table 1, and linked existing Thai registry snapshot

First published October 22, 2022. The Buriram trial randomized 40 people with Parkinson disease and analyzed 19 in the cannabinoid arm and 17 in placebo after eight weeks. Both sublingual preparations used olive oil of unreported grade. The adjusted between-arm estimates for UPDRS III, timed up and go and gait velocity were 1.28 points (95% CI −3.86 to 6.41), 0.49 seconds (−1.08 to 2.06), and −0.06 m/s (−0.17 to 0.06). Anxiety and depression comparisons were inconclusive. These are contrasts for added cannabinoid extract. Separate clinical postural instability, rest tremor and bradykinesia scores are not shown. The trial has registry identifier TCTR20210303005.
