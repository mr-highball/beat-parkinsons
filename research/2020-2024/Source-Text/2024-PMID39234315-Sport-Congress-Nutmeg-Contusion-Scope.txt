The SQU Medical Journal publication spans pages 420–478. Screening 211 structured members identified one nutmeg research abstract, SA33. Fifteen rats were split among control, contusion and nutmeg extract groups. The abstract reports better gait after oral ethanolic nutmeg seed extract in the contusion model, without an effect estimate or uncertainty. Nearby papers on sleep report separate exercise and music interventions. Neither the rat result nor those neighboring abstracts measure insomnia after culinary nutmeg. The metadata DOI 10.18295/squmj.12.2023.093 resolves to a different chronic kidney disease review. The publisher PDF and the exact PMC and PubMed identities locate this conference collection.

November 26 supplement follow-through

The original SA33 abstract evaluates oral ethanolic nutmeg extract after rat muscle contusion. It reports a gait comparison in that injury model without measuring Parkinson depression or fatigue. A separate human knee osteoarthritis abstract compares strengthening exercises with strengthening plus turmeric supplementation. It evaluates physical function and pain in that setting. The capsule composition and culinary preparation are unspecified. A student sleep abstract reports coffee use for stimulation and sleep habits without a separate measured Parkinson fatigue response.
