Title: Benefits of betanin in rotenone-induced Parkinson mice.
PMID: 34436745
DOI: 10.1007/s11011-021-00826-0
First published: 2021
Authors: Thong-Asa W, Jedsadavitayakol S, Jutarattananon S
Journal: Metabolic brain disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34436745/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse study (n=40 male ICR mice across 4 groups) testing oral betanin (100 and 200 mg/kg/48 h) against subcutaneous rotenone (2.5 mg/kg/48 h): betanin 100 mg/kg attenuated rotenone-induced motor dysfunction/bradykinesia proxies and nigrostriatal neurodegeneration. Limits: purified betanin in rotenone mice; human whole-beetroot betanin plasma bioavailability is negligible (Clifford 2017).
