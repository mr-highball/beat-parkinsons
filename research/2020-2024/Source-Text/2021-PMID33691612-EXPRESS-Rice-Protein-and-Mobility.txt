Jadczak and colleagues randomized 70 prefrail or frail older adults to rice-protein powder (36) or whey-protein powder (34) with the same six-month exercise program. The abstract reports no statistically significant between-arm differences across its primary and secondary comparisons apart from energy and fat intake. It supplies neither a component-level standing-balance estimate nor an equivalence test. The official trial plan identifies SPPB and Timed Up and Go. Neither available source identifies the rice variety or verifies a cooked whole-grain exposure.

The outcome publisher prints the registration identifier with an extra terminal zero. The study name, authors, site, age range, interventions and endpoints match the separately registered EXPRESS pilot. The institutionally archived thesis protocol and preliminary feasibility chapter supply no verified final arm-specific SPPB result. Treat the registration, protocol, preliminary reports and outcome article as one cohort.
