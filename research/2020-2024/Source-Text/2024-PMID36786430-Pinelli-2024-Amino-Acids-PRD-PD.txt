Title: Can we add whey protein supplementation in patients with Parkinson's disease without interfering with levodopa response?
PMID: 36786430
DOI: 10.1080/00207454.2023.2178433
First published: 2024
Authors: Pinelli G, Siri C, Ranghetti A, Cereda V, Maestri R, Canesi M
Journal: The International journal of neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36786430/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized trial in n=51 PD patients combining protein-redistribution diet with timed amino acid supplementation: maintained motor function without worsening UPDRS III or motor complications when protein/amino acids were properly timed away from daytime levodopa.
