Factual extract of the official ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 record checked 2026-11-14 (US Eastern).

Three-arm randomized parallel participant-masked proof-of-concept trial recruited 32 moderately active men aged 18–45. Intervention was a blackberry-purée high-protein dairy drink, compared with commercial high-protein milk and low-protein milk. Registered primary outcomes included self-reported muscle soreness on a 0–10 pain scale through 72 hours post exercise, isometric strength and oxidative stress markers. Registry shows actual completion on 2023-05-31 and has no posted results. Carey repository thesis Chapter 5 cites this registration and reports collected outcomes.
