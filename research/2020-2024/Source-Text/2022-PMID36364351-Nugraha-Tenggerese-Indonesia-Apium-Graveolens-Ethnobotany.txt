Title: Phytochemistry and Pharmacology of Medicinal Plants Used by the Tenggerese Society in Java Island of Indonesia.
PMID: 36364351
PMCID: PMC9693994
DOI: 10.3390/molecules27217532
First published: 2022
Authors: Nugraha AS, Agustina RP, Mirza S, Rani DM, Winarto NB, Triatmoko B et al.
Journal: Molecules
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36364351/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Ethnopharmacological review of 41 medicinal plants used by the indigenous Tenggerese community of Java, Indonesia, identifying Apium graveolens among the 5 most popular healer-used plants, traditionally employed for hypertension. Traditional practice context without controlled celery-stalk PD evidence.
