Gong and colleagues studied isolated filbertone in cell and
mouse models. In the Parkinson-related model results, the
measured outcomes include markers of dopaminergic neurons
and neurotrophic or inflammatory processes. The main paper
does not supply a clinical or isolated rest-tremor effect.
A separate earlier filbertone paper measures animal motor
tasks. The authors cite that study as prior work.

This source uses isolated filbertone without a culinary
hazelnut-oil arm or oral human absorption measurements.
Preprint DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-4100942/v1 describes the same
study family.
