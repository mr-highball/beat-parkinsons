Title: Helicobacter pylori eradication improves motor fluctuations in advanced Parkinson's disease patients — registry linkage and food scope
PMID: 33945559
PMCID: PMC8096108
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0251042
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8096108/
Published: 2021-05-04.
Archive scope: Original article, tables and official registry comparison. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

Forty people with Parkinson disease and motor fluctuations enrolled. Twenty-two tested positive, received the eradication regimen, and had 17 confirmed eradications and five failures. In the successful subgroup, diary off time averaged 4.0 hours after intervention versus 4.7 hours before, and on time 11.8 versus 10.9 hours. Only the total Wearing-off Questionnaire score retained significance in the authors' Bonferroni adjustment of the endpoints compared in the successful subgroup. The separate tremor item concerns wearing off, and the study does not provide a dietary resting-tremor comparison.

Table 5 records higher combined fruit and vegetable intake in the successful- versus failed-eradication groups, at 2.4 versus 1.4 servings per day. Smoking and sex also differed. Neither specific fruit intake nor fruit assignment was reported. The single-arm regimen and small success/failure strata limit causal interpretation about food and symptoms.

The official registry verifies retrospective registration, the planned one-month diary outcome and a separate UPDRS measure. See the registry note for intervention reporting discrepancies. The Discussion compares this result with a separate randomized placebo-controlled report, PMID 32894625, with different participant and outcome scopes.
