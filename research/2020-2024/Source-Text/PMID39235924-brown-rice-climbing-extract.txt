Uthman and colleagues used concurrent 15-day iron exposure and dietary interventions in male flies. The diet replaced half the sucrose with 1% weight/volume ground Danboto brown rice or white rice. Table 1 says the diets were cooked at 100 C. Figure 2A shows better negative geotaxis with brown-rice feed plus iron compared with iron. The assay counted 8-cm climbs within six seconds. Its stated denominator differs between the methods and figure caption. Neither a human postural measure nor a joint rice-plus-levodopa arm appears in the report.
