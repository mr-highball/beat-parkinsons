Title: Effects of Freeze-Dried Whole Grapes on Cognitive Performance in Postmenopausal Women
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7257957/
DOI: 10.1093/cdn/nzaa057_012
First published: May 29, 2020
Coverage: Factual extract from the primary conference abstract.

The University of Delaware team randomized 40 postmenopausal women to 46 g of whole grape powder or placebo powder for 12 weeks. Thirty-two completed. The study assessed anxiety and depression at both visits and used mixed-effects models for group, time and their interaction. The authors reported no clear anxiety or depression effect. The abstract gives no symptom-scale names, anxiety scores, direction of the contrast, uncertainty interval or exact anxiety p value. A clinically diagnosed Parkinson cohort was outside the study population.
