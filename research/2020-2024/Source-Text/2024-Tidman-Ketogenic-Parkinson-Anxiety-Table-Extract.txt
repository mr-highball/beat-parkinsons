Avocado oil mentioned in a Parkinson ketogenic anxiety follow-up

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11457624/
Coverage: Original change table and qualitative interview data
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published June 13, 2024. Seven Parkinson participants continued from an earlier 12-week uncontrolled ketogenic pilot. At 24 weeks the Parkinson Anxiety Scale mean change was −6.29, SD 2.87 and median −7 (p=.0313). Participant 019 mentioned either coconut or avocado oil in discussing adaptation. The interview does not establish a particular refined oil dose, the individual PAS change, or any avocado-oil-specific intervention.
