Title: Preexisting Bipolar Disorder Influences the Subsequent Phenotype of Parkinson's Disease

Design: Parkinson phenotype cohort

Investigators identified prior bipolar disorder in 250 of 2,660 Parkinson patients and compared phenotype by history. The groups differ in psychiatric and medication context, including psychotic symptoms. The study does not isolate dried food intake or repeated visual episodes after ingestion.
