Cavendish bananas and tension anxiety
https://pubs.sciepub.com/jfnr/10/12/2/
selected methods and findings paraphrase

Published December 2022. An open randomized trial assigned 13 healthy adults to two Cavendish bananas daily and seven to no bananas for two weeks. The between-group change rate for the POMS2 tension–anxiety subscale did not show a clear difference. Outcome-driven responder subgroups do not provide a randomized effect estimate. Dole supplied the fruit. Healthy-volunteer selection and the small sample limit the comparison.
