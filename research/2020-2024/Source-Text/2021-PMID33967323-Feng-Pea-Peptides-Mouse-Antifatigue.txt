Title: Anti-fatigue effects of pea (Pisum sativum L.) peptides prepared by compound protease.
PMID: 33967323
PMCID: PMC8076353
DOI: 10.1007/s13197-020-04737-3
First published: 2021
Authors: Feng T, Huang Y, Tang Z, Wei D, Mo J
Journal: Journal of food science and technology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33967323/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Primary Abstract Facts: 30-day mouse experiment administering enzymatic protease-hydrolyzed Pisum sativum peptides vs control; prolonged weight-loaded forced swimming time, lowered blood urea nitrogen and lactic acid, and raised muscle/hepatic glycogen. Isolated enzymatic peptide hydrolysate in healthy rodents undergoing forced swimming; does not evaluate cooked whole green peas or Parkinson disease fatigue.
