Title: An Adaptive Measure of Visuospatial Impairment in Dementia with Lewy Bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9274351/
PMID: 35844276
PMCID: PMC9274351
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13488
Publication date: 2022-06-13
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The study compares performance among 22 DLB, 22 Parkinson and 22 age-matched control participants. Visual task accuracy and threshold are perceptual measures, not spontaneous visual episodes. No dried fig intake is assigned or measured.
