Title: Utility of the 20-Item Noise Pareidolia Task (NPT-20) for Assessing Visuoperceptual Disturbances Associated with Complex Visual Hallucinations in Parkinson's Disease
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9941920/
PMID: 36825060
PMCID: PMC9941920
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13599
Publication year: 2022
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete official PMC HTML.

The 20-item noise task tests whether perceiving nonexistent targets in ambiguous images distinguishes Parkinson participants with a complex hallucination history. Its sensitivity and specificity depend on the task-error threshold. This is a diagnostic task response rather than a dietary effect.

A two-error cut-off yielded 40% sensitivity and 100% specificity in the sample. A one-error cut-off yielded 75% sensitivity and 81% specificity. The reported classification performance distinguishes a clinical history and is not a change in spontaneous episode frequency.
