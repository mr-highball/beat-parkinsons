Sixty adolescents in one group completed pain assessments before and after receiving the dates, lemongrass and young coconut water mixture. Table 2 shows 23, 31 and 6 participants in the three ordered pre-intervention bands and 37, 20 and 3 in the post-intervention bands. The source changes the descriptive category labels between measurement times, so the numerical band counts and author interpretation should be kept separate. The authors report Wilcoxon Z = -7.103 with p displayed as .000. The report explicitly recognizes natural decline in menstrual pain across measurement days. There is no concurrent untreated or blinded group, no isolated fruit contrast and no identified Parkinson cohort. Fruit drying is unspecified.
