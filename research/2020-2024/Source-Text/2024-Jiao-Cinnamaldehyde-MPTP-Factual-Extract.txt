Title: Cinnamaldehyde Attenuates the Expression of IBA1 and GFAP to Inhibit Glial Cell Activation and Inflammation in the MPTP-Induced Acute Parkinson's Disease Model
Authors: Panpan Jiao and colleagues
Identifiers: PMID 39741957, PMCID PMC11688134, DOI 10.1155/padi/9973140
First published: 2024-12-24
Original source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11688134/
Text object: https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC11688134.1/PMC11688134.1.txt, version 1 CC BY 4.0
Coverage: Full primary body, Methods §§2.1–2.2, Results §3.2 and Figure 2. Factual extract.

The study administered isolated cinnamaldehyde as gavage after acute MPTP induction and reports enhanced crossing and rearing, pole performance and rotarod retention. The abstract interprets the battery as reduced anxiety. Figure 2 and the Results distinguish increased locomotor counts from an inconclusive group comparison for urination frequency (p>.05). No separately interpretable independent anxiety-specific behavioral measure was reported. Clinical anxiety and ground cinnamon were untested.
