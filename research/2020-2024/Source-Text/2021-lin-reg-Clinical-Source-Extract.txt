Recorded multiodor cognitive program in dementia
2021-12-23
https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05168098

The first public registry entry followed the stated January 31, 2021 study completion. The 30-participant pilot registered cognitive games that used rosemary essential oil among 15 odors, a board-game group and a control. It included the Geriatric Depression Scale. The official API has no posted registry results. The matching 2022 paper reports one comparison across these groups, with rosemary used as a smell.
