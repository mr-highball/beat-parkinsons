Title: l-theanine adjunct to sertraline for major depressive disorder: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial.
PMID: 37084960
DOI: 10.1016/j.jad.2023.04.029
First published: 2023
Authors: Shamabadi A, Kafi F, Arab Bafrani M, Asadigandomani H, A Basti F, Akhondzadeh S
Journal: Journal of affective disorders
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37084960/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Six-week double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in 60 randomized (50 completed; 25/arm) adults with DSM-5 major depressive disorder receiving sertraline 100 mg/d plus L-theanine 200 mg/d or placebo. Repeated-measures GLM showed a significant time-by-treatment interaction on Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (p=0.014), with greater HDRS reduction at weeks 2 (p=0.02), 4 (p=0.03), and 6 (p=0.01) and higher week-6 remission (p=0.02); adverse events were comparable. Limits: isolated 200 mg/d L-theanine (far higher than 1 cup brewed black tea) as sertraline adjunct in non-PD MDD.
