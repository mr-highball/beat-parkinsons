Factual extract from original Methods 2.2–2.5 and Results 3.1–3.3, PMID 35684048, DOI 10.3390/nu14112249, first online 2022-05-27. Piper nigrum underwent 70% ethanol extraction at 50 C for 16 hours, concentration, filtration and freeze drying. The stage experiment administered 25 mg/kg caffeine and 500 mg/kg extract orally to C57BL/6N mice; EEG and neck EMG characterized wake, NREM and REM sleep and their bouts. Figure group sizes 7–8. Authors report no significant REM-sleep-duration difference between groups. There is no Parkinson model or measured dream-enactment event frequency. The separate pentobarbital model uses drug-induced righting-reflex time and is not dream enactment. Original official full XML saved in the staging folder.
