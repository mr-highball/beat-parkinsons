Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article, depression section and cardiovascular table.

The depression section and neuroprotection table combine animal extract and constituent experiments with citations to clinical depression work using ethanolic extract. The source reports no ground-saffron Parkinson depression comparison. The cardiovascular table's fatigue entry refers to atrioventricular-node electrophysiology in a rabbit preparation. It is not a physical or patient-reported fatigue outcome. The review supplies no clinical fatigue severity contrast for oral dried saffron in Parkinson disease.
