Dementia pilot using multiple odors including rosemary
2022-09-20
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9531625/

Lin and colleagues analyzed 28 of 30 randomized dementia participants. Median depression scores changed from 3 to 0 in the odor program, 2 to 0 in the board-game group and 1 to 0 in the control group. The group sizes were 9, 10 and 9 and the reported posttest comparison was inconclusive. Rosemary was one essential oil among several scents used. The trial gives no separate rosemary estimate and studied neither dietary leaves nor Parkinson depressive symptoms.
