Title: Consumption of a fermented dairy beverage improves hippocampal-dependent relational memory in a randomized, controlled cross-over trial.
PMID: 35282787
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2022.2046963
First published: 2023
Authors: Cannavale CN, Mysonhimer AR, Bailey MA, Cohen NJ, Holscher HD, Khan NA
Journal: Nutritional neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35282787/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Single-blind randomized 4-week crossover trial (NCT02849275) in 26 healthy adults (25-45 y) consuming 8 oz/day fermented dairy kefir beverage (25-30 billion CFU live kefir cultures) vs isocaloric non-fermented milk control. Kefir increased fecal Lactobacillus (+235%, p<0.01) and improved hippocampal-dependent relational memory (misplacement p=0.04, object-location binding p=0.03), but DASS-42 DEPRESSION, ANXIETY, and STRESS scores and 24-h urinary free cortisol were ALL NULL (all p>0.08).
