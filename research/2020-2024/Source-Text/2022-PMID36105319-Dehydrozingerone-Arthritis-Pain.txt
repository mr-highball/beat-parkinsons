Title: Dehydrozingerone and induced hypersensitivity in an arthritis rat model

Date basis: First publication date in the original article.

Reviewed: 2026-11-25

The study compared purified dehydrozingerone with the untreated arthritis-model group. The main results and Figure 2 report longer heat withdrawal latency and higher mechanical withdrawal threshold after treatment, with six animals per group and both contrasts marked p < 0.05. The abstract lists reductions in threshold and latency among its results, conflicting with the reported graph direction and the main results. The figure and its outcome-specific text give the interpretable signs.

The intervention was an isolated constituent linked to Zingiber officinale. This inflammatory arthritis model has no Parkinson population, culinary ginger exposure, clinical pain score or dyskinesia comparison.
