Title: Isolated mangiferin and behavior in the Yan postpartum model
Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pharmacology/articles/10.3389/fphar.2022.840567/full
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35721155/
PMID: 35721155
PMCID: PMC9204178

Publication date: 2022-06-03
Date basis: Publisher publication date.
Reviewed: 2026-11-24
Review depth: Full publisher article, including original design, results, Figure 2 groups and Figure 3.

The investigators compare isolated mangiferin with a postpartum-like mouse model control. Figure 2 describes reduced time on the behavioral inactivity measure used in the novelty-suppressed feeding test, and reduced immobility in swim and suspension tests. The novelty-suppressed feeding analysis has 29 mice across four groups; the two immobility tests each describe nine mice per group. The original reports a graded novelty-suppressed feeding difference at p<.001 versus the model group, with a residual difference from unmodeled controls. In Figure 3, hippocampal PSD95 and BDNF differences between intervention and model were inconclusive.

The formulation and model concern isolated mangiferin and experimentally measured behavior. The study did not investigate dried mango fruit or depressive symptoms in people with Parkinson disease.
