Original study scope
This perspective identifies sardines and salmon as EPA and DHA sources, cites omega-3 supplement studies on anxiety, and discusses corn oil and other linoleate-rich oils in a proposed inflammation pathway.

Continuing evidence gaps
The food recommendations and cited nutrient supplements do not isolate the two fish preparations in Parkinson anxiety. The corn-oil hypothesis has no assigned oil comparison with a depressive symptom endpoint.
