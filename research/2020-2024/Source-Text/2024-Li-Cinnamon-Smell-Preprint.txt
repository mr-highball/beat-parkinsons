Original preprint factual summary. Cinnamon is one of seven identification stimuli in a combined trained diagnostic classifier. Table 4 gives AUC 0.87 (95% CI 0.85–0.90) for Parkinson or dementia with Lewy bodies versus control in three pooled cohorts. No cinnamon ingestion, challenge treatment or change in olfactory impairment was studied. Publication family: PMID40268961.
