Title: Dietary Supplementation with Popped Amaranth Modulates the Gut Microbiota in Low Height-for-Age Children: A Nonrandomized Pilot Trial.
PMID: 37509852
PMCID: PMC10379428
DOI: 10.3390/foods12142760
First published: 2023
Authors: Calva-Cruz OJ, Ovando-Vázquez C, De León-Rodríguez A, Veana F, Espitia-Rangel E, Treviño S, et al.
Journal: Foods (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37509852/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Non-randomized 3-month pilot trial (Calva-Cruz et al., 2023, Foods, PMC10379428) administering 4 g/day popped amaranth grain to stunted low height-for-age Mexican children (n=9 enrolled, n=7 completers) alongside n=21 normal height-for-age reference controls. Popped amaranth modulated fecal microbiota (decreasing Alistipes putredinis and Bacteroides coprocola and increasing Akkermansia muciniphila). Limits: tiny non-randomized pediatric pilot (n=7 completers), popped grain rather than boiled amaranth, and no constipation, stool frequency, or Parkinson's symptom endpoints.
