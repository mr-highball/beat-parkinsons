Title: Acute cognitive performance and mood effects of coffee berry and apple extracts: A randomised, double blind, placebo controlled crossover study in healthy humans.
PMID: 34380382
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2021.1963068
First published: 2022
Authors: Jackson PA, Haskell-Ramsay C, Forster J, Khan J, Veasey R, Kennedy DO et al.
Journal: Nutritional neuroscience
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34380382/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized double-blind placebo-controlled 4-way crossover trial in n=46 healthy adults (mean age 23 y) comparing 1100 mg coffee berry extract, 1100 mg coffee berry + 275 mg apple extract, 100 mg coffee berry + 275 mg apple extract, and placebo at 1, 3, and 6 h post-dose. Adding 275 mg apple extract produced no mood effect and any cognitive speed change occurred only in bundled coffee-berry arms without an apple-alone arm.
