Title: Anti-inflammatory, anti-apoptotic, and neuroprotective potentials of anethole in Parkinson's disease-like motor and non-motor symptoms induced by rotenone in rats.
PMID: 37204660
DOI: 10.1007/s11011-023-01230-6
First published: 2023
Authors: Moradi Vastegani S, Khoshnam SE, Mansouri E, Ghafouri S, Bakhtiari N, Farbood Y, Sarkaki A
Journal: Metabolic brain disease
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37204660/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical rotenone-induced Parkinson's rat study (male Wistar rats, rotenone 2 mg/kg s.c. x 5 weeks with concomitant intragastric isolated anethole 62.5, 125, 250 mg/kg/d or L-DOPA positive control; erratum published at PMID:40029401). Reported significant attenuation of rotenone-induced motor deficits and anxiety- and depression-like behaviors, reduced striatal TNF-alpha, IL-6, and caspase-3, elevated IL-4, improved striatal neuron survival, and higher striatal dopamine. Exposure is isolated anethole rather than whole ground anise seed, and independence from companion Vastegani 2023 rotenone-anethole papers (PMID:36075399, PMC10653401) is unconfirmed.
