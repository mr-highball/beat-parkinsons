Sesame oil cake extract and memory tests

Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/13/8/2606
Coverage: Original article, factual extract.

A 12-week placebo-controlled double-blind pilot enrolled older adults with memory impairment and used an extract of residual material from sesame oil production. Authors reported verbal learning improvements. The discussion explicitly indicates that attention was not assessed. No group received ordinary culinary toasted sesame oil.
