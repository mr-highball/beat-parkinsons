Adverse event reporting in a second white bean extract trial
June 3, 2024 · DOI 10.1038/s41598-024-63443-8 · NCT02930668
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-63443-8
Factual extract from original study Methods and adverse-event Results.

Jäger and colleagues randomized 90 adults with overweight or moderate obesity to two white kidney bean extract formulations or placebo for twelve weeks. The original paper reports 19 adverse events, with no insomnia or sleep disturbance named in its published summary. Sleep is not a measured endpoint there. These context records involve extracted material and clinically distinct participants. The absence of a named adverse event does not quantify an insomnia change.
