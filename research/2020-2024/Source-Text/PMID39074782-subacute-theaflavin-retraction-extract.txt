Original factual extract from the journal notice

The editors describe apparent overlaps in the upper and lower rows of Fig. 4A, changes in scale in two upper-row panels, and overlaps and internally duplicated regions in Fig. 5A. Authors attributed the problem to assembly of images from a core facility and supplied some new panels based on re-imaged slides. The editors cited unresolved concerns about the top of Fig. 4 and the reason for the original duplication when retracting the paper. This notice applies to the whole subacute report and identifies the article by DOI. It is distinct from two chronic MPTP/probenecid papers involving the first author.
