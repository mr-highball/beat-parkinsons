Title: Neuropharmacological evaluation of different species of Curcubitaceae seeds extract in experimental animals
PMID: 36967516
First published: 2023
Authors: Tuseef Sayyar H, Afroz S, Khan A
Journal: Pakistan Journal of Pharmaceutical Sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36967516/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Sixty-day rodent behavioral study evaluating ethanol SEED extracts (25, 50, 100 mg/kg) of three Cucumis melo varieties including Santa Claus melon (Inodorus Group), reticulatus, and flexuosus on Elevated Plus Maze, head-dip, phenobarbitone sleep, and Rotarod. At 100 mg/kg, reticulatus and Santa Claus seed extracts increased open-arm entries without impairing Rotarod coordination while showing general CNS depression at highest doses. Non-flesh SEED extract in non-PD rodents, NOT raw honeydew melon flesh.
