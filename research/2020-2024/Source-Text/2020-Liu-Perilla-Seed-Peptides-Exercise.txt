Perilla seed peptides and exercise fatigue measures in mice
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32984958/
Coverage: Author abstract factual summary.

First published September 28, 2020. The original abstract reports greater exercise tolerance and changed fatigue-related markers after a preparation of hydrolyzed purple perilla seed peptides in mice. This is the same article indexed as AGR:IND607176280 and PMID:32984958. It uses seed and purified peptide material, with no dried leaf intake or reported Parkinson fatigue.
