Title: Nutritional counseling frequency and baseline food pattern predict implementation of a high-protein and high-polyunsaturated fatty acid dietary pattern
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34656026/
PMID: 34656026
DOI: 10.1016/j.clnu.2021.09.022
Publication year: 2021
Registry ID: DRKS00010049
Coverage: Distilled author abstract, within the NutriAct trial family.

Of 502 randomized participants, baseline food records were available for 242 intervention and 246 usual-care participants. By 12 months the intervention arm's food records indicated more protein, fiber and unsaturated fats and less carbohydrate and saturated fat. The abstract gives diet-adherence findings and no boiled-barley or GAD-7 anxiety result. Its parent trial is the protocol and registration cited in the barley review.
