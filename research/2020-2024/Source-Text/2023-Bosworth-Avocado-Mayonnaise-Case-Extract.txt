Avocado oil mayonnaise in a non Parkinson ketogenic case

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9870323/
Coverage: Original menu and clinical narrative
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published January 9, 2023. A case report in a 47-year-old woman with Down syndrome and Alzheimer disease describes less observed anxiety after broader ketogenic dietary changes. The example menu includes avocado-oil mayonnaise. No anxiety scale, independent oil comparison or refining method was provided, and the participant did not have the target Parkinson phenotype.
