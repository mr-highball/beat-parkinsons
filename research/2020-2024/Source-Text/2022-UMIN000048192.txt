Title: Processed oats, glucose and a separate sleep questionnaire
First published or posted: 2022-06-29
Registry ID: UMIN000048192
Source URL: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000054921
Text type: original-source evidence summary

First disclosed June 29, 2022, with a January 6, 2026 update. The five registered breakfast arms include rice, fruit granola, baked oatmeal, oats and skipped breakfast. Glucose is primary. The later registry narrative describes 22 participants in an oatmeal-based versus white-rice lunch comparison after skipping breakfast, with an OSA-MA sleep questionnaire as a secondary outcome. No numerical sleep results are posted. The different meal timing and sample descriptions leave the relationship between the registry narrative, the 2025 lunch abstract and the later breakfast report partly unresolved.
