Green and gold kiwi protein digestion registration and results
https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT04356573

The registry dates its first public posting to April 22, 2020, following the April 21 retrospective registration date used by the paper. It reports results first posted November 4, 2021. The posted outcome modules concern whole-body net protein balance and skeletal-muscle protein metabolism using stable-isotope methods. The record links the Park crossover and gives no separate attention outcome.
