Original study scope
The review describes turmeric-derived curcumin and diverse neurologic and inflammatory findings, including Parkinson-related models. Acridine orange terminology is a laboratory stain, not a fruit exposure, and dates are temporal.

Continuing evidence gaps
Separate isolated or formulated curcumin from culinary turmeric. No attributable ground-turmeric Parkinson comparison reports the three frozen pain, fall or bradykinesia endpoints.
