Title: Biochemical and behavioral effects of decreasing dietary linoleic acid and increasing eicosapentaenoic acid and docosahexaenoic acid in a rat chronic monoarthritis model

The investigators compared diets with different linoleic acid and marine omega-3 content in a rat monoarthritis model. An oxidized corn oil diet served as a high-linoleic comparator. The comparison changed several fatty acids together. It changed concentrations of several mediators but did not detect differences in thermal or mechanical hypersensitivity or voluntary loading of the affected hind limb.

The tested oil was oxidized and embedded in a mixed dietary comparison. This independent experiment gives clinical-transfer context, with the isolated effect of refined corn oil and Parkinson pain still open.
