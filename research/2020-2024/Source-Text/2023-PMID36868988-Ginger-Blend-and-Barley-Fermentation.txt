Original study scope
The review describes a multi-component product containing ginger-root extract, L-theanine, probiotics and other ingredients and reports mood results attributed to the whole formulation. It separately cites GABA concentrations after barley fermentation compared with heat-treated barley.

Continuing evidence gaps
The blend provides no attributable effect for grated ginger. The barley comparison measures food chemistry rather than anxiety, and the preparation differs from the frozen barley exposure.
