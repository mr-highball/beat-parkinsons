Alpos and colleagues, original Sections 2.4 and 3.3

Original source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8622574/

First published October 22, 2021. Seventeen adult volunteers chewed 5 g cooked black-bean samples with different electric-field or calcium processing histories. They spat out each bolus when they judged it ready to swallow. Median chewing times were 28.5, 29.0 and 24.75 seconds across samples A, B and C. Other analyses concerned particle size, salivary enzyme activity and digestion in vitro. The protocol did not require swallowing. It did not measure aspiration or clinical swallowing difficulty.
