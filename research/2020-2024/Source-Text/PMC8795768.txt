Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article, Table 2, Sections 5.3.5–5.3.7 and references.

The review distinguishes isolated crocetin from whole saffron. The sleep section cites two trials in healthy adults with mild sleep complaints. The 2010 study explicitly used crocetin from Gardenia jasminoides and emphasized awakening episodes. The 2018 trial tested EEG delta power and a sleep inventory, with other sleep parameters inconclusive in the original abstract. The 2010 preparation schedule must be read from the primary report and trial materials. The review inconsistently describes the human dose as per kilogram in the sleep section and as a daily amount elsewhere.

The depression section describes rodent restraint, swimming and oxidative stress outcomes. The neuropathic pain section describes a mouse nerve-injury model. These sections do not establish a dried-date fruit exposure or a Parkinson fatigue or depression result on the named saffron preparation. Subjective refreshment after sleep and daily fatigue severity are distinct outcomes.
