Title: Butylphthalide and motor tests in A53T alpha-synuclein mice
PMID: 34121985
PMCID: PMC8193045
DOI: 10.3389/fnins.2021.647266
First published: 2021-05-28
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8193045/
Coverage: original testing methods, Figure 1 and results. Local factual extract.

The A53T transgenic mouse study compares a drug form of DL-3-n-butylphthalide with its controls. The treated group had a longer mean rotarod retention time in Figure 1B relative to untreated A53T animals. The open-field, olfactory and fear-conditioning comparisons address separate endpoints. These measures include motor coordination and behavior. The trial used a constituent drug and did not track a separate rest-tremor score or raw celery stalk ingestion.
