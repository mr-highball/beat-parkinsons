Title: Vitamin A5/X controls stress-adaptation and prevents depressive-like behaviors in a mouse model of chronic stress.
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8355947/
PMID: 34401411
PMCID: PMC8355947
DOI: 10.1016/j.ynstr.2021.100375
Publication date: 2021-08-03
Coverage: Factual summary from the original publication. Official complete PMC JATS body.

The mouse stress study compares retinoid-related interventions and matched vehicle. Sunflower oil is shared in injections. The design measures behavior after other treatments and cannot isolate an effect of ingested refined sunflower oil.
