Title: Red Bean Extract Inhibits Immobilization-Induced Muscle Atrophy in C57BL/6N Mice.
PMID: 31532323
DOI: 10.1089/jmf.2019.4426
First published: 2020
Authors: Kim C, Kim MB, Hwang JK
Journal: Journal of medicinal food
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31532323/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse study (Kim, Kim & Hwang, 2020, J Med Food) testing oral red/adzuki bean (Vigna angularis) extract in hindlimb-immobilized C57BL/6J mice. Extract dose-dependently increased grip strength, exercise endurance, muscle weight, and myofiber cross-sectional area while downregulating MuRF1/MAFbx proteolysis genes and activating PI3K/Akt/mTOR signaling. Limits: disuse skeletal muscle atrophy model using concentrated extract rather than boiled or cooked beans; peripheral muscle strength/endurance does not measure Parkinson's fatigue, hand dexterity, or gait.
