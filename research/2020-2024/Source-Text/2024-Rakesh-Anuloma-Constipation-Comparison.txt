Title: A Controlled Clinical Study to Evaluate the Comparative Effect of Anuloma DS Tablet and Lactitol + Ispaghula Powder in Functional Constipation
Source: https://ojs.ijcp.in/index.php/IJCP/article/view/1087
DOI: 10.59793/ijcp.v35i3.1087
First published: 2024-08-23
Later journal copy: https://doi.org/10.59793/51tyzs05
Text coverage: factual extraction of the design and Tables 2–5 from the original journal report.

The open-label comparison enrolled 62 adults with functional constipation and analyzed 60, 30 per group. Anuloma DS tablets were taken with warm water and lactitol plus ispaghula powder with warm milk. The tablet contained common cumin and multiple other ingredients, including senna, chebulic myrobalan, licorice and ginger. Its individual cumin amount and degree of grinding were not reported. The trial excluded neurological illness and the study endpoints were bowel symptoms.

After 15 days, mean Constipation Assessment Scale scores went from 5.03 to 1.73 with the tablet and 5.77 to 5.60 with the active comparator. Patient Assessment Scale means went from 11.8 to 6.0 and 12.3 to 11.9. Table 4 gives separate within-arm p values, including a value printed as less than 0.00. Table 3 gives p=.08 for the between-group rank comparison for stool-frequency change, despite the narrative's favorable frequency claim. That table reports a favorable group comparison for stool consistency, with the p value rounded to 0.00. The tables do not establish a levodopa, gastric emptying, or OFF-time effect.

The original August 2024 report, the December 2024 publisher PDF and the February 2025 journal copy name the same authors, report the same enrollment and analysis sizes and reproduce the numerical tables. They are one evidence family. A candidate registry (CTRI/2022/07/044510) matches the planned formulation, comparator, center and follow-up length. The article does not cite that ID and the equivalence is not confirmed.
