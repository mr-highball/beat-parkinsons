Title: ALTIS lentil-based functional food pilot
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04223206
Publication date: 2020-01-10
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Original access depth: registry_and_protocol_member
Registration: NCT04223206
Conference protocol member: 54ASM-0185, DOI 10.1111/eci.13369

The completed 20-participant dialysis pilot registers a combination of lentil flour, pea protein and micronutrients. Its primary measure is muscle mass. The linked original conference member reports protocol design and preliminary nutrition questionnaire information. The registry had no results posted on review. It does not test canned lentils or prespecify Parkinson walking slowness.
