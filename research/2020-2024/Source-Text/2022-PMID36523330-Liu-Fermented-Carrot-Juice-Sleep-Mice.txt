Title: Carrot-based fermentation juice rich in sleep-promoting components improved sleep in mice.
PMID: 36523330
PMCID: PMC9745110
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2022.1043055
First published: 2022
Authors: Liu D, Zhang J, Chen J, Zhang C, Yi H, Liu D
Journal: Front Nutr
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36523330/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical mouse study comparing Levilactobacillus brevis YSJ3 fermented carrot juice (FCJ) vs unfermented carrot juice (UCJ) and negative control. Only FCJ enriched in fermentation-derived GABA and short-chain fatty acids extended sleep duration and reduced anxiety relative to BOTH negative control and unfermented carrot juice, demonstrating the sleep effect came from bacterial fermentation metabolites rather than plain unfermented carrot.
