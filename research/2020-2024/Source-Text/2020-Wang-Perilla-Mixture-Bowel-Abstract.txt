The paper describes 120 children grouped by treatment into equal arms. One arm used a multi-herb prescription including perilla leaf, with an additional modification for dry stools. The other received a different active treatment. The abstract does not document random assignment or identify the initial bowel status for each arm.
The abstract reports 11 versus 14 constipation cases and claims p<.05. Ascertainment, underlying table, analysis denominators and test are unavailable. On the assumption of 60 independent observations per group with those counts, a two-sided Fisher test would be approximately p=.654. This conditional check does not identify the authors' test. The study leaves any distinct Perilla effect unresolved.
