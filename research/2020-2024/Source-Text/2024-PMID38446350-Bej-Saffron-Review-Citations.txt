Title: Therapeutic potential of saffron in brain disorders: From bench to bedside
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38446350/
PMID: 38446350
DOI: 10.1002/ptr.8169
Date basis: 2024 first publication and May 2024 issue.
Coverage: Author abstract and public Wiley reference listing. Complete body remains inaccessible.

Bej and colleagues discuss multiple saffron constituents and distinguish preclinical and human studies across several neurologic and psychiatric conditions. The public reference listing links the original 2005 ethanol-extracted stigma trial in non-Parkinson depression and separate Alzheimer trials. The authors report that they generated no new data. The abstract's disease names and mood references do not establish a clinical ground-saffron Parkinson depression estimate. The original full review and its intervention table remain open.
