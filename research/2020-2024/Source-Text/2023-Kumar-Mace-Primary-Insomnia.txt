Mace milk preparation in uncontrolled primary insomnia
Source: https://doi.org/10.4103/AYUHOM.AYUHOM_48_22
Coverage: factual extract of primary methods and ISI results
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Kumar and colleagues published on 2023-06-26 a single-arm prospective study registered as CTRI/2018/09/015865. Thirty-two completed the mace aril milk preparation. The report records 25 participants improved on the difficulty-falling-asleep item, one unchanged, over the initial 30 days. The participants had primary insomnia, with a neurological cause excluded. The authors’ follow-up intervals are inconsistent between abstract and methods. Mace is the aril rather than ground nutmeg seed. Original text: https://somatheeram.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/SchlafproblemeStudie_4.pdf.
