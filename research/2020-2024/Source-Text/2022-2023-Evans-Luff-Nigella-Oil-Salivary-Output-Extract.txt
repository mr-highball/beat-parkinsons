2022–2023 | Evans, Luff and coauthors | Nigella oil and salivary output after radiation

Sources: 2022 meeting abstract in doi:10.1177/01945998221107672d and full article doi:10.1002/lio2.1122 (PMID 37621284), publicly published July 25, 2023. The report names the 2022 presentation as its earlier version. The initial sample, allocation and methods match.

The four arms crossed sham or salivary-region irradiation with water or Nigella sativa oil by gavage. Figure 2D gives pilocarpine-stimulated saliva output at days 3, 16 and 60. At day 3 the irradiated-water, irradiated-oil and unirradiated-water means were 0.020 g, 0.1217 g and 0.143 g respectively. The article reports p<.001 for irradiated water versus irradiated oil, and for irradiated water versus sham water. At day 60 all surviving groups had recovered saliva output. The earlier meeting abstract gives 100% 60-day survival in its irradiated-oil group, while the full article gives 83%. This is one study family and measures gland output in a dry-mouth model, not spontaneous drooling.
