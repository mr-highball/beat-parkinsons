Title: Thirty Days of Montmorency Tart Cherry Supplementation Has No Effect on Gut Microbiome Composition, Inflammation, or Glycemic Control in Healthy Adults.
PMID: 34604282
PMCID: PMC8481367
DOI: 10.3389/fnut.2021.733057
First published: 2021
Authors: Hillman AR, Chrismas BCR
Journal: Frontiers in nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8481367/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized placebo-controlled trial (Hillman & Chrismas, 2021, Front Nutr, PMC8481367; NCT04467372) in 58 healthy adults consuming Montmorency tart cherry concentrate or freeze-dried capsules vs matched placebos for 30 days. Neither concentrate nor freeze-dried capsules altered fecal microbiome composition or richness, inflammatory markers, or glycemic control vs placebo (complementing Lear et al. 2019 PMID:31085979 null 4-week gut microbiome RCT in 28 adults). No constipation severity endpoint.
