Original source scope
Eight participants with Parkinson disease entered three active cannabis-formulation arms. All formulations used olive oil as the carrier. Table 3 lists one constipation event and one anxiety event in the same formulation arm of three people.

Evidence boundary or continuing gap
The events are not validated symptom-severity comparisons. No oil-free arm or culinary grade is specified and no dried date fruit is tested. The reported drug formulation comparisons do not isolate the oil.
