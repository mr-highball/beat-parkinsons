Title: Christmas pancakes, optional jam and a plank performance test

Coverage: original author abstract and report recipe. Local factual extract.

The investigators assigned eight participants to pancakes with or without added chili and assessed prone plank endurance. Both preparations used a common flavored batter and could be served with sugar plus strawberry or blackcurrant jam. Jam was a serving component rather than the treatment contrast, and consumption by jam type is not reported. None of the plank time comparisons estimates a strawberry effect. The endpoint, preparation and study population are outside the raw strawberry and Parkinson fall-frequency claim.
