The authors describe five groups in a haloperidol rat study with a saline group, levodopa/carbidopa comparison and two oral ethanolic fruit-extract doses, 100 and 200 mg/kg. The acute model used single treatments and the chronic model used 14 days. The broad motor and behavioral assessment, biochemical measures and histology favored the extract according to the authors. The abstract gives no separately measured resting-tremor result, analyzed counts or confidence intervals. The intervention is a concentrated fruit extract. Evidence on ordinary raw fig intake in human Parkinson disease remains open.
