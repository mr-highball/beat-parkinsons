Round 1 full publication review. The archive retains the focal exposure and outcome boundaries for each queued claim.

The study compares a multiherb dose and dopaminergic medication in a rodent parkinsonism model. Sunflower oil is the injected vehicle, not a separately assigned oral dietary exposure. Open-field and rotarod show motor task variation. Narrative observations of acute rigidity and balance do not give an independent dietary oil effect.
