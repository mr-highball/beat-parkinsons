Title: A Review on Response to Device-Aided Therapies Used in Monogenic Parkinsonism and GBA Variants Carriers: A Need for Guidelines and Comparative Studies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9535575/
PMID: 35662127
PMCID: PMC9535575
DOI: 10.3233/JPD-212986
Publication date: 2022-05-30
Date basis: first public pre-press date in original publisher JATS; issue release 2022-09-02
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The review compares diverse device therapies and motor responses in genetic Parkinson series. A cited case reports intermittent visual hallucinations in a device-treatment setting. No included dried fig exposure or food-timed episode count was identified.
