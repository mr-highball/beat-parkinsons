Title: Exploring antioxidative, cytotoxic and neuropharmacological insights into Bixa orellana leaves: Experimental and in silico approaches.
PMID: 38468966
PMCID: PMC10926075
DOI: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e27001
First published: 2024
Authors: Sarkar, Mitra, Aktaruzzaman, Abid, Rahman
Journal: Heliyon
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38468966/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse neuropharmacological study (n=5 mice/group) testing oral methanol (MBOL) and n-hexane (NBOL) extracts of Bixa orellana leaves (200 and 400 mg/kg). Both extracts significantly increased elevated plus maze open-arm duration (P < 0.05) and hole-board head dipping (MBOL 400 mg/kg: 53.90 +/- 1.16), while significantly depressing open-field and hole-cross locomotion across observation intervals (P < 0.05; MBOL 400 mg/kg hole-cross 3.60 +/- 0.40 at 120 min and 2.40 +/- 0.51 at 180 min), indicating concurrent CNS depressant/sedative activity. Limits: different plant part (leaves, not seeds), small n=5/group, sedation confounds exploration.
