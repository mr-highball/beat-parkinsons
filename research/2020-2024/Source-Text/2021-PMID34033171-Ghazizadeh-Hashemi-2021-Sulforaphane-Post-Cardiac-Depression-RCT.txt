Title: Efficacy and safety of sulforaphane for treatment of mild to moderate depression in patients with history of cardiac interventions: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial.
PMID: 34033171
DOI: 10.1111/pcn.13276
First published: 2021
Authors: Ghazizadeh-Hashemi F, Bagheri S, Ashraf-Ganjouei A, Moradi K, Shahmansouri N, Mehrpooya M
Journal: Psychiatry and clinical neurosciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34033171/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Six-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in n=66 randomized (n=60 completed: 30 sulforaphane vs 30 placebo) adults with history of cardiac intervention (PCI/CABG) and mild-to-moderate depression. Repeated-measures analysis showed significantly greater Hamilton Rating Scale for Depression (HAM-D) improvement with sulforaphane vs placebo (p<0.001) and higher >=50% response rate at week 6 (30% vs 6.67%, p=0.042; remission HAM-D<=7: 23.33% vs 3.33%, p=0.052) with similar side-effect frequency. Limits: isolated sulforaphane supplement in non-PD post-cardiac depression.
