Archive scope: Factual extract from selected abstracts within the original research and education poster collection. Member designs and endpoints stay separate.
Reviewed: 2026-11-19

- P-14-005, Maugeri and colleagues, used a combined cassia bark and turmeric-root extract in amyloid-challenged THP-1 cells. Reported reductions in reactive oxygen species, cytokines and pathway phosphorylation are laboratory results from the same study family as the 2023 article. Neither ingredient is isolated by the blend comparison, and the cell system models Alzheimer-related inflammation. See 2023-PMID38138438-Cinnamon-Turmeric-Cell-Extract.txt. P-14-016 tested a separate multi-herb mixture containing cassia in an atopic dermatitis mouse model. P-14-034 tested cinnamon-derived fractions in colorectal cell lines.
- P-14-015, Kaftan Ocal and colleagues, pretreated SH-SY5Y cells with olive leaf extracts or isolated oleuropein before erastin challenge. The abstract reports better viability at 100 micrograms/mL for each tested leaf cultivar and at 10 micromolar for oleuropein compared with erastin (p<.05). The intervention is leaf extract or purified compound. This is one cellular viability assay, without oral olive oil, human participants or anxiety ratings. The congress preview repeats the abstract. P-14-024 tested hydroxytyrosol in mercury-challenged human erythrocytes, and P-14-026 tested a Picual extra-virgin oil extract in endothelial cells. These are separate studies.
- P-15-013 tested hydromethanolic saffron tepal extracts in antioxidant assays and computer docking. The samples are discarded floral material. The results give neither a ground-stigma exposure nor a depression comparison. P-09-014 examined essential oils recovered from sweet-orange peel and albedo. Waste citrus and spent coffee grounds appear as separate materials in P-09-007.
- P-14-033 used mass spectrometry to characterize green Arabica bean tissues. P-09-023 used roasted coffee waste as a microbial feedstock. Neither measures fatigue. P-07-009 or P-14-003 used grape pomace or grape plant extracts. P-18-008 examined isolated resveratrol and biomarkers in old rat skeletal muscle. P-14-013 paired isolated food compounds with purified ovalbumin or other substrates in digestive-enzyme assays.
- P-14-021 and P-15-007 tested hydrolyzed oat globulins or oat-derived phenolics in model systems. P-31-019 used banana lectin in allergy-related work. P-20-006 tested lipase on coconut oil. P-15-016 exposed colorectal cells to wild strawberry extract. P-09-011 used tomato-pomace pectin in a material assay, and P-14-020 used lycopene in cell models. P-09-018 names barley as a prospective plant assay model without a grain intervention result.

These abstracts establish preparation or component leads. No member above tested the frozen culinary ingredient against its named Parkinson outcome.
