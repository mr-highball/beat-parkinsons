Title: Watermelon and others plant foods that trigger headache in migraine patients.
PMID: 33892616
DOI: 10.1080/00325481.2021.1922211
First published: 2021
Authors: Silva-Néto RP, de Almeida Soares A, Augusto Carvalho de Vasconcelos C, da Silva Lopes L
Journal: Postgrad Med
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33892616/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Cross-sectional clinical interview survey (Silva-Neto et al., 2021, Headache, n=3,935 migraine patients) assessing self-reported plant food triggers of acute headache. Only 0.43% (17/3,935) named cucumber as a migraine trigger (far below watermelon at 29.5%). Observational non-PD headache trigger survey without pickled preparation specification or controlled provocation.
