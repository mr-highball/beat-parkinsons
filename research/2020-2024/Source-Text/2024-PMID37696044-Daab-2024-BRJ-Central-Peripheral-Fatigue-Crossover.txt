Title: Chronic beetroot juice supplementation attenuates neuromuscular fatigue etiology during simulated soccer match play.
PMID: 37696044
DOI: 10.1139/apnm-2023-0179
First published: 2024
Authors: Daab W, Zghal F, Nassis GP, Rebai H, Moalla W, Bouzid MA
Journal: Applied physiology, nutrition, and metabolism = Physiologie appliquee, nutrition et metabolisme
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37696044/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=13 soccer players taking BRJ vs placebo for 7 days: significantly attenuated both central (voluntary activation) and peripheral neuromuscular fatigue after intermittent exercise (p<0.001). Limits: athletic non-PD sample.
