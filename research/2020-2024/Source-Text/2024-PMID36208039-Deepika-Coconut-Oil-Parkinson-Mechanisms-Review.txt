Title: Neuroprotective role of coconut oil for the prevention and treatment of Parkinson's disease: potential mechanisms of action.
PMID: 36208039
DOI: 10.1080/02648725.2022.2122296
First published: 2024
Authors: N P D, Kondengadan MS, Sweilam SH, Rahman MH, Muhasina KM, Ghosh P, Bhargavi D, Palati DJ
Journal: Biotechnology & genetic engineering reviews
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36208039/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Narrative review (Deepika et al., 2024, Biotechnol Genet Eng Rev; online 2022) summarizing mechanistic and preclinical evidence for coconut oil (92% saturated fatty acids; ketone-body precursors) in oxidative stress, neuroinflammation, mitochondrial dysfunction, and apoptosis pathways relevant to Parkinson disease and Alzheimer disease. Context-only review (aligned with Subramaniyan et al. 2026 PMID:42154358 and Price & Ruppar 2023 PMID:38007248); confirms that no randomized clinical trial isolates whole refined or virgin coconut oil in diagnosed Parkinson disease.
