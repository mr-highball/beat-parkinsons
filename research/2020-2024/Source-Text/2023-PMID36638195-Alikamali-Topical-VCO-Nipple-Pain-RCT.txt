Title: Comparing the Efficacy of Breast Milk and Coconut Oil on Nipple Fissure and Breast Pain Intensity in Primiparous Mothers: A Single-Blind Clinical Trial.
PMID: 36638195
DOI: 10.1089/bfm.2022.0120
First published: 2023
Authors: Alikamali M, Emadi SF, Mahdizadeh M, Emami Z, Akbari H, Khodabandeh-Shahraki S
Journal: Breastfeeding medicine : the official journal of the Academy of Breastfeeding Medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36638195/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single-blind randomized clinical trial (Alikamali et al., 2023, Breastfeed Med; IRCT20190724044318N1) in 106 primiparous breastfeeding mothers with nipple fissures comparing topical virgin coconut oil (0.5 mL applied to skin 3x/day) vs topical breast milk. Topical VCO reduced nipple fissure scores (p=0.002 day 7, p<0.001 day 14) and topical nipple pain VAS (p<0.001 day 7, p=0.036 day 14), corroborated by Sahin et al. 2023 (PMID:36763614, n=90 topical postpartum nipple pain) and Yilmaz et al. 2025 (PMID:40858855, n=50 surgical palatal dressing). Limits: external topical application to injured skin/mucosa, unusable for oral dietary virgin coconut oil and Parkinson pain.
