Title: Neuroaspis PLP10, a nutritional formula rich in omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids with antioxidant vitamins including gamma-tocopherol in early Parkinson's disease: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
PMID: 34607196
DOI: 10.1016/j.clineuro.2021.106954
First published: 2021
Authors: Pantzaris M, Loukaides G, Paraskevis D, Kostaki EG, Patrikios I
Journal: Clinical Neurology and Neurosurgery
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34607196/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 5 review).

Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
30-month DB-RCT in 40 early Parkinson's disease patients comparing 20 mL/day Neuroaspis PLP10 (omega-3/omega-6 + antioxidant vitamins) vs 20 mL/day pure virgin olive oil control on UPDRS-III and Hoehn & Yahr. Neuroaspis delayed UPDRS progression vs virgin olive oil placebo (p <= 0.05). Because olive oil served only as an active lipid comparator without an oil-free arm or symptom-item breakdown, it provides no isolated olive oil efficacy sign.
