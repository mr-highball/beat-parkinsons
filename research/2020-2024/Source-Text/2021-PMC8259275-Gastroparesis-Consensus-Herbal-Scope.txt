Title: UEG and ESNM consensus on gastroparesis

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8259275/

Coverage: Factual extract of the complete consensus and herbal discussion after the therapeutic statements.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The consensus names ginger in a functional dyspepsia literature example and identifies a lack of data for those herbal options in gastroparesis. A separate 106-person herbal combination comparison failed to show a gastric-emptying change. These are different clinical conditions, exposures and endpoints from constipation severity. Orange labels describe consensus colors. No date-fruit comparison was identified.
