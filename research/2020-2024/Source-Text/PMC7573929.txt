Title: Neuroprotective Potency of Saffron Against Neuropsychiatric Diseases, Neurodegenerative Diseases, and Other Brain Disorders: From Bench to Bedside

The narrative review covers saffron and its constituents in models and clinical trials, with disease symptoms and medication side effects discussed separately. Fatigue and insomnia appear as aspects of mood disorders or their treatment; green tea catechins appear in a list of compounds with possible antidepressant effects. No standalone dried saffron fatigue, ground saffron insomnia or brewed green tea fatigue comparison was identified in this article.
