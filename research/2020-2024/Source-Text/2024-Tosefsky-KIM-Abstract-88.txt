KIM conference abstract on trial progress
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10854369/
PMCID: PMC10854369
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13966
Registration: NCT05469997
Coverage: 2024 conference supplement, complete numbered child abstract 88 only

The February 9, 2024 conference supplement includes the numbered child abstract 88 on NCT05469997. It describes the two eight-week randomized-order Mediterranean-ketogenic phases, washout, planned microbial and clinical measures, and trial status. The child reports that recruitment or study conduct was ongoing after an April 2023 start. It does not give clinical outcome estimates or a processing grade, dose or intake for olive oil. The PMCID and DOI identify the whole supplement, while the child number identifies the KIM report.
