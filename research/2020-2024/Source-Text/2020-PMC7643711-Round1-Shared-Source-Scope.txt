The scopes below refer to the indexed original and specify what bears on each frozen exposure. A scope exclusion leaves the efficacy question open.

Claim 0121 | PMID:33193868 | The panel reviews nickel in coffee and measurements of nickel handling with different beverage matrices. It reports no coffee-related Parkinson fatigue comparison.
Claim 0348 | PMID:33193868 | Chickens are the model for nickel toxicity after experimental diets. Chicken meat is not the administered food, and the reviewed chicken study does not measure Parkinson fatigue.
Claim 0382 | PMID:33193868 | Lentils are listed among naturally nickel-rich foods in discussion of systemic nickel allergy. The report does not measure anxiety after cooked lentils.
Claim 0502 | PMID:33193868 | Date is the cutoff for nickel data submissions. Dried date consumption is not part of a pain outcome here.
Claim 0874 | PMID:33193868 | An isolated rainbow trout intestinal preparation is used to study uptake interactions. It supplies tissue-specific exposure context without a human consumption or mood comparison.
Claim 0878 | PMID:33193868 | Orange juice is a vehicle in a nickel absorption comparison. The readout is nickel absorption rather than clinical constipation after raw orange.
