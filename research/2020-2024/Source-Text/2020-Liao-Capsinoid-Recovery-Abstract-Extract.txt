Liao and colleagues’ 2020 journal abstract reports a nine-person crossover comparing 12 mg of isolated capsinoids and placebo after cycling with the same meal. The authors report higher fat oxidation and inconclusive glycogen restoration. The 2018 preprint and 2019 registry have markedly similar sample and protocol details.
The journal appears in a 2021 issue. The original full body remains open, including direct registry citation and any outcomes absent from the abstract. The accessible abstract supplies no subjective fatigue comparison. Treat the preprint, registry and journal as one likely cohort pending an explicit identifier.
