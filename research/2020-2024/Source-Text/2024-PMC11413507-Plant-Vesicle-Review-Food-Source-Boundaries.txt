Title: Applications of plant-derived extracellular vesicles in medicine
PMID: 39309692
PMCID: PMC11413507
DOI: 10.1002/mco2.741
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11413507/
Date basis: 2024-09-20 original publication.
Coverage: Factual extract of the complete primary publication and relevant ingredient contexts.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The review describes isolated vesicle preparations from blueberry, carrot, ginger and other plants. It cites different cell, animal and registered clinical contexts. The constipation example involves broccoli-derived particles in a mouse model, not ginger or dietary broccoli. The grape oral mucositis and ginger or aloe polycystic ovary syndrome registrations concern separate exposures and outcomes. The latter registry was withdrawn with no enrollment.
