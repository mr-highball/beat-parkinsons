Source: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlepdf/2023/fo/d2fo01835g
DOI: 10.1039/d2fo01835g
Format: Original methods and results, paraphrased.
Design: Seventy adults with diarrhea randomized to water-extracted Cinnamomum cassia or placebo for eight weeks.
Outcome: In Table 2, mean transit in hours was 19.2 at baseline and 21.7 at follow-up with extract, and 25.6 and 15.6 with placebo. Treatment-by-time p=.019. This was a transit measure in diarrhea, not a constipation severity estimate.
