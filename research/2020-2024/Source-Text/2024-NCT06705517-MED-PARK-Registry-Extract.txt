MED-PARK registry and planned bowel outcomes
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT06705517
Registration: NCT06705517
Coverage: Official registry first post and current status and outcome fields

MED-PARK first posted on November 26, 2024. The registry plans 44 participants assigned to a six-month modified Mediterranean diet or their usual diet. The primary comparison is change in the combined MDS-UPDRS Parts I and II. Secondary outcomes include Gastrointestinal Symptom Rating Scale constipation and constipation-related quality of life. The retrieved record still lists recruiting from its July 11, 2025 update, with no posted results as checked November 16, 2026. The last registry update predates its estimated completion date. The linked 2025 protocol describes the dietary advice.

Registry recheck, 2026-11-24
The official record still displays the July 11, 2025 update, RECRUITING status and no posted results. Its February 2026 primary completion and September 2026 study completion entries are estimates. The estimated dates do not establish completed recruitment or results. The registry and published protocol provide no separately assigned olive-oil grade or constipation estimate.
