Round 1 affected claim: 0128, fresh rosemary and depressive symptom severity.

The observational comparison assessed 41 people with Parkinson disease, 41 people with HIV and 37 controls. Depression and anxiety were measured separately. The supplement reports Parkinson models of quality of life, instrumental daily activity, physical daily activity and motor-related daily activity. In the Parkinson quality-of-life model, adding the mood step increased model R-squared by .28 (p < .001), and the Beck Depression Inventory II predictor was significant (p < .001). In the Parkinson instrumental daily activity model, the additional mood step gave p=.15. In the Parkinson physical activity model and motor-related daily-activity model the mood-step p-values were .43 and .94, respectively.

The article's author list includes Rosemary Fama. The abstract and analyzed supplemental models provide no assessed rosemary intake, intervention, or direction of effect from eating rosemary. These results concern associations with depression scores and functioning. The main original article and full model reporting remain needed for the exact source review.
