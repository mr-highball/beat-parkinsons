Title: Lavender essential oil on postmenopausal women with insomnia: Double-blind randomized trial.
PMID: 33905827
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2021.102726
First published: 2021
Authors: Dos Reis Lucena L, Dos Santos-Junior JG, Tufik S, Hachul H
Journal: Complement Ther Med
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33905827/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial (Dos Reis Lucena et al., 2021, Complement Ther Med, RBR-5q5t5z) in 35 postmenopausal women with clinical insomnia inhaling Lavandula angustifolia oil (n=17) vs sunflower oil placebo (n=18) for 29 days alongside sleep hygiene. Primary between-group PSQI sleep quality was NULL (p=0.22), with a non-significant WASO trend (p=0.07). Non-oral inhalation route and null primary endpoint.
