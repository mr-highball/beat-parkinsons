Current exact disposition: related
Olive oil is discussed as a Mediterranean pattern ingredient and in a Korean adaptation. The Thai proposal's projected gastrointestinal benefit is not an observation comparing culinary olive oil with a control.

Claim 0111 | PMID:37334427 | toasted cumin | swallowing difficulty severity
Current exact disposition: related
Cumin is one of several spices in the Indo-Mediterranean pattern discussed, not a randomized ingredient in the Thai proposal. No measured cumin-specific Parkinson swallowing or bowel comparison is reported.

Claim 0225 | PMID:37334427 | grated turmeric | swallowing difficulty severity
Current exact disposition: related
The viewpoint describes turmeric in diet patterns and claims dyspepsia context. The cited primary trial assessed isolated curcumin added to famotidine for functional dyspepsia, a different exposure and endpoint from Parkinson swallowing difficulty.
