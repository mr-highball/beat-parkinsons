Title: Effect of two oral formulations of cannabidiol on responses to emotional stimuli in healthy human volunteers: pharmaceutical vehicle matters.
PMID: 34076067
PMCID: PMC8827362
DOI: 10.1590/1516-4446-2020-1684
First published: 2022
Authors: Crippa JA, Pereira Junior LC, Pereira LC, Zimmermann PM, Brum Junior L, Rechia LM et al.
Journal: Braz J Psychiatry
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8827362/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized placebo-controlled trial (Crippa et al., 2022, PMC8827362) testing two oral CBD formulations dissolved in corn oil versus corn-oil placebo on anxiety and facial emotion recognition in healthy adults. Neither formulation altered facial emotion recognition or subjective anxiety versus corn-oil vehicle; evaluates recipient emotion perception rather than PD hypomimia and uses corn oil solely as vehicle.
