Title: USDA ARS project records for strawberry supplementation and gait among older adults with obesity
Source: https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/project/?accnNo=440957
Companion source: https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/project/?accnNo=442237
Date basis: official project start dates in 2021. The primary public posting dates for these pages are unspecified.
Coverage: selected project descriptions and registry reconciliation, retrieved 2026-11-19 UTC.

USDA ARS accession 440957 (8050-51000-102-013-T, 2021-09-01 to 2024-01-31) and 442237 (8050-51000-102-015-A, 2021-10-01 to 2024-01-01) describe the same-named collaboration. Participants were to be older adults with obesity and elevated glycemic risk, given freeze-dried strawberry or control powder for about three months. The mobility description specifies gait while performing a challenging cognitive task. Both pages describe the proposed design and offer no numerical gait results.

The title, intervention, duration, setting and timing match the NCT03162913 study family. This is an inference from the concordance rather than an explicit cross-identifier on either project page. The two project records are not independent cohorts. The official ClinicalTrials.gov record checked 2026-11-19 reports completed enrollment of 47, preferred treadmill gait speed as a secondary outcome, no results posted and four references all labeled background. The 1999 Joseph rat study is one of those references and its motor result was specific to blueberry.

The 2021 Miller publication in healthy older adults dates to a distinct cohort, NCT02051140. Its mobility results should not be used as the missing NCT03162913 results. The 2024 industry collaborator summary listed the obesity study as in process and supplies no outcome.
