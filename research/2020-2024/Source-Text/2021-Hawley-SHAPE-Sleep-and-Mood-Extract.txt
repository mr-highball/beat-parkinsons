SHAPE follow-up sleep and mood outcomes
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8182110/
PMCID: PMC8182110
DOI: 10.1093/cdn/nzab041_014
Registration: NCT03603041
Coverage: Original June 7, 2021 conference report; timing and outcome results checked

The 2021 report describes 39 postmenopausal women across five groups, including seven receiving whey protein with 4.1 g/day soybean oil. It reports a time effect for subjective sleep. The reported global sleep-score changes were minus 20.2% for the whey-plus-oil group and minus 30.3% for whey alone. The abstract calls the between-group sleep evidence inconclusive and supplies no adjusted oil-specific contrast. The companion body-composition abstract uses the same participants and formulation. These are multiple records from the SHAPE trial, without a confirmed corn-oil or Parkinson insomnia comparison.
