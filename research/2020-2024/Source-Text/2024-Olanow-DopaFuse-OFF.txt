DopaFuse micropump and reported OFF time
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38698639/
selected verified methods and findings

First published May 2, 2024. A distinct 16-person open study reported day-15 OFF time lower by 1.72 hours (SE 0.37, p = .0004) versus conventional tablet treatment. The original device report identifies the paste vehicle as Miglyol 812 N medium chain triglyceride. Multiple aspects of the drug administration change concurrently; this comparison does not supply an isolated culinary oil effect.
