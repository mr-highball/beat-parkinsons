Title: Japanese Parkinson insurance claims and prescribing study
PMID: 37426443
PMCID: PMC10327598
DOI: 10.3389/fneur.2023.1162016
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10327598/
First publication: 2023-06-23
Coverage: Original NCBI PMC JATS article body checked and summarized.

Participants entered the retrospective study at a diagnosis or treatment index date defined in insurance claims. Outcomes were prescribing patterns, readouts such as drug regimen complexity, and coded comorbidities. Pain is discussed among nonmotor indications that may be treated with drugs. The article does not report a date-fruit exposure or patient-rated pain change. Calendar index date describes cohort time.
