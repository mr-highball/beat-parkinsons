Registry for a mixed polyphenol supplement
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05829382
Coverage: Factual summary of the official registry.

First posted April 25, 2023. The program tests a mixed supplement that lists blueberry, kale, rosemary and grapefruit among ingredients. Its assigned outcomes are blood biomarkers, including CD38 and NAD-related measures, inflammatory markers and oxidative measures. It supplies no separate food arms or voice, smell, rest tremor or stride outcomes. The registry lists completed and no structured results. A linked journal paper reports selected biomarker results.
