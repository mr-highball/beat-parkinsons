Verified Round 5 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
In vitro cell-only study: 50 nM anthraquinone isolated from Pleurotus ostreatus attenuated 6-OHDA cytotoxicity and ROS in human SH-SY5Y neuroblastoma cells. Cell-line assay with isolated compound only (population_fit = unusable for human symptom inference); no mammalian in vivo PD motor or clinical outcome. Paired with Cilerdzic 2019 (PMID:31002611, in vitro AChE/tyrosinase inhibition) and Araujo-Rodrigues 2025 (PMC12735695, HMC3 microglia and C. elegans A-beta/Tau paralysis).
