Title: Hypertension treatment with Combretum micranthum or Hibiscus sabdariffa, as decoction or tablet: a randomized clinical trial
PMID: 32948827
DOI: 10.1038/s41371-020-00415-1
First published: 2021
Authors: Bourqui A, Niang EAB, Graz B, Diop EA, Dahaba M, Thiaw I, Soumare K, Valmaggia P et al.
Journal: Journal of Human Hypertension
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32948827/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Multicentric 6-month randomized clinical trial in Senegal enrolling 219 uncomplicated hypertensive adults (SBP 140-180 and/or DBP 90-110 mmHg) across 5 arms including Hibiscus sabdariffa (bissap) brew (10 g dried calyx/day), bissap tablets (2x375 mg/day), kinkeliba brew/tablets, and captopril (2x50 mg/day). Herbal brews lowered SBP by -20.7 +/- 15.1 mmHg (vs -19.7 +/- 16.7 mmHg for captopril, P<0.001 from baseline), with 75% of bissap participants achieving >=10 mmHg SBP drop and 49% reaching <140/90 mmHg (vs 40% captopril). Authors specifically cautioned that future studies should consider starting brews at lower doses. No orthostatic tilt testing or PD outcomes.
