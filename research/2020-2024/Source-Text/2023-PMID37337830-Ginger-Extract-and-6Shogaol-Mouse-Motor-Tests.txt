Title: Heated ginger extract and isolated 6-shogaol in a mouse motor experiment

Inspected extent: Original preparation description, Figure 1 and results, intestinal and brain measures and references.

The investigators contrasted a heated ginger rhizome extract and
isolated 6-shogaol in separate oral treatment arms in a Parkinson-like
mouse model. Figure 1 reports eight animals per group. Both treated
groups had shorter pole-descent times than model controls. The report
specifically describes improvement in open-field distance and rotarod
retention in the extract group.

The extract came from heated, dried ginger processed with ethanol.
Its exposure differs from fresh grated or ground culinary ginger.
The experiment reports intestinal barrier and neuropathology markers
alongside task performance. It supplies no separate passive rigidity
score, stride length or bowel symptom rating and no clinical
Parkinson comparison. The original therefore adds motor-model context
to claim 0626 without a directional assessment for that claim.
