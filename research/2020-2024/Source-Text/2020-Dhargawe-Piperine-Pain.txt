Isolated oral piperine and experimental pain
https://www.phcogres.com/sites/default/files/PharmacognRes-12-2-176.pdf
Selected methods and findings paraphrase

Dhargawe and colleagues published independent experimental comparisons in 2020. In a six-rat-per-arm tail-flick experiment, orally administered isolated piperine at 50 mg/kg yielded 6.71 ± 0.26 seconds at 60 minutes compared with 2.40 ± 0.17 seconds with gum acacia (reported p = .004). Other tests differ in species and method. This is a reflex after a concentrated isolated constituent, with no culinary pepper, Parkinson population or pain severity scale.
