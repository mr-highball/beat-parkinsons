Title: A Standardized Withania somnifera (Linn.) Root Extract with Piperine Alleviates the Symptoms of Anxiety and Depression by Increasing Serotonin Levels: A Double-Blind, Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Study.
PMID: 37878284
DOI: 10.1089/jicm.2023.0279
First published: 2024
Authors: Majeed M, Nagabhushanam K, Murali A, Vishwanathan DT, Mamidala RV, Mundkur L
Journal: Journal of integrative and complementary medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37878284/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

90-day double-blind placebo-controlled RCT (CTRI/2022/05/042640, n=70) of 500 mg Ashwagandha root extract + 5 mg piperine vs placebo in adults with mild-to-moderate anxiety and depression. Improved HARS, HDRS, GSQS sleep, and QoL (p<0.001). Bundled with Ashwagandha; cannot attribute to piperine or black pepper alone.
