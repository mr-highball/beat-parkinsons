Isfahan registry and the planned MDS-UPDRS items
Primary source: https://irct.ir/trial/75378
Coverage: factual extract of the registry and protocol.

The official IRCT20121216011763N61 record <https://irct.ir/trial/75378> describes 92 PD participants, 100 mg saffron daily versus 100 mg lactose for 12 weeks, and prespecified broad motor/nonmotor symptoms with MDS-UPDRS. It is already cataloged in `research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md#iranian-registration-of-the-saffron-powder-trial`. The later 2025 protocol <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2024-093167> specifies first-class Sargol powder from Mojtahedi, 100 mg tablet, and planned HPLC crocin checks before and after tablet preparation. Sargol is dried red stigma grade; powder in a fixed tablet is close to, but not identical to, unconstrained culinary use. Planned testing does not establish actual batch composition.

The protocol names all four MDS-UPDRS parts. The corresponding results paper (first online 24 December 2025, DOI 10.1039/D5FO01924A) reports broad outcomes in its abstract, including PDQ-39 mobility and daily-activity domains, but no accessible item-specific speech, drooling, smell or fall counts. A mobility-domain change is not a fall-frequency result. The full original table and public individual data were not retrieved. Registration, protocol and results describe one study family.
