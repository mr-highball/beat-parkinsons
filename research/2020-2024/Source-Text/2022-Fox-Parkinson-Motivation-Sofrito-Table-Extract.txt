Parkinson diet patterns motivation and pooled sofrito

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9739738/
Coverage: Original Table 2 and Supplementary Table S4
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

Published December 6, 2022. In the Parkinsonism survey, Mediterranean and MIND scores were associated with lower self-rated loss of motivation. The estimates per index point were −1.39 and −2.46. The pooled sofrito question accepts tomato sauce and may cover several vegetables. Its comparison on the overall symptom score was inconclusive, with adjusted p=.5. It does not resolve braised leek intake or a leek-specific apathy outcome.
