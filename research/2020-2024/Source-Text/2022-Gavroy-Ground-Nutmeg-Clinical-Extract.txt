Title: Intoxication à la noix de muscade, une épice méconnue
Source: https://stm.cairn.info/article/FMU_122_0119/pdf?lang=fr
DOI: 10.3166/afmu-2022-0380
Original issue: Annales françaises de médecine d'urgence 2022;12(2):119–121
Cairn digital posting: 2025-03-26
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated complete original case review

An adult without recorded neurological history presented after
intentional excess ingestion of ground nutmeg. The clinical account
puts symptom onset approximately six hours after exposure and
presentation about seven hours after exposure. The neurological
exam describes slightly unsteady gait without a focal finding.
It does not document an observed tremor or separately assess
rest-tremor severity. The exposure and population differ from the
ordinary culinary Parkinson claim.
