Title: Linalool and Geraniol Defend Neurons from Oxidative Stress, Inflammation, and Iron Accumulation in In Vitro Parkinson's Models.
PMID: 39199163
PMCID: PMC11351228
DOI: 10.3390/antiox13080917
First published: 2024
Authors: Pandur E, Major B, Rák T, Sipos K, Csutak A, Horváth G
Journal: Antioxidants (Basel, Switzerland)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39199163/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

In vitro laboratory study (Pandur et al., 2024, Antioxidants, PMC11351228) exposing retinoic-acid-differentiated SH-SY5Y cells to rotenone and 6-OHDA with isolated linalool (major Lavandula angustifolia monoterpene) and geraniol compared with rasagiline. Linalool lowered ROS, IL-6, IL-8, IL-1beta, and modulated iron-metabolism genes (TfR1, HO-1, ferroportin). Limits: isolated compound in cell culture only; no animal or human PD symptom outcomes.
