Title: Tributyrin reverses the deleterious effect of saturated fat on working memory and synaptic plasticity in juvenile mice: differential effects in males and females
Publication date: 2024-06-07 (online first)
PMID: 38852700
DOI: 10.1016/j.ejphar.2024.176726
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38852700/

Text coverage: Distilled original methods, Figure 1 and the eight-week comparisons in Figures 3 and 4.

The 40% high-oleic sunflower formulation and palm-kernel formulation were each mixed into standard chow. After eight weeks, both diets were associated with poorer spatial learning and memory in the eight-arm radial maze in male and female mice. The two-hour comparison in the Y-maze instead highlighted poorer performance after palm-kernel oil in males. The chronic result concerns a distinct task and both high-fat diets, with neither a normal-fat sunflower arm nor a matched-energy control for isolating sunflower oil. The test concerns ordinary mice exposed to concentrated diets, without verification of cold pressing or human Parkinson executive function.
