CPO-9 reports a 30-person uncontrolled ginger-extract comparison in pregnancy nausea and vomiting. Four participants reported heartburn. Its first author, gestational range, comparator structure, treatment length and adverse-event count match a 2017 full paper at DOI 10.18203/2319-2003.ijbcp20173273. Treat them as one likely family. Other ginger abstracts concern oral formulations, uses as one of several compounds, and distinct animal outcomes.

Additional members study anise essential oil, green tea as a co-exposure in animals, Avena sativa seed extract, grape-seed extract, banana in an extract comparison, and tomato juice as an assay input. One curcumin abstract describes it among compounds. Coffee appears as a source of a compound. The brine shrimp are assay organisms rather than a seafood intervention. Kale occurs as a surname. There is no identified date-palm exposure. The endpoints and preparations remain distinct by original member.
