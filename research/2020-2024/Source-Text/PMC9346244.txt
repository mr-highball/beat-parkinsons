Title: Deep Brain Stimulation of the Nucleus Basalis of Meynert for Parkinson's Disease Dementia: A 36 Months Follow Up Study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9346244/
PMID: 35937485
PMCID: PMC9346244
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13510
Publication date: 2022-07-27
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

Five of six participants from the earlier basal forebrain stimulation trial enter long-term follow-up. Individual NPI hallucination scores include improvement, no change and worsening. This open longitudinal phase reuses the 2017 crossover cohort and has no dried fig exposure.

At the 36-month visit, a caregiver reported fewer visual hallucinations in one participant and no change in another. Two had higher NPI hallucination scores. These are descriptive results without a long-term sham comparator. The earlier double-blind crossover is NCT01701544 and the main report is PMC5838617.
