Four-week single-arm study of Yokukansankachimpihange in dementia with Lewy bodies, with 11 of 13 participants analyzed. The multi-herb formula contains dried Citrus unshiu peel (chimpi). Patients or bed partners reported no falls associated with oversedation or muscle relaxation. There was no prespecified all-cause fall-frequency comparison, raw sweet-orange exposure, isolated peel arm or separate diagnosed Parkinson cohort.
