Title: Granola, EEG sleep and insomnia questionnaires
First published or posted: 2024-01-08
Registry ID: UMIN000053189
Source URL: https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000060703
Text type: original-source evidence summary

First publicly disclosed January 8, 2024. The single-arm protocol planned 27 adults with stress and insomnia tendency consuming 50 g test cereal at breakfast daily. EEG sleep measurements were primary. Athens Insomnia Scale, Epworth daytime sleepiness and OSA-MA were secondary. The English eligibility text refers to women despite a broader sex field. The 2025 linked publication reports 12 men and 15 women. The registration and PMID 40181940 are one study.
