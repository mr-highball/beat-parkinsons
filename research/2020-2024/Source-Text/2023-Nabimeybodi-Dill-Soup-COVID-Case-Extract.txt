Nabimeybodi et al., 2023. Persian Medicine for the Treatment of Severe COVID-19: a Case Report.
Source: https://publish.kne-publishing.com/index.php/APTJ/article/download/12488/11839/
Access and coverage: Publisher PDF, case presentation, soup preparation and day-by-day table, printed pp. 24–26.
Reviewed: 2026-11-14 local date (America/New_York).

A 78-year-old man with severe COVID received multiple dietary and drug interventions. The soup directions list chopped Anethum graveolens and allow either fresh or dried. The table records soup intake but does not identify the herb form actually consumed. Combined weakness and lethargy was 9/10 before the advice, 5/10 on March 13 and about 8/10 on March 15, with fatigue again listed March 20. There was no isolated dill intervention or comparison. Dryness or stickiness of the throat appeared in the report and salivation or drooling was not scored.
