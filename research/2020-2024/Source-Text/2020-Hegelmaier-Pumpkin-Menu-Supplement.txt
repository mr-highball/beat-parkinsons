Figure S2 displays total UPDRS III scores before and after the 14-day intervention. The caption describes a dotted trace for the vegetarian diet plus enema and a solid trace for vegetarian diet alone. Its axes and legend are total motor scores, not transcripts or separate endpoint observations. The other supplementary figures concern bacterial abundance and its relationship with cumulative levodopa dose. Appendix A in the main article lists pumpkin seed oil alongside other oils in the menu. No source note or figure identifies its oil grade, actual consumption, or individual hand scores. Part III is motor examination. Dyskinesia, saliva and handwriting are distinct outcomes, and the study does not report them as quantified comparisons. The experiment provides clinical diet context and no ingredient-level contrast.
