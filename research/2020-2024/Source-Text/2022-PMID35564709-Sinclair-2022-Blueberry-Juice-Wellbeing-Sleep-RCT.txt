Title: Effects of Montmorency Tart Cherry and Blueberry Juice on Cardiometabolic and Other Health-Related Outcomes: A Three-Arm Placebo Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 35564709
PMCID: PMC9103925
DOI: 10.3390/ijerph19095317
First published: 2022
Authors: Sinclair J, Bottoms L, Dillon S, Allan R, Shadwell G, Butters B
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9103925/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Three-arm double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in 45 randomized (44 completed) healthy adults comparing 60 mL/day blueberry concentrate, tart cherry concentrate, or placebo for 20 days. Primary SBP was null (p>0.05), but blueberry reduced total/LDL cholesterol and improved psychological indices vs placebo adjusted for baseline: BDI (b=1.90, 95% CI 0.09-3.72, p=0.041), STAI state anxiety (b=5.76, p=0.018), STAI trait anxiety (b=7.18, p=0.023), and COOP WONCA (b=0.31, p=0.019). Epworth Sleepiness Scale, PSQI, and ISI sleep outcomes showed no significant benefit. Limits: juice concentrate rather than raw/frozen fruit; small 3-arm non-PD sample (~15/arm).
