Title: Short-term cannabidiol with delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol in Parkinson disease: a randomized trial
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11102313/
PMCID: PMC11102313
PMID: 38487964
DOI: 10.1002/mds.29768
First public article date: 2024-03-15
Accessed: 2026-11-17
Text coverage: Curated original Methods and Table 2

In NCT03582137, Liu and colleagues randomized 61 people with
Parkinson disease to a cannabinoid formulation or placebo. Both
groups received sesame oil. The clinical MDS-UPDRS rest tremor
item sum (3.17+3.18) changed by −1.16 in the drug group and −0.10
in the placebo group. The drug-minus-placebo difference was
−1.06 points (95% CI −2.14 to 0.02, p=.055). The sensor-derived
rest tremor comparison was also inconclusive. Results for the
primary motor total are separate from the rest-tremor score.

Shared sesame oil prevents separating its effect. This study
provides no oral peanut-, soybean- or corn-oil exposure comparison.
