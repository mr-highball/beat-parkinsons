Title: Pharmacological update of mirtazapine: a narrative literature review

The narrative review discusses uses of mirtazapine and individual reports of drug-related perceptual changes, including a visual hallucination case. These reports concern medication effects and provide no dried fig exposure or recurrent visual episode-rate comparison.
