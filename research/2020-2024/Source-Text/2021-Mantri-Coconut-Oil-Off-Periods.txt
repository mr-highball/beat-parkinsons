Scope: Factual methods and Table 3 extract.

Fox Insight participants who reported Parkinson medication OFF periods completed an online survey. The analysis includes 2,110 respondents, and 1,416 of 2,106 respondents to the coping-strategies question described strategies. Six free-text descriptions were assigned to the entire supplement category in Table 3. Examples in that category include coconut oil along with several distinct products. The publication gives no separate coconut-oil count or dose, refining method, comparison, or daily OFF-time measurement linked to its use. The survey characterizes participant-reported coping practices.
