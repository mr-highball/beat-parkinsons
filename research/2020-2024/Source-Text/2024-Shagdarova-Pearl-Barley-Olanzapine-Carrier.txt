Pearl barley as a shared carrier in an olanzapine experiment

PMID: 39457671
PMCID: PMC11504887
DOI: 10.3390/biomedicines12102358
Coverage: Original Methods and Figure 6 in public JATS, factual text

Shagdarova and colleagues published October 16, 2024. Female mice received pearl barley porridge with peanut oil as the carrier for control, olanzapine or olanzapine plus one of three experimental additives. The open-field test counted sector crossings and rearings under light and dark conditions. The paper presents these as locomotor measures and gives comparisons of the additives. All groups shared the porridge. No separate barley, peanut-oil or clinical anxiety effect was estimated.
