Title: Salvia plebeia and rosmarinic acid in dexamethasone-related muscle atrophy in cultured myotubes

Checked: 2026-11-25

Kim and colleagues compared an ethanol extract of Salvia plebeia and isolated rosmarinic acid in a C2C12 myotube model of dexamethasone-induced atrophy. The report describes better preservation of myotube measures and changes in measured atrophy-related proteins and signaling in treated cells. This is annual sage, a distinct species from common culinary sage Salvia officinalis. The extracted and isolated materials and the cell atrophy endpoints provide no fresh, dried or ground culinary sage comparison for clinical pain, insomnia or Parkinson muscle rigidity.

The original is cited by the 2026 review PMC13252942. The compound link provides laboratory context for Round 1 claims 0025, 0060 and 0370 without a symptom-direction estimate.
