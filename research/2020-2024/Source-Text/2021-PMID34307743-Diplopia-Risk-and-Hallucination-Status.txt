Title: Prevalence and Risk Factors for Double Vision in Parkinson Disease.
PMID: 34307743
PMCID: PMC8287164
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13220
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8287164/
First publication date: 2021-04-22 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: Official PMC complete article page, including body and displayed study observations.

A Parkinson registry analysis compares reported double vision and associated patient characteristics. The nonmotor questionnaire asks about auditory or visual hallucinations as a combined presence outcome. Its observed association with diplopia does not identify diet, dried figs or frequency after exposure.
