Achour M et al. Rosemary tea consumption alters peripheral anxiety and depression biomarkers: A pilot study in limited healthy volunteers. J Am Nutr Assoc. 2022;41. First online 2021-02-10.
DOI: 10.1080/07315724.2021.1873871
PMID: 33565922
Primary source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33565922/
Coverage: factual extract of author abstract only.

Twenty-two healthy people drank an infusion of dried leaves for ten days. The reported BDNF and TNF-alpha plasma measures increased. The accessible abstract gives no concurrent control group or reported depressive-symptom scale. A dried-tea biomarker comparison cannot establish the fresh rosemary outcome in the frozen claim.
