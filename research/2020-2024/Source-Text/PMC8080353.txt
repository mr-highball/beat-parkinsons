Title: Timeline of Rapid Eye Movement Sleep Behavior Disorder in Overt Alpha-Synucleinopathies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8080353/
PMID: 33155696
PMCID: PMC8080353
DOI: 10.1002/ana.25952
Publication date: 2020-11-20
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: Population-based cohort on dream enactment chronology

Clinical records and polysomnography where available were used to classify REM sleep behavior disorder in diagnosed synucleinopathies. The endpoints are timing of dream enactment and diagnosed disease, distinct from waking visual episodes after ingestion.
