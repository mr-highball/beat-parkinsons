Title: Substantia nigra hyperechogenicity and brain ventricular size as biomarkers of early dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11475835/
PMID: 39407323
PMCID: PMC11475835
DOI: 10.1186/s13195-024-01590-w
Publication date: 2024-10-15
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The study evaluates substantia nigra echogenicity and ventricular size as possible early dementia with Lewy bodies markers. Sonographic markers and clinical diagnosis are the comparison. No dried fig ingestion or food-timed clinical symptom effect is measured.
