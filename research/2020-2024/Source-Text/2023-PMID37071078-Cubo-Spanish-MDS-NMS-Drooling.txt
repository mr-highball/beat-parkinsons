Title: Expanded and Independent Spanish Validation of the MDS-Non Motor Rating Scale
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10105117/
PMID: 37071078
PMCID: PMC10105117
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13658
Publication date: 2023-03-13
Date basis: electronic publication in original JATS
Source depth: factual extract of published methods, convergent-validity table and correction

Cubo and colleagues enrolled 364 patients with Parkinson disease, including 160 in Spain and 204 in the Americas, to validate the Spanish MDS-NMS instrument. In the convergent-validity table, the MDS-NMS gastrointestinal domain correlated with MDS-UPDRS Part II item 2.2, saliva and drooling, at Spearman r=0.50. The correlation against the separate swallowing item 2.3 was r=0.48.

These are correlations of a multidimensional symptom domain with individual independent scale items. They do not quantify change after any exposure. The 2024 erratum replaces a sentence on who the translation could reach and mentions no change to the table.
