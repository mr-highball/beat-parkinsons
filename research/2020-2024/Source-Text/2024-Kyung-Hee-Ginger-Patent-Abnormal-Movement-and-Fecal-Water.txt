Dried ginger extract and isolated shogaol in patent animal comparisons
May 16, 2024
https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2024101890A1/en
Factual extract from original WO patent disclosure, Examples 3 and 6–7, verified Tables 4 and 12.

In an inventor-reported 6-OHDA and levodopa mouse study, Example 6 records repeated axial, limb and orolingual abnormal involuntary movement scores. Table 12 reports AIM area under the curve (mean ± SE) of 276.75 ± 26.00 with levodopa, 144.00 ± 15.56 with dried-ginger extract, 169.58 ± 22.61 and 106.25 ± 12.02 in the two isolated shogaol arms, and 171.00 ± 13.67 with the amantadine positive control. The disclosed example does not establish animal sample size, administration route or masking. The priority-related later Korean publication identifies a dried-rhizome ethanol extract. The family disclosures reproduce this experiment and do not provide replication.

In a separate 6-OHDA mouse experiment, Example 3 reports fecal water of 57.31 ± 2.46% with levodopa and 69.54 ± 1.51% with levodopa plus isolated shogaol. KR20240067803A, published May 17, 2024, reproduces Table 4. This is a fecal water measure and the treatment in this experiment is the isolated constituent. The related Kim articles use a different model and the 2026 publication's bead-expulsion test differs from this endpoint. Animal and control overlap is unverified. Neither experiment estimates effects of the culinary preparation in Parkinson patients.
