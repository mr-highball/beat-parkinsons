Title: Tissues toxicity attenuation by vitamin E on oxidative damage induced by diazinon
Source: https://www.eaht.org/upload/pdf/eaht-37-4-e2022036.pdf
PMID: 36916049
PMCID: PMC10014748
DOI: 10.5620/eaht.2022036
Publication date: 2022-11-10
Text coverage: Distilled publisher paper including Methods and Results. Issue date December 2022.

The study allocated 30 male Wistar rats among five arms. It compared a corn-oil injection control, toxicant, vitamin E, and vitamin E administered before or after the toxicant. The Methods identify corn oil as a solvent for both administered compounds. Tissue and circulating markers were measured. No no-oil control, food exposure, Parkinson cohort or fall-frequency outcome was reported.

FEBS P26.55 describes a four-arm male Wistar liver experiment by overlapping investigators, with a corn-oil solvent control and vitamin E before exposure. This paper adds the postexposure treatment arm and more tissues. The overlap in design is sufficient to flag possible shared data and insufficient to establish the same animals. Do not count the documents as independent evidence for an oil effect.
