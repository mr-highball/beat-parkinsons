Title: Comparing the Effects of Consuming Almonds or Biscuits on Body Weight in Habitual Snackers: A 1-Year Randomized Controlled Trial.
PMID: 37156442
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2023.05.015
First published: 2023
Authors: Brown, Ware, Gray, Tey, Chisholm
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37156442/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

1-year pragmatic parallel RCT (ACTRN12618001758291) in 136 nonobese habitual discretionary snackers randomized to daily almonds (>=42.5 g/d or 10% TE) or isocaloric biscuits. No statistically significant between-group differences for body weight (P = 0.275), body composition, or other nondietary outcomes including sleep and physical activity (all P >= 0.112). Diet quality (MUFA, PUFA, fiber, vitamin E, magnesium) significantly improved (all P <= 0.033). Limits: healthy non-PD adults, secondary sleep measures null without printed sleep-scale means.
