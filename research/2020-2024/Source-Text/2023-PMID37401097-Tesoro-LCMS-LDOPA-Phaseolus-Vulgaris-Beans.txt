Title: A validated LC-MS/MS method for quantitative determination of L-dopa in Fagioli di Sarconi beans (Phaseolus vulgaris L.).
PMID: 37401097
DOI: 10.1002/jms.4952
First published: 2023
Authors: Tesoro C, Lelario F, Ciriello R, Bianco G, Acquavia MA, Montoro P, et al.
Journal: Journal of mass spectrometry : JMS
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37401097/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Validated analytical LC-ESI/MS/MS study (Tesoro et al., 2023, J Mass Spectrom) quantifying L-DOPA in fresh, dried, and pod samples of four Phaseolus vulgaris L. (Fagioli di Sarconi) ecotypes. Confirmed trace-only L-DOPA levels of 0.020 +/- 0.005 to 2.34 +/- 0.05 ug/g dry weight—orders of magnitude below therapeutic levodopa doses and distinct from L-DOPA-rich Vicia faba or Mucuna pruriens. Corroborates Avula et al. 2025 (PMID:40002056) finding undetectable L-DOPA in black turtle beans (PV-03).
