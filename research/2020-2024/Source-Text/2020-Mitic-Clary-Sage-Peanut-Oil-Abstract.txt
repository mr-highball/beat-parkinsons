Topical peanut comparator in the clary sage report
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32688401/
PMID: 32688401
DOI: 10.1055/a-1211-6325
Coverage: author abstract; full journal article unresolved; related thesis checked separately

The July 20, 2020 journal abstract describes clary sage diluted in peanut oil and peanut oil control in a 30-person dermal comparison, with a separate inhalation experiment. The abstract supplies no clinical anxiety or depression score and no oral peanut oil comparison. A related Vienna thesis dated January 2013 reports acute mood dimensions after two dermal sessions. Its Table 8 mood difference across conditions gives p=.458 and calm-versus-restless difference p=.934. Neither compares peanut oil against no oil. Body site and inhalation duration differ across the thesis, symposium and journal descriptions. Cohort overlap is plausible and unverified. The primary journal body and the first public release date of the thesis remain unresolved. [Related university thesis](https://services.phaidra.univie.ac.at/api/object/o%3A1295523/download).
