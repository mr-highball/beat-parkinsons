Title: Apple polyphenol extract ameliorates sugary-diet-induced depression-like behaviors in male C57BL/6 mice by inhibiting the inflammation of the gut-brain axis.
PMID: 38406886
DOI: 10.1039/d3fo04606k
First published: 2024
Authors: Xie Y, Wu Z, Qian Q, Yang H, Ma J, Luan W et al.
Journal: Food & function
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38406886/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled mouse study (N=30 male C57BL/6 mice, 3 groups x 8 weeks: control, 60% high-sucrose/sucralose diet, and high-sucrose diet + 500 mg/kg/d oral apple polyphenol extract [APE]). APE significantly ameliorated diet-induced depression-like behaviors (reducing forced swim and tail suspension immobility and marble burying), improved Morris water maze cognition, lowered serum corticosterone/ACTH, raised IL-10, inhibited gut-brain NF-kB inflammation, and restored colonic/hippocampal tight junction proteins. Limits: non-PD mouse model and concentrated polyphenol extract.
