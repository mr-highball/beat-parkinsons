Title: The Effect of Hydroalcoholic Extract of Pistachio on Anxiety and Working Memory in Ovariectomized Female Rats.
DOI: 10.22123/phj.2021.270525.1080
First published: 2020
Authors: Hakimizadeh E, Fatemi I, Allahtavakoli M
Journal: Pistachio and Health Journal
Source: https://doi.org/10.22123/phj.2021.270525.1080
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 6 review).

Verified Round 6 Primary Facts & Critical Appraisal:
Preclinical study published in a regional non-PubMed journal (Pistachio and Health Journal 2020;3(3):72-83; cited in Mandalari 2021 PMC8747606 Ref [71]) testing oral hydroalcoholic pistachio extract (10 and 100 mg/kg x 60 days) in ovariectomized (OVX) female rats on elevated plus maze (EPM) anxiety-like behavior and Y-maze working memory. Reported reduced EPM anxiety-like behavior and improved Y-maze working memory at 100 mg/kg. Limitations: hydroalcoholic solvent extract (not whole raw/roasted culinary kernels), non-PD estrogen-deficiency rat model, regional non-PubMed journal with full statistical tables/n unavailable for independent verification, and contradicted on whole-kernel anxiety by Golchin 2015 (PMC4434487 null OFT anxiety).
