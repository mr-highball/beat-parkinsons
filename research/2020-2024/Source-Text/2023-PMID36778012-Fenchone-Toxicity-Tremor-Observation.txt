Title: Fenchone, a monoterpene: Toxicity and diuretic profiling in rats
PMID: 36778012
PMCID: PMC9909529
DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2023.1119360
Publication date: 2023-01-26
Citation: Bashir and colleagues. Frontiers in Pharmacology 14, 1119360.
Source: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pharmacology/articles/10.3389/fphar.2023.1119360/full
Coverage: Primary study body and acute behavioral Table 1.

The compound was a commercial isolated fenchone standard. In the acute oral toxicity study, the same observation protocol was used for a 2,000 mg/kg treatment and vehicle. Table 1 records neither convulsions nor tremors in either group. It records itching after fenchone only, despite the broad wording in the article's conclusion. Methods describe an initial animal followed by four more. Some later quantitative tables label N=6. A separate arm measured urinary and electrolyte outcomes. The toxicology observation was broad and in healthy rats. Its null sign on the combined observation supplies no estimate of Parkinson resting tremor or the effect of toasted seed.
