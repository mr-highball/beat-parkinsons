Title: Therapeutic potential of Ajwa date fruit extract in an experimental lung injury model
DOI: 10.3390/app12136784
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/12/13/6784
Published: 2022-07-04, Applied Sciences 12(13), article 6784.
Archive scope: Original publisher article, preparation, experimental model and endpoint fields. Reviewed 2026-11-20.

The authors tested an Ajwa date-pulp extract in a chemically induced animal lung injury model. Reported endpoints include histology, oxidative stress, inflammatory measures and tissue signalling. The extract, provoked lung injury and tissue outcomes differ from eating dried fruit and rating pain in Parkinson disease.

The 2026 hesperidin review cites this report and the separate 2018 liver report. The two models use different agents, tissues and experiments. Neither reference is a clinical date-fruit or Parkinson symptom outcome.
