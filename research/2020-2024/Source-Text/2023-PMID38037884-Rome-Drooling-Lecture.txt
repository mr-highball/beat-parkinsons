The neurological rehabilitation lecture in article part art014 distinguishes saliva production, anterior spill and posterior spill. It describes impaired oral control and clearance as possible mechanisms and discusses assessment in neurological populations. This is a published educational lecture. It gives no original food trial or raw-fig drooling estimate.
