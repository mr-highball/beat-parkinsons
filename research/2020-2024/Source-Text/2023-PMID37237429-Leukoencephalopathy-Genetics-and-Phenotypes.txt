Title: Genotyping and clinical manifestations in adult leukoencephalopathies
PMID: 37237429
PMCID: PMC10351660
DOI: 10.1002/acn3.51794
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10351660/
First publication: 2023-05-26
Coverage: Original NCBI PMC JATS article body checked and summarized.

The clinical series evaluated 129 people with adult leukoencephalopathy using whole-exome sequencing and targeted repeat-expansion methods. The primary outcomes were diagnosed genetic causes and associated phenotypes. The motor and nonmotor findings are clinical presentation data, without a Parkinson-specific dried-date intervention or pain-severity comparison. The source provides differential-diagnosis context for motor presentations.
