Methylmercury exposure and dysarthria in a 2023 preprint
Source: https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202312.0642/v1/download
DOI: 10.20944/preprints202312.0642.v1
Coverage: original 2023 preprint methods and Table 5; journal version cross-checked

The 2023 preprint presents the same analysis as the later journal report, including 197 historically methylmercury-exposed residents and 130 control-area residents. Table 5 reports examiner-detected dysarthria in 24.9% and 1.6%, respectively. The exposure was historically contaminated seafood. Species, cooking method, formal intelligibility scores and an analysis in Parkinson disease are unavailable.
