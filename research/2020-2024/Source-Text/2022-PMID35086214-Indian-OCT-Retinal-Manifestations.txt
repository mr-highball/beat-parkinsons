Title: Assessment of retinal manifestations of Parkinson's disease using spectral domain optical coherence tomography: A study in Indian eyes.
PMID: 35086214
PMCID: PMC9023951
DOI: 10.4103/ijo.ijo_1409_21
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9023951/
First publication date: 2022-02-01 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

The Parkinson study compares retinal structure using optical coherence tomography and discusses visual complaints. Retinal dimensions and vision findings are distinct from a measured visual hallucination rate by food exposure. Dried fig use was not studied.
