Title: Effects of Timing and Types of Protein Supplementation on Improving Muscle Mass, Strength, and Physical Performance in Adults Undergoing Resistance Training: A Network Meta-Analysis.
PMID: 38039960
DOI: 10.1123/ijsnem.2023-0118
First published: 2024
Authors: Zhou HH, Liao Y, Zhou X, Peng Z, Xu S, Shi S, et al.
Journal: International journal of sport nutrition and exercise metabolism
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38039960/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and network meta-analysis of 116 RCTs (n=4,711 adults) evaluating protein types and timing for muscle mass, strength, and physical performance: red meat improved lean mass and muscle strength, but no protein source significantly enhanced gait speed, Timed Up and Go, or 6-minute walk distance overall.
