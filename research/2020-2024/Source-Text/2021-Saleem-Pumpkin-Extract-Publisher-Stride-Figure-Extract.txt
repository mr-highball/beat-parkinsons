Saleem pumpkin seed extract and measured stride length

Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11011-021-00707-6
Coverage: Original author abstract and publisher Figures 1–4, factual extract.

Saleem and colleagues, Metabolic Brain Disease 36:1231–1251, 2021, DOI 10.1007/s11011-021-00707-6, PMID 33759084. Springer lists first online publication March 24, 2021. Its original Figure 4 labels stride length in centimeters and uses separate baseline control, disease control, standard comparator and three methanolic Cucurbita pepo seed extract arms. Approximate figure-derived means are 9.1 cm in the baseline control, 5.7 cm in the disease control, 8.4 cm with the standard comparator and 6.4, 7.3 and 8.0 cm in the ascending extract arms. This is a measured stride endpoint in haloperidol-exposed rats. Numeric means are approximate readings from the graph. Comparator footnotes, animal counts for that figure and gait protocol are not visible in the public figure or abstract. Figures 1 and 2 label several phenolic peaks including ferulic acid, but separate sample identities and active compound attribution require the article methods. The primary test material was a methanolic seed extract rather than roasted seed oil, and no Parkinson cohort was enrolled.
