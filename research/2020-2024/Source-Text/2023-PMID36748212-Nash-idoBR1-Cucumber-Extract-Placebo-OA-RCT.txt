Title: Standardised ido-BR1 Cucumber Extract Improved Parameters Linked to Moderate Osteoarthritis in a Placebo-controlled Study.
PMID: 36748212
DOI: 10.2174/1573397119666230206105703
First published: 2023
Authors: Nash RJ, Mafongang A, Singh H, Singwe-Ngandeu M, Penkova YB, Kaur T et al.
Journal: Curr Rheumatol Rev
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36748212/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (Nash et al., 2023, Curr Rheumatol Rev, n=101 enrolled / 91 completed adults with moderate knee osteoarthritis) comparing standardized idoBR1 Cucumis sativus extract (Q-Actin 20 mg/day and 100 mg/day) against cellulose placebo for 180 days. Both doses produced statistically significant, dose-dependent improvements in WOMAC, VAS pain, and LFI parameters relative to placebo. Limits: concentrated fresh-cucumber extract rather than pickled whole cucumber, conducted by the same Phytoquest/Nash research group without independent academic replication, abstract omits exact confidence intervals, non-Parkinson knee OA population.
