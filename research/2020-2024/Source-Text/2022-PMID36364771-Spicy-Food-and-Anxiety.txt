Title: Zhang et al. Frequency of spicy food consumption and symptoms of anxiety in college students

Cross-sectional online dietary and DASS-21 survey in college students. Section 3.1 reports 1,717 participants analyzed from 1,867 initial responses. The abstract instead says 1,771. The body denominator is used when describing the results, and the discrepancy remains documented.

Adjusted Table 2 anxiety odds were 1.50 (95% CI 1.13–1.99) for spicy food at least three days per week and 0.99 (0.78–1.27) for one or two days per week, relative to none or rare use. The Table 3 intensity categories gave no adjusted anxiety association. Frequency was a food category without identified pepper species, culinary preparation, or amount. The study did not establish Parkinson status or within-person symptom change. This outcome is relevant to reach and uncertainty for claim 0096.
