Coverage: Original Methods, Results headed Elevated Plus-Maze (EPM) Test and Anxiety Index (AI), and Figure 4 caption. The following is an original factual summary.

The model had 36 male Wistar rats across four groups of nine.
The saline injection control received water by gavage.
D-galactose model rats received water, aqueous ferulic acid, or ferulic-acid-loaded polymeric nanocapsules by gavage.
Free and nanoencapsulated ferulic acid used the same 0.5 mg/kg dose.
Treatment lasted eight weeks. The experiment included no empty-nanocapsule arm.

Figure 4 panel A assesses elevated-plus-maze open-arm time.
Figure 4 panel B assesses the study's anxiety index.
One-way ANOVA for open-arm time: F(3,32)=5.87, p=.0026.
One-way ANOVA for anxiety index: F(3,32)=6.84, p=.001.
The D-galactose plus water group showed less open-arm time and a higher anxiety index than the saline plus water group.
The nanoencapsulated ferulic acid group showed greater open-arm time and a lower anxiety index than the D-galactose plus water group.
The figure caption marks significance against the saline plus water or against the D-galactose plus water group. It provides no pairwise result comparing free ferulic acid with nanoencapsulated ferulic acid for anxiety.
The separate Y-maze entry result is a locomotor observation from a different test, not a locomotor-adjusted anxiety result.

No dried chive exposure or Parkinson disease participants.
