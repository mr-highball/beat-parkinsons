Title: 2023 Society of General Internal Medicine proceedings and culinary exposure context
PMCID: PMC10323075
DOI: 10.1007/s11606-023-08226-z
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10323075/
Published: 2023-06-23, electronic journal publication.
Coverage: Complete original PMC HTML, 13,990 numbered paragraphs and 1,733 identified member controls. The 16 unusually short titles were manually matched to their controls.

Control 3867696, Par5474, reports a diet limited to applesauce and gelatin during illness. The patient had severe hypokalemia with poor overall intake and diuretic use. This case does not isolate an apple effect or test the frozen culinary preparation. Control 3875759, Par9319, records absence of coffee and other caffeinated drinks in a post-infectious insomnia case. This is beverage history without an assigned contrast or Parkinson fatigue measurement.

Control 3855082, Par5436, mentions poultry as a possible foodborne route in the discussion. The case describes unwashed vegetables and no identified chicken food exposure. Control 3876210 records proximity to chickens on a farm and unpasteurized dairy consumption. Control 3875049 uses chicken in chickenpox. Control 3848171 uses cherry for a retinal color. Control 3873911 uses Fig as a figure label. The remaining apple and orange matches include an apples-and-oranges title, software, a city and discoloration. Kale, Sage, Rosemary and Melissa appear as names in member author lists. Turkey is an affiliation. The date matches describe time, recordkeeping or study eligibility.

The search screened the named member text and distinguished observed exposures from neighboring abstracts and citations. The two actual food-history contexts do not support directional Parkinson verdicts.
