Pediatric case report (Mansouri et al., 2023, Ugeskr Laeger) of a 4-week-old boy with constipation and infantile colic who developed seizures, myoclonic jerks, and irritability after being fed a star anise herbal mixture, with rapid recovery and normal workup supporting star anise intoxication. Demonstrates ongoing real-world risk when star anise is used as a digestive/constipation home remedy.
