Title: Acute effects of a chewable beetroot-based supplement on cognitive performance: a double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover clinical trial.
PMID: 37875637
PMCID: PMC10799154
DOI: 10.1007/s00394-023-03265-y
Registry ID: NCT05509075
First published: 2024
Authors: Vaccaro MG, Innocenti B, Cione E, Gallelli L, De Sarro G, Bonilla DA, et al.
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37875637/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled two-period crossover RCT (NCT05509075) in n=44 healthy adults (mean age 32.7 y) given a single 3 g dose of chewable Beta vulgaris extract (RedNite) vs maltodextrin placebo (4-day washout): 90 min post-dose, active beetroot improved frontal lobe functions (+2.57%), cognitive flexibility (+11.16%), and RAVLT immediate (+20.69%) and delayed (+12.34%) recall; digit span, working memory, and processing speed were null; BDI-II depression and STAI anxiety showed mixed results confounded by sequence/period effects. Limits: acute extract tablet in young healthy adults.
