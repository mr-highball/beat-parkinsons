Title: Chronic and postprandial effect of blueberries on cognitive function, alertness, and mood in participants with metabolic syndrome - results from a six-month, double-blind, randomized controlled trial.
PMID: 38432713
PMCID: PMC10972710
DOI: 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2023.12.006
Registry ID: NCT02035592
First published: 2024
Authors: Curtis PJ, van der Velpen V, Berends L, Jennings A, Haag L, Minihane AM, et al.
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10972710/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Secondary analysis of a 6-month double-blind RCT (NCT02035592) in adults with metabolic syndrome without cognitive impairment (n=115 chronic 6-mo; n=33 24-h postprandial) consuming 1/2-cup or 1-cup equivalent freeze-dried blueberry powder vs isocaloric placebo. Postprandial Bond-Lader calmness improved after 1 cup (+11.6%, p=0.01, q=0.04), whereas 24-h postprandial Power of attention was slower with blueberry (1419 vs 1315 ms, p<0.001, q<0.001). Over 6 months, all chronic cognitive domains (attention, executive function, memory), alertness, mood, and Leeds Sleep Evaluation Questionnaire outcomes were null vs placebo. Limits: non-PD MetS adults; secondary endpoints.
