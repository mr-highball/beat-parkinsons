Fermented perilla extract and rodent sleep

Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jff.2024.106345
Coverage: Publisher citation and available report summary
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

The 2024 article and the same lead author's February 2023 dissertation describe closely matching fermented perilla extract sleep experiments. The authors report NREM and slow-wave effects in rodents. Full outcome figures were unavailable through the checked journal route. The extract is a processed leaf preparation and the rodent endpoints do not establish a culinary shiso effect on Parkinson insomnia.
