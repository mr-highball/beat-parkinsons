Rosemary in a mixed essential-oil massage trial
2022-03-02
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8887855/

Mehrabian and colleagues compared an essential-oil massage blend with control in nursing-home residents, analyzing 19 per arm. The intervention contained lavender, chamomile and rosemary in a jojoba carrier. The mean HADS depression rating for the intervention group reached 4.15 at week seven and the repeated-measures depression caption reports F(3,36)=17.23, p<.001. Groups differed at baseline and the text and baseline table print different depression comparators. The design combines oils and massage, without a daily oral rosemary or Parkinson exposure.
