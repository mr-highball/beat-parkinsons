Title: Roselle for hypertension in adults
PMID: 34837382
PMCID: PMC8626866
DOI: 10.1002/14651858.CD007894.pub3
First published: 2021
Authors: Pattanittum P, Ngamjarus C, Buttramee F, Somboonporn C
Journal: Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34837382/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. Cochrane systematic review update (searched through August 2021) applying strict primary-hypertension placebo-controlled eligibility criteria. Only 1 parallel RCT (n=60 T2D adults, 52 analyzed, 5600 mg extract capsules vs lactose placebo x 8 weeks) met strict inclusion criteria and showed null/imprecise BP differences (SBP MD +1.65 mmHg, 95% CI -7.89 to 11.19; DBP MD +4.60 mmHg, 95% CI -1.38 to 10.58; very low-certainty evidence due to attrition and baseline imbalance). Highlights methodological heterogeneity across hibiscus BP trials.
