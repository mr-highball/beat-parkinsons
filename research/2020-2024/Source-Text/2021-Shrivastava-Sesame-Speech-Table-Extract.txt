Sesame oil swishing and reported speech difficulty

Primary source: https://www.scielo.br/j/pboci/a/fkJKytSzks83kFk4zNgmRDb/?lang=en
Coverage: Publisher full original and denominator audit
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published October 29, 2021. The authors describe 62 people with oral submucous fibrosis equally randomized to sesame oil swishing or placebo capsules and no dropout. Table 3 lists speech difficulty present/absent/reduced as 29/3/0 versus 24/2/0 at baseline and 11/14/7 versus 13/7/8 at day 90. The implied counts differ from the stated 31 per arm and the control total changes during follow-up. A reliable between-arm effect cannot be calculated from these data. It is subjective speech difficulty, and the paper does not confirm ingestion, toasted oil or Parkinson disease.
