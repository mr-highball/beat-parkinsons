Design: Complete psychiatry congress e-poster collection

The official PMC HTML contains 808 individually headed member articles. The member on excessive nutmeg use reports auditory hallucinations, mania and insomnia. A separate dementia-with-Lewy-bodies case describes visual hallucinations. An electroconvulsive-therapy review summarizes earlier DLB case reports. The inspected members do not pair dried fig ingestion with visual hallucination frequency.

Member locators: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13070663/#subarticle297 (nutmeg), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13070663/#subarticle832 (visual case), and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13070663/#subarticle1099 (electroconvulsive therapy review).
