A separate dementia-with-Lewy-bodies case describes visual hallucinations. An electroconvulsive-therapy review summarizes earlier DLB case reports. The official HTML and structured article XML contain the same 808 unique member identifiers with titles and abstract bodies. Four members contain saliva-related terms.
