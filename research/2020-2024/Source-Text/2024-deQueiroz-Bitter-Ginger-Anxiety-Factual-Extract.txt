Publication: de Queiroz et al., Complementary Therapies in Medicine 80, 103021. Published online January 8, 2024. PMID 38199583, DOI 10.1016/j.ctim.2024.103021.
Sources: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38199583/ and https://simul-europe.com/2024/mascc/Files/1542.pdf
Coverage: Original abstract and investigator poster. Original full tables were unavailable.

The single-arm study reports that 35 adults with advanced cancer enrolled and 16 completed eight weeks. The abstract reports an improvement on the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale anxiety score (p = .032) without a numeric change estimate. The poster describes capsules with Zingiber zerumbet rhizome extract. The abstract and poster do not establish an isolated zerumbone quantity. The earlier 2023 oncology abstract 123541 reports matching numbers and results, indicating the same study. The precise publication date of that conference record is unavailable. Absence of a comparison arm and high attrition limit causal interpretation.
