The tea member describes 10,923 older Chinese respondents. Green tea use was reported by 2,143. The adjusted odds ratio for mild cognitive impairment among green tea drinkers versus people without habitual tea consumption was 0.80 (95% interval 0.68–0.95). The report is observational. The other two tea-type intervals include 1, including the flower tea comparison despite broader language in the abstract conclusion. The study supplies neither a Parkinson population nor a fatigue or olfaction outcome.

Other members describe the EGGSPDITe randomized egg intervention for prediabetes and separate protocol and remote-delivery snapshots. These records identify an egg exposure in a different population and context. They do not supply relevant reported effects on the frozen Parkinson outcomes. The distinct records should remain linked to the same intervention program.
