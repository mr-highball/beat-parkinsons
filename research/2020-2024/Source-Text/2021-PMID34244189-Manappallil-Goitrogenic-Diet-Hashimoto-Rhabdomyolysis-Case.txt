Title: Hashimoto's thyroiditis aggravated by goitrogenic diet presenting as rhabdomyolysis worsened by alcohol intake.
PMID: 34244189
PMCID: PMC8273470
DOI: 10.1136/bcr-2021-243385
First published: 2021
Authors: Manappallil RG, Muralidharan R, Shalu S, Haneef F
Journal: BMJ Case Rep
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34244189/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single case report of a young man with undiagnosed Hashimoto thyroiditis who presented with severe muscle aches, stiffness, and rhabdomyolysis after heavy alcohol consumption and raw cruciferous (broccoli; review text cautions broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage) intake; improved on levothyroxine and IV fluids. Heavily confounded non-PD case.
