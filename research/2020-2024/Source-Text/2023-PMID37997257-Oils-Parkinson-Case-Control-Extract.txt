Title: Edible cooking oils and case-control Parkinson comparisons
PMID: 37997257
DOI: 10.1080/1028415X.2023.2277974
First published: 2023-11-23
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37997257/
Coverage: original author abstract, indexed by PubMed and Europe PMC. Full outcome tables remain open.

This cross-sectional case-control comparison included 120 people with Parkinson disease and 50 healthy participants. The dietary questionnaire asks about cooking oils and mayonnaise. Olive oil intake was higher among cases, with the reported odds of case status 49 percent higher per intake unit (p=.02). The abstract gives no serving definition for this estimate or separate extra-virgin oil category. Observed case status and current food use can reflect changes after diagnosis. The reported UPDRS associations concern animal oil or butter and are not an olive oil estimate for rest tremor. The incidence or case-status comparison addresses a different question from easing existing symptoms.
