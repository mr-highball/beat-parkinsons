Original study scope
Rhinitis guidance describes an odor-identification test using examples including coffee, cinnamon and sardines, allergic-food reports for banana, and drug interaction context for bitter orange juice. Turkey can denote the country and dates are temporal. Neither odor identification nor rhinitis measures the frozen Parkinson symptoms.

Continuing evidence gaps
Find an independently controlled culinary exposure and matching Parkinson outcome. Do not interpret an odor test as feeding evidence.
