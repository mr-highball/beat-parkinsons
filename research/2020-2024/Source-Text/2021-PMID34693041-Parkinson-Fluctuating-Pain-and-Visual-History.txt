Title: Fluctuating pain in Parkinson's disease: Its prevalence and impact on quality of life
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8511840/
PMID: 34693041
PMCID: PMC8511840
DOI: 10.1016/j.ensci.2021.100371
Publication year: 2021
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

Records from 331 Parkinson patients are grouped by no pain, nonfluctuating pain and fluctuating pain. The fluctuating-pain group had more people with a visual hallucination history than the no-pain group. This is symptom co-occurrence, without a fig exposure or a change in visual episodes.

The original groups include 223 patients with no pain, 73 with nonfluctuating pain and 35 with fluctuating pain. Visual hallucination history is extracted from medical records.
