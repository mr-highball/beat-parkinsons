Original study scope
Two separate trial tables identify oral crocin versus citalopram in burning mouth syndrome and saffron versus duloxetine in fibromyalgia. Their main pain outcomes and mood data belong to those distinct cohorts. Date and millet findings did not supply a matching food exposure.

Continuing evidence gaps
The underlying reports now have local texts at research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID30456199-Shakiba-Saffron-Duloxetine-Fibromyalgia.txt and research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-PMID31763210-Pakfetrat-Crocin-Citalopram-Burning-Mouth.txt. Both compare an active drug with a preparation other than culinary saffron. Mood contrasts are inconclusive, fatigue was measured only in the fibromyalgia trial, and neither supplies the frozen Parkinson outcomes. Ordinary-food transfer and independent matching clinical comparisons remain open.
