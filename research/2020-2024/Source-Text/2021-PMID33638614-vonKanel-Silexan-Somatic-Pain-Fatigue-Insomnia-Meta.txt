Title: Therapeutic effects of Silexan on somatic symptoms and physical health in patients with anxiety disorders: A meta-analysis.
PMID: 33638614
PMCID: PMC8035468
DOI: 10.1002/brb3.1997
First published: 2021
Authors: von Känel R, Kasper S, Bondolfi G, Holsboer-Trachsler E, Hättenschwiler J, Hatzinger M, Imboden C, Heitlinger E et al.
Journal: Brain and behavior
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33638614/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Individual-patient-data meta-analysis (von Kanel et al., 2021, Brain Behav, PMC8035468) pooling 5 randomized placebo-controlled trials of oral Silexan 80 mg/day (10 weeks) in subthreshold anxiety (Kasper 2010, 2015, 2016) and GAD (Kasper 2014, 2017; n=1,172 analyzed across age subgroups). Silexan beat placebo on HAMA somatic anxiety subscore (SMD -0.31, 95% CI -0.52 to -0.10, p=0.004; consistent in >=60 yr subgroup ES 0.32, p<0.0001), HAMA items 7 (somatic muscular aches/tension), 9 (cardiovascular), 10 (respiratory), and 12 (genitourinary — bundling urinary urgency with sexual/menstrual complaints), while item 11 (GI) did not reach significance. On SF-36 physical health (4 trials), Silexan improved bodily pain, role-physical, general health (SMDs 0.25-0.31), vitality/fatigue, and HAMA item 4 insomnia (ES 0.30 across all 5 trials). Review/meta-analysis summarizing underlying Silexan RCTs.
