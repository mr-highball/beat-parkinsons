Title: Easy to Swallow Rice Cake as a Carbohydrate Source during Endurance Exercise Suppressed Feelings of Thirst and Hunger without Changing Exercise Performance
Source: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnsv/66/2/66_128/_pdf
Identifiers: PMID 32350174, DOI 10.3177/jnsv.66.128
First published: April 2020
Coverage: Factual extract from original Methods and Table 3.

Six trained men received a steamed sweet potato and rice cake mixture and carbohydrate gel in randomized crossover periods. Perceived exertion with gel versus cake was 12.7 versus 13.2 at 30 minutes, 14.0 versus 13.5 at 60 minutes and 18.2 versus 18.0 after time trial. The condition test was p = .80 and condition-by-time interaction p = .65. The report gives no separate boiled sweet potato or Parkinson fatigue result.
