Mixed polyphenol supplement biomarker report
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11352085/
Coverage: Factual summary of the primary report.

First published August 17, 2024. The NCT05829382 crossover report tests a mixed polyphenol supplement in healthy participants and gives blood biomarker and epigenetic findings. Ingredient lists include blueberry, kale, rosemary and grapefruit. The assigned formulation leaves individual ingredients and preparation-specific effects inseparable. No named Parkinson target symptom from this experiment was measured.
