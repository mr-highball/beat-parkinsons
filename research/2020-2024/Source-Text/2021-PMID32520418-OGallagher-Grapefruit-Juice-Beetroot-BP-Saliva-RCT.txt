Title: Grapefruit juice enhances the systolic blood pressure-lowering effects of dietary nitrate-containing beetroot juice.
PMID: 32520418
DOI: 10.1111/bcp.14420
First published: 2021
Authors: O'Gallagher K, Borg Cardona S, Hill C, Al-Saedi A, Shahed F, Floyd CN, McNeill K, Mills CE
Journal: British journal of clinical pharmacology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32520418/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Randomized placebo-controlled 3-way crossover trial in 11 healthy volunteers comparing 70 mL nitrate-rich beetroot juice with 250 mL grapefruit juice vs water. Adding grapefruit juice significantly lowered systolic BP (-3.3 mmHg at 5 h, p=0.02) and pulse pressure (-4.2 mmHg at 2.5 h, p=0.0003) and reduced saliva volume (-0.34 mL/min, 95% CI -0.05 to -0.68) despite lower plasma/salivary nitrite.
