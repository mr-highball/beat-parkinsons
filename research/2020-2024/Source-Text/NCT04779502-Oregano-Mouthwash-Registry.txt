Registered oregano mouthwash and odor outcomes
Primary source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04779502
Coverage: factual extract of official interventions and outcomes.

The March 3, 2021 first-posted registry compares an oregano essential-oil mouthwash, chlorhexidine mouthwash and water in a halitosis study. Its registered main measures are tongue and floss odor scores. The completed record had no posted results in the checked snapshot. The exposure and outcome differ from eating fresh oregano and measuring clinical drooling.
