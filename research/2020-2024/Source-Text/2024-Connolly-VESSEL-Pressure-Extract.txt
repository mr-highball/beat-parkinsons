Title: Cruciferous vegetables lower blood pressure in adults with mildly elevated blood pressure in a randomized, controlled, crossover trial: the VEgetableS for vaScular hEaLth (VESSEL) study
PMID: 39218859
PMCID: PMC11367748
DOI: 10.1186/s12916-024-03577-8
First public publication: 2024-09-02
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11367748/
Format: Factual extraction from the original Methods and Table 2.
The crossover compared a boiled, blended cruciferous soup with a root-and-squash soup for two weeks each. The active recipe listed 40% broccoli, 25% cauliflower, 25% cabbage and 10% kale, without naming the cabbage cultivar. In 17 analyzed participants with mildly elevated blood pressure, the primary 24-hour systolic difference was −2.5 mmHg (95% CI −4.1 to −0.9, p = .002). No adverse events were reported. The trial did not separately compare raw red cabbage, assess standing lightheadedness, or recruit for Parkinson disease. A 2025 glycaemic analysis reports the same intervention, not a second pressure trial.
Registration: ACTRN12619001294145.
