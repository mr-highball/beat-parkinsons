Title: Effect of thymol on oxidative stress and reelin signaling pathway in Alzheimer's disease model
Publication date: 2024-02-01 (PMC citation)
PMID: 38665779
PMCID: PMC11042864

Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11042864/
Text coverage: Full primary methods and Figure 5 results.

The sunflower vehicle group received a course of oral and injected saline before its oral oil. The untreated control group did not undergo that combined schedule. Among 50 male rats in five groups, the vehicle group made more radial-maze working-memory errors than controls at 72 hours (p=.0127). It also made more reference-memory errors at 72 hours (p=.0380) and 96 hours (p<.0001). The 48- and 96-hour working-memory passages do not report the corresponding significant vehicle-control contrast. These observations compare a combined vehicle history with untreated controls. Sunflower oil is also a thymol carrier. No matched handling-only control, identified pressing grade or Parkinson cohort isolates a sunflower effect.
