Title: Blueberry supplementation reduces the blood lactate response to running in normobaric hypoxia but has no effect on performance in recreational runners.
PMID: 33781280
PMCID: PMC8008513
DOI: 10.1186/s12970-021-00423-7
First published: 2021
Authors: Brandenburg JP, Giles LV
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8008513/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized placebo-controlled crossover trial in n=11 runners completing a 30-min hypoxic time trial after 4 days blueberry vs placebo. Blueberry lowered post-run blood lactate (p=0.036) but had NO effect on distance run, heart rate, SaO2, or ratings of perceived exertion (RPE). Limits: acute athletic endurance in non-PD runners; null fatigue.
