# Oral dill extract in a perinatal mouse organ study

Ajarem et al., Coatings 11(7), 838, July 13, 2021.
DOI: 10.3390/coatings11070838
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2079-6412/11/7/838
Coverage: Factual summary from the full original.

The Methods describe 500 mg/kg of orally administered Anethum graveolens extract prepared from powdered plant material using 70% methanol. The plant organ is unspecified. The Methods give nicotine from gestational day 12 to postnatal day 15, while the abstract gives postnatal day 5 as the end. The paper assesses offspring liver and kidney injury, oxidative stress and inflammation. It does not supply a maze anxiety endpoint. Its dose differs tenfold from the maternal dose in the 2025 related anxiety-like behavior report. The available reports do not establish whether the animal cohorts are independent.
