Title: Effects of Selenium Supplementation in Patients with Mild Cognitive Impairment or Alzheimer's Disease: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
PMID: 35956381
PMCID: PMC9370215
DOI: 10.3390/nu14153205
First published: 2022
Authors: Pereira ME, Souza JV, Galiciolli MEA, Sare F, Vieira GS, Kruk IL, et al.
Journal: Nutrients
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35956381/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis (PMC9370215) of selenium interventions (including Cardoso 2016 Brazil nut trial) in MCI and Alzheimer disease: increased blood/CSF selenium and GPx activity (p=0.00001), while noting small sample sizes, missing SDs preventing cognitive meta-analysis, and cautioning about supranutritional selenium side effects.
