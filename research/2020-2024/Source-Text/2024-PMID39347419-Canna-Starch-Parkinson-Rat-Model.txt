Title: Therapeutic potential of Canna edulis RS3-resistant starch in a Parkinson disease rat model
PMID: 39347419
PMCID: PMC11438014
DOI: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e38072
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11438014/
Date basis: 2024-09-18 electronic publication, preceding the September 30 issue.
Coverage: Factual extract of the complete original rat study, experimental groups and behavioral results.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The study assigned 60 rats across control, model, comparator and three Canna edulis resistant-starch groups. It reports group differences in pole and hanging behavior and measures of the substantia nigra. The intervention began with modeling and used purified resistant starch. No human stool outcome or Parkinson symptom assessment is reported.

The introduction gives banana yam and ginger yam as names for the canna plant. The discussion has an isolated banana RS3 wording. The title, test-material description and group methods identify Canna edulis. There is no separately assigned Musa banana or Zingiber officinale ginger exposure.
