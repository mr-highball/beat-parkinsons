Title: Adherence to diet low in fermentable carbohydrates and traditional diet for irritable bowel syndrome
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32086111/
PMID: 32086111
DOI: 10.1016/j.nut.2020.110719
Publication date: 2020-01-07
Date basis: First-publication date in Europe PMC.
Review depth: abstract
Checked: 2026-11-25
Inspected extent: Final paper abstract, full original 2015 trial article and its linked 2018 post-hoc poster P1704.

The final paper reports food diaries and IBS symptom scores from 66 participants in the previously published Böhn low-FODMAP versus traditional-diet trial. The 2018 P1704 version identifies oranges as an example in a low-FODMAP fruit group. It reports coffee retained at about 73 percent of baseline consumption in the traditional advice arm. The final abstract describes sustained coffee intake and multiple dietary substitutions. No result estimates the separate effect of an orange or a cup of brewed coffee. Differences between the multi-food diets cannot establish a food-specific change in constipation or fatigue.

Study lineage: NCT02107625, parent publication PMID 26255043 and UEG P1704. These are one participant lineage and no separate randomized ingredient cohort.
