Title: Secondary analysis of a randomized trial testing community health educator interventions for diabetes prevention among refugees with depression: effects on nutrition, physical activity and sleep.
PMID: 37700288
PMCID: PMC10496245
DOI: 10.1186/s12966-023-01509-y
Registry ID: NCT02502929
First published: 2023
Authors: Wagner JA, Bermúdez-Millán A, Buckley TE, Buxton OM, Feinn RS, Kong S
Journal: The international journal of behavioral nutrition and physical activity
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10496245/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Secondary analysis of a 12-15 month RCT (NCT02502929) in n=188 Cambodian-American refugees with depression receiving Eat, Walk, Sleep lifestyle counseling vs social services. EWS substantially increased brown rice consumption (p<0.001, d=0.76) and activity (p=0.039) and improved HbA1c, but NO intervention changed 7-day actigraphy sleep duration, timing, efficiency, or WASO. Multi-component behavioral program; does not isolate brown rice.
