Horiuchi et al., January 12, 2023. PMID 36678258; PMCID PMC9864878; DOI 10.3390/nu15020388.
Thirteen healthy adults completed a double-blind crossover in hypoxia. The tart cherry capsule condition had cycling exhaustion time of 940 seconds (SD 84), compared with 912 (SD 63) with flour capsules, p=.01. Perceived exertion was a stopping criterion. The oral preparation was a manufactured capsule and the result is exercise endurance under hypoxia.
