Title: Sub-acute toxicological and behavioural effects of two candidate therapeutics, cinnamaldehyde and eugenol, for treatment of ESBL producing-quinolone resistant pathogenic Enterobacteriaceae.
PMID: 32027395
DOI: 10.1111/1440-1681.13276
First published: 2020
Authors: Dhara L, Tripathi A
Journal: Clinical and experimental pharmacology & physiology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32027395/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Subacute 14-day oral toxicology and behavioral study in Swiss albino mice administering isolated eugenol (7.34 to 70 mg/kg/d) or cinnamaldehyde (0.91 to 10 mg/kg/d). Evaluated novelty-suppressed feeding (NSF), novel object recognition (NOR), tail suspension test (TST), social interaction test, food/water intake, hematology, and liver/kidney histology. Found NO significant behavioral alterations across NSF, NOR, TST, or social interaction and no significant toxicological changes, demonstrating null behavioral effects of isolated oral eugenol in unlesioned mice.
