Title: Differences in Symptomatic Presentation and Cognitive Performance Among Participants With LATE-NC Compared to FTLD-TDP

The autopsy study compares clinical and cognitive profiles of LATE neuropathologic change and FTLD-TDP. Visual hallucinations are retrospective symptoms distinguished by pathology. It does not follow a Parkinson culinary intervention.

The paper assesses differences in clinical presentation and cognitive performance with sensitivity analyses by pathology and age. Its charted symptom variables concern distinct autopsy-defined groups.
