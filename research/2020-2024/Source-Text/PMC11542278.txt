Title: Sex Differences in Neuropsychiatric Symptoms in Alpha-Synucleinopathies: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11542278/
PMID: 39385552
PMCID: PMC11542278
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.14227
Publication date: 2024-10-09
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The systematic review compares sexes across Parkinson disease, Parkinson dementia and dementia with Lewy bodies. The analysis groups neuropsychiatric measures, including psychosis-related outcomes. Sex is a comparison variable and dried fig intake is unmeasured.
