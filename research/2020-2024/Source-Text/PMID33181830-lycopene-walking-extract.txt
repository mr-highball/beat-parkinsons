Original factual extract of primary Methods and Table 3

The Framingham Offspring Study analyzed 2,422 participants for gait speed. The measure was the faster of two usual-pace four-metre trials. Nutrient estimates used repeated Willett food-frequency questionnaires. Table 3 gives a fully adjusted association of +0.0043 m/s/year more favorable speed change per +10 mg/day estimated lycopene (SE 0.0010, p < .01). The Cardiovascular Health Study validation lacked lycopene and examined vitamin C and β-carotene. It supplies no additional lycopene estimate. The data do not report a Parkinson-specific estimate, stride length or differences among tomato preparations.
