Original study scope
The systematic review extracts randomized drug trials reporting orthostatic hypotension or relevant monitoring in Parkinson disease and identifies inconsistent adverse-event measurement across trials.

Continuing evidence gaps
The comparisons concern antiparkinsonian drugs and orthostatic hypotension rather than measured fresh sage. They do not determine a sage effect on fall frequency or fatigue severity.
