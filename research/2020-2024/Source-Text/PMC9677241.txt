Title: Digital Histological Study of Neocortical Grey and White Matter Tau Burden Across Tauopathies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9677241/
PMID: 36269086
PMCID: PMC9677241
DOI: 10.1093/jnen/nlac094
Publication date: 2022-10-21
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete official PMC HTML publication; local research digest

Digital histology quantifies grey and white matter tau burden across different tauopathies. Clinical overlap and visual symptoms are context for diagnostic interpretation rather than a measured dried fig exposure or Parkinson visual episode follow-up.
