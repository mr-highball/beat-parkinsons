Independent 2020 oat milk dysphonia case

Source: https://www.jiaci.org/revistas/vol30issue3_5-5.pdf
Coverage: Original clinical report, factual extract.

The 2020 report's second case was a 62-year-old man with ischemic heart disease. Oropharyngeal itching, dysphonia and dyspnea appeared within five minutes of oat milk, followed by urticaria. The first patient, age 14, had oral itching and erythema without reported dysphonia. The report cites the separate 2013/2016 case in a 70-year-old man. These observations concern acute allergy to oat milk. They give no acoustic loudness measure, Parkinson cohort or rolled-oat comparison.
