Stathis and Papadopoulos evaluated the seven-item PDQoL7 in 60 participants with idiopathic Parkinson disease. Its one-week nonmotor questions group salivation with several other symptoms, including pain, fatigue, dizziness and constipation. The attached English form confirms a single frequency response for that group of symptoms. Correlations against PDQ-8 and EQ-5D-5L reported in the paper are for summary indices.

This construct differs from a standalone saliva or drooling severity item. The validation supplies quality-of-life measurement context and no raw-fig exposure or drooling-specific estimated change.
