Publication: D. Xiao, B. Shukitt-Hale and B. Burton-Freeman, The Bramble, Summer 2023, printed pages 9, 11 and 17.
Source: https://www.raspberryblackberry.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/NARBA-2023-BRAMBLE-Summer-Vol-38-Digital-Issue.pdf#page=17
Coverage: Visually verified Figure 7 and same-cohort identity. Factual extract.
The investigators report the NCT03879213 crossover comparing a raspberry powder meal with a control meal. Figure 7 at six hours shows about 6.9 versus 9.5 eight-box between-search errors and better strategy use following the raspberry meal. The plotted comparisons are marked p < .05. This is an acute spatial working-memory task and shares the cohort of the 2026 article DOI 10.1017/S0007114525105497. The whole freeze-dried powder differs from intact frozen fruit. No Parkinson group was studied. The accessible final abstract supplies no full outcome table.

November 27, 2026 Figure 7 reinspection
The 2026 abstract reports fewer errors and better strategy in an acute meal crossover with freeze-dried red raspberry powder. The earlier investigator report for the same study plots about 7.0 versus 9.9 total eight-box errors and 6.9 versus 9.5 between-search errors at six hours. These are approximate graphical readings, not final table values. The registry lists 30 actually enrolled while the final abstract describes 36 adults. The tested-outcome denominator and numerical journal table remain unverified. The caption describes least-squares means and SEM with marked p<.05 contrasts at six hours. Exact intervals, analysis denominator and the other cognitive results were not verified.
