Original study scope
This longitudinal-record analysis compared neuropsychiatric questionnaire items in cognitively normal older adults with or without prior traumatic brain injury. The analysis uses the Neuropsychiatric Inventory Questionnaire and adjusts for clinical and demographic factors.

Continuing evidence gaps
Sleep and apathy are neuropsychiatric outcomes in a brain-injury analysis. There is no assigned or measured fresh-sage exposure and no sage contrast in Parkinson disease.
