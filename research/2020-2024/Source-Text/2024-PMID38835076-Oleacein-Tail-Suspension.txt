Title: A rare olive compound oleacein functions as a TrkB agonist and mitigates neuroinflammation both in vitro and in vivo
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11151522/
PMID: 38835076
PMCID: PMC11151522
DOI: 10.1186/s12964-024-01691-x
First public: 2024-06-04
Coverage: Factual extract from original methods and results.

Researchers orally administered isolated oleacein, an olive oil secoiridoid, at 10 mg/kg daily for ten days and used lipopolysaccharide to induce inflammatory changes in mice. They measured tail-suspension immobility and molecular outcomes. The oleacein group had less tail-suspension immobility than the LPS comparison group. The authors discuss the possibility of relevance to anxiety, but tail suspension is a depression-like behavioral proxy and the study did not report a separate anxiety assay. The exposure was an isolated compound and the model was inflammatory, without a culinary olive oil or Parkinson anxiety comparison.
