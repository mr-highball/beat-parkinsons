Title: Biochemical and behavioral effects of rosmarinic acid treatment in an animal model of Parkinson's disease induced by MPTP
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36526017/
Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0166432822005265
PMID: 36526017
DOI: 10.1016/j.bbr.2022.114257
Publication date: 2022-12-13 (first electronic publication; 2023 journal issue)
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Author abstract, PubMed dates and publisher preview. Original complete results tables remain open.

Investigators compared isolated oral rosmarinic acid and a vehicle in mice, with and without a chemical model of Parkinson-related injury. The abstract reports less of the model-associated hyperlocomotion with the compound, alongside changes in biochemical measurements. Higher activity in the earlier injected-rat study and less excess activity in the challenged mice are different outcomes under different conditions. Neither article provides independent measurement of apathy severity, comparison of dried culinary sage, or evidence from people with Parkinson disease.
