Title: The effect of novel simple saffron syrup on fatigue reduction in patients with multiple sclerosis
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32716906/
PMID: 32716906
DOI: 10.1515/jbcpp-2020-0063
First public: 2020-07-27
Coverage: Factual methods and Table 2 extract from the original author-shared publication.

Ashtiani and colleagues followed 30 people with multiple sclerosis for 60 days in one group receiving syrup containing 0.5% saffron water extract. The study identified registration IRCT2017061226667N6; the current official registration body remains unverified. Table 2 reports fatigue means of 71.10 (SD 7.76), 48.40 (9.95) and 35.30 (15.34) at baseline, day 30 and day 60. Its Methods describe nine items scored 1–7, with an untransformed maximum of 63. A scaling or conversion reconciling the reported baseline value could not be verified. The authors' within-group decline has unclear units and no control. Extract syrup and multiple sclerosis do not match dried culinary saffron in Parkinson disease.
