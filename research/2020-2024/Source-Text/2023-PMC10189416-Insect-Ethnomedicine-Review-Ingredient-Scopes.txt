Title: Unravelling the potential of insects for medicinal purposes – A comprehensive review

This review surveys insects used in traditional medicine. An account puts an ant preparation into coffee, the banana reference names an insect and the egg references include insect life stages.

Claim 0121 | brewed coffee | fatigue severity | related. Coffee is a carrier listed in an ethnomedicinal account of ants for vision, without a separately attributable coffee or Parkinson outcome.
Claim 0309 | raw banana | anxiety severity | excluded. Banana appears in a name for an insect and does not describe the fruit intervention.
Claim 0329 | poached egg | fall frequency | excluded. The entries refer to insect life stages or compounds in allergy models rather than an assigned culinary hen egg exposure.
Claim 0502 | dried date | pain severity | excluded. Calendar dates and bibliographic dates do not identify an exposure to dried date fruit. The article gives no date-fruit comparison.
