Title: Differential plasma branched-chain amino acid responses following the consumption of Greek-style yogurt and skimmed milk.
PMID: 36989535
DOI: 10.1139/apnm-2022-0416
First published: 2023
Authors: Brown JK, Perry CGR, Prior ET, Phillips SM, Skelly LE, Josse AR
Journal: Applied physiology, nutrition, and metabolism = Physiologie appliquee, nutrition et metabolisme
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36989535/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Human crossover kinetic trial comparing isonitrogenous doses (2x20 g protein) of fermented dairy (Greek-style yogurt) vs skimmed milk over 4 h postprandially. Peak plasma leucine and total branched-chain amino acid (BCAA) concentrations and AUCs were GREATER after fermented dairy than skimmed milk with similar time to peak—demonstrating fermented dairy matches or exceeds fluid cow milk in delivering levodopa-competing LNAAs.
