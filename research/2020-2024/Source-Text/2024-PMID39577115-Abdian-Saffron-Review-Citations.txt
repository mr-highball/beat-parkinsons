Title: Saffron and its major constituents against neurodegenerative diseases: A mechanistic review
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39577115/
PMID: 39577115
DOI: 10.1016/j.phymed.2024.156097
First public: 2024-09-27
Coverage: Author abstract and publisher-deposited reference list. Complete body remains inaccessible.

Abdian and colleagues report 64 studies in their systematic analysis, plus additional contextual reports. The publisher's 160-reference deposit includes trials of saffron in non-Parkinson major depression, saffron petal for non-Parkinson depression, the Ashtiani multiple-sclerosis syrup study and distinct isolated-constituent experiments. The review's subject labels include Parkinson disease, but the public abstract reports no ground-stigma Parkinson depression outcome of its own. Its citations cover preparations that cannot be treated as interchangeable. The full body and tables remain open.
