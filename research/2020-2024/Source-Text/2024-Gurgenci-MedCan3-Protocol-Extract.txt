Title: MedCan 3 advanced-cancer symptom trial — protocol and comparator scope
DOI: 10.1186/s13063-024-08091-z
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13063-024-08091-z
First published: 2024-05-01.
Coverage: Factual complete original protocol; planned outcomes are not trial results.
Reviewed: 2026-11-17

Gurgenci and colleagues describe randomized oral THC/CBD versus a matched oil placebo for distressing symptoms in advanced cancer, linked to ACTRN12622000083796. Total ESAS distress and individual symptom items are planned. The protocol does not identify walnut oil in its comparator description; the current registry separately supplies that composition.

The planned comparison cannot isolate a component of the mixed vehicle. The protocol reports recruitment beginning August 8, 2022, while the current registration lists first participant December 14, 2022. Preserve event-label and date uncertainty rather than harmonizing them silently.

A May 30, 2024 correction, DOI 10.1186/s13063-024-08195-6, concerns an author affiliation, not measured outcomes or ingredients. No completed trial result, oil-specific effect or Parkinson cohort follows from this protocol. Other MedCan formulations and earlier studies are separate trial families.
