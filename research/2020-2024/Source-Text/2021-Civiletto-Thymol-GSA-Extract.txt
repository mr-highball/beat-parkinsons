Thyme and Oregano Terpenoids Activate Autophagy and Protect Against Hepatic Steatosis
Gabriele Civiletto and colleagues. Innovation in Aging 5(Suppl 1), 334. December 17, 2021.
DOI 10.1093/geroni/igab046.1297. Original member: https://academic.oup.com/innovateage/article/5/Supplement_1/334/6466071
Complete official proceedings: https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC8679246.1/PMC8679246.1.pdf
Coverage: complete original conference member on printed page 334. Local text records selected facts.

The research team used a skeletal muscle autophagy reporter in zebrafish to screen edible molecules. The abstract reports isolated thymol responses in fish, human cells and mouse tissues and lower liver triglycerides and damage markers after supplementation in high-fat-fed mice. It does not give numeric result estimates in the conference text. The work uses an isolated constituent rather than culinary herbs and measures no gait, anxiety or sleepiness outcomes. The later publication DOI 10.1038/s43587-025-00957-4 has overlapping model and investigator scope and extends the experiments to separate locomotor and motor measures.
