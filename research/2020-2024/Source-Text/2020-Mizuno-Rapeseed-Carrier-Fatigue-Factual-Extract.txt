Mizuno and colleagues, Methods and Table 2

Original source https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7352232/

First published June 2, 2020. Participants with mild fatigue received placebo or one of two ubiquinol doses. All formulations contained rapeseed oil, with additional oil used in placebo in place of ubiquinol. At week 12, reported mean change in post-task fatigue VAS was −2.1 for placebo, −10.9 for the lower dose and −3.6 for the higher dose. Neurologic disease was excluded. The protocol supplies no known refined grade or zero-oil comparison, and the measured effects cannot be assigned to oil alone.
