Title: The rodent model of impaired gastric motility induced by allyl isothiocyanate, a pungent ingredient of wasabi, to evaluate therapeutic agents for functional dyspepsia
PMID: 33357770
DOI: 10.1016/j.jphs.2020.10.006
First published: 2021
Authors: Hashimoto K, Tashima K, Imai T, Matsumoto K, Horie S
Journal: Journal of Pharmacological Sciences
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33357770/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Conscious male ddY mouse 13C-acetic acid breath-test study demonstrating that oral allyl isothiocyanate (80 mM AITC) acutely impairs gastric motility (used as an experimental model of functional dyspepsia reversible by prokinetic drugs itopride, mosapride, acotiamide, and daikenchuto). Isolated high-concentration AITC in non-PD mice; opposes intracolonic prokinetic AITC findings in dogs.
