Title: Parizad and Abbasi Maleki on Rhus coriaria fruit extract and tail suspension
Publication date: 2023
Source: https://brieflands.com/journals/jjnpp/articles/139330
DOI: 10.5812/jjnpp-139330
Coverage: Original Methods, Results and Figures 1–2.
Reviewed: 2026-11-26

The investigators report lower tail-suspension immobility in mice after an injected ethanolic Rhus coriaria extract, with the significant comparisons concentrated above the lowest tested group. The open-field crossings and rearings did not show a statistically clear extract-related change. The behavioral direction is favorable in this assay. The locomotor finding is the separate null result. Route, extract preparation, species and endpoint limit transfer to oral ground sumac and depressive symptoms in Parkinson disease.
