Study design and exposure
- Rotenone mouse model of Parkinson's disease: mice received oral rotenone (5 mg/kg) concomitantly with intraperitoneal Hidrox (HD, an aqueous olive-pulp polyphenol extract rich in hydroxytyrosol, 10 mg/kg i.p.) for 28 days.
- Evaluated locomotor activity, catalepsy, histological nigral damage, tyrosine hydroxylase (TH), dopamine transporter (DAT), alpha-synuclein accumulation, oxidative stress, neuroinflammation, and apoptosis.

Key findings
- Hidrox co-treatment attenuated rotenone-induced catalepsy and locomotor impairments, reduced alpha-synuclein accumulation and TH/DAT loss, and lowered oxidative and neuroinflammatory markers.

Relevance and limits for Parkinson's food experiments
- Provides mechanistic olive-polyphenol (hydroxytyrosol) animal evidence relevant as indirect context for olive oil bradykinesia/rigidity claims (e.g., 0629).
- Limits: Intraperitoneal aqueous olive-pulp extract (Hidrox) rather than ingested culinary extra-virgin olive oil; mouse rotenone prevention/co-treatment model without human Parkinson clinical outcomes.
