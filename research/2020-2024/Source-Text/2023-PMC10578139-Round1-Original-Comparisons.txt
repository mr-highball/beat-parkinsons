A multiple sclerosis diet review cites a mixed modified ketogenic animal diet containing canola and flaxseed oils among other fats. Human fatigue data reflect other ketogenic regimens and patients with multiple sclerosis.

Canola oil is one of several fat sources in a mixed diet in an EAE model. The human fatigue context comes from a different disease and assignment.

Flaxseed oil is a component of a mixed EAE diet, with no separate oil comparison or measured Parkinson anxiety.
