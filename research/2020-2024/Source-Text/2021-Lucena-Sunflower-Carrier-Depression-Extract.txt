Depression reporting in the sunflower-carrier inhalation trial
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33905827/
PMID: 33905827
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2021.102726
Coverage: Original author abstract and official study identity verified; original full depression table remains open

Lucena and colleagues first published online on April 24, 2021. The author abstract analyzes the trial of lavender aroma versus the sunflower-oil carrier in 35 women with postmenopausal insomnia, 17 and 18 per arm. Both groups received sleep-hygiene guidance. The reported depression p = .025 describes change in the lavender arm. The abstract supplies no numerical between-arm depression estimate. Registry RBR-5q5t5z reports 36 included and 33 who performed the intervention. Its figures and the article's 35 are kept under their original denominators. The cohort lacks a Parkinson diagnosis and the protocol does not test oral refined oil.
