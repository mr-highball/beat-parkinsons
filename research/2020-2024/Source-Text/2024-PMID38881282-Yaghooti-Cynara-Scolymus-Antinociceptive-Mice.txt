Title: Assessment of antinociceptive property of Cynara scolymus L. and possible mechanism of action in the formalin and writhing models of nociception in mice.
PMID: 38881282
PMCID: PMC11220375
DOI: 10.3344/kjp.23355
First published: 2024
Authors: Yaghooti, Alimoahmmadi
Journal: The Korean journal of pain
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38881282/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Controlled mouse nociception study (n=6/group; 30 mice in primary dose-response plus antagonist experiments) administering hydroethanolic Cynara scolymus leaf extract (HECS; 50, 100, 200 mg/kg i.p.). HECS produced dose-dependent, statistically significant antinociception in both neurogenic and inflammatory phases of the formalin test and in acetic acid-induced writhing (P < 0.001), without altering open-field locomotor activity. Analgesia was reversed by naloxone, atropine, and flumazenil (opioidergic, cholinergic, GABAergic involvement). Limits: intraperitoneal leaf extract in non-PD mouse nociception assays.
