Title: Prebiotic effect, bioactive compounds and antioxidant capacity of melon peel (Cucumis melo L. inodorus) flour subjected to in vitro gastrointestinal digestion and human faecal fermentation
PMID: 35337586
DOI: 10.1016/j.foodres.2022.111045
First published: 2022
Authors: Gómez-García R, Sánchez-Gutiérrez M, Freitas-Costa C, Vilas-Boas AA, Campos DA, Aguilar CN
Journal: Food Research International
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35337586/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

In vitro simulated GI digestion and human fecal batch fermentation study of Cucumis melo Inodorus PEEL flour (industrial by-product), showing increased phenolic bioaccessibility and short-chain fatty acid (acetate, propionate, butyrate) production comparable to FOS. Non-flesh peel flour in an in vitro fermentation assay only; no human clinical bowel-movement or constipation trial.
