The study evaluated topical patches containing a supercritical extract from Zanthoxylum schinifolium in a rat model of migraine. Table 4 names six groups, with nominal n=10 per group. Mean head-scratching counts in the 30–60-minute interval were 14.25 ± 1.98 in the model group and 2.60 ± 1.07 in the 70 mg patch group. The article marks the contrast p < .01 and reports an 81.75% relative reduction for this interval. These are behavioral counts in an animal experiment. Underlying counts cannot be independently checked from the reported summaries.

The article is related to the pepper pain question through genus and pain-related behavior. It uses a different species and a topical extract. The results do not estimate a human Parkinson response to toasted culinary Sichuan pepper. Separate animal tolerability observations do not establish human safety.
