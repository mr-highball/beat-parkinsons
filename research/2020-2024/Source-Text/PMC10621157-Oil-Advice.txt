Published protocol with optional cooking oils
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10621157/
PMID: 37919711
PMCID: PMC10621157
DOI: 10.1186/s12902-023-01485-1
Coverage: primary article body, Table 2 and outcome assessment

The 2023 protocol for ChiCTR2200061991 studied a combined self-management program in older adults with type 2 diabetes and mild cognitive impairment. Table 2 offers olive, peanut, walnut, soybean and rapeseed oils as optional choices under dietary advice. The design assigns the support program and gives no separate oil arm, processing grade, intake or ingredient-specific adherence. The planned anxiety and depression instruments apply to the full program. Results report: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40279960/
