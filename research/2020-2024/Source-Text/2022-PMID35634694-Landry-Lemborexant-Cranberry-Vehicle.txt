The randomized crossover tested lemborexant, alcohol and their combination in healthy adults. Outcomes included body sway, attention, memory and drug levels. Alcohol with lemborexant worsened several cognitive measures in the reported comparisons. The journal report calls the administration drink nonalcoholic. The earlier M211 methods give the more specific cranberry-beverage description and its use as a shared administration vehicle.

The conference and journal reports identify the same study number and ClinicalTrials.gov registration. The poster reports 32 randomized and 18 completing all four treatments. The current registry gives actual enrolment of 24. Retain the differing denominators under their source labels. The study does not assign a separate cranberry arm or measure depressive symptoms. The beverage supplies no dried-cranberry comparison for Round 1 claim 0722.
