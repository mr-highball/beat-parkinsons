Title: Crocetin and motor measures in an experimental Parkinson model
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7569465/
PMID: 33101635
PMCID: PMC7569465
DOI: 10.1155/2020/9864370
Publication: 2020-10-10
Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article, Methods and Figure 1.

The investigators studied purified oral crocetin in a Parkinson-like mouse model and an accompanying cell model. Figure 1 reports improved rotarod time before loss of footing, hanging time, and pole performance under crocetin. A forced-swim outcome also moved toward the untreated control values while motor ability changed. An experimentally timed rod or pole task is not a count of recurrent falls and is not a passive resistance measure of rigidity. Forced swimming is a behavior proxy with possible motor confounding. No arm consumed culinary ground stigma and no person with Parkinson disease participated.
