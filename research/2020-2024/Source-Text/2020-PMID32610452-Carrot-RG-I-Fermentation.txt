Title: Carrot-derived rhamnogalacturonan-I in human microbiota and gut barrier laboratory models
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7400138/
PMID: 32610452
PMCID: PMC7400138
DOI: 10.3390/nu12071917
Publication date: 2020-06-29
Checked: 2026-11-25
Review depth: full_text

The authors compared a purified carrot-derived rhamnogalacturonan-I preparation with other substrates in fecal fermentation and barrier models. The reported changes included fermentation products such as acetate and propionate and model-specific differences in microbial composition and barrier readouts. No participant ate carrot during the laboratory exposure. The preparation is distinct from steamed carrot, and bacterial and barrier measures supply no constipation severity estimate. The later randomized human cRG-I trial PMID 41011488 measured constipation separately. Round 1 scope: 0623.
