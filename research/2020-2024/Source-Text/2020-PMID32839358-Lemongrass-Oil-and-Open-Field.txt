Title: Inhibitory potentials of Cymbopogon citratus oil against aluminium-induced behavioral deficits and neuropathology in rats

Coverage: Original full-text JATS, methods, results and Figure 1 caption.

Gbadamosi and colleagues assigned 40 male rats to five groups of eight: vehicle, steam-distilled Cymbopogon citratus essential oil, aluminum chloride, and two sequential oil/toxin schedules in opposite order. Oil-alone and vehicle groups did not differ significantly in open-field lines crossed. The toxin group crossed fewer lines; both sequential groups crossed more than toxin alone (each reported p<0.05). Figure values are graphical and not transcribed. A nonsignificant result does not establish equivalence. Open-field exploration and locomotion in a toxin model are not incident falls, and oral essential oil is not minced culinary lemongrass or a Parkinson population. This is a distinct study family from Costa 2011.
