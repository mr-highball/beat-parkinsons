Title: Risk of Poisoning from Garden Plants: Misidentification between Laurel and Cherry Laurel.
PMID: 36355976
PMCID: PMC9697506

First published: 2022
Authors: Malaspina P, Betuzzi F, Ingegneri M, Smeriglio A, Cornara L, Trombetta D
Journal: Toxins
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36355976/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Botanical authentication and toxicology study (Malaspina et al., 2022, Toxins, PMC9697506) differentiating edible Mediterranean culinary laurel (Laurus nobilis L.) from morphologically similar poisonous ornamental cherry laurel (Prunus laurocerasus L.), which contains cyanogenic glycosides. Essential botanical-safety reference confirming culinary bay leaf claims apply strictly to authenticated Laurus nobilis, excluding Prunus laurocerasus, Cinnamomum tamala (tejpat), and Caribbean Pimenta racemosa.
