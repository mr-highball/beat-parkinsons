López-Ríos et al., Central nervous system activities of extract Mangifera indica L. PMID 32473365. J Ethnopharmacol 260 (2020) 112996. DOI 10.1016/j.jep.2020.112996. Coverage: original factual extract of the human mood comparisons and COMT assay.

The first acute trial tested 500 mg of mango leaf extract or placebo. Table 2 reports POMS fatigue 2.10 (SD 1.10) before and 1.12 (SD 1.04) at 90 minutes with the extract, compared with 2.05 (SD 1.24) and 0.37 (SD 0.99) with placebo as printed. The authors report a within-extract Wilcoxon p=.015, an unspecified nonsignificant placebo test and no corresponding between-condition fatigue estimate. Study 2 did not reveal a clear leaf-extract POMS result at 60 minutes. Its arms also included caffeine and a combination. Pure mangiferin inhibited COMT in vitro, which does not establish effects of dietary mango on levodopa disposition or fatigue in Parkinson disease.
