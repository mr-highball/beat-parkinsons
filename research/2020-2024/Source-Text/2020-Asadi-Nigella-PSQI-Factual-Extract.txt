Topic: Oily Nigella extract and postmenopausal sleep quality
Source: https://publish.kne-publishing.com/index.php/JFRH/article/download/4672/4256/6483
Coverage: Primary Methods and Tables 1–2, factual extract
Asadi and colleagues assigned 60 postmenopausal participants to nigella, zolpidem or placebo for eight weeks. The Methods describe an oily seed extract and give inconsistent amounts. Table 2 prints PSQI 7.2 to 6.6 for nigella and 11 to 9.5 for placebo, with overall p=.45. The prose gives a nigella end point of 6.0 and the table gives a positive change for a falling score. The categorical sleep-quality p=.07 is a separate within-arm result. Table 1 shows 7 of 20 with good sleep at baseline and 8 of 20 at follow-up. Those paired marginals differ by one. The standard uncorrected McNemar statistic can therefore be at most 1, which cannot give p=.07. A paired cross-tab or a published correction is needed.
