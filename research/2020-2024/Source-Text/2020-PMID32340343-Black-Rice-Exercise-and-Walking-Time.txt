Title: Combined black rice germ and bran supplement and exercise in older adults
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7215642/
PMID: 32340343
PMCID: PMC7215642
DOI: 10.3390/ijerph17082931
Publication date: 2020-04-23
Reviewed: 2026-11-26
Coverage: Original Methods, Results, Figure 4 and Table 6.

The investigators report four groups: black rice germ and bran (30), exercise (30), the combined intervention (28), and control (32). Table 6 reports median six-meter time for the combined arm from 5.35 seconds at baseline to 4.76 seconds at week 24. The supplement-only arm reports 4.52 to 4.37 seconds without a significant within-arm change. The original Results report differences for several changes when the combined program is compared with exercise alone, without presenting those contrast values in the table. The table's gait row uses seconds, although introductory language calls it gait speed.

This trial evaluates a pigmented-rice supplement rather than cooked brown rice. The motor measures are walking time, sit-to-stand and grip. It has no separate postural-stability comparison in Parkinson disease.
