The formulation combines edible crab and virgin coconut oil. The researchers examined cell and rat disease models and reported favorable broad behavioral, tissue and biochemical results for the combination. The abstract does not provide individual motor tests, analyzed group counts, or separate human tremor outcomes. At this access depth there is no verified isolated coconut-oil contrast from this paper. The complete original remains an access follow-up for arm details and estimates.

This experiment is a separate publication from the 2022 cell and fly toxicology study and the distinct 2024 P90 meeting abstract on plain virgin coconut oil in mice. The 2022 and 2024 Deepika reports repeat the Kerala practice description and belong to one cultural lineage.
