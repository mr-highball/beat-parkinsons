Corn oil label in an early SHAPE conference listing
Source: https://venuewest.eventsair.com/QuickEventWebsitePortal/isbnpa2020annualmeeting/eventinfo/Agenda/AgendaItemDetail?id=bfc9453b-0013-4a09-8424-a4aa2ca8d04d
Registration: NCT03603041
Coverage: Original investigator poster listing and arm denominators checked

The ISBNPA 2020 listing describes a 16-week trial in postmenopausal women. One six-person arm received 25 g whey protein with 4.3 g of comparator oil described as corn. The listing reports an overall intervention effect for sleep efficiency (p = .03) and the global sleep-quality score (p = .01). It supplies no numerical whey-plus-oil versus whey-only estimate. The published May 2020 report has the same five group sizes, 6, 4, 10, 6 and 8, and describes soybean oil in the comparator arm. The discrepancy leaves the conference listing insufficient to establish actual corn-oil exposure.
