Title: Bixin attenuates mechanical allodynia, anxious and depressive-like behaviors associated with experimental diabetes counteracting oxidative stress and glycated hemoglobin.
PMID: 34107278
DOI: 10.1016/j.brainres.2021.147557
First published: 2021
Authors: Gasparin, Rosa, Jesus, Guiloski, da Silva de Assis
Journal: Brain research
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34107278/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

17-day controlled experiment in streptozotocin-induced diabetic rats treated orally with vehicle, bixin (apocarotenoid extracted from Bixa orellana seeds; 10, 30, or 90 mg/kg p.o.), or insulin (6 IU s.c.). Repeated oral bixin significantly attenuated mechanical allodynia (electronic von Frey, all doses), anxious-like behavior (elevated plus maze, all doses), and depressive-like behavior (forced swimming test, 30 and 90 mg/kg) without altering open-field locomotor performance, while normalizing brain/spinal/nerve GSH and lipid peroxidation and lowering HbA1. Limits: isolated seed apocarotenoid in diabetic rat model rather than ground seed or human PD.
