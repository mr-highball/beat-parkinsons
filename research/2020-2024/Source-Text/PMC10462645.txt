Title: α-Synuclein pathology in post-mortem retina and optic nerve is specific for α-synucleinopathies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10462645/
PMID: 37640753
PMCID: PMC10462645
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-023-00570-5

The postmortem study examines alpha-synuclein pathology in retinal and optic nerve tissue across diagnoses. Histopathology offers an ocular biomarker contrast without food exposure or measured visual episode change.
