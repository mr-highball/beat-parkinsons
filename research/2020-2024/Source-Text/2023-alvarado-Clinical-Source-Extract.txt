Inhaled rosemary oil and depression symptom scores
2023 (March–April issue; first exact online date unverified)
https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/599198262.pdf

Alvarado-García and colleagues assigned existing classes to inhale rosemary essential oil or remain on a waiting list, analyzing 37 and 36 participants. Zung depression scores went from 49.65 to 45.86 with the oil and from 49.39 to 49.81 in the comparison group. Posttest p=.020 was reported for the group contrast. It was not a comparison of changes. The rosemary leaves were sourced fresh, then dried and hydrodistilled. Course allocation, an unblinded waiting-list comparison, and the inhaled product limit transfer to dietary leaves and Parkinson disease.
