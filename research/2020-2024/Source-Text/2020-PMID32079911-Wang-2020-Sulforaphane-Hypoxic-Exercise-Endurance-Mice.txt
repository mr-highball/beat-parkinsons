Title: Nrf2 Activation Enhances Muscular MCT1 Expression and Hypoxic Exercise Capacity.
PMID: 32079911
DOI: 10.1249/MSS.0000000000002312
First published: 2020
Authors: Wang L, Zhu R, Wang J, Yu S, Wang J, Zhang Y
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32079911/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical study in C57BL/6J mice showing sulforaphane pretreatment prolonged incremental treadmill exercise endurance under acute hypoxia via skeletal muscle MCT1 upregulation and lower blood lactate. Measures acute hypoxic athletic endurance in healthy mice rather than chronic PD fatigue.
