Title: Eugenol and its association with levodopa in 6-hydroxydopamine-induced hemiparkinsonian rats: Behavioural and neurochemical alterations.
PMID: 32353201
DOI: 10.1111/bcpt.13425
First published: 2020
Authors: Moreira Vasconcelos CF, da Cunha Ferreira NM, Hardy Lima Pontes N, de Sousa Dos Reis TD, Basto Souza R, Aragão Catunda Junior FE, Vasconcelos Aguiar LM, Maranguape Silva da Cunha R
Journal: Basic & clinical pharmacology & toxicology
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32353201/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical intrastriatal 6-OHDA hemiparkinsonian Wistar rat model testing oral isolated eugenol (0.1, 1, or 10 mg/kg/d), levodopa (25 mg/kg), or eugenol 10 mg/kg + levodopa 12.5 mg/kg for 14 days. Reported reduced oxidative stress and behavioral disturbances and increased reduced glutathione with eugenol + levodopa compared with levodopa alone. Tests isolated synthetic/purified eugenol rather than culinary ground Pimenta dioica fruit and conflicts with post-lesion worsening reported in 6-OHDA/MPTP mouse models (Kabuto 2011; Vora 2022).
