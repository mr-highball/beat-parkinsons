Coverage: Selected factual extract from the original six-page article. Methods pp.872–873 and Figures 1–2 on printed p.873 inspected. CC BY 4.0 original.

The comparison selected four trained people with Parkinson disease and four untrained controls. The report describes bilateral biceps surface EMG and total UPDRS. Figure 1 displays four treated participants' pairs of traces with visually smaller post-session amplitudes. Figure 2 displays four treated participants' composite scores at the initial assessment and at 30, 45 and 60 minutes. The figures supply no control curves, reproducible between-group estimate or separately defined rest-tremor measure. The authors report that controls lacked the improvements.

Methods describes a 10-minute assessment, while the abstract and results describe 30 minutes. The report does not specify randomization, masking, group-specific baseline comparability, a clear medication-timing protocol or adverse-event ascertainment. Protocol and controlled outcome data remain unresolved. The associated ceremonial context describes this same report. No food claim receives an assessment from this comparison.
