Title: Genetics Contributes to Concomitant Pathology and Clinical Presentation in Dementia with Lewy Bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8461715/
PMID: 34308904
PMCID: PMC8461715
DOI: 10.3233/JAD-210365
Publication date: 2021-07-23
Source depth: PMC original full body from structured article text; locally distilled

Date basis: PMC first electronic pre-press date. The electronic journal version is dated 2021-08-31.
Design: Genetic association study

The study considered 190 probable dementia-with-Lewy-bodies cases and 2,552 controls. Genetic risk and Alzheimer co-pathology were compared with clinical features. Hallucination strata describe phenotype, without a dried food intervention or an episode-frequency response.
