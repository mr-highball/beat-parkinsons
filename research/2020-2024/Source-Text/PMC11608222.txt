Title: Identifying Parkinson's disease and its stages using static standing balance
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11608222/
PMID: 39616268
PMCID: PMC11608222
DOI: 10.1038/s41746-024-01351-x
Publication date: 2024-11-30
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The study uses standing balance features among 210 enrolled participants to identify Parkinson disease or separate clinical stages. Postural measures and diagnostic model performance do not represent visual hallucination episodes after eating.
