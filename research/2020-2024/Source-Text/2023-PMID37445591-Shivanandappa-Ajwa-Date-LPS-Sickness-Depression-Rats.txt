Title: Phoenix dactylifera (Ajwa Dates) Alleviate LPS-Induced Sickness Behaviour in Rats by Attenuating Proinflammatory Cytokines and Oxidative Stress in the Brain.
PMID: 37445591
PMCID: PMC10341400
DOI: 10.3390/ijms241310413
First published: 2023
Authors: Shivanandappa TB, Alotaibi G, Chinnadhurai M, Dachani SR, Ahmad MD, Aldaajanii KA
Journal: Int J Mol Sci
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10341400/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Preclinical rodent behavioral study (Shivanandappa et al., 2023, PMC10341400) evaluating dried Ajwa date fruit aqueous-ethanolic extract (250 and 500 mg/kg) in LPS-induced acute neuroinflammation sickness-behavior rats. Dried Ajwa fruit extract significantly reduced immobility in the Forced Swim Test and Tail Suspension Test (p<0.01-0.001) and attenuated pro-inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-alpha) and oxidative stress. Limits: rodent acute LPS inflammatory depression model using concentrated extract rather than clinical Parkinson depression.
