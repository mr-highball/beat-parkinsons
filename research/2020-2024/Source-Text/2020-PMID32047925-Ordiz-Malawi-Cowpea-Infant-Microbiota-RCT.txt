Title: The effect of legume supplementation on the gut microbiota in rural Malawian infants aged 6 to 12 months.
PMID: 32047925
PMCID: PMC8287943
DOI: 10.1093/ajcn/nqaa011
Registry ID: NCT02472262
First published: 2020
Authors: Ordiz MI, Janssen S, Humphrey G, Ackermann G, Stephenson K, Agapova S, et al.
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32047925/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Double-blind randomized controlled trial (Ordiz et al., 2020, Am J Clin Nutr, PMC8287943, NCT02472262) analyzing 1,249 stool samples from 236 rural Malawian infants (6-12 months) supplemented daily with cowpea flour, common bean flour, or control. Neither cowpea nor common bean significantly altered fecal 16S microbiota alpha-diversity or overall weighted UniFrac configuration. Companion 48-week trial in 331 Malawian children aged 12-23 months (PMID:29490090, NCT02472301) also found null cowpea effects on linear growth and intestinal permeability. Limits: pediatric microbiome/growth trials without bowel symptom or neurological endpoints.
