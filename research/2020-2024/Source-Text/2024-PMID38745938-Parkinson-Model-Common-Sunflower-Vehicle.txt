Title: Non-motor symptoms associated with progressive loss of dopaminergic neurons in a mouse model of Parkinson's disease
Publication date: 2024-04-30
PMID: 38745938
PMCID: PMC11091341
DOI: 10.3389/fnins.2024.1375265
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11091341/
Text coverage: Distilled primary full text, exposure allocation, outcome schedule and results boundaries.

The investigators evaluated gait in male and female animals and operant sensation seeking and buried-food olfaction in male animals. The treated genotype and controls both received tamoxifen in sunflower oil. The paper tracks consequences of progressive dopaminergic loss and related behavioral domains under that shared administration. The common injected oil vehicle supplies no estimate of oral cold-pressed oil. The behavioral endpoints are model behavior rather than a separate clinical executive-function measure.
