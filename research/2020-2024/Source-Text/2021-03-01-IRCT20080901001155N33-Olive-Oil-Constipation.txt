Registered comparison of extra virgin and ordinary olive oils
Source: https://en.irct.ir/trial/54490
Registration: IRCT20080901001155N33
Coverage: Original official registration March and May revisions and published report linkage

IRCT20080901001155N33 was first registered March 1, 2021, with 150 planned participants. The registration compares polyphenol-rich and ordinary olive oils for four weeks in adults with chronic constipation. Rome III symptom scoring is planned at baseline and week four and Bristol stool assessments are planned weekly. The May 7, 2021 registry version changes both arms from a written 4 mL/L per day to 20 mL/day. The parenthetical about two tablespoons appears in both versions. The 2025 report describes two tablespoons of oil consumed raw daily and analyzes 140 across the two groups. The registry and article use the same trial and ethics identifiers. The trial excludes neurological causes of constipation and contains no no-oil comparator.
