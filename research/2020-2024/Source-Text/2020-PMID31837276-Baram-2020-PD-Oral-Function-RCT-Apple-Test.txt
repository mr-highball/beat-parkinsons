Title: Improvement of oral function and hygiene in Parkinson's disease: A randomised controlled clinical trial.
PMID: 31837276
DOI: 10.1111/joor.12924
First published: 2020
Authors: Baram S, Karlsborg M, Bakke M
Journal: Journal of oral rehabilitation
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31837276/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Randomized controlled clinical trial in n=29 moderate-to-advanced PD patients testing standardized jaw-opening/chewing and oral-hygiene exercises. Standardized apple slices were used solely as a diagnostic masticatory test bolus rather than a dietary intervention.
