Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Ethnobotanical survey (Petran et al., 2024, Front Pharmacol, PMC11150775) interviewing 326 mothers in Southern Romania regarding folk plant remedies for children. Documents occasional traditional use of Prunus avium (sweet cherry) STALKS (stems, not raw or dried cherry fruit) for pediatric urinary tract ailments, distinct from eating cherry fruit and supplying no controlled urinary urgency evidence.
