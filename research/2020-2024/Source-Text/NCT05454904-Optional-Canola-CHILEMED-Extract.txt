# Optional canola and psychological outcomes in CHILEMED

Original source https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05454904
Source coverage official registration and original protocol cross-reference

NCT05454904 first posted July 12, 2022. CHILEMED compares low-fat counseling, Mediterranean counseling and Mediterranean counseling with psychological support among adults with metabolic syndrome. Mediterranean guidance offers canola cooking or dressing oil or avocado as alternatives. No fixed oil dose or pressing method is specified. The published protocol includes a Depression Anxiety Stress Scale among secondary measures. An oil-specific anxiety estimate requires a separate exposure comparison.
