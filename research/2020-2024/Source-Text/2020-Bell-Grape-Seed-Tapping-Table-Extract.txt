Grape seed extract and simple and complex finger tapping

Primary source: https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/88613/3/Bell%20et%20al%2C%20Nutritional%20Neuroscience%2C%20author%20final%20copy.pdf
Coverage: Original full author manuscript and official registry identity
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published online January 16, 2020. Sixty healthy adults aged 18–30 received 400 mg grape-seed polyphenol extract or maltodextrin for 12 weeks. The baseline-adjusted simple tapping estimates in 30 s at weeks 6 and 12 were 123.24 and 125.71 with extract versus 124.36 and 121.43 with placebo. Treatment-by-visit p=.005. Complex sequences showed no clear interaction. The Methods identify NCT03526406. A preliminary page instead names NCT03579095, whose official record assigns American ginseng. The study used isolated seed extract and healthy participants.
