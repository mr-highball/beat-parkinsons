Title: Effects of artichoke on blood pressure: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
PMID: 33465383
DOI: 10.1016/j.ctim.2021.102668
First published: 2021
Authors: Moradi, Sohrabi, Golbidi, Yarmohammadi, Hemati
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33465383/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 8 RCTs evaluating artichoke supplementation on resting blood pressure. Pooled overall effects were null (SBP WMD -0.77 mmHg, 95% CI -2.76 to 1.22; DBP -0.11 mmHg, 95% CI -1.72 to 1.50), while hypertensive subgroup analysis showed reductions in SBP (-3.19 mmHg) and DBP (-2.33 mmHg). Updated 2025 meta-analysis (Jafari, PMID 41270328) reported pooled SBP -2.49 mmHg and DBP -1.53 mmHg. Limits: supine/resting BP only; no postural challenge or orthostatic lightheadedness assessed.
