Title: Premotor and fronto-striatal mechanisms associated with presence hallucinations in dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8403753/
PMID: 34461436
PMCID: PMC8403753
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2021.102791
Publication date: 2021-08-23
Source depth: PMC original full body from structured article text; locally distilled

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC article metadata and PubMed.
Design: Clinical imaging and symptom phenotype comparison

The study compares 10 dementia-with-Lewy-bodies participants reporting a sensed presence and 24 without it. It describes sensed presence as perceiving somebody nearby without seeing or hearing them. The PET and clinical comparisons are context for separating this experience from visual episodes.
