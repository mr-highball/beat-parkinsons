Title: Role of Vacha (Acorus calamus Linn.) in Neurological and Metabolic Disorders
PMID: 32325895
PMCID: PMC7230970
DOI: 10.3390/jcm9041176
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7230970/
Date basis: 2020-04-19 electronic publication, original journal header.
Coverage: Factual summary of the complete review and ethnomedicine tables.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The principal plant is Acorus calamus. Tables describe it together with black pepper, ginger, or saffron in different remedies. The saffron and horse-milk example is embedded in a separate customary application. The cited neurological and digestive formulations are mixtures, with no single-food effect. The Asparagus racemosus entry describes a different species from edible garden asparagus. The review contains no identified date-palm comparison.
