Persian barley water in a multicomponent hyperthyroidism case
Source: https://doi.org/10.1159/000541255
PMID: 39236697
DOI: 10.1159/000541255
Coverage: factual extract from primary publisher abstract

Mohammad Mahdi Parvizi and colleagues. Complementary Medicine Research 31(6):572–576. First published September 5, 2024.
The report concerns a 27-year-old woman with uncontrolled hyperthyroidism and anxiety among several symptoms after previous methimazole and prednisolone treatment. The Persian medicine treatment package included lifestyle changes, lemon balm tea, and Persian barley water. The authors describe improved anxiety and other symptoms around two months with thyroid indicators also changing. The abstract does not give an anxiety scale, exact barley-water recipe or dose, or a single-intervention comparison. The preparation differs from eating boiled barley grains or porridge, and the case gives no Parkinson comparison.
