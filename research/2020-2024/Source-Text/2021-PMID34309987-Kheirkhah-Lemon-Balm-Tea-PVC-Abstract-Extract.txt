Title: Brewed lemon balm and ventricular premature beats in an open-label randomized study
Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ijcp.14644
PMID: 34309987
DOI: 10.1111/ijcp.14644
Publication date: 2021-07-26
Date basis: The publisher gives July 26 as first publication. PubMed displays August 1 as the electronic publication date.
Text coverage: Publisher and NLM author abstracts. Main body and participant flow were unavailable.
Reviewed: 2026-11-18

In the 12-week randomized study, 60 patients with moderate to low-grade premature ventricular contractions were enrolled. Both groups received lifestyle recommendations. The intervention group also received teabags with 2 g dried lemon balm leaves per 250 mL hot water, twice daily. This is a human brewed-leaf exposure with no matched tea placebo.

The adjusted 24-hour Holter premature-ventricular-beat means were 2142.39 with tea and 3126.05 in controls (p=.017). The total heartbeat finding was a within-tea-group change. The abstract reports favorable adjusted contrasts for triglycerides, total cholesterol and fasting glucose, with unclear LDL and HDL differences. It lists 40 women as 67.8%, which implies a denominator of 59, and gives 60 as enrolled. The accessible abstract does not reconcile these denominators.

Cardiac ectopy, total heartbeats and measured metabolic markers are different endpoints from depressive symptom severity. The abstract supplies no Parkinson cohort, mood comparison or formal postural-blood-pressure outcome. The public registry cross-reference and exact analyzed sample remain follow-up items.
