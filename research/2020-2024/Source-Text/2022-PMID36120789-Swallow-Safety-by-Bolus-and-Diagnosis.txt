Title: Swallow Safety and Laryngeal Kinematics: A Comparison of Dysphagia Between Parkinson’s Disease and Cerebrovascular Accident
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9661323/
PMID: 36120789
PMCID: PMC9661323
DOI: 10.3233/JPD-223272
Publication date: 2022
Coverage: Paraphrased original PMC JATS methods and results.

The final referred cohort comprised 60 people with Parkinson disease and 50 with cerebrovascular accident, all with oropharyngeal dysphagia. The analyses covered 844 swallows and test boluses of thin liquid, nectar-thick liquid and pudding consistency. The study compared laryngeal timing, swallowing impairment and airway invasion. Table 2 and its accompanying prose describe the swallow-level event distributions. These denominators are repeated swallows of standardized assessment textures. The study offers no fig exposure or direct drooling severity comparison.
