Marjoram oil registry for bruxism and sleep complaints
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06674733
Registration: NCT06674733
Coverage: Official ClinicalTrials.gov protocol

First posted November 5, 2024, with 30 actually enrolled and no posted results. The study compares marjoram oil application with botulinum toxin for bruxism and lists a joint symptom index, ultrasound and a sleep-quality and insomnia questionnaire. Bruxism and sleep quality do not identify REM dream enactment. The record gives no oral fresh herb or Parkinson intervention. A 2025 abstract has matching design details and title but does not print this trial ID.
