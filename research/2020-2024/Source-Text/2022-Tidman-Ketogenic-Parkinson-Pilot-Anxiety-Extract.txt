Ketogenic Parkinson pilot and baseline to twelve week anxiety

Primary source: https://doi.org/10.2217/nmt-2021-0033
Coverage: Original article Table 2 and study design
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

First published February 18, 2022. In the one-group 12-week Parkinson diet pilot, the reported change in total PAS was −3.06 points (SD 4.06, n=16, p=.0086). The later seven-person follow-up recruited from this pilot, so the two do not constitute independent replications. Neither assigns an avocado-oil-only contrast or identifies refined oil consumption.
