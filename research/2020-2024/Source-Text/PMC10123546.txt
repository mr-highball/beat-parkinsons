Title: Immunohistochemical Detection of Synuclein Pathology in Skin in Idiopathic Rapid Eye Movement Sleep Behavior Disorder and Parkinsonism
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10123546/
PMID: 33232556
PMCID: PMC10123546
DOI: 10.1002/mds.28399
Publication date: 2020-11-24
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: Clinical skin-biopsy assay comparison

Investigators tested an immunohistochemical assay for pathologic alpha-synuclein in skin across sleep behavior disorder, Parkinson disease, atypical parkinsonism and controls. Biopsy assay performance is the outcome; it does not measure exposure-linked visual symptoms.
