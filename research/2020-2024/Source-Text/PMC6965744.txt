Title: Electrophysiological resting state networks of predominantly akinetic-rigid Parkinson patients: Effects of dopamine therapy
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6965744/
PMID: 31954989
PMCID: PMC6965744
DOI: 10.1016/j.nicl.2019.102147
Publication date: 2020-01-10
Source depth: complete original PMC structured article body; local research digest

Date basis: Electronic publication date in PMC and PubMed.
Design: Electrophysiologic cohort and medication comparison

Nineteen patients with predominantly akinetic-rigid Parkinson disease and twelve controls were recruited. Resting electrophysiological networks were compared with regard to dopamine therapy and diagnosis. The contrast concerns drug effects and physiological networks, without food exposure or visual episode counting.
