Perilla seed oil in a fixed-order constipation pilot
Source: https://doi.org/10.3390/dietetics2020015
Coverage: factual extract of primary methods and Section 3.3
Reviewed: 2026-11-15

Kawamura and Sugita assessed eight sedentary women in an oil phase followed by a placebo phase after a ten-month washout. Constipation score within the oil phase fell at weeks two and three (p = .035 and .020) and had p = .052 at the endpoint. The design was nonrandomized, used within-phase comparisons and tested seed oil. Carryover and time effects remain possible.
