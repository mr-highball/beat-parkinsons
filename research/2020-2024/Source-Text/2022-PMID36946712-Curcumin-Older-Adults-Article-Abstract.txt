Registry results: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03085680?tab=results

The published pilot describes the same 9 active and 8 placebo allocations, approximately 12 weeks of follow-up, a 400-m walking assessment and the curcumin intervention. It identifies the supplement as Curcumin C3 Complex. The reported Cohen effect sizes favor the supplement on the Short Physical Performance Battery (d=.75) and knee extension and flexion peak torque (d=.69 and .82). The abstract provides no arm-level walking or separate pain estimate. The source only supports scope and the results specified in the abstract. Full article methods and tables were not retrieved in this pass. The published erratum confirms the placebo was microcrystalline cellulose and fixes the manuscript label. It supplies no revised outcome estimates.

The publicly posted registry supplies the walking and pain arm means. The matching cohort and endpoints support treating the publications as one trial, with no independent replication implied by two reports.
