Title: Brain glucose metabolism and nigrostriatal degeneration in isolated rapid eye movement sleep behaviour disorder

The study examines cerebral glucose metabolism and nigrostriatal degeneration in isolated REM sleep behavior disorder. Imaging and prodromal clinical measurements do not record dried fig intake or visual hallucinatory effects after a meal.
