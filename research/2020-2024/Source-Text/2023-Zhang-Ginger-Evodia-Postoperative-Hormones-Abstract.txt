Title: Topical ginger and Evodia after laparoscopic gastric cancer surgery — hormones and complications
Original title: 基于ERAS理念探究循经热熨法对腹腔镜胃癌术后患者胃动素、胃泌素及并发症的影响
Source: https://zyyj.cbpt.cnki.net/portal/journal/portal/client/paper/9715ca90ccc8258062716e54a2072286
Date basis: 2023-04-10 original journal release, Research of Traditional Chinese Medicine 36(4), pp. 29–32.
Coverage: Factual extract of the original publisher abstract. Full tables remain unavailable.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The abstract describes 100 randomized patients with gastric cancer after laparoscopic anastomosis. Both groups received postoperative care and one group also received a heated dried-ginger and Evodia pack. The abstract reports 47/49 classified as improved versus 36/48 controls. Motilin and gastrin were higher in the intervention group on postoperative days 3 and 5. The reported complication proportions were 8.16% and 27.08%. No item-level constipation estimate is given.

The lead author, hospital, enrollment, completer denominators, schedule and part of the funding lineage match the 2022 report. The anatomical wording differs. Cohort reuse is likely and the two reports are treated as one possible trial family until the full methods or thesis resolve the relationship.
