Acute drooling after a snack containing watermelon seed paste
Publication: 2022-11-10
Source: https://d197for5662m48.cloudfront.net/documents/publicationstatus/93623/preprint_pdf/440d5aa127355bd36cce371d29ebe9d8.pdf
Coverage: Kimberley Cousins, Scott Sicherer and Amanda Agyemang, original author preprint first posted November 10, 2022. No journal version verified.
DOI: 10.22541/au.166805825.55777232/v1

The two-year-old developed swelling, hives, vomiting, gasping and excess drooling after a mixed snack that included watermelon seed paste. Skin testing to the snack and to roasted seed butter was positive using nonstandardized materials. The case did not include a confirmatory oral challenge. Watermelon flesh had previously been tolerated. The measured context is acute allergy in a child, without a Parkinson drooling severity comparison.
