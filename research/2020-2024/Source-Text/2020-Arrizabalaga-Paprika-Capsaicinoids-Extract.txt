Title: Determination of capsaicinoids and carotenoids for the characterization and geographical origin authentication of paprika by UHPLC–APCI–HRMS
Authors: Ane Arrizabalaga-Larrañaga and colleagues
Date: 2020-11-10 first publication date recorded by the University of Barcelona
Identifier: DOI 10.1016/j.lwt.2020.110533
Sources: https://diposit.ub.edu/dspace/bitstream/2445/173386/1/704257.pdf
https://diposit.ub.edu/items/3ffc3878-2697-4697-9651-de7c1d26f939/full
Coverage: Original factual extract of Methods and Supplementary Tables S1–S2.

Commercial paprika samples (n=136) included products from La Vera and Murcia in Spain, Hungary and the Czech Republic. The lab measured several capsaicinoids and carotenoids using liquid chromatography and high-resolution mass spectrometry. Among the La Vera samples, mean total capsaicinoids were 942 ± 554 mg/kg in the hot grade, 9 ± 6 mg/kg in sweet, and 31 ± 31 mg/kg in bittersweet. These are compositional results. The spice grades cannot be assumed to deliver a uniform constituent exposure, and the study did not measure health outcomes.
