Title: Anorectal manometry and abdominal-pain reporting in Parkinson disease
PMID: 37476327
PMCID: PMC10354598
DOI: 10.1002/mdc3.13755
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10354598/
First publication: 2023-05-08
Coverage: Original PMC article HTML body and Tables 2–3 checked and summarized.

The retrospective Parkinson and constipation series uses high-resolution anorectal manometry and a balloon expulsion test. Table 2 records abdominal pain as present in 21 of 36 participants with symptom data. It is a presence count and supplies no graded pain outcome. The study compares patterns of anal pressure, rectal sensation and expulsion, including sex-stratified comparisons. There is no date-fruit exposure group or change in pain following ingestion.
