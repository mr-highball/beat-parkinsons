Title: A date pit induced aorto-oesophageal fistula: a case report and concise literature review
PMID: 33614050
PMCID: PMC7885142
DOI: 10.1093/omcr/omaa140
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7885142/
Publication: 2021-02-15 electronic publication
Coverage: Original NCBI JATS full case.

An adult accidentally swallowed a date pit and later developed painful swallowing and upper gastrointestinal bleeding. Clinicians reported a vascular and oesophageal injury. This single foreign-body report supplies a mechanical safety observation. It gives no risk estimate, dried date flesh intervention or Parkinson pain comparison.
