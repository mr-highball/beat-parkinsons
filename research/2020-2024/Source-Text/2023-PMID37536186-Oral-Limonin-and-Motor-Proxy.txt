Title: Oral administration of Limonin (LM) exerts neuroprotective effects by inhibiting neuron autophagy and microglial activation in 6-OHDA-injected rats
PMID: 37536186
DOI: 10.1016/j.intimp.2023.110739
Publication year: 2023
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37536186/
Coverage: Author abstract, including the species, oral constituent exposure and reported outcomes.

The author abstract reports improved motor activity in rats exposed to a neurotoxin, with tissue and cellular outcomes under isolated oral limonin. Motor activity is reported broadly in the available description. A separate resting-tremor result, a fresh-fruit preparation and an equivalent dietary exposure remain unverified.
