Avocado oil comparator and acute tranquility

Primary source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10175152/
Coverage: Original VAMS definition and outcome table
Text: original factual summary prepared November 16, 2026 UTC.

In the 40-person CBD trial, mean tranquility scores were 297.3 to 301.3 in the mint-flavored avocado oil comparator and 303.0 to 302.4 with CBD. The adjusted comparison is p=.85. This is one more outcome from the existing NCT04590495 study family. It has no oil-free arm, confirmed refined grade or Parkinson anxiety result.
