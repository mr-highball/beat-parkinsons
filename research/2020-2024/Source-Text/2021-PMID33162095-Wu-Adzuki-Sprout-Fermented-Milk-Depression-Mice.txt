Title: Effect of adzuki bean sprout fermented milk enriched in γ-aminobutyric acid on mild depression in a mouse model.
PMID: 33162095
DOI: 10.3168/jds.2020-19154
First published: 2021
Authors: Wu Z, Wang P, Pan D, Zeng X, Guo Y, Zhao G
Journal: Journal of dairy science
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33162095/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 2 review).

Preclinical mouse study (Wu et al., 2021, J Dairy Sci) evaluating GABA-enriched (241.30 +/- 1.62 ug/mL) adzuki bean (Vigna angularis) sprout fermented milk prepared with four lactic acid bacteria strains (Streptococcus thermophilus, Lactobacillus bulgaricus, Lactobacillus plantarum, and Lactobacillus brevis J1) in a chronic-depression C57BL/6 mouse model. Treatment increased social interaction, movement-related pleasure, GABAB-cAMP-PKA-CREB signaling, and hippocampal 5-HT, norepinephrine, and dopamine. Limits: heavily bundled multi-component dairy-probiotic-sprout-GABA formulation cannot isolate cooked or boiled adzuki beans; non-PD mouse model.
