Kiwi extract mouse memory report with inconsistent methods
Original article Methods, Figure 1 and ethics statement. DOI 10.13005/bpj/2931.
Kaur and colleagues’ 2024 article describes dried kiwi extract with a scopolamine model, water-maze and other behavioral measures. Methods describe a six-day intervention and animal ethics approval. Figure 1 gives a different 21-day schedule and dose labels, while the final ethics section denies animal or human experiments. The original leaves group-level data and procedures unresolved. The published account supplies no patient objective attention measure or Parkinson comparison.
