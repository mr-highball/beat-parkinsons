Original study scope
This review gives clove and nutmeg odors as cues in a rodent discrimination and reversal-learning task. Changing the reward contingency measures learning and cognitive flexibility.

Continuing evidence gaps
The sensory stimuli are genuine ingredient-related leads. Reward choices in a smell-cue task do not measure clinical anxiety, pain or depression after eating those ingredients.
