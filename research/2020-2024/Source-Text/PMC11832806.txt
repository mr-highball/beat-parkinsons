Title: Early Changes in the Locus Coeruleus in Mild Cognitive Impairment with Lewy Bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11832806/
PMID: 39535454
PMCID: PMC11832806
DOI: 10.1002/mds.30058
Publication date: 2024-11-13
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The researchers measure locus coeruleus MRI differences between mild cognitive impairment with Lewy bodies and healthy controls. These measures evaluate neurodegeneration and diagnosis; the study does not track visual episodes after eating dried figs.
