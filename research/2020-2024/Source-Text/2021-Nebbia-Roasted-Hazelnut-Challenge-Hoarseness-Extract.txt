Nebbia et al. Oleosin Cor a 15 is a novel allergen for Italian hazelnut allergic children. Pediatr Allergy Immunol. Published online 2021 Jul 5.
DOI 10.1111/pai.13579. PMID 34146442. PMCID PMC8596585.
Original: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8596585/
Coverage: Factual Methods and Table 1 outcome summary from the primary report.

The Turin cohort comprised 27 children with hazelnut allergy and ten sensitized children who tolerated hazelnuts. The supervised challenge used finely ground roasted whole hazelnut. Table 1 records hoarseness for three participants in the roasted challenge symptom column. Proteins associated with oil bodies of raw and roasted nuts were analyzed separately by laboratory immunoblotting. The study did not administer expressed oil or measure acoustic voice intensity in a Parkinson population. This was a separate cohort from the later Rome report with pooled pediatric food challenges.
