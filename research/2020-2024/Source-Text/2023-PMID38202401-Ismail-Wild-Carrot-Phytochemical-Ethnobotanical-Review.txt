Title: The Wild Carrot (Daucus carota): A Phytochemical and Pharmacological Review.
PMID: 38202401
PMCID: PMC10781147
DOI: 10.3390/plants13010093
First published: 2023
Authors: Ismail J, Shebaby WN, Daher J, Boulos JC, Taleb R, Daher CF et al.
Journal: Plants (Basel)
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38202401/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Comprehensive phytochemical and ethnobotanical review (1927-2023) of wild Daucus carota subspecies documenting traditional carminative, diuretic, antilithic, and anti-inflammatory folk uses across Mediterranean and global regions; distinguishes wild subspecies from cultivated D. carota ssp. sativus and emphasizes lack of human clinical efficacy trials.
