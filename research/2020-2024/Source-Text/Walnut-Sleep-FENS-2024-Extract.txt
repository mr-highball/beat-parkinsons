Title: Walnut Consumption Improves Sleep Quality: A Randomized-Controlled Trial
Publication: Proceedings 91(1), 381, published 28 February 2024 after FENS 2023.
Authors: Izquierdo-Pulido and colleagues
DOI: 10.3390/proceedings2023091381
Registry: NCT04799821
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2504-3900/91/1/381
Coverage: Original factual extract of the crossover design and reported sleepiness comparison.

The proceedings report describes 80 young adults, mean age 24.1 years. The intervention was 40 grams of whole walnuts daily for eight weeks, with a nut-free control period and a two-week washout. Actigraphy measured sleep measures. An Epworth Sleepiness Scale assessment is included and the abstract reports lower daytime sleepiness with p=.004. The abstract omits the scale estimate and arm-specific uncertainty. The 2025 article uses the same registration and explains the smaller analyzed population. The program tested whole walnuts in young adults, with no oral walnut oil comparison or Parkinson cohort.
