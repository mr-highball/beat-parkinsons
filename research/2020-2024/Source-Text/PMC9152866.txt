Title: Effective Treatment of Adult Parasomnias with Keishikaryukotsuboreito in Four Cases

Design: Four-case clinical series using a traditional Kampo formula

In the Parkinson case, the authors report that daytime visual hallucinations and dream enactment ceased two days after oral TJ-26 Keishikaryukotsuboreito began. The six-month statement reports continued use, stable concurrent Parkinson medicines and no parasomnia recurrence. It supplies no separate six-month visual episode count. Table 2 lists cinnamon bark, peony root, jujube, oyster shell, longgu, licorice root and ginger. The granules contained no fig, with no magnesium composition reported. One uncontrolled case cannot attribute the observation to an individual constituent.
