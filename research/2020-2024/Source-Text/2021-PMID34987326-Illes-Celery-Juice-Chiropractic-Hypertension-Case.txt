Title: Blood Pressure Change After Celery Juice Ingestion in a Hypertensive Elderly Male.
PMID: 34987326
PMCID: PMC8703128
DOI: 10.1016/j.jcm.2021.04.001
First published: 2021
Authors: Illes JD
Journal: J Chiropr Med
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34987326/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 3 review).

Single clinical case report of a 74-year-old hypertensive man (baseline BP 150/80 mmHg) with neck pain who consumed daily celery juice for 6 months alongside 14 cervical chiropractic mobilization visits; BP decreased to 118/82 mmHg. Uncontrolled single-patient co-intervention; no PD diagnosis and no orthostatic hypotension assessment.
