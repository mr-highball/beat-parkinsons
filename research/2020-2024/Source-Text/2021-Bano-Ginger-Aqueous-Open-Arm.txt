Aqueous ginger rhizome extract and rat open-arm time
Source: https://www.nepjol.info/index.php/AJMS/article/view/34480/28861
DOI: 10.3126/ajms.v12i6.34480
Coverage: Original Methods, Results and Table 3, factual text

Bano, Sharif and Badawy published June 1, 2021. The authors prepared a filtered aqueous extract from peeled supermarket ginger rhizomes without formal botanical authentication or constituent quantification. Six male rats per arm received oral extract equivalent to 500 mg/kg of ginger material daily or saline for three weeks. Table 3 open-arm time was 30 ± 0.6 versus 20 ± 0.5 seconds (mean ± SEM). The authors report a significant increase and label the table comparison p < .001. Open-field crossings were 117 ± 15 versus 120 ± 20 and were not significantly altered. The preparation was filtered extract and the outcome an animal behavioral proxy.
