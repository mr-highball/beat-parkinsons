Title: Unraveling the Puzzle: A Case of Intricate Neurological Presentation Attributable to Hypomagnesemia
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11392027/
PMID: 39268027
PMCID: PMC11392027
DOI: 10.7759/cureus.69273
Publication date: 2024-09-12
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The patient entered hospital with very low serum magnesium and coexisting electrolyte abnormalities. Visual hallucinations first appeared after extubation, when measured serum magnesium had risen to 2.2 mg/dL. They persisted during further correction and oral supplementation, then were absent the day after quetiapine and melatonin were added. Multiple therapies and the changing critical-care state preclude attributing the visual course to magnesium alone. There is no Parkinson or dried fig comparison.
