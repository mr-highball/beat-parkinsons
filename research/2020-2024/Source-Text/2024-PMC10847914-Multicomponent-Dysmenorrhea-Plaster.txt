This study compared a multicomponent topical plaster in 18 female rats in a model of primary dysmenorrhea. Cinnamon, clove, ginger and Sichuan pepper occur together in the formula. The ingredients were not assigned individually. No culinary preparation or Parkinson clinical outcome was studied.
