Title: Differentiating Prodromal Dementia with Lewy Bodies from Prodromal Alzheimer's Disease: A Pragmatic Review for Clinicians
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11136939/
PMID: 38720013
PMCID: PMC11136939
DOI: 10.1007/s40120-024-00620-x
Publication year: 2024
Text coverage: Selected paraphrased findings checked against complete PMC JATS.

The pragmatic review distinguishes prodromal Lewy body dementia from prodromal Alzheimer presentations. It emphasizes clinical features including early visual hallucinations and fluctuations. These are diagnostic observations without a fig efficacy comparison.

The original synthesis distinguishes prodromal criteria from features of established dementia. It covers comparative presentation and clinical measures.
