Title: Antidiarrheal, anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects of Anethum graveolens L. fruit extract on castor oil-induced diarrhea in rats
PMID: 39115258
DOI: 10.1111/nmo.14892
First published: 2024
Authors: Brinsi C, Jedidi S, Sammari H, Selmi S
Journal: Neurogastroenterology & Motility
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39115258/
Coverage: Local factual extract from verified primary abstract/record (Round 4 review).

Source basis: PubMed primary abstract. PLANT-PART DISTINCTION RECORD: Aqueous extract of Anethum graveolens FRUIT/SEED (50, 100, 200 mg/kg oral) reduced castor-oil-induced hypersecretion and peristalsis in rats (n=6/group) comparably to loperamide. Tested dill fruit/seed rather than dill leaf/weed, and studied acute secretory diarrhea rather than constipation.
