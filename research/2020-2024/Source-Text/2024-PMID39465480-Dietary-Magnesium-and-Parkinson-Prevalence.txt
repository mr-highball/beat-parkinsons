Title: Correlations Between Dietary Magnesium Consumption and Magnesium Depletion Score in Relation to Parkinson's Disease: A Population-Based Study
Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12011-024-04428-6
PMID: 39465480
DOI: 10.1007/s12011-024-04428-6
Publication date: 2024-10-28
Source depth: original publisher abstract and publication metadata; journal body unavailable on the public page; local research digest

This NHANES analysis includes 20,010 adults over 40, 240 with Parkinson disease. The adjusted magnesium depletion score association is an odds ratio of 1.47 per unit (95% CI 1.16–1.86). The authors report insufficient evidence for a clear association between dietary magnesium intake and Parkinson disease in the main intake analysis, and describe a nonlinear model separately. The study compares recorded disease status, with no visual hallucination outcome or isolated dried fig exposure. The first online date precedes the July 2025 issue.
