Title: A Han Chinese Family With Early-Onset Parkinson's Disease Carrying Novel Frameshift Mutation and Compound Heterozygous Mutation of PRKN Appearing Incompatible With MDS Clinical Diagnostic Criteria
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7586315/
PMID: 33154736
PMCID: PMC7586315
DOI: 10.3389/fneur.2020.582323
Publication date: 2020-10-09
Date basis: Publication date in official NCBI PMC JATS XML metadata (Frontiers in Neurology 11:582323).
Coverage: Paraphrased clinical narrative and Table 1 from official NCBI PMC JATS XML.

This genetic case report describes two affected Han Chinese sisters (III-4, age 45, onset age 34; III-8, age 40, onset age 25) carrying compound heterozygous PRKN mutations causing early-onset Parkinson disease. The proband (III-4) initially developed lower-limb resting tremor accompanied by fatigue complaints at age 34.

Table 1 records baseline lifestyle history including "Green tea intake" coded as "Never" for III-4 and "Quit" for III-8, alongside MDS-UPDRS, MoCA, MMSE, HAMD, and HAMA scores. The report contains no brewed green-tea dietary intervention, no validated fatigue rating scale, and no tea-versus-fatigue comparison.
