Title: Coffee effectively attenuates impaired attention in ADORA2A C/C-allele carriers during chronic sleep restriction
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33373678/
Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pnpbp.2020.110232
PDF: https://juser.fz-juelich.de/record/905084/files/1-s2.0-S0278584620305480-main.pdf?version=1
PMID: 33373678
DOI: 10.1016/j.pnpbp.2020.110232
Trial: DRKS00014379
Publication date: 2020-12-26
Date basis: PubMed electronic publication. 2021 journal issue.
Review depth: Original published PDF, Methods and Results section 3.1.1, and registry publication link.

Twenty-six healthy ADORA2A C/C participants contributed to the analyzed behavioral comparison, with 12 assigned to regular coffee and 14 to decaffeinated coffee. Brewed regular coffee preserved several measures of attention relative to decaffeinated coffee during restricted sleep. Self-rated Karolinska sleepiness increased in both groups during sleep restriction. The authors give a time-by-group interaction of F(6, 672) = 3.64, p = .001, with no detected between-group difference at individual days. The sleepiness and attention results are different outcomes.
