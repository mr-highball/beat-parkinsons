Title: Grey matter networks in women and men with dementia with Lewy bodies
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11016082/
PMID: 38615089
PMCID: PMC11016082
DOI: 10.1038/s41531-024-00702-5
Publication date: 2024-04-13
Date basis: electronic publication date in original JATS
Source depth: complete original PMC JATS body; local research digest

The cross-sectional multicenter analysis uses structural imaging to compare grey matter networks in women and men with dementia with Lewy bodies. Image-derived network patterns are the comparison. Participants' dried fig intake is not an exposure in the analysis.
