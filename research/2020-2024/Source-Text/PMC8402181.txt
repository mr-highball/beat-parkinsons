Title: Pain Improvement in Parkinson's Disease Patients Treated with Safinamide: Results from the SAFINONMOTOR Study
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8402181/
PMID: 34442442
PMCID: PMC8402181
DOI: 10.3390/jpm11080798
Publication date: 2021-08-16
Retrieved (UTC): 2026-11-19
Text coverage: Factual extract from the original PMC XML article body.

This prospective, single-arm study enrolled 50 Parkinson participants, of whom 44 completed follow-up. In a secondary analysis, the King's Parkinson's Disease Pain Scale mean total changed from 40.04 (SD 36.18) at baseline to 22.60 (SD 21.42) at six months, p<0.0001. The scale covers several pain domains. Without a concurrent control the change cannot isolate the medication effect. This study is distinct from the registered randomized NCT03841604 comparison cited in the discussion. Neither study tests dried dates.
