Morehen and colleagues published online August 6, 2020. Eleven male rugby players completed a single-blind crossover of Montmorency concentrate and matched placebo around two successive matches. On the one-item five-point fatigue scale, mean ratings were 3.3 versus 3.3 at 24 hours and 3.5 versus 3.4 at 48 hours. The article reports no clear comparison or group change for fatigue, with limited washout. The concentrated drink and match-related fatigue differ from the frozen Parkinson claim.
