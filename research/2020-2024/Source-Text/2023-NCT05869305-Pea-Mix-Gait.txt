Mixed plant protein and six meter gait testing in sarcopenia
Source: https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05869305
Registration: NCT05869305
Coverage: official protocol including comparator and gait outcome

First posted 2023-05-22. People with sarcopenia are assigned 12 weeks of whey isolate, a soy isolate, pea isolate and brown-rice protein mix, or nonprotein placebo. Six-meter gait speed is specified as a physical-performance measure. Registry status remains unknown and no results are posted as captured 2026-11-16. The protein blend cannot isolate the effect of cooked green peas.
