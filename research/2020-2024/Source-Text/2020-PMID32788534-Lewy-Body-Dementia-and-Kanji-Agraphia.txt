Title: Dementia with Lewy Bodies with Pure Agraphia for Kanji (Japanese Morphograms).
PMID: 32788534
PMCID: PMC7807109
DOI: 10.2169/internalmedicine.4777-20
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7807109/
First publication date: 2020-08-12 (Europe PMC original-search record).
Coverage: NIH PMC EFetch complete original article JATS, including the body and displayed study observations.

This case describes writing-specific impairment and recurrent clinical visual hallucinations in probable Lewy body dementia. Diagnostic imaging and the course of symptoms inform the differential diagnosis. No dried fig exposure or food comparison was reported.
