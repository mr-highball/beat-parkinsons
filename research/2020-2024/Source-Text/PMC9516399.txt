Title: The neuropsychotropic effects of saffron: overview of systematic reviews and meta-analyses
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9516399/
PMID: 36249453
PMCID: PMC9516399
DOI: 10.22038/AJP.2021.19300
Publication: 2022 issue, September–October
Coverage: Factual extract. Official PMC XML main article, literature search, review table and discussion.

This is an overview of earlier reviews. Insomnia and Parkinson appear in its search terms. It cites global Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index context and a pooled safety table that includes reported insomnia and tremor events. A general sleep score and adverse events do not supply a separately measured Parkinson insomnia result. A cited meta-analysis mentions favorable secondary fatigue, vigor and other mood outcomes across preparations and populations. These citations are evidence trails. The overview supplies no independent dried-saffron Parkinson fatigue cohort, and contains no dried-date pain assignment.
