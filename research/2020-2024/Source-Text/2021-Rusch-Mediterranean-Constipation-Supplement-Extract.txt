The authors averaged three 24-hour recalls at baseline and at week five (n = 8). Table 4 reports estimated oleic acid 31.0 ± 2.4 and 53.5 ± 8.6 g (paired p = .026), fiber 24.8 ± 2.1 and 36.2 ± 3.6 g (p = .027), and total fat 95.4 ± 7.3 and 127.1 ± 15.1 g (p = .035). The table reports nutrient estimates. It does not report an isolated olive-oil intake, processing grade, or an oil-specific association with the GSRS outcome.
