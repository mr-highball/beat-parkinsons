Title: Isolated dehydrozingerone and mouse immobility and exploration
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8460585/
PMID: 34181212
PMCID: PMC8460585
DOI: 10.1007/s43440-021-00252-0
Publication date: 2021-06-28
Date basis: First publication date in the original article.
Source depth: Full original methods and results, including open field, forced swim and tail suspension outcomes.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25

C57BL/6 male mice received purified dehydrozingerone, escitalopram or vehicle, with six animals allocated per group. At the one-hour assessment the dehydrozingerone groups showed reduced immobility compared with vehicle in forced swim and tail suspension. Findings differed at three and six hours. The paper also measures open-field locomotion and reports changes in monoamines and a docking analysis. Its behavioral experiments are not a clinical measure of depressive symptoms or anxiety, and did not assess involuntary movements after levodopa.

Dehydrozingerone is a distinct compound from 6-shogaol and zingerone. The study does not test any ground or grated ginger exposure or a Parkinson population.
