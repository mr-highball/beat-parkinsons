# Mixed polyphenol formulations and biomarker outcomes

## Registry for a mixed polyphenol supplement

First posted April 25, 2023. The program tests a mixed supplement that lists blueberry, kale, rosemary and grapefruit among ingredients. Its assigned outcomes are blood biomarkers, including CD38 and NAD-related measures, inflammatory markers and oxidative measures. It supplies no separate food arms or voice, smell, rest tremor or stride outcomes. The registry lists completed and no structured results. A linked journal paper reports selected biomarker results. [Original source](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05829382) · [Local text](Source-Text/2023-NCT05829382-Polyphenol-Blend-Biomarkers.txt)

## Mixed polyphenol supplement biomarker report

First published August 17, 2024. The NCT05829382 crossover report tests a mixed polyphenol supplement in healthy participants and gives blood biomarker and epigenetic findings. Ingredient lists include blueberry, kale, rosemary and grapefruit. The assigned formulation leaves individual ingredients and preparation-specific effects inseparable. No named Parkinson target symptom from this experiment was measured. [Original source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11352085/) · [Local text](Source-Text/2024-Torkildsen-Polyphenol-Blend-Biomarker-Extract.txt)
