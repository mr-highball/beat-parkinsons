## Dysarthria during a seafood dinner outbreak traced to roe

Statens Serum Institut’s November 17, 2021 report describes a family botulism outbreak following a shared meal of fish, shrimp and tuna mousse with lumpfish roe. Investigators identified botulinum toxin in an opened jar of the roe. One patient described difficulty speaking clearly, while two others had clinician-described dysarthria. The outbreak report links the vehicle to the roe. It does not give a shrimp-specific preparation or a quantitative motor-speech outcome.
