# Altemor frailty trial registration

## Registered fall-risk scale and fatigue measures

NCT06352099 first posted April 8, 2024. The current registry gives an actual May 2, 2024 start and February 17, 2025 completion. It lists the Trail Making Test as primary and the Hunova Silver Index, Modified Fatigue Impact Scale, Short Physical Performance Battery, HADS and quality of life among other measures. The Silver Index estimates fall risk from multiple functional measures. The latest record shows completion, with no tabulated registry results. The first-version outcome fields remain access-limited.
