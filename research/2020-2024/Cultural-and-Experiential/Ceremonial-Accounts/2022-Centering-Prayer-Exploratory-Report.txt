Context and exposure: Four people with Parkinson disease were trained in centering prayer. Four untrained people with Parkinson disease served as selected controls. The relationship of the paper's earlier video account to these participants is unknown.

Reported direction: The authors report smaller EMG amplitudes and lower total UPDRS after prayer and say controls lacked these improvements.
Authenticity: The publisher's six-page original and its two figures were inspected. This record describes the same exploratory comparison as the linked study extract, and paraphrases the CC BY 4.0 source.
Efficacy evidence: Figures show treated participants only. The separate rest-tremor outcome, reproducible between-group effect and medication-state details remain unreported. The timing descriptions conflict.
Open trail: Seek a protocol or registration, reproducible control data, a defined tremor phenotype and clarification of the assessment time. The practice is outside the food experiment's frozen vocabulary.
