Title: Parkinson's UK caregiver discussion incorporating finely chopped vegetables and courgette into PD constipation routines
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/any-wives-with-a-husband-diagnosed-like-a-chat/31042
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2022-02-02
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2022-02-02; caregiver courgette/vegetable constipation reply posted 2022-07-27, post #153).

In a 2022 Parkinson's UK Community Forum peer-support thread for spouses of people with Parkinson's disease, a caregiver replied in July 2022 (post #153) while working through strategies for their husband's constipation. Alongside physician-prescribed lactulose (replacing Dulcolax), planned golden linseed, and increased water consumption, the caregiver described encouraging vegetable intake for a reluctant vegetable eater by cutting vegetables finely into mixed stir-fries and baking courgette (zucchini) cake, which their spouse enjoyed. All personal identifiers and forum handles are omitted. This account reflects practical household dietary inclusion of courgette alongside medical laxative therapy without isolating any independent symptom effect of zucchini.
