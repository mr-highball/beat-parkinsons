Summary of public Cure Parkinson's HealthUnlocked thread titled 'Sulphoraphane in canola / rapeseed oil?' (posted 2023-05-07; privacy-safe summary with all member handles omitted).

A participant noted that canola/rapeseed (Brassica napus) belongs to the same cruciferous plant family as broccoli, kale, cabbage, and Brussels sprouts, and asked whether cold-pressed canola oil delivers sulforaphane alongside its low saturated fat, omega-3/omega-6 fatty acid balance, and vitamin E and K content. Discussion highlighted the distinction between lipophilic culinary seed oils and glucosinolate-rich whole Brassica sprouts or florets (as glucosinolates partition mainly into the defatted seed meal/pomace rather than pressed oil). No individual Parkinson's motor or cognitive symptom improvements or worsenings were reported.
