Title: Parkinson's UK Forum caregiver report of urologist-suggested high-strength cranberry, D-mannose, and probiotics for PD urgency and UTIs
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/d-mannose/36938
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2023-07-08
Date basis: Public thread opening post dated 2023-07-08; reply dated 2024-02-16.

In a Parkinson's UK public forum post dated July 8, 2023 (all personal names/handles omitted), a caregiver reported that a spouse with Parkinson's disease who experienced recurrent UTIs over two years and intermittent urinary urgency had seen a urologist; after normal bladder scan and prostate check, the urologist suggested trying D-mannose sachets, probiotics, and high-strength cranberry supplements to possibly help prevent UTIs. The caregiver asked whether others found these supplements helpful.
Reply (February 16, 2024): One member endorsed D-mannose for preventing urinary infections without reporting an isolated cranberry outcome.
Observation and limits: Reflects real-world urology-adjacent preventative interest in high-strength cranberry alongside D-mannose for Parkinson's urinary symptoms/UTIs, but contains no firsthand cranberry before-and-after urgency measurement and bundles multiple supplements. Cross-linked with Cho 2021 (PMID:32945735), Vidlar 2016 (PMID:26049866), and Cochrane 2023 (PMID:37068952, noting null UTI prophylaxis in neurogenic bladder).
