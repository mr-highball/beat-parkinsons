Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion on Sinemet protein timing mentioning orange juice and lemon juice
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/managing-protein/32341
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2022-05-28
Date basis: Opening post timestamp (2022-05-28) and follow-up reply (post #4, 2022-06-01) on Parkinson's UK Community Forum topic 32341.

In a May-June 2022 public thread on the Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussing how to space dietary protein around Sinemet doses, participants and caregivers described taking levodopa 30 to 60 minutes before meals or waiting two hours after protein. A recently diagnosed contributor sharing their three-times-daily Sinemet schedule noted that their clinical guidance made no mention of orange juice, although they had read about lemon juice being suggested for a similar absorption purpose. No individual efficacy outcome for either citrus juice was reported. All names and account handles are omitted.
