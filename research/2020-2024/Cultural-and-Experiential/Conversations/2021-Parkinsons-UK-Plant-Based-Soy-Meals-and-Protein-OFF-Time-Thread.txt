Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion of switching to plant-based soy meals and managing daytime protein OFF periods
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/satisfying-immediate-protein-free-meals/30602
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2021-12-29
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2021-12-29; replies through 2022-01-03).

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread started on December 29, 2021, a person diagnosed with Parkinson's for 8 years reported that eating protein-rich meals appeared to trigger 1-to-2-hour OFF episodes despite attempting meal-medication spacing, and asked for satisfying lower-protein meal ideas. On December 31, 2021, a caregiver replied that their father experienced perceived improvement in medication absorption after gradually transitioning to plant-based/vegan meals incorporating soya-based dishes, vegetables, pasta, and baked potatoes, as well as overnight oats with yogurt and fruit timed around early medications. Subsequent replies through January 3, 2022 noted that protein tolerance varies widely between individuals and often requires careful timing rather than total protein elimination. All personal names and handles are omitted. This thread provides balanced lived-experience context on plant-soy and dairy protein management without isolating culinary tofu in a controlled trial.
