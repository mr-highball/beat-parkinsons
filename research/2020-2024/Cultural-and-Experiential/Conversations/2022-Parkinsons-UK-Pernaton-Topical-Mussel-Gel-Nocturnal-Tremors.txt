Title: Parkinson's UK forum firsthand report of topical green-lipped mussel gel (Pernaton) for nocturnal leg tremors
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/tremors-while-sleeping/31680
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2022-04-03
Date basis: Thread opening timestamp (2022-04-03) and firsthand reply timestamp (2022-04-08; companion reply on topic 30371 dated 2022-04-07) on public Parkinson's UK Community Forum.

In an April 2022 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread asking about nocturnal tremors during Madopar therapy (topic 31680; echoed in an April 7, 2022 post by the same contributor on topic 30371—deduplicated as a single contributor), a participant reported that applying a small amount of Pernaton (a commercial topical gel formulated with green-lipped mussel extract) to the legs and thighs at bedtime coincided with an estimated ~95% perceived reduction in nighttime leg tremors and twitches. All personal identifiers are omitted. Because Pernaton is a topical massage gel containing additional cooling/essential-oil ingredients rather than orally eaten cooked mussels, this uncontrolled single-person observation provides zero evidence for dietary cooked mussels reducing Parkinson rest tremor.
