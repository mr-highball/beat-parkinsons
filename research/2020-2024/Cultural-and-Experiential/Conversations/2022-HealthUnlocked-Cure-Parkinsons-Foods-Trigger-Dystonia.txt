Summary of public Cure Parkinson's HealthUnlocked thread titled 'Foods trigger dystonia?' (posted 2022-04-20; privacy-safe summary with all member handles omitted).

An opening post asked whether dietary triggers could provoke painful toe-curling dystonia in Parkinson's disease. In the replies, one commenter shared an informal dietary avoidance list that named Brussels sprouts, buckwheat, and winter squash among numerous foods hypothesized to relate to inflammatory flare-ups. Multiple replying members pushed back directly against blanket food avoidance, reporting that they ate those listed foods routinely without inflammatory symptoms or dystonia worsening and emphasizing instead that PD dystonia frequently tracks dopaminergic medication wearing-off, levodopa protein timing, hydration, and off-period fluctuations. The thread illustrates both informal patient food-trigger hypotheses and experiential counter-evidence within the same community.
