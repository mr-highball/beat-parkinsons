Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Caregiver Report of Refractory Constipation Persisting Despite Daily Apples and Laxatives
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/advice-needed-please/35031
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2023-11-07
Date basis: Public Discourse thread posts spanning 2023-01-17 to 2023-11-07 (apple report in Post #6 on 2023-11-07).

In a 6-post public Parkinson's UK forum thread titled 'Advice needed please' (January 17 to November 7, 2023), caregivers discussed severe Parkinson-related constipation and levodopa absorption concerns. In Post #6 (November 7, 2023), a spouse caring for a person 8 years post-diagnosis taking Sinemet reported that severe constipation (no bowel movement for 3 days) persisted despite daily apples, plums, pears, grapes, flaxseed on breakfast cereal, fruit yogurts, abundant fluids, morning and evening Cosmocol laxatives, and two bisacodyl tablets nightly. This account provides important real-world balance showing that routine dietary apples and mixed fruit may be insufficient for refractory PD gut dysmotility.
