Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion contrasting chicken, red meat, fish, and prawns in levodopa-protein interference
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/eating-protein-is-causing-my-parkinson-s/24668
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2020-07-28
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public community forum (2020-07-28; caregiver chicken-vs-prawns observation dated 2020-08-02; follow-up replies through 2021-08-11).

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened in July 2020 with replies extending to August 2021, a participant with five years of Parkinson's disease reported that midday meat meals appeared to worsen both motor symptoms and neuropathic pain. Several community members responded with firsthand accounts of levodopa blockade: one noted severe worsening of dyskinesia and bradykinesia an hour after virtually any protein; another reported Sinemet failing if taken within 2 hours of protein; and others described reserving meat or fish for evening meals and spacing levodopa 1 hour before or 2 hours after protein. Notably, on 2020-08-02 a caregiver reported that her husband experienced the strongest adverse medication interaction with chicken, whereas fish and prawns were "not so bad." All personal names and handles are omitted. The thread reflects real-world clinical heterogeneity in dietary protein-levodopa interactions documented in large cohort surveys.
