Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion on whole-food omega-3 sources including walnuts and chia seeds versus supplements
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/omega-3/39705
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2024-03-12
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on the public Parkinson's UK Community Forum (2024-03-12; caregiver and peer replies inspected from 2024-03-13 through 2024-03-16).

In a March 2024 public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread asking whether members take omega-3 or omega-3-6-9 supplements and whether they help, a caregiver replied on March 13, 2024 that her husband with Parkinson's disease does not take omega-3 capsules; instead, he eats fatty fish at least three times weekly and she cooks primarily with olive oil while easily incorporating plant omega-3 foods such as walnuts and chia seeds into everyday dishes. The opening poster noted a fish allergy and expressed interest in vegan options like chia seeds; subsequent peer replies (March 13-16, 2024) mentioned ground flaxseed, canola oil, soybean oil, and algae-based supplements, while emphasizing consulting healthcare professionals before starting supplements. All personal identifiers and usernames are omitted. This discussion reflects practical whole-food culinary integration of walnuts in PD households rather than evidence of symptom modification.
