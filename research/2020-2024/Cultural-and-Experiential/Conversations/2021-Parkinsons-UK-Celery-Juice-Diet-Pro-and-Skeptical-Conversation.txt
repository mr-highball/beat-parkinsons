Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Conversation: Morning Celery Juice Recommendation and Community Skepticism
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/treating-parkinsons-with-diet/29027
Source kind: Public forum conversation
Publication date: 2021-08-25
Date basis: Published forum reply dates (2021-08-25 to 2021-08-27; later neurologist-diet commentary 2024-12-31).

Public Parkinson's UK forum discussion (19 posts, started 2021-08-11) on dietary approaches to Parkinson's. On 2021-08-25, one contributor recommended drinking a morning glass of raw celery juice preceded by lemon water, describing perceived benefits outweighing the taste, and another briefly pointed to the 'Medical Medium' blog. Two days later (2021-08-27), a skeptical responder challenged those claims by quoting the blog's non-medical basis and linking a BBC News critical review of the viral celery-juice trend. Later discussion (2024-12-31) summarized neurologist guidance favoring a balanced diet and consistent medication timing over unproven food-group elimination or cure protocols.
