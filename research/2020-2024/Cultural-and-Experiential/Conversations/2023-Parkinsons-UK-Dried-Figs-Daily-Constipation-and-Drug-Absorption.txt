Title: Parkinson's UK Forum reports of daily dried figs with fiber cereal or flaxseed for PD constipation and medication absorption
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/stomach-discomfort/38268/2
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2023-11-11
Date basis: Public forum reply date on Parkinson's UK Discourse (2023-11-11, thread 38268 reply 2; companion thread 28715 reply 5 posted 2021-07-19).

Two independent Parkinson's UK public forum threads document routine firsthand use of dried figs for Parkinson's-related constipation and bowel-mediated medication absorption.

In a November 2023 discussion on abdominal discomfort and trapped wind in newly diagnosed Parkinson's disease (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/stomach-discomfort/38268/2, reply dated 2023-11-11), a participant advised checking constipation as the root cause and reported adding two to three dried figs daily plus a teaspoon of flaxseed to breakfast cereal, explicitly noting that prescription macrogol osmotic laxatives were still used whenever dietary figs and flaxseed alone were not enough.

In an earlier July 2021 discussion on dopaminergic medication side effects (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/ropinerole-side-effects/28715/5, reply dated 2021-07-19), another participant with Parkinson's disease noted that even slight constipation impaired their medication absorption and reported eating three dried figs daily with high-fiber breakfast cereal, careful hydration, and occasional macrogol if a bowel movement was missed. Both reports reflect real-world multi-component bowel routines rather than controlled single-food evidence.
