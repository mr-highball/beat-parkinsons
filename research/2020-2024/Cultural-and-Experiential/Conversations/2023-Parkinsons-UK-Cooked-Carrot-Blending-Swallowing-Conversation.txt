Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Conversation: Blending Cooked and Steamed Carrots for Eating and Swallowing Difficulties
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/blending-food/29827
Source kind: Public forum conversation
Publication date: 2023-09-23
Date basis: Published forum reply dates discussing cooked carrots (2023-09-23 and 2023-12-12; thread opened 2021-10-16).

Public Parkinson's UK forum thread (10 posts, 2021-10-16 through 2024-02-19) initiated by a caregiver seeking advice on foods suitable for blending for a parent with Parkinson's struggling to eat solids. Multiple replies specifically recommended cooked or steamed carrots blended with broth and soft vegetables (such as sweet potatoes, spinach, and peas) into smooth soups or purees, alongside commercially prepared soft-bite/pureed meals. Mirrors speech-language texture-modification principles without constituting controlled evidence that carrots reverse neurogenic PD dysphagia.
