Privacy-safe summary: An unmedicated person with progressing Parkinson's disease asked for advice regarding worsening constipation and irregular bowel movements. Among 26 community replies, one contributor reported that eating vegan meals containing beans or chickpeas (without meat or dairy) every second day alongside frequent walking and avoiding prolonged sitting seemed to help keep stools moving. Providing crucial real-world counterbalance, other participants reported that high-fiber dietary routines, fluids, and herbal teas alone had failed to relieve severe PD constipation for a spouse until consistent nightly polyethylene glycol (MiraLAX) was adopted, while others credited coffee, prunes, figs, flax, chia, magnesium, or inulin.

Evidence and limits: Unverified personal anecdotes bundling chickpeas with other legumes, meat/dairy avoidance, and physical activity; preserves both favorable household observation and non-response to diet-only fiber strategies in Parkinson's disease.
