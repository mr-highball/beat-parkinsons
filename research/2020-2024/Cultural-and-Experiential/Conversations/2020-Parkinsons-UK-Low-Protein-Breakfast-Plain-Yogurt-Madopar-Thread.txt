Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion weighing plain yogurt protein grams against morning Madopar timing
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/low-protein/24249
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2020-06-20
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2020-06-20; follow-up replies through 2020-06-25).

In a public Parkinson's UK forum thread started on June 20, 2020 asking for low-protein breakfast ideas to prevent morning meals from blunting Madopar (levodopa/benserazide), contributors shared contrasting day-to-day strategies involving plain yogurt. One person with Parkinson's detailed tracking breakfast protein under 15 g total—including 5.1 g of protein from plain yogurt eaten with granola and homemade dried-fruit compote—while strictly spacing meals 1 hour before and 30 minutes after Madopar and shifting higher-protein meals to the evening. The opening poster similarly noted alternating toast with bran flakes, yogurt, and fruit while experimenting with early-morning medication timing. On June 25, 2020, a caregiver replied describing how switching a spouse's breakfast from cow's dairy to soy products markedly reduced severe mid-morning OFF periods (note: this shares the same caregiver narrative previously archived from a November 2018 thread and is not counted as an independent observation). All member names and handles are omitted. Together these accounts illustrate real-world individual variation in tolerating plain yogurt alongside levodopa timing.
