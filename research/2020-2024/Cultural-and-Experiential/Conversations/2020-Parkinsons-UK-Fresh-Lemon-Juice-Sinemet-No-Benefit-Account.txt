Title: Parkinson's UK forum firsthand no-benefit account of fresh lemon juice with Sinemet Plus 125
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/ginseng/3419
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2020-02-21
Date basis: Public post timestamp (post #6, 2020-02-21) on Parkinson's UK Community Forum topic 3419.

In a February 2020 post on the public Parkinson's UK Community Forum, a person living with Parkinson's disease taking Sinemet Plus 125 reported that their medication had gradually stopped controlling tremors and took a long time to kick in even when swallowed with a large glass of water containing two tablespoons of fresh lemon juice. The contributor noted having been told this reflected an absorption problem and asked the community for advice. All personal details and handles are omitted. This firsthand no-benefit observation illustrates real-world patient use of fresh lemon juice to aid levodopa absorption while demonstrating that gastric acidification with culinary lemon juice does not reliably overcome delayed ON or tremor non-response.
