Title: Parkinson's UK insomnia thread suggesting bedtime chamomile tea alongside caffeine reduction
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/sleep/34609
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2022-12-07
Date basis: Opening post dated December 7, 2022 (bucket 2020-2024); chamomile tea suggestion reply posted March 12, 2023.

In a Parkinson's UK forum thread opened on December 7, 2022, a person with a seven-year Parkinson's history described chronic severe insomnia (awake 22 hours, unresponsive to prescribed clonazepam and sporadic zopiclone) accompanied by balance problems and frequent OFF periods. Other participants shared fragmented sleep, vivid dreams, nighttime shaking, and substantial sleep improvements after eliminating caffeinated drinks. In a March 12, 2023 reply, a contributor suggested limiting caffeinated tea after early afternoon and trying bedtime chamomile tea (paired with valerian), magnesium glycinate, and a mindfulness meditation app. Because chamomile was mentioned as a multi-component suggestion without a reported isolated before-and-after outcome, the thread serves strictly as experiential context.
