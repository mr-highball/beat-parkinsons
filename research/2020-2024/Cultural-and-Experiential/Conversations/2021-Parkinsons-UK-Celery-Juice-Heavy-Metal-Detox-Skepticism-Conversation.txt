Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Conversation: Critical Debate Over Viral Celery Juice and Heavy-Metal Detox Claims
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/heavy-metal-detox/29558
Source kind: Public forum conversation
Publication date: 2021-09-21
Date basis: Published forum thread dates (2021-09-21 to 2022-03-30).

Public Parkinson's UK forum thread (7 posts, 2021-09-21 to 2022-03-30) asking whether anyone had tried the 'Medical Medium' heavy-metal detox protocol associated with celery juice. Multiple responders warned that the promoter lacked medical training, cited BBC News medical skepticism regarding celery-juice cure claims, and characterized the detox framing as pseudoscientific while agreeing that ordinary green smoothies and vegetables are tasty, healthy nutrition rather than a Parkinson's cure. One participant voiced openness to exploring whole foods out of dissatisfaction with standard medication side effects. Preserves both community skepticism and patient motivations without PII.
