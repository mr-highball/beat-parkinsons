Title: Public Parkinson forum firsthand reports of using kefir alongside laxatives for severe PD constipation and gastric symptoms
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/faecal-impaction-severe-constipation/26759
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2021-02-16
Date basis: Post date on Parkinson UK public forum thread 26759 (2021-02-16), cross-referencing stomach-problems thread 29843 (2021-10-17).

In a February 2021 Parkinson UK discussion on severe constipation and fecal impaction (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/faecal-impaction-severe-constipation/26759), a person living with Parkinson disease for 15 years described losing thirst drive and routinely mixing a bedtime sachet of macrogol (Movicol) into kefir or yogurt every evening to maintain bowel movements. In an October 2021 thread on persistent stomach problems (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/stomach-problems/29843), another participant noted having previously made and drunk daily kefir before stopping and wondered whether resuming kefir might ease returning GI discomfort. These accounts reflect common patient adoption of plain kefir for PD gut care alongside prescription laxatives.
