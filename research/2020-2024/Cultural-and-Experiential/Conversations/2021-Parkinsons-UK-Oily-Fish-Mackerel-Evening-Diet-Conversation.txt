Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion on evening oily fish (mackerel, sardines, salmon) versus plant-based diets for fatigue, pain, and levodopa duration
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/treating-parkinsons-with-diet/29027
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2021-08-11
Date basis: Direct firsthand post timestamps (2021-08-11 through 2021-08-19) in public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread 29027.

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread from August 2021 discussing dietary approaches to Parkinson disease (topic 29027), community members shared contrasting firsthand experiences involving oily fish and protein timing. One young-onset participant described consuming a single large 6 pm evening meal of vegetables, extra-virgin olive oil, chillies, and oily fish (explicitly naming sardines, salmon, and mackerel) for vitamin D and essential fats alongside daytime fasting and elimination of sugar, bread, and pasta—reporting dramatic subjective relief of daytime fatigue, back pain, arm tremor, and nighttime leg dystonia over two months. A caregiver similarly reported improved tremor, dystonia, and fatigue after shifting a partner toward more fish and vegetables with less daytime protein and bread. By contrast, the thread initiator reported after one week of removing animal protein in favor of a plant-based diet that prescribed medications lasted noticeably longer and turning in bed improved. All personal identifiers are omitted. The thread illustrates real-world evening-timed oily-fish adoption alongside daytime protein-redistribution benefits, but multiple simultaneous diet changes preclude attributing effects to mackerel.
