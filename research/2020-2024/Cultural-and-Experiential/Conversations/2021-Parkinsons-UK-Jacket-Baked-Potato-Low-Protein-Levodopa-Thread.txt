Title: Parkinsons UK forum discussion on jacket/baked potatoes as satisfying low-protein daytime meals to avoid levodopa OFF episodes
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/satisfying-immediate-protein-free-meals/30602
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2021-12-29
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinsons UK Community Forum (2021-12-29; replies 2021-12-31 to 2022-01-03).

In a public Parkinsons UK Community Forum thread opened on 2021-12-29, a 58-year-old participant diagnosed with Parkinson disease eight years earlier reported that over the preceding four months eating almost any protein seemed to switch their medication OFF for 1 to 2 hours despite timing adjustments. Seeking convenient, satisfying low-protein supermarket meals, they asked other protein-sensitive patients whether jacket potatoes were a reliable default option. On 2021-12-31, a caregiver replied that their father had gradually transitioned to plant-based daytime meals—including baked potatoes with vegetables and meat-free sauces, plus overnight oats for breakfast—and noticed improved medication absorption, while adding on 2022-01-03 that individual trial and error with daytime vs evening protein distribution is often needed. All account names and personal identifiers are omitted. The discussion highlights baked/jacket potatoes as a low-protein caloric vehicle rather than a direct pharmacological treatment for PD motor symptoms.
