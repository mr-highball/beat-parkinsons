In a public Cure Parkinsons HealthUnlocked thread opened on 2022-01-08 comparing evidence-backed supplements and dietary patterns for Parkinson disease, a participant summarized plant-forward dietary scoring frameworks (such as pescatarian/Mischley-style diets) that award high nutritional priority to fresh fruits, vegetables, and whole grains/pseudocereals including quinoa, farro, teff, and buckwheat, while another participant expressed skepticism toward extrapolating rodent studies to human PD care. In a companion 2023-03-07 thread (posts/149218704/curcumin-update-2023-03-06), a member shared savory cooked-quinoa recipes (such as turmeric quinoa patties) as practical ways to incorporate anti-inflammatory spices and whole plant foods. All handles and personal details are omitted. Neither thread provides an isolated before-and-after PD symptom outcome attributable to boiled or cooked quinoa, and patients using levodopa must still consider individual meal-protein timing.
