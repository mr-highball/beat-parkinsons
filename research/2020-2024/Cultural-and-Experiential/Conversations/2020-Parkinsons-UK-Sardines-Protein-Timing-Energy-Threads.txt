Title: Parkinson's UK forum accounts on spacing sardines on toast with Madopar and eating sardines for morning energy
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/low-protein/24249
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2020-06-21
Date basis: Primary low-protein lunch report timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2020-06-21, topic 24249), paired with a 2026-04-15 breakfast energy reply (topic 49040).

In a June 2020 Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on low-protein daytime eating to prevent meals from blunting Madopar (topic 24249), a participant described fasting for one hour before and thirty minutes after every Madopar dose while keeping daytime meals light—using options such as hummus and pitta, vegetable soup, beans on toast, or sardines on toast at lunch—and timing their last daytime dose before an evening main meal. In a separate April 2026 Parkinson's UK thread addressing increased tremor and low energy (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/more-tremors-a-bad-day-or-meds-not-working/49040), another member who was titrating off a dopamine agonist remarked that eating sardines for breakfast provided perceived slow-release energy despite low motivation and fatigue. Alongside a 2021 Parkinson's UK thread (topic 29027) noting sardines shifted to 6 pm evening dinners, these firsthand posts illustrate how people with Parkinson's vary in how they balance nutrient-dense sardines against levodopa timing. All personal identifiers are omitted; no clinical symptom scales were recorded.
