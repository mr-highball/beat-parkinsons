Title: Parkinson's UK forum conversations on cooked/dietary spinach for refractory constipation and bloating alongside standard laxatives
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-struggles/35980
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2023-04-07
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2023-04-07; caregiver spinach observation posted 2023-04-10; cross-referencing earlier 2018-07-14 / 2018-07-18 constipation thread https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-any-cure/14613).

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened on April 7, 2023 ("Constipation struggles"), a caregiver described severe refractory constipation (going up to three days without a bowel movement despite twice-daily macrogol sachets, bedtime senna, increased fruit, vegetables, and flaxseed, plus recent hospital enema) accompanied by distressing sudden bowel urgency when away from home. In an April 10, 2023 reply while discussing morning movement and diet, the caregiver remarked that giving their spouse a large portion of spinach once or twice previously had seemed to help, noting plans to try it again alongside medical review. In an earlier Parkinson's UK constipation discussion opened July 14, 2018 (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-any-cure/14613, reply dated July 18, 2018), a person with Parkinson's undergoing medical testing for bloating, constipation, abdominal pain, and low iron while taking macrogol and iron tablets reported eating small meals with spinach and kale to ease post-meal fullness and discomfort, without reporting symptom resolution. Other forum threads (such as 2012 discussions of Sinemet and existing 2017/2023 archived smoothie and puree threads) also reference Popeye spinach imagery or blending cooked spinach into purees for swallowing difficulty. All personal identifiers are omitted. These real-world accounts illustrate routine culinary integration of spinach in PD bowel and meal care without isolating an independent therapeutic effect.
