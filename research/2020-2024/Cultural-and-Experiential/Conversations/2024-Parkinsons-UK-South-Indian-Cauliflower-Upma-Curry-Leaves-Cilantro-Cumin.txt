Title: Parkinson's UK comfort-food recipe discussion combining tempered curry leaves, cumin seeds, and fresh cilantro
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/comfort-food-recipes/10204
Source kind: conversations
Publication date: 2024-04-26
Date basis: Exact public forum post timestamp (Parkinson's UK Discourse topic 10204, post #3 dated 2024-04-26).

In a public Parkinson's UK comfort-food thread (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/comfort-food-recipes/10204, post dated 2024-04-26), a community member shared a low-carbohydrate South Indian cauliflower upma adaptation prepared by tempering 4-5 curry leaves (Murraya koenigii) and 1/4 teaspoon cumin seeds with mustard seeds, ginger, turmeric, green chili, onion, and tomato in ghee or oil, finished with chopped fresh cilantro garnish. The post documents typical real-world household spice quantities and multi-ingredient culinary pairing among people living with Parkinson's disease without claiming any relief or worsening of motor or non-motor PD symptoms.
