Title: Parkinson's UK forum caregiver account of shifting beans, lentils, and plant proteins to evening meals for levodopa
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/plant-based-diet/22438
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2020-01-17
Date basis: Reply timestamp on Parkinson's UK public Community Forum thread opened 2020-01-14 (caregiver reply dated 2020-01-17).

In a January 2020 Parkinson's UK Community Forum discussion on adopting a plant-based diet while taking levodopa, a caregiver whose partner with Parkinson's followed a plant-based diet explained their practical strategy for avoiding medication interference. Specifically, they reported spacing protein at least an hour from levodopa and avoiding high-protein plant foods—explicitly naming meat substitutes, beans, and lentils—during the daytime, instead consuming the day's protein portion in the evening so daytime levodopa functioned reliably when mobility was most needed. They also noted checking nutrition labels because some plant-based dairy alternatives are low in protein, and emphasized monitoring vitamin B12 status. All personal names and account handles are omitted.
