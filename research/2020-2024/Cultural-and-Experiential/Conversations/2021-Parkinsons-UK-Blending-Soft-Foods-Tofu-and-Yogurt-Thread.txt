Title: Parkinson's UK forum discussion suggesting soft tofu and Greek yogurt for blended dysphagia-friendly meals
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/blending-food/29827
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2021-10-16
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2021-10-16; soft-food/tofu/Greek-yogurt reply dated 2023-09-23).

In a Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread opened on October 16, 2021, a caregiver requested practical food ideas for a parent with Parkinson's disease who was struggling to eat solid foods and needed blended meals. In a detailed response on September 23, 2023, a community contributor suggested soft, easily blended protein sources including tofu, soft-cooked fish or chicken, and Greek yogurt alongside cooked vegetables, oats, and peeled soft fruits to maintain nutrition while minimizing chewing effort. All account names and handles have been omitted. While illustrating real-world caregiver interest in soft soy curd and strained yogurt for texture-modified PD meals, the thread contains no measured swallowing-safety assessment or symptom follow-up.
