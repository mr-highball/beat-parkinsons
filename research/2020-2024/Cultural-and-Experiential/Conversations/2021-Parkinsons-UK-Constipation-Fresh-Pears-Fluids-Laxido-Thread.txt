Title: Parkinson UK forum thread noting fresh pears alongside fluids and prescription macrogol for constipation and wind
Collection: Cultural and experiential

Publication date: 2021-08-21
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson UK public forum thread 29143 (2021-08-21; pear reply timestamp 2021-08-27).

In an August 2021 Parkinson UK Community Forum thread regarding persistent constipation and trapped wind in a participant living with Parkinson disease for seven years on maintenance macrogol, a fellow participant replied that generous fluid intake together with fresh pears, grapes, and apples were their preferred everyday fruits to soften stools, while also noting ongoing reliance on prescription macrogol (Laxido), lactulose, and occasional suppositories whenever bowels became stubborn. All personal names and identifiers are omitted. The exchange illustrates everyday dietary inclusion of pears by PD patients alongside pharmacologic bowel regimens without isolating pear efficacy.
