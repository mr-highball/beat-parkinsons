Title: Parkinson UK forum reports contrasting strong prune-juice constipation relief against refractory failure despite daily plums and pears
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/advice-needed-please/35031
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2023-01-17
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson UK public forum thread 35031 (2023-01-17; replies dated 2023-11-07), corroborated by Parkinson UK thread 50272 (2026-10-15 to 2026-10-19).

In a Parkinson UK Community Forum thread initiated in January 2023 with follow-up replies in November 2023 (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/advice-needed-please/35031), participants responding to a family seeking help for severe PD constipation reported contrasting experiences. One participant with Parkinson disease reported that drinking regular prune juice made a substantial difference after struggling with the same symptom months earlier. Immediately afterward, a caregiver described their spouse remaining severely constipated for three days despite eating plums, pears, apples, grapes, flaxseed, fruit yogurts, and ample fluids alongside twice-daily macrogol and nightly bisacodyl, necessitating referral to a continence clinic and Bristol stool charting. In a separate October 2026 Parkinson UK thread on slow-motility PD constipation (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/slow-motility-constiption/50272), another caregiver noted that excessive wheat bran had worsened bulked constipation despite three liters of daily water, whereas one to two glasses of daily prune juice proved very effective for both the caregiver and their spouse with Parkinson disease. All usernames and personal details are omitted. These uncontrolled community reports underscore individual heterogeneity in PD constipation.
