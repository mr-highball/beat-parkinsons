Title: Parkinson's UK Forum Discussion Mentioning Fresh Apples, Pears, and Grapes for Constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-and-wind/29143
Source kind: Public patient forum discussion
Publication date: 2021-08-27
Date basis: Public Discourse thread posts spanning 2021-08-21 to 2021-08-27 (apple mention in Post #10 on 2021-08-27).

In a 14-post public Parkinson's UK forum thread titled 'Constipation and Wind' (August 21-27, 2021), a person diagnosed with Parkinson disease 7 years earlier sought advice for persistent constipation and straining despite Movicol and Fybogel. In Post #10 (August 27, 2021), another community member with the same problem shared their routine: drinking ample fluids so stools do not dry out, choosing fresh fruit — specifically pears, grapes, and apples — and using prescribed laxatives (Laxido powder, Lactulose, or suppositories when stubborn). This account illustrates everyday dietary apple use alongside medical laxatives in PD, but cannot isolate baked or raw apples from co-interventions.
