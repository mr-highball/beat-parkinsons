In public Cure Parkinsons HealthUnlocked threads on PD constipation (opened 2024-11-10 at posts/151436591 and an earlier 2020-07-31 thread at posts/144007536), participants compared dietary fiber habits for managing slow bowel transit. One caregiver reported that eating vegan meals centered on beans, chickpeas, or lentils without meat or dairy every second day—paired with regular walking and hydration—appeared to keep bowel movements moving more reliably. Other participants emphasized prunes, figs, water kefir, and bran mixtures, while one PD patient cautioned in the 2020 thread that dietary fiber remedies that initially work frequently lose sufficiency or require increasing amounts as Parkinson disease progresses. All usernames and identifying details are omitted. These threads reflect generic legume/bean use rather than isolated pinto beans and complement existing archived Parkinson UK accounts showing mixed patient experiences with daytime legume protein and levodopa timing.
