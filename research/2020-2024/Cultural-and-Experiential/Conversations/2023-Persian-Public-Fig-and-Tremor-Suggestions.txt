The opening post is framed as advice for shaking of the hands and feet (لرزش دست و پا). It lists figs (انجیر), dates (خرما), almonds and olives within a broader pattern of foods regarded as warming. The later post names Parkinson disease (پارکینسون) and again includes figs within a multicomponent regimen promoted for shaking. These are recommendations from the same channel. Neither message describes use in a documented individual, a before-and-after comparison, or an adverse-outcome check. The resemblance is a repeated recommendation within one source family.

The posts do not identify fig cultivar or raw versus dried preparation, tremor at rest versus during action, concurrent medication, diagnosis confirmation or a separate outcome for fig or date. The first post concerns shaking generally. The second promotes a treatment claim without a reported observation. They provide leads for the vocabulary and the history of the claim. There is no measured or self-reported benefit to use as an effect estimate.
