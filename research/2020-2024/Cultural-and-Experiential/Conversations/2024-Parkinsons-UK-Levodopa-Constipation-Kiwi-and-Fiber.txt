Reported direction: Mixed experiential observations regarding dietary fruit/fiber and Parkinson's constipation; no standalone efficacy claim for kiwi fruit.
Authenticity: Publicly accessible Parkinson's UK community forum thread inspected on 2026-11-26; all member names and handles omitted for privacy.
Efficacy evidence: Anecdotal community discussion only; does not establish clinical Parkinson's efficacy.

Thread summary (7 posts, 2024-10-09 to 2025-07-30):
- A newly diagnosed participant awaiting a Parkinson's nurse appointment asked whether starting levodopa worsens constipation.
- In a follow-up post (2024-10-14), the participant noted preexisting chronic constipation despite eating one kiwi fruit daily alongside bran cereal with prunes, high-fiber vegetables and beans, and prescribed lactulose.
- Another long-term participant (2024-11-20) reported that abundant fruit, dietary fiber, senna, and macrogol (Movicol) had failed to produce consistent relief unless taken at very high laxative doses, underscoring wide inter-individual variability in Parkinson's constipation.
- Other replies described individual relief with daily dried prunes/apricots or osmotic laxatives.
