Title: Parkinson's UK Forum discussion about coffee, tremor and shoulder symptoms
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/coffee-and-shoulder/40026
Publication date: 2024-04-07
Date basis: Opening post date shown on the public forum. Selected replies through April 17, 2024.

Someone who reported a Parkinson diagnosis and had yet to start Parkinson medication described more prominent symptoms after brewed coffee. They described one-sided shaking and separately asked about a frozen shoulder. The post gives no isolated measured tremor contrast.

Replies ranged from worse tremor after coffee, with tiredness or stress also present, to little baseline tremor with or without instant coffee in a caregiver's account. Another participant described the reaction as less pronounced after starting medication. The same forum account reported worsening after coffee in the 2019 thread. Its later update provides follow-up within one account.

A reply reported perceived pain relief after a few days of using a topical ginger poultice. The preparation is unspecified and the reply does not establish a response for frozen shoulder.
