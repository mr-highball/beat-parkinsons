Title: Public Parkinson forum experiences with fresh grapes in managing chronic constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/advice-needed-please/35031
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2023-11-07
Date basis: Post date on Parkinson UK public forum thread 35031 (2023-11-07), cross-referencing earlier community constipation thread 79 (2010-11-06).

Across public Parkinson UK forum discussions of chronic constipation, fresh grapes appear both as an informal dietary tip and in no-benefit caregiver reports. In a November 2010 constipation thread (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation/79), one community member recommended increasing fluid intake and eating a handful of grapes daily (or prunes as a last resort). Conversely, in a November 2023 thread seeking help for severe constipation (forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/advice-needed-please/35031), a caregiver reported that their spouse with Parkinson disease had not had a bowel movement for three days despite daily grapes, apples, plums, pears, flaxseed, fruit yogurts, ample fluids, morning-and-evening macrogol (Cosmocol), and nighttime bisacodyl. These firsthand accounts illustrate real-world variability and cannot isolate an effect of raw or frozen grapes.
