Title: Parkinson's UK forum accounts of strawberry taste and smell during hyposmia and levodopa initiation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/40824
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2024-06-16
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum (2024-06-16; balanced with corroborating hyposmia thread https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/fortunate-or-not/43653 dated 2025-02-02).

In a June 2024 introductory thread on the public Parkinson's UK Community Forum, a newly diagnosed 70-year-old participant who had lost smell and taste decades earlier reported that shortly after starting levodopa/benserazide (Madopar), they ate a strawberry and perceived a definite hint of strawberry taste alongside faint garden flower scents, wondering whether the dopaminergic medication was responsible; other participants replying in the thread noted persistent long-term loss of smell or taste despite PD medication. In a separate February 2025 forum thread on anosmia/hyposmia (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/fortunate-or-not/43653, post dated 2025-02-02), another participant remarked that while pungent onion odors were barely noticeable, they particularly missed the aroma and flavor of strawberries. All personal identifiers are omitted. Together these accounts illustrate how strawberries are commonly referenced in PD lived experience of olfactory loss without implying that eating strawberries improves or worsens olfactory impairment.
