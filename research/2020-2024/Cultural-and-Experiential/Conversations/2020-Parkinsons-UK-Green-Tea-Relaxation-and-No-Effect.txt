Title: Parkinson's UK Forum discussion on green tea, daytime relaxation and no noticed Parkinson symptom effect
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Conversation
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/green-tea/24120
Publication date: 2020-06-08
Date basis: Opening post date and replies all posted on 2020-06-08 on the public forum.
Reviewed: 2026-11-21 UTC

A participant asked whether powdered or "Zen" green tea helps Parkinson symptoms. Two respondents shared contrasting everyday experiences with standard brewed green tea bags: one reported that drinking brewed green tea seemed to promote daytime relaxation (and had not tried it at night), while another participant who routinely drank a cup of green tea in the morning explicitly reported never noticing any effect on Parkinson symptoms. Neither respondent had tried powdered matcha/Zen green tea or reported standardized symptom ratings, caffeine intake, or medication schedules. The thread preserves both a mild perceived relaxation account and a direct no-benefit report.
