Privacy-Safe Summary of Observations:
In a Parkinson's UK public forum thread on severe PD insomnia and fragmented sleep (2-3 hours/night, restless legs, nightmares, and daytime exhaustion), one community member with Parkinson's reported that after prescription/supplemental melatonin failed to help and worsened dream intensity, they shifted to a calming bedtime ritual consisting of a mug of hot mint tea paired with evening meditation, noting modest subjective relaxation as part of a broader sleep-hygiene routine. Other participants in the same threads emphasized medication timing adjustments, clonazepam, or clinician review, and reported persistent refractory sleep fragmentation despite herbal teas or behavioral rituals.

Attribution & Species Limits:
The participant wrote 'mint tea' without distinguishing Mentha spicata (spearmint) from Mentha x piperita (peppermint), and bundled the beverage with meditation. A targeted search of the entire Parkinson's UK forum specifically for 'spearmint' returned zero dedicated PD symptom-effect threads.
