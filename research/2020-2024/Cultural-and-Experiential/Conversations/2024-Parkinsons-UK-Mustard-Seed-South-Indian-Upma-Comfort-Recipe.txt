In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum thread on comfort-food recipes (post dated April 26, 2024), a participant shared a lower-carbohydrate adaptation of South Indian upma made with riced cauliflower tempered in oil or ghee with 1/4 teaspoon mustard seeds, 1/4 teaspoon cumin seeds, curry leaves, chopped ginger, turmeric powder, green chili, onion, tomato, black pepper, and lime juice. No usernames or identifying details are included. The post illustrates ordinary culinary incorporation of lightly fried/toasted mustard seeds in everyday home cooking among people with Parkinson's disease, with no claim of direct symptom modification.
