Title: Parkinson's UK Forum: Community discussion on GranaGard pomegranate seed oil versus eating whole pomegranates
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/granagard-pomegranite-oil/22959
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2020-02-15
Date basis: Opening post timestamp (2020-02-15) inspected directly via Parkinson's UK Forum public thread record.

In a February 2020 Parkinson's UK Forum thread (4 posts), a community member inquired whether anyone had tried GranaGard, a commercial nano-emulsified pomegranate seed oil supplement marketed around preclinical neuroprotection, noting an intention to try it for Parkinson's disease. Replies reflected clear community skepticism and food-vs-supplement distinction: one respondent pointed out on the same day that the product materials did not substantiate Parkinson's disease claims; after the original poster shared a press release on preclinical brain protection, another member replied (2020-02-16) that while they enjoy eating pomegranates for their taste and antioxidant content, they were reluctant to spend money on a manufactured pomegranate oil supplement, emphasizing "there is a huge difference between mice and men." No participant reported measured Parkinson's symptom changes from raw pomegranate arils or GranaGard.
