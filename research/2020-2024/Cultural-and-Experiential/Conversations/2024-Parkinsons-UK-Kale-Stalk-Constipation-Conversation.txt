Title: Public Parkinson's forum discussion mentioning kale and leafy greens while managing severe constipation
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/constipation-and-the-role-of-stool-softeners/38908
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2024
Date basis: Opening post date (2024-01-10) on the public Parkinson's UK Discourse forum thread.

In a January 2024 public thread on the Parkinson's UK community forum posted on behalf of a person living with Parkinson's disease experiencing refractory constipation and difficulty clearing oral stool softeners, the firsthand account explicitly described kale—particularly the fibrous stalk—as the best food they had found for keeping digestion moving, even while continuing to struggle with severe bowel symptoms. Other commenters in the thread emphasized maintaining ~2 L/day fluid intake, consulting clinicians regarding docusate/lactulose, or adding papaya. In an earlier July 2018 Parkinson's UK constipation thread (Topic 14613), another contributor similarly reported eating small meals containing spinach and kale to ease bloating and post-meal fullness. These lived-experience notes align directionally with pilot fiber-constipation trial data while underscoring that severe PD constipation often requires medical management.
