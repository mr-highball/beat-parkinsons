Reported direction: One caregiver described worsening joint stiffness with seasonal raw tomato use and improvement after stopping. Other accounts reported no comparable arthritis flare.

A caregiver described fresh tomatoes eaten with other ingredients and a period of worse joint stiffness and pain that interfered with walking. They reported improvement after stopping tomatoes. They also named weather and another nightshade as possible contributors. The account describes joint disease. The thread supplies no controlled repeat exposure, Parkinson diagnosis or clinical muscle rigidity measurement.

Other members describe gastrointestinal symptoms without clear arthritis aggravation, or no known arthritis flare in the person they observed. Cooking was a suggestion, with no resulting observation reported. The distinct observations and hypotheses do not establish a common tomato response.
