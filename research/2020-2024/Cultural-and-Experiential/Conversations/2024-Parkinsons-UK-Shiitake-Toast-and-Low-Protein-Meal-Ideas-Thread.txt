Title: Parkinson's UK forum cookbook discussion sharing cooked shiitake mushroom toast in low-protein daytime meals around levodopa
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source: https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/t/cook-book/40040
Source kind: Conversation
Publication date: 2024-04-08
Date basis: Opening post timestamp on Parkinson's UK public forum topic 40040 (2024-04-08; shiitake lunch post 2024-04-16; low-protein reply 2024-05-14), cross-referenced with topic 24249 (2020-06-20) and topic 46401 (2025-08-21).

In a public Parkinson's UK Community Forum cookbook thread opened on 2024-04-08 (topic 40040), a participant posted on 2024-04-16 inviting meal ideas and sharing their own lunch of Jerusalem artichoke soup paired with cooked shiitake mushroom toast and a microgreen salad. A follow-up reply on 2024-05-14 from a spouse of a person with Parkinson's emphasized low-protein daytime meals (keeping protein below one-third of meal composition alongside carbohydrate, fat, and fiber, spaced 30-60 minutes from Madopar) to minimize competition with levodopa absorption, noting that vegetarian, vegetable-forward meals helped manage medication reliability. A separate Parkinson's UK low-protein breakfast/lunch thread (topic 24249, opened 2020-06-20) likewise included cooked mushrooms on toast as a savory low-protein staple, while a 2025-08-21 thread (topic 46401) noted that frozen mushrooms often have an unappealing texture compared with fresh mushrooms despite chopping difficulties from tremor. Neither thread reported a specific before-and-after symptom change attributable to shiitake mushrooms. All account names and personal identifiers are omitted.
