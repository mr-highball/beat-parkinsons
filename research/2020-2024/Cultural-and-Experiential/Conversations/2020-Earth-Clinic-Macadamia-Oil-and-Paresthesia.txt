Title: Public account of oral unrefined macadamia oil and perceived change in tingling
Source kind: Public health-forum conversation
Original thread: https://answers.earthclinic.com/muscle-and-bone-pain-in-shoulder-and-leg-pains.html
Republished copy: https://www.earthclinic.com/mobile/cures/peripheral-neuropathy-treatment/macademia-nut-oil/
Date: Original reply displayed December 25, 2020. The current republished page displays December 27, 2020. The experience itself was recalled as occurring in 2011.
Text coverage: Privacy-preserving summary of one firsthand account and its copied version.

One participant recalled that toe numbness and tingling subsided after a period of consuming unrefined macadamia nut oil. The page repeats the same experience, and a later clarification changes the described serving size. There is no independent diagnosis, stable dose record, movement measure or comparison with an untreated period beyond personal recall. Unrefined does not establish cold pressing. The reported sensory experience does not describe Parkinson disease or resting tremor. The two versions are one account.
