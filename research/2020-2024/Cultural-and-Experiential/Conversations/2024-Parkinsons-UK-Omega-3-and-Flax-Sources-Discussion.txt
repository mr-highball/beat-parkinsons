Efficacy evidence: General dietary exchange only; supplies no directional clinical signal.

Thread summary (2024-03-12 to 2024-03-16):
- A participant asked whether members take omega-3 or omega-3-6-9 supplements and whether they help Parkinson's symptoms.
- Peer and caregiver replies emphasized obtaining omega-3 from whole foods such as fatty fish, olive oil, walnuts, chia seeds, and ground flaxseeds, plus plant oils (canola, soybean) or vegan microalgae supplements, advising consultation with clinicians before starting supplements.
- No member reported measurable motor, cognitive, mood, or fatigue improvement attributable to flaxseed oil.
