El-Assri and colleagues collected ethnobotanical reports in six Taounate communes from May 2020 to May 2021. Their common fig row lists El karmôss and Chriha as local names and includes both fruits and leaves in the plant-part column. Honey and a preparation without a solvent share a separate column. The row does not pair each name or solvent with a part. It leaves fruit drying and fig-specific indications unspecified. The 2003 Taounate and 2017 Ouezzane papers appear in its comparison literature.
