Reported direction: The review describes a customary practice without attributing a symptom direction to sesame oil alone.
Authenticity: Published review and its source table were inspected. The cited case remains separately tracked.
Efficacy evidence: Secondary descriptive context for spinal cord injury. An isolated topical or oral sesame oil effect was not measured.

Practice and context: The authors describe topical self-massage with sesame oil in a traditional account of managing Vata. The source is a review about spinal cord injury. The table describes the practice alongside Dashamularishta without an isolated sesame-oil outcome or controlled comparison.

Efficacy boundary: Do not transfer the review's pooled or narrative spinal injury findings to ingested cold-pressed or toasted sesame oil and Parkinson pain, depression, anxiety or fatigue. These recorded practices have different routes and include additional interventions.

Lineage: Review reference 18 points to PMID 30147009. The indexed follow-up records the currently verified access depth. This is one reported traditional lineage rather than independent corroboration.
