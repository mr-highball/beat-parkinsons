Exposure and context: The treating authors diagnosed Kampavata using clinical history and examination and tracked Parkinson symptom items. The course included named oral botanical formulas, topical medicated oils, heated grain bundles and other treatments. The article does not document a standalone culinary sesame oil exposure or confirm a base oil for each topical formula.

Observed outcomes: On the reported ordinal scale, rigidity and body bradykinesia each declined from 2 to 1 over the mixed course. Motivation/initiation declined from 2 to 0. Depression stayed at 1. Falling, rest tremor and swallowing were scored 0 across the visits. These observations are patient-specific and span overlapping treatments.

Efficacy boundary: There is one patient and no comparator. The report cannot separate the pharmacologic, manual, heat or ingredient contributions. Scores at zero throughout show no symptom change; they do not establish prevention. This course is separate from the Sharma and colleagues case in the same year.
