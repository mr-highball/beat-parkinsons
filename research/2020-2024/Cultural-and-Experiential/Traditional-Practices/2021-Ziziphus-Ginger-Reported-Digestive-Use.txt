Reviewed: 2026-11-20

The review attributes use of Ziziphus xylopyrus fruit powder with a small amount of ginger for indigestion or stomach pain to earlier South Asian plant-use reports. The row does not give a treated comparison, recorded response, or timing independent of the compound preparation. The earlier sources need checking for shared reporting and the original wording.

The Ziziphus genus includes plants called Chinese date or jujube, distinct from date palm. This source is a record of reported use and supplies no measured Parkinson symptom response.
