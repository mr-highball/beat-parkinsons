Jeddi and colleagues surveyed 500 residents of six Taounate communities during January–April 2023. Their common fig entry uses Karmous-Chriha, identifies voucher BLUMP 468 and lists fruit with the codes Pow/In. The table legend defines those codes as powder and inhalation. Inf is the separate code for infusion. The row groups its preparation and use categories and leaves the corresponding routes, drying state and outcomes unresolved. Its 45 mentions are use citations from the survey.

Open trail: Seek observation-level information that distinguishes oral powder from inhalation, clarifies the fruit's drying state and identifies individual use cases. The published row does not establish a visual episode or Parkinson diagnosis.
