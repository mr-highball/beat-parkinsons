Song et al. (Journal of Food Biochemistry, 2022; PMID:36369998) review traditional cold-press and modern extraction methods for walnut kernel oil (Juglans regia and J. sigillata; 52-70% kernel lipid), its rich linoleic/alpha-linolenic acid, tocopherol, sterol, squalene, and phenolic profile, and popular culinary reputation as a nutritious, "intelligence-boosting" finishing oil. The authors emphasize that processing and storage heavily influence flavor and oxidative degradation, and explicitly conclude that clinical studies in humans remain warranted to substantiate therapeutic claims—underscoring the gap between popular reputation and verified Parkinson symptom evidence.
