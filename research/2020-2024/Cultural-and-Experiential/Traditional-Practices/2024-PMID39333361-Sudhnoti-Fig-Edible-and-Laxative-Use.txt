In Table 2, row 48 gives Ficus carica as Tossa, voucher MUH-99, and identifies fruit in a decoction. It records eleven citations across the plant entry, rather than eleven measured beneficial outcomes or eleven reports of a single indication. The priority here is the reported preparation and use.

Rows 49–51 distinguish Ficus johannis, Ficus palmata and Ficus religiosa. Their named preparations and indications do not substitute for the Ficus carica record. The separately cataloged oral fig decoction in Tarfaya is a useful comparison of traditional preparations from a different regional source.
