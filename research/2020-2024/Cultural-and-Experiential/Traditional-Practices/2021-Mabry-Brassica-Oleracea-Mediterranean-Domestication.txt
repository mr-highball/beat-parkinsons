Mabry et al. (Molecular Biology and Evolution, 2021; PMID:34157722; PMC8476135) trace the Mediterranean domestication of Brassica oleracea from eastern Mediterranean wild relatives into diverse culinary morphotypes including broccoli (B. oleracea var. italica), long cultivated across Italy and the Mediterranean as a cool-season staple vegetable (corroborated by Mittal et al., 2025, PMID:40007500). In traditional culinary practice, broccoli florets are eaten raw, lightly steamed, boiled, or roasted as everyday digestive-fiber vegetables, distinct from modern concentrated glucoraphanin extracts or raw broccoli-seed preparations.
