Bao et al. (Fitoterapia, 2023; PMID:36462661) review the culinary and medicinal heritage of Zanthoxylum bungeanum Maxim. (Chinese prickly ash / huajiao) centered in Sichuan Province, China. As a dual medicinal-and-edible condiment, huajiao forms the signature numbing-tingling (ma) backbone of Sichuan cuisine, driven by pericarp volatile oils and alkylamides. In Traditional Chinese Medicine materia medica, Z. bungeanum is traditionally described as warming the middle burner, easing abdominal or tooth pain, expelling parasites, and soothing itching. Publication date (2023) is separate from classical TCM textual lineages; culinary tradition neither proves symptom relief nor rules out salivation/tingling discomfort in people with Parkinson's disease.
