The folk-therapy article describes a cooked liquid containing black beans with lotus seeds, Erythrina and mulberry leaves. A nearby different mixture names Vigna unguiculata; that identity cannot be assigned to the black beans in this mixture. The paper mentions a questionnaire but gives no black-bean-specific sleep response, comparator or Parkinson subgroup. It does not establish canned preparation. A 2024 Vietnamese publication repeats a related black-bean and lotus sleep recommendation, which does not independently establish an outcome.

Claim 0594 | Related evidence: The black bean, lotus and leaf mixture is described as a customary sleep remedy. There is no measured sleep outcome, confirmed canned preparation, unambiguous taxon or Parkinson group.
