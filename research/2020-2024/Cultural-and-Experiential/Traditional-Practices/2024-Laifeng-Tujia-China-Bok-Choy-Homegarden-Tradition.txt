Efficacy evidence: Customary East Asian staple vegetable heritage only; documents ordinary cooked/steamed culinary consumption rather than medicinal treatment for Parkinson's symptoms

Hou, Yu & Yao (2024, J Ethnobiol Ethnomed, PMID:39623503, PMC11610127) surveyed traditional homegardens of Laifeng Tujia ethnic communities in the Wuling Mountains of central China and documented bok choy / pak choi (Brassica rapa var. chinensis) as the third most widely maintained staple food plant (Relative Frequency of Citation RFC=0.897). Across East Asian culinary heritage, bok choy is routinely steamed, blanched, or stir-fried as an everyday leafy vegetable—contrasting starkly with extreme raw-consumption thyroid case reports (Chu & Seltzer 2010, PMID:20484407)—and is not traditionally prescribed as a targeted remedy for Parkinson's motor or non-motor symptoms.
