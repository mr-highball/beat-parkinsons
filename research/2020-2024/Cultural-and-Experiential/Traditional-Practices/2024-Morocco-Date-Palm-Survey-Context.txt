Title: Date palm reports in a Daraa Tafilalet ethnobotanical survey
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12009623/
PMID: 40259956
PMCID: PMC12009623
DOI: 10.22038/AJP.2024.24096
Publication: November–December 2024
Source type: Traditional practice survey with separately cited historical background
Coverage: Distilled original survey methods, Results and date-palm discussion.

The investigators collected questionnaire responses from 142 people in southeastern Morocco in June 2021. Their survey concerns locally used products for perceived immune support. Respondents listed date palm fruit as the most frequently used medicinal plant in that survey. The later discussion mentions headache among prior reported medicinal uses of date palm, attributing the description to earlier literature. The new respondents' survey provides no count of headache users, fruit drying method, measured pain effect or diagnosis-specific result. The customary use and the authors' literature citation describe different evidence.
