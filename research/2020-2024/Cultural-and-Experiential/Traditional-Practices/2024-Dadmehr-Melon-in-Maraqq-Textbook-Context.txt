The authors interpret older medical texts, including Avicenna's Canon, on abdominal disturbances and neurologic complaints such as headache, melancholia and epilepsy. Table 1 lists Cucumis melo under the traditional name Bīttīkh among remedies for the broader marāqq-related category. The melon part, preparation, date of practice, diagnosis and observed patient response are unspecified. The historical list provides context for customary use.
