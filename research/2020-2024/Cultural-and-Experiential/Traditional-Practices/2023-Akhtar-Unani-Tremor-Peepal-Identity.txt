The Majoon-e-Jograj Gogul composition names peepal (Ficus religiosa). The common culinary fig is Ficus carica. The ingredient-list match from a search aggregator did not hold against the original PDF. The article describes reported traditional tremor use for the formulation and discusses ginger, black pepper and almond among its components. Use the 2010 original for the patient observations. The species, part and mixture prevent a raw common-fig inference.

The Safoof-e-Rasha section retells a separate 2011 clinical series. The original measured rest and postural frequencies separately, and its composition has no fig species. Raasha is used in the review as a historical tremor term. Matching it to a modern diagnosis requires case-level information.
