Title: Thirisadham, a Siddha three-spice formulation
Source: https://www.jahm.co.in/index.php/jahm/article/view/703
DOI: 10.70066/jahm.v11i3.703
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Traditional practice discussed in a published review
Publication date: 2023-04-20
Date basis: Journal posting date of the inspected record. The original tradition is treated as undated.
Text coverage: Publisher abstract and formulation identity checked against the separately dated 2025 Siddha neuroscience review.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25, source and claim attribution review
Reported direction: Authors describe possible neuroprotective properties of the constituents. They do not report a measured Parkinson symptom change from the combined formulation.
Authenticity: The preparation has a traceable name and ingredient list in a published account. No early source or date of origin was established.
Efficacy evidence: Review of separate constituent studies. No controlled culinary cardamom or clove comparison for the frozen Parkinson symptoms was reported.

The local names Elakkai, Kirambu and Lavangapathiri refer to ingredients
of the Siddha preparation. The 2025 review describes these as cardamom
(Elettaria), clove (Syzygium aromaticum) and Indian bay leaf
(Cinnamomum tamala). The last ingredient is kept separate from a
culinary cinnamon-bark exposure. The formula and individual spices
can lead to different evidence questions.
