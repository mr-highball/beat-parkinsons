Community and context: The author group situates the discussion in Ayurvedic clinical training and text interpretation. It groups mustard with other pungent dietary exposures and associates excess Katu rasa with Kampa, or shaking. The authors state that the classical texts they summarize do not describe causes specific to Kampavata, so their discussion draws on the wider category of Vatavyadhi.

Reported direction: Worsening is proposed for excessive pungent exposure as a class. The mustard species, seed preparation, timing, quantity and tremor subtype are not specified.

Evidence and limits: The document supplies an attributable modern interpretation and a trail into the traditional vocabulary. There is no individual exposure record, observed response, diagnosis, comparison or isolated mustard effect. A classical-text passage separately identifying mustard for Parkinson rest tremor has not been verified.

Related research: The 2024 Wright mixed broccoli and white-mustard tea reports contain both isolated favorable and transient unfavorable reports, with no mustard-only comparison. The 2019 and 2025 seed processing experiments assess chemistry.
