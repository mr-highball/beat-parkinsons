Efficacy evidence: Practitioner survey of customary uses.

The West Bank survey interviewed 100 traditional medicine practitioners in 2023. The reported top plants include anise (Pimpinella anisum), ginger (Zingiber officinale) and spearmint (Mentha spicata), alongside two other identified species. Cultural familiarity and frequency of recommendations document customary use. The abstract supplies no corresponding Parkinson diagnosis, before-and-after findings, or speech intelligibility scores. Cinnamon is absent from the reported top five and its inclusion among the remaining species is unverified.
