Idm'hand and colleagues interviewed residents of five communes of Tarfaya province in southern Morocco from December 2016 through December 2017. The interviews were conducted in Hassani. The fig table row identifies an orally consumed fruit decoction used for constipation. The table does not separate fresh from dried fruit or report a preparation quantity.

The survey records a customary gastrointestinal use in its own setting. The earlier Ouezzane survey describes leaves and different indications. The separately registered Iranian trial concerns a fig-food intervention in adults with irritable bowel syndrome.

Open trail: Verify the preparation details and the geographical scope of the term Chriha against separate original accounts. No patient-level outcomes were available in this source.
