de Oliveira et al. (Plants, 2022; PMID:36501350; PMC9739750) review the history, taxonomy, and metabolite diversity of commercial Vanilla planifolia and Vanilla x tahitensis alongside wild relatives across the genus. Cured vanilla pods represent one of the world's most cherished culinary condiments, containing a complex spectrum of vanillin, vanillic acid, p-hydroxybenzaldehyde, and minor aromatic phenolics (~1.5-2.5% vanillin by cured-pod weight)—sharply contrasting with industrial synthetic isolated vanillin used at supradietary doses in rodent pharmacology. Publication date (2022) is distinct from pre-Columbian Mesoamerican cultivation; culinary appreciation does not verify clinical neurological efficacy.
