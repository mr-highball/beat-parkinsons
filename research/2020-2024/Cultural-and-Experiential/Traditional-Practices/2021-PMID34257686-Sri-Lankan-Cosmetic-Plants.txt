Date basis: Journal online publication. Interviews took place from January 2018 to August 2019.
Reviewed: 2026-11-25
Reported direction: Practitioners described intended uses for skin, hair and oral care. The study did not record a change in a Parkinson symptom.
Authenticity: Primary survey. Of 210 identified specialists, 90 Ayurveda physicians and 30 other traditional practitioners participated across nine provinces. The investigators recorded 133 plant species in 64 families and compared reports with Ayurveda references.
Efficacy evidence: Citation frequency and agreement describe use within the interview sample. The authors did not test those preparations in people with Parkinson disease or measure falls.
