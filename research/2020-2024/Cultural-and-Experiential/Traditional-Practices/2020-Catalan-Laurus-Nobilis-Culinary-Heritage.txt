Gras et al. (2020, Foods, PMID:33383896, PMC7824323) synthesized 10,078 wild food plant use reports across the Catalan linguistic area (overall food ICF=0.97) and found Laurus nobilis L. among the top five most cited culinary plants (accounting for 4.71% of all use reports). Traditionally, dried whole leaves are steeped as an aromatic seasoning in stews, broths, and preserved foods and removed before eating, rather than consumed in large gram-level ground doses. This culinary pattern is key transferability context when evaluating high-dose herbal infusions or essential oils against frozen ground bay leaf claims in Parkinson's disease.
