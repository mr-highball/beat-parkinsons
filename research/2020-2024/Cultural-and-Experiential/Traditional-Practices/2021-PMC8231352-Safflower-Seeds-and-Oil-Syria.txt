Table 2 documents internal uses of Carthamus tinctorius seed oil in central Syria. Reported purposes include heart-disease prevention and use for menstrual problems, abdominal pain after childbirth, and joint pain. The table identifies oil from the seed and gives no pressing method, preparation grade or measured dose. The oil entry gives no Parkinson diagnosis or use for dysphagia.

Reported direction: Intended easing of some reported pains and preventive use for heart disease. The entry supplies no measured change in swallowing.
Authenticity: Original ethnomedicinal survey and publisher Table 2. This is a regional record of reported practices.
Efficacy evidence: No assigned clinical comparator, systematic symptom change or verification of independent outcomes. The account documents traditional uses at their reported specificity.
