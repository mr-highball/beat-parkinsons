The interview table records a leaf and stem-bark decoction of Pycnanthus angolensis for insomnia and hypertension in Ile-Ife, southwestern Nigeria. The English name African nutmeg refers to this species in the study. It differs from Myristica fragrans, the culinary ingredient in the frozen experiment. The survey documents a reported practice with no separate clinical insomnia trajectory or confirmed Parkinson cohort.
