The Chimpi ingredient is dried Citrus unshiu peel, a mandarin preparation. The author describes its inclusion in Yokukansankachimpihange and the prescription context for sleep disorders. The article evaluates the finished mixture in dementia with Lewy bodies and focuses on REM sleep behavior. The narrow safety inquiry covers falls in connection with oversedation or muscular relaxation. The account does not provide observed fall frequency after raw sweet orange in Parkinson disease. This cultural note and the companion clinical note describe the same article and should share one evidence family.
