Title: Melon fruit and seed in a Persian traditional-medicine review of sweating
Source kind: Traditional practices, literature-based historical documentation
Publication date: 2020-12-25
Date basis: Date of the inspected modern paper. Individual customary use dates are unspecified.
PMID: 34466623
PMCID: PMC8343902
DOI: 10.31661/gmj.v9i0.2003
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8343902/
Coverage: Original body and separate treatment-purpose and ingredient tables.

The review examines sweating in classic Persian medical writings. Its condition table includes ra'shah, a historical tremor label. A separate table lists Cucumis melo as Bettikh and names fruit or seeds among plants used to promote sweating. The tables establish two distinct findings about customary uses. A melon-specific tremor remedy or observed patient response is unverified.
