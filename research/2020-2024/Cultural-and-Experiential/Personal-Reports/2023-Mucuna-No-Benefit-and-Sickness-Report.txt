An anonymized writer reporting tremor and other motor symptoms describes trying a commercial Mucuna preparation, feeling sick and noticing no other benefit. The writer separately describes some improvement after beginning prescribed medication.

Reported direction: No perceived benefit and an adverse experience under the reported preparation.
Authenticity: Original public post inspected; diagnosis, product composition, dose and timing are not independently verified.
Efficacy evidence: One uncontrolled report, with no standardized rest-tremor score or reliable treatment comparison. Lower weight than controlled studies; temporal association does not establish causation.
