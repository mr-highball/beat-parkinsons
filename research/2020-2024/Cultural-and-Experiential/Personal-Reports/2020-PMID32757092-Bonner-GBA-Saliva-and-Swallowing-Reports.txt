Design: Qualitative interviews with 15 people with GBA-associated Parkinson disease, five people with idiopathic Parkinson disease, and five clinical experts

Figure 2 maps reports from the 15 GBA-associated Parkinson participants. Seven reported excessive drooling or saliva, five dry mouth, four chewing or swallowing difficulties and nine an eating or drinking impact. Among this interview sample, excessive drooling or saliva and chewing or swallowing difficulties were reported in stages 2 or 3. The age and stage distribution, prompted and spontaneous responses and the five-person idiopathic group do not support a comparative prevalence estimate. One eating-and-drinking narrative concerns a loss of smell or taste. An impact on eating and drinking does not establish a specific food as its cause or remedy. No raw-fig comparison.
