Design: Cross-sectional thematic analysis of the November 2018 Fox Insight off-period survey

Of 13,359 people invited and 2,684 starters, 2,110 met this paper's analysis criteria. The overlap in survey date, invitation count, screening language and starter count links this analysis to the 2020 Chahine paper. Its more general symptom and strategy questions and its eligibility and response denominator differ. The articles should not count as independent samples.

Table 3 lists 57 of 1,498 specific trigger accounts in an intake category covering hunger, delayed food or irregular meals. Among 1,416 accounts describing coping strategies, 97 mention eating or drinking strategies. Examples include beverages, candy, citrus and protein. The paper does not enumerate separate effects or directions by ingredient and symptom. There is no measured raw-fig exposure or drooling-specific estimate attached to these counts.

The independently listed supplemental table could not be verified through its repository and PMC download routes. No supplemental saliva results are inferred.
