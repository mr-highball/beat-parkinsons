Reported direction: Unspecified; descriptive frequency of bothersome off-period drooling mentions without food exposure attribution.
Authenticity: Peer-reviewed thematic coding of 2,106 respondents from the Fox Insight Parkinson cohort.
Efficacy evidence: Cross-sectional symptom-burden counts only; supplies no food-specific intervention or drooling severity change.
Design: Thematic coding of open-ended answers to an observational questionnaire

The Fox Insight survey invited 13,359 people, 2,684 started and 2,106 contributed to the final most-bothersome-symptoms analysis. Respondents indicated that they experienced off periods. They were asked about up to three symptoms that bothered them most during off periods. Table 3 counts 26 reports within the drooling class, which includes descriptions of excess or escaping saliva. The table describes answers that investigators considered unlikely to be captured by standard wearing-off questionnaires. The count does not estimate drooling prevalence or grade severity in the full sample. No recorded food comparison or raw-fig intervention.

Lineage: The 2021 Mantri companion analysis used the November 26, 2018 survey and the same invitation and starter counts with a different analysis sample and questions. The sponsor-held earlier poster lists 11 journaling participants and 14 phone interviews. The journal describes 12 journaling participants and 14 phone interviews and separately reports the 2,106 responses. The reported counts for those stages cannot establish individual overlap or explain the difference of one. Treat the two journal reports as a shared survey family.
