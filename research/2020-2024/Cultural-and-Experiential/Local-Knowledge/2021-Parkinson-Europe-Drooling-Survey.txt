Title: Parkinson's Europe patient and caregiver survey on drooling
Collection: Cultural and experiential
Source kind: Structured patient and caregiver survey
Source: https://parkinsonseurope.org/app/uploads/2024/02/epda-sialorrhea-survey-report.pdf
Publication date: 2021
Date basis: The original report states a June 2021 preparation date. Responses were collected in October and November 2020.
Reviewed: 2026-11-20
Reported direction: Respondents described existing drooling and its daily impact. No isolated ingredient effect was observed.
Authenticity: Original association report and corrected journal reproduction of the severity chart were inspected.
Efficacy evidence: Voluntary patient and caregiver reports with no dietary assignment or controlled comparison.

Among 382 respondents, the original summary gives rounded shares of 26% mild, 35% moderate and 27% severe. The sample included people living with Parkinson disease, caregivers and a minority reporting related conditions. Responses describe effects on communication, swallowing and social comfort. The survey does not establish population prevalence or the direction of any food effect.

The later journal panel used the same responses. Its corrected chart gives 25.9% mild, 34.8% moderate and 27.2% severe, with 1.3% normal, 8.4% slight and 2.4% unavailable. The correction restores values in the displayed figure and does not add participants. The report identifies Merz Therapeutics as its project partner.
