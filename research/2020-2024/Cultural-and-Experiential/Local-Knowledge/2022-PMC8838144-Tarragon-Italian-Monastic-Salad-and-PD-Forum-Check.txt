Historical Culinary Record (PMC8838144):
Cornara et al. (2022, PMC8838144) documented the botanical ingredients of the 17th-century Italian Capuchin monks' traditional mixed salad (misticanza / preboggion-style culinary greens) described by Padre Salvatore Ravecca (1633), confirming Artemisia dracunculus (dragoncello / tarragon) among aromatic garden herbs blended raw in small culinary quantities to balance bitter wild greens and aid palatability and digestion. No historical claim linked tarragon to tremor, rigidity, gait, cognitive impairment, or neurodegenerative symptoms.

Parkinson Community Coverage Note:
A Round 7 search of the Parkinson's UK public community forum (https://forum.parkinsons.org.uk/) returned zero threads mentioning tarragon or Artemisia dracunculus for any Parkinson disease symptom, confirming absence of documented English-language PD patient self-reports.
