Title: Neelum Valley wild-plant inventory and separately reported sleep practices
Source kind: Ethnobotanical field survey
PMID: 34324561
PMCID: PMC8321310
Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8321310/
Date basis: Original survey article published 2021-07-29 and retracted 2023-06-14.
Archive scope: Retracted original, retained to distinguish the botanical names and document why the source was excluded. Reviewed 2026-11-20.
Retraction: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0286781

The article reports a 103-species inventory and lists Viscum album and a Withania entry under distinct reported sleep uses. Neither identifies Solanum tuberosum. The PLOS editors withdrew the article over authorship, competing-interest and peer-review concerns. These entries require independent corroboration and carry no efficacy weight.
