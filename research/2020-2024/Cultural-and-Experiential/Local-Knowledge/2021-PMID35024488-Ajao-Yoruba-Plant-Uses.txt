The dicot and monocot tables identify sweet basil as Ocimum basilicum, mango as Mangifera indica, and also record sweet orange, lemongrass and turmeric. The listings distinguish culinary usage and other purposes that involve different parts or forms of the plants. Sweet basil has separate stomach-related uses in the inventory. Mango is food, while the text gives other purposes for plant parts distinct from the fruit. The source gives no comparison for basil and constipation, or raw mango and insomnia.
