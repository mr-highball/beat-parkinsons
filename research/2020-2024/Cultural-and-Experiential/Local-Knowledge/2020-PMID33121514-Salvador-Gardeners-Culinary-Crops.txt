The researchers combined grower interviews with visits to 17 urban gardens in Salvador, Bahia, Brazil, including eight communal gardens and nine private gardens. Gardeners commonly named arugula, basil, chives, kale and orange among other crops. The inventory found Cymbopogon citratus, lemongrass, in seven gardens. Interviewees included it among locally used medicinal plants. The paper does not link these plant records to measured falls, balance, or a Parkinson treatment result.
