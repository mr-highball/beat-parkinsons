The investigators interviewed 150 residents in the Isparta region of Turkey. The fruit was dried, ground or pounded and used as a culinary spice. Residents also described sour sauce from the fruit and tea from other plant parts. One passage describes the perceived effect of fruit tea on the throat. It does not describe emotional relaxation or a depressive outcome.

Reported direction: Documented uses and self-reported household practices, without measured mood or Parkinson outcomes.
Efficacy evidence: The field report identifies local culinary preparations and meanings. Diagnosis, longitudinal depression outcomes and controlled comparisons were unavailable.
