Source locator: Table 2, Salvia officinalis voucher HUMPOM663
Reported direction: Customary use for kidney stones, renal colic, and promoting urination. Clinical direction unmeasured.
Authenticity: Original fieldwork table and botanical identification verified.
Efficacy evidence: Community use reports, without measured symptom change or clinical controls.

The northeastern Morocco survey collected plant-use information from 488 participants from February 2018 through January 2020. The entry for common sage records seven mentions across its listed uses. These uses are associated with leaves or aerial parts prepared as an oral infusion or decoction. The table gives a combined mention count and does not allocate reports to individual indications or preparations.

The reported renal colic use supplies pain context. The report of use as a diuretic supplies neighboring urinary context and does not establish whether urgency increased or decreased. The survey does not measure urinary urgency, quantify pain changes, or describe a cohort with Parkinson disease.
