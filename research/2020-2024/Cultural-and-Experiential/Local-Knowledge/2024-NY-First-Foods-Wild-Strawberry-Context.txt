The assessment describes wild strawberries among traditional First
Foods that contribute to Tribal food security in New York State.
Its discussion connects growing conditions, access to wild foods
and food sovereignty. The attribution for the First Foods sentence
is a regional EPA climate adaptation report (reference 421).

This is a traceable description of food use and access. The passage
provides no firsthand symptom comparison, fall count or Parkinson
treatment claim. It does not give an origin date for the practice
or establish the equivalence of the wild fruit and cultivated
strawberries in the experiment.
