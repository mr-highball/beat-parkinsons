Source locator: Table 2, Moraceae, Ficus carica under the name Inza.
Reported direction: Community members describe customary use for warts and stomach disorders.
Authenticity: Original botanical use table verified.
Efficacy evidence: Reported uses without a clinical exposure and outcome comparison.

The survey table groups common fig's fruit and latex on the same line and gives 21 citations for the record. It does not assign the reports separately to the plant parts or the individual indications. The nearby constipation and digestion entries refer to white mulberry, Morus alba. These community uses do not document a tremor, a Parkinson diagnosis or a measured change after raw fig.
