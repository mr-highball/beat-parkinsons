# Lemongrass flavor in a sleep trial

## Flavored placebo and separate nightmare reporting

The 64-person randomized trial used lemongrass, peppermint and mint flavorings in its placebo. One active-arm participant and no placebo participant reported nightmares. This adverse-event count gives no estimate of dream enactment or an isolated lemongrass effect. Actigraphy and overall sleep-quality comparisons were inconclusive.
