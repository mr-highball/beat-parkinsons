# Food experiment evidence traced through Spanish-language records

## 2021 macronutrient intake and cognition in Monterrey

López-Botello and colleagues reported one 24-hour dietary recall and MoCA screening in 21 outpatients with idiopathic Parkinson disease in MDS abstract 327, published in the September 2021 supplement. The abstract gives a mean MoCA of 22 ± 5 and a positive rank correlation between protein intake and MoCA (Spearman ρ = .474, p = .03). It also states that participants with excessive protein intake showed cognitive impairment. No group counts or adjusted model are provided to reconcile those statements. Carbohydrate and lipid comparisons were inconclusive. Individual foods, item-level cognitive outcomes, and effects of a change in intake are unreported. [MDS abstract 327](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/does-macronutrient-dietary-intake-of-patients-with-parkinson-disease-correlates-with-the-cognitive-function-moca/) · [September 10, 2021 journal supplement](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.28794).

The same center’s abstract 326 reports 31 outpatients and negative associations between Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index and body weight (r = −.545, p = .002), body mass index (r = −.383, p = .033), arm circumference (r = −.436, p = .016), and arm muscle area (r = −.431, p = .015). Its nutrition exposure is anthropometry. The report contains no measurement of food consumption, and overlap with abstract 327 is unverified. [MDS abstract 326](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/sleep-quality-and-nutrition-effects-in-patients-with-parkinsons-disease/).

## 2022 eating behavior during COVID-19 in Monterrey

In MDS abstract 13, López-Botello and colleagues assessed 50 people with Parkinson disease surveyed from August to October 2021. During the pandemic, 74% reported less motivation, 70% more stress, and 56% sleep problems. Restrained-eating scores correlated with age (r = −.46, p < .001). The abstract reports no individual food exposure or association of eating scores with a specific Parkinson symptom. The same author team presented eating behavior and body mass index at IAPRD in 2022 and in an August 2023 journal supplement. Participant overlap could not be verified from the accessible publisher and author records. [2022 MDS abstract 13](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/does-aging-influence-eating-behavior-during-covid-19-pandemic-in-patients-with-parkinsons-disease-in-northeastern-mexico/) · [2022 IAPRD program, P223](https://iaprd-parkinson.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Final-printed-program-IAPRD-2022.pdf) · [2023 journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.parkreldis.2023.105759).

## Nutritional assessments with levodopa and stimulation in Barakaldo

The first verified report in this series is Ayo Mentxakatorre’s September 2022 MDS abstract 985, also reported in Spanish Society of Neurology abstract 18070. Both give 26 patients, with associations of disease duration with lower weight or poorer nutritional markers in the oral and intestinal levodopa groups. The 2023 society report gives 45 patients at baseline and 37 reassessed at six months. The 2024 MDS report gives 87 patients at baseline (33 oral levodopa, 24 intestinal levodopa/carbidopa gel and 30 deep brain stimulation). The 2023 and 2024 reports both describe higher Mini Nutritional Assessment scores at six months in the intestinal gel group and no significant weight difference between treatment groups at follow-up. These are likely successive reports from the same research program. They did not measure the effect of any individual food. [September 2022 MDS abstract 985](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/nutritional-status-in-advanced-parkinsons-disease-preliminary-results/) · [2022 Spanish proceedings, printed p. 307](https://static.elsevier.es/congresos/pdf/151/neurologyperspectives2022.pdf) · [2023 proceedings, abstract 19321](https://static.elsevier.es/congresos/pdf/160/neurologyperspectives2023.pdf) · [2024 MDS abstract 344](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/nutritional-status-implication-at-6-months-in-parkinsons-disease-follow-up-results/) · [2025 and 2026 follow-ups](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Multilingual-Followup.md#nutritional-status-during-parkinson-treatments-in-barakaldo).

## 2021 formulated kefir and exercise exhaustion in healthy adults

Lee and colleagues’ February 25, 2021 report describes a double-blind crossover of 16 healthy, untrained men aged 20–30. Each four-week period used a heat-treated, spray-dried commercial kefir preparation or a matched, acidified skim-milk placebo. The report gives mean post-period exercise times to exhaustion of 11.76 and 9.08 minutes, respectively. The abstract and Results section give different p-values for that comparison, with both below .01. The study measured exercise performance and biochemical responses in healthy volunteers. It gives no plain kefir or patient-reported Parkinson’s fatigue result for `0950`.
