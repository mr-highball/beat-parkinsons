# Spearmint and urine output

## Spearmint leaf extract and five-hour rat urine output

Parmar, Jadon and Purohit assigned six rats per group and report five-hour urine output of 10.42 and 14.12 mL in two ethanolic leaf extract groups versus 2.14 mL in the blank group. The animals were housed three together for collection, leaving the observational unit unclear. Urine output and urgency are different outcomes, and an ethanolic extract cannot establish the effect of culinary dried spearmint in Parkinson.
