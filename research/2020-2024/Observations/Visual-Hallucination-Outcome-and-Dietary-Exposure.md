# Visual hallucination outcomes and measured dietary exposure

The 2015–2019 sources used first onset, adverse-event incidence and elicited perceptions. The 2020–2024 originals show why those measures need their own comparators. Nighttime distress on a sleep scale, the clinical hallucinations and psychosis item, the perception score used to predict cognitive impairment, and face pareidolia during a task have different denominators and time windows. [Earlier source comparison](../../2015-2019/Observations/Fig-and-Visual-Hallucination-Source-Contexts.md) · [Sleep and stimulation](../Source-Text/PMC7293679.txt) · [Pooled drug comparisons](../Source-Text/2021-PMID33864229-Gocovri-Pooled-Nonmotor-Outcomes.txt) · [Baseline perception and later cognition](../Source-Text/2023-PMID36647231-Visual-Hallucinations-and-Future-Cognition.txt).

The EASE LID extension sampled participants from earlier randomized studies as well as a stimulation cohort. Its ever-reported adverse-event proportion is a different outcome from the pooled parent trials' change in the hallucinations and psychosis item. Count the overlapping trial lineage once when interpreting corroboration. Open-label opicapone and apomorphine reports provide separate medication comparisons. [Long-term safety](../Source-Text/2020-PMID31929122-Gocovri-Long-Term-Hallucination-Safety.txt) · [Opicapone](../Source-Text/PMC7055125.txt) · [Apomorphine](../Source-Text/PMC9979890.txt).

The de novo DUPARC protocol includes nutrient diaries and nonmotor phenotyping. The original protocol provides neither an individual dried fig variable nor an outcome estimate for that food. The Annonaceae questionnaire study, by comparison, does identify lifetime exposures and reports a binary visual hallucination item by clinical profile. Its adjusted estimates concern membership in a composite profile. The study does not give a visual hallucination effect estimate for each exposure, nor does it investigate common fig. [DUPARC protocol](../Source-Text/PMC7293131.txt) · [DUPARC registration](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT04180865.txt) · [Annonaceae original](../Source-Text/2022-Cleret-de-Langavant-Annonaceae-Cognition.txt).

The later unusual sensory experiences survey used separate questionnaire items and a one-month reporting period. Its trigger and duration questions preserve symptom nuance without an itemized diet analysis. The historical source gives a different type of evidence and explicitly separates the fresh harvest from year-round dried preparations.
