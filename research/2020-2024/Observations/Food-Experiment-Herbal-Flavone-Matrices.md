# Herbal flavone preparations and absorption

Earlier celery-leaf and parsley studies measured plasma apigenin after hydrolysis. The newer feeding work separately identifies circulating metabolites and reports a four-hour peak for apigenin-4-prime-glucuronide after a parsley drink, six hours for parsley with yogurt and two hours for chamomile tea. Preparation and the compounds present in the administered foods varied. The newer data refine the exposure question and leave the raw celery-stalk dose, its comparability to the earlier whole-plant solvent extract in mice, and the human rest-tremor effect unresolved.
