# Constituent models and clinical fatigue measures

The 2021 perimenopause trial reports an improvement within its saffron extract group on the SF-36 energy/fatigue scale. Comparative change over time was inconclusive. That contrasts with the separately studied whole-powder comparison and clinical fatigue scale in Parkinson disease in the next period. Delivery, population and outcome definitions matter when judging whether the two observations corroborate each other.

The 2024 EGCG mouse study used rotarod and suspension latency. The equipment label includes fatigue and does not identify a clinical fatigue measure. The 2022 ginger review links gut symptoms to animal tissue and cellular outcomes, and describes a lack of prevention of post-levodopa abnormal movements with one isolated constituent. These distinct endpoints remain available for comparison with the proposed human studies.
