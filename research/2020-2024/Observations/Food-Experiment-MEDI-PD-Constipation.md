# Whole diet counseling and constipation

MEDI-PD randomized 45 people and analyzed 36 after nine did not receive the assigned intervention. The earlier registry and protocol define constipation as a clinical outcome. The later group comparison is inconclusive, even though both arms improved. The supplement adds whole-diet nutrient estimates and anxiety measures. The trial leaves the individual contributions of olive oil, grain, fiber and other foods unresolved. [Registry and Results](../Food-Experiment-MEDI-PD-Constipation.md)
