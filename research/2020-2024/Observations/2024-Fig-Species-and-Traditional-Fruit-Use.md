# Common fig identity and traditional preparation

The 2024 interviews in Sudhnoti distinguish an edible or laxative common-fig fruit decoction from uses of three other Ficus species. The recorded preparation and botanical voucher make it possible to check which fruit entry the use belongs to. The study provides no clinical tremor result. [2024 survey](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2024-PMID39333361-Sudhnoti-Fig-Edible-and-Laxative-Use.txt)

Earlier descriptions use different source boundaries. The Cazorla field study documents fresh and dried preparations separately. The Bangladesh laboratory article attributes paralysis use to sacred fig, while the 2014 review lists that citation under common fig. The botanical identity, preparation and symptom all need independent verification.
