# Parkinson symptom assessments in the original 2023 reports

The 2013 Grossi paper used separate clinical and executive assessments for apathy and anxiety. The 2023 10/66 analysis separates apathy from depression using a caregiver or informant questionnaire in people with parkinsonism and dementia. The interval exercise trial measured mood and listed apathy as a possible modifier for future research. These measures answer different questions about motivation, emotion and functioning. [Grossi original](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID23242363-Apathy-Executive-Tests-and-Anxiety.txt) · [10/66 apathy](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37427840-Parkinsonism-Apathy-and-Caregiver-Scales.txt) · [HIIT crossover](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37371330-Exercise-Crossover-and-Outcome-Scales.txt).

The Care4PD questionnaire groups gait difficulty and falls as a reason for concern. It does not give fall rates after an exposure. The PLA2G6 series counts presenting hand tremor. The deep brain stimulation case documents resting-tremor history and a motor scale total, with a procedure-related question. [Care4PD survey](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37239208-Care4PD-Bothersome-Symptom-Survey.txt) · [PLA2G6 series](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37403138-PLA2G6-Phenotype-and-Hand-Tremor.txt) · [Deep brain stimulation case](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37458360-DBS-Rubrospinal-Activation-Case.txt).

The manometry series records whether abdominal pain was present. The neurogastroenterology review distinguishes upper abdominal discomfort and pain from defecatory dysfunction. Japanese insurance claims characterize prescription and diagnosis patterns. The genetic differential diagnosis cohort covers clinical manifestations of adult leukoencephalopathies. The 2023 CHAMPION protocol and 2026 result compare anesthesia methods. They offer no observation of pain under a food exposure. [Manometry series](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37476327-Manometry-and-Abdominal-Pain-Presence.txt) · [Neurogastroenterology review](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37258277-Parkinson-GI-Symptom-Contexts.txt) · [Japanese claims](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37426443-Japanese-Claims-and-Prescribing.txt) · [Leukoencephalopathy cohort](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37237429-Leukoencephalopathy-Genetics-and-Phenotypes.txt) · [CHAMPION protocol](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37253497-CHAMPION-Anesthesia-Protocol.txt) · [CHAMPION results](../../2025-2029/Source-Text/2026-PMID42102350-CHAMPION-Anesthesia-Results.txt).

In the neck-stimulation cohort, the registry reports improved normal provoked-cough status. The swallowing sound index was unchanged and videofluoroscopic airway outcomes were inconclusive. These measurements describe separate functions in one study family.
