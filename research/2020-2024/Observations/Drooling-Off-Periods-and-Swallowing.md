# Drooling in off periods and in swallowing studies

The [COPARK cohort](../Source-Text/2020-PMID32880023-Rascol-COPARK-Saliva-and-Drooling.txt) used two instruments with different thresholds and denominators. In the paired group, the clinical item was higher in the off state. The [correction](../Source-Text/2020-DOI10.1007-s00702-020-02264-1-COPARK-Figure-Correction.txt) is required to read the three categories of Figure 2. The earlier conference abstract used different sample counts and some different estimates within the same research family.

The [Chahine report](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Personal-Reports/2020-PMID32258226-Chahine-Off-Period-Drooling-Reports.txt) documents drooling among the most bothersome off-period answers. The [Mantri companion analysis](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Personal-Reports/2021-PMID34322575-Mantri-Fox-Insight-Food-and-Off-Periods.txt) records both food-related triggers and food-based coping strategies for off periods in the same survey family. These qualitative categories have different denominators and do not identify a change in drooling after a food. The [Bonner interviews](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Personal-Reports/2020-PMID32757092-Bonner-GBA-Saliva-and-Swallowing-Reports.txt) keep dry mouth, saliva escape, chewing or swallowing, and eating or drinking impacts separate.

The [Dumican pilot](../Source-Text/2020-PMID32538868-Dumican-Swallow-Airway-Pilot.txt) records a saliva complaint in the enrollment screen and analyzes airway invasion at the level of swallows. Earlier work on divided attention measured drooling frequency and severity separately. Clinical saliva grades, reports of a symptom being bothersome, and instrumented swallow safety can occur together without measuring the same outcome. [Earlier sources and the divided-attention lineage](../../../experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food/followup/fig-drooling-priority-six-nov20-145/README.md)

No source here assigns or contrasts raw fig consumption. The observations can help identify endpoints for further evidence collection.
