# Dried fig composition and visual symptom measures

The 2019 USDA source identifies dried, uncooked figs and quantifies pantothenic acid. It gives a direct measurement of the food's composition. The 2021 and 2024 researchers measured pantothenic acid in postmortem brain tissue from separate samples. These records connect the named food to the measured nutrient. They do not establish that intake changed tissue levels or visual hallucination frequency. [2019 food composition](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2019-FDC174665-Dried-Fig-Pantothenic-Acid.txt) · [2021 Parkinson disease dementia study](../Source-Text/PMC8468190.txt) · [2024 dementia with Lewy bodies study](../Source-Text/PMC11307062.txt).

The 2024 dementia with Lewy bodies sample had lower mean levels in primary visual cortex. The earlier Parkinson disease dementia comparison found lower levels in three other regions and no clear difference in primary visual cortex. Both use tissue comparisons between people with a diagnosis and controls. Visual hallucinations enter the 2024 discussion as a possible explanation for the anatomical pattern. Neither paper gives individual dietary histories, an intake contrast, or the number of visual episodes by intake group. Retain the difference across diagnoses and follow the 2026 metabolomics review for primary human intake or symptom leads. [Original cross-diagnosis comparison](../Source-Text/PMC11307062.txt) · [Metabolomics review accession](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41712554/).

The 2020 EANM cardiac MIBG abstract and 2021 journal article share a 95-person mild cognitive impairment cohort. Recurrent visual hallucinations contribute to consensus diagnostic features. The full 2021 paper supplies results for a possible Lewy body subgroup and separates diagnostic performance from clinical history. A broader Parkinson psychosis imaging synthesis also has two forms. Its 2022 preprint and 2024 paper share a protocol, with the journal version extending the evidence base from 18 to 29 reports. Their imaging findings still mix hallucination and delusion contexts. [EANM congress](../Source-Text/PMC7498986.txt) · [Cardiac MIBG cohort](../Source-Text/PMC8205462.txt) · [Preprint](../Source-Text/2022-PPR588814-Parkinson-Psychosis-Imaging-Preprint.txt) · [Journal review](../Source-Text/PMC11538965.txt).

The earlier period records caregiver or clinician descriptions alongside visual-learning tasks. The 2024 ratings study adds a direct check on who reports a symptom. Agreement between patients and care partners was poor for the summary score and six of its nine items. EAN congress members separately study autonomic response to a liquid meal, visual PET findings, and visual task or resting EEG findings. The abstracts report different exposures and participants. Each distinction matters when looking for a longitudinal symptom measure with a known exposure.
