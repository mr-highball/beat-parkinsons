# Orange pomace outcomes and bowel habits before Parkinson diagnosis

The [2019 beverage study](../../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#orange-pomace-and-stool-frequency-outside-parkinsons-disease) reported more frequent and softer stools with orange pomace, alongside gas and bloating. The [2022 crossover](../Source-Text/2022-PMID36102329-Orange-Pomace-and-Bowel-Movement-Crossover.txt) compared processed pomace added to juice with the same juice in healthy adults. Weekly frequency was inconclusive. Its secondary product main effect for a week with a no-movement day favored the pomace period, although baseline imbalance, the inconclusive interaction and different p-values in prose and supplement limit interpretation.

The [2024 analysis](../Source-Text/2024-PMID39159603-Constipation-and-Incident-Parkinson-Cohort.txt) studies baseline bowel habits as a predictor of later Parkinson diagnosis. The cohort result provides symptom-history context and does not test whether food changes constipation severity in people with Parkinson disease. The [2026 intervention](../../2025-2029/Observations/2026-Citrus-Fibre-Blend-and-Constipation.md) gives a different kind of comparison in people with functional constipation.
