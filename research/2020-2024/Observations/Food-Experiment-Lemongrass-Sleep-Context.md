# Lemongrass bedtime use and measurements

The 1986 volunteer study measured reported sleep and dream recall after a dried-leaf drink and placebo. The later Grande-Terre survey records residents using a leaf infusion at bedtime for a combined stress or insomnia category. It describes customary use, with no measured change in either condition.
