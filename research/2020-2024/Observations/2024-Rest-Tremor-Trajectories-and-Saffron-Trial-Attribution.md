# Rest-tremor trajectories and the saffron add-on contrast

The [2024 Parkinson imaging cohort](../Source-Text/2024-PMID39557871-Parkinson-Rest-Tremor-and-Caudate-Imaging.txt) defines rest-tremor subgroups across two years. The [action-tremor trajectory study](../Source-Text/2024-PMID39679819-Parkinson-Action-and-Rest-Tremor-Trajectories.txt) reports a clearer longitudinal change in action tremor. These measures complement the [earlier clinical descriptions](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/PMC4240284.txt). None assigned raw fig. The [Cazorla record](../../2000-2004/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2000-Cazorla-Fresh-and-Dried-Fig-Practices.txt) supplies food-preparation history.

The [2024 curcumin review](../Source-Text/2024-PMID39555102-Curcumin-Review-and-Saffron-Combination-Trial.txt) cites the [2016 randomized source](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27723543-Curcumin-Saffron-and-Depression-Contrasts.txt). Its reported p=.031 compares pooled active arms with placebo. Its saffron add-on comparison at the same curcumin dose gives p=.766 on depression severity. The earlier [Bacopa combination pilot](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-Cicero-Saffron-Combination-Cognition-and-Depression.txt) uses a different mixture and a different population. Both highlight the need for the appropriate arm contrast before attributing change to saffron.
