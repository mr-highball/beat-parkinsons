# Curcumin delivery and coffee outcome boundaries

The [2020 original](../Source-Text/2020-PMID32782446-Injectable-Curcumin-and-Thermal-Response.txt) compares a local hydrogel with carrier and untreated conditions in an animal neuropathic pain setting. Its finding belongs to the thermal withdrawal assay. The [2016 study](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27260322-Oral-and-Topical-Curcumin-Osteoarthritis-Pain.txt) reported different results for oral and topical formulations, with no clear oral pain effect. These preparations and outcomes give context for the ground-turmeric rating.

The [2023 polysomnography study](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37229165-Coffee-Black-Tea-and-Objective-Sleep-Bruxism.txt) reports more bruxism episodes among coffee drinkers in a referred sample. The [Sister Study](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37334620-Sister-Study-Parkinson-Ascertainment-Coffee-and-Fatigue.txt) measures coffee and diagnostic risk and describes fatigue separately. Compare the [2019 experience](../../2015-2019/Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2019-Parkinsons-UK-Coffee-and-Persistent-Fatigue.txt), which reports continued tiredness after coffee without a clinical comparison. Measure and setting matter before transferring any signal to Parkinson fatigue.
