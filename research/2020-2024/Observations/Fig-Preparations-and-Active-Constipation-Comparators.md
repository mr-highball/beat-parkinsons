# Fig preparations and active constipation comparators

The 2022 older-adult walnut–fig syrup trial found an inconclusive response difference compared with lactulose. The mixture arms cannot identify a fig-specific effect. The registry and article describe different treatment durations, so the result record and protocol remain linked at their verified scope. [Published trial](../Source-Text/2022-Joudikhajeh-Walnut-Fig-Syrup-Active-Comparison.txt) · [2020 registration](../Source-Text/Trials/IRCT20200714048108N1.txt).

The preceding period contains the 2016 controlled paste study and a separate flaxseed–fig caplet pilot. The paste trial compared against placebo, while the open caplet study used a baseline comparison. The pattern of results cannot establish which preparation is better. In the earlier 20-patient abstract, the fig arm improved from baseline and the abstract states the defecation outcomes did not differ significantly from active fiber. These observations answer different questions about preparation and comparator. [2016 paste trial](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID27440682-Fig-Paste-and-Functional-Constipation.txt) · [Flaxseed–fig pilot](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-Tofighi-Flaxseed-Fig-Caplet-Pilot.txt) · [Original fiber-comparison abstract](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2010-Kim-FASEB-Ficus-Fiber-Comparisons-Abstract.txt).

Earlier animal paste and dried-fruit extract results concern different endpoints. The canine experiment uses fixed successive periods. The rat abstract and isolated-tissue study have incomplete body verification. The earlier fieldwork records a mixed respiratory decoction and topical or veterinary uses, with a neighboring mallow laxative record. The 2022 trial excluded Parkinson disease, and the clinical comparators supply no estimate of Parkinson visual symptoms.
