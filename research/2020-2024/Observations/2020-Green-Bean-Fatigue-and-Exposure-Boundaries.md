# 2020 green beans, fatigue and assigned exposure

The earlier Parkinson nutrition reviews listed prebiotic food sources without individual symptom estimates. The Nurses’ Health Study cohort separates the fatigue component from the wider frailty composite and quantifies it by total vegetable intake. String beans appear in questionnaire validation. The exact cooked-bean comparison remains open because the main body gives a pooled estimate and the supplemental tables were unavailable. The 2019 Parkinson model used orange odor in a sensory task separately from bowel measurements. The newer clinical constipation trial used an orange-flavored probiotic product. One tests a sensory stimulus and the other assigns a probiotic formulation. Both leave the raw orange comparison unresolved.
