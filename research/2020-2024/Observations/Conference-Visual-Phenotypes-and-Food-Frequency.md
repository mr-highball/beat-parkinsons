# Clinical definitions and separate conference findings

Two complete congress collections illustrate why publication-level keyword matches need member-level inspection. The psychiatry collection includes a nutmeg adverse-use case, a dementia-with-Lewy-bodies visual case and a separate review of electrical treatment. The movement disorders collection includes distinct medication studies of amantadine, inhaled levodopa and pimavanserin, and independent inherited-neurodegeneration cases. The study designs distinguish adverse-event occurrence, global severity and visual symptoms. [Psychiatry collection](../Source-Text/PMC13070663.txt) · [Movement disorders collection](../Source-Text/PMC7017830.txt) · [Nutmeg case](../Source-Text/2020-EPA-Nutmeg-Mania-and-Insomnia.txt).

The preceding period includes the structural insular review and the Lewy body family-history cohort. Both were released electronically in 2019 and later assigned to 2020 issues. Subsequent work separates clinical visual symptom presence at diagnosis from time to dementia, and the later nested study uses a structured frequency measure. [2019 insular review](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC7648006.txt) · [2019 family history study](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC7029358.txt) · [Prodromal cohort](../Source-Text/PMC7317511.txt) · [Nested visual frequency study](../Source-Text/2021-PMID34238577-Parkinson-Visual-Phenomenology.txt).

The microbiome comparison gives food-frequency categories for 17 foods and beverages. It measures fecal bacterial taxa and describes visual hallucinations in disease background. Its food list supplies no dried fig measure. The newer imaging preprint groups several spontaneous hallucination modalities and also measures induced sensed presence. Those groupings and the pantothenic-acid review's disease-status findings do not provide a dried fig or counted visual episode effect.
