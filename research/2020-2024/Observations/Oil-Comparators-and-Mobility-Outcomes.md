# Oil comparators and mobility outcomes

The [NUTRIMAL main report](../Source-Text/2021-PMID33871558-NUTRIMAL-Gait-Balance-and-Mixed-Oil.txt) supplies prespecified clinical comparisons and exploratory gait speed. Its intervals for gait and standing balance span zero. The control and protein-only drinks both contained high-oleic sunflower and corn oil. The [interindividual report](../Source-Text/2022-PMID35191215-NUTRIMAL-Individual-Response.txt) and [oxylipin report](../Source-Text/2023-PMID36645979-NUTRIMAL-Oxylipins-and-Mobility.txt) belong to the same trial family.

The [COPD report and publisher supplement](../Source-Text/2024-PMID39181037-COPD-Corn-Soy-Capsule-Comparison.txt) identify a different carrier mixture with soybean and corn oil in equal proportions. Its depression contrasts have opposing point estimates and wide intervals. The accessible material names gait and postural balance tests, and group-specific estimates remain behind the main article access route.

The [previous-period ENRGISE report](../../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Corn-Oil-Comparator-Trails.md#enrgise-walking-speed-and-fall-events) directly lists rare serious fall events and uses a corn-oil capsule. The newer studies give more details on mixtures and functional testing. The distinctions matter when assessing a named oil, refining grade, and recurrent Parkinson falls.
