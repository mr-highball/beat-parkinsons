# Date-palm parts and pain outcomes

The 2015 whole-date crossover provides the closest human food preparation and a pain diary, with a 0.03-point unadjusted separation between the reported period means and no paired interval. In 2016, researchers used ethanol extracts from dried ripe fruits and measured chemically and thermally evoked behaviors. These findings concern different outcomes and routes. The reduced activity in the extract groups gives a second explanation for delayed movements.

The 2024 Moroccan interview survey reports local date-palm use for perceived immune support. Its historical headache reference has a separate lineage and supplies no observed effect size. Topical bergamot in dementia is a different exposure and route, even though an observer-rated pain item was measured. Keep those outcomes and customary reports available in their respective source records.
