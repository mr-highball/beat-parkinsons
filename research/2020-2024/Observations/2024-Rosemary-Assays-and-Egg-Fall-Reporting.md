# Rosemary bowel assays and reporting of falls

The [egg-white pilot](../Source-Text/2021-PMID34730461-Egg-White-Protein-and-Physical-Function.txt) follows an [earlier registry](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT03530774.txt) that listed falls. The author abstract reports strength and function without the registered fall contrast. A [recipe and spice-packet trial](../Source-Text/PMC8145475.txt) assessed physical performance in a separate sample.

The [rosemary preprint](../Source-Text/2024-PMID38801915-Rosemary-Wet-Stool-and-Transit.txt) is a distinct line of evidence from the [earlier Parkinson massage feasibility trial](../../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Rosemary-Lemon-Balm.md#abdominal-massage-and-constipation-in-people-with-parkinson-disease). It tested a leaf extract, with oral wet-stool and injected diarrhea-model results under different conditions. Neither study identifies a food preparation that reproduces the experimental effect.
