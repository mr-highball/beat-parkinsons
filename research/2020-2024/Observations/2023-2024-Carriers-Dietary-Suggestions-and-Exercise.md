# Clinical carriers, dietary suggestions and exercise evidence

The [small Parkinson formulation trial](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37476317-Parkinson-Cannabinoid-Olive-Carrier-and-Adverse-Events.txt) records one constipation event and one anxiety event in a three-person active arm. All arms contained an olive oil vehicle. Earlier [Crohn trial records](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-PMID28349233-Cannabidiol-Olive-Oil-and-Crohn-Activity.txt) also hold olive oil across comparisons. Conditions and interventions differ. Neither isolates the effect of an olive oil grade or gives an attributable clinical symptom contrast from eating it.

The [Parkinson autonomic review](../Source-Text/2024-PMID37694308-Parkinson-Autonomic-Review-Olive-Oil-and-Kefir.txt) labels its extra-virgin olive oil advice as untested and names kefir in the broader discussion of probiotic foods. The [exercise-soreness synthesis](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37408367-Curcumin-Exercise-Soreness-and-Strength-Meta-Analysis.txt) concerns formulated supplements. Its individual results are preserved in the [earlier-period observations](../../2015-2019/Observations/2015-2019-Curcumin-Exercise-Timing-and-Soreness.md).
