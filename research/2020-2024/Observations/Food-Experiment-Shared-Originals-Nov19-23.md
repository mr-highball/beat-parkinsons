# Food sources across veterinary, cellular and animal model reports

The veterinary chapter records food-related descriptions for specific animal species and plant parts. Dog and cat hazards, seed versus ripe flesh, and oil used as a drug solvent have distinct meanings. An actual food name or cross-reference identifies the material at most. It cannot join a different entry's symptom to that material.

The 2022 mouse study and its 2023 congress presentation report the same rotenone study family. Olive oil was shared with the vehicle control and was not an assigned oral preparation. The 2023 amyloid cell paper and 2024 spice-blend poster likewise represent one study family. In the same 2024 collection, olive leaf and purified oleuropein changed cell viability, while saffron tepals supplied different extracts from culinary ground stigma. These additional studies strengthen distinctions between actual ingredients, derived compounds and carriers. The earlier UEG comparisons recorded human digestive measures and procedure outcomes, with different exposure routes and entirely separate cohorts.
