# Mangiferin across different behavioral models

The [2022 Yan original](../Source-Text/2022-PMID35721155-Mangiferin-and-Postpartum-Model-Behavior.txt) reports favorable changes on selected immobility measures in postpartum-like mice. The paper also reports inconclusive differences for two hippocampal markers. The [2017 chronic stress study](../../2015-2019/Observations/2017-Mangiferin-and-Sucrose-Direction.md) uses sucrose consumption and locomotion alongside immobility measures. The observations concern independent models with a common isolated compound and distinct endpoints.

A [fresh versus dried mango crossover](../Food-Experiment-Mango-Sleep-Fatigue.md#fresh-versus-dried-mango-with-glucose-and-satiety-outcomes) directly compares culinary exposures on glucose and appetite, leaving the clinical depressive-symptom outcome unmeasured.
