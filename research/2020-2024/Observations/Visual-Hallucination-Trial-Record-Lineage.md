# Visual hallucination trial record lineage

TOP HAT has a 2019 ClinicalTrials.gov record and a 2020 ISRCTN registration. The July 2026 ISRCTN registry snapshot lists the study as ongoing with planned completion in July 2028 and no result. A published blinded analysis from the trial estimates a meaningful change on rating scales without identifying results by randomized arm. These sources describe separate record depths in the same trial family. [ISRCTN registration](../Source-Text/Trials/ISRCTN51996779.txt) · [NCT registration](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT04167813.txt) · [Blinded calibration analysis](../../2025-2029/Source-Text/PMC12080632.txt).

The 2016 apomorphine trial matches across the US and EU records. The EU public summary is marked ongoing, and the US entry is unknown. Neither registry has posted randomized results. Historical publications in the US record supply earlier clinical background, not post-registration arm results. Follow the original publication and other registry outcome routes separately.
