# Ginger preparations and tremor measurements

The [2021 rhizome water-extract report](../Source-Text/2021-PMID33494465-Ginger-Water-Extract-and-Experimental-Allodynia.txt) describes short-lived changes in provoked hypersensitivity in mice. The [2022 constituent experiment](../Source-Text/2022-PMID35745645-Shogaol-and-Experimental-Allodynia.txt) gives favorable higher-dose and inconclusive lower-dose findings on related measures. The [later ginger essential-oil study](../Source-Text/2023-PMID38106635-Ginger-Essential-Oil-Myelin-and-Traditional-Citations.txt) instead measures myelin and time to fall from a rotating rod. Its opening discussion points to customary pain uses. This distinction prevents treating the rod result as pain relief.

The [earlier human trials](../../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Ginger-Pain-Origins.md) include favorable exercise soreness findings and a powdered-ginger knee trial with no clear benefit. The new animal findings add preparation and outcome context. They give no fresh grated ginger comparison in Parkinson pain.

The [Parkinson tremor cohort](../Source-Text/2023-PMID37982119-Parkinson-Rest-and-Postural-Tremor-Clinical-Comparators.txt) reports separate rest and postural tremor subscores in clinical treatment comparisons. The generic tremor keyword is insufficient to link food with rest tremor.
