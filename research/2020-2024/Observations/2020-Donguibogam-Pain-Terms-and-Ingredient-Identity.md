# Turmeric and botanical identity in a Donguibogam analysis

A 2020 secondary study links Curcuma longa rhizome to a pain-related skincare term in a historical Korean medical text. The setting, mixed prescriptions and lack of patient-level response differ from the frozen oral culinary turmeric questions. The separate Asparagus cochinchinensis record describes a different species from culinary asparagus.

The original term association can be tagged as cultural context for pain. It contributes no additional independent clinical comparison alongside the previous period and its formulation- and endpoint-specific turmeric findings.
