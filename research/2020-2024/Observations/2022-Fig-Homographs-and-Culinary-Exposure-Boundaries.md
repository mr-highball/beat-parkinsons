# Fig homographs and culinary exposure boundaries

Four original reports examined Parkinson gut or motor features or compared tremor measurement. Their references to figures produced apparent fig results. None documents raw Ficus carica intake. The [2023 Persian discussion](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2023-Persian-Public-Fig-and-Tremor-Suggestions.txt) discusses actual figs, alongside other ingredients, without an observed rest tremor outcome. The [earlier 2019 Parkinson community discussion](../../2015-2019/Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2019-Parkinsons-UK-Constipation-Prunes-Figs-Dates-Flaxseed.txt) concerns bowel symptoms. The [later Chitrakoot record](../../2025-2029/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2026-PMID42613621-Chitrakoot-Fig-Fruit-and-Constipation.txt) documents another bowel-use context without individual response.

The [2012 multi-oil tests](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-PMID22662127-Combined-Oil-Diet-and-Mouse-Memory.txt) kept high-oleic sunflower oil in both comparison diets. The [corn oil](../Source-Text/2021-PMID34076067-Cannabidiol-Corn-Oil-Vehicle-and-Emotion-Recognition.txt) and [sesame oil](../Source-Text/2020-PMID32634571-Cannabidiol-Formulations-and-Pharmacokinetics.txt) formulation trials introduced those oils as drug vehicles. Their endpoints give no isolated culinary oil effect.
