# Lemon-balm preparation and behavioral outcomes

The 2015–2019 reports separated warm-water concentrate, dry extract and infusions, with different outcomes and cohorts. The separate Melissa arm in a chronic stress model was inconclusive even though the comparison blend without Melissa showed an effect. Serbian interviews from that period recorded both perceived relief and no change during co-use. Neither interview table isolates Parkinson depression.

In this period, blinded mouse behavioral assays favored two groups receiving a hydroalcoholic extract, with a conflicting middle-dose label. A different experiment found opposing sucrose-preference results under different diets after injected extract. The later report from the Antistress project tests serum cytokines, and the chronic groups may overlap the prior behavioral work. The Niš ethnobotanical survey confirms dried-leaf tea as a reported community practice and gives intended uses rather than a symptom trajectory. The studies refine what to search for without establishing an effect in the frozen population.
