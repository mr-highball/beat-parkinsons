# Cantaloupe preparation and resting tremor

The 2013–2014 case-control family adds a cucurbit frequency estimate for Parkinson occurrence, with cantaloupe pooled with three other foods. The 2020 diet study records cantaloupe served to people diagnosed with Parkinson disease. It advances exposure documentation from recalled grouped intake to a named item in a shared menu. Its supplement reports composite motor change, leaving the cantaloupe comparison and the resting-tremor item open. The 2020–2024 traditional sources document two historical treatment contexts without a melon-specific observed tremor response.
