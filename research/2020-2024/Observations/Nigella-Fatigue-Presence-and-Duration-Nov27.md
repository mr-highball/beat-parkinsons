# Nigella Fatigue Presence and Duration

The open-label 2021 oil trial randomized 183 (91 oil and 92 usual care). Table 2 reports 6.3 ± 4.1 days of lethargy or fatigue in 62 participants with that symptom versus 5.4 ± 3.2 days in 56 controls with the symptom, p=.18. The numerical duration was longer with oil. The estimate is inconclusive and covers a symptomatic subset during acute illness. The open-label 2022 seed-oil softgel comparison reports day-four fatigue in 12/30 versus 25/30, adjusted OR 0.15 (95% CI 0.04–0.48). Its endpoint is fatigue presence on a particular day. The acute-illness setting, formulation and reporting limit transfer to ground culinary nigella and Parkinson fatigue severity. The older acute trial and the later acute trial report different outcomes and participant sets. This explains why the numerical directions cannot be pooled.
