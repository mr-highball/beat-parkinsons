# Cardamom formulation and depression comparisons

The original randomized tulsi–cardamom report supplies an older-adult between-group post-treatment comparison missing from the abstract. Its intervention and active comparator differ in multiple components. The earlier Crohn trial gives cardamom to both arms, and the newer oil study measures animal immobility alongside changes in locomotion. Each original can support an observation specific to its own design.

A favorable result for the blend keeps its clinical relevance as context. No retrieved comparison isolates ground cardamom and depressive symptom severity in Parkinson disease.
