# Medicinal citrus and raw orange

Earlier human studies describe a blended fruit drink with physical therapy and pasteurized juice products. The later Parkinson record study adds clinical population context and documents medicinal citrus prescription frequencies. It gives no orange consumption or constipation change. Together the studies leave a gap between the raw fruit exposure and the clinical outcome.
