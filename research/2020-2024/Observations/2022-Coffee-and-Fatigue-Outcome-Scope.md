# Coffee illustrations and measured fatigue outcomes

The 2022 editorial uses an everyday coffee reference to introduce the difference between tiredness and disabling fatigue. The article does not report an intervention or participant-level coffee comparison. Its publication date reflects the publisher's first release in July 2022.

The 2020 vegetable cohort measures fatigue as one component of frailty and separately reports a total-vegetable estimate for that component. The article's string-bean entry describes food-frequency validation. Whether the supplementary analyses isolate string beans still requires checking the actual tables.
