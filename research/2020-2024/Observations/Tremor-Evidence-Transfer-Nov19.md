# Tremor endpoints in models and cooking-oil surveys

In the 2020 mouse experiment the grade of the olive oil was described and the administration route was injection. Tests report coordination, movement and strength. The subsequent butylphthalide comparisons use a drug, including in a different genetic model, and score other motor functions. The 2023 dietary case-control result concerns presence of Parkinson disease. Keeping source type, food preparation and exact outcomes separate supports the review of minimal directional signals.
