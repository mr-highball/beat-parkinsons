# Green-bean fatigue and trial outcome availability

The 2020 frailty cohort reports a lower pooled vegetable fatigue-component incidence. Its longer-term population measure differs from the previous period's pediatric green-vegetable advice and questionnaire measures. The new population check confirms Parkinson disease is one of several self-reported diagnoses. Separate exposure and subgroup estimates remain unavailable. [Original cohort](../Source-Text/2020-PMID33022693-Green-Bean-Questionnaire-and-Aggregated-Frailty-Fatigue.txt)

The 2021 nigella preprint records the planned HADS comparison and the 2022 outcomes paper reports metabolic findings. The repeated publications identify one trial. The earlier crushed-seed mood study used a different measurement. Comparison across preparations and populations remains open.
