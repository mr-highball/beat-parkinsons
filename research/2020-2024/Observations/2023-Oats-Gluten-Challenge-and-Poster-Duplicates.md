# Oats and duplicate conference reports in 2021–2023

The 2021 UEG report and the 2023 journal article report matching celiac presentation data and the same 74 patients in the gluten challenge. Habitual gluten-free oat use was less frequent among patients reporting acute nausea or vomiting. The comparison is observational. The authors discuss both increased tolerance and avoidance by people with symptoms. The result is counted once across the poster and full article. The 2021 Nutrition and UEG abstracts also repeat onion and other dietary comparisons in liver disease. The UEG and EHMSG green-tea reports repeat the same randomized comparison. Matching the exposure, population, sample and exact outcomes controls publication duplication in Oracle.
