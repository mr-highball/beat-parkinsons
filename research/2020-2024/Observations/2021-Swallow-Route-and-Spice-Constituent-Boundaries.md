# Swallow testing and spice constituents across periods

The [2006 black pepper oil trial](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-Ebihara-Baseline-Olfaction-Extract.txt) observes reflex latency after inhalation. The [separate menthol original](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-PMC1885135-Ebihara-Menthol-Swallow-Reflex.txt) directly tests the reflex under pharyngeal stimulation. The [2013 piperine trial](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID24326980-Oral-Piperine-and-Swallowing-Physiology.txt) instead measures an oral nectar formulation, with an inconclusive lower-concentration arm containing the neurodegenerative participants. The [2021 food chemistry study](../Source-Text/2021-PMID33732511-Lee-Piperine-in-Pepper-and-Food-Matrices.txt) adds measurements in commercial food matrices but no data tying food particles or contact concentration to the reflex. Do not reuse the pooled class estimate as a separate data point for pepper.

The [current audit](../../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Round-1-Black-Pepper-Eugenol-and-Clinical-Nov24.md) also traces earlier eugenol studies. They differ by administration, measured behaviors and motor effects. A review that names several compound sources cannot apportion the outcomes among culinary ingredients.
