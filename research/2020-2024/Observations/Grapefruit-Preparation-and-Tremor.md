# Grapefruit preparations and resting-tremor evidence

Earlier human comparisons in 2000 and 2002 isolated fresh fruit or pulp and measured changes in cardiovascular drug exposure. The 2005 Parkinson reports tested grapefruit juice with cabergoline and measured drug concentrations. Later cabergoline studies measured clinical motor outcomes after cabergoline treatment or a separate drug interaction. Those results leave the route, preparation and clinical outcome as separate questions. [Fresh pulp](../../2000-2004/Source-Text/2002-PMID12040751-Grapefruit-Pulp-and-Calcium-Antagonists.txt) · [Juice figure](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-Nagai-Nomoto-Cabergoline-Figure4-Extract.txt) · [Resting-tremor pilot](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2008-PMID19092251-Cabergoline-Resting-Tremor-Pilot.txt)

The 2014 and 2015 naringin studies examined isolated constituent effects in different animal models. The 2015 study reports early tissue preservation alongside limited evidence of reversing established injury. The preceding period also includes a planned nasal aroma crossover with no posted comparison. In 2023, oral limonin and injected nootkatone experiments report different motor proxies. The underlying tissue and motor outcomes help frame questions for whole-fruit research. Each study's dose, timing and outcome sets the limits of that comparison.
