# Coffee, fig and clinical comparison limits

The [community study](../Source-Text/2022-PMID36352842-Parkinson-Community-Coffee-and-Fatigability.txt) counts both confirmed cases as easily fatigued and one as a coffee drinker. It cites the [earlier measured fatigue comparison](../../2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID24868347-Filipino-Parkinson-Fatigue-Measurement.txt) for symptom context. The [2019 discussion](../../2015-2019/Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2019-Parkinsons-UK-Coffee-and-Persistent-Fatigue.txt) is separately attributable to public reporters and includes persistent fatigue and a caregiver's perception of mobility with medication.

The [multiple system atrophy cohort](../Source-Text/2022-PMID36397048-Multiple-System-Atrophy-Longitudinal-Motor-and-Nonmotor.txt) includes a rest tremor measure without a raw fig comparison. The [2019 fig discussion](../../2015-2019/Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations/2019-Parkinsons-UK-Constipation-Prunes-Figs-Dates-Flaxseed.txt) documents a different symptom context. The other pinned Parkinson clinical and model sources have the same absence of measured raw fig exposure. The isolated-curcumin pole result is a separate preparation and outcome proxy [recorded in its own source](../Source-Text/2022-PMID36678536-Curcumin-and-Timed-Locomotion-in-Parkinson-Model.txt).
