# Visual endpoints and magnesium citations

The 2023 hypomagnesemia review describes hallucinations in the cognitive impairment discussion. For the patients selected for its analysis, the inclusion rules required a motor or ocular-motor abnormality that improved after correction. This design cannot estimate how often hallucinations resolve. The original 2020 case records nighttime hallucinations during a relapse, but no sensory modality or separate trajectory. The original 2010 teaching case describes hallucinations during delirium and overall improvement after intravenous replacement, without specifying whether the experiences were visual. The 1960 case text remains unavailable through the public publisher preview. [Review and citation scope](../Source-Text/PMC10547470.txt) · [2020 original case](../Source-Text/PMC6950808.txt) · [2010 case and recovery](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/2010-PMID20493607-Regolisti-Magnesium-and-Delirium.txt) · [1960 access record](../../1960-1964/Source-Text/1960-PMID13840893-Magnesium-Deficiency-Tetany-Access.txt).

These cases add context to the earlier archived 2011, 2023 and 2024 patient timelines. Acute low serum magnesium, repeated symptoms during illness, and symptom recovery around co-treatment are separate observations. The USDA dried fig entry establishes the presence of magnesium in the food. The clinical cases neither fed that food nor counted visual episodes in people with Parkinson disease. [Earlier case timelines and food analysis](Dried-Fig-Magnesium-and-Visual-Symptom-Timing.md).

The 2017 randomized basal forebrain stimulation trial reports exploratory changes on a broad symptom scale and individual visual hallucination observations. At 36 months the same cohort has differing caregiver-reported change, including fewer visual hallucinations in one participant, no change in another and higher hallucination scores in two. The long-term paper and its earlier review citation therefore do not provide independent confirmation of the initial trial result. Neither phase studies food. [Parent crossover trial](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC5838617.txt) · [Registry](../../2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/NCT01701544.txt) · [Same-cohort follow-up](../Source-Text/PMC9346244.txt).

The published TOP HAT method names the Scale for the Assessment of Positive Symptoms hallucination score, which includes more than a simple visual episode count. Its registry has no posted results. The 2022 review also describes early uncontrolled medication reports. A different congress poster presents oral BBP-671 effects on blood acetyl-CoA without administering pantothenic acid or treating Parkinson visual symptoms. The BBP-671 record separates healthy-volunteer observations from the trial's rare metabolic disease groups. [Ondansetron review](../Source-Text/PMC8882165.txt) · [TOP HAT registry](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT04167813.txt) · [BBP-671 congress member](../Source-Text/PMC9134814.txt) · [BBP-671 registration](../Source-Text/Trials/NCT04836494.txt).

The 2022 plasma analysis records visual symptom scores among Lewy body diagnostic groups. Its pTau181 measurements are explicitly reused in the later plasma paper. Qualifying visual symptoms, repeated scores and symptom onset should remain distinct from an ingested-exposure response. [Earlier pTau181 cohort](../Source-Text/PMC9540809.txt) · [Later plasma panel](../Source-Text/PMC10755229.txt).
