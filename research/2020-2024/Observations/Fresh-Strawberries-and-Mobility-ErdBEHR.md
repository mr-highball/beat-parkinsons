# Fresh strawberries and mobility in ErdBEHR

The [2022 registration](../Source-Text/Trials/DRKS00026998.txt) and the [2024 main report](../Source-Text/2024-Hartmann-ErdBEHR-Strawberry-and-Chair-Rise.txt) document an actual fresh-strawberry comparison. The 30-second chair-rise test improved across groups. The low- and high-dose fresh-only groups had similar model estimates, and the overall group-by-visit test did not identify different changes. All groups consumed strawberries. The primary outcomes were clinical biomarkers, and the cohort had neither a Parkinson-specific enrollment design nor prospective fall counting.

The [2018 older-women cohort](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID30153877-Sim-Fruit-Injurious-Falls.txt) has a clinically consequential fall admission endpoint but groups seven fruits together in its relevant estimate. ErdBEHR isolates different whole-fruit amounts in two arms and uses a different endpoint. The two sources leave the strawberry-specific Parkinson falls question open.

The [2025 microbiome paper](../../2025-2029/Source-Text/2025-PMID39772703-ErdBEHR-Strawberry-Microbiome.txt) analyzes a subset of the same trial on separate endpoints. The registration and both publications count as one intervention family.
