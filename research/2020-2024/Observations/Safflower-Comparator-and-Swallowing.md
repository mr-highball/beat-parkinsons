# Safflower comparator and swallowing in 2020–2024

The prior period established which Huntington trial used safflower and which planned swallowing testing. TRIHEP3 later reported adjusted Huntington total motor and caudate changes without a specific swallowing or dystonia result. The Glut1 crossover reports episodic movement counts, with dystonia in the baseline table and no separate dystonia change. [Prior period](../../2015-2019/Observations/Safflower-Comparator-and-Swallowing.md) · [TRIHEP3 paper](../Source-Text/2024-PMID39804569-TRIHEP3-Safflower-Comparator.txt) · [Glut1 paper](../Source-Text/2024-PMID38725190-Glut1-Safflower-Crossover.txt).

The ALS and singer trials bring the comparison closer to swallowing through instruments containing an item about it. The ALS full paper confirms the decline in both groups on the total function scale. It gives only baseline bulbar onset and a separate progression composite containing tube feeding. The singer reflux totals moved in different directions across two reports. Arm-level item changes remain unavailable. That combination leaves both directions open for the specific Parkinson question. The central Syrian survey adds documented traditional preparations and indications without a swallowing observation.
