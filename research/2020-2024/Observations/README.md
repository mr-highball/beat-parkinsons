## Major observations

- Seed amplification and research staging introduced biological measures alongside clinical diagnoses. Assay positivity varied across Parkinson’s subgroups. [Clinical and diagnosis](../Clinical-and-Diagnosis.md)
- Phase 3 inosine increased urate but did not slow measured progression and increased kidney stones. Isradipine also missed its progression endpoint. Early antibody trials missed their primary endpoints, while phase 2 lixisenatide showed a motor signal with frequent gastrointestinal adverse effects. [Drugs and remedies](../Drugs-and-Remedies.md)
- Focused ultrasound, continuous subcutaneous levodopa delivery and adaptive stimulation had different control groups, sizes, and adverse event profiles. These studies address symptom management. [Procedures and devices](../Procedures-and-Devices.md)
