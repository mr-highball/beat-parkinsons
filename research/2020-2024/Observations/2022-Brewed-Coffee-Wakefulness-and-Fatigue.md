# Brewed coffee, wakefulness and measured fatigue

The [2022 British Museum account](../Cultural-and-Experiential/Ceremonial-Accounts/2022-British-Museum-Yemeni-Coffee-and-Devotional-Wakefulness.txt) documents a historical interpretation of brewed coffee during Yemeni Sufi dhikr. It describes extended wakefulness without participant-level fatigue measures. The [2018 academic review](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMID29417473-Coffee-Cacao-Research-and-Ceremonial-Wakefulness.txt) repeats the broader cultural lineage. Read alongside the [2018 coffee crossover](../../2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC6213082.txt) for a contemporary controlled fatigue comparison in a different population. The uninspected early text and reported date of the practice remain distinct from the date of this museum publication.
