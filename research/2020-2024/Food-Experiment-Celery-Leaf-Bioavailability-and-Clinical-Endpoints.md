# Reported outcomes during butylphthalide treatment

## Conference results from the likely matching cognition trial

Han and colleagues’ 2024 congress report describes 287 participants at ten centers, randomized to pharmaceutical DL-3-n-butylphthalide 200 mg three times daily or matching placebo for 48 weeks. Participants received stable dopaminergic medication. In the intention-to-treat analysis, MoCA change was 1.74 versus 0.90 (p = .0059) and MMSE change was 1.00 versus 0.19 (p = .0001). The report summarizes treatment-related adverse events as uncommon and largely mild gastrointestinal symptoms. It does not present a dyskinesia event count, MDS-UPDRS Part IV score or item-level data. The design, eligibility, duration and senior investigator match ChiCTR1800017084, which registered Parts III and IV as secondary outcomes. The report omits the registry identifier. Keep the study link provisional and the secondary endpoint open for fuller results.
