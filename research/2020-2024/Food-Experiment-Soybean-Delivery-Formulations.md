# Soybean oil in experimental nutrient-delivery foods

## Soybean oil in pea protein and alginate beads

Food Hydrocolloids 154:110101, 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.foodhyd.2024.110101. The original abstract and publisher snippets describe soybean-oil-containing pea-protein and alginate gel beads containing two model nutrients. Raising alginate from 0.2% to 1.0% increased measured hardness from approximately 307 to 792 g. The experiment measured physical properties, structure and simulated digestive release. The suggested dysphagia application was untested in people in the accessible sections. No oil-only clinical comparison or verified refining grade was reported.
