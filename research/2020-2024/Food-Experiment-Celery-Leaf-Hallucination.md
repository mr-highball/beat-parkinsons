# Celery leaf and hallucinations

## 2022 Review of celery root case and hallucinations

Le and colleagues describe mania and hallucinations within 48 hours of celery-root capsules in the 2016 Khalid case. The patient was taking venlafaxine and St John's wort. The review reports improvement after stopping celery and leaves the hallucination modality unspecified. Its nearby description of visual hallucinations concerns a different ginseng and phenelzine case. The timing and hallucinations are review-derived. The accessible original abstract confirms mania and elevated venlafaxine, and the complete case remains inaccessible. [Review, section 3.1 and Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9881059/) · [Original case and source lineage](../2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-Khalid-Celery-Root-Mania-Extract.txt)

## Registered dried stalk-and-leaf comparison in diabetes

IRCT first registered the Mohsenpour trial March 1, 2021. The official record specifies stalk-and-leaf powder and metabolic outcomes. Its approved publication reports general weekly safety calls and 36 participants analyzed. The current registry entry leaves actual first enrollment blank, and the authors describe retrospective registration.
