# Celery leaf and hallucinations

## 2022 Review of celery root case and hallucinations

Le et al. 2022, PMID 34370637, PMCID PMC9881059, DOI 10.2174/1570159X19666210809100357, section 3.1 and Table 1, describe the 2016 Khalid case: a 52-year-old patient on venlafaxine and St John's wort developed mania and hallucinations within 48 hours of celery root capsules; the review says symptoms resolved on discontinuing celery. The review does not identify hallucination modality or report frequency. This is secondary description of a single case, root preparation and concurrent drugs; it is not Parkinson-specific evidence for dried leaf. The original publisher abstract was accessible but the full original case remained inaccessible.
