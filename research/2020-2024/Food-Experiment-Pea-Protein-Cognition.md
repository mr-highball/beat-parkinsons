# Pea protein breakfasts and cognitive outcome trails

## Pea-protein breakfast outcome report and open task results

The 2021 Griffith and Leidy conference abstract describes 37 participants and breakfast doses and comparators matching registered NCT03595436 methods. The original abstract provides appetite and food intake outcomes. It contains no executive-function result. Treat the publication as a likely companion pending full study linkage and preserve the cognitive-reporting trail.
