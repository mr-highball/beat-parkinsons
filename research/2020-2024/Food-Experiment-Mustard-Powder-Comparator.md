# Mustard seed powder in both arms of a clinical formulation

## Ground mustard in the comparator and intervention

The October 30, 2024 primary trial report describes nine participants with virally suppressed HIV. The randomized study assigned broccoli seed extract with mustard seed powder or a comparator with mustard seed powder and maltodextrin. Both sachets used mustard. Outcomes included inflammation and metabolic laboratory markers, with general adverse-event monitoring. The comparison provides an example of oral exposure in a clinical formulation, without a mustard-free control, Parkinson-specific participants or a visual hallucination frequency result.
