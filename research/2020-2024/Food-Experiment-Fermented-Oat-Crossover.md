# Registered fermented oat crossover

## Registered mental health composite

The University of Eastern Finland registered the study in NCT06393114 on May 1, 2024, after the reported January 22 start. The registry lists 57 enrolled and a completed study. It specifies mental health from the GHQ-12 as a secondary measure, without a dedicated insomnia measure. The November 15 registry snapshot has no posted results. The later original reports give the cohort data.
