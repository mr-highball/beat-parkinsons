# Spanish-language dietary research follow-up

## 2021 cracker, vanilla yogurt and water in a clinical swallowing test

Galende and Hidalgo studied seven adults aged up to 65 with Parkinson’s at Hoehn and Yahr stages 2–3 in San Luis, Argentina. The clinical swallowing assessment used a plain water cracker as the solid, vanilla yogurt as the semisolid and water as the liquid. Oral residue was observed in five of seven participants with the cracker, and in none with yogurt or water. The report describes difficulty with chewing and transport in three of seven with the cracker, and use of water to assist some participants with bolus preparation and swallowing. One participant showed an apparent blockage with the cracker. These were clinical observations with no instrumental confirmation of airway entry. The boluses differ in both texture and ingredients, and the study included no potato preparation. [Primary report, Methods and Figures 4–9](https://metavoces.unsl.edu.ar/docs/2021/Revista-Metavoces-N23-2021-Art%C3%ADculo%204.pdf#page=7)

The journal lists the article in issue 23 of 2021. Universidad Nacional de San Luis announced the issue’s launch on December 9, 2021. The PDF and issue page provide public reading access, with no eligible text reuse license located in the sources checked. ## 2020 reported coffee and MoCA in a Costa Rican clinic

Torrealba-Acosta et al. reported a clinical and genetic study of 118 Parkinson cases and 97 unrelated controls at Hospital San Juan de Dios. The preprint was public on September 30, 2020. The journal article appeared August 4, 2021 and retained the same coffee and cognition finding. Coffee data were collected only in the Parkinson group. Table 1 records coffee for 105 of 113 with available intake history. The report describes a typical two to three cups per day for more than 15 years, without specifying preparation. Multivariable regression associated reported coffee with lower Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA) scores. Supplementary Figure 2C labels the fitted coefficient −166.82339, SE 59.399964 and t = −2.81. The figure shows a transformed scale extending beyond the 0–30 MoCA score and does not specify how to convert the coefficient to raw points. The association is observational. Fatigue was not measured. ## Thickened contrast in the published Mataró cohort

Nascimento and colleagues’ 2020 paper reports fluoroscopy in 50 people with Parkinson disease and 12 controls. The liquids used water and Gastrografin, with a commercial starch thickener in the thicker conditions. Among the 40 people tested in both medication states, unsafe swallowing was reported for 10 OFF and 15 ON with thin liquid, 12 in each state with nectar viscosity, and one in each state with pudding viscosity. The results text gives 2.56% ON for the pudding condition, while Table 3 gives 2.50% for the same count. No aspiration was observed. The highest viscosity reduced penetration and increased some residue measures. The study used no potato and supplies no estimate for baked potato. Its overlapping 2019 abstract and likely overlapping 2017 dissertation provide the report chronology.
