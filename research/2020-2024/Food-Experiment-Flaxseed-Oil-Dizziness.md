# Flaxseed and fish oil comparison with dizziness counts

## Flaxseed and fish oil comparison with dizziness counts

The February 2022 retrospective report compares 60 people receiving flaxseed oil with 60 receiving fish oil in type 2 diabetes with coronary disease. Table 2 lists dizziness in 3/60 per group. Prescribing was observational and both groups received an oil. The report gives no oil pressing specification, posture-specific dizziness, symptom severity or Parkinson comparison.
