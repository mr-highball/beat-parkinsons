# European dietary and botanical registrations, 2020–2024

## Short-chain fatty acid and prebiotic trial

DRKS00027061 was retrospectively registered November 19, 2021. The randomized study compared propionic and butyric acid, 2′-fucosyllactose, and their combination alongside Parkinson’s medication. Each group received at least one active supplement. A November 2023 preprint reported motor outcomes, with a peer-reviewed revision and a separate constipation analysis from the same 72-person cohort. These preparations supply defined compounds in capsules. [Primary registration](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00027061/details) · [2023 preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2023.11.01.23297866) · [Later outcome reports](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-European-Registry-Followup.md#short-chain-fatty-acid-and-prebiotic-outcomes)

## Personalized nutritional counseling

DRKS00034152 was registered April 24, 2024. NutriPark compares personalized nutritional counseling with an initial paper-based counseling period followed by personalized counseling. It lists change in UPDRS or medication requirements and quality of life after six months. The available registration reports planned recruitment. Searches by identifier and study name found no result. [Primary registration](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00034152/details)

## Sesame and linseed oil in the NEUROBIS placebo

CTIS 2023-507715-35-02 describes a phase II trial of a pharmaceutical cannabis extract versus a 9:1 sesame and linseed oil placebo in people with Parkinson’s, Alzheimer’s disease or amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. The registered objectives include Parkinson’s subgroup UPDRS. The oils are given as a blend in the drug comparison, and the trial gives no independent estimate for either oil. The public record showed recruitment and no results. A January 2023 regional funding notice documents the proposed trial under an earlier phase III description. CTIS lists authorization on July 17, 2024 and its current public JSON publication timestamp in July 2026. The date of first public registry display is unverified. [Primary CTIS registration](https://euclinicaltrials.eu/ctis-public/view/2023-507715-35-02?lang=en) · [Primary CTIS data](https://euclinicaltrials.eu/ctis-public-api/retrieve/2023-507715-35-02) · [Regional funding notice](https://www.regione.piemonte.it/governo/bollettino/abbonati/2023/03/attach/dgr_06375_1050_28122022.pdf)
