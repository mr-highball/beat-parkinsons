# Olive oil and gait, 2020–2024

## Olive oil within the oldest-old walking and diet factor

Rolandi and colleagues published the population-based oldest-old study in August 2024 (PMID 39096236, DOI 10.1093/geronb/gbae132). Of 501 participants at the study wave, 97 had dementia and 404 without dementia formed the analysis population. The 14-item Mediterranean diet screener includes olive-oil questions. Walking speed was measured separately, and the analysis placed it with the aggregate diet score and current activities in a lifestyle factor. None of the published contrasts estimates walking speed by olive-oil exposure, cold-pressed grade or amount. Parkinson appears in an author affiliation. The study supplies no Parkinson gait subgroup estimate. [Primary report and Table 1](https://academic.oup.com/psychsocgerontology/article/79/10/gbae132/7726776)

## Extra-virgin oil and measured gait in severe obesity

The DieTBra trial randomized 111 adults with severe obesity to extra-virgin olive oil alone, a traditional Brazilian diet alone, or the diet plus extra-virgin oil for 12 weeks. Table 4 gives six-meter walking-speed changes of −0.01, +0.04 and +0.01 m/s (overall p=.283). Adding oil to diet did not produce a clear walking-speed contrast (reported pairwise p=.302). The covariate analysis reported a walking-speed result for the diet-only group. Oil grading beyond extra virgin and Parkinson-specific outcomes were unreported.
