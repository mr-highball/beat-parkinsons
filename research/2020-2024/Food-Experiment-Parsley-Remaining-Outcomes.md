# Parsley preparation and Parkinson symptom evidence

## Consumed dried parsley powder and measured human flavone kinetics

Healthy male adults consumed dried powdered parsley leaves in yogurt in one intervention. The reported apigenin-4′-glucuronide plasma time to maximum was six hours after the yogurt formulation and four hours after the parsley drink. Other preparations included chamomile or isolated compound. The measurements concern metabolites, without levodopa kinetics or a dyskinesia, handwriting or swallowing outcome. [Borges primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35452808/).

## Parsley-containing noodle puree and recovered bolus viscosity

A noodle purée included a combined garlic and parsley ingredient amount of 1.9%. Five healthy adults orally processed the recipes and spat out the samples at the point they felt ready to swallow. For the noodle purée, mean shear viscosity at 50 per second fell from 4809.85 to 1872.71 mPa·s, a reported 61.07% reduction (P<.0001). This compares bolus properties before and after oral processing, without ingestion, a parsley-free comparison, specified fresh versus dried herb or a clinical swallowing outcome. [Ismael-Mohammed full article and Tables 2 and 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10490504/).
