# Swallowing discomfort in a soybean oil capsule trial

## Swallowing diaries and soybean oil in both capsule arms

Xie, Tang, Yao and colleagues, Asia Pacific Journal of Clinical Nutrition, March 1, 2024. DOI 10.6133/apjcn.202403_33(1).0012. The randomized 12-week pediatric trial assigned 62 girls aged 6–10 to decaffeinated green tea polyphenols capsules or matched placebo. Each active capsule held 200 mg of polyphenols and 300 mg soybean oil. Each placebo capsule held 500 mg of soybean oil within the same type of capsule. Caregiver diaries included swallowing adaptation. Some children reported mild swallowing discomfort attributed in the report to large capsules, with no stated arm-specific count or validated dysphagia measurement. The comparisons do not isolate an effect of the oil and the preparation grade is unverified. The trial did not study people with Parkinson disease.
