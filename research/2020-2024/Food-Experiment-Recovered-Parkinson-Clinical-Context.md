# Recovered Parkinson clinical reports

## Composite motor scores during deep brain stimulation PET imaging

Jørgensen and colleagues studied 13 people with Parkinson disease treated with subthalamic stimulation and 10 healthy controls. The PET design examined serotonin receptor binding as stimulation was turned off, with part-level MDS-UPDRS measures. Table 1 reports mean part III scores of 10 with stimulation on and 37 with stimulation off among participants with available data. It gives no separate rigidity result. Fatigue appears as background and acute back pain is mentioned in screening. Neither was a reported trial outcome or a culinary comparison.
