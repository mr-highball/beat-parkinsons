# Thymol conference experiments

## Thyme and oregano constituent in autophagy experiments

The 2021 proceedings describe isolated thymol experiments in reporter zebrafish, human cells and mouse models. The measured outcomes include skeletal muscle autophagy in fish and liver markers in mice fed a high-fat diet. No culinary thyme or oregano was administered. The conference abstract gives no stride-length, anxiety or daytime sleepiness measures.

The same research group subsequently published broader isolated-terpenoid experiments, including motor tests in aging mice. Overlapping cell, fish and high-fat-fed mouse experiments should not be counted as independent.
