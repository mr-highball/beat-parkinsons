# Movement and Rehabilitation

### 2024 — Voice therapy for Parkinson-related dysarthria

In the pragmatic PD COMM trial, intensive Lee Silverman Voice Treatment improved self-reported voice handicap compared with control and with the tested usual speech therapy. Reported adverse events included vocal strain and the trial could not blind participants. [Primary source](https://www.bmj.com/content/386/bmj-2023-078341)
