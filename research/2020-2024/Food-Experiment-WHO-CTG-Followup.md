# Dietary trial follow-up from WHO ClinicalTrials.gov records, 2020–2024

## Prebiotic fiber bar registration and outcomes

NCT04512599 first posted on August 13, 2020. Hall and colleagues reported the results on February 18, 2023. Twenty people with Parkinson disease, ten on treatment and ten untreated, ate a bar combining resistant starch, rice bran, resistant maltodextrin, and inulin for ten days. The open-label study used each participant's baseline and had no comparison group. Total gastrointestinal symptoms improved among treated participants (adjusted p = .04), with no significant change in constipation or bowel movement frequency (p = .23 and .54). Median exploratory UPDRS went from 11.5 to 9.0. The study provides a formulated-food comparison over time without separating rice bran or any other component. Rice bran in the bar also differs from refined rice bran oil for `0801`. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04512599) [Primary report, PMID 36801916](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9938693/) [Local results](Source-Text/PMC9938693.txt#L150)

## Lion's mane mycelium registration

NCT04428983 first posted on June 11, 2020. The registration describes capsules containing erinacine A-enriched *Hericium erinaceus* mycelium versus placebo, with Parkinson motor, sleep, depressive, and cognitive assessments. The November 2026 registry extract gives unknown status and no posted results. An exact identifier search located no linked primary outcome. ## Bran and psyllium registrations

NCT04829760 first posted on April 2, 2021. It randomized people with Parkinson disease and constipation to psyllium, coarse wheat bran, or maltodextrin. The registry records 35 enrolled and completed status. Weight is the primary endpoint and bowel outcomes are secondary. No registry or linked primary outcome was located. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04829760) [Local registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT04829760.txt)

NCT04976959 first posted on July 26, 2021. It tests a drink combining oat, corn, wheat and sorghum brans with inulin and resistant maltodextrin. It targets microbiome changes and includes participants with Parkinson disease and matched healthy volunteers. The archived November 2026 registry record gives unknown status and no posted results. An exact identifier search located no linked primary outcome. The trial does not isolate oats or other bran components. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04976959) [Local formulation](Source-Text/Trials/NCT04976959.txt#L80)

## Resistant starch and bowel outcomes

Becker and colleagues first published on November 25, 2021. RESISTA-PD included 32 Parkinson participants assigned resistant starch, 25 assigned dietary instructions, and 30 healthy controls assigned resistant starch. At eight weeks the Parkinson starch group showed a within-group median constipation score change from 5 to 3.5 (p = .257), a Beck Depression Inventory change from 6.5 to 3 (p = .001), and increased fecal butyrate. The clinical table reports within-group tests. These results concern a commercial resistant-starch preparation in an open-label study and do not isolate a culinary food. [Primary report, PMID 34839011](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9684155/) [Local Table 2](Source-Text/PMC9684155.txt#L132) [2016 registration](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-WHO-CTG-Followup.md#resistant-starch-registration)

## Plant-rich diet feasibility registrations

## Food4PD functional drink registration

NCT06697626 first posted on November 20, 2024. The feasibility trial compares a drink fortified with chicory inulin and B and D vitamins with placebo in people with Parkinson disease and constipation. It plans constipation, broader gastrointestinal, quality-of-life, and microbiome measures. The archived November 2026 registry record reported actual primary completion and no posted results, and the identifier search located no linked primary outcome. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06697626) [Local registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT06697626.txt)

## SENSS lifestyle trial registration

NCT06669455 first posted on November 1, 2024. It registers a 12-month randomized comparison of a personalized program covering stress, exercise, nutrition, sleep and self-management. The primary endpoint is PDQ-39 quality of life at 12 months. The April 1, 2026 registry version available on November 12 listed 256 planned participants, active status with recruitment ended, estimated completion in March 2027 and no posted results. ## PARKA supplement and exercise registration

NCT04322461 first posted March 26, 2020. The registry describes two months of commercial MCT supplementation with supervised exercise in separate Parkinson and Alzheimer cohorts. It records 14 participants and no posted results. The linked 2025 conference abstract gives a different description of the administered supplement. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04322461) [Local registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT04322461.txt) [Conference results and discrepancy](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-WHO-CTG-Followup.md#parka-conference-outcome-and-supplement-discrepancy)

Two preserved copies of the November 3, 2020 registry version show a planned enrollment of 20 and the same Betaquik regimen with CANTAB cognition as a primary outcome. The May 2022 actual start and 14 enrolled are from the later version. Vitaflo's product sheet describes a 20% liquid MCT emulsion sourced from coconut and palm kernel oils. The archived trial fields do not distinguish product weight from MCT weight in their 50 g/day entry or verify dispensing. The hospital lists a May 24, 2024 PIVOT presentation on preliminary PARKA results, with no poster or outcome table in the public listing. [Preserved 2020 registry version](https://web.archive.org/web/20210329181318/https://www.clinicaltrials.gov/ct2/show/NCT04322461) [Manufacturer product sheet](https://www.vitaflousa.com/sites/g/files/lpfasj266/files/2020-06/Betaquik_datasheet.pdf) [Hospital 2024 presentation list, p. 62](https://www.cdrv.ca/clients/SanteEstrie/Sous-sites/Centres_de_recherche/CDRV/Recherche/Publications-scientifiques/Publications_2024.pdf#page=62)

The investigators' May 2023 bulletin describes PARKA as exercise plus a ketone supplement in Parkinson and Alzheimer disease. The bulletin names MCT in its separate discussion of Alzheimer and mild cognitive impairment studies but gives no PARKA formulation, so the product reported at recruitment remains ambiguous. [Investigators' bulletin, PDF p. 6](https://www.cdrv.ca/clients/SanteEstrie/Sous-sites/Centres_de_recherche/CDRV/mieux-vieillir/Journal-Encrage/CdRV-journal-Encrage-2023VF.pdf#page=6)

## Whey formula with soy lecithin during rehabilitation

NCT05523791 first posted on August 31, 2022. The trial compared a whey protein formula containing soy lecithin with magnesium. Both groups followed protein redistribution and received four weeks of rehabilitation. The September 2022 conference report gives group counts of 19 and 25. The February 17, 2023 article abstract gives 26 and 25, with a combined count of 51 matching the registry. The full article reports 63 randomized and 53 retained, with group counts of 27 and 26 in Table 1. The paper cites the 2022 presentation and gives the registration identifier. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05523791) · [2022 conference abstract 1039](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/can-we-add-whey-protein-supplementation-in-patients-with-parkinsons-disease-without-interfering-with-levodopa-response/) · [2023 article, author-posted copy](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/368508922_Can_we_add_whey_protein_supplementation_in_patients_with_Parkinson%27s_disease_without_interfering_with_levodopa_response)

Both reports found inconclusive between-group changes in UPDRS Part III, timed up-and-go, six-minute walk and dopaminergic doses. The conference report describes dose decreases within both groups. In the later article, Table 2 gives p = .52 for the between-group levodopa change and p = .35 for total levodopa-equivalent dose, with a within-group reduction flagged for total dopaminergic dose in the magnesium group. Soy lecithin was an emulsifier in the whey formula. The comparison gives no standalone soy or soybean-oil result. [2023 article, Tables 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1080/00207454.2023.2178433)

## Gluten-free diet in Parkinson disease and multiple system atrophy

NCT05238545 first posted February 14, 2022. The Prague hospital registered a rater-blinded, 12-month comparison of a gluten-free diet and a usual gluten-containing diet in idiopathic Parkinson disease and multiple system atrophy. A parallel preclinical line was described separately. The November 2026 registry recheck still returned the April 8, 2022 update, with 90 planned participants across diagnoses, unknown status and no posted registry results. The grant identifier NU21-04-00443, Prague team and intervention link the trial to a series of primary conference reports. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05238545) · [Local registration](Source-Text/Trials/NCT05238545.txt)

In 2023 the investigators reported six people with Parkinson’s on the gluten-free diet and five on a standard diet after six months. Within the diet arm, MDS-UPDRS II (p = .07) and MoCA (p = .06) changes were inconclusive. Serum triglycerides fell (p = .03). In 2024 the team reported the first 20 people with Parkinson’s followed on the intervention for a year. MoCA and Timed Up and Go each improved from baseline (p < .001). Early MDS-UPDRS changes were inconclusive at one year. The 2024 international and Czech congress reports give the same group size, demographics and clinical p-values. The earlier subgroup reports do not establish an individual food effect. [2023 abstract 1374](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/gluten-free-diet-in-parkinsons-disease-patients-feasibility-safety-and-preliminary-results/) · [2024 abstract 860](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/long-term-evaluation-of-gluten-free-diet-in-parkinsons-disease-patients/) · [2024 Czech congress poster](https://www.czech-neuro.cz/content/uploads/2025/04/sbornik-abstrakt-csns-2024.pdf) · [Larger 2026 report](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-WHO-CTG-Followup.md#twelve-month-gluten-free-diet-outcomes-in-prague)

The November 30, 2023 Czech and Slovak Neurology Congress poster IC-3 reports the same six and five participants, group characteristics and symptom p-values as the 2023 MDS abstract. Its wording places the favorable direction against the standard-diet group, while MDS describes longitudinal changes in the intervention arm and no significant changes in controls. Neither abstract gives a between-arm effect estimate. The Czech report also gives a nonsignificant blood FOXP3+ CD4+ regulatory T-cell difference (p = .12) and a qualitative Tr1-cell trend. Both are peripheral immune measurements. The 2024 MDS report specifies the one-month Part I result (p = .028), which was inconclusive at 12 months, and seven altered stool metabolites at 12 months in the intervention subgroup. The abstracts give no ingredient-level exposure measurements. [Czech congress program](https://mhconsulting.cz/pdf/program.php?id=175) · [2023 Czech abstract, PDF p. 33](https://www.czech-neuro.cz/content/uploads/2024/09/sbornik-abstrakt-36.-cesky-a-slovensky-neurologicky-sjezd.pdf#page=33) · [2024 MDS abstract 860](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/long-term-evaluation-of-gluten-free-diet-in-parkinsons-disease-patients/)

The Czech Ministry of Health amendment first published October 4, 2024 reports that the hospital had enrolled 64 patients with Parkinson disease. It gives June 2024 as the last enrollment in that cohort and June 2025 as the projected end of its one-year follow-up. It gives no allocation, participant flow, completion count, or separate multiple-system-atrophy count. The 2026 congress report describes 57 participants with Parkinson disease. The available reports do not link participants across those counts or establish how many of the 64 were randomized or analyzed. [2024 ministry amendment, p. 1](https://smlouvy.gov.cz/smlouva/soubor/37205540/ISRS%200426-2024.pdf?backlink=mtvkn#page=1) · [Public filing date](https://smlouvy.gov.cz/smlouva/30409472?backlink=mtvkn) · [2026 congress results and project extension](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-WHO-CTG-Followup.md#twelve-month-gluten-free-diet-outcomes-in-prague)

## Vutiglabridin phase 2a registration

NCT06329141 first posted on March 25, 2024. The randomized, double-blind trial compared two doses of the synthetic glabridin analogue with placebo in early Parkinson disease. The prespecified primary outcome was MDS-UPDRS Part III change at 24 weeks. The July 8, 2026 version lists 99 enrolled, completed status, and no posted results. The investigators subsequently reported results at the 2026 international congress. ## Aerosolized capsaicin as a cough test stimulus

NCT05700838 first posted January 26, 2023. Curtis and colleagues published the exact-NCT one-arm cough training study online on October 8, 2024. Participants inhaled aerosolized capsaicin during reflex cough tests before and after voluntary cough training. Voluntary cough peak flow increased after training and reflex cough peak flow had no clear change. Capsaicin was a test stimulus and not an assigned treatment. A separate continuing trial, NCT05700825, registers in-person versus telehealth airway protection rehabilitation, with capsaicin or barium allergy among exclusions. The two records give no oral ground cayenne estimate for swallowing difficulty, claim 0724. [Published outcome and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11647975/) [Single-arm study registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05700838) [Rehabilitation delivery registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05700825)


## Randomized and rollover ENT-01 outcomes

Camilleri and colleagues first published the KARMET results on November 8, 2022. The paper reports 150 dosed, of whom 135 completed at least seven days of treatment and entered the primary efficacy analysis. During the fixed-dose period, complete spontaneous bowel movements averaged 3.2 per week with ENT-01 and 1.2 with placebo (modeled rate ratio 2.78, 95% CI 1.61–4.81). The contrast was inconclusive two weeks after treatment (rate ratio 1.65, 95% CI 0.93–2.93). Nausea occurred in 32/93 and 3/57, and diarrhea in 18/93 and 3/57, respectively. The 11-person hallucination subgroup used a broader psychosis severity scale in an exploratory analysis. It gives no separate visual hallucination frequency result. The December 20, 2022 erratum is indexed without an abstract and the publisher route did not expose its contents. [Primary full article, Tables 2–4](https://escholarship.org/content/qt30q0z0rt/qt30q0z0rt.pdf#page=6) · [Erratum](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36598175/) · [2018 registration and posted results](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-WHO-CTG-Followup.md#rasmet-and-karmet-registrations-and-early-ent-01-outcomes)

The follow-on safety study NCT04483479 first posted July 23, 2020, and its results posted March 19, 2024. Of 27 participants starting the single-arm study, 12 completed. The posted record counts 23 participants with treatment-related adverse events, including recurrent diarrhea in eight, dizziness in eight, and recurrent vomiting in five. The study ended early, citing recruitment and retention problems during the pandemic, and the record provides no efficacy analysis. The tested intervention was synthetic squalamine phosphate. [Official results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04483479?tab=results) · [Local registry and results](Source-Text/Trials/NCT04483479.txt)

Enterin’s January 27, 2022 initial KARMET release reported 13 participants with baseline psychosis, while the November paper’s relevant analysis included 11. The release also mentions an MMSE subgroup. Both sources describe exploratory results in small subsets, with no estimate for visual hallucinations alone. [Primary sponsor release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/01/27/2374392/0/en/enterin-meets-study-endpoints-for-the-phase-2b-karmet-study-involving-patients-with-parkinson-s-disease.html) · [Separate dementia trial registration](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-WHO-CTG-Followup.md#ent-01-parkinson-dementia-registration)
