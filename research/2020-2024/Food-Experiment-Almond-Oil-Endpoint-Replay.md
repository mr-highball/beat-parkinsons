# Almond oil preparation and endpoint search

## Room temperature and heated extraction of Comuna almond kernel oil

The 2021 laboratory comparison sourced Comuna almond kernels and compared hydraulic pressing at room temperature against screw pressing at assigned temperatures from 50 to 200 °C, using different processing conditions. The authors called the resulting oils virgin and reported generally better quality indices with the room-temperature hydraulic method and greater yields for some heated screw-press conditions. The same cultivar therefore supplies distinguishable processing exposures in the original experiment. The study measured chemical and organoleptic properties, with no clinical endpoint. [Primary materials, methods and oil quality results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8151718/) · [Licensed local full text](Source-Text/PMC8151718.txt)

## Topical almond oil massage comparator in restless legs syndrome

The 2021 hemodialysis trial assigned 105 women to foot reflexology, aromatherapy massage or a plain foot-massage control. The control used topical almond oil, and the reflexology arm also used almond oil with an additional procedure. The original report shows a significant eight-week severity result only in the aromatherapy arm. Restless legs ratings describe a different clinical construct from sustained dystonic contractions. There was no oral, oil-free massage comparison or Parkinson cohort. [Primary Methods and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8544351/) · [Licensed local full text](Source-Text/PMC8544351.txt)

## Almond oil tested only in silymarin formulation screening

The 2023 Parkinson-model paper included almond oil in a bench screen of drug solubility. The final intranasal silymarin formulation tested in animals used Labrafil M 1944 CS (LMCS) as the selected oil phase. Almond oil was screened in vitro and was not the oil administered in that formulation. Motor results from the rats cannot be attributed to almond oil or used as evidence of an oral effect. [Primary formulation Methods and animal results](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9961237/) · [Licensed local full text](Source-Text/PMC9961237.txt)

## Possible almond diluent during topical frankincense use in focal dystonia

An April 11, 2023 primary case report describes two people with benign essential blepharospasm, a focal dystonia, who used topical frankincense. One stopped botulinum toxin injections and the other’s average treatment interval increased from 3.5 to eight months. The authors state that cottonseed oil, almond oil or coconut oil was used as diluent after local burning. They do not map the diluents to specific people or outcome periods. The observations are uncontrolled, involve ongoing or previous botulinum treatment, and do not isolate an almond-oil effect or establish ingestion, processing grade or Parkinson-related dystonia. [Primary case article, Discussion and patient observations](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10214279/) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2023-Frankincense-Blepharospasm-Possible-Almond-Diluent.txt)

## Registered facial photography endpoints in the topical almond oil trial

NCT06571721 was first posted August 26, 2024. It registered topical almond oil alone, vitamin E added to almond oil, and tretinoin in a castor-oil formulation. The primary outcome is photographic wrinkle-severity change. Secondary outcomes concern pigmentation, skin hydration, sebum, tolerability and transepidermal water loss. The registered protocol does not identify cold pressing or measure voluntary or spontaneous facial movement. [Official trial registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06571721) · [Local factual extract](Source-Text/NCT06571721-Topical-Almond-Oil-Registered-Facial-Measures.txt)

## Cold-pressed topical almond oil and registered wrinkle severity

A two-arm study of topical almond oil and 0.5% retinol lotion first posted on August 18, 2021 and lists study completion on October 5, 2022. The almond-oil arm explicitly specifies cold pressing. The primary endpoint is facial wrinkle severity and length on a high-resolution facial modeling system at 12 weeks. The record reports no posted results, movement outcome or oral exposure. This 12-week study has a different identifier and design from the later 16-week Khan comparison.
