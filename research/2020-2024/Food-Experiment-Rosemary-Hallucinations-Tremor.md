# Rosemary on a Parkinson dietary menu

Hegelmaier and colleagues published February 2020. Their 14-day vegetarian dietary intervention enrolled 16 people with Parkinson disease; 10 additionally received bowel cleansing. Appendix A names rosemary among many permitted seasonings. The paper does not specify its fresh or dried state, amount, or consumption by individual participants. UPDRS Part III includes rest tremor, but Figure 4 and the corresponding supplement report composite Part III results, not rosemary-specific or rest-tremor item outcomes. The multi-food pattern and additional bowel cleansing preclude attributing the composite change to rosemary.
