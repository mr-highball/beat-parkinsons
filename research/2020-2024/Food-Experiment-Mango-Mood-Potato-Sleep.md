# Mango leaf extracts and measured depressed mood

## Non-soluble mango leaf extract and POMS depression-dejection

Dodd and colleagues studied 300 mg mango leaf extract or matched placebo in generally healthy adults. Supplementary File 4 gives a treatment main effect of F = 0.72 (p = .398) and treatment-by-time F = 0.31 (p = .731) for the POMS Depression–Dejection subscale. Estimated means at 300 minutes were 43.68 (SE 0.36) for the extract and 44.22 (SE 0.36) for placebo. The short-term mood assessment provides no dried mango flesh exposure or Parkinson depression outcome.
