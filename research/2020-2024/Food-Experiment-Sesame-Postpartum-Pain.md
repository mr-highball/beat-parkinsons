# Sesame oil and postpartum after-pain

## Oral sesame oil and postpartum after-pain

In a 2024 placebo-controlled trial, 86 multiparous postpartum participants received a 1.1 g sesame oil or placebo capsule every six hours for 24 hours. The publisher's accessible abstract reports lower postintervention after-pain intensity and pain-quality scores in the sesame group, each p<.001. Individual arm means, dose-specific results and oil roasting remain unverified because the full article was not accessible. Its 2021 registry describes possible rescue medication and contains a conflicting placebo schedule. This is a different N1 study from the 2025 N2 episiotomy trial in primiparous women and does not estimate effects of toasted sesame oil in Parkinson disease. [Publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2210803324000423) · [Official 2021 registration](https://irct.ir/trial/53863) · [Factual extract](Source-Text/2020-2025-sesame-pain-primary-extract.txt)

## Registered postpartum comparison

The N1 record was first registered March 10, 2021. Its summary gives a four-hour placebo interval, while the detailed methods and published abstract give six hours. The registry's masking field describes no blinding, while the published abstract describes triple blinding. Actual dosing and masking remain unverified. The registry describes capsule preparation from oil without specifying roasting or extraction. [Original registration](https://irct.ir/trial/53863)
