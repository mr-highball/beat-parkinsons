## Whole walnuts and anxiety during university examination stress

Herselman and colleagues’ 2022 randomized study assigned whole walnuts or control around examination periods. It assessed separate DASS-21 anxiety and POMS tension-anxiety measures. Examination timing affected multiple scores. The DASS-21 anxiety model reported a time effect (p = .0053) without a significant between-arm difference. On the POMS tension-anxiety domain the visit 2 to 3 within-arm mean change was −4.94 for control and −4.10 for walnut. The anxiety findings are distinct from the paper’s broader claims about mental health, stress and mood. The trial used whole walnuts and students. It supplies no oil-specific Parkinson anxiety effect. [Primary Results 3.2, Figures 2 and 4](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/14/22/4776)
