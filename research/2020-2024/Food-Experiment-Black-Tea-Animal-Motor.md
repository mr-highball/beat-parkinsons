# Black tea preparations and animal motor outcomes

## Green semi-fermented and fully fermented tea extracts

Lee and colleagues, November 17, 2022 online and 2023 in print. The paper compared green, semi-fermented and fully fermented C. sinensis tea extracts from the Hadong Green Tea Institute in an MPTP mouse model. The authors identify the fully fermented arm as black tea in the introduction. Publisher Methods preview describes ground dried leaves, distilled-water extraction at 121 °C for up to 15 minutes at the stated 1:10 ratio and freeze-drying. The preview therefore identifies a concentrated, dried experimental preparation. The paper reports 25 μg/mL for cell-free antioxidant assays. The publisher supplement's Fig. S1 labels each extract as 100 mg/kg/day orally for four weeks following model induction. It shows untreated, model-only and levodopa groups. The timeline caption refers generally to sample injection, while each tea group specifies the oral route. Table S1 gives the semi-fermented extract the highest nominal theanine value, 1183.02 ± 45.18, in the same significance group as green tea, 1171.80 ± 19.96. Fully fermented tea had 638.90 ± 6.35. Semi-fermented tea's GABA, 93.95 ± 1.15, differed from the other two extract groups. The table displays no units. [Publisher supplement](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0963996922011917-mmc1.docx) · [Local primary report extract](Source-Text/PMID36737888-hadong-tea-motor-extract.txt) · [Publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0963996922011917) · [PubMed, PMID 36737888](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36737888/)

The abstract attributes improved behavior generally to fermented tea, and the publisher's Highlights specify semi-fermented tea. The original Fig. 4A resolves the behavioral arm attribution. The MPTP-only group's plotted motion-test duration is about 55 s (letter b), compared with about 300 s in untreated controls, 330 s with levodopa, 340 s with green tea, 320 s with semi-fermented tea and 300 s with fully fermented black tea. The figure labels all five groups letter a. These are approximate readings from the bars. The graph does not distinguish the three tea arms by letter. The figure has only a generic motion-test axis label. The apparatus, movement criteria and test limits still require full Methods, so the result cannot be attributed to rotarod, timed walking or rest tremor. [Publisher Fig. 4](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0963996922011917-gr4.jpg)

The conference abstract repeats the same three Hadong tea categories and several biological endpoints. It provides no basis to count an independent behavioral experiment. The 2021 study by the same seven authors examined partly oxidized teas in cells and did not test motor outcomes. Their related isolated L-theanine report explicitly names rotarod testing. That assay cannot be attributed to the three-tea experiment. ## Retraction of subacute theaflavin model

The editors published the retraction for the 2012 subacute theaflavin paper online July 26, 2024. The print notice appeared May 7, 2025. The notice reports apparent overlaps and resizing in Fig. 4A and overlapping or internally duplicated regions in Fig. 5A. The authors offered replacement images from located slides. The editors retained concern about the top portion of Fig. 4 and the reason for original duplication. The notice retracts the entire identified paper, including its use as a source for the reported motor experiments.
