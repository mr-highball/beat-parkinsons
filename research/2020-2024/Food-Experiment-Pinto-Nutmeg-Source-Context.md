# Pinto bean sleep and nutmeg motor source context

## Grouped legume preference and sleep quality in students

November 17, 2021. Delgadillo-Arteaga and colleagues surveyed Mexican university students during COVID-19 restrictions. Table 2 gives an odds ratio of 1.59, 95% CI 1.22–2.08, for the beans, chickpeas and lentils preference item. Footnote a defines the item as dislike. A separate meal item pooling rice, pasta and beans gives 1.07, 95% CI 0.87–1.32. The available responses do not isolate pinto intake, boiling, an insomnia severity effect or a Parkinson group. ## Cooked kidney beans in a laboratory fermentation assay

January 8, 2024. Table 1 and section 3.3.5 compare boiled, roasted and grilled kidney beans and lentils in a digestion and culture assay using fecal material from three healthy donors. The reported cooking differences for legumes are between boiling and grilling and differ by donor. The study reports microbial outcomes in the assay and does not identify a pinto cultivar or test sleep or Parkinson outcomes.
