## Bio-enhanced turmeric and severe chemoradiotherapy dysphagia

First published June 23, 2021. Soni and colleagues randomized 60 postsurgical oral-cancer patients to two oral doses of bio-enhanced turmeric (BCM-95) or placebo during chemoradiotherapy. The original abstract reports grade 3 dysphagia in 25% and 20% on the two doses versus 60% on placebo, with reported p=.025 and .010 against placebo. The abstract cites CTRI/2015/12/006413 and December 4, 2015. The official protocol and full paper table remain to be retrieved. This was a capsule intervention in treatment-related oral injury.
