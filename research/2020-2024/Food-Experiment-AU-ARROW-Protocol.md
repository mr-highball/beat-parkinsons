# AU-ARROW lifestyle study

## AU-ARROW trial registration

ACTRN12621001760864 first posted December 23, 2021. It compares a two-year multidomain lifestyle program with general lifestyle education in older adults at elevated dementia risk. The registration lists a global cognition composite and describes repeated visits. The linked publication is the protocol. The registry results field did not contain outcome estimates when checked on November 16, 2026. ## Olive oil instructions and planned anxiety and insomnia measures

Gardener and colleagues published the protocol April 9, 2024. Table 1 includes two tablespoons per day of extra-virgin olive oil and using it as the primary cooking oil within a MIND dietary program. The schedule includes the Depression, Anxiety, Stress Scale and the Insomnia Severity Index. Diet, physical activity, cognitive training and monitoring are part of the same intervention. Processing method, isolated oil effects and Parkinson symptom results remain unestablished.
