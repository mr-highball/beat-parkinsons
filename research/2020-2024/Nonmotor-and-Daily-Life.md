# Nonmotor and daily life

### 2020 — Telephone cognitive behavioral therapy for Parkinson’s depression

Dobkin and colleagues randomized 72 people with Parkinson’s and depression to usual care with or without telephone-based cognitive behavioral therapy. Depression improved more in the telephone therapy group, and the difference persisted at six-month follow-up. This trial evaluated a selected group able to participate in telephone therapy. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32238507/)

### 2021 — Aspiration pneumonia incidence and mortality in national claims

Won and colleagues used South Korean national insurance data to compare 10,159 people newly diagnosed with Parkinson’s with matched controls. Parkinson’s was associated with greater incidence of coded aspiration pneumonia, and mortality after a first episode was high. Claims data can miss cases and cannot identify which swallowing interventions would reduce that risk. [Primary source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33758213/)
