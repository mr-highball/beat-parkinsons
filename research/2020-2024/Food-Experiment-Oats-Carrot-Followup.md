# Oats and carrot evidence

## 2020 Green oat extract and cognitive tracking tasks

Kennedy and colleagues randomized 132 healthy adults to green oat extract capsules or placebo. Some dose and time contrasts favored the extract on computer-based spatial working memory and cursor tracking with serial subtraction. The report found no treatment effect on mood. Cursor tracking with a computer mouse was not clinical hand dexterity or micrographia. The extract was administered in capsules to adults without a Parkinson diagnosis. [Local full text](Source-Text/PMC7352613.txt) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12061598)

## 2020 Diet patterns and prodromal features

Molsberry and colleagues studied dietary patterns and reported prodromal features in the Nurses' Health Study and Health Professionals Follow-up Study. The probable REM sleep behavior disorder outcome was questionnaire-based. Higher overall pattern scores were associated with lower odds of excessive daytime sleepiness, among other outcomes. The cohort for the prodromal feature analysis excluded diagnosed Parkinson's, and the paper does not estimate dream enactment or sleepiness by raw carrot intake. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000010523)

## 2021 Registration for green oat extract during smoking reduction

NCT04749017 first posted February 10, 2021. It registered a placebo-controlled green oat extract study during smoking reduction. The registry displayed no summary results in the retrieved record. The published study includes actigraphy. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04749017) · [2024 primary result](#2024-green-oat-extract-and-sleep-in-smokers)

## 2021 Avenanthramide in an oxidative stress cell model

Hou and colleagues synthesized three avenanthramides found in oats and tested them in PC12 cells. The primary abstract reports changes in Nrf2-related markers and protection against oxidative damage with avenanthramide-2c in the cell system. The design provides no culinary exposure, patient outcome, or evidence on worsening rigidity and dyskinesia after cooked oats. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33781769/)

## 2021 Black carrot anthocyanins in a Parkinson cell model

Zaim and colleagues studied a black carrot anthocyanin extract in an MPP+-challenged cell model. The report gives changes in cell survival and oxidative stress and apoptosis markers. It does not measure a clinical symptom or administer whole roasted or steamed carrots. [Primary full report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8554904/)

## 2022 Dietary patterns and individual nonmotor symptoms in Parkinson disease

Lawrie and colleagues compared three diet indices with individual nonmotor measures, including apathy, insomnia and daytime sleepiness. The paper reported significant associations for fatigue and depression with the Healthy Diet Indicator and no other significant symptom associations. The cross-sectional analysis did not isolate a preparation-specific oat or carrot item. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35512773/) · [Primary report](https://doi.org/10.1080/07315724.2022.2056544)

## 2024 Green oat extract and sleep in smokers

The main results report 162 adults who smoked randomized to wild green oat extract capsules or placebo for eight weeks (80 extract, 82 placebo). Table 4 lists 145 at week eight (72 extract, 73 placebo), the same group sizes the abstract describes as assigned. Table 4 reports a change in actigraphic wake after sleep onset of −0.7 minutes with extract and +5.4 minutes with placebo (p = .047 for the comparison). Sleep efficiency worsened in the placebo group in a within-arm comparison. Cognitive tests did not show a clear between-arm effect. The study addresses sleep in smokers, without rolled oats, Parkinson's, or a clinical insomnia severity comparison. [Local full text, Table 4 and supplement discussion](Source-Text/PMC11220258.txt) · [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnut.2024.1405156)

## 2024 Oral purple carrot extract in reserpine-treated rats

Custódio-Silva and colleagues reported reduced catalepsy time and fewer oral movements in reserpine-treated rats given purple carrot extract. The public abstract gives no separate stride length, executive function or human symptom outcome. The first public journal report was January 25, 2024. It is linked to the earlier research program without assuming independent replication. [Primary publisher abstract](https://www.benthamscience.com/article/137940) · [2019 primary abstract](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Oats-Carrot-Followup.md#2019-purple-carrot-extract-at-a-neuroscience-congress)

## Overall diet scores and probable REM behavior in Chinese adults

First online September 7, 2022. The cross-sectional study included 71,640 adults free of diagnosed Parkinson disease. The authors considered questionnaire-defined probable REM sleep behavior disorder alongside four other features. The adjusted odds ratio for at least two versus no features comparing extreme modified Alternate Healthy Eating Index quartiles was 0.64 (95% CI 0.49–0.85). The abstract names daytime sleepiness and constipation as individually associated with the score. It does not provide a raw carrot contrast or a count of dream-enactment episodes. The original supplement and item-level questionnaire data remain separate follow-through. [Local factual extract](Source-Text/2022-Zhang-Prodromal-Diet-REM-Factual-Extract.txt) · [Zhang and colleagues, primary indexed report](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.29208)
