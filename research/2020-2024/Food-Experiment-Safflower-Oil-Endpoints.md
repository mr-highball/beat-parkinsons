# Safflower oil and neurologic outcomes in 2020–2024

## Friedreich ataxia safflower walking and falls results

Results first posted on 27 May 2022 for [NCT04102501](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Oil-Endpoints.md#friedreich-ataxia-rt001-registration). In the encapsulated USP safflower arm, one-minute walking distance at 11 months changed by −220.42 inches (SE 78.27, n=20), versus −193.83 (SE 73.16, n=25) with the synthetic drug RT001. The between-arm test gave p=0.8061. The systematic adverse-event table reports five falls among 32 safflower participants with five affected, versus seven among 33 RT001 participants with seven affected, over twelve months. This is a fall-event comparison against an active drug, with a 5% reporting threshold for nonserious events and no preintervention fall frequency. A numerically lower fall count and worsening walking distance can coexist. The population had Friedreich ataxia and the oil's cold-press grade is unverified. Lynch et al. published their original trial paper online on 3 December 2022 and reported 45 completers among 65 recruited. It confirms the comparative design and lack of significant effects for RT001 across its main and secondary endpoints.

[NCT04102501 posted results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04102501?tab=results) · [Registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT04102501-results-2022.txt) · [Lynch et al., PMID 36462055](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00415-022-11501-4)

## Safflower comparator in ALS bulbar outcomes

NCT04762589 first posted in February 2021. Weemering et al. published their phase 2 report in 2023. Twenty-two ALS participants received encapsulated USP safflower oil and 21 received synthetic RT001 during 24 weeks of blinded treatment. The mean modeled ALSFRS-R total fell 4.91 points (95% CI 2.58 to 7.23 points of decline) in the safflower arm and 3.00 points (0.70 to 5.31 points of decline) on RT001. The mean difference was 1.90 points in favor of RT001 (95% CI −1.39 to 5.19, p=0.25). ALSFRS-R includes items on speech, swallowing and walking. Table 3 reports the ALSFRS-R and ALSAQ-40 changes as overall scores. A speech or swallowing change cannot be recovered from those totals. This comparator trial supplies the exposure and a composite including relevant bulbar domains in another neurodegenerative condition. It leaves cold pressing, speech intelligibility and clinical dysphagia effects unresolved.

The full paper distinguishes bulbar onset at baseline from changes with treatment. Tube feeding appears only as part of a combined progression endpoint. The publisher lists a supplement whose download was inaccessible, and the official registry has no posted results. [Original paper](https://doi.org/10.1111/ene.16020) · [Full-paper text record](Source-Text/2023-PMID37550954-RT001-ALS-Safflower-Comparator.txt) · [Registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT04762589.txt).

## FORCES sarcopenia safflower oil trial

NCT06361511 first posted on 12 April 2024. The April 2026 record plans an enrollment of 66 and compares two supplied foods each day containing 12 g/day total high-linoleic or high-oleic safflower oil for 24 weeks. Secondary outcomes include timed 400 m walking, handgrip, chair rise, and a Short Physical Performance Battery composite that includes three standing balance positions. Movement-limiting neurological and neuromuscular conditions are excluded. The study lists recruitment underway with no results in the 14 November 2026 retrieval. The protocol does not establish the exact culinary grade. A later data release could inform the comparative oil-composition, walking and static balance questions in sarcopenia.

[NCT06361511](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06361511) · [Registry text](Source-Text/Trials/NCT06361511.txt)

## Safflower flavonoid prevention study in a Parkinson model

Ablat et al. published their experiment online in August 2022. The intervention was a standardized safflower flavonoid extract in a rotenone rat model. The indexed report identifies a plant preparation distinct from the culinary seed oil. The [2026 corrigendum](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Oil-Endpoints.md#corrigendum-to-the-safflower-flavonoid-prevention-study) is part of the same study trail. Consult the author or journal for the corrected material before relying on specific effect estimates.

[Ablat et al., PMID 35940347](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35940347/)

## Safflower pigment and depression-like behavior in a Parkinson model

Zhang et al. reported lower immobility on forced swim and tail suspension tasks and higher sucrose intake after intragastric hydroxysafflor yellow A in a rotenone-and-stress mouse model. This is a purified pigment study with depression-like outcomes. The paper did not use safflower seed oil or diagnose apathy, and the direction of a dietary oil effect cannot be recovered from the pigment result.

[Zhang et al., PMID 38822547](https://www.pjps.pk/uploads/2024/04/1714043435.pdf)

## Registered safflower comparator in progressive supranuclear palsy

The US entry first posted June 24, 2021 and specifies 960 mg oral safflower oil capsules in the comparator arm of a randomized RT001 study. The primary endpoint is change in the 28-item progressive supranuclear palsy rating scale at 48 weeks. This composite contains gait-related items. The registration supplies neither a separate walking-speed result nor the oil pressing method. The US entry shows Unknown recruitment status and no posted results. The matching EU protocol provides an earlier public record. ## Earlier European registration of the same RT001 trial

Poland registered the same protocol RT001-013 in the European register on April 20, 2021 as EudraCT 2020-003911-90. It lists an oral soft-capsule placebo without naming safflower. The Polish record gives August 29, 2022 for the end of the global trial and has no results in the checked record. The oil identity comes from the later US entry. The registers represent one trial.
