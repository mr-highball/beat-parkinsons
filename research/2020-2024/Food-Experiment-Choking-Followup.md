# Food experiment choking evidence

## Xanthan-thickened test liquids in Parkinson videofluoroscopy

The sponsor of `NCT03192358` first posted results on July 14, 2022. Seventeen people with Parkinson’s were evaluated in each consistency condition. Tests used water with barium and, for the four thicker conditions, Resource ThickenUp Clear xanthan gum thickener. From thin to extremely thick, the registry counts of people with unsafe swallows, defined as a Penetration–Aspiration Scale score above 2, were 2, 1, 1, 0, and 1 of 17. Counts with pharyngeal residue of concern were 2, 3, 3, 1, and 2. Counts with multiple swallows per bolus were 6, 3, 2, 2, and 2. The posted counts do not establish individual transitions or a baked-potato effect. The registry separately reports results for amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. ## Baked potato eaten by a resident with Parkinson’s in an Indiana inspection

An Indiana Department of Health inspection records that on February 9, 2022 a resident had eaten baked potato during a meal that also included chicken nuggets and diced peaches. The resident’s clinical record lists Parkinson’s disease and dysphagia. A family member reported difficulty with the nugget coating during that meal and severe choking on a liquid on an earlier occasion. The resident and a dietitian’s earlier note reported no known episodes in their respective accounts. The inspection documents actual baked-potato consumption without a recorded potato-specific coughing, obstruction, aspiration, or swallowing severity measure. It does not quantify the potato portion separately. The survey ended February 9 and the public form is marked March 16, 2022. [Primary state CMS-2567, event 5G6N11, F0689, pp. 15–17](https://www.in.gov/health/reports/QAMIS/ltccr/5g6n11_2567.pdf#page=15)

## Yogurt bananas rice and cookies in a Parkinson imaging study

Oh and colleagues’ April 11, 2023 report examined swallowing and brain glucose metabolism in 82 people with Parkinson’s. ## Apple-flavored test liquids in a Parkinson pressure study

Saleem, Miles, and Allen’s June 12, 2024 report examined 21 participants with mild to moderate Parkinson’s. During manometry they swallowed an apple-flavored saline test medium at three volumes and three viscosities using a separate gum-based thickener. Volume and viscosity affected some timing and upper-esophageal pressure metrics. A separate videofluoroscopy protocol found aspiration during a 100 mL straw-drinking task in four participants. Two of those participants also aspirated on smaller liquid or paste trials. These are findings for test volumes and viscosities, with no whole apple or potato challenge. [Primary article, Methods, Results, and Table 3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00405-024-08774-y) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC11416356.txt)

## Repetitive tongue pressure studies in Urayasu

Hayasaka and colleagues’ 2021 Japanese Neurological Society abstract `MSO-01-2` compared five Parkinson’s participants in an oral levodopa group with five receiving intestinal levodopa–carbidopa gel. It reports tongue pressure and time from pressure onset to peak. The time to peak was 1.7 versus 1.4 seconds (p = .09). The abstract’s statement that peak pressure was lower in the gel group conflicts with the displayed means of 26 kPa for oral therapy and 29 kPa for gel. [Primary 2021 abstract, p. S410](https://www.neurology-jp.org/Journal/public_pdf/61_supplement_05.pdf#page=1)

The 2023 IAPRD poster `P_148` and the February 25, 2024 article report a pilot of ten people with Parkinson’s and eight controls. The paper measured maximal isometric tongue pressure against the hard palate 20 times at 10-second intervals. Table 1 reports lower mean maximal pressure (32.0 versus 42.0 kPa, p = .01) and longer mean time to peak (1.38 versus 0.52 seconds). The area under the pressure curve did not differ significantly between groups. This was a pressure task without a food or liquid bolus, and the later study compared treatment regimens rather than diagnostic groups. Participant overlap with that later study is unverified. [2023 congress book, poster P_148](https://www.iaprd-world-congress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/IAPRD-2023-Book-of-Abstracts.pdf) · [2024 primary report, methods and Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10909619/) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC10909619.txt) · [2026 treatment-regimen report](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#levodopa-regimen-and-repeated-tongue-pressure)

## Oropharyngeal freezing in a German Parkinson cohort

Labeit and colleagues’ May 8, 2020 report used endoscopic swallowing assessment in 50 people with Parkinson’s, 50 patients with post-stroke dysphagia and 50 healthy controls. Oropharyngeal freezing was recorded in 17, two and zero participants, respectively. ## Green jelly and white bread in Münster swallowing studies

Labeit and colleagues’ March 11, 2020 intestinal levodopa–carbidopa gel study analyzed 11 patients with Parkinson’s and paired endoscopy before and after treatment. The tests used green jelly, blue-dyed liquid and white bread. Mean white-out duration on solid swallows decreased from 984 to 699 ms (p = .002). The pooled penetration or aspiration score changed from 0.3 to 0.5 (p = .690), and the pooled overall swallowing score from 14.9 to 13.0 (p = .312). The exposure was a medication infusion. The food-specific timing measure used the same solid at both visits. An October 2020 study from the same group compared these consistencies under cognitive and motor dual-task conditions and is cataloged separately. [Primary infusion report, protocol and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7086436/) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC7086436.txt) · [Dual-task consistency comparison](Food-Experiment-DUMAS-Swallowing.md#2020-swallowing-by-food-consistency-under-dual-task-conditions)

The group’s November 12, 2022 report used the same three food and drink consistencies and separately tested placebo tablets and capsules in 66 people with idiopathic Parkinson’s. Table 1 reports dysphagia on food or liquid testing in 46 of 66, and six had a highest food or liquid Penetration–Aspiration Scale score of at least 3. The reported food and liquid outcomes pool the boluses. The December 22, 2023 DIGEST-FEES validation reanalyzed 66 exams with the same mean age, sex and stage counts, protocol, and score distribution. It cites an earlier 49-person mixed-neurogenic study as the video source, so the apparent overlap with the 2022 cohort is inferred from the exact matching characteristics. The validation reports a correlation between the safety subscore and the highest penetration or aspiration score (r = .63), without food-specific rates. ## Yogurt rice porridge and boiled rice in Korean cough screening

Lee and colleagues’ May 11, 2023 retrospective pilot analyzed 219 people with Parkinson’s referred for videofluoroscopy. The protocol specified curd-type yogurt, rice porridge and boiled rice mixed with undiluted barium, and diluted contrast liquid by spoon, straw and cup. Testing stopped if severe aspiration occurred. The study classified 125 patients with aspiration (Penetration–Aspiration Scale score ≥6) and 94 without it. Voluntary peak cough flow at or below 153 L/min was associated with higher odds of aspiration (adjusted OR 3.648, 95% CI 1.797–7.407), and the threshold had sensitivity 73.1% and specificity 51.1% in this cohort. The outcome pools all tested materials and gives no food-specific event rates. The 103-person Lee row in the later MDS review lacks a source identifier and cannot be assigned to this 219-person cohort. [Primary study, §§2.2–2.3 and Tables 2–4](https://doi.org/10.3390/medicina59050921) · [Local article text](Source-Text/PMC10223645.txt) · [2026 review and citation caveat](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#respiratory-biomarkers-and-swallowing-in-a-congress-review)

## Hiroshima swallowing tests with measured water volumes

Japan’s trial registry first posted `jRCTs062220013` on May 9, 2022. The open-label, single-arm study enrolled 27 people with Parkinson’s at Hoehn–Yahr stages 2–4. Two withdrew within four weeks. The November 7, 2023 analysis of 25 completers used a 3 mL water bolus with 30% barium before and after eight weeks of cervical interferential current stimulation. Oral residue was present in 22 of 25 at baseline, 14 at week 8 (p = .012), and 19 at week 16 (p = .270 versus baseline). Penetration or aspiration was present in 10, nine, and 11 participants at those visits, respectively. The study used the same bolus at each visit and had no comparison group. [Trial registry and participant flow](https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/en-latest-detail/jRCTs062220013) · [Published protocol, June 10, 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37342176/) · [Primary November report, Methods and Table 2](https://doi.org/10.3389/fneur.2023.1279161) · [Local November article text](Source-Text/PMC10664245.txt)

A December 6, 2023 baseline-only article from the same registration used 3 and 10 mL water-barium boluses in all 27 enrollees. Penetration or aspiration occurred in 11 of 27 for 3 mL and 14 of 27 for 10 mL. The authors found no significant difference in those rates and reported more participants above the laryngeal elevation delay threshold with 10 mL (14 versus five, p = .010). Higher baseline motor rigidity was associated with penetration or aspiration for the 10 mL bolus (OR 1.294, 95% CI 1.035–1.617). The 2024 primary cough report from the same 25 completers used inhaled nebulized 1% citric acid as the cough provocation. Normal cough responses increased from four to 15 of 25 at week 8, and the videofluoroscopic penetration or aspiration rate remained similar. The exposure under study was electrical stimulation. The water, contrast medium and citric acid served as measurement challenges. [Primary December report, Tables 2–3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10072-023-07238-1) · [Local December article text](Source-Text/PMC11021328.txt) · [Primary 2024 cough report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11102488/) · [Local 2024 article text](Source-Text/PMC11102488.txt) · [2026 age subgroup and MDS abstract](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#age-subgroup-of-the-hiroshima-water-bolus-cohort)

The same research group summarized the improved cough response and lower oral residue in 2024 MDS abstract `655`. That abstract again describes 25 participants with the same protocol. [2024 MDS abstract 655](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/cervical-percutaneous-interferential-current-stimulation-improves-cough-reflex-and-oral-residue-in-patients-with-parkinsons-disease/) · [Local protocol text](Source-Text/PMC10277457.txt)

## Oven-baked potato wedges in an observed Parkinson’s meal

Forsberg’s University of Copenhagen thesis, defended June 8, 2022, and the same study’s journal report, published online May 31, 2024, describe six participants observed at two lunches. The sole participant with Parkinson’s disease was a man with advanced disease living at home. His first lunch included boiled potatoes, pork chops, brown sauce and jam. His second included oven-baked potato wedges, beef rolls, other vegetables, sauce and flatbread. The thesis gives 114 g as the served portion of baked potatoes and vegetables combined, with the wedges cut to require two or three bites. The journal’s Methods section lists the man’s age as 89, while Table 1 lists 79 for the same participant. [2022 thesis, pp. 47–50 and Paper V](https://researchportal.hkr.se/ws/portalfiles/portal/45429347/PhD_Thesis_Sarah_Forsberg_2022.pdf#page=47) · [2024 report, Methods, Tables 1–2 and Figure 2](https://researchportal.hkr.se/ws/files/79817276/Sarah_Finger_Food_Meals_as_a_Means_of_Improving_Published.pdf#page=5) · [Publication record, PMID 38819410](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38819410/)

The observer considered chewing, coughing and residue. Participant 1’s account describes prolonged chewing without apparent food accumulation during the regular meal, then dry beef and use of dipping sauce during the finger-food meal. The authors report that he could handle and eat components independently, but do not document the amount of potato he ate or a swallowing outcome tied to a specific potato bite. Several foods and aspects of meal service changed between lunches, and there was no instrumental swallowing assessment or separate potato comparison. The thesis reports photographing plates before and after meals. The journal says the underlying participant data are unavailable because participants did not consent to public sharing. ## Asparagus avoidance in Parkinson’s family focus groups

In a separate Forsberg study published April 18, 2022, two digital focus groups included five spouses of people with Parkinson’s and one person with Parkinson’s. Four participants reported severe chewing and swallowing difficulty in their spouses. They described avoiding asparagus and green beans because of their stringy texture and avoiding some thick-skinned tomatoes because of perceived choking risk. Participants preferred baking or blanching carrots, broccoli and cauliflower to make them more tender.
