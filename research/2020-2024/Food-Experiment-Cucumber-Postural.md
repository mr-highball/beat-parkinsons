# Cucumber and postural instability

## 2020 Cucumber in vegetarian Parkinson intervention

Hegelmaier et al., 2020, PMID 32041265, PMCID PMC7072275, DOI 10.3390/cells9020376, provided a 14-day ovo-lacto vegetarian diet to 16 people with Parkinson disease; 10 also received bowel cleansing. Table 3 lists cucumber among many meal ingredients, without individual cucumber consumption, amount or raw preparation. The original UPDRS III includes posture and postural stability, but results report total motor scale and no separately estimated postural-instability item. The mixed regimen and enema cannot isolate an effect of cucumber on posture. The same menu lists spinach alongside other vegetables and pumpkin seed oil alongside other oils. It gives no separate facial-expression or rigidity item outcome, oil processing or spinach preparation.
