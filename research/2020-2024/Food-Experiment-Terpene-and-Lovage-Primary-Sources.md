# Terpene and lovage primary sources, 2020-2024

## Injected beta-myrcene and barbiturate sleep in model mice

Chen and colleagues injected isolated beta-myrcene in a mouse model made with DL-4-chlorophenylalanine. In Table 1 the high-dose group was scored asleep in 12 of 12 after one dose and 11 of 12 after seven days, versus 1 of 12 model controls at each time. Sleep was induced with a barbiturate. The authors also observed suppressed activity, which complicates interpretation of sedation and sleep behavior. The article gives no clinical insomnia estimate and tested neither lemongrass nor other culinary intake.
