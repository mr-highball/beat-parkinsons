# Parkinson-related clinical records from recovered JSPEN supplements

## Oral feeding and survival after gastrostomy

Tomita and colleagues reviewed records of 91 deceased patients meeting UK Brain Bank Parkinson disease criteria. Thirteen continued oral feeding until death. Among those who stopped eating by mouth, median survival was 27.6 months for 30 patients receiving gastrostomy feeds, 6.3 months for 14 receiving nasogastric feeds and 0.8 months for 34 receiving intravenous nutrition alone. The 2020 abstract groups feeding routes separately and appears to reanalyze the 91-patient Utano dataset reported in 2018. The groups differed across presentations. The comparison is retrospective and specifies no foods or formula compositions. [2020 S-10-10, printed p. 163](https://web.archive.org/web/20220728002617id_/https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/2/Supplement1/2_91/_pdf/-char/ja#page=73) · [Earlier Utano records](../2015-2019/Food-Experiment-NHO-Middle.md#2018-and-2019-tube-feeding-after-oral-intake-ceased)

At Higashiomi General Medical Center, Shiraishi and colleagues reported that two of ten Parkinson patients continued eating orally alongside gastrostomy in a series spanning April 2014 through March 2019. Later reports from the center extended observation through 2020 and then March 2023. The 2024 abstract covers 201 gastrostomies, including 38 patients with progressive neurologic disease. It reports continued oral intake in 56% of this pooled subgroup and oral intake and return-home rates of 40% or less in Parkinson and progressive supranuclear palsy. It does not give exact Parkinson-specific denominators or identify foods. [2020 S-18-4, printed p. 215](https://web.archive.org/web/20220728002617id_/https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/2/Supplement1/2_91/_pdf/-char/ja#page=125) · [2021 report](Food-Experiment-JSPEN-Modern.md#oral-intake-alongside-gastrostomy) · [2024 MO-16-3, printed p. 739](https://web.archive.org/web/20250323185652id_/https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/6/Supplement1/6_568/_pdf/-char/ja#page=172) · [Hospital's record of the February 16 presentation](https://higashiomi.hosp.go.jp/files/gyoseki05.pdf)

## Oral intake after pneumonia

Tazumi and colleagues reviewed 17 Okazaki City Hospital inpatients with parkinsonism, pneumonia and severe dysphagia between June 2018 and May 2019. Seven resumed oral intake. The report describes medication and nutrition routes without listing specific foods. It likely overlaps the same hospital's 11 Parkinson-only participants after a specialist-referral pathway in the 2021 journal paper, with identical dates and seven people eating orally at discharge. The team's 136-patient journal cohort instead used 2015–2017 admissions. [2020 S-10-9, printed p. 162](https://web.archive.org/web/20220728002617id_/https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/2/Supplement1/2_91/_pdf/-char/ja#page=72) · [University presentation record](https://web.db.tokushima-u.ac.jp/cgi-bin/edb_browse?ACT=BROWSE&EID=368125) · [Prior journal findings](Food-Experiment-JSPEN-Citation-Followup.md#three-oral-meals-after-pneumonia-and-neurology-referral)

## Named formula and gel hydration by gastrostomy

Shinohara reported an older man with parkinsonism and advanced dementia whose care team changed his gastrostomy regimen from an unnamed liquid formula to Medief Pushcare 400 kcal/160 g plus PG Water EJ. The abstract reports no reflux or diarrhea during the new regimen and says it was easier and faster for caregivers to administer. The manufacturer lists milk and soy protein, dietary fiber, medium-chain triglycerides, vegetable and fish oils in the formula. The contemporaneous sheet does not identify the particular vegetable oil, while a 2025 sheet lists soybean and rapeseed oil. PG Water EJ is a gelled hydration product containing glucose and dextrin. The patient had no identified idiopathic Parkinson diagnosis, and the report gives no oral exposure or isolated ingredient effect. [2024 MO-01-5, printed p. 572](https://web.archive.org/web/20250323185652id_/https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/6/Supplement1/6_568/_pdf/-char/ja#page=5) · [Contemporaneous formula sheet](https://www.nestlehealthscience.jp/sites/default/files/2024-08/medif-mpc.pdf) · [2025 formula sheet](https://www.eiyounet.nestlehealthscience.jp/sites/default/files/products-pdf/medif-mpc.pdf) · [Hydration label](https://www.nutri.co.jp/products/pg/index.html)

Miyazaki and Katsura reported continued Racol semisolid gastrostomy nutrition in a 79-year-old woman with an unspecified Parkinson-related illness. A replacement was considered because of prescribing arrangements but never given. Her caregiver's anxiety eased after the care team agreed to continue the original product. No patient motor, swallowing or gastrointestinal comparison was reported. The regulatory ingredient table identifies soybean, shiso and palm oils in this formula. This same product appears in the earlier Takahashi Parkinson case alongside concurrent intestinal levodopa treatment. [2024 R-07-4, printed p. 271](https://web.archive.org/web/20250323185149id_/https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/6/Supplement1/6_229/_pdf/-char/ja#page=43) · [Japanese regulator, Table 2](../2010-2014/Food-Experiment-JSPEN-Followup.md#racol-nf-semisolid-formula-composition) · [Earlier Parkinson case](Food-Experiment-JSPEN-Citation-Followup.md#formula-choice-during-continuous-intestinal-levodopa)

## Blended meals after aspiration-prevention surgery

Takahashi and colleagues described an 87-year-old man with Parkinson disease and previous gastrectomy who developed aspiration pneumonia while receiving nasogastric feeds. He resumed unspecified blended meals on hospital day 49 after surgery to prevent aspiration, and later met his nutrition needs orally during multidisciplinary rehabilitation. The report gives no named food or measured texture-specific effect. [2024 MO-17-5, printed p. 753](https://web.archive.org/web/20250323185652id_/https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ejspen/6/Supplement1/6_568/_pdf/-char/ja#page=186) · [JST bibliography](https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=202402263386938964)
