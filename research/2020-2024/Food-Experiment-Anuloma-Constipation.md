# Constipation studies using the cumin-containing Anuloma DS tablet

## Single-arm functional constipation study

Varuni's original July 11, 2023 report followed 30 adults taking a tablet that included common cumin, senna, ajmoda, haritaki, licorice, ginger and rock salt. Table 2 reports a mean spontaneous bowel movement score of 1.20 (SD 0.41) at day seven and 2.20 (SD 0.76) at day 30, with p<.0001 for the reported comparison. The earliest tabulated value is already after treatment started. The study used no comparator, excluded neurologic disease and gave no levodopa or motor outcome. [Original Methods and Table 2](https://www.ijcp.in/Pages/Post_Detail.aspx?wid=20623)

## Comparative constipation study and duplicate publications

The earliest verified report appeared on August 23, 2024. The December 2024 and February 2025 publisher copies contain the same arms, sample and tables. The latter has a different DOI. Keep these publications as one evidence family. The reported comparator includes both lactitol and ispaghula, and the preparations were taken with different liquids. At day 15 the Constipation Assessment Scale favored the tablet descriptively. The stool-frequency comparison in Table 3 was p=.08. The table and narrative differ on the strength of that outcome. [August original](https://doi.org/10.59793/ijcp.v35i3.1087) · [December publisher PDF](https://ijcp.in/Admin/CMS/PDF/05_Clinical-Study-Anuloma-DS%20_CLG02.pdf) · [February duplicate](https://doi.org/10.59793/51tyzs05) · [Factual extract](Source-Text/2024-Rakesh-Anuloma-Constipation-Comparison.txt)

CTRI/2022/07/044510 is a plausible registration match. An indexed copy of the registry and the report match the center, comparator, intended sample of 60 and 15-day design. The primary article does not identify the registration. The official registry body, allocation process, and participant linkage remain open. The single-arm 2023 article has a different design, schedule and sample.
