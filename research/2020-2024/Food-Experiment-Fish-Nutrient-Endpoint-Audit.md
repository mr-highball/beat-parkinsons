# Vitamin D3 trial registration and preprint in Parkinson disease

## Vitamin D3 protocol in Parkinson disease with vitamin D deficiency

NCT06539260 first appeared August 6, 2024 after its recorded July 1 completion. The public protocol lists motor and balance scales, cognitive measures and separate self-rated depression and anxiety scales among secondary outcomes. One registry description calls the comparison untreated, while the intervention field specifies a placebo. The current record is completed and has no results table. [Local official fields](Source-Text/Trials/NCT06539260.txt) · [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06539260)

## First public vitamin D3 preprint

The December 16, 2024 preprint describes the same registered cohort as the July 2025 journal report. It compares oral supplemental vitamin D3 with placebo in 30 people with Parkinson disease and low vitamin D. The intervention is an isolated nutrient. Neither the preprint nor the journal report supplies separate changes by arm for anxiety or depression. [Local preprint extract](Source-Text/2024-Li-Parkinson-Vitamin-D-Preprint-Extract.txt) · [Original preprint](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-5145248/v1) · [2025 journal results](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Fish-Nutrient-Endpoint-Audit.md)
