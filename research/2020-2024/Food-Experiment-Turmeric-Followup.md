# Turmeric preparation and symptom outcomes

## Motor and nonmotor Parkinson scale coverage

The 2022 Ghodsi trial tested nanomicelle curcumin with dopaminergic medication. Its abstract reports an inconclusive visit-level MDS-UPDRS and PDQ-39 comparison, despite a Part III group-by-time result of p = .04. The accessible abstract and IRCT protocol provide no standalone results for speech, handwriting, freezing, falls, attention, daytime sleepiness, or time in the OFF state. The scale instrument includes individual speech, handwriting, freezing, daytime sleepiness, and OFF-time items. A composite change does not reveal individual items or hours. The article’s full tables were unavailable in this pass. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35636380/) · [Local registration and results trail](Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#nanomicelle-curcumin-as-an-add-on) · [Original scale](https://www.movementdisorders.org/MDS-Files1/Resources/PDFs/MDS-UPDRS.pdf)

Donadio and colleagues’ phospholipid curcuminoid cohort separately reported OFF-state motor examinations and composite nonmotor outcomes. No separate smell test, freezing count, attention test, speech measurement, or daily OFF hours appear in its full results tables. The two groups selected different medication strategies. [Primary full report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9210322/) · [Dated cohort note](Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#phospholipid-curcumin-and-composite-outcomes)

## Turmeric extract and sleep in mice

Um and colleagues reported that an oral turmeric extract shortened sleep latency and increased non-REM sleep in mice. The 2021 laboratory study used electroencephalography, and the paper reports a role for histamine H1 receptor blockade. It studied extracted material and acute mouse sleep outcomes without a daytime sleepiness score or Parkinson model. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34003596/)

## Two olfactory recovery cases after turmeric extract

Chabot and Huntwork reported two COVID-19 cases in 2021. The first person reported smell and taste returning ten minutes after a single turmeric-extract capsule with black pepper extract. The second took turmeric extract with black pepper and *Boswellia serrata* extracts and reported gradual improvement. Both used 95%-curcuminoid turmeric extract and gave subjective ratings. The uncontrolled cases leave spontaneous recovery and co-interventions unresolved. [Primary case report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8502749/) · [Local CC BY article text](Source-Text/PMC8502749.txt)

## Posted curcumin walking and cognitive outcomes

NCT03085680 first posted results on February 2, 2022. After 12 weeks, mean 400-m walking speed changes were +0.014 m/s (SD 0.090) in the nine-person curcumin arm and +0.103 m/s (SD 0.100) in the eight-person placebo arm. The registry labels a cognitive outcome attention and memory, describes it as the full MMSE, and reports mean changes of +0.33 and −2.75 points, respectively. It posts no statistical comparison for either outcome and reports no adverse events in either arm. The article first published July 6, 2022 and describes the same 17-person pilot as a curcumin C3 Complex study in older adults with low-grade inflammation. It provides no counts of falls or freezing episodes. [Official posted results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03085680?tab=results) · [Local registry text](../2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT03085680.txt) · [Primary article, first online July 6, 2022](https://link.springer.com/article/10.14283/jfa.2022.47) · [2023 placebo erratum](https://doi.org/10.14283/jfa.2023.26)

## Curry intake and longitudinal cognitive scores

Ng and colleagues analyzed habitual curry intake and cognitive testing in 2,734 older community-dwelling Singaporeans. Frequent intake was associated with higher adjusted backward digit span scores overall (p = .012), and the group-by-time term was inconclusive (p = .849). Forward digit span was inconclusive. Curry frequency is a mixed-food proxy and the cohort did not give a fresh turmeric or Parkinson-specific estimate. [2022 primary report, Table 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8952785/) · [Local CC BY article text](Source-Text/PMC8952785.txt)

## Curcumin formulation and automated gait in mice

The 2022 MPTP study compared oral purified curcumin in saline suspension with an oil-based curcumin formulation and vehicle controls. The oil formulation improved more footprint contact and stance parameters. It also improved rotarod distance relative to the MPTP group (p = .0014), while the saline suspension comparison was inconclusive for that outcome. Automated paw stance and contact measures are distinct from clinical freezing episodes. [Primary report, Figure 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9372469/) · [Local CC BY article text](Source-Text/PMC9372469.txt)

## Curcumin with levodopa in a rodent motor model

Abdel-Salam and colleagues studied curcumin, levodopa and rasagiline alone and in combinations in a 2023 rotenone experiment. Several measures of rearing, suspension and stair climbing improved relative to rotenone controls. Time to cross a wooden beam did not clearly improve with any treatment. The experiments did not track levodopa wearing-off or count gait freezing. [Primary report, Figure 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9869885/) · [Local CC BY article text](Source-Text/PMC9869885.txt)

## Curcumin and olfactory testing in chronic kidney disease

Malekmakan and colleagues’ March 2023 three-arm trial randomized patients to curcumin, olfactory training, or placebo for 12 weeks, with 58 participants reported in Table 2. Curcumin increased the mean Iran Smell Identification Test score from 14.0 to 14.7, an inconclusive within-group difference (p = .066). The authors found greater improvement with training and report a curcumin within-group change on an olfactory quality-of-life questionnaire (p = .045). The study compares isolated oral curcumin in chronic kidney disease. [Primary report, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10031463/)

## Curcumin and Epworth daytime sleepiness outside Parkinson’s

The 2023 Iranian randomized trial assigned 124 students with premenstrual syndrome and dysmenorrhea to curcumin or placebo across three cycles. Both groups took piperine. The between-group post-treatment Epworth Sleepiness Scale score was inconclusive (p = .83), and the difference in the proportion improving in sleepiness severity was also inconclusive (24.6% vs 18.3%, p = .52). The article abbreviates primary dysmenorrhea as PD. Participants did not have Parkinson’s disease. [Primary report, Tables 1–2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10719725/) · [Local CC BY article text](Source-Text/PMC10719725.txt)

## Plant extract cognition trial registration

UMIN000054532 first posted May 31, 2024, with a target of 40 participants. The primary endpoint was standardized composite memory at 24 weeks. Secondary measures included MoCA-J, the MIRUDAKE eye-tracking test and CogEvo. The registry refers to test food containing plant extracts and records no posted results. The publication identifies the exposure as a mixture of *Curcuma longa* extracts. [Official registry](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000062302) · [Local registry text](Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000054532.txt) · [2026 results](../2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Turmeric-Followup.md#turmeric-extract-and-eye-tracking-attention)

## Lipidated curcumin and daytime dysfunction or divided attention

Cox and colleagues randomized 80 healthy adults aged 50–80 to 12 weeks of Longvida or placebo. The article reports no clear PSQI treatment differences in the ANOVAs. A separate 12-week chi-square analysis of change direction on the PSQI daytime-dysfunction component favored curcumin (n=76, p=.018). The author supplement, Table S12, reports 12-week worsening in 2.6% with curcumin and 23.7% with placebo. Improvement was 2.6% and 5.3%, respectively, and the largest separation was the placebo group deterioration. Four weeks gave p=.566. The component covers daytime function and does not give a separate sleepiness item or Epworth estimate. A divided-attention recognition-deficit result was marginal before adjustment (p=.050) and after additional adjustment (p=.060). Arrow Flanker reaction times showed no direct treatment effect. Formulation, population and outcome scope limit transfer to grated turmeric in Parkinson disease. [Original source](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/12/6/1678) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2020-Cox-Longvida-PSQI-Daytime-Dysfunction.txt)

## Turmeric extract pain formula with planned PSQI daytime dysfunction

First posted 2024-02-08. The biomarker-selected inflammation formula for chronic pain contains turmeric extract 400 mg, black-pepper preparation 10 mg, ashwagandha 500 mg and olive-leaf extract 250 mg. Sleep is a secondary outcome, including the PSQI daytime dysfunction component. No separate Epworth comparison, Parkinson cohort, or grated-turmeric-only arm is specified. Registry status was unknown and no results were posted as captured 2026-11-16. The other biomarker-directed combinations do not furnish a randomized isolated turmeric comparison.
