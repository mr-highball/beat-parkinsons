# Nutmeg reports and tremor phenotyping

## Agitated behavior in an accidental nutmeg case

January 6, 2020. Duarte and colleagues report an adult who described a large nutmeg exposure before disorganized speech, agitation and aimless running. The case resolved after observation and treatment. The product grind is not specified, and the clinical narrative gives no tremor measurement or posture. Agitated running is a different observation from rest tremor. This remains an acute individual safety context. ## French original reports and clinical examination limits

Gavroy's 2022 issue article describes occasional unsteadiness on examination after an excess ground-nutmeg exposure, with no focal deficit or reported tremor. It does not assess a Parkinson rest-tremor outcome. The original examines this episode in a single adult. The current distributor lists a 2025 online posting for the 2022 journal issue. [Original article pp. 119–121](https://stm.cairn.info/article/FMU_122_0119/pdf?lang=fr) · [Local factual text](Source-Text/2022-Gavroy-Ground-Nutmeg-Clinical-Extract.txt)

The corrected proof of Bardèche-Trystram and colleagues' report was available November 26, 2023. The accessible case excerpt describes confusion and hallucinations after intentional excess powdered nutmeg with alcohol and medications. The publisher's full case is restricted. The later congress abstract describes the same presentation and differs in a medication name. The pair is one apparent case family with unresolved medication details and no verified resting-tremor examination.
