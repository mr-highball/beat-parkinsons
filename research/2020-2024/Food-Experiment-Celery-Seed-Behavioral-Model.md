## Celery seed extract and conflicting dose labels in mice

The laboratory paper measured locomotion, stereotypy and social interaction after an extract of dried celery seeds in alcohol-exposed mice. The protocol and outcome tables disagree on both group doses. No visual hallucination count or dried-leaf exposure was reported.
