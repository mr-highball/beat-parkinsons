Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked celery stalk reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: In the MPTP Parkinson's mouse model (Chonpathompikunlert et al., 2018, PMC5859653, n=48), oral whole-plant Apium graveolens methanolic extract (250 and 375 mg/kg x 21 d) directly attenuated motor slowness/bradykinesia across narrow-beam crossing latency (Fig 3a, p<0.05 days 3 and 7), swimming disability score (Fig 4b, p<0.05), and grid-walk stepping (Fig 2, p<0.05) while preserving SNpc TH+ neurons (excluding Fig 5 rotarod due to text inversion). Viña & Chaves (2007) verify flavones (apigenin/luteolin) in celery petioles. Accounting for both whole-plant methanolic extraction and thermal cooking differences—while strictly excluding celery seed extracts and synthetic NBP—this supports only a minimal directional lean (Maybe True, 50.005).
