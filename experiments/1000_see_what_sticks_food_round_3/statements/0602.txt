Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: decaffeinated
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming decaffeinated coffee worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial evaluates decaffeinated coffee on Parkinson's bradykinesia severity; isolated caffeine PD motor trials (Postuma 2012/2017) cannot transfer to decaffeinated coffee, UK Biobank (Zhang 2024) found decaf null for PD risk, and preclinical EHT studies (Lee 2011; Yan 2018) show non-caffeine constituents require synergy or supraphysiologic dosing.
