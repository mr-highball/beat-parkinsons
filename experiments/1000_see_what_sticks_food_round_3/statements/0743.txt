ID: 0743
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): controlled older-adult trials directly testing fresh dairy milk or milk protein show null gait-speed effects—Huschtscha 2021 (n=37 older adults 2x2 RCT) found 500 mL/d fresh dairy milk did NOT change 4-m gait speed versus control or resistance training alone, Zhu 2015 (n=196 older women 2-yr whey RCT) was null on TUG, and Dewansingh 2018 (36-RCT MA) found dairy protein null on physical performance—with no Parkinson's plain-milk gait-speed trial.
