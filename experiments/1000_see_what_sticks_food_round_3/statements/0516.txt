Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked clams reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human clinical trials, PD observational studies, or targeted animal experiments evaluate cooked clams for muscle rigidity severity in Parkinson's disease. Existing clam research centers on freshwater Corbicula fluminea extracts for liver enzymes, sleep (Shimazoe 2022), rodent exhaustion (Huang 2013), and MPTP mouse locomotion (Hsieh 2017), alongside shellfish protein/LNAA composition (Tabakaeva 2018), vitamin B12 nutrition (Venugopal 2017), and contaminant/allergy safety (heavy metals PMC4427851; tropomyosin Khora 2016; domoic acid PMC13302463). East Asian shijimi traditions (Kuwano 2026) do not test muscle rigidity severity, leaving this claim Unknown (Researched).
