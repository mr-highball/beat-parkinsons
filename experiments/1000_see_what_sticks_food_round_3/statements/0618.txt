Statement: Consuming decaffeinated coffee reduces olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial evaluates decaffeinated coffee on Parkinson's olfactory impairment; in a crossover trial in healthy adults (Han 2020, n=49), acute olfactory threshold, discrimination, and identification did not differ between caffeinated and decaffeinated coffee, and no study compares decaffeinated coffee against a non-coffee control for hyposmia.
