Symptom: dystonia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked chicken worsens dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial or cohort study has found that baked chicken worsens dystonia in Parkinson's disease. Although grilled/barbecued chicken contains detectable harmane (Louis 2007), a dedicated case-control study (Louis 2014, 104 dystonia cases vs 107 controls) explicitly found blood harmane was NULL in dystonia (adjusted OR 1.07, 95% CI 0.58-1.97, p=0.84).
