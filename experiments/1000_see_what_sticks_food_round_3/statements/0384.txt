Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted chicken worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial has isolated roasted chicken meat for levodopa-induced dyskinesia severity in Parkinson's disease. While evening protein redistribution can shift peak-dose dyskinesia timing in levodopa-treated patients (Carter 1989; Phillips 2018 mixed ketogenic/low-fat menus), dietary protein competes with rather than augments acute levodopa entry, and no trial links roasted chicken specifically to worsened dyskinesia.
