Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming baked chicken reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): Evidence for chicken extracts on attention is consistently null across the largest double-blind RCTs, and no trial has tested baked chicken meat in Parkinson's disease. Suttiwan 2018 (n=235) was null on Stroop and Sustained Attention to Response Task (SART), Chan 2016 (n=102) was null on Continuous Performance Test and Digit Vigilance, Wu 2020 (n=73) was null on CANTAB reaction time (p=0.145), Young 2015 (n=46) was null on Arrow Flankers, Yamano 2013 (n=20) showed virtually identical post-rest attention reaction times vs placebo (487.8 vs 489.0 ms), and the Charernboon 2016 meta-analysis pooled sustained attention as null (SMD -1.55, 95% CI -4.77 to 1.67).
