Statement: Consuming poached cod worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial or urology cohort links poached Atlantic cod fillet to worsened urinary urgency frequency. In the nationwide VA IC/BPS food-sensitivity cohort (Jarman 2023, N=425), non-spicy lean meats/fish (e.g., tuna 2% IC/BPS, 1% HC) were among the least bothersome items with no cohort difference (p>0.05), and the OAB bladder-irritants review (Ha 2025) does not implicate lean white fish.
