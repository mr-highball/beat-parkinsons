ID: 0584
Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: grilled
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grilled cod worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial has tested whether grilled Atlantic cod fillet alters fine hand dexterity in Parkinson's disease. In older adults, 12 months of cod protein hydrolysate showed null grip strength (Nygard 2021, n=86) and did not measure fine finger dexterity.
