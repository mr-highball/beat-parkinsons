ID: 0401
Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted chicken worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled videofluoroscopic or clinical dysphagia trial has tested whether boneless roasted chicken worsens Parkinson's swallowing difficulty. Emergency choking series (Ekberg 1992; Saccomanno 2023, n=138) document mechanical airway obstruction from meat bones/chicken bones (9% meat bones) or poorly chewed boluses rather than a physiological worsening of neurogenic dysphagia by roasted chicken meat, while Parkinson's UK forum contributors informally favor moist stews/soups over dry textures when swallowing is difficult.
