Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human trial tests raw culinary celery stalk for pain in Parkinson's disease. Existing human pain trials either evaluated celery SEED extracts (Hessami 2021; Vaidya 2025 with Boswellia; Nahid 2009 with saffron/anise) or uncontrolled 5-herb commercial blends (Żęgota 2021 Tregocel; Hedaya 2017), none of which isolate raw celery stalk. In rodents, Sukketsiri et al. (2016) measured hepatic oxidative enzymes rather than pain behavior in arthritic rats, and Atta & Alkofahi (1998) screened an ethanolic A. graveolens extract of unspecified plant part in non-PD acute writhing/hot-plate assays.
