ID: 0861
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: reduced facial expressiveness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise worsens reduced facial expressiveness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) worsens reduced facial expressiveness in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses reduced facial expressiveness; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
