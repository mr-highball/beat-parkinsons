Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No human clinical trial or animal paradigm has tested ground cayenne pepper for freezing of gait (FOG) in Parkinson's disease. Preclinical PD rodent studies (Ogunruku 2019; Wei 2025; Zhao 2017) assessed general rearing, open-field distance, and rotarod balance rather than episodic gait freezing, and the 12-week Capsifen 6-minute walk test in obese non-PD adults (Thornton 2026) had a severe baseline-swap artifact and no FOG assessment.
