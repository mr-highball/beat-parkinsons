Category: herb
Ingredient: culinary lavender
Preparation: dried
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried culinary lavender worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether dried culinary lavender worsens olfactory impairment severity in Parkinson's disease because PD lavender literature tests scent discrimination diagnostically (PMID:38402774) rather than oral consumption, and an open-label oral lavender syrup trial in acute COVID-19 anosmia (PMID:37663382) does not transfer to neurodegenerative PD hyposmia.
