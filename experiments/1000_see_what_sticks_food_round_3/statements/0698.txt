Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Unknown (open): zero PD clinical trials isolate refined corn oil on levodopa absorption or daily OFF time; Chagas 2014 used corn oil strictly as a shared CBD carrier without OFF-time endpoints, and Kashima 2021 contrasted thermally oxidized heated corn oil against unheated corn oil on gastric emptying in healthy adults.
