Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked celery stalk reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical trial tests whether cooked celery stalk eases or worsens constipation in Parkinson's disease. Traditional Italian and regional ethnobotany note general digestive/diuretic culinary uses of aerial celery (Chiocchio 2024; Nugraha/Samaha record), whereas a Parkinson's UK forum conversation specifically lists cooked celery among fibrous vegetables advised against during PD gastroparesis and severe bloating. Celery seed extracts and NBP drugs are strictly separated.
