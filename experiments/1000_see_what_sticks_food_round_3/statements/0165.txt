ID: 0165
Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (50.005): Six independent double-blind oral capsaicin or encapsulated chili crossover RCTs in healthy adults report reduced exercise rating of perceived exertion (RPE), attenuated neuromuscular potentiated-twitch fatigue, or prolonged time to exhaustion (de Freitas 2018a/2018b/2019/2022; Giuriato 2022; Roopashree 2024), consistent with a 21-trial acute exercise meta-analysis (Huang 2026, g=0.20, p<0.01). The signal is strictly minimal and heavily tempered because five competing oral trials were null—including 3 g/day whole ground cayenne capsules over 7 days with 6.3x GI distress (Opheim & Rankin 2012), chronic 12-week Capsifen on POMS Fatigue (Thornton 2026, p=0.240), and three acute capsaicin crossovers (Padilha 2020; de Salles Painelli 2026; Melo 2026)—with zero Parkinson's disease fatigue trials.
