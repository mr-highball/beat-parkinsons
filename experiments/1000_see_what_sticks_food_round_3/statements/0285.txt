Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no completed clinical trial or mammalian Parkinson's lesion model tests brewed chamomile tea on rest tremor severity; registered PD trial NCT05696665 bundles chamomile with saffron with no posted results, Shabir 2022 tested invertebrate rotenone Drosophila climbing/jumping, and Dalui 2025 tested non-PD restraint-stress mice rather than dopaminergic parkinsonism.
