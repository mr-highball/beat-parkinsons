Category: food
Ingredient: cashew
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted cashew reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): oral whole cashew nut kernels (100 mg/kg) reduced mechanical allodynia and thermal hyperalgesia in rat osteoarthritis (Fusco 2020, n=20) and acute carrageenan inflammation (Cordaro 2020, n=20, same laboratory), supported by CNSL anacardic-acid antinociception in mice (Gomes Junior 2020) and traditional Iranian kernel use, while zero human clinical pain trials, raw-vs-roasted differences, and one extreme-intake eosinophilia-myalgia case keep the signal minimal.
