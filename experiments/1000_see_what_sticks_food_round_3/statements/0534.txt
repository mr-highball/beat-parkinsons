ID: 0534
Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human levodopa-induced dyskinesia trial exists for ground clove, and animal eugenol PD models show conflicting timing-dependent effects—including worsened striatal dopamine loss and motor deficits when eugenol is given AFTER dopaminergic lesioning—leaving dyskinesia effects unresolved.
