ID: 0124
Category: food
Ingredient: cashew
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted cashew reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial or Parkinson's gait study tests roasted cashew nuts on walking speed; oral cashew kernels improved sciatic functional index in knee-osteoarthritis rats secondary to joint pain relief (Fusco 2020) and anacardic acids prevented rotenone open-field hypokinesia (Medeiros-Linard 2018), neither of which measures Parkinson's gait velocity.
