Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No human clinical trial or Parkinson's rigidity/catalepsy assay has tested whether ground cayenne pepper worsens or eases muscle rigidity. Preclinical PD models of Capsicum extract or capsaicin (Ogunruku 2019; Wei 2025; Zhao 2017) measured rearing, open-field locomotion, or rotarod balance without muscular tone or rigidity scoring, and TRPV1 dyskinesia pharmacology (Gonzalez-Aparicio 2014) evaluated L-DOPA-induced involuntary movements rather than cogwheel rigidity.
