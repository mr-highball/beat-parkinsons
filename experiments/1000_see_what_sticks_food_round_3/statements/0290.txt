Ingredient: chamomile

Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial evaluates oral brewed chamomile tea on urinary urgency frequency; Sharifi 2017 (n=80) reduced pediatric nocturnal/daytime enuresis using TOPICAL chamomile oil rather than oral tea, and Parkinson's UK forum posts explicitly flag bedtime herbal tea fluid volume as a potential nocturia trade-off.
