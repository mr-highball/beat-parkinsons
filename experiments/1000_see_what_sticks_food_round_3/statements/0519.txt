Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked clams worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for cooked clams affecting pain severity is Unknown (Researched). Huang et al. 2023 (PMID:38136188, PMC10740935) tested freshwater clam extract ONLY bundled with red seaweed (Sarcodia montagneana) in mouse osteoarthritis (preventing standalone clam attribution), while Yeh et al. 2017 (PMID:29201211, PMC5704299) measured post-fracture inflammatory cytokines in rats without behavioral nociception or pain outcomes, and local PMC7765232 reports trace endogenous PEA chemistry only. Zero clinical pain trials exist for cooked clams.
