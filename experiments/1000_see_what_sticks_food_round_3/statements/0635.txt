Statement: Consuming cooked collard greens reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for cooked collard greens reducing constipation severity in PD is Unknown (Researched). Nutritional profiling shows collard greens supply dietary fiber (~3.39 g/100 g fresh; Canazza 2024) and steamed collards exhibit in vitro bile-acid binding (Kahlon 2008), alongside prominent cultural culinary consumption across the US South (Yang 2013) and East Africa (sukuma wiki, Ikuomola 2025). However, zero human or animal trials have tested cooked collard greens on stool frequency, stool consistency, or bowel transit in Parkinson's or non-PD constipation.
