Ingredient: cashew
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted cashew reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): oral cashew-derived anacardic acids (10-100 mg/kg/d) dose-dependently prevented rotenone-induced rotarod balance and coordination deficits in a rat Parkinson's model (Medeiros-Linard 2018), backed by nigrostriatal TH preservation (Augusto 2020) and chemical quantification of anacardic acids in processed edible cashew nuts (Mattison 2018), though isolated constituent dosing and zero human PD balance trials keep inference minimal.
