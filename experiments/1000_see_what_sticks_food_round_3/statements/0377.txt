Statement: Consuming roasted chicken reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial has tested whether roasted chicken reduces bradykinesia in Parkinson's disease. While steamed chicken breast plus resistance training improved muscle strength in non-PD adults (Uchida 2024) and acute chicken broth improved cycling power in healthy men (Barbaresi 2021), neither evaluates parkinsonian motor slowness, and high-protein chicken meals taken alongside levodopa can instead compete with levodopa transport (Uhe 1992).
