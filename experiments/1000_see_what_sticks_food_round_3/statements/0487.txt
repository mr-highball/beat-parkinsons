Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A double-blind older-adult trial found 2 g oral ground cinnamon had a null effect on working memory (Lee 2014), with no human Parkinson's executive-function RCTs available; executive function effects remain unresolved.
