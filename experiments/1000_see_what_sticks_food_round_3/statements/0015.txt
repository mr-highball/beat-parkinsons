Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No reliable evidence links raw cantaloupe to worsening or easing Parkinson's insomnia. In Milesi et al. (2009, n=70), although day-28 transverse FARD sleep-trouble scores differed (p=0.026), baseline scores were already lower on Extramel (1.28 vs 1.94) and within-group day-0 to day-28 change was non-significant in both arms (p=0.110 vs p=0.230). In the UK Women's Cohort Study (Noorwali et al., 2018, n=12,478), melon intake showed a null association with sleep duration (-1 min/d, p=0.2).
