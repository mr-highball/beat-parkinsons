Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: decaffeinated
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming decaffeinated coffee reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): randomized surgical and colonic-manometry trials directly testing decaffeinated coffee against water show accelerated bowel recovery and colonic motility—Dulskas 2015 (n=90 colectomy RCT: decaf first bowel movement 3.00 vs 4.14 d water, p<0.05), Rao 1998 (n=12 crossover: decaf increased colonic pressure waves p<0.01 and propagated contractions p<0.05), Brown 1990 (n=14: decaf increased rectosigmoid motility p<0.05 in responders), and Watanabe 2021 MA (13 RCTs showing no difference between caffeinated and decaf coffee). Tempered by a null colectomy RCT (Nasseri 2023, n=99, p=0.53), null NHANES decaf constipation association (Kong 2024, n=7,844), and absence of PD trials.
