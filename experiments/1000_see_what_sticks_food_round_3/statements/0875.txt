Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: ground
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cumin reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The sole true Cuminum cyminum human cognitive RCT (Ito 2026, PMC13135935 Table 4) showed completely null effects on Executive Function (p=0.629) and Cognitive Flexibility (p=0.793); preclinical mouse and rat memory studies do not establish human executive-function benefit in Parkinson's disease.
