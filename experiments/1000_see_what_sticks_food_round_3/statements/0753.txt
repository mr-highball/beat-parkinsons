ID: 0753
Category: food
Ingredient: cranberry
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked cranberry worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): three cranberry DB-RCTs found completely null executive-function contrasts—COMBAT executive composite p=0.430 and TMT p=0.639 (Flanagan 2022, n=60), null TMT/Stroop (Crews 2005, n=50), and null CogniFit reasoning (CRANMOOD 2026, n=72)—while selected episodic/short-term memory subtests improved in non-PD adults without cooked-cranberry testing.
