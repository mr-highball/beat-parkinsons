Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical evidence indicates raw celery stalk worsens or eases anxiety in Parkinson's disease. Direct human data on raw celery in Brookie et al. (2018, n=422) were completely null for anxiety (r=+0.008, NS), and the Shayani Rad et al. (2023) BAI anxiety trial evaluated concentrated celery SEED extract rather than culinary celery stalk.
