ID: 0466
Category: herb
Ingredient: cilantro
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh cilantro worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Dietary fresh cilantro leaves improved associative and spatial memory and lowered brain AChE in mice (Mani 2011), with no evidence of cognitive worsening, but zero human trials or prefrontal executive-function assessments exist for fresh cilantro leaves.
