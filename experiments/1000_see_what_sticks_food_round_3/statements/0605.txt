Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Maybe False (limited_clinical_link toward easing): two multicenter double-blind PD RCTs of isolated caffeine—bridged to brewed coffee via Haskell-Ramsay 2018 (100 mg/220 mL) and Jeon 2019 HPLC—demonstrate modest short-to-medium-term easing of PD daytime sleepiness: Postuma 2012 (n=61 somnolent PD: CGI-C +0.64, PP ESS -1.97, ITT ESS -1.71) and Café-PD (Postuma 2017, n=118: 6-mo MDS-UPDRS 1.8 -0.49 [-0.79 to -0.20], 3-mo SCOPA-sleep daytime -1.09 [-1.88 to -0.30], attenuating by 12-18 mo). Direct brewed-coffee crossovers outside PD (Haskell-Ramsay 2018 n=59 alertness p<0.0005; Hindmarch 1998/2000) corroborate acute anti-somnolence, while PD espresso n-of-1 trials (Ferreira 2016 n=4) were mixed.
