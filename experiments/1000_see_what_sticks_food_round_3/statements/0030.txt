ID: 0030
Category: spice
Ingredient: caraway
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted caraway reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial has tested toasted culinary Carum carvi for anxiety in Parkinson's disease or general populations. A single small healthy-mouse polyphenol-extract study (Es-Safi 2021, PMC8000502, n=5/group) reported anxiolytic-like light-dark/open-field changes but used an un-toasted polyphenolic fraction and contained suspect day-21 zero-variance FST reporting, while human carvone fragrance inhalation (Heuberger 2001) raised BP/restlessness rather than calming mood.
