Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: poached
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached cod worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No adult clinical trial has evaluated poached Atlantic cod fillet on attention performance in Parkinson's disease or healthy adults. In the Norwegian Mommy's Food pregnancy cod RCT (NCT02610959, n=137), maternal cod intake improved maternal iodine while secondary 11-month infant Bayley-III cognitive composite was slightly lower (95 vs 99, p=0.045; Markhus 2021), a result contradicted within the SAME trial by Kvestad 2021 (null infant ASQ-2 p=0.633 and improved socio-emotional ASQ:SE p=0.020); maternal-fetal infant neurodevelopment does not transfer to adult PD attention, and older-adult dietary pattern cohorts (Mumme 2022) do not isolate cod.
