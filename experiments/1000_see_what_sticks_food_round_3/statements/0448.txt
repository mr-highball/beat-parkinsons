Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried chives worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human or animal studies evaluate dietary dried chives (Allium schoenoprasum) on olfactory impairment in Parkinson's disease. Clinical literature linking chives and olfaction (e.g., Varvyanskaya 2024, PMID:39104271) uses chives exclusively as a Sniffin' Sticks odor-identification test descriptor/distractor rather than a dietary intervention.
