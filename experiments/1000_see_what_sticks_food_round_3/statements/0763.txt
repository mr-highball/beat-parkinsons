Statement: Consuming cooked cranberry reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): radiation-cystitis dysuria burning with 72 mg PAC capsules (Hamilton 2015, n=41; contrasted with null dysuria in Stonehouse 2025, n=150) and an open-label RA DAS28 juice study lacking isolated pain VAS contrasts (Thimoteo 2019, n=41) do not establish a cooked-cranberry effect on Parkinson pain severity.
