ID: 0005
Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Available human data do not indicate that raw cantaloupe worsens or eases daytime sleepiness in Parkinson's disease. In the Milesi et al. (2009, n=70) double-blind trial of cantaloupe juice concentrate, Epworth Sleepiness Scale scores were completely null between groups at day 28 (p=0.729), and no whole-cantaloupe daytime sleepiness trials exist in Parkinson's.
