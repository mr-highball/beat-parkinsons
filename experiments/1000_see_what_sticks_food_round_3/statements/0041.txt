ID: 0041
Category: spice
Ingredient: caraway
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground caraway reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial has evaluated ground culinary caraway for insomnia severity in Parkinson's disease or general populations. Preclinical reports involve isolated intraperitoneal carvone potentiating pentobarbital anesthesia (de Sousa 2007) or modulating sleep-deprived mouse locomotion (Kishimoto 2024), or a confounded inhaled nutmeg-plus-caraway oil blend (Yang 2025), while human (+)-carvone fragrance inhalation (Heuberger 2001) did not produce subjective sedation.
