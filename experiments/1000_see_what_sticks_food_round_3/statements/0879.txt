ID: 0879
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted cumin worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The 2026 older-adult cumin essential oil RCT (Ito 2026) improved composite psychomotor speed (p=0.012) but was null on isolated motor speed (p=0.093) without reporting Trail Making Peg scores; lacking human PD dexterity trials of toasted culinary cumin, hand dexterity effects remain open.
