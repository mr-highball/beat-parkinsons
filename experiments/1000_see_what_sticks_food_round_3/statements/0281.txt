ID: 0281
Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): although long-term oral chamomile extract modestly lowered resting mean arterial blood pressure in GAD responders (Mao 2016, P=0.0063) and oral beverages lowered resting BP in one trial (Awaad 2018), Amsterdam 2009 found null resting SBP/DBP (n=57), Zick 2011 found identical dizziness adverse events vs placebo (p=1.00), and zero trials measured standing BP or orthostatic lightheadedness.
