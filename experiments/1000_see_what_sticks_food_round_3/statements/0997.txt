ID: 0997
Category: spice
Ingredient: dill seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground dill seed reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground dill seed reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in Parkinson's disease because no study measures postural blood pressure changes or orthostatic symptoms, and a small metabolic-syndrome dill RCT showed no resting blood pressure effect (PMID:23351341).
