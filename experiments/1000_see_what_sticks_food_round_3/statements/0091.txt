ID: 0091
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: raw
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw carrot reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In the only human mobility study with an isolated carrot arm (Schrager et al., 2015), 7 older adults consuming carrot juice for 6 weeks showed a null change in usual gait speed (1.16 to 1.20 m/s, p=0.350). Preclinical purple carrot root extract reduced reserpine catalepsy in rats (Custódio-Silva 2024) without measuring walking velocity, leaving raw carrot and Parkinson's walking slowness open.
