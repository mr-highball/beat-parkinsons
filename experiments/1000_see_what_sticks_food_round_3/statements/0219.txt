ID: 0219
Category: spice
Ingredient: celery seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground celery seed reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): a double-blind placebo-controlled trial of encapsulated celery seed (1.5 g/day x 6 wk, n=80 women; Hessami 2021) significantly improved the FSFI pain domain (p=0.033), corroborated by controlled equine osteoarthritis analgesia with Indian celery seed extract (Battaglia 2019, n=20), rodent gouty-arthritis anti-inflammatory models (Li 2019), and bundled human OA/dysmenorrhea RCTs (Vaidya 2025, Nahid 2009), though non-PD pain phenotypes and absent PD pain trials keep inference minimal.
