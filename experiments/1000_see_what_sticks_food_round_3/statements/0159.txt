Preparation: ground
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No human clinical trial has tested whether ground cayenne pepper alters levodopa pharmacokinetics, gastric emptying of levodopa, or daily OFF time in Parkinson's disease. Preclinical co-administration of oral Capsicum annuum extract potentiated L-DOPA rearing responses in rotenone rats (Ogunruku 2019), and one Parkinson's UK forum member reported delaying medication after chili powder, but neither establishes clinical OFF-time reduction.
