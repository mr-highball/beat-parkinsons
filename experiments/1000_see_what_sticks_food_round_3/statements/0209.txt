Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial tests ground celery seed on executive function impairment; although synthetic pharmaceutical NBP improved global dementia screening scores (MMSE/MoCA) in low-quality Chinese PDD RCTs (Zhong 2019 meta-analysis), retrospective PD cohorts (Wang 2024, Li 2026), and a four-drug cocktail (Zhang 2019), none separately assessed executive function impairment or tested culinary ground celery seed.
