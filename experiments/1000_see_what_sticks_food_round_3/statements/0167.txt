ID: 0167
Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial has evaluated ground cayenne pepper on fine manual dexterity (such as Nine-Hole Peg Test, Purdue Pegboard, or MDS-UPDRS finger tapping) in Parkinson's disease or general populations. Thornton 2026 measured isometric handgrip strength (not fine finger dexterity) in obese non-PD adults with small baseline-drifting interactions, and rodent PD studies assessed gross locomotion rather than human hand dexterity.
