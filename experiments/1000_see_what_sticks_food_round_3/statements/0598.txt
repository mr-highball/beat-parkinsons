ID: 0598
Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: poached
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached cod worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): Controlled human cod-protein and white-fish aging studies show NULL effects on walking speed and mobility, and no trial tests poached Atlantic cod fillet on Parkinson's gait velocity. In community-dwelling older adults, 12 months of daily Atlantic cod fillet hydrolysate (Nygard 2021, n=86) was null on Short Physical Performance Battery (p=0.41) and walking speed, NCT02045355 terminated without posted mobility results, and Seniors-ENRICA found white fish null on functional impairment (Garcia-Esquinas 2019).
