ID: 0078
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed carrot reduces hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for steamed carrot and Parkinson's hand dexterity impairment remains open. No trial tests steamed carrots on Parkinson's hand dexterity; a Parkinson's UK forum conversation documents practical grip/chopping barriers with raw hard carrots, and Forsberg 2022/2024 examined eating ergonomics of cooked vegetables. No clinical trial evaluates steamed culinary carrot on hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's.
