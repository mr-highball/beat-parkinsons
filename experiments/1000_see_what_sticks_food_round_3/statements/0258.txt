ID: 0258
Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Traditional ethnobotanical records in Italy, Lebanon, and Indonesia document aerial Apium graveolens stems and leaves as mild diuretic and antihypertensive foods/herbs (Chiocchio 2024; Nugraha 2022; Samaha 2019), but no human clinical trial evaluates whether raw celery stalk eases or worsens urinary urgency frequency in Parkinson's disease. Traditional Unani/Arabic Karafs celery seed uses (Al-Asmari 2017) are strictly separated by plant part.
