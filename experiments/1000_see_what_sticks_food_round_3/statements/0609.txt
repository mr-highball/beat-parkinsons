Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Maybe False (limited_clinical_link toward worsening): in the multicenter double-blind Café-PD trial (Postuma 2017, n=118 analyzed PD, caffeine 200 mg BID vs placebo, bridged to brewed coffee via Haskell-Ramsay 2018 and Jeon 2019 HPLC), caffeine modestly worsened MDS-UPDRS 4.1+4.2 dyskinesia scores across combined visits (+0.25 pts, 95% CI 0.04 to 0.45; +0.36 [0.02 to 0.70] at 18 mo), corroborated by historical clinical challenge data (Shoulson & Chase 1975, n=6 PD) showing prolonged levodopa dyskinesia duration. Tempered by null 6-week dyskinesia in Postuma 2012 (n=61, only 14 with baseline dyskinesia) and observational CALM-PD data (Wills 2013, HR 0.61 [0.37-1.01]).
