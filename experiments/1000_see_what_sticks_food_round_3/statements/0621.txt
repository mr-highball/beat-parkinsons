ID: 0621
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: decaffeinated
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming decaffeinated coffee worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): two non-PD balance crossovers conflict—a better-controlled 4-arm crossover in older adults (Richardson 2024, n=22, mean age 68 y) found decaffeinated coffee had NO effect on standing center-of-pressure postural control versus matched placebo or no-fluid control (p>0.05), whereas a smaller unstandardized trial in young adults (Cildir 2021, n=15 in decaf arm, milk/sugar allowed, after 48-h caffeine abstention) saw lower before-after SOT composite/C5 scores (p=0.001). No PD balance trial exists, leaving the claim open.
