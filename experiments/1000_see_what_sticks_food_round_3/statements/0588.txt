Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: poached
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached cod worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No human clinical trial has evaluated whether poached Atlantic cod fillet worsens orthostatic lightheadedness or standing blood pressure in Parkinson's disease. In Zucker fa/fa rats, baked Atlantic cod fillet had NULL effects on resting blood pressure (Vikoren 2021), and human cod metabolic crossovers (Ouellet 2007; Aadland 2015) did not report orthostatic standing challenges.
