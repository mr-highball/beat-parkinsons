Statement: Consuming soaked chia seed reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for soaked chia seed reducing fatigue severity in PD is Unknown (Researched). Despite rich Mesoamerican and Tarahumara endurance traditions for soaked chia drinks (Cahill 2003), two controlled human athletic endurance crossovers found null effects: Illian et al. 2011 (PMID:21183832, n=6 trained men) saw no running-performance advantage for omega-3 chia loading versus carbohydrate Gatorade, and Nieman et al. 2015 (PMID:25988762, n=24 runners) found chia seed oil did not extend time to exhaustion or reduce perceived fatigue compared with flavored water. No trials evaluate clinical PD fatigue.
