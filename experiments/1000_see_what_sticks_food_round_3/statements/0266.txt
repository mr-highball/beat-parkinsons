ID: 0266
Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no controlled human trial tests brewed chamomile tea on validated daytime sleepiness scales (e.g., Epworth Sleepiness Scale); Zick 2011 reported non-significant daytime-functioning trends in primary insomnia (n=34), whereas a systematic adverse-event review across 72 trials (Ostovar 2025) listed mild self-limiting drowsiness among the most reported minor adverse events without a placebo-controlled excess estimate.
