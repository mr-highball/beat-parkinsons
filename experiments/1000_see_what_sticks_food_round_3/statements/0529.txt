ID: 0529
Category: food
Ingredient: clams
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked clams reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for cooked clams affecting walking slowness in PD is Unknown (Researched). Local cohort record PMC10497173 pools clams together with all shellfish in general older-adult frailty analyses without isolating cooked clams or PD motor gait/fall outcomes, and Hsieh 2017 (MPTP mouse locomotor rescue from freshwater clam extract) did not report gait velocity or falls. No controlled human trials test cooked clams on PD walking slowness.
