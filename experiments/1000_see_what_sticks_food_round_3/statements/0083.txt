Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming steamed carrot worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No reliable clinical evidence links steamed carrot to worsening or easing Parkinson's pain. A quasi-experimental stage-1 labor pain report of carrot juice (Fazdria et al., 2024) was audited in full text (PMC10983098) and disqualified as internally incompatible (misidentifying laboring primigravida participants as '90 individuals with T2DM' and contradicting measurement intervals as 30 minutes, 60 minutes, 30 hours, and 8 weeks). Other pain studies either showed null SF-36 bodily pain in a bundled raw vegetarian diet (Donaldson 2001) or tested carrot seed capsules / multi-herb wild-carrot halva for female dyspareunia (Sadeghi 2020; Molkara 2018).
