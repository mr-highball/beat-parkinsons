Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial shows oral ground cayenne pepper worsens or eases depressive symptoms in Parkinson's disease. In the 12-week double-blind Capsifen RCT (Thornton 2026, n=34 completers), the POMS Depression intervention-by-time interaction was null (p=0.798; Bonferroni post-hoc comparisons null), Parkinson's observational data (Sauerbier 2018) do not isolate cayenne, and preclinical TRPV1 pharmacology is conflicting (TRPV1 antagonists show antidepressant effects in Iglesias 2022, whereas a capsaicin nanoemulsion eased nicotine-withdrawal immobility in mice in Krishnamoorthy 2023).
