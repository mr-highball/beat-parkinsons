ID: 0079
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted carrot worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical evidence indicates roasted carrots worsen or ease insomnia in Parkinson's disease. In the UK Women's Cohort Study (Noorwali et al., 2018, n=13,037), carrot intake was null for sleep duration (-1 min/d, p=0.5), and in mice (Liu et al., 2022), sleep prolongation occurred only with Lactobacillus brevis-fermented carrot juice compared against unfermented carrot juice.
