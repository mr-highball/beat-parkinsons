ID: 0447
Category: herb
Ingredient: chives
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh chives reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human clinical trials and zero Parkinson's disease animal models evaluate fresh common chives (Allium schoenoprasum) for muscle rigidity severity (strictly excluding pooled Allium cohorts dominated by garlic/onion and excluding Chinese chives Allium tuberosum, verified as the species tested in Takemoto 2024 PMC10933941). Existing primary evidence consists solely of an acute mouse global cerebral-ischemia stroke model (Singh 2018), phenolic/ferulic acid chemistry (Dai 2024; Ferreira 2022), an unisolated PD vegetarian menu listing (Hegelmaier 2020), and culinary/ethnobotanical context.
