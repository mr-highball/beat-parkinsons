ID: 0582
Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: poached
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming poached cod reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial has evaluated poached Atlantic cod fillet on fall frequency in Parkinson's disease or older adults. In community-dwelling older adults, 12 months of Atlantic cod protein hydrolysate had null effects on Short Physical Performance Battery (p=0.41) and mobility (Nygard 2021, n=86), NCT02045355 terminated without posted functional results, and Seniors-ENRICA found white fish null on functional impairment (Garcia-Esquinas 2019).
