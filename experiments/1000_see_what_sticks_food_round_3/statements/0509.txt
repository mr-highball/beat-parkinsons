Statement: Consuming cooked clams worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for uncontaminated cooked clams affecting executive function impairment is Unknown (Researched). Observational Pacific Northwest CoASTAL cohort studies (Grattan et al. 2016, n=513; Grattan 2018; Grattan 2021; and local PMC13302463) document memory decrements linked to repeated dietary exposure to DOMOIC ACID—a marine harmful algal bloom (Pseudo-nitzschia) neurotoxin—accumulated in contaminated Pacific razor clams. Because domoic acid poisoning represents environmental biotoxin contamination rather than an intrinsic effect of safe culinary cooked clams (exposure_fit: unusable), those findings are excluded from ordinary-food efficacy scoring while preserved transparently as a seafood-safety caveat. No trials test clean cooked clams on PD executive function impairment.
