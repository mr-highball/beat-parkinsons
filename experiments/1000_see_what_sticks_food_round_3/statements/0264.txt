ID: 0264
Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no controlled clinical trial tests oral brewed chamomile tea for constipation severity; topical abdominal chamomile oil after cesarean section shortened flatus but did NOT significantly shorten time to first defecation (Khadem 2018, n=142), oral chamomile drops reduced postoperative flatulence rather than constipation (2023 RCT, n=64), and oral rodent studies show antispasmodic/antidiarrheal slowing of hypermotility (Sebai 2014, Mehmood 2015, Asadi-Shahmirzadi 2012).
