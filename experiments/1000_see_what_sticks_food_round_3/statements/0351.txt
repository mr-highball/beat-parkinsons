ID: 0351
Category: food
Ingredient: chia seed
Preparation: soaked
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming soaked chia seed worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human clinical trials, observational PD cohorts, or targeted animal models test soaked chia seed (Salvia hispanica) for dyskinesia severity in Parkinson's disease. Available chia literature covers cardiometabolic biomarkers, non-PD constipation (Poltiyelova 2025), rodent antinociception, conflicted AD rodent cognition, and in vitro chia peptide neuroinflammation/oxidative stress assays (Martínez Leo 2020a/b), none of which measure dyskinesia severity. Traditional Mesoamerican soaked chia uses (Cahill 2003) provide dietary context only, leaving this claim Unknown (Researched).
