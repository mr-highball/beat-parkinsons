Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward worsening): three randomized controlled elimination/challenge trials directly testing cow's milk in children with refractory chronic constipation show cow's milk worsens constipation in cow's-milk-intolerant subsets—Iacono 1998 (NEJM, n=65 DB crossover: 68% resolved on soy vs 0% on cow's milk; 100% relapsed on DB cow's milk challenge), Dehghani 2012 (n=140 RCT: 80% vs 47.1% response p=0.0001; 42.8% relapsed on challenge), and Mohammadi Bourkheili 2021 (n=70 RCT: 71.4% vs 11.4%, p<0.001)—supported by Wegh 2022 review and lengthened adult GI transit in Jianqin 2016 (n=45 A1/A2 vs A2 milk). Tempered by null adult transit/stool consistency in Greenway 2026, scarce adult allergy data (Carroccio 2006), and absence of PD plain-milk constipation trials (fermented probiotic milk trials like Yang 2023 do not transfer to plain pasteurized milk).
