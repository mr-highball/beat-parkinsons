Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried date reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): blinded human acute alertness testing was inconsistent across date cultivars (Altuwairki PMC13440079, n=35), mouse fruit extracts were sedating rather than alerting (Sheikh 2016), and no clinical trials test dried dates for Parkinson daytime sleepiness.
