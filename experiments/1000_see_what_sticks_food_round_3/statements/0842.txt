ID: 0842
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise reduces daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) reduces daily off time in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses daily off time; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
