ID: 0870
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted cumin worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In true Cuminum cyminum (excluding Nigella sativa), a 2026 older-adult cumin essential oil trial improved composite psychomotor speed and reaction time but was null on isolated motor speed (p=0.093) and excluded PD; an MPTP mouse aqueous-extract study carries an unresolved n=15 versus n=5 reporting discrepancy, leaving toasted cumin's effect on Parkinson's bradykinesia open.
