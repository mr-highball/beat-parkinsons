ID: 0932
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): the sole Parkinson's plain dark-chocolate RCT (Wolz 2012, n=26, 200 g 80% dark vs white chocolate) found null between-group composite UPDRS-III (-0.54, 95% CI -1.50 to 0.42, p=0.258) without isolating muscle rigidity; NCT02275884 has no posted results and Al-Okbi 2025 (rotenone rats) had zero motor-behavior tests.
