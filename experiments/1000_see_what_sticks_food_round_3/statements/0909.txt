Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human balance data exist for cooked curry leaf, and preclinical dopaminergic rodent studies conflict directly between extract motor rescue (Reddy 2020; Anjum 2025) and fraction-induced catalepsy prolongation (Patil & Langade 2021), leaving Parkinson's postural instability open.
