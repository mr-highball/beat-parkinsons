ID: 0938
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): the sole Parkinson's plain dark-chocolate RCT (Wolz 2012, n=26, 200 g 80% dark vs white chocolate) found NO significant between-treatment effect on composite UPDRS-III (-0.54, 95% CI -1.50 to 0.42, p=0.258) and did not isolate rest tremor; NCT02275884 has no posted results, and PD chocolate surveys (Wolz 2009; Schaeffer 2021) measure intake/craving rather than tremor severity.
