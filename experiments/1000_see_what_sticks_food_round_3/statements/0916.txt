ID: 0916
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): multiple controlled non-PD trials of plain dark chocolate and cocoa flavanols show acute or subacute attention gains—Sasaki 2024 (n=18 middle-aged crossover of 25 g plain dark chocolate maintained Stroop selective-attention accuracy p=0.014/0.006), Field 2011 (n=30 dark vs white chocolate improved CRT attention/SWM), Scholey 2010 (n=30 RVIP speed), Karabay 2018 (n=48 spatial visual search), and Desideri 2012 / Mastroiacovo 2015 (n=90+90 MCI/older adults faster TMT-A p<0.0001). Tempered by large null trials—COSMOS-Mind (Baker 2023, n=2,262 3-yr cocoa extract null p=0.28), FlaSeCo 2020 (n=100 dark chocolate TMT-A p=0.93), Altinok 2025 (n=36 null), Akyurek 2024 (n=48 null), and bundled CANN 2023—and zero PD attention trials.
