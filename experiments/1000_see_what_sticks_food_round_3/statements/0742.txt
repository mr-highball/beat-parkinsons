ID: 0742
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: visual hallucination frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk worsens visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial or Parkinson's cohort evaluates pasteurized cow's milk on visual hallucination frequency; existing dairy epidemiology (Park 2005; Hughes 2017; Olsson 2020; Hajji-Louati 2024; Zhang 2021 MR) measures incident Parkinson's diagnosis risk rather than symptom severity, and a 6-month PD whey-protein RCT (Tosukhowong 2016, n=32) reported null composite UPDRS without isolating pasteurized cow's milk or this symptom.
