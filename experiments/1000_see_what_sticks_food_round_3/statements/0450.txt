ID: 0450
Category: herb
Ingredient: chives
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh chives worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human clinical trials and zero animal nociception studies evaluate fresh common chives (Allium schoenoprasum) for pain severity (strictly separating common chives from Chinese chives Allium tuberosum, garlic, and onion). Parvu et al. (2014, PMID:24781739) reported lower nitro-oxidative inflammatory markers in turpentine-oil-inflamed rats without conducting any pain behavior assay, while Monash University FODMAP guidance notes fresh chives are low-FODMAP and generally well tolerated without triggering fructan-driven abdominal pain.
