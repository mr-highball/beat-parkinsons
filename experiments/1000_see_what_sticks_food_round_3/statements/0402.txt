Statement: Consuming baked chicken reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): Evidence for baked chicken reducing urinary urgency frequency is insufficient for a directional lean. One prospective UK cohort of 6,424 women (Dallosso 2003, cited by Turmel 2017) listed higher chicken intake alongside bread and vegetables as associated with lower 1-year incident overactive bladder onset, but the abstract omits the chicken-specific adjusted OR/CI, does not specify baking vs other cooking methods, measures new-onset OAB incidence rather than reducing urgency frequency in existing PD, and lacks independent replication or any RCT. In the controlled VA IC/BPS cohort (Jarman 2023), chicken was simply non-bothersome (2% IC/BPS, 0% controls)—a neutral tolerability profile rather than active urgency relief.
