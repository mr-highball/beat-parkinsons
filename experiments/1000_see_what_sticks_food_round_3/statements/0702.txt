Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): all five human cognitive RCTs used corn oil solely as an omega-3/flaxseed placebo capsule with flat or null placebo trajectories (Ogawa 2023; Howe 2018; Ciappolino 2020; Keim 2018; Zhang 2017), providing zero evidence that culinary refined corn oil worsens Parkinson executive function.
