Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: baked
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked chicken worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No human clinical trial has tested whether baked chicken worsens neurogenic orthostatic hypotension or standing lightheadedness in Parkinson's disease. Preclinical studies of chicken extract in DOCA-salt hypertensive rats (Matsumura 2001) examined resting arterial hypertension rather than human orthostatic blood-pressure drops, and human chicken meal crossovers (Uhe 1992) did not report standing blood pressure.
