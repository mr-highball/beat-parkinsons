ID: 0595
Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: poached
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming poached cod reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled videofluoroscopic or clinical dysphagia trial has tested whether boneless poached Atlantic cod fillet reduces swallowing difficulty in Parkinson's disease. Tablet-swallowing difficulty in the 12-month older-adult cod hydrolysate trial was null (Nygard 2021, p=0.11), emergency choking series highlight mechanical FISH-BONE foreign bodies (67% of choking admissions in Saccomanno 2023, n=138) rather than boneless poached fillet physiology, and Parkinson's UK forum members mention flaky moist fish only as an informal soft-texture meal preference.
