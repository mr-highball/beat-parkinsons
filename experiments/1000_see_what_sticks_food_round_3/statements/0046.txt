ID: 0046
Category: spice
Ingredient: caraway
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted caraway worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): while an aqueous Carum carvi fruit extract induced acute and 8-day diuresis and natriuresis in normal Wistar rats (Lahlou 2007, PMID:17113735) echoing Moroccan traditional diuretic use, urine output in healthy rats does not establish clinical urinary urgency frequency in Parkinson's disease, and a 12-week human triple-blind RCT of aqueous caraway extract (Kazemipoor 2013, PMC3844184, n=60) reported unchanged urine specific gravity and no clinical urinary adverse events.
