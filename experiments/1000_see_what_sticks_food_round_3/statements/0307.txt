ID: 0307
Category: food
Ingredient: cherry
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried cherry worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal, 50.005): two older-adult clinical insomnia DB-RCTs (Pigeon 2010 n=15; Losso 2018 n=8, PSG +84 min p=0.0182) plus actigraphy DB-RCTs of tart cherry concentrate and freeze-dried sweet cherry (Howatson 2012, Garrido 2013, Chung 2022) favor sleep improvement with zero worsening reports, tempered by a null 500 mg/d tart-cherry powder insomnia crossover (Tucker 2024, n=34, p>0.05), three null healthy-adult trials (Hillman 2026, Kimble 2022, Sinclair 2022), and absence of whole-dried-cherry or Parkinson disease trials.
