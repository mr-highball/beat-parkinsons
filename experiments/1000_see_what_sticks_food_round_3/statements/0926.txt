ID: 0926
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): controlled non-PD trials and a 7-RCT meta-analysis (Shateri 2023, executive time WMD -11.77, p=0.03) show modest easing of executive-function performance under plain dark chocolate or cocoa flavanols—Sasaki 2024 (n=18 plain dark chocolate crossover preserved Stroop accuracy p=0.006), Tsukamoto 2025 (n=18 cocoa flavanol improved reverse-Stroop interference p<0.01), and Desideri 2012 / Mastroiacovo 2015 (n=90+90 MCI/older adults faster TMT-B p<0.05 & p<0.0001). Heavily tempered by major null trials—COSMOS-Mind (n=2,262 3-yr cocoa extract null executive composite), FlaSeCo 2020 (n=100 dark chocolate TMT-B p=0.66), Nemoto 2022 (n=88 dark chocolate null direct Stroop/TMT between groups), Altinok 2025 (n=36 null), and bundled CANN 2023—capping confidence at minimal_signal.
