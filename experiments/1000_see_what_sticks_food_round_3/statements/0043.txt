ID: 0043
Category: spice
Ingredient: caraway
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted caraway reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): two independent laboratories demonstrated oral monotherapy Carum carvi seed-extract antinociception across mouse writhing and biphasic formalin assays (Wakrim 2025, PMC12715987, 71.7% writhing and 30-39% formalin inhibition) and rat chronic formalin nociception/paw edema (Seddighfar 2020, PMID:32113847, p<0.001), with zero controlled monotherapy evidence of worsening pain. Human evidence remains inconclusive and caps certainty at minimal_signal: oral ground caraway (2 g/d) was null for anti-TB GI pain in Latif 2025 (PMC12104075, with NCT06157034 blinding/placebo discrepancies), topical hot caraway oil poultices missed significance vs heat-matched olive oil on IBS-SSS (Lauche 2015, p=0.139 despite higher adequate relief), and bundled peppermint+caraway RCTs (Menthacarin, COLM-SST) are strictly excluded as multi-herb confounded.
