ID: 0619
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: decaffeinated
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming decaffeinated coffee worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial measures orthostatic lightheadedness symptoms after decaffeinated coffee in Parkinson's disease; controlled hemodynamic studies outside PD show decaffeinated coffee generally has null acute blood-pressure effects compared with water or regular coffee (Nussberger 1990; Lima de Castro 2024; Aoun 2021 intradialytic hypotension 26% vs 27%, p=0.539) and fails to replicate caffeine's pressor prevention of postprandial hypotension (Heseltine 1991), while Corti 2002 saw supine BP/MSNA elevation only in 9 non-habitual drinkers.
