Ingredient: cow's milk

Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial evaluates pasteurized cow's milk on Parkinson's drooling severity; in a landmark double-blind sensory RCT (Pinnock & Arney 1993), perceived thicker saliva (+42%), throat coating (+39%), and urge to swallow (+31%) occurred equally after cow's milk and a matched soy-milk placebo, showing no cow's-milk-specific salivary effect.
