Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no controlled human trial tests brewed chamomile tea on executive function impairment; the only human attention report is an uncontrolled 3-patient adolescent ADHD case series (Niederhofer 2009), and rodent scopolamine/diabetes studies (Khan 2025, Iacob 2024) evaluate spatial/passive-avoidance memory rather than executive function impairment.
