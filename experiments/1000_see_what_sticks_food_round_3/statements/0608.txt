Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial evaluates brewed coffee on Parkinson's drooling severity; Fox Insight PD survey data (Gabbert 2023, n=4,892) showed no significant association between coffee drinking (p=0.0823) or weekly dosage (p=0.9754) and saliva/drooling, and a controlled crossover outside PD (Hildebrandt 2013, n=35) found caffeinated drinks did not alter salivary flow.
