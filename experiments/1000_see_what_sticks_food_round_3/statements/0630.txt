ID: 0630
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): in a double-blind PD crossover (Deleu 2006, n=12), caffeine before levodopa shortened latency to walking response and increased walking response magnitude, corroborated by controlled older-adult gait-speed trials of caffeine (Marinho 2024, n=15: faster 10-m walk p=0.008 & TUG p=0.002; Waer 2024, n=85: faster 10-m walk p<0.05). Tempered by null walking speed in Norager 2005 (n=30 >=70 y), null Senior Fitness Test under brewed coffee in Richardson 2024 (n=22 older adults), null general UPDRS gait in Postuma 2012, and missing quantitative walking-speed CIs in Deleu 2006's abstract, capping confidence at minimal_signal.
