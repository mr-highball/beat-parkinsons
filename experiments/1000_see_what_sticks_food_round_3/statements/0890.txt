ID: 0890
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: ground
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cumin worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human walking-speed trial tests true ground cumin; the 2026 older-adult cumin oil trial did not assess gait and was null on isolated motor speed, while an MPTP mouse study has unresolved sample-size discrepancies, leaving Parkinson's walking slowness open.
