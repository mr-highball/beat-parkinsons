ID: 0614
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): the largest double-blind RCT directly testing brewed coffee on fine manual dexterity (Gerdes 2022, n=107 surgeons, 340 mL caffeinated vs decaf coffee) found completely null effects (p=0.079 and p=0.511), and Fuller 2021 found overall nine-hole peg test performance null (gain only in post-hoc high-fatigue subgroup). While acute caffeine sped finger-tapping latency in PD (Deleu 2006 n=12) and healthy adults (Fagan 1988 n=18), some PD patients report coffee-induced hand shaking (Parkinson's UK 2024), leaving net hand dexterity open.
