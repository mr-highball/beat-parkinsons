Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Maybe True (limited_clinical_link toward easing): a direct Parkinson's crossover trial after 7-day caffeine washout (Sharma 2022, n=24 PD + 42 older controls, 100 mg caffeine in decaf coffee vs decaf coffee) significantly reduced choice-reaction-time errors (p=0.049), improved Stroop accuracy (p=0.018), speeded Stroop RT in PD (p=0.049), and improved dual-item RSVP accuracy (p=0.037), while paradoxically impairing single-item RSVP (p=0.044). Direct brewed-coffee crossovers outside PD (Haskell-Ramsay 2018 n=59; Han 2020 n=49; Hindmarch 1998/2000) corroborate acute vigilance/attention gains, tempered by Café-PD global MoCA (-0.66 pts, not isolated to attention) and Akyurek 2024 null RSVP.
