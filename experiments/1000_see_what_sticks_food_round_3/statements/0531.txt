ID: 0531
Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human constipation trial exists for ground clove; a single healthy-mouse charcoal-transit study (Agbaje 2008) showed accelerated gut propulsion with oral aqueous clove extract, which is too indirect to determine Parkinson's constipation effects.
