ID: 0899
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human levodopa-induced dyskinesia trial tests cooked Murraya koenigii leaves; rat neuroleptic/reserpine orofacial-dyskinesia assays differ mechanistically from levodopa PD dyskinesia and directly conflict with a 2021 follow-up where curry leaf fractions prolonged catalepsy and blunted dopaminergic movement.
