ID: 0070
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: raw
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw carrot reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: In a progressive reserpine-induced Parkinson's disease rat model (Custódio-Silva et al., 2024), chronic oral purple carrot root extract (400 mg/kg/d across 51 preventive/protective days) significantly reduced bar catalepsy duration—the standard rodent assay for parkinsonian akinesia/bradykinesia—while preserving tyrosine hydroxylase-positive dopaminergic neurons in both the SNpc and VTA. Mechanistic corroboration comes from Zaim et al. (2021), where black carrot anthocyanins protected human dopaminergic SH-SY5Y cells against MPP+-induced oxidative apoptosis. Because evidence rests on an anthocyanin-rich purple carrot root extract in a rodent PD model rather than culinary orange raw carrots in human PD (and a tiny non-PD carrot-juice pilot n=7, Schrager 2015, showed null gait speed), this supports only a minimal directional lean (Maybe True, 50.005).
