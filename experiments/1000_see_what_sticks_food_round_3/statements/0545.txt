Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity

Statement: Consuming ground clove worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical dysphagia study tests culinary ground clove in Parkinson's disease; topical clove-oil gargles reduced postoperative sore throat while a single case report linked repeated concentrated clove oil ingestion to transient dysphagia and dysarthria, leaving dietary ground clove swallowing effects open.
