ID: 0020
Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for raw cantaloupe and Parkinson's pain remains open. Although a 28-day stress trial of coated cantaloupe concentrate (Milesi et al., 2009, n=70) noted a lower day-28 transverse score on a single FARD Pain item (p=0.023), baseline pain was lower in the active arm (1.25 vs 1.81) without baseline-adjusted ANCOVA, and a dedicated 6-month randomized trial in women with chronic knee/back pain using melon SOD (Koike et al., 2022, n=46) was completely null on VRS pain (p=0.31), JKOM (p=0.15), and RDQ (p=0.28). Preclinical studies tested rat paw edema without pain behavior (Ezzat 2019) or melon seeds rather than edible flesh (Wahid 2020).
