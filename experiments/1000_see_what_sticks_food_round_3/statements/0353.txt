Ingredient: chia seed
Preparation: soaked
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming soaked chia seed worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for soaked chia seed affecting executive function impairment is Unknown (Researched). Zero human cognitive trials test chia seeds on attention or executive function, and preclinical rodent cognition studies are directly conflicted: Schreyer et al. 2020 (PMID:33106576) reported improved spatial learning in APP23 mice but null cognitive flexibility, Rui et al. 2018 (PMID:30110883) found null Morris water maze performance with elevated amyloid-beta42/neuroinflammation in SAMP8 mice, Bilgic et al. 2018 (PMID:30624431) found worse water-maze cognition with pretreatment and null effect with treatment in AlCl3 rats, and Nemeth et al. 2015 (PMID:26469777) reported sex-specific guinea pig Y-maze effects.
