Ingredient: clams
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked clams reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One MPTP Parkinson's mouse model provides a narrow preclinical locomotor signal: Hsieh et al. 2017 (PMID:28126590) reported that oral freshwater clam (Corbicula fluminea) extract improved MPTP-impaired locomotor behavior, preserved substantia nigra pars compacta TH-positive dopaminergic neurons, suppressed GFAP/TNF-alpha/iNOS/IL-1beta neuroinflammation, and elevated BDNF/GDNF/NGF in C57BL/6 mice. Supporting mechanistic context includes glial anti-neuroinflammatory activity (Lin et al. 2022, PMC8836940), human prediabetes TNF-alpha lowering (Huang et al. 2022, PMC9027770), and boiled clam soup bioactives (Yu 2020). Because evidence rests on a single murine MPTP model using concentrated freshwater clam extract (with no human PD motor trials and noting protein-levodopa OFF-time competition if cooked clams are eaten alongside levodopa), certainty is minimal_signal (50.005) and is not extrapolated to other motor symptoms.
