ID: 0548
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): the MS anxiety pilot bundled 60 mL coconut oil with 800 mg/d EGCG (Platero 2020, n=51; unusable attribution), open-label chemotherapy QOL lacked a validated anxiety scale (Law 2014), MVP cohorts give composite PRO-PD without oil grade, and non-PD rodent models do not establish Parkinson anxiety efficacy.
