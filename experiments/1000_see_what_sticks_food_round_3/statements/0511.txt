Category: food
Ingredient: clams
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked clams reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Controlled preclinical evidence provides a directional anti-fatigue signal for freshwater clam extract: Huang et al. 2013 (PMID:23531600, PMC6270442; n=32 rats, 8/group) demonstrated that 7 days of oral Corbicula fluminea extract (20 mg/kg/day) extended inclined treadmill time to exhaustion by 27.4% (61.13 +/- 1.47 vs 48.0 +/- 2.16 min, P<0.001) while lowering post-exercise lactate, CPK, LDH, AST, ALT, and TNF-alpha. A concordant non-significant trend toward lower subjective fatigue appeared in the 12-week human freshwater clam extract RCT (Shimazoe et al. 2022, n=34; held contextual to avoid overclaiming a trend), and East Asian shijimi soup traditions (Kuwano 2026; Yu 2020) provide culinary context. Remote extract-to-cooked exposure, species difference (freshwater vs marine clams), and lack of a powered human PD fatigue trial cap certainty at minimal_signal (50.005).
