Symptom: anxiety severity

Statement: Consuming dried chervil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): exhaustive PubMed, Europe PMC, and ClinicalTrials.gov searches confirm zero clinical trials and zero in vivo animal studies evaluating garden chervil (Anthriscus cerefolium) as an active treatment for anxiety. In human skullcap anxiety research (Wolfson & Hoffmann 2003, PMID:12652886; Brock 2012), culinary chervil was used solely as an inert placebo comparator, and all remaining literature is strictly in vitro phytochemistry or historical European herbalism (strictly excluding wild Anthriscus sylvestris and toxic Apiaceae lookalikes).
