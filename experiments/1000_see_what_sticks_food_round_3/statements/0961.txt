ID: 0961
Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried date reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): zero human insomnia trials test whole dried date fruit; positive human sleep RCTs tested date seed/pit powder (Momeniyan 2025) and rat sleep EEG studies tested date palm floral spathe (Rahimi 2017, 2019), while fruit-extract mouse data measured only pentobarbitone sedation (Sheikh 2016).
