ID: 0536
Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground clove worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In the sole oral animal clove fatigue test (Alembagheri 2023 full text), aqueous clove extract had a null effect on rat forced-swimming endurance; human aromatherapy trials used an inhaled four-oil blend (Hawkins 2022), and Clovinol tested acute alcohol hangover (Mammen 2018), leaving Parkinson's fatigue unresolved.
