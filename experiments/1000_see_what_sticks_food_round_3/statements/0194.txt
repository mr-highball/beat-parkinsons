Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity

Statement: Consuming fresh celery leaf worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial or observational study has tested whether fresh celery leaf worsens neurogenic swallowing difficulty (dysphagia) in Parkinson's disease. While Wuthrich & Hofer (1984, PMID:6734454, n=31) established IgE-mediated celery-mugwort-spice allergy capable of acute oral/pharyngeal swelling in sensitized individuals, acute allergic angioedema is pathophysiologically distinct from PD neurogenic dysphagia.
