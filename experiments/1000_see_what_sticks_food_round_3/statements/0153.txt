Preparation: steamed
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming steamed cauliflower worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No standalone human clinical trial or Parkinson animal model evaluates whether steamed cauliflower worsens urinary urgency frequency. Mature cauliflower delivers predominantly indole-3-carbinol rather than broccoli-sprout sulforaphane levels (Song 2007; Wang 2020; Shukla 2024; Bafumo 2024), so purified sulforaphane trials (Santos 2023 FXTAS null after correction; Kassar 2025 schizophrenia) and bundled multi-vegetable trials (Connolly 2024 VESSEL; Navarro 2014) do not establish a whole-cauliflower effect on Parkinson's urinary urgency frequency.
