Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried celery leaf worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): in the MPTP Parkinson's mouse model (Chonpathompikunlert et al., 2018, PMC5859653, n=48), oral dried whole-plant Apium graveolens methanolic extract (250 and 375 mg/kg/d x 21 d—directly incorporating dried leaves) significantly reduced traversal latency across a fixed 100 cm narrow beam (Fig 3a, p<0.05 at days 3 and 7, directly reflecting faster gait traversal) alongside reduced grid-walk foot faults (Fig 2) and SNpc TH+ neuron preservation, with zero evidence of worsening walking speed. Leaf chemistry confirms celery leaves carry abundant bioavailable apigenin/apiin (Zhao 2008, Cao 2009, Mencherini 2007) and 3-5x higher phthalides than stalks (Kurobayashi 2006). Strictly excluding synthetic DL-NBP drug trials (Zhou 2019), this yields a minimal preclinical signal toward easing walking slowness (Maybe False, anchor 50.005).
