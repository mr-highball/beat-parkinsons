Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): oral virgin coconut oil showed no significant between-group pain difference in chemotherapy patients (Law 2014 Table 3, n=60, NS), human trials showing pain relief used strictly topical skin/mucosal or non-ingested mouth-rinse routes (Alikamali 2023, Agarwal 2025), Phillips 2018 tested a whole ketogenic PD diet, and rodent writhing assays are non-PD preclinical models.
