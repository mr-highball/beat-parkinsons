ID: 0891
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: For true Murraya koenigii leaf (excluding curry powder blends), a bundled 3-herb human IBS trial was null on HADS anxiety (Lauche 2016) and mouse elevated-plus-maze studies directly conflict between anxiolytic hydroalcoholic extract (Dahiya 2016) and sedative/reduced open-arm aqueous extract (Sharma 2017), leaving anxiety effects open.
