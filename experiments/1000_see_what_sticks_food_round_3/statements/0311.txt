ID: 0311
Category: food
Ingredient: cherry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw cherry reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): three RCT meta-analyses found no pooled resting blood pressure effect for cherry supplementation (Norouzzadeh 2023, Eslami 2022, Moosavian 2022), individual trials that lowered resting SBP did not assess standing BP drop, and no study measures orthostatic lightheadedness.
