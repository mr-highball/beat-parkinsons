ID: 0945
Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried date reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): the only double-blind sugar/fiber-matched human whole-date-flesh crossover (Altuwairki PMC13440079, n=35) found Khassab dates significantly reduced calmness vs placebo (p=0.001) and Barhi was null (p=0.43), conflicting with uncontrolled/pilot mood studies and rodent models, while positive human DASS anxiety trials tested date seed powder rather than dried fruit.
