Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: dream enactment frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces dream enactment frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no controlled study tests brewed coffee or caffeine on dream enactment frequency in Parkinson's disease or RBD; in a cross-sectional survey of 96 polysomnography-confirmed iRBD patients (Jun 2022), 26.0% (25/96) perceived caffeine as an aggravating factor and 74% reported no aggravation, while Arnaldi 2017 notes caffeine is not protective against iRBD.
