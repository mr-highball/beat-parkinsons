Statement: Consuming ground celery seed reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): in a triple-blind, placebo-controlled crossover RCT in 50 hypertensive adults (Shayani Rad 2023, IRCT20130418013058N8), 1.34 g/day celery seed extract reduced Beck Depression Inventory fatigue and loss-of-energy items (P<0.01), while a bundled Boswellia plus celery seed OA trial also assessed FACIT-F (Vaidya 2025); single BDI items rather than a standalone fatigue scale, unregistered endpoint, seed-extract matrix, and non-PD cohort keep inference minimal.
