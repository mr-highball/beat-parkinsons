Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): older-adult gait and balance trials using corn-oil placebo gelcaps (ENRGISE NCT02676466; Engelen 2024; Tessier 2021; NUTRIMAL 2021) found null mobility contrasts and lacked oil-free comparators, leaving zero attributable evidence for refined corn oil on Parkinson freezing of gait frequency.
