Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming steamed collard greens worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for steamed collard greens worsening orthostatic lightheadedness severity is Unknown (Researched). Johnson et al. 2019 (PMID:30709032, PMC6413025) studied resting blood pressure and lipids in spontaneously hypertensive rats fed collard-supplemented diets (n=10/group) without standing orthostatic challenge, and zero human trials evaluate collard greens on orthostatic blood pressure or lightheadedness in PD.
