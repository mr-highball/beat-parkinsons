ID: 0282
Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): two double-blind placebo-controlled oral chamomile RCTs reduced non-Parkinson's pain—oral drops in cyclic mastalgia (Saghafi 2018, n=60, P=0.007) and oral capsules in endometriosis pelvic/menstrual pain (Khalajinia 2024, n=102, P<0.001, unusually flat placebo)—supported by a primary dysmenorrhea systematic review (Niazi 2021) and tea glucuronoxylan antinociception in mice (Chaves 2020), whereas oral chamomile failed to prevent paclitaxel neurotoxicity (Nikkhah 2026, n=80), topical trials test a different route, and no PD pain trial exists.
