Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming canned chickpeas reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal, 50.005): two human crossover feeding trials of canned drained chickpeas (Murty 2010, n=42; Pittaway 2007, n=27) reported improved perceived bowel function/health, tempered by a null stool-frequency crossover in 12 healthy adults with mild gas/bloating (Dahl 2014) and zero clinical trials in diagnosed Parkinson constipation.
