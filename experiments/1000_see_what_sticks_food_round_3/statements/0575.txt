Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: poached
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached cod worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial has evaluated poached Atlantic cod fillet for daytime sleepiness in Parkinson's disease. Sleep/daily-functioning fish trials (Hansen 2014, PMID:24812543) tested fatty Atlantic salmon rather than lean cod fillet, and a cross-sectional Spanish adult survey (Marin Ferrandis 2025) did not isolate poached cod or measure daytime somnolence causally.
