ID: 0965
Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried date reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): a 12-week whole-date RCT (60 g/day vs raisins, Butler 2022 n=61) found null resting blood pressure changes (p>0.05), and zero studies evaluate dried dates on orthostatic standing BP or lightheadedness in Parkinson disease.
