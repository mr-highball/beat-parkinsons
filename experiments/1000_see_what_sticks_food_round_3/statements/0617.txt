ID: 0617
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Maybe True (limited_clinical_link toward easing): in Postuma 2012 (n=61 PD, 6-week double-blind RCT of oral caffeine 100-200 mg BID vs placebo, bridged to brewed coffee via Haskell-Ramsay 2018 and Jeon 2019 HPLC), caffeine significantly reduced the UPDRS Part III rigidity subcomponent by -1.01 points (95% CI -2.0 to -0.7) alongside total UPDRS III (-3.15, -5.50 to -0.83), matching Round 2 black tea precedent 0631. Tempered by the larger 6-18 month Café-PD trial (Postuma 2017, n=118 analyzed PD) showing null total MDS-UPDRS III (-0.48, -3.21 to 2.25; rigidity subscore not isolated), suggesting possible tachyphylaxis.
