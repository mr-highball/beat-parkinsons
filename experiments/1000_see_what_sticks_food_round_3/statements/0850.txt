ID: 0850
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) reduces fall frequency in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses fall frequency; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
