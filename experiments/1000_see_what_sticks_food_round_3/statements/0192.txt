Statement: Consuming dried celery leaf worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): consistent with Round 1 celery-stalk (0281) and Round 3 celery-seed (0222) precedents, Chonpathompikunlert et al. (2018, PMC5859653, n=48 MPTP PD mice) showed that oral dried whole-plant Apium graveolens methanolic extract (125-375 mg/kg/d x 21 d—directly including dried leaves) significantly reduced separately scored resting tremor (Fig 4a, p<0.05, outperforming Tidomet Plus at 375 mg/kg on day 7) alongside SNpc TH+ neuron preservation and MAO-A/B inhibition. Leaf chemistry verifies dried/fresh celery leaves are rich in bioavailable apigenin/apiin (Zhao 2008, Cao 2009, Mencherini 2007) and carry 3-5x higher phthalides than stalks (Kurobayashi 2006). Strictly excluding synthetic pharmaceutical DL-NBP capsules (Zhou 2019) and noting the high-dose dried-leaf hyperthyroidism case (Rouhi-Boroujeni 2016, 4 g/d x 45 d), the sole controlled PD-model signal points toward easing rather than worsening rest tremor (Maybe False, anchor 50.005).
