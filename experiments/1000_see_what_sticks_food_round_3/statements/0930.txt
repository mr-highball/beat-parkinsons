ID: 0930
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate reduces hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial, Parkinson's cohort, or controlled experiment tests plain dark chocolate on hand dexterity impairment; the sole Parkinson's dark-chocolate RCT (Wolz 2012, n=26, 200 g 80% dark vs white chocolate) measured only composite UPDRS-III motor score (null between-group -0.54, p=0.258), NCT02275884 has no posted results, and Coe 2022 tested a cocoa beverage for 6-minute walk fatigue.
