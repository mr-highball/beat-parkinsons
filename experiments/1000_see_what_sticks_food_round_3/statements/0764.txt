ID: 0764
Category: food
Ingredient: cranberry
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked cranberry worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): no human clinical trial tests cooked cranberries on postural instability; in rotenone rats (Witucki 2022), concentrated cranberry juice shortened corrective stepping distance (-11%, p<0.05) but high-dose juice alone reduced counted midbrain cells by 15% (n=3/group), leaving the preclinical balance/safety direction mixed and unverified in humans.
