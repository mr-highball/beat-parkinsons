ID: 0397
Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: baked
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming baked chicken reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial shows baked chicken reduces or worsens Parkinson's rest tremor. While barbecued/grilled chicken contained 8.48 ng/g of the tremor-linked beta-carboline harmane (Louis 2007) and blood harmane is elevated in PD cases vs controls (Louis 2014), baked chicken was not tested for harmane formation, no PD tremor-severity dietary study exists, and the population-based Faroe Islands study (Ferrer & Louis 2020) found NO association between dietary meat consumption or doneness and essential tremor or blood harmane after correction (contradicting an earlier male-only US clinic finding, Louis 2008).
