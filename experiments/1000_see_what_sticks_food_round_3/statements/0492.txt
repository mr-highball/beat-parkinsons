Symptom: postural instability severity

Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Three independent preclinical Parkinson's laboratories—including exact oral ground Ceylon cinnamon feeding in A53T and MPTP mice plus independent cinnamaldehyde and procyanidin PD models—consistently improved rotarod balance retention; with zero human Parkinson's balance RCTs and a grounded no-reversal caregiver account, evidence provides a minimal directional lean toward easing postural instability.
