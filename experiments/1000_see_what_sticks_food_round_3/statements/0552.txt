Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): coconut oil produced 0% response as the placebo arm in an MDD DB-RCT (Mozaffari-Khosravi 2013, n=62), the MS BDI-II pilot bundled coconut oil with 800 mg/d EGCG (Platero 2021, n=51; unusable attribution), and non-PD rodent assays do not transfer to clinical PD depression.
