Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical swallowing study tests culinary ground cinnamon in Parkinson's disease; though dry loose powder poses an obvious aspiration risk if swallowed dry, evidence for food-incorporated ground cinnamon on dysphagia remains open.
