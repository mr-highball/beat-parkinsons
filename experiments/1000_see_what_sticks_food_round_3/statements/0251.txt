ID: 0251
Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked celery stalk worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Clinical trials of celery stems/leaves do NOT replicate the blood-pressure reductions seen with high-dose celery seed extracts, and no study evaluates orthostatic lightheadedness in Parkinson's disease. In Mohsenpour et al. (2023, n=36 DB-RCT), 750 mg/d dried celery stem-and-leaf powder was completely null for SBP (p=0.334) and DBP (p=0.751); Mayra 2019 (mixed raw celery salad crossover) was null on BP; and the Liu et al. (2025) meta-analysis confirmed antihypertensive signals were driven by celery SEED preparations (>1,000 mg/d; e.g., Shayani Rad 2022). Illes (2021) was a single confounded chiropractic+celery-juice case report, while Indonesian Tenggerese (Nugraha 2022) and Lebanese (Samaha 2019) ethnobotany provide traditional antihypertensive context without PD orthostatic evidence.
