Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical swallowing study evaluates pasteurized cow's milk on Parkinson's dysphagia; in a double-blind sensory RCT (Pinnock & Arney 1993), subjective throat coating (+39%), urge to swallow (+31%), and thicker-feeling saliva (+42%) occurred identically after cow's milk and a matched soy placebo, refuting a cow's-milk-specific swallowing/mucus effect.
