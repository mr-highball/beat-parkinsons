Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground celery seed worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): two independent Parkinson's rodent studies of celery-seed-derived DL-3-n-butylphthalide showed significantly improved olfactory performance—shortening buried-pellet latency (p<0.0001) and doubling olfactory marker protein in rotenone PD rats (Wang 2024, n=60) and improving olfactometry in A53T alpha-synuclein PD mice (Li 2021, n=30)—with zero worsening evidence, though synthetic isolated drug dosing and absent human olfactory trials keep inference minimal.
