ID: 0449
Category: herb
Ingredient: chives
Preparation: dried
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried chives reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human clinical trials or Parkinson's disease animal models evaluate dried common chives (Allium schoenoprasum) for orthostatic lightheadedness severity (strictly excluding garlic, onion, and Chinese chives Allium tuberosum). Available primary studies are limited to ex vivo erythrocyte rheology (Arihan 2021: null in healthy blood, adverse in sickle cell anemia), acute mouse stroke neuroprotection (Singh 2018), phytochemical profiling (Dai 2024; Ferreira 2022), and ethnobotanical/low-FODMAP culinary guidance.
