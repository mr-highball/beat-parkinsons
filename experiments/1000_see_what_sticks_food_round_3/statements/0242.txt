Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked celery stalk reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human clinical trial tests cooked celery stalk on executive function impairment in Parkinson's disease. Boonruamkaew et al. (2017) reported improved spatial and non-spatial memory with lower brain AChE in healthy non-PD mice given whole-plant methanolic Apium graveolens extract, but did not measure human attention/executive control or cooked culinary stalks; synthetic NBP cognitive studies (Zhou 2019; Sukmueng 2026) represent a distinct pharmaceutical drug.
