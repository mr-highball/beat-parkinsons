Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward worsening): in a direct randomized crossover trial of plain 72% dark chocolate versus 0% cocoa white chocolate (Fox 2019, n=16 healthy adults, 100 g/d for 5 days), dark chocolate significantly firmed stool consistency (Bristol Stool Score 3 vs 4, p=0.011) with a trend toward slower colonic transit (21 vs 17 h, p=0.075), matching patient perceptions in a 1,088-person gastrointestinal survey where chocolate ranked #1 as a constipating food (48-64%; Muller-Lissner 2005). Positive bowel-softening cocoa trials (Sarria 2012; Castillejo 2006) tested fiber-enriched soluble cocoa drinks or cocoa-husk fiber supplements rather than plain dark chocolate, and no PD trial exists.
