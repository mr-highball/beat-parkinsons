Category: food
Ingredient: chia seed
Preparation: soaked
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming soaked chia seed reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for soaked chia seed reducing orthostatic lightheadedness severity is Unknown (Researched). Systematic reviews and meta-analyses of adult chia seed RCTs (Saadh et al. 2025, PMID:39672763; TaghipourSheshdeh et al. 2025, PMID:39225983) evaluate resting supine/seated systolic and diastolic blood pressure in hypertension, diabetes, or obesity cohorts, where modest resting BP reductions do not measure—and could theoretically differ from or oppose—standing orthostatic blood-pressure stability or lightheadedness in PD neurogenic orthostatic hypotension. Zero tilt-table or standing orthostasis studies of chia seed exist.
