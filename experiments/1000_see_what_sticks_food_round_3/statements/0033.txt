Category: spice
Ingredient: caraway
Preparation: ground
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground caraway reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no controlled human trial tests monotherapy ground Carum carvi for constipation in Parkinson's disease or functional constipation. Monotherapy human motility data actually point away from pro-motility benefit—50 mg caraway oil trended toward slowing orocaecal transit in healthy adults (Goerg & Spilker 2003, p=0.06)—while ex vivo human gut strips show opposing spasmolytic and pro-secretory actions (Krueger 2020). Clinical laxative trials showing bowel relief are strictly excluded as confounded multi-herb formulas containing stimulant senna/aloe (Ghafarzadegan 2025) or misidentified Bunium persicum (Yousefi 2019).
