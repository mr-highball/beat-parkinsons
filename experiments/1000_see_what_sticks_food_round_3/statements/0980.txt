ID: 0980
Category: spice
Ingredient: dill seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted dill seed reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether toasted dill seed reduces constipation severity in Parkinson's disease because no controlled study tests dill seeds on stool frequency or colonic transit (Haidari 2020 used dried dill leaves, not seeds, and Bordbar 2020 used a 3-herb oil blend).
