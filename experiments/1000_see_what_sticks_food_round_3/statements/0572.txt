Ingredient: cod
Preparation: poached
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached cod worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial has tested whether poached Atlantic cod fillet worsens Parkinson's bradykinesia. In older adults, 12 months of daily Atlantic cod fillet protein hydrolysate (Nygard 2021, n=86) had null effects on Short Physical Performance Battery (p=0.41), gait speed, and grip strength, white fish was null on functional impairment in Seniors-ENRICA (Garcia-Esquinas 2019), and cell-free Atlantic cod beta-parvalbumin inhibition of alpha-synuclein fibrils (Werner 2018) is purely in vitro biophysics.
