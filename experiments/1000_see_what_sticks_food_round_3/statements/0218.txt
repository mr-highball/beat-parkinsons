ID: 0218
Category: spice
Ingredient: celery seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground celery seed worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): two placebo-controlled RCTs confirm celery seed extract lowers resting/24-hour blood pressure in hypertensive (Shayani Rad 2022, n=52, SBP -11.2 mmHg, p<0.001) and metabolic syndrome adults (Escobedo-Gutierrez 2026, n=28, SBP p=0.001), while Shayani Rad 2023 reported lower scores on the Beck Anxiety dizziness item (P<0.001) without any postural context; because no trial measured supine-to-standing BP drops or orthostatic lightheadedness, direction remains open.
