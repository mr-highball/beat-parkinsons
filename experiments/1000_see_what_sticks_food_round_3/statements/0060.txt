Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human clinical trials evaluate ground Elettaria cardamomum for insomnia (an aromatherapy + TMS registry entry has no results and does not test oral ground spice). Preclinical evidence is limited to crude cardamom extract prolonging pentobarbital-induced anesthesia in healthy mice (Gilani 2008, PMID:18037596), which does not translate to clinical PD insomnia (matching Round 1 claim 0945).
