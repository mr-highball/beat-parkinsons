ID: 0903
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A 2025 Cureus anemia trial of concentrated iron-standardized curry leaf extract (Orgen-I, 18 mg elemental iron/day) reported higher descriptive visit-4 'energetic' ratings without a validated fatigue scale, baseline scores, or between-group p-values alongside severe internal reporting contradictions; driven by anemia-iron repletion rather than culinary cooked curry leaf (and noting iron-levodopa chelation cautions), Parkinson's fatigue effects remain open.
