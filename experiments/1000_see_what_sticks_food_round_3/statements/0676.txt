Category: spice
Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground coriander seed reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for ground coriander seed affecting executive function impairment is Unknown (Researched). While Alqudah et al. 2024 (n=86 students, powdered seed) reported improved self-reported prospective/retrospective memory (PRMQ, P=0.006), Koppula 2021 improved passive avoidance in MPTP mice, and Mima 2020 (PMC7071483) improved Barnes-maze spatial memory in SAMP8 mice, Zargar-Nattaj et al. 2011 (PMC3218779) reported mixed offspring passive-avoidance results (worse at 1 h, better at 24 h-1 wk). Critically, none of these human or animal studies administered dedicated attention or executive-function tests (executive function impairment; outcome_fit: remote), and leaf-extract memory studies (Mani 2011) are excluded.
