Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial isolates plain pasteurized cow's milk on fall frequency in Parkinson's disease or older adults; Iuliano 2021 (BMJ cluster RCT, n=7,195 aged-care residents) reduced falls (HR 0.89, p=0.04) using bundled milk + yogurt + cheese foodservice menus (unable to attribute to plain milk alone), while isolated milk/whey mobility trials (Huschtscha 2021 n=37; Zhu 2015 n=196) and a 36-RCT meta-analysis (Dewansingh 2018) found null physical-performance gains.
