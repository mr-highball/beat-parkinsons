Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward worsening): two controlled non-PD bladder trials—bridged to brewed coffee via Jeon 2019 HPLC and matching Round 2 black tea precedent 0641—show caffeinated beverages worsen urinary urgency: Wells 2014 (n=11 completed OAB women DB crossover) found decaffeinated vs caffeinated drinks reduced urgency (p<0.01), frequency (p<0.05), and ICIQ-OAB (p<0.01), and Bryant 2002 (n=95 continence patients RCT) showed caffeine reduction improved urgency (p=0.002) and frequency (p=0.037), supported by Gleason 2013 (NHANES n=4,309) and Park 2023 review. Tempered by Swithinbank 2005 (n=69 null), Ha 2025 mixed review, and Sauerbier 2018 null PD NMSS urinary domain.
