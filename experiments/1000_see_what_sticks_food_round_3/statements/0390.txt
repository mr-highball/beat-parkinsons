Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted chicken reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): Controlled human studies of chicken extracts show null overall sleep quality and insomnia outcomes, and no study has tested roasted chicken meat in Parkinson's insomnia. In Wu 2020 (n=73), 6 weeks of hydrolyzed chicken extract had null total PSQI score (p=0.935); in the post-hoc poor-sleeper subgroup of the same trial (Phua 2026, n=52), global PSQI (p=0.176), sleep latency (p=0.847), sleep duration (p=0.259), and sleep efficiency (p=0.392) were all null (only single-item Component 1 subjective quality reached p=0.006 for a hydrolyzed peptide tablet); and Young 2015 was null on GHQ-Sleep.
