Category: herb
Ingredient: celery leaf
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh celery leaf worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): consistent with Round 3 celery-stalk precedents (0230, 0237), no controlled human trial evaluates fresh celery leaf for depressive symptom severity. Human observational raw-celery intake was completely null for anxiety (r=+0.008) and depression (r=-0.060; Brookie 2018, n=422), Shayani Rad 2023 tested excluded celery SEED extract (1.34 g/d), Boonruamkaew 2017 and Jager 2013 provide only healthy-mouse solvent-extract/in vitro MAO-A data, and Rouhi-Boroujeni 2016 is a single high-dose (4 g/d x 45 d) dried-leaf hyperthyroidism case.
