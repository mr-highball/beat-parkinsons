Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces dream enactment frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No human clinical trial, observational cohort, or animal experiment has tested whether ground cayenne pepper alters REM sleep behavior disorder (RBD) or dream enactment episodes in Parkinson's disease. Sleep PSG studies of evening Tabasco/mustard (Edwards 1992) and intraoral capsaicin (Stuck 2016) enrolled healthy sleepers without RBD and reported sleep disruption rather than dream-enactment outcomes.
