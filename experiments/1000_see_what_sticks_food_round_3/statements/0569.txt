Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: baked
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked cod worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial has tested baked Atlantic cod (Gadus morhua) fillet for anxiety in Parkinson's disease or general populations. Fish-anxiety trials in the archive tested high-EPA/DHA fatty Atlantic salmon against mixed meat (Hansen 2014, PMC4276975), which is nutritionally distinct from lean white cod fillet, and white-fish aging cohorts (Garcia-Esquinas 2019) showed null mental-health associations.
