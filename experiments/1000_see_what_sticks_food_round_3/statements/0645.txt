Category: food
Ingredient: collard greens
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked collard greens reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human clinical trials, PD observational cohorts, or preclinical Parkinson's models test cooked collard greens (Brassica oleracea var. viridis) for fatigue severity. Analytical studies prove broccoli sulforaphane findings cannot be transferred to collards: edible collard leaves contain low total glucosinolates (2.64 umol/g DW) with no detectable leaf glucoraphanin (Đulović 2023, PMC9965355), marked cultivar variability down to 1.0% glucoraphanin (Lee 2017, PMC5612653), progoitrin content (Felker 2016), and thermal myrosinase inactivation/leaching during cooking (Song & Thornalley 2007). Local NCT05532358 mentions collards only in vitamin K exclusion criteria. Southern US (Yang 2013), East African sukuma wiki (Ikuomola 2025), and Mediterranean domestication (Mabry 2021) records provide cultural dietary context only, leaving fatigue severity Unknown (Researched).
