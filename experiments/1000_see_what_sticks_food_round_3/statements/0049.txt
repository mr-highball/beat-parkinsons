ID: 0049
Category: spice
Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human clinical trials evaluate monotherapy ground Elettaria cardamomum for anxiety (Cohen 2010 tested confounded baked-apple-cardamom chewing gum during nicotine withdrawal). Preclinical rodent anxiety evidence is contradictory and methodologically compromised: Al-Dalaeen 2026 (PMC12859731) reported reduced marble burying in obese mice but suffers severe full-text statistical/reporting inconsistencies while its companion study (PMID:40005380) found null open-field anxiety; Masoumi-Ardakani 2017 used parenteral i.p. extract in PTSD rats; Abu-Taweel 2020 tested perinatal maternal exposure; and isolated 1,8-cineole (31-46% of cardamom oil) showed no anxiolytic effect (Figueiredo 2019).
