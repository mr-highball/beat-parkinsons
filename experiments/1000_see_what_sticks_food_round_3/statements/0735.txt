Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial tests plain pasteurized cow's milk on Parkinson's pain severity; outside PD, exercise-induced muscle soreness trials are predominantly null or unclear (Rankin 2018 n=18; Pearson 2023 n=24 milk protein null on DOMS/PPT; only Rankin 2015 n=32 favored 500 mL milk via magnitude-based inference), while conventional cow's milk worsened abdominal pain in milk-intolerant adults in Jianqin 2016 (n=45) but was null in Greenway 2026.
