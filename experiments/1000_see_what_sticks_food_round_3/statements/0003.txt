Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical trial tests whether raw cantaloupe worsens or eases constipation in Parkinson's disease. Prokinetic rodent laxative findings (Gao et al., 2012) used rectal extracts of the bitter non-edible muskmelon stalk (Pedicellus Melo), not oral raw cantaloupe flesh, and a human crossover of unripe Apulian melon (Khalil et al., 2025) did not measure constipation. Public Parkinson's UK forum discussions preserve mixed lived experience: one contributor favored melon as a soluble-fiber fruit alongside warm water, while another warned fruit intake alone failed to prevent severe colonic obstruction.
