Ingredient: celery seed

Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground celery seed reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial or constipation model tests ground celery seed on constipation severity; in a 28-person metabolic syndrome RCT of celery seed extract (Escobedo-Gutierrez 2026), mild diarrhea occurred in 1/14 active vs 1/14 placebo and constipation in 0/14 vs 1/14 as incidental AE tracking only, while colitis rat models (Minaiyan 2021) and bundled dyspepsia trials (Azimi 2017) omit constipation outcomes.
