Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: poached
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming poached cod reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): Observational European multicohort evidence does NOT support an independent antidepressant effect of lean cod fillet, and no controlled cod depression trial exists. In the four-cohort European Southern European Atlantic Diet (SEAD) study (Carballo-Casla 2023, n=13,297), individual food components including cod were NOT independently associated with depression risk, and Seniors-ENRICA (Garcia-Esquinas 2019) found white fish null on mental health.
