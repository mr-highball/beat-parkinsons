ID: 0868
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: visual hallucination frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise reduces visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) reduces visual hallucination frequency in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses visual hallucination frequency; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
