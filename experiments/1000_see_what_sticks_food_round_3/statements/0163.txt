Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial has evaluated ground cayenne pepper for Parkinson's drooling (sialorrhea). While acute oral capsaicin transiently stimulates salivary gland secretion via trigeminal reflexes in healthy volunteers (Duner-Engstrom 1986; Kanehira 2011; Houghton 2020; Gardner 2020; Han 2025), that is an acute healthy-subject secretion proxy rather than clinical PD drooling (which stems primarily from impaired swallow clearance), repeated exposure stimulated salivation in only 9/23 subjects with habituation (Nasrawi 1990), and chronic oral capsaicin rinses in burning mouth syndrome did not alter resting salivary flow (Jankovskis 2021).
