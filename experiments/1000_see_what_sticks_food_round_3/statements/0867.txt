ID: 0867
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) worsens urinary urgency frequency in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses urinary urgency frequency; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
