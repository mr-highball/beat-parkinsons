Symptom: insomnia severity

Statement: Consuming steamed cauliflower worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical evidence indicates steamed cauliflower worsens or eases Parkinson's insomnia. In the UK Women's Cohort Study (Noorwali et al., 2018, n=12,842), cauliflower consumption had a completely null relationship with sleep duration (-0.03 min/d, p=0.2).
