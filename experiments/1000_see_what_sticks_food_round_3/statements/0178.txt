Preparation: ground
Symptom: visual hallucination frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No human clinical trial, observational cohort, or experimental model has tested whether ground cayenne pepper alters visual hallucinations or Parkinson's disease psychosis. Archived PD non-motor surveys (Sauerbier 2018) and dopaminergic rodent studies (Wei 2025; Serra 2021) contain no hallucination or psychosis outcomes.
