Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): an 8-week triple-blind RCT directly testing plain 78% dark chocolate versus milk chocolate (Abdoli 2024, n=60 menopausal women) found NO significant effect on overall PSQI sleep quality or any PSQI subdomain (p>0.05), and Hernandez-Gonzalez 2021 (n=19) tested timing of milk chocolate rather than plain dark chocolate or insomnia severity.
