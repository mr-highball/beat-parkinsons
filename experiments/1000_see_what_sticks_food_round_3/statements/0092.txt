Category: food
Ingredient: cashew
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted cashew worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): no controlled evidence indicates roasted cashews worsen anxiety; whole cashew-nut feeding eased elevated-plus-maze and open-field anxiety-like behavior in dyslipidemic rats (Dias 2023), corroborated by GABAA-mediated anxiolytic activity of cashew anacardic acids in mice (Gomes Junior 2018, bridged to processed kernels by Mattison 2018), whereas human anxiety trials and Parkinson's cohorts are absent and one allergic case reflects IgE anaphylaxis fear rather than dietary pharmacology.
