Symptom: urinary urgency frequency

Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human clinical trials evaluate ground green cardamom on urinary urgency frequency. Notably, both preclinical pharmacology (Gilani 2008, PMID:18037596: crude cardamom extract increased diuresis and natriuresis in rats) and Ladakh Amchi ethnobotany document diuretic activity—pointing away from reducing urgency frequency—yet rodent urine volume does not directly measure PD overactive bladder or urgency.
