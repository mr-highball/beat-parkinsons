ID: 0934
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial tests plain dark chocolate on orthostatic blood-pressure drops or lightheadedness symptoms in Parkinson's disease; physiological studies outside PD measured resting blood pressure lowering (Grassi 2016) or reduced supine cardiac baroreflex sensitivity after an acute cocoa drink in healthy older adults (Hamaoka 2026, n=17, p=0.018, resting BP/HR null) without assessing orthostatic lightheadedness.
