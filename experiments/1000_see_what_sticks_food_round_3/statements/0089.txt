ID: 0089
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming steamed carrot worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Qualitative PD eating-difficulty studies, food-texture rheology, and Parkinson's UK forum caregiving discussions consistently emphasize texture preparation: raw hard carrot requires high masticatory effort (Peyron 2004; Oncescu Moraru 2019), whereas steaming, cooking, or pureeing softens carrots and slows pharyngeal flow (Forsberg 2022, 2024; Moritaka 2012; Ettinger 2014). However, no controlled clinical trial measures whether steamed carrot alters neurogenic Parkinson's dysphagia severity, leaving the claim open.
