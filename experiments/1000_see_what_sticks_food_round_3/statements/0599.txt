Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward worsening): controlled human trials and three meta-analyses demonstrate that caffeine and brewed coffee acutely increase anxiety and jitteriness—especially at >200 mg or in low-habitual consumers and ADORA2A rs5751876 TT carriers (Rogers 2010 n=379; Haskell-Ramsay 2018 n=59 brewed coffee crossover; Ramirez-delaCruz 2024 n=29; Zhang 2026 15-RCT MA SMD 0.51; Liu 2024 MA SMD 0.94; Klevebrant 2022 MA). Tempered by null PD anxiety contrasts in Postuma 2012 (n=61), Postuma 2017 (n=118), Sauerbier 2018 (n=139), and Gabbert 2023 (n=4,914) and lower BAI at 400 mg/d in OAB women (Staack 2022), keeping evidence at minimal_signal.
