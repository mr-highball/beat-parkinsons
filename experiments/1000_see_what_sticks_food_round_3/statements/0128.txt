Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed cauliflower reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for steamed cauliflower and Parkinson's constipation is open and mixed. Non-PD observational FODMAP surveys diverge (Kaneko 2019 n=1,024 linked cauliflower with lower GI problems in Japanese checkups, whereas Abraham 2023 n=604 noted cauliflower intolerance in IBS-diarrhea vs IBS-constipation), Fleury 2021 (local synthesis) pooled broccoli and cauliflower in a Geneva PD FFQ without isolating cauliflower, and Parkinson's UK forum accounts preserve both a confounded cauliflower-bhaji constipation relief anecdote and gastroparesis/low-FODMAP avoidance warnings for bloating.
