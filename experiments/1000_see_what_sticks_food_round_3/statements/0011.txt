ID: 0011
Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two independent double-blind randomized trials of coated cantaloupe juice concentrate (Extramel 10 mg/d) in stressed healthy adults point away from worsening and toward modestly easing executive dysfunction: Carillon et al. (2014, n=61) directly assessed Stroop and reverse-Stroop executive inhibition/cognitive flexibility and reported significant gains vs placebo at day 28 (+13.9%, p<0.04) and day 84 (+20.8%, p<0.001), supported by Milesi et al. (2009, n=70) improving the FARD Cognitive pole (p=0.032). Because the frozen claim predicts worsening while the indirect human RCT signal leans toward easing, this warrants a minimal directional verdict of Maybe False (50.005), bounded by coated-concentrate and non-PD transferability limits.
