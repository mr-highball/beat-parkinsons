Statement: Consuming ground celery seed worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): oral whole-plant Apium graveolens extract lowered separately scored resting tremor in MPTP parkinsonian mice (Chonpathompikunlert 2018, n=48, standardized to apigenin and luteolin also present in celery seeds), with zero evidence of worsening; Zhou 2019's synthetic NBP PD tremor table duplicates PDQ-39 values (unusable) and a 2019 Parkinson's UK celery-seed supplement post lacks diagnosis and attribution, keeping the easing lean minimal.
