Symptom: insomnia severity

Statement: Consuming ground celery seed reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): in a triple-blind, placebo-controlled crossover RCT in 50 hypertensive adults (Shayani Rad 2023), 1.34 g/day celery seed extract reduced the Beck Depression Inventory insomnia item (P<0.01); although Zhou 2019 also reported lower PSQI with synthetic NBP in 103 PD patients, its Table II printed baseline PSQI duplicates the HAM-D baseline without correction (reliability unusable), leaving only a minimal non-PD extract lean.
