Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity

Statement: Consuming ground curry leaf reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: No standing blood-pressure or orthostatic-dizziness study tests ground curry leaf; a weak quasi-experimental rural hypertension report (Gopal 2023) measured resting blood pressure rather than postural lightheadedness, leaving Parkinson's orthostatic lightheadedness open.
