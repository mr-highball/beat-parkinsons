ID: 0864
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: small handwriting severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise reduces small handwriting severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) reduces small handwriting severity in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses small handwriting severity; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
