Ingredient: caraway
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted caraway worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero clinical trials, pharmacokinetic studies, or patient case reports evaluate toasted Carum carvi for Parkinson's dyskinesia severity. While Naghibi et al. (2015, PMC4310175) documented marked TSH elevation when Carum carvi capsules were co-ingested with levothyroxine, no study has tested whether culinary caraway alters levodopa absorption, motor fluctuations, or dyskinesia.
