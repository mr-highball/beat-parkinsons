Ingredient: chia seed
Preparation: soaked
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming soaked chia seed reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: A single controlled rodent experiment provides a weak preclinical signal favoring reduced depressive-like behavior: Bilgic et al. 2018 (PMID:30624431) reported that whole dietary chia seeds attenuated depression-like behavior in Porsolt's forced swim test in both pretreatment and treatment arms of aluminum chloride-induced Alzheimer's disease rats, with no opposing controlled depression studies found. A separate rodent paper (Adel et al. 2019, PMID:31278713) claimed antidepressant potential in its title but did not run forced swim or tail suspension assays and is kept contextual. Because there are zero human clinical depression trials of soaked chia seed in PD or non-PD cohorts and Bilgic 2018 lacked arm-level abstract FST means, certainty remains minimal_signal (50.005).
