Symptom: freezing of gait frequency

Statement: Consuming ground celery seed worsens freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial or preclinical study isolates ground culinary celery seed on freezing of gait frequency; Zhou 2019 (synthetic NBP, n=103 PD) reported only a pooled off-medication UPDRS-III non-tremor subtotal with unrectified Table II anomalies and no separate freezing of gait frequency estimate, while rodent NBP models (Wang 2024, Hu 2023) measured general open-field/rotarod activity rather than freezing of gait frequency.
