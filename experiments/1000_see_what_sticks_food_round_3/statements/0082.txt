ID: 0082
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: raw
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw carrot reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No study evaluates raw carrot consumption on orthostatic blood pressure or postural lightheadedness in Parkinson's disease. In 187,453 US health professionals (Borgi et al., 2016), higher carrot intake was associated with slightly lower incident hypertension (HR 0.95, 95% CI 0.91-0.99), a single-arm fresh carrot juice pilot showed a borderline resting SBP decrease (Potter 2011, p=0.06; DBP NS), and the QUENCH double-blind purple-carrot RCT (Wright 2013, n=16) was completely null for blood pressure. Resting blood-pressure signals neither test nor imply relief of neurogenic orthostatic lightheadedness.
