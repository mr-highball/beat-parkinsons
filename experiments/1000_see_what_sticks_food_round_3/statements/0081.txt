ID: 0081
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed carrot reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for steamed carrot and Parkinson's muscle rigidity severity remains open. Custódio-Silva 2024 showed oral purple carrot extract reduced bar catalepsy in reserpine rats, but did not isolate mechanical muscle rigidity from akinesia and tested unheated purple carrot extract rather than steamed culinary orange carrot. No clinical trial evaluates steamed culinary carrot on muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's.
