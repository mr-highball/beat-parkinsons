Category: food
Ingredient: cauliflower
Preparation: raw
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw cauliflower reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical trial tests raw cauliflower for depressive symptoms in Parkinson's disease. Individual-food analysis in Brookie et al. (2018, n=422) found cauliflower null for depressive symptoms (r=-0.035, NS), while an umbrella review (Li et al., 2022) pooled all cruciferous vegetables with predominantly low-quality observational evidence (68%) and zero Parkinson's trials.
