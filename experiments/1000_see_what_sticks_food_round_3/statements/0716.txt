ID: 0716
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial tests plain pasteurized cow's milk on Parkinson's bradykinesia severity; a 6-month PD whey-protein RCT (Tosukhowong 2016, n=32) showed null composite UPDRS scores despite raising plasma BCAAs, and dairy epidemiology (Park 2005; Hughes 2017; Olsson 2020; Hajji-Louati 2024; Zhang 2021 MR) measures PD diagnosis incidence rather than bradykinesia severity.
