ID: 0615
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward worsening): randomized trials directly testing brewed caffeinated coffee show clear nighttime sleep disruption—the NEJM CRAVE randomized case-crossover (Marcus 2023, n=100) showed caffeinated coffee cut nightly sleep by 36 minutes (397 vs 432 min, 95% CI -47 to -25), and Hindmarch 2000 (n=30 brewed coffee crossover) worsened LSEQ sleep onset, duration, and quality (p<0.001)—corroborated by caffeine-timing sleep RCTs (Drake 2013; Gardiner 2025; Pauchon 2024). In PD, Café-PD (Postuma 2017, n=118) showed small non-significant nighttime sleep worsening (CIs crossing zero) while morning-only dosing (Postuma 2012 PSQI; Staack 2022 ISI) was null, keeping evidence at minimal_signal.
