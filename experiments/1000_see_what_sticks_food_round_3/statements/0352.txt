Rationale: Evidence for soaked chia seed affecting dystonia severity is Unknown (Researched). No controlled clinical trials or PD animal experiments evaluate soaked chia seed on dystonia severity. Public Parkinson's UK community threads (2017 turmeric/black-pepper/chia routine; Thread 7765 cramps/spasms remark) contain uncontrolled, multi-ingredient personal anecdotes with uncertain independence across posts, and in vitro chia peptide microglial/neuronal oxidative-stress assays (Martínez Leo 2020) lack motor symptom outcomes.
