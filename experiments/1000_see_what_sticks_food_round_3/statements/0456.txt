Category: herb
Ingredient: chives
Preparation: dried
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried chives reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human clinical trials or animal bladder studies evaluate dried common chives (Allium schoenoprasum) on urinary urgency frequency. A Traditional Persian Medicine review (Kolangi 2018, PMID:29623832) preserves a classical caution listing Allium schoenoprasum among potentially renal-irritant herbs, but this lacks modern clinical verification and provides no direct evidence on PD urinary urgency.
