Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Both published human cinnamon sleep trials used non-oral routes—inhaled cinnamon leaf oil in brain-tumor patients (Zhang 2026) and intranasal bark spray for allergic rhinitis (Steels 2019)—leaving oral ground cinnamon's effect on Parkinson's insomnia unresolved.
