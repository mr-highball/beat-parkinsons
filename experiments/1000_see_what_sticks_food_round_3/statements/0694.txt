Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted coriander seed worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for toasted coriander seed worsening urinary urgency frequency is Unknown (Researched). Local archived human testing of dried coriander seed infusion (Udupihille 1986) found null diuretic effects at 100 g/L, and Aissaoui 2007 tested intravenous infusion in anesthetized rats measuring acute urine output/electrolytes rather than oral intake or bladder urgency/overactive bladder symptoms. Zero clinical urinary urgency studies exist for toasted coriander seed.
