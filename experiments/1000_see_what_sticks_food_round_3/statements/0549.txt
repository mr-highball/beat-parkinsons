ID: 0549
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): a 2026 older-adult crossover found no difference between coconut oil and canola oil on FAIR-2 attention (p=0.632; Diekmann 2026, n=29), the 24-week VCO-AD DB-RCT was overall null (Fernando 2023, n=120), a 21-day AD pilot bundled a Mediterranean diet without isolated attention testing (de la Rubia Orti 2018), and no trial isolates refined coconut oil in Parkinson attention.
