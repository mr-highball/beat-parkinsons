Ingredient: cauliflower
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted cauliflower worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No study evaluates roasted cauliflower on postural blood pressure or orthostatic lightheadedness in Parkinson's disease. While the VESSEL crossover trial (Connolly et al., 2024, n=17) found boiled 4-vegetable cruciferous soup (25% cauliflower alongside 40% broccoli, 25% cabbage, 10% kale) lowered 24-h SBP by 2.5 mmHg (p=0.002) with zero orthostatic adverse events, individual cauliflower intake in 187,453 US health professionals (Borgi et al., 2016) trended slightly upward for hypertension (HR 1.06, 95% CI 0.99-1.14).
