ID: 0027
Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Although ethnobotanical and preclinical reviews note traditional diuretic and anti-urolithiatic uses of Cucumis melo fruit and seeds (Wright et al., 2007; Ullah et al., 2015; Allam & Sabra, 2024), no controlled human trial tests whether eating raw cantaloupe increases or eases urinary urgency frequency in Parkinson's disease.
