ID: 0855
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise worsens low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) worsens low voice volume severity in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses low voice volume severity; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
