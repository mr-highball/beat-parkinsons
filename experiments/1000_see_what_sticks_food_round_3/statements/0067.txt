ID: 0067
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed carrot reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Preparation-specific human observational evidence directly undercuts extrapolating raw-carrot mood associations to steamed carrots: in Brookie et al. (2018, n=422 young adults), cooked/processed carrots were completely null for both depressive symptoms (r=+0.017, NS) and anxiety (r=+0.057, NS), and even raw carrots were null for anxiety (r=-0.085, NS). In mice (Liu et al., 2022), anxiolytic activity occurred only after Lactobacillus brevis fermentation (producing GABA/SCFAs) relative to unfermented carrot juice. No clinical trial tests steamed carrot on Parkinson's anxiety severity.
