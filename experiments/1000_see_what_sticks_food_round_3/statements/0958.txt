Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried date reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): whole Ajwa dates produced null POMS fatigue in an uncontrolled human study (Abdullah 2019), acute alertness was cultivar-inconsistent (Altuwairki PMC13440079), athlete fatigue testing used date seed powder rather than fruit (Moslemi 2023), and anemia trials did not assess fatigue severity.
