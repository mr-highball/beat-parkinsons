ID: 0514
Category: food
Ingredient: clams
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked clams reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two randomized human trials of freshwater clam (Corbicula fluminea) extract support a modest directional signal for improved sleep onset and maintenance: Shimazoe et al. 2022 (DOI 10.31989/ffhd.v12i6.928, n=34 analyzed double-blind parallel RCT) found 12 weeks of normal-dose freshwater clam extract softgels significantly improved OSA-MA sleep onset, sleep maintenance, and sleep duration compared with placebo; and Chen et al. 2023 (PMID:33421042, n=52 randomized postoperative septoturbinoplasty pilot) found 14 days of clam extract capsules significantly reduced sleep disruption and serum TNF-alpha (P<0.05, though partially mediated by nasal-blockage relief). Boiled freshwater clam soup carries ornithine and taurine bioactives (Yu 2020; Kuwano 2026), bridging cooked culinary intake to extracts. Certainty is capped at minimal_signal (50.005) because both trials tested concentrated freshwater Corbicula extracts rather than whole cooked clams (and not marine clam species) in non-PD populations.
