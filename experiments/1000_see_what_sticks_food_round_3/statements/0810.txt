ID: 0810
Category: herb
Ingredient: culinary lavender
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed culinary lavender reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether brewed culinary lavender reduces daytime sleepiness severity in Parkinson's disease because controlled oral lavender oil trials show placebo-equivalent (null) sleepiness without wake-promoting benefit (PMID:33221027, PMID:33300578) and no brewed lavender tea trial evaluates Epworth Sleepiness Scale scores.
