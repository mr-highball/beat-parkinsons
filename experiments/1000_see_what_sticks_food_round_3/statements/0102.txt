Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted cashew reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): in a 6-OHDA-lesioned Parkinson's mouse model, co-treatment with the cashew constituent anacardic acid (2 mg/kg) significantly reduced L-DOPA-induced axial, limb, and orofacial (ALO) dyskinesia scores without diminishing L-DOPA motor benefit (Ryu 2018), bridged chemically to processed cashew nuts (Mattison 2018), though isolated HAT-inhibitor dosing and absence of whole-cashew or human PD trials keep inference minimal.
