Category: food
Ingredient: chickpeas
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked chickpeas worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): human chickpea tolerance data showed no abdominal pain worsening (Dahl 2014, n=12), chickpea flour acted as an inert placebo in a pain RCT (Mozafari 2021), preclinical antinociception used crude methanol extracts or isolated biochanin A, and no Parkinson pain trials exist.
