Ingredient: caraway
Preparation: ground
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground caraway worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial or Parkinson's disease study has evaluated ground Carum carvi for attention impairment. Preclinical evidence is limited to distilled caraway essential oil improving spatial memory and AChE markers in an aluminum-chloride rat neurotoxicity model (Auti & Kulkarni 2025, PMC12296912), which tests neither culinary ground caraway nor human PD attention/executive domains.
