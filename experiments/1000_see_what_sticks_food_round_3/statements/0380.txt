ID: 0380
Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: baked
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming baked chicken reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial has tested baked chicken meat for excessive daytime sleepiness in Parkinson's disease. In the hydrolyzed chicken extract trial (Wu 2020 n=73; post-hoc poor-sleeper re-analysis Phua 2026 n=52, same parent trial), overall PSQI total score was null (p=0.935) and PSQI daytime dysfunction did not reach significance (p=0.064), while Young 2015 found null GHQ-Sleep scores.
