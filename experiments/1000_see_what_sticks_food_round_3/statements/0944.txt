ID: 0944
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): two controlled peripheral-artery-disease trials of plain >85% dark chocolate or cocoa beverage showed significant walking gains—Loffredo 2014 (n=20 PAD crossover: 40 g >85% dark vs milk chocolate acutely increased maximal walking distance +11% and walking time +15%, p<0.001) and COCOA-PAD (McDermott 2020, n=40 completed 6-mo DB RCT: cocoa improved 6-minute walk distance +42.6 m, p=0.005)—and PD/MS feasibility pilots leaned weakly positive with CIs spanning zero (Coe 2022 PD n=30 6MWT ES 0.11 [-0.11 to 0.26]; Coe 2019 MS n=40 6MWT ES 0.45 [-0.18 to 1.07]). Strongly tempered by the 2-year COSMOS RCT (Chou 2026, n=603 older adults: 500 mg/d cocoa extract completely NULL on walking speed, SPPB, and TUG), capping evidence at minimal_signal.
