Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial or animal constipation model tests roasted cashew nuts on stool frequency or constipation severity; the 8-week roasted cashew RCT (Meneguelli 2024, n=64) measured intestinal permeability (null) rather than bowel habits, an acute cashew-byproduct beverage crossover (Santana 2026, n=27) found null GI symptoms, and antidiarrheal studies used non-nut stem bark or leaves.
