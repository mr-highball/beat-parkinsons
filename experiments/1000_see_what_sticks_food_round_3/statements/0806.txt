Statement: Consuming brewed culinary lavender reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether brewed culinary lavender reduces attention impairment in Parkinson's disease because oral Silexan was equivalent to placebo on objective sustained-attention driving tests (PMID:33221027), subjective concentration improvement in MDD was an unadjusted exploratory item (PMID:42550483), and no brewed tea or PD attention study exists.
