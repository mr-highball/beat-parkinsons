ID: 0550
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): no human PD trial isolates refined coconut oil for bradykinesia; whole ketogenic/MCT PD diets showed no between-diet motor superiority (Phillips 2018, Choi 2024, Tosefsky 2026), and preclinical rat studies tested hot-processed VCO without task-specific estimates (Deepika 2026) or isolated lauric acid (Zaidi 2020).
