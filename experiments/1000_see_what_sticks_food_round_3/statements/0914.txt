ID: 0914
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: ground
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground curry leaf reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human gait-speed data exist for ground curry leaf, and preclinical dopaminergic motor studies directly contradict one another—with toxin-model extract rescue (Reddy 2020; Anjum 2025; Shabir 2022) opposed by fraction-induced catalepsy prolongation and locomotor suppression (Patil & Langade 2021)—leaving Parkinson's walking slowness unresolved.
