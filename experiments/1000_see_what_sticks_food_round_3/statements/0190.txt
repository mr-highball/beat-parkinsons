Statement: Consuming fresh celery leaf worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): matching Round 3 celery-stalk precedent (0253), Chonpathompikunlert et al. (2018, PMC5859653, n=48 MPTP PD mice) evaluated balance via two independent, internally coherent assays—narrow-beam foot-slip errors (Fig 3b) and dynamic forelimb drag-test balance (Fig 1)—showing oral whole-plant Apium graveolens methanolic extract (250 and 375 mg/kg x 21 d, containing aerial leaves) significantly improved balance and preserved SNpc TH+ neurons vs MPTP+vehicle (p<0.05; Fig 5 rotarod excluded for text inversion). Leaf chemistry verifies fresh celery leaves supply higher bioavailable apigenin/apiin (Zhao 2008, Cao 2009, Mencherini 2007) and 3-5x higher phthalides (Kurobayashi 2006) than stalks. Strictly excluding synthetic DL-NBP capsules (Zhou 2019), this yields a minimal preclinical signal toward easing rather than worsening postural instability (Maybe False, anchor 50.005).
