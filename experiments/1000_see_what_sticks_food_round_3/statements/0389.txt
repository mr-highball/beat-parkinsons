Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: poached
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached chicken worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial has evaluated whether poached chicken worsens fine hand dexterity in Parkinson's disease. In older adults with knee osteoarthritis, a multi-ingredient Essence of Chicken plus hydrolyzed collagen II supplement improved grip strength vs glucosamine (Chen 2023) without testing manual dexterity or poached chicken meat, and Uchida 2024 assessed non-PD resistance training rather than fine finger dexterity.
