Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human clinical trials evaluate monotherapy green cardamom (Elettaria cardamomum) on executive function. Recent human cognitive RCTs reporting executive/attention benefits are strictly excluded on botanical and attribution boundaries: Thomas 2026 (PMC13662912, n=96) tested black cardamom (Amomum subulatum, a different genus/species), and Wightman 2023 (PMC10598389) tested a 10-herb blend. Preclinical rodent memory studies used essential oil in AlCl3 rats (Auti 2019) or suffered major full-text reporting discrepancies in obese mice (Al-Dalaeen 2026).
