Symptom: attention impairment

Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial shows oral ground cayenne pepper improves attention in Parkinson's disease; the 12-week Capsifen encapsulated capsaicinoid RCT in older overweight adults was null on NIH Toolbox Flanker Attention (p=0.209) and Pattern Comparison Processing Speed (p=0.196), while observational chili cohorts conflict (15-year prospective cognitive decline in Shi 2019 vs cross-sectional MMSE gains in Tian 2020).
