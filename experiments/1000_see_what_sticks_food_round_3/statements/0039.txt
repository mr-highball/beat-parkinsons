Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground caraway reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial or Parkinson's disease study has evaluated ground Carum carvi for executive function impairment. Preclinical evidence is limited to distilled caraway essential oil improving spatial memory and AChE markers in an aluminum-chloride rat neurotoxicity model (Auti & Kulkarni 2025, PMC12296912), which tests neither culinary ground caraway nor human PD attention/executive domains.
