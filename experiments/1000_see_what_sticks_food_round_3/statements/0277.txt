ID: 0277
Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): four oral human clinical trials report improved subjective sleep quality—including exact brewed chamomile tea in postnatal women (Chang & Chen 2016, n=80, P=0.015), oral chamomile solution in chemotherapy (Nikkhah 2026, n=80, PSQI P=0.027), and oral extracts in older adults (Adib-Hajbaghery 2017, n=60, P<0.05; Abdullahzadeh 2017, n=77, P<0.001; pooled PSQI WMD -1.88 in Kazemi 2024)—while the sole double-blind placebo-controlled chronic primary insomnia pilot was statistically null (Zick 2011, n=34) and no PD insomnia trial exists.
