ID: 0623
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: decaffeinated
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming decaffeinated coffee reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial evaluates decaffeinated coffee versus a non-coffee control on Parkinson's rest tremor severity; isolated caffeine tremor trials cannot transfer to decaffeinated coffee, UK Biobank (Zhang 2024) found decaf null for PD incidence, and PD forum discussions mention switching to decaf merely to avoid caffeinated coffee's tremor-worsening effect rather than showing intrinsic rest-tremor relief.
