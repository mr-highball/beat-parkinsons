Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical trial, observational cohort, or Parkinson animal model tests whether raw cantaloupe worsens olfactory impairment severity. Existing human melon studies evaluated coated SOD concentrates in non-PD adults for stress, fatigue, or musculoskeletal pain (Milesi 2009; Carillon 2014; Koike 2022) without validated olfactory impairment severity endpoints in Parkinson's disease.
