Category: food
Ingredient: clams
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked clams worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for cooked clams affecting fall frequency in PD is Unknown (Researched). Local cohort record PMC10497173 pools clams together with all shellfish in general older-adult frailty analyses without isolating cooked clams or PD motor gait/fall outcomes, and Hsieh 2017 (MPTP mouse locomotor rescue from freshwater clam extract) did not report gait velocity or falls. No controlled human trials test cooked clams on PD fall frequency.
