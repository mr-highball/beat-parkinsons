Statement: Consuming poached chicken worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial has specifically tested whether poached chicken worsens Parkinson's muscle rigidity. While high-protein meals can blunt levodopa ON states generally (Barichella 2006), no study has isolated poached chicken breast on MDS-UPDRS Part III rigidity subscores, and healthy-adult steamed chicken strength trials (Uchida 2024) do not assess parkinsonian rigidity.
