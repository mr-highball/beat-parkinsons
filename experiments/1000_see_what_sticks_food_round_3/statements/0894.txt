ID: 0894
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Rodent gastrointestinal transit studies of Murraya koenigii leaves directly conflict—accelerating diabetic transit (Tembhurne 2011) versus slowing transit as an antidiarrheal (Ramasamy 2016)—while human trials were either bundled and null (Lauche 2016) or lacked validated constipation metrics and statistics (Patel 2025), leaving Parkinson's constipation unresolved.
