Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: baked
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked cod worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial or observational study has linked baked Atlantic cod fillet to worsened Parkinson's rest tremor. Population-based Faroese meat/seafood tremor data (Ferrer & Louis 2020) showed no dietary meat/fish association with tremor or blood harmane after correction, and baked cod has low mercury levels (Dahl 2020).
