Ingredient: corn oil

Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): PD finger tapping was only recorded under a shared corn-oil carrier design (de Faria 2020, n=24), while older-adult handgrip trials used corn oil strictly as omega-3 placebo (Smith 2015; Engelen 2024) without testing fine dexterity or culinary corn oil.
