Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted chicken worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (50.005): Contrary to the frozen claim that roasted chicken worsens anxiety, two independent double-blind human RCTs of aqueous chicken soup or Essence of Chicken reported modest reductions in subjective tension/anxiety—Midoh & Noguchi 2009 (n=30 crossover, lower POMS Tension-Anxiety vs placebo, p<0.05) and Young, Benton & Carter 2015 (n=46 completers, lower VAS Anxious/Composed ratings, p<0.04, though GHQ-Anxiety was null)—bridged to thermally cooked chicken breast histidine dipeptides (carnosine/anserine) by Kouriki-Nagatomo 2019. Signal remains minimal because a larger hydrolyzed chicken extract RCT was null on STAI-State (p=0.354) and STAI-Trait (p=0.342) anxiety (Wu 2020, n=73), dry-heat roasting differs from aqueous chicken broth/extract, and no Parkinson's anxiety trial exists.
