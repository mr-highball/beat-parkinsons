ID: 0620
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: decaffeinated
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming decaffeinated coffee reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): ordinary decaffeinated coffee shows null analgesic effects across controlled non-PD trials—Haskell-Ramsay 2018 (n=59) found regular brewed coffee reduced headache versus decaf (p=0.0049) and placebo (p=0.015) while decaf was null vs placebo, Aoun 2021 (n=139) found dialysis headache identical between regular and decaf (p=0.522), and switching habitual drinkers to decaf provoked withdrawal headache (van Dusseldorp 1990). Camfield 2013 (n=60) reduced acute headache only with a specialized chlorogenic-acid-enriched green-roasted decaf blend, leaving ordinary decaffeinated coffee for PD pain open.
