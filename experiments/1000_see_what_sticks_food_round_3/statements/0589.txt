Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: grilled
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grilled cod reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): Evidence does NOT support grilled lean Atlantic cod fillet reducing clinical pain. In the prospective Seniors-ENRICA-1 cohort (Carballo-Casla 2022, n=950 older adults), oily fish was associated with reduced pain worsening whereas WHITE fish (lean cod-type fish) was explicitly NULL for pain. Human cod-protein feeding trials measured only systemic inflammatory biomarkers—hs-CRP reduction (Ouellet 2008, n=19) and serum neopterin (Helland 2021, NCT02350595) and urinary oxidative stress (Parra 2007)—without clinical pain outcomes; Mendelian randomization linked non-oily fish to higher serum uric acid (Ou 2024) without clinical gouty pain; and Parkinson's UK forum discussions (Thread 4189) reflect cod liver oil capsules rather than lean grilled cod fillet.
