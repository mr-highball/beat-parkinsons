Ingredient: chia seed
Preparation: soaked
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming soaked chia seed worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Opposing evidence leans against the claim that soaked chia seed worsens constipation: a 2025 randomized multi-armed prospective adult chronic constipation trial (Poltiyelova et al. 2025, ACG S1203; n=29 completers of 71 randomized) found chia seeds 2 tbsp/day significantly increased complete spontaneous bowel movements per week (P=0.001, comparable to PEG P=0.019), supported mechanistically by hydrated mucilage bulking and colonic fermentation (Ang 2023; Arioglu-Tuncil 2025). Confidence stays at minimal_signal (50.005) because Poltiyelova 2025 is a high-attrition conference abstract in non-PD chronic constipation, PD-specific fiber trials (NCT07914387) have no results yet, and PD community discussions document both benefit from soaked chia (PUK 8647) and serious warnings that excess bulk fiber can compact slow-transit PD bowels (PUK 7765).
