ID: 0172
Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): Controlled human dietary chili trials found null effects on resting blood pressure without testing standing orthostatic hypotension (Ahuja 2007 4-week 55% cayenne crossover, n=36; Janssens 2013 meal red chili RCT), while isolated human case reports run in opposite hemodynamic directions—vasovagal cardioinhibitory syncope with a NEGATIVE head-up tilt test (explicitly not orthostatic hypotension, Huang 2024) and seated chili syncope (Villagran 2021) versus hypertensive crises after heavy chili ingestion (Patane 2009; Patane 2010).
