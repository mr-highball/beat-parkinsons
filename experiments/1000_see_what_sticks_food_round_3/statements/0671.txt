ID: 0671
Category: spice
Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted coriander seed reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One human RCT of coriander seeds provides a modest directional signal for depressive symptoms: Alqudah et al. 2024 (DOI 10.1108/nfs-08-2023-0193 / local NCT05999071; n=86 university students) reported significantly improved Hospital Depression Scale scores after 30 days of 500 mg dried powdered Coriandrum sativum seeds twice daily compared with placebo (P=0.002). Analytical roasting comparisons (Zhang et al. 2015, PMID:25148954) showed raw and roasted coriander seeds preserve core linalool and tripetroselinin chromatographic/bioassay profiles (though Serag 2019 notes phenolic losses on roasting), while inhaled coriander fruit volatile oil reduced forced-swim immobility in AD rats (Cioanca 2014) and Iranian Traditional Medicine lists coriander among foods for depressed patients (Sobhani 2022 / Tavakkoli-Kakhki 2014). Non-PD population, unroasted capsule form, retrospective registration, and lack of arm-level means cap certainty at minimal_signal (50.005).
