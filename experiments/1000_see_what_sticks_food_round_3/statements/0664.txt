ID: 0664
Category: spice
Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground coriander seed reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Human and preclinical coriander SEED studies provide a consistent anxiolytic signal: Alqudah et al. 2024 (DOI 10.1108/nfs-08-2023-0193 / local NCT05999071; n=86 university students) found 500 mg dried powdered Coriandrum sativum seeds twice daily for 30 days significantly improved Hospital Anxiety Scale scores versus placebo (P=0.04), reinforced by two independent mouse seed-extract experiments—Mahendra & Bisht 2011 (PMID:22022003, PMC3195130; oral fruit/seed extract 100-200 mg/kg anxiolytic across 4 models) and Emamghoreishi et al. 2005 (PMID:15619553; aqueous seed extract 100 mg/kg i.p. anxiolytic on EPM)—plus Persian traditional calming use (Sobhani 2022). Because Alqudah 2024 enrolled healthy young students (non-PD), was retrospectively registered, and omitted arm-level means/CIs in the abstract, certainty is graded minimal_signal (50.005).
