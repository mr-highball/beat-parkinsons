Symptom: constipation severity

Statement: Consuming baked cod reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): Controlled human Atlantic cod fillet trials show NULL effects on bowel output and gastrointestinal complaints. In the 8-week Norwegian RCT of 750 g/week baked/cooked Atlantic cod fillet (Bratlie 2021, NCT02350595, n=65 analyzed), high cod intake had no significant effect on 24-hour faecal wet weight, dry mass, or faecal bile acids vs control (p=0.53-0.65), and 12 months of cod fillet hydrolysate did not alter GI complaints in older adults (Nygard 2021, p=0.64).
