Category: herb
Ingredient: cilantro
Preparation: dried
Symptom: dystonia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried cilantro reduces dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or animal dystonia research exists for dried cilantro leaves; anticonvulsant KCNQ2/3 studies do not establish an effect on Parkinson's dystonia.
