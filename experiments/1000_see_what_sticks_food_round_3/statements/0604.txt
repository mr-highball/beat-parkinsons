Symptom: daily off time
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): in a double-blind Parkinson's crossover study (Deleu 2006, n=12), oral caffeine before levodopa shortened levodopa Tmax and shortened latency to onset of walking and finger-tapping motor response—easing acute delayed-ON latency rather than worsening OFF time. However, Deleu 2006 tested isolated caffeine capsules on single-dose onset latency rather than 24-hour diary OFF time, Moccia 2016 (n=79 PD 4-yr) found null UPDRS-IV complications, and Café-PD (Postuma 2017, n=118) showed increased dyskinesia without isolated OFF-time gains, capping confidence at minimal_signal.
