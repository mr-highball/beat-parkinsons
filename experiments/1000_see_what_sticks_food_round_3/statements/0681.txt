ID: 0681
Category: spice
Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted coriander seed reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Human seed RCT data provide a tentative sleep-quality signal that must be weighed carefully against contrary preclinical seed evidence: Alqudah et al. 2024 (DOI 10.1108/nfs-08-2023-0193 / local NCT05999071; n=86 university students) found 500 mg dried powdered coriander seeds twice daily for 30 days significantly improved Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) scores versus placebo (P=0.03), bridged to toasted seed by Zhang 2015 roasting retention. However, Hajhashemi & Safaei 2015 (PMID:26779267, PMC4698858; confirmed seed extract in full text, 250-1000 mg/kg p.o., n=6-8 mice/group) explicitly found NO effect of oral coriander seed extract on pentobarbital sleep onset or duration, and positive rodent sleep findings in Rakhshandeh 2012 (PMID:21988208) tested aerial parts/leaves rather than seeds and are excluded. Non-PD student sample, PSQI global sleep proxy, retrospective registration, and the null oral seed mouse study hold certainty at minimal_signal (50.005).
