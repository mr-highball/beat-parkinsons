Ingredient: chia seed

Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming soaked chia seed worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (Researched) for whether pre-soaked chia seed worsens dysphagia in PD. A well-documented clinical case report (Simmelink, Rawl, Browne & Scobey 2017, DOI 10.5430/crim.v4n2p49) demonstrated acute complete distal esophageal impaction requiring neonatal gastroscope disimpaction after a 39-year-old man with chronic intermittent dysphagia (suspected eosinophilic esophagitis) swallowed 1 tablespoon of DRY chia seeds followed by water, because dry seeds absorb up to 27 times their weight in water and expand rapidly in situ. Conversely, bench rheology studies (Li 2024; Ribes 2021) investigate pre-hydrated chia mucilage gum as a dysphagia-thickening hydrocolloid. Because the case report tested dry chia ingestion (unusable exposure match to pre-soaked chia) and zero human videofluoroscopic swallowing trials have tested pre-soaked whole chia seeds in PD dysphagia, directional evidence is insufficient.
