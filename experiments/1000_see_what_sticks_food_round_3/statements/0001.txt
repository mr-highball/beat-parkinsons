ID: 0001
Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two independent double-blind randomized controlled trials of coated cantaloupe juice concentrate (Extramel 10 mg/d) in stressed healthy adults reported statistically significant improvements in selective attention and cognitive performance: Carillon et al. (2014, n=61) showed Stroop/reverse-Stroop attention gains of +13.9% at day 28 (p<0.04) and +20.8% at day 84 (p<0.001) vs placebo, corroborated by Milesi et al. (2009, n=70) showing improved FARD Cognitive pole (p=0.032) and a concentration trend (p=0.091). Because both trials used proprietary coated concentrate in non-PD stressed adults (and sponsor funding applies), this yields a minimal directional signal (Maybe True, 50.005) consistent with Round 1 cantaloupe fatigue precedent.
