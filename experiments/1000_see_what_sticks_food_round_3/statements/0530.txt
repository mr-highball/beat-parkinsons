ID: 0530
Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No placebo-controlled clinical trial evaluates oral ground clove on attention; the sole clove-derived cognitive report (Bell 2021) lacked a placebo arm, excluded Parkinson's disease, tested isolated beta-caryophyllene with rice-bran oil, and showed sample-count discrepancies, leaving attention impairment unresolved.
