Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): Oral chili and capsaicin studies show conflicting gastrointestinal motility signals rather than a consistent worsening or easing of Parkinson's constipation. Human IBS-D crossover trials (Gonlachanvit 2009; Aniwan 2014) showed acute abdominal burning and rectal urgency followed by desensitization without altering whole-gut or colonic transit, rat oral chili left total gut transit null (Kang 1993), Capsimax beadlets did not alter bowel tolerability (Deshpande 2016), PD rats showed blunted capsaicin colorectal motility (Sawamura 2024), and a 2026 preclinical study reported anti-secretory chloride-channel motility inhibition (Sun 2026).
