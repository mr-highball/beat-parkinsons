ID: 0086
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted carrot worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for roasted carrot and Parkinson's rest tremor severity remains open. Custódio-Silva 2024 did not score rest tremor in reserpine rats and tested purple carrot root extract rather than roasted orange carrot; a Parkinson's UK forum conversation highlights practical hand-tremor frustration chopping hard fresh carrots rather than an ingested biological tremor effect. No clinical trial evaluates roasted culinary carrot on rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's.
