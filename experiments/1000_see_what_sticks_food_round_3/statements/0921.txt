Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): three controlled non-PD trials of plain 78-85% dark chocolate show reductions in depressive symptoms or negative affect—Abdoli 2024 (n=60 menopausal women 8-wk triple-blind RCT: 12 g/d 78% dark vs milk chocolate reduced depression score MD -2.3, p=0.003, d=-0.54), Shin 2022 (n=48 3-wk RCT: 30 g/d 85% dark chocolate lowered PANAS negative affect p<0.05), and Sathyapalan 2010 (n=10 CFS DB crossover: 85% dark chocolate improved HADS)—corroborated by Jackson 2019 (NHANES n=13,626 dark chocolate OR 0.30 for PHQ-9 >=10) and Fusar-Poli 2022 MA (g=-0.42). Tempered by null depression in FlaSeCo 2020 (n=100 older adults) and absence of PD depression RCTs.
