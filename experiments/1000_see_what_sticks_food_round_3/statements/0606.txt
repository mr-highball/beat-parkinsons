Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming decaffeinated coffee reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): neither cohort data nor controlled coffee trials show that regular decaffeinated coffee reduces depressive symptom severity—the Nurses' Health Study (Lucas 2011, n=50,739) found decaffeinated coffee completely null for depression risk while caffeinated coffee was inverse, and Haskell-Ramsay 2018 (n=59) found regular brewed decaf did not significantly improve overall mood over placebo.
