ID: 0274
Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): all three oral chamomile sleep/daytime-functioning trials assessing fatigue showed no statistically significant between-group effect—including brewed chamomile tea on the Postpartum Fatigue Scale (Chang & Chen 2016, n=80) and standardized oral extract on the Fatigue Severity Scale in primary insomnia (Zick 2011, n=34, -4.2 pts NS)—as confirmed by Kazemi 2024, leaving no established easing or worsening direction.
