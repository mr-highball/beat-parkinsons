ID: 0928
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): two controlled non-PD trials of plain 72-85% dark chocolate significantly reduced Chalder Fatigue Scale scores—Nemoto 2022 (n=88 analyzed middle-aged adults 4-wk RCT of 72% dark chocolate vs no chocolate: CFS reduction rate p=0.035) and Sathyapalan 2010 (n=10 CFS DB crossover of 85% dark chocolate: CFS 33->21.5, p=0.01 vs worsening on control p=0.03)—corroborated by Scholey 2010 (n=30 cognitive fatigue). In Parkinson's disease, a 6-day feasibility RCT of high- vs low-flavonoid cocoa beverage (Coe 2022, n=30 PD) showed only a small non-significant 6MWT fatigability point estimate (ES 0.11, 95% CI -0.11 to 0.26; CI spanning zero), keeping evidence at minimal_signal.
