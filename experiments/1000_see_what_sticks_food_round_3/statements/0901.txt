ID: 0901
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human cognitive trial tests botanical Murraya koenigii leaves—Ng 2006 assessed multi-spice turmeric curry dishes rather than curry leaf—and rodent M. koenigii memory models do not establish human Parkinson's executive-function improvement.
