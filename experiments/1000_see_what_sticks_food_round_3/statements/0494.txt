Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human or footprint stride-length measurement tests ground cinnamon in Parkinson's disease; while animal models improved open-field mobility, stride length itself remains untested and a caregiver noted persistent shuffling despite daily Ceylon cinnamon.
