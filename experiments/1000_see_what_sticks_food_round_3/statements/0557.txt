Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): oral virgin coconut oil showed no statistically significant between-group sleep-difficulty difference at any cycle in chemotherapy patients (Law 2014 Table 3, n=60), aromatherapy trials used coconut oil only as an inhaled placebo, and no study evaluates refined coconut oil for Parkinson insomnia.
