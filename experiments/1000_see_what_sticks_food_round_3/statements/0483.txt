ID: 0483
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A double-blind trial in older adults found 2 g oral ground cinnamon left post-prandial working memory unchanged (Lee 2014), and a 2024 systematic review confirmed no other isolated oral cinnamon cognitive trials; with zero Parkinson's attention trials, ground cinnamon effects on attention impairment remain open.
