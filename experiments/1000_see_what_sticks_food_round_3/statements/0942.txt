Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial tests plain dark chocolate on urinary urgency in Parkinson's disease; a 2025 NHANES case-control analysis (Ha 2025, PMID:39841448) found chocolate intake was NOT significantly associated with urgency urinary incontinence, and a 51-study bladder-irritant systematic review (Ha 2025, PMID:40105788) found inconsistent evidence across 3 chocolate studies.
