Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried chervil worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero clinical trials or observational cohorts evaluate dried garden chervil (Anthriscus cerefolium) on neurogenic swallowing difficulty in Parkinson's disease. A single clinical allergy case report (Van Der Brempt 2020, archived locally) documented acute allergic uvular/throat swelling and voice change after eating chervil soup, representing isolated IgE hypersensitivity rather than worsening of neurogenic PD dysphagia.
