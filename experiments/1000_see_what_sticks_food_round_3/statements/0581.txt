ID: 0581
Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: grilled
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grilled cod worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No adult clinical trial has tested grilled Atlantic cod fillet on executive function in Parkinson's disease or older adults. As noted for attention, the Norwegian Mommy's Food pregnancy cod RCT (Markhus 2021; Kvestad 2021, NCT02610959) produced conflicting infant developmental scores (lower 11-month infant Bayley-III cognitive composite p=0.045 vs null ASQ-2 p=0.633 and improved ASQ:SE p=0.020) that do not transfer to adult PD executive function, and older-adult dietary pattern cohorts (Mumme 2022) do not isolate grilled cod.
