Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked celery stalk worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for cooked celery stalk and Parkinson's insomnia remains open. Although the UK Women's Cohort Study (Noorwali et al., 2018, n=11,865) noted a modest observational association of -12 min/day shorter self-reported sleep duration per 80 g/d celery (p=0.007), baseline sleep averaged a normal 7.5 hours, no clinical insomnia instrument was administered, cooked celery was not separated from raw, and residual confounding cannot be excluded. Strictly maintaining plant-part separation, PSQI/insomnia gains reported for synthetic NBP (Zhou 2019) and celery SEED extract (Shayani Rad 2023) do not transfer to culinary celery stalk.
