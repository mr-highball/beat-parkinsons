Ingredient: cilantro

Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried cilantro reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human urinary-urgency evidence exists for dried cilantro leaves; a single rat experiment found dried leaf extracts increased urine volume (diuresis) rather than reducing urgency, leaving urinary urgency effects in Parkinson's disease open.
