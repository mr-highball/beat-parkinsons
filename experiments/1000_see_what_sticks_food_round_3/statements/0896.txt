ID: 0896
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground curry leaf reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Human trials of Murraya koenigii leaves were either bundled and null on HADS depression (Lauche 2016) or lacked a validated depression scale and statistics (Patel 2025 iron-anemia supplement), while rotenone PD mouse tail-suspension was null (Anjum 2025) and a single aqueous mouse FST study showed concurrent locomotor sedation (Sharma 2017); depression effects remain open.
