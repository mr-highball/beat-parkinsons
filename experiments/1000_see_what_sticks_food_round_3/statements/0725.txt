ID: 0725
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): human evidence on plain pasteurized cow's milk and executive function is null or indirect—Crichton 2012 (n=38 mixed dairy) and Zajac 2025 (n=263 MFGM milk powder) were null on executive function, Nouchi 2023 (n=66) tested an acidified milk-protein drink rather than plain pasteurized milk, and cohort reviews (Kesse-Guyot 2016; Gao 2025; Lee 2018) report verbal-memory signals or insufficient evidence (pooled RR 1.21, CI crossing 1.0).
