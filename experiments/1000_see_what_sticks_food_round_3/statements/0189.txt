Statement: Consuming dried celery leaf worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): human celery-leaf clinical studies (Mohsenpour 2023 dried stem+leaf DB-RCT; Yusni 2018 leaf-extract prediabetes study) evaluated cardiometabolic biomarkers only and reported zero data on olfactory impairment severity. Celery seed extracts and synthetic DL-NBP drug trials are strictly excluded, and ethnobotanical or Parkinson's UK forum discussions provide no clinical evidence that dried celery leaf affects olfactory impairment severity.
