Category: herb
Ingredient: celery leaf
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried celery leaf reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human clinical trials evaluate dried celery leaf for insomnia. Celery seed extract sleep data (Shayani Rad 2023) and synthetic DL-NBP trials (Zhou 2019) are strictly excluded as distinct plant parts/drugs, parenteral celery-leaf fractions prolonging mouse ketamine anesthesia (Bursac 2005) do not establish clinical sleep benefit (matching Round 1 claims 0143/0389), and heavy dried-leaf supplementation (4 g/d x 45 d) triggered a single hyperthyroidism case (Rouhi-Boroujeni 2016).
