ID: 0289
Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial tests oral brewed chamomile tea on Parkinson's swallowing difficulty severity; xerostomia saliva-substitute trials bundled chamomile with linseed as an oral rinse for dry mouth (Morales-Bozo 2017, n=74), and acute viral pharyngitis used a topical chamomile throat spray as active comparator without placebo (Okonogi 2023, n=60).
