Symptom: hand dexterity impairment

Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Unknown (open): no controlled trial tests refined coconut oil on fine hand dexterity; Parkinson UK forum accounts conflict (one perceived dexterity gain on 1 tsp/wk vs multi-month no-benefit and ketogenic worsening reports) without oil-grade verification or clinical testing.
