Ingredient: chickpeas

Symptom: executive function impairment

Statement: Consuming roasted chickpeas worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): chickpeas served only as an optional potato/chickpea low-lutein control arm with null Group x Time cognitive interactions in older adults (Scott 2017, n=40), chickpea resistant starch had null working-memory effects in aged mice (Kadyan 2024), and no clinical trials test roasted chickpeas for Parkinson executive function impairment.
