Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): clinical evidence on brewed coffee or caffeine for pure executive function is null or conflicting—Sharma 2022 (n=24 PD + 42 controls) explicitly found NO selective Stroop interference/inhibition effect (F<1), Todorovic 2021 (n=16) found caffeine alone null on TMT-A/B, Café-PD (Postuma 2017, n=118 PD) reported slightly worse global MoCA (-0.66 pts, not isolated to executive function), and observational cohorts conflict (Cornelis 2020 UKB worse TMT-B vs Zhang 2026 JAMA better global TICS).
