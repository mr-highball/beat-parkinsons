Category: food
Ingredient: cashew
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: dystonia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted cashew worsens dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial or whole-cashew animal experiment tests roasted cashew nuts on dystonia severity; preclinical PD studies of purified cashew-derived anacardic acids (Medeiros-Linard 2018, Augusto 2020) assessed general rotenone open-field/rotarod performance and nigrostriatal TH without separately measuring dystonia severity.
