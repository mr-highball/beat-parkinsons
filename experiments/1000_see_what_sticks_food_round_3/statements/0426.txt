Category: food
Ingredient: chickpeas
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted chickpeas worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): zero human Parkinson trials evaluate roasted chickpeas for postural instability severity; aged-mouse testing of chickpea resistant starch found null motor coordination/mobility (Kadyan 2024), while rodent PD motor benefits used purified pharmaceutical biochanin A (Wang 2015; Yu 2017; El-Sherbeeny 2020; Li 2021; Tian 2026) rather than whole culinary chickpeas.
