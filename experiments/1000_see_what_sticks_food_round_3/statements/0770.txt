ID: 0770
Category: food
Ingredient: cranberry
Preparation: dried
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried cranberry worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal, 50.005): dried cranberry powder DB-RCTs outside Parkinson disease reduced OAB urgency episodes (-2.81/day, p=0.0069 per-protocol; ITT null; Cho 2021 n=98), OABSS (p=0.039; Ito 2026 n=25), male LUTS IPSS (-4.1 vs -1.5, p<0.001; Vidlar 2016 n=124), and UTI-related urgency/frequency (RR 0.29, p<0.01; Stonehouse 2025 n=150) with zero worsening signals, tempered by Cho 2021 ITT null, null Cochrane neurogenic-bladder UTI prophylaxis (RR 0.97, n=464; Williams 2023), null nursing-home/radiotherapy trials, and absence of a Parkinson urinary urgency RCT.
