ID: 0853
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise reduces hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) reduces hand dexterity impairment in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses hand dexterity impairment; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
