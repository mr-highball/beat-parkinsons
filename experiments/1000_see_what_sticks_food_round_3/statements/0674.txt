Statement: Consuming ground coriander seed reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for ground coriander seed reducing levodopa-induced dyskinesia severity in PD is Unknown (Researched). Mohan et al. 2015 (local extract, PMID:26040026) showed oral ethanolic coriander seed extract reduced tacrine-induced vacuous chewing movements in rats, but cholinergic tacrine orofacial chewing models tremulous jaw/tardive oral dyskinesia rather than dopaminergic levodopa-induced dyskinesia (AIMs) in PD (outcome_fit: remote). No clinical PD dyskinesia or 6-OHDA/L-DOPA AIMs studies test coriander seed.
