ID: 0715
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): human evidence on cow's milk and attention/processing speed is conflicting or indirect—Jianqin 2016 (n=45 milk-intolerant adults) saw slower SCIT processing speed on A1/A2 conventional milk versus A2-only milk (no milk-free comparator), Zajac 2025 (n=263 MFGM milk powder) was null on primary cognition, Crichton 2012 (n=38 mixed dairy) was null on attention, and Lee 2018 SR concluded evidence for milk and cognition is insufficient (pooled RR 1.21, CI crossing 1.0).
