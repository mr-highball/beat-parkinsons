Preparation: ground
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No human clinical trial or Parkinson's gait-kinematics study has measured stride length after ground cayenne pepper consumption. Thornton 2026 assessed 6-minute walk distance (with a pronounced baseline-swap artifact and no spatial-temporal stride-length measurements) in obese non-PD adults, and rodent PD studies (Wei 2025; Tyagi 2024) did not report footprint stride length.
