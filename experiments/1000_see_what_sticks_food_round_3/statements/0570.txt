Statement: Consuming grilled cod worsens apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial or cohort study has evaluated whether grilled Atlantic cod fillet worsens or eases apathy in Parkinson's disease. Existing cod feeding RCTs (Aadland 2015; Ouellet 2007; Bratlie 2021) measured cardiometabolic, amino-acid, and gut endpoints without apathy scales.
