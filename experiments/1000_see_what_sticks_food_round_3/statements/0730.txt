ID: 0730
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): clinical sleep trials do NOT show plain unfortified pasteurized cow's milk improves or worsens adult insomnia—Valtonen 2005 used ordinary daytime pasteurized milk as the placebo control, Paterson 2026 (n=21 DB crossover) found pre-sleep casein and alpha-lactalbumin null on sleep duration/latency/efficiency (p>0.05), Komada 2020 & Peres 2025 confirm positive dairy sleep RCTs tested fermented yogurt, melatonin/tryptophan-enriched night-milk, casein-hydrolysate supplements, or milk+honey/malted cereal (Fakhr-Movahedi 2018; Brezinova 1972), and Kahn 1989 (n=17) applies solely to cow's-milk-allergic infants under 30 months.
