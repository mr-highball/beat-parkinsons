ID: 0908
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Six independent controlled rodent studies testing oral Murraya koenigii leaf extracts across inflammatory, thermal, glutamate, and diabetic neuropathic pain models consistently show antinociceptive easing with zero studies reporting pain worsening; lacking human clinical pain trials of cooked culinary curry leaves in Parkinson's disease, this yields a minimal directional lean against worsening pain (Maybe False).
