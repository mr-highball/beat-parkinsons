Ingredient: cashew

Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted cashew worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): no controlled study suggests roasted cashews worsen depression; hydroethanolic extract of Anacardium occidentale fruit kernels reversed chronic-stress anhedonia and forced-swim immobility in rats (Desire 2023, n=42) and oral cashew anacardic acid reduced mouse TST/FST immobility via serotonergic pathways (Gomes Junior 2019), but absence of human trials and non-roasted kernel extract preparations keep the easing lean at minimal_signal.
