ID: 0723
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no human clinical trial evaluates plain pasteurized cow's milk on Parkinson's levodopa-induced dyskinesia; dietary-protein lowering in PD can actually unmask peak-dose dyskinesia when levodopa is unadjusted (Barichella 2006), while Liu 2021 tested high-dose intragastric isolated casein (6.75 g/kg) in MPTP mice (generic toxin motor recovery rather than human levodopa-induced dyskinesia).
