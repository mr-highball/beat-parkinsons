Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No study evaluates raw cantaloupe on orthostatic blood pressure or postural lightheadedness in Parkinson's disease. Existing vascular findings diverge in direction and exposure: in 187,453 US health professionals (Borgi et al., 2016), higher cantaloupe intake was modestly linked to higher incident hypertension (HR 1.07, 95% CI 1.01-1.13), whereas preclinical studies of injected cucurbitacins (Yuan 2019) or melon seed kernels (Wahid 2023) lowered rodent BP, and Rodriguez et al. (2000) documented anaphylactic hypotension only in 2/53 IgE-sensitized melon-allergic adults.
