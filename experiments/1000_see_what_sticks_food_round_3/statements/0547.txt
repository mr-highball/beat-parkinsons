Category: spice
Ingredient: clove

Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human walking-speed data exist for ground clove, and rodent eugenol PD studies conflict directly by timing—with post-lesion eugenol worsening akinesia and striatal dopamine loss—leaving Parkinson's walking slowness unresolved.
