ID: 0475
Category: herb
Ingredient: cilantro
Preparation: dried
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried cilantro reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human pain trial tests dried cilantro leaves (the migraine syrup RCT used coriander fruit/seed, and a 2021 leaf-nanoparticle analgesia paper was retracted in 2026); the sole remaining mouse study used a parenteral leaf/stem fraction that also reduced locomotion, leaving pain relief unresolved.
