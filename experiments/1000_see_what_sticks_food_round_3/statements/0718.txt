ID: 0718
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk worsens daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Maybe True (limited_clinical_link toward worsening): multiple controlled Parkinson's trials directly measure daily OFF/ON time under higher versus lower or evening-redistributed dietary protein (Barichella 2006 n=18: balanced daytime protein increased total OFF 271 vs 164 min/d and postprandial OFF 79 vs 49 min, both p<0.0001; Tsui 1989 n=10 DB crossover: higher OFF hours; Carter 1989 n=5: 1.6 g/kg protein lowered ON time to 51% vs 77% evening-restricted; Eriksson 1988; Nutt 1984 NEJM n=9: oral LNAAs/protein reversed levodopa response), directly bridged to pasteurized cow's milk via human amino-acid kinetic RCTs showing robust postprandial plasma EAA/BCAA/leucine surges from pasteurized/skim milk and bovine casein/whey (Milan 2024 n=20 500 mL pasteurized milk; Burke 2012 n=15 skim milk peak ~25 min; Boirie 1997; Tosukhowong 2016 n=32 PD whey BCAAs). Tempered by real-world heterogeneity (Virmani 2016 n=877: 5.9% overall / 12.4% fluctuators; Nutt 1989; Cucca 2015; mixed PD forum experiences) and absence of a PD trial isolating pasteurized milk alone.
