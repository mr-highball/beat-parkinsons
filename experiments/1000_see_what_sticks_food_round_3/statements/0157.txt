Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (50.005): Four independent controlled Parkinson's rodent studies show Capsicum annuum extract or purified capsaicin eases parkinsonian motor slowness/akinesia—including oral C. annuum ethyl-acetate extract restoring cylinder rearing and potentiating L-DOPA in rotenone rats (Ogunruku 2019), intraperitoneal capsaicin restoring open-field locomotion and rotarod latency in MPTP mice (Wei 2025), i.p. capsaicin easing 6-OHDA locomotor deficits (Zhao 2017), and delayed i.p. capsaicin reducing rotational asymmetry in MPP+ rats (Kim 2019). Signal remains minimal because no human PD clinical trial has tested oral ground cayenne pepper.
