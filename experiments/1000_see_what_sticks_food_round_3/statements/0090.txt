ID: 0090
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming steamed carrot worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Traditional Ayurvedic and Mediterranean sources describe carrot roots and wild carrot as diuretic and anti-urolithiatic (Ismail 2023), and rat studies show hydroethanolic/ethanolic carrot root extracts protect against kidney stones or gentamicin nephrotoxicity (Bawari 2020; Sodimbaku 2016). However, no human trial evaluates whether steamed carrots worsen or ease urinary urgency frequency in Parkinson's disease.
