Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried date worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal / eases, 50.005): a human randomized crossover of whole dry Tamr-stage Ajwa dates (~50 g/d, Eid 2015 n=21) reported higher stool frequency (1.40 vs 1.21/d; unpaired Huo 2022 CI -0.17 to +0.55), concordant with rat date-pulp GI transit acceleration (Souli 2014) and zero constipation-worsening trials, while non-PD sampling and paired-CI reporting limits cap confidence at minimal_signal.
