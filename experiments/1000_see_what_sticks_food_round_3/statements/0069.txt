ID: 0069
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming steamed carrot worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for steamed carrot on Parkinson's attention impairment is open. A 12-week single-arm study of black carrot anthocyanin extract in 50 cognitively impaired older adults (Muduroglu-Kirmizibekmez et al., 2025) had no control group and reported only EEG connectivity and gut microbiota shifts without any post-intervention clinical attention or cognitive test scores; Schrager et al. (2015, n=7 carrot juice arm) found null Trail Making Test-B change (p=0.804); and preclinical carrot-seed extract studies conflict in direction (Vasudevan 2006 vs Jafarinejad Bajestani 2022) while testing seeds rather than steamed roots.
