ID: 0077
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw carrot reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human study or targeted Parkinson animal assay evaluates whether raw carrot reduces fatigue severity. Existing carrot human studies examined cardiometabolic markers (Wright 2013 QUENCH null; Potter 2011 pilot), older-adult gait/TMT-B (Schrager 2015 null), or cross-sectional mood (Brookie 2018), while preclinical reserpine rat data (Custódio-Silva 2024) used purple carrot extract and measured catalepsy/oral movements without scoring fatigue severity.
