Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Across four independent preclinical Parkinson's laboratories—including three experiments feeding exact oral ground Ceylon cinnamon powder in A53T and MPTP mice plus independent cinnamaldehyde and procyanidin PD models—cinnamon consistently eased pole-test bradykinesia proxies and dopaminergic loss; lacking human Parkinson's trials, this establishes a minimal directional lean toward easing bradykinesia (with Ceylon preferred over high-coumarin Cassia for safety).
