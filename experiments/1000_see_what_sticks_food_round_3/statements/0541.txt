ID: 0541
Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human balance data exist for ground clove, and rodent eugenol PD models show opposite pre- versus post-lesion outcomes—with post-lesion eugenol worsening beam balance and striatal dopamine loss—leaving postural instability effects unresolved.
