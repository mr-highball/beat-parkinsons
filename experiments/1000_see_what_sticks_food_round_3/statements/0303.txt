Ingredient: cherry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cherry worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal, 50.005): two human sweet-cherry RCTs reported statistically significant gains on executive-related tasks—verbal fluency in older adults with dementia (Kent 2017, n=49, p=0.014) and Digit Span Backward working memory (Arbizu 2025, n=40, p=0.01)—with zero worsening signals, balanced by null 3-back (Kimble 2022), TMT/DSST (Arbizu 2025), and acute (Keane 2016) results and no raw-whole-cherry PD trial.
