Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial evaluates brewed coffee on oropharyngeal dysphagia in Parkinson's disease; Fox Insight (Gabbert 2023, n=4,892) showed a marginal binary inverse link (p=0.0497) with completely null dosage (p=0.6961) and duration (p=0.4785) trends—consistent with reverse causation from dysphagia reducing beverage intake (Cassani 2017)—while Lohsiriwat 2006 (n=12 healthy) measured lower esophageal sphincter relaxation rather than oropharyngeal swallowing.
