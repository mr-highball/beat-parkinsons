Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Six independent controlled rodent depression studies—including two directly testing oral Cinnamomum verum extract plus cinnamon oil and cinnamaldehyde models—consistently ease depressive-like behavior with zero evidence of mood worsening; lacking an isolated oral human Parkinson's depression RCT, this yields a minimal directional lean against worsening (Maybe False).
