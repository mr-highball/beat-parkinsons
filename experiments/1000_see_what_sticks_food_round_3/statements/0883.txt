ID: 0883
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cumin worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: In true Cuminum cyminum (excluding Nigella sativa), a triple-blind placebo-controlled surgical RCT (Amin 2024, n=72) showed significantly lower postoperative abdominal pain frequency at three timepoints alongside rodent antinociceptive/neuropathic-pain easing (Nouri 2024; Koohsari 2020) and zero studies showing pain worsening; tempered by null menstrual-pain (Omidvar 2019) and two null rodent assays, this yields a minimal lean against worsening pain (Maybe False).
