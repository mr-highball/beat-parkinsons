Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no randomized trial evaluates brewed coffee or caffeine on Parkinson's apathy severity; observational PD data conflict (Cho 2018 cross-sectional de novo PD n=196 linked coffee drinking to lower lack-of-motivation prevalence, whereas Sauerbier 2018 n=139 found null NMSS mood/apathy), and a controlled fatigue trial (Ataka 2008 n=18) found caffeine had no effect on subjective motivation.
