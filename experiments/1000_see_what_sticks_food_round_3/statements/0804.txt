ID: 0804
Category: herb
Ingredient: culinary lavender
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed culinary lavender reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Minimal signal (Maybe True, anchor 50.005) that brewed culinary lavender reduces anxiety severity, backed by an oral lavender herbal tea RCT in older adults (PMID:32444033) and four placebo-controlled DB-RCTs of oral Lavandula angustifolia oil Silexan (PMID:20512042, PMID:24456909, PMID:26293583, PMID:26718792), tempered by two null oral trials (PMID:38754638, PMID:37663382) and no clinical trial in diagnosed Parkinson's disease.
