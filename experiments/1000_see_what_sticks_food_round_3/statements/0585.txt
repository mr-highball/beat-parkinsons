Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: baked
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming baked cod reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial has tested baked Atlantic cod fillet for insomnia in Parkinson's disease or general populations. Fish-sleep RCTs (Hansen 2014, PMID:24812543) tested fatty omega-3/vitamin-D-rich Atlantic salmon rather than lean white cod fillet, and a cross-sectional Spanish adult survey (Marin Ferrandis 2025) listed pooled white fish without cod-specific preparation, effect size, or causal testing.
