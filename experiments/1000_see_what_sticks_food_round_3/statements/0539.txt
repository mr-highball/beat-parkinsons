ID: 0539
Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground clove worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson's olfactory-training RCTs (Haehner 2013; Dou 2025) sniffed clove/eugenol essential oil as one of four inhaled odors rather than testing oral ground clove ingestion; the effect of eating ground clove on Parkinson's olfactory impairment remains open.
