Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: speech intelligibility impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial tests brewed coffee on speech intelligibility in Parkinson's disease; Fox Insight PD survey data (Gabbert 2023, n=4,892) showed no significant association between coffee drinking (p=0.1615) or weekly dosage (p=0.7086) and speech problems.
