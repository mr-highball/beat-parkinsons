ID: 0424
Category: food
Ingredient: chickpeas
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted chickpeas worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): zero studies test isolated roasted chickpeas on orthostatic blood pressure or lightheadedness; mixed-legume seated BP trials (Jenkins 2012; Tovar 2014) are offset by a null pulse-lunch BP trial (Bertrand 2026) and do not measure standing orthostasis in Parkinson disease.
