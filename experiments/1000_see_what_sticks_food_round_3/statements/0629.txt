Symptom: visual hallucination frequency

Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial evaluates brewed coffee on visual hallucination frequency in Parkinson's disease; controlled and survey studies outside PD found null links between caffeine/coffee and hallucination-like experiences (Agoston 2022 DB RCT n=181; Agoston 2018 n=2,259), and Sauerbier 2018 (n=139 PD) showed null NMSS perceptual/hallucination scores.
