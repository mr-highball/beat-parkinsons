ID: 0857
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise reduces olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) reduces olfactory impairment severity in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses olfactory impairment severity; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
