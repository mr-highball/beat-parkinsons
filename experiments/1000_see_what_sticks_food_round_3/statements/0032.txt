ID: 0032
Category: spice
Ingredient: caraway
Preparation: ground
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground caraway worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): neither human clinical trials nor Parkinson's disease animal models (MPTP, 6-OHDA, alpha-synuclein) have tested ground Carum carvi for bradykinesia severity. Available preclinical motor observations are strictly non-parkinsonian—null rotarod deficit in healthy mice (Showraki 2016) and actophotometer counts with essential oil in AlCl3-treated rats (Auti & Kulkarni 2025)—while the 1664 English shaking-palsy receipt mentions caraway only inside an unverified multi-herb mixture.
