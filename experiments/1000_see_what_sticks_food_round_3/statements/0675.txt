ID: 0675
Category: spice
Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dystonia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground coriander seed worsens dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human clinical trials, PD cohorts, or targeted animal models test ground coriander seed (Coriandrum sativum fruit/seed) for dystonia severity in Parkinson's disease. Verified seed-specific research focuses on migraine pain (Delavar Kasmaei 2016), student anxiety/depression/sleep/memory (Alqudah 2024), MPTP mouse open-field/rotarod rescue (Koppula 2021), roasting volatile/lipid retention (Zhang 2015; Serag 2019; local Elmassry 2018), and multi-spice metabolic registries (local PolySPice NCT02599272), none of which directly evaluate dystonia severity. Coriander LEAF (cilantro) studies (e.g., Lee 2026; Saeki 2022) are strictly separated from seed claims, and Persian/Ayurvedic/Sri Lankan ethnobotany (Sobhani 2022; local Otoom 2006) provides traditional context only, leaving dystonia severity Unknown (Researched).
