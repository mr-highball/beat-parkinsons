Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached chicken worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No controlled trial shows poached chicken breast worsens or eases Parkinson's pain. Purified native chicken sternum cartilage type II collagen trials (Wei 2009 in RA) use an undenatured anatomical cartilage tissue completely distinct from poached chicken muscle meat (exposure_fit: unusable); a 24-week knee OA trial of Essence of Chicken plus hydrolyzed collagen II (Chen 2023, n=160) did NOT beat placebo on WOMAC pain; dietary poultry explained <=0.3% of population serum urate variance (Major 2018) and showed no gout causal effect in Mendelian randomization (Ou 2024).
