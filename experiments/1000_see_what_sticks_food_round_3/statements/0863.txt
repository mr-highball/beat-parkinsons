ID: 0863
Category: spice
Ingredient: culinary star anise
Preparation: ground
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground culinary star anise reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground culinary star anise (Illicium verum) reduces shortened stride severity in Parkinson's disease because no controlled human oral I. verum trial assesses shortened stride severity; toxic Japanese star anise (I. anisatum) adulteration carries severe seizure/neurotoxicity risks and green anise (Pimpinella anisum) is a distinct plant family.
