ID: 0053
Category: spice
Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom worsens drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial has tested ingested ground Elettaria cardamomum on Parkinson's drooling (sialorrhea). Ito & Yamamura (2010, n=43 healthy adults) showed acute salivary flow stimulation from inhaling cardamom essential-oil odor strips (0.182 g vs 0.130 g rest), but brief olfactory stimulation in healthy subjects does not establish clinical worsening of PD sialorrhea—which is driven primarily by impaired swallowing clearance—from culinary ingested ground cardamom (consistent with Round 1 claim 0748).
