ID: 0326
Category: herb
Ingredient: chervil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried chervil reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): exhaustive literature verification across PubMed, Europe PMC, and ClinicalTrials.gov confirms ZERO human clinical trials and ZERO in vivo animal symptom studies of garden chervil (Anthriscus cerefolium) for daytime sleepiness severity (strictly excluding wild Anthriscus sylvestris, Chaerophyllum, and poison hemlock Conium maculatum). Available records are limited to in vitro phytochemistry/antioxidant profiling (Slimestad 2022; Stojkovic 2021, 2024; Fejes 2000), an unisolated PD vegetarian menu listing (Hegelmaier 2020), historical 1890 herbalism, and a single umbellifer diet forum mention.
