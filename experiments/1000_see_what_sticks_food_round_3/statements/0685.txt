Category: spice
Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground coriander seed worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for ground coriander seed worsening orthostatic lightheadedness severity is Unknown (Researched). Local archived studies of coriander seed powder in adults (Zeb 2018; Iqbal 2018) and anesthetized rat arterial pressure (Jabeen 2008) measured resting blood pressure rather than standing orthostatic BP drop or clinical lightheadedness symptoms. While hypotensive effects in resting hypertension warrant monitoring in PD neurogenic orthostatic hypotension, zero orthostatic challenge studies exist.
