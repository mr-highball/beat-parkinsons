ID: 0687
Category: spice
Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground coriander seed reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: A controlled MPTP Parkinson's mouse study provides a preclinical balance/coordination signal: Koppula et al. 2021 (local PMC8222636, PMID:34177406; n=60 Swiss albino mice, 10/group) found that 20-21 days of oral Coriandrum sativum fruit (seed) extract (100, 200, 300 mg/kg p.o.) dose-dependently rescued day-20 rotarod balance retention time from 27 +/- 3 s in MPTP controls to 37 +/- 4 s, 44 +/- 3 s, and 51 +/- 2 s (P<0.05 to P<0.001; day-4 retention non-significant). An opposing acute route caveat is preserved: Emamghoreishi et al. 2005 (PMID:15619553) observed reduced rotarod neuromuscular coordination after acute intraperitoneal aqueous seed extract in healthy mice. Because evidence rests on a single rodent PD model using seed extract (not human PD postural pull-test or posturography trials with ground culinary seed), certainty is minimal_signal (50.005).
