ID: 0720
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): large prospective cohorts of non-fermented cow's milk and depression conflict directly in opposite directions—UK Biobank + MR (Wu 2024, n=357,568) linked semi-skimmed milk to lower depression risk (HR 0.88, skimmed null), whereas KIHD (Hockey 2022, n=2,603 Finnish men, 24-yr follow-up) linked non-fermented dairy/milk to 2-fold HIGHER depression risk (HR 2.02, 95% CI 1.20-3.42), Idrus 2026 (n=966 older Australians) found non-fermented milk null on GDS-15, and Hu 2026 MA was null—with zero plain cow's milk depression RCTs.
