Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried date reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal, 50.005): an open-label RCT of 3 whole dried Tamr Khudary dates/day improved SF-36 Mental Health (+5.64, 95% CI 0.57 to 10.70; Alalwan 2020 n=96) alongside concordant antidepressant-like effects across three independent rodent fruit-pulp depression models (Asdaq 2025; Shivanandappa 2023; Youssef 2022), tempered by open-label expectancy, null uncontrolled POMS depression (Abdullah 2019), seed-vs-fruit distinctions (Momeniyan 2025), and zero PD depression RCTs.
