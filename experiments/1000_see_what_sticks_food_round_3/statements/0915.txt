Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate worsens apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial or Parkinson's cohort evaluates plain dark chocolate on apathy severity; Mesoamerican and early modern cacao texts (Dillinger 2000) describe traditional stimulating use for apathetic/exhausted states (customary context only), while Wolz 2012 (n=26 PD) measured only composite UPDRS-III motor score.
