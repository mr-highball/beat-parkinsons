Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): an exact brewed chamomile tea RCT in 80 sleep-disturbed postnatal women reduced Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale scores at 2 weeks (Chang & Chen 2016, P=0.020, attenuated by 4 weeks), supported by an exploratory double-blind RCT analysis showing lower HAM-D scores with oral chamomile extract in GAD (Amsterdam 2012, n=57, P<0.05), while unblinded usual-care control, transient duration, secondary GAD design, and absent PD depression trials keep inference minimal.
