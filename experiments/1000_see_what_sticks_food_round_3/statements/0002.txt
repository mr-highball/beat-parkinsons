ID: 0002
Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human study or Parkinson animal model tests whether raw cantaloupe worsens bradykinesia severity. Existing melon trials used proprietary coated melon SOD concentrates (Extramel/GliSODin) in non-PD adults without PD motor outcomes (Koike 2022 null; Milesi 2009 and Carillon 2014 lacked motor endpoints), Hegelmaier 2020 bundled melon into a multi-food PD vegetarian menu, and preclinical rotarod screens tested non-flesh seeds or peels (Sayyar 2023; Dokkada 2025).
