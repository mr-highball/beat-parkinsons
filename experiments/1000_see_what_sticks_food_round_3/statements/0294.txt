Category: food
Ingredient: cherry
Preparation: dried
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried cherry reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal, 50.005): 12-week Montmorency tart cherry concentrate improved Digit Vigilance sustained attention accuracy (+3.3%, p=0.035) and false alarms (-1.2, p=0.005) in middle-aged adults (Kimble 2022, n=50), alongside Digit Span concentration gains from dark sweet cherry powder drink (Arbizu 2025, n=40, p=0.006), tempered by null RVIP/TMT/DSST subtests, null acute/pediatric ADHD trials, and no whole-dried-cherry or Parkinson trials.
