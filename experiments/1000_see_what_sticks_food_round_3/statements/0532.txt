Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One small human adjunct trial of oil-reduced clove bud capsules (Sadeh 2020, limited by table-labeling and registry-verification flaws) and two independent controlled rodent depression studies of oral clove essential oil (Liu 2015; Brahmi 2025) point toward easing depressive symptoms with zero opposing studies; lacking clean, replicated human trials of culinary ground clove in Parkinson's disease, this yields a minimal directional lean toward easing.
