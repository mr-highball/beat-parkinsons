ID: 0095
Category: food
Ingredient: cashew
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted cashew reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): oral cashew-derived anacardic acids (10-100 mg/kg/d) dose-dependently prevented rotenone-induced open-field hypokinesia in a rat Parkinson's model (Medeiros-Linard 2018), supported by nigrostriatal tyrosine hydroxylase preservation in rotenone mice (Augusto 2020) and LC-MS detection of anacardic acids in processed edible cashew nuts (Mattison 2018), though no human or whole roasted-cashew Parkinson's bradykinesia study exists.
