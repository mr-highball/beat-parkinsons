Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Consistent with Round 1 celery-stalk precedent (claim 0281), Chonpathompikunlert et al. (2018, PMC5859653, n=48 MPTP PD mice) directly evaluated postural balance with two independent, internally consistent assays—narrow-beam foot-slip errors ('used to assess balance', Fig 3b) and dynamic forelimb balance on the drag test (Fig 1)—showing oral whole-plant Apium graveolens methanolic extract (250 and 375 mg/kg x 21 d) significantly improved balance and preserved SNpc dopaminergic neurons vs MPTP+vehicle (p<0.05; Fig 5 rotarod was excluded due to an internal text inversion). Viña & Chaves (2007) verify fresh celery petioles contain apigenin (4.61 ug/g) and luteolin (0.88 ug/g). Strictly separating celery stalk from celery seed and synthetic NBP drugs, this yields a minimal directional signal (Maybe True, 50.005).
