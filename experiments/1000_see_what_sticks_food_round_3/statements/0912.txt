ID: 0912
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The sole human urinary trial including Murraya koenigii (Sengupta 2011, n=46 men with BPH) co-formulated curry leaf extract with Tribulus terrestris against tamsulosin without a placebo arm, leaving isolated cooked curry leaf's effect on Parkinson's urinary urgency unresolved.
