ID: 0998
Category: spice
Ingredient: dill seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground dill seed worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Minimal signal against worsening (Maybe False, anchor 50.005): oral ground dill seed eased primary dysmenorrhea VAS pain in a placebo- and mefenamic-acid-controlled DB-RCT (PMID:25097605; Cochrane MD -1.15 vs placebo, PMID:27000311) and a 3-arm comparative spice trial (PMID:31383440), contradicting the hypothesis that ground dill seed worsens pain severity, though Parkinson's musculoskeletal/neuropathic pain has never been tested.
