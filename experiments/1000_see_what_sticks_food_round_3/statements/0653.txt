Statement: Consuming cooked collard greens worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for cooked collard greens affecting pain severity is Unknown (Researched). Yeboah et al. 2026 (PMID:42884697, PMC13666909; n=56 obese Zucker rats) measured metabolic and inflammatory biomarkers (such as IL-6) on 4% collard greens powder/extract diets without any behavioral nociception or pain endpoints, and Bouranis et al. 2024 (PMID:39021604) performed human metabolomics only. Zero clinical or animal pain trials exist for cooked collard greens.
