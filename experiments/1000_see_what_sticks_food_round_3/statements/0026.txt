Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical trial tests whether raw cantaloupe alters oropharyngeal swallowing difficulty in Parkinson's disease. A double-blind food challenge (Rodriguez et al., 2000, n=53) confirmed IgE-mediated oral allergy syndrome (oral/throat symptoms) in 14/53 adults with suspected melon allergy, which represents pollen-food allergy rather than neurogenic PD dysphagia, and no PD swallowing studies evaluate raw cantaloupe bolus mechanics.
