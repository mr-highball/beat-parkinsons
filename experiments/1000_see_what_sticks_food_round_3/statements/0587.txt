ID: 0587
Category: food
Ingredient: cod
Preparation: poached
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached cod worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial has isolated poached Atlantic cod fillet for Parkinson's muscle rigidity severity. Older-adult cod hydrolysate trials (Nygard 2021) did not measure parkinsonian rigidity, cell-free alpha-synuclein assays (Werner 2018) are test-tube biophysics, and Parkinson's UK forum comments about stiffness (Thread 4189) focused on cod liver oil supplements rather than lean poached cod fillet.
