ID: 0139
Category: food
Ingredient: cauliflower
Preparation: raw
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw cauliflower worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Qualitative studies in older adults with Parkinson's and motoric eating difficulties (Forsberg et al., 2022, 2024) and mastication bolus analysis (Peyron et al., 2004) document practical mechanical challenges with raw cauliflower—hard texture requiring cooking/blanching for easier chewing/swallowing, large vegetable bolus particle sizes, and floret crumbling during hand grasp—but no controlled clinical trial tests whether raw cauliflower alters neurogenic Parkinson's hand dexterity impairment.
