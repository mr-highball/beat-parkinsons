Category: food
Ingredient: cranberry
Preparation: dried
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried cranberry worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): Cochrane pooled GI adverse events did not differ clearly from placebo (RR 1.33, 95% CI 1.00-1.77; Williams 2023), cranberry powder microbiota feeding trials did not assess constipation (Rodriguez-Morato 2018), and no PD constipation trial isolates dried cranberries.
