ID: 0061
Category: spice
Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: reduced facial expressiveness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces reduced facial expressiveness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): zero human PD trials evaluate ground Elettaria cardamomum for reduced facial expressiveness (Hegelmaier 2020 listed cardamom only inside an unisolated vegetarian menu; Nobari 2023 notes a historical 5-herb Iranian formula naming Amomum). Preclinical motor data are strictly non-culinary and mutually conflicting: cardamom oil improved open-field locomotion in reserpine depression rats (Abdel-Rasoul 2023) and beta-cyclodextrin 1,8-cineole reduced reserpine catalepsy (Araujo-Monteiro 2026), whereas high-dose cardamom extract/oil caused rotarod movement neurotoxicity (Masoumi-Ardakani 2016), perinatal exposure delayed locomotor development (Abu-Taweel 2018), and isolated 1,8-cineole potentiated haloperidol catalepsy (Figueiredo 2019).
