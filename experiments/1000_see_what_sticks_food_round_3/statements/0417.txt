Category: food
Ingredient: chickpeas
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked chickpeas reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): an uncontrolled single-arm breast-cancer trial of Persian chickpea broth (Heydarirad 2019, n=40) cannot establish causal attribution, while the only double-blind controlled human exertion crossover of a chickpea beverage (Hiruncharoen 2026, n=14) was null on perceived exertion and time to exhaustion, and no Parkinson fatigue trials exist.
