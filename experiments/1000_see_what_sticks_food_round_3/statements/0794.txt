Ingredient: cucumber
Preparation: pickled
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming pickled cucumber worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal / eases, 50.005): two human non-PD pain trial streams—standardized Cucumis sativus fruit extract Q-Actin in knee OA (Nash 2018 n=115; Nash 2023 n=91; tempered by Hausenblas 2025 null WOMAC interaction p=0.13) and dill pickled-cucumber brine for acute painful cramps (Tapper 2022 PICCLES n=74, p=0.03; Miller 2010 n=10)—lean against pain worsening and toward modest pain easing, while extract/brine-to-whole-pickle gaps, acetic-acid attribution, and absence of PD pain trials keep confidence minimal.
