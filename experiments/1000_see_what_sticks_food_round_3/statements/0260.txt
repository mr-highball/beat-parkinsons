ID: 0260
Category: herb
Ingredient: chamomile
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed chamomile worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): two double-blind placebo-controlled oral chamomile extract RCTs in GAD showed lower anxiety severity—HAM-A reduction (Amsterdam 2009, n=57, P=0.047) and lower continuation GAD symptoms (Mao 2016, n=93 responders, P=0.0032 despite non-significant primary relapse HR 0.52, P=0.16)—with zero evidence of worsening, tempered by null oral chemotherapy GAD-7 (Nikkhah 2026, n=80), inconclusive postoperative brewed tea vs black tea (Saidi 2023, n=96), and absent PD trials.
