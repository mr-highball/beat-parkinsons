ID: 0893
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human cognitive trial tests botanical Murraya koenigii leaves—the Singapore curry-consumption MMSE cohort (Ng 2006) reflects turmeric-based curry spice blends rather than curry leaf—and rodent M. koenigii memory studies do not measure clinical attention, leaving Parkinson's attention effects open.
