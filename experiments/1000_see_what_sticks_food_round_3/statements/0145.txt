Ingredient: cauliflower
Preparation: raw
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw cauliflower reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human study tests whether raw cauliflower reduces pain severity in Parkinson's disease. A single heavily confounded case report (Manappallil et al., 2021) described hypothyroid rhabdomyolysis muscle stiffness and aches after heavy alcohol plus raw crucifer intake in undiagnosed Hashimoto thyroiditis, whereas Felker et al. (2016) showed typical culinary cauliflower servings deliver minimal goitrin. Bundled cruciferous feeding lowered IL-6 (-19%) with mixed IL-8 (+16%) and null CRP/TNF-alpha in healthy young adults (Navarro 2014) without measuring pain or rigidity.
