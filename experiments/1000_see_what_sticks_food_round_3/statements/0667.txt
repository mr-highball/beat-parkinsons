Category: spice
Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted coriander seed worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Opposing preclinical PD-model evidence leans against the claim that toasted coriander seed worsens bradykinesia: Koppula et al. 2021 (local PMC8222636, PMID:34177406; n=60 Swiss albino mice, 10/group) demonstrated that 21 days of oral Coriandrum sativum fruit (seed) extract (100, 200, 300 mg/kg p.o.) in MPTP+probenecid Parkinsonian mice significantly restored day-21 open-field line crossings, rearing, and grooming and reduced immobility time (P<0.05 to P<0.001; non-sedating in seed-only 300 mg/kg controls), while Mohan et al. 2015 (local extract, PMID:26040026) found oral ethanolic coriander seed extract (100-200 mg/kg p.o. x 15 d) increased locomotion (P<0.05) in tacrine-treated rats. Zhang 2015 bridges roasting retention. Important caveats preserved: acute intraperitoneal aqueous seed extract in healthy mice reduced spontaneous activity (Emamghoreishi 2005, route-dependent sedation), and leaf-extract PD rodent studies (Lee 2026; Saeki 2022) tested cilantro leaves rather than seeds and are excluded. Animal extract models and zero human PD motor trials cap certainty at minimal_signal (50.005).
