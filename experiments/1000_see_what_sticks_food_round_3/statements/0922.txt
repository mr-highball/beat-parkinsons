Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: dream enactment frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate reduces dream enactment frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no controlled study evaluates plain dark chocolate on dream enactment frequency in Parkinson's disease; a single historical case report (Vorona & Ware 2002, n=1, cited by Jun 2022) described exacerbation of REM sleep behavior disorder after unspecified chocolate ingestion, which is anecdotal and does not isolate plain dark chocolate or establish directional efficacy.
