ID: 0877
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted cumin reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A small 3-arm menstrual-spice study without a placebo group (Omidvar 2019, ~10 per arm) qualitatively mentioned reduced menstrual fatigue under 3 g/d cumin without validated numeric scores, while the placebo-controlled 2026 older-adult cumin oil RCT (Ito 2026) was null on POMS2 mood/fatigue; Parkinson's fatigue effects remain open.
