ID: 0935
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): controlled non-PD pain trials fail to isolate a cocoa-specific analgesic effect of plain dark chocolate—Hajati 2023 (n=30 DB crossover) found 70% dark, milk, and white chocolate reduced experimental masseter pain equally versus no chocolate (non-specific sweet-taste analgesia), Nemoto 2022 (n=88 72% dark chocolate RCT) found NO direct between-group SF-36 bodily pain difference, Nuha 2023 (n=45 dysmenorrhea) lacked a placebo arm and lost to ibuprofen (p<0.05), and Marcus 1997 (n=63) was null on migraine triggering (p=0.83).
