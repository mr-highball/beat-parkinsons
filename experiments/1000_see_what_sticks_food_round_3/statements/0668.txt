ID: 0668
Category: spice
Ingredient: coriander seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground coriander seed reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for ground coriander seed reducing constipation severity is Unknown (Researched). Clinical bowel trials containing coriander seed all used multi-herb formulas containing potent stimulant laxatives or other botanicals—Ghafarzadegan et al. 2025 (PMID:39951927, 5-herb formula containing Cassia senna and Aloe barbadensis) and Moazzam et al. 2022 (PMID:36325243, 5-herb open-label Jawarish Shahi in IBS)—preventing attribution to coriander seed alone (attribution_fit: remote). Ex vivo gut strips (Jabeen et al. 2009, PMID:19146935) showed dual spasmogenic and spasmolytic activity, and traditional digestive uses (Sobhani 2022) are carminative/context-only.
