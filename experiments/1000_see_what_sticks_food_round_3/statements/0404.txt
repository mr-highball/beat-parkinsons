Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: poached
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached chicken worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial has shown that poached chicken worsens walking slowness in Parkinson's disease. Healthy-adult steamed chicken breast resistance-training studies (Uchida 2024) and knee-OA Essence-of-Chicken-plus-collagen studies (Chen 2023) measured muscle strength and fat-free mass without reporting PD gait speed, and while daytime protein loads can lengthen OFF time in levodopa-fluctuating PD (Barichella 2006), no trial has measured walking velocity after poached chicken.
