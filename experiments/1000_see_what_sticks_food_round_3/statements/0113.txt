ID: 0113
Category: food
Ingredient: cashew
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted cashew worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): although cashew nut supplementation modestly lowered resting systolic blood pressure in T2DM adults (Mohan 2018, -4.9 vs -1.7 mmHg, P=0.04; Jalali 2020 meta-analysis WMD -3.39 mmHg, P=0.01), controlled feeding trials in non-diabetic adults showed null resting blood pressure (Baer 2019, Mukuddem-Petersen 2007), and no trial measured standing blood pressure, postural drops, or orthostatic lightheadedness.
