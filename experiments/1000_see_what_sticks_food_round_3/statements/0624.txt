ID: 0624
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no clinical trial measures stride length under brewed coffee or caffeine in Parkinson's disease or older adults; Postuma 2012 reported non-significant general UPDRS gait items (CIs spanning zero) without measuring stride length, and CRAVE (Marcus 2023, n=100) measured daily step count (+1,058 steps/d) rather than spatial stride length.
