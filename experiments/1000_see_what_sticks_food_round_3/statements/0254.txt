ID: 0254
Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: reduced facial expressiveness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk reduces reduced facial expressiveness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human trial or symptom-specific Parkinson animal assay tests whether raw celery stalk reduces reduced facial expressiveness. While whole-plant Apium graveolens methanolic extract improved general MPTP mouse locomotion and SNpc TH+ neurons (Chonpathompikunlert 2018), it did not separately score reduced facial expressiveness; human stem-and-leaf powder trials lacked neurological endpoints (Mohsenpour 2023); and clinical trials of celery seed extracts or synthetic dl-3-n-butylphthalide (NBP) are strictly excluded under plant-part and pharmaceutical separation.
