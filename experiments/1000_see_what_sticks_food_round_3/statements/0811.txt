ID: 0811
Category: herb
Ingredient: culinary lavender
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed culinary lavender reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Minimal signal (Maybe True, anchor 50.005) that brewed culinary lavender reduces depressive symptom severity, supported by three oral lavender tea/infusion trials (PMID:32444033, PMID:26523950, PMID:24578844) and two placebo-controlled Silexan DB-RCTs in MADD and MDD (PMID:26718792, PMID:38558147), balanced by a null venlafaxine add-on DB-RCT (PMID:28892932), null menopausal RCT (PMID:38754638), and absence of Parkinson's clinical studies.
