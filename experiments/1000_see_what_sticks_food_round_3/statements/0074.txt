ID: 0074
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed carrot reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for steamed carrot and Parkinson's dyskinesia severity remains open. Custódio-Silva 2024 reported reduced vacuous oral movements in reserpine rats, which models tardive/orofacial dyskinesia rather than levodopa-induced dyskinesia in PD, and used purple carrot root extract rather than steamed orange carrot. No clinical trial evaluates steamed culinary carrot on dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's.
