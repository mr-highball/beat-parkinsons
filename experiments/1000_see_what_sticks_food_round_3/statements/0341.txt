Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming soaked chia seed reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for soaked chia seed reducing anxiety severity is Unknown (Researched). Preclinical rodent findings conflict: Bilgic et al. 2018 (PMID:30624431) found whole chia seeds ineffective against elevated plus-maze anxiety in AlCl3 AD rats, Rivero-Salgado et al. 2024 (PMID:38890948) saw no anxiolytic change in open-field testing of a chia-amaranth-curcumin diet, and Adel et al. 2019 (PMID:31278713) reported light-dark changes confounded by reduced open-field and home-cage locomotion. No human clinical trials evaluate chia seed for anxiety.
