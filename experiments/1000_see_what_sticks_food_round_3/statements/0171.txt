Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (50.005): Contrary to the frozen claim that ground cayenne worsens olfactory impairment, a controlled intranasal MPTP Parkinson's mouse study (Wei 2025, NPJ Parkinson's Disease) found intraperitoneal capsaicin monotherapy (1 mg/kg/day x 7 days, independent of nicotine) significantly eased PD olfactory impairment across four behavioral olfactory tests (buried food pellet, olfactory preference, social odor block, and day-17 odor avoidance) while protecting olfactory bulb dopaminergic neurons and suppressing cGAS/TBK1/STING and MAPK neuroinflammation. Human intranasal Capsicum spray caused no olfactory impairment (Bernstein 2011), and people with PD on public forums commonly use chili/Tabasco as a trigeminal flavor workaround for hyposmia (not reversing smell loss). Signal remains minimal because no human PD oral cayenne olfaction trial exists.
