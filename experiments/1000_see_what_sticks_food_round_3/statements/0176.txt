Symptom: speech intelligibility impairment

Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): No clinical trial or observational study has evaluated whether ground cayenne pepper affects speech intelligibility or hypokinetic dysarthria in Parkinson's disease. Oral capsaicin experiments measure acute mucosal burning sensation, salivary flow, or burning-mouth discomfort (Duner-Engstrom 1986; Houghton 2020; Jankovskis 2021) without speech-clarity or articulation endpoints.
