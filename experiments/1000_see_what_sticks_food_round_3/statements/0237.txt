Symptom: depressive symptom severity

Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence for raw celery stalk and Parkinson's depressive symptoms remains open. Although whole-plant Apium graveolens methanolic extract lowered forced-swim and tail-suspension immobility and brain MAO-A in healthy non-PD mice (Boonruamkaew et al., 2017), direct human observational data for raw celery in Brookie et al. (2018, n=422) were completely null for depressive symptoms (r=-0.060, NS). Strictly enforcing plant-part separation, the Shayani Rad et al. (2023) depression/anxiety RCT tested concentrated celery SEED extract (1.34 g/d), NOT culinary celery stalk.
