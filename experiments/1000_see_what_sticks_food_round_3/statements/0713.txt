ID: 0713
Category: food
Ingredient: cow's milk
Preparation: pasteurized
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming pasteurized cow's milk reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (Researched): no randomized clinical trial tests plain pasteurized cow's milk on anxiety severity in Parkinson's disease or general adults; UK Biobank + FinnGen MR (Wu 2024, n=357,568) linked semi-skimmed and full-cream milk to lower incident anxiety diagnosis risk (HR 0.90 and 0.84; skimmed milk null), whereas Hu 2026 (n=123,519 youth MA) found overall milk/dairy anxiety-depression odds null (OR 0.70, CI crossing 1.0), and diagnosis-risk cohorts do not establish symptom severity reduction.
