Statement: Consuming toasted cumin worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: In true Cuminum cyminum (excluding Nigella sativa), the 2026 older-adult cumin oil RCT found a null effect on Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (Ito 2026), tempering a single mouse pentobarbital-sleep study (Nouri 2024); Parkinson's insomnia effects remain open.
