Category: herb
Ingredient: cilantro
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried cilantro worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human insomnia study tests dried cilantro leaves—the published PSQI trial used coriander seeds—and a single parenteral mouse pentobarbital-sleep study of aerial-part fractions is too indirect to determine dried cilantro's effect on Parkinson's insomnia.
