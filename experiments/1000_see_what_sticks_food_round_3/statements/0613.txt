Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee reduces freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): Kitagawa 2007 evaluated oral caffeine 100 mg/day—equivalent to ~1 cup of brewed coffee (Haskell-Ramsay 2018; Jeon 2019)—in people with Parkinson's disease and freezing of gait (FOG), reporting improvement in the 'total akinesia' FOG subtype (no benefit in 'trembling in place') with tolerance restored after a 2-week washout. Because Kitagawa 2007 was uncontrolled with exact sample size and quantitative FOG scores missing from the indexed abstract (reliability_fit=remote), while Gabbert 2023 (n=4,889 PD) showed null coffee-freezing association (p=0.2364) and Postuma 2012/2017 did not isolate FOG, evidence stays at minimal_signal.
