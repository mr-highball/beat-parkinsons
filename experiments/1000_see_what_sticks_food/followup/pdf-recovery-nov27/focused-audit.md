## 0325 roasted carrot and executive function

The 2026 CIRCUS report includes carrot in the common-produce diet with 39 adults participating and 36 completing. It reports lipid changes and no detected difference in office blood pressure or vascular measures. Neither the 2026 report nor the existing metabolomics abstract supplies the registered executive test results or an isolated roasted-carrot comparison. The repository abstract describes 15 participants divided among no fasting, water-only fasting and fasting with carrot juice plus water. The three Stroop tasks show no reported group main effect. The time-related changes include both fasting groups, with no usable independent carrot comparison. The rat study used carrot juice with water/feed and dopamine comparators. Its short discussion calls a faster return to the shock-associated compartment better memory. This interpretation and the apparent contrast need clarification. Passive avoidance is not a clinical executive-function measure. The CIRCUS protocol, 2025 biomarker abstract and 2026 lipid abstract are counted once as a cohort.
