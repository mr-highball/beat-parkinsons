## First source review

The paired result was inconclusive and its discordant counts were unreported. A separate morning cohort had 5/11 acute somnolence reports after oral tea without a comparator. The active preparation was stronger than customary. This is an indirect worsening signal from the beverage, with a binary waking outcome in healthy volunteers. The four Parkinson aroma-oil cases provide a different-route observation with unchanged daytime sleep.
