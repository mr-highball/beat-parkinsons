# Source routes and remaining limits

- Thai YTPS article names TCTR20250522004 and describes conduct in 2024. Only the registry page shell was verified, so public posting and registered outcomes remain open. The article assigns capsule totals inconsistently between its methods and abstract. The rheumatoid-arthritis article names CTRI/2020/03/024187. Its main pain tables and complete original registry remain unverified. A same-author 2021 case uses the newer recipe, while the unrelated 2012 paper with the same formula name includes no black pepper. [Thai original](https://doi.org/10.1515/jcim-2025-0212) · [Thai registry route](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/show/TCTR20250522004) · [RA original](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378874124001326) · [Earlier trial](https://ijcrr.com/article_html.php?did=1834)
- The Langley-Brady 2020 dissertation title is verified in the university record but no publicly accessible dissertation body was recovered. The official trial first posted in February 2018 names weekly SF-MPQ-2 and daily VAS. It has no posted arm-level results. The 2026 qualitative study was checked at author-abstract depth. There is no verified linkage of its nine participants to the intervention cohort. [University dissertation record](https://augusta.elsevierpure.com/en/persons/dawn-l-langley-brady/) · [Official trial JSON](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT03449303) · [Intervention article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pmn.2022.12.008) · [Photovoice report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42913343/)
- The earlier pepper screen's long-pepper abstract identifies Piper retrofractum, a different species from Piper nigrum. The full topical nanoemulsion paper named analgesia as a possible use but used physicochemical measurements, with biological efficacy work still prospective. [Long-pepper primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37712769/) · [Topical formulation original](https://brieflands.com/journals/ijpr/articles/159933.pdf)
- The knee osteoarthritis trial's separate Persian pain paper and English biomarker paper share ingredients, comparator, duration, group sizes and most investigators. An explicit cross-publication linkage and authoritative registry history remain open. A separate curcumin and piperine stent-symptom registry lists actual enrollment of nine, with no posted results. [Original Persian paper](https://hsr.mui.ac.ir/article-1-1095-fa.pdf) · [English biomarker article](https://doi.org/10.1002/ptr.6671) · [Official stent registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02598726)
- Mones and Weiss pages 512–518 were inspected in original PMC images with native OCR as a reading aid. The mean example interval shortened after some stimulations, and the authors discuss pain in interpreting the stimulation response. No food comparison is reported. The 1989 JNNP letter was recovered in an archival copy of the publisher PDF. The existing 1990 Sage and 1988 dystonic-parkinsonian source pairs remain open at abstract scope.
