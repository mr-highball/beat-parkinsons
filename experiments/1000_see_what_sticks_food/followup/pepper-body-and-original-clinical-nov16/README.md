# Pepper pain originals and clinical queue follow-through

Reviewed November 16, 2026 UTC for the original frozen experiment.

## Clinical pepper interventions

Claim 0312 remains Unknown at 50. The closest comparison is the earlier commercial ground-pepper add-on to a fixed turmeric regimen, which gave an inconclusive chronic-pain contrast outside Parkinson disease. The Thai trial compared an oral mixture, massage and both. The Cartilox trial compared a piperine-containing blend plus exercise with exercise alone. It reported a brief early symptom increase, lower mean pain at day 14, and zero for both arms at day 30. Its numerator and printed percentage differ. The two studies assign several simultaneous exposures, with no separable cracked-pepper result. The rheumatoid-arthritis mixture also leaves the original noninferiority calculation and exact arm-level pain tables open. [Updated claim evidence](assessment-additions.tsv) · [Chronological records](source-accessions.tsv)

Two primary Viphyllin papers investigate different groups and routes. One oral extract study uses a diabetic-rat thermal withdrawal reflex, and an injected mouse study has several nociception assays. The 2025 isolated-piperine mouse paper also separates higher thermal response latency from the inconclusive low-dose mechanical response. None measures Parkinson pain severity after consuming culinary pepper. [New study evidence](assessment-additions.tsv)

The Persian Mixodin trial provides numerical pain and function outcomes for 60 adults with knee osteoarthritis. Mean week-four pain was 4.53 on the three-extract blend and 4.37 on naproxen, with an inconclusive group comparison (p=.747). The same setting, interventions and authors link this clinical paper to the likely companion biomarker report. The open Italian neuropathic-pain study favored its combination add-on, which also contained curcumin and lipoic acid. Its correction concerns trademarks. Neither comparison gives an isolated cracked-pepper clinical effect. [Persian original](https://hsr.mui.ac.ir/article-1-1095-fa.pdf) · [Italian original](https://doi.org/10.2147/JPR.S48432)

## Focused literature and registry coverage

This follow-through retains the exact 68 appearances from the previous three count-complete searches and updates 27 dispositions. Twenty-four appearances now have full-text review. Six remain pending. The overlapping Alzheimer patent examples and antioxidant formulation publications are accounted for by publication. The former measure cognition and pathology in mouse models. The latter describe multiple disease contexts and give no cracked-pepper clinical pain estimate. The nicotine patent examples concern formulation.
