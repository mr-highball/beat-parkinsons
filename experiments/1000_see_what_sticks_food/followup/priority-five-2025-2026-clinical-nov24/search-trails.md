# Search trails and verified outcome boundaries

Oracle revision 104 preceded external original lookup. It resolved the previously archived Parkinson diet study through both PMID and PMCID and resolved the cited Jackson blend crossover. The new Lee original was retrieved after a DOI miss. The batch used 54 verified structured PMC article bodies and six authenticated PMC HTML originals.

The primary [UK Biobank supplemental table](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC12615346.1/mmc1.pdf) was downloaded from NLM's public archive. Table S23, PDF page 102, was checked by text extraction and rendered table inspection to keep the anxiety and incident Parkinson estimates in their respective columns. The main article reports the 90th-versus-10th-percentile comparison and identifies the multiple-testing correction. Neither source gives a grade for olive oil or a Parkinson subgroup anxiety severity comparison.

The cited [mixed beverage trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7468953/) distinguishes observed acute mental fatigue from alertness, other mood measures and mechanism. The [banana author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2858379/) separates healthy adult plasma measures from an animal tissue experiment. Both sources were recorded with full original depth and treated as contextual for the frozen endpoints.
