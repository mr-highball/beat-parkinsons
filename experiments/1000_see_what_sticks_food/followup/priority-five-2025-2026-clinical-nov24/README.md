The UK Biobank supplementary analysis reports incident anxiety HR 0.905 and incident Parkinson HR 1.01 for higher olive-oil intake. The table does not identify cold-pressed grade or rate anxiety in a diagnosed Parkinson population. The earlier direct symptom evidence controls the minimal easing tier.

The Jackson crossover measured less mental fatigue after two sage-containing blends. The active recipes differ in their fruit or coffee-berry extracts and all contain beetroot, ginseng and sage extract. The Lee original study found higher circulating salsolinol in healthy adults after banana intake and no clear change in sampled brain regions of rats from a separate experiment. No anxiety endpoint was measured. The applied scorecards retain the preparation and endpoint limits.
