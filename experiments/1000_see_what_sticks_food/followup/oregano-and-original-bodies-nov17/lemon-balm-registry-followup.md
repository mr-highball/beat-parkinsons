# Lemon balm tea crossover results check

The original UMIN record for UMIN000055134 lists follow-up as complete on January 31, 2025. The registration describes 120 mL brewed lemon balm tea versus hot water in a randomized crossover for students with premenstrual symptoms. Depression is a secondary DASS-21 outcome. Fields for actual enrollment, results and publication are empty. The anticipated start date does not verify actual enrollment. [Current UMIN record](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/icdr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000062988) · [Registration history](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_his_list.cgi?recptno=R000062988) · [Japanese national registry mirror](https://rctportal.mhlw.go.jp/en/detail?trial_id=UMIN000055134)

The university ethics record identifies the Wakui crossover as 2023-25. The listing for 2024-12 explicitly identifies it as an amendment to that protocol. Both refer to the same trial. No outcomes are given.
