# Oregano anxiety and original source follow-up

## Oregano and anxiety

Three Europe PMC expressions returned 230 appearances across five pages. The broad anxiety and stress query supplied 226 appearances for human-candidate title triage. The two narrower queries supplied four appearances. Three ClinicalTrials.gov expressions supplied 25 appearances, with overlap between expressions. The review checked the registry interventions and outcome fields and followed selected literature candidates to their original bodies. [Exact queries and counts](oregano-query-receipts.tsv) · [Retrieved membership and ranks](oregano-query-membership.tsv)

The dried oregano powder trial randomized 24 healthy soldiers and measured exercise blood markers. It measured no anxiety outcome. A separate four-period crossover in 20 adults used an oregano-containing mixed-spice meal and a social stress task. Its reported endpoints were metabolic measurements. The trial did not report an anxiety scale. NCT06889961 uses six spices and a broad mental-health measure. Its eligibility criteria exclude Parkinson disease, and the checked registry had no results. These comparisons leave claim 0634 Unknown. ## Ginger and pain

A randomized active-comparator trial in 122 students reported inconclusive menstrual-pain differences between ginger capsules and mefenamic acid. The trial reported no equivalence margin or placebo arm. This comparison adds an uncertainty check for claim 0725, which remains Maybe True at 51%. The weak favorable lean rests on other human evidence and is limited by differences in preparation, population and type of pain.
