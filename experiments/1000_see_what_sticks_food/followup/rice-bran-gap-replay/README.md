## Evidence and remaining trails

- 0426. Part IV was a secondary endpoint in the Thai Parkinson study. The meeting abstract reports a motor-complications aggregate without an item-level painful OFF-state dystonia result. Samad's 2015 animal study reports isolated or broader motor behaviors, including vacuous oral chewing and rotarod coordination. The companion primary abstracts share methods and do not establish distinct cohorts or a dystonia grade. The 2024 chamuangone-enriched study used its drug challenge and plant mixture without a plain oil arm. The observed catalepsy and walking measures do not establish clinical dystonia.
- 0678. Hayakawa's trained human panel measured perceived flavour and aroma while sampling fresh oil. Koeda's controlled odor crossover recorded intensity of the presented rice-oil and cypress stimuli in healthy adults, with rice oil rated markedly weaker. Neither assesses whether chronic olfactory impairment changed. Instrumental volatile assays, electronic noses, cosmetic applications and rice bran processing are different study targets. The existing butylphthalide olfaction study used soybean oil as the relevant vehicle.
- 0866. The Thai Parkinson report has no acoustic or item-specific speech result in public. The Aoyagi trial includes wet-hoarse dysphonia inside a swallowing scale grade, with no separate rating of voice strength. Akahane reports hoarseness long after exposure to contaminated rice oil, with no acoustic measure. The comparative hoarseness table and ascertainment details were not recovered from the publisher preview. For the Koeda inhalation study, Methods and Figure 1 disagree on the amount applied to mask cotton. The direction of odor-strength and swallowing contrasts printed in the research report is unaffected by transcribing that discrepancy.
- 0934. The Thai study administered the closest verified clinical preparation. It gives a total MDS-UPDRS and no handwriting item 2.7, writing sample, letter size or related follow-up. The older and newer animal movement and grip tasks do not measure handwriting. Exact native queries found no intervention result tied to small handwriting after edible rice bran oil.
- 0492. Aoyagi and colleagues provide a new candidate for the previously assessed swallowing claim. The control session produced no clear within-session difference in repeated saliva swallowing or either water swallowing test. Six participants had unspecified neurodegenerative diagnoses. The paper neither identifies individual Parkinson diagnoses nor presents separate subgroup results, and it studied odor exposure without ingestion. UMIN000053271 is the crosswalk for this clinical report. The healthy Koeda fMRI study uses a separate cohort, no diagnosed dysphagia and a different protocol.

The next recoverable source is a published Thai full report or item-level dataset. The registry mentions future IPDShare availability for two years after publication and provides no dataset link, oil lot or active carrier. Exact author, trial title, Thai-language and registry-ID searches surfaced the meeting report and the registration record without an accessible individual or item dataset. Separate sources for the Japanese oil lot and the Yusho item table would refine interpretation. The acoustic outcome search yielded no corresponding consumption comparison.
