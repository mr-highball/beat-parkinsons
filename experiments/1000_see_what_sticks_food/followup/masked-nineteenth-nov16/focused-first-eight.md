# Nineteenth masked batch independent primary review — 2026-11-16

Query design and provisional appraisal used ingredient, preparation and endpoint only. Web discovery is targeted and selected, not a full native provider run. Review subject bodies before applying direction; no results establish a direct food-specific Parkinson effect.

## 0712 raw grapes / hand dexterity
NEW original 2016-02-11 Lamport et al. PMID26864371 DOI10.3945/ajcn.115.114553 https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/52909/1/Am%20J%20Clin%20Nutr-2016-Lamport-775-83.pdf . Randomized 25 healthy working mothers to crossover daily 355 mL Concord grape juice vs matched placebo, 12-week periods separated by four weeks. Grooved Pegboard times averaged across hands: condition×study-phase F(1,21)=9.61 p<.01. In first phase placebo was faster 60.4 s (SE 1.9) vs juice 63.2 s (SE 2.3), p<.05; second phase no difference. Time/sequence interaction is not a uniform worsening; juice processing, selective population and non-PD limit use. Table 2 pooled visit results do not supply a baseline-adjusted independent effect.

NEW Bøhn et al. 2021-11-22 PMID34802467 DOI10.1186/s40795-021-00482-8 NCT00972972 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8607697/ randomized 60 older men with subjective memory complaints to 9 weeks bilberry/red-grape mixture vs placebo. No significant group difference in grooved pegboard change; dominant hand reported p=.41. The bilberry mixture and juice prevent attributing the estimate to raw grape.

NEW Bell et al. 2020-01-16 PMID31942838 DOI10.1080/1028415X.2019.1708033 NCT03526406 in the Methods, with conflicting cover identifier NCT03579095, original https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/88613/3/Bell%20et%20al%2C%20Nutritional%20Neuroscience%2C%20author%20final%20copy.pdf : 60 healthy adults aged 18–30, 400 mg purified grape-seed polyphenol vs placebo, 12 weeks. Simple finger-tapping treatment×visit F(1,60)=8.36 p<.01, placebo declined weeks 6–12 while extract remained stable; most outcomes inconsistent, and extract is not fruit. In combination the phase-specific unfavorable juice comparison, null mixed-juice result and extract-favored finger proxy do not support assigning a raw-grape/PD causal direction.

## 0709 baked rye bread / gait freezing
Prior NCT03275948 baked rye kernel bread trial measures cognition and mood only; prior PMID24464413 celiac Parkinsonism case used gluten-free diet and reported walking improvement, without separate rye intake or freezing. Dietary protein timing and unvalidated overall gait improvements are not event-frequency tests. No newly verified isolated rye intervention with FOG outcome.

## 0568 fresh chervil / dystonia; 0556 dried lovage / walking slowness
Existing Hegelmaier 2020 PMID32041265 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7072275/ Appendix A includes chervil and lovage in a mixed vegetarian menu; exposure amount/preparation and separate dystonia or timed walking are unavailable. Distinguish Levisticum officinale aerial leaves from Sichuan lovage Ligusticum chuanxiong rhizome in herbal formulas. Searches located lovage leaf/stem extracts for metabolic endpoints and rhizome reports, not separately measured target symptoms. Chervil is Anthriscus cerefolium, not chervil root or other Apiaceae; no measured new clinical dystonia outcome.

## 0637 ground sumac / urinary urgency
Existing 2019 PMID31261419 is Rhus aromatica bark combination, not Rhus coriaria fruit; no assigned single-ingredient effect. Official registry DRKS00000268 https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00000268 specifies pumpkin oil, fragrant sumach bark and hop extract, overactive bladder, excludes neurological conditions. Laboratory Rhus coriaria fruit antimicrobial assays do not measure urgency. No new independent exact oral food/urgency trial found.

## 0562 ground anise / daytime sleepiness
Existing 2024-01-16 primary Mosaffa-Jahromi et al. DOI10.3389/fphar.2024.1331177 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10824915/ ; IRCT20120506009651N3. 225 adults with acute COVID and GI symptoms, 45 active/180 placebo; ground Pimpinella anisum seed and rock candy ratio 2:1, two 25-g total sachets in water daily for two weeks, placebo wheat flour and rock candy. AEs reported 2 mild throat itching and 1 thirst in active vs 3 cough and 2 cramps placebo; no day-sleepiness instrument or explicit systematic questioning of somnolence. No recorded sleep AE is not evidence of no effect. Different population and high dose, uncertain placebo blinding since taste differs. Existing 28-day aqueous-extract mouse study reports drowsiness at highest dose only without affected-animal counts. Same 2016 anise-oil IBS trial measures tiredness, which is not sleep propensity; cannot infer tested daytime sleepiness.

## 0701 cold-pressed sesame oil / olfaction
Existing PMID31703081 uses sesame oil as olfactory test stimulus, not ingestion intervention. 2025 intranasal sesame pilot PMID40716461 DOI10.1016/j.ctim.2025.103210 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40716461/ randomized 102 healthy volunteers to sesame, medicated oil or control for nasal barrier/safety, not reported olfactory score and route not oral. Study of scent inhalation PMID35566012 describes mouse stress behavior, not a smell-function test. Do not mistake nasal congestion relief or ingredient odor recognition for reversal of olfactory deficit.

## 0688 refined soybean oil / freezing of gait
Existing 2024 PMID39554352 https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/fsn3.4265 equal-fat soybean oil versus perilla diets in rotenone mice. Open-field immobility 135.0 versus 72.76 seconds is not episodic gait freezing, no clinical Parkinson patients and refining of soybean oil not specified; anxiety/sedation can alter freezing. No human ingredient-specific gait-freezing test established.
