## 2018 registration / 2020 first online paper: NCT03526406 and Bell et al.

Registry first posted 2018-05-16; completed 2019-02-28; last update posted 2020-08-20. Official CP9700 protocol: 60 actual participants, randomized parallel 400 mg capsule daily versus maltodextrin, healthy adults, finger tapping primary outcome at 2/4/6 h and 6/12 weeks. No registry results posted. https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03526406

Bell L et al. Nutr Neurosci, PMID 31942838, DOI 10.1080/1028415X.2019.1708033, first online 2020-01-16; journal 2022; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31942838/ . The Reading author manuscript https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/88613/3/Bell%20et%20al%2C%20Nutritional%20Neuroscience%2C%20author%20final%20copy.pdf Methods Design explicitly names NCT03526406, 60 aged 18–30, 30 per group; Intervention names MegaNatural AZ 400 mg purified Vitis vinifera seed polyphenol extract vs 400 mg maltodextrin. Methods Outcome Measures: dominant index finger simple taps on one key in 30 s; complex four-finger key sequences. Table 3 reports baseline-adjusted estimated marginal mean (SE) simple taps at weeks 6 and 12: placebo 124.36 (1.66) and 121.43 (1.73), extract 123.24 (1.66) and 125.71 (1.73); treatment-by-visit p=.005. Complex sequences placebo 20.48 (.73) and 21.14 (.67), extract 20.57 (.72) and 21.03 (.65), interaction not significant. Interaction is across the two follow-ups, not a separately established baseline-to-week-12 treatment effect. Multiple outcomes, healthy young adults, extract rather than raw whole fruit and Parkinson population limit transfer. Paper discussion says placebo also benefited on some other tasks, no consistent cognitive benefit.

Critical provenance: the repository cover/preliminary page prints NCT03579095 while the manuscript Methods prints NCT03526406. Official NCT03579095 first posted 2018-07-06 is a separate Reading 200 mg American ginseng Cereboost/placebo trial, completed 2019, with a different principal intervention and schedule (14 days); its registry lists a ginseng Bell paper, PMID34396468. The identifier on the first page cannot be treated as the grape trial; the actual Methods and matching design support the CP9700 join. Do not count the two registrations as two grape cohorts. Exact full article and registry support the association; no assumption that same participants enrolled in unrelated trials.

## 2024 registry / 2026 report: NCT06309914

First posted 2024-03-13, last update 2024-07-30, completed with 76 actual overall enrollees. Four parallel arms placebo, Enovita grape-seed extract, Mirtoselect bilberry, Virtiva ginkgo; primary FTT registered at baseline, 60/180 min and day 28, explicitly bilateral speed and number of taps. No official results posted. https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06309914

Papa et al., 2026-04-30, Journal of Food Science and Nutrition Research 9:18–23, DOI 10.26502/jfsnr.2642-110000189, https://www.fortunejournals.com/abstract/exploring-the-neurocognitive-potential-of-a-sustainable-grape-seed-extract-results-from-a-4week-pilot-study-6740.html and original PDF https://cdn.fortunejournals.com/articles/exploring-the-neurocognitive-potential-of-a-sustainable-grape-seed-extract-results-fro-6740.pdf (web PDF rendering accessible; direct PowerShell request Cloudflare denied). Original abstract explicitly names NCT06309914; Methods confirms four-arm parent and reports just grape seed and placebo (16+16). Enovita is water extract of Vitis vinifera seeds recovered from white wine production, 150 mg twice daily for 4 weeks; placebo rice flour/magnesium stearate. Main text outcome measures, tables 2–3 and figure report POMS and 4-part continuous performance working memory; text search throughout six pages has no finger/tapping mention or tapping outcome. No numerical tapping result verified. Working memory errors mean change −1.19 vs +1.06, group-time p=.050; this cognitive task is not the registered motor measurement. The grape and ginkgo papers referring to this parent may share its placebo arm; do not count as independent grape cohorts.

## 2025 registration: NCT07208916

First and last posted 2025-10-06; official status recruiting as last verified 2025-10; estimated completion 2026-04-30 is not proof of completion. Single-group, unmasked, planned 35 healthy women ≥65; two 36 g grape-powder packets mixed with water daily, for two 2-week periods. Secondary measures grip dynamometer and bilateral pinch gauge plus other outcomes; no control arm, no posted results. https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07208916 . Search of exact ID and investigator/source routes surfaced registry mirrors, no authentic result publication or numerical grip/pinch data. Recheck PI Brittany Adams / Western New England University and official results on update. No verified identification of powder with fresh whole raw grapes beyond its description; no PD outcome.

## Scope and disposition

One genuinely related 2020 controlled manual-speed source (seed extract, healthy young adults); registered but unpublished hand outcomes for 2024 Enovita and 2025 grape powder. NCT03579095 is American ginseng and not an independent grape trial. No claim of proven efficacy for raw grapes or PD hand dexterity; confidence judgment remains for Dexter.
