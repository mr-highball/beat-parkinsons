## Findings closest to the hypotheses

- **0712, raw grape and hand dexterity.** Concord grape juice slowed pegboard performance in the first crossover phase (63.2 vs 60.4 s), with no clear difference in phase two. The bilberry–grape drink comparison was inconclusive. The grape-seed extract trial favored simple tapping across two follow-up visits (interaction p=.005), with inconclusive complex tapping. Its Methods identify the CP9700 registry despite a cover identifier that belongs to a ginseng study. A separate grape-seed report omits its registered tapping outcome. These trials vary by food matrix, hand task and population. [Concord outcome](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Grape-Hand-Function.md#concord-juice-and-grooved-pegboard-in-a-crossover) · [Grape seed results and registry reconciliation](grape-registry-followthrough.md)
- **0705, refined avocado oil and anxiety.** In seven people with Parkinson disease, an uncontrolled ketogenic-diet continuation reported mean PAS change −6.29 (p=.0313). A single interview mentions coconut or avocado oil, with oil identity and the individual's PAS change unverified. A separate acute comparison of mint-flavored avocado oil and CBD in healthy adults found an inconclusive tranquility contrast (p=.85). [Parkinson diet follow-up](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Avocado-Oil-Outcomes.md#avocado-oil-mentioned-in-a-parkinson-ketogenic-anxiety-follow-up) · [Oil comparator](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Avocado-Oil-Outcomes.md#avocado-oil-comparator-and-acute-tranquility)
- **0637, ground sumac and urgency.** The fragrant-sumac bark combination trial stopped after seven active and two placebo recipients. The sponsor report gives no efficacy analysis. The 2019 same-product observation enrolled 117 women in a different, uncontrolled cohort. The product's bark and co-ingredients leave culinary ground fruit unresolved. [Randomized trial and original sponsor report](rye-and-sumac-followthrough.md)
- **0697 and 0702, oil and oral outcomes.** The sesame-swishing study reports speech-difficulty categories with arm totals that conflict with its stated allocation. No defensible between-arm speech estimate or listener intelligibility result was identified. The periodontal drops study reports stimulated saliva rising in both arms, with the soybean vehicle identified only by secondary sources. [Original table and access audit](oil-and-cayenne-followthrough.md)
- **0640 and 0566, motor findings in models.** The rat dyskinesia study used a TRPV1 antagonist with another drug. The original did not administer capsaicin. The same-accession cayenne meal thesis measured metabolism and differed from the registry on allocation. The nine-ingredient ginger-containing decoction improved mouse stride in its CatWalk figure, with no preparation-specific effect estimate.
