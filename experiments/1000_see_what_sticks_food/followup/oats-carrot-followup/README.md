# Oats and carrot claim follow-up

On 2026-11-13, this pass traced eight frozen oats claims and seven frozen carrot claims. All 15 retained Unknown after examining primary papers, individual tables, older and newer reports, trial registrations, exposure details, and the outcomes of both directions. The dated catalog received five notes and two licensed source texts. [Claim dispositions](claim-searches.tsv) · [Open trails](open-trails.tsv)

## Primary results and remaining questions

- The 2017 Parkinson survey gives an oatmeal item and total symptom burden, without identifying cooked or rolled oats or estimating the seven individual motor and nonmotor endpoints. In adults who smoked, a 2024 randomized wild green oat extract trial found a between-arm difference for actigraphic wake after sleep onset. The extract, population and endpoint leave the rolled-oats insomnia claim unresolved. A 2020 extract trial measured computerized tracking in adults, and the 2025 oats study combined oats with probiotics in a rotenone rat model. [2017 oatmeal survey note](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Oats-Carrot-Followup.md#2017-oatmeal-and-aggregate-parkinson-symptom-burden) · [2024 trial note](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Oats-Carrot-Followup.md#2024-green-oat-extract-and-sleep-in-smokers) · [2025 model note](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Oats-Carrot-Followup.md#2025-oats-and-probiotics-in-a-rotenone-rat-diet)
- Raw and cooked carrots were recorded separately in a 2025 Parkinson case-control questionnaire, and the estimates are for dietary patterns and diagnosis. A prospective 2020 study connects dietary patterns with probable REM sleep behavior disorder and daytime sleepiness, without individual carrot estimates. A 2022 Parkinson dietary-pattern study measured apathy, insomnia and sleepiness and found no significant associations for these outcomes. [2025 carrot questionnaire note](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Oats-Carrot-Followup.md#2025-raw-and-cooked-carrot-items-in-a-parkinson-case-control-analysis) · [2020 and 2022 sleep reports](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Oats-Carrot-Followup.md#2020-diet-patterns-and-prodromal-features)
- A 2015 carrot juice control arm yielded executive and gait measures outside Parkinson's. A 2019 conference abstract and 2024 paper studied oral purple carrot extract in reserpine-treated rats, with motor and swallowing measures. Full 2024 methods and any overlap in samples remain open. A 2021 black carrot cell paper cites a 2014 model study of polyphenol-rich extracts from other plants. [Carrot juice trial and conference note](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Oats-Carrot-Followup.md) · [Purple carrot journal report](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Oats-Carrot-Followup.md#2024-oral-purple-carrot-extract-in-reserpine-treated-rats) · [2014 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24502982/)
- An older outbreak linked a carrot and millet dish to auditory hallucinations after Jimson weed contaminated millet. A separate case of very high carrot intake recorded constipation. The older carrot fiber study and a Parkinson whole-diet trial add separate digestive evidence. Raw-carrot aroma ratings describe food sensory characteristics, while a Parkinson olfaction study recorded nutrient density alongside smell measures.
