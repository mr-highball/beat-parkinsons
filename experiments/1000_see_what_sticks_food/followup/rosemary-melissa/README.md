# Rosemary and lemon balm evidence follow-up

This pass revisited all 17 frozen rosemary and lemon balm claims. The vocabulary uses *Rosmarinus officinalis*, *Salvia rosmarinus*, *Melissa officinalis*, alternate common names and shared constituents. Europe PMC reconciled all four Parkinson-focused expressions with 125 result appearances and all five symptom expressions with 32. PubMed reconciled the four Parkinson-focused expressions and identified 87 distinct PMIDs. The occurrence of *Melissa* as a person’s name and *neurocirculatory dystonia* as a historical diagnostic label accounted for some lexical matches. The latter label concerns autonomic complaints, and the patent titles do not establish movement dystonia. The citation pass reconciled 1,223 reference and citing appearances across 32 endpoints. The narrow automated routing marked no edges for its next stage, so the batch also inspected titles and pursued related primary reports independently. That recovered oral and inhaled pressure comparisons, Korean massage trials, a lemon balm Parkinson model and a bladder frequency example reported in a patent. Figure 4 of the September 2026 oil paper shows shorter first-fall latency and more falls with the highest rosemary and lemon balm oil exposures. At the middle exposure the graph marks more falls for lemon balm, despite the general reassurance in the Discussion. The Results narrative reverses the plotted latency direction.
