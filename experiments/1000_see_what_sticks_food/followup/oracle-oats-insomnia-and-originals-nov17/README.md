## Claim 0174

The plain rolled-oat arm in the 2005 chronic insomnia trial had inconclusive changes and comparisons. The design included no randomly assigned no-oat arm. The newer Xie trial favored a combination of oat straw extract, melatonin, valerian and other ingredients. In the first granola study, subjective insomnia scores improved from baseline while EEG sleep outcomes were inconclusive. In a separate personalized granola study the overall insomnia score did not change significantly, although one exploratory subgroup improved. The whole-grain cohort associated consumption with lower prevalent insomnia odds and found no clear association with incident insomnia. Both arms of the Mah enzyme crossover received the same oatmeal and inulin. Extracted oat fiber doses did not differ on exploratory sleep duration in a separate uncontrolled study. None of these comparisons identifies the effect of eating rolled oats on insomnia in Parkinson disease. The verdict remains **Unknown, 50**, with 15 study assessments, including four pending.
