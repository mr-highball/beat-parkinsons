## Primary findings and differences in fit

- A single case described anaphylaxis after a dish containing baked eggplant. The report describes acute allergic symptoms, with no smell or dystonia outcome. A distinct eggplant extract study measured rotenone-associated injury in cells. [Local 2015–2019 sources](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md)
- Human butternut squash reports describe skin contact. The 2024 patient reported tolerating oral consumption of both zucchini and butternut squash. Hegelmaier's Parkinson intervention menu listed zucchini and Muscat pumpkin, with aggregate motor data and no relevant ingredient-specific outcome. The 1983 and 2020 bitter-zucchini reports and the 2000 blinded zucchini allergy challenges provide separate adverse-exposure contexts. [Local 1983 report](../../../../research/1980-1984/Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md) · [Local 2000 report](../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md) · [Local 2020–2024 sources](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md)
- Cooked button mushroom studies assessed the product's aroma with a trained panel or instrument. The 2026 Agaricus protein-fraction study found a motor signal in a mouse Parkinson model, with olfaction and executive function outside its accessible abstract. The native text archiver excluded PMC9210002 from source-file copying because the case is CC BY-NC. The catalog note links to the openly readable full article. [Local 2020–2024 sources](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md) · [Local 2026 sources](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Squash-Mushroom-Eggplant.md)
- The 2024 randomized whole-food study combined white button and oyster mushrooms without assigning roasting. It reported improved immediate memory in both arms, improved delayed list recall in controls only, and unchanged attention tasks. The pooled EPIC Norfolk analysis associated mushroom intake with better scores on some executive measures. Later checks retrieved the 2020 randomized capsule trial and 2025 randomized whole-cremini trial. Each reported no treatment effect on the reported cognitive battery. All were outside a Parkinson trial and differed in preparation.
