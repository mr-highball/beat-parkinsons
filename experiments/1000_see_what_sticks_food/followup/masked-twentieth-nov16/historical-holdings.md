The primary bound volume is from University of Maryland Baltimore Digital Archive, included in Medical Heritage Library; contributor metadata: https://archive.org/metadata/californiamedici9416cali. Its public PDF, DjVu text and OCR XML were successfully downloaded in this folder (63,022,463; 2,932,915; and 32,146,409 bytes respectively). Volume identity verified; original issue page boundary not established. Preceding leaves include February journal matter and some March advertising; the binding reorganizes or excludes issue material, and no independently authenticated match of the PMC scan fpage 2 was found. Search through complete IA OCR `chive`, `chives` did not produce the ingredient; occurrences of `Archives` are frequent. The observed OCR cannot establish the specific target scan's exact token, so remain pending. Avoid replacing PMC with the kidney paper or inferring a false match from this partial boundary.
