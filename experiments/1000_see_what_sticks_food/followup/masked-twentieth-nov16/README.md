## Outcomes closest to the hypotheses

- **0726, refined corn oil and insomnia.** In the Narayan pilot, the week-two ISI difference was −1.9 points for CBD versus corn oil (95% CI −5.0 to 1.1). The study used corn oil in both groups. Objective sleep efficiency favored CBD. The registry describes a different dose and duration, and a cognitive companion comes from the same cohort. In the Dalle exercise trial, the primary paper and supplement omit the registered PSQI. A separate study measured sleep with identical pharmaceutical-grade corn oil in both arms. The proposed military report link remains uncertain and the linked Azhar results were retracted. [Insomnia original](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Corn-Oil-Followup.md#corn-oil-vehicle-in-the-randomized-insomnia-comparison) · [Military and resistance follow-through](corn-oil-trial-followthrough.md)
- **0794 and 0808, potatoes and carrots with sleepiness.** A pediatric mash crossover registered repeated acute sleepiness. Its likely matching journal abstract gives other outcomes and does not establish the boiling step or sleepiness data. Three fried or baked potato protocols have no results. Tağraf's randomized Parkinson diet study reports an inconclusive Epworth comparison and does not isolate carrot. An earlier cross-sectional study finds serum alpha- and beta-carotene associations in opposite directions. [Earlier potato study](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Potato-Sleepiness-Followup.md#registered-pediatric-potato-meals-and-acute-sleepiness) · [Parkinson diet comparison](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Carrot-Sleepiness-Followup.md)
- **0729, refined coconut oil and daily OFF time.** The Fox survey names coconut oil among several products in a six-response supplement category. The group counts cannot identify how many people used coconut oil or whether OFF time changed. Choi reports the same two ordinal OFF means at baseline and day seven with inconsistent table and prose details. [Survey and primary randomized comparison](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Oil-Followup.md#coconut-oil-as-a-reported-off-period-coping-strategy)
- **0707 and 0715, depressive symptoms and attention.** The Song mouse report describes opposite directions of immobility in its Results and Discussion. The original figure and Methods leave even the proxy interpretation uncertain. The almond syrup trial reported lower parent-rated inattention in children taking methylphenidate. The between-arm baseline difference was substantial and the printed Tukey contrast does not identify a baseline-adjusted estimate.
