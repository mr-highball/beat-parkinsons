# ACTRN12620000070932 companion and version follow-through, 2026-11-16

Official ANZCTR https://www.anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?ACTRN=12620000070932 gives prospectively registered 29 January 2020, actual first participant 16 March 2021, actual final data 30 September 2022, last updated 3 February 2023. The current record describes 200 mg CBD in 2 mL corn-oil solution or corn-oil placebo for three weeks, yet its primary sleep latency assessment text says two active dosing weeks. No public change history or amendment explaining the divergence was identified in the displayed official record. A last-update date does not establish when each field was first added or changed.

The main study PMID38174873, DOI 10.5664/jcsm.10998 and original companion Narayan et al., DOI 10.1007/s00213-024-06674-x, both identify the same trial and 30 analyzed participants. Companion first published 17 August 2024, issue February 2025: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00213-024-06674-x . One week single-blind corn-oil placebo run-in preceded two weeks randomized 150 mg CBD or corn-oil-only placebo, 1.5 mL sublingually at bedtime, 15 analyzed per arm. Supplier Brains Bioceutical and compounding Aspa; processing/refinement of oil is not stated. Methods contain an obvious unit anomaly "150mL" for the target dose; abstract, 100mg/mL concentration and 1.5mL imply 150mg, not 150mL. An author amendment correcting the protocol dose or duration was not identified.

Same-cohort companion supplementary material is https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs00213-024-06674-x/MediaObjects/213_2024_6674_MOESM1_ESM.docx (HTTP 200; 59129 bytes; local companion-supp.docx and derived companion-supp.txt). CONSORT: 44 start run-in, 8 placebo responders excluded, 34 randomized (18 vs 16), 32 completed and 30 analyzed (15 each), with two extra randomized CBD completions excluded per predetermined sample target; analysis with 32 reportedly did not change overall conclusions. Cognitive task between-treatment effects p>0.05. Daily calmness, clear-headedness, coordination and dry-mouth outcomes differed; without a no-oil arm none isolates the effect of corn oil. Supplement provides cognitive task means and SDs, not an independent sleep trial.
