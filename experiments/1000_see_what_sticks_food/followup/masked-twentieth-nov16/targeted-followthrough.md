## 0674 ground cumin and standing lightheadedness
Agah et al., 2013, PMID 24829694, original full text https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3990147/ Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion. In 2009 the uncontrolled IBS series enrolled 57; 50 were analyzed. Oral cumin drops contained 2% essential oil, 10 drops twice daily for four weeks. The discussion says some patients had nausea, abdominal pain and dizziness after consumption, without a dizziness count, posture, severity, timing or control. Two withdrew for severe nausea, one for abdominal pain and two for inadequate relief; two were lost after treatment. The Results' opening “Twenty eight patients included” conflicts with its own abstract and analysis (57 and 50); do not silently adopt 28 as the enrollment. The existing 2010–14 Food-Experiment-Allspice-Cumin text already covers bowel outcomes, but not this dizziness passage. Candidate related-only assessment could point to a dated expansion of that existing source, explicitly essential oil, non-PD, uncontrolled and not orthostatic. It does not establish rate, causality, or a direction for the frozen claim.

2025 infusion trial https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12310444/ Table 4 shows systolic 144.18→136.84 vs control 146.34→142.00 mmHg and a group-time interaction p<.001; diastolic 87.41→78.66 vs 87.45→83.56. Resting BP is not an orthostatic symptom and the intervention includes diet modification; no dizziness match in body. Existing followup/allspice-cumin covers this context. Randomized essential-oil diabetes study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5506625/ has no adverse-event text or standing-symptom comparison; do not infer zero. The hypertension paper https://www.actamedindones.org/index.php/ijim/article/download/387/pdf/2163 identifies Nigella sativa (black cumin), a different taxon, so its orthostatic finding is not evidence for Cuminum cyminum.

## 0745 pasteurized cow milk and intelligibility

## 0749 cold-pressed walnut oil and dream enactment
2024 Kamoun primary https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38421528/ tests 15 g whole walnuts alongside training in elderly men versus training without walnuts; uses sleep quality, not documented dream enactment.
