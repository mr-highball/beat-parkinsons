### Primary comparisons and competing evidence

- **Black tea.** The 2012 secondary examiner-rated score comparison at three weeks was −0.97 (95% CI −2.50 to 0.56). The limb-only six-week comparison was −1.22 (−2.47 to 0.03). The longer Café-PD study reported −0.48 (−3.21 to 2.25) on total motor score at six months. The 2024 responder trial reported 12/15 versus 2/12 on the composite with imbalanced starting scores. The newer observational PPMI preprint used caffeine categories over one to four years. Publisher access failed, including the original methods and tables, and the caffeine source and adjustments are unverified. The experimental lean is mainly for a short-term symptom comparison. The black-tea cohort gives composite rather than bradykinesia scores. [2012 original](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Black-Tea-Motor.md#randomized-caffeine-trial-and-separately-reported-gait-and-rest-tremor) · [Café-PD](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Black-Tea-Motor.md#randomized-caffeine-capsule-study) · [2024 motor responders](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Black-Tea-Motor.md) · [Preprint, observational cohort and tea composition](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Black-Tea-Motor.md)
- **Flaxseed oil.** Table 1 in a 25-completer binge-eating trial gives BAI change from 7.5 to 4.5 with flaxseed oil and 6.8 to 6.7 with placebo. The reported final p value is .31. Moderate anxiety was excluded. Animal results vary across tests. The two flax-derived omega-3 plus vitamin E Parkinson trials reported total UPDRS and Part I respectively, without anxiety items. [Original Table 1 and Parkinson trials](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Flax-Anxiety.md) · [Rotenone mouse study](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Followup.md#flaxseed-oil-and-anxiety-like-behavior-in-mice) · [Maternal separation results](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Flaxseed-Followup.md#flaxseed-oil-in-the-ufrgs-maternal-separation-experiments)
- **Cinnamon.** In the migraine trial, pain VAS moved from 7.61 to 3.33 with bark powder and from 7.54 to 6.77 with placebo. There were 43 completers of 50 randomized. The registered enrollment gives 44 and the registration dates after study completion. Two later and earlier reports appear to use the same cohort, with the 2024 paper confirming the identity. The multiple-sclerosis trial gives a favorable VAS comparison with substantial attrition and higher baseline pain in the cinnamon arm. The registered measure was PQAS. Early menstrual pain comparisons were inconclusive and ibuprofen had lower scores. Another dysmenorrhea paper likely overlaps this sample, and a different team's abstract has conflicting direction wording. [Muscle soreness original](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Pain-Fatigue.md#cinnamon-powder-and-exercise-related-muscle-soreness) · [Menstrual and joint pain](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Pain.md) · [Migraine and multiple sclerosis](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Pain.md)
- **Saffron.** The 2020 extract study favored the Insomnia Severity Index (p = .017). The 2025 insomnia trial favored pooled extract arms on the Athens Insomnia Scale by −0.95 (95% CI −1.79 to −0.11). The two separate dose contrasts were inconclusive after adjustment. Its preprint represents the same trial. A distinct older-adult pilot reported an ISI interaction p = .3. The 2026 low-mood trial reported inconclusive sleep disturbance and favorable daytime impairment. These different endpoints and formulation matter when drawing a limited connection to a Parkinson powder trial using overall PSQI. ## Results that do not support a directional grade

- **0001 and 0118, olive oil and postural instability and basil and falls.** VIVI-OLIVE bundles extra-virgin oil with diet counseling. The clinical falls registration with holy basil combines different botanical ingredients. Both lack posted results. FOOP-Sarc later reported a gait-speed outcome in a different population, and the sweet basil animal experiment used distilled oil and a rotarod. [VIVI-OLIVE and FOOP-Sarc](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Marjoram-Olive-Outcomes.md) · [Holy basil registration](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Basil-Parsley.md#holy-basil-mixture-and-registered-fall-frequency)
- **0050 and 0059, parsley constipation and cauliflower anxiety.** The parsley sauce report has an independent provenance concern. The healthcare worker survey groups cauliflower with cabbage and broccoli and leaves the exposure category coding uncertain. [Parsley source provenance](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Basil-Parsley.md#parsley-sauce-meal-and-bowel-symptoms) · [Cauliflower table](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Egg-Pepper-Cauliflower.md#pooled-cruciferous-vegetables-and-anxiety-in-healthcare-workers)
- **0075 and 0120, roasted turkey attention and ground cinnamon fatigue.** A controlled-order meat dinner study reports no next-morning attention difference, with meat species and preparation unverified in the accessible abstract. The recovered glutathione review assigns its mouse fatigue experiment to curcumin. A Parkinson diet comparison used cinnamon in both groups. [Meat dinner study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32681417/) · [Recovered glutathione manuscript context](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Pain-Fatigue.md#curcumin-fatigue-and-cinnamon-references-in-a-glutathione-review)
- **0140, dried spearmint insomnia.** The 142-person Falcone extract trial found inconclusive sleep-domain comparisons. A previous older cohort favored sleep initiation with extract. A six-person preliminary crossover's one-week latency difference did not persist across the full two weeks in the available protocol summary. The later blend gives a small scale difference with inconclusive primary outcomes.
