# Japanese physiological proceedings page recovery

The Ninth FAOPS Congress proceedings were published June 20, 2019. The official publisher PDF includes 314 physical pages. The downloaded PDF SHA-256 is 3d61764a883523f23e639afecff60f2bdc2f86ac79357c905569b5bff9b22112. Its embedded text layer corrupts characters. [Publisher PDF](https://jps.biomedcentral.com/counter/pdf/10.1007/s12576-019-00673-w.pdf) · [Author and issue index](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31222665/)

Poppler rasterized every physical page at 220 dpi and the existing Windows English OCR Pascal unit produced a nonempty file for each of the 314 images. Native Free Pascal verification read 2,630,219 OCR bytes after trimming page-edge whitespace. The four short outputs were checked visually. Physical page 3 is the plenary divider, 11 the symposia divider, 96 a tutorial page explicitly marked without an abstract, and 284 the author index divider.

The native body matcher identifies 13 potato or tuber token occurrences, of which two are actual potato tokens, 32 Parkinson or medication-context matches, one OFF-time match and 39 broad model-marker matches. The two actual potato tokens are both within 1P-135 on physical page 123, printed S121. The visually checked original panel concerns white-skinned sweet potato and a pancreatic insulin endpoint. The sole matched OFF-time phrase is 1P-112 on physical page 119, printed S117. The original visual panel uses that phrase for the mouse light cycle in a caffeine timing comparison. The Parkinson and medication result excerpts identify other topics, including respiration and swallowing, DBS, receptor or neural mechanisms and model behavior.

The first replay has full physical-page OCR coverage.
