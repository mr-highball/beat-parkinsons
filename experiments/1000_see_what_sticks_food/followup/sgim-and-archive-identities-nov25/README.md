# Internal medicine supplements and archive identity checks

The two original internal medicine supplements supplied 27 exact dispositions across 18 frozen Round 1 claims. Four checks provide related context and 23 exclude sources whose exposures or outcomes fall outside the frozen questions. Companion studies and the separately identified proceedings brought the assessment and contextual-family additions to 13. Seven scorecards retained their verdicts, anchors and directional family counts.

The native replay leaves 30,822 original checks across 21,057 source keys and 393 frozen claims. The 26 remaining checks in the recovered-PDF group require the exact PET commentary or iodine letter. Both publisher PDF links returned subscription previews. The 2015 molecular imaging and 2022 nuclear medicine congresses have their own dated notes and verified DOIs.
