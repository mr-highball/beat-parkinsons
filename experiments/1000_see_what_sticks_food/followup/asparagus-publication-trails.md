# Asparagus publication trails

The frozen asparagus hypotheses cover steamed asparagus and swallowing difficulty (`0433`), steamed asparagus and drooling (`0647`), and roasted asparagus and fatigue (`0837`). Searches on November 12, 2026 used `Asparagus officinalis`, `enzyme-treated asparagus`, `ETAS`, `ONR-8`, `アスパラガス`, and terms for Parkinson's, levodopa, fatigue, swallowing, and drooling. Full text, trial histories, proceedings, publisher indexes, and citation lists were checked where accessible.

| Lead | Result and remaining access |
| --- | --- |
| Schrader et al., first online 2010 | The original case letter has no PubMed abstract and the publisher routes did not provide the full text. A subsequent primary case series gives case details from the letter, and a clinical report reproduces the bezoar image. These records describe an intestinal tube complication. Follow the original letter and preparation details. [Case and publisher access](../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-PubMed-Followup.md#asparagus-and-a-jejunal-levodopa-tube) |
| Waki et al., 2013 ESPEN | The official program identifies poster LB004-MON in Leipzig. The DOI resolver did not provide the abstract. Several authors overlap the 2016 paper and study overlap remains unverified. [Primary program](https://espen.org/files/Espen_Leipzig_2013_Final_Programme.pdf) · [Proceedings DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0261-5614(13)60606-8) |
| Takanari et al., 2016 | Full text shows a decrease in post-stress fatigue within the extract period. The figure significance marker is relative to day zero. The volunteers were healthy. [Dated note](../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#asparagus-stem-extract-and-fatigue-after-laboratory-stress) |
| Kato et al., May and June 2016 | The issue indexes identify two blinded ONR-8 crossover studies with different eligibility criteria and page ranges. The ingredient developer's registry summary of the June study gives insomnia and vigilance results in healthy evening-type men. The May fatigue result remains unverified from the original study. The issue and article routes require access for the full reports. [May issue](https://mp.medicalonline.jp/products/article_list.php?magazine_code=ai6yrtyb&number=5&volume=44&year=2016) · [June issue](https://mol.medicalonline.jp/archive/search?issue=6&jo=ai6yrtyb&vo=44&ye=2016) · [Developer's study summary](https://www.hokkaido-bio.jp/healthy-do/material/11) |

A 2019 investigator post identifies the interim conference presentation and UMIN's proceedings index does not supply its abstract.
