# Almond and avocado oil search

The native Europe PMC and PubMed searches reconciled both disease and exposure expressions. Each retrieved one almond oil paper and zero avocado oil papers. The almond record concerns a vehicle used in an estradiol experiment. PubMed preserved the submitted multilingual expressions and dropped several non-English aliases in its returned translations. Both the expression and translation remain available for audit. The OpenAlex pass covered nine disease, model, symptom and regional expressions. Eight succeeded initially, and the ninth returned HTTP 429. The retry recovered both records reported for that query. The combined files have 71 result appearances across 70 distinct work IDs. We screened all titles, used original tables, figures and protocols for plausible candidates, and left access gaps where only abstracts were available. An almond oil clinical query also returned skin preparations, oil chemistry and aromatherapy comparators. The Chinese Parkinson patent example identifies a sweet almond carrier where portions of the machine translation say apricot. Its later citing patent reports outcomes for a different blend. Avocado oil and rotenone co-occur in a kidney epithelial cell experiment. The FASEB abstract retrieved with it concerns hypertensive rat kidneys. Neither record measured Parkinson symptoms. The other-symptom OpenAlex query surfaced an avocado oil hypercholesterolemia rat study, and the Spanish and Portuguese result set contained a tourism dissertation and a food formulation study.
