# Sage and lemon balm counterevidence

This Round 1 batch checked the existing claim records and evidence rubric in Oracle before requesting missing originals. Authenticating 32 publication bodies resolved 113 exact claim–source checks. Five contained relevant context and 108 had no matching exposure and outcome. Exact checks and eight new assessment entries are recorded separately. Seven scorecards kept their published directions and anchors after validation.

- **Fresh sage and pain (0025).** The 2004 postoperative comparison found more pediatric pain with sage rinse than with an active local analgesic. The 2026 follow-up review draws its sage data from that same trial. Neither estimates the effect of eating fresh sage on Parkinson pain. Two related registry records still supply no arm-specific pain results.
- **Brewed lemon balm and depression (0133).** The 2025 scoping review groups dried leaf powder, an extract and a combination trial. In the combination trial, the isolated contrast for adding lemon balm to the same egg preparation was inconclusive. The review supplies no independent cohort or brewed-tea Parkinson result.
- **Fresh sage and fatigue (0883).** The previously assessed menopause trial has a newly indexed PMCID text with its inconclusive exhaustion contrast. An older dried-sage crossover reported different alertness effects before and during laboratory stress. It adds context about timing without providing a fatigue estimate in Parkinson disease.
- **Unresolved sources.** The 16 remaining publications in this batch represent 75 queued checks. Two highly reused PMC accessions lead to collections with different DOIs from the targeted publications. They remain open for exact original access. The deduplicated Oracle lookup log records 46 new exact-identity misses across confirmed or still-open sources and linked studies.

The replay leaves 29,568 exact checks across 20,810 publication keys and 380 claims. All 61,252 original memberships remain in the denominator. Round 2 remains queued.
