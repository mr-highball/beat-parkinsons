# Bok choy and fennel seed follow-up

The November 13, 2026 pass covered the 11 frozen statements involving cooked bok choy or toasted and ground fennel seed. It tested both directions and looked for exact or near exposure matches in original methods, arms, outcome tables and adverse event reports. All 11 exact claims remain Unknown. This pass identified human measurements outside Parkinson’s, one confirmed ingredient in a pooled falls questionnaire, animal experiments and a contaminant incident.

## Search coverage

- The native Europe PMC disease searches retrieved zero bok choy records and one fennel record, a trans-anethole melanogenesis study. Seven focused searches reconciled all reported records and pages. The counts by query were 0, 1, 3, 1, 41, 77 and 1. The first issued fennel search included Persian green cumin as an erroneous alias. It contributed no disease result and was removed from the corrected PubMed exposure plan. The Persian exact fennel term رازیانه was retained. - The native PubMed search expects the exposure in the second parenthesized clause. The first attempt put disease in that position, producing an overbroad and incomplete disease search. The corrected input yielded two bok choy results and one fennel result, all retrieved. Two results concerned sulforaphane or broccoli and the fennel result was a spice review used as a citation lead. PubMed dropped some unrecognized terms from its translation. Regional-language searches supplemented those terms. - Regional web checks combined 小白菜, 青梗菜 and 上海青 with 帕金森, 跌倒, 姿势不稳 and dietary terms, including search engine queries scoped to ChiCTR, CNKI and Wanfang. Japanese checks paired チンゲンサイ and 青梗菜 with パーキンソン病, 姿勢 and 転倒, including J-STAGE, UMIN and jRCT. These found symptom and dietary guidance but no isolated cooked-bok-choy outcome. Persian checks paired رازیانه with پارکینسون and terms for tremor, fatigue and sleep. These surfaced the original 6-OHDA paper and related clinical symptom papers without fennel exposure. ## Primary records

- The Taiwanese prospective study lists bok choy inside a dark-green vegetable category. A crossover feeding study verifies that women ate steamed bok choy with rice and measures glucose. The two publications leave exposure and postural outcome in separate studies. The earlier Loew meal paper is already catalogued. Its author institution provides an abstract with standing and tilt responses but restricts the published article and test-menu details. [Taiwanese cohort](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Bok-Choy-Fennel.md#bok-choy-in-a-taiwanese-prospective-falls-questionnaire) · [Controlled rice meal](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Bok-Choy-Fennel.md#steamed-bok-choy-in-a-controlled-rice-meal) · [Loew author repository](https://archive-ouverte.unige.ch/unige:28141)
- The 2010 healthy-mouse fruit extract lowered rotarod retention, while the 2015 6-OHDA study reported improvement only for peri-lesion seed extract. The 2023 MPTP conference report describes additional animal locomotor, coordination and learning results. The 2024 Koppula report shares authors and closely related cellular assays but used LPS in the in vivo arm. Its full PDF shows an early null rotarod result and longer retention on day 20. None of these measures isolated Parkinson resting tremor, micrographia, dystonia, OFF time, clinical freezing or postural instability after an edible toasted seed exposure. [2010 study](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Bok-Choy-Fennel.md#fennel-fruit-extract-and-reduced-rotarod-retention-in-mice) · [2015 study](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Bok-Choy-Fennel.md#fennel-seed-extract-in-a-6-ohda-rat-model) · [2023 abstract](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Bok-Choy-Fennel.md#aqueous-fennel-fruit-extract-in-an-mptp-model) · [2024 original PDF](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Bok-Choy-Fennel.md#fennel-extract-in-a-separate-mouse-neuroinflammation-experiment)
- A fennel capsule trial gave a null daytime dysfunction comparison. A small crushed seed infusion study used a subjective item spanning memory and focus, and mentioned fatigue without direction in an unclassified item. Fennel powder in Ghaffari’s trial improved a menopause composite that includes frailty but gives no item-level fatigue score. The 2026 seed oil massage trial reported a global sleep score and no standalone daytime dysfunction result. - A poison centre report identified a separate thorn-apple exposure in tea also containing ground fennel. It reported somnolence, lethargy and blurred vision. The Slovak abstract and Czech inspection refer to the same incident. A separate seizure case involved essential oil in cake and epilepsy.
