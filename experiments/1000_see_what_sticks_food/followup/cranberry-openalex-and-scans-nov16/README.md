# Cranberry research and original journal page scans

The cranberry OpenAlex replay identified two related studies with verified original results. The final Beckner altitude comparison found no detected POMS depression difference between the fiber/polyphenol mixture and placebo at high altitude. Its preliminary abstract used a comparison involving low-altitude placebo. Cranberry extract was one ingredient. Wang reported no detected effect of cranberry anthocyanin extract in either mouse immobility assay. The new results enter the study ledger with their exposure and outcome limits.
