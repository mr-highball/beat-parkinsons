## Brewed lemongrass and dream enactment

Europe PMC returned nine appearances for the broader sleep expression and zero for the specific REM and Portuguese expressions. All nine received recorded dispositions. The indexed 1986 animal report found no effects in its oral infusion hypnotic battery. An independent 1984 thesis found that injected oil extended barbiturate-induced sleep. The oil and administration route differ from brewed tea, and the table caption and Methods give different barbiturate doses. [1984 thesis](../../../../research/1980-1984/Food-Experiment-Lemongrass-Barbiturate-Sleep.md#injected-lemongrass-oil-and-barbiturate-sleep-in-rats) · [Literature query receipt](focused-queries.tsv) · [Exact search dispositions](focused-dispositions.tsv)

The 2024 blinded sleep trial used lemongrass flavoring in the comparison beverage. One nightmare was reported in the active mixture group and none in the flavored comparator. The 2025 postpartum report tested a lemongrass and lemon drink and reported more participants sleeping over eight hours, but describes the same counts as a between-group and temporal comparison. Both cover sleep outcomes without measuring witnessed dream enactment or REM without atonia. The registry search returned two sleep protocols and zero results for its targeted REM expression. ## Cracked black pepper and sustained attention

The broader Europe PMC title-anchored query retrieved all 290 appearances over three pages. Eight selected ranks have a recorded depth and disposition. The other 282 remain unscreened. The previously assessed human dried-pepper crossover reported inconclusive condition-by-time contrasts. A newly cataloged mouse study reported a favorable spatial-maze comparison at some times after concentrated pepper extract, with unclear crossing counts and an inconsistency in the control-group reporting. The study did not measure sustained attention.
