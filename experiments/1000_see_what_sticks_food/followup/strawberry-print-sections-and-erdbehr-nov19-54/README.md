# Strawberry falls, recovered print sections, and ErdBEHR

Four recovered print collections give full-context decisions for **27 original pairs across 18 claims**. Two retain related exposures and 25 resolve unrelated terms or body scopes without the queried exposure. The related entries are an egg-containing regimen in a 1953 obesity editorial and a strawberry urticaria example in an Epitome bound with 1907 material. Neither measures falls. The 1878 member names and the 1916 examples about perception and setting are separate from food interventions. [Source boundaries](source-boundaries.tsv) · [Exact pair decisions](dispositions.tsv).

The German register led to the ErdBEHR randomized trial. Healthy older adults receiving lower and higher amounts of fresh strawberries improved by about two chair rises on average across groups. The reported group-by-visit test was p = 0.999, and all groups ate strawberries. The trial neither counted falls nor enrolled a Parkinson cohort. The microbiome analysis is a subset from the same intervention. The new clinical and laboratory context leaves claim 0413 **Unknown at 50**.
