## Evidence and remaining questions

The 2017 baseline study measured black tea intake and all self-reported Parkinson symptoms. Its adjusted total-score estimate leaned toward greater burden, with a confidence interval spanning zero. It did not isolate rest tremor. The small 2025 LRRK2 subgroup had a favorable association on a different composite. The randomized short caffeine study still provides the closest individual rest-tremor estimates. Brewed black tea (0904) retains Maybe True at 51 on a weak constituent link. Two checked registries collect tea use and Parkinson examination data, including a black-tea item in PROBE-PD. Neither has posted results.
