# Additional primary leads

## Species-identified pain models and clinical comparators

A July 2014 mouse study tested an essential oil obtained from dried pericarp of green *Zanthoxylum schinifolium*. Middle and high oil dose groups showed effects in acetic acid writhing, hot plate and tail-flick assays. Verapamil comparison and combination arms permit separate reading of oil-only and added-verapamil results. The model assays and extracted oil differ from toasted whole pepper and pain in Parkinson’s. [Primary paper, PMID 25114705](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4119645/)

Jeong and colleagues reported a 102-person placebo-controlled trial of two oral *Zanthoxylum piperitum* leaf extract groups for mild knee osteoarthritis in 2021. The indexed primary abstract reports pain reduction in both test groups and ability to perform daily activities improvement in one group. The Korean full report is needed to verify the placebo-adjusted estimands. In 2025, Imphat and colleagues randomized 60 people with primary knee osteoarthritis to a *Zanthoxylum rhetsa* pericarp essential oil spray or diclofenac spray. The primary abstract describes no significant difference in mean change at rest or following a 20-meter walk, with both arms serving as active treatment comparisons. These provide human research trails for pain under two distinct species, plant parts and routes. [Korean primary indexed record](https://koreantk.com/ktkp2014/thesis/thesis-view.view?ctrlNo=HHJHBQ_2021_v31n4_167&tempLang=en) · [Thai primary report, PMID 40403616](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40403616/)

## Authentication and oral endpoint leads

Bader and colleagues used a human sensory panel to compare isolated alkylamides from red *Zanthoxylum bungeanum* and green *Zanthoxylum schinifolium* fruits in 2014. The authors reported differences in perceived oral tingling and numbing and salivation for individual alkylamides. The outcomes remain oral sensation and gland response. The 2019 xerostomia study discusses this experiment, and the manufacturer document repeats the same nine-person pilot as the published trial. A separate 2026 review’s description of a 30-person controlled crossover has no independently verified primary match in this pass. [Alkylamide primary report listing](https://portal.fis.tum.de/de/publications/all-trans-configuration-in-zanthoxylum-alkylamides-swaps-the-ting/) · [Xerostomia primary report](https://doi.org/10.1002/cre2.149) · [Manufacturer dataset identification](https://www.oryza.co.jp/cms/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/2929a3d9d160155a44d5c8c8dafe9239-1.pdf)

The chronic stress papers in the dated notes use red or green pericarp preparations. A March 2018 mouse study instead used a hexane extract of authenticated *Zanthoxylum alatum* seeds from a deposited voucher. Kew records *Z. alatum* Roxb. as a synonym of *Z. armatum* var. *armatum* and distinguishes the later *Z. alatum* Wall. homonym at another species. Confirm the study's voucher and exact taxon authority if adding it as a species-level comparison. [Primary report, PMID 29569964](https://doi.org/10.1080/13880209.2017.1391298) · [Kew taxon record](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A775559-1)

An older Chinese allergy trail includes a 2009 conference abstract on allergens and a separate physician-hosted 15-person original clinical report listing oropharyngeal, gastrointestinal and circulatory manifestations after eating pepper. Before treating these as independent cohorts, check participant overlap and the original journal issue. The 2024 Z. bungeanum seed-allergen paper independently describes its own patient-group characteristics and cites earlier clinical work. [2009 abstract](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaci.2008.12.707) · [Earlier clinical report hosted by the clinician](https://www.haodf.com/neirong/wenzhang/178058.html) · [2024 primary allergen study](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jafc.4c00351)

## Overdose and abnormal movements

Yang Yuxin reported fatal poisoning after oral ingestion of concentrated *Zanthoxylum bungeanum* oil in a December 21, 2021 preprint. The accessible abstract does not enumerate motor manifestations. The page points to a separately hosted case file and explicitly reserves reuse rights. For observed movements, trace Yuan et al., *Acute toxicity of essential oil of Zanthoxylum bungeanum*, *Lishizhen Medicine and Materia Medica Research* 21 (2010) 2696–2697, DOI 10.3969/j.issn.1008-0805.2010.10.128. It is cited by a 2017 review that describes twitching in rodent overdose experiments. The toxicology search also identified Wang Zhuo, *Rescue and nursing of one case of Sichuan pepper poisoning*, *Journal of Qilu Nursing* 17 (2011) 107–108, as a human lead. The primary text was not retrieved. Involuntary movement in acute poisoning and levodopa-induced dyskinesia call for separate outcome assessment. [Preprint record](https://doi.org/10.22541/au.164010684.42286820/v1) · [Yuan citation in review, reference 104](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms18102172)
