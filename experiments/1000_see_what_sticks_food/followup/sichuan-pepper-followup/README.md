# Sichuan pepper symptom evidence follow-up

This pass reviewed ten frozen ground or toasted Sichuan pepper claims. Each has at least one dated local assessment and remains Unknown. The closest human studies measured components, medicinal preparations or different symptoms. One Japanese case reported sleep and depression outcomes in Parkinson’s during a combination treatment and rehabilitation. Studies on whole pepper preparation recorded chemistry and oral sensation.

## Endpoint distinctions

The 2019 Chinese Z. bungeanum decoction patent showed improved performance on a general pole task in MPTP mice. In the Parkinson case, walking improved following a Japanese pepper formula and rehabilitation. The two studies lack freezing counts, repeat rigidity measurements or dyskinesia ratings. A previously cataloged related Japanese species distillate measured rota-rod latency and pole descent through intranasal dosing. [Chinese patent experiment](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Sichuan-Pepper-Followup.md#chinese-patent-experiment-on-oral-pepper-decoction-and-pole-performance) · [Japanese case](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sichuan-Pepper-Followup.md#walking-insomnia-and-depression-in-a-japanese-parkinson-case) · [Mouse study](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#distilled-japanese-pepper-in-experimental-parkinson-models)

A mixed-oil capsule study reported changes in subjective difficulty conversing and swallowing saliva. A different experiment tested lip tingling after local pepper application. The studies do not report acoustic voice volume. Human pressure records include baseline-rest measurements after a formula and severe hypotension during an allergic reaction after eating pepper. No standing pressure or Parkinson-related postural symptom was measured. [Oil capsule](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Sichuan-Pepper-Followup.md#pepper-oil-capsule-and-dry-mouth-outcomes) · [Resting pressure trial](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Sichuan-Pepper-Followup.md#resting-blood-pressure-after-daikenchuto) · [Allergy cases](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Sichuan-Pepper-Followup.md#immediate-reactions-after-eating-sichuan-pepper)

The 2019 extract RCT in healthy adults had a favorable acute attention speed result, worse performance on a separate name-to-face memory task, and lower mental fatigue at day 56 after a baseline imbalance. Related gamer trials register attention, fatigue and sleep outcomes without posted results. In the chronic stress mouse studies, some immobility outcomes improved after extract or oil, while the lower oil dose did not improve all measures. The 2024 Parkinson insomnia and depression observations were made during multiple concurrent treatments. The human formula pain results depend on the clinical context and analysis. An uncontrolled constipation series reported within-group abdominal pain improvement, a placebo-controlled IBS trial found no significant abdominal pain difference, and a chronic constipation trial recorded within-group secondary improvement. A topical isolated compound changed mechanical pain behavior in a mouse model, with an inconclusive heat result.
