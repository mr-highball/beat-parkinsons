# Date, fig and orange exposures in archived unclassified articles

This review reconciled the final 8,502-row [archived text screen](claim-local-text-screen.tsv) with the original selection. It includes `unclassified abstract` pairs with an exposure or related-exposure flag and an outcome flag in the archived text where the indexed title and abstract lack either side. The frozen category is food and the frozen ingredient is date, fig or orange. All 199 eligible pairs were orange across 195 articles. There were 139 pairs for raw orange and falls (`0259`) and 60 for raw orange and constipation (`0878`). Date and fig contributed zero rows because the local matching rules use fruit-specific terms. The additional 400 date records did not add qualifying pairs. The [date metadata review](date-metadata-screen.md) and [targeted fruit research](priority-food/README.md) document the separate date and fig evidence and the broader orange search.

The native Pascal matcher extracted orange-related lines for 196 pairs. A complete archived-text search located the remaining three matches in PMC9528901, PMC10937726 and PMC12303304. Each of those was a figure label, with orange marking a fibril incubation time, the Harvard Biomarkers Study cohort, or a visit in a foot-reflexology study, respectively. The review checked the methods and outcomes in the eight pairs with citrus, orange flavor or orange scent. The 199 selected pairs had no Agent Orange match in the reviewed exposure lines.
