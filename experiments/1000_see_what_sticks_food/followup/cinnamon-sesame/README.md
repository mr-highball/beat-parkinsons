# Cinnamon and sesame oil evidence follow-up

This pass focused on 16 claims that lacked a study assessment. Europe PMC reconciled four Parkinson-focused queries with 130 appearances and five symptom queries with 71 appearances. PubMed returned 77 distinct PMIDs across four completed expressions. Searches used botanical names, culinary names and selected constituents. Registry and citation searches supplied follow-up for ingredient names absent from some titles. [Europe PMC disease queries](epmc-disease-queries.tsv) · [Symptom results](epmc-symptom-results.tsv) · [PubMed query translation](pubmed-disease-queries.tsv)

ClinicalTrials.gov retrieved 167 cinnamon-expression registrations, 111 sesame-expression registrations and two joint Parkinson results. The primary API documents show that NCT00766948 matches the given name Cinnamon Bloss and that NCT02818777 names sesame oil as the CBD vehicle. The known randomized cannabinoid trial NCT03582137 did not appear in that joint query. The original publication confirms the oil in both arms, and the separately retrieved posted results give four and three falls. The cinnamon-patch registration was recovered by investigator and outcome search and verified against the article. [Joint registry results](ctg-parkinson-inventory.tsv) · [Parkinson vehicle results](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Sesame.md#sesame-oil-carrier-and-symptom-outcomes-in-a-parkinson-trial) · [Patch registration](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Sesame.md#registration-of-a-patch-trial-for-overactive-bladder)

The citation pass reconciled 34 endpoints and 1,190 reference or citing appearances. The automatic routing sent none to a further stage, so the manual review used author, formula and endpoint searches as well. The original patch table gives an urgency-frequency result separate from ordinary daytime voiding frequency. The oral cassia mouse assay counts voids after bladder injury. A separate rat study found different sensory responses depending on whether the bladder was inflamed. The original IRCT record identifies the sesame versus sunflower arms and liver outcomes. The 2022 companion report establishes refined, odorless oils for the trial family. The 2023 depression report does not provide a comparison of processing methods. The earlier first-pass note for the sunflower comparator summarized the mood abstract before the processing method was verified. Exact clinical comparisons remain open for the specified preparations. For walking slowness, models measured general activity, pole descent and rod retention. For handwriting and acoustic voice volume, no qualifying cinnamon exposure was identified.
