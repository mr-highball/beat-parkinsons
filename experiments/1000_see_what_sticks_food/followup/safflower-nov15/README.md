## Candidate detail reviewed after the dated files were prepared

- NCT07800702 first posted on 2 September 2026 and lists oral NOW-brand safflower oil as a three-gram daily comparator in professional female voice users, with phonation pressure, voice aerodynamics and self-ratings. The registry says not yet recruiting and has no results. Check the oil's product and processing, product identity over time and whether the future paper includes intelligibility or validated functional voice measures. [Official record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07800702)
- NCT02453061 first posted in 2015. The protocol used oral safflower oil against triheptanoin during the six-month blinded phase in early Huntington disease. Registered measures include the Huntington motor and psychiatric scales. The original report found no between-arm difference in total motor score through six months. The European results landing page gave no matches to the strings apathy, fall, or dysphagia. Screen the downloadable report and registered scale components for item-level data. [US registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02453061) · [EU results](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/trial/2014-005112-42/results) · [Mochel et al.](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000210194)
- The published alternating hemiplegia of childhood trial used whole oral safflower oil as a matched comparator. Its attack outcomes and adverse-event tables warrant independent screening for endpoint relevance before assessing any claim. [Primary crossover report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5625655/)
- NCT02960217 posted systematic adverse events. The result used the label gait disturbance for one of 42 blinded safflower participants and zero of 43 blinded active participants. Open-label active extension reported two of 33. Note the nonspecific MedDRA event label, additional arm and the absence of timed walking slowness. Reconcile with the previous Glut1 note and keep the resulting claims separate. [Official results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02960217?tab=results)
