# Original fig and apple papers with follow-up on oil carriers

The raw-fig review reached the next 50 bodies in the frozen archive order, ranks 95 through 144. The archived texts matched their exact source identities. Several studies measured resting tremor directly in people with Parkinson’s using medication, deep-brain stimulation, ultrasound or a walking task. These designs provide no fig comparison. The eating example in one study describes a kinetic task, and the KORA-Age study measures action tremor. [Ranked scope](fig-archive-scope.tsv) · [Full-text decisions](dispositions.tsv)

The raw-apple review authenticated 29 of 30 original reports in the next priority-five and priority-six scope. A prospective cohort measured apple consumption against later Parkinson incidence. A primate study used apple as a reward cue to assess motivation and memory. Neither measured symptom severity following a raw-apple intervention. One Science paper remained held when the publisher HTML and PDF routes returned 403. [Apple scope](apple-original-scope.tsv) · [Access hold](original-access-holds.tsv)

The oral-oil review checked whether an oil carrier served as an independently measured intervention. The 2014 cannabidiol trial reports a mean baseline-to-final UPDRS III change of 2.17 points (SD 8.23) in the corn-oil placebo and no separately reported resting-tremor result. Its three groups all used the same carrier. A separate small open-label study reports total motor scores during cannabidiol in corn oil. Its Methods mention an identical placebo, while the patient results provide no placebo phase. The Tabriz soybean-oil comparator remains limited to the registry and author abstract. [2014 corn-oil trial](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Corn-Oil-Followup.md) · [2009 open-label original](https://spdbcfmusp.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/j-psychopharmacol-2009-zuardi-979-83.pdf) · [Tabriz trial and oil-grade gap](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Clinical-Trails.md)

During this review the 2024 cannabidiol/THC trial provided a specific check on how the scale labels appear in source tables. The two formulations share sesame oil. The clinic MDS-UPDRS rest-tremor item sum 3.17+3.18 had a drug-minus-placebo difference of −1.06 points (95% CI −2.14 to 0.02, p=.055), while the separate sensor-derived rest-tremor contrast was inconclusive. The comparison cannot isolate the effect of sesame oil or the three oils in the current review. [Trial's original Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11102313/#T2)
