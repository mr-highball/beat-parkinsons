# Original source and access routes

The Kaur and Gupta review’s publisher returned 403 for the body. The indexed abstract and publisher snippets list saffron among several herbs and Parkinson symptoms in broad context. DOI and institutional searches identified no authenticated open copy. The Bej Wiley full route resolves to an abstract plus references. The Abdian publisher route returned 403 and the author page gives abstracts and citing literature. The Alzheimer natural-products review offers a paid body. The Gamus publisher’s Hebrew and English abstracts place cranberry with urinary infection and acupuncture with depression. The Hebrew full PDF requires a subscription or purchase. Three earlier depression-claim reviews were upgraded from access-only to verified abstract depth. There is no retrieved source-specific ground saffron or dried cranberry comparison from these five review bodies.
