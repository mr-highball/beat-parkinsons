# Blimbo registry screen — masked twenty-first batch

## Coverage and method

Screened all 14 distinct candidate registrations returned by the four saved ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 searches in `../registry/1` through `../registry/4`. The search expressions and result inventories are preserved at the batch root under `registry-expression-1.txt` … `registry-expression-4.txt` and in the numbered query receipts. The scope map is `registry-scope.tsv` (24 claims). For each registration, reviewed the structured registration details: official identifier, first/last posted dates, recruitment status, whether ClinicalTrials.gov has structured results, condition, intervention and arm descriptions, and primary/secondary/other outcome measures and descriptions. The four query batches returned 4, 1, 6 and 3 distinct records respectively (14 total); all 14 inventories were examined. API query timestamps are 2026-11-16 05:34:02–05:34:06 UTC. No additional registry page was needed for these returned records.

The four-column screen is `registry-screen.tsv`. `context_only` marks a record with a related ingredient or outcome term that does not establish the mapped ingredient–endpoint pair at the frozen food/preparation and endpoint level. `no_exposure_in_screened_fields` marks a lexical retrieval with no in-scope assigned exposure. The screen contains five context-only records and nine without an in-scope exposure. No registration supplies a direct match to the frozen food preparation plus named endpoint; NCT07221123 is the closest cognition lead because it pairs clove odor training with Trail Making Test B, but that remains an indirect bridge. The single record with posted structured registry results is NCT01961349. The registered alertness/sedation outcome concerns procedural sedation and satisfaction. The drug code EES0000645/A is unrelated to experimental claim 0645. No assigned refined culinary soybean oil or Parkinson daytime sleepiness comparison was established.

## Pertinent companion reports

- **NCT04710394 (VOLT):** ClinicalTrials.gov first posted 2021-01-14, completed, no structured registry results. The credited randomized report is Lee et al., “Efficacy of Combined Visual-Olfactory Training With Patient-Preferred Scents as Treatment for Patients With COVID-19 Resultant Olfactory Loss,” *JAMA Otolaryngology–Head & Neck Surgery* (2023), [PMID 36649132](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36649132/) / [PMC9857399](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9857399/). It reports UPSIT smell-function results and confirms clove as one odor in conventional and patient-preferred training arms. It is useful adjacent clove/sensory evidence, but has no Parkinson population and does not assess the mapped clove executive-function endpoint. Thus trial-result evidence exists in a paper; the registry’s `has_results=false` remains accurate.
- **NCT05829382 (DailyColors):** ClinicalTrials.gov first posted 2023-04-25, completed, no structured registry results. The credited report is Torkildsen et al., *Antioxidants* 2024;13(8):995, [PMID 39199240](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39199240/) / [PMC11352085](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11352085/). It reports a short cross-over trial of the multi-ingredient DailyColors blend. Registered and reported outcomes focus on blood biomarkers and epigenetic measures, not the mapped voice, olfaction, tremor or stride outcomes; the specific food contributions cannot be isolated. The batch record’s ingredient terms are formulation-level component matches, not individual assigned-food arms.

Other records were completed/recruiting protocols without structured results or had records without a plausible in-scope exposure/outcome pair. This absence is not evidence of a null food effect. No study is newly added to the canonical evidence catalog by this screening packet; these are lead-screening and source-identity notes for Dexter’s review.

## Screen limitations and resume route

The pass assessed all fields in the downloaded structured registrations and checked the two directly useful companion papers above. It did not perform a systematic paper search for every unrelated/false-positive registry candidate. Registry posting date/status/result flags are recorded in the inventories; the claim-specific outcome assessment and companion-report linkage are summarized in the four-field TSV. Follow-up can resume from a candidate’s official page using `https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT########` and from the paired article locators above. Registry status reflects the record snapshot retrieved on 2026-11-16 and can change later.

Additional scent/executive-function context: NCT07221123 explicitly assigns a conventional odor kit including cloves and lists Trail Making Test B under cognition. This makes it a context-only exposure/outcome lead for claim 0648, with clear route/form and disease-population limits; it is not an unexposed lexical false positive.
