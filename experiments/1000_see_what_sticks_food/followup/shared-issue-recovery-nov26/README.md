# Shared original publications and exact source checks

This November 26 Round 1 review grouped outstanding checks by publication identity, queued membership and priority. It began with the rubric and batched exact-ID Oracle lookups, then inspected accessible original bodies, tables and numbered proceedings members. Each disposition names the frozen claim, exposure or exact mismatch, relevant outcome scope, and dated local text. Index abstracts and registry records located originals. A retrieved response was counted as an original only after checking its source identity and scope.

The review resolved 121 claim–source checks across 19 originals. Sixty-six are related contexts and 55 are scope exclusions. The 53 affected scorecards retained their recorded verdicts and score anchors. Twenty dated catalog texts were added, including three linked papers or corrections, and two existing texts were extended. The [source attempts](source-attempts.tsv) record additional archive requests whose authenticated bodies or exact proceedings members remain open.
