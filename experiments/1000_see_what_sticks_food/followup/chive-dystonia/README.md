# Fresh chives and Parkinson dystonia

Claim 0047 asks whether consuming fresh chives reduces dystonia severity in people with Parkinson’s disease. The focused follow-up retrieved no study with a verified fresh chive exposure and a separately measured Parkinson dystonia outcome. The evidence class remains Unknown. The background evidence below supplies no directional assessment for that exposure and outcome.

## Exposure and outcome checks

The searches distinguished common chives (*Allium schoenoprasum*), garlic or Chinese chives (*Allium tuberosum*), bulb onion (*Allium cepa*) and garlic (*Allium sativum*). The NLM Chive descriptor maps both chive species, so it helped retrieve records and cannot confirm species. The closest whole-food source listed chives on a Parkinson study menu but gave no species, preparation, individual exposure or distinct dystonia measurement. Common-chive chemistry and the earlier outcome search remain documented locally. [NLM descriptor](https://meshb.nlm.nih.gov/record/ui?name=Allium+schoenoprasum) · [Previous common-chive research note](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chive-Outcomes.md) · [Parkinson study menu and assessment](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md#2020--chives-on-a-multi-food-parkinson-study-menu)

Dystonia includes distinct patterned muscle contractions or postures. Blepharospasm is focal cranial dystonia and was assessed alongside separately identified eyelid-opening apraxia. Levodopa-related dystonia can occur in OFF, biphasic or peak-dose states. Generic dyskinesia, rigidity, limb grip, imposed bar posture and smooth-muscle spasms each measure different phenomena. MDS-UPDRS item 4.6 rates the fraction of OFF time with painful dystonia. The Burke–Fahn–Marsden exam and rating can document clinical distribution and severity. ## Primary evidence reviewed

- The 1977 Parkinson report gives contrasting individual meal observations for two levodopa phases. The 1991 meal experiment included dystonia on a motor-state continuum and reported dyskinesia or restlessness without separate dystonia results. In 2012 a neurologist recorded apparent dystonia resolution after a multi-food intervention in one patient, with the exact added foods unavailable in the public abstract. [1977 report](../../../../research/1975-1979/Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md) · [1991 study](../../../../research/1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md) · [2012 report](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md)
- Reports in 1997 and 1999 describe the same Japanese family's retrospective garlic response in familial paroxysmal dystonic choreoathetosis. The garlic preparation and dose were unspecified. There was a caffeine challenge and no controlled garlic challenge. [Family reports](../../../../research/1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md)
- Chinese chive material was tested as a seed fraction acting on isolated smooth muscle, as fresh and steamed extracts in liver and kidney cells, and as compounds isolated from dried shoots exposed to cultured muscle cells. A later paper tested purified vesicles from material sold as fresh Chinese leek in muscle-wasting mice. [Seed and cooking studies](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md) · [Dried shoots](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md#2021--dried-chinese-chive-shoot-fractions-and-muscle-cell-proliferation) · [Chinese leek vesicles](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Chive-Dystonia.md#2025--vesicles-isolated-from-fresh-chinese-leek-in-a-muscle-wasting-model)
- Onion studies measured isolated ileal spasm, water-maze performance, or rodent motor behavior after administration of extracts. One onion-peel study's prose and table disagree on the direction of the catalepsy result. A separate onion–garlic–wild-rice blend improved rotating-rod performance at the highest dose, and neither bar nor pole outcome had a significant comparison against MPTP alone. A conference abstract reports motor coordination without numerical group results or a dystonia assessment.
