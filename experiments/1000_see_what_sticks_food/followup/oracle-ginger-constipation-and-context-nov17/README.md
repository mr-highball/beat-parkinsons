# Ginger constipation outcomes and exact archive context

The 2018 Iranian randomized trial reports a lower follow-up constipation-domain score with ginger capsules in adults with IBS. Table 4 gives 1.09 versus 1.80 after treatment, p = .016 between groups. The ginger arm started with a higher mean, and the paper gives no baseline-adjusted constipation change or separate stool count. A distinct 2019 IBS-C trial also favored dry ginger on a constipation scale. This result slightly strengthens the lean against the frozen worsening claim 0581, which remains **Maybe False at 52**, previously 51. Grated ginger and Parkinson constipation remain untested. [2018 trial and registry](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Ginger-Constipation-Comparators.md#ginger-capsule-trial-and-constipation-domain-in-adults-with-ibs)

The same lead author published a three-arm IBS trial in 2025. Its accessible abstract reports a favorable total gastrointestinal comparison with ginger. A constipation-domain result remains open. The public records establish different enrollment years, sponsors, registration IDs and designs. They leave individual participant overlap unverified. The 2025 paper calls registration prospective and the WHO display lists registration after enrollment started. The target field says 66 and the narrative says 96.
