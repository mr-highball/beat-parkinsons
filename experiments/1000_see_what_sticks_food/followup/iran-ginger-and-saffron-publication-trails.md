# Ginger and saffron Parkinson’s publication trails

Follow-up on November 12, 2026 covered the Iranian standardized ginger trial, human Parkinson’s studies with verified ginger exposure, and additional controlled saffron results. Three primary reports newly document outcomes after multi-ingredient ginger exposures. The ginger registry still shows recruitment complete without posted results. The saffron searches found no additional controlled Parkinson’s report of fatigue, depression, or the individual symptoms checked below.

## Iranian standardized ginger trial

The English and Persian IRCT records returned HTTP 200 and agree on registration and latest update on April 22, 2026 (1405/02/02). The record lists recruitment complete, anticipated recruitment April 21–July 22, and empty actual recruitment-date fields. Forty-eight is the target enrollment. The primary outcome is total MDS-UPDRS. The registration gives no separate dyskinesia or stride-length endpoint and posts no results. It describes making the datasets available six months after the primary results publication. [English IRCT](https://irct.ir/trial/87235) · [Persian IRCT](https://fa.irct.ir/trial/87235) · [Dated registration note](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Iran-Registry-Followup.md#standardized-ginger-powder-in-parkinson-disease)

Searches used `IRCT20251028067794N1`, `IR.MUI.PHANUT.REC.1405.014`, both registry titles, `مازیار قاسمی` and `رضا امانی` with `زنجبیل`, `پارکینسون` and Isfahan University of Medical Sciences, and the equivalent Latin-script searches. The Persian title begins `بررسی اثر مکمل‌یاری زنجبیل بر روی شاخص های آنتی اکسیدانی و اکسیدانی، پیامد بالینی و کیفیت زندگی در بیماران مبتلا به پارکینسون`. Search routes included SID, Magiran, IranDoc’s Ganj, university and conference listings, and the Isfahan Parkinson Charity Association, where a 2023 report identifies Amani’s affiliation without describing this study. Both direct Iranian ethics certificate routes and the WHO ICTRP trial page timed out. [Official API record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT07882108) · [Dated study note](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md#ginger-pd-supplement-trial)

## Controlled saffron replication and item-level outcomes

Europe PMC returned 24 records for `(TITLE_ABS:saffron OR TITLE_ABS:"Crocus sativus") AND TITLE_ABS:parkinson* AND (TITLE_ABS:random* OR TITLE_ABS:clinical OR TITLE_ABS:trial)`. All 24 were checked. The primary Isfahan trial, its protocol and the isolated-crocin trial were among them. The known Tehran trial was found separately through its publisher. Searches used both Iranian trial IDs, titles, investigator and institution names in English and Persian, conference and thesis routes, and outcome terms including PDSS, insomnia, AMT, executive function, rigidity and the individual MDS-UPDRS symptoms. [Prior fatigue and depression audit](fatigue-and-depression-replication.md)

- **Isfahan, IRCT20121216011763N61.** The protocol defines the 15-item PDSS as a total score and AMT as a 10-item global cognitive screen. The public results give no estimate for the insomnia items or an executive-function domain. The RSC canonical article path and PDF requests returned HTTP 403 in the direct recheck. The guessed conventional first supplement path returned 404. Europe PMC lists no PMCID or supplementary file. OpenAlex identified no open repository copy and Crossref listed no related object. The previously reported lower depression and fatigue scores remain the available directions for `0063` and `0485`. [Primary report, PMID 41439298](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41439298/) · [Protocol, Outcomes](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/15/10/e093167) · [Publisher article](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5FO01924A)
- **Tehran, IRCT20090117001556N145.** The independent capsule study reported a favorable HAM-D comparison and inconclusive GDS and MDS-UPDRS depressed-mood comparisons. The public publisher preview gives no fatigue estimate or separate motor item estimates. OpenAlex and Crossref disclosed no repository full text or linked results object. [Publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2210803324001258) · [Dated evidence](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#saffron-capsules-and-depressive-symptoms)
- **Pakistan, NCT05696665.** ClinicalTrials.gov continues to return `UNKNOWN`, last known recruiting and last updated January 25, 2023, with `hasResults=false`. Exact investigator, study title, acronym and HEC grant `9447` searches found the known Parkinson’s letter and a same-grant saffron–chamomile trial in a depression cohort with a different trial ID, NCT04935671. Neither supplies outcomes for the registered Parkinson’s cohort. [Official study API](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05696665) · [Aga Khan University publication record](https://scholars.aku.edu/en/publications/role-of-saffron-and-chamomile-in-parkinson-diseases/) · [Independent depression-cohort trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9547202/)

The targeted searches recovered no additional replication for `0063` or `0485` and no directional item-level evidence for `0218` (insomnia), `0469` (executive function), `0417` (rigidity), `0947` (facial expressiveness), `0252` (voice), `0857` (drooling), `0984` (falls) or `0730` (olfaction). Full outcome tables for the two controlled Iranian trials remain the immediate access gap.
