# Original publication boundaries

The authenticated article scope includes the matched JATS main body, available captions, in-article appendices and structured tables. Separate supplementary files keep their own review state. Ingredient aliases were checked independently against the article text. Common collisions were dates on a calendar or in a surname, abbreviated figure citations, colour and staining references, and the name Agent Orange.

The first stage manually checked ambiguous citrus, fig and date passages. The second stage inspected each of 30 additional exceptional main texts. Twenty-seven have a complete article body for the assigned ingredient-scope check. The three retained originals are two conference collections and a curriculum compilation with unresolved member or appendix parity. Chinese jujube was distinguished from date-palm fruit by species.

For the 210 earlier held scopes, authenticated complete articles allowed 46 exclusions. Official PMC EFetch supplied one of those full texts and returned front matter only for 163 other previously held identities. The remaining earlier collection has unresolved member parity.
