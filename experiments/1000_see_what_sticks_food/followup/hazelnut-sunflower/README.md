## What the original evidence showed

- The 2017 Parkinson cohort poster contains a sunflower-oil row that is absent from the journal article. Its estimate is +2 total PRO-PD points for each step in reported oil-use frequency (95% CI −10 to 13, p = .787). The table does not give symptom-level effects, oil grade or a causal comparison. The preliminary 2016 presentation and later same-project reports belong to the same study family. [Dated note and original poster](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#sunflower-oil-frequency-and-overall-parkinson-symptoms)
- Eight of 24 people reported easier swallowing after rinsing with cold-pressed sunflower oil versus two after water in the medication-related dry mouth crossover. The comparison was unadjusted, participants spat out the oil, and the report gives no instrumental, Parkinson-specific or vocal-loudness result. Infant cases identify a possible consequence of aspirating oil during repeated oral administration. The two findings reflect distinct routes, outcomes and populations. [Crossover](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#cold-pressed-sunflower-oil-rinse-and-subjective-swallowing) · [Infant cases](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#sunflower-oil-and-infant-aspiration-reports)
- Hazelnut oil was an actual supplemental fat in controlled feeding, including a 2013 trial and an older-adult working-memory pilot in 2023. Their selected outcomes include fatigue-inertia, mood or working memory. Daytime sleepiness and the other frozen hazelnut endpoints were not reported. The 2013 erratum corrected a physical-activity result's wording and left the mood table unchanged. A 2024 chocolate comparison measured salivary secretion and found no clear difference when hazelnut oil replaced some cocoa butter. It did not measure clinical drooling. [2013 feeding crossover](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md) · [2013 erratum](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6272819/) · [2023 pilot](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#hazelnut-oil-in-an-older-adult-working-memory-trial) · [2024 chocolate experiment](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#hazelnut-oil-chocolate-and-measured-salivary-secretion)
- The animal vehicle work includes a rare sunflower versus saline comparison with four rats in each healthy subgroup. Neither catalepsy measure nor akinesia differed clearly between them. In a 2026 model the rotenone injury was compared with injected sunflower oil, so the contrast estimates injury under that design. The Ginger PD registry lists sunflower seed oil in both doses of its ginger capsule and plans composite measures. Bench dysphagia designs report texture measures.
