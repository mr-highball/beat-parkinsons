# Source follow-up after the initial oil pass

Reviewed November 13, 2026. The ten study families below are now in dated research notes, with 17 related assessments across seven claims. Official registrations were filed under their own first dates. The [earliest registration](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md), [2010–2014 records](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md), [2015–2019 records](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md), [2020–2024 results](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md) and [2025–2029 results](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md) link the original sources.

| 2010-06-15 | Lespérance, ISRCTN47431149, 0033 | Of 432 adults with a major depressive episode, 214 received sunflower oil containing 2% fish oil. The baseline IDS-SR30 score was 43.28 and the adjusted mean during the eight-week trial was 32.26. The overall difference against the active fish-oil group was 1.32 points (95% CI −0.20 to 2.84, p = .088). The MADRS contrast was also inconclusive (p = .053). The design gives no pure sunflower-oil contrast, no grade and no Parkinson cohort. [Original paper and Table 2](https://www.psychiatrist.com/PublishingImages/v72n0805.pdf) · [First online date](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20584525/) |
| 2016-08-10 | Jenkins, PMID27521997, 0364 and 0772. Hand strength is also contextual for 0194. | Sixty-five older adults received either high-oleic sunflower oil (35 people, 6 g/day) or rumenic-acid rich CLA for eight weeks. Trail Making B in the sunflower group was 93.8 to 78.4 seconds in men and 82.5 to 74.3 seconds in women. There was a visit improvement across groups and no treatment difference in that task, handgrip strength or local handgrip fatigability. Exact processing remains unclear and the study did not measure hand dexterity, clinical fatigue or a Parkinson group. The sponsor’s patent reproduces the cognition results for the same cohort. [Journal abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27521997/) · [Sponsor patent, Table 2](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3474839B1/en) |
| 2017 thesis, 2019 article | ACTRN12614000130662, 0033 and 0145 | Two drops of unscented sunflower oil on lapel wadding served as the placebo. The thesis reports NPI anxiety changes of −2.88 with placebo versus −0.57 with the essential oils pooled (p = .09), and depression changes of −1.0 and −2.8 (p = .15). A Parkinson diagnosis was excluded from the per-protocol set as a protocol violation. Ingestion and processing grade were absent and no Parkinson subgroup was analyzed. [Official registry](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=365702) · [Thesis results, pp. 163–167](https://researchers-admin.westernsydney.edu.au/ws/portalfiles/portal/94874239/uws_44603.pdf) · [Published trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30670268/) |
| 2019-04-23 first results, 2020-10-26 aggregate | BENEFIC NCT02551419, 0772 | The 2019 Methods specify refined, bleached, winterized and deodorized high-oleic sunflower oil in a beverage. The 2020 aggregate report used the same formulation. In Table 2, sunflower arm switching times were 130 to 131 seconds, and total Trail Making errors 1.7 to 2.5. The MCT group’s errors were 2.7 to 1.8, with adjusted p = .017 between arms. There was no clear switching-time contrast (p = .664). Keep the imaging phase, aggregate report and white-matter subsample under one study ID. No cold-pressed oil, Parkinson cohort or oil-free control. [2019 Methods](https://alz-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1016/j.jalz.2018.12.017) · [2020 Table 2](https://alz-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/alz.12206) · [Imaging subsample](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8596139/) |
| 2020-11-10 | Zarei et al., PMID33170419, 0772 | Wistar rats ate native or repeatedly heated sunflower or canola oils, or heated oils with ginger or turmeric, for 90 days. The original abstract reports poorer memory and motor coordination in each heated oil group relative to its respective native control (p < .05). Native grade and exact test-specific values require the full paper. There is no Parkinson model or isolated cold-pressed arm. [Original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33170419/) |
| 2021-08-03 | Foshati et al., PMID34358723, 0033 | Seventy-three adults with major depression were randomized to consume 25 mL/day of sunflower or extra-virgin olive oil for 52 days. The original abstract reports no clear within-arm BDI-II change, with between-arm p = .021. HAMD-7 decreased more with olive oil (p = .001). Sunflower refining grade, exact baseline and final scores and registration linkage remain unresolved. [Original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34358723/) |
| 2022-03-25 | Mengelberg et al., ANZCTR 366439, 0033, 0145 and 0772 | Twelve-month MCI trial, 72 randomized and 60 analyzed. Registry identifies sunflower oil, and the paper reports 1857 mg/day linoleic acid in the comparator, with orange oil and vitamin E present in both arms. Table 2 gives sunflower-comparator Trail Making B scores of 49.8 to 56.8, with no treatment difference (p = .91). Overall GDS and GAI treatment p values were .95 and .64. APOE ε4 interactions favored DHA for depression and anxiety (p = .04 and .02). Grade was unreported and the paper is CC BY-NC-ND. [Official registry](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=366439) · [Original Methods and Table 2](https://mro.massey.ac.nz/server/api/core/bitstreams/1cfccc85-5836-44c6-9e62-bb0946fa1bf5/content) |
| 2024-09 | Mulkijanyan et al., GIBS 2024 poster abstract PP23, 0401 and 0579 | In a mouse liver-injury assay, oral cold-pressed hazelnut oil plus CCl₄ yielded pentobarbital-induced sleeping times of 8.8 and 10.6 minutes on days 3 and 10, versus 14.9 and 21.5 with CCl₄ alone and 9.6 in the intact group. Methods and Table 1 give different oral oil volumes (0.2 and 0.1 mL). The endpoint reflects drug-induced sleep duration in a hepatic injury model and cannot establish insomnia or daytime sleepiness after toasted oil in Parkinson’s. No linked full journal article was found through the exact title or author. [Original symposium proceedings, pp. 71–72](https://ikip.tsmu.edu/files/ABSTRACT_BOOKGIBS_2024Final_Version1_1727420154.pdf#page=71) |
| 2024-12-20 | Keremane et al., PMID39704875, 0145 and 0772 | In a distinct intergenerational rat study, native sunflower and canola arms were controls for their heated counterparts. The original abstract reports more anxiety-like behavior and reduced memory and motor coordination on the heated diets. The exact individual arm values, processing grade and lineage details beyond the abstract remain open. The study did not use a Parkinson model. [Original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39704875/) |
| 2026-06-15 | Lopresti and Smith, ACTRN12625000047493, 0772 | Eighty-two healthy teenagers received sunflower-oil placebo or lutein plus zeaxanthin for six months. Objective attention and processing speed favored the carotenoids (p = .026 and .034). Visual reasoning, nonverbal memory and self-reported attention and sleep showed no clear between-arm difference. Extraction grade and arm-specific scores remain open. [Original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42413424/) · [University record](https://researchportal.murdoch.edu.au/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Supplementation-with-lutein-and-zeaxanthin-increases/991005904178407891) |

The DICe registration, NCT03727386, resolves to a 2022 [Bournemouth thesis](https://eprints.bournemouth.ac.uk/37440/). The registry still has no posted results. It is linked as the same study family in the checkpoint rather than counted as a new cohort. The thesis gives the KTC product and actual analyzed results, and the [2018 manufacturer specification](https://www.tfsltd.co.uk/media/filer_public/f5/d0/f5d0b0d4-a0c8-4072-872c-6503e8b4dd2d/sunflower_oil_-_june_2018.pdf) supplies evidence about refining.

## Checked exclusions

- US20150209402A1 lists both oils among possible formulations, but only canola, coconut and olive oils were administered in the reported swallowing-related examples. [Original patent, Examples 1–3](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20150209402A1/en)
- Tang’s 2025 scopolamine mouse experiment included sunflower oil at 40 g/kg in every diet. The contrasts tested vitamins and sunflower lecithin at the same oil exposure. [Primary report, Table 1](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/17/3/553)
- Kheirouri’s Parkinson case–control paper is already cataloged under canola and safflower. Its public abstract groups nonhydrogenated plant oils. Publisher full text and exact FFQ item values remain unavailable from the checked sources. [Local note](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Canola-Safflower.md#cooking-oil-exposures-in-the-tabriz-approved-parkinson-reports) · [Original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37997257/)
- The 2024 Parkinson ketogenic study mentions sunflower in its background, citing a different ketogenesis study. Its own treatment comparison does not provide a sunflower intervention result. [Primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10983636/)
- In healthy volunteers, DRKS00011922 observed greater saliva production with sunflower oil rinsing than with saline. It was a three-participant fixed-sequence oral microbiome pilot and did not measure swallowing, voice or Parkinson’s. [Official registry](https://drks.de/search/de/trial/DRKS00011922) · [Primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8060239/)

## Registry and text follow-up

- The official Shiraz protocol gives May 14, 2016 and identifies its retrospective registration, sample, dose and duration. The depression scales are absent from its outcome list. Both 2021 papers identify a thesis by the same investigator and describe matching interventions, with distinct grant numbers (94-01-87-10037 for the metabolic paper and 94-01-84-9923 for the mood paper). The accessible primary abstract and publisher preview give no sunflower arm-specific depression scores or processing grade. The complete mood article, supplemental Table 5, letter, reply and source thesis remain open. [IRCT](https://en.irct.ir/trial/17869) · [Metabolic report](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ijcp.14254) · [Mood report](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2212267221010443) · [Letter and linked reply](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35276417/)
- ACTRN12614000874617 was registered August 14, 2014. The 2018 thesis was publicly deposited March 9, 2020, and names a 3 g/day sunflower comparator, with 1857 mg/day linoleic acid, manufacturer and independent testing laboratory. It also documents a dominant-hand Coin Rotation Task. The sunflower group’s age- and sex-standardized scores were −0.25 to −0.12 (between-arm p = .38). The thesis permits private research copies only. The linked journal paper is CC BY-NC-ND. [ANZCTR](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=366439) · [Public deposit date](https://mro.massey.ac.nz/items/2639b071-e66e-453c-9f5c-a85b535beb54/full) · [Thesis](https://mro.massey.ac.nz/server/api/core/bitstreams/75f1ac35-0219-4a6b-9048-0ae5691e99c2/content) · [Journal report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9321856/)
- BENEFIC registration first appeared September 16, 2015. The official record calls the comparator placebo, while the 2019 original identifies the refining and the 2020 report says that it used the same formulation. The 2020 report and 2021 subsample were outside the PMC plain-text license scope. [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02551419) · [First-phase methods](https://alz-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1016/j.jalz.2018.12.017) · [Aggregate study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8048678/) · [Subsample](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8596139/)
- ACTRN12625000047493 posted January 20, 2025 and linked results August 25, 2026. The registry describes the placebo as sunflower oil with ingredients matched apart from the carotenoids. Individual participant data access is subject to approval. [ANZCTR](https://anzctr.org.au/ACTRN12625000047493.aspx)
