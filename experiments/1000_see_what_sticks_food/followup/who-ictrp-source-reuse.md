# Reuse checks for three WHO ICTRP-linked reports

Checked November 12, 2026. The repository's plain-text collection uses verified CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA copies. The review checked the PDF opening and closing pages, publisher rights information and alternate versions indexed by title, author, registry ID and preprint DOI. The dated research notes preserve the reports’ findings and primary links. [Source-text catalog policy](../../../research/README.md#local-source-text)

## Kashikar and Rao, 2019

The clinical report for CTRI/2018/09/015592 is in IJAPC volume 11, issue 3, printed pp. 48–59. The cover gives no reuse terms. Both the opening and closing printed pages carry a Greentree Group Publishers copyright line. The exact 2019 issue page displays CC BY-NC 4.0. The journal-wide publication ethics page instead says CC BY 3.0, and the legacy declaration asks authors to transfer copyright to the journal. The PDF supplies no article-specific license to resolve those conflicting notices. [Journal PDF](https://www.ijapc.com/upload/MNAPC-V-11-I3-23-p-48-59.pdf) · [2019 issue](https://www.ijapc.com/layout/issues.php?i=3&v=11&y=2019) · [Publication ethics](https://www.ijapc.com/layout/publication.php) · [Copyright and declaration](https://www.ijapc.com/layout/d_agree.php)

Author and institutional searches did not yield a separately licensed copy of the clinical report. The authors' related WJPR paper, DOI 10.20959/wjpr201911-15857, studies rotenone-induced parkinsonism in rats and has a third author. [Institutional publications list](https://sdmayurvedacollegeudupi.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/2019-Publications-Acheivements.pdf) · [WJPR study](https://wjpr.net/abstract_show/13117)

## Anzar and colleagues, 2023 marigold cognition and mood report

The publisher's archived issue list identifies CTRI/2022/06/043208's cognition article in volume 12, issue 4, May 2023, pp. 1–7. The archived publisher PDF and the sponsor's PDF are byte-identical (SHA-256 `37ba96b32fd7287b37a193b9bc7d8287d6a5abbfa5f14fb07750693a571e5647`). The first and last pages, PDF metadata, issue entry and publication certificate give no reuse license. [Archived issue list](https://web.archive.org/web/20231001153818id_/https://ijmar.in/past-issue/) · [Archived publisher PDF](https://web.archive.org/web/20240424035319id_/https://ijmar.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/1-2.pdf) · [Sponsor PDF](https://www.olivelifesciences.com/_files/ugd/f1b368_a6fb64d788cb4d07bda7164c5c46da23.pdf) · [Publication certificate](https://web.archive.org/web/20240424023906id_/https://ijmar.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Certificate-Of-Publication-1-2.pdf)

The archived form page is accessible but the linked agreement returned HTTP 404 from Wayback. The sponsor download page and SlideShare mirror provide no explicit reuse license. Exact-title, author, registry and repository searches yielded no separately licensed version.
