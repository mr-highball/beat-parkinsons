# Fennel seed clinical endpoint follow-up

This November 15, 2026 UTC pass revisited claims 0214, 0351, 0383, 0488 and 0529. The frozen exposures are toasted or ground fennel fruits, commonly sold as seeds of Foeniculum vulgare. The search followed cooking chemistry, motor phenotypes, medication wearing-off, gastric emptying, visual adverse effects and evidence from neighboring preparations.

## Claim assessment

| Claim | Source closest to the outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 0214 · toasted seed worsens rest tremor | The direct roasting study measured volatile chemistry. Oral extract shortened imposed bar posture in haloperidol-treated mice, and earlier coordination and seizure reports measure different motor phenomena. |
| 0351 · ground seed reduces small handwriting | The handwriting, micrographia and dexterity searches found no indexed primary match. The mechanically ground-seed menopause trial measures a symptom composite. Existing fennel motor models measure grip, general movement or coordination. The ground-seed writing comparison remains open. |
| 0383 · toasted seed reduces dystonia | Shorter imposed posture after fennel extract and reserpine or haloperidol belongs to the catalepsy assay. Neither published protocol scores patterned dystonic contractions. |
| 0488 · toasted seed worsens daily OFF time | Menopausal and postoperative studies do not supply levodopa or OFF outcomes. The Choi and Asano models distinguish drug-delayed stomach emptying from basal conditions. Zhang measures intestinal progression and stomach emptying separately, with evidence only for slower intestinal progression in selected arms. The Annahazi group reports region-specific isolated stomach muscle responses. The Parkinson Camicinal comparison independently illustrates how a gastric intervention and daily OFF diaries can be measured together. [Parkinson comparator](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Ground-Cumin-OFF-Time.md#pharmaceutical-gastric-motility-trial-measured-parkinson-off-time-by-diary) |
| 0529 · toasted seed reduces visual hallucinations | The Slovak and Czech publications describe one product contamination incident. Regulator-confirmed contamination, disorientation, mydriasis and altered vision do not measure formed hallucination frequency. The processed fennel itself remains untested for this outcome. |

## Open trails

Find the Asano original body and examine formulation-specific contrasts and the relevant 1977 rabbit stomach and 2002 combined-formulation primary reports. The 2007 CYP3A4 microsomal result used erythromycin as substrate. Pursue primary coadministration experiments and any levodopa-specific evidence before applying the mechanism to claim 0488. Check author-associated gastric motility and postoperative fennel publications for additional direct measures, registry result postings, seed preparation and group assignment.
