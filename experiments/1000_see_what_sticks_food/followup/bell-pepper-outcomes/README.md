# Bell pepper symptom and preparation review

The Phillips trial served whole red or green capsicum in both Parkinson diet groups. The difference in daytime sleepiness was −0.45 points for ketogenic versus low-fat diet. The diets varied many ingredients. Hegelmaier listed paprika as a vegetable and published total motor scores and individual motor totals. The report gives no separate gait or hand outcomes. The 2009 nonpungent extract trial measured sleepy, alert and tired ratings. It published an overall adverse-event comparison of p=.09. The 2016 Tianjin study gives no clear sweet-pepper trend for handgrip. In the 2020 extract trial the 10 m walk and handgrip were eligibility measures. Accelerometer and vigor outcomes were measured during follow-up. The subgroup with low initial activity had a different activity response from the full sample. These studies leave the five frozen preparation-specific claims open.
