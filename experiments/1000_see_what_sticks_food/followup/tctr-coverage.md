# Thai Clinical Trials Registry follow-up

The November 12, 2026 pass tested the registry's public keyword form and read nine of nine exact official XML records from indexed candidates and primary report citations. The form's response did not supply a usable result set or count for any tested term, including the known visual-cue trial's title. The available browser runtime could not open the form. The WHO exact-trial request timed out. TCTR's March 18, 2026 announcement also says that WHO does not import its unknown-status records. The national Parkinson search and pagination remain unreconciled. [Query and access ledger](tctr-queries.tsv) · [Public search](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/public/pages/search.php) · [Official search manual](https://thaiclinicaltrials.org/public/download/manual/manual_search_en.pdf) · [TCTR announcement](https://www.thaiclinicaltrials.org/)

Four of the nine official records concern medicinal cannabis with Parkinson participants and report links. Both Buriram randomized comparisons gave sublingual olive oil in both arms. Another trial compared a medicinal cannabis oil with rice bran oil placebo, with the carrier in the active oil unspecified in the sources reviewed. The nationwide uncontrolled cohort used a cannabis and coconut oil formulation and reported four-week pain, drowsiness labeled as insomnia, and appetite estimates in a Parkinson subgroup. These comparisons leave the separate effects of culinary olive, rice bran, and coconut oil unresolved. Five exact records were controls or were excluded for condition or intervention. Exact record retrieval uses `foodexperiment.tctr OUTPUT_DIR TCTR_ID ...`. The FPC 3.3.1 i386-win32 run verified the requested ID and registration date for each export. It writes the individual provider responses and a tab-separated snapshot of HTTP status, bytes, access, and registry fields. The November 12 snapshot is under `build/tctr/native`. The exact-ID ledger preserves its status and byte counts. [Native source](../tools/foodexperiment.tctr.lpr) · [Build script](../tools/build-tctr.ps1)

The Thai *Mucuna pruriens* crossover publication and the Thai food-habits survey were citation leads. No exact TCTR identifier surfaced for either. The CheckPD public registry and screening outcome are already cataloged. PMC Cloud listed four versions across the three Thai primary articles with PMC records. PMC9894020.1 carries CC BY-NC-SA. PMC11324265.1, PMC13435194.1, and PMC13435194.2 carry CC BY-NC. The native collector confirmed that each falls outside the archive's CC0, CC BY, and CC BY-SA scope. TCTR provides public record exports and reserves rights in its site notice. The public terms describe the registry's ability to share records, and the review found no full-record redistribution grant. The dated notes provide original summaries with exact provider links.
