## Dried dates and apathy severity · 0758

The Eid crossover used whole dried Ajwa at the Tamr stage. It collected subjective mood in bowel diaries and published no mood result. The 2013 meeting abstract and later registry represent the same study and give no mood values. The 2015 fruit-drink crossover recorded better self-rated alertness in healthy adults with comparator composition uncertain. The 2019 study had one group and a slight unmarked pre-to-post drop in vigor. The 2026 matched yogurt comparison measured POMS vigor before dosing. Its later alertness and calmness observations used Bond–Lader ratings. These are different measurement constructs and preparations. ## Dried figs and visual hallucination frequency · 0175

The 1998 case narrative reports an allergic reaction following fresh edible fig and recurrent symptoms after kiwi and dried fig. The different species Ficus benjamina is the inhaled ornamental allergen context. The Iranian randomized fig-and-olive study gives no hallucination-specific outcome or isolated fig arm. Its adverse-event description includes a single severe-hiccup withdrawal. The official 2014 registration gives a different planned dose, schedule and target sample. It lists diarrhea monitoring and leaves visual hallucinations outside the registered outcome scope. [Registration comparison](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Edible-Fig-Adverse-Contexts.md#iranian-registration-for-fig-and-olive-adjunct-therapy) Publisher review citations involving Ficus religiosa leaf motor studies, Ficus carica leaf toxicology and eye irritation do not bear on hallucinations after eating dried fig. One Ficus review remains abstract-only with its publisher access gap. The initial FIG-V02/11 pending row advanced to a full-text scope exclusion in the follow-through table. The FIG-V03/2 pending row remains open. ## Refined corn oil and pain or fatigue · 0278 and 0694

Re-reading the 1995 scan showed that the 0.37-point physician-rated pain increase formerly assigned to corn oil is from the fish-oil column. The corn-oil arm’s patient-rated and physician-rated changes from diclofenac withdrawal are separate, and the paper reports no clear between-oil differences at this visit. The Table 2 and Table 3 fatigue comparisons measure hours until fatigue, with uncertain within-arm changes during rheumatoid arthritis therapy changes. They leave clinical Parkinson fatigue severity and pain direction unresolved. [Corrected pain and fatigue figures](../../../../research/1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Falls-Rigidity-and-Pain.md)
