The 2025 primary rat study gives an additional direction for an evoked thermal response. At week seven, withdrawal followed approximately 9.7 seconds with ginger water extract versus 11.5 seconds in the stressed ovariectomized control and 7.6 seconds in the unstressed sham group. Shorter latency is compatible with faster response or greater thermal sensitivity according to the article's assay interpretation. The model and extracted preparation differ from clinical pain severity and grated ginger. The paper reports sucrose and swim behavior without a dedicated anxiety measure, and its methods leave ten animals outside the enumerated groups.
