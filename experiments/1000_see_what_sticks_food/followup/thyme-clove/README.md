The dried thyme trial reports a favorable anxiety contrast in students. The clove odor item in a Parkinson cohort did not distinguish the freezing groups with statistical clarity. Oral eugenol given before and after toxin exposure produced different motor results in mice. The 2022 oral abstract and 2024 poster belong to the previously cataloged Hamdi rat study family. [2020–2024 sources](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Thyme-Clove.md)

The 2006 ground-clove gel reduced pain in a dental needle challenge. Subsequent inhalation and dental active-comparator studies gave inconclusive between-group results. Both human nasal-eugenol reports found no clear change in salivary flow. In isolated bladder tissue, thyme essential oil affected evoked tension and spontaneous variability differently.
