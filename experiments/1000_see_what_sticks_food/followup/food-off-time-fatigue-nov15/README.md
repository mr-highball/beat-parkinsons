# Banana anxiety, egg falls and other endpoint reviews, November 15, 2026

## 0309 and 0329 moved to Maybe False

**0309, raw banana worsens anxiety.** A randomized conference study reported lower Hamilton anxiety ratings in a banana-only group of adolescents compared with controls. A separate adult schizophrenia inpatient study reported lower endpoint HARS ratings in all four banana arms. It does not establish random allocation, and antipsychotic treatment history can confound the comparison. A 20-person open randomized Cavendish trial did not show a clear tension–anxiety difference. The larger postoperative report also points inverse, but the tabulated test statistic cannot be reconciled with the stated sample sizes and spread. The two first comparisons support a minimal qualitative lean against worsening, **Maybe False at 51**. Preparation and transfer to Parkinson disease remain open. The assessed banana–levodopa reports concern drug response and did not quantify anxiety. **0329, poached eggs worsen falls.** The MVP Parkinson baseline graph shows a near-zero inverse sign for egg frequency and the self-reported falls item. In an independent older-adult case–control study, fall-with-fracture cases ate a mean 2.03 egg portions per week and controls ate 2.21 (unadjusted p = .07). Both point away from the frozen worsening direction. The case–control paper gives no adjusted egg estimate, and the baseline graph gives no numeric coefficient or uncertainty. Preparation is unspecified in both. A minimal convergence judgment supports **Maybe False at 51**. A controlled poached-egg comparison with prospectively counted Parkinson falls remains open. ## Other comparisons and access

| 0163, refined corn oil and rigidity | Rechecked animal uses of corn oil as an injected toxin vehicle and clinical levodopa absorption context. Existing human oral cannabinoid and enteral comparisons lack an oil-specific rigidity estimate. |
| 0312, cracked black pepper and pain | Ground pepper added to turmeric gave an inconclusive human chronic-pain contrast. The independent oral piperine tail-flick animal contrast is positive for longer withdrawal latency. A second rat paper used pain response to verify the model before treatment. The linked 2021 source with a claimed analgesic result remains abstract-only. The recovered 2015 review's assay used a distinct synthetic compound. |
| 0364, refined sunflower oil and fatigue | Registered fatigue times are premeal and later follow-up. The high-oleic whole-meal randomized comparison publishes postmeal attention. A weak positive fatigue tile pools four oil types, and the comparison against refined sesame does not give a sunflower arm fatigue trajectory. |
| 0432, ground turmeric and freezing of gait | Small inverse sign for all spices together. Individual ground turmeric and episode frequency remain unresolved. Clinical composite and speed outcomes do not quantify freezing episodes. |
| 0564, boiled shrimp and constipation | Fish and shrimp share one item inside a southern dietary-pattern score associated with lower constipation odds. Original comparative evidence concerns a mixed shrimp gel in mice or extracted shell material. No isolated boiled-shrimp human bowel effect verified. |
| 0583, grilled salmon and bradykinesia | Small inverse baseline sign for pooled non-fried fish and slow movement. A child salmon-versus-meat RCT measured dexterity; original table is still open. A mixed-fish hip-fracture trial terminated at 14 recruits and has no posted outcomes. |
| 0634, dried oregano and anxiety | Injected extract shifted a rodent anxiety proxy, while a separate oral experiment assessed forced-swim behavior. Human inhaled marjoram trial found no clear between-arm anxiety result. The species and preparation differ. |
| 0662, dried basil and constipation | Dried sweet basil trial lists adverse events with no reported constipation severity or episode count. Related seed formulations involve different plant material. |
| 0854, fresh oregano and daily OFF | A controlled diet supplied oregano among other foods and reported medication dosage. The marjoram tea pilot used a different plant and a motor composite. Neither gives an oregano meal contrast on daily OFF time. |
| 0906, dried sage and apathy | The early Parkinson pilot gives cognitive or salivation outcomes. Related Alzheimer and healthy adult records do not establish sage-specific changes in Parkinson apathy. |
