Potentially important primary 0309 lead: [Jiang et al. PMID 40331125, DOI 10.1159/000545174](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40331125/), first published 14 March 2025, [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12052346/). The paper describes prospective random assignment of 77 breast-surgery patients per group to one added banana daily versus none, with standard nutrition, through three months after discharge. Table 2 reports self-rated anxiety scores 27.64 ± 4.27 versus 32.16 ± 4.33, t=-2.08 and p<.05, favoring banana. This is not PD; fruit cultivar and ripeness are not established here. Material reporting concern: using the reported group n, means and SD, the independent-sample t magnitude is approximately 6.5 rather than the reported 2.08. The paper also reports 58 participants with anxiety score >=50, difficult to reconcile with the group mean and SD figures. Verify raw data or an author correction before trusting the numerical result. The article was not listed by PMID in current followup/assessments.tsv at review.
