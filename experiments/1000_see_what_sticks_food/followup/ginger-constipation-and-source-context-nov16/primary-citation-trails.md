# Primary citations from the natural-compounds review

The retrieved original review is secondary evidence. Its Terpenes section and Table 4 led to the following original-study records. Identity searches found no previous standalone entry for these originals in the dated catalog, although several occur in reference lists. References shared across claims point to the same experiment. [Retrieved review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8155682/)

| Claims | Original study and observed scope | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0067, 0753, 0814 | Do Vale and colleagues measured longer pentobarbital-induced loss of righting and lower rotarod performance in mice after injected isolated myrcene. The maze data give no evidence of reduced anxiety. | Original full tables checked and archived as a factual text. [Dated original](../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Myrcene-Barbiturate-Context.md#injected-isolated-myrcene-and-pentobarbital-sleep-in-mice) |
