# Sixteenth-round masked source audit

Review date 2026-11-15; ingredient and symptom fields used before checking frozen directions. Ranked web searches below are complementary and lack provider total-hit reconciliation. An accessible retrieved source and its clinical scope are not proof of absence of other evidence. No tracked file changed.

- **0660, plain dark chocolate / low voice volume:** [PMID 22584952](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22584952/) is an investigator-blinded, single-dose, 26-person Parkinson crossover of dark and cocoa-free white chocolate. Its cataloged composite motor difference is inconclusive and the abstract does not report acoustic voice intensity or speech item. A PD vocal-intensity trial without chocolate is a different intervention. Further item-level original trial data could resolve whether voice was separately measured; no claim-specific direction here. Existing source: `research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md`.
- **0619, raw blackberry / dream enactment:** [Zhang et al. 2023, DOI 10.3390/nu15245115](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/15/24/5115), already cataloged, used NHANES two-day dietary recalls, n=29,217, including blackberry ingredients and mixed preparations. Fully adjusted sleep-difficulty odds ratio 0.63 (95% CI 0.40–0.97) is a cross-sectional association in noninstitutionalized US adults, not a raw-fruit intervention, Parkinson subgroup, REM sleep behavior or episode frequency. Sleep difficulty must not be equated to dream enactment. Existing source `research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md`.
- **0518, fresh spearmint / fatigue:** [Herrlinger et al. 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29314866/) randomized 90 adults with age-associated memory impairment to 600 or 900 mg/day aqueous leaf extract or placebo, not fresh leaves or Parkinson disease. The existing primary-results review reports no significant individual mood comparison. A separate [spearmint and green-tea blend trial](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Celery-Spearmint.md) found fatigue comparison intervals spanning zero; no isolated spearmint effect. The chewing-gum trial's fatigue finding did not replicate. No clear claim-specific direction.
- **0451, dried chervil / attention:** *Anthriscus cerefolium* should not be conflated with Sichuan lovage. The existing [Parkinson menu review](../../../followup/chervil-lovage-outcomes/README.md) finds chervil in a bundled diet without verified dried status or attention outcomes. The complementary searches did not produce a matched controlled clinical comparison; continue checking actual formulation and cognitive tests.
- **0574, ground cardamom / attention:** The already cataloged [2026 study family](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md) uses *Amomum subulatum* black cardamom extract, not ground *Elettaria cardamomum*. At day 14, extract alone improved shifting-attention correct responses vs placebo by 7.46 at 1 h (p<.001), with an 8 h significant contrast only for combined extract/caffeine. Trial family CTRI/2024/09/074472; healthy adults, task attention, no Parkinson; do not treat two publications as independent cohorts or assume green-cardamom clinical benefit. Related oral oil and mouse maze studies test other formulations and task domains. Further ordinary ground green-cardamom outcome data needed.
- **0488, toasted fennel / daily OFF time:** [Nemati et al. 2018](https://brieflands.com/journals/jjnpp/articles/67391) report reserpine-model rats treated with fennel essence, with bar-test and open-field activity improvements versus reserpine. Neither the culinary roasted seed preparation nor levodopa diary-defined OFF time was measured. Human fennel postoperative gut recovery and isolated mouse gastric effects do not establish a motor fluctuation benefit or harm. Existing source `research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Bok-Choy-Fennel.md` and `research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Fennel-Seed-Clinical-Endpoints.md`.
- **0614, cold-pressed safflower oil / muscle rigidity:** Existing [2024 Huntington trial](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Comparator-Trials.md) reports 6-month UHDRS total motor changes +0.7 (95% CI −0.4 to 2.0) safflower vs +1.3 (0.0 to 2.7) triheptanoin; difference +0.6 (−1.0 to 2.5). Rigidity is part of the composite but no separate item reported, grade unspecified, active comparator and distinct disease. 1980s ataxia trials likewise lack rigidity result. Saffron clinical trials are a different species. No justified oil-rigidity direction from total scores.
- **0492, refined rice bran oil / swallowing:** [Aoyagi 2026](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ehpm/31/0/31_25-00319/_html/-char/en), released January 17, DOI 10.1265/ehpm.25-00319, UMIN000053271, is already cataloged and assessed. Thirty-four dysphagic older adults in crossover had Boso rice oil on nasal gauze for five minutes as low-odor control versus hinoki oil; six had unspecified neurodegenerative conditions. Rice-oil session median change RSST 0 (95% CI −0.5 to 0, p=.067), modified water swallow 0 (p=.739), 30 mL water swallow 0 (p=.257). No ingestion, untreated comparator or Parkinson subgroup. [Manufacturer](https://www.boso.co.jp/riceoil/about/industry.html) describes extraction/refining of its rice-bran oil family, but does not verify the exact study lot. Neither null within-session result nor hinoki-vs-control comparison establishes the effect of consuming the oil.
