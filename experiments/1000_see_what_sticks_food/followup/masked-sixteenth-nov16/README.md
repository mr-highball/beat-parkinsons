## Findings closest to the hypotheses

- **0503, ground nigella and bradykinesia.** Three mice per group in the Kusumawati study gave a favorable open-field distance and speed signal for isolated oral thymoquinone, with inconclusive beam and hanging-wire results. The original Rathod and Aswar figure gives no significant catalepsy comparison for either dose of nigella seed extract alone. In the same paper, the final multi-herb product was evaluated for composition, laboratory outcomes and toxicity. An older thymoquinone-only chlorpromazine study reported favorable motor proxy measures. Together these show preparation- and outcome-specific animal findings and leave the human ground-seed effect open. [2025 original comparisons](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Nigella-Seed-Gap-Replay.md#isolated-thymoquinone-and-movement-outcomes-in-a-mouse-study) · [2016 original abstract](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Nigella-Anise.md#isolated-thymoquinone-and-chlorpromazine-motor-outcomes)
- **0589, ground cayenne and falls.** Two independent original cases documented symptoms or fainting and hemodynamic changes after oral chili. The older full paper records seated syncope after mole poblano, explicitly without falling to the floor. The 2024 case reports a marked pressure and pulse change and calls the chili cayenne in discussion without characterizing its form. These reports identify events in susceptible individuals and leave a Parkinson falls rate and the specified ground spice unresolved. [Both original cases](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cayenne-Motor-and-Face.md#recurrent-fainting-reported-after-chili-ingestion)
- **0496, refined canola and dexterity.** One oral canola experiment saw reduced BOLD activation during instructed bilateral tapping. The later study monitored the paced task and found no significant variation in tapping rate, with an inconclusive BOLD comparison at three hours. The canola comparator in a hand osteoarthritis thesis discarded an invalid grip measure. These records give actual exposure and task context without an estimate of refined canola’s effect on Parkinson dexterity. [2003 imaging](../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Canola-Dexterity-Comparators.md) · [Later measured task and arthritis comparator](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Canola-Dexterity-Comparators.md)
- **0500, 0550, and 0647.** An eight-herb thyme product gave an inconclusive urgency-intensity result in an uncontrolled study. Correct cardamom essence recognition was 36 of 87 among Parkinson cases and 49 of 58 among controls in one diagnostic test, with an inconclusive case-control difference in the natural-odor study. A later review extracts a favorable saliva-production estimate from a high-risk asparagus oral liquid plus mecobalamin trial. The original asparagus report remains unavailable and the reported outcome is unstimulated saliva output. [Thyme measure](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Thyme-Clove.md#thyme-in-an-eight-herb-urinary-urgency-study) · [Cardamom comparison](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md#recognition-of-cardamom-essence-in-a-parkinson-smell-test) · [Asparagus exact original locator](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Asparagus-Salivary-Outcome.md)
- **0622, 0661, and 0624.** A Parkinson case-control questionnaire pools spinach and leek in a single weekly leafy-green intake item. It cannot identify preparation or measure either food’s effect on dyskinesia or rigidity. The separate progressive supranuclear palsy safflower comparator remains a prospect for fall outcomes. The European record lists trial end in August 2022 and the US record names the oil. Published arm-level falls and oil processing remain open.
