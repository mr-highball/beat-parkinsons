## Evidence and remaining gaps

- The raw romaine crossover measured postprandial metabolites and inflammatory markers in healthy young men. The mixed-salad trial used raw celery stalk with romaine and spinach, recorded a favorable vascular dilation result and found no pressure difference. The Parkinson menu identifies iceberg and Lollo rosso rather than romaine, and its celery entry leaves the plant part unstated. A red-lettuce microgreen extract thesis describes improved fly climbing in a Parkinson model without the romaine cultivar or the claim endpoints. [2019 original reports](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Romaine-Celery-Stalk-Cabbage.md) · [2020–2021 original reports](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Romaine-Celery-Stalk-Cabbage.md)
- A 2012 case describes a celery stalk found at a laryngeal obstruction after choking in a patient with stroke history and vascular dementia. The report gives no preparation. A separate selected sensitization cohort reported oral reactions from celery stalk. These observations document adverse events with the food and different conditions, and neither measures Parkinson dysphagia severity. [2011 and 2012 original reports](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Romaine-Celery-Stalk-Cabbage.md)
- Red cabbage had no resting-pressure effect in a microgreen crossover, while a cooked mixed-vegetable soup containing cabbage lowered ambulatory systolic pressure. Both lack an orthostatic symptom measure. Red cabbage extracts reduced cytotoxicity in a Parkinson cell model, with differences by drying and assay duration. A normally aging mouse study reported cognitive changes and no motor effect. The available evidence leaves cooked red cabbage and the specified clinical mood and rigidity outcomes open. [2023–2024 comparisons](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Romaine-Celery-Stalk-Cabbage.md) · [2025 microgreen crossover](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Romaine-Celery-Stalk-Cabbage.md#2025-red-cabbage-microgreens-and-blood-pressure)
- Sauerkraut clinical reports focus on microbiome, metabolic and inflammatory outcomes in healthy adults. Parkinson-derived fecal samples fermented separately prepared Brussels sprout and white cabbage fibers ex vivo. The sensory studies examine flavor using microwaved or thawed Brussels sprouts. The endpoint PubMed replay found a 2024 paper whose figure labels a nematode thrashing-rate comparison with isolated sulforaphane as dyskinesia. The study measures motility and has no levodopa-induced dyskinesia endpoint or sprouts exposure. These distinctions matter to 0258, 0374 and 0891. ## Additional original records from the successful replay

- Mayama and colleagues compared 1-mm sauerkraut strips and nonfermented pickles in 13 older adults with dysphagia risk. Global microbial diversity did not change. Several bacterial taxa differed by food, and the primary abstract reports no drooling score. The registered umbrella record first appeared on April 1, 2022 and specifies a fermented-food microbiome outcome alongside swallowing rehabilitation. Its displayed intervention comparison describes carbonated and ordinary thickened water. [Primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41539433/) · [Official UMIN registry](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000052859) · [National portal](https://rctportal.mhlw.go.jp/detail/um?trial_id=UMIN000046319)
- Bester and colleagues' January 7, 2026 preprint reports a fermented red cabbage, beetroot and caraway blend in healthy volunteers. Stress and total DASS-21 improved within the blend group, while depression did not differ across arms (p = .607). The registry first posted February 4, 2026 and describes 2019–2020 enrollment and completion. The public record and deposited data describe the same study. A plain-text copy of the CC BY preprint is pending because Europe PMC has metadata only and native publisher downloads returned HTTP 403. [Primary preprint](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202601.0406) · [Official registration](https://www.isrctn.com/ISRCTN75418579) · [Author data record](https://reshare.ukdataservice.ac.uk/858349/) · [Source access receipts](source-access.tsv)
- The US and international isothiocyanate/levodopa patent records are one application family. The examples evaluate cell apoptosis under isolated-compound exposures. This route does not provide an oral sprout feeding comparison or a dyskinesia assessment.
