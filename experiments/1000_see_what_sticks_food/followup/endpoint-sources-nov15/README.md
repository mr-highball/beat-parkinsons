# Exposure and outcome follow-through

November 15, 2026. Two previously Unknown claims now have a minimal lean against their stated worsening direction. The changes are reviewer judgments about transfer from human comparisons outside Parkinson disease.

## Weak directions

- **0883, fresh sage worsens fatigue: Maybe False, 51.** A placebo comparison of extract from fresh sage tips reported a lower secondary Profile of Mood States fatigue score (p=.029). The report gives no clearly separated fatigue arm-by-time table, and its mental exhaustion before sleep item found no clear change. An independent dry-extract trial found an inconclusive exhaustion comparison (final 1.16 versus 1.43, p=.634). This supports a minimal inference against worsening from the fresh botanical source, with a substantial gap between concentrated extract in menopausal participants and culinary leaves in Parkinson disease. [Placebo comparison](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#fresh-sage-tip-extract-and-menopausal-fatigue) · [Separate dry extract](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#sage-extract-and-exhaustion-in-a-registered-menopause-trial)
- **0128, fresh rosemary worsens depressive symptoms: Maybe False, 51.** Adjunctive dried milled leaves and placebo showed BDI-II means going from 33.46 to 14.19 and 42.20 to 29.52, respectively, with a group-by-time p=.005. The supplement group started with lower scores (p=.003) and the analysis used 51 completers without an explicitly adjusted baseline model. A student trial also reported a favorable depression comparison. The work-stress extract post-hoc comparison was inconclusive (p=.058). The newer Kuwata report’s 42.9 and 44.5 values are anxiety, and the acute aroma study’s original depression table remains inaccessible. The population, treatment background and dried versus fresh transfer limit the grade.
