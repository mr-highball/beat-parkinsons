## Raw orange and constipation

Two previously queued papers were recovered. One uses orange as a fluorescent laboratory reagent, and the other uses orange for eye-movement traces. Their original reports give anatomical or neurological context and no raw orange intervention. [Previously pending source receipts](open-trails.tsv)

The 2011 Chagas series combines physiotherapy with a blended orange, papaya and prune drink. Constipation scores declined in 11 of 12 participants after six weeks, and four reported bloating or flatulence. A separate 2012 trial assigned healthy adults to two pasteurized orange juice products and found an inconclusive product-by-time contrast for stool frequency (p=.79). The time result includes the temporary week-four increases in both arms. Both comparisons leave raw orange in Parkinson disease unresolved. [Chagas and pasteurized juice primary records](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Orange-Papaya-Constipation.md)

The two Lima studies made public in 2025 report associations between orange frequency and Bristol stool form. Neither reports a directional orange estimate or a preparation-specific result. The larger student study excluded diagnosed constipation and diarrhea. The Parkinson prescription study records constipation and medicinal citrus at initial encounters and follows no post-treatment constipation change. The prior Fox Insight result points in the opposite direction for pooled fresh fruit using a binary bowel-frequency category. The minimal lean against worsening remains based on the older orange-frequency and pomace evidence. ## Olive oil, dark chocolate and whole-diet counseling

MEDI-PD assessed 46 participants, randomized 45 and analyzed 36 after nine did not receive their allocated intervention. It reports an inconclusive between-group constipation result (p=.600) and anxiety group-by-time result (p=.745). The protocol describes whole-diet counseling, without a separated olive oil dose, grade or dark chocolate treatment. Supplementary nutrient totals do not identify consumed olive oil. The existing weak leans for 0072, 0100 and 0343 retain their earlier evidence and comparator limits.
