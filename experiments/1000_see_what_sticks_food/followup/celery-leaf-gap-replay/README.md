# Celery leaf and four unresolved outcomes

The 1990 mastication study measured parotid flow in ten healthy participants. Chewing celery elicited more flow than parafilm, but the indexed report does not name the plant part or measure drooling. Whole dried plant extract altered motor tasks in MPTP mice without a dystonia measure. The clinical butylphthalide study used a synthetic drug and a motor examination after overnight medication withdrawal. Time spent OFF during daily life was outside that comparison. Visual hallucination frequency after dried leaves remains an open outcome.
