## Claim judgments

- **0063, ground saffron worsens depressive symptoms. Maybe False at 58.** The Isfahan Parkinson trial used 100 mg daily Sargol powder in tablets and reported an adjusted difference of −4.58 points (95% CI −8.34 to −0.81) on the registered secondary depression scale. The separate Tehran capsules gave a favorable Hamilton comparison, with an unspecified preparation and inconclusive secondary mood results. The 2015 and 2018 non-Parkinson adjunct reports share a registry but give different participant counts and inconclusive p-values of .560 and .056. The 2005 positive placebo comparison used an ethanol extract of dried stigma. An extract blend trial outside Parkinson found an inconclusive depression-scale difference. [Isfahan trial](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#saffron-powder-and-depressive-symptoms) · [Shiraz report family](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Saffron-Depression-Comparators.md#linked-shiraz-reports-and-their-unresolved-differences) · [Earlier extract](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Saffron-Depression-Comparators.md)
- **0121, brewed coffee worsens fatigue. Unknown at 50.** The small Parkinson espresso crossover gave mixed participant-level results between caffeinated and decaffeinated espresso. The added diet cohort reports a separate fatigue outcome for broad dietary patterns and groups coffee with tea in the dietary index. The published data yield no coffee-specific fatigue estimate. [Espresso trial](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Coffee-Followup.md) · [Diet cohort and supplement](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Beverage-Fatigue-Followup.md#plant-diet-quality-and-separately-reported-fatigue)
- **0218, ground saffron reduces insomnia severity. Maybe True at 51.** The Parkinson powder trial measured overall sleep quality. Extract trials outside Parkinson give mixed insomnia-scale comparisons. The exact scope adds no separate Parkinson insomnia result. [Isfahan trial](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#saffron-powder-and-depressive-symptoms) · [Insomnia scales](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Saffron-Insomnia.md)
- **0417, ground saffron worsens muscle rigidity. Unknown at 50.** The Tehran capsule trial gives an inconclusive Parkinson motor total (p = .424). No separate rigidity estimate or confirmed ground-saffron preparation is available. The animal constituent comparisons used injection and catalepsy. [Tehran registry and outcome](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Saffron-Clinical-Endpoints.md#tehran-registry-and-the-accessible-motor-total) · [Animal proxy](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Saffron-Rigidity-Context.md#injected-safranal-and-catalepsy-in-a-parkinson-model)
- **0777, ground cardamom reduces depressive symptoms. Unknown at 50.** The 2019 cardamom oil body uses “central nervous system depression” for reduced locomotor behavior in aluminum-exposed rats. The separate reserpine oil study and older-adult tulsi–cardamom trial remain available at abstract depth and provide no isolated clinical ground-cardamom estimate.
