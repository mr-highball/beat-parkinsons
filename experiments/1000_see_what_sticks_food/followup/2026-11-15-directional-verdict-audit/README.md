# Directional verdict audit, November 15, 2026

The evidence review changed the brewed-coffee verdict to Unknown. The two remaining tentatively opposing verdicts concern distinct endpoints from the same Isfahan saffron cohort. They are reviewer judgments under the original [experiment rules](../../AGENTS.md).

## 0121, brewed coffee and fatigue

The 2016 Ferreira full report confirms four randomized blinded single-person crossover sequences, with three participants providing three complete pairs and one providing two. The four participants were men with Parkinson disease and daytime sleepiness. The two products were machine-brewed regular Delta Platina espresso (100 mg caffeine per cup) and machine-brewed Novadelta Light decaf (2 mg per cup), prescribed at two to four cups per day. Capsule packaging delivered the coffee to the brewing machine. The run-in used regular espresso for one week, and the two-day washout periods used decaf. The authors expressly considered rebound from caffeine withdrawal. The treatment contrast can inform the choice between regular and decaffeinated espresso in a selected group. Both groups consumed the frozen brewing category, so the trial has no estimate of adding brewed coffee versus abstaining. Product and caffeine differences make attribution to caffeine alone uncertain as well. [Primary study, Procedures, Investigational Therapy and Discussion](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neurology/articles/10.3389/fneur.2016.00027/full)

Fatigue VAS was a separate secondary outcome. The main endpoint was daytime somnolence. Table 4 shows the following individual comparisons. The p-values are the authors’ one-tailed tests. No population-effect estimate was reported. [Primary Table 4](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neurology/articles/10.3389/fneur.2016.00027/full#T4)

The 2009 congress report names fatigue VAS as secondary and shares the products’ contrast, patient count and completion pattern. It gives no fatigue values. The earlier 2002 report gives only a nonspecific VAS and differs on whether all four completed three pairs. The Brazilian filtered-coffee crossover has three participants with Parkinson disease and three with stroke, pooled motor/cognitive results and no fatigue endpoint. The MVP qualitative fatigue heatmap pools coffee and espresso and provides no coefficient, uncertainty interval or brewing breakdown for the pair. The Postuma placebo trial gives an isolated-caffeine fatigue estimate of −2.85 points with 95% credible interval −7.73 to +2.06 at six weeks. The UK cross-sectional comparison tested a combined sleep/fatigue domain and gave no ingredient-level fatigue-only estimate. The Saudi mixed-caffeine study gives OR 0.94 (0.37 to 2.35) for frequent versus infrequent exposure. The 2018 Korean cohort contrast is a mood/cognition domain in the accessible result, with no isolated fatigue estimate checked here. None supplies the missing brewed-coffee fatigue contrast. The ParkWest, PPMI and biobank joint analyses remain unverified. ## 0063, ground saffron and depressive symptoms

The Isfahan results abstract describes a randomized, triple-blind, 12-week 100 mg/day saffron powder versus placebo comparison in 92 people with Parkinson disease. The adjusted mean depression difference is −4.58 (95% CI −8.34 to −0.81, ANCOVA p=.01). The original March 5, 2024 IRCT version names DASS-21 in the secondary outcomes. CRP drove sample size. The final protocol specifies first-class Mojtahedi Sargol powder in one daily tablet after lunch, matched lactose placebo, and planned HPLC checking of crocin before and after tableting. This product contains the ground saffron material, with a study-specific dose and delivery. The public results abstract confirms powder and dose. Actual lot and analytic composition, attrition by arm, the final testing family and multiplicity handling remain unverified. The linked Isfahan protocol has an explicit registry ID. The outcome report’s identity is strongly supported by the shared authors, regimen, size and funding award, with an explicit report registry citation unavailable in the accessible abstract. [Results abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41439298/) · [Original registry](https://irct.ir/trial/75378?revision=296350) · [Protocol PDF](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/bmjopen/15/10/e093167.full.pdf)

The independent Tehran trial is explicitly linked to IRCT20090117001556N145. The randomized study reports 52 people with Parkinson disease and depression, 25 in the 15 mg saffron capsule twice-daily arm and 27 in the placebo arm, for eight weeks alongside usual treatment. The original HAM-D result favors saffron (time-by-treatment p=.006, week-eight comparison p=.045). Those are two analyses of one cohort. GDS-30 and the MDS-UPDRS Part I depressed mood item supplied inconclusive secondary results. The primary publication’s Acknowledgments identify Green Plant Life as medication supplier and describe the work as Lalezari’s postgraduate thesis, supported by TUMS grant 57533. Brand, content processing and powder versus extract remain unverified. A 15 mg product from the same supplier in another trial cannot resolve this product identity. [Registry](https://irct.ir/trial/65247) · [Primary article preview and Acknowledgments](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2210803324001258)

The grade is Maybe False at 58. The originally registered symptom measure and known placebo contrast in Isfahan support the lean. Tehran supplies independent symptom-level corroboration with unverified preparation. The secondary endpoint, unknown multiplicity adjustment, inconsistent Tehran secondary mood outcomes, and fixed tablet regimen keep the evidence below a definitive grade. The Tehran assessment is related for the frozen ground form.

## 0485, dried saffron and fatigue

The same Isfahan report gives adjusted fatigue difference −9.20 (95% CI −13.76 to −4.65, ANCOVA p<.001), with independent confirmation of published magnitude unavailable. The linked protocol specifies a Persian 16-item Parkinson Fatigue Scale scored 16–80 and the dried red-stigma Sargol powder tablet. The fatigue measure is relevant to symptom severity, particularly physical fatigue and its impact. The abstract specifies powder and fatigue without independently displaying the full scale or tablet lot. Powdering preserves the dried-stigma material and the trial varies assignment of that material versus placebo. The study does not estimate ordinary dietary doses or preparation in meals. [Results abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41439298/) · [Protocol PDF](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/bmjopen/15/10/e093167.full.pdf) · [Scale development](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15619463/)

Original revision 296350 named broad MDS-UPDRS, which includes a fatigue item, and omitted PFS-16. BMJ received its protocol manuscript September 2, 2024. Its originally submitted version is unavailable. Food & Function received the results manuscript April 27, 2025. The PFS-16 first appears in the archived July 30 revision whose page shows an August 9 update. An undated public protocol-review response names fatigue among secondary outcomes. The final protocol first published October 29. These facts establish a late public listing and leave the original plan and actual recruitment dates unresolved. They do not prove post hoc measurement or selective omission. Fatigue was secondary across multiple outcomes. The external MS, COPD and pulmonary sarcoidosis studies are related and give no independent replicated dried-saffron Parkinson fatigue contrast. [Original register](https://irct.ir/trial/75378?revision=296350) · [First PFS-16 register entry](https://irct.ir/trial/75378?revision=350954) · [Editorial and registration receipts](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Saffron-Registry-History.md)

The grade is Maybe False at 53. The randomized group contrast, closely matching material and favorable adjusted fatigue result support a weak direction despite substantial selective-reporting and transfer uncertainty. Revisit the grade if the primary full manuscript, earlier dated protocol or statistical plan can establish the fatigue outcome history or overturn the published contrast.
