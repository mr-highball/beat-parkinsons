# 2014 — Earliest report of the post-radiotherapy olive-oil mouthcare cohort

Martín Martín, López and Cerezo published the Spanish report in Avances en Odontoestomatología 30(3):161–170, May–June 2014. The paper was received 15 January and accepted 19 March 2014. DOI 10.4321/S0213-12852014000300010. It describes about two weeks of topical toothpaste, rinse, gel and spray containing olive oil, betaine and xylitol for dry mouth after head and neck radiotherapy. A 100-mm VAS item asked about difficulty swallowing because of oral dryness. In the uncontrolled pre/post comparison the report describes improvement across all eight VAS items, with throat dryness the only nonsignificant item. This includes reported improvement on the swallowing item. [Spanish original, pp. 165–167 and Figure 3](https://scielo.isciii.es/pdf/odonto/v30n3/original8.pdf)

This Spanish report and the English article published online 25 November 2016 (PMID 27920611, DOI 10.1016/j.rpor.2016.09.008) almost certainly describe the same cohort. They share three authors and the same topical regimen, questionnaires, follow-up, and Table 2 numbers. Both recruited 40, 25 men and 15 women, mean age 65 years (47–99), with 38 completing treatment. The tumor site counts 12 pharynx, 14 larynx, 12 oral cavity, two other, and xerogenic-medication distribution match. Both describe the same nasogastric tube patient with baseline complete aphagia who discontinued after three days because he could not swallow the saliva produced, and a second early withdrawal because the products did not improve on previous products. Both retain second-visit answers for discontinuers. This concordance supports one cohort, with the Spanish original as the earliest publication and the English article as a companion report. The articles do not provide participant-level linkage.
