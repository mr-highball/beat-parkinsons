## 0457 boiled pinto beans reduce small handwriting

Existing PMID36501214 pools beans in a dietary survey and motor aggregate that includes handwriting, without pinto, preparation or item-level comparison; not a result for boiled pinto. https://www.michaeljfox.org/sites/default/files/media/document/041819_MJFF_DIET_GUIDE_0_1.pdf lists pinto beans as possible food but reports no patient micrographia comparisons. No distinct original controlled pinto/handwriting source located. Unknown, no evidence for opposite direction.

## 0375 raw watermelon worsens drooling

Existing PMID3192863 concerns acute oral allergic symptoms in a selected sensitive population, not drooling severity. Newly checked Karami Nogourani et al. 2012-03-11 https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2012/569327 primary crossover uses 15 healthy dental students chewing five commercial flavored gums, no verified fruit material. Watermelon-flavored gum salivary flow 0.88±0.39 mL/min before vs 3.63±0.81 at six minutes, p=.001; chewing all flavors increased salivation, no nonflavored gum comparator. Saliva production is not PD drooling, which also involves swallowing/oral handling; watermelon flavor is not raw fruit. Context only, no weak direction.

## 0351 ground fennel seed reduces micrographia

Prior crude fennel seed peripheral mouse study PMID31489666 has inconsistent abstract significance (p > .05), no human handwriting. Methanolic extract hind-paw grip study PMID33598155 in local record is distinct from culinary powder. Additional 2023 primary https://www.mdpi.com/2218-1989/13/3/391/pdf?version=1678185006 DOI10.3390/metabo13030391 evaluates fennel extract nanosuspension after sciatic injury in mice; ipsilateral hind-limb grip and sciatic functional index, not handwriting or PD, no ground seed treatment. Publisher HTML access returned 429; accessible PDF Methods 2.7.3 and Results 3.2. It adds peripheral functional context only and cannot resolve direction. Cohort independence from earlier publications is not established solely by different publication date.

## 0426 refined rice bran oil reduces dystonia

Existing TCTR20210807002 randomized Thai PD placebo used rice bran oil with prespecified UPDRS IV but no separate dystonia item or oil refining disclosure. Original 2014 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9351805/ (also FDA-authority copy https://www.fda.gov.tw/tc/includes/GetFile.ashx?id=f636694852860381089) uses 0.4 mL/day oil by oral tube to prevent haloperidol-associated vacuous chewing and coordination impairment in rats; another 2024 https://www.um.edu.mt/library/oar/handle/123456789/122437 uses Garcinia-enriched oil, not plain oil comparator. Tardive chewing and catalepsy are not dystonia; ordinary refined grade not established. Unknown.

## 0321 toasted sesame oil worsens reduced facial expressiveness

Existing primary 2019 https://www.ijapc.com/upload/MNAPC-V-11-I3-23-p-48-59.pdf registry CTRI/2018/09/015592 gives facial score 3.30 to 0.78 in 23 people with parkinsonism after a multi-component treatment course including rectally administered processed sesame oil plus other botanicals and purgative. Uncontrolled before/after, treatment route and formulation differ, no oil-only effect or untreated trajectory. This is weakly favorable within full regimen but does not establish dietary toasted sesame direction. Unknown remains defensible; could argue weak against worsening only with an exceptionally broad, poorly supported route/multicomponent bridge.
