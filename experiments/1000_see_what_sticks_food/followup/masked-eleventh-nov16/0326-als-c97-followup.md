The MND Association research team's January 10, 2020 meeting account describes preliminary results for three randomized arms. It reports no observed improvement in swallowing for the EVOO diet-only or swallowing-exercise-only arm, and trends toward less coughing with oral intake and slightly increased weight in the combined arm. It specifies a target of three to four tablespoons of EVOO per day. The account is secondary and gives no arm sizes, endpoint scale, estimates, p-values or between-group tests. The page displays an August 11, 2026 update beside the original January 10, 2020 date. [Organiser's C97 meeting account under Swallowing exercises and EVOO](https://mndresearch.blog/2020/01/10/dysphagia-and-nutritional-management-highlights-from-perth/)

The presenter, three-arm comparison and nutrition/swallowing aims link C97 to the existing Flood/Niven pilot family. The dietary intake companion abstract reports pooled intake and provides no swallowing contrast for the oil alone. The meeting account refines the older note that a primary estimate isolating the dietary arm is missing. Retain one study-family entry and retain 0326 Unknown. Neither the magnitude of the ALS diet-only change nor its uncertainty transfers to swallowing severity in Parkinson disease.
