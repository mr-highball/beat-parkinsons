## 0462 cooked edamame and rigidity

Nagashima et al., PMID26810548, first published 2016-01-06, DOI 10.1016/j.jns.2016.01.005, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26810548/ . Seven PD participants with wearing off and dyskinesia and five healthy controls underwent an acute crossover of levodopa/carbidopa alone or with 11 g ground soybeans. The abstract reports longer ON (p=.028) and lower abnormal involuntary movement score (p<.001). UPDRS III was measured but separate rigidity item is not given in abstract. Ground mature soybean is not verified cooked green edamame; original item table remains open. No defensible ingredient-specific rigidity direction follows from ON duration or dyskinesia.

## 0388 baked turkey and standing lightheadedness

Existing PD post-meal standing study PMID8984127 measures orthostatic blood pressure following lunch without identifying turkey or subjective dizziness; PMID40360851 studies mixed meal and seated pressure. A separate tilt-table paper https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=ff702c8d5a2b08377e63192a7d8b1323115755e5&repid=rep1&type=pdf describes liquid meal as calorically comparable to a turkey sandwich and cola; participants did not receive an actual turkey sandwich. Do not convert the analogy into turkey exposure. No baked turkey-specific effect.

## 0319 fresh chervil and executive function

Wolfson and Hoffmann 2003 PMID12652886 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12652886/ investigates skullcap versus placebo in healthy volunteers; primary PubMed abstract does not name placebo. Brock's Westminster thesis https://westminsterresearch.westminster.ac.uk/download/08742bb81b8e3e1be1718deb660149c8d1ddbe2675b1a81246704ee452bc3af6/5319796/Christine_BROCK.pdf pp. clinical trials reports a personal communication from Hoffmann identifying placebo as Anthriscus cerefolium. It describes 19 healthy adults and self-rated cognition/energy over two hours; this is secondary testimony, not direct confirmation of placebo material, botanical part, preparation, or executive task. Original study full body and quantitative placebo data remain open. The hypothesized chervil placebo is no proof fresh leaves change PD executive function. Existing PMID35551255 and PMID38977255 are chemistry and in vitro extract work only.

## 0331 fresh cilantro and swallowing

Chen et al. 2013 PMID23509907 DOI10.1186/1710-1492-9-11 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3608993/ original case: mixed salad with coriander dressing, then running, followed by acute dysphagia and allergic symptoms; prick testing positive to fresh coriander and tomato. Later ingestion of the ingredients without exercise reportedly produced local symptoms only. No clean isolated oral challenge, PD cohort, chronic swallow rating, or estimate of ordinary culinary effect; already catalogued as related context.

## 0378 ground sumac and daytime sleepiness

Ardalani et al. 2016 DOI10.1039/C5RA22840A https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/getauthorversionpdf/c5ra22840a used 1 g/day dried Rhus coriaria powder plus captopril versus captopril and starch for eight weeks in hypertension. Narrative attributes one drowsiness to sumac while Table 3 lists no drowsiness and a weakness event, creating an unresolved source conflict. This is spontaneous reporting without ESS or PD cohort; retains existing related assessment and does not justify direction by itself.

## 0355 toasted mustard seed and small handwriting

Wright 2024 DOI10.18103/mra.v12i1.4955 https://esmed.org/MRA/mra/article/download/4955/99193547682/ Table 1 self-report example has handwriting score 1 throughout, alongside broccoli and ground white mustard tea. Self-selection, multicomponent preparation, no control and no letter-size measurement prevent an isolated toasted mustard or PD micrographia estimate.

## 0441 refined grapeseed oil and dream enactment

Official https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02841618 retrieved and saved NCT02841618.json: last posted 2026-04-21, active not recruiting, no resultsSection. It registers grapeseed oil cookies and a blood-linoleic-acid/sleep questionnaire correlation, not dream enactment. Healthy adults, oil refining and individual active comparator effects remain unresolved. Previously reviewed Bae ambient grapeseed carrier, Yang aromatherapy and Chen Gua Sha are not evidence of a consumed refined oil effect; Chen trial does not identify oil and is topical.

## 0330 toasted pumpkin seed oil and dream enactment

Existing IRCT20180424039397N1 trial used intranasal whole pumpkin preparation in sesame oil and reports aggregate PSQI rather than separate dreams or dream enactment; no Parkinson cohort. No comparison of ingested toasted pumpkin seed oil and dream episodes found in the focused searches. No direction derived from absence.
