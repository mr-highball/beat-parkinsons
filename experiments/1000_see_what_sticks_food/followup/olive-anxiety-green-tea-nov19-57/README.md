# Olive oil and anxiety, brewed green tea and fatigue

Three PubMed queries returned 11 records and three ClinicalTrials.gov queries returned 13 query memberships across ten distinct registrations. Every accessible page in the six queries was screened. The tea registry searches retrieved the older polyphenol trial and two unrelated interventions. The olive registry results contain whole diets or different assigned interventions. These searches make no claim of closing the original evidence queues. [Full queries and counts](search-coverage.tsv) · [Publication screening](pubmed-screen.tsv) · [Registration screening](registry-screen.tsv)

The [original Ilgin rat experiment](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Olive-Oil-Anxiety.md) verifies cold pressing for both oils. It reports more elevated plus-maze open-arm time under the higher-phenol oil versus water, while open-field center time and the two oil arms did not differ clearly. The [older Wistar study](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Olive-Oil-Anxiety.md) uses a small sample and gives conflicting descriptions of the control. The [Kimizoglu author abstract](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Olive-Oil-Anxiety.md#aged-rats-and-open-field-behavior) reports no clear open-field result. [DieTBra and the published correspondence](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Olive-Oil-Anxiety.md#original-dietbra-commentary-trail) remain one trial family, with the original detailed arm results and the authors' response still open.

The [Chen trial and dissertation](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#green-tea-and-fatigue-in-parkinsons-disease) report one cohort of 14 Parkinson completers. Their mean Fatigue Severity Scale score rose 3.7 points, with no control arm or paired variance reported. A [postoperative study](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Green-Tea-Outcomes.md#brewed-green-tea-versus-water-for-postoperative-fatigue) found less fatigue in a different population and context. The [Jiang publisher preview](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Green-Tea-Fatigue-Models.md) names traditional tea use in its introduction and fly locomotor behavior in its results. The full-body and preprint search remains open. [Open trails](open-trails.tsv) · [Additional claim assessments](related-assessments.tsv)

Both claims retain Maybe True under the checked rubric. Olive oil and anxiety (0343) uses the 50.005 minimal easing tier. Its leading human evidence is the [separately measured HADS anxiety association](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Facial-Falls-and-Voice.md#olive-oil-intake-and-hads-anxiety-in-a-convenience-survey), with uncertain oil grade, multiplicity and population fit. Brewed green tea and fatigue (0948) uses the 51 limited clinical link tier from the exact preparation and outcome in the small Parkinson cohort. The scores describe the strength of the research link.
