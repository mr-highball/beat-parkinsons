# Round 1 dietary cohorts, ginger and original access

The November 25 review resolved 53 exact checks across 20 original publications. It recorded 40 related contexts and 13 scope mismatches. All 38 rechecked scorecards matched their published verdicts and anchors. Five of the closed checks concern the raw fig and rest tremor claim. The Parkinson symptom verdict for that claim remains open.

Twelve checks came from two previously held sources. One is a chapter with food-processing sections whose chapter numbering and pagination are now verified. The other is a published congress index with a companion abstract collection. All 206 member numbers align, and the 13 editorial title variants were checked individually. Eighteen checks from the earlier staged access list still require original access.

Two independent ginger experiments now have exact indexed source records and contextual scorecard entries for claim 0626. One tests isolated 6-shogaol in a mouse model. The other compares a heated ginger extract and isolated 6-shogaol in separate mouse arms. Neither measures passive rigidity after grated ginger. The two human ginger registries still have no public outcome estimates in the inspected records. A separate 2026 paper was captured at abstract depth.

The queue retains 31,808 exact checks across 21,427 sources, with 2,368 on claim 0559. The new Oracle-first batch adds 29 questions, with three partial matches and 26 misses.
