# Japanese and Korean trial registry follow-up

Public searches ran November 12, 2026 UTC. UMIN-CTR and jRCT are Japanese primary registries. CRIS is maintained by the Korea National Institute of Health. This pass checked their public interfaces for food, diet, supplement, botanical and aroma exposures and traced plausible leads to primary publications. Records and linked publications were reconciled by trial ID, investigator, design, intervention, and cohort. [UMIN snapshot documentation](https://www.umin.ac.jp/ctr/csvdata.html) · [jRCT public search](https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/search) · [CRIS public search](https://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/search/listDetail.do)

## Coverage

- **UMIN-CTR.** The bilingual November 12 JST snapshot contained 63,222 unique trial IDs and 350,754,212 extracted CSV bytes. All records were parsed. Of 746 Parkinson mentions, 416 were in title or condition, 50 in other study fields, and 280 in exclusion criteria alone. Exposure terms flagged 108 records. Candidates were screened against intervention, outcome, population and status fields. The November 12 snapshot was served with `Last-Modified: Wed, 11 Nov 2026 16:34:15 GMT`. UMIN supplied the data. This pass transformed its bilingual CSV into searchable fields, matched trial and exposure terms, then retained counts and original evidence summaries. Full snapshot and extracted screening text are local build inputs under UMIN's use terms. [Bilingual snapshot and terms](https://www.umin.ac.jp/ctr/csvdata.html)
- **jRCT.** The Japanese and English broad-keyword searches returned all 400 and 340 rows over eight and seven pages. The union contained 407 trial IDs. The published title or condition contained Parkinson terms in 172, and title or condition exposure terms flagged 20. The 50 first-page results of the supplementary Japanese condition search all occur in the broad union. That query reported 172 hits. Further Japanese condition requests encountered HTTP 403 or a timeout. Separate English condition and historical-name requests returned HTTP 403. Direct records for selected candidates remained reachable via the Japanese and English registry pages. The public search listings sometimes shorten titles. The screen used those listings, manual title review and direct detail checks for plausible candidates. Full intervention fields were available for the selected details. [jRCT public search](https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/search)
- **CRIS.** Korean and English broad-keyword searches returned all 76 and 75 rows over four pages each, yielding 77 unique IDs. All 77 contained Parkinson terms in title or condition. Six exposure flags came from the bilingual title, intervention and outcome fields, supplemented by manual title review for brand names and Korean medicine. Three historical terms returned HTTP 200 with an empty body, which the public page handles as no records. The ICD G20-only endpoint probe also returned an empty body. The separate government open-data API requires a service key. The public site provided directly accessible records and their review/registration dates. [CRIS public search](https://cris.nih.go.kr/cris/search/listDetail.do) · [Government API description](https://www.data.go.kr/data/3033869/openapi.do?recommendDataYn=Y)

The Japanese health ministry's administrative list pairs jRCTs031200172 with a bacterial intervention title. The official record, published protocol and published results identify the intervention for that ID as elobixibat. This discrepancy supplies a follow-up lead without a second verified trial. [Administrative list](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/content/10800000/001286605.pdf) · [jRCT record](https://jrct.mhlw.go.jp/latest-detail/jRCTs031200172) · [Trial report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38264844/)

The Ninjin'yoeito trial jRCTs031200079 allowed Parkinson dementia, while its published efficacy cohort comprised Alzheimer's disease and dementia with Lewy bodies. The CRIS self-management trial KCT0006790 provided diet-management texts to both arms, alongside other education. The cohort and interventions in the primary reports delimit their use for the frozen claims. [Ninjin'yoeito trial report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41342654/) · [Self-management trial report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35643018/)

## Reproducibility

Build the native Windows i386 program with [build-east-asia.ps1](../tools/build-east-asia.ps1). It reads quoted multiline UTF-8 UMIN CSV, native jRCT result HTML and CRIS XML. It checks row counts for each completed live query and deduplicates public trial IDs. Invoke `foodexperiment.eastasia.exe umin EXPERIMENT_DIR OUTPUT_DIR UMIN_CSV` or `foodexperiment.eastasia.exe jrct|cris EXPERIMENT_DIR OUTPUT_DIR`. An optional saved-page directory on the live modes reprocesses downloaded pages. It validates counts for the available query series and records their replay status. The Japanese and English jRCT search pages in this pass were parsed from the original saved HTML because subsequent search requests encountered access errors.

The UMIN ZIP SHA-256 is `f55aa7aef9c419045023f23ddf2b0281a1d8e008642657309f2ec53cb99adb0b`. The extracted CSV SHA-256 is `4481587876b9da1644f76fff53f8404dc153a2c04e5cbd4349f6499e5e4d3ef4`. The local tool matches names in English, Japanese and Korean and historical forms including paralysis agitans, shaking palsy, 振戦麻痺 and 진전마비. The exposure screen uses the frozen ingredients, aliases, expansions and generic nutrition and botanical terms in those languages. A lexical flag can reflect a false match or an exclusion, and the registry interfaces can omit a mention outside the fields they index.
