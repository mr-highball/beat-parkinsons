# Round 1 screening and focused food-evidence follow-up

The Oracle-first selection covered 121 publication identities and 269 queued claim–source checks. Of 78 newly selected PMC Cloud records, 69 had identity-checked bodies. They let the review close 158 exact checks at priority six, with 67 related contexts and 91 source-scope exclusions. The other 111 selected checks remain open. Three older publisher trails still lack complete originals. Four high-priority PMC OAI-PMH requests returned unsupported full-text format. Complete positive-control and focused article bodies from the same API verified the retrieval method.

The new source texts include 68 original-body summaries and six focused records. The original pregnancy trial used topical grapeseed-oil massage and measured edema. The conference abstract studied oral grape-seed powder and gives within-arm mood changes with an unclear between-arm estimate. The rechecked migraine article used grape-seed extract and had inconclusive adjusted DASS depression and anxiety comparisons. The latter now carries canonical PMID, PMCID and DOI fields in the catalog. The original families are distinct, with no defensible symptom sign for oral refined grapeseed oil. Both grapeseed-oil claims moved to Unknown (Researched) after the focused exposure and source checks were completed.

Qeqchi fieldwork identified black beans as customary food and described other botanical sleep remedies separately. A Vietnamese folk-therapy article reports a mixed black-bean practice without a measured sleep response or verified canned preparation. The Chota Nagpur black-pepper lead is traceable to original fieldwork, with an intranasal mixture described in a later review. The original recipe-level table could not be accessed. These records carry their actual source and exposure context.

All 40 checked scorecards kept their existing verdicts and anchors. The original replay preserves 61,252 memberships, with 28,038 remaining exact checks across 20,296 publication keys and 364 claims.
