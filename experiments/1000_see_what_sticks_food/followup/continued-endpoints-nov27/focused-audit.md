# Focused outcome and trial-identity audit

## 0139 Ground nigella and fatigue

The open-label 2022 seed-oil softgel comparison reports day-four fatigue in 12/30 versus 25/30, adjusted OR 0.15 (95% CI 0.04–0.48). Its endpoint is fatigue presence on a particular day. The acute-illness setting, formulation and reporting limit transfer to ground culinary nigella and Parkinson fatigue severity. The open-label 2021 oil trial randomized 183 (91 oil and 92 usual care). Table 2 reports 6.3 ± 4.1 days of lethargy or fatigue in 62 participants with that symptom versus 5.4 ± 3.2 days in 56 controls with the symptom, p=.18. The numerical duration was longer with oil. The estimate is inconclusive and covers a symptomatic subset during acute illness. The closer ground-seed trial found mixed day-ten findings. Other families report no separately attributable fatigue result. The public EVACUR registration gives 59 enrolled, completed May 7, 2025, and no posted results as of the November 27 recheck. The FACIT-F protocol compares a botanical blend with starch placebo and does not assign nigella separately. The day-four report retains a minimal lean against worsening with substantial outcome and preparation uncertainty.

## 0147 Raw broccoli and attention

The 2020 NCT04521868 record names two different sites in separate fields and has no posted results. Chen analyzed 42 long-stay patients at Jiangyin and reports no clear MCCB composite or subscore difference. The exact attention values remain unavailable. Huang analyzed 77 patients at Xiangya and used the PANSS cognitive factor, with no separate attention measure in the paper or supplement. Participant overlap remains unresolved, so the reports contribute one linked family of uncertain independence. Preparation and population differ from the frozen claim.

## 0308 Refined corn oil and daytime sleepiness

The original Methods says 106 agreed to participate. A third-party excerpt of the author's flow figure describes 78 in overall analyses, 44 receiving omega-3 and 34 receiving corn-oil ethyl esters. Randomized and ESS-specific denominators remain unverified. The abstract describes an inverse correlation but prints r=0.30. A secondary review gives p=.247 for its reported arm means. The original table, timepoint and sign remain open. This comparison supplies no estimate for refined culinary corn oil in Parkinson disease. The official NCT01216982 result check remains open. Registry status is UNKNOWN, with no posted results. The given comparator wording and missing report identifier leave its linkage to PMID 24690964 unconfirmed.
