Most promising human directional lead: Canheta et al. [PMID 32675019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32675019/), online 10 June 2020, NCT02463435. Three active arms in severe obesity: 52 mL extra-virgin olive oil daily, traditional Brazilian diet, or their combination for 12 weeks. The abstract reports anxiety prevalence reductions within respective groups 38.23%, 45.83%, and 33.33%. There was no usual-diet no-intervention arm, so these within-arm changes are not an isolated oil effect; the study was not in Parkinson patients. Current assessment ledger does not list this PMID. Check full text and between-arm contrasts before inferring any stronger direction.
