# 2004 EASD and 2011 SGIM original review

The two original supplements cover 1,084 printed pages and resolve 55 exact original claim–source pairs across 44 frozen claims. Nineteen pairs retain real ingredient or ingredient-derived context, and 36 resolve documented mismatches. The pairs cover 23 food, 12 herb, 9 oil and 11 spice hypotheses.
