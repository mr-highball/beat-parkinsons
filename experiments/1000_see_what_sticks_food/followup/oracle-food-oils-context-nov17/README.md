## Corn oil and rigidity, 0163

The native Europe PMC run read every page in three focused expressions, retrieving 0, 3 and 11 appearances. The abstract matches include animal oil vehicles, clinical feeding cases, and studies whose outcomes concern a different exposure or disease. The official registry checks for `corn oil Parkinson`, `maize oil rigidity` and `Parkinson levodopa enteral nutrition` each returned zero within those terms. The results are recorded with their depth for follow-up. The authorized original Chagas table gives a six-week motor score comparison with corn oil shared across the placebo and both CBD groups. The Part III p-value is .675 and individual rigidity outcomes are absent. Pomponi’s randomized DHA/EPA comparison used equicaloric corn oil and reported inconclusive aggregate motor changes in its abstract. The full methods and numerical tables remained gated, leaving part-specific outcomes and oil grade unverified. A secondary extraction of enrollment and selection criteria cannot substitute for the original. Shared vehicles, active comparisons and multicomponent feeding cases leave an isolated refined corn oil effect unresolved. Claim 0163 remains **Unknown, 50**. ## Coconut oil and fatigue, 0229

The original PPI-1011 healthy-volunteer paper identifies coconut oil in the active and placebo vehicles, without a stated virgin grade. The lowest single-dose group had one fatigue event among six participants and pooled placebo had zero among ten. The repeated-dose table lists none. The study has no fatigue severity scale or oil-free arm. Its registration belongs to 2023 and the article to 2025. The separate Choi ketogenic-diet and MCT paper corresponds to NCT04584346, a study already in the evidence ledger. Participant reports describe both improvement and worsening. The isolated virgin-oil question remains open and **Maybe False, 51** is retained. The Kennedy healthy-adult breakfast bar includes coconut oil with multiple nutrients and reports an acute mental-fatigue contrast. The Cohen cancer diet permits coconut oil among optional fats and reports no significant between-diet difference in its energy item. The Lee multiple-sclerosis pilot allows coconut oil, MCT oil or coconut milk within a ketogenic diet; no 12-week between-diet fatigue difference was reported. These distinct populations and combined exposures provide indirect context and do not isolate virgin coconut oil in Parkinson fatigue. ## Olive oil and walking slowness, 0570

The original Rolandi cohort explicitly includes olive oil within a Mediterranean diet screener and measures walking speed separately. The lifestyle factor combines diet, walking and activity. No individual oil estimate or Parkinson cohort is reported. MED-PARK’s registered diet comparison remains without posted results and does not specify a separate gait velocity endpoint. Neither the later Guk dietary-frequency comparison nor the existing Buriram shared vehicle result identifies a cold-pressed oil gait effect. Claim 0570 remains **Unknown, 50**. The targeted literature searches used relevance-ranked web results without exposed stable totals. ## Black tea and resting tremor, 0904

The MENASA protocol and original supplement ask about black tea separately from black tea with milk. They collect tea frequency and duration and a separate yes/no history of rest tremor. Neither field provides a severity comparison or established preparation. Three scoped registry expressions returned zero.
