The full crossover report and linkage between 2019 cohorts remain unresolved.

The Parkinson cannabis-formulation trial records one constipation event and one anxiety event with an olive-oil vehicle in all groups. It gives no effect of a specific olive oil grade. The prior ground-stigma clinical evidence retains its existing role.
