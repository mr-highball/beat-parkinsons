# Human comparisons and symptom outcomes, November 15, 2026

## 0874, poached trout and depressive symptoms

**Maybe False at 51.** In 96 randomized older adults, the group supplied trout scored 0.17 points lower on the adjusted GDS-15 comparison (95% CI −0.48 to 0.15; p = .295). Cooking method was unspecified. A small Parkinson fish-oil trial also favored lower clinician-rated depression on MADRS and CGI, with an inconclusive Beck score. Another Parkinson omega-3 trial had more responders on the Hamilton scale. Oily-fish advice in angina leaned toward worse mood, and a large marine-capsule prevention trial reported more incident depression or clinically relevant symptoms without a clear mean PHQ-8 change. The supplied-fish estimate and the Parkinson clinician ratings give a minimal lean against worsening. Neither clinical population nor preparation match in one study. ## Other outcome comparisons

- **0025, fresh sage and pain.** The original two-hour throat-pain result favored 15% leaf extract spray against a solvent-matched 0.3% spray. Pediatric postoperative pain favored benzydamine over common sage rinse when both arms also received an oral anti-inflammatory. A high-dose animal safety observation found delayed pain response alongside neurological signs. These related results have very different routes, symptoms and exposures and leave fresh dietary sage and Parkinson pain open. [Clinical spray](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Sage-Pain-Comparisons.md) · [Surgical comparison](../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Sage-Pain-Comparisons.md) · [Animal observations](../../../../research/2025-2029/Source-Text/PMC11949616.txt#L157)
- **0333 and 0934, avocado oil and drooling, rice bran oil and handwriting.** Lower unstimulated salivary flow coexisted with more drooling in a new Parkinson case–control report. The prior oil rinses did not test swallowed avocado oil. The Thai Parkinson trial has a rice bran oil comparator and global MDS-UPDRS, with no separate handwriting score or grade. A recent Parkinson cannabinoid oil trial used medium-chain triglyceride in both arms. - **0502, 0604 and 0758, dates, apricots, pain and apathy.** Final caregiver-rated abdominal pain scores were 0.2 in each Ajwa and no-date group in the prior child study. Existing obstetric studies used syrup or sap. A case after ingestion of an apricot pit addresses obstruction. The Ajwa schizophrenia report found lower composite negative symptoms without an item for passive or apathetic withdrawal. These studies leave the frozen clinical symptom directions unresolved. - **0542 and 0710, strawberry attention and pepper executive function.** A four-berry smoothie favored some attention outcomes. Two separate strawberry powder reports gave inconclusive direct attention comparisons. Two JPFSM student reports combined pepper extract with exercise or mouth rinse, and give no isolated supplement effect. The separate completed UMIN000054104 midlife study publishes no results. - **0810 and 0837, cantaloupe and asparagus fatigue.** Two studies favored a coated concentrate from a proprietary cantaloupe cultivar on some fatigue scores. Other melon products had inconclusive fatigue results, and protective coating is integral to the tested formulation. Shatavari root extract improved fatigue at week eight but not clearly at week four. It uses a different species than culinary asparagus. - **0765, 0878 and 0953, buckwheat attention, orange constipation and barley anxiety.** A buckwheat product crossover gave three versus one participant reporting improvement in nausea and concentration, without a separate concentration contrast. Parkinson orange frequency estimates include no constipation outcomes. Immature trifoliate orange extract reduced a constipation score in an uncontrolled spinal cord injury study. The Wayne State dissertation gives the preparation for the already cataloged cooked barley flour crossover and reports improvement regardless of meal, with statistical tables restricted.
