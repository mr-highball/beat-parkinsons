## Outcome evidence recorded

- The same Parkinson pilot supplied the broad urinary-problem item and aggregate motor outcomes for the ungraded-canola menu. Urgency frequency, daily off time, dyskinesia severity, facial expression, postural instability, handwriting size, voice loudness and freezing frequency need separate measurements. The raw lightheadedness and medication observations add context to 0241. [Parkinson pilot and menus](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Canola-Outcomes.md#symptom-reporting-in-a-parkinson-diet-trial-with-canola-on-the-menu)
- The Happy Nurse registry shows no posted results, but its protocol identifies 47% rapeseed comparator and the authors' published table gives anxiety estimates. The plain-text 2015 protocol and dated 2014 registry snapshot preserve those links locally. Mouse anxiety studies yielded a benefit for ALA-rich rapeseed versus ALA-poor palm during development, greater anxiety-like behavior for prenatal high-fat feeding and for certain rapeseed diets versus sunflower, and no improvement for fortified rapeseed in a metabolic-syndrome model. The directions apply to the individual comparisons. [2015–2019 anxiety papers](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Canola-Outcomes.md) · [Japanese rat study](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Canola-Outcomes.md#high-oleic-canola-and-ordinary-rapeseed-oils-in-an-anxiety-behavior-assay)
- Canola control mobility and gait-balance data were available in the 2022 and 2023 healthy-adult trials. The 2025 muscle-imaging report reused the 2022 cohort. A separate 6-OHDA model used canola as the vehicle during motor testing. [Human gait notes](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Canola-Outcomes.md) · [Imaging follow-up](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Canola-Outcomes.md) · [Motor assay note](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Canola-Outcomes.md#canola-vehicle-in-a-parkinson-model-with-motor-assays)

## Candidate dispositions and pending routes

- PMID 25799588 measured fear-conditioned freezing with fish oil, soybean oil and a synthetic fat mixture. Move to a locomotor freezing paper for 0993. PMID 31680826 measured Parkinson-related degeneration after rapeseed pomace solid extracts. The outcome and exposure differ from the hypotheses. PMID 38111605 is a combined MCT analysis. Keep the original publications as independent units only when trial identifiers and participant methods differ. [Fear experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25799588/) · [Pomace experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31680826/) · [MCT combined analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38111605/)
- The targeted registry also returned NCT05454904, NCT06164613, NCT07055620 and NCT06425094. The first three concern Mediterranean patterns or labor. For the microalgae trial, the published comparator disclosed no edible canola dose. Check registry wording against the paper when screening exposures. [Microalgae trial report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11944429/)
- A same-registration UMIN000046861 report evaluated SF-36 and subjective fatigue. Keep its outcomes in the same study family as the timed mobility report. No anxiety-specific endpoint result was given. [Kojima secondary outcomes](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2023.1296896/full)
- PMID 12510011 is a simvastatin-plus-diet factorial trial in hypercholesterolemic men. Recover a study-arm oil composition, oil-specific contrast and anxiety outcome before treating its general mood results as an independent estimate. Search provider aliases separately for small handwriting to avoid microscopy hits and for freezing of gait to avoid fear-conditioning and food storage. Revisit local electronic Parkinson item datasets or unpublished diary supplements for urgency episode counts, voice volume, handwriting size and off-time hours. Product-grade verification and a composition-matched oil comparator remain separate tasks.
