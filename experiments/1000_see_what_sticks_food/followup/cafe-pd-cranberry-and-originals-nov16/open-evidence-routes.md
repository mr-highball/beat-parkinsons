# Source access and open evidence routes

## Café-PD and caffeine dose escalation

- ClinicalTrials.gov lists a fatigue item for NCT01738178. The journal and institution-hosted PDF give daytime sleepiness and composite UPDRS results. The PMC associated-data section lists the exact 81.5 KB DOCX for Table e-1, which the article cites for adverse events. The PMC direct file returned HTML, the PMC Open Data file was absent, Europe PMC supplementaryFiles reported that this article is not open access in that service, and the publisher supplemental landing returned a restriction. The accompanying comment is separately identified by its own DOI. No fatigue estimate follows from the supplement title or these access responses. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01738178) · [Full manuscript and file listing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5664303/) · [Institutional journal PDF](https://joseph.research.mcgill.ca/publications/Medical/Postuma2017.pdf)
- Altman's 2011 original measures FSS at repeated visits and gives a baseline mean of 35.72 (SD 12.47). It explicitly reports sleepiness and other changes separately, and references Supporting Tables 1 and 2. Public body and known Wiley supplement routes did not supply an authenticated FSS follow-up table in this review. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.23873) · [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01190735)

## Cranberry and pepper formulation

- Gamus's 2015 review now has a confirmed publisher landing, with Hebrew abstract and downloadable-PDF listing. The association download and checked static route returned HTML, not an independently accessible full PDF. The abstract assigns cranberry, depression and Parkinson outcomes to different interventions. The original exact queue disposition remains pending at abstract depth. [Publisher article record](https://www.ima.org.il/MedicineSite/Article.aspx?NewspaperArticleId=3540) · [Download listing](https://www.ima.org.il/MedicineSite/Download.aspx?fi=52618) · [English author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25796668/)
- Twenty related or ambiguous pepper search appearances need a deeper open-original search. The human oral rheumatoid arthritis blend comparator is inconclusive (difference in VAS 3.00 mm, 95% CI −1.82 to 7.84, p=.219, after allocation of 70). Preparation and component effects remain open. The Thai 66-person low-back-pain trial uses multiherb YTPS, massage, or both. Its clinical context differs from oral cracked pepper for Parkinson pain. [Focused review ledger](focused-dispositions.tsv) · [Rheumatoid arthritis original](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38316221/) · [Thai low-back-pain original](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41277745/)

## Saffron and green tea

- The 2024 Bej review exposed abstract and bibliography. The 2024 Abdian review exposed author abstract and indexed introductory text. The 2011 da Rocha article remains abstract depth and concerns Alzheimer disease, mentioning fatigue and Crocus in different contexts. None was promoted to whole-body review. [Bej](https://doi.org/10.1002/ptr.8169) · [Abdian](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0944711324007542) · [da Rocha](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20874701/)
- The 2026 Jiang report describes isolated EGCG in PINK1 flies with motor outcomes, while fatigue appears in traditional-use background. Public author abstract only was verified. The already cataloged Sakakima habitual-tea cohort gives sleep and constipation associations and no fatigue measure. Its reports cannot be counted as independent fatigue results. [Jiang original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42785532/) · [Sakakima original](https://doi.org/10.1177/02601060261493252)
- The official Isfahan version history dates the separate Parkinson Fatigue Scale addition to July 2025. The published peer-review archive includes a version listing fatigue without a confirmed date for first internal inclusion. The new check found no separately verified outcome cohort. [Original registry](https://irct.ir/trial/75378?revision=296350) · [Revision](https://irct.ir/trial/75378?revision=350954) · [Protocol and history](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/15/10/e093167)

## Blueberry and original clinical material

- The 2026 blueberry parent paper's supplement gives baseline characteristics. The trial registry reports changes on MPAM-R of +4 (SD 18) for powder and 0 (SD 12) for placebo, with no inferential comparison. Its outcome description and publication list different score ranges. The nested microbiome report uses the same cohort and has inconclusive between-arm mood interactions. [Official registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05735587) · [Parent paper](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/nutrients/nutrients-18-03694/article_deploy/nutrients-18-03694.xml) · [Original baseline supplement](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/nutrients/nutrients-18-03694/article_deploy/nutrients-18-03694-s001.zip)
- The original restless-legs case body was read. Its listed movement video was not independently reviewed. The body reports no food intervention and no measured dystonia outcome. [Original case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6353513/)
