# Café-PD fatigue, ingredient formulations and original clinical context

Reviewed November 16, 2026 UTC against the original 1,000 frozen claims.

## Apple, black pepper and dried cranberry

Three Europe PMC searches returned 68 appearances across three complete result pages. The apple search returned three related original sources, the dried-cranberry search one plant-genetics result, and the black-pepper search 64 appearances. The four apple and cranberry originals were read at full-text depth. The pepper results received indexed-abstract dispositions, including 20 with citations, preparation details or original results still open. [Query receipts](focused-query-receipts.tsv) · [Identities and ranks](focused-results.tsv) · [Decisions and review depths](focused-dispositions.tsv)

The apple studies used cutting and eating a piece of apple as a standardized motor task. They measured rest tremor separately or used it as a predictor. The 2019 publication reused part of the earlier sample and the 2020 report reanalyzes the related task data. The 2018 paper's 12.7% is the prevalence of measurable rest tremor among 63 participants with measurable peak-dose dyskinesia. It supplies no food-effect estimate. Claim 0833 remains Unknown. [Study identifiers and fit](assessment-additions.tsv)

The uncontrolled Chavanelle pilot lists headache and low back pain among adverse events with a five-extract product containing black pepper. The abstract search returned a human rheumatoid-arthritis blend comparison, a Thai low-back-pain multiherb treatment study, and a human neuropathic-pain exposure that includes piperine. Each mixes ingredients or treatment routes and does not isolate cracked pepper in Parkinson pain. Claim 0312 remains Unknown. [Original source dispositions](focused-dispositions.tsv)

The earlier pending Gamus review has a publisher Hebrew abstract and an exact PDF download listing. The accessible summary discusses depression under acupuncture, Parkinson symptoms under Tai Chi, and cranberry under urinary infections. The linked download returned a web page, leaving the earlier exact body entry pending. Claim 0722 stays Unknown. [Open evidence](open-evidence-routes.md)

## Coffee, blueberry and saffron

The authenticated Café-PD publisher and public mirror identify a table e-1 supplement referred to under adverse events. A registered fatigue item is separate from the published daytime sleepiness result. The original DOCX bytes and a separate group fatigue result were not recovered. Claim 0121 remains Unknown. [Source access](open-evidence-routes.md)

The later blueberry feasibility paper confirms that the previously registered constipation event in the powder arm occurred at about six weeks, was considered possibly related and did not lead to withdrawal. Increased bowel frequency led to a different participant's withdrawal. This is one trial, with no constipation severity measure or Parkinson subgroup. Claim 0746 stays Maybe True at 52. [Revised assessment](assessment-revisions.tsv)

The earlier saffron cross-period observation now distinguishes DASS depression in the original 2024 public registration from the separate Parkinson Fatigue Scale added to the July 2025 public revision. The internal outcome selection date remains unknown. Three pending review originals and the green-tea EGCG fly-study body remain open. [Source routes](open-evidence-routes.md)
