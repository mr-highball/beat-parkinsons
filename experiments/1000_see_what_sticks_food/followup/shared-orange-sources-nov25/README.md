# Shared originals and orange source checks

A November 25 Round 1 review worked through 234 exact claim–source candidates attached to 56 shared publications. Each publication was queued under at least three frozen claims. Full original text supported 222 dispositions in 55 publications, including 66 related contexts and 156 scope exclusions. The project added 70 related assessments and revalidated 64 scorecards against their prior verdicts and anchors.

The replay leaves 31,332 exact pairs across 21,285 source keys and 396 claims. Raw orange and constipation (0878) dropped from 414 to 361 original checks. Raw fig and rest tremor (0559) dropped from 2,338 to 2,333. Ground saffron and smell impairment (0730) cleared its last queued original. The original concerns chemically modified compounds and leaves the frozen hypothesis open. Round 1 has 75 directional and 925 open verdicts.

The 2001 EANM proceedings scan resolves particular uses of oats, egg and orange juice in diagnostic tests. Its page span and erratum still need follow-up before the 12 candidate pairs can be closed. A separate 2018 laboratory table supplies chemical composition measurements for basil and cumin. Four scorecards now identify these as contextual inputs, including the distinction between verbal learning in a purified-compound pilot and executive impairment in Parkinson disease.
