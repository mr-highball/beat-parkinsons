# Cooking-oil comparator follow-up, November 14, 2026

## Primary reports and datasets

| Study family | What the reports establish | Remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ACTRN12617000027314, Phillips Parkinson diet trial | ANZCTR posted the registration in 2017. The 2018 journal article and congress presentation report the same diet assignment. The publisher lists an appendix, two menu files and a CONSORT checklist. The appendix’s single study-results table reports sensitivity analyses on the four MDS-UPDRS part scores. The registry dates both the sharing statement and supplied results to July 16, 2021 and lists no supporting documents. | The explicit individual-participant data response and access terms are unavailable in the accessible views. No item-level file was found in the publisher supplements or conference report. Where individual symptom data become available, verify diary content and actual oil exposure before attributing an effect to oil. |
| CAAE 79991417.9.0000.0089, Brazilian AGE and functional cohort | The 2019 proceedings and 2024 full paper share ethics ID, 20 Parkinson participants, 20 controls, method and identical exposure summary. The 2024 paper adds frying days and measures motor function. It gives r=−.59 for dietary AGE and handgrip while the earlier paper prints r=−.26. | Both coefficients describe overall dietary AGE, not frying. No named oil is reported. The completed 2018 dissertation was identified bibliographically and no public full copy was located. The common sample makes these two publications one study. |
| IRCT20170423033605N2, childhood enuresis | The full license-verified article describes a topical cold-pressed coconut and paraffin comparison and reports 1.81 versus 3.38 bedwetting episodes at week eight. Urgency and abnormal voiding frequency were exclusion criteria. | The oil route, outcome and population differ from 0882. No matching oral study estimator was recovered. The public article verifies the IRCT registration number. |
| Shehata PMID32707436 and Deepika PMID42217587 | Shehata used oral virgin coconut oil and levodopa/carbidopa in an adult rat experiment. Deepika used a hot-processed virgin coconut oil in a rotenone Parkinson model. Both primary abstracts report behavior generally. | The retrieved publisher material did not give a clinical dyskinesia measure or an abnormal-involuntary-movement score. Figures and fuller protocol are open leads. |
| Koyuncu PMID32892250, Parkinson voice | The full published paper lists three-month VHI-10 scores and describes the ketogenic protocol generally. | The paper gives no identifiable oil or refining method and no acoustic or standardized intelligibility outcome for 0963. |
