## 0483 · Ground black pepper and dream enactment

## 0491 · Dried celery leaf and visual hallucinations

The 2016 primary abstract describes mania after celery root extract, with concurrent venlafaxine and St John's wort. A 2022 review describes hallucinations in the same case without specifying their modality or frequency. The full original case is still unavailable. The Phillips trial describes visual hallucinations that resolved following a pramipexole reduction. The menu sources mentioning celery specify stalk or seed, and participant-specific consumption is unavailable. ### PubMed and cited original reports

The two PubMed-only records concern a genetic PARK2 case with hypomimia and an experimental vector using a chicken beta-actin promoter. Both were reviewed at the abstract level. The Renoudet primary abstract describes clinician-observed hypomimia improvement in one patient on a multi-component diet. The body and detailed diet remain restricted. Baroni's omnivorous comparator lists several foods including unspecified meat. Hegelmaier used an ovo-lacto vegetarian diet and reported a global motor score. Neither provides individual chicken consumption or facial-expression scores. [Genetic case](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32647592/) · [Experimental record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42185734/) · [Renoudet original publication](https://doi.org/10.1089/jmf.2012.0060) · [Baroni original publication](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21535916/) · [Hegelmaier full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7072275/)

Two reports in the overlapping Mischley cohort connect chicken frequency to baseline overall symptoms. The 2017 coefficient was +13.4 (95% CI −5.8 to 32.5) per intake category. The 2025 baseline coefficient for less than monthly intake was −64.326 (SE 32.791). Their exposure coding differs. The 2025 change analysis concerns whole-diet adherence. The reports do not provide the preparation or a facial-item comparison.
