# Original access and eligibility decisions

The recovery pass verified 24 previously unavailable whole originals. Twenty-three came from the official article-specific PMC reader and one from the publisher PDF for the adenosine review. The latter covers pages 111–134 and uses SAGE in a cited gene-expression study. The Cochrane pergolide review uses its official first publication date of April 26, 1999 rather than a later indexed version.

The 27 selected checks left open include incomplete meeting collections, scan-only reader fronts and original PDF routes that returned wrappers or access pages. The [access table](retained-access.tsv) records the exact claim/source pairs. A front-matter record or a reference list cannot establish body coverage. The Gamus publisher's title page and issue listing establish a 2015 Hebrew body and a separate English abstract, without public full-body access.

The exact replay separates ingredient references such as verified oil carriers and food-derived compounds from author, publisher, geography, reagent and software matches. A clinical outcome and an unrelated mention of a food do not furnish an exposure-specific estimate.
