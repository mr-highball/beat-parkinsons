# Turkey, shiitake, and arugula follow-up

The November 13, 2026 pass reviewed 13 frozen claims, including ten without a prior study-level assessment. All 13 remain unresolved. Primary records yielded mixed or null cognitive and motor results, adverse reactions, food texture data, and clinical vascular measurements. The records leave a gap between the frozen food preparation and symptom measured in Parkinson's. [Claim search dispositions](claim-searches.tsv) · [Open trails](open-trails.tsv)

## Primary results and access

- The 1989 oral protein challenge reported worse tapping and walking after the larger oral protein load in three people with Parkinson's. The lower-load case did not show this decline. The separate tryptophan depletion trial found mixed reaction and delayed-recall findings without a motor-score change. The 2021 levodopa challenge concerned acoustic speech and medication state. None isolated turkey exposure. [1989 report](../../../../research/1985-1989/Food-Experiment-Turkey-Shiitake-Arugula.md) · [2006 report](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Turkey-Shiitake-Arugula.md) · [2021 source text](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7892955.txt)
- Three primary shiitake studies measured textures of puree with gums, enzyme-modified whole or sliced mushrooms, or material for printing. Some products reached IDDSI Level 5 or 6. The studies report no clinical Parkinson swallowing response. The native archive found no eligible text at the tested PMC13476414.1 metadata endpoint. The 2017 whole-mushroom bowel obstruction report is readable under CC BY-NC and was summarized. [2021 source text](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC8465407.txt) · [2025–2026 studies](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Turkey-Shiitake-Arugula.md) · [2017 report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6688735/)
- Parkinson model studies with lentinan or a lentinan-containing nanocomposite reported behavioral signals. The source summaries distinguish isolated compounds from culinary shiitake. Publisher full-text and supporting-information coverage remains incomplete. The French poison-centre series measured dermatitis after raw or undercooked mushroom. [2024 lentinan report](https://doi.org/10.1021/acschemneuro.4c00164) · [2026 nanocomposite abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42015685/) · [French primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35404185/)
- Rucola appeared in a bundled Parkinson menu without ingredient-specific outcomes. Rocket beverage and arugula extract affected pressure in healthy volunteers. A separate sensory study tested perception of fresh leaf aroma. The 2018 injected erucin study measured rotations in Parkinson-model mice. The 2024 erucin study instead tested polyglutamine-associated outcomes in worms.
