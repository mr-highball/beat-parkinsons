## Primary follow-up

- Walnut motor results depend on model and endpoint. The fly study tested dietary cold-pressed oil and saw better climbing than rotenone alone. The independent cell experiment used culture survival and oxidative stress. Neither estimates rigidity, dystonia, dyskinesia, falls or bradykinesia in people. The whole-diet Parkinson pilot provides clinical composite scores with no item-level effect from walnut oil. [Fly and culture experiments](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#cold-pressed-walnut-oil-and-fly-climbing-after-rotenone) · [Parkinson diet source](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7072275.txt#L123)
- Walnut memory sources were checked against a common endpoint and preparation. An early rat study and later scopolamine studies reported favorable maze or avoidance results. A direct preparation comparison favored aqueous enzyme extraction over cold pressing on its two reported measures, while the cold-pressed versus vehicle contrast requires the full report. In 2025, the delayed multiple-T-maze study found no clear between-diet difference on its primary performance comparisons. The 2018 healthy-adult oil milkshake study reported faster extinction of learned fear and the original mood tables remain unlocated. [Preparation comparison and other models](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#walnut-oil-and-scopolamine-memory-models) · [Later null comparison](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#walnut-oil-and-a-delayed-multiple-t-maze-test) · [Human study and conference](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#walnut-oil-and-learned-fear-extinction)
- The walnut pain study separated its orally tested Soxhlet kernel oil from an ethyl acetate extract and isolated linoleic acid. The oil improved some evoked rodent pain measures. Hazelnut oil was gavaged in an acute gastric injury experiment and applied in a separate postoperative adhesion experiment, neither of which assessed pain severity. The English hazelnut nonmotor query also returned industrial-pressure studies. [Walnut pain model](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#solvent-extracted-walnut-oil-and-evoked-pain-in-mice) · [Hazelnut gastric and surgery experiments](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#oral-hazelnut-oil-and-experimental-gastric-lesions)
- Macadamia gastrointestinal study forms include intragastric emulsion, ingested milkshake blends and mouse gavage. Gastric emptying, mouth-to-caecum transit and the colitis stool component differ from clinical constipation. Exact official ClinicalTrials.gov retrieval verified NCT01510223 first posted January 16, 2012 and NCT02908932 September 21, 2016. Both are completed and lack results in their official record. A separate Osaka UMIN registration focuses on insulin sensitivity. The likely 2011 Limerick thesis and conference bodies remain open. [Official registry fields](../../../../research/2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/NCT01510223.txt) · [RRIPP-3 official registry fields](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT02908932.txt) · [Osaka record](../../../../research/2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/UMIN000013142.txt)
- Ingredient forms account for the main swallowing false matches. The original Teuber study tested residual proteins from oils against sera, not ingested oil. The Danish challenge tested whole roasted hazelnuts. A Dutch challenge included difficulty swallowing on its list of possible allergic symptoms. The macadamia case used meal in the ingested challenge history and oil only in a later-described skin test. A Japanese two-case report included a history of moisturizer use and diagnosed allergy to the nut. A 2023 tactile study applied hazelnut base oil to skin in both arms. [Gourmet oil and macadamia case notes](../../../../research/1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md) · [Whole roasted challenge](../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md) · [Dutch challenge and tactile study](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#hazelnut-challenge-swallowing-symptoms-and-product-distinction) · [Japanese case report](https://doi.org/10.3388/jspaci.37.99)

Additional interactive searches used `fındık yağı`, `huile de noisette`, `マカダミアオイル`, `核桃油`, `胡桃油`, `榛子油` and English clinical synonyms. The Chinese searches found animal learning studies and a distinct hickory oil review. The term “walnut oil” also retrieves `Canarium indicum` cosmetic oil, a different genus. The title search for macadamia and neurobehavior omitted a 2025 rat dichromate injury paper that reported molecular and histological outcomes in the public preview. The available preview gives no cognitive or motor result. [2025 primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40818780/)

The 2025 fibromyalgia article led to UMIN000043490 and the lead author's university dissertation from the 2021 academic year. The registry reports null State-Trait Anxiety Inventory comparisons. The dissertation's raw state anxiety scores and the article's percentage-change analysis had opposite numerical trends without clear differences. The registry, dissertation and article describe the same crossover. A 2020 conference abstract reports a geranium aromatherapy case with little change in STAI and gives no carrier name. The university repository's public posting date and reuse license could not be verified during its service outage. [Registry and dissertation note](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#macadamia-oil-as-a-comparator-in-the-fibromyalgia-trial-registry-and-dissertation) · [Journal study](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#macadamia-nut-oil-in-a-fibromyalgia-aroma-comparison)

The walnut oil bulbar search found a 2026 food engineering experiment. The investigators used walnut oil in a polysaccharide hydrogel for three-dimensional printing. The printed food met IDDSI level 4 on fork and spoon texture tests. They measured no patient swallowing or drooling outcome. [Primary article and supplement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13297913/)
