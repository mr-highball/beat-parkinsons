The cardiac bypass trial reports a favorable bowel result for extra-virgin olive oil versus usual care in its author abstract. The published abstract does not provide separate estimates or scoring detail. The NHANES association uses a whole-diet score and fat ratio. The existing Fernandez Parkinson trial compares immediate and delayed diet counseling, with inconclusive between-group results. These findings preserve a minimal easing tier for claim 0072. Its leading evidence still comes from the earlier comparison between oil grades outside Parkinson disease.

The exact-check records cover pain, apathy, other motor and nonmotor clinical outcomes and ingredient mentions in experimental or diet-pattern contexts. They distinguish action tremor from rest tremor and raw food from analog products, synthetic compounds and experimental vehicle uses.
