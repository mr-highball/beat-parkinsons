# Search trails and access

Oracle revision 103 preceded the primary retrieval. The pinned batch contains 60 verified publication identities, 52 with structured PMC bodies and eight with matching PMC HTML bodies. The short HTML letter PMID 41000058 was checked against its matched citation metadata, complete letter paragraphs and concluding disclosures.

The original-source lookup resolved the locally indexed [Fernandez Parkinson dietary trial](../../../../research/2025-2029/Source-Text/2026-Fernandez-Mediterranean-Counseling-Preprint.txt) and [NUTRI-GUT-PD protocol](../../../../research/2025-2029/Source-Text/PMC13405298.txt). The final Fernandez journal abstract supports the preprint's between-arm conclusion and clarifies the time labeling. The journal body remains pending. Its preprint, journal abstract and registry describe the same trial family.

The [cardiac bypass trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40642904/) required external lookup after an exact Oracle miss. The publisher and NLM routes made the author abstract available and the publisher page includes disclosures. Neither route exposed the full outcome tables. The NHANES article body summarizes the fat-ratio finding in its Table S2 discussion. Supplement routes did not provide its full supplemental table during this pass, and no olive-oil-specific estimate was inferred.

ClinicalTrials.gov API confirmed no posted outcome results for MED-PARK (NCT06705517, recruiting), PRIME (NCT07097103, not yet recruiting), or NUTRI-GUT-PD (NCT07213856, recruiting). The research-status ledger records how these designs differ from an isolated culinary oil comparison.
