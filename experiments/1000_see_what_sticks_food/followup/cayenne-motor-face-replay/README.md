- **0327 dystonia.** One uncontrolled blepharospasm case reports a severity improvement after a transdermal capsaicin patch. Five separately tested healthy adults showed no clear blink-rate effect. Dystonia in Parkinson disease under oral ground cayenne remains open.
- **0520 postural instability.** The topical cayenne and kaolin report directly measured seated balance on a force plate and did not detect a change. The 2018 paper has related acute muscle and sensory outcomes. Clinical standing postural response after ground cayenne remains open.
- **0589 falls.** The original knee injection trial posted 4 of 189 versus 2 of 136 participants with falls under adverse-event collection. The related injection trial does not enumerate a fall row above its table threshold. Neither registration tests ground cayenne or people with Parkinson disease.
- **0673 facial expressiveness.** Healthy spontaneous blinking, involuntary eyelid contraction, capsaicin burn and perception of others' faces are different measures. No branch linked ground cayenne to observed facial expressiveness in Parkinson disease.
- **0798 walking slowness.** The older-adult chili cohort measured timed walks as part of sarcopenia and reported a model for the composite. The controlled extract trial held treadmill speed fixed and measured exhaustion; corrections are linked. Neither directly estimates ordinary clinical walking speed after the frozen culinary preparation.
