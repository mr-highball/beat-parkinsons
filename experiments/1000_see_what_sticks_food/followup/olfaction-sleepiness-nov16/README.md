## Claim 0098

The Parkinson cohort reports an association between whole-diet scores and daytime sleepiness. Its separate analysis of pooled beans uses total symptom burden. A healthy-adult crossover administered boiled black common beans and measured vascular and metabolic responses. The MIND trial in women with diabetes grouped bean intake, and its adjusted daytime dysfunction contrast crosses zero.
