## Claim-level outcomes

- **0011 dried tarragon and facial expressiveness.** The French tarragon primary preservation study measures volatile composition and instrumental taste. The Russian tarragon bar registry measures product ratings and uptake of a marker, without observing facial movement. This remains a search gap with no study-assessment row.
- **0565 dried tarragon and speech intelligibility.** The original German case concerns airway edema after mugwort occupational exposure and separate tarragon skin sensitization. No oral tarragon provocation or measured speech effect was reported. The negative tarragon oil patch-testing lead remains an original-text access gap under PMID 891181.
- **0572 fresh tarragon and freezing frequency.** Injected tarragon oil caused abnormal gait and ataxia during mouse toxicity observation. Isolated estragole given orally produced no detected gross coordination change in healthy mice. The oral extract disease models measured nerve conduction. Neither gait cueing cases nor association studies of olfaction and gait freezing assign tarragon exposure.
- **0763 fresh tarragon and dystonia.** The isolated-compound tissue study measures evoked contractile behavior. The primary species-specific oil experiments use induced seizures or gross motor performance. The Russian thesis table access gaps remain open, with no matched clinical dystonia result.
- **0871 independent update.** The same 2016 human trial now has public endpoint means for both arms. Registry analyses apply each p value within an arm. Neither source gives a posture-specified between-arm change estimate or orthostatic symptom count.
