# PRIME registry and results follow-through

The official record NCT07097103 was checked on November 16, 2026. It was first posted July 31, 2025 and most recently posted July 30, 2026. July 2026 verification gives a status of not yet recruiting. September 1, 2026 is the estimated start and no actual start is given. The archived API response has `hasResults=false` and SHA-256 `50ba47d911e487bd09dd453e7560b391b45c29f78ef78876b4ddb09ba1a37c7b`. Estimated primary completion is July 11, 2029. Its single linked publication is the methods article. [Official trial JSON](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT07097103) · [Original protocol and visit schedule](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-PRIME-Outcome-Schedule.md)

The exact registry identifier and combinations of PRIME, Fognani, Rotondo, Sardaro, Parkinson, Mediterranean diet, microbiome, gait speed and olive oil were searched for result publications. No new primary outcome report was identity-matched. The different PRIME-NL and PRIME-UK programs have overlapping names. The next check should confirm actual start or newly posted results, then compare prespecified constipation, stride and gait speed with ingredient exposures.

Independent cataloged Parkinson comparisons include the Rusch whole-diet trial with an inconclusive between-arm constipation result and the Fernandez 2026 whole-diet trial with an inconclusive bowel-movement result. The MED-PARK record has no posted results as checked. No olive-oil-only arm was identified in these studies.
