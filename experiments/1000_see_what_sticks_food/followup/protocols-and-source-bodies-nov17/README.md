## Ginger pain

Two focused Europe PMC expressions were retrieved completely. The Parkinson, ginger and pain expression returned zero. The expression for pain and fresh, grated or brewed preparations returned 24 appearances, including patents and two primary reports followed to their bodies. These count the specified indexed expressions and leave other providers and citation routes open. [Queries and coverage](focused-queries.tsv) · [Ranked screening decisions](focused-dispositions.tsv)

The fresh-ginger drink study reported incompatible baseline pain values, without a reliable between-arm estimate. A later article reproduced its methods and data and has unverified independence. The separate nursing-student tea study had favorable post-dose comparisons but gave no verified fresh or grated preparation. An exercise-versus-tea preprint had conflicting exercise results and unclear allocation. A 2025 survey listed ginger use but the regression labels could not be reliably matched to ginger. All measured menstrual pain outside Parkinson disease. Claim 0725 stays Maybe True at 51% based on the other evidence already assessed. ## Oregano and stride

An original GSA member describes isolated thymol and autophagy. A later publication by the same group extends the work to aging mice. Treadmill endurance, grip and rotarod outcomes were favorable in the reported comparisons. The work did not test fresh oregano, stride length or Parkinson disease. Overlapping components of these reports are retained as one study family. Claim 0490 stays Unknown.
