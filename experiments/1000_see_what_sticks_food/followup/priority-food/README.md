# Orange, shrimp, and fig follow-up

Reviewed November 12, 2026. The frozen exposures are [raw orange and fall frequency](../../statements/0259.txt), [boiled shrimp and fall frequency](../../statements/0322.txt), and [raw fig and rest tremor severity](../../statements/0559.txt). The search considered both improvement and worsening, and checked food preparation, clinical population, and the outcome actually measured.

## Retrieval

The existing native Free Pascal ingredient tool searched Europe PMC titles and abstracts in six queries. It retrieved all 203 returned appearances across 197 distinct source keys. The unqualified token `fig` was omitted from the ingredient search because it retrieves figure references. The fig query used species, translated names, plural, and multiword food terms. The orange query used sweet-orange names to reduce the Agent Orange collision. Screening considered all returned titles and read abstracts or full text for clinical and plausible food leads. The original expressions have a different scope. They searched article text for broad terms including `orange`, `fall`, and `fig`. The completed original searches returned 20 shrimp rows and 220 fig rows. Earlier partial responses contained 764 of 765 and 3,028 of 3,030 orange rows, and 126 of 127 shrimp rows from the other shrimp expression. A retry with 500 results per page returned HTTP 503 for five original queries across the experiment. A subsequent retry with 100 per page sorted by print date retrieved all 4,385 rows for the broad fig expression in 44 pages. The checkpoint still had the three orange and shrimp count gaps and one outstanding date expression. The completed title-and-abstract queries have narrower scope than those broader records. Independent searches combined the culinary names with `fall frequency`, `recurrent falls`, `fallers`, `gait`, `postural instability`, `levodopa`, `orthostatic hypotension`, `rest tremor`, `tremor at rest`, and study-design terms. Examples included `Parkinson laranja crua quedas`, `帕金森病 虾仁 跌倒`, `帕金森病 无花果 静止性震颤`, `パーキンソン病 イチジク 振戦`, and `Ficus carica Parkinson tremor anjeer`. Citation checks followed relevant food surveys, levodopa interaction papers, and botanical or constituent reviews to primary sources. Registry searches used ClinicalTrials.gov, ChiCTR, ANZCTR, the Iranian Registry of Clinical Trials, and WHO portal indexing. These searches were limited to the records and full texts exposed by the indexes.

The two original fig expressions yielded 4,385 distinct keys because all 220 rows from the first expression also appeared in the second. Screening those titles and abstracts for botanical and food-specific fig expressions yielded no matches. The orange and shrimp checkpoint rows contained four keys that matched the narrower food or juice terms, including a US cohort study of sweetened beverages and frailty in older women and three nonclinical references to shrimp or prawns. This check used indexed titles and abstracts. ## Sources and applicability

### 0259 · Raw orange and falls

- Two Brazilian surveys recorded orange consumption in people with Parkinson’s. The larger US cohort combined orange and juice and analyzed mortality. The prior analysis of the US cohorts studied incident diagnosis. None provided a fall estimate for eating raw orange. [Dietary surveys and cohort](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#orange-consumption-in-parkinsons-diet-surveys)
- The MVP study report found an exploratory correlation of canned fruit frequency with more frequent falls. The preparation and food group differ from raw orange, and the report gives no adjusted orange-specific estimate. The smell-and-freezing study used orange as an odor test item. Its gait phenotype is outside the fall count endpoint. [MVP report assessment](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#food-frequency-associations-in-the-mvp-10-year-report) · [Smell test and freezing study, PMID 34456839](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34456839/)
- The levodopa pharmacokinetic comparison and gastric scintigraphy study used orange juice as a vehicle across their comparisons. An in-vitro stability paper tested pimavanserin in orange juice. The registered ANZCTR study used juice in both arms and enrolled healthy adults to measure learning and cognition. These records give no isolated fruit or juice effect on falls. [Levodopa study](../../../../research/1980-1984/Food-and-Nutrition.md#1981--orange-juice-as-a-levodopa-vehicle) · [Scintigraphy study](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#orange-juice-as-a-scintigraphy-vehicle) · [Pimavanserin bench study, PMID 35107816](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35107816/) · [ANZCTR registration](https://anzctr.org.au/Trial/Registration/TrialReview.aspx?id=392018)
- An isolated vitamin C pharmacokinetic study found no clear levodopa change in the overall sample and increased exposure in participants with lower baseline absorption. A blinded crossover of supplemental vitamin C reported modest functional improvement and no clear change in on–off patterns. Hesperidin patent EP1621201A1 described rat EEG experiments. The newer sweet orange oil paper used cultured cells. [Pharmacokinetic study](../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-PubMed-Followup.md#supplemental-ascorbic-acid-and-levodopa-exposure) · [Earlier crossover](../../../../research/1980-1984/Food-and-Nutrition.md#1983--ascorbic-acid-for-levodopa-onoff-fluctuations) · [Patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP1621201A1/en) · [Cell study, PMID 41366672](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41366672/)

### 0322 · Boiled shrimp and falls

- A 117-person Parkinson’s study reported inconclusive fall comparisons by total protein intake. A separate case–control study defined the recorded shellfish group as snails and clams and assessed hair metals. The protein-and-levodopa chart study reported motor fluctuations after protein-rich meals in a subset of patients and did not test boiled shrimp or count falls by intake. [Falls and shellfish records](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#total-protein-intake-and-falls) · [Levodopa interaction study](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#protein-rich-meals-and-reported-levodopa-fluctuations)
- The directly indexed shrimp paper tested shrimp proteins in an antibody assay. The pawpaw paper used brine shrimp as laboratory organisms. A valbenazine trial reported a shellfish allergy reaction in a participant with Huntington’s disease. Astaxanthin animal work and the EP1867327A1/US2007293568A1 patent family concern an isolated constituent. Astaxanthin registry leads concerned Alzheimer’s disease or spinal vascular disease. [Shrimp protein assessment](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#shrimp-antigens-in-a-laboratory-assay) · [Pawpaw assay, PMID 19711911](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19711911/) · [Huntington’s trial, PMID 37210099](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37210099/) · [Astaxanthin patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP1867327A1/en) · [Alzheimer’s registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05015374) · [Spinal vascular registration](https://www.chictr.org.cn/hvshowprojectEN.html?id=296611&v=1.0)

### 0559 · Raw fig and rest tremor

- The fig ingredient query retrieved an article about the *Ficus* genus and several figure references. Citation follow-up recovered the *Ficus carica* ethanolic fruit-extract experiment in haloperidol-exposed rats and a 2025 bark-extract formulation report. The fruit-extract abstract reports motor coordination, behavior, biochemical assays, and histology without a separate rest tremor result. Its DOI route and exact-title search did not yield the full text. [Fruit-extract record](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#fig-fruit-extract-in-a-rat-model) · [Bark-extract record](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#date-palm-and-fig-preparations-in-animal-models)
- The review of *Ficus* and neurologic disorders cites an isolated luteolin study in microglia for one Parkinson’s mechanism. The traditional Iranian paper is a literature review. An Unani literature table associates fig fruit with general or mental debility and supplies no human Parkinson’s tremor outcome. Human fig-paste and fig trials measured functional constipation or irritable bowel syndrome with constipation. [Luteolin experiment](https://doi.org/10.1080/01480545.2018.1504961) · [Iranian literature review](https://doi.org/10.61186/pbp.7.1.4) · [Unani literature table](https://www.alternative-therapies.com/pdfarticles/10993.pdf) · [Fig paste trial, PMID 27440682](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27440682/) · [Irritable bowel trial, PMID 30318190](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30318190/)

No verified primary human report in this pass matched the frozen preparation and outcome for any of the three claims. The plant and seafood constituents, pooled dietary groups, laboratory exposures, and missing full texts leave the directions open.
