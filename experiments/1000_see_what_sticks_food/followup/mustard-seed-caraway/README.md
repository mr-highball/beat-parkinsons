## Outcomes in people with Parkinson’s

The 2017 Flisar panel included caraway and reported discrimination only for the five-spice combination. A later Belgian study gives a separate dried-caraway item. The 2021 Figshare workbook's Kruidengeuren sheet, with PD status in column B and paired Geur and Correct fields, gives 0/34 correct in Parkinson’s and 5/53 controls. The native Free Pascal reader counts participant rows by disease group and checks the adjacent Correct value for each Karwij answer. Figure 2 and the main text identify caraway as rarely recognized in either group. Dried caraway was a sniffed stimulus. The 2015 Zucco mustard odor study and the 2019 De Rosa mustard flavor study also assess sensory performance. They have no clinical result after dietary ground mustard. [Caraway workbook and paper](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Mustard-Seed-Caraway.md#caraway-as-a-dried-odor-in-parkinson-disease) · [Earlier Flisar panel](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Mustard-Seed-Caraway.md#caraway-in-a-parkinson-spice-discrimination-panel) · [Mustard stimulus studies](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Mustard-Seed-Caraway.md#mustard-as-an-odor-or-flavor-test-stimulus)

Hegelmaier’s Appendix A lists mustard on the multi-item Parkinson diet menu without naming its preparation or plant part. The separately published report contains a total motor score with no item-level rest tremor or postural stability result. Wright’s eight-completer 2021 report used ground white mustard as a myrosinase source in a broccoli seed tea and reported an improvement in urgency and frequency and no improvement in lost sense of smell. The 2024 report uses a related mixture. Among its 17 self-reporting volunteers, 13/14 with urinary urgency reported attenuation, with transient worsening described in some volunteers who exceeded the usual schedule. Table 1 shows one diary with unchanged dyskinesia and handwriting scores. Table 2 reports attenuation in 6/8 with broad sleep impairment and 1/10 with tremor. Tremor subtype, validated insomnia, separately measured letter size, exact cohort overlap, and mustard-only effects are unverified. ## Other primary trails

- The 2015 Paradkar and Sarawade black mustard seed report used oral extract and measured activity counts and block-test catalepsy in drug-exposed rats. An unrelated 2021 study of brown mustard extract used leaves. The two studies supply no human seed comparison or item-level falls, dyskinesia, dexterity, postural or stride result. A separate 2015 injected black mustard extract and 2025-online white mustard oil report give different stress-behavior models. - Sohn’s 2012 screen selected white mustard aqueous extract as one of 12 early yeast growth leads out of 270. The authors subsequently reported five extracts with a significant cytotoxicity reduction. White mustard was absent from those five. Table 1 also separates a positive perilla stem sample from a leaf sample that failed the initial growth screen. The readouts concern viability in yeast expressing human alpha-synuclein. The test material is described as commercially prepared water extract and the accessible method does not establish a culinary preparation. Gul’s 2023 thesis abstract reports ex vivo vascular relaxation from caraway extract, a separate route and endpoint from orthostatic dizziness.
