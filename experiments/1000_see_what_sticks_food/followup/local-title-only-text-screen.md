# Title-only records with archived text

On 2026-11-12, `build/local-article-recheck/claim-local-text-screen.tsv` contained 265 rows classified `title only, needs access` across 36 PMC documents. The unit is a frozen claim paired with a source, so one proceedings bundle can account for many rows. This review examined the archived text for the 112 rows across 27 documents with at least one exposure or outcome hit. Of those, 87 rows across 14 documents matched both sides, 21 matched the outcome alone, and four matched the exposure alone. Of the 153 rows with neither hit, 112 mapped to six books whose archived `.txt` files contained only metadata. The full book PDFs were recovered and searched, and the other 41 rows in five available conference texts received targeted ingredient and alias checks.

The review used the frozen ingredient and symptom, known aliases, relevant preparations and constituent names, and the immediate context in the archived text. For conferences, the review inspected the individual abstract by its heading or number. Publication dates, publisher names, affiliations, figure colors, author names, and whole-bundle co-occurrences could all give lexical matches. The table lists all 87 both-side rows. No individual study supplied a directional comparison for the exact Parkinson’s population, ingredient preparation, and outcome in a frozen claim.
