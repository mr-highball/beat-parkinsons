## Outcomes closest to the hypotheses

- **0668, refined peanut oil and falls.** The TEMPUS-FUGIT protocol specifies 1 g peanut oil as the ENHANce omega-3 placebo and planned falls tracking. The feasibility report and publisher supplement give no arm-level fall counts. A separate contemporary peanut butter trial reports a 1.23-second improvement on five-times sit-to-stand, with inconclusive gait speed and no verified prospective fall estimate. Neither comparison identifies a Parkinson fall effect of refined oil. [Protocol and publication trail](enhance-0668.md) · [Peanut butter outcome](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Pumpkin-Peanut-Oil.md#peanut-butter-and-mobility-tests-in-older-adults-at-fall-risk)
- **0680 and prior 0480, pumpkin oil and speech or pain.** The recovered cancer-treatment trial tested topical 10% cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil with Solcoseryl against benzydamine. The composite PROMS score moved from 81.76 to 24.29 versus 72.06 to 82.35. The paper reports no separate speaking or mouth-pain item and no listener intelligibility score. The NCT04303312 linkage is inferred from study details. [Primary source extract](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Pumpkin-Peanut-Oil.md#combined-cold-pressed-oil-and-solcoseryl-in-oral-mucositis) · [Earlier pain claim review](source-correction-0480.md)
- **0704, baked cod and smell.** Pooled fish intake was associated with lower prevalent impairment, OR 0.65 (95% CI 0.44–0.96). The corresponding five-year incidence estimate was 1.23 (0.75–2.02). Cooking, cod and Parkinson effects were unidentified. [Cohort and both outcomes](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#pooled-fish-and-olfactory-impairment-in-the-blue-mountains-cohort)
- **0703, dark chocolate and speech.** The direct Parkinson crossover found an inconclusive one-hour motor composite contrast of −0.54 points (95% CI −1.50 to 0.42). No speech item or listener intelligibility result was reported. The distinct one-week trial has no posted results. [Original motor data](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#dark-chocolate-crossover)
- **0551, 0608 and 0618.** A perilla-containing tea trial reports within-arm PSQI measurements with no verified change contrast. A three-oil anise combination and its sunflower-oil control both showed lower insomnia ratings. The whole-macadamia study gives a Bristol p value without direction, and its companion and supplements give no bowel values. Each remains indirect for the frozen exposure and clinical outcome.
