## 0693 baked tofu / drooling
NEW Zhang et al. Food Chemistry electronic publication 2026-03-30, PMID41931940 DOI10.1016/j.foodchem.2026.149088, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41931940/ and https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S030881462601246X . Author abstract compares in-vivo chewing of standard tofu and 15% or 50% soybean residue (okara) added tofu, reporting increased salivary secretion rate with okara formulations. This concerns acute saliva secretion while chewing, not saliva escaping, baked tofu or Parkinson disease. Exact participant count, numeric arm estimates and preparation require original body; publisher returned 403 in this pass. Do not interpret increased flow as increased drooling.
Existing 2026 MDS tofu-diagnosis observational association is not a drooling measurement. 2012 swallowing food physics original https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jisdh/23/4/23_195/_pdf includes tofu oral processing; needs specific clinical item comparison, not simply food texture. Contrast speech/swallowing measures against drooling occurrence.

## 0606 ground anise / apathy
Existing Ghoshegir 2014 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4333517/ and original PDF https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/3260/2e5af6df31936b776cf4ae1ae9550eed9dee.pdf; 47 functional-dyspepsia patients anise seed powder 3 g after each meal vs 60 cornstarch for four weeks, with week-12 follow-up. Table 2 SF-36 vitality baseline 51.3 vs 52.8; week 12 74.5 vs 35.7, between-arm p=.0001, with marked worsening in placebo arm. This is vitality and GI symptom quality of life, not validated apathy or Parkinson disease. The January 2015 report shares registration and sample and is same cohort. Existing study text already at research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Nigella-Anise.md; propose related context only with no fatigue-to-apathy equivalence or standalone direction.

## 0540 ground paprika / orthostatic lightheadedness
Existing DOI10.1080/19476330903274286 compared smoked and unsmoked oral paprika in healthy men; supine systolic pressure changes little, with no upright response or lightheadedness. UMIN000056794 measures exercise BP for xanthophyll capsules, not culinary paprika, no numerical results. Blood pressure alone is not the claimed symptom and no opposing or favorable food clinical finding isolated.

## 0618 cold-pressed macadamia oil / constipation
Existing NCT01510223 mixed oil shakes gastric and mouth-to-caecum transit, no stool-specific endpoint; PMID39974866 colitis mice stool composite concerns diarrhea and bleeding. NEW 2020 conference abstract https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7258298/ randomized 35 adults at cardiometabolic risk to eight-week whole mixed raw/roasted macadamia nuts (15% calories) and usual diet crossover. Self-reported bowel frequency did not significantly differ; Bristol stool type differed (p=.049) but direction and quantitative values not given. Whole nuts include fiber and are not pressed oil; no Parkinson disease, constipation-specific score, or verified benefit/harm direction. Full independent paper or protocol linkage remains open.
