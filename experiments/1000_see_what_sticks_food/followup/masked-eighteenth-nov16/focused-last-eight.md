# Eighteenth masked transfer review

All eight claims were considered at Unknown (50) with the frozen directions masked. The 16 existing assessment rows were reviewed with their original sources and related claim notes. Seven new assessment proposals are staged, including a separate recovered publication for 0680. One wording correction affects the existing 0703 cocoa-beverage row. A separate recommendation updates an older pending 0480 record for the recovered oil formulation and published result. This was a focused literature and provenance review. It does not replace the experiment’s full provider-page receipts.

| Claim | Closest evidence and competing result | Masked transfer decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0704 · Baked cod and olfactory impairment | The 2015 Blue Mountains cohort compared pooled habitual fish intake with objectively classified smell impairment. Top-quartile prevalence OR 0.65 (0.44–0.96) and mild prevalence OR 0.50 (0.31–0.82) suggest lower impairment. Five-year incident OR was 1.23 (0.75–2.02), and long-chain n−3 intake was inconclusive. Ådén 2011 contains a PD cohort and primary smell testing, with published nutrient-density results and no cod estimate. Postviral omega-3 plus training favored odor threshold in a small pilot. A larger post-COVID randomized supplement trial gave an inconclusive identification difference. | Prevalence, incidence and change after supplementation test different effects, with possible reverse causation from smell function to dietary choices. Fish intake pools species and cooking. In particular, evidence from isolated fish oil does not calibrate a serving of baked lean fish. A fish odor stimulus in a diagnostic test is a separate exposure. |
| 0703 · Plain dark chocolate and speech intelligibility | The 2012 Wolz randomized 200 g plain dark vs cacao-free white chocolate in 26 people with PD. One-hour composite UPDRS III difference −0.54 (−1.50 to 0.42). Registered seven-day dark chocolate crossover is a distinct protocol with no posted outcomes. | UPDRS III includes speech but its published total cannot recover the item’s direction. The primary paper reports no listener intelligibility test. The feasibility cocoa beverage trial measured fatigue and fatigability. No distinct speaking outcome from a whole food comparison was verified. |
| 0551 · Dried shiso and insomnia | Earlier extract and inhaled-oil animal models report sleep-stage or drug-induced sleep outcomes. Bormate’s 2023 thesis and 2024 article form a separate fermented extract line with oral EEG/EMG NREM and induced-sleep effects. The 2019 patent reports a larger effect with fermented extract than unfermented. The 2025 depression trial reports secondary sleep improvement in a waitlist comparison of a multiherb formula. Official KCT0012330 details a perilla+peppermint crossover and currently reports no results. | No verified human contrast isolates shiso for insomnia. In the healthy-adult trial with the closest food matrix, orally consumed dried leaf powder was tested for blood pressure and metabolic markers. Sleep sedation in rodents does not calibrate human insomnia symptoms at a culinary dose, and combination efficacy is unassigned. |
| 0539 · Dried summer savory and depressive symptoms | The cisplatin rat studies use a methanolic extract. Cisplatin shortened immobility and delayed its first occurrence, and the intermediate savory dose moved the measures back toward control. The assay authors flag anxiety and movement as alternative explanations. The related 2018 tissue, 2019 behavior and later suspension reports should be treated as a potentially overlapping study family. Dried leaf or whole-plant human trials measure osteoarthritis pain or reflux. | Restoration of altered immobility after toxicant exposure does not tell which way depressive symptoms change after consuming dried savory in PD. Human work with marjoram, other savory species, or dried savory for pain/reflux cannot assign this role. |
| 0636 · Toasted fennel and gait freezing | Hegelmaier 2020 Appendix A lists fennel seed in the shared vegetarian package. Both patient groups received the package and the publication reports composite UPDRS III. The reserpine, 6-OHDA, MPTP and LPS studies measured gross movement or balance on motor tasks. The 6-OHDA study’s peri-lesion course improved some measures while the post-lesion course had no clear effect. The fennel-oil zebrafish study produced no clear immobility effect. | A rodent coordination, open-field or bar test is insufficient to identify discrete gait freezing. Fennel preparation and individual exposure remain unspecified in the only close PD diet source. It used the old composite, with no separately observable freezing result. |
| 0554 · Smoked paprika and drooling | Bolus and capsaicin stimulation studies show increased saliva in healthy people. Historical paprika study p. 222 documents a rationale and earlier saliva findings, with remainder gated. Menicagli examined protein precipitation outside the body in saliva donated by three people. The selected studies with isolated capsicum stimuli in PD assessed different functions including swallowing and cough. | Secretion, extracted salivary protein, oral clearance and involuntary external drooling differ. In Parkinson disease drooling can coexist with limited saliva production because clearance is affected. Whole smoked paprika can vary in pungency. No original study located here measures the joint preparation and patient outcome. |
| 0678 · Refined rice bran oil and olfactory impairment | A trained panel characterized the aroma of rice bran oil during eating. Koeda’s healthy crossover found Boso Komeabura odor rated 0.4 vs 2.9 for hinoki. Aoyagi’s separate dysphagia cohort used the rice oil as a smell stimulus and measured saliva or water swallowing. Manufacturer information supports a refined product family. | Test-stimulus intensity and acceptance are not measures of participants’ odor identification or hyposmia after habitual consumption. Oil roasting chemistry and electronic-nose work describe the product. None of these tests estimates participant olfactory impairment after eating refined oil. |
| 0680 · Toasted pumpkin seed oil and speech intelligibility | Hegelmaier’s mixed PD diet lists pumpkin seed oil. Composite UPDRS III includes speech, with no discrete item result or oil grade. Zaki 2024 used a topical preparation with 10% cold-pressed oil plus Solcoseryl for cancer-treatment mucositis. Aggregate PROMS improved versus benzydamine, whose arm had burning-related attrition. The source instrument includes difficulty speaking and restriction of speech from mouth lesions. | No item result or listener-scored intelligibility was published. The chronic disease and mucosal injury mechanisms differ, and combined active ingredients and product grade prevent attribution to toasted oil. Rodent tongue histology and the chemical analysis of roasted-seed oil supply no speech performance result. |

The later cohort, completion and outcome remain unresolved. Its cohort neither duplicates nor independently replicates a demonstrated insomnia outcome by endpoint.
