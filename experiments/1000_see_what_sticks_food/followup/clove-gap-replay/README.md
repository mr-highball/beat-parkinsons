## Outcome boundaries and open leads

- In the 2024 randomized Lomi study, gargled clove oil reduced incidence of sore throat after intubation at six hours. Hoarseness was bundled into grade 3, while the severity contrast combined grades 2 and 3. Full paper tables, isolated hoarseness counts and measured voice loudness were not available in the accessible report. Seek the full article and independent voice measures if they were collected. The 2025 lozenge registration lists three arms and no public results. It has no acoustic or intelligibility endpoint.
- Bell's 2017 food experiment verifies consumed ground clove in an apple-based snack with three other spices. Its increase in the immediate satisfied rating for the 5% blend does not give a sustained apathy score. The 2018 Masala study pairs Parkinson apathy with composite odor scores. Its accessible abstract does not expose odor-level results or clove preparation. The 2026 Tağraf plant-rich intervention measured Starkstein apathy, but the published diet and registry do not name clove. Individual records are not public. NCT07599930 is a separately cataloged mixed-spice snack protocol with cloves and HADS or sleep outcomes in perimenopausal women, with no Parkinson apathy comparison.
- Figure 6 in Dou reports MDS-UPDRS part totals, including Parts I, III and IV, but provides no separate apathy, speech, facial expression or time-spent-OFF estimates. The inhaled eugenol was one of four scents. Preserve any eventual item-level data as indirect because the trial neither ingested ground clove nor isolated the scent. MDS-UPDRS clinical speech item 3.1 and facial expression item 3.2 also differ from direct decibel measurement or a listener comprehension experiment.
- The 2017 clove-bud KD5040 formula and 2011 isolated eugenol studies use levodopa in experimental animals. Neither measures daily motor OFF hours in humans. The on-treatment human dietary literature and the 1989 Bellonnet diet/motor-fluctuation thesis need ingredient-level records before they could address clove. The observed clove-oil absorption study transferred echinacoside, while the rat jejunal study in this packet measured alanine. Neither estimates levodopa absorption or motor fluctuations. A 2025 Chinese UPSIT longitudinal study reported fluctuation of Parkinson nonmotor symptom items, but its primary Methods list 40 odors without clove.
- The masked-hexanal studies used isolated eugenol as odor control. Face ratings were judgments of other people's images, with participants' own expression unmeasured. Non-Parkinson odor-evoked facial expression studies checked during this pass did not establish a clove intervention for the frozen phenotype.
- The Lomi primary full report, Masala body/item-level methods, Bellonnet print thesis, ingredient-level diet diary data and any eventual lozenge results are open trails. No source in this stage supports assigning a true or false direction.
