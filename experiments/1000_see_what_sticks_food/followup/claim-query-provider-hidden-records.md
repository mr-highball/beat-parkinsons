# Europe PMC orange and shrimp count gaps

The November 12, 2026 recheck reproduced all three differences from the original audit. On each PMC-restricted query, a single request with `pageSize=1000` could hold every reported match. The `idlist`, `lite`, and `core` responses still returned 159 of 160 records for `0259-1`, 416 of 418 for `0259-2`, and 27 of 28 for `0322-2`. The identifier responses reconcile with the previously paginated sets. [Original query audit](claim-query-audit.tsv) · [Earlier source-partition recheck](claim-query-provider-count-gaps.tsv)

Nonoverlapping `PUB_YEAR` ranges and their complement confined the gaps to 2026. Including the returned recent `EXT_ID` values matched all 50, 73, and 10 expected rows. Excluding those same IDs left reported counts of one, two, and one, with no result objects. The residual counts matched `EXT_ID:PMC*`, `PMCID:PMC*`, `OPEN_ACCESS:Y`, and `HAS_PDF:Y`. `FIRST_PDATE` ranges separated the broader orange query’s two counted matches. Bisection on the indexed external identifiers narrowed each one-hit residual to an exact PMCID. For each appearance, the original expression conjoined with `SRC:PMC AND EXT_ID:<PMCID> AND PMCID:<PMCID>` returned HTTP 200, one count, and zero result objects. [Exact identifier and source ledger](claim-query-provider-hidden-records.tsv) · [Europe PMC search field reference](https://europepmc.org/docs/EBI_Europe_PMC_Web_Service_Reference.pdf)

Two public articles account for all four appearances. The two orange queries matched a preprint on astrocyte modulation during intracortical microstimulation in awake mice. Its full text refers to Parkinson’s in neurostimulation background and to neural activity rising and falling. The index matched `BODY:orange` and `BODY:raw`, while the public cloud text and XML yielded neither literal term. The broader orange and shrimp queries both matched a South Florida groundwater study, which uses orange as a figure color, discusses shrimp fisheries and falling water levels, and cites an author named Parkinson. Neither study tested a food intervention or Parkinson’s falls. [Astrocyte preprint and full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13733783/) · [Groundwater article and full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13646008/)

An exact `PMCID` lookup returned the public MED bibliography for each identifier. The corresponding exact `EXT_ID` lookup returned one count and no row, and the standalone `SRC:PMC AND EXT_ID` lookup returned zero. The MED entries also returned zero when conjoined with their original query expressions. An XML-format core request for each PMCID reproduced the one-count and empty-list behavior in an original-query scope. NIH reports one public version for each article, and both versioned cloud text objects were retrieved. The groundwater record’s hidden index entry matched `FIRST_PDATE:2026-01-01`. The public MED record and full text date publication to October 8. The public identifier reconciliation closes the four gaps for evidence screening. The original PMC result-object shortfall remains in the raw API audit.
