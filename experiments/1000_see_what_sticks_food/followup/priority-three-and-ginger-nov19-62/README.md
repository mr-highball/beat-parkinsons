# Ingredient preparations and symptom measurements

Twelve original articles resolve 74 previously queued claim–source pairs. Eleven bodies came from the official PMC XML. The matching ginger review's XML lacked a body, so its full original HTML was checked separately. Twenty-eight pairs retained related evidence and 46 lacked a matching exposure and symptom comparison. The source decisions do not change the frozen claim verdicts. A [perimenopause trial](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2021-Lopresti-Affron-Perimenopausal-Mood.txt) reported better energy/fatigue scores over time within its standardized saffron extract group. Change between groups was inconclusive. The cited [four-herb comparison](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC5437055.txt) included ginger and cinnamon alongside saffron and Tribulus, with outcomes for the combined formula. The [EGCG mouse study](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC11060057.txt) recorded longer rotating-rod latency after injected pretreatment at one month (p<.05). Fatigue is part of the equipment label. The study evaluated motor behavior and tissue outcomes. The [ginger review](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC9578130.txt) traces the lack of clear prevention of post-levodopa abnormal movements to an already cataloged zingerone experiment.

The [official ChiCTR record](../../../../research/2025-2029/Source-Text/Trials/ChiCTR2500096034.txt) lists swallowing function and drooling severity as primary outcomes. The journal article names the SCS-PD drooling measure as primary, gives a later conduct window than the initial plan and includes orange juice within program guidance. The [qualitative journey map](../../../../research/2025-2029/Source-Text/PMC12999412.txt) shares the registration and provides patient-experience context. No separate fruit exposure was tested.

Two PubMed searches and a ClinicalTrials.gov query covered four returned memberships. The direct Ginger-PD record remains recruiting, with active ginger extract in both arms and no posted comparative results. Twelve priority-three pairs remain queued. These include the original Wood, Simon and Jiang bodies that could not be authenticated at full-text scope.
