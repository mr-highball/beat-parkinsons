# Dill leaf outcomes and trial follow-up

Searching by IRCT20120704010181N12 recovered the current registry project after the legacy numeric path led to a different trial. The original diabetes paper reports three of 21 participants with esophageal motility complaints initially and five of 21 after dried leaf capsules. Placebo gave five of 21 at both visits. The paper gives within-arm p-values and no between-arm comparison. [Dill results and registrations](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Dill-Leaf-Outcomes.md) · [Exact registry search](irct-exact-search.tsv)

The official Thai registration lists DASS-21 and Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index outcomes after six weeks of an extract beverage. The completed registry gives no posted results and leaves the plant part unclear. Separate rodent extract studies measured rotarod performance during alcohol withdrawal and offspring developmental reflexes after maternal exposure. The 2025 newborn study's institutional record provides metadata and an abstract. Its exact assays and group data remain under review. [Thai export receipt](thai-exact-trial.tsv) · [Rodent motor studies](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Dill-Leaf-Outcomes.md)

The nine initial Europe PMC full-metadata queries returned HTTP 503s or receive timeouts. Identifier replays reconciled the hand, psychiatric and stress expressions at 0, 4 and 44 appearances. Six expressions still need an accessible route. Three PubMed Parkinson expressions completed. The broadest recovered two existing papers that use dill pickle as an odor-test stimulus. Their outcome measures concern smell identification and dopaminergic imaging.
