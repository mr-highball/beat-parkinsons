# Chive preparation, constituent and anxiety measures

The oral common-chive leaf extract experiment measured sensorimotor outcomes and memory after cerebral ischemia. The powdered onion experiment measured anxiety-related behavior under a different Allium species and plant part. Purified oral ferulic acid increased open-arm time in one experiment and increased dark residence while reducing locomotion in another. The 2023 dark-compartment result and the authors' interpretation should both be retained. [Leaf and earlier compound studies](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Chive-Anxiety-Followup.md) · [Dark-compartment results and assay validation](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chive-Anxiety-Followup.md#oral-trans-ferulic-acid-and-dark-compartment-residence)

The Simran supplement identifies open-arm entries and gives neurochemical correlations without treatment-arm effects for that measure. The Rossato trial reports open-arm and anxiety index comparisons against the accelerated-aging vehicle group with no direct pairwise comparison between nanoencapsulated and free compound for these outcomes. The Deng public abstract gives direction in an inflammatory mouse model with exact group data still inaccessible. Clinical anxiety after dried chives in Parkinson disease remains unresolved.
