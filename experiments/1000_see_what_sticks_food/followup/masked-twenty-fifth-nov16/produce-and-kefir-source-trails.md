## 0950: plain kefir and fatigue

New related candidate Smoak et al., 2021, PMID34519716, DOI 10.1249/MSS.0000000000002690, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34519716/ . Twenty-four cancer survivors (9 men, 15 women; mean age 61 ± 9.9) in a structured exercise program, 8 oz kefir after each of three weekly exercise sessions for 12 weeks versus exercise alone. Author abstract reports fatigue improvement 39.3%, p=.017; the abstract alone does not establish whether p is an interaction, or whether the kefir was plain. A 2026 secondary review https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12843756/ reports the revised Piper fatigue scale, control improvement 5.1%, group-by-time p=.030 and pooled time p=.017. These later numerical distinctions are secondary reports, pending original article tables. It describes a nonrandomized comparison; lack of placebo and Parkinson population are material. Publisher https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000002690 original article body not yet recovered; plain versus flavored product is unverified. Nonrandomized selection, exercise, formulation and the different clinical population limit transfer.

Author Smoak's institutional 2019 dissertation record https://digscholarship.unco.edu/dissertations/644/ describes same intervention and investigators, 19 cancer survivors, Piper fatigue group-by-time p=.002. It is likely an earlier subset of the 24-person paper, not an independent trial, although participant-level identity is not documented. Record date created 12-2019, embargo 12-2021; do not assume 2019 public date. PDF link https://digscholarship.unco.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1646&context=dissertations returned HTTP 403. Do not merge its statistics with the later cohort.

Official UMIN000049551, https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000056148, first disclosed 18 November 2022, 75 healthy adults assigned 1.0 g kefir powder, 0.4 g kefir powder, or placebo daily for 12 weeks. Physical-fatigue VAS is secondary, not the primary PSQI; record last modified 2025-11-21 lists publication unpublished and no results. Dried powder is not necessarily plain liquid kefir. Already catalogued 2021 SYNKEFIR 16-person crossover measures exercise time to exhaustion, not chronic symptom fatigue.

## Other assigned foods

Its 2023 Koempel report uses eight-foot walk, baseline speed subscore 2.6 ± 1.3 among 28; 82% attrition prevented follow-up comparison. Plain blackberry consumption was documented in a plate-waste exercise, but gardening and educational coexposures and missing longitudinal comparison prevent a food effect. This is already catalogued in research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries-Source-Followup.md. Rat blackberry feeding and motor tasks are separate preclinical context.
