# Pinto beans in Parkinson symptom research

The Fox cohort evaluates whole-diet score associations with insomnia and drooling. Its aggregated symptom score includes handwriting, and its diet items pool beans. The source gives no individual boiled or canned pinto-bean exposure or corresponding symptom comparison. [Primary study and supplementary bean analysis](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Pinto-Endpoints.md)

The Parkinson registry hit uses kidney beans as objects moved with a spoon. Europe PMC saliva and related results include lectin tracers, plant assays and food-model terminology.
