# OpenAlex egg, bell pepper and cauliflower search replay

The six expressions from the November 13 plan reconcile to 75 appearances. The first replay completed four expressions and was rate limited on two. A second run recovered all 28 appearances for those two expressions. The native validation checks submitted text, reported counts, within-query identity and rank, and each authored screening row. Sixty appearances were excluded within scope, five were related, and ten remain pending. Some source identities recur across the searches. The classical egg meal hypotheses and hemiplegia dysarthria oil report describe different exposures. Anda thylam came from egg yolk but the deposited abstract does not establish the administration route. Sun's abstract gives an egg-to-osteosarcopenia association and gait by diagnosis. The accessible supplement is a reviewer response. The original article could still clarify whether it measured gait by egg intake. ISRCTN13356971, the 2018 Taso preprint and the Liao journal abstract share a nine-person protocol and strongly match as one cohort. The record calls the registration prospective despite dates after study completion. The measured effects concern exercise metabolism, with no verified clinical fatigue comparison. Confirm the explicit registry link in the gated journal body if it becomes available. A separate ACTIFOX report with a fatigue endpoint is already catalogued.
