# Claims 0067 and 0192 search record and open questions

Reviewed 2026-11-14 US Eastern. The native provider receipts use 2026-11-15 UTC. Claim 0067 states that raw mango reduces insomnia severity in Parkinson disease. Claim 0192 states that raw mango worsens fatigue severity in Parkinson disease. Both remain Unknown. A direct comparison would identify consumption of uncooked Mangifera indica fruit and give a Parkinson-specific result for insomnia severity or fatigue severity.

The population and exposure closest to the frozen statements were the 79 patients with Parkinson disease in the Recife 2020 thesis and 2022 journal report. A mango-specific FFQ line and a 24-hour recall are verified. The authors administered only UPDRS II/III. The appended full questionnaire includes a binary item for insomnia or hypersomnolence under Part IV. This was outside the domains used, and the item would not have separated insomnia severity. Motor-examination anchors with fatigue during repeated movements are distinct from fatigue severity. The journal and originating dissertation are one cohort.
