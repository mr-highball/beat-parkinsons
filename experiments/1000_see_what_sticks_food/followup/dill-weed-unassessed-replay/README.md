## Endpoint checks

- Rest tremor and dystonia. No direct phenotype-specific human Parkinson comparison surfaced. In the stroke article, Table 8 used aggregate NIHSS and the report did not break out a resting-tremor or dystonia item. The animal sowa paper evaluated withdrawal locomotion and rotarod, and hallucinations appear in the description of human alcohol withdrawal. The Sharma neuroblastoma paper primarily tests a cell stress model. An isolated phellandrene paper (PMID 39886788) is indexed and described as a review and does not establish an original human dill study. Visceral ileum contractility addresses a different muscle system and exposure route.
- Fatigue. The original acute-COVID formula paper has a directional result for the entire formula with P=.118 and conflicting claims of statistical significance in its abstract. The cooked soup case has actual soup consumption and repeated weakness/lethargy scores, but neither the recipe option chosen nor the component effect is known. The case table also mentions fatigue later. The 2002 tablet trial has a potentially opposed adverse-complaint signal without counts, composition or comparative fatigue results. In the dysmenorrhea report, the indexed abstract attributes systemic fatigue improvement to cumin and symptom relief of pain to dill seed. The exercise crossover provides timed endurance and a dill-brine product label. The previous topical hemodialysis dill paper used ItchyQoL and PSQI with no separate fatigue measurement. The public Thai beverage registration has no fatigue-specific outcome or posted results.
- Visual hallucinations. Bibliographic and regional poison searches separated dill fruit, seed, leaf oil chemistry, allergy, mislabeled plants and product contamination. In the Najem ethnobotanical primary, the voucher row describes oral fruit use. Its literature-toxicology entry credits Ouahdi, and the multi-plant narrative's hallucinations cannot be assigned to the dill row. No accessed source gives a visual-hallucination case series or frequency under verified fresh dill exposure. The actual IQF regulatory notice supplies no patient symptom records. A 2025 Ras El Hanout survey shows dill occurrence in multi-plant mixtures and reports the dill allergy literature, while hallucination entries concern other plant rows.
- Dexterity. The 2024 mixed stroke trial records aggregate neurological outcomes and everyday feeding or dressing independence without finger performance. The 2025 sowa withdrawal primary has only broader model coordination. The 2023 registered older-adult study confirms actual dill as one of several meal seasonings and measures consumption and liking or flavor. The Michigan Parkinson Foundation 2024 newsletter, printed page 5, has a fresh chopped dill recipe without participant records. The Parkinson dill-pickle odor probes (PMID 17508142 and 18838108) do not establish dietary exposure.
- Drooling. The closest local authentic dried-leaf capsule source groups reported gastrointestinal symptoms without saliva volume or drooling. The 2000 food-allergy case describes tongue and throat reactions, with fresh dill specified for skin testing. The indexed dental-gel source PMID 40951559 evaluated topical antimicrobial activity with no Parkinson salivary outcome.
