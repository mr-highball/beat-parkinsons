The complementary ClinicalTrials.gov API search `walnut oil` returned 38/38 records with no next page. `Juglans regia pain` returned 0/0; this narrow result is not disproof. Registered arm verification distinguishes mixed diets, whole walnuts and rats from assigned human oil intake. The earlier component crossover's posted results add nonsystematic diarrhea after a large solvent-extracted oil dose, not a pain estimate. The current MedCan 3 registration separately lists a mixed placebo including cold-pressed walnut oil and planned symptom outcomes, with no verified results.
