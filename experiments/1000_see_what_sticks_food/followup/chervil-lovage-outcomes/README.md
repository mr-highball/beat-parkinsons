# Chervil and lovage leaf claim follow-up

On 2026-11-13 the review checked ten chervil and ten lovage leaf hypotheses. All 20 retain Unknown. The batch records 38 claim and study assessments. The dated catalog has four notes and five licensed primary texts. [Claim-by-claim search map](claim-searches.tsv) · [Eight open trails](open-trails.tsv)

## Primary evidence

- The Parkinson menu in Hegelmaier's appendix names both herbs. The report gives global motor score changes in diet-alone and diet-plus-enema groups. The menu contains no separate herb exposure, fresh or dried status, or frozen endpoint estimates. The French constipation handout lists chervil among fiber-containing herbs without patient results, while a separate garden chervil soup allergy report describes acute diarrhea and upper-airway symptoms. [Parkinson diet and outcomes](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chervil-Lovage-Outcomes.md#2020-parkinson-diet-appendix-and-individual-symptom-coverage) · [Handout and allergy case](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Umbellifer-Herbs.md)
- Glasgow researchers tested tomato, onion and lovage soup with and without inulin in an acute crossover and a separate six-week trial. The acute report included urinary metabolites and a lovage tracer. The six-week report confirms fresh lovage as an input to cooked, canned soup and records pooled gastrointestinal distress and reflux. Neither report isolates lovage or measures the Parkinson symptoms. A separate Iranian registry and publication concern lovage syrup or extract in cystinuria. [Registrations and acute results](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Chervil-Lovage-Outcomes.md) · [Six-week feasibility report](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chervil-Lovage-Outcomes.md#2020-feasibility-and-adverse-events-in-the-six-week-lovage-soup-trial)
- An isolated culinary-species root arm reduced mechanical pain-like behavior in rat cystitis. Tested dried lovage leaf extracts altered inflammatory enzyme activity in vitro, and the fresh-leaf paper used simulated digestion. Two rat neuroinflammation studies considered spatial memory after lovage extract, with exercise also included in the second study. Their public abstracts do not establish the plant organ or a human attention outcome. [Root pain comparison](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Chervil-Lovage-Outcomes.md#2019-lovage-root-and-pain-like-behavior-in-rat-cystitis) · [Dried leaf and animal studies](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chervil-Lovage-Outcomes.md)
- The apparently relevant Chinese Parkinson patent and Zhichan powder paper concern chuanxiong, the Sichuan lovage rhizome name collision. The Taiwanese insomnia prescription study also identifies Sichuan lovage rhizome, and its design addresses prescription patterns. A German toxicology report confirmed aconitine after monkshood leaves were confused with culinary lovage. Garden chervil extract and radical-scavenging research used lab assays and supplied no symptom outcomes.
