# Safflower comparator follow-through

The Huntington and Baylor trials each give a verified oral exposure but resolve different questions. The eight scoped Parkinson hypotheses remain Unknown. The grade in every trial remains unspecified and evidence derived from a total score cannot establish the change in one of its items. [Claim rationales](claim-rationales.tsv)

| Study | Catalog records | Fit to the experiment |

Both groups received triheptanoin in the second half of the study, and the post hoc 12-month REV-HD comparator belongs to another study. |
| Baylor 2020 pilot, PMID 34705542 | [2021 report and table check](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Comparator-Trials.md#baylor-randomized-pilot-of-singing-self-perception) | Seven safflower and nine fish-oil completers. The voice handicap interaction's p=0.046 is nominal against the prespecified Bonferroni threshold of 0.017. Across-group change over time was p=0.005. |
| Baylor 2021 singers, NCT05141045 | [2021 registration](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Comparator-Trials.md#baylor-college-singers-registration), [2023 paper](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Comparator-Trials.md#baylor-alternating-allocation-safflower-comparator-and-voice-range) | One subsequent study. The paper describes alternating assignment and prioritizes Singing Voice Handicap Index. The registration lists randomization and prioritizes Evaluation of Ability to Sing Easily. The two measures yield different comparisons. Safflower mean change was −1.23 dB for maximum singing sound pressure and +1.50 dB for available sound pressure range at minimum pitch, with each within-arm interval crossing zero. |
| Baylor professional voice users, NCT07800702 | [2026 registration](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Safflower-Comparator-Trials.md#baylor-professional-voice-user-trial-registration) | A separate registered study plans vocal aerodynamics and reports no results. Its phonation pressure, vocal effort and acoustic output are adjacent to speech but provide no listener intelligibility measurement. |

The Baylor 2020 and 2021 cohorts both used the Reflux Symptom Index, which includes a swallowing question. Their whole-scale directions differed after safflower (−1.00 and +0.33 points, respectively). The separate swallowing responses were unavailable and neither cohort measured Parkinson dysphagia. TRIHEP3 collected its apathy-containing PBA-s and motor scores with dysarthria, bradykinesia, gait, tandem walking, retropulsion and arm rigidity items. Public sources did not provide item-specific distributions. The EU adverse event and participant count displays differ from the full paper and the registry gives two descriptions for the comparator. The 2023 Baylor RSI comparative interval excludes zero but its reported comparison p-value is 0.0792. Both are preserved for source follow-through. [Open trails](open-trails.tsv)

[Source inspection receipt](source-inspection.tsv) records the method and scope. Original paper text came from publisher records and author-shared publisher copies. The Neurology supplementary filenames were discoverable but retrieval was unsuccessful. The study supplies a public analysis plan and offers anonymized data by qualified investigator request. Product identification stopped at the documented brand because ingredient-based matching against the current retail catalog would not verify the historic lot or grade.
