Two official IRCT protocols match the brewed preparation and a separate depression measure. One compares lemon balm with black tea in nurses using DASS-21. The other describes tea versus rose inhalation in a gynecology-resident crossover using Beck depression scores. Both have unresolved results. A likely related 2024 author abstract reports no clear difference in a tea-versus-rose comparison. It conflicts with the second registry on plant identity and timing and supplies no separate numerical depression result. The 2026 ANZCTR bridge registers a matched-program tea comparison with anxiety and wellbeing outcomes. A 2021 pooled depression estimate is secondary evidence. Its depression study list was reconstructed from other records and remains unverified against the original figure labels.
