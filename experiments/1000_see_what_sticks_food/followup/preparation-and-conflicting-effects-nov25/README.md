# Preparation and comparison audit

This pass rechecked four Round 1 scorecards against three independent trial families. Five dated plain-text catalog records were added and four existing registry or results records were corrected. Three registry digests had been present with headers the exact Oracle lookup could not recognize. The 17-person curcumin trial contributed its separately posted pain endpoint. No frozen claim or published direction changed. The original Europe PMC queue remains at 30,274 pairs across 388 claims.

- **0131, refined soybean oil and resting tremor.** The original crossover identifies soybean oil as the comparator and excludes Parkinson disease. Acoustic values fell slightly in that arm. The active drug reduced them more, and blinded listener ratings were inconclusive. Refined oil and limb rest tremor remain unresolved. [Original full text digest](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-PMC6592780-Soybean-Oil-Voice-Tremor-Comparator.txt) · [Posted results](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2018-NCT01864525-Acoustic-Results-Extract.txt)
- **0507, ground turmeric and bradykinesia.** The curcumin pilot's 400-m change favored placebo by 0.089 m/s. Both groups gained speed. The exploratory interval spans zero, whereas grip and separate motor outcomes point in the other direction. The walking result remains visible as conflicting context and loses its transferred worsening vote. The existing minimal easing judgment still rests on the earlier rodent timing studies. [Posted endpoints](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-NCT03085680-Walking-and-Pain-Results.txt) · [Published abstract](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-PMID36946712-Curcumin-Older-Adults-Article-Abstract.txt)
- **0772, cold-pressed sunflower oil and executive function.** The DHA arm's relative advantage on working-memory response time is present in the original. Overall attention, processing speed and the working-memory score have differences with intervals spanning zero. Oil pressing grade and an oil-free comparison are absent. [Original full text digest](../../../../research/2010-2014/Source-Text/2013-PMID23515006-Sunflower-Oil-Cognitive-Comparator.txt) · [Prospective registration](../../../../research/2010-2014/Source-Text/Trials/ACTRN12610000212055.txt)
- **0812, ground turmeric and pain.** At 90 days, the unadjusted curcumin-minus-placebo difference in pain changes was −1.07 points on a 0–10 scale. The sample had 17 people and low baseline pain. The registry supplies no between-arm analysis or change dispersion. This contributes indirect context while preserving the pre-existing minimal easing judgment. [Posted pain results](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2022-NCT03085680-Walking-and-Pain-Results.txt)

The curcumin trial registry, posted results and journal abstract count as one cohort per claim. The journal correction confirms the placebo was microcrystalline cellulose. The full journal study remains open.
