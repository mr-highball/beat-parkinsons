# Stride, saffron and fatigue evidence, November 15, 2026

## Two minimal directional changes

**0229, virgin coconut oil worsens fatigue, moved to Maybe False at 51.** In the original 2014 randomized chemotherapy study, EORTC fatigue changed from 49.34 to 45.88 on virgin coconut oil and from 36.38 to 47.26 on control. The authors report no significant symptom differences at individual cycles and supply no adjusted fatigue trajectory. An exploratory Parkinson ketogenic diet assigned a meal plan naming virgin coconut oil and had a small favorable fatigue item comparison. The separate MVP Parkinson baseline coconut-oil and fatigue tile is inverse. The trial and cohort comparisons converge weakly against worsening, with baseline imbalance, dietary components, unspecified oil grades and outcome confounding. The later Parkinson MCT trial gave an inconclusive fatigue contrast, and a virgin coconut oil trial in acute COVID-19 reported only aggregate symptom resolution. **0663, poached salmon reduces anxiety, moved to Maybe True at 51.** A randomized forensic-inpatient trial offered grilled or boiled Atlantic salmon versus other meats. The state anxiety scores changed 33.71 to 29.79 versus 32.37 to 32.42, group-by-time p = .043. Trait anxiety did not show a significant effect. The published effect-size entry is inconsistent with the F statistic and has been kept out of the judgment. The independent MVP baseline report's pooled non-fried fish and self-rated anxiety comparison has an inverse sign in people with Parkinson disease. This supports a minimal favorable direction. Neither source isolates poaching and the Parkinson cohort pools species and reports no pairwise uncertainty. Other mixed-fish clinical comparisons vary by population and outcome. ## What the closest studies measured

| Claim | Review result |
| --- | --- |
| 0391, refined soybean oil and daily OFF time | A 1994 oral soybean-oil meal delayed gastric emptying in non-Parkinson participants. A separate Parkinson controlled-release levodopa trial found delayed absorption after a standard meal of unspecified oil content. The studies cannot be joined as a clinical oil effect. The closest rotenone trial assigned soybean oil as an animal dietary comparator. |
| 0417, ground saffron and rigidity | Injected crocin and crocetinate studies used a toxin-model mobility or catalepsy endpoint. The oral Parkinson studies offer a total motor scale without a separate published rigidity item. |
| 0418, cold-pressed flaxseed oil and olfaction | The human postviral pilot used long-chain EPA/DHA and measured threshold, discrimination and identification. The threshold signal does not identify a clinical effect of plant ALA or flaxseed oil. |
| 0435, refined olive oil and facial expressiveness | The Parkinson case consumed extra-virgin oil before the dietary change. Facial-expression training and clinical levodopa studies use different exposures. |
| 0469, dried saffron and executive function | The Isfahan trial's adjusted AMT total difference was +0.78 (95% CI 0.16–1.40), and the PDQ-39 cognitive quality-of-life domain difference was −8.07 (95% CI −14.39 to −1.75). The product was milled Sargol stigma powder. The 2026 ADHD extract trial gives inconclusive working-memory and reaction-time comparisons. The clinical Parkinson executive outcome remains open. |
| 0471, cooked brown rice and postural instability | A randomized brown-rice protein hydrolysate trial reports p = .04 for a physical-performance composite including balance and p = .02 for chair stands. No separate standing-balance or cooked whole-rice comparison. |
| 0474, toasted cumin and rigidity | Related originals concern experimental muscle relaxation and animal movement after essential oils or aqueous extracts. No human rigidity comparison tested toasted common cumin. |
| 0486, cracked black pepper and dystonia | The isolated piperine levodopa study included axial movements in a total dyskinesia score. It does not give a separate dystonia result. |
| 0590, rolled oats and postural instability | The MiniMeal protocol reports collection complete and no single-leg balance result. The trial assigns bioprocessed oat bran and chickpea products to older adults outside neurodegenerative disease. The separate Parkinson oatmeal tile is nearly zero with an inverse sign. |
| 0631, cooked green beans and executive function | Authenticated common-bean mouse work tested cooked unspecified plant tissue and memory or motor tasks. No verified immature pod or human executive outcome. |
| 0792, dried marjoram and insomnia | A program with combined sleep education and mixed aroma improved sleep ratings. The PCOS marjoram-cinnamon tea study gives an unquantified sleep observation. Neither isolates dried oral marjoram. |
| 0839 and 0972, ground sage and falls or stride | The closest recorded paw stride effect used purified ellagic acid by gavage. Sage is a possible source rather than the test exposure. Clinical stride and fall comparisons remain open. |
| 0268, cold-pressed hazelnut oil and pain | A rat study measured ethanol-related gastric injury, with no pain behavior. Whole hazelnut food allergy cases reported pain and did not measure cold-pressed oil. |
