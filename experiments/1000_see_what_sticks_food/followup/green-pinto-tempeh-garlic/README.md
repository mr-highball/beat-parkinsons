# Green beans, pinto beans, tempeh, and garlic

The November 13, 2026 UTC pass covered 11 frozen statements. The six dated notes and initial handoff supply ten related assessments across six statements. Separate crossover and plant-assay handoffs supply two additional related assessments. None of the reviewed comparisons isolates the specified food and preparation while measuring the named symptom in people with Parkinson.

| 0123 cooked tempeh and shortened stride | In a tuberculosis randomized comparison, boiled soy tempeh increased six-minute walking distance from baseline more than control. The endpoint comparison was inconclusive. The official results confirm change means and show one recorded vomiting event in the food group. A paraquat rat thesis reports inconclusive forestride comparisons for fermented velvet bean extracts. | 0273 boiled pinto and insomnia, 0444 canned pinto and drooling, 0457 boiled pinto and small handwriting | Exact and broader searches covered sleep onset, salivary secretion, sialorrhea, handwriting and micrographia. Pinto feeding protocols report lipids, fermentation, glycemia or bowel measures. The Parkinson feeding-simulation trial uses kidney beans as objects for spoon transfer. [Open leads](open-trails.tsv) · [Official feeding-simulation registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02600858) |
| 0561 cooked green beans and fatigue | A study indexed as green bean sports drink reported swim time in mice. The full paper identifies mung bean and the publisher retracted it after finding compromised peer review. The Parkinson pilot used green beans among suggested foods and did not report a fatigue outcome. [Original paper](https://benthamopen.com/contents/pdf/TOBEJ/TOBEJ-9-318.pdf) · [Publisher retraction](https://benthamopen.com/contents/pdf/TOBEJ/TOBEJ-10-113.pdf) · [Parkinson pilot](<../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md#green-bean-menu-option-and-parkinson-motor-scores>) |
| 0631 cooked green beans and executive impairment, 0665 cooked green beans and rigidity | A common-bean pod methanol extract lowered rat haloperidol catalepsy means and changed memory-task outcomes. The cultivar and edible preparation were unspecified. A 2013 chromatography screen confirmed levodopa and carbidopa only for fava under its assay. A later common-bean concentration was traced to a review, with the underlying assay still unverified. [Rat study](<../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md#common-bean-pod-extract-and-rat-catalepsy>) · [Seed chromatography](<../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md#green-bean-seed-assays-and-a-later-levodopa-citation>) |
| 0736 cooked garlic and urinary urgency | The garlic-soak study reported lower urinary frequency in two uncontrolled prostate-disease groups, using inconsistent frequency and nocturia descriptions. A separate case-control study reports lower benign prostatic hyperplasia odds. Cooked garlic studies confirm metabolite exposure, and an injected allicin study tests mouse motor behavior. None measured a change in urgency episodes with cooked garlic in Parkinson. [Garlic soak](<../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md#garlic-extract-and-reported-urinary-frequency-in-prostate-disease>) · [Case–control study](<../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md#dietary-garlic-intake-and-benign-prostatic-hyperplasia-odds>) · [Urine chemistry](<../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md#cooked-garlic-metabolites-in-urine>) |
| 0864 cooked green beans and fall frequency, 0957 steamed green beans and reduced facial expression | The 2025 Parkinson pilot offered green beans in a mixed diet, with inconclusive aggregate motor change. Supplement records use apple-based adherence and group-level fiber intake. Actual green bean exposure, steaming, fall rates and facial-expression scores were unavailable. [Dated primary review](<../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md#green-bean-menu-option-and-parkinson-motor-scores>) · [Article and supplement](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41531-025-00885-5) |
| 0888 boiled pinto and constipation | The 2011 symptom paper reports some stool changes on canned pinto. An independent 2020 crossover found no clear difference in combined stool changes between canned pinto and canned green beans. The 2022 mixed-cooked-pulse pilot reported reduced constipation severity in an uncontrolled design that included pinto. The canned studies combine stool frequency and consistency without a dependable direction. [2011 analysis](<../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md#canned-pinto-beans-and-reported-stool-changes>) · [2020 primary crossover](https://medwinpublishers.com/FSNT/FSNT16000211.pdf) · [2022 pulse rotation](<../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Green-Pinto-Tempeh-Garlic.md#cooked-pulse-rotation-and-constipation-severity>) |

## Search records

- Full text of the 2007 review cited for the 0.25% levodopa value and the 2003 review underlying its plant table were inspected. Neither supplies that value for common bean. A separate 2018 primary color assay found 0.25 ± 0.09% for one of 33 Iranian dried-seed accessions. A 2023 validated LC–MS/MS comparison found 0.020–2.34 µg/g dry weight across uncooked samples including four Italian common-bean ecotypes, with Munachedda Nera pods highest. The papers used different materials and methods. Both are chemistry studies with no symptom outcome. [2007 review](https://www.wisdomlib.org/uploads/journals/ansci/v-27-1-1-55-jul-sep-2007/1388728.pdf) · [2003 review](https://nopr.niscpr.res.in/bitstream/123456789/12261/1/NPR%202%283%29%20126-133.pdf) · [2018 primary paper](https://doi.org/10.21577/0100-4042.20170193) · [2023 primary paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37401097/)
- Appendix A of the 2020 Hegelmaier Parkinson study lists garlic among vegetables in the mixed dietary intervention. The paper reports aggregate motor outcomes and no urinary urgency measure. The Indonesian preliminary tempeh trial used steamed soy tempeh for a month and measured handgrip and body size. Its protocol lacks a gait or stride outcome.
