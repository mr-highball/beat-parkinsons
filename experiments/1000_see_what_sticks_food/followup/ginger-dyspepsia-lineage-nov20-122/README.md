# Ginger dyspepsia and gastric comparisons

The original enrollment chronology precedes the registry posting. The four-week article reports epigastric pain in 34 of 51 completers at baseline and 19 at follow-up. Eleven of 62 starters discontinued, and there was no comparison group. An eight-week dissertation and later reports supply matching cohort descriptors, different endpoints and a repeated safety subset. [Four-week original](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37771933/) · [Dissertation](https://hdl.handle.net/2437/404325) · [Registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06313814)

The healthy ultrasound crossover measured faster emptying with no clear symptomatic change. A separate healthy placebo comparison using a paracetamol proxy gave an inconclusive difference. The Parkinson MRI trial compared DA-9701 and domperidone. A companion paper reuses its baseline measurements and adds attrition context. The original diary endpoints remain open. [Ultrasound crossover](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18403946/) · [Proxy comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8317647/) · [Parkinson trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29705555/)

The eight related assessments preserve differences in formulation, population, outcome and independence. Claims 0581, 0725 and 0937 retain their previous verdicts. This follow-up adds no original Europe PMC pair dispositions. Proceedings and incomplete whole-article trails remain open.
