# Chinese butylphthalide primary studies

On November 12, 2026, the eight primary references numbered 11–18 in the 2019 Parkinson’s disease dementia meta-analysis were traced individually. Seven had accessible primary outcomes, including one full report and six abstracts. Searches used translated title fragments, Chinese author and title variants, journal issue and page, and DOI. The Chinese Medical Association publisher’s issue catalog allowed a complete sweep of all 24 issues of volume 44 of *Chinese Journal of Practical Medicine*. The National Science and Technology Journals Platform, CNKI and CQVIP provided additional primary abstracts. [Archived discovery review](../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/PMC6587622.txt)

SinoMed `2019297821` is a separate study by Ying Xiaowei and colleagues (应小卫等), volume 26(6), with 90 participants at Wenling First People’s Hospital. It differs from the volume 25(1) reference 12 in author, issue, and study record despite the matching sample size. [SinoMed primary abstract](https://www.sinomed.ac.cn/article.do?ui=2019297821)

An independent 2021 Guangdong meeting abstract by Wei Huan et al. reported a favorable MoCA visuospatial/executive result for pharmaceutical butylphthalide in 30 randomized Parkinson’s participants who screened below 26 on MoCA. The abstract gives p < .05 for the reported cognitive findings but no arm-level subscore or clear statistical contrast for each domain. No full journal report was recovered through exact Chinese title, investigator, and institution searches. The same authors’ later conference abstract PU-0070 describes a matching 12/30 split by cognitive status and a cross-sectional imaging and electrophysiology comparison. It reports no drug exposure or treatment outcome. Participant overlap is plausible, and the later abstract provides no replication. The 2021 study is adjacent to [0164, dried celery leaf and executive function](../statements/0164.txt). The result is for a combined domain and an isolated pharmaceutical. [2021 proceedings, printed p. 60](https://files.sciconf.cn/upload/file/20210731/20210731001342_48745.pdf) · [Later proceedings, PU-0070, printed pp. 1280–1281](https://files.sciconf.cn/upload/file/20230420/20230420232808_51579.pdf) · [Dated note](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#pharmaceutical-butylphthalide-and-cognitive-outcomes)
