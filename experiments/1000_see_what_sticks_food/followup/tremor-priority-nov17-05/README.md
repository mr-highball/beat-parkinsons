# Dutch registry results and original resting-tremor sources

The No Guts No Glory crossover's newer Dutch registry posted results in August 2026. Among 49 Parkinson participants, 47 completed. The registry reports no significant effect on the overall MDS-UPDRS Part III score, with no separate rest-tremor values. The combined BrAIN diet includes apples with peel and olive oil among food choices. Neither ingredient has an assigned contrast and the oil grade and participant-level food intake are unreported. The earlier registry and the later protocol record different enrollment figures. The new registry distinguishes the original target of 100 from 107 enrolled across diagnoses.
