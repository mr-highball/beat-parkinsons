# Multilingual OpenAlex discovery and primary report checks

On November 12, 2026, the eight title-and-abstract queries retrieved all 538 results reported by OpenAlex. They cover 517 distinct work IDs, with 201 labeled Portuguese, 210 Spanish, 97 French and nine Russian. The returned records contain an abstract index for 440 of the distinct IDs. Twenty-one appearances repeat a work across queries. Separate indexes sometimes assign more than one work ID to one study, as happened for the Medellín thesis and article. This is a discovery search of OpenAlex’s records indexed in those four languages. The queries use broad nutrition vocabulary and selected food terms. Records indexed in other languages or without relevant title or abstract terms can fall outside the result set. The work ID audit reconciles all 517 IDs with 48 priority source checks and 469 contextual reviews. Two additional OpenAlex works came from citations. Contextual rows distinguish indexed abstracts, full texts, secondary mentions and unresolved access.
```powershell
& "$experiment/build/followup-3.3.1-i386-win32/bin/foodexperiment.openalex.exe" abstracts `
  "$experiment/build/regional-index/openalex-20261112-offset" `
  "$experiment/build/regional-index/abstracts.tsv"
& "$experiment/tools/build-scan.ps1"
& "$experiment/build/scan-3.3.1-i386-win32/bin/foodexperiment.scan.exe" scan-index `
  $experiment "$experiment/build/regional-index/abstracts.tsv" `
  "$experiment/followup/hal-lexical-vocabulary.tsv" `
  "$experiment/build/regional-index/openalex-regional-lexical-replay.tsv"

$followup = "$experiment/followup"
$capture = "$experiment/build/regional-index"
$openalex = "$experiment/build/followup-3.3.1-i386-win32/bin/foodexperiment.openalex.exe"
$scan = "$experiment/build/scan-3.3.1-i386-win32/bin/foodexperiment.scan.exe"
& $scan scan-index $experiment "$capture/abstracts.tsv" `
  "$followup/openalex-spanish-vocabulary.tsv" "$capture/openalex-spanish-replay.tsv" es
& $openalex audit-pairs "$capture/openalex-spanish-replay.tsv" "$followup/openalex-spanish-pair-reviews.tsv"
& $openalex audit-context "$capture/abstracts.tsv" "$followup/openalex-regional-priority.tsv" `
  "$followup/openalex-spanish-context.tsv" "$followup/openalex-portuguese-remaining.tsv" `
  "$followup/openalex-french-russian-initial-triage.tsv" "$capture/openalex-context-replay.tsv"

foreach ($language in @(@{name='portuguese'; code='pt'}, @{name='french'; code='fr'},
    @{name='russian'; code='ru'})) {
  $prefix = "openalex-$($language.name)"
  $vocabulary = "$capture/$prefix-plus-hal.tsv"
  @((Get-Content "$followup/$prefix-vocabulary.tsv");
    (Get-Content "$followup/hal-lexical-vocabulary.tsv" | Select-Object -Skip 1)) |
    Set-Content $vocabulary -Encoding utf8NoBOM
  & $scan scan-index $experiment "$capture/abstracts.tsv" $vocabulary `
    "$capture/$prefix-replay.tsv" $language.code
  $reviews = '-'
  if ($language.code -eq 'pt') { $reviews = "$followup/$prefix-pair-reviews.tsv" }
  & $openalex audit-pairs "$capture/$prefix-replay.tsv" $reviews
}
```
