# Full individual olive-oil originals and remaining access gaps

The extra-virgin olive-oil claim had 51 original queue records. All 46 individual papers reached main-text depth. They include a dietary-pattern study of Parkinson incidence, mouse studies using olive oil as a carrier, and separate studies of an injected olive-pulp extract or oil-soluble formulation. The full comparisons do not estimate the effect of extra-virgin olive oil on resting-tremor severity. Five conference collections remain open. The partly inspected collection PMC10300527 contains separate posters on olive phenolics in tumor cells and Parkinson resting tremor as background to lab work. The turkey tranche authenticated 12 of 30 individual originals. Several concern Turkey as a location while others assess methods for characterizing Parkinson motor outcomes. The remaining 18 require original source access. The fig tranche authenticated 11 of 30 papers that did not already join to a local archive body. The verified texts address imaging, medication, motor phenotype or scale measurement, with no raw-fig intervention. Nineteen fig originals remain open after the checked retrieval routes.
