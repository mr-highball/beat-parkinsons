# Original evidence and unresolved data routes

## Coffee and fatigue

- ClinicalTrials.gov API query on November 16, 2026 used Parkinson Disease as condition and coffee as other terms. All nine registered records are listed with original intervention and outcome limits in the adjacent ledger. A registry search indexes descriptors and cannot recover all unpublished analyses. [Registry API query](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies?query.cond=Parkinson%20Disease&query.term=coffee&countTotal=true&pageSize=100)
- A longer Parkinson caffeine dose-escalation pilot has a registered six-week FSS outcome, with no posted result and an unavailable full fatigue table in the published supplement. Its exposure was caffeine, with habitual drinks allowed. The separate randomized study measures fatigue with encapsulated caffeine and is accounted for once. [Dose-escalation registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01190735) · [Original randomized study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3414662/pdf/znl651.pdf)
- The 2025 MED-PARK original recommends non-decaffeinated coffee and tea in a six-month diet package. Its outcomes include NMSS and MDS-UPDRS I+II. The latest registered outcome status checked in the preceding catalog review has no posted results. [Protocol](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/bmjopen/15/10/e101946.full.pdf) · [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06705517)
- NET-PD cohort Methods cite a 2006 Swearingen dissertation titled *Estimating Caffeine Usage in Two Futility Clinical Trials in Parkinson’s Disease*. Targeted title and institutional searches did not yield the manuscript or the original item forms. Coffee preparation ascertainment and fatigue linkage cannot be verified from the published cohort analysis. [Primary NET-PD manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3056438/)
- The 2026 Boscaini instant-coffee study in healthy adults is already cataloged. Supplementary Figure S10a uses a drowsiness/fatigue composite. S10h uses a separate visual analogue fatigue scale, without marked differences. A label scan of the public workbook and demographics file did not identify a matching numerical outcome table. Coded variables and exact intervals remain unverified. [Original supplementary figure](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13100100.1/41467_2026_71264_MOESM1_ESM.pdf#page=11) · [Official workbook](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13100100.1/41467_2026_71264_MOESM3_ESM.xlsx) · [Demographic data](https://zenodo.org/records/18348935)
- Genetic context was checked separately. A Singapore LRRK2 susceptibility study pools caffeine from coffee and tea. A small matched Greek GBA cohort records coffee history and phenotype. A UK Biobank study gives coffee and CYP1A2 associations with incidence. None reports the ingredient-specific clinical fatigue comparison. [Singapore original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10485627/) · [Greek original](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.3233/JPD-171221) · [UK Biobank original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13586350/)

- The official Café-PD registration NCT01738178 lists a fatigue item among six-monthly MDS-UPDRS assessments. The main paper reports a separate daytime somnolence item estimate, with no fatigue-specific result verified. The current registry lists 119 enrolled and the paper reports 121 randomized. Original supplement or study data are the next access route. [Official registry JSON](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT01738178) · [Main original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5664303/)

## Original clinical supplements

- The main 2017 sargramostim paper reports drug-related bone pain and total UPDRS III. A rest-tremor item contrast was not established in the three listed supplements. The 2026 intestinal-infusion paper also reports broad composite outcomes; its supplement scoring scheme requires an item-level check. [Sargramostim original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5445595/) · [Infusion cohort original](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3425/16/3/343)
- The Herb 2016 original supplement is listed by the publisher. Direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. The main-paper dispositions are already recorded. [Listed author supplement](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/suppl/10.3233/JPD-150753/suppl_file/sj-docx-1-pkn-10.3233_jpd-150753.docx)
