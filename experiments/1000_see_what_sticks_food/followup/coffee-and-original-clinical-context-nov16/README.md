# Coffee fatigue, rosemary supplements and original clinical context

Reviewed November 16, 2026 UTC against the original 1,000 frozen claims.

## Coffee and fatigue severity

Claim 0121 remains Unknown at 50. The existing manual espresso crossover gives three individual comparisons in one direction and one in the opposite direction, with two brewed espresso conditions. An earlier encapsulated-caffeine trial and the Saudi survey give imprecise fatigue estimates for caffeine. New sources refine the search boundaries. The 2008 NET-PD report compares aggregate caffeine against progression endpoints; the 2022 crossover measures attention using instant decaf with and without added caffeine; the 2005 motor endurance and 2026 pre-dose fatigue studies use populations outside Parkinson disease. [New assessments](assessment-additions.tsv) · [Chronological source accessions](source-accessions.tsv)

The Europe PMC search used five expressions, including preparation aliases, scale names, language variants, fatigue and alertness/withdrawal terms, and indexed original bodies. It retrieved all 16 appearances (15 source identities) across five returned pages including two zero-hit responses. Outcome terms index unequally across languages and records. Citation chasing followed the original FS1 and FS-TOO analyses, the comparator caffeine-fatigue work and the Nordic walking study cited by Yang. Nordic walking has separate apathy and fatigue estimates in 20 patients but no included dietary exposure. [Exact query receipts](focused-query-receipts.tsv) · [Result identities](focused-results.tsv) · [Screening depth and decisions](focused-dispositions.tsv)

The ClinicalTrials.gov Parkinson-and-coffee query returned nine records. The two registered caffeine-pill trials are known. One has an imprecise published fatigue estimate, and the other still lacks an accessible dose-escalation fatigue supplement. The MED-PARK original protocol recommends non-decaf coffee and tea alongside the rest of the assigned diet. The registered outcomes can provide broader symptom context after release, with no stand-alone coffee comparison. ## Rosemary and depressive symptom severity

Claim 0128 remains Maybe False at 51. The newly retrieved botanical blend supplements give one broader psychological quality-of-life domain and inflammatory biomarkers. They leave the inconsistent PROMIS depression coding and timing of the restricted BDI-II subgroup unresolved. The current trial record already reports secondary depression measures. The history endpoint did not yield a historical version. The 2023 ERP data DOI and separate 2025–26 mood data did not resolve to confirmed public files in the checked routes. No outcome or direction was inferred from those files. [Original supplement and data routes](open-evidence-routes.md) · [Revised assessment](assessment-revisions.tsv)
