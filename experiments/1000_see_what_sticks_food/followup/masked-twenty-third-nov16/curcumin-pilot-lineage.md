# 0805 curcumin pilot: primary study identity and attention outcome

PMID30747752, DOI 10.1097/JCP.0000000000001012, Kucukgoncu, Guloksuz and Tek, Journal of Clinical Psychopharmacology 39(2):182–184, March/April 2019. The author article explicitly states registration NCT02476708: this is the same trial, not an additional study. NLM PubMed EFetch XML and headers are retained; PubMed indexed 2019-02-13 and has no abstract. An accessible public third-party rendering of the primary pages is https://www.scribd.com/document/812215423/kucukgoncu2019 , pp. 182–184; its text extraction interleaves two-column adjacent letters, so identify the actual trial title and Table 1 carefully. The original publisher DOI https://doi.org/10.1097/JCP.0000000000001012 resolves to an inaccessible LWW route in the web tool; bibliographic record https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30747752/ . The article identifies Yale ethics protocol 1412015121 and registry, and its authors and title agree with NLM.

The paper reports 12 schizophrenia outpatients randomized 1:1, and Table 1 describes 5 analyzed per arm. Eight weeks of adjunctive randomized treatment: attention/vigilance mean change +9.20±18.18 curcumin versus −6.80±12.79 placebo; Mann–Whitney Z=1.257, p=.209. Working memory +10.60±15.17 versus −4.00±5.52, Z=2.200, p=.028; composite +6.00±6.28 versus −3.80±8.84, Z=1.485, p=.138. These are separate outcomes, and small sample plus wide dispersion makes the attention direction imprecise. Parkinson and fresh grated turmeric were not studied.

The author paper states a 300-mg surface-controlled water-soluble formulation containing 30% or 90 mg pure curcumin per capsule, and 180 mg total curcumin per day, supplied by Theravalues. Arithmetic gives two 90-mg active equivalents per day; the extracted text does not itself independently verify capsule schedule. Official current registry at https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT02476708 (retrieved as an official API record) instead specifies 600-mg curcumin capsules three times daily, 1800 mg/day, and posts no results. The article does not explain the discrepancy; 180 mg active versus 1800 mg labeled registry cannot be reconciled solely by the reported 30% concentration. Preserve both source statements and leave actual preparation/schedule unresolved.

Plausible next source is an accessible publisher PDF or authorized author manuscript to verify typesetting against the public text rendering, and original dated trial materials/history or product label to reconcile the two regimens. The existing NCT02104752 / PMID28965778 Wynn 360-mg Theracurmin trial is separately referenced by the 2019 paper and is not the same enrollment.
