# 0670 dill and perinatal nicotine: original figures and supplement

2025 Maodaa et al., PMID40455124, DOI 10.1007/s10735-025-10457-9, published 2025-06-02: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10735-025-10457-9 . Publisher HTML is a subscription preview, but its Fig. 1–11 images are public at version-matched Springer media URLs and are saved as fig1.png through fig11.png. The 11 images and the original DOCX supplement were downloaded HTTP 200. article.html, article.headers, supp.docx, supp.headers, supp-unpacked/ and extracted supp-text.txt preserve source receipts. Main article body and figure legends remain gated; no sample-size n, individual data, age at behavioral test, extract plant organ, extraction method or symbol definitions were found in the preview and supplement.

The primary abstract says pregnant mice received 50 mg/kg Anethum graveolens extract orally GD1 through postnatal day 15 and nicotine 0.25 mg/kg subcutaneously GD12 through postnatal day 15. Fig. 1 shows four groups: control, extract only, nicotine only and extract plus nicotine. The endpoint is neonatal animal behavior after maternal treatment, not fresh dill consumption by an adult with Parkinson disease. The preview does not establish leaf, seed or other plant part.

Figure 4 visibly labels four measures: A entries into closed arm, B entries into open arm, C time in closed arm (seconds), D time in open arm (seconds), i.e. elevated-plus-maze anxiety-like proxies. Read approximately from axes: nicotine-only vs nicotine-plus-extract has ~37 vs ~26 closed entries, ~16 vs ~22 open entries, ~265 vs ~205 closed seconds and ~82 vs ~118 open seconds. These are graphical approximations, not original tabulated means or clinically validated anxiety severity; retain unrounded graphs for verification. Asterisks and hashes are shown but their definitions are in inaccessible legend, so no pairwise p-value can be assigned from symbols. Figure 3 presents neonatal righting, rotating and cliff avoidance, separate developmental motor outcomes.

Official Supplementary Table I explicitly labels closed/open entries and closed/open time in two-way ANOVA. For nicotine×extract interaction it prints F=18.21 (p<.001) for closed entries, F=0.4679 (p<.05) for open entries, F=13.83 (p<.01) for closed time, and F=11.40 (p<.01) for open time. The dill main effect for closed entries prints F=7.375 and p>.05. The last pair and especially F=0.4679 with p<.05 appear inconsistent, but degrees of freedom are not given; they should not be silently corrected or treated as verified pairwise contrast. The supplementary table supplies no explicit n or group means.

Five 2025 authors overlap with the seven-author 2021 report, and the 2025 reference list cites it. The earlier study measured newborn liver and kidney injury and oxidative/inflammatory outcomes, not maze anxiety. Its Methods say powdered Anethum plant material (organ unspecified) extracted in 70% methanol, 500 mg/kg orally to dams GD1–PN15 and 0.25 mg/kg subcutaneous nicotine GD12–PN15; pups culled to eight per litter and killed PN22. Its abstract instead gives nicotine through postnatal day 5. The 2025 abstract gives 50 mg/kg, tenfold lower than 2021 Methods; 2025 extract method and organ remain unknown in accessible original text. Overlapping authors, same species, four-arm structure and similar nicotine schedule suggest a possible study-family connection but do not prove reused litters or data. Do not treat as independent replicate or same cohort without source confirmation.
