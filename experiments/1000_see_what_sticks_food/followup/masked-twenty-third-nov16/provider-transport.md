# Provider retrieval and reconciliation

Europe PMC's native WinHTTP collector completed 24 of 24 focused expressions with its default sort. The native POST route recovered the same 31 membership rows. An earlier invocation passed an unintended quotation mark as the sort value on Windows and received HTTP 503. Passing the collector's existing `-` placeholder for the optional value resolved the failure. The first successful routes both used page size 25. The provider reported and returned 31 appearances, including 17 MED, 13 PAT and one PPR. These appearances include another version of one review and do not represent 31 independent studies. PubMed completed the 24 mapped searches and returned 17 query and PMID memberships, all shared with Europe PMC after native reconciliation. EFetch recovered the author abstract for each of the 17 records. Matching the records across providers does not establish full text. ClinicalTrials.gov completed four exact expressions with 10 unique records. One lexical flag combined an intervention and an outcome keyword for the same claim. Inspection also recovered the distinct Wynn trial and oat-bran comparator and corrected the identity of the terminated Yale attention comparison.
