# Soybean oil clinical search and endpoints

The nine Europe PMC expressions retrieved all 83 results. They include 82 unique source keys and cover the 14 frozen soybean oil claims, authors and comparator formulations. The official ClinicalTrials.gov Parkinson and soybean-oil expression returned zero records. Searches of English and Persian IRCT indexes returned two omega-search records, 21 author-search records and 610 indexed Persian Parkinson appearances on 25 pages. The original Tabriz registry matches the published Parkinson cohort and names Zahravi soybean oil capsules as the comparator. The reported outcomes concern biomarkers, and RAPA was measured at baseline. The oil grade and individual symptom results remain open. [IRCT registration](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Clinical-Trails.md) · [Published comparison](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Clinical-Trails.md)

Three longitudinal trials contain falls in soybean-oil, mixed-oil or ambiguous-oil comparator groups. Counts are for adverse events. The 2013 pilot used a soybean and fish-oil blend. The 2010 Alzheimer trial aggregated corn or soy oil allocation. The 2024 vascular-aging trial used soybean oil softgels and excluded Parkinson disease. The opposite nominal directions and different fall definitions preclude pooled inference about the frozen refined-oil fall claim. The animal and food-texture comparisons add related evidence for other endpoints.
