## Boiled pinto beans and Parkinson insomnia

The Aguilera article identifies the seed as Phaseolus vulgaris var. Pinta. The full methods describe ethanol extraction and reconstitution, and the 16% plasma melatonin difference compares fasted extract-fed rats with a separate chow control. The review trail described a sleep benefit, but the original reports biomarkers without measured sleep. Neither the preparation nor the comparison directly matches the clinical claim. [Original methods and local text](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Pinto-Nutmeg-Source-Context.md#pinta-bean-sprouts-and-plasma-melatonin-in-rats)

Footnote a in Delgadillo-Arteaga Table 2 defines the 1.59 odds ratio as a preference item for grouped beans, chickpeas and lentils. Its separate meal item pools rice, pasta and beans. Tume Tables 3 and 4 model sleep classification and canary bean intake separately against fecal bacteria. Supplement S1 gives canary and bayo marginal frequencies for 69 analyzed people. It does not provide a pinto item or a joint sleep comparison. Peru’s official agricultural questionnaire gives bayo and pinto separate entries. The public university and author records and the PROMED registry provided no accessible individual food-by-sleep dataset. The registry describes secure access by investigator request. The matching 44-person 2024 PROMED results abstract reports cardiometabolic outcomes. [Mexican study](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Pinto-Nutmeg-Source-Context.md#grouped-legume-preference-and-sleep-quality-in-students) · [Peruvian study](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Pinto-Nutmeg-Source-Context.md#bean-frequency-and-sleep-measured-against-fecal-bacteria) · [Complete S1](https://www.renhyd.org/renhyd/article/download/2596/1371/15751) · [Peru agricultural questionnaire](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2018-62/01_CUESTIONARIO_PEQUENOS_MEDIANOS_2018.pdf) · [PROMED registry, NCT03975556](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03975556)

The author response confirms the quantifiable-only analysis, the lack of information about preparation and the absence of new data. The cited preparation study tested cooked kidney beans and lentils in a laboratory fermentation assay with fecal samples from three donors. It provides no pinto cultivar or sleep outcome. ## Ground nutmeg and Parkinson rest tremor

The 2017 Spanish case and 2018 meeting account both specify intention tremor. The overlap in authors, hospital, age and course makes shared patient identity plausible. Reported nutmeg amounts differ. The earlier record includes diazepam co-ingestion, ongoing fluoxetine and a positive benzodiazepine screen. The meeting account reports no chronic medication and a negative screen. The conflict leaves attribution uncertain and the reports count as one provisional family. [Earlier original](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Pinto-Nutmeg-Source-Context.md#intention-tremor-in-an-earlier-powdered-nutmeg-case) · [Meeting account](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Nutmeg-Rest-Tremor.md#intention-tremor-after-powdered-nutmeg-exposure)

The Illinois series describes a recorder judging a recorded tremor unrelated to exposure and reports tremor in a severe case with multiple coexposures. The 2009 California proceedings abstract and 2011 paper have the same authors, time window, 119 exposures and intent counts. The original meeting member gives no tremor subtype or count. Its reported eight children under 13 differs from three in the Illinois paper’s description of the California results. The authentic four-page 2011 body remains open. The publisher full-body route returned 403, the author profile links to PubMed, and the one-page citation surfaced by discovery contains no original journal pages. [Illinois original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4057546/) · [California original meeting member](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Nutmeg-Rest-Tremor.md#california-nutmeg-cohort-in-the-original-meeting-proceedings) · [California journal abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21495887/)

The Messiha and Zaki abstract measures mouse locomotor activity after injected nutmeg extract. The exact commercial document listing shows only the abstract to public readers. Texas A&M’s holdings affirm a printed copy of the 1984 volume 26 supplements 1 and 2. No complete body or figure has been recovered.
