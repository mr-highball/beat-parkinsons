# Whole-food comparisons and symptom outcomes, November 15, 2026

## 0860, raw grapes and anxiety

**Maybe False at 51.** A randomized 12-week grape fruit-extract trial in 111 healthy older adults reported lower Hamilton anxiety scores versus placebo (p < .05). The reported 24.9% decline was within the extract group. Oral dried whole grapes reduced open-field thigmotaxis in a rotenone Parkinson rat model at day 38 (p = .006), with an inconclusive elevated-plus-maze anxiety index. A separate grape concentrate study in A53T mice found no clear open-field center-time effect. The closest whole-fruit human study used freeze-dried grape powder in 40 randomized postmenopausal women and reported no clear anxiety finding and no numerical effect. The extract trial and rat comparison support a minimal lean against the frozen worsening direction. Transfer to raw fruit and clinical Parkinson anxiety remains uncertain. A new whole-grape powder trial in students lists Hamilton anxiety as an outcome and had no posted results. ## Endpoint and exposure checks

- **0017 and 0447, grilled trout and cold-pressed olive oil with stride.** In the human exergame trial, the oil-by-time p values were .753 for single-task stride and .537 for dual-task stride. The prior .774 value describes the single-task time main effect. The oil supplement comparison leaves whole grilled trout and cold-pressed olive oil unresolved. - **0153 and 0533, grilled pepper and daytime sleepiness or dexterity.** The Parkinson diets include sweet pepper in both groups and report composite results. The OSA protocol lists optional bell pepper and plans Epworth sleepiness, with no verified pepper preparation. The paper’s body and ethics ID identify NCT06782737. Its abstract prints the number for a different diabetes study. The correct record has no posted results. - **0175 and 0413, dried fig and hallucinations, raw strawberry and falls.** The original fig case describes repeated high potassium after dried figs, with no visual hallucination outcome. The published strawberry powder comparison measured fear of falling (reported p = .518), with no fall-incidence outcome. A separate completed strawberry registration lists gait and no posted results. - **0379 and 0569, steamed brown rice or cold-pressed almond oil and dream enactment.** Human rice-product trials measure REM share or duration. The sedative rat report measures vigilance states, without REM atonia or enactment. The almond search found general sleep-quality and insomnia outcomes with topical, inhaled or intranasal oil. Whole almonds and expressed oil differ, and the available trials give no clinical dream-enactment comparison. - **0576, cold-pressed avocado oil and daytime sleepiness.** Whole avocado in HAT increased the nighttime-sleep component by 3.20 points on its 0-to-100 scale (95% CI 0.38 to 6.02). It did not report daytime sleepiness. The two Brazilian reports document safety monitoring with no specific daytime sleepiness result. The prior avocado-oil comparator trial recorded similar rising sleepiness in both arms and had no unexposed comparator. Laurel infusion tolerability was assessed without a fatigue scale. Fatigue in a probable liver injury case indicates symptom occurrence in a toxicity setting and leaves the ground-leaf culinary claim unresolved.
