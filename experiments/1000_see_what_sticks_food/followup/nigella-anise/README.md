The direct disease search includes anise as a Parkinson odor stimulus, thymoquinone cell experiments, drug delivery systems, other plant species and several preprint versions. The focused expressions also surface wrong-species star anise, agricultural or chemical papers, and background mentions. A contextual hit is not a directional clinical comparison. [Disease dispositions](disease-source-dispositions.tsv)

The 2013 nigella trial had timed attention findings and an inconclusive accuracy result. The 2022 oil-plus-sertraline study reported a favorable depression contrast, while the 2024 three-ingredient hypothyroidism trial found similar final BDI-II scores. Ground seed in a premenstrual trial improved a composite mood score and did not report a separate fatigue result. The 2024 anise powder pain study and earlier dyspepsia reports measured abdominal symptoms in different populations. The shared registrations, chronology and outcome limitations are recorded in the dated notes. Both use their existing provider pagination and reconciliation logic. Replaying the 11 completed focused expressions from the checkpoint reproduced the result ledger byte for byte. A separate native anethole expression reconciled all 1,302 results across 14 pages.
