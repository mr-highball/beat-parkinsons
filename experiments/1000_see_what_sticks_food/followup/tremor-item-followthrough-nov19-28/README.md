## Evidence decisions

- **Baked turkey (0392).** Original Figures 2–3 record general tremor returning after an oral protein challenge in two participants receiving continuous intraduodenal levodopa. The first participant followed a different medication and protein schedule and shows no corresponding post-challenge tremor. Methods give a clinical severity scale without identifying rest, postural or action tremor. The table has tapping, walking and plasma measurements. The original graph changes the recorded observed sign for the protein challenge, while the food and symptom fit leaves the frozen verdict open. [1989 primary-source extraction](../../../../research/1985-1989/Source-Text/1989-PMID2795076-Protein-Load-and-Tremor-Extract.txt)
- **Refined soybean oil (0131).** The newly located crossover evaluates ground soybeans added to standard medication. The authors report longer motor ON and less dyskinesia, with no separate resting-tremor result in the abstract. The earlier octanol and octanoic-acid comparisons use oil as a vehicle or a placebo and address essential postural or voice tremor. The ingredient form, outcome and comparison prevent carrying either direction through to the claim. [2016 primary abstract extraction](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2016-PMID26810548-Soybeans-Levodopa-Crossover-Extract.txt) · [Related assessments](related-assessments.tsv)
- **Mucuna.** The plant is outside the frozen ingredient list. The 2004, 2017 and 2024 acute studies supply ON duration, composite motor and pharmacokinetic findings. The 2017 supplemental tables include patient-level motor totals and confirm reuse of one pharmacokinetic participant previously reported by Cassani. The 2018 report shares the registration and adds seven early powder-phase withdrawals, including three for worsening general motor performance. These results belong in their own chronology with the 2025 Ghana outcome audit.
