## Screening decisions

- Of 139 distinct OpenAlex works, 49 are biodiversity occurrence downloads. Several other results are loose lexical matches. In the two PDSAFE abstracts, Chivers-Seymour is a researcher’s surname. The trial tests physical therapy and measures falls. It does not include a chive exposure. The French results were horticulture records. The bowel query returned a crop chapter and a broad gastroenterology guideline. [PDSAFE primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30944149/) · [Chives in a separate Parkinson diet intervention](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chives-Cilantro-Curry-Leaf.md#chives-in-a-vegetarian-parkinson-intervention)
- The curry leaf branch returned paraquat and rotenone animal studies, a haloperidol catalepsy study, a reserpine oral movement study, work on commercially sourced isolongifolene and a brain-slice essential oil experiment. Plant occurrence downloads, antibacterial experiments, and reviews do not supply symptom data. Curry powder and curry leaf are different exposures. The Reddy and Patil papers report opposing catalepsy directions across different preparations and models. [Reddy Tables 2–5](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chives-Cilantro-Curry-Leaf.md#curry-leaf-aqueous-extract-in-paraquat-exposed-rats) · [Patil Figures 3–4](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chives-Cilantro-Curry-Leaf.md#curry-leaf-fractions-prolonged-drug-induced-catalepsy)
- The cilantro branch returned leaf and fruit models, biochemical screening, and human allergy and anaphylaxis records. The MAO-B experiment explicitly tested cilantro leaf and separately tested curry powder. Neither enzyme measurement nor the cell studies supply a symptom comparison. The university trial reports anxiety after coriander seed capsules. A separate mouse paper reports anxiety-like behavior after an extract made from dried leaves. The plant parts, preparations and populations determine where these records fit. ## Clinical gaps

The human Parkinson diet appendix names chives and gives a composite motor score for a multi-item dietary intervention. The coriander form in the same menu is ambiguous. The included human allergy cases concern acute swallowing or throat symptoms outside Parkinson’s. The retrieved curry leaf behavioral reports are animal models. The public curry leaf figure supplies approximate beam, pole and open-field estimates. The 2025 cilantro article preview gives group grip strengths and leaves rotarod, pole and the second model motor estimates open. The 2014 Kyung Hee dissertation matches the earlier Korean patent figures. The later article describes a distinct experiment from the same program.
