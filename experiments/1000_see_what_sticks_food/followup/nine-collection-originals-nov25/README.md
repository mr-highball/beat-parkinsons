# Nine original bodies and a follow-up on preparation and endpoints

The exact source audit resolved 122 claim–source pairs across nine identity-verified bodies. Thirty-seven give related contexts such as constituents, mixed diets, laboratory preparations, food identity or food used as an experimental vehicle. Eighty-five are matches such as researcher names, figure and timing terms, experimental drug names, localities or different plant species. The original bodies and numbered member boundaries were retained in the source notes.

- The chronic inflammation review cites original assays on saffron extract and PPAR alpha and a study of turmeric and ginger powders in rat arthritis. The receptor experiment supplies no fatigue measurement. The arthritis experiment reports lower paw swelling and does not measure pain. The clinical Parkinson claims retain their existing direction or uncertainty.
- ACNP M211 identifies a cranberry beverage as a shared administration vehicle in the lemborexant and alcohol crossover. The later paper uses a less specific beverage description and reports body sway and cognitive outcomes. The registration and publication concern one study. The registry and poster give different denominators. The design cannot isolate dried cranberry or a depression effect.
- The archive retains cystic fibrosis diet and grape-constituent contexts, an Alzheimer diet questionnaire grouping eggs with other protein foods, and veterinary food examples. Several promising common names resolve to distinct plants, including onion grass, fool's parsley and poison sumac.

The current Oracle rubric verified 71 distinct scorecards, including four new source-family entries for the receptor, rhizome and beverage studies. All published verdicts and their anchors were preserved. The exact queue holds 33,886 pairs across 21,721 source groups. Eight priority-three checks and 449 mapped original-access holds remain.
