# Coffee, green tea, and saffron replication search

On November 12, 2026, focused searches used ingredient names, preparation and constituent variants, fatigue and depression scale names, trial identifiers, and author and citation trails. The search covered [0121, brewed coffee and fatigue](../statements/0121.txt), [0948, brewed green tea and fatigue](../statements/0948.txt), [0485, dried saffron and fatigue](../statements/0485.txt), and [0063, ground saffron and depressive symptoms](../statements/0063.txt). The searches found further context and no new independent trial of the same culinary preparation and outcome. The two existing Parkinson’s saffron trials show partial agreement on depressive symptoms with different scales and preparations.
