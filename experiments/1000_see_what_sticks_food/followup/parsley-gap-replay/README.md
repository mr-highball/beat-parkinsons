## Findings and open trails

- Hegelmaier Appendix A lists parsley among many herbs and seasonings. It lists no dried/fresh form, participant amount or herb-free contrast. The public supplementary file identifies Figure S2 as total UPDRS III. Table 4 reports levodopa dose, including a one-year mean decrease of 36 mg/day with bowel cleansing and diet and reported mean rise of 100 mg/day with diet alone. Dose change cannot be converted to daily OFF hours or dyskinesia. The study did not report objective micrographia or a handwriting item. Ingredient records and clinical item sheets remain the closest access gaps.
- The genuine dried-powdered-leaf human preparation in Borges was administered with yogurt. Time to peak for the apigenin-related plasma analyte differed from a parsley drink. This is a constituent exposure measurement without a Parkinson population, swallowing assessor, dyskinesia scale, writing task or levodopa assay.
- Ventilacion's original reports dried parsley tolerance as patient history after fresh and cooked allergic reactions. It provides no supervised graded dried parsley swallowing assessment. PMID 17982932 concerns basil allergy. The 2014 Arslan parsley case is severe allergic exposure with no preparation-matched Parkinson outcome. The 2019 Costa programme documents menu design and cooking workshops. In the 2023 follow-up family, five healthy adults orally processed the purée and spat it out before swallowing. The 2026 hospital implementation documents use of the same recipe in the programme, gives aggregate food acceptance and production information and states clinical outcomes were not assessed. A recipe's physical properties cannot identify effects of the dried herb on Parkinson dysphagia.
- Isolated apigenin in model studies can support mechanistic follow-up. Among the measured outcomes are rotarod, locomotion, gait and molecular measures. The indexed models and reviews did not establish a dried parsley dose or levodopa-induced abnormal involuntary movement response. Other directly measured dyskinesia treatments, including Tianqi Pingchan granule, do not provide an authenticated parsley ingredient contrast.
- The next recoverable material is the original Hegelmaier meal and clinical item records, item data from a registered Parkinson cohort with verified parsley intake, or dysphagia programmes that identify preparation and instrumented outcomes at individual meal level. All four claims remain open.
