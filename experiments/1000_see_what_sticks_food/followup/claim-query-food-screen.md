# Food records matching one exact-query flag

This pass reviewed all 215 claim–source pairs in the saved exact-query Europe PMC screen that met the food, human-evidence-candidate, and one-flag criteria for the assigned claims. An exposure flag means the matching program found an ingredient or related exposure term. The outcome flag means it found a claim symptom term. Of the 215 rows, 39 had only the exposure flag and 176 had only the outcome flag. The 215 pairs cover 211 distinct source keys and 28 frozen claims. Claims `0259`, `0322`, and `0559` were handled in a separate review. There was no pair overlap with the 18 priority dispositions at the initial screening snapshot. [Exact-query screen](claim-query-screen.tsv) · [Priority dispositions](claim-query-priority-dispositions.tsv) · [Food dispositions](claim-query-food-dispositions.tsv)

The ledger records 161 title-and-index decisions, 39 abstract decisions, and 15 full-text decisions. Nine pairs supplied related evidence. The strongest are a randomized osteoporosis-education study that recorded falls and counted broccoli and kale within an estimated calcium total, and a companion analysis of a clinical meal that used cold potato salad and apple puree. The PPMI coffee studies did not report a separate fatigue severity estimate. The nutrition and fatigue study measured nutritional status without collecting individual food intake. None of the checks supplied directional evidence for a frozen ingredient, preparation, and symptom combination. [Falls and meal studies](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md) · [Nutritional status study](../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#nutritional-status-and-fatigue-severity)

Focused searches also found an observational Fox Insight fresh-fruit study and a Mexican multicomponent nutrition study. Fox Insight linked daily pooled fresh fruit to higher odds of its binary constipation category. The category included non-daily bowel movements and missing responses, and the analysis did not distinguish individual fruit or timing of exposure before symptoms. The Mexican study reported lower dysphagia and constipation frequencies after a combined program. The NUTRI-GUT-PD study has a published protocol and pending results.
