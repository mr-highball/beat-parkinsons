# Canola and safflower oil follow-up

This November 13, 2026 UTC checkpoint covers 17 canola oil claims and 14 safflower oil claims. It adds 14 related and three pending assessments. No eligible primary result resolves the proposed direction for the named oil grade and Parkinson symptom.

## Tabriz-approved Parkinson cohort

Kheirouri, Alizadeh and Keramati’s cooking-oil paper was first public in November 2023. The comparison used 120 Parkinson cases and 50 healthy controls. The public primary abstract reports a case-control association for non-hydrogenated plant oils and within-Parkinson symptom correlations for animal oil and butter. It does not report separate canola or safflower estimates. The abstract, Crossref links, native OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar work records, publisher abstract, PDF and supplementary routes, and author, grant and university searches were checked. The full oil tables and instrument could not be retrieved. [Local primary report trail](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Canola-Safflower.md#cooking-oil-exposures-in-the-tabriz-approved-parkinson-reports)

The July 2023 micronutrient paper and November 2023 dietary-pattern paper both give 120/50 participants from Isfahan, a 147-item questionnaire and neurologist-rated MDS-UPDRS. They cite the same primary validation. The latter gives the grant and approval identifiers. Its item inventory covers the four MDS-UPDRS parts. The validation source itself describes 168 items, and the 2010 validation companion reports aggregate liquid oil. Cohort overlap is plausible. The matching oil rows, preparation coding, and item-level symptoms require the actual oil article or data dictionary. [Instrument note](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Canola-Safflower.md#food-questionnaire-used-in-the-iranian-oil-study-trail) · [Open trails](open-trails.tsv)

An independent Ahvaz study published February 11, 2025 tested a 147-item adaptation of the TLGS printed questionnaire. The native Europe PMC supplementary download returned the DOCX. Its Persian screenshot asks for tablespoons of generic liquid oil, and its supplementary Table 2 aggregates vegetable oils separately from hydrogenated fats. The study used a different sample and ethics approval. Its two example screenshots do not show the entire item inventory or establish the Isfahan oil coding. [Ahvaz methods and supplement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11815801/#sec16) · [Europe PMC supplement archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11815801/supplementaryFiles)

## Search records

- Europe PMC completed four native disease expressions and five broader symptom or model expressions. The broader searches retrieved 316 records including production, food-physics, animal, and clinical reports. PubMed retrieved 39 distinct identifiers across four queries. Exact-title follow-up recovered the 1986 standing-pressure study and an official 2020 adverse-event table even though the safflower symptom query returned zero.
