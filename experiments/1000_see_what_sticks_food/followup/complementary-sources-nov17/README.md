## Dried celery leaf and visual hallucinations

Hazard’s 1883 original describes uncontrolled cases using a mixed commercial preparation with coca, viburnum and celery. Its dried-leaf description is about coca. None of its case notes supplies a visual hallucination count or verifies dried celery leaf. The 2024 seed-extract mouse paper has inconsistent dose labels and reports behavioral outcomes that do not measure hallucinations. An already cataloged trial of dried celery stems and leaves reported no general side effects but did not ascertain visual hallucination frequency. The searches and full reports leave claim 0491 Unknown. ## Pumpkin seed oil and standing lightheadedness

A placebo-controlled capsule trial reported inconclusive systolic and diastolic changes after seated rest. The original registration has 80 recorded enrollees while the paper identifies 76 randomized. The registry repeats the capsule dose in both arm descriptions and supplies no posted results. The sponsor, investigator, site and regimen indicate a likely shared study. The clinical paper supplies the comparison and the registry gives the planned outcome boundary. [Original trial](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Pumpkin-Capsule-Seated-Pressure.md) · [Official registry and lineage](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Pumpkin-Hair-Registry.md)

The EMA assessment lists hypotension for an ethanolic seed extract in a 2,245-patient uncontrolled study. It does not report a count for hypotension or a standing assessment. The earlier study and the separate 2000 author abstract have matching sample and symptom results, raising possible overlap. The abstract does not enumerate hypotension. The seated results, surveillance and prior active tamsulosin comparison leave claim 0938 Unknown.
