# Cooked onion and speech intelligibility evidence search

Local access date 2026-11-14 in America/New_York. API receipts after 00:00 UTC are dated 2026-11-15.

Claim 0024 states that consuming cooked onion worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease. The evidence status stays Unknown at 50%. The closest published evidence intersects with only part of the claim. The 2019 allergy case pairs actual cooked onion with acute dysphonia, without a Parkinson cohort or a listener outcome. The 2024 parallel trial pairs a concentrated outer-peel extract with a self-rated acute respiratory hoarseness item, outside Parkinson disease. The Parkinson diet studies cover a mixed dish or composite diet and do not report an isolated cooked-onion association with listener-rated speech.

## Decisive boundaries and source routes

- Parkinson speech intelligibility can be measured from listener transcriptions of patient speech. Clinical speech ratings, self-rated speech difficulty, acoustic phonation, swallowing and hoarseness can be relevant but represent different measures. A randomized Parkinson speech treatment study measured listener intelligibility without an onion exposure. [Primary intelligibility study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32639484/)
- The Rusch Parkinson pilot used Mediterranean-diet counseling and an at-least-twice-weekly sofrito goal. The PREDIMED sauce field allows tomato plus onion, leek or garlic. Follow-up randomized Parkinson work reported gut symptom outcomes and baseline UPDRS summaries. The MVP questionnaire included both a broad sofrito or generic tomato sauce item and a self-rated speech item, without a cross-tabulation or model joining them. The reported sofrito association was with total symptom burden. [PREDIMED instrument](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3419206/) [Rusch pilot](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8733603/) [Rusch follow-up](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11396875/) [Fox report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9739738/)
- The active 2024 extract was made from outer onion peel heated in water and then concentrated and dried. The group with extract began with a higher mean self-rated hoarseness score. The adjusted comparison gave p=.038 on that item, with a p=.164 overall respiratory symptom severity comparison. Korean journal hosting also exposes the original PDF. The trial registry has unknown status and no posted result summaries despite the journal report. [Original Korean journal PDF](https://e-nrp.org/pdf/10.4162/nrp.2024.18.1.33) [Registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05666752)
- The 2019 cooked-onion event differed from the 2000 report of one individual reacting to raw or lightly cooked onion while tolerating thoroughly cooked onion. Cooked-bulb exposure, laboratory skin test, vapor irritation, raw oral exposure and oral powder challenge were recorded separately. The individual clinical details establish neither a consistent onion dose-response nor a Parkinson speech result. [Cooked-onion case](https://www.termedia.pl/doi_ft/10.5114/ada.2019.82829) [Raw or lightly cooked onion case](https://www.elsevier.es/en-revista-allergologia-et-immunopathologia-105-pdf-download-10017984)
- NCT04647071 is completed without a posted results module, and the published results assess respiratory symptoms from a concentrated onion and garlic mixture. The later immune analysis is linked to the same registration and does not add an independent speech study. NCT02449590 concerns a meal preparation incorporating freeze-dried onion powder and metabolic or biomarker measurements. NCT06658444 is topical ultrasound delivery of an onion gel for cesarean scars. [Respiratory registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04647071) [Linked immune article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11394485/) [Meal registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02449590) [Topical registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06658444)
- Reflux reports such as those from Kashmir and Croatia contain an onion frequency or a garlic/onion response category and laryngeal outcomes, without assigning a dietary exposure to each hoarseness measurement. Medication and broad dietary/lifestyle treatment coincide in both. The 1990 provocation used a raw slice and measured lower esophageal outcomes. [Kashmir original](https://www.ijorl.com/index.php/ijorl/article/download/3396/1955) [Croatian original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10775783/) [Raw slice trial abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2327378/)
- Other onion matches include an onion word used in an aphasia script, CEPA as a Parkinson severity study acronym, and the common name Dayak onion for an unrelated Eleutherine botanical. Participant smell-identification tests, non-oral applications, bulb extracts and shallot/other allium species were tracked separately from cooked bulb exposure. The Turkish Parkinson menu assigns 59 g onion in a paper model without participants. South African qualitative records described an onion-and-honey respiratory remedy and a separate inhalation route without speech measurement. [Turkish primary study](https://openaccess.izu.edu.tr/server/api/core/bitstreams/d588102a-0228-4d56-9656-96a9cc43cce5/content) [South African interviews](https://hsag.co.za/index.php/hsag/rt/printerFriendly/2773/5469)

## Open leads

- Obtain the full Allen 1990 original and short letters indexed at PMIDs 1595664 and 2220746. Available abstract results concern esophageal reflux following raw onion, and no corresponding culinary-cooked Parkinson result was found. Obtain the full Aziz 1998 publication if it becomes available. Its abstract documents 300 g lightly fried yellow onion and flavonol pharmacokinetics.
- The 1999 allergic-dysphonia article concerns multi-food skin provocation. The index and available publisher preview do not resolve an onion-specific result or include a culinary oral test. [Original publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0194599899702329)
- The full Italian Parkinson diet author manuscript yielded neither the questionnaire's complete individual items nor a cooked-onion/speech score analysis. The original questionnaire codebook or approved individual-level dietary and neurological records would be needed to determine whether such an analysis is even possible.
- Direct publisher PDF retrieval for Cho yielded a challenge page, and native redistributable full-text retrieval declined the article's noncommercial license. NLM and the original publisher rendered full text and tables. A factual extract records the measured endpoint. Direct BMJ PDF access for Menon's 1969 letter returned HTTP 403, but the publisher-indexed correspondence showed the design and printed results, and NLM confirms the bibliographic IDs. EPMC initial transport errors and their full retries are retained alongside those source access gaps.
