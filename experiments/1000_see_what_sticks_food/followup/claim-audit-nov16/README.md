## 0085 · Ground allspice and walking slowness

Earlier Europe PMC and PubMed searches covered ordinary spice and botanical names. Expanded constituent searches returned isolated eugenol results. The original paper's narrow-beam result changed direction with treatment timing. Related studies report experimental motor behavior under different tasks and exposures. The OpenAlex routes had HTTP 429 responses, and the reviewed model outcomes give no ordinary ground-spice or human Parkinson gait estimate. ## 0158 · Grilled sardines and speech intelligibility

The added records distinguish a Bordeaux outbreak after contaminated preserved sardines from historical methylmercury exposure through unspecified seafood. The second Bordeaux publication covers the same outbreak. The later Minamata paper reuses 197 exposed participants from a 2007 report and changes control selection. It measures examiner-rated dysarthria, while food species, grilling and clinical Parkinson speech remain unmeasured. The earlier neurological table and trial-level fish and speech item data remain access leads. ## 0273 · Boiled pinto beans and insomnia

The older kidney-bean yogurt conference abstract and full paper describe one uncontrolled cohort with a within-person sleep-quality change. The randomized MIND comparison changes multiple foods and reports a grouped bean intake score. Two completed exact pinto registries list metabolic or vascular endpoints and no posted results. A separate Mucuna and soybean registry plans sleep measures; its genus and preparation differ. Culinary pinto exposure and clinical insomnia have not been jointly measured in the reviewed sources.
