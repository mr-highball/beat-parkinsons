## Walnut oil, whole walnuts and mixed diets

The three focused Europe PMC expressions retrieved five appearances. The acute walnut-oil experiment measured learned fear extinction. A secondary review gives a total mood interval from −10.75 to +0.07 points. The recovered official review supplement gives no anxiety or depression component scores. The 2026 whole-walnut crossover stays at author-abstract depth for the mood components. ## Peanut-oil spread follow-through

The ARISTOTLE companion paper gives raw HADS anxiety and depression scores for the same 63 participants. The oil-based spread group's depression mean fell from 7.21 to 3.63, with inconclusive adjusted six-month depression contrasts versus roasted peanuts or peanut butter. The comparison groups all received peanut-derived products, and the oil grade is unverified. Claims 0773 and 0782 retain one assessment each for this cohort and stay **Unknown at 50**.
