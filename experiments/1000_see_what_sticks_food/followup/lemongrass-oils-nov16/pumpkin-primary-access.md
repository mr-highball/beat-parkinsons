## Maiya 2017 Texas Woman's University / 2018 abstract, NCT02727036

Exact thesis title is `Effect of supplementation with pumpkin seed oil versus pumpkin seeds on blood pressure and menopausal symptoms in non-hypertensive postmenopausal women`, by Madhura Maiya; investigator's university-hosted CV https://www.uttyler.edu/academics/colleges-schools/health-professions/files/madhura-maiya-cv.pdf identifies October 2017 dissertation and the Texas Woman's doctoral program. Its intervention identity is 2 g/day oil vs 4.1 g/day seeds, 12 weeks. This is not a Lyttle or University of the West Indies dissertation. Lyttle belongs to a separate Gossell-Williams 2008 rat paper https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/ptr.2381; its use as an automatic registry result is bibliographic background, not the randomized trial.

The institutional CV and thesis committee listing https://media-speakerfile-pre.s3.amazonaws.com/documents/3e10586e50dd5a737e05329e68da2e081461087379.pdf confirm author/institution, not original outcome tables. Previously tried repository handle https://hdl.handle.net/11274/9763 returned 403; no full thesis recovered on exact-title/author searching. The registered trial's 2018 conference abstract P08-074 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6265820/ reports no clear oil-arm BP change and seed-arm systolic reduction 118 to 114 mmHg; no depression-specific or orthostatic outcome. A secondary systematic review https://oamjms.eu/index.php/mjms/article/download/6903/6114/55347 claims oil reduced diastolic BP and menopausal symptoms; without the thesis tables or denominators this cannot resolve its contradiction with conference report or establish between-arm effects. Keep exact table access open.

## Wong 2019, NCT03716960

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31445363/ identifies same registry: 12 oil vs 11 placebo postmenopausal participants, 3 g/day 6 weeks, brachial and central BP. Abstract reports within-oil systolic BP decrease p<.05 but not a quantified interaction or orthostatic symptoms. No complete publisher tables or verified institutional accepted manuscript found in this targeted pass. Registry is retrospective 2018-10-23 after documented June 2018 completion and lacks results. ## Separate 2011 primary human trial

Gossell-Williams et al. PMID21545273 original full paper https://www.styriangold.ca/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/Pilot-study-on-PSO-suplement-2011.pdf : 35 randomized, 21 pumpkin vs 14 wheat germ oil; questionnaire data available 18 vs 13. Depression item median 0→0 vs 0→1, endpoint p=.046; within-oil p=.364, comparator deterioration p=.009, 18 symptom items no multiplicity correction, no depression diagnosis or PD. Oil-group diastolic BP 81.10±7.94→75.67±11.93 mmHg, within-group p=.046; no standing BP, and table endpoint comparison is not an adjusted treatment-change estimate. Different 2008–10 cohort, not these three registered trials. Some paper tables use different baseline Ns (21 vs 18), so use relevant analyzed rows.
