# Lemongrass sleep / dream enactment, regional source follow-through, 2026-11-16

Claim 0753 is minced lemongrass and insomnia; 0796 is brewed lemongrass and dream enactment. Existing 1986 Leite dried-leaf tea placebo sleep/dream recall, 2025 Aoyama oil case, 2026 Kumboyono oil reports and registrations are already catalogued and not new studies. Dream recall or disturbing dreams differ from witnessed enactment/REM atonia.

## Original Thai oral exposure paper

Wongmanit et al., 2023, Pharmacognosy Journal 15(6):976–986, https://www.phcogj.com/sites/default/files/PharmacognJ-15-4-976.pdf . Original methods randomized 64 stage-3 CKD patients (32 each) to capsules from aqueous Cymbopogon citratus dried-leaf extract vs starch placebo for 90 days; 61 completed. The extract was produced by boiling and spray-drying, not fresh minced herb or a tea serving. Participants were asked to maintain their regular sleep pattern while safety symptoms and laboratory measures were tracked; paper reports no validated insomnia score, dream enactment, or sleep comparison. It is a genuine oral trial but not evidence for either endpoint. Publisher PDF covers methods and results; not counted as a new related efficacy assessment.

## Brazilian and Thai primary practice records

Brazilian Ministry of Health formulary https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/acoes-e-programas/plantas-medicinais-e-fitoterapicos-2/arquivos/publicacoes/formulario-de-fitoterapicos-da-farmacopeia-brasileira-sem-marca/%40%40download/file names Cymbopogon citratus (capim-santo, capim-limão, capim-cidreira) leaf infusion as traditional use. This is formulary guidance, not measured insomnia or REM behavior outcomes. The same common name erva-cidreira may refer to Melissa officinalis or Lippia alba; botanical confirmation required (Fiocruz https://fiocruz.qualificaaps.com.br/assets/tcr/1750104217.pdf).

Thai university primary practice description https://www.med.tu.ac.th/department/attm/?page_id=1793 identifies ตะไคร้, fresh or dried crushed stems/rhizomes boiled, or chopped lightly roasted then steeped; describes GI/urinary applications, not measured sleep or dreams. It is preparation context, not a clinical trial. Thai Chiang Mai repository https://archive.lib.cmu.ac.th/full/T/2536/anato0736kp_ch1.pdf discusses existing Leite 1986 and other laboratory evidence, not a new human study. Thai cannabis-tea insomnia paper https://he02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/HSJT/article/download/257162/177427 has no verified lemongrass ingredient in available indexed PDF text; do not misclassify it as lemongrass exposure.
