## Drug exposure trail

A standardized caraway seed fraction increased plasma exposure to rifampicin, isoniazid and pyrazinamide in Wistar rats. A different small, fixed-order human study used the same extraction method and saw increased exposure to the same three drugs. Taking peppermint and caraway oil capsules produced measurable plasma carvone in another human trial. Caraway extracts also suppressed induced CYP1A1 activity in rat hepatoma cells. The pharmacologic relationships established by these studies are restricted to their measured products, drugs and markers. No clinical assay of caraway and levodopa, another Parkinson drug, or a linked Parkinson symptom was verified in this replay.
