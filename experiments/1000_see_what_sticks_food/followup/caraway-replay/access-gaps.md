# Caraway source access and continuation

- Wüthrich and Hofer (PMID 6734454). The primary abstract and publisher DOI locate the 1984 German report. The full report could clarify what established the 26% clinical count, the actual preparations and any airway symptoms. [Indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6734454/) · [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1055/s-2008-1069310)
- Niinimäki and colleagues (PMID 7552932). The abstract verifies powdered spices for skin testing, but yields pooled oral symptoms. The original issue is *Ann Allergy Asthma Immunol*, 75(3), 280–286. [Indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7552932/) · [CNRS record](https://pascal-francis.inist.fr/vibad/index.php?action=getRecordDetail&idt=3698763&lang=en)
- Moneret-Vautrin and colleagues (PMID 12078423). The primary manuscript copy was located. Cases 283 and 77 give additional clinical detail and use the terms cumin and caraway inconsistently, leaving botanical ingestion unresolved. [Primary manuscript](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11297522_Food_allergy_and_IgE_sensitization_caused_by_spices_CICBAA_data_based_on_589_cases_of_food_allergy)
- The Netherlands submitted the 2000 draft carvone assessment report on October 16, 2000. The official EU decision confirms the submission, and the 2012 RIVM dossier provides a summary of the acute study. The original assessment report and study-level observations were not independently obtained. [EU decision](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32008L0044) · [RIVM dossier](https://www.enfo.hu/sites/default/files/carvone%20CLH06316e80-54ad-455b-9ef2-5774d45aaf99.pdf)
- Buchbauer and colleagues (DOI 10.1002/ffj.1600) report mouse locomotor activity by optical isomers in the abstract, with inverse R/S–optical labels in the title. Full methods are needed to examine the actual vial or analytical identification. [Publisher record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/ffj.1600)
- Nogoceke's matched dissertation and journal abstracts distinguish acute and prolonged locomotor tests. The full dissertation figures or journal body would refine timing and between-group comparisons. [Original UFPR record](https://acervodigital.ufpr.br/xmlui/handle/1884/38022?show=full) · [Primary journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neulet.2016.03.013)
