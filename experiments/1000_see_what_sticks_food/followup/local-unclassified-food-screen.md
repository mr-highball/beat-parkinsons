# Food exposures in archived unclassified articles

This screen uses the November 12, 2026 `build/local-article-recheck/claim-local-text-screen.tsv` snapshot. It includes every `unclassified abstract` pair for which the archived body has a true `text_ingredient` or `text_related` flag and a true `text_outcome` flag, and the indexed title and abstract miss the exposure or the outcome. The frozen category is `food` and the ingredient is outside the previously investigated date, fig and orange trails. The denominator is 253 pairs across 218 PMC articles. The parent cohort is 819 pairs, including 452 for foods. The remaining 566 pairs comprise 199 date, fig and orange rows and 367 herb, spice and oil rows.

The screen used the frozen IDs, exposure expansions and symptom aliases to locate text in the archived articles. Each line below identifies the matched term's role. A common disposition is shared only where the articles use the ingredient term in the same way. The separate [targeted screen](local-unclassified-intervention-screen.md) includes selected overlapping reports.
