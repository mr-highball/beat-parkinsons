The original Table 2 provides an aggregate vegetable comparison without separating cooked green beans or a Parkinson fatigue population. Its sleep questionnaire comparisons were inconclusive, and the shared oil prevents attribution to olive oil. Claim 0343 keeps its previous weak anxiety lean from separate evidence.
