## Focused reassessment

The Newcastle sage trial registry confirms a cognitive primary endpoint. The secondary schedule and any facial-expression result remain open. The original military omega-3 comparison distinguishes participation agreement from allocation and leaves the original Epworth analysis denominator and time point unverified. The induced oral-discomfort trial pairs grapeseed oil with another ingredient and supplies no posted efficacy result. A prior laboratory use of the formulation cannot establish product identity or an isolated oil effect.

## Review workflow

The coordinator deduplicated study identifiers, queried Oracle in shared batches, and retrieved original bodies once per identity. Three Terra reviewers and the coordinator assessed separate publication lists. The reader-depth audit rejected two abstract-only pages that had passed the ingestion screen. The source and scorecard changes were grouped for one exact-queue replay and catalog refresh. The coverage receipt measures lookups and dispositions. It provides no cross-model token comparison.
