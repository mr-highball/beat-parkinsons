# Publication access and identity follow-up

The full printed page sets for [1949 occupational medicine abstracts](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1036187/) and [the 1988 BMJ subject and author index](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1833619/) were recovered. The [page receipt](official-page-images.tsv) records hashes, printed page order and official URLs. Selected study and index contexts close 24 mappings. Two 1949 date-fruit mappings and seven 1988 mappings remain open because the OCR or index context alone cannot establish the exact scope. The BMJ volume page pointers refer to independent articles.

## Matched identity, incomplete original

- [AJNR symposium issue PMID6410704](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6410704/) and [1927 BMJ issue PMC2453796](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2453796/) had prior issue-level records. Whole issue coverage and mapped member text were not recovered in this pass.
- [SNO 2021 issue](https://academic.oup.com/neuro-oncology/issue/23/Supplement_6) has an official member table. The independently indexed body encountered during review ends partway through the issue, before all members can be reconciled. The 17 checks remain open.
- [2008 FEBS abstracts PMC7163982](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7163982/) and [2014 FEBS abstracts PMC7163937](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7163937/) have publisher records and isolated repository posters. Coverage of all mapped members remains open.
- [2007 stress congress PMC1949331](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1949331/) and [2013 AES abstracts PMC3966633](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3966633/) did not yield verified complete bodies in this pass. The original PDF route or member matching remains open.
- [2019 FEBS posters DOI 10.1002/2211-5463.12675](https://doi.org/10.1002/2211-5463.12675) appear to run through p.431 in an author-posted render. Interleaved columns prevent complete member authentication. A thyme-containing animal tincture was identifiable under P-25-006, with no assigned Parkinson symptom outcome. Other visible ingredient mentions await exact attribution. All 21 mapped checks stay open.
- [Springer congress PMC7102104](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7102104/) has a prior 337-page PDF receipt. Printed p.1010 is absent from that copy, and the publisher route provided a subscription preview. All 18 checks stay open.
- [Neuropathology meeting PMC8095753](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8095753/) matches the inherited PMID16922993. One university member record cannot cover the whole supplement. All 17 checks stay open.
