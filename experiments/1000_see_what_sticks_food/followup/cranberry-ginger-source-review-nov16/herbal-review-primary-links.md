* Ref 137: Wood et al., PMID36972800, PMCID PMC10315404, DOI 10.1016/j.ajcnut.2023.03.017, 25 March 2023, [original PubMed abstract/figure 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36972800/), [original full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10315404/), registered NCT04084457. In 61 healthy adults 65–80, 26 g/day freeze-dried wild blueberry powder vs matched placebo for 12 weeks; 27 per arm completed follow-up. Task-switch accuracy favored blueberry (p=.026), immediate recall favored blueberry (p=.043), but delayed word recall favored placebo (p=.029). Freeze-dried powder mixed in water is not raw whole fruit or PD, and different tasks must not be pooled. The review's short favorable summary omits the delayed-recall counterfinding.
