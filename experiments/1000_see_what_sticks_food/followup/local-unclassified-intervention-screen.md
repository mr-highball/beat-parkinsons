# Targeted food and plant interventions in archived unclassified records

The 2026-11-12 snapshot contained 2,215 claim–source pairs labeled `unclassified abstract`. Of 829 pairs with both sides flagged in full text, 19 matched the title expression below after joining `claim-local-text-screen.tsv` to the snapshot's `academic-screen.tsv` on `source_key`. A second search within all 2,215 selected 32 pairs using the follow-up title expression or the ten PMC IDs in the last ten-source group described below. The union was 51 pairs across 21 full texts. Archived text, methods, and available tables determined each disposition.
