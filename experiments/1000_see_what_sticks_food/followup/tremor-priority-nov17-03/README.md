## What the sources measured

- Both KIM diet periods encouraged olive oil. The clinical paper reports mild, intermittent tremor exacerbation for two ketogenic-period participants and one triglyceride-period participant. It does not specify tremor posture, isolated oil exposure or oil grade. The public file contains aggregate Part III and general motor ratings without a rest-tremor item. Extra-virgin olive oil (0577) remains Unknown. [KIM events and public data](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Oil-Followup.md#kim-intermittent-tremor-adverse-events-in-both-diet-periods)
- The larger Prague 57-person trial reported an inconclusive whole-diet motor result without separate rest-tremor scores. The 2014 congress case and 2025 focus-group report describe overall or perceived symptom changes after gluten withdrawal alongside distinct medication histories. The 2020 congress case separates improved neuropathy from Parkinson progression. Cooked wheat berries (0627) remain Unknown.
