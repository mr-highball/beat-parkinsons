# Archived food exposures, grape juice trial lineage and source reliability

The native review checked all 382 archived priority-five pairs on 29 ingredients other than date. The 368 distinct source keys resolved to their expected archive paths in Oracle. Full-text review recorded 59 related contexts and 323 exclusions. Author names, figure labels, reagents, geographical terms and biological eggs were screened alongside botanical and exposure variants. Five evidence assessments link the new clinical and cultural records to Round 1. [Exact dispositions](dispositions.tsv) · [Source inventory](source-inventory.tsv) · [Evidence assessments](assessments.tsv)

The original 2020 grape-juice study shows 24 randomized and 19 analyzed, with nine exercise-only and ten exercise-plus-juice participants. Its abstract calls the analyzed group randomized, and the 2023 review narrative reverses the arm counts. The original groups share aquatic exercise. Table 3 gives group-by-time p values of .11 for walking distance, .28 for mobility, and .70 for balance. Berg Balance is separate from fall counts. Neither this paper nor the 2021 report measured an isolated anxiety or rigidity outcome. [Original report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32798568/) · [Linked 2021 report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7887390/)

Both grape papers cite the same Brazilian registration. Its crossover plan differs from the reported parallel analyses. The 2021 paper has 18 completers in the text and 19 in its baseline table. The two publications also report different product composition. They remain linked in one study family while allocation and overlap are open. [Official WHO registry](https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=RBR-2c4xxb)

The MENASA protocol records black tea and ever having rest tremor as separate fields. The protocol gives no black-tea symptom estimate. The MVP survey reports fatigue associations with overall MIND and Mediterranean scores without a bell-pepper contrast. The lower Mekong survey lists reported Curcuma longa use without an ingredient-specific pain measurement. [MENASA protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12264538/) · [MVP study](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/14/23/5185) · [Lower Mekong survey](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4782577/)

Publisher retraction checks identified two exact archive sources with withdrawals. The retracted Neelum plant inventory has separate botanical taxa in the reported sleep examples. The withdrawn alpha-synuclein mouse paper used an unspecified mint cue. Both pairings are excluded. [Neelum notice](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0286781) · [Mouse study notice](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13024-024-00762-4)
