## Ground anise and pain

The frozen statement says that ground anise worsens Parkinson pain. Ghoshegir and colleagues studied ground anise in 47 people with dyspepsia against cornstarch in 60. Week-four mean epigastric pain severity was 0.38 versus 2.05, with p=.0001 on the study scale. The week-four frequency table also favors anise. Its earlier quality-of-life article reports the same cohort. A separate acute-COVID study reports favorable abdominal-pain trajectories. Its Table 2 describes a treatment-vs-placebo RR of 0.55 and labels the event improvement, which conflicts with that interpretation. The tested sachet also contained rock sugar. An indexed Ushaq capsule trial found lower radicular pain, with actual anise exposure still unverified. These findings weakly oppose worsening in gastrointestinal pain and leave Parkinson pain types and the exact clinical effect open.
