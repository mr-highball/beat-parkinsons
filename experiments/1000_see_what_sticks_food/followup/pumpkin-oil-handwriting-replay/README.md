# Cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil and handwriting · 0977

The Parkinson vegetarian menu lists pumpkin seed oil among other foods, without individual exposure, processing or handwriting results. A registered resistance-training study in older men lists pumpkin seed oil among the comparator's alternative names. It gives exercise and strength outcomes and does not establish the actual oil composition or pressing method. The accepted manuscript names the comparator brand and blend. A matching 2015 NIH label names cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil. Design and investigator details strongly support linking the paper and registry. The product lot and separately measured pumpkin-oil intake remain unverified.
