# Herb, spice and oil exposures in archived unclassified articles

This screen uses the November 12, 2026 `build/local-article-recheck/claim-local-text-screen.tsv` snapshot. It includes every `unclassified abstract` pair for which the archived body has an exposure flag (`text_ingredient` or `text_related`) and an outcome flag, and the indexed title and abstract miss the exposure or the outcome. The frozen category is herb, spice or oil. The selected 367 pairs span 183 PMC articles, with 126 herb pairs, 84 spice pairs and 157 oil pairs. Exposure expansions and symptom aliases directed a review of the archived text. Pascal extracted exposure lines for 364 pairs. The other three pairs were checked in the complete archived text. For actual exposures and test materials, the review examined the study design, preparation and relevant outcomes.
