## 0025, fresh sage and Parkinson pain

Two independent randomized, placebo-controlled menopause trials reported a favorable item comparison for joint and muscular discomfort after a common-sage extract was swallowed. Dimpfel used a tablet made from fresh sage tips. The authors report p<.05 in their 48-person per-protocol analysis, with no arm-specific item values or intention-to-treat item result. Zeidabadi used a dry extract and reported p<.001 after treatment, with inconsistent table formatting and numerical descriptions. The individual item includes joint pain and is closer to the frozen endpoint than a general menopause or premenstrual composite. The result transfers only weakly across preparation, population and pain type. The frozen worsening claim is **Maybe False, 51**. A direct whole-leaf Parkinson comparison remains open.
