## 0553 attention fit

Diekmann et al. measured the FAIR-2 K performance score, which combines accuracy and speed. Baseline and 1.5, 3.0 and 4.5 h were sampled. The within-fats estimate across 25 g and 50 g fat meals was −3 (−14, 9) for canola compared with coconut. The attention outcome was secondary to a metabolism trial. Higher fat across oils gave −12 (−25, −0.6, p=.039) while carbohydrate and fiber were lower. The same-direction canola iAUC estimate also spans zero (−1,750, 95% CI −4,900 to 1,400). That contrast supplies more specific oil-type evidence than the amount-of-fat contrast, with insufficient precision for a weak oil-specific lean. [Original full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12881678/) · [Official registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05208346)

Shen's study used healthy adults and randomized meal order. Both author summaries mention a no-added-fat speed advantage, with the academic summary identifying psychomotor reaction time and the popular summary identifying latency of correct responses. Neither gives task-level values, a speed–accuracy model, exact oil-only test, adjustment, effects by time, or a separate fatigue estimate. The VAS activation result concerns the rapeseed-versus-polar-lipid contrast. The public metadata lists both attachment records as restricted to Lund University access. There is no DOI or public attachment URL on the source record. The primary author abstracts support a related row and leave the body and pairwise results open. [Author summaries](https://lup.lub.lu.se/student-papers/record/9089334) · [Repository API record](https://lup.lub.lu.se/student-papers/search/publication?q=id%20exact%209089334&format=json)

Vaisman's relative TOVA contrasts involve replacement with fish oil or marine phospholipids. Within the canola comparator, total TOVA change was small relative to its variance. The journal contains two different placebo means, −0.42 in the abstract and −0.45 in Table 5. Canola's standardized latency component was −0.02 ± 1.17 and variability −0.30 ± 1.44 in Table 5. Gustafsson's additional replacement study measures a teacher-rated inattention/cognitive subscale and cannot isolate the effect of the rapeseed oil in the comparator. The lower spontaneous alternation in mice on canola is a working-memory result in a different disease model. These are distinct cohorts with separate contrasts.
