## Findings closest to the hypotheses

- **0641, cooked spinach and attention.** The perinatal registry led to Colombage’s original thesis. Its food diaries record 28 and 102 spinach entries in the low- and high-flavonoid groups. Its combined attention-task accuracy had a condition-by-visit interaction (p = .006). Spinach was one choice among several foods, with no ingredient or cooking contrast. The separate red spinach extract crossover found no clear Stroop differences. [Perinatal registry and attention outcomes](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Spinach-Attention-Comparators.md#optional-spinach-intake-and-measured-postpartum-attention) · [Red spinach extract crossover](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Spinach-Attention-Comparators.md#red-spinach-extract-and-stroop-performance)
- **0553, refined canola and attention.** Vaisman’s canola control changed by −0.45 ± 2.51 on total TOVA in the original table (p = .418 for its within-arm contrast), while the same article’s abstract gives −0.42. Shen’s rapeseed breakfast study reports inconclusive selective attention and working memory but faster psychomotor responses after the lipid-free meal relative to the added-lipid meals. The full thesis is institutionally restricted. The earlier canola–coconut attention comparison spans zero. The differences among task endpoints and formulations leave refined canola and Parkinson attention unresolved. [Original TOVA results](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Canola-Attention-Comparators.md) · [Shen’s author abstract](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Canola-Attention-Comparators.md)
- **0531, ground nutmeg and daytime sleepiness.** Cushny’s 1908 paper summarizes earlier drowsiness and stupor cases. A separate 2015 original describes accidental excessive intake in a food dish and next-morning drowsiness, with alcohol co-exposure. A young-adult fruit-syrup study measured task performance without a sleepiness scale or a verified ground seed exposure. The cases support acute toxic drowsiness and leave ordinary culinary use in Parkinson’s unresolved. [1908 proceedings](../../../../research/1905-1909/Food-Experiment-Nutmeg-Poisoning.md) · [2015 original report](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Nutmeg-Paprika.md#accidental-ground-nutmeg-intake-and-next-morning-drowsiness) · [Dose and source review](0531-nutmeg-source-check.md)
- **0644 and 0689, lentils and pea protein.** The same Sher meal study appeared at World Sleep 2019 and in SLEEP in 2020. The 20 healthy adults showed an inconclusive next-morning Epworth contrast. Lentils were in one mixed, lower-energy meal, with canning unreported. The separate pea-protein trial registry mentions exploratory cognition while its likely conference companion reports appetite and intake. Executive-function results remain open. [Lentil meal and study family](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Sleep-Comparators.md) · [Pea protein registry](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Pea-Protein-Cognition.md)
- **0546 and the earlier 0518 review.** The established Quinn Alzheimer trial record gives 33 of 164 pooled corn-or-soy placebo participants and 42 of 238 DHA participants with reported falls. Oil-specific allocation is unknown. Separately, the Null paper explicitly permits spearmint and dill. The previous exact screen overlooked spearmint in the permitted list. The corrected review keeps the fatigue testimonials in other conditions distinct from verified ingredient exposure and retains Unknown.
