## 0641 cooked spinach / attention
Existing Bondonno 2014 PMID24676365 original https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/getauthorversionpdf/C3FO60590F: 30 healthy crossover participants; thawed frozen spinach versus rice milk, attention 1170 vs 1166 p=.70, continuity 92.1 vs 92.2 p=.84. Processing beyond thawing not specified. Morris et al. 2017-12-20 DOI 10.1212/WNL.0000000000004815 original https://n.neurology.org/content/neurology/90/3/e214.full.pdf: older nondemented cohort; food-frequency exposure combines cooked spinach, cooked kale/collards/greens and raw lettuce; 19-test global cognitive decline highest vs lowest quintile beta .05 SD/year 95% CI .02 to .07 (age-adjusted); does not isolate spinach or attention. Observational association and inconclusive acute test do not settle food-specific PD attention.

## 0524 cooked curry leaf / swallowing

## 0498 dried chives / drooling

## 0601 ground fennel / attention
Existing Kwiatkowska 10.5604/01.3001.0055.0077 primary https://publisherspanel.com/api/files/view/2759148.pdf Table 3: ten menopausal recipients consumed crushed seed infusion for two weeks; question combined memory/concentration/focus from 0 to 12 points p=.014 within recipients; laboratory-only controls did not answer symptom survey. Not a between-arm estimate, objective attention test, ground seed ingestion, or PD cohort. Distinct placebo-controlled seed evidence found for Nigella sativa was not fennel.

## 0632 cold-pressed peanut oil / dystonia
Existing PMID37192671 oil-based subcutaneous levodopa primate model had dystonic postures but no independent dystonia contrast, oral oil or cold-press verification. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37192671/. Enumerated oil excipients are not proof of ingestion or oil effect. No new separately controlled patient dystonia comparison established.

## 0546 refined corn oil / falls
Existing ENRGISE PMID30541065 randomized oil comparators: registered serious fall adverse events 1/71 corn-oil only versus 1/109 fish-oil only, not prospective total fall frequency; no PD. Existing Chinese healthy older-adult corn oil trial measured timed up-and-go, not daily falls. https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02660073 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36571774/. STRENGTH https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7667577/ compared omega-3 carboxylic acids with corn oil but cardiovascular outcomes; available main adverse-event table does not establish fall-frequency effect. No plausible directional inference from no finding.
