# Paprika follow-up access and measurement gaps

- Fong 2016 poster DOI 10.1016/j.freeradbiomed.2016.10.141 gives the gait methods and qualitative interpretation in an abstract. The 2017 thesis author abstract was recovered from machine-indexed metadata under W3005721879. Institutional web, Handle API, and repository REST routes for handle 2123/18042 returned access errors in the native retrieval. The thesis abstract provides no gait estimates and differs from the poster on neuronal viability. Full-text institutional accession or author publication data could resolve route, sample identity, gait-subset size, metrics, and figure-level results. [Abstract](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.freeradbiomed.2016.10.141) · [University handle](https://ses.library.usyd.edu.au/handle/2123/18042) · [Indexed author abstract](https://api.openalex.org/works/W3005721879)
- The original report for 1998 PMID9789242 supplied an indexed abstract for occupational rhinoconjunctivitis and a distinct oral paprika allergy history. The full Spanish report and detailed actual paprika treatments were unavailable during this pass. The 2020 South African case has an indexed title and abstract route requiring further original-text access. Neither available report gives an intelligibility comparison. [1998 primary index](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9789242/) · [2020 report](https://doi.org/10.10520/ejc-caa-v33-n3-a12)
- The two Boudreau tongue-task papers used different tasks. Institutional versions or author data could establish participant overlap and whether any unreported speech sample was collected. Existing primary records give force outcomes. [2007 report](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pain.2007.07.019) · [2010 report](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2842.2010.02103.x)
- NCT05626582 has protocol characteristics similar to the Jackson 2025 step-length trial. A confirmed registry ID-to-report link is pending. NCT05471557 is explicitly identified in the 2026 report, whereas NCT07054840 was an ongoing registry record. [2025 report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12416618/) · [NCT05626582](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05626582) · [NCT05471557](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05471557) · [NCT07054840](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07054840)
- Parkinson dietary cohorts, event diaries and food-frequency forms remain a way to locate separately recorded paprika exposure. The next comparison needs cooking or ingredient detail, a separate Capsicum cultivar/preparation field, and repeated standardized values for the specific endpoint. Commercial forms with distinct smoked and ground codes merit separate searching in local-language theses and abstract-only meeting sources. The extensive indexing search did not retrieve an eligible primary exposure-outcome comparison.
