- **Saliva and drooling.** The 1985 proceedings include a study of enzyme secretion in isolated rat salivary tissue. A 1992 randomized crossover assessed measured saliva output, reported dryness and taste separately in healthy volunteers. The saliva-output change was inconclusive across regimens. These outcomes supply no raw-fig comparison for clinical Parkinson drooling.
