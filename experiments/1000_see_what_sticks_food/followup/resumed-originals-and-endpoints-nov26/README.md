## Cinnamon and pain

The 2014 clinical report describes a painful palatal lesion after eating cinnamon-containing cake. Attribution remains limited by a single uncontrolled case, unspecified cinnamon form and other cake ingredients. The 2015 mint case provides withdrawal and re-exposure evidence for contact lesions from the flavored product. Older patch-test and gum series add further clinical context. The 74-case modern series and its 40 literature cases report separate groups. Access to the primary symptom table remains open.

The existing minimal easing signal relies on controlled powder studies in other pain conditions. The scorecard now carries five more clinical context families on contact reactions and retains its four qualifying easing families. The source reports do not establish a signed effect on clinical Parkinson pain from the frozen ground-cinnamon preparation.
