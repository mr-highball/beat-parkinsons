## Clinical evidence

Three Europe PMC expressions retrieved all 12 ranked appearances, and
the ClinicalTrials.gov expression retrieved both records. The source
dispositions preserve the 2025 retraction of the celery-containing
dyspepsia trial. Its results were excluded. The separate human seed
safety reports and preprints were assessed by trial family.

The 2016 primary abstract confirms mania and elevated venlafaxine after
celery-root extract in a patient taking venlafaxine and St John's wort.
The 2022 review describes hallucinations in that same patient without
specifying their modality. The review's explicit visual-hallucination
example concerns a separate ginseng and phenelzine case. A 2009 original
describes transient visual hallucinations after venlafaxine initiation
without reported celery exposure. Recent alcohol withdrawal and
biochemical abnormalities were alternative explanations in that case.

28-participant seed-extract comparison reports mild events in both arms

## Oracle lookup

The original query using “hallucinations” required a second request with
“visual hallucinations.” The native Oracle now returns the same
canonical claim for either phrase. In 11 warm repetitions at the same
claim revision, the two-call path returned 1,980 response bytes and took
508.93 ms median. The single-call path returned 1,512 bytes and took
291.82 ms median. An explicitly auditory query retains its own scope.

The Oracle located 23 of the 66 originals as exact archival bodies on
both catalog revisions. Searching local text for the other 43 found
19 citation or index leads.
