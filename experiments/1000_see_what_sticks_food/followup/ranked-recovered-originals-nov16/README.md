# Recovered original symptom reports and ranked source review

The exact review follows the original 54,158-pair Europe PMC queue at commit `ad832af8`. The selection contains 56 claim–source pairs from twenty disjoint source groups. The prior reservation was excluded by exact key. Forty-one pairs initially reached original full text or the peer-reviewed final version of the same study. Five legacy records initially had abstract access. Official NLM scan images, original JNNP publisher PDFs and the CDC archive supplied the five complete reports and resolved the remaining fifteen pairs. The full PNAS paper describes postural tremor and drooling in a rhesus model. All five pages and the table of clinical signs were inspected for the orange-falls pair 0259. It supplies no fall-frequency outcome. The linked PMC accession is PMC384076. [Original scan and review text](../../../../research/1980-1984/Food-Experiment-Legacy-Symptom-Measures.md)

The 1993 levodopa withdrawal report describes increasing leg pain after 96 hours in one Parkinson participant and visual hallucinations at 23 hours in a different participant. The two events establish symptom context for 0025, 0175 and 0502. The 1993 formulation crossover quantifies stride length by medication and supplies context for 0972. [Original articles and dated summaries](../../../../research/1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Legacy-Symptom-Measures.md)

The 1996 dementia comparison directly measured caregiver-rated apathy and gives group means. This changes the disposition of the source for 0758 from excluded at abstract depth to related at full-text depth. The 1998 CDC-hosted paper distinguishes parent survey questions about pain from the published dementia outcomes. [Original studies and dated summaries](../../../../research/1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Legacy-Symptom-Measures.md)

The 2026 infusion report counts falls as treatment-emergent adverse events by deep brain stimulation history. These are participant safety counts in the parent trial. The report gives context for 0839 and leaves the culinary ground-sage question open. [Original Table 2 and dated summary](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Infusion-Fall-Events.md)

For three Research Square exact keys, the direct preprint body remained access-limited. Full primary context came from matching final journal papers, [digital gait](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12984-023-01198-5), [walking-speed validation](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-51766-5) and [Parkinson PET/fMRI](https://doi.org/10.1093/braincomms/fcaf308). Their published methods and results support the stated context. The other clinical body reviews use the linked direct originals.
