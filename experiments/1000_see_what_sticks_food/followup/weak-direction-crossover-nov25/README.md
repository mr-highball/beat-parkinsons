- **0948, brewed green tea and fatigue.** ProDiet randomized ordinary brewed tea, concentrated EGCG capsules or placebo capsules among men at elevated prostate cancer risk. At six months, fatigue during the preceding month was reported in 5%, 24% and 18%, respectively. The brewed tea arm was open label and the measure was symptom occurrence. Among five healthy adults in a separate acute crossover, total fatigue declined within the tea condition, with an inconclusive beverage-by-time interaction. The small Parkinson pilot with rising Fatigue Severity Scale scores remains the closest exposure and outcome. Compare it with the contradictory human evidence without combining different scales or treating the capsule arm as another cohort. The rubric retains one worsening family at 51.
