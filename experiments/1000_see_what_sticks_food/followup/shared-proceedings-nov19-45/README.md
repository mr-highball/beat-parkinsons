# Original proceedings review

The original 2018 IPLeiria, 2018 European geriatric medicine and 2019 SGIM supplements provide whole-collection access for 85 original claim–source pairs across 64 frozen claims. The source replay resolves 46 related and 39 excluded contexts. The reviewed pairs map to 42 food, 18 herb, 12 oil and 13 spice hypotheses. Related records identify genuine exposure or exposure-derived context and retain the observed endpoint. They supply no new symptom verdict.
