Part III item 3.1 is clinician-rated speech, combining volume, prosody and articulation; 3.10 gait and 3.12 postural stability are examined motor items. Item 2.12 assesses walking and aid/support needed to prevent falls, not a count or rate of falls. The listed parts have no olfactory impairment item. The older UPDRS falling item differs from the revised MDS-UPDRS. A scale's content establishes what an administered item could measure, not that investigators released item-level results.
