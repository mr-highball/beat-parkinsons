## What the current evidence measures

- **Brewed black tea (0904)** retains Maybe True at 51. A short isolated-caffeine trial's two rest-tremor estimates both favored caffeine slightly with intervals spanning zero. The longer Café-PD report gives an overall motor outcome. Its main results give no separate examination rest-tremor estimate. The newly checked tea and carbamazepine paper studies induced tremor in rats and uses a different significance comparator. The MENASA protocol records black-tea intake and a history of rest tremor without an observed tea effect. [Claim](../../statements/0904.txt) · [Short caffeine trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3414662/) · [Longer caffeine trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5664303/)
- **Raw grapefruit (0009)** stays Unknown. A five-person Parkinson report measured approximately 1.7-fold plasma cabergoline when coadministered with grapefruit juice. It did not measure separate rest tremor. The rat study measured onset of induced seizure tremor under carbamazepine. [Claim](../../statements/0009.txt) · [Original conference abstract summary](../../../../research/2005-2009/Source-Text/2005-Nagai-Grapefruit-Cabergoline-Factual-Extract.txt) · [Beverage comparison summary](../../../../research/2010-2014/Source-Text/2014-PMID28911389.txt)
- **Extra-virgin olive oil (0577)** stays Unknown. The one-person case included extra-virgin olive oil in a multifood diet and reported little tremor improvement after later dietary additions. A randomized supplement study used virgin olive oil as its comparison and reported composite motor outcomes. The observed differences leave the extra-virgin grade and isolated rest-tremor effect unresolved. [Claim](../../statements/0577.txt) · [Original case abstract summary](../../../../research/2010-2014/Source-Text/2012-Renoudet-Diet-Dystonia-Case-Abstract-Extract.txt)
- **Cold-pressed flaxseed oil (0732)** stays Unknown. Two independent trials measured motor composites after flaxseed-derived omega-3 with vitamin E. The first trial reports total UPDRS change of −3.3 versus +4.4. The published results give no separate rest-tremor outcome or oil pressing method. The existing plasma fatty-acid correlations with rest tremor were near zero. [Claim](../../statements/0732.txt) · [Original-methods summary](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-Taghizadeh-Flaxseed-UPDRS-Extract.txt)
- **Ground nutmeg (0986)** stays Unknown. The 2015 report mentions tremor in the discussion of earlier cases and describes its own subsequent neurological examination as unremarkable. The later poison-center series groups myoclonus with other signs. Earlier reports of intention tremor and tremor of unspecified posture remain relevant context. [Claim](../../statements/0986.txt) · [Original 2015 case](https://www.njmonline.nl/article_ft.php?a=1536&d=1013&i=179)
- **Raw celery stalk (0281)** stays Unknown. The original mouse paper separately measured resting tremor and reported lower scores with a whole-dried-plant solvent extract. The human synthetic-drug report pools rest and action tremor. Its published baseline means exceed the described subtotal's maximum, and its tremor rows nearly reproduce the quality-of-life values. [Mouse original Figure 4](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12906-018-2166-0/figures/4) · [Human Table II](https://www.spandidos-publications.com/10.3892/etm.2019.7397) · [Local source note](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Herb-Spice-Followup.md#butylphthalide-gait-and-tremor-composite-table-check)
