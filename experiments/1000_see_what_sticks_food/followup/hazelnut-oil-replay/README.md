# Hazelnut oil symptom follow-up

This follow-up revisited all 14 frozen hazelnut-oil claims, prioritizing cold-pressed oil and pain or handwriting, and toasted oil and rigidity. It recovered an actual roasted-kernel oil preparation and a cold-pressed oral oil study with near-outcome measures. No new study measured a named Parkinson symptom under the corresponding frozen oil preparation.

## Sources and applicability

- **Rigidity, facial expression and rest tremor.** The Italian/Turkish kernel oil study roasted kernels before grinding and hydraulic pressing. A separate cuticle preparation used supercritical carbon dioxide. The measured muscle-cell markers and cell shape do not provide a rigidity assessment. The Parkinson menu lists whole hazelnuts and specifies other oils. Its motor scale includes rigidity, facial expression and rest tremor, while the reported total gives no sign-specific or oil estimate. [Cell experiment](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Oil-Replay.md#roasted-kernel-oils-in-cultured-skeletal-muscle-cells) · [Parkinson diet](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Oil-Replay.md#whole-hazelnuts-on-a-parkinson-diet-menu-with-total-motor-scores)
- **Handwriting and freezing of gait.** A spinal cord injury study measured grip and dexterous digit and wrist movements with a hazelnut spread and sunflower-oil carrier shared between relevant arms. The original drug contrast does not isolate oil or measure writing. The whole-hazelnut mouse locomotion study is already linked to 0007 and assesses open-field movement rather than freezing of gait. A drug-induced whole-nut bracing result also holds the nut cue constant. [Digit function](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Oil-Replay.md#hazelnut-spread-carrier-and-measured-wrist-and-digit-function) · [Earlier open-field note](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Macadamia-Hazelnut-Followup.md#ground-hazelnut-and-exploratory-locomotion-in-mice)
- **Pain and orthostatic symptoms.** The 2025 gastric experiment verifies cold-pressed oil given orally and reports smaller lesions and reduced histopathological injury. Neither pain behavior nor clinical pain severity was measured. Two acute allergy cases reported pain and one reported a blood-pressure drop after a baked food containing hazelnut. They provide no oil grade, standardized pain severity or orthostatic test. The registry and literature searches also ruled out oil as a consumed exposure in a paclitaxel study that used hazelnut oil only as an odor. [Oral oil experiment](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Oil-Replay.md#oral-cold-pressed-oil-and-gastric-lesion-area-in-rats) · [Allergy cases](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Oil-Replay.md#abdominal-pain-in-two-hazelnut-food-allergy-cases)
- **Oral function.** A hazelnut-containing mouthwash formulation led to an ex vivo tongue wetting study. Wetting and adhesiveness differ from saliva production, drooling, voice or swallowing function. The registered Parkinson diet uses a hazelnut-containing adherence questionnaire and plans swallowing-related items. The published item numbers conflict with the standard scale and the assigned fat recommendation is olive oil. [Formulation and ex vivo result](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Oil-Replay.md#hazelnut-oil-in-a-sheep-tongue-wetting-experiment) · [Parkinson registration](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Oil-Replay.md#hazelnuts-in-a-parkinson-diet-adherence-questionnaire)
- **Urinary urgency, sleep and visual hallucinations.** The existing consumed-oil study measured contaminant excretion and specified an hourly voiding schedule. Oil extraction and roasting history remain unsettled. The earlier hazelnut-oil pentobarbital experiment measured drug metabolism in liver-injured mice. The general cognitive and registry screens did not provide a matched visual-hallucination, insomnia or daytime sleepiness result. [Urinary exposure study](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Walnut-Gap-Followup.md#oral-hazelnut-oil-and-timed-urine-collection) · [Earlier drug response note](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#cold-pressed-hazelnut-oil-and-drug-induced-sleep-in-a-liver-injury-assay)

Remaining leads include the full 2026 ex vivo gargle methods, the conference supplement’s underlying item-level matches, lot provenance for historic human oil products and unpublished individual questionnaire results from multi-food Parkinson studies. The available measurements do not establish a direction for the frozen claims.

The 2010 aggregate allergy supplement was traced to an issue DOI and 26 native exact-record expressions. Its index matches hazelnut oil, orthostatic hypotension and analgesia.
