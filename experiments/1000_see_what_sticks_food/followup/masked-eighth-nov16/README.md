## What showed a direction

- For 0290, a caregiver observed difficulty with dry versus moistened baked potato in one person with Parkinson disease. In a separate cohort, people with existing swallowing disturbances reported 67 ± 83 g/day of unspecified potato versus 50 ± 35 g/day in those without disturbances, p = .002. Dry and moistened samples are both baked potato, and the cohort measured intake after swallowing difficulty was already present. The raw observations are preserved. The closest Parkinson meal observation reports difficulty attributed to chicken coating, and potato-specific results were absent from the mashed-potato swallowing protocols. A new baked potato sensory experiment found differences by cultivar. A separate boiled potato sample passed fork cut and failed fork pressure. Neither texture study measured symptom severity.
