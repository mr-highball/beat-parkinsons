## 0250 toasted Sichuan pepper and insomnia
Kour et al. 2019 original https://www.jetir.org/papers/JETIR1903823.pdf Table 10 p170; dried powdered Zanthoxylum armatum fruit extracted 1:1 ethanol/water and freeze-dried; 125,250,500 mg/kg orally 1h before pentobarbital in Swiss mice, n6/group. Loss-of-righting duration at 500mg/kg 52.73±3.85 min vs vehicle 79.90±2.65; table marks 500mg/kg **. Latency 5.8±1.62 vs2.55±1.14 min lacks significance mark, even though narrative claims all doses significant and conflicts with table; do not assert onset significance. Drug-induced anesthesia proxy, related Zanthoxylum taxon, extract not toasted spice, no PD or clinical insomnia. No food-specific direction.

Opposing experimental route: 2013 original indexed abstract https://psa.pastic.gov.pk/SearchArticleView.aspx?S_id=27211&articledetailId=14144 (Muhammad et al., Journal of Chemical Society of Pakistan 35(6):1595–1600) reports injected Z. armatum leaf essential oil 100/200mg/kg prolonged phenobarbitone-induced sleep in mice and reduced locomotion. Different tissue, extraction, administration and anesthetic; full numerical table not inspected. Mixed-ingredient PD case with Japanese Z. piperitum and rehabilitation already catalogued. NCT07752329 Nepalese pepper extract sleep disturbance remains pending results.

## 0256 cold-pressed flaxseed oil and insomnia
Already-assessed IRCT20230616058497N1 primary https://jn.nutrition.org/article/S0022-3166%2825%2900818-1/pdf 68 migraine patients, 20g/day flaxseed powder vs roasted wheat powder for 8 weeks, ISI change −4.6±6.5 vs−1.6±4.9 p=.029; whole seed matrix not isolated oil, not PD. Distinct earlier uncontrolled roasted flaxseed sleep-latency report does not isolate treatment effects. A 2024 flax oil human randomized tobacco study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11054311/ gives food-grade oil vs mustard oil but measures dependence and hematology, not insomnia; grade unspecified. No new controlled isolated-oil insomnia result found. Patent https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240173295A1/en has small multi-ingredient drink groups including flax oil and sleep questions; no isolated oil/PD inference.

## 0252 ground saffron low voice volume

A speech-item scale is not a measured acoustic loudness result.
