# Food candidates identified in archived article text

The November 12, 2026 archived full-text screen found 207 human-evidence-candidate pairs across all categories where the archived text matched both an ingredient or related exposure and a symptom, and the title and abstract lacked at least one of those matches. The food category accounted for 109 pairs. This pass checked 101 food pairs covering 98 source keys and 28 claims. The remaining eight food pairs involve four source keys reviewed separately, PMIDs 32041265, 25112783, 31703081, and 40268961. The archived text supplied both flags absent from title and abstract for 80 pairs, the exposure alone for 15, and the outcome alone for six. The review inspected the context of the matched terms in the archived article text and checked study methods or results tables when the article actually involved the food or measured the symptom. The ledger records 12 related pairs and 89 exclusions. Orange frequently labeled a figure color and Apple frequently named a device or manufacturer. Other matches were a gait sign called rocket, shrimp alkaline phosphatase in genotyping, a spoken strawberry cue, and mental imagery of swallowing a grape. No pair supplied a directional estimate for the frozen preparation and symptom.

The cutting-and-eating study used apple in a motor assessment. The final model for that task did not include a tremor estimate. The investigators’ later reanalysis reused the cohort and treated rest tremor as a predictor of motor performance. A separate apple-slice study assessed chewing. The previously cataloged studies using cold potato salad and apple puree measured food intake or plate-to-mouth movements. For `0121`, an infusion study listed coffee among management measures for hypotension and reported fatigue only for an infusion substudy. Two additional cohorts collected beverage and fatigue data and reported them in separate analyses. Following the sarcopenia literature led to Kim et al. In their 2026 study, coffee use and fatigue scores differed by sarcopenia status. The main text and four tabular supplements give no direct association between the two measures. That study lies outside the 101 archived candidate pairs.
