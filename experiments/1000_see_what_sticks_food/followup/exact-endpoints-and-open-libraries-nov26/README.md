## Shiitake and swallowing

The 1992 series records persistent swallowing difficulty with a shiitake obstruction confirmed by endoscopy. The 2009 letter records transient eating symptoms and probable eosinophilic esophagitis. A 2019 meeting member documents shiitake obstructing the airway after a mixed meal in a patient with multiple sclerosis. The original reports do not establish roasting. Their denominators and outcomes differ and provide no population rate.

The 2023 cooking comparison shows generally lower hardness and greater instrumental adhesion under longer oven treatment. The separate Parkinson physiology study varies liquid and thickened boluses and does not test solid consistency in its manometry protocol. These sources describe why food texture matters but provide no measured direction for the frozen roasted-shiitake comparison.
