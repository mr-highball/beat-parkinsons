## Primary-source comparisons

- **0798, cayenne and walking slowness.** A crossover in 19 healthy athletes found similar maximal sprint times and fatigue with encapsulated cayenne and placebo. Gastrointestinal symptoms were more frequent or severe in the combined rating after cayenne. [Cayenne methods and outcomes](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Cayenne-Performance.md)
- **0893, pepper and drooling.** A randomized healthy-student experiment found greater salivary secretion after black pepper oil odor. The official preview exposes one page, leaving the flow estimates and full methods open. The outcome concerns secretion and gives no Parkinson drooling measure. [Original abstract and access limits](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Black-Pepper-Salivation.md)
- **0852, ginger and orthostatic lightheadedness.** The hypertensive water and powdered-ginger arms in an older-women study had similar decreases in resting systolic pressure. The authors acknowledge that the taste was recognizable. No standing challenge or postural symptom measure appears in the report. [Full text figures and group counts](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Ginger-Orthostatic-Followup.md#dissolved-ginger-and-resting-pressure-in-older-women)
- **0994, sesame oil and walking slowness.** The direct timed walks come from a topical oil knee osteoarthritis comparison and an earlier oral sesame seed study. One critical-illness registry plans Timed Up and Go using injected oil as a comparator. The 2024 Parkinson congress item republishes the same 15-person 2017 rectal oil cohort without separate walking estimates. [Topical study](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Sesame-Mobility.md) · [Oral seed study](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Sesame-Mobility.md) · [Injected comparator](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sesame-Mobility.md) · [Same cohort report](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Black-Lentil-Conference-Republication.md)

- **0764, fresh shiso and urgency.** The patent crossover favored concentrated red-perilla extract for nighttime void counts, starting at only 0.24 voids per night. An oral Perilla extract also prolonged the interval between bladder voids in hypertensive rats. The frozen symptom counts episodes of an urgent need to urinate. Both results concern void timing. The plant part, fresh culinary exposure and Parkinson urgency remain open. [Human patent and table discrepancy](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Rosemary-Lemon-Balm.md#rosmarinic-acid-and-overnight-urinary-frequency-in-a-patent-study) · [Animal comparison](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Shiso-Followup.md#perilla-extract-and-micturition-in-hypertensive-rats)
- **0916, ground cinnamon and urgency.** The randomized abdominal patch trial favored cinnamon on its urgency-frequency item, with an inconclusive daytime void-frequency result. An oral cassia mouse study used a later crude powder batch from the same manufacturer and found fewer voids after acute chemical cystitis. In a separate oral cinnamon rat study, the higher dose reduced the bladder-distension response with induced cystitis and increased it in uninflamed controls. The oral studies still leave the direction of ground cinnamon for Parkinson urgency unresolved.
