# Remaining reports, formulations, and symptom measurements

- Retrieve the full Kumagai 1999 report, DOI 10.1046/j.1468-1331.1999.650613.x, to verify whether the clinical course records dystonia separately from improved breathing, cognition and biochemical presentation. The accessible original abstract supplies broad improvement only. Other Leigh cases with parkinsonism and dystonia are distinct patients, and cannot substitute for the index case's oil exposure or response. [Kumagai original index](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10457398/) · [Saluja distinct case](https://doi.org/10.1093/qjmed/hcad256)
- Trace supplier specifications and actual lot records for Tabriz Zahravi softgels, the Lowell research placebo, and the Nordic Naturals vascular-aging placebo. Brand identity, commercial edible labeling, oxidation chemistry, the mention of a vitamin, and the phrase soybean oil do not establish the processing method of the study lot. The Tabriz 2023 publisher abstract and original Iranian registry give no separate Parkinson symptom scores. [Tabriz original publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nupar.2023.06.001) · [Local Tabriz registry trail](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Clinical-Trails.md#registered-soybean-oil-comparator-in-the-tabriz-parkinson-trial)
- Trace the University of Guelph S2015UT01 study report, thesis or grants. The sponsor's project page describes planned identification of crude oil candidates and mouse motor testing, with no completed endpoint report. No primary stride or dystonia report from this exact project was verified. [Sponsor project record](https://gfo.ca/research-projects/s2015ut01/)
- Confirm the composition of the emulsion in the 2023 TUG-891 saliva secretion study by article methods or author materials. The report's accessible text identifies vegetable oil and a contrast that keeps the emulsion while adding an agonist. A refined rapeseed rinse in the Nachtsheim study increased stimulated flow modestly and is a different ingredient. Salivary-flow data in controlled Parkinson cohorts can inform mechanism, but oil exposure and drooling severity would have to be recorded together. [Agonist experiment](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physbeh.2023.114160) · [Nachtsheim thesis methods](https://jlupub.ub.uni-giessen.de/server/api/core/bitstreams/dbb93040-b575-4a69-9d2f-876acc6a2602/content) · [Clinical Parkinson drooling and saliva-flow outcomes](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12371465/)
- Seek food-item and clinical-item cross-tabulations in Oxford and other longitudinal data with a coded usual cooking-fat field. The Oxford questionnaire can identify soya frying oil but its published symptom analysis uses dietary patterns and selected items. The result paper does not supply a named soybean oil and item-level estimate. Any gait score should be distinguished from actual stride distance. Any MDS-UPDRS II or III speech item should be distinguished from measured connected-speech intensity, and any dyskinesia count or catalepsy assay from clinically rated dystonia. [Oxford article](https://doi.org/10.1080/07315724.2022.2056544) · [Frying-fat lookup and limitations in the local catalog](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Oil-Apathy-Clinical.md#2022--oxford-cohorts-cooking-fat-field-and-apathy-analysis)
