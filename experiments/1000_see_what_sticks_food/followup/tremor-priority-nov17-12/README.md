# Rest-tremor searches by ingredient, processing and measured outcome

The original-source tranche authenticated 111 main texts among 119 assignments. This includes 60 archived fig papers, 29 baked-turkey records and 22 across shiitake, caraway and three oils. Eight pairs retain access holds. The Huperzia formulation paper distinguishes soybean oil screened during formulation from the oleic acid selected for its mouse comparison. One proceedings volume contains separate mouse corn-oil and human Parkinson dietary studies, with member coverage still open. Hegelmaier's mixed-diet table names cantaloupe melon, with preparation and individual intake unspecified. The 14-day and one-year results use motor scale totals. The author reply from a separate case-control family gives a pooled cucumber, cantaloupe, zucchini and winter squash Parkinson-occurrence estimate of 1.00 (95% CI 0.72–1.38) per daily frequency. Both sources leave the raw cantaloupe and resting-tremor comparison open.
