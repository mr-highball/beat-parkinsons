# Red cabbage, standing symptoms and original-source review

Eighty-four claim–source pairs were resolved across 71 original main
texts. These include a traumatic fracture case recovered from indexed
sections of the official PMC reader after XML retrieval failed. One
boxing study still has two open pairs. The available main-text sections
describe motor tests without establishing an actual falls endpoint, and
the results tables remain unverified. [Verified scope](pinned-scope.tsv) ·

## Claim 0165

Four Europe PMC expressions yielded 21 ranked appearances. The native
ClinicalTrials.gov search retrieved all 22 records across three pages.
Screening checked actual assigned ingredients and comparison groups.
One blueberry–date comparison measured orthostatic hypotension and
asked both groups to avoid cabbage. The separate Parkinson trial
assigned isolated sulforaphane. [Literature expressions](focused-queries.tsv) ·
[Ranked screening](focused-results.tsv) · [Registry expression](registry-queries.tsv) ·
[Registry screening](registry-dispositions.tsv)

The Danish MAXVEG trial included red cabbage in both active vegetable
diets and reported lower 24-hour diastolic pressure change in the
bitter/strong diet versus usual diet. Its endpoint did not distinguish
standing pressure or symptoms. The Galena trial compared fermented or
pickled cabbage and cucumber with usual diet. Its between-arm systolic
pressure comparison at week six was inconclusive and its food-arm
denominators differ between the paper and posted registry. Both trials
leave red-cabbage intake and preparation unresolved for the frozen
claim. [Original MAXVEG results](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Red-Cabbage-Orthostasis.md#2021-maxveg-ambulatory-pressure) ·
[Galena article and registry](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Red-Cabbage-Orthostasis.md#2022-fermented-and-pickled-mixed-vegetables)

Red cabbage was separately named and weighed in Peluso's mixed
vegetable trial, which reported metabolic and inflammatory markers
without pressure measurements. The VESSEL trial used cooked mixed
soup without a specified cabbage cultivar. The older-adult microgreens
comparison reported similar resting pressures. The Happy Kraut trial
plans general adverse-event monitoring without a named standing
endpoint. A second German registry lists red cabbage in background
material, and a separate mixed-ferment preprint reports no
symptom-specific safety counts. The USDA microgreen project has a
planned pressure trial. [Peluso exposure and VESSEL registry](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Red-Cabbage-Orthostasis.md) ·

## Oracle lookup

Of the 72 originals in scope, 17 had an exact archived body. Citation
search located leads for 31 of the other 55. The Oracle now accepts
DRKS identifiers in mixed batches. Using the same indexed documents, 11
warm native tests retrieved the protocol and both registry extracts in
one request instead of three. Median time was 0.89 ms versus 2.95 ms
and decoded response size was 784 versus 1,132 bytes. The catalog
revision advanced from 32 to 33 for the identifier backfill. Both
managed web listeners returned the expected trial and publication paths.
