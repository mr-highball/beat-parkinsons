## Evidence judgments

| Claim | Judgment and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| [0072, extra-virgin olive oil and constipation](../../statements/0072.txt) | Maybe True at 53. The extra-virgin versus refined clinical comparison remains the basis of the weak favorable inference. PRIME schedules constipation symptom scores and separate stool-form ratings within a whole-diet and activity trial. Outcomes remain pending. |
| [0271, ground ginger and dyskinesia](../../statements/0271.txt) | Unknown at 50. Two ginger registrations list composite motor measures. The accessible Wood abstract discusses ginger for nausea, and dyskinesia in a separate drug context. The body is still needed for exact citation context. |
| [0272, brewed black tea and walking slowness](../../statements/0272.txt) | Unknown at 50. The exact paper measures a different disease context without a tea exposure. The earlier caffeine and observational gait records give no directly attributable brewed-tea walking-speed result. |
| [0296, refined corn oil and bradykinesia](../../statements/0296.txt) | Unknown at 50. A rat experiment reports earlier onset of a combined motor phenotype with corn oil and rotenone. The endpoint groups bradykinesia, postural instability and rigidity. The oil-only comparison had no phenotype during observation, and neither arm gives a human bradykinesia severity result. |
| [0362, dried thyme and anxiety](../../statements/0362.txt) | Maybe True at 52. The dried-leaf trial gives a favorable HADS anxiety result in students. The wild-thyme extract study gives an inconclusive GI quality-of-life anxiety contrast and has unresolved differences with its public registration. The animal studies measure exploratory behavior. |
| [0447, cold-pressed olive oil and shortened stride](../../statements/0447.txt) | Unknown at 50. PRIME specifies same-foot and height-normalized stride with extra-virgin oil recommended within the diet. Actual intake, cold pressing and observed changes remain unverified. |
| [0470, cold-pressed flaxseed oil and constipation worsening](../../statements/0470.txt) | Maybe False at 51. The matched dialysis registry and journal abstract establish an oil comparison with inconclusive group-by-time difference. Oil pressing and transfer to Parkinson constipation remain unresolved. The solvent-extracted mouse oil and seed-containing foods provide preparation-specific context. |
| [0570, cold-pressed olive oil and walking slowness](../../statements/0570.txt) | Unknown at 50. The PRIME protocol schedules wearable walking speed and a separate ten-meter walk. The study compares a diet and activity, with results pending. |
