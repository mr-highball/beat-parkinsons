### Saffron

SOAP06_13 (layout line 48913) is a dose-response systematic review and pooled analysis across polyphenol interventions for depression and anxiety in diverse clinical and nonclinical populations. It does not identify a Parkinson saffron subgroup or any ground- or dried-saffron symptomatic estimate. The only saffron occurrence in the source extraction is its reference to Lopresti and Drummond on local lines 48970 onward. The original report pooled active arms for the main result and randomized people with major depressive disorder across placebo, two curcumin doses, and curcumin plus saffron.
