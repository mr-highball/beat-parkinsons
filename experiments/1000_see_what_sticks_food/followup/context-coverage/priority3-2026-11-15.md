Watermelon recognition was associated with existing rigidity, with odds ratio .866 per score point and q = .022. The measured outcome was correct odor recognition. That association cannot estimate a watermelon intake effect. Lin explicitly overlaps a report by Chiu from the same center and assessment window. The serial olfaction analysis from that center may also have participant overlap.
