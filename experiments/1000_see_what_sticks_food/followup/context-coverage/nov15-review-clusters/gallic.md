# Gallic acid neurobiology review

The archived March 2023 review has 15 distinct original memberships. Earlier historical screening counted 11 appearances across seven IDs in a different subset. [Original archived text](../../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC10015939.txt)

## Black tea pharmacokinetics

Reference 146 administered a brewed black tea exposure or gallic-acid tablet to ten healthy volunteers and measured plasma and urine pharmacokinetics. The study did not measure Parkinson bradykinesia. [Original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11285327/)

## Mixed extract toxicology

The cited ginger, turmeric and red sage experiment is a mixed supplement reproductive-toxicology comparison in rats, with gallic acid as the majority component. It supplies no ingredient-specific clinical symptom result. The isolated gallic acid experimental mechanisms mentioned elsewhere are different exposures. [Primary toxicology report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20422471/)

## Natural sources and date metadata

Sumac appears as a natural source of gallic acid without a tested culinary sumac intervention. A date-fruit administration and pain outcome could not be identified in the original. The article's experimental gallic-acid work supplies no direct primary test for either frozen food exposure.
