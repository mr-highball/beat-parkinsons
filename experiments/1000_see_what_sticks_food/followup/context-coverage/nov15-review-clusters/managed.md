# MANAGED therapy review

The original June 2025 narrative review is indexed as PMID 40559212 and PMC12196375. Section 7 describes essential oils among several different interventions. Its 16 indexed claim memberships concern sage, cinnamon, clove or oregano. [Archived original, Section 7](../../../../../research/2025-2029/Source-Text/PMC12196375.txt#L130)

## Primary source corrections in its aroma section

Reference 92 administered alcoholic dried Salvia officinalis leaf extract orally for Alzheimer disease. The study assessed cognition and supplied no Parkinson motor or fatigue outcome. Its abstract describes 42 randomized; the Results describe 39, with 30 completing. Reference 93 uses clary sage and coriander essential oils in vitro. Reference 94 uses methanol and dichloromethane aerial-plant extracts in PC12 cells. The laboratory preparations and outcomes are distinct from assigned fresh or ground sage. [Dated clinical primary](../../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Sage-Savory-Outcomes.md#sage-leaf-extract-and-cognition-in-alzheimer-disease) · [Clary sage original](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34500751/) · [Plant cell-model original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25243877/)

## Preparations and claimed endpoints

The cinnamon, oregano and clove occurrences in the oil section are concentrated-oil skin irritation context. The general anxiety discussion identifies lavender, lemon and bergamot scents, and the separate movement and fatigue sections concern other modalities. An independently followed clove-oil toxicity case reports acute poisoning in a young child without a clinical symptom comparison for ground clove.
