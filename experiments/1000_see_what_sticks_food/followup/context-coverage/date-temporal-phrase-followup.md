# Date fruit and temporal phrasing in Parkinson records

The archived original search results include three papers where the title and abstract describe a Parkinson symptom but “to date” refers to prior work. The studies provide no date-fruit exposure in their indexed methods. The exact decisions use the abstract as the review basis.

- **0502, PMID 25628915.** The fMRI study applied contact heat to 14 untreated Parkinson participants and 17 controls. The comparison concerns brain activation during experimental pain. The introductory phrase describes what studies have previously focused on. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25628915/)
- **0758, PMID 25379547.** The study validates a Spanish apathy instrument in 130 people with Parkinson disease and 70 controls. The temporal phrase concerns earlier instrument availability. There is no dietary intervention. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25379547/)
- **0758, PMID 32067288.** The 25-participant crossover compares 5-hydroxytryptophan with placebo and reports no effect on the Apathy Scale in that comparison. The temporal phrase refers to the previous research on that supplement. The study does not test dried fruit. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32067288/)
