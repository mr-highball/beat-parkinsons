# Post-traumatic olfactory training provenance

## Rezaeyan original cohort and Methods

The initial structural MRI report PMID 33957160 / DOI 10.1016/j.neurad.2021.04.035 first appeared May 3, 2021 and was issued June 2022. The abstract gives 25 adults randomized to 9 untreated, 9 fixed four-odor training and 7 rotating four-odor training for 16 weeks. The publisher's limited Methods preview verifies 30 initially enrolled, age 20–45, head injury within two years, baseline anosmia required and IUMS grant 97-3-4-12900. The full original 2021 text was unavailable via Elsevier's public API, its ScienceDirect direct route or its subscription EM-consulte page. The latter's graphical abstract and thumbnail returned HTTP 200 with zero bytes. [NLM record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33957160/) · [ScienceDirect excerpts](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0150986121001036) · [EM-consulte](https://www.em-consulte.com/article/1526127/reorganizing-brain-structure-through-olfactory-tra)

The full December 3, 2024 functional-connectivity report PMID 39626165 / PMC11615909 / DOI 10.1177/19714009241303129 supplies the missing formulation. Its Introduction explicitly says it used prior structural results on the participants in the current report and directs readers to the 2021 paper at reference 23. The same 25 people with 9/9/7 study arms, demographic means, modalities and training schedule corroborate this identification. Count one Rezaeyan training cohort. The 2024 article names thyme in the four stable odors and in rotating month one, cardamom in month two and saffron and cinnamon in month three. These were inhaled commercial odorants from Adonis Gol Darou. It reports a follow-up composite smell score difference for fixed vs control (p=.023) and a follow-up odor identification difference for rotating vs control (p=.044). Both trained arms had within-group gains in composite and identification scores. The modified arm has 7 in Methods and Table 1 and 6 in olfactory Table 2. The tests measure full regimens after trauma. They provide no isolated ingredient estimate or clinical Parkinson contrast. [2024 primary Methods and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11615909/?report=classic)

The archived trauma scoping review accurately attributes these odors. The competing 2023 cognition review calls the first-month fourth odor clove, consistent with the detailed registered plan. The available primary 2024 cohort Methods identify the delivered training odor as thyme. Distinguish the reported exposure from the protocol's planned clove. [Trauma scoping review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10374209/) · [Cognition systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9891899/)

## The cited registration and realized study

Rezaeyan's 2024 Ethical Approval names IUMS code IR.IUMS.FMD.REC.1397.065 and registration IRCT2017102034225N1. The linked IRCT source gives October 20, 2017 registration while recruiting, planned recruitment July 23, 2017 to February 19, 2019, with both actual date fields blank. Its public status is Recruitment complete, update count is 0 and last update is blank. WHO's historical February 22, 2018 mirror shows Recruiting and marks the registration nonprospective. The mirror displays July 23, 2017 as first enrollment, the date the primary site labels expected. Do not report it as verified actual first enrollment. [Original IRCT registration](https://en.irct.ir/trial/26206) · [WHO ICTRP snapshot](https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT2017102034225N1)

The register targets 120 adults at Rasoule Akram Hospital ENT center, with intervention and untreated groups, ages 23–43 and married/sexually active eligibility. The summary describes 16 weeks. The detailed intervention specifies 12 weeks, with one fixed regimen of rose, eucalyptus, lemon and clove. Olfaction measured by Sniffin' Sticks at baseline and one day after intervention is primary. Larson sexual satisfaction is secondary. The published cohort has three arms and 25 analyzed ages 20–45, 16 weeks of training, fixed thyme and a second, rotating-regimen intervention. Thus the published report clearly cites this registration, but the current public record does not document advance specification of the published intervention, analysis arms, final clinical population or imaging analyses. Protocol and results URLs and results posting fields are blank in the WHO mirror. No protocol attachment or later public revision is present in the retrieved IRCT source. [Original IRCT registration](https://en.irct.ir/trial/26206) · [2024 Ethical Approval and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11615909/?report=classic)

## Hosseini and other reports

Hosseini PMID 33350991 is an independent publication with its own approval (IR.TBZMED.REC.1398.022) and resting-state MRI acquisition (64-channel head coil, TR 2100 ms). It gives 19 initially recruited, 16 analyzed, all male, ages 18–36, two hyposmic and 14 anosmic. Each fixed or untreated group had eight people. The 2024 Rezaeyan acquisition used task-stimulated MRI with 20-channel coil and TR 3000 ms. The publications share coauthor Arash Zare-Sadeghi and IUMS Finetech and Hazrat Rasoul Hospital ENT affiliates. The fixed and untreated Rezaeyan arms each happened to include 8 men and one woman. The younger age bound, baseline anosmia eligibility and individual Methods support separate designs and weigh against all 16 from Hosseini being the exact 16-man subset. Participant identifiers, recruitment period and enrollment-site linkage for Hosseini are unavailable. Partial participant overlap is possible and these sources should not be counted as independent clinical replications. The verified Hosseini thyme extract in a four-odor regimen remains directly attributable to that publication. [Hosseini publisher and CC BY license](https://ane.pl/index.php/ane/article/view/2282) · [Hosseini Methods](https://ane.pl/index.php/ane/article/download/2282/2274/2299) · [Rezaeyan Methods and Table 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11615909/?report=classic)

Rezaeyan's cited 2023 cross-sectional morphology report PMID 36610937 compares 39 patients with post-traumatic anosmia and 39 healthy controls. It uses a different IUMS grant 97-2-75-12631 and allows presentation within three years of injury. It supplies no olfactory-training treatment comparison. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36610937/) · [Publisher Methods preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0150986122001705)
