# Date and fig lexical routing, November 15, 2026

The native indexed matcher now requires an explicit food, preparation, cultivar or plant name for date and fig. Title and abstract are evaluated independently for these two ingredients. Across 29,243 original date and fig query appearances, no indexed record contained one of the qualifying fruit phrases. Calendar wording drove the date matches. The four fig matches were references to numbered figures. A bare food term with no qualifying context may rank lower and remains open.

The paired native replay checked all 61,252 exact claim–source pairs. It removed 1,248 indexed ingredient flags across 1,147 source keys. Both queues retain all 59,520 open pairs and the same review and access states. Among the open pairs, 170 moved from priority three to five, 743 from five to six, and 267 retained their previous priority. Priority three now has 1,839 pairs. All 18 controls passed, including named dates and figs, date/figure references and mixed food and calendar contexts.
