## Turmeric and pain

Gonehal and Biswas reported severe headache, fatigue and high blood lead in an adolescent. Public-health investigators attributed the contamination to imported turmeric. Discontinuation coincided with calcium and zinc supplementation. Blood lead and headache frequency improved over follow-up, while reported pain intensity and mental fog persisted. The causal exposure in this case is lead contamination. [Adolescent case, Control ID 4271805](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12572470/#Par186)

Singh and colleagues summarized 17 selected adult turmeric-associated liver-injury case reports. Right-upper-quadrant pain was reported in 37.5% of the cases with symptom data following supplement use. The review has no comparative analgesic test, standardized culinary ground preparation or Parkinson population. For claim 0812, this is a low-fit related context about pain as a liver-injury presentation. Six other turmeric claims receive contextual exclusions. The catalog records the review as a case synthesis and the separate contamination vignette. [Herbal liver injury review, Control ID 4267863](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12572470/#Par9673)
