# Nutmeg and carrot original-source review

The November 25 review reconciled a frozen batch of 36 checks across 30 sources. Eighteen checks now have full original scope through 14 sources. Fifteen provide related context and three were excluded from the exact pair. The other 18 checks remain open, including the carrot-related iron-deficiency case whose exact body is still inaccessible.

The review publishes 17 dated source notes, including five cultural records. It adds 18 source assessments and five contextual evidence families. The three claim-specific scorecards were validated by the Oracle and remain Unknown. The replay leaves 31,591 exact checks across 21,363 source keys. The original fig and rest-tremor queue contains 2,338 pairs.

## Findings

- The [1889 reprint](../../../../research/1885-1889/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/1889-PMID36024268-Shoemaker-Nutmeg-Sleep-and-Diarrhea.txt) attributes nutmeg use for insomnia to a clinician and discusses coma at higher exposures. The 1891 reprint repeats that attribution. The [1994 field survey](../../../../research/1990-1994/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/1994-PMID8072304-Maluku-Java-Nutmeg-Uses.txt) independently documents customary insomnia use without a measured comparison. The [2020 abstract](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMC13070663-EPA-Nutmeg-Original-Scope.txt) identifies a case of mixed insomnia in a different context, acute mania after unusually high use.
- The [1976 printed index](../../../../research/1975-1979/Source-Text/1976-PMC1638127-BMJ-Nutmeg-Index-Scope.txt) locates the [Venables letter](../../../../research/1975-1979/Source-Text/1976-PMID942686-Venables-Nutmeg-Diarrhea-and-Toxicity.txt). Its patient had thyroid cancer-associated secretory diarrhea and fewer daily stools during medicinal-dose nutmeg use. The separate constipation letter by Dietz and Stuart remains open. The [Gorontalo survey](../../../../research/2025-2029/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2025-PMID40330798-Gorontalo-Nutmeg-Diarrhea.txt) ascribes a constipation adverse report to guava, and the [Ile-Ife survey](../../../../research/2020-2024/Cultural-and-Experiential/Traditional-Practices/2021-PMID34394387-Ile-Ife-African-Nutmeg-and-Insomnia.txt) identifies African nutmeg as a distinct species.
- The [2026 carrot-juice study](../../../../research/2025-2029/Source-Text/2026-IJHESS10930-Carrot-Juice-Constipation.txt) reports constipation in 12 of 15 before and 3 of 15 after juice in a single pregnancy cohort. A comparison with steamed carrot in Parkinson disease requires stronger data on the preparation, population and concurrent care. The [Parkinson classifier](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32477243-Parkinson-Classifier-Carrot-Software.txt) uses a software package named carrot.
