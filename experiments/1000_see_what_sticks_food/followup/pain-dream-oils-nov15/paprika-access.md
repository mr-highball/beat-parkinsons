# Paprika knee study: Japanese source/access follow-up, 2026-11-15

## Primary article and population

Umigai N, Saito T, Yamashita S, Suzuki N, Takara T, Kahara T. β-クリプトキサンチン含有パプリカ色素摂取が膝関節に関連するQOLに与える影響について — ランダム化二重盲検プラセボ対照並行群間比較試験. Japanese Pharmacology and Therapeutics 2017;45(2):223–233. Original publisher issue index https://www.lifescience.co.jp/yk/yk17/ykj1702.html confirms Japanese title, first author and pagination; Riken Vitamin's institutional bibliography https://www.rikenvitamin.jp/corporate/info/technology/ronbun/bn.html confirms all six authors and 2017, but neither provides tables. The author profile https://www.researchgate.net/scientific-contributions/S-I-Yamashita-2132216394 supplies the original English abstract. It describes **healthy volunteers with mild knee pain**, not diagnosed osteoarthritis. Of 32 randomized, paprika oleoresin 20 mg capsules versus placebo for 12 weeks: VAS and total JKOM declined within both groups without significant between-group differences; a general-activities JKOM subscale favored paprika. No actual VAS means, SD, confidence interval, between-group p or allocation count is supplied in this abstract. This is a no-significant-difference report rather than evidence of equivalent effects; no dietary ground paprika or Parkinson disease.
