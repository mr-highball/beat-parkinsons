## Chao 2025, diabetes olfactory training (0979 query rank 5)

Original indexed record https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40908892/ , Wiley original abstract https://dom-pubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/dom.70103 , first online 2025-09-05; issue 27(12):7040–7051, DOI 10.1111/dom.70103, PMID40908892. Sixty people with type 2 diabetes and mild cognitive impairment were randomized open label to 16 weeks of twice-daily six-odor training (rose, eucalyptus, lemon, clove, coffee, cinnamon) or routine care. Abstract reports better overall olfactory performance with training, without numerical smell results, and MoCA change +1.9 versus +0.4 (p=.001), which is cognition rather than smell. Cinnamon exposure is inhalation in a mixture; neither ingredient-specific effect nor dietary treatment is established, and participants were not reported as Parkinson patients.
