# Paprika and allspice endpoint review

Six Europe PMC expressions returned 105 distinct records. The 89 patent titles describe tobacco manufacture, beverage or food production. The [other 16 records](record-screen.tsv) were checked for preparation and endpoint. The two whole-allspice cardiovascular experiments include anesthetized or injected animal comparisons. A paper that describes central nervous system depression measured acute physiological suppression and analgesia. Its abstract supplies no depressive symptom score. [Search requests](ingredient_queries.tsv) · [Returned records](ingredient_results.tsv)

An intact-rat experiment found less open-arm exploration after repeated isolated capsaicin. The sensory-denervated study observed more center exploration and inconclusive elevated plus-maze outcomes. The mice that received Capsicum fruit fractions also showed muscle relaxation or induced sedation. Smoked paprika composition varies across finished products. The Parkinson outcomes and the actual culinary preparations remain open.
