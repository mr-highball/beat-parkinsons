# Grated ginger and orthostatic lightheadedness · 0852

Two Parkinson diet menus include minced ginger. The trial records lightheadedness safety events by diet, without a standing trigger or confirmation of individual ginger intake. One separate low-fat participant with pre-existing postural hypotension and falls required a midodrine increase. [Original Parkinson diet results and recipes](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Ginger-Orthostatic-Followup.md#minced-ginger-in-parkinson-diet-plans-and-lightheadedness-reports)

The healthy-men meal crossover tested finely chopped ginger, with no reported standing challenge. A different, smaller acute study used ginger extract and reported lower cuff systolic values at two hours from the higher-dose arm baseline. An uncontrolled extract study measured ambulatory pressures in type 2 diabetes. The three reports do not provide a Parkinson standing symptom comparison.
