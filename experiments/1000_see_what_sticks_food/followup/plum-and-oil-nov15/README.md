# Corn oil, carrot, and motor or nonmotor comparisons, November 15, 2026

## Bowel evidence

**0190, refined corn oil and constipation severity.** The original 2014 crossover lists three constipation events during extra-virgin olive oil. Its event list is thresholded and gives no exact corn-arm count. One of 18 participants who started corn capsules reported possibly or definitely treatment-related constipation in a separate 2015 trial, versus two of 35 receiving fish oil. The independent 2018 corn versus coconut oil crossover lists one corn-period constipation event judged treatment-related without providing complete arm-specific counts. The 2025 cardiovascular secondary analysis gives opposite numerical directions for constipation in its Asian and non-Asian subgroups relative to omega-3. The 1988 trial records looser stools and cramps in three healthy people after a high single-dose bolus. Bowel effects differ across dose, population, comparator and reporting. A controlled Parkinson constipation severity estimate remains open. **0623, steamed carrot and constipation severity.** The 1979 original reports greater wet stool weight in five healthy people on raw carrot and unchanged intestinal transit. A separate 1978 concentrated-fiber study also reports a bulk increase. The existing 1994 comparisons show more fecal bulk with raw or processed carrot. This is consistent evidence for a physiological bulk effect under the tested conditions. The available reports leave the direction of Parkinson constipation symptoms after steamed carrot unresolved. [Original 1978 and 1979 reports](../../../../research/1975-1979/Food-Experiment-Bowel-and-Motor-Outcomes.md) · [Earlier catalog review of 1994 feeding studies](../../../../research/1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#carrot-preparation-and-fecal-bulk-outside-parkinsons-disease)

**0943, ground rosemary and constipation severity.** A small abdominal-massage comparison reports defecation benefit with either rosemary or peppermint essential oil, with unclear endpoint definition, cohorts and response percentages. The current official rosemary-extract diabetes trial has no posted results. Its protocol asks a combined bowel adverse-event question. Neither supplies the ground rosemary effect. ## OFF time and other outcomes

**0391, refined soybean oil and daily OFF time.** An original patent describes 43% shorter OFF time in an eight-hour Parkinson assessment and lists soybean oil as a possible carrier elsewhere. The matched regulatory record and paper verify the clinical group took levodopa and carbidopa dispersed in water. The distinct DopaFuse trial used a medium-chain triglyceride paste and changed drug delivery. The ground soybean and levodopa crossover also used a different exposure and measured an acute drug response.
