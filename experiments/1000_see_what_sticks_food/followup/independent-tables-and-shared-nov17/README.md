## Corn oil and depressive symptoms

The 2016 coronary rehabilitation trial assigns EPA/DHA or a 50:50 corn and soybean oil capsule blend. The original reports inconclusive treatment-by-time differences on Hamilton and BDI-II scales. Neurological conditions were excluded, refining grade was unspecified, and there was no oil-free study arm. The 2009 registration and 2016 report are one trial family. For the separate Pomponi Parkinson study, the readable publisher material indicates 24 randomized after 96 screened, with equicaloric corn oil as comparator. Secondary review tables list 12 per arm, but the primary Hamilton table and corn oil dose and grade remain inaccessible through the routes checked. ## Dried celery leaf and visual hallucinations

Zhou and colleagues gave Parkinson participants pharmaceutical butylphthalide. The adverse-event report names a rash, elevated ALT and mild gastrointestinal symptoms. Its stated count and itemized patient counts disagree. It supplies no separate visual hallucination frequency. The existing root-extract case remains one patient with concurrent drugs and botanical products, and the original sensory modality remains unverified. The dried leaf case studied a thyroid outcome.
