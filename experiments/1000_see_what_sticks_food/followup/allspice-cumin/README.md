## Findings from original records

- A UK survey recorded cumin use in 139 people with Parkinson's. Exploratory comparisons using NMSS and HADS did not survive correction. The article supplies no preparation-specific result, effect estimate or pairing with any individual cumin claim. [Survey, Section 3.5 and Table 4](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Allspice-Cumin.md#cumin-intake-in-a-uk-parkinson-dietary-survey)
- An aqueous cumin fruit extract produced no day-five rotarod contrast and longer retention by day 20 in MPTP mice. The same study used passive avoidance and open-field tasks. The behavioral figures say n = 5 and Methods says 15 animals per group. Separate cumin rodent anxiety studies used extracts. Isolated cuminaldehyde studies report fibrillation or water-maze outcomes. None establishes the effect of toasted cumin on postural instability, muscle rigidity, anxiety, attention or dyskinesia. [Primary cumin mouse study](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Allspice-Cumin.md#cumin-fruit-aqueous-extract-in-an-mptp-mouse-experiment) · [Cumin rodent anxiety comparisons](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Allspice-Cumin.md#cumin-preparations-in-additional-rodent-anxiety-tasks)
- The 2024 abdominal surgery randomized trial found earlier first flatus and first defecation after cumin extract capsules. Its Table 2 supports p = .004 and p < .001, respectively. The abstract prints the opposite inequality. The IBS oil-drop case series found changes in stool consistency and frequency without a comparator. Neither study measured chronic Parkinson constipation. An essential-oil trial and a ground-seed infusion trial measured resting pressure in metabolic syndrome without an orthostatic symptom outcome. [Postoperative Table 2](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Allspice-Cumin.md#postoperative-bowel-motility-after-cumin-extract-capsules) · [IBS series](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Allspice-Cumin.md#cumin-essential-oil-drops-and-bowel-symptoms-in-irritable-bowel-syndrome) · [Cumin seed infusion](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Allspice-Cumin.md#cumin-seed-infusion-and-blood-pressure-in-metabolic-syndrome)
- Isolated eugenol pretreatment improved beam crossing in Vora's MPTP model, while all tested post-treatment doses worsened it. Two higher pretreatment doses improved catalepsy and post-treatment gave no clear catalepsy benefit. A distinct 6-OHDA rat study reported some behavioral benefit. Kabuto and Yamanushi reported no improvement in levodopa-related abnormal behavior with eugenol in another lesion study. These are mixed constituent findings that cannot establish a ground-allspice direction. [Vora and Vasconcelos primary comparisons](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Allspice-Cumin.md#oral-eugenol-timing-and-motor-tasks-in-toxin-models) · [Earlier eugenol report](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Allspice-Cumin.md#isolated-eugenol-after-a-dopamine-lesion-and-with-levodopa)
- NCT03926442 and its two main articles describe a pumpkin spice blend including allspice and non-Parkinson vascular or metabolic measurements. The 2022 severe allspice allergy case lacks accessible case details. The similarly titled 2015 poster is a possible earlier report of the same case. The cumin adverse records describe either an inhalational event or acute allergy after mixed foods. They do not supply chronic Parkinson swallowing outcomes.
