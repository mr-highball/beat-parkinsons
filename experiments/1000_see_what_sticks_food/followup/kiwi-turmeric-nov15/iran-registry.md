## Persian-FACT: IRCT20220304054179N1

- [IRCT 62308](https://en.irct.ir/trial/62308); [WHO ICTRP exact ID](https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT20220304054179N1). Official IRCT registration 2022-04-13, timing `registered_while_recruiting`; last update 2022-04-13, update count 0. WHO registration 2022-04-13, prospective registration No; lists recruitment Recruiting, whereas current IRCT says Recruitment complete. IRCT expected recruitment 2022-03-12 to 2022-09-22; actual start, end and completion fields empty. ICTRP calls 2022-03-12 first enrolment but IRCT explicitly labels it *expected*; do not treat as verified actual enrolment. Neither source separately establishes first public posting date.
- WHO: planned 150, parallel, randomized double-blind, functional dyspepsia; ground anise, fennel, Trachyspermum and 'caraway' seed 8 g sachets twice daily for two weeks; control corn starch/wheat bran/barley flakes plus 10% herbal mix. Primary: postprandial fullness, 2 weeks, Gastrointestinal Symptoms Severity Questionnaire. Secondary: epigastric pain and burning, same time and questionnaire. WHO result fields empty.
- Identifier-matched published primary [Samimi et al. 2024, DOI 10.5812/jjnpp-148163](https://brieflands.com/journals/jjnpp/articles/148163) reports the study as May–December 2022 and seed species *Cuminum cyminum*, cumin, rather than assuming WHO's 'caraway' identifies the botanical species; reports 1:1 trial design, whereas registry protocol summary says 105:45. The paper gives 13 April 2022 as registration. It reports results, so an empty WHO results field is not an absence of published findings. It is a mixed preparation in non-Parkinson functional dyspepsia, with no single-spice estimate. Its PDF Methods section has a malformed identifier IRCT202203040504179N1, whereas its footnote and HTML give the matching registry identifier. Confirm original protocol/paper differences before treating any as an amendment.

## Parkinson curcumin/resistance study: IRCT20191231045968N1

- [IRCT 45058](https://en.irct.ir/trial/45058); [WHO ICTRP exact ID](https://trialsearch.who.int/Trial2.aspx?TrialID=IRCT20191231045968N1). IRCT registration 2020-07-20, retrospective, last update 2020-07-20, zero updates; recruitment complete. WHO also says registration 2020-07-20, prospective registration No and recruitment Complete. IRCT expected recruitment start 2020-06-06 and end 2020-06-20; actual start/end and completion fields empty. ICTRP calls June 6 first enrolment but the original calls it expected. First public posting date is not separately established.
- WHO design: open-label randomized factorial, target 40, four groups of 10: curcumin plus resistance training, curcumin, resistance training, control. The intervention text describes 500 mg capsule twice daily for 8 weeks, each containing 450 mg turmeric powder plus 50 mg turmeric concentration said equivalent to 47.5 mg curcumin; training three times weekly for 8 weeks. The intervention-1 narrative also mentions training, creating an internal arm-description ambiguity; do not silently treat that text as clear proof of curcumin-alone implementation. Not culinary ground turmeric alone.
- Primary: UPDRS, neurologist's examination at baseline and 8 weeks. Secondary at same times: malondialdehyde, insulin resistance (HOMA), glutathione, HDL cholesterol, total antioxidant capacity, total cholesterol, fasting glucose, insulin, LDL cholesterol, triglycerides. WHO result, result URL, publication date and outcome fields empty; exact-identifier web searches did not identify a linked original results publication. Registry recruitment complete is not a measured efficacy result.
- Important internal discrepancy: IRCT protocol summary says Parkinson grades 2–3 and ages 18–50, but its structured inclusion section and WHO entry state ages 50–80. Actual eligibility remains unresolved without original protocol/results. No contact attempted.
