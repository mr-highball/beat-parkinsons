The coffee claim (0121) predicts worse Parkinson fatigue from brewed coffee. Its checked grade is Maybe False at the 50.005 minimal-signal anchor. Filtered coffee reduced acute mental-fatigue ratings in healthy habitual caffeine users on an abstention schedule. An independent Parkinson capsule trial measured fatigue separately from sleepiness and reported imprecise easing estimates. The measured caffeine component provides a limited bridge. The four-person espresso study gives mixed individual effects, and the Turkish-coffee study has an inconclusive result with later raw means favoring water. The newly recovered espresso breakfast study shared espresso and had no coffee-free comparison.
