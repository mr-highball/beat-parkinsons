## Exposure and outcome checks

- **Raw apple (0833)** remains Unknown. The German pilot tracked raw apples or dried rings during a four-week high-fiber intervention with lactulose. Supplementary Figure 1A identifies four participants below the study's adherence criterion. The high-versus-low-fiber plot uses total Part III. Neither the published supplement nor the public script listing supplies an apple-assigned or individual rest-tremor comparison. [German registry](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/DRKS00034528.txt) · [Supplement review](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md#apple-adherence-and-motor-outcomes-in-the-bedarf-supplement)
- **Cold-pressed flaxseed oil (0732)** remains Unknown. The original versions of the two Iranian registrations differ from later clinic and intervention entries. Taghizadeh's later registration version adds the clinical motor scale after the trial paper. The Tamtaji versions list molecular outcomes, while the paper gives motor composite scores. The WHO and source registry label an enrollment date differently. Separate rest scoring, oil grade and medication state remain open. [Official Tamtaji versions](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2017-IRCT2017061234497N1-Registration-History.txt) · [Trial family review](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#official-flax-trial-revision-histories-and-rest-tremor-reporting)
- **Brewed black tea (0904)** retains Maybe True at 51. The original breath-testing paper lists black tea, coffee or water as a breakfast option and records clinical tremor phenotypes without relating the findings to beverage selection. The short isolated-caffeine trials and related clinical studies remain the source of the prior indirect lean. [2009 primary-source extract](../../../../research/2005-2009/Source-Text/2009-PMID19941660-Tea-Breath-Test-Extract.txt) · [Frozen claim](../../statements/0904.txt)
- **Fennel, shiitake, dill, curry leaf and cardamom** retain Unknown. The primary comparisons concern essential oil or solvent extracts, smell tests, different mushrooms, mixed diets or compounds. The cardamom toxicology paper uses the name Amomum cardamomum. Kew treats the Linnaean name as a synonym of Elettaria cardamomum. Identity of the actual study material and a clinically measured rest-tremor result remain open.
