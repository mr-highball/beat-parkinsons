## Primary observations

- In a 2015 hospital incident analysis, 11 of 119 selected reports involved parkinsonism. The authors pooled obstructing materials across diagnoses. A separate report described actual food obstruction in a patient with Parkinson’s and cervical osteophytes, followed by jelly aspiration in a swallowing test. The obstructing food was unnamed. [2015 incident and case reports](../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-JSDR-Early-Abstracts.md#2015-hospital-food-choking-incidents-by-diagnosis)
- A 2023 case records boiled beans among the foods resumed after surgery and rehabilitation for stroke-related dysphagia in a person with Parkinson’s. The report leaves species and bean-specific outcomes open for claim 0077. A separate healthy-volunteer bolus study used boiled potato. The 2024 Ishikawa study measured gummy chewing and pooled dietary nutrients. Neither design yields an outcome for baked potato in Parkinson’s. [2023 case](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-JSDR-Recent-Abstracts.md#carbonated-water-and-boiled-beans-after-surgery-for-wallenberg-syndrome) · [2023 food bolus study](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-JSDR-Recent-Abstracts.md#texture-and-bolus-controls-outside-parkinson-disease) · [Ishikawa comparison](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Japanese-Diet-Measurement.md)
- A 2025 report describes two stage IV Parkinson cases. Water helped clear esophageal porridge retention in one. The other silently aspirated water and showed better clearance when alternating porridge with jelly. The observations are case-specific and distinguish esophageal retention from airway aspiration. [2025 case comparison](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-JSDR-Recent-Abstracts.md#water-and-jelly-between-porridge-swallows)
- A citation trail recovered a 2002 report on 15 Parkinson participants. Mean pharyngeal delay and passage were shorter on capsaicin contrast jelly, with no significance tests reported. A later uncontrolled six-month report found no clear group-level videofluoroscopy change in a mixed cohort. The oral jelly studies, the 2025 ground red chili and mochi test in healthy adults, and clinical ground cayenne remain separate exposures. ## Replay

Use the inventory to save each accessible source to `jsdr-<meeting>.pdf` in the capture directory. The screen verifies each PDF against the inventory before using its extracted text. The upstream PyMuPDF 1.24.11 CLI supplied UTF-8 text, with form-feed page breaks. The Free Pascal tool verifies the extracted page count, preserves each one-based PDF page index, and validates the frozen inputs before joining the vocabularies. Japanese matching removes line breaks and spaces, which can join word fragments. Page extraction can miss text in images, figures or unusual encodings. Contextual review used the original PDF when extraction or layout was ambiguous.

```powershell
$experiment = 'experiments/1000_see_what_sticks_food'
$followup = "$experiment/followup"
$capture = "$experiment/build/jsdr-proceedings"
$bin = "$experiment/build/followup-3.3.1-i386-win32/bin"
$inventory = "$followup/jsdr-proceedings-inventory.tsv"
Import-Csv $inventory -Delimiter "`t" | Where-Object sha256 | ForEach-Object {
    $stem = "$capture/jsdr-$($_.meeting)"
    python -m pymupdf gettext -mode simple -output "$stem.formfeed" "$stem.pdf"
    if ($LASTEXITCODE) { throw "Text extraction failed for $stem" }
}
& "$bin/foodexperiment.jsdr.exe" $inventory "$followup/jsdr-topic-vocabulary.tsv" $capture $experiment "$followup/jsdr-claim-vocabulary.tsv"
if ($LASTEXITCODE) { throw "Proceedings screen failed" }
& "$bin/foodexperiment.crossref.exe" audit-topics "$capture/review-keys.tsv" "$capture/all-reviews.tsv" "$followup/jsdr-proceedings-reviews.tsv"
```
