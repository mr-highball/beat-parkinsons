The full boxing study compared mobility and balance over time in a single Parkinson exercise cohort, with considerable attrition. Its outcome tables do not count falls and report no separate rigidity measure. The cerebellar case combined visual symptoms with subacute gait dysfunction during enterocolitis. Parkinson diagnosis, fig exposure and measured hallucination frequency were outside its scope. The earlier UMIN and later jRCT registrations and the 2025 ODYSSEI publication form one medication trial family. In the efficacy population, dyskinesia arose in 39/103 versus 44/107. Time to onset did not clearly differ. The adverse-reaction table counts affected participants with visual hallucinations or limb pain. It provides no measured rate of hallucination episodes or pain intensity. The comparison also provides no test of ground ginger or existing dyskinesia severity.
