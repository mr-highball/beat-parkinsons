# Date fruit, pain, and apathy primary-source follow-up

November 12, 2026. This follow-up traced oral dried date fruit and Parkinson’s pain severity ([0502](../statements/0502.txt)) or apathy severity ([0758](../statements/0758.txt)). It also followed neighboring clinical studies where either the food preparation or the symptom could be verified. No located primary report measured the frozen exposure and symptom together in people with Parkinson’s. [Prior Iranian registry sweep](date-palm-registry-recheck.md)

The later dataset follow-up records the original NeuroFiber pilot abstract, the Stanford registry’s date-paste ingredient and pain collection, the food fields in Parkinson’s cohorts, and the individual date-remedy and pain fields in an independent dysmenorrhea survey. [Date fruit dataset follow-up](date-fruit-item-level-data.md)

## Archive and complementary search

The read-only archive check used `build/archive-body-nov12-continuation`. `claim-query-screen.tsv` has 17,599 search appearances for `0502` and 3,541 for `0758`; `claim-local-text-screen.tsv` has 2,086 and 704 corresponding local rows. The query title filter was `\b(phoenix dactylifera|date palm|palm date|date fruit|dried dates|dried fruit|ajwa|khassab|barhi|mazafati|deglet noor)\b` after selecting the two IDs in the first column. No query title matched. The independent local filter required `text_outcome=True` and either `text_ingredient=True` or `text_related=True`. It returned one pair, `0502` with `PMC7569450`. That review mentions an aqueous Ajwa extract in a rat liver cancer study and date palm pollen in another toxicology study. Neither mention supplies a pain outcome. The targeted archive filters do not imply manual review of every unflagged row. [Archive source](../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7569450.txt#L345) · [Primary liver study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28830415/)

The complementary pass used exact phrases and variants in PubMed-indexed results, publisher pages, institutional repositories, trial records, and cited primary papers. English exposure variants included `dried date fruit`, `Phoenix dactylifera`, `date palm`, `Ajwa`, `Mazafati`, `Barhi`, `Khassab`, and `Deglet Noor`. Pain terms included `pain severity`, `arthritis`, `osteoarthritis`, `dysmenorrhea`, `postoperative pain`, and `labor pain`. Apathy terms included `apathy`, `loss of motivation`, `anhedonia`, `initiative`, and `withdrawal`, with `depression`, `alertness`, and `vigor` used to locate adjacent outcomes. The translated queries included `خرما شدت درد کارآزمایی`, `خرمای خشک درد پارکینسون`, `خرما بی انگیزگی پارکینسون`, `التمر شدة الألم تجربة سريرية`, `التمر المجفف اللامبالاة انعدام الدافعية باركنسون`, `dattes séchées apathie Parkinson`, `Phoenix dactylifera dattes douleur arthrose dysménorrhée`, and `kurma intensitas nyeri dismenore`. Results were followed through authors, institution, identifiers, or references when the study appeared to administer or measure date fruit or to analyze the symptom in Parkinson’s. This pass does not quantify holdings of inaccessible repositories or completeness of web indexing.

| Acute labor pain, Sohrabi et al., 2022 | Persian Tables 2–3 show lower scores with date syrup than placebo at three post-intervention stages. The difference from honey-saffron syrup was clear only at stage two (p = .015). The pain and labor-progress reports identify the same trial, `IRCT20190924044873N1`. The consumed preparation was syrup and the pain occurred during labor. [Existing dated note](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#date-syrup-honey-saffron-syrup-and-labor-pain) |
| Menstrual pain, Atulmuniroh, available October 29, 2025 | Seventeen adolescents had fewer moderate or severe pain ratings after three days of avocado, pineapple, and date juice. The thesis reports no usable control ratings and no single-ingredient comparison. [Dated thesis note](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Date-Pain-Followup.md#avocado-pineapple-and-date-juice-in-adolescents-with-menstrual-pain) |
| Parkinson diet and pain or motivation, 2021–2022 | Palavra et al. reported higher total sugar intake in people with Parkinson’s who reported chronic pain than in those without pain. Fox et al.’s MIND and Mediterranean scores were inversely associated with self-reported muscle pain and lack of motivation. Fox’s supplement Tables S1–S4, retrieved from the NLM PMC cloud package, confirm the motivation and pain items and give combined fruit units or berry items without date-specific exposure. The papers analyze nutrient totals or broad diet scores. [Palavra primary study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2021.628845/full) · [Fox dated note and supplement](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#motivation-and-muscle-pain-in-the-parkinson-diet-score-survey) |

| Older adults with depression, Abdelhamid et al., 2016 | The comparison of three dates daily with aerobic exercise reports a lower Geriatric Depression Scale score in both groups, with no statistically clear between-group difference and no individual apathy measure. The thesis’s separate roasted-seed crossover had no clear alertness or headache treatment effect. These exposures and outcomes in healthy participants leave Parkinson apathy and pain severity unresolved.
