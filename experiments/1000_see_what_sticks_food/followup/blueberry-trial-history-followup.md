## Current design and outcome fit

The current record describes 24 enrolled, Hoehn–Yahr stages 1–3, and randomized parallel comparison of 22.5 g/day freeze-dried wild blueberry powder and a nutrient-matched powder for eight weeks. Each powder used 170 g pineapple juice as vehicle. The record describes coin-toss allocation with stratification at study end, and outcome-assessor masking for initial analysis followed by unmasking for follow-up analyses. The public record lacks arm-specific enrollment and analysis counts. [Design and intervention modules](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT07720869)

Primary measures are gait velocity, center-of-pressure ellipse area, step count, and timed up-and-go. Secondary gait variability covers step length, width and duration across normal, fast and dual-task walking. The LSU announcement gives no contrast, effect magnitude, confidence interval, walking condition, timepoint or fall count. Gait variability is the closest registered match.
