# Strawberry falls and recovered print volumes

Independent library scans recovered three original journal issues. Their print dates, issue numbers and first folios match the archived records. The three strawberry matches name a vascular skin lesion rather than an assigned ingredient. A separate epilepsy conference abstract uses strawberry to describe a perceived smell elicited by electrical stimulation. Its matching full manuscript confirms the member's exposure. The incomplete 492-page conference supplement stays pending. The original 1989 author abstract reports that 38% of 100 Parkinson participants fell and 13% fell more than weekly. The later Ibadan study obtained separate histories at one, six and twelve months and reported falls for 42% of its Parkinson group. These papers help define the clinical endpoint. Neither supplies a strawberry comparison. PubMed's complementary posture search returned the previously cataloged Miller trial. The corresponding registry query returned one unrelated drug trial.
