The 2024 controlled crossover exposed 19 men with type 2 diabetes to ground cinnamon in water or capsules during meal sessions. Its smell outcome rates meal pleasantness. The measurement leaves clinical olfactory impairment and Parkinson disease unaddressed. The respiratory trial publicly registered in 2026 used seven oral essential oils and planned to collect adverse smell changes. Registration followed reported study completion. No results for that comparison were posted at review.
