## Bowel and adverse-event results

Three official ClinicalTrials.gov intervention queries filtered to posted results yielded 18, 18 and 3 appearances for `blueberry`, `blueberries` and `Vaccinium`, respectively. Each reconciled with its reported total and had no next page. The 39 appearances represented 19 distinct trials. The registry safety screen included gastrointestinal events, constipation, altered bowel frequency and stool terms, and speech-related event terms. NCT01823991 combined blueberry-derived anthocyanins with omega-3 treatment and reported constipation in 1/18 versus 0/18. NCT02446314 reported loose stool or diarrhea in 1/30 placebo, 0/30 low-dose powder, 1/31 higher-dose powder and 2/31 extract under a 5% reporting threshold. NCT04421950’s gastrointestinal event category contained nausea or vomiting. NCT02972996 and NCT02139878 reported no nonserious events with a 0% threshold. None of these fields supplies a measured constipation outcome for a Parkinson cohort.
