# Flaxseed oil follow-up

Searches completed November 13, 2026. This checkpoint follows frozen claims 0048, 0054, 0076, 0256, 0418, 0549, 0732, 0873, 0899 and 0913. All ten specify oral cold-pressed flaxseed oil. Four other flaxseed-oil claims already had assessments and were checked for overlapping studies.

## Search records

- [Disease expressions](disease-expressions.tsv), [focused expressions](expressions.tsv) and [targeted expressions](targeted-expressions.tsv) were replayed with the Free Pascal Europe PMC client. The two disease expressions retrieved 107 results in total. The seven grouped expressions retrieved 259 results across eight pages, including the disease queries. The 20 additional expressions supply two per claim and retrieved all seven hits across 20 pages. The seven hits included a Parkinson-model isolated ALA experiment, the older-adult flaxseed oil cognition trial, and five unrelated uses of rigidity or stiffness. Counts are provider hits and can overlap. [Provider audit](provider-audit)
- [PubMed disease MeSH and title expressions](mesh.tsv) returned all three exact oil/indexed records and all 164 records from the broader ALA expression. The latter also matches unrelated uses of the abbreviation. The exact oil records contained two combination trials and an intranasal formulation. For PubMed, searches used MeSH and title/abstract terms to capture studies whose public summaries omit the food name. [PubMed query ledger](provider-audit/pubmed-disease-queries.tsv)
- [ClinicalTrials.gov search](registry-expression.txt) reconciled all 74 studies from the registry API. The tool's broad exposure dictionary flagged 63 candidate fields for review. The Japanese trial record, Iranian trial IDs and EU trial records were checked individually. [Registry audit](provider-audit/registry-query.tsv)
- [Citation seeds](citation-seeds.tsv) produced 713 edges from 14 fully retrieved Europe PMC reference and citation requests. The local review also followed journal tables, author-hosted manuscripts, publisher supplementary files, original workshop and conference records, and registry cross-references. Additional searches used Chinese 亚麻籽油, Japanese 亜麻仁油 and アマニ油, Persian روغن بذر کتان and روغن تخم کتان, French huile de lin and Spanish aceite de linaza with motor and nonmotor endpoints. [Citation ledger](provider-audit/citation-queries.tsv)
- Outcome terms covered benefit, adverse effects and null findings. The eligibility review checked whether a source administered the oil orally, verified cold pressing, enrolled people with Parkinson’s and measured the frozen symptom. Whole flaxseed, seed powder, pure ALA, plasma fatty acid levels, multicomponent regimens and nonoral formulations were retained where the result provided relevant context.

## Study findings

- Two independent Parkinson trials tested combined flaxseed-derived omega-3 and vitamin E. The earlier original gives total UPDRS only. The second gives subscores, with Part II and Part III values printed at p=.05 and no symptom-item table. [2015–2019 research note](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#flaxseed-derived-omega-3-plus-vitamin-e-and-updrs-parts)
- The original Parkinson plasma fatty-acid supplement contains individual speech, handwriting, finger tapping, rest tremor, rigidity and body bradykinesia ratings. The age-adjusted rigidity correlation weakened under sex adjustment. The oil exposure was unmeasured. [2020–2024 research note](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#plasma-alpha-linolenic-acid-and-individual-parkinson-symptoms)
- An oral flaxseed table oil trial in healthy older adults found a phonemic-fluency result and no clear overall Frontal Assessment Battery result. Lower baseline fluency and multiple outcomes limit interpretation. The same registration and results use one study identity. [Flaxseed oil and cognition](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#flaxseed-table-oil-and-executive-function-in-older-adults)
- The first public flax-oil odor learning result was an April 1999 NIH workshop report. Its later 1999 paper and the 2001 detailed report show the same second-generation odor discrimination and 82% lower olfactory-bulb DHA in deficient animals. They count as one study family. [1995–1999 research note](../../../../research/1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#first-rat-reports-of-flax-oil-and-odor-discrimination)
- The sleep leads used roasted whole seed in a young-adult pilot and powder in migraine. The pilot's Epworth table and narrative disagree. The randomized powder trial reports a lower insomnia score. [Roasted seed pilot](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#roasted-flaxseed-and-sleep-measurements-in-young-adults) · [Flaxseed powder trial](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#flaxseed-powder-and-insomnia-severity-in-migraine)
- Head-to-head fish and flax oil trials establish different blood fatty-acid responses in the tested populations. These measurements leave the direction of a Parkinson symptom open. The active NEUROBIS trial uses a 9:1 sesame–linseed blend as comparator for cannabis and has no posted results. [Fish and flax oil comparison](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#flaxseed-oil-versus-fish-oil-in-a-human-fatty-acid-comparison) · [NEUROBIS registry follow-up](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Flaxseed-Oil.md#neurobis-linseed-blend-registry-follow-up)

All ten claims remain Unknown following this checkpoint. Unretrieved Iranian registry records, trial outcome tables and raw data, further olfactory work, and recent isolated ALA model results are listed in [open trails](open-trails.tsv).
