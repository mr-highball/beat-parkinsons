# EU Clinical Trials Register Article 45 follow-up

The November 12, 2026 UTC replay reconciled **six Article 45 records** in the `Parkinson` search. The registry’s public tab uses `art45=1`. The ordinary result page shows the Article 45 count and leaves the separate tab’s result body empty. The Article 45 request returned all six document references on one page. Seven other disease formulations each returned zero, including `Parkinsonism`, `Parkinson's disease`, `paralysis agitans`, `shaking palsy`, `Doenca de Parkinson`, `Doença de Parkinson`, and `parkinsonismo`. [Article 45 search](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/search?query=Parkinson&art45=1) · [Registry search guide](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/doc/How_to_Search_EU_CTR.pdf) · [Query and access ledger](eudract-article45-queries.tsv)

All six individual study records returned HTTP 200. Four concern Wolff–Parkinson–White syndrome or related arrhythmias. Two distinct benzatropine document references have the same title and cite the 1973 physostigmine report in three patients with schizophrenia and drug-induced anticholinergic toxicity. The two records differ in the registry’s age flags and have no linked study document, so the common published report is counted once and both registry references are retained. None of the six supplies a dietary or culinary intervention in Parkinson’s disease. All six detail pages have empty document-link and document-date fields. [Record dispositions and publications](eudract-article45-dispositions.tsv) · [Physostigmine report](https://doi.org/10.1176/ajp.130.2.141)

Article 45 covers older paediatric studies of products with EU marketing authorisation and uses its own record format. These six references are separate from the legacy protocol-index reconciliation. The review resolves the Article 45 result set for the expressions recorded above.
