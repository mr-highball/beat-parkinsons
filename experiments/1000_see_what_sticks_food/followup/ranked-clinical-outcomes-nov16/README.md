# Full-source review of twenty ranked clinical records

The reserved scope contains 40 exact claim–source pairs across 20 priority-four records. Thirty-eight pairs reached full body depth across 19 originals. Two pairs for the Parkinson pain review by Jacob Sage remain open at abstract depth. The source queue records these as source reviews and preserves the assessed food verdicts. ## What the originals measured

Aarsland and colleagues recorded a nonzero hallucination domain in 236 of 537 and apathy in 291 of 537 Parkinson dementia participants recruited for a rivastigmine trial. The findings describe one trial cohort at baseline. They do not enumerate visual episodes or estimate a food effect. Goto and colleagues recorded visual hallucinations in 16 of 19 selected Lewy body dementia cases. Their imaging study also used resting tremor as one alternative diagnostic sign, without an item-specific tremor comparison. Ravina and colleagues separately describe one participant who stopped during placebo after visual hallucinations worsened. The Dalfó chapter recovered from the University of Barcelona thesis contains the original case Table I. Its patient fields concern demographic data and pathology. Recurrent hallucinations are described in the introduction and Salmon appears as an author in the references. [Baseline inventory](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Clinical-Outcome-Boundaries.md#baseline-hallucination-and-apathy-scores-in-parkinson-dementia) · [Diagnostic imaging](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Clinical-Outcome-Boundaries.md#visual-hallucination-prevalence-and-parkinson-sign-criteria) · [Dalfó case table](../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Clinical-Outcome-Boundaries.md#cortical-receptor-assays-and-the-lewy-body-case-table)

The original Wilson disease article includes drooling in two histories and resting right-hand tremor in case 5. The complete astrocytoma case letter on page 653 reports marked resting tremor. The SAGE memory-notebook article supplies separate DASS-21 anxiety scores for participants and spouse coaches. The Parkinson veering paper measures depression as a covariate, and the FACETS registry, protocol and final results distinguish mood and pain domains from the multiple-sclerosis intervention’s primary fatigue outcome. These sources provide clinical contexts with their actual populations and exposures. ## Retained original-source gaps

Springer supplied a subscription preview for Jacob Sage’s 2004 pain review, including its opinion statement and public bibliography. The exact 0025 and 0305 rows retain abstract-stage review. The full review’s discussion and referenced examples are still open. [Publisher preview](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11940-004-0011-x) · [Author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15043802/)

The Wood, rosmarinic-acid and Kaur–Gupta review trails remain at abstract or bibliography depth. Gaire’s original experiment uses synthetic 6-paradol and a mouse ischemic-deficit endpoint. Ito’s mice received injected isolated rosmarinic acid. Fagundes reports lower forced-swim immobility in a rosemary–daylily mixture, with no statistically significant tail-suspension results and no rosemary-only arm. The Ahmad trial cited by an intermediate saffron review used saffron plus chamomile as an adjunct. It belongs to the already reviewed non-Parkinson family and supplies no separate saffron effect.
