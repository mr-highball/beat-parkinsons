# Weak directional evidence audit, November 15, 2026

At the start of the audit, 988 of the 998 Unknown claims had at least one nonpending assessment. Across those 998 claims, the ledger contained 2,540 related rows, three inconclusive rows and 97 pending rows. No row had the exact role supports or opposes in that snapshot. Nine Unknown claims had no assessment and one had pending evidence only. This distinguishes review coverage from the direction of the clinical evidence. The hypotheses were frozen as 500 reduces and 500 worsens.

Every Unknown claim remains eligible for directional rereview. This checkpoint verifies a selected set against original data. An indirect or uncontrolled result may inform a small reviewer judgment if the connection to the actual frozen claim is clear and competing evidence is considered. Score values in the Maybe band express small qualitative leans on the existing scale. They have no statistical calibration. An unavailable source, a missing estimate, or the sign of an unrelated outcome supplies no directional evidence by itself.

## 0072 — Extra-virgin olive oil and constipation

**Maybe True at 53.** In 140 adults with chronic constipation, four-week mean symptom scores changed from 11.27 to 3.47 for extra-virgin olive oil and from 11.37 to 7.74 for refined olive oil. The adjusted comparison favors the exact oil grade by 4.22 points. Both arms received olive oil, and the trial excluded neurologic causes of constipation. The Parkinson trial of a whole Mediterranean diet plus standard constipation care found an inconclusive difference versus standard care alone (p = .60). The matching food grade and symptom score support a small favorable inference across populations. A controlled isolated-oil result in Parkinson disease remains open. [Oil trial, original Methods and Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12694839/) · [Parkinson diet trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11396875/) · [Local oil evidence](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#extra-virgin-olive-oil-and-constipation-outside-parkinsons)

## 0814 — Fresh basil and insomnia

**Maybe True at 51.** In 60 menopausal participants, a randomized sweet-basil leaf extract trial reports one-month insomnia index values of 5.6 and 11.0 for extract and placebo (p < .001). An independent 78-person postpartum trial of shade-dried sweet basil powder gave inconclusive results on overall sleep quality (17.63 versus 16.15, adjusted p = .400). The controlled insomnia result supports a minimal benefit lean for the botanical source. The different populations, fresh leaf versus extract, and the non-supportive powder comparison leave substantial uncertainty. [Insomnia trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36750371/) · [Postpartum trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11795059/) · [Local extract and cohort notes](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Basil-Parsley.md#sweet-basil-extract-and-insomnia-in-menopause)

## 0948 — Brewed green tea and fatigue

**Maybe True at 51.** Mean Fatigue Severity Scale score rose from 28.4 to 32.1 in the 14 completers after three months of brewed tea. The original authors report stable medication use. The dissertation and article describe the same cohort. The study gives no concurrent comparison, paired uncertainty interval, or exact fatigue p value. The exact preparation, Parkinson population and symptom provide a minimal descriptive lean toward worsening. That observed change cannot separate the tea from background variation. An open-label randomized postoperative trial found lower fatigue with brewed tea versus water, in another clinical setting. This weighs against a more general worsening inference. [Local original Table 1](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/2015-Chen-Brewed-Green-Tea.txt#L164) · [Original postoperative trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8563416/) · [Local study-family review](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#green-tea-and-fatigue-in-parkinsons-disease)

## 0724 — Ground cayenne and swallowing difficulty

**Unknown at 50.** The original Parkinson habanero extract crossover measured timing variables with wide uncertainty. Shorter duration of laryngeal closure or upper esophageal sphincter opening does not have a consistent clinical meaning. A separate capsaicin wafer crossover enrolled nine participants with Parkinson disease and gave no separate clinical difficulty result for that group. Those isolated or extract exposures offer a plausible sensory route. They leave the sign of clinical swallowing difficulty after ground cayenne unestablished. ### 0470 — Cold-pressed flaxseed oil and constipation

## 0181 — Dried cherry and fatigue worsening

**Maybe False at 51.** After 12 weeks, a controlled oral tart cherry concentrate trial reported lower mental fatigue ratings during a cognitive battery (−9.5 points, 95% CI −16.5 to −2.5, p = .009). Its treatment-by-repetition interaction was inconclusive. An ulcerative-colitis comparison was numerically favorable for clinical fatigue severity (−2.05, 95% CI −4.87 to 0.77), and an older mixed-juice insomnia study was numerically unfavorable with an inconclusive comparison. Dried cherry sports studies also supply inconclusive or different outcomes. The main result supports a minimal lean against worsening, with major differences in preparation, fatigue time scale and population.
