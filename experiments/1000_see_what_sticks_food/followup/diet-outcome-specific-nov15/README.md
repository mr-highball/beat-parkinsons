# Food exposure and outcome checks

November 15, 2026. The audit added 15 assessments and revised two existing rows. Twelve original source records were filed in their publication or registration periods, including an official ANZCTR record and one licensed full-text article. The existing verdicts retain their confidence scores.

## Controlled comparisons and source limits

- **0209 and 0788, ground clove and apathy or anxiety.** Sadeh and colleagues studied a capsule of extracted clove with citalopram in a depression cohort. Methods identify the Beck depression questionnaire, and the surrounding results describe depression using the same table values. Table 2 says anxiety. There is no separately named anxiety test or verified motivation item. The IRCT identifier appears in the report, while the official record and its first posting remain inaccessible. The publisher's 2020 issue assignment conflicts with the 2019 PDF footers, so the record uses the former provisionally. A mouse injection model and a healthy-participant eugenol odor experiment do not supply ground-clove clinical outcomes. [Sadeh primary table and publication note](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Clove-Remaining-Outcomes.md#clove-capsule-adjunct-and-a-mislabeled-beck-table) · [Mouse outcomes](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Clove-Remaining-Outcomes.md#injected-clove-oil-and-immobility-or-movement-in-mice)
- **0304 and 0607, baked rye bread and low voice volume or raw romaine and handwriting.** A Parkinson food questionnaire named rye bread and measured mycotoxins. A separate Parkinson diet trial measured self-reported voice handicap, with rye intake and acoustic voice volume unreported. A large study grouped romaine with leaf lettuce and found lower risk on a broad physical-function scale. Its items exclude handwriting, and its population and exposure do not isolate the frozen comparison. [Rye questionnaire](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#rye-bread-on-a-parkinson-food-questionnaire) · [Voice measure](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Oil-Comparator-Trails.md#self-reported-voice-handicap-under-a-whole-diet-intervention) · [Lettuce cohort](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Romaine-Celery-Stalk-Cabbage.md#grouped-lettuce-intake-and-later-physical-function)
- **0477, ground bay leaf and constipation.** Two randomized studies used actual ground leaf in capsules or cookies. They measured metabolic outcomes or acute digestive comfort rather than constipation over time. The laurel extract patent gives separate within-arm questionnaire results. A later healthy-adult safety study used extract and reported adverse-event occurrence, without a separate constipation score. The 2021 paper’s full table remains access-limited. A similarly titled tear-fluid and constipation patent tests black turmeric, a different plant. [Ground capsule methods](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Bay-Leaf-Followup.md#ground-bay-leaf-capsules-in-a-metabolic-trial) · [Cookie crossover](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Bay-Leaf-Followup.md#bay-leaf-powder-cookies-and-acute-digestive-comfort) · [Extract safety study](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Bay-Leaf-Followup.md#laurel-leaf-extract-safety-comparison-in-healthy-adults) · [Different patent exposure](https://patents.google.com/patent/JP7412743B2/en)
- **0096 and 0924, ground cayenne and anxiety or cold-pressed macadamia oil and anxiety.** The Peruvian pepper thesis used a different species and extract. Its anxiety table contains counts greater than the stated group totals. The RRIPP-3 military trial compared oral macadamia oil with krill oil. Its anxiety contrasts were inconclusive, and it did not establish pressing grade or a separate oil effect. [Rocoto thesis source](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Cayenne-Followup.md#rocoto-extract-and-anxiety-ratings-in-medical-students) · [RRIPP-3 anxiety analysis](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#state-anxiety-in-the-oral-macadamia-comparator)
- **0685 and 0772, ground paprika and executive impairment or cold-pressed sunflower oil and executive impairment.** The paprika-pilot and UMIN000046785 describe one 31-person uncontrolled extract cohort. Its before-exercise and after-exercise TMT-B p-values were .039 and .061. The later 21-person controlled crossover had an inconclusive TMT-B interaction (p = .877). The 2026 crossover remains unpublished. In a sunflower-oil comparator trial working-memory reaction time favored DHA in healthy adults, with no oil-free group or verified pressing grade. A distinct comparator trial reports mixed attention and name-to-face contrasts. [Paprika pilot](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Egg-Pepper-Cauliflower.md#original-pilot-cohort-and-trail-making-values) · [Sunflower and DHA trial](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#high-oleic-sunflower-comparator-and-working-memory-reaction-time) · [Sunflower and Zanthoxylum trial](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Sichuan-Pepper-Followup.md#extract-and-cognitive-task-fatigue-in-healthy-adults)
- **0285 and 0667, fresh tarragon and pain or raw pear and daily OFF.** Oral extract from dried tarragon aerial parts lowered formalin-evoked scores in a fructose rat model. The closest human study used topical oil and found an immediate knee pain difference with inconclusive later visits. The 2007 Parkinson diet crossover measured daily OFF and put equal unspecified fruit portions in both menus. Six of 18 participants had been in the earlier crossover. There is no raw pear-specific estimate. [Tarragon original](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#oral-tarragon-aerial-extract-and-formalin-response-in-fructose-fed-rats) · [OFF diary and fruit](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#generic-fruit-in-a-short-parkinson-off-diary-comparison)
- **0115 and 0160, fresh dill and bradykinesia or refined soybean oil and apathy.** Focused literature expressions retrieved no records. The dill clinical stroke report measures an aggregate score after a mixed extract. The sole open soybean apathy pair with an archived body reports oil assignment and mouse plasma oxylipins without apathy. [Dill stroke comparison](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Dill-Rice-Stroke.md#randomized-dill-and-rice-extract-trial-after-minor-stroke-or-tia) · [Exact source ledger](../context-coverage/exact-reviews.tsv)
- **0725 and 0759, ginger or peanut oil and pain.** Ginger extract and matched control gave inconclusive pain measurements in a craniotomy study. The original supplement's exact pain and anxiety values remain unverified. This comparison enters the competing evidence for the existing minimal ginger lean. Both post-COVID arms received the same peanut oil, and joint pain was recorded as presence rather than severity.
