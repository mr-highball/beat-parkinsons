## Primary findings

[Hegelmaier 2020](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4409/9/2/376), archived primary `research/2020-2024/Source-Text/PMC7072275.txt`, lists millet among many cereals in a 14-day ovo-lacto vegetarian Parkinson diet. Six had diet alone and ten also bowel cleansing. Table 1 shows a baseline UPDRS IV total in the larger cohort, and Fig. 4 reports follow-up total UPDRS III; neither is an individual dyskinesia severity result. It gives no species, boiled preparation, individual millet intake or millet-only comparison. Existing reference `research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Chive-Outcomes.md#facial-expression-in-the-chive-containing-parkinson-menu` supplies clinical context, not ingredient effect. A staged related-only row addresses 0867.

The [Bu 2024 preprint record](https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.21203/rs.3.rs-5338203/v1) and [Bu 2025 published full report](https://www.ejog.org/article/S0301-2115%2825%2900135-6/pdf) share authors and the same 70 participants and 35/35 arms, meal and outcome, so are one cohort. All received about 300 g millet porridge, then were compared by self-selected epidural versus no epidural during labor; pain was measured at four time points. The post-meal difference is between analgesia groups, with no no-millet comparator, no PD, and unspecified species/preparation details. The 2022 [prior preprint](https://assets-eu.researchsquare.com/files/rs-2293383/v1/479d02a8-815e-499a-bfbf-7873b259fd36.pdf?c=1681973073) shares two authors, institutions and a similar protocol, but reports 86 rather than 70 participants. Overlap is unresolved: do not treat as independent trials. Preprint DOI landing pages returned inaccessible in the web tool; Sciety abstract metadata and the original 2022 preprint PDF were accessible. The 2025 publisher article HTML route returned HTTP 403; its original journal PDF was accessible. The 2024 record is the earliest verified public source for the 70-person report and is staged in the 2020–2024 bucket.

[Patel 2012 original](https://hrcak.srce.hr/file/129346) describes eight people after contaminated bavta millet flour bread and vegetables; flour contained monocrotophos, and severe abdominal pain was part of acute organophosphate poisoning. Pesticide contamination, flour bread and a non-Parkinson population do not establish an effect of clean boiled millet. This is separate from the cataloged Austrian carrot/millet dish contaminated with Jimson weed. The potential adverse context is kept without treating toxin-mediated pain as an inherent millet effect. `2010-2014-Food-Experiment-Millet-Endpoint-Replay.md` and `2020-2024-Food-Experiment-Millet-Endpoint-Replay.md` stage both sources; conservative `annotations.tsv` and `assessments.tsv` preserve exact mismatch. Factual extracts are original summaries, not verbatim article reproductions.

A 2023 study (PMID 36962678) compares fortified versus unfortified fermented finger-millet porridge in children with cerebral palsy; both groups receive porridge and the reported outcomes are nutrients and morbidity, not a Parkinson pain or dyskinesia comparison. Prior registered pearl-millet feeding NCT02152150 concerns iron and reaction time, not these endpoints. Dietary protein/levodopa interactions and pharmacokinetic changes do not by themselves establish dyskinesia severity after millet.
