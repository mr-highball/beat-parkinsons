## Cold-pressed flaxseed oil and anxiety

Claim 0141 received a minimal easing signal at 50.005. The original Patil study verifies actual use of oral cold-pressed oil and a direction in a light–dark avoidance comparison. Healthy mice, six per arm, unmatched saline, possible locomotor confounding, and missing pairwise tests for elevated-plus-maze time limit interpretation. The human Beck anxiety comparison is imprecise and its oil pressing method is unverified. Other rodent results range from favorable proxies with different preparations and unknown pressing to mixed or inconclusive comparator results. The review retains these as separate source families and keeps the Parkinson clinical comparison open.
