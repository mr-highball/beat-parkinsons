# ClinicalTrials.gov outcome recheck

On November 12, 2026, this pass followed all 17 pending study–ingredient pairs in the earlier registry dispositions, covering 13 distinct studies. Each current record was retrieved from the ClinicalTrials.gov API, whose data timestamp was November 10, 2026 at 10:00:05 UTC, and compared against the saved registry text and earlier dispositions. The [study–ingredient audit](clinicaltrials-results-recheck.tsv) records status, posted results, last update, primary and study completion dates, publication findings, and research links. [Earlier registry dispositions](registry-dispositions.tsv) · [ClinicalTrials.gov API](https://clinicaltrials.gov/data-api/api)

The current records match the earlier status and posting date for every pair and list no posted results. Among the 13 studies, three are completed, three have unknown status, five are recruiting, one is active and no longer recruiting, and one is not yet recruiting. The recruiting MED-PARK, Ankara DBS diet, and Medipol L-theanine records retain estimated study completion dates that had passed at the time of review.

## Outcome and publication findings

- **Green tea polyphenols, NCT00461942.** The 2009 proceedings entry and the funder’s final report are linked to the registered 410-participant Parkinson’s study. The funder reported a more favorable composite UPDRS change at six months, no difference at 12 months, and slightly more insomnia with the extract. Fatigue and brewed tea were outside the comparison. This resolves the earlier publication search status for this registration. The full conference abstract was inaccessible. [Existing research note](../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Tea-Followup.md) · [Conference proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1353-8020(09)70555-3) · [Funder’s results](https://www.michaeljfox.org/grant/ability-slow-disease-progression-and-safety-and-tolerability-green-tea-polyphenols-early)
- **Meals Study, NCT07625605.** NEALS abstract 68 reports seven-week MIND diet adherence results for 11 ALS and 20 healthy participants. Investigators, institutions, diet tracking, and study materials point to the same study. The abstract omits the NCT identifier and the Parkinson’s arm. The completed registration has 43 enrolled across the three arms, including Parkinson’s. A qualitative ALS publication under the same IRB approval reports patients’ experiences of ALS and gives no intervention outcomes. The Parkinson’s and ingredient-specific outcomes remain open. [Research note](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md#mind-diet-feasibility-in-als-and-healthy-controls) · [NEALS abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mus.70018) · [Qualitative ALS study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40851522/)
- **Stanford NeuroFiber, NCT07914387.** Stanford cites NCT07127120 as an earlier Sorridi pilot of the branded bar. The earlier study’s conference abstract was presented in 2026. The sponsor reports that 18 people with Parkinson’s finished four weeks and that 63–75% of those with baseline constipation improved. The full conference abstract was inaccessible and the pilot had no control arm. Its registry gives less specific ingredient information than the Stanford record. There are no estimates for separate food ingredients and no reported Stanford outcomes. [Research note](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md#earlier-neurofiber-pilot) · [Pilot registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07127120) · [AAN abstract DOI](https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000212978) · [Sponsor results](https://www.sorriditherapeutics.com/grants#research)
- **Bioactivated glucoraphanin, NCT07360977.** An earlier registry replica records recruitment and May 19, 2026 projected primary and study completion. The current record lists active and no longer recruiting, October 14, 2026 actual primary completion, and February 2027 estimated study completion. The current status was already in the prior experiment snapshot. A September paper acknowledges the same project code and describes exploratory work in a neuronal cell line, with separate funding listed for the cell work. No participant outcomes were located. [Research note](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#registered-bioactivated-glucoraphanin-study) · [Earlier captured registry](https://trial.medpath.com/clinical-trial/122d68d425a28922/nct07360977-glucoraphanin-myrosinase-buffered-solution-neurodegenerative-diseases-treatment) · [Current registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07360977) · [Cell study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13607179/)
- **RESET, NCT07592767.** The sponsor announced first dosing in October 2026. A 2022 publication reports pharmacokinetics for JOTROL in healthy volunteers in the independent NCT04668274 trial. ## Search coverage and access

The [Europe PMC query record](clinicaltrials-results-recheck-queries.tsv) preserves 13 quoted registry identifier searches and 13 quoted official-title searches. All 26 returned HTTP 200, and all 53 returned records were retrieved, representing 50 distinct PMIDs. Bibliographic screening found the MED-PARK protocol, the GRA-MYR-ND cell study, and reviews or incidental mentions. Broader web and institutional searches followed exact titles, trial acronyms, investigators, protocol numbers, funding, conference proceedings, and citations. Those routes located the public findings that were absent from the exact searches. The per-study rationales preserve the search terms and distinctions.

The public registry history pages served a generic site shell, and attempts to fetch full version history through the site’s internal endpoint returned HTTP 403. The indexed Ginger-PD history showed its first version submitted October 12 and first posted October 20, 2026. The current API shows a later October 21 posting. The older GRA-MYR-ND replica allowed the separate status comparison above. The current API shows six other studies with multiple postings, and the complete version histories were unavailable. [Ginger-PD history](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07882108?tab=history)
