## Evidence fit

- Celery leaf searches distinguish fresh leaves, dried leaves, stems, seed, root, whole celery extracts, mixed preparations and isolated butylphthalide. The 2018 MPTP mouse study of a methanolic whole dried celery extract found changes in motor tasks. The 2020 Parkinson diet lists celery without a plant part and mint without a species. The reported motor score is aggregated across components of a vegetarian intervention. A 2023 stem-and-leaf powder trial found no between-arm seated pressure effect. A 2024 hypertension study reported lower pressure after decoction and gave no standing symptom measure. [2015–2019 original reports](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Celery-Spearmint.md) · [2020–2024 original reports](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Celery-Spearmint.md)
- The 2009 nasal polyposis crossover reported no between-arm difference in ability to smell or sleep. A separate thesis that discussed the trial identifies the high- and low-rosmarinic teas as spearmint. A 2014 osteoarthritis trial comparing two spearmint teas did not find a statistically significant walking-distance difference. The 2021 spearmint and green tea extract blend trial found null primary latency and REM outcomes and a small favorable secondary insomnia score difference. Morning sleepiness, depression and fatigue comparisons spanned zero. [Nasal polyps trial](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Celery-Spearmint.md#2009-brewed-mint-and-reported-smell-in-nasal-polyposis) · [Walking distance trial](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Celery-Spearmint.md#2014-brewed-spearmint-and-walking-distance-in-knee-osteoarthritis) · [Sleep trial and results posting](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Celery-Spearmint.md#2021-spearmint-and-green-tea-extract-blend-and-sleep-symptoms)
- The 2025 tuberculosis paper calls the oil peppermint in its title, uses *Mentha spicata* in the clinical methods and uses *Mentha piperita* in its oil analysis. The registration specifies *M. spicata*. The mint arm reported no epigastric pain during washout. The papers provide no voucher or clear botanical authentication that resolves the discrepancy. Durr's 2025 dissertation record and 2026 report describe mint-flavored gum and improvement on two Parkinson gait or balance scales. Timed walking and mobility were null and the report gives no plant species or prospective fall count. Morshedi's 2016 assay identifies spearmint oil in methods and Ogawa's 2020 study uses dementia with Lewy bodies and purified protein terminology. Both were found through supplementary routes. Ogawa reported that a spearmint extract inhibited α-synuclein fibril formation in vitro. These two distinct assays do not measure symptom direction. ## Late primary leads

- Fazel's original Persian abstract from the Babol University journal reports lower post-cesarean flatulence-related pain with oral Supermint oil at later observation times (p < .001). Its 107 total conflicts with one Persian abstract transcription showing 37 placebo plus 60 active. The paper is indexed as volume 7 issue 1, pages 28–33, in 2005. The English title calls it Supermint oil and the exact product species requires verification. Related reports from the same center may share participants. [Original abstract in issue index](https://www.magiran.com/volume/25188) · [Babol article metadata](https://doaj.org/article/ad9620e64bb64daaae0a6fec5fb0a543)
- Najafi and colleagues' original 2013 report of 101 children undergoing colonoscopy calls the product Supermint oil and peppermint oil. Table 1 reports mean pain scores of 4.20 versus 5.60 (p = .01). Methods describe oral drops and a control without administration. The report provides no botanical voucher. [Primary article](https://journal.nkums.ac.ir/files/site1/user_files_358a93/nabavi1983-A-10-25-67-257cfed.pdf)
- Nematshahi and colleagues' full report on a mint product following cholecystectomy gives 60 active and 47 placebo participants. The English results and line graph favor the active preparation later in follow-up, while the first 20-minute contrast is null. The Persian abstract reverses the direction in its wording. The linked registration identifier appears with a different date string in a registry mirror. A separate breast cancer trial explicitly describes the Barij Essence Supermint drops it used as peppermint, adding a product identification discrepancy. Species, lots and registry confirmation remain open. These procedural pain studies use commercial oil preparations in non-Parkinson populations. [Cholecystectomy report](https://jmp.ir/files/site1/user_files_8d9738/a9151737407-A-10-1813-2-fa5ae28.pdf) · [Breast cancer trial](https://doi.org/10.1177/1534735420967084)
