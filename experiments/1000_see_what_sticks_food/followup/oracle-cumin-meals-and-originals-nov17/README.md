Both the 2023 single-arm and August 2024 comparative Anuloma DS reports identify common cumin in the tablet. They enrolled adults with functional constipation. The comparative report's day-15 mean constipation scores fell from 5.03 to 1.73 with the mixture and from 5.77 to 5.60 with the active comparator. The stool-frequency between-group comparison was p=.08. The authors' narrative is more favorable than that comparison. The original August, December and February copies reproduce the same data. CTRI/2022/07/044510 matches several design elements, and the article gives no identifier to authenticate the linkage. The coarsely crushed cumin in Persian-FACT was one ingredient of an active mixture. The comparison sachets included 10% of that mixture. Constipation favored the active arm at week one (p=.049) and was inconclusive at week two (p=.152). The original figures are in Table 3. In a separate Parkinson crossover, a higher-dose Mucuna arm produced 221 versus 177 minutes of ON compared with levodopa plus benserazide, while matched levodopa comparisons gave shorter ON on Mucuna. The single-dose crossover clarifies a concurrent-treatment alternative for the combination case and supplies no isolated cumin or daily OFF comparison. Claim 0064 remains Unknown.
