# Primary terpene studies and sparse-claim provider follow-through

Reviewed November 16, 2026 UTC for the original frozen experiment.

## Lovage urinary symptoms

The distinct Ivanov study reported falling urgency severity at days 7 and 37 after a lovage-root, rosemary and centaury product. Its 125-person urinary infection cohort had no control group. It measured urgency intensity and urinary frequency separately. The registry posted its adverse-reaction result and marks the urgency outcome as unposted. The older randomized comparison with fosfomycin is a different cohort. Its 2023 biomarker subset gives a six-item symptom composite and no standalone urgency estimate. Claim 0841, dried lovage leaf and frequency of urgency episodes in Parkinson disease, remains Unknown. ## Primary compounds and delivery routes

The myrcene research separates drug-induced sleep from clinical insomnia. The Freitas author abstract shows longer pentobarbital-induced sleep following acute oral isolated myrcene and shorter sleep after repeated dosing with a different drug-challenge interval. The authors relate both findings to barbiturate metabolism. A full 2024 mouse paper gives sleep counts after injection, and a 2026 author abstract gives qualitative findings after inhalation. Both measure barbiturate-induced sleep. The original result for minced lemongrass remains open. [Claim assessments](assessment-additions.tsv) · [1993 author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8257941/) · [2024 primary paper](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8247/17/9/1161) · [2026 author abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42858151/)

Klauke's figures show oral beta-caryophyllene lowered some evoked mouse pain responses with inconclusive early formalin and tail-flick findings. Bahi used the same isolated compound by injection and recorded acute mouse behaviors. The thesis figures from Melo give more open-arm exploration after oral isolated carvacrol and disagree on one sample size. The matching 2009 journal body remains open. Pires's abstract and Results disagree about the direction of marble burying after injected synthetic carvacryl acetate. The graph itself remains unavailable. Piccinelli's journal abstract and thesis summary disagree about forced-swim immobility in the same experiment. These discrepancies and preparation boundaries are carried in the evidence records. ## Complementary provider coverage

Four focused PubMed searches reconciled every reported appearance. The single result is an analytical study of lovage roots, selected through the word “attention” in a storage discussion. Its body gives chemical concentrations and no attention outcome. The raw PubMed overlap fields refer to a header-only comparison input used for this retrieval, so they give no evidence of source novelty. PubMed omitted an unindexed chervil synonym and the quoted phrase for lovage leaf in some translated queries. ClinicalTrials.gov returned both of two results for the product and urinary-outcome search. Manual product-formulation checks identified lovage root despite zero native exact ingredient matches. OpenAlex returned HTTP 429 on all six English, German and French expressions, leaving unknown result counts and no retrieved pages. Some aliases are missing from index coverage, and other providers and article bodies remain open.
