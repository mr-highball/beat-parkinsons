# Bay leaf symptom evidence follow-up

The November 14, 2026 pass expanded eight low-coverage ground bay leaf claims and added newly found oral evidence for the previously assessed drooling claim 0299. All remain Unknown for the frozen conditions. The closest reports tested extracts, volatile inhalation, a concentrated infusion, or animal and cell outcomes.

## Findings

A patent-disclosed randomized trial in healthy Japanese adults gives median constipation ratings falling from 2.0 to 1.0 in the leaf extract arm (P=.021) and from 2.0 to 1.5 in the placebo arm (P=.086) after four weeks. The table gives tests within each group and no between-group test or effect estimate. Its single-exposure comparison reports more collected saliva within the extract arm and improved measured oral moisture versus placebo. An earlier rat paper reports slower transit after injection of aqueous leaf extract. The original text of a 2021 Japanese journal trial may clarify overlap and which outcome was first reported. [Japanese patent outcome](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Bay-Leaf-Followup.md#constipation-and-salivation-in-a-japanese-laurel-extract-study) · [U.S. equivalent, Table 11](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20230330002A1/en) · [Rat transit](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Bay-Leaf-Followup.md#intestinal-transit-and-diarrhea-in-rats)

The registry UMIN000041594 lists 44 participants, Tokiwa funding, the same exposure schedule and matching types of oral outcomes. A 2022 sponsor filing gives the same enrollment and two gum result P values matching the patent. The sources strongly link the registry, journal and patent to the same cohort. UMIN does not name the product, enumerate the original questionnaire items or post results. The 2021 journal full text remains access-restricted. [Official registration](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000047482) · [Filed product G1435](https://www.fld.caa.go.jp/caaks/s/cssc01/fwccd01?c__id=a09F900004sk9JGIAY) · [Accessible sponsor filing copy](https://db.plusaid.jp/foods/G1435/) · [Journal publisher](https://www.lifescience.co.jp/yk/yk21/yke2111.html)

The higher concentration of inhaled leaf volatiles produced less favorable subjective ratings on several dimensions in a nine-person task experiment. The lower concentration preserved late visual vigilance. Neither the activity item nor the true-hit index quantified apathy or fatigue. Oral adverse reports add fatigue with probable liver injury and acute infant drooling, hypotonia and lethargy. A small separate 2026 four-week trial reported zero adverse events for 12 healthy adults on standardized leaf extract and 10 on placebo. The indexed abstract does not give fatigue-specific results. The trial differs from the adverse case in preparation, duration and participants. Its registered trial ID remains unresolved among three similar unnamed-product registrations. The Korean Parkinson model papers and patents measured neuronal markers or cell viability. The animal seizure experiments recorded motor impairment with concentrated oil and no quantified gait. An earlier ethanol study supplies related gastric-emptying evidence with no levodopa data. No levodopa treatment effect, dream enactment count or Parkinson gait outcome was verified.
