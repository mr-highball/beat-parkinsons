## Fresh rosemary and depressive symptom severity · 0128

The chronic myofascial pain trial used a blend of rosemary, ashwagandha and sesame extracts. Full-sample BDI-II results were inconclusive between randomized groups (p = .149). The baseline-restricted subgroup comparison gave p = .016. The current public registration lists Beck depression among secondary measures without that subgroup rule. The paper’s PROMIS depression comparison gives p = .798 between groups. Its Methods states that higher symptom scores are worse, while the Results describes score increases as improvements. These discrepancies limit inference even for the blend. The study does not identify an effect of fresh rosemary in Parkinson disease. [Official trial registration and original outcome tables](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Rosemary-Preparation-and-Mood.md#botanical-blend-trial-registration-and-mood-outcomes)

The fresh-derived rosemary drink in the Riby program contained an extract and hydrolat. At most it informs a different preparation and acute subjective ratings outside Parkinson disease. From the published rounded table means, contentedness changed +4.30 with rosemary versus +4.12 with water. Separate within-arm tests describe pre/post changes for each group and do not establish a rosemary-specific benefit. The 2025 preprint and 2026 publication are one report. The 2023 paper in the same program gives identical participant summaries, so an additional independent trial is unestablished. The separate 20-person and 80-person beverage experiments measured affect, alertness or mental fatigue. Neither gives a clinical depression outcome. A numeric table remains inaccessible in the larger Moss report. The mixed-species rosemary capsule trial gives exploratory fatigue scores with inconclusive treatment-by-time tests. The dried infusion study measured circulating biomarkers.
