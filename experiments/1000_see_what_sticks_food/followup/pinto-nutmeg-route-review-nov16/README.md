## Boiled pinto beans and insomnia · 0273

The expanded Europe PMC pinto-sleep and PubMed PE802 captures share the same nine PubMed IDs. Each verified author abstract uses bean-derived leucagglutinin as a laboratory tracer. The studies map neural circuitry, including a pharmacologically induced sleep experiment that uses the bean substance only as a tracer. The distinct P02 set has its own nine existing abstract dispositions. [Exact screen](dispositions.tsv) · [Prior P02 dispositions](../claim-audit-nov16/focused-dispositions.tsv)

The completed NCT06123208 trial compared whole cooked pinto, pinto flour and a shrimp control. The official registry lists 36 actually enrolled, a metabolic outcome and no posted results. The consent instructs participants to stay awake during a measurement, and it describes appetite ratings. Boiling and an insomnia measure remain unspecified. [Registry and consent text](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Pinto-Sleep-Ascertainment.md#cooked-pinto-meal-crossover-and-metabolic-room-consent)

PROMED asked sleep questions in a multi-food intervention. Its published supplement identifies beans generically, and the supply table has no named pinto. The matched published outcomes abstract reports other endpoints. A separate white kidney extract study reports no observed sleep abnormalities and gives no sleep instrument or event counts. These reports leave the clinical boiled-pinto insomnia hypothesis open. [Original studies and findings](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Pinto-Sleep-Ascertainment.md)
