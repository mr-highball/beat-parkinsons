# Outside-query food archive review

The denominator is the 625 food claim–PMCID pairs in [archive-body-outside-query.tsv](archive-body-outside-query.tsv) after excluding 51 pairs already handled in the first, protocol, oil or preclinical disposition ledgers. Exclusion used the exact claim ID and PMCID. Multiple claims from the same article were retained when the earlier screen covered a different claim.

The [pair-level dispositions](archive-body-food-dispositions.tsv) identify the source passage and explain whether the food or compound was assigned, measured, listed or mentioned in passing. Review of the body text and plausible primary citations accounted for the preparation, the outcome and whether participants had Parkinson disease. The review also checked publication status for cited experimental results.

All 625 pairs across 481 source IDs have dispositions in 530 rows. This covers 453 citation-trail pairs and 172 pairs with unclassified titles and subjects, including 200 with a direct ingredient flag. Exact claim–PMCID keys were checked against the denominator and the earlier disposition ledgers. The completed file has no duplicate keys, and each local source reference resolves. No reviewed pair supplied a directional result matching the frozen preparation, symptom and human Parkinson population.

## Source follow-up

- Grape interventions outside Parkinson disease include a single-dose juice crossover that found greater calmness but no alertness effect, and a grape extract trial that reported lower Hamilton anxiety scores in healthy older adults. One cited whole-grape powder rat study was retracted. [2013 proceedings summary](../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#acute-concord-grape-juice-crossover-2013-conference-report) · [2017 juice and extract reports](../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#acute-purple-grape-juice-and-mood-full-report) · [Retraction](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#retraction-of-a-grape-powder-rat-study)
- All seven legacy BIA 6-512 registrations enrolled healthy volunteers for phase I pharmacokinetic or tolerability work, even though ClinicalTrials.gov lists Parkinson disease as the condition. No results were posted as of November 12, 2026. RESET is a separate resveratrol study in people with Parkinson disease. [BIA 6-512 primary records](../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#trans-resveratrol-pharmacokinetic-registrations-in-healthy-volunteers) · [RESET registration and status](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md#reset-resveratrol-trial)
- The broccoli seed tea reports in Parkinson disease were uncontrolled self-experiments. One report tabulates brain fog or confusion and motor symptoms, but neither reports a validated attention outcome or fall frequency after eating broccoli florets. [Dated summary and primary sources](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#broccoli-seed-tea-self-experiments-in-parkinsons-disease)
- The cited 2012 subacute MPTP theaflavin mouse experiment was retracted. The separate chronic mouse experiments used isolated theaflavin. [Primary reports and retraction](../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#isolated-theaflavin-in-mptp-mouse-studies)
- The HELI trial grouped potatoes with grains in a multidomain program and compared coaching intensity across diet and sleep components. [2025 design and 2026 results](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#potatoes-within-the-heli-multidomain-lifestyle-intervention)
- The cited Alisky 2006 letter reports coffee with diphenhydramine in a healthy adult and methylphenidate in a separate person with Parkinson disease. [Dated source note](../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#coffee-and-methylphenidate-in-two-separate-sedation-cases)
- Dark chocolate trials outside Parkinson disease include a null first-hour state anxiety result in the detailed results of a healthy-adult snack trial and lower anxiety scores in a small unblinded trainee nurse study. A separate trial found lower negative affect after 85% dark chocolate without measuring clinical anxiety.
