# Cold-pressed hazelnut oil and visual hallucinations · 0381

The 2017 primary trial administered hazelnut oil capsules in two of four dietary groups of overweight or obese women. The measured outcomes were metabolic and included methylation, anthropometry and lipids. The first paper does not establish cold pressing or a hallucination endpoint. The original Parkinson vegetarian menu lists whole hazelnuts. The older-adult crossover registry NCT06192407 specifies a hazelnut-oil arm and working-memory tasks and imaging, with no posted results. These source contexts leave the frozen symptom comparison open.
