# Tarragon, summer savory and oregano follow-up

Searches ran November 12, 2026 US Eastern time. The OpenAlex timestamps record the same run in UTC on November 13. The search covered the frozen claims naming tarragon (*Artemisia dracunculus*), summer savory (*Satureja hortensis*) and oregano (*Origanum vulgare*). Search terms included both English spellings of savory, scientific names and local names in Persian, Spanish, French, German and Russian. Screening distinguished marjoram (*O. majorana*), Lebanese oregano (*O. syriacum*), Bakhtiari savory (*S. bachtiarica*) and other species of *Artemisia* and *Satureja*.

## Additional discovery and screening

- Regional searches combined `ترخون`, `مرزه` and `پونه کوهی` with `پارکینسون`, `دیستونی`, `درد` and `بی خوابی`. Russian, French, German and Spanish searches paired local names with symptom terms such as dystonia, bradykinesia and drooling. Follow-up with indexed English and Persian records for `Artemisia dracunculus` and formalin pain led to two reports with matching design and investigators. English-language queries for tarragon and human pain led to a 2026 Iranian topical osteoarthritis trial. The article supplies the IRCT accession. The subsequent registry follow-up verified the December 11, 2021 first posting. The underlying master’s thesis remains an open trail. [Early pain report](../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#tarragon-and-formalin-responses-in-diabetic-rats) · [2026 trial](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#topical-tarragon-oil-extract-and-osteoarthritis-pain)
- Searches combining scientific and common names with `MPTP`, `6-OHDA`, `rotenone`, `haloperidol`, `gait`, `rotarod` and clinical symptom terms led to *Origanum majorana* tea in Parkinson’s, an oregano infusion thesis with a separate haloperidol control, and an existing cell-free screen of tarragon and summer savory oils. The thesis animals were healthy, the clinical study used another species and the protein assay measured fibrillation. None supplies an effect on the frozen ingredient and symptom together. [Marjoram tea](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#clinical-study-of-a-different-origanum-species) · [Oregano infusion thesis](../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#oregano-leaf-infusion-and-mouse-coordination-and-sedation) · [Cell-free assay](../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#related-cell-free-protein-assays)
- Oregano and `off time`, `dystonia`, `sialorrhea`, `stride length` or `polysomnography` searches returned general Parkinson care, the wrong-species marjoram trial and two reports of the same crossover study in healthy men. The crossover measured EEG and overnight sleep architecture. No dedicated daytime-sleepiness outcome or separate peer-reviewed trial report was located. Searches for `oregano`, `odor`, `hyposmia` and `Parkinson` identified an older study using a pizza-labeled odor and later work using neat or dried oregano as a diagnostic stimulus. The studies measured smell recognition. A 2017 conference proceedings item also lists oregano among test odors, with no independently verified item-level result. [Crossover study](../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#oregano-extract-crossover-eeg-and-overnight-polysomnography) · [1997 diagnostic study](../../../research/1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md) · [2015 study](../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#oregano-odor-in-early-unilateral-parkinsons) · [2022 study](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#dried-oregano-as-a-smell-test-stimulus) · [2017 congress proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.27087)
- Summer savory and `sleepiness`, `insomnia`, `tail suspension`, `reflux` or `pain` retrieved a rat locomotor experiment, a likely shared cisplatin cohort across reports, a human reflux trial and rodent pain assays. The reflux trial’s accessible paper gives a group-level result and no individual symptom data. The subsequent registry follow-up verified the July 30, 2015 first posting. - A healthy-adult tarragon trial has no results in ClinicalTrials.gov. The public protocol uses a soy-protein tarragon complex, with generic adverse-event collection and no named sleepiness, fatigue or orthostatic symptom measure. The Dutch and Lithuanian Parkinson surveys concern use of natural products or complementary treatment. Their public reports provide no named oregano exposure or matched outcome. A separately cataloged case lists oregano among recommended seasonings for constipation without establishing what the patient used. Web search supplies ranked results with no auditable total. The notes record the primary documents screened and preserve open trails. Local full text was added for five eligible CC BY papers. Source notes retain results for restricted or abstract-only publications and identify the available version.

The subsequent native Iranian registry search recovered six original records. The registry and publication for the almond-oil trial report different chronology, allocation, dosing and blinding. The distinct sesame-oil and dental gel registrations have no verified results. The dry savory powder study found no clear between-group knee pain difference, while the topical mucositis gel study found a faster reduction in mouth pain.
