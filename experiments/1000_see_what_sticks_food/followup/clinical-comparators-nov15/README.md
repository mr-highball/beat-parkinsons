- **0020, roasted potato and daily OFF time.** The 2006 Parkinson diet crossover compared two-month regimens. Mean daily OFF time was 164 minutes with formulated low-protein products and 271 minutes with the balanced comparison (p < .0001). In the 2007 seven-day periods, all six participants completed 84 total diary-days, with 3.5 versus 5 hours of daily OFF time (p = .01). - **0030, brewed green tea and smell impairment.** In the Parkinson green-tea study, hyposmia appeared in 4/5 nondrinkers, 16/22 nondaily drinkers and 60/78 daily drinkers (p=.854). The three daily-volume categories were nonmonotonic (p=.519). In the CHRIS general-population study of 6,690, black or herbal tea users had lower odds of objectively tested hyposmia (adjusted OR 0.90, 95% CI 0.84 to 0.96). The supplement defines this as a different tea exposure. Both studies are observational. An earlier preparation study found that established smell impairment influenced concentration choices, providing a plausible reverse direction.
