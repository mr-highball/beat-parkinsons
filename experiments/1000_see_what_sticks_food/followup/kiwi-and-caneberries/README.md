## Primary evidence and data access

- Kiwifruit and movement. One Parkinson diet intervention specifically named kiwi and raspberry, and its UPDRS III includes gait and postural stability. It reports composite scores for a multi-food intervention. Hardy kiwi juice showed a motor signal in MPTP mice. Common green kiwi juice reduced spontaneous movement in ordinary mice, and its rotarod table showed a small fall-time reduction despite the paper’s claim of no muscle relaxation. The mixture of kiwi, strawberry and dragon fruit extracts in a third experiment has no kiwi-only arm. [Parkinson menu and kiwi model studies](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md) · [Mixed extract study](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md#combined-kiwi-strawberry-and-dragon-fruit-extracts-in-mice)
- Kiwifruit and attention. In the 2015 crossover thesis, previously frozen kiwifruit pulp in breakfast did not show a clear effect on choice reaction time or Stroop. The 2023 crossover with whole fresh kiwi had an imprecise estimate for self-rated waking alertness. The 2022 conference abstract and 2024 thesis belong to that 2023 clinical study. A separate December 2021 registry plans morning COMPASS testing after freeze-dried fruit, with no results and a public status of not yet recruiting. [2015 thesis](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md#kiwifruit-pulp-at-breakfast-and-attention-tasks) · [Fresh fruit crossover](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md#fresh-and-dried-kiwi-and-waking-alertness) · [Separate registry](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/ACTRN12621001685808.txt)
- Blackberry and raspberry executive outcomes. The Whyte parallel trial thawed frozen berries and combined four fruits in one smoothie, with some improved attention and switching measures. Park and colleagues’ Oregon blackberry-only powder poster found higher NIH Toolbox composites, no clear virtual maze flexibility result, and an increase in depression scores. The 2026 older-adult raspberry powder crossover found fewer spatial working memory errors and better strategy. The three-arm mouse study with freeze-dried red and yellow raspberry found no clear memory or anxiety-like change. The Taiwanese black raspberry powder pilot measured global dementia rating. These are separate cohorts, while registrations, abstracts and later papers from each study were linked together. [Thawed berry trial](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md#thawed-berry-mixture-and-executive-tasks) · [Oregon and raspberry clinical reports](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md) · [Mouse dementia model](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md#red-and-yellow-raspberry-in-a-mouse-dementia-model)
- Raspberry and orthostasis. The three-arm crossover with frozen raspberry drinks improved flow-mediated dilation with no clear change in resting blood pressure. The eight-week frozen raspberry trial abstract also summarizes blood pressure as unchanged, while Table 4 reports a systolic time-by-treatment interaction (p = .03). A diabetes crossover reports a tendency toward lower resting pressure. None reports standing measurements or a lightheadedness endpoint. A positive raspberry oral challenge reproduced respiratory allergy with normal blood pressure, while a separate 2014 case reported shock after eating raspberry. [Frozen raspberry drink and diabetes studies](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md) · [Seated blood pressure](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md#frozen-raspberries-and-seated-blood-pressure) · [Oral challenge](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries.md#raspberry-oral-challenge-and-respiratory-reaction)
- Blackberry and gait, sleep, pain and voice. The 2009 aged-rat blackberry diet improved performance on a narrow plank, with no measured clinical walking speed. A controlled trial of whole frozen blackberries tested fuel utilization at a fixed walking task without gait speed outcomes. The 2023 NHANES study used general sleep difficulty and pooled forms. USDA preparation codes can distinguish raw and frozen blackberries, but the sleep outcome does not identify dream enactment. The 1999 case report’s conflicting fruit taxonomy precludes a firm blackberry attribution. No preparation-matched pain or voice intensity outcome was located. - Parkinson data access. The MIND study’s berries question supplies a generic exposure and an association with aggregate PRO-PD scores. The MEDI-PD trial and Lithuanian cohort collected detailed dietary recalls and clinical symptom outcomes but published no contrast joining the named food and preparation to the frozen symptom. The existing Brazilian Parkinson study reported three participants with sporadic kiwi intake and no stratified outcome. PRISM and the gold-kiwi powder Parkinson trial measured gastrointestinal outcomes. ## Follow-up on primary publications and data access

- The BerryCare program registered fresh blackberry eating and physical function on March 26, 2018. Its official JSON reports 38 participants across five listed sites, completed status, no posted results and no individual data-sharing plan. The 2023 single-site paper measured eight-foot gait speed and separately records consumption of plain berries. It gives a baseline gait subscore of 2.6 ± 1.3 among 28 older adults. Attrition of 82% prevented a before-and-after comparison. The complete eight-page CC BY 4.0 paper is archived as UTF-8 text. The same-program thesis repository dates its public availability to July 25, 2021, and its PDF returned HTTP 403. The same-program qualitative report first appeared in 2022, with a 2024 issue date. A University of Kentucky announcement confirms an additional Boyle County site, and the NIH progress report describes planned expansion. The additional public records did not report further gait outcomes. [Local registry](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT03477305.txt) · [Local paper](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2023-Koempel-BerryCare.txt) · [Primary thesis](https://uknowledge.uky.edu/foodsci_etds/72/) · [Boyle County announcement](https://superfund.engr.uky.edu/news/berrycare-kicks-off-boyle-county-senior-center) · [NIH project progress](https://tools.niehs.nih.gov/srp/programs/progress_report.cfm?Project_ID=P42ES0073809103)
- Xiao and colleagues’ summer 2023 report for NCT03879213 confirms that the spatial working memory result occurred at six hours. The authors list Profile of Mood States among the study measures and state that the other measured outcomes did not attain statistical significance. They provide no separate anxiety estimate. The January 2026 paper reports results from the same study. Its publicly downloadable 19,324-byte supplement contained a single recipe table. The active meal used 12.5 g red raspberry powder in its drink and 12.5 g in its breakfast components, with adjustments to flavorings, sugars and other recipe quantities between meals. No CANTAB or POMS outcome estimates appear in the supplement. Searches for the registration and study author in Dryad, Figshare, OSF and USDA Ag Data Commons yielded no public participant-level dataset. [2023 investigator report](https://www.raspberryblackberry.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/NARBA-2023-BRAMBLE-Summer-Vol-38-Digital-Issue.pdf) · [2026 article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41536265/) · [Publisher supplement](https://static.cambridge.org/content/id/urn%3Acambridge.org%3Aid%3Aarticle%3AS0007114525105497/resource/name/S0007114525105497sup001.docx)
- Carey and colleagues first published July 20, 2019. Red raspberry freeze-dried powder preserved novel object recognition and shortened Barnes maze escape latency in a high-fat diet mouse study. A separate APP/PS-1 experiment did not show a clear cognitive benefit. The publisher and coauthor publication listing supplied the Carey reference, and no redistributable full text or underlying data file was identified. [Dated study note](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Kiwi-and-Caneberries-Source-Followup.md#freeze-dried-red-raspberry-and-cognition-in-high-fat-fed-mice) · [2019 primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31328696/) · [2022 primary report](https://doi.org/10.1080/09637486.2022.2141209)
- The Georgia State series includes a 2019 angiotensin II mouse experiment, a separate high-fat and high-sucrose mouse experiment first published in 2022, and two angiotensin II rat abstracts in 2022 and 2024. Both rat reports found lower systolic blood pressure after raspberry. The abstracts share investigators, timing, species and grant identifiers. Their cohort overlap remains unresolved. The 2022 rat abstract’s PMC metadata license fell outside the archive scope. The 2024 publisher PDF returned HTTP 403 to the native download, and third-party records disagreed on its license. NIFA confirms the common grant but did not identify raw data. ### Later publications connected to the original study families

- The indexed publisher preview of Meister and colleagues’ high-fat, high-sucrose mouse experiment gives a raspberry-only systolic pressure of 98.9 ± 6.25 mmHg versus 120.6 ± 6.79 mmHg on the high-fat, high-sucrose diet (p < .0001). The combination of the two berries yielded 106.0 ± 9.10 mmHg (p = .002). The March 2022 cardiac tissue paper shares investigators, funding and feeding schedule, and the later paper refers to its body-weight measurements as previously published. Treat the tissue paper as a likely companion. [Publisher Figure 2 preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0955286322002935) · [Companion cardiac tissue report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35487829/)
- Carey reported a preceding experiment at the March 2017 Berry Health Benefits Symposium, with the same 4% freeze-dried red raspberry exposure, high-fat diet, roughly five-month Barnes maze signal and cortical inflammation result. It names the powder supplier. The 2017 report and the 2019 journal article are treated as one probable study family. [Proceedings, p. 48](https://berryhealth.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/2017_BHBS_PRE-PROCEEDINGS.pdf) · [2019 journal article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31328696/)
- Kitamura’s investigator-authored section in the February 20, 2025 symposium report explicitly presents the 2021 paper as the underlying experiment. It gives cohort sizes of nine controls, nine MPTP mice and eight juice-plus-MPTP mice. It reports a significant attenuation of motor impairment for the juice group (p < .01) after pole, catalepsy and beam-walk testing, and attenuation of the reduction in tyrosine hydroxylase measures (p < .05). The dose preparation in the original study used juice pressed from previously frozen hardy kiwifruit. The 2025 report says testing occurred 21 days after final MPTP, while the 2021 publisher preview says 22 days. The KAKEN final report first appeared on January 30, 2025 and identifies the same original publication and research program. These reports do not constitute independent confirmations.
