Whole edible seed, toasted seed, ground seed, cold-pressed oil, extracts and isolated constituents were traced independently. The exact methods distinguish *Nigella sativa* (kalonji) from *Cuminum cyminum*, *Foeniculum vulgare*, and *Trachyspermum ammi*. A publication mentioning black soybean seed coat is a lexical false match. Common names such as black cumin and black caraway require a botanical check because they can apply to different species. A primary preparation study reports a thymoquinone signal after one heating process and absent detection under another condition.
