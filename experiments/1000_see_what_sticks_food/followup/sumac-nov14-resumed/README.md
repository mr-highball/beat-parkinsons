- The Parkinson donor cells showed lower peroxide and also lower MTT viability in two donor cultures at higher exposure. ATP varied by donor and treatment. Donor baseline UPDRS data cannot establish change after sumac. In a separate mouse experiment, open-field crossings and rearings had non-significant comparisons. The fruit drink in healthy adults measured exercise pain and supplied no gait endpoint.
