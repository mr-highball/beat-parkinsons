# Original green tea polyphenol trial outcome access

The exact Chan P2.204 record is the December 2009 abstract in *Parkinsonism & Related Disorders* 15 supplement, printed S145. A publisher issue index exposes the title and PDF link. The direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. No original abstract text, smell test or item data were recovered. The official registration NCT00461942 lists motor progression, cognition, mood and daily-life quality and has no posted result section. [Issue index](https://www.prd-journal.com/issue/S1353-8020%2809%29X7002-1?page=15&pageStart=10) · [Proceedings DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1353-8020(09)70555-3) · [Official trial registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00461942)

A contemporaneous Spanish December 2009 conference report on printed page 30 independently describes the same authors, 32 sites and 410 participants. The article paraphrases the six- and twelve-month total UPDRS and insomnia results and provides no olfaction outcome. Some wording treats the intervention as tea and the participants as tea drinkers, whereas the funder describes oral tea polyphenols and a restriction on heavy tea drinking. Preserve the observed registry enrollment count of 480 separately from the funder count of 410. The reports do not resolve the flow denominator. A public regulatory submission's section II.C.1.e cites the funder for the qualitative results, and the Chinese institutional summary identifies the research without individual outcome data. [Spanish report](https://getm.sen.es/pdf/2012/02/revista_II_N4.pdf) · [Funder account](https://www.michaeljfox.org/grant/ability-slow-disease-progression-and-safety-and-tolerability-green-tea-polyphenols-early) · [Public submission](https://downloads.regulations.gov/FDA-2021-P-1115-0006/content.pdf) · [Chinese institutional summary](https://neurology.91health.net/Html/News/Articles/41.html)

Indexed relevance results located the corresponding records above. Provider-wide results were not enumerated.
