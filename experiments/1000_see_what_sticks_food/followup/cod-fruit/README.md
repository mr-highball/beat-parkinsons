# Cod, pineapple, plum, and cantaloupe follow-up

The November 13, 2026 UTC pass covered eight frozen claims. It supplies 19 related assessments across all eight. Each clinical direction remains unresolved. [Claim rationales](claim-rationales.tsv) · [Open trails](open-trails.tsv)

## Findings by ingredient

- Cod hydrolysate added to a cycling drink gave an inconclusive time-to-exhaustion comparison in healthy men. A separate older-adult study found inconclusive grip and gait results after cod hydrolysate. Parkinson food surveys measure general fish intake or overall dietary intake, and an olfactory assessment used unspecified fish as an odor stimulus. None measured fatigue severity or micrographia after grilled cod or olfaction after baked cod. [Cycling trial](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#cod-protein-hydrolysate-and-cycling-exhaustion) · [Older adult trial](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#cod-hydrolysate-and-physical-function-in-older-adults)
- The 2020 inpatient Parkinson menu lists pineapple, plum and cantaloupe, with motor improvement on the aggregate UPDRS III. Its component rest tremor and hand measures are unavailable separately. The blueberry Parkinson trial used pineapple juice in both groups, and a respiratory-training study used pineapple tidbits plus juice as a swallowing test bolus. The latter gives a documented bolus exposure without a fruit effect on dexterity. [Parkinson menu](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#pineapple-plum-and-cantaloupe-in-a-parkinson-dietary-intervention) · [Blueberry vehicle](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Registry-Followup.md) · [Swallowing trial](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Choking-Followup.md#pineapple-and-vanilla-pudding-in-respiratory-swallow-training)
- Injected bromelain pretreatment improved rat forelimb and stepping proxies in a 6-OHDA model. Pineapple or juice ranked among self-reported bladder flare triggers, yet a MAPP case-crossover pooled pineapple fruit and juice and yielded inconclusive flare odds, 0.87 overall and 1.32 in the suspected-trigger subgroup. An additional Veterans Affairs sample asked about pineapple juice sensitivity. None identifies raw pineapple effects on Parkinson dexterity or urinary urgency. [Animal outcomes](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#injected-bromelain-and-rat-motor-responses) · [Case-crossover](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#pineapple-intake-and-bladder-or-pelvic-pain-flares) · [Veterans study](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#pineapple-juice-sensitivity-in-a-veterans-cohort)
- Earlier and later plum preference surveys found different directions at different life stages. A primary culture study found no clear effect from plum or prune extracts under rotenone. A later study found a protective cell-survival result under paraquat and an inconclusive result under rotenone. A 1972 bench letter records darkening when powdered levodopa was mixed with plum pieces. These observations do not measure dyskinesia following poached plum ingestion. [Early preference](../../../../research/1985-1989/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#early-adult-plum-preference-and-parkinson-case-status) · [Current preference](../../../../research/1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#current-plum-preference-and-fish-intake-in-parkinson) · [Culture comparison](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cod-Fruit.md#plum-extract-under-paraquat-and-rotenone) · [1972 letter note](../../../../research/1970-1974/Food-and-Nutrition.md)
- Two Extramel trials reported improved fatigue-related measures. The 2010 GliSODin trial, 2020 SODB exercise study and 2022 GliSODin mobility trial found inconclusive between-group fatigue effects. The proprietary concentrates, coatings, participant groups and scales varied. No trial tested raw cantaloupe for Parkinson fatigue or rest tremor.
