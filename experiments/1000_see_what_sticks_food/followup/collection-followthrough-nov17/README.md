Three focused title-and-abstract queries returned zero corn-oil and sleep results. Extending the corn-oil term to methods and broader indexed text returned 19 appearances across 15 source keys, including original clinical reports that name corn oil only in their methods. Each retrieved appearance has a disposition at its verified depth. This shows a coverage gap in the narrower expressions and leaves each unmatched outcome open. ## Corn oil and insomnia severity

The 2018 overnight crossover gives 26 complete records. Corn oil was held constant in both capsules. The measured sleep-onset and wake-after-onset comparisons were inconclusive, and the PSQI was used for screening. A separate 2026 postherpetic-neuralgia trial used corn oil in a topical comparison paste and reports global sleep-quality scores. The adjusted active-versus-comparison difference was −1.95 points, with topical co-ingredients varying and no isolated oral corn-oil exposure. [Overnight study and tables](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Corn-Oil-Sleep-Laboratory.md) · [Topical comparison and tables](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Corn-Oil-Topical-Sleep-Quality.md)

Food2Learn describes a mixed-oil adolescent comparator and a planned 64-person sleep substudy. The original protocol names two sleep questionnaires and activity monitoring, while the current registry has no posted results. The author's thesis reports cognition and mood in later chapters, and the named sleep scales were found in the protocol. Numerical arm-specific sleep results remain open. The official NCT01216982 registration shares ethics identifier M-10023 with a Johnston observational report. The accessible Dretsch 2014 abstract does not identify that trial directly or give sleep values. A purported online full-text copy renders a different study. ## Soybean oil and swallowing

Three Europe PMC expressions returned ten appearances. Three ClinicalTrials.gov expressions returned four, including two studies that use soybean-oil-containing capsules but measure other outcomes. Capsule administration and absence of particular reported adverse events leave swallowing severity unmeasured. Existing model foods and gel studies also do not supply graded Parkinson dysphagia outcomes. ## Mango and fatigue

The 2024 randomized resistance trial tested mango stem bark mixed with a flower extract. Bench-press strength and repetitions had favorable results in some arms. Subjective fatigue and mango fruit were not evaluated. The earlier IUPHAR member has matching investigators, doses, sample size and training arms and appears to be the same trial. Its abstract provides no numerical results. [Primary trial and likely abstract lineage](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Mango-Bark-Exercise.md)
