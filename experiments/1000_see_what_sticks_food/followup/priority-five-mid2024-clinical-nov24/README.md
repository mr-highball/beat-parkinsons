The multiple sclerosis coffee cohort had no clear difference in measured fatigue across four intake groups. A separate randomized trial reports less multiple sclerosis fatigue with isolated caffeine. Its complete outcome tables remain unavailable. These results add a beverage and constituent comparison outside Parkinson disease.

The new lemon balm review makes a descriptive reanalysis of an existing diabetes trial. Its interval and the investigator's adjusted model should be read separately. The review has no additional brewed-tea cohort. Clinical stimulation and olfaction reports supplied symptom measures without assigning raw fig or ground sage.
