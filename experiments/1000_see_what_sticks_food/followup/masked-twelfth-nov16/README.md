## Findings closest to the claims

- **0389 celery and daytime sleepiness.** The official Iranian registry gives March 4, 2024 and recruitment complete. The randomized protocol uses bedtime dry extract in older adults and prespecifies overall sleep quality. It gives no result or independently specified daytime sleepiness outcome. The Persian intervention section and English detailed section use mg for cornstarch where the English summary has an mL inconsistency. [Primary registration and limitations](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Celery-Older-Adult-Sleep-Protocol.md)
- **0393 walnut oil and anxiety.** The acute oil trial found faster extinction of a conditioned prediction, and a chemistry trial verifies a similar alpha-linolenic acid content in cold-pressed oil. The 2016 whole-walnut crossover's pooled tension-anxiety comparison was inconclusive (11.2 versus 11.9, p = .507), with different trends across its sex strata. A separate academic-stress trial found a time effect without a clear treatment effect on DASS anxiety. A 2026 crossover favored whole walnuts on total mood. The tested oil's pressing method and anxiety subscale remain open. The whole-food anxiety and oil-learning findings do not identify a direction for clinically rated Parkinson anxiety. [Oil trial](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tree-Nut-Oils.md#walnut-oil-and-learned-fear-extinction) · [Whole-walnut tension data](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Walnut-Bread-Tension-Comparisons.md) · [Later outcomes](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Whole-Walnut-Mood-Crossover.md)
- **0371 and 0408 saliva and drooling.** The original cinnamaldehyde mouth rinse did not increase saliva beyond its vehicle. Chewing cinnamon-flavored gum produced more saliva alongside the other gums. The mint and eugenol combination registration plans collected saliva flow in Sjögren syndrome and has no posted results. Collected secretion and clinical drooling severity are distinct. [Cinnamaldehyde trial and preprint family](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cinnamaldehyde-Human-Saliva.md) · [Chewing trial](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Watermelon-Flavored-Gum.md#cinnamon-flavor-and-chewing-context) · [Official registration](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Spearmint-Eugenol-Xerostomia-Protocol.md)
- **0437 shrimp and speech.** Danish outbreak investigators detected botulinum toxin in roe served at a shared dinner that contained shrimp. Patients described unclear speech or had clinician-described dysarthria. Attribution was to contaminated roe, and the report gives no separate boiled-shrimp contrast. [Clinical and food investigation](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Danish-Seafood-Speech-Outbreak.md)
- **0326 olive oil and swallowing.** A cohort of 63 stenting patients improved in measured dysphagia grades after the procedure and bundled postoperative care, which included unspecified olive oil and cola. The earlier technique report recommends the oil as a lubricant. Procedure and care effects are inseparable, and patient overlap across the papers remains unresolved. The older 2009 series describes postoperative feeding. [2023 cohort](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Oesophageal-Stenting-Olive-Oil-Cohort.md) · [Earlier technique](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Oesophageal-Stenting-Olive-Oil-Technique.md)
