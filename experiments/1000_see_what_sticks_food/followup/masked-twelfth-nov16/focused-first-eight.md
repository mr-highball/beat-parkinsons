## 0360: primary open lead resolved in part, not an independent replication

EP2792364A1, published 2014-10-22, https://patents.google.com/patent/EP2792364A1/en ; official EPO original https://data.epo.org/publication-server/rest/v1.2/publication-dates/20141022/patents/EP2792364NWA1/document.pdf . Example 1: crushed bay leaves extracted with 95°C water, concentrated and spray dried. Experimental example 1: 25, 50 or 100 mg/kg oral extract; 30 minutes later 4 g/kg ethanol; rat open-field observation at 1, 2, 4 and 8 h, five minutes each. Authors report distance and duration significantly restored relative to ethanol control; figure 2 contains plotted results. It is total exploratory movement in intoxicated rats, not walking speed, Parkinson disease or culinary ground leaves. No claim-specific weak direction justified from it alone. Existing followup/open-trails.tsv already points to this patent and Korean 2014 journal G704-001403.2014.20.1.010. Their participant/data overlap is not resolved and they must not be counted as independent evidence. The single-extract experiment must be distinguished from the later multi-ingredient compositions in the same patent.

## 0422: apathy outcome without verified oat exposure

Tağraf et al. 2026, DOI 10.1177/1877718X261422067, published online 2026-02-10; https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/1877718X261422067 . Existing local extract research/2025-2029/Source-Text/2026-Tagraf-Ketoflex-Starkstein-Apathy-Extract.txt and existing 0160 assessment; do not add as independent new study. Forty PD patients, personalized plant-rich ketogenic/time-restricted intervention vs control; apathy measured with Starkstein scale. Time-by-group test favors program, but original text inconsistently describes between-group posttest p=.333 and elsewhere p<.001. No cooked oats assignment or actual intake established. Hence no valid oat-specific direction; measured apathy is not enough to attribute diet effect to oatmeal. A separate 2022 Parkinson observational analysis https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35512773/ found no other significant dietary-pattern/apathy associations; it does not isolate oatmeal. Existing prior oatmeal-MVP total symptom finding is not apathy-specific.

## Remaining six pairs: contextual dispositions

* 0358: 2004 Aguiar and Saliba, PMID15311322, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15311322/ , already assessed: 80 healthy male recruits, 40 per dentifrice, 28 days. Experimental almond oil toothpaste and control yielded no significant salivary-flow difference. Preparation is expectorated dentifrice, salivary flow is not Parkinson drooling severity, and cold pressing is unreported. Do not infer protection or worsening from a null test.
* 0365: original Nigella seed randomized mild COVID study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9517329/ tests whole/powder doses against charcoal, but no intelligibility measurement; existing CTRI201705008698 is mixed external fumigation and pediatric communication, not oral seed/speech intelligibility.
* 0369: prior 2019 MPTP mouse ethanolic extract and 2026 essential-oil rotarod studies are already assessed; no fresh culinary leaf/freezing-of-gait frequency measured. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8551917/ uses rat fear-conditioning freezing, not locomotor freezing in PD; distinguish them.
* 0395: capsaicin sensory/lab tests and earlier tongue-force or wet-voice measures are not smoked whole paprika or listener intelligibility. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10842799/ tests oral capsaicin burn not speech.
* 0452: prior Phillips diet protocol uses canola as one component and broad urinary item between diet patterns; urinary frequency or urgency specifically and isolated cold-pressed canola effects are not established. No new source from targeted urinary searches.
* 0482: https://www.openaccessjournals.com/pdfdownload.php?aid=13050&download=articles%2Fcase-report-of-speech-language-therapy-work-for-voice-adequacy-of-a-male-transgender.pdf uses a sentence mentioning blackberry juice as a speech sample, not consumption intervention. No inference to frozen fruit or voice volume. Existing mixed berry/diet and boysenberry studies remain contextual only.
