# Cherry, cranberry and watermelon follow-up

This review covers 12 frozen claims across dried cherry, cooked or dried cranberry and raw watermelon. The evidence has no qualifying directional clinical comparison of the specified preparation and Parkinson outcome. The symptom route followed outcomes in either direction and checked primary tables, alternate preparations, related disease models and adverse ingestion reports.

All 28 distinct indexed records were screened by title and candidate records by abstract or full text. The broad query includes cherry-red signs, cherry laurel, pitanga or Surinam cherry (Eugenia uniflora), chokecherry and plant diseases. These exposures differ from culinary Prunus avium or Prunus cerasus. Cranberry and watermelon abstracts can mention an ingredient as a source of a compound or in a citation. The result tables preserve each source ID. Separate symptom-specific, regional and author searches used public web results without an enumerable total.
