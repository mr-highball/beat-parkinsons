The MITOS crossover pooled tomato paste eaten as served and paste further cooked with meals. Its main executive function estimate and the first-period, post-inclusion and lycopene-response sensitivity estimates all had intervals spanning zero. The clinical evidence includes a randomized whole-diet comparison in diagnosed Parkinson disease, and a separate tomato-containing fruit and vegetable supplement study used within-period TESEN comparisons in healthy adults.
