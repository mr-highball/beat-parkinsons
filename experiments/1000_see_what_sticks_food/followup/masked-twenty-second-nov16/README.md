- **0850, raw cucumber and postural instability.** All 101 inherited source pairs received a body-level review. Their independence and any causal Parkinson pain effect remain unresolved. - **0658, dried shiso and fatigue.** The Kim ocular trial inconsistently reports sample size. Its binocular near-point distance moved from 7.96 to 7.27 cm with aqueous Perilla extract and 9.00 to 9.67 cm with placebo. The study measures eye accommodation after screen work. The unrelated bog-bilberry registry does not identify this study. One gastrectomy formula registry reports adding perilla and 10 g/day. The linked journal protocol lists neither perilla in Table 1 nor the same dose. It specifies 30 g/day. The terminated trial supplies no posted fatigue estimates. The other candidates use seed peptides, isolated flavonoids, or multi-herb formulas. A pooled omega-3 cohort found faster global decline and gives no flax-specific estimate. A new registered trial lists an executive composite and has no results. For avocado, the exact linked avocado-and-mango article and supplement omit registered cognition and ocular outcomes. Its planned executive outcomes remain open.
