# Herb review of archived articles outside the claim-query results

On November 12, 2026, the archive inventory contained 388 herb claim–article pairs outside the earlier query join. The union of the first, protocol, oil and preclinical ledgers covered 46 of these exact keys. The remaining 342 pairs span 172 PMC articles and 43 frozen claims. All 342 have an article-level assessment and a local source reference. A Free Pascal check expanded each row's claim IDs, verified unique coverage against the inventory after exclusions, and checked all referenced local text lines. [Outside-query inventory](archive-body-outside-query.tsv) · [Herb dispositions](archive-body-herb-dispositions.tsv)

The review grouped the eligible pairs by PMCID, read the ingredient or constituent passages and their study context, and followed plausible preparations and outcomes to primary reports. Publisher names accounted for 124 pairs across 66 articles. Journal names accounted for 13 pairs across six articles. Author names or surnames accounted for 44 pairs across 17 articles. Constituent citations accounted for 62 pairs across 31 articles. Seven pairs across two articles traced to already cataloged human studies of pharmaceutical butylphthalide. The reviewed set yielded no new human outcome comparison with a frozen culinary preparation and symptom. Coverage is specific to these archived pairs and their citation trails. ## Access and interpretation limits

- The publicly accessible Nematolahi report gives favorable depression direction without arm-level values. The primary article route did not provide the outcome table during this review. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29389474/) · [Publisher article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ctcp.2017.11.004)
- The original 42-participant Araki report is indexed by JST and its author abstract is available through EBSCO. The original outcome table remains unavailable on those routes. The November 2020 open paper covers a subset of the same trial. [J-GLOBAL record](https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/en/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=202002236563683261) · [Original trial abstract](https://openurl.ebsco.com/contentitem/gcd:144863681?id=ebsco:gcd:144863681&sid=ebsco:plink:crawler) · [Open post hoc report](https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/12/11/3551)
- Imani and Modaresi's journal full text requires a Magiran subscription. The abstract and Movement Disorder Society abstract provide the model, preparation, comparator and direction for the reported tasks. Their matching details warrant treating the two reports as one experiment. [Journal abstract and access information](https://www.magiran.com/paper/2516690/comparative-effect-of-rosemary-hydroalcoholic-extract-and-selegiline-on-parkinson-s-behavioral-symptoms?lang=en) · [Conference abstract](https://www.mdsabstracts.org/abstract/comparing-the-effects-of-rosemary-and-selegiline-on-behavioral-symptoms-of-parkinsons-animal-model-in-mice/)
- The primary study cited in PMC12786970 for a butylphthalide mechanism concerns tubastatin A. The review itself flags the mismatch. The underlying citation supplies no celery-leaf result. [Archived review and table note](../../../research/2025-2029/Source-Text/PMC12786970.txt)
