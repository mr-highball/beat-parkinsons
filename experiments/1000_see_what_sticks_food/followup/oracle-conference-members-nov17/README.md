The current depths, original member identifiers and remaining routes are separately recorded. Parkinson-focused ranks 26, 36, 37, 40 and 41 still need deeper review.
[Earlier focused screens](../oracle-outcome-boundaries-nov17/README.md) · [Exact deeper review](coconut-member-depth.tsv)

One author editorial discusses fatigue in a lagundi trial and separately cites a 77-person hospitalized COVID-19 trial of virgin coconut oil. The oil trial reported similar mean symptom-resolution times and more adverse events in the oil arm. It provides no separate fatigue result. The allergic-rhinitis trial compared oral virgin coconut oil plus loratadine with loratadine alone and chiefly measured nose symptoms and gastrointestinal side effects. The 2012 thesis, 2013 abstract and 2016 full paper are one study. These clinical trials are distinct from each other and from the previously assessed 76-person COVID-19 trial. ## Saffron and executive function, 0469

The registered SaiLuoTong trial reported favorable executive-switching comparisons with one-tailed tests, with both published intervals spanning zero. The study compared a three-extract formula with placebo in a mild cognitive impairment population. The conference abstract is the same study as the peer-reviewed 2023 paper. The original trial also supplies an inconclusive secondary depression result, added as indirect context for 0063. Claim 0469 stays **Unknown, 50** and 0063 stays **Maybe False, 58**.
