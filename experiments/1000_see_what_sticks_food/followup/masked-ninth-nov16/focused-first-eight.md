## 0275 fresh celery leaf / worsens bradykinesia

Zhou et al. 2019 original PMID30988766, DOI 10.3892/etm.2019.7397, https://www.spandidos-publications.com/10.3892/etm.2019.7397, ChiCTR1800018892, reports a more specific outcome than the existing assessment implies. Off medication for 12 hours, the UPDRS III non-tremor sum, named bradykinesia plus rigidity, includes items 18–19 and 22–31: speech, facial expression, rigidity, limb and body movement, posture and gait. At week 24 changes mean ± SD were −7.14 ± 5.81 (NBP n43) versus +0.50 ± 9.48 (control n60), p<0.001 (Table II). Participants were randomized with blinded evaluators but knew treatment; 131 randomized and 103 followed to end. Synthetic dl-3-n-butylphthalide 200 mg three times daily for 24 weeks, concomitant medications, no placebo, not fresh celery, no isolated bradykinesia estimate; table has apparently repeated values in other scales requiring caution. This cannot alone assign dietary direction; correct the existing characterization to combined item-derived motor subscore. Existing Hegelmaier 2020 PMID32041265 includes celery in mixed diet and total UPDRS III only, no isolated fresh-leaf effects. Unknown remains reasonable.

## 0267 refined sunflower oil / worsens dysphagia

Ludwar 2020 online/2022 issue PMID33314461 original https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/odi.13752, crossover 26 enrolled, 24 analyzed. Questionnaire easier swallowing 8/24 following sunflower oil vs 2/24 after water, p=.031; multiple individual questions, not blinded to sensory products. Intervention was cold-pressed oil swished then spat out for one week, in medication-induced xerostomia; it was not swallowed refined oil or a PD swallowing scale. Stimulated and unstimulated salivation changes favored water (both p=.010). An extremely tentative Maybe False under a maximally permissive 51% rubric can be argued from the same-ingredient mucosal contact and subjective swallowing improvement, but product, exposure duration, route and population bridges are substantial; Unknown is also defensible, and this result does not establish ingestion prevents/worsens PD dysphagia. Existing aspiration case reports are uncontrolled and not oil-grade-specific. New PMID40597497 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40597497/ tests sunflower oil polysaccharide surimi gels by mechanical IDDSI texture, not clinical swallowing; no direction.

## 0298 ground nutmeg / worsens apathy

Another original PMID25050233 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25050233/ is rat antidepressant-like testing, not PD apathy; no calibrated culinary human comparison found. Unknown.

## 0310 cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil / reduces dyskinesia

Original Hegelmaier 2020 https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4409/9/2/376 lists pumpkin seed oil as one of several permitted oils, not assigned use, and outcome reports overall UPDRS III rather than levodopa-induced dyskinesia. Existing haloperidol rodent extract studies assess broad motor performance, not isolated cold-pressed oil or dyskinesia severity. No defensible direction from these sources.
