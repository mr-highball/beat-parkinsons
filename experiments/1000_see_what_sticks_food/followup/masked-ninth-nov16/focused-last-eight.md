# Ninth masked group, final eight claims

## 0433 steamed asparagus and swallowing

The published Parkinson family focus groups report avoidance of stringy asparagus. The report contains no verified steamed preparation or change in swallowing severity. A distinct occupational allergy case reports dyspnea and dysphagia following asparagus consumption, with an unspecified preparation. This is acute allergic dysphagia. A PEG-J knot case instead concerns obstruction in the medication delivery tube. [Indexed allergy abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15146878/) · [Local source note](../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Asparagus-Swallowing-Allergy.md)

## 0334 raw watermelon and speech

The indexed ingestion series reports oral symptoms in six of 29 people selected for watermelon sensitivity. Swallowing, breathing and speech changes during a food-allergic event have different meanings. No listener-based speech intelligibility measure or Parkinson population was verified. [Earlier primary study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3192863/) · [Local source note](../../../../research/1985-1989/Food-Experiment-Cherry-Cranberry-Watermelon.md#oral-symptoms-after-watermelon-ingestion)

## 0286 fresh lemon balm and speech

The essential tremor study concerns sodium oxybate. Parkinson speech studies compare cueing, levodopa state and long-term neurostimulation. Several original matches are people called Melissa. The existing rosmarinic-acid-rich botanical trial measures cognition in Alzheimer disease, with no speech-specific outcome. ## 0291 cooked curry leaf and micrographia

The mixed Parkinson diet lists curry leaves without preparation, individual consumption or writing item estimates. ## 0335 refined sunflower oil and urinary urgency

The cross-sectional PD food-frequency poster reports the total symptom score with an imprecise slope. No urinary urgency frequency item or refining grade is available. The TAPIR meal study excludes swallowing disorders and measures intestinal lipids rather than bladder symptoms. [Baseline PD poster](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Sunflower-Oils.md#sunflower-oil-frequency-and-overall-parkinson-symptoms) · [Original TAPIR study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11908603/)

## 0293 refined peanut oil and olfaction

APOLLO tested an intranasal comparator in postviral loss. Peanut oil participants improved by 3.88 TDI points, with no untreated comparison and an inconclusive adjusted between-group result. Three indexed entries are protocol versions or results of this one registered trial. The registry language names peanut oil, without a verified refining process or a dietary exposure.
