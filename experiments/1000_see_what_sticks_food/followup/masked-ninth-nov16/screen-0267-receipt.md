Ludwar 2020 primary https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdfdirect/10.1111/odi.13752, first publisher posting 12 December 2020 (Wiley issue), PubMed online date 19 December: 26 randomized, 24 completed; dry mouth burden 0–10 VAS primary. Cold-pressed oil from roasted kernels swished 5–8 minutes twice daily then spat; seven days per phase, seven-day washout. Examiners blinded, participants necessarily knew their products, two withdrew in water-first phase. Easier swallowing is one yes/no subjective after-use item, 8/24 oil vs 2/24 water, McNemar p=.031; not disease-specific dysphagia severity and many questions tested without reported multiplicity adjustment. Overall dry mouth burden change between products p=.067, not significant; the following objective salivation changes favored water: stimulated −0.09 ±0.29 vs +0.09 ±0.16 mL/min, unstimulated −0.07 ±0.19 vs +0.04 ±0.16, each p=.010. No home-use diary; crossover period and subjective carryover possible despite washout.
