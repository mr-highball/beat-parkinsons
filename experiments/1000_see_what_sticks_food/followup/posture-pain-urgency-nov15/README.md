# Posture, pain, urinary urgency and motor outcomes, November 15, 2026

## Three original-source follow-ups

- **0458, steamed spinach and postural instability.** The SPISAR registration and newly recovered university thesis specify baseline and follow-up balance after spinach extract plus exercise. The paper reports strength outcomes, and the thesis body gave no located balance estimate. The thesis describes maltodextrin as the placebo while the registry lists sucrose. These records describe the same cohort, and the balance question stays open. [Dated thesis and source pages](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Posture-Pain-and-Urgency.md#spinach-extract-thesis-and-planned-balance-assessment)
- **0167, frozen blackberry and pain.** The closest controlled study compared freeze-dried blackberry purée in milk with two milk drinks after strenuous exercise. Immediate soreness averaged 2.3, 4.0 and 3.9. Soreness was unmeasured before exercise, there were calorie and carbohydrate differences, and separate later comparisons were inconclusive. Another juice trial reported lower exercise soreness, and its Methods list maqui berry and other fruits without blackberry. The exercise result is too dependent on the meal and setting to infer a direction for frozen blackberries and Parkinson pain. [RubusElite thesis findings and limitations](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Blackberry-Pain.md#rubuselite-thesis-muscle-soreness-results) · [Mixed juice formulation](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Posture-Pain-and-Urgency.md#mixed-fruit-juice-and-exercise-soreness-ingredient-correction)
- **0593, ground sage and urinary urgency.** In the common-sage extract trial the menopause scale includes bladder problems without separate bladder or urgency results. Two other trials used *Salvia miltiorrhiza* root. The 2024 trial shows a favorable six-week urgency contrast at one dose and inconclusive urgency results at 12 weeks. The 2026 trial gives an inconclusive individual urgency comparison at 12 weeks (p = .257) despite a favorable storage composite. Official Korean records confirm the 2021 registration and no posted results for the former, and the April 9, 2026 retroactive registration and posted results for the latter. Its registry summary describes a composite and frequency and gives no individual urgency value. ## Remaining source outcomes

| Claim | Closest observation and current limit |

| 0131, refined soybean oil and rest tremor | The capsule trials measured essential postural or voice tremor, or compared two octanol formulations. The drug comparison furnishes no oil-only effect on Parkinson rest tremor. |
| 0242, canned lentils and walking slowness | Mixed dietary programs do not isolate lentils. The injected red lentil extract study measured persistence of imposed posture in rats. |
| 0271, ground ginger and dyskinesia | NCT05001217 remained completed with no posted result. Oral isolated zingerone did not improve reported post-levodopa behaviors in the available mouse report, without use of a graded dyskinesia severity score. |
| 0324, refined corn oil and shortened stride | The older claudication trial reports inconclusive walking distance. The COPD trial uses soy and corn oil with maltodextrin, and the functional measures do not distinguish a stride-length estimate. |
| 0332, fresh parsley and fatigue | The randomized feeding comparison verified fresh and heated parsley intake but measured biomarkers. The separately withdrawn parsley registry planned blood-pressure and urine measures. |
| 0359, grated turmeric and rigidity | The clinical studies use formulated curcumin and report composite motor outcomes. Rat induced catalepsy and a mouse behavior abstract do not provide clinical passive rigidity after grated turmeric. |
| 0370, ground sage and rigidity | The archived matches largely use SAGE as a publisher or refer to other botanicals. Extract and rosmarinic-acid work gives related movement context without a measured ground-sage effect. |
| 0493, frozen raspberry and anxiety | Dried-juice powder is described in a nicotine-withdrawal mouse patent and in two uncontrolled reports during smoking cessation. A human raspberry meal study gave no separate anxiety estimate. |
| 0611, fresh thyme and dyskinesia | The clinical case reports resolution of peak-dose dyskinesia after a luteolin and palmitoylethanolamide combination. Its reports give no culinary thyme exposure or separate component effect. Injected carvacrol in rats supplies a distinct movement proxy. |
