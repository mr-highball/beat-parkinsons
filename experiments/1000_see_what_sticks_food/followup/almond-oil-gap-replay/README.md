## Source distinctions

- The native Parkinson title-and-abstract search returned the same 2026 estradiol animal oral-vehicle report already cataloged. The deposited UEL thesis and journal paper describe one study family. All arms received almond oil. The 2019 naringenin and 2023 silymarin Parkinson models expose an additional index pitfall. The former lists almond oil in preliminary lab screening, mentions Capryol 90 and vitamin E in the formulation Methods and Capmul MCM in Figure 2. The latter selects Labrafil M 1944 CS. Neither incorporated almond oil based on the recovered dosed-formulation descriptions.
- The 1999 Beirut Canon edition is a dated witness of a much older historical text. Oral intake of almond-oil-soaked raisins is explicit in the primary Arabic. The translation phrase mashed raisins is not necessary to the original recommendation and was not imported. The solid preparation and processing grade were not documented. The independently edited Ibn al-Tilmīdh formulations distinguish sweet oil recipes for other thoracic or throat indications from the explicit hoarseness recipe, which does not list oil. The original print facsimile was located. Its target page could not be visually rendered through the web reader. The wording was verified in the print-paginated Arabic transcription.
- A single 2021 laboratory study deliberately used the Comuna cultivar and found distinct oil characteristics for room-temperature hydraulic pressing and screw pressing with heat. The 2002 human crossover verifies actual oral almond oil consumption but does not provide chain-of-custody proof of cold pressing or refining. Pharmaceutical, fixed culinary, bitter essential, heated press, cold expression and topical uses remain different descriptions. A genus or commercial INCI name does not establish the process.
- The 2026 Khan trial uses different registered arms and duration from the earlier NCT05011461 cosmetic retinol protocol. Related appearance datasets are not equivalent to UPDRS facial expression item results. The registered 2024 record and the 2026 journal report were filed in their first public year buckets.
