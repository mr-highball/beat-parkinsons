The June 2015 author abstract compares full and half servings of rice in 39 older nursing-home residents. The half serving produced less frequent postprandial hypotension. Rice grade, posture and arm estimates remain to be verified from the complete body. Earlier Korean work measured pressure after rice-containing meals, including a randomized water comparison. These reports supply related meal context and unresolved participant overlap. Vloet's liquid-carbohydrate crossover documented smaller seated blood-pressure changes and a shorter pressure-drop duration with the lowest carbohydrate load. The oats breakfast cohort recorded equal counts of dizziness or nausea across post-meal pressure groups. A separate crossover in older adults with hypertension recorded lower absolute supine and upright pressure after a meal, with the authors reporting preserved orthostatic control. The studies use different exposures and distinguish symptoms from pressure on standing.
