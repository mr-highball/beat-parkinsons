## Original comparisons

- **0821, grated turmeric and daytime sleepiness.** The original Longvida workbook shows 12-week daytime dysfunction worsening in 1/38 curcumin recipients versus 9/38 on placebo and improvement in 1/38 versus 2/38. The whole category comparison gave p=.018 at 12 weeks and p=.566 at four weeks. The component combines sleepiness and difficulty maintaining enthusiasm. No separate item result was available. The other curcumin trial's Epworth comparison gave p=.83. The small Parkinson nanomicelle trial reports global PSQI with no separate daytime outcome. The extract trials and pain protocol leave the grated turmeric claim unresolved. [Cox workbook and source facts](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Turmeric-Followup.md#lipidated-curcumin-and-daytime-dysfunction-or-divided-attention) · [Pain formula registry](registry-close-leads.md) · [Parkinson curcumin outcomes](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Iran-Registry-Followup.md#curcumin-nanomicelles-and-sleep-or-fatigue)
- **0816 and 0748, swallowing.** Nigella oil mouth rinse during chemotherapy improved normal-food tolerance at earlier observations. Table 2 reports 26/27 versus 20/25 on day 28 and p=.159 for the diet categories. The original table qualifies the abstract's broader account. Separately, a mortadella challenge caused allergic dysphagia in a person with cardamom sensitization. An isolated ground-cardamom challenge in another person produced hives and gastrointestinal symptoms without a reported swallowing effect. The routes, symptoms and allergy or mucositis settings leave Parkinson directions unresolved. [Nigella Table 2](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Nigella-Anise.md#nigella-mouth-rinse-and-normal-food-counts) · [Cardamom allergy and dysphagia](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md#2023-cardamom-in-allergic-reactions-with-dysphagia-and-presyncope) · [Ground cardamom case](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md#ground-cardamom-challenge-in-an-allergy-case)
- **0939, refined olive oil and apathy.** Apathy decreased in one uncontrolled Alzheimer cohort during sublingual cannabinoid treatment carried in olive oil of unspecified grade. The 2022 preprint and 2023 article describe the same 30 patients. Di Santo's adjusted diet nonadherence model gave OR 2.76 (95% CI 0.83–9.21), p=.099 for apathy, despite the prose describing an association. Parkinson surveys report favorable motivation for overall diet scores in one cohort and inconclusive individual apathy in another. The refined-oil comparison, primary KIM study and supplements leave the individual oil-to-apathy effect unresolved. [Cannabis carrier and diet analyses](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Marjoram-Olive-Outcomes.md#apathy-ratings-in-a-cannabis-preparation-carried-in-olive-oil) · [Parkinson dietary patterns](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Oil-Apathy-Clinical.md) · [Original collection and publisher access](olive-oil-access-followthrough.md)
- **0931 and 0940, watermelon and buckwheat.** A crossover in ten young adults reports a resting systolic pressure difference at two hours after commercial juice (−13.1 mm Hg, p=.02), with p=.91 for the whole time interaction. Standing lightheadedness was unmeasured. The Tartarian buckwheat case series describes dyskinesia in patients with toxic peripheral neuropathy. The accessible author abstract does not define the movement or preparation and the separately retrieved patent concerns the gallbladder. [Watermelon comparison](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cherry-Cranberry-Watermelon.md#commercial-watermelon-juice-and-resting-pressure-in-a-pilot-crossover) · [Buckwheat case-series access](produce-source-trails.md)
- **0903 and 0695, green peas and marjoram.** Three trial protocols specify gait outcomes after mixed pea protein or a pea beverage and have no posted results. Marjoram massage and inhalation studies specify topical blends, general sleep or bruxism measurements. The oral Parkinson work gives aggregate outcomes without a dream-enactment result.
