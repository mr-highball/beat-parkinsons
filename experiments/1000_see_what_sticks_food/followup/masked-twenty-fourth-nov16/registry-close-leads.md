# Five official trial checks — 2026-11-16

All five current protocol JSON files were retrieved directly from https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/{NCT_ID}, HTTP 200, saved by NCT identifier. All show hasResults=false; this means no registry results posted, not evidence of no outcome. Web searches of each exact NCT identifier plus results/publication and intervention terms returned registry mirrors but did not identify an identifier-matched primary results article. Search is focused, not an exhaustive publication census.

- NCT06499012, first posted 2024-07-12, completed, last updated 2025-11-24: randomized 12-week prototype powder vs placebo in 50–70-year-olds with sarcopenia and knee/hip OA. Active contains soy isolate and peptides, wheat protein, pea protein and pea peptide plus aloe and excipients; control has aloe and excipients. SPPB composite primary includes gait speed, chair stand and balance; no separate posted gait result, no whole cooked pea and no Parkinson-specific population. Actual enrollment and protocol details are in original JSON.
- NCT05869305, first posted 2023-05-22, status unknown, last updated 2023-05-24: assigned 12-week sachets of whey isolate, soy/pea isolate/brown-rice protein blend, or nonprotein placebo in sarcopenia. Six-meter gait speed is primary physical performance measure; no posted data or proof of whole cooked peas. Mixed plant arm does not identify pea-specific effect.
- NCT06946316, first posted 2025-04-27, recruiting, last updated 2026-02-23: 150 planned postmenopausal women and men aged at least 50, randomized to 1% chocolate milk, pea beverage or almond beverage with resistance training three times weekly for 12 months; 375 mL immediately and 375 mL one hour after training. Secondary walking speed over 10 m and timed up-and-go planned, not posted. Pea beverage does not establish whole cooked peas or Parkinson treatment.
- NCT07665398, first posted 2026-06-24, active not recruiting, last updated 2026-07-02: proprietary essential-oil blend including Origanum majorana, lavender and vetiver, inhalation twice daily plus extraoral massage daily versus sesame-oil carrier with same routes in bruxism. Ultrasound masseter thickness and elasticity primary; jaw pain, headache, tooth sensitivity, fatigue and perceived stress/anxiety VAS secondary. Bruxism can occur awake or asleep; no dream enactment/REM behavior item in specified outcomes, no fresh culinary marjoram, and no posted results. Cited articles in registry are background, not publications of this trial.
- NCT06247813, first posted 2024-02-08, current overall status unknown, last updated 2024-09-19: chronic pain patients assigned one of three formulas according to urine biomarkers. Inflammation formula contains turmeric extract 400 mg, black-pepper preparation 10 mg, ashwagandha 500 mg and olive leaf extract 250 mg; not fresh grated turmeric or isolated turmeric effect. Secondary PSQI is registered including its daytime dysfunction component and subscales, as distinct from direct ESS sleepiness measurement; no arm-level results posted. Single-group, biomarker-selected, multi-component formulation and no identified Parkinson cohort.

No exact prepared-food plus target-symptom estimate is available from these registrations. Study absence in a focused search does not establish absence of an effect or an unpublished report.
