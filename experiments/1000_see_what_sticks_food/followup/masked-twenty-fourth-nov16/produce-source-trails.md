## 0931 watermelon and orthostatic lightheadedness

Hussein, Hookings and Mills, 2023, Proceedings of the Nutrition Society 82(OCE1), E31, online 8 March 2023, DOI 10.1017/S0029665123000393. Original conference abstract: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-nutrition-society/article/impact-of-watermelon-juice-on-blood-pressure-in-young-healthy-adults-a-pilot-randomised-controlled-crossover-trial/48A18A288E2DC14D06B82B32A7E2B7EE . Ten healthy adults, 18–30, single-blind randomized crossover, 500 mL commercial watermelon juice (~1 g citrulline) versus low-nitrate water; 15-minute intervals for two hours. Systolic treatment effect p<.001, treatment × time interaction p=.91; change at 2 h -5.3 ± 4.06 mmHg, difference versus control -13.1 mmHg (95% CI -24.09 to -2.11), p=.02, without multiplicity correction. This is a small abstract and neither orthostatic pressure nor symptoms nor raw whole fruit were tested. Do not treat a sitting/unspecified BP response as orthostatic lightheadedness or infer a Parkinson effect. Related-only candidate; exact DOI absent from local research search at review time.

Counterpoint: PMID34344546, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34344546/, randomized crossover 21 healthy postmenopausal women, two 360 mL servings of 100% juice daily for four weeks versus isocaloric placebo, no change in ambulatory blood pressure or vascular measures; NCT03626168 family already indexed locally. It does not measure the target symptom. Existing NCT05892328 also reports no ambulatory BP difference with whole-fruit beverages over four weeks. Neither null vascular result establishes absence of postural symptoms.

## 0942 pomegranate and apathy

ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05142085 already catalogued: nano-pomegranate seed oil in Parkinson, nonmotor scale includes loss of interest; registered outcome is not a posted apathy result, and oil differs from raw arils. NCT07766564 uses a pomegranate-acai mixture in healthy participants and cannot isolate arils. Search for pomegranate juice apathy/motivation, Parkinson and seed oil returned cognitive trials instead: PMID31711104, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31711104/, 12-month juice versus matched placebo tests visual/verbal memory, not apathy; PMID23970941 is an earlier separate memory trial. No distinct direct apathy comparison established by these routes. Existing 2014 animal peel-extract sucrose preference is not a validated PD apathy outcome.
