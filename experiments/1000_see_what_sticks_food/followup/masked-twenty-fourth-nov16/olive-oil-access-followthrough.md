## Persistent access limits and cohort context

PMID 20532731 is identity-verified as Sellitto et al., “A subtle case of iron-deficiency anemia,” Internal and Emergency Medicine 6(2):191–192 (2011), DOI 10.1007/s11739-010-0412-7; PubMed classifies it as a letter/case report. NLM metadata concerns iron-deficiency anemia and jejunal neoplasms, not the candidate exposure or endpoint. Springer has a subscription preview; ResearchGate explicitly says no full text is available there. Since the exact body remains unavailable, retain pending/access_only rather than infer a body-level exclusion from metadata.

Other independent human context (not a P24 exact-pair override): AU-ROOAD/NCT03824197, PMID 36501136 / PMCID PMC9736478, randomized MCI participants to 30 mL/day refined extra-light olive oil (n=12) or EVOO (n=13) for six months; 25 completed. The trial assesses BBB/imaging, biomarkers, and cognition, not apathy or PD; refined-oil versus EVOO differences were not significant. The P24 exact-source PMC7591702 study (PMID 33173523) links apathy to broad MEDAS nonadherence, not isolated oil: Table 4 OR 3.02 (0.86–10.63), p=.085 unadjusted and 2.76 (0.83–9.21), p=.099 adjusted, despite prose calling the association significant.

The baseline had 21 pending/access_only rows. Four new full-body resolutions above resolve three as related and one as excluded, reducing pending/access_only to 17 if these overrides are integrated.
