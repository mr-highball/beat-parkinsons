# P24 preliminary source findings, 2026-11-16

Targeted web searches (ranked discovery, not all-page provider census): "Levisticum officinale sleep REM dreams trial lovage"; "Artemisia dracunculus apathy depression randomized human clinical tarragon"; "Origanum vulgare salivary flow human oregano oral trial"; "Melissa officinalis trial urinary urgency bladder nocturia human"; "Pimenta dioica allspice depression clinical trial human mood"; "Pimenta dioica attention cognitive healthy volunteers allspice"; "Origanum majorana sleep REM behavior Parkinson trial polysomnography"; "Melissa officinalis bladder overactive urgency trial"; "allspice pumpkin pie spice randomized cognitive attention study"; "Pimenta dioica depression behavior animal forced swim extract fruit"; "Artemisia dracunculus human depression apathy trial extract mood". These do not establish exhaustive coverage.

0754: Existing Parkinson menu PMID32041265 names lovage in a mixture, without dream enactment reporting or proof of an individual dried-leaf dose. EMA assessment https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/herbal-report/final-assessment-report-levisticum-officinale-koch-radix_en.pdf concerns root, not leaf or REM behavior. No direct new result identified.

0756: Existing 2018 PMID29861834 mouse social-defeat evidence is extract, not dried culinary leaves or clinical apathy. Human randomized tarragon study PMID27097076 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27097076/ studied impaired glucose tolerance and metabolic endpoints, not apathy. Apathy, depression and nonspecific exploratory activity must remain separate.

0695: Parkinson randomized marjoram tea primary abstract PMID34455218 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34455218/ 60 randomized, 51 completed, 30 days; NMSS and BDI changes reported, not separate dream enactment. Existing marjoram oil massage PMID28761497 addresses sleep rather than episodes. A Korean EEG aromatherapy primary abstract https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART001691795 exposes 29 healthy women to marjoram oil for three minutes, measures brain EEG, not observed sleep behaviors; nonoral oil.

0783: Existing 2025 original https://www.mdpi.com/2072-6643/17/15/2480 PMID40806065 documents 91 adults, 7 days of ~5 g oregano essential-oil jelly dissolving in mouth and salivary pH, not salivary flow or clinical drooling; no Parkinson cohort. An independent 2021 trial https://www.jrmds.in/articles/efficacy-of-oregano-essential-oil-mouthwash-in-reducing-oral-halitosis-a-randomized-doubleblind-clinical-trial-70248.html randomized oregano essential-oil mouthwash versus chlorhexidine and water for 7 days: oral malodor/BANA, not ingested fresh leaf or drooling. No directional result for drooling.

0713: Existing perilla rosmarinic acid patent tracks nocturnal voiding, not lemon balm or urgency. Targeted Melissa search recovered unrelated mood study PMID12062586 and secondary review without an individually verified urgency contrast; oral urinary urgency remains open.

0809/0720: Existing eugenol odor task and rodent eugenol depression-like results cannot be equated with ground Pimenta dioica berry or Parkinson symptoms. Primary exposure-only controlled trial https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2022.811433/full used mixed pumpkin-pie spice (cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, allspice; unknown ingredient proportions), not an isolated allspice arm, and measured postprandial metabolic/vascular endpoints, not depression or attention. 1996–97 Suárez PMID9404514 https://tropicalstudies.org/rbt/attachments/volumes/vol44-3A/06_Suarez_Pimenta_dioica.pdf reports CNS depression in rat extracts; pharmacologic CNS suppression is not clinical depressive symptom severity, and is not an attention result. The earlier animal constituent and human odor assessments are already catalogued.
