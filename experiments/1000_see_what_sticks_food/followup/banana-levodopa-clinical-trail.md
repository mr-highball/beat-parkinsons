# Banana and levodopa clinical source trail

November 12, 2026. The frozen raw banana outcomes are apathy severity (`0196`), anxiety severity (`0309`) and dyskinesia severity (`0999`). The sources below distinguish clinical medication response, food intake, drug compatibility and symptom estimates.

## Manufacturer table and archive search

A Tokyo University of Pharmacy and Life Sciences teaching directory preserves a Doparl interview form dated October 2008. Its printed page 11 gives one-hour levodopa remaining as 40.0% with banana and water and 47.0% with banana and milk. The water row has 56.2% at 30 minutes. Ogo et al. cite 56.2% at 30 minutes and 47.0% at one hour for the water mixture in the 2005 bulletin. The 2008 form identifies plain Doparl fine granules, and the Ogo discussion names an EC-Doparl formulation. The original bulletin is needed to determine whether the numbers refer to the same experiment. The table values were checked against the rendered columns and the Japanese text. The recovered 2008 PDF has SHA-256 `3739fa8951b6fe5fd7cd79bfdea91b955750bf22ce1f57ff29583657b212f93b`. The local note records the table values and the copy's uncertain first upload date. [2008 primary product form](https://www.ps.toyaku.ac.jp/~kosugi/zemi2010/iform/Levodopa.pdf#page=14) · [Dated catalog note](../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#doparl-granule-compatibility-tables-in-the-2008-product-form)
