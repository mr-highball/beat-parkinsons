# Final eight focused claims

- **0445 grilled salmon and visual hallucinations.** The prospective diet and questionnaire cohort pooled salmon-type fish with other fatty fish for its reported non-monotonic associations. Existing Parkinson food records give no independent grilled-salmon episode frequency. Several original matches refer to the surname Salmon or RNA software. [Original fish cohort](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Fatty-Fish-Psychotic-Like-Symptoms.md)
- **0374 steamed Brussels sprouts and dyskinesia.** Direct food analysis verifies heat-sensitive sulforaphane content. A mirrored thesis gives a late mouse dyskinesia result for injected sulforaphane, with numerical scores, study identity and methods pending. The wild-type drug group is distinct from the transgenic groups. An ex-vivo fiber study and nematode thrashing outcomes do not measure human dyskinesia. [Source and thesis access notes](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Steamed-Brussels-Sprout-Sulforaphane.md)
- **0317 dried celery leaf and drooling.** The chewing study measured parotid output and did not establish dried leaf. Saliva production and severity of saliva loss or inability to swallow differ. No matched clinical contrast was identified in the scoped sources. [Earlier primary report](../../../../research/1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Romaine-Celery-Stalk-Cabbage.md#1990-celery-mastication-and-parotid-saliva)
- **0313 fresh dill weed and swallowing.** A dried dill capsule comparison measured pooled esophageal complaints in diabetes. A cooked dill allergy case has acute throat swelling. The thyroid registry evaluates nodule size and blood measures and mentions aspiration as part of a biopsy. A clinical swallowing result after fresh dill remains open. [Dill and symptom measures](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Dill-Leaf-Outcomes.md#dried-leaf-capsules-and-esophageal-motility-symptoms) · [Official thyroid protocol](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04482907)
- **0342 ground mustard and depressive symptoms.** The source with a measured human outcome used combined herbal patches. The existing injected extract and animal oil studies are formulation and population analogues. No isolated oral ground-mustard comparison was verified. [Topical trial](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Mustard-Patch-and-Depression.md)
- **0346 ground sumac and hallucinations.** Existing exposures involve acute allergies after mixed meals. Neither identifies a visual hallucination effect. Culinary Rhus coriaria is distinct from poison sumac and no poisoning label was used to infer a direction. [Original case context](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Outcomes.md)
- **0397 toasted hazelnut oil and urinary urgency.** The timed urination study used contaminated oil and measured pooled outputs and excretion. The design does not identify an urgency episode or roasting grade. Distinct pumpkin-oil and mixed botanical bladder studies use a different ingredient. [Timed-collection study](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Hazelnut-Walnut-Gap-Followup.md#oral-hazelnut-oil-and-timed-urine-collection)
- **0314 cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil and facial expressiveness.** A clinical Parkinson menu lists pumpkin-seed oil alongside other permitted ingredients. Its motor total gives no facial-expression item effect, individual consumption or verified pressing grade. [Diet menu](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Pumpkin-Peanut-Oil.md#pumpkin-seed-oil-in-a-parkinson-diet-menu)
