## 0340 toasted cumin worsens attention impairment

Ito et al., first published 20 April 2026, PMID42088310, DOI10.3389/fnut.2026.1784027, original https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/nutrition/articles/10.3389/fnut.2026.1784027/full, registry UMIN000050328 https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_his_list.cgi?recptno=R000057216. Cumin essential oil distilled from Indian Cuminum cyminum seeds (not toasted whole spice) in capsule containing 25 mg cuminaldehyde daily vs matched medium-chain triglyceride placebo for 12 weeks, 43 randomized/38 analyzed, n19 each, age 65–86 healthy and cognitively intact, non-PD. On Cognitrax, complex attention 26.5±30.8 to 18.5±16.5 vs 16.5±12.7 to 22.2±30.9, lower better, group×time p=.062; simple attention 29.0±25.6 to 36.9±9.3 vs 37.0±5.7 to 30.9±24.8, higher better, interaction p=.057. Both observed changes favor cumin oil but are statistically inconclusive and have imbalanced baselines/wide SD. Other domains psychomotor speed p=.012 and reaction time p=.022; many domains and company-affiliated authors limit inference. Registry disclosure 31 May 2024 postdates 2023 trial. Under permissive weak rubric a 51% Maybe False is arguable: two congruent objective attention trends oppose worsening, but isolated concentrated essential oil and non-PD strongly limit transfer, so Unknown remains reasonable. No significant attention effect established.
