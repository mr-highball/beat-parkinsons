The 2023 ECRO saltiness abstract repeats the 148-person study already assessed for claim 0730. Participants rated the same eight solutions in randomized order during one session. The full study measured smell to classify participants at baseline and excluded neurodegenerative disease. At 0.04 g/mL, group mean saltiness ratings in participants with hyposmia were 4.46 for pure salt, 4.53 for the mixed rosemary product and 4.44 for the orange–saffron mix on a 0–6 scale. These are descriptive group means. The product's reported 30% refers to its lower sodium chloride concentration, with daily intake unmeasured. The rosemary starting material was one part of a mixed herb extract dried onto salt. The same-product 58-person study uses a different published sample and its participant overlap is unresolved.
