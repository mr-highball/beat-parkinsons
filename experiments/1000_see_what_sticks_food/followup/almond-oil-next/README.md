## Frozen claim leads and gaps

| Claim | Closest measured evidence and remaining question |
| --- | --- |
| 0029 visual hallucination frequency | The 2013 memory-care crossover poster gives a measured total Neuropsychiatric Inventory difference. Two cardiac trials give delirium composites. The sources supply no visual hallucination counts. The original item-level data and Parkinson oral-exposure case data remain leads. |
| 0093 low voice volume | The searches covered hypophonia, loudness, hoarseness, dysphonia and related speech outcomes. No oral oil comparison reported a Parkinson speech loudness outcome. The oral hygiene trial’s salivary-flow measure does not substitute for speech volume. |
| 0186 daily OFF time | The earlier rat oral vehicle and newer zebrafish movement experiment do not sample diaries or levodopa response. Actual ingredient-level exposure in oral levodopa and meals studies remains a priority. |
| 0358 drooling severity | A 2004 dentifrice did not change flow significantly. A 2025 supervised chewing comparison recorded smaller increases for whole almonds than wax gum. These are flow measurements during or after separate oral exposures. Drooling requires a severity or volume result, including swallowing and clearance. |
| 0545 reduced facial expressiveness | The search found Parkinson botanical and model records without an almond-specific facial movement score. Track ingredient exposure in interventions that report examination items rather than only motor totals. |
| 0569 dream enactment | The memory-care comparator measured actigraphic duration. A 2026 whole-almond trial measured REM duration and latency with no clear between-arm difference. The recovered prospective registration explicitly excluded parasomnias and major neurological disorders. Both exposures and outcomes differ from oil ingestion and a recorded dream enactment event. |
| 0715 attention impairment | Both cardiac trials give composite delirium results. The sweet almond-powder plus candy Alzheimer trial gives an attention-domain contrast (p=.776). These three studies have measured related outcomes and leave the frozen population and oil preparation unresolved. |
| 0742 falls frequency | A clothing patch trial compared aromatic oil carriers at a shared exercise program and reported sway and fear. The larval skin extract model measured distance and immobility. Neither measured falls after ingestion. The Thai registration remains a protocol lead. |
| 0989 dystonia severity | The Parkinson vehicle and soaking exposure in a larval mobility test give no human dystonia outcome. Follow frozen symptom and formulation terms together with common dystonic postures and painful cramps in primary records. |
| 0998 muscle rigidity | Two topical osteoarthritis comparisons reported WOMAC stiffness. The 2024 study had a nonsignificant between-group test, and the 2026 study focused its post hoc comparisons on tarragon. Joint stiffness and examination-rated Parkinson muscle rigidity require separate interpretations. |

The three oldest source branches cover a dentifrice, a memory-care sleep poster, and topical comparators in cardiac, musculoskeletal and balance trials. Later records expanded the outcomes to stimulated salivary flow after eating a whole almond, polysomnographic sleep stages, Alzheimer attention and larval movement. The record review distinguishes seed oil, whole nuts, seed powder, an almond-skin extract, commercial dentifrice, topical massage, and aroma delivery. No direction was established for an exact frozen exposure and outcome. ## Primary material to revisit

- Both historic provider links returned HTTP 404 for the 2013 Hanson poster. The original poster itself was recovered from coauthor Linda Sackett-Lundeen’s deposit. Its model estimates total weekly Neuropsychiatric Inventory 7.97 with almond control and 8.68 with lavender (difference 0.71, SE 0.23, p=.0022). The report gives only totals. The poster estimates 42.46 more minutes of sleep with lavender (SE 15.69, p=.0084) and 6.31 percentage points greater sleep percentage (SE 2.10, p=.004), using the same participants as the abstract. No deposit reuse license was confirmed. [Original poster on coauthor profile](https://www.academia.edu/95545646/P3_272_Lavender_essential_oil_improves_sleep_in_residents_of_a_memory_care_assisted_living_facility) · [Archived abstract summary](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Almond-Oil-Followup.md#almond-oil-comparison-in-a-memory-care-crossover)
- The Thai study lists TCTR20211114002. The registry site returned a public interface, and the original protocol details or outcome dataset were not recovered. The Indian sleep registration was recovered and dated. It lists sleep structure without dream enactment and excludes parasomnias and major neurological disorders. The published 2026 supplement has group aggregate tables without raw data, REM atonia or enactment. Supplementary Table 2 reports lower REM duration by the end of both whole-almond and control arms, with the main adjusted between-group comparison inconclusive. [Thai original](https://doi.org/10.4235/agmr.23.0008) · [Indian registry and results family](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Almond-Oil-Followup.md#whole-almond-sleep-trial-registration) · [Primary supplement](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13454318.1/Data_Sheet_1.PDF)
- A Parkinson tactile massage report and a Japanese Parkinson massage comparison were checked for an oil product. The Swedish paper describes two products and does not establish almond oil. The Japanese comparison identifies jojoba. Avoid inferring almond ingredients from a generic massage or oil label. [Swedish study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3878723/) · [Japanese study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41250562/)
- The recovered 2022 Kishore Parkinson-model full paper identifies an oral water extract of ground almond kernels, pumpkin kernels, nigella and peppers. Table 4 labels suspension duration as muscular rigidity and reports 21 ± 1.2 versus 6.7 ± 1.2 seconds at the highest mixture dose versus reserpine alone. The methods describe a high-bar catalepsy time to return the paws to the table, with unequal meaning from suspension endurance. Species names and reserpine dose descriptions also conflict across sections. The mixture has no isolated oil arm or dependable human rigidity interpretation. The earlier almond-carrier Chinese patent is a separate record. [Kishore primary paper, methods and Table 4](https://wjpr.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/article_issue/91288427098d8d893fe8fdb89b6cb5b7.pdf) · [Prior patent assessment](../almond-avocado-oils/README.md)
- A separate 2020 chlorpromazine-model experiment evaluated a methanolic preparation of almond, peanut and watermelon seeds. It found lower catalepsy and higher locomotion at higher mixture doses. The original author article grants CC BY 4.0. Follow first public dates, measured outcomes and related seed exposures before filing this source and the Kishore report in the period catalogue. [Nandagopal primary paper](https://www.journals.innovareacademics.in/index.php/ajpcr/article/download/36549/21843)
- The 2007 Parkinson diet study with low-protein foods from Aproten/Heinz found 3.5 vs 5 hours of diary OFF time (p=.01). The original gives the vendor but does not list almond oil. A dated manufacturer catalogue or diet worksheet would be needed to connect the study to almond oil. Its 2006 predecessor is already in the archive. [2007 primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2413077/) · [2006 source note](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#low-protein-replacement-foods-and-levodopa-off-time)
- Two case branches connect almond-containing oils to lipoid pneumonia. The 2015 CC BY hospital report identifies one child admitted after nasal oil use. The 2024 Vigo case identifies a reportedly consumed laxative blend containing olive, almond and mineral oil and histology with lipid-laden macrophages. The single blended exposure and the distinct respiratory endpoint leave the frozen drooling claim unresolved. Historical bitter almond and contaminant toxicity require a separate preparation identity. [2015 primary paper](https://www.ijsr.net/archive/v4i2/SUB151280.pdf) · [2024 publisher case report](https://www.minervamedica.it/en/journals/minerva-respiratory-medicine/article.php?cod=R16Y2024N02A0084)
- The 2019 Ghafarzadeh before–after study reported 343 of 442 participants meeting an Insomnia Severity Index threshold before two weeks of ten whole almonds daily, versus 306 afterward (p<.001 in the reported paired analysis). It supplies no concurrent comparison group or enactment measure. A cited rat EEG/EMG study first appeared in December 2015 and examined a water extract of almond seed. Its primary abstract reports reduced open-field locomotion and prolonged NREM, without an estimate of REM behavior. Check the original article for the exact route, REM values, and source rights. [Student study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6635337/) · [Rat study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26711831/)
- Two oral almond syrup randomized ADHD trials provide more specific attention-related leads. The 2019 trial used almond extract in syrup and reported parent-rated inattention scores of 9.4 with methylphenidate and 8.9 with almond syrup by week 8 (p=.71). Teacher scores were 10.3 and 9.6 (p=.61). The later study separately adds almond syrup, fig–almond syrup or placebo syrup to methylphenidate. Trace its domain means, registrations, and preparation standardization before any row. Both trials concern pediatric ADHD and use syrup. [2019 full paper](https://superfoodvegan.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/287028244.pdf) · [Later primary trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13097199/)
- The Aguiar 2004 university PDF and Mohajeri 2026 primary full text supplied outcome detail. Confirm applicable edition-specific redistribution terms before retaining source extracts. The 2026 acute cardiac trial’s figure shows total-scale values above the stated maximum at the last time point, so the assessment uses the prespecified reported composite comparison and omits graph estimates. [Aguiar primary record](https://doi.org/10.1590/S1806-83242004000200014) · [Mohajeri primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13092157/) · [Kharatha primary paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ctim.2026.103398)
