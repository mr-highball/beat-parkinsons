# Chinese Clinical Trial Registry follow-up

The November 12, 2026 review used indexed copies of official ChiCTR records in Chinese and English, exact registration and publication identifiers, and cited primary publications. Direct requests to the study search and a known English record returned HTTP 405 with an Aliyun browser-check page. The WHO exact-trial route timed out. One indexed official institution-filtered result page reported two Harbin ketogenic-diet records and showed both references. This count applies to that page. The broad national registry search and its pagination remain unreconciled. [Search and access ledger](chictr-queries.tsv) · [Official Harbin listing](https://www.chictr.org.cn/searchproj.html?country=%E4%B8%AD%E5%9B%BD+&createyear=&institution=%E5%93%88%E5%B0%94%E6%BB%A8%E5%8C%BB%E7%A7%91%E5%A4%A7%E5%AD%A6%E7%AC%AC%E5%9B%9B%E9%99%84%E5%B1%9E%E5%8C%BB%E9%99%A2)

Six previously uncataloged registrations concern two ketogenic-diet designs, two medicinal-formula trials, and two probiotic or prebiotic preparations. The Harbin registrations distinguish a whole-diet comparison within participants with Parkinson’s from a separate planned parallel usual-diet comparison. The former published symptom outcomes in 16 diet completers, with 11 withdrawals and healthy relatives serving as microbiota references. The 2016 Congrong Shujing registry matches an April 2020 controlled report. A 2025 report studied the same named formula in a separate cohort using a matched placebo. The named studies supply related preparation or symptom evidence without a single ordinary culinary ingredient contrast. The indexed candidate that named garlic, onion, banana, tomato, potato, and spinach enrolled patients with bowel dysfunction after stroke. Other exclusions included medical capsules with diet eligibility restrictions, stool-transplant studies, microbiota observations, and dietary advice within a broader caregiver intervention. The previously cataloged strain-defined fermented milk report gives ChiCTR1800016795, and its exact public registry record was not independently retrieved during this pass. The separately indexed ChiCTR2000034107 spectroscopy outcome lead remains open pending a primary publication link to the registry identifier.
