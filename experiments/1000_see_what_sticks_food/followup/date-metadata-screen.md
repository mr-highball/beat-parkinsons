# Dried dates and pain search recovery

The November 12, 2026 UTC recovery completed Europe PMC query `0502-2`, the date-and-pain search for [claim 0502](../statements/0502.txt). The provider reported 17,599 results. The first core run saved 9,400 rows across 94 pages before a WinHTTP timeout. The identifier replay reconciled 17,599 unique provider `source:id` keys across 176 pages after resuming from a transient HTTP 503. The metadata recovery matched all 9,400 original rows and reused 2,608 exact provider keys from other queries in the same saved core export. It retrieved the remaining 5,591 metadata records in 82 disjoint, source-filtered lookup batches. All 82 requests returned HTTP 200 with the requested keys. The merged metadata contains 13,319 abstracts and 4,280 records with title and bibliography alone. The native metadata screen checked all 17,599 titles and available abstracts. It matched food-specific names and preparation terms including *Phoenix dactylifera*, date palm, date fruit, dried or whole dates, Ajwa, Zahedi, Medjool, Deglet Nour, and named seed, pulp, syrup, extract, and sap forms. It also checked food-use phrases and non-English date-palm strings. The plain words `date` or `dates` occurred in 1,022 rows, including 277 of the 5,591 recovered rows. Food-specific terms matched zero titles or abstracts in the recovered query. A synthetic fixture containing dried dates, *Phoenix dactylifera*, pain, and Parkinson’s triggered all four intended flags.

The provider's all-field index was searched separately by intersecting the original query with `"Phoenix dactylifera" OR "date palm" OR "date fruit" OR "dried dates" OR "whole dates" OR "Ajwa" OR "Medjool" OR "Zahedi" OR "date seed" OR "date syrup" OR "date sap"`. The complete result contained 32 keys, all in the 17,599-ID replay. All had a PMCID. The full-text XML endpoint returned 22 records and HTTP 500 for ten. Sixteen of the supplied XML records contained a date-palm phrase or cultivar string. NCBI BioC, indexed PMC text, and publisher PDFs supplied seven more contexts on the first pass. Exact-source and identifier probes resolved the indexed term for the nine remaining records. The only date-palm phrase hit came from a 2016 conference review of infectious disease epidemiology. The eight other records matched only `Zahedi`, with seven in the body field and one in the references field. Versioned NIH cloud PDFs, the Cochrane review’s reference list, indexed PMC text, and a PDF from the Europe PMC bulk archive supplied contexts for all nine. The alternatives exposed conference bundles where XML held only the front matter, a wrong article association for the tegaserod PMID, and two public PMC versions under one MALDI PMID. The bulk PDF’s author index cross-references K. Zahedi to a mouse acute-phase protein paper. None of the 32 inspected match contexts supplied a dried-date intervention measuring Parkinson’s pain. The separate date-palm and pain literature search followed cited primary studies and covered people and animal models outside Parkinson’s. It recovered a placebo-controlled exercise study reporting less 24-hour muscle soreness with date seed powder in healthy runners, alongside independent animal studies of fruit and palm-heart extracts, seed fractions, a seed lectin, and palm sap. The recovered 2021 exercise and 2019 labor registrations document different outcome lists from the corresponding pain reports. The foods, preparations, populations, and outcomes tested in these sources leave the frozen dried-date Parkinson’s pain comparison unresolved. ## Reproduction

Build with [build-idrecovery.ps1](../tools/build-idrecovery.ps1). Outputs are under the experiment's `build` directory. The original `claim-search-resume` and `date-identifier-replay` snapshots remain available as inputs.

- `foodexperiment.idrecovery.exe ID_REPLAY_DIR CORE_DIR 0502-2 RECOVERY_DIR` validates the original provider identifiers, groups missing IDs by source, and logs query expressions, request times, response statuses, and transport errors in `requests.tsv`. `batch-ids.tsv` enumerates disjoint request IDs, `inventory.tsv` records each key’s provenance and abstract availability, and `merged_metadata.tsv` contains bibliography in identifier replay order.
- `foodexperiment.idoverlay.exe ID_REPLAY_DIR CORE_DIR RECOVERY_DIR 0502-2 OUTPUT_DIR` produces the depth-compatible combined export at `build/claim-search-complete-date-overlay`. The query row records `Retrieved` with 17,599 reported and retrieved and preserves 94 in the original core page field. `overlay-provenance.tsv` records the separate 176 identifier pages, 82 lookups and original transport error. It retains all 51,508 result rows from the other queries.
- `foodexperiment.datescreen.exe RECOVERY_DIR OUTPUT_DIR [EXACT_ID_JSON]` reconciles titles and available abstracts with the recovery inventory. The optional input is the single complete Europe PMC all-field exact-phrase query result in `resultType=idlist` JSON. With that input, the tool retrieves public XML for every returned PMCID and records access, matching text contexts, and record IDs.
