# Search routes and source limits

The first pass used branch-local Oracle catalog revision 90. It covered 60 original identifiers and 28 scoped claims, including the cultural blends. Of the 60 source questions, 49 were new and all missed at verified original depth. The comparison batch had zero strict hits across 51 new source questions. The separate citation follow-up recovered one already indexed grape-seed oil original among seven logged questions. Six other accession or inventory questions required retrieval.

The archive route verified 48 full regular structured bodies, one short structured Spanish review and 11 complete PMC HTML originals. The ethnobotanical scope includes the published supplement's Table S1 as a separate object. Its constipation entry belongs to Premna serratifolia. Main article and supplement were checked before excluding nutmeg and sumac. [Publisher supplement](https://www.mdpi.com/article/10.3390/life12050733/s1)
