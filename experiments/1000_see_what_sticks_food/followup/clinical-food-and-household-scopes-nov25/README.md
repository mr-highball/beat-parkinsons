# Round 1 clinical, food and household source audit

The November 25 review resolved **62 exact checks across 11 original articles**. Twenty-six provided related context and 36 were outside scope. The archive adds four primary research texts and three historical records. The original raw fig queue stayed at 2,338.

An original Wellcome folio distinguishes anise scent, external nutmeg and an oral mixed-spice remedy for loose stools. The Folger reading copy confirms a separate anise scent entry. The manuscripts document intended uses. The oral ground-nutmeg Parkinson insomnia claim still has no defensible directional signal.

The carrot fiber trial includes constipation on its symptom scale and reports a statistically clear change only for diarrhoea. The orange-fiber original used overlapping single-group pilot samples. The review added 33 related Oracle assessments and four contextual scorecard source families. Thirty-seven scorecards passed rubric validation.

The native replay leaves **31,609** source checks across **21,365** sources. Round 1 has 75 directional findings and 925 open claims. The 203 shared-source pairs and separate 39 UEG 2025 pairs awaiting fuller original access remain queued.
