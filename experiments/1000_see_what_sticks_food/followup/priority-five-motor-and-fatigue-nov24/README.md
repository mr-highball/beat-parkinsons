## Green tea and fatigue

Claim 0948 retains Maybe True with a 51-point evidence anchor for a tentative worsening direction. The one uncontrolled, 14-person brewed-tea cohort gives the direct fatigue score movement. Randomized isolated caffeine and an acute postoperative brewed-tea comparison had favorable estimates with different applicability.

The Kagoshima green-tea cohort is recorded separately as an observational family. It included 105 people with Parkinson disease and studied nighttime sleep and other nonmotor symptoms. Fatigue and daytime sleepiness were outside the specified analysis. The completed extract trial has no posted registry summary results. The 12-family scorecard records one worsening signal, ten contextual families and one uncertain family. A controlled brewed-tea comparison with a fatigue outcome and the full Jiang experimental article remain open.

Two newly cataloged public reports from one forum participant add context on perceived medication wearing off and muscle symptoms during a switch between green tea and coffee. Their endpoints do not add a fatigue signal.
