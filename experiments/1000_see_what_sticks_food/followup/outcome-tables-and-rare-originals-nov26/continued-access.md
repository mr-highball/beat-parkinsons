## Measured outcome gaps

- The [Shiraz multiple-sclerosis publication](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00207454.2020.1750398) names the thesis and first author-version posting, with the original fatigue model and oil-specific exposure still unavailable.
- The [2017 MVP cohort analysis](https://doi.org/10.1155/2017/6405278) supplies an adjusted aggregate PRO-PD estimate. The 2024 baseline report supplies one balance heatmap cell, with no numerical item estimate or coconut-oil grade. The report offers a dataset application path without a public download.
- [NCT01216982](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01216982) planned Epworth sleepiness and lists corn oil. The published Dretsch abstract gives a corn-oil ester comparator, without identifying the same registration or giving arm-level Epworth results.
- [NCT03796455](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03796455) and its final COPD publication report different doses for the blended comparator. Its arm-level gait speed and balance values remain unavailable. Neither source separately measures a corn-oil Parkinson stride effect.
- The original [2017 ethnobiological article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28017696/) abstract verifies the survey, whose black-pepper insomnia entry still traces to a secondary source pending access to the original table. The [later review table](https://www.revistamedicinamilitara.ro/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/3-Traditional-Ethnomedical-and-Ethnobotanical-Applications-and-Uses-of-Piper-Nigrum.pdf) describes a route distinct from oral culinary use.
- The [fennel journal abstract](https://www.magiran.com/volume/35927) supplies a p-value without direction. The later oral-extract trial reports equal end-of-study fatigue counts, while the original and registered comparator descriptions differ. Neither route supplied a fuller fatigue table in this pass.
- The official [Nigella oil trial registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06298760) remains without numerical outcomes. The linked article abstract is available, with its matched-placebo fatigue estimates unverified. The separate [EVACUR protocol](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/85/NCT07398885/Prot_000.pdf) tracks fatigue in systemic lupus under a multi-ingredient intervention.
- Full cranberry and brown-rice animal study outcome tables remain open. The related cranberry juice trial has a current recruitment record and cognitive outcomes with no Parkinson-specific bradykinesia result.
