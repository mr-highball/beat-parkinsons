Ordinary lentils are *Lens culinaris* (also *Vicia lens*, *Lens esculenta* and *Ervum lens*). Names such as dal require checking ingredients. Grass pea is *Lathyrus sativus*, including almorta and kesari dal, and can be associated with neurolathyrism. *Vicia faba* and *Mucuna pruriens* have separate levodopa literature. Kidney beans are seed cultivars of *Phaseolus vulgaris*, which also includes culinary groups such as green, black and pinto beans. A shared species can prompt follow-up without identifying the frozen cultivar or preparation. The 2018 Anselmi rat experiment used pea lectin from *Pisum sativum*. The alpha-synuclein antibody assay's generic bean agglutinin was concanavalin-A from jack bean. ## Main primary findings

- The three Parkinson diets mentioning lentils provide composite motor scores, with decreases in the small Baroni and Hegelmaier comparisons and an inconclusive before-and-after change in Bedarf. None reports preparation-specific intake linked to rigidity, walking speed or facial expression. Nutri-Park measured gait and included an example dinner containing 150 g of unspecified legumes. It supplies no named ingredient intake paired to gait speed. [Baroni trial](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-and-Nutrition.md#2011--potato-and-olive-oil-in-a-protein-redistributed-menu) · [Hegelmaier and Gatti](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md) · [Bedarf pilot](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md)
- An injected red lentil extract reduced drug-induced catalepsy in rats at the higher doses in the primary report. Parkinson diagnosis, oral boiled exposure and separately measured rigidity would be needed to address the frozen claim. The isolated kidney bean lectin study measured reporter changes in nematodes and the isolated lentil lectin study tested antibody binding. [Red lentil and kidney bean laboratory findings](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md) · [Lentil lectin assay](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md#isolated-lentil-lectin-in-an-alpha-synuclein-antibody-assay)
- The only kidney bean intervention string in the previous trial screen was a training prop. The primary 2019 report linked to NCT02600858 confirms that Parkinson participants moved raw kidney beans between cups. The 2021 and 2024 secondary analyses are from the same study family. Brain Bites names a chicken and lentil dish but the provisional entry has no posted results or recipe specifications. [Raw bean feeding simulation](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md#kidney-beans-as-objects-in-a-parkinson-feeding-simulation) · [Local registry text](../../../../research/2015-2019/Source-Text/Trials/NCT02600858.txt) · [Brain Bites](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md#lentil-dish-in-the-proposed-brain-bites-parkinson-meal-box)
- Human poisoning records in the United Kingdom, Japan and France concern acute gastrointestinal illness and inadequately prepared beans. The British series may overlap. The Japanese ministry bulletin and later chapter report the same outbreak. Kidney bean allergy cases include skin and oral effects after mixed meals or cooked beans. No source establishes worsening of Parkinson drooling or visual hallucinations. [UK investigations](../../../../research/1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md) · [Japanese government report](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md) · [French outbreak and allergy cases](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Kidney-Bean.md)
- Parkinson diet questionnaires pool bean or legume species. The 2022 MVP tables provide a pooled food item against total symptom burden and separate sleep, anxiety, drool and hallucination severity only against whole-pattern scores. The Cassani dietary comparison shows higher pooled legume intake among people with existing swallowing problems. It does not link a particular food to drooling. The original whole-lentil metabolic trial included weekly gastrointestinal symptom ratings without measuring sleep or Parkinson outcomes.
