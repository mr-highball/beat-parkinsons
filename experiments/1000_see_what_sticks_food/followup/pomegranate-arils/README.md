# Raw pomegranate arils follow-up

The November 13, 2026 UTC checkpoint covers frozen IDs 0537, 0635, 0692 and 0942. The search looked for benefit, worsening, and no clear difference after the specified oral raw arils. It also followed formulations and measurements that could lead back to those outcomes. Twelve related or pending assessment rows were added across the four claims. The evidence leaves all four directions unresolved.

## Clinical registration and study identity

The later exact-ID recheck adds an investigator conference lead and records current enrollment, outcome and history gaps. [Registration and presentation follow-through](../pomegranate-nano-pso-registration-recheck-nov16/README.md)

The official source for NCT05142085 was already present in the catalog under its generic Nano-PSO title. Its text identifies the active material as nanodroplet pomegranate seed oil. The current public API still gives `hasResults=false`, a November 2021 verification date, first and last public posting of December 2, 2021, and status `UNKNOWN`. The listed completion dates are estimates. The structured enrollment is estimated at 170 and the narrative contains other planned counts. The registry lists 12 references as background, with none tagged as results. Native history endpoints returned HTTP 403. Search by NCT, INNN0121, full title, Marie-Catherine Boll, Nora Kerik Rotenberg, Ulises Rodríguez Ortiz, Distribuidora Biolife and the terms GranaGard, Nano-PSO, omega-5, granatin and granada produced no matching primary human results. Sponsor pages give product identity and promotional material. They do not supply the Parkinson trial dataset. [Official study](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05142085) · [Official API record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies/NCT05142085) · [Local registration text](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/Trials/NCT05142085.txt)

The 2024 Sánchez-Camacho paper uses the named GranaGard formulation in rats and has a distinct author list and design. The 2021 GranaGard trial enrolled patients with multiple sclerosis and measured cognition. COGMEG5 shares the sponsor and specifies GranaGard in a different older-adult cohort with cognitive impairment. These are separate from the Boll registration. [Mexican animal paper and MS pilot](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Pomegranate.md#nanoemulsified-seed-oil-in-a-separate-mexican-rat-study) · [COGMEG5 registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06520878)

Tapias found worse rearing and more nigral cell loss with diluted juice in one rat model. The Poznań reports found more favorable postural, olfactory and vertical-activity comparisons using another juice and exposure protocol. The 2019 and 2021 Poznań reports share a grant and animal approval, and their independence remains unverified. The July 2021 conference and August 2021 journal report are treated as one study family. Fathy’s paraquat experiment is separate. Pomegranate appears in Hegelmaier’s Parkinson menu with many other foods and no verified aril preparation, intake or relevant nonmotor outcome.
