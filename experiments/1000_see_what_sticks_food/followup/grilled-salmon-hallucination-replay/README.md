# Grilled salmon and visual hallucination frequency · 0445

The Parkinson diet trial links different salmon preparations to different arms. The low-fat plan uses canned salmon and the ketogenic plan cooks a fillet in a pan. One ketogenic participant's visual hallucinations resolved after a pramipexole reduction. Individual salmon intake, timing and hallucination frequency were unreported. A separate Parkinson chart review reports less common hallucinations among people reporting high-protein-associated motor fluctuations in a subgroup. It gives neither a salmon contrast nor type or frequency of hallucinations. A trial in forensic inpatients assigned salmon prepared by grilling or boiling alongside other treatment conditions and measured anxiety. The separate marine fish poisoning case reports identify *Sarpa salpa*. Neither supplies a Parkinson grilled-salmon hallucination comparison. An exact registry lead, NCT03851861, links to a Parkinson Mediterranean pilot with gastrointestinal outcomes and no reported individual salmon intake.
