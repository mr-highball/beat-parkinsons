# Round 1 dietary protein and levodopa direction audit

This checkpoint follows the controlled protein comparisons from the [direction audit](../direction-bias-audit-nov21/README.md). It focuses on whether a measured adverse or favorable effect can transfer to an exact frozen food preparation and symptom. Round 2 remains queued.

## The clearest trade-off

In the six-person Barichella crossover, the formulated low-protein regimen reduced daily OFF time from 5 to 3.5 hours (p = .01) and lengthened daily ON with dyskinesia from 4.5 to 6 hours (p = .04). The same comparison can favor one outcome and worsen another. The full methods identify all six participants as a subset of the preceding 18-person crossover. Count both reports as one family. Each follow-up regimen was seven days. The abstract's 42 days per regimen refers to the aggregate across six participants when reconciled with the methods and figure. [Original 2007 article, Methods and Figure 1](../../../../research/2005-2009/Source-Text/PMC2413077.txt) · [2006 author abstract](../../../../research/2005-2009/Source-Text/2006-PMID16773618-Low-Protein-Foods-and-Off-Time-Abstract.txt)

Pincus and Barry's full 1987 report distinguishes immediate responses in two clinical subgroups from a fixed-order inpatient comparison performed in some participants. Its three figures are from one participant and show more dyskinesia on lower daytime protein and worse disability on higher protein. Three related author abstracts supply additional adverse or favorable motor observations. Subject-level overlap across these reports remains unresolved. [Printed pages 133–137](../../../../research/1985-1989/Source-Text/1987-Pincus-Barry-Daytime-Protein-and-Motor-Fluctuation.txt) · [Seven-person comparison](../../../../research/1985-1989/Source-Text/1987-PMID3632370-Amino-Acids-and-Dyskinesia.txt) · [Eleven-person series](../../../../research/1985-1989/Source-Text/1987-PMID3827678-Protein-Diet-Bradykinesia-and-Chorea.txt) · [Sixteen-person series](../../../../research/1985-1989/Source-Text/1988-PMID3126411-Redistribution-Dopa-Resistant-Off.txt)

Nutt's oral meal comparison and fixed-infusion challenge separate plasma kinetics from clinical response. Carter reported different average ON time under three regimens despite similar hourly mean plasma levodopa. Tsui reported three higher peaks on the higher-protein regimen with poorer performance. The ordinary-lunch kinetics study found no peak or timing difference and collected no separate clinical motor endpoint. Juncos's very-high-protein formula and ordinary-allowance regimens also gave different results. Virmani's chart review identified reports of an interaction in 52 of 877 levodopa users and in 52 of 421 with motor fluctuations. Weight loss was recorded by 12 of 20 with documented diet changes. The chart history does not establish a causal population frequency, whether the changes improved symptoms, or a risk for particular ingredients. The newer crossover's registry presents descriptive motor daily-activity means without a paired contrast or separate items. Its actigraphy assesses nighttime sleep efficiency and shows very similar summaries. The posted adverse events have uncertain attribution and the registry gives inconsistent washout durations. ## Application to the frozen claims

The 0020 scorecard separates the Barichella and Baroni families and the 0113 scorecard records the actual design and direction of the older dyskinesia observation. Both retain Unknown. A whole-diet reduction of OFF time supplies no isolated roasted potato effect, and longer periods with dyskinesia under a dietary regimen supply no baked trout severity estimate. The other audited transfer paths remain open. A weak directional score still requires a specific, evidenced bridge from the observed exposure to the frozen one. Measuring how a compound or a varied menu behaves does not establish that eating a specific culinary preparation would reproduce the comparison.
