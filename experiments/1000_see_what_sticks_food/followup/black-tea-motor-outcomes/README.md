Two LRRK2 samples reported lower overall MDS-UPDRS II levels with recalled lifetime caffeinated black tea. The idiopathic comparison coefficients were inconclusive. A PPMI supplementary table supplies the same coefficient as the article but disagrees with its cohort label and sample count. Neither public table gives a separate walking or tremor result. The original Parkinson caffeine trials distinguish early uncontrolled rest-tremor improvement and withdrawal reports from inconclusive randomized item comparisons. An older gait survey groups black and oolong tea and reports examiner-rated gait quality.
