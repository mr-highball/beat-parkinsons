# Raw strawberry and Parkinson falls, checkpoint 26

Claim 0413 remains **Unknown (50)**. The Western Australian cohort followed 1,429 older women for up to 14.5 years. Its fruit questionnaire identified strawberries, then included them in a group with six other fruits. Original Supplementary Table S4 gives adjusted HR 1.00 (95% CI 0.98–1.03) per 20 g/day for that group. The estimate is for fall-related admissions, with no individual strawberry estimate, total fall count or Parkinson stratum. The companion whole-fruit crossover randomized six fruits together and found inconclusive postural sway (time-by-phase p=.457). A 2025 SAGE cross-sectional analysis lends context to produce intake and reported injurious falls, with separate results by sex and only combined fruit and vegetable exposure.
