# Round 1 original-source and nonmotor outcome review

November 26, 2026. The Oracle was queried for exact identities before original full-body retrieval. We selected publications that appeared in several open checks and checked each assigned ingredient, preparation and symptom against the body. The 50 authenticated originals cover 200 exact memberships, including 76 related contexts and 124 exclusions. All 200 came from priority 6 of the existing queue. An exclusion here resolves the relevance of a publication to a specific claim.

The targeted review updated two clinical rationales and corrected five existing scorecard family classifications. The menopause trials report a composite and a urinary leakage item. Urgency frequency stays open. A separate 2021 Moroccan field survey documents customary sage use for renal complaints with no measured clinical effect. The original controlled studies of ginger powder in healthy adults and during chemotherapy, and ginger extract in patients with migraine, give inconclusive anxiety comparisons in their own populations. The separate generalized anxiety trial has an internally inconsistent abstract and restricted original arm data.

The original search still has 28,973 queued pairs across 20,672 publication keys and 374 claims. The frozen hypotheses and all 1,000 recorded verdicts and anchors were checked for stability. This packet records the completed source review and its access limits. Round 2 remains queued.
