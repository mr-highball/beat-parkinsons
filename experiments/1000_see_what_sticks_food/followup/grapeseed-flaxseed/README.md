## Findings

- A small 2014 wristband patent reported improvements in hand performance with topical oil. The Parkinson rat study used a hydroalcoholic seed extract and measured gait, coordination and limb movements. The rat study of drug-induced oral dyskinesia used an injected haloperidol nanocapsule containing grapeseed oil. None of these outcomes quantify human Parkinson speech intelligibility or dystonia. [2010–2014 notes](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Flaxseed-Followup.md) · [2015–2019 notes](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Flaxseed-Followup.md)
- Registered oral grapeseed cookies have related grip, gait and sleep measures. The published randomized paper reports fatty acids and cardiolipins. The insomnia trial plans ISI, actigraphy and EEG and its recipe is undisclosed. The terminated hospitalized-adult study used grapeseed oil on gauze and had no posted results. [2020–2024 notes](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Flaxseed-Followup.md)
- The UFRGS dissertation gave the product identity and full appendix. In the same research program, a flaxseed-oil chow changed an elevated-plus-maze outcome, left open-field crossings and rearings without clear diet effects, and increased some inflammatory measures. Isolated alpha-linolenic acid changed measured behavior in two Parkinson mouse models. Its dose and administration are distinct from the frozen oil exposure. [UFRGS dissertation](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Flaxseed-Followup.md#flaxseed-oil-in-the-ufrgs-maternal-separation-experiments) · [Isolated ALA studies](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Flaxseed-Followup.md#isolated-alpha-linolenic-acid-and-motor-behavior-in-two-parkinson-models)
