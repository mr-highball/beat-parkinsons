- **0001, cold-pressed olive oil and postural instability.** Four rodent reports used olive oil to deliver a different intervention. The 2019 lesion experiment used olive oil in both decisive arms, and the rotarod measured time before falling. The oil grade remained unspecified. The human gait evidence and the pending VIVI-OLIVE protocol remain separately recorded. The clinical claim remains Unknown. - **0025, fresh sage and pain.** The palliative cancer trial randomized sage tea rinse versus saline. Few participants reported mouth or swallowing pain at baseline, and the authors omitted these pain measures from the between-group analysis. Earlier spray and postsurgery comparisons address different exposures. The clinical claim remains Unknown. [Original trial assessment](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sage-Pain-Comparisons.md)
- **0937, freshly grated ginger and anxiety.** Two rat-maze reports used standardized mixtures of ginger and ginkgo. At 1 mg/kg the earlier test was inconclusive, while the later 2.5-to-1 mixture increased open-arm behavior. The earlier series also reported different behavior at its low and high doses. The combined extracts and maze endpoints leave the clinical claim Unknown. [Separated original reports](../../../../research/1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Ginger-Ginkgo-Maze.md)
