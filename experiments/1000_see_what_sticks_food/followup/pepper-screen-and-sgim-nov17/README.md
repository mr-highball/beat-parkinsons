## Poached chicken and fatigue severity

The 2016 SGIM case describes fatigue and other signs after a diet of almost entirely fried chicken and pasta. The patient had undetectable vitamin C and symptoms resolved after supplementation. The authors attribute the presentation to scurvy in a restricted diet. The case has no Parkinson diagnosis, unconfounded chicken comparison or measure of change in fatigue severity attributable to food. Claim 0348 remains Unknown at 50.
