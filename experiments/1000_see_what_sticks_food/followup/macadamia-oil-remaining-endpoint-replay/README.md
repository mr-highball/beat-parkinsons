# Cold-pressed macadamia oil and dyskinesia

The first search design used masked fields: cold-pressed macadamia oil and dyskinesia severity. The frozen direction was checked afterward: claim 0212 predicts worsening. No direct evidence was identified that favors either direction. Whole nuts, unspecified-grade oil, individual fatty acids and topical products are different exposures.

The closest new primary source is Tardiff et al. 2022, PMID 35060064. Male rats received custom food containing macadamia and soybean oil in a YTX-7739 pharmacology experiment. The drug's vehicle was methylcellulose, not macadamia oil. The paper does not identify cold pressing or individual consumed amount, and measured fatty-acid desaturation rather than levodopa-induced dyskinesia. Its “PD response” means pharmacodynamic response. The dated note and factual extract give source and figure results behind one related assessment. [Primary result notes](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Macadamia-Dyskinesia.md) Native merge validated the assessment and one dated annotation against an isolated copied catalog and article text.

The rat paper cites two Dutch human YTX trials. The official multiple-dose record NL-OMON55131 was published 13 August 2020; its publicly accessible PDF describes randomized YTX-7739 versus placebo in healthy adults and Parkinson patients, with safety and pharmacokinetics, but no macadamia-oil intervention. A sponsor presentation describes YTX-7739 given with a low-fat meal; it does not establish the identity or ingestion of macadamia oil. These are drug studies, not a human continuation of the rat oil dietary experiment. [Official Dutch record](https://onderzoekmetmensen.nl/en/node/55131/pdf) · [Sponsor protocol presentation](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1445283/000119312521163842/d186146dex991.htm).

The exact native ClinicalTrials.gov query [registry-expression.txt](registry-expression.txt) and receipt [registry-query.tsv](registry-query.tsv) returned 6/6 records in one page. They concern healthy or cardiometabolic participants, skin use, psychiatric research, or active comparator oils; none has Parkinson in its title or condition. NCT01510223 assigns a macadamia and olive oil mixture for digestive/cardiovascular measurements; NCT02908932 uses macadamia oil as a comparator to krill oil in military trainees. Neither identifies a cold-pressed product or isolated oil effect on Parkinson dyskinesia. Other registries remain open.
