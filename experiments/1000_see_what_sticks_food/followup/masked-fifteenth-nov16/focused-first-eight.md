# Fifteenth masked group: focused source follow-through

Search performed 2026-11-16, using ingredient/preparation and endpoint without frozen direction. This is a selective web/primary follow-through; native provider batch and exact pair reconciliation are handled separately. Searches and access limitations below are not claims of all-index coverage.

## 0627 cooked wheat berries / resting tremor
Existing assessment records celiac disease and parkinsonism following gluten withdrawal (PMID24464413) and separate celiac/thyroid case (PJNS2020-15-2-30); neither isolates cooked wheat berries or resting-tremor difference. This pass found no measured whole-wheat comparison. A gluten-restriction case in a person with celiac disease is not evidence that wheat causes or reduces rest tremor in people with PD generally. Unknown remains appropriate.

## 0587 raw watermelon / apathy
Belem cohort reported 15 weekly, 9 monthly, 16 rare/never consumers but did not report preparation or apathy by watermelon intake (research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cherry-Cranberry-Watermelon.md). Existing NCT03626168 companion trial used pasteurized juice in 16 postmenopausal participants and cognitive tests, not an apathy instrument; no inference from cognition or energy. A separately identified watermelon fresh-juice RCT [PMC12515702](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12515702/) measures exercise and soreness, not apathy. No supportable directional lean identified.

## 0461 fresh tarragon / walking slowness
Existing Nowras PMID32245296 oral aqueous extract improved ordinal EAE clinical scores (weakness/paralysis), not gait speed; the 2017 oral extract stress experiment records maze entries but not walking speed; 2013 intraperitoneal essential-oil toxicity included abnormal gait at very high doses (research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md and 2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md). A separate original [Jahani 2019, PMC6937744](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6937744/) used intraperitoneal ethanolic extract; open-field total distance was generally unchanged, except a significant decrease in Swiss mice at 100 mg/kg versus vehicle (Figure 3, n=10), without Parkinson or gait velocity. The study is already catalogued for antidepressant tests, but this specific motor context could support a cautious related-only assessment if Dexter considers endpoint proximity sufficient. Ethanol extract injection, normal animals, distance vs speed and nonmonotonic response make either clinical direction indefensible.

## 0424 fresh marjoram / drooling
Existing PD RCT PMID34455218 [original](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1353802021003072): tea prepared using 5 g dried Origanum majorana leaves/100 mL boiling water, 15 min, filtered; 30-day double-blind, 28 tea and 23 placebo completers. Authors report improved aggregate NMSS; no drooling-item contrast is available in the accessible abstract/snippets, and infusion is not fresh-leaf eating. A distinct [2019 neurofeedback study](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/0611/61dcd56e505ce959908531f36296c39de808.pdf) exposed bruxism patients to inhaled essential oil and assayed salivary myeloperoxidase; sample counts are repeated assays, not flow rate or drooling. No directional grade.

## 0527 ground cardamom / low voice
Existing PMID32041265 contains cardamom in a multicomponent PD diet, outcome composite motor score without separately reported voice intensity. Search yielded product-label or ethnomedical claims for hoarseness and mixtures, including official [Arya Vaidya Sala therapeutic index](https://www.aryavaidyasala.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/THERAPEUTIC-INDEX-2023-new.pdf); no single-ingredient controlled acoustic loudness estimate. Hoarseness is not low volume. No new assessment proposed.

## 0464 ground allspice / falls
Existing eugenol MPTP mouse study PMID36002316 shows prevention vs post-treatment narrow-beam crossing in opposite directions; isolated constituent, mouse performance rather than falls. Existing 2022 pumpkin-spice mixed-meal crossover included allspice in an unquantified blend but did not measure falls. The 2022 allspice allergy case remains original body blocked (PMID35577235); allergic syncope or falls cannot be imputed from its title. No clinical fall direction.

## 0571 cold-pressed canola oil / anxiety
New source candidate: Jazayeri et al. 2008, PMID18247193, DOI 10.1080/00048670701827275, Australian and New Zealand Journal of Psychiatry 42(3):192–198, issue March 2008; first earlier online date not established. [Original full paper, Methods and Table 4](https://www.vetproplus.com/_files/ugd/0de77a_fdd53ec3d2cb49b7b99982bace70c678.pdf). In an eight-week double-dummy trial, 60 adults with major depression were randomized to EPA alone, fluoxetine plus placebo oil, or both; 48 (16 each) with >=4 weeks were analyzed. Fluoxetine arm received two 550 mg rapeseed-oil softgels daily, each with 11 mg vitamin E. Reported anxiety adverse events were 9/16 for fluoxetine+rapeseed, 1/16 EPA alone, 3/16 EPA+fluoxetine; overall three-group Fisher p=.009; authors report anxiety restricted to early weeks. Neither extraction grade nor oil-only comparison is given. Drugs and EPA differ across arms, severity change was not quantified for anxiety, and denominator is analyzed subgroup rather than all 60 assigned. Therefore it cannot isolate oil, establish worsening or benefit, or support a directional lean in Parkinson disease. Other in-catalog anxiety contexts include inconclusive HADS for NCT02151162 blended oil and opposing/heterogeneous animal models. Candidate is complementary related-only, not a directional change.

## 0479 refined peanut oil / drooling
Existing rigorous blinded oil challenge PMID9133891 observed no allergic reactions to refined oil in 60 peanut-allergic persons, six crude-oil reactions; no drooling measurement. Acute oral allergy and salivary management are distinct. Generic oil-pulling review searched peanut oil but does not itself establish an individual peanut-oil saliva trial. No new directly measured clinical or related oil-specific drooling result verified.
