# Independent 0480 transfer evaluation

Retain Unknown at 50. Two human studies suggest less pain in the specific setting of male chronic prostatitis/chronic pelvic pain. An effect in that setting remains plausible. The oral study's numeric pain contrast and oil processing are unresolved. The topical study primarily compares local ultrasound coupling and delivery systems. Their direction is insufficient to infer Parkinson pain after oral cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil. A small controlled oral comparison for another pain subtype does not resolve the uncertainty.

- **Tantawy et al., September 18, 2018.** PMID 30271759, PMCID PMC6149936, DOI 10.2147/RRU.S167896. Three randomized groups of 20. A used 3 mL pharmaceutical-grade pumpkin seed oil as the coupling medium for trans-perineal ultrasound. B used regular gel and active ultrasound. C received sham ultrasound. Processing was unreported. Table 2 pain mean (SD) at baseline and post-treatment: A 17.20 (2.22) to 3.71 (1.75), B 17.80 (1.90) to 5.21 (2.31), C 16.90 (2.10) to 14.62 (1.73). Change A −13.49, B −12.59, C −2.28. Unadjusted additional change A vs B was −0.90 points. Table 3's −6.35 A vs B and p=.0001 are the NIH-CPSI total comparison. The text reports differences in subscores, but Tables 2–3 give no pain-specific pairwise p-value or interval. The paper gives a contradictory duration for the active gel arm, whose Methods say five sessions weekly for “three consecutive days” while A/C and the study period describe three weeks. Verified primary full XML is preserved at `primary/Tantawy-2018-pmc.xml`. - **Khalil et al., April 30, 2025.** The recoverable original abstract randomized 25 men each to 5 mg tadalafil + 1010 mg pumpkin seed oil versus tadalafil. Clinically significant overall improvement 11/25 oil combination and 12/25 tadalafil alone. Abstract describes the pain domain as the exception to otherwise unclear advantage. The numeric pain results, adjustment, processing and precise trial procedures remain open. Crossref lists original article 449998 with the PDF below. - **Gossell-Williams et al., May 5, 2011.** Original Methods specify purchased Soft-gel Technologies capsules, 2 g/day *C. pepo* seed oil, with 21 randomized to PSO and 14 to wheat germ oil. No pressing method stated. Joint-pain medians 2 (IQR 1–2) to 1 (1–2) versus 1.5 (0–2) to 1 (0–2), between arms at week 12 p=.663. The within-arm p=.030 belongs to PSO and does not identify a between-arm effect. This is an inconclusive oral contrast for a different pain subtype.
