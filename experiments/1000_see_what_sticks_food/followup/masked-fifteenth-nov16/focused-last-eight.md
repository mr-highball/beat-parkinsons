# Fifteenth masked evidence review

All eight claims remain Unknown at 50 after assessing each preparation and outcome without consulting the frozen direction. Strong signals in separate cohorts were acute Parkinson gait and balance during mint gum chewing (0494), and improved daytime dysfunction during an intranasal whole-pumpkin preparation (0582). Neither anchors a weak directional prediction for its named exposure and endpoint. The confirmed peach carrier results fill a reporting gap for 0610. Falcone's separate randomized study used 900 mg/day of a standardized aqueous spearmint extract in 142 healthy, active adults.
