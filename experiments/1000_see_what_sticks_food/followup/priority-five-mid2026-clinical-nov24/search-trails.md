# Search trails and verified original access

The initial exact publication pass used branch Oracle revision 105. It verified 54 PMC structured article bodies and six authenticated alternate HTML originals. Three exact accession hits identified existing source texts. New targeted registry and grape-seed original searches used revision 106.

The Oracle registry lookup found NCT06309914 and NCT07882108. ClinicalTrials.gov API identifies the former as completed without registry results, and the latter as recruiting without results. The 2026 Ginkgo companion describes the four-arm parent study and reports only chosen product arms. The [Papa study PDF](https://cdn.fortunejournals.com/articles/exploring-the-neurocognitive-potential-of-a-sustainable-grape-seed-extract-results-fro-6740.pdf) reports the separate grape seed extract comparison. Its Table 2 was read in text and checked visually on printed page 21. The POMS tension contrast was kept separate from the working-memory result and from the Ginkgo findings.

The 2026 literature review led to the [Indonesian health ministry archive](https://www.badankebijakan.kemkes.go.id/repositori/id/eprint/5470/). The scanned excerpt documents field survey methods and includes one preparation table page. Its published page 25 gives two local plant names for a traditional constipation preparation. The study's partial public access does not expose enough detail to identify those plants or verify the later review's ginger and turmeric blood-pressure entries from the primary report. The two catalog records state which findings come from the scan and which are attributed to the review.
