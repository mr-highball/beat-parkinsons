# Fatigue, motor symptoms, bowel outcomes and mood, November 15, 2026

## Direction change

**0262, cold-pressed sesame oil reduces depressive symptoms — Maybe True at 51.** The original abstract from a 60-person randomized trial concludes that depression scores fell with sesame compared with sunflower oil in women with fatty liver disease. The p value of .013 does not identify a between- or within-arm analysis, and the original arm values, instrument and uncertainty are unavailable. Matching companion Methods describe refined, odorless oils used with calorie restriction. The population and preparation materially limit transfer. A Parkinson study gave sesame oil as the vehicle in both arms, and animal behavioral endpoints are mixed. The lean comes from the oral human comparison. ## Closest evidence for the remaining claims

- **0348, poached chicken and fatigue.** The 30-person chicken soup crossover gave an inconclusive POMS fatigue contrast (−0.5 versus +0.1, p = .61). J-STAGE reports February 2009 availability and a December 2008 acceptance. Chicken essence lowered task-induced fatigue in the Nagai arithmetic comparison and the Yamano post-rest comparison at one week. Their other task or later time comparisons gave mixed findings. The 2015 extract trials provide inconclusive ordinary tiredness results, with cohort overlap unresolved. No arm assigned ordinary poached chicken or measured Parkinson fatigue. [Soup comparison](../../../../research/2005-2009/Food-Experiment-Chicken-Fatigue.md) · [Laboratory fatigue trial](../../../../research/1995-1999/Food-and-Nutrition.md#1996--chicken-extract-and-laboratory-induced-mental-fatigue) · [Later extract trials](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Chicken-Fatigue.md)
- **0382, boiled lentils and anxiety.** The original randomized 12-person six-condition crossover used canned lentils in breakfast burritos, in whole or blended form. The conference abstract groups higher anxiety in a broader short-term mood pattern, with no separate anxiety magnitude and no main or interaction effect in anxiety area under the curve. The 2014 food and appetite paper, 2017 conference abstract and official registration are one study. [Original methods and registry](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Meal-Anxiety.md) · [Mood outcomes](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Lentil-Meal-Anxiety.md)
- **0227, cracked black pepper and constipation.** A healthy-adult capsule pilot showed an imprecise numerical delay in upper digestive tract transit (p = .09). Isolated piperine slowed transit in some animal settings, while a crude extract gave both stimulatory and inhibitory results and later lesion studies measured fecal proxies. The new mixed-formula pilot is uncontrolled and its older-subgroup counts exceed the reported enrollment. There is no direction for clinical Parkinson constipation. [Powder capsule pilot](../../../../research/1990-1994/Food-Experiment-Black-Pepper-Transit.md) · [Model findings](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Black-Pepper-Transit.md) · [Uncontrolled symptom study](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Black-Pepper-Transit.md)
- **0243, ground cinnamon and anxiety.** Moura and Churihar report inconclusive standalone cinnamaldehyde maze contrasts. Combinations with other drugs changed selected measures. The Jiao paper describes improved anxiety in its abstract, while Figure 2 mainly gives mobility outcomes and an inconclusive urination contrast. Publicly available Qubty figures identify open-arm testing in injured mice and the abstract reports an inconclusive anxiety difference. The Qubty body remains restricted. The soluble extract prediabetes registry has glucose results. The earlier human laboratory stress comparison gives inconclusive state anxiety. [Moura and registry](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Anxiety.md) · [Churihar](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Anxiety.md) · [Jiao and Qubty originals](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Cinnamon-Anxiety.md)
- **0525 and 0266, spearmint stride and dried basil OFF time.** An extract of hybrid peppermint increased measured stride 6.05 versus 4.27 cm in rotenone mice. The human spearmint tea trial measured six-minute distance, and the Parkinson gum work used unidentified mint flavor. The basil literature provides animal motor tests and no clinical OFF duration after dried basil. [Mint stride outcomes](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Mint-Stride.md) · [Basil model boundaries](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Basil-Off-Replay.md)
- **0109 and 0169, almond and grapeseed oil depression.** Almond appears as an inhaled carrier and grapeseed as a shared massage carrier. Neither contrast estimates edible oil effects. A separate 50-person extract study gave a DASS depression comparison of −5.64 versus −5.56 after baseline adjustment (p = .95). [Carrier trials](../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Carrier-Oils-Mood.md) · [Separate extract study](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Mood.md)
- **0113 and 0119, trout dyskinesia and sardine anxiety.** The small Pincus study addresses protein schedules and dyskinesia. The official DHA trial's incomplete incidence counts concern isolated fatty acid. A separate marine omega-3 trial reports lower anxiety in students using EPA-rich capsules. The pooled dietary fish studies leave baked fish species and Parkinson symptom effects unresolved.
