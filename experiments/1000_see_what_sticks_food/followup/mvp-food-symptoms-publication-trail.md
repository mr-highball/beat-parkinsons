# MVP food frequencies and individual Parkinson’s symptoms

Checked 12 November 2026. The investigator’s report gives an exploratory association between pooled fresh vegetables and greater motivation in Parkinson’s disease. Its absolute baseline correlation is 0.166368. The available records do not identify the effect of raw kale or raw cucumber, provide an adjusted estimate for motivation, or measure within-person motivation change after changing vegetable intake. The nearest frozen claims are `0532` and `0204`. The publications support recording food-group context for both, with no directional verdict update. [Primary result and claim links](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-MVP-Followup.md#fresh-vegetables-motivation-and-other-individual-symptoms)

## Measured fields and preparation fit

The live Bastyr request form lists 68 food-frequency fields and 33 individual PRO-PD symptoms plus the three composite fields. Its current attachment is the 2021 data dictionary, which contains 676 variable rows, including 68 food-frequency and 15 MIND fields. Survey versions changed over the study, and the 2026 validation describes 35 symptoms. The dictionary and form expose field definitions and request options. They do not reveal participant responses, dates of field availability or overlap of complete exposure and symptom records by wave. ## Access for unresolved outcome estimates

The report’s short link redirects to the verified Bastyr form. The form permits requesting Food Frequency, Dietary Behaviors and PRO-PD together. It asks for a signed agreement, institution and investigator information, hypothesis, proposed use, applicable ethics-review material, security and a justification for variables. Options include analysis by Bastyr, collaborative analysis or applicant analysis. The agreement requires safeguards, bars re-identification, and specifies PI review and written consent before publication or presentation. The dataset is held by Bastyr, with de-identified subsets available to approved researchers under agreement. The public attachments are instrument and agreement files. The form has not been submitted. A request for aggregate results could ask for the numeric signed baseline coefficients and analytic denominators for the printed heatmap, then request estimates for fresh vegetables and `motivation`, cooked oats and `motivation`, and coffee/green tea and `fatigue` separately. For a repeated analysis the necessary fields include linked de-identified survey waves, elapsed visit time, baseline symptom severity, food frequency and comparable adjustment variables. Food and symptom repeat coverage, missingness and attrition would determine which analyses are feasible. Even approved access to the documented variables would leave the raw-kale and raw-cucumber hypotheses unresolved without ingredient-specific measurement.

## Coconut oil balance and other individual item pairs

The December 2024 report's duplicate baseline heatmaps show a small inverse oil-frequency and balance association, a near-zero inverse oatmeal and dyskinesia association, and a near-zero positive turkey and general tremor association. The baked preparation and resting component are unavailable. The published graph uses the oatmeal field and the questionnaire also defines a distinct cooked-oats field. Aggregate requests could include numeric values, denominators and uncertainty for these pairs, with availability of an exact preparation and symptom checked before analysis. ## Individual anxiety, dyskinesia, speech and insomnia

The same baseline Parkinson graph shows small inverse olive oil and anxiety and coconut oil and dyskinesia associations, with a tiny positive turkey and general speech association and near-zero inverse generic spice and insomnia association. These are graphed estimates with no oil grade or roasted-turkey details. The recipe, exposure and outcome-specific denominator and uncertainty remain open. ## Fish anxiety, coconut fatigue and oatmeal balance

The two printed copies support inverse signs for non-fried fish and anxiety and for coconut oil and fatigue. The association between oatmeal and imbalance is near zero, also inverse. The dictionary does not isolate salmon, poaching, virgin coconut oil or rolled oats. Pair-specific sample sizes and uncertainty remain open. ## Olive oil frequency and pooled tremor in the baseline graphic

In the 10-year MVP report, the olive-oil-frequency tiles for pooled tremor on PDF pages 64 and 65 match legend colors close to and below zero. They give an approximately inverse sign for this descriptive association. The plot does not identify oil grade, separate rest tremor from other tremor, adjust the pair for confounding or supply uncertainty. The separate Parkinson whole-diet ketogenic trial reports early adverse events that combine tremor and rigidity while using extra-virgin olive oil among many menu components. Neither study isolates the named preparation and rest-tremor endpoint.
