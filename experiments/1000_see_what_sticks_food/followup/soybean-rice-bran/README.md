# Soybean oil and rice bran oil follow-up

This November 13, 2026 UTC pass covers all 28 frozen claims. Fourteen name refined soybean oil and 14 name refined rice bran oil. It adds 26 related assessments and one pending assessment across 17 claims. The evidence leaves the original clinical directions unresolved.

## Soybean oil

| Frozen claims | Evidence reviewed |
| --- | --- |
| 0071 walking slowness, 0856 bradykinesia | The rotenone mouse comparison included 5% soybean oil in both healthy and toxin controls. The 5% perilla substitution improved cylinder rearing, with no clearly reported equal-fat pole difference. The 2011 rat fat comparison and earlier Parkinson diet models measured broader movement. No soybean lot grade or clinical walking-speed estimate was verified. [Dated soybean comparator](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#assigned-soybean-oil-diet-in-a-rotenone-mouse-comparison) |
| 0107 dyskinesia, 0391 daily off time | Ground soybeans improved acute dyskinesia and on-period when taken with levodopa in seven people. The assigned preparation was beans and the measure was short-term. Rat studies give fewer oral movements in soybean comparator groups than under hydrogenated fat. [Primary crossover note](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#ground-soybean-crossover-alongside-levodopa-and-carbidopa) · [Rat fat comparisons](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#soybean-oil-compared-with-hydrogenated-fat-in-rat-movement-tests) |
| 0131 rest tremor, 0767 daytime sleepiness | A human essential tremor trial tested the same active drug in two formulations, one with soybean oil. Sedation and worsened tremor were pooled and six-hour registry and publication values differ. A patent used refined soybean oil as placebo and measured nocturnal sleep. Its daytime dysfunction endpoint belonged to a separate test. [Essential tremor results](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#essential-tremor-under-two-octanol-formulations) · [Sleep patent](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#refined-soybean-oil-placebo-in-an-exploratory-sleep-patent) |
| 0439 swallowing, 0702 drooling, 0828 voice volume | Native searches returned oral coating, saliva physicochemistry, mouthfeel, food formulation and parenteral nutrition. The focused sources did not measure these symptom outcomes with refined soybean oil in Parkinson. A soy-sensitive patient challenge concerned allergic reactions. [Screening routes and remaining gaps](open-trails.tsv) |
| 0478 dystonia, 0609 falls, 0688 freezing of gait, 0966 stride | The equal-fat rotenone comparison found more open-field stopping on soybean than on perilla. Stopping includes immobility during exploratory behavior. The oil was a diet comparator and no gait-episode, fall, stride or dystonia assessment was reported. An essential tremor trial mentioned transient gait instability under an unidentified 1-octanol formulation. [Comparator outcomes](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#assigned-soybean-oil-diet-in-a-rotenone-mouse-comparison) |
| 0160 apathy | The Tabriz paper reports a pooled non-hydrogenated plant oil category in the abstract. Soybean-brain studies assessed gene expression and endocrine endpoints. No clinical apathy estimate tied to refined soybean oil was located. [Tabriz lead](../canola-safflower/README.md#tabriz-approved-parkinson-cohort) |

## Rice bran oil

| Frozen claims | Evidence reviewed |
| --- | --- |
| 0073 speech intelligibility, 0245 facial expression, 0492 swallowing, 0866 voice volume, 0934 handwriting | A Parkinson trial assigned rice bran oil as a cannabis comparator and reports composite MDS-UPDRS. The oral oil grade, active carrier, arm-level adverse events and item estimates were unreported. The publicly stated IPD release is pending an accessible record. [Official registry note and snapshot](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#rice-bran-oil-placebo-and-missing-parkinson-item-data) |
| 0228 walking slowness, 0361 falls, 0476 rigidity, 0848 stride, 0861 hand dexterity | The haloperidol model with chamuangone-enriched oil found generally improved strides, climbing and catalepsy. The low-dose catalepsy mean increased at one time point. Samad’s solvent-extracted oil report found rotarod and chewing improvements with inconclusive pairwise open-field differences. A hexane rice bran extract study measured rat movement and grip. No preparation and endpoint combination resolves the human claim. [Enriched oil](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#chamuangone-enriched-rice-bran-oil-and-rat-motor-assays) · [Extracted oil](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#extracted-rice-bran-oil-and-haloperidol-motor-outcomes) |
| 0407 apathy, 0426 dystonia, 0678 olfaction | A depression trial reports some lower questionnaire scores with rice bran extract. Rat chewing or catalepsy is distinct from measured dystonia. A rat olfaction study uses butylphthalide and soybean oil. No verified rice bran oil apathy, dystonia or olfaction outcome was located. [Extract depression trial](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#rice-bran-extract-and-depression-scores) |
| 0801 constipation | A four-oil blend containing 26.02% rice bran oil improved stool measures in a loperamide mouse model. A defatted bran trial reports changes in Bristol stool form, and a fermented bran registry prespecifies bowel outcomes with results unpublished. The prior Parkinson bar study used multiple fiber ingredients and had an inconclusive constipation estimate. [Dated oil blend and defatted bran evidence](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#rice-bran-oil-blend-and-stool-outcomes) · [Fermented bran registry](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Rice-Bran-Oils.md#fermented-rice-bran-and-bowel-diary-registration) |
