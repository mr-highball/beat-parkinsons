# Thirteenth masked evidence review, November 16, 2026 UTC

All 24 claims remain Unknown. This checkpoint added 14 study assessments and filed 14 dated plain-text source extracts or registration summaries. Six statement rationales now identify the related evidence and its limits. Some studies reported measurable changes, but the tested preparation, clinical outcome, or participant group differed from the respective hypothesis.

- **0420 ground mustard and drooling.** In a five-person meal experiment mustard rice increased collected saliva relative to unspiced rice. The original available abstract leaves the ingredient preparation unresolved. A separate Parkinson cohort reported lower resting flow than controls. Missed swallowing and saliva escaping the mouth are different from secretion during a meal. Neither source estimates the effect of ground mustard on Parkinson drooling. The original mustard tables and overlap with the 1963 report remain open. [Mustard meal](../../../../research/1965-1969/Food-Experiment-Mustard-Meal-Saliva.md) · [Parkinson saliva comparison](../../../../research/1995-1999/Food-Experiment-Parkinson-Saliva-and-Rice-Bran-Injection.md)
- **0528 toasted sesame oil and attention.** The astaxanthin plus sesamin trial reported differences in processing and psychomotor speed. Its attention rows are inconclusive. The presscake extract trial measured memory and explicitly omitted attention. Several cannabinoid studies used sesame oil as the vehicle. [Attention table](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Oat-Thyme-Sesame-Endpoint-Comparisons.md#astaxanthin-and-sesamin-mixture-with-attention-measures) · [Presscake trial](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sesame-Cake-and-Sumac-Gall-Cognition.md#sesame-oil-cake-extract-and-memory-tests) · [Registry review](registry-screen-summary.md)
- **0501 rolled oats and voice.** The 2016 paper identifies the 2013 poster as the same patient. The 2020 paper documents an independently reported second adult with acute dysphonia after oat milk. Both concern allergic reactions and have no acoustic vocal volume measurement. [Report linkage](oat-case-identities-and-microarray-access.md) · [Independent case](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Oat-Voice-and-Fermented-Milk-Mobility.md#independent-2020-oat-milk-dysphonia-case)
- **0446 ground sumac and attention.** The Rhus javanica gall trial found lower ASRS scores and faster Tower of Hanoi completion, with inconclusive number-count results. The trial used medicinal galls rather than fruit of the culinary species. [Primary trial](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sesame-Cake-and-Sumac-Gall-Cognition.md#rhus-javanica-gall-extract-and-attention-tasks)
- **0526 kefir and falls.** The official sarcopenia trial tested a specified Yakult strain product. Walking and mobility outcomes were listed and no fall-count outcome was named. The prior kefir powder trial measured bone outcomes. [Trial record](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Oat-Voice-and-Fermented-Milk-Mobility.md#strain-assigned-fermented-milk-in-sarcopenia)

The earlier 0393 exact receipt incorrectly said walnut oil was absent. The corrected receipt distinguishes the mention from actual assigned or measured use.
