## New related assessment candidate 0420: mustard and whole-mouth secretion

Blumberger W, Glatzel H, *Wirkungen von Riech- und Schmeckstoffen der Nahrung auf die Speichelsekretion: Untersuchungen über Wirkungsdauer und Sekretmenge*, Nahrung **11** (1967), 767–776, DOI [10.1002/food.19670110727](https://doi.org/10.1002/food.19670110727). Original German abstract reproduced at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/247995765_Wirkungen_von_Riech-_und_Schmeckstoffen_der_Nahrung_auf_die_Speichelsekretion_Untersuchungen_uber_Wirkungsdauer_und_Sekretmenge . Five healthy volunteers gave multiple whole-saliva samples before and during a 14-minute meal. Unspiced rice produced about three times fasting secretion; mustard-added rice about four times fasting secretion. Authors report mustard raised secretion versus unspiced rice in each participant. These are approximate ratios relative to fasting, not directly measured between-arm percent changes, and no significance statistic is reported in the abstract. Mustard species, processing, dose, exact salivation collection values and repeat schedule not confirmed. It tests acute secretion during eating in five healthy people, not drooling or Parkinson disease. Abstract accessible; ResearchGate says no full text and direct Wiley DOI open failed. Proposed related row is conservative, without implying more saliva necessarily worsens drooling or contradicts the rat intervention causally. Frozen claim asserts reduction of drooling; no defensible sign determined.

1963 earlier same-author first paper PMID [14117840](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14117840/), journal 23:97–118, German title “Beiträge zur Physiologie der Gewürze und Würzstoffe. I. Gewürzwirkungen auf Speichelmenge und Speichelzusammensetzung.” Abstract not at PubMed; [indexed abstract](https://drive.eurekamag.com/research/013/996/013996052.php) reports human testing, mustard among condiments and transient secretion. [1977 official Federal Register](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/e1/Federal_Register_1977-08-26-_Vol_42_Iss_166_%28IA_sim_federal-register-find_1977-08-26_42_166%29.pdf), p.43094, states healthy young men ate test foods with mustard of unspecified source and short-term saliva rose; that is regulator's summary, not an independent trial. Same-author 1963 versus 1967 sample overlap unresolved. Existing 0420 rat arterial AITC preparation reported reduced carbachol-induced salivation; route, species and outcome differ.

## 0403: caraway tremor citation, not a verified experiment

Obeid et al., 2020, *Biochem Cell Arch* 20 suppl 2, 3933–3943, https://connectjournals.com/file_full_text/3219400H_3933-3943.pdf . The introduction attributes hand tremor with prolonged/excess caraway ingestion to Jazayeri, *Tongue of Plants*, 6th ed., Sepehr Press Tehran 1981, pp.279–281. The article's **own** 70:30 ethanol-water seed extract experiment tested PTU-induced hypothyroidism and thyroid hormones/tissue in rats, not tremor. The 2010 Dehghani study https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=4113f8bff0d661d01c52f72e68133b26a60b5637&repid=rep1&type=pdf repeats same sourced anecdotal claim; neither is an independent human case. Direct 1981 book pages, plant vernacular/species identity, preparation, cases and rest-state remain unverified. Do not turn this into measured clinical tremor evidence. Existing isolated carvone acute toxicity has rat body tremor but not clinical resting tremor; direction remains unknown.

## 0380: false match avoided

The 2017 Krithika primary toxicology study https://storage.googleapis.com/innctech/wjpps/article_issue/1483179612.pdf reports tremor/staggering in rats at 1600 mg/kg, **Hydrocotyle javanica whole-plant methanol extract**, not dill; Anethum is mentioned in a nearby reference. The species distinction prevents incorrect dill attribution. Original aqueous dill leaves seizure study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3957353/ tests PTZ seizure outcomes, not resting tremor. Existing assessments retain extract/seed/mixed-stroke limits.

## 0495 and 0526: falls must actually be counted

Existing 0495 osteoporosis education trial named cooked kale in calcium questionnaire, did not isolate roasted kale. Prospective older-women study PMID30153877 associates vegetable intake with first injurious-fall hospitalization, HR per 75 g serving 0.90 (95% CI 0.82–0.99), but not kale-specific, not recurrent falls or PD; current ledger already includes it for other foods. Existing 0526 kefir powder/calcium trial NCT02361372 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4675497/ measures bone density/markers, not falls; 40 completed of 69 recruited, per authors. Its hip BMD proxy cannot establish fall incidence. Separate cluster dietary intervention PMID34670754 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34670754/ reports milk, yogurt and cheese, falls HR 0.89 (0.78–0.98), but does not assign kefir; no kefir-specific or PD inference.

## 0478 and 0401: route and sedation distinction

Existing 0478 adult Leigh case used intravenous soybean-containing ketogenic emulsion for systemic respiratory/metabolic crisis and gives no post-treatment dystonia-specific score. Propofol case reports link dystonia to administered anesthetic in an intravenous lipid emulsion, not oral refined soybean oil; no product-specific causal result. Existing 0401 hazelnut-oil work reports pentobarbital sleep time in toxic liver-injured mice, not spontaneous sleep or insomnia. [DailyMed topical blend](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/getFile.cfm?setid=e3f5c322-e846-43b6-aa05-5777e6c14a70&type=pdf) lists hazelnut carrier and multiple aromatics applied to neck; no controlled outcome, oral toasted oil or Parkinson patient.
