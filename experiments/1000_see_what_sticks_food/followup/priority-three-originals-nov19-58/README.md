# Priority-three original sources and saffron outcomes

The Kaur and Gupta review is the shared source behind four pending saffron pairs. The official exact DOI deposit lists 205 references. It identifies independent original reports on [crocin and depressive-like behavior in mice, saffron and climbing in flies, and crocin and human motor totals](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Saffron-Review-Primary-Trails.md). The readable Inoue body distinguishes saffron mixed into feed from isolated crocetin and confirms that the outcome was climbing. The Tang and Soleymani abstracts identify a forced-swim proxy and aggregate MDS-UPDRS results, respectively. These different endpoints cannot supply an original rigidity, insomnia or fatigue estimate for ground saffron.

The 2024 Bej and Abdian bibliographies also establish source lineage for non-Parkinson depression studies and the [Ashtiani syrup study in multiple sclerosis](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2020-PMID32716906-Ashtiani-Saffron-Syrup-Fatigue.txt). The latter's reported baseline fatigue mean exceeds the untransformed scale maximum as described in the Methods. The Wood drug review and the Simon artificial membrane experiment remain available through their exact original author abstracts and official references. The twelve priority-three source pairs retain their full-body access gaps. [Source-specific routes and gaps](open-trails.tsv)

Three targeted PubMed queries returned 13 memberships, with the Isfahan report shared across the two saffron queries. The official ClinicalTrials.gov query returned one combined-ingredient study and no posted results. The [official Tehran IRCT record](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/IRCT20090117001556N145-Tehran-Saffron-Endpoints.txt) reports prospective registration and the two named primary scales. Linkage to its publication is preserved separately.
