# Archived article screening

The native follow-up tool linked recovered Europe PMC records to chronological plain-text articles using PMC IDs. The initial snapshot covered 6,557 claim–source pairs and 3,858 distinct PMC IDs. The first 94 date-and-pain pages raised coverage to 8,102 pairs and 4,961 PMC IDs. The complete date metadata overlay raised that to 8,502 pairs and 5,262 PMC IDs across the same 418 frozen claims. The ledger records the local file, text scope and ingredient, constituent and outcome flags for each pair. The bibliographic ledger identifies records with no corresponding local article. [Local text screen](claim-local-text-screen.tsv) · [Bibliographic screen](claim-query-screen.tsv) · [Date metadata recovery](date-metadata-screen.md)

The scanner starts at a recognized English article heading where possible and stops at the first recognized bibliography heading. It records when a boundary is absent. It matches specific date-fruit and fig terms in place of the isolated words `date` and `fig`. The match flags can still capture personal names, publication disclosures, generic background, or different parts of a large conference collection. Study design, preparation, and reported outcomes determine the dispositions. The added date-and-pain pages produced no new human-candidate pairs under the specified local text screen.

Three licensed source copies added four joined pairs, bringing the refreshed join to 8,506 pairs and 5,265 PMCIDs. The Zhang mouse chows contained coconut and soybean oils and did not measure fall frequency (`0151`, `0609`). The Keio survey measured drooling without a fig exposure (`0188`). The KIM report lists apathy at baseline and does not compare dried dates (`0758`). Its coconut-oil-related pairs appear in the independent sweep instead of this join. The review dispositioned all 819 unclassified pairs with both exposure and symptom flags in the archived text and at least one flag missing from the title and abstract.
