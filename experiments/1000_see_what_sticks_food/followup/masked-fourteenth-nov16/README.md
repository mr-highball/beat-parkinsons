## Evidence closest to the hypotheses

- **0406 brewed lemongrass and daytime sleepiness.** In a 50-person three-night comparison, the two tea conditions had 16 and 17 reports of feeling sleepy in the morning against 13 on placebo. The authors reported no significant paired difference. A separate uncontrolled morning group documented symptoms in five of 11 volunteers, and another morning-use group reported one participant with somnolence and dizziness. The recorded formulation matches brewed leaf tea. The volunteers were healthy and the outcomes do not establish clinical Parkinson daytime severity. The later four-case Parkinson essential-oil series used inhaled or topical exposure and the daytime-sleep item remained unchanged. [Primary table and timing](../../../../research/1985-1989/Food-Experiment-Lemongrass-Symptoms.md#morning-sleepiness-and-acute-somnolence-in-the-1986-paper) · [Later route and symptom comparison](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Lemongrass-Symptoms.md#parkinson-aroma-oil-case-series-and-distinct-questionnaire-items)
- **0515 boiled barley and orthostatic lightheadedness.** Pearled barley was in a mixed home dinner for spinal-cord injury participants, with no identified effect on maximum post-meal pressure decline across the home diets. The laboratory breakfast, which did not contain barley, reported a different estimate. No standing-lightheadedness outcome was assessed. [Original table](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Barley-Meal-and-Bean-Sleep.md#pearled-barley-in-a-low-glycemic-home-dinner)
- **0431 brewed lemongrass and urgency.** A mixed essential-oil cystitis report includes lemongrass and measures daily void frequency. That measure and oil combination leave a brewed-lemongrass urgency effect unresolved. [Primary report](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Oil-Blend-Urinary-and-Texture.md#mixed-essential-oil-capsules-and-urinary-frequency)
- **0439 soybean oil and dysphagia.** A pediatric capsule trial recorded some mild swallowing discomfort in a design with soybean oil in both arms. A fish-gel experiment separately found reduced mechanical hardness at higher soybean oil concentrations. Neither provides an oil-specific clinical Parkinson swallowing comparison. [Capsule diaries](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Soybean-Capsule-Swallowing.md) · [Fish gel experiment](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Oil-Blend-Urinary-and-Texture.md#soybean-oil-gradient-and-fish-gel-texture)
- **0535 and 0558 chicken or milk and facial signs.** A report of corticobasal degeneration records hypomimia before a later diet history that named milk and occasional chicken. No relation to preparation or timing of eating was observed. [Original case](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Facial-Observation-and-DHA-Dyskinesia.md#chicken-and-milk-in-a-corticobasal-case-history)
