## New related source 0515: barley-containing diet and post-meal blood pressure

Farrow et al., “The Effect of a Low-Glycemic Index Diet on Postprandial Hypotension in Individuals With Chronic Spinal Cord Injury: Results From a Pilot Study”, first published 2025-02-14, PMID 40008157, DOI 10.46292/sci24-00044, original https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11848139/ ; full HTML staged as PMC11848139.html. Randomized crossover, eleven chronic spinal-cord-injury participants; low- and high-glycemic diets matched for energy/macronutrients. Table 3: the low-GI at-home dinner was a mixed Southwest bowl containing pearled barley, beans, beef, vegetables, oil and other ingredients; barley cooking method and individual ingredient consumption are not stated. The comparator dinner was different mixed foods. Results, At-home setting: nine had both usable meal records, only 83 of 144 meals had valid BP, 13 low-GI and 17 high-GI recordings met postprandial hypotension criteria, no diet main effect for maximum systolic drop (p=.994) and no meal-by-diet interaction (p=.653). No result isolates the barley bowl or barley from other ingredients.

The often-quoted laboratory breakfast difference (maximum SBP fall 14 ± 12 vs 24 ± 25 mmHg, p=.056 abstract, .057 body, n=11) concerns a different breakfast **without barley**; never assign it to the barley dinner. The study measures postprandial BP, not symptoms on standing, and SCI is not PD. It is a conservative related source and cannot set direction for boiled barley/orthostatic lightheadedness. Existing Parkinson meal study PMID8984127 had no named barley or symptom and remains separate. First publication date 2025-02-14 is from original PMC citation metadata.

## Existing evidence and targeted follow-through

* 0559 raw fig/rest tremor: 2023 haloperidol-model fruit extract already assessed. Singh/Verma 2025 https://abap.co.in/index.php/home/article/download/2375/513, DOI 10.5530/ctbp.2025.4.42, mentions fruit and tremor in background but actually tests hydroalcoholic **bark phytosomes** in rats, with antioxidant enzymes as outcomes. Its discussion cites previous fruit catalepsy work, not a new observed tremor trial. No new whole-fruit tremor result established.
* 0431 brewed lemongrass/urgency: existing 2014/2018 paired tea reports and 1986 volunteer polyuria context retain the key differentiation of urine volume or void count from sudden urge. PMID25440110 repeats the 2014 study; no new independent trial inferred. Concentrated extract CKD trial already assessed.
* 0399 dried sweet basil/executive function: existing fresh-leaf-supplement aging mouse and seed extract are assessed. 2018 PMID29546690 https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29546690/ reports oral sweet-basil leaf extract in ischemic mice and water-maze/elevated-plus-maze memory; not dried culinary leaves, Parkinson or executive control. Human holy basil Ocimum tenuiflorum is a different species and must not be credited to O. basilicum.
* 0472 toasted Nigella seed/drooling: assessed 2025 chewed seeds measured pH, not flow; 2023 irradiated mouse oil measured stimulated saliva, and 2026 human oil-rinse volume may include retained rinse. None measures toasted seed and involuntary saliva loss.
* 0453 ground cardamom/orthostatic lightheadedness: prior blood pressure trials and allergy cases are catalogued. 2011 Al-Braik et al. https://smj.org.sa/content/smj/32/2/171.full.pdf reports possible herbal-adverse-reaction mixtures; table extraction interleaves columns and cardamom must not be casually linked to dizziness or low pressure without visually resolving case IDs, coexposures and posture. This route is not an assessment. Differentiating resting pressure from actual standing symptoms remains essential.
* 0530 cold-pressed canola/postural instability: existing UMIN000033447 gait-balance trial https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9213591/ uses canola as active comparator, not no-oil randomization; oil press grade unreported and balance index is not Parkinson pull-test. Other UMIN000046861 has exercise in all arms. Neither isolates the cold-pressed oil effect.
* 0428 toasted pumpkin seed oil/shortened stride: existing 2020 PD diet includes pumpkin seed oil within a menu and aggregate motor score; the 2026 extract rodent beam times are not measured stride length or toasted oil. No new isolated preparation/outcome study found.
