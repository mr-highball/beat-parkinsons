# Focused evidence for the last eight claims

- **0594 canned black beans and insomnia.** A general-population study of pooled beans reports a favorable association with self-rated sleep duration and quality. A different subanalysis measured insomnia with genetically proxied food preferences. Canned black beans and Parkinson insomnia were not isolated, and the spinal injury study's black and pinto beans in a mixed dinner address post-meal pressure. [Primary studies](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Barley-Meal-and-Bean-Sleep.md)
- **0558 pasteurized cow's milk and facial expressiveness.** The corticobasal case names unspecified milk in a diet history. Hypomimia had already been documented, with no milk-linked facial change. Earlier Parkinson fermented dairy evidence used a strain-specific product and composite motor outcome. [Case history](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Facial-Observation-and-DHA-Dyskinesia.md#chicken-and-milk-in-a-corticobasal-case-history)
- **0460 dried tarragon and executive function.** Published oral-extract rodent studies concern spatial learning and memory. The controlled metabolic tarragon trial did not identify a clinical executive comparator. A maze learning result does not measure executive dysfunction after dried culinary leaves. [Existing primary extract evidence](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Tarragon-Savory-Oregano.md#tarragon-extracts-in-chronically-restrained-rats)
- **0412 fresh shiso and dream enactment.** The available perilla leaf animal recording did not establish a clear REM-duration difference. REM time by itself supplies no count of motor enactment or loss of atonia. Recheck related oils and isolated aroma compounds if registrations report polysomnography or bed-partner outcomes. [Existing sleep-stage experiment](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Shiso-Followup.md#leaf-extract-and-sleep-stage-measures-in-rodents)
- **0509 ground allspice and dream enactment.** A 2011 rat study used isolated eugenol and carvone odor pulses while EEG-related field potentials and EMG staged sleep. Cortical odor responses varied by stage. No eugenol-specific motor enactment outcome was assessed. The prior deprivation study likewise measures other consequences of sleep loss. [Primary olfactory methods](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Eugenol-Odor-and-REM-Recording.md)
- **0456 ground Sichuan pepper and rigidity.** The 1960 Japanese pepper fraction caused a decrease in recorded tone in one comparison and convulsions after more severe exposure. The two findings refer to animal toxicology and use a crude fraction and different routes. No repeated clinical rigidity score was available from the earlier mixed daikenchuto Parkinson case. [Primary author abstract](../../../../research/1960-1964/Food-Experiment-Japanese-Pepper-Motor-Toxicology.md)
- **0555 refined safflower oil and hand dexterity.** Original diet studies altered several oils and DHA together, reporting rodent motor open-field or forelimb outcomes. A targeted search for pegboard or manual-dexterity outcomes found other interventions and unrelated uses of oil as a possible carrier. Retain patient hand-dexterity and verified refining grade as open.
- **0468 cold-pressed walnut oil and dyskinesia.** The walnut fly experiment tested climbing. The human isolated-DHA pilot offers a clinical dyskinesia context with different onset denominators in the preliminary conference report and official results. Peak severity did not separate in the original abstract. Neither DHA exposure nor the fly test supplies a culinary walnut oil severity comparison. [Conference and registry linkage](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Facial-Observation-and-DHA-Dyskinesia.md#dha-pilot-conference-results-and-registry-denominators)
