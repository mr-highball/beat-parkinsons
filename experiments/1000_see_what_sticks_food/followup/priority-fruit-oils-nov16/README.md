# Fruit, oil and spearmint source review, November 16, 2026 UTC

All 46 pinned priority-three pairs received full-body dispositions, 13 related and 33 excluded. The review added 20 dated factual texts, 12 study assessments, and a clarification to one existing assessment. Five claim rationales were refreshed. | Claim | Evidence judgment |
| --- | --- |
| [0140, dried spearmint and insomnia](../../statements/0140.txt) | Unknown at 50. The 2019 blend conference report found a preliminary 2.15-point ISI contrast. The final report found a smaller adjusted effect and inconclusive primary outcomes on the same trial family. Neither isolates dried spearmint. |
| [0144, toasted sesame oil and pain](../../statements/0144.txt) | Maybe True at 51. The oral postpartum studies retain their weak favorable signal. In dyspepsia, white sesame was given in both arms, with an inconclusive separate epigastric pain contrast. Oil processing was unspecified. |
| [0145, cold-pressed sunflower oil and anxiety](../../statements/0145.txt) | Unknown at 50. The waiting-list trial combined oil with positive suggestion, and relaxation was one element of a well-being composite. It gave no isolated anxiety or relaxation estimate. |
| [0181, dried cherry and fatigue worsening](../../statements/0181.txt) | Maybe False at 51. The longer concentrate study favored mental fatigue. The separate acute concentrate crossover adds an inconclusive comparison on a similar task outcome. Neither establishes transfer to dried fruit or Parkinson fatigue. |
| [0205, refined corn oil and depressive symptoms](../../statements/0205.txt) | Unknown at 50. The Parkinson study gives response proportions for fish oil versus corn oil. Multiple independent comparator trials likewise lack an oil-free arm. The oil grade and independent direction remain unresolved. |
| [0232, dried mango and depressive symptoms](../../statements/0232.txt) | Unknown at 50. A fresh-mango trial has a planned depression questionnaire and no posted results. An experiment offered dried mango among six snacks and measured aggregate intake and acute affect, with no separate exposure or depression estimate. |
| [0262, cold-pressed sesame oil and depressive symptoms](../../statements/0262.txt) | Maybe True at 51. The fatty-liver report describes lower depression scores with refined sesame against refined sunflower. The public abstract leaves the p-value contrast and instrument unresolved. The weak lean continues to depend on this indirect comparison. |

The two Carney cohorts each gave sertraline to both arms and used corn oil as an active comparator to a different omega-3 formulation. Several additional comparisons used corn-oil capsules in adolescent or adult depression. None isolates corn oil from background treatment or natural change. The accessible Pomponi Results preview counted 96 screened and all 24 randomized completing, with the exact depression outcome table still inaccessible.
