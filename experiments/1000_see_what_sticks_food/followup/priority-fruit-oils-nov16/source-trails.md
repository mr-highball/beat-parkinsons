## Corn oil

- Searches included exact Carney trial IDs, `"depression" "corn oil" "randomized" trial placebo EPA DHA major depressive disorder clinical`, and `"Pomponi" "DHA" "Parkinson" 2014 "NCT" depression`. Final JAMA and the manuscript differ in the earlier Carney raw Beck means. The 2019 table uses a separate cohort. Preserve the published final values and the difference, and do not assign the change within an oil arm to the oil. [Published 2009 report](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/184742) · [2009 author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3477788/) · [2019 original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6550340/)
- Gabbay lists 51 randomized in the paper and 16 in its named imaging registry. The flow says those enrolled for imaging were recruited from GCO 12-1321. The link does not resolve how the 16 map to the full reported cohort. The Lahore article names 70 participants and tabulates 61, with a within-arm p=.007 called insignificant and no direct group estimate. The OCEAN endpoint estimates come from the official results rather than the unavailable published body tables. [Gabbay report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6625364/) · [Gabbay registration](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00962598) · [Lahore report](https://www.ijmrhs.com/medical-research/role-of-omega3-polyunsaturated-fatty-acid-in-the-management-of-major-depressive-disorder.pdf) · [OCEAN results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02057406?tab=results)

## Spearmint, sunflower and sesame oil

- Searches for spearmint insomnia, dried leaf, sleep latency and the exact proprietary blend found the 2019 SLEEP presentation and the later registered Arizona trial. They report the same blend and schedule and overlapping investigators, with different sample counts and model estimates. They are treated as one study family. The older six-person pilot mentioned in the protocol still needs its original presentation. [Preliminary report](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article-abstract/42/Supplement_1/A160/5451737) · [Later full report](https://www.mdpi.com/1660-4601/18/6/3044) · [Original protocol](https://cdn.clinicaltrials.gov/large-docs/43/NCT03567343/Prot_000.pdf)
- Cold-pressed sunflower oil, anxiety scales and placebo terms returned several different oils, preparation grades and depression outcomes. The sunflower waiting-list study registered relaxation individually and analyzed it in a five-item composite. Its instruction and exposure cannot be disentangled. [Official registration](https://drks.de/search/en/trial/DRKS00028118) · [Original article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12144-022-04206-4)
- Atefi's original abstract describes 30 g/day sesame versus sunflower alongside calorie restriction, with 53 of 60 completing. The open companion papers identify both oils as refined. The mood paper's accessible abstract neither names the depression instrument nor identifies the test behind p=.013. The registered metabolic outcomes omit mood. The full manuscript remains gated. Zobeiri's original Table 3 gives epigastric pain decreases 0.4348 with cinnamon macerated in sesame and 0.6154 with sesame control (p=.69). Its registry gives bloating as the sole primary outcome and no confirmed oil grade.
