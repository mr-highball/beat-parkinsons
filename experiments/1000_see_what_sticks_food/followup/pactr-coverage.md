# Pan African Clinical Trials Registry search

The [public PACTR search](https://pactr.samrc.ac.za/Search_v2.aspx) was queried on November 12, 2026 through the provider’s basic search form. The native [FPC client](../tools/foodexperiment.pactr.lpr) reuses server form state, follows result pages and checks unique result links against the displayed total. Zero counts require the registry’s explicit no-results marker. The [query and result ledger](pactr-queries.tsv) retains all 16 expressions, including exact registration controls and a two-page dysphagia search. The [screening ledger](pactr-dispositions.tsv) covers every distinct trial returned.

The disease terms returned nine distinct records. Two trials had a named plant exposure in Parkinson’s. The Ghana *Mucuna pruriens* trial and the Sousse marjoram biomarker trial both appeared in the WHO ICTRP export. The primary registry still labels the Sousse trial Recruiting and gives no publication link. The journal paper names the registration, reports outcomes and gives different conduct dates. The earlier clinical symptom pilot is a separate study. [Ghana trial and reports](../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-ISRCTN-Followup.md#ghana-mucuna-trial-interim-report) · [Sousse registration](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#marjoram-tea-biomarker-trial-registration) · [Sousse results](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#marjoram-tea-and-oxidative-stress-biomarkers)

For the 2021 symptom pilot, searches used the DOI, PMID, PII, Sousse ethics identifier `CEFMS 29/2019`, named investigators, *Origanum majorana*, *marjolaine* and *Mardkouch*, with placebo and outcome-table terms. The publisher preview gives preparation and aggregate outcomes. The openly indexed material and linked citation records did not expose the arm-level tables or an individual symptom estimate. The direct PACTR result is the later biomarker study. A linked conference review describes the earlier trial at aggregate scale level. [Publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1353802021003072) · [Bibliographic record](https://openalex.org/W3197449008) · [Congress abstracts](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11925478/)

The scientific name `Laurus nobilis` retrieved two registrations for Sousse bay-leaf infusion in healthy volunteers. The 2026 article identifies the 2024 lipid report as the first component of the same project. Both reports analyze 55 completers with the same arm sizes, so count them as linked reports with possible participant overlap. The second registry’s outcome table lists inflammation and blood counts without naming the published mood scales. The bay-leaf controls establish searchable plant names and accessions. Their endpoints and brewed preparation do not address the frozen ground-bay claims. [Dated registrations and lipid report](../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#bay-leaf-infusion-registrations-and-lipid-outcomes) · [Dated mood report](../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Evidence.md#bay-leaf-infusion-and-stress-in-healthy-adults)

The dysphagia probe retrieved 14 records over two pages. Review found none enrolled a named Parkinson population. The registry also returned stroke and small-vessel disease trials in the Parkinson keyword results, including incidental disease mentions in record text. Reconciled counts describe the public search results on the recorded date. They leave differently indexed records and subsequent updates open.

The 2024 lipid paper identifies the withdrawn Research Square preprint as its earlier deposit. The preprint’s LDL receptor estimates closely match the later intervention arm. The 2026 paper also identifies the 2024 paper as part of the same project. Both registries list May 4–31, 2022 as the actual study dates and May 10, 2022 as ethics approval, with registration on May 11 and 13. The lipid paper gives 2019–2020 as its conduct period and the inflammation paper gives May 2022. The 2022 pre-post mood paper and the 2025 lipid conference abstract add reports with similar settings and participant counts. Count these as potentially overlapping records.
