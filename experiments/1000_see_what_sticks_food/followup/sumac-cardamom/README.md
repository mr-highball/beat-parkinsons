## Original evidence and discrepancies

- In a randomized depression trial in women without Parkinson’s, Beck scores improved by about 41% after sumac fruit powder and 37% after placebo, both with a calorie-restricted diet. The between-arm difference was not statistically significant. In an injected sumac extract mouse study, tail-suspension behavior changed and open-field locomotion did not. [2020 and 2023 reports](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md)
- The sumac extract dyspepsia trial found a better constipation subscore than omeprazole for both extract arms in the adjusted week-eight model only. The comparison was against an active drug and provides an opposite-direction gastrointestinal lead for 0210. The actual table gives p-values and directions without coefficients. A prior animal experiment reported reduced chemically induced diarrhea from crude extract, measuring a different outcome. [2025 results](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md#2025-sumac-aqueous-extract-and-constipation-scores-in-dyspepsia) · [2014 report](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md#2014-sumac-extract-and-experimental-diarrhea)
- A sumac hypertension trial's narrative describes drowsiness and its table reports weakness. Its insomnia entry is in placebo. Cardamom powder pressure trials yielded differing resting blood-pressure results across populations. None measured posture-linked symptoms. [Sumac sleep and pressure findings](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md#2016-sumac-powder-hypertension-and-sleep-adverse-events) · [Cardamom randomized results](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md#2020-and-2022-green-cardamom-powder-and-blood-pressure-in-type-2-diabetes)
- Hegelmaier’s Parkinson menu includes cardamom and reports aggregate motor improvement. Item-level speech, rigidity, postural stability and tremor data and a cardamom-only comparison are unavailable. Baert’s cardamom odor test had a null case-control contrast, while Jacob and Nagaraj reported differences under other methods and in other samples. [Parkinson menu and diagnostic tests](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md)
- Acute allergy reports include dysphagia, abdominal pain or presyncope after cardamom-containing foods. These outcomes describe a distinct mechanism from Parkinson motor swallowing or orthostatic lightheadedness. Two 2026 black cardamom extract reports measured attention and share one registration. [2023 case reports](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md#2023-cardamom-in-allergic-reactions-with-dysphagia-and-presyncope) · [2025 case and 2026 attention study](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Sumac-Cardamom.md)
