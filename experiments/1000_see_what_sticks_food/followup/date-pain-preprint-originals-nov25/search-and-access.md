# Original versions and remaining outcomes

The branch-local Oracle first pass located five provisional preprint access notes and the green-bean paper note. It returned no exact record for the ISRCTN COMBAT registration or the RECOVER journal article. The follow-up used each inherited DOI, exact title and known publisher or repository route. [Oracle batch log](../../../oracle-coverage/batches.tsv)

- **RECOVER**. The official NLM HTML reproduces the complete February 23, 2023 medRxiv version including methods, clinical endpoint tables and figures. Its citation DOI and PMID differ from those of the 2024 PLOS paper. They share the same cohort, so the archive indexes each version without double-counting the study. [Original preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9980238/) · [Final paper](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0282451)
- **ME/CFS and Parkinson stimulation**. The publisher search exposed the complete searchable version 1 PDF and its original outcome tables for each preprint. Searches for date-fruit terms and direct checks of study design, participant and intervention fields found no fruit exposure. [ME/CFS PDF](https://www.preprints.org/frontend/manuscript/88aa093c80f43dd8f7dafc9eb6bb4f80/download_pub) · [Stimulation PDF](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202404.0032/v1/download)
- **Cannabidiol review and equine fractures**. The publisher's searchable full cannabinoid review includes separate Parkinson and pain sections. The first-posted Authorea manuscript includes clinical and imaging observations for all three equine cases. Its date, title and complete author group matched the preprint DOI, with the journal paper dated separately. [Cannabidiol review](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202512.1733) · [Equine manuscript](https://doi.org/10.22541/au.171579005.59559989/v1)
- **Cranberry**. The official ISRCTN registered outcome fields and API status for the separate cranberry-beverage crossover added no new depression comparisons. Previous COMBAT reports together with the protocol establish screened baseline PHQ-9 and a planned follow-up. Searches by trial name and identifiers returned no post-treatment estimate by randomized arm. [COMBAT register](https://www.isrctn.com/pdf/ISRCTN76069316) · [Separate beverage crossover](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05008549)
- **Green beans**. The cohort's questionnaire-validation item for string beans and the pooled vegetable fatigue incidence are separate reported results. Supplemental Table 2 is the component analysis cited for total vegetables, and Supplemental Table 3 concerns vegetable-group and frailty associations. The archive link did not return the attachment. Preserve the original pending pair until the supplement is inspected. [Cohort article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7727483/)
