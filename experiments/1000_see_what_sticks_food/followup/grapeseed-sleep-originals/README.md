# Grapeseed oil sleep outcomes and access

The November 15 original-source pass for claim 0441 traced inhalation, ambient exposure, topical massage and oral food. The first three assessed studies used different routes and measured polysomnography or global or composite sleep quality. None measured dream-enactment frequency in Parkinson disease. [2016 insomnia abstract and 2019 Bae dissertation](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Sleep-Context.md) · [2022 massage trial](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Grapeseed-Sleep-Context.md)

The 2016 author abstract reported no clear grapeseed-versus-untreated difference on the listed measures and gives no arm tables. A contemporary Korean guideline reports the trial size, but its numerical contrasts are for lavender. The Bae thesis printed inconsistent placebo-change figures, and its adjusted comparison of lavender plus carrier versus carrier was inconclusive. Its single pleasant-dream anecdote came from the lavender arm. These results leave the effect of any consumed oil open.

Three distinct ClinicalTrials.gov records were retrieved again, with no posted results at the time of review. NCT02841618 specifies grapeseed oil cookies and a secondary correlation with an unspecified sleeping questionnaire. Its dissertation and 2022 article report lipid measures, with pilot counts that differ between the registry and appendix. NCT04677920 names a global PSQI correlation. NCT04677946 plans insomnia and sleep measures without identifying the oil. Oil refining and dream-enactment outcomes remain unverified. The 2022 paper's supplementary-table action returned HTTP 403. Two attempted PMC supplemental paths returned HTTP 404. Table 3 measures periodic limb movements and the discussion describes individual dream and shouting observations without event counts. The pending material identity is retained. An earlier Nanjing nonrandomized 32-person Parkinson study, online February 23, 2023, measured aggregate motor and nonmotor scores. The original methods did not verify lubricant composition.
