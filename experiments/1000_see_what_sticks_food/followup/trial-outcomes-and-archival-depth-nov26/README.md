## Trial and preparation evidence

The EXPRESS outcome abstract identifies the rice-protein and whey-protein comparison with shared exercise. The original report does not state the standing component or rice variety. The black-rice supplement trial reports walking time, and the dewaxed whole-rice comparison reports cognitive outcomes. These distinct studies provide no isolated posture effect for cooked brown rice in Parkinson disease.

The matched hemodialysis results verify the analyzed arms but do not disclose a HADS result. The pumpkin-oil adjunct abstract gives a qualitative pain-domain comparison and overall response counts, with the numerical pain table and cold-press designation still open. The Sichuan-pepper paper verifies the ordering of its animal groups. Its short record leaves the direct processing contrast and botanical identity unresolved.

## Historical context

The 1813 tea article describes comparison of green and Bohea tea leaves and attributes aging customs to its observers. The 1994 Harveian oration lists nutmeg and lemon balm in historical pharmacy. Both originals are indexed and have cultural topic links, with symptom effects left ungraded.
