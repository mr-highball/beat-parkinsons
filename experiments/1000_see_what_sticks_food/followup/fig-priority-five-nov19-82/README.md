The 2016 human fig-paste trial randomized 80 young adults with functional constipation and excluded neurological disorders. Compared with placebo, the assigned paste shortened transit more and favored stool form and abdominal discomfort. The weekly bowel-movement and abdominal-pain comparisons remained inconclusive. The paper confirms the identity of the 80-participant registration; the 40-participant protocol is a separate lead with no posted results. These are distinct measurements and preparations from the dried fig and Parkinson visual-frequency claim, which remains Unknown.
