## Article identity and citations

The publisher's 2015 dairy reproduction guide covers reporting design, feed details and adverse events in cattle studies. These are methodological examples. PubMed's related PMC link resolves to a separate 2016 conference proceedings record. The reviewed PDF matches the original PMID, DOI and title. Four earlier exact decisions bring the source denominator to 51. [Correct publisher PDF](https://www.journalofdairyscience.org/article/S0022030215006657/pdf) · [Earlier identity check](../masked-twenty-ninth-nov16/original-source-notes.md)

The veterinary chapter is a secondary source with different preparations and study purposes. This pass records nine contexts, including veterinary uses of anise and cardamom, local topical spearmint, and clove-oil anesthesia in fish. A sunflower reference led to the 2002 original dementia trial. An agitation response of at least 30 percent was reported in 21 of 35 recipients of topical lemon balm and 5 of 36 sunflower comparators. The comparison measured topical preparations and an agitation scale in severe dementia. Oral sunflower oil and Parkinson depressive and anxiety symptoms remain outside those data. [Original chapter](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7151902/) · [Dated trial text](../../../../research/2000-2004/Food-Experiment-Sunflower-Aromatherapy-Comparators.md)

## Conference originals and study families

The original 2024 and 2025 abstract books were reviewed by searchable full-PDF extraction with abstract boundaries. Individual graphics were outside the text extraction. The intake joins each disposition to its exact claim and original provider key and checks that no already imported exact pair is repeated. [2024 book](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11688754/) · [2025 book](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12755232/)

- **Canola comparison.** The 2024 ketone-ester pilot's safety and functional abstracts reuse one cohort. Its preprint describes measured fatigability, Trails A, grip and tension-anxiety comparisons using a canola-containing drink for the control. The results were inconclusive, with no independently assigned oil-free comparator. The 2025 version also reports gait measures. [Preprint estimates](../../../../research/2020-2024/Food-Experiment-Canola-Sleep-Preprint.md#fatigability-attention-and-anxiety-in-the-same-canola-comparator-preprint) · [Original exploratory journal report](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Canola-Fatigue.md#walking-distance-in-the-canola-comparator-pilot)
- **Blueberry parent and nested report.** GSA abstract 2436 concerns freeze-dried powder, mood and stool microbiota, with 18 stool contributors from 22 completers. The 2026 microbiome publication identifies the nested study NCT05817383 under parent NCT05735587. The parent reports adverse events elsewhere. Stool microbiome outcomes neither measure nor duplicate those adverse-event counts. [Conference and nested original reports](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Blueberry-Gap-Replay.md)
- **Fermented black soybean.** Abstract 4106 gives functional measures for 116 randomized older adults. A likely associated registry lists 141 participants and a brief title about algae while assigning fermented versus nonfermented black soybean and adlay. The linked identifier and enrollment split require confirmation. This preparation is distinct from common black beans. [Original abstract and official registration](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Black-Soybean-Adlay-Frailty.md)
- **Coffee at breakfast.** Coffee and tea are components of a redesigned breakfast routine. The project tracked engagement and independence in four nursing homes, with fatigue and a separate coffee effect outside the reported comparisons.
