## Sunflower oil and depressive symptoms

Three Europe PMC expressions yielded 64 ranked appearances across three
pages. The ClinicalTrials.gov expression yielded 28 records over three pages.
The screens retain indexed abstracts, authenticated bodies and trial protocols
at their recorded depths. The oral comparator results outside Parkinson
include different processing grades, mixed oils, co-interventions and
population limits. Animal vehicle studies include injected sunflower oil.
The only Parkinson registration in this result set used sunflower oil as an
excipient in both ginger groups. [Literature expressions](focused-queries.tsv) ·
[Literature dispositions](focused-results.tsv) · [Registry query](registry-queries.tsv) ·
[Registry dispositions](registry-dispositions.tsv)

Two Parkinson depression trials used fish oil compared with mineral oil or
corn oil. In the mineral-oil pilot, MADRS and CGI favored fish oil, with an
inconclusive Beck comparison and a two-sided MADRS responder comparison
of p=.075. In the corn-oil trial, the author abstract reports Hamilton response
in 75% with DHA plus EPA and 25% with corn oil. The accessible extract lacks
exact analyzed denominators. A separate cross-sectional preprint measured
circulating fatty acids and a Parkinson depression item. Its adjusted
linoleic-acid analysis supplies no clear affective association. None measures
isolated refined sunflower intake and Parkinson depressive severity.

## Oracle lookup

Catalog revision 27 held local trial texts whose ACTRN, ISRCTN or IRCT IDs
required separate text searches. In 11 warm passes, the six text-search
responses totaled 4,188 bytes at a 7.33 ms median. One exact request took
0.81 ms median and returned 993 bytes, with five archived registrations and
one empty match. The empty match led to a citation, and the official
registration was added to the catalog. The timed samples measure local tool
responses, with the citation follow-up outside the timed batch.
