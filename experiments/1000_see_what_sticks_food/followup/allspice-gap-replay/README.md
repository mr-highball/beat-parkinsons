# Ground allspice endpoint screening

Native and institutional searches ran November 15, 2026 UTC. This packet addresses the five ground-allspice statements without an assessment when the search opened. The freeze and the durable assessment table were checked independently.

| Claim | Outcome | Findings in this pass |
|---|---|---|
| 0238 | Reduced visual hallucination frequency | No primary result measuring hallucination frequency after oral ground allspice. Acute confusion in the clove oil case and seizure or general CNS measures do not measure hallucinations. |
| 0509 | Reduced dream enactment frequency | The existing Khan rat paper tested post-REM-deprivation behavior with pharmaceutical eugenol exposures. The other sedative and righting-reflex experiments measured induced or acute CNS depression. The endpoints do not count dream enactment episodes. One low-fit assessment is proposed. |
| 0720 | Worsened attention impairment | Three independent human eugenol odor studies were verified. Instructions to attend altered olfactory EEG signals in two. A separate manual Stroop study yielded a null eugenol-versus-air comparison. A rat task used named allspice as an odor cue and assigned the pharmacological manipulations to other compounds. Three low-fit assessments are proposed. |
| 0787 | Worsened speech intelligibility impairment | The recovered original clove oil plus alcohol case documented transient inability to speak in acute intoxication with depressed consciousness. It used a different species, formulation, population and symptom construct. One low-fit assessment is proposed. |
| 0802 | Reduced small handwriting severity | No eligible original measured writing or micrographia after the target preparation. Rotarod, startle, tonic flexion, reaction time and gross movement observations do not resolve writing outcomes. |

All five directions remain Unknown. 0238 and 0802 retain zero study assessments for this pass. No related outcome was treated as evidence against ground allspice.

## Local prior coverage

- Ground allspice means the dried culinary fruit of *Pimenta dioica*, including authoritative synonyms and regional names in the exact plans. The other eight frozen ground-allspice claims already have assessment rows. Existing allspice/cumin and paprika/allspice reports include the leaf extraction cardiovascular experiments, 2002 fruit aqueous suspension, fruit essential-oil chemistry, human allergy precedents, eugenol and levodopa-lesion studies, eugenol induced-sedation and forced-REM-deprivation models, and 2025–2026 paraquat behavioral papers. Current five claims were explicitly searched using their own symptom terms.
- Local existing searches were read from `followup/allspice-cumin` and `followup/paprika-allspice-outcomes`. The older ClinicalTrials.gov trial NCT03926442 measured endpoints after a spice blend. Its allspice component cannot be isolated. Leaf, fruit, essential oil, insoluble residue, medicinal isolate, another *Pimenta* species, clove and mixed spice preparations were screened separately.
- The 1995 UCR thesis on leaf fractions has a public repository original and complete institutional search text. Its later article uses nearly identical procedures and may report the same experiments. The thesis had a 24-rat oral arm with three recorded features and separate injected neurologic screening. No duplicate cohort weight was assigned. Provider accession dates are distinct from thesis date and national bibliography coverage.
- The 2015 ACAAI program lists poster P114 and shared authors with the 2022 article, supporting a possible reporting lineage. The overlap and original patient preparation, symptoms, timing, and component testing remain unverified. The 2022 article has no public index abstract. No new direct speech or swallowing data were assigned.
