# Raspberry official trial registry and source follow-through

On November 16, 2026, ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 reported and returned 85 records for the raspberry intervention expression, with total count enabled, page size 100 and no next-page token. Native FPC replay verified 85 unique NCT identifiers and reproduced the structured screen byte for byte. Four records are related contexts, 11 are excluded, one is pending and 69 retain protocol context for follow-through. Eight include a results section in the captured metadata. The protocol-context category preserves named interventions and outcomes and needs further primary-publication review where applicable. [Exact official query](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies?query.intr=raspberry&countTotal=true&pageSize=100) · [Per-registration ledger](raspberry-registry-screen.tsv) · [Chronological accessions](raspberry-registry-accessions.tsv)

NCT03140280 first posted in 2017 and reported result data in November 2022. The 52-week adverse-event module names confusion, depression and insomnia, each affecting one of the 18 participants in the uncontrolled dietary group. The June 2022 paper explicitly names the same registration. Its 15-person paired analysis reports no adverse events over three months. The publisher's two-page supplement shows anthocyanin and blood chemistry results for those 15 and supplies no event tabulation. The windows, denominators and participant overlap prevent reconciliation of the apparent reporting discrepancy. The reports do not show that raspberry caused an event or measure an anxiety contrast. Both report views are preserved for claim 0493 follow-through. The original Hatcher P01-033 conference report supplies a six-month juice-concentrate comparison in postmenopausal women and measured bone outcomes. A prior thesis of uncertain publication history still needs its original text. Neither a posted result from the co-ingredient seed-oil trial nor a raspberry-specific effect from the diet that allowed raspberry in a permitted-food list was identified in the registry capture. A separate postpartum anxiety registration leaves raspberry consumption unidentified. The source review did not locate a result that estimates frozen raspberry and Parkinson anxiety.
