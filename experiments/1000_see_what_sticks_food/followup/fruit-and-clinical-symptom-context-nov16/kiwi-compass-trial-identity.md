# Kiwi COMPASS protocol identity follow-through

The official ANZCTR record ACTRN12621001685808, UTN U1111-1268-2664, was retrieved November 16, 2026. First registration and last update are December 9, 2021. It lists 42 as the target and keeps the recruitment field as not yet recruiting. There is no recorded actual enrollment, linked results document or identified results publication. The registry morning COMPASS test is a planned secondary outcome. The New Zealand health ethics report, page 19, confirms the title, investigator and November 24, 2021 approval under 2021 EXP 11089. Study conduct and outcomes remain unverified.
