# Macadamia and hazelnut oil follow-up

Reviewed November 13, 2026. The frozen statements specify oral intake. All ten macadamia claims in this pass specify cold-pressed oil. Hazelnut statements distinguish cold-pressed and toasted oil. Original preparations, participant groups and measured outcomes determined the fit of each result.

## Search coverage

Native Free Pascal retrieved all reported pages across three disease-wide Europe PMC searches, three disease-wide PubMed searches and 40 symptom-specific Europe PMC searches. The focused queries pair the oil with the outcome or a close synonym, and pair the ingredient family or constituent with a disease or model term and that outcome. No focused macadamia record was returned. One focused hazelnut record reported food chemistry in blended oils. Disease-wide Europe PMC records covered a Huntington lipidomic analysis and an experimental pancreatitis treatment. PubMed covered the Huntington analysis and a hazelnut paper reviewing neurodegeneration. ClinicalTrials.gov returned 58 of 58 records, including the existing macadamia-containing healthy transit and military comparator studies and a topical lotion for uremic itch. Europe PMC returned 311 reference and citation edges from 16 endpoint routes at eight seeds. Manual screening reached relevant case reports and related food products outside the citation router’s lexical matches. Indexed provider results do not cover all dissertations, conference papers, or Japanese trials.

## Primary source recovery

- A University of Limerick DSpace API search for `Oonagh Markey` resolved three items. The exact thesis item has handle `10344/4174` and UUID `526f562b-c821-4753-bb5c-f3ab45f6da9a`. The `ORIGINAL` bundle returned `Markey_2011_transit.pdf`. The source PDF has a live repository download cover sheet and a locally recorded SHA-256 for the bytes retrieved. The record dates first online posting to November 17, 2014, separate from original thesis submission in November 2011 and the current platform's 2025 accession. Chapter 6 was recovered and verified against tables and study arms. [Primary research note](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Macadamia-Hazelnut-Followup.md#markey-dissertation-results-for-the-macadamia-oil-arm) · [Institutional API](https://researchrepository.ul.ie/server/api/core/items/526f562b-c821-4753-bb5c-f3ab45f6da9a) · [Official trial record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01510223)
- Macadamia meal and oral kernels are compatible with adverse airway symptoms in allergy reports. The 1999 primary meeting record gives an 18-year-old and a 30 mm raw kernel skin-prick wheal. The 2015 case gives severe hoarseness and a 20 mm macadamia skin-prick wheal. The 2024 ice cream case gives excessive drooling and shock. The preparations differ across the records and neither skin contact with oil nor consumption of whole kernel foods establishes the effect of the frozen oral oil exposure. [1999 primary meeting abstract 556](https://www.academia.edu/21894579/555_Identification_and_characterization_of_IgE_binding_epitopes_of_patatin_a_major_food_allergen_of_potato) · [2015 primary meeting abstract P60](https://doi.org/10.1111/imj.12869) · [2024 clinical case](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11192167/)

A botanical oil research hit with Parkinson-model motor outcomes used marula, a different species. ## Remaining primary trails

- Obtain the complete Ikeguchi Pharmacometrics methods and primary 2024 meeting P06 results, and monitor the UMIN dose and vigilance records. Search study-era supplier or author documentation for the pressing method. The confirmed human results measured daytime sleepiness and fatigue, while the newer trial plans psychomotor vigilance. Dream enactment and manual dexterity remain unmeasured. The 2025 Japanese Pharmaceutical Society program identifies poster 28P-am364. Its original result abstract is an additional lead. [Primary trial registry](https://center6.umin.ac.jp/cgi-open-bin/ctr_e/ctr_view.cgi?recptno=R000063079) · [2024 meeting metadata](https://jglobal.jst.go.jp/en/detail?JGLOBAL_ID=202502246801217301) · [2025 meeting program](https://forum.nacos.com/145nenkai/pharm145_program.pdf)
- Seek the 2011 Markey and Shafat conference body for the first public results. The investigators’ record dates the abstract to September, while the proceedings cover the annual meeting on October 1–5 and are dated November. Crossref identified the separate author index, but native HTTPS returned 403 from the publisher and 404 from the former host for the index PDF. The abstract page remains unverified. A later product index names a cold-pressed liquid from the thesis’s supplier, while the thesis gives no product code or date-specific label. The Korean patent’s worked examples name canola, soybean and two palm formulations. Macadamia and hazelnut are proposed ingredient alternatives, with no measurements for either. ## Named Japanese oil preparations and vigilance registry

The original Japanese patent JP2026034397A names Nisshin in its three-person test and Summit Oil Mill in its 16-person test. The latter closely matches the earlier university trial design and is kept as probable overlap. Dated supplier records describe pressing without temperature or a clinical lot. UMIN000059952 still has no posted results and lists alertness rather than hand dexterity. The 2014 topical comparator had a separate oral glucose load, with no oil ingestion or Parkinson measurement. The primary 2024 P06 and 2025 28P-am364 result abstracts remain open.
