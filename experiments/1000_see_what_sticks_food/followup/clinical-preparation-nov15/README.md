## Clinical outcomes and preparation

- **0264 and 0281, raw celery stalk and walking or rest tremor.** One Parkinson lifestyle report recommends mixed vegetable juice and records an individual walking testimonial without verified celery intake or timed gait. Zhou's pharmaceutical butylphthalide trial assigned 131 people and followed 103 to the final visit. Gait is one of several elements in the non-tremor subtotal. The published tremor numbers duplicate the entire quality-of-life row, and the designated subtotal combines rest with action or postural tremor. A separate mouse report found lower scored rest tremor after an oral whole-plant solvent extract. - **0573, cold-pressed peanut oil and fatigue.** In the follow-up of a randomized vitamin D trial, fatigue was present in 63/73 participants receiving oil alone at baseline and 28/73 at each follow-up. Both arms received identical oil. Their comparison evaluates adding vitamin D, and the individual outcome is fatigue presence. Pressing information and a Parkinson cohort are unavailable. - **0777, ground cardamom and depressive symptoms.** The Crohn trial reports a Hamilton depression measure while both arms contain cardamom seed. The randomized difference tests added wormwood. The older-adult blend paper still supplies only a significant within-arm finding in its accessible abstract. Its original table remains an access lead. The previously documented generalized anxiety trial still has internally conflicting public wording, and the Parkinson extract registry remains without posted results.
