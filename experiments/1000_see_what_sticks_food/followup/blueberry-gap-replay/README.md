In B02, a primary case record PMID29799310 has blueberries in a fixed diet when a patient with progressive apraxia and corticobasal degeneration was mute. Preparation and onset of exposure are unknown and no idiopathic PD or causal comparison exists. PMC13439031, PMC13254489 and PMC12755232 gave wrapper pages, not abstract text; PMC5149585 and PMC2464721 gave CAPTCHA. Other conference records and citation leads still require presentation-level context. Do not interpret wrapper-only or CAPTCHA results as negative searches.

The 2020 Koyuncu voice trial's readable primary report measures patient-reported VHI-10, with per-item numbers but no explicit food list or blueberries, and no objective loudness or listener-scored intelligibility outcome. It is not a blueberry trial. The 2015 bilberry-enriched rat model PMID26273789 reported more blood-derived phagocyte influx after toxin injury, with no blood-brain-barrier leakage difference. Bilberry, blueberry, extract, isolated anthocyanins and a whole diet cannot be treated as equivalent; this preclinical biomarker result is not a human symptom direction. Further manual screening of B01/B02, source citations, registry supplement material and diet records remains open.
