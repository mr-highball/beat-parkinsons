# Inclusive tremor research and cultural records

This checkpoint expands collection to traditional practice, ceremonial and faith-healing accounts, local knowledge and public personal experiences. These are active source leads with explicit provenance and lower causal weight than controlled intervention studies. Longstanding use is investigated; missing modern studies do not disprove it. Source authenticity, historical attribution and treatment efficacy remain separate questions. [Record policy](../../../../research/Cultural-and-Experiential.md)

Every one of the 33 existing periods has a separate cultural category and index link. Six initial cultural records preserve an 1896 historical-source candidate, an Indian practitioner text, Tanzania qualitative healing accounts, two contrasting public Mucuna experiences and the context of an already cataloged patient survey. The qualitative interview and survey contexts reuse existing accessions rather than adding cohorts. A separate 2000 clinical abstract supplies an original clinical access lead, with the complete report still open. These records do not change frozen claims or clinical grades.

The browser now defaults to all frozen findings and open questions. Rest-tremor lookup can browse 210 ingredient questions: 26 have recorded assessments and the later-round questions remain unreviewed. Source leads outside this vocabulary are also available. The split tabs distinguish Studies & analyses from Culture & experience, with All research as the inclusive default. Colors and custom glyphs distinguish collections; they are not efficacy grades. [UI behavior and build](../../../tools/parkinsons-oracle/README.md#browser-interface)

The source-kind pager filters the selected collection before filling a displayed page and continues through nonmatching candidate batches. Counts for a filtered page do not pretend to be an exact whole-catalog total. Cultural retrieval uses required collection metadata and validates the source path. Older uncurated material remains in the all-source view. Client sequence checks retain only the current request's results; source-reader navigation preserves the originating view.

First Oracle outcomes for three distinct cultural-source questions were one hit and two partials at revision 68. These questions differ from the earlier no-archive source batches, so their rates are not a comparable trend. The pre-Oracle local-search ordering exception in the Mucuna packet is retained. Repeated IDs, web follow-through and the known managed-process binding failure add no evidence denominators. [Coverage ledger](../../../oracle-coverage/README.md)

Open trails include original historical passages and editions, unverified diagnoses and preparations in personal reports, primary tables and study overlap, Chinese practitioner attribution, ritual-specific outcomes and source-quality issues in a small prayer comparison. Staged leads require source review and chronological accession before entering the canon. The existing 1,000-claim investigation and later-round sequencing remain intact.

Native validation checks all 33 category links and six records' collection metadata, date buckets, source fields, reported direction, authenticity, efficacy limits and reused accessions. Fourteen shared presentation fixtures run natively and as pas2js-generated JavaScript; they cover mixed source kinds, empty and nonmatching batches, partially consumed pages, Windows paths and query construction.
