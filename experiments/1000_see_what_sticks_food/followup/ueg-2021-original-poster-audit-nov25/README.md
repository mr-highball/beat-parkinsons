# UEG 2021 original poster audit

Riga Stradiņš University preserves the 938-page original issue. The complete poster section spans printed pages 262–907. The audit checked the 631 numbered body pages, 11 section dividers and four blank pages that follow the publisher's pagination. It screened 1,081 distinct numbered member headings and inspected the ingredient and Parkinson context candidates.

**Twenty-five Round 1 pairs were resolved** at full-text depth. Ten supply related preparation or clinical context and 15 are outside scope. All 25 scorecards passed revalidation. The round has 75 directional findings and 925 open claims. The replay leaves 31,671 exact checks across 21,376 source keys.

The oats, green tea and onion records include repeated reports of the same cohorts. The 203 remaining pairs from the seven-source batch stay open. The previously staged 39 UEG 2025 pairs also stay open. A candidate University of Vinnytsia repository attachment returned a missing-file response and the organizers' flipbook returned an access response.
