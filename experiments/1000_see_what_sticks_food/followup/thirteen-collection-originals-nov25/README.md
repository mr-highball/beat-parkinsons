# Thirteen original bodies and follow-up study families

The exact source review resolved 148 claim–source pairs across 13 verified bodies. Seventy-five identify related foods, named compounds, preparations, ingredient uses or professional guidance. Seventy-three identify context mismatches such as author names, technical labels, imagery, wrong species, or calendar chronology. Conference and compound results stay with their own reports.

- The 2014 phytochemical review cited a favorable rat grape-powder result. The recovered original is the same study withdrawn in 2025. The record preserves the original and the notice and assigns zero efficacy weight to that family. Three related animal studies use different experimental populations and processed powder.
- The published 2017 grape fruit extract trial retains a minimal easing signal for raw-grape claim 0860. Its healthy older adult sample and extract limit the transfer, and the registry first posted after recorded primary completion. The 2024 trial of the same branded extract reported cognitive measurements. The active college grape-powder trial had no posted results.
- The Eilat cinnamon abstract and linked original report the same extract and fly-climbing experiment. A separate Eilat abstract reports more mouse wakefulness with isolated EGCG, with less total sleep. These measures leave the frozen ground-cinnamon and brewed-tea symptom questions distinct.
- Distinct records for a sesame constituent formulation and a combined oregano and cranberry extract add preparation and attribution context.

All 83 affected scorecards retained their published verdict and anchor. One existing family was enriched with its primary source and 11 families were added across affected claim ledgers. The exact queue holds 33,738 pairs across 21,708 sources. Eight priority-three checks and 449 mapped original-access holds remain.
