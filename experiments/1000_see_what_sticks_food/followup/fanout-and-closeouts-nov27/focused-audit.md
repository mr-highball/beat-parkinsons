## 0391 · Refined soybean oil and daily Parkinson OFF time

The exact queue is empty for this claim. The investigator thesis summary and the indexed original Table 2 identify ground soy alongside levodopa/carbidopa. In seven people with Parkinson wearing off, the median acute ON period was 270 minutes with soy and 150 minutes with medicine alone (p=.028). The tests do not isolate refined soybean oil or a daily OFF-time endpoint. The full publisher body, exact preparation beyond ground soy, and later patient-level phase measurements were unavailable. The publisher index independently confirms the ground-soy comparison and does not add the missing measurements.

The two live PubMed searches examined the full available abstracts for six Parkinson or levodopa matches and two OFF-term homographs. The six include preclinical cinnamon formulations, an oil reference diet, an olfaction vehicle, and an aging-diet study. ClinicalTrials.gov exact count queries for Parkinson and soybean oil, soy oil or Glycine max oil returned zero each. Existing cultural leads and public forum searches added no attributable daily OFF comparison. The verified aging-diet original contrasts mixed oil diets in mice, with soybean oil in both conditions. Completion of the research checks retains the Unknown verdict and the publication-access limits.

## 0285 · Fresh tarragon and Parkinson pain

Focused PubMed Parkinson and tarragon query returned zero. The broader tarragon and pain query supplied 245 unique PubMed IDs. All titles were screened and all 240 available abstracts were scanned for the named plant, variants and extract identifiers. Five did not provide abstract text. An isolated aliased constituent, two reviews and studies of other Artemisia species are kept separate. The Intravit original lists an unspecified Artemisia ingredient in a combined product and measures retinal outcomes, without pain data.

The five missing abstracts are retained at their actual depth. PMID 32067232 identifies dermatitis cases involving named other Artemisia species. PMID 23620953 concerns topical argy wormwood oil or massage, with the original outcomes and botanical confirmation unavailable. PMID 16249521 is antimalarial history at available metadata depth. PMID 12029020 is a drug-test interpretation case with an introductory publisher extract. PMID 6780271 concerns an Artemisia decoction for gallstone disease, with exact species and full results unverified.

The 2010 mouse neuropathy original was already archived. Its Table 3 reports partially normalized evoked sensory thresholds after oral PMI-5011. The table labels its latency column with an inverse-second unit, so its unit is retained as an inconsistency. The two relevant IRCT topical registrations remain recruitment-complete with no visible outcome values. The published knee OA trial has an immediate topical effect and uncertain effects after stopping, with protocol discrepancies. Neither animal extract nor topical trial supplies a fresh oral tarragon Parkinson pain result.

The 2025 Cayoose Creek Band chapter adds a named report of customary leaf use for toothache and tea for menstrual discomfort. The book's copyright date is used for filing. The age of the underlying account is unresolved. The direction and partial review status remain in place, with original trial results still missing.
