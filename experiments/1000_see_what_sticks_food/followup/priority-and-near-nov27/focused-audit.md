## 0338 · Cold-pressed grapeseed oil and Parkinson pain

Three count-complete PubMed searches used named oil variants with Parkinson or levodopa, pain or nociception, and oral use plus pain. The counts were 0, 3 and 0 respectively. The three records were all reconciled to their author abstracts. PMID 31548590 uses the oil as a carrier in a topical eucalyptus animal formulation. The other reports test a bone scaffold or a topical azithromycin preparation. Neither author abstract reports an isolated oral grapeseed oil or Parkinson pain outcome.

ClinicalTrials.gov returned zero for Parkinson with grapeseed oil as the intervention term. A broader pain and oil query returned three records, all checked. NCT07630571 remains completed with no posted results. It uses grapeseed oil with C60 in an induced oral-discomfort experiment and has a discrepancy between its single-arm wording and structured parallel design. NCT06294769 and NCT04927741 describe unrelated topical comparator arms. Both are completed with no results posted. Keep all unknown outcome and oil-processing fields.

The archived Parkinson's UK discussion concerns topical massage. The 2008 herbal book describes a carrier; its separate raisin-and-gin arthritis custom is a different exposure. Current exact source coverage is zero outstanding. The targeted clinical and cultural checks are recorded as completed. The open scorecard and Unknown verdict remain.

## 0561 · Cooked green beans and fatigue
