# Strawberry and micrographia evidence trail

The frozen hypothesis 0349 specifies raw strawberry intake and less severe small handwriting in Parkinson disease. Renoudet, Costa-Mallen and Hopkins report that a neurologist observed apparent resolution of micrographia six months after one person added foods rich in fisetin and hexacosanol. The published abstract gives no food quantities, raw preparation, comparative diet period or handwriting score. Publisher PDF and XML and the legacy publisher PDF returned HTTP 403. [Frozen hypothesis](../statements/0349.txt) · [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22846082/) · [Publisher record and references](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1089/jmf.2012.0060)

The citation check inspected 27 references and 15 distinct citing records across three indexes. The 2014 review identifies strawberry and wheat germ as constituent sources. Public commentary later attributes strawberries and whole grains to the intervention. Its stated serving amount remains a secondary lead because the primary case report has not been recovered. [Europe PMC citation index](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/MED/22846082/citations?format=json) · [OpenAlex citation index](https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=cites:W2160048888) · [Dated citation comparison](../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#strawberry-and-handwriting-citation-check) · [Secondary commentary and its sources](https://nutritionfacts.org/video/coffee-put-to-the-test-for-treating-parkinsons-disease/)

The Europe PMC search returned 40 records for the title and abstract combination of fisetin or hexacosanol with Parkinson or micrographia. The primary case above was the human report measuring that symptom among the returned records. ClinicalTrials.gov returned zero records in two searches pairing Parkinson with fisetin or hexacosanol as interventions. These searches do not address unregistered studies, cohorts that pool fruits, or records indexed under other terms. [Europe PMC query](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=TITLE_ABS%3A%28fisetin%20OR%20hexacosanol%29%20AND%20TITLE_ABS%3A%28Parkinson%2A%20OR%20micrographia%29&format=json) · [Fisetin trial query](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies?query.cond=Parkinson&query.intr=fisetin&countTotal=true) · [Hexacosanol trial query](https://clinicaltrials.gov/api/v2/studies?query.cond=Parkinson&query.intr=hexacosanol&countTotal=true)

The Bonn fiber pilot recommended strawberries alongside other foods. Its intervention tracked apple intake and fiber and used a motor examination that did not score handwriting. The toxin-model studies of isolated fisetin differ in model, route and endpoints, with a null result for nigral cells and striatal dopamine in one 6-OHDA experiment. A 2026 rotenone mouse study of strawberry leaf extract reported better performance on some motor tasks. Fruit preparation and human handwriting remain unresolved.
