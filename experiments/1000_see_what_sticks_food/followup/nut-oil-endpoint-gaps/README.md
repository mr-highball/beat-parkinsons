# Hazelnut and walnut oil symptom follow-up

All seven repeat Europe PMC expressions reached their reported totals with 17 appearances. Three hazelnut expressions returned zero. Complementary methods and registry searches identified an oral hazelnut oil trial with urine collection and a bedside walnut oil comparator. The linked papers pooled urine volume across sampling periods and exposures, which limits urgency inference. The bedside crossover reported a nominal nocturnal bathroom item contrast without a multiple-item adjustment or stand-alone daytime sleepiness result. The route and batch grade remain open.
