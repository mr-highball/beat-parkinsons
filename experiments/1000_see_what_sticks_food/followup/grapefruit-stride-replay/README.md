# Raw grapefruit and shortened stride · 0853

A February 2005 primary abstract measured about 1.7 times the cabergoline concentration with grapefruit juice in five people with Parkinson disease. A November 2005 journal figure shows cabergoline concentrations with and without seven days of juice coadministration. Participant overlap across those reports is unverified. Neither gives measured walking change or raw fruit consumption. Original Japanese 2004 page S172 and Movement Disorders 2005 page S123 remain body-pending. The 2025 tendon experiment directly measured mice's stride using CatWalk, with locally applied grapefruit-derived vesicles in a tendon injury model.
