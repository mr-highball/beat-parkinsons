| Claim | Evidence and judgment |
| --- | --- |
| [0878, raw orange and constipation worsening](../../statements/0878.txt) | Moved from Unknown to Maybe False at 51. Older adults who reported consuming oranges had lower unadjusted Rome III constipation prevalence, 31/120 versus 9/19, p=.054. One pomace trial found more frequent stools. A later pomace crossover and a pasteurized-juice diary were inconclusive. The survey did not establish raw preparation, and the studies give no raw-orange Parkinson severity comparison. |
| [0883, fresh sage and fatigue worsening](../../statements/0883.txt) | Maybe False at 51. A fresh-tip extract favored less menopausal fatigue. The dry-extract comparison was inconclusive. An essential-oil comparison in healthy adults used a different sage species and acute task fatigue. |
| [0897, extra-virgin olive oil and insomnia worsening](../../statements/0897.txt) | Unknown at 50. A topical extra-virgin olive oil thesis gives within-arm broad sleep-quality changes. Oral comparator trials use unspecified or refined oil. No reviewed result isolated oral extra-virgin oil in Parkinson insomnia. |
| [0899, cold-pressed flaxseed oil and rigidity reduction](../../statements/0899.txt) | Unknown at 50. Two flaxseed-derived omega-3 and vitamin E trials reported aggregate Parkinson outcomes. The first public registration did not list UPDRS, and a post-publication revision changed the dose, clinic and endpoints. No separate rigidity or isolated cold-pressed oil estimate was reported. |
| [0904, brewed black tea and resting tremor reduction](../../statements/0904.txt) | Maybe True at 51. The caffeine trial reports a favorable but inconclusive resting-tremor comparison at weeks three and six. Tremor worsening in other caffeine and coffee users and the difference in caffeine dose remain important. |
| [0923, fresh spearmint and depression worsening](../../statements/0923.txt) | Unknown at 50. Spearmint extract doses gave mixed results and the blended extract comparison spanned zero. Peppermint extract reduced immobility in a mouse model with concurrent locomotor changes, leaving the clinical effect of fresh spearmint unresolved. |
