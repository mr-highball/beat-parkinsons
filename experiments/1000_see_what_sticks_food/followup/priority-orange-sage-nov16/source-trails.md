# Additional search coverage and open trails

## Orange

The published Brazilian survey's Table 3 gives the comparison by orange consumption. Its prose describes 74.1% in a way that obscures the denominator. The table shows that 89 of 120 consumers were classified as nonconstipated. There was no adjusted estimate and the questionnaire only identifies the item as orange. The 2019 orange-juice study used one group in sequential periods, so its unchanged baseline-to-day-60 frequency is inconclusive. [Original Brazilian survey, Table 3](https://www.e-publicacoes.uerj.br/ceres/article/download/2125/2113/11181) · [2019 diary and local factual text](../../../../research/2015-2019/Food-Experiment-Food-Followup.md#pasteurized-orange-juice-and-bowel-diary-outcomes)

On November 16 the official PubMed ESearch returned 35/35 for `(orange[Title/Abstract] OR "Citrus sinensis"[Title/Abstract]) AND (constipation[Title/Abstract] OR laxation[Title/Abstract] OR "bowel frequency"[Title/Abstract] OR "intestinal transit"[Title/Abstract])`. Indexed titles and abstracts were screened. It returned zero for `(orange[Title/Abstract] OR oranges[Title/Abstract]) AND Parkinson*[Title/Abstract] AND (constipation[Title/Abstract] OR bowel[Title/Abstract])` and for `(laranja[Title/Abstract] OR naranja[Title/Abstract]) AND (constipacao[Title/Abstract] OR constipação[Title/Abstract] OR estreñimiento[Title/Abstract] OR estreñimiento[Other Term])`. A separate Portuguese web search for `"laranja" "constipação" consumo frutas idosos estudo` led to the Brazilian paper. Spanish and Portuguese phrases included `"naranja" fruta estreñimiento ensayo clínico evacuaciones` and `"laranja" "in natura" constipação estudo ensaio`. The web provider does not supply a full denominator.

ClinicalTrials.gov API searches `orange constipation`, `citrus constipation` and `orange bowel movement` returned 137, 4 and 83 appearances, respectively. All returned pages were obtained (100+37, 4 and 83) and screened for orange and citrus terms in registry fields. Shared orange juice in a yacon study and orange-pomace trials gave no separate raw-orange comparison. The exact Brazilian questionnaire, underlying data, and raw-orange comparisons in people with Parkinson disease remain open. [Registry for juice and pomace](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03749031) · [Yacon comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25692980/)

## Sage and tea

The original Menosan article reports a secondary POMS fatigue comparison with p=.029. The printed scores do not clearly identify arm and assessment time. Its 280 mg thick extract is equivalent to 3400 mg tincture. The public page for the article's EudraCT identifier returned “Trial requested was not found.” In the other sage trial, Figure 1 documents 26 initial participants, 22 at first testing and 17 analyzed. That count matches the official registry. The combination used common sage aqueous extract and Spanish sage essential oil. [Menosan primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7881233/) · [EudraCT route](https://www.clinicaltrialsregister.eu/ctr-search/trial/2016-005033-77/DE) · [Cycling trial and participant flow](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnut.2021.771518) · [Cycling registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04804657)

The Postuma 2012 publisher PDF Table 2 reports resting-tremor week-three and week-six comparisons of −0.32 (95% interval −0.73 to 0.10) and −0.28 (−0.83 to 0.27). Action tremor appears in a separate row. The supplement route returned a browser challenge. The 1987 oral caffeine and coffee perception record remains at author-abstract depth. A tea-specific effect on resting tremor remains open. [Postuma primary report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3414662/) · [Older caffeine abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3796831/)

## Olive oil, flaxseed oil and spearmint

The chronic insomnia trial used olive oil as placebo without grade or amount, and the Insomnia Severity Index categorized baseline severity. Its efficacy outcomes used other sleep measurements. The refined-oil comparison used three 1-g capsules per day as placebo in healthy adults. The topical extra-virgin olive oil thesis had 81 participants versus 86 planned. Table 4-7's separate within-arm PSQI coefficients cannot establish a treatment contrast. The OPERA association estimates are self-reported diet items and broad sleep-quality ratings. Oral extra-virgin olive oil and Parkinson insomnia remain open. [Insomnia comparator trial](https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6882-10-29) · [Refined-oil trial](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13010248) · [OPERA paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12051364) · [Topical thesis factual text](../../../../research/2020-2024/Source-Text/2024-Pan-Topical-EVOO-Sleep-Thesis.txt)

The first IRCT version for Taghizadeh specified 1800 mg omega-3 plus 200 IU vitamin E and no UPDRS. The 2019 record lists 1000 mg plus 400 IU and adds UPDRS. The accepted 2017 manuscript's dose agrees with the later record. Its digitized tables do not supply item-level rigidity. The adjacent rigidity and bradykinesia passage in PMID33374784 concerns niacin. The independent Tamtaji trial reports Part III totals, with no rigidity item. Protocol and item data, manufacturer and pressing method remain open. [First official version](https://en.irct.ir/trial/6103?revision=6103) · [2019 official version](https://en.irct.ir/trial/6103?revision=103566) · [Earlier accepted manuscript](https://magistralbr.caldic.com/storage/product-files/1634072890.pdf) · [Separate trial](https://magistralbr.caldic.com/storage/product-files/1339864240.pdf)

Herrlinger Table 4 gives placebo 1.80 to 2.10, 600 mg 1.30 to 2.11, and 900 mg 3.83 to 2.32 for depression-dejection. The overall comparison was inconclusive (p=.0862). The Methods identify dried, water-extracted spearmint aerial parts from Kemin Foods without a trademark. The study excluded recent depression and Parkinson disease. In the separate blended extract study, the original adjusted POMS contrast was −0.22 (95% CI −1.1 to 0.66). Its registry results report within-arm changes of +0.29 and +0.74. The 2024 peppermint mouse result uses a different species, oral ethanol extract and a movement-dependent assay. No study here gives a clinical estimate for fresh spearmint in Parkinson depressive symptoms. [Herrlinger article](https://doi.org/10.1089/acm.2016.0379) · [Blend paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8000032/) · [Blend registry results](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03567343?tab=results) · [Peppermint report](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0302102)
