The dysmenorrhea publication trail surfaced two uncontrolled date-containing beverage cohorts and a separate premenstrual symptom paper from the Barhi date feeding project. The premenstrual paper’s accessible results address timing and hormone correlations. Its conclusion about cramps supplies no between-arm severity estimate. The dated entries cross-link the earlier conference and nutrient reports and retain the differences between the primary reports and a later review.
