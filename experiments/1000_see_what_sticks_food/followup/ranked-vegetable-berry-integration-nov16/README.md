# Ranked original-source review and food study assessments

Sixty exact claim–source pairs across 20 disjoint source groups reached the full body of the original. The reports include measured and planned outcomes for anxiety, insomnia, falls, apathy, dystonia and tremor. The full text resolves many search matches generated by publisher names, country names, device names, trial wording or keywords in figure references. The food preparation and measured endpoint were checked for every pair. [Original reviews](dispositions.tsv) · [Source access](ranked-source-access.tsv) · [Native verification](verification.txt)

The seven study assessments integrate separately recovered primary and registry context. The Himalayan raspberry series measures blood pressure with asymptomatic low readings and changes in treatment. The egg articles report gait by diagnosis and dysarthria during a different exposure. Capsinoid and EGCG studies report metabolic or animal outcomes. They leave the frozen symptom directions open. The official registry links the protocol and gives no fielded results. Parkinson disease, cold pressing and an ingredient-specific effect remain unestablished.
