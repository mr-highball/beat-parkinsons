# P27 ClinicalTrials.gov screen

Screened 196 distinct registry accessions: 167 excluded as non-matches, 26 related/indirect records retained, and 3 pending because the receipt lacks intervention/arm detail. The 196 inventory rows reconcile one-to-one with 196 official structured study-detail rows and 196 lexical-flag rows. The lexical matcher reports 33 paired arm-plus-measure candidates; all were reviewed against the actual intervention, arm, outcome, population, and summary fields and assigned a rationale in `registry-screen.tsv`.

Each row retains first-posted and last-posted dates, current registered status, study type, and the `has_results` flag. 11 records carry a posted-results flag in the inventory; this task screened the registry record and records that flag, but did not independently extract ResultsSection outcome tables. Three entries (NCT05898438, NCT07817836, NCT07707297) lack intervention/arm detail in the supplied structured extract and are left pending.

Priority review: NCT06437860 and NCT06605898 register computerized attention measures in MIND-diet arms that include EVOO as one component (not an isolated refined-oil intervention); NCT07741773 registers EVOO phenolic supplement versus placebo with general cognitive function (not attention or speech); NCT07756658 tests tyrosol plus creatine in healthy adults, with exercise outcomes primary and no oil intervention. NCT04787497 is high-phenolic EVOO in multiple sclerosis with a broad cognitive battery. Thyme/parsley hits are indirect: thyme honey mouth-rinse registrations measure xerostomia/salivary outcomes (NCT05247008, NCT05859711), thyme essential-oil mouthwash measures gingivitis (NCT07905456), and parsley appears as a background ingredient in an acute mushroom meal study with finger-tapping outcomes (NCT05594329). Attention-task studies using olive oil are predominantly placebo/comparator arms to fish-oil or astaxanthin trials (e.g., NCT01075932, NCT01075919, NCT03781557, NCT07876193). Actual refined olive-oil feeding exposures were identified in NCT04419948 (platelet/glycemic endpoints) and NCT05771571 (antioxidant/LDL endpoints), neither with attention/speech measures. Intravenous refined-oil emulsions (NCT00672854, NCT03201588) are route/population mismatches. No registration establishes a compatible Parkinson attention/speech endpoint for the mapped food exposure.

Source boundary: local receipts `receipts/registry-inventory.tsv`, `registry-study-details.tsv`, and `registry-lexical-flags.tsv` contain the official ClinicalTrials.gov retrieval fields. Dispositions are registration-scope screening, not results analysis or outcome estimates. No tracked files were changed.
