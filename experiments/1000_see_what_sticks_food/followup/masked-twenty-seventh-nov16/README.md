## Relevant comparisons

- **0969, refined olive oil and attention.** The PREDIMED-Plus observational analysis pooled refined and olive-pomace categories. The basic top-tertile association with lower attention attenuated after adjustment. The fully adjusted estimate per 10 g/day was −0.006 z-score units, with a confidence interval from −0.047 to +0.035. The earlier randomized 1-back imaging comparison also reported an inconclusive group-by-time contrast. Behavioral trials using olive oil as a common vehicle or comparator give no oil-only estimate. [Cohort and adjustment](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Marjoram-Olive-Outcomes.md#common-olive-oil-intake-and-longitudinal-attention-scores) · [Randomized product and comparator studies](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Olive-Oil-Attention.md)
- **0945, ground cardamom and daytime sleepiness.** The acute and 14-day black-cardamom extract papers share CTRI/2024/09/074472. The papers measure cognitive-task performance and have no daytime sleepiness severity scale. Their product differs from culinary green cardamom. [Original article and registration reconciliation](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Cardamom-Attention-Trials.md)
- **0887, fresh thyme and attention.** One juvenile rat study found higher Y-maze spontaneous alternation after isolated thymol. The assay concerns spatial working memory and exploration. A separate 2022 model reports attention-related behavior in its abstract, with the numerical tables and correction open. [Dated primary figure](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Thyme-Attention-Models.md) · [Provider notes](herb-provider-review.md)
- **0959, ground paprika and insomnia.** A human insomnia crossover identifies paprika oleoresin as a colorant in a cannabinol preparation. The matched comparison varied cannabinol. The result has no separate paprika estimate and gives no culinary preparation or Parkinson subgroup. [Original trial formulation](../../../../research/2025-2029/Food-Experiment-Paprika-Gap-Replay.md#paprika-oleoresin-as-a-colorant-in-a-clinical-sleep-trial)

- **0815, ground bay leaf and attention.** The earlier Japanese report found a 30-minute vigilance advantage at the lower odor load ((p=.026)), with the clearest late interval at 20–30 minutes ((p=.030)). The higher odor load did not show a comparable accuracy difference. The later experiment used the same team and protocol features, and possible participant overlap remains open. Both comparisons used inhaled odor. [Earlier Japanese original](../../../../research/2010-2014/Food-Experiment-Bay-Leaf-Followup.md#earlier-japanese-bay-leaf-odor-vigilance-experiment)
- **0909, ground paprika and dyskinesia.** The injected-capsaicin animal abstract reports fewer abnormal involuntary movements and supplies no detailed scoring or verified levodopa regimen. A separate experiment found reduced spontaneous movement and reduced drug-elicited activity, raising a competing explanation. Neither measured Parkinson dyskinesia after oral ground paprika.
