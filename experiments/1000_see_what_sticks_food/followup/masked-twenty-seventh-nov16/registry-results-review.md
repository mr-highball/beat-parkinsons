# Posted ClinicalTrials.gov result follow-through

The review retrieved the original structured result sections for all 11 registrations carrying a results flag. The source-specific notes record which outcomes were posted and identify shared vehicles, dietary bundles and incomplete reporting. [Protocol screen](registry-screen.tsv) · [Trial inventory](registry-inventory.tsv)

- **NCT06460181**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06460181?tab=results; registered 2024-06-14, results 2026-05-26. Healthy recreationally active women 18–39 received 12 mg astaxanthin in olive oil (n=13) or olive-oil placebo softgel (n=12 analyzed) for four weeks. Both formulations contain oil; amount and refining status unspecified. Psychomotor vigilance mean±SD 402.7±49.9 to 426.4±42.4 ms active vs 400.0±51.7 to 406.6±83.1 placebo; task switching 937.2±198.6 to 760.9±136.4 vs 948.0±214.7 to 830.6±354.9; incongruent flanker 567.4±98.6 to 512.1±77.7 vs 570.3±107.8 to 545.4±87.9. These are arm summaries, not a no-oil comparison; no causal oil effect or PD transfer follows.
- **NCT03345550**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03345550?tab=results. Mild traumatic brain injury; oral fish oil versus visually matched olive-oil placebo, 3 months; trial terminated. Registry cognitive-impairment composite lists Brief Test of Attention, Trails, Stroop and other tests but marks that outcome NOT_POSTED. Other outcome results are available; do not represent missing cognitive results as null or apply them to PD.
- **NCT03472664**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03472664?tab=results. Adults with mild cognitive impairment receive a modified Mediterranean ketogenic diet with supplied extra-virgin olive oil vs low-fat diet for 16 weeks. Cognitive composite (mPACC5) includes timed executive processing: baseline mean±SD 0.02±0.71 (n=33) vs -0.02±0.58 (n=37), week 16 0.06±0.80 (n=33) vs 0.04±0.63 (n=36). ADAS-Cog 12, a broad measure including attention, baseline 11.85±5.45 (n=34) vs 13.03±4.78 (n=38), week 16 11.97±6.50 (n=33) vs 12.03±4.97 (n=38); higher indicates more impairment. This compares whole diets and different populations and cannot isolate oil, an attention-specific component or refined preparation.
- **NCT03689530**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03689530?tab=results. Prediabetes peer-support versus enhanced care; reported 12-month change in frequency of meals cooked with olive oil median 0.00 (IQR -4.30 to 7.50; n=132) vs 0.00 (-6.60 to 8.60; n=139), p=0.3341 mixed model. Oil frequency is itself the outcome; no PD attention, gait, dexterity or voice outcome.
- **NCT01813110**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01813110?tab=results. Oral olive-oil capsules as placebo versus prescription omega-3 before experimental endotoxin challenge; posted outcomes CRP, TNF-alpha and IL-6. No listed PD attention or motor/voice endpoint and no untreated oil comparison.
- **NCT02092649**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02092649?tab=results. Healthy young adults, oral omega-3 vs olive-oil placebo capsules, 12 weeks; reported endpoints resting metabolism, VO2, fat/carbohydrate oxidation, variability and triglycerides. No PD or target endpoint. Linked publication PMID 28212390 is a report of the same intervention, not an independent oil arm.
- **NCT01692340**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01692340?tab=results. Healthy volunteers consumed labeled tomato carotenoids mixed with olive oil on an English muffin. Outcomes measure carotenoid kinetics and metabolites; no independent oil comparator or target PD symptom. Registry links PMID 26674763 and 26561629.
- **NCT00672854**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00672854?tab=results. Hospital patients requiring parenteral nutrition received IV olive-oil-based ClinOleic or soybean-based Intralipid. Results include infections, ICU stay, inflammatory markers and mortality, not target symptoms. IV emulsions cannot stand as culinary oils; linked PMID 22488002 concerns same trial.
- **NCT02391701**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02391701?tab=results. Atlantic whole-diet intervention provides olive oil among multiple foods; family control receives no intervention. Reported results cover cholesterol, anthropometry and glucose, not PD attention, gait, dexterity or voice; oil cannot be isolated. Linked PMID 38324314 and 27539113 are diet-program reports.
- **NCT01445886**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01445886?tab=results. Indigo naturalis in olive oil applied topically to nail folds compared with calcipotriol for psoriasis; results concern nail psoriasis and global assessments, not eating oil, PD or target endpoints; PMID 25738921 is linked.
- **NCT03207711**, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03207711?tab=results. Type 2 diabetes diet education in both arms encourages olive oil and other foods; mindfulness differs between arms. Posted measures include craving, impulsivity, glycemic and dietary outcomes. Attention to cravings is mindfulness training, not a measured PD attention impairment, and no randomized olive-oil contrast exists. Linked PMID 31467583 and 30545813 listed in protocol.

No inference that unlisted outcomes were impossible to measure elsewhere: this receipt describes accessible registry tables and registered arms. For two attention-adjacent registries, the exact within-arm statistics above should not be mistaken for treatment estimates or independent evidence that olive oil caused changes.
