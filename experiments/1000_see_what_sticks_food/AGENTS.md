# Food thought experiment rules

## Generation

- Follow the native Free Pascal and work-record guidance in [Athena](../tools/vendor/athena/README.md#standards-map). Use Pascal for generation, scanning, evidence ledgers, and validation. Shell scripts may build and run the Pascal tools.
- Define the generation vocabulary without opening the research archive or looking up outcomes. The generator must use only the experiment inputs. When an independent blind vocabulary pass is needed, delegate it to The Brain with only the experiment inputs.
- Generate exactly 1,000 hypotheses. Use 250 each for foods, herbs, spices, and edible oils. Each category must contain 125 reducing and 125 worsening claims.
- Give each claim one ingredient, one compatible ordinary culinary preparation, and one Parkinson’s symptom or symptom outcome. Use each ingredient and symptom pair once. Use fixed-width IDs and the same UTF-8 text format for every statement. Treat the statements as intentionally arbitrary research hypotheses.
- Verify every generated file, then freeze the ordered claim fields and record the seed, input hashes, and claim hash. Evidence review starts only after the freeze. Keep the original directions and wording during review.

## Evidence assessment

- Start each post-freeze question with the project's [oracle-first workflow](../oracle-coverage/README.md). Record the first local outcome, then continue every required catalog and external evidence check. Use the tracked gaps to prioritize additions to the appropriate research period.
- Start cultural and forum source searches with the [MCP catalog lookup](../tools/parkinsons-oracle/README.md#cultural-and-conversational-lookup). Use `source_kind: "cultural"` or `source_kind: "conversations"` and relevant exposure or symptom terms. Refresh the index after publication and verify the dated paths through the appropriate category filter.

- Scan the entire local research catalog after the freeze. Log file coverage, unavailable full text, and any read or extraction failures. Search both directions and deduplicate reports of the same study.
- Evaluate human Parkinson’s evidence against the named ingredient, preparation, and symptom. Record related preparations, isolated constituents, combined interventions, other endpoints, and nonhuman studies as indirect leads.
- In exact source-context reviews, retain genuine ingredient, derived-product, constituent, sensory-stimulus and mixed-meal studies as `related` when preparation, population, route or symptom differs. State the mismatch and preserve separate member boundaries. A different outcome alone does not remove a genuine food lead. Use `excluded` for verified contextual mismatches such as author names, places, brands, calendar dates, image colors or technical labels rather than a studied food exposure.
- Resolve botanical identity before transferring common names. A differently named preparation of the actual ingredient can be related; a homonymous but unrelated plant does not establish exposure to the frozen ingredient. Keep proposed mechanisms and untested constituents explicitly marked as such.
- Keep source eligibility separate from clinical direction. Neither `related` nor `excluded` establishes that a frozen claim is true or false. A source-wide ingredient mention and an unrelated member's symptom result do not form an exposure–outcome comparison. The clinical assessment ledger accepts its own established roles; preserve source exclusions in the exact-context and provenance records.
- Start with at least two targeted external searches for each locally Unknown or Maybe claim, then broaden them as needed. Share a query only when it covers each mapped claim. Record its date, text, results, screening depth, and provider or access limits. Save new relevant sources or original summaries in the appropriate local research period before citing them.
- Use `oracle_evidence_rubric` and the [shared evidence scorecard](EVIDENCE-SCORECARD.md) for every new or reassessed verdict. A defensible minimal sign qualifies for Maybe at the 50.005 anchor. Record who observed it, the independent source families, the five fits, contrary evidence, the selected tier and the symptom effect. Scores represent ordinal evidence tiers. Preserve measured effect sizes separately.
- Complete the versioned scorecard and family ledgers before publishing a new or reassessed verdict. Run `oracle_check_scorecard` on the draft before handoff or publication. Verify the committed scorecard and its verdict through the claim-specific MCP lookup. Preserve prior scores until each claim is reassessed individually.
- Judge the sign against the frozen wording. If evidence leans toward easing and the frozen claim says worsening, use Maybe False for a weak lean. Explain the observed finding, the limits of transfer from the source to the frozen preparation, population and outcome, and the strongest competing evidence. Weigh credible indirect models, measured constituents and firsthand accounts at their actual evidential strength. Missing research, shared ingredients across arms, or a null result by itself contributes no directional evidence.
- Record a concise rationale and resolvable local evidence paths for every directional verdict. Record the search and scope limits when a verdict remains Unknown. Preserve findings that conflict with the chosen verdict.

## Follow-up evidence review

- Include cultural, traditional, ceremonial and public experiential claims under the [separate chronological record policy](../../research/Cultural-and-Experiential.md). These are lower-weight efficacy leads, with provenance, contrary accounts and diagnostic uncertainty retained. Do not equate a historical tremor term with confirmed Parkinson disease or turn a documented practice into a verified outcome.
- Collect relevant public Reddit and other forum threads as conversational evidence. Use the opening post year or an explicit first documented capture date, and file summaries under the corresponding period's Cultural-and-Experiential/Conversations folder. Follow the [cultural record policy](../../research/Cultural-and-Experiential.md) for the flexible fields. Attribute firsthand reports, caregiver observations and suggestions distinctly. Preserve differences and uncertainty, do not copy account identifiers, and do not count repeated posts as independent evidence or assign a clinical verdict from the conversation alone.
- Research outside the frozen vocabulary belongs in the catalog and related-source view. Do not add or alter frozen claims to make such sources fit. Keep graded clinical evidence, reported experiences and unreviewed hypotheses visibly distinct.

- Treat the frozen direction as a working premise. Search for evidence that supports it, challenges it, or qualifies when it applies. A verified finding requires evidence that bears on the claim. An untested premise stays open.
- Exhaust accessible pages for the recorded searches and reconcile returned counts. Use the first-pass searches, broader ingredient searches, relevant compounds, alternative names, trial registries, and other appropriate sources. Continue following plausible references and citing papers while they produce new evidence. The original run screened up to 20 results per query and followed references once. Preserve those limits in the historical ledgers.
- For each language used in a search, verify that the native matcher handles its script and that the vocabulary covers the frozen ingredients and symptoms. Keep broader context reviews for records that fail a lexical pair match. Record where a provider lacks an abstract or an original report is inaccessible.
- Keep a resumable record of coverage, case-level or group-level results, conflicting evidence, preparation and endpoint fit, access gaps, and open trails. A zero-hit query, missing abstract, or inconclusive result leaves the hypothesis open. Record evidence outside the frozen ingredient set in the research catalog when relevant.
- Place every new study or local source text under `research/YYYY-YYYY` using its first public publication date, or its first public registry posting date for a registration. Link the entry from its period index and the applicable experiment records. Distinguish the date of newly released results from the date of the registration they belong to.
- Preserve the first-pass ledgers and the frozen claim wording. Publish revised verdicts only when the assessed evidence warrants a change under the original verdict thresholds. Verify the freeze, verdict files, and research references after each publication.

## Completion

- Verify the frozen hash and all 1,000 immutable claim fields. Validate the verdict count, references, and required search coverage before publishing the manifest.
- Maintain a round-level research-depth ledger (`followup/research-status.tsv`) with header `id	research_status	lab_clinical_checked	cultural_checked	scorecard_checked	last_checked_utc	search_basis	open_gaps`. Keep the canonical `verdict` field in `verdicts.tsv` as `Unknown` for open claims; mark a claim `research_status = researched` ONLY after verifying (1) `lab_clinical_checked = yes` (baseline catalog + Europe PMC + context-queue screening + live external PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov lab/clinical discovery), (2) `cultural_checked = yes` (Oracle MCP `source_kind: "cultural"` and `"conversations"` lookup + external traditional/ethnobotanical/public-forum discovery), and (3) `scorecard_checked = yes` with a recorded version 1 scorecard (`scorecard_status = recorded`). Validated researched `Unknown` claims surface as **`Unknown (Researched)`** in Oracle MCP (`display_verdict`) and the Nyx web UI, distinguishing exhaustively audited open hypotheses from partial `Unknown` or `Unreviewed` claims. Verify the ledger with native `foodexperiment.researchstatus.exe verify` (or `verify-complete` before closing a round).
- Record narrative checkpoints and coverage limits in `findings.md`. Regenerate `manifest.md` with the native review `summary` command and verify that it includes the current findings.
- Report all five verdict totals, coverage limits, and the strongest unexpected supported improvements, worsening effects, and reversals. Link any highlighted result to its claim and source.
- Give future experiments their own folder-local `AGENTS.md` covering generation, freeze, evidence, and completion. Adapt the topic and rules to that experiment.
