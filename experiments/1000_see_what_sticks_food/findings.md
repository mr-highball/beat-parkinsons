### Measured outcomes from comparator studies

- A randomized open-label trial of brewed green tea versus water after gastric surgery reported lower fatigue with tea on postoperative days 3–6. Its public registration lists fatigue as secondary and followed enrollment. The uncontrolled 14-person Parkinson pilot recorded a higher mean fatigue score after three months. A separate insomnia study used green tea extract, chicory extract and collagen, with lower physical fatigue and an inconclusive general-fatigue contrast. These studies leave the controlled Parkinson comparison open.
