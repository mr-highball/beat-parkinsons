ID: 0151
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Coconut-containing experimental diets changed laboratory motor performance, and one extra-virgin oil ALS experiment timed grip endurance on a grid. The Parkinson dietary crossover used a coconut-derived fraction and lacked a fall-frequency contrast. Refined coconut oil and spontaneous Parkinson falls remain open.
