Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: An older-adult trial of tulsi and cardamom extract did not publish a clear between-arm change in its accessible abstract. A Crohn trial measuring depression included cardamom in both groups. Ground cardamom and Parkinson depression remain unresolved.
