ID: 0777
Category: spice
Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The full randomized tulsi–cardamom trial reports lower older-adult depression score ranks than green tea at eight weeks. The comparison varies both plants and cannot identify a cardamom component effect. The Crohn trial uses cardamom in both arms, and the perinatal feeding work does not measure depression. Oral cardamom oil improves forced-swim behavior and increases locomotion in a reserpine model. The full behavioral values remain unverified and altered locomotion can affect immobility. The frozen culinary exposure and Parkinson endpoint remain open.
