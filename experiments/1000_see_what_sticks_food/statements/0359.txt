Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral isolated curcumin reduced time on the bar and grid in Cui's rotenone rats (Figure 1C, p<.05, six per compared group). A 2025 investigator abstract describes a shorter bar-test latency with turmeric extract, with effect sizes, route and cohort membership unresolved. These imposed-posture tests depend on movement initiation and have remote fit to passive clinical rigidity. The associated Manukyan article did not detect a rearing difference. Ghodsi's randomized nanomicelle pilot and Donadio's phospholipid cohort reported composite scores without a passive-rigidity comparison. The positive proxy findings and the clinical gap leave grated turmeric and Parkinson rigidity open.
