ID: 0359
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cui's oral isolated-curcumin comparison and a 2025 turmeric-extract abstract favor shorter imposed-posture latency in rodents. Manukyan detected no rearing difference. The newly checked Xu injection and Liu gavage studies report favorable model movement or coordination findings, with limited effects at lower doses in Liu's study. Rotarod, open-field and posture-release tasks do not measure examiner-rated resistance to passive movement. The Parkinson nanomicelle and phospholipid reports lack a separate rigidity comparison. The exact grated-turmeric symptom effect remains open.
