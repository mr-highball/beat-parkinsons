Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson clinical trials of formulated curcuminoids (Ghodsi 2022 nanomicelle, Donadio phospholipid cohort) report composite motor scores without releasing a standalone passive muscle rigidity item, while rodent rotenone catalepsy models test isolated intragastric or injected curcumin; grated culinary turmeric and clinical rigidity remain unresolved.
