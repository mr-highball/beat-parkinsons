ID: 0392
Category: food
Ingredient: turkey
Preparation: baked
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming baked turkey reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The baseline turkey figure shows a near-zero association with general tremor. Broad meat, fish and poultry categories have inconclusive Parkinson motor findings. Blood harmane was higher in Parkinson cases than controls, with similar levels in two motor subtypes and no measured turkey exposure. Essential-tremor diet findings do not establish a baked-turkey effect on Parkinson rest tremor. Two original protein-challenge figures show unspecified tremor returning during levodopa infusion. These figures do not establish the rest subtype or ingredient-specific exposure.
