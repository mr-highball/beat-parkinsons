Ingredient: turkey
Preparation: baked
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming baked turkey reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A historical turkey-leftover account describes weaker perceived medication effect without a specific tremor observation. The later clinical studies measured general motor symptoms, a mixed tremor scale or OFF time during different meal exposures. The baseline turkey-frequency tile is near neutral for pooled tremor, and general tremor returned during a 1989 protein challenge with unspecified food and subtype. A baked-turkey effect on resting tremor remains open.
