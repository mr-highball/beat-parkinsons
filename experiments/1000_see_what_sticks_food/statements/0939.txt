Statement: Consuming refined olive oil reduces apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Apathy was lower after an uncontrolled cannabis extract delivered in olive oil. The oil grade is unspecified and no oil comparator was used. Parkinson diet-pattern surveys report favorable motivation in one cohort and inconclusive apathy in another. The refined-oil trial and Parkinson comparisons supply no separate oil-specific apathy estimate.
