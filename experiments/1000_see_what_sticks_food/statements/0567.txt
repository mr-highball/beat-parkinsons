ID: 0567
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried basil reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A sweet basil extract trial reports menopausal composites that include fatigue, with no separate estimate. The holy basil study's fatigue comparison was inconclusive and tested a different species. A dried-sweet-basil effect on Parkinson fatigue remains unresolved. Related cultural/experiential records (1) provide uncontrolled context without changing the open efficacy rating.
