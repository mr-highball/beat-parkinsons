Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two randomized human gastrointestinal trials outside Parkinson disease report lower pain with anise versus placebo: ground anise powder lowered week-4 epigastric pain in functional dyspepsia (0.38 vs 2.05 vs cornstarch; IRCT2013101214980) and enteric-coated anise oil eased abdominal pain in IBS (NCT02364830). An acute COVID-19 anise-and-rock-sugar sachet trial favored abdominal pain with an ambiguous table ratio label (RR 0.55), Ushaq capsule composition remains pending, and rodent assays provide preclinical context. Gastrointestinal non-Parkinson pain etiology keeps the lean against worsening at minimal_signal.
