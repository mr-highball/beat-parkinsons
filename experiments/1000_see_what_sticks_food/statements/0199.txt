ID: 0199
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Two randomized ground-anise studies give favorable gastrointestinal pain comparisons outside Parkinson disease. The dyspepsia trial reports a week-four epigastric pain score of 0.38 with anise versus 2.05 with cornstarch. The COVID-19 study's modeled ratio remains ambiguous. Methanolic extract assays add preclinical context. The minimal lean against worsening concerns gastrointestinal pain with uncertain transfer.
