ID: 0251
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground turmeric worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: One combined fall-or-dizziness event was reported in each of three Alzheimer arms: placebo, 1 g/day curcumin and 4 g/day curcumin. The trial gives no separate or recurrent fall count, and neither balance measures nor rotarod latency establish Parkinson fall frequency after ground turmeric.
