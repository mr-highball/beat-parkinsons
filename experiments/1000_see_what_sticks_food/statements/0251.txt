ID: 0251
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground turmeric worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: One combined fall-or-dizziness event was reported in each original Alzheimer arm. Parkinson studies of formulated curcumin report aggregate motor outcomes or safety without separate fall rates. The stopped multi-supplement arm provides no curcumin-specific contrast. Independent dietary curcumin studies in different Huntington models report opposing rotarod results. The Parkinson rodent synthesis favors longer time on the rod with substantial heterogeneity. The Singh protein and cell outcomes add molecular context and measure neither a fall nor a motor task. The clinical effect of ground turmeric on Parkinson fall frequency remains open.
