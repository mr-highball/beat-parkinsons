ID: 0313
Category: herb
Ingredient: dill weed
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh dill weed worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An allergy case reports swelling after food containing dill. In a separate diabetes trial of dried leaf capsules, esophageal complaints increased from 3 to 5 of 21 with dill and stayed at 5 of 21 in controls, with no published between-arm test. Fresh dill and clinical Parkinson swallowing severity remain open.
