ID: 0724
Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Small Parkinson habanero-extract and isolated capsaicin trials measured acute timing with imprecise contrasts. The original Kyushu poster elicited swallows after brief local capsaicin contact in anesthetized rats. The independent Niigata line observed fewer mechanically triggered swallows after prolonged contact. A healthy-volunteer red-pepper sauce comparison favored brief preconditioning on a cued task, with no clear improvement under continuous exposure. The 2026 crossover administered the same capsaicinoids in both arms. These route, timing and outcome differences leave the effect of eating ground cayenne on clinical Parkinson swallowing difficulty open.
