ID: 0724
Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Small Parkinson habanero-extract and isolated capsaicin trials measured acute swallowing physiology with imprecise contrasts. Shorter duration is not consistently a clinical improvement. The originals leave the direction of clinical difficulty severity after eating ground cayenne unresolved.
