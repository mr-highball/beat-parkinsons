Category: spice
Ingredient: sumac
Preparation: ground
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground sumac reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The fragrant-sumac bark combination trial stopped after nine participants and released no efficacy analysis. A separate uncontrolled same-product study addressed urination frequency and other bladder symptoms. Neither identifies a ground Rhus coriaria fruit effect, and no Parkinson urgency direction was established.
