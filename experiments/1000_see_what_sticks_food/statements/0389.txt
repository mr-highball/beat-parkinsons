Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity

Statement: Consuming fresh celery leaf reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The official older-adult celery protocol targets overall sleep quality after a bedtime dry-extract capsule. It has no results or separate planned daytime sleepiness measure. Fresh culinary leaf and waking sleepiness in Parkinson disease remain untested in these records.
