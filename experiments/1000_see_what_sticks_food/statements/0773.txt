ID: 0773
Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined peanut oil worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral comparator arms using peanut oil show some within-arm improvement in depression scores. These include healthy volunteers, irritable bowel syndrome and a vitamin D trial, all without an independent refined-oil comparison. The peanut-only arm of a topical and inhalational trial also improved. Planned diabetes program depression results are unavailable. These exposures and populations leave direction unresolved for refined peanut oil in Parkinson disease.
