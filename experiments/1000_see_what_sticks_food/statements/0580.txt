Statement: Consuming raw arugula worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Rocket beverage and extract studies measured blood pressure in people without Parkinson's. A separate arugula case reports allergy. No study reported Parkinson fatigue after raw arugula.
