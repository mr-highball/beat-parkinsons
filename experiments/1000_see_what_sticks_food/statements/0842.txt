ID: 0842
Category: herb
Ingredient: oregano
Preparation: dried
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried oregano worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A mouse study reported longer drug-induced sleep after oregano infusion. A healthy-volunteer crossover found no apparent change in overnight sleep architecture with oregano extract. Neither measured daytime sleepiness after dried oregano in Parkinson’s.
