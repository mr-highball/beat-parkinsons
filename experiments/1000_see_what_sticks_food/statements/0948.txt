ID: 0948
Category: food
Ingredient: green tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed green tea worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: The uncontrolled 14-person Parkinson brewed-green-tea pilot reports mean Fatigue Severity Scale rising from 28.4 to 32.1 with higher medians in the same cohort. Paired variance and an exact fatigue p value are unavailable. An unblinded randomized beverage arm outside Parkinson had less reported fatigue than the capsule placebo in ProDiet, while the concentrated EGCG capsule arm had more. These are adverse symptom occurrences, with no baseline-adjusted fatigue severity comparison. The small Watanabe crossover and postoperative whole-brew trial supply contrary contexts, and the customary and constituent sources vary in preparation, timing and measurement. The closer pilot retains a tentative worsening lean. A controlled brewed-green-tea Parkinson fatigue comparison and independent verification of the unexplained change remain open.
