Category: food
Ingredient: green tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed green tea worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Mean fatigue rose 3.7 points in 14 Parkinson study completers after brewed green tea. The uncontrolled study supports a minimal worsening lean, with no paired data or exact p value in the same-cohort thesis. Non-Parkinson postoperative and mixed-diet studies give favorable or inconclusive fatigue findings but differ in setting and exposure. The MS diet allowed green or white tea without isolating it.
