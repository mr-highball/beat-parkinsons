Category: food
Ingredient: green tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed green tea worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Fourteen diagnosed Parkinson participants completed three months of brewed green tea. Their mean Fatigue Severity Scale score rose from 28.4 to 32.1; the same cohort's dissertation gives medians of 27 and 30.5. This is one uncontrolled observation with no paired variance or exact fatigue p value. An isolated caffeine comparison in Parkinson disease estimated −2.85 FSS points at six weeks (95% credible interval −7.73 to 2.06). A postoperative brewed-tea trial favored less fatigue in a different setting. Unadjusted correlations in the Shimbo survey and a caregiver report during combined food and walking changes do not isolate a Parkinson effect. The exact clinical observation retains a tentative worsening direction. A controlled brewed-tea comparison and the complete Jiang fly report remain open.
