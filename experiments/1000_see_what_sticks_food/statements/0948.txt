ID: 0948
Category: food
Ingredient: green tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed green tea worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Fourteen diagnosed Parkinson participants completed three months of brewed green tea. Their mean Fatigue Severity Scale score rose from 28.4 to 32.1, and the same cohort's dissertation gives median scores of 27 and 30.5. This is one uncontrolled observation with no paired variance or exact fatigue p value. A postoperative brewed-tea trial favored less fatigue in a different clinical setting. Parkinson observational sources use ungraded tea, pooled domains or total caffeine and provide no comparable separate brewed-tea change. The exact exposure and outcome support a tentative worsening link. No controlled Parkinson fatigue estimate or recovered full body for the Jiang fly paper was verified.
