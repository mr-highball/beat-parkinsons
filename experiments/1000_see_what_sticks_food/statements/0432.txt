ID: 0432
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: ground
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground turmeric reduces freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study measures freezing of gait frequency after consuming ground turmeric. The Parkinson baseline figure (MVP-2024) plots only an unquantified pooled-spice tile; an older-adult curcumin pilot (NCT03085680) tracked walking speed without freezing counts; ZAZ2 tested multi-botanical granules on composite UPDRS; and rodent gait or beam-crossing studies (PMID35966793, PMID36742141) recorded no freezing episodes.
