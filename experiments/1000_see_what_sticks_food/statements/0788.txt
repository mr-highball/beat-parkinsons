Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The oral clove study's anxiety-labelled table corresponds to Beck depression methods and has no separate anxiety test. A Parkinson multi-odor trial reports anxiety changes but did not isolate eugenol or ground clove. The frozen direction remains unresolved.
