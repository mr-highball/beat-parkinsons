ID: 0796
Category: herb
Ingredient: lemongrass
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: dream enactment frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed lemongrass reduces dream enactment frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study measures witnessed dream enactment or REM sleep without atonia after brewed lemongrass in Parkinson disease. A 50-volunteer brewed lemongrass tea study (Leite 1986) found no clear sleep or dream-recall differences, and a rat tea EEG study (PMID3762195) found no slow-wave or REM sleep changes. Inhaled or topical aroma reports, flavored-placebo nightmare counts, and mixed postpartum drinks do not test brewed tea or dream enactment.
