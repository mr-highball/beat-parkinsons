Category: spice
Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Green cardamom powder has isolated digestive adverse-event reports outside Parkinson disease. An uncontrolled cardamom-containing mixture improved composite IBS severity. Another mixture trial prints selected bowel comments and supplies no arm prevalence or validated constipation severity. The direction for ground cardamom remains open.
