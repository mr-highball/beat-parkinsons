ID: 0881
Category: food
Ingredient: grape
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming frozen grape reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: The independent red grape seed-extract Parkinson rat comparison reports lower cumulative muscle stiffness scores versus both untreated and vehicle lesion controls. The effect has a focused rigidity readout, while the exposure is a hydroalcoholic seed extract and the model differs from human Parkinson disease. The new isolated-resveratrol mouse study improved open-field movement and rotarod retention without a separate rigidity measurement. The earlier red grape juice comparison reported drug-induced turns without a verified juice-versus-lesion contrast, and the clinical juice-plus-exercise report used baseline motor scores. The original seed-extract signal retains minimal easing for the frozen grape hypothesis. No whole frozen fruit and clinical rigidity comparison is verified. A second model source reports better fly climbing after a combined whole-grape and Japanese knotweed extract. It does not separate grape effects or stiffness. It is distinct from the seed-extract stiffness result, which remains the basis of the minimal easing lean. A traditional-medicine review lists grape among sources of procyanidins and discusses Parkinson motor signs elsewhere. It provides no independent grape-specific rigidity comparison and does not change the lean based on the closer lesion-model finding. Powdered dried fermented grape skin reduced abnormal wing posture and improved locomotion in some PINK1 fly comparisons. Skin material, whole frozen grapes, fly motor outcomes and clinical rigidity are distinct. The purchased proanthocyanidin reagent and wild-grape amurensin G papers measure cellular protection without rigidity endpoints. The closer rat stiffness comparison remains the basis of the minimal easing lean. The polyphenol article reviews isolated resveratrol and related experimental findings. There is no additional measured culinary grape rigidity contrast. The prior seed-extract motor finding remains the closest directional evidence. The resveratrol review identifies grapes as a compound source without a frozen grape or rigidity comparison.
