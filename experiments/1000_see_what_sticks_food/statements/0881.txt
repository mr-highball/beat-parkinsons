ID: 0881
Category: food
Ingredient: grape
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming frozen grape reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A grape-seed extract reduced scored stiffness in a Parkinson-model rat experiment. A separate juice study measured turning, and the human Parkinson juice study recorded motor scores at baseline only. None establishes a rigidity direction for frozen whole grapes.
