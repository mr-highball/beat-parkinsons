Category: food
Ingredient: grape
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming frozen grape reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One controlled 6-OHDA medial-forebrain-bundle rat Parkinson model experiment (PMID25050252, PMC4075680; 38 Wistar rats across five groups of 7-8) reports significantly lower three-stage cumulative muscle stiffness/rigidity scores after 28 days of oral hydroalcoholic red grape (Vitis vinifera) seed extract 100 mg/kg/day versus untreated lesioned and vehicle-lesioned controls (Kruskal-Wallis and median test, p<.01, Figure 2B), while remaining above intact/sham controls (p<.05). A separate 6-OHDA rat red-grape-juice study measured apomorphine turning without a verified juice-versus-lesion contrast (PMC4075659), and a human Parkinson grape-juice plus aquatic-exercise trial recorded motor scores at baseline only (PMID32798568). Seed-extract rather than whole frozen-grape exposure (with seedless grape variability) and animal-only rigidity data keep the easing signal at minimal_signal.
