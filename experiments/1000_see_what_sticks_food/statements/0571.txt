Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed canola oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Anxiety events differed among depression-treatment arms using rapeseed-oil placebo and different active drugs. GAD-7 was inconclusive for a formulation containing a hydrogenated rapeseed excipient. Animal and mixed-diet findings depend on oil processing and co-exposures. No cold-pressed Parkinson direction was established.
