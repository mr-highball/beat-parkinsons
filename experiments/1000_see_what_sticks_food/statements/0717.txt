ID: 0717
Category: oil
Ingredient: grapeseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed grapeseed oil worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Grape seed extract improved rat stride and rotarod measures. Registered human grapeseed cookies list gait speed but provide no fall counts or published gait result. The direction for Parkinson fall frequency is unresolved.
