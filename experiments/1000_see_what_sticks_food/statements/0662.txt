Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried basil worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: An oral aqueous extract of authenticated dried sweet basil leaves accelerated acute intestinal marker transit in conventional rats compared with saline. Delayed colonic transit is frequent in Parkinson disease, but this is a short-term proxy with preparation and population gaps and questionable lower-dose significance. Other-species slowing, isolated tissue relaxation and customary reports do not supply a contrary sweet-basil transit or clinical effect. This is a minimal easing lean for the worsening hypothesis.
