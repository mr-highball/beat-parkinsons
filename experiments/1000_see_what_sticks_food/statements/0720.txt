ID: 0720
Category: spice
Ingredient: allspice
Preparation: ground
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground allspice worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Isolated eugenol odor produces variable evoked potentials across human attention paradigms and no clear manual Stroop change. The allspice leaf study measured general CNS activity after injection. These measures do not establish an attention change after ingesting ground allspice berries.
