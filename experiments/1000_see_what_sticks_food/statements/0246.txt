Category: food
Ingredient: watermelon
Preparation: raw
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw watermelon reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Belem Parkinson cohort recorded watermelon intake without preparation or bradykinesia by intake. The animal studies use juice, isolated lycopene or a mixed seed extract and measure movement proxies. These studies leave the effect of raw watermelon on clinical bradykinesia unresolved.
