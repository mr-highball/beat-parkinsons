ID: 0774
Category: spice
Ingredient: nutmeg
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground nutmeg reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An individual case improved after powdered nutmeg in milk. The student infusion reports lack a usable between-group estimate or combine sleep education. A separate mace-aril study lacked a comparator, and the mixed-botanical trial was inconclusive across active arms. Parkinson insomnia remains open.
