Category: spice
Ingredient: mustard seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted mustard seed reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In a small healthy-adult crossover, a meal with prepared mustard and hot sauce lowered sleep efficiency. Parkinson volunteers using mixed broccoli and mustard tea reported less broad sleep impairment in an uncontrolled series. Toasted mustard alone and clinical insomnia severity remain open.
