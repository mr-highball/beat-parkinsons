ID: 0561
Category: food
Ingredient: green beans
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked green beans reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson prebiotic pilot listed green beans among several menu options and reported composite motor and gastrointestinal scores. Its report and registration supply neither identifiable cooked bean consumption nor fatigue severity. In the Nurses' Health Study, 78,366 initially nonfrail older women were observed. Self-reported Parkinson disease was one of 13 conditions in the multimorbidity measure. No Parkinson-specific estimate is given. String beans appear in food-frequency validation. The vegetable-category fatigue-component HR 0.83 (95% CI 0.78–0.88) concerns the subset free of baseline frailty components and compares at least five daily servings with under two. Cooking and bean-specific response remain unverified. Supplemental Tables 2–3 remain inaccessible and the exact pair is pending.
