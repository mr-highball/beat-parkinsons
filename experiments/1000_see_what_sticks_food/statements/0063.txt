ID: 0063
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground saffron worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 58
Rationale: A randomized Parkinson trial of 100 mg whole saffron powder tablets reported a 4.58-point depression improvement versus placebo. The separate Tehran trial favored saffron on Hamilton scores and had inconclusive secondary measures. A new selected non-Parkinson cohort had a smaller favorable result after standardized extract, while linked Shiraz comparisons remain inconclusive. The two Parkinson trials carry the lean against worsening and leave ordinary culinary exposure uncertain.
