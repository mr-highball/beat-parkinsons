Ingredient: romaine lettuce
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw romaine lettuce worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The mixed-salad crossover measures vascular outcomes, and a red-lettuce fly study measures climbing. Three wild-lettuce case reports involve different species or exposure routes, with no Parkinson resting-tremor result. The cited tremor in the 1998 report belongs to a separate valerian case.
