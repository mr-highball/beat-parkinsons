ID: 0340
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted cumin worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A small randomized older-adult pilot of distilled cumin oil had favorable numerical changes on simple and complex attention, with inconclusive group interactions. Food chemistry detects cuminaldehyde after dry roasting. This gives a weak lean against worsening, with dose, food matrix and Parkinson transfer unverified.
