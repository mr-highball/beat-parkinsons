ID: 0340
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted cumin worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human comparison establishes an attention effect for toasted cumin. The only clinical trial (PMID42088310) tested distilled cumin essential oil in older adults and found inconclusive group interactions on both simple and complex attention. Analytical studies confirm cuminaldehyde after dry roasting without measuring cognition, a UK Parkinson dietary survey found no corrected nonmotor cumin association, and EEG odor or rodent memory assays do not test clinical attention. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
