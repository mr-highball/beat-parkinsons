ID: 0899
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The age-adjusted plasma ALA correlation favored less rigidity, with a weaker sex-adjusted result. A combined-regimen narrative described improvement. The two flaxseed omega-3 plus vitamin E trials give aggregate results and omit a separate rigidity estimate. The first trial’s registry changed dose and outcomes after publication. No contrast isolates cold-pressed flaxseed oil.
