ID: 0285
Category: herb
Ingredient: tarragon
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh tarragon reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized trial in 90 adults with knee osteoarthritis (PMID42866378, PMC13657636) reports lower immediate post-three-week WOMAC pain with topical 3% tarragon oil extract in sweet almond oil versus vehicle and routine care (10.13 vs 13.73 and 13.43, p=.001), while one- and three-week post-stop contrasts were inconclusive (p=.376 and .858), scent prevented blinding, and the 2021 registry disagrees on chronology, randomization, schedule and blinding. Supporting oral animal evidence shows aqueous dried tarragon aerial-part extract lowered formalin-evoked pain in fructose-fed rats (PMID26170888, p<.001), whereas ethanolic extract PMI-5011 in high-fat-diet mice normalized evoked neuropathy thresholds rather than spontaneous pain (PMID20396384), and a completed dental-gel registry (IRCT20241115063712N1) has no public results. Topical and dried-extract exposures, non-Parkinson cohorts, transient effect, and registration discrepancies keep the easing signal at minimal_signal.
