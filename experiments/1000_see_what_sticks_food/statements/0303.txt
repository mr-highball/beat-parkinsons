ID: 0303
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A paraquat rat article labeled rotarod performance as rigidity and reported falls. A separate mouse study found that other curry leaf fractions prolonged haloperidol-related bar-test immobility. The studies give no clinical muscle-tone comparison for cooked curry leaves.
