ID: 0524
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Steamed idli containing ground curry leaves was tested for texture and mouthfeel. Fresh-leaf chewing was tested for salivary pH. Neither study measured swallowing difficulty or swallowing safety in people with Parkinson disease.
