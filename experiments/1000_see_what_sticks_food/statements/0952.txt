ID: 0952
Category: herb
Ingredient: spearmint
Preparation: dried
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried spearmint reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled clinical study tests consuming dried spearmint for Parkinson drooling severity. A Parkinson gum-chewing study (PMID20385891) increased swallow frequency without identifying flavor or measuring drooling severity; healthy-volunteer spearmint gum trials increased salivary secretion without clinical drooling outcomes; and a Sjogren multi-ingredient spray trial (NCT06313905) has no posted results or drooling endpoint.
