Category: herb
Ingredient: spearmint
Preparation: dried
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried spearmint reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Spearmint-flavored gum increased collected saliva during chewing in healthy volunteers. The two possibly overlapping reports disagree on peak timing. Faster swallowing during gum chewing in Parkinson is a separate pathway. Neither the gum studies nor the Sjögren spray registration measures drooling after eating dried spearmint.
