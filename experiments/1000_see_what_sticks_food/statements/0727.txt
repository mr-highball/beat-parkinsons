ID: 0727
Category: food
Ingredient: egg
Preparation: poached
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming poached egg reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An egg-recipe trial found no clear effect on a physical performance composite that included an eight-foot walk. Two Parkinson studies list eggs among many foods. The recent observational abstract associates weekly egg intake with osteosarcopenia and gives gait results by diagnosis. An egg-specific walking estimate remains open.
