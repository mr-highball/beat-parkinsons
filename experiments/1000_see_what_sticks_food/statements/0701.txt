Symptom: olfactory impairment severity

Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil reduces olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Sesame oil has been used as an odor stimulus for diagnostic identification. An intranasal study gave no smell-function result. Neither measures olfaction after consuming cold-pressed sesame oil in Parkinson disease.
