ID: 0296
Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The combined corn-oil and rotenone animal study reached a motor phenotype earlier than rotenone alone, with no phenotype in the oil-only group during observation. The endpoint combined several motor signs and the authors replaced three animals. Existing gavage and mixed-oil models leave a clinical bradykinesia severity contrast for refined corn oil unresolved.
