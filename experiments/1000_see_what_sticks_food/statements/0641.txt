ID: 0641
Category: food
Ingredient: spinach
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked spinach worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A perinatal mixed-food study recorded spinach intake and an attention accuracy interaction across groups. A red spinach extract crossover had inconclusive Stroop scores. These studies and the previous thawed-spinach findings leave a cooked-spinach-specific Parkinson attention effect unresolved.
