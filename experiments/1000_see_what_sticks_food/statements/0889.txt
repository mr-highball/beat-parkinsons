Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral cinnamon powder favored motor tasks in two Parkinson models. Pole descent, rotarod and general movement are different measures from clinical walking slowness after eating ground cinnamon.
