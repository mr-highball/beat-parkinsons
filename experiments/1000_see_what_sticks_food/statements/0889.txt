ID: 0889
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Three oral ground Cinnamomum verum experiments from one research program report improved rotarod, open-field movement, and shorter timed pole turning/descent in aged A53T (PMID32889602, n=5-6/group; 2025 correction replaced separate benzoate histology panels) and MPTP mouse Parkinson models (PMID24946862, PMID31119595). No human clinical gait-speed trial isolates ground cinnamon (Park 2026 OA15.04 reports intake behavior only). Same-program mouse agility proxies without clinical gait data sustain only a minimal_signal against worsening.
