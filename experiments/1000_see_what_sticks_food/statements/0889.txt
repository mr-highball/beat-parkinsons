ID: 0889
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Oral ground C.verum favored movement measures after MPTP and timed pole turning/descent in aged A53T mice, giving a minimal favorable motor-proxy lean against the worsening hypothesis. Small same-program studies, proxy ambiguity, an image correction and no clinical gait-speed comparison limit confidence. Human Parkinson walking benefit remains unverified.
