Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: ground
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground sage worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral common-sage extract improved several motor tests in a rotenone rat report, and isolated rosmarinic acid altered activity in mice. Neither reported a rigidity measure. The pediatric cream abstract reported no measurable tone effect and unpaired subjective spasticity reports. The effect of ground sage on Parkinson rigidity remains unresolved.
