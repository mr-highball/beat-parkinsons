Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh basil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: One Parkinson diet trial included fresh basil in both menus. Another used mixed foods and reported a composite motor score. Rodent investigations used extracts and motor proxies. The isolated effect on clinical rigidity remains unresolved.
