Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: small handwriting severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined peanut oil reduces small handwriting severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson lesion-model study measured poorer skilled reaching after a corticosterone mixture containing peanut oil. It did not isolate the oil effect or measure handwriting. The refined-oil micrographia claim remains open.
