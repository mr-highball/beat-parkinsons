ID: 0785
Category: oil
Ingredient: pumpkin seed oil
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted pumpkin seed oil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson diet pilot listed pumpkin seed oil and included rigidity in a composite score without giving separate effects. In one rotenone mouse experiment, seed extract shortened bar-test immobility late and prolonged it at a lower dose earlier. Toasted oil and clinical rigidity remain open.
