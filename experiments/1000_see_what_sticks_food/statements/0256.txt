ID: 0256
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A roasted-seed pilot reported shorter sleep latency but no clear global sleep-quality difference. A separate powder trial reported improved insomnia scores in migraine. Neither study tested oral cold-pressed oil or Parkinson insomnia.
