Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized intention-to-treat trial in 68 adults with migraine (PMID41478596, IRCT20230616058497N1) reports greater eight-week Insomnia Severity Index reduction with oral flaxseed powder versus roasted wheat powder (-4.6 vs -1.6, p=.029) alongside headache-severity improvement. An uncontrolled 46-student pilot of roasted whole flaxseed (DOI10.5455/jrtm.2022/97416) found shorter polysomnography sleep latency (p<.001), inconclusive global PSQI (p=.635), and a text-versus-table Epworth Sleepiness Scale discrepancy without a comparison arm. Powdered whole seed rather than cold-pressed flaxseed oil and a migraine rather than Parkinson cohort keep the easing signal at minimal_signal.
