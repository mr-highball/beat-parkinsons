Ingredient: cilantro
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh cilantro worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: One case reported dysphagia after a mixed meal containing coriander followed by exercise. A separate fresh cilantro challenge provoked an allergic reaction with no reported dysphagia. The cases give no swallowing outcome in Parkinson’s.
