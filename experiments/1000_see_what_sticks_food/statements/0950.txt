ID: 0950
Category: food
Ingredient: kefir
Preparation: plain
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming plain kefir reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cancer survivors reported less fatigue with kefir during exercise, but the journal and dissertation cohorts likely overlap, assignment was nonrandomized, the control received no matched drink and kefir flavor is unverified. A second oncology trial reports a favorable physical symptom aggregate without a tiredness item estimate. Kefir and water both improved acute recovery after exercise in adolescents. The exact preparation and Parkinson fatigue direction remain open.
