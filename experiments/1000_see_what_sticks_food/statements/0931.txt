ID: 0931
Category: food
Ingredient: watermelon
Preparation: raw
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw watermelon reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The resting-pressure juice crossover and separate raw-flesh and ambulatory studies give mixed or inconclusive results. A dementia case places watermelon in a visual error and measures positional blood pressure without an assigned exposure. A comparison measuring standing lightheadedness after raw watermelon in Parkinson disease remains open.
