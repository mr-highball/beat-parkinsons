ID: 0703
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: speech intelligibility impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson dark-chocolate crossover gives a one-hour composite motor difference of −0.54 points (95% CI −1.50 to 0.42). Its separate speech-intelligibility estimate was unreported. The registered one-week comparison has no posted results.
