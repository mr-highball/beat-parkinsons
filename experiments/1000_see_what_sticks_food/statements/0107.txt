ID: 0107
Category: oil
Ingredient: soybean oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A small Parkinson crossover favored ground soybeans with levodopa on a dyskinesia scale. Rat diet studies measured spontaneous mouth movements. In a separate rotenone mouse study, soy oil was the vehicle rather than the administered curcumin dose. Refined soybean oil and clinical dyskinesia remain unresolved.
