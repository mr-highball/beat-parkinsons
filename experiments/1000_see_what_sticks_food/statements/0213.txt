ID: 0213
Category: food
Ingredient: cranberry
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked cranberry reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Three cranberry beverage or powder trials found no clear effects on their respective attention measures in people outside Parkinson disease. Selected memory results favored cranberry. Cooked cranberry and clinical Parkinson attention remain open.
