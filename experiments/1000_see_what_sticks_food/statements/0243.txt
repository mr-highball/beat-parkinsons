ID: 0243
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Standalone cinnamaldehyde maze findings were inconclusive in several experiments. Isolated injected beta-caryophyllene affected mouse behavior, and the human stress and Parkinson survey data give no result attributable to ground cinnamon. Evidence direction remains unresolved for the frozen claim.
