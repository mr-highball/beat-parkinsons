ID: 0267
Category: oil
Ingredient: sunflower oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined sunflower oil worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A randomized dry-mouth crossover found easier swallowing after cold-pressed sunflower oil rinsing in eight of 24 versus two with water. Measured saliva flow favored water. Parkinson dry-mouth and swallowing ratings co-occur, giving a weak lean against worsening. Refined ingestion and Parkinson swallowing severity remain untested.
