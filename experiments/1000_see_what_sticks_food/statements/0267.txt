Ingredient: sunflower oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined sunflower oil worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized crossover in 24 adults with medication-related xerostomia reports easier subjective swallowing in 8/24 after a cold-pressed roasted-kernel sunflower oil mouth rinse (spat out) versus 2/24 after water (unadjusted p=.031; measured saliva flow favored water; PMID33314461). Parkinson surveys link dry mouth with swallowing complaints without assigning oil, a baseline PRO-PD poster found null total symptoms (+2, p=.787), and an infant aspiration case series documents lipoid pneumonia with repeated oral oil without causal swallowing-severity testing. Remote rinse-vs-refined exposure and non-Parkinson xerostomia keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal.
