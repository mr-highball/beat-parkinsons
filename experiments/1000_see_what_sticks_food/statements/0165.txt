ID: 0165
Category: food
Ingredient: red cabbage
Preparation: raw
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw red cabbage reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Mixed vegetable diets that included red cabbage report ambulatory or clinic pressure outside Parkinson disease. Microgreens had similar resting pressures to the comparison. No verified trial isolates raw mature red cabbage and measures standing lightheadedness in Parkinson disease. The available evidence does not favor a direction.
