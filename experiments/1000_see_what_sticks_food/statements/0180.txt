Category: spice
Ingredient: nigella seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nigella seed worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Two separately registered, placebo-controlled trials at the same site found favorable sleep measures with a proprietary nigella oil extract. The postmenopausal extract comparison reports p=.45 and inconsistent PSQI and dose figures. Oil results weakly oppose worsening, with ground seed and Parkinson insomnia untested.
