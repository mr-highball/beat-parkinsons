Category: spice
Ingredient: nigella seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nigella seed worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two separately registered, placebo-controlled trials from the same research site report improved subjective PSQI (PMID37139438, 45 and 90 days) and objective actigraphy sleep efficiency, latency, and duration (PMID38288065, 7 days) using a proprietary nigella oil extract (BCO-5) in non-restorative sleepers. Competing trials show a null postmenopausal PSQI contrast with numeric discrepancies (PMID33603811, p=.45), superior latency/duration for melatonin over oil in an active crossover (PMID42819242), and a null multi-herb insomnia formula (p=.27). No controlled evidence shows insomnia worsening; proprietary oil extract transfer and absence of ground-seed or Parkinson trials keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal.
