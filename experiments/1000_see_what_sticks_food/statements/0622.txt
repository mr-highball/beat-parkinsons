ID: 0622
Category: food
Ingredient: spinach
Preparation: raw
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw spinach worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson diet plan offers spinach or kale and one ketogenic participant had worsened dyskinesia and medication reduction. The person's leaf choice is unknown and the Part IV outcome is composite. Neither the case nor the trial estimates a raw spinach effect.
