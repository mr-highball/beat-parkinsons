Ingredient: oats

Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming rolled oats worsens low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An oat milk allergy case documented acute dysphonia. The Parkinson oatmeal survey reported a total symptom score and the levodopa study measured acoustic outcomes. A rolled-oat comparison with measured vocal loudness remains unresolved.
