Category: spice
Ingredient: fennel seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted fennel seed reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Concentrated fruit extract impaired rotarod retention in healthy mice. Two Parkinson-model reports found improved performance under different extraction and exposure regimens. No study measured postural instability after toasted seeds in people with Parkinson’s.
