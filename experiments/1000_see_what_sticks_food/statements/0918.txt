ID: 0918
Category: herb
Ingredient: shiso
Preparation: dried
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried shiso worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The shiso rice trial measured odor pleasantness in healthy participants. In mice, induced anosmia blocked the forced-swim effect of inhaled isolated perillaldehyde. The inhibition of this behavioral response does not measure restoration of smell or an oral dried-shiso effect.
