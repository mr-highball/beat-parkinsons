ID: 0994
Category: oil
Ingredient: sesame oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Topical sesame oil in knee osteoarthritis met a reported noninferiority comparison for an eight-meter walk. Oral sesame seeds with standard care produced a post-treatment Timed Up and Go difference. Several other routes involve injected or rectal oil. The 2024 congress item reports the same 15-person Parkinson cohort as the 2017 comparison. No study compared cold-pressed sesame oil consumed for Parkinson walking slowness.
