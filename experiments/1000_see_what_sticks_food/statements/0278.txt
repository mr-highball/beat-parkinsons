Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The rheumatoid arthritis comparison recorded a rise in patient-rated pain after diclofenac withdrawal in the corn-oil arm. The previously cited physician pain estimate belongs to fish oil. Between-oil changes were inconclusive. Osteoarthritis comparator and local procedure pain results leave refined edible corn oil and Parkinson pain unresolved.
