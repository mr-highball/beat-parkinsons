Ingredient: macadamia oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed macadamia oil worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Anxiety comparisons between oral macadamia and krill oils were inconclusive in military trainees. An unrelated topical study used oil in both arms. Neither identifies cold-pressed oral oil and a Parkinson-specific effect.
