Category: food
Ingredient: potato
Preparation: baked
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked potato worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An inspection confirms baked potato consumption during a meal, with difficulty attributed to chicken coating. Family accounts report choking or coughing in different contexts. A Wisconsin Parkinson study used mashed potato but published only water results. A Pará Parkinson study pooled outcomes across mashed potato, water and cracker. The effect of baked potato on swallowing severity remains open.
