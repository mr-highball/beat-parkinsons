ID: 0128
Category: herb
Ingredient: rosemary
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh rosemary worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Two small dried rosemary capsule trials outside Parkinson disease reported lower depression scores. A newer heterogeneous synthesis is inconclusive for pooled depression. Fresh-derived beverage studies omitted clinical depression, a dementia odor comparison is inconclusive, and massage and inhalation results use different routes. The minimal lean against worsening rests on the dried oral trials.
