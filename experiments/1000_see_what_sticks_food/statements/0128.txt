ID: 0128
Category: herb
Ingredient: rosemary
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh rosemary worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One dried rosemary ground-leaf capsule trial added to SSRIs outside Parkinson disease reports a favorable BDI-II group-by-time contrast (PMID36343423, n=51 completers, p=.005) despite baseline imbalance without covariate adjustment, alongside a student capsule abstract reporting lower depression whose poster-extracted endpoint CI crosses zero (PMID29389474, SMD -0.30). Competing controlled comparisons are inconclusive for rosemary extract in methadone maintenance (Heidarzadeh 2026), a post hoc healthy-worker reanalysis (UMIN000036762, baseline-adjusted p=.058, no adjusted estimate; raw medians 46.5 versus 46.0), and a botanical blend (PMID38887495, p=.149), while fresh-derived rosemary-water trials omit clinical depression and a 2026 review pools heterogeneous end scores at SMD -0.29 (95% CI -1.01 to 0.42). No evidence indicates worsening; the dried-leaf SSRI trial retains a minimal_signal against worsening.
