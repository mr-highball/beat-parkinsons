ID: 0999
Category: food
Ingredient: banana
Preparation: raw
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw banana reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A secondary summary of a Parkinson case describes worsening dyskinesia during suspected banana-associated levodopa wearing off. Laboratory tests found loss of levodopa when the drug was mixed directly into banana preparations. The case gives no verified preparation or severity measure.
