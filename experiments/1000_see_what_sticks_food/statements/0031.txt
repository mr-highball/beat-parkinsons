ID: 0031
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In 97 people with Parkinson disease, gaining recognition of the coconut test odor was associated with worse standing lightheadedness scores (B = 1.147, nominal p = .003). The study used odor strips. The effect of consuming refined coconut oil remains unresolved.
