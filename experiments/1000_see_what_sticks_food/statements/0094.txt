Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: ground
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground curry leaf worsens daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Curry leaf extracts altered intestinal charcoal transit in opposite directions across models. The human iron-tablet study measured lower levodopa exposure. The curry leaf food studies measured iron or in vitro properties, with no levodopa or daily OFF-time comparison.
