ID: 0805
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Formulated oral curcumin gave a favorable Trail A result, an adjustment-sensitive acute vigilance difference and imprecise Yale attention change. Other controlled Flanker and cognitive-battery comparisons gave inconclusive differences. The Parkinson studies do not release a separate attention result. The findings leave the effect of grated turmeric on Parkinson attention unresolved.
