ID: 0805
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Randomized formulated-curcumin trials outside Parkinson disease conflict across attention endpoints: Small 2018 (n=40) reported secondary Trail Making A improvement (p=0.04) and an exploratory 2026 eye-tracking test favored extract (p<0.05 across 35 outcomes), whereas Santos-Parker 2018 Flanker (p=0.97), Rainey-Smith 2016 battery (p=0.25), Kucukgoncu 2019 CPT (p=0.209), and Cox 2015 adjusted vigilance (p=0.168) were inconclusive; no Parkinson grated-turmeric attention trial exists.
