ID: 0705
Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined avocado oil worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A small uncontrolled Parkinson diet follow-up reported improved anxiety with unassigned oil use. Avocado oil and CBD produced inconclusive acute tranquility differences, and a fibromyalgia placebo blend does not isolate avocado oil. The refined oil direction remains unresolved.
