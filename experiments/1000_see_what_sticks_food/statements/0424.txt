ID: 0424
Category: herb
Ingredient: marjoram
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh marjoram worsens drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson pilot reported improvement on the composite nonmotor scale after drinking strained marjoram tea. The accessible results give no separate drooling comparison or estimate for consuming fresh leaves.
