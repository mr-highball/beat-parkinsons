ID: 0499
Category: food
Ingredient: kidney beans
Preparation: canned
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming canned kidney beans worsens drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson diet survey grouped beans and legumes and reported drooling against a whole diet score. A separate Parkinson feeding simulation used kidney beans as task objects. Neither study estimated drooling after eating canned kidney beans.
