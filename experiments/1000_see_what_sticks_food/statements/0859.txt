ID: 0859
Category: oil
Ingredient: walnut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed walnut oil reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Whole walnut studies showed task-dependent fall latency in rats, null rotarod results in aged mice and favorable dual-task postural sway in healthy older men. Human fall counts after cold-pressed walnut oil remain unresolved.
