ID: 0573
Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed peanut oil worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Fatigue became less common in a post-COVID comparison that used the same ungraded oral peanut oil in both arms. A rat dopamine formulation also used peanut oil as a carrier and recorded no fatigue outcome. Fatigue severity after cold-pressed peanut oil in Parkinson disease remains unresolved.
