ID: 0965
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined canola oil worsens low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The randomized Parkinson diet trial lists canola among low-fat plan ingredients and reports motor sums with speech items. No arm-level acoustic volume or isolated canola exposure is published. A primary oral olive-oil challenge used canola as diluent and recorded throat symptoms without measuring vocal intensity.
