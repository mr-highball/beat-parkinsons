Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined olive oil worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The prospective cohort pooled refined and pomace oil. The fully adjusted attention estimate was −0.006 z-score units per 10 g/day (95% CI −0.047 to 0.035), with an attenuated top-tertile comparison. A refined-oil trial gives an inconclusive 1-back activation interaction. Observational usual cooking-oil exposure and common olive-oil vehicles do not isolate a refined-oil attention effect in Parkinson disease.
