Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground sichuan pepper reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Japanese formula trial found no significant resting pressure change, and an acute allergy case showed severe hypotension. Neither assessed standing symptoms after culinary ground pepper in Parkinson’s.
