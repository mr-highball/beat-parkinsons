Statement: Consuming minced lemongrass reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: One mouse study plotted more and earlier falls after lemongrass essential oil at two doses, while its prose gives the opposite latency sign.
