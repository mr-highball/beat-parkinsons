Category: oil
Ingredient: soybean oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil worsens low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The essential voice tremor crossover used soybean oil of unspecified grade as placebo and measured sustained vowel amplitude modulation. It gives no isolated refined oil estimate or measure of habitual Parkinson voice volume.
