Statement: Consuming ground sichuan pepper worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: A Parkinson case combined an oral multiherb formula containing Japanese pepper with intravenous levodopa for fecal impaction; neither ground Sichuan pepper nor dyskinesia was independently assessed.
