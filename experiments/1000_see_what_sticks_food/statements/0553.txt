ID: 0553
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined canola oil worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The original canola child comparison showed no clear within-arm objective attention change. Another meal study found a general psychomotor difference but inconclusive selective attention. Existing canola meal data also leave the selective attention direction unresolved. Processing and Parkinson effects remain unverified.
