ID: 0074
Category: oil
Ingredient: pumpkin seed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two independent controlled studies converge in an easing direction for oral pumpkin seed oil on depressive symptoms: a 12-week double-blind randomized human trial in 35 postmenopausal women (31 analyzed; Gossell-Williams 2011, PMID21545273) reported median depression item staying at 0 (IQR 0-1) on 2 g/day Cucurbita pepo seed oil versus rising from 0 to 1 (IQR 0-2) on wheat germ oil (unadjusted endpoint p=.046 across 18 exploratory items), and a six-week chronic mild stress rat experiment (DOI10.21608/ejchem.2024.310875.10173, grouped with the related 2022 cortical report as one program) reported higher sucrose preference and shorter tail-suspension immobility with oral pumpkin seed oil versus stressed controls. Active wheat-germ-oil comparator, floor effect, unverified cold-press extraction, exploratory single-item rating, and absence of a Parkinson cohort keep the easing lean at minimal_signal.
