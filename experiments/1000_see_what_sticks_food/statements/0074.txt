ID: 0074
Category: oil
Ingredient: pumpkin seed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: One non-Parkinson oil trial had a lower endpoint depression item versus wheat germ oil. The pumpkin arm's median was zero at both visits, while the comparator worsened. Unreported pressing and an exploratory item with a floor leave a causal reduction in Parkinson unresolved.
