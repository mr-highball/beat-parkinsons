ID: 0326
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: extra-virgin
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: After oesophageal stenting, measured swallowing grades improved with bundled postoperative care that included unspecified olive oil. The controlled topical Sjögren comparison was inconclusive. The ALS dietary-only estimate and an isolated dietary extra-virgin olive oil comparison for Parkinson swallowing remain open.
