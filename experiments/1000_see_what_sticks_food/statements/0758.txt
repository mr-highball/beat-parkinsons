Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried date worsens apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The whole dried Ajwa crossover collected subjective mood diaries without publishing apathy results. The 60-person schizophrenia trial found larger improvement in the composite negative symptom scale with Ajwa, but the apathy-related item was unavailable and 13 eligible participants dropped out or were excluded. The earlier 44-person Ajwa trial measured quality of life, and the cognition thesis has unresolved cohort overlap. Two acute healthy studies report alertness changes, while an uncontrolled whole-fruit study shows a small uncertain reduction in vigor. Mouse extract studies report reduced exploration with sedation or greater sucrose preference in a stress model. Parkinson cohorts measured apathy separately without an isolated date exposure. A direction for dried dates and Parkinson apathy remains open.
