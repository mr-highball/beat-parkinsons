Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried date worsens apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Clinical imaging and diet cohorts assessed apathy without an isolated dried date comparison. The whole dry Ajwa crossover collected an unpublished mood diary, and the adjunct schizophrenia trial gives composite results. Date animal studies measured movement, nociception or reward-related proxies outside the frozen preparation and endpoint.
