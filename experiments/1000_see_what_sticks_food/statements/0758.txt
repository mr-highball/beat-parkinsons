ID: 0758
Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried date worsens apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Across 40 related assessments, the whole dried Ajwa date crossover (NCT02288611, n=21) never published its mood diary, the Ajwa schizophrenia adjunct trial reported composite ratings only, and an acute date-drink alertness crossover (n=15) lacked verified fruit maturity and an apathy scale; dried date effects on Parkinson apathy remain open.
