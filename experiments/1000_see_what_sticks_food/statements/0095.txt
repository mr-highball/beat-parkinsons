ID: 0095
Category: food
Ingredient: oats
Preparation: rolled
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming rolled oats worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A randomized hospital nutrition package that included fortified oatmeal reduced observed falls. The group also received supplements and dietary counseling. No rolled oats contrast or separate Parkinson falls estimate was reported.
