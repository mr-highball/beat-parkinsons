ID: 0921
Category: oil
Ingredient: macadamia oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed macadamia oil worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Closest hand-function observations are one cervical-myelopathy case with a topical essential-oil blend carried in macadamia oil (grip 17.8 kg at week 1 to 27.3 kg at week 8; no fine-dexterity score) and one carpal-tunnel case with qualitative writing/coin-pickup/clothespin improvement during a blended topical protocol. Neither isolates macadamia oil or confirms cold pressing; no Parkinson dexterity estimate. The oral rat macadamia/soybean diet study measured fatty-acid pharmacodynamic markers, not hand function. No defensible direction for the claim. Open: find human Parkinson-specific pegboard/tapping/handwriting data or isolated macadamia exposure and verify preparation.
