Category: spice
Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two periodical originals and two ethnobotanical reports clarify food and customary pain uses without measuring response. An oral crude extract lowered writhing in mice, with sedation and movement confounding. Ordinary dietary powder did not effectively reduce swelling in an abstract-level rat study, with no pain estimate. No eligible direction transfers to ground cardamom and Parkinson pain. The publisher preview confirms ground dehusked seeds and leaves conflicting ten-day versus month-long exposure descriptions. The powder arm data and isolated pain outcome remain open.
