Category: food
Ingredient: asparagus
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed asparagus reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A later review reports higher unstimulated salivary flow in a high-risk trial of asparagus oral liquid with mecobalamin. The original trial body remains unavailable. Saliva output and the combined liquid regimen leave steamed asparagus and Parkinson drooling unresolved.
