ID: 0992
Category: herb
Ingredient: bay leaf
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground bay leaf reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Fatigue accompanied probable liver injury during concentrated laurel infusion use. A separate healthy-adult infusion trial asked about tolerability and a small extract study reported no adverse events, but neither gave a fatigue scale. The case concerns toxicity and gives no direction for ordinary ground-leaf exposure in Parkinson fatigue.
