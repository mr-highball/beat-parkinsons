ID: 0312
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: cracked
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cracked black pepper worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The closest clinical add-on comparison for ground black pepper gave inconclusive pain results. Clinical mixtures and topical studies leave the cracked-pepper exposure unresolved. Isolated piperine changed some evoked pain responses in rodents, with lower-dose and assay-specific variation. No clinical cracked-pepper Parkinson pain estimate was identified.
