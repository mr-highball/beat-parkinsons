ID: 0938
Category: oil
Ingredient: pumpkin seed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed pumpkin seed oil worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A placebo trial gives inconclusive seated-pressure comparisons for a branded oil capsule. Regulatory surveillance reports hypotension for an ethanolic seed extract without event-specific counts or posture. The active comparator and earlier resting-pressure or rodent results do not establish cold-pressed oil and standing lightheadedness in Parkinson disease.
