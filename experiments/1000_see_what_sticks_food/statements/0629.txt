ID: 0629
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human study isolates cold-pressed olive oil for bradykinesia severity. The Parkinson baseline graphic (MVP-2024) plots only an unquantified self-rated slowness tile rather than examiner-rated bradykinesia, and observational or whole-diet Mediterranean studies (PMID29081890, PMID32319358, PMID21535916) evaluate total scores or dietary patterns without isolating olive oil grade or bradykinesia. Buriram (TCTR20210303005) tested cannabinoids in a shared olive-oil vehicle with inconclusive UPDRS III, and MPTP mouse oral olive-oil motor comparisons (PMC10526674) were unclear while injected EVOO is non-oral and non-bradykinesia. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
