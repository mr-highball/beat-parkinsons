ID: 0629
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The oral olive-oil vehicle arm in MPTP mice had no clear pole or rotarod improvement, with different routes for the model control. Injected extra-virgin oil and injected olive-pulp extract favor other model motor outcomes. The dietary oil ageing study reports rotarod coordination without parkinsonism, and the olive tyrosol study reports aggregate markers in worms. The MVP slowness tile has no effect estimate or grade. Parkinson diet trials report composite motor outcomes, and the cannabinoid comparisons do not isolate the oil. These comparisons leave the cold-pressed oral oil effect on clinical bradykinesia open.
