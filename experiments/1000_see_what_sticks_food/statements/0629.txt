ID: 0629
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: The small baseline association with movement slowness gives a minimal favorable lean as a bradykinesia proxy. Whole-diet motor evidence is mixed. The randomized Buriram study measured a composite, and the smaller cannabis case series records bradykinesia at baseline. Both use common ungraded carriers without an oil-specific contrast.
