Statement: Consuming fresh basil worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Oral sweet basil extracts reduced motor impairment in mouse ischemia models, including inclined beam performance. Related publications vary occlusion duration and treatment timing. The human mixed diet gives a motor total without basil-specific stride length.
