Category: food
Ingredient: grape
Preparation: raw
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw grape reduces hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Concord juice had a slower pegboard time in one phase, mixed bilberry–grape juice was inconclusive, and a seed extract favored simple tapping across two follow-up visits. Preparation, motor tasks and healthy or non-Parkinson populations differ. Raw grape effects on Parkinson hand dexterity remain unresolved.
