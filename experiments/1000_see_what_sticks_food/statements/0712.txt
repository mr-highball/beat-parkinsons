ID: 0712
Category: food
Ingredient: grape
Preparation: raw
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw grape reduces hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study evaluates eating raw grapes on Parkinson hand dexterity, and non-Parkinson grape-product trials diverge in opposite directions across tasks. A grape-seed extract trial (NCT03526406) favored simple finger tapping (interaction p=.005) with complex tapping inconclusive, whereas a Concord grape juice crossover (PMID26864371) was slower on grooved pegboard in phase one (63.2 vs 60.4 s) and a mixed bilberry-grape juice trial (NCT00972972) was null on dominant-hand pegboard (p=.41).
