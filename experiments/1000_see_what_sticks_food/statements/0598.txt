ID: 0598
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The published MCT crossover now reports global Parkinson cognition rather than a separate executive outcome. The adjusted phase comparisons are inconclusive and participants selected C8/C10 supplements. Its prior registry and materials are a likely shared family with a design discrepancy. A small extra-virgin oil trial outside Parkinson disease observed slower switching than sunflower oil, and a healthy-adult whole-diet crossover did not detect an executive task difference. None provide the separately measured refined coconut oil and Parkinson executive-function comparison.
