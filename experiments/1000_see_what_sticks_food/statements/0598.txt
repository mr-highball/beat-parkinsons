Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A small feasibility pilot outside Parkinson disease found slower number–letter switching with extra-virgin coconut oil compared with sunflower oil. Results from medium-chain triglyceride formulations and mixed diets do not isolate refined coconut oil. The specific Parkinson executive-function direction remains unresolved.
