ID: 0598
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An extra-virgin coconut-oil feasibility pilot outside Parkinson disease found slower number-letter switching than sunflower oil. The healthy-adult ketogenic crossover found inconclusive executive comparisons and listed coconut oil only as an optional fat. MCT blends and other diets do not isolate refined coconut oil. The Parkinson executive-function direction remains unresolved.
