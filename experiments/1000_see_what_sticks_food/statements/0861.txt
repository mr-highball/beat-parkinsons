Statement: Consuming refined rice bran oil worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Enriched or extracted rice bran oil improved some rat coordination tasks. A historical contaminated oil exposure is not a culinary intervention. The selected original sources did not measure clinical hand dexterity after ingesting refined rice bran oil.
