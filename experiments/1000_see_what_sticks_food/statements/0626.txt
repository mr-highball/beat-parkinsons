ID: 0626
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: grated
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grated ginger worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A healthy-adult ginger extract trial had an inconclusive mechanical shoulder-hardness contrast and a favorable subjective subgroup. The Parkinson formula reports and isolated mouse constituents do not isolate grated ginger or show the direction of clinical rigidity. Ginger-PD has no posted item estimates.
