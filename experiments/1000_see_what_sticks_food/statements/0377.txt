ID: 0377
Category: oil
Ingredient: sesame oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Isolated sesamol reduced catalepsy in a rat model. A Parkinson trial held sesame oil across arms and reported a motor composite. Neither resolves clinical rigidity after cold-pressed sesame oil.
