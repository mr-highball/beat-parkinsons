Ingredient: rice bran oil

Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined rice bran oil worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Japanese dysphagia crossover used documented rice bran oil as an inhaled control, with no clear pre-to-post saliva or water swallowing change in that session. Neurodegenerative diagnoses and subgroup effects were unspecified. Refined oral rice bran oil and Parkinson swallowing remain unresolved.
