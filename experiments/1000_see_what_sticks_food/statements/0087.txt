ID: 0087
Category: food
Ingredient: millet
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked millet worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Iron-biofortified versus conventional pearl millet increased light physical activity and had reported reaction-time findings in the same feeding program. Other work measured grip strength. These outcomes leave hand dexterity after cooked millet in Parkinson disease open.
