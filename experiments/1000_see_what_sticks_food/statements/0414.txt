Statement: Consuming fresh marjoram reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson pilot reported improvement on the composite nonmotor scale after drinking strained marjoram tea. The accessible results give no separate urinary urgency comparison or estimate for consuming fresh leaves.
