Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground sage worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The oral sage extract study measured paw-print function after peripheral nerve injury. It supplies no evidence of clinical fall frequency after ground sage.
