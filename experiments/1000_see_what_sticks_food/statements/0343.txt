Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: The small Parkinson and non-Parkinson associations use ungraded olive oil and have confounding and multiplicity limits. DieTBra compares active dietary programs, and MEDI-PD's anxiety interaction is inconclusive (p=.745). AU-ARROW plans DASS in a multidomain intervention with extra-virgin oil. The minimal lean for cold-pressed oil still rests on the earlier observational findings.
