Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: The quantified Religa survey associates olive-oil quantity with lower HADS anxiety outside Parkinson disease (adjusted −1.86 points, 95% CI −3.54 to −0.18). Oil grade was unknown and the unadjusted item did not survive multiplicity correction. The small Parkinson heatmap supplies contextual evidence. A verified cold-pressed oil comparison shows favorable exploratory behavior on selected tests in young rats, with other maze results inconclusive and no clinical outcome. DieTBra uses exact cold-pressed oil outside Parkinson, but all active arms improved and the critique reports lower post-treatment anxiety in diet alone. MEDI-PD's whole-diet comparison is inconclusive. The observed human ingredient–symptom association supports a minimal easing lean. Direct matched clinical and DieTBra arm results remain open. The same-cohort TANDIM follow-up found no olive-oil-specific anxiety association despite lower anxiety after whole-diet assignment. Its oil association concerned depression responders.
