ID: 0974
Category: herb
Ingredient: marjoram
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh marjoram reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Inhaled sweet marjoram oil lowered resting pressure against air in nine healthy people. A later crossover in nurses did not confirm a comparable between-group effect. Standing lightheadedness after fresh-leaf ingestion in Parkinson remains unmeasured.
