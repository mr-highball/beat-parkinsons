ID: 0511
Category: herb
Ingredient: bay leaf
Preparation: ground
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground bay leaf worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Case reports link intact bay leaves to acute swallowing difficulty through impaction. A metabolic trial used ground bay leaf capsules, excluded neurologic disease and did not measure swallowing. The effect of ground bay leaf on Parkinson swallowing severity remains open.
