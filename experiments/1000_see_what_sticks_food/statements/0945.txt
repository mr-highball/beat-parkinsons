Category: spice
Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The green-cardamom extract animal experiment measures drug-induced sleep. Acute and repeated black-cardamom extract reports share one registration and measure timed cognitive tasks with no daytime sleepiness scale. The results do not establish a daytime symptom direction after eating ground cardamom.
