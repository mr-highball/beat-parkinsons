ID: 0118
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried basil reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral distilled sweet basil oil at high exposure increased rotarod falls in healthy mice. A frailty protocol registers clinical falls with mixed holy basil, ginger and turmeric and has no results. Dried culinary sweet basil and Parkinson falls remain unresolved.
