Symptom: dream enactment frequency

Statement: Consuming cold-pressed walnut oil reduces dream enactment frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A bedside walnut and jojoba comparator study measured sleep disruption from bad dreams. The whole-walnut sleep trial measured sleep continuity and sleepiness. No oral cold-pressed oil trial measured dream enactment in Parkinson disease.
