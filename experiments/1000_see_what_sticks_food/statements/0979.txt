ID: 0979
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson cohorts used cinnamon as a diagnostic odor. A five-odor program including cinnamon improved a composite smell score after infection. Ground cinnamon in an oral diabetes study gave an inconclusive meal-smell contrast and the mixed oil registration has no posted smell results. The clinical effect of eating ground cinnamon on Parkinson olfactory dysfunction remains unresolved.
