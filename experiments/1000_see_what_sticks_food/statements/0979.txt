ID: 0979
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson cohorts used cinnamon strictly as a diagnostic scratch-and-sniff odorant; an oral ground Cinnamomum cassia crossover in diabetes (Moreira 2024, n=19) found inconclusive breakfast-smell pleasantness (all p>=0.80) without a clinical olfactory scale, inhaled multi-odor smell training (Chao 2025, n=60) did not test ingestion or isolate cinnamon, and mixed essential-oil registration NCT07518394 posts no results; oral ground cinnamon effects on Parkinson olfaction remain open.
