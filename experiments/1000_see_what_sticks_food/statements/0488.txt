ID: 0488
Category: spice
Ingredient: fennel seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted fennel seed worsens daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Fennel preparations differ by model. Oral oil or extract improved drug-delayed emptying in two mouse reports. A separate oil study found nonsignificant stomach effects despite slower small-bowel progression. Human postoperative tea data measured bowel recovery. Toasted seed, levodopa concentrations and diary OFF time have no joint result.
