Category: food
Ingredient: quinoa
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled quinoa worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A red quinoa ethanol extract attenuated rotenone-related climbing and movement changes in flies. The dissertation and journal abstract describe the same experiment. Human walking effects from boiled quinoa remain unresolved.
