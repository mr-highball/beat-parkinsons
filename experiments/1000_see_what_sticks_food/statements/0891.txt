Ingredient: brussels sprouts

Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted brussels sprouts worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Sensory and composition studies compare thawed, steamed or microwaved Brussels sprouts and their odor characteristics. They measure product smell or liking and do not estimate olfactory impairment after eating roasted sprouts.
