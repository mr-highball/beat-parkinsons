Category: food
Ingredient: banana
Preparation: raw
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw banana worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One four-arm randomized adolescent conference abstract reports lower anxiety in the banana-only arm (regression -7.84, p<.001; preparation and full methods unavailable), alongside a nonrandom inpatient Ambon-banana comparison with baseline imbalance and a postoperative trial with conflicting reported t statistics (PMID40331125). Competing evidence includes a small null randomized healthy-adult Cavendish banana trial (n=20), uncontrolled Parkinson individualized nutrition with banana-flavored liquid, and separate levodopa-interaction observations without anxiety scores. No controlled Parkinson raw-banana anxiety trial exists; the adolescent randomized abstract sustains only a minimal_signal against worsening.
