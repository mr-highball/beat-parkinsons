ID: 0309
Category: food
Ingredient: banana
Preparation: raw
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw banana worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Two controlled whole-banana studies reported lower anxiety in adolescents or schizophrenia inpatients. A small randomized healthy-adult study was inconclusive. The later postoperative trial has serious numerical inconsistencies. This is a minimal lean against worsening, with raw preparation and transfer to Parkinson disease unresolved.
