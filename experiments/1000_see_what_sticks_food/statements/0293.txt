Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined peanut oil worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Smell test scores improved by 3.88 points in the intranasal peanut oil arm of a post-COVID trial. The difference from intranasal vitamin A was inconclusive. The registry and article describe the comparator differently. The trial gives no estimate for refined oral peanut oil in Parkinson disease.
