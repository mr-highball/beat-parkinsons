ID: 0496
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined canola oil worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two canola meal studies used finger tapping during fMRI. The study that monitored tapping found no significant rate variation across trials. An arthritis comparator discarded invalid grip data. These studies leave refined canola’s effect on Parkinson hand dexterity unresolved.
