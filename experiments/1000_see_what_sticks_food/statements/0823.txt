ID: 0823
Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Canine corn-oil diets give comparator-sensitive detection results without established refining grade. Other studies use injections, an odor-pen solvent or oil preference after induced smell loss. These exposures do not resolve the direction for ingested refined oil in Parkinson disease.
