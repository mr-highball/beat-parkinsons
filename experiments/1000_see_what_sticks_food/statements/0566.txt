ID: 0566
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: ground
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground ginger worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The confirmed Parkinson case measurements preceding a ginger formula and a later change during other mixed therapy do not isolate ginger. A nine-component ginger-containing decoction improved mouse stride performance and supplies no ingredient-specific human result.
