Ingredient: sichuan pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground sichuan pepper reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Exercise with inhaled Zanthoxylum limonella oil improved selected eyes-closed sway measures versus almond scent. Reach and fear-of-falling interactions were inconclusive. Related Japanese pepper improved a rodent coordination measure. Neither tests ground Sichuan pepper and Parkinson postural instability.
