ID: 0581
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: grated
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grated ginger worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 52
Rationale: Two small randomized IBS trials of dry or encapsulated ginger favor lower constipation scores. The 2018 trial reports an endpoint difference with unequal baseline means and no adjusted change estimate. Other IBS evidence is composite or inconclusive, and the Parkinson registrations provide no reported constipation result. The added human constipation result modestly strengthens the weak lean against worsening. Fresh grated ginger in Parkinson disease remains untested.
