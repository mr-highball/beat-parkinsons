ID: 0581
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: grated
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grated ginger worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Controlled processed-ginger studies outside Parkinson disease generally favor lower constipation scores, including a small separate ginger-powder arm in IBS-C. The mixed-IBS pilot found no significant composite difference, and the Parkinson ginger registrations provide no reported constipation result. Fresh grated ginger remains untested and the limited transfer supports only a minimal lean against worsening.
