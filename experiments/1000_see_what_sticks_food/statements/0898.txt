Category: food
Ingredient: oats
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked oats worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oat bran served as a trial comparator with planned cognitive testing, and green oat extract affected cursor tracking in healthy adults. An enriched meal package containing oatmeal reported falls. Those outcomes leave isolated cooked oats and manual dexterity in Parkinson disease unresolved.
