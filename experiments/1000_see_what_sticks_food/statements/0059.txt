ID: 0059
Category: food
Ingredient: cauliflower
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted cauliflower worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A cross-sectional healthcare worker survey groups cauliflower with other cruciferous vegetables and leaves exposure coding unclear. The Parkinson cohort has no cauliflower and anxiety comparison. Roasted cauliflower and clinical anxiety remain unresolved.
