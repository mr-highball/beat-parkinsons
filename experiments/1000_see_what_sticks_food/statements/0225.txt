ID: 0225
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Processed turmeric and curcumin trials report favorable swallowing outcomes in oral disease. One chemoradiotherapy trial reported grade 3 dysphagia in 25% and 20% with turmeric capsules versus 60% with placebo. A separate nano-curcumin cohort had less deterioration on an instrumental swallowing scale. An oral fibrosis trial has small event counts and an inconsistent printed p-value. Formulations and injury mechanisms differ substantially from grated turmeric and Parkinson dysphagia.
