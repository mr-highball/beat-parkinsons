ID: 0225
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two randomized chemoradiotherapy trials report less severe dysphagia with formulated turmeric or curcumin versus placebo: bio-enhanced turmeric capsules lowered grade 3 dysphagia (25% and 20% vs 60% placebo; PMID34161952 abstract) and nanocurcumin prevented worsening on instrumental Penetration-Aspiration Scale scores in 64 completers (2.91 vs 3.72 from equal 2.91 baselines; ARSHIDA-REF2023-063210). An oral-fibrosis trial has sparse counts and discordant p values, and a Parkinson multi-supplement registry reports pill-swallowing complaints without isolated turmeric severity data. Radiation mucositis and formulated extracts differ substantially from neurologic Parkinson dysphagia and grated turmeric, keeping the lean at minimal_signal.
