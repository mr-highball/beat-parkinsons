Statement: Consuming dried cherry worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: The longer concentrate comparison favors task-related mental fatigue. Acute ratings and the four-week rugby trial were inconclusive. The cherry-cocoa design and exertion contrast are unverified. Fibromyalgia pain, perceived recovery and aggregate athlete well-being add context with no confirmed fatigue comparison. The lean against worsening remains minimal because the applicable data use concentrates and other populations.
