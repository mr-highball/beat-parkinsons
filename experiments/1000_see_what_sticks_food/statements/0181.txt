ID: 0181
Category: food
Ingredient: cherry
Preparation: dried
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried cherry worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized tart-cherry concentrate trial outside Parkinson disease reports lower session mental fatigue by 9.5 points versus placebo (NCT04021342, 95% CI 2.5 to 16.5, p=.009; treatment-by-repetition p=.257). Multiple competing randomized trials report null fatigue differences for tart-cherry products in ulcerative colitis (n=35), acute task fatigue (n=27, p=.690), mixed cherry-apple juice (n=15), rugby players (n=11), marathon runners (n=35), sprinters (n=40), and pretraining athletes. No controlled trial tests whole dried cherries or Parkinson fatigue; the concentrate mental-fatigue result retains a minimal_signal against worsening.
