ID: 0121
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: An acute paper-filtered coffee crossover in healthy habitual users reported less mental fatigue than flavored water. Isolated caffeine in the short Parkinson randomized trial favored lower Fatigue Severity Scale scores, with intervals spanning both directions. More closely matched instant-coffee versus decaf driving comparisons produced inconclusive fatigue outcomes even when sleepiness improved. The 2006 and 2007 reports share their young participants. In a six-person crossover using the same decaf base and nap opportunity, added caffeine reduced post-nap fatigue, with limited transfer to habitual coffee and Parkinson fatigue. The longer Café-PD trial reports somnolence separately and supplies no standalone fatigue estimate. Withdrawal and mixed individual reports qualify the acute observations. The surviving comparisons support only a minimal easing lean against the frozen worsening wording.
