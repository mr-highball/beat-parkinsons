ID: 0121
Category: food
Ingredient: coffee
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed coffee worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The small espresso crossover gives individual fatigue differences in both directions and uses brewed coffee in both arms. Observational caffeine histories do not isolate coffee. One caffeine-capsule trial gives an imprecise FSS estimate. The later Café-PD study lists fatigue but its main paper reports daytime sleepiness separately and the fatigue result remains unverified.
