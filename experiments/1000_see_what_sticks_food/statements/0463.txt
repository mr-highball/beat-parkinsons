ID: 0463
Category: oil
Ingredient: sunflower oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sunflower oil reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Sunflower oil was used as an injected vehicle in a rat study. The motor outcomes do not isolate an effect of eating cold-pressed sunflower oil on human muscle rigidity. The Congrong Shujing experiment includes injected sunflower-oil emulsion in its vehicle and disease-model conditions. The isolated effect of eating cold-pressed sunflower oil on clinical rigidity remains unmeasured. Two additional sources use or cite sunflower oil as a drug vehicle, without isolating culinary intake or measuring its clinical rigidity effect.
