Ingredient: sunflower oil

Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sunflower oil reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Sunflower oil was used as an injected vehicle in a rat study. The motor outcomes do not isolate an effect of eating cold-pressed sunflower oil on human muscle rigidity.
