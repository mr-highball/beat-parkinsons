ID: 0140
Category: herb
Ingredient: spearmint
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried spearmint reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A 142-person concentrated spearmint extract trial reported inconclusive sleep comparisons, while a smaller separate trial favored sleep initiation. Preliminary and final reports of one spearmint and green-tea blend trial show some favorable insomnia scores with inconclusive primary sleep outcomes. These exposures and populations leave a dried spearmint effect on Parkinson insomnia unresolved.
