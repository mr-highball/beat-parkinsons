Category: herb
Ingredient: spearmint
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried spearmint reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in 90 older adults with age-associated memory impairment (PMID29314866) reports improved Leeds Sleep Evaluation Questionnaire ease of getting to sleep with 900 mg/day dried aqueous spearmint extract versus placebo (p=.0046, d=0.805; overall p=.0170), while sleep quality (p=.4267) and ease of awakening (p=.9016) were null. A larger 90-day trial of the same proprietary extract in 142 healthy active young adults (PMID30802720) found inconclusive LSEQ and PSQI comparisons, a spearmint-plus-green-tea blend trial (PMID33809544) had null primary outcomes alongside an exploratory ISI gain (p=.044) without isolated attribution, and a rat pentobarbital-sleep study (PMID30158996) prolonged induced sleep duration. Sponsor funding, concentrated extract capsules rather than culinary dried spearmint leaves, non-Parkinson cohorts, and the competing null trial in younger adults keep the easing lean at minimal_signal.
