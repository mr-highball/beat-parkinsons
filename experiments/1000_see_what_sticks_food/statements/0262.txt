Category: oil
Ingredient: sesame oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized weight-loss diet trial comparing refined sesame oil against refined sunflower oil concludes lower depression in its abstract (p=.013; DOI10.1108/NFS-12-2022-0407), though exact contrast definitions, arm estimates, an oil-free arm, and cold-pressed processing remain unavailable. A Parkinson cannabinoid trial gave sesame oil to both arms with inconclusive depression scores (PMID38487964), and rat forced-swim outcomes varied across endpoints. The human oral oil comparison sustains only a minimal_signal easing lean.
