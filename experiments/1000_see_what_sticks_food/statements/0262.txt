Category: oil
Ingredient: sesame oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A randomized comparison reports less depression with refined sesame oil than with sunflower oil during a weight-loss diet. The exact contrast and arm values are unavailable. Parkinson vehicle arms both used sesame oil, and cold-pressed oil was not tested. The oral human result supplies a weak favorable lean.
