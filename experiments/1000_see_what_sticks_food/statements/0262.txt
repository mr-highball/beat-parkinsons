Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True

Rationale: The 2023 abstract favors refined sesame oil against refined sunflower oil on depressive symptoms during weight loss. The exact test for p=.013, arm estimates and effects of cold-pressed oil are unavailable. The 2025 biomarker report and 2022 companion papers share the trial lineage without an additional depression result. A Parkinson cannabinoid trial used sesame oil in both arms and did not isolate its effect. The indirect human comparison retains a minimal easing lean. The diabetic-rat report compares injected sesame oil as vehicle and sesame-only in distinct arms. The resulting hippocampal biomarker measures do not transfer to human depressive symptom severity after cold-pressed culinary oil. The sesamin review provides context about a sesame lignan and concentrates on cancer research. The distinct human refined-oil comparison still supplies the closest minimal easing signal. The pharmacokinetics review identifies a sesame oil delivery vehicle. Pressing method and depressive symptoms are outside that comparison.
