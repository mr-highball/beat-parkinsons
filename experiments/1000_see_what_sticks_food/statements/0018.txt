Category: herb
Ingredient: tarragon
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh tarragon worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Concentrated tarragon essential oil impaired motor performance or produced ataxia in injected animal toxicity and seizure-model studies. An oral extract study measured general maze activity. These exposures and endpoints leave the direction of fresh tarragon on Parkinson postural instability unresolved.
