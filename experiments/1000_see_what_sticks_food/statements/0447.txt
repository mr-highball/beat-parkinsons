ID: 0447
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study establishes a cold-pressed olive oil effect on shortened stride severity in Parkinson disease. A randomized fish-versus-olive-oil exergame trial (PMID30866834) found inconclusive single- and dual-task stride interactions (p=.753 and .537) with oil grade unspecified; NCT05485402 found no four-meter gait-speed difference; a stroke mouse model found no clear footprint benefit; and the PRIME whole-diet stride protocol (NCT07097103) awaits results.
