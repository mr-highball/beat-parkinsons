Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The fish-versus-olive-oil exercise trial gives inconclusive single- and dual-task stride interactions (p=.753 and .537) with olive oil grade unspecified. PRIME separately plans wearable same-foot and height-normalized stride, with extra-virgin olive oil recommended within a whole diet. Pressing, actual intake and measured outcomes are unresolved.
