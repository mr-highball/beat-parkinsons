Category: food
Ingredient: fig
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw fig reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The oral fig-and-almond syrup arm recorded 3 of 40 tremor events versus 0 of 40 in each comparison arm. The Iranian register identifies a differently composed almond-and-raisin comparator, and both sources concern children with ADHD receiving methylphenidate. Sparse counts, an unspecified tremor phenotype and no raw-fruit comparison block an eligible sign for Parkinson resting tremor. The Parkinson diet shares figs across groups; the remaining fruit, bark and flower-bud studies do not measure the frozen outcome.
