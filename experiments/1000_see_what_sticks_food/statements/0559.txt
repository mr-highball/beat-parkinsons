ID: 0559
Category: food
Ingredient: fig
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw fig reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The fig-and-almond syrup arm recorded three tremor events among 40 children versus zero among 40 in each comparator arm. All participants took methylphenidate and the groups differed in formulation. The adverse reports lack a rest-tremor definition and the population differs from Parkinson disease. Gafoor's recovered article tests a concentrated dried-fruit extract against motor tasks in rats, with numeric table cells still missing. The fig-containing walnut-bar experiment tests a baked mixture and measures catalepsy and coordination, without isolating fig or measuring rest tremor. The Parkinson diet shares figs across groups. The checked Unani composition names peepal, a different species. A separable raw common-fig effect on Parkinson rest tremor remains open.
