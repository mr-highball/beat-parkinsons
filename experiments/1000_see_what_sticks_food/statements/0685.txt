ID: 0685
Category: spice
Ingredient: paprika
Preparation: ground
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground paprika worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A healthy student crossover using paprika extract found no clear treatment-by-time effects on executive tests. A separate placebo-controlled extract trial has no posted results. Ground paprika and clinical Parkinson executive function remain open. A separate standardized capsaicinoid trial gives favorable working-memory and post hoc timed-test estimates with inconclusive adjusted primary Part B and other executive measures. Its preparation and population do not resolve ground paprika in Parkinson disease.
