ID: 0883
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh sage worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One placebo-controlled menopause trial of fresh Salvia officinalis tip extract reports lower secondary POMS fatigue (PMID33615001, p=.029, without an arm-by-time fatigue table; separate sleep-exhaustion item unchanged). A dry-extract menopause trial gave an inconclusive exhaustion contrast (IRCT2015111713940N2, p=.634), Spanish-sage essential oil reduced acute healthy task fatigue (different species), Newcastle PD sage trial omitted fatigue, and a single multi-exposure FAERS report cannot attribute fatigue to sage. Extract transfer and multiplicity keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal.
