ID: 0883
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh sage worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A small placebo-controlled fresh-sage-tip extract trial favored less menopausal fatigue. A dry-extract exhaustion comparison was inconclusive. Spanish-sage essential oil lowered some acute task-fatigue ratings in healthy volunteers. The minimal lean against worsening rests chiefly on the first trial and transfer from an extract and menopause to fresh culinary sage and Parkinson fatigue.
