Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined peanut oil worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Both post-COVID groups received the same ungraded oral peanut oil and reported joint pain. Animal and topical comparisons use other routes. A dopamine nanoparticle experiment used peanut oil as a carrier and did not measure pain. Refined peanut oil and clinical Parkinson pain remain unresolved.
