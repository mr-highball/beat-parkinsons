ID: 0163
Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The CBD trial used corn oil in every arm and reported only an aggregate motor comparison. The DHA trial used corn oil as an active comparison without reporting a separate rigidity result. Enteral feeding cases changed multiple ingredients, feeding or medication timing. The refined-oil effect on Parkinson rigidity remains unresolved.
