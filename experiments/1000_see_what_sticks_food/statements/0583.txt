ID: 0583
Category: food
Ingredient: salmon
Preparation: grilled
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grilled salmon reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The pooled non-fried fish heatmap gives a small inverse graphical association with self-rated Parkinson slowness. It lacks a salmon or grilling field and a pair-specific estimate. The German preschool and infant trials report inconclusive peg-test and Bayley psychomotor comparisons without a signed estimate for either endpoint. Mouse locomotion favored an isolated roe fraction, with remote preparation and outcome fit. The mixed-fish hip-fracture trial stopped early and gives no results. No interpretable direction transfers to grilled salmon and clinical Parkinson bradykinesia. Obtain the full child trial tables and continue the original-pair and preparation-specific searches.
