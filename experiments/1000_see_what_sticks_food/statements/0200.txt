Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh chervil reduces apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson diet menu names chervil and does not give an apathy outcome. Another diet study reports an inconclusive apathy association. The crossover interviews describe existing apathy as a barrier and the registered scale has no posted post-diet arm outcome. A separate individualized diet trial has inconsistent reported apathy comparisons and no isolated chervil intake. The fresh-leaf claim remains unresolved.
