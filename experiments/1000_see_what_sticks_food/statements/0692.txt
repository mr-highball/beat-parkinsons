ID: 0692
Category: food
Ingredient: pomegranate arils
Preparation: raw
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw pomegranate arils worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A pomegranate-peel botanical blend improved a follow-up alertness rating in healthy adults, with an inconclusive omnibus interaction. Juice and rind evidence addresses distinct outcomes. Parkinson seed-oil results remain pending and raw arils have no verified daytime-sleepiness estimate.
