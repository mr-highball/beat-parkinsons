ID: 0443
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked curry leaf worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cooked curry leaf (Murraya koenigii) has no clinical Parkinson resting-tremor trial: oral methanol and ethanol leaf extracts reversed reserpine- and haloperidol-induced rat orofacial dyskinesia (VCMs, tongue protrusions, catalepsy; PMID:24250488, PMID:22136413) and improved paraquat/rotenone coordination without isolating Parkinsonian limb rest tremor or cooked culinary leaf.
