Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined rice bran oil worsens low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson trial used rice bran oil as a placebo and reported aggregate motor changes. Separate poisoning and odor studies include hoarseness or wet voice after swallowing. No study gives low voice volume after ingesting refined rice bran oil.
