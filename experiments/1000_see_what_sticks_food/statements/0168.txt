Ingredient: nutmeg
Preparation: ground
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nutmeg worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human study estimates whether consuming ground nutmeg worsens constipation severity. A single medullary thyroid carcinoma case (PMC1673758) reduced secretory diarrhea from 6.7 to 2.6 stools/day after replacing prior drugs with high-dose ground nutmeg, which cannot estimate constipation from culinary intake. The 1976 nutmeg-constipation letter (PMID942762) lacks verified accessible full text, and animal studies measure dry stool output, experimental diarrhea (aqueous extract null), or charcoal transit proxies rather than clinical constipation. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
