Statement: Consuming ground nutmeg worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A high-intake ground-nutmeg case reported fewer stools, and independent extract studies showed reduced stool output or shorter marker travel. One aqueous comparison was inconclusive. The direction leans weakly toward worsening constipation. The human constipation letter remains pending and the extrapolation to usual intake and Parkinson disease is uncertain.
