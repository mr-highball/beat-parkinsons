Category: spice
Ingredient: paprika
Preparation: ground
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground paprika reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two toxicity reports describe tremor during isolated oral capsaicin or concentrated dermal capsaicinoid gel exposure in rodents. A separate Capsicum extract study measures rearing. These preparations and outcomes leave the Parkinson resting-tremor effect of ground paprika unresolved.
