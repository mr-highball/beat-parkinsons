Symptom: rest tremor severity

Statement: Consuming ground paprika reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Ground paprika has no controlled Parkinson resting-tremor study: acute rodent toxicity studies of isolated oral capsaicin (PMID:8887888) and concentrated dermal capsaicinoid gel (PMID:41674738) noted nonspecific toxic tremor without testing culinary sweet paprika or clinical rest tremor.
