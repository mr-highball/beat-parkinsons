ID: 0711
Category: food
Ingredient: kiwi fruit
Preparation: raw
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw kiwi fruit reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson diet pilot listed kiwi and included postural stability in a composite motor score without reporting the item separately. Hardy kiwi juice improved selected animal motor tasks. The effect of raw kiwi on clinical postural instability remains open.
