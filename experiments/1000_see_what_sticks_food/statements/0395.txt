Statement: Consuming smoked paprika reduces speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Tongue capsaicin studies measured movement or force tracking. An inhalation case measured qualitative wet voice after stroke. Parkinson cough rehabilitation studies used capsaicin for cough testing. These endpoints leave speech intelligibility after smoked paprika open.
