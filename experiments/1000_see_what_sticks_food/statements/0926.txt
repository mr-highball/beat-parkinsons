ID: 0926
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground black pepper reduces hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Observational PD subgroup of 13 participants with genetic variants shows plasma piperine versus total motor score r=-0.466, p=.108; pepper exposure source and dose are not verified, and no hand-specific dexterity score is reported. Total motor correlation cannot establish ground-pepper hand change.
