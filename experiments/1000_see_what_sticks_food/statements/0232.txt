ID: 0232
Category: food
Ingredient: mango
Preparation: dried
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried mango worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A dried mango crossover measured glucose and appetite without depressive severity. The newer experiment offers dried mango among optional snacks and reports only aggregate intake and momentary affect. Fresh-mango and MiniMeal protocols have no posted depression results. The mango leaf extract trials give mixed mood estimates. An isolated dried mango effect on Parkinson depressive symptoms remains unresolved.
