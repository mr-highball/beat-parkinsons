ID: 0167
Category: food
Ingredient: blackberry
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming frozen blackberry worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The freeze-dried blackberry milk trial found lower immediate soreness after exercise. It had no soreness baseline, used unequal drink composition, and reported inconclusive separate later comparisons. A mixed-juice study cited for blackberry used other fruits. The frozen blackberry and Parkinson pain direction remains unresolved.
