ID: 0415
Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed avocado oil worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cold-pressed avocado oil produced no clearly marked contrast in open-field crossings in obese mice. A maternal oil study reported less activity in adult rat offspring and a Huntington model reported fewer ipsilateral turns. Walking slowness in Parkinson disease remains open.
