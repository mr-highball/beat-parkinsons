ID: 0964
Category: food
Ingredient: pineapple
Preparation: raw
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw pineapple reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Painful-bladder surveys identified pineapple or juice as symptom triggers. A separate case-crossover fruit-or-juice flare result was inconclusive. A small survey lists pineapple separately but only pools incontinence impact. The larger urgency-incontinence study groups high-acid foods without a verified pineapple item. Raw pineapple and Parkinson urgency frequency remain unresolved.
