ID: 0782
Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed peanut oil worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The oral comparator gives no clear within-arm anxiety change and differs from roasted peanuts. Topical peanut carrier reports and a composite mouse oil substitution give no independent human oil contrast. The diabetes support trial reports only program-level anxiety. Cold-pressed peanut oil and clinical Parkinson anxiety remain unresolved.
