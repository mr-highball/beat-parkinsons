ID: 0277
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Contrary to the worsening direction, ground anise powder favored vitality in dyspepsia and enteric anise oil had lower post-treatment tiredness ratings in IBS. Vitality combines energy and fatigue, the tiredness comparison used oil, and neither trial enrolled Parkinson patients. These convergent comparisons support a minimal lean against worsening.
