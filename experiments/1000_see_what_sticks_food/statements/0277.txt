ID: 0277
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two randomized gastrointestinal trials outside Parkinson disease report lower fatigue-adjacent ratings with anise versus placebo: ground anise powder favored week-12 SF-36 vitality in functional dyspepsia (IRCT2013101214980) and enteric-coated anise oil showed lower tiredness in IBS (3.15/2.74 vs 3.94/3.54, three-arm p<.001, though a later review disagrees; NCT02364830). A ground-anise menopause trial omitted separate fatigue severity and a honey-mixture survey gave no isolated estimate. Non-Parkinson GI cohorts and generic vitality/tiredness outcomes keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal.
