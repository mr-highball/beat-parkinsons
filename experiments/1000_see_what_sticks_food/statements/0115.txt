ID: 0115
Category: herb
Ingredient: dill weed
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh dill weed reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral dill-extract studies measured motor tasks in withdrawal and development models. Related dill and rice stroke studies found unchanged open-field crossings, better aggregate rodent neurological scores and one early human total NIHSS contrast. Fresh dill effects on Parkinson bradykinesia remain unresolved.
