ID: 0665
Category: food
Ingredient: green beans
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked green beans reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Common-bean pod extract affected drug-induced catalepsy in rats. A Parkinson diet pilot listed green beans and reported aggregate motor scores, and a primary bean assay measured levodopa in uncooked material. None gives a rigidity estimate for cooked green beans.
