Statement: Consuming frozen raspberry worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: An older-adult randomized meal comparison reported better six-hour working-memory performance after whole freeze-dried raspberry powder. Acute testing, recipe differences, powder rather than frozen intact fruit and no Parkinson cohort limit this to a minimal lean against worsening.
