ID: 0949
Category: food
Ingredient: raspberry
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming frozen raspberry worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized 36-person older-adult meal crossover reports fewer six-hour post-meal spatial working-memory errors and better strategy after whole freeze-dried red raspberry powder versus control (NCT03879213, p<.05). A black-raspberry Alzheimer pilot measured global CDR without executive subscales, four-berry and multicomponent capsule trials do not isolate raspberry, and rodent studies are split between high-fat-diet benefit and Alzheimer-model null results. Acute meal testing, powder vs intact frozen fruit, and non-Parkinson population keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal.
