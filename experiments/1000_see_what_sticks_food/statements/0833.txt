ID: 0833
Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Earlier motor studies used apple in a performance task and measured resting tremor separately. A later pilot tracked apples or dried rings as part of a high-fiber diet with lactulose. The recovered supplement gives only overall motor scores. An isolated effect of eating raw apple on rest tremor remains unresolved.
