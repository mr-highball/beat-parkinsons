ID: 0833
Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Earlier motor studies used apple in a performance task and measured resting tremor separately. A high-fiber diet pilot tracked apples or dried rings and reported only overall motor scores. The Dutch BrAIN crossover lists apples with peel among food choices and reports no significant effect on the total motor score. An isolated effect of eating raw apple on rest tremor remains unresolved.
