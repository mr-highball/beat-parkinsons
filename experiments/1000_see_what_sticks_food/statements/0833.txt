ID: 0833
Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Mixed menus and the high-fiber pilot provide aggregate motor outcomes. Studies using apple as a movement, eating or chewing task measure oral function or motor characteristics. The 2025 and 2023 chewing reports share one cohort. No isolated raw-apple effect on resting-tremor severity was measured in these comparisons.
