ID: 0833
Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Raw apple has no isolated clinical or whole-fruit Parkinson resting-tremor study: Parkinson motor-task cohorts used apples only as chewing/eating test props and mixed menus reported composite UPDRS only; isolated quercetin attenuated 6-OHDA rat muscle tremor (PMID:26217978) and harmaline mouse essential tremor (PMID:41354816, n=32, p<0.0001), but quercetin is ubiquitous across many foods and neither study tested an apple preparation.
