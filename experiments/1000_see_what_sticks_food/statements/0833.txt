Symptom: rest tremor severity

Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Quebec motor studies used apple cutting and eating as one performance task. They assessed resting tremor separately and pooled some participant data across reports. The shared vegetarian menu and the apple-puree lunch also do not isolate a measured change after eating raw apple. Worsening and improvement remain unresolved.
