Statement: Consuming refined safflower oil reduces hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Safflower and corn oil amounts changed together in a mouse DHA diet. The combined DHA and exercise group improved forelimb stepping. The study cannot resolve an effect of refined safflower oil on hand dexterity in people with Parkinson’s.
