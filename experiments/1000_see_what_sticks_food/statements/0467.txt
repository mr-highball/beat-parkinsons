ID: 0467
Category: oil
Ingredient: grapeseed oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined grapeseed oil worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Topical grapeseed-oil massage was evaluated for pregnancy edema, with no anxiety outcome. The seed-powder conference abstract reported within-arm anxiety improvement in mild cognitive impairment without a clear between-arm estimate. In a distinct migraine trial the adjusted anxiety contrast for oral seed extract was inconclusive (p=.47). The animal oil study measured spatial memory and acetylcholine. The evidence supplies no defensible direction for oral refined grapeseed oil and Parkinson anxiety.
