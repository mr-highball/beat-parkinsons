ID: 0570
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Baseline Parkinson walking difficulty is inversely associated with unspecified olive oil. The oldest-old study combines oil with other foods in a diet score and gives no oil-specific gait estimate. Timed-walk tests in obesity and sarcopenia remain inconclusive. The Buriram cohort used a shared sublingual oil vehicle, and PRIME plans mobility comparisons within a dietary intervention. Cold-pressed olive oil and clinical Parkinson walking speed remain unresolved.
