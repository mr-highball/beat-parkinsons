ID: 0570
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Baseline Parkinson walking difficulty is inversely associated with unspecified olive oil. Timed-walk comparisons in obesity and sarcopenia remain inconclusive. The single Buriram cohort gives no clear difference in gait velocity after cannabinoids were added to the shared sublingual oil vehicle. PRIME has planned mobility outcomes. Cold-pressed olive oil and clinical Parkinson walking speed remain unresolved.
