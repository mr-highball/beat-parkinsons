Ingredient: mustard seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground mustard seed worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Human odor and flavor studies used mustard as a stimulus. The 2024 combined tea report shows one participant stayed at a smell score of zero, and does not give a group estimate. An isolated nasal mustard constituent result does not resolve dietary ground seed in Parkinson disease.
