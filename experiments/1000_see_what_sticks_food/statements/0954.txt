ID: 0954
Category: oil
Ingredient: macadamia oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed macadamia oil worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Closest night-sleep lead is RRIPP-3, where macadamia-oil capsules were the active comparator to krill oil in trainees; no inert/no-oil group and no clear group differences in sleep-related measures, with no insomnia-severity endpoint. Separate 8 g macadamia-oil bread meals in healthy adults reduced acute postmeal sleepiness/fatigue versus controls in the same two-experiment family; this is daytime somnolence, not insomnia, and cold pressing is unverified. No direction for Parkinson insomnia. Open: verify original RRIPP-3 sleep tables/insomnia measurement and any Parkinson-specific insomnia outcomes attributable to oil.
