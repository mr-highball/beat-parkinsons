Ingredient: chives
Preparation: dried
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried chives worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Purified ferulic acid generally increased apparatus latency to fall in MPTP mice. Common chives contain ferulic acid, but dried-chive intake and spontaneous falls in Parkinson's were untested in these studies.
