Statement: Consuming frozen raspberry reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: A state-anxiety comparison favored a dried berry mixture with 16.7% raspberry over cocoa in an exploratory healthy-volunteer analysis. The frozen-fruit thesis measured oxidative biomarkers. The recovered isolated ketone paper assessed depression-like behavior in mice, and the human raspberry powder experiment has no separate anxiety estimate. The direction for frozen raspberry and Parkinson anxiety remains unresolved.
