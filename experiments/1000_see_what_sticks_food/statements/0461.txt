Category: herb
Ingredient: tarragon
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh tarragon reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral tarragon extract lowered a composite weakness score in autoimmune encephalomyelitis mice. Injected extract generally left open-field distance unchanged, with less travel at the highest observed dose. No measured Parkinson gait-speed comparison after fresh tarragon.
