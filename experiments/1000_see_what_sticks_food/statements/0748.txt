ID: 0748
Category: spice
Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A cardamom-containing food challenge produced acute allergic dysphagia in one case with unspecified spice preparation. A separate isolated ground-cardamom challenge caused hives and abdominal symptoms without documented dysphagia. Odor increased saliva in healthy volunteers. The allergic response and salivary measure cannot resolve Parkinson dysphagia after ground cardamom.
