Category: spice
Ingredient: sumac
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground sumac reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Both arms in a registered non-Parkinson ground sumac trial improved on BDI-II during calorie restriction. The difference in change was small and inconclusive (p=.78). An appetite companion reports other outcomes from the same study. The effect on depressive symptoms in Parkinson disease remains unresolved.
