Category: spice
Ingredient: sumac
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground sumac reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Ground sumac and placebo produced similar BDI-II changes in an exact-preparation weight-loss trial outside Parkinson disease (between-arm p=.78). The newly verified oral fruit essential-oil rat study found reduced forced-swim immobility and more climbing without independent locomotor testing. The distinct injected-extract mouse study found reduced tail-suspension immobility with a null open-field test. The preparations, animal populations and behavioral endpoints leave no defensible Parkinson clinical direction.
