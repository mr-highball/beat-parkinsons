ID: 0067
Category: food
Ingredient: mango
Preparation: raw
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw mango reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson diet cohort recorded mango without a graded insomnia result. Other mango studies measured a sleep item or omitted sleep. Isolated myrcene altered barbiturate-induced sleep in animals, with opposite results under different schedules, and none of those comparisons administered raw mango. The exact clinical claim remains open.
