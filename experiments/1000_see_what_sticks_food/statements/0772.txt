ID: 0772
Category: oil
Ingredient: sunflower oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sunflower oil reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The refined-oil DICe pilot favored sunflower over coconut for exploratory switching. MEMO and the Jenkins trial did not distinguish high-oleic sunflower from active comparators for executive change. Seed oil improved recognition memory in scopolamine-treated mice and students improved recall in a single-group study. Very high-fat sunflower and palm-kernel diets worsened radial-maze performance. A second rat study found more working-memory errors after a combined sunflower and saline regimen than in untreated controls. The handling difference limits oil attribution. The Parkinson clinical record measured a composite and the Parkinson models used a shared injected vehicle. No interpretable direction transfers to cold-pressed sunflower oil and a separate Parkinson executive outcome.
