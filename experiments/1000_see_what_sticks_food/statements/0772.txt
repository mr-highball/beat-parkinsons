ID: 0772
Category: oil
Ingredient: sunflower oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sunflower oil reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Sunflower comparator trials report mixed attention, memory and executive outcomes. A Mediterranean diet intervention replaced several oils including sunflower, giving a favorable global cognitive result but an inconclusive verbal-fluency comparison. No reviewed comparison isolates cold-pressed sunflower oil and Parkinson executive function.
