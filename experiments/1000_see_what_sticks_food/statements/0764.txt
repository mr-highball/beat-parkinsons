Ingredient: shiso
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh shiso worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One patent-reported human randomized crossover in 14 healthy men (WO2019230626A1) reports -0.24 fewer nighttime urinations per night versus starch after 28 days of commercial red-perilla (Perilla frutescens) water extract (p<.05) from a low 0.24 baseline, while daytime frequency and daily urine volume contrasts were inconclusive and table-versus-prose daytime standard errors conflict. Independently, oral perilla extract prolonged micturition inter-void intervals by 27% versus controls in female spontaneously hypertensive rats without altering maximum bladder pressure (PMID29268303). Extract-versus-fresh-shiso exposure, small non-Parkinson healthy-male patent cohort, void-frequency rather than urgency-episode measurement, and daytime null contrasts keep the easing signal contradicting the worsening hypothesis at minimal_signal.
