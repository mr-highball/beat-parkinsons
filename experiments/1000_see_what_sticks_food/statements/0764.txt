Category: herb
Ingredient: shiso
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh shiso worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A 14-person red-perilla extract crossover found 0.24 fewer nighttime voids per night versus starch, with a very low baseline and an inconclusive daytime comparison. A separate oral perilla-extract study prolonged rat void intervals by 27%. Both experiments favor lower void frequency with their tested extracts. The frozen endpoint is the frequency of urgent episodes. Neither source measures urgency, fresh culinary shiso or the response in Parkinson disease.
