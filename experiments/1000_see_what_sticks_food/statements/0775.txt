ID: 0775
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: small handwriting severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed reduces small handwriting severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson thesis used a multiingredient honey mixture containing anise seed. It reports no handwriting-size outcome tied to intake. Isolated anise oil and constituent studies used different motor measures.
