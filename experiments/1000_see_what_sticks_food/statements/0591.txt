Symptom: constipation severity

Statement: Consuming fresh chives reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A different Allium species improved fecal excretion and intestinal transit as an ethanolic extract in mice. The chive-containing Parkinson study recorded baseline stool form and no clinical constipation severity result after a fresh-chive comparison.
