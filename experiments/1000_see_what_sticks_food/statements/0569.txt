ID: 0569
Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: dream enactment frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil reduces dream enactment frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The recovered memory-care poster measured actigraphic total sleep with topical and diffused almond oil. The whole-almond supplement reports within-arm REM changes, and adjusted between-group contrasts remain inconclusive. Dream enactment after oral cold-pressed almond oil was unmeasured.
