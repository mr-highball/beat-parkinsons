Statement: Consuming refined grapeseed oil worsens daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: One Parkinson diet interview describes prolonged OFF after a high-fat meal of unidentified oil composition. A separate mixed breakfast delayed levodopa absorption in healthy volunteers. Daily OFF after refined grapeseed oil remains unmeasured.
