ID: 0126
Category: food
Ingredient: green tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: small handwriting severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed green tea reduces small handwriting severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study measures handwriting size after brewed green tea in Parkinson disease. The brewed green tea Parkinson pilot (DOI10.4236/jbbs.2015.56020) and LRRK2/Kagoshima cohorts report daily-living composites or nonmotor scales without isolated handwriting size; the six-month Parkinson caffeine capsule trial (PMID28954882) reported no MDS-UPDRS-II item differences; and a healthy caffeine crossover (PMID17010462) measured pen velocity/fluency rather than micrographia.
