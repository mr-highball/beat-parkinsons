Statement: Consuming boiled shrimp worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Dysarthria or unclear speech occurred in poisoning and infection reports. A Danish investigation traced the toxin to roe in a shared seafood meal. The available reports do not estimate boiled shrimp's own effect on Parkinson speech intelligibility.
