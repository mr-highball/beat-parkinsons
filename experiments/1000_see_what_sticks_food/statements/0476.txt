ID: 0476
Category: oil
Ingredient: rice bran oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined rice bran oil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: An oral red rice bran oil comparison in haloperidol rats reported less catalepsy, a partial rigidity proxy. The water-control comparison showed a smaller opposite sign, the results wording and figure markings differ, and the oil refining is undocumented. The model-specific contrast weakly favors easing. Human Parkinson rigidity with refined oil remains untested.
