ID: 0476
Category: oil
Ingredient: rice bran oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined rice bran oil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: An oral red rice bran oil comparison in haloperidol rats reported less catalepsy, a partial rigidity proxy. The water-control comparison showed a smaller opposite sign, the results wording and figure markings differ, and the oil refining is undocumented. The model-specific contrast weakly favors easing. Human Parkinson rigidity with refined oil remains untested. The verified 2015 extracted rice bran oil experiment measured chewing stereotypies and rotarod performance. Its overlapping companion reports do not supply separately measured clinical rigidity or dystonia.
