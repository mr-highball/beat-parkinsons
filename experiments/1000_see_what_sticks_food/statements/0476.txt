ID: 0476
Category: oil
Ingredient: rice bran oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined rice bran oil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In a haloperidol rat study, catalepsy with low-dose enriched rice bran oil was higher at the early assessment and lower later. The design did not isolate plain refined oil or measure clinical rigidity.
