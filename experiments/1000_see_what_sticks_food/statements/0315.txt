Statement: Consuming fresh cilantro reduces apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Clinical Parkinson diet menus list coriander among many seasonings. A separate experiment used visual and odor exposure to live plants and measured general mood in healthy students. Neither assessed apathy after eating fresh cilantro. The September 2025 online publication identifies orally administered coriander leaves in Parkinson models. The accessible Results excerpt identifies grip strength, rotarod and pole testing. Those outcomes do not measure apathy or facial expressiveness. The complete primary Methods and outcome tables, including fresh versus dried processing and any further measurements, remain inaccessible. The 1893 printed translation reports a warning about fresh coriander juice and attributes confusion and pathological sleepiness to Galen. The juiced plant part is unspecified. The account gives neither a patient-level comparison nor a measured Parkinson symptom effect. Sleepiness does not establish apathy or facial-expression impairment. Current targeted PubMed and trial-registry searches did not find a matching Parkinson comparison. The remaining original Methods and complete outcome scope warrant a named access hold.
