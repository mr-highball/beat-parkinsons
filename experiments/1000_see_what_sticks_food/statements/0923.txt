Statement: Consuming fresh spearmint worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The aqueous spearmint trial’s two doses pointed in different directions for depression-dejection, with baseline imbalance and an inconclusive overall comparison. The blended extract trial was inconclusive. Peppermint extract reduced mouse tail-suspension immobility alongside motor recovery. The sources leave fresh spearmint and Parkinson depressive symptoms unresolved.
