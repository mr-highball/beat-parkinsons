ID: 0769
Category: spice
Ingredient: paprika
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground paprika worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A paprika oleoresin trial in adults with mild knee pain found no clear pain-VAS or total JKOM contrast. A separate carotenoid trial lists adverse events in both groups and did not measure pain severity. The registered knee study has no reported results and its match to the paper is unverified.
