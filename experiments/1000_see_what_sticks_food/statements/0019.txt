ID: 0019
Category: spice
Ingredient: nigella seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted nigella seed reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: One randomized non-Parkinson comparison favored nigella oil extract on depression while both arms used sertraline. Its printed change and means differ by one point. A ground-seed trial found lower premenstrual mood symptoms, and mixed-product depression comparisons were inconclusive. A minimal favorable lean for toasted seed depends on transfer across dose form, diagnosis and co-treatment.
