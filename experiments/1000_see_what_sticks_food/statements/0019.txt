ID: 0019
Category: spice
Ingredient: nigella seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted nigella seed reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized non-Parkinson depression trial reports lower DASS-21 scores when nigella oil extract is added to sertraline versus sertraline alone (PMID35548175, 18.6 vs 23.4, p<.001, with a one-point arithmetic discrepancy in printed change). A ground-seed premenstrual trial favored a composite mood domain rather than depression severity (PMID41267796), while crushed-seed Bond-Lader mood (PMID24412554) and mixed-product depression trials in hypothyroidism (p=.79) and multiple sclerosis were inconclusive, and a hemodialysis trial has unreported HADS results. Oil preparation, co-treatment, reporting discrepancy, and absence of toasted-seed or Parkinson trials keep the easing lean at minimal_signal.
