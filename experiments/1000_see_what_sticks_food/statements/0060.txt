ID: 0060
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried sage worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A registered dormitory-room trial found lower global sleep-quality scores with sage tea than with its brown-sugar control. That oral common-sage result and menopause extract signals give a minimal lean toward improvement. A small primary-insomnia trial found an inconclusive comparison with active chamomile, and the Parkinson dried-leaf trial did not measure sleep. Preparation, sugar, clustering, reporting and population differences leave substantial uncertainty.
