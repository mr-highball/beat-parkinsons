ID: 0060
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried sage worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A dormitory-room trial found better global sleep-quality scores with sage tea than its brown-sugar control. Room allocation, unequal sugar and missing responder counts limit that result. The dried-sage versus chamomile pilot was inconclusive at days 14 and 21 and reported baseline scores below its stated eligibility range. The student comparison and menopause extract results support a minimal lean against worsening. The Parkinson dried-leaf trial reported no sleep estimate.
