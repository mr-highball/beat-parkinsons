ID: 0060
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried sage worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two placebo-controlled oral Salvia officinalis extract trials in menopause (Zeidabadi 2020 dry extract, n=59, PSQI 9.4 to 5.6; Dimpfel 2021 fresh-tip extract, n=78) and a dormitory student tea trial versus brown sugar (Rigi 2024, n=128, room-allocated with unequal sugar and baseline typo) report better global sleep scores. An active-controlled primary-insomnia pilot comparing dried sage tea against chamomile was inconclusive (n=30, ISI p=.78/.83, baseline below eligibility) and the Newcastle Parkinson dried-sage pilot reported cognition and salivation without a sleep estimate. No controlled evidence shows insomnia worsening; the non-Parkinson sleep trials sustain a minimal_signal against worsening.
