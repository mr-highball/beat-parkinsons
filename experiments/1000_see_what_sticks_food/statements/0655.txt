Statement: Consuming cold-pressed walnut oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: The walnut-oil milkshake trial measures laboratory fear extinction and has a secondary report of an inconclusive overall mood comparison. Cold-pressed walnut oil and Parkinson depressive symptoms remain unresolved.
