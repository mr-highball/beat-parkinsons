ID: 0030
Category: food
Ingredient: green tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed green tea worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The closest tea consumption studies have no verified brewed-tea comparison against olfactory scores. Two Parkinson studies record tea and hyposmia separately, with their joint analysis and preparation unverified. Smell tests that present tea as an odor measure a different exposure. The clinical direction remains open.
