ID: 0030
Category: food
Ingredient: green tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed green tea worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human study shows that brewed green tea alters olfactory impairment severity in Parkinson disease. The PENSA double-blind EGCG trial (PMID40664536) found null 12-month olfaction change (+0.1, 95% CI -1.3 to 1.5, p=.853), the Kagoshima Parkinson cohort (PMID42816813) found null cross-sectional hyposmia by green-tea frequency (p=.854) and volume (p=.519), CHRIS (PMID42830263) excluded green tea, acute coffee/caffeine crossovers were null, and mouse concentrated-extract nasal histopathology (NTP-TR-585) did not test smell function.
