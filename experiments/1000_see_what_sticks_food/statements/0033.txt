ID: 0033
Category: oil
Ingredient: sunflower oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined sunflower oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two additional oral comparator trials measured standardized depression scales outside Parkinson disease. One used high-oleic oil of unverified refining grade. The other used sunflower and fish oil together. The inhalation trial gave both groups the same sunflower carrier. The evidence leaves the effect of isolated oral refined sunflower oil on Parkinson depressive symptoms unresolved.
