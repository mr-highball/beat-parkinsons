ID: 0033
Category: oil
Ingredient: sunflower oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined sunflower oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson depression trials used mineral or corn oil comparators. The blood fatty-acid preprint measures circulating levels and cannot identify intake. Outside Parkinson, varied sunflower comparators lack an oil-free arm, and two newly indexed registrations have no posted mood results. The refined-oil weight-loss trial still lacks accessible arm-level depression data. Evidence leaves the effect of isolated oral refined sunflower oil on Parkinson depressive symptoms unresolved.
