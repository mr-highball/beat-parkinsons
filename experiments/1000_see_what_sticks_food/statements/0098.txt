Category: food
Ingredient: black beans
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled black beans worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson cohort associates daytime sleepiness with whole diet scores. The boiled black bean crossover measures vascular and metabolic responses. The MIND daytime dysfunction contrast is inconclusive, and the soybean extract studies concern task-day or waking-time measures in healthy adults. The effect of boiled black common beans on Parkinson daytime sleepiness remains unresolved.
