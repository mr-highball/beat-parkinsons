ID: 0160
Category: oil
Ingredient: soybean oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil reduces apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Oxford cohort assessed a separate Parkinson apathy item and used a diet instrument that can code soya oil. Its publication gives whole-diet estimates. The Ketoflex trial tests a full dietary program, and the soybean-oil comparator studies report aggregate NPI in Alzheimer disease. A refined soybean-oil effect on Parkinson apathy remains unresolved.
