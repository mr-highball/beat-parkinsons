ID: 0035
Category: spice
Ingredient: paprika
Preparation: smoked
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming smoked paprika worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Isolated oral capsaicin had divergent anxiety-like behavior findings in different rat models. A runner crossover shows an imprecise anxiety-event difference, and a human rocoto trial used another pepper species with insufficient group comparisons. Smoked paprika and clinical Parkinson anxiety remain untested.
