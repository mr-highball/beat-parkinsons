ID: 0137
Category: spice
Ingredient: sichuan pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground sichuan pepper worsens low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A mixed pepper-oil product improved reported conversation difficulty in xerostomia. A different experiment measured lower-lip tingling. The papers did not measure acoustic loudness or worsening Parkinson’s hypophonia after ground pepper.
