ID: 0338
Category: oil
Ingredient: grapeseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed grapeseed oil worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The 30-participant registry describes brief induced oral pain with a C60-in-grapeseed comparator and no posted results or verified pressing grade. The earlier lab use of a similar material does not establish product identity or a pain result. Topical and preclinical contexts do not isolate oral cold-pressed grapeseed oil in Parkinson disease. Two additional topical pain registries and two laboratory carrier publications supply formulation context. The herbal record describes a topical carrier. No source yields a clinical comparison of orally consumed cold-pressed oil.
