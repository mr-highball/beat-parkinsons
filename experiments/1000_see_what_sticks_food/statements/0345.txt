ID: 0345
Category: food
Ingredient: tomato
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked tomato reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Prepared tomato diets in animal studies yielded striatal biochemical results. Isolated lycopene studies measured other aspects of movement. Clinical reports distinguish gait speed and maximum step length from same-foot stride. The PRIME protocol plans direct stride measurement in a whole-diet intervention. Cooked tomato and shorter stride in Parkinson disease remain unresolved.
