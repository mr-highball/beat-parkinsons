ID: 0993
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed canola oil worsens freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Canola oil is a comparator in a healthy older-adult ketone-ester pilot and a menu constituent in the Parkinson diet trial. The 2024 conference study that measured freezing is a separate anxiety and sleep protocol. Existing gait comparators did not count freezing events.
