Category: food
Ingredient: salmon
Preparation: poached
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming poached salmon reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized trial in forensic inpatients reports lower state anxiety over time with Atlantic salmon versus meat control (PMID25431880, p=.043; trait anxiety inconclusive; grilling and boiling pooled). Competing trials show null anxiety after angina fish advice with optional fish oil (+0.82, 95% CI -0.57 to 2.22) and null SDQ emotional symptoms in FINS-TEENS mixed oily-fish lunches (high-baseline subgroup favored meat), while Parkinson cohorts (MVP-2024, Fox Insight, Lithuanian MedDiet) pool fish or whole diets without species or poaching estimates. The non-Parkinson salmon trial sustains a minimal_signal easing lean.
