Symptom: anxiety severity

Statement: Consuming poached salmon reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A randomized Atlantic salmon trial outside Parkinson disease found lower state anxiety over time, with no clear trait anxiety result. An independent Parkinson baseline non-fried fish item points toward lower anxiety. Species and poaching are unavailable in the Parkinson figure, and the salmon trial pooled grilling and boiling. This supports a minimal favorable lean.
