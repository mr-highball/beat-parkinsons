ID: 0668
Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined peanut oil reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A sarcopenia trial family used peanut oil and planned falls surveillance. No arm-specific falls result was recovered. A separate peanut butter trial improved a sit-to-stand test, with inconclusive gait speed and no verified fall-frequency comparison. Neither identifies a refined peanut-oil direction in Parkinson disease.
