ID: 0048
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Plasma ALA had no clear association with Parkinson finger tap or hand movement ratings. A flaxseed-oil trial outside Parkinson’s found no clear difference in a learned movement sequence. Neither tested manual dexterity after cold-pressed oil in Parkinson’s.
