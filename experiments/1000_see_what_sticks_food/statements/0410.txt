ID: 0410
Category: food
Ingredient: cranberry
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked cranberry worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The animal work measures corrective stepping or other motor proxies after juice or extract. A healthy-volunteer cranberry-juice registration gives no posted result or explicit bradykinesia measure. The cooked cranberry claim remains unresolved. The resveratrol review lists European cranberry without a cooked fruit assignment or bradykinesia comparison. The original aged-rat study reports a favorable inclined-screen latency result and no accelerating-rotarod diet comparison effect. These measure balance and strength or coordination and do not supply a bradykinesia contrast.
