ID: 0633
Category: herb
Ingredient: rosemary
Preparation: dried
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried rosemary worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Dried rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis) has no controlled clinical or preclinical resting-tremor comparison: a Parkinson vegetarian diet pilot (PMID:32041265) listed rosemary in a shared menu with aggregate UPDRS-III only, and isolated carnosic/rosmarinic acid rodent studies targeted levodopa dyskinesia (PMID:34434108) or general MPTP locomotion rather than rest tremor.
