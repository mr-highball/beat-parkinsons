ID: 0836
Category: spice
Ingredient: sichuan pepper
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted sichuan pepper worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An extract trial in healthy adults found faster acute attention and fewer false alarms alongside worse name-to-face accuracy. Healthy gamer registrations provide no results, and clinical Parkinson’s attention after toasted pepper remains unresolved.
