ID: 0836
Category: spice
Ingredient: sichuan pepper
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted sichuan pepper worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in 82 healthy adults aged 30-55 (Kennedy 2019, PMID31835620, PMC6950039, NCT03673930) reports faster acute Speed of Attention (p=.039) and fewer rapid visual information processing false alarms (p=.025) with 80 mg/day standardized Zanthoxylum armatum extract in MCT oil versus sunflower-oil placebo, while baseline false alarms were higher in the extract arm, name-to-face memory accuracy moved unfavorably (p=.040), and chronic secondary memory was the registered primary endpoint. Two healthy-gamer extract crossover registrations targeting attention speed and accuracy (NCT07047105, NCT07752329) have no posted results. Standardized extract versus culinary toasted Sichuan pepper, healthy non-Parkinson sample, mixed cognitive domain signs, and baseline imbalance keep the easing lean contradicting the worsening hypothesis at minimal_signal.
