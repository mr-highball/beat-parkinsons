ID: 0171
Category: spice
Ingredient: fennel seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted fennel seed worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized open-label dysmenorrhea trial (n=80) reports lower three-month menstrual weakness scores with fennel soft capsules versus no medication (6.65 to 2.88 vs 6.39 flat, p=.001; abstract prints a conflicting control value; PMID25085020). Two other dysmenorrhea trials were inconclusive for fatigue (essential oil overall p=.08 with differential discontinuation; 2007 extract p=.058 without reported direction), and crushed-seed infusion and honey-mixture surveys lack controlled fatigue data. Unblinded design, reporting conflict, null comparators, and non-Parkinson menstrual weakness keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal.
