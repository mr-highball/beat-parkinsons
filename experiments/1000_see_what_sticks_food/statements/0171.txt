Statement: Consuming toasted fennel seed worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One unblinded trial randomized 80 students and reports a favorable menstrual-weakness contrast for fennel soft capsules. An independent extraction reports 38 analyzed controls. Other fatigue results range from inconclusive comparisons to an author abstract for fixed-order fennel-drop treatment. Differences in preparation and population keep the inverse lean at the minimal tier.
