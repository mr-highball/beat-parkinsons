ID: 0171
Category: spice
Ingredient: fennel seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted fennel seed worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One unblinded trial randomized 80 students and reports a favorable menstrual-weakness contrast for fennel soft capsules. An independent extraction reports 38 analyzed controls. Other fatigue results range from inconclusive comparisons to an author abstract for fixed-order fennel-drop treatment. Differences in preparation and population keep the inverse lean at the minimal tier. The independent Delaram fennel-drops trial reports a pooled emotional domain including fatigue. Its overall between-group test and mixed domain do not identify a fatigue effect. The original dysmenorrhea trial's results report 384 participants in three analysis groups despite crossover wording in the methods. Fatigue appears in background only. The extracts and pain results add no measurement of toasted fennel seed or Parkinson fatigue severity. The indexed publisher table gives 4/36 versus 5/36 before treatment and 8/36 versus 8/36 afterward. The printed p values are .147 and .21. The model behind them is unverified and the paper's placebo differs from the registry mirror's active control.
