Category: spice
Ingredient: fennel seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted fennel seed worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Fennel oil shifted menstrual fatigue ratings toward lower severity (overall p=.08), and a separate capsule study found lower menstrual weakness against no medication. A 2007 extract abstract gives fatigue p=.058 without a direction. These indirect results weakly oppose worsening, with substantial uncertainty about toasted seed and Parkinson fatigue.
