Category: food
Ingredient: button mushroom
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted button mushroom worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The whole UV-cremini trial found an inconclusive RBANS attention interaction (p = .705) without a roasting assignment. The mixed button-and-oyster feeding trial found inconclusive attention comparisons and control-only improvement on a separate delayed list-recall task. Roasted button mushrooms and Parkinson executive impairment remain unresolved.
