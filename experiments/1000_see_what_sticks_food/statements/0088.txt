ID: 0088
Category: spice
Ingredient: mustard seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground mustard seed reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson self-experiment included ground white mustard in broccoli tea and described balance, with no recorded fall count or mustard-only comparison. Black mustard extract changed animal motor tests without a clinical fall-frequency measure. The bibliographic lead resolves to a historical whole-seed pamphlet and its review without a fall measure. A verified 2018 external seed acupressure trial shows inconclusive mobility, balance and fear-of-falling group comparisons. Neither supplies an oral ground-seed fall-count estimate.
