Statement: Consuming cold-pressed walnut oil worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: The oil improved climbing in a toxin-exposed fly model, and oil alone reduced cell viability at two higher doses in a separate model. Neither measures dyskinesia after oral cold-pressed walnut oil in Parkinson disease.
