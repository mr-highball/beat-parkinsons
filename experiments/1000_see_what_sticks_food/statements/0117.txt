Category: spice
Ingredient: sichuan pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground sichuan pepper reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in 82 healthy adults aged 30-55 (Kennedy 2019, PMID31835620, PMC6950039, NCT03673930) reports lower day-56 cognitive-task mental fatigue with 80 mg/day standardized Zanthoxylum armatum extract in MCT oil versus sunflower-oil placebo (p=.006), although pretreatment mental fatigue was already higher in the extract arm and primary registered endpoint was chronic secondary memory. Two healthy-gamer extract crossover registrations with fatigue outcomes (NCT07047105, NCT07752329) have no posted results. Standardized extract versus culinary ground Sichuan pepper, non-Parkinson healthy sample, and baseline imbalance keep the easing signal at minimal_signal. The primary day-56 analysis adjusts to each participant's day-1 pre-dose score, and Figure 8 has 39 analyzed per arm. Baseline imbalance remains a limitation. The multicomponent formula describes customary use for fatigue without isolating Zanthoxylum.
