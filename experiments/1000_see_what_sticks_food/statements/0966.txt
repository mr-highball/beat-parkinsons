ID: 0966
Category: oil
Ingredient: soybean oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A synuclein mouse study reports forepaw stride variability of 15.76% with control diet and 10.18% with curcumin. Each diet contained the same amount of soybean oil. The Angelman-model experiment also held soybean oil constant between two blends. A separate ordinal stepping comparison uses spinal injury and provides no stride distance.
