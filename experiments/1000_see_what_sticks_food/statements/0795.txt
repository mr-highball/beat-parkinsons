Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: ground
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground sage reduces olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson diagnostic test used dried sage as an odor. A separate common-sage oil aromatherapy study required intact smell and measured satisfaction. The effect of consuming ground sage on clinical olfaction remains unknown.
