Category: herb
Ingredient: bay leaf
Preparation: ground
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground bay leaf reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A leaf-extract Parkinson model measured neuron histology and a separate leaf-oil seizure study reported motor impairment. Neither comparison measured stride length after culinary intake.
