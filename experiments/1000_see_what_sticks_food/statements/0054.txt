Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A before-and-after whole-seed study reported conflicting Epworth significance, with a 0.18-point mean decrease in students. There is no isolated cold-pressed oil comparison for daytime sleepiness in Parkinson’s.
