ID: 0329
Category: food
Ingredient: egg
Preparation: poached
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached egg worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A Parkinson baseline chart shows a near-zero inverse egg frequency and falls association. An independent unadjusted older-adult comparison showed slightly lower egg intake among fracture-producing fall cases, with p = .07 and no adjusted egg estimate. Taken together these provide a minimal lean against worsening. Neither comparison resolves poaching or causation.
