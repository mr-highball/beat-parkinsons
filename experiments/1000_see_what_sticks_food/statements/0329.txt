ID: 0329
Category: food
Ingredient: egg
Preparation: poached
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached egg worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled or confounding-adjusted study estimates how eating poached eggs affects fall frequency in Parkinson disease or outside it. The Parkinson baseline figure (MVP-2024) plots only an unquantified near-zero inverse tile for unspecified egg frequency without coefficient, uncertainty interval, or poaching identity. An older-adult case-control study (PMID36915191) reports 2.03 versus 2.21 weekly egg portions in fall-with-fracture cases versus controls (unadjusted p=.07) with no preparation-specific or adjusted fall-frequency estimate. Nutri-Park compared protein timing rather than eggs, and remaining dietary or metabolic records omit fall counts; the claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
