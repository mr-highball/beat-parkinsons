ID: 0096
Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson survey measured cayenne frequency without an anxiety-specific result. A student survey associated frequent spicy-food intake with anxiety and did not identify ground cayenne. Related mixed-meal and extract studies and public accounts leave the exact ingredient effect unresolved. Public accounts describe acute distraction or perceived relief with oral contact or unspecified formulation. A multi-ingredient poisoning report describes anxiety, and clinical cough challenge papers use capsaicin only as a provocation measure. The new experimental stimulation system combines delivery and external stimulation. None isolates ground cayenne and measured Parkinson anxiety.
