Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: In the human Parkinson baseline graphic, more olive oil is weakly associated with less pooled self-reported tremor. The oil grade, separate rest component, precision and confounding are unresolved. Trials of mixed diets describe both tremor or rigidity worsening and inconclusive overall motor results. Injected extra-virgin oil has favorable motor-proxy results in mice without a rest score. The human symptom signal provides a minimal easing lean for the frozen hypothesis.
