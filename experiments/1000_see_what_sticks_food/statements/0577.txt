ID: 0577
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: extra-virgin
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Extra-virgin olive oil appears in one uncontrolled mixed-diet case. One diet crossover reported mild tremor worsening in both periods without identifying position or oil grade. The Dutch BrAIN crossover reports no significant change in the overall motor score with mixed-diet olive oil. Other trials use shared oil or a virgin-oil comparator. MED-PARK has no posted results. A separate effect on rest tremor remains unresolved.
