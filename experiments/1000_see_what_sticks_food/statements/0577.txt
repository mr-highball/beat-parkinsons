ID: 0577
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: extra-virgin
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A baseline graphic gives a weak inverse estimate for pooled tremor with ungraded olive oil. A whole ketogenic diet reports early worsening in a combined tremor-or-rigidity measure. A cannabinoid randomized trial reports only a composite motor result, and a six-person series gives baseline tremor only. Neither resolves a rest-tremor effect for extra-virgin olive oil.
