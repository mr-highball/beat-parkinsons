ID: 0577
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: extra-virgin
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An uncontrolled Parkinson case included extra-virgin olive oil and reported little tremor improvement after additional dietary changes. A pooled tremor association, a combined tremor-or-rigidity adverse-event measure, and trials using olive oil as a comparator or vehicle leave the isolated rest-tremor effect unresolved.
