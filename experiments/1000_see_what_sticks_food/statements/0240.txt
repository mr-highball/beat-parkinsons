ID: 0240
Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: grilled
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grilled bell pepper worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Tianjin journal study included a timed 4 m walk within its composite sarcopenia analysis. It reports no separate pepper and gait coefficient. Parkinson studies report a diet-wide motor or mobility comparison, and isolated capsiate produced mixed findings for timed running. The grilled-pepper effect on Parkinson walking remains open.
