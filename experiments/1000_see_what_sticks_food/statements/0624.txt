ID: 0624
Category: oil
Ingredient: safflower oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed safflower oil reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Friedreich ataxia trial reports falls among recipients of USP safflower oil and active RT001. The progressive supranuclear palsy trial has no posted falls results. Neither verifies a cold-pressed preparation or a Parkinson fall-frequency effect.
