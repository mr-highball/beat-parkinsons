ID: 0879
Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw bell pepper worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A small randomized trial found a favorable immediate postexercise fatigue contrast for purified red paprika xanthophyll in athletes. The later daily-fatigue registration has no posted outcome values. The measured comparisons do not isolate raw bell pepper or Parkinson fatigue. The completed acute crossover specifies fatigue in a cayenne add-on supplement comparison and has no posted results. The single-group registry reports favorable tiredness ratings after a multi-ingredient product. No component-specific change or concurrent control is reported. The latest public UMIN record still has no reported everyday-fatigue arm values. Twenty-three were enrolled in a healthy-adult trial of a paprika pigment extract. The older ministry mirror's 30 figure is the recruitment target. The available records leave the daily-fatigue comparison open.
