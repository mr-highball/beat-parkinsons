ID: 0272
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An isolated-caffeine crossover reported earlier walking response to levodopa without defining a gait-speed endpoint in the accessible abstract. Separate examiner gait scores in the placebo trial were inconclusive. The tea surveys and cohort analyses report other endpoints or grouped exposures. The three original source contexts supply no measured brewed-black-tea walking effect in Parkinson disease.
