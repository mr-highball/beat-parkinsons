ID: 0272
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: LRRK2 cohorts measured composite motor scores and a controlled caffeine study found an inconclusive gait-item contrast. Rodent extract studies reported rotation, locomotion and motion-test duration. Brewed-black-tea walking speed in Parkinson disease remains unresolved.
