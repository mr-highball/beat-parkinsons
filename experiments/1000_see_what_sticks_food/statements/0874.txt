ID: 0874
Category: food
Ingredient: trout
Preparation: poached
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached trout worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: The randomized trout feeding study numerically favored lower GDS-15 depression scores (adjusted difference −0.17, 95% CI −0.48 to 0.15). Parkinson fish-oil pilots favored clinician-rated depression, while broader fish and supplement results were mixed. These findings give a minimal lean against worsening. The trout trial did not specify poaching or enroll a Parkinson cohort.
