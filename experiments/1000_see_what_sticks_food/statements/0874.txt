ID: 0874
Category: food
Ingredient: trout
Preparation: poached
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached trout worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled trial shows that eating poached trout affects depressive symptom severity. The only randomized trout feeding trial (JGG2017-SHAPOURI-113, n=96 older adults) found a null adjusted GDS-15 difference (-0.17, 95% CI -0.48 to 0.15, p=.295) without specifying poaching or Parkinson disease. Angina fatty-fish advice had an interval crossing zero, and marine omega-3 supplement evidence is contradictory across small unspecified-species Parkinson pilots and VITAL-DEP (PMID34932079, higher incident depression with null mean PHQ-8). The SU.VI.MAX fatty-fish group includes trout and gives opposing recurrent prescription associations by smoking status. A serum analysis from the same parent cohort is inconclusive overall. These proxy endpoints and grouped exposures supply no defensible direction for the frozen claim.
