Category: spice
Ingredient: nigella seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nigella seed worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: The open-label randomized Nigella oil comparison reported less fatigue during acute COVID, and an extract experiment favored endurance. The closer ground-seed trial gave mixed results and the pooled formulation trial was inconclusive. The direction leans weakly against worsening, with uncertain transfer from oil and acute symptom presence to Parkinson fatigue severity.
