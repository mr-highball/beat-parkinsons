Category: spice
Ingredient: nigella seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nigella seed worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One open-label randomized trial in acute COVID-19 reports fewer participants with day-4 fatigue after oral Nigella sativa seed oil versus control (12/30 vs 25/30, adjusted OR 0.15, 95% CI 0.04 to 0.48; PMID36425571). Competing randomized evidence includes closer ground-nigella-seed trials showing mixed/null day-10 fatigue (PMID36142070) and null physical PMS symptoms (PMID41267796), plus a null nigella-flax multiple-sclerosis fatigue trial (DOI10.34172/hpp.44662). Seed oil, open-label design, acute infection binary presence, and absence of Parkinson fatigue trials keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal.
