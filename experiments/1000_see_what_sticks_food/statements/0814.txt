Preparation: fresh
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh basil reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Sweet-basil leaf extract reduced insomnia scores in menopausal participants, while an independent dried-basil postpartum trial was inconclusive. Isolated myrcene animal reports used drug-induced sleep and varied by exposure schedule. A minimal favorable lean relies on the human basil extract comparison and limited transfer to fresh basil and Parkinson disease.
