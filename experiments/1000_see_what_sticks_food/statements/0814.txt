ID: 0814
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh basil reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized non-Parkinson menopause trial reports lower one-month insomnia scores with sweet-basil (Ocimum basilicum) leaf extract versus placebo (IRCT20200104046001N1, n=60, 5.6 vs 11.0, p<.001). An independent triple-blind postpartum trial of dried sweet-basil leaf powder versus placebo found no clear total sleep-quality difference (IRCT20220926056046N2, 17.63 vs 16.15, adjusted p=.400), and injected isolated myrcene assays in mice measured barbiturate-induced sleep with opposite acute versus repeated timing effects. Absence of fresh culinary basil and Parkinson insomnia trials plus the null postpartum trial keep the easing lean at minimal_signal.
