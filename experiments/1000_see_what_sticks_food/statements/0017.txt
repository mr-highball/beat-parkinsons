Symptom: shortened stride severity

Statement: Consuming grilled trout reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Injected DHA lengthened strides in lesioned rats. In older adults without Parkinson disease, fish oil plus training versus olive oil plus training gave time-by-group p = .753 for single-task stride and .537 for dual-task stride. The roles of oral whole trout and grilling remain unresolved.
