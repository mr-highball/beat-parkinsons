Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The urinary trial with extra-virgin oil as a comparator ended with no posted results. An unrelated saw-palmetto paper and registry disagree about their placebo. Parkinson diet-pattern cohorts use broad urinary measures without an isolated oil exposure. The enriched-oil prostate pilot reports an inconclusive aggregate symptom comparison. Urgency frequency after culinary extra-virgin oil remains unresolved.
