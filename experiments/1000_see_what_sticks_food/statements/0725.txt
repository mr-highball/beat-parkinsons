ID: 0725
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: grated
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated ginger reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Parkinson interviews and controlled whole-rhizome comparisons support a minimal favorable lean. The fresh-drink and exercise-versus-tea reports give conflicting pain data. The separate tea study reports favorable post-dose ratings with unknown root processing, and the survey has ambiguous ingredient labels. Capsule results are mixed. Preparation, diagnosis and pain differences limit the lean for grated ginger.
