Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil worsens apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study isolates virgin coconut oil for apathy severity. The Parkinson baseline figure (MVP-2024) plots only an unquantified tile for motivation/initiative without oil grade, adjustment, or error limits. The Parkinson ketogenic MCT feeding study (PMID38561682) found no clear between-group diary apathy difference without isolating virgin coconut oil, and KIM (NCT05469997) reported no post-diet apathy comparison. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
