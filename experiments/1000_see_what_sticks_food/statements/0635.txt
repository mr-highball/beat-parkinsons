Statement: Consuming raw pomegranate arils worsens visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson seed oil study is still awaiting outcome results. In the separate stroke trial, a reported episode was auditory and began before any pomegranate supplement. The transplant case remains access-limited and no raw-aril visual-frequency outcome was identified.
