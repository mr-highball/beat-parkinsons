ID: 0439
Category: oil
Ingredient: soybean oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Capsule swallowing discomfort occurred in a design with soybean oil in both arms. Soybean-oil gels have measured texture properties but no observed patient swallowing effect or refined-oil comparison.
