Symptom: fatigue severity

Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A randomized refined sesame versus sunflower oil study favors sesame for fatigue outside Parkinson disease. The original abstract does not identify the statistical contrast or provide arm values. A Parkinson open-label cannabidiol trial reported fatigue with a sesame vehicle and lacks an oil comparator. The human oil comparison gives a minimal lean against worsening, with cold-pressed sesame and Parkinson fatigue unresolved.
