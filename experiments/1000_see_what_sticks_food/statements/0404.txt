Category: oil
Ingredient: sesame oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized weight-loss diet trial comparing refined sesame oil against refined sunflower oil favors sesame on fatigue in its abstract (p=.002; DOI10.1108/NFS-12-2022-0407), though exact contrast definitions, arm means, an oil-free arm, and cold-pressed grade remain unavailable. An open-label Parkinson cannabidiol trial logged fatigue in 8/13 participants taking sesame-oil carrier without an oil comparator (PMID33381646). The human oral comparison sustains only a minimal_signal against worsening.
