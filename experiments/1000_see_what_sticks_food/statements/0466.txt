ID: 0466
Category: food
Ingredient: peach
Preparation: raw
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw peach reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In 176 people with Parkinson disease, correct identification of a peach odor had an inconclusive association with a composite urinary symptom score after correction (q = .371). The study did not measure raw peach intake or a separate urgency-frequency response.
