Statement: Consuming steamed carrot reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Carrot juice served as comparator in an older-adult walking study and its usual gait-speed change was inconclusive. Purple carrot extracts affect other motor outcomes in rats. Provider and collection sources did not identify steamed carrot intake and a measured Parkinson stride length.
