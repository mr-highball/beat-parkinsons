ID: 0196
Category: food
Ingredient: banana
Preparation: raw
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw banana reduces apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson trial of a personalized plant-rich diet reported a favorable apathy change, with inconsistent reporting of the final group comparison and no banana-specific exposure. Controlled banana feeding measured peripheral biomarkers. Earlier vigor and fatigue ratings were inconclusive, and the diagnostic odor association concerns smell identification. A measured apathy effect of eating raw banana remains unresolved.
