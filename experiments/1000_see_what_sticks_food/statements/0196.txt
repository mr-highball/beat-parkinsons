Statement: Consuming raw banana reduces apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A small banana trial outside Parkinson disease found inconclusive vigor and fatigue mood changes. The Parkinson banana odor and apathy association used odor identification, and earlier feeding and levodopa sources give no attributable apathy measurement after eating raw banana.
