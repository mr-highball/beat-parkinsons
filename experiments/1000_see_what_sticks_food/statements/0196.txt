ID: 0196
Category: food
Ingredient: banana
Preparation: raw
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw banana reduces apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A personalized plant-rich Parkinson diet trial (PMID:41666212) reported apathy change without isolating raw banana, while a small open whole-banana trial outside Parkinson disease (DOI:10.12691/jfnr-10-12-2) reported inconclusive POMS2 vigor and fatigue subscales without an apathy instrument; raw banana effects on Parkinson apathy remain open.
