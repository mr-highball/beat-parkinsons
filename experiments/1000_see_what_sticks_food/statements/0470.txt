ID: 0470
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: The registered hemodialysis trial found lower Rome III scores within all three oil groups, with an inconclusive interaction and unverified flaxseed pressing grade. The lipid trial reports few constipation adverse events. A rodent comparison used solvent-extracted oil and wet stool, with a nonmonotonic response. The human oil observations provide a minimal lean against worsening, with transfer to Parkinson disease unresolved.
