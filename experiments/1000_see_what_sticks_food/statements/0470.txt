ID: 0470
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled comparison against an inactive control isolates cold-pressed flaxseed oil for constipation severity. The registered hemodialysis trial (NCT02103556, PMID25238699) compared flaxseed, olive, and active laxative mineral oil (n=50) and found an inconclusive between-oil interaction (p=.15) with unverified pressing grade; a lipid trial (sjams.2018.v06i01.047) logged 2/57 vs 5/55 constipation adverse events against liquid paraffin without severity scoring; plasma ALA was unrelated to Wexner constipation (PMID34127758); and petroleum-ether extracted oil gave a nonmonotonic rodent wet-stool response. Consistent with claim 0072, the claim moves from legacy 51 to open. Two placebo or active-control studies report bowel improvements from flaxseed baked into cookies or prepared as flour. These seed-containing foods provide no isolated flaxseed oil comparison.
