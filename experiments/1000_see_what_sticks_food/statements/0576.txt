Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed avocado oil reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Daytime sleepiness rose similarly after avocado placebo and CBD in healthy adults without an unexposed group. A mixed-oil fibromyalgia placebo recorded somnolence. Whole fruit increased reported nighttime sleep duration in HAT, and the Brazilian oil trial does not report sleepiness separately. Cold-pressed oil in Parkinson disease remains untested.
