ID: 0639
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Two single-blind oral flaxseed-oil reports from one investigator group show lower rest and movement arthritis pain. Cohort independence, dose and processing are uncertain. Other rheumatoid arthritis and postoperative knee comparisons were inconclusive, and Parkinson combination trials did not supply pain items. The oral pain results support a minimal favorable lean across conditions.
