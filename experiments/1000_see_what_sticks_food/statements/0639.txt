ID: 0639
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One single-blind oral flaxseed-oil osteoarthritis trial (DOI10.36347/sjams.2019.v07i09.031) and a potentially overlapping rheumatoid-arthritis report from the same investigators (Vyas 2020, independence uncertain) report lower rest and movement arthritis pain, though pressing grade is unreported and dosing/numeric reporting is inconsistent. Competing trials show null pain contrasts for flaxseed versus corn oil after ACL surgery (p=.96), versus linoleic acid in rheumatoid arthritis (n=22), and versus vitamin E in cyclic mastalgia (p=.142), while Parkinson flaxseed-omega-3 plus vitamin-E trials omit pain items. The single-blind oral arthritis pain comparison retains only a minimal_signal easing lean.
