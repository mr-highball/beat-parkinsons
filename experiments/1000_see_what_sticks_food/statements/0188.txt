ID: 0188
Category: food
Ingredient: fig
Preparation: raw
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw fig reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A child developed immediate drooling during a fresh-fig allergic reaction. Fig-and-almond syrup and an almond–raisin comparator had similar drooling counts in children receiving methylphenidate. The Parkinson surgery series describes less drooling in two patients who continued infusion. The effect of raw figs on Parkinson drooling severity remains uncertain.
