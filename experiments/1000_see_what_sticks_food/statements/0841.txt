ID: 0841
Category: herb
Ingredient: lovage leaf
Preparation: dried
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried lovage leaf reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two independent urinary infection cohorts reported lower symptom scores after a lovage-root, rosemary and centaury product. One is uncontrolled and the other compares the blend with fosfomycin. The available urgency results concern symptom severity, and neither cohort measured episodes after dried lovage leaf in Parkinson disease.
