Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted carrot reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Carrot-juice controls had inconclusive Trail Making B change in older adults. A multicomponent capsule containing carrot had favorable within-period TESEN changes in healthy adults. The purple carrot extract model used an animal memory task. Roasted carrot and Parkinson executive impairment remain unresolved.
