ID: 0593
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: ground
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground sage reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The historical account reports acute urge sensations without a controlled comparison. One common-sage extract trial reports an inconclusive urogenital composite and another an inconclusive urinary leakage item (p=.369). Neither discusses separate urgency frequency. Two different-species root extracts have inconclusive week-12 urgency items. Traditional common-sage renal use includes diuretic purposes, without measured symptom change.
