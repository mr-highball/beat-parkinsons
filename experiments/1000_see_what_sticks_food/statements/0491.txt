Preparation: dried
Symptom: visual hallucination frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried celery leaf reduces visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The original celery case series used a mixed product without recording visual hallucination frequency. A seed-extract animal study reports behavioral proxies with conflicting dose labels. Root interaction and leaf thyroid cases do not isolate dried celery leaf, and pharmaceutical NBP is a distinct exposure. A dried stem-and-leaf trial reported general tolerability without measuring hallucinations. No defensible direction for the frozen symptom.
