Category: herb
Ingredient: celery leaf
Preparation: dried
Symptom: visual hallucination frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried celery leaf reduces visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The primary celery-root abstract confirms mania with concurrent venlafaxine and St Johns wort. Later reports leave the original hallucination modality and frequency unresolved. The dried stalk-and-leaf trial registered metabolic outcomes and monitored general side effects. A seed-extract trial recorded mild events, and the Parkinson pharmaceutical trial supplies only aggregate PDQ-39 scores. Clinical evidence leaves the frozen dried leaf and Parkinson visual hallucination question open.
