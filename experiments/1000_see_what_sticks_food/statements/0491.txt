ID: 0491
Category: herb
Ingredient: celery leaf
Preparation: dried
Symptom: visual hallucination frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried celery leaf reduces visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study measures visual hallucination frequency after consuming dried celery leaf in Parkinson disease. The celery-root mania case (PMID34370637) involved concurrent venlafaxine and St. John's wort without visual-frequency counts; a Parkinson butylphthalide trial (PMID30988766) reported only aggregate PDQ-39 scores with inconsistent adverse-event totals; and celery-seed safety (NCT06061926), Alzheimer composite (Gu 2024), and thyroid case reports omit visual hallucination frequency.
