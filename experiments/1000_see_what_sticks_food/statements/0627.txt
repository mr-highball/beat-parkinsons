Category: food
Ingredient: wheat berries
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked wheat berries reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cooked wheat berries have no isolated Parkinson resting-tremor comparison: a 57-person Parkinson gluten-free diet trial (NCT05238545) gave an inconclusive composite motor result without isolating whole wheat berries or rest tremor, and celiac withdrawal case reports mix diagnoses and medication changes.
