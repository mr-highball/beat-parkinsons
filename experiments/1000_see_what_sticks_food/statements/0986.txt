ID: 0986
Category: spice
Ingredient: nutmeg
Preparation: ground
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nutmeg worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Poison-center and high-intake reports describe unspecified tremor, intention tremor, myoclonus or seizure. Other original cases do not document tremor or a separate rest examination. Exact findings after ordinary ground-nutmeg intake in people with Parkinson disease remain unresolved.
