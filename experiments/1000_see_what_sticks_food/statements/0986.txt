ID: 0986
Category: spice
Ingredient: nutmeg
Preparation: ground
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nutmeg worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Upper-limb tremor followed excess nutmeg in one report with tremor posture unspecified. The potentially overlapping Spanish reports describe intention tremor with conflicting medication histories, and a poison-center recorder judged a recorded tremor unrelated to exposure. Injected mouse extract studies report locomotor effects. Ordinary ground nutmeg and Parkinson rest-tremor severity remain unresolved.
