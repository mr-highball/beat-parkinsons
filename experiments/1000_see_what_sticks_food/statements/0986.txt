ID: 0986
Category: spice
Ingredient: nutmeg
Preparation: ground
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nutmeg worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Ground nutmeg has no culinary-dose Parkinson resting-tremor trial: poison-center series and accidental overdose reports (PMID:39559662, PMID:24452991, PMID:24998027, PMID:26219944, DOI:10.3166/afmu-2022-0380) document acute toxicosis with mixed upper-limb or intention tremor alongside delirium or hyponatremia rather than culinary intake or Parkinson rest tremor, while a haloperidol mouse extract study (Palupi 2024) tested rotarod/cylinder only.
