ID: 0986
Category: spice
Ingredient: nutmeg
Preparation: ground
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nutmeg worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Poison-center and high-intake reports describe unspecified tremor, intention tremor, or myoclonus. A separate case mentions tremor in its discussion and records an unremarkable neurological examination. An authenticated overdose letter describes a seizure without specifying a tremor phenotype. Parkinson rest tremor after ordinary culinary ground-nutmeg intake remains unresolved.
