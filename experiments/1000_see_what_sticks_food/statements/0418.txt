ID: 0418
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Developmental rats receiving flaxseed oil as part of an omega-3-adequate diet made fewer odor discrimination errors than deficient rats. A separate mouse model with other oils found no behavioral effect. Neither result establishes a clinical cold-pressed oil effect in Parkinson’s.
