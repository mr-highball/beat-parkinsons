ID: 0042
Category: spice
Ingredient: nutmeg
Preparation: grated
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grated nutmeg worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A high-dose misuse case reports recurrent dysphoria and psychosis in a person with a prior bipolar diagnosis. Rodent extract studies reported less immobility. The clinical relevance of either result to ordinary grated nutmeg and Parkinson depression remains unresolved.
