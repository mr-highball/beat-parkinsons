ID: 0042
Category: spice
Ingredient: nutmeg
Preparation: grated
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grated nutmeg worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A high-dose misuse case reports recurrent dysphoria and psychosis in a person with a prior bipolar diagnosis. Rodent extract studies reported less immobility. The clinical relevance of either result to ordinary grated nutmeg and Parkinson depression remains unresolved. The eugenol review reports nutmeg as one possible constituent source, but the mood trail tests purified eugenol, not grated nutmeg. Irie and Keung describe less immobility and Tao and colleagues compare eugenol and analogs, with overlapping authors and unknown independence of all behavioral work. No comparable nutmeg composition, oral intake or Parkinson depression outcome closes the claim.
