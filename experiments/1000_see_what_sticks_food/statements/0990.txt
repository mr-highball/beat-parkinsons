Ingredient: blackberry

Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw blackberry reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: BerryCare measured an eight-foot walk at baseline and documented fresh berry intake separately. Attrition prevented a before-and-after comparison. Rat plank performance concerns coordination, and no Parkinson walking-speed estimate followed raw blackberry intake.
