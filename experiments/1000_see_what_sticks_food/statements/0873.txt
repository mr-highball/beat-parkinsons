Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Flaxseed table oil has a small healthy-adult fluency difference and an inconclusive total frontal battery. A pooled omega-3 cohort has faster global cognitive decline without a separate oil estimate. A new executive-composite registry has no results. These measures and exposures leave the frozen comparison unresolved.
