ID: 0145
Category: oil
Ingredient: sunflower oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sunflower oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Anxiety values and the exact statistical contrast are unavailable for the refined sunflower and sesame comparison. A separate depression trial combines oral sunflower oil with positive suggestion and reports composite well-being including relaxation, without an isolated anxiety outcome. Neither study identifies a cold-pressed sunflower effect on Parkinson anxiety.
