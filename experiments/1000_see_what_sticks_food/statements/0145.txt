ID: 0145
Category: oil
Ingredient: sunflower oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sunflower oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Anxiety values and the exact statistical contrast are unavailable for the refined sunflower and sesame comparison. A separate depression trial combines oral sunflower oil with positive suggestion and reports composite well-being including relaxation, without an isolated anxiety outcome. Neither study identifies a cold-pressed sunflower effect on Parkinson anxiety. The original Labrousse combined diets both include sunflower oil. Pressing method and anxiety are outside the reported comparison. The reviewed cannabinoid patent mentions sunflower oil as a vehicle. It gives no cold-pressed oil comparison or anxiety estimate.
