Ingredient: lentils

Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming canned lentils worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Mixed Parkinson menus listed lentils and measured broad motor scores or gait speed without an isolated intake comparison. The red lentil rat experiment used injected extract and imposed-posture scoring. Walking slowness after canned lentils remains unresolved.
