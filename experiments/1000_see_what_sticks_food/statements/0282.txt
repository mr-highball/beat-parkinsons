Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground black pepper worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A concentrated pepper extract partly countered acute caffeine-related sleep disruption in mice. Human studies used leaves or botanical mixtures, without isolating ground peppercorns. Generic spice frequency has only a near-zero baseline association with Parkinson insomnia.
