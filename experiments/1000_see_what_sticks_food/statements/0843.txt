Ingredient: avocado oil

Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined avocado oil reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A human metabolic syndrome trial found no clear blood-pressure difference between avocado and soybean oils. Rat studies report lower pressure in hypertensive animals, with no clear change in normotensive rats. Refined oil and Parkinson orthostatic symptoms remain open.
