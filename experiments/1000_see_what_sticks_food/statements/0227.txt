ID: 0227
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: cracked
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cracked black pepper reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A powder capsule pilot showed inconclusively slower upper-gut transit. Piperine and extract experiments give mixed bowel proxies. The human mixed-tablet pilot has conflicting counts and no controls. Cracked black pepper and clinical Parkinson constipation remain unresolved. Related cultural/experiential records (1) provide uncontrolled context without changing the open efficacy rating. The autism review mentions constipation as clinical context and piperine in a separate developmental-mouse model. The primary piperine abstract has no constipation endpoint. No cracked-black-pepper Parkinson result. A separate uncontrolled multi-herb study with black pepper and senna reports within-person improvement. The effect of cracked black pepper remains undefined.
