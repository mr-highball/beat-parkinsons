Preparation: cracked
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cracked black pepper reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A powder capsule pilot showed inconclusively slower upper-gut transit. Piperine and extract experiments give mixed bowel proxies. The human mixed-tablet pilot has conflicting counts and no controls. Cracked black pepper and clinical Parkinson constipation remain unresolved.
