ID: 0687
Category: herb
Ingredient: cilantro
Preparation: dried
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried cilantro reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized placebo-controlled 30-day trial in 86 university students (NCT05999071, DOI10.1108/nfs-08-2023-0193) reports lower anxiety with dried powdered Coriandrum sativum seed capsules versus starch placebo (p=.04), although retrospective registration after completion, shifting anxiety from secondary to primary, and absent posted arm-level data limit reliability. Supporting animal evidence shows aqueous and oil fractions of hydroethanolic cilantro leaf extract produced anxiolytic signals in mouse elevated plus maze and light-dark box tests without rotarod deficit (DOI10.18203/2319-2003.ijbcp20202936), whereas an earlier dried-cilantro-leaf mouse study (PMID26109787) is unusable for direction due to internal abstract-versus-methods route and extraction conflicts. Seed-versus-leaf plant-part differences, non-Parkinson population, and reporting gaps keep the easing signal at minimal_signal.
