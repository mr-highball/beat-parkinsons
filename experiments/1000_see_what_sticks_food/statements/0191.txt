Category: spice
Ingredient: sichuan pepper
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted sichuan pepper reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Six Parkinson’s participants reported easier defecation and higher bowel frequency during uncontrolled treatment with a formula containing Japanese pepper. The study gives no effect estimate for toasted Sichuan pepper.
