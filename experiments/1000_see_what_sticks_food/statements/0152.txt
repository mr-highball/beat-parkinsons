Category: food
Ingredient: kiwi fruit
Preparation: raw
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw kiwi fruit worsens freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Hardy kiwi juice improved selected motor tests in toxin-exposed mice. Common green kiwi juice reduced spontaneous activity in a separate ordinary mouse study. Neither measured gait-freezing frequency after eating raw kiwi.
