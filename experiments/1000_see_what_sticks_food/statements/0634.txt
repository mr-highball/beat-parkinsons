ID: 0634
Category: herb
Ingredient: oregano
Preparation: dried
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried oregano worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The ground, dried oregano intervention in soldiers measured exercise biomarkers without an anxiety outcome. A depleted Syrian oregano comparator gives no contrast against absence of oregano. Leaf infusion and isolated constituents changed some animal behaviors with motor or preparation confounding. No defensible direction for dried oregano and Parkinson anxiety.
