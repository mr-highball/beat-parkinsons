ID: 0634
Category: herb
Ingredient: oregano
Preparation: dried
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried oregano worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Reported oregano use in a Parkinson cohort lacks an ingredient-specific anxiety estimate. A trial of strained sweet-marjoram tea in Parkinson disease reports aggregate nonmotor and depression outcomes. Outside Parkinson, marjoram-blend massage had no clear anxiety advantage over carrier massage, and two registry outcomes are pending. The exact dried oregano and Parkinson anxiety effect remains unresolved.
