ID: 0025
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh sage worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Two placebo-controlled oral sage extract trials report less menopausal joint and muscular discomfort, with incomplete or inconsistent item estimates. An unusually high plant intake case and a small dried-extract adverse-event count raise a separate abdominal pain concern. Neither measures fresh culinary sage or pain severity in Parkinson disease. The controlled discomfort results retain a minimal lean against worsening.
