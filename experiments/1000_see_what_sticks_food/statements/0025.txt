ID: 0025
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh sage worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Two placebo-controlled oral common-sage extract trials reported lower menopausal joint and muscular discomfort. One used fresh-tip extract with a per-protocol result and no item effect size. The dry-extract trial reports a favorable item comparison with inconsistent numbers. Local throat and surgical comparisons do not establish a culinary effect. The two oral results give a minimal lean against worsening, with no fresh-leaf Parkinson pain comparison.
