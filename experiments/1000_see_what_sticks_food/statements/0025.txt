ID: 0025
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh sage worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Concentrated common-sage spray reduced acute sore-throat pain versus its control. A surgery comparison favored benzydamine over sage rinse. The palliative sage-rinse study recorded oral pain at baseline and omitted it from comparisons. High-dose animal safety observations use extract. Fresh dietary sage and clinical Parkinson pain remain unresolved.
