Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: ground
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cumin reduces daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson case included a cumin-containing tablet within a combined regimen and reported less OFF in percentage terms with the ordinal score unchanged. There are no diary hours or an ingredient-specific comparison. Rat pharmacokinetics and patent examples concern other drugs and concentrated exposures. Ground cumin effects on daily OFF time remain unresolved.
