Statement: Consuming toasted buckwheat groats reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: The Tartary buckwheat sprout-powder preprint found an inconclusive primary cognitive result and mixed exploratory performance, including faster Flanker and Stroop responses and less improvement on one sustained-attention variability measure. It did not test toasted groats or Parkinson’s.
