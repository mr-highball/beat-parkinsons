Ingredient: green peas

Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked green peas worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Three official protocols use protein blends or a pea beverage and include gait testing, with no posted results. Isolated pea lectin with paraquat affected forced forelimb behavior in rats. The walking effect of cooked green peas in Parkinson remains unresolved.
