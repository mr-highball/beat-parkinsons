Category: herb
Ingredient: tarragon
Preparation: dried
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried tarragon worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: 2014 crossover in ten trained men, nine with exercise data, combined aqueous Russian tarragon extract or placebo with creatine in both arms; sprint peak power, mean power and total work interactions p=.92, .27 and .27. These are performance tests rather than clinical fatigue after dried culinary leaves.
