ID: 0694
Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Active-comparator trials gave inconclusive fatigue outcomes. The rheumatoid arthritis source measured time until fatigue during analgesic withdrawal, with inconclusive within-arm changes. The Japanese desk-work trial has no public arm results located. Refined corn oil and Parkinson fatigue severity remain unresolved.
