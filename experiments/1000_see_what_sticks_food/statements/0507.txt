Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: ground
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground turmeric reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Oral isolated curcumin improved mean open-field speed or timed pole descent in independent Parkinson-model rodent comparisons. The original Ramkumar study used an isolated derivative and balance-dependent beam, rotarod and hanging tasks, so its broader motor outcomes contribute context. A human registry outside Parkinson disease reported 0.089 m/s less walking-speed gain with curcumin than placebo in 17 older adults, with no posted between-arm analysis. The adverse skilled-reaching result concerns task success in young mice. Ghodsi's nanomicelle trial and Donadio's phospholipid cohort supply composite Parkinson motor scores without a separate bradykinesia estimate. The close animal timing outcomes retain a minimal easing signal despite conflicting indirect human walking evidence and the unresolved culinary exposure.
