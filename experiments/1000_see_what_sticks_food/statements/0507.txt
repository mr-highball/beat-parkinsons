Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: ground
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground turmeric reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Oral curcumin improved mean open-field speed or timed pole descent in independent Parkinson-model rodent comparisons. The standardized turmeric-root curcumin abstract adds broader motor and memory outcomes in a separate rat model with no verified speed endpoint. The older-adult registry reported 0.089 m/s less walking-speed gain with curcumin than placebo in 17 participants and no between-arm analysis. Human Parkinson carrier studies supply composite motor scores. The closely matched animal timing outcomes retain a minimal easing signal while culinary ground turmeric and separate clinical bradykinesia remain unresolved. The FXTAS clinical review describes patient-perceived benefit for a broad list of dietary options including turmeric. It does not identify the specific options, ground turmeric exposure, or a bradykinesia comparison. Its motor assessment citation concerns a separate study. Purified curcumin nanoparticles increased the food-dependent basal slowing response of cat-2 nematodes. This is an ecological locomotor assay with a different meaning from Parkinson slowness. It gives no extra easing or worsening evidence for ground turmeric, leaving the prior rodent timing observations as the closest directional lead. The independent Zhong mouse experiment favors isolated curcumin on pole descent (reported p=.0187). That result is consistent with earlier animal speed proxies. The comparative human walking signal, dietary model limits and absence of an exact ground-turmeric Parkinson bradykinesia result retain the minimal tier. The original registry shows an exploratory unadjusted interval spanning zero for the 400-m change contrast, and the journal abstract describes favorable estimates on different motor measures in the same cohort. The walking result remains opposing context, with no transferred bradykinesia direction.
