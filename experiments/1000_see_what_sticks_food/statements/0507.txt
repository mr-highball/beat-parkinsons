ID: 0507
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: ground
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground turmeric reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Nanomicelle or phospholipid curcuminoids appear in Parkinson studies. Donadio's analyzed groups were 19 supplemented and 14 untreated, with +5 and +11 mean changes on a composite OFF motor examination. The available reports supply no ground turmeric comparison or separate bradykinesia estimate. Injected curcumin pretreatment partly protected open-field movement in a rat injury model. A separate dietary isolated-curcumin study reported poorer skilled reaching and inconclusive spontaneous movement.
