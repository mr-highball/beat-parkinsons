ID: 0056
Category: food
Ingredient: lentils
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming boiled lentils reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Lentils were a menu option in three Parkinson diet studies. Their published motor totals give no lentil-specific rigidity estimate. Injected red lentil extract shortened a drug-induced catalepsy response in rats. Boiled lentils and clinical rigidity remain open.
