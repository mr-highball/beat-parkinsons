Statement: Consuming fresh lemon balm reduces speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: A lemon balm extract trial in Alzheimer dementia reported composite measures and no separate speech intelligibility estimate. Fresh leaf and Parkinson dysarthria remain untested in the located studies.
