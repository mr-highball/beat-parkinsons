ID: 0083
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted cumin reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson diet survey recorded cumin intake and anxiety measures. It did not isolate toasted cumin or a specific anxiety effect. Rechecked the culinary Cuminum cyminum rat paper and the Iranian mixed Bunium and black pepper preparation. The first measured urinary stress biomarkers and memory, and the second used a different species in a mixture with memory outcomes. The traditional calming attribution concerns that mixture and the cited Persian passage remains unverified. No separate toasted cumin and Parkinson anxiety estimate emerged.
