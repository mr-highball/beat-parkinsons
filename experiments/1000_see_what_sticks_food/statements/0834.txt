Symptom: low voice volume severity

Statement: Consuming refined peanut oil worsens low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: All 60 allergy-trial participants tolerated the refined oil challenge. Pediatric reports list pooled hoarseness and separate labial oil challenges without oil-specific voice change. The acute allergy outcomes leave habitual Parkinson voice volume unresolved.
