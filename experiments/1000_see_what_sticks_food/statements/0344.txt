ID: 0344
Category: spice
Ingredient: caraway
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted caraway worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson smell study found that 0 of 34 Parkinson participants and 5 of 53 controls correctly identified dried caraway by smell. A separate Parkinson study tested a five-spice diagnostic panel. The effect of consuming toasted caraway remains open.
