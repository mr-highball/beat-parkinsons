Statement: Consuming toasted cumin worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The MPTP mouse study found longer rotarod retention on day 20 after aqueous cumin fruit extract given before toxin exposure. Day 5 was inconclusive and the paper gives conflicting animal counts. This observed benefit concerns prevention and model coordination. The heated spice chemistry varies by preparation, with no measured bridge to the tested extract or a standalone clinical postural-instability result. The Parkinson food survey records cumin intake without a postural estimate. Human trials and customary source checks do not furnish a separate outcome after toasted culinary cumin.
