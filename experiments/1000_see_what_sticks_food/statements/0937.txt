ID: 0937
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: grated
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated ginger reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A randomized craniotomy study's supplement gives inconclusive patient anxiety VAS comparisons for ginger extract. Root powder results in healthy adults are inconclusive and the SSRI adjunct abstract conflicts with itself about direction. The Parkinson extract trial has no posted item results. Rat extract studies offer indirect behavior data. Grated ginger and clinical Parkinson anxiety remain unresolved.
