ID: 0047
Category: herb
Ingredient: chives
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: dystonia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh chives reduces dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study tests consuming fresh chives for Parkinson dystonia severity. Parkinson diet menus (PMID32041265) list chives without species, individual dose, or separate dystonia scores; meal-protein dystonia reports (PMID1866021, PMID839864) omit chives; and onion, garlic, or Chinese-chive extracts and cellular assays do not evaluate fresh common chives or spontaneous Parkinson dystonia.
