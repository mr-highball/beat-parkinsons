ID: 0407
Category: oil
Ingredient: rice bran oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined rice bran oil worsens apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An oral rice-bran extract depression trial did not assess apathy. A cosmetic injection case described apathetic consciousness during acute embolism, with the oil identity inferred and unconfirmed. Refined rice-bran oil intake and clinical Parkinson apathy remain unresolved. The two pressurized whole-brown-rice reports favor lower apathy scores in healthy older adults. Brown rice versus white rice does not isolate refined oil, and measured contamination effects address a different exposure. No direction transfers to the frozen oil and Parkinson comparison.
