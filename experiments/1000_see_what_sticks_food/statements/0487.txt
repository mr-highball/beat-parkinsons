ID: 0487
Category: oil
Ingredient: grapeseed oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined grapeseed oil reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Nonoral grapeseed sleep comparisons and healthy-adult oil cookies provided no clinical Parkinson insomnia outcome. A planned insomnia trial names a linoleic-acid cookie without identifying the oil. A separate oral proanthocyanidin-extract pilot reports within-arm improvement in menopause and gives no placebo-relative insomnia contrast in the available abstract. This preparation differs from refined grapeseed oil.
