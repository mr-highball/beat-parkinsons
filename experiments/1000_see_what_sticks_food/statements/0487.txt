Statement: Consuming refined grapeseed oil reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: The officially registered grape-seed proanthocyanidin trial reports within-arm sleep change at abstract depth. The primary placebo-relative insomnia table is unavailable.
