ID: 0513
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study isolates virgin coconut oil for daytime sleepiness severity. The Parkinson baseline figure (MVP-2024) gives only an unquantified inverse tile without oil grade, adjustment, or uncertainty. The Parkinson ketogenic diet trial (PMID30098269) varied entire meal plans rather than isolating virgin coconut oil, pooled caregiver narratives (10.18103/mra.v12i4.5316) lack standardized scores and stratification, and ISRCTN13221188 provides no Epworth or virgin-oil estimate. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
