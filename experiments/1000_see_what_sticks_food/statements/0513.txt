ID: 0513
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The newly published Parkinson MCT pilot reports Epworth means of 7.3 at baseline and 8.0, 7.5 and 7.6 after its three phases, without a clear overall phase effect. It did not assign virgin coconut oil. The Phillips diet pattern reports a 0.45-point descriptive daytime-sleepiness advantage but changes multiple foods. The caregiver and participant accounts describe varied sleep experiences without an isolated oil effect. An unquantified exposure tile and these different comparisons do not establish the named virgin-oil effect.
