ID: 0604
Category: food
Ingredient: apricot
Preparation: raw
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw apricot worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Both arms of a Parkinson diet trial offered uncooked apricot among several fruit choices. The reported pain item contrasts the diets. One migraine diary reported fewer apricot entries on headache days and a medication-overuse-headache survey listed apricot triggers. Neither measured Parkinson pain severity by actual raw apricot intake. A functional constipation trial includes dried apricot or apricot juice in mixed fruit arms. Its symptom questionnaire includes abdominal pain, with no separate pain estimate. Raw apricot and Parkinson pain remain open.
