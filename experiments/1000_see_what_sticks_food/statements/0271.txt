ID: 0271
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground ginger worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No completed controlled clinical study tests ground ginger for Parkinson dyskinesia severity. Active or completed Parkinson ginger registrations (NCT07882108, IRCT20251028067794N1, NCT05001217) have no posted dyskinesia results. Preclinical signals diverge across preparations: a patent table (WO2024101890A1) reports lower AIM AUC for dried-rhizome extract with unverified route, n, and masking, whereas isolated zingerone (PMID21769642) did not suppress lesioned hindlimb stiffening and Duzhong Fang used a multi-herb blend.
