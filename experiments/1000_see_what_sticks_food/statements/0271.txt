ID: 0271
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground ginger worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Registered Parkinson ginger programs use powder tablets or two extract strengths and give no posted dyskinesia result. An inventor patent reports less abnormal involuntary movement with dried-rhizome extract in levodopa-treated mice, and the isolated zingerone observations gave no observed suppression. The animal preparation, disclosed methods and opposite results across compounds leave ground ginger and clinical dyskinesia unresolved.
