ID: 0271
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground ginger worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One inventor-reported levodopa mouse experiment found lower abnormal involuntary movement AUC with dried ginger rhizome extract and two isolated 6-shogaol comparisons. Commercial ground dried ginger contains measured 6-shogaol, supplying a specific preparation bridge. Different extract exposure, uncertain route and sample size, a null zingerone experiment and no direct human ground-ginger comparison limit the easing lean to minimal_signal.
