ID: 0066
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined canola oil worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson and other human diets include canola or rapeseed alongside other exposures. The MS mixed-diet cohort has an inconclusive unadjusted total fatigue comparison and a favorable original abstract statement. Secondary MFIS subscales show little change. The original model, isolated intake and refining grade remain unresolved. The original cancer trial compares rapeseed and fish oil and gives an inconclusive fatigue contrast with inconsistent follow-up and table denominators. Its preliminary poster likely describes the same cohort. The newly disclosed canola and MCT crossover registers mental-fatigue ratings but has no published results and uncertain recruitment chronology. Oil refining and clinical transfer to Parkinson fatigue remain open. The adjusted multiple sclerosis fatigue model is still inaccessible.
