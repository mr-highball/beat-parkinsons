Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson studies involving olive oil either used it as a shared vehicle (Buriram TCTR20210303005 cannabis crossover), recorded baseline posture only, or reported composite diet scores (MVP cohort), while VIVI-OLIVE (NCT07829484) combines diet counseling with exercise outside Parkinson disease; isolated cold-pressed olive oil effects on Parkinson postural instability remain open. Traced the olive-oil observation to the already recorded 2017 baseline cohort. Table 2 reports −31.4 points per food-frequency category for total PRO-PD after the additional income adjustment (95% CI −44.7 to −18.1). The later review does not supply grade or postural-instability effects. The gut-brain review names olive oil as one element of a Mediterranean dietary pattern. Parkinson postural instability appears in clinical background. The source gives no postural outcome by olive oil grade or consumption.
