ID: 0001
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson studies involving olive oil either used it as a shared vehicle (Buriram TCTR20210303005 cannabis crossover), recorded baseline posture only, or reported composite diet scores (MVP cohort), while VIVI-OLIVE (NCT07829484) combines diet counseling with exercise outside Parkinson disease; isolated cold-pressed olive oil effects on Parkinson postural instability remain open.
