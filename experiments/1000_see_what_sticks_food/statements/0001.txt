Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed olive oil worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: VIVI-OLIVE registers falls and gait for a diet-plus-extra-virgin-oil bundle. The Buriram study gives a timed mobility comparison for cannabis in a common olive carrier. The six-person series documents baseline postural instability only. An isolated cold-pressed oil effect on clinical Parkinson postural instability remains unresolved.
