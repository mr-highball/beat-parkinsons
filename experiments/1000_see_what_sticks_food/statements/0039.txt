ID: 0039
Category: oil
Ingredient: sesame oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized weight-loss diet trial comparing refined sesame oil against refined sunflower oil reports lower anxiety in its abstract (p=.011; DOI10.1108/NFS-12-2022-0407), though exact contrast definitions and arm means remain unavailable, no oil-free control was included, and the oil was refined rather than cold-pressed. Injected sesame oil increased rat anxiety-like behavior (PMID24409407), inhaled aroma studies test a non-oral route, and a Parkinson cannabinoid trial gave sesame oil to both arms (PMID38487964). The human oral comparison retains only a minimal_signal against worsening.
