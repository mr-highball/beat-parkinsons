ID: 0039
Category: oil
Ingredient: sesame oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed sesame oil worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A randomized comparison reports less anxiety with refined sesame oil than sunflower oil during a weight-loss diet. The exact contrast, arm values and cold-pressed exposure are unverified. Injected sesame oil increased anxiety-like behavior in rats. The closer oral human result gives a weak lean against worsening.
