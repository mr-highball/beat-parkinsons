ID: 0020
Category: food
Ingredient: potato
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted potato reduces daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study isolates roasted potato for daily OFF time in Parkinson disease. Low-protein redistribution crossovers (PMID16773618, PMID18019394, 3.5 vs 5 hours daily OFF, p=.01) varied formulated low-protein diets rather than roasted potato; Baroni menus allowed potatoes in both arms with inconclusive ordinal OFF distributions (p=.054); Dresden measured ON-state cold potato salad intake; and N-DOSE (NCT05589766) awaits posted results without a potato-specific OFF analysis.
