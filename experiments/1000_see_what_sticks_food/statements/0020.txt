ID: 0020
Category: food
Ingredient: potato
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted potato reduces daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The low-protein product diet reduced daily OFF in the Barichella research family, including a six-person follow-up drawn from the earlier 18-person trial, and increased time ON with dyskinesia in the follow-up. Both Baroni comparison menus allowed potatoes. Dresden measured cold potato salad intake while ON. These comparisons supply no roasted-potato estimate for daily OFF, and the N-DOSE plan supplies no such analysis.
