ID: 0020
Category: food
Ingredient: potato
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted potato reduces daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Both Baroni menus allowed potatoes and gave inconclusive ordinal OFF distributions. The Dresden study measured cold potato salad intake during medicated ON visits. Lunch reduced levodopa concentration variability during infusion without an identified potato exposure or daily OFF endpoint. The direction for roasted potato and daily OFF time remains unresolved.
