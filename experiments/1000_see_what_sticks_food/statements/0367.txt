Statement: Consuming fresh rosemary worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Essential oil studies reported higher resting pressure, while oral extract and infusion cohorts reported lower resting pressure. Neither direction supplies symptomatic standing measurements with fresh rosemary in Parkinson disease.
