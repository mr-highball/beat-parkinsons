ID: 0961
Category: oil
Ingredient: hazelnut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed hazelnut oil worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cold-pressed hazelnut-oil samples show differing filbertone signals by kernel preparation. Parkinson-model studies of isolated filbertone report rodent motor-task and tissue findings. Human oral-oil studies provide other endpoints, leaving the cold-pressed oil effect on Parkinson resting tremor unresolved.
