ID: 0961
Category: oil
Ingredient: hazelnut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed hazelnut oil worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cold-pressed hazelnut oil has no clinical or preclinical resting-tremor comparison: analytical studies measure filbertone in cold-pressed oils (Bendini 2014), whole hazelnuts appear only in a mixed Parkinson menu (PMID:32041265), and isolated filbertone mouse experiments (PMID:36958418, PMID:39636503) measured rotarod, treadmill, and neurotrophic markers without rest tremor.
