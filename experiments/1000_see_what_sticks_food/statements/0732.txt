ID: 0732
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Plasma alpha-linolenic acid had near-zero cross-sectional associations with examiner-rated rest tremor. Two flaxseed-derived omega-3 plus vitamin E trials report composite motor changes without a rest-tremor item or verified pressing grade. A combination case also leaves the effect of cold-pressed flaxseed oil unresolved.
