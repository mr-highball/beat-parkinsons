ID: 0732
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Plasma alpha-linolenic acid has near-zero cross-sectional associations with Parkinson rest tremor. Two flax-derived omega-3 and vitamin E trials report composite scores. Their registry revisions changed clinic and supplement quantities and leave separate rest scores unverified. Oil pressing method and the isolated effect remain unresolved.
