ID: 0732
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cold-pressed flaxseed oil has no isolated resting-tremor effect: in early Parkinson disease (PMID:34127758, n=38), plasma alpha-linolenic acid showed a near-zero correlation with examiner-rated rest tremor (rho=0.059, p=0.728), and two flaxseed omega-3 plus vitamin E RCTs (PMID:28342967, DOI:10.1016/j.clineuro.2018.12.006) co-administered vitamin E without reporting an isolated rest-tremor item.
