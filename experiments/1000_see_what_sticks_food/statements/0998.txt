Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two human osteoarthritis trials provide measured stiffness readouts for topical sweet almond massage or placebo. Joint stiffness is different from Parkinson muscle rigidity, and the oral cold-pressed exposure remains unmatched.
