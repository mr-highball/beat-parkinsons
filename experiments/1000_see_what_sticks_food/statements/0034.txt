Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground nutmeg reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Nutmeg solvent extracts changed spontaneous activity in healthy mice and some oral extract contrasts improved coordination in toxin or drug models. The higher dose in one solvent series was inconclusive. The studies leave ground nutmeg and clinical bradykinesia open.
