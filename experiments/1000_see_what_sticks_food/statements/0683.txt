ID: 0683
Category: food
Ingredient: onion
Preparation: raw
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw onion worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The diagnostic study found lower odds of correctly identifying an onion odor at higher rigidity scores. The association was inconclusive after correction (q = .119). The effect of eating raw onion on rigidity remains unresolved.
