Category: food
Ingredient: shiitake mushroom
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted shiitake mushroom reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The immediate crossover favored one chicken–shiitake smoothie spillage component with an inconclusive total and no aspiration observed in either group. Different ingredients and viscosity prevent attribution. The pooled case report and a single public eating-difficulty account supply no roasted shiitake Parkinson swallowing comparison. Three additional original case reports document mechanical esophageal impaction, suspected food-related esophageal symptoms and airway obstruction in multiple sclerosis. These are adverse observations in separate settings with unspecified mushroom preparation. Oven-treated samples show reduced hardness and increased adhesion in laboratory tests, with no comparable oral endpoint. Parkinson manometry used liquid or thickened boluses and provides no tested bridge to the solid ingredient. An independent 2020 two-case report adds bowel-obstruction observations after pieces of shiitake. Preparation and Parkinson status are unspecified, and pharyngeal swallowing severity was unmeasured.
