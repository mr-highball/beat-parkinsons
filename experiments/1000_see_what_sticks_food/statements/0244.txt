ID: 0244
Category: food
Ingredient: shiitake mushroom
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted shiitake mushroom reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A blinded instrumental crossover reports better premature-spillage scores for a chicken–shiitake smoothie than thin Ensure in older adults with swallowing complaints. The overall comparison was inconclusive, the formulas differ in ingredients and thickness, and a Parkinson subgroup was not reported. The Kameda series lists shiitake and Parkinson cases in separate pooled results. Direction for roasted shiitake and Parkinson swallowing severity remains open.
