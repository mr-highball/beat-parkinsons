Category: herb
Ingredient: dill weed
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried dill weed reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unspecified dill tablets and a topical preparation improved questionnaire totals in other clinical settings. The tablet source does not identify plant part or insomnia subitems. The topical baseline has a reporting conflict, and the completed extract-beverage trial has no public results. Injected mouse locomotion does not measure sleep. The dried leaf and Parkinson insomnia comparison remains open.
