ID: 0357
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: cracked
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cracked black pepper worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The oral dried-pepper crossover did not establish a sustained-attention difference. An Alzheimer mixture reports concentration changes without a verified between-arm item. Separate animal reports use concentrated pepper extracts, combinations or oil and measure memory or spatial attention. The oil paper has unresolved comparator provenance. New blend protocols do not have posted results. The direction for cracked pepper and attention impairment in Parkinson disease remains unresolved. The archived seven-page original identifies descriptive primary-task reaction times under both conditions and statistically inconclusive adjusted attention comparisons. The exposure was encapsulated black pepper fruit in young adults with low energy.
