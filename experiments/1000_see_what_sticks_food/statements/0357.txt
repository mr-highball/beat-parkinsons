ID: 0357
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: cracked
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cracked black pepper worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The oral dried-pepper attention comparison did not establish a difference. In Alzheimer disease, a mixed capsule reports reduced concentration impairment without a verified between-arm item, and a canine mixture measured spatial attention. The concentrated mouse extract measured maze memory. New blend registrations plan timed cognitive tasks and have no reported outcomes. The direction for cracked pepper and sustained attention in Parkinson disease remains unresolved.
