Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: dystonia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed peanut oil reduces dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinsonian primates showed dystonia, and a drug in injected peanut oil reduced combined levodopa-induced dyskinesia. No separate dystonia comparison or isolated cold-pressed oral oil exposure was available.
