ID: 0674
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: ground
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cumin reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A cumin essential-oil case series reported unquantified dizziness without a posture or control. The ground cumin and essential-oil trials measured resting blood pressure, and the Parkinson diet survey supplied no ingredient-specific standing symptom. Direction remains unresolved.
