Category: spice
Ingredient: nigella seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nigella seed worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Isolated thymoquinone and Nigella extract studies report findings across animal movement and imposed-posture tasks. The additional extract study gives a catalepsy contrast without measured movement speed. These different models and preparations leave ground Nigella and clinical Parkinson bradykinesia unresolved.
