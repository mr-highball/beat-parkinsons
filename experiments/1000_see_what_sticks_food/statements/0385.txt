Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: visual hallucination frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh basil worsens visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two Parkinson trial menus use fresh basil. One ketogenic participant's hallucinations resolved after a medication change and the report gives no link to basil or hallucination frequency. The Serbian infusion survey recorded anxiety, while a separate hallucination case involved five supplements and holy basil extract.
