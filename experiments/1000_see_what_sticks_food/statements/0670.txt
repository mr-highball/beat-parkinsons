ID: 0670
Category: herb
Ingredient: dill weed
Preparation: dried
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried dill weed worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Boiled seed had favorable anxiety ratings after labor, and oral maternal extract changed mouse maze outcomes under a toxin model. The earlier stroke experiment used a herb and rice blend. The botanical part or preparation differs or remains unclear, and the completed dill extract mood registration has no posted results. Dried dill weed effects in Parkinson disease remain uncertain.
