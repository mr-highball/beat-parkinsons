ID: 0860
Category: food
Ingredient: grape
Preparation: raw
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw grape worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One placebo-controlled trial reports lower Hamilton anxiety scores with grape fruit extract in healthy older adults with low baseline anxiety (PMID29163162). Competing evidence includes an inconclusive 12-week whole-grape powder trial in postmenopausal women (PMC7257957, n=32 completers, numeric contrast omitted), mixed rotenone-rat behavioral proxies with dried Corinthian currants (open-field thigmotaxis p=.006 vs elevated-plus-maze p=.929), a null A53T mouse concentrate test, an order-affected acute juice study, and a pending college-student Hamilton trial (NCT07214428). No clinical trial tests raw grapes or Parkinson anxiety; the older-adult extract trial sustains a minimal_signal against worsening.
