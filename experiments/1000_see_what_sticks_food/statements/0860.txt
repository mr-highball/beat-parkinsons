ID: 0860
Category: food
Ingredient: grape
Preparation: raw
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw grape worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A placebo-controlled grape fruit-extract trial found lower Hamilton anxiety scores in healthy older adults. Dried whole grapes favored one open-field anxiety proxy in Parkinson-model rats, with an inconclusive maze measure and a separate inconclusive mouse study. A human whole-grape powder trial found no clear anxiety effect. The first two results give a minimal lean against worsening; raw grape and clinical Parkinson anxiety remain untested.
