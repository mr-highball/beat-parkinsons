ID: 0530
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed canola oil reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In two trials of generally healthy adults, a canola comparator had inconclusive or modest within-arm changes in walking balance or mobility. The Parkinson whole-diet trial only published the motor aggregate. The cold-pressed oil effect on instability remains unresolved.
