ID: 0133
Category: herb
Ingredient: lemon balm
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed lemon balm reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Independent placebo comparisons favor powdered lemon balm or extract in non-Parkinson populations. Infusion chemistry establishes limited overlap, and brewed tea in burn patients gave an ambiguous between-group change. A separate aqueous-extract screening result numerically favored placebo with baseline imbalance. Direct Parkinson evidence remains absent.
