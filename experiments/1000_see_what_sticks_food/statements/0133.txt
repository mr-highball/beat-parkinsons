ID: 0133
Category: herb
Ingredient: lemon balm
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed lemon balm reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Some placebo comparisons favor dried or powdered lemon balm leaf and extracts in non-Parkinson populations. A postoperative dried-leaf study also favored the capsule, with inconsistencies in its reported test. The brewed burn-patient comparison was ambiguous, and adding lemon balm to an egg isolate did not establish incremental benefit. Two other positive comparisons used mixtures, and an aqueous-extract screening result numerically favored placebo with baseline imbalance. Infusion chemistry supports limited transfer. Direct Parkinson evidence remains absent.
