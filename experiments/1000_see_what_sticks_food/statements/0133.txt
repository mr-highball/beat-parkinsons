ID: 0133
Category: herb
Ingredient: lemon balm
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed lemon balm reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Four non-Parkinson randomized oral lemon-balm trials report lower depressive symptoms versus placebo using powdered leaf capsules in stable angina (PMID29908682), leaf extract in type 2 diabetes (PMID37131158, n=44 completers, p=.001), phospholipid extract in emotional distress (PMID37927585), and postpartum capsules (p=.004, unresolved attrition). Competing controlled trials show null depression differences for dried-leaf lemon balm added to egg isolate (PMID21631360), baseline-imbalanced aqueous extract (PMID25680840), and inconclusive between-arm brewed tea in burn injury (DOI10.5812/traumamon.33630), while brewed-tea registrations await results and direct Parkinson depression trials remain absent. Preparation, population, and null-trial limits keep the easing lean at minimal_signal.
