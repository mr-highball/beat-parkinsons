ID: 0133
Category: herb
Ingredient: lemon balm
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed lemon balm reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Non-Parkinson capsule and extract studies favor lemon balm in some comparisons. A postpartum capsule study showed a smaller increase in depressive symptoms, with unclear attrition handling. Brewed tea in burn patients was inconclusive, and an egg-isolate add-on study found no clear difference between active arms. Two newly verified brewed-tea protocols await interpretable depression results. Direct Parkinson evidence remains absent.
