Ingredient: arugula

Symptom: reduced facial expressiveness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw arugula reduces reduced facial expressiveness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson pilot included rucola in a shared menu and facial expression in the motor examination, publishing only aggregate results. The isolated erucin model measured rotation. No separate raw arugula effect on facial expression is available.
