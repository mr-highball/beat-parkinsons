ID: 0746
Category: food
Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw blueberry reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 52
Rationale: A six-week clinical crossover outside Parkinson disease favored freeze-dried whole blueberry powder on constipation severity by 0.3 points, with its confidence interval reaching zero after rounding. A separate feasibility trial reported one constipation event around six weeks in the powder arm, judged possibly related. These exposures and populations weakly inform a raw-blueberry improvement hypothesis.
