ID: 0746
Category: food
Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw blueberry reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: The controlled whole-blueberry powder crossover in 43 adults with functional gastrointestinal disorders gives a small favorable constipation score difference of -0.3 (95% CI -0.7 to 0.2), with uncertainty on both sides of zero. Eating powder made from whole berries provides a limited preparation bridge to raw blueberries. Constipation reports during powder supplementation, including resolution after a pause in one uncontrolled case, limit that transfer. The ACID safety comparison used purified bilberry and black currant rather than blueberries. Parkinson food cohorts and the registered blueberry motor trial do not give a blueberry-specific constipation severity contrast. The sign supports only a minimal easing lean.
