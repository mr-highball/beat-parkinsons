ID: 0146
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two cinnamon leaf studies in rats report shorter catalepsy with several preparations. The powder-in-water comparison was inconclusive. The human added-formula trial reports aggregate UPDRS measures without a separate rigidity result. Preparation and outcome gaps leave the Parkinson claim unresolved.
