ID: 0146
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The original Wang leaf-powder-in-hydrosol comparison reports shorter rat catalepsy. The Lee leaf-powder-in-water comparison has no detected difference, while some processed leaf preparations favor shorter latency. An oral ground-bark A53T experiment measures pole timing and rotarod performance. These animal outcomes differ from passive clinical rigidity. The human modified-formula trial reports composite motor results without isolating cinnamon or the rigidity item. The frozen worsening hypothesis stays open.
