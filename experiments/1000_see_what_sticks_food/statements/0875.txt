Category: spice
Ingredient: mustard seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground mustard seed reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In an uncontrolled Parkinson study, 13 of 14 participants who tracked urgency reported initial improvement with broccoli and ground white mustard tea. Some later reported transient worsening or fading benefit. The contribution of ground mustard by itself remains open.
