ID: 0490
Category: herb
Ingredient: oregano
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh oregano worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Rodent infusion and isolated oil studies report coordination, exploratory movement or inconclusive rotarod performance. The isolated thymol aging model reports endurance, grip and rotarod differences. These do not establish fresh oregano exposure or a stride-length contrast in Parkinson disease.
