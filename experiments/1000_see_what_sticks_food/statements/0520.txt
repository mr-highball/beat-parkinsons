Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A seven-person conference study found inconclusive overall sway comparisons after unidentified oral spice. Nominal pairwise changes could reflect practice or measurement order. The topical cayenne balance comparison was inconclusive and no ground cayenne Parkinson result was reported.
