ID: 0417
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: ground
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground saffron worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled clinical study isolates ground saffron for examiner-rated muscle rigidity severity in Parkinson disease. A randomized Tehran Parkinson trial of unspecified saffron capsules (n=52; DOI10.1016/j.hermed.2024.100968) found an inconclusive total MDS-UPDRS Part III contrast (p=.424) with no separate rigidity item; uncontrolled supplement charts and vegetarian menus do not isolate saffron; and injected safranal, crocin, or crocetinate rodent catalepsy assays are remote proxies.
