ID: 0417
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: ground
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground saffron worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled clinical study isolates ground saffron for examiner-rated muscle rigidity severity in Parkinson disease. A randomized Tehran Parkinson trial of unspecified saffron capsules (n=52; DOI10.1016/j.hermed.2024.100968) found an inconclusive total MDS-UPDRS Part III contrast (p=.424) with no separate rigidity item; uncontrolled supplement charts and vegetarian menus do not isolate saffron; and injected safranal, crocin, or crocetinate rodent catalepsy assays are remote proxies. Crocetin and crocetinate models distinguish isolated preparations from ground saffron. The review's structural rigidity refers to the molecule. Xi and Qian and the historical compendium add provenance with no Parkinson muscle-rigidity measurement. The Rao fly study compared saffron extract and isolated crocin and reported favorable locomotor readouts. Different preparations and fly outcomes do not measure examiner-rated rigidity after ground saffron in people.
