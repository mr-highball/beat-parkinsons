ID: 0417
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: ground
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground saffron worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Injected isolated safranal shortened hindlimb release time in a mouse stiffness assay. The randomized Tehran saffron capsule trial reported an inconclusive total motor comparison and no separate rigidity result; capsule preparation remains unspecified. The combination extract and crocin studies likewise give aggregate motor outcomes. Clinical rigidity after ground saffron consumption remains unresolved.
