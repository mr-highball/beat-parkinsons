ID: 0024
Category: food
Ingredient: onion
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: speech intelligibility impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked onion worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study measures listener-rated speech intelligibility after cooked onion in Parkinson disease. A heated onion-peel extract trial (Cho 2024) reported adjusted self-rated URI hoarseness (p=.038) with baseline imbalance, null overall WURSS (p=.164), and no speech intelligibility metric; an allergy case (Albanesi 2019) recorded acute anaphylactic dysphonia; and Parkinson diet cohorts pool sofrito or omit cooked-onion speech contrasts.
