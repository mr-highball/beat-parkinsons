Category: food
Ingredient: onion
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: speech intelligibility impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked onion worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A cooked-onion allergy case recorded acute dysphonia. The heated peel-extract trial uses about 1,000 mg active extract in 1,200 mg of capsules and reports a respiratory hoarseness item with baseline imbalance. Listener-rated speech after cooked onion in Parkinson disease remains unmeasured.
