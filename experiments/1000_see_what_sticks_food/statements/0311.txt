ID: 0311
Category: food
Ingredient: tomato
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked tomato reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Tomato paste in healthy adults yielded inconclusive executive contrasts in both the main analysis and sensitivity tables. A multicomponent capsule trial reported favorable within-period TESEN changes. Clinical Parkinson executive scores improved after a whole-diet intervention. The isolated cooked-tomato direction remains open.
