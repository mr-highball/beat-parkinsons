ID: 0299
Category: herb
Ingredient: bay leaf
Preparation: ground
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground bay leaf worsens drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Acute infant drooling followed concentrated decoction in a case series. A healthy-adult extract tablet study reported a change in saliva weight, and its closely linked Japanese registry gives no Parkinson drooling outcome. The ground-leaf comparison remains open.
