ID: 0433
Category: food
Ingredient: asparagus
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed asparagus reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson families described avoiding stringy asparagus. A cook with asparagus allergy reported acute dysphagia after ingestion and an earlier case concerns a feeding tube obstruction. Preparation and symptomatic change after a defined steamed-asparagus comparison remain unverified.
