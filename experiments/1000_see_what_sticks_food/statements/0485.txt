ID: 0485
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: dried
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried saffron worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 55
Rationale: The Isfahan trial directly assessed Parkinson fatigue after swallowing tablets containing dried Sargol stigma powder versus placebo. The adjusted difference was −9.20 points (95% CI −13.76 to −4.65), favoring less fatigue. The primary sample-size calculation was for a biomarker. The original public registration omitted the standalone fatigue scale and the first archived revision listing it postdates receipt of the outcome manuscript. The fixed preparation and single eligible Parkinson trial keep the direction provisional. Non-Parkinson extract comparisons are mixed or inconclusive. The Ashtiani syrup lacks a control and its initial fatigue mean exceeds the scale range described in its own methods. The matched Parkinson sample supports easing and opposes the frozen worsening claim. Earlier protocol materials and independent Parkinson fatigue comparisons remain open. The original fibromyalgia extract-versus-duloxetine report supplies an inconclusive Global Fatigue Index contrast with a wide interval. Its active comparator and non-Parkinson population limit transfer.
