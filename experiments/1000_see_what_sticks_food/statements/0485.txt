ID: 0485
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: dried
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried saffron worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 53
Rationale: The randomized Isfahan report gives fatigue scores 9.20 points lower with saffron powder than placebo. Its standalone fatigue scale first appears in a public revision after manuscript receipt. A perimenopausal extract preprint reports lower fatigue on a different scale, and another non-Parkinson extract trial is inconclusive. The limited Parkinson evidence gives a weak lean against worsening.
