ID: 0953
Category: food
Ingredient: barley
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled barley worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The cooked barley-flour study reports anxiety improvement in both periods and an inconclusive barley–rice contrast. Roasted barley comparisons gave mixed within-condition results. Pearl barley was a shared vehicle in a mouse locomotion experiment. Evidence for boiled intact barley and Parkinson anxiety remains unresolved.
