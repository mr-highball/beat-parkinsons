ID: 0953
Category: food
Ingredient: barley
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled barley worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One exploratory randomized trial in healthy adults reports favorable POMS 2 tension-anxiety change with boiled waxy barley versus rice, though final group medians were equal at 43, foods were visibly different, and eight participants were excluded (PMID39064742). Competing trials show a null cooked barley-flour versus rice-flour crossover (n=23), inconclusive DASS anxiety with multi-component talbinah, no anxiety scale when both arms ate barley (PMID40336123), and whole-diet NutriAct excluding gait-affecting Parkinson disease. Matching boiled preparation in the exploratory healthy-adult trial sustains only a minimal_signal against worsening.
