Category: food
Ingredient: barley
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled barley worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: An exploratory randomized trial in healthy adults favored boiled waxy barley over rice for change in tension–anxiety. Final group medians were equal. Participants could see their assigned food, eight were excluded, and the cooked-flour crossover gave an inconclusive comparison. The matching preparation and measured mood outcome give a minimal lean against worsening. Parkinson anxiety remains untested.
