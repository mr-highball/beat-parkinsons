ID: 0141
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled clinical comparison establishes an anxiety effect for cold-pressed flaxseed oil. An oil-alone human anxiety trial (DOI10.23937/2474-3631/1510010) found an inconclusive final Beck anxiety contrast (p=.31) with low baseline scores and unverified pressing. Parkinson omega-3 plus vitamin-E trials report no isolated anxiety item or oil-only arm, and rodent elevated-plus-maze proxies (rotenone, maternal separation with null light-dark and higher amygdala inflammation, haloperidol oil-alone null) do not supply an eligible clinical sign. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open. A newer randomized migraine study reported a larger DASS-21 anxiety decrease with flaxseed oil plus L-carnitine than placebos. It cannot separate the two active ingredients or establish the effect of cold-pressed oil in Parkinson disease.
