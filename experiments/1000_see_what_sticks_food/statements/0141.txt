ID: 0141
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A small flaxseed-oil trial favored oil numerically on Beck anxiety, with an inconclusive final comparison and pressing unreported. Some animal anxiety tests favor oil, while the haloperidol poster reports a favorable combined oil and alpha-lipoic acid arm without an oil-only estimate. Parkinson combination trials report no separate anxiety result. The lean for cold-pressed flaxseed oil in Parkinson disease remains very weak.
