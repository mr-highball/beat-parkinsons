ID: 0141
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A small flaxseed-oil trial showed a favorable numerical Beck anxiety direction, with an inconclusive final comparison and pressing unreported. Animal anxiety tests offer partial support. Parkinson combination trials supply no separate anxiety estimate. Transfer to cold-pressed oil in Parkinson disease is very uncertain.
