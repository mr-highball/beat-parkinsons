ID: 0141
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: The original oral cold-pressed flaxseed oil comparison shows less anxiety-like avoidance in a healthy mouse light–dark test. The exact preparation and behavioral endpoint allow a minimal easing signal. Small animal groups, possible locomotor effects, unmatched saline and unclear elevated-plus-maze reporting limit transfer. An oil-only human trial is imprecise, and milk-vehicle and other model findings are mixed. No separate Parkinson anxiety effect is established. The abstract reports anxiety-related improvement with exercise plus flaxseed oil in obesity-model mice. The separate oil-only numerical contrast, pressing grade and human transfer remain unconfirmed. The PCOS mouse abstract names anxiety-related tests while combining the behavioral findings. The separate oil outcome, grade and relevant tables remain unavailable.
