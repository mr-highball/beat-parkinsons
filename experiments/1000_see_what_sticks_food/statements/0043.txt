ID: 0043
Category: oil
Ingredient: safflower oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: speech intelligibility impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed safflower oil reduces speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The 1982 ataxia crossover reports a small timed-speech gain. Baylor singer trials measured acoustic range and self-perception, and Huntington and ALS publications report totals that include speech items. No result evaluates Parkinson speech intelligibility with verified cold-pressed oil.
