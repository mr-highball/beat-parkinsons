ID: 0560
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: ground
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cumin reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A randomized coarsely ground cumin-containing mixture favored the week-one constipation score, with an inconclusive week-two result and a small proportion of the same mixture in control. Protocol and report differ on seed naming and trial methods. Independent oral cumin extract studies report bowel changes in other clinical settings. Rat extract findings vary by model. This gives a minimal favorable lean, with the effect of ground cumin alone in Parkinson disease unresolved.
