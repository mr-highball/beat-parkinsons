ID: 0560
Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: ground
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cumin reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One triple-blind randomized trial outside Parkinson disease reports earlier first postoperative defecation after oral Cuminum cyminum extract capsules versus placebo (PMID38965524). A randomized multi-herb digestive mixture favored constipation at week one (p=.049) but was inconclusive at week two (p=.152) with 10% mixture in control and cannot isolate cumin, while an IBS essential-oil study lacked a control and rat studies diverge across wet stool versus slowed charcoal transit. No trial isolates ground cumin in chronic Parkinson constipation; the postoperative oral-extract RCT sustains only a minimal_signal easing lean.
