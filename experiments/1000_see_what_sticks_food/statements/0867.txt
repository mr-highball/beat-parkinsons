Statement: Consuming boiled millet worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson diet included millet in a multiingredient menu. Preparation, participant intake and follow-up dyskinesia severity were unavailable. The reported motor total cannot establish a millet effect.
