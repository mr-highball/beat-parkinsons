ID: 0428
Category: oil
Ingredient: pumpkin seed oil
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted pumpkin seed oil worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The original haloperidol rat figure shows longer stride after methanolic pumpkin seed extract. Isolated p-coumaric acid also improved stride in a mouse model. Roasted oil contains small amounts of the constituent, with no established dose equivalence or measured effect on Parkinson stride.
