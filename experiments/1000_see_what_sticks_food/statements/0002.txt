ID: 0002
Category: herb
Ingredient: celery leaf
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh celery leaf worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Raw celery leaves contain butylphthalide, and human feeding studies show plasma apigenin. The pharmaceutical mouse report uses locomotion and coordination measures. A clinical butylphthalide registration planned Part IV motor complications and the likely matching conference report gives cognitive outcomes. Fresh celery leaf and clinical dyskinesia severity remain unresolved.
