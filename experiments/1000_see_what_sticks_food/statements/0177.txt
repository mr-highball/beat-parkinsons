ID: 0177
Category: oil
Ingredient: macadamia oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed macadamia oil reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The recovered original dietary trial tested different formulated fat mixtures and measured plasma lipids, with two transient gastrointestinal complaints during the macadamia-rich period. Other human comparisons measured cognition, digestion or fatigue. Whole-kernel canine toxicity and rat brain tissue findings supply no transferable effect direction for dietary cold-pressed oil and Parkinson resting tremor.
