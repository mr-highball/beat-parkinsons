ID: 0144
Category: oil
Ingredient: sesame oil
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted sesame oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized postpartum trial reports lower after-pain intensity and quality with 1.1 g oral sesame-oil capsules versus placebo (n=86; abstract only, roasting unverified, registry interval conflict; DOI10.1016/j.hermed.2024.100885), alongside an oral episiotomy-pain trial favoring sesame at later doses with an inconclusive overall group-by-time interaction (PMID41354976). Topical limb-trauma massage trials use a non-oral route, while dyspepsia and Parkinson cannabinoid studies used sesame oil in both arms or open-label without an oil comparator. Abstract-only reporting, unverified toasting, and postpartum pain limit the easing lean to minimal_signal. The multiple-sclerosis dronabinol crossover used sesame oil in both arms. Its drug contrast cannot inform the sesame-oil direction. The topical trauma trials and shared-carrier clinical comparisons now have separate study-family records.
