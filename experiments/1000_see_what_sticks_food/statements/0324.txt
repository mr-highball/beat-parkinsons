Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Horse measurements supply direct stride and human studies report gait speed, walking distance or timed mobility. Their comparator oils, co-interventions, outcomes and populations do not isolate refined corn oil or Parkinson stride length. The NUTRIMAL trial adds uncertain gait-speed contrasts using a sunflower and corn oil mixture. The COPD supplement identifies a different soybean and corn blend.
