ID: 0821
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The 12-week Longvida trial found less worsening on a combined PSQI daytime dysfunction component. Few improved in either arm, and no separate sleepiness item was given. Epworth was inconclusive in another formulated trial and was unreported in the Parkinson nanomicelle study. No directional result was found for eating grated turmeric and daytime sleepiness.
