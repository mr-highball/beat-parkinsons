ID: 0471
Category: food
Ingredient: brown rice
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked brown rice worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cooked brown rice and related feed experiments give a favorable timed-climbing signal in iron-exposed flies. Matched wild-type brown-versus-white comparisons are descriptively favorable and the normal cornmeal comparison differs. Rat evidence and a processed human hydrolysate trial give general motor outcomes without a separate clinical postural-stability result. A bridge to the frozen posture endpoint remains unresolved.
