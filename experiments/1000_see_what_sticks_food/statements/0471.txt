ID: 0471
Category: food
Ingredient: brown rice
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked brown rice worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A rat study found greater ambulation and inconclusive pole times, while cooked ground brown rice improved a fly climbing task. Human Parkinson postural testing linked to cooked brown rice remains unresolved.
