ID: 0710
Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: grilled
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grilled bell pepper reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The red pepper extract pilot and two exercise studies report within-condition executive task changes. The controlled college study gave inconclusive between-condition effects, and the separate adult registry has no published results. None identified grilled pepper for Parkinson executive function.
