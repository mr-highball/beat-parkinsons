ID: 0090
Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed peanut oil reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The recovered 1949 Parkinsonian series reports smaller tremor amplitude under drug treatment with unchanged incidence and frequency. Peanut oil was only an injected drug carrier, with no oil-only comparison. The separate oral oil studies address allergy, activity, metabolism or other outcomes. No signed result transfers to cold-pressed peanut oil and Parkinson resting tremor.
