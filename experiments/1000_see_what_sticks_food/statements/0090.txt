ID: 0090
Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed peanut oil reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Historical reports identify peanut oil as a formulation carrier and give no oil-only comparison. The 1951 Parkinson review describes an unhelpful drug formulation for tremor and rigidity, with the cited 1949 primary body still open. The evidence leaves the effect of cold-pressed dietary peanut oil unresolved.
