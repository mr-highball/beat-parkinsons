ID: 0061
Category: oil
Ingredient: safflower oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed safflower oil reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An earlier safflower crossover gave similar standing readings and did not measure lightheadedness. A later cold-pressed oil trial lowered measured blood pressure outside Parkinson disease and did not report an orthostatic symptom estimate.
