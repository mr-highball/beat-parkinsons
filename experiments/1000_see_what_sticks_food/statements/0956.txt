ID: 0956
Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two randomized human controlled trials report reduced acute procedural pain with topical clove preparations: a split-mouth placebo-controlled trial in 73 adults found glycerin gel prepared with ground clove lowered dental needle-insertion pain versus placebo (PMID16530911, p=.005), and a double-blind crossover in 34 hemodialysis patients found topical eugenol nanoemulsion lowered cannulation puncture pain versus no intervention and placebo gel (PMID32951708, p=.001; placebo mean omitted from public abstract). Competing studies show inconclusive three-arm postoperative headache contrasts for inhaled clove aromatherapy (PMID40012817, p=.20 at 8 h and p=.31 at 24 h), an unquantified null mouse hot-water report for ground clove powder (CCRAS 1997-1998), and assay-dependent rat essential-oil nanogel formalin versus hot-plate results (PMC9123718). Topical procedural versus oral chronic Parkinson pain and competing null trials keep the easing signal at minimal_signal.
