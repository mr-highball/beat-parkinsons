ID: 0956
Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Topical ground clove and topical eugenol studies report dental pain relief. Ground clove powder in mice gave an inconclusive analgesia comparison. Isolated oral eugenol in a nerve injury model changed one thermal response but not mechanical responsiveness at observable depth. Neither result identifies a clinical direction for ingested ground clove and Parkinson pain.
