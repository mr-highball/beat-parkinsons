ID: 0214
Category: spice
Ingredient: fennel seed
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted fennel seed worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: High-dose fennel oil toxicity reports describe tremor in rats with uncertain cohort overlap. Isolated fenchone had no tremors in a healthy-rat toxicity comparison. The Parkinson models measure imposed posture or coordination, and crushed-seed feeding measures general activity. Roasting changes fruit volatiles. No source supplies a transferable resting-tremor direction for eating toasted fennel seed.
