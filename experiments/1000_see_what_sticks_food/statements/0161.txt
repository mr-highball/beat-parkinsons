ID: 0161
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 52
Rationale: Several randomized human oral cinnamon studies favor lower measured pain, including bark powder in migraine and primary menstrual pain. The Parkinson case used multiple treatments. Isolated beta-caryophyllene reduced some mouse pain responses and gave inconclusive results on others. Differences in clinical pain and culinary exposure limit the favorable lean.
