ID: 0878
Category: food
Ingredient: orange
Preparation: raw
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw orange worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized non-Parkinson trial reports higher stool frequency (p=.0281) and softer stools (p=.04) with added orange pomace versus control, alongside increased gas/bloating and an unavailable separate constipation severity score (NCT02979496). Competing evidence includes a null pomace crossover for primary stool-frequency interaction (PMID36102329, p=.46), sequential null pasteurized orange juice (PMID30638420), an unadjusted older-adult survey (p=.054), uncontrolled multi-component Chagas and trifoliate-orange studies, and a Fox Insight pooled-fruit binary bowel category pointing opposite. No trial tests raw whole orange in Parkinson constipation; the pomace stool-frequency trial retains a minimal_signal against worsening.
