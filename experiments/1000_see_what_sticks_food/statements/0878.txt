ID: 0878
Category: food
Ingredient: orange
Preparation: raw
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw orange worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Older adults reporting orange consumption had lower unadjusted Rome III constipation prevalence, with preparation unspecified. Pomace trials give mixed stool-frequency results. A pooled-fruit Parkinson survey points the other way on a binary bowel-frequency category. The Lima studies give no orange-specific direction, and the Chagas series combines fruits and physical therapy. The lean against worsening remains minimal. An isolated raw-orange comparison in Parkinson disease remains open.
