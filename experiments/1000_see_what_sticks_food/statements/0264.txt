Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A whole-program Parkinson testimonial describes walking improvement without confirmed individual celery intake. A drug trial measured a gait-containing composite and a mouse study measured beam crossing after extract. Raw celery stalk and clinical walking speed remain unresolved.
