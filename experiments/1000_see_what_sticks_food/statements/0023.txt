ID: 0023
Category: food
Ingredient: sweet potato
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming boiled sweet potato reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Boiled sweet potato with stew was tested in a run-based oxygen uptake study. A separate steamed sweet potato rice cake trial measured immediate cycling exertion and found no clear difference. Neither study measured fatigue severity in Parkinson disease.
