Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Both arms of the Parkinson low-fat versus ketogenic trial included cinnamon. The daytime sleepiness item favored the ketogenic plan by 0.45 points, with no ground cinnamon intake contrast. The cassia fragrance study assessed sleep quality.
