ID: 0551
Category: herb
Ingredient: shiso
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried shiso reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Perilla extracts and inhaled oil showed rodent sleep effects. A perilla-containing tea trial gives inconclusive within-arm sleep-quality changes and no adjusted between-arm estimate. Other combination results remain pending. Dried shiso in Parkinson insomnia remains unresolved.
