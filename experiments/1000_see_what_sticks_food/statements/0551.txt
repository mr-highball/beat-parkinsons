ID: 0551
Category: herb
Ingredient: shiso
Preparation: dried
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried shiso reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Three independent controlled rodent sleep experiments using Perilla frutescens (shiso) leaf preparations converge on an easing direction for sleep onset and duration: oral ethanolic Perillae Herba extract shortened pentobarbital-induced sleep latency in mice and increased total sleep in rats (Kwon 2017, DOI10.20307/nps.2017.23.1.53), oral Bacillus-fermented perilla leaf extract increased rodent NREM and slow-wave sleep and shortened sleep latency (Bormate 2024, DOI10.1016/j.jff.2024.106345), and inhaled perilla leaf essential oil shortened sleep latency and prolonged sleep duration in PCPA-induced insomnia mice alongside documented Traditional Chinese Medicine insomnia context (Zhong 2021, PMID33246117). Conversely, a randomized human functional-dyspepsia trial of an eight-herb perilla formula (NCT05948215, Liu 2024, PMID39256061; n=26) showed inconclusive within-arm PSQI changes (p=.207 vs p=.141) without isolating perilla, a Korean multi-herb depression trial lacked a separate insomnia estimate (SAR2025-113), and two combination registrations (KCT0012330, NCT05668104) have no posted results. Preclinical-only isolated leaf extracts/oil, absence of oral culinary dried shiso human trials, and non-Parkinson models keep the easing lean at minimal_signal.
