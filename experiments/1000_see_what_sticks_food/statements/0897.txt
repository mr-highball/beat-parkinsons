Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: extra-virgin
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The KIM trial phases encouraged unspecified olive oil and its insomnia event concerned an overall diet. A cannabis series recorded a within-dose insomnia change with shared ungraded olive oil. AU-ARROW plans the Insomnia Severity Index within a multidomain program using extra-virgin oil. A Parkinson insomnia effect specific to the oil remains open.
