ID: 0897
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: extra-virgin
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The KIM trial phases encouraged unspecified olive oil and its insomnia event concerned an overall diet. A cannabis series recorded a within-dose insomnia change with shared ungraded olive oil. AU-ARROW plans the Insomnia Severity Index within a multidomain program using extra-virgin oil. A Parkinson insomnia effect specific to the oil remains open. The gut-brain review mentions insomnia clinically and olive oil within a Mediterranean pattern. There is no separate Parkinson insomnia result by extra-virgin olive oil intake. In the NHANES report, olive oil contributes to the dietary score, insomnia is a covariate, and depression is the modeled outcome. The flavonoids review describes a dietary pattern and the cannabidiol review points to a Crohn comparison. The original abstract and registry show a medication comparison with olive oil held in both arms and no clinical insomnia measure.
