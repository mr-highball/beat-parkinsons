Category: food
Ingredient: tomato
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked tomato reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Whole tomato powder and tomato-enriched animal feed altered striatal dopamine after experimental injury. The human diet study reported composite effects of sofrito. Cooked tomato and clinical rigidity were not isolated.
