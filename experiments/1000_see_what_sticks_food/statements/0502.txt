ID: 0502
Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried date reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In the 21-person crossover of whole dried Ajwa dates, abdominal pain diary values were 0.22 versus 0.25 after the date and control periods. The healthy cohort gave no clear difference or paired interval, and the whole-date mouse comparison also gave no clear separation. Dried-date extracts altered evoked responses but also suppressed movement or prolonged sleep. The Parkinson fiber-bar records leave date paste attribution and individual pain results unresolved. The verified date-palm migraine registry tests a distillate and posts no result, while the Parkinson pain trial uses a different botanical formulation. The frozen dried-date effect on Parkinson pain remains open.
