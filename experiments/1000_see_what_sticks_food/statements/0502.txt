ID: 0502
Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried date reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The 21-person crossover of whole dried Ajwa dates reported abdominal pain diary values of 0.22 after dates and 0.25 after control, with no clear difference or paired interval. The separate 131-child whole-date study found similar improvement counts across all arms, and the whole-date mouse comparison gave no clear separation. Date syrup trials reported lower pain at several points during labor. The saffron comparison gave an inconclusive added-date contrast during labor and a later postpartum difference. The syrup preparations, acute obstetric setting and comparator limitations leave transfer open. Dried-date extracts affected evoked responses alongside reduced movement or prolonged sleep. Parkinson fiber-bar records still lack an isolated date-paste and pain comparison. The migraine registry posts no results and uses a distillate. The frozen whole dried-date effect on Parkinson pain remains open.
