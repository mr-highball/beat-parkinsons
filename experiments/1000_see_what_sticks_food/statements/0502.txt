ID: 0502
Category: food
Ingredient: date
Preparation: dried
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried date reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Fresh-date extract increased hot-plate latency in a diabetic-rat study and the tail-flick contrast was inconclusive. Clinical Parkinson reports describe back pain and off-period pain during procedures or changing medication use. The evidence leaves the effect of dried dates on Parkinson pain severity unresolved.
