ID: 0332
Category: herb
Ingredient: parsley
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh parsley worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A controlled diet provided both fresh and heated parsley and measured antioxidant outcomes. The Tlemcen survey pooled fatigue across respondents and did not report change by fresh parsley use.
