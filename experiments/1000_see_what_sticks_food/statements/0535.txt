ID: 0535
Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: poached
Symptom: reduced facial expressiveness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached chicken worsens reduced facial expressiveness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study isolates poached chicken for reduced facial expressiveness in Parkinson disease. Overlapping Parkinson cohort reports (PMID29081890, PMID40732955) analyze composite PRO-PD totals without a facial-expression item or poached preparation; Phillips 2018 (PMID30098269) included simmered chicken soup inside a whole ketogenic diet reporting total MDS-UPDRS III; and a corticobasal case history supplies no timed exposure contrast. The final original-source comparison is closed. Targeted PubMed searches returned no matching Parkinson facial-expression and chicken study; seven broad results concern unrelated topics. The ClinicalTrials.gov search returned no records. The mixed diet and overlapping cohort findings do not isolate poached chicken or a facial-expression item. Cultural and conversational results lack a measured food-specific facial outcome.
