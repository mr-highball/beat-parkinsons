Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: poached
Symptom: reduced facial expressiveness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming poached chicken worsens reduced facial expressiveness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study isolates poached chicken for reduced facial expressiveness in Parkinson disease. Overlapping Parkinson cohort reports (PMID29081890, PMID40732955) analyze composite PRO-PD totals without a facial-expression item or poached preparation; Phillips 2018 (PMID30098269) included simmered chicken soup inside a whole ketogenic diet reporting total MDS-UPDRS III; and a corticobasal case history supplies no timed exposure contrast.
