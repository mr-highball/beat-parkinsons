ID: 0812
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground turmeric reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: An unblinded non-Parkinson trial reports a favorable turmeric powder and naproxen comparison, with unresolved enrollment and design differences versus its registry. A hand-osteoarthritis pilot gives an inconclusive primary pain comparison and favorable three-month within-arm changes. Isolated curcumin animal results use different exposures and pain proxies. The unblinded whole-powder comparison supplies a minimal favorable lean, with Parkinson pain unresolved.
