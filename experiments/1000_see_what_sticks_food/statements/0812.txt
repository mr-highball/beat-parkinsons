ID: 0812
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground turmeric reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Whole turmeric powder with naproxen had a larger pain reduction than naproxen alone in an unblinded dysmenorrhea trial (7.0 versus 5.6), with an unresolved registry design discrepancy. Formulated nano-curcumin improved a secondary pain score in coronary slow flow. The double-blind whole-powder hand-osteoarthritis pilot found no clear between-group difference. A new low-certainty Cochrane synthesis of four knee trials estimates 0.72 points less pain on a 0–10 scale (95% CI 0.06 to 1.39 points less), below its 1.5-point threshold. A separate positive proprietary-extract trial remains under overlap review. In the same 39-person spondyloarthritis survey reported in English and French, patients described less pain with turmeric without a quantified comparison, known preparation or Parkinson cohort. The Parkinson nanomicelle pilot supplies no separate pain item. Taken together, the evidence supports a minimal easing signal with the clinical effect of ground turmeric in Parkinson disease unresolved.
