ID: 0812
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground turmeric reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: An unblinded primary-dysmenorrhea trial reports larger VAS pain reduction when whole turmeric powder is added to naproxen (7.0 vs 5.6, p<.001), with an unresolved design and sample discrepancy versus its registry (149 parallel vs 90 crossover) and an implausible spread value, while an independent double-blind placebo-controlled trial in 42 completers with coronary slow flow phenomenon (PMID39085864, PMC11290174) reports improved secondary SF-36 Bodily Pain with 80 mg/day formulated nano-curcumin versus placebo (+13.9 vs -2.4, between-group and ANCOVA p<.001). Conversely, a double-blind hand-osteoarthritis pilot of whole turmeric powder (n=27) was inconclusive on primary one-month and between-group comparisons at all visits despite favorable three-month within-arm changes, and other formulated-extract or black-pepper-addition trials gave null primary between-arm pain contrasts. Non-Parkinson cohorts, formulated nano-curcumin versus culinary ground turmeric, attrition, registry conflicts, and null whole-powder pilot data keep the easing lean at minimal_signal.
