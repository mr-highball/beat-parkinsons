ID: 0609
Category: oil
Ingredient: soybean oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The vascular-aging trial counted injurious falls in 14/51 on soybean oil and 17/51 on marine omega-3. Supplement 2 identifies four tested oil lots without a processing grade. This trial excluded Parkinson disease, and the earlier Alzheimer trials used mixed or ambiguous formulations. The refined-oil Parkinson fall-frequency claim remains unresolved.
