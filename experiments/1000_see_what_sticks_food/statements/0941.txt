Statement: Consuming fresh celery leaf worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Whole dried celery extract improved some beam and grid outcomes in MPTP mice. The Parkinson mixed diet reports a motor total. These measurements give no fresh celery leaf effect on stride length.
