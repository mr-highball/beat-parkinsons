Statement: Consuming refined safflower oil reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A synbiotic protocol lists safflower oil in its placebo and has no posted bowel results. The Glut1 trial reported one constipation event in each blinded exposure period, and an oil-only pressure trial mentions bloating without a stool outcome. These comparisons do not give a direction for refined safflower oil and Parkinson constipation.
