Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled pinto beans worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson and MIND sleep findings concern whole-diet scores or grouped beans. The regional studies measure legume preference or compare intake and sleep separately with a gut bacterium. A Pinta sprout experiment measures rat melatonin rather than sleep. The boiled-pinto crossover measured other outcomes, leaving the effect on clinical Parkinson insomnia unresolved.
