ID: 0420
Category: spice
Ingredient: mustard seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground mustard seed reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A five-person mustard-meal study reported more whole saliva than unspiced rice. The mustard form is unverified, and the outcome is short-term secretion. A Parkinson comparison found lower resting flow despite excess-saliva complaints. Ground mustard and clinical drooling remain unresolved.
