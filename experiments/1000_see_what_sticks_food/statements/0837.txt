ID: 0837
Category: food
Ingredient: asparagus
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted asparagus worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: In a randomized healthy-adult crossover, the published figure suggests about two POMS fatigue T points more reduction after a mental stress task with same-species stem extract than with placebo. The original reports only within-period significance. Roasting, industrial extraction, population and clinical fatigue differ. Other supplement studies do not establish a stronger direction.
