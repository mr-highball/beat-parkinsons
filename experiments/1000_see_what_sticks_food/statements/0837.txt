ID: 0837
Category: food
Ingredient: asparagus
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted asparagus worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Culinary asparagus stem extract studies give within-period or uncontrolled fatigue results. A comparison of a different species and root extract improved perimenopausal fatigue at week eight, with no clear effect at week four. Roasted shoots and clinical Parkinson fatigue remain unresolved.
