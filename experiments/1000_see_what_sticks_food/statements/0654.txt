Category: spice
Ingredient: sichuan pepper
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted sichuan pepper worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral Sichuan pepper decoction shortened a mouse pole task after pre-exposure dosing. The related Japanese pepper experiment used a distilled intranasal preparation. Neither measured clinical bradykinesia after toasted pepper.
