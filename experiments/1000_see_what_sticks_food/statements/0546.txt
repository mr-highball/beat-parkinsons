Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Published serious falls in ENRGISE were rare. The Quinn Alzheimer trial’s pooled corn-or-soy placebo reports 33 falls-affected participants of 164 versus 42 of 238 with DHA. Oil-specific allocation, refining and clinical Parkinson fall frequency remain open.
