ID: 0546
Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The crude ENRGISE serious fall comparison and the pooled Alzheimer corn-or-soy comparison point to slightly more participants with falls in the comparator groups. Event counts are sparse or imprecise, neither study identifies recurrent Parkinson falls and the pooled study does not isolate corn oil. Timed mobility studies and chemically injured animals measure different outcomes. The COPD and NUTRIMAL trials add mixtures rather than refined corn oil attribution. No observed direction transfers to the frozen comparison. Original full-text and direct fall-count searches remain open.
