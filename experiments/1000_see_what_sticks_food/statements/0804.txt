Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: dream enactment frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed peanut oil worsens dream enactment frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The tauopathy mouse study measured REM without atonia and used peanut oil as the drug comparator vehicle. The independent Alzheimer-model study tested a drug and REM stage duration. Neither supplies dream-enactment frequency after cold-pressed oil.
