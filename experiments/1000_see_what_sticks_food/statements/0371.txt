Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Cinnamon-flavored gum increased saliva during chewing, as did other flavors. An isolated cinnamaldehyde rinse showed no output increase beyond vehicle. These exposures and secretion measures leave the effect of ground cinnamon on Parkinson drooling unresolved.
