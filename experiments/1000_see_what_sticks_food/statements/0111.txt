Category: spice
Ingredient: cumin
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted cumin worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Cumin and ginger saliva sprays were studied for salivary flow and dry mouth, with a favorable flow result in a hemodialysis crossover. Those trials leave swallowing severity after eating toasted cumin in Parkinson disease open.
