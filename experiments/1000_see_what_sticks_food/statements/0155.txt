Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: speech intelligibility impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed peanut oil worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Crude peanut oil provoked brief allergic mouth or airway symptoms in six of 60 peanut-allergic participants. The trial did not verify cold pressing or measure Parkinson speech intelligibility.
