ID: 0229
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled or confounding-adjusted study isolates virgin coconut oil for fatigue severity in or outside Parkinson disease. The Parkinson baseline figure (MVP-2024) plots only an unquantified tile without coefficient, grade, or uncertainty. Whole-diet ketogenic trials (Phillips 2018, military crossover, Choi 2024, KIM MCT diet where 9/37 improved and 9/37 worsened) do not isolate virgin coconut oil, and an unblinded chemotherapy study (PMID25163649) showed baseline imbalance (49.34 vs 36.38) with non-significant individual timepoint contrasts. Cyclist (FNRI-2007), runner (p=.99), and infection trials were null or omitted fatigue. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
