ID: 0229
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A new fixed-sequence Parkinson pilot finds slightly lower fatigue in the MCT-only phase than in the initial diet, and higher fatigue in the subsequent ketogenic/MCT phase than in the initial diet. The overall symptom contrast was inconclusive. The prior registry and informal report are a likely shared family, with a protocol discrepancy. The Phillips whole-diet trial reports a favorable descriptive fatigue comparison, while Choi and KIM do not provide a consistent sign. In the isolated virgin-oil chemotherapy trial, the endpoint difference is small, raw differences change sign across cycles and the groups began at different fatigue levels. The Parkinson baseline tile has no preparation or estimate. The exact virgin-coconut-oil effect remains open.
