Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A Parkinson baseline coconut-oil association and an exploratory fatigue item in an oil-containing Parkinson diet lean away from worsening. A randomized virgin coconut oil chemotherapy study has a favorable descriptive trajectory but inconclusive timepoint comparisons and baseline imbalance. Mixed MCT studies are uncertain. This is a minimal lean against worsening with the isolated Parkinson oil effect unresolved.
