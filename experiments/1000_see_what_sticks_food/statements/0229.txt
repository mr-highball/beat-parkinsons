Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: virgin
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming virgin coconut oil worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A Parkinson baseline coconut-oil association and an exploratory fatigue item in an oil-containing Parkinson diet lean away from worsening. A chemotherapy trial has a favorable descriptive trajectory but inconclusive timepoint comparisons and baseline imbalance. The whole-diet military crossover favored lower fatigue during extended wakefulness, with similar fatigue during normal-sleep adaptation and no individual oil data. A historical virgin-oil cycling comparison was inconclusive for perceived exertion. The minimal lean against worsening remains, with the isolated Parkinson oil effect unresolved.
