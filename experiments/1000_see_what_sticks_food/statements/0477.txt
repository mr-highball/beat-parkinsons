Statement: Consuming ground bay leaf reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Ground bay leaf trials measured metabolic outcomes or immediate digestive comfort. A Japanese extract trial reports within-arm constipation ratings without an independently verifiable between-group comparison. Ground bay leaf and Parkinson constipation severity remain unresolved.
