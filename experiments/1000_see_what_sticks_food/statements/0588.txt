ID: 0588
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two independent 12-week randomized placebo-controlled human trials in renal disease favored oral curcumin capsules for olfactory function over placebo (Nasution 2026 n=48 Sniffin Sticks composite TDI delta +3.56 p=0.010 and +4.00 p=0.002 vs +0.875; Malekmakan 2023 n=58 double-blind QOD p=0.045, self-assessment p=0.047, 20% vs 0% normosmia shift p=0.032), opposing the frozen worsening premise; non-Parkinson renal populations, formulated curcumin capsules instead of fresh grated turmeric, and capsule-standardization/Table 2 reporting limits keep the tier minimal.
