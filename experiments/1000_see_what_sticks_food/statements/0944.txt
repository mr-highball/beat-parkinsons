Category: herb
Ingredient: dill weed
Preparation: dried
Symptom: dystonia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried dill weed worsens dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A methanolic dill aerial-parts extract inhibited rat ileum contractions in vitro. A sowa withdrawal study measured locomotion and rotarod performance. Neither experiment assessed neurological dystonia after ordinary dried dill weed.
