Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: raw
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw carrot reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A randomized plant-rich Parkinson diet study observed no clear change in Epworth daytime sleepiness and gives no raw-carrot exposure. General-population blood carotenoid associations vary in direction, with no raw-carrot-specific result.
