ID: 0406
Category: herb
Ingredient: lemongrass
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed lemongrass worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled clinical comparison establishes that brewed lemongrass worsens daytime sleepiness severity. In the 1986 healthy-volunteer tea study (Leite 1986), morning sleepiness after two active brew conditions (16/50 and 17/50) versus placebo (13/50) gave an inconclusive paired comparison, while the 5/11 acute morning somnolence reports came from a separate uncontrolled group without a validated sleepiness severity scale. A four-case Parkinson essential-oil report (PMID41250562) tested inhaled/topical oil and found daytime sleep unchanged in all four cases. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
