Category: herb
Ingredient: lemongrass
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed lemongrass worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: In the same 50 healthy adults, 17 reported waking sleepy after coded brewed lemongrass and 13 after the matched placebo. Sixteen reported it after the labeled active tea. These are two contrasts within one family. The paired result was inconclusive and the active infusion was stronger than the investigators' customary recipe. A separate morning ingestion group in the same paper recorded acute somnolence in 5 of 11 without a comparator. A four-case Parkinson essential-oil series recorded no daytime sleep change with a different route. The blinded comparison supplies a small, clinically indirect worsening lean for the tea. The binary waking item, absent paired transition counts and lack of a Parkinson tea trial limit transfer to daytime sleepiness severity.
