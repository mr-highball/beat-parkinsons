ID: 0406
Category: herb
Ingredient: lemongrass
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed lemongrass worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Two active brewed lemongrass conditions had 16/50 and 17/50 morning sleepiness reports versus 13/50 after placebo, with an inconclusive paired result. Five of eleven separately reported acute somnolence after a morning brew without a control. The direction is weak and indirect because these groups were healthy and did not measure clinical daytime sleepiness severity. Parkinson aromatherapy reports used another route.
