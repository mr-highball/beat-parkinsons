Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed lemongrass worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: In the same 50 healthy adults, 17 reported waking sleepy after coded brewed lemongrass versus 13 after coded matched placebo. Sixteen reported it after labeled active tea. These are one cohort, and the net difference is four responses. The individual paired transitions are unavailable. Exact paired sensitivity over all compatible tables gives p values from .1250 to .5847. The binary waking item, stronger-than-customary brew and uncontrolled acute complaints limit clinical transfer. The separate Parkinson essential-oil case series reports no apparent daytime sleep change on a different route. A field survey and forum document bedtime use and suggestion without daytime symptom outcomes. The controlled waking comparison retains a minimal clinically indirect worsening lean.
