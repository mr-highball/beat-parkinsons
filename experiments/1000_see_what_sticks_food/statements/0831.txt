Statement: Consuming fresh shiso reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Intracerebral isolated green-leaf chalcone in 6-OHDA mice improved aggregate behavior; no clinical rigidity or fresh-leaf ingestion. Separate 2024 rotenone mouse perilla seed-oil study improved pole descent, a motor-speed task, and did not measure rigidity.
