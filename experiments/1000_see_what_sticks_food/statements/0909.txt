Ingredient: paprika
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground paprika worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The author abstract reports fewer abnormal involuntary movements after injected isolated capsaicin in lesioned rats. It leaves the levodopa regimen and scoring protocol unverified. A separate study found lower spontaneous and drug-elicited movement, making general activity suppression a competing explanation. Ground paprika capsaicinoid content varies, and no Parkinson dyskinesia direction can be assigned to oral ground paprika.
