ID: 0784
Category: herb
Ingredient: marjoram
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh marjoram reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The dried-leaf infusion pilot reports Parkinson nonmotor totals with no separate attention estimate. Nasal oil studies measured EEG or stress and the mouse study measured memory after injected extract. None measured attention impairment after eating fresh marjoram.
