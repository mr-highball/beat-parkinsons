ID: 0369
Category: herb
Ingredient: lemon balm
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh lemon balm worsens freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An oral lemon balm extract improved model mouse movement and rotarod retention. A concentrated oil study in healthy mice reports motor impairment at its highest level. Neither experiment measured episodes of freezing after fresh lemon balm.
