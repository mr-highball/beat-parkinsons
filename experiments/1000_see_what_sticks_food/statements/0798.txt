ID: 0798
Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In 19 healthy trained men a seven-day 3 g/day commercial cayenne capsule crossover found no between-treatment difference in fastest or mean 30-m sprint time; gastrointestinal symptom score was 6.3 times higher. Sprint speed and capsule are not everyday walking or a Parkinson trial. Existing chili FFQ study reports composite sarcopenia without a chili-specific walking contrast.
