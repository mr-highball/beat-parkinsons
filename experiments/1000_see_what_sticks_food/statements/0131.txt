ID: 0131
Category: oil
Ingredient: soybean oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Ground soybeans were compared during standard levodopa treatment, with no separate resting-tremor estimate in the available abstract. Oil-vehicle and placebo comparisons address essential postural or voice tremor and do not isolate a refined-oil effect. No signed comparison transfers to this claim. The complete crossover confirms the auditory and acoustic outcomes measure essential voice tremor. Acoustic means in the soybean-oil condition fell slightly. The oil-free comparison needed for ingredient attribution was absent.
