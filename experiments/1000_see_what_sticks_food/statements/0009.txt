ID: 0009
Category: food
Ingredient: grapefruit
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw grapefruit reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Grapefruit appeared in a mixed diet with a composite motor outcome. A five-patient grapefruit-juice study measured cabergoline in blood, and a rat study measured seizure-provoked tremor onset with a different drug. Rest tremor after eating raw grapefruit remains unresolved.
