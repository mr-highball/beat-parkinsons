ID: 0009
Category: food
Ingredient: grapefruit
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw grapefruit reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Clinical studies measured resting tremor during cabergoline treatment and described some improvement during a separate antibiotic interaction. Grapefruit juice raised drug levels in a small study that did not measure tremor. Fresh pulp was tested with other medicines. The shared meal, induced animal signs and public accounts do not establish a raw-grapefruit effect on resting tremor.
