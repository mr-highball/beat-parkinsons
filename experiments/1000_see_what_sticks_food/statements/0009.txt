ID: 0009
Category: food
Ingredient: grapefruit
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw grapefruit reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Grapefruit appeared in a mixed diet with a composite motor outcome. A five-person juice study measured blood cabergoline concentrations. Two rodent studies measured drug-induced signs such as tremor. The Japanese review gives no separate quantified selegiline interaction or rest-tremor estimate. The effect of raw grapefruit on clinical rest tremor remains unresolved.
