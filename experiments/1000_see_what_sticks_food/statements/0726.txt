ID: 0726
Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Corn-oil studies use a shared carrier, mixed or topical formulations, or tests outside Parkinson disease. Overnight polysomnography in healthy people gave inconclusive comparisons and used corn oil in both arms. Topical postherpetic neuralgia treatment measured global sleep quality. The adolescent protocol gives no verified sleep results. No rated Parkinson insomnia comparison isolates refined corn oil.
