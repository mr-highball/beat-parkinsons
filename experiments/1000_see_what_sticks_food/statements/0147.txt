ID: 0147
Category: food
Ingredient: broccoli
Preparation: raw
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw broccoli reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Randomized sulforaphane and glucoraphanin capsule trials conflict on attention: Hei 2022 (n=172 schizophrenia) found inconclusive attention/vigilance change numerically favoring placebo (p=0.263) and Nouchi 2022 (n=144 older adults) found null Coding (p=0.83) and Stroop (p=1) despite Symbol Search p=0.03, while Ghannadi 2025 (n=63 pediatric ADHD methylphenidate adjunct, abstract-only) favored sulforaphane without confirming botanical source; the recorded searches did not locate a raw-broccoli Parkinson attention comparison. The publisher abstract confirms the direction of the parent and teacher inattention ratings and their effect-size notation. Full values, the botanical source and raw exposure remain unverified. The two separate trial registrations report no results. A possible official pediatric adjunct registry has the same setting and broad design as the reported trial. Its planned enrollment and dose units differ from the published abstract, so the linkage remains provisional and does not add an independent outcome. The pediatric inattention result remains an observed improvement without an eligible raw-broccoli Parkinson effect. The broccoli-derived nutraceutical schizophrenia trial reports no statistically significant overall or individual cognitive-domain differences. The attention-specific follow-up estimate is absent from the checked report. The 2020 NCT04521868 record names two different sites in separate fields and has no posted results. Chen analyzed 42 long-stay patients at Jiangyin and reports no clear MCCB composite or subscore difference. The exact attention values remain unavailable. Huang analyzed 77 patients at Xiangya and used the PANSS cognitive factor, with no separate attention measure in the paper or supplement. Participant overlap remains unresolved, so the reports contribute one linked family of uncertain independence. Preparation and population differ from the frozen claim.
