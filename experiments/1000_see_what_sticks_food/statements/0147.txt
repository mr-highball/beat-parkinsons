Category: food
Ingredient: broccoli
Preparation: raw
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw broccoli reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Attention-vigilance results were inconclusive in adults receiving a sulforaphane adjunct for schizophrenia. A pediatric adjunct trial favored the supplement on rated inattention. Older adults showed task-dependent processing results. The product forms and populations leave raw broccoli effects on Parkinson attention open.
