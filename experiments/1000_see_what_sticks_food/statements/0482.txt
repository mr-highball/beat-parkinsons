Category: food
Ingredient: blackberry
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming frozen blackberry reduces low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study evaluates frozen blackberry on vocal loudness or low voice volume severity in Parkinson disease. Boysenberry-apple trials measure provoked throat irritation or a seven-item pediatric respiratory composite without acoustic loudness; Parkinson diet cohorts (MVP, Thai diet survey) pool berry species without voice-volume contrasts; and ethnobotanical jam or anaphylaxis reports supply no controlled loudness measurement.
