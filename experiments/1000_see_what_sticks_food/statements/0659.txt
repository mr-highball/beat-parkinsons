ID: 0659
Category: herb
Ingredient: summer savory
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh summer savory reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized pediatric chemotherapy-mucositis trial (n=60) reports faster local pain reduction with topical summer-savory extract gel added to routine care versus placebo gel (IRCT2016061813691N7). The closer oral dried-leaf knee-osteoarthritis trial (n=39 analyzed of 60) found an inconclusive between-group pain contrast (p=.38; IRCT2013112515542N1), and rodent assays split between formalin/writhing attenuation and null 6-OHDA rat carvacrol hyperalgesia (PMID29489959). Topical mucositis route and the null oral knee trial keep the easing lean for fresh culinary summer savory at minimal_signal.
