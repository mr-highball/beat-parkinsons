Statement: Consuming fresh summer savory reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: In 39 of 60 knee osteoarthritis participants, reported pain change favored oral dried leaf powder over placebo (−2 versus −0.5, p = .38). Topical mucositis and oral animal findings provide limited corroboration. Attrition, conflicting dose details, different preparation, and the Parkinson pain gap keep the lean minimal.
