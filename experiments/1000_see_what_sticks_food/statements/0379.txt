ID: 0379
Category: food
Ingredient: brown rice
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: dream enactment frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed brown rice reduces dream enactment frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Human rice germ, bran or protein studies measured REM share or duration without recording dream enactment. The sedative rat study measured vigilance states without REM atonia or dream behavior. Steamed whole brown rice and a Parkinson population remain unresolved.
