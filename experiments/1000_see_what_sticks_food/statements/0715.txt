ID: 0715
Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A pediatric ADHD trial reports lower inattention scores after almond syrup, with a baseline imbalance and no clearly reported adjusted comparison for the endpoint. An Alzheimer powdered-almond trial was inconclusive. Differences in formulation and population leave cold-pressed oil and Parkinson attention unresolved.
