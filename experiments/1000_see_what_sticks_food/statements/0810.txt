ID: 0810
Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two independent randomized placebo-controlled human trials of coated proprietary Cucumis melo cantaloupe juice concentrate (Extramel 10 mg/day) report reduced subjective fatigue in stressed or healthy adults: a four-week trial in 70 adults reported 26% greater day-28 FARD weariness improvement versus placebo (Milesi 2009, PMID19754931, PMC2757026), and an 84-day trial in 61 healthy adults reported lower day-84 Prevost physical fatigue versus placebo (84.4 vs 93.2, p<.02; day-28 null; Carillon 2014, PMID24949549, PMC4073155, NCT01767922). Conversely, three competing randomized trials of melon SOD/gliadin or dried melon concentrate were null on fatigue (Houghton 2010 PMID21044831 n=38; SODB 2020 PMID32053942 p=.068; GliSODin 2022 PMID35628874 n=46 p=.15). Proprietary palm-oil-coated concentrate versus raw cantaloupe, non-Parkinson cohorts, and three competing null trials keep the easing lean at minimal_signal.
