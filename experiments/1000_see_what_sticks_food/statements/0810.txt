ID: 0810
Category: food
Ingredient: cantaloupe
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw cantaloupe reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two trials favored a coated proprietary cantaloupe concentrate on some fatigue outcomes. Other melon preparations had inconclusive fatigue results. Protective coating changes the tested formulation. The evidence leaves fatigue after eating raw cantaloupe in Parkinson disease unresolved.
