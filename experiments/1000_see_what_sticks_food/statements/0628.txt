ID: 0628
Category: oil
Ingredient: peanut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed peanut oil reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinsonian monkeys developed rigidity after MPTP before receiving 7-nitroindazole in injected peanut oil. No independent rigidity result was reported for the oil, and the cold-pressed oral preparation remains untested in this record.
