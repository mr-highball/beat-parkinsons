ID: 0348
Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: poached
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming poached chicken reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A chicken-breast dipeptide drink reduced weekly self-rated fatigue at the higher dose in healthy adults. Other routine-fatigue comparisons of soup or extract were inconclusive. A trial of steamed chicken in older women measured lean mass and strength. The separate 2018 meal crossover has no posted results. Fatigue in an unrelated restricted-diet scurvy case resolved with vitamin C. The effect of eating poached chicken on Parkinson fatigue remains open.
