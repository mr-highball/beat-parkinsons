ID: 0348
Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: poached
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming poached chicken reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Chicken essence reduced some task-induced fatigue measures in healthy adults. A soup crossover and extract comparisons for ordinary fatigue were inconclusive. Neither ordinary poached chicken nor a Parkinson fatigue outcome was tested.
