ID: 0348
Category: food
Ingredient: chicken
Preparation: poached
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming poached chicken reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Chicken essence reduced some task-induced fatigue measures in healthy adults. Soup and extract comparisons for routine fatigue were inconclusive. In a restricted fried-chicken-and-pasta diet case, symptoms resolved after vitamin C treatment for scurvy, leaving no isolated chicken effect. The direction for poached chicken and Parkinson fatigue remains unresolved.
