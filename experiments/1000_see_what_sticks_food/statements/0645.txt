ID: 0645
Category: herb
Ingredient: spearmint
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed spearmint worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: People with Parkinson had more difficulty recognizing the spearmint test odor than a group with drug-induced parkinsonism. A nasal-polyps crossover compared two mint teas and reported no clear smell-diary difference. These studies do not give a brewed spearmint treatment estimate for Parkinson olfactory impairment.
