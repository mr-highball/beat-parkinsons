Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming frozen blueberry reduces low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson powder trial gives no posted voice-volume outcomes and diet menus do not verify individual frozen berry intake. In a separate case, hoarseness progressed after blueberry avoidance and imaging identified an anatomical cause. No study supplies a frozen-blueberry vocal-volume estimate in Parkinson disease.
