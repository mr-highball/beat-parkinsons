ID: 0642
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two randomized non-Parkinson trials of formulated turmeric extracts in knee symptoms report favorable walking speed (+0.08 vs +0.01 m/s, p<.05 with minor intergroup conflict; PMID29316908) and 80-meter walk time (-11.0 vs -6.8 s, Mann-Whitney p=.0011; PMID34045905). Competing trials show null 4-meter gait speed in healthy aging (p=.9; PMID29951592), null 40-meter walking with Curcugen (p=.604; PMID35010916), an older-adult pilot numerically favoring placebo (n=17, +.014 vs +.103 m/s), and null isolated curcumin open-field speed in rotenone rats. Formulated extracts and non-Parkinson knee cohorts keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal.
