ID: 0642
Category: spice
Ingredient: turmeric
Preparation: grated
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grated turmeric worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Two separate trials of manufactured turmeric extracts found favorable short-course walking speed or time in people with knee symptoms. Other trials gave inconclusive speed results and a small older-adult pilot numerically favored placebo. This gives a minimal lean against worsening with no fresh grated root or Parkinson walking estimate.
