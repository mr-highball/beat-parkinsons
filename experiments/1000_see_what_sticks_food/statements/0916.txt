ID: 0916
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A topical cinnamon patch improved the urgency item in overactive bladder, and high-dose oral cassia powder reduced voids in chemical cystitis mice. Daytime frequency was inconclusive in the patch trial. In a separate oral cinnamon rat study the bladder-distension response fell with cystitis and rose in controls. The dose, route and condition differences leave the direction of ground cinnamon for Parkinson urgency unresolved.
