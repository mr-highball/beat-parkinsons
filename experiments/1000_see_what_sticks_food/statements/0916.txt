ID: 0916
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon reduces urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One double-blind randomized placebo-controlled trial in 66 outpatients with overactive bladder reports lower end-of-treatment Overactive Bladder Symptom Score (6.33 vs 9.73, p<.001), urgency-frequency item (2.82 vs 4.18, p<.001), and Urgency Severity Scale (2.67 to 1.64) after a topical cinnamon patch versus placebo patch, while daytime voiding frequency was inconclusive (p=.79; NCT03532789). Supporting oral rodent evidence shows Cinnamomum cassia powder lowered cyclophosphamide-cystitis void counts (PMC8716214), whereas a rat distension assay diverged between cystitis (reduced) and uninflamed controls (increased). Topical route, non-Parkinson OAB etiology, and animal divergence keep the easing signal at minimal_signal.
