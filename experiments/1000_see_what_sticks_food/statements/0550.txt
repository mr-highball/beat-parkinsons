Category: spice
Ingredient: cardamom
Preparation: ground
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cardamom reduces olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Patients with Parkinson’s identified commercial cardamom essence less often in one diagnostic test. A different natural-odor study found no clear case-control difference. Both presented odors and did not measure change after eating ground cardamom.
