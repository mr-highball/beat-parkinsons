ID: 0281
Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The synthetic butylphthalide trial pools rest and action tremor. Its off-medication subtotal exceeds its stated scale range and duplicates quality-of-life values. An independent drug trial reports total UPDRS, and a mouse study reports less rest tremor with whole-celery solvent extract. These sources leave the human effect of raw stalk unresolved.
