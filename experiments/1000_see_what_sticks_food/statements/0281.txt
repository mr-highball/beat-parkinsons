ID: 0281
Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A pharmaceutical trial has an internally inconsistent rest-plus-action tremor subtotal. Other primary reports measure aggregate motor scores or use rest tremor in baseline criteria. A whole-celery solvent extract reduced resting tremor in a mouse model. These comparisons leave the effect of raw celery stalk in people with Parkinson disease unresolved.
