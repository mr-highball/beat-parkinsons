Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The synthetic butylphthalide trial’s printed composite tremor scores duplicate its quality-of-life row. An MPTP mouse study reported lower rest tremor with whole-celery solvent extract. The human evidence does not resolve rest tremor after raw stalk.
