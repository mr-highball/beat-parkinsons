ID: 0281
Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A whole-celery solvent extract reduced a separate resting-tremor score in a mouse model, opposite to the hypothesized direction. Fresh stalk and extract contain some of the same measured compounds, but no study establishes their causal contribution or comparable exposure. Pharmaceutical trials report aggregate or inconsistent motor scores. The human raw-stalk direction remains unresolved.
