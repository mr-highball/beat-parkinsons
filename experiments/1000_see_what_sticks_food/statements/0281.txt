ID: 0281
Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Whole-celery solvent extract lowered a separate resting-tremor score in mice, opposite to the hypothesis. Fresh stalk and extract share measured compounds, and healthy adults absorbed apigenin after a celery-leaf meal. The leaf reports reuse the same results with inconsistent sample counts. The active constituent and comparable raw-stalk exposure remain unverified. Pharmaceutical studies report aggregate or inconsistent motor scores. The human raw-stalk direction remains unresolved.
