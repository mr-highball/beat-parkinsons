ID: 0281
Category: food
Ingredient: celery stalk
Preparation: raw
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw celery stalk worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Zhou pharmaceutical trial combines rest and action tremor and gives an inconsistent subtotal. Other pharmaceutical comparisons report motor total, baseline phenotype or a pooled response criterion. The whole-plant mouse extract study reports lower rest tremor, with a different preparation and population from raw stalk in Parkinson disease.
