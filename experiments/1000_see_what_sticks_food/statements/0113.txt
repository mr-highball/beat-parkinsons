ID: 0113
Category: food
Ingredient: trout
Preparation: baked
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked trout worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Protein-regimen comparisons in levodopa-treated Parkinson disease reported more dyskinesia during lower-protein daytime conditions. One crossover also reported less daily OFF, a different motor endpoint. These regimens did not test baked trout. The isolated DHA trial gives incomplete dyskinesia incidence counts and no baked-trout severity result.
