Category: spice
Ingredient: sichuan pepper
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted sichuan pepper reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Dry-fried or raw huajiao volatile fractions reduced evoked mouse writhing relative to vehicle. The body and species voucher remain unverified. An uncontrolled mixed capsule improved oral discomfort, while a multicomponent IBS trial was inconclusive. Whole toasted pepper and clinical Parkinson pain remain unresolved.
