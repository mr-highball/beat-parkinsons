ID: 0515
Category: food
Ingredient: barley
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled barley worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An older Parkinson cohort had a larger fall in supine pressure after lunch, while the orthostatic pressure fall was smaller after the meal. The report does not identify boiled barley or give a lightheadedness score.
