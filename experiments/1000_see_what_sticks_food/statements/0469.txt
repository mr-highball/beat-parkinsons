ID: 0469
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: dried
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried saffron reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson whole-saffron powder trial reported global cognition without a separate executive estimate. A dried-extract childhood ADHD trial gave inconclusive executive-related comparisons. SaiLuoTong improved a switching measure by a one-tailed test in older adults with mild cognitive impairment, but its interval spans zero and it combines three ingredients. Dried saffron and executive function in Parkinson disease remain unresolved.
