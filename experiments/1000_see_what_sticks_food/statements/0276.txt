Symptom: speech intelligibility impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh thyme reduces speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Thyme drops and lozenges improved selected subjective hoarseness or speaking comfort measures after vocal loading. Objective measures in the smaller trial were inconclusive. Fresh thyme and listener-rated Parkinson speech intelligibility remain unresolved.
