Statement: Consuming dried thyme reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: An injected thyme extract improved rat balance after cerebral ischemia. Related paraquat, carvacrol and thymol experiments use different preparations or motor outcomes. The effect of eating dried thyme on Parkinson postural instability remains unresolved.
