Statement: Consuming dried cilantro worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: A Parkinson diet listed coriander without identifying plant part, preparation or a standing outcome. Seed powder trials measured general blood pressure, and dried-leaf extracts increased urine output in rats. Orthostatic symptoms after dried cilantro remain unresolved.
