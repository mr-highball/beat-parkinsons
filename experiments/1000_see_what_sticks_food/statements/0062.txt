ID: 0062
Category: herb
Ingredient: cilantro
Preparation: dried
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried cilantro worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Coriander seed blood-pressure trials outside Parkinson disease found no clear between-arm difference versus placebo and measured no postural orthostasis, while leaf studies are limited to rat diuresis and in vitro ACE assays and a Parkinson diet listed coriander without plant part or standing symptoms; dried cilantro effects on Parkinson orthostatic lightheadedness remain open.
