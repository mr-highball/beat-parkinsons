ID: 0537
Category: food
Ingredient: pomegranate arils
Preparation: raw
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw pomegranate arils reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A pediatric fruit study reported stimulated saliva after pomegranate without separating fruit and juice or using a matched stimulated baseline. A Parkinson trial planned a saliva outcome for nanoemulsified seed oil and has no posted results. Raw arils and clinical drooling remain open.
