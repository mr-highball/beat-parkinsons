ID: 0962
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: One Parkinson case reported longer medication ON time with cinnamon powder and honey. Both illustrated diaries mark 15 minutes of dyskinesia and give no severity rating or separate cinnamon effect.
