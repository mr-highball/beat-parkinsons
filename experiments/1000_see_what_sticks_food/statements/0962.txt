ID: 0962
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson case used cinnamon powder with honey and reports longer medication ON time. Each illustrative diary shows 15 minutes of dyskinesia, without a severity measure or independent food comparison. An A53T ground-cinnamon study measures locomotor tasks, and cinnamaldehyde experiments measure neuronal effects. None isolate dyskinesia severity after ground cinnamon.
