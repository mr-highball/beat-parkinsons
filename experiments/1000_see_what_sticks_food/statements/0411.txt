ID: 0411
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An uncontrolled Kampo study reports no clear NPI apathy change from a low baseline. Related dementia reports mix treatment ingredients and give inconsistent apathy results. The original cinnamon survey did not isolate the clinical apathy item.
