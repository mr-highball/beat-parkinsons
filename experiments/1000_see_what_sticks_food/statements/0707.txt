ID: 0707
Category: spice
Ingredient: sichuan pepper
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted sichuan pepper reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson case describes mood change during combined daikenchuto and rehabilitation. Extract studies in mice show behavioral changes, with one report internally inconsistent. A controlled Nepalese pepper extract trial in healthy adults did not give a clear mood contrast at individual tests and did not measure depression. The toasted spice direction remains unresolved.
