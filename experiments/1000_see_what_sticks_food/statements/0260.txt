ID: 0260
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground black pepper reduces olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Ground pepper served as an odor stimulus. Clinical inhaled pepper oil recorded odor identification at baseline only, and the experimental mouse result used isolated piperine. These comparisons leave the effect of eating ground pepper on Parkinson olfaction unresolved.
