ID: 0082
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Parkinson trial of isolated caffeine capsules reports a 6-week UPDRS III bradykinesia subscore difference of -1.70 points (95% CI -3.10 to -0.31; limb-only CI crossed zero; PMID22855866), bridged to brewed black tea by infusion chromatography showing lower caffeine per cup (PMID40647065). Competing evidence includes an inconclusive longer-term caffeine total-motor trial (-0.48, 95% CI -3.21 to 2.25; PMID28954882), an observational PPMI preprint reporting faster progression (body unverified), non-replicated LRRK2 composite findings, and mixed rat exploratory activity. The direct Parkinson bradykinesia constituent RCT supports limited_clinical_link (51), matching claim 0904.
