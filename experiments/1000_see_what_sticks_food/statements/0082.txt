ID: 0082
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: The Parkinson caffeine trial reported a six-week bradykinesia subscore improvement of −1.70 points (95% CI −3.10 to −0.31), with the limb-only interval crossing zero. Black-tea infusion chromatography gives a measured link at lower exposure than the capsule trial. The independent PPMI preprint found worse bradykinesia with habitual caffeinated coffee, a different product and nonrandomized long-term exposure. The longer capsule trial and direct tea cohorts give total motor or incidence results without an interpretable bradykinesia comparison. The short randomized comparison remains the most informative result on the exact symptom and supports a limited easing lean. A matched, brewed-black-tea Parkinson trial remains open.
