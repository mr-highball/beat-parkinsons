ID: 0082
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A short Parkinson caffeine trial favored caffeine on a secondary bradykinesia subscore. Limb-only and longer motor comparisons were inconclusive, and a caffeine preprint reports faster progression. Two actual brewed-tea animal experiments differ on exploratory crossings and do not measure bradykinesia. A weak short-term constituent inference remains plausible without a verified brewed-tea comparison.
