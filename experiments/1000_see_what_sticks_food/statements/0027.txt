ID: 0027
Category: herb
Ingredient: curry leaf
Preparation: ground
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground curry leaf reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Different curry leaf extracts improved toxin-model movement initiation or worsened a drug-model catalepsy proxy. The 2012 extract result at 120 minutes was 110.0 seconds, and the 2025 administration route remains unverified. The diet pilot does not isolate ground curry leaf or report clinical bradykinesia by food.
