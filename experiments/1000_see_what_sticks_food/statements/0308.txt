Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson CBD comparison used corn oil in both arms and measured Epworth sleepiness. The linked results from another registered corn-oil comparator were retracted. An esterified corn-oil study gives a correlation with omega-3 status. No qualifying refined-corn-oil effect is isolated.
