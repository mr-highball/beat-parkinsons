Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The original military study distinguishes 106 agreeing to participate from 78 allocated to the omega-3 or corn-ester groups. Its original Epworth analysis details remain inaccessible and the cohort and comparator do not fit the claim. The Parkinson CBD study used a corn-oil comparator, and another registered comparison has retracted results. No comparison isolates refined corn oil.
