Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The original Methods says 106 agreed to participate. A third-party excerpt of the author's flow figure describes 78 in overall analyses, 44 receiving omega-3 and 34 receiving corn-oil ethyl esters. Randomized and ESS-specific denominators remain unverified. The abstract describes an inverse correlation but prints r=0.30. A secondary review gives p=.247 for its reported arm means. The original table, timepoint and sign remain open. This comparison supplies no estimate for refined culinary corn oil in Parkinson disease. The official NCT01216982 result check remains open. Registry status is UNKNOWN, with no posted results. The given comparator wording and missing report identifier leave its linkage to PMID 24690964 unconfirmed. The Parkinson CBD comparison treats corn oil as placebo. Another registered comparison has retracted results.
