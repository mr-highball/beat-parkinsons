ID: 0218
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground saffron reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A Parkinson Sargol powder trial favored overall sleep quality without a separate insomnia-severity result. Extract trials in other populations vary. One recent Affron study reports inconclusive PROMIS sleep disturbance and a favorable result on sleep-related impairment. The endpoint and formulation differences limit the lean toward benefit.
