ID: 0218
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground saffron reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two separate placebo comparisons of oral standardized stigma extract in adults without Parkinson disease favor a specifically measured insomnia outcome, one on the Insomnia Severity Index and one on pooled Athens Insomnia Scale. Oral stigma preparations share the botanical source and documented constituents. The Parkinson Sargol powder trial favors overall sleep quality but does not isolate insomnia severity. The older-adult ISI pilot is inconclusive, the perimenopause preprint has an inconclusive global sleep result, and another Affron trial has inconclusive sleep disturbance. Different preparations, populations and sleep endpoints limit inference to a minimal easing signal. The application to Parkinson insomnia and the newer extraction and registration trails remain open. The cardiovascular review traces a hypnotic lead to an injected mouse study of pentobarbital-induced sleep, with extract and safranal effects and no apparent crocin effect in this assay. Its adverse-insomnia lead points through Bostan to the existing petal versus fluoxetine trial with three events per arm. These comparisons add preparation and attribution limits. The closer oral-extract clinical results retain the minimal easing lean. The full 1994 oral pistil-extract study found longer hexobarbital-induced loss of righting reflex in the middle arm (p<.05), with no marked significance in the other arms. This independent original joins the animal sedative context alongside the separate 2009 extract and safranal study. It gives no spontaneous insomnia or ground-stigma Parkinson measurement. The study registration confirms both PROMIS scales were separate secondary endpoints and Parkinson disease was excluded. The sleep-disturbance estimate is inconclusive, while the broader impairment measure favors extract.
