ID: 0218
Category: spice
Ingredient: saffron
Preparation: ground
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground saffron reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two separate placebo comparisons of oral standardized stigma extract in adults without Parkinson disease favor a specifically measured insomnia outcome, one on the Insomnia Severity Index and one on pooled Athens Insomnia Scale. Oral stigma preparations share the botanical source and documented constituents. The Parkinson Sargol powder trial favors overall sleep quality but does not isolate insomnia severity. The older-adult ISI pilot is inconclusive, the perimenopause preprint has an inconclusive global sleep result, and another Affron trial has inconclusive sleep disturbance. Different preparations, populations and sleep endpoints limit inference to a minimal easing signal. The application to Parkinson insomnia and the newer extraction and registration trails remain open.
