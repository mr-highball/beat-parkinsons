ID: 0868
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: grated
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grated ginger reduces olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Separate multiscent programs incorporating ginger found an inconclusive randomized TDI comparison and a favorable nonrandomized identification result. Both used olfactory training. Parkinson ginger-odor threshold testing also does not estimate a treatment effect of eating grated ginger.
