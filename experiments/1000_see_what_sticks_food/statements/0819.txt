ID: 0819
Category: food
Ingredient: kiwi fruit
Preparation: raw
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw kiwi fruit worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Fresh green kiwi yielded an imprecise change in self-rated waking alertness. The controlled breakfast study gives inconclusive choice reaction-time and Stroop results after previously frozen kiwi pulp, and the separate COMPASS registry lacks results. The new kiwi meal experiment measured pig brain bioamines. These outcomes leave the direction for raw kiwi and Parkinson attention impairment unresolved.
