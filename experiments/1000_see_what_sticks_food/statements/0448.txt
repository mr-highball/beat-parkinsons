ID: 0448
Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral ground red chili increased acute abdominal pain in IBS-D. The six-week trial found less meal-provoked burning and an inconclusive pain comparison. A formulated chili extract report conflicts internally about early abdominal pain. Topical capsaicin deliberately provoked hand pain in another study. Ground cayenne effects on Parkinson pain remain unresolved.
