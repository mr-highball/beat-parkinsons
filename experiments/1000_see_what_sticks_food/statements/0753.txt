ID: 0753
Category: herb
Ingredient: lemongrass
Preparation: minced
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming minced lemongrass reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The blinded healthy-volunteer leaf tea study gave no clear separation in sleep reports. Favorable inhaled oil findings had design limits. Isolated myrcene changed barbiturate-induced sleep under injection, oral and inhaled conditions, with opposite associations across acute and repeated oral schedules. The leaf-food comparison in Parkinson insomnia remains open.
