ID: 0648
Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground clove worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A six-odor training trial including clove reported better cognition in people with diabetes and mild cognitive impairment. A separate clove-oil compound study used two active doses and self-reports without placebo. Inhaled eugenol yielded an inconclusive Stroop contrast. The results do not isolate ground clove or Parkinson executive function.
