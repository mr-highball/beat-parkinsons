Statement: Consuming cold-pressed macadamia oil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Whole macadamia kernels caused weakness with occasional stiffness in dogs. Rat oil exposure was studied for chemically induced tissue injury and cerebellar markers. No cold-pressed oil or Parkinson rigidity comparison is established.
