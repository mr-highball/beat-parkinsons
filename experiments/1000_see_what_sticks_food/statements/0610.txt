ID: 0610
Category: food
Ingredient: peach
Preparation: raw
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw peach worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The APSEP crossover measured tapping and fine-motor responses with peach juice in every drug and placebo condition. The Parkinson diet studies do not separate peach intake or hand outcomes. Raw-peach effects on hand dexterity remain unresolved.
