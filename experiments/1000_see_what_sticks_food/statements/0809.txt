Category: spice
Ingredient: allspice
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground allspice reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Injected allspice leaf extracts suppressed CNS activity in rats. The original study did not measure mood. Isolated eugenol experiments give favorable and inconclusive behavioral signals with different formulations. Ground allspice berry and Parkinson depressive severity remain unmeasured.
