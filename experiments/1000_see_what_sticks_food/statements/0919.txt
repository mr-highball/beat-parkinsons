Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted bell pepper worsens apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson diet menus include baked capsicum without a published apathy-item comparison. One dietary trial reports apathy scale changes with conflicting post-treatment comparisons and no identifiable roasted pepper exposure. Another trial gives baseline apathy without a published arm response. Isolated capsiate gave no identified willingness-to-invest-effort change. No direction for clinical apathy after roasted bell pepper is established.
