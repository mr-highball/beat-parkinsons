Statement: Consuming fresh lemon balm worsens dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: A small child bruxism study reports inconclusive temporal muscle contraction results after oral lemon balm tincture. A Parkinson survey records lemon balm use without a dystonia comparison. No direction for fresh lemon balm and dystonia is estimable.
