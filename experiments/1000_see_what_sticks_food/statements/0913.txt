ID: 0913
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled or statistically clear evidence links cold-pressed flaxseed oil to bradykinesia severity. In early Parkinson disease (PMID34127758), plasma alpha-linolenic acid was non-significantly correlated with examiner-rated body bradykinesia (age-adjusted p=.171, sex-adjusted p=.372) and did not track dietary intake. Flaxseed omega-3 plus vitamin E trials (PMID28342967, PMID30554092) combined vitamin E, lacked isolated bradykinesia items, and reported inconclusive UPDRS Part III, while older-adult FAB motor series (p=.140) and rat activity (p=.595) were null and isolated-ALA animal tasks do not transfer. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
