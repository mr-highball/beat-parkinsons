ID: 0913
Category: oil
Ingredient: flaxseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed flaxseed oil reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Plasma ALA had weak inverse associations with examiner-rated body bradykinesia in early Parkinson disease. Independent flaxseed-derived omega-3 plus vitamin E trials favored total scores and the smaller trial had an inconclusive examiner motor contrast in a favorable direction. Dietary PUFA and plasma values did not clearly correlate. The combined indirect signal gives a minimal favorable lean with no isolated cold-pressed oil or randomized bradykinesia item result.
