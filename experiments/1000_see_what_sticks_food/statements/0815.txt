ID: 0815
Category: herb
Ingredient: bay leaf
Preparation: ground
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground bay leaf worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two closely related bay-odor reports found better late visual vigilance at the lower odor load in healthy men. The earlier 11-person comparison reported p=.026 across 30 minutes and the later nine-person comparison recorded 93.8% versus 88.0% at 30 minutes. The high odor load showed no clear accuracy benefit and the samples may overlap. The exposure was inhaled; effects of eating ground bay leaf on Parkinson attention remain open.
