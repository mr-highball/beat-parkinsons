ID: 0413
Category: food
Ingredient: strawberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw strawberry reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The randomized Parkinson comparison counted falls with strawberry extract in both formulations. A seven-fruit group gave a near-null admission estimate and a six-fruit crossover gave inconclusive sway results. The recovered inclined-screen comparison improved with blueberry and cranberry, with no strawberry arm. Separate strawberry extract studies measured operant responses or taste learning following irradiation. These and the clinical and laboratory context leave no transferable sign for raw strawberries and recurrent Parkinson falls.
