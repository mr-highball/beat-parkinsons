ID: 0413
Category: food
Ingredient: strawberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw strawberry reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A Parkinson trial counts people with falls, but both groups received strawberry extract and the comparison was a cannabinoid drug. The strawberry-containing fruit group was null for fall admissions and the separate six-fruit crossover had inconclusive postural sway. Powder, leaf, odor and constituent results test different exposures or endpoints. No observed direction transfers to eating raw strawberries and recurrent falls in Parkinson disease.
