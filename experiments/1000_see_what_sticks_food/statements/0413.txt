ID: 0413
Category: food
Ingredient: strawberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw strawberry reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The randomized Parkinson comparison counted falls with strawberry extract in both formulations. The seven-fruit admissions estimate was near null and the six-fruit sway crossover was inconclusive. The 2015 strawberry-powder rats fell sooner than blueberry-fed rats from a stationary rod and later from a wide plank. Strawberry and control did not differ clearly across the three timed tests. The older extract studies and 2006 irradiated-rat learning study add distinct preparations and outcomes. A purified procyanidin B2 comparison counted fewer provoked falls under experimental demyelination at the higher dose, with no strawberry arm and a figure-to-prose latency conflict. The single-group Rett extension adds two people with a fall during a cannabidiol formulation study, with no fruit assignment or recurrence count. These sources give no transferable direction for raw strawberries and recurrent Parkinson falls. Twelve original records and related full-body paths remain open.
