ID: 0413
Category: food
Ingredient: strawberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw strawberry reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The randomized Parkinson comparison counts people with a fall, with strawberry extract in both formulations. The strawberry-containing seven-fruit group was null for admissions and the six-fruit crossover was inconclusive for postural sway. A large cohort finds a strawberry intake association for cognition, and the geriatric survey reports flavor preference. Neither measures falls. The laboratory extracts, food-reaction entries and grouped overeating reports add exposure context without a transferable sign for raw strawberries and recurring Parkinson falls.
