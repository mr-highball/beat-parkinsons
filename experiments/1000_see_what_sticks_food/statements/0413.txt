ID: 0413
Category: food
Ingredient: strawberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw strawberry reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The cohort that queried strawberry pooled it with six other fruits for fall-related hospital admissions (adjusted HR 1.00, 95% CI 0.98 to 1.03). A separate randomized six-fruit comparison reported inconclusive postural sway (p=.457). The Parkinson odor study and mouse leaf-extract results assess different exposures or outcomes. Powder gait results in the older obesity cohort remain unavailable. These sources leave raw strawberry and fall frequency in Parkinson disease unresolved.
