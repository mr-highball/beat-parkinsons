Statement: Consuming raw strawberry reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The published freeze-dried strawberry trial measured fear of falling rather than prospective falls, with a reported p = .518. A separate completed strawberry gait registration has no posted results and did not name a prospective fall endpoint. Raw strawberry and fall frequency in Parkinson disease remain unresolved.
