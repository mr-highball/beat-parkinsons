ID: 0643
Category: herb
Ingredient: lovage leaf
Preparation: dried
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried lovage leaf reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The clinical-use survey identifies root decoction for joint pain and a separate leaf tea for inflammation. Root powder reduced a rat cystitis pain proxy, and leaf extracts were tested only in enzyme assays. The patent results use a different species and rhizome. A dried lovage leaf effect on Parkinson pain remains unresolved.
