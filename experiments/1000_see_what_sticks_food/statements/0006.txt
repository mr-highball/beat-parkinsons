ID: 0006
Category: herb
Ingredient: shiso
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh shiso reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Dried leaf extract gave similar stool-day and constipation-score changes to placebo. Seed-oil data favor a selected score in athletes. The mixed-tablet patent evidence differs by formulation and one table contradicts its narrative. No result isolates whole fresh shiso for constipation in Parkinson disease. The direction remains unresolved.
