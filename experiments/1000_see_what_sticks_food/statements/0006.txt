ID: 0006
Category: herb
Ingredient: shiso
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh shiso reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Dried leaf extract and placebo yielded similar stool-day and constipation-score changes. Separately, seed oil favored selected constipation ratings in athletes, with a weak fixed-order pilot in sedentary adults. The seed-oil signal and absence of a fresh-leaf estimate leave the direction open.
