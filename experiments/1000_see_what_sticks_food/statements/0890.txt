ID: 0890
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed canola oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two other-oil or active-product comparisons in osteoarthritis supply no clear pain difference or oil-only estimate. A Mediterranean arthritis program reports less pain while changing many foods. The Sunola trial has an unresolved oil identity. Clinical Parkinson pain under cold-pressed canola remains unresolved.
