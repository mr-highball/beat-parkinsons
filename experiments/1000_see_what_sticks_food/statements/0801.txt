Category: oil
Ingredient: rice bran oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined rice bran oil reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An open-label Parkinson study reported no clear change in constipation severity or bowel frequency after a multicomponent bar containing rice bran. The effect of refined rice bran oil remains unresolved.
