Statement: Consuming cold-pressed walnut oil worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Walnut oil accelerated experimentally conditioned fear extinction. Inconclusive anxiety measures in whole-walnut comparisons and a mixed-oil comparator do not establish a direction for cold-pressed walnut oil in Parkinson anxiety.
