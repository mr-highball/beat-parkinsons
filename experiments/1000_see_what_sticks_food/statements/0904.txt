ID: 0904
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: A Parkinson placebo comparison separates rest tremor and reports a small favorable isolated caffeine estimate with intervals spanning zero. Chromatography verifies the link to brewed black tea, with different amounts and food matrix. Open-label mixed responses and reported worsening with coffee limit transfer. The brewed-tea effect remains uncertain.
