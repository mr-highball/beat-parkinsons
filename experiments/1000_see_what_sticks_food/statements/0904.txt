ID: 0904
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Isolated caffeine favored less Parkinson rest tremor at weeks three and six with intervals spanning zero. The trial used higher amounts than measured servings of black tea. Separate sources include worsening in some caffeine or coffee users, an acute oral caffeine test without clear worsening and tea cohorts with inconclusive or conflicting composite outcomes. The constituent link gives a minimal favorable lean for brewed black tea.
