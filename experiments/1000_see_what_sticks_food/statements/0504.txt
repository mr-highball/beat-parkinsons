ID: 0504
Category: herb
Ingredient: tarragon
Preparation: dried
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried tarragon worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Nonmonotonic female rat grip weakening followed oral tarragon extract. Injected essential oil reduced motor coordination. A mixed-extract patent reports greater grip in a different mouse model. Clinical manual dexterity and eating dried leaves remain unresolved.
