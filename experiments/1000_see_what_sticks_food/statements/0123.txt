Category: food
Ingredient: tempeh
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked tempeh worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A randomized pulmonary tuberculosis study found a greater improvement in six-minute walking distance with boiled soy tempeh. Stride length and a Parkinson cohort were not measured.
