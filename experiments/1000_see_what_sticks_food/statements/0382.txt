ID: 0382
Category: food
Ingredient: lentils
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming boiled lentils reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A small crossover describes worse acute anxiety at some times after canned lentil meals. It reports no overall anxiety AUC difference or separate anxiety estimate. No boiled-from-dry exposure or Parkinson clinical anxiety was tested.
