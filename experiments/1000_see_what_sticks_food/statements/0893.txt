ID: 0893
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: cracked
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cracked black pepper reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Two healthy-adult odor experiments found higher saliva secretion after pepper oil. One mixed whole-pepper and capsaicin care case reported increased drooling with inseparable exposures. Clinical drooling depends on saliva clearance as well as secretion. The available studies give no defensible direction for eating cracked pepper in Parkinson disease.
