ID: 0438
Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A 2014 trial used corn oil in all arms and reports only aggregate motor change. A separate six-person open-label trial follows cannabidiol in corn oil and gives no independent placebo arm or rest item. The omega-3 comparator also leaves oil grade and separate resting tremor unresolved.
