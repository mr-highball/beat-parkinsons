ID: 0438
Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: rest tremor severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil reduces rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Refined corn oil has no isolated Parkinson resting-tremor comparison: Parkinson cannabidiol trials (PMID:25237116, PMID:18801821, PMID:31909680) used corn oil as a shared carrier across arms and an active-lipid trial (DOI:10.1016/j.baga.2014.03.004) lacked a no-oil control or isolated tremor contrast.
