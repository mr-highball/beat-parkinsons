ID: 0722
Category: food
Ingredient: cranberry
Preparation: dried
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried cranberry worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: COMBAT confirms baseline screening without a randomized post-treatment depression comparison. In healthy students taking a cranberry drink, one depression-dejection subscale score was higher at week 4. Later values and the HADS depression comparisons were inconclusive. The separate mixed snack and extract studies leave the direction for dried cranberry and Parkinson depression open.
