ID: 0623
Category: food
Ingredient: carrot
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed carrot reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Feeding studies report greater stool bulk with raw or processed carrot and unchanged transit in one original. The Parkinson dietary trial did not isolate carrot, and a high-intake case gives no comparison. Clinical constipation severity after steamed carrot remains unresolved.
