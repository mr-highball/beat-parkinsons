Category: herb
Ingredient: shiso
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh shiso reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Purified perillaldehyde and cold-pressed perilla seed oil separately improved some timed movement measures in mouse models. The preparations differ from fresh shiso leaves and the studies give no clinical bradykinesia measure.
