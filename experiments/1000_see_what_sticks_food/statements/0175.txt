ID: 0175
Category: food
Ingredient: fig
Preparation: dried
Symptom: visual hallucination frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried fig worsens visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Parkinson cohorts and cases describe first onset, visual components of hallucinosis or illusions, and symptoms during medication treatment. The fig and olive trial and the dried fig allergy case do not measure visual episode frequency. Effects of eating dried figs in Parkinson disease remain unresolved.
