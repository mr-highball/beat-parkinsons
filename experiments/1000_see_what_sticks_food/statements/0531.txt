ID: 0531
Category: spice
Ingredient: nutmeg
Preparation: ground
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground nutmeg worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Original case sources document drowsiness during excessive nutmeg exposure. One accidental food-associated report includes alcohol co-exposure. A fruit-syrup study measured task performance without a sleepiness scale. Routine ground-spice effects on Parkinson daytime sleepiness remain unresolved.
