Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A case describes tongue swelling and breathing or swallowing symptoms after aniseed liqueur. Speech intelligibility after ground anise seed intake in Parkinson disease remains unmeasured.
