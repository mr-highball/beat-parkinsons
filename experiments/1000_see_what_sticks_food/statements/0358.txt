ID: 0358
Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil worsens drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral dentifrice and whole-almond chewing studies report stimulated salivary flow without measuring drooling. Swallowing and clearance contribute to Parkinson drooling, so salivary-flow observations do not resolve the direction of severity after swallowing the frozen oil.
