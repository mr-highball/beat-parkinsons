ID: 0767
Category: oil
Ingredient: soybean oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Healthy adults assigned soy-oil capsules reported nearly unchanged sleep-quality scores after eight weeks. The only drowsiness complaint was in the lysolecithin arm. The registry postdates follow-up and lists no separate sleepiness endpoint. Oil refining and a Parkinson daytime sleepiness effect remain unresolved.
