ID: 0760
Category: food
Ingredient: brown rice
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming steamed brown rice worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A liquid-meal study reported postural intolerance with presyncope. A separate lunch study found a smaller orthostatic pressure drop after eating. The reports do not isolate steamed brown rice or estimate orthostatic lightheadedness severity.
