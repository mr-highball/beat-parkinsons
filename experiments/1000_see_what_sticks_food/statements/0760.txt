ID: 0760
Category: food
Ingredient: brown rice
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming steamed brown rice worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled trial tests steamed brown rice on orthostatic lightheadedness severity in Parkinson disease. A randomized within-person study in 39 nursing-home residents (PMID26094950) reported less frequent seated postprandial hypotension after a half versus full rice serving without reporting rice grade, standing posture testing, or lightheadedness severity. Parkinson glucose and mixed-meal autonomic challenges document postprandial pressure drops without isolating steamed brown rice or standing symptom ratings.
