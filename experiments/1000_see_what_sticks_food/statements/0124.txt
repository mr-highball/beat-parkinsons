ID: 0124
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: cracked
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cracked black pepper reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: In a five-person Parkinson and Lewy body dementia aroma series, two improved on swallowing tests, one was unchanged and two dropped out. Other aroma series gave mixed results. Topical piperine in burning-mouth syndrome reported binary dysphagia with different baseline and follow-up denominators. Swallowing severity after eating cracked pepper remains open.
