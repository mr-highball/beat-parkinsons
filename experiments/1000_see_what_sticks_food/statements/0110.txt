Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: The Parkinson baseline graphic suggests slightly less self-rated balance difficulty with more coconut oil of unspecified grade. An independent multiple-sclerosis trial showed a favorable balance trajectory after extra-virgin oil plus EGCG. The combined findings give a minimal lean against worsening, with substantial preparation, confounding and outcome uncertainty.
