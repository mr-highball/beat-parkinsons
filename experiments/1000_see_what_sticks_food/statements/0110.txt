ID: 0110
Category: oil
Ingredient: coconut oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined coconut oil worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled study isolates refined coconut oil for postural instability in Parkinson disease or outside it. The Parkinson baseline figure (MVP-2024) plots only an unquantified tile without numeric coefficient, confidence interval, or oil grade, while PRO-PD observational analyses report only composite total scores. The multiple-sclerosis balance report (PMID36594273) bundled extra-virgin oil with EGCG, had baseline imbalance (49 vs 43), and reported within-arm tests only, while the Parkinson MCT ketogenic study (PMID38561682) showed no clear mobility difference. The claim therefore moves from legacy 51 to open.
