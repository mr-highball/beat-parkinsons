ID: 0205
Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson corn-oil comparison favored DHA plus EPA on depression response while the comparator also had responders. The authentic table and oil grade remain open. In non-Parkinson studies, both the coronary mixed-oil trial and the Alzheimer OmegAD full-sample depression comparison were inconclusive. None isolates refined corn oil against no oil. Supplemental COPD estimates under a 50:50 corn and soy comparator have opposite depression signs across active arms, with both intervals spanning zero.
