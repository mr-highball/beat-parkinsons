Symptom: constipation severity

Statement: Consuming cold-pressed macadamia oil worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The mixed-oil transit study provides no clinical constipation outcome. A whole-nut crossover reports no clear bowel-frequency difference and a stool-type difference without direction. Cold-pressed macadamia oil in Parkinson constipation remains unresolved.
