ID: 0362
Category: herb
Ingredient: thyme
Preparation: dried
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried thyme reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: One dried-thyme trial reported more students in its normal-range HADS anxiety category. The reported baseline spread, categorical cutoffs and registry design limit interpretation. A wild-thyme extract trial is inconclusive. Isolated oral carvacrol and an injected derivative altered mouse behavior, with reporting gaps. The human thyme comparison supports a minimal favorable lean.
