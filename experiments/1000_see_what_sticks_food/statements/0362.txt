ID: 0362
Category: herb
Ingredient: thyme
Preparation: dried
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried thyme reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One randomized student trial of oral dried Thymus vulgaris leaves reports more participants in the normal HADS anxiety range at one month (40/53 vs 31/53 from 27/53 vs 30/53 baseline; PMID38039009), but carries major reporting and registry discrepancies (implausible baseline SD, overlapping cutoffs, contradictory within-control test, and 86 triple-blind registered vs 106 double-blind reported). An independent wild-thyme extract trial found null FDDQL anxiety (+3.21, p=.410; PMID42930530), and preclinical assays test isolated terpenes or extracts in rodents. The student oral-leaf comparison sustains only a minimal_signal easing lean.
