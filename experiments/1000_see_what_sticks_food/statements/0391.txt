Ingredient: soybean oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil reduces daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: An authenticated investigator thesis summary and indexed article clarify acute ON medians of 150 and 270 minutes with the ground-soy comparison. Post-180-minute data were self-recorded. The tested preparation and outcome do not isolate refined soybean oil or daily OFF time. The frozen clinical direction stays open. An independent medication-formulation crossover confirms that first-morning time to ON can vary by tablet formulation. It does not compare soybean oil or estimate a daily OFF response to oil.
