Preparation: refined
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined soybean oil reduces daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Ground soybean prolonged part of an acute levodopa response. The related clinical drug-delivery comparisons used water or medium-chain triglyceride paste, with soybean oil only listed as a hypothetical carrier in one patent. A daily OFF effect from refined soybean oil remains unmeasured.
