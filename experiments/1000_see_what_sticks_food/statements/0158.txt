Statement: Consuming grilled sardines worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: The Bordeaux outbreak reports dysarthria after contaminated preserved sardines. The distinct Minamata study reuses an earlier exposed cohort and reports examiner-rated dysarthria after historical exposure through unspecified seafood. The Parkinson diet data pool oily fish. A grilled-sardine and speech-intelligibility contrast remains unresolved.
