Category: oil
Ingredient: corn oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined corn oil worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral corn-oil trials report sparse adverse-event counts and a very high-dose bolus produced loose stools in three participants. Cohorts, active comparators and event reporting differ. None measures a refined corn-oil effect on Parkinson constipation severity.
