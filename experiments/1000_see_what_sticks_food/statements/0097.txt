ID: 0097
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A meal crossover measuring supine pressure and a ground cassia beverage trial gave inconclusive acute contrasts. Other populations have mixed resting or ambulatory pressure results. Active-standing lightheadedness after ground cinnamon in Parkinson disease remains unmeasured.
