Statement: Consuming cold-pressed macadamia oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The RRIPP-3 macadamia comparator study found inconclusive between-group mood results. A terminated BRAVO registry contains a macadamia-oil comparison beverage and negative-affect outcomes without posted estimates. Neither study identifies a cold-pressed oil effect in Parkinson disease.
