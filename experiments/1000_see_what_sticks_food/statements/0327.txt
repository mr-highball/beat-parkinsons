Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One published human clinical case of focal eyelid dystonia (blepharospasm; Leon-Sarmiento 2005, PMID16258648) reports a decline in rated dystonia severity from 4 at baseline to 1 at six months following transdermal capsaicin patch treatment, alongside an inconclusive blink-rate check in five healthy volunteers (17 +- 3 vs 15 +- 5). Uncontrolled single-patient design, topical isolated capsaicin patch rather than oral ground cayenne pepper, and focal non-Parkinson blepharospasm rather than Parkinson dystonia keep the easing signal at minimal_signal.
