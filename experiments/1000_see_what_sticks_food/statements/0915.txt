ID: 0915
Category: food
Ingredient: potato
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted potato worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A two-variant genetic proxy for potato liking was associated with insomnia, but did not measure potato intake, preparation or Parkinson insomnia severity. A cohort measured boiled or mashed potato and sleep duration with unclear direction. A completed pooled-preparation sleep-efficiency trial has no posted results.
