ID: 0851
Category: food
Ingredient: millet
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming boiled millet reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A labor analgesia study compared epidural selection while both groups ate millet porridge. Poisoning cases involved pesticide-contaminated flour. Neither provides a clean boiled millet comparison for Parkinson pain.
