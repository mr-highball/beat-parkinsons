ID: 0906
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: dried
Symptom: apathy severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried sage reduces apathy severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The dried-leaf Parkinson pilot reports cognition and salivation without an apathy result. In healthy adults, the controlled dried-leaf comparison gave higher alertness before the stress task and lower task-associated alertness after the same dose. A probable mixed-sage trial family gives no clear mood contrast. Compound studies describe movement in animals, and a Parkinson effort task uses apathy as a participant characteristic. These outcomes do not establish a directional effect on clinical apathy from dried sage. The pilot's secondary timetable and the original constituent study tables remain open.
