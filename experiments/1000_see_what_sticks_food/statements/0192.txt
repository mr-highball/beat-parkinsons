ID: 0192
Category: food
Ingredient: mango
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw mango worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The Parkinson diet cohort recorded mango without measuring fatigue severity. The mixed smoothie comparison was inconclusive and only some flavors contained mango. A pulp trial reported biomarkers. The bark-and-flower blend changed some exercise performance measures in healthy men but measured no validated subjective fatigue. The raw-fruit claim remains open.
