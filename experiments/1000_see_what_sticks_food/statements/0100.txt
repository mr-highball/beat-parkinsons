ID: 0100
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 52
Rationale: Randomized comparisons against water or no snack weakly favor lower anxiety outside Parkinson disease and leave expectancy effects possible. The masked fatigue crossover leaves its paired contrast unresolved. MEDI-PD limits dark chocolate within a whole diet and gives an inconclusive Parkinson anxiety interaction (p=.745). This evidence supports only a weak lean against worsening.
