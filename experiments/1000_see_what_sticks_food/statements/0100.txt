ID: 0100
Category: food
Ingredient: dark chocolate
Preparation: plain
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming plain dark chocolate worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two open-label randomized trials outside Parkinson disease report lower state anxiety after solid dark chocolate versus no snack before thyroid biopsy (n=99, STAI group-by-time p=.01) or mineral water in trainee nurses (n=47, 3 days), leaving expectancy effects unresolved. Competing randomized evidence includes a null acute state-anxiety trial in healthy adults (n=90), a small masked chronic-fatigue crossover without a direct between-treatment contrast (n=10), a brewed-cocoa crossover null versus water, and MEDI-PD restricting dark chocolate within whole-diet Parkinson counseling (HAM-A p=.745). No controlled evidence shows anxiety worsening; the non-Parkinson easing trials retain a minimal_signal against worsening.
