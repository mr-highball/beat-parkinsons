ID: 0058
Category: spice
Ingredient: paprika
Preparation: smoked
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming smoked paprika worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Hot-pepper surveys report self-attributed bladder symptoms. Grouped spicy foods had an inconclusive overall flare estimate. Urgency improved in studies of oral mixtures with Capsicum, whose individual contribution is unidentified. Smoked paprika and Parkinson urgency frequency remain open.
