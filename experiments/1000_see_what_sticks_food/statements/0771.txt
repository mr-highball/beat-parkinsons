Category: spice
Ingredient: clove
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground clove reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral eugenol shortened narrow-beam crossing when given before toxin exposure and prolonged crossing after exposure in mice. Clinical fall frequency after ground clove remains untested.
