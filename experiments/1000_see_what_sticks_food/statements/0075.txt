Category: food
Ingredient: turkey
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted turkey reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Amino-acid depletion affected some attention-adjacent measures in early Parkinson's. The diet cohort pooled poultry and only reported a total symptom estimate. Neither tested attention after roasted turkey.
