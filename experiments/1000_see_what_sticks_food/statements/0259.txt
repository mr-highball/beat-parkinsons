Category: food
Ingredient: orange
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw orange worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: The CAIFOS cohort grouped oranges with other citrus. The adjusted hazard ratio per 20 g/day for first fall hospitalization was 1.01 (95% CI 0.99–1.04); a companion fracture analysis used the same source population and was also inconclusive. The Altemor blend improved a fall-risk screen in a small non-Parkinson pilot, and the SAGE survey associated low combined fruit and vegetable intake with injurious falls in older adults. The four-week Kampo combination in dementia with Lewy bodies used dried mandarin peel and reported only the absence of falls linked to sedation or muscle relaxation. The PRIME Parkinson protocol plans fall diaries for whole-diet and exercise arms and has no results. No interpretable comparison links raw orange intake to fall frequency in Parkinson disease.
