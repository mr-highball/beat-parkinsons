Category: food
Ingredient: orange
Preparation: raw
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw orange worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Prospective older-women citrus cohort (Sim et al. 2018, n=1,429) reported a null association for fall hospitalization (adjusted HR 1.01 per 20 g/day, 95% CI 0.99-1.04, p=0.345), while the Giovannini 2025/2026 Altemor pilot RCT (PMID:40694049, NCT06352099, n=36 elderly) tested a multi-ingredient diosmin+hesperidin+proanthocyanidins blend without isolating raw orange or enrolling Parkinson patients.
