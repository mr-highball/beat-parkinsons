ID: 0640
Category: spice
Ingredient: cayenne pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground cayenne pepper reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A mouse study gives capsaicin-probe effects on a separate drug's dyskinesia response. A rat study instead used the antagonist capsazepine, and an isolated capsaicin comparison measured hyperactivity. The verified ground-cayenne meal study measured metabolites and energy expenditure. Ground cayenne and Parkinson dyskinesia remain unresolved.
