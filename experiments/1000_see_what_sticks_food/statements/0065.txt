ID: 0065
Category: oil
Ingredient: walnut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed walnut oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Whole walnut oil increased evoked pain thresholds in inflammatory and diabetic mice. Independent analyses show comparable major linoleic-acid proportions in cold-pressed oils, providing a limited preparation bridge and a very weak favorable lean. One animal report, different lots and minor constituents, uncertain dose transfer and a comparator discrepancy limit confidence. Clinical Parkinson pain benefit remains unverified.
