ID: 0065
Category: oil
Ingredient: walnut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed walnut oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: One controlled rodent experiment reports higher evoked mechanical and thermal pain thresholds with whole Juglans regia walnut oil in inflammatory and diabetic mice (n=7/group; mechanical fold below tramadol; DOI10.1016/j.bjp.2018.03.007), supported by independent composition analyses linking linoleic proportions across extraction methods (PMID30065427, PMID30651416) and a green-walnut injection assay. No controlled human clinical pain trial tests cold-pressed walnut oil (pain rose in an uncontrolled advanced-cancer mixed-oil ketogenic cohort, and ACTRN12622000083796 uses walnut oil in its placebo without results). Preclinical transfer keeps the easing lean at minimal_signal.
