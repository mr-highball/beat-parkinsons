ID: 0065
Category: oil
Ingredient: walnut oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed walnut oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Whole walnut oil improved evoked pain thresholds in mice, with a limited composition bridge to cold-pressed oil. Pain increased during an uncontrolled advanced-cancer diet offering a mixed-oil shake, without an oil-specific comparison. Related preparations and planned trials leave isolated Parkinson pain benefit unverified. Small nonhuman evidence, material differences and a comparator discrepancy limit the favorable lean.
