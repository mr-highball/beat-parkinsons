ID: 0026
Category: oil
Ingredient: grapeseed oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed grapeseed oil reduces hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A small wristband patent reports improvement with topical grapeseed oil, and a Parkinson rat study measured stride and grid descent after hydroalcoholic seed extract. Registered oral oil work concerns grip strength and has no posted result.
