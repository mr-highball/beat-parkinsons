ID: 0072
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: extra-virgin
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 53
Rationale: The active-oil trial outside Parkinson disease gives a favorable 4.22-point adjusted constipation difference for extra-virgin versus refined oil. MEDI-PD's whole-diet comparison is inconclusive, and the MED-PARK protocol has no reported bowel results or assigned oil grade. The weak favorable lean rests on the active comparison outside Parkinson disease.
