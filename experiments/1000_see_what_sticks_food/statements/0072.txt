ID: 0072
Category: oil
Ingredient: olive oil
Preparation: extra-virgin
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming extra-virgin olive oil reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: A randomized active-oil comparison in 140 adults with functional constipation favored raw extra-virgin over refined olive oil on constipation and stool form. It excluded neurological causes. The Albacete original shows a favorable association for virgin olive oil, contrary to a secondary review classification, with only 11 low consumers and no controlled Parkinson analysis. MEDI-PD and the independent Fernandez waitlist trial found inconclusive Parkinson whole-diet comparisons. Fernandez measured olive oil within dietary adherence and did not assign isolated oil or grade. The ulcerative-colitis crossover between-oil result and an isolated Parkinson trial remain open. The matched active-oil comparison supports a minimal easing lean.
