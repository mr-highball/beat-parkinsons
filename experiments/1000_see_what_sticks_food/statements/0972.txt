ID: 0972
Category: herb
Ingredient: sage
Preparation: ground
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground sage reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Oral sage extract improved an index based on paw length and toe spread in mice. The result gives no stride-length estimate for ground sage in Parkinson disease.
