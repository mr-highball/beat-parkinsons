Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed canola oil reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A canola-containing low-fat diet included reports of lightheadedness and a midodrine change. A cold-pressed turnip-rapeseed crossover measured resting pressure. Standing-triggered lightheadedness severity after cold-pressed canola remains unresolved.
