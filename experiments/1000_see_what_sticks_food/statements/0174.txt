ID: 0174
Category: food
Ingredient: oats
Preparation: rolled
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming rolled oats reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A chronic insomnia trial used plain rolled oats as a comparator. Oat-arm changes and randomized contrasts were inconclusive. New trials with mixtures, extract or a shared oat challenge, plus a pooled whole-grain cohort, do not isolate rolled oats or resolve Parkinson insomnia.
