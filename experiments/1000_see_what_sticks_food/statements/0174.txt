ID: 0174
Category: food
Ingredient: oats
Preparation: rolled
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming rolled oats reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A chronic insomnia trial used plain rolled oats as a comparator and measured sleep diaries. Oat-arm changes and between-condition interactions were inconclusive. Extract and mixed-product studies leave rolled oats and Parkinson insomnia unresolved.
