ID: 0120
Category: spice
Ingredient: cinnamon
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground cinnamon worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Both arms of a Parkinson diet trial contained cinnamon. A separate distilled three-herb trial in multiple sclerosis reported a group-by-time fatigue effect and inconsistent table details. The endometriosis capsule registration gives no fatigue results. Ground cinnamon itself has no verified Parkinson fatigue comparison.
