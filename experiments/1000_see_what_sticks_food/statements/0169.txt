ID: 0169
Category: oil
Ingredient: grapeseed oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined grapeseed oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: A topical grapeseed-oil massage trial measured pregnancy edema and had no depression endpoint. A mild cognitive impairment conference abstract reported a within-arm decrease after oral seed powder but gave no clear between-arm mood estimate. The separate migraine extract trial had an inconclusive adjusted depression contrast (p=.95). These exposures do not give an attributable effect of orally consumed refined grapeseed oil on Parkinson depression.
