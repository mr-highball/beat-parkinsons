ID: 0852
Category: spice
Ingredient: ginger
Preparation: grated
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming grated ginger worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Randomized older-women trial used powdered ginger in water; hypertensive SBP changed 145.82→142.54 mmHg on ginger versus 150.00→147.30 on control, similar changes. It measures resting pressure, no standing challenge or lightheadedness; grating and Parkinson status do not match. In an independent 22-man meal study, ginger was finely chopped and serial BP was measured; standing symptom was not assessed.
