ID: 0307
Category: food
Ingredient: arugula
Preparation: raw
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw arugula reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether raw arugula reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in Parkinson's disease because dietary nitrate studies assess resting blood pressure in healthy adults (which would not be expected to alleviate orthostatic hypotension) and no study has measured standing blood pressure or orthostatic symptoms in PD.
