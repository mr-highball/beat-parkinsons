Statement: Consuming toasted buckwheat groats reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: isolated rutin — the signature flavonoid concentrated in buckwheat groats — reduced anxiety-like behavior across three independent stress, reserpine, and seizure rodent models, though no human clinical trial has tested toasted buckwheat groats on Parkinson's anxiety.
