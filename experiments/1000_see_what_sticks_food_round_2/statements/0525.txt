ID: 0525
Category: food
Ingredient: beetroot
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted beetroot reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: controlled beetroot trials show seated BP reductions in hypertensives without controlled orthostatic lightheadedness worsening, upright physiology trials are null, and one unblinded COPD run-in postural hypotension event warrants monitoring in PD without establishing directional clinical evidence.
