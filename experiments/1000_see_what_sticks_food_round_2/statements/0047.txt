ID: 0047
Category: spice
Ingredient: allspice
Preparation: ground
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground allspice worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human clinical study evaluates ground allspice for orthostatic lightheadedness severity in Parkinson's disease. Intravenous P. dioica leaf extracts lowered anesthetized rat blood pressure (ED50 45-53.9 mg/kg; Suarez 1997/2000), whereas 14-day ORAL aqueous fraction caused no systolic BP or heart-rate change, and no human orthostatic challenge exists. Verdict remains Unknown.
