Ingredient: brown rice
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked brown rice reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: three human RCTs (double-blind rice bran extract, cooked pre-germinated brown rice, and brown rice bran powder) significantly reduced depressive symptom scores in non-PD adults, though healthy older-adult pressurized brown-rice trials showed null SDS depression and no PD depression trial exists.
