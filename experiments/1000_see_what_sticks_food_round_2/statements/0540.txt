Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: raw
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw bell pepper worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains open for raw bell pepper and constipation severity. A solitary phytobezoar case report (Mohseni & Kruse 2019) reflects mechanical foreign-body obstruction from swallowing an intact mini pepper rather than functional constipation, and no PD GI trials exist.
