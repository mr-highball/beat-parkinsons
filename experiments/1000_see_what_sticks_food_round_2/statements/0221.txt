Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: raw
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled trial evaluates whether raw apple intake worsens or eases swallowing difficulty severity in Parkinson disease. Raw crisp apple is used diagnostically to measure PD chewing efficiency, while pureed apple-pear acceptance data (de Luis 2001) did not measure swallowing safety or test raw whole apple.
