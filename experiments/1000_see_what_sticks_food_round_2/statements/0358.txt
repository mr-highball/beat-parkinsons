ID: 0358
Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined avocado oil reduces freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or Parkinson's preclinical evidence evaluates refined avocado oil for freezing of gait frequency. Wistar rats fed an avocado oil diet for 20 days prior to intrastriatal quinolinic acid (Huntington's excitotoxicity model) showed fewer ipsilateral turns and preserved striatal GABA, but this is a Huntington excitotoxic lesion rather than a Parkinson's nigrostriatal model and does not evaluate freezing of gait frequency. Verdict remains Unknown.
