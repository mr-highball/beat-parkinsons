Category: food
Ingredient: blackberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw blackberry reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains open for raw blackberry and attention impairment. Mixed-berry attention gains (Whyte 2019) cannot be attributed to blackberry alone, the Park 2025 blackberry-powder poster omits attention-specific subtest estimates and raw data (while reporting null virtual maze flexibility and higher depression), and animal studies measured memory rather than attention.
