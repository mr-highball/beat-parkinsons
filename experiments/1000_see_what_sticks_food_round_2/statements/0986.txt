ID: 0986
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined canola oil reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no trial isolates refined canola oil on Parkinson's depression; Happy Nurse (Watanabe 2018) used a 47% rapeseed-oil multi-oil placebo showing null primary 26-week HADS (p=0.557), Boone 2022 tested preterm toddlers vs omega-3-6-9, Souto 2020 tested maternal pregnancy high-fat overload, and Spanish Toxic Oil Syndrome reflected toxic aniline adulteration.
