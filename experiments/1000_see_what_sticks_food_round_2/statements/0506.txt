Ingredient: beetroot
Preparation: pickled
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming pickled beetroot reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: four non-PD double-blind beetroot-juice RCTs (including an older-adult T2DM crossover improving simple reaction time by 13.9 ms, p=0.009) show modest attentional-speed gains, tempered by the null 13-week older-adult RCT Babateen 2022, a null meta-analysis, pickling nitrate loss, and lack of PD trials (minimal_signal).
