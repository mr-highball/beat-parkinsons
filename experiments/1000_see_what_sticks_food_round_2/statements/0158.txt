ID: 0158
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical study or symptom-matched animal experiment tests ground anise seed for swallowing difficulty severity in Parkinson's disease. Isolated IgE-mediated aniseed allergy case report (angioedema/oropharyngeal reaction) rather than a controlled study of culinary ground anise on PD swallowing difficulty severity; note also Ghoshegir 2015 (PMC4354059) explicitly excluded dysphagia. Verdict remains Unknown.
