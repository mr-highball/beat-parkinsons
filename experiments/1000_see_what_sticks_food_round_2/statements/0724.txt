ID: 0724
Category: food
Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming frozen blueberry worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: a double-blind whole-blueberry powder crossover in FGID adults improved global abdominal relief (53% vs 30%) with a borderline GSRS abdominal pain reduction (-0.3, p=0.08), opposing worsening, whereas full-text review of a knee OA trial (Du 2019) shows null between-group WOMAC pain vs placebo and no PD pain trial exists.
