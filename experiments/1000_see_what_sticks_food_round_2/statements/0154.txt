ID: 0154
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human trial or ground anise-seed study evaluates postural instability in Parkinson's disease. Isolated anethole increased rotarod latency in rotenone-treated rats (Moradi Vastegani 2023, PMC10653401), but rodent rotarod retention is a non-specific gross motor-coordination proxy rather than standing postural stability (matching Round 1's conservative handling for falls), so the verdict remains Unknown.
