Ingredient: canola oil

Symptom: rest tremor severity

Statement: Consuming refined canola oil worsens rest tremor severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no human trial or animal PD model isolates refined canola oil on rest tremor severity; Phillips 2018 bundled unspecified canola oil inside a whole low-fat PD menu, Kheirouri 2023 pooled all non-hydrogenated plant oils, Sampaio 2017 used canola solely as solvent vehicle, and Pohl 2019/2021 tested defatted rapeseed pomace rather than culinary oil.
