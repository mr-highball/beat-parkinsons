ID: 0233
Category: food
Ingredient: apricot
Preparation: raw
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw apricot reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No study evaluates raw culinary apricot (Prunus armeniaca) on drooling severity in Parkinson disease. Saliva-volume testing with Japanese apricot gel (Mizuhashi 2021) involved a different botanical species (Prunus mume) and topical xerostomia gel, which cannot transfer to raw Prunus armeniaca or PD drooling.
