ID: 0141
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: A double-blind randomized trial of exact ground Pimpinella anisum seed powder (4 g/day x 8 weeks) reduced DASS-21 anxiety in menopausal women versus placebo (30.40 to 4.73 vs 27.93 to 21.73, p < 0.001; Begum/Heyat 2026, n=60), with partial rodent support (Es-Safi 2021; Moradi Vastegani 2023) offset by a null aqueous-extract rat EPM assay (Gamberini 2015) and non-isolatable multi-herb trials. Absence of Parkinson's cohorts and reporting-quality caveats keep the easing lean at minimal_signal (Maybe True).
