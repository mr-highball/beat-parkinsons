Preparation: roasted
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted bell pepper reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No human clinical trial tests roasted sweet bell pepper for Parkinson pain severity, and pungent chili/capsaicin analgesic trials cannot be transferred to capsaicin-free sweet bell pepper. However, a controlled multi-cultivar rodent study explicitly testing all four sweet bell pepper colors orally against vehicle and aspirin (Mazhar 2021, N=70) found consistent antinociceptive and anti-inflammatory effects, yielding a minimal_signal preclinical easing lean (Maybe True for 'reduces').
