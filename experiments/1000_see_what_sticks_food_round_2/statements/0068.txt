Ingredient: almond

Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted almond worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): no controlled evidence indicates roasted almonds worsen fatigue, while one raw-almond exercise-recovery RCT showed lower POMS fatigue across recovery (p=0.051) alongside an older march-exhaustion trial, tempered by two null resting-mood RCTs (Coates 2020, Siegel 2023) and absence of roasted-almond or Parkinson's fatigue trials.
