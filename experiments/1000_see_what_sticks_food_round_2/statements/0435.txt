ID: 0435
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: dream enactment frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried basil reduces dream enactment frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or preclinical study evaluates dried sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) for dream enactment frequency in Parkinson's disease. Existing human sweet-basil trials targeted anxiety, depression, menopausal sleep, or pain and omitted dream enactment frequency (while Holy Basil/O. tenuiflorum studies are excluded as a distinct species), so the verdict remains Unknown.
