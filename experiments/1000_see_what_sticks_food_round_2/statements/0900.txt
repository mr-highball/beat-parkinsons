Category: food
Ingredient: buckwheat groats
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted buckwheat groats reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: whole common buckwheat flour in aging mice, buckwheat seed extracts in amyloid-beta mice, and oral rutin in MPTP Parkinson rats improved cognitive and working-memory tasks, while a 2026 human Tartary buckwheat sprout-powder RCT was mixed (null primary PROMIS Cognitive Function p=0.59 and interference RT, but faster Flanker/Stroop reaction times p<0.001 and higher Cognitive Abilities responder rate RR=1.32).
