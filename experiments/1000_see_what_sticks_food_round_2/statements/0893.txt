Statement: Consuming cooked buckwheat groats worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Maybe False: rather than worsening constipation, oral rutin normalized delayed gastric emptying, fecal water loss, and colonic contractility in 6-OHDA Parkinson rats and buckwheat resistant starch accelerated mouse intestinal motility, alongside reduced abdominal bloating in a small human buckwheat crossover, though no human PD constipation RCT exists.
