ID: 0622
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Maybe False: the multicenter double-blind Café-PD trial found caffeine capsules slightly worsened dyskinesia severity (+0.25 MDS-UPDRS 4.1+4.2 points across 3-18 months) alongside historical challenge reports of longer dyskinesia duration, opposing the claim that brewed black tea reduces dyskinesia, though brewed tea itself has not been tested in PD.
