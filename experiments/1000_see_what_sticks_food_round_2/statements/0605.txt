Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: cracked
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cracked black pepper worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): two independent human controlled force-plate trials (older adults and chronic stroke) showed acute reductions in postural sway with inhaled black pepper essential oil, contradicting "worsens postural instability", though exposure was inhaled oil rather than ingested cracked pepper, GRADE certainty is very low, and Parkinson's patients were excluded.
