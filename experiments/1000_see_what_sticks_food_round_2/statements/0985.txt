Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming refined canola oil reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no trial establishes whether refined canola oil eases or worsens constipation; Morvaridi 2020 reported EVOO lowered GSRS constipation in ulcerative colitis but canola-arm baseline/change numbers remain behind paywall, while Sunvold 1995 and Leite 2013 bundled canola with fiber changes or palm olein.
