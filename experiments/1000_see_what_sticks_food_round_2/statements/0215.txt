ID: 0215
Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: raw
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No controlled trial tests raw whole apples for Parkinson pain severity (and clinical trials of 'bitter apple' / Citrullus colocynthis are unrelated Cucurbitaceae homonyms). However, a 12-week human pilot of dried apple peel powder in chronic joint pain (Jensen 2014) and a controlled rat inflammatory hyperalgesia experiment using oral thinned apple polyphenols (Interdonato 2023) both lean toward pain reduction, supporting minimal_signal easing (Maybe False against 'worsens').
