Symptom: constipation severity

Statement: Consuming baked apple worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No clinical trial tests baked whole apples specifically in Parkinson constipation. However, a 12-week double-blind RCT in constipated adults (Sekikawa 2025) and a mechanistic rat loperamide constipation model (Zhu 2023) show improved bowel frequency/evacuation, supported contextually by soluble-pectin slow-transit constipation RCT data (Xu 2014, source unspecified) and a Parkinson's UK patient report (2021), while honestly tempered by null bowel-frequency findings for apple pomace in healthy non-constipated adults (Alexander 2023) and a caregiver report of refractory PD constipation despite daily apples (2023). Overall: minimal_signal easing (Maybe False against 'worsens').
