ID: 0443
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh basil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) essential oil reduced VAS migraine pain intensity versus placebo in a triple-blind trial (Ahmadifard 2020, PMID:32155616, n=144; dose-by-time p < 0.001), and oral as well as systemic O. basilicum essential oil reduced chronic muscle hyperalgesia and inflammatory nociception in mice without motor impairment (Nascimento 2014, PMC4307262; Bae 2020; Venancio 2011), while topical sesame-macerated basil oil matched diclofenac in knee OA without a placebo arm (Askari 2024). Because human evidence uses topical essential oil outside Parkinson's disease rather than oral fresh culinary basil, the easing lean stays at minimal_signal (Maybe True).
