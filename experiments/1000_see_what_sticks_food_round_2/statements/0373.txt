Statement: Consuming raw banana worsens daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Unknown (open): fresh raw banana polyphenol oxidase halves oral levodopa bioavailability in rats and degrades catechol analogs in vitro, creating a plausible interaction risk if mixed directly with levodopa, but zero verified human studies quantify daily OFF time in Parkinson's disease.
