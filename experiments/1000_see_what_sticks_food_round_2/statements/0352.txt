Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined avocado oil worsens daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or Parkinson's preclinical evidence evaluates refined avocado oil for daily off time. Wistar rats fed an avocado oil diet for 20 days prior to intrastriatal quinolinic acid (Huntington's excitotoxicity model) showed fewer ipsilateral turns and preserved striatal GABA, but this is a Huntington excitotoxic lesion rather than a Parkinson's nigrostriatal model and does not evaluate daily off time. Verdict remains Unknown.
