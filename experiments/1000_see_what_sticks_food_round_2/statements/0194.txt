Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: baked
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked apple worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No controlled trial tests baked whole apples for anxiety severity in Parkinson disease. However, an uncontrolled 1-month apple juice pilot in moderate-to-severe Alzheimer disease (Remington 2010) and a controlled rotenone PD mouse experiment using the apple dihydrochalcone phloretin (Shirgadwar 2023) both point toward lower anxiety rather than worsening, supporting a minimal_signal easing lean (Maybe False against the 'worsens' claim) while explicitly tempered by null acute mood results for whole apple in healthy adults (Bondonno 2014).
