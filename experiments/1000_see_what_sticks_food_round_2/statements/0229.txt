Statement: Consuming dried apricot worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No randomized controlled trial isolates dried apricots alone for chronic Parkinson constipation. However, an isolated add-on colonoscopy RCT (Yasar 2017, N=128) demonstrated superior bowel cleansing when stewed apricot juice was added to senna (p<0.001), aligning with bundled mixed dried-fruit RCT stool-weight gains (Steenson 2025), widespread customary constipation use, and firsthand Parkinson's UK forum reports — yielding a minimal_signal easing lean (Maybe False against 'worsens'). Practical safety caveat: poorly chewed dried apricots can rarely swell and cause mechanical ileal obstruction/intussusception, and bitter apricot kernels carry acute cyanide toxicity.
