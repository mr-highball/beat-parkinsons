Statement: Consuming steamed brussels sprouts worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: human sulforaphane RCTs show null dedicated executive-function (Stroop, reverse Stroop, TMT adj p=1) and schizophrenia MCCB cognitive outcomes despite narrower processing-speed gains, and no trial tests steamed Brussels sprouts on PD executive function.
