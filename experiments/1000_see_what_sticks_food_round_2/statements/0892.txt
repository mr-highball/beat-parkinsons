Statement: Consuming toasted buckwheat groats worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: rather than worsening bradykinesia, oral rutin (buckwheat's signature flavonoid) protected open-field motor initiation/rearings, locomotor activity, and nigral dopaminergic neurons across two independent 6-OHDA Parkinson rat studies, though no human PD motor trial has tested toasted buckwheat groats.
