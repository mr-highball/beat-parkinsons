Statement: Consuming ground black pepper reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): one human RCT of inhaled black pepper oil alleviated withdrawal somatic anxiety and two rodent studies of Piper nigrum extract and piperine showed anxiolytic effects, while another aroma study was null on subjective nervousness despite autonomic stress blunting; no trials test oral ground black pepper for Parkinson's anxiety.
