Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed avocado oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical trial tests culinary cold-pressed avocado oil for pain in Parkinson's disease or osteoarthritis. Positive osteoarthritis VAS pain meta-analyses (Simental-Mendia 2019) evaluated avocado-soybean unsaponifiables (ASU), a 1:2 avocado-plus-soybean pharmaceutical extract that cannot be attributed to isolated culinary avocado oil, so the verdict remains Unknown.
