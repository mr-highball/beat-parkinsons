ID: 0819
Category: food
Ingredient: broccoli
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted broccoli reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: purified sulforaphane rescued narrow-beam balance in rotenone PD mice and motor coordination in 6-OHDA PD mice and cooked broccoli retains partial gut-mediated sulforaphane delivery, though roasting lowers bioavailability ~3-fold and no human PD postural trial exists.
