ID: 0421
Category: food
Ingredient: barley
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked barley worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): isolated hordenine (a malted/germinated barley-rootlet alkaloid) improved gait and posture in PD mice via DRD2 signaling, but ungerminated cooked/boiled barley grain does not supply pharmacologic hordenine and no human trial tests barley for Parkinson's postural instability.
