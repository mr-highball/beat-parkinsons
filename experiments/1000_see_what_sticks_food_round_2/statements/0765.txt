Symptom: anxiety severity

Statement: Consuming raw Brazil nut worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no human Brazil nut anxiety trial exists, the whole-Brazil-nut animal study was null for anxiety (opposing hexane-extract and oil rodent studies), and the largest older-adult selenium mood RCT (n=501) was completely null.
