Ingredient: brown rice

Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed brown rice reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: a double-blind rice-bran extract RCT significantly reduced Epworth Sleepiness Scale scores (despite baseline imbalance) and an open brown-rice crossover registry summary reported less post-lunch drowsiness than white rice, though no PD daytime-sleepiness trial exists.
