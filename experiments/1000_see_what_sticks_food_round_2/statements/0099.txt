ID: 0099
Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical trial evaluates oral cold-pressed sweet almond oil for general or Parkinson's pain severity. Faghihi 2022's oral constipation trial recorded only a bowel-secondary subitem (CAS rectal pain with bowel movement (0.88 -> 0.15 vs control 0.73 -> 0.69, baseline imbalanced p = 0.005)) with baseline imbalance, while numerous aromatherapy/massage RCTs used topical sweet almond oil strictly as an external placebo vehicle, so the verdict remains Unknown.
