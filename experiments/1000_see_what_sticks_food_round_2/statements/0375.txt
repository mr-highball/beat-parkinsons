Statement: Consuming baked banana worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Unknown (open): observational epidemiology is conflicting and sex-divergent (inverse in Singapore cohort; lower in men but higher in women in Tianjin), clean RCTs are null on depressive symptoms, and a postoperative banana report carries unreconciled statistical anomalies; no baked-banana or PD trials exist.
