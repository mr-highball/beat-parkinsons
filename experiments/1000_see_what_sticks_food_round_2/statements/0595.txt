ID: 0595
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: ground
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground black pepper worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): a topical piperine mouthrinse case series showed a 1-point ESS drop within normal non-sleepy range confounded by oral pain relief, while oral black pepper RCTs were null on energy/alertness.
