ID: 0840
Category: food
Ingredient: brown rice
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed brown rice reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: two 12-to-24-month RCTs of pressurized whole brown rice vs white rice in older adults improved FAB frontal sub-item 1 and CADi cognitive processing speed, tempered by null executive outcomes in a rice-tocotrienol trial and absence of a PD trial.
