ID: 0071
Category: food
Ingredient: almond
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted almond worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): a 20-week RCT in 175 poor sleepers found improved subjective sleep quality, efficiency, and sleep latency with whole raw almonds (p=0.012-0.016), corroborated by reduced ISI scores in a large before-after student study (p<0.001), opposing insomnia worsening while lacking roasted-almond and Parkinson's trials.
