ID: 0153
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed reduces orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human study evaluates ground anise seed for orthostatic lightheadedness in Parkinson's disease. Aqueous anise-seed extract lowered resting arterial blood pressure in rats via calcium-channel inhibition (Pontes 2019), but resting rodent hypotension does not test human orthostatic reflexes or symptomatic lightheadedness, so the verdict remains Unknown.
