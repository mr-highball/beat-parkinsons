Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: raw
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw bell pepper worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence is open for raw bell pepper and depressive symptom severity in Parkinson disease. Purified nonpungent sweet-pepper capsinoids showed null chronic mood effects in older adults (Yokoyama 2020), and multi-herb mouse mixtures (de Munter 2021) cannot isolate bell pepper.
