Statement: Consuming steamed artichoke worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False

Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): controlled mouse assays show dose-dependent antinociception for Cynara scolymus leaf extract (P<0.001) without isolated human worsening, though the lone isolated-artichoke human epigastric-pain RCT item was statistically inconclusive (Holtmann 2003 p=0.1786), Giacosa 2015 bundled ginger, and no steamed-bud or Parkinson's pain trial exists.
