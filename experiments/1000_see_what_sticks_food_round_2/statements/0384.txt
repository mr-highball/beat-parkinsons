Symptom: insomnia severity

Statement: Consuming baked banana worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): a small controlled clinical trial in primary insomnia found bedtime banana significantly reduced PSQI scores (p<0.05), contradicting the hypothesis that baked banana worsens insomnia, though small sample size (n=21), lack of baked-banana testing, null PSG total sleep time gain in the banana arm, and absence of Parkinson's insomnia trials keep certainty minimal.
