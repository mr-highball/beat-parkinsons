ID: 0087
Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical trial evaluates oral cold-pressed sweet almond oil for general or Parkinson's anxiety severity. Faghihi 2022's oral constipation trial recorded only a bowel-secondary subitem (PAC-QOL constipation-related worry/anxiety (33.5 -> 6.0 vs control 35.9 -> 26.0, baseline imbalanced p = 0.001)) with baseline imbalance, while numerous aromatherapy/massage RCTs used topical sweet almond oil strictly as an external placebo vehicle, so the verdict remains Unknown.
