ID: 0434
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried basil worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: In symptomatic cohorts outside Parkinson's disease, sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum, distinct from Holy Basil) leaf extracts eased rather than worsened depressive symptoms (Talaei 2025, n=57 MDD: BDI 39.44 to 21.04 vs 40.30 to 29.57, p < 0.001; Karimi 2024, n=60 menopausal women: MRS psychological 2.8 vs 4.5, p = 0.001), supported by non-retracted CUMS mouse models. However, a triple-blind trial of exact dried sweet-basil leaf powder in non-depressed postpartum women showed no preventive effect (Abdi 2025, n=77; EPDS 1.63 vs 1.61, p = 0.966), and zero Parkinson's trials exist, keeping the lean against worsening at minimal_signal (Maybe False).
