ID: 0093
Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or experimental evidence tests oral cold-pressed sweet almond oil for executive function impairment in Parkinson's disease. Oral almond-oil trials addressed lipids or constipation rather than executive function impairment, and topical massage trials used almond oil as an external vehicle, so the verdict remains Unknown.
