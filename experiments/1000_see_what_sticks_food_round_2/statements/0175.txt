Category: spice
Ingredient: annatto
Preparation: ground
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground annatto worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): two double-blind human RCTs of isolated annatto constituents (Annatto-GG 300 in D'Adamo 2026 p>0.44; annatto tocotrienol in Shen 2018 p=0.079) showed no statistically significant vitality/fatigue difference, and no ground-seed or Parkinson's fatigue trial exists.
