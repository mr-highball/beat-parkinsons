Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed canola oil worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no trial tests cold-pressed canola oil on Parkinson's insomnia; Stubbs 2025 found null 12-week sleep differences between ketone ester and canola oil placebo in older adults, Boone 2022 found null toddler sleep differences, and Watanabe 2018's transient 13-week ISI gap used a 47% rapeseed oil blend that was null at 26 and 52 weeks.
