ID: 0906
Category: food
Ingredient: buckwheat groats
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked buckwheat groats reduces low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no controlled trial tests cooked buckwheat groats on Parkinson's low voice volume severity; uncontrolled adverse clinical reports document acute IgE buckwheat anaphylaxis with laryngeal hoarseness/dysphagia and rare neuropathy with hoarseness, dysphagia, or facial paralysis in diabetic users of Tartary buckwheat products, but these reflect allergy or confounded toxicity rather than a reproducible PD symptom effect.
