Symptom: depressive symptom severity

Statement: Consuming boiled barley reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): one double-blind cooked waxy barley RCT showed a significant between-group reduction in validated J-PHQ-9 depressive symptom scores (p=0.009) alongside positive bundled-barley Talbinah elderly data, but two isolated barley trials were null on depression subscales (Araki 2024, Alhassani 2018) and no Parkinson's depression trial exists.
