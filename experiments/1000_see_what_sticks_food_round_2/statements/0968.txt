Rationale: Maybe False: three double-blind human RCTs of ergothioneine—an antioxidant confirmed to double in human blood after daily white button mushroom intake—improved sleep length, sleep initiation, and sleep quality in non-PD adults, opposing worsening, though no trial tests whole cooked A. bisporus on PD insomnia.
