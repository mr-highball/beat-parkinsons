Ingredient: black beans

Symptom: freezing of gait frequency

Statement: Consuming boiled black beans worsens freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether boiled black beans worsens freezing of gait frequency in Parkinson's disease because no controlled study of Phaseolus vulgaris black beans has assessed freezing of gait frequency.
