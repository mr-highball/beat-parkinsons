ID: 0544
Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: grilled
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming grilled bell pepper reduces drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Insufficient evidence to determine whether grilled sweet bell pepper reduces drooling severity in Parkinson disease. Pungent chili/capsaicin trials (such as topical capsaicin ointment/troches for dysphagia or TRPV1 urinary/bladder instillation) are strictly excluded because sweet bell pepper (Capsicum annuum var. grossum) is virtually capsaicin-free.
