ID: 0041
Category: spice
Ingredient: allspice
Preparation: ground
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground allspice reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human clinical study or whole ground Pimenta dioica fruit trial evaluates executive function impairment in Parkinson's disease. Isolated eugenol rodent data conflict between null 14-day oral mouse behavior (Dhara 2020) and paraquat-rat rescue framed around clove eugenol (Uchewa 2025), and culinary pumpkin-spice blend trials (Huang 2022) measured vascular rather than neuropsychological endpoints, so the verdict remains Unknown.
