Statement: Consuming steamed brown rice worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: two double-blind rice-bran arabinoxylan RCTs improved bodily or cancer-related pain and a fermented brown-rice milk RCT eased abdominal pain in non-PD adults, opposing worsening, though no trial tests steamed whole brown rice on Parkinson pain.
