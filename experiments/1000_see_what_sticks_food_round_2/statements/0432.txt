ID: 0432
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried basil reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or non-retracted animal study evaluates dried sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) for attention impairment in Parkinson's disease. Existing non-retracted rodent studies tested memory (NOR/MWM) rather than attention, retracted cognitive papers (PMID:31997265, PMID:29356981) are excluded, and Holy Basil (O. tenuiflorum) trials are a distinct species, leaving the verdict Unknown.
