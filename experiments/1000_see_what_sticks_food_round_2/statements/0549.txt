Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: raw
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw bell pepper worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Insufficient evidence to determine whether raw sweet bell pepper worsens insomnia severity in Parkinson disease. Pungent chili/capsaicin studies are excluded because sweet bell pepper is virtually capsaicin-free.
