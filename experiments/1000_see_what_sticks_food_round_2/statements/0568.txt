Category: food
Ingredient: black beans
Preparation: canned
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming canned black beans worsens daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether canned black beans worsens daily off time in Parkinson's disease because no PD levodopa-challenge or motor-diary study has tested canned black beans, even though non-PD trials show lower postprandial EAA spikes from canned black beans than animal proteins.
