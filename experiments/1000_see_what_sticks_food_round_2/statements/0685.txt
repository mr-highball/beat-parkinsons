ID: 0685
Category: food
Ingredient: blackberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw blackberry worsens dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or preclinical study evaluates true Rubus blackberry on levodopa-induced dyskinesia severity in Parkinson disease. The frequently retrieved MPTP mouse dyskinesia paper titled 'blackberry' (Fahimi & Jahromy 2018) actually administered Morus nigra (black mulberry) juice and is strictly excluded.
