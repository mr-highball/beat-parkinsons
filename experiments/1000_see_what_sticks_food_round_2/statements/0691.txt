Statement: Consuming raw blackberry worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No randomized controlled trial has tested raw blackberries on insomnia severity in Parkinson disease. However, a large, population-weighted multivariable NHANES analysis (Zhang 2023, N=29,217) identified an isolated, statistically significant inverse association between blackberry consumption and reported sleep difficulty (OR 0.63, 95% CI 0.40-0.97, p=0.036), supporting a minimal_signal easing lean (Maybe False against 'worsens') while noting cross-sectional limitations and possible residual confounding.
