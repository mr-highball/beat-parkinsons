ID: 0536
Category: food
Ingredient: beetroot
Preparation: pickled
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming pickled beetroot reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: multiple non-PD double-blind RCTs (including a 12-week middle-aged/older adult gait-speed RCT, a 122-patient multicenter COPD shuttle-walk RCT, postmenopausal 6MWT trials, and a baked whole-beetroot velocity crossover) show modest walking-speed/distance gains with beetroot, tempered by competing null walking trials, pickling nitrate loss, and lack of PD trials (minimal_signal).
