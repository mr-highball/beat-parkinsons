ID: 0503
Category: food
Ingredient: beef
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked beef reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: one 24-week older-adult RCT pairing cooked lean red meat (including beef cuts) with resistance training showed a modest secondary gait-speed improvement (+0.05 m/s, p=0.042), but pooled protein meta-analyses are null for gait speed, beef was combined with lamb/veal and exercise, and no PD clinical trial exists, limiting support to minimal_signal.
