Ingredient: button mushroom

Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted button mushroom reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: lyophilized whole Agaricus bisporus improved narrow-beam balance in aged rats and an A. bisporus protein extract reduced beam-walking errors in MPTP Parkinson mice, though roasting denatures tyrosinase and no human PD postural trial exists.
