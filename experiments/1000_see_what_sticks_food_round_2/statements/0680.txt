Statement: Consuming frozen blackberry worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence is open for frozen blackberry and constipation severity in Parkinson disease. Traditional astringent framing pertains primarily to tannin-rich leaves/roots, lived-experience bowel regularity was confounded by prunes and other berries, and no controlled blackberry GI motility trials exist.
