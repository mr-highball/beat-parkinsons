Statement: Consuming canned black beans reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether canned black beans reduces constipation severity in Parkinson's disease because the sole controlled human GI crossover of whole black beans reported null stool-frequency and stool-consistency effects in healthy adults and no chronic constipation or PD trial exists.
