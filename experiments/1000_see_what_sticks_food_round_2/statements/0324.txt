Ingredient: asparagus

Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted asparagus reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether roasted asparagus reduces daytime sleepiness severity in Parkinson's disease because the larger blinded ETAS50 replication trial showed null daytime sleepiness outcomes (p=0.777 and p=0.201) and no study has evaluated roasted spears or PD daytime sleepiness.
