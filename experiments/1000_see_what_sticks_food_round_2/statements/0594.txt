ID: 0594
Category: spice
Ingredient: black pepper
Preparation: cracked
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cracked black pepper worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): three independent PD rodent models (rotenone, Thy1-SNCA, 6-OHDA) consistently show oral piperine eases bradykinesia (pole test and open-field movement velocity), contradicting "worsens bradykinesia", though trace i.p. guineensine caused acute catalepsy in healthy mice and no human PD trial tests culinary cracked black pepper.
