ID: 0238
Category: food
Ingredient: apricot
Preparation: dried
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried apricot worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence is open for dried culinary apricot and fatigue severity. Fermented Prunus mume (Japanese apricot) vinegar showed null fatigue results in non-PD adults (Choi 2022) and represents a separate botanical species, while culinary Prunus armeniaca flesh has never been tested on PD fatigue.
