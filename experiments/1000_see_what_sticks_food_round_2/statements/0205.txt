ID: 0205
Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: raw
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw apple reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human trial demonstrates that raw whole apples improve or worsen executive function impairment in Parkinson disease. The best isolated whole-apple human crossover RCT (Bondonno 2014) reported null effects across computerized cognitive performance, an Alzheimer apple-juice pilot (Remington 2010) showed null cognitive scores, and bundled coffee-berry + apple-extract testing (Jackson 2022) cannot isolate apple.
