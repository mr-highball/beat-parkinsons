Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Enteric-coated Pimpinella anisum oil capsules significantly reduced constipation severity and defecation difficulty versus placebo and peppermint oil in a double-blind IBS trial (Mosaffa-Jahromi 2016, PMID:27815079, n=120; p < 0.0001), aligning with widespread MENA traditional constipation use. However, the popular Brazilian tea trial (Picon 2010) is confounded by stimulant Senna, ground aniseed powder acted as an antidiarrheal in COVID-19 GI patients (PMC10824915), and no Parkinson's or ground-seed constipation trial exists, capping the easing lean at minimal_signal (Maybe True).
