Category: food
Ingredient: broccoli
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted broccoli worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no clinical gait-speed study tests roasted broccoli in Parkinson disease, and preclinical injected sulforaphane beam-traversal or osteoarthritis gait-symmetry models do not establish a walking-speed effect after roasted broccoli.
