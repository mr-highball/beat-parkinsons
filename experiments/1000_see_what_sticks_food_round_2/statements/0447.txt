ID: 0447
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh basil reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No reliable clinical or preclinical evidence tests fresh sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) for walking slowness in Parkinson's disease. Existing haloperidol and rotenone rat extract papers (Behera 2023; Gayakwad 2026) contain disqualifying internal reporting contradictions, high-dose oral basil EO caused mouse motor impairment (Sa Filho 2026), and Holy Basil (O. tenuiflorum) studies are excluded as a distinct species. In addition, Askari 2024 (PMC11103000, n=86 knee OA) directly measured an 8-m walking test for topical sesame-macerated basil oil and found completely NULL performance even within-group (13.34 -> 13.30 s, p = 0.975; baseline imbalanced p = 0.006). Verdict remains Unknown.
