Category: food
Ingredient: almond
Preparation: raw
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw almond worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): five adult RCTs including a 128-person raw whole-almond trial consistently show null effects on attention, while pediatric ADHD syrup trials are active-controlled or bundled with raisins and baseline-imbalanced; no Parkinson's attention trial exists.
