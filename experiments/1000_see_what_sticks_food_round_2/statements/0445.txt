ID: 0445
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: small handwriting severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming fresh basil reduces small handwriting severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No reliable clinical or preclinical evidence tests fresh sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) for small handwriting severity in Parkinson's disease. Existing haloperidol and rotenone rat extract papers (Behera 2023; Gayakwad 2026) contain disqualifying internal reporting contradictions, high-dose oral basil EO caused mouse motor impairment (Sa Filho 2026), and Holy Basil (O. tenuiflorum) studies are excluded as a distinct species. Verdict remains Unknown.
