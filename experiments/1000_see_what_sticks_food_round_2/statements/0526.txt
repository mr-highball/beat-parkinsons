Symptom: pain severity

Statement: Consuming roasted beetroot reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: multiple non-PD double-blind beetroot-juice RCTs ease provoked post-exercise pressure pain/soreness and delay PAD claudication pain, supported by a 6-RCT meta-analysis and rodent antinociception, but null marathon/PAD trials, juice-vs-roasted preparation differences, and absence of PD pain studies hold this at minimal_signal.
