Statement: Consuming ground annatto reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): three independent controlled rodent EPM studies of oral bixin, dietary annatto-derived geranylgeraniol, and Bixa orellana leaf extract show anxiolytic activity, while human trials of isolated tocotrienols/GG in non-anxious adults did not administer dedicated anxiety scales and no ground-seed or Parkinson's anxiety trial exists.
