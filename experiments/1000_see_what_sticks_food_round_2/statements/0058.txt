Category: food
Ingredient: almond
Preparation: raw
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw almond worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): multiple human trials of raw or whole almonds show higher stool frequency (Creedon 2022 Table 5 P=0.034), fewer hard stools (Sartori 2026 P=0.002), and lower hemodialysis constipation (Lambert 2020), supported by oral sweet almond oil RCT relief (Faghihi 2022), opposing the worsening hypothesis while lacking a controlled Parkinson's trial.
