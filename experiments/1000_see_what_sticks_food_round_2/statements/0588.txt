Ingredient: black beans

Symptom: speech intelligibility impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled black beans worsens speech intelligibility impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether boiled black beans worsens speech intelligibility impairment in Parkinson's disease because no controlled study of Phaseolus vulgaris black beans has assessed speech intelligibility impairment.
