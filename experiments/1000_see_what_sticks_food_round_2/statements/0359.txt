Ingredient: avocado oil

Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined avocado oil worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or Parkinson's preclinical evidence evaluates refined avocado oil for insomnia severity. Cold-pressed avocado oil (4 mL/kg x 90 d) in DIO mice showed null EPM and open-field crossings versus obese controls (PMC9319255), while maternal/lactation avocado oil induced anxiogenic-like EPM/OFT behavior and lower adult offspring locomotion in rats (PMID:40024360); neither tested insomnia severity or human PD. Verdict remains Unknown.
