Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked apple worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No human trial isolates baked whole apple on Parkinson bradykinesia (Phillips 2018 included baked apple crisp within a broader low-fat menu without an apple contrast, and Apple Watch/iOS bradykinesia studies are technology homonyms). Two independent mouse PD models testing oral phloretin (an apple-specific dihydrochalcone) both reported eased hypokinesia/motor deficits, giving a preclinical minimal_signal easing lean (Maybe False against 'worsens').
