ID: 0364
Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed avocado oil worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or Parkinson's preclinical evidence evaluates cold-pressed avocado oil for postural instability severity. Wistar rats fed an avocado oil diet for 20 days prior to intrastriatal quinolinic acid (Huntington's excitotoxicity model) showed fewer ipsilateral turns and preserved striatal GABA, but this is a Huntington excitotoxic lesion rather than a Parkinson's nigrostriatal model and does not evaluate postural instability severity. Verdict remains Unknown.
