Category: food
Ingredient: brussels sprouts
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed brussels sprouts reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: purified sulforaphane reduced anxiety-like behavior across two independent mouse stress and neuropathic-pain models, bridged to steamed Brussels sprouts by confirmed sulforaphane retention on steaming, though no human Parkinson anxiety trial has tested steamed Brussels sprouts.
