Statement: Consuming steamed brussels sprouts worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: rather than worsening constipation, a 4-week controlled trial of sulforaphane-rich cruciferous sprouts reduced Constipation Scoring System scores in constipated adults and a mixed Brussels-sprouts vegetable crossover shortened gut transit, though 23% of UK adults perceive Brussels sprouts cause flatulence and no PD trial isolates steamed Brussels sprouts.
