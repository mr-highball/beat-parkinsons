ID: 0097
Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No oral clinical trial evaluates cold-pressed sweet almond oil for insomnia in Parkinson's disease or general populations. The lone insomnia trial containing an almond-oil arm used intranasal drops as a carrier comparator to Violet oil and CMC placebo without verified pairwise superiority over placebo (Feyzabadi 2018, n=75), while whole-almond trials do not isolate extracted oil, so the verdict remains Unknown.
