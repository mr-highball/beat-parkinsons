Category: food
Ingredient: buckwheat groats
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming toasted buckwheat groats reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: 28-day oral Tartary buckwheat extract extended exhaustive swimming endurance and improved glycogen/lactate anti-fatigue markers in mice, while a 2026 human Tartary buckwheat sprout-powder RCT showed borderline null ITT PROMIS Fatigue (p=0.058) and nominally favorable per-protocol fatigue (p=0.02), with no human PD trial of toasted groats.
