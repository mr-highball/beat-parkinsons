ID: 0710
Category: food
Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming frozen blueberry reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: four non-PD blueberry RCTs (including clinically diagnosed MDD/GAD adults and adolescents) eased depressive symptoms or positive affect, but a directly opposing 6-week depressed emerging-adult RCT (worse BDI-II/PHQ-9 vs placebo), three chronic nulls, and zero PD depression trials cap confidence at minimal_signal.
