Statement: Consuming raw blueberry worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: multiple non-PD blueberry RCTs (including a 6-month MCI older-adult RCT and pediatric MANT trials) improved attention/processing speed, opposing the claim that raw blueberry worsens attention, though null trials and meta-analytic heterogeneity limit confidence to minimal_signal.
