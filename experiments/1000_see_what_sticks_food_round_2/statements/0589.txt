Ingredient: black beans

Symptom: urinary urgency frequency

Statement: Consuming boiled black beans worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether boiled black beans worsens urinary urgency frequency in Parkinson's disease because no controlled study of Phaseolus vulgaris black beans has assessed urinary urgency frequency.
