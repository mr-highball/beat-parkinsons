ID: 0155
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: reduced facial expressiveness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens reduced facial expressiveness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical study or symptom-matched animal experiment tests ground anise seed for reduced facial expressiveness in Parkinson's disease. Human RCTs of ground P. anisum powder (Begum/Heyat 2026 n=60; Ghoshegir 2015 n=107; Mosaffa-Jahromi 2024 n=225) evaluated menopausal/psychological or GI symptoms and did not measure reduced facial expressiveness. Verdict remains Unknown.
