Ingredient: brussels sprouts

Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed brussels sprouts reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: double-blind sulforaphane RCTs lowered HAM-D depression in post-cardiac adults and negative mood in older adults alongside three rodent depression models, bridged to steamed Brussels sprouts by confirmed sulforaphane content after steaming, though no PD depression trial has tested Brussels sprouts.
