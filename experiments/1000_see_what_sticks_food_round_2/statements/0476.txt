Category: food
Ingredient: beef
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: daily off time
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked beef reduces daily off time in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Maybe False: controlled PD protein-redistribution and high- vs low-protein crossover trials consistently show higher daytime dietary protein and plasma LNAAs increase daily OFF time in levodopa-treated fluctuators, and cooked beef delivers a dense postprandial LNAA load, opposing the frozen claim that cooked beef reduces daily OFF time; absence of a beef-only PD trial and wide inter-patient variability keep the tier at limited_clinical_link.
