Ingredient: black tea

Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea reduces muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Maybe True: a 6-week double-blind Parkinson disease caffeine RCT reduced clinician-rated UPDRS III muscle rigidity by 1.01 points (bridged to brewed black tea via chromatography), tempered by null 6-18 month total motor scores in Café-PD and absence of a direct brewed black tea PD trial.
