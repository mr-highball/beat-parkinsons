Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or experimental evidence tests oral cold-pressed sweet almond oil for urinary urgency frequency in Parkinson's disease. Oral almond-oil trials addressed lipids or constipation rather than urinary urgency frequency, and topical massage trials used almond oil as an external vehicle, so the verdict remains Unknown.
