Category: food
Ingredient: Brazil nut
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted Brazil nut worsens constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: human Brazil nut trials measured gut permeability rather than constipation, and a healthy-rat high-dose (10%) Brazil nut study reported delayed gastric emptying and enteric-glia shifts without demonstrating clinical constipation.
