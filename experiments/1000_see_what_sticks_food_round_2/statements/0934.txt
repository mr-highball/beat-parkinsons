Statement: Consuming baked butternut squash worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Maybe False: rather than worsening fatigue, 14-day oral Cucurbita moschata fruit extract dose-dependently extended exhaustive swimming endurance, raised muscle/liver glycogen, and lowered post-exercise lactate, ammonia, and creatine kinase in mice, though no human Parkinson's fatigue trial has tested baked butternut squash.
