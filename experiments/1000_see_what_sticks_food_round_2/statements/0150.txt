ID: 0150
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed reduces low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical study or symptom-matched animal experiment tests ground anise seed for low voice volume severity in Parkinson's disease. Human RCTs of ground P. anisum powder (Begum/Heyat 2026 n=60; Ghoshegir 2015 n=107; Mosaffa-Jahromi 2024 n=225) evaluated menopausal/psychological or GI symptoms and did not measure low voice volume severity. Verdict remains Unknown.
