Statement: Consuming steamed asparagus reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether steamed asparagus reduces attention impairment in Parkinson's disease because evidence is confined to stem-extract stress-task proxy counts in healthy volunteers without confirmed between-arm attention gains in the larger Cognitrax trial or any PD study.
