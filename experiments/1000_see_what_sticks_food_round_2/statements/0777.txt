ID: 0777
Category: food
Ingredient: Brazil nut
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted Brazil nut reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: one 6-month randomized pilot trial in older adults with MCI found 1 Brazil nut/day improved verbal fluency (an executive-retrieval measure, p=0.007) and constructional praxis (p=0.031) vs usual diet, but high attrition, unblinded control, lack of PD trials or replication, and selenium upper-limit safety limits restrict this to minimal_signal.
