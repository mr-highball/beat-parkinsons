Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or experimental evidence tests oral cold-pressed sweet almond oil for walking slowness in Parkinson's disease. Oral almond-oil trials addressed lipids or constipation rather than walking slowness, and topical massage trials used almond oil as an external vehicle, so the verdict remains Unknown.
