Category: food
Ingredient: Brazil nut
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted Brazil nut worsens bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no Brazil nut study tests bradykinesia; a single rat paraquat study used inorganic drinking-water selenium rather than Brazil nuts or selenomethionine, while long-term human L-selenomethionine follow-up (SELECT, n=34,887) showed near-null PD incidence.
