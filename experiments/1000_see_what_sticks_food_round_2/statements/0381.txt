Statement: Consuming raw banana reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): one randomized crossover showed raw Cavendish banana attenuated acute post-exercise energy-level drop vs water (p<0.05), but benefits matched a plain sugar drink, RPE was unchanged across two cycling trials, and a 2-week resting raw-banana RCT was null on POMS2 fatigue/vigor; no Parkinson's fatigue trials exist.
