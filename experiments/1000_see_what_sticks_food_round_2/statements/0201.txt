Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No human trial evaluates chronic raw apple intake on clinical depression scales in Parkinson disease. While acute single-meal whole-apple testing in healthy volunteers showed no mood shift (Bondonno 2014), two independent 2024-2025 mouse studies of oral apple polyphenol extract (Xie 2024; Qian 2025) consistently reduced depression-like immobility via gut-brain anti-inflammatory pathways, yielding a minimal_signal preclinical easing lean (Maybe False against 'worsens').
