ID: 0149
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed reduces freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical study or symptom-matched animal experiment tests ground anise seed for freezing of gait frequency in Parkinson's disease. Isolated anethole improved rotenone rat rotarod latency, footprint stride length, and striatal firing/dopamine, but did NOT directly assay freezing of gait frequency (no catalepsy/rigidity, tremor, dyskinesia, freezing, or bradykinesia velocity metrics) and tested isolated anethole rather than ground P. anisum seed. Verdict remains Unknown.
