ID: 0723
Category: food
Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw blueberry worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no controlled human trial demonstrates an effect of raw blueberry on orthostatic lightheadedness severity in Parkinson disease, and the completed PD wild blueberry trial (NCT07720869) has no posted results.
