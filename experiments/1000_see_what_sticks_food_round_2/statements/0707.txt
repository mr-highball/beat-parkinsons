Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw blueberry reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no controlled human trial demonstrates an effect of raw blueberry on bradykinesia severity in Parkinson disease, and the completed PD wild blueberry trial (NCT07720869) has no posted results.
