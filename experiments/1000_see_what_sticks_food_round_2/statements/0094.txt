Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or experimental evidence tests oral cold-pressed sweet almond oil for fatigue severity in Parkinson's disease. Oral almond-oil trials addressed lipids or constipation rather than fatigue severity, and topical massage trials used almond oil as an external vehicle, so the verdict remains Unknown.
