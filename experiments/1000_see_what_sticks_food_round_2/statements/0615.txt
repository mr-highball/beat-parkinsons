Statement: Consuming brewed black tea worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.

Rationale: Maybe False: brewed black tea crossover RCTs in non-PD adults consistently ease acute attention and vigilance impairments, opposing the claim that brewed black tea worsens attention, though single-cup null data, chronic PD MoCA uncertainty, and lack of PD attention trials limit confidence to minimal_signal.
