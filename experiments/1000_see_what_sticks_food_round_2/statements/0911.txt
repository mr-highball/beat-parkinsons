Category: food
Ingredient: buckwheat groats
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cooked buckwheat groats reduces postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: isolated rutin (abundant in buckwheat groats) protected motor coordination in 6-OHDA Parkinson rats and rescued chronic-stress muscle coordination deficits in mice, though no human Parkinson balance trial has tested cooked buckwheat groats.
