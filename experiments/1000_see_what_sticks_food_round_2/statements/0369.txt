ID: 0369
Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: visual hallucination frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed avocado oil reduces visual hallucination frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or Parkinson's preclinical evidence evaluates cold-pressed avocado oil for visual hallucination frequency. Cold-pressed avocado oil (4 mL/kg x 90 d) in DIO mice showed null EPM and open-field crossings versus obese controls (PMC9319255), while maternal/lactation avocado oil induced anxiogenic-like EPM/OFT behavior and lower adult offspring locomotion in rats (PMID:40024360); neither tested visual hallucination frequency or human PD. Verdict remains Unknown.
