Ingredient: apricot
Preparation: dried
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried apricot reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence is open for dried apricot and anxiety severity. No clinical trial or PD study has assessed dried apricot intake on anxiety, and an acute apricot-juice tasting experiment in anorexia nervosa and controls (Marzola 2020) showed no therapeutic anxiety benefit.
