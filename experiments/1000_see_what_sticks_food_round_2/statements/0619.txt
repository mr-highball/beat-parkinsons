ID: 0619
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea reduces depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: a large older-adult cohort linked habitual black/green/oolong tea to lower GDS depression scores and an MDD RCT showed adjunctive L-theanine lowered HDRS scores, tempered by null caffeine RCT BDI outcomes in non-depressed PD cohorts and no direct PD brewed-tea depression trial.
