ID: 0516
Category: food
Ingredient: beetroot
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled beetroot worsens executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: two recent non-PD RCTs show modest improvements in Trail-Making Test B and frontal/cognitive-flexibility tasks with beetroot juice or extract (with zero controlled worsening, opposing the frozen worsens claim), but a larger 13-week null older-adult RCT, a null 13-trial meta-analysis, boiling nitrate loss, and lack of PD trials keep this at minimal_signal.
