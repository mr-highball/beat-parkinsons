ID: 0818
Category: food
Ingredient: broccoli
Preparation: raw
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw broccoli worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: a double-blind knee osteoarthritis broccoli-soup RCT reduced WOMAC pain by 4.2 points and three mouse models demonstrated sulforaphane antinociception across neuropathic and inflammatory pain, pointing toward pain easing rather than worsening, though no PD pain trial tests raw broccoli.
