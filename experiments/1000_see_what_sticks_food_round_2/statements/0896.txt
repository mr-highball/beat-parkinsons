Rationale: Maybe False: oral Tartary buckwheat whole-seed extract and isolated rutin consistently reduced depression-like behavior across five independent rodent models, though a large 2026 human RCT of Tartary buckwheat sprout powder in non-depressed older adults showed null exploratory PROMIS Depression scores (p=0.189 ITT) and no PD depression trial exists.
