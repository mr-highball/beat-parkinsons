ID: 0159
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: urinary urgency frequency
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens urinary urgency frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human study tests ground anise seed for urinary urgency in Parkinson's disease. Aniseed essential oil reduced total urine volume in healthy rats via renal Na+-K+ ATPase activation (Kreydiyyeh 2003), while traditional Iranian texts describe mild diuresis (Shojaii 2012); neither evaluates neurogenic detrusor overactivity or clinical urinary urgency frequency, leaving the verdict Unknown.
