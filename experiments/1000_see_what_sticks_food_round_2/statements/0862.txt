Category: food
Ingredient: brussels sprouts
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming steamed brussels sprouts reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: purified sulforaphane improved bradykinesia-related motor deficits across rotenone, 6-OHDA, and MPTP Parkinson mouse models and steamed Brussels sprouts retain measurable sulforaphane, although an uncontrolled PD broccoli-seed-tea log showed 0/13 bradykinesia improvement and no clinical trial exists.
