ID: 0518
Category: food
Ingredient: beetroot
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming boiled beetroot worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: multiple double-blind non-PD RCTs of beetroot juice, betalain concentrate, and baked whole beetroot ease exercise-induced and subjective exertional fatigue (opposing the frozen claim that boiled beetroot worsens fatigue), but competing null trials (long COVID, older-adult leg fatigue), boiling nitrate loss, and lack of PD fatigue trials hold confidence at minimal_signal.
