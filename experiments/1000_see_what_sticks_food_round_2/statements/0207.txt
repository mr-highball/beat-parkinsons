Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No clinical trial tests raw whole apples for Parkinson fatigue scales (such as FSS or PFS-16). Nevertheless, a controlled human crossover trial of isolated apple polyphenols (Ataka 2007) and a rat forced-swimming study of aqueous apple extract (Alembagheri 2023) both show reduced physical fatigue, supported contextually by bundled grape+apple polyphenol endurance data (Deley 2017) and Persian Medicine tonic framing. This supports a minimal_signal easing lean (Maybe False against 'worsens').
