ID: 0214
Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: baked
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked apple worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains open for baked apple and orthostatic lightheadedness severity. Chronic whole-apple RCTs consistently show neutral resting blood pressure, no study tested postural blood-pressure drops or neurogenic orthostatic hypotension symptoms, and baked apple has not been evaluated.
