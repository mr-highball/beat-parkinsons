ID: 0507
Category: food
Ingredient: beetroot
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: bradykinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted beetroot reduces bradykinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: three independent controlled PD-model rodent studies show oral Beta vulgaris methanolic extract or purified betanin ease reserpine akinesia, haloperidol catalepsy, and rotenone locomotor slowness, but absence of human PD trials and negligible human plasma betanin from whole beetroot limit confidence to minimal_signal.
