ID: 0990
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: fall frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed canola oil reduces fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no Parkinson's gait or fall trial isolates cold-pressed canola oil; older-adult RCTs using canola oil as a comparator show null between-arm differences on walking speed (Mutoh 2022 p=0.695), step length (p=0.785), Timed Up and Go with exercise (Kojima 2023), and 6-minute walk/SPPB (Stubbs 2025 p=0.461/0.099), while MCT outperformed canola only on Lissajous balance index (p=0.009, canola within-arm null p=0.410).
