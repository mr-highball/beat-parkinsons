ID: 0732
Category: food
Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming frozen blueberry reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no controlled human trial demonstrates an effect of frozen blueberry on walking slowness in Parkinson disease, and the completed PD wild blueberry trial (NCT07720869) has no posted results.
