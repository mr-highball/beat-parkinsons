ID: 0538
Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted bell pepper worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No controlled human trial demonstrates an effect of roasted sweet bell pepper on attention impairment in Parkinson disease. The only randomized crossover trial of sweet-pepper-derived paprika xanthophylls (Kim & Wadazumi 2025) was null between groups on Stroop and TMT-B.
