ID: 0196
Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: baked
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming baked apple reduces attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human trial demonstrates that baked whole apples improve or worsen attention impairment in Parkinson disease. The best isolated whole-apple human crossover RCT (Bondonno 2014) reported null effects across computerized cognitive performance, an Alzheimer apple-juice pilot (Remington 2010) showed null cognitive scores, and bundled coffee-berry + apple-extract testing (Jackson 2022) cannot isolate apple.
