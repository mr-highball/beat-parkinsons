Ingredient: blackberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw blackberry worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No human clinical trial tests isolated raw blackberries on anxiety severity in Parkinson disease (Whyte 2019 tested a 4-berry mixture with null negative-affect change). In a controlled Wistar rat experiment comparing three doses of Rubus fruticosus juice against vehicle and diazepam (Fernandez-Demeneghi 2019), the intermediate dose produced a diazepam-like anxiolytic effect (while low and high doses were null), supporting a minimal_signal preclinical easing lean (Maybe False against 'worsens').
