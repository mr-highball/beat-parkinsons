ID: 0988
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: dystonia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed canola oil reduces dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no human trial or animal PD model isolates cold-pressed canola oil on dystonia severity; Phillips 2018 bundled unspecified canola oil inside a whole low-fat PD menu, Kheirouri 2023 pooled all non-hydrogenated plant oils, Sampaio 2017 used canola solely as solvent vehicle, and Pohl 2019/2021 tested defatted rapeseed pomace rather than culinary oil.
