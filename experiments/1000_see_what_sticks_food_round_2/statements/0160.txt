ID: 0160
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical study or symptom-matched animal experiment tests ground anise seed for walking slowness in Parkinson's disease. Isolated anethole improved rotenone rat rotarod latency, footprint stride length, and striatal firing/dopamine, but did NOT directly assay walking slowness (no catalepsy/rigidity, tremor, dyskinesia, freezing, or bradykinesia velocity metrics) and tested isolated anethole rather than ground P. anisum seed. Verdict remains Unknown.
