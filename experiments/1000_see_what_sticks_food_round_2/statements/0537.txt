Category: food
Ingredient: bell pepper
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted bell pepper reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence is open for roasted bell pepper and anxiety severity in Parkinson disease. Purified nonpungent sweet-pepper capsinoids showed null chronic mood effects in older adults (Yokoyama 2020), and multi-herb mouse mixtures (de Munter 2021) cannot isolate bell pepper.
