ID: 0715
Category: food
Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: raw
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw blueberry reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: five non-PD blueberry powder RCTs (including three in middle-aged/older adults) improved executive task-switching, inhibition, or lexical access, tempered by null 6-month MetS and 6-week emerging-adult chronic trials, freeze-dried powder vs raw fruit preparation differences, and absence of PD trials.
