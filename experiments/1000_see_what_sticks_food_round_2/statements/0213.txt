Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: baked
Symptom: olfactory impairment severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked apple worsens olfactory impairment severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or preclinical trial has evaluated ingested baked apples for Parkinson olfactory impairment. Existing olfactory-training data (Power Guerra 2024) used inhaled stewed-apple odorant alongside two other added scents with no incremental benefit over standard 4-odorant training.
