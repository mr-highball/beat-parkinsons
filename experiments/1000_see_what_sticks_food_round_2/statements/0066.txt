Category: food
Ingredient: almond
Preparation: raw
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw almond reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): one 24-week RCT in prediabetic adults found improved CANTAB executive function (P=0.01) with daily almonds, whereas three competing adult RCTs (Adams 2026, Coates 2020, Mustra Rakic 2022) found null between-group executive contrasts and no Parkinson's trial exists.
