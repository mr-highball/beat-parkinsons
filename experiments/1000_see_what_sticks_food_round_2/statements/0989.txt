ID: 0989
Category: oil
Ingredient: canola oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: executive function impairment
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed canola oil reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: human randomized trials show null cognitive differences for canola vs coconut oil on attention/memory (Diekmann 2026) and VCO in AD (Fernando 2023), NIH-AARP (n=521,120) links canola replacing butter/margarine to lower AD mortality, and fortified rapeseed oil improved db/db mouse memory, whereas non-isocaloric refined Mazola canola chow impaired 3xTg mouse Y-maze alternation alongside +5.8 g weight gain (Lauretti 2017) and MCT outperformed canola placebo in APOE4-/- AD (Xu 2020) — conflicting, active-comparator, or confounded evidence.
