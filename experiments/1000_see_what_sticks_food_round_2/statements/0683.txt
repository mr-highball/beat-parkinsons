Ingredient: blackberry
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming frozen blackberry worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence is conflicting and remains open for frozen blackberry and depressive symptom severity. Although rat forced-swim (Fernandez-Demeneghi 2019) and mouse LPS-depression (Custodio 2025) models point toward lower depressive-like behavior, the only human blackberry trial report (Park 2025, n=29 older adults, freeze-dried powder) noted statistically higher depression scores (p=0.047) in an incompletely reported conference poster, precluding any reliable directional lean.
