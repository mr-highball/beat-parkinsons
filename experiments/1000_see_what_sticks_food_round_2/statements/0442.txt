ID: 0442
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: orthostatic lightheadedness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried basil worsens orthostatic lightheadedness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or preclinical study evaluates dried sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) for orthostatic lightheadedness severity in Parkinson's disease. Existing human sweet-basil trials targeted anxiety, depression, menopausal sleep, or pain and omitted orthostatic lightheadedness severity (while Holy Basil/O. tenuiflorum studies are excluded as a distinct species), so the verdict remains Unknown.
