ID: 0704
Category: food
Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming frozen blueberry worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: two non-PD blueberry RCTs (including clinically diagnosed MDD/GAD adults) lowered GAD-7/STAI anxiety scores alongside acute postprandial calmness gains, opposing worsening, though one emerging-adult RCT found worse 6-week GAD-7 vs placebo and no PD anxiety trial exists.
