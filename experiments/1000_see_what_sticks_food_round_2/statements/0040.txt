Category: spice
Ingredient: allspice
Preparation: ground
Symptom: dystonia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground allspice worsens dystonia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical study or whole ground allspice experiment evaluates dystonia severity in Parkinson's disease. Isolated eugenol PD animal models conflict by timing and species—aggravating post-MPTP akinesia (Vora 2022) and 6-OHDA striatal dopamine loss (Kabuto 2011) while improving motor metrics in other rat protocols (Moreira Vasconcelos 2020; Uchewa 2025)—so no directional verdict is defensible (Unknown).
