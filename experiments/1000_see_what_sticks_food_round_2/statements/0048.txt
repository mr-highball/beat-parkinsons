ID: 0048
Category: spice
Ingredient: allspice
Preparation: ground
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground allspice worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No human clinical study evaluates ground allspice for pain severity in Parkinson's disease. Intraperitoneal P. dioica leaf extracts produced acute CNS depression/analgesia partly confounded by peritoneal irritation (Suarez 1997), oil/eugenol caused mucosal irritation in arthritic rats (Shelly-Campbell 2015), and Caribbean folk records describe topical crushed berries for sore joints and dysmenorrhea tea (PMC3891794), with zero controlled oral ground-allspice pain studies. Verdict remains Unknown.
