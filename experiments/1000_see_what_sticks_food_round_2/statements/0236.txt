Statement: Consuming dried apricot reduces executive function impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Insufficient evidence for dried apricot and executive function impairment in Parkinson disease. Apricot juice served only as an inactive comparator in an 8-week MCI cognitive trial (May 2025) without benefit, and no dried-apricot PD cognitive studies exist.
