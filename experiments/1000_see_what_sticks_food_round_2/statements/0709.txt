Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity

Statement: Consuming frozen blueberry reduces daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Unknown: no controlled human trial demonstrates an effect of frozen blueberry on daytime sleepiness severity in Parkinson disease, and the completed PD wild blueberry trial (NCT07720869) has no posted results.
