ID: 0441
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: low voice volume severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried basil reduces low voice volume severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or preclinical study evaluates dried sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) for low voice volume severity in Parkinson's disease. Existing human sweet-basil trials targeted anxiety, depression, menopausal sleep, or pain and omitted low voice volume severity (while Holy Basil/O. tenuiflorum studies are excluded as a distinct species), so the verdict remains Unknown.
