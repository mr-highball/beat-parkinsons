ID: 0145
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two independent double-blind randomized trials outside Parkinson's disease found Pimpinella anisum eased rather than worsened depressive symptoms: 4 g/day ground anise seed powder lowered DASS-21 depression in menopausal women (22.00 to 3.47 vs 19.47 to 14.20, p < 0.001; Begum/Heyat 2026, n=60) and enteric-coated anise oil lowered BDI-II in IBS patients (11.55 to 7.34 vs 11.02 to 10.11 at week 6, p < 0.001; Mosaffa-Jahromi 2017, n=120), supported by rodent depression models and classical Persian melancholia use (while explicitly excluding Ghoshegir 2015's abstract typo 'postpartum depression' for PDS). Non-PD cohorts and trial reporting anomalies keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal (Maybe False).
