Category: food
Ingredient: butternut squash
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: swallowing difficulty severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted butternut squash reduces swallowing difficulty severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no clinical trial tests roasted butternut squash on Parkinson's swallowing difficulty; Forsberg 2022 reported low finger-food liking (12%) for butternut squash in Swedish older adults, a PD dietitian guide adapts roasted butternut puree to IDDSI swallowing consistency as a compensatory strategy, and raw squash pulp can rarely cause contact dermatitis.
