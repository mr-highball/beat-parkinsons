ID: 0436
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: fresh
Symptom: drooling severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming fresh basil worsens drooling severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or preclinical study evaluates fresh sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) for drooling severity in Parkinson's disease. Existing human sweet-basil trials targeted anxiety, depression, menopausal sleep, or pain and omitted drooling severity (while Holy Basil/O. tenuiflorum studies are excluded as a distinct species), so the verdict remains Unknown.
