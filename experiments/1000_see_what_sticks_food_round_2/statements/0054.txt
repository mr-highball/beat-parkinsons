Statement: Consuming roasted almond worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False (minimal_signal toward easing): controlled human evidence provides no indication that roasted almonds worsen anxiety, and one raw-almond RCT showed lower post-exercise POMS tension (p=0.033) alongside roasted-almond mental-stress HRV improvement (p=0.031), but three null RCTs including a dedicated 12-week anxiety/depression trial (P=0.945) and absence of Parkinson's anxiety data keep certainty minimal.
