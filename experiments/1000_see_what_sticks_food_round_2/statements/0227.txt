Ingredient: apricot
Preparation: dried
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried apricot worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Insufficient evidence for dried apricot and attention impairment in Parkinson disease. Apricot juice served only as an inactive comparator in an 8-week MCI cognitive trial (May 2025) without benefit, and no dried-apricot PD cognitive studies exist.
