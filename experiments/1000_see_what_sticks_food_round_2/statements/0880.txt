ID: 0880
Category: food
Ingredient: brussels sprouts
Preparation: steamed
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming steamed brussels sprouts worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: rather than worsening postural instability, purified sulforaphane rescued narrow-beam balance in rotenone PD mice and motor coordination in 6-OHDA PD mice, bridged analytically to steamed Brussels sprouts, though uncontrolled PD seed-tea logs reported no motor gain and no human balance trial exists.
