Ingredient: butternut squash

Symptom: depressive symptom severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming baked butternut squash worsens depressive symptom severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: rather than worsening depression, 28-day oral feeding of baked Cucurbita moschata (exact botanical species and baked preparation of butternut squash) and isolated beta-carotene reduced mouse forced-swim immobility and raised brain serotonin, norepinephrine, and BDNF, aligned with Korean traditional use and observational beta-carotene meta-analyses, though no human PD depression trial exists.
