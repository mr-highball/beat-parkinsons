Ingredient: butternut squash
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: walking slowness
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted butternut squash reduces walking slowness in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no trial tests roasted butternut squash on Parkinson's walking slowness; the Rush Memory and Aging Project cohort associated pooled multi-food dietary carotenoids and beta-carotene with slower composite parkinsonian sign progression in non-PD older adults, but cannot isolate single foods or diagnosed PD walking slowness.
