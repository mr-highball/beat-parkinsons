ID: 0431
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried basil worsens anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Two randomized non-Parkinson's trials of sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum, strictly separated from Holy Basil/Tulsi) found reductions rather than worsening in anxiety-related scores: a dried-leaf extract syrup significantly lowered HAM-A anxiety in adults with major depression on sertraline (33.19 to 12.81 vs 34.53 to 24.80, p < 0.001; Talaei 2025, n=57) and leaf extract capsules lowered the pooled MRS psychological subscale in menopausal women (2.8 vs 4.5, p = 0.001; Karimi 2024, n=60), supported by non-retracted preclinical anxiolytic studies. Non-PD populations, concentrated extract forms, single-blind/dosing-text caveats in Talaei 2025, and zero PD data keep the lean against worsening at minimal_signal (Maybe False).
