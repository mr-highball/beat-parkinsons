ID: 0413
Category: food
Ingredient: barley
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: fatigue severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming boiled barley reduces fatigue severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): two independent RCTs of boiled/cooked waxy barley reported fewer "feeling tired" days vs white rice (p<0.0001) and better PHQ-9 energy/fatigue scores with higher barley beta-glucan (p=0.002), though POMS 2 fatigue was only a trend, outcomes were secondary/single-item, and no Parkinson's fatigue trial exists.
