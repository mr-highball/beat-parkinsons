Statement: Consuming cooked barley reduces insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): one double-blind cooked waxy barley RCT showed significant between-group improvement in PSQI global sleep score (p=0.004) and PHQ-9 sleep-problems score (p=0.019) with higher barley beta-glucan content, but both arms ate barley without a non-barley control and no Parkinson's insomnia trials exist.
