Category: food
Ingredient: apple
Preparation: raw
Symptom: postural instability severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming raw apple worsens postural instability severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: No human trial tests raw whole apples on Parkinson postural stability (pull test or posturography; Apple Watch/iOS balance trials are device homonyms). In a controlled rotenone PD mouse experiment (Shirgadwar 2023, N=50), oral phloretin — an apple-signature dihydrochalcone — significantly improved accelerating rotarod balance performance at both 50 and 100 mg/kg (p<0.01), yielding a minimal_signal preclinical easing lean (Maybe False against 'worsens').
