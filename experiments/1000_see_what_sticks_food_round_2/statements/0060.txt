Ingredient: almond

Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted almond worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown (open): two large multi-month whole-almond RCTs found no significant effect on Epworth (p=0.592/0.096) or Karolinska (p=0.892) daytime sleepiness, and no trial has tested roasted almonds or Parkinson's daytime sleepiness.
