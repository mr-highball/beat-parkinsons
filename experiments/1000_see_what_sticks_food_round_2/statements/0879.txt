Ingredient: brussels sprouts
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming roasted brussels sprouts worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: rather than worsening pain, purified sulforaphane consistently reduced neuropathic and inflammatory pain across three independent mouse models, bridged to roasted Brussels sprouts via dry-heat bioactive retention and gut-microbial glucoraphanin conversion despite heat loss of plant myrosinase, with no clinical PD pain trial.
