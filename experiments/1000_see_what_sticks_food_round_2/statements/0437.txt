ID: 0437
Category: herb
Ingredient: basil
Preparation: dried
Symptom: dyskinesia severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming dried basil reduces dyskinesia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No reliable clinical or preclinical evidence tests dried sweet basil (Ocimum basilicum) for dyskinesia severity in Parkinson's disease. Existing haloperidol and rotenone rat extract papers (Behera 2023; Gayakwad 2026) contain disqualifying internal reporting contradictions, high-dose oral basil EO caused mouse motor impairment (Sa Filho 2026), and Holy Basil (O. tenuiflorum) studies are excluded as a distinct species. Verdict remains Unknown.
