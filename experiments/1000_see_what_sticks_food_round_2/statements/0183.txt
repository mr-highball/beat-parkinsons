Symptom: pain severity

Statement: Consuming ground annatto reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): four independent oral rodent models using Bixa orellana seed oil, bixin, and annatto-derived geranylgeraniol consistently reduce acute and neuropathic pain, though human trials of isolated tocotrienols in non-pain volunteers were null (p=0.761), human DOMS/knee trials bundled ginger, and no ground-seed or Parkinson's pain trial exists.
