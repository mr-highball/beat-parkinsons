ID: 0530
Category: food
Ingredient: beetroot
Preparation: boiled
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming boiled beetroot reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: a 12-week double-blind RCT in middle-aged/older adults found FDR-significant stride-length gains with beetroot juice + resistance training (q<0.001), alongside MPTP mouse step-length reversal with oral betanin, but lack of PD clinical trials and nitrate leaching during boiling restrict confidence to minimal_signal.
