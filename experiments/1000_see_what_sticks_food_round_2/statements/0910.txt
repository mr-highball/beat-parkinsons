ID: 0910
Category: food
Ingredient: buckwheat groats
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted buckwheat groats worsens pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: rather than worsening pain, buckwheat food products significantly reduced abdominal pain in a human non-celiac gluten-sensitivity crossover trial (p=0.03) and isolated rutin reduced inflammatory and neuropathic pain across three independent rat models, though rare diabetic neuropathy case reports with Tartary buckwheat products warrant caution and no PD pain trial exists.
