Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea reduces freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: an uncontrolled PD clinical study reported 100 mg/day caffeine eased the total-akinesia subtype of freezing of gait (with tolerance over months), bridged to brewed black tea by infusion chromatography, but lacks controlled replication or brewed-tea testing.
