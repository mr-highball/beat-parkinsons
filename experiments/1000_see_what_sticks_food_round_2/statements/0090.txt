ID: 0090
Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: constipation severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil reduces constipation severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Oral sweet almond oil significantly reduced functional constipation severity in older adults in a randomized trial (Faghihi 2022, PMC9239810, n=79; CAS 7.81 to 1.15 vs control 7.73 to 6.00, p < 0.001), aligning with Traditional Persian Medicine laxative use. Because the trial lacked Parkinson's patients, lacked a placebo oil comparator, did not confirm cold-pressed extraction, and caused frequent watery stools (53.8% BSFS Type 7), the easing direction remains at minimal_signal (Maybe True).
