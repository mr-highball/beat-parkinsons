ID: 0618
Category: food
Ingredient: black tea
Preparation: brewed
Symptom: daytime sleepiness severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming brewed black tea worsens daytime sleepiness severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 51
Rationale: Maybe False: two double-blind Parkinson disease caffeine RCTs (bridged to brewed black tea by infusion chromatography) and three brewed black tea non-PD crossover RCTs indicate modest easing of daytime sleepiness/sedation rather than worsening, though benefits attenuate over 12-18 months and no direct PD brewed-tea trial exists.
