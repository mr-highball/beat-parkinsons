ID: 0845
Category: food
Ingredient: brown rice
Preparation: cooked
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming cooked brown rice worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe False
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe False: three double-blind RCTs of rice bran extract, fermented unpolished rice-germ GABA, and rice bran tocotrienols improved polysomnographic sleep latency, sleep efficiency, or sleep disturbance in non-PD adults, opposing worsening, though no trial isolates cooked whole brown rice on PD insomnia.
