Statement: Consuming raw bell pepper worsens fall frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown

Rationale: Insufficient evidence to determine whether raw sweet bell pepper worsens fall frequency in Parkinson disease. Pungent chili/capsaicin studies are excluded because sweet bell pepper is virtually capsaicin-free.
