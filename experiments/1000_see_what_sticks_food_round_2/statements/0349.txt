ID: 0349
Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: attention impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined avocado oil worsens attention impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or animal study isolates refined avocado oil's effect on attention in Parkinson's disease. Rudisill 2023 used mint-flavored avocado oil solely as the placebo carrier against cannabidiol with no oil-free comparator, so no causal effect on vigilance or attention can be attributed to avocado oil (Unknown).
