ID: 0095
Category: oil
Ingredient: almond oil
Preparation: cold-pressed
Symptom: freezing of gait frequency
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming cold-pressed almond oil reduces freezing of gait frequency in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or experimental evidence tests oral cold-pressed sweet almond oil for freezing of gait frequency in Parkinson's disease. Oral almond-oil trials addressed lipids or constipation rather than freezing of gait frequency, and topical massage trials used almond oil as an external vehicle, so the verdict remains Unknown.
