Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground bay leaf reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Evidence remains Unknown (open) for whether ground bay leaf reduces pain severity in Parkinson's disease because essential-oil rodent analgesia is confounded by sedation, isolated alkaloid effects diverge across pain assays and ASIC subtypes, and no clinical human pain trial exists.
