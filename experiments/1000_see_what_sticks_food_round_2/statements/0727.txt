ID: 0727
Category: food
Ingredient: blueberry
Preparation: frozen
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming frozen blueberry worsens shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no controlled human trial demonstrates an effect of frozen blueberry on shortened stride severity in Parkinson disease, and the completed PD wild blueberry trial (NCT07720869) has no posted results.
