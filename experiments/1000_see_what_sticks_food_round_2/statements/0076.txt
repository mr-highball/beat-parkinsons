ID: 0076
Category: food
Ingredient: almond
Preparation: roasted
Symptom: pain severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming roasted almond reduces pain severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True (minimal_signal toward easing): two independent 8-week crossover RCTs of whole raw almonds reduced task-evoked exercise muscle pain (-24% to -37%, p<0.05), though competing trials show null DOMS at 4 weeks (Nieman 2022) and worsened abdominal pain from high-dose almond powder sachets in IBS-D (Darvishmoghadam 2019), and neither roasted almonds nor Parkinson's pain has been tested.
