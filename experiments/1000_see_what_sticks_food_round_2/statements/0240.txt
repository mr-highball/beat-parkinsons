Ingredient: apricot
Preparation: dried
Symptom: hand dexterity impairment
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming dried apricot worsens hand dexterity impairment in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Insufficient evidence to determine whether dried culinary apricot worsens hand dexterity impairment in Parkinson disease. No study isolates edible dried Prunus armeniaca flesh on hand dexterity impairment.
