Ingredient: broccoli
Preparation: raw
Symptom: anxiety severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming raw broccoli reduces anxiety severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: Maybe True: broccoli floret extract and isolated sulforaphane reduced anxiety-like behavior across three independent mouse models, bridged to raw broccoli by intact myrosinase bioavailability, though human sulforaphane trials have not established an anxiety-specific benefit and no PD trial exists.
