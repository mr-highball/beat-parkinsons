ID: 0905
Category: food
Ingredient: buckwheat groats
Preparation: toasted
Symptom: insomnia severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming toasted buckwheat groats worsens insomnia severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: Unknown: no human Parkinson's sleep trial tests toasted buckwheat groats; a 2026 Tartary buckwheat sprout-powder RCT found null ITT PROMIS Sleep-Related Impairment (p=0.122; exploratory PP p=0.03) and pediatric buckwheat-honey cough trials do not transfer to groats or PD insomnia severity.
