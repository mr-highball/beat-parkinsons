Category: oil
Ingredient: avocado oil
Preparation: refined
Symptom: muscle rigidity severity
Direction: worsens
Statement: Consuming refined avocado oil worsens muscle rigidity severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Unknown
Confidence: 50
Rationale: No clinical or Parkinson's preclinical evidence evaluates refined avocado oil for muscle rigidity severity. Wistar rats fed an avocado oil diet for 20 days prior to intrastriatal quinolinic acid (Huntington's excitotoxicity model) showed fewer ipsilateral turns and preserved striatal GABA, but this is a Huntington excitotoxic lesion rather than a Parkinson's nigrostriatal model and does not evaluate muscle rigidity severity. Verdict remains Unknown.
