ID: 0157
Category: spice
Ingredient: anise seed
Preparation: ground
Symptom: shortened stride severity
Direction: reduces
Statement: Consuming ground anise seed reduces shortened stride severity in people with Parkinson's disease.
Verdict: Maybe True
Confidence: 50.005
Rationale: In a rotenone rat model of Parkinson's disease (Moradi Vastegani 2023, PMC10653401, n=48), oral isolated anethole—the dominant essential-oil constituent of anise seed—significantly restored directly measured footprint stride length across all three doses (62.5-250 mg/kg/d; p < 0.05 to p < 0.001) alongside L-DOPA positive control and reduced striatal alpha-synuclein/MAO-B. Because no human trial or whole ground anise-seed gait experiment exists and anethole also occurs in star anise and fennel, the easing lean stays strictly at minimal_signal (Maybe True).
