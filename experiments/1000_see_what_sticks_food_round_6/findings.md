- **Saffron (*Crocus sativus*) — direct diagnosed-PD triple-blind clinical trial (`0849`, `direct_clinical_signal` at `55`, eases -> Maybe False; plus `0862` pain at `50.005`):** Tightly calibrated with Round 1 (`0063` depression and `0485` fatigue at `55`), the Isfahan triple-blind, placebo-controlled PD RCT (`Hajhashemy 2025/2026`, `PMID:41439298`, `IRCT20121216011763N61`, n=92 receiving 100 mg/day milled dried Sargol stigma powder × 12 weeks) directly improved prespecified DASS-21 Anxiety (`adj MD -4.75`, `p=0.009`) alongside corroborating non-PD RCTs (`Mazidi 2016`, `Kell 2017`, `Lopresti 2018`, `Milajerdi 2018`, `Pouchieu 2023`), while explicitly preserving six null non-PD anxiety RCTs (`Lopresti 2025` n=202, `Amadieu 2025`, `Dormal 2025`, `Jackson 2021`, `Askari 2023`, `Moazen-Zadeh 2018`). On pain ([0862](statements/0862.txt)), `Hajhashemy 2025/2026` PDQ-39 Bodily Discomfort (`adj MD -7.50`, `p=0.02`) and `Hamidi 2020`/`Meamarbashi 2015` are held at `minimal_signal` (`50.005`) due to competing null RA pain (`Sahebari 2021` `PMC8264227`) and trim-and-fill null dysmenorrhea (`Mohammadi 2026`).
